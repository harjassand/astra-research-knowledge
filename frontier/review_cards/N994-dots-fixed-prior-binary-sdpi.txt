N994-dots-fixed-prior-binary-sdpi | A binary-input channel has the same fixed-prior Umegaki SDPI constant with any finite quantum reference
status=source_derived_unreviewed; exact scoped internal acceptance, where present, is bound by knowledge/reviews/A993-dots-binary-entropy-source-read.json.

EXACT CLAIM
For every finite-output classical channel T with binary input and every full-support fixed prior pi, let K=A_0+A_1, Y_y=T_{y0}A_0+T_{y1}A_1, and q_y=(T pi)_y. For arbitrary positive A_0,A_1 on any common finite-dimensional quantum reference, sum_{y:q_y>0} D(Y_y || q_y K) <= eta(T,pi) sum_{i=0}^1 D(A_i || pi_i K), where eta(T,pi) is the scalar fixed-prior Umegaki SDPI constant. Scalar references give the matching lower bound, so allowing an arbitrary finite quantum reference does not change this fixed-prior constant.

SOURCE-REPORTED INTERFACE
Finite-output stochastic channel with input alphabet {0,1}; full-support fixed prior pi; arbitrary finite-dimensional positive operator weights A_0,A_1; omit zero output-prior symbols.

BOUNDARIES
This fixes the binary transmitted alphabet and the prior. It is not the global-prior binary coefficient in N970, not an arbitrary-alphabet theorem with arbitrary references, not a reduction of multi-symbol quantum-memory problems, and not a t log t matrix-SDPI separation.

An exact finite-dimensional inequality for Umegaki relative entropy; no acquired channel experiment, measurement protocol, complexity bound, or resource estimate is supplied.
