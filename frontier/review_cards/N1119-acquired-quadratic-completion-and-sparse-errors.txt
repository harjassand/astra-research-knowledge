N1119-acquired-quadratic-completion-and-sparse-errors

Acquire a low-rank quadratic completion despite unknown diagonal and sparse corruption

EXACT CLAIM
For raw alternating B=off(D)+S over F2 with unknown symmetric rank(D)<=r and at most M unordered sparse edges, acquire a retained exact completion after O(sqrt(rM)+r) coordinate deletions. Acquisition is unconditional and subexponential for r=o(n),rM=o(n^2). With the local-phase theorem, log(2+(m+M)/n)=o(log n) gives original-input subexponential exact counting.

INTERFACE
Raw explicit binary matrix, fixed-locality foreground tables, exact arithmetic. No hidden D,S, diagonal, column code or pivot witness is supplied. A polynomial-size dovetailed budget portfolio is required if favorable parameters are unknown.

BOUNDARIES
The retained representation search is weaker than full-matrix editing. Pivot columns may be erased externally; cleaned code need only contain the true column space. Pure quadratic sums and error-free diagonal completion already have ordinary algorithms. Generic dense quadratic plus nonlinear counting remains open.

COST AND INFORMATION BOUNDARY
Time poly(n)A_n(r)A_n(s0)2^r for acquisition, s0=min(n,floor(2M/delta)+r delta); final deleted s<=min(n,floor(2M/delta)+3r delta). Counting adds 2^(s+r+F(n,m+M+O(n))) with polynomial space, exact unary diagonal/refinement corrections and divisions only after inner reconstruction.

PROOF SPINE
Enumerate unordered clean-column/erasure candidates; puncture supports of short codewords until unique decoding; decode and directly validate symmetry/rank/residual. Use Z4 refinement and streamed Gaussian Fourier coefficients to convert the completion into unary modifications.

EXECUTION AND EVIDENCE
No generic SAT/circuit counter, practical speed advantage, measured device or historical-priority claim. Saved finite executions are producer evidence, not an intake replay. The general replacement engine is not implemented in the phase packages.
