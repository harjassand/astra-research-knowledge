EXACT CLAIM
A shared-boundary linear elastic bridge compares repair stiffness under positive tensile drive; finite probes require joint calibration and zero drive supplies no signal. Cubic nutrient closure and observation aliases retain their separate accepted units. Delayed precursor stock can keep adding stiffness after feed stops. For a linear port with delta=delta0/(1+k*R), equilibrium spring energy is k*delta0^2/[2*(1+k*R)^2], maximized at k=1/R with delta0^2/(8*R). The potential drop delta0^2/(2*R) is available only from a specified constrained initial port state, not as free work from an already relaxed comparator. A closing conjugate force pair Fb changes readout to(delta0-R*Fb)/(1+k*R); a single-midpoint force uses a different compliance.

Quasistatic positive Hookean stiffnesses, fixed common displacement/geometry and actual force attachment. The spring bound uses R>0,k>=0 and the stated linear port. Work release additionally requires a prepared initial state and paid loading/reset path. Earlier cubic, stock, calibration and observation units retain their own assumptions; no physical valve follows from a signal equation.
