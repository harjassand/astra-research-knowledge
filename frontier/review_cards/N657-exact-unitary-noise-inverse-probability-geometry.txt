Claim (source-reported exact derivation). For an orthogonal unitary basis \(\{U_g\}_{g=1}^{d^2}\), \(\operatorname{Tr}(U_g^*U_h)=d\delta_{gh}\), and a full-support random-unitary channel \(\mathcal N_p(X)=\sum_g p_gU_gXU_g^*\), the manuscript solves its Choi objective exactly:
\[
 M(\mathcal N_p)=d^{-4}\sum_g p_g^{-1},\qquad \beta=M^{-1}=\frac{d^4}{\sum_g p_g^{-1}}.
\]
If any probability is zero, the range constraint gives \(M=\infty\) and \(\beta=0\). The algebraic identity for \(M\) is separate from N402; interpreting \(\eta=1-\beta\) as the complete contraction coefficient is conditional on N402.

For a finite projective-Weyl label group \(|G|=d^2\), generator \(L=\sum_{s\in S}\gamma_s(\operatorname{Ad}U_s-\mathrm{id})\), and fixed positive rates whose jumps generate \(G\), let \(\ell(g)\) be directed shortest word length, \(r=\max_g\ell(g)\), and \(c_g=(\ell(g)!)^{-1}\sum_{s_1\cdots s_{\ell(g)}=g}\prod_j\gamma_{s_j}\), with \(c_e=1\). The stated fixed-group asymptotic is
\[
 1-\eta(e^{tL})=\frac{d^4}{\sum_{\ell(g)=r}c_g^{-1}}t^r+O(t^{r+1}).
\]
If the jumps generate a proper subgroup, \(\eta(e^{tL})=1\) at every finite time. For one qubit with \(X,Z\) rates \(a,b>0\), the exact formula is \(1-\eta=(1-e^{-4at})(1-e^{-4bt})\). For \(n\) equal-rate qubits,
\[
\eta_n(t)=1-(1-e^{-4\gamma t})^{2n},\qquad T_n(\epsilon)=-\frac{1}{4\gamma}\log\!\left[1-(1-\epsilon)^{1/(2n)}\right].
\]
The source contrasts this exact transition with the CP-replacer Doeblin baseline; it claims a factor-two difference in the center for this family only. Weighted shortest paths and coefficients can be computed in \(O(|G||S|)\) arithmetic operations by breadth-first search and dynamic programming at fixed group presentation.

Scope: the shortest-path calculation is \(O(|G||S|)\) at fixed group presentation; \(|G|=4^n\) for \(n\) qubits, so this is not a polynomial-in-qubit-count result. The scalar tensor-product formula is exact for the stated family and does not claim a new general quantum mixing theorem.
