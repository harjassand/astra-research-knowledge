INTERFACE AND STRONGEST END-TO-END CANDIDATE
For every fixed a>0, the campaign reports a candidate using whole-vector value queries to a globally L-Lipschitz conservative field g=grad F, with F in C^{1,1}, and fixed sigma/L>0, to approximate N(E g(G), sigma^2 I_d) within total variation epsilon using C_a d^a poly_a(1/epsilon) queries on every execution and polynomial real-arithmetic/storage cost. It supplies no derivative, vector-Jacobian, potential-value or conditional-mean access. Exact-real Gaussian seeds are part of the model; a finite-bit output requires comparison to a commonly rounded target. This fixed-a bound does not imply polylogarithmic queries: constants, interpolation order and expression-tree height depend on a, and the attempted retuning incurs larger dimension-dependent cost.

NEWTON–STEIN RIGHT INVERSE — BASELINE ONLY
For symmetric matrix residual R_m in homogeneous Gaussian chaos m, the report constructs a skew-gauge right inverse to the baseline linearized Gaussian Stein operator. With Frobenius norms for matrix fields it reports dimension- and degree-independent bounds

    ||D u||_2 <= ||R||_2,       ||J_u||_2 <= (2+sqrt(2)) ||R||_2.

This exact baseline inverse leaves a quadratic residual for the full nonlinear equation. The full perturbed operator includes K D_G u, unbounded on ordinary Gaussian L2; therefore the baseline bounds do not justify a Neumann series or close the full inverse. Acquiring all actions and implementing a positive full-law Newton step with an every-execution polylogarithmic query cap remain open.

TWO-QUERY BOUNDED STEIN ACTION
Given v!=0, u=v/||v||, Z_perp~N(0,I-uu^T), R=s chi_3, the report defines

    J_s(c;v)=||v|| [g(c+s Z_perp+R u)-g(c+s Z_perp-R u)]/(2R).

It claims E[J_s(c;v)|c,v]=E[Dg(c+sZ)]v and ||J_s(c;v)||<=L||v|| on every execution. A random smoothing scale also supplies the Stein-resolvent action at two value queries. This removes an assumed derivative action but does not produce one realized symmetric matrix, its square root, or the needed Gaussian completion. The uniform–chi_3 representation has classical antecedents; originality of this particular action is unresolved.

FAILURES AND DECISIVE GAP
The frozen-source central-secant rotation fails on g_k(x)=sin(kx)/k: the report gives a nonvanishing small-time variance defect. This refutes that channel, not the exact two-query action or the sampling target. Covariance matching and an unbiased expected matrix action do not specify the full Gaussian law or a realized matrix. The generic expected-matrix-action lower bound is not a lower bound for active deterministic gradient-value queries. The decisive gate remains an acquired positive full-law construction with all adaptive centers, source noise, precision, tail, arithmetic and query costs charged. N125 blocks only its stated positive-Newton exactness route; N590 and N663 have different sampler interfaces and guarantees.
