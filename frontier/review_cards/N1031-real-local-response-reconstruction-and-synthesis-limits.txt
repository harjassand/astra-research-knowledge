EXACT CLAIM
For finite N>=2, supplied complex y,z and real symmetric nearest-neighbour H, nonzero s_j=Im(conj(y_j)*y_(j+1)) and Im<y,z>=0 determine a unique H satisfying Hy=z. Its hoppings are J_j=sum_(k<=j)Im(conj(y_k)*z_k)/s_j; the real diagonal follows row by row. This compiles one vector response at one design energy. It does not ensure a common response for several inputs, positive signal energy, a stable bath completion, uniform bandwidth or a physical gate. The source also reports failed five-mode dynamics and improved reciprocal/local numerical trials, whose governing files were not supplied.

INTERFACE AND ASSUMPTIONS
One supplied pair y,z at one design energy, N>=2 and a real symmetric tridiagonal matrix class. Check every adjacent current factor nonzero and the global imaginary inner product exactly zero. Reconstruct and verify the full Hy=z; positivity, simultaneous responses, full self-energy/source couplings and finite-band errors are separate constraints.
