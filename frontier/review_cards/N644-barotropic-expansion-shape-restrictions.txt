Claim: In a homogeneous spatially flat GR model with separately conserved barotropic components p_i=p_i(ρ_i), ρ_i+p_i≥0, and 0≤c_{s,i}^2=dp_i/dρ_i≤1, allowing a separate constant vacuum term, set x=a^-3 and Y(x)=H^2(x). The source derives Y′≥0 and 0≤xY″≤Y′. Hence Y is convex in x and concave in u=x^2. A source-reported BAO-only check found a sequence satisfying these radial shape restrictions within 0.781 marginal standard deviations of each of six radial measurements, with reported constrained-fit χ²=0.9542; it reports no compelling violation.

Does not cover interacting fluids, all scalar-field models, modified gravity, or non-flat cosmology.
Proof spine: Use x=a^-3 and separate conservation to derive xρ′=ρ+p and xρ″=c_s^2ρ′. Sum densities in the flat Friedmann equation to get Y′≥0 and 0≤xY″≤Y′; transform to u=x² for concavity. The report evaluates the finite radial constraints in inverse-square distance coordinates without assuming Gaussian errors there.
