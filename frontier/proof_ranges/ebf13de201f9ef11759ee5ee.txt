# Relative calibration, singular thresholds, and finite decision payoff

2026-10-16. Root internal derivation; not external verification or historical novelty clearance. This theorem uses the supplied faithful-output CQ radius as a mathematical premise; its algebra and faithful endpoint are reconstructed separately by the workers. It does not acquire a material or device response.

## 1. Multiplicative stability, including a checkable additive interface

For faithful normalized prepared outputs sigma_i, write H(sigma)=min_p Tr[(sum_i p_i sigma_i^-1)^-1] and C=1-H. Suppose another normalized family tau_i obeys (1-epsilon)sigma_i <= tau_i <= (1+epsilon)sigma_i for all i, with 0<=epsilon<1. Then

    (1-epsilon) H(sigma) <= H(tau) <= (1+epsilon) H(sigma),
    |C(tau)-C(sigma)| <= epsilon (1-C(sigma)).

Proof: inversion reverses the two Loewner inequalities, summation with any prior preserves them, inversion reverses again, and trace preserves order. Thus the inequalities hold pointwise in p and after taking minima. This proof does not differentiate an optimizer, assume a unique maximizing prior, or replace an operator inequality by entrywise bounds.

If sigma_i >= lambda I and ||tau_i-sigma_i||op <= delta < lambda, the premise holds with epsilon=delta/lambda. A certified candidate q is therefore guaranteed to dominate every calibrated family in that ball whenever q >= C(sigma)+(delta/lambda)(1-C(sigma)); clip the upper bound at one. Conversely q < C(sigma)-(delta/lambda)(1-C(sigma)) certifies failure for every such family. An intermediate q is UNKNOWN from this bound. This is a sufficient uncertainty propagation operation, not a confidence region: actual apparatus membership, lambda, delta and joint statistical coverage must be acquired independently. It does not preserve a fixed witness or its risk gap automatically.

## 2. Exact two-state noncommuting crossover

Let X,Z be Pauli matrices and 0<r<1, 0<theta<=pi/2. Prepare

    sigma_+ = [I+r(cos(theta) Z+sin(theta) X)]/2,
    sigma_- = [I+r(cos(theta) Z-sin(theta) X)]/2.

Unitary conjugation by Z exchanges the states. The dual objective H(p) is convex and symmetric under p->1-p, so its minimum is at p=1/2. Direct Pauli inversion gives

    C_complete = r^2 sin(theta)^2 / [1-r^2 cos(theta)^2].

The reference-free ordinary SLD contraction coefficient is instead

    C_ordinary = r^2 sin(theta)^2.

To prove the second assertion let z=2p-1, A=1-r^2 cos(theta)^2, B=r^2 sin(theta)^2. The normalized binary-input SLD ratio is B A (1-z^2)/(A-B z^2). Since A-B=1-r^2>0, it decreases in z^2 and is maximized at z=0, with value B. This follows from the full-rank qubit metric |v'|^2+(v.v')^2/(1-|v|^2) and input Fisher information 1/[p(1-p)]; the accompanying exact symbolic test reconstructs those expressions. At r=1 with theta>0, every interior-prior mixture of the two distinct pure outputs is faithful on their two-dimensional union support. The same qubit metric calculation is therefore legal there and the ratio is exactly sin(theta)^2 for every interior prior; this proves the ordinary endpoint directly. The singular complete formula, independently reconstructed by two workers but already implicit in the older N402 Choi range formulation, yields C_complete=1 there, because the two distinct pure output supports have zero intersection.

The complete-to-ordinary separation is an exact specialization of the supplied operation and established SLD machinery, not a claimed historically new formula. It exhibits why a rank-sensitive complete coefficient is not a practical benefit by itself. With r=1-eta, the denominator is (1-r^2)+r^2 sin(theta)^2. Consequently C_complete>=c is exactly equivalent to

    r^2 sin(theta)^2 >= [c/(1-c)](1-r^2),  0<c<1.

Small-angle high-order activation therefore needs depolarizing contamination small compared with angle squared. Merely nonzero distinction of pure states does not make a robust effect.

## 3. Uniform bounded-loss payoff bound, even with an arbitrary reference

Let T prepare the two states above, and T0 prepare their common average sigma_bar. Each sigma_i is trace distance d=r sin(theta)/2 from sigma_bar. For every cq input with arbitrary untouched reference, the output states of T and T0 are trace distance <=d: use block positivity and the triangle inequality on sum_i(sigma_i-sigma_bar) tensor R_i. This bound is uniform in reference dimension and input label.

For binary labels +/-1 and estimates clipped to [-1,1], squared loss is in [0,4]. Therefore every fixed receiver's risk changes by at most 4d=2r sin(theta), and so does optimal risk, by taking infima over the same receiver set. Since an erasure experiment E_q always allows the receiver to ignore its classical register and use only the unchanged reference, its optimal risk is no larger than T0's. Hence

    [MMSE(E_q)-MMSE(T)]_+ <= 2r sin(theta)

for every encoding, every q and every finite reference. The bound may be loose, but rules out a nonvanishing risk benefit as theta->0 even while the pure-output complete coefficient stays one. For a general bounded loss of range L, the same proof gives L*r*sin(theta)/2. No tomography or reference preparation is free.

No finite confidence ball in operator norm around a singular family excludes arbitrarily small depolarizing contamination: tau_i=(1-eta)sigma_i+eta I/d is faithful and converges in norm. Thus the exact rank endpoint C=1 cannot be certified from such a ball alone; one can certify a finite q margin with a positive spectral floor and adequate calibration. This is a limitation of the proposed evidence interface, not proof that an experiment is impossible.

## 4. Consequence and next decisive test

The useful operation is a certified contraction interval plus a separately certified finite decision witness and fixed-receiver risk margin. A large radius, a well-conditioned input, and numerical reproducibility do not supply statistical calibration or a useful payoff. The receiving test must acquire preparation/receiver/reference errors and confidence coverage on independent calibration records, then compare the paid finite witness to the strongest source receiver on fresh evaluation data. No physical experiment or advantage is established here.
