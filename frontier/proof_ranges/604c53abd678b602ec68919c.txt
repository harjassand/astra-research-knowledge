6. The interacting calculation and its full cost
The new operation does not require a different expensive inverse at every derivative order.
At fixed explicit dictionaries, the residual least-squares problem separates into the same two matrices used for the balanced lower and upper response problems:
\[
H_f=N^\top(S_f+A_f^\top S_e^+A_f)N,
\]
\[
H_g=S_g+A_g^\top S_o^{-1}A_g.
\]
Here \(N\) is an exact rational basis for population balance. Cross terms cancel by skew-adjointness and exact conservation.
The matrices are reused at every derivative order. Only the right-hand sides change through \(A_1\) acting on the preceding trial derivatives.
The fresh run started from all allowed words through footprint eight and used ten residual-driven enrichment stages. It ended with 53,088 lower-trial variables, 53,024 upper-trial variables and approximately 3.04 million commutator nonzeros. Four trial orders were stored.
The discovery rule is heuristic. Its correctness comes from the subsequent exact check, not from assuming its selected dictionary is adequate.

Peak memory was approximately 1.93 GiB. A later reconstruction of all eight exact packets, independent of the numerical optimizer, took another 14.72 seconds.
All response coefficients in these runs were newly calculated. The two large anchors reused the dictionary whose acquisition was charged to the central run. The benchmark uses a fixed centre and enrichment settings selected during exploratory development; that development is excluded from the timing. Existing compiler caches may also be used.
This is an instance-specific construction-and-verification cost, not a universal optimizer-runtime theorem.
7. What the matched comparison establishes
Using the identical newly computed witnesses, independent value envelopes give
\[
\sup D-D(V_0)\le0.0002573809386.
\]
The derivative-based certificate gives
\[
\sup D-D(V_0)<0.000002977476.
\]
The certified regret is therefore 86.44 times narrower without requiring correspondingly narrower absolute transport intervals.
This is a controlled comparison of what information can be extracted from the same calculation. It is not an 86-fold runtime speedup, a lower bound against value-based algorithms, or a claim that another solver could not construct better witnesses.
