## 5. The threshold is necessary

The acoustic matrix for the rank-one direction \(e_1\otimes(1,k)\) is

\[
 M_\ell(k)=\begin{pmatrix}
 12+8k^2&16k&-2\ell k&\ell(3+k^2)\\
 16k&8+12k^2&-\ell(1+3k^2)&2\ell k\\
 -2\ell k&-\ell(1+3k^2)&4+7k^2&k\\
 \ell(3+k^2)&2\ell k&k&7+4k^2
 \end{pmatrix}.
\]

For \(a_A=(a,b,c,d)^T\), direct differentiation gives

\[
 D^2F_\ell(A)[e_1\otimes(1,k),e_1\otimes(1,k)]
 =a_A^TM_\ell(k)a_A.
\]

The origin of the defining polynomial is also explicit. Put \(v_\ell=\ell^2\) and \(z=k^2/(1+k^2)^2\). Then

\[
 \frac{\det M_\ell(k)}{(1+k^2)^4}
 =3(32-v_\ell)(28-3v_\ell)
 +(504v_\ell-5952)z
 +768(v_\ell-5/2)z^2.
\]

The discriminant of this quadratic in \(z\) is

\[
 -576(48v_\ell^3-2545v_\ell^2+29712v_\ell-97344).
\]

The relevant double zero at \(\ell=\kappa\) has

\[
 z_* =\frac{248-21\kappa^2}{64\kappa^2-160}\in(0,1/4).
\]

Let \(j\in(0,1)\) be the smaller positive root of \(z_*(1+j)^2=j\), and put \(k=\sqrt j\). To supply an explicit nonzero null vector without relying on a determinant sign alone, solve

\[
 \begin{pmatrix}
 12+8j&16j&-2j\\
 16&8+12j&-(1+3j)\\
 -2\kappa^2&-\kappa^2(1+3j)&4+7j
 \end{pmatrix}
 \begin{pmatrix}\rho\\\sigma\\\chi\end{pmatrix}
 =\begin{pmatrix}-3-j\\-2\\-1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

This matrix is invertible. Its determinant is

\[
 -12[(6\kappa^2-56)j^3+(\kappa^2-4)j^2
       +(4\kappa^2-40)j+\kappa^2-32]>216,
\]

using \(8<\kappa^2<9\) and \(0<j<1\).

Define

\[
 A_* =\begin{pmatrix}\kappa\rho&k\kappa\sigma\\k\chi&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then \(M_\kappa(k)\operatorname{vec}(A_*)=0\). The first three equations are the displayed linear system; the fourth follows by reducing its numerator using

\[
 m(\kappa)=0,\qquad
 (248-21\kappa^2)(1+j)^2-(64\kappa^2-160)j=0.
\]

The verifier checks that exact polynomial reduction. Approximate values, used only for orientation, are

\[
 k\approx0.5048513584,\qquad
 A_*\approx\begin{pmatrix}-0.8778893063&0.2029501591\\-0.3572039876&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

At \(\ell=0\), \(M_0(k)\) splits into two positive-definite blocks. Their determinants are

\[
 96(k^2-1/4)^2+90>0,
 \qquad 28+64k^2+28k^4>0.
\]

Thus \(E_0=\operatorname{vec}(A_*)^TM_0(k)\operatorname{vec}(A_*)>0\). Since the acoustic expression is affine in \(\ell\) and vanishes at \(\kappa\),

\[
 \operatorname{vec}(A_*)^TM_\ell(k)\operatorname{vec}(A_*)
 =E_0(1-\ell/\kappa)<0\qquad(\ell>\kappa).
\]

Rank-one convexity fails for every \(\ell>\kappa\), and an output reflection handles \(\ell<-\kappa\). Since quasiconvexity implies rank-one convexity, this proves the necessity and completes the sharp classification.
