4. Quantitative consequence from the exact H remainder
------------------------------------------------------
The decomposition supplies a stronger coercive estimate whenever nu>0:

  integral[F(B+Dphi)-F(B)]
    >= (4nu/1875) integral|Dphi|^4.                 (A)

This holds with the source's zero-trace bounded-domain and periodic contracts.
The same constant transfers to zero-end-profile periodic strips under the
source's zero-extension/periodization argument, with no dependence on finite
strip width. This transfer uses the stated trace/gluing contract, not an
assumption that arbitrary strip boundary values vanish.

Here is the full constant calculation. For real vectors x,h in R²,

  |x+h|^4-|x|^4-4|x|^2 x.h-|h|^4/3
   =6(x.h+|h|^2/3)^2+2(x1 h2-x2 h1)^2 >=0.

For C A=(a-d,b+c), applying this to x=C B, h=C Dphi and integrating removes
the affine linear term. Hence H's Jensen gap is at least
(1/3) integral|C Dphi|^4. For f=phi1+i phi2,

  2 barpartial f=C Dphi,
  |Dphi|^2=2(|partial f|^2+|barpartial f|^2),
  partial f=Beurling(barpartial f).

Bañuelos–Janakiraman, Theorem1.1, gives the complex-valued L4 norm bound
sqrt(24), not the conjectured sharp norm3. Therefore

  ||Dphi||_4^2 <= (25/2)||C Dphi||_4^2,
  integral|Dphi|^4 <= (625/4) integral|C Dphi|^4.

Combining gives (A). The primary theorem was inspected live at
https://www.math.purdue.edu/~banuelos/Papers/Beur_Ahl.pdf . Its use here is a
standard elliptic estimate, not a claim of a new transform bound. The exact
Bregman identity and all rational constants are checked by
verify_margin_v0002.py and recorded in MARGIN_v0002_RECEIPT.json.

For the original G, alpha=3,beta=35/24,gamma=1,delta=-5/2, so

  nu=3-15/8=9/8,
  integral[G(B+Dphi)-G(B)] >= (3/1250) integral|Dphi|^4.

The previously supplied lower constant was1/2500000; the new proved lower
constant is exactly6000 times larger. This is an improvement of an analytic
lower bound, not a numerical speedup or a physical capability measurement.
The same uniform3/1250 bound holds for every original alpha3 base family
member on I=[-21/8,-5/2], because alpha_*(r)<=15/8 there.

No positive bound in (A) is asserted at nu=0. Sharp J_r is QC, extreme and
non-polyconvex, but pure-fourth-order coercivity at that boundary is a
separate open question. The positive margin also gives uniqueness of the
affine minimizer for its affine boundary values and strong gradient-L4
convergence of any minimizing sequence with vanishing excess energy. Those
are variational consequences, not a supplied storage-evaluation algorithm.
