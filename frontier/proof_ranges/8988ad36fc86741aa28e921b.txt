\section{The quadratic endpoint admits finite compression}
Write
\[
 \mathcal G_A(K)=\Tr KA^{-1}K,
 \qquad D_2(A\Vert B)=\log\Tr A^2B^{-1}.
\]
All inverses are on the effective output support. This is standard Petz
R\'enyi order two, not the sandwiched divergence or an unspecified quantum
$\chi^2$. The associated quadratic generator is $(x-1)^2$.

\begin{theorem}[Explicit finite reference cutoff]\label{thm:rld}
Let $P,Q$ be finite-dimensional channels with common input, and let $b$ be the
effective output dimension of $Q$. If complete Petz $D_2$ dominance of $P$ over
$Q$ fails on faithful pairs, it fails with a reference of dimension at most
$1+b^2$. For POVMs $E,F$, if the target $F$ has $v$ nonzero outcomes, the bound
improves to $1+v$. Hence complete $D_2$ dominance of rational complex strictly
positive POVMs is decidable by a total exact algorithm.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
First, for a faithful state $\rho$ and traceless Hermitian $H$,
\[
 \Tr[\Phi_R(\rho+tH)]^2[\Phi_R(\rho)]^{-1}
 =1+t^2\mathcal G_{\Phi_R(\rho)}(\Phi_R(H)). \tag{2}
\]
The linear term is $2t\Tr H=0$ and there is no remainder. Conversely every
faithful pair is of this form with $\rho=\sigma$, $H=\alpha-\sigma$, $t=1$.
Since logarithm is increasing, complete $D_2$ order is exactly complete
comparison of these RLD quadratic forms.

Suppose the quadratic comparison fails at a faithful input $R$ and Hermitian
traceless tangent $H$ on $\mathbb C^a\otimes\mathbb C^n$. Put
\[
 W=\Tr_a R>0,\quad
 \widetilde R=(I\otimes W^{-1/2})R(I\otimes W^{-1/2}),\quad
 h=(I\otimes W^{-1/2})H(I\otimes W^{-1/2}).
\]
Then $\widetilde R>0$, $\Tr_a\widetilde R=I_n$, and $h=h^*$.
For $\Phi=P,Q$ put $A_\Phi=\Phi_R(\widetilde R)$ and
$K_\Phi=\Phi_R(h)$. Reference congruence commutes with the channels.
Put $C=I\otimes W^{1/2}$. The exact cancellation identity
\[
 \Tr (CK_\Phi C)(CA_\Phi C)^{-1}(CK_\Phi C)
 =\Tr W\Tr_{\rm out}(K_\Phi A_\Phi^{-1}K_\Phi)
\]
shows that the original violation is $\Tr W\mathcal D<0$, where
\[
 \mathcal D=\Tr_{\rm out(P)}K_PA_P^{-1}K_P
       -\Tr_{\rm out(Q)}K_QA_Q^{-1}K_Q.
\]
Consequently some unit reference vector $\xi$ satisfies
$\langle\xi,\mathcal D\xi\rangle<0$.

Fix an orthonormal basis $(e_c)_{c=1}^b$ of the target effective output and set
\[
 z_c=A_Q^{-1}K_Q(e_c\otimes\xi).
\]
Let $S$ span $\xi$ and every reference component of these $b$ vectors. Each has
$b$ reference components, so $d=\dim S\le1+b^2$. Choose an isometry
$V:\mathbb C^d\longrightarrow\mathbb C^n$ onto $S$, and compress the entire
physical baseline and tangent:
\[
 \widetilde R'=(I_a\otimes V^*)\widetilde R(I_a\otimes V),\qquad
 h'=(I_a\otimes V^*)h(I_a\otimes V).
\]
This preserves faithfulness, Hermiticity, and $\Tr_a\widetilde R'=I_d$.
For either output, $A_\Phi'$ and $K_\Phi'$ are the corresponding common
reference compressions. Write $\xi'=V^*\xi$.

Every $z_c$ lies in $\mathbb C^b\otimes S$. Therefore
\[
 A_Q'(I_b\otimes V^*)z_c
 =(I_b\otimes V^*)K_Q(e_c\otimes\xi)
 =K_Q'(e_c\otimes\xi').
\]
Thus the compressed target inverse problem has exactly the compressed solution,
and each target scalar quadratic term is preserved. For a source basis vector,
use
\[
 \langle u,A^{-1}u\rangle
 =\sup_z\{2\operatorname{Re}\langle u,z\rangle-\langle z,Az\rangle\}.
 \tag{3}
\]
Compression restricts this supremum to the source output tensored with $S$,
so each source term can only decrease. Summing the partial-trace basis terms
gives $\langle\xi',\mathcal D'\xi'\rangle<0$.

For POVMs the output matrices are block diagonal. A target inverse solution for
outcome $j$ occupies only its own classical block and has the one reference
component $z_j=(r_j^F)^{-1}k_j^F\xi$. Hence
$S=\operatorname{span}\{\xi,z_1,\ldots,z_v\}$ suffices, and $d\le1+v$.

To restore a physical scalar witness, choose sufficiently small $\eta>0$ and
the faithful reference state
\[
 W_\eta=(1-\eta)|\xi'\rangle\langle\xi'|+\eta I_d/d.
\]
Then $\Tr W_\eta\mathcal D'<0$. Undo the congruence:
\[
 R'=(I_a\otimes W_\eta^{1/2})\widetilde R'(I_a\otimes W_\eta^{1/2}),\qquad
 H_0'=(I_a\otimes W_\eta^{1/2})h'(I_a\otimes W_\eta^{1/2}).
\]
The matrix $R'$ is faithful and has trace one. Set
$H'=H_0'-(\Tr H_0')R'$. This is traceless and preserves the difference, since
\[
 \mathcal G_A(K-cA)=\mathcal G_A(K)-2c\Tr K+c^2\Tr A
\]
and both channels preserve trace. For small nonzero $t$, $R'+tH'$ is also a
faithful state; (2) gives the required finite-reference state-pair violation.

Finally fix a reference dimension $d\le1+v$. Existence of a violation is a
first-order real-algebraic sentence in the real and imaginary coordinates of
$R'>0$, $H'=H'^*$, $\Tr R'=1$, $\Tr H'=0$, and output inverse variables.
Introduce $T_iM_i(R')=I_d$ for each output block. Strict positivity of the
baseline and nonzero effects ensure the inverses exist. Negativity of the
source-minus-target quadratic form is a polynomial inequality. Rational
complex effects give rational real coefficients. Real quantifier elimination
decides the sentence. Testing the finitely many dimensions $1,\ldots,1+v$
therefore decides complete order. No efficiency claim is made.
\end{proof}

The theorem is independent of the nonlocal-game reduction. It needs no
condition-number promise, no free Positivstellensatz, and no substitution of
arbitrary operator tuples for physical reference states. The mathematical bound
also holds for nonzero, possibly singular POVM effects because a faithful
physical-reference input makes each nonzero measurement block invertible.
The effective-support convention handles general channels.
