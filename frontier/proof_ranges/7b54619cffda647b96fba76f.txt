1. The physical problem has not been simplified
The Hamiltonian and physical dephasing remain
\[
H(V)=
-\frac t2\sum_i(X_iX_{i+1}+Y_iY_{i+1})
+\frac U4\sum_iZ_iZ_{i+1}
+\frac V4\sum_iZ_iZ_{i+2},
\]
\[
\mathcal L_V O
=
i[H(V),O]
+\frac d4\sum_i(Z_iOZ_i-O).
\]
Both diagonal interactions are retained. The calculation does not expand around weak interaction or asymptotically dominant dephasing.
The output is the same infinite-temperature density-response second-moment coefficient,
\[
D(V)=
\lim_{T\to\infty}
\frac{1}{2T\chi}
\sum_xx^2
\left\langle
e^{T\mathcal L_V}(n_x-\tfrac12),n_0-\tfrac12
\right\rangle,
\qquad \chi=\frac14.
\]
This includes all particle-number sectors of the product-trace ensemble. It is not the auxiliary Hodge energy from the previous run, a finite-ring substitute, or a modified-noise transport coefficient.
