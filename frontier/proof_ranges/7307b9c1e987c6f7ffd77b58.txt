1. STRONGEST END-TO-END SAMPLER PROOF CANDIDATE

File: r16_integrable_mean_mechanism.txt, frozen version 1.

For every FIXED a>0, globally L-Lipschitz conservative g=grad F, F C^{1,1},
and fixed sigma/L>0, the internally reconstructed construction targets
N(Eg(G),sigma^2 I_d) to TV epsilon with

  C_a d^a poly_a(1/epsilon)

whole-vector g-value queries on EVERY execution, and polynomial total real
arithmetic/storage. The written proof states sigma>=L; the fixed-ratio
extension uses a constant number of independent larger-noise calls and their
average, as in R20 section4. Convexity is unnecessary. No derivative, VJP,
potential-value, conditional-mean or hidden-basis oracle is supplied.

Mechanism: a symmetric Gaussian Stein resolvent gives an exactly invariant
skew flow; dimension-free one-slot derivative bounds control its finite
mean-value circuit; uncached Gaussian center-noise absorption recursively
eliminates every temporary exact mean; root replication restores the desired
noise. Root reconstructed the load-bearing proof. This is an internal proof
candidate extending/specializing the pinned OpenAI construction, not an
externally checked theorem or a new general subpolynomial sampling claim.

It does NOT imply polylogarithmic queries. Its constants, interpolation orders
and expression-tree height depend on fixed a. Substituting a=1/log d is
unjustified. R21's quantitative analysis of the present fixed-depth retuning
leaves an exp(c sqrt(log(d/epsilon))) type cost on that route, rather than the
requested polylog bound. That is a route limitation, not a problem lower bound.

The exact-real model includes Gaussian seeds. Finite-bit output must compare
to a commonly rounded target: a discrete output has TV one from an unrounded
continuous Gaussian. Unknown bit lengths of arbitrary oracle reals are not
claimed to be bounded.
