Catalyst removal and a positive-rate obstruction
The channel-level lead yields a structural result beyond transferring undecidability:
An LOCC device whose memory loses no relative entropy of entanglement on a maximally mixed input implements exactly the same channel when that memory is replaced by one separable state.
The replacement can be chosen independently of the device. For stationary devices, the same replacement reproduces their entire multi-time input–output process.

Pushing this further gives an asymptotic consequence:
\[
\boxed{
E_{\mathrm{seq}}^{\mathrm{cat}}(\mathcal N)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal N\in\overline{\mathrm{LOCC}}.
}
\]
Here \(E_{\mathrm{seq}}^{\mathrm{cat}}\) is the entanglement cost of sequential, universally usable channel simulation, allowing arbitrary finite-dimensional catalysts that are returned exactly. Their dimensions may grow without any prescribed bound.
Thus, exact catalysts cannot turn a genuinely non-LOCC channel into a zero-rate operation. Without additional uncharged catalysts, a positive, channel-dependent entanglement-rate threshold admits an exponential strong converse. Even with arbitrary exactly returned catalysts, sublinear supplied entanglement forces simulation error to approach one.
The proofs follow. Historical originality and independent verification remain outstanding. The entropy machinery is established prior mathematics; the proposed contribution is retaining an operationally realizable substitute throughout the argument, rather than concluding only that an output state is unentangled.
1. Exact removal from a single resource-neutral input
All systems below are finite-dimensional. LOCC means local quantum operations and classical communication, with arbitrary finite protocols and classical randomization. No bound is imposed on the number of communication rounds.
Let Alice and Bob possess an auxiliary memory
\[
C=C_A C_B
\]
in an arbitrary state \(\tau\). Consider an LOCC channel
\[
\Lambda:AB\,C_A C_B\longrightarrow A'B'\,C_A'C_B'.
\]
Its effective system channel, for memory initialization \(\xi\), is
\[
\mathcal N_\xi(X)
=
\operatorname{tr}_{C'}\Lambda(X\otimes\xi).
\]
Write
\[
E_R(\xi)
=
\min_{\gamma\in\operatorname{SEP}}
D(\xi\Vert\gamma),
\]
using natural logarithms. The minimum exists: the separable-state set is compact, relative entropy is lower semicontinuous, and a full-rank separable comparator makes the minimum finite.
Set
\[
\eta_{AB}
=
\frac{I_A}{d_A}\otimes\frac{I_B}{d_B},
\qquad
\tau_\eta'
=
\operatorname{tr}_{A'B'}\Lambda(\eta_{AB}\otimes\tau).
\]
Theorem 1 — Protocol-preserving replacement
Choose any closest separable state
\[
\gamma_*\in\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{\gamma\in\operatorname{SEP}}
D(\tau\Vert\gamma).
\]
If
\[
E_R(\tau_\eta')=E_R(\tau),
\tag{1}
\]
then
\[
\boxed{\mathcal N_\tau=\mathcal N_{\gamma_*}.}
\tag{2}
\]
Consequently, \(\mathcal N_\tau\) is itself LOCC.
In particular, when \(C'=C\),
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{tr}_{A'B'}\Lambda(\eta_{AB}\otimes\tau)=\tau
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mathcal N_\tau\in\mathrm{LOCC}.
}
\tag{3}
\]
This requires return only on one maximally mixed product input, not on every input. The memory may be mixed or rank-deficient, and it may become correlated with the system output.
The conclusion is stronger than membership in the closure of LOCC: a separable initialization of the same device implements the channel exactly.
The information inequality behind the proof
Let \(\mathcal F\) be a compact convex set of states containing a full-rank state, and put
\[
R_{\mathcal F}(\tau)=\min_{\gamma\in\mathcal F}D(\tau\Vert\gamma).
\]
Fix a minimizer \(\gamma_*\). Suppose an instrument \(\{\mathcal I_y\}\) sends every free state, conditionally on each nonzero-probability outcome, to a free state.
Write
\[
\mathcal I_y(\tau)=p_y\tau_y,
\qquad
\mathcal I_y(\gamma_*)=q_y\gamma_y.
\]
Data processing for the instrument with its outcome recorded gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mathcal F}(\tau)
&\ge
D(p\Vert q)+\sum_y p_yD(\tau_y\Vert\gamma_y)\\
&\ge
D(p\Vert q)+\sum_y p_yR_{\mathcal F'}(\tau_y)\\
&\ge
D(p\Vert q)+
R_{\mathcal F'}\!\left(\sum_y p_y\tau_y\right).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]
The last step is convexity. Therefore
\[
\boxed{
D(p\Vert q)
\le
R_{\mathcal F}(\tau)
-
R_{\mathcal F'}(\bar\tau').
}
\tag{5}
\]
This is the entropy-disintegration mechanism underlying Piani’s restricted-measurement results, not a new entropy inequality. The important feature here is that the initial comparator \(\gamma_*\) remains fixed. arXiv
Proof of Theorem 1
Choose a finite product-state ensemble
\[
\{r_x,\rho_x^{AB}\}
\]
whose states span the Hermitian operators on \(AB\), with every \(r_x>0\), and whose average is \(\eta_{AB}\).
Such an ensemble is explicit: locally, take computational-basis states together with the plus/minus real and imaginary superpositions of every pair of basis vectors, uniformly weighted; then tensor the two local ensembles.
Choose a product informationally complete POVM \(\{M_y\}\) on \(A'B'\). Preparing the ensemble, applying \(\Lambda\), measuring the output and retaining the memory defines the instrument
\[
\mathcal I_{x,y}(\xi)
=
r_x\,\operatorname{tr}_{A'B'}
\!\left[
(M_y\otimes I_{C'})
\Lambda(\rho_x\otimes\xi)
\right].
\tag{6}
\]
For a separable memory initialization, each conditional memory output is separable: every operation in this experiment is LOCC. The average actual memory output is precisely \(\tau_\eta'\).
Applying (5) and (1) gives
\[
D(p\Vert q)=0,
\]
where
\[
p_{x,y}
=
r_x\operatorname{tr}\!\left[M_y\mathcal N_\tau(\rho_x)\right],
\qquad
q_{x,y}
=
r_x\operatorname{tr}\!\left[M_y\mathcal N_{\gamma_*}(\rho_x)\right].
\]
Hence \(p_{x,y}=q_{x,y}\) for every \(x,y\). Informational completeness of the measurement gives
\[
\mathcal N_\tau(\rho_x)=\mathcal N_{\gamma_*}(\rho_x).
\]
The input states span the Hermitian operators, so the channels coincide.
Finally, \(\gamma_*\) has a finite convex decomposition into product states. Alice and Bob can prepare it using shared randomness and local preparations, then run \(\Lambda\). Thus \(\mathcal N_{\gamma_*}\) is LOCC. ∎
The operational distinction is essential: the proof does not merely find a separable state with suitable output statistics. It finds a separable input to the original protocol, thereby retaining the original operational restriction.
2. A compositional principle, not just a one-use statement
The same substitute works at every time horizon
Suppose \(C'=C\), and define the memory evolution under a maximally mixed system input by
\[
\Phi(\xi)
=
\operatorname{tr}_{A'B'}\Lambda(\eta_{AB}\otimes\xi).
\]
Assume
\[
\Phi(\tau)=\tau.
\tag{7}
\]
For every \(n\), compose the device \(n\) times, retaining its memory between calls. Under the product maximally mixed input, the final memory is
\[
\Phi^n(\tau)=\tau.
\]
Theorem 1 applied to this complete \(n\)-use device gives
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal P_n^\tau=\mathcal P_n^{\gamma_*}
\qquad\text{for every }n.
}
\tag{8}
\]
Here \(\mathcal P_n^\xi\) is the full \(n\)-slot input–output process initialized with memory \(\xi\).
The state \(\gamma_*\) is the same for every \(n\). Equality of the full process maps implies equality under external adaptive interventions, not merely equality of separate one-use marginals.
One can also choose a stationary separable substitute. Since \(\Phi\) preserves separability and fixes \(\tau\),
\[
D(\tau\Vert\Phi^j(\gamma_*))
\le D(\tau\Vert\gamma_*)=E_R(\tau).
\]
Thus every \(\Phi^j(\gamma_*)\) is another minimizer. A convergent subsequence of the Cesàro averages
\[
\frac1N\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}\Phi^j(\gamma_*)
\]
has a separable limit \(\gamma_\infty\) satisfying
\[
\Phi(\gamma_\infty)=\gamma_\infty,
\qquad
D(\tau\Vert\gamma_\infty)=E_R(\tau).
\]
It therefore reproduces every process in (8).
This does not identify the internal memory states. It identifies their complete externally accessible process while the memory remains internal.
The underlying condition is free tomography
The argument applies beyond entanglement whenever the free-state sets are compact and convex with full-rank members, conditional free instruments preserve those sets, and freely implementable preparations and measurements suffice for process tomography.
Under those conditions:
\[
\boxed{
\text{No loss of relative-entropy resource on a tomographic average input}
\ \Longrightarrow\
\text{exact replacement by a free initialization}.
}
\tag{9}
\]
For example, consider randomized stabilizer circuits: Clifford gates, stabilizer preparations, Pauli measurements, discarding and classical feed-forward. Take
\[
\mathcal F_C
=
\operatorname{conv}\{\text{pure stabilizer states on }C\}.
\]
Product Pauli eigenstates supply a spanning input ensemble averaging to the maximally mixed state; Pauli measurements supply output tomography; their conditional actions preserve the stabilizer polytope.
Therefore, an exactly returned magic-state catalyst cannot enable a channel outside this randomized stabilizer-operation class. The same circuit with one mixed stabilizer initialization implements the channel.
This addresses a precise published question: the January 7, 2026 version of Son and coauthors’ paper explicitly leaves robust catalysis under LOCC and under stabilizer operations unresolved. The statement here applies to those finite-dimensional operational classes, with the convex classical-randomization convention stated above. arXiv
