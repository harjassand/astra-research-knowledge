The first two assessments were informed by current work on depth-four identity testing and entropy inequalities under Boolean restrictions. 

An important correction during the investigation was that ordinary Komlós discrepancy was not an appropriate new target: September 2026 preprints already contain constant-discrepancy existence proofs and a polynomial-time spectral signing construction. I am not claiming those results. 

The exact-feasibility question is different. Recent matroid-discrepancy work gives, among other results, polynomial-time weighted rounding with additive error proportional to column sparsity. The proposed theorem instead gives a square-root sparsity bound and exact marginals, but does not yet provide an efficient algorithm.
