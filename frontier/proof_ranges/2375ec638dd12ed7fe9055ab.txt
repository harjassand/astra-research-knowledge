## 9. What has and has not been established

**Established internally:** moment matching in the stated laminate model; electrostatic matching in the ideal dielectric model; surviving optical contrast in a sourced-but-approximate reference; its sensitivity to damping; a harmonic covariance limit; a quantitative experiment and acquisition gate.

**Experimentally established elsewhere:** constituent isotope heterostructuring and polariton measurements; isotope mechanical differences; an hBN/organic-superconductor response motivating the task; competing cavity-control methods.

**Not established:** fabrication of this balanced pair, actual matching of its interfaces, the target's response, lower total cost than the best alternative for a consequential output, exceptional technological benefit or historical novelty.

The precise next missing scientific measurement is the dark-equilibrium target response to a spectrally qualified, nuisance-bounded pair. The optical proof does not manufacture that response.
