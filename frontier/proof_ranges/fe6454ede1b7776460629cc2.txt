I. EXACT FINITE-N STATIONARY MIXTURE
----------------------------------
Let tau_N(B)=2^(-N) Tr B and X_N=J/sqrt N. Let beta_N=log(1+1/nu_N),
b_N=beta_N sqrt N. The inherited stationary-sector formula implies exactly
  2^N rho_N = exp(-beta_N J_z) h_beta_N(J^2),
  h_beta(j)=(2j+1)/Z_j(beta),
  Z_j(beta)=sinh[(2j+1)beta/2]/sinh(beta/2).
Euler's product (NIST DLMF 4.36.1) gives
  u/sinh u = product_{n>=1} (1+u^2/(pi^2 n^2))^(-1)
           = E exp(-S u^2).
Because (2j+1)^2/4=j(j+1)+1/4, functional calculus gives the EXACT identity
  2^N rho_N = C_beta integral exp[-s beta_N^2/4]
                       exp[-s beta_N^2 J^2-beta_N J_z] mu(ds),
  C_beta=sinh(beta_N/2)/(beta_N/2).
Define
  H_N(A,b)=exp[-A J^2/N-b J_z/sqrt N],
  Z_N(A,b)=tau_N H_N(A,b),
  eta_N(A,b)=H_N(A,b)/(2^N Z_N(A,b)).
Then rho_N is exactly the positive mixture of eta_N(s b_N^2,b_N) with
  W_N(ds)=C_beta exp[-s beta_N^2/4] Z_N(s b_N^2,b_N) mu(ds).
W_N is a probability measure by taking traces of the exact identity.

Under tau_N, j/sqrt N has Maxwell density
  f_r(r)=8 sqrt(2/pi) r^2 exp(-2r^2), r>0,
and m/sqrt N conditional on r becomes Uniform[-r,r]. Therefore
  Z_N(A,b) -> integral_{R^3} G(x) exp[-A|x|^2-bz] dx
            = K(A,b),
  G(x)=(2/pi)^(3/2) exp(-2|x|^2),
  K(A,b)=(1+A/2)^(-3/2) exp[b^2/(8+4A)].
This Riemann-sum limit follows from the inherited Maxwell local CLT, or
directly from binomial multiplicities. For all s>=0, uniformly when b_N bounded,
  Z_N(s b_N^2,b_N) <= tau_N exp[-b_N Jz/sqrt N]
                         = cosh[b_N/(2sqrt N)]^N <= exp(b_N^2/8).
The central radial exponential commutes with Jz and is <=I, so this domination
is valid. Thus W_N -> K_b(s)mu(ds) in L1 of densities. In particular this
limiting density integrates to1. Moving thresholds 1/b_N^2 -> c^2 are harmless
since mu is continuous (it is Exp(pi^2) convolved with an independent law).

The critical collective density is exactly
  F_b(x)=G(x) exp(-bz) (b|x|/sinh(b|x|)).
Its radial law stays exactly G's Maxwell law, while angular directions tilt
toward -z. Its positive Gaussian mixture consists of
  V_s=(4+2s b^2)^(-1) I,
  m_s=-b(4+2s b^2)^(-1) e_z,
with weights K_b(s)mu(ds). This scalar interpretation is useful, but the proof
below actually constructs finite-N separable states and lifts in trace norm.

II. EXPLICIT FINITE-N FULLY SEPARABLE ENSEMBLE FOR 0<A<1
------------------------------------------------------
Let R_1,...,R_N be iid uniformly distributed unit vectors on S^2, and
  U_N=N^(-1/2) sum_i R_i,
  omega(R_i)=(I+R_i dot sigma)/2 (a pure qubit state).
For A in (0,1) and real b define
  alpha_A=9A/[4(1-A)],
  h_A=-3b/[2(1-A)],
  p_A(u)=exp[-alpha_A|u|^2+h_A u_z],
  sigma_N(A,b)= E[p_A(U_N) tensor_i omega(R_i)] / E[p_A(U_N)].
This is an exactly positive, normalized FULLY SEPARABLE state for every N.
The classical weighting correlates the otherwise independent Bloch vectors;
it does not introduce entangling preparation operations. Its permutation and
axial invariance follow from those of the measure and the weight.

LEMMA (trace-norm lift).
For any fixed A in (0,1), b real,
  D(sigma_N(A,b),eta_N(A,b)) -> 0.
The same holds for A_N->A and b_N->b within this open parameter range.

Proof. Equip operators with normalized tracial L2 norm
  ||T||_{2,tau}=(tau_N T* T)^(1/2).
Use Fourier convention p(u)=(2pi)^(-3) integral p_hat(k) exp(i k dot u) dk.
The Gaussian p has integrable Fourier transform. Its unnormalized relative
operator is
  T_N[p]=E[p(U_N) tensor_i(I+R_i dot sigma)]
        =(2pi)^(-3) integral p_hat(k) B(k/sqrt N)^(tensor N) dk,
where for t=|v|,
  B(v)=sinc(t) I+i d(t) v_hat dot sigma,
  d(t)=(sin t-t cos t)/t^2,
with the continuous extension at0. The formula follows by integrating the
uniform sphere; the vector integral is the derivative of sinc.
B is the image of a modulus-one classical function under the unital positive
POVM integration f->E[f(R)(I+R dot sigma)], so ||B(v)||op<=1. Explicitly its
two eigenvalues are sinc(t) +/- i d(t).
For fixed k,
  B(k/sqrt N)^(tensor N)
   = [sinc(t)^2+d(t)^2]^(N/2)
       exp[i 2 theta(t) k_hat dot J],
  theta(t)=arg(sinc(t)+i d(t))=t/3+O(t^3),
  [sinc(t)^2+d(t)^2]^(N/2)->exp(-|k|^2/9).
Since ||k_hat dot J||_{2,tau}=sqrt N/2, the O(N^(-3/2)) error in 2theta
causes only O(N^(-1)) error in tracial L2 for each fixed k. Hence
  ||B(k/sqrt N)^(tensor N)
       -exp(-|k|^2/9) exp[i(2/3)k dot X_N]||_{2,tau}->0.
The uniform operator norm bound and integrable p_hat permit dominated Fourier
integration. Therefore T_N[p] is tracial-L2-close to the Weyl quantization
  Q_N(q)=(2pi)^(-3) integral q_hat(l) exp(i l dot X_N) dl,
  q(x)=E_{Z~Normal(0,2I/9)} p((2/3)x+Z).
A Gaussian integral with the stated alpha,h gives exactly
  q(x)=C'(A,b) exp[-A|x|^2-bz],
  C'(A,b)=(1-A)^(3/2) exp[b^2/(4(1-A))].

It remains to prove Q_N(q) is tracial-L2-close to C'H_N(A,b), rather than
assuming weak collective convergence proves trace-norm convergence.
The following THREE normalized traces close that gap.

(1) tau_N[H_N(A,b)^2] -> integral G(x) exp[-2A|x|^2-2bz] dx.
This is the Maxwell j/m Riemann sum. H_N is uniformly bounded in operator
norm by exp(b^2/(4A)), since m>=-j and j(j+1)>=j^2.

(2) For every fixed k,l,
  tau_N[exp(i k dot X_N) exp(i l dot X_N)] -> exp[-|k+l|^2/8].
Indeed the trace is the Nth power of the one-qubit normalized trace
  cos(|k|/(2sqrt N)) cos(|l|/(2sqrt N))
    -(k_hat dot l_hat) sin(|k|/(2sqrt N)) sin(|l|/(2sqrt N)),
whose expansion is 1-|k+l|^2/(8N)+O(N^-2).
Fourier domination then gives tau_N[Q_N(q)^2]->integral G q^2.

(3) For every fixed k,
  tau_N[H_N(A,b) exp(i k dot X_N)]
       -> integral G(x) exp[-A|x|^2-bz] exp(i k dot x) dx.
To verify this directly, in the spin-j block use the character of the
SL(2,C) fundamental matrix
  exp[-b sigma_z/(2sqrt N)] exp[i k dot sigma/(2sqrt N)].
If its eigenvalues are exp(+/-eta_N/2), then
  N eta_N^2 -> b^2-|k|^2-2i b k_z.
For j/sqrt N->r its character divided by2j+1 tends to
  sinh[r sqrt(b^2-|k|^2-2i b k_z)] /
          [r sqrt(b^2-|k|^2-2i b k_z)].
The entire analytic extension at zero is understood, so no branch choice
affects the expression. This equals the spherical average of
exp[(-b e_z+i k) dot r u]. Multiplying the central exp[-Aj(j+1)/N]
and averaging the Maxwell j weights gives exactly the asserted integral.
For domination, the absolute block trace divided by2j+1 is <=exp(|b|j/sqrt N),
and after the radial factor it is <=exp(b^2/(4A)), independent of j,N.
This also permits integrating against the Gaussian q_hat to obtain the
cross trace tau_N[H_N Q_N(q)]->integral G exp[-A|x|^2-bz]q(x).

Since q=C' exp[-A|x|^2-bz], (1)-(3) imply
  ||Q_N(q)-C'H_N(A,b)||_{2,tau}^2 -> 0.
The scalar normalizers satisfy E p(U_N)->integral G q=C'K(A,b) by the
ordinary bounded-continuous CLT (p is bounded for alpha>0), and
Z_N(A,b)->K(A,b)>0. Thus
  ||2^N sigma_N(A,b)-H_N(A,b)/Z_N(A,b)||_{2,tau}->0.
Finally tau_N|T|<=||T||_{2,tau} proves the claimed full trace-norm convergence.
The argument is locally uniform in fixed compact subsets of 0<A<1 and
bounded b; therefore converging parameter sequences have the same limit.
QED.

III. EXPLICIT SEPARABLE APPROXIMANT TO THE WHOLE STATIONARY STATE
---------------------------------------------------------------
For each N use the exact probability weight W_N from I and define
  Sigma_N = integral_{0<s<1/b_N^2} sigma_N(s b_N^2,b_N) W_N(ds)
          + W_N([1/b_N^2,infinity)) I/2^N.
This is a manifestly normalized FULLSEP state. The s=0 point has mu mass0.
For s in the good interval, the lemma applies pointwise as N->infinity;
all distances are bounded by1. W_N has a fixed integrable domination given
in I. Moving thresholds converge, and the single threshold has mu mass0.
Convexity of trace distance and dominated convergence imply
  limsup D(rho_N,Sigma_N)
       <= integral_{s>=c^2} K_{1/c}(s)mu(ds)=delta(c).
This is a concrete finite-N preparation ensemble, not solely a moment match.

IV. EXPONENTIAL BOUND AND LARGE-c ASYMPTOTIC
-------------------------------------------
K_b(s) decreases strictly with s. At s=c^2,
  K_{1/c}(c^2)=(2/3)^(3/2) exp[1/(12c^2)].
Write S=E_1+R, R=sum_{n>=2}E_n independent. The exact telescoping product
  E exp(pi^2 R)=product_{n>=2}(1-1/n^2)^(-1)=2
gives P(S>=t)<=2 exp(-pi^2 t) by conditioning on R (the conditional
probability is <=exp[-pi^2(t-R)] even when R>=t). Thus
  delta(c)<=2(2/3)^(3/2) exp[-pi^2 c^2+1/(12c^2)].
The upper bound can be clipped at1 because delta is a probability.
Moreover P(S>=t)~2exp(-pi^2t), since R has exponentially faster tails.
The residual excess S-c^2 conditional on exceeding c^2 stays O(1), whereas
K_{1/c}(c^2+u)->(2/3)^(3/2) for fixed u as c->infinity. Splitting bounded
u and tail u, or using the exponential density expansion, gives
  delta(c)~2(2/3)^(3/2) exp(-pi^2c^2).

V. FAILED ROUTE AND EXACT REMAINING GATES
----------------------------------------
An attempted construction using only mixtures of IDENTICAL weakly polarized
product qubits fails: their limiting F/G ratio is a positive mixture of
exponentials of affine functions and is log-convex. The target log ratio
  -bz+log(br/sinh br)
has strictly negative radial curvature for b>0. This excludes that restricted
construction only. Non-identical pure products have covariance below I/4;
the exact ensemble in II exploits this distinction.
