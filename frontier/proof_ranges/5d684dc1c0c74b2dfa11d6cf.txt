## 6. Evidence limits, primary overlap and next decisive theorem

Calderón's binary SOS theorem, semidefinite strong duality, null Lagrangians, and the heat/exact-form strategy are prior machinery. The [Bandeira–Pedregal author preprint](https://matematicas.uclm.es/omeva/wp-content/preprints/preprint10-08.pdf), section 3 and Theorem 2, already formulates quartic quasiconvexity at zero through Fourier coefficient optimization. The [Guerra–Teixeira da Costa paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.09063) already studies plane-wave searches and low-wave rigidity. The searches here did not establish historical priority for the reflection classification, explicit F* certificate, open neighborhood, or transfer separator. No comprehensive literature clearance was performed.

The early sampled acoustic LP failed held-out rank-one testing and remains rejected. The 24 later local Fourier searches supplied no lower bound; their source log is the last evaluated iterate before the saved final update. The new positive certificate removes the need to continue those searches for F*. No optimizer failure is converted into an impossibility theorem.

The higher-leverage next target is a cone inclusion. Let C_R={p:∃K skew, Q8(p,K)≥0}. Let C_H be the cone of coefficients admitting γ≥0 and a globally closed homogeneous-quadratic ω such that E_(F_p−γq²)+ω≥0 on every compatible jet. An inclusion C_R⊆C_H would settle the whole nine-dimensional class by the analytic proof above. Pure compatible jets show the reverse inclusion C_H⊆C_R. This is a sufficient-certificate classification target; quasiconvexity might hold by other methods even if this particular inclusion fails.

The computable inner cone C_H,SOS further requires a positive semidefinite bilinear Gram on the 24 monomials. F* and the explicit cube lie in this inner cone. A proof C_R⊆C_H,SOS would suffice. An exact separating rank-one-convex input would refute only this identity-diffusion SOS certificate class; it would not refute quasiconvexity, the unrestricted pointwise positivity cone C_H, or N740's broader constant-anisotropic-diffusion gate. A failed numerical SOS search establishes none of these separations.

The source's independent moment-completion target remains valid: every compatible gradient's nine Jensen moments must admit the stated positive 8-by-8 completion if the entire class is quasiconvex. Neither the current example certificate nor its small open neighborhood proves that universal theorem.
