# Local operation derivations and route-specific falsifiers

Author: gd_physical_operation. Derived 16 October 2026. These are mathematical statements about declared ideal operations. None is an experimental result or an externally reviewed theorem about the supplied memory.

The supplied structured compiler is used with its original conventions: D[A](rho) = A rho A† - {A†A,rho}/2, h in radians per second with hbar = 1, and the unhalved diamond norm. The sources, including all generator changes, remain read-only.

## 1. The exact two-Kraus pair channel has an ordinary coherent dilation

Let S1 and S2 be commuting Hermitian Pauli checks. Let P_ab be their joint spectral projectors, with bit 1 denoting eigenvalue -1. Suppose a Hermitian Pauli Q anticommutes with both checks. Write Pi = P_11. The intended completed channel is

    C(rho) = Q Pi rho Pi Q + (I-Pi) rho (I-Pi).

Prepare two syndrome work ancillas a,b and a flag f in 000. Coherently extract the two check bits into a,b, compute f = a AND b with a Toffoli, and invert the syndrome-extraction circuit before changing the data. Then apply controlled-Q from f to the data and discard/reset f.

On an arbitrary data vector the resulting isometry is

    V|psi> = (I-Pi)|psi> |000> + Q Pi|psi> |001>.

This follows by decomposing |psi> into the four P_ab eigenspaces. The work ancillas return to 00 on every branch. Tracing the flag gives exactly C, including cross-syndrome coherences. No measurement of the two individual check bits is needed.

For the supplied 4D pair checks each stabilizer has weight six and their union has eleven data qubits. A direct CNOT construction uses 12 two-qubit gates for the two forward parity extractions and 12 for their inverses. A conventional six-CNOT Toffoli plus one controlled one-qubit Pauli gives 31 two-qubit gates, three ancillas, basis rotations and one flag reset. This is an explicit conservative construction under all-to-all required connectivity, not a gate-count lower bound. Routing, native-gate conversion, leakage protection and fault tolerance are additional costs. No 11-qubit realization with this complete error budget was acquired.

The order of uncomputation matters: extracting the full syndrome, changing the data, and simply resetting the syndrome registers is a different channel.

## 2. Measuring the two checks separately gives a distinct, usable refinement

If both check bits are irreversibly measured, with Q applied only on outcome 11, the channel is

    C_fine(rho) = Q P_11 rho P_11 Q + sum_(ab != 11) P_ab rho P_ab.

Define the joint check-dephasing channel

    E_S(rho) = sum_ab P_ab rho P_ab
             = (rho + S1 rho S1 + S2 rho S2 + S1 S2 rho S2 S1)/4.

Because Q maps P_11 into P_00 and leaves the other branches' update rule unchanged,

    C_fine = E_S composed with C = C composed with E_S.

One verifies this by inserting P_ab on both sides of the input. Both maps have the same definite-syndrome classical transition. They differ on coherences between inactive joint eigenspaces.

An exact witness uses three data qubits, S1 = Z1 Z2, S2 = Z2 Z3, Q = X2, and

    |psi> = (|000> + |001>)/sqrt(2).

Both components are inactive. C preserves |psi><psi|. C_fine removes its 000/001 off-diagonal entries. Their output difference has eigenvalues +1/2 and -1/2, so its unhalved trace norm is 1. Thus

    ||C - C_fine||_diamond >= 1.

Correct syndrome populations alone therefore do not demonstrate the stated two-Kraus channel.

A conditional transfer lemma is available for the finer channel. If the supplied location-only envelope really controls every Pauli history and the fixed decoder corrects its entire good history span, replace each ideal pair channel by C_fine at the same clock locations. For any definite-syndrome Pauli summand each P_ab is either scalar zero or scalar one; the surviving summand takes exactly the same legal pair action. The same good schedule then makes every recovered Kraus operator scalar on the full logical code. Conditional trace preservation gives the identity logical channel. This is a direct local extension of the quantum-span argument, conditional on all original global probability, topology and decoder hypotheses. It neither verifies those hypotheses nor transfers robustness to the active uncompleted generator. The modified generator must be named separately.

## 3. Every data face lies in 22 completed pair supports

In one CSS sector each face is incident to four edge checks. An edge participates in three outgoing direction-pair predicates at its starting vertex. This gives 12 edge-check incidences. Exactly one pair predicate uses both positive edges at the minimal corner of this face, giving one duplicate. Consequently eleven distinct pair-channel supports contain a given face in this sector. The dual sector contributes eleven more.

This agrees with double counting: there are 12 L^4 streams, each on 11 data qubits, and 6 L^4 data qubits, hence average incidence 22; translation and coordinate permutation symmetry make this incidence uniform. The accompanying enumeration independently checks the primal count for L=4.

This counts operation support, not just the face written by a successful correction. Null proposals still acquire/reset syndrome information.

## 4. Finite-duration independent-start circuits incur a scheduling cost

This lemma concerns a direct implementation in which every pair stream starts a full gadget according to its own rate-kappa Poisson clock, and a gadget occupies its constituent data qubits for a fixed duration tau. It is not a universal limitation on simultaneous continuous reservoir engineering.

Select one data qubit in a tagged gadget's support. At least 21 other correction streams touch that qubit. Conditional on the tagged independent clock mark, their future starts remain Poisson with total rate 21 kappa. The probability that another one starts during the tagged duration is therefore

    p_collision >= 1 - exp(-21 kappa tau).

This is a lower bound from only one constituent; considering all eleven data qubits can increase it. Past-start overlaps and floor/control events add further contention. Requiring this lower bound to be at most 0.01 implies

    kappa <= -log(0.99)/(21 tau).

For tau = 1,5,10,100 microseconds the ceilings are 478.6,95.72,47.86,4.786 per second per pair stream. The expected offered occupation of a data qubit is 22 kappa tau. Rejecting, queuing, synchronizing or merging conflicting starts changes the clock/channel model and requires a new proof or a charged approximation.

The 1% number is an illustrative scheduling tolerance, not the supplied memory threshold. Likewise 1 microsecond is a hypothetical entire-gadget duration, not a measured 11-qubit gadget. The proof supplies no numerical fault threshold with which to accept any of these rates.

At a published mean T1 of 68 microseconds, an amplitude-damping-only illustration has rate 14,706/s. Adding the optimal reset-compiling Z floor of kappa_AD/4 would give proposal rate kappa_AD, already 30.7 times the 1-microsecond illustrative direct-circuit ceiling. This exposes a poor route under those deliberately stated simplifications. The T1 alone does not acquire an AD-only model, and the calculation is not a memory impossibility proof.

## 5. A matched floor changes the physical channel; compilation alone is not suppression

For one involution P and real h, the declared generator is

    L = gamma D[P] - i h[P,.], gamma > 0.

It has the exact proposal rate and unitary

    lambda = gamma + h^2/gamma,
    U = (sqrt(gamma) P + i h I/sqrt(gamma))/sqrt(lambda),
    L = lambda(Ad_U - Id).

Multiplying out U rho U† proves the identity; U†U=I. For unknown |h| <= u, minimizing the worst-case rate gives gamma=u and lambda <= 2u. The installed floor damps coherences between the two P eigenspaces by exp(-2 gamma t). The proposal representation may depend on the unknown h mathematically. The hardware only needs the actual floor and actual error generator; it need not implement an unknown h-dependent U at physical proposal times.

If residual detuning is bounded by Delta f in Hz and H=(Delta omega/2)Z, then u=pi Delta f. A 100 Hz hypothetical bound therefore requires gamma=314.2/s and costs up to 628.3/s per data qubit in this modewise compiler. Six such data modes owned by one fine cell cost up to 3,769.9/s. These numbers are algebra, not acquired detuning bounds.

For constant unknown h, an equally informed incumbent can use an ideal echo. Choose R with R P R = -P. A midpoint R and final frame restoration cancel the static Hamiltonian exactly:

    R exp(-i h P t/2) R exp(-i h P t/2) = I.

If the same gamma floor is present, D[P] is invariant under this toggling and echo leaves its dephasing. Without installing the extra floor, the ideal echo has no such extra dephasing. Finite pulse duration, pulse errors and the noise spectrum must be paid in a physical comparison; an echo is not asserted to eliminate white Markov noise or arbitrary time-dependent bath couplings. For two pulses of width tau_p, the uncompensated drift during them has the elementary norm budget at most 4 |h| tau_p, plus their actual control errors.

For amplitude damping, direct evaluation on matrix units gives

    kappa_AD D[sigma_minus] + (kappa_AD/4)D[Z]
      = kappa_AD (R0 - Id),

where R0(rho)=|0><0| Tr rho. The added floor turns T2=2T1 damping into T2=T1 reset dynamics. It enables this exact finite-rate representation by destroying extra coherence; it does not make the unencoded qubit a better memory.

## 6. Ordinary quantum trajectories retain a continuous no-jump operation

For a Lindblad generator the infinitesimal instrument has

    K0 = I - (iH + sum_j L_j†L_j/2) dt,
    Kj = sqrt(dt) L_j.

The no-jump map is trace-nonincreasing and its probability generally depends on the input state. Continuous deterministic evolution and random jumps reproduce the master equation in the dt limit. This established method is supported by Dalibard, Castin and Molmer (1992), pp. 580-582.

The structured compiler's stronger demand is a finite-rate input-independent CPTP proposal schedule with no residual continuous drift. Failure of that stronger representation for pure Hamiltonians or amplitude damping is not an inability to implement, simulate or analyze those physical processes. Ordinary trajectories alone do not supply the common schedule needed by the supplied quantum-memory proof.

A system-only floor also does not regularize arbitrary continuous system-bath coupling. For a joint Hamiltonian h Z_S tensor X_B with only gamma D[Z_S tensor I_B], the Hamiltonian direction lies outside the single noise direction. The supplied Choi range criterion fails for every finite rate. A physical scalar-detuning model must therefore justify its bath reduction; merely observing dephasing is insufficient.

## 7. A quantified remainder does not silently inherit the all-time result

Suppose an actual finite-system CPTP generator L_actual differs from a modeled CPTP generator L_model by a bounded superoperator R. Duhamel's formula and diamond contractivity give

    ||exp(T L_actual)-exp(T L_model)||_diamond <= T ||R||_diamond.

If R is a sum of N local residuals, each with bound delta, the available elementary bound is T N delta. Encoding and decoding cannot increase it. For N proportional to L^4, a fixed unmodeled local remainder does not yield the claimed exponential-time estimate through this bound. That is a missing transfer argument, not a proof that the actual memory fails.

A measured sparse fit must therefore include a quantitative remainder and its time validity, or an independently justified full physical catalog. Finite tomography cannot certify exact absent directions for all later times.

## 8. High-fidelity coherent gates are not automatically rare CPTP faults

Let U be any unitary not proportional to I. There is no decomposition

    Ad_U = (1-p) Id + p N, with N CPTP and p < 1.

Its normalized Choi matrix is rank one on |U>. If such a decomposition existed, positivity of p J(N) = |U><U| - (1-p)|I><I| would require |I> to lie in span{|U>}; otherwise choose a vector orthogonal to |U> but not to |I>, giving a negative expectation. This contradicts the assumption on U.

The conclusion holds even for a unitary arbitrarily close to I. Gate infidelity or a small diamond distance can support other error analyses, but does not by itself supply the source's rare-proposal mixture. Actual randomization, a proved structured generator remainder with paid floors, or a different noisy-circuit theorem is needed. Physically twirling errors also changes the experiment and must include the twirling gates and frame/measurement adaptations.
