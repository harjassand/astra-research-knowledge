## 7. A limited extension beyond symmetry

This is a stability corollary, not a solution for general quartics.

Write an arbitrary homogeneous quartic perturbation in the four matrix entries as \(P(A)=\sum_{|\beta|=4}p_\beta A^\beta\), with \(\|p\|_1=\sum|p_\beta|\). Direct differentiation of a degree-four monomial gives

\[
 |D^2P(A)[B,B]|\le12\|p\|_1|A|^2|B|^2.
\]

Let \(\eta_*=(1-(29/10)/\kappa)/2=3c_*\). Since

\[
 D^2N(A)[B,B]=8(A\!:\!B)^2+4|A|^2|B|^2,
\]

\(\eta_*N+P\) is convex whenever \(\|p\|_1\le\eta_*/3=c_*\). The already established quasiconvexity of \(F_*-\eta_*N\) then gives quasiconvexity of \(F_*+P\). No rotational symmetry of \(P\) is needed.

Moreover, \((F_*+P)(I)\le-3/10+c_*<0\), so the same scaling-and-minor argument proves non-polyconvexity. Thus the proof also yields an explicit neighborhood of non-polyconvex quasiconvex quartics in all 35 coefficient directions. It does not classify the full quartic cone.
