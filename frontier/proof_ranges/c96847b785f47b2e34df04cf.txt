## A concrete replacement for collar reset

Fresh uniform Pauli randomization on ell collar qubits gives, after averaging
the current key, I/d tensor the outside marginal for any correlated state.
The same conditional-history probability bound therefore applies to pooled
outcomes. Comparison/evolution after the randomization must be fixed independently
of the current key. Earlier key/outcome dependence is allowed. Selecting only
current-key-specific counts is invalid, as the critic's exact counterexample
demonstrates. The label preparation remains required.

The depolarizing identity is established mathematics; see the original
[private quantum channels paper](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~rdewolf/publ/qc/AMTW00.pdf).
The application here substitutes2ell fresh random bits and calibrated local
gates per trial for an ideal dissipative collar replacement. Gate duration,
correlations during pulses, randomness, label preparation, controlled-U,
interrogation, readout and entropy disposal remain physical costs. No joule
advantage or available device gate fidelity follows from the identity.
