  r_1=3|<J>|^2/[j(j+1)],
exactly 0<=r_1<=9/5. The coherent seed attains 9/5; the extreme cat
attains 0. Thus for all w_1,w_2,w_3>=0,
  k=w_2+max{(9/5)w_1+(1/5)w_3, 2w_3}.              (16)

V. EXACT SPIN-5/2 FAILURE OF THE TWO-ORBIT LIFT; ACTUAL SUPPORT REPAIR

This section falsifies only the proposal that the same coherent/cat
mixture works in all spin dimensions. It does not falsify G1/G2.

Take j=5/2, d=6, total T=5/2, and BC vector
  xi=(|L=3>+|L=5>)/sqrt(2).
Both BC labels have positive output-swap parity, so (5) constructs an
exact legal broadcaster. Its full marginal spectrum is
  lambda_1=3/5,
  lambda_2=31/100+5sqrt(77)/588,
  lambda_3=31/105+25sqrt(77)/882,
  lambda_4=1/7+13sqrt(77)/294,
  lambda_5=109/924+13sqrt(77)/396.
All are positive, so this witness also obeys the positive-HS subclass.
The rank-four star block on BC [3,5] is
  [[33/14,39sqrt(77)/49],[39sqrt(77)/49,39/14]].
Its expectation on xi is 18/7+39sqrt(77)/49.

Both coherent and extreme-cat seeds have the same even-rank profile:
  r_2=25/14, r_4=3/14,
hence every mixture has canonical lambda_4=1/42. C2 domination on rank
four would require star <=9+3/14=129/14. Instead the exact margin is
  18/7+39sqrt(77)/49-129/14
    =39sqrt(77)/49-93/14>0.                           (17)
For example sqrt(77)>17/2 proves (17) immediately. The corresponding
channel lambda_4 is approximately 0.530866, whereas the maximal-output
cloner has lambda_4=13/84; componentwise domination by that cloner is
therefore also exactly false. Even its lambda_1 is 3/5, while maximal-
output lambda_1=6/7, so the profile tradeoff matters.

Unrestricted support is larger. By (13), r_2+r_4=2 for every pure state,
so r_4<=2. The explicitly normalized seed
  |eta>=(2|5/2,5/2>+sqrt(5)|5/2,-1/2>)/3
has all quadrupole expectations zero: its populated magnetic labels
differ by 3, eliminating rank-two off-diagonal expectations, and
<J_z^2>= (4/9)(25/4)+(5/9)(1/4)=35/12=j(j+1)/3.
Thus r_2=0, r_4=2. Its orbit proves exactly
  k(P_4)=2, V(P_4)=9.                               (18)
The witness in (17) has ratio 14/[18-(18/7+39sqrt(77)/49)]<2. An explicit
full-domain repair is Psi=(Psi_coh+Psi_eta)/2. Eta's harmonic covariance
profile r_l is
  (r_1,r_2,r_3,r_4,r_5)=(5/21,0,40/27,2,242/189),
so its canonical eigenvalues are
  (5/63,0,40/189,2/9,22/189).
The half-mixture with coherent eigenvalues
  (5/7,5/14,5/42,1/42,1/462)
dominates 2lambda_l-1 in every sector (sqrt(77)<9 suffices to verify

VI. PRECISE REMAINING ARBITRARY-SU(2)-CHANNEL GATE

For a fixed j define the actual compact orbit-profile hull
  K_j=conv{(r_1(psi),...,r_{2j}(psi)):||psi||=1}.
All SU(2)-averaged canonical comparators have eigenvalues
  (r_1/3,r_2/5,...,r_{2j}/(4j+1)), r in K_j.
The exact full-domain C2 question for a legal covariant channel profile
lambda is whether there exists ONE r in K_j with
  r_l>=(2l+1)(2lambda_l-1) for every l.               (19)
This follows either directly by covariance domination or from support
separation. Covariant joint Choi profiles are exactly generated by PSD
multiplicity blocks in (4)-(6), with both output parities available.
The spin-3/2 proof completely characterizes K_j. The spin-5/2 witness
proves that replacing K_j by coherent/cat hull is invalid, while (18)
shows why rejecting a restricted hull cannot be promoted to a falsifier.

An additional exact algebraic profile reduction is available for every j.
Let q_L=||P_L(psi tensor psi)||^2. Only L with (-1)^{2j-L}=+1 occur, and
sum q_L=1. Equations (3) give
  r_l=d(2l+1) sum_{L=symmetric} {j j l;j j L} q_L.    (20)
Thus the orbit-profile hull is an affine image of the convex hull of
attainable symmetric-pair spin probabilities, not the whole probability
simplex. At spin 5/2, q has labels 1,3,5, so K_j lies in an affine plane.
The exact coefficient table is j_five_halves_fierz_table.json; negative
coefficients in that table rule out treating every probability vector as
a realizable pure seed. Characterizing the true plane region or proving
(19) by another construction is the first remaining substantive gate.
No all-j finite-C theorem or exact >2 witness has been established here.
