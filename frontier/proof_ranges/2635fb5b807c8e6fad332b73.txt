4. A decisive counterexample to the weaker assumption
The distinction between bounded overlap and bounded weighted strength is necessary.
Fix \(\beta,h,\lambda>0\). On \(N+1\) spins, consider
\[
H_N
=
-h\sum_{i=0}^{N}Z_i
-\frac{\lambda}{N}Z_0\sum_{j=1}^{N}Z_j.
\]
Every interaction is at most two-local. Every displayed Hamiltonian coefficient is nonzero. The total incident interaction strength is bounded by \(h+\lambda\), independently of \(N\).
Set
\[
t=\tanh(\beta h),
\qquad
A_N=
\frac1N\sum_{j=1}^{N}Z_0Z_j-tZ_0.
\]
Its coefficient norm satisfies
\[
\|u^{(N)}\|_2^2=t^2+\frac1N,
\]
which stays bounded away from zero.
Conditioned on \(Z_0=s\), the leaves are independent with mean
\[
t_s=\tanh\!\left(\beta\left(h+\frac{\lambda s}{N}\right)\right),
\qquad
|t_s-t|\le\frac{\beta\lambda}{N}.
\]
Since \(H_N\) and \(A_N\) commute,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Cov}^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\rho_N}(A_N,A_N)
&=\operatorname{Var}_{\rho_N}(A_N)\\
&\le
\frac1N+\frac{(\beta\lambda)^2}{N^2}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus
\[
\boxed{
\frac{\operatorname{Cov}^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\rho_N}(A_N,A_N)}
{\|u^{(N)}\|_2^2}
\longrightarrow0.
}
\tag{13}
\]
The obstruction is already classical: an average over many leaves becomes nearly deterministic and cancels a local term.
The center participates in \(N\) pair interactions. Therefore this disproves the desired inequality under bounded locality and bounded weighted interaction strength alone, while leaving the bounded-incidence theorem intact.
