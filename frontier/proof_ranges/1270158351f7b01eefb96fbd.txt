8. What failed when extending the mechanism
I pursued different operations rather than stopping when the simplest domain failed.
Onsite quartic confinement alone does not control generic imaginary neighbour interactions. At a proposed boundary \(y_i=r_i\), set \(x_i=0\) and send a neighbouring \(x_j\) to either infinity. The imaginary gradient contains a term proportional to
\[
-C_{ij}x_j.
\]
No finite rectangular imaginary tube can remain invariant.
The paired-quartic construction supplies the missing quadratic control of \(x_j\). That closes the certificate for the stated family—but only because those interactions are actual model terms.
Quadratic whitening transfers rather than removes the obstruction. In the scalar action
\[
S(z)=\frac12(m-ic)z^2+\frac g4z^4,
\]
the substitution
\[
z=(m-ic)^{-1/2}w
\]
makes the quadratic coefficient real but replaces the quartic coefficient by
\[
\frac{g}{(m-ic)^2}.
\]
Its imaginary cubic drift generally points outward in one real direction. A fixed strip can fail again.
A curved tube is not automatically a repair. With unchanged horizontal Brownian noise, a boundary with a nonzero real normal component can be crossed by the noise. A broader construction must change the noise geometry and account for the corresponding drift corrections—not merely propose a better-shaped region.
These failures identify the missing operation more precisely than “find a better sampler.”
9. The exact remaining scientific-computation problem
The mathematical object worth retaining is:
\[
\boxed{
\text{acquired confinement and contraction}
\;+\;
\text{a real positive anchor}
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\text{correct complex observables}.
}
\]
The next decisive theorem would acquire a same-answer stochastic geometry for conventional onsite-quartic fields with generic imaginary neighbour couplings, without requiring the paired quartics and without supplying an expensive transport map as an oracle.
A precise candidate is a holomorphic noise kernel
\[
K_\zeta=B_\zeta B_\zeta^\top
\]
that is real positive definite on the anchor interval. Its construction must include the divergence correction to the drift, avoid inaccessible poles, preserve a domain compatible with the diffusion directions, and yield quantitative mixing, moment, and numerical-error bounds. Finite diagnostics or a learned kernel would not establish those properties.
Two additional obligations remain inseparable from that theorem: the error bounds must be useful at actual computational budgets, and a matched nonfactorising benchmark must show an advantage over specialised contour, tensor, interpolation, or stochastic methods.
Completion assessment: this run preserves a proof candidate for selecting the correct complex integral, an acquired multivariate coefficient certificate, a finite-resource reduction, and explicit failures of several extensions. It does not establish a broadly applicable or competitively affordable new scientific capability. The unresolved obstruction is now the acquisition of a correct-cycle stochastic geometry whose guarantees survive both realistic models and realistic numerical budgets.
