## Scope against N794, N806 and N809

- **N794 / left-SO(2), nine coefficients:** the explicit (8\times8) matrix
  above is in exactly this dual basis and closes the completion for the
  modulation family. It is not a completion theorem for all compatible
  gradients. N794's full nine-moment compatibility gate remains open.
- **Full35 quartics:** the global modulation identity itself applies without
  symmetry and the closed-cone polar argument extends to 35 coefficients.
  The explicit small dual matrix above does not extend by this derivation,
  because N794's (8\times8) SOS interface uses left-SO(2) reduction.
- **N806/N809 local results:** N806 handles fixed-contact cubic/cross-Hessian
  control in the nine-coefficient class; N809 upgrades all-background
  stability to full35 at simple rank-three transverse contacts, for
  determinant-balanced laws relatively close in (L^2/L^4) to a fixed
  rank-one direction. Rank-two tangent contacts, large-transverse profiles,
  and global Morrey closure remain open. Those local hypotheses do not cover
  the connected-triad profile above.
- **Affine optimization:** the flux identity and its (Y_B) hold separately
  for every (B), so its conclusion survives complete affine minimization.
  This is stronger than a fixed-background evaluation. It does not permit
  independently optimizing spatial packets without the exact cross terms.
