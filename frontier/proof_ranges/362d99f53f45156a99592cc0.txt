## Stronger orientation comparator and frontier coverage

[Nishio et al., JAC2024, DOI10.1016/j.jallcom.2023.172992](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jallcom.2023.172992) already reports uniaxially oriented NITE islands on LaAlO3(110), reduction of crystallographic variants during nitride growth/annealing, S=.74, Ku~.63MJ/m3 and Hc319kA/m at300K. This is the closest obtained orientation-control comparator. The publisher abstract and indexed primary DENSO technical-report excerpts were inspected; the complete manuscript/technical PDF and raw data were not acquired. The319kA/m film result exceeds the183kA/m scale of the2024 powder benchmark, so that powder scale must not be used as a universal current FeNi coercivity ceiling. This does not transfer film properties, density or energy product to a bulk specimen.

The narrower question remains whether directional loading during nitrogen-mediated transformation can change a retained chemical-axis distribution in fixed, non-epitaxial material, and whether another cycle rewrites it. The present search found no demonstration of that complete operation. This is not exhaustive historical novelty certification. The2026 IEEE bulk-realization item in the supplied provenance was not acquired as a full manuscript, so no global current record is asserted.

## What changes the experimental decision

1. **Reject the old readout as sufficient.** A complete superlattice pole figure generally gives V*S^2 weighting. The exact counterexample in `MEASUREMENT_DESIGN.md` holds phase volume and total superlattice signal fixed while selective ordering of fixed axes produces an apparent write contrast6/5. Greater signal precision cannot cure this identifiability failure.
2. **Use a gate the existing instruments can test.** Registered multi-energy reciprocal-space/pole maps can provide a chemically separated order-weighted tensor if acquired nuisance responses are linearly distinguishable, the integration model is validated and angular missing mass is bounded. Existing resonant-XRD experiments establish availability of the measurement family, not successful acquisition on the proposed stress apparatus.
3. **Upgrade the tensor honestly.** For volume-weighted axes, acquire orientation-resolved order or calibrated W, phase volume f and a valid uniform lower order bound s. The root-supplied matrix sandwich has been independently proved and audited in `MATRIX_SANDWICH_AUDIT.md`. Aggregate S values do not supply s. Even a certified volume-tensor change still needs grain/population tracking to distinguish reorientation from replacement, growth or loss.
4. **Run the matched causal comparison before hardware scale-up.** Orthogonal stress during NITE must beat chemistry without load, final-metal loading and physically comparable direct-stress/field annealing. Loading into an isolated island is unknown: substrate bending does not itself establish grain stress, and the original platform was chosen partly to relieve reaction strain. A whole-film easy-axis change may be shape, residual stress, phase fraction or grain motion.

## Costs and unresolved interfaces

The acquisition contract requires furnace time and gas throughflow, load-transfer calibration at reaction temperature, oxygen/nitrogen phase checks, beamtime over several energies and angular settings, reference specimens, instrument response calibration, registration of the same illuminated material, and post-cycle damage/mass-balance assessment. The spectral condition number, missing angular mass, actual photon background and orientation-dependent S are unmeasured. Therefore no end-to-end exposure, experiment price, scalable writing rate, energy budget or manufacturing yield is justified. Published throughflow is not net reagent consumption; theoretical density is not achieved density; local order is not a complete useful magnet.
