## Explicit dual completion from the flux identity

Use the N794 basis

\[
f=(u^2,v^2,uv,w^2,uw,vw,qu,qv,qw),
\quad
u=|A_{\cdot1}|^2,\ v=|A_{\cdot2}|^2,\ w=A_{\cdot1}\cdot A_{\cdot2},\ q=\det A.
\]
For a periodic modulation \(\varphi=rV(k\cdot x)\), an affine background
\(B\), and normalized torus average, let

\[
m_i(B,\varphi)=\mathbb E f_i(B+D\varphi)-f_i(B).
\]
Write \(W=V'\), \(P=rW\otimes k\), and \(Q=V\otimes\nabla r\). The
global identity gives

\[
m\cdot p=
\mathbb E\,\mathcal R_F(B+P,Q)
+\mathbb E\,a(k\cdot x)r^2\int_0^1t\,
D^2F(B+tP)[V\otimes k,V\otimes k]dt,
\]
where \(F=\sum_i p_i f_i\). For a quartic,

\[
\mathcal R_F(C,Q)=\int_0^1(1-s)D^2F(C+sQ)[Q,Q]ds.
\]

For each point, rotate output coordinates by \(U\in SO(2)\) with
\(Ue_1=V/|V|\), and set \(x(A)=\operatorname{vec}(U^TA)\) in N794's
row-major order. For a spatial vector \(b=(b_1,b_2)\), set

\[
z(A,b)=\begin{pmatrix}b_1x(A)\\b_2x(A)\end{pmatrix}\in\mathbb R^8.
\]

If \(Q_F(K)\) is the N794 acoustic Gram matrix for any skew completion
\(K\), its defining identity is

\[
D^2F(A)[V\otimes b,V\otimes b]
=|V|^2 z(A,b)^TQ_F(K)z(A,b).
\]

Consequently the following matrix is an explicit completion:

\[
\begin{aligned}
Y_B={}&\mathbb E\int_0^1(1-s)|V|^2\,
 z(B+P+sQ,\nabla r)z(B+P+sQ,\nabla r)^Tds\\
&+\mathbb E\,a(k\cdot x)r^2\int_0^1t|V|^2\,
 z(B+tP,k)z(B+tP,k)^Tdt.
\end{aligned}
\]

The weights are nonnegative, so \(Y_B\succeq0\). Each outer product has
off-diagonal block \(b_1b_2xx^T\), which is symmetric. Therefore it
annihilates all six skew-block constraints \(\langle C_j,Y_B\rangle=0\).
Expanding \(Q_F(K)=\sum_i p_iB_i+\sum_j k_jC_j\) and comparing the
coefficients of the arbitrary \(p_i,k_j\) gives

\[
\langle B_i,Y_B\rangle=m_i(B,\varphi),\qquad
\langle C_j,Y_B\rangle=0.
\]

Thus the N794 dual completion is explicit for this family, rather than only
existential by finite SDP duality. The formula is compatible with every
affine background. For each fixed quartic \(F_p\), if its complete affine
quadratic has a minimizer \(B_*(p)\), then \(Y_{B_*(p)}\) certifies the moment
vector at that quartic's optimizer and its pairing equals the optimized gap.
The optimizer can depend on \(p\), so these are background-indexed
completions, not one common background selected for every coefficient vector.
Nonnegativity for all \(B\) makes each quadratic bounded below; its Hessian
is positive semidefinite for compatible gradients, so its minimum is
attained (with the usual pseudoinverse when singular).

An exact check uses \(r=1+\tfrac12\cos x\), \(V=(\cos y,\sin y)\),
\(k=e_2\), and \(B=0\). Its moment vector and one completion are

\[
m=\left(\frac3{128},\frac{227}{128},\frac{17}{128},0,0,0,0,0,0\right),
\qquad
Y=\operatorname{diag}\left(\frac1{512},0,0,\frac{17}{256},0,0,0,\frac{227}{512}\right).
\]

The nine exact pairings \(\langle B_i,Y\rangle\) equal the displayed \(m_i\);
all six skew constraints vanish. The check is in
[`verify_global_moment_bridge.py`](verify_global_moment_bridge.py), with
machine-readable output in
[`verification_results.json`](verification_results.json).
