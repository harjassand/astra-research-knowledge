## 14. Explicit rational compiler for the finite-input upper bound

The existential 16(T+1)-state net is not needed as an inaccessible oracle. There is a slightly larger O(T)-state rational construction for (4), with an elementary, albeit inefficient, polynomial-time compiler.

Let n=ceil(sqrt(2(T+1))), and for integers -n<=a,b<=n use both signs of

\[
 u_{a,b}=\frac{(2an,\ 2bn,\ n^2-a^2-b^2)}{n^2+a^2+b^2}.
 \tag{24}
\]

These are rational unit vectors. There are at most N=2(2n+1)^2 of them, including duplicates. The stereographic map

\[
 (s,t)\longmapsto\frac{(2s,2t,1-s^2-t^2)}{1+s^2+t^2}
\]

has differential operator norm 2/(1+s^2+t^2)<=2. Every point of the upper hemisphere has inverse coordinates (x/(1+z),y/(1+z)) in [-1,1]^2. A nearest grid point differs in parameter norm by at most sqrt(2)/(2n), hence on the sphere by at most sqrt(2)/n. The negative chart covers the lower hemisphere. Thus the code is a chord sqrt(2)/n-net.

Set alpha=1-1/n^2 and w_i=u_i/alpha. The proof of Section 8 gives B^3 subset conv{w_i} and alpha^(T+1)>=1-(T+1)/n^2>=1/2. Therefore exact compensated readout at delta=1/3 is valid through T.

All chart coordinates, alpha, gates, and the six preparations are rational with O(log(T+2)) bit length. Every required barycentric problem has four linear equality constraints (three coordinates and total mass). A point in a 3D convex hull can be represented with at most four vertices: if a representation uses more, an affine dependence allows the coefficients to be shifted until one vanishes, without violating nonnegativity. Repeating proves the claim. An affinely independent support can be extended to four vertices with zero extra coefficients because the full polytope is three-dimensional.

Consequently a compiler may enumerate all four-vertex bases and solve their 4-by-4 rational systems, accepting the first nonnegative solution. There are O(N) target points for each of five gates, and O(N^4) bases per target: O(N^5) constant-size rational systems suffice, plus preparation systems. Fixed-dimension determinant formulas show the transition weights have O(log(T+2)) bit length, with constants independent of T. Each row has at most four nonzero entries. This yields a polynomial compilation bound and an O(T log(T+2))-bit sparse-kernel description, not a claim of a competitive compiler.

The time-dependent compensated readout coefficient alpha^(-t) can have O(T log(T+2)) bits. Evaluating and sampling those rationals incurs additional arithmetic and randomness costs, neither of which is bounded by the m-state register count. For arbitrary continuously supplied x,u, their input precision and encoding costs remain separate; the fully finite six-preparation/single-readout experiment avoids that input-precision issue.

Finite rational mesh and sampled rational barycentric identities are checked by verify_rational_mesh.py. These are finite checks of the formulas, not an independent proof of the asymptotic estimates.
