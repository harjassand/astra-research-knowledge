Main conclusions
----------------
1. The proposed cause-conditioned clock and covariance formulas are correct,
   with the support and initial-law qualifications below.
2. Tracing commutes with Doob conditioning. Using the unconditional pGT clock
   generally does not preserve conditioned means. A common mean clock exists
   exactly when the feasible-cause clocks agree on their overlapping supports.
3. Cause-specific conditional moment certificates yield rigorous joint
   cause/deadline enclosures. They can be verified with rational uniformization
   rather than uncertified floating-point matrix exponentials.
4. Exact conditional state-retention marginals are available. Weighted sums of
   conditional clock errors are supermodular; the worst-cause maximum is not.
   Saturated per-cause variance quotas give a valid submodular-cover alternative.
5. General signed-reward covariance is valid, but signed-reward variance can
   increase when more states are retained. Even for two nonnegative reward
   coordinates, covariance need not improve in positive-semidefinite order.
6. Conditioning does not repair the rare-tail obstruction when a rare trap and
   an ordinary route terminate at the same scientific outcome. Relative-tail
   protection remains absent.

1. Definitions and support
--------------------------
Q is a finite row generator. B is an absorbing target set, partitioned into
disjoint causes B_a. Every transient state reaches B almost surely. Let U=E\B
and A=Q_UU. The initial row distribution mu is supported on retained states R;
R contains B. I=U\R is discarded, and S=R\B is transient retained.

The full expected occupation row is eta_U=mu_U(-A)^(-1). It is independent of
the choice of R. Write

  G=(-Q_II)^(-1),   K=Q_RR+Q_RI G Q_IR.

For each cause a, c^a_x=P_x(hit B_a) is the full committor, with target boundary
values 1 on B_a and 0 on other targets. Equivalently

  c^a_U=(-A)^(-1) q_a,       q_a=Q_U,B_a 1.

Let p_a=mu c^a. Conditioning is defined only for p_a>0; statewise formulas
containing 1/c^a_r apply only where c^a_r>0. The conditioned initial law is

  mu^a_r=mu_r c^a_r/p_a,

not generally mu. The conditioned occupation is

  eta^a_i=eta_i c^a_i/p_a.

This follows by conditioning the future cause at each occupied state. It also
follows directly from the Doob Green matrix. Zero-committor states are removed
from the conditioned chain. Formulas using the original G and zero entries of
c^a remain valid without forming 0/0 expressions.

2. Trace/conditioning commutation and clocks
--------------------------------------------
On positive-committor states, Q^a=C^(-1)QC, where C=diag(c^a). The transient
diagonal entries are unchanged. On the discarded positive support,

  G^a=C_I^(-1) G C_I.

Consequently its trace is

  K^a=C_R^(-1) K C_R.

This is exactly the Doob transform of the unconditioned trace. In contrast, the
mean correction computed after conditioning is

  alpha^a_r=1+(Q_RI G c^a_I)_r/c^a_r.

The unconditional correction is alpha_r=1+(Q_RI G1)_r. Thus these two procedures
differ: conditioning the existing unconditionally clocked model yields
diag(alpha)^(-1)K^a, while mean-correcting the conditioned trace yields

  Qhat^a=diag(alpha^a)^(-1)K^a.

The committor itself is unchanged by any positive diagonal time change.

COMMON-CLOCK THEOREM. A positive retained clock d preserves the conditional
mean hitting time for every feasible retained starting state and every cause
if and only if d_r=alpha^a_r for every pair (r,a) with c^a_r>0. Hence a common
clock exists iff alpha^a and alpha^b agree wherever both causes are feasible.
There is no condition to impose for an impossible cause at a state.

Proof. The full conditional mean on S solves

  (-K^a_SS)t^a=alpha^a_S.

The trace with clock d solves the same invertible system with right side d_S.
Equality of the entire mean vector is equivalent to equality of right sides.
An assertion only about one specified mixed initial law is weaker and does not
give this pointwise necessity.

Since the causes exhaust absorption, sum_a c^a_r=1 and

  alpha_r=sum_a c^a_r alpha^a_r.

The ordinary mean clock is the statewise committor-weighted average. It can
therefore preserve the unconditional mean while badly missing conditional ones.

EXACT COUNTEREXAMPLE. States are r,iB,A,B. From r, rates to A and iB are 999/1000
and 1/1000; from iB, rate to B is 1/10. Retain r,A,B. Then

  alpha_r=101/100,  alpha^A_r=1,  alpha^B_r=11.

True conditional means are 1 and 11. Both conditional means of the ordinary
clocked reduction are 101/100. Trace branching probabilities remain exact.
There is no common clock that fixes both means.

A mixture that first draws cause a with probability p_a, then uses mu^a and
Qhat^a, preserves the cause probabilities and all conditional means. As a
single Markov representation this uses cause-labelled retained states, up to
|S| times the number of causes; its size and committor preprocessing are costs.
It is a standard conditioning construction, not a free universal |S|-state model.

3. Conditional stopped-clock and reward covariance
--------------------------------------------------
Under the natural trace coupling for the conditioned chain,

  That^a=integral_0^T 1_R(X_t) alpha^a(X_t) dt.

The stopped martingale construction from the supplied source applies to Q^a.
It gives zero conditional mean error and

  V_a(R)=E[(T-That^a)^2 | cause a]
        =(2/p_a) sum_{i in I} eta_i (G c^a_I)_i.

In particular W2(L(T|a), L(That^a|a)) <= sqrt(V_a). The displayed coupling
need not be optimal for W2; a large V_a does not prove a poor reduced law.

For a signed state reward f, let A_f=integral_0^T f(X_t)dt. Its conditioned
reduced reward, per reduced physical time, is

  fhat^a_r=[f_r+(Q_RI G(c^a_I .* f_I))_r/c^a_r]/alpha^a_r.

Here .* denotes componentwise multiplication. For rewards f,g, the residual
errors are conditionally centered and have covariance

  C^a_fg=(1/p_a) sum_i eta_i [f_i(G(c^a_I .* g_I))_i
                                  +g_i(G(c^a_I .* f_I))_i].

Proof. Apply the source identity to Q^a. Its return reward is
C_I^(-1)G(C_I f_I), and eta^a=eta C_I/p_a, so the committor factors cancel as
displayed. The complete covariance matrix is positive semidefinite, even when
off-diagonal entries and rewards have either sign.

SIGNED-REWARD SELECTION FAILURE. In the unit-rate chain r->i1->i2->B, take
f_r=0,f_i1=1,f_i2=-2. Discarding both i1,i2 gives residual variance 6. Retaining
i1 and discarding only i2 gives variance 8. More retention increased this
particular coupling-error variance by removing a cancellation.

For the two nonnegative occupation rewards 1_{i1},1_{i2}, the before/after
covariances in the same example are

  C_before=[[2,1],[1,2]],   C_after=[[0,0],[0,2]].

Their difference [[2,1],[1,0]] has determinant -1, so it is indefinite. Separate
nonnegative-reward variance quotas can be monotone; covariance in PSD order is
not a valid universal selection objective.

4. Exact cause/deadline interval certificates
---------------------------------------------
Let F_a(t)=P(T<=t | a), Fhat_a(t)=P(That^a<=t | a). For any delta>0, conditional
centering and one-sided Cantelli give

  Fhat_a(t-delta)-V_a/(V_a+delta^2)
       <= F_a(t)
       <= Fhat_a(t+delta)+V_a/(V_a+delta^2).

Multiply by p_a and clip to [0,p_a] to obtain an interval for the scientific
joint probability P(cause=a,T<=t). For a one-sided joint tail, use complements
within p_a. This is an absolute error enclosure, not a relative rare-tail bound.
It is stronger than the delta^(-2) two-sided Markov penalty at the same delta.

All quantities entering this statement can have rationally verified enclosures.
For a rational killed generator A, rational t>=0, initial row mu and nonnegative
committor c with Ac<=0, choose rational lambda>=max_i(-A_ii), and set

  P=I+A/lambda,  x=lambda*t,  p=mu*c,
  S_N=sum_{j=0}^N x^j/j!,
  Z_N=sum_{j=0}^N (x^j/j!) mu P^j c.

Uniformization gives the exact joint CDF p-mu exp(At)c. Since
0<=mu P^j c<=p, any rational U_x>=exp(x) supplies the enclosure

  (p S_N-Z_N)/U_x
       <= p-mu exp(At)c
       <= p-Z_N/U_x.

Its width is p(1-S_N/U_x). With L+2>x, an exact upper bound is

  U_x=sum_{j=0}^L x^j/j!
      + [x^(L+1)/(L+1)!]/[1-x/(L+2)].

This comes from bounding the remaining positive exponential-series terms by a
geometric series. It is a checkable finite arithmetic certificate, although
uniformization can be expensive on stiff chains. Matrix exponential numerical
agreement alone is not used as a certificate.

CHECKED DEADLINE EXAMPLE. From r, rates are 1/2 to A and 1/2 to iB; from iB,
rate to B is 100. For cause B, p_B=1/2, alpha^B=101/100, V_B=1/5000. With t=1
and delta=1/10, the conditional Cantelli penalty is 1/51. Exact rational
uniformization and the clock certificate give

  0.28509170474353823 <= P(cause=B,T<=1) <= 0.3415458085252234.

The saved results.json contains the exact rational endpoints, all series
parameters, a much narrower full-generator reference enclosure, and verifies
that the latter lies inside the reduced-clock interval. Decimal endpoints here
are for readability; the rational values are the certificate.

5. Joint deadlines for several additive rewards
-----------------------------------------------
For a vector A of rewards and its coupled reduced vector Ahat, put e=A-Ahat.
Under cause a, E[e]=0 and E[ee^T]=C^a as above. For delta_l>0, define

  eps_a=sum_l C^a_ll/(C^a_ll+delta_l^2).

Coordinatewise Cantelli plus a union bound gives the valid orthant enclosure

  Phat_a(Ahat<=d-delta)-eps_a
       <= P_a(A<=d)
       <= Phat_a(Ahat<=d+delta)+eps_a.

All vector inequalities are coordinatewise. Multiplication by p_a produces a
joint cause/reward-deadline certificate. Correlations need not be ignored: the
following rational quadratic certificate can exploit the full covariance.

Choose a symmetric H, a vector h and scalar h0, and nonnegative tau_l. Require
the quadratic q(e)=e^T H e+2h^T e+h0 to be nonnegative everywhere, via

  [[H,h],[h^T,h0]] >= 0.

To bound the union e_l>=delta_l, let v_l be the lth coordinate unit vector and
additionally require, for every l,

  [[H, h-(tau_l/2)v_l],
   [(h-(tau_l/2)v_l)^T, h0-1+tau_l delta_l]] >= 0.

Then q(e)>=1 whenever at least one of these error thresholds is crossed, so

  P_a(any e_l>=delta_l) <= tr(H C^a)+h0.

For e_l<=-delta_l change the minus sign in the off-diagonal block to plus.
Rational PSD witnesses, verified for example by exact LDL or principal minors,
give finite exact certificates. Minimizing the objective is an ordinary moment
SDP; no new SDP solver or improved complexity is claimed here. The coordinate
Cantelli sum is always an explicit feasible witness: sum_l

  q_l(e)=(e_l+C_ll/delta_l)^2/(delta_l+C_ll/delta_l)^2.

For each l choose tau_l=2/(delta_l+C_ll/delta_l); q_l-1-tau_l(e_l-delta_l) is a
nonnegative square. The other q_j are nonnegative. Expectations give exactly
the displayed sum of Cantelli penalties. Zero-variance coordinates may be
dropped or handled by the same algebra with C_ll=0.

For a single weighted deadline v^T A<=t, the variance is v^T C^a v and the scalar
Cantelli certificate applies directly. Arbitrary joint reward CDF evaluation of
the reduced model is itself a computational problem; the covariance identity
does not make that task free. Positive elapsed time alone uses a small ordinary
semigroup, while general joint rewards may require Feynman-Kac inversion or a
larger augmented representation.

6. Cause-aware retained-state selection
---------------------------------------
Keep the original eta and full committors fixed. Define

  z=G^T eta_I,   u^a=G c^a_I.

For any discarded j, the exact conditional certificate decrease is

  Delta_a(j)=V_a(R)-V_a(R union {j})
            =2 z_j u^a_j/(p_a G_jj).

Proof. If H=I\{j}, its killed Green matrix is
G_HH-G_Hj G_jH/G_jj. Expanding the before/after bilinear forms produces
(eta_I^T G)_j (G c^a_I)_j/G_jj, multiplied by 2/p_a.

Each V_a is decreasing and supermodular. For example choose a uniformization
rate for the full chain and expand each G_D=(1/lambda)sum_{k>=0}P_D^k. Every
positive path contribution to eta_D^T G_D c_D survives precisely if none of its
vertices have been retained. The benefit of hitting a fixed path is a coverage
function, hence submodular; nonnegative sums preserve this property. The
series converges by transience.

Weighted objective. For fixed w_a>=0,

  W(R)=sum_a w_a V_a(R)

is decreasing supermodular. The usual cardinality-greedy guarantee concerns
benefit removed, not remaining error or actual CDF error. All weighted scores
can be computed with one forward solve after setting

  c_w=sum_a (w_a/p_a)c^a,  u_w=G(c_w)_I,
  Delta_W(j)=2 z_j(u_w)_j/G_jj.

Choosing w_a=p_a recovers the unconditional source clock objective, since
sum_a c^a=1. This can ignore a scientifically important rare cause.

EXACT ONE-SLOT EXAMPLE. Let r branch to iA and iB with probabilities 999/1000 and
1/1000, at total exit rate 1. Trap mean times are 2 and 10, and traps lead to
different causes. Conditional bounds are V_A=8,V_B=200. The unconditional
decreases from retaining iA and iB are 999/125 and 1/5, so unconditional greedy
chooses iA and leaves worst-cause V=200. Worst-cause choice iB leaves V=8.
This compares error certificates only, not actual conditional CDF accuracy.

WORST-CAUSE CAUTION. max_a V_a is generally not supermodular. With two equally
likely disjoint unit-rate traps, V_A=V_B=2. Retaining either one leaves the maximum
at 2; retaining both lowers it to 0. Worst-error benefit is 0 for each singleton
and 2 for the pair, violating submodularity. Consequently the familiar greedy
benefit guarantee does not transfer to a maximum across causes.

Robust quota-cover selector. For desired per-cause upper bounds tau_a, let

  b_a=(V_a(R0)-tau_a)_+,
  F_a(S)=V_a(R0)-V_a(R0 union S),
  F_sat(S)=sum_a w_a min{F_a(S),b_a},  w_a>0.

F_sat is monotone submodular. Full saturation is equivalent to meeting every
nontrivial per-cause quota. With additive positive state costs kappa_j, the next
state score is exactly

  [sum_a w_a min{Delta_a(j),(V_a(R)-tau_a)_+}]/kappa_j.

This does not stall at the max-objective singleton example. Greedy ratio is the
standard submodular-cover algorithm, not a newly proved optimization primitive.
The saved script implements this selector with exact shared Green-matrix
rank-one updates. It checks that the rare-cause quota example retains only iB,
and that both zero-error quotas in the two-trap example retain both traps.
If exact-feasible optimum cost is OPT and Q=sum_a w_a b_a, reaching deficit at
most epsilon>0 has the usual (1+ln(Q/epsilon))*OPT cost bound. Each individual
quota violation is then at most epsilon/w_a. Exact saturation has no such
uniform logarithmic bound for arbitrary real increments without a positive
granularity assumption. Nonadditive fill or factorization costs invalidate the
simple additive-cost guarantee and must be measured separately.

Deadline-informed quotas. Fix scientific margin delta_a and tolerated
conditional one-sided probability penalty e_a in (0,1). Cantelli supplies the
sufficient clock-variance quota

  tau_a=e_a delta_a^2/(1-e_a).

The reduced CDF boundary mass and committor probability are still needed to turn
the resulting timing penalty into a useful absolute or relative deadline bound.
Choosing delta or quotas adaptively from R does not automatically preserve the
fixed-objective submodular guarantee.

For a nonnegative (or everywhere nonpositive) reward f, variance is
2(eta_I.*f_I)^T G(c^a_I.*f_I)/p_a. Its exact marginal has the same bilinear
formula and it is decreasing supermodular. Saturated cover therefore extends to
per-cause, per-nonnegative-reward variance quotas. Arbitrarily signed rewards
and PSD covariance quotas are excluded by the counterexample above.

7. Costs and classical comparator
---------------------------------
The equally informed classical baseline computes all committors from the full
killed generator, and then uses the exact identity

  P(cause=a,T<=t)=p_a-mu exp(A t)c^a_U.

This obtains all cause/deadline probabilities using one full semigroup action
and the committor columns. Robust matrix-exponential/Krylov/Schur/spectral and
uniformization methods are all relevant implementations. A comparison that
only checks unconditional pGT, poorly chosen retained states, or bulk CDF error
does not address this baseline.

For dense n-state inputs, a full factorization costs O(n^3), committor and
occupation solves O(n^2 times number_of_causes), and full inverse storage O(n^2).
The retained-state selector using explicit G pays those costs; it has not made
the physical rate model or inverse free. Once G is stored, Schur updates cost
O(|I|^2) per retained state. Updating z and all u^a costs O(number_of_causes*|I|)
in addition. Dense fill, stiffness, numerical conditioning and rational bit
growth are separate resource costs. Sparse factorization or matrix-free solves
may outperform explicit Green storage, but cheap exact all-state scores do not
follow without an implementation and benchmark.

Cause-specific reduced models share the trace but use different clocks. A
block-mixture Markov model may need up to number_of_causes*|S| states. Full
cause-conditioned covariance for d rewards needs forward solves with c^a.*f_l
and transpose solves with eta.*f_l; the factorization is reusable but these
right-hand sides and covariance evaluation are costs. No speedup is established
merely by counting the smaller retained matrix for each separate cause.

The exact Laplace comparator is also classical:

  E[exp(-sT)1_{cause=a}]=mu(sI-A)^(-1)q_a.

Eliminating I exactly yields an s-dependent Schur complement involving
(sI-Q_II)^(-1). It retains excursion-time memory. A fixed mean clock captures a
limited moment feature, not this entire frequency-dependent object; preserving
the exact kernel may retain the original model complexity.

8. Remaining rare-tail obstruction
----------------------------------
If the direct route and the rare trap have the SAME terminal cause, its committor
is 1 everywhere. Conditioning leaves the source counterexample unchanged:

  T=X+JY, X~Exp(1), J~Bernoulli(M^(-3)), Y~Exp(mean M),
  That=(1+M^(-2))X, V=2/M -> 0.

At deadline t=M, true survival is asymptotic to M^(-3)/e while reduced survival
is exp[-M/(1+M^(-2))]. It still misses asymptotically all of that rare tail.
Relabelling the trap path as a separate cause can expose it to conditional
selection, but may require scientific path information unavailable in the
original outcome labels. It is not a general solution to same-product traps.
