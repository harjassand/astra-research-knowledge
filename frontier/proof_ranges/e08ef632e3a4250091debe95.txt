## Curvature and the six universal moments

Let \(\psi_1,\psi_2:\mathbb T\to\mathbb R^m\) be smooth periodic profiles,
\(a=(r,s)\) a closed amplitude path, and

\[
  \phi(x,y)=r(x)\psi_1(y)+s(x)\psi_2(y),\qquad
  Q=(0\mid r\psi_1'+s\psi_2').
\]

For a polynomial \(F:\mathbb R^{m\times2}\to\mathbb R\) and a constant
background \(B\), use the profile one-form

\[
 \alpha_r(a)=\mathbb E_y DF(B+Q)[(\psi_1\mid0)],\qquad
 \alpha_s(a)=\mathbb E_y DF(B+Q)[(\psi_2\mid0)].
\]

Its scalar curvature is

\[
 K_{F,B,\psi}(r,s)=\partial_r\alpha_s-\partial_s\alpha_r
 =\mathbb E_y\!\left(
 D^2F(B+Q)[(0\mid\psi_1'),(\psi_2\mid0)]
 -D^2F(B+Q)[(0\mid\psi_2'),(\psi_1\mid0)]\right).
\]

If \(\deg F\le4\), then \(\deg K\le2\). Green's theorem with winding
multiplicity gives

\[
 \oint_a\alpha=\int_{\mathbb R^2} w_a(r,s)K(r,s)\,dr\,ds
 =K_{00}M_{00}+K_{10}M_{10}+K_{01}M_{01}
  +K_{20}M_{20}+K_{11}M_{11}+K_{02}M_{02},
\]

where \(K=K_{00}+K_{10}r+K_{01}s+K_{20}r^2+K_{11}rs+K_{02}s^2\) and
\[
 M_{ij}=\int_{\mathbb R^2}w_a(r,s)r^is^j\,dr\,ds,\qquad i+j\le2.
\]

Thus the six moments are sufficient. The supplied source uses this cancellation
inside an identity whose other two terms are nonnegative rank-one Bregman and
acoustic remainders; our result below settles the converse for universal
cancellation, not the independent validity of that source identity.
