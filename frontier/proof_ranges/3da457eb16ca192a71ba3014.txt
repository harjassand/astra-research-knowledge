The necessary object is not a complete microscopic model. It is the generator’s action, modulo a common energy shift, on the subspace relevant to the specified prediction.
The test can accept Hamiltonians that differ arbitrarily elsewhere. It deliberately does not certify those unvisited sectors.
The converse uses no engineered chain.
Take
\[ K=\frac M2 I,\qquad H_\theta=\frac M2 I+\theta Z,\qquad \psi=|+\rangle, \qquad \theta=\frac{2\varepsilon}{T}. \]
For \(MT\ge2\) and \(0<\varepsilon\le1/4\), both Hamiltonians satisfy the spectral promise. At time \(T\),
\[ D(\rho_{H_\theta}(T),\rho_K(T)) = |\sin(\theta T)| = \sin(2\varepsilon) \ge\varepsilon. \tag{15} \]
To account for failures, grant the experimenter the stronger canonical forward isometry
\[ W_{\theta,u}x = e^{-uH_\theta}x\,|{\rm success}\rangle + \sqrt{I-e^{-2uH_\theta}}x\,|{\rm failure}\rangle. \tag{16} \]
This even exposes coherent failure information. It does not supply the inverse isometry or an arbitrary-input unitary completion.
For an energy \(E\), the output flag has amplitudes
\[ \left(e^{-uE},\sqrt{1-e^{-2uE}}\right). \]
Its angular derivative satisfies
\[ |\alpha_u'(E)|^2 = \frac{u^2}{e^{2uE}-1} \le \min\!\left\{\frac4{M^2},\frac{2u}{M}\right\} \]
along the relevant energy interval. Consequently,
\[ \boxed{ \|I-W_{0,u}^\dagger W_{\theta,u}\| \le \min\!\left\{ \frac{2\theta^2}{M^2}, \frac{\theta^2u}{M} \right\}. } \tag{17} \]
This bound is uniform over the filter duration.
Purifying an adaptive protocol and tracking the overlap of its two possible states yields
\[ D_{\rm final} \le \frac{2|\theta|\sqrt R}{M} \]
after \(R\) raw attempts. Tracking duration-weighted overlap loss similarly gives
\[ D_{\rm final} \le |\theta|\sqrt{\frac{2\bar S}{M}}, \]
where \(\bar S\) is the average expected total filter duration under the two hypotheses.
Successful discrimination with probability \(2/3\) requires \(D_{\rm final}\ge1/3\). Therefore,
\[ \boxed{ R\ge\frac{M^2T^2}{144\varepsilon^2}, \qquad \bar S\ge\frac{MT^2}{72\varepsilon^2}. } \tag{18} \]
These match the upper orders.
Longer filters do not invalidate the lower bound when their unsuccessful attempts are charged. An oracle supplying arbitrarily long, normalized successful states for free would be a different interface.
The prepared states generally involve coherent many-body operations. They are not obtained merely by moving a single-site probe around the system.
All their ingredients depend on the known candidate \(K\). The inverse in (5) has bounded condition number, and standard quantum matrix-function methods provide compilation routes when the appropriate known-model access is available. Those compilation methods are prior work, not part of the claimed invention. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.01838)
For a known local candidate and efficiently preparable \(\psi\), this gives a route using a trusted quantum simulator. It does not guarantee efficient classical preparation or simulation for arbitrary \(K\).
