# Weighted-alpha correction

Append-only correction dated 14 October 2026. This note supplements the frozen `proof_alpha_and_countermodel.md`; it does not alter that file or its original manifest.

The phrase “exact symmetric killed local reference matrix” in the frozen proof is incorrect for `K = c.refraw[target_block]`. `model.Compiler.local_weighted` constructs the raw killed generator in function coordinates. It is generally not Euclidean symmetric. Its exact reversibility relation is

`p_i K[i,j] = p_j K[j,i]`,

where `p` is the normalized local equilibrium mass in `c.prob[target_block]`. Equivalently, `K` is self-adjoint in `L2(p)`, with inner product `fᵀ D_p g`. The real similarity transform `D_p^(1/2) K D_p^(-1/2)` is symmetric. `model.Compiler.refsym` stores a floating-point construction of that square-root similarity for numerical eigensolver use; the exact argument below uses rational `K`, `p`, and `u` and does not rely on that floating-point array.

Here is the correct exact alpha argument. The target-killed local chain is a finite irreducible reversible generator with a nonempty positive killing set. Thus `K` is an irreducible nonsingular M-matrix, `K^(-1)` is entrywise nonnegative, and `K` is positive definite as a self-adjoint operator on `L2(p)`. For an exact `u > 0` with `delta = ||K u - 1||_infinity < 1`,

`K u >= (1-delta) 1`, hence `K^(-1) 1 <= u/(1-delta)` componentwise.

Since `K^(-1) >= 0`, its induced (ordinary row-sum) infinity norm satisfies

`||K^(-1)||_infinity = ||K^(-1) 1||_infinity <= ||u||_infinity/(1-delta)`.

For every matrix, `rho(K^(-1)) <= ||K^(-1)||_infinity`. Reversibility makes the spectrum real and positive: `K` is similar to the symmetric positive-definite square-root transform, so `rho(K^(-1)) = lambda_max(K^(-1)) = 1/lambda_min(K)` (with `lambda_min` understood in the reversible `L2(p)` spectrum). Therefore

`lambda_min(K) >= (1-delta)/||u||_infinity`.

The norm step is an induced infinity-norm bound on the raw inverse; it does not assume that raw `K` or raw `K^(-1)` is Euclidean symmetric. The frozen proof's subsequent product/reference extension is unchanged: the local spectral floor is interpreted in the corresponding product reversible inner product under the packet's stated Kronecker-sum construction.
