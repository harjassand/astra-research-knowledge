## 6. The norm-optimal Hodge correction is certified, not supplied

Let grad be an oriented graph gradient, partial=grad*, and let a source b=partial F have one explicit finite-energy feasible flow F. Let g_* be the orthogonal projection of F onto closure(range grad). Then partial g_*=b and Pi F=F-g_*. Define E_b=||g_*||^2.

For any potential phi and any explicitly represented w with partial w=b,

\[
\boxed{2\langle b,\phi\rangle-\|\operatorname{grad}\phi\|^2
\le E_b\le\|w\|^2.}
\tag{13}
\]

The exact gap is

\[
\boxed{\Gamma=\|w-\operatorname{grad}\phi\|^2,\qquad
\|F-w-\Pi F\|\le\sqrt\Gamma.}
\tag{14}
\]

Proof: w-g_* lies in ker partial and is orthogonal to g_* and every gradient. Expand both quadratic errors. There is no inverse spectral-gap constant. Existence of g_* follows from Hilbert projection, not from an oracle for an infinite Laplacian.

The repair in Section 3 supplies a feasible w_0 directly. Adding divergence-free cycle fields leaves the source equation exact. Potentials and cycles are optimized in finite explicit dictionaries, then coefficients are rounded and the complete infinite residual is checked. A fixed dictionary requires finite Gram assembly and a finite quadratic solve, not a hidden infinite inverse. Poor dictionaries return wide intervals honestly.
