## Energy-descent and locality results

I reconstructed the source identity by a common-final-level calculation. The
two positive energy drops x,y give dissipation `-(x+y)/2`, and the coherent
correction supplies `(x+y)/2-sqrt(xy)`. Their sum is exactly `-C†C`.
Degenerate source levels produce no singular energy denominator. The star's
principal-minor bound is entrywise sharp, not a control-energy or mixing optimum.
The reservoir-only rank argument is valid: positivity makes ker K invariant
under the diagonal distinct-frequency matrix, while positive diagonal entries
exclude every coordinate eigenvector from that kernel.

Strict locality is a different contract from the source's global H-filtered
jumps. For a unique ground state with faithful local marginal, an A-only CPTP
map whose output ground energy does not increase must preserve the pure ground
state. A Stinespring purification then factors, and full Schmidt rank forces its
isometry to be identity tensored with one environment vector. The local channel
is therefore identity. This theorem does not restrict a mathematical witness
comparing two different Hamiltonians, as in N778/N779.

The robust proof also checks. A gap Delta turns heating epsilon on the ground
input into fidelity at least `1-epsilon/Delta`. Uhlmann and the smallest marginal
eigenvalue s bound the Stinespring operator distance. The trace-distance
contraction then gives the stated diamond bound and necessary heating allowance
`epsilon>=Delta s d_act²/8`. The gap and s must be acquired; the estimate can
become weak when s is small.

For a strict local classical minimum, the discrete Kraus-column energy argument
forces every allowed channel to fix that input. In generator form, subtracting
the jump's scalar column uses the displayed Lindblad gauge with the correct
sign. Negative-semidefinite drift with zero diagonal then has zero column.
Because the adjusted Hamiltonian is still in the allowed local operator span,
it cannot leave the strict minimum either. Products of local operators need
not remain in that span; nonlocal support, transient heating or changing H
is a genuine changed resource. Standard coercivity remains an open rate gate.

The final revision distinguishes a strictly local full semigroup, which is
identity, from retaining nonlocal native evolution under H. In the latter case
the nonnegative ground-input jump drift forces the added local jumps to be
scalar. The remaining local Hamiltonian commutator is negative semidefinite
with trace zero, hence zero; uniqueness and full Schmidt rank make that added
local Hamiltonian scalar too. Native `-i[H,.]` survives. Calling the entire
native evolution an identity channel would be incorrect and is not claimed.

The report also properly labels exact faithful-ground rigidity as known via
Frey–Funo–Hotta rather than a new primitive. Its simple Ising escape example
assumes supplied collective nucleation and conditional growth jumps. Those
jumps are partial basis injections, so coherences do not feed populations and
their energy drift is diagonal. Every nonground configuration has an exit of
rate at least one, and every event reduces down-spin count. The n-event gamma
tail and resulting trace-error bound follow. This is a scoped toy sufficiency
check under changed actuator support, not an acquired material controller or
advantage over direct reversal when temporary heating is legal.

The final finite-window Theorem4 is also valid. With energy safety,
`E_t=Phi_t*(E)` is positive and at most E, and its ground-space columns vanish.
All-input ground-population convergence makes each diagonal expectation of E_t
in a fixed finite basis vanish. Thus its trace and operator norm vanish. The
positive excited gap gives `E_T<=(||E_T||/Delta)E`, yielding a strict contraction
for some finite T. Conversely, positivity iterates that contraction and energy
monotonicity handles the remaining partial interval. This proves equivalence
for convergence to the ground subspace; it does not require a limiting density
inside a degenerate ground space. Instantaneous `Q>=aE` remains only sufficient.
Four separate nondegenerate-star fixtures have rank-one instantaneous Q but
strict positive finite-window contraction, with the two-window inequality
verified directly. A full channel-action certificate can remain expensive.

I checked the exact three-level fixture separately, obtaining derivatives+1/5
and-1/10 before/after completion. Six local amplitude-damping fixtures on a
faithful entangled ground state also satisfy the stated necessary heating bound.
The latter use a diamond lower witness, not a claim to have solved a diamond SDP.
