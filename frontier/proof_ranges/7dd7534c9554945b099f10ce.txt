Three separate checks establish the reported global result.

Seven freshly calculated value-anchor certificates prove
\[

The final trial operators have footprint at most 18; their full commutator outputs have footprint at most 20.
For every ring with \(n>40\), the per-site inner products of these translation-orbit operators equal their infinite covariance inner products exactly. Consequently, the same finite algebra supplies the same residual bounds for every \(n\ge41\).
For the infinite result, the proof takes the thermodynamic limit at positive Laplace parameter first. Finite-time Markovian locality supplies that passage; it is not used as a long-time mixing assumption. arXiv

\[
|\kappa_{n,\lambda}(V+s)|
