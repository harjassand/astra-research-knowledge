## 5. Vanishing TV errors and entropy transfer

### 5.1 Rebuild the finite word families

In source Theorem 5.1 replace the fixed tolerance \(10^{-4}\) at level i by
\[
 \vartheta_i=2^{-K_i/16}\longrightarrow0
\]
in both (W3) and (W4). This creates new finite word families and a modified symbolic system. The random-word proof is unchanged except that the bounds \(F_i,E_i\) in (5.10),(5.12) acquire \(+\log_2(1/\vartheta_i)=K_i/16\). More explicitly, if \(K=K_{i-1}\), the source bound becomes \(\log_2 F_i\le B_0(K)-14-3K_i/16\). Since \(B_0(K)\le2^{K/4}\le2^{K/3}/8\le K_i/8\) by (5.15),(5.7), this is at most \(-K_i/16\). Likewise \(\log_2 E_i\le B_0(K_i)-14+K_i/16-\ell_{i+1}/2\), which is less than \(-2^{K_i}/4\) by \(B_0(K_i)\le2^{K_i/4}\) and \(\ell_{i+1}\ge2^{K_i}\). The distance-failure bound \(T_i\) is unchanged. The growth estimates (5.6)–(5.7) therefore still make \(F_i<1/2\), \(E_i<1/4\), and \(T_i<1/4\) at every level, increasing the freely chosen initial \(K_1\) if needed. Properties (W1),(W2), the length/cardinality growth, zero entropy, and recognition estimates are unchanged.

### 5.2 Small sets of list values

For \(L_{i,D}(x,t)\) from (6.1), put \(q=D-1\), \(N=q\ell_i\). Retaining the atom bound in (W3), the source boundary-start decomposition and modified (W3),(W4) give
\[
 \mu_0\{L_{i,D}(\cdot,t)\in A\}
 \le \beta_i+\vartheta_i+\vartheta_{i+1}
      +2|A|2^{-q(\ell_i-2K_i)},\quad \beta_i=D_i^*/N_i\to0, \tag{5.1}
\]
for every \(A\subset W_i^q\), allowed D, and integer t. For any fixed \(\alpha>0\),
\[
 \sup_{|A|\le2^{(1-\alpha)N}}
 \mu_0\{L_{i,D}(\cdot,t)\in A\}\to0, \tag{5.2}
\]
uniformly in tests and t, since \(2K_i/\ell_i\to0\).

Let \(J:(M,\mu)\to(X,\mu_0)\) be the hypothetical isomorphism and \(\lambda=J_*\widetilde\nu\), extending J arbitrarily off D. Then
\[
 \lambda=p\lambda_K+(1-p)\lambda_{bad},\qquad
 \lambda_K=J_*\widetilde\nu_K\ll\mu_0.
\]
By (4.1), all shifts of \(\lambda_K\) uniformly transfer the small-set estimate (5.2). The heavy-atom argument from source Proposition 6.3 then gives
\[
 H_{\lambda_K}(L_{i,D}(\cdot,t))\ge(1-2\alpha)N
\]
for sufficiently large i, uniformly in allowed D and t. Concavity of Shannon entropy yields the exact contamination factor
\[
 H_{\lambda}(L_{i,D}(\cdot,t))\ge p(1-2\alpha)N. \tag{5.3}
\]
For example, take \(p>.999\), \(\alpha=.002\); the coefficient exceeds .994. This is enough for the source's .99 threshold after cell conditioning along the selected tests. Define the transported modulus \(\omega_K^X(s)=\sup_{\mu_0(B)\le s}\lambda_K(B)\); since J is an isomorphism on D, \(\omega_K^X(s)\le\omega_K(s)\to0\).
