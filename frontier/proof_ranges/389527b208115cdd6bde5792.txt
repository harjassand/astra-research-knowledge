## 10. Resource ledger and precise remaining theorem

**Proved repair cost:** polynomial in m, explicitly represented TT ranks, numbers of geometric poles and all rational bit lengths; each routed component has rank <=2 times the source rank. This is unconditional for the stated explicit source format and compatible total mass. Completion energy is either computed or bounded; small energy is not automatic.

**Quantum algebra cost:** fixed-range contact branching is at most C_physical^N per represented atom/core arrangement, and contractions are polynomial in the resulting explicit ranks/poles. Repeated rank growth is charged.

**Fixed-dictionary solution cost:** finite Gram assembly, gauge-fixed rational quadratic solve, and full residual verification are polynomial in the explicit dictionary and coefficient dimensions. The dictionary is constructed, not supplied as a correct high-precision witness. Its adequacy is decided by its certificate.

**Precision:** exact rational final data are used in executed certificates. Poles close to one, long delayed point masses, cancellations and intermediate denominator lengths are not unit-cost operations. Inputs outside the supported prototype are rejected or require a separately costed implementation.

**Physical inputs:** full local Hamiltonian coefficients and the original pure-dephasing model are still supplied or separately calibrated. No Gibbs specimens, quantum hardware, simulated artificial noise, infinite state, global spectral gap or uncharged operator inverse is used. Unmodelled memory, leakage, long-range terms, finite temperature and real-device transfer remain outside the result.

**Open theorem, not a hidden assumption:** Along a constructive approximation of the actual degree-N response, produce coefficient fields and Hodge potentials/flows with certified residual tolerances, tensor ranks and pole/state counts bounded by C_physical^N epsilon^-c, and total discovery, rounding, repair, solve, bit and verification costs of the same form.

The new results mean exact population balance need not add an independent R^m cost or an exponential rank penalty. The missing theorem now specifically concerns rank/spectral-scale approximability of near-minimum-energy Hodge corrections and interaction-generated fields, rather than merely writing a compact feasible flux. Proving it would combine with the prior logarithmic degree bound to give the desired polynomial inverse-accuracy transport prediction. It has not been proved here.

The Cauchy example only refutes exact rank preservation by general inverse operations. It neither disproves this approximate-rank target nor supplies a lower bound for the physical class. No impossibility claim about polynomial compression is made.
