## Illustrative material screen

The BaTiO3 calculation takes the frozen polynomial coefficients B=-1.69e8 and
D=2.37e9 from Table 1 of Bar et al., arXiv:2604.26089v1, with
f0=A P^2+B P^4+D P^6. It sets a=2A by coexistence; it does not claim a
self-consistent temperature-dependent fit. It uses Q12=-0.045, Q11=0.11,
C11=178 GPa and C12=96.4 GPa listed in the same manuscript.

For symmetric in-plane strains exx=eyy=e and zero out-of-plane stress,
C_effective=2(C11+C12-2 C12^2/C11). The local effective shunt is assumed to
apply this equibiaxial loading. A real patterned frame has not been designed or
measured to meet it. The manuscript's end-note temperature formula for the
sixth-order coefficient is not used; the positive Table 1 value is used explicitly.

At U=0.55b the model requires approximately 2.50 MV/m peak field, 0.370 ratio of
reference to active effective biaxial stiffness, 74 MPa in-plane active stress,
and 0.118 MJ/m^3 circulated work. 500 nm thickness is an illustrative voltage
conversion only. These are qualification targets, not achieved performance.
