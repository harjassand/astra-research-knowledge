## Smallest decisive test

Use one platform, ZnS/GaP(001), because that is where the source already measures a core-specific optical response. Keep it within its evidenced regime rather than transferring field thresholds from a different ZnS TEM sample or carrier kinetics from GaAs/Si.

1. On an undoped 20–25 nm ZnS/GaP(001) specimen, map a sparse local network and identify a candidate interacting pair. Determine both full Burgers vectors, slip planes, core classes, signs, and starting separation before assigning a target reaction. The 20 nm source film has few threads and clean mobility measurements; the 25 nm comparison has more pinning and a smaller light/dark response. If no naturally occurring, geometrically compatible pair is found, stop: a carrier-selective reaction is not yet instantiated in this platform.
2. Randomly assign independent matched pairs or regions to a 350 °C dark anneal and a 350 °C, 365 nm light anneal. Measure the specimen temperature, hold time, optical intensity, illuminated area, and exposure duration. Use the source’s 0.07–0.67 mW/cm² range as a starting range, not a known dose recipe; its exposure duration and absorbed dose are absent. Include a temperature-matched dark control. If claiming schedule selection, add a constant-light arm with the same integrated photon fluence as the schedule arm; first measure whether the mobility response is fast enough for pulsing.
3. Keep the imaging beam off during the controlled anneal, as in the source’s post-cycle imaging workflow, and include an imaging-dose control because ECCI itself injects carriers. Reimage the same registered region after each independent cycle. Do not treat successive light/dark cycles on one evolving network as independent replicas.
4. After the gate is removed and the sample cools, identify the final topology under multiple diffraction conditions. Count annihilation only when the line pair is topologically resolved and the expected Burgers-vector closure/product is confirmed; distinguish this from a junction, an exported line, a hidden/out-of-view line, or contrast loss. Inspect any residual stacking fault or interfacial segment and record plastic strain, so zero net Burgers content is not mistaken for zero damage.
5. Compare reaction-outcome frequencies across independently prepared pairs, with sample size set from a predeclared meaningful effect and the wide pinning variability already observed in these sources. A pilot with one event can establish feasibility but cannot establish selection. A positive result would show a scoped mechanism; throughput and material-quality claims would still require an area/time and energy comparison against ZnS donor-doping and strain-engineered growth controls.

The practical pivot is therefore from “post-growth repair at useful throughput” to “does the already measured ZnS core-selective glide control change one mapped reaction outcome during a 350 °C strain-relaxation cycle?” If it does not, the evidence supports selective glide or growth engineering only. A maximum segment speed, a retained displacement, or a lower line density in a doped as-grown film cannot substitute for the missing reaction-outcome measurement.
