Making M large and gamma small separates channel discovery from event rarity.
Nothing here says that all rare events require 1/gamma work.

A weighted-neighbor sampling oracle at A could reveal the modified channel
quickly, and an aggregate exit-rate oracle at A reveals the answer directly.
Those are stronger input services. Charging their acquisition is the exact
interface issue, not a reason to disallow them in a useful implementation.

retaining regularity/geometry would require a separate construction; none is
claimed here. A practical positive direction must declare its channels or an
overlap/envelope condition that can actually be acquired and checked.
