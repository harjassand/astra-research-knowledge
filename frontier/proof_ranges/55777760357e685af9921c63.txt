## 10. What was not established

The entire left-SO(2) family still permits unequal coefficients of \(qu\) and \(qv\), a \(qw\) term, and more general reflection-even terms. The proof here does not show that every rank-one-convex choice in that cone has a compatible heat correction, nor does it prove universal nine-moment completion.

Exploratory computations examined scalar and tensor-valued corrections in the larger quarter-turn sector, as well as a proposed universal positive linear transfer from acoustic blocks. Some tensor searches closed numerical gaps left by scalar searches on other sampled inputs. A universal transfer was not obtained. Numerical residuals do not prove infeasibility or separation of those correction cones. Crucially, the final sharp slice does **not** require the tensor terms: the exact scalar certificate in Sections 2–3 suffices. Tensor necessity for this slice would have been an incorrect conclusion.

The strongest preserved extension mechanism is to construct compatible corrections on the faces forced by all acoustic equality cases in the full left-SO(2) cone, allowing a changed heat metric or tensor components when necessary. The distinct counterexample direction remains a genuinely compatible resonant Fourier construction with exact moments and a separately certified rank-one-convex integrand. No such counterexample was produced here.

No repository write was performed. The selective GitHub searches did not supply a directly applicable additional theorem. The fixed-size acoustic and moment-completion representations from the prior report were research aids; neither is assumed to provide the missing global positivity.
