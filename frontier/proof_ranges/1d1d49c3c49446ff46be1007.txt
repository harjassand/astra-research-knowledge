## New consequence: sampling cost versus information cost

`TRANSIENT_INFORMATION.md` supplies a new scoped derivation. For the actual
source family, let `mu=E V_0`, `sigma2=Var(V_0)`,
`D=max(mu-a,b-mu)<mu`, `q=(D/mu)^2` and

`B=[((1-q)^(-3/2)-1-3q/2)/q^2] (sigma2/mu^2)^2`.

The exact Gaussian overlap identity gives

`chi2(f_t || G_mu) <= B exp(-2t/3)`.

Therefore any test receiving **only N independent output velocities** from
either `f_t` or `G_mu`, with equal priors and average error at most `1/4`,
needs

`N >= (log 2 / B) exp(2t/3)`.

For the equal `{1,1.1}` mixture, `B≈9.666615263e-6`, and the lower bound
is approximately `71,705 exp(2t/3)` outputs. A quartic Hermite statistic
gives a matching exponential order under known mean and variance. This
quantitatively explains the source's honest warning that 4,500 samples do
not resolve its tiny fourth-moment transient.

This refutes the named transfer “uniform work per velocity implies uniform
work for resolving the transient from those velocities.” It does **not**
lower-bound a model-aware solver with moment access or every scientific
task, and it does not invalidate the exact sampler.
