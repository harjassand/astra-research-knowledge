\section{What the previous covariance candidate would add}

The exact original candidate assumes $H=\sum_a h_aP_a$, both \eqref{eq:dictionary} and incident weighted strength $g$, no background outside that bounded-incidence dictionary, and an unrestricted Gibbs state. It proposes
\begin{equation}
 C\succeq c I_m,\qquad c=\exp[-C_{k,\Delta}(1+\beta g)^2].
 \label{eq:candidate}
\end{equation}
Here $C_{k,\Delta}$ is a claimed finite constant, distinct from the covariance matrix $C$.

\begin{corollary}[Explicitly conditional all-directions upgrade]\label{cor:conditional}
\emph{If} \eqref{eq:candidate} holds, then in its exact setting (take $\ell=k$ above),
\begin{equation}
 F_M\succeq\frac{c}{c+\nu}\,\beta^2C
 \succeq\frac{c}{c+\nu}F_Q,
 \qquad
 F_M\succeq\frac{\beta^2c^2}{c+\nu}I_m.
 \label{eq:conditional}
\end{equation}
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For every eigenvalue $\lambda\ge c$, $\lambda^2/(\lambda+\nu)\ge[c/(c+\nu)]\lambda$. Apply functional calculus to \eqref{eq:chain}, then use $F_Q\preceq\beta^2C$.
\end{proof}

This would say that one fixed noisy separable measurement is uniformly competitive with the quantum information metric in \emph{every} parameter direction, not just highly informative directions. It is not used elsewhere in this note and is not promoted to an established unconditional result.

A targeted reconstruction of the predecessor's key step found no decisive defect in the exact-adjoint-before-truncation ordering: for local maps $\mathcal G_i$ exactly self-adjoint in the KMS inner product,
\[
 \langle\mathcal G_iP_a,\mathcal G_jP_c\rangle_K
 =\langle P_a,\mathcal G_i\mathcal G_jP_c\rangle_K.
\]
Only then are local approximations inserted. Disjoint approximate supports annihilate the product, so the estimate need not bound $\Cov_\rho(P_a,P_c)$. The former manuscript's coefficient counting and choice of spatial decay exponent can absorb bounded-degree volume growth. Nevertheless, the Gaussian locality, joint frequency/spatial tails, complete constants, and combined universal theorem remain subject to independent proof reconstruction. A targeted recheck is not external verification or Lean formalization.

Most importantly, the auxiliary map need not generate a completely positive semigroup. An information inequality for it does not produce a physical rapidly mixing Gibbs sampler.
