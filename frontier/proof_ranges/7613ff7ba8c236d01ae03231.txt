2. Four response branches and phase reversal

Let A_D^+, A_D^-, A_L^+, A_L^- be acquired outward/inward responses at
the actual forces, shape, position, temperature, charge history and
optical states. For each ell define the four-cycle drifts by integration:

  d_DL=int(A_D^+-A_L^-) d ell,
  d_LD=int(A_L^+-A_D^-) d ell,
  d_DD=int(A_D^+-A_D^-) d ell,
  d_LL=int(A_L^+-A_L^-) d ell.

Under branch reproducibility and negligible or explicitly subtracted
switching displacement,

  d_DL+d_LD=d_DD+d_LL.

Therefore d_LD=-d_DL requires that the sum of the two fixed-state
drifts vanish; the simple two-state theorem has the stronger assumption
A_D^+=A_D^-, A_L^+=A_L^-. The optical phase-antisymmetric part is

  (d_DL-d_LD)/2 = (1/2)int(A_D^++A_D^- -A_L^+-A_L^-) d ell.

If state preparation changes hidden pinning/core variables between
cycles, these four branches need not exist as single-valued functions.
Failure of the identity then rejects the proposed reduced acquisition
contract, not all possible defect transport.

3. Stress-only countermodel and admissible comparator

At one fixed optical state, take dimensionless activated odd glide laws

  v_C(F)=sinh(F), v_N(F)=sinh(2F)/2,

with C moving to the left and N to the right for F>0. Their effective
mobilities are sinh(F)/F and sinh(2F)/(2F). Expansion at F=0.2 and
contraction at F=-2 give A_out=0.0049668546, A_in=0.2900128292 and
d/a=-0.2850459746. This is an exact constitutive counterexample to the
claim that all fixed-light mechanical cycles must be null. It assigns
no force scale, activation volume, or mobility to ZnS.

A fair stress-only comparator optimizes the allowed stress tensor,
amplitude, waveform and frequency within the same measured temperature,
damage, strain-closure and time constraints. A fixed-light comparator
also optimizes a constant intensity/wavelength, including darkness.
An optical advantage needs a measured expansion of the attainable
displacement/cost/time frontier, not merely superiority over one
symmetric sinusoidal waveform. Spatial stress gradients and deliberate
pinning control belong in the allowable baseline when feasible.
