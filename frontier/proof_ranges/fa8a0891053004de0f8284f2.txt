6. The exact missing obstruction
The crucial question is now:
Can the self-consistent relation between velocity, strain, and pressure control cumulative-strain noncommutation and spatial variation without first assuming the regularity one wants to prove?

There is an automatic estimate, but it falls short in an identifiable way.
For \(\nu>0\), multiply
\[
\mathcal LB=S
\]
by \(B\), use the Frobenius inner product, and integrate. Symmetry and periodic integration by parts give
\[
\frac12\frac{d}{dt}\|B\|_{2,F}^2
+
\nu\|\nabla B\|_{2,F}^2
=
-\int(\operatorname{div}B)\cdot u.
\]
Since
\[
\|\operatorname{div}B\|_2
\leq\sqrt3\|\nabla B\|_{2,F},
\]
Young’s inequality yields
\[
\boxed{
\|B(t)\|_{2,F}^2+
\nu\int_0^t\|\nabla B\|_{2,F}^2\,ds
\leq
\frac3\nu\int_0^t\|u(s)\|_2^2\,ds
\leq
\frac{3t}{\nu}\|u_0\|_2^2.
}
\tag{14}
\]
The last inequality uses the usual unforced kinetic-energy inequality. arXiv
Equation (14) supplies a spatially averaged bound. It does not supply the bounded primitive and spatial-supremum action in (11), the quadratic-form control in (13), or a bound on the cubic commutator.
That is the unresolved step—not an omitted algebraic calculation.
A major consequence would require a noncircular improvement of this estimate in a consequential class of self-consistent flows. Merely assuming (11) and invoking the theorem does not accomplish that. Nor can the arbitrary matrix pulse examples be treated as finite-energy fluid histories without a separate realization theorem.
7. What should be retained
The retained mathematical content is the sharp local double-commutator estimate, its integrated tensor comparison, the diffusion extension, and counterexamples showing that both cubic noncommutation and spatial-gradient costs can be necessary.
The underlying machinery—Fer factorization, exponential derivatives, symmetric/skew decomposition, and the subsequent energy argument—is classical. Selective prior-art searches did not establish whether the precise combination above already appears in matrix evolution, geometric integration, or fluid-regularity literature. Historical originality remains unresolved.
The accompanying checks passed: 120 local matrix/parabolic cases, 36 trajectory comparisons, sharp-coefficient examples, and exact symbolic determinant and trace identities for the pulse construction. These are implementation diagnostics, not independent review, formal verification, or a fluid simulation.
There is also no automatic computational speedup. Evaluating the certificate requires the actual strain history, or validated approximations sufficient to bound \(B\), the commutators, and—in the viscous case—spatial derivatives or quadratic forms. Input acquisition and unresolved oscillations cannot be ignored.
The research outcome is therefore a rigorously derived, cancellation-sensitive growth estimate with an explicit nonlinear closure problem—not yet a new foundational mathematical power with independently major consequences.
