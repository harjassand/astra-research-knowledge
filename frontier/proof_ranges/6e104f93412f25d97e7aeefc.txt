Why this addresses the original obstruction
This is not a fresh state with matching statistics. It replaces a measurement isometry on the existing state, with a bound before the environment is discarded.

Consequently, applying any common subsequent channel cannot increase the trace-distance error. The guarantee also extends to other references correlated with the same original network state, through purification and trace-distance contraction.

There are two essential boundaries.

First, this is a state-specific guarantee under source independence, not a diamond-norm approximation on arbitrary inputs. Second, equation (3) uses the microscopic source marginal and measurement realization. Observed agreement certifies that a replacement exists; it does not reveal the matrices needed to implement it.

For general POVMs, the statement concerns a selected local projective dilation. It does not identify that coherent dilation with every physical instrument implementing the same POVM.
