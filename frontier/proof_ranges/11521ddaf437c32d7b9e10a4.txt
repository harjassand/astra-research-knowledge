## Capital compatibility and failures

N776's transport certificate is a current quadratic-form guarantee under
actual-D phases and dephasing, rather than low-energy preparation or a local
all-state cooling operation. Its finite current floor cannot supply the missing
strict local cooling actuator or escape lemma. Dephasing can change coherences
while conserving energy; it provides no strict energy descent by itself.

N778's local UCP comparison maps are classical witnesses comparing different
Hamiltonians with a common environment. They are not supplied physical channels.
Setting both Hamiltonians equal and demanding an implemented strict-local
all-state energy-safe operation exposes the obstruction above, while leaving
the paired comparison theorem unchanged. The fixed-charge proof's strong Kraus
preservation remains necessary whenever restricting a physical operation to
a sector. Ordinary covariance is not enough.

N779 compares Gibbs free energies of different Hamiltonians and charges a
channel entropy penalty. A local energy-safe cooling semigroup cannot be
inferred from that comparison. Entropy disposal, locality and a mixing bound
remain separate obligations. Conversely, the present source's global spectral
filters do not acquire the local channel or realistic environment promised by
those comparison tasks. These are incompatible transfer interfaces, not
refutations of their individual theorems.
