## B. Channel powers admit dimension-independent classical approximation

Use the same \(\Psi\) and define
\[
\Lambda_n=\Psi\circ\Phi^n.
\]
Every \(\Lambda_n\) is entanglement breaking.

Because \(0\preceq\Psi\preceq\mathsf I\) as a Hilbert–Schmidt operator,
\[
(\mathsf I-\Psi)^2\preceq\mathsf I-\Psi.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|(\Phi^n-\Lambda_n)(A)\|_{2,\tau}^2
&\le
\langle\Phi^n(A),(\mathsf I-\Psi)\Phi^n(A)\rangle_\tau\\
&\le
2\langle A,\Phi^{2n}(\mathsf I-\Phi^2)A\rangle_\tau\\
&\le
\frac{2n^n}{(n+1)^{n+1}}\|A\|_{2,\tau}^2,
\end{aligned}
\]
where the last line uses
\[
\max_{0\le x\le1}x^n(1-x)
=\frac{n^n}{(n+1)^{n+1}}.
\]

Thus
\[
\boxed{
\|\Phi^n-\Lambda_n\|_{2\to2}
\le
\sqrt{\frac{2n^n}{(n+1)^{n+1}}}
\le
\sqrt{\frac2{n+1}}.
}
\tag{23}
\]

The same bound holds for the normalized Hermitian \(\infty\to1\) norm, since
\[
\|A\|_{2,\tau}\le\|A\|_\infty,
\qquad
\|X\|_{1,\tau}\le\|X\|_{2,\tau}.
\]

No spectral gap is required, and \(\Psi\) need not commute with \(\Phi\).

**This is not a diamond-norm estimate and does not establish that any fixed power is exactly entanglement breaking.**
