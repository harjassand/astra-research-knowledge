## Acquisition and decisive next measurement

The source's 25/(C R)² estimate is conservative variance-based five-sigma
planning for two equally sampled independent completed Bernoulli settings. It
is not an exact finite-sample guarantee, an attempted-shot budget or a rate
measurement. At its C=.52,R=.03 scenario it gives 102,729 completed shots and
34.243 minutes at an assumed 50 Hz. Controls, reset preparation, switching,
rejection, chemical identity checks, calibration and replacement add cost.

For independent irreversible loss q over N shots, the exact 90% survival
ceiling is q<=1-.9^(1/N), not exactly -log(.9)/N. At N=102,729 the difference
is only 5.26e-13 absolute; the source's latter expression is an appropriate
first-order planning approximation. At least 2,995,731 failure-free independent
trials are required to exclude q>=1e-6 at 95% one-sided confidence. That is
evidence to acquire, not a qualified apparatus survival rate. Dependence/drift
needs a separate model.

The static differential Ca+ polarizability -44.32(32) atomic units is correctly
identified as a static quantity in [Huang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.07828).
It does not predict the dynamic/tensor 2.7-micrometre phase. The known standing-
wave atomic control experiment [Ricci Vasquez et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.02597v2)
also does not qualify ultrafast molecular waveform stability or this readout.

The next measurement should acquire the full pulse-sequence joint law:
both ancilla quadratures, accepted/attempted counts, survival/identity, temporal
envelopes/spectral phase, programme swap, phase randomization, common-branch,
individual-pulse, atomic-background/off-resonant and beyond-overlap delay
controls. Its target is the controlled molecular order-odd probability
difference after actual backgrounds, with complete trial and systematic-error
costs. The packet's illustrative .015 signal/.0015 systematic gate can be
retained as a declared planning target. It is not achieved. If contrast or
survival fails, no theorem here repairs it by assigning a longer lifetime.
