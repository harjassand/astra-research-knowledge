# Actual-boundary static product obstruction

## Stronger result
The exact native-product obstruction extends to the stronger, actual-target-boundary class: arbitrary positive static product factors on continuation states, with the target prescribed exactly one. No restrictions on low-count factor values are imposed.

Every such exponential-time barrier has initial value at least exp(−δ), with exact rational δ=2.666777495494622…×10^−22<3×10^−22. Thus it exceeds 1−3×10^−22. Adding the existing overflow allowance and clipping at one yields only one. This quantifies failure of this entire static product interface, not a lower bound on the actual first-hit probability.

## Class and time orientation
On continuation states n∉A let g(t,n)=exp(λ(1−t)+F(n)), where F(n)=Σ_iφ_i(n_i) and every φ_i(0),…,φ_i(162) is a finite unrestricted real number. On A prescribe g=1, and at overflow prescribe g=0. Require the full actual generator supercaloric inequality ∂_t g+Qg≤0 on continuation states for0≤t≤1. Nonnegative terminal continuation values are automatic.

For λ≥0 the target-entry ratio exp(−λ(1−t)−F(n)) is largest at t=1. All other generator ratios are independent of time. Therefore the all-time condition is equivalent to the static terminal-boundary condition Qhat exp(F)/exp(F)≤λ, with target value one and overflow zero. In particular this terminal residual condition is necessary even if only times below1 are imposed, by continuity.

Negative λ loses no candidates: replace λ by zero and F by F+λ. Initial values are unchanged. The new target-entry ratios equal the old ones at t=0, which were maximal when λ<0, while all continuation ratios are unchanged. The old residual at t=0 was≤λ<0, so the new residual is≤0. Thus λ≥0 is without loss here too.

Convex averaging over graph automorphisms again reduces arbitrary site factors to two profiles: corners and internal-column sites. The new target-entry term is rate×exp(−F(n)), also convex in the log profiles. The objective and constraints are invariant under those automorphisms. This reduction requires no low-count normalization. A constant shift from the corner profile to the inner profile leaves F unchanged and accounts for one redundant coordinate.

- A target-entry transition contributes −rate×F(s), since its ratio is exp(−F(s)).

## What remains open
This result covers arbitrary static one-site product shapes, with a common exponential time factor and the exact target boundary, for the actual eight-site killed process. It also covers native-boundary candidates because replacing a native target value≥1 by one reduces entering-target drift. It does not rule out genuinely time-dependent local profiles, sums of products, pair factors, or richer tensor representations. The actual rare-event probability remains uncertified beyond the established overflow control.

This is standard exponential Lyapunov convexity plus an exact finite occupation-measure certificate, not a new general optimization framework. It supplies a precise representation obstruction and explains why further static-product optimization is unproductive for this benchmark.
