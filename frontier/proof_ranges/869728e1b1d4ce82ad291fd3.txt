## 1. The exact input contract

Let H be a complex Hilbert space, a>0, P>=0 bounded, and let
Phi(X)=sum_i V_i X V_i* be a bounded positive subunital map: Phi(I)<=I.
Write g_a(Z)=Z(I+aZ)^-1 and F_P(X)=g_a(Phi(X)+P).
The source-spectrum note, sections 2 and 6, proves that F_P has a unique bounded
positive fixed point X_P and the modulus

    ||F_P(X)-F_P(Y)|| <= g_a(||X-Y||),   X,Y>=0.                  (1)

For clarity, if ||X-Y||<=d, positivity gives Phi(X)<=Phi(Y)+dI.
Operator monotonicity and functional calculus give
g_a(Z+dI)-g_a(Z)<=d/(1+ad) I; interchange X,Y. This proves (1),
including noncommuting X,Y. The fixed point is an identified target in this
section. An arbitrary positive subsolution A<=F_P(A) is a different contract.

For a single channel this covariance update is a classical discrete Riccati /
Kalman covariance operation, with observation H_obs=I and noise a^-1 I.
This note does not invent the operation or general goal-oriented approximation.
Its purpose is an exact error and transfer contract for this capital.
