## 10. Consequence for the current lower-bound programme

Assume the independently audited explicit-model exponential maximum-state lower bound: each fixed binary two-dimensional interpretation program distinguishing the specified opposite-answer family, on arbitrarily large inputs of atom size `N`, has a run with maximum universe size at least `exp(c_I N)` for some `c_I>0`.

For a fixed BGS term program, apply the present compiler. If its two native runs have common maximum hereditary size `S`, the interpretation runs have size at most `C(N+S+4)^d`. Consequently

`C(N+S+4)^d >= exp(c_I N)`.

Thus `N+S+4 >= C^{-1/d} exp(c_I N/d)`, and, for sufficiently large inputs, `S >= exp(c_P N)` for any sufficiently small fixed positive `c_P`. The constants depend on the fixed program. This transfers the exponential maximum-state obstruction to the specific standard Rossman/GKPS BGS term formalism, including `Card`, with unrestricted finite runtime.

This conclusion remains conditional on the base/support/expander lower-bound inputs. The present reconstruction and the low-dimensional compiler have both passed the independent mathematical audit in `../space_simulation_audit/INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.md`; this is not formal certification or external expert validation. It is not an unrestricted algorithmic-space lower bound, a P-versus-NP claim, or a claim about all formalisms merely known to be polynomial-time equivalent to CPT.
