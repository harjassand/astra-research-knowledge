## 1. Exact theorem endorsed

Let the average be normalized on T^2=(R/2pi Z)^2. Let

\[
\phi=(f,g),\qquad f(x,y)=F(x)+H(y),
\]

where F,H are real periodic W^{1,4}(T) functions and g is an arbitrary real
periodic W^{1,4}(T^2) function. Set

\[
D\phi=V=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}F'(x)&H'(y)\\g_x&g_y\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Retain the original r=1 integrand

\[
J_1(V)=\frac18\big((a-d)^2+(b+c)^2\big)^2
 +(ad-bc)^2+(a^2+c^2)(ad-bc).
\]

For every real scalar s,

\[
\boxed{\left\langle J_1(sI+V)-J_1(sI)\right\rangle
\ge\frac1{128}\left\langle M_4+s^2(a^2+c^2+d^2)\right\rangle}
\tag{A}
\]

where

\[
M_4=a^4+b^4+c^4+d^4+a^2b^2+a^2c^2+a^2d^2
 +b^2c^2+b^2d^2+c^2d^2.
\]

Consequently,

\[
\boxed{\left\langle J_1(sI+V)-J_1(sI)\right\rangle
\ge\frac1{256}\langle|V|^4\rangle
 +\frac{s^2}{128}\langle a^2+c^2+d^2\rangle.}
\tag{B}
\]

Here |V| is the Frobenius norm. Thus equality of the Jensen energy with zero
forces Dphi=0 almost everywhere. Neither the amplitude of F,H nor the size,
sign, frequency, concentration, or two-dimensional dependence of g is
restricted. Every real s is covered by the same identity.

This is a restricted test-class theorem. Standard quasiconvexity at sI
quantifies over arbitrary compatible test gradients; (A) does not establish
that quantifier. Left multiplication by a rotation U in SO(2) transfers (A)
jointly to background sU and perturbation Uphi, because column norms and
determinants are preserved. It does not authorize rotating only the
perturbation while keeping its background fixed. Separation is tied to the
chosen input coordinates and first output component.

## 2. Full Taylor reconstruction and null terms

For arbitrary real a,b,c,d,s, put j=ad-bc. Direct reconstruction gives

\[
\begin{split}
J_1(sI+V)={}&2s^4+s^3(5a+3d)\\
&+s^2\big((2a+d)^2+c^2+3j\big)+sC_1(V)+J_1(V),
\end{split}
\tag{C}
\]

where

\[
C_1(V)=a^3+5a^2d-4abc+ac^2+2ad^2-2bcd+c^2d.
\]

In particular J_1(sI)=2s^4 and DJ_1(sI):V=s^3(5a+3d).
For every smooth periodic compatible gradient, <a>=<d>=<j>=0.
The determinant identity is the periodic integration by parts

\[
\int (f_xg_y-f_yg_x)=0.
\]

Strong W^{1,4} approximation extends it to the stated Sobolev class. Thus
the integrated expansion is A+sC+s^2Q with

\[
A=\langle J_1(V)\rangle,\quad C=\langle C_1(V)\rangle,
\quad Q=\langle(2a+d)^2+c^2\rangle.
\]

For the separated class the stronger separate identities <ad>=<bc>=0
hold by periodic slicing. No cubic mean such as <a^3> is presumed zero.

The proposed row stress is

\[
S_1=-3s^2b+\tfrac12 b^3,\qquad
S_2=3s^3+7s^2a+5sa^2+\tfrac12 a^3.
\]

The independently verified pointwise identity is

\[
J_1(sI+V)-2s^4=5s^3a+S_1c+S_2d+G.
\tag{D}
\]

There is no omitted determinant term in (D). The terms 3s^3d,
-3s^2bc, 7s^2ad, 5sa^2d, and (a^3d+b^3c)/2 are contained in the stress
pairing itself. The remainder has the exact form

\[
\begin{split}
G={}&J_1(V)-\tfrac12(a^3d+b^3c)\\
&+s(a^3-4abc+ac^2+2ad^2-2bcd+c^2d)\\
&+s^2(4a^2+c^2+d^2).
\end{split}
\tag{E}
\]

The verifier compares every coefficient of (C), (D), and the independently
multiplied rational Gram against the original quartic. G has exactly 26
nonzero monomial coefficients and is homogeneous of degree four in
(a,b,c,d,s).

## 4. Integration and the Sobolev endpoint

For smooth separated f, S_1 depends only on y and S_2 only on x, so

\[
\operatorname{div}S=\partial_x S_1+\partial_y S_2=0,
\qquad \langle S_1g_x+S_2g_y\rangle=0.
\tag{G}
\]

For the asserted rough class, F',H' lie in L^4, hence S lies in
L^{4/3}(T^2), and Dg lies in L^4. Their product is integrable. Periodic
Sobolev slicing gives integral_x g_x(x,y)=0 for almost every y and
integral_y g_y(x,y)=0 for almost every x. Fubini and the separate dependence
of S_1,S_2 prove (G) directly for W^{1,4} data. Also <a>=0. Integrating
(D) and using (F)-(G) proves (A).

The usual smooth density proof is valid as well. Periodically convolve F
and H in one variable and g in two variables, obtaining
f_epsilon=F_epsilon(x)+H_epsilon(y). This preserves exactly the required
separation. The gradients converge strongly in L^4. For fixed s, a quartic
polynomial obeys

\[
|J_1(sI+X)-J_1(sI+Y)|
\le K(|s|+|X|+|Y|)^3|X-Y|,
\]

so its integrals converge by Holder. The quartic right side of (A) converges
by the same argument; its quadratic part converges because strong L^4
implies strong L^2 on a finite measure space. Cubic stresses converge strongly
in L^{4/3}, which also makes their L^{4/3}-L^4 pairings converge. Thus no
unproved weak-limit, concentration, or endpoint claim is needed.

No extension by zero is used here. Such extension or localization can
destroy the separated form, and this audit does not transfer (A) to all
zero-boundary maps on arbitrary domains.

## 6. General-first-potential obstruction and adversarial check

For smooth arbitrary f, compatibility a_y=b_x=f_xy gives exactly

\[
\operatorname{div}S=
\left(4s^2+10sa+\tfrac32(a^2+b^2)\right)f_{xy}.
\tag{J}
\]

Both the coefficient and the absence of other chain-rule terms were checked.
The coefficient is not everywhere nonnegative: at a=-10s/3,b=0 it is
-38s^2/3. Even nonnegativity would not alone give a sign for its pairing
with g. For smooth data the exact remaining energy identity is

\[
\langle J_1(sI+D\phi)-2s^4\rangle
=\langle G\rangle-
\left\langle g\left(4s^2+10sf_x+\tfrac32|Df|^2\right)f_{xy}\right\rangle.
\tag{K}
\]

For W^{1,4} data the stress pairing in (D) is well-defined, whereas the
pointwise product in the right side of (K) need not be. The smooth mixed-source
formula must not be silently promoted to a product of rough distributions.

As a concrete attempt to falsify an overbroad cancellation claim, take

\[
f=\sin x\sin y,\qquad g=\cos x\cos y.
\]

Then a=cos x sin y, b=sin x cos y, c=-b,d=-a and f_xy=cos x cos y.
Exact trigonometric moments give

\[
\boxed{\langle S\cdot Dg\rangle=-s^2-9/64\ne0,}
\qquad
\boxed{\langle J_1(sI+D\phi)-2s^4\rangle=s^2/2+17/32>0.}
\]

The audit computed these means over rational circular moments. This is a
counterexample to discarding the stress pairing for arbitrary f. It is not
a counterexample to conformal quasiconvexity. A larger symbolic family
g=lambda cos x cos y is retained in the receipt; it is diagnostic rather
than an all-gradient search or certification. Within the asserted separated
class, the exact positive certificate and cancellation rule preclude a
counterexample once the preceding proofs are accepted.

## 7. Ordinary convex/minor support is a different obligation

This nonlinear, field-dependent stress does not supply a conventional
pointwise support of the form

\[
J_1(I+V)\ge J_1(I)+DJ_1(I):V+\lambda\det V
\quad\text{for every matrix }V.
\]

The quadratic Taylor part is (2a+d)^2+c^2+(3-lambda)(ad-bc).
There is no b^2 term, so positive semidefiniteness forces lambda=3. On
V=t diag(1,-2), the resulting exact residual is

\[
-t^3+\frac{97}{8}t^4.
\]

It is negative for 0<t<8/97; the exact audited value at t=1/100 is
-703/800000000. This only rules out that ordinary pointwise affine-minor
support. A constant matrix perturbation is not a zero-mean periodic
compatible test gradient, so this does not refute the restricted theorem or
full quasiconvexity. Conversely the restricted theorem does not repair the
missing all-gradient stress pairing in (K).

The tools used here—rational Gram positivity, polynomial completion,

classical. This audit establishes correctness of the displayed restricted
