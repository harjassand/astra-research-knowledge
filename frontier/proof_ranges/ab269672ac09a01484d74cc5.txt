For each candidate rate s>=0 and any predictable eta_i in [0,1], independent of s,
define

    E_n(s)=product_{i=1}^n [1-eta_i
             + eta_i (G_i-ell_i)/(T_i s+b_i-ell_i)].           (R2)

For every s>=sigma, each factor is nonnegative and has conditional expectation at
most one by (R1). Thus E_n(s) is a nonnegative supermartingale starting at one.
It is continuous and nonincreasing in s. Ville's inequality yields

    P{there exists n: E_n(sigma)>=1/alpha} <= alpha.

Let L_n be the supremum of rejected nonnegative rates
{s:E_n(s)>=1/alpha}, using L_n=0 when that set is empty. Then

    P{forall n, L_n<=sigma} >= 1-alpha.                       (R3)

One can retain the running maximum of L_n. A fixed convex mixture of several
predictable betting strategies is also a nonnegative supermartingale, with the same
monotonicity and coverage. A finite rate grid returns a conservative lower certificate
without a multiple-grid testing penalty: any rejected grid point above sigma would
also imply E_n(sigma)>=1/alpha. A root solver using exact/rigorously bounded numerical
score evaluations gives the continuous version.

Each critic may be bad; coverage still holds. There is no guarantee that it captures
all microscopic EP or even all observed path irreversibility. Improving a critic is
a power question. Memoryless/finite-resolution observations must commute with

4. Preserved failed novelty route and elementary fallback
--------------------------------------------------------
I initially reconstructed an apparently preferable symmetric Fenchel dual. Let
S=(P+RP)/2, x=(dP-dRP)/(dP+dRP), and F(x)=2x artanh x. Then
D(P||RP)=E_S F(x). F is even and F''=4/(1-x^2)^2. An odd critic h gives
D>=E_P[h-F*(h)] and |h|<=H gives a score range of exactly 2H.
However, write h=F'(t), |t|<1. Then F*(h)=2t^2/(1-t^2), and

    h-F*(h)=log((1+t)/(1-t))+2t/(1+t)
             = f+1-exp(-f),   f=2 artanh t.

Also h=f+sinh f. Thus this is exactly the known NWJ/Kim-Otsubo score with a changed
clipping parameter. It is NOT a new way around exponential score-range costs.
The finite diagnostics use this equivalent parameterization; H=B+sinh B.

With fixed H and equal-duration independent blocks, a simpler uniform union-bound
certificate is

    [sum_{i<=n} G_i-H sqrt(2n log(n(n+1)/alpha))]_+/(nT).
