Research proof draft; internally checked, external review pending
Abstract
A computable complementary-channel completion is given for the uniformly gapped
inverse-Jordan kernels produced by the supplied companion channel-order reduction.
The completion is a genuine rational Stinespring construction. Its soundness holds for
coherent inputs, arbitrary Hermitian tangents, and every finite quantum reference, with
no state-conditioning or reference-size bound. An exact purification duality controls all
leaf scores, and sector-copy padding absorbs the remaining error. A zero-rate coding
argument compiles every finite-dimensional half-erasure helper into finitely many uses of
the fixed qubit half-erasure channel. Together with the companion source theorem, the
unrestricted complete information-order equivalence, and established capacity bridges,
the assembled proof gives the classification: exact zero quantum capacity and exact zero
private capacity are co-recursively enumerable complete for finite rational channels, while
their positive-capacity complements are recursively enumerable complete. The proof relies
on the supplied companion research theorem and has not undergone external review. It is
an exact zero-versus-positive result; no capacity approximation, fixed-gap promise, efficient
reduction, or computable positive-capacity witness bound is asserted.
