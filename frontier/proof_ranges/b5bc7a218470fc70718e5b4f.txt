# Mixed electric-field and shear-stress control: conditional derivations

Date: 2026-10-15 (Australia/Brisbane). These are new worker derivations under stated models, not measured material performance, externally reviewed theorems, or originality claims.

## 1. What structural coordinate is controlled

Let s = +/-1 label two *experimentally verified inversion-related* GaAs variants in fixed laboratory axes. Translation-only antiphase variants need not reverse a tensor; the measured domain pair, its atomic registry, and tensor transformation must establish this hypothesis. An APB label alone is insufficient.

For zincblende axes x=[100], y=[010], z=[001], use engineering shear gamma = 2 epsilon_xy and its conjugate stress tau = sigma_xy. Let C=C44, e=e14, d=e/C, and epsilon^gamma be the permittivity at fixed shear. The local, small-signal electric enthalpy is

    H_s(gamma,E) = C gamma^2/2 - s e E_z gamma - epsilon^gamma E_z^2/2.

This is a restricted quasistatic reactive constitutive model. It omits wall-core chemistry, flexure, incompatible substrate strain, electrode fields, free-carrier electrochemical forces, and dissipation. It is used to identify a possible conjugate term, not to supply the wall kinetics.

At independently prescribed external shear tau, minimizing H_s - tau gamma gives

    gamma_s = (tau + s e E)/C,
    g_s(E,tau) = -tau^2/(2C) - s d E tau
                 - (epsilon^gamma + e^2/C) E^2/2.

The variant pressure contribution, defined positive when s=+ grows, is

    p_em = g_- - g_+ = 2 d E_z sigma_xy.                (1)

In full tensor notation it is 2 E_i d_iJ sigma_J. Pure hydrostatic loading gives zero in this cubic tensor. A uniaxial stress sigma along [110] has sigma_xy = sigma/2, so its contribution is d14 E_z sigma; treating that uniaxial stress as a pure shear overstates the pressure by two. Coherency and field redistribution add forces; an actual specimen requires the virtual work of its complete elastic/electrical boundary-value problem.

### One-port self-actuation null

If the specimen is stress-free, tau=0 and the minimized energy is

    g_s(E,0) = -(epsilon^gamma + e^2/C) E^2/2,

independent of s. The strain induced by GaAs's own piezoelectric response reverses with s; substituting that strain into a mixed term without accounting for its generation is an invalid sign-selection argument.

More generally add a passive external spring K(gamma-gamma0)^2/2. Minimization gives

    H_min,s = K gamma0^2/2 - (s e E + K gamma0)^2/[2(C+K)]
              - epsilon^gamma E^2/2,
    H_min,- - H_min,+ = 2 e E K gamma0/(C+K).          (2)

With zero preload gamma0=0, even a finite passive mechanical environment supplies no linear inversion-odd bias. An independently controlled preload, imposed strain, or acoustic stress is indispensable in this model. Equation (2) also shows that load stiffness and transmitted strain cannot be assigned independently.

## 2. Synchronous AC control and a joint loss bound

For independently imposed, locally overlapping E=E0 cos(omega t) and tau=tau0 cos(omega t+phi), equation (1) yields

    mean(p_em) = d E0 tau0 cos(phi).                  (3)

Phase reversal by pi changes the mean bias; stress alone or electric drive alone does not. This average is applicable when boundary dynamics are slow relative to omega and the reactive constitutive model remains valid. At appreciable dispersion the independently acquired complex coupling and mode overlap replace real d and phi. A field generated by the *same local GaAs piezoelectric response* is subject to the one-port null, not automatically equation (3) with an s-independent tau.

Assume that the cycle-averaged irreversible power density of the two local generalized drives is a measured positive-semidefinite quadratic form

    q = (a |E0|^2 + b |tau0|^2 + 2 Re[c E0* tau0])/2,
    a >= 0, b >= 0, |c|^2 <= ab.                    (4)

The normalization is per actual active volume. a includes electrical dissipative response after field transfer; b includes acoustic/elastic damping; c allows cross dissipation. It is not legitimate to add both port input powers as separate local losses if they include reversible energy transferred between ports. The laboratory energy balance must sum net source powers and separately identify stored energy and useful wall work.

For the real reactive law |mean(p)| <= |d| |E0 tau0|, the arithmetic-geometric-mean inequality gives

    q >= (sqrt(ab)-|c|)|E0 tau0|
      >= (sqrt(ab)-|c|)|mean(p)|/|d|.                (5)

Proof: the diagonal terms divided by two are at least sqrt(ab)|E0 tau0|, and the cross term is at least -|c||E0 tau0|. This proves (5). The bound is nontrivial only when the acquired loss matrix has a strictly positive gap sqrt(ab)-|c|. A singular loss matrix can have a lossless direction, so passivity alone does not prove a strictly positive universal cost.

With negligible dissipative cross coupling, phase 0 or pi, and no amplitude caps, the exact minimum for specified p is

    q_min = |p| sqrt(ab)/|d|,
    E0/tau0 = sqrt(b/a).                            (6)

This equality is attainable only if both optimum amplitudes and spatial profiles are realizable. Finite field, stress, power, and heating limits generally raise the cost. For a uniform Ohmic region a=Sigma; for small mechanical loss b=omega s'' approximately omega eta/C. These substitutions are *model cases*, not certified high-temperature GaAs coefficients.

For an overdamped freely mobile wall with v=M p, a required displacement L entails integral p dt = L/M. Therefore (5) gives

    Q_loss/V_active >= (sqrt(ab)-|c|) L/(|d| M).     (7)

Opposing curvature/pinning, extra active acoustic volume, and imperfect field overlap raise the required drive. If v=M(p-p0) with a constant opposing p0 during the whole active interval t, replace L/M by L/M+p0 t. An activation-limited, history-dependent wall must use its measured law instead of this linear model.

## 3. Static stress: leakage, time and pulsing

In a uniform quasineutral isothermal Ohmic region maintained at field E, local conduction loss is Sigma E^2. Under |tau|<=tau_max, real constant d and M, and v=M(p-p0) over an active interval t,

    integral |E| dt >= (L/M+p0 t)/(2 |d| tau_max).

Cauchy-Schwarz then proves

    W_J/V_active >= Sigma (L/M+p0 t)^2
                    /[4 d^2 tau_max^2 t].           (8)

For p0=0 the same inequality remains a necessary bound when all additional forces oppose advance. Constant local E and maximal permitted shear attain its electrical part in the ideal linear model; pulsing cannot lower that electrical cost at the same displacement and active time. Pulsing might change temperature, carrier populations, pinning, or M, but that is a different coupled kinetic model requiring acquisition.

The bound is intentionally **not universal for depleted semiconductors**. In a depletion or space-charge region diffusion and electrochemical gradients can balance drift. In that case Sigma E^2 inferred from a neutral bulk conductivity is an invalid dissipation estimate. Use measured leakage, generation-recombination dynamics and the complete port energy balance. Conversely a low-impedance contact can maintain a bulk field despite charge relaxation; screening does not make imposed voltage impossible. It makes the field/charge/current boundary conditions and their loss indispensable.

Under isolated uniform bulk free-charge relaxation, E(t)=E(0) exp(-t/tau_M) with tau_M=epsilon/Sigma. Its pressure impulse at fixed shear is

    integral p dt = 2 d tau E(0) tau_M.              (9)

This does not imply a universal AC penetration threshold. For example, a lumped blocking-electrode capacitance C_b in series with a semiconductor admittance G_s+i omega C_s has

    V_s/V_ext = i omega C_b/[G_s+i omega(C_s+C_b)].   (10)

Its corner depends on the full geometry, including C_b, not just epsilon/Sigma. Real traps, depletion, distributed transport and contacts require a measured transfer function H_E(r,omega,T,n,X). Equation (10) is one explicit counterexample to using the bare Maxwell time as every device's drive cutoff.

## 4. Depletion overlap and fixed-interface obstruction

For a rigidly translating vertical wall of height h, the mean bulk pressure from the independently prescribed fields is

    p_eff = (1/h) integral_0^h 2 d(z) E(z) tau(z) dz.

If the nonnegligible electric field occupies a height fraction f, and throughout that region |d|<=d_max, |E|<=E_max, |tau|<=tau_max, then

    |p_eff| <= 2 d_max E_max tau_max f               (11)

(with the corresponding factor 1 rather than 2 for equal-frequency in-phase peak AC amplitudes). This follows by the triangle inequality. A low-leakage thin depletion layer cannot be credited with whole-wall bulk pressure. A bowed or surface-driven wall needs a wall-tension/anchor calculation; it is not covered by the rigid-wall mobility reduction.

Let the physical operation preserve the polarity trace on the GaAs/Si interface. If that trace contains both signs, an entirely s=+ film including its interface is unreachable by interior wall relocation alone: the prescribed negative trace would have to change. This is a boundary-condition obstruction, independent of actuator strength or feedback accuracy. A boundary may instead close above the interface, leaving an APB-free *upper region* and a defective basal region. Those are different retained endpoints. To eliminate the basal trace requires interface reconstruction, export through a permitted boundary, or material replacement, with its own atomic pathway and cost.

## 5. Final preference is not activation-barrier control

For one activated step sweeping volume V_step, local detailed balance gives a forward/backward ratio multiplier exp(p V_step/(k_B T)) when p is the only added thermodynamic affinity. This fixes a ratio; it does not fix either absolute rate. A symmetric-saddle illustrative pair is

    r_+ = r0 exp[p V_step/(2 k_B T)],
    r_- = r0 exp[-p V_step/(2 k_B T)],
    v = 2 ell r0 sinh[p V_step/(2 k_B T)].           (12)

A large total domain volume V_domain can make p V_domain enormous while p V_step remains negligible. Vacancy supply, bond exchange and pinning can keep r0 tiny. The 3.8 eV activation fit in Barrett et al. is a surface-density kinetic result with a proposed vacancy-mediated mechanism, not a measured phase-reversible directional mobility for an arbitrary APB.

The calculation file evaluates p=540 Pa at 923.15 K. Tenfold single-step forward/backward event-odds bias would require V_step >= k_B T ln(10)/p, about 5.4e-23 m^3. If a step advances one ~0.283 nm layer, this corresponds to a square cooperative patch around 0.44 micrometres wide. Neither that cooperative step nor a lowered saddle is acquired. V_step is the net swept step volume, not an independently measured forward transition-state activation volume. An atomic-pair-volume normalization instead changes the odds by only about two parts per million. Many weakly biased steps can still accumulate if their measured absolute mobility is sufficient; no universal lower bound on switching volume is claimed. This is a mechanism discriminator, not a claimed microscopic model.

## 6. A matched optical comparison without mismatched peaks

For the incoming restricted equal-color model p_peak=A I_peak^(3/2), duty D gives p_mean=D A I_peak^(3/2). At a fixed desired *mean pressure*, I_peak=(p_mean/(A D))^(2/3). If one loss channel has q_peak=B_m I_peak^m,

    q_mean = B_m (p_mean/A)^(2m/3) D^(1-2m/3).       (13)

Thus reducing duty reduces a linear-absorption term as D^(1/3), increases a two-photon term as D^(-1/3), and increases a three-photon term as D^(-1). Peak damage/field limits further restrict D. This result assumes unchanged material coefficients, overlap, temperature and kinetics; carrier accumulation and thermal feedback can invalidate it. It explains why neither optical peak pressure nor average incident intensity alone is a matched retained-output comparison. Reactive cavities do not remove local absorption at a given internal field.

## 7. Limits of an odd factorial discriminator

For comparable initial walls, define signed displacement X(E,tau), with the sign fixed in laboratory coordinates. The contrast

    Delta = [X(+E,+tau)-X(-E,+tau)
             -X(+E,-tau)+X(-E,-tau)]/4              (14)

selects the component odd in each independently controlled drive. It removes terms depending only on E, only on tau, E^2, or tau^2. It does **not** by itself distinguish conservative piezoelectric pressure from an E*tau-dependent contact heating, electrochemical force, or carrier pathway. Independent d calibration, actual local field/shear, phase, temperature/carrier measurements, a tensor-zero orientation, structural endpoint and retention remain necessary. Irreversible wall histories require randomized matched initial specimens/regions, not a naive four-step reversal on one already altered wall.
