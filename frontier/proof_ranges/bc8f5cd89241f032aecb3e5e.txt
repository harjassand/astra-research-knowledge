The result does not close the separate information, approximation, and acquisition requirements of Astra's broader problem.

There is also a simple example demonstrating that the nontracial extension applies beyond EB channels.

Take any faithful qubit state \\(\sigma\\), and define

\\[ T\_\*(\rho)=\tfrac12\rho+\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}(\rho)\sigma. \\]

It admits the symmetric broadcaster

\\[ \mathcal B\_\*(\rho) = \tfrac12(\rho\otimes\sigma+\sigma\otimes\rho). \\]

The channel preserves \\(\sigma\\) and is KMS-symmetric. Yet its Choi matrix has a negative partial-transpose eigenvalue for every faithful diagonal qubit \\(\sigma\\), so it is not entanglement breaking. The theorem therefore applies to genuinely quantum, nontracial examples.
