## Numerical checks and restricted benchmark

The script checks exact symbolic identities with SymPy and all real stationary
roots at 1,203 sampled control points for each of two normalized paths. It also
checks the mean-clamp instability with a direct four-cell Hessian eigenvalue.
Floating-point checks are not interval certificates or independent proof review.

It compares against a *constant quadratic gate* k q^2/2 during traversal, matched
to the same global curvature margin. The required k=mu-a+9b^2/(20c). At U=0.55b
the quartic gate circulates approximately 57.47% of the quadratic gate's ideal
work, but requires a larger peak bias. This is not an optimization over arbitrary
charge control, feedback, evolving constraints or multicaloric protocols.
