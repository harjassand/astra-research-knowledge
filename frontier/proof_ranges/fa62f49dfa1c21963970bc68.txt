III. PURE-STATE TIME-REVERSAL SUPPORT IDENTITIES

Vectorize pi into |psi> tensor Theta|psi>. The rank-l multipole satisfies
  r_l(psi)=d ||P_l(|psi> tensor Theta|psi>)||^2.        (12)
For half-integer j, Theta^2=-I, hence <psi|Theta psi>=0. Its symmetric-
pair probability is 1/2. Pair-spin swap parity is (-1)^{2j-l}; hence
  sum_{l odd}r_l=d/2,
  sum_{l even,l>0}r_l=d/2-1.                         (13)
For the highest rank n=2j odd, (13) gives r_n<=d/2. The extreme cat
  |cat>=(|j,j>+|j,-j>)/sqrt(2)
has r_n=d/2: the two equal-extreme-spin components contribute 1/2, and
the opposite-extreme-spin part of |cat> tensor Theta|cat> is antisymmetric,
orthogonal to maximal total spin. Its orbit therefore proves exactly
  k(P_{2j})=d/2, V(P_{2j})=4j+1.                    (14)

More generally, the full odd and even traceless frames have support
d/2 and d/2-1, respectively. Their star bounds are exact in every even
dimension d, and are useful without re-testing Clifford variants.
Let F be register swap and P the antisymmetric maximally entangled
spin-singlet projector. Completeness gives
  K_all=dF, sum_l(-1)^l K_l=d^2 P.
Therefore
  W_odd=[d^2(P_AB+P_AC)-d(F_AB+F_AC)]/2,
  W_even0=[d^2(P_AB+P_AC)+d(F_AB+F_AC)]/2-2I.
Their top eigenvalues are respectively
  d(d+2)/2 = V_odd+k_odd,
  d^2/2-2 = V_even0+k_even0  (d>=4).                 (15)

Proof of (15): for each vector v use u=S_AB tensor v_C,
v'=S_AC tensor v_B, w=v_A tensor S_BC. Their overlaps are 1/d,-1/d,1/d.
On u+v', P_AB+P_AC has eigenvalue 1+1/d and F_AB+F_AC eigenvalue -1,
giving the values in (15). Put x=u-v'. On span{x,w}, W_odd has matrix
[[d^2/2,0],[d,0]], with eigenvalues d^2/2 and 0. W_even0 has matrix
[[d^2/2-d-2,-d],[-d,-2]]. Its top eigenvalue is <=d^2/2-2 because at
t=d^2/2-2 the characteristic product is
(t-(d^2/2-d-2))(t+2)-d^2=d^2(d/2-1)>=0, and t exceeds both diagonal
entries. On the orthogonal complement, P_AB+P_AC=0 and swap-sum norm<=2;
the remaining tops are <=d and <=d-2. These are bounded by (15). The
d=2 even traceless subspace is zero. All vector subspaces above and their
orthogonal complements are invariant under the two star operators.

Consequently every self-compatible channel covariant under the compact
symplectic group USp(d) has both nontrivial harmonic-sector eigenvalues
<= (d+2)/(2(d+1)); the uniform-pure-state canonical EB channel has
eigenvalue 1/(d+1) on both sectors and gives full-domain C=2. This is a
second multiplicity-free family elimination; it does not imply the
individual SU(2) harmonic bounds, since USp(d) merges many harmonics.
