## Mechanical realization and work

Use local strain e and positive effective moduli C and K:

f(q,e)=f0(q)-hq+C(e-alpha q^2)^2/2+K e^2/2.

Eliminating e gives e*=C alpha q^2/(C+K),
u(K)=2 alpha^2 C K/(C+K), and
partial_K f_eff=e*^2/2=(q^4/4) du/dK.

The gate saturates at u_max=2 C alpha^2. A requested U<u_max needs
K/C=U/(u_max-U). A finite preload changes the quadratic coefficient and must
be included; pure quartic control is not automatic.

At fixed temperature the bare material-plus-shunt reversible work is
h dq+(q^4/4)du. Stage 2 supplies U qB^4/4; stage 3 returns the same amount.
Net work is zero only for the stipulated equal-free-energy, quasistatic endpoints
with ideal recovery. One phase-conversion stroke is not a complete thermodynamic
cycle. At coexistence latent heat is balanced by internal-energy change. Finite
thermal fluctuations and kinetic/control losses are not included in this formula.
