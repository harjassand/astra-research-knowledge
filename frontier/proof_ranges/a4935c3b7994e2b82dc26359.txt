4. The infinite domain can be solved and verified without truncating its tail
For a shifted geometric sequence
\[
u_{q,a}(g)=1_{g\ge a}q^{g-a},
\]
the exact overlap is
\[
\boxed{
\langle u_{q,a},u_{r,b}\rangle
=
\frac{q^{c-a}r^{c-b}}{1-qr},
\qquad c=\max(a,b).
}
\]
Products give separated-field overlaps. Tensor-train overlaps are finite products of small transfer matrices. Thus norms, Gram matrices and complete residuals are evaluated over the whole infinite lattice using finite rational arithmetic.
Finite-range quantum commutators also remain computable. They move Majorana endpoints and distinguish a finite number of near-contact cases. A long gap is retained as a shifted functional argument, not visited site by site.
The implemented algebra retains nonzero nearest- and next-nearest-neighbour diagonal interactions and all generated commutator terms. It does not truncate outputs merely because they leave the trial dictionary.
Certifying the minimum-energy projection
Let \(\nabla\) be the graph gradient and \(\partial=\nabla^*\). Given a source \(b=\partial F\), let \(g_*\) be the gradient component of \(F\), and define
\[
E_b=\|g_*\|^2.
\]
For any potential \(\phi\) and any explicitly constructed flow \(w\) satisfying \(\partial w=b\),
\[
\boxed{
2\langle b,\phi\rangle-\|\nabla\phi\|^2
\le E_b\le\|w\|^2.
}
\]
Their gap is exactly
\[
\boxed{
\Gamma=\|w-\nabla\phi\|^2.
}
\]
Moreover,
\[
\boxed{
F-w\text{ is exactly balanced},\qquad
\|F-w-\Pi F\|\le\sqrt{\Gamma}.
}
\]
This converts a feasible compressed repair into an accuracy-certified approximation of the orthogonal balance projection, without an inverse spectral-gap constant.
The solver starts with a feasible flow, adds explicitly divergence-free cycle fields, and optimizes finite potential and cycle dictionaries. Final coefficients are rounded, and the full infinite residual is recomputed exactly. A poor dictionary returns a wide interval rather than an unsupported prediction.
Executed result on the actual current source
For \(b=\delta_{(0,1)}-\delta_{(1,0)}\), the calculation used geometric tails together with a short local basis. It produced
\[
0.42510190863487\le E_b\le0.42510190864763,
\]
with exact width
\[
\Gamma\approx1.2751786347077035\times10^{-11}.
\]
Thus the balanced projection is approximated with norm error at most
\[
\boxed{3.571\times10^{-6}}
\]
in the normalized edge metric. The energy interval’s \(10^{-11}\) width must not be confused with \(10^{-11}\) vector accuracy.
The recorded computation used 136 potential variables, 306 cycle variables and 1,489 residual atoms. Discovery and exact checking took 5.121 seconds, with approximately 113.6 MiB peak memory, using one BLAS thread. Imports and research development are excluded from that timing.

5. A matched baseline prevents a false speedup claim
I implemented a compact sparse alternative for the same infinite-graph quantity. It includes all exterior residuals, so its intervals are also genuine infinite-domain certificates.
Representation	Potential / cycle variables	Certified interval width	Recorded time
Compressed geometric fields	136 / 306	\(1.2752\times10^{-11}\)	5.121 s
Compact square, radius 64	4,225 / 8,450	\(8.0127\times10^{-11}\)	0.051 s
Compact square, radius 128	16,641 / 33,282	\(1.3371\times10^{-12}\)	0.227 s


The sparse method wins on runtime in this two-gap example. The compressed representation is much smaller, but dense rational contraction costs dominate at this dimension.
This is not evidence that the compressed method will automatically win at higher degree. It establishes that an actual non-exponentially-local current projection admits a small, exactly certifiable infinite representation. Higher-degree performance still requires testing and analysis.
