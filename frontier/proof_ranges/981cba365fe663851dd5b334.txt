## 5. Infinite geometry is contracted, not truncated

For a shifted geometric sequence

\[
u_{q,a}(g)=1_{g\ge a}q^{g-a},\quad |q|<1,
\]

q=0 denotes a point mass at a. Its exact overlap is

\[
\boxed{\langle u_{q,a},u_{r,b}\rangle=
\frac{q^{c-a}r^{c-b}}{1-qr},\quad c=\max(a,b).}
\tag{10}
\]

For separated atoms, multiply one-dimensional overlaps. For matrix-geometric TT cores, form the finite transfer matrices

\[
E_i=\sum_{p,q}\frac{\overline{A_{ip}}\otimes A'_{iq}}{1-\overline{q_{ip}}q'_{iq}}.
\tag{11}
\]

The ordered product E_1...E_m is the full infinite inner product. Real rational data give rational answers. These are infinite sums, not finite samples plus a guessed tail.

For at most s poles and bond dimension R, a safe dense arithmetic count for all routed component norms is O(m^2s^2R^6), with corresponding polynomial storage. This is intentionally conservative. Numerator/denominator growth, close-to-one poles and finite-precision errors are separately paid. The implementation uses exact fractions for final certificates. It does not declare ill-conditioned numerical discovery trustworthy.

The prototype explicitly expands finite prefix windows arising from delayed point masses; their lengths count as input complexity. It does not claim logarithmic cost in an arbitrarily large delay when exact q^a fractions themselves have O(a) bits. Long geometric decay lengths, in contrast, are represented by the rational pole and do not require visiting that many coordinates.

### 5.1 Exact quantum commutators in the same algebra

A Majorana atom records its ordered site types (a, b, or paired ab) and a separated or matrix-product gap function. Hopping moves one Majorana endpoint. A diagonal commutator must meet a singly occupied site, which remains present; all other changes lie within the Hamiltonian range of that site.

Partition each relevant gap into its finitely many near-contact values and a far region. Within a branch, the action only shifts coordinates, inserts fixed short gaps, or merges a short gap with a free gap. It never needs to enumerate a long gap. The number of branches is bounded by C_range^N at fixed interaction range. Every independent unbounded input gap survives in exactly one output gap within the local branches used by the implementation. Local degree changes remain fully present.

`gap_algebra.py` implements this contact branching in exact Majorana arithmetic for finite separated geometric fields. It regenerates all commutator outputs; none outside a trial dictionary is discarded. The TT extension uses the same finite coordinate restrictions and shifts, with their rank and pole-count growth charged. Repeated applications can still grow rank: this is precisely the unresolved problem, not an omitted cost.

### 5.2 Nonlocal fields are valid transport witnesses

For odd f with exact PAf=0 and even g, use the original balanced expressions L(f),U(g). They remain valid for the finite-degree geometric fields above, and

\[
U-L=\|Sf-j+Ag\|_{S^+}^2+\|Sg+Af\|_{S^+}^2.
\tag{12}
\]

A direct approximation proof avoids assuming exponential locality of the true response. Each represented field has exponentially summable coefficients. Truncate a balanced field at gap radius R; its defect is finite, mean zero, and exponentially small up to a polynomial factor in R. Route that defect to a finite root using legal paths of polynomial length. The correction norm is bounded by path length times the defect l1 norm, hence tends to zero exponentially up to a polynomial. The repaired compact field is exactly balanced and converges in the fixed-degree commutator graph norm. Truncate g directly. The finite-packet inequalities and exact residual identity pass to the limit.

Thus the computed nonlocal certificate bounds the original infinite-system D through the already reconstructed finite-packet macroscopic transfer. The true pressure can have algebraic tails; a geometric trial's certified error accounts for the mismatch. No false exponential-tail premise is used.

Fixed-packet parameter-quadratic lower/upper design certificates likewise remain valid. No new parameter-design benchmark was executed here.
