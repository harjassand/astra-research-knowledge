# Independent reconstruction: integer block gluing and exact compressed filler

Date: 2026-10-11. Status: complete internal independent derivation, subsequently compared with `../consequence_clone/block_marginal_finite_population.md`; no gap found in its fixed-block proof. Parent requested this final substantive gate. No source/report edits or additional literature campaign were made. This proof concerns overlapping unconditional block marginals, not conditional stochastic kernels.

## 1. Exact interface

Let S be a deterministic automaton state set, d=|S|, initial state s0, and f:S x A -> S. Supply an integer k>=1 and a rational probability table kappa on A^k. For k>=2, write h for histories of length ell=k-1 and require stationarity of the overlap table:

    lambda(h)=sum_a kappa(h a)=sum_a kappa(a h).

For k=1 the unique history is the empty word and lambda(empty)=1. Write

    kappa(b)=u_b/M,  lambda(h)=v_h/M,
    v_h=sum_a u_(ha)=sum_a u_(ah),
    u_*=min{u_b:u_b>0}.

Zero blocks are allowed. Histories with v_h=0 can be deleted. Let B be the number of supported histories. Every positive v_h is at least u_*.

For n>=k, an admissible input law prescribes Law(X_t,...,X_(t+k-1))=kappa for every window. The initial ell-prefix law is then lambda. No independence from the automaton state, conditional Markov law, or restriction on long-range correlations is imposed. The conclusion below uses exactly this interface. Lengths n<k require their own boundary convention and are not silently included.

## 2. Whole-path rounding and the initial prefix

Take a population P>=1. An augmented automaton state is (s,h), with update

    (s,h) --a--> (f(s,a),shift(h,a)).

Only blocks h a with u_(ha)>0 are permitted. Normal histogram vertices x have total P on S x supp(lambda). A normal leaf edge e=(x,z,y) has integer allocation z(s,h,a), source rows x, destination histogram y, no forbidden blocks, and budgets

    |V_e(h,a)-P u_(ha)/M|<=d,
    V_e(h,a)=sum_s z(s,h,a).

There are d terms in each block count, despite dB augmented states.

For an actual admissible length-n law, form its P-scaled flow after the first ell letters. Add source edges of values P lambda(h) entering (f*(s0,h),h). At the end, send every augmented state (s,h) to an original-state collector s, and send collector s to the sink with value P p(s), where p is the actual original terminal law. All node balances are integer. Floor/ceiling integral-flow rounding, proved in `FINITE_POPULATION_THEOREM.md`, produces P paths.

Each source-history edge is rounded within its own floor/ceiling interval. Thus the initial normal counts K_h obey

    floor(P lambda(h))<=K_h<=ceil(P lambda(h)),
    sum_h K_h=P,
    x0(s,h)=K_h if s=f*(s0,h), and zero otherwise.

In particular K_h=0 when lambda(h)=0. The weaker condition |K_h-P lambda(h)|<=1 is sufficient on positive histories but must not by itself replace this zero-support clause.

Each block count differs by less than d, and zero-flow block edges stay zero. Each original-state collector edge is rounded within one, so the normal terminal empirical law g obeys

    TV(p,g)<=d/(2P).                                    (1)

The original d, not dB, appears in (1). The source-prefix edges and terminal collectors are both load-bearing: they make the initial word prefix legitimate and retain the smaller original terminal error.

## 3. Residual integer tables

Put

    L=P u_*+dM,
    beta=dM/L,
    F=dM^2.

For each leaf e define

    C_e(h,a)=L u_(ha)-M u_* V_e(h,a),
    R_x(h)=L v_h-M u_* sum_s x(s,h).

All these are integers. For a positive block, the budget gives

    C_e(h,a)>=dM(u_(ha)-u_*)>=0.

For a zero block, both u_(ha) and V_e(h,a) are zero, hence C_e(h,a)=0. The exact conservation identities are

    sum_a C_e(h,a)=R_x(h),                              (2)
    sum_(h,a):shift(h,a)=h' C_e(h,a)=R_y(h'),            (3)
    sum_(h,a) C_e(h,a)=sum_h R_x(h)=F.                  (4)

Equation (3) uses equality of the prefix and suffix marginals of kappa. Original-state transitions disappear after summing over s, so no extra condition on f is needed.

At an initial normal histogram, positive histories satisfy K_h<=P v_h/M+1 and therefore

    R_x0(h)>=M(d v_h-u_*)>=0.

Zero histories have K_h=0 and R_x0(h)=0. At later graph vertices, nonnegativity follows directly from (2)-(3). Thus every vertex used by a positive-length witness has a legitimate F-slot residual history partition. Vertices with a negative R can simply be excluded; they are not used by these witnesses.

The probability tables C_e/F are exactly the residual clique tables

    [kappa-(1-beta) kappa'_e]/beta,

where kappa'_e(h,a)=V_e(h,a)/P. Their prefix and suffix tables are R_x/F and R_y/F. In particular adjacent residual clique tables agree on their overlap, with one common denominator F independent of n.

## 4. Integer gluing lemma, proved by slots

**Lemma.** Suppose a sequence of integer k-block tables c_t on A^k all has total F, and the suffix-(k-1) count table of c_t equals the prefix-(k-1) count table of c_(t+1). Then F deterministic words, counted with multiplicity, realize all c_t/F simultaneously.

**Proof.** At each layer t, partition a common ordered slot set [F] into history blocks of the corresponding overlap counts. For each table c_t, partition every source history block h into letter bins of sizes c_t(h,a). Assign that letter to the source slots in its bin. Match, within each destination history h'=shift(h,a), all incoming source slots bijectively to the destination history block. Matching is possible because the incoming and destination counts agree. This gives a permutation pi_t of [F]. Start one word at each initial slot, using its history as the initial prefix, and emit the assigned letters while following the permutations. Inductively its current history is the actual suffix of its word. Every slot is used exactly once at every layer, so the t-th block counts equal c_t. QED.

This is a constructive integer version of chain junction-tree gluing. It uses no independent Markov draws. It applies even if the residual tables vary with t and have zeros.

Applying the lemma to C_e along a normal histogram graph path gives F filler words. Canonical source and destination history partitions make each leaf a fixed pair of maps

    a_e:[F]->A,   pi_e:[F]->[F],

with pi_e a permutation and

    h_x(i) -> h_y(pi_e(i))=shift(h_x(i),a_e(i)).          (5)

A single uniform initial slot remains uniform at every leaf. Its initial prefix has law R_x0/F, and (5) proves that every later block has the required residual table C_e/F. Repeated use of the same edge deliberately reuses the same letter and permutation maps; this is allowed long-range dependence.

## 5. Exact complete law and its denominator

The P normal words have weight (1-beta)/P=u_*/L each; the F filler words have weight beta/F=1/(ML) each. Therefore the repaired law is a uniform population of

    R=ML=M(Pu_*+dM)

words counted with multiplicity: M u_* copies of each normal word, and one of each filler word. Its count of block h a at leaf e is

    M u_* V_e(h,a)+C_e(h,a)=L u_(ha)=R kappa(h a).

Its initial prefix counts likewise equal

    M u_* K_h+R_x0(h)=L v_h=R lambda(h).

Thus every prescribed window and the initial prefix are exact, and every zero-probability block is absent from every sampled word. Let p' be its original terminal law. Since the filler has total mass beta,

    TV(p',g)<=beta,
    TV(p',p)<=d/(2P)+dM/(P u_*+dM).                    (6)

Both bounds are uniform in n. No full-joint-law approximation is claimed. Choosing P=ceil(2dM/(u_* epsilon)) makes (6) at most 3epsilon/4.

The exact seed denominator satisfies

    R<=dM^2(2/epsilon+1)+M u_*.

Its bit length is independent of n. Sampling the uniform R-seed by binary rejection uses O(log R) fair bits in expectation; this does not bound the worst-case number of flips for non-dyadic probabilities.

## 6. The compressed map must carry the original state

For each filler leaf, define the TOTAL map

    T_e:[F] x S -> [F] x S,
    T_e(i,s)=(pi_e(i),f(s,a_e(i))).                     (7)

This is defined for all original states s. It need not be invertible on [F] x S; only the slot coordinate is a permutation. At a concatenation node with earlier left child and later right child, compose T_right after T_left. The endpoint residual history partitions coincide because they are functions of the shared normal histogram vertex. Thus (5) remains valid after composition. The original state coordinate retains any dependence on the earlier input prefix.

This proves the crucial repeated-DAG-node gate: the same node may be entered with different original states, but (7) is a total state map and is valid for all of them. Entering slot i always specifies the canonical source history, regardless of the earlier path. No state/history correlation is discarded.

For a uniform initial filler slot i, let h0(i) be its initial history. Initialize the original state to f*(s0,h0(i)), then apply the root map. Enumerating the F initial slots gives the exact filler terminal counts. Mixing the normal and filler counts gives the exact repaired terminal law with denominator R. One does not multiply a sequence of rational conditional probabilities.

For a random-access input query, retain the sampled initial slot. A query in positions 1,...,ell reads h0(i). For a later position, descend the DAG to its leaf, applying the stored slot permutation whenever skipping a whole earlier node. Output the leaf letter at the resulting slot. Repeated or adaptive queries belong to the same sampled deterministic word. The terminal state computed by (7) is consistent with those answers.

The normal branch is handled by analogous P-slot permutations on canonical augmented-state blocks. Its initial slot has prefix h and state f*(s0,h). The full uniform R-seed selects a normal slot, with multiplicity M u_*, or one filler slot.

## 7. Explicit resource dependence

There are at most

    H=binom(P+dB-1,dB-1)

normal histogram vertices. If r is the number of positive k-block cells, edge allocations have at most dr nonzero cells. Enumerating all weak compositions of P into those cells examines at most binom(P+dr-1,dr-1) arrays, and each determines its source and destination histograms. Budget comparisons use exact rational arithmetic in the supplied table bit lengths. Unsupported histories and blocks are deleted exactly.

A Boolean-power witness DAG for tail length m=n-ell has W=O(H^2 log(m+1)) distinct nodes and depth O(log(m+1)). Canonical prefixes f*(s0,h) cost O(B ell) automaton transitions to precompute. Filler leaf construction and map composition require O(FdW) table operations up to ordinary alphabet/history preprocessing; each entry stores a slot index and original state. Normal maps use O(PW) slot entries. Exact terminal counting adds O(F+P+d) operations after the maps are available. Coordinate queries use O(log(m+1)) table lookups plus ordinary index and history-access costs. Whole-word output still costs Omega(n).

The charge F=dM^2 is essential. The explicit array implementation is polynomial in F,H,P,d,log n and table sizes, but can be exponential in the binary length of the common denominator M, even when the smallest positive kappa atom is bounded below. A small-bit rational seed is not by itself a small-bit compilation algorithm. No analogue of the k=1 interval-partition optimization has been proved here for arbitrary overlapping blocks; permutations can move the seed between different history partitions.

Uniform terminal optimization via the relaxed graph needs rational arithmetic, without enumerating F. Its binary-horizon and arbitrary-horizon error guarantees therefore retain their graph-level resource bounds. The stronger exact random-access witness and terminal-law compiler must additionally pay for F.

## 8. Scope tests and failure examples

**Zero-block exclusion is necessary.** With d=1, P=2 and kappa(00)=kappa(11)=1/2, the normal two-word edge containing blocks 01 and 11 satisfies absolute discrepancy at most one in every block cell. If the forbidden zero cell 01 is allowed, its residual count is negative. Thus budgets alone do not replace the requirement that every zero target block has zero normal count.

**Overlap consistency is necessary.** A target table with kappa(01)=1 has prefix delta_0 and suffix delta_1. Two consecutive windows cannot both have that table, since their shared letter would have to be both 1 and 0. The column identity (3) is exactly where the stationary overlap condition is used.

**Block marginals are weaker than a conditional Markov promise.** Let k=2 and kappa be uniform on the four binary pairs. The length-three law uniform on 000,010,101,111 has both consecutive pair marginals kappa, but X3=X1 deterministically. Conditional on the full past (or a DFA state remembering X1), the next bit is not a fresh fair coin. The construction proved here is valid for block laws and does not enforce a prescribed conditional transition kernel.
