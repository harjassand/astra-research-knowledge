## 1. One common physical experiment

Let rho be the fixed input density operator. Include initial motion, the physical
bath and any purification reference in the input description. V_A and V_B are
isometries into the SAME final space describing the two actual controlled
histories, with a fixed relative phase reference. The ideal controlled dilation
is |A><A| tensor V_A + |B><B| tensor V_B; the label starts in |+>.

Let P be one common survival/acceptance projector on the final non-label space.
The survival flag and final label X/Y measurement commute. A physical instrument
can use a Naimark space, but that common instrument is additional supplied
structure, not something determined by two channel marginals. Define

    p_j = Tr(P V_j rho V_j†),    q_j=1-p_j,
    z_s = Tr(P V_A rho V_B†),
    z_l = Tr((I-P) V_A rho V_B†),
    L = Tr(V_A rho V_B†) = z_s+z_l,
    S = (p_A+p_B)/2,    Q=1-S.

These are probabilities and complex amplitudes for the same input/instrument.
They are dimensionless. Branch channels calibrated with an unrelated bath
distribution or different reset cannot silently supply these quantities.

The probability of acceptance is S. For S>0 the accepted label coherence is

    m = 2 z_s/(p_A+p_B) = z_s/S.                         (1)

Thus accepted fringe contrast is not the unconditional overlap. In particular,
|m| <= 2 sqrt(p_A p_B)/(p_A+p_B); unequal survival alone can reduce it.
