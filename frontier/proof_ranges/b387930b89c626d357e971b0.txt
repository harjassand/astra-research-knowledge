1. Complete theorem on the negative-delta half of the contact face
----------------------------------------------------------------
For A=[[a,b],[c,d]], set

  u=a^2+c^2, j=ad-bc,
  H=((a-d)^2+(b+c)^2)^2,
  F=alpha H+beta u^2+gamma j^2+delta u j.

For EVERY real alpha,beta,gamma,delta with delta<=0, the following are
equivalent:

  F is rank-one convex;
  F is quasiconvex at every affine background;
  P=[[6beta+delta,delta],[delta,gamma]] is PSD and
  Q=[[8alpha,delta],[delta,2beta+delta]] is PSD.

The quasiconvexity contract is every bounded planar Lipschitz domain and
every W0^(1,4) perturbation. The periodic analogue also holds. The coefficient
space is the exposed acoustic-contact face inside the nine-dimensional
left-SO(2)-invariant quartic space; this is not the general planar Morrey
implication. The delta>0 half remains outside this theorem.

For delta<0, the two PSD conditions are equivalently

  2beta+delta>0,
  gamma >= delta^2/(6beta+delta),
  alpha >= delta^2/[8(2beta+delta)].

Here 2beta+delta>0 already implies 6beta+delta>0 for delta<0. These formulas
characterize the entire negative-delta domain, not a compact r interval or a
fixed saturated-beta slice. At delta=0 the conditions are simply
alpha,beta,gamma>=0.

2. Necessity and the exact one-generator operation
-------------------------------------------------
The full necessity proof is independently derived in immutable REPORT.md,
Sections 3–4, and checked in FACE_EXACT_RECEIPT.json. It is short enough to
recall its decisive points.

Target rotation reduces rank-one directions to e1 tensor (n,m). A conformal
acoustic limit gives P>=0. At A=[[0,2x-y],[y,0]] in direction e11 the exact
acoustic form is

  2[8alpha x^2-2delta xy+(2beta+delta)y^2],

forcing Q>=0. These are direct acoustic tests, not Gram infeasibility claims.
Quasiconvexity implies rank-one convexity, so they are necessary for both.

For delta<0 and P,Q>=0 put

  p=6beta+delta,
  rho=p/delta,
  lambda=delta^2/p,
  mu=gamma-lambda,
  nu=alpha-delta^2/[8(2beta+delta)].

P,Q and delta!=0 force p>0, 2beta+delta>0, lambda>0, mu>=0, nu>=0. Moreover

  rho+2=3(2beta+delta)/delta<0,

so rho<-2. The exact coefficient identity is

  F=lambda J_rho+mu j^2+nu H,

  J_r=3r/[8(r+2)] H+r(r-1)u^2/6+j^2+r u j.

The newly checked universal certificate proves J_rho quasiconvex for every
rho<-2. The determinant square is polyconvex and H is convex, proving
sufficiency. At delta=0 the nonnegative three-generator combination is
immediate. This closes the full negative-delta theorem.

Conversely the parametrization with lambda>0, rho<-2, mu,nu>=0 maps into the
PSD cone explicitly:

  P=lambda (rho,1)^T(rho,1)+diag(0,mu),
  Q=lambda*rho/[3(rho+2)] (3,rho+2)^T(3,rho+2)+diag(8nu,0).

Their determinants are lambda*mu*rho^2 and
