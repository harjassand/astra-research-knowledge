\[
T\!\left(\mathcal B_a(|\psi_i\rangle\langle\psi_i|),
       |\psi_i\rangle\langle\psi_i|\right)\le b
\quad (a=1,2;\ i=1,\dots,n),
\]
where \(T=\|\cdot\|_1/2\).

Take a Stinespring isometry \(V:A\to A_1A_2R\) and set \(|\Omega_i\rangle=V|\psi_i\rangle\). For \(P_i=|\psi_i\rangle\langle\psi_i|\), trace-distance/fidelity duality gives

\[
\langle\Omega_i|P_i^{A_1}\otimes I|\Omega_i\rangle\ge1-b,
\qquad
\langle\Omega_i|I\otimes P_i^{A_2}|\Omega_i\rangle\ge1-b.
\]

Since \(I-P_i\otimes P_i\le(I-P_i)\otimes I+I\otimes(I-P_i)\),

\[
q_i:=\langle\Omega_i|P_i^{A_1}\otimes P_i^{A_2}\otimes I_R|\Omega_i\rangle\ge1-2b.
\]

For \(b<1/2\), project \(|\Omega_i\rangle\) onto \(|\psi_i\rangle^{\otimes2}\otimes R\), normalize the resulting environment vector to \(|e_i\rangle\), and fix its phase so the overlap is nonnegative. Then

\[
\big\|\,|\Omega_i\rangle-|\psi_i\rangle^{\otimes2}|e_i\rangle\,\big\|_2^2
=2(1-\sqrt{q_i})\le4b.
\]

Define linear maps from \(\mathbb C^n\):

\[
W|i\rangle=\sqrt{p_i}\,V|\psi_i\rangle,
\qquad
S|i\rangle=\sqrt{p_i}\,|\psi_i\rangle^{\otimes2}|e_i\rangle.
\]

Then \(\|W-S\|_2\le2\sqrt b\), while \(\|W\|_2=\|S\|_2=1\). Their Gram density matrices obey

\[
G=W^\dagger W,\qquad K=S^\dagger S,
\]

with entries

\[
G_{ij}=\sqrt{p_ip_j}\langle\psi_i|\psi_j\rangle,
\qquad
K_{ij}=\sqrt{p_ip_j}\langle\psi_i|\psi_j\rangle^2\langle e_i|e_j\rangle.
\]

Schatten Hölder gives the cardinality- and dimension-free estimate

\[
\boxed{\ \|G-K\|_1
=\|W^\dagger W-S^\dagger S\|_1
\le(\|W\|_2+\|S\|_2)\|W-S\|_2
\le4\sqrt b.\ }
\]

Unlike a pairwise overlap bound, this is a trace-norm statement about the entire prior-weighted Gram matrix. It detects overcomplete frames: many individually small overlap errors may still produce a constant trace-norm gap. The exact fixed-point equation \(G=K\) forces every nonzero overlap to have modulus one (because \(|\langle e_i|e_j\rangle|\le1\)); hence the pure states fall into identical-state/orthogonal-class blocks. This is the exact no-cloning geometry in Gram form.

### Missing stability step

A useful sufficient lemma would be:

> For every \(\varepsilon\), there exists \(f(\varepsilon)\to0\), independent of dimension, finite cardinality, and prior, such that whenever \(G_{ij}=\sqrt{p_ip_j}\langle\psi_i|\psi_j\rangle\) and \(K_{ij}=G_{ij}\langle\psi_i|\psi_j\rangle\langle e_i|e_j\rangle\) with \((\langle e_i|e_j\rangle)\) a correlation matrix and \(\|G-K\|_1\le\varepsilon\), there exists one EB channel \(\Lambda\) with \(\sum_i p_iT(\Lambda(P_i),P_i)\le f(\varepsilon)\).

If true, the broadcaster identity yields a prior-average EB reconstruction for every finite prior. For a finite family, minimax applies because trace-distance loss is convex in \(\Lambda\) and linear in the prior; it upgrades prior-average reconstruction to one channel with uniform error. For an infinite family in a fixed finite-dimensional space, compactness of the EB channel set plus the finite-intersection property extends the result. This would settle the pure-state capacity-free case with a dimension-free modulus. I have not proved the stability lemma. The hard issue is the global trace-norm stability of approximate fixed points of the Schur map \(G\mapsto G\circ C\circ H\); entrywise or Hilbert–Schmidt estimates alone do not control overcomplete frames.
