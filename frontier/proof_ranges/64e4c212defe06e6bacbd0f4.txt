## 7. A connected fourth response is not an anharmonicity certificate

Let half the specimens/rotational sectors be harmonic Gaussian systems with
control variance 1 and half with variance 3. Then

    L(epsilon)=.5 exp(-epsilon²/2)+.5 exp(-3epsilon²/2)
              =exp(-epsilon²) cosh(epsilon²/2),
    log L=-epsilon²+epsilon⁴/8+O(epsilon⁸).

Its fourth derivative at zero is 3, although every conditional sector is
harmonic and Gaussian. More generally the derivative is 3 Var(variance).
The source explicitly warns about mixtures; this is an exact falsifier of any
later promotion from nonzero fourth connected response alone to intrinsic
anharmonicity or useful complex chemistry.
