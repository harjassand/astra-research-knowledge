## 8. Primary comparators and useful next research

[Bandyopadhyay et al., Conclusive Exclusion of Quantum States](https://arxiv.org/html/1306.4683v3),
Sections II, III and VII.2, already provides state exclusion, arbitrary subsets,
SDPs/duals and worst-case variants. The anti-trine and generic loss-to-measurement
optimization cannot be promoted as new. The source's candidate distinction is
its paid physical loss construction, realistic nuisance/error interface and
hard-exposure comparison. Priority of that combination remains UNKNOWN.

[Acin, Statistical distinguishability between unitary operations](https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0102064v2)
is a primary comparator for unitary discrimination. The adaptive/noisy angular
argument above is reconstructed directly rather than inferred from an abstract.
[Huang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03030) supplies a strong controlled
many-body learning comparator; it prevents any general claim that model learning
must use full tomography or exponential time.

[Zhou et al., QEC-enhanced metrology](https://arxiv.org/html/1706.02445v2),
the code conditions in Section 0.5 and the SDP in Section 0.14, already develops
codes protecting against noise while retaining a signal, under fast controls
and suitable ancillas. The local density guard construction here retains physical
dephasing and concerns compressed local input plus a finite exclusion loss; it
does not establish a new QEC principle or outperform their metrological theorem.

[Mortimer et al., certified steady-state observables](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.13646v2)
already supplies scalable moment relaxations and stationary-observable bounds.
It is the strongest concrete next payoff-certificate comparator. A worthwhile
new experiment would combine a guarded probe with certified action differences
on an extended interacting device, comparing total loss acquisition/construction
cost with these moment methods and strong decision-aware sensing. Current
certificates must prove a nonvanishing action margin; a small measurement SDP
does not produce that margin by itself.

For the complexity premise, the classical primary reference is
[Karp, Reducibility among Combinatorial Problems (1972)](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4684-2001-2_9).
Primary full-text retrieval attempts were unavailable in this run. Exact Cover's
standard NP-completeness is the named imported premise; the sensor reduction
itself is fully written above and exhaustively checked on small incidence inputs.
No priority claim is made for this local-code hardness formulation.

The deepest useful synthesis is therefore an explicit boundary: local nuisance
rejection can be constructively cheap even in an extended finite-density device;
generic code search can already contain Exact Cover; and neither fact supplies
the large-system physical action-loss table. N771's objective-specific defect
criterion and the detuning theorem share a design strategy, but their distinct
algebras and acquired inputs must not be interchanged. Existing architecture can
store these proofs, conditional interfaces and failures without a new agent role
or generic orchestration layer.
