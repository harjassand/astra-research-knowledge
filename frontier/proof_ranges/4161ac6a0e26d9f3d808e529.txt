## Intervention compatibility and retained-reward mapping

The source physical ledger calls for testing carrier-gated reaction selection on one mapped specimen and actual platform, with dark/constant-drive/dose-matched controls and heating, drift, beam and mechanical work measured. The closest concrete pilot is the supplied ZnS/GaP optical-glide platform: the v2 report uses 20 nm ZnS films, a 365 nm LED, ECCI in an environmental SEM, 350 °C anneal cycles, cooling to room temperature for same-location imaging, eight micrographs across four light levels, and 148 counted misfit lines. It analyzes only the longest dislocations for the carrier-mobility comparison because pinning biases glide. Those are useful acquisition and selection facts, not a qualified repair result: the endpoint survey must retain all predeclared target classes, follow boundary motion, and measure fault/strain/function, rather than keep only the longest lines. It specifically warns that this platform does not establish a room-temperature electric-drive/optical-lock combination. The proposed certificate therefore does not assume a universal temperature or conflate ZnS, GaAs/Si, SiC, and SrTiO3 results.

Use the ECCI same-location/low-drift setup as the first structural screen for this thin-film platform, then acquire the costlier multi-\(g\) target line/fault assay only for registered candidates and their guard collars. For a growth-engineering comparator, the credited forward-graded ZnSSe/GaAs work uses high-resolution X-ray diffraction and asymmetric dislocation-density/strain profiles; its overshoot interface promotes annihilation/coalescence at the population level. That is a meaningful growth/strain baseline, but rocking-curve density and optical quality do not assign a given line's Burgers vector or prove retained device benefit. Compare it as a distinct grown architecture, not as the same post-growth optical reaction experiment.

The current capital route N777 is compatible with the *measurement contract* (same specimen, legal controls, causal endpoint, and complete energy/damage ledger) but supplies no acquired dislocation-reaction control pair. N783 suggests a useful distinction between first repair and retained reward. The explicit mapping here is: configuration = registered defect network plus measured carrier/environment state; event = target reaction; immediate reward = newly qualified functional output; damage/cost = new defect/fault, plastic slip, and full intervention energy; retention horizon = the specified operating cycles after controls are removed. N783's Markov-reward inequalities do **not** transfer to the crystal: this task has no measured full-state transition kernel or conservative potential. Therefore the required output is a directly measured retained performance trajectory, not an imported committor guarantee.
