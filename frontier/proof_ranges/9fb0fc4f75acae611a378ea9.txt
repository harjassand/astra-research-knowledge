## 1. Research decision and decisive objective

Independent exploratory directions were general arithmetic circuit lower bounds, resolution of singularities in positive characteristic, rigorous critical three-dimensional Ising universality, and exponential sunflower bounds. Proposed operations were, respectively, cancellation-aware complexity accounting; a blowup invariant resistant to Frobenius-induced residual-order increases; controlled renormalisation preserving the observables needed for a scaling theorem; and correlated sampling that removes unnecessary independent-sampling losses. No general construction was established for the first three. The sunflower route produced an exact obstruction, recorded in Appendix A.

The selected mathematical objective emerged from investigating dependent coordinate representations:

> If N affine hyperplanes form an irredundant cover of F_p^d and their normal vectors span dimension r, prove r log p <= C N, with C absolute.

All logarithms in this note are natural. An irredundant cover covers every point and gives each hyperplane a private point covered by no other hyperplane. Write the normal vectors as the columns of A, and let W = ker A <= F_p^N. Then r = codim W and the objective is |F_p^N/W| <= exp(C N).

The attraction is not a small-parameter theorem: a successful operation would remove the entire logarithmic loss in this covering problem. This is nevertheless a narrower objective than general circuit lower bounds or resolution of singularities. No implications for those problems, or for a civilisation-changing technology, have been established.

### Prior mathematics and an important correction

Nagy–Pach–Tomon's 2021 precursor proves a log-log-loss covering bound through group-ring identities and arithmetic-set compression. Its mechanism constructs representatives from a product alphabet of size O(log p). The 2026 published paper *Hyperplane covers of finite spaces and applications* reports the corresponding Omega(r log p / log log p) hyperplane bound. The exact 2021 proofs were inspected; the 2026 publication and abstract were checked, but the full published PDF could not be retrieved in this run. [R1, R2]

Their distinct 2022 preprint *Irredundant hyperplane covers* was withdrawn. Its arXiv notice reports an error invalidating most of the paper. It cannot supply the desired exponential theorem. The reset-and-double coordinate chain used below already appears in that withdrawn argument: it is NOT claimed as a new invention. The proposed repair is an exact marginal-generator lift with a separately stated feasibility condition. [R3, R4]

## 2. Operation and its exact input contract

Let p be an odd prime, N >= 1, and W <= F_p^N. For x in (F_p^*)^N define the nonzero legal directions

    D(W,x) = (W intersect product_i {0, x_i, -x_i}) minus {0}.

For a direction w, let s(w) in {0,1}^N be its support indicator. Define the support cone

    C(W,x) = cone {s(w): w in D(W,x)}.

The usable hypothesis is the existence of a **common positive clock vector**:

    there exists r in R_{>0}^N such that
    r belongs to C(W,x) for every x in (F_p^*)^N.                 (CB)

Equivalently, for every x there are nonnegative coefficients lambda_{x,w} satisfying

    sum_w lambda_{x,w} 1_{w_i != 0} = r_i, for every i.          (1)

The r_i may differ between coordinates; they must not depend on the current state. The stronger unit-balance condition takes r = (1,...,1).

Conic feasibility, linear programming, and generator intertwining are established mathematical tools. Naming the cone does not constitute a new primitive. The candidate operation is the construction of a coset-preserving process with exactly prescribed scalar marginal generators, provided (CB) can actually be established from structural information.

### Complexity boundary

There are finitely many x, so choices of coefficients exist as ordinary finite mathematical data under (CB). A conic representation can be chosen with at most N directions at each x. However, enumerating states, legal directions, or all scaling classes can be exponential. No efficient direction oracle, polynomial-time LP acquisition, mixing-time bound, or efficient representative sampler is proved. This is a conditional existence theorem, not an efficient algorithm hidden behind a supplied oracle.
