## 8. Extension to many fixed changes of representation

Here is a general form of the obstruction. Let `U_1,...,U_M` be fixed, deterministic, real Hilbert-space isometries of Hermitian matrices, each fixing `I`. Define

\[
\mathcal K=\operatorname{conv}\bigcup_{j=1}^M U_j(S_k).
\]

The chaining proof applies to `Tr(G U_j(vv^*))`: the covariance bound and the increment norm are unchanged. Take `u=r+log(2M)` and a union bound. Except with probability at most `e^-r/2`,

\[
\sup_{X\in\mathcal K}\operatorname{Tr}(GX)
\le48\sqrt r+8\sqrt{\log(2M)}.
\tag{20}
\]

If

\[
k\le d/2^{26},\qquad \log(2M)\le d^2/2^{20},
\]

the right side is less than `d/36`, because

\[
48\sqrt{5/2^{26}}+8/\sqrt{2^{20}}<1/36.
\]

The same state construction and energy/operator bounds again give a PPT state at half trace distance at least `1/576` from `K`. This even permits a fixed exponentially large family of representation changes, under the explicit exponent bound. The family must be fixed before drawing `G`; adaptive choices depending on the target are outside the theorem. Arbitrary non-isometric changes, local filtering chosen from the target, and unlimited continuous families are also outside its scope.

This width mechanism is an elementary extension of established convex geometry. Its exact formulation and priority need specialist comparison; it is not presented as a transformative new theory.
