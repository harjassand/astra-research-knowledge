## 7. Resource accounting and the exact remaining gap

The distinction between established operations and missing capabilities is sharp.

| Component | What is actually available |
|---|---|
| Input | Explicit graph and binary-encoded rational edge weights |
| Matching verification | Check edge membership and that every vertex is covered exactly once |
| Positive lift | Exact identity (1), requiring no preprocessing |
| Compatible bipartition | Exactly \(n\) fair bits from a supplied matching |
| Conditional matching sample | Polynomial-time approximate sampling on an explicitly constructed bipartite graph |
| Ideal conditional chain | Exact stationary target, but exponential mixing already on cycles |
| Independent-cut estimator | Exact expectation identity, but exponential relative second moment |
| Small-gadget elimination | Exact rational operation; no exponentially precise cancellation |
| Uniform global-cycle proposal | Would imply \(\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{RP}\) under the stated interface |
| General compressed elimination or balanced global transport | Not established |

The bipartite conditionals do not conceal an oracle for general matching counts. Conversely, neither the polynomial \(P_G\) nor the marginal matrix \(R\) has been promoted into a fictitious efficient oracle.

The proof gap is also **not** merely a conjectured polynomial mixing time for the original sampler. That conjecture is false by (8)–(9). The simple global-walk repair also fails on the gadget family above. A further change in the mechanism is necessary.

The strongest surviving mathematical conclusions are:

\[
\sum_S Z_S=2^n Z_G,
\]

\[
\frac{\mathbb E Y^2}{Z_G^2}
=
\mathbb E_{M,N\sim\pi_G}2^{c(M,N)},
\]

\[
\operatorname{Cov}(X)=\frac14(I-R),
\]

and

\[
\operatorname{gap}(K_{C_{2n}})=2^{1-n}.
\]

Together they exhibit an explicit setting where positive representations, efficient conditional sampling, and favourable local covariance all coexist with exponential global sampling cost.

**No new polynomial-time capability for general perfect matching counting has been established here.** The exact positive lift and its obstructions are the retained results; they are not substitutes for the requested breakthrough. The selective prior-art search found the recent two-matroid algorithm, existing bipartite algorithms, and established general-graph chain barriers, but did not establish novelty for the retained calculations or supply the missing general transport mechanism.
