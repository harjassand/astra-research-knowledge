## New dimension bound and an exact same-record decision

The root first explored a partial-trace module norm, which introduced a product
interaction budget. The independent critic found a simpler maximal-eigenvector
argument. The physical worker and root reconstructed it independently. Retain
one nonnegative component of the mixed reset before using the source remainder:

    p_tau(t) >= t^2(1-Lambda t/2)^2 ||R||^2/(4d),
    0<t<2/Lambda, Lambda=2J_S+Rcap.

At t=1/Lambda the sufficient denominator is16 Lambda²d, improving the source's
d² under its original assumptions. A rank-one uncoupled residual gives failure
sin²(epsilon t/2)/d, so a dimension-independent null-readout certificate is
impossible in this interface. This is sharp dimension dependence for a fixed
mixed-reset/X-readout protocol, not a lower bound on all quantum algorithms.
Since d=2^ell, collar-spin scaling remains exponential.

The seven-failure retained-memory record is the important control. At its
original N=100million, t=1/50, d=8, Lambda=879/50 and w=1/5, exact binomial
domination with q_up=161/10^9 gives interval[29/500,171/500] at assumed
gamma=1e-8, and[3/125,47/125] at assumed gamma=1e-7, with99% confidence.
Both are positive. The original bound was inconclusive even at gamma=0.
The proofs and two independently written rational checkers are retained.
The count generator remains numerical simulation, and its physical trial
count is not the simulator runtime. No hardware error budget was measured.
