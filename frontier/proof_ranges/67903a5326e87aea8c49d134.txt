## 3. The exact endpoint certificate

In the remainder of this section put \(\ell=\kappa\). Choose

\[
\begin{aligned}
t_0&=\frac{254064\kappa^4-12066397\kappa^2+87457992}{107712},\\
t_1&=\frac{\kappa(2551152\kappa^4-120422989\kappa^2+869186184)}{4582656},\\
t_2&=-\frac{\kappa(30008208\kappa^4-1412052067\kappa^2+10194361080)}{50409216},\\
t_3&=\frac{\kappa(2383152\kappa^4-111980897\kappa^2+796906920)}{3877632},\\
t_4&=\frac{64560\kappa^4-3046321\kappa^2+22009128}{71808},\\
t_5&=-\frac{641424\kappa^4-30344323\kappa^2+215742840}{646272}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Let \(S_*=S(\kappa,t)\). Partition it into a top-left \(2\times2\) block \(C\), top-right block \(B\), and bottom-right \(4\times4\) block \(T\):

\[
 S_*=\begin{pmatrix}C&B\\B^T&T\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 T=\begin{pmatrix}
 t_0&t_1&t_2&t_3\\
 t_1&11-2t_5&1-t_4&t_4\\
 t_2&1-t_4&7&t_5\\
 t_3&t_4&t_5&4
 \end{pmatrix}.
\]

### Lemma C

The matrix \(T\) is positive definite, and

\[
 (\det T)C=B\operatorname{adj}(T)B^T.
\]

Consequently \(S_*\succeq0\), with rank exactly four.

### Exact verification of Lemma C

Work in the degree-six number field \(\mathbb Q[\kappa]\), reducing polynomial expressions by \(m(\kappa)=0\). The four entries of the displayed Schur-complement identity reduce to zero.

For the leading principal minors \(\Delta_j=\det T_{1:j,1:j}\), exact rational Horner interval arithmetic on

\[
 I=\left[\frac{295538664457927}{10^{14}},
          \frac{295538664457928}{10^{14}}\right]
\]

gives the following enclosures. The decimal endpoints in this display are terminating rationals and are deliberately wider than the computed bounds:

\[
\begin{aligned}
13.44712&<\Delta_1<13.44713,\\
73.04076&<\Delta_2<73.04077,\\
58.26760&<\Delta_3<58.26761,\\
28.91777&<\Delta_4<28.91779.
\end{aligned}
\]

The interval \(I\) contains exactly one root of \(m\); a rational Sturm count also identifies it as the unique root in \((2.95,2.96)\). All four minors are strictly positive, so Sylvester's criterion gives \(T\succ0\). The Schur complement vanishes, hence

\[
 S_*=\begin{pmatrix}BT^{-1}\\I_4\end{pmatrix}
 T\begin{pmatrix}T^{-1}B^T&I_4\end{pmatrix}\succeq0.
\]
