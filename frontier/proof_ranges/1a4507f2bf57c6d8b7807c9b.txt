## 8. A fully charged route from projections to the interacting inverse

At degree <=N let W_N be the local unprojected skew operator and Pi the exact balance projection. No matrix for Pi is supplied. The compressed Hodge solver approximates its action with an explicit norm certificate (14).

Set

\[
h=(1+M_N^2)^{-1},\qquad \theta=\sqrt{1-h}.
\]

For balanced x_j, compute an exactly balanced q_j approximating Pi(z-W_N x_j) to error at most eta, then update

\[
x_{j+1}=(1-h)x_j+hq_j.
\tag{15}
\]

The ideal map contracts by theta, since for K=Pi W_N Pi,

\[
\|(1-h)e-hKe\|^2=(1-h)^2\|e\|^2+h^2\|Ke\|^2
\le(1-h)\|e\|^2.
\]

Consequently, starting at zero,

\[
\boxed{\|x_j-x_N\|\le\theta^j\|z\|+2\eta.}
\tag{16}
\]

Indeed the geometric error sum is h eta/(1-theta)=(1+theta)eta<=2eta. This controls O((1+M_N^2) log(1/epsilon)) outer iterations without a physical relaxation gap or spatial radius. Any approximation of W_N x_j and any rank reduction must be included in eta; unsupported rank truncation is not part of the proof.

A useful direct stopping certificate is also available. For balanced x put r=z-(I+W_N)x. Let L_pressure be a lower Hodge-energy bound for the visible part of r, with the original Hilbert scaling restored. Then

\[
\boxed{|\kappa_N-\langle z,x\rangle|
\le\|z\|\sqrt{\|r\|^2-L_{\rm pressure}}.}
\tag{17}
\]

It follows from ||(I+K)^-1||<=1 and the orthogonal decomposition ||Pi r||^2=||r||^2-E_(partial r). The right side uses a computable energy lower bound, not the exact unknown pressure. Add the original degree allowance before claiming an interval for full D.

### Representation reduction is itself charged

A represented tensor can be rounded without enumerating coordinates: left/right environment Gram matrices use (11), so a bond reduction is a finite weighted singular-value problem. Candidate rational gauge changes can be checked by exact contraction of the full difference. This does not require a minimum singular-value promise. Standard tensor rounding is prior art; the infinite geometric physical index is reduced by the explicit Gram contraction here.

Rounding may break balance. Repair its actual defect with Section 3 and evaluate the **combined** change in the Hilbert norm. If that exceeds the allocated eta, retain more rank or reject the step. A small raw tensor-rounding error is not enough when an ill-chosen conservation repair has high energy. Alternatively, store the coefficient fields of explicit cycles, whose divergence remains zero under coefficient rounding.

This is a rigorous solver architecture and error budget. Its outer iteration estimate is not a bound on the work required to close every inner Hodge gap. The latter depends on the discovered tensor ranks and poles and remains the main unresolved theorem.
