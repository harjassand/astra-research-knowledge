Undecidability of complete quantum
channel information order
A constructive reduction for rational strictly positive measurements

Abstract
Complete symmetric logarithmic derivative information order is co-recursively enumer-
able complete, already for pairs of measurement channels specified by finite strictly positive
POVMs with rational complex entries. Its failure is recursively enumerable complete. The
same classification holds for unrestricted complete Umegaki relative-entropy order, in which
every finite untouched reference and every pair of input states are allowed. There is no
total computable bound, from a rational POVM pair, on a reference dimension sufficient
to detect every failure of complete SLD order.
The proof reduces the finite-dimensional nonlocal-game promise supplied byMIP∗ = RE
to a uniformly gapped polynomial on the actual two-factor normalized Jordan domain. A
quantitative Pauli anchor and local completely positive smoothing establish soundness. An
ordered-word graph construction compiles the polynomial to inverse-Jordan kernels, which
are embedded into normalized strictly positive POVMs with a dimension-independent
global completion. All these reduction steps, and the equivalence between the two channel
orders, are proved below. The nonlocal-game hardness theorem is the external complexity
premise. A further normalization using its perfect consistent strategies gives a computable
positive entropy margin. Finite tensor amplification then proves that the bounded complete
entropy defect cannot be uniformly approximated even at the fixed additive error1/4.

The classification concerns a generic complex class. It does not assert hardness for a transpose-

The only external hardness premise is the following consequence of Ji, Natarajan, Vidick,
Wright and Yuen [1]: from a Turing machine and its input one can effectively construct a finite
rational two-player nonlocal gameG such that
halting =⇒ω∗(G) = 1, nonhalting =⇒ω∗(G)≤ 1

Here ω∗ is the supremum over finite-dimensional tensor-product strategies. Attainment of the
supremum is not assumed.
Section 2 proves the information-order equivalence. Sections 3–6 encode the game on the
actual normalized tensor-Jordan domain and obtain a polynomial with a uniform positive gap
in the low-value case and a strict negative witness in the high-value case. Section 7 compiles
ordered words to finitely many inverse-Jordan denominators. Sections 8–9 construct legitimate
POVMs and control all states and tangents. Section 10 finishes the reduction and the upper
computability bound.
Sections 11–14 strengthen the result: the finite nonnegative valueV(E,F ) in (64) has no
total algorithm for approximation even at the fixed additive tolerance1/4. This uses the
perfect-consistency part of the external theorem to obtain a witness margin independent of
the unknown strategy dimension.
The construction is a finite symbolic algorithm for the output POVMs. It is not an efficient
procedure to find an upper-value game’s strategy or its witness dimension.
