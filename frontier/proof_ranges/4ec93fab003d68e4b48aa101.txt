Let \(U,V\) be bounded self-adjoint involutions on an arbitrary complex Hilbert space: \(U^2=V^2=I\). Let \(a,b>0\), \(A=aU\), and \(B=bV\). Scalar offsets in the observables only translate the reports and may be added without changing any variance statement. Assume \([U,V]\ne0\).

Write
\[
J(s,t)=\tfrac12(e^{isA}e^{itB}+e^{itB}e^{isA}),
\qquad \widehat\mu(s,t)=\int e^{i(sx+ty)}\mu(dx,dy).
\]
We seek a normalized POVM \(M\) and **independent positive scalar probability noises** \(\nu_1,\nu_2\) satisfying the equality at every real frequency
\[
\widehat M(s,t)=\widehat\nu_1(s)\widehat\nu_2(t)J(s,t).
\tag{1}
\]
Both noise variances are finite. Means may be subtracted from the reports; they play no role in the result. Dependence between the two scalar noises is not admitted by this theorem.

**Theorem 1 (complete independent-noise variance region).** The attainable pairs \((v_1,v_2)=(\operatorname{Var}\nu_1,\operatorname{Var}\nu_2)\) are exactly
\[
v_1>0,\quad v_2>0,\qquad
\boxed{v_1v_2\ge\|[A,B]\|^2.}
\tag{2}
\]
Every pair in this region is attained by explicit centered, two-sided geometric laws on \(2a\mathbb Z\) and \(2b\mathbb Z\). In particular, for \(w_1,w_2>0\),
\[
\boxed{\min(w_1v_1+w_2v_2)
=2\sqrt{w_1w_2}\,\|[A,B]\|.}
\tag{3}
\]
For the Pauli pair \(A=a\sigma_z\), \(B=b\sigma_x\), the region is \(v_1v_2\ge4a^2b^2\), and the least unweighted sum is \(4ab\).

The optimizing POVM is statistically equivalent to a four-outcome unsharp joint measurement. Its unbounded geometric magnitudes are independent classical randomness. Conditioning the reports on the four signs halves both added variances while retaining unbiasedness. Thus (2) identifies an exact cost of the scalar-convolution reporting contract, not an improvement in information or measurement precision over the finite joint measurement.
