## What is measurable now, and what would establish reaction selection

The control already measured most cleanly is the same-stress, core-conditioned relative mobility

\[
\Gamma(P)=\mu_{Zn}(P)/\mu_{S}(P).
\]

The 2026 source reports a change from $\Gamma>10$ at low illumination to nearly 1 at its highest optical power in the 20 nm sweep; its additional light/dark tests show relative shifts of roughly 20× and 5× for 20 and 25 nm films. This establishes non-proportional control of two actual dislocation classes, within that high-temperature, epitaxial regime. The next new quantity is not another maximum velocity. It is the conditional reaction outcome for a pre-identified pair, for example

\[
\Theta=\frac{\Pr(\text{verified target annihilation}\mid\text{light gate, mapped pair})}
{\Pr(\text{verified target annihilation}\mid\text{matched dark/thermal control, mapped pair})},
\]

with competing outcomes (junction formation, pinning, surface export, climb, motion out of view, and contrast loss) reported separately. No source supplies $\Theta$, a reaction yield per area, a retained-defect fraction after operation, or a processing throughput.
