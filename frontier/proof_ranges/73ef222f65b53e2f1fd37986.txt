## 2. Sharp completion disk

For every finite-dimensional experiment above,

    |z_s| <= sqrt(p_A p_B),
    |L-z_s| <= sqrt(q_A q_B).                            (2)

Given only p_A,p_B,z_s satisfying the first inequality, the second inequality
describes the EXACT possible set of L, if each output sector may have dimension
at least two. It is not merely a one-sided loose error bound.

Proof. Use Hilbert-Schmidt vectors A_j=P V_j sqrt(rho) and
B_j=(I-P)V_j sqrt(rho). Their squared norms are p_j and q_j. Their inner products
are z_s and z_l, respectively. Cauchy-Schwarz proves (2). For sharpness take a
pure input and normalized branch output vectors in C² direct-sum C². For
0<p_A<1 and any z_l with |z_l|<=sqrt(q_A q_B), choose

    v_A=(sqrt(p_A),0,sqrt(q_A),0),
    v_B=(conj(z_s)/sqrt(p_A), sqrt(p_B-|z_s|²/p_A),
         conj(z_l)/sqrt(q_A), sqrt(q_B-|z_l|²/q_A)).

They have norm one, branch survival probabilities p_A,p_B, and overlaps z_s,z_l
in the respective sectors. Each one-vector isometry can be completed to a
unitary on a larger common space. Boundary cases follow directly or by limits.
The entire disk lies in |L|<=1 since
sqrt(p_A p_B)+sqrt(q_A q_B)<=1. QED.

For equal survival p_A=p_B=1-q, this reduces to

    L in (1-q)m + disk(radius q).                        (3)

The source target Im L is therefore only certified from the accepted fringe
once the missing coherence allowance q and apparatus errors are paid. For
unequal survival the geometric radius sqrt(q_A q_B) is smaller than Q.
There is no theorem that lost histories have zero, positive or molecularly
irrelevant coherence just because they were discarded.

Example: p_A=p_B=.99, z_s=.99 and z_l=-.01 give m=1 but L=.98 and full X-failure
probability .01. Perfect surviving fringes need not certify perfect full control.

Confidence conversion must optimize the union of these disks over ONE valid
joint confidence region for p_A,p_B,z_s. Empirical ratios, separately favorable
fits or a conditional confidence interval without the attempt count are not
such a region. No such experimental region is supplied by the packet.
