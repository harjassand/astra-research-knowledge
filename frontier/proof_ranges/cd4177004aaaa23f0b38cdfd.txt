3. Stronger mechanism: acquire paired local responses
Instead of treating each coefficient as an independent acquisition object, partition the edges into cells \(K\). Cells may share boundary vertices.
For each cell, retain two pieces of reference information:
\[
E_K^0=\sum_{e\in K}c_e^0(\nabla h_0)_e^2,
\]
and the boundary injection pattern generated by the reference current.
After acquiring the target coefficients inside a requested cell, compute two complementary responses:
\[
D_K=
\min_{\substack{h=h_0\\\text{on the cell boundary}}}
\sum_{e\in K}c_e(\nabla h)_e^2,
\]
\[
N_K=
\min_{\substack{f\text{ has the reference}\\
\text{cell injection pattern}}}
\sum_{e\in K}\frac{f_e^2}{c_e}.
\]
The first allows the cell’s potential to relax while preserving its boundary values. The second allows its current to reroute while preserving its boundary injections.
These are acquired local solves, not supplied exact-response oracles. Every coefficient and both solves must be charged.
A global certificate from compatible local pieces
The resulting bounds are
\[
\boxed{
\frac{C_0^2}{\sum_K N_K}
\le C(c)\le
\sum_K D_K.
}
\]
The local potentials agree at shared boundaries, so they assemble into a globally admissible potential. The local currents retain the correct cell injections, so their combination remains globally conserved.
The key local inequality is
\[
\boxed{D_KN_K\ge (E_K^0)^2.}
\]
Its proof is short. For any admissible local potential \(h\) and current \(f\), their boundary-work pairing is fixed:
\[
\sum_{e\in K}(\nabla h)_ef_e=E_K^0.
\]
Weighted Cauchy–Schwarz gives the inequality, including when the two local minimisations are performed independently.
Now normalise:
\[
w_K=\frac{E_K^0}{C_0},
\qquad
u_K=\frac{D_K}{E_K^0},
\qquad
v_K=\frac{N_K}{E_K^0}.
\]
Zero-reference-work cells can be omitted. Then
\[
u_Kv_K\ge1,
\qquad
\boxed{
\frac{C_0}{\mathbb E_wv}
\le C(c)\le
C_0\mathbb E_wu.
}
\]
This creates the proposed interface:
Sample cells according to their reference work; acquire their paired responses; certify the global observable from those acquisitions.

The construction can repair the original approximation
Merging cells enlarges the space of permitted local relaxations. Therefore
\[
\sum_K D_K \quad\text{and}\quad \sum_K N_K
\]
can only decrease under coarsening. The upper conductance bound falls, and the lower bound rises.
Singleton-edge cells recover the first mechanism. One whole-network cell gives the exact answer, after terminal sets are contracted appropriately.
That endpoint is mathematically useful but computationally uninteresting: it acquires and solves the full target problem. Monotone improvement alone does not establish affordability.
