## Strongest process comparators and scope limits

| Comparator | What the primary source supports | Why it is not the proposed operation |
|---|---|---|
| ZnS:Al/GaP (the 2026 paper above) | More than 10× lower Zn-core line density after growth at higher donor concentration, with S-core density nearly unchanged. | Static growth incorporation and strain relaxation; not a later light-gated reaction or finished-material repair. |
| Carrier-assisted filtering in indium-free AlGaAs/GaAs/Ge/Si, Hughes et al. (2019), Fig. 5 and Sec. III.C | In the $x=0$ double heterostructure (measured residual tensile strain after cooldown: 0.17%; the Fig. 5 scan temperature is not separately stated), a 5 kV scanning electron beam, about 3 nA absorbed current, one frame/s, and estimated carrier concentration $10^{17}$ e–h pairs/cm³ act on a 1000 µm² observation area starting near $10^7$ threads/cm². The in-field count first rises about 20% as clustered threads separate, then falls about 30% from the peak after 40 min. The fast first stage spans roughly 300 frames at about 100 nm/frame; slower threads remain and the count has not reached steady state. | This is genuine carrier-assisted, area-local filtering: mobile threads glide out of the imaged area. It is not selection of an annihilation reaction. The paper assigns less than 5% count change to sources and reactions, does not resolve products by Burgers-vector closure, and reports no separate post-beam retention audit. It explicitly treats reaction-based filtering as a distinct preceding operation. |
| Forward-graded ZnS\(_y\)Se\(_{1-y}\)/GaAs(001), Ocampo et al. (2010), publisher abstract | X-ray-derived type-A/type-B densities are each about $1.5\times10^8$ cm⁻² with no graded buffer. At grading coefficient 27 cm⁻¹, the abstract reports type A/B densities of $1.6\times10^8$/$1.9\times10^8$ cm⁻²; at 54 cm⁻¹ it reports $2\times10^8$/$3\times10^8$ cm⁻². An overshoot interface reduces top-layer densities by promoting annihilation/coalescence, with type B removed more. | Strong same-family strain-engineering comparator, but not carrier-gated. Only the publisher abstract was accessible, so exact post-overshoot densities, reaction geometry, temperature, and retention duration were not audited. |
| Electrical in-situ TEM of Inconel 718, Zhang et al. (2020), JMST Figs. 2–4 | The authors report dislocation annihilation followed by regeneration under current; Fig. 3 is a heat-only comparison at 0, 203, and 260 s. | Metallic current/Joule-heating/electron-force debate, not semiconductor carrier-state gating. The accessible publisher page gave the abstract and figure captions; it is a cautionary comparator, not evidence for the ZnS mechanism. |

Do not combine these operating windows into one demonstrated protocol. The 2023 ZnS field experiment is a tip-driven nanoscale TEM configuration with strong, nonuniform local fields and no stated specimen temperature. The ZnS optical experiment uses a different epitaxial geometry and 350 °C anneals followed by room-temperature imaging. The GaAs/Si experiment uses a 30 kV electron beam to generate carriers and a residual tensile stress at room temperature. Each is a distinct material state and stimulus.
