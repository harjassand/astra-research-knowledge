## What the source actually establishes

The precise model is the normalized spatially homogeneous, isotropic-cutoff,
three-dimensional constant-rate Maxwell collision equation, with centered
Gaussian scale-mixture initial velocities. An independently sampleable scalar
mixing law `V_0` on a known strictly positive bounded interval `[a,b]` is
supplied. Neither experimental velocity samples alone nor a generic gas
automatically provide that representation or latent sampler.

1. **Finite marked genealogy.** An order-`k` mixing moment can be estimated
   unbiasedly by a bounded product using no more than `k-1` separating
   splits, `2k-1` nodes and `k` initial draws, independently of elapsed time.
   Shared ancestry is essential. This is a meaningful compression of the
   usual full Wild genealogy, whose expected leaf count is `exp(t)`; that
   comparison is to a specified algorithm, not every possible solver.
2. **Bounded signed full-law information.** A random-degree Laguerre
   representation gives an unbiased density-ratio estimator with a uniform
   deterministic envelope. It supplies more than moment agreement.
3. **Actual ideal sampling.** For `0<a<b<3a/2`, the source applies Huber's
   credited linear Bernoulli factory to those signed queries, then rejection
   samples an actual `f_t` velocity. Its expected signed-query count is at
   most `19 C^2 I`, uniformly over all finite `t>=0`. For the narrow source
   fixture the bound is `292.39841191645786`; expected degree per query is
   `5/6` and expected proposals `3.36804839632687`.
4. **Broader approximation.** For arbitrary `0<a<b`, its finite-`n` mark
   sample has ideal continuous-law TV error at most
   `sqrt(15/2) Var(V_0)(1-exp(-t/3))/(4 a^2 n)` and a uniform bracket bound.
   It uses at most `n-1` splits. Its residual mixing variance at infinite
   time is `Var(V_0)/n`, so it is genuinely an approximation.
5. **Robustness, not acquisition.** A supplied W1 error in the initial
   mixing law gives a time-uniform output TV bound. It does not acquire the
   latent law from physical velocities or certify the collision model.
