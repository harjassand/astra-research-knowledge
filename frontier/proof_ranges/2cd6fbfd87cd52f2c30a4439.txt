\section{What remains unproved and what was checked}
The decisive missing result is a presentation-specific construction of suitable corrections or exact dilations, together with a compatible low-rank-debt completion theorem permitting genuine $o(n)$ enlargement and vanishing generator edits. No such result was obtained for a consequential infinite group. Uniform edge-intertwiner gaps also fail in the unrestricted modular gluing problem; the independent consequence report supplies a fixed two-dimensional example. This rejects that shortcut, not the gluing question itself.

A separate compatibility check matters: a central extension $1\to\mathbb Z\to\widetilde\Gamma\to\Gamma\to1$ with finite abelianization of $\widetilde\Gamma$ forces $H^2(\Gamma;\mathbb R)\ne0$. The five-term sequence contains $0=H^1(\widetilde\Gamma;\mathbb R)\to\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb Z,\mathbb R)\to H^2(\Gamma;\mathbb R)$, so its transgression is injective. One cannot directly apply a vanishing-$H^2$ hypothesis to this base group.

The current predecessor comparison includes strict-stability obstructions \cite{BL}, unnormalized cohomological repair \cite{DGLT}, and the conditional arithmetic consequences and weaker stability results in the June 2026 version of \cite{DV}. A September 2026 preprint already claims non-hyperlinear groups by another route \cite{ALT}; that is a source-reported result, not independently certified here. Merely obtaining the existence conclusion would therefore not establish this campaign's originality.
