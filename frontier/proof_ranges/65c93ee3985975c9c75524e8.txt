## Status

This investigation establishes an exact-certificate theorem for the one-parameter family

\[
 F_\ell(A)=u^2+v^2+4w^2+\frac72q^2+\ell q(u+v),
 \quad A=(x\mid y),\quad
 u=|x|^2,\ v=|y|^2,\ w=x\cdot y,\ q=\det A.
\]

It proves the sharp equivalence of quasiconvexity and rank-one convexity in this family. In particular, it proves quasiconvexity of the previously retained example with \(\ell=-29/10\), which remains non-polyconvex.

**The full nine-coefficient left-SO(2)-invariant quartic family and the general planar Morrey problem are not resolved here. Historical novelty and external proof review remain unestablished.** The certificate has been independently reconstructed and checked with exact rational polynomial arithmetic, root isolation, and rational interval bounds. It has not been elaborated in a proof assistant.

The heat-flow/exact-two-form framework is credited prior work of Bruno and Pasqualotto [1]. The specific anisotropic endpoint, its certificate, the sharpness calculation, and the consequences below were derived in this investigation. No unverified Astra/OpenAI repository theorem is a premise.
