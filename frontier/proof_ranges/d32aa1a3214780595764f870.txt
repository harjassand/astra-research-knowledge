# Promoting acquired linear knowledge to stochastic path prediction

Private construction, 2026-10-24. Historical novelty is UNKNOWN. This draft
uses established strong Markov lumpability and polynomial factorization; the
candidate contribution is the finite least-refinement construction for arbitrary
rational linear observables, with source-level rather than population-state
enumeration. Species-partition and rule-based reductions are mandatory priors.

## 1. Obtainable contract and the change of scientific question

Input is a finite list of integer jumps nu_j in Z^n and polynomial propensities
a_j in Q[x], given by explicit sparse coefficients. They define a nonexplosive
continuous-time Markov chain on N^n: a_j(x)>=0 there, and a_j(x)=0 whenever
x+nu_j is outside N^n. The generator is

    A phi(x) = sum_j a_j(x) [phi(x+nu_j)-phi(x)].

Independent symbolic parameters may be included by requiring each coefficient
identity for all allowed parameter values in an open set. Repeated-reactant
propensities must be the actual stochastic falling-factorial polynomials, not
silently substituted deterministic powers. A specified stopped process is another
valid contract, but its boundary propensities must be represented and checked.

This is not an algorithm for recognizing every nonnegative polynomial rate or
every nonexplosive source. In the executable scientific cases, source syntax
gives positive mass-action rates and boundary validity, and separate conservation
or proper linear Lyapunov certificates warrant nonexplosion. Those certificates
were constructed and exactly checked in source_warrants.py; they are paid
preprocessing. An arbitrary source without such a warrant remains outside the
claimed acquired interface. A stopped source need not satisfy the universal-grid
necessity argument below, so its leastness requires a separate domain argument.

The acquired knowledge is a rational row space W0, represented by a full-row-rank
matrix L0. It may have been acquired from earlier deterministic tasks, or may just
encode the scientific observables. The new task is exact path-law prediction for
L0 X_t, uniformly over initial populations. This is stronger than retaining its
instantaneous drift. The desired output is the least rational linear extension
of W0 that is a strong Markov lumping, and an executable reduced jump generator.
We do not require full system identification or an arbitrary nonlinear sufficient
statistic. For one fixed finite population class, a smaller non-universal model
may be sufficient; our minimality does not cover that different task.

## 2. Finite action table

For a current full-row-rank rational L with row space W, group source reactions
by their projected increment delta=L nu_j. For every **nonzero** increment, form

    q_(L,delta)(x) = sum_{j:L nu_j=delta} a_j(x).

The factorization condition is that every such q factors through y=Lx. A jump
with projected increment zero is invisible at this stage and needs no rate test.
It must be regrouped after every refinement: it can become visible later.

For a polynomial q=sum_alpha c_alpha x^alpha, write its row gradient as

    grad q(x) = sum_beta v_beta x^beta,   v_beta in Q^(1 x n).

Then q factors through L iff every v_beta belongs to W. Necessity follows from
directional derivatives along ker L; sufficiency follows because q is constant
on every affine L-fiber. A rational right inverse R gives an explicit factor
qbar(y)=q(Ry), even though Ry need not be a nonnegative population. qbar is
nonnegative on the attainable lattice image because it equals q at its actual
nonnegative preimages. Off-image nonnegativity is not promised.

## 3. Algorithm

Repeat the following using exact rational arithmetic:

1. Put the current row basis L into reduced row echelon form.
2. Group every source jump by delta=L nu_j.
3. For each nonzero group, sum its propensities and extract all gradient
   coefficient rows v_beta.
4. Add every independent v_beta to W.
5. If the rank did not grow, stop. Otherwise regroup all source jumps and repeat.

At termination compile one jump delta with propensity qbar_delta(y) for each
nonzero group. This is the new stochastic operation. It can answer later
first-passage, distributional, intervention and likelihood questions that depend
only on these variables, with each downstream numerical/statistical calculation
still paid and verified at its own accuracy.

## 4. Correctness and minimality

**Theorem.** Under the contract of Section 1, the algorithm terminates after at
most n-rank(W0) strict rank-increasing rounds plus one terminal check. Its final
row space is the unique least rational linear strong Markov lumping that contains
W0, where strong lumpability is required over all N^n initial states and means a
common reduced generator for every state on each fiber.

**Sufficiency.** On termination each q_(L,delta)=qbar_delta o L. Thus for every
bounded test function h on the image lattice,

    A(h o L)(x) = sum_delta qbar_delta(Lx)
                         [h(Lx+delta)-h(Lx)].

This is an intertwining of generators. Uniqueness/nonexplosion of the specified
jump process gives the common projected Markov law. It preserves the full path
law, rather than only finitely many moments. Standard strong-lumpability or
martingale-problem reasoning supplies this implication; it is not claimed new.

**Necessary polynomial factorization for a rational lattice projection.** Suppose
L* is rational and defines a universal strong lumping. Distinct nonzero projected
jumps lead to distinct next projected states. Equality of their instantaneous
rates on each integer fiber implies that each grouped polynomial q* is constant
on every nonnegative integer L*-fiber. For any integer k in ker L*, the polynomial
q*(x+k)-q*(x) vanishes for every sufficiently large nonnegative integer x. Such a
Cartesian integer grid is Zariski dense, so the difference is identically zero.
A polynomial periodic along k is constant along that direction: substituting
x+t k produces a polynomial in t with period one. Integer kernel vectors span
ker L* because L* is rational. Consequently all gradient coefficient rows of q*
belong to row(L*).

**Leastness induction.** Let W* be any rational linear universal strong lumping
containing the current W. Its jump grouping refines the current grouping. A
nonzero current group cannot contain a W*-invisible jump. Hence each old q_delta
is a sum of the q*'s of its finer groups, each of which factors through W* by the
previous paragraph. Every row added in step 4 therefore belongs to W*. Initially
W0 is contained in every candidate by definition, so this containment is preserved
at every iteration. The terminating W is itself valid, and is contained in every
valid candidate. This proves uniqueness and leastness, not merely a small rank.

**Warm acquisition.** If W0 was the least polynomial deterministic drift quotient
for the *actual stochastic drift* b(x)=sum_j nu_j a_j(x), preserving original
observables O, then every universal rational stochastic lumping preserving O
contains W0. Coordinate test functions show b descends through any such lumping.
Thus starting from that previously acquired drift quotient cannot rule out a
smaller valid stochastic answer. It does not by itself establish a runtime win:
the strongest retaining compiler may start from the identical W0.

## 5. Costs and precision

Let M be the number of source reactions and P the total expanded propensity
support, with input degree D. Each round groups M explicitly computed jumps,
adds/subtracts sparse coefficient dictionaries and reduces at most nP gradient
coefficients. No population count cutoff, full CTMC generator, or measured
normalizer is used. The number of rounds is bounded by n-rank(W0)+1. Arithmetic
work is polynomial in n, M and the explicit sparse polynomial representation
(counting monomial exponent/gradient work). This is not a claim of polynomial
cost in a succinct circuit description whose expanded P is exponential.

All added rows are sums of original propensity-gradient coefficient rows, rather
than iterated polynomial compositions. Before row reduction their coefficient
bit lengths are bounded by the source rational coefficient arithmetic, degree
factors and summation over at most M channels. Canonical rational basis entries
are ratios of minors; ordinary exact rank algorithms therefore have polynomial
bit complexity in this expanded representation. The implementation records
operation counts and coefficient bits; no unit-cost real arithmetic is assumed.

The final qbar can be stored as an arithmetic circuit q(Ry), avoiding compulsory
dense monomial expansion. Dense expansion, if used, is separately charged and
can grow as binomial(r+D,D). Exact rational evaluation on integer populations
is possible. Floating-point stochastic simulation and matrix exponentiation
introduce distinct rounding/truncation issues; the algebraic proof is not a
binary64 trajectory certificate.

The prototype stores a monomial as a tuple of repeated variable indices; its
input-size accounting therefore includes degree in that representation. A
bit-polynomial implementation for binary-encoded large exponents must instead
retain compact exponent vectors when differentiating. The source examples have
degree at most two. Initial image membership is not supplied for free: a given
microscopic initial population certifies y0=Lx0. From that starting point the
proved reduced jumps remain in L(N^n), so simulation does not require solving a
fresh integer-fiber feasibility problem at each step. A later added observable
needs its own initial value, obtainable here from the specified source initial
population; it cannot in general be reconstructed from the old macrostate alone.

## 6. Sharp exclusions and attempted falsifications

* **Irrational encodings:** [1,sqrt(2)] is injective on N^2. It can encode a whole
  count pair in one exact real without satisfying real-fiber factorization. Thus
  rationality is essential to the lattice minimality statement, and rank alone
  is not a bit-cost compression claim.
* **Fixed conservation classes:** on x1+x2=1, the propensity x1(x1-1) vanishes.
  A global coefficient test can add x1 even though this reaction never fires in
  that class. This is not the least model for every fixed finite initial task.
  More strongly, on a bounded finite population domain, a rational row using
  large base weights can injectively encode the entire population vector. Rank
  alone would then misrepresent storage and decoding work; our universal and
  bit-complexity qualifications must not be dropped.
* **Drift cancellation:** opposite projected jumps with equal hidden-dependent
  rates cancel in b but add in quadratic variation. Deterministic CLUE alone
  cannot warrant stochastic reuse. A source-derived phosphorylation intervention
  supplies a nonnegative, boundary-valid example.
* **Grouping is not once-only:** a zero projected jump can become nonzero after
  adding a required variable; freezing the initial grouping can accept an invalid
  model. A dedicated audit must force this case.
* **Controls:** a shared rate parameter may be varied only if the certificate is
  uniform in that parameter. Individually varying source channels is a changed
  mathematical actuator interface, not automatically a biological capability.
* **Observable histories:** a smaller non-Markov predictive representation may
  answer selected tasks. We do not prove optimality among nonlinear, history-
  dependent, distribution-specific or approximate representations.
* **Hidden explosion:** an invisible 2B -> 3B channel with rate B(B-1) can explode
  although an observed constant coordinate has a zero projected generator.
  Nonexplosion is an independent theorem premise, not a consequence of the
  grouped-rate test.

The phrase mean-preserving in a development-receipt key means unchanged
**deterministic drift equations**. It does not mean unchanged finite-time
stochastic expectations. For the source intervention, the added generator acting
on the old free-kinase drift is -2*eta*kOnE^2*E*S3; stochastic mean differences can
therefore appear at order t^2. Keep this distinction in all interpretations.

## 7. Comparators and acquisition repayment

The strongest structural stochastic compiler receives the same reaction list,
old W0, every computed coefficient table and all exact arithmetic. It can implement
this algorithm and retain the resulting generator; there is no distinct learner-
architecture advantage. Established species-partition lumping, rule-based
fragmentation/network-free simulation, dependency pruning, and state-level
bisimulation on affordable finite classes must also be credited.

The substantive candidate is a computable route from one acquired scientific
representation to the *least extra linear knowledge needed for a changed semantic
task*. Whether this goes beyond known generalized lumpability algorithms remains
to be checked. Acquisition is repaid on a specified workload only if the paid
representation/refinement/verification plus later reduced calculations cost less
than the strongest full retaining alternative for that same workload. Existing
published reduced rules may make the setup unnecessary. No repayment is inferred
from a dimension reduction, a single runtime, or an authored algebraic fixture.
