2. HOMOGENEOUS IRREDUCIBLE SPD M-MATRICES HAVE NO INACTIVE NODES

Now impose the additional physical promises
  alpha_i=alpha>0 for EVERY physical node (unknown alpha is allowed),
  K_ij<=0 for i!=j, and the off-diagonal graph is connected.
K remains symmetric positive definite. Thus it is an irreducible SPD
M-matrix, using this precise sign/connectivity definition.

Claim: K^-1 e1 has every coordinate strictly positive.
Proof: choose s>lambda_max(K). Then A=sI-K is entrywise nonnegative
and its off-diagonal graph is connected. Its eigenvalues lie in (0,s),
so
  K^-1=(1/s) sum_(j=0)^infinity (A/s)^j.
Every summand is entrywise nonnegative. For each coordinate i, a path
from 1 to i makes some (A^j)_(i1)>0. Hence (K^-1)_(i1)>0.

Since W is K-invariant and K is invertible, K|_W is an invertible map
from W onto W. Consequently K^-1e1 belongs to W. W contains a strictly
positive vector. Its weighted signed-block classification therefore has
no inactive coordinates, and all signs within each block are equal.
Orienting each block positively makes them unsigned synchrony blocks.

For homogeneous alpha, their orthonormal vectors are simply
  v_b=1_(B_b)/sqrt(m_b), m_b=|B_b|.
The reduced cubic coefficients are
  alphabar_b=alpha/m_b.
The measured-node block is singleton, so alphabar_1=alpha. This alpha
is identified directly at the port as -G^3 Fh(0)/6.

3. EXACT SINGLE-PORT PHYSICAL NODE CENSUS

Recover the canonical minimal model without being supplied N, as in
canonical_reduction.txt. Under the preceding homogeneous M-matrix
promises, each ratio
  m_b=alpha/alphabar_b
is a positive integer, and there are no inactive nodes. Therefore
  N=sum_(b=1)^r alpha/alphabar_b.
The IO germ, and a finite adaptive exact certificate, determine the
PHYSICAL number of nodes, even if full physical K is unidentifiable.
Two homogeneous irreducible SPD M-matrix networks with identical IO
must have the same physical node count and the same multiset of block
cardinalities (with the measured singleton anchored).

The reduced Kbar is itself an irreducible SPD M-matrix: inter-block
entries are sums of nonpositive physical edges divided by positive
normalizations, and original graph connectivity induces quotient graph
connectivity. Conversely, if a canonical irreducible SPD M-matrix has
alphabar_b=alpha/m_b for positive integers m_b, m_1=1, it has a physical

(Kbar_bb-lambda)/m_b, and across blocks they are

internal eigenvalues can remain hidden even when the node census is fixed.
