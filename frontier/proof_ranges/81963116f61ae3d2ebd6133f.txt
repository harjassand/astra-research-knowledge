## 2. A precise additive-expansion target is unattainable

The strongest concrete obstruction is elementary and unconditional.

### Bounded-exponent scalarisation theorem

Let
\[
A\subseteq\{0,1,\ldots,B\}^{n},
\]
and let
\[
E_\ell(A)=
\bigcup_{k=1}^{\ell}
\{a_1+\cdots+a_k:a_i\in A\}.
\]

For arbitrary positive integer weights \(\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_n\), define
\[
A^\beta=\{\langle\beta,a\rangle:a\in A\}.
\]

Then
\[
\boxed{
\left|\bigcup_{k=1}^{\ell}kA^\beta\right|
\le |E_\ell(A)|
\le(\ell B+1)^n.
}
\]

This holds **regardless of the magnitude or representation cost of the weights**.

**Proof.** Every vector sum belongs to the integer box
\[
\{0,1,\ldots,\ell B\}^{n}.
\]
The scalar sumset is its image under \(v\mapsto\langle\beta,v\rangle\). Taking an image cannot increase cardinality.

Moreover, the first inequality can be made an equality: choose
\[
\beta_j=(\ell B+1)^{j-1}.
\]
Radix uniqueness makes this scalar map injective on the entire box. ∎

Thus enormous weights can remove collisions between exponent vectors. They cannot create additional exponent vectors.

### Consequence for a recent general-circuit lower-bound proposal

Theorem 2 of Anand Kumar Narayanan’s 2026 preprint *Arithmetic circuit lower bounds from sumset expansion* proposes a sufficient construction with
\[
1\le r\le n,\qquad
m=n\binom{n+r-1}{r},\qquad
s=n^{\omega(1)}.
\]
Its exponent matrix has \(n\) columns and entries bounded by \(2^{n^{O(1)}}\). With iteration parameter \(\ell=s/r\), the required scalar sumset cardinality is at least \(4^s\), after choosing a large weight vector \(\beta\). These are the parameters printed in the version inspected on 11 October 2026. [ECCC](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/138/download)

For any fixed \(c\) such that \(B\le2^{n^c}\), the theorem above gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\log_2
\left|\bigcup_{k=1}^{\ell}kA^\beta\right|
&\le n\log_2(\ell B+1)\\
&\le n+n\log_2s+n^{c+1}\\
&=o(s).
\end{aligned}
\]
The last step follows because \(s\) grows faster than every fixed power of \(n\).

Consequently,
\[
\left|\bigcup_{k=1}^{\ell}kA^\beta\right|
\le2^{o(s)}<4^s
\]
for sufficiently large \(n\).

**The expander required by that particular parameter regime cannot exist, even nonconstructively.**

This does not make the conditional implication logically false. It makes its hypothesis unattainable under the simultaneously stated bounds. The argument does not address the paper’s separate lower-depth theorems, and it does not establish \(VP\ne VNP\).

This is an elementary deduction, not a claim of historical originality.
