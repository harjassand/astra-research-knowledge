Take two parallel A-to-B edges, c_1=1,c_2=M, u_A=0,u_B=1, and j=(1,0).
The flow and potential are feasible and q=(1,0) is normalized, but E_q[a]=1
while E(u)=cap=1+M. The absent flow edge has positive Dirichlet energy.
Therefore feasibility, monotonicity and normalization do not certify the upper
bound obtained from sampled weights. Absolute continuity of the energy measures
with respect to q, and usable domination/tail bounds, are separate obligations.
In a continuum model the same issue also affects resistance if j is nonzero
where j dot grad(u)=0; both energy measures must be covered.

Sampling and precision
----------------------
If independently certified 0<=a<=A_* and 0<=b<=B_* are available, n iid
samples and Hoeffding plus a union bound give, with probability >=1-delta,

  [1/(mean(b)+B_* sqrt(log(4/delta)/(2n))),
   mean(a)+A_* sqrt(log(4/delta)/(2n))].

Obtaining relative width 1+epsilon still needs a small primal-dual gap and
domination bounds comparable to the respective means. A rare missed channel
can make A_*/E(a) arbitrarily large. A computed flow with conservation error
is not an admissible unit flow until corrected/certified. Exact boundary traces,
outgoing sampling probabilities, weight evaluation and numerical roundoff must
also be accounted for. Unit-flow normalization removes one partition function;
it does not deliver the missing global overlap and tail information.

Stronger existing comparator
-----------------------------
The Berman-Konsowa flow principle already constructs the flow-induced path law
and supplies the lower bound

  cap >= E_path[(sum_{e in path} j_e/c_e)^(-1)].

By Jensen this is at least 1/R(j), so it is at least as strong as the Thomson
lower endpoint here. On a single series path, it gives the exact capacity for
any potential; the cross-flux upper endpoint is exact only for the harmonic
potential. On parallel edges with all j_e>0 the Berman-Konsowa expectation is
sum c_e, although Monte Carlo variance may still be arbitrarily large.
Neither case proves that arbitrary unknown channels are easy to acquire.

Hence the proposed construction has no demonstrated advantage over the
strongest relevant path-flow baseline. The remaining worthwhile question is
an acquisition-inclusive theorem for a concrete high-dimensional model class,
with exact feasible flow generation, certified energy coverage and finite-cost
tails. Without that structure, root/HIDDEN_CHANNEL_BOUND.txt gives a scoped
oracle obstruction to eliminating channel discovery.
