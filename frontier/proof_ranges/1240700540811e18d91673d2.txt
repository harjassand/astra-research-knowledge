## 6. Consequences and boundaries

### Square-root discrepancy under arbitrary matroid constraints

For a Boolean constraint matrix \(W\) with at most \(\Delta\) nonzero entries in each column, compute the integer
\[
q=\lceil\sqrt{\Delta}\rceil
\]
and apply the theorem to the rational matrix \(A=W/q\). This yields
\[
\boxed{
\|W(\mathbf1_B-x)\|_\infty
\le C\lceil\sqrt{\Delta}\rceil
=O(\sqrt{\Delta}),
}
\]
with either a nonincreasing prescribed cost or an explicit exactly unbiased distribution.

The relevant August 2026 matroid-discrepancy paper provides a constructive cost-nonincreasing guarantee linear in column sparsity. The proposed theorem changes the asymptotic guarantee, although its large constants and polynomial degree do not make it a practical replacement. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13983)

### A broader algorithmic transfer principle

The same precision-reset argument applies to Boolean-vertex polytopes with bounded exchange width, a polynomial-time separation procedure, and a controlled rational description.

Bounded exchange supplies enough face directions. The polyhedral interface supplies exact endpoints and linear resets. The spectral argument supplies quantitative descent.

For general matroids, the required interface is derived from independence access; it is not an extra assumption.

The broader operation is therefore:

> **Preserve combinatorial feasibility and fixed input-defined equations exactly, while allowing bounded drift in the analytic certificate and resetting precision inside every reached face.**

Neither negative curvature alone nor an existential chain of faces gives this conclusion.

### The long-edge obstruction remains intact

Nothing here removes the obstruction from large coupled feasible exchanges.

An even cycle has two alternating perfect matchings. At their midpoint, an appropriate scalar row gives unavoidable error proportional to the cycle length. Each side of the bipartition separately defines a partition matroid, so arbitrary matroid intersections remain outside the dimension-independent theorem. proof

The proof also explicitly rejects a uniform lower bound on feasible step length and rejects independent dyadic coordinate rounding as an exact feasibility repair.

## 7. What is established at this stage

The attached argument supplies a complete proposed route from the inherited curvature machinery to a **deterministic polynomial-bit basis algorithm and a deterministic construction of its exactly unbiased output distribution**. It does not leave global nonconvex minimization, exact transcendental operations, approximate marginal correction, or a stronger matroid oracle as deferred operations.

The accompanying program passed exact checks of the dimension and progress inequalities, a precision-reset example, and 80 face-recovery and terminal-cost cases across five small matroid families. Those are limited diagnostics, not verification of the full theorem or an implementation of the full algorithm.

**The substantive advance is a paper-level computational construction, not another existential extension.** Its correctness as a whole and its historical novelty still require independent scrutiny. The conservative algorithm also remains far from a practical solver.
