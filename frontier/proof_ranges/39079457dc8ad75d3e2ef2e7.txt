## Finding

The generalized statement in work/FO-root/ACOUSTIC_FACE_OBSTRUCTION.md is correct under its stated hypotheses. The only wording clarification is that “C² homogeneous quartic” should explicitly mean a homogeneous quartic polynomial; coefficient topology and coefficientwise closure are being used. I found no counterexample with an invertible background and nonzero probe vectors.

The specific F-star-to-G result in work/FO-profile-prior/cone_followup/REPORT.md is a correct specialization. Its exact verifier passed when run from a copy. Independent exact checks reproduced the target acoustic zero and lifted-barycenter witness, checked the F-star acoustic Gram, and verified the coercive constant with exact LDL pivots. The obstruction separates a positive right-orbit/polyconvex synthesis cone; it does not establish non-quasiconvexity of G.

Invertibility is essential. If B0=0, the older F-star law has zero acoustic Hessian there but a negative lifted Jensen gap. This is a counterexample only to a weakened theorem with singular background, not to the stated theorem.
