## Hodge correction and estimate of the surviving cubic

In Type II write $X=D\varphi=\begin{psmallmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{psmallmatrix}$ and $r=d-\lambda a$. Set
$$
\varphi_1^0=\varphi_1+\lambda^{-1}\partial_1\Delta^{-1}r,\qquad
\varphi_2^0=\varphi_2-\partial_2\Delta^{-1}r.
$$
For zero-mean Fourier mode $(\xi,\eta)$, the Riesz symbols are $T_{ij}=\xi_i\xi_j/(\xi^2+\eta^2)$. Direct substitution gives
$$
d_0-\lambda a_0=r-(T_{22}+T_{11})r=0.
$$
Both corrected rows are still gradients, and Riesz transforms give
$$
\|X-X_0\|_p\le C_{p,\lambda}\|r\|_p,\quad p=2,4.
$$
The relation $d_0=\lambda a_0$ is the closedness condition for the stream potential $\psi$ satisfying
$$
\varphi_1^0=\psi_2/\lambda,\quad \varphi_2^0=\psi_1,\quad
a_0=\psi_{12}/\lambda,\quad b_0=\psi_{22}/\lambda,\quad c_0=\psi_{11}.
$$
Therefore Lemma 3.1 and the Hodge estimates imply
$$
\|a_0\|_{8/3}^2\le C_\lambda(\|c\|_2+\|r\|_2)\|X\|_4,
\qquad \|a_0\|_4\le C_\lambda\|Y\|_4.
$$
Interpolate $L^{8/3}$ and $L^4$ to get $\int|a_0|^3\le\|a_0\|_{8/3}^2\|a_0\|_4$. Also
$$
\int|a^3-a_0^3|\le\|a-a_0\|_2(\|a\|_4+\|a_0\|_4)^2,
$$
so
$$
|\mathbb E a^3|\le C_\lambda(\|c\|_2+\|r\|_2)\|X\|_4\|Y\|_4. \tag{A}
$$
This is the new differential input. Determinant balance alone cannot prove it: the incoming note provides a bounded balanced finite law violating the same estimate, while the Fourier/Hodge argument uses actual curl compatibility.
