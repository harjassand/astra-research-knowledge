5. Closing the hidden-cost obligations
The two potentially expensive components are acquiring the Gaussian cell weights and implementing the base spin models. Neither is treated as a free oracle.
Gaussian weights: no rare-event rejection oracle
Completing the square gives
\[
G_C=
\exp\left\{
e_C+\frac12d_C^\top Kd_C-q_C(y_*)
\right\}I_C,
\]
where
\[
q_C(y)=
\frac12(y-Kd_C)^\top K^{-1}(y-Kd_C),
\]
\[
y_*=\arg\min_{y\in C}q_C(y),
\qquad
I_C=\int_Ce^{-\phi(y)}dy,
\qquad
\phi(y)=q_C(y)-q_C(y_*).
\]
The potentially enormous prefactor is handled in logarithmic form. Its exponent is rational and can be computed exactly.
The mode \(y_*\) can be acquired by enumerating at most \(r\) active constraints and solving rational optimality systems. This costs \((2n+1)^{O(r)}\) polynomial-bit operations, already within the stated rank-dependent bound.
For the remaining integral, define
\[
V(t)=\operatorname{vol}
\{y\in C:\phi(y)\le t\}.
\]
Convexity gives
\[
V(t)\le t^rV(1)\quad(t\ge1),
\qquad
I_C\ge e^{-1}V(1).
\]
Layer-cake integration yields
\[
I_C=\int_0^\infty e^{-t}V(t)\,dt
\]
and the relative tail bound
\[
\frac{\int_T^\infty e^{-t}V(t)\,dt}{I_C}
\le
e\int_T^\infty e^{-t}t^r\,dt.
\]
Thus a cutoff
\[
T=O\!\left(r^2+\log(1/\eta)\right)
\]
suffices for relative accuracy \(\eta\), without knowing the original Gaussian cell probability.
A particularly useful implementation is the positive sum
\[
(1-e^{-s})
\sum_{k=1}^{J}e^{-(k-1)s}V(ks),
\qquad
s=O(\eta),\quad Js\ge T.
\]
It avoids subtracting nearly equal estimated volumes.
Each sublevel body has rational linear constraints and one convex quadratic constraint. The note constructs rational inner and outer radius guarantees with polynomial encoding length. Classical convex-volume approximation, including its rounding and finite-grid implementation, then supplies the required relative volume estimates. math.cmu.edu
This matters for thin or far-shifted cells: their small widths and probabilities are not ignored, but their bit lengths, rather than their reciprocals, enter the construction.
Base sampling and partition estimation
For the residual interaction, the spectral Poincaré theorem of Eldan–Koehler–Zeitouni gives continuous-time heat-bath gap at least \(\gamma\), or random-site discrete-time gap at least \(\gamma/n\), for arbitrary external fields. arXiv
Here all supplied fields are bounded by \(L\). A conservative restarted sample with TV error \(\tau\) therefore needs
\[
O\left(
\frac n\gamma
\left[n(L+1)+\log(1/\tau)\right]
\right)
\]
single-site updates. A dense update costs \(O(n)\) arithmetic operations.
For \(Z_0(c)\), interpolate the interaction through
\[
0,\frac{J_0}{n},\ldots,J_0.
\]
Each successive partition ratio is the expectation of
\[
\exp(E_0(X)/n),
\qquad
E_0(X)=\frac12X^\top J_0X,
\]
which lies between \(1\) and \(e^{1/2}\). Consequently, ordinary bounded-variable estimation provides relative partition estimates without large-variance importance weights.
A conservative bound is
\[
O\left(
\frac{n^5}{\gamma\eta^2}
\left[n(L+1)+\log(n/\eta)\right]
\log(n/\zeta)
\right)
\]
arithmetic operations, multiplied by polynomial bit-arithmetic overhead. For \(J_0=0\), this annealing cost disappears: the base law is a product distribution.
Preprocessing, precision, memory, and output
The complete accounting is
\[
T_{\mathrm{prep}}
\le
T_{\mathrm{arrangement}}
+
M\left(T_{\mathrm{Gaussian\ weight}}+T_{\mathrm{base\ partition}}\right)
+
T_{\mathrm{normalisation}}.
\]
An incremental arrangement implementation makes \(O(mM)\) rational linear-programming calls. Each cell needs at most \(2n\) interval constraints.
Estimating every log weight to error \(O(\epsilon)\) controls the normalised mixture globally. The required per-cell relative tolerance does not become \(\epsilon/M\); the union bound affects confidence through \(\log M\).
Weights are stored as logarithms. Categorical sampling subtracts the largest log weight and discards sufficiently small terms with an explicit total-mass budget. Memory is
\[
M\,\operatorname{poly}
\left(n,r,B,\log(1/\epsilon),\log(1/\delta)\right)
\]
bits. Each subsequent draw also pays for writing its \(n\) output spins.
The output is \(\log Z\), or a compact scientific-notation representation—not the expanded digits of an exponentially large partition function.
Finally, the rational model factors are supplied inputs. Learning an unknown physical model is not included. Parameter acquisition and calibration require a separate cost model. This is equilibrium computation, not simulation of the physical time needed to reach equilibrium.
