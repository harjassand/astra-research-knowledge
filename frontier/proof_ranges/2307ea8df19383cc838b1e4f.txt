## 3. New theorem: a local protected sensor in an arbitrary finite-density device

This extends the source's four-candidate sensor search to a precisely defined
many-site class. It does not extend the source's payoff calculation.

Let N hard-core sites have an explicitly listed diagonal nuisance Hamiltonian

    V_xi = sum_l xi_l Q_l,
    Q_l = sum_S c_lS product_(i in S) n_i,

where every monomial has at least two distinct sites. Coefficients xi_l can be
arbitrary unknown values; neither weak interactions nor a norm bound is needed.
Known on-site energies on the sensor/readout block are matched or compensated.
The only phase-sensitive unknown term is the unit-magnitude link p-q. Admit a
constant-size active block B containing p,q and enough empty channels/known
connections to implement the required phase-independent readout when that
unknown link is off. Other interactions may produce an arbitrary finite-density
state outside the sensor.

Construct a guard set Z outside B by taking the union of S minus B over every
listed monomial with |S intersection B|=1. Prepare Z empty and B with exactly one
particle; freeze hopping across the boundary of B and all hopping into Z, and
disable reservoirs acting on B or Z. During interrogation, switch off every
other hopping edge inside B so p-q is isolated. During readout, switch p-q off
and enable only the admitted calibrated known controls inside B. Keep the actual
local pure dephasing. Any additional background Hamiltonian is supported outside
B union Z and is phase independent; it may evolve the unreset background.

**Proposition A.** On this admitted subspace, V_xi acts as identity on the active
one-excitation sector tensor an operator on the remaining background. The
sensor and known readout are therefore independent of every xi_l and of the
remaining background state. The p-q probe has exactly the source's two-level
Bloch law with dephasing gamma=(d_p+d_q)/2; uniform d gives gamma=d.

**Proof.** A monomial with at least two sites in B vanishes because B contains
one particle. A monomial with exactly one active site has an empty guard factor
by construction, and also vanishes. A monomial with no active site acts only on
the background/guards and is identity on the sensor. Frozen hopping and disabled
reservoirs preserve the vacuum guards and active particle number. Local Z noise
does not change those occupations. Thus the restricted Hamiltonian splits into
sensor and background parts; the dissipator splits likewise. A pure local
preparation of the sensor/guards factorizes them from any remaining state. On
the pair coherence, the two local dephasing contributions are d_p/2 and d_q/2.
All other local Z operators act trivially on this encoded coherence. QED.

This is a constructive sufficient compiler: scan the explicit monomial list and
form a union. Its work is linear in that list's incidence length, with ordinary
index/bit costs; it does not enumerate 2^N states or search for an optimal guard.
If each active site belongs to at most r monomials of degree at most k, then
|Z|<=|B|r(k-1). Local reset, freezing and readout can consequently have size
independent of N on bounded-degree models. They still require real calibrated
operations and finite error budgets. On long-range/dense templates the guard
can grow with N. Imperfect guards restore unknown interaction-dependent
detuning/leakage and must be bounded, not ignored. Terms linear in n_i, unknown
non-density interactions, unfreezable transport, extra phase-sensitive links or
unsupported noise invalidate the stated structural theorem.

For a k_B-site active readout block with uniform d, a conservative dephasing
TV budget is k_B*d*t_readout/4 from the jump unravelling. Spectator/guard Z jumps
do not decohere the active state. Known control graph geometry, bandwidth,
loading and switching remain explicit requirements; no constant resource bound
is asserted for an arbitrary graph without the admitted block.

The Hamiltonian-difference half-range remains sqrt(3) for any N because all
nuisances cancel between trine labels and the unknown link is still one link.
Thus the same all-adaptive identification obstruction remains valid on this
extended access class. **A control separation only follows after independently
acquiring the required good-action/loss geometry of that larger device.** No
such larger stationary-output certificate is supplied here. The cheap protected
probe does not make the many-body payoff cheap.
