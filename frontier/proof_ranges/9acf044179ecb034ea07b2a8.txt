## 1. Definitions and resource contract

We set hbar=1. For density matrices, D(rho,sigma)=||rho-sigma||_1/2. Let n_x=|1><1| on qubit x and let N=sum_x n_x. Our known candidate is

    K=N.

The prediction input is

    psi=|+>_a tensor |0>_(all other qubits),

where a is an accessible qubit. For an unknown, time-independent Hamiltonian H define

    E_T(H)=sup_(0<=t<=T) D(rho_H^a(t),rho_K^a(t)).                 (1)

Here rho_H^a is the reduced state on a after evolving the full input psi. A tolerant certifier must accept with probability at least 2/3 whenever E_T(H)<=eta, and reject with probability at least 2/3 whenever E_T(H)>=3/4. It may behave arbitrarily in the intermediate region. The candidate, tolerances and prediction horizon are fixed before the validation observations.

### 1.1 Available experiments

Fix a set S of controllable sites containing a. A fresh experiment starts with all sites outside S in the known vacuum. Within S, the experimenter may prepare an arbitrary state entangled with arbitrary retained quantum memory. During the experiment, the system evolves under H. Arbitrary known controls, joint operations, measurements, resets and feedback on S plus the memory are allowed. These controls have no strength bound. No operation may prepare or act on the other system sites during this evolution.

The total native evolution time in one fresh experiment is at most tau. Dividing a coherent run into pieces does not reset this cap. All feedback operations are counted within that same experiment. A new experiment requires a fresh vacuum outside S. A previous bath state cannot be reinserted as the next bath.

For the lower bound we grant MORE terminal information than local readout: at the end, the entire system state may be handed to the experimenter as a quantum output, with arbitrary subsequent collective processing and quantum memory. Thus the lower bound includes protocols using only local measurements. All discarded experiments and all postselected failures count. No inverse of the unknown dynamics is supplied.

The raw experiment count is a worst-case cap. Experiment duration can be selected adaptively from earlier classical outcomes. Within an experiment the controls may use feedback. The supplementary expected-total-duration lower bound in Section 7 assumes the duration is selected at the start of each experiment; it does not assert an optional-stopping theorem for arbitrary outcome-dependent termination within a run.

Bath preparation, port operations, readout and classical computation are granted free in the lower bound. Charging their actual costs cannot invalidate it. The theorem assumes a fixed, memoryless unknown Hamiltonian across fresh experiments.

### 1.2 An ambient class for the exact/tolerant comparison

The polynomial upper bound will hold whenever H conserves excitation number, annihilates the vacuum, and restricts on the one-excitation sector to I+C with ||C||<=2. The relevant lower-bound chains lie in this class. The ambient class allows zero hopping. Exact-null examples therefore exist, including H=N plus arbitrary occupation-diagonal interactions that vanish on the vacuum and one-excitation sectors. Exact completeness is not asserted for a class in which the null hypothesis has been excluded by a strict nonzero-hopping promise.
