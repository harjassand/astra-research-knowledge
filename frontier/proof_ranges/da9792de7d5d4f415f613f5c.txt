## Status

This package establishes elementary identities in a specified scalar Landau model,
checks two switching paths numerically, and provides an illustrative BaTiO3 parameter
screen. It does not establish historical novelty, a new law of physics, validity
of the scalar approximation for an actual sample, device feasibility, measured
energy recovery, a refrigerator coefficient of performance, or industrial advantage.
No physical experiment or atomistic simulation was performed.
