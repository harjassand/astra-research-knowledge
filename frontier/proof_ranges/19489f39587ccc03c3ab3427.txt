Derivation
Define
\[
x=a^{-3}=(1+z)^3.
\]
Separate energy conservation gives
\[
\dot\rho_i=-3H(\rho_i+p_i),
\qquad
\dot x=-3Hx,
\]
and therefore
\[
x\rho_i'(x)=\rho_i+p_i.
\]
Differentiating once more,
\[
x\rho_i''(x)=c_{s,i}^{\,2}\rho_i'(x).
\]
The assumptions consequently imply
\[
\rho_i'\geq0,
\qquad
0\leq x\rho_i''\leq\rho_i'.
\]
In the spatially flat Friedmann equation, \(H^2\) is proportional to the sum of the densities. Thus, writing \(Y(x)=H^2(x)\),
\[
Y'\geq0,
\qquad
0\leq xY''\leq Y'.
\]
