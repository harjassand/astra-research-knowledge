7. Non-polyconvexity and limits of the result
--------------------------------------------
Every sharp base member remains non-polyconvex. Indeed,

  F(I)=(r+2)(r+3)/6 <= -5/128.

At J=diag(1,-1), H(J)=16 and alpha_*<=15/8. Bounding the remaining terms over
I gives F(J)<=4507/128. Give +/-40I probability 1/[2(1+40^2)] each and +/-J
probability 40^2/[2(1+40^2)] each. This law has mean matrix zero and mean
determinant zero, but its mean F value is strictly negative because

  1600 F(I)+F(J) <= (-8000+4507)/128 <0.

It therefore violates convex Jensen in (A,det A). The law is not asserted to
be a compatible gradient law and is not a quasiconvexity counterexample.

The certificate here proves quasiconvexity at the sharp acoustic floor on I.
It does not prove the same statement for every r<-2 or r>0, exclude every
transformed isotropic decomposition, establish an extreme-ray result, or
prove a uniform pure-fourth-order Jensen constant. Those are separate
obligations. The direct non-SOS contact constraints were also reconstructed
as exploratory work, but no non-SOS obstruction is claimed or needed because
the exact SOS certificate succeeds.
