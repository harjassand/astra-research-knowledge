### A broader completion operation

For a fixed gate field, component averaging

\[
(\mathcal A_\Gamma\psi)(z)=|C_z|^{-1}\sum_{w\in C_z}\psi(w)
\]

is a linear idempotent operation on vertex potentials. It makes the potential constant across closed dual edges. It can act on z_2+phi_z, where phi is any bounded local stationary field, not only zero. The affine mean flux survives because the correction remains stationary and integrable.

For |phi|<=B, truncating this operation on unresolved components has squared scalar error at most

\[
2T_R+8B^2\mathbb P(R_z>R),
\]

and hence coarse-current error at most eight times this expression. Equation (11) supplies the remaining probability. This allows finite local stream modifications to be completed into globally legal conserved currents before they are evaluated or optimized. It is not a universal completion theorem for arbitrary defects, and no density theorem for the resulting family is claimed.
