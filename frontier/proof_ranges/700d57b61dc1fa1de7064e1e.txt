## 2. Arithmetic with an isolated integer input

Fix a conventional universal machine with positive integer program codes k. Let T(k) be its exact finite runtime when it halts, and let K be its halting set. Use a harmless compulsory initial step, so every halting runtime satisfies T(k)>=1. Choose an encoding in which an algorithm with integer constant n has a program of length O(log(n+2)) plus the fixed algorithm description. Binary self-delimiting encodings have this property.

The relation R={(k,t): k halts in exactly t steps} is computably enumerable (in fact decidable for finite k,t). DPRM supplies an integer polynomial for R. Introduce nonnegative arithmetic-gate witnesses and separate positive and negative polynomial parts. This gives a fixed finite list of integer quadratic polynomials

f_j(k,t,w), j=1,...,s,

such that there exists w in N^d with all f_j(k,t,w)=0 if and only if t=T(k). The list also excludes k=0; for instance add the equation k-u-1=0 with a nonnegative witness u. No real parameter occurs in these equations.

For real a>=0 define

S_a(k,t,w)=(k-a)^2 + sum_j f_j(k,t,w)^2.

Every term in the second summand is an integer square at integer controller counts. Thus, with r=1/sqrt(8),

8 S_a(k,t,w)<1

holds for some integer controllers exactly when a lies within distance r of some k in K. Indeed it forces all f_j=0; the remaining inequality is |k-a|<r. Conversely, a halting k has a witness and satisfies the inequality throughout that interval. Because 2r<1, the intervals around distinct integers are disjoint.

Compactify a=(1-theta)/theta for theta>0 and put

S_tilde_theta=theta^2 [theta(k+1)-1]^2 + theta^4 sum_j f_j(k,t,w)^2.

This is precisely theta^4 S_a, extends polynomially to theta=0, and has degree at most four separately in theta and in all controller variables. The first term has controller degree two. Coefficients are integers.

## 3. Compile into the same identical-flow construction

Use the source compiler with 8 S_tilde_theta-theta^4 in place of 2 S_tilde_theta-theta^4. In full, expand

8 S_tilde_theta(n)-theta^4 = sum_{|beta|<=4} q_beta(theta) (n)_beta,

where n=(k,t,w). Set b_beta=2+||q_beta||_1, h_beta(theta)=b_beta+q_beta(theta), B(n)=sum b_beta(n)_beta, Bbar(x)=sum b_beta x^beta, and eta=Bbar(1). Then all h_beta are between 2 and 2+2||q_beta||_1 on [0,1]. Include every multi-index through degree four, allowing zero q_beta.

All controller species n and one additional species Y have immigration and death at rate one. Include immigration of Z at eta, and the two channels 2Z->3Z and 2Z->Z at rate one. For every beta include:

* X_beta+2Z -> X_beta+Z at b_beta;
* X_beta+Y+2Z -> X_beta+Y+3Z at 2b_beta;
* X_beta+2Y+2Z -> X_beta+2Y+Z at 2b_beta;
* X_beta+2Z -> X_beta+3Z and X_beta+2Z -> X_beta+Z, each at h_beta(theta);
* X_beta+3Z -> X_beta+4Z and X_beta+3Z -> X_beta+2Z, each at h_beta(theta).

Parallel channels can be merged. This is one fixed finite graph, with a fixed number of species, polynomial rate constants of parameter degree at most four, and a common finite bound 1<=rate<=C. Source and product molecularities are at most eight. Every jump changes one coordinate by one. There is no linear stoichiometric conservation law.

Its exact threshold in controller state (n,y) is favorable only when y=1, in which case

D-H=theta^4-8 S_tilde_theta(n).

The source switching lemma therefore proves finite-mean almost-sure explosion whenever this is positive at any integer controller state, and nonexplosive positive recurrence otherwise. All conclusions use the minimal chain killed at explosion, on the closed irreducible class N^(d+3) x {1,2,...}, since w has d coordinates; all controllers range in N and Z>=1. The common initial state has every coordinate equal to one.

The complete deterministic vector field, independent of theta, is

x_i'=1-x_i, y'=1-y, z'=eta-Gamma(x,y)z^2,
Gamma(x,y)=Bbar(x)[2(y-1/2)^2+1/2]>=1.

It has the common globally asymptotically stable equilibrium 1. With u=(x,y),

Q(u)=integral_0^1 [Gamma(1+s(u-1))-eta]^2 ds/s,
V(u,z)=||u-1||^2+(z-1)^2+Q(u),

V is a coercive rational polynomial of degree at most twelve and Vdot<=-||u-1||^2-(z-1)^2. Thus all source deterministic guarantees are preserved. All stochastic coordinate drifts are also independent of theta.

## 4. Exact phase diagram and computable robustness

The explosive parameter set is exactly

E = union_{k in K} I_k,
I_k = (1/(k+1+r), 1/(k+1-r)),  r=1/sqrt(8).

These intervals are disjoint and wholly inside (0,1), since k>=1. Endpoints and theta=0 are nonexplosive and positive recurrent. Their accumulation at zero causes no exception to the switching lemma.

At theta_k=1/(k+1), define the explicitly computable rational radius

epsilon_k = 1/[8(k+1)^2].

If |theta-theta_k|<epsilon_k, then

|1/theta-(k+1)| < (1/8)/(1-theta_k/8) <= 1/7 < 1/4.

(The bound remains valid for k>=0; here k>=1.) If k halts, the distance to k is less than 1/4<r, so the whole ball is explosive. If k does not halt, its distance to every halting integer is greater than 3/4>r, so the whole ball is nonexplosive and positive recurrent.

Hence the same exact undecidability persists even with a computable, nonzero, instance-specific robustness radius supplied as a promise. This is robustness to variation along the designated one-dimensional polynomial rate family. It is NOT a theorem about arbitrary independent perturbations of every rate constant, nor a uniform lower bound on epsilon_k over all k.
