## C. A spectral obstruction to broadcasting

For a canonical comparator
\[
\Psi(X)=d\sum_y p_y\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_yX)\rho_y,
\]
its Hilbert–Schmidt superoperator trace satisfies
\[
\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathrm{HS}}\Psi
=
d\sum_y p_y\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_y^2)
\le d.
\]
The identity direction contributes one. Taking the trace of (1) on the traceless subspace gives
\[
\boxed{
\sum_{j=1}^{d^2-1}(2\lambda_j^2-1)_+
\le d-1.
}
\tag{24}
\]

Eigenvalues are counted with multiplicity. A bistochastic self-adjoint channel violating this inequality cannot be self-compatible.

## D. The construction extends to any number of outputs

For an \(r\)-output broadcaster with identical marginals, the covariance recursion becomes
\[
C_{n+1}
=
\frac1rD^{-1}\bigl(C_n+(r-1)T\bigr)D^{-1}.
\]
Its comparison fixed point is
\[
H=(r-1)(rD^2-I)^{-1}
\]
on the eigenspaces \(|\lambda|>r^{-1/2}\).

The same finite-measurement and regression argument gives
\[
\boxed{
\Psi\succeq
\left(\frac{r\Phi^2-\mathsf I}{r-1}\right)_+,
\qquad
\mathsf I-\Psi
\preceq
\frac{2r}{r-1}(\mathsf I-\Phi).
}
\tag{25}
\]

The complete \(r\)-output proof, a sharper broadcaster-dependent covariance bound, and a simultaneous trace-norm reconstruction corollary are in the attached record.

# 4. What remains unresolved

### The sharp constant

The proof gives four, not two.

The standard universal-cloning example shows that a universal constant below two is impossible; its common marginal is the depolarizing channel with
\[
\lambda=\frac{d+2}{2(d+1)}.
\]
This channel’s compatibility threshold is also recorded in the primary compatibility literature. [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/1099-4300/24/6/805)

For completeness, the broadcaster is
\[
\mathcal B_d(X)
=
\frac2{d+1}P_{\mathrm{sym}}(X\otimes I)P_{\mathrm{sym}}.
\]
Expanding \(P_{\mathrm{sym}}=(I+S)/2\) gives
\[
\Phi_d(X)
=
\frac{(d+2)X+\operatorname{Tr}(X)I}{2(d+1)}.
\]

Every trace-preserving entanglement-breaking channel \(\Lambda\) satisfies
\[
\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathrm{HS}}\Lambda\le d.
\]
Therefore, summing the comparator inequality over a traceless orthonormal basis forces
\[
d^2-d
\le C(d^2-1)(1-\lambda)
=C\,\frac{d(d-1)}2,
\]
so \(C\ge2\).

The resulting rigorous interval is
\[
\boxed{2\le C_*\le4.}
\]
