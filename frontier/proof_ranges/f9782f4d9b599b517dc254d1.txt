0. Exact theorem, convention and scope

Let n>=1, M_n=M_n(C), sigma>0 with Tr sigma=1, s=sigma^(1/2),
u=s^(1/2). All inner products below are the unnormalized Hilbert--Schmidt
inner product <X,Y>=Tr(X*Y). Set

  Gamma(X)=s X s,    Psi=Gamma^(1/2),    Psi(X)=u X u.

Let (P_t)_(t>=0) be a continuous semigroup of unital completely positive maps
on M_n, and use the POSITIVE Heisenberg generator L:

  P_t=exp(-t L).

Assume only KMS symmetry, <X,P_tY>_sigma=<P_tX,Y>_sigma, where
<X,Y>_sigma=Tr(X*sYs). This is equivalent to L*Gamma=Gamma L, with * on
a superoperator denoting its Hilbert--Schmidt adjoint. No GNS symmetry,
commutation with the modular group, diagonal Kraus form, or special dimension
is assumed. Put

  H=Psi L Psi^(-1).

Then there are N<=n^2-1 Hermitian, Hilbert--Schmidt traceless matrices B_j
such that, for all X in M_n,

  H(X)=sum_j H_(B_j)(X),
  H_B(X)=(V_B X+X V_B)/2-B X B,
  V_B=2 Lyap_s^(-1)(B s B),    Lyap_s(V)=sV+Vs.

In particular the displayed sum contains the full generator, including all
Hamiltonian terms allowed by KMS symmetry. It is not a statement only about
the dissipative part of a chosen physical GKSL representation.

Conversely, any finite Hermitian B family with this formula gives a legal
sigma-KMS-symmetric QMS on M_n after the inverse transport, and each summand
separately does so. Each H_B is Hilbert--Schmidt selfadjoint and positive
semidefinite as a superoperator. The empty family gives the zero generator.

The theorem is about a full matrix algebra. An arbitrary proper finite
C*-subalgebra need not admit such a formula with B_j required inside that
subalgebra: on a diagonal C^2 every such H_B vanishes on the algebra, whereas
a two-state classical Markov chain need not vanish. This qualification does
not restrict finite-dimensional quantum systems on full M_n or their matrix
amplifications.

1. Facts before choosing a CP gauge

KMS symmetry gives

  H*=Psi^(-1) L* Psi=Psi L Psi^(-1)=H.

Both Psi and Psi^(-1) are CP maps: they are filters X->uXu and
X->u^(-1)Xu^(-1). Thus

  exp(-tH)=Psi P_t Psi^(-1)

is CP, although usually not unital. It preserves adjoints, so its derivative
does as well. Unitality of P_t gives L(I)=0, hence H(s)=0. Sigma stationarity
also follows directly: L*(sigma)=L*Gamma(I)=Gamma L(I)=0.

These three facts -- selfadjointness, a CP exponential, and the faithful
positive ground state s -- are all the conditional-Choi proof below uses.
An HS similarity by general invertible matrices would not suffice; here
the similarity and its inverse are specifically CP filters.

2. First proof: average an arbitrary physical GKSL representation

Take any finite physical GKSL description with Schrödinger jumps K_j and a
Hermitian physical Hamiltonian Q. With D=sum_j K_j*K_j and G=D/2-iQ, the
positive Heisenberg generator is

  L(X)=G X+X G* - sum_j K_j* X K_j.

No tracelessness or KMS symmetry of this starting CP-noise map is needed.
Define A=u G u^(-1) and W_j=u K_j* u^(-1). Direct transport gives

  H(X)=A X+X A* - sum_j W_j X W_j*.

Its Hilbert--Schmidt adjoint is

  H*(X)=A* X+X A - sum_j W_j* X W_j.

Since H=H*, average these exact formulas. Put V=A+A* and

  B_(j,1)=(W_j+W_j*)/2,
  B_(j,2)=(W_j-W_j*)/(2i).

These are Hermitian and the cross terms cancel exactly:

  (W_j X W_j*+W_j* X W_j)/2
     =B_(j,1) X B_(j,1)+B_(j,2) X B_(j,2).

Therefore H(X)=(VX+XV)/2-sum_B BXB. Evaluating at s gives

  Vs+sV=2 sum_B B s B.

Because s>0, Lyap_s is invertible. Consequently

  V=sum_B 2 Lyap_s^(-1)(BsB),

which is exactly the asserted sum. The possible skew weighted drift and
skew CP-noise component cancel because the TOTAL H is selfadjoint; they
have not been discarded independently. In particular Q can be nonzero.
This proof establishes the representation using finite GKSL as its sole
general structure theorem. The next proof establishes it without that
structure theorem, and improves the noise count to n^2-1.

3. Independent proof: conditional Choi positivity and a real factorization

Let G_0=-H, so exp(tG_0) is CP. Choose an HS orthonormal Hermitian basis

  F_0=I/sqrt(n), F_1,...,F_(n^2-1),    Tr F_a=0 for a>=1.

There is a unique complex coefficient matrix c for

  G_0(X)=sum_(a,b=0)^(n^2-1) c_ab F_a X F_b.

Uniqueness follows because the matrices F_b^T tensor F_a form a complex
basis of M_(n^2). Each multiplication map X->F_a X F_b is individually HS
selfadjoint. Thus G_0=G_0* implies c_ab=conjugate(c_ab). Adjoint preservation
then implies c_ab=conjugate(c_ba). Consequently c is REAL SYMMETRIC.

Use the unnormalized Choi convention

  C(T)=sum_(i,j) E_ij tensor T(E_ij),
  |A>=(I tensor A)|Omega>,    |Omega>=sum_i |i> tensor |i>.

Because every F_b is Hermitian,

  C(F_a (.) F_b)=|F_a><F_b|.

Thus c is literally the Choi matrix in the orthonormal basis |F_a>.
Let P=I-|I><I|/n be the orthogonal projection onto vec(I)^perp. For every
z in that complement, CP of exp(tG_0) and the finite-dimensional expansion
exp(tG_0)=id+tG_0+O(t^2) give

  0<=<z,C(exp(tG_0))z>=t<z,C(G_0)z>+O(t^2).

Divide by t>0 and let t decrease to zero. Hence P C(G_0) P>=0. Equivalently,
the REAL symmetric principal matrix c_red=(c_ab)_(a,b>=1) is PSD.

IMPORTANT: the Choi compression is onto vec(I)^perp, not vec(s)^perp.
The semigroup at time zero is the identity map even in the nonunital
weighted coordinates. Replacing I by the faithful fixed vector s here
would be an incorrect proof.

Factor c_red over the reals:

  c_red=sum_(j=1)^N v_j v_j^T,   N=rank(c_red)<=n^2-1,
  B_j=sum_(a>=1) (v_j)_a F_a.

Then B_j=B_j*, Tr B_j=0. Since c_0a=c_a0 are real, define the Hermitian
matrix

  K_0=(1/sqrt(n))sum_(a>=1)c_a0 F_a+c_00 I/(2n).

The full expansion is exactly

  G_0(X)=sum_j B_j X B_j+K_0 X+X K_0.

Put V=-2K_0. Thus H(X)=(VX+XV)/2-sum_j B_jXB_j. Finally H(s)=0 gives
Lyap_s(V)=2 sum_j B_jsB_j and the desired V=sum_j V_(B_j). This proves
necessity for EVERY finite-dimensional KMS QMS without assuming any
canonical CP gauge, preferred Kraus family, or absence of a Hamiltonian.

4. Sylvester inverse, complete converse and positivity of each summand

For s>0,

  Lyap_s^(-1)(Z)=integral_(0)^infinity exp(-ts) Z exp(-ts) dt.

The integral is norm absolutely convergent. Differentiating the integrand
and integrating proves the inverse equation. In an eigenbasis of s,
[Lyap_s^(-1)(Z)]_ab=Z_ab/(s_a+s_b); every denominator is positive, proving
uniqueness on all matrices. In particular V_B is Hermitian and PSD.

For an arbitrary finite Hermitian B family define V=sum_B V_B and
Phi_B(X)=sum_B BXB. Then H=({V,.}/2)-Phi_B is HS selfadjoint and H(s)=0.
The exponential of Phi_B is CP, since its series is a nonnegative sum of
CP compositions. The exponential of -{V,.}/2 is the CP filter
X->exp(-tV/2)Xexp(-tV/2). The finite-dimensional Lie product formula
therefore makes exp(-tH) a norm limit of CP maps, hence CP. Its inverse
transport P_t=Psi^(-1)exp(-tH)Psi is CP, and

  P_t(I)=Psi^(-1)exp(-tH)(s)=Psi^(-1)(s)=I.

KMS symmetry follows from H=H*. Sigma stationarity follows as in Section1.
Thus this is a legal QMS. Apply the argument to one B at a time to establish
the claim for each summand.

For completeness, positivity of H_B as an HS operator is not inferred merely
from its displayed matrix coefficients. Instead P_t is UCP, so its operator
norm on M_n is at most one. H_B is HS selfadjoint and has real eigenvalues;
similarity preserves eigenvalues, so exp(-t lambda) is an eigenvalue of
P_t for each eigenvalue lambda of H_B. It must have modulus <=1 for all
t>=0. Hence lambda>=0. This also proves H>=0 for the full representation.

5. Exact identity shifts and CP gauge conditions

For real c, direct expansion and the Sylvester identity give

  V_(B+cI)=V_B+2cB+c^2 I,
  H_(B+cI)=H_B.

The second equality holds as a superoperator on ALL M_n, not only on a
fixed-state tangent space. In detail, the new drift adds
c(BX+XB)+c^2X, and the new BXB term adds exactly the same expression.
Thus an arbitrary Hermitian family may be centered by c=-Tr B/n without
changing the generator. Real centering with respect to s or sigma is also
allowed; imposing two distinct weighted centerings simultaneously does not
follow from one scalar shift. The Choi proof already supplies HS-traceless
noises and requires no preliminary centering of a physical CP map.

Real orthogonal mixing of the Hermitian noise list preserves the sum.
An arbitrary complex unitary Kraus mixing need not retain Hermiticity of
each listed noise. A scalar B gives zero H_B.

If a canonical physical CP map is desired, set K_j=u B_j u^(-1). Then
Tr K_j=Tr B_j=0. The physical CP noise Phi(X)=sum_j K_j*XK_j is KMS
selfadjoint because its weighted transport is sum_j B_jXB_j. This is a
consequence of the construction, not an extra hypothesis on the original
GKSL gauge. It matches the traceless-noise canonical gauge in Amorim--Carlen.

6. Recovery of the complete physical Hamiltonian

Set V=sum_j V_(B_j), C=u V u^(-1), K_j=u B_j u^(-1). Exact inverse
transport, not a discarded commutator, gives

  L(X)=(C*X+XC)/2-sum_j K_j* X K_j,
  L_*(rho)=(C rho+rho C*)/2-sum_j K_j rho K_j*.

Here L_* is the POSITIVE Schrödinger adjoint, exp(-tL_*) is the state
evolution, and L_*=Psi H Psi^(-1). Evaluating H(s)=0 shows

  D:=sum_j K_j*K_j=(C+C*)/2.

The matrix Q=(C-C*)/(4i) is Hermitian. Therefore the exact physical negative
Lindblad generator is

  -L_*(rho)=sum_j K_j rho K_j* - {D,rho}/2 - i[Q,rho].

It is trace preserving, CP, and sigma stationary. In general C is not
Hermitian, so Q need not be zero or commute with s. Every originally allowed
Hamiltonian contribution is included through equality of the total physical
superoperator. A Hamiltonian in an arbitrarily chosen earlier GKSL gauge
need not equal this Q: the usual Kraus identity shifts can change its
displayed Hamiltonian while keeping the same total generator. A purely
Hamiltonian KMS-symmetric generator must be zero as a superoperator: its
eigenvalues are purely imaginary, whereas its similarity H is selfadjoint,
so all eigenvalues vanish and selfadjointness forces H=0. There is no omitted
independent unitary component that can be added to this H.

7. Matrix amplification with arbitrary faithful product reference

Let m>=1 and let tau>0 be any ancilla density matrix, r=tau^(1/2). For
L_tilde=L tensor id_m and the product reference sigma tensor tau,

  s_tilde=s tensor r,
  Psi_tilde=Psi_sigma tensor Psi_tau,
  H_tilde=H tensor id_m.

The amplified representation uses B_j tensor I_m. Since

  Lyap_(s tensor r)(V_(B_j) tensor I_m)
      =(sV_(B_j)+V_(B_j)s) tensor r
      =2(B_j s B_j) tensor r,

uniqueness proves

  V_(B_j tensor I_m)=V_(B_j) tensor I_m.

Thus every matrix amplification is covered without new gauge, dimension,
or normalization conditions. Physical jumps and Hamiltonian amplify as
K_j tensor I_m and Q tensor I_m. This includes a tracial ancilla and any
faithful nontracial product ancilla. The same tensor formula is not asserted
for a correlated reference Omega: its weighted transport is different. If
an amplified QMS is KMS symmetric for such an Omega, the general full-M_(nm)
theorem applies anew with s=Omega^(1/2).
