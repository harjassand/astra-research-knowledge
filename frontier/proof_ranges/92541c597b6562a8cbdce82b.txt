But the resulting physical model has an uncomputable compatibility boundary. There cannot be a universally terminating, sound-and-complete procedure that acquires or certifies every compatible realization from its observable description.

The assumptions are consequential. Permitting an unrestricted global common source makes every joint output distribution realizable. Supplying a finite quantum dimension makes the problem algebraic and decidable. Classical independent-source networks also have effective finite hidden-cardinality descriptions; the quantum dimension obstruction does not follow merely from having a causal network.

Priority and verification obligations
The forward Bell embedding is Fritz’s. Quantifying measurement dependence through causal networks and constructing nonlinear network inequalities also have prior literature, including Chaves and collaborators. Neither general idea is claimed new here. 

The precise candidate contribution is the coherent source-local replacement estimate, its quantum Bell-extraction consequence, and the explicit concave-quadratic, marginal-calibration reduction. The selective search did not establish priority for these statements.

Astra’s N675 was inspected for comparison at revision b2a7a8fa91a8fcf6da05f36100ae4ce64a72a729. It concerns a different channel-order problem and remains marked unreviewed; none of its claimed reductions is a premise here.

The remaining obligations are therefore specific: independent reconstruction of the written proof, historical-priority determination for the exact statements, and—still absent—a generally applicable new efficient mathematical operation. The computability result is not being presented as completion of the broader Genesis mission.
