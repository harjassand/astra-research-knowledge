## 7. Decisive experiment

### Qualification before the target measurement

Prepare A/B parent-derived matched regions and homogeneous-isotope sham pairs. Measure actual layer thicknesses and alignment. Fit both optical responses with one consistent dielectric model, including uncertainties; use withheld frequencies/momenta to test the fit. Require a robust, prespecified spectral intervention, for example at least 10% difference over the mean in a preregistered witness integral with less than 2 percentage points uncertainty. This is a chosen engineering gate, not a published result.

Measure static capacitance/contact potential, laminate force–curvature response, target-relevant nonresonant strain markers, surface/edge contamination and temperature stability. Matching A_el/B_el/D_el is not a substitute for these measurements. Optical characterisation is performed before dark equilibrium measurement or at decreasing powers with recovery tests; the s-SNOM tip and illumination are not assumed nonperturbative.

### First scientific output

On kappa-ET, compare raw magnetic force gradients Y=dFz/dz at registered heights and several temperatures, including above Tc. Do not turn a local force contrast immediately into a bulk superfluid-density percentage: depth inversion is model dependent.

Use a difference-in-differences observable

    D = [Y_A(Ts)-Y_B(Ts)] - [Y_A(Tn)-Y_B(Tn)],

where Ts is superconducting and Tn is normal. Normal-state subtraction is not assumed to cancel every temperature-dependent electrostatic or magnetic background. Multiple heights and temperatures, contact-potential compensation, sham inversions and blinded assignment are needed.

A provisional minimum useful target is |D|=2 pN/micrometre. This is a detection target, NOT a predicted material effect. With k=2.8 N/m and representative f0=77 kHz from the existing apparatus [1], it corresponds to a frequency contrast of 0.0275 Hz. A five-standard-error test requires a standard error no larger than 0.0055 Hz, apart from bounded systematic error.

Require a combined independently calibrated systematic budget below 0.4 pN/micrometre. A bound should be formed from measured derivatives, not assumed tolerances:

    B_sys >= |dY/dz| delta_z + |dY/dT| delta_T
           + |dY/dV| delta_V + sum_ab |dY/dstrain_ab| delta_strain_ab
           + probe and transfer/background terms.

Strain sensitivities and thermal responses must be calibrated on comparable regions. The resonant target mode itself cannot serve as an unqualified strain thermometer, because changing its dynamics is part of the intended intervention. If independent bounds cannot be acquired, no clean attribution is established.

### Sample cost

Let sigma_pair be the standard deviation of a complete independent paired D estimate, including material variability. For a planning Gaussian model,

    N_pairs >= (5 sigma_pair / D_target)^2.

At D_target=2 pN/micrometre, sigma_pair values 0.5, 2 and 10 give about 2, 25 and 625 pairs. At an ASSUMED eight minutes per complete pair, acquisition times are 0.27, 3.33 and 83.33 hours. These are conditional arithmetic, not measured throughput. Two pairs would not support robust variance or nuisance inference; the low-noise row is not a recommendation to use only two. Fabrication, optical qualification, cooling, independent repeats and failed devices are extra. Image pixels and repeat scans of one defective interface are not independent material pairs.

### Rejection and interpretation

Reject the device pair before superconductivity testing if spectral contrast collapses under measured damping/thicknesses, or if static/elastic/interface matching cannot meet the error budget. A matched optical signal alone is not success on the scientific task. If the target contrast is below the acquisition budget, report a directional upper bound at the achieved uncertainty, not a universal refutation of cavity effects.

A positive result exceeding controls establishes sensitivity to the particular external dynamical substitution within bounded alternatives. It does not prove attractive pairing, specify the whole interaction kernel, uniquely establish electromagnetic rather than acoustic coupling, or demonstrate improved Tc. A useful next-level study would compare several independently qualified balanced thickness patterns to distinguish specific competing target models whose predicted signals exceed the error budget.
