## 1. Exact statement

Write
\[
\Delta_n^\circ=\{p\in(0,1)^n:\sum_i p_i=1\},
\]
with the conventional singleton simplex when \(n=1\). Probability distributions are row vectors. Suppose that, for every finite alphabet size \(n\), a finite-valued functional
\[
D_n:\Delta_n^\circ\times\Delta_n^\circ\longrightarrow[0,\infty)
\]
has the following properties.

**Regularity and normalization.** For each fixed full-support reference \(q\), the map \(p\mapsto D_n(p,q)\) is continuous throughout the open simplex and \(C^\infty\) in some neighborhood of \(q\). Also \(D_n(q,q)=0\). No analyticity, nondegenerate Hessian, or differentiability in \(q\) is assumed.

**Invariance under independent uninformative augmentation.** For all full-support \(p,q\in\Delta_n^\circ\) and \(r\in\Delta_m^\circ\),
\[
D_{nm}(p\otimes r,q\otimes r)=D_n(p,q),
\qquad
D_{mn}(r\otimes p,r\otimes q)=D_n(p,q).
\tag{1}
\]
The second equality is redundant when relabeling invariance is assumed, but it is stated explicitly to avoid that extra convention. Full tensor additivity is not assumed.

**Universal complete monotonicity.** For every full-support \(p,q\) and every irreducible continuous-time Markov generator \(L\) satisfying detailed balance
\[
q_iL_{ij}=q_jL_{ji},
\]
the function
\[
g(t)=D_n(pe^{tL},q)
\]
is completely monotone:
\[
(-1)^k g^{(k)}(t)\geq0\quad(k=0,1,2,\ldots;\ t>0).
\tag{2}
\]

### Classification theorem

These conditions hold **if and only if** there exists one power series, independent of the alphabet and of \(q\),
\[
\Psi(z)=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}a_kz^k,
\qquad a_k\geq0,
\tag{3}
\]
with infinite radius of convergence, such that
\[
\boxed{D_n(p,q)=\Psi\!\left(\chi^2(p\Vert q)\right)},
\qquad
\chi^2(p\Vert q)=\sum_i\frac{(p_i-q_i)^2}{q_i}.
\tag{4}
\]
In particular, data processing is a **consequence**, not a hypothesis.

### Replication consequence

Every nonzero functional in this class obeys, for \(s=\chi^2(p\Vert q)>0\) and every integer \(N\geq1\),
\[
\boxed{
D_{n^N}(p^{\otimes N},q^{\otimes N})
\geq \frac{(1+s)^N-1}{s}\,D_n(p,q).
}
\tag{5}
\]
This bound is attained by \(D=c\chi^2\), so its universal factor cannot be improved.

Consequently, under the stated regularity, **there is no nonzero tensor-additive functional having universal complete monotonicity**. Even growth of order \(O(N)\) under independent replication is impossible within the classified class.
