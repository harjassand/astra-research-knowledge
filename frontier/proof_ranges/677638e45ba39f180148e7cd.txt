## 5. Contact shear to a restricted compatible Jensen class

The source contact-shear theorem supplies an invertible $A$, a rank-one radical $R=a\otimes b$, and $v=A^{-1}a$ with $b\cdot v=0$; it also proves invariance of the cubic restriction along $R$ on the contact plane. The straightening map is
\[
\Psi_h(x)=x+v h(b\cdot x),\qquad \det D\Psi_h=1.
\]
The source leaves arbitrary coupled residual profiles unresolved. A narrower transfer can be proved. On the two-torus, assume $b\in\mathbb Z^2$ is primitive; choose $c\in\mathbb Z^2$ so rows $b,c$ form a unimodular matrix. For periodic Lipschitz scalar $h$ and vector-valued $g,k$, define
\[
\Psi_h(x)=x+v h(b\cdot x),\quad
w(z)=g(b\cdot z)+k(c\cdot z),\quad
u(x)=A\Psi_h(x)+w(\Psi_h(x)).
\]
This is an affine map plus a periodic displacement. Change variables $z=\Psi_h(x)$ and set $s=b\cdot z$, $t=c\cdot z$. Then
\[
Du=A+k'(t)\otimes c+
\big(g'(s)+(A+k'(t)\otimes c)v h'(s)\big)\otimes b.
\]
For fixed $t$, the vector increment has mean zero in $s$. Rank-one convexity makes $\eta\mapsto F(A+k'(t)\otimes c+\eta\otimes b)$ convex, so Jensen in $s$ gives an average at least $F(A+k'(t)\otimes c)$. Jensen in $t$, using $\mathbb E k'=0$, then gives
\[
\mathbb E F(Du)\ge F(A).
\]
This covers a separated shear-plus-two-phase profile class only. It does not address general coupled $w(s,t)$, arbitrary compatible gradients, or the full positive-definite Schur inequality.

For $G$, take $A=I$, $a=e_1$, $b=e_2$, $v=e_1$, $c=e_1$, $h'(y)=\cos(2\pi y)$, $g=0$, and $k'(x)=\alpha\cos(2\pi x)e_2$. The actual periodic map
\[
u(x,y)=\left(x+\frac{\sin(2\pi y)}{2\pi},
y+\frac{\alpha}{2\pi}\sin\big(2\pi x+\sin(2\pi y)\big)\right)
\]
has determinant-one gradient. Exact trigonometric integration gives
\[
\mathbb E G(Du)-G(I)=\frac{309\alpha^4+560\alpha^2+108}{96}>0.
\]
At $\alpha=1/3$ this is $4699/2592$. It is a checked positive example, not a negative Jensen witness.
