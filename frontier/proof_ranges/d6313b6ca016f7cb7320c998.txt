# Original-source catalytic evidence acquisition

2026-10-16, Australia/Brisbane. Public-source acquisition and conditional reanalysis. No experiment was performed and no newly invented physical operation is claimed.

**Result:** the original Xu final SI, Fig. 2 and Fig. 6 workbooks were acquired, together with a 5 kHz current waveform and additional control workbooks. The final Fig. 2 cells preserve the supplied checkpoint's arithmetic: dynamic/static ethane ratio **0.22117569**, suppression-equivalent deficit **7.76612553 s**, and activity-scaled avoided ethane **2.48432950 events per total nominal Pd atom**. These are conditional aggregate inferences. They do not measure a retained-state lifetime or establish its physical carrier.

The added Wang Chem comparator's original supplement and combined article/SI PDF were also acquired. Its condition-specific performance and energy values are separated below.

## Exact final endpoints and the calculation

The following are numeric source cells from `final_Fig2_MOESM20.xlsx`, sheet `Figure 2 b`, columns C and D. They are processed conversion/selectivity endpoints; the workbook contains no chromatograms, peak areas, calibration formulas or replicate labels.

| Condition and source row | Conversion (%) | Paper selectivity (%) | Reconstructed ethane / incoming acetylene | Reconstructed net ethylene / incoming acetylene |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| No applied voltage, row 4 | 89.35448 | 35.06258 | 1.65488375 | -0.76133895 |
| Static +1.5 V, row 10 | 80.79704 | 47.75220 | 0.88403625 | -0.07606585 |
| +1.5 V 10 s / -0.1 V 0.1 s, row 11 | 81.02334 | 80.55926 | 0.19552733 | 0.61470607 |

The preprint Methods, printed pp. 17-18, define conversion using feed/effluent concentrations and define `S = a/(a+b)` when their differences are interpreted as common-normalized molar flows, with `a` acetylene consumption and `b` ethane formation. Under the two-step C2-only model:

`b/Ain = X(1/S - 1)` and `net ethylene/Ain = X(2 - 1/S)`.

Thus the dynamic conventional net-ethylene selectivity is about **75.8678%**, rather than the paper's 80.5593% branching metric. Negative net ethylene in the baseline is physically possible because the inlet already contains twenty times as much ethylene as acetylene. The final SI Table 1 productivity is internally consistent with this interpretation: `9.918*(2-1/0.8055926) = 7.524566 mol ethylene/gPd/h`, versus its printed 7.525.

The static-endpoint mixture null has `b_average/b_static >= 10/10.1 = 0.990099`, granting zero ethane during the negative pulse. The final source ratio is 0.22117569, which implies a positive-phase mean at most 0.22338745 of the static rate and deficit `10 - 10.1*0.22117569 = 7.76612553` suppression-equivalent seconds. This rejects that restricted null **if** endpoint molar flows, calibration, periodic operation, nonnegative ethane production and closed cycle inventories are valid. It does not identify when the deficit occurs or whether it is electronic, adsorbate, hydrogen, membrane, thermal or reactor memory.

Using nominal 10 mg of 1 wt% Pd/AC and final SI activity 9.918 mol/gPd/h gives 0.939673 micromol total Pd and 2.334457 micromol extra avoided ethane per cycle, or 2.4843295/Pd. Relative to the supplied exact results, the ratio changes by -0.00030154, the equivalent time by +0.00304553 s and the event count by -0.00503546/Pd. A separate 0.5 mL/min feed scenario referenced explicitly to 298.15 K and 1 atm gives 2.4886516/Pd.

The metal-site-only one-shot purge null permits at most one product per total Pd atom and no influence on later turnovers. It remains conditionally insufficient. Support/pore inventories and persistent effects of a purge were not measured. The conclusion cannot be promoted to an exclusion of all desorption mechanisms.

## True molar flow remains an acquisition gap

The concentration notation, hydrocarbon GC-FID method and source endpoints do not establish an outlet total-flow correction. Raw chromatograms, response factors, reference gases, inlet/outlet flow logs and conservative-tracer measurements were not acquired for Xu.

For literal gas mole fractions, a sensitivity model can solve contraction exactly. Let `z=0.01`, `yAout=z(1-Xc)` and `yCout=z*Xc*(1/S-1)`. With only `A+H2->B` and `B+H2->C`, no storage/leaks and no inlet ethane,

`q=Fout/Fin=(1-z)/(1-yAout+yCout)`.

The resulting q is 0.98319622 for static +1.5 V and 0.98994297 for the optimum. The dynamic true conversion becomes 0.81214189, net ethylene/Ain becomes 0.61858098, ratio becomes 0.22269341, equivalent deficit 7.75079654 s and activity-scaled count 2.43203352/Pd. This is an explicit alternative model, not a measured correction or confidence interval. Nitrogen normalization would require measured nitrogen or another conservative tracer; hydrocarbon FID alone does not supply that channel.

Final SI Table 2 reports 99.0% total-carbon closure for no voltage/static +1.5 V and 98.6% for an unspecified oscillating condition, with non-C2 products. Because total C2 feed/Ain is 21, a 1.4% total-carbon gap is 0.294 Ain carbon equivalents. This is material to a reconstructed net gain. It is not an instrument uncertainty estimate, and its oscillation duration is not identified. C2-only net ethylene therefore remains qualified.

## Pulse identity and complete recorded electrical integral

Final grid `Figure 2 c/d!D24` gives the 10 s/0.1 s optimum, rounded slightly relative to row 11 above. `D25` gives the separate 10 s/1 s point: **82.298% conversion and 78.329% selectivity**. Final SI printed p. 13 describes 10 s/1 s but prints 81.0% conversion and rates whose implied selectivity is 80.49%. These are unresolved source inconsistencies, not substitute measurements.

The [Supplementary Fig. 7 source workbook](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41557-026-02107-8/MediaObjects/41557_2026_2107_MOESM8_ESM.xlsx), `Figure 7 b!B2:D55000`, contains **54,999 time/potential/current samples** at 0.2 ms spacing over 0.0002-10.9998 s. Its caption identifies the 10 s/1 s waveform. Trapezoidal integrals of its recorded channels, including charging, are:

| Recorded quantity | Value |
|---|---:|
| Signed charge | 0.018958355 C |
| Positive / negative-magnitude charge | 0.176513375 / 0.157555020 C |
| Absolute charge | 0.334068395 C |
| Signed terminal integral of V*I | 0.278055401 J |
| Absolute terminal integral of V*I | 0.278801142 J |
| Absolute-charge electron equivalents / nominal total Pd | 3.68465940 |

The quoted 4.41 mA agrees closely with the **3-11 s tail mean** (4.41489 mA), not the full positive phase or cycle. A start at 2 s gives 5.38338 mA; at 4 s, 3.97706 mA. A cutoff-dependent tail average cannot establish total switching charge or energy.

The recorded potential overshoots to -0.411 and +1.68 V. Current equals -3.28 A for nine samples and +3.28 A for twenty-six; instrument range saturation cannot be excluded without acquisition settings/logs. Missing 0 and 11 s endpoint samples were not imputed. These are integrals of published channels, not an independently calibrated electrical measurement, wall-plug work or full process energy. Charge is not equivalent to acquired hydrogen uptake. **No optimum 10 s/0.1 s waveform was acquired**, so this integral must not be assigned to optimum performance.

Further bookkeeping issues: `Figure 7 c` labels current mA but stores 0.00441 at D25, whereas SI/plot use 4.41 mA. D24 stores 0.00131. Treating those cells as amperes is context-supported but the header conflict is retained. `Figure 7 d!D25` gives 3.3811% maximum FR, which also differs from SI's printed 4.64%. The SI's displayed rate-difference expression recalculates to a magnitude near 100.02 mA, not 95.04 mA. None of these FR values resolves the charging inventory.

## What the mechanistic controls establish

The original Fig. 6 workbook supplies processed wavenumber/absorbance arrays. Panel a has 21 spectra at 56 s intervals; b and c each have 12 at 42 s intervals. The [final caption](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41557-026-02107-8/figures/6) identifies a/b as +1.5/-0.75 V on H-preloaded Pd foil, c as -0.75 V with 0.25% acetylene, and d as clean Pd foil in 0.25% acetylene under alternating **400 s** +1.5/-0.75 V holds. Panel d has 61 spectra at 42 s intervals. These data show slow polarity-associated surface-spectral response in a different regime; they do not resolve the 0.1 s pulse or directly measure productive mixed-feed hold-only outputs.

The Pd-foil method removes porous-carbon physisorption and preloads hydrogen rather than continuously cofeeding it. Final SI's low-current control reports no detected gaseous products after one hour at -0.5, -5 and -50 microampere, with an approximate 1 ppm FID detection limit. Its twelve-hour -50 microampere source row gives 0.667 nmol/min ethane from ethylene and 0.709 nmol/min ethylene from acetylene. This supports the authors' desorption interpretation within that foil protocol. It does not exclude chemically stored hydrogen or support inventories in the productive powder/mixed-feed reactor.

Panel d contains several exactly identical adjacent stored spectra. Panel a has a missing wavenumber at source row 1664. The analysis retains these facts without manufacturing independence, interpolation or an allegation about their origin. Absorbance is not calibrated surface coverage here. No interferograms, scan provenance, fitting residuals or coverage-to-moles calibration was acquired.

Final SI controls also include gas-substitution CV, oxidation-product NMR/GC-TCD, quasi-RHE validation and pure-substrate flow tests. Their conditions differ from productive mixed-feed cycling. They constrain particular alternatives but do not jointly close calibrated transient chemistry, reactor response, accessible inventory or repeatability across electrode preparations.

## Added Chem comparator and correction gate

The Wang et al. [primary article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chempr.2026.103063), published online 5 May 2026 and assigned to Chem 12, 103063, 8 October 2026, was acquired as [publisher combined PDF](https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S2451929426001294-mmc2.pdf), together with its [79-page supplement](https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S2451929426001294-mmc1.pdf). Both identify the exact title; the combined PDF metadata includes the DOI. Both files record 7 October 2026 in document metadata. Their public landing pages returned 403 in shell; actual PDFs returned HTTP 200 and valid PDF signatures.

Its Fig. 3G 200-hour test uses **Cu nanowire arrays**, 250 mL DMF with 0.1 M Bu4NBF4, 0.5% acetylene/20% ethylene/79.5% Ar at **2 sccm**, and **15 mA** (1 mA/cm2). The default DMF/water ratio is 20:1. The 100 sccm / about 3 ppm result uses a separate two-cell configuration, Fig. 3E/S29. Table S3 places throughput and lifetime together; it does not establish 200 hours at 100 sccm.

Main Methods state three replicates, means/SD, hydrocarbon FID plus TCD, and no iR compensation. SI Fig. S10 provides calibration-curve plots; S11/S12 provide chromatogram and GC-MS figures. These are acquired PDF plots, not raw instrumental exports or a complete near-ppm LOQ validation package. Its selectivity is the conventional concentration-based expression that subtracts ethane and twice C4 products per acetylene consumed, unlike Xu's metric.

The reported electrical energy is **8.51 kWh/kg newly formed ethylene** at 5 mA/~2.5 V in the 0.1 M electrolyte condition. Raising electrolyte to 1 M gives ~1.7 V and 5.80 kWh/kg, a separate condition. This does not establish complete solvent recovery, pretreatment, pumping, separation, replacement or wall-plug cost parity with Pd pulses.

One source reconciliation is material: SI Fig. S31's caption says 70 mL DMF, 35% acetylene at 2 sccm and 100 mA, but its plot shows about 237 mL DMF, and its note's 5.36 mmol acetylene consumed in 200 hours fits the 0.5%-acetylene feed much more closely. The two stated feed fractions differ seventy-fold. Its claimed water stoichiometry and solvent lifetime should retain this unresolved condition identity.

This is an essential-output comparator with stronger reported purification/lifetime endpoints, not a matched superiority determination. Feed, catalyst, electrolyte, product denominator and complete costs differ.

The [Pd2/ND@G author correction](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-76860-2), 17 August 2026, was acquired. It corrects a copied Supplementary Fig. 6 TEM line scan and duplicate `Up 4`/`Up 2` source entries. The notice states the SI/source file were corrected; it does not state a catalytic endpoint correction. Any reuse of that catalyst's structural evidence must use the corrected files, which were not acquired in this worker.
