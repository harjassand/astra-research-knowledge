The strongest statement justified by this comparison is:

The derivation provides a worst-case polynomial scalar-prediction guarantee for the specified pure-dephasing interacting class that is not supplied by the precise comparator results inspected.

The construction also leaves general hardness intact. It is restricted to one dimension, fixed positive physical dephasing, specified bounded-range interactions, tracial response, and additive accuracy. Exponentially small requested error can still require exponentially many resources.
