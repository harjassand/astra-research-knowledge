EXACT PRIMARY-LITERATURE PRIORITY AUDIT
2026-10-15. Source availability and internal proof reconstruction only.

The strongest settled historical finding is narrow but decisive: the
loadbearing exponential-anticommutator lemma is an immediate corollary
of an original arbitrary-Hilbert-space theorem published in 1993.
The exact MH scalar-noise formulation and the C-infinity unbounded
counterexample remain untraced as first publications. The full compact
cone-valued characterization is not declared subsumed or original.

A. UCHIYAMA: ACTUAL THEOREMS, NOT ABSTRACTS

M. Uchiyama, Commutativity of selfadjoint operators, Pacific J. Math.
161(2) (1993), 385-392. Original publisher PDF:
https://msp.org/pjm/1993/161-2/pjm-v161-n2-p10-s.pdf

Theorem 7 and proofs on printed 388-389/PDF 5-6. Theorem 1's printed
inequality is non-strict >=0, despite OCR often reading it as >0.

Theorem 1's exact interface is bounded C,D>=0 and positivity of
CD^n+D^nC for every positive integer n, equivalent to commutation.
Taking D=exp(A), C=exp(t_0 B) is sufficient for the MH necessity once
the actual noise moments justify Laplace positivity. This source already
removes finite dimension, discrete spectrum and eigenvector hypotheses.

Theorem 7's different interface is selfadjoint A, bounded X>=0 and some
a>=||X|| with

 exp(tA)<=exp(t(A+epsilon X))<=exp(t(A+epsilon aI))
 for every t,epsilon>0.

The non-strict a>=||X|| was checked visually; extracted text can read
the same sign as >. It concludes XA is contained in AX. The source proves it through
spectral order, not a bound on the norm of exponentials. At the supplied
Astra pin, FU's compact-scalar-noise rigidity theorem ALREADY records
this 1993 theorem as its predecessor. The current audit does not
rediscover that prior adjudication. The newer MH argument is another
short transfer from the same paper, this time its Theorem 1.
