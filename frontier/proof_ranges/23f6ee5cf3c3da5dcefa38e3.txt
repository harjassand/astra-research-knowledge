## 7. Why one monotone linear diffusion is not a universal operation

The following is a failure of a broad proof mechanism, not a quasiconvexity counterexample and not the principal advance of this note.

### Theorem D: a strictly coercive polyconvex example defeats every fixed constant-coefficient linear parabolic monotonicity rule

Set

    F_eta(A)=(det A)^2+eta |A|^4, eta=1/10000.

It is polyconvex and satisfies the uniform zero-boundary Jensen estimate

    integral [F_eta(A0+Dphi)-F_eta(A0)]
       >= (eta/3) integral |Dphi|^4.                        (7.1)

Nevertheless no one real constant-coefficient operator

    Lf=sum_{i,j=1}^2 C^{ij} partial_i partial_j f

with uniformly positive symmetric principal symbol can make integral F_eta(Df) nonincreasing for every smooth periodic f under partial_t f=Lf. The C^{ij} may be arbitrary real 2x2 output matrices, not just scalar diffusivities.

**Exact compatible heat increase.** Take the smooth periodic potential

    phi_1=cos x+cos y,
    phi_2=(cos 2x+cos 2y)/2
          +(4/3)[cos(x+y)+cos(x-y)].                        (7.2)

Under ordinary heat let a=e^{-t}, b=e^{-4t}, c=(4/3)e^{-2t}. Its gradient is

    [ -a sin x,                    -a sin y                 ]
    [ -2 sin x(b cos x+c cos y),    -2 sin y(b cos y+c cos x)].

Direct exact trigonometric averaging gives

    E q(Dphi_t)^2 = (1/2)a^2(c-b)^2,

    E |Dphi_t|^4 = (5/4)a^4+2a^2b^2+4a^2c^2
                  +(5/4)b^4+10b^2c^2+5c^4.

Their derivatives at zero are respectively 1/3 and -34621/81. Consequently

    d/dt E F_eta(Dphi_t)|_{t=0}
       = 235379/810000 >0.                                 (7.3)

All numbers in this display are checked using rational polynomial and trigonometric-moment arithmetic.

**Why changing a fixed linear metric does not cure universality.** The energy F_eta is invariant under both output and input O(2), including reflections. If a fixed operator L were energy-decreasing for every periodic field, each operator obtained by conjugating output and square-torus dihedral symmetries would also be energy-decreasing for every field. The derivative of the energy is linear in the operator's coefficients, so averaging these inequalities preserves the property.

Output dihedral averaging sends any real 2x2 matrix M to (tr M/2)I, including when M has a skew part. Input dihedral averaging then sends the coefficient tensor to kappa delta_{ij} I. Uniform strong parabolicity implies kappa>0. Thus kappa times the ordinary Laplacian would have to decrease the energy universally, contradicting (7.3).

Exact differential forms, arbitrary polynomial divergences, or tensor Gram representations cannot change this integrated derivative. The theorem concerns fixed linear parabolic monotonicity, not nonlinear flows, nonlocal temporal certificates, sums of separately controlled energies, or direct polyconvexity. Indeed (7.1) is already a direct proof for this example. QED.

The estimate (7.1) follows from determinant Jensen and the elementary identity

    |A+B|^4-|A|^4-4|A|^2 A:B
      = |B|^4/3+6(A:B+|B|^2/3)^2
        +2(|A|^2|B|^2-(A:B)^2) >= |B|^4/3.
