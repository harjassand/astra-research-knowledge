## 10. Two decisive adversarial tests

### 10.1 The unrestricted actuator can make the proposal unnecessary

If the actuator can add -D and doing so is allowed by the scientific task, it sets every detuning to zero. With baseline eta, its kinetic rate is

    q_cancel = 2 epsilon^2 |T_xy|^2/eta >= q_*.

For |Delta|>=eta,

    q_cancel/q_* = 2|Delta|/eta,

which is unbounded as detuning grows. No spectroscopy is needed if the same native common multiplier can simply be set to zero. This is a stronger direct controller, not a straw-man full-model-identification method.

Preserving D may be a legitimate physical or functional requirement, but it must be established independently. A material that does not permit coherent cancellation but does permit calibrated Hamiltonian-referenced reservoir coupling has not been identified and validated in this investigation. A programmable demonstration that artificially prohibits cancellation would test the mathematics; by itself it would not establish new engineering power.

### 10.2 A uniform all-observable quantum-to-kinetic theorem is false

Take N independent hard-core dimers, one particle in each, with H=J sum_i X_i, D=0 and independent dephasing eta. Choose 0<J<eta/4. For one dimer the infinite-temperature Z autocorrelation c(t) satisfies

    c''+eta c'+4J^2 c=0,  c(0)=1, c'(0)=0,

and is positive and decreasing. For the product observable Z_1...Z_N, independence gives correlation c(t)^N. Since c(t)=1-2J^2 t^2+O(t^3), Laplace scaling yields

    integral_0^infinity c(t)^N dt
       ~ sqrt(pi)/(2 sqrt(2) J sqrt(N)).

The independent kinetic flip chain instead gives

    integral_0^infinity exp[-4N J^2 t/eta]dt = eta/(4N J^2).

The ratio grows as sqrt(2pi) J sqrt(N)/eta. Hence fixed small J/eta does not give a volume-uniform approximation for all zero-frequency observable quadratic forms. This remains true in an exceptionally simple member of the physical class.

It does not disprove a volume-uniform theorem for the local current. It proves that a general norm or all-observable Schur-complement argument cannot supply that theorem. A successful extension must exploit current locality, conservation and the physical control errors specifically.
