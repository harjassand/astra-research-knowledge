## What the source actually establishes

### Exact task and sufficient certificate

Inputs are finite Hermitian matrices `h0,h1` on `A tensor C`, with all active
interactions and all changes included. Every compatible Hamiltonian pair is

    H_j(K)=h_j tensor I_R + I_A tensor K,

where any finite R and any Hermitian K on C tensor R are permitted, with no
norm, locality, sign, spectral gap, state-preparation or correlation-decay
promise. E_j are unconstrained minima on the entire Hilbert space.

The operation is

    L(h0,h1)=max_{Phi UCP on A} lambda_min[h1-Phi(h0)].

Unitality cancels the common K exactly, and complete positivity preserves
`H0>=E0 I` in the presence of arbitrary entangled C,R. Hence every feasible
residual is a universal comparison certificate. Mere positivity is insufficient:
partial transposition of a singlet gives a negative eigenvalue.

### Converse, quantitative witness and its scope

Minimax converts the optimal UCP certificate to a minimizing state rho_AC.
Purifying rho into a finite R converts channel optimization into minimizing
h0 at a fixed CR marginal sigma. Its conditional-energy SDP has strictly
feasible primal and dual on the support of sigma. A dual Z together with

    K=-V Z V^dagger + M(I-VV^dagger)

and `M>=B0+B0^2/eta` produces `Delta(K)<=L+eta`. This proves exact closure
extrema, not merely a sufficient family of maps. An R dimension at most dr
suffices. Smoothing rho controls the dual conditioning and makes the witness
energy scale polynomial in local matrix dimensions and inverse accuracy.

I reconstructed the key steps, including support compression, the purification
reachability argument, the explicit Slater points, and the block penalty bound.
No proof gap was found in this unrestricted finite-dimensional contract. This
is same-team analytic reconstruction, not external validation or formal proof.
The resulting K can be nonlocal, very strong and unlike a real material. It
proves what the permitted information class cannot exclude, not how to build
that environment economically in a laboratory.

### Positive and negative examples

For the two-active-spin, two-collar-spin motif

    h0=S0.S1+S0.S2+S1.S3,
    h1=2*S0.S1+S0.S2+S1.S3,

the exact rational UCP witness certifies

    -3/4 <= E1(K)-E0(K) <= -22421/100000

for all unrestricted common environments. Independent Pauli/Kronecker
reconstruction confirms that the certificate's h matrices are precisely this
motif, rather than simply verifying arbitrary supplied matrices. Exact
unitality and 16 positive LDL pivots for each of the Choi and residual matrices
pass. The simpler symbolic channel gives the weaker `-(5-sqrt(13))/8` upper
bound. Nonunitary maps are necessary for a strictly positive reverse residual
in this trace-zero example; mixed-unitary maps preserve trace.

The independent rational near-extremal fixture at collar exchange
`t=-59307/100000` encloses Delta in `[-0.22421874,-0.22421872]`. It brackets the
sharp uniform upper endpoint to within 0.00000874 of the certificate. This is
exact finite arithmetic, not a numerical match with the optimizer.

For the kagome patch, an arbitrary common environment can ferromagnetically
align the five collar spins into total S=5/2. Projected boundary spins equal
S_C/5, yielding a positive +1/4 energy change in the strong-penalty limit. At
finite M=100 the supplied norm/block bound gives `Delta>=47/476>0`; numerical
replay agrees. The actual antiferromagnetic 12-site kagome control has a negative
change. Thus the same local patch genuinely does not determine the sign under
the unrestricted-environment contract. Globally valid stability constraints or
more optimization cannot rule out an explicitly permitted counterenvironment.

### Costs and scientific power

The algorithm starts after acquiring h0,h1 and verifying that the environment
is exactly common. Checking arbitrary global term lists may require reading
them all. Hamiltonian model construction, omitted electronic terms, error
calibration and a useful comparison radius are not acquired by the theorem.

Dense input storage is O(d^2 r^2), Choi storage O(d^4), image arithmetic
O(d^4 r^2), and dense spectral verification O(d^6+(dr)^3). Standard additive
weak SDP optimization can be polynomial in supplied matrix dimensions and bit
accuracy under the stated rational bounded-input contract. Neither that fact
nor the empirical cutting-plane convergence is polynomial in physical-spin
count. At eight active qubits the full Choi has about 4.3 billion entries.

Noncommon model errors eta0,eta1 enlarge both interval sides by eta0+eta1.
Exactly common environment-only errors cancel; common errors touching A do not
generally cancel. Unknown common errors touching A have residual
`delta h-Phi(delta h)` and require their own bound.

The control graphs are canonical small models built by the producer; their
full ground energies were calculated by ordinary numerical diagonalization.
The dimer is an intentionally easy diagnostic. No independently supplied hard
molecular benchmark or measured matched advantage was completed. The exact
scientific target is still useful certified differences in correlated matter
with affordable acquisition, model errors and active-region costs.
