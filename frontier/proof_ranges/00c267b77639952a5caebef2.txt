# Autonomous quantum control and loss echo research decision

16 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane. **Mission not achieved.** This campaign constructed a modified local quantum-control primitive with a nontrivial two-counter-fault theorem, repaired a finite one-use encoded example with genuinely noncommuting gates, and developed a separate loss-echo sensing operation through exact reconstruction, finite-duration acquisition and counting-noise tests. It did not construct a complete noisy reusable autonomous controller, prove a 2D continuous-time threshold, establish a major new foundational theorem, or acquire a new physical capability. Historical novelty and external mathematical correctness remain unestablished.

The best next mathematical investment is the missing **general damaged-domain discharge and reuse operation**, using the local construction below as a concrete receiver. More conditional service inequalities would leave that obstruction intact. The independent sensing line should retain its target-specific discriminator, but full hidden-rate inversion does not presently merit the main tranche: familiar realization theory owns the broad mechanism, and finite-noise conditioning is poor.

## Frontier A construction and exact surviving theorem

The strongest new construction is a **crossed plaquette execution surface**, independently invented by s01. Two offset tilings partition the square lattice into 2×2 gate supports. Each site has a finite execution residue `c in Z_q`, even `q>=8`, an initialized affine color, and replicated finite program words. A successful gate jump applies its prescribed local quantum channel **and increments all four participant counters in the same atomic CPTP transition**. A timing repair cannot independently authorize quantum progress. All guards, counter writes, and color/program repairs have fixed finite support; the largest counter repair read box is 6×6. This modifies the controller and is not a theorem about the source's original generator.

The tile-validity constraint makes a clean site's execution counter uniquely determined by its six crossed-tile neighbors. Consequently an isolated onsite classical-control fault is repaired without omitting or replaying a gate; a simultaneous quantum action of that physical fault is still an ordinary local CP error. Clean accepted events are a linear extension of the actual per-wire gate DAG, so their channel agrees with the intended circuit on an arbitrary reference. This includes nonunitary prescribed channels, with their reset ports declared.

The substantive extension is a collective counter repair. When an invalid tile has no enabled singleton repair, it searches assignments changing at most two counters inside the tile and validating its overlap-distance-two moat. A fixed minimum-Hamming, lexicographic choice makes this a complete finite transition rule. For at most **two counter-only onsite corruptions before retirement**, at arbitrary separated times with ideal gate and repair interleavings, the rule retires the counter discrepancy in at most two repair events. Total enabled repair rate is at least one in the normalized base model. The final valid surface equals the clean ghost surface or differs by one entire tile's one-step advance/rollback. Thus the quantum history has at most **one local omitted, extra, or replayed tile operation**, with a preparation-port charge if rollback crosses initialization. Counter repair does not claim to reconstruct that operation's unknown quantum past.

The proof uses a distance-four property. Any valid surface differing from a reachable clean surface at at most four sites must have a complete crossed tile as its support. Its unchanged opposite-tile neighbors restrict the changes to one step, and mixed changes violate tile validity. During a two-fault episode no gate touches a dirty site; accepted exterior gates extend the clean ghost history. The moat excludes an off-target collective repair. This is a pathwise proof, not an inference from simulation. S06 independently checked the argument and supplied exhaustive L4 and sampled L6/L8 checks; those checks have finite scope.

The [complete transition table](workers/s01_controller_repair/TRANSITION_TABLE.md), [proof](workers/s01_controller_repair/REPORT.md), and [independent adversary](workers/s06_controller_adversary/REPORT.md) retain the exact model and corrections. In particular, an earlier claim that all neighboring tiles freeze was false: clean neighboring sites may progress; the ghost-surface argument handles that progress.

### Failures that prevent promotion

- Three counter corruptions on one even tile, to residues 1,2,3 from a flat zero surface, defeat the current singleton-plus-collective rule. The relevant ideal service class has no repair. This disproves a general eroder/service assertion for this table, not every possible architecture or its noisy dynamics under additional faults.
- Two faults making a 2–2 split in the four program copies can stall a ready tile. Three identical wrong copies can select and preserve a wrong program word. Thus the two-counter theorem is **not** a general two-onsite-fault theorem for all registers or a bound on persistent program-source fanout.
- A repaired valid counter field may encode a skipped noncommuting operation. Classical validity cannot erase that error. The rule must deliver its quantum discrepancy to an actual fault-tolerant gadget/EC interface.
- Local support and finite alphabet do not establish level-uniform service, overlap, damage erosion or self-simulation. The source's synchronous exRec constants cannot be copied to the enlarged guards and fault packets.

### Independent architecture and controlled recombination

S02 independently used quantum occupancy as execution history: an idempotent local move consumes occupied input/vacant output ports, applies a gate and transfers the unknown data. This gives clean exact-once execution on a one-shot DAG; permanent fresh ports cost circuit-volume area. Encoded bank parity can also disambiguate isolated counter repairs. With a trusted bank witness and clean exterior, an isolated tile's counter history is exactly recoverable. That is a receiving lemma, not a noisy-bank theorem.

Recombination into a finite carousel fails at a precise interface. Two adjacent sites can corrupt counter **and** bank parity on two overlapping tiles so that neither individual tile has a compatible repair or abort. A joint seven-site or growing damaged-domain discharge is needed. A counterfeit packet travelling through later correct CNOTs is ordinary propagation of one bad input, and was explicitly **not** counted as many fresh faulty locations. The real issues are lineage, predecessor retirement, EC boundaries and reuse. See [candidate](workers/s02_alternative_architecture/CANDIDATE_SPEC.md) and [recombination tests](workers/s02_alternative_architecture/RECOMBINATION_TEST.md).

S03 then built a restricted finite receiver rather than assuming exact history recovery. Five disjoint tracks carry a `[[5,1,3]]` code. Each applies physical `X` then a cyclic Clifford `C`; their transversal logical channels genuinely do not commute. Faulty phase bits can skip, repeat or reorder those operations on one track. An emergency counter discharge need not decide whether a gate occurred. A prescribed qutrit Gram-syndrome recovery corrects the resulting arbitrary one-site output error, including vacancy leakage, and a one-use transfer moves the code into a different bank.

Adversarial testing found that readiness based on raw occupancy caused a one-fault vacancy stall. The corrected gate acts on the vacancy sector without blocking. Root found and repaired the output-bank version: the terminal jump discards the **unowned** B banks before recovery and transfer, so a noisy phantom cannot prevent service. For a local A/B fault, tracing B gives Kraus matrices `<b|M_alpha|vacuum>` on one A site; the explicit recovery corrects their span. S06 audited this repair. A tripled one-use terminal flag prevents one flag fault from replaying the transfer. The future seam requires both spent authorization and occupied output; a phantom alone cannot launch a successor.

The resulting [finite discharge theorem](workers/s03_history_interface/FINITE_DISCHARGE.md) covers its specified one-onsite fault family and a two-counter fork confined to one data track, arbitrary ideal interleavings, and an arbitrary reference. Conditional on no additional faults, the fork completes with mean at most `191/30` and tail at most `11 exp(-t/4)` in its unit-rate base model. Additional physical noise during that wait is explicitly additional fault exposure. Static program/color, the fixed-support atomic recovery jump, broader clusters, a noisy implementation of that jump, and indefinite bank reuse are outside the theorem. This is an actual finite one-use operation with a restricted contract, **not the requested complete recursive noisy controller**.

Root also supplied [all-history channel verification](root/ALL_HISTORY_CHANNEL_CONTRACT.md): saturate `D×k` Kraus path-map spans over finite control/fault-budget states, then test either the prescribed decoder or Knill–Laflamme recoverability. It handles infinitely many finite loop histories without a trajectory cutoff. Exact five-qubit replay accepts arbitrary noncommuting single-site loops and rejects an erased logical-X loop. Finite-dimensional reachability and KL are established methods; this explicit controller receiver is not claimed as a new foundational theorem. Service, graph closure and recursive composition remain separate.

## Frontier B operation, experiment and resource boundary

S04 pursued hidden kinetic sensing after two other mechanisms failed their predecessor/access tests. Let `A` be an unknown conservative finite-state Markov generator, `b` a preparation, and `c=1^T` the sole total-survival detector. With no intervention, `c exp(At)b=1` reveals no internal rates. A single fixed, uncalibrated state-dependent loss field `D` gives illuminated generator `M=A-D`. Two loss pulses separated by darkness produce

    Y(t1,tau,t2) = c exp(M t2) exp(A tau) exp(M t1) b.

When the on-mode is minimal and D is genuinely diagonal with distinct entries, single-pulse Hankel data and mixed echo data recover A and M in a common realization basis; diagonalizing their difference and normalizing by the aggregate output fixes physical state axes up to permutation. The exact proof and three-state reconstruction survive the adversarial audit. Two positive two-state models have identical entire single-pulse survival but different echo responses. Finite positive-duration data replace derivatives when sampling/log branches are controlled. Root's two-incommensurate-grid extension removes the **exact** rate-cap premise, but gives no uniform finite-precision inverse. Fast mixing, nearly uniform loss and singular Hankel matrices remain decisive.

This is a specialized realization operation, with substantial [bilinear](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0610633) and [restricted switched-system](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sysconle.2007.04.006) predecessors. The exact specialization's priority was not established. Illumination that also changes transitions preserves a stochastic-similarity ambiguity; a true diagonal-loss law must be independently acquired. Exact parameter count is not acquisition efficiency: the compact and richer synthetic Fisher designs can be severely ill-conditioned. Those are fixture-specific results, not universal impossibility bounds. A constrained joint likelihood fit to the same observations is the relevant incumbent.

S05 produced a more obtainable **target**, the two-state dark relaxation rate `g=a+r`. For fixed but unknown preparation and probe,

    Y(W)-Y(0) = B(1-exp(-gW)),
    [Y(2W)-Y(0)]/[Y(W)-Y(0)] = 1+exp(-gW).

Common detector gain and offset cancel. A fourth wait supplies a held-out model check; three saturated settings cannot validate two-state physics. For the exact single-pulse aliases, a finite delayed-probe classifier, with independently supplied ±0.5% dose/drift bounds and gain at least 0.75, has an explicit 5% per-world error guarantee at **52,861 independent reset trials per arm**. Model time has no supplied physical conversion. Same-data profile likelihood also succeeds; no estimator advantage is claimed.

The independent finite-data theorem is a resource limit: if all losses lie in `[d-delta,d+delta]`, `delta/d<=1/6`, any terminal-survival assay has pairwise KL at most `16(delta/d)^2`. Even adaptively chosen, independently reset assays need at least `(d/delta)^2/72` samples for two-world success at least 2/3. The echo target matches that sample exponent on compact rate classes, with supplied nonzero gain/contrast. Kill-time records, state-resolved measurements and transition-changing controls are different, stronger interfaces. See [finite-data proof and synthetic test](workers/s05_independent_challenger/FOLLOWUP.md).

The first credible physical demonstration would separate single-pulse-equivalent kinetic models using held-out dark waits and raw total counts, with measured OFF leakage, stable preparation/probe, dose and detector drift, and independent same-sample state-resolved truth. Related [ion-trap work](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12525174/) already measures two-isomer kinetics with stronger readout; continuous photodestruction exists on a different platform. The combined high-rate, unchanged-A, total-only interface has not been acquired. Full costs include preparation/reset, count independence, timing, illumination, calibration, discarded trials, detector efficiency and independent validation. No experiment, superior physical capability, exceptional new theorem or civilisation-scale enabling operation was demonstrated. The negative-response network challenger was also retained as a negative control: its strongest equally informed empirical port method ties it exactly.

## Allocation decision and research architecture

Recommend approximately **60%** of the next reasoning tranche to a genuine finite damaged-domain eroder/discharge and causally drained recycler; **25%** to a protected independent high-leverage invention frontier, with this sensing line continued only if a new measurement interface or consequential target is actually available; **15%** to independent attacks on concrete receiving constructions and eventual external specialist review. These are prospective allocations, not measured compute shares.

The next A deliverable must include all registers and boundaries, noisy program/color/bank storage, arbitrary allowed local faults, joint retirement/service, a code-correctable quantum shadow, protected wait volume, and a recursive compilation preserving those conditions. It must defeat the saved three-counter and two-program traps. Raising the finite fault order alone is insufficient if a finite trap can persist and corrupt reused outputs. A proof that assumes this eroder or a perfect encoded move has transferred the obstruction rather than removed it.
