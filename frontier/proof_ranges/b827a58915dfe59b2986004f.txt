## 10. Reproduction and sources

Run, with the bundled helper modules in the same directory:

    python verify_circulation.py

It uses Python's exact Fraction arithmetic and SymPy. No optimizer, stored numerical graph potential, third-party manuscript, or previous verification JSON is an input. Nine grouped checks include all 35 monomial column identities, scalar margins, degree-two profile curvature, the exact eight-loop area moments, G's acoustic certificate, a nonzero-cubic optimized example, orientation-reversed compatible fields, timing convexity algebra, and all 32,805 graph inequalities.

The code proves only the finite algebra it executes. Rank-one supporting inequalities, Green's theorem, approximation, Parseval positivity, and telescoping proofs are established analytically in this note. prior_replay.log preserves the successful nine-group replay of the preceding GLOBAL_COMPATIBILITY verifier.

Primary literature consulted:

- G. Bruno and F. Pasqualotto, Quasiconvexity for the Dacorogna–Marcellini Energy, arXiv:2608.06367v1 (2026). Its theorem covers both left and right rotational invariance, not the entire left-invariant cone. The heat-flow argument is not a premise here.
- K. Astala, T. Iwaniec, I. Prause and E. Saksman, A hunt for sharp L^p-estimates and Rank-One Convex Variational Integrals, arXiv:1403.1095v1. Rank-one supporting inequalities and integrated positive map classes are important predecessors.
- A. Guerra and R. Teixeira da Costa, Numerical evidence towards a positive answer to Morrey's problem, arXiv:2006.09063. Finite-wave, laminate and counterexample-search context.
- T. Lyons and D. Yang, The theory of rough paths via one-forms and the extension of an argument of Schwartz to rough differential equations, arXiv:1503.06175v2 (2015). Polynomial one-form integrals and finite-order path encodings are established machinery, not inventions claimed here.
- P. Bernard, Young measures, superposition and transport, arXiv:math/0701451; Indiana Univ. Math. J. 57 (2008). Closed/holonomic measures and minimizing curves are prior variational frameworks. Their results do not automatically establish the required global calibration.
- R. Rios-Zertuche, Deformations of closed measures and variational characterization of measures invariant under the Euler–Lagrange flow, arXiv:1810.07838v1. Further closed-measure context, not a proof premise.
