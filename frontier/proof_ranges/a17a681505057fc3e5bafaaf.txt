## 6. Independent augmentation identifies one entire series

For any full-support references \(q\) and \(r\),
\[
\chi^2(p\otimes r\Vert q\otimes r)=\chi^2(p\Vert q).
\]
Combining (1) and (14), and varying \(p\) near \(q\), identifies all power-series coefficients for reference \(q\otimes r\) with those for \(q\). Varying the other tensor factor identifies them with those for \(r\). Thus
\[
a_k(q)=a_k(q\otimes r)=a_k(r).
\]
They are universal coefficients \(a_k\); no continuity in \(q\) is needed.

To prove infinite radius of convergence, fix any \(z>0\), choose \(n>z+1\), and let \(q\) be uniform on \(n\) states. Put
\[
p=(1-\theta)q+\theta\delta_1,
\qquad
\theta=\sqrt{\frac z{n-1}}<1.
\]
This \(p\) has full support and \(\chi^2(p\Vert q)=z\). Equation (14) gives \(\sum_ka_kz^k=D_n(p,q)<\infty\). Since this holds for every positive \(z\), the series has infinite radius. The necessity of (3)–(4) is proved.

## 7. Converse and automatic data processing

Let \(D=\Psi(\chi^2)\) with (3). Continuity, smoothness, normalization, and (1) are immediate.

For a reversible generator, decompose the initial deviation into orthonormal eigenvectors:
\[
u=\sum_jb_je_j,
\qquad
\chi^2(p_t\Vert q)=\sum_jb_j^2e^{-2\lambda_jt}.
\tag{15}
\]
This is the Laplace transform of the finite positive measure
\(\sigma=\sum_jb_j^2\delta_{2\lambda_j}\). Then
\[
D(p_t,q)=\int e^{-\lambda t}\,d\mu(\lambda),
\qquad
\mu=\sum_{k\geq1}a_k\sigma^{*k}.
\tag{16}
\]
The measure is positive and finite: its total mass is
\(\sum_ka_k[\chi^2(p\Vert q)]^k=\Psi(\chi^2(p\Vert q))\).
Hence (2) holds. This completes the classification proof.

For data processing, let \(K\) be a stochastic matrix and remove any output coordinates with zero \(qK\)-mass. The output likelihood ratio is the conditional mean of the input likelihood ratio under the joint law \(q_iK_{ij}\). Conditional Jensen therefore gives
\[
\chi^2(pK\Vert qK)\leq\chi^2(p\Vert q).
\]
Because all \(a_k\) are nonnegative, \(\Psi\) is nondecreasing on \([0,\infty)\). Therefore
\[
D(pK,qK)\leq D(p,q).
\tag{17}
\]
