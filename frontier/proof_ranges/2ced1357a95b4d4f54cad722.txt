## 5. Product statistics and complete error/cost contract

Conditional on the complete stationary code, assume product records are iid. Within each record the observation time is uniform on [0,T] and independent of its ancestry-pattern draw; patterns are iid from one fixed code-independent stationary law, and carbon labels are conditionally independent given time, pattern and actual code. This full joint sampling law is an idealized interface; raw detector ion counts need not realize it. The post-isolation critic supplied an exact time-pattern coupling counterexample to the marginal-only interpretation of draft 01; see JOINT_ANCESTRY_REVIEW_CORRECTION.md and the preserved draft.

Put R_W=((1+|b|)/a)^4. The corrected per-product statistic has |W4|<=R_W. For N such products,

    Var(C_hat) <= 16 n^2 K_code/T + R_W^2/N,   n=4.

The first term is code variance, the second conditional sampling variance. Independently prepared phase and telegraph campaigns then give

    Var(M_hat) <= 16 Var(C_hat) + Var(Q_hat).

Chebyshev gives a conservative confidence radius; a Gaussian interval is not implied. Independent repeat campaigns or an acquired mixing/covariance model may improve it. For symmetric contrast R_W=a^-4, so the purity penalty remains a^-8. The decoder's coefficients 4 and -1 can make a very small joint contrast expensive. For the exact witness, contrast 0.98, common duration/counts in the two independent campaigns, error target (9/8192)/4 and Chebyshev failure 0.05, the conservative bound used by the checker gives sufficient N=10,595,346,359 products and T about 2.399e12 tau per campaign. These are loose sufficient upper budgets, not lower bounds or an optimized campaign; they support no practical advantage claim. If waveform bias alone spends that error budget, uniform normalized feed error must be at most about 1.3733e-5. Count calibration, backgrounds, uncertain normalization, catalyst ageing and isotope-induced history changes need additional acquired bounds.

If actual normalized feed values stay in [-1,1] and differ from the intended code by at most epsilon_u at every ancestral entry, telescoping products gives at most 4epsilon_u error in each corrected expectation. Thus 4C-Q has at most 20epsilon_u waveform error across both campaigns. If an unaccounted fraction eta of histories can lie outside the code prehistory, a conservative corrected-readout error is 2eta R_W per campaign, hence 10eta R_W after decoding. Valid tighter purity/history assumptions can improve this, but must be stated. A measured whole-product response matrix requires its own conditioning and error budget.

Preparation covers the relevant prehistory or carries that bias; a common delay is a statistic's gauge, not permission to skip wash-in. The chemical feed is constant, but isotope dose, three-level transitions, switching hardware bandwidth, labelled inventory, detector/product recovery and total catalyst time are paid. No reaction-hazard/rate or spectral model is silently supplied.
