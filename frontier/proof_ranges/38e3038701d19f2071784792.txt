## 9. Reproducibility and provenance

`check_model.py` performs six groups of exact symbolic checks, 1,000 random four-port passive-network diagnostics, and a 40,804-point matching-grid check. `model_checks.json` contains the executed results. These computations test the declared network equations only. They do not constitute a fluid/material simulation, an independent proof audit, or experimental validation.

Selected primary-source anchors (bibliographic identifiers):

- Lizée et al., *Strong Electronic Winds Blowing under Liquid Flows on Carbon Surfaces*, Physical Review X 13, 011020 (2023), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevX.13.011020. Figure 4(f): glycerol/graphene current at zero longitudinal bias with a 10 V back gate.
- Coquinot, Bocquet and Kavokine, *Hydroelectric energy conversion of waste flows through hydroelectronic drag*, PNAS 121, e2411613121 (2024), DOI 10.1073/pnas.2411613121; arXiv:2403.20209v2. Coupled transport, reciprocal pumping proposal, and theoretical material estimates.
- Coquinot et al., *Momentum tunnelling between nanoscale liquid flows*, arXiv:2501.01253. Prior theory/simulation of solid-mediated liquid coupling; not this remote electrical topology.
- Viola and DiVincenzo, *Hall Effect Gyrators and Circulators*, Physical Review X 4, 021019 (2014), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevX.4.021019; arXiv:1312.5190v3. Contact losses and finite-frequency reactive alternatives.
- Büttiker, *Absence of backscattering in the quantum Hall effect in multiprobe conductors*, Physical Review B 38, 9375 (1988), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevB.38.9375. Multiprobe chiral transport framework.
- Mahoney et al., *On-Chip Microwave Quantum Hall Circulator*, Physical Review X 7, 011007 (2017), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevX.7.011007. Experimental microwave device; not a DC efficiency precedent.
- Keerthi et al., *Water friction in nanofluidic channels made from two-dimensional crystals*, Nature Communications 12, 3092 (2021), DOI 10.1038/s41467-021-23325-3. Fabricated slit geometries, slip variability and noncontinuum qualifications.
- Winn et al., *Unidirectional flow from continuous broken symmetries*, arXiv:2603.27474 (2026). Experimentally grounded nonlinear soft-valve alternative.
- Energy Recovery, *PX Q400 Product Brochure* (November 2024). Manufacturer-specified bulk comparator, not independently reproduced performance in this investigation.

No novelty claim is made for Onsager reciprocity, gyrators, quantum Hall chirality, hydraulic pressure exchange, standard network elimination, or their mathematical analogies. The candidate originality is the specific stationary-interface, neutral-liquid, remote electronic composition and its experimentally testable operating contract. The literature search did not establish historical priority.
