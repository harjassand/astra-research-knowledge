# Exact fixed-rate channel-compiler radius

Independent audit, 16 October 2026. Verdict: the proposed factor-two refinement and minimax claim are correct, with the scope and norm conventions below. No canonical source was edited.

## Statement and conventions

Let d >= 2, nu > 0, and let E: M_d(C) -> M_d(C) be complex-linear, Hermiticity preserving and trace annihilating (Tr E(X)=0 for every X). Use the ordinary, unhalved diamond norm, for which channels have norm 1 and channel distances are at most 2. Define

J(Phi) = (Phi tensor Id)(|Omega><Omega|),  |Omega> = d^(-1/2) sum_k |k,k>.

Then the largest eta such that D + E/nu is CPTP for every such E with ||E||_diamond <= eta is exactly 2 nu/d^2. Here D(X)=Tr(X) I/d. The closed ball at equality is included.

The source REGULARIZED_ROBUST_MEMORY.md explicitly uses normalized Choi and a channel-distance bound 2, so these conventions match. Its current nu/d^2 sufficient bound is valid but not sharp.

## Sufficiency, including the lost factor two

H=J(E) is Hermitian and has zero partial trace over the output, hence Tr H=0. Its Jordan decomposition H=H_+ - H_- satisfies

Tr H_+ = Tr H_- = ||H||_1/2.

Consequently ||H||_op <= ||H||_1/2. In addition, the normalized maximally entangled projector has trace norm 1, so the diamond-norm definition directly gives ||J(E)||_1 <= ||E||_diamond. Therefore

J(D+E/nu) >= (1/d^2 - ||E||_diamond/(2 nu)) I >= 0.

Complete positivity follows from the Choi criterion, and trace preservation follows from trace annihilation of E. Trace zero alone would suffice for this spectral inequality, but the stronger partial-trace-zero condition is essential for the trace-preserving conclusion.

## Sharpness

Choose Hilbert–Schmidt orthogonal unitaries U,V, i.e. Tr(U^* V)=0, and denote their conjugation channels by C_U,C_V. Their normalized Choi vectors u=(U tensor I)Omega and v=(V tensor I)Omega are orthonormal. The maximally entangled input proves

||C_V-C_U||_diamond >= || |v><v| - |u><u| ||_1 = 2.

The triangle inequality and channel norm 1 give the reverse bound. Thus E=(eta/2)(C_V-C_U) has diamond norm exactly eta, is HP and trace annihilating, and its Choi matrix has eigenvalues -eta/2,+eta/2,0,...,0. The minimum eigenvalue of J(D+E/nu) is exactly 1/d^2-eta/(2 nu), negative whenever eta>2 nu/d^2.

For d=2 take U=I,V=Z. In a Bell basis J(D+E/nu) has diagonal entries 1/4-eta/(2 nu), 1/4+eta/(2 nu), 1/4, 1/4. At eta=nu/2 it is positive semidefinite with eigenvalues 0,1/2,1/4,1/4; every larger eta fails CP.

## Minimax among fixed CPTP baselines

For a CPTP baseline N, define r(N) as the supremum of radii eta for which N+E/nu is CPTP for every admissible E in that diamond ball. Choose the d^2 Weyl unitaries X^a Z^b; their normalized Choi vectors {u_i} form an orthonormal basis for every integer d, not only prime d. Since Tr J(N)=1, some i has

<u_i,J(N)u_i> <= 1/d^2.

Choose any j unequal to i and E=(eta/2)(C_j-C_i). The preceding norm calculation applies, and

<u_i,J(N+E/nu)u_i> <= 1/d^2-eta/(2 nu).

Thus every eta>2 nu/d^2 fails universally for every N. Combined with sufficiency for D,

max_{N CPTP} r(N) = r(D) = 2 nu/d^2.

The proof establishes an optimizer, not uniqueness or nonuniqueness. Say “uniqueness is not addressed,” rather than suggesting that other optimizers have been demonstrated.

## Scope limits and useful stress test

- For d=1 the admissible perturbation space is {0}, so the universal radius is unbounded, not 2 nu.
- With a half-diamond-distance convention, the numerical radius is nu/d^2. With an unnormalized Choi matrix all intermediate Choi quantities gain a factor d; the standard-diamond answer remains 2 nu/d^2.
- The minimax baseline is fixed before E is selected. The statement does not cover E-dependent baselines, adaptive or increased proposal rates, multiple alternative compiler structures, restricted perturbation classes, or other norms.
- Importantly, in the depolarizing sharpness example U=I one has E=(eta/2)(C_V-Id), itself a Lindblad proposal generator. The full generator nu(D-Id)+E is then Lindblad at every eta>=0 and can be compiled at total rate nu+eta/2. Its failure at the original fixed rate nu is not nonphysicality or a logical-error lower bound.
- Hence d^2 is unavoidable for this fixed-rate affine uniform-ball compiler, not a universal obstruction to robust quantum memories. The source's other probability, topology, and recovery premises are outside this audit.
