6. A separate empirical test: can any depth make the pair bound?
I did not stop after finding the torque-test limitation. I derived a second constraint that does not require population stationarity.
Exact perspective geometry
Fix the adopted mean distance and measured sightlines. Let \(R\) be projected separation and \(z\) the unknown depth difference. Exact midpoint geometry gives
\[
r^2=R^2+z^2.
\]
The relative velocity reconstructed from proper motions and radial velocities has the affine form
\[
\mathbf v(z)=\mathbf w+z\mathbf b,
\]
where \(\mathbf w,\mathbf b\) are determined by the fixed observational inputs. The report derives these vectors explicitly, including perspective.
An isolated Newtonian two-body orbit can be strictly bound only if
\[
\eta(z)
=
\frac{\sqrt{R^2+z^2}\,|\mathbf w+z\mathbf b|^2}
{2G_NM}<1.
\]
Define \(\eta_{\min}\) by minimizing over physically admissible depth. Writing
\[
B=|\mathbf b|^2,\quad
d=\mathbf w\cdot\mathbf b,\quad
V=|\mathbf w|^2,
\]
differentiation reduces all interior extrema to
\[
\boxed{
3Bz^3+4dz^2+(V+2BR^2)z+2dR^2=0.
}
\tag{5}
\]
Evaluating the real roots and limiting endpoints gives the envelope without an eccentricity, orbital-phase or semimajor-axis prior.
Results
Four systems in the harmonized 33-pair cohort violate the binding condition at the adopted central measurements:
Archive ID	\(\eta_{\min}\)	Adopted total mass	Minimum total mass permitting binding
3	1.3335	\(2.223\,M_\odot\)	\(2.964\,M_\odot\)
33	1.3994	\(2.100\,M_\odot\)	\(2.939\,M_\odot\)
42	1.1468	\(1.520\,M_\odot\)	\(1.743\,M_\odot\)
59	1.3352	\(2.180\,M_\odot\)	\(2.911\,M_\odot\)


The constraint means something precise:
Holding those measured central inputs and masses fixed, changing the unknown line-of-sight separation cannot produce a bound isolated Newtonian two-body orbit.

It does not mean Newtonian gravity is false. A pair may be unbound or perturbed; an unresolved companion may contribute mass or velocity; or stellar-atmosphere corrections, mass estimates or astrometry may be wrong.
In the separate primary 32-pair table, only one system violates this envelope: HD189739/HD189760, the same pair as archive ID 59. That system was already identified as problematic in the primary study. This calculation reproduces a known observational issue; it does not discover a new anomaly. arXiv
