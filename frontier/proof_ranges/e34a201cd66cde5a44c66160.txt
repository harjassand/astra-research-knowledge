## 9. How equality cases supplied the constructive step

The successful discovery step was to impose acoustic equality cases on a candidate global positive representation.

For a plane-wave second derivative \(H=a\otimes b\otimes b\), the two derivatives of the gradient are proportional:

\[
 B_1=b_1(a\otimes b),\quad B_2=b_2(a\otimes b).
\]

Every two-form vanishes on this pair. If also

\[
 D^2F(A)[a\otimes b,a\otimes b]=0,
\]

then any positive corrected heat representation must vanish on the corresponding bilinear jet vector. Its positive Gram matrix must annihilate that vector.

At the endpoint above, the critical acoustic jet provides two independent kernel constraints for the scalar six-vector representation. Independence follows directly from the last two coordinates of the two kernel vectors: their determinant is proportional to $DC(1-j^2)$, where $D>216$ is the three-by-three determinant in Section 5 and $C=-24[\kappa^2(j-1)^2+4j^2-2j+4]<0$ is its third Cramer numerator. Restricting the correction to that forced rank-four face turns the remaining construction into exact linear equations over \(\mathbb Q[\kappa]\). The resulting matrix has a strictly positive four-dimensional block and a vanishing Schur complement, as proved above.

This is an application of elementary PSD kernel constraints and facial reduction, not a claim to have invented those general facts. Its demonstrated value here is that local equality cases determined the correction that proves a sharp global theorem. A stronger inequality guessed away from the acoustic equality set was not needed.
