Near a crossing belonging to band n in stage m, its local reference loop and
direction are among finitely many fixed band-n types. Fix a slightly larger
closed comparison collar around the chi=1 plateau, inside the same local type
and away from every other control support. For points at the plateau edge,
the comparison slab may leave chi=1 but must remain within this collar; the
collar's bounded bump derivatives give the same local estimate. On slabs of
length 4 log(1/eta_m(t_nu)), the sharper finite-frame residual is
 r_{n,m}(t)=O_n((eta_m(t)+t^(-3/2))(1+log(1/eta_m(t)))+eta_m(t)^2).
For fixed m, this divided by eta_m^(3/4) tends to zero. The graph correction is
bounded by C_n Omega_n(t) t^(-9/16). Dividing it by the desired eta_m^(3/4)
multiplies Omega_n by D_m^(3/4). Put a_nu=eta_m(t_nu)^(3/4). Finite-cluster
Taylor expansion and finite-matrix inversion therefore give the ENTIRE local
matrix expansion
 E_j^(n)=a_nu z_nu B_n(s(j),b(j))
          +O_n(a_nu^2+r_{n,m}(j)+Omega_n(j)j^(-9/16)).
This is a bound in full p_n-by-p_n matrix norm. The matrix B_n has uniformly
bounded norm over the finitely many band-n crossing types and b near a, and
its chosen off-diagonal entry has the source's fixed nonzero sign. Choose T_m
so that r_{n,m}/a_nu and D_m^(3/4)Omega_n are smaller than any prescribed band-n
fraction, simultaneously for n<=m and all later applicable times in that stage.
Then ||E_j^(n)||<=C'_n a_nu, with constants INDEPENDENT of m, and the chosen
entry has its signed leading term uniformly over all other controls. The entire
local norm bound is needed for Step 7; a bound only for the selected entry does
not suffice.

epsilon_m->0 be smaller than 2^(-m) min_{n<=m}rho_n times the needed finite

 |F_hi(j)| <= epsilon_m |r_hi(j)-1| outside that pair's plateaux,

all O_n(eta_nu^(1/4)) crossing trial bounds are <rho_n/10,
