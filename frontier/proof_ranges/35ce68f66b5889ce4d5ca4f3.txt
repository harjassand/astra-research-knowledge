THEOREM
For every integer d>=3 let Phi be the supplied channel
 Phi_ab(X)=tau(X)I+a(X-X^T)/2+b[(X+X^T)/2-tau(X)I], tau=Tr/d,
where a,b are real and Phi is CPTP and admits a CPTP two-output broadcaster
with both marginals Phi. These maps are unital and HS-self-adjoint.
There is ONE canonical pure-ensemble EB channel Psi with
 Psi >= 2|Phi|-I
as self-adjoint operators on the real Hilbert space of Hermitian matrices
with inner product tau(XY). Here |Phi| is its HS spectral absolute value,
not asserted to be a physical channel. Hence
 tau(A^2)-tau(A Psi(A)) <=2[tau(A^2)-tau(A Phi(A))]
for EVERY Hermitian A on the FULL algebra M_d. The same C2 holds for
finite tensor products, including signed spectra and all entangled global
observables, by the already independently proved positive tensor lemma.
The d2 case follows from the separately proved qubit theorem.
Constant2 is optimal in each dimension for this family.

This is the defining O(d)-covariant two-sector family. No statement about
arbitrary group representations, arbitrary unital self-compatible channels,
or membership of all channels in its sandwich/mixture closure is made.
The proof below is elementary and does not import a model-generated premise.

1. CHOI TRIANGLE AND NEGATIVE EIGENVALUES
Let F be the swap, e=sum_ij |ii><jj|=d P_Omega, and I the identity on d^2.
The normalized Choi matrix is
 J_ab=(a+b)P_Omega/2 +(b-a)F/(2d)+(1-b)I/d^2.
Its eigenvalues on Omega, symmetric-perpendicular, and antisymmetric are
 [2+(d-1)(d a+(d+2)b)]/(2d^2),
 [2-d a+(d-2)b]/(2d^2),
 [2+d a-(d+2)b]/(2d^2), respectively.
Thus these three numerators are nonnegative, exactly the CP condition.
Adding the first numerator to (d-1) times the third gives
 2d+2d(d-1)a>=0; adding it to (d-1) times the second gives
 2d+2d(d-1)b>=0. Therefore a,b>=-1/(d-1)>=-1/2.
The third CP inequality also gives (d+2)b<=2+d a.

2. TWO EXACT COMPATIBILITY BOUNDS
Let R=(id tensor B)(P_Omega) be the normalized Choi state of the supplied
broadcaster. It is positive, normalized, and its three one-site marginals
are I/d. For any Hermitian frame A_i,
 Tr R sum_i A_i^T tensor(A_i tensor I+I tensor A_i)
     =2 sum_i tau(A_i Phi(A_i)).

(a) Full traceless frame. For any tau-orthonormal traceless Hermitian basis,
 sum_i A_i^T tensor A_i=d e-I.
Thus its three-register star is d(e_AB+e_AC)-2I. The top eigenvalue of
e_AB+e_AC is d+1: on the span of |Omega>_AB tensor v_C and
|Omega>_AC tensor v_B, the two contraction vectors have overlap1/d;
the equivalent unnormalized two-vector matrix is [[d,1],[1,d]].
It vanishes on the orthogonal complement. Hence
 2[n_a a+n_s b] <= d(d+1)-2=(d-1)(d+2),
where n_a=d(d-1)/2 and n_s=(d-1)(d+2)/2. Equivalently
             d a+(d+2)b <= d+2.                    (O1)

(b) Antisymmetric frame. Let A_ij=-i sqrt(d/2)(E_ij-E_ji), i<j;
these n_a matrices are tau-orthonormal. Their pair sum is
 sum_ij A_ij^T tensor A_ij=(d/2)(e-F).
Their star is (d/2)K with
 K=e_AB+e_AC-F_AB-F_AC.
We prove K<=d I. Let U be the sum of the three contraction ranges,
spanned by u1(v)=sum_i |ii> tensor v, u2(v)=sum_i |i> tensor v tensor|i>,
u3(v)=v tensor sum_i |ii>. For each fixed coordinate v, their Gram matrix
has diagonal d and off-diagonal1, and different coordinate triples are
orthogonal. Thus U has dimension3d when d>1. The restrictions of K have
column matrix
       [[d-1, 1, 0],
        [1, d-1, 0],
        [-1,-1, 0]]
with eigenvalues d,d-2,0. This is a self-adjoint restriction in the stated
positive Gram metric. U is invariant under contractions and swaps. On
U-perpendicular the contractions vanish and K=-F_AB-F_AC<=2I<=d I.
Consequently the antisymmetric star is <=d^2 I/2 and
 2 n_a a <=d^2/2, i.e.
                  a <= d/[2(d-1)].                (O2)
No componentwise domination by a particular cloning channel is assumed.

3. ACTUAL CANONICAL PURE-ENSEMBLE COMPARATORS
For a pure-state ensemble with barycenter I/d, define the canonical map
 Gamma(X)=d integral rho Tr(rho X) dmu(rho).
It is CPTP, unital, EB, HS-positive and HS-self-adjoint.
Use two probability ensembles obtained from Haar measure on O(d):
 R: rho=|u><u| for a uniformly distributed real unit vector u.
 C: rho=|psi><psi|, psi=(u+i v)/sqrt2, with (u,v) a uniformly distributed
    real orthonormal two-frame.
Their barycenters are I/d. Both orbit ensembles are invariant under
complex conjugation as well as O(d). Their maps therefore act by one
scalar on imaginary antisymmetric Hermitians and one on real symmetric
traceless Hermitians. This assertion is elementary: coordinate sign flips
isolate each off-diagonal pair, conjugation separates its real/imaginary
parts, permutations equate the respective scalars, and a45degree plane
rotation sends a diagonal difference to a symmetric off-diagonal matrix.
Their identity eigenvalue is1.

For rho=|psi><psi| put t=|psi^T psi|^2. Its antisymmetric HS projection has
ordinary squared Frobenius norm (1-t)/2. Summing the canonical quadratic
form over the tau-orthonormal antisymmetric basis gives d(1-t)/2.
Dividing by n_a yields eigenvalue a_Gamma=(1-t)/(d-1).
For any pure ensemble the full HS superoperator trace of Gamma is d:
indeed sum_i tau(H_i Gamma(H_i))=integral sum_i Tr(rho H_i)^2=d,
where H_i is a tau-orthonormal complete Hermitian basis. Thus
 1+n_a a_Gamma+n_s b_Gamma=d,
which determines b_Gamma=[d-2+d t]/[(d-1)(d+2)].
For R, t=1: (a_R,b_R)=(0,2/(d+2)).
For C, t=0: (a_C,b_C)=(1/(d-1),(d-2)/[(d-1)(d+2)]).
These are actual canonical pure ensembles, not a relaxed covariance hull.

4. ABSOLUTE-SPECTRUM DOMINATION
Set mu=(2a-1)_+ and p=(d-1)mu. By (O2), 0<=p<=1.
Take Psi=p Gamma_C+(1-p)Gamma_R. Its two eigenvalues are
         mu, nu=(2-d mu)/(d+2),
with nu>= (d-2)/[(d-1)(d+2)]>=0.
Since a,b>=-1/2, the positive parts of 2|a|-1 and 2|b|-1 equal those of
2a-1 and 2b-1. Therefore mu>=2|a|-1.
If a<=1/2, mu=0 and CP gives
 (d+2)b<=2+d a<=2+d/2,
so 2b-1<=2/(d+2)=nu. If a>1/2, (O1) gives
 2b-1 <=[d+2-2d a]/(d+2)=[2-d mu]/(d+2)=nu.
In both cases nu>=2|b|-1, using nu>=0 for the negative-half interval.
On the identity all maps have eigenvalue1. This proves Psi>=2|Phi|-I
on every Hermitian direction, completing the local theorem.

The integral can be replaced by at most d^4 pure atoms: its normalized
Choi state is in the convex hull of compact trace-one matrices rho^T tensor
rho on dimension d^2. Caratheodory in real affine dimension at most d^4-1
preserves that Choi state exactly, including both marginals. This is a
finite existence bound, not an efficient design or acquisition algorithm.
The qutrit special case has separately reconstructed explicit21 and18 atom
recipes, retained with their respective derivations.

5. TENSOR, CLOSURE AND SHARPNESS
For local Phi_j let F_j=|Phi_j| and P_j=Psi_j; these are positive contractions
of their real test Hilbert spaces. The prior elementary tensor lemma says
 I-P_j<=2(I-F_j) for every j implies
 I-tensor_j P_j<=2(I-tensor_j F_j).
One proof: P_j>=2F_j-I implies F_j<=(I+P_j)/2, so
 tensor F_j<=tensor[(I+P_j)/2]<=(I+tensor P_j)/2.
The last step is the scalar inequality product(1+x_j)/2 <=(1+product x_j)/2
for x_j in[0,1], applied in the product eigenbasis. Different factors commute
and no commutation between F_j and P_j is required. Also
 tensor_j |Phi_j|>=tensor_j Phi_j, because both are diagonal in the product
of local self-adjoint eigenbases and product |lambda_j|>=product lambda_j.
Tensor products of canonical pure ensembles remain canonical and pure.
Thus the bound holds on the full global algebra, not just product observables.
Dense output costs may grow exponentially with the number of factors.

The separately proved mixture and bistochastic adjoint-sandwich closure
applies: I-F^dag Psi F=(I-F^dag F)+F^dag(I-Psi)F, with nonnegative first
term; rank-one refinement of the resulting canonical POVM preserves the
quadratic domination. The resulting channels need not retain O(d) covariance.
No assertion that this closure includes every legal channel is made.

For sharpness, the universal symmetric cloner
 B(X)=2/(d+1) P_sym(X tensor I)P_sym,
 P_sym=(I+F)/2,
is CPTP with identical unital marginal
 Phi(X)=lambda X+(1-lambda)tau(X)I,
 lambda=(d+2)/(2(d+1)). These assertions follow by expanding P_sym and
using partial traces of I,F; trace B(X)=Tr X. This member of the family
has V=d^2-1 and s=lambda V on a full traceless tau-orthonormal frame.
The pure-ensemble support k=d-1 for every barycenter-I/d pure ensemble,
because every pure state has squared full Bloch length d-1. Thus
 (V-k)/(V-s)=d(d-1)/[d(d-1)/2]=2.
No constant less than2 can work for all members in any fixed dimension.
