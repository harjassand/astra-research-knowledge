## 11. Reproducibility and provenance

The principal proof is self-contained after standard heat-semigroup calculus, Stokes' theorem, elementary polynomial algebra, and the implication quasiconvexity => rank-one convexity. Its decisive finite identities are fully explicit above.

Run:

```bash
python verify_sharp_scalar.py
python verify_explicit.py
```

The first script is the principal standalone endpoint verifier. It imports SymPy only; all constants and the universal one-form are written in the script. It independently verifies the root, the PSD Schur certificate, the universal compatible-jet identity, the acoustic null witness, the differentiated acoustic matrix, and the interior/non-polyconvexity calculations. Its output is `sharp_scalar_verification.json` and `sharp_scalar_certificate.json`.

The second independently checks a simpler rational, strictly positive six-by-six certificate at the retained \(\ell=-29/10\) interior point. It supplies a redundant proof route rather than a dependency of the sharp theorem.

`exploration/` preserves discovery scripts and selected numerical artifacts. They are not substituted for proof. Pickle files, when included there, are local trusted research snapshots, not necessary inputs to either standalone verifier and not intended for execution from an untrusted source. The package manifest records exact file hashes. No third-party manuscripts are redistributed.
