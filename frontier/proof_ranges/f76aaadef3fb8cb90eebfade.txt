## Uniform interval arithmetic and API hypotheses

Both trial potentials are fixed binary64 vectors with exact zero at t. For each possible graph in the coefficient box, the same topology and rooted spanning tree apply. Directed interval residual accumulation encloses that graph's exact residuals, and subtree interval sums enclose its admissible routed flows. Applying (3) with an outward square-root upper bound and outward endpoints therefore encloses T uniformly over the coefficient box.
