## 4. Scientific and computational scope

The standard relative entropy of coherence and its thermodynamic role have
substantial prior literature; see Baumgratz, Cramer and Plenio,
[Quantifying Coherence](https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.0275), and Santos et al.,
[The role of quantum coherence in non-equilibrium entropy production](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41534-019-0138-y).
The full relevant entropy split of the latter was inspected. This note
claims a separately derived, restricted certificate improvement, not a new
entropy measure or priority clearance.

Post-acquisition the extra computation is scalar. The true limitations remain:
acquire K and the kinetic expectation with sufficient confidence, justify the
loaded reference equilibrium histogram, preserve the intervention/sector
contract, and obtain a margin larger than all errors. Kinetically inactive
off-resonant couplings can give a small budget despite a pessimistic norm;
the theorem does not prove that their physical acquisition is economical.

and resulting histogram enclosures on independently generated complex block
Hamiltonians. It also checks the sharp resonant two-level identity. Floating
checks support the implementation; the argument above is the universal proof.
