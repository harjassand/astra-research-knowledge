## 2. A fixed-charge two-label rare-overlap counterfamily

Assume only fixed-reference equilibrium-label sampling is available. Unknown
label-preserving host terms are not supplied as a complete Hamiltonian that an
algorithm could solve instead. Let m>=2, theta=2^-m and B=m*log2/beta. The two
possible reference histograms have rare-label probabilities

    p0=theta,       p1=2theta.

They are realized exactly in the SAME total-charge-one sector of two fermion
modes, with basis |10>,|01>, label P_rare=|10><10| and unknown diagonal reference
Hamiltonian

    H_p=diag[-log(p)/beta, -log(1-p)/beta].

Its partition function is1 and its Gibbs histogram is(p,1-p). Every label-
changing term is zero: T=0, tau=0 and all source/entropy/activity budgets equal
C=1. This family has no non-Gaussian or coherence uncertainty to blame.

Apply the SAME declared intervention V=cI-B P_rare, where

    A0=2-theta,       A1=3-2theta,
    c=log(A0*A1)/(2beta).

The exact free-energy changes are

    DeltaF0=+gamma,       DeltaF1=-gamma,
    gamma=log(A1/A0)/(2beta)
          >=log(10/7)/(2beta)>0.                              (6)

Indeed `Z_(H_p+V)/Z_H=exp(-beta*c)[1+(2^m-1)p]`, giving A0 and A1. The ratio
(3-2theta)/(2-theta) decreases with theta and theta<=1/4. Thus an increasingly
strong local level shift produces opposite signs with a nonvanishing native
thermal-energy margin from increasingly rare reference weight. The input
level/shift energies scale as B; no fixed-norm contradiction is inferred.
