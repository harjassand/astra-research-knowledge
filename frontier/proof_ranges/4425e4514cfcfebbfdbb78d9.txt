## 6. A continuation that failed: projected maximum-entropy repair

I next tried to combine upward entropy improvements on coordinate projections.

For \(J\subseteq[n]\), let \(p_J\) be the projected law and \(\mathcal F_J\) its support. Define

\[
R(J)=
\max H(Y),
\]

where \(Y\) is supported on \(\mathcal F_J\) and can be coupled with \(X\sim p_J\) so that \(X\subseteq Y\).

The intended route required this repaired entropy profile to satisfy the usual submodularity inequality. It does not.

Take

\[
\mathcal F=
\{\varnothing,\{a\},\{b\},\{a,b\},\{a,b,c\}\},
\]

with the uniform distribution.

On all three coordinates, the original law already maximizes entropy on its five-point support, so

\[
R(abc)=\log5.
\]

On coordinate \(c\), the initial probability of presence is \(1/5\), which can be increased to \(1/2\). Thus

\[
R(c)=\log2.
\]

On \(ac\), the projected support is the chain

\[
\varnothing\subset\{a\}\subset\{a,c\},
\]

with probabilities

\[
\left(\frac25,\frac25,\frac15\right).
\]

The uniform law on those three points stochastically dominates this law: it can be obtained by moving mass upward. Therefore

\[
R(ac)=\log3,
\qquad
R(bc)=\log3.
\]

Consequently,

\[
R(ac)+R(bc)=\log9
<
\log10=R(abc)+R(c).
\]

This is an exact counterexample to the required submodularity. **The projected entropy-repair quantities cannot be assembled as an ordinary entropy rank function.** The failed step cannot be restored by changing constants.

## 7. The strongest surviving proof obligation

The representation theorem makes a global mass-transport formulation available, but I did not establish its universal feasibility.

For a fixed family \(\mathcal F\), seek nonnegative coordinate weights

\[
\lambda_i\ge0,\qquad \sum_i\lambda_i=1,
\]

nonnegative upward flows \(f_{AB}\) for \(A\subsetneq B\) in \(\mathcal F\), and nonnegative residuals \(s_A\), satisfying

\[
\boxed{
2\sum_{i\in A}\lambda_i-1
=
s_A
+
\sum_{B\subsetneq A}f_{BA}
-
\sum_{A\subsetneq C}f_{AC}
\qquad(A\in\mathcal F).
}
\tag{12}
\]

Such a certificate would prove the increasing-weighted half-frequency statement. Indeed, multiply by an increasing probability \(p(A)\) and sum:

\[
\begin{aligned}
2\sum_i\lambda_i\Pr_p(i\in A)-1
&=
\sum_A s_Ap(A)
+\sum_{A\subsetneq B}f_{AB}\bigl(p(B)-p(A)\bigr)\\
&\ge0.
\end{aligned}
\]

At least one coordinate must therefore have probability at least \(1/2\).

For a fixed family, finite-dimensional minimax and the dual cone of nonnegative increasing functions show that this certificate is also necessary for its universal increasing-weighted statement. This is consistent with the earlier Polymath linear-programming formulation; it is not an independently new target introduced here. [Gowers's Weblog](https://gowers.wordpress.com/2016/02/13/func3-further-strengthenings-and-variants/)

On a full Boolean cube, the certificate is explicit: put flow \(\lambda_i\) on each edge \(A\to A\cup\{i\}\) and take all residuals zero. The divergence is exactly

\[
\sum_{i\in A}\lambda_i-\sum_{i\notin A}\lambda_i
=
2\lambda(A)-1.
\]

For a general union-closed family, those coordinate-addition edges need not exist. Replacing them with available unions creates merges and unequal multiplicities. **A general construction that balances those merges is the surviving mathematical requirement.** Neither the uniformization theorem nor the entropy characterization supplies it.

## 8. Assessment against the mission

The established output is a **specific representation and sampling capability**: increasing weighted laws can be converted into uniform union-closed models while preserving local statistics, with explicit error bounds and without uncharged enumeration or rare-event conditioning. The accompanying arguments give an exact universal weighted–unweighted equivalence and a complete characterization of entropy stagnation under self-union couplings.

The prior-art check limits what can be claimed. Increasing-weighted formulations and coupling approaches predate this investigation. Zargar also developed non-uniform versions for particular product-weighted distributions; that work is distinct from the arbitrary increasing-weight conversion proved above, but it rules out treating “non-uniform union-closed distributions” as a new subject. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.19338v2) I have not established priority for the exact uniformization theorem or its combination of consequences, and these proofs have not been independently reviewed or formally verified.

**The decisive objective remains open in this investigation.** The construction removes a genuine representational restriction, but it does not yet provide the structural inequality needed to reach \(1/2\). The five-set counterexample eliminates the projected-submodularity continuation; the remaining route requires a universal structural construction such as (12), rather than another reformulation or another restricted entropy calculation.
