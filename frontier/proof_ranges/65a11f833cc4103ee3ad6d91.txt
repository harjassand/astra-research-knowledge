## 1. Physical construction

Let L denote h10BN and H denote h11BN. Read from the face nearest the target:

    A = C | L H H L H L L H | C
    B = C | H L L H L H H L | C

C is an identical h10BN end cap. The reference calculation uses eight 10 nm internal slabs and two 5 nm caps: 90 nm total. Each isotope occupies 40 nm internally. B is both the complement and the reversal of A.

These are proposed dimensions, not a fabricated specimen. Multi-slab isotope heterostructures and dry transfer are established [2]; a qualified ten-piece capped laminate and its matched interfaces are not established by that work.

A practical matched pair would use two regions cut from the same prefabricated parent laminate, mounting one face up and the other face down on randomly assigned nearby target regions. This reduces growth and thickness differences but does not guarantee identical buried interfaces. A live in-situ flipping device is NOT assumed. A homogeneous-isotope sham laminate must undergo the same orientation and transfer process.
