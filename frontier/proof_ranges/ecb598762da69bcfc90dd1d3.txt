## 4. Multiscale completion controls energy as well as representation

A short feasible flux is not sufficient if its norm is huge. The reference distribution in (1)–(4) is therefore an algorithmic choice.

Define normalized one-dimensional profiles

\[
\rho_j(s)=2^{-j}(1-2^{-j})^s,\quad
\mu_j(g)=\prod_{i=1}^m\rho_j(g_i),\quad \mu_0=\delta_0.
\]

For b_J=mu_J-mu_0, split

\[
b_J=\sum_{j=1}^J(\mu_j-\mu_{j-1}).
\]

Repair each difference using rho_(j-1) as reference, route it, and add. At L=2^j,

\[
\|\rho_j\|_2^2=1/(2L-1)\le1/L,
\quad \|\rho_{j-1}\|_2^2\le2/L.
\]

The primitive between these normalized profiles is

\[
p_j(s)=q_{j-1}^{s+1}-q_j^{s+1},\quad q_j=1-2^{-j},
\]

and ||p_j||_2^2<=L/2. The i-th Cartesian flux has squared norm at most 2^(m-i-1)L^(2-m). Summing over i gives at most 2^(m-1)L^(2-m). Routing (6) consequently gives

\[
\|w^{(j)}\|\le m2^{(m-1)/2}2^{j(1-m/2)}.
\]

By the triangle inequality,

\[
\boxed{\|w_J\|\le m2^{(m-1)/2}
\sum_{j=1}^J2^{j(1-m/2)}.}
\tag{8}
\]

For m>=3,

\[
\boxed{\|w_J\|^2\le
\frac{m^2 2^{m-1}}{(2^{m/2-1}-1)^2}<24m^2.}
\tag{9}
\]

For m=4, the bound is 128. For m=2 the displayed estimate is 8J^2; no dimension-independent energy bound is asserted there. In m=1 the required large-scale energy genuinely grows.

There are O(m^2J) separated routed terms, or comparable TT data with explicit level summation. The geometric scales are written using O(J)-bit dyadic poles. A conservative exact-arithmetic algorithm is polynomial in m,J and all coefficient sizes, not in (2^J)^m. This is a theorem for this explicit source class and its linear combinations, not an assertion that all actual response defects admit such a decomposition cheaply.

`multiscale_repair.py` constructs the m=4,J=40 case, representing scale 2^40 with 478 routed separated atoms. It verifies the exact source equation. The energy bound 128 is analytic; the full 40-level exact norm was not computed. Smaller cases have exact evaluated norms. In contrast, the direct point-reference repair of the same large-scale source has enormous exactly calculated energy (see separable_repair_checks.json). The multiscale step changes that energy bill without changing the physical graph.
