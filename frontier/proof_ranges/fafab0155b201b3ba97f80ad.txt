6. The nonlinear optimisation reduction
There is no need to form a pseudoinverse or insert an artificial regularization floor.
Introduce \(t\in\mathbb R^{|S|}\) and impose
\[
y=\widehat D_Sa+\widehat\Gamma_St.
\]
The online problem is
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\min_{a,t,z}\quad&
a^\top\widehat Q_0a+
t^\top\widehat\Gamma_St+
\psi(\widehat D_Sa+\widehat\Gamma_St,a,z),\\
\text{subject to}\quad&
(\widehat D_Sa+\widehat\Gamma_St,a,z)\in\mathcal P.
\end{aligned}
}
\tag{18}
\]
It is an explicit convex QP. Singular directions of \(t\) are harmless redundant variables and can also be eliminated exactly.
The full and reduced problems have the same feasible local tuples by (16). Since \(\psi\ge0\), the multiplicative bound (17) survives adding \(\psi\). It therefore survives taking minima. An optimiser of the reduced problem is near-optimal for the true partial-energy problem.
Polynomial-time solvability of rational convex quadratic programs is established prior mathematics, including degenerate cases; the construction does not require a strictly feasible interior or a fixed active set. ScienceDirect
A force-driven corollary
The result also handles a common signed physical objective without incorrectly claiming relative accuracy for arbitrary differences of energies.
For homogeneous constraints \(Ax=0\), let a passive convex local device have potential \(\psi(y)\), with \(\psi(0)=0\) and an origin-feasible convex domain. Under local force \(b\), define
\[
V=\sup_y
\left[
b^\top y-\psi(y)-\tfrac12\mathcal E_S(0,y)
\right].
\tag{19}
\]
This is the negative minimum total potential, measured relative to the undeformed state.
From (12), the conservative energy
\[
E_{\rm safe}(y)=(1+\theta)\widehat{\mathcal E}_S(0,y)
\]
satisfies
\[
\mathcal E_S(0,y)\le E_{\rm safe}(y)
\le\kappa\,\mathcal E_S(0,y),
\qquad
\kappa=\frac{1+\theta}{1-\theta}.
\]
Let \(V_{\rm safe}\) be (19) with \(E_{\rm safe}\). Then
\[
\boxed{V/\kappa\le V_{\rm safe}\le V.}
\tag{20}
\]
For the lower bound, scale a true optimiser toward the origin by \(1/\kappa\). Convexity gives
\(\psi(y/\kappa)\le\psi(y)/\kappa\), while the bulk energy scales quadratically.
Thus force-driven nonlinear contact can receive a conservative relative potential-value certificate. This is not a claim that the exact active contact set or every contact force is recovered correctly when the system is arbitrarily close to an active-set transition.
7. Full-state recovery is possible—but not free
A reduced optimiser gives local variables and an energy guarantee. A full physical field has \(N\) entries and must be charged separately.
