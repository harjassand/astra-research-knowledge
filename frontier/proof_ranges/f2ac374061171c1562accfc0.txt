## 9. Exact checks and one useful rational-endpoint variant

The executable `verify_strengthening.py` uses the small decidable gate relation k=v^2+1, t=k^2, rather than pretending to instantiate the universal machine. It passed 35 reconstructed factorial coefficients, positivity-envelope bounds, 3,564 exact rational-parameter witness-band checks, the harmonic-generator identity, band-width algebra, and 39 immigration-death passage recurrences. Output is in `verification_results.txt`. These checks support the algebraic interfaces and do not constitute proof of universality or an independent audit.

For an even simpler phase diagram, replace every factor 8 in the threshold/compiler by 16. All proofs are unchanged with r=1/4. Then

I_k=(4/(4k+5), 4/(4k+3)),
w_k=8/[16(k+1)^2-1],
epsilon_k=1/[16(k+1)^2].

The last radius guarantees |a-k|<1/15<r. Thus the exact noncomputable phase volume can be a sum of positive rational widths, with every band endpoint rational. The displayed test script checks the factor-eight version; the rational-endpoint variant follows by replacing its scalar threshold and is also covered by the same proof.

A related 2025 title, Alhazov et al., *The busy beaver game for reaction systems*, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11047-025-10037-6 , concerns qualitative set-rewriting reaction systems with finite state spaces. The primary paper explicitly identifies that finite-state distinction. It does not supply or refute the unbounded stochastic lifetime theorem proved here. More generally, neither use of a busy-beaver argument nor noncomputability arising from a halting encoding is itself a novel foundational technique.
