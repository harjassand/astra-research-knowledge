## 1. Target and input

    F=Q+max_(||z||_1<=1) [2h Re(c* z)-alpha z* B z].

All complex subset amplitudes are allowed. Rates/controls and the ACTUAL full-joint Schur channel remain supplied assumptions. A reduced coherence measurement does not qualify them. Restrict first to nonnegative singleton-supported z, and consider the case that its optimum has mass one and positive Lagrange multiplier. Sort eta decreasing. For an active prefix A define d_j=1-eta_j² and

    A2=sum_A eta_j²/d_j, A1=sum_A eta_j/d_j, A0=sum_A 1/d_j,
    D=(1+A2)A0-A1²>0,
    tau=(t A0-A1)/D, mu=(t A1-(1+A2))/D.

Choose a prefix satisfying tau>0, mu>0, and eta_j>=k:=mu/tau on A while every excluded eta_j<=k. Let

    v_{ {j} }=(tau eta_j-mu)/d_j on A, all other coordinates zero.

Then v>=0, sum v=1 and tau=t-s with s=sum eta_j v_j. These formulas follow by solving the singleton KKT equations, whose Hessian is diag(d)+eta eta^T. Sorted prefix sums find a valid A with O(m log m) sorting and O(m) arithmetic; ties at the threshold simply have zero weight. ## 2. The full exponentially many gradient coordinates collapse

For any nonempty subset S,

    (Bv)_S=c_S [s+sum_(j in S∩A) v_j(1/eta_j-eta_j)].

Since active eta_j>0, v_j(1/eta_j-eta_j)=tau-mu/eta_j. Thus the full objective residual r=h c-alpha Bv is

    r_S=alpha tau (prod_(j in S)eta_j)
             [1-|S∩A|+k sum_(j in S∩A)1/eta_j].       (1)

For S contained in A define

    g(S)=(prod_S eta_j)[1-|S|+k sum_S 1/eta_j].

If |S|=ell, all selected eta_j lie in [k,1). Differentiating its multiaffine polynomial with respect to one selected eta_j gives

    prod_(S\{j}) eta * [1-ell+k sum_(S\{j})1/eta] <=0.

Therefore g(S) decreases in every selected eta. A valid nonempty active prefix has 0<k<1 because k<=eta_j<1. Its maximum at fixed ell is at most k^ell<=k. Its minimum over ell-element subsets of A is attained by the ell largest eta values. Write these prefix values as

    G_ell=(1-ell) prod_(j<=ell)eta_j
                    +k sum_(j<=ell) prod_(h<=ell,h!=j)eta_h.

They can be computed in one prefix scan. If S also has inactive labels, its residual is g(S∩A) times their eta product. When S∩A is nonempty this cannot increase its absolute value. When it is empty, that product is <=k because at least one inactive eta<=k. An active singleton attains g=k. Consequently the EXACT infinity norm of the entire 2^m-1-coordinate residual is

    ||r||_infinity=alpha tau M,
    M=max(k,max_(1<=ell<=|A|)(-G_ell)).                 (2)

This is not a numerical sampling assertion. It is a proof covering every complex perturbation direction through the complex-l1 dual norm.

## 3. Exact value and a certified bracket

The general concave tangent bound from the supplied Schur proof gives

    L=Q+2h eta·v-alpha v^T Bv <= F
      <= U=Q+alpha v^T Bv+2alpha tau M.              (3)

Here v^T Bv=sum_j d_j v_j²+(eta·v)², computed in O(m) arithmetic. The active-gradient equations and sum v=1 give L=Q+alpha v^T Bv+2alpha mu. Hence

    U-L=2alpha tau (M-k).                            (4)

In particular **v is a globally optimal full Schur witness if and only if every G_ell>=-k**.
