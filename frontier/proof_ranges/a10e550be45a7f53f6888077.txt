## New acquired interface: a late-time approximate comparator

For a model-validated gas in the same family, calibrated independent initial
velocities can acquire `mu` through `|X|^2/3`; no latent mixing inversion is
needed. The proof gives an explicit median-of-means measurement budget
`O((b/a)^2 epsilon^-2 log(1/zeta))`. Above the stated logarithmic time
threshold, a `G_mu_hat` output is conditionally within `epsilon` TV of
`f_t`, with calibration confidence `1-zeta`.

This is a different, weaker approximate output. It is a useful matched
baseline when assessing scientific value of an exact transient sampler.
It is not a new physical actuator, nor validation of the mixture or Maxwell
collision assumptions. Outputs sharing a calibration estimate are only
conditionally iid; `M` joint outputs have the separately charged bound
`min(1,M epsilon)`. Sensor bias/noise, independence, preparation, units,
finite-bit Gaussian generation and model mismatch remain open.
