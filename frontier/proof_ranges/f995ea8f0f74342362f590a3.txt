The campaign investigated energy conversion, thermal storage, caloric transport, molecular product memory, mechanical sensing, photonic interfaces, viscoelastic feedback, system identification and measurement acquisition. Several proposed mechanisms survived as conditional models; their inaccessible primitive or stronger incumbent often remained decisive.

Authentic historical measurements were acquired and reanalysed. On viscoelastic trajectories, ideal gross work was 0.127922 rather than 0.118429 k_B T for the strongest tested delayed pair, about 8.0% higher. A standard record-reset bill alone was 1.26948 k_B T, so this does not establish net advantage. Another noise/drift proposal gained 0.000199% with a 99% interval crossing zero. Millions of rows, exact synthetic checks and apparatus literature are evidence of those analyses, not new experiments or energy extraction.

There were also real local software executions. A deliberately constructed private-table source implemented an address pin before its hidden lookup. Its marked-erasure model has matching query orders Theta(R) with the pin and Theta(R+sqrt(MR)) with optimal passive access, charging setup. At M=2^22,R=2^16, measured complete method lifecycles were 0.089 s versus 0.143 s; whole invocations including imports/export were 0.49 s versus 0.53 s. The warm/query improvement shrinks substantially after cold costs. This demonstrates the supplied intervention in that custom computational source, not acquisition of an external scientific controller or a novelty-cleared general learning advance.

Common surviving obstacles were useful information assumed before acquisition; control/measurement calibration not actually obtained; stronger same-information methods matching or beating a candidate; previously known mechanisms; preparation/reset/precision costs erasing apparent gains; and finite examples leaving the important quantifier unresolved. A failed construction does not close the scientific opportunity. The negative results identify what a replacement must acquire or change.
