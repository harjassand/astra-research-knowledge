1. Complete ansatz and its exact nullspace

Let u: R^2 -> R^2, A=Du with row-major coordinates (a,b,c,d), and write the
six compatible second derivatives as

 z=(u1_xx,u1_xy,u1_yy,u2_xx,u2_xy,u2_yy),
 B1=(z0,z1,z3,z4), B2=(z1,z2,z4,z5).

The vector space of scalar polynomials of bidegree (2,2) in (A,z) has
dimension dim Sym^2(R^4) dim Sym^2(R^6)=10*21=210. There are no coefficient
symmetry assumptions in this count. In particular, it includes every local
homogeneous A^2 H^2 polynomial correction on compatible planar second jets.

For component i, the Euler operator is

 Ei(L)=-Dx L_(ui_x)-Dy L_(ui_y)+Dx^2 L_(ui_xx)
                  +DxDy L_(ui_xy)+Dy^2 L_(ui_yy).

There is no factor two on the mixed term, because ui_xy is one independent
coordinate of the symmetric Hessian. Product-rule total derivatives use all
compatible third and fourth derivatives. They produce an integer matrix E
with 696 rows and 210 columns.

This formal Euler kernel is the relevant integral nullspace. If Ei(L)=0,
differentiate the integral along A0+D(t phi), D^2(t phi); integration by parts
shows it is constant, and the t=0 value vanishes. Conversely, integral
invariance under all compactly supported perturbations implies the Euler
identity, and arbitrary finite compatible jets are realized by local smooth
polynomials multiplied by a cutoff. The same local argument works inside
a periodic cell. Thus the finite coefficient calculation has the required
variational meaning; it is not a sampled-field test.

There are 80 homogeneous cubic polynomial one-form coefficients on R^4.
Their derivatives are closed two-forms with homogeneous quadratic
coefficients. The kernel consists exactly of gradients of homogeneous
quartic scalar polynomials, of dimension C(7,3)=35. Polynomial radial
homotopy proves both assertions: for a closed homogeneous quadratic two-form
Omega, alpha=(1/4)i_Euler Omega satisfies d alpha=Omega; a closed cubic
one-form is similarly the differential of a quartic scalar. Therefore the
closed quadratic two-form space has dimension 80-35=45.

The pullback Omega_A(B1,B2) is injective, even without closedness. Its six
Hessian minors are

 z0 z2-z1^2,       z0 z4-z1 z3,       z0 z5-z1 z4,
 z1 z4-z2 z3,      z1 z5-z2 z4,       z3 z5-z4^2.

Unique monomials z0z2,z0z4,z0z5,z2z3,z1z5,z3z5 show linear independence.
Consequently Omega is unique for a given pullback density. Its primitive
alpha is not unique: it is determined modulo a quartic scalar differential.

The independent checker implements monomials as sorted lists of jet labels,
rather than the author's exponent-array implementation. It constructs all
80 pullbacks d(p dA_j), the integer matrix N of size 210 by 80, and verifies
E N=0 over the integers. Exact elimination modulo the prime 1009 gives

 rank(E mod 1009)=165, rank(N mod 1009)=45.

A nonzero modular minor is a nonzero rational minor. Thus rank_Q E>=165,
rank_Q N>=45, and exact EN=0 forces their ranks to add to at most 210. They
therefore equal 165 and 45, respectively, and ker E=im N over Q and R.
This certifies the complete 45-dimensional nullspace and equality with the
closed quadratic two-form pullbacks. The independent checker also checks
rank 60 for the pullback of all 60 quadratic two-form coefficients, and a
45-dimensional exact kernel for the rank-15 closure constraints.

As an auxiliary cross-check, imposing left SO(2) invariance on closed forms
gives dimension 15. The author's complete density count 72 also agrees with
the rotation weights: Sym^2(R^4) has weights 0,+2,-2 of multiplicities 4,3,3;
Sym^2(R^6) has corresponding multiplicities 9,6,6, giving 4*9+3*6+3*6=72.
