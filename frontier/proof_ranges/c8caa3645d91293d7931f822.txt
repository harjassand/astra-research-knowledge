### Theorem B — Removal of reversibility

For an arbitrary stationary, self-compatible \\(T\\), without KMS self-adjointness or normality, there exists a single \\(\sigma\\)-preserving KMS-self-adjoint entanglement-breaking channel \\(\Psi\\) such that

\\[ \boxed{ I-\Psi\preceq 8\left(I-\frac{T+T^\sharp}{2}\right). } \tag{B} \\]

Equivalently, every Hermitian \\(A\\) satisfies

\\[ \boxed{ \langle A,(I-\Psi)A\rangle\_\sigma \le8\bigl(\\|A\\|\_\sigma^2-\langle A,T(A)\rangle\_\sigma\bigr). } \\]

In particular, taking \\(\sigma=I/d\\) gives a dimension-independent common classical comparator for every bistochastic self-compatible quantum channel, without imposing Hilbert–Schmidt self-adjointness.

These statements use the channel and its compatible extension as supplied mathematical objects; they do not assert efficient construction or acquisition.
