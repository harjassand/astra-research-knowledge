## Why every affine background is covered

Put X=Dφ. The half-turn identity X(x,y+π)=−X(x,y) kills the odd terms in the
quartic expansion, giving

\[
 \mathbb E[G(B+X)-G(B)]
 =\mathbb E G(X)+\tfrac12\mathbb E D^2G(X)[B,B]
 =\mathbb E G(X)+\tfrac12\mathbb E D^2G(B)[X,X]. \tag{3}
\]

The second equality is polarization of a homogeneous quartic. At each fixed
B, D²G(B) is a constant quadratic form nonnegative on rank-one directions.
Indeed the existing explicit G acoustic Gram is reconstructed in the checker
from the differentiated polynomial, its positive leading six-by-six block,
rank six and zero Schur complement; left rotational invariance supplies every
output direction. Fourier/Parseval for a periodic gradient now makes the last
term in (3) nonnegative: each real sine/cosine gradient coefficient is an
output vector tensored with the real spatial wave vector. This argument is
valid for the actual W^{1,4} gradient, hence also W^{1,2}; no inverse averaged
Hessian, finite Fourier cutoff in x, or bound on B is required. B=0 gives
equality in the affine infimum assertion.
