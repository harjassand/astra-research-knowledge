# Exact small interfaces for the two reciprocal cuts

This note describes exact representations and known moment projections. It supplies no same-instance runtime advantage or foundational novelty claim.

## Direct projection: two second-order cones per pair

Let \(W=L(VV^*)\), let \(A\) be the four-row complex linear map with \(a=AV=(x,y,u,v)\), and let \(W_a=AWA^*\). All quantities below are affine in \(W\), provided the required entries of \(W\) are represented.

Define the two quadratic-form matrices by

\[
L(|\alpha u+\beta v|^2)=(\alpha,\beta)K(\alpha,\beta)^*,
\quad
L(|\alpha\bar y+\beta\bar x|^2)=(\alpha,\beta)C(\alpha,\beta)^*.
\]

Thus \(K\) is the \((u,v)\) principal submatrix of \(W_a\), while \(C\) is the conjugated \((y,x)\) submatrix. Both complete cut families are exactly

\[
H_-=C-a_ia_jK\succeq0,\qquad H_+=b_ib_jK-C\succeq0.
\]

For \(H=\begin{psmallmatrix}p&r+is\\r-is&q\end{psmallmatrix}\), positive semidefiniteness is equivalent to

\[
\boxed{\left\|(p-q,\,2r,\,2s)\right\|_2\le p+q.}
\]

Each matrix therefore uses one four-dimensional Lorentz cone. There are no new fourth moments. Finite scalar cuts for selected \((\alpha,\beta)\) are outer approximations of these cones; imposing both cones for a selected bus pair enforces every direction for that pair.

This is the smallest direct interface in the sense that it introduces no new moment degree. It is the projected mechanism already under discussion, not an independent scientific advance. The following aggregate moment model is a useful explicit same-mechanism comparator.

## Four-feature aggregate order-two interface

Instead of a degree-four moment relaxation in all \(2m\) real voltage coordinates of a pair's support, use the four complex aggregate coordinates \(a=(x,y,u,v)\). Couple only their Hermitian second moment to the original model:

\[
W_a=AWA^*.
\]

Do not interpret aggregate current coordinates as independent physical inputs. Their second moments are fixed linear images of the original voltage moments. If \(A\) has complex row rank below four, work with an independent aggregate basis, or include the induced linear ideal through the relaxation's full degree. Omitting these dependencies gives a valid weaker outer approximation but changes its claimed exact equivalence.

The pair constraints and cuts are invariant under the common phase action \(a\mapsto e^{i\theta}a\). Any physical aggregate voltage vector supplies a valid phase-averaged extension with the same \(W_a\). It is therefore sufficient, for a valid pair strengthening coupled through \(W_a\), to set all moments with unequal numbers of unbarred/barred factors to zero. This does not require changing the original model's angle reference: the aggregate extension is linked through phase-invariant \(W_a\), not through its first moments.

Index the ten unordered pairs \(P=\{(i,j):1\le i\le j\le4\}\). Introduce the Hermitian 10-by-10 fourth-moment matrix

\[
T_{(i,j),(k,l)}=L(a_i a_j\bar a_k\bar a_l).
\]

It stores 100 real scalar variables before equality elimination. Repeated indices use the same variable. The phase-averaged moment sequence has 1 normalized constant, 16 real second-moment entries, and 100 real fourth-moment entries: 117 in total, with only the 100 fourth moments new when \(W_a\) is linked to the baseline. Fixed-power equations reduce the independent degrees of freedom further.

The complete aggregate real order-two moment PSD condition decomposes into the following blocks:

- \(W_a\succeq0\), a 4-by-4 Hermitian block;
- \(T\succeq0\), a 10-by-10 Hermitian block;
- \(H_0\succeq0\), the 17-by-17 Gram block on \(f=(1,\{a_p\bar a_q\}_{p,q=1}^4)\), with
  \[
  (H_0)_{0,0}=1,\quad(H_0)_{0,(p,q)}=(W_a)_{p,q},
  \quad(H_0)_{(p,q),(r,s)}=T_{\operatorname{sort}(q,r),\operatorname{sort}(p,s)}.
  \]

The conjugate phase blocks duplicate PSD conditions. The charge-zero block can be transformed into a 17-by-17 **real symmetric** block using \(1,|a_p|^2,\operatorname{Re}(a_p\bar a_q),\operatorname{Im}(a_p\bar a_q)\). A real-only solver represents the remaining Hermitian blocks by real matrices of dimensions 20 and 8. The combined real dimensions are 17+20+8=45, matching the full aggregate degree-two basis, but there are no cross-charge blocks or unbalanced moment variables.

For any real quadratic phase-invariant inequality

\[
g=c+\sum_{r,s}G_{r,s}a_r\bar a_s\ge0,
\]

the full affine aggregate localizer decomposes into the scalar

\[
L(g)=c+\sum_{r,s}G_{r,s}(W_a)_{r,s}\ge0
\]

and the Hermitian 4-by-4 block

\[
(M_g)_{p,q}=c(W_a)_{p,q}
+\sum_{r,s}G_{r,s}T_{\operatorname{sort}(r,p),\operatorname{sort}(s,q)}\succeq0.
\]

Include six such scalar/block pairs: the lower and upper voltage bounds for \(x,y\), and the two valid current upper bounds \(1-a_i|u|^2\ge0\), \(1-a_j|v|^2\ge0\).

For \(h_i=\bar a_1a_3-1\) impose

\[
(W_a)_{3,1}=1,\qquad
T_{\operatorname{sort}(3,p),\operatorname{sort}(1,q)}=(W_a)_{p,q}
\quad(1\le p,q\le4).
\]

Use the analogous equations for \(h_j=\bar a_2a_4-1\). These are two complex scalars and two 4-by-4 complex equality matrices before removing duplicate/dependent equations. Every remaining allowed degree-two multiplier carries nonzero phase charge and has expectation zero. Thus these constraints implement all fixed-power equality localizers for the phase-averaged aggregate order-two relaxation.

After these exact equalities are imposed, the charge-zero Gram columns \(a_3\bar a_1,a_1\bar a_3,a_4\bar a_2,a_2\bar a_4\) all equal the constant column. Delete those four duplicate columns/rows to reduce \(H_0\) from 17 to **13** real dimensions; the full matrix is its congruence extension. Keeping the equality constraints is essential when making this reduction. The PSD block dimensions are then 13 real, 10 complex, and 4 complex.

The exact upper and augmented lower identities in `REPORT.md` are contained in this interface. They therefore prove both cuts. The interface may constrain more than the two projected cones; no claim is made that every second-moment point satisfying the cones admits this aggregate moment extension.

The decomposition follows the elementary Fourier/charge decomposition of the full real moment Gram matrix. It is an exact reduction of a known finite moment model under stated coupling and symmetry, not a new hierarchy or a novel consistency principle.

## Honest size and cost comparison

For a support of \(m\) original complex voltages, a full real order-two moment matrix has dimension \(\binom{2m+2}{2}\), and order three has dimension \(\binom{2m+3}{3}\). The aggregate order-two dimension is fixed at 45 before phase decomposition.

| Original complex support \(m\) | Full real order-two matrix | Full real order-three matrix | Aggregate new fourth moments |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4 | 45 | 165 | 100 real variables per pair |
| 12 | 325 | 2925 | 100 real variables per pair |
| 24 | 1225 | 20825 | 100 real variables per pair |

The report's executed support range of 4–24 is source-reported; the supporting cut archive was not recovered. Dense matrix dimensions are representation counts, not measured solver times. Pair overlaps can permit sharing fourth moments, while independent pair models can repeat them. Six complex 4-by-4 localizers, fixed-power equality matrices, and original-model coupling constraints must also be counted. The aggregate approach can be weaker than a full support moment model because it omits other network constraints and mixed moments outside the aggregate span.

For the direct two-cone projection, assembling \(B_iWB_j^*\) naively costs \(O(s_i s_j)\) arithmetic, where \(s_i,s_j\) are row supports. Dense support storage can require \(O(m^2)\) original second-moment entries. A chordal or branch-based baseline may not contain these entries; adding them can require fill, consistency constraints, and larger cliques. Treating them as cost-free would invalidate the comparison.

With a rank-\(r\) factor \(W=RR^*\), the same entry can be evaluated using \((B_iR)(B_jR)^*\), costing \(O((s_i+s_j)r)\) before shared precomputation. All-pairs separation still scans \(O(n_f^2)\) fixed-injection pairs unless a selection scheme is used. A factorized primal objective does not itself certify a global lower bound; dual reconstruction and verified positivity must be timed separately.

The strongest relevant comparisons are a pinned strengthened QC/RLT/OBBT formulation, a verified low-rank implementation of the same conic relaxation, and a selective sparse order-two moment implementation with and without these pair blocks. Established [model intersections and lifted cuts](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.04644), [selective moment constraints](https://molzahn.github.io/pubs/molzahn_hiskens-sparse_moment_opf.pdf), and [minimal second-order sparsity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.19232) prevent using plain SDP alone as the strongest control.
