## Proposed certificate: targeted 3D closure + slip + retained output

The certificate is a four-state decision (`PASS`, `FAIL`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, `NOT RUN`) with immutable pre/post acquisition records. It deliberately does not require tracing unrelated network branches. It does require following the target component and any branch that reaches the guard-collar boundary; an unresolved crossing makes the result inconclusive.

1. **Predeclare the observable and blind set.** From the material's allowed finite Burgers catalog \(\mathcal B\) and stacking-fault translation catalog \(\mathcal R/\Lambda\), enumerate all crystallographically legal target and substitute products. For each physically available diffraction vector \(g\), record the cost of a registered image/scan and the classes it can see. In the ideal kinematic contrast rules, a dislocation is extinct only when both \(g\cdot b=0\) and \(g\cdot(b\times u)=0\); a stacking fault with translation \(R\) is extinct when \(g\cdot R\in\mathbb Z\). Pick a minimum-cost reflection cover that gives each nonzero \(b\in\mathcal B\) and each non-lattice \(R\in\mathcal R\) at least one non-extinct condition. Add opposite \(\pm g\) conditions where sign/dipole polarity is needed. Then verify these conditions under the actual foil thickness, excitation error, detector and material structure factors. If there is no such set of allowed reflections, add an independent measurement mode or report that class as blind. For finite catalogs an ideal finite cover exists if every reciprocal-lattice vector is available: nonzero \(b\) cannot be orthogonal to a spanning reciprocal basis, and \(R\notin\Lambda\) means some reciprocal vector has \(g\cdot R\notin\mathbb Z\). Real instruments provide only a subset of reflections with usable structure factor and signal, so the material-specific cover can still fail.

   For the ZnS/f.c.c. pilot, an exact cover fixture for the supplied partials uses \(G=\{(0,2,-2),(2,0,-2),(3,1,1)\}\). The first two conditions occur in the cited ZnS electric-glide microscopy; the third is an added out-of-plane allowed f.c.c. reflection whose measured structure factor and usable contrast must be checked in the actual foil. The matrix has determinant \(-20\). For \(b_1=\frac16[1,-2,1]\), \(b_2=\frac16[2,-1,-1]\), and \(b_1+b_2=\frac12[1,-1,0]\), the signed \(g\cdot b\) triplets are \((-1,0,1/3)\), \((0,1,2/3)\), and \((-1,1,1)\). Thus each line class is visible in at least one condition and the triplets distinguish the three. The fault phases \(g\cdot R\bmod1\) are \((0,0,1/3)\) and \((0,0,2/3)\) for the two partial shifts; they are detectable but identify only the shift modulo a perfect-lattice translation. Since \(b_1+b_2\in\Lambda\), \(R=b_2\) aliases \(R=-b_1\), and \(R=b_1\) aliases \(R=-b_2\). Planar fault contrast alone therefore cannot assign the signed bounding partials; the line contrast/Burgers circuit is still required. This exact table chooses a finite test set; it does not certify that these reflections have adequate experimental signal or that all ZnS substitute classes were covered.

2. **Register the target in 3D before intervention.** Place fiducials around \(U\), measure orientation, local thickness, strain and temperature, then map the target line and its fault boundaries. For near-surface bulk screening, ECCI can image individual dislocations over wider areas; it remains diffraction-condition and near-surface limited. For an electron-transparent target, use 4D-STEM/CBED or multi-\(g\) weak-beam/diffraction-contrast TEM, with a small targeted tilt set where depth/connectivity is ambiguous. Atom-resolved displacement phase or a Burgers circuit around the target core is a local cross-check. Map only the target component and the routes from it to the guard collar; do not infer connectivity from a crossing in a projection.

3. **Track boundary flux and reaction topology.** At each detected junction, assign line sense and verify \(\sum_i\epsilon_i b_i=0\) against the predeclared reaction catalog. For each fault patch, measure its translation class and verify that its boundary partials match the mapped lines. At the closed boundary of the target-plus-collar volume, record every crossing with position, signed \(b\), and line direction. Follow an outgoing line to a measured free-surface sink or to a mapped product; otherwise fail closed as `INCONCLUSIVE`. If the active device boundary itself is not fully covered by a bulk-appropriate assay, no claim about boundary export from the whole active volume is made.

4. **Measure the path-dependent strain term.** Track specimen fiducials or an in-situ marker field during the operation and integrate signed swept areas by slip system:

   \[
   \Delta\beta^p_{\rm path}=V^{-1}\sum_s b_s\otimes n_s\,\Delta A_s.
   \]

   Compare this with the independently registered displacement field and surface step. Report the uncertainty/tolerance on the tensor (or engineering shear) separately from the post-treatment elastic-strain map. For the simple opposite pair above the predicted shear scale is \(|b|Ld/V\), even though signed net \(b=0\). A final strain snapshot alone cannot close this path integral.

5. **Run the retained operating readout.** Restore controls and return the specimen to the declared duty temperature, stress, illumination/electrical bias, and ambient. Re-measure the declared functional metric after a fixed dwell and over a fixed cycle count, against same-specimen baseline and matched dark, constant-drive, dose/heating, and readout-beam controls. Pre-register a minimum improvement \(\Delta P_{\min}\), allowed drift/damage, and a non-inferiority margin for the other required outputs. Use the actual product output: for a semiconductor device this could be four-terminal I–V/Hall transport or time-resolved photoluminescence lifetime, but only if that is the property the dislocation is alleged to harm. Record wall-plug optical/electrical energy, heating, mechanical work, dose, readout time, preparation and control costs. “Retained” means the lower confidence bound on the improvement persists through that stated horizon after the repair controls have been removed; it does not imply indefinite lifetime.

6. **Report bounds, not zero by fiat.** Calibrate a worst-case detection probability over the allowed \(b,R,u\), target locations, foil thicknesses and overlap conditions using reference specimens whose line/fault content is independently known, and a blinded second reader. Image insertions can measure analysis-pipeline sensitivity only; they do not calibrate physical detector sensitivity. Report upper confidence bounds on undetected harmful line length and fault area at resolution \(r\), with assay sensitivity and field coverage. A zero image count without such a calibration is only “none detected.” A literature workflow that extracts 80% of line content on its example cannot be silently treated as a 100%-complete detector.

The retained certificate is the bounded vector

\[
\mathbf z_{\rm post}=\big(\{L_b(U)\}_{b\in\mathcal B},\{A_R(U)\}_{R\in\mathcal R},N_{\rm substitute\ junctions},\mathcal X_{\partial U},\Delta\beta^p_{\rm path},P_{\rm service}(0{:}T)\big),
\]

where line length is unsigned by Burgers class, fault area is resolved by translation class, \(\mathcal X_{\partial U}\) lists individual boundary crossings rather than just their signed sum, and the last coordinate is the measured performance trajectory after controls are removed. The thresholds and confidence bounds on each coordinate are specified before treatment.

**Pass rule.** A `PASS` requires all of: (a) a registered pre-treatment harmful target; (b) post-treatment target-class line and fault upper bounds below the predeclared tolerance in \(U\) plus its guard collar; (c) no unresolved boundary crossings and no above-tolerance substitute junction/fault/surface step in the assayed region; (d) accumulated plastic distortion below the threshold; and (e) retained operating improvement over the matched controls for the stated horizon. A clean endpoint circuit, a disappearing line in one reflection, a reduced fault area, a fast glide trace, or a first-hit repair event alone is a `FAIL` for certification. An uncalibrated/badly oriented measurement, missing boundary path, below-cutoff defect class, or unresolved overlap is `INCONCLUSIVE`, not `PASS`.
