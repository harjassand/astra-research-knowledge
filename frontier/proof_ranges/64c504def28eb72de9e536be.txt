## 4. A certified open set in all nine coefficients

For each basis f_i, construct a fixed symmetric 24-by-24 matrix R_i with ZᵀR_iZ=ΣD²f_i(A)[Bi,Bi]. A deterministic construction sends a coefficient c x_k x_l z_s z_t to Gram positions (k,s),(l,t), dividing c by two for distinct positions. All nine identities are checked exactly.

The exact row-sum bound is

    K=max_r Σ_s Σ_i |(R_i)rs| = 82.

For any real perturbation δ with max_i|δ_i|≤η=1/82000, the symmetric perturbation matrix has operator norm at most ηK=1/1000. Therefore the same ω and γ give R+Σδ_iR_i≥(1/1000)I. The preceding proof makes F_p*+δ quasiconvex, with uniform Jensen constant at least 1/72000.

The non-polyconvex witness has basis moment vector

    m=(49,49,49,0,0,0,1176/25,1176/25,0),
    Σ|m_i|=6027/25.

Its perturbed energy remains at most

    −833/250 + (1/82000)(6027/25) = −166453/50000 < 0.

Hence every coefficient vector in this closed cube is non-polyconvex and quasiconvex. In particular its interior is an explicit open subset of the full nine-dimensional left-SO(2) quartic space. No classification of the entire cone is implied.
