## Exact profile obstruction to direct generation

Consider the smooth periodic potential

\[
\begin{aligned}
\psi_1&=\cos x-\sin x+\cos y-\sin y,\\
\psi_2&=\cos x+\sin x+\cos y-\sin y+\cos(x+y)+\sin(x+y).
\end{aligned}
\]

Its three frequency classes \(e_1,e_2,e_1+e_2\) form a primitive connected
triad. Its exact zero-background nine-moment vector is

\[
\left(\frac{27}{2},\frac{31}{2},\frac{19}{2},
\frac{11}{2},\frac{11}{2},\frac{11}{2},0,-2,-1\right).
\]

The gradient covariance \(C=\mathbb E[D\psi(D\psi)^T]\) in row-major
coordinates is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0&0\\0&1&0&1\\0&0&2&1\\0&1&1&2
\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det C=1,
\]
and \(\mathbb E|D\psi|^4=48\). Thus this is a genuinely multidirectional,
orientation-sensitive moment profile, not a rank-one or reflection-even
example.

For any proposed affine carrier \(k=(p,q)\), a representation
\(\psi=rV(px+qy)\) would force

\[
\det(\psi,\partial_{(q,-p)}\psi)=0
\]
pointwise: the carrier is constant along \((q,-p)\), leaving only the scalar
amplitude to vary. Let \(D_x=\det(\psi,\psi_x)\) and
\(D_y=\det(\psi,\psi_y)\). Direct exact Fourier calculation gives

\[
\widehat D_x(1,2)=i/2,\quad \widehat D_y(1,2)=0;
\qquad
\widehat D_x(0,1)=1,\quad \widehat D_y(0,1)=1/2.
\]

The first coefficient of \(qD_x-pD_y\) forces \(q=0\); the second then
forces \(p=0\). So no nonzero affine carrier works, for any loop shape,
including after an invertible linear change of input coordinates. At \(k=0\)
the image would lie on one fixed line, but \(\psi(0,0)=(2,3)\) and
\(\psi(\pi,0)=(0,-1)\) are not collinear.

The companion class \(z(x)e^{iny}\) also cannot contain this profile: the
complex field \(\psi_1+i\psi_2\) has nonzero vertical Fourier frequencies
\(-1,0,1\), while that class has a single vertical frequency \(n\). Under
the exact packet rule, the primitive triad must be kept in one packet, so it
cannot be split into several positive blocks. This refutes a direct
support-partition generation of all compatible profiles by the current
modulation and one-coordinate-envelope families. The exact moments above are
reported to make the obstruction auditable; I have not proved that this
moment vector lies outside the conic hull of family moment vectors, nor that
it lacks a PSD completion. It is not a Morrey counterexample.
