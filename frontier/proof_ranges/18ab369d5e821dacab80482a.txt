## Reproduction and significance

`exact_query_counterexamples.py --output-dir <new-owned-directory>` uses only
the standard library and a direct two-by-two inversion. The exact inequalities
give complete finite counterexamples. They do not establish universal
algorithmic hardness, a physical oracle, or a practical advantage. Combined
with the finite-precision positive extension in the hidden-gate audit, these
results delimit which acquisition contracts can legitimately reuse the
one-edge rule.
