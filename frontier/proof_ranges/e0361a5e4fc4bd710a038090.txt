7. The mechanism extends to multiway interactions
The same proof gives an exact theorem for every finite simple \(r\)-uniform hypergraph, \(r\geq2\), and nonnegative symmetric kernels \(W:[0,1]^r\to[0,\infty)\) with each single-coordinate marginal equal to one.
The concentration constraints become
\[
d(e)\geq pk_e,
\qquad
d(e)-d(\{v\})\geq k_e
\quad(v\in e).
\tag{14}
\]
These still involve only singleton conditioning, so the same imported realization theorem applies.
The discrepancy norm must change with the interface. For \(r>2\), use
\[
\|F\|_{r-1,\square}
=
\sup_{0\leq f_i\leq1}
\left|
\int
F(x_1,\ldots,x_r)
\prod_{i=1}^r f_i(x_{[r]\setminus\{i\}})
\,dx
\right|.
\tag{15}
\]
The corresponding optimum being greater than one is again necessary and sufficient for uniform counting continuity. Negative certificates again produce finite-step kernels converging to one in \(L^1\); strict negative certificates give strong-\(L^p\) convergence with diverging counts.
The exact positive completion is now
\[
W_L(x_1,\ldots,x_r)
=
A_L(x_1,\ldots,x_r)
+
\frac{\prod_{i=1}^r r_L(x_i)}
{\bar r_L^{\,r-1}}.
\]
This extension does not substitute pairwise observations for higher-order information. The stronger discrepancy in (15) is an explicit part of the theorem, and no efficient procedure for acquiring it is claimed.
8. Computational resources, status, and remaining limits
For rational \(p\), deciding whether \(\gamma_H(p)>1\) requires rational linear programming of size exponential in the pattern’s vertex count:
\[
2^{O(n)}\operatorname{poly}(\operatorname{bit}(p))
\]
bit time, with the input-graph factor understood.
A positive result supplies a quantitative continuity certificate. A negative result supplies a finite counterexample description. However, at construction scale \(L\), its explicit label domain can have size \(2^{LM}\). The compact description is not a polynomial-size expanded matrix, and the theorem does not provide a polynomial-time algorithm for arbitrary graph-count integration.
For each fixed graph, the critical exponent is a computable real algebraic number: the bounded feasibility problem has coefficients polynomial in \(p\), so its parameter boundary is determined by finitely many polynomial sign conditions. This is an effective statement, not an efficient symbolic-threshold algorithm.
The executed checks include 13 exact rational primal–dual LP certificates, three exactly checked normal-relation descriptors, and five exact rational critical kernels for \(F_3\). They test the implementation and constructions; they do not independently validate the general proof or establish novelty.
The final proof does not depend on the recent OpenAI Sidorenko construction examined during direction selection. That source’s repository reports a formalized counterexample statement, but no replay of that formalization was performed here. 
The remaining boundaries are precise. A polynomial-size replacement for the exponential criterion is not supplied. Strong-\(L^p\) behavior exactly at \(\gamma=1\) is a different endpoint problem. Extending the construction to bounds conditioned on several vertices would require additional mathematics: the cited entropy work includes non-simple settings where the polymatroid relaxation is not tight. Simply replacing the finite criterion with a general entropy cone would relocate the difficulty. arXiv
What has been established here is the sharp marginal-normalized counting criterion, its constructive converse, and its multiway extension, relative to the stated published import. What remains unestablished is historical priority, independent validation, and a basis for calling it an independently major foundational breakthrough.
