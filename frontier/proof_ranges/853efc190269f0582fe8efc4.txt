Physical operation decision, 15 October 2026
Capital pin: a6190a1083f688a7e4bf64adbc5244ef150ff2c4
Status: NO_NEW_PHYSICAL_OPERATION_ACQUIRED. Park device development.

The salt proposal has not acquired a selectively reactive, load-bearing, reversible NH4SCN interface. The primary salt experiment establishes a bulk hydrostatic dissolution/precipitation response. It does not establish the nonhydrostatic contact response needed by the supplied proposal. Its cooling-cycle capacity and efficiency are design/model results, distinct from the directly measured temperature response. Source: Zhang et al., Extreme barocaloric effect at dissolution, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-10013-1 ; acquired helper main page, Extended Data Fig.7/8 captions and Supplementary Video 2 description; helper SI inspected. Original helper bytes are retained in sources/salt_helper_main.html and salt_helper_si.pdf.

One intervention changes the exposed common-solution assumption: export the dissolved inventory rapidly into a physically isolated reservoir, then close the flow path before warm regeneration. Pumps and valves are obtainable. The required simultaneous material response is not. Export can compete with growth on the original exposed faces, and isolation does not remove supersaturation or nuclei already exported. No same-platform NH4SCN loaded-contact data were acquired showing a usable interval between dissolution and competing precipitation. This intervention therefore remains an experimentally unresolved escape, not an acquired operation. No coating, calcite kinetics or assumed selectivity is credited.

The decisive test should be smaller than a refrigerator. On one NH4SCN specimen at a fixed cold temperature, apply a specified contact stress and pulse the solution flow into a separately sampled reservoir. Vary residence time only. Record contact displacement, actual wetted area, force, exported dissolved inventory, precipitation everywhere, calorimetric heat and pump/actuator work. After the cold pulse, take the exported inventory through the proposed warm regrowth stroke and record restored mass, geometry and solution composition. Exposed-face, zero-stress and inert mechanical controls are required to interpret this intervention. These are proposed measurements, not reported experiments.

Two necessary gates reject apparent successes early. The exported composition must exceed the measured warm-regrowth equilibrium threshold. In a restricted constant-enthalpy cold-stroke model,

  Qc = Delta_h_diss * n_retained - W_contact - E_other_cold.

With leading contact work W_contact ~ sigma*v_s*n_gross, positive cooling requires

  n_retained/n_gross > (sigma*v_s + E_other_cold/n_gross)/Delta_h_diss.

All quantities must be measured in that same material and regime. The enthalpy/work approximation cannot replace the calibrated first-law balance. If regrowth consumes salt elsewhere, fails to restore the load-bearing geometry, or requires an external separator, that state/resource enters the cycle boundary. Rapid visible retreat alone is not a pass. This condition extends the decision to finite-rate exports without claiming a universal impossibility theorem for all selective interfaces.
