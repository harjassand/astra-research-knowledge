## 9. Checks and failures retained

- Forty-eight independent finite degenerate S+A models gave 192 derivative-inequality checks at orders zero through three. The maximum observed error/bound ratio was about 0.2362. A separately constructed exact symbolic five-dimensional model verified the complete rational error identity and its first three derivatives.
- 112 wide-word columns at spans 4,8,14,18,20,22,26 were compared with an independently written Python commutator implementation. Unsupported encodings were rejected.
- Every saved exact central and anchor packet was reconstructed without numerical optimization. All exact functionals and residual Gram matrices agreed. A deliberately corrupted f coefficient was rejected for population imbalance.
- 2952 exact rational point checks independently tested the Bernstein polynomial enclosure implementation.
- The earlier 20-bit packed encoding was unsuitable when exploratory enrichment applied a commutator to an already extended residual. The exact metadata checker rejected twenty malformed discovery words. The encoder was widened to 28 bits, an explicit range guard added, and the entire reported fresh run restarted from footprint-eight dictionaries. No invalid packet enters any result here. Prior packets with smaller footprints were not affected by this discovery-only range issue.

These checks validate finite algebra and implementation behavior, not the infinite-dimensional proof. No laboratory data or exact full infinite inverse is available for independent comparison.
