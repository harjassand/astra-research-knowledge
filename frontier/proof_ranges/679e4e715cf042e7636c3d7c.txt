# Applying the inspected polyconvexity criteria to F

Append-only clarification, 15 October 2026. The four original priority
files remain byte-for-byte unchanged. This note answers the additional
criterion-applicability gate; it adds no historical originality claim.

Theorems 1.1/2.1 in
[Dacorogna–Koshigoe (1993)](https://numdam.org/item/AFST_1993_6_2_2_163_0.pdf)
assume hypothesis (H), invariance under both left and right proper
rotations. The candidate fails the right-invariance hypothesis, since
`F(e11)=1` while `F(e11 R(pi/4))=14/9`. Their special rotational
criteria therefore do not decide this F.

**Aubert's general planar criterion does apply.**
[Aubert (1988), Theorem 1.3](https://numdam.org/item/M2AN_1988__22_1_5_0.pdf),
printed pp.8–10, gives the determinant-support characterization for
differentiable functions on all planar matrices. In equivalent notation,

\[
F\text{ is PC}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\forall A\ \exists\lambda(A)\in\mathbb R\ \forall H:
F(A+H)-F(A)-DF(A):H-\lambda(A)\det H\ge0.
\]

For this homogeneous quartic it specializes exactly to

\[
\boxed{\forall A\ \exists\lambda\ \forall H:\quad
F(H)+DF(H):A+\tfrac12D^2F(A)[H,H]-\lambda\det H\ge0.}
\]

Here F is the explicit nine-coefficient polynomial in the priority
addendum, not an unrestricted unknown polynomial. This is an applicable
complete *globally quantified* criterion, not a solved coefficient
classification. The second-order consequence alone is rank-one
convexity (Aubert, Corollary 1.4 and following Remark 1); replacing the
displayed all-H inequality by its Hessian would lose the PC obligation.

Its application at the four exact contacts supplies a useful exact check.
Write (P(k)=17k^4-314k^2+17), use the author's displayed (A(k)), and
let (R(k)=e_1\otimes(1,k)). Nonnegativity of the support polynomial
forces its Hessian at zero to annihilate the acoustic-null vector R.
Consequently any PC support at this A must use the unique value

\[
\lambda(A(k))\equiv
\frac{56694784-3364864k^2}{3}\pmod {P(k)}.
\]

The complete vector identity
`D²F(A(k)) R(k) = lambda(A(k)) cof R(k)` is checked modulo P.
Testing the support inequality on every radial increment (H=tA(k))
then reduces to the necessary condition (2F(A(k))-\lambda(A(k))q(A(k))
\ge0). Its exact remainder is

\[
\frac{881721344}{153}(48246-2597k^2)>0
\qquad(P(k)=0).
\]

Indeed both positive roots in (r=k^2) are below their sum (314/17),
and (48246-2597(314/17)>0). Thus these contact and radial consequences
of Aubert's criterion **do not exclude polyconvexity**. They are not
sufficient to prove the all-A, all-H statement. At A=0 a support exists
with lambda=0 because F is pointwise nonnegative, but that single
background also does not decide the global criterion.

The supplied source and Fantuzzi's Proposition 2.3 use the same classical
minor-support principle. No complete eliminated PC criterion for this
left-only nine-coefficient class was found in the inspected special
rotational results. **PC and QC of F remain UNKNOWN.** The exact symbolic
substitution and necessary-condition checks are reproducible with
`verify_pc_criterion_v0002.py`; its separate receipt does not claim to
check the globally quantified support inequality.
