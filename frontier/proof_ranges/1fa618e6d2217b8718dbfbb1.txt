No locality lemma from N711 is used in this proof.
Suppose a family of systems and parameter directions satisfies
\[ \frac{u_n^TF_Qu_n}{\|u_n\|_2^2}\longrightarrow\infty, \]
while \(\beta,g,k,\ell,\Delta,\eta\) remain fixed.
Equation (3) immediately yields
\[ \boxed{ \frac{u_n^TF_{\mathrm{loc}}u_n}      {u_n^TF_Qu_n} \longrightarrow1. } \tag{13} \]
For example, if a collective direction has \(\|u_n\|^2=\Theta(n)\) and quantum Fisher information \(\Theta(n^{1+\alpha})\), \(\alpha>0\), the relative information loss is \(O(n^{-\alpha})\).
Thus, when superextensive equilibrium sensitivity exists, an entangling readout is unnecessary to preserve its leading information.
This does not assert that every model exhibits enhanced scaling. It also does not convert equilibrium sensitivity into a free metrological advantage: preparing equilibrium states, especially across competing phases, remains a separate resource.
For a scalar perturbation
\[ H(t)=H_0+tA_u, \]
let
\[ f=u\cdot Y,\qquad v=\operatorname{Var}(f),\qquad C_u=u^TCu,\qquad q=\|u\|_2^2. \]
Then
\[ C_u\le v\le C_u+\nu q, \qquad \frac{d}{dt}\mathbb Ef=-\beta C_u. \tag{14} \]
The information obtainable from the first moment through local error propagation is
\[ F_{\mathrm{mom}}=\frac{\beta^2C_u^2}{v}. \]
It satisfies
\[ F_{\mathrm{mom}} \le u^TF_{\mathrm{loc}}u \le u^TF_Qu \le\beta^2C_u, \]
and therefore
\[ \boxed{ \frac{F_{\mathrm{mom}}}{u^TF_Qu} \ge \frac{C_u}{C_u+\nu q}. } \tag{15} \]
This is not merely an existence statement about a complicated likelihood statistic. When the response is enhanced, the ordinary sample mean of one explicitly computable feature is nearly optimal.
At a calibrated reference point \(t_0\), the locally unbiased estimator
\[ \widehat t = t_0+ \frac{\overline f-\mathbb E_{t_0}f}{-\beta C_u(t_0)} \]
has exact variance
\[ \operatorname{Var}_{t_0}(\widehat t) = \frac{v}{N\beta^2C_u^2}. \]
Calibration acquisition and finite-displacement bias control are not included in that formula.
Equation (14) implies
\[ (v-\nu q)_+\le C_u\le v. \]
Therefore a directly measured scalar variance gives
\[ \boxed{ \beta^2\frac{(v-\nu q)_+^2}{v} \le u^TF_{\mathrm{loc}}u \le u^TF_Qu \le \beta^2v. } \tag{16} \]
The lower expression is defined as zero when \(v=0\).
This simultaneously bounds the information of the actual local measurement from below and the information of every possible quantum measurement from above. If \(v\gg\nu q\), the ratio of the certificate’s endpoints,
\[ \left(1-\frac{\nu q}{v}\right)^2, \]
is close to one.
It also certifies the static susceptibility:
\[ \boxed{ \beta(v-\nu q)_+ \le -\frac{d}{dt}\mathbb Ef \le \beta v. } \tag{17} \]
The certificate requires the Gibbs-family assumption, a known perturbation direction, temperature, structural strength bounds, and detector calibration. It does not require reconstructing the full Hamiltonian, evaluating its partition function, solving for an SLD, or measuring dynamical response.
The feature is bounded by
\[ |f|\le L_u,\qquad L_u=(3/\eta)^k\|u\|_1. \]
Using \(2M\) independent copies, form
\[ Z_j=\frac{(f_{2j-1}-f_{2j})^2}{2}. \]
Then
\[ \mathbb EZ_j=v,\qquad 0\le Z_j\le2L_u^2. \]
