2. The one-extra-state obstruction, with complete proof
------------------------------------------------------
Model class M is closed under adding one microscopic state whose observation label
duplicates an existing label. This is true for unrestricted finite hidden topology
and state count. Take any base model A=(Q,pi,h) in M with sigma_A>0, any state a in S,
and any w in (0,1), epsilon>0. Append state b, leave all old off-diagonal rates as
before, and set

    q'_ab=epsilon,
    q'_ba=w pi_a epsilon/(1-w),
    q'_ib=q'_bi=0 for i!=a.

Adjust the two diagonals to make rows sum to zero, and assign h'(b)=h(a). The new
chain is finite, irreducible, and has reciprocal support. Its stationary distribution is

    pi'_i=w pi_i for i in S;       pi'_b=1-w.

Indeed, old internal stationarity is scaled by w, and the new bridge has equal
stationary flows pi'_a q'_ab=pi'_b q'_ba=w pi_a epsilon. The bridge contributes
zero EP, and every old edge contributes w times its former EP. Therefore

    sigma(Q')=w sigma_A.                                      (N2)

Couple a path of the base chain started at pi with a path of Q' started inside S.
Until the first bridge jump, the old jump rates agree. Conditional on a base path,
the probability that the independent bridge clock never fires is

    exp[-epsilon integral_0^T 1{X_t=a} dt] >= exp(-epsilon T).

The stationary probability of starting inside S is w. Thus for every measurable
event E in the observed-record space, including independent algorithm randomization,

    P_Q'(E) >= w exp(-epsilon T) P_A(E).                       (N3)

Suppose the same procedure L_T satisfies P_Q{L_T<=sigma(Q)}>=1-alpha for every
Q in M. Let E={L_T>=c sigma_A}, choose w<c, and use (N2). Then E is a coverage
failure for Q', giving

    w exp(-epsilon T) P_A(E) <= alpha.

Let epsilon decrease to zero, then w increase to c. This proves (N1).
The limiting model itself need not be irreducible: every positive epsilon used in
the argument is admissible, so the proof uses no inadmissible model as a premise.

Fixed-time consequence: an honest sequence L_T over M cannot converge in probability
to sigma_A at any driven base A. For c>alpha, convergence would give P_A(E)->1,
contradicting the ceiling alpha/c<1 at every T. This is stronger than saying that
the convergence rate is unknown. The entire pointwise consistency of uniformly
honest bounds fails over this extension-closed class.

Anytime consequence: let T_k increase to infinity and assume
P_Q{forall k, L_{T_k}<=sigma(Q)}>=1-alpha for every Q. Apply (N3) on the longest
finite prefix to E_N={there exists k<=N: L_{T_k}>=c sigma_A}. The same limiting
argument bounds P_A(E_N) by alpha/c; continuity from below gives the bound for
the event with any finite k. No deterministic duration can evade this result.

n-replicate consequence: for n independent stationary trajectories of finite durations
T_1,...,T_n, the no-bridge lower domination is
w^n exp[-epsilon sum_i T_i] P_A^{replicates}(E). Consequently

    P_A^{replicates}{L>=c sigma_A} <= min(1, alpha/c^n).        (N4)

To attain power p>alpha, it is necessary that
n >= ceil(log(p/alpha)/(-log c)). For alpha=.05,p=.95,c=.9 this is 28 independent
stationary starts, regardless of each finite trajectory's duration. Splitting one
trajectory into 28 segments does not supply 28 stationary independent starts.
No attainability theorem for this replica floor is claimed.

Scope: known fixed hidden-state count excluding this extension, a certified mixing
bound, interventions, nonstationary active-state initialization, or a known microscopic
generator are outside (N1)'s contract. Those may permit useful inference. The added
state is hidden even when the base record distinguishes every base state: its label
duplicates a, so the extension does not enlarge the observation alphabet.
