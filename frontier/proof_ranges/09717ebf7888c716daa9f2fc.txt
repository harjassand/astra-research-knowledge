4. A second complete deduction: parallel slabs are unstable
A natural competitor to the three-sector partition divides the plane into three parallel Gaussian slabs of equal measure.
This competitor can be ruled out for every positive correlation, without assuming the standard-simplex conjecture.
Proposition. For every fixed \(0<\rho<1\), the equal-measure three-slab partition of \(\mathbb R^2\) admits arbitrarily small measure-preserving perturbations that strictly increase its Gaussian noise stability.
Proof
Set \(a=\Phi^{-1}(2/3)\), and begin with
\[
A_1^0=\{x_1<-a\},\quad
A_2^0=\{-a\le x_1\le a\},\quad
A_3^0=\{x_1>a\}.
\]

For small \(\varepsilon>0\), rotate the outward normals of the two extreme halfspaces toward the same side:
\[
u_\varepsilon=(-\cos\varepsilon,\sin\varepsilon),
\qquad
v_\varepsilon=(\cos\varepsilon,\sin\varepsilon).
\]

Let \(H_1=\{u_\varepsilon\cdot x>a\}\) and \(H_3=\{v_\varepsilon\cdot x>a\}\). Both retain Gaussian measure \(1/3\).
Temporarily treat \(1-1_{H_1}-1_{H_3}\) as the middle-cell indicator. Expanding the corresponding quadratic noise-stability functional gives
\[
\widetilde S_\rho(\varepsilon)
=-\frac13+4S_\rho(H_1)
+2\Pr(u_\varepsilon\cdot X>a,\;
       v_\varepsilon\cdot Y>a).
\]

The halfspace stability \(S_\rho(H_1)\) is rotation-invariant. The correlation of the two scalar variables in the final probability is
\[
t_\varepsilon=\rho\,u_\varepsilon\cdot v_\varepsilon
=-\rho\cos(2\varepsilon).
\]

For a bivariate standard Gaussian, differentiation of the upper-tail probability with respect to its correlation gives the positive joint density \(\varphi_2(a,a;t)\). Since
\[
t_\varepsilon=-\rho+2\rho\varepsilon^2+O(\varepsilon^4),
\]

we obtain
\[
\widetilde S_\rho(\varepsilon)
-\widetilde S_\rho(0)
=
4\rho\,\varphi_2(a,a;-\rho)\varepsilon^2
+O(\varepsilon^4)>0
\]

for sufficiently small \(\varepsilon\).
There is one issue: \(H_1,H_3\) overlap. However, any point in their intersection satisfies
\[
x_2>\frac a{\sin\varepsilon},
\]

so its Gaussian measure is at most
\[
\exp\left(-\frac{a^2}{2\sin^2\varepsilon}\right).
\]

Remove the overlap from \(H_3\) and replace it with an equal-measure subset of the remaining middle region. This restores an exact three-cell partition with all probabilities equal to \(1/3\).
The correction changes the noise-stability functional by at most a constant times the overlap measure, which is smaller than every positive power of \(\varepsilon\). It therefore cannot cancel the positive quadratic improvement.
The resulting exact balanced partition strictly improves the slabs. \(\square\)
This eliminates an entire natural one-dimensional configuration class for every \(0<\rho<1\). It does not eliminate general curved or nonconical competitors. Historical novelty of this perturbation argument has not been established.
