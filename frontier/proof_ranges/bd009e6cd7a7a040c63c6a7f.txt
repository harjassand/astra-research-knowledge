## Positivity with nonzero circulation

Let \(F(x,y)=(x^2+y^2)^2\), \(\psi_1=\cos y\), \(\psi_2=\sin y\),
and let \((r,s)\) traverse the counterclockwise unit square with unit width
on each of its four edges. At \(B=(1,1)\),
\(F_{xy}=8xy\) and \(gf'-fg'=-1\), so the curvature is the constant \(-8\).
The square has \(M_{00}=1\), hence its profile circulation is exactly \(-8\).

The compatible potential is \(\phi(x,y)=r(x)\cos y+s(x)\sin y\). Exact
normalized integration of \(F(B+D\phi)\) over the four strips gives
edge integrals \(148/15,1349/120,1349/120,148/15\), total
\(2533/60\). Dividing by the period 4 gives mean energy \(2533/240\), while
\(F(B)=4\). The Jensen gap is therefore \(1573/240>0\) despite the nonzero
negative circulation. Convexity also gives positivity for every constant
background. Thus circulation cancellation is a sufficient route to
positivity through the supplied identity, not a necessary condition for
positivity itself.
