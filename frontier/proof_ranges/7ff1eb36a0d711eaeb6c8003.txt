## Primary checks and an additional preparation hazard

The molecular prior-art collision is substantive. Rivera et al. describe an
adiabatic triplet valence-bond rearrangement leaving an excited product, followed
by energy transfer to a new reactant. Their transient spectra support the
carrier identification; the reported submicron gain is about100-120, while the
approximately300 powder comparison is qualitative. Product powder diffraction
showed amorphization, so this result does not show extended-covalent-lattice
repair with pristine structural retention. [Rivera2023](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2023/sc/d3sc01074k)

The final Hamdi paper supports the specified model inputs, neutral defect
formation energy7.2eV, excitation4.53eV and geometry relaxation.47eV. It does
not compute repair. Importantly, it predicts that UV absorption in the triplet
photoionizes the defect into a dark state; continuous preparation can therefore
terminate the very state proposed for recycling. This loss cannot be omitted by
assigning preparation and chain lifetimes independently. A pulse-off sequence
or another preparation method needs its own acquired survival and cost account.
The first arXiv version instead uses1.8ns radiative lifetime; its numbers must
not be substituted silently for the final published2.4ns model. The final PDF
was acquired, text-read at the relevant passages and visually checked at page3.
[Hamdi2020 final](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41524-020-00451-y.pdf),
[Hamdi preprint v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2008.06857v1)

Plo et al.'s primary abstract and summary explicitly conclude that the studied
ubiquitous UV center is a carbon dimer, using isotope-selective carbon doping,
polytype variation and pressure response. This removes its use as experimental
confirmation of a Stone-Wales center; it does not exclude nearby emission from
some other Stone-Wales population. The full underlying data were not reanalyzed.
[Plo2025](https://journals.aps.org/prx/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevX.15.021045)

Bui et al. measured single-vacancy creation at50-90keV; damage below80keV is not
explained by the elastic ground-state threshold alone. Javed et al. show that
oxygen changes beam-induced pore growth/shape; molecular oxygen without beam did
not reproduce that growth. Their reported pressures are objective-gauge readings,
and the impingement analysis includes an estimated factor10 between gauge and
sample pressure. Hence even the atmosphere label is an acquired measurement
interface. Neither paper establishes optical repair. Primary Wiley abstract,
results/method passages and Javed PDF selected results/methods were read; page7
of Javed was visually checked. [Bui2023](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/smll.202301926),
[Javed2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-76612-2.pdf)

The Lang-Kimerling primary abstract supports recombination-enhanced GaAs defect
annealing. The source's additional silicon, nanotube and pyrene comparator claims
are retained as source-reported where full primary passages were not acquired.
No broad novelty clearance is claimed. [Lang-Kimerling1974](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.33.489)
