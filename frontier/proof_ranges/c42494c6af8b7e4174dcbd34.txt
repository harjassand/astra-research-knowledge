CRAY result — integrate resonant blocks before sampling
The strongest surviving opening is exact conditional integration across spectral resonances, with a local theorem showing that it removes a genuine variance singularity without changing the original integral.

I also constructed a complete independent-sampling estimator for conventional onsite-quartic actions. It runs in seconds on the tested models, rather than requiring the previous prohibitive stochastic time-stepping bound. However, its useful guarantee selects a strongly gapped, effectively perturbative regime—and specialised methods beat it there. That estimator should not become the next breakthrough candidate.

The unresolved opportunity is more specific: acquire an affordable decomposition that integrates the genuinely dangerous resonances while controlling the remaining global cancellation. The local mathematics is established below; the necessary global theorem is not.
