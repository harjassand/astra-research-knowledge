## 1. The original joint Pauli CTMC is exact with an arbitrary reference

Let S be a stabilizer group with no -I, P_0 its code projector, and V an
encoding isometry onto that whole code. Let P_s be its nonzero joint syndrome
projectors. For every physical Pauli label e, choose representative E_e and
write, for an arbitrary logical-reference operator X_LR,

    X_e = (E_e V tensor I_R) X_LR (V^dagger E_e^dagger tensor I_R).

Its syndrome s(e) depends only on Pauli commutation. Therefore

    (P_s tensor I_R) X_e = X_e(P_s tensor I_R)
                         = 1_(s=s(e)) X_e.                  (Q1)

This identity is on the entire logical operator space, including off-diagonal
logical matrix units. It is not a statement limited to classical codewords.

For a jump J_alpha=Q_alpha f_alpha(S), with a fixed unitary Pauli string Q and
a scalar function of the stabilizer syndrome, let r_alpha(s)=|f_alpha(s)|^2.
Then J_alpha^dagger J_alpha=|f_alpha(S)|^2 and direct multiplication gives

    D[J_alpha](X_e)
       = r_alpha(s(e)) [X_(e+q_alpha)-X_e].                 (Q2)

No commutation of Q_alpha with f_alpha(S) is needed. The rate is evaluated
at the pre-jump syndrome. A Hamiltonian H(S) which is scalar on each entire
syndrome eigenspace has [H(S),X_e]=0.

Let p_t(e) solve the finite joint Pauli-label forward equation with transition
rates r_alpha(s(e)), starting at identity. Substituting its forward equation
and (Q2) shows that sum_e p_t(e)X_e solves the quantum master equation with
the correct initial value. Uniqueness of a finite-dimensional linear ODE gives

    (exp(tG) Enc tensor Id_R)(X_LR) = sum_e p_t(e) X_e.       (Q3)

The same p_t works for every logical input and every R. Linear dependence among
some Pauli-orbit states does not matter: the proof constructs a solution and
does not infer a unique decomposition from a density matrix.

The no-jump part is also exact. If R_op=sum_alpha J_alpha^dagger J_alpha, it
is a stabilizer function, with eigenvalue r(s) on P_s. Thus

    exp[-itH(S)-tR_op/2] E_e V
      = exp[-itH(s(e))-t r(s(e))/2] E_e V.                  (Q4)

Normalized no-jump evolution leaves this orbit density operator unchanged;
its survival weight is exp[-t r(s(e))]. It cannot filter a logical amplitude
inside the syndrome space. Conditioning by a logical operator that merely
commutes with the checks, but is not scalar on the syndrome space, would break
this conclusion.

For the specified generator the seven active projectors at a vertex are
orthogonal, and sum_O J_(v,O)^dagger J_(v,O)<=kappa I. On the invariant family
it is exactly a rate-kappa proposal with a null action if no pattern is active.
A four-outgoing-edge correction is one two-face Pauli transition, not two
independent face jumps. Products of X,Z,Y corrections differ from the binary
sum of their labels only by scalar phase, which cancels in (Q3).

A Y_f jump toggles both CSS labels. The joint distribution retains this
correlation. For a function of the Z-error label alone, its flip rate is
lambda_Z+lambda_Y and its sweep depends on that sector only, so its marginal
closes in the original constant-rate model. The other marginal has rate
lambda_X+lambda_Y. Their independence is unnecessary; a union bound controls
the two failures. If acceptance reads both syndromes, marginal Markov closure
can fail, even though the joint lemma still holds and a proposal envelope may
still be available.

## 2. The final recovery and the exact Pauli diamond constant

For each nonzero syndrome P_s choose a Pauli R_s taking it to the code:
R_s P_s R_s^dagger=P_0. Only characters of the independent stabilizers occur;
inconsistent redundant-check patterns have zero projector. Define

    D_s = V^dagger R_s P_s,
    Dec(X) = sum_s D_s X D_s^dagger.                         (Q5)

Since V V^dagger=P_0,

    D_s^dagger D_s=P_s,      sum_s D_s^dagger D_s=I.

This is a full CPTP decoder on the physical Hilbert space, including states
outside the code. Bare compression by V^dagger is not TP there; the syndrome
recovery in (Q5) is indispensable.

In the toric code, a decoded residual Pauli with both CSS 2-chains homologically

space. Therefore (Q3) followed by this fixed decoder is a logical Pauli channel

    Lambda_T = sum_e p_T(e) Ad_(L_e).

Let p_nonI be its total nonidentity logical Pauli weight. The channel is

identity is at most 2p_nonI. The normalized logical maximally entangled state
