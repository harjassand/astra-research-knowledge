1. PATH HELLINGER DOMINATION LEMMA
Let Q be any finite stationary CTMC on the support of pi, and Q* its stationary adjoint. Assume all hidden variables are physically even. Let S=(Q+Q*)/2. Write p,q,s for the stationary path densities of Q,Q*,S on [0,T] with respect to a common path-skeleton/time reference measure. For finite entropy production, reverse edges share support; infinite entropy production makes every finite lower bound automatic.

Stationarity gives the same escape rate lambda_i for Q and Q* at every hidden state. S has the same escape rate. Their path densities therefore share the initial factor pi_(x0) and integrated holding factor exp(-integral lambda_(X_u)du). Only the jump factors differ. On every path,
  sqrt(p q)
    =pi_(x0) exp(-integral lambda) product_jumps sqrt(Q_ij Q*_ij)
    <=pi_(x0) exp(-integral lambda) product_jumps [(Q_ij+Q*_ij)/2]
    =s,                                                        (H1)
by edgewise arithmetic-geometric mean. This is pointwise path-density domination, not an assertion that Q* and Q trajectories have a common transition kernel.

Put r=sqrt(pq), m=(p+q)/2, and A=integral r. Both m-r and s-r are nonnegative, and each integrates to 1-A. Hence
  TV(m,s)=(1/2)integral |m-s|
    <=(1/2)integral[(m-r)+(s-r)]=1-A.                    (H2)
For finite path KL, Jensen under p gives
  A=E_p exp[-(1/2)log(p/q)]
    >=exp[-D(p||q)/2]=exp[-T sigma/2].                  (H3)
Here D(p||q)=T sigma is the usual stationary all-even finite-CTMC forward-versus-reversed-path relative entropy. Equations (H1)-(H3) imply
  TV((P_Q+P_Q*)/2,P_S) <=1-exp(-T sigma/2).             (H4)
No hidden dimension, rate cap, stationary-mass lower bound, or reversibility of the observed process appears in this lemma.

2. EVERY TIME-SYMMETRIC OBSERVED TEST HAS LINEAR TRANSFER
Let f be any observed finite-window statistic, bounded in an interval of length R>0, and pointwise invariant under reversing the time labels. The stationary Q* path law is the reversal of the stationary Q path law, so
  E_Q f=E_Q* f=E_(P_Q+P_Q*)/2 f.
By (H4) and the range bound on expectations,
  |E_Q f-E_S f| <=R[1-exp(-T sigma/2)].                 (H5)
If f has expectation at least zero under every stationary reversible hidden CTMC and E_Q f<=-delta<0, then
  sigma >= -(2/T)log(1-delta/R) >=2delta/(R T).         (H6)
The formula is interpreted only for delta<R; an endpoint gap delta=R requires infinite entropy production. The present witness has R<=2, so the safe bound is
  sigma >= -(2/T)log(1-delta/2) >=delta/T.              (H7)
This transfer applies to any finite symmetric equilibrium-separation statistic, not only reflection copositive tests. It is particularly useful here because every reflection Gram kernel of the target is CP.
