## Prior work / novelty boundaries

- Jun, Gavrilov and Bechhoefer (2014), High-precision test of Landauer's principle
  in a feedback trap. PRL 113, 190601; arXiv:1408.5089. Barrier/tilt restoration
  protocols are established prior art.
- Akcay et al. (2008), Influence of mechanical boundary conditions on the
  electrocaloric properties of ferroelectric thin films. JAP 103, 024104;
  DOI: 10.1063/1.2831222. Static mechanical constraints affecting transition
  order and electrocaloric response are established prior art.
- Gottschall et al. (2018), A multicaloric cooling cycle that exploits thermal
  hysteresis. Nature Materials 17, 929-934; DOI: 10.1038/s41563-018-0166-6.
  Multiple physical stimuli in caloric cycles are established prior art.
- Kvasov and Tagantsev (2013), Role of high-order electromechanical coupling
  terms in thermodynamics of ferroelectric thin films. PRB 87, 184101;
  DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevB.87.184101. Higher-order coupling limits simple models.
- Luk'yanchuk et al. (2019), Harnessing ferroelectric domains for negative
  capacitance. Communications Physics; DOI: 10.1038/s42005-019-0121-0.
  Charge-controlled/negative-capacitance approaches are necessary comparators.
- Bar et al. (2026), Domain-induced control of latent heat in freestanding
  BaTiO3 membranes. arXiv:2604.26089v1, a preprint dated 28 April 2026.
  Relevant evidence and parameters, not validation of the proposed gate.
