## References

[1] Krishnakumar Balasubramanian and Shiva Kasiviswanathan. *A Dimension-Free Bound on the Poincaré Constant of Isotropic Log-Concave Measures*. arXiv:2610.07728v2, revised 8 October 2026. Section 7, especially Lemma 7.1, supplies the credited operator-comparison context. The paper's full claimed KLS theorem was not independently reviewed here.

[2] Jean-Christophe Bourin and Mitsuru Uchiyama. *A matrix subadditivity inequality for f(A+B) and f(A)+f(B)*. arXiv:math/0702475, 2007. A broader classical result than the trace-power inequality proved directly in Section 4.1.

[3] Alex Townsend and Heather Wilber. *On the singular values of matrices with high displacement rank*. arXiv:1712.05864v2, 2018. Relevant established source-to-singular-spectrum methodology for linear matrix equations; not an asserted equivalence with the nonlinear setting here.

[4] Marius Lemm and Angelo Lucia. *On the critical finite-size gap scaling for frustration-free Hamiltonians*. arXiv:2409.09685, 2024.

[5] V. Arvind, Abhranil Chatterjee, Utsab Ghosal, Partha Mukhopadhyay, and C. Ramya. *On Identity Testing and Noncommutative Rank Computation over the Free Skew Field*. arXiv:2209.04797, 2022.

[6] William Arveson. *Subalgebras of C*-algebras III: multivariable operator theory*. arXiv:funct-an/9705007v2, 1997. Also, *The curvature invariant of a Hilbert module over C[z_1,...,z_d]*, arXiv:math/9808100, 1998. Established context for multivariable shifts, contractive Hilbert modules, and module-growth invariants. No novelty is claimed for those objects themselves.
