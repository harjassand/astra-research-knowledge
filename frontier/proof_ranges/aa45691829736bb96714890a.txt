## 6. Universal channel proof: the easy direction and the exact interfaces

If Lambda tensor Lambda is EA, it converts every pair in (11) into a separable two-qubit state. It also preserves separability of the unpaired factors. Each component becomes fully separable, and so does the mixture. More generally, it annihilates all two-producible inputs at every size. This direction requires no normal form and holds on the channel boundary.

For the converse first assume Lambda is **strictly positive**: every nonzero positive semidefinite input has a positive definite output. The quantum Sinkhorn theorem supplies invertible filters A_0,B such that

\[
\Phi=\Phi_{A_0}\circ\Lambda\circ\Phi_B
\quad\text{is CPTP and unital},\qquad \Phi_X(Y)=XYX^\dagger. \tag{20}
\]

This is an established external theorem, not something certified by our numerical scaling script; the exact strict-positivity hypothesis and its role are retained [F21, Sec. II; Aubrun–Szarek 2017, Prop. 2.32 cited there]. Normal-form entanglement analysis is also established [F18].

Invertible product congruences preserve separability in both directions, and prefilters map the positive cone onto itself. Consequently Lambda tensor Lambda is EA iff Phi tensor Phi is EA. By the identical-unital theorem [F12, Prop. 2], if Lambda tensor Lambda is not EA, then s=||T_Phi||_F^2>1.

The crucial same-channel identity is

\[
(B\otimes B)|\psi^-\rangle=(\det B)|\psi^-\rangle.        \tag{21}
\]

It is valid for any complex two-by-two B. It would not hold with arbitrary different filters on the two sites. This is why identical noise, not merely independent noise, is essential.

Let Psi_0=Phi_{A_0} circle Lambda and H_0=Psi_0^*(I). Trace preservation in (20) gives B^dagger H_0 B=I and det H_0=|det B|^{-2}. Rescale Psi_0 by (det H_0)^{-1/2}, corresponding to a scalar rescaling of A_0, to obtain a CP map Psi with H=Psi^*(I) and det H=1. Equation (21) then gives **exactly**

\[
(\Psi\otimes\Psi)(S_-)
=(\Phi\otimes\Phi)(S_-)=:\tau.                          \tag{22}
\]

Tau is normalized with both local marginals I/2. Its collective pair spin has zero mean and

\[
\operatorname{tr}(\tau J_{\rm pair}^2)=\frac{3-s}{2}=2v_0,
\quad v_0=(3-s)/4<1/2.                                  \tag{23}
\]

No input filter B is physically applied to R_N. Only an output filter is used as a mathematical entanglement witness. This distinction is essential for a channel-independent input family.

## 7. Why factorial tails remove the filtering obstruction

Write the eigenvalues of H as e^{+b},e^{-b}, with b>=0. In (11), every pair is mapped to the normalized state tau. The unpaired 2j-qubit factor contributes a positive trace z_j satisfying

\[
0<z_j\le e^{2bj},\qquad z_0=1.
\]

After normalization of Psi^{tensor N}(R_N), the spin-label mixing probabilities obey

\[
\widetilde p_{N,j}
=\frac{p_{N,j}z_j}{\sum_kp_{N,k}z_k}
\le\frac{p_{N,j}}{p_{N,0}}e^{2bj}
\le(2j+1)^2 f_j e^{2bj}.                                \tag{24}
\]

Thus the expected active-spin energy is bounded, uniformly in N, by the finite number

\[
C(b)=\sum_{j=0}^\infty j(j+1)(2j+1)^2 f_j e^{2bj}.        \tag{25}
\]

The normalized output has a mixture of n-j independent pair states tau and a state on 2j active qubits. Pair means vanish; the active total-spin operator has J_active^2<=j(j+1)I. Consequently

\[
\left\langle J^2\right\rangle_{
\Psi^{\otimes N}(R_N)/\operatorname{tr}\Psi^{\otimes N}(R_N)}
\le Nv_0+C(b).                                         \tag{26}
\]

Whenever N(s-1)>4C(b), the right side is <N/2. The filtered output is entangled. A product filter cannot create entanglement from a fully separable input, so Lambda^{tensor N}(R_N) was already entangled.

This changes the obstruction in the prior thermal approach: exponential sector weights can be overwhelmed by a fixed exponential filter tilt, whereas the factorial weights tolerate **every fixed tilt**. The latter assertion is exactly (18), not an analogy between unrelated Gaussian limits.

To turn A into a legal local Kraus operator, divide it by its operator norm. This changes success probability but not the normalized state in (26). Success may decrease exponentially with N. No nonvanishing unfiltered-distance bound follows from this argument.

### Boundary channels

For arbitrary Lambda outside the two-qubit EA set, choose a sufficiently small fixed epsilon>0 such that

\[
\Lambda_\epsilon=D_{1-\epsilon}\circ\Lambda
\]

is still outside that set. Such an epsilon exists: one two-qubit input gives an entangled output, and entanglement persists in an open neighborhood of that finite-dimensional output because the separable set is compact. Lambda_epsilon is strictly positive. The preceding proof applies to the **same R_N** and yields entanglement after Lambda_epsilon at every sufficiently large even N. Since its extra depolarization is local, Lambda^{tensor N}(R_N) must also be entangled. All such outputs retain singleton separability and depth<=2. This proves Theorem B for every qubit CPTP map, including rank-deficient/boundary channels.
