5. QUANTUM SELECTED-RANGE TRUNCATION WITHOUT ANGLE LOSSES

Files: r24_quantum_entangling_encoder.txt sections5–10;
root_tag_component_truncation.txt; root_kernel_occupation.txt.

Target: arbitrary rational k-local projectors of sufficiently small relative
rank p(k,Delta), bounded overlap Delta, prepare a trace-epsilon approximation
to some exact common-kernel state in time polynomial in n, explicit local
matrix sizes/bits, log(1/epsilon), with no spectral gap or angle promise.

Owned tags and a triangular graph map M yield a selected exact-kernel range
on a logical consistency space D. Logical QLLL dimension bounds control the
RANK of each connected tag component independently of graph amplitudes.

The central comparison lemma is elementary but decisive: if M,N are
injective on the SAME r-dimensional domain, then

 D_tr(unif RanM,unif RanN)<=rank(M-N)/r.

Proof: their images share dimension at least r-rank(M-N); remove this common
intersection and bound the remaining projector trace norms. There is no
inverse Gram or singular-angle factor.

R24 applies this to connected-polymer truncation and nested logical kernels:
uniform Ran(M_L|D_L) lies within delta_tag(L)+delta_cons(L) of an exact
selected common-kernel uniform state. Both tails decay geometrically at
sufficiently small fixed p,tag fraction. L=O(log(n/epsilon)) suffices.

This is an approximation theorem about uniform RANGES, not the state produced
by applying M_L to uniform logical input. Preparing that range and resolving
the logical constraints remain open. Dense maps on L sites cost d^O(L), which
is quasipolynomial in variable local dimension and violates the full target.
Five exact rational diagnostics check the stated finite triangular examples;
they are not evidence for a general preparation theorem.
