COMPARISON-ONLY ACQUISITION OF HIDDEN TRIANGULAR BOOLEAN DECODERS
13 October 2026. Internally proved and independently checked in this campaign.
Scientific novelty UNKNOWN. No civilization-enabling breakthrough established.

A comparison usually reveals much less than a numerical rank. For the class
below, translation pairs expose one entire coordinate function. Selecting the
most significant exposed coordinate before interpolating it makes full decoder
acquisition polynomial and avoids both rank enumeration and rare-prefix rejection.

Theorem and interface

Fix n>=2 and 1<=d<=n. Let u=Lx+c be an unknown invertible affine transformation
of F_2^n. Suppose the hidden rank, written most significant coordinate first, is

  Q(x)=(q_1(x),...,q_n(x)),
  q_i(x)=u_i+g_i(u_1,...,u_(i-1)),  deg(g_i)<=d.

The input includes n, d, this class promise, and exact chosen-pair access to
  C(x,y)=1[Q(x)<lex Q(y)].
The learner does not receive L, c, ranks, bit labels, g_i, target code, fiber
samplers, or translation directions. All random inputs below are fresh uniform
vectors. Addition is over F_2. Put

  M_d=sum_(j=0)^d binom(n,j), P=binom(n,2).

There is a Las Vegas learner acquiring an exact compact representation of
every q_i in its actual significance order (raw ANF expansion is separately
computable without further comparisons), with expected comparison count at most
  D+6P,  D=sum_(j=0)^d binom(n,j+1).
It terminates almost surely under the promise. With a cap
  T=ceil(log_2(P/beta)), 0<beta<1,
on the matching probes of each tournament pair, it instead returns either an
exact decoder or UNKNOWN. The probability of UNKNOWN is at most beta; its
worst comparison count is at most D+(2T+2)P. It never substitutes an
estimated coordinate on a capped failure. For fixed d>=2, this is O(n^(d+1))
comparisons, including the capped logarithmic term. In particular d=2 gives
expected O(n^3) and worst O(n^3+n^2 log(1/beta)) comparisons.

The acquired representation also provides an exact inverse rank map and an
exact uniform sampler for any requested rank prefix, without further queries.
Learner algebra, source rank generation, transmission, storage, checking and
randomness are additional charged resources, described below.

Proof

1. Translation exposes an actual coordinate.

For v!=0, let delta=L v and let j(v) be its first nonzero coordinate. Earlier
u_i are unchanged by x->x+v, so earlier q_i are unchanged, while q_j flips.
Consequently the first rank coordinate on which Q(x),Q(x+v) differ is j(v),
independently of x, and
  f_v(x):=1+C(x,x+v)=q_(j(v))(x).
Here 1+C means Boolean complementation. Thus each chosen translation supplies
exact labels of one actual degree-at-most-d rank coordinate using one comparison.

2. Select the next coordinate with a short randomized tournament.

Suppose q_1,...,q_k are known. Maintain a basis of
  S_k={v:(Lv)_1=...=(Lv)_k=0}.
Initially S_0=F_2^n. A basis of S_k contains a vector with (Lv)_(k+1)=1,
since this is a nonzero linear functional on S_k. Its exposed coordinates
therefore include q_(k+1), and no exposed coordinate is earlier.

To compare the priorities of two directions v,w in this basis, draw uniform x
and query f_v(x),f_w(x), using two source comparisons. Repeat until the values
agree, say at a. If the two exposed coordinates are distinct, their values are
jointly uniform on F_2^2, because Q is bijective. The match probability is
exactly 1/2. If they are the same coordinate, it is one.

At a match, if j(v)<j(w), the earlier differing rank coordinate of x+v,x+w
has values 1-a,a, so C(x+v,x+w)=a. If j(w)<j(v), it is 1-a. If the two
indices coincide, either direction remains a valid representative. One more
comparison thus selects a direction of the earlier coordinate, with harmless
ties. A tournament on the n-k basis directions returns a witness for q_(k+1).

There are exactly P pair contests over all stages. Each costs at most five
comparisons in expectation: at most two match attempts on average, with two
comparisons each, followed by one priority comparison. Conditional on any past
transcript the same bound holds. A cap of T has conditional failure probability
at most 2^-T per contest. A union bound gives P 2^-T<=beta. No erroneous
priority decision is made at any completed match under the class promise.

3. Interpolate the winner on the acquired prefix and update the subspace.

Let v_1,...,v_k be the earlier winning translations and let W_k be their
span. They are independent and W_k intersects S_k only at zero: the first
active coefficient in any nonzero combination toggles its corresponding
already acquired bit, while every later winning direction preserves that bit.
Thus W_k is a direct complement to S_k.

Write h_k(a)=q_(k+1)(sum_(i<=k) a_i v_i). This has degree at most d in k
variables. Query the winning translation only on the M_d(k)=sum_(j=0)^d
binom(k,j) low-weight inputs a, interpreted as raw inputs in W_k. Mobius
inversion recovers every coefficient of h_k. Its value at zero is q_(k+1)(0).

For any v in S_k, all earlier hidden u coordinates remain fixed, so
  q_(k+1)(x+v)+q_(k+1)(x)=(Lv)_(k+1), for every x.
In particular, ell(v)=q_(k+1)(v)+q_(k+1)(0) is a linear functional
on S_k. Its value on the winner is already one, so acquire it at all OTHER
basis directions using n-k-1 additional comparisons to the winning translation.
The kernel of this row supplies S_(k+1), with dimension n-k-1. No global
polar form or nonlinear equation solver is needed.

Over all stages, interpolation uses exactly
  sum_(k=0)^(n-1) M_d(k)=D
comparisons, response rows use exactly P, and tournaments use at most 5P in
expectation. The capped maximum is D+(2T+2)P, with UNKNOWN probability at most
P*2^-T. This proves the stated comparison bounds.

4. Compile inverse evaluation and prefix sampling.

The final winning directions form a basis V. Every later direction v_j,
j>i, belongs to S_i and therefore preserves q_i globally, while v_i flips it.
Consequently in the ACQUIRED coordinates t=V^-1 x,
  q_i(Vt)=t_i+h_(i-1)(t_1,...,t_(i-1)).
The stored model is V and these prefix polynomials, not a supplied target
encoding. Evaluating all bits gives the complete rank. For an inverse rank,
solve the bits t_i sequentially and output Vt. To sample a prefix of length k,
choose the n-k suffix bits of t independently and uniformly, solve its prefix
bits sequentially, and output Vt. This is a bijection from suffix seeds to the
requested 2^(n-k)-point fiber. It uses exactly n-k random seed bits and zero
additional comparisons. No rejection or hidden prefix table is used.

Quadratic complete flags and the exact boundary

For d=2, write B_i(x,v)=q_i(x+v)+q_i(x)+q_i(v)+q_i(0). The complete flag
condition is that there exist v_1,...,v_n with
  B_i(.,v_j)=0 and q_i(v_j)+q_i(0)=1[i=j], for all i<=j.
These directions are independent: in a nontrivial zero combination use its
least active index i and the response of q_i. All active directions lie in
rad B_i; only v_i contributes one, a contradiction. In the affine coordinates
dual to this basis, q_i has no quadratic monomial involving indices j>=i,
has coefficient one on u_i, and has no later linear coefficient. Hence it
has exactly the triangular form above. Conversely that form supplies the flag.

A partial flag is insufficient. The bijection
  Q=(x0+x2*x3, x1+x2*x3, x2, x3)
has valid initial flag directions e0,e1. But for v=e2 the comparison-extracted
function is x2+x0*x3, not any output coordinate. Thus the theorem does not
extend merely because some initial fibers can be sampled. A separate three-bit
quadratic permutation in triangular_translation.py supplies another explicit
outside-class witness. Polynomial interpolation or local checks alone cannot
certify that an arbitrary comparison oracle belongs to the promised class.

Information lower bound

Even with L=I,c=0 supplied, the freely chosen triangular polynomials give
  2^D distinct total orders,
  D=sum_(i=1)^n sum_(j=0)^d binom(i-1,j)
   =sum_(j=0)^d binom(n,j+1).
They give distinct permutations, and a permutation is uniquely determined by
its total order ranks. An exact binary-answer identification tree therefore
has worst depth at least D. The same average lower bound holds for an
almost-sure exact Las Vegas learner on the uniform class. A capped learner
with UNKNOWN is a different guarantee; its information bound must charge the
erasure probability, rather than transferring D unchanged.

For fixed d>=2, the expected upper/lower ratio tends to one: 6P=o(D).
For fixed beta the capped worst count is also D+o(D). In fact, a zero-semantic-
error capped learner with per-instance success at least 1-beta has worst-query
count at least D+log_2(1-beta): for some fixed random seed it completes on at
least (1-beta)*2^D distinct targets, each needing a distinct completed leaf.
The 6P term is leading when d=1, so the stated expected upper/lower ratio
there is seven, not one. The inherited tailored
affine learner is the stronger baseline there. This is a query lower bound,
not an optimality theorem for time, memory, output representation or noisy access.

Resource accounting

Each comparison transmits two n-bit arguments and returns one bit: 2nQ input
bits and Q returned bits. At most nTP random input bits are drawn by the capped
tournament. Canonical prefix coefficients require D bits; the acquired basis requires
n^2 bits. Caching its inverse adds n^2 bits. Actual interpreter/dictionary/JSON storage is larger.

Interpolation takes O(2^d D) elementary field operations in total.
Subspace kernels, polynomial evaluation and retained-basis algebra are charged
separately; for fixed d the straightforward implementation is polynomial,
but the comparison bound does not make all bit arithmetic free.

If a source rank evaluation costs C_Q(n), uncached source work is at most
2Q C_Q(n), plus argument, comparison and source setup cost. The executed dense
quadratic teacher has a conservative O(n^3) bit-work bound per fresh rank.
Its lazy memoization charges U such evaluations, U<=min(2Q,2^n), plus O(nQ)
bit work for keys/rank comparisons; its logical key+rank payload is 2nU bits.
Python allocator, dictionary and peak-RAM overheads were not profiled. No target
truth is accessed by the learner; coefficient/rank validation runs after return.
Source setup, acquisition and validation wall times are recorded separately.

Evidence and prior-method boundaries

The current optimized executable is triangular_compact.py. Six preselected
quadratic cases, on the SAME teachers/seeds as the earlier full-raw variant,
acquired all actual raw coordinate polynomials exactly at n=8,12,16,24,32,64.
The n=32 case used 8,084 comparisons; n=64 used 53,746. Source-free inverse
and prefix execution passed. Learned models contain only acquired V and h_i.
The earlier full-raw variant and its nine cases are preserved separately.

The separate independent compact implementation checked 18 cases: ten cubic
cases n=4..8 and eight quartic cases n=5..8, with every rank, inverse target,
output-prefix target and free-suffix assignment checked. A forced five-miss
cap returned UNKNOWN without a guess. Its proof and receipts are in
luna08_ordinal_challenge/COMPACT_V3_RESULT.txt and compact_tournament_audit.py/json.
Previously completed, preserved variant checks cover 33 quadratic cases through
n=12 and 18 cubic/15 quartic cases through n=9. PRNG finite checks support
implementations, not independent entropy certification.

Triangular polynomial permutations are classical; see Maubach,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.5800 . Exact one-bit lexicographic preference
elicitation is also prior, e.g. Booth et al. 2009,
