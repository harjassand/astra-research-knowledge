## Exact obstruction: endpoint sums lose both topology and history

Represent a dislocation network in a volume by its Burgers-labelled line current

\[
\alpha(x)=\sum_j b_j\otimes t_j\,\delta_{\Gamma_j}(x),
\]

where \(b_j\) is the signed Burgers vector, \(t_j\) is the oriented line tangent, and \(\delta_{\Gamma_j}\) is line measure. With a fixed orientation convention, the Burgers circuit around an oriented surface \(S\) reads

\[
B(S)=\oint_{\partial S}\beta^p\,dx=\int_S\alpha n\,dA
    =\sum_{p\in S\cap\Gamma_j}\!\operatorname{sgn}(p,S,\Gamma_j)b_j.
\]

This is a **signed** intersection count. Two opposite lines crossing the measurement surface contribute \(+b-b=0\), and a closed line can cross a spanning surface with cancelling signs. Thus zero circuit closure is necessary for the absence of a net enclosed Burgers charge, but it is not a bound on unsigned line length, fault area, or the topology of a network. At a true junction, the oriented incident Burgers vectors must sum to zero; measuring only their total over a large surface does not identify which junction or branches produced it.

An exact partial-reaction example is

\[
b_1=\tfrac16[1,-2,1],\qquad b_2=\tfrac16[2,-1,-1],\qquad
b_1+b_2=\tfrac12[1,-1,0]\ne0.
\]

The two partials may approach, removing their intervening fault ribbon, while leaving the perfect dislocation with Burgers vector \(b_1+b_2\). Consequently, *fault-contrast disappearance is not line removal*. This exact rational identity is rechecked in [exact_checks.py](exact_checks.py) and [exact_checks.json](exact_checks.json); it is also preserved as N1 in the supplied physical-operation ledger.

A second exact counterexample isolates plastic history. Take two parallel, opposite-Burgers lines \(+b\) and \(-b\), both length \(L\), on the same slip plane, separated by glide distance \(d\). Their signed net Burgers vector is zero both before and after they annihilate. The sweep area is \(Ld\). For specimen volume \(V\) and slip-plane normal \(n\), the accumulated volume-averaged plastic distortion in the ideal parallel geometry is

\[
\Delta\beta^p=\frac{Ld}{V}\,b\otimes n,
\qquad \Delta\varepsilon^p=\operatorname{sym}(\Delta\beta^p).
\]

The pair is gone at the endpoint, but its plastic shear need not be. If the same opposite pair exits at free surfaces, the endpoint can again have zero internal net Burgers content while retaining a surface step and the same swept-area contribution. Endpoint elastic strain can relax; it is not a measurement of the accumulated plastic distortion.

These examples give three distinct checks: signed Burgers balance, unsigned residual line/fault content, and path-integrated slip. None substitutes for the other two.
