## Precise remaining step

For the left-\(SO(2)\) class, prove or refute:

> For every periodic or zero-boundary \(\varphi\in W^{1,4}(\Omega;\mathbb R^2)\) and every constant \(A_0\), the nine moments \(m_i=\fint_\Omega f_i(A_0+D\varphi)-f_i(A_0)\) admit a PSD completion \(Y\) satisfying the 15 linear equations above.

This is the exact whole-profile lemma that would combine covered and internally resonant sectors without requiring each correlated component to have nonnegative gap. For the full 35-coefficient class, an analogous certificate or a different global coercive residual is still needed. A profile decomposition route must preserve affine boundary data, determinant balance, and the entire \(B\)-dependent gap with an error controlled by the total fourth moment. No such decomposition was proved here. The contact-specific reduced-Schur estimate from N806 remains useful near a contact but is insufficient for large transverse mass.
