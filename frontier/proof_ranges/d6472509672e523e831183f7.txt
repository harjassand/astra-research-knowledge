12 Scope and verification status
The completed reduction is an exact qualitative test. It does not imply noncomputability of
arbitrary-precision numerical capacity approximation or of a fixed-gap promise problem. The
positive-capacity witness can occur at an unknown, arbitrarily large blocklength; no computable
witness bound, practical code construction, or polynomial-time reduction is claimed. The
zero-rate helper code transports a fixed finite logical system with vanishing error while using
increasingly many helper qubits. It does not transmit quantum information at positive rate
through the half-erasure channel alone.
The new completion applies to the supplieduniformly gappedsource family. It is not a
compiler for every arbitrary ungapped POVM pair. All actual reference quantifiers in its
soundness proof are unrestricted and finite; no equal-reference-marginal restriction, scalar-
denominator replacement, realification, or state condition-number assumption is used.
This is a research proof draft. Its components and assembled reduction have received
internal analytic checks; external review remains pending. The classification relies on the
explicitly supplied companion source theorem and complete-order equivalence, together with
the complementary completion and helper proofs given here. No author attribution, peer-review
claim, or historical originality claim is made.
