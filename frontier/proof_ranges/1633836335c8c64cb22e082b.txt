# Autonomous quantum memory reconstruction and further theorem candidates

16 October 2026, Australia Brisbane.

The specified time-independent 4D sweep-memory argument survived a fresh
adversarial internal reconstruction. No invalid implication was found in the
canonical proof under its exact initialization, noise, correction and recovery
assumptions and the named published LSS and HT imports. The inspected primary
predecessors do not already prove that conjunction. External mathematical
correctness and historical originality remain unestablished, so a historically
significant discovery cannot yet be declared established.

A stronger modified construction emerged from the audit: complete the sweep
updates into local CPTP proposal maps and add a weak local depolarizing floor.
The written derivation gives a fixed local-generator tolerance for coherent
Hamiltonian errors and general local dissipative errors. Its additional steps
survived a separate direct quantum reconstruction in
[CPTP_REGULARIZATION_AUDIT.md](trajectory/CPTP_REGULARIZATION_AUDIT.md).
It is a separate conditional theorem candidate; it does not enlarge the
verdict for the original generator.

## Exact original theorem

There exist a fixed integer b and positive epsilon,A,c, independent of storage
time and size, such that for sufficiently large M and L=bM the following holds.
Use the periodic cubical 4D toric code, with 6L^4 data qubits, six logical
qubits, distance L^2 and cell Hilbert dimension 64. Start in any code-space
logical state, including arbitrary external reference entanglement.

At every primal and dual vertex, use the specified rate-kappa local sweep
correction: no incoming syndrome edges, a nonempty even outgoing set, fixed
pairing, and its one/two positively based face flips. The exact engineered
generator has seven active syndrome-conditioned jump patterns per vertex per
sector, 14L^4 correction terms and at most 24-qubit support per correction.
Add independent single-face X,Y,Z faults at fixed rates lambda_P, with

    max(lambda_X+lambda_Y,lambda_Z+lambda_Y) <= epsilon*kappa.

One fixed ideal final syndrome decoder satisfies, for every T>=0,

    || Dec_L exp(T L_total) Enc_L - Id ||_diamond
       <= min(2, A L^4 (1+kappa*T) exp(-c*L)).

The norm is unhalved. The proof also bounds any earlier change of decoded
logical homology. The scale is exponential in linear size L, equivalently
exp(O(n^(1/4))) in physical qubit count. Perfect encoding and readout are
assumptions; the block grid, envelopes and time slabs are analytical objects.

The concise reconstructed proof is
[PRINCIPAL_RECONSTRUCTION.md](lead/PRINCIPAL_RECONSTRUCTION.md). The unchanged
original theorem and full proof inputs are identified by SOURCE_SNAPSHOT.json
and included in the review archive under inherited/.

## New mathematical consequences

For the original generator, the proof extends to bounded-support Pauli-string
faults Q f(S), with scalar local stabilizer functions and a small sum of
maximum rates per cell. Every independent proposal becomes a conservative
envelope birth, so cross-sector acceptance need not leave separate marginal
Markov chains. This covers restricted false correction activations and
syndrome-diagonal calibration errors. The two separate extension audits are
trajectory/STRUCTURED_FAULT_EXTENSION.md and envelope/PAULI_PROPOSAL_EXTENSION.md.

The stronger modified construction goes beyond this positive-trajectory class.
Replace each vertex corrector by its completed CPTP channel C_v, with active
Kraus Q_O Pi_O and an inactive projector, or full inactive-pattern splitting.
Its time-independent generator is kappa(C_v-Id). Add weak complete
depolarization D_a on fixed local patch types, at rates nu_a. For any local
Hermiticity-preserving trace-annihilating perturbation E_(x,a), require

    ||E_(x,a)||_diamond <= nu_a/d_a^2,
    sum_a nu_a <= a sufficiently small fixed multiple of kappa,

where d_a is patch Hilbert dimension. The normalized Choi matrix of D_a is
I/d_a^2, so Psi_(x,a)=D_a+E_(x,a)/nu_a is CPTP. Exactly,

    nu_a(D_a-Id)+E_(x,a) = nu_a(Psi_(x,a)-Id).

This represents the perturbed generator as state-independent local Poisson
channel proposals. On every good clock/location schedule, expand arbitrary
fault Kraus operators in local Paulis. Each surviving recovered Kraus operator
is scalar*Id because the same support envelope certifies every Pauli history.
Trace preservation fixes the total weight, avoiding any absolute-coefficient
growth with time or volume. The same exponential lifetime estimate follows.

The result would give a fixed local-norm neighborhood tolerating Hamiltonian
drift, non-Pauli dissipation and changes of the correction jumps, uniformly in
L and T. It explicitly changes the baseline generator, adds real dephasing
and depolarization terms, and may enlarge constant jump support. It supplies
no such neighborhood for the original generator. Complete derivations are
[REGULARIZED_ROBUST_MEMORY.md](lead/REGULARIZED_ROBUST_MEMORY.md) and
[ROBUST_REGULARIZED_SWEEP_THEOREM.md](coherent_frontier/ROBUST_REGULARIZED_SWEEP_THEOREM.md).

The reusable operation is a sufficient support-certificate transfer: universal
local-Pauli recovery on good proposal schedules gives arbitrary local channel
recovery by Kraus linearity; a positive Choi interior converts local generator
errors into those proposals. Classical eroder stability alone is insufficient:
the model still needs the support envelope and accumulated logical correctness.
Kraus linearity and Choi positivity themselves are established mathematics.

The focused [priority addendum](literature/robust_addendum/ROBUST_REGULARIZATION_PRIORITY_ADDENDUM.md)

## Physical implementation and quantitative limits

Neither construction provides a useful numerical threshold. The displayed
conservative original recipe already has R=17360, k=69444 and a sufficient
coarse size margin of about 8.89*10^7. These are loose proof margins, not lower
bounds on useful devices. The regularized construction's tolerance additionally
contains the inverse square of a fixed patch Hilbert dimension and can be
extremely small. The universal radius-one patch already contains
6*(2*1+1)^4=486 qubits, so that version's tolerance is at most beta*kappa*2^(-972).
Smaller specified patch catalogs can improve this dimension factor. A full-patch
floor cannot be replaced by independent single-site noise under the same Choi
interior argument. No finite-device performance is inferred from existence.

An injective bounded-density placement of a graph with 4D ball growth in
ordinary 3D cannot keep physical edge length constant: packing a radius-r graph
ball gives c*r^4<=C*rho*(r*ell)^3, hence ell=Omega(L^(1/3)) at fixed density.
Synthetic dimensions or mediated interactions change the resources. The
supplied graph therefore does not automatically supply a credible device.

Conditioned reservoirs, the added floor, preparation accuracy, energy and
entropy removal, reset, calibration, readout noise and recovery latency are
unacquired. Mathematical local-norm robustness is a stronger theoretical
property than exact-model protection, while physical feasibility remains a
separate unanswered question. See lead/PHYSICAL_AND_THRESHOLD_BOUNDARY.md.

## Protected independent invention and decision

One investigator independently selected exact permanent computation using
commuting square-zero exclusion, before literature or Astra capital intake.
The exact representation is known zeon algebra. The proposed linear row-state
compression requires at least binomial(n,k) dimensions; finite-group filters
need 2^(n-1) elements, with the optimum already established. Treating exact
Gaussian norms as cheap primitives hides a permanent oracle. The determinant
also has the row-state bound, so this is not a general complexity separation.
The operation was stopped after comparison with Ryser/Glynn and current
primary sources. No independent foundational breakthrough was found.

Seven distinct Sol Max investigators were used, with no Luna workers and no
nested agents. Five initial workers attacked the proof and literature, one
protected independent invention, and the seventh was retained until the core
argument survived, then assigned the coherent-noise frontier. Follow-up reviews
reused those same investigators. The thirteen-worker ceiling was not a target.

The strongest next decision is external adversarial mathematical review of
the original reconstruction and the regularized extension, with a separate
priority review. The new robustness mechanism gives a substantive reason to
retain the programme. After that review, the consequential mathematical target
is a useful quantitative bound or removal of the artificial floor/completion
while retaining fixed local-generator robustness. A comparable construction
with physically three-dimensional bounded resources would be a further major
target, requiring a new mechanism rather than an embedding assertion.
