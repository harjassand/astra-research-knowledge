## 7. Finite computation with a proved nonlocal error

The exact current is not finite range, as required by the previous box-closure no-go theorem. Nevertheless it has finite-radius legal approximations with a computable tail.

Define beta_R(z)=beta(z) when C_z lies entirely within radius R of z, and zero otherwise. This decision can be made by exploring the component up to the boundary of the radius-R window, with a finite guard layer. An unresolved component need not be explored further.

Let k_R be the rotated gradient of z_2+beta_R, multiplied by the open-edge indicator. Multiplication is essential: truncation decisions at the two dual endpoints can differ. The truncated current remains legal but in general is **not** conserved.

Put

\[
T_R(b)\ge\mathbb E[R_z^2\mathbf1\{R_z>R\}].
\]

An explicit rational choice is T_0=m_2(b) and, for R>=1,

\[
T_R=16b^2u^TA^{R-1}
\left[(R+1)^2I+(2R+3)AS+2A^2S^2\right]u,
\quad S=(I-A)^{-1}. \tag{23}
\]

This follows by summing the tail identity

\[
\mathbb E[R_z^2\mathbf1_{R_z>R}]
=(R+1)^2\mathbb P(R_z\ge R+1)
+\sum_{r\ge R+2}(2r-1)\mathbb P(R_z\ge r)
\]

against (11). Only a two by two rational matrix inverse and power are required.

Stationarity and open-edge clipping give

\[
\mathbb E|k-k_R|^2\le8T_R. \tag{24}
\]

Lift k_R using the same forced-good operation to obtain J_R. The exact same finite module-overlap calculation now proves

\[
\boxed{\|J-J_R\|^2\le\frac{16\chi C(p)}{L^2}T_R(b).} \tag{25}
\]

Thus the completion error is an output of the construction, not an input oracle.

For the normalized current F=LJ, which has response 2chi, the checker finds the following sufficient cutoffs for ||F-F_R|| <= 10^-6:

| p | Sufficient coarse radius R |
|---:|---:|
| 0.10 | 8 |
| 0.20 | 18 |
| 0.25 | 27 |
| 0.30 | 46 |
| 1/3 | 74 |
| 0.35 | 101 |
| 0.40 | 1280 |

The deterioration near the explicit counting threshold is real. These are rigorous ensemble L2 error statements, not pointwise worst-case error bounds on arbitrary configurations.

If a finite calculation evaluates ||J_R||, a sharper valid certificate follows from the known full-current response:

\[
D(p)\ge\frac{(2\chi/L)^2}
{2\chi\left(\|J_R\|+4\sqrt{\chi C(p)T_R}/L\right)^2}. \tag{26}
\]

A cheaper alternative evaluates only E|k_R| squared and bounds K by
(sqrt(E|k_R| squared)+sqrt(8T_R)) squared. Either can tighten the energy of this particular witness. Neither proves that the resulting lower hierarchy converges to the true diffusion coefficient. The ordinary local primal hierarchy remains available for upper bounds.
