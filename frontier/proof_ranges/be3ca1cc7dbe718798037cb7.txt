\section{Rare-event acquisition: exact kinetics can remain hidden}

The proposed scientific-computing capability was trustworthy pathway and rate
acceleration without a supplied route, committor or transition-state map.
Automatic basin boosts overlap
\href{https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.78.3908}{Voter's hyperdynamics};
corrected learned proposals already occur in FlowRES. The missing general
operation is not another proposal density, but acquisition of an important
unseen kinetic gate. The following finite-state construction makes that boundary
exact. It is not a universal lower bound on atomistic simulation.

\subsection{Exact oracle contract and construction}

Let $M=2^d$, with known states $A,B,u_j,v_j$ for $j\in\{0,1\}^d$.
The nonzero off-diagonal rates are
\[
 q(A,u_j)=q(B,v_j)=1/M,\quad q(u_j,A)=q(v_j,B)=1,\quad
 q(u_j,v_j)=q(v_j,u_j)=\theta_j>0.
\]
Diagonals make row sums zero. Topology, endpoints and all displayed known
rates are supplied. An adaptive query at any chosen $j$ reveals exact
$\theta_j$, hence both unknown local rows, at unit cost.
Exact sampling of the known equilibrium is free. Arithmetic and randomness
may even be free for the lower bound. Listing $M$ successors costs $M$ outputs
and reveals no $\theta_j$.

Detailed balance gives the same law for every parameter vector:
\[
 \pi(A)=\pi(B)=1/4,\qquad \pi(u_j)=\pi(v_j)=1/(4M).
\]
Write $f(x)=x/(1+2x)$ and $S=\sum_j f(\theta_j)$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact route and clock]\label{thm:corridor}
The committor with boundary values $h(A)=0,h(B)=1$, stationary reactive flux,
successful-excursion route law and mean passage time are
\[
 h(u_j)=f(\theta_j),\quad
 h(v_j)=\frac{1+\theta_j}{1+2\theta_j},\quad
 \operatorname{cap}(A,B)=\frac{S}{4M},\quad
 w_j=\frac{f(\theta_j)}{S},\quad \E_A\tau_B=\frac{2M}{S}.
\]
For $s\ge0$, writing
$b(s)=M^{-1}\sum_j\theta_j/(s+1+2\theta_j)$,
\[
 \E_Ae^{-s\tau_B}=\frac{b(s)}{s(s+2)+b(s)}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The two interior harmonic equations are
$(1+\theta)h(u)=\theta h(v)$ and
$(1+\theta)h(v)=1+\theta h(u)$; solve them and sum equilibrium successful
fluxes from $A$ to get the capacity and route law.
Each attempt consists of an $A$ holding time of mean one and an interior
excursion of mean one: the total exit hazard from $\{u_j,v_j\}$ to
$\{A,B\}$ is identically one. Its success probability averaged over the
uniformly chosen corridor is $S/M$. Renewal gives $T=2+(1-S/M)T$.
For the transform $F_x$, the backward equations give
\[
 (s+1)F_A=M^{-1}\sum_jF_{u_j},\quad
 F_{u_j}=\frac{(s+1+\theta_j)F_A+\theta_j}{(s+1)(s+1+2\theta_j)}.
\]
Substitution yields the displayed transform; its value at zero is one.
\end{proof}

Set $\varepsilon=M^{-2}$. In model 0 every $\theta_j=\varepsilon$;
in model $J$ one hidden $\theta_J=1$. Then
\[
 T_0=2(M^2+2),\qquad
 T_J=\frac{6M(M^2+2)}{M^2+3M-1},\qquad
 w_J=\frac{M^2+2}{M^2+3M-1}\ge1-\frac3M.
\]
Equilibrium is identical while the mean changes from approximately $2M^2$
to $6M$ and the unseen corridor dominates reactive flux.

\begin{theorem}[Acquisition lower bound]\label{thm:query}
An algorithm making at most $q$ adaptive exact local-rate queries has
transcript total-variation distance at most $q/M$ between model 0 and a
uniformly hidden model $J$. Hence:
\begin{itemize}
\item For $M\ge3$, mean estimation within 25\% with failure probability at
most $\alpha$ on every model requires $q\ge(1-2\alpha)M$.
\item A route sampler with TV error at most $\delta$ on every hidden model
requires $q\ge M(w_J-\delta)-1$.
\item Full passage-law sampling with TV error at most $\delta$ on every model
requires $q/M\ge1/2-12/M-2\delta$. Thus $M\ge96$, $\delta=1/16$ imply
$q\ge M/4$.
\end{itemize}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Couple random bits and known samples. Until the hidden label is queried,
transcripts agree. A uniformly hidden label lies among the null transcript's
$q$ inspected labels with probability at most $q/M$.
The 25\%-relative intervals for $T_0,T_J$ are disjoint for $M\ge3$;
successful estimation therefore distinguishes with total error at most
$2\alpha$. For route sampling the chance of outputting the hidden label is
at most $(q+1)/M$, even with one final unqueried guess.
For the full clock, a central crossing under model 0 has instantaneous rate
at most $M^{-2}$, so $P_0(\tau_B\le12M)\le12/M$.
Since $T_J\le6M$, Markov's inequality gives
$P_J(\tau_B\le12M)\ge1/2$. Triangle inequality for the two sampling errors
and data processing yield the asserted bound.
\end{proof}

The equally informed baseline queries all $M$ gates and computes these
quantities in $O(M)$ arithmetic, matching acquisition order. This is exponential
in label length $d$, but linear in an explicitly listed graph's size.
Parameters require $O(d)$ bits. The lower bound grants exact rates, free
randomness and unlimited computation; precision improvements cannot evade it.
It says nothing against additional symbolic structure that identifies a gate
or globally certifies a restricted effective response.

Finite low-order fits, held-out force agreement and endpoint equilibrium do
not certify absence of an uninspected spike. Sparse microscopic forces need
not imply a sparse committor or eliminated kinetic generator. A proof-carrying
factorization with a verified tractable elimination width is a stronger possible
interface, but no new acquisition mechanism supplying it was found.
The exact rational verifier independently solves generators for
$M=2,3,4,8,16,32$ in both model classes and checks committors, capacity,
mean times and three transform values. These finite checks corroborate
implementation; the all-$M$ argument is the proof above.
