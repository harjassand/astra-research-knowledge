## 9. Reproducible exact finite evidence

The supplied Python program performs exact core checks, optional numerical witness discovery, and separate rational certificate replay.

Command used:

    python astra_clock_lift_verify.py --search --trials 40 --scales 12 \
      --output astra_clock_lift_results.json

Seed: 20261012. The fresh, preserved run produced:

| Family | Covers checked | Scaling certificates |
|---|---:|---:|
| Standard six-line central pencil in F_5^2 | 1 | 64, all sign classes |
| Random greedy irredundant affine covers of F_5^2 | 40 | 598 |
| Random greedy irredundant central covers of F_5^3 | 39 | 619 |
| Total | 80, including the separate pencil | 1,281 |

One additional central cover had size 11 and was skipped by the configured size cap of 10. The affine size cap was 9. Small covers used all sign classes; larger covers used sampled scalings. Distinct rows in the table do not assert distinct isomorphism types or disjoint certificate sets.

All accepted coefficients were reconstructed as rational numbers and checked exactly against legal finite-field directions. An additional replay used only Python integers and fractions.Fraction:

    python astra_clock_lift_verify.py --replay astra_clock_lift_results.json

All 1,281 positive certificates passed this replay. No numerical LP infeasibility report was accepted as a proof. The five-coordinate counterexample was checked in all 32 sign classes and has the separate analytic proof above.

In the standard pencil, 24 of the 64 scaling classes had no full-support legal direction but did have an exact fractional balancing certificate. Thus balancing cannot be replaced indiscriminately by searching for a single fully supported move.

**Limitations:** these are small-field, nonexhaustive tests, not evidence of the required large-prime asymptotic theorem. In particular K_0>5, so the entropy inequality itself is numerically weaker than the trivial p^N bound over F_5; the tests concern feasibility of the mechanism, not a nontrivial asymptotic rank improvement. Internal exact replay is not independent expert review or a proof-assistant verification.
