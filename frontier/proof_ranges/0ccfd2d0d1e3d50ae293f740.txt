## 6. Common physical accounting

A complete description must include the target electronic/vibrational Hamiltonian, isotope-layer kinetic energies and force constants, Coulomb and photon coupling, elastic contact, dissipative reservoirs, sample temperature and the measurement probe. In an electromagnetic reduction, the same measured complex dielectric tensor defines the Green tensor, optical loss and equilibrium noise through fluctuation–dissipation [7]. One cannot choose a large field enhancement and an unrelated negligible linewidth/noise.

For example, the symmetrized electric-field covariance is proportional to

    (hbar mu_0/pi) integral_0^infinity omega^2
      coth[hbar omega/(2 k_B T)] Im G(omega) domega.

For differences between causal, statically matched environments, Kramers–Kronig similarly constrains spectral redistribution: assuming Delta r(infinity)=0,

    Delta r(0) = (2/pi) integral Im Delta r(omega) / omega domega.

A positive change in one spectral interval is not an unconstrained addition of fluctuations everywhere.

The target many-body response is NOT supplied or simulated. The decisive unknown is its response to the measured change of environment, after independently bounded contact, strain, charge, temperature and probe effects. Contact-transmitted acoustic changes remain an alternative retarded mechanism. A positive result alone would not uniquely establish an electromagnetic mechanism or identify pairing glue.

The assembly is passive while interrogating equilibrium. This does not imply zero total energy: enrichment/material production, fabrication, cryogenic refrigeration, optical qualification and probe operation all cost energy. Full laboratory wall-plug cost has not been measured. This is a measurement proposal, not an energy source or a demonstrated low-energy technology.
