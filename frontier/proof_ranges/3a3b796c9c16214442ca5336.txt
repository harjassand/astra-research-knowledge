SU3 WALL: AN ANALYTIC LOW-WINDOW JACOBI BOUND AND THE WEIGHTED COUNT
Date: 2026-10-08
Status: INTERNALLY-AUDITED-PROOF-CANDIDATE on the explicit CPV/source-map premise.

Main claim. For EVERY a>=b>=1,N=a+b,B=b+1 and d=dim V_(a,b), the
actual weighted operator L_w=L_T/N^2+L_S/(N sqrt(C_S)) satisfies

 Tr_HS 1_(0,t](L_w)
   <=100000 d[(N^2/B)t^2+N^3t^3], 0<t<=1/(100N).         (0.1)

This proves the previously conditional hypothesis H on its required
small window. It does NOT prove the stronger interpolation inequality
for every output p+q<=a. A narrower near-wall Jacobi theorem suffices;
away from the wall the ordinary Casimir count already has the needed
order. The proof retains the actual quartic second-tensor block.

1. EXACT SOURCE INPUT, LABELS AND ISOSPECTRAL JACOBI FORM

Use Tr_fund(t_A t_B)=delta_AB/2 and the previously derived quantities
C2,C3,alpha=C3/C2,S=(D-alpha T)/N,C_S=Delta/N^2.
On End(V_(a,b)) let the output highest weight be(p,q). The adjoint
action has triality zero, so p congruent q mod3. By taking Hermitian
adjoints, the(p,q) and(q,p) blocks have the same L_S spectrum. It is
enough to consider p<=q. Put

 delta=(q-p)/3, R=p+delta, Q=R+delta,
 c=C2(p,q)=R^2+Rdelta+delta^2+2R+delta,
 J_max=min(R,b).                                         (1.1)

For p+q<=a the exact LR/GT multiplicity interval has indices
j=delta,...,J_max. This is only a cardinality/basis index statement,
not a claimed eigenbasis for L_S. Empty intervals are omitted.

Here is the source multiplicity substitution explicitly. Its shifted
input weights are(a+1,b+1),(b+1,a+1),(p+1,q+1), so
ell=N+1-Q,n=N+1-R and

 xi=(N+1-Q,b+1,b+1-delta; b-Q,b-R,0).

Since R<=(p+q)<=a, its minimum first-three coordinate is b+1-delta;
its maximum last-three coordinate is b-J_max. Hence its source
multiplicity is J_max-delta+1. On the source indices
i=1,...,J_max-delta+1, the reversed coordinate is j=J_max-i+1,
equivalently i+xi_b=b-j+1. These facts account for every output in
the admitted range, including multiplicities truncated by b<R.

The Crampé--Poulain d'Andecy--Vinet primary tridiagonal matrices with
shifted labels(a+1,b+1),(b+1,a+1),(p+1,q+1) give the full L_S matrix
through the exact quartic map in ../MISSING_LABEL_MAPPING.txt. Source:
https://arxiv.org/html/2110.03521v1, Sections1.1--1.3, equation(18).
Relabel its basis by j=b-m in reverse order. Define the EXACT parameter

 v=(2a+b+3)/3-2alpha
   =(2a+b+3)b(b+2)/(3C2)>0.                              (1.2)

The two off-diagonal entries of the ACTUAL L_S block between j,j+1 are

 A_j=(a+1-Q+j)(j+1)(j+1-delta)(N+Q+2-v-j)/N^2,
 C_j=(Q-j)(R-j)(b-j)(v+j+1)/N^2.                         (1.3)

Here K_(j+1,j)=A_j and K_(j,j+1)=C_j. For a nonempty low block on
the narrow range used below, every interior A_j,C_j is positive;
A_(delta-1)=0 and C_Jmax=0. Positive diagonal similarity with ratio
D_(j+1)/D_j=sqrt(A_j/C_j), applied as D^(-1)KD, makes the off-diagonal
entries sqrt(A_j C_j).
An alternating sign change then makes them NEGATIVE. Denote this
isospectral real symmetric Jacobi matrix by J_K. Similarity preserves
all eigenvalues. It need not be unitary in the CPV basis's physical
Gram metric, and no Loewner comparison is made in that unnormalized
basis. All subsequent quadratic forms concern J_K in Euclidean space.

The off-diagonal orientation can also be checked before symmetrizing.
At the edge j,j+1 the source edge parameter is i+xi_b=b-j,
lambda_-=(b-a)/3-Q-1,lambda_+=2(a+2b+3)/3. Its upper cubic factor
is minus(a+1-Q+j)(j+1)(j+1-delta), and its lower cubic factor is
(Q-j)(R-j)(b-j). Inserting these in -Y-2alpha X gives the two
positive rates(1.3) in the stated reversed order. The scalar part
of the quartic map changes only the diagonal.

2. EXACT NATURAL-GAUGE POTENTIAL IDENTITY

Let d_j be the diagonal of J_K and set

                   P_j=d_j-A_(j-1)-C_j.                 (2.1)

It is an exact potential, not a Gershgorin approximation. Set
z=(v-b)/2 and w=a+1-v. Direct substitution of the source diagonal
formula and the quartic map gives

 N^2 P_j=(delta+1)(delta+2)j^2+L_1 j+L_0,

 L_1=(delta+1)[(N+2)w-delta(delta+2)
                                      -R(2z+delta+1)],
 L_0=R^2 z(z+delta)+Rdelta[(z+b)^2+v]+Rdelta^2 z
             +R[2z^2+b(v+1)]+delta(delta+1)(z+b)(z+b+1). (2.2)

For an algebraic verification, the source power-sum variables on the
j-coordinate are
j+a+1/2-Q, j+1/2, j+1/2-delta,
j-Q-1/2, j-R-1/2, j-b-1/2,
and Lambda=N+Q+3. The source diagonal substitution cancels the cubic
and quartic j powers. Its quadratic coefficient is(delta+1)(delta+2).
The linear coefficient is exactly L_1. The checker first replaces the
quartic-map scalar term -(4/3)alpha C3 by -(4/3)alpha^2 C2. With that
replacement its constant equals L_0 plus(4/3)alpha(C3-alpha C2), which
vanishes by definition of alpha. Retaining the original scalar term
cancels that remainder identically. This states exactly where the
central relation enters rather than discarding a constant term.
These coefficient equalities establish(2.2) by polynomial expansion;
exact_potential.py reproduces the rational algebra, not a numerical fit.
In particular the second-adjoint Gram degeneration is not discarded.

3. NARROW NEAR-WALL QUADRATIC FORM -- THE LOAD-BEARING ESTIMATE

Assume

           epsilon=b/N<=1/100, c<=N/100.                  (3.1)

Then N>=100 and R<=sqrt(c)<=sqrt(N/100)<=N/100,
Q<=2R<=N/50. Thus p+q=2R+delta<=3R<=3N/100<a, so all source and

The formula(1.2), C2>=a^2/3 and b+2<=3b give

 v<=9b^2/N=9epsilon b,
 -b/2<=z<=-0.455b, z^2>=b^2/5,
 w=a+1-v>=0.98N.                                        (3.2)

For the first inequality, (2a+b+3)<=2N+3, N>=100 and a>=0.99N;
the resulting coefficient is below9. All displayed constants are
fixed before parameters. The source off-diagonal factors in(1.3)
are positive in the open interval because a-Q>=0.97N and v>0.

The identity

 delta(delta+2)+R(delta+1)<=c

follows since its subtraction from c is R^2+R-delta>=0. Also
2z=v-b<=0. It follows that

 L_1 >=(delta+1)[(N+2)w-c]
      >=(3/4)(delta+1)N^2.                               (3.3)

In L_0, only R^2 zdelta and Rdelta^2 z may be negative; all other
terms are nonnegative because v>0,z+b>0. Therefore

 L_0>=R^2 z^2-(b/2)delta R(R+delta)
     >=R^2 z^2-bdelta R^2.                               (3.4)

For any existing j>=delta, delta<= (delta+1)j. With u=bR/N,
bdelta R^2/N^2<=epsilon(c/N)delta<=0.0001(delta+1)j.
Equations(2.2)--(3.4), including the nonnegative quadratic term, give

                  P_j>=0.7(delta+1)j+0.2u^2.             (3.5)

This also holds at j=delta=0. For the downward factor in(1.3),

 A_(j-1)=(a-Q+j)j(j-delta)(N+Q+3-v-j)/N^2
         >=0.97*0.9891 j(j-delta)
         >=0.9j(j-delta).                               (3.6)

Use a>=0.99N,Q<=0.02N and v/N<=9epsilon^2<=0.0009,

 C_j<=(2R^2)b(v+j+1)/N^2
     <=18epsilon u^2+4epsilon(R^2/N)j

The restriction j>=1 in this last estimate matters: it uses j+1<=2j.
No positive lower bound is assigned to the soft j=0 slot.

Let x be supported on the j>=1 coordinate subspace of J_K. Edgewise

 2sqrt(A_j C_j)|x_j x_(j+1)|
      <=(A_j/2)|x_(j+1)|^2+2C_j|x_j|^2.

Summing the ACTUAL Jacobi quadratic form, using(2.1), yields

 <x,J_K x>>=sum_j[(1/2)A_(j-1)-C_j+P_j]|x_j|^2
          >=sum_j[0.45j(j-delta)
                         +0.7(delta+1)j-0.0004j]|x_j|^2

The positive u^2 remainder is dropped only after its coefficients

4. SORTED SPECTRUM AND THE SOFT SLOT

Write increasing physical eigenvalues of L_S as kappa_l, and let
j_l=delta+l-1. If delta>=1, (3.8) applies to the entire block, so
ordinary Hermitian min--max gives

                   kappa_l>=(2/5)j_l(j_l+1).             (4.1)

If delta=0, remove only its first j=0 coordinate. The tail has indices
1,...,J_max and satisfies(3.8). Cauchy interlacing yields
kappa_(l)>=tail_eigenvalue_(l-1)>=(2/5)(l-1)l for l>=2.
For l=1, positivity of the ACTUAL L_S gives kappa_1>=0. Thus(4.1)
holds on EVERY narrow near-wall block in(3.1), including truncated
nonstable blocks and singleton intervals. The scalar output can be
removed before counting. This is stronger than the proposed quadratic
comparison on this narrow range; it does not assert that comparison
on every output p+q<=a or in the regular interior.

5. COUNT ON THE THIN-WALL WINDOW

Let T=N^2 t<=N/100. A counted weighted mode lies in an output with
c<=T and satisfies

 kappa_l<=N sqrt(C_S)t=(sqrt(C_S)/N)T<=2(B/N)T.            (5.1)

The last inequality follows directly from the exact C_S expression,
C2>=N^2/4 and N>=2: C_S<=(5/2)b(b+2)<4B^2. Set beta=B/N.
For a positive j_l, (4.1),(5.1) imply j_l^2<=5beta T.
The j=0 slot, when present, counts as only one label.

For fixed j, there are j+1 choices of(delta,l) with j=delta+l-1.
Thus the number of possible label pairs is at most
10(1+beta T). For fixed delta, the Casimir ball p+q<=2sqrt(T) has
at most3sqrt(T) values of p<=q, when T>=3. Each representation has
dimension at most8T^(3/2). Doubling for p>=q gives

          Tr 1_(0,T/N^2](L_w)<=480 T^2(1+beta T)          (5.2)

when epsilon<=1/100. The scalar mode is excluded explicitly. Below
T=3 there is no nontrivial output because the adjoint is the least
nonzero root-lattice Casimir.

6. AWAY FROM THE WALL AND THE UNIFORM CONSTANT

If epsilon>=1/100, beta>=1/100. The same low window has
p+q<=2sqrt(T)<=a, so its exact multiplicity is at most min(p,q)+1.
There are at most9T output labels, each has dimension<=8T^(3/2),
and its multiplicity is at most3sqrt(T), for T>=3. Dropping the
positive L_S term now gives the SU3 ordinary count

 Tr 1_(0,T/N^2](L_w)<=216 T^3
                    <=21600 beta T^3.                   (6.1)

Equations(5.2),(6.1) are bounded by24000[T^2+beta T^3] uniformly
in ALL a>=b>=1 on0<T<=N/100. For small N where this interval lies
below3, its positive count is zero, so no extrapolated finite patch
is needed. Finally d>=BN^2/4 converts this to(0.1), even with constant
96000; we round upward to100000.
