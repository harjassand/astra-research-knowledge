4. NEWTON STEIN NORMAL FORM — SERIOUS OPEN SAMPLER CANDIDATE

File: r21_polylog_mean_compiler.txt, final normal-form sections.

R21 found an exact algebraic right inverse of the baseline linearized
Gaussian Stein operator by allowing a divergence-free skew gauge. For a
symmetric matrix R_m in homogeneous Gaussian chaos m, use row divergence
delta and D_ij w=partial_j w_i, and define

 s=delta delta R_m, phi=-s/(m+2)^2, u=grad phi,
 w=delta R_m+grad delta u,
 B=(Dw-Dw^*)/(m+2), J_u=-R_m-Du^*+B.

Then delta w=0, delta B=w, delta J_u=u, and POINTWISE

 sym(J_u+Du^*)=-R_m.

Root independently reconstructed these identities. For m=0 they reduce to
u=-R_m Z/2, the first covariance correction. Unlike that correction alone,
the gauge supplies a right inverse for every symmetric chaos residual.

The baseline inverse also satisfies genuine dimension- and degree-independent
Gaussian L2 bounds (matrix norm is Frobenius):

 ||Du||_2<=||R||_2,  ||J_u||_2<=(2+sqrt(2))||R||_2.

These follow from Gaussian creation/annihilation norms and the orthogonal
divergence-free projection of delta R. The full perturbed operator includes
K D_Gu, which is unbounded on ordinary Gaussian L2. Thus a small operator
norm for K does not justify a Neumann series in that norm.

The landmark opportunity is a positive Newton construction with genuinely
quadratic FULL-LAW residual contraction and constant recursive branching.
Depth O(log log(d/epsilon)) could then produce polylogarithmically many
queries. A dimension-free bounded inverse for the FULL linearization,
acquisition of its actions, summable positive implementation, and every-run
query cap remain UNPROVED. Formal cancellation is not that theorem.
