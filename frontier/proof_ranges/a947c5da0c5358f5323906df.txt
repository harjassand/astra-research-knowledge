# First gate: classical fast-forwarding of reversible dynamics

Candidate capability: given neighbor access to a reversible Markov kernel P and initial state x, sample P^t(x,.) or estimate (P^t f)(x) for arbitrary |f|<=1 with work polynomial in sqrt(t), not t. Explicit full-matrix algorithms and full-vector output are different access models and are not ruled out here.

Mechanism tested: replace t transitions by Chebyshev polynomials of degree O(sqrt(t log(1/epsilon))), then sample their signed endpoint measures.

## Exact obstruction to this mechanism
On the infinite d-regular tree (d>=3), let P=A/d. The coefficient of z^k in T_k(z) is 2^(k-1), for k>=1. At each vertex y at distance k from x, all lower powers vanish and P^k(x,y)=d^(-k). There are d(d-1)^(k-1) such vertices. Hence

sum_y |T_k(P)(x,y)| >= [2(d-1)/d]^(k-1).

This holds equally in a finite d-regular graph whose radius-k ball about x is a tree. Any single signed-particle representation W delta_Y with E[W 1(Y=y)]=T_k(P)(x,y) has E|W| >= the displayed norm and E W^2 >= its square. Spectral l2 contraction therefore does not imply usable l-infinity observable variance. This is not a general algorithmic lower bound.

A positive alternative on any d-regular graph is the universal-cover decomposition: delete immediate reversals in a simple random walk. Conditional on reduced length R_t=r, its endpoint is a nonbacktracking r-step walk. R is the birth-death chain 0->1 and, at positive radius, +1 with (d-1)/d and -1 with 1/d. Thus E R_t >= (d-2)t/d. This positive local representation still uses linear expected path length when d>=3. At d=2 it does recover diffusive compression, which is standard one-dimensional structure.

Separate and weaker locality observation: any positive law supported in the radius-r ball has TV error at least Pr(R_t>r) on the tree. This only covers bounded-radius local-output procedures, not explicit-state access or global jumps.

Primary prior art:
- Apers and Sarlette, Quantum Fast-Forwarding: Markov Chains and Graph Property Testing, arXiv:1804.02321. Quantum amplitude preparation and its normalization cost are not classical endpoint sampling. https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.02321
- Alon, Benjamini, Lubetzky, Sodin, Non-backtracking random walks mix faster, arXiv:math/0610550. Nonbacktracking/Chebyshev representation is established. https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0610550
- Biswas, Pyne, Rubinfeld, Local Access to Random Walks, ITCS 2022. Neighbor access with global labels, mixing bounds, and multiple time queries is stronger/different and can yield sublinear dependence on the queried horizon. https://drops.dagstuhl.de/opus/volltexte/2022/15620/pdf/LIPIcs-ITCS-2022-24.pdf

Decision: the proposed generic classical short-polynomial sampling mechanism fails its variance gate. No new sampling capability or general no-fast-forwarding theorem is claimed. Stop this route rather than extend a specialist negative result.
