## Proposed operation

Temporarily add a local positive quartic free-energy term by engaging an initially
unloaded elastic shunt coupled to electrostrictive strain. Apply a conjugate field
to traverse the now-convex free energy. Remove the shunt with compensating bias,
return the field to zero, and recover the shunt work. Original endpoint material
free energies are restored rather than permanently modified.
