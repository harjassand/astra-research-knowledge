## The concrete full-output scientific workload

[Barp, Barp, Briol, Ueltschi](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/75301/),
*A numerical study of the 3D random interchange and random loop models*,
J. Phys. A 48 (2015) 345002,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8113/48/34/345002),
[arXiv:1505.00983v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1505.00983v2), dated 2015-07-14.
Their u=1 model has independent rate-1 nearest-neighbor transpositions on a cube
at horizon beta. Their scientific outputs include the joint cycle/shadow-length
partition and its moments, with cubes L=80 and L=160; a displayed full-partition
sample uses beta=1. They report numerical critical parameters in [0.313,0.361]
across their model family. Their double-bar variants and quantum-spin ensembles
weighted by 2^(number of loops) are different measures.

The endpoint permutation determines all u=1 shadow lengths. Hence the original
all-permutation TV guarantee transfers by projection to the joint cycle partition,
macroscopic-cycle events, and every bounded cycle statistic. This is full
correlation information; a one-walker density mean does not determine it.

At rate 1, beta=O(1), and M<=3n edges, the exact Poisson-event incumbent needs
M beta expected swaps, one n-label array, and an O(n) cycle scan. It already
avoids n! states and performs work on the order of explicit output size. It has
zero mathematical approximation error under ideal arithmetic/randomness, and
[COSTS.md](COSTS.md) gives a constructive charged finite-bit variant. No measured
constant-factor win is claimed either way.

The cubic lattice branches, so the delivered path exterior-square reduction does
not apply. The generic killed-pair representation has binomial(n,2) states.
At L=160 even a single 8-byte pair vector requires roughly 67 TB; dense pair
preprocessing is still larger. There is no stiff rate hierarchy to eliminate.
[HYPOTHESIS_TRANSFER.md](HYPOTHESIS_TRANSFER.md) proves that full-cube shuffling
has TV error at least 7/13-25^3/L^3>.5079 throughout beta<=1, L>=80. The exact
arithmetic is saved in [STATIC_COST_RECEIPT.json](STATIC_COST_RECEIPT.json).

This identifies an actual scientific workload and gives it the strongest simple
same-information endpoint algorithm. It supplies no reason to run another
synthetic graded-rate benchmark or to claim physical diffusion acceleration.

## Quantum random-SWAP transfer

[Shi, Dong, Petersen, Johansson](https://people.kth.se/~kallej/papers/multiagent_ieeetac16quantum.pdf),
*Reaching a Quantum Consensus: Master Equations That Generate Symmetrization and
Synchronization*, IEEE TAC 61(2), 374-387, February 2016,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.2015.2434034), Eq. (4), supplies the exact
fixed-graph H=0 subcase G=sum_e alpha_e(Ad_SWAPe-I). Eq. (5) defines the uniform
permutation twirl as the asymptotic target. The paper also allows switching graphs
and Hamiltonians; those generalizations must not be imported into the fixed-rate
interchange contract without a new proof.

The random permutation mixture is established prior structure, adjacent to
[Mazzarella, Sarlette, Ticozzi](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.4077v1),
*Consensus for Quantum Networks: From Symmetry to Gossip Iterations*, v1 dated
2013-03-17, later IEEE TAC 60(1), 158-172 (2015),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.2014.2336351). No novelty is assigned to using
permutations to realize gossip or to uniform conditional block shuffles.

For any local dimension and arbitrary reference system,

    (1/2)||Phi_r-Phi_q||_diamond <= TV(p_r,p_q).

The complete proof and physical-model mismatch budget are in
[HYPOTHESIS_TRANSFER.md](HYPOTHESIS_TRANSFER.md). Thus an already valid supplied-
model pair certificate can certify a randomized quantum channel without a
density-matrix enumeration. This is a mathematical transfer, not an acquired
physical implementation. Applying one sampled permutation realizes one channel
trial, not a classical full density-matrix output or an estimate without sampling
uncertainty.

Three distinctions defeat premature promotion. For asymptotic symmetrization,
one exact uniform permutation directly gives the target. For local reduced
states, the n-state heat equation is enough. A finite-time reference-uniform
channel simulator could need full correlations, but this search found no supplied
scientific decision/rate package that both requires that transient channel and
demonstrates useful stiffness. A generic SWAP/partial-SWAP experiment is not
evidence of the required independent Poisson SWAP generator. Physical generator
calibration, rate uncertainty, routing, gate errors, reset, energy, and measurement
repetitions remain unacquired.

Even if such a package is later found, give the incumbent the same final-permutation
compression before physical execution. Exact microscopic classical simulation
does not require executing every microscopic SWAP on hardware. Both algorithms
must route their sampled final permutations or may use the same virtual relabeling
when permitted. The boundary-attempt count alone is not a physical gate-cost gain.

## SSEP and low-order statistical mechanics

[Franco, Goncalves, Neumann](https://arxiv.org/html/1301.4935v3),
*Phase transition in equilibrium fluctuations of symmetric slowed exclusion*,
v3 dated 2013-11-27, SPA 123(12), 4156-4185 (2013),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.spa.2013.06.016), defines symmetric bond clocks,
one slow bond of rate alpha n^(-beta), and an equilibrium Bernoulli start on the
infinite 1D lattice. Its results concern density, current and tagged-particle
fluctuations; limiting covariances are computed through heat semigroups.

The finite closed-graph binary occupancy model is an exact interchange projection.
Its occupancy label is valid; physical tagged-particle motion is not the
interchange label. One-time equilibrium occupancy already has the invariant
Bernoulli law. Two-time fluctuations retain dependence and require the saved
initial configuration or a separate multi-time contract. Infinite-volume
truncation and time-process approximation are additional gates. An unweighted
one-endpoint sampler does not automatically solve the paper's full fluctuation
problem. Exact k-particle SSEP duality and its smaller moment generators must be
the same-information incumbents; the algebra is retained in the transfer note.

a family-wide defeat.
