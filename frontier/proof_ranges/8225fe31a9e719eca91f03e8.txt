## 5. Approximation does not restore the missing composition rule

A possible rescue would be to require the output to be almost independent of the catalyst. Perhaps finite marginal-return power would then disappear at a success probability bounded away from zero.

That stronger conjecture is false.

Fix any target state \(\chi\), a separable state \(\omega\), a number \(0<q<1\), and an integer \(K\ge1\).

Let \(\tau_k\) be a locally flagged state holding \(k\) copies of \(\chi\), with \(\tau_0\) a product vacuum. Use the finite catalyst
\[
\tau^{(K)}=\sum_{k=0}^{K}r_k\tau_k,
\tag{26}
\]
where
\[
r_k=(1-q)q^k\quad(k<K),
\qquad
r_K=q^K.
\tag{27}
\]

The protocol is as follows.

On flag \(0\), fail. On a flag \(1\le k<K\), emit one copy of \(\chi\), decrement the flag, and succeed. On flag \(K\), succeed using a shared coin: with probability \(1-q\), emit \(\chi\) and decrement; with probability \(q\), emit \(\omega\) and leave the catalyst at \(K\).

Its total success probability is exactly \(q\). The normalized successful joint state is
\[
\boxed{
\Omega^{(K)}
=
\sum_{j=0}^{K-1}r_j\,\chi_S\otimes\tau_j
+
q^K\,\omega_S\otimes\tau_K.
}
\tag{28}
\]

Therefore
\[
\operatorname{tr}_S\Omega^{(K)}=\tau^{(K)}
\tag{29}
\]
exactly, while
\[
\operatorname{tr}_C\Omega^{(K)}
=
(1-q^K)\chi+q^K\omega.
\tag{30}
\]

More strongly,
\[
\frac12
\left\|
\Omega^{(K)}-\chi\otimes\tau^{(K)}
\right\|_1
\le q^K.
\tag{31}
\]

Thus one obtains arbitrarily accurate output and arbitrarily small joint deviation from product return, at a fixed positive success probability, with a finite catalyst for every requested accuracy.

The expected number of stored target copies is
\[
\sum_{k=0}^{K}kr_k
=
\frac{q(1-q^K)}{1-q},
\tag{32}
\]
which remains bounded as \(K\) increases.

There is nevertheless no free resource generation. The catalyst already contains copies of \(\chi\); failure can destroy its usefulness; and its maximum storage and Hilbert-space dimension grow with \(K\). Its preparation is not supplied for free by the construction.

Most importantly, a subsequent selective operation can condition on the small exceptional part in (28). A small unconditional correlation bound does not guarantee small catalyst disturbance after **every** later postselection.

This construction falsifies a dimension-uniform robust extension of the exact impossibility based only on success probability, marginal return and small unconditioned output–catalyst correlation.
