## 8. Exact consequence and provenance

Conditional on the source243 group/base/HF-transfer proofs and our independently audited history-independent interpretation coding, **there is an ordinary-PTIME binary-vocabulary query Q not decided by CPS(Card)** as explicitly defined above. Hence this counting model does not capture PSPACE either. This answers negatively the standard-counting version of the question raised in the final paragraph of Ferrarotti–Schewe, rather than a broader unspecified collection of possible counting primitives.

The **qualitative** consequence does not require KOW expanders or the new linear-logarithmic support estimate. On source243's original grids, N=Theta(L^3), every constituent of a finite interpretation trace has orbit at most S^2 where S is peak state size. If S=N^{O(1)}, source243 yields support O(L log^2 L). Its fixed-width HF transfer applies exactly as in its proof. Choosing a finite B bounding the two actual runtimes and state sizes makes the same translation exact; formula length depends on B but width does not. Thus the two outputs agree. The only changed resource step is the orbit bound being state-local rather than a total-trace count.

The **exponential** consequence additionally uses the new collective-cofilling/expander argument: for each fixed deciding ASM M, on one input of each sufficiently large hard pair its peak number of active objects is exp(Omega_M(N)). If the intermediate fixed-term or interpretation compiler costs C W^d, the exponent constant is simply divided by d. This preserves the same original atom size N.

Do not credit the noncapture query, rank-independent group-support method, or arbitrary-rank HF transfer to this bridge. They are source243's supplied inputs. Do not credit the standard idea of storing finite function graphs as new by itself. The separately checkable claim is its runtime-independent space accounting and the resulting precisely scoped counting-space consequence.

## 9. Falsification and independent-audit gates

A direct counterexample to any of the following invalidates this bridge:

- Two distinct allowed source functions have identical graph codes or a graph lookup misdecodes a zero/atom/high-rank value.
- A source deletion fails to delete, a duplicate update becomes a clash, or a clash partially changes a state.
- A sibling update is seen by a parallel rule during the same source step.
- A non-Boolean guard is interpreted as true merely because it is nonempty.
- Some source primitive creates superpolynomial hereditary footprint from polynomial arguments; the stated signature excludes the obvious powerset/succinct-number failures.
- The source definition omits from Active(S) data actually needed in a nonzero dynamic tuple. Reading §2.3 shows that it explicitly includes every such argument and value.
- The live coded root retains old graph wrappers after deletion or accumulates a step-index ordinal.

The accompanying finite test checks the function-graph/update part with actual HF pair codes. It does not generate a full BGS syntax tree or replace independent proof review.
