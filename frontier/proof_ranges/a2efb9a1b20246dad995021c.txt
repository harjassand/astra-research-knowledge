\section*{4. Important obstruction: the coefficient is not robust at pure reset}

Let $\mathcal A_\gamma$ be qubit amplitude damping, $0<\gamma<1$, with Kraus operators
$K_0=\operatorname{diag}(1,\sqrt{1-\gamma})$ and $K_1=\sqrt\gamma|0\rangle\langle1|$.
Then
\[
 \eta_D^c(\mathcal A_\gamma)=\eta_{\rm CMI}(\mathcal A_\gamma)=1,
 \qquad \eta_D^c(\mathcal A_1)=\eta_{\rm CMI}(\mathcal A_1)=0. \tag{7}
\]
The lower bound one can be proved directly, without N402's universal upper argument. Take $C=\operatorname{diag}(1-r,r)$, $B=|1\rangle\langle0|$, $\tau=I/2$, $0<r<1$, $q=1-p$, and
\[
 \omega_p=\begin{pmatrix}qC&0\\0&p\tau\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 H_p=\begin{pmatrix}0&\sqrt{pq}B\\\sqrt{pq}B^*&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Both partial traces of $H_p$ vanish, and $\omega_p\pm hH_p\succeq0$ whenever $h^2\le r/2$, independently of $p$. Put $s=1-\gamma>0$. The input relative-entropy Hessian is
$2pq\log(2qr/p)/(qr-p/2)$; the output Hessian is
$2pqs\log[2qsr/(p(1+\gamma))]/[qsr-p(1+\gamma)/2]$.
Both are asymptotic to $2p\log(1/p)/r$. Taking the perturbation $h\to0$ and then $p\downarrow0$ gives KL ratios tending to one. A uniform classical bit selecting $\omega_p\pm hH_p$ has $I(A:U|R)=\frac12\sum_\pm D(\omega_p\pm hH_p\|\omega_p)$, because its reference marginal is independent of the bit. The same holds at the output, proving the CMI claim too. Data processing supplies both upper bounds. The same direct construction proves coefficient one for every divergence in Section~1: $q_C(B)=1/r$ and $q_{\mathcal A_\gamma(C)}(\mathcal A_\gamma(B))=1/r$, so its new Hessian limits give the matching ratio without importing N402's universal theorem. The conditional-difference and generalized-information variants follow from the same fixed-reference witnesses and data processing. At $\gamma=1$ the channel is a replacer, so all these coefficients vanish.

Common Stinespring isometries yield
\[
 \|\mathcal A_\gamma-\mathcal A_1\|_\diamond
 \le2\sqrt{2(1-\sqrt\gamma)}\longrightarrow0. \tag{8}
\]
Consequently no algorithm with a uniformly bounded number of black-box channel uses can estimate $\eta_D^c$ to any additive error $\varepsilon<1/2$ with success probability at least $2/3$ for every channel. The two required estimate intervals are disjoint; an adaptive $N$-use distinguishing strategy has output trace distance at most $N\|\mathcal A_\gamma-\mathcal A_1\|_\diamond/2$. Choose $\gamma$ sufficiently close to one to contradict the required constant distinguishing advantage. This is an information-access obstruction, not an impossibility for exact supplied Choi matrices or promised spectral margins.
