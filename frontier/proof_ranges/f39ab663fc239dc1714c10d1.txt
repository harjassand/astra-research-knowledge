## 4. From the certificate to all compatible oscillations

This section proves the analytic implication, rather than assuming gradient-moment completion.

Choose a square flat torus \(Q\), a smooth periodic vector function \(\varphi\), and a constant matrix \(A_0\). Set

\[
 \varphi_t=e^{t\Delta}\varphi,\qquad
 A_t=A_0+D\varphi_t.
\]

The matrix field \(A_t\) is periodic and remains a gradient of an affine-plus-periodic map. In particular, its derivative pair has the compatible form \((X\mid Y),(Y\mid Z)\). Define

\[
 \mathcal F(t)=\int_Q [F_\kappa(A_t)-F_\kappa(A_0)].
\]

Differentiation and periodic integration by parts give

\[
 -\mathcal F'(t)
 =\int_Q\sum_{i=1}^2D^2F_\kappa(A_t)[\partial_iA_t,\partial_iA_t].
\]

The pullback of an exact form is exact, so

\[
 \int_Q(d\alpha)_{A_t}(\partial_1A_t,\partial_2A_t)
 =\int_Qd(A_t^*\alpha)=0.
\]

Combining Lemmas B and C therefore yields

\[
 -\mathcal F'(t)=\int_Q
    (z_+(t)^TS_*z_+(t)+z_-(t)^TS_*z_-(t))\ge0.
\]

The nonconstant periodic heat modes decay, so \(D\varphi_t\to0\) smoothly as \(t\to\infty\) and \(\mathcal F(t)\to0\). Hence the following positive representation holds:

\[
 \boxed{
 \int_Q[F_\kappa(A_0+D\varphi)-F_\kappa(A_0)]
 =\int_0^\infty\!\int_Q
      (z_+^TS_*z_++z_-^TS_*z_-)\,dx\,dt\ge0.}
\]

Equivalently, each quadratic term on the right is the squared norm of a fixed linear transformation of the six bilinear compatible-jet quantities. The rank-four factorization in Lemma C supplies that transformation explicitly.

This controls arbitrary smooth periodic gradients, not a selected family of waves or an arbitrary law on matrices. Resonant interactions are included without a nonresonance assumption.

For a smooth compactly supported perturbation in a bounded domain, extend it by zero into a larger periodic square and apply the identity there. At the initial time the relative energy outside the original domain is zero. Finally, approximate any \(\varphi\in W^{1,4}_0\) in the \(W^{1,4}\) norm by smooth compactly supported maps. Quartic growth and Hölder's inequality make the integrals continuous under this approximation. Thus \(F_\kappa\) is quasiconvex.

An output reflection changes \(q\) to \(-q\) and leaves \(u,v,w\) unchanged. Therefore \(F_{-\kappa}\) is quasiconvex as well. For \(|\ell|\le\kappa\),

\[
 F_\ell=\frac{1+\ell/\kappa}{2}F_\kappa
       +\frac{1-\ell/\kappa}{2}F_{-\kappa},
\]

so the whole interval is quasiconvex.
