## 0. What is being preserved

The original goal was to find a mathematical operation that permits consequential spectral or computational conclusions without acquiring an exponentially large representation first. The preserved mechanism separates three quantities that cannot be conflated:

1. the norm of a source;
2. the Hilbert-space rank or singular-value summability of a source;
3. the dimension of the spaces generated by repeatedly applying a supplied operator algebra to that source.

The results are a constructive positive-operator recursion with a uniform error bound; an exact source-rank classification; an optimal Schatten-class transfer law; a finite-precision obstruction; and an explicit replacement of an inadequate single-operator representation by a commuting row representation. The last construction acquires its representation from formulas, not from an assumed spectral oracle. It also retains the large multiplicities rather than making them disappear.

These results do **not** acquire suitable curvature certificates for arbitrary mathematical-physics or complexity-theory targets. Treating such acquisition as automatic would relocate the original obstruction. In particular, the product-degree example is a structural test, not an algorithmic speedup: its diagonal formula is simpler than the iterative method.
