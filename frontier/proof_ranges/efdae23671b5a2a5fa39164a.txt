### Theorem C: finite unital size, bounded witness, and sample count

Suppose Lambda is unital with Bloch matrix T, let s=||T||_F^2, and let omega_N=Lambda^{tensor N}(R_N). Then exactly

\[
\langle J_a\rangle_{\omega_N}=0,\quad
\langle J^2\rangle_{\omega_N}
=\frac{N(3-s)}4+\frac{sC_N}3.                              \tag{5}
\]

The known two-qubit EA criterion [F12] is s<=1. When s>1, the sufficient finite-size condition

\[
N>\frac{16s}{3(s-1)}                                     \tag{6}
\]

already guarantees bound entanglement, detected by three collective second moments. This is sufficient, not an exact finite-N separability boundary.

There is a bounded-score version with no postselection. Let delta=s-1>0 and t=2delta/3. On one preparation, choose a in {x,y,z} uniformly, measure every local sigma_a/2, sum to obtain j_a, and return the score

\[
S=\exp(-t j_a^2/N)\in[0,1].                               \tag{7}
\]

For every fully separable state, the expected score is at most

\[
B_N(t)=\frac{1+2/\sqrt{1+t/2}}3+\frac{t^{3/2}}{\sqrt N}.    \tag{8}
\]

If N delta>=4096, the target's expected score exceeds B_N(t) by at least delta^2/72. Consequently

\[
\inf_{\sigma\in\mathrm{SEP}_N}
\operatorname{TV}(\mathcal M_N(\omega_N),\mathcal M_N(\sigma))
\ge\delta^2/72,                                          \tag{9}
\]

where M_N records the chosen axis and sum. Half trace distance has the same lower bound. An independent-copy test at the midpoint of the guaranteed score gap has total error at most alpha using

\[
m\ge \left\lceil10368\,\delta^{-4}\ln(2/\alpha)\right\rceil. \tag{10}
\]

The constants are conservative analytic bounds, not numerical optima. The score requires calibrated spin axes and a known or conservatively bounded delta. Acquisition/calibration costs, N local measurements per preparation, and repeated state preparation remain real costs.
