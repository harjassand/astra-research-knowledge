## 10. Verification, references and attribution

Run:

    python verify_global_compatibility.py

It uses SymPy only. No optimizer output, pickle or prior verification JSON is an input. Nine groups check the circular flux identity for all 35 quartic monomials with symbolic affine background and wavevector; the scalar square completions and invariant leading coefficient; G's exact acoustic Gram; two full-rank modulated examples, including nonzero cubic data and exact affine minimization; exact deterministic packet gluing; the negative compatible interaction; and the variable-phase correction with a convex signed example.

The flux identity is proved analytically for general C² functions and general convex loops; polynomial checking at the circular carrier is not substituted for that proof. Fourier moment factorization, approximation, the curve parametrization and the probabilistic/variational conclusions likewise have arguments above.

`prior_replay.log` records twelve successful groups from the earlier tangent verifier. This continuity audit is not a premise of Theorem 2.1 or its quartic corollary. The earlier near-line result and its analytic classification remain internally supported, not externally validated. No repository write was performed.

Primary literature consulted:

[1] K. Astala, T. Iwaniec, I. Prause, E. Saksman, *A hunt for sharp L^p-estimates and rank-one convex variational integrals*, arXiv:1403.1095v1; especially Proposition 5.1 on radially linear maps. https://arxiv.org/html/1403.1095v1

[2] G. Bruno and F. Pasqualotto, *Quasiconvexity for the Dacorogna–Marcellini Energy*, arXiv:2608.06367v1 (2026). Heat/exact-form proof for that energy and quartics invariant under both left and right rotations, not the full left-invariant cone. https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06367v1

[3] A. Guerra and R. Teixeira da Costa, *Numerical evidence towards a positive answer to Morrey's problem*, arXiv:2006.09063. Fourier-wave, laminate and low-wave prior context. https://arxiv.org/html/2006.09063v1

[4] S. Gutierrez, *A Necessary Condition for the Quasiconvexity of Polynomials of Degree Four*, Journal of Convex Analysis 13 (2006), 51–60. Primary bibliographic/abstract record and prior supplied source context: cubic and fourth-order resonance conditions have established antecedents. Its PDF was not newly re-audited in this run. https://www.heldermann-verlag.de/jca/jca13/jca0514_b.pdf

[5] J. Voss et al., *Numerical approaches for investigating quasiconvexity in the context of Morrey's conjecture*, arXiv:2201.06392. Finite-element and other compatible searches are established practice. https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.06392

[6] P. Pedregal, arXiv:1905.06571v4 (2025), claims the complete planar implication through a fixed-point argument. That claim was inspected, not independently validated or used as a theorem premise. https://arxiv.org/html/1905.06571v4

[7] A. Agazzi, G. Bruno, A. Guerra, F. Pasqualotto, *Morrey's problem in R^(2x4) and R_sym^(3x3)*, arXiv:2608.12298 (2026). Its counterexamples are in the stated larger/symmetric spaces and are not silently transferred to planar 2x2 gradients. https://arxiv.org/html/2608.12298

The targeted literature search did not establish novelty for the modulation calibration or the resonance-block formulation. Absence of a matching search result is not priority clearance. No third-party manuscripts are redistributed.
