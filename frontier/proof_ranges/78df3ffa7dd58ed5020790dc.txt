## Finite-dimensional realization and access costs

Each positive finite measure is realized by a 10-dimensional diagonal Hamiltonian (H=\mathrm{diag}(0,\omega_1,\ldots,\omega_9)) and a Hermitian star observable with (A_{0i}=A_{i0}=\sqrt{w_i Z/(1-e^{-\beta\omega_i})}) and all other entries zero. The Gibbs partition function is 2.9392215381684369392; ‖H‖=40. The observable operator norms are ‖A\(_+\)‖=6.4434207899234197175 and ‖A\(_-\)‖=6.4539131035328054714. These norms are above one. This witness therefore does not establish the same gap under a separate promise ‖A‖≤1.

A coherent purification can be prepared with (O(d)) controlled rotations and angle precision (O(\log(1/\epsilon))) in an ideal gate model. These are representation-level costs for this explicit 10-level instance; actual gate synthesis, thermalization, readout, and physical runtime have not been acquired or measured. A Euclidean-data estimator is not granted the Gibbs probabilities, this state-preparation circuit, or an eigensystem oracle.
