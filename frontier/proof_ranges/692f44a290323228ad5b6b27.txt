## Vacuum-information hierarchy and the same-coupling improvement

I read the complete root proof. For r=k+1, the hidden interaction is supported
only on the fully occupied r-particle bath state. Both baths have unique vacuum
ground state, and their Hamiltonians agree on every bath sector N_B<=k. Any
available word contains at most k creations in total, so all of its intermediate
vectors and propagations remain in that common subspace. This proves equality
of the entire stated multi-time information contract, at arbitrary times.
Unlimited repetitions whose total additions exceed k are not covered.

The initial root construction was strengthened here to keep the same coupling
in both designs. With `a=r/[2(4r²+1)]`, use

    HA,1=n_d,  HA,0=(2r+1)n_d-aI/2,
    Hc=(d†c1+c1†d)/2.

The hidden spectator sector has design1 block
`[[r,1/2],[1/2,1/(8r)]]`, determinant-1/8. Its exact rational trial gives
energy<=-a. The design0 block is `[[3r,1/2],[1/2,1/(8r)]]`, determinant+1/8,
and every other block is nonnegative before its scalar. Thus the quadratic
contrast is+a/2 and the hidden contrast is<=-a/2. This aligns the obstruction
with the same-bath/same-coupling contract rather than changing the actuator.

Eight exact rational block fixtures and four independently built full-Fock
fixtures pass. The proof, not these tests, supports all k. The construction
does not give finite-temperature indistinguishability, a two-body/local bath,
a constant margin, or bounded bath/designed energy scales as k grows. Its
O(1/k) margin and growing r-body interaction are recorded explicitly.

An acquired fixed total charge Q<=k removes this particular obstruction,
because the full target sector has N_B<=Q and the interaction vanishes there.
This is a precise positive transfer from charge-aware discipline, not proof
that one-particle spectra acquire the required sector. Macroscopic materials
need a justified excitation/particle-hole encoding or other model-error bound.
