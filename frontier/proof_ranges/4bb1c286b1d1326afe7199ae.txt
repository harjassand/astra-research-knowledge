## Statement

Identify R² with C, use normalized integration in y∈R/(2πZ), and let

\[
 G(A)=3(u+v-2j)^2+\frac{35}{24}u^2+j^2-\frac52ju,
 \quad u=|A_1|^2,\ v=|A_2|^2,\ j=\det A.
\]

For every L>0, every pair of **complex** periodic amplitudes
α,β∈W^{1,4}(R/(LZ);C), and every constant B∈R^{2×2}, set

\[
 \phi(x,y)=\alpha(x)e^{iy}+\beta(x)e^{-iy}.
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{\inf_B\mathbb E\,[G(B+D\phi)-G(B)]
       =\mathbb E G(D\phi)\ge\tfrac12\mathbb E|D\phi|^4.}
\]

The amplitudes, phases, x period, variation speed and polygonal strip widths
are arbitrary. No circulation cancellation or finite graph is imposed. Thus
the theorem includes every R²-valued first-harmonic transverse profile
A(x)cos y+C(x)sin y with arbitrary vector-valued A,C, including interacting
clockwise and counterclockwise carriers. It controls the complete signed
interaction rather than assigning nonnegative energy to each transition.

This is a restricted-family theorem for the particular non-polyconvex G.
It does not establish quasiconvexity of G for arbitrary gradients, the complete
left-invariant quartic implication, or a new physical operation. Historical
originality and external mathematical review are UNKNOWN.
