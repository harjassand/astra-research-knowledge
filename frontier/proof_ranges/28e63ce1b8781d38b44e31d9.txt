## 1. Exact contract

H=D+T and inverse temperature beta>0. Let

    rho=e^(-beta H)/Z_H,
    sigma=sum_i P_i rho P_i,
    rho_D=e^(-beta D)/Z_D,
    tau=Tr(rho T), a=-tau>=0.

    f(x)=(1+x)/2 log(1+x)+(1-x)/2 log(1-x), 0<=x<=1.
