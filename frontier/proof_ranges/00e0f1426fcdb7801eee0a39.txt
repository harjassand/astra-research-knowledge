No computational-complexity assumption is involved. Even an optimal passive test with unlimited processing obeys it.
Now apply the field in equation (1).
Let
\[ \chi_r(t) = \left.\frac{d}{dh}\mathbb E_h f(X_t)\right|_{h=0}, \]
starting in the unperturbed stationary law.
Write
\[ S=\frac12(Q_r+Q_r^*). \]
Differentiating the controlled generator and using stationarity gives
\[ \chi_r(t) = -\int_0^t \langle Sf,e^{sQ_r}f\rangle_\pi\,ds. \tag{12} \]
For the ladder,
\[ Sf=-f+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}(d_1+c_\ell), \]
while
\[ e^{sQ_r}f=e^{-s}(f+\varepsilon s\,d_1). \]
Orthogonality therefore gives
\[ \langle Sf,e^{sQ_r}f\rangle_\pi = e^{-s}\left(-1+\frac{\varepsilon^2s}{2}\right). \]
Integrating,
\[ \boxed{ \chi_r(t) = 1-e^{-t} -\frac{\varepsilon^2}{2} \left[1-(1+t)e^{-t}\right]. } \tag{13} \]
At \(t=1\), the gap from the equilibrium response is
\[ \chi_{\mathrm{eq}}(1)-\chi_r(1) = c_*\varepsilon^2, \qquad c_*=\frac{1-2/e}{2}>\frac18. \tag{14} \]
The passive signal requires \(2r\) perturbative steps. The response signal appears at second order, independently of \(r\).
That is the operational role of the new construction.
Choose
\[ h=\frac{\varepsilon}{100} \]
and measure the two endpoint means \(m(+h)\), \(m(-h)\) at time one. Form
\[ D(h)=\frac{m(h)-m(-h)}{2h}. \]
For \(|h|\le1\), the first three field derivatives of the generator have sup-norm operator bounds below \(7\). The time-ordered derivative formula then bounds
\[ \left|\frac{d^3m(h)}{dh^3}\right|<500. \]
Consequently, each central difference differs from its exact susceptibility by at most \(500h^2/6\). Comparing the two hypotheses,
\[ \boxed{ D_{\mathrm{eq}}(h)-D_r(h) \ge \frac{13}{120}\varepsilon^2 > \frac{\varepsilon^2}{10}. } \tag{15} \]
The equilibrium reference is explicitly computable:
\[ m_{\mathrm{eq}}(h) = \tanh(h)\,[1-e^{-\cosh(h)}]. \]
Estimate each endpoint mean to accuracy
\[ a=\frac{h\varepsilon^2}{80} =\frac{\varepsilon^3}{8000}. \]
Hoeffding’s inequality gives a sufficient sample count per field sign
\[ N= \left\lceil \frac{2}{a^2}\log\frac8\zeta \right\rceil = O\!\left(r^6\log(1/\zeta)\right). \tag{16} \]
Before each trial, turn off the field and wait
\[ L\ge2\log(8N/\zeta). \]
The explicit regeneration coupling makes each initial law sufficiently close to stationarity. Coupling the sequential experiment to independent stationary trials charges the resulting error and proves the stated polynomial total duration.
Field and timing precision need only polynomial accuracy. For example, absolute setting errors bounded by \(\varepsilon^3/10^8\) fit within the decision margin.
These are conservative bounds with large constants. The result establishes a scaling separation, not a practical low-sample protocol.
A trivial construction could attach an unobserved dissipative subsystem to an equilibrium signal. That would not make the observed process intrinsically nonequilibrium.
