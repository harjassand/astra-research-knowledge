\section*{3. A general channel-order equivalence and its finite certificate}

\textbf{Theorem 2 (complete Petz order).} For arbitrary finite channels, the following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item complete measured-$\chi^2$/SLD dominance;
\item complete dominance for the \emph{modular Petz} divergence with $f_1(x)=(x-1)^2/(x+1)$;
\item simultaneous complete dominance for every operator-convex modular Petz divergence $D_f$.
\end{enumerate}
Affine tangents at one are immaterial. The all-state statement uses standard extended Petz support values, allowing infinity. This result needs no N402 premise.

\textit{Derivation.} For faithful $\rho,\sigma$, $X=\rho-\sigma$, and $s>0$, introduce an untouched qubit and
\[
 \Omega_s=\frac{\operatorname{diag}(\rho,s\sigma)}{1+s},\qquad
 H=\begin{pmatrix}0&X\\X&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Solving its off-diagonal SLD equation gives the exact identity
\[
 q_{\Omega_s}(H)=4(1+s)\langle X,(L_\rho+sR_\sigma)^{-1}X\rangle
              =4(1+s)D_{f_s}(\rho\|\sigma),\quad
 f_s(x)=\frac{(x-1)^2}{x+s}. \tag{10}
\]
Apply the complete local SLD order to this block test. Every normalized operator-convex function has the positive representation
\[
 f(x)=b(x-1)^2+\int_{[0,\infty)}f_s(x)\,d\nu(s),\quad
 b\ge0,\quad\nu\ge0,\quad\int\frac{d\nu(s)}{1+s}<\infty. \tag{11}
\]
Take $s\downarrow0$ and $sD_{f_s}\to D_{(x-1)^2}$ as $s\to\infty$, then integrate the inequalities. Conversely
$D_{f_1}(\sigma+tH\|\sigma)=t^2q_\sigma(H)/2+O(t^3)$, so triangular dominance implies the local SLD order, which is exactly global measured-$\chi^2$ order. On PSD boundaries use the spectral perspective
$\sum_{ij}(\lambda_i-\mu_j)^2(\lambda_i+s\mu_j)^{-1}|\langle e_i,v_j\rangle|^2$, omit $0/0$, and apply positive integration/Tonelli. This proves the extended statement without assuming invertible ambient outputs.

This connects complete binary estimation with all modular Petz orders, including pointwise Petz R\'enyi comparison in its data-processing range. It does \emph{not} imply that their linear contraction coefficients all coincide. The coherent-qubit and integral machinery is closely related to Hirche [1]; the all-$f$ equivalence is assessed as a short corollary-level extension.

\textbf{Exact finite sufficient certificate.} A state-independent HP unital lift $J:\mathcal L(B)\to\mathcal L(C)$ with $\M^*J=\N^*$ and complete square domination certifies $\M\succeq^c_{\rm SLD}\N$. Let $C_\Phi$ denote the unnormalized Choi matrix and, in row-major Hilbert--Schmidt coordinates, set
\[
 D_\N=C_\N\otimes I_B,\quad D_\M=C_\M\otimes I_C,\quad T=I_A\otimes[J].
\]
Zhu--Wang's CP outer-defect criterion [2] is $D_\N-T^*D_\M T\succeq0$. Together with the linear HP, unitality and reconstruction constraints, this is precisely the affine SDP
\[
 \boxed{\begin{pmatrix}D_\N&T^*D_\M\\D_\M T&D_\M\end{pmatrix}\succeq0.} \tag{12}
\]
The equivalence follows by congruence to
$\operatorname{diag}(D_\N-T^*D_\M T,D_\M)$ and is valid for singular $D_\M$.
Its main block has order $d_A(d_B^2+d_C^2)$. A second exact formulation uses a contraction between minimal Stinespring spaces intertwining every lifted observable. These are finite formulations of an established criterion, not new characterizations of general entropy dominance. A generic CVXPY builder is included, but was not solved because that solver stack is absent; the explicit qutrit witness was reconstructed numerically. Exact or interval certificates and conditioning promises remain necessary for rigorous numerical decisions.

\textbf{Remaining ordering gap.} Necessity of a common signed lift for unrestricted complete measured-$\chi^2$ order is still open in general; a full-output-span sufficient structural case is known [1]. Theorem 2 does not close it, and the finite reference bound for the signed-lift criterion cannot simply be transferred to the weaker unsmoothed order. No polynomial algorithm for arbitrary complete entropy-order membership has been established.
