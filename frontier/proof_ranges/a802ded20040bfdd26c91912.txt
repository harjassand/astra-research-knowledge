Finite contract and construction
--------------------------------
Let a finite undirected network have conductances c_e>0 and terminals A,B.
Use each edge once, oriented in the direction of an admissible potential u:
u_A=0, u_B=1, and Delta_e=u(head)-u(tail)>0 on every active flow edge.
Let j_e>=0 be an exactly conserved unit A-to-B flow, with no incoming flow
at A or outgoing flow at B. Strict increase of u makes its support acyclic.
Zero-potential-drop edges can be ignored only if their flow is zero.

Discrete integration by parts gives sum_e j_e*Delta_e=1. Consequently

  q_e=j_e*Delta_e

is a probability distribution. It has a constructive sampler: draw a directed
flow path using normalized outgoing flows, draw s uniformly in [0,1], and return
the edge of that path whose potential interval contains s. The probability that
the path uses an edge is j_e (prove inductively in topological order using flow
conservation); the conditional probability of selecting that edge is Delta_e.
Thus its marginal is exactly q. Acquiring the graph and outgoing flow tables,
checking conservation and monotonicity, and drawing a path are charged costs.
An explicit graph generally requires O(|E|) input/storage before these checks;
path length is not assumed dimension independent or small.

If every edge with positive Dirichlet energy has q_e>0, define on q's support

  a_e=c_e*Delta_e/j_e,       b_e=j_e/(c_e*Delta_e).

Then a_e*b_e=1 and

  E_q[a]=sum_e c_e*Delta_e^2=E(u),
  E_q[b]=sum_e j_e^2/c_e=R(j).

Classical Dirichlet-Thomson gives

  1/E_q[b] <= cap(A,B) <= E_q[a].

At the harmonic potential h and unit harmonic flow
j_e=c_e*Delta_e h/cap, a is constantly cap and b constantly 1/cap. This
zero-variance ideal supplies neither an efficient acquisition algorithm nor a
certificate of closeness of an approximate pair.
