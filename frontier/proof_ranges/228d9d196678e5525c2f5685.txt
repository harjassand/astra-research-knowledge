## Closed-cone generation and its limit

Let \(C_d\) be the cone of rank-one-convex homogeneous planar quartics in a
fixed coefficient basis of dimension \(d=9\) (left-SO(2) invariant) or
\(d=35\) (unrestricted). Let \(S_d\) be all moment vectors

\[
m(B,\varphi)_i=\mathbb E f_i(B+D\varphi)-f_i(B)
\]
from the global modulation family, with arbitrary affine \(B\). The proved
identity gives \(S_d\subseteq C_d^*\). Conversely, if a coefficient vector
\(p\) is nonnegative on every element of \(S_d\), take the allowed
zero-carrier ellipse modulation

\[
\varphi_\varepsilon(x)=\varepsilon a\sin(b\cdot x),\qquad
D\varphi_\varepsilon=\varepsilon(a\otimes b)\cos(b\cdot x).
\]

For quartic \(F_p\), exact Taylor expansion gives

\[
m(B,\varphi_\varepsilon)\cdot p
=\frac{\varepsilon^2}{4}D^2F_p(B)[a\otimes b,a\otimes b]
+\frac{3\varepsilon^4}{8}F_p(a\otimes b).
\]

Divide by \(\varepsilon^2\) and let \(\varepsilon\to0\). Nonnegativity on
all modulation moments forces every acoustic Hessian to be nonnegative, so
\(p\in C_d\). For any \(a\ne0\), choose a nondegenerate ellipse with
\(V(0)=a\); rational spatial directions are dense and the acoustic form is
continuous. Hence

\[
(\operatorname{cone}S_d)^*=C_d,
\qquad
\overline{\operatorname{cone}S_d}=C_d^*.
\]

In the nine-dimensional case N794 identifies \(C_9^*\) with the set of
moment vectors admitting its (8\times8\) PSD completion. In dimension 35,
the same polar statement is valid, but no N794 nine-moment/8-by-8 interface is
being asserted.

The inclusion still needed for global closure is

\[
\{m(B,D\psi):D\psi\text{ any compatible periodic gradient}\}
\subseteq C_d^*.
\]

That is the rank-one-convex-to-quasiconvex implication itself. The polar
generation result characterizes the target cone; it does not put arbitrary
compatible gradients into it. The exact negative interaction increment
\(-95/256\) for determinant-square in the source packet independently rules
out adding positive block margins without cross-resonance control.
