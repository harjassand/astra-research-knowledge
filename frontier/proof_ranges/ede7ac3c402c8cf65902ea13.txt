## 3. Tensor positivity is not tested beyond the cluster size

For any map Q such that Q and Q tensor Q are positive, applying Q at every site to any two-producible state gives a positive operator. Proof: in every product-of-clusters summand, each cluster has one or two sites, so its image is positive; tensor and sum these images. This argument is independent of the special R_N coefficients.

Accordingly, tests on the R_N family cannot detect a failure of Q^(tensor n) on genuinely higher-cluster inputs once positivity on one and two factors is known. This is a structural limitation of the source family, not a numerical failure or an objection based on an open problem's age. Recasting its binary noise survival as tensor-stable positivity would change the universal input quantifier.

## 4. Exact obstruction to naive regularization

The prior Casimir proof correctly states an ordinary single-copy relative-entropy law and expressly does not assert the same regularized law. That caution is necessary.

Let P_0 be the N-qubit singlet projector. The exact single-copy fully separable maximum is 1/(n+1). For two copies, take the product across laboratories of local Bell states between each laboratory's two copy registers. This alternative is separable across the N laboratories, although each laboratory uses within-laboratory cross-copy entanglement. Because P_0 is real,

    expectation(P_0 tensor P_0) = Tr(P_0 P_0^T)/2^N
                                = rank(P_0)/2^N.

At N=4 this equals 1/8, strictly larger than (1/3)^2=1/9. The fresh script checks the same strict inequality exactly through N=24. Thus the single-copy product overlap bound does not multiply against the permitted regularized alternatives. This falsifies that particular proof transfer; it does not determine the actual regularized entanglement.

## 5. Why this ceased to be the main high-upside route

The old family proves a nontrivial contrast between singleton separability and channel-robust entanglement. But the above certificates leave no acquired bridge from that result to bipartite NPT bound entanglement, all-tensor positivity, quantum capacity, or a general secret-key theorem. General nonunital survival can still rely on exponentially small postselection. The previous restart already supplied a concrete channel for which leading Gaussian fluctuations have a separable realization.

The decision was therefore to stop extending that family as the main research route. This is a comparison of available mathematical mechanisms, not a claim that nobody could ever extract an important result from it.
