## 10. Supported conclusions about discovery

The productive representation change was to exchange background and perturbation in the quartic Hessian, then use the determinant's one-quadric geometry to propagate acoustic equality into a cubic identity. A rank-one positivity condition thereby supplied an almost-everywhere nullspace constraint, not merely an SDP representation.

In the invariant cone, rotation covariance then made low-rank Hessian remainders rigid. This produced the explicit exceptional class and an actual compatible Jensen proof on every singular averaged-Hessian face. The steps cover all nine coefficients; the underlying closure covers all 35 planar quartic coefficients.

The compatible heat-increase example shows why the previous successful certificate cannot be elevated to a universal monotone linear-flow principle. It does not show that positive completion, quasiconvexity, or a more general flow is impossible. The current theorems are structural progress, not a claim to have met the full exceptional-theorem objective.
