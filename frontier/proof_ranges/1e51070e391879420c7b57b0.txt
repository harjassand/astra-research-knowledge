15 Scope and relation to existing frameworks
The reduction uses generic complex POVMs and opposite Pauli chirality. It does not transfer
automatically to real or transpose-compatible denominator tuples. Finite cutoffs or lift
characterizations for narrower classes are compatible with the theorem.
The admissible inverse kernels always act on a finite-dimensionalspatial two-factordomain. A
compression that mixes the factors can destroy that domain. Results for free spectrahedra or
unconstrained matrix evaluations, such as the framework in [4], therefore cannot be used to
infer a cutoff here without proving that the spatial domain is preserved.
Complete channel orders and contraction-coefficient frameworks are developed in [2]; estimation
and complete information comparisons are discussed in [3]. These are contextual references,
not substitutes for any of the reduction lemmas above. This note makes no claim of exhaustive
novelty verification. The exact theorem concerns qualitative preorder membership; the additive
theorem concerns the bounded entropy defectV. Neither identifies numerical contraction
coefficients, covers all restricted-marginal variants, or asserts a common Hermiticity-preserving
lift characterization for generic measurement pairs.
Every indispensable new step of the reduction has been included: the honest domain realization,
augmented-anchor estimates, local smoothing, question normalization, finite gapped penalty,
ordered inverse compiler, rational complex numerator implementation, faithful slice embedding,
and uniform global completion. The additive strengthening also includes the Schmidt-sector
argument, dimension-independent faithful normalization, explicit entropy-filter margin, and
finite tensor amplification. The imported nonlocal-game complexity theorem remains the
external premise. The note’s research status remains AI-generated and not peer-reviewed.
