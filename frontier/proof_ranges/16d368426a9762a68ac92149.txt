## Exact claims and constants

For `P = Law(m+V+Z)`, `Q = N(m, sigma^2 I_d)`, and an independent copy `V'`, the density ratio is

\[
\frac{dP}{dQ}(x)=\mathbb E_V\exp\!\left(\frac{(x-m)^\top V}{\sigma^2}-\frac{\|V\|^2}{2\sigma^2}\right).
\]

For fixed `v,v'`, integrating the product of these two likelihood ratios against `Q` gives `exp(v^T v'/sigma^2)`. Consequently

\[
1+\chi^2(P\|Q)=\mathbb E_{V,V'}e^{V^\top V'/\sigma^2}.
\]

When the scalar coordinates `V_i` are independent, the expectation factors, so the quoted identity is

\[
\chi^2(P\|Q)=\prod_{i=1}^d\mathbb E e^{V_iV_i'/\sigma^2}-1.
\]

The prime must mean an independent copy of the whole vector. For each centered coordinate with `|V_i| <= b_i` and `v_i = Var(V_i)`, put `U = V_i V_i'/sigma^2`. Then `E[U] = 0`, `|U| <= b_i^2/sigma^2`, and `E[U^2] = v_i^2/sigma^4`. Taylor's integral remainder gives

\[
e^u\le 1+u+\tfrac12e^{|u|}u^2,
\qquad
\mathbb E e^U-1\le \frac{e^{b_i^2/\sigma^2}v_i^2}{2\sigma^4}.
\]

Thus

\[
\chi^2(P\|Q)\le
\exp\!\left(\sum_i\frac{e^{b_i^2/\sigma^2}v_i^2}{2\sigma^4}\right)-1,
\qquad
\operatorname{TV}(P,Q)\le \tfrac12\sqrt{\chi^2(P\|Q)}.
\]

For rare coordinates `X_i`, suppose `|X_i| <= A w` and `Pr(X_i != 0) <= C w`, with independent coordinates and `V_i = X_i - E[X_i]`. Then `|V_i| <= 2 A w`, `v_i <= C A^2 w^3`, and, for fixed `A,C,sigma` and small `w`, the exponent is at most a constant times `d w^6`. Also `|E[X_i]| <= C A w^2`, so

\[
\|\mathbb EX\|_2\le CA\sqrt d\,w^2.
\]

At `w=d^(-1/4)`, this permits a mean norm of order one while the one-query output `X+Z` is within `O(d^(-1/4))` TV of `N(E[X], sigma^2 I_d)`. Here “aggregate mean shift” means Euclidean norm; the sum of the coordinate means can instead be order `sqrt(d)`.

For the vector result, define a centered proxy by

\[
\mathbb E e^{t^\top V}\le e^{t^\top S t/2}\quad(t\in\mathbb R^d),\qquad S\succeq0.
\]

Conditioning on `V` and applying this bound to `V'` gives

\[
\mathbb E_{V'}e^{V^\top V'/\sigma^2}\le
e^{V^\top S V/(2\sigma^4)}.
\]

Use the Gaussian identity `exp(V^T S V/(2 sigma^4)) = E_H exp(H^T S^(1/2)V/sigma^2)`, where `H ~ N(0,I_d)`. Applying the proxy bound again and integrating the Gaussian quadratic form yields

\[
1+\chi^2(P\|Q)\le\det(I-S^2/\sigma^4)^{-1/2},
\qquad \|S\|_{op}<\sigma^2.
\]

No coordinate independence is used in this version. For `X=g(G)`, with `G ~ N(0,I_d)` and `g:R^d -> R^d` globally `L`-Lipschitz in Euclidean norm, each scalar projection `t^T g(G)` is `L||t||_2`-Lipschitz. Gaussian concentration therefore makes `g(G)-m` subGaussian with proxy `L^2 I_d`. Averaging `k` independent queries gives `S=L^2 I_d/k`, hence

\[
\chi^2(\mathcal L(\bar X+Z)\|N(m,\sigma^2I_d))
\le \left(1-\frac{L^4}{k^2\sigma^4}\right)^{-d/2}-1,
\quad k>L^2/\sigma^2.
\]

Solving the TV bound `0.5 sqrt(chi^2) <= epsilon` gives the exact sufficient threshold

\[
k\ge \frac{L^2/\sigma^2}{\sqrt{1-\exp[-2\log(1+4\varepsilon^2)/d]}}.
\]

For small epsilon and large d, this is

\[
k\sim \frac{L^2}{\sigma^2}\frac{\sqrt d}{\sqrt8\,\varepsilon}.
\]

The executable count is the larger of one and the ceiling of the sufficient threshold. If L=0, one query still reveals the unknown constant mean m; the asymptotic expression without this floor incorrectly tends to zero. For positive L, the bound counts whole-vector queries, or kd real coordinates read. Summation costs O(kd) arithmetic and O(d) working storage. It assumes L and sigma (or certified bounds for them) are known when choosing k.
