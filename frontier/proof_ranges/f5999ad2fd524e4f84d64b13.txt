1. EXPLICIT ISOMETRIC BROADCASTER

Define U:C^3 -> C^3 tensor C^3 by

 U e1 = -i L2 v / sqrt(3),
 U e2 =  i L1 v / sqrt(3),
 U e3 = v.

The displayed expectation identities show that the three images are
orthonormal. They all belong to the symmetric total-spin-two subspace.
Therefore B(X)=U X U^dagger is CPTP with equal two marginals. Its normalized
pure Choi state is |psi><psi|, where

 psi=(-i|e1> L2 v+i|e2> L1 v)/3 + |e3>v/sqrt(3).

The reference marginal is I/3. In the real vector basis Ji^T=-Ji, so
the star observable is

 H=-J1(A)L1-J2(A)L2.

Its only nonzero reference blocks are H13=-iL2, H31=iL2,
H23=iL1, H32=-iL1. Direct substitution gives

 <psi|H|psi> = 2/sqrt(3)+2/sqrt(3) = 4/sqrt(3).

This exceeds 20/9, since 4/sqrt(3)>20/9 is equivalent to
36>20sqrt(3), or 1296>1200 after squaring positive sides.

The BC marginal is the uniform mixture of the total-spin-two states
with m_z=-1,0,1. To see this, the normalized vectors L1v/sqrt(3)
and L2v/sqrt(3) form an orthonormal basis of the span of |2,+1>,|2,-1>.
For each of |2,+1> and |2,-1>, an individual output has populations

populations are (1/6,2/3,1/6). Their uniform mixture is
