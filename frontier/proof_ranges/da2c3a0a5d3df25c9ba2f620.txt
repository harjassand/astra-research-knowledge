## 6. What this adds, comparator and remaining operation

The new input representation is an acquired bounded unbiased nonlinear
correction, rather than a solved field or a supplied unknown-mean vector law.
The composed output is a generative noisy observation of selected nonlinear
solutions, with full joint-law error and nested cost accounted for. This is a
proved compatible interface between the two capital mechanisms. It does not
solve the general unbounded conservative-gradient N687 sampler, whose oracle
and every-run query target are different.

The strongest matched sampler is stochastic FORS, already reconstructed in
N722. This compiler *uses* it and claims no algorithmic advantage over an
equally informed FORS implementation supplied the same bounded PDE oracle.
Agarwal–Claisse branching/supersolution methods can also supply bounded inputs
in covered regimes. Sample-mean Gaussian postprocessing is a useful control,
not the strongest baseline. No historical novelty or previously unattainable
scientific answer has been established by the composition alone.

For equal radii h and a simple independent tree per requested point,
`R^2=r*h^2/sigma^2` and each vector acquisition costs order r tree roots.
Consequently naïve total node work can grow quadratically in the number of
observations. Dimension-free oracle counts at fixed R are not a field-wide
speed theorem. Small sigma also increases cost as approximately sigma^−2;
the compiler does not evade high-precision mean acquisition by renaming it.

For downstream M independent synthetic observations, product-TV costs at most
M*epsilon. Conditioning on a rare event of probability rho generally needs
error small compared with rho. A Gaussian observation sampler is not an
evaluated likelihood density or a free rare-event/posterior oracle. Noise scale
sigma, predictor/tube, full model and primitive costs remain essential inputs.

The supplied prototype and replay use floating-point Gaussian, exponential and
acceptance calculations. They are diagnostic implementations, not interval-
validated continuous-TV executables: finite machine output has TV1 from an
unrounded continuous target. Deployment needs a commonly rounded target or
exact-real semantics plus certified primitive/acceptance errors. Outward cap
rounding, node ceilings and empirical moments do not close that arithmetic gate.

`updates/EG/package/frontier_campaign/computation_opening/THEOREM.md`, and
