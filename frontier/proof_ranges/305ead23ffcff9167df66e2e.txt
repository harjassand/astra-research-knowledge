## 7. Executed actual-current projection on the infinite graph

For m=2 use orientations

- e1(a,b): (a,b)->(a+1,b);
- e2(a,b): (a,b)->(a,b+1);
- e3(a,b): (a,b+1)->(a+1,b).

Take w_0=e3(0,0), so partial w_0=delta_(0,1)-delta_(1,0). The two triangle cycles are

\[
T_0(g)=e_1(g)-e_2(g)-e_3(g),
\]
\[
T_1(g)=e_1(g+e_2)-e_2(g+e_1)-e_3(g).
\]

Both have identically zero divergence. The repeated use of e2 as a coordinate direction and edge type is confined to these displayed formulas.

Use 17 one-dimensional functions: delta_0,...,delta_9 and tails beginning at 10 with ratios 1-2^-k, k=0,...,6. Antisymmetric potential products give 136 variables. Symmetric products multiplying the two face types give 306 cycle variables. The unknown potential and flow are not reconstructed on a grid.

Floating discovery is followed by rounding to denominator 2^52 and exact rational evaluation. The final verifier separately constructs the full 1489-atom residual and confirms that its infinite norm equals the variational gap exactly.

Result:

\[
\boxed{0.42510190863487\le E_b\le0.42510190864763,}
\]

\[
\Gamma=1.2751786347077035\times10^{-11}\quad\text{(decimal summary of an exact fraction)}.
\]

Thus the normalized balanced projection is computed with norm error at most 3.571e-6. The largest pole is 63/64; no coordinate cutoff occurs. The exact fractions, coefficients and standalone verifier are included.

The recorded discovery plus exact verification took 5.1210 seconds, peak 116332 KiB, one BLAS thread. This timing starts inside the certify function, after imports. It includes Gram construction, discovery, exact quadratic evaluation and the independently expanded residual. It excludes development, literature search and any physical-model acquisition. These are container observations, not a machine-independent complexity bound.

**This number is E_b, not D.** It resolves the first non-exponentially-local current projection identified in Section 2, not every subsequent interacting correction.
