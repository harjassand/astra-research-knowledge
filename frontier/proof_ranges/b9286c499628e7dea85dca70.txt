10 Computability classification and the reference obstruction
Apply the game reduction withr = 3/4 and γ = 1/4. In the nonhalting case the external
game hasω∗≤ 1/2 =r−γ, so Proposition 6.1 gives a uniform polynomial gapg =γ/2 = 1/8.
Lemma 7.1 yields a positive inverse-kernel gap, and Proposition 9.1 yields complete SLD
dominance.
In the halting caseω∗ = 1, so a finite strategy scoring above7/8 =r +γ/2 exists, whether
or not value one is attained. The faithful polynomial witness transfers exactly through the
graph compiler and the negative POVM slice. Complete SLD dominance fails. Every output
operation uses finite rational arithmetic and effective bounds; no witness dimension is needed
to compute the POVMs. We have therefore reduced halting to failure and nonhalting to
dominance.

10.2 No computable sufficient SLD reference cutoff
For a fixed reference dimensionn, existence of a faithful SLD violation is a first-order real-
algebraic question. Use real and imaginary matrix coordinates forρ,H,L i; impose trace and
Hermiticity constraints, positive definiteness by principal-minor conditions, and the polynomial
Sylvester equations. The Fisher difference is polynomial in these variables once the scoresLi
are included. Real quantifier elimination therefore decides whether a violation exists at that
fixed size.
