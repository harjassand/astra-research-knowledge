FIRST DERIVATION AND EXACT ORIGINAL-MODEL RESULT

The first derivation was frozen in s41_primitive/first_derivation_frozen.txt
before primary-source comparison. It begins from
  xdot=-Kx-diag(alpha)x^3+e1*u, y=x1,
where K is symmetric SPD. Heterogeneous unknown positive alpha is allowed.
Set q(s)=(sI+K)^-1 e1, k=K e1, H1(s)=q1(s). Define
  F_R(s,t)=-H3(c+R,c-R,t), c=(s-t)/2.
The output frequency is s. Then exactly
  F_R(s,t)=sum_r alpha_r q_r(s)q_r(t)q_r(c+R)q_r(c-R).
The known port factor is P_R=q1(c+R)q1(c-R). For hidden r,
  q_r(c+R)q_r(c-R)=k_r^2 R^-4+O(R^-6),
whereas P_R=-R^-2+O(R^-4). Thus, for known alpha1,
  B(s,t)=lim R^4 [F_R-alpha1*q1(s)q1(t)*P_R]
        =sum_(r>1) beta_r q_r(s)q_r(t), beta_r=alpha_r k_r^2.
Unknown alpha1 can be eliminated at finite R by J_R=F_R/P_R:
  -(4/3)R^2[J_R-J_(2R)]=B+O(R^-2).

Let K=U Lambda U^T, a=U^T e1, and choose spectral signs ai>0.
Distinct eigenvalues and nonzero overlaps allow linear identification of
Lambda and a_i^2. With v_i(s)=ai/(s+lambda_i),
  B(s,t)=v(s)^T A v(t),
  A=U^T diag(0,beta2,...,betan) U.
For n distinct low frequencies V=[v(s1),...,v(sn)] is Cauchy-invertible:
  A=V^-T [B(si,sj)] V^-1.
If all hidden beta_r are nonzero and pairwise distinct, diagonalizing A
recovers all rows of U up to hidden signed permutation. Its zero eigenvector
is the known port row a. Consequently K is recovered, and then
  alpha_r=beta_r/(U_r Lambda a)^2, r>1.
The port alpha is recovered from its leading coefficient or from H3 once
the hidden contributions are known. Homogeneous known alpha is a special
case. No quartic-tensor optimization or order-n nonlinear IO jet is used.

This proof also shows why the ambient linear nullspace is harmless to this
specific contraction: every ambient null tensor satisfying the physical
port contraction identity has zero contraction with Lambda*a twice. The
identity follows from the high-pair asymptotic and does not claim that the
entire ambient tensor is linearly observable.
