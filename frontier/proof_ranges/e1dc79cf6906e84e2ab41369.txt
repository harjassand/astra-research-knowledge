The unclipped tilted density is

    p_m(y) = q_m(y) exp(w(y)) / Z,
    Z = exp(||mu||^2/(2m)) >= 1.

Completing the square shows p_m=N(mu/m,I_d/m). We now compare p_hat_m to p_m
without replacing <y,X> by the much weaker deterministic bound R||y||.

Let tau(t)=(|t|−1)_+ and delta(y)=w(y)−w_hat(y). Jensen gives

    delta(y)^2 <= E_X tau(<y,X>)^2.

If m>=R^2, then E_q exp(2w)=exp(2||mu||^2/m)<=e^2, and
exp(2w_hat)<=e^2. The elementary inequality

    |exp(a)−exp(b)| <= |a−b|(exp(a)+exp(b))

and Cauchy–Schwarz imply

    A_1 := E_q |exp(w)−exp(w_hat)|
         <= 2e (E_{q,X} tau(<y,X>)^2)^(1/2).

Conditional on X=x, with y independent of X,

    G=<y,x> ~ N(0,sigma_x^2), sigma_x^2=||x||^2/m<=R^2/m.

For sigma>0, exponential Markov and the Gaussian moment generating function give

    E[G^2 1{|G|>1}]
      <= exp(−1/(4sigma^2)) E[G^2 exp(G^2/(4sigma^2))]
      = 2 sqrt(2) sigma^2 exp(−1/(4sigma^2)).

The zero-variance case contributes zero. Because tau(G)^2<=G^2 1{|G|>1},

    E_{q,X} tau(<y,X>)^2
      <= 2 sqrt(2) (R^2/m) exp(−m/(4R^2)).

Consequently

    A_1 <= 2^(7/4) e sqrt(R^2/m) exp(−m/(8R^2))
         <= 10 exp(−m/(8R^2)).

Normalization costs no additional exponential factor: |Z−Z_hat|<=A_1 and

    TV(p_m,p_hat_m)
      <= (1/2)(A_1/Z + |Z−Z_hat|/Z)
      <= A_1/Z <= A_1.

For independent factors, product-TV subadditivity followed by contraction under
the sum map yields

    TV(Law(sum_{i=1}^m Y_i), N(mu,I_d))
      <= sum_i TV(p_hat_m,p_m)
      <= 10m exp(−m/(8R^2)).

This is a full-distribution argument, not a moment or observable calculation.
No selected-range truncation, signed acceptance, unknown density evaluation,
conditional sampler, or unproved Bernoulli factory is used.

For the explicit m above, m>=R^2 and m<=65sL. Also m/(8R^2)>=8L. Therefore

    10m exp(−m/(8R^2)) / epsilon
      <= (650/64) L exp(−7L)
      <= (650/64) exp(−6L) < 1,

where L>=log64 and L<=exp(L). The proposed m is sufficient. The code instead
finds a smaller integer satisfying the same proved inequality on its decreasing
branch m>=8R^2; it does not infer m from empirical diagnostics.
