SIGNED PHASE FEEDBACK: A DIFFUSIVE RESPONSE THEOREM
12 October 2026. Root construction. Internally proved; priority UNKNOWN.

Purpose. The independent inventor's one-sided phase construction has response
scale gamma^(-1/3). Its conjugate two-cycle counterexample has scale gamma^(-1).
Conjugates do not invariably force the latter. Independent random signed phase
updates give an intermediate gamma^(-1/2) operation. This is a scoped operation
for modified dynamics, not an inverse of an arbitrary original process.

1. Model and formula

Let V be finite and P any stochastic matrix, accessed by a local row sampler.
On the compact state space of unit phases x_v and y=exp(i theta), let theta have
generator gamma partial_theta^2. At rate one at each v, independently choose
w~P(v,.) and a fair sign sigma, and replace x_v by y^sigma x_w. All other x
coordinates remain fixed. Fix 0<gamma<=1.

For phi_(v,k)=x_v y^k, k in Z, the exact local identity is

 L phi_(v,k) = -(1+gamma k^2) phi_(v,k)
              +(1/2) sum_w P_vw [phi_(w,k+1)+phi_(w,k-1)].

Let W_n be a P-walk starting at v, and K_n a simple symmetric integer walk
starting at zero, independent of W. Define

 A_n = product_(j=0)^n (1+gamma K_j^2)^(-1),
 h_v(x,y) = sum_(n>=0) E[A_n y^(K_n) x_(W_n)].                 (1)

The expectations in (1) are over the two algorithmic walks, not a stationary
law or an acquired hidden-state replica.

2. Uniform block estimate, including constants

Set L=ceil(sqrt(8/gamma)), and r=253/256. For a charge walk started at any
integer k, set I=sum_(j=0)^(L-1) (k+S_j)^2. Then

 E I=L k^2+L(L-1)/2 >= L k^2+L^2/4.

Using E(k+S_j)^4=k^4+6 k^2 j+3 j^2-2j and Cauchy--Schwarz,

 E I^2 <= L sum_j E(k+S_j)^4
         <= L^2 k^4+3 k^2 L^3+L^4 <=16(E I)^2.

Paley--Zygmund gives P(I>=E I/2)>=1/64. On this event I>=L^2/8, so gamma I>=1.
Since product_j(1+gamma K_j^2)>=1+gamma I,

 E[product_(j=0)^(L-1)(1+gamma K_j^2)^(-2)]
 <=1-(1/64)(1-1/4)=r,

uniformly in the starting charge. Applying the Markov property to consecutive
blocks proves

 E A_n^2 <= r^floor(n/L).                                    (2)

Using n+1 instead of n gives a slightly sharper bound; (2) suffices. In
particular E A_n <= r^(floor(n/L)/2), and the series (1) and its first two theta
derivatives converge uniformly, because |K_n|<=n. Every summand is a finite
linear combination of smooth cylinder functions. The finite-V jump operator
is bounded. Thus h is in the Feller generator domain.

3. Poisson equation and actual integrated response

The weighted-walk Neumann identity telescopes on finite partial sums; applying
L leaves -x_v and a remainder whose sup norm is at most E A_N. By (2) it tends
to zero. Hence

                    -L h_v=x_v.                             (3)

Alternatively, the exact first-moment dual is the continuous-time P/charge
walk, with total jump rate one and killing gamma K_s^2. Its survival dominates
the absolute first moment. Its mean lifetime is sum_n E A_n: at charge K_n its
next holding/killing time has mean 1/(1+gamma K_n^2), and survival through n
previous stages supplies the other factors. This is finite by (2). Duhamel
iteration of the generator identity proves the dual, with the remainder
bounded by a Poisson jump-count tail on each finite interval. Consequently

 h_v(x,y)=integral_0^infinity E_(x,y)[x_v(t)] dt,
 integral_0^infinity |E_(x,y)[x_v(t)]| dt <= C L.              (4)

Stationary laws exist by compactness/Feller continuity. Equation (3) proves
their x_v means vanish. There is no claim that the full process has mixed or
that its stationary law has been computed.

4. Sharp order of the response

The upper bound in (4) is O(gamma^(-1/2)), uniformly over P and V. For the
single-vertex case with x=y=1, all terms in (1) are positive. Since log(1+z)<=z,
Jensen gives

 E A_n >= E exp[-gamma sum_(j=0)^n K_j^2]
         >= exp[-gamma n(n+1)/2].

Summing through n=floor(gamma^(-1/2)) gives a universal positive multiple of
gamma^(-1/2). Thus the worst-case response norm is Theta(gamma^(-1/2)). The
phase itself has relaxation time 1/gamma; the target observable has a shorter
response even though signed charge can return to zero.

5. Exact rational, finite-variance evaluator

Let s=255/256. Independently draw M with P(M=m)=(1-s)s^m and J uniform on
{0,...,L-1}; set N=L M+J. Generate N steps of both walks. Return

 Y=A_N y^(K_N) x_(W_N)/p_N,
 p_N=((1-s)/L)s^M.

Absolute convergence and conditional expectation give E Y=h_v. Equation (2)
gives

 E|Y|^2 =sum_n E[A_n^2 |x_(W_n)|^2]/p_n
          <= L^2/[(1-s)(1-r/s)] =32640 L^2,
 E N=255 L+(L-1)/2 <256 L.

This is an expected row-query count and second-moment bound, not a pointwise
output bound. For rational gamma, exact rational row sampling, and Gaussian-
rational initial phases/coordinates, each output is a finite rational complex
number. Negative powers of unit rational phases are rational conjugates.
Choose L by integer comparisons gamma L^2>=8; no square-root oracle is needed.
The bit sizes of A_N and phase powers are polynomial in N and input bit size.
All moments of N are finite and polynomial in L at fixed order; exact expected
bit work is therefore polynomial under a polynomial-cost row interface.

The constants deliberately favor a short proof over an efficient numerical
method. For fixed absolute error eta, this construction uses O(L^3 eta^-2)
row queries before confidence amplification. It is not a constant-precision,
constant-work algorithm as gamma tends to zero.

6. Interpretation and remaining gate

The one-sided, symmetric-sign, and cancelling two-cycle examples prove three
different response scales: gamma^-1/3, gamma^-1/2, gamma^-1. The controlling
object is the occupation of Fourier charge near zero in a local dual, not
merely graph size, the existence of feedback, or the presence of noise.

These examples do not classify arbitrary edge-dependent signs. A proposed
cycle-charge criterion must charge its acquisition and rule out long zero-
charge traps. A graph with positive and negative cycles can have recurrent or
drifting charge; a nonzero charge on each selected cycle is not by itself a
proof of ballistic charge growth. Physical real-valued/Hermitian couplings
often force conjugate returns. Constructing a useful locally checkable charge-
occupation certificate for such systems is the unresolved major opening.

Prior-art status: stochastic duality, killed random walks, Monte Carlo Neumann
series, harmonic-oscillator Feynman--Kac estimates, and noise-enhanced decay are
established mechanisms. The theorem here is an internally reconstructed
model-specific combination. No original general principle or technological
advantage has been established. See the independent inventor's phase note
and gamma_clock_adversary's primary-source audit for the exact operator-class
collision with one-sided Hill potentials and classical cubic damping.
