Exact setting: finite two-player game G, binary acceptance predicate,
arbitrary joint question distribution mu, independent rounds mu^n.
Let d=|A||B|, ell=log d, v=omega_co(G), epsilon=1-v. For a fixed repeated
commuting strategy, a proper coordinate subset C, p=P[win every C]>0,
write tau=(log(1/p)+|C|ell)/(n-|C|), q=mean remaining conditional win and
alpha=1-q. The production proves

  q <= v+4112 tau^(1/4),
  epsilon <= alpha+65536[sqrt(alpha) tau^(1/4)+sqrt(tau)].

The resulting all-wins bound is
  omega_co(G^n)<=exp[-2^(-75) epsilon^3 n/(epsilon+ell)]
for epsilon>0 and all n>=1. epsilon=0 has the trivial bound 1.
The independent scalar threshold deduction gives, for 0<delta<=epsilon,
  P[W>=ceil((v+delta)n)]
     <= exp[-2^(-78) delta^5 n/(epsilon^2(delta+ell))].
Singleton answer alphabets and all-n endpoints are treated separately.
