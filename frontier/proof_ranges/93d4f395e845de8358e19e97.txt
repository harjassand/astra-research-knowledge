## Broad Morrey/laminate preprint: status held open

Pedregal, *Morrey's conjecture: rank-one convexity implies quasi-convexity for two-dimensional, two-component maps*, arXiv:1905.06571v5 (last revised 18 June 2026), states that every homogeneous two-component planar gradient Young measure is a laminate and hence that rank-one convexity implies quasiconvexity in `2×2`. If correct, its Jensen consequence subsumes the nonnegative FL Jensen inequality for planar periodic gradients; it does not by itself give the FL exact two-term identity or its explicit `mu_F/12` quantitative bound. It does not extend to the FL theorem's general `m×n` range.

The source's stated mechanism is triangulated piecewise-affine approximation, prescribed `(H_n)` rank-one decomposition directions (“molds”), and a set-valued fixed point synchronizing weights between the two scalar components. The load-bearing Proposition 7.1 asserts that each second-component gradient measure can be decomposed along the directions fixed by the first component. Its proof invokes Claim 7.3 (generic full range of a linear operation), then uses a local neighborhood/dimension-matching argument to conclude that the full relevant image fills the target space, followed by density and upper continuity. In the v5 text as reviewed, the generic-rank/surjectivity and dimension-to-full-image steps are asserted in prose; this report does not independently reconstruct the matrix ranks, prove the fixed-point hypotheses in full, or close those implications. This is a precise audit target, not a demonstrated flaw. Accordingly, the sweeping Morrey theorem is recorded here as **the authors' claim, not independently verified and not used as a premise**. No conclusion that it is false is warranted.
