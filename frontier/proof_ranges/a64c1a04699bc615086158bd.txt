# Primary-source prior-art audit

Accessed 2026-10-16. This is a targeted comparison, not exhaustive historical priority clearance. Only primary sources support the dispositions below.

| Result | Primary comparison | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Markov-chain second-moment matrix P_ij²/R_ij and embedded-chain treatment of eventual hitting | [Kuruganti and Strickland (1996), §3.2, equations (6)–(7)](https://www.informs-sim.org/wsc96papers/036.pdf) derives the squared-density transition matrix. Their §3 explicitly removes irrelevant holding times for the hitting probability considered. | Established machinery. |
| Approximate importance-sampling proposals can perform poorly | [Hartmann and Richter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.09606) gives nonasymptotic error bounds in terms of proposal mismatch, with diffusion path examples. | Broad sensitivity claim is prior art; no exact finite-chain threshold equivalence established by this reading. |

Its zero-variance/infinite-duration pathology is not identical to the packet's finite-mean/infinite-second-moment fixture. |
| Work × normalized second moment ≥ squared mean square-root cost, with inverse-square-root-cost optimal proposal | [Cugini, Atif and Subaşı, 13 March 2026, Theorem 1, equations (7), (9), (10)](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13495v1#S2) states exactly this inequality and optimizer for an arbitrary target distribution and cost. | **Direct exact prior art.** Set target law to the success-conditioned full-path law, cost to C, and weight to W/p. No new inequality or optimizer remains. |
| Cost-aware proposal proportional to contribution divided by square-root cost | [2024 wildfire importance-sampling paper](https://www.mdpi.com/2571-6255/7/12/455), search-indexed primary equations (25)–(26), gives this form. The direct March 2026 match above already decides the packet inequality's novelty. |
| Fixed-level splitting normalizing-constant unbiasedness | [2006 Winter Simulation Conference paper](https://people.bordeaux.inria.fr/pierre.delmoral/wsc06split.pdf); [continuous-time unbiased-simulation paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11009-021-09886-2) describes fixed-size survivor resampling as an unbiased SMC variant. The audit independently reconstructs the conditional-expectation argument in `AUDIT.md`. | Standard algorithm property under ideal randomness and valid continuation dynamics. |
