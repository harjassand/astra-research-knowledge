1. CONDITIONAL-FEATURE IDENTITIES

The rational future vector F depends only on (Y0,Yh). For an arbitrary equilibrium competitor let xi(X0)=E[F|X0]. Deterministic emissions imply

  output k+1: xi=(0,0,0,e_k).
Conditional past/future independence given X0 and hidden reversibility identify every reflected statistic with the product of its conditional future features. Consequently
  p0=E[1_output0],
  s2=E[1_output0 |x|^2],
  R_h=E sum_k r_k^2,

where

The last identity is the Markov property for the shifted future cylinder. It assumes no invariance of the competitor's feature span under its generator. It uses neither rates nor derivatives nor hidden cardinality. On marker states r_k=0 identically, because I0, psi_k, F_x and F_y vanish there.

For the nonreversible target, these reflected norm identities are justified separately by the exact projected Gram proof in hidden_realization/v2 Section4: future features belong to H0 and Pi h_A^(Q*)=h_A^Q. 2. POLYGON AND CIRCLE ESTIMATES

The projection estimate dist(x,P)<=3||(l_k(x)_-)_k||_2 is valid. At a projected edge or vertex, the KKT equation is d=-N^T lambda with lambda>=0 and the one or two active facet values at x are Nd. Hence

The exact adjacent-normal inverse bound ||N^-1||_F<3, or the exact reciprocal one-normal bound, gives |lambda|<=3|d|. Inactive negative facets only enlarge the right side.

For every y=sum_i lambda_i v_i in P, choose k with lambda_k>=1/5. The exact minimum chord separation is >5/8. Therefore
  1-|y|^2=sum_(i<j)lambda_i lambda_j |v_i-v_j|^2
       >=lambda_k sum_(j!=k)lambda_j |v_k-v_j|^2
       >=(1/8)sum_(j!=k)lambda_j |v_k-v_j|
       >=|y-v_k|/8.
This proves dist(y,{v_i})<=8(1-|y|^2). It is a linear defect estimate, not a square-root approximation.

The five-vector polynomial remainder obeys

3. ROUNDING, SYMMETRIC COUPLING, AND THE LINEAR WITNESS

Round y=proj_P x to a nearest vertex v and use the exact field Z_v; use field zero on markers. The two-time hidden feature law is symmetric. Exact monotonicity at the ten canonical frame points and ||Z_i-Z_j||_2<=6 give
