ASTRA Primitive Genesis — Research result
This run produced a conditional entropy theorem, an exact counterexample to its most natural missing hypothesis, and an infinite family showing that the obstruction cannot be repaired by a constant-factor adjustment. It did not establish the requested foundational breakthrough.

The mechanism investigated is:

Construct globally constrained dynamics whose individual coordinates nevertheless follow exactly prescribed, low-entropy processes. Use their stationary distributions to bound the information capacity of the entire quotient space—without requiring independence.

The construction works under a precise compatibility condition. The unresolved issue is whether the full structure of an irredundant hyperplane cover supplies that condition. Merely having enough legal moves does not: I constructed an explicit counterexample.

I compared four directions before selecting the mechanism developed below.

Resolution of singularities in positive characteristic	Construct a descending invariant that remains informative when blowups increase residual order.	No valid replacement invariant emerged. I did not construct a uniformly controlled transformation. This remained an exploratory opportunity rather than a result.
