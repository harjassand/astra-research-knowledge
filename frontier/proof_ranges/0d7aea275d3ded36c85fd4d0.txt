\section{Why low rank does not supply free dimension credit}
If a unitary on a larger space compresses exactly to an already unitary $U_s$ on the original space, the original space is invariant: equality of norms forces its off-diagonal block to vanish. Exact compression for every generator therefore cannot repair any nonzero relator defect. Generator edits are necessary.

Even allowing edits, a universal low-rank completion theorem is false. A standard obstruction uses $\mathrm{BS}(2,3)=\langle a,t\mid ta^2t^{-1}=a^3\rangle$. The word $v=[a,tat^{-1}]$ is nontrivial by Britton's lemma but trivial in every finite quotient. Indeed, if $a$ has finite order $d$, equality of the orders of $a^2,a^3$ forces $\gcd(d,6)=1$; the defining relation then makes $tat^{-1}$ a power of $a$.

Every finite-dimensional unitary representation also kills $v$, by residual finiteness of finitely generated linear groups in characteristic zero. Soficity of Baumslag--Solitar groups supplies permutation approximations with defining-relator rank $o(n)$ but $\|v(U)-I\|_{2,n}\to\sqrt2$. Cornulier gives the soficity input explicitly \cite{Cornulier}; the precise finite-residual and linear-group source interfaces are recorded in the adversarial supplement.

For completeness, the resulting dimension accounting is elementary. Suppose exact enlarged generators $V_s$ act on dimension $n+m$, and their upper-left compressions differ from $U_s$ by at most $\delta$ in norm normalized by $n$. Padding $U_s$ by $I_m$ gives
\[
\|U_s\oplus I_m-V_s\|_{2,n}^2\leq\delta^2+6m/n:
\]
the two cross blocks contribute at most $2m/n$ and the lower-right block at most $4m/n$. Telescoping the length-eight word $v$ yields
\[
\sqrt2-o(1)\leq8\sqrt{\delta^2+6m/n}.
\]
Thus $m=o(n)$ forces $\delta\geq1/(4\sqrt2)-o(1)$. This rules out generic completion of $o(n)$ rank debt, not completion for a specially justified high-rank group class. The qualitative obstruction is established mathematics, not a novelty claim of this campaign.
