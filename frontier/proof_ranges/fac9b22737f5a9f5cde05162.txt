## Proof audit

For an arbitrary coefficient-convergent approximating sequence write

$$
H_n=\sum_i c_{ni}h_{S_{ni}}+P_n,\qquad h_S(A)=F(AS),
$$

where c_ni are nonnegative and P_n is polyconvex. Quartic homogeneity lets us normalize every nonzero S_ni to Frobenius norm one by absorbing its fourth power into c_ni. Singular matrices occur only in the compact closure of this normalized orbit.

Define

$$
\ell(K)=D^2K(B_0)[a_0\otimes b_0,a_0\otimes b_0]
$$

and let Lambda(K) sum the two probes D²K(B0)[a0⊗e_j,a0⊗e_j] in an orthonormal input basis. The chain rule gives

$$
D^2h_S(B_0)[a_0\otimes b,a_0\otimes b]
=D^2F(B_0S)[a_0\otimes(S^Tb),a_0\otimes(S^Tb)].
$$

Since ||B0 S||_F >= sigma_min(B0)||S||_F, the strict bound gives, with m=c sigma_min(B0)^2 |a0|^2 > 0,

$$
\ell(h_S)\ge m|S^Tb_0|^2,\qquad
\Lambda(h_S)\ge m\sum_{j=1}^2|S^Te_j|^2=m.
$$

The same probes are nonnegative on every homogeneous polyconvex quartic: restriction to a rank-one line is convex, and the quartic is C². Positivity of all conic weights and summand probes, coefficient convergence, and ell(H)=0 imply

$$
\sum_i c_{ni}\le \Lambda(H_n)/m=O(1),\qquad
\sum_i c_{ni}|S_{ni}^Tb_0|^2\le \ell(H_n)/m\longrightarrow0.
$$

Thus orbit mass is bounded and concentrates at

$$
Z=\{S:||S||_F=1,\ S^Tb_0=0\}.
$$

For any neighborhood U of Z, compactness gives eta_U=min_{S outside U, ||S||_F=1}|S^T b0|>0. Therefore mass outside U is at most ell(H_n)/(m eta_U²) and tends to zero. This quantitatively closes the possible escape to singular right transforms.

Every S in Z has rank one. Its columns lie in the one-dimensional space b0-perpendicular, and normalization excludes rank zero. Write S=v⊗w. Then AS=(Av)⊗w, and every difference of two such arguments has rank at most one. The strict Hessian assumption implies rank-one convexity of F. Its restriction to the subspace {z⊗w} is therefore ordinary convex, so h_S(A)=F(AS) is convex in A. For every finite law, its Jensen functional j_mu(S)=E_mu h_S(A)-h_S(Abar) is nonnegative on Z.

For fixed finite mu, j_mu is continuous and bounded on the compact normalized sphere. Given epsilon>0, choose U so its negative part is at most epsilon on U. Bounded total orbit mass controls the contribution from U; boundedness of j_mu and vanishing mass outside U control the complement. Hence liminf_n sum_i c_ni j_mu(S_ni)>=0.

For each polyconvex P_n, the lifted mean condition E A=Abar and E det A=det Abar allows ordinary convex Jensen in (A,det A), giving J_mu(P_n)>=0. Finally, J_mu is continuous in the finite-dimensional quartic coefficient space because mu has finite support. Passing to H_n -> H proves the claim for every such law. No compactness of the P_n or closedness of the polyconvex subcone is needed.

This checks the assignment's specific proof steps: the chain-rule direction is S^T b; invertible B0 supplies a uniform lower bound; the null set is rank one because the input dimension is two; limiting atoms are convex; and arbitrary finite lifted-barycenter laws enter by Jensen. The proof would not supply these estimates for signed orbit combinations, a zero probe, singular B0, or a generator lacking the strict estimate.
