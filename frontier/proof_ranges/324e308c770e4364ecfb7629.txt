## 9. Reproducible checks

Run `python verify.py`. The script uses NumPy, SciPy, and SymPy, and writes `verification_results.json`.

The recorded run checked:

- 120 random matrix and parabolic first-jet cases in dimensions 2, 3, 5, and 8;
- equality cases for the sharp local coefficient at five spectral widths;
- 36 nonzero-final-primitive trajectory comparisons;
- five pulse amplitudes against the exact spectral-radius formula;
- the determinant and trace identity in (8) by exact symbolic rational arithmetic.

All assertions passed in the recorded run. The largest relative discrepancy in the transformed-generator identity was approximately `1.38e-15`. These finite calculations test algebraic implementation and examples; they do not constitute a proof of the universal theorem, an independent review, a PDE simulation, or a formal verification.

## 10. Priority and significance boundary

Known starting machinery: the exponential derivative formula, Fer factorization, symmetric/skew-symmetric decomposition, the matrix logarithmic-norm energy argument, scalar maximum principles, quadratic-form energy estimates, and fluid continuation criteria.

The candidate contribution to be checked for priority is the particular spectral-width-sharp double-commutator growth bound, its integrated tensor comparison, and the canonical parabolic-primitive formulation with an explicit diffusion penalty. The presence of a derivation in this note does not establish historical originality. Selective searches did not resolve whether an equivalent result is already present in matrix evolution, geometric integration, weighted parabolic systems, or fluid-regularity literature.

The strongest retained result is an internally proved, cancellation-sensitive growth and continuation estimate with sharp matrix-level obstructions. The independently major consequence is missing: a noncircular estimate for the new geometric quantities in the actual nonlinear problem.
