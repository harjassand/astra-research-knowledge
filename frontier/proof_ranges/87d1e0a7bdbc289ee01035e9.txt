6. Why arbitrary active port controls do not defeat the construction
Scalar moment matching is not enough for this step. The proof bounds the entire controlled quantum experiment.
Let \(B_{xy}=|J_{xy}|\) be the hopping adjacency matrix. Its row sums are at most one.
For a purified state, define
\[
p_x(t)=\langle n_x\rangle,\qquad q_x(t)=\sqrt{p_x(t)}.
\]
Outside the controlled ports, the control Hamiltonians and all density interactions commute with \(n_x\). Cauchy–Schwarz gives
\[
|\dot p_x|
\le
2\sum_yB_{xy}\sqrt{p_xp_y},
\]
and therefore, with the standard limiting interpretation at \(p_x=0\),
\[
\dot q_x\le\sum_yB_{xy}q_y.
\]
Since the outside starts empty and \(q_x\le1\) on controlled sites,
\[
q_x(t)
\le
(e^{tB}\mathbf1_S)_x
\le
Q_R(t):=\sum_{k\ge R}\frac{t^k}{k!}
\]
at an endpoint distance \(R\) away. For \(t\le R/2\),
\[
\boxed{Q_R(t)\le\frac{2t^R}{R!}.}
\tag{11}
\]
This holds uniformly over all port inputs, entangled memories, injected excitations and port-control strengths.
Let \(V_g(t),V_b(t)\) be the forward isometries describing the complete controlled experiments, retaining all measurement records. The two generators differ only by
\[
H_b-H_g=c_\delta(n_{L_d}-n_{R_d}),
\qquad c_\delta=(1+\delta)/2.
\]
Common control operations preserve cross overlaps. Between controls,
\[
\frac{d}{dt}(V_g^\dagger V_b)
=
iV_g^\dagger(H_g-H_b)V_b.
\]
At either endpoint,
\[
\|V_g^\dagger n_xV_b\|
\le
\|n_xV_g\|\,\|n_xV_b\|
\le Q_R(t)^2.
\]
Consequently, a complete experiment of duration \(u\le\tau\) satisfies
\[
\boxed{
\|I-V_g(u)^\dagger V_b(u)\|
\le
\frac{8u^{2R+1}}{(2R+1)(R!)^2}
=:\zeta(u).
}
\tag{12}
\]
The squared arrival amplitude is the important information bound. It includes the full quantum output, not just the observed local correlator.
After \(N_{\mathrm{exp}}\) adaptive fresh experiments, purified overlap bookkeeping gives
\[
D_{\mathrm{final}}^2
\le
\min\{1,\,2N_{\mathrm{exp}}\zeta(\tau)\}.
\]
Distinguishing the hypotheses with success probability \(2/3\) requires \(D_{\mathrm{final}}\ge1/3\). Substitution proves (2).
The duration bound follows by using
\[
\zeta(u)
\le
\frac{8\tau^{2R}}{(2R+1)(R!)^2}\,u
\]
in each adaptive history block. The complete bookkeeping is in the note.
This proves the active observation bound independently of the earlier Genesis or quantum-snapshot arguments.
