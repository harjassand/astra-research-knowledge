## 3. Exact closed heat certificate for F*

Let z=(z0,...,z5) and form compatible second jets

    B1=[[z0,z1],[z3,z4]], B2=[[z1,z2],[z4,z5]].

Let Z=(a z0,...,a z5,b z0,...,d z5)ᵀ. The complete rational certificate is `checks/FSTAR_CLOSED_HEAT_CERTIFICATE.json`, SHA256

    b4f049d37ccdfc28257dd66e714cc4822e1d35f39f1a53e5fce607f0375d6ec2.

It supplies γ=83/1000, a symmetric rational 24-by-24 R, and a homogeneous-quadratic two-form ω, with the exact polynomial identity

    Zᵀ R Z = Σ_i D²(F*−γq²)(A)[Bi,Bi] + ω_A(B1,B2),
    dω=0,  R−(1/500)I > 0.                         (1)

### Analytic implication, including the boundary and infinity steps

Put G=F*−γq². Because ω is quadratic and closed, Cartan's formula with the Euler field gives the global polynomial primitive α=(1/4)i_Euler ω. For v(x)=A0x+ψ(x), ψ Schwartz, (Dv)*ω=d((Dv)*α). Every coefficient of the pulled-back one-form contains a derivative of Dv and is rapidly decreasing. Therefore its exterior derivative has zero integral over R². Integrating (1) gives

    ∫ Σ_i D²G(Dv)[∂i Dv,∂i Dv] ≥ 0.              (2)

For φ smooth and compactly supported, let φ_t=e^(tΔ)φ and A_t=A0+Dφ_t. The relative energy

    E(t)=∫[G(A_t)−G(A0)−DG(A0):Dφ_t]

is finite. Integration by parts gives E'(t)=−∫Σ_iD²G(A_t)[∂i A_t,∂i A_t]≤0. Quartic growth bounds its integrand by a constant times |A0|²|Dφ_t|²+|A0||Dφ_t|³+|Dφ_t|⁴. In dimension two the heat estimates ||Dφ_t||_p≤C_p t^(−1+1/p)||Dφ||_1 for p=2,3,4 make E(t)→0 as t→∞. Strong heat continuity in these spaces and Hölder's inequality give E(t)→E(0) at zero. Thus E(0)≥0. Since ∫Dφ=0, this is quasiconvexity for G.

Approximation in W^(1,4), together with the quartic difference bound C(|A|+|B|)³|A−B|, passes the result to every bounded Lipschitz domain and every W^(1,∞)_0 perturbation. Finally q is a null Lagrangian: the mean determinant of A0+Dφ is q(A0). Jensen therefore proves quasiconvexity of γq² for γ≥0. Consequently F* is quasiconvex.

This reconstructs the heat/exact-form mechanism already present in [Bruno–Pasqualotto, sections 2–3](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06367v1) and N740. The new finite operation is the actual closed positive certificate for this orientation-sensitive input, not invention of that general analytic method.

### Uniform Jensen gap

Let H(A)=|A|⁴ and ρ=1/500. The compatible jet convention gives Σ|Bi|²≤2|z|², while

    Σ D²H(A)[Bi,Bi]≤12|A|²Σ|Bi|²≤24|A|²|z|².

Thus (1) also certifies quasiconvexity of G−(ρ/24)H and hence F*−(ρ/24)H. The elementary inequality

    H(A+B)−H(A)−DH(A):B ≥ (1/3)|B|⁴

follows, after |B|=1 normalization, by completing the square 6(t+1/3)²+2s²+1/3, where t=A:B and s²=|A|²−t². It follows that for every admitted Ω,A0,φ,

    ∫Ω [F*(A0+Dφ)−F*(A0)] ≥ (1/36000)∫Ω |Dφ|⁴. (3)

The same conclusion holds for periodic perturbations by the periodic version of the heat argument, or by tiling and cutoff. The old Fourier searches for a negative F* gap are now mathematically excluded in their stated compatible scope.
