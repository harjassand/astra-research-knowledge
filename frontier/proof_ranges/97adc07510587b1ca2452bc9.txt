## 6. Optional sharper all-identifier bound

If q equiprobable accessible states have all pairwise trace distances <=D, then
every q-label measurement has average success <=[1+(q-1)D]/q. Indeed fix a
reference j, sum Tr(rho_j E_i)=1, and bound each i!=j difference by D.
Averaging is unnecessary because the bound holds for each reference. Hence its
worst-label error is at least (q-1)(1-D)/q. For q=3 and the source's hard-cap
distance D=sin(sqrt(3)pi/4), this gives a stronger error bound

    (2/3)[1-sin(sqrt(3)pi/4)] > 0.01470821568954.

This elementary multiway bound is an internally reconstructed improvement of
the *bound*, not a claim of exact optimal identification error or a new general
discrimination theorem. The producer's weaker 0.011031 bound remains valid.
