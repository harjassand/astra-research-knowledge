### 8.4 Depolarizing channels in every dimension

For
\[
\mathcal D_\lambda(X)=\lambda X+(1-\lambda)\operatorname{Tr}(X)I_d/d,
\qquad -1/(d^2-1)\le\lambda\le1,
\]
for \(d\ge2\), unitary covariance and convexity make \(\sigma=I_d/d\) optimal. The one-dimensional channel is a replacer and has coefficient zero. The Choi eigenvalues are
\([1+(d^2-1)\lambda]/d\) once and \((1-\lambda)/d\) with multiplicity \(d^2-1\). Substitution and simplification give
\[
\boxed{\qquad
\eta(\mathcal D_\lambda)=
\frac{(d^2-1)\lambda^2}{1+(d^2-2)\lambda}.
\qquad} \tag{8.7}
\]
The singular endpoints follow from (8.2), or directly from the displayed formula. For a qubit and \(\lambda=1/2\), the value is \(3/8\). The exact rational Gram and inverse-Choi certificates for this case are included in exact_depolarizing_certificate.py.
