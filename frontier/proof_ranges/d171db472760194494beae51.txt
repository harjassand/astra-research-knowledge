The population identity is not an exact-zero detector from finite data. The finite procedure returns a confidence bound and tests separated hypotheses such as
\[ \Gamma=0 \quad\text{versus}\quad \operatorname{tr}\Gamma\ge g>0. \]
Normalize time so the supplied microscopic exit-rate bound is one. Assume every observed label has probability at least \(\rho>0\).
Allow independent measurement noise through a known full-rank confusion channel. Let its corrected indicators be bounded in absolute value by \(\kappa\). A singular or uncalibrated sensor is not covered.
For desired operator error \(\eta\), one conservative protocol uses
\[ u=\frac{\eta\rho^2}{100k^2}, \qquad s=\frac{u}{1000}. \]
It measures stationary probabilities and two-time statistics at \(s,2s\), plus terminal means at those times under the four fixed fields.
The time-curvature estimator is
\[ \frac{m(2s)-2m(s)+m(0)}{s^2}. \]
Under the four fields, exit rates are at most two, so its bias is bounded using a third-time-derivative bound of \(64\). Combining this with (5), bounded-observation concentration, and explicit propagation through \(M^{-1}\), gives
\[ \boxed{ N_{\mathrm{trials}} = O\!\left( k^2\kappa^4u^{-6}\log\frac{k}{\zeta} \right) } \tag{15} \]
for failure probability at most \(\zeta\).
This is a conservative polynomial upper bound, not an optimized or practically small sample estimate. Neither hidden state count nor minimum microscopic stationary probability appears.
Stationary starts are not free. A supplied mixing bound
\[ \sup_\nu\operatorname{TV}(\nu e^{tQ},\pi) \le M_0e^{-\gamma t} \]
permits waiting
\[ \gamma^{-1} \log\frac{4M_0N_{\mathrm{trials}}}{\zeta} \]
between trials. Total experimental time includes those waits. Under (8), a reset coupling supplies \(\gamma=\lambda\).
Time, sensor, and field-setting precision must fit the stated error budget. The required precision is inverse-polynomial, with logarithmic encoding length. There is also a direct kinetic-calibration tolerance: if the four implemented generators differ from their targets by row-operator norm at most \(\delta_K\), then the induced certificate error is bounded by
\[ \boxed{ \|\Delta\Gamma\|_{\mathrm{op}} < 50k\Lambda\delta_K. } \tag{16} \]
Thus the active conclusion does not depend on exact algebraic concealment. It tolerates bounded model and intervention errors relative to a nonzero certificate margin.
After estimating \(L\), its rate error must also be charged. If
\[ \max_a\sum_{b\ne a}|\widehat L_{ab}-L_{ab}| \le\delta_L, \]
add \(Te^H\delta_L\) to the path bound, together with initial-distribution error. For the residual, use
\[ \operatorname{tr}\Gamma \le \operatorname{tr}\widehat\Gamma+k\eta. \]
