## 2. What survives reconstruction

**The task really changes.** The new performance is finite-device stationary
throughput J on four hard-core sites, not the infinite-chain second-moment
coefficient D in N768. The new all-identifier comparison repairs an interface
limitation of the earlier *kind of argument* on a different example. It does
not discharge N768's dictionary, infinite-system acquisition or generality gates.

**Minimal conflict reduction is valid with its quantifiers retained.** All
acceptable sets must be subsets of the same admitted action universe. Their
intersection is empty, and every proper subfamily has a common action. Map each
action u to one i with u not in G_i: under true model i, the exclusion error event
is contained in the decision-error event. Conversely, minimality supplies u_i
acceptable to every model except i. Applying u_i after exclusion outcome i
preserves the entire error vector. Hence the minimax errors agree for each fixed
acquisition strategy and after optimizing over the same acquisition class. This
does not imply that pairwise overlaps form an equivalence relation, nor that
finding every conflict or its useful loss is inexpensive.

**The payoff certificate is a paid, finite full-state calculation.** Neutral
occupation-pair coordinates have dimension 70. Trace replacement plus the exact
left-inverse condition ||RA-I||<=q<1 establishes an inverse bound; the floating
R and polynomial trial are proposals only. The checker includes the complete
residual polynomial above trial degree, the rational sqrt(3) surrogate error and
the phase Taylor tail. The trace functional has norm sqrt(8)<3. The scalar
certificate follows from ||A^-1||<=||R||/(1-q). Twirling ensures that every full
steady state has the same neutral throughput, even without proving uniqueness
in every nonneutral charge block. There is no supplied spectral gap or hidden
replacement by a one-particle output. Full-density diagnostics are separate.

Shared polynomial subtraction retains nuisance correlations. The positive
ordering margins prove g_4 is the largest of six response types throughout the
four-parameter box; they do not optimize continuous flux/drain controls. The
fixed-probe anti-trine measurement has its stated analytic dual witness, and
payoff-operator domination permits reassignment of drain-2/8 outcomes to drain4.
The same measurement is optimal for each admitted nuisance value on this probe;
no optimality over all preparations or acquisition schedules follows.

**The all-adaptive bound survives a careful hard-cap reading.** On the full Fock
space, changing two trine labels changes only a hopping block with eigenvalues
plus/minus sqrt(3), repeated over spectator configurations, and zeros. The
half-spectral-range bound is sqrt(3). Divide evolution into unknown-query slots:
known controls, feedback record processing and common Markovian channels are
label independent and contract the Bures angle. Purifying measurements and
padding idle branches gives the same bound for adaptive schedules under a hard
total exposure cap. Serialize parallel specimens and charge their summed
exposures. This yields A_B<=sqrt(3)T and trace distance<=sin(sqrt(3)T) while the
angle is below pi/2. Helstrom then gives the reported binary obstruction, which
applies to a uniformly successful three-label identifier after coarse graining.
It is not an expected-time, wall-time or uncharged multiprobe statement.
