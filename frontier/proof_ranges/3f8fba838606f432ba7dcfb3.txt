For every family P(g) of m-by-m row-stochastic matrices, define an operator on (R^3)^m by

\[
 (\mathcal T_Pb)_j=\sum_g\nu(g)\sum_iP(g)_{ij}gb_i,
 \qquad \|b\|_{1,2}=\sum_i\|b_i\|.
\]

Let

\[
 F_m(T)=\sup_{P_1,\ldots,P_T}
 \|\mathcal T_{P_T}\cdots\mathcal T_{P_1}\|_{1,2},\quad F_m(0)=1,
 \qquad
 \rho_m=\lim_{T\to\infty}F_m(T)^{1/T}.
 \tag{1}
\]

The supremum includes arbitrary time-inhomogeneous stochastic kernels; the current gate may enter the whole transition matrix. The matrices are not required to commute, be reversible, or have any fixed stationary distribution. The family of such operators is compact and bounded. Since F_m is submultiplicative, the limit is the infimum of the displayed roots. This is an ordinary joint spectral radius for a particular structured family, not a new definition of spectral radius.
