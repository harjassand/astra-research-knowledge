## Moment-environment results: exact surviving scope

The signed-band theorem uses mass W and first absolute-energy moment T. I
reconstructed its mean-bath compression, filling complementary negative-energy
modes before subtracting the uncoupled bath reference. That reference is
essential: the retained negative block is `Tr B-x†Bx`. The endpoint dilation
has spectrum at the band endpoints; compressing added empty/filled modes returns
the normalized original Hamiltonian. Rayleigh–Ritz therefore gives both ground
energy inequalities. Because the bounding baths realize the same moments, the
extrema are exact within the declared information class. A prescribed geometry,
fixed bath dimension or canonical charge changes that class.

The symmetric theorem instead uses W and the squared-energy moment S. Its
matrix inverse Jensen bound follows from a positive2-by-2 block integral and
Schur complement; its upper bound follows from the scalar chord integrated
against a positive matrix measure. No exchange of T and S is legal.

For the causal argument, the finite positive-temperature Gibbs state makes the
anticommutator operator inner product positive. CAR orthonormalizes the linear
annihilation fields. The Liouvillian `[X,H]` is self-adjoint in that inner
product. Bath annihilator commutators are exactly linear only because the bath
is quadratic. Thus the orthogonal-complement self-energy has no bath row or
column; its retarded sign gives

    0<=-Im G(iomega)<=(omega I+Gamma(omega))^-1.

The physical direct-sum interpolation keeps the impurity Hamiltonian fixed.
Its partition-function derivative then has the stated negative sign. Integrating
the resolvent trace yields the log-determinant width. The free resonant impurity
attains it, so the uniform width cannot be improved merely by using a different
correlated impurity solver. This does not compute the correlated reference value.
Grand-canonical minima, genuine quadratic-bath qualification, moment acquisition,
reference solver intervals and physical model error remain separate obligations.

I did not repeat the producer's rational secular-root computation or every
producer replay. Their evidence is assessed by the responsible audit agent.
My separately authored tests include interacting impurities and complex rank-one
matrix hybridization, but are finite diagnostics rather than another proof of
the universal theorem.
