Result
------
For every r in the closed interval I=[-21/8,-5/2], define

  u=a^2+c^2, v=b^2+d^2, j=ad-bc,
  H=(u+v-2j)^2,
  F_(alpha,r)=alpha H + r(r-1)u^2/6 + j^2 + r u j.

Then

  F_(alpha,r) is quasiconvex
    if and only if it is rank-one convex
    if and only if alpha >= alpha_*(r):=3r/[8(r+2)].

The new load-bearing result here is a rational compatible-heat certificate at
alpha=alpha_*(r) throughout I. The previous source certificate required
alpha=3 (or a subtraction improvement of 3/16000), so it did not settle this
boundary. Here alpha_*(r) ranges from 63/40 to 15/8. This is a theorem on the
saturated beta slice above, not the full nine-coefficient invariant cone or
the general planar Morrey problem.

Exact algebra has been checked by a portable SymPy verifier. External review,
formal proof verification, and historical priority remain unresolved. The
proof uses the established heat-flow/exact-two-form method of Bruno and
Pasqualotto; that method is not claimed as a new contribution.
