## 7. Prior work and the significance that is justified

Computational complexity of gradient-descent trajectories is not itself a new subject. Chatziafratis, Roughgarden and Wang established that **online gradient descent can encode arbitrary polynomial-space computations**, including simple learning settings. A claim that this investigation discovered computational universality of gradient descent in general would therefore be wrong. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.01280)

Welper’s examined neural-training universality construction explicitly uses **stop-gradient operations** and a **conditional-gradient update on a simplex**. Those differ materially from differentiating one actual scalar objective and applying ordinary full-batch Euclidean gradient descent, as done here. They do not, by themselves, establish priority for the present theorem. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2007.13664v1)

The specific combination derived here is:

**an explicit gradient-exchange construction; a single rational strongly convex squared-ReLU loss; conditioning arbitrarily close to one; globally contractive ordinary gradient descent; fixed-gap undecidability of its actual loss-decay exponent; and an unconditional finite-horizon prediction barrier.**

The reusable mathematical mechanism is the extension outside the valid computational region that restores the reciprocal structure required by a scalar potential. The consequential result is the separation between **easy optimisation** and **hard prediction of its actual convergence rate**.

This goes beyond a representation counterexample: it supplies explicit reductions for numerical prediction tasks with fixed output accuracy. It does not supply a faster general-purpose solver, establish robustness at fixed physical precision, or demonstrate a practical advantage for learning systems.

**The construction and reductions are the research result reached here. Historical novelty, independent correctness and the mission’s transformative significance have not yet been established; I am not presenting this as completion of the broader mission.**
