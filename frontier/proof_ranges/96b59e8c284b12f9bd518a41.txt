## 8. Proof, checking and missing measurements

The written proofs establish the conditional mathematical statements. The
independent checker constructs 900 completion vectors, checks a full joint
GKSL countermodel, an eight-dimensional encoded-cat countermodel, finite
envelope matching, a rank-one postselection failure and 160 finite
history-tree lower tails. It also independently replays the carrier algebra and
integrates spatial pulse unitaries by adaptive quadrature. These finite floats
are diagnostic evidence, not interval-certified proofs of universal claims.

Physical closure requires the packet's actual common-dilation response,
complete new-sequence ancilla quadrature map and retained contrast, pulse
envelopes/spectral phase, reset distribution, accepted/attempted counts,
branch-dependent survival/chemical identity, and matched spectroscopy comparator.
None is acquired here. The survival theorem pays unknown erasure; it does not
make a destructive experiment nondestructive or remove the shot budget.
