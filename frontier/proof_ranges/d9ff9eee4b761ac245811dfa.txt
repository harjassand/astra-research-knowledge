Research date: 15 October 2026. Worker: nonlinear_qc / phase_geometry.
Repository source pin: `b7cada92d2eb304578be5ed974f1e44b10a89928`.

## Result and status

The particular retained quartic G is quasiconvex at **every** affine background.
The proof uses a rational closed two-form and a rational positive semidefinite
Gram identity for the full compatible second-jet density. No prescribed phase,
Cartesian partial minimum, frequency truncation, or numerical search conclusion
is a premise. The same G is non-polyconvex and lies on an acoustic boundary.

This is a complete internal proof with independently checkable finite algebra.

and the positive reduced LDL pivots using a separate Fraction-based checker.
That is same-team checking, not external mathematical review. Historical novelty

## 1. Integrand, domain and complete statement

For `A=[[a,b],[c,d]]`, set

    u=a^2+c^2,    v=b^2+d^2,    j=ad-bc,
    G(A)=3(u+v-2j)^2+(35/24)u^2+j^2-(5/2)ju.             (1.1)

Theorem. For every bounded Lipschitz domain `Omega` in R2, every constant
matrix `B in R^(2x2)`, and every `phi in W_0^(1,4)(Omega,R2)`,

    integral_Omega G(B+Dphi) >= |Omega| G(B).             (1.2)

In particular, this proves the usual quasiconvexity condition, whose test class
is W0^(1,infinity). The periodic counterpart also holds: for every periodic
`phi in W^(1,4)(T2,R2)` and every B,

    <G(B+Dphi)> >= G(B).                                 (1.3)

All input frequencies, nonlinear resonances, amplitudes and phases are included.

## 3. Why the correction has zero integral

Let E be the radial vector field on R4. Because omega has quadratic coefficients
and form degree two, its total Euler degree is four. Cartan's formula and
closedness give the global polynomial primitive

    alpha=(1/4) i_E omega,    d alpha=omega.               (3.1)

The standalone verifier also checks this formula directly, component by
component, so no topological existence argument is hidden here.

For `w(x)=Bx+psi(x)` with psi Schwartz, put `A(x)=Dw(x)`. Pullback commutes
with exterior differentiation:

    A^*omega=d(A^*alpha).

Every coefficient of A^*alpha contains a derivative of A. Since A-B and all
its positive-order derivatives are rapidly decreasing, A^*alpha is rapidly
decreasing. Stokes' theorem on expanding squares, or integration of a rapidly
decreasing divergence, therefore gives

    integral_R2 omega_A(partial_x A,partial_y A)=0.       (3.2)

The same identity holds on a torus by Stokes, since A and its pullback primitive
are periodic. The map w itself need not be periodic when B is nonzero.

Its second jets have the displayed compatible pattern because mixed partials
commute. Integrating (2.2) and using (3.2) yields

    integral [d1 D^2G(Dw)[partial_x Dw,partial_x Dw]
              +d2 D^2G(Dw)[partial_y Dw,partial_y Dw]]
       = integral Z^T Q Z >=0.                          (3.3)

This controls the full nonlinear jet density together. It is not obtained by
requiring the uncorrected density to be pointwise nonnegative.

## 4. Global compatible energy decrease under anisotropic heat

First take `phi in C_c^infinity(R2,R2)` and a constant B. Define

    phi_t=exp[t(d1 partial_xx+d2 partial_yy)] phi,
    A_t=B+Dphi_t,
    E(t)=integral_R2 [G(A_t)-G(B)-DG(B):Dphi_t].            (4.1)

The positive constants (2.1) give an ordinary uniformly elliptic Gaussian heat
kernel. For t>0, phi_t is Schwartz. Quartic growth makes E(t) finite. Integration
by parts, the constant diffusion coefficients and (3.3) give

    E'(t)
      =-integral [d1 D^2G(A_t)[partial_x A_t,partial_x A_t]
                  +d2 D^2G(A_t)[partial_y A_t,partial_y A_t]]
      =-integral Z_t^T Q Z_t <=0.                        (4.2)

All fields remain actual gradients throughout this flow. The affine background
B is preserved; no boundary interpolation or independently chosen gradient
law is introduced.

For p=2,3,4 the anisotropic Gaussian and Young's convolution inequality give

    ||Dphi_t||_p <= C(D,p) t^(-(1-1/p)) ||Dphi||_1.       (4.3)

Since G is quartic,

    |G(B+H)-G(B)-DG(B):H|
       <= C_G (|B|^2 |H|^2+|B| |H|^3+|H|^4).            (4.4)

Equations (4.3)-(4.4) show E(t)->0 as t->infinity. Strong continuity of the heat
semigroup in L2,L3,L4 and the quartic difference estimate show E(t)->E(0) as
t decreases to zero. Since E is nonincreasing with limit zero,

    E(0)>=0.

The constant linear term integrates to zero because `integral Dphi=0`. This
proves the Jensen inequality for every smooth compactly supported perturbation
of every B.

For a Lipschitz domain Omega and phi in W0^(1,4), choose smooth compactly
supported approximants in Omega converging strongly in W1,4; equivalently,
zero-extend and use the defining W0 density. The quartic difference bound

    |G(A)-G(C)|<=C_G(|A|+|C|)^3 |A-C|

and Holder's inequality pass the Jensen inequality to the limit. This proves
(1.2). On the torus, the same calculation uses periodic heat, whose nonzero
gradient modes decay to zero, and periodic Stokes. Approximation proves (1.3).

## 5. Exact non-polyconvexity and boundary scope

Direct substitution gives

    G(I)=-1/24,    G(diag(1,-1))=1271/24.

Let J0=diag(1,-1). Give each of `+36I,-36I` mass `1/[2(1+36^2)]`, and each
of `+J0,-J0` mass `36^2/[2(1+36^2)]`. Then

    E A=0,    E det A=0,
    E G(A)=[36^4(-1/24)+36^2(1271/24)]/(1+36^2)
           =-1350/1297<0.                               (5.1)

If G were convex as a function of `(A,det A)`, Jensen at the lifted barycenter
would force the expectation to be at least G(0)=0. Thus G is non-polyconvex.
This finite law is not a gradient law. Its negative value does not contradict
the quasiconvexity theorem just proved.

Moreover,

    G(I+t e12)=-1/24+3t^4.                               (5.2)

The quadratic acoustic curvature there is zero. For every epsilon>0,
`G-epsilon |A|^4` has negative rank-one second derivative at `(I,e12)` and is
therefore not rank-one convex or quasiconvex. No uniform strict subtraction
margin is claimed. The new result includes this acoustic-boundary integrand;
it does not promote it to the strict Fstar regime.
