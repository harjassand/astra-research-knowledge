## Initial discarded opening

Before the Gaussian task was assigned, this branch tested one-pass event-pivotal
sensitivity for additive interacting particle systems: a time-expanded event
DAG identifies event deletions that remove every source-to-target path; Poisson
add/drop identities turn pivotal counts into rate derivatives. The relevant
general Monte Carlo identities are already in
[Bordenave and Torrisi (2008)](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/CCFF00C6FC6F1DB452499243F5DD7DD5/S0001867800002536a.pdf/monte-carlo-methods-for-sensitivity-analysis-of-poisson-driven-stochastic-systems-and-applications.pdf).
Graph acquisition and all-event dominator computation still incur full-history
cost; rare successful-history acquisition remains. No matched variance theorem
or new major consequence was established, so that candidate was stopped.
