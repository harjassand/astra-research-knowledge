### 3.3 Route through the actual exclusion graph

Replace a Cartesian edge g->g+e_i by

\[
g\to g+e_1\to g+e_2\to\cdots\to g+e_i.
\tag{5}
\]

Every step is legal: the leftmost particle first moves left, and the introduced vacancy is transferred through the intervening gaps. Path divergence telescopes. Path length and congestion are at most m, giving

\[
\|\mathcal RF\|_{\rm edge}\le m\|F\|_{\rm grid}.
\tag{6}
\]

The payload for physical move j is the suffix sum F_j+...+F_m in the base coordinate g; its actual source vertex is g+e_(j-1), with e_0=0. This labeling retains parallel physical edges and avoids hiding a coordinate shift.

**The suffix sum does not require rank-m direct addition.** Use the upper-triangular block core

\[
\mathbb B_i(s)=
\begin{pmatrix}B_i(s)&H_i(s)\\0&D_i(s)\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{7}
\]

The upper-right block of its ordered product is the sum of terms with exactly one H. To select F_j+...+F_m, use the original B cores before j, then the block cores from j onward with the top input and bottom output boundaries. Therefore:

> **Rank-preserving exclusion completion.** Each routed edge-family payload has TT bond dimensions at most 2r_i. The graph defect is canceled exactly. Neither a multidimensional Poisson solve nor a geometric grid is constructed.

For normalized quantum balance G=c partial, divide the routed flow by c. The factor is not free and affects energy. In rational unnormalized Majorana coordinates, an explicit quantum lift avoids square roots: a leftward move of particle j is represented by two words with adjacent a,a or b,b singles and all other sites paired, with coefficient -1/(2t). Inserting one zero gap into the source payload gives the full lift. The prototype checks PA_0(lift)=b exactly.
