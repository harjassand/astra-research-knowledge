Transport and failure interfaces
--------------------------------
For insulated rings with through-plane active ceramic disks, the most favorable
parallel-path ionic conductivity is f_device*kappa_c. Separate circular cells
in hexagonal packing have f_device <= pi/(2*sqrt(3))*f, before dead margins,
seals and interfacial losses. At the illustrative S=2 GPa limit this geometric
factor is about 0.650. Shrinking tiles does not remove this strength/fill penalty.
A dense ion-blocking tensile skin in series instead blocks through-plane ionic
transport. Electronic isolation of reinforcement is an independent requirement;
a conductive ring contacting both electrodes would short the separator.

The stress solution does not certify out-of-plane stability. A compressed open
disk can buckle; the critical stress scales as E_c*(thickness/a)^2 with a factor
set by its actual edge restraint. A frictionless ring supplies radial pressure
but no transverse support by itself; even a simply supported boundary cannot be
assumed. A compliant ring is not a clamped boundary.
In a perfectly bonded symmetric laminate the total in-plane resultant can be
zero, so treating core compression alone as whole-laminate Euler loading is
incorrect. Delamination and local compressed-core buckling still require checks.

Internal stress also stores elastic energy. For a uniform isostrain two-phase
laminate with biaxial moduli M_i=E_i/(1-nu_i), core stress -P and balancing
reinforcement stress fP/(1-f), its stored energy per composite volume is

  U=f*P^2/M_c + f^2*P^2/[(1-f)*M_r].                        (4)

This expression is for the laminate, not the annular stress solution. It shows
why debonding can release significant energy; it is not itself an interfacial
fracture calculation. At f=0.85,M_c=200 GPa,M_r=300 GPa the value is about
2.21 MJ/m^3, or 111 J/m^2 for 50 micrometre total thickness. Actual energy
release depends on crack geometry and constraints.

The engineering gate is therefore explicit: a chemically compatible, ionically
acceptable, electronically insulating tensile load path must sustain the required
stress and interference, transfer it uniformly into the active ceramic, avoid
buckling/debonding and relax negligibly. Grain-boundary and crack-tip reactions
remain relevant even if the elastic construction works. Lateral lithium growth
must not create a new interface route through the separator.

Prior-art and matched comparison boundary
-----------------------------------------
Shrink-fit rings, residual stress and composite prestressing are established
mechanics. Residual compression is already a solid-electrolyte strategy, including
ion exchange and thermal gradients. The candidate must beat these and Cui's
direct self-contained ring demonstration at matched chemistry, thickness, active area,
current, areal capacity and cycle count; root has established no such result.
Root's independent synthesis is the load-path calculation plus exact
strength/fill/interference gates in the battery task; its architecture was found
to be existing prior art. Worker reports contain the targeted prior-art comparison
and the primary-method correction. This route does not survive as a discovery.
