## 8. Why this is not an input-coordinate relabelling of known isotropic energies

The following auxiliary separation concerns a specified positive-mixture construction only. It is not a computational lower bound and does not replace the quasiconvexity proof above.

Consider the known rotationally invariant boundary energies

\[
 W_t(A)=|A|^4+4t|A|^2\det A+(6t^2-2)(\det A)^2,
 \quad |t|\le1,
\]

and their invertible input-coordinate transforms \(W_t(AS)\). Include arbitrary positive multiples, positive finite sums, and nonnegative determinant-square terms. The retained \(F_*\) is not in this cone.

To prove this, put

\[
 X=(s,r,w',d')=(u+v,u-v,2w,2q),\quad
 J_0=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1),\quad X^TJ_0X=0.
\]

Every transformed generator has the form

\[
 (v^TX)^2-\tfrac12(v^TJ_0v)(d')^2,
\]

where \(v^TJ_0v\ge0\). Therefore every positive sum under consideration has a representation

\[
 X^TZ X-\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}(J_0Z)(d')^2+\delta(d')^2,
 \qquad Z\succeq0,\quad\delta\ge0.
\]

In these variables,

\[
 F_* =\tfrac12s^2+\tfrac12r^2+(w')^2+\tfrac78(d')^2-\tfrac{29}{20}s d'.
\]

Quadratic polynomials representing the same function on the light cone differ by a multiple of \(X^TJ_0X\). Consequently a necessary representation would have, for some \(\tau\),

\[
 Z_{00}=\tfrac12+\tau,\quad Z_{11}=\tfrac12-\tau,
 \quad Z_{22}=1-\tau,\quad Z_{03}=-\tfrac{29}{40},
 \quad Z_{33}=\tfrac14+\tfrac\tau3-\tfrac{2\delta}{3}.
\]

Positivity forces \(-1/2\le\tau\le1/2\). The principal \((0,3)\) minor would require

\[
 \frac{841}{1600}
 \le (\tfrac12+\tau)Z_{33}
 \le(\tfrac12+\tau)(\tfrac14+\tfrac\tau3)
 \le\frac5{12},
\]

which is false. This proves the claimed exclusion. It does not rule out all other uses of known quasiconvex energies, general output-coordinate transformations, or unrelated sufficient criteria.
