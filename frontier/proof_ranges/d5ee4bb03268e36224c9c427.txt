## Status and scope

This investigation does not prove that every rank-one-convex planar quartic is quasiconvex. It does not construct a compatible negative quasiconvexity gap. It develops a joint representation and two positive results which do not require resonance-separated carriers:

1. A two-column Bregman identity represents the full energy of an arbitrary slice-profile loop as nonnegative remainders plus one joint circulation. The representation is available for every smooth periodic potential, not only modulation templates.
2. For polynomial energies of degree at most four, vanishing polynomial circulation through degree three yields all-affine Jensen positivity. For two profile coordinates, six signed area moments suffice. This holds in every target dimension with two input coordinates, without rotational invariance. A concrete finite-difference operation constructs nontrivial interacting loops satisfying the condition; it does not preserve the energy of an arbitrary original loop.
3. An exact rational graph calibration proves a uniform all-affine inequality for every closed walk in a specified library of two interacting opposite-carrier profiles for the previously certified non-polyconvex integrand G. Negative strip energies are allowed. All 32,805 edge/width inequalities are verified exactly.

The circulation identity is not itself a positivity proof for unrestricted loops. The positive results impose either exact moment cancellation or a finite-library calibration. Their ingredients—rank-one supporting inequalities, Green's theorem, polynomial line integrals, finite differences, perspective convexity, and shortest-path potentials—are established mathematics. Historical priority for the particular combination has not been established. This is not a claim to have invented those general tools or a fundamentally new universally applicable variational principle.
