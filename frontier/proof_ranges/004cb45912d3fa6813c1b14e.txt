## Decisive physical gate

The candidate is falsified for a chosen chemistry if no product state with enough extractable free energy exists before relaxation, or if the measured return/attrition/conditioning terms exceed the available budget. The first experiment must make one joint measurement at a fixed chemical composition, pressure, temperature, and throughput regime of:

1. wall work needed to prepare the addressed reactant and its state distribution;
2. probabilities p, s, and ℓ in that same reaction environment;
3. product density matrix, or a state-resolved proxy with a validated lower bound on extractable work, before collisional relaxation;
4. energy lost by molecules, collected optical/other output, heat, product composition, oxygen-side output, and net work actually delivered back to the cyclic bus;
5. complete detector, pulse, feedback/memory reset, source, extraction, cooling, separation, compression, and thermal-utility costs;
6. saleable CO rate, purity, and pressure at the comparator boundary.

Controls: no reaction/no extractor; prepared reactant with no extractor; reaction plus product-state extractor; detuned extractor; and a matched SOEC output run. An anti-Stokes photon, lost coherence, or reduced gas temperature alone does not pass. The same run must produce saleable CO at the specified purity and pressure and demonstrate lower total charged electricity and thermal exergy than a matched SOEC boundary, with uncertainty and throughput reported. The product-state work must be traced to a bus that subsequently reduces source work; a changed molecular population alone is not enough.

The current CO2 centrifuge result fails the co-occurrence gate: the 6.14 eV tail is protected from dissociation by a centrifugal barrier, while the more effective transfer states are less rotationally excited. The result therefore stops at a mathematically complete countermodel, conventional thermodynamic obstruction, quantified thresholds, and an unpassed physical gate. It is not a validated chemistry, scalable throughput claim, or civilization-enabling capability.
