## Result

The global modulation identity gives more than nonnegativity: for every left-
SO(2)-invariant quartic and every affine background, it yields an explicit
positive-semidefinite completion of the N794 nine-moment functional. The
completion is an (8\times8) integral of acoustic rank-one atoms. An exact
rational example verifies all nine dual equations.

There is also a useful cone statement. In either the nine-dimensional
left-SO(2) coefficient space or the full 35-dimensional planar quartic space,
the **closed conic hull** of moment vectors from the positive modulation family
is the full dual cone of rank-one-convex quartics. This follows by polarizing
the family and taking the small-amplitude limit of a single rank-one wave.
It says the positive profiles generate every dual moment functional after
closure. It does not say that the moment vector of every compatible gradient
belongs to that cone; proving that inclusion is the unresolved global
quasiconvexity step.

I tested profile generation separately. The exact periodic potential below has
a connected primitive triad, full-rank gradient covariance, and an exact
nine-moment vector, but it cannot be a convex-loop modulation for any affine
carrier and cannot be a one-coordinate complex envelope. Its connected
frequency classes cannot be split by the current resonance-separated,
support-partition packet rule. This is an exact obstruction to that direct
profile-generation scheme. It is not a separating functional for its
nine-moment vector and is not a negative Jensen gap.
