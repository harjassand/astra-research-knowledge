II. ALL-j MAXIMAL-OUTPUT-SPIN CLONER: EXACT FULL-DOMAIN C=2

Let P_{2j} be the maximal-total-spin projection in H_j tensor H_j.
Define the supplied broadcaster
  B_j(X)=d/(4j+1) P_{2j}(X tensor I)P_{2j}.           (7)
Compression and identity adjoining are CP. Irreducibility gives
Tr_C P_{2j}=(4j+1)I/d, proving TP. The maximal-spin subspace is symmetric
under output swap, so the two marginals coincide. Covariance gives the
unital and HS-self-adjoint conditions. This is the N=2j to M=4j qubit
Werner symmetric cloner viewed as one spin-j input and two spin-j outputs.

The coherent-orbit canonical EB map is
  Psi_j(X)=d int_{SU(2)} Tr(pi_g X) pi_g dg,
  pi_g=U_j(g)|j,j><j,j|U_j(g)^*.
Its rank-l eigenvalue is
  c_l(j)=(2j)!(2j+1)!/[(2j-l)!(2j+l+1)!],             (8)
with c_0=1. The exact marginal eigenvalues of (7) are
  lambda_l(Phi_j)=c_l(j)/c_l(2j).                    (9)

Independent derivation of (9): identify the maximal output subspace with
H_{2j}; let D:H_{2j} states -> H_j states be partial trace after embedding.
Then Phi_j=(d/(4j+1)) D D^*. In tau-normal tensor bases, D^*(T_l^j)=b_l
T_l^{2j}; D(T_l^{2j})=((4j+1)/d)b_l T_l^j, so lambda_l=b_l^2.
At the highest-weight vector, the embedding is |2j,2j> -> |j,j>|j,j>,
giving b_l=<j,j|T_l^j|j,j>/<2j,2j|T_l^{2j}|2j,2j>. The squared zonal
expectation is (2l+1)c_l, yielding (9). Formula (8) can also be derived
directly by expanding the coherent overlap ((1+x)/2)^{2j} in Legendre
polynomials and integrating by parts with Rodrigues' formula.

Proof of the componentwise C2 inequality. Set n=2j and
  f_l(n)=1/c_l(n/2)=prod_{r=0}^{l-1}(n+r+2)/(n-r)
        =prod_r(1+x_r), x_r=2(r+1)/(n-r)>0.
For l<=n, x_r at 2n is <=x_r/2. Expand the positive product by subsets:
  f_l(2n)-1<=prod_r(1+x_r/2)-1
           <=(prod_r(1+x_r)-1)/2.
The second inequality uses 2^{-|S|}<=1/2 for every nonempty subset S.
Consequently f_l(2n)<=(1+f_l(n))/2, or
  lambda_l=c_l(n/2)/c_l(n)<=(1+c_l(n/2))/2.           (10)
Thus Psi_j>=2Phi_j-I as an HS operator on the full Hermitian space, i.e.
  I-Psi_j<=2(I-Phi_j),                               (11)
for every observable simultaneously. Equality occurs at l=1; l>=2 is
strict. No Clifford algebra or numerical premise is used in this proof.

For A_i=J_i, i=x,y,z, exactly V=j(j+1), k=j^2 (maximum spin length j,
coherent orbit attains it). Equation (9) gives lambda_1=(2j+1)/(2j+2),
so V-s=j/2 and (V-k)/(V-s)=2 for every j>0. Alternatively the exact
star -J_A dot(J_B+J_C) has top eigenvalue j(2j+1), attained at BC spin
2j and total spin j. This supplies a legal normalized projector Choi
state via (5), and saturates V+k. It eliminates this whole linear-spin
attack, including the large-j slow-mode regime lambda_1 ->1.

Asymptotics for (7), not for arbitrary covariant channels. Write L=l(l+1).
At fixed l as n=2j ->infinity,
  c_l(j)=1-L/n+(L+L^2/2)/n^2+O(n^-3),
  lambda_l=1-L/(2n)+(3L/4+L^2/8)/n^2+O(n^-3),
  c_l(j)-(2lambda_l-1)=L(L-2)/(4n^2)+O(n^-3).
For l/sqrt(n)->alpha, c_l(j)->exp(-alpha^2) and
lambda_l->exp(-alpha^2/2); the coherent-support deficit ratio tends to
1+exp(-alpha^2/2)<=2, and unrestricted support can only reduce it.
At l=n, lambda_n is exponentially small, asymptotic to
(3sqrt(3pi n)/8)(27/64)^n. These formulas do not assert that coherent
states optimize the classical support for arbitrary higher-harmonic frames.
