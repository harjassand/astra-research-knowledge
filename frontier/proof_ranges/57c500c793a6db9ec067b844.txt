## Research basis and selection

Several opportunities were screened: force-gated mechanochemistry, pressure-quenched superconductivity, nanoscale spin-noise control, barocaloric response and cavity materials. Each has experimental leverage, but no independent new consequential operation was established in the first four during this investigation. The selected obstruction is the difficulty of changing a retarded material interaction without simultaneously changing composition, contact, static screening or mechanical loading.

Keren et al. [1] report a reduced local Meissner response in hBN-covered kappa-ET (Tc 11.5 K), with coupling near the target C=C mode at 1470 cm^-1. The inferred effective superfluid suppression is large, but its depth profile is not determined. Their controls and optical measurements motivate, rather than validate, the proposed substitution. Chen et al. [2] demonstrated isotope-stack-dependent phonon-polariton dispersions. Falin et al. [3] measured different hBN elastic properties for different boron isotopes, which makes naive 'isotope means no mechanical change' reasoning unacceptable.
