## 5. A failed positive-generator transfer, proved exactly

Consider the closed conic hull of all right-coordinate images of homogeneous left-and-right-SO(2)-invariant rank-one-convex quartics. The known isotropic cone and its quasiconvexity are given in [Bruno–Pasqualotto, Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06367v1#A1). F* is not in this generated cone.

Define the linear functional on the nine coefficients by

    ell(p)=(10/7)[4(p1+p2)−(p3+p4)]+p7+p8.

For an isotropic quartic a|A|⁴+bq|A|²+cq², rank-one tests give a≥0 and |b|≤4a. If a=0, then b=0 and c≥0. After A→AS with S invertible, factor a|det S|²>0 and normalize T=SSᵀ/|det S| so det T=1. Write

    T=[[h+delta,k],[k,h−delta]], h>0,
    h²−delta²−k²=1, beta=(b/a)sign(det S), |beta|≤4.

The normalized transformed polynomial is σ²+beta qσ+chi q², where σ=(h+delta)u+2kw+(h−delta)v. Direct coefficient evaluation yields

    ell=(10/7)(2h²+14delta²+4)+2beta h
         ≥(20/7)(h−7/5)²+20delta²+4/35 > 0.

The cq² ray has ell=0. Positivity survives sums, positive scaling, limits and singular right-coordinate limits. But ell(p*)=−3/35. This exactly refutes the named positive-generator transfer for F*. It does not cover arbitrary separately added convex quartics or arbitrary quasiconvex constructions. The heat certificate succeeds by a broader compatible-jet operation.
