## 2. Why ordinary locality cannot be the compression argument

The actual three-particle gap graph has vertices (a,b)>=0 and unoriented edges in directions (1,0), (0,1), and (1,-1), with reflecting boundaries. Its positive Laplacian is the sum of vertex differences along available edges.

The polynomial

\[
h(a,b)=(a-b)(2a+b+1)(a+2b+1)
\]

is harmonic at every vertex, including the boundary. For

\[
b=\delta_{(0,1)}-\delta_{(1,0)},\qquad \langle b,h\rangle=-12.
\]

If the finite-energy pressure gradient solving Lp=b decayed exponentially, connectedness of exterior annuli would make p approach a common constant exponentially. After subtracting the constant, summation by parts against the polynomial h would give -12=<Lp,h>=<p,Lh>=0. Thus exponential locality of this projection is false.

This source is not an artificial unrelated example. With U_z denoting the coefficient in H_diag=U_z sum ZZ, exact identities are

\[
A_0J=0,\quad A_V^2J=-8U_z^2(J-F_{\rm mid}),\quad
F_{\rm mid}=\sum_iZ_{i-1}j_iZ_{i+2},
\]

\[
PAF_{\rm mid}=t^2(V_{1,2}-V_{2,1}).
\]

Here V_(a,b)=sum_x Z_x Z_(x+a) Z_(x+a+b), so the shifted gaps are exactly the displayed dipole. For the earlier U=4 centered-occupation convention, U_z=1. `structural_tests.py` reconstructs the harmonic identities and physical commutators exactly. This obstruction was located in Astra N754's actual source and independently checked here; it does not rule out low-rank approximation or imply that D itself has the same tail.

Three routes were therefore separated:

1. Exponential physical-support locality of the balance projection is unavailable.
2. A short formula for a separable inverse does not guarantee low output rank: even (Lambda X+X Lambda)=11^T gives the full-rank Cauchy matrix X_ij=1/(lambda_i+lambda_j). This exact-rank example is not an approximate-rank lower bound for the quantum class.
3. Conservation can instead be built into a nonlocal functional representation, with its approximation error computed over the entire infinite graph. This route is developed below.
