## 6. A controlled occupation bound

We next prove the full active observation statement, not just agreement of a passive scalar correlator.

Consider a number-conserving hopping Hamiltonian with arbitrary occupation-diagonal interactions, and arbitrary controls on S plus an auxiliary memory. Write B_(xy)=|J_(xy)| for its nonnegative hopping adjacency matrix. Assume its row sums are at most one, as in (18). All sites outside S start in the vacuum.

For a purified state define

    p_x(t)=<n_x>,       q_x(t)=sqrt(p_x(t)).

For x outside S, the control terms and all occupation-diagonal terms commute with n_x. For a hopping edge, Cauchy-Schwarz applied to sigma_x^-|Psi> and sigma_y^-|Psi> gives

    |d p_x/dt| <=2 sum_y B_(xy) sqrt(p_x p_y).

Thus in the upper-Dini-derivative sense

    d q_x/dt <=sum_y B_(xy)q_y.                                 (21)

One may justify points where p_x=0 by replacing sqrt(p_x) by sqrt(p_x+epsilon^2), applying the comparison inequality, and taking epsilon down to zero. At sites in S simply use q_x<=1. Arbitrary local instruments are purified and their records retained; they do not change an outside occupation expectation instantaneously.

Let y(t)=exp(tB)1_S. On S, y>=1; outside S, y'=By; and q(0)<=y(0). The comparison principle proves q<=y. If dist(x,S)>=R, powers B^k 1_S vanish at x for k<R, while their entries are at most one for all k. Therefore

    q_x(t) <=Q_R(t):=sum_(k>=R) t^k/k!.                         (22)

For 0<=t<=R/2,

    Q_R(t)<=2t^R/R!.                                           (23)

This proof permits arbitrary port-control strength, entanglement between ports and memory, many excitations injected at the ports, and density-density interactions throughout the chain. It bounds all inputs uniformly. If V_s(t) is the resulting isometry from input ports and memory to the joint output, then

    ||n_x V_s(t)|| <=Q_R(t).                                   (24)

The arbitrary interacting controls are NOT restricted to the one-excitation sector used in constructing the target prediction.
