14 The additive approximation obstruction
Theorem 14.1 (No total additive approximation algorithm). There is no total algorithm
which, given rational complex strictly positive POVMsE,F and rationalε >0, returns a
rationalv satisfying
|v−V (E,F )|≤ ε.
The conclusion holds with the supremum in(64) restricted to faithful state pairs, for which the
value is finite and nonnegative.
Proof. Given a Turing machine, compute its hard game and the POVMs from the preceding
reduction. In the nonhalting case complete SLD and therefore complete entropy dominance
hold, soV = 0. In the halting case Lemma 12.1 gives a perfect maximally entangled strategy.
Section 12 supplies a faithful Fisher witness and game-computable rational constantsh,κ,δ G
satisfying Lemma 13.1. Apply that lemma withd0 =δG to compute a positive rationalεG
and a faithful entropy witness with defect at leastεG. All constants can be computed without
finding the strategy or its dimension. Thus halting impliesV≥ εG.
A purported approximation algorithm at tolerance εG/4 would return v ≤ εG/4 in the
nonhalting case andv≥ 3εG/4 in the halting case. Comparing withεG/2 would decide halting,
a contradiction.
The primitive gapεG depends effectively on the game. This is enough to amplify to a universal
numerical accuracy obstruction.
Corollary 14.2(Fixed additive error is already undecidable). There is no total algorithm
which approximatesV(E,F ) to additive error 1/4 on every finite rational complex strictly
positive POVM pair.
Proof. First, unrestricted complete entropy dominance tensorizes. IfE⪰ c
REF, then for any
joint input state pair tok copies, replace the channelsE byF one factor at a time. At each
replacement, all other unprocessed inputs and already-produced outputs are an untouched

marginal constraint. Chaining the inequalities yields
E⊗k⪰c
REF⊗k.
For the hard instance compute the positive rationalεG and choosek =⌈1/εG⌉. The product
effects of the tensor-power POVMs are still rational, strictly positive and normalized. In
the nonhalting case tensorization givesV(E⊗k
G ,F⊗k
G ) = 0. In the halting case use thek-fold
products of the faithful entropy-witness states. Relative entropy is additive on tensor products,
so their defect is at leastkεG≥ 1. An additive-1/4 approximation is at most1/4 in the first case
and at least3/4 in the second. Thresholding at1/2 decides halting, which is impossible.
The tensor-power output can be enormous; this is a computable reduction, not a polynomial-
time claim. Exact-order undecidability alone would not establish the fixed-error result. The
computable primitive margin, its dimension-independent normalization, and the final tensor
amplification are all used.
A bare approximate reference-dimension cutoff, without an effective fixed-reference optimization
routine, is a separate question. Fixed-reference faithful state pairs can still be arbitrarily ill
conditioned. The argument above therefore makes no unqualified approximate-reference-cutoff
claim. It does exclude a cutoff together with a total fixed-reference approximation procedure
sufficient to approximateV at every requested accuracy.
