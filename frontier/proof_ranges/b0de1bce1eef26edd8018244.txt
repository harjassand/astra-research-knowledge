## 3. Uniform quartic coercivity in the complete invariant cone

Let F:R^(2x2)->R be a left-SO(2)-invariant rank-one-convex homogeneous quartic. Define

    mu_F=min {F(R): rank R=1, |R|=1}.                        (3.1)

This is nonnegative: the restriction t->F(tR)=t^4F(R) is convex. Let

    V(theta)=(cos theta,sin theta),     phi=r(x)V(k.x).

### Theorem 3.1

For every constant matrix B,

    integral [F(B+Dphi)-F(B)]
        >= (mu_F/12) integral |Dphi|^4.                     (3.2)

Consequently the same bound holds after minimizing over *all* B. It imposes no smallness on the amplitude, no near-line assumption, and no boundedness condition on the affine optimizer.

### Proof: two scalar completions

First consider the polynomial f(t)=F(C+tQ), with rank Q<=1. Write

    f(t)=A t^4+L t^3+C2 t²+D t+E,    A=F(Q)>=0.

Its second derivative is nonnegative for all real t. For A>0 this implies C2>=3L²/(8A). Thus

    f(1)-f(0)-f'(0)-A/3
      = 3(L+4A/3)^2/(8A) + C2-3L²/(8A) >=0.                (3.3)

If A=0, global nonnegativity of f'' forces L=0 and C2>=0, and the same inequality follows. Hence the first term in (2.4) is at least F(Q)/3, or mu_F |grad r|^4/3.

For the second term, let

    g(s)=D²F(B+sW tensor k)[V tensor k,V tensor k]
         =A2 s²+L2 s+C2'.

This is a nonnegative quadratic. Output rotational invariance and orthonormality of V,W give

    A2=D²F(W tensor k)[V tensor k,V tensor k]
       =4F(W tensor k).

For A2>0, C2'>=L2²/(4A2), and

    integral_0^r s g(s) ds - A2 r^4/36
      = r²(L2+4A2 r/3)^2/(8A2)
        + (r²/2)(C2'-L2²/(4A2)) >=0.                        (3.4)

The degenerate case A2=0 is again immediate. The second term of (2.4) is therefore at least mu_F r^4|k|^4/9.

Finally V and W are orthonormal, so

    |Dphi|²=|grad r|²+r²|k|²,

and for nonnegative x,y,

    x²/3+y²/9 >= (x+y)²/12.

Equations (2.4), (3.3), (3.4) prove (3.2). The case k=0 uses only the first term and is included. QED.

The constant is independent of k and of the amplitude. A critically concentrated zero-boundary template phi_rho(x)=rho^(1/2)phi(x/rho) remains in the class, with amplitude rho^(1/2)r(x/rho) and carrier k/rho. In two dimensions its fourth gradient integral is unchanged, so (3.2) controls this concentration without an equiintegrability assumption. This does not express arbitrary concentrated profiles in the template.

No claim of optimality is made for 1/12. Nonnegative Jensen holds even when mu_F=0. For the non-polyconvex invariant quartics considered in the earlier program, positivity on nonzero rank-one matrices gives mu_F>0. The coercive theorem itself states mu_F explicitly and does not need the long contact classification to be applicable.

### A field far from every rank-one matrix line

On the standard two-dimensional torus take

    r(x,y)=1+(1/2)cos x,
    phi=r(x,y)(cos y,sin y).                                (3.5)

Its covariance in row-major matrix coordinates is exactly

    C=diag(1/16,9/16,1/16,9/16).

The maximum variance captured by a unit rank-one direction is 9/16 (attained by e12), while the total is 20/16. Thus no rank-one line captures more than 9/20 of its second moment. The smallest possible relative transverse L² ratio is sqrt(11/9)>1. This is a globally multidirectional field, not a near-line perturbation in disguised coordinates.

For the prior exactly certified integrand

    G=3(u+v-2q)^2+(35/24)u²+q²-(5/2)qu,                     (3.6)

mu_G=3. The verifier independently reconstructs its global positive acoustic Gram. For (3.5), b_G=0,

    E|Dphi|^4=33/16,
    Delta_G(Dphi)=8139/1024.

A second explicit example uses the genuinely two-dimensional amplitude

    r=1+(1/3)cos x+(1/4)cos y+(1/5)cos(x+y),
    phi=r(cos y,sin y).                                     (3.7)

It has exact mean gradient and determinant zero, positive-definite covariance and H, and nonzero cubic vector b. Its support has six frequency classes (0,1), (0,2), (1,-1), (1,0), (1,1), (1,2), forming one connected component through 12 primitive triad and 18 primitive quartet relations (unordered signed tuples, including conjugate tuples). These exact combinatorial counts are checked in the verifier. Thus its internal resonances are not removed by the packet decomposition of Section 5. Complete rational affine minimization gives

    Delta_G(Dphi)=3646241010554033/507189555936000.

The latter is approximately 7.189109018274296. Its significance is not a small numerical gap, but an exact internally resonant, full-rank example in a class proved positive for every amplitude and every affine background. The theorem is not obtained by these finite tests.
