### 8.5 Prescribed input marginals and correlation-only encoding

Let a prescribed input state \(\omega_A\) be faithful. The value in (3.2) is unchanged even when the pairs defining the complete coefficient must satisfy **both**
\[
\rho_R=\sigma_R,\qquad \rho_A=\sigma_A=\omega_A. \tag{8.8}
\]
This includes the input-dependent complete coefficient of [3, Definition 12], which fixes only the second input marginal.

To prove the stronger claim, take any of the near-extremising pairs from Section 7. They already have equal input marginals, say \(\nu_A\). Choose \(0<\varepsilon<1\) small enough that
\[
\tau_A=\frac{\omega_A-\varepsilon\nu_A}{1-\varepsilon}\succeq0.
\]
Add one orthogonal reference flag and define
\[
\widetilde\rho=\varepsilon\rho\oplus(1-\varepsilon)\tau_A,
\qquad
\widetilde\sigma=\varepsilon\sigma\oplus(1-\varepsilon)\tau_A.
\]
Their reference marginals agree, and both input marginals are exactly \(\omega_A\). Relative entropy before and after the channel is multiplied by \(\varepsilon\), so the ratio is unchanged. Thus reference dimension at most \(m^2+2\) suffices for a prescribed faithful marginal. The same flagged construction works for the binary CMI witnesses, keeping \(I(U;A)=I(U;R)=0\).

For a nonfaithful prescribed \(\omega_A\), restrict the channel input to \(S=\operatorname{supp}\omega_A\). Finite relative entropy forces both compared states to lie in \(R\otimes S\). Applying the faithful argument on \(S\) proves that the input-dependent complete coefficient depends on \(\omega_A\) **only through its support**, and equals (3.2) for the restricted channel. A one-dimensional support gives coefficient zero.

The requirement that a pair have a prescribed marginal must not be confused with fixing its second state to the product \(\rho_R\otimes\omega_A\). The latter is a different contraction problem, and no equality with (3.2) is claimed for it.
