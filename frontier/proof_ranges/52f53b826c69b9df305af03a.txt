## Exact compatible resonance check

I independently implemented exact Laurent-coefficient integration for the supplied tangent quartic

\[
 G=3(u+v-2q)^2+\frac{35}{24}u^2+q^2-\frac52qu,
 \quad u=a^2+c^2,\ v=b^2+d^2,\ w=ab+cd,\ q=ad-bc,
\]

on

\[
 \psi=\cos(x+y)+\cos(x+2y)+\cos(2x+3y),\qquad
 \varphi=(\tfrac23\psi_y,\psi_x),\qquad X=D\varphi.
\]

The three frequencies satisfy \((1,1)+(1,2)=(2,3)\), so this is a smooth periodic resonant gradient, not a probability mixture of three one-wave laws. The covariance has exact rank three. Exact torus integration gives \(H=\mathbb E D^2G(X)\succ0\) (its four leading principal minors are positive rational numbers),

\[
 \mathbb E DG(X)[I]=\frac{85}{18}\ne0,
 \qquad
 \Delta_G(X)=\frac{6488961661323546029}{309344140144800}>0.
\]

For the named contact line \(R=e_{12}\), the relative fourth-moment ratio is
\[
 \frac{\|X-(X:R)R\|_4^4}{\|X:R\|_4^4}=\frac{27833}{9440},
\]
so this example has substantial transverse fourth moment at that contact. It is outside the supplied moving theorem's spectral scope for this \(G\): the cited contact has a rank-two transverse Finsler remainder, whereas that theorem assumes a simple transverse contact. The exact positive gap is only a finite profile check. It neither proves the moment completion for other fields nor establishes full quasiconvexity of \(G\).

The independent check is in [exact_resonant_profile.py](exact_resonant_profile.py), with the complete rational moments and affine minimizer in [exact_resonant_profile.json](exact_resonant_profile.json). It used Python 3.14.3 and SymPy 1.14.0. The computation verifies this profile's Laurent moments and optimized gap; rank-one convexity of \(G\) is the separate source certificate, not a consequence of this script.
