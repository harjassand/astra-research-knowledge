# What a finite physical experiment can qualify

16 October 2026. New audit derivations, not an external proof review or a historical novelty claim. All distances below use half trace/diamond distance. A supplied theorem and a physically acquired assumption remain separate.

## 1. Exact identification criterion for the intended continuation

Fix a repeatable initialisation procedure, accessible quantum/classical ports, a last recovery time H, and the allowed continuation/test class. Hidden bath degrees of freedom are absorbed into a finite-horizon operational process X. They are not asserted to be independently controllable. Represent X in a finite-dimensional real Hermitian space with affine causal constraints A(X)=a and positivity. Write C for the admissible process set, L=span(C-C), and assume X_0 is in the relative interior of C. This includes a fixed-rank face when that face is justified independently.

An experiment supplies probabilities p_k(X)=<T_k,X>, k=1,...,K. Let M(Z)=(<T_k,Z>)_k. The proposed endpoint/recovery effect F is identifiable from these exact probabilities on C if and only if

    ker(M|_L) is contained in ker(F|_L).                 (1)

Equivalently, there exist real c_k and a known affine offset c_0 such that

    <F,X> = c_0 + sum_k c_k p_k(X),  for every X in C.  (2)

Proof. If (1) holds, the functional F factors through the finite-dimensional quotient L/ker M; extend the resulting functional on range M to R^K. This gives F|_L=sum c_k T_k|_L, and evaluation at any known point of the affine hull fixes c_0. Conversely (2) annihilates ker M. If (1) fails, select Z in L with M(Z)=0 and <F,Z> nonzero. Relative interior gives X_0 +/- t Z in C for sufficiently small t>0. These physically admissible processes have identical experiment probabilities and different target probabilities. Infinite shots do not resolve the difference. QED.

The same criterion must hold for every F in a proposed universal continuation class. Complete finite-horizon tomography is sufficient, but is unnecessary for a single effect. Conversely a good fit to an undercomplete design supplies no bound on a target outside the measured span. A small tensor-network ansatz does not remove the nullspace unless its approximation error and admissibility are separately justified. A gate-set gauge must be fixed by trusted references, or the claimed target must be gauge-invariant.

For a fixed instrument whose outcome probabilities are all reported, normalization constraints are included in A. For nonunitary measures and adaptive testers, the T_k are the actual complete tester effects, including their ancillas and calibration. This statement does not identify an arbitrary-bath dilation from its operational process.

The linear process representation is itself a qualified premise. If two nominally identical system CP maps send different physical control signals into the bath, a system-only intervention model need not compose as assumed. Include those actual control/environment ports (or the finite classical waveform-command interface) in the declared process, independently bound the spillage, or reject that representation. A fit in an incorrect port model does not pass (1).

## 2. A finite-sample acquisition certificate and its cost

Assume n_k independent repetitions of a binary tester T_k on the same repeatably initialized process. Let p_hat_k be its frequency. With probability at least 1-alpha, simultaneously

    |p_k-p_hat_k| <= beta_k,
    beta_k = sqrt(log(2K/alpha)/(2 n_k)).               (3)

This is Hoeffding plus a union bound. If a trusted calibration/systematic bound changes the k-th ideal tester probability by at most sigma_k, replace beta_k by beta_k+sigma_k. Repeatability, initialisation, drift and calibration are premises; bootstrapping a fitted process does not establish them.

For (2), the following upper bound then holds for every F represented by the design, without a second union bound over F:

    p_F <= c_0 + sum_k c_k p_hat_k
                    + sum_k |c_k|(beta_k+sigma_k).    (4)

One may minimize the right side over every valid dual representation c. Alternatively maximize <F,X> over the positive causal-process confidence region. Both are reviewable finite optimizations. If the confidence set is empty, the calibration/model is falsified; a projection onto a plausible point must not silently replace it.

For one fixed coefficient vector, put a_k=|c_k| and S=sum_k a_k^(2/3). Minimizing total shots N=sum n_k subject to sum a_k beta_k<=epsilon, while ignoring integer rounding, gives

    n_k = [log(2K/alpha)/(2 epsilon^2)] S^2 a_k^(2/3),
    N_min = [log(2K/alpha)/(2 epsilon^2)] S^3.          (5)

Proof. Lagrange multipliers give n_k proportional to a_k^(2/3). Substitution saturates the error constraint and yields (5). Rounding each positive n_k upward preserves the certificate. QED. This is a sufficient Hoeffding-design allocation, not an information-theoretically optimal quantum sample complexity.

For example, K=324, alpha=0.01, all a_k=1, epsilon=0.01 requires approximately 1.88e12 shots under this deliberately unconditioned L1 bound. It is not the shot requirement of the Giarmatzi experiment: its dual coefficients, actual target, covariance, physical constraints and systematic budget would have to be computed. With just one directly performed endpoint test the analogous bound is 26,492 shots. The difference is information breadth and conditioning, not a hidden theorem advantage.

If an ideal process X_ref has an already proved endpoint bound B_F, and the design certifies |<F,X-X_ref>|<=epsilon_F, then the actual bounded-horizon effect obeys p_F<=B_F+epsilon_F. This remains valid with shared bath and correlated controls because it compares the complete tested process. It does not convert a terminal process radius into independent local residual factors or qualify all future bath inputs.

## 3. Two exact bath/seed falsifiers

### 3.1 Unpopulated bath sector

Suppose every tested bath initial state and its controlled trajectory lie in a bath subspace B_obs. Adjoin an orthogonal bath state |h>, never populated in these tests, and add

    H_hidden = M X_S tensor |h><h|,

where X_S flips the controlled parity. Every measured probability is unchanged for every M. The uniform own-odd operator norm is at least |M| on a different allowed bath input. Thus no system-only initialized-bath design, even with infinite shots and a complete process reconstruction on its tested horizon, gives a finite arbitrary-bath norm envelope unless the unobserved sectors are excluded or bounded by an independent model. This is an identifiability theorem, not a limitation of a particular estimator.

### 3.2 Perfect reduced branches and zero modulo-bath error can still expose the coin

For a syndrome qubit S and bath qubit B, let a fair bit choose

    U_0 = I_S tensor I_B,
    U_1 = Z_S tensor X_B.                              (6)

Each branch implements exactly its intended system gate on every reduced system input, including inputs correlated with B. The bath unitary disappears under partial trace. Also each branch's error is purely bath-supported, so an error certificate which discards arbitrary pure-bath terms reports zero.

On |+>_S|0>_B, however, the averaged joint output is

    rho_actual = (|+0><+0| + |-1><-1|)/2.

The desired full-joint dephaser with unchanged bath gives

    rho_target = (I_S/2) tensor |0><0|.

Their trace distance is 1/2. The bath contains a perfect record of the coin. A later controlled-Z, controlled by B, returns the actual system to |+>, whereas it leaves the target dephased. A seed copy released only after H produces identical records up to H and fails at a later continuation. Finite data therefore qualify privacy only to their continuation boundary, unless a no-return architecture is trusted.

This does not contradict DCG theorems. It refutes the inference from a modulo-pure-bath or reduced-gate certificate alone to the required common-bath hidden-channel certificate. Ng-Lidar-Preskill's particular Eq. (40) uses a specified common unperturbed bath unitary and avoids this specific inference error.

## 4. A constructive restricted acquisition theorem

The following gives an actual sufficient model that need not bound the norm of an infinite entire bath. It is a new assembly of elementary ingredients for this audit; no priority claim is made.

Declare a finite controlled boundary R (data, affected spectators, accessible retained bath and leakage levels), a projection P onto its retained energy/level space, and recovery deadline H. Assume P commutes with the intended stabilizer S. Fix two physical control paths C_b(t) supported on the data in R, with endpoints C_0=I and C_1=S up to phases. The reference Hamiltonian on P is

    H_ref,b(t) = H_safe(t) + H_ctrl,b(t),
    [H_safe(t), C_b(s)] = 0 for all relevant t,s.       (7)

H_safe and its retained-bath evolution are the same in both branches. It may be large. The reference endpoint is (S^b tensor I_B) U_safe. During the real window write the restricted actual Hamiltonian as

    P H_b(t) P = H_ref,b(t) + K_b(t),
    delta_b = integral ||K_b(t)|| dt,
    gamma_b = integral ||(I-P)H_b(t)P|| dt.           (8)

All baseline terms failing to commute with the complete path, pulse errors, drive crosstalk and retained cross-boundary couplings are in K_b. gamma_b is a bound on the actual coupling out of the retained space, not a measured average leakage probability. Omitted external data couplings must be charged or the region expanded. Assume initial joint states, including a reference, obey Tr[(I-P)rho]<=nu. The private fair coin is independent of these inputs and has no returning carrier before H.

Then, over this input class, the actual averaged operation has distance from the stipulated ideal physical dephaser plus U_safe at most

    epsilon <= min(1, sum_b p_b(delta_b+gamma_b) + 2 sqrt(nu)). (9)

An independent seed bias |p_1-1/2|, trusted seed-input correlation radius epsilon_seed, and separately proved outside-footprint process tail epsilon_out can be added. None is inferred from a Ramsey contrast.

Proof. First replace H_b by its P/Q block diagonal part. For P-supported inputs Duhamel uses only the cross block acting on the reference P trajectory, and gives operator error at most gamma_b; the possibly huge Q H_b Q is unrestricted. Next replace PHP by H_ref on P, paying delta_b. A pure input splits into P|psi>+Q|psi>; the Q piece has norm <=sqrt(nu) and the difference of two unitaries on it has norm <=2sqrt(nu). The same argument holds with an arbitrary reference. Pure-state trace distance is no greater than vector distance, and purification/convexity handles mixed states. Finally average branches and trace genuinely discarded environments. Equation (7) factors the reference into its physical S^b endpoint and a common U_safe. QED.

This is an energy/initialisation-constrained channel guarantee. At nu=gamma=0 it is a uniform joint guarantee on the declared finite space. It is not the original unrestricted arbitrary-bath contract. If local application histories can escape or change P, a local factor cannot be reused without a conditional-uniform reachability argument; a complete-horizon hybrid bound remains available.

### 4.1 Explicit coefficient acquisition, rather than supplied fine envelopes

Within a complete stationary n-qubit retained Hamiltonian model, let

    H = sum_{j=1}^m theta_j P_j,

where the P_j are distinct nonidentity Pauli strings. A known coarse bound ||H||<=Lambda is required. All retained bath qubits must be controllable/preparable for this construction. For each j choose a Pauli Q_j anticommuting with P_j, prepare rho_j=(I+Q_j)/d, and measure R_j=-i P_j Q_j after short evolution t. Then

    a_j(0)=0,  a'_j(0)=2 theta_j,
    |theta_j-a_j(t)/(2t)| <= Lambda^2 t.              (10)

Proof. Pauli orthogonality gives Tr(i[H,R_j]rho_j)=2theta_j; all k!=j terms vanish. The second derivative is bounded by ||[H,[H,R_j]]||<=4Lambda^2. Taylor's integral remainder gives |a_j(t)-2theta_j t|<=2Lambda^2t^2. QED.

With N_j +/-1 shots, a simultaneous mean-error radius

    beta_j = sqrt(2 log(2m/alpha)/N_j)

gives the actual finite-confidence coefficient interval

    |theta_j-theta_hat_j| <= beta_j/(2t) + Lambda^2t + sigma_j/(2t). (11)

sigma_j bounds SPAM, timing and repeatability error in a_j. For a specified time-dependent finite dictionary, repeat this complete stationary construction at justified waveform segments or acquire an independently justified interpolation budget. A sampled fitted waveform alone supplies no uniform continuum envelope.

For K_b(t)=sum theta_j f_bj(t)P_j with interval radii r_j, define w_bj=integral |f_bj(t)|dt. A conservative computable charge is

    delta_b <= sum_j w_bj (|theta_hat_j|+r_j).         (12)

One may instead maximize the operator norm over the simultaneous coefficient confidence region, which is often smaller. Thus the fine action envelope can be acquired in a trusted complete finite model. Completeness, Lambda, gamma, nu, privacy and physical locality remain independently justified structural assumptions. Arbitrary inaccessible bath states do not become measured through (11).

### 4.2 Correctable footprint and its limit

The data region R_data must contain every data location the pulse or its retained bath excitation can affect before recovery H, including pulse tails and seed-bearing carriers. Require

    P_code A P_code = lambda_A P_code
    for every operator A supported on R_data.         (13)

For a stabilizer code, absence of a nontrivial logical Pauli supported in R_data is an equivalent algebraic test. The sufficient size test |R_data|<d_code is conservative. Shared-bath coefficients do not alter the operator-span condition, but bath-mediated propagation to other data does alter R_data. Equation (13) is not inferred from a small channel residual.

Example design only: a weight-two Shor-code stabilizer Z_1 Z_2 has a two-data-qubit support, which is correctable for a distance-three code. Apply real parallel Z rotations with a common reserved idle/pulse window, forbid correction/reuse until completion, and isolate any retained sidecar bath from all other data until recovery. Any omitted neighbor influence is paid in epsilon_out or expands the region. This demonstrates compatibility of finite pulses and a correctable support in a restricted architecture; it supplies no numerical device pass or scalable memory advantage. Intermediate product rotations can violate other stabilizers, so this example does not inherit an overlapping multi-parity product theorem by endpoint equivalence.
