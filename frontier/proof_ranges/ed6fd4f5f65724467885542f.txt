# Source check: catalyst padding and the missing factorial

**Finding:** verified under the propensity convention that Petrack–Doty explicitly define for the paper. The rate adjustment in Definition 2.7 / Algorithm 1 misses a factor of `δ_o!` when `δ_o` copies of the new catalyst species `K` are added. A one-reactant channel padded from order 1 to order 3 is slowed by exactly two under the printed formula. In a CRN with a competing order-3 channel, this changes both the continuous-time law and the next-reaction probabilities.

I checked the v2 source (`arXiv:2508.04079v2`, submitted 12 February 2026), including `sections/definitions.tex` and `sections/transformation.tex`; the downloaded source archive had SHA-256 `9db2fc54c4fefce52d12e93d2f18529d5a655fa631877604cb40e2adc7c3dfc4`. The paper states its propensity convention in Eq. (1): for reactant multiplicity vector `r` and volume `V`,

\[
p_{c,V}(r,p,k)=\frac{k}{V^{\lVert r\rVert-1}}
\prod_A\frac{c(A)_{\underline{r(A)}}}{r(A)!}
=\frac{k}{V^{\lVert r\rVert-1}}\prod_A\binom{c(A)}{r(A)}.
\]

The source explicitly says its adjusted rate constant is substituted into Eq. (1). Definition 2.7 sets

\[
\bar k=k\,\frac{V^{\delta_o}}{m_{\underline{\delta_o}}},
\qquad \delta_o=\operatorname{ord}(\mathcal C)-\operatorname{ord}(r),
\]

and Algorithm 1 adds `δ_o K` to both sides of the reaction and calls this adjustment with `m=k_0`, the fixed number of `K` molecules in the simulated configuration. The references are [the v2 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04079v2) and its [HTML text](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.04079v2).

For local source replay, the defining propensity and alternate-convention note are in `sections/definitions.tex` lines 32–56, Definition 2.7 is at lines 202–219, Algorithm 1 is in `sections/transformation.tex` lines 33–56, and the Lemma 3.1 propensity calculation is at lines 129–160.

For an original reaction of order `q`, its propensity is

\[
p=\frac{k}{V^{q-1}}\prod_A\binom{c(A)}{r(A)}.
\]

After padding with `δ` copies of the single new species `K`, the printed adjustment gives

\[
p'=\frac{kV^\delta}{m_{\underline\delta}V^{q+\delta-1}}
\binom{m}{\delta}\prod_A\binom{c(A)}{r(A)}
=\frac{p}{\delta!},
\]

because `binom(m,δ)=m_{\underline δ}/δ!`. This is an exact factor, independent of `m` and `V`; it is not an asymptotic issue.

## Order-three counterexample

Take the order-three CRN

\[
A\xrightarrow{k}B,\qquad C+D+E\xrightarrow{\ell}F,
\]

at volume `V=1`, in the configuration `A=C=D=E=1`, with `k_0=m=3` copies of `K`. Algorithm 1 pads the first reaction to `A+2K → B+2K` and assigns it `k'=k/6`. Under Eq. (1), its original propensity is `k`, but the padded propensity is

\[
\frac{k}{6}\binom{3}{2}=\frac{k}{2}.
\]

The second reaction is already order three and has propensity `ℓ`; any `W` added to products to equalize generativity is inert and does not affect either propensity. Set `k=ℓ=1`. The first channel is selected next with probability `1/2` in the original CRN and `1/3` in the transformed CRN. Thus, under the displayed convention, this example refutes the equal-propensity step in Lemma 3.1 and the claimed exact discrete-time simulation, in addition to changing the continuous-time holding rate. For the one-channel padded reaction alone, the embedded jump sequence is unchanged, but its exponential waiting-time rate is halved, so continuous-time simulation already fails.

## Repair and scope

Under the paper's Eq. (1) convention, the order-adjusted rate constant for this padding operation must be

\[
\boxed{\bar k_{\rm binom}=k\,\frac{V^{\delta_o}\,\delta_o!}{m_{\underline{\delta_o}}}
=k\,\frac{V^{\delta_o}}{\binom{m}{\delta_o}}.}
\]

More generally, the multiplier is the product of factorials of the newly added multiplicities, divided by their falling factorials. Here Algorithm 1 adds all extra reactants as copies of one fresh species `K`, so the correction is exactly `δ_o!`. No correction is needed for `δ_o=0` or `1`; the printed rate change is `1/δ_o!` for each padded channel. If all channels have the same `δ_o`, their embedded reaction choices retain their relative probabilities, but the continuous-time clock is rescaled by `1/δ_o!`. If accessible channels have different `δ_o`, as in the example, even next-reaction probabilities change. The already-uniform case (`δ_o=0` for every channel) is unaffected.

The source notes that an alternative convention omits each `r(A)!`; under a consistently factorial-free propensity law, the printed catalyst adjustment is the natural one for the added `K` copies. That does not reconcile the theorem as written: the paper defines its own kinetics by Eq. (1), then says Definition 2.7 substitutes the adjusted constant into Eq. (1). Converting an input reaction from the binomial convention to a factorial-free convention also requires a reaction-specific rate-constant conversion (`k_ff = k_binom / ∏_A r(A)!`), which is not part of Algorithm 1.

There is a related source inconsistency in the proof of Lemma 3.1: its displayed propensity calculation uses `V^{||r'||}` rather than Eq. (1)'s `V^{||r'||-1}` and omits all reactant-factorial denominators. Definition 2.7's displayed expanded adjusted-propensity formula likewise does not follow by substituting its printed `bar k` into Eq. (1). These are further reasons the printed proof does not establish the stated simulation result under its declared convention. They do not alter the direct `1/2` counterexample or the corrected rate constant above.
