Theorem
-------
For every real r<-2, let

  A=[[a,b],[c,d]], u=a^2+c^2, v=b^2+d^2, j=ad-bc,
  H=(u+v-2j)^2=((a-d)^2+(b+c)^2)^2,
  F_(alpha,r)=alpha H+r(r-1)u^2/6+j^2+r u j,
  alpha_*(r)=3r/[8(r+2)].

Then F_(alpha,r) is quasiconvex if and only if it is rank-one convex if and
only if alpha>=alpha_*(r). Quasiconvexity holds at every constant affine
background, on every bounded Lipschitz planar domain, for W0^(1,4)
perturbations. The periodic analogue also holds.
