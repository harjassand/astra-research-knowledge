**5. Approximate commutation need not permit nearby exact repair.**

For explicit trace-preserving conditional expectations on fixed matrix algebras, the completely bounded commutator tends to zero while every exactly commuting repaired pair on the same ambient algebra stays a nonzero distance away. Reviewed factor-algebra constructions strengthen the obstruction. This is an all-size mathematical counterexample, not a numerical failure to find a repair.

The ambient algebra, norm, trace, pair of maps and allowed repair are essential. Hot or pure ancillas, discarded environments, marginal return and joint return can change the conclusion. Those variants were analysed separately; they cannot be used interchangeably to infer a physical impossibility or free catalytic operation.

Why this matters: approximate compatibility is not automatically a stable route to an exact composable mathematical operation. It exposes a real interface failure relevant to operator algebras and quantum information. Novelty for the exact formulations and any technological consequence remain UNKNOWN.
