1. COMPLETE PARAMETERIZATION OF ALL EQUIVALENT PHYSICAL MODELS

Fix the canonical minimal r-node model with full closure and choose one
representative (Kbar,alphabar). Every finite physical realization with the
same IO germ is obtained, up to physical relabelings, as follows.

(a) Partition an active subset of {1,...,N} into r nonempty blocks
    B_1,...,B_r, with B_1={1}. Other coordinates are inactive.
(b) Choose alpha_i>0, with
      sum_(i in B_b)1/alpha_i=1/alphabar_b,
    and choose signs s_i in {+1,-1}, with s_1=+1.
    The alpha_i of inactive coordinates are arbitrary positive numbers.
(c) Put Z_b=1/alphabar_b and form the orthonormal block matrix
      V_ib=s_i/(sqrt(alpha_i)*sqrt(Z_b)) for i in B_b, 0 otherwise.
(d) Choose any positive-definite self-adjoint operator H on
    W_perp=(range V)_perp, and put
      K=V Kbar V^T + P_Wperp H P_Wperp.
    The second term means an arbitrary SPD operator on W_perp extended
    by zero on W. When N=r, this term is absent.

There are no further restrictions in the unrestricted real SPD class.
In particular H can couple inactive coordinates to antisynchronous
directions and can mix residual directions belonging to different blocks;
it need not be block diagonal in physical coordinates. It must have no
cross term with range V, exactly because K is symmetric and W-invariant.

Sufficiency: K is SPD, preserves range V, and on that subspace
alpha*x^3=V[alphabar*xi^3] for x=Vxi. V^T e1=e1 and h(Vxi)=xi1.
Thus every zero-initial input trajectory is x=Vxi for the canonical model,
with exactly its IO response. The physical closure equals range V: a
proper smaller closure would project to a proper closure of the canonical
full-closure model.

Necessity: for any physical realization, its closure W is classified by
disjoint weighted signed blocks, as proved in canonical_reduction.txt.
The canonical restriction is signed-permutation equivalent to the fixed
(Kbar,alphabar); reorder and orient the blocks to match it. Their weights
must satisfy the displayed harmonic constraints. Symmetry and invariance
split K orthogonally into the fixed V Kbar V^T and an arbitrary SPD
restriction on W_perp. This proves exhaustion of all equivalent models.

The parameterization exhibits the exact information lost: arbitrary
inactive coordinates, choices of node coefficients within harmonic block
sums, physical block expansion, and hidden SPD dynamics on W_perp.
