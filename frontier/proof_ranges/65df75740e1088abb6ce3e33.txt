## 4. What strong $L^4$ does and does not give for Schur loss

If fixed $F$ is quartic and $X_n\to X$ strongly in $L^4$, its moment data converge at least linearly in the $L^4$ distance on bounded $L^4$ sets:
\[
|c_n-c|+|b_n-b|+\|H_n-H\|\le C_F\|X_n-X\|_{L^4}.
\]
This follows from degree-four, degree-three, and degree-two polynomial difference bounds and Hölder. It does not control the inverse of a Hessian whose smallest eigenvalue tends to zero.

For an invertible $H_n$ in the orthogonal splitting $W\oplus N$, with $N=\ker H_0$ and $H_0|_W\succ0$, write
\[
H_n=\begin{pmatrix}A_n&B_n\\B_n^T&D_n\end{pmatrix},\quad
S_n=D_n-B_n^TA_n^{-1}B_n,\quad
r_n=b_{n,N}-B_n^TA_n^{-1}b_{n,W}.
\]
Then exactly
\[
\frac12b_n^TH_n^{-1}b_n
=\frac12b_{n,W}^TA_n^{-1}b_{n,W}
+\frac12r_n^TS_n^{-1}r_n.
\]
Moment convergence handles the first term. Continuity at the singular limit additionally requires $r_n^TS_n^{-1}r_n\to0$. Equiintegrability or strong $L^4$ convergence supplies no such relative rate by itself.

In the source packet, the compatible concentration sequence has fixed fourth-moment mass on shrinking supports and is not equiintegrable; it does not decide the strong-$L^4$ compatible case. The separate bounded law is not compatible. A conditional first-order cancellation holds for this $G$ along a fixed perturbation of a one-line base $\zeta e_{12}$. At $R=e_{12}$, $H_R=D^2G(R)\succeq0$ has kernel $\mathbb RI$, and $R\perp I$. With $m_j=\mathbb E\zeta^j$, $m_1=0$, $m_2>0$, the limiting blocks are $H_0=m_2H_R$ and $b_0=m_3DG(R)$. Quartic Euler gives $H_RR=3DG(R)$, so a range solution is $z_0=(m_3/(3m_2))R$, which lies in $(\ker H_R)^\perp$. For a fixed perturbation $X_\epsilon=\zeta R+\epsilon Z$,
\[
b_1:I=m_2D^2G(R)[Z,I]=0,\qquad
H_1[I,z_0]=\frac{m_3}{3m_2}\mathbb E[\zeta D^3G(R)[Z,I,R]]=0,
\]
because $D^3G(R)[Z,I,R]=2D^2G(R)[Z,I]=0$. This removes the first-order kernel cubic along this fixed perturbation only. It does not control higher-order Schur degeneracy or arbitrary sequences. Compatible strong-$L^4$ loss continuity remains open in this audit.
