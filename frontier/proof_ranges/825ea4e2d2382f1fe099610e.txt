## Result

For A=[[a,b],[c,d]], set

\[
 u=a^2+c^2,\quad v=b^2+d^2,\quad j=ad-bc,\qquad
 G(A)=3(u+v-2j)^2+\frac{35}{24}u^2+j^2-\frac52ju.
\]

Define the linear Cauchy--Riemann defect and its fourth power by

\[
 \mathcal C A=(a-d,b+c),\qquad H(A)=|\mathcal C A|^4=(u+v-2j)^2.
\]

The exact source certificate implies the stronger statement

\[
 \boxed{G-\kappa H\text{ is quasiconvex at every affine background},
 \qquad \kappa=\frac3{16000}.}                    \tag{1}
\]

The subtraction vanishes on the conformal plane. Nevertheless, its linear
defect is elliptic on actual compactly supported gradients. Consequently, on
every bounded Lipschitz domain Omega, for every constant B and every
phi in W0^(1,4)(Omega;R2),

\[
 \boxed{\int_\Omega [G(B+D\phi)-G(B)]
       \ge\gamma\int_\Omega |D\phi|^4,\qquad
       \gamma=\frac1{2500000}.}                  \tag{2}
\]

The same constant works on every periodic rectangle, and on every strip
(0,T) x T with zero profiles at both x ends, independently of T>0. Here and
below T as a space means R/(2 pi Z); y integration on strips is normalized.
