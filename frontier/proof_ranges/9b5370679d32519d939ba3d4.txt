## 1. Exact statement accepted

Let a,b>0 and let U,V be bounded self-adjoint involutions on a complex Hilbert space, with nonzero commutator. Put A=aU, B=bV and

\[
\delta=\frac12\|[U,V]\|\in(0,1],\qquad C=\frac12(UV+VU).
\]

For **independent positive scalar probability laws** ν₁,ν₂ with finite variances v₁,v₂, require a normalized POVM M satisfying, for all real s,t,

\[
\widehat M(s,t)=\widehat\nu_1(s)\widehat\nu_2(t)
\frac{e^{isA}e^{itB}+e^{itB}e^{isA}}2.
\]

The set of variance pairs for which some such ν₁,ν₂,M exist is exactly

\[
v_1>0,\quad v_2>0,\qquad v_1v_2\ge4a^2b^2\delta^2=\|[A,B]\|^2.
\]

Every positive finite pair in this region is attained by centered two-sided geometric laws on 2aℤ and 2bℤ. On the product boundary those laws, up to arbitrary coordinate translations, are the only attaining laws. The weighted minimum is 2√(w₁w₂)‖[A,B]‖ for positive finite weights.

This statement concerns positive scalar multiplier factors and a specified characteristic operator. It does not assume or construct latent simultaneous sharp outcomes of A and B, and independence of the multiplier laws does not imply independence of the final reports.
