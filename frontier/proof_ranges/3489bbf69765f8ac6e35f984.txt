2. SINGLE-HERALD GAUSSIAN COUNT COMPILER: UNDISPLACED BASELINE

Contract: rational, symmetric, entrywise nonnegative Bargmann kernel K with
ket/bra exchange symmetry, spectral radius rho(XK)<1, and globally zero
displacement. For a physical interpretation the kernel must describe a valid
density operator; positivity of its entries alone does not certify physicality.
One physical mode is observed at a nonzero-probability photon event h, encoded
in binary. Return the remaining count vector in binary to requested TV error
epsilon. The theorem does not cover arbitrary complex/signed kernels, multiple
herald modes, or displacement coupled into the herald.

Eliminate the unobserved doubled modes using rational Schur operations. The
effective herald matrix is [[a,b],[b,a]]. If k is the number of cross pairs,
then k=h modulo two and its unnormalized weight is
  w(k)=h!^2 a^(h-k)b^k /
       [k! 2^(h-k) (((h-k)/2)!)^2].
Its consecutive ratio is
  w(k+2)/w(k)=(b/a)^2 (h-k)^2/[(k+1)(k+2)],
which decreases. Exact ratio comparisons locate a mode using O(log(h+1))
comparisons. A dyadic rejection envelope samples this one integer using
certified log-factorial ratios, without constructing h tokens or an h-sized
factorial. Reverse Schur sampling then reconstructs aggregate counts using
binomial and negative-binomial laws. The loop shape is 1/2 plus the routed
edge count; omitting that count would be incorrect.

The ideal finite-bit construction has polynomial cost in the complete rational
input length, number of modes, log(h+1), and log(1/epsilon), using certified
polynomial-time elementary-function evaluations. Graph operations, stored
routing coefficients, and the number of scalar calls are O(modes^3). Small
Schur pivots and large output counts are charged through their bit lengths.
The implementation uses FLINT/Arb ball arithmetic. Its enclosure logic and
randomness caps were audited, but a uniform polynomial performance proof for
the actual Arb refinement implementation has not been supplied. Timings are
observations, not such a proof.

The final endpoint allocates epsilon/4 to capped uniform-integer ticket draws,
3epsilon/8 to the matching law, and 3epsilon/8 across reverse scalar calls.
It charges truncation, downward acceptance rounding, rejection caps and fixed
fallbacks. Guarantees assume independent unbiased random bits and valid
library enclosures; seeded runs are reproducibility diagnostics.

This law is a classical trinomial bridge. Generic log-concave rejection already
provides the relevant oracle mechanism: Chewi et al.,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.14166. The complete compiler is an extension of Astra
N417/N418, whose supplied research status remains unverified externally. It is
not a new general solution to Gaussian boson sampling. Prior-art comparisons
include repeated-pair hafnians, photon-subtracted Gaussian formulas and existing
loop-soup constructions; the packet records their exact interface differences.

2A. DISPLACED EXTENSION AND ITS COEFFICIENT GUARANTEE

The final targeted extension admits an explicitly supplied rational nonnegative
displacement vector ell with the same ket/bra exchange symmetry. The positivity,
normalizability, physicality and single-herald promises still apply. Displacement
must be nonnegative in this representation; this does not include arbitrary
complex phases. Schur elimination now leaves [[a,b],[b,a]] and singleton
weights (c,c), all nonnegative. Put
  H_n(a,c)=[z^n] exp(a z^2/2+c z).
The cross-count law becomes
  w(k)=b^k H_(h-k)(a,c)^2/k!,  0<=k<=h.
It need not be unimodal: an exact h=2 counterexample is preserved. However,
H_(2j) and H_(2j+1) are each log-concave in j. Each is a convolution of the
log-concave sequence (a/2)^q/q! with respectively c^(2r)/(2r)! or
c^(2r+1)/(2r+1)!. Convolution preserves log-concavity with no internal zeros.
Multiplication by the reciprocal factorial then proves log-concavity of each
parity subsequence of w. Two envelopes can therefore be combined without
acquiring either parity normalizer.

THEOREM D — UNIFORM COEFFICIENT EVALUATION.
For rational a,c>=0, binary n>=0 and p>=1, one can recognize H_n=0 or produce
an absolute-width 2^(-p) enclosure of log H_n in deterministic bit work
polynomial in the input bit length, log(n+1), and p. An exact expanded rational
coefficient is not promised: that output can itself require Omega(n) bits.

Proof mechanism. For a,c>0 define d=c/sqrt(a), sigma=(-1)^n and
m=(d+sqrt(d^2+4n))/2. Folding the Gaussian moment integral before numerical
evaluation gives the positive identity
  H_n=a^(n/2)/(n! sqrt(2pi)) integral_0^infinity
      t^n exp[-(t-d)^2/2] [1+sigma exp(-2dt)] dt.
Normalize the integrand by its value at t=m. Its integral I is at least 1/3,
its tails outside |t-m|<=R are at most 6 exp(-R^2/2), and its modulus is less
than 32 on radius-one disks about the retained interval when m>=4(R+1).
Odd parity uses -expm1(-2dt), preserving the common zero as c tends to zero.
These bounds are uniform in n and displacement. Take R=O(sqrt(p)); small n
then needs only O(p) terms of the positive finite sum.

For large n, certified Taylor quadrature supplies the implemented evaluator.
An independent explicit proof uses rational Lagrange interpolation on panels
of width 1/2. With q+1 equally spaced nodes, the pointwise error is at most
(128/3)(2/3)^(q+1), and the total absolute quadrature weight is at most
2R(2e)^q. Thus q=O(p+log R) and polynomial guard precision suffice. This
alternative closes the existence proof without assuming a derivative oracle
or an unproved conditioning bound for the implemented Taylor recurrence.
The logarithmic prefactor uses m-d=n/m and certified logGamma evaluation;
its cancellation requires O(log n) extra precision, not O(n) precision.
Full bounds, elementary cases and executable alternate quadrature are in
work/gaussian_displaced_audit/report.txt and work/gaussian_transfer/displaced_followup.txt.

For sampling, exact peak comparisons would hide an unnecessary precision cost.
Instead, bisect the monotone within-parity log ratio, stopping when its entire
enclosure lies in [-delta,delta], delta=1/[4(h+1)]. Any rise away from this
anchor is then less than a factor two. Inflating dyadic-block heights by two
gives a combined envelope with mass at most 12 times the target mass.
A common exact dyadic log reference, certified downward rounding, and capped
rejection give the requested TV approximation without a normalizing oracle.

Given k, two independent own-pair counts q have weights proportional to
  (a/2)^q c^(n-2q)/[q!(n-2q)!], n=h-k.
Their successive ratios decrease, so the established scalar sampler applies.
The remaining singleton counts are n-2q. A source Poisson count and reverse
Schur reconstruction complete the sample. The endpoint allocates epsilon/4
to ticket failures and 3epsilon/4 across at most max_scalar_calls+4 scalar
draws, including the three matching draws and source Poisson draw.

Consequently the supplied-model, one-herald polynomial-bit construction extends
to nonnegative displacement. The actual Python/Arb implementation remains
ordinary audited numerical software; uniform worst-case runtime and complete
formal verification of that implementation are not claimed. These coefficients
are classical Gaussian moments/Hermite and parabolic-cylinder quantities; see
https://dlmf.nist.gov/12.7#E3 and https://dlmf.nist.gov/12.10. Neither the integral
identity nor generic log-concave rejection is a novel principle. Priority of
this complete encoded conditional interface remains unresolved.
