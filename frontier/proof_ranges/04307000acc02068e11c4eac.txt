3. Replacement: distinct-neighbour mass and an exact composition law
For a real \(n\times n\) matrix \(M\), define
\[
\beta(M)=
\max_{w\in\Delta_n}\min_i(Mw)_i,
\qquad
\Delta_n=\{w\ge0:\mathbf1^Tw=1\}.
\]
For a graph adjacency matrix, \((Aw)_i\) is the total mass of the distinct outneighbours of \(i\).
The dual form is
\[
\beta(M)=
\min_{p\in\Delta_n}\max_j(M^Tp)_j.
\]
In particular, probability vectors satisfying
\[
Mw\ge b\mathbf1,\qquad M^Tp\le b\mathbf1
\]
certify \(\beta(M)=b\).
Exact substitution law
Replace vertex \(i\) of \(G\) by any nonempty finite digraph \(H_i\). As before, inter-block arcs are complete exactly where \(G\) has an arc. Set
\[
c_i=\beta(A_{H_i}).
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{
\beta(G[H_1,\ldots,H_n])
=
\beta\!\left(A_G+\operatorname{diag}(c_1,\ldots,c_n)\right).
}
\]
Proof. A probability vector on the expanded graph determines block masses \(w_i\) and conditional probability vectors \(q_i\) within the blocks.
For a vertex \(u\) in block \(i\), the outneighbour mass is
\[
(A_Gw)_i+w_i(A_{H_i}q_i)_u.
\]
The minimum over all vertices is therefore
\[
\min_i\left[
(A_Gw)_i+
w_i\min_u(A_{H_i}q_i)_u
\right].
\]
For fixed \(w\), each internal minimum is at most \(c_i\). These bounds can all be attained simultaneously, because the conditional vectors \(q_i\) can be chosen independently. Thus the best value for fixed \(w\) is
\[
\min_i\bigl[(A_Gw)_i+c_iw_i\bigr].
\]
Maximizing over \(w\) proves the identity. A zero block mass causes no difficulty because its internal contribution vanishes. \(\square\)
Consequence: invariance under acyclic substitution
Every finite acyclic digraph has a sink, so its \(\beta\)-value is zero. Consequently,
\[
\boxed{
\beta(G[H_1,\ldots,H_n])=\beta(G)
}
\]
whenever every \(H_i\) is acyclic, even when the block sizes differ.
For the spectral counterexamples,
\[
\beta(D_m)=\frac14
\]
for every \(m\), despite the spectral-radius density eventually exceeding \(1/3\).
This identifies a genuine difference between the representations: acyclic internal paths can increase spectral growth, but they cannot increase this worst-case distinct-neighbour mass. It still supplies no universal one-third bound.
