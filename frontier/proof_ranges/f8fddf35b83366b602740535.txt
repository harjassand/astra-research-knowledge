6. An explicit irrational threshold
Let \(F_k\) consist of \(k\geq2\) triangles sharing one central vertex and otherwise disjoint. The criterion gives
\[
\boxed{
p_c(F_k)
=
q_k
:=
\frac{1+\sqrt{1+8k}}2.
}
\tag{12}
\]
This family also has an elementary proof that does not require the general normal-polymatroid import.
Upper bound
For a forest with one distinguished root edge in each nontrivial component, integrate leaves toward that root. A root edge uses the total support-mass bound; every other forest edge uses a conditional section bound.
Average two constructions over the symmetries of \(F_k\):
- The star consisting of all \(2k\) spokes, with one spoke chosen as root.
- One rooted two-spoke tree, together with the \(k-1\) remaining rim edges as separate root components.
Mix the second construction with weight \(z\). Every edge obtains a coefficient strictly greater than one precisely when one can choose
\[
\frac{k}{p(k-1)}
<
z
<
\frac{p-1}{2(k-1)},
\qquad 0<z<1.
\]
Such a choice exists for
\[
p(p-1)>2k.
\]
The preceding dyadic summation therefore proves continuity for \(p>q_k\).
Matching lower bound
Set \(q=q_k\), let \(b\downarrow0\), and put
\[
a=b^{q-1},\qquad c=1-a-b,\qquad u=b^{-2/q},
\]
\[
z=\frac{1-a/b-bu}{c},
\qquad
w=\frac{1-bz}{c}.
\]
On three blocks \(A,B,C\) of measures \(a,b,c\), define
\[
W_b=
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & b^{-1} & 0\\
b^{-1} & u & z\\
0 & z & w
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{13}
\]
For sufficiently small \(b\), all entries are nonnegative. Direct substitution gives row integral one on all three blocks.
Moreover,
\[
\|W_b\|_q^q
=
2ab\,b^{-q}
+b^2u^q
+2bc\,z^q
+c^2w^q
\longrightarrow4.
\]
For every \(1\leq r<q\),
\[
\|W_b-1\|_r\longrightarrow0.
\]
Since \(q>2\), the associated operators converge to \(J\) even in Hilbert–Schmidt norm.
Nevertheless, put the central vertex of \(F_k\) in \(A\) and all outer vertices in \(B\). Its contribution is
\[
ab^{2k}(b^{-1})^{2k}u^k
=
b^{q-1-2k/q}
=
1.
\]
The all-\(C\) contribution tends to one. Consequently,
\[
\boxed{\liminf_{b\downarrow0}t(F_k,W_b)\geq2.}
\]
This proves failure at the exact threshold.
For the bowtie \(F_2\), every proper edge-subgraph reduces, after integrating out degree-one vertices, to a triangle or forest. Their densities tend to one in this construction. The full six-edge pattern retains a defect that every proper edge-subpattern misses.
The representation that failed
Equal-scale rooted-forest tests suggest the simpler quantity
\[
\max_{F\subseteq H}
\left(
2+\frac{|E(F)|-|V(F)|}{\nu(F)}
\right),
\]
where \(\nu(F)\) is matching number.
For the bowtie this is \(5/2\), whereas the exact threshold is
\[
\frac{1+\sqrt{17}}2>\frac52.
\]
The discrepancy is not a numerical issue. It comes from two different concentration scales. This is why the final criterion retains separate \(k_e\) and the full subset-deficit structure.
