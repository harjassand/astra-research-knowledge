Let G be any real homogeneous quartic on R^(2x2), invariant under left
SO(2). Write G(X)=T(X,X,X,X), T symmetric four-linear, and let M>0 bound
the four-linear operator norm. Suppose a number delta>0 satisfies

\[
q(R):=\tfrac12D^2G(I)[R,R]\ge\delta|R|^2
\quad\hbox{for every rank-one }R. \tag{1}
\]

Then, for H=((a-d)^2+(b+c)^2)^2,

\[
G+\alpha H\quad\hbox{is all-background quasiconvex whenever}\quad
\alpha\ge 25144M^2/\delta+(9391/4)M. \tag{2}
\]

The contract is every bounded planar Lipschitz domain, every affine B,
and every vector perturbation in W0^(1,4), with the periodic analogue.
An additional nu H gives the inherited Jensen margin
(4nu/1875) integral |Dphi|4. Any finite classical L4 Cauchy-Riemann
estimate gives an existential version; the displayed constants use the
inherited nonsharp sqrt(24) complex Beurling bound.

## Fourier second variation

For any smooth compactly supported vector field phi and X=Dphi,

\[
\int q(X)\ge\delta\int|X|^2. \tag{3}
\]

Indeed the Fourier transform of X has the rank-one form
i phihat(xi) tensor xi. Since q has real coefficients, its Hermitian
extension on complex phihat is the sum of its values on the real and
imaginary parts. Apply (1) separately to these two real rank-one matrices
and use Plancherel. Fourier normalization factors occur on both sides
and cancel. Pointwise positivity of q on arbitrary full-rank matrices
is neither assumed nor needed.

## Complete all-background Taylor estimate

Left SO(2) invariance permits a target rotation writing any background as
B=sI+E with s>=0 and E anticonformal. This follows by rotating the
conformal component to a nonnegative multiple of I; anticonformal
components remain anticonformal. Also |CB|2=2|E|2 for
CA=(a-d,b+c).

The exact quartic Bregman remainder is

\[
6T(B,B,X,X)+4T(B,X,X,X)+T(X,X,X,X).
\]

Its conformal quadratic term is s2 q(X), so (3) controls its integral.
Since |I|=sqrt(2), the two conformal cross terms satisfy

\[
12s|T(I,E,X,X)|
\le12\sqrt2M s|E||X|^2
\le\frac\delta4s^2|X|^2
 +\frac{288M^2}{\delta}|E|^2|X|^2,
\]
\[
4s|T(I,X,X,X)|
\le4\sqrt2M s|X|^3
\le\frac\delta4s^2|X|^2
 +\frac{32M^2}{\delta}|X|^4.
\]

The other terms have total negative bound
8M|E|2|X|2+3M|X|4, since
4M|E||X|3 <=2M|E|2|X|2+2M|X|4. Consequently

\[
\begin{split}
\int[G(B+X)-G(B)-DG(B):X]\ge{}&
\frac\delta2s^2\int|X|^2
 -(288M^2/\delta+8M)|E|^2\int|X|^2\\
&-(32M^2/\delta+3M)\int|X|^4. \tag{4}
\end{split}
\]

The H Bregman remainder has the pointwise bound

\[
H(B+X)-H(B)-DH(B):X
\ge |CB|^2|CX|^2+|CX|^4/5.
\]

For compatible zero-boundary gradients,

\[
\int|CX|^2=\int|X|^2,\qquad
\int|X|^4\le(625/4)\int|CX|^4.
\]

The first identity is the null integral of det X. The second is the
inherited nonsharp complex Cauchy-Riemann bound, also proved in the pinned
N873 source. Thus the choice

\[
\alpha\ge \tfrac12(288M^2/\delta+8M)
 +\tfrac{3125}4(32M^2/\delta+3M)
=25144M^2/\delta+(9391/4)M
\]

cancels both negative terms in (4). Linear terms integrate to zero.
Strong W^(1,4) approximation and zero extension give the stated bounded
domain contract. The same large-box cutoff argument as the inherited
completion proof gives the periodic contract. The stronger pointwise
inequality H(B+X)-H(B)-DH(B):X >=|CX|4/3 gives the reserve margin.

## Scope of the strictness parameter

If q is strictly positive on every unit rank-one matrix, compactness gives
a single delta>0 in (1), independent of B and phi. M and delta are
mathematical bounds. This lemma does not claim an optimal coefficient,
an algorithm for acquiring delta from arbitrary real data, or fixed-alpha
ROC/QC equality. It proves exactly the strict branch needed for the
existential H-projection composition.
