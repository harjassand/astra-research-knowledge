For an imaginary-antisymmetric witness \(f\) obeying
\[ \boxed{PAf=0} \]
and a real-symmetric witness \(g\), define
\[ \begin{aligned} L(f) &= 2\langle J,f\rangle-\langle f,Sf\rangle -\langle Af,S^+Af\rangle,\\ U(g) &= \langle J-Ag,S_{\mathrm{odd}}^{-1}(J-Ag)\rangle +\langle g,Sg\rangle . \end{aligned} \]
Then
\[ L(f)/n\le\kappa_n\le U(g)/n. \]
More importantly, their difference is exactly computable:
\[ \boxed{ U(g)-L(f) = \|Sf-(J-Ag)\|_{S^+}^{\,2} + \|Sg+Af\|_{S^+}^{\,2}. } \]
This is a certificate, not an error estimate divided by an unknown relaxation time.
The underlying symmetric/skew variational reasoning has precedent in nonreversible Dirichlet principles. The contribution being investigated is its explicit constrained implementation and consequences for this physical task, not a claim to have invented variational response theory. Given an approximate off-diagonal witness \(g_0\), the required diagonal correction \(\phi\) solves
\[ M\phi=-b, \qquad M=-PA S_{\mathrm{odd}}^{-1}AP. \]
For hopping amplitudes \(t_e\),
\[ \boxed{ (M\phi)(\eta) = \frac{2}{d} \sum_{e=\{i,j\}}t_e^2 \big[\phi(\eta)-\phi(\eta^{ij})\big]. } \]
This is a classical exclusion-process Laplacian.
It is an exact static constraint operator—not an assertion that the quantum dynamics has become classical. The diagonal interactions disappear from this particular constraint equation because they commute with \(\phi\), not because their dynamical effects are neglected.
There is also a constructive representation advantage. Exchanges preserve the degree of a diagonal monomial \(Z_C=\prod_{i\in C}Z_i\). A source of degree at most \(p\) can therefore be corrected in a space of dimension
\[ N_p=\sum_{k=0}^{p}\binom nk. \]
For fixed \(p\), exact rational solution and verification have polynomial cost in \(n\) and the input bit length.
The remaining difficulty is not hidden: I have not proved that high-accuracy transport always admits a small \(p\) or a compact off-diagonal witness.
Dropping the balance constraint gives a false positive even for
\[ S=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}, \qquad A=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}, \qquad J=(1,0). \]
The true response is zero. An inadmissible trial that simply discards its undamped residual produces a purported lower bound of one.
This is why exact balance is load-bearing rather than optional numerical refinement.
A finite-ring inverse calculation would not suffice. The investigation completes the following additional argument.
First, take the infinite-system limit at a positive Laplace parameter \(\lambda\). Finite-range locality and a uniform contraction bound justify that limit.
Second, eliminate the even-parity block. The regularized current response is governed by the positive operator
\[ T_\lambda = S_{\mathrm{odd}}+\lambda I + C^*(S_{\mathrm{even}}+\lambda I)^{-1}C. \]
After subtracting \(\lambda I\), this operator is monotone as \(\lambda\downarrow0\), while the odd subspace has the directly known damping floor \(d\). This establishes existence of the zero-frequency limit without supplying a global relaxation rate.
Third, the evenly extended stationary current correlation has a positive spectral measure. The proof uses that positivity, with explicit tail bounds, to turn the Laplace-limit statement into the ordinary long-time limit of the integrated current variance.
Finally, the continuity equation gives
\[ \frac{d^2}{dT^2} \sum_x x^2 \langle e^{T\mathcal L}(n_x-\tfrac12),n_0-\tfrac12\rangle = 2\sum_x\langle e^{T\mathcal L}j_x,j_0\rangle. \]
These steps connect the current certificate to the actual density-response second moment.
No uncontrolled interchange of the finite-size and infinite-time limits is used. Conversely, the argument does not provide a useful convergence rate for a finite experiment or establish a Gaussian density profile.
