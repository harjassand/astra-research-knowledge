## Integration-derived fixed-charge theorem and decisive counterexample

The full proof is in `derived/FIXED_CHARGE_THEOREM.md`; source and new material
must remain separately attributed.

For finite nonnegative integer N_A,N_C, assume h_j conserve local total charge
and fix Q. Allow every finite nonnegative-integer-graded R, with arbitrary charge
multiplicities, and all K commuting N_C+N_R. Define V_Q as local charge<=Q.
Then the sharp closure comparison for total-Q ground energies is

    L_Q=max_{Phi: every Kraus commutes with N_A}
        lambda_min_{V_Q}[h1-Phi(h0)],
    inf Delta_Q=L_Q(h0,h1), sup Delta_Q=-L_Q(h1,h0).

This is a finite SDP with Choi support constrained to equal input/output active
charges. Covariance alone is insufficient. The converse constructs a
charge-compatible purification, proves every fixed-marginal total-Q state is
reachable with block-charge Kraus operators, uses the correct sectorwise
strictly feasible primal, and extracts a block-charge dual Z. Its quantitative
smoothing uses tau=min{1,epsilon/[4(B0+B1)]} when B0+B1>0; the zero-norm case
has both local Hamiltonians zero and is exact without smoothing. It needs no
negative charges or externally supplied marginal. R dimension at most D_Q
suffices. A preassigned restricted charge spectrum, locality, bounded K,
non-Abelian symmetry or generic fermionic locality are not covered.

For the original motif at fixed Q=2 (two down spins among four, half filling),
take the exactly common local collar field

    K=100*(2 I-N2-N3),  R trivial.

The source interval is invalid for this different output. An exact
six-dimensional rational positive-definite shift and trial vector certify

    E1^2(K)>=-9/97,
    E0^2(K)<=-1/4,
    Delta_2(K)>=61/388>0.157.

Numerical diagnostic: `Delta_2(K)=+0.2499742386534716`. The positive conclusion
comes from the rational bound. An explicit source-channel calculation shows
`Phi(P_active_charge0)=P_active_charge0/4`, and the optimized rational Choi has
nonzero forbidden equal-charge-complement entries. The obstruction is exactly
failure to preserve the requested sector.

The repaired charge-aware universal interval for this motif/Q is precisely
`[-3/4,+1/4]` in closure. Identity attains the perturbation bounds as feasible
certificates. Two local minimax states prove that no strong-charge channel can
improve either endpoint: an active singlet with an empty-charge collar for
the forward order, and `|up up down down>` for the reverse order. Details and
finite environment constructions are in the proof.

Twenty independently generated full-rank states on a six-dimensional total-Q
space, with nontrivial cross-active-charge coherences, were reconstructed from
their fixed-marginal purifications by the grouped charge-preserving channel
construction. Maximum normalization/state errors were below2.23e-15, and
charge leakage was exactly zero numerically. These finite diagnostics exercise
the new reachability step but do not prove the universal theorem.

Charge symmetry reduces the Choi block dimension to `sum_q d_q^2`. For k binary
occupancy variables this is `binom(2k,k)`, whose squared entry count still grows
as `16^k/(pi*k)`. The theorem removes a sector-interface gap; it does not remove
the main exponential computational bottleneck. It should guide a truthful
fixed-electron-number research contract rather than be promoted as an efficient
chemistry algorithm.
