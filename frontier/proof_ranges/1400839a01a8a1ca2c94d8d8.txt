## Covalent fractionation: hypothesis and exact rejection criterion

The proposed operation loads a mixed H/D surface population, strips H
preferentially, and separately collects the retained D-rich inventory. The
elementary screening inequality follows by integrating the two logarithmic
hazards. Starting at D fraction0.10, reaching0.95 at D recovery>=1/2 requires
a uniform stripping hazard allowance of at least1+log(171)/log2≈8.41785.
These are chosen experimental criteria. Readsorption, isotope exchange and
heterogeneous/mixed loading lie outside the stripping-only calculation.

[Soong et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12663420/) establish
reversible electrochemical hydrogenation and different isotope responses on
graphene. Their separate-isotope experiment supports the material lead; it
does not measure the proposed mixed-inventory separation. The reported coverage
is an operating point, not a maximum-capacity theorem. The mixed hazards,
loading partition, side reactions and product balance are missing.

The24.3pL collector calculation has its stated single-cycle liquid-carrier
scope. Multi-cycle, exchange-assisted, aprotic and gas collection need their
own balance; they are not ruled out or demonstrated. Source purity, collection,
energy, full-cycle frequency and endurance are unresolved. A zero-point-energy
Boltzmann factor is not a measured rate or separation factor.

Graphene membrane pumping is established prior art; the
[2017 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.07562v2) reports separation and projects
scale/energy benefits. These projections are not plant demonstrations. The2026
PNAS H-bond-motif separator was bibliographically confirmed, but its full text
was not recovered in this root pass. No numerical strongest-method advantage
is claimed from its inaccessible details. A full priority search is still open.
