DIRECTOR ELLIPTIC SOURCE COUNTEREXAMPLE V1
2026-10-09. Exact internally derived counterexample to a stated auxiliary lemma. It does not by itself disprove the source's final Green-kernel rank theorem.

Primary source actually accessed
Mario Bebendorf, Low-rank approximation of elliptic boundary value problems with high-contrast coefficients, arXiv1410.3717v1.
HTML https://arxiv.org/html/1410.3717v1, Appendix Lemma8 and Eq9, Theorem4.

The PDF text was independently checked, so this is not solely an HTML rendering issue.

1. Exact premise being challenged

Lemma8 allows every harmonic u in H1(D), where D is a bounded domain and K is a C1,1 subdomain. It asserts a constant depending on D,K but independent of u, with
  ||u||_(1,D) <= C(D,K)||u||_(1,K).
The source defines ||u||_(1,U)^2 = ||u||_L2(U)^2 + diam(U)^2 ||grad u||_L2(U)^2.
There is no finite-dimensional or polynomial-degree cutoff in the lemma statement. The text immediately before Eq9 specifically says the analysis cannot restrict to H1_0(D).

2. Three-dimensional exact counterexample

Let D=B_1(0) in R3 and K=B_r(0), with any fixed 0<r<1. Both domains are smooth. For each positive integer n let
  u_n(x1,x2,x3)=Re((x1+i x2)^n).
This real homogeneous polynomial is harmonic: its x1,x2 Laplacian vanishes by holomorphy, and its x3 derivative vanishes. It belongs to H1(D), is nonzero, and is homogeneous of degree n.

By the change of variables x=r z,
  ||u_n||_L2(B_r)^2 = r^(2n+3)||u_n||_L2(B_1)^2,
  ||grad u_n||_L2(B_r)^2 = r^(2n+1)||grad u_n||_L2(B_1)^2.
Since diam(B_r)=2r, these identities yield EXACTLY
  ||u_n||_(1,B_r)^2 = r^(2n+3)||u_n||_(1,B_1)^2.
Therefore
  ||u_n||_(1,D)/||u_n||_(1,K) = r^(-n-3/2) -> infinity.
No finite C(D,K) independent of u can satisfy the stated lemma. For the ordinary unweighted H1 norm the ratio also diverges, so the conclusion is not a notation issue.

3. Checks against hidden restrictions

The dimensions match the source's R3 Newton-kernel appendix. D and K satisfy boundedness and C1,1 regularity, with K compactly contained in D. Every u_n is harmonic throughout D. No boundary value is required by Lemma8. For the use inside X(D), choose a larger ambient domain Omega containing closure(D), so the imposed zero condition on boundary(D) intersect boundary(Omega) is vacuous. Set coefficient A=I and v=u_n. Then v is A-harmonic and the auxiliary harmonic lift phi_v^D equals v, so the same example violates the reverse flux inequality Eq9 on the actual infinite-dimensional source space.

4. Scope of the consequence

Theorem4's proof invokes this reverse-domain norm transfer, via Eq9, to convert local flux approximation to an ordinary L2 approximation with its displayed coefficient dependence. That proof chain cannot be treated as established as written. Unique continuation says the interior seminorm is definite on harmonic functions; it does NOT provide a uniform bounded inverse for restriction to a smaller domain. Thus source Lemma2's positivity may survive even though the quantitative reverse bound fails.

This record does not claim that lowrank Green approximation is impossible, that the final rank statement is false, or that no alternative proof exists.
