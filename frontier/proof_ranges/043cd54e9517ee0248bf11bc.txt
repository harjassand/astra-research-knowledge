For $\theta\succ0$ on its support define
\[
 \Sld_\theta(X)=\tfrac12(\theta X+X\theta),\qquad
 q_\theta(X)=\langle X,\Sld_\theta^{-1}X\rangle_{\rm HS}.
\]
Measured Pearson divergence satisfies
$\chi^2_{\rm m}(\rho\|\sigma)=q_\sigma(\rho-\sigma)$, with the usual extended support convention.
For channels $\M,\N$ with common input, write $\M\succeq_{\rm SLD}\N$ when
\[
 \chi^2_{\rm m}(\M\rho\|\M\sigma)\ge
 \chi^2_{\rm m}(\N\rho\|\N\sigma)\quad\hbox{for every state pair }\rho,\sigma. \tag{1}
\]
The superscript $c$ requires this after every finite untouched quantum reference.
Set, for $k\ge2$,
\[
 h_k(p)=\frac1k+\frac{(p-1/k)^2}{1-1/k}
       =p^2+\frac{(1-p)^2}{k-1}. \tag{2}
\]

\textbf{Theorem 1 (operational transfer).} Suppose $\M\succeq_{\rm SLD}\N$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item For every $k$-letter input ensemble, with arbitrary priors, its optimal guessing probabilities obey
\[
 \boxed{\ p_{\rm guess}(\M)\ge h_k(p_{\rm guess}(\N)).\ } \tag{3}
\]
A single explicit physical POVM on the $\M$ output achieves the bound.
\item For every fixed encoding $\mathcal E$ of a $d$-dimensional logical system, $d\ge2$, let $F^*(\Phi\mathcal E)=\max_{\mathcal D}\Fe(\mathcal D\Phi\mathcal E)$, where $\Fe$ is overlap with the normalized maximally entangled logical state. Then
\[
 \boxed{\ F^*(\M\mathcal E)\ge h_{d^2}(F^*(\N\mathcal E)).\ } \tag{4}
\]
An explicit CPTP decoder on the $\M$ output achieves the right side.
\end{enumerate}
Thus optimal average message error and logical entanglement infidelity transfer with
$\varepsilon_\M\le2\varepsilon_\N-k\varepsilon_\N^2/(k-1)$, using $k$ letters in (3) and $k=d^2$ in (4). These are averaged criteria, not worst-case input or diamond-norm guarantees. For $k=1$ or $d=1$ the task is trivial.

\textit{Proof of (3).} Write $\theta_x=\M(\rho_x)$, $\theta=\sum_xp_x\theta_x$, and use $\beta_x,\beta$ for $\N$. Compress to $P=\supp\theta$; every $\theta_x$ with nonzero prior lies in $P$. The inverse is completely positive there because
\[
 \Sld_\theta^{-1}(C)=2\int_0^\infty e^{-t\theta}C e^{-t\theta}\,dt. \tag{5}
\]
Consequently $G_x=\Sld_\theta^{-1}(p_x\theta_x)$ is a POVM on $P$, since $\sum_xG_x=\Sld_\theta^{-1}(\theta)=I_P$. Complete it arbitrarily on $P^\perp$. Its success is
\[
 Q_\M=\sum_xq_\theta(p_x\theta_x)
 =\sum_xp_x^2[1+\chi^2_{\rm m}(\theta_x\|\theta)]. \tag{6}
\]
Zero-prior terms are omitted in the right side of (6). Apply (1) to each $(\rho_x,\bar\rho)$ to obtain $Q_\M\ge Q_\N$.
For a target POVM $\{F_x\}$ with success $p$, set $A_x=p_x\beta_x-\beta/k$ and $B_x=F_x-I/k$. Then
\[
 \sum_xq_\beta(A_x)=Q_\N-1/k,\quad
 \sum_x\Tr A_xB_x=p-1/k,\quad
 \sum_x\langle B_x,\Sld_\beta B_x\rangle
 =\sum_x\Tr\beta F_x^2-1/k\le1-1/k.
\]
Metric Cauchy--Schwarz gives $(p-1/k)^2\le(Q_\N-1/k)(1-1/k)$. Optimize $p$ and use the source POVM. No common simulation map or positivity of a signed lift is used.

\textit{Proof of (4).} For $L$ with $d$-dimensional logical input put $\theta=L(I/d)$ and, on its effective output support, define
\[
 \mathcal R_L=\frac1dL^*\circ\Sld_\theta^{-1},\qquad
 F_{\rm SLD}(L)=\Fe(\mathcal R_LL)
 =\frac1{d^3}\sum_{ij}q_\theta(L(E_{ij})). \tag{7}
\]
Equation (5) proves complete positivity. Moreover $\mathcal R_L^*(I)=\Sld_\theta^{-1}(\theta)=I$, so it is trace preserving. Extend to unused output levels by a CPTP replacer, discarding cross blocks. The ambient Moore--Penrose superoperator alone need not be CP.

For any decoder $\mathcal D$ set $U_{ij}=L(E_{ij})-\delta_{ij}\theta$ and $V_{ij}=\mathcal D^*(E_{ij})-\delta_{ij}I/d$. Direct expansion gives
\[
 \sum_{ij}q_\theta(U_{ij})=d^3F_{\rm SLD}(L)-d,\quad
 \sum_{ij}\langle U_{ij},V_{ij}\rangle=d^2[\Fe(\mathcal DL)-d^{-2}].
\]
Since $\mathcal D^*$ is unital CP, Schwarz and $\sum_{ij}E_{ij}^*E_{ij}=dI$ yield
\[
 \sum_{ij}\langle V_{ij},\Sld_\theta V_{ij}\rangle\le d-d^{-1}.
\]
Thus
$[\Fe(\mathcal DL)-d^{-2}]^2\le[F_{\rm SLD}(L)-d^{-2}](1-d^{-2})$.
The optimal fidelity is at least $d^{-2}$, by a replacer decoder, so $F_{\rm SLD}(L)\ge h_{d^2}(F^*(L))$.

Finally, (1) is equivalent to the local inequalities $q_{\M\sigma}(\M H)\ge q_{\N\sigma}(\N H)$ for traceless Hermitian $H$ at faithful $\sigma$: apply (1) to $\sigma+tH,\sigma$, and cancel $t^2$; the reverse uses $H=\rho-\sigma$. Sum at $\sigma=I/d$ over a Hermitian orthonormal basis. The identity direction $I/\sqrt d$ contributes exactly $d$ on each side, hence $F_{\rm SLD}(\M)\ge F_{\rm SLD}(\N)$. Input preprocessing preserves (1), proving (4) for every encoder. Common faithful regularization covers boundary input pairs.

\textbf{Exact sharpness and a failed stronger claim.} Let $0<a<1$. On a classical binary input, let $\M$ reveal the input with probability $a^2$ and otherwise erase it; let $\N$ be the BSC of bias $a$. The signed observable lift
\[
 T=\begin{pmatrix}(1+1/a)/2&(1-1/a)/2\\
 (1-1/a)/2&(1+1/a)/2\\1/2&1/2\end{pmatrix}
\]
has row sums one, $\M T=\N$, and $D_{n_x}-T^{\mathsf T}D_{m_x}T=0$ for each input letter. On off-diagonal target matrix units the remaining outer square defect has nonnegative entries $n_x(0),n_x(1)$; the input first dephases, so that defect is CP. This certifies dominance even for every quantum reference. Uniform-prior success is
\[
 p_\M=(1+a^2)/2=h_2((1+a)/2)=h_2(p_\N),\qquad
 \frac{\varepsilon_\M}{\varepsilon_\N}=1+a\longrightarrow2. \tag{8}
\]
Thus (3) is exact at $k=2$ and no universal linear error factor below two is possible. Yet $p_\M<p_\N$: a universal physical simulator and exact guessing-probability dominance are false. The quadratic bridge provides a weaker, quantitatively controlled operational order.
