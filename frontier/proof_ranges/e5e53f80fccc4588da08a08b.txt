5. NODE-CENSUS ACQUISITION COST: A MATCHED WEAK-COUPLING EXAMPLE

Even within the homogeneous irreducible SPD M-matrix class, exact census
does not provide a uniform finite-noise capability. Compare alpha=1 and
  K2=[[2,-epsilon],[-epsilon,3]], physical N=2,
with physical N=3 and
  K3=[[2,-epsilon/sqrt(2),-epsilon/sqrt(2)],
      [-epsilon/sqrt(2),3.5,-0.5],
      [-epsilon/sqrt(2),-0.5,3.5]],
for 0<epsilon<=1/2. Both are irreducible SPD M-matrices. Their one-port
linear transfer functions agree exactly. Their reduced systems share
Kbar=K2, with cubic coefficients (1,b), where b is respectively 1 or 1/2:
  ydot=-2y+epsilon z-y^3+u,
  zdot=epsilon y-3z-b z^3.

For |u|<=U, positive cubic damping and lambda_min(Kbar)>=1 give
||(y,z)||<=U and |z|<=epsilon U/3. Put Y=sup|delta y| and
Z=sup|delta z| for the two systems on any finite interval. Monotonicity
of the cubic gives
  Y<=epsilon Z/2,
  Z<=epsilon Y/3 + epsilon^3 U^3/162.
Since epsilon^2/6<=1/24<1/2,
  Z<=epsilon^3 U^3/81,
  Y<=epsilon^4 U^3/162.
These uniform bounds hold for every bounded input and every observation
duration. The signal distinguishing node counts 2 and 3 may therefore be
arbitrarily small while both networks satisfy all census promises.

With N_read independent Gaussian readouts of variance sigma^2, the
KL bound is O(N_read U^6 epsilon^8/sigma^2). Constant-error discrimination
requires N_read=Omega(sigma^2/(U^6 epsilon^8)). This is a real acquisition
limit, not merely bad conditioning of our particular Lie-jet formulas.
The bound also applies by conditional KL to adaptive bounded-input
protocols, since the same realized observation history prescribes the
same bounded input in the two conditional models.

Recovering an integer block size m from alphabar=alpha/m also needs
precision fine enough to distinguish alpha/m from alpha/(m+1), a gap
alpha/[m(m+1)]. Exact rank identities and rational integer ratios must
not be described as robust statistical certificates without a separate
noise, separation and precision analysis.

The census is an internally proved, concrete structural consequence.

nonlinear realization/synchrony theory, and landmark significance are OPEN.
