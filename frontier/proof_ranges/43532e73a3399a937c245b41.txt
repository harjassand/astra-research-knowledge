## Nuisance-separation criterion

For an angular cell, let y be the vector of corrected observations across acquired energy/radial channels. Suppose the experimentally justified linear model is

    y = a g + U b + e.

Here g is the chemical-order response, columns of U are acquired nuisance responses, b are unknown amplitudes, and e is bounded noise/model error. Uniform identification of a in this model is possible exactly when g is outside the column span of U. If g lies in that span, changing a can be compensated by changing b; nonnegative-amplitude models retain ambiguous interior cases unless extra budgets are known.

For g outside that span, let P be the orthogonal projector onto its complement. Then

    w = P g/(g^T P g),  a = w^T y - w^T e.

This follows because w^T g=1 and w^T U=0. It also supplies a stability bound |error in a|<=||w|| ||e||; rank alone is insufficient when the denominator is small. At least rank(U)+1 independent channels are required in the unrestricted-amplitude model. The word "channels" includes radial, polarization or other contrast channels; two arbitrary photon energies are not universally sufficient. Oxides containing Fe can also resonate, so an Fe-edge enhancement alone does not certify that g is outside the acquired nuisance span.

No g or U response curves for an axis-writing specimen were acquired here. The arrays in `measurement_results.json` are synthetic algebra checks. This rank gate must be evaluated on raw controls before claiming a chemical-axis measurement. Averaging opposite magnetic fields removes charge-magnetic interference terms odd in magnetization, but not an even magnetic intensity contribution; polarization/geometry controls or a validated small-contribution bound are still required.

## Angular completeness and uncertainty

A finite list of pole intensities does not determine an arbitrary orientation tensor. Six matrix components do not mean six sampled pole directions suffice: those are values of an orientation density, not quadratic projections of its moment. Full angular acquisition, an explicitly validated finite-support model, or rigorous unsampled-mass bounds are needed.

Suppose corrected chemical pole mass a_j is acquired as an integral over angular cell j, the cells cover the unoriented sphere, and every n in that cell lies within antipodal angle delta of representative n_j. Set A=sum a_j and

    Q_hat = sum a_j n_j n_j^T/A.

The identity ||nn^T-n_j n_j^T||_op=sin(angle(n,n_j)) gives

    ||Q_order-Q_hat||_op <= sin(delta).

For D=Q_xx-Q_yy, the absolute geometric error is at most 2 sin(delta). This uses the nuclear norm of the rank-two dyad difference, whose two nonzero singular values are sin(angle). It does not require a smooth orientation distribution. Two-degree cells, for example, give operator error at most .0349 and D error at most .0698 before counting/calibration errors.

If the observed integrated mass is A and an independently established missing mass is at most m_max, write d_obs=trace(diag(1,-1,0) W_obs). Without restrictions on missing-axis directions, the sharp interval is

    (d_obs-m_max)/(A+m_max) <= D_order <= (d_obs+m_max)/(A+m_max).

It is sharp when the missed region permits x and y axes; otherwise use the extrema of n_x^2-n_y^2 on that region. With no bound on missing mass, the observed poles cannot exclude nearly arbitrary missing texture. Register the illuminated region during tilts: changing beam footprint can change which islands are sampled and mimic texture.

For a background-free order-pole sample with N independent photons, the D sample mean has variance at most 1/N. For two independent equal-count intervention arms the variance of their contrast is at most 2/N. These are conditional counting bounds, not an exposure forecast. Background subtraction, resonance contrast weights and weak-signal extraction increase variance. The G17 calculated powder intensity table cannot be converted into a same-specimen photon budget without actual flux, background and geometry measurements.

## Upgrade to the volume-axis target and causal rewrite

If every included orientation has the same nonzero S, Q_order=Q_volume. That equality must be measured or independently justified; it fails in the exact counterexample above. Orientation-resolved S_i and a_i permit f_i=a_i/S_i^2. A common independent lower bound s, absolute W and phase volume f instead give sharp semidefinite bounds described in `MATRIX_SANDWICH_AUDIT.md`. Aggregate powder or film order values are not common lower bounds.

After the tensor is identified, demonstrate load transfer into reacting grains and control mass loss, nucleation, dissolution, fragmentation, growth and particle rotation. Track marked parent lattice orientations with fundamental maps and microscopy. A population tensor change alone does not prove that a fixed grain's chemical axis turned. A21's images were cut after each stage and do not revisit the same location, so those published images do not already establish the stronger rewrite claim.

Use matched orthogonal loading, unloaded chemistry, final-metal loading, and nitrogen-free direct-stress/field annealing where physically comparable. Retain raw count maps, uncertainty propagation and predeclared acceptance thresholds through x-to-y repeated cycles. Report undefined Q when no ordered phase exists. No thermal/chemical apparatus design should assume a favorable stress response before this gate passes.
