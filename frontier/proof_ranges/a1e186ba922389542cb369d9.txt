5. The reference current must also conserve exactly
Correctly sketching \(\Gamma\) is not sufficient. An approximate load vector can accidentally inject a tiny current into a component that is about to become isolated. Its apparent energy can then diverge even though the physical terminal resistance remains finite.
The repair is to approximate the feasible current, not independently rounded potential drops.
Compute an approximate baseline potential \(v\), and form
\[
\boxed{
\widehat j
=
WB^\top v+T(f-Lv).
}
\tag{18}
\]
Then
\[
B\widehat j=f
\tag{19}
\]
exactly.
Choose the solve accuracy so that
\[
\widehat j^\top W^{-1}\widehat j
\le(1+\eta^2)c.
\tag{20}
\]
Define
\[
\widehat z=W^{-1}\widehat j.
\]
A uniform feasible-current lemma
Because \(\widehat j-j_*\) is a circulation, electrical optimality gives the exact identity
\[
\widehat j^\top W^{-1}\widehat j-c
=
\|W^{-1/2}(\widehat j-j_*)\|^2.
\tag{21}
\]
For every support \(S\), let
\[
e_S=\widehat z_S-z_S.
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{
e_S\in\operatorname{range}(\Gamma_{SS}),
\qquad
e_S^\top\Gamma_{SS}^+e_S\le\eta^2c.
}
\tag{22}
\]
Proof. Put
\[
h=W^{-1/2}(\widehat j-j_*).
\]
Conservation implies \(\Pi h=h\), and (21) gives \(\|h\|^2\le\eta^2c\).
For
\[
A_S=E_S^\top W^{-1/2}\Pi,
\]
we have
\[
\Gamma_{SS}=A_SA_S^\top,\qquad e_S=A_Sh.
\]
The minimum-norm solution characterization gives
\[
e_S^\top(A_SA_S^\top)^+e_S\le\|h\|^2.
\]
This proves both claims. ∎
Because \(M_d\succeq\Gamma_{SS}\), the variational characterization of inverse quadratic forms further gives
\[
e_S^\top M_d^+e_S\le\eta^2c.
\tag{23}
\]
This estimate has no factor involving the smallest positive eigenvalue of the damaged graph or of the selected cycle matrix.
Exact detection of disconnected terminals
Every vector in \(\ker M_d\) also lies in \(\ker\Gamma_{SS}\). Equation (22) therefore implies
\[
\widehat z_S\perp\ker M_d
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
z_S\perp\ker M_d.
\tag{24}
\]
Together with the exact kernel equality from (17), this makes the small linear-system consistency test detect the correct infinite-resistance cases.
Relative error for finite resistance
Write
\[
q_d=z_S^\top M_d^+z_S,\qquad c_d=c+q_d.
\]
By (23),
\[
\left|
\widehat z_S^\top M_d^+\widehat z_S-q_d
\right|
\le
2\eta\sqrt{cq_d}+\eta^2c
\le
(\eta+\eta^2)c_d.
\tag{25}
\]
