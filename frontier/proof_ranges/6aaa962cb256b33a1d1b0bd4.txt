5. What remains unresolved
The constructive gap is still the passage from reconstructions that work separately for individual tests to one linear, Hermiticity-preserving reconstruction that satisfies all reference-coupled tests simultaneously. The inspected paper obtains that conclusion for a stronger, Hermitian-smoothed comparison and for full-span sources; it does not establish the unrestricted converse. 

The counterexample does not refute that converse. Its comparable endpoint has a physical lift; its nearby full-span points are not comparable. It instead eliminates approximation as a general way to close the gap.
