## 5. An elementary dimension-asymmetric separable ball

**Lemma.** If E=E* acts on C^r tensor C^b and

\[
 \|E\|_\infty\le\epsilon\le\frac1{2r-1},
\]

then I+E is separable. The dimension b does not enter the bound.

**Proof.** Write E as r-by-r blocks E_ij. Each block has operator norm at most epsilon. For i<j define

\[
 H_{ij}=(E_{ij}+E_{ij}^*)/2,\quad
 K_{ij}=(E_{ij}-E_{ij}^*)/(2i),
\]

both Hermitian contractions of norm at most epsilon. Let P_ij=|i><i|+|j><j|, X_ij=|i><j|+|j><i|, and Y_ij=i(|i><j|-|j><i|). The two matrices

\[
 \epsilon P_{ij}\otimes I+X_{ij}\otimes H_{ij},\quad
 \epsilon P_{ij}\otimes I+Y_{ij}\otimes K_{ij}
\]

are separable. For example the first equals

\[
 \frac{P_{ij}+X_{ij}}2\otimes(\epsilon I+H_{ij})
 +\frac{P_{ij}-X_{ij}}2\otimes(\epsilon I-H_{ij}),
\]

a sum of positive tensors. The analogous identity holds for Y and K. Sum these over i<j. Their off-diagonal blocks reproduce all of E's off-diagonal blocks. The remaining diagonal block i is

\[
 I+E_{ii}-2\epsilon(r-1)I\succeq[1-(2r-1)\epsilon]I\succeq0.
\]

Those diagonal blocks are themselves separable tensors. This proves the lemma. No commutativity of the blocks is assumed. The result is not claimed to be an optimal or historically new separable-ball radius.

## 6. From small Schmidt norm to genuine r-EB maps

Now choose the good realization under d>=C r^3 and q_r(G)<=1/(64r). Keep W, rho, P from (10),(12).

For every complex isometry U:C^s -> C^d with s<=r, any vector in the range of U tensor I has Schmidt rank at most s. Therefore

\[
 \|(U^*\otimes I)G(U\otimes I)\|_\infty\le q_s(G)\le q_r(G).
\]

Consequently the first-factor compression of W is

\[
 (U^*\otimes I)W(U\otimes I)=I_s\otimes I_d+E_U,
\qquad \|E_U\|_\infty\le1/(2r)\le1/(2s-1).
\]

By Section 5 **every such compression is separable**, not merely positive or PPT.

To see that this is precisely enough for r-EB, take any pure input vector psi in C^r tensor C^d, of Schmidt rank s<=r. It can be written as

\[
 \psi=(A\otimes V)\Omega_s,
\]

where V:C^s -> C^d is an isometry, A:C^s -> C^r is arbitrary, and Omega_s=sum_i e_i tensor e_i is unnormalized. Direct expansion of the matrix units gives

\[
 (\operatorname{id}_r\otimes P)(|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|)
 =(A\otimes I)(V^T\otimes I)J(P)(\overline V\otimes I)(A^*\otimes I). \tag{14}
\]

The middle factor is the compression with U=overline V, divided by d. It is separable; the outer local filter preserves separability. Every positive input is a finite sum of such pure projectors, proving r-EB.

The adjoint map has coefficient matrix g^T. Its Choi perturbation is obtained by swapping the two tensor factors of G (because G is real). Swap preserves the Schmidt ranks in (6), the operator norm, and the exact transpose symmetries. The same argument proves r-EB for P*.

Indecomposability and the diamond-norm gap follow from Section 4, since q_r<=1/(64r)<=1/64. This proves Theorem A.
