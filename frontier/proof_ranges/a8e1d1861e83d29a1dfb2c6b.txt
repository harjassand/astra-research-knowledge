10. Resource accounting and supported outcome
For the certified infinite-spectrum class, the additional costs are explicit: interior-point and covariance linear algebra, exact cutoff calculation, generation of the required covariance prefix, and any extra output modes requested for waveform accuracy. The earlier compiler’s integration, sampling, root isolation, finite-precision and memory costs remain charged.
