One fixed, parameter-independent, noisy separable measurement is uniformly competitive with the quantum information metric in every bounded-incidence local parameter direction.
Equation (20) remains conditional on N711. The unconditional theorem controls additive loss; it does not prevent a poorly conditioned direction from remaining poorly conditioned.
Nor does either result automatically outperform existing learning algorithms. Arbitrary-temperature Hamiltonian learning and efficient local lattice learning already have substantial positive results.
