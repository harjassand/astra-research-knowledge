## 7. What survives the priority comparison

The constructive result is broader than the original triangle setting. It applies to arbitrary channels with a returned source port, outputs an actual quantum operation, and has a reference-preserving error guarantee with an optimal sample order. **None of its proofs uses Fritz’s embedding or MIP\(^*=\)RE.**

But the comparison materially limits the novelty claim.

Coherent channel filtering and one-query pre/post-processing are already developed in the quantum-filter literature. Programmable measurements already supply close relatives of the pure-state special case. Random-purification and random-Stinespring constructions already establish major ways of replacing inaccessible descriptions by operationally useful quantum representations. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.20173v2)

The possible contribution remaining here is therefore narrower:

> **The explicit arbitrary-mixed-state, arbitrary-channel, one-query formula (4), with its completely positive error decomposition, flagged diamond-norm guarantee, and sample-access resource accounting.**

I have not established whether that exact combined statement is already explicit—or an immediate specialization—in the prior literature. More importantly, I have not established a consequential application that improves the best end-to-end complexity of an independently major problem.

Those are substantive unresolved obligations, not merely presentation work. They prevent treating the result as completion of Genesis.

The investigation does establish a useful answer to the original implementation question:

**A state-specific existential replacement need not be implemented by reconstructing its hidden matrices. Independent quantum replicas can implement a related, context-composable operation directly. But that does not provide unrestricted purification access, preserve every desired residual instrument, or make rare conditioning inexpensive.**

## Research packet

The packet contains the complete derivations, circuit specification, resource contracts, rejected stronger interfaces, and actual verification results.

The executed checks include 72 arbitrary-channel trials, 24 SWAP specializations, 12 weighted-routing trials, 30 full-purification overlap trials, and 80 exact rational scalar fixtures. The largest direct-routing discrepancy was approximately \(1.14\times10^{-15}\) in trace norm. These are finite diagnostics, not formal verification or independent review; no quantum hardware was used.
