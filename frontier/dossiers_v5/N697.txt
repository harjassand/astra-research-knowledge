UNIFORMIZATION
Let F be an explicitly finite union-closed family, |F|=m≥2, and p a strictly positive law on F satisfying p(A)≤p(B) whenever A⊆B. For k≥1 set b=ceil(log₂m), L_k=2kb+1. The report defines a product-likelihood superlevel family U_k={(A_1,…,A_k)∈F^k: ∏_j p(A_j)≥τ_k}. For a suitable threshold it is closed under coordinatewise union and invariant under block permutations. If Q_k is uniform on U_k, then Q_k(x)≤4L_k p^⊗k(x), and for 1≤ℓ≤k,

D((Q_k)_{1:ℓ}||p^⊗ℓ)≤(ℓ/k)log(4L_k),
TV((Q_k)_{1:ℓ},p^⊗ℓ)≤√(ℓ log(4L_k)/(2k)).

The report's randomized threshold selection and capped rejection replace 4L_k by 8L_k with controlled failure probability. For an explicitly listed F and rational p with B-bit probabilities, it reports polynomial bit work and O(kL_k log(L_k/δ)) draws from p for failure tolerance δ; U_k may be exponentially large and is not enumerated.

CONSEQUENCE AND NON-RESULTS
For every universal constant c, the uniform-family abundance statement is equivalent to the increasing-weighted version over nontrivial union-closed supports. This is a reduction/equivalence, not proof of Frankl's half-frequency conjecture or any improved constant. The exact criterion for when self-union coupling cannot raise entropy is already recorded in N504/LA224; it is not promoted again here.

The report's projected maximum-entropy repair fails submodularity on an explicit five-set family; this refutes that proposed assembly route only. It does not prove a general impossibility. Existing weighted formulations and specialized weighted results are credited in the source; priority for the arbitrary-weight uniformization theorem remains unknown.

RELATIONS
