EXACT CLAIM | On positive weighted forests formed from two hidden stars, possibly joined by one edge, each action selects common real x and observes full Lx with independent Gaussian noise variance sigma^2 under the same pathwise coordinate budget B. Worst-case connectivity error <=delta forces every randomized nonadaptive design to satisfy q[pi^2+8a^2B/sigma^2]>=n(1-2delta)^2. At B=1/2, fixed small sigma/a and fixed delta, two adaptive full-vector actions suffice while nonadaptive action count is Theta(n).
PROOF SPINE | Cold review reconstructed the compact-prior lower bound, Fisher-score projection, full-data Schur complement, hidden-label ridge average and TV path argument. A standard-basis schedule gives the nonadaptive upper order. This is connectivity on the hidden-two-star promise, not support recovery or arbitrary graphs.
LIMITS | Fixed series resistance changes observations to L(I+rL)^-1x; a reviewed full-transcript obstruction needs B=Omega(d) for its noise law. Two routed O(1/n)-resistance channels remain conditional, with noise/switching/current/rail/settling unacquired. Perfectly floating unused terminals admit an O(1)-action ordinary baseline, while leakage and charging grow with terminal count. No hardware advantage.
NEXT OBLIGATION | Acquire and validate the full physical graph/control/noise/time law, then compare total costs against the strongest ordinary baseline.
