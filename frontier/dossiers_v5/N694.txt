N694-codimension-threshold-global-spectral-regularity | A codimension threshold for recovering global spectral regularity
status=source_derived_unreviewed; the pasted report contains detailed proof arguments, preserved unchanged. The universal mathematical claims and cited imported results were not independently audited.

Let C_P(mu)=sup_f Var_mu(f)/int |grad f|^2 dmu. The report claims:

1. If n>=3 and a smooth positive rotationally invariant probability density on R^n has a codimension-two marginal that is kappa-strongly log-concave, then C_P(mu)<=n/((n-1)kappa). The full density need not be log-concave.
2. For every fixed n>=4 there are smooth positive isotropic rotationally invariant measures whose marginals of dimension at most n-3 are 1/500-strongly log-concave while C_P tends to infinity. They also obey E exp(<t,X>)<=exp(|t|^2/2) for all t.
3. For fixed n>=5, 0<eta<1/2 and K>0, there is a smooth positive isotropic rotationally invariant mu with D(mu||gamma_n)<=eta, every projection onto a subspace of dimension 1 through n-4 having curvature at least (1-eta)I, and C_P(mu)>=K. A witnessing bottleneck separates two sets each of probability at least 1/3.

The positive proof reconstructs the radial law from the codimension-two marginal by adding an independent Exp(1) energy: R_*=W^{-1}(W(S)+E). It then combines radial and angular control. The higher-codimension examples show that marginal curvature (even near-Gaussian marginal curvature plus small full KL divergence) need not control the global Poincare constant.

The transport corollary is scoped: separately contractive Gaussian transports of almost-full marginals do not yield a uniformly Lipschitz Gaussian representation of the full law. The source explicitly says this is not failure of distributional identification by all projections and not generic sampling hardness; its displayed construction admits Gaussian rejection sampling. Rotational symmetry is essential to the positive argument, and the non-radial extension remains open. Figalli–Jerison is credited for the simpler non-log-concavity observation; Caffarelli’s contraction theorem is imported. No foundational or historical-priority claim is certified.
