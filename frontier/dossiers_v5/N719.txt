It outputs -infinity for U=0 and ell=0 for T<=0 when a path exists. Missing path means U=0 by convention; for T>0 zero-only paths do not contribute. It computes log U without materializing a potentially enormous probability denominator. This is a proof candidate, not validated software or an established algorithmic advance.

Mechanism: order relevant activities by (rate,index), partition paths by their minimum-rate anchor, and use a topological skip/select scan rather than scanning in rate order.
