CENTRAL THEOREM
For each finite ground set E, let C_E be a closed class of polymatroids, stable under restriction/relabeling and under conditional copying with extensions remaining in the corresponding class. For every h∈C_E and protected family F, the report proves there is g∈C_E preserving every protected marginal, satisfying I_g(A;B|C)=0 for every partition separating F, and with g(E)≥h(E). The proof maximizes total rank on the compact fiber of protected marginals; any positive separating conditional-mutual-information defect can be removed by an internal copy while increasing total rank by exactly that defect.

COPY-HIERARCHY CONSEQUENCE
For K_E=∩_P Q_P(E), where P ranges over finite Shannon/conditional-copy programs, the report argues that K is itself closed and copy-stable. Thus MAXE and GMAXE completions remain inside K, and the closed consequence cones of finite copying and mixed copying/MAXE agree. This is a closure statement: it does not turn every boundary inequality into an exact finite-copy proof or characterize the almost-entropic cone.

FINITE CERTIFICATES AND LIMITS
For rational L valid on K and every rational ε>0, the report derives a finite-copy certificate for L(h)+εu(h)≥0. A one-step MAXE certificate with multipliers totaling Λ yields the explicit depth-T consequence L(f)+(Λ/T)(u(f)−f(E))≥0, using at most m∑_{t=0}^T q^t labels and an exponential-size Shannon rank system in the resulting label count. No polynomial-time entropy algorithm is claimed.

The attempted bound controlling the whole profile by copy defect is refuted by the report's entropy-level example. Lattice-quantized rank classes terminate exactly, but a uniform quantization/finite-termination principle for general real profiles remains open. The target equality K_E=closure(almost-entropic cone) is unresolved. The source's prior-art discussion credits existing MAXE/GMAXE questions and a same-field linear-polymatroid result; exact novelty is not established.
