## Strongest finding: a compressed and optimized Egyptian-fraction construction

An independently reconstructed route now gives, for every C>16/log 2 and all sufficiently large b, every unreduced input `1<=a<b` a distinct positive Egyptian-fraction expansion of length <=C log log b. The proof bypasses the release's difficult dense-family/descent proposition, random residues and divisor moments. Its sole deep imported input is classical quantitative Vinogradov; the primorial growth estimate is elementary.

The source's separate quantitative prescribed-denominator construction contains the structural ingredients. The new work transfers them uniformly to all numerators and optimizes them: use `ell(u)=ceil(5log u/(6log 2))+1` primorial primes, count bad residue images, and represent a good prime by three positive rational divisors using a bilinear Fourier bound. Quantitative Vinogradov gives a nine-term supply for every sufficiently large odd integer. A common K covers all coefficients through `b^(r+1)`. An unreduced greedy prefix supplies divisor density, and largest-odd grouping uses nine terms per group with contraction <2/b^r. For each fixed r the leading coefficient is `(16+15/r)/log 2`; choosing r before the b-limit gives every C>16/log 2.

Complete reconstructed proof and costs: `notes/frontier_number3/cycle19/025_OPTIMIZED_CONSTRUCTION.md`. The preliminary 257/log 2 transfer remains at `025_UNIFORM_TRANSFER.md`. Root-assigned checker frontier_geometry2 independently reconstructed the proof, found no missing interface assumption, sharpened the endpoint to 5/6, and removed the separate PNT input. Its complete independent proof and adversarial exact fixtures are `notes/frontier_geometry2/cycle19/EGYPTIAN_GATE.md`, `work/frontier_geometry2/cycle19/egyptian_gate_checks.py`, and `results/frontier_geometry2/cycle19/EGYPTIAN_GATE_CHECKS.json`. This is an internal independent mathematical check, not external validation, formal certification or priority verification.

The same grouping retains the exact prescribed denominator m in an expansion of one using the source's marked greedy prefix, T=2mK and R<2m. The new marked/counting extension passed a separate complete reconstruction in Section10 of the checker's note. It yields the same length coefficient, and exact marker padding then gives

`liminf log log v(k)/k >= log 2/16`,

`liminf log log F(k)/k >= log 2/16`,

where v(k) is the smallest denominator absent from exact k-term expansions of one and F(k) counts those expansions. These slopes improve the source's displayed prescribed-denominator coefficient log 2/257. See Section 8 of the optimized note.

Costs are part of the result. Before repeat cleanup, maximum denominator is exp(O(log b log log b)) and total bit volume O(log b (log log b)^2). Cleanup preserves length but has no demonstrated bound preserving that sharper denominator cap. A general sorted-denominator inequality gives a distinct certificate with O((log b)^(17+delta)) total bits for each delta>0; marked expansions of one have the sharper O((log m)^(16+delta)) bound. These are polynomial certificate sizes, with large exponents; they are not polynomial acquisition. An integer-bit-length factorial cutoff and exact power comparison make K formation polynomial without a real-logarithm oracle. Greedy preparation and ancestry-based divisor selection are polynomial once supplies are available, but exhaustive supply acquisition is exponential in log b, and cleanup runtime is unbounded here. No useful numerical onset is given.
