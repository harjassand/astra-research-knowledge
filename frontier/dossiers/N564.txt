Claim: For compactly supported probability measures eta<=cx mu on R^d, let gamma_r=N(0,r^2 I), f_mu=mu*gamma_r and E_r(mu)=D(f_mu||gamma_r), using the same Gaussian for smoothing and reference. The source proves E_r(mu)>=E_r(eta)+D(f_mu||f_eta). The matching-reference condition is essential; this is not monotonicity of arbitrary relative entropy or of mutual information alone.
Interface: Convex order uses all finite convex test functions; measures are compactly supported and r>0. Gaussian reference covariance matches the smoothing covariance exactly.
Proof spine: Write log(f_eta/gamma_r) as a log-Laplace transform, hence a convex function. Gaussian addition preserves convex order, and subtraction of the two relative entropies gives the claimed nonnegative integral plus D(f_mu||f_eta).
Costs: This is an analytic lemma, not a low-cost convex-order decision procedure. The source identifies convex order and reverse KL triangle inequalities as established neighboring ideas; originality of this exact formulation remains uncertain.
Failure: Changing the reference Gaussian breaks the claim; mutual information alone can move in the wrong direction under mean-preserving compression. The source includes explicit counterexamples. This intake did not reconstruct the proof.
Closest comparator: Convex order and reverse KL triangle inequalities have prior literature cited in the packet. The source does not claim those ingredients as new primitives.
Open verification: Independently check integrability and convex-order steps, then compare the exact matched-Gaussian inequality against prior formulations and identify a theorem-level consequence beyond this lemma.
