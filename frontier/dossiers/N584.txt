Interface: The channel is the memoryless thermal attenuator with bath mean photon number nu=1. The result applies to arbitrary deterministic qubit codes over m channel uses, with total average input energy E and entanglement fidelity F_e; it does not assume a maximum photon cutoff or a specific code family. The 1392 example additionally assumes average energy at most 10 photons per use and target fidelity 0.99.
