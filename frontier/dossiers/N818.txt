status=source_derived_unreviewed; separate FP worker/root sources and same-team review; frontier capability NOT_ACHIEVED.

Classical rare-event gateway: in a stated local-row oracle with N hidden terminal channels, H0 has p0=2^-L/(N+1) and HJ has pJ=2p0. Adaptive random-stopping algorithms giving 1/4 relative error with 2/3 success on every promised instance need at least N/3 expected candidate-terminal queries under H0, or a hard per-run cap of at least N/2. A full N-row scan matches the order. For N=1024,L=8, 288276 iid paths mean about 863702 simulated transitions but about 1125 informative terminal queries; these are different cost units. No atomistic rate or new algorithm is acquired.

Remote response: a local positive frustration-free spin chain with exact product ground has chi_m(e)=(m-1)/m+1/(m^2 e). The e and 2e models have equal first 2m-2 source moments and first differ at moment 2m-1. Unknown-generator evolution-only discrimination costs at least 1/(3e) summed interrogation time under the stated fixed-control model; exact supplied symbolic rows admit an O(m) tridiagonal solve. The worker's mention of N816 is an external, unmerged local lineage at this intake base and is preserved as source wording only. No N816 dependency or canonical status is asserted here.

Routing repair: a proposed DNA frame retains covalent routing junctions and uses reciprocal sacrificial templates to fill eligible gaps. If surviving material admits two different target routings, no repair process seeing only that survivor can infer the lost bit with success above 1/2 on an equal two-world prior. A sufficient abstract restriction is a unique orientation/register-compatible continuation matching with retained, addressable covalent anchors, eligible 3-prime primer and intact template. The all-eligible two-copy patch CTMC has mean loss time (mu+3lambda)/(2lambda^2). If a new lesion is repair-eligible with probability p and ineligible damage remains functional only until the second copy is hit, the exact mean becomes [(3-2p)mu+3lambda]/[2lambda((1-p)mu+lambda)]. At mu/lambda=1000 it falls from 501.5/lambda at p=1 to 46.5/lambda at p=.99. Under independent routing-anchor loss hazard rho, S_frame(T)=exp(-K rho T) S_patch(T)^N; 90% survival with K=100 and lambda T=1 requires rho/lambda<=0.001053605 even before patch failures. These are hypothetical model gates, not measured damage or enzyme rates. Existing matching-staple repair and designed origami gap fill are incumbents. The proposed topology-preserving, no-per-site-staple operation has no physical demonstration or cost advantage.

These are three separate contract-specific results, not a common impossibility theorem. Their executable checks are finite fixtures; correctness, priority and physical transfer remain unreviewed. The decisive tests are (i) acquire hidden reactive-channel information and calibrated entry flux; (ii) pay terminal-coupling precision on an identified sample against same-input solvers; (iii) demonstrate sequence-and-routing restoration in a 2D anchored corridor with anchor-erased controls, chemistry/access checks and a matched full-cost staple-replacement baseline.
