N544-sharp-spherical-retention-and-state-complexity | Spherical action retention is exactly sharp and yields a T power law for positive Markov states

Claim: For the standard SO(d) action, eta_(m,d)=sup_(v_1,...,v_m in S^(d-1)) integral max_j <u,v_j> d sigma(u) is the constrained spherical quantization coefficient. The source proves R_T<=eta_(m,d)^T with equality attainable for the retention problem; eta_(m,2)=m sin(pi/m)/pi; eta_(m,3)<=1-1/m (no equality claim); and 1-eta_(m,d)=Theta_d(m^(-2/(d-1))) for fixed d. For p_*(+|x,g_1,...,g_t,u)=(1+delta<u,g_t...g_1 x>)/2, 0<delta<=1/d and 0<epsilon<delta/(2d), the minimum number of positive Markov states uniformly simulating all preparations, readouts and gate words through horizon T is Theta_(d,delta,epsilon)(T^((d-1)/2)). The static positive factorization size is exactly d+1.
Interface: Fixed dimension d>=2; x,u range over the Euclidean unit ball; each supplied gate is in SO(d); controls in the retention experiment are independent Haar draws. The simulator has an m-state Markov memory, arbitrary time/current-gate measurable stochastic updates and uniform additive probability error epsilon. The exponent is fixed-d and the constants depend on d, delta and epsilon.
Proof spine: The spherical coefficient equals one minus half the optimal mean squared nearest-codeword distance. A conditional-mean argument gives the exact retention contraction and a nearest-codeword construction attains it. Cap covering and packing establish the fixed-d quantization rate. A bounded terminal score converts retention into the state lower bound; a circumscribed polytope and barycentric stochastic updates give the matching upper bound. A regular simplex gives the exact static d+1 factorization and rank lower bound.
Costs: The theorem counts states, not bits or runtime. Gate acquisition, net generation, triangulation, barycentric map size, probability precision, transition-table description and sampling are not bounded. For d=3, T-state order means Theta(log T) classical bits under ideal state encoding, not Theta(T) bits.
Failure: Continuous Haar controls are essential to the displayed coefficient. The result does not imply a lower bound for signed coordinates or an ordinary finite-precision simulator without charging its full persistent memory and numerical resources. Finite groups can admit bounded-state exact constructions.
Closest comparator: Spherical quantization and manifold approximation have established literature and are not claimed as inventions. The candidate contribution is the operational positive-composition retention law and its matching state-count construction; priority remains UNKNOWN.
