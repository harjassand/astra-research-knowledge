N114 | Stable amplitudes do not give uniformly log-concave Born laws
status=source_derived_unreviewed;COMPLETE supplied Z evidence package;branch-specific internal proof/audit status below,external correctness/priority unresolved;checks SOURCE-REPORTED unless marked otherwise.
Notation local.

Selected positive ground amplitudes are real stable in the admitted model. For fixed-charge disjoint k-configuration coherences, normalized 2v_Av_B/||v||^2<=2/(k+1), sharp; vacuum/full GHZ fidelity in this basis <=1/2 for n>=2. Squaring stable amplitudes can destroy dimension-independent Born-law curvature: the explicit unique-ground K_kk family has gap 2 yet forces fractional-log-concavity alpha<=3/(k+2). This rules out that proposed transfer only; it proves neither slow mixing nor hardness. Unbalanced cat strengthening was rejected; maximum is 1 (k=1),2/3 (k=2),1/2 (k>=3). Prior work on exponentiated strongly-Rayleigh laws is credited.
