Under the source's ideal-gas, cylindrical continuum no-slip, quasi-steady common-temperature assumptions, P is external and p internal absolute pressure, V is cavity volume, a and L are throat radius and length, T temperature, and mu viscosity. With n=pV/(RT) and C=pi*a^4/(16*mu*L*V), the source derives n'=pi*a^4*(P^2-p^2)/(16*mu*L*R*T), p'=C*(P^2-p^2)+p*(T'/T-V'/V), and, for P>0, q'=C*P*(1-q^2)+(c+theta-r)*q for q=p/P, c=-V'/V, theta=T'/T, r=P'/P.
