N681 | Replica-programmed quantum operations admit an exact one-query contraction with a completely positive error term

INTERFACE
Let sigma be an unknown finite-dimensional state on A, available through independent copies. A channel C:L(A tensor B) -> L(A tensor C) is physically available once through identified input/output A ports; B may be entangled with an arbitrary untouched reference. Source copies are independent of B and the device environment. Clean ancillas and controlled swaps are available. This is sample-plus-routed-device access, not observation of a probability table alone.

For any Kraus representation {K_alpha} of C, define

    M_alpha = Tr_A[(sigma tensor I) K_alpha],
    F_sigma(rho) = sum_alpha M_alpha rho M_alpha^dagger,
    G_sigma(rho) = Tr_A C(sigma tensor rho).

The source report states that F_sigma is the purification-return success operation and R_sigma := G_sigma - F_sigma is completely positive. The construction is independent of the Kraus representation and purification choice.

PROVISIONAL ONE-QUERY IDENTITY
For every integer n>=1, a routing superposition over n independent source copies, controlled swaps around one ordinary invocation of C, and a |+_n> return test realizes the successful operation

    F_(sigma,n) = (1 - 1/n) F_sigma + (1/n) G_sigma
                = F_sigma + (1/n) R_sigma.

The channel itself is not controlled. The report's proof attributes the exact 1/n term to the n diagonal replica terms and the F_sigma contribution to the n(n-1) off-diagonal terms.

FLAGGED ERROR AND COST
With a distinguished failure flag completing each trace-nonincreasing operation to a channel, the report states

    (1/2) || Fhat_(sigma,n) - Fhat_sigma ||_diamond
       = (1/n) ||R_sigma^dagger(I)||_infinity <= 1/n.

For L adaptive uses with fresh replicas, its telescoping bound is sum_j 1/n_j. A reported universal lower bound is epsilon >= 1/(n+2), already for a known two-qubit SWAP channel and pure qubit program states. Thus the worst-case sample order is reported as Theta(1/epsilon), independent of dimension. The L-use allocation O(L^2/epsilon) is an upper bound; replica reuse and better multi-use costs are unresolved.

SCOPE AND LIMITS
This does not reconstruct sigma, its purifier, Kraus matrices or hidden effects from observed probabilities. It requires independent quantum samples and physical access to the specified channel ports. It does not implement the earlier trace-preserving square-root isometry, provide unrestricted purification access, enable retry on an unknown destroyed input, or make rare conditioning cheap: the report gives conditional-error scale 1/(n p) at success probability p. Its canonical-purification lower bound is specific to that representation, and the live-purification latency obstruction is only for the stated no-prefetch contract. The source itself identifies prior quantum-filter, programmable-measurement and random-purification machinery; exact novelty of the combined formula and a consequential end-to-end application remain open.

SOURCE-REPORTED CHECKS
The report states 72 arbitrary-channel trials, 24 SWAP specializations, 12 weighted-routing trials, 30 full-purification overlap trials, and 80 exact rational scalar fixtures; maximum direct-routing discrepancy is reported near 1.14e-15. No code or receipts accompanied the supplied report, so these checks were not independently reproduced. No quantum hardware or formal certification is reported.

Context only: that work concerns source-local replacement from its stated source-isolation information; N681 instead assumes independent quantum samples and a routable channel. Neither result supplies the other's access model, and N681 does not claim reconstruction from outcome probabilities. No implication or proof transfer is asserted.
