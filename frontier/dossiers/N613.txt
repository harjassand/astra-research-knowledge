Claim: For the report's exact midpoint-geometry and fixed-adopted-distance observation model, r^2=R^2+z^2 and v(z)=w+zb. Minimizing eta(z)=sqrt(R^2+z^2)|w+zb|^2/(2 G_N M) over admissible depth reduces interior extrema to 3Bz^3+4dz^2+(V+2BR^2)z+2dR^2=0, where B=|b|^2, d=w dot b, V=|w|^2; evaluating real roots and endpoints gives the minimum. At the source's adopted central inputs, four pairs in its harmonized 33-pair cohort have eta_min>1 (IDs 3,33,42,59; range 1.1468–1.3994), so no choice of line-of-sight depth alone makes those fixed inputs a bound isolated Newtonian two-body pair.
Interface: The inequality is conditional on the adopted central astrometry, radial velocities, component masses, exact perspective reconstruction and isolated two-body Newtonian model. It is a feasibility envelope over unknown depth, not a probabilistic orbit fit or a gravity test.
Proof spine: Differentiate eta(z), factor the positive denominator, and reduce its interior critical points to the displayed cubic. Compare all real critical points in the physically admissible domain with its limiting endpoints.
