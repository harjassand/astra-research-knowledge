## Arbitrary-mask progress and obstruction

For a fixed mask W, a value-independent circuit for (Z_W,Y)↦ZYᵀ can be contracted with X and reverse differentiated to recover every (XY)_ij in W. Total arithmetic charge is O(T+ND+|W|), plus acquisition P and stored tape. Value-dependent zero-pruned traces do not automatically supply that circuit.

Graia's optimal deterministic FSMM theorem does not prove this source product fast: at the dense N×D output endpoint its two sketches may both be identities, so the oracle multiplication is the original sparse ZYᵀ. Ambient n cannot be freely replaced by √nnz(input). The tempting rescaling shortcut was retracted.

There exist legal masks W with |W|=Θ(N^(15/8)) for which every independently charged complete-block cover costs Ω(N²), even allowing arbitrary inner-coordinate subsets and ideal full-block matrix multiplication. The proof uses one Bernoulli mask with a simultaneous rectangle-density bound. It does not restrict cross-block algebraic sharing or general circuits.

## Newly verified primary prior

Alman–Vassilevska Williams, arXiv:2610.06783v1, submitted 5 October 2026, claims a deterministic requested-output algorithm using O(N²/D^0.063) operations on O(log N)-bit integers for D≤N^(1/18), |W|≤N²/√D, with general regime D≤N^ε for every ε<0.1204. It derives explicit truly subquadratic 3SUM and truly subcubic APSP algorithms. This updates the imported literature state; its reductions are not independently certified here.

Full technical reading of its identity, error cancellation, tiling, pruned leaf counts, box data structure, and bit costs shows that the threshold ε*=log4/(5log10) comes from its ten-term output-local packing. The minimal tile side is D^(8.3048...+o(1)). The new α value cannot be substituted to shrink this exponent below4. A different output-local identity/packing is required for D=N^(1/4).

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06783v1. Saved technical HTML and parsed text are under sources/frontier_tensor_bridge/cycle19/prior/.
