Claim: For a finite orthogonal gate alphabet in a finite-dimensional real space, with all gate weights positive and the identity included, the source proves rho_m=1 if and only if the generated group has a nonzero unit-vector orbit of size at most m. A dense SO(3) action therefore has rho_m<1 for each fixed m, even without a quantitative spectral gap; this argument gives no rate as m grows.
Interface: rho_m is the asymptotic growth rate of the stated positive-lift operator family. The identity gate and strictly positive weight for every alphabet member are part of the theorem. It does not cover arbitrary action sets or signed implementations.
Proof spine: A finite orbit gives a permutation lift and nonzero fixed dual vector. Conversely, compactness attains a finite-horizon norm-one maximizer; equality forces each active conditional direction to be deterministic. Identity and positive gate weights give nested active direction sets, and a repeated finite set yields a gate-invariant orbit.
Costs: Qualitative fixed-m contraction does not quantify 1-rho_m. The source’s separate spectral-gap theorem is needed for the quantitative Theta(1/m) result.
Failure: The assumptions on identity and positive weights cannot be silently omitted. This intake has not independently checked the compactness/equality argument.
