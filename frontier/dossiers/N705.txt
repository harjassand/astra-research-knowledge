For finite H_S tensor H_B, d=dim(H_S), H=I_S tensor K+V with Hermitian K,V and ||V||op<=v, all subsystem-only terms and subsystem-bath coupling belong in V. At beta>0 let rho=exp(-beta H)/Z and X=x tensor I_B for any possibly non-Hermitian x. No bath dimension, bath energy scale, geometry, gap, commutation or mixing restriction is assumed. The claimed local KMS bound is K_rho(X)=Tr(sqrt(rho)X^dagger sqrt(rho)X)>=4 exp(-4 beta v)||x||op^2/(9d)>=4 exp(-4 beta v)Tr_S(x^dagger x)/(9d^2). This is a quadratic form, not ordinary uncentered variance or a transport coefficient. When v=0 it is exactly Tr_S(x^dagger x)/d.

More generally, lambda>sqrt(3) gives (1-3/lambda^2)^2 exp[-(lambda+1)beta v]||x||op^2/d. The singular-profile extension for lambda>0 replaces the numerator by sum_a(s_a(x)-3||x||op/lambda^2)_+^2. For Hermitian A,B with ||A-B||<=v, low/high spectral projections separated by g obey ||QP0||<=v/g, using a Sylvester integral. If X commutes with P0, compression error is <=3||QP0||^2||X||. Applied to I tensor K versus H, each low bath level supplies a copy of x; retained singular values after widening by lambda v are thermally integrated and divided by the scalar min-max bound Z<=d exp(beta v)Z_B. No false operator-order monotonicity of exp or efficient spectral-projector access is assumed.

The finite-band form K_{rho,Omega}(X)=sum_{|E_i-E_j|<=Omega}sqrt(p_i p_j)|X_ij|^2 obeys K_{rho,7v}(X)>=exp(-5 beta v)||x||op^2/(72d). Width-v bath windows enlarged by 3v on each side and a bounded overlap count supply this absolute floor. It is not a constant fraction of all KMS weight, excludes no zero-frequency contribution, and gives no nonzero-frequency absorption, conductivity, rapid mixing or finite-resolution measurement algorithm.

For Hermitian A centered at its thermal mean, BKM covariance dominates KMS. Static susceptibility is at least (4beta/(9d))exp(-4beta v) Var_tauS(A).
