Claim: For weak mediator interactions additive over neutrons and with isotope-independent electronic response, first-order shifts of transition j satisfy δν_j^new(A,40)=X_j(A−40). A sum or integrable continuum of mediators changes X_j but does not create another isotope direction under these assumptions. In the source’s four-isotope calcium fit, it reports Q_required=+1.979934960 GHz·u² versus Q_recoil=−1.012869689 GHz·u² using published central recoil coefficients.
Interface: First-order perturbative shifts, neutron-additive coupling, isotope-independent electronic response, and the source’s 729 nm/570 nm calcium transitions and four-isotope data. Nuclear polarization is omitted from the comparison.
Proof spine: Linearity makes every mediator contribution proportional to the same isotope vector A−40 for each transition; integrating/summing mediators only changes its scalar coefficient. Eliminating the leading radius term yields the reported opposite-sign required/recoil coefficient comparison.
Costs: The calculation is a finite isotope-shift algebra/fit; source data, covariance, and code are not present in the attached record, and no acquisition/runtime claim is made.
Failure: It does not exclude nonfactorized or nonlinear new physics and is not evidence for a new force. The missing nuclear-polarization contribution is identified by the source as a potentially adequate Standard Model explanation; the comparison is incomplete.
Closest comparator: The source cites the calcium King-plot experiment; the primary PRL reports nuclear polarization as a potentially large remaining Standard Model contribution: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.134.233002. This intake’s calculation remains source-reported and does not supersede that analysis.
