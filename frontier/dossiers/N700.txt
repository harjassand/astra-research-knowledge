Let H=(V,E) be a finite simple graph with n vertices and m>=1 edges, p>1 and B>1. Kernels W:[0,1]^2->[0,infinity) are nonnegative, symmetric, have row integral exactly one almost everywhere and ||W||_p<=B. The observable is t(H,W)=integral product_{uv in E} W(x_u,x_v); discrepancy is the usual cut norm, not merely pairwise pointwise or operator approximation.

Define gamma_H(p) by minimizing d(V) over nonnegative edge scales k_e with sum k_e=1 and increasing supermodular set functions d with d(empty)=0, d(uv)>=p k_uv, d(uv)-d(u)>=k_uv and d(uv)-d(v)>=k_uv. The proposed sharp criterion is gamma_H(p)>1: all counts are finite and |t(H,U)-t(H,W)|<=C ||U-W||_square^((gamma-1)/(m-1+gamma-1)) for cut-norm differences <=1. If gamma<=1, the response constructs bounded finite-step normalized symmetric kernels W_L with ||W_L-1||_1->0 but liminf t(H,W_L)>1 for each B>1. If gamma<1, it claims strong-Lp and operator-norm convergence to one while counts diverge. B=1 forces W=1; strong-Lp continuity exactly at gamma=1 is a separate unresolved endpoint.

Proof mechanism: dyadic edge slices produce increasing supermodular relative-entropy deficits and a tail exponent gamma-1. Telescope one untruncated distinguished edge at a time to use cut norm; do not assume cut norm makes pointwise truncation Lipschitz. For the converse, convert deficits to singleton-conditioned entropy bounds, import normal-polymatroid optimality, realize shared-coordinate finite relations, and add the positive filler r(x)r(y)/bar r to repair every marginal exactly. This imported realization theorem is a substantive premise, not a general assertion that Shannon relaxations are tight.
