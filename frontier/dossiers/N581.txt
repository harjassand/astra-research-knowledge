N581-affine-mixture-envelope-obstruction | Affine-mixture rejection envelopes can incur exponential overhead even for an easy product target

Claim: For π(λ)=2^(−wt(λ))/(3/2)^m on F_2^m, equivalently independent Bernoulli(1/3) bits, let Q be any mixture of at most K uniform laws on affine subspaces and assume π(λ)≤ρQ(λ) pointwise. For integers 0≤D<m and t with positive numerator, the source derives ρ≥[Pr(Bin(m,1/3)≤t)−K(2/3)^(m−D)]/Pr(Bin(D+1,1/2)≤t). For m divisible by 8 it gives the weakened bound ρ≥[1−exp(−m/288)−K(2/3)^(m/8)]exp(m/112) for a stated choice of D,t, when the bracket is positive; hence polynomial K entails exponential overhead in that regime. Separately, the simpler rank-bound affine-cover construction obeys C/Z≥(4/3)^m/K. The target itself is exactly sampled by independent bits, so this is an affine-mixture representation barrier, not intrinsic sampling hardness.
Interface: Target distribution π on F_2^m; proposal Q is a mixture of at most K uniform distributions on affine subspaces. The lower bound concerns pointwise domination π≤ρQ, the condition used for rejection sampling. The affine-cover inequality applies to the separately stated uniform-rank-cover construction.
Proof spine: For an affine subspace of dimension at most D, its target mass is bounded by (2/3)^(m−D). For higher-dimensional pieces, information-set coordinates are unbiased under their uniform law, which bounds their mass on the low-Hamming-weight tail by a Bin(D+1,1/2) probability. Remove the union of low-dimensional components from {wt≤t}, compare remaining target mass to proposal mass, and derive the displayed ratio.
Costs: No sampler cost lower bound follows: π is sampled exactly with m independent Bernoulli(1/3) bits. The source supplies an exact finite formula and reports enumeration of every affine subspace through m=6 plus exact rational examples at m=100,250,500; intake did not rerun that checker.
Failure: Applies only to mixtures of uniform affine subspace laws under pointwise domination and to the specified envelope construction. It does not rule out multiplicative factorizations, product sampling, dual-kernel sampling, or other compact representations; the example is deliberately easy to sample.
Closest comparator: The source distinguishes product sampling, affine stratification and weighted/code-like models. It makes no claim that these models or exact sampling are novel; the result is a scoped obstruction to this proposal representation.
Open verification: Reconstruct the low/high dimensional mass bounds and the finite domination formula, verify constants and rounding in the m divisible by eight corollary, and preserve the product-sampler escape when interpreting the asymptotic rejection lower bound.
