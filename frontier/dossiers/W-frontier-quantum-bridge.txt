## 4. Accuracy bits and the area-law boundary

**Precision ingredient available.** The source recursion has fewer than J=32768(n+1)^3 levels, each adding at most 18(3/4)^L clean-workspace operator error. For epsilon=2^-B, set

    L=4(B+ceil log2(36J)),
    eta<=2^-B/[2(S+1)].

The skeleton contributes at most epsilon/2; slot errors contribute at most epsilon/2. Its width Q=O((n+1)^4L^2) and slot count S=O((n+1)^4L^3). Retaining the manuscript's actual Solovay--Kitaev recurrence epsilon_next<=C epsilon^(3/2), length_next<=5length gives word length O(log^4(2/xi)), since log_(3/2)5<4. The complete oracle circuit, addresses and structure generation are polynomial in n,B.
