Claim: For the single-mode thermal attenuator Phi_{eta,nu}, 0<eta<1 and nu>0, set G=1+(1-eta)nu and assume eta>G-1. Let R=(eta-(G-1))/(G-1)>0. For every fixed distinct normalized pure finite-energy competitor phi, define q_phi=||T_phi g_*||^2/||g_*||^2>0, with T_phi the coefficient map for its complementary output. Then rho_p=(1-p)|psi><psi|+p|phi><phi| obeys limsup_{p down to 0} I_c(rho_p,Phi_{eta,nu})/[p log(1/p)] <= -q_phi < 0 (natural logarithms), hence has negative coherent information for all sufficiently small p. The source reports this sign without a finite receiver-relative-entropy assumption.
Interface: One use of a single-mode thermal attenuator with the full complementary output, including the thermal-environment purifier. The seed is pure, finite-energy and obeys the stated exponential-moment condition; the competitor is a fixed, distinct pure finite-energy state. The mixture weight tends to zero while both states remain fixed. This does not cover competitors depending on p or general entangled block-code inputs.

Costs: No algorithmic or physical acquisition cost is claimed. The restriction is a weighted exponential photon-number moment depending on eta and nu; the sign conclusion is for each fixed seed/competitor pair and all sufficiently small mixture weights, with no uniform threshold in p supplied.
