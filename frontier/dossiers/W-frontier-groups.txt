## Source claims and unresolved premises

Family250's global Boone–Higman claim takes a word-problem decider as supplied input; it does not acquire one from a presentation. The action-envelope route is valuable because a known twisted Brin–Thompson criterion converts faithful two-transitivity and finitely generated point stabilizers into a finitely presented simple overgroup. The hardest unreviewed premise is the bounded compiler: Theorem6.2 through Lemmas7.1–7.2 and AppendixA.1/A.5 must cover malformed sectors, equation use in both directions and arbitrary contexts. Our local kernel and affine proofs do not discharge this premise. The universal-F∞ companion also relies on uniformly controlled fillings and products of regular modules; reading its complete argument is not an independent adoption of that theorem.

Family251's strong-Ulam claim uses countable discrete G, all Hilbert dimensions, suprema over all group pairs and all group elements, full operator norm, and correction on the same Hilbert space. Its local metric transfer, exact-fixing and circuit comparison were reconstructed. An internal peer gate of Proposition5.1's support/commutation and full-norm scope passed conditionally on sparse/frame inputs; see [PEER_GATES.md](</Users/harjas/Documents/ChatGPT/Literature Digestion/notes/frontier_functional/cycle19/PEER_GATES.md>).
