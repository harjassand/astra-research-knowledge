Claim: Let {D_n}_{n>=1} be finite-valued nonnegative functionals on strictly positive finite probability simplices, normalized by D_n(q,q)=0. Assume data processing under rectangular stochastic maps whose input and output distributions have full support, and for each fixed q assume continuity in p on the open simplex and C^2 regularity away from p=q. The source states that universal convexity of t↦D_n(p exp(tL),q) for every finite alphabet, full-support p,q and irreducible q-reversible continuous-time Markov generator L is equivalent to one alphabet-independent Ψ in C([0,infinity))∩C^2((0,infinity)) with D_n(p,q)=Ψ(χ²(p||q)), Ψ(0)=0, Ψ'(s)>=0 and Ψ'(s)+sΨ''(s)>=0. No tensor-additivity or trace-form premise is needed. As separate corollaries, tensor additivity forces D_n=c log(1+χ²), c>=0 (the order-two Rényi divergence); within that additive class, requiring D'''(t)<=0 along every finite reversible relaxation forces D=0. Under the same data-processing and regularity assumptions, convexity along every irreducible finite stationary generator, including nonreversible ones, forces D=0 without tensor additivity.
Interface: Finite alphabets, full-support row distributions p,q and rectangular stochastic maps with full-support outputs. A generator has nonnegative off-diagonal entries, zero row sums and acts by p_t=p exp(tL); q is stationary, and q_i L_ij=q_j L_ji in the reversible theorem. For each fixed q, regularity is in p only: continuity on the open simplex and C^2 away from p=q. The source does not assume differentiability at equality or regularity in q. Here n indexes alphabet size; the additive conclusion is named “order-two Rényi divergence” and is not denoted D_2.
Proof spine: Sections 2–3 derive exact refinement invariance, transverse Hessian scaling and a rare-clone curvature identity; taking the clone mass to zero forces gradient chord slopes to agree. Section 5 identifies exactly the scalar convexity conditions. Section 6 solves the tensor-additive functional equation and the third-derivative obstruction. Section 7 uses nonreversible cycle generators to force the zero functional.
