Claim: For a probability law nu on SO(d), define eta_m(nu)=sup_(||v||=1,n_1,...,n_m in S^(d-1)) integral max_j <n_j,g v> dnu(g). The source derives R_T<=inf_(1<=k<=T) eta_m(nu^(*k))^floor(T/k). If nu has support size at most m, eta_m(nu)=1, so a one-step coefficient gives no contraction in that regime. A finite group of rotations nevertheless admits a uniform-in-horizon exact weak-readout realization using a finite invariant polytope. The sharp law m_*(T)=Theta(T) for A={I,R_x,R_x^(-1),R_z,R_z^(-1)}, with the two named-axis rotations having cosine 3/5 and sine 4/5, delta=1/3 and epsilon=1/72, is explicitly unresolved in this investigation.
Interface: Independent controls with law nu, finite-state Markov updates, and all persistent classical memory charged. The block bound is an interface for products of k controls. The five-gate question uses the same d=3 state-count contract as the sphere theorem. The stated unresolved status is local to this investigation and does not assert the question is globally open.
Proof spine: Apply the one-step conditional-mean contraction to k independent controls jointly, whose product has law nu^(*k), for floor(T/k) blocks; leftover steps have coefficient at most one. If support size is <=m, choose codewords containing the images of each state under every supported control, giving eta_m(nu)=1. The finite-group exact realization uses the group translates of a circumscribed simplex. These facts limit direct use of the continuous-Haar exponent.
Costs: The block profile is a state-retention bound. The finite-alphabet five-gate asymptotic state count is unknown here. Compilation, gate acquisition, precision, and runtime are not established.
Failure: The Haar one-step coefficient cannot be substituted for the fixed alphabet. The density of the specified gate group establishes density only, not a quantitative lower bound. Solving this question would still require separate significance and priority review.
Closest comparator: Finite-control block products, finite cyclic code formulas and invariant polytopes are the packet comparators. No global open-problem or novelty claim is made.
Open verification: Prove or refute m_*(T)=Theta(T) for the exact five-gate qubit alphabet at delta=1/3 and epsilon=1/72 under the charged Markov-memory interface; perform a dedicated prior-art review before calling it open.
