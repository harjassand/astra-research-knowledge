INTERFACE
Finite-dimensional channels P,Q share an input. Complete comparison quantifies every finite untouched quantum reference and every faithful input-state pair; reference marginals need not agree. D_f is the standard modular Petz divergence, not the sandwiched Rényi divergence. Claims below are campaign-reported proofs; this intake did not independently reconstruct them.

FIXED-GENERATOR CLASSIFICATION
For each single fixed continuous operator-convex generator f with finite f(0+) and nonzero interior resolvent measure, the report proves the equivalence

    P >=_complete,D_f Q  iff  P >=_complete,SLD Q.

This is a one-generator-at-a-time statement, not a requirement to compare all f simultaneously. The proposed reverse mechanism appends a coherent finite reference and uses rational resolvent filters concentrating at a support point of the representing measure; the argument is reported to include atomic, diffuse, and singular measures. Affine terms do not affect state-pair order. Zero/affine and pure quadratic generators are separate cases.

Consequences reported for every fixed standard Petz Rényi parameter 0<alpha<2, Umegaki relative entropy, and state-pair quantum Chernoff divergence: the complete channel preorders coincide with complete SLD order. This concerns qualitative order only; numerical divergence values and contraction coefficients are not thereby equal. It does not cover sandwiched Rényi divergences.

QUADRATIC ENDPOINT
For standard Petz D_2(A||B)=log Tr(A^2 B^{-1}), if P does not dominate Q in complete D_2 order, the report gives a faithful witness with untouched-reference dimension at most 1+b^2, where b is Q's effective output dimension. If Q is a POVM with v nonzero outcomes, the bound is 1+v. The report derives an exact finite real-quantifier-elimination decision procedure for rational strictly positive POVM pairs; it makes no efficiency claim. It notes that generic endpoint decidability already follows from Volcic's rational Positivstellensatz, so the specific candidate advance is the small target-only cutoff and its elementary proof, not decidability in isolation.

An explicit rational qubit measurement instrument is reported to preserve complete D_2 relative to the identity channel while reducing SLD information from 1 to 16/25 on the stated tangent. This unconditionally separates the orders for general channels. Separation when both compared channels are restricted to POVMs is only conditional on the Dots reduction in N675.

CONDITIONAL CLASSIFICATION AND BOUNDARIES
If the Dots complete-SLD hardness reduction recorded in N675 is valid, the fixed-order problem is coRE-complete for each 0<alpha<2 on rational strictly positive POVM pairs, while alpha=2 is decidable independently of that reduction. The main analytic equivalence and finite cutoff do not depend on Dots. This consequence is not an unconditional undecidability result, an efficient algorithm, or an ordinary capacity theorem.

A separate complete proof in the same packet reports, for a finite-dimensional channel N with its full Stinespring complement N^c and fixed qubit half-erasure channel S_2,

    N^c >=_complete,RE N  iff  P(N tensor S_2)=0  iff  Q(N tensor S_2)=0.

Arbitrarily long finite codes are allowed. Transferring the Dots reduction to ordinary capacities still requires a complement-preserving construction; that gate remains open. This operational result is retained in this family card, not split into another card.
