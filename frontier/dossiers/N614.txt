Claim: For a closed glutamate pool with symmetric isotope-independent racemization, each isotope-class handedness imbalance m_j contracts by the same factor e^(-2K), and positivity implies 2K <= -ln(max(|e_14|,|e_15|)). The report converts the three published central values to e_14=0.0527903 and e_15=0.0175575, giving at most 4.24 handedness-halving times under this model. For a racemic background plus one L-biased source followed by isotope-independent racemization, the source's heavy-nitrogen fraction is q_excess=(q_L-d q_D)/(1-d), with d=D/L; the central measurements imply delta15N_excess about -590.5 per mil. A fit with a typical-range source remains compatible with individual quoted spreads after modest shifts, so the central-value inference is not a calibrated exclusion.
Interface: Mix isotope delta values only after conversion to heavy-atom fractions q=R/(1+R), R=R_AIR(1+delta/1000). The racemization inequality assumes a closed pool without later formation, exchange, selective destruction or protected compartments. The one-source invariant assumes the stated background/source mixture and isotope-independent racemization. The printed measurement uncertainties use different definitions and raw replicate covariance was unavailable.
Proof spine: Write n_j=L_j+D_j and m_j=L_j-D_j. The closed symmetric racemization equations conserve n_j and solve m_j(t)=m_j(0)e^(-2K); requiring |m_j(0)|<=n_j(0) yields the positivity bound. In the source mixture, isotope-heavy fractions mix linearly in q, and solving the L/D-weighted mass balance for the excess source gives q_excess.
Costs: The source reports a typical-source compatibility fit with D/L=0.8124, D delta15N=275.82 per mil, L delta15N=220.29 per mil and source delta15N=-20 per mil, each within one quoted spread under its separate definition. It also describes an illustrative D/L standard-error interpretation. No joint likelihood was reconstructed from raw scans.
Failure: The -590.5 per mil source value is model-dependent and unstable as D/L approaches one; the uncertainty fit prevents treating it as a required exotic reservoir. The closed-pool racemization bound is not an age estimate and does not apply to open histories. This does not establish contamination, overturn the source paper, or identify a chemical history.
