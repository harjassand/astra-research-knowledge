A separate finite-support distribution approximates the full CDF uniformly in absolute Kolmogorov distance, with support size and bit cost polynomial in 1/epsilon; it can be sampled with expected polynomial cost. At fixed rational t>0, a relative approximation for lower-tail CDF values is also claimed. These guarantees do not imply relative accuracy for arbitrarily small upper survival tails, total variation for continuous hit times or exact continuous-time sampling.

The marked-event construction additionally approximates hit-label sub-CDFs and distributions over explicitly supplied time bins. For K-event feedback observing only past labels and those bins, it reports a transcript total-variation guarantee with charged approximation error. It excludes finer continuous-time observations, hidden trajectories and arbitrary nonproduct posteriors.

The packet reports a positive-quadrature boundary-resolvent implementation and one 32-component, two-target Laplace-transform enclosure with width 2^-48; this is not a full exact continuous-time sampler or a full CDF implementation.
