Claim: Let C be a binary linear code of length L, dimension k and relative distance Delta_C>0, and G=F_2^L/C^perp with its L coordinate generators. Suppose the specified finite presentation and relation distribution have a unitary stability modulus f in the source precise sense: relation defect epsilon yields an exact representation rho:G->U(D), rank-r partial isometry w:C^d->C^D and eta=f(epsilon), with average_i ||U_i-w*rho(s_i)w||_F^2/d<=eta and both normalized rank deficits <=eta. For epsilon<=1/2, the attached derivation gives flexible permutation stability with F(epsilon)=min{1,2,000,000 f(2epsilon)/Delta_C}; where the second entry is below one, an exact action on N>=d labels has N<=(1+2,000,000f(2epsilon)/Delta_C)d and average generator error at most 2,000,000f(2epsilon)/Delta_C in the charged metric. For the CVY v2 Reed-Muller/Hadamard application, the reported modulus retains its q^(-c) floor and its separate presentation-dependent linear alternative.
Interface: The finite code presentation, coordinate generator distribution and relation sampling distribution are fixed as in the cited CVY Theorem 4.1. The unitary input guarantee includes both partial-isometry rank deficits; their dimension changes are charged before applying N555 with the character gap kappa=4Delta_C. For larger error the trivial action is the stated fallback. Delta_C denotes code relative distance, not N554 copying map.
Proof spine: Convert normalized Hamming relation error to the unitary Frobenius convention, use the code minimum distance to give every nontrivial character energy at least 4Delta_C, and apply the N555 mixed-copy rounding theorem. Embed the finite-dimensional partial-isometry corner in a common coordinate space, extend missing coordinates trivially, and charge all added labels. Substitute the source CVY parameters and preserve the additive q^(-c) floor.
Costs: This is a stability/existence transfer, not a polynomial-time decoder. The stated presentation is quasi-polynomial in k for the concrete sequence; the packet does not meet the sharper polynomial-in-k presentation target. No uniform floor-free poly(log k) epsilon^b guarantee for every positive epsilon is established.
Failure: Do not erase the additive q^(-c) term, the separate presentation-dependent linear alternative, or the charged added-label convention. This does not establish arbitrary-code permutation testability, a non-sofic group, or the random-complex first-stage repair. It is conditional on the precise external CVY and Becker-Chapman theorem interfaces.
Closest comparator: The packet cites Chapman-Vidick-Yuen arXiv:2311.04681v2 Theorem 4.1 and Becker-Chapman arXiv:2005.06652v1 Theorem 1.2, and compares its motivation with Chapman-Lubotzky arXiv:2311.06706v3 Section 6.4. The source notes the permutation presentation problem was described as open in the cited July 2026 CVY version; current priority is not established here.
