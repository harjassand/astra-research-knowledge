Claim: For every finite alphabet, let D_n be finite-valued and nonnegative on strictly positive probability distributions, with D_n(q,q)=0. Assume continuity in p throughout each open simplex, C-infinity regularity near each full-support q, and invariance under independent uninformative augmentation in both tensor orders: D_nm(p⊗r,q⊗r)=D_n(p,q) and D_mn(r⊗p,r⊗q)=D_n(p,q), without presuming relabeling invariance or full tensor additivity. If for every full-support p,q and every irreducible q-reversible continuous-time Markov generator L, t↦D_n(p exp(tL),q) is completely monotone for t>0, then and only then there is one alphabet- and q-independent power series Ψ(z)=Σ_{k>=1} a_k z^k, a_k>=0, with infinite radius of convergence, such that D_n(p,q)=Ψ(χ²(p||q)). Data processing follows from the classification and is not a premise. For s=χ²(p||q)>0 and every integer N>=1, the source gives D_{n^N}(p^⊗N,q^⊗N)>=((1+s)^N-1)/s · D_n(p,q), sharply attained by D=cχ². Thus no nonzero tensor-additive member, or member growing only O(N) under independent replication, lies in the class.
Interface: Finite-state distributions only. Each fixed q has continuity throughout the open simplex and C-infinity regularity in a neighborhood of q; no analyticity, nondegenerate Hessian or differentiability in q is assumed. Independent augmentation identities are explicitly required in both tensor orders; full tensor additivity is not assumed. Complete monotonicity means (-1)^k d^k/dt^k D_n(p exp(tL),q)>=0 for every integer k>=0 and t>0, for every irreducible q-reversible generator. The result does not cover nonreversible generators or a no-smoothness setting.
Proof spine: The proof represents each relaxation as a positive Laplace transform, then uses asymptotic spectral extraction and valid near-reset reversible generators to realize arbitrary q-orthonormal eigenbases. Positivity forces each smooth Taylor jet to be radial. A positive-measure argument recovers the global function without assuming analyticity. Independent augmentation identifies one alphabet-independent series and forces nonnegative coefficients; conversely, each monomial chi-square power is completely monotone along reversible relaxation, and the stated data processing and replication consequences follow.
Costs: No algorithmic, physical or runtime improvement is claimed. The source contains one illustrative exact third-derivative example, but its symbolic-check script was not supplied or rerun. No benchmark or empirical dynamics are involved.

It does not classify nonsmooth functionals, nonreversible relaxation, empirical or quantum dynamics, or Polish-space divergences.
