STATEMENT: On(C^d)^tensorN, FULL Schur isotypic omega_lambda=P_lambda/TrP_lambda has depth EXACT ell(lambda)=nonzero Young-diagram rows. EVERY density supported only in that sector has depth>=ell(lambda). Invertible local filters preserve exact depth.
