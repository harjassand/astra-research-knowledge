# Family116 characteristic-zero independent check

The characteristic-zero source proof survives an independent reconstruction of monodromy, field transfer, formula/ABP conversion, Wronskian/minor multiplicity, and rational bit bounds. This is internal proof review, with no fresh Lean build or external validation. The Lean scope document asserts universal detection but excludes the quantitative construction/dimension statements from its selected theorem.

The strongest new deduction uses **full evaluated matrices**. The source's explicit Volterra tuple at dimension `2nm` hits every nonzero polynomial with an acyclic `m`-state coefficient realization, improving its quadratic first-column dimension to linear full-response dimension. Thus dimension `4ns` suffices for the source's size-`s` formula trees. The proof reconstructs a nonzero bivariate Volterra kernel, bounds a diagonal derivative by rational row-span closure, and recovers its first homogeneous part with invertible Cauchy moments. It uses the source's finite-word faithfulness, but does not use the scalar Moura bound. Root, experiments, analysis and crossfield independently reconstructed the core argument. These internal audits are not external validation; historical novelty remains UNKNOWN.

For arbitrary proper cyclic `m`-state recognizable series, the fixed **m-point scalar grid** `ell T_i`, `ell=1,...,m`, each at dimension `2nm`, hits every nonzero series. The derivative numerator is divisible by the length marker lambda, and after division has lambda-degree at most `m-1`; a nonzero coefficient therefore survives some grid point. Specialization does not use rational row-dependence coefficients. Alternatively, a single nilpotent marker tuple has dimension `2nm(m+1)`, or the grid direct sum has dimension `2nm^2`. Every query is jointly nilpotent and defined under proper-series semantics.

The exact output-only residual learner must retain all matrix entries. Full-width prefix chains and the scalar grid recover a minimal `r<=m` weighted automaton using at most `m(1+nr)` exact queries of dimension at most `2nm(r+1)`, feature length `4n^2m^3`, plus polynomial exact field work. Residual combinations share hidden transitions, so `m` suffices; comparison with an unrelated m-state hypothesis instead needs `2m`. A conservative `2m` learner has at most `2m(1+nr)` queries, dimension at most `4nm(r+1)`, feature length `32n^2m^3`. Individual scalar-seed queries offer a different matrix-size/query-count tradeoff, fully ledgered but not implemented here. All full responses, description sizes, response heights and oracle work are charged. There is no coefficient-bit bound from `n,m` alone.

The independent proof, alias and complete ledger are in `notes/frontier_check_hitting/cycle19/FULL_MATRIX_VOLterra_AND_CYCLIC_ALIAS.md`. New code `work/frontier_check_hitting/cycle19/reconstruct_scalar_grid.py` passes three exact fixtures: native two-state cycles (10 calls, maximum dimension 24), the same target with conservative comparison bound `2m` (20 calls, dimension 48), and a reversed full-operator alias (rank four, 45 calls, dimension 80). A separate reachable-space closure certifies all-word equality, and finite Hankel minors certify fixture minimality. Full data and bit payload costs are saved in `SCALAR_GRID_RECONSTRUCTION_CHECK.json`. These runs test the implementation; they do not replace the proof.

The original first-column length-marker consequence and learner remain separately saved in `CHARZERO_AUDIT_AND_MATRIX_ORACLE_ACQUISITION.md`, `work/frontier_check_hitting/cycle19/reconstruct_abp.py`, and `RECONSTRUCTION_CHECK.json`. Nine original fixtures pass all-word closure certificates; crossfield independently passed eight fresh adversarial fixtures. Their evidence is not relabeled as a test of the new full-response algorithm.

Two general adversarial deductions sharpen the interface. A suitable linear combination of `n` kernels has first jet at order `n`, attaining the `m=2` bound. All degree-`2k` binary homogeneous polynomials fit `m=3*2^k-2` acyclic states, so any faithful fixed first-column feature needs dimension at least `4^k=(m+2)^2/9`. The source's quadratic state scale is therefore near optimal for that particular interface.
