Claim: In a homogeneous spatially flat GR model with separately conserved barotropic components p_i=p_i(ρ_i), ρ_i+p_i≥0, and 0≤c_{s,i}^2=dp_i/dρ_i≤1, allowing a separate constant vacuum term, set x=a^-3 and Y(x)=H^2(x). The source derives Y′≥0 and 0≤xY″≤Y′. Hence Y is convex in x and concave in u=x^2. A source-reported BAO-only check found a sequence satisfying these radial shape restrictions within 0.781 marginal standard deviations of each of six radial measurements, with reported constrained-fit χ²=0.9542; it reports no compelling violation.
Interface: Assumes flat homogeneous GR, separate conservation, barotropic pressure response, the stated null-energy and causal sound-speed bounds, and the source’s DESI DR2 compressed radial-distance likelihood. Does not cover interacting fluids, all scalar-field models, modified gravity, or non-flat cosmology.
Proof spine: Use x=a^-3 and separate conservation to derive xρ′=ρ+p and xρ″=c_s^2ρ′. Sum densities in the flat Friedmann equation to get Y′≥0 and 0≤xY″≤Y′; transform to u=x² for concavity. The report evaluates the finite radial constraints in inverse-square distance coordinates without assuming Gaussian errors there.
Costs: The source reports 13 compressed DESI distances with covariance for its ΛCDM baseline and six radial distances for the shape test; the actual input files and code are absent from this intake, so those calculations are not reproducible from the preserved report alone.
Failure: These are necessary shape conditions, not a characterization or identification of dark energy. An acceptable BAO-only fit does not resolve DESI+CMB+supernova comparisons and is not a new law. The numerical values remain source-reported and unverified by this intake.
Closest comparator: The source identifies barotropic-fluid predecessors. The BAO measurement source is DESI DR2 Results II, arXiv:2503.14738; this intake’s report-level shape check is distinct from the collaboration’s combined-probe cosmological inference.
Open verification: Acquire the exact compressed measurements, covariance, likelihood implementation, and analysis scripts; independently reproduce the radial fit and test robustness to data covariance and competing physical assumptions before any cosmological inference.
