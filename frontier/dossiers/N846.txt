N846-optical-loss-attribution-and-saturable-bucket-boundary | Optical loss flags do not identify target absorption; focusing result is conditional
Status: source_derived_unreviewed. Mission status: NOT_ACHIEVED.

A total photon-loss flag measures extinction from the accessible interface. It does not reveal which unobserved internal sink received the energy. The source gives two target/parasitic sink assignments with the same accessible passive instrument, including under adaptive external control, and a one-port resonator counterexample in which total loss falls while a target absorber takes positive power.

A separate positive two-finite-power construction is conditional: it assumes 0<a<b and (ab)^(1/3)(a^(1/3)+b^(1/3))<=1. After a fixed unitary target-plane map, a thin homogeneous saturable mask with acquired response and complete collection yields a convex loss deficit whose ideal full-control maximum concentrates delivered power into one mode. Variable delivery, phase-only control, powers outside the convexity condition, incomplete collection and response history invalidate broader interpretations. Existing nonlinear-bucket focusing and iTRAN are strong comparators. Only synthetic model checks were run.
