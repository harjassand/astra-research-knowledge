Thus this prescribed-coordinate delete/prune operation has no uniform constant-retention guarantee.
Interface: The construction represents a linear 3-uniform hypergraph as exact sunflower triples of projection rows, appends an identity coordinate, and blocks each triple with a q-color coordinate. The rank is quadratic in the hypergraph vertex count; the existence proof is probabilistic.
Proof spine: A random triple system with two-color multiplicities is sparsified and collision edges deleted to obtain a linear hypergraph with independence number o(N). The projection representation makes its edges exactly the sunflower triples; erasing the color coordinate leaves maximum safe retention alpha(H).
Costs: This is an existential asymptotic obstruction, not an efficient construction. A separate exact rank-three code has maximum safe retention 1/3 after deleting any coordinate.
