Claim: For the finite design prior and shift broadcaster in N552, with K=m^L and D_s=binom(d+s−1,s), the source derives exact Holevo capacity C=(1/L)Σ_{r=1}^L log D_{m^r} and exact actual-prior loss δ=χ(F)−χ(ΦF)=(log D_K−log d)/L. Along the explicit sequence d=m²K² this δ diverges. Thus this construction does not satisfy N368’s bounded-capacity premise or N371’s small-Holevo-loss premise and does not resolve N371. For a reweighted level distribution p_1,…,p_L, the same shift channel has marginal error ½(p_1+p_L+Σ_{r=1}^{L−1}|p_{r+1}−p_r|)≥max_r p_r, so error at most b forces L≥1/b and H(p)≥log(1/b) for this construction. This last inequality is not a universal lower bound for arbitrary broadcasters.
Interface: The formulas use the same finite-dimensional direct-sum family and marginal channel as N552. C is the Holevo capacity supremum over finite priors on that finite family; δ is evaluated for the stated exact finite design prior. The reweighting bound applies only to the described level-shift broadcaster. It does not quantify a universal optimum over channels, input families or reweightings outside this construction.
Scope and resource accounting: The source computes the block-diagonal average state and the entropy of each rank-L family state to obtain C. The marginal shift removes the top copy sector, giving the exact difference δ. A dimension lower bound along d=m²K² shows divergence. For reweighted sectors, total variation between the initial and shifted level distributions gives the stated endpoint/variation expression, bounded below by the maximum weight; the support and Shannon-entropy consequences follow only for this shift mechanism.
Costs: The same K=m^L copy resource and rapidly growing symmetric-space dimension as N552 apply. The source gives exact information quantities but no resource-bounded preparation or physical acquisition. Diverging C and δ are precisely why the example cannot test bounded-capacity or small-loss stability.
Failure: Do not use this construction as a counterexample to N368 or N371: its capacity and actual-prior Holevo loss diverge. It establishes no matching lower bound for N368’s logarithmic rate and closes no N371 gate. The reweighting inequality concerns only this shift construction; it is not a general converse for approximate broadcasting.
