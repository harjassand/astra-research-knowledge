Claim: For F=[[4,0,0,1],[0,9,4,0],[0,4,9,0],[1,0,0,4]], there are no A_i in GL(2,C) such that (A_1 tensor A_2)F(A_3 tensor A_4)^T is a nonnegative four-port weighted perfect-matching gadget signature in the cited Cai–Liu sense. The same obstruction holds with F replaced by F^k for every integer k>=1 under the stated identical serial grouping.
Interface: The port flattening is (1,2)|(3,4), with each port dimension two. All four transformations are independent and invertible over C; transposes are ordinary, not conjugate transposes. The result is restricted to one tensor and its identical serial powers.
Proof spine: The invariant K(M)=MJM^TJ transforms by similarity and a common scalar under the full local group. F gives roots {25,9,169,25}, normalized to {13,5,5,3}. The necessary complementary-product inequalities for a nonnegative matching signature fail for each possible parity-block pairing; powers give the same strict failures raised to k.
Costs: This is a finite-dimensional algebraic obstruction with four 2-by-2 gauges; no search over k is needed because the inequalities fail for every positive integer power.
Failure: This excludes only the stated local gauges and identical serial powers. It does not imply counting hardness, absence of an FPRAS, or impossibility of nonlocal, approximate, singular, heterogeneous, multiwire, larger-port, or other gadget representations. The imported necessary condition is essential.
Closest comparator: Cai and Liu (2019), https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.10493, Theorem 4.1, supplies a necessary condition only. Four-qubit invariant theory is established context; source states the exact obstruction priority was not audited. No novelty or complexity separation is claimed.
Open verification: Independently verify the full-complex local invariant, imported necessary matchgate condition, parity handling, and the all-k inequalities. Keep the global EPR counting/oracle gate open.
