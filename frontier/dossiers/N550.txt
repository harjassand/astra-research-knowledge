Symbols and resource contracts LOCAL to this card.

Claim: The packet reports three synthetic diagnostics. In a three-dimensional non-Gaussian quartic exact-i.i.d. tilt model, 25 repetitions at 32,000 accepted draws per regime produced mean relative Frobenius errors 1.70% for B, 1.71% for D and 1.52% for K; local generation/fitting time excludes physical preparation and equilibration. A two-dimensional non-affine torus calculation used deterministic stationary-grid solutions, not random observed samples: at grid side 192, three-regime D error was 0.0288%, while the two-regime weighted system had condition number about 198 and 3.74% error. A quartic-family entropy-production estimate reported 1.63% mean relative error at 32,000 draws per regime over 12 repetitions, with the quartic potential family supplied and an independently sampled reference based on 750,000 additional baseline draws. These are source-reported synthetic outcomes, not empirical scientific validation or finite-sample theorems.
Interface: The 3D study uses exact independent synthetic stationary draws and a specified quartic potential; the torus study solves a discretized stationary PDE and computes grid scores/Hessians; the entropy-production study supplies the quartic potential family and draws a separate reference. None uses real physical data or charges physical stationary preparation.
Reported diagnostic scope: The handoff records generated sample populations, numerical stationary grids, estimates, errors, replication counts and selected controls. The finite proof-check output tests floating-point consistency only. The packet scripts/results are preserved; no script or experiment was rerun during repository intake.
Costs: Reported CPU timings omit physical perturbation, calibration, mixing/equilibration and instrument costs; the entropy-production reference uses 750,000 additional exact independent baseline draws (1,050,000 rejection proposals). Grid computation is exponential in dimension. No costed physical or noisy-correlated stationary-snapshot acquisition is demonstrated.
Failure: The source explicitly reports model-misspecification, rank/conditioning, score/Hessian acquisition and high-dimensional sample barriers. The two-regime torus estimate is ill-conditioned despite full rank; deterministic-grid precision is not sample-based recovery.
Closest comparator: The 3D finite-force Gaussian-moment comparator is not state of the art. The source separately supplies a stronger same-sample score-moment comparison in N551. No broad estimator dominance or novel physical capability is claimed.
Open verification: Replicate with independently generated finite noisy stationary data under charged mixing/preparation costs and controlled misspecification; first establish that the needed density-ratio gradients or score structure can be estimated in the intended dimension.
