Claim: For a nonexplosive stochastic mass-action CTMC with at most R non-null reactions, input order at most k, positive rates bounded below by known kappa_min, and the stated deterministic reset interface, let x(S)_i=M 1[i in S], M=max(k,1). The infinitesimal departure hazard is q(S)=sum_{A subseteq S} theta_A, hence q(b)=sum_A theta_A product_{i in A} b_i is a nonnegative sparse multilinear polynomial whose nonzero monomials identify exactly the distinct reactant supports. The source gives PositivePeel using O(R[(k+1)log(d+1)+2^k]) exact correlation/point-query types.
Interface: d labeled species; known bounded input order k, rate floor kappa_min, rate ceiling, and controlled exact molecule-count resets; complete labeled destructive endpoint states and calibrated short observation times. Duplicate reactions sharing a reactant support are aggregated at this stage. Randomized finite-sample recovery additionally assumes independent runs, known rate/resource bounds, and no hidden species or readout noise.
Proof spine: The t-downarrow-0 departure rate is a positive sum of enabled channel propensities. On binary occupancy masks it is a sparse nonnegative multilinear polynomial. Positive Fourier differences D(B,H) test for an extension support; binary peeling recovers a maximal support, and Boolean Möbius inversion extracts its aggregate coefficient before subtraction.
Costs: The query count is not experiment count: each correlation is estimated from short-time endpoint samples. Finite-sample guarantees depend on kappa_min, time bias, rates, R, k, ell and confidence. Very small rates, high order, reset fidelity, complete counting, computation, sample volume and raw-data retention may dominate.
Failure: Support recovery does not identify product complexes or separate channels with identical reactant and product complexes. It excludes null reactions; exact controlled resets and complete state observation are essential. The attached note provides no supporting code or run receipts, and does not establish realistic cell-scale acquisition.
Closest comparator: Positive sparse polynomial and weighted-hypergraph reconstruction are established neighboring problems; the attached note explicitly does not claim an optimal decoder or novelty of those ingredients.
