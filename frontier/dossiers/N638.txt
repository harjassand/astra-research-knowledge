Claim: Define LY(r), r>1, by zero-freeness of the flattened polynomial f_K(z,w)=sum_xy K_xy z^x w^y when all variables lie in the open polydisk of radius r. For every complex 2^n-by-2^n K in LY(r), the maximum-modulus eigenvalue is unique, nonzero and algebraically simple, and every other eigenvalue obeys |lambda/lambda_*|<=r^-2; its rank-one spectral projector factors into LY(r) vectors. Also, if K_1,...,K_T each lie in LY(r), M=K_T...K_1, e=|0^n>, and a=e^T M e !=0, then B=(Me)(e^T M)/a has rank one and ||M-B||_F <= 2(1+r^2)^(n/2)r^(-2T)||Me||_2 <= 2(1+r^2)^(n/2)r^(-2T)||M||_2.
Interface: All matrices may be complex, nonnormal, non-PSD and varying; they share the same supplied whole-layer LY(r) promise. Products and contractions use bilinear transposes without conjugation. The estimate is conditional on the global zero-free promise for every factor.
Proof spine: Import two zero-free contraction/limit facts; establish eigenvector comparison via holomorphic diameter contraction, then use generic perturbation or the product theorem to obtain spectral separation. For varying products, propagate polynomial ratios and apply Parseval to bound the Frobenius residual from the explicit rank-one B.
Costs: The explicit sufficient product length for relative operator-norm error epsilon is T >= [log(2/epsilon)+(n/2)log(1+r^2)]/[2log r], conditional on the shared whole-layer LY(r) promise; this is a theorem bound, not an implementation-cost guarantee.
Failure: A zero-free promise does not recognize itself from local input, compute M e or e^T M, normalize thermal quantities, or provide physical acquisition/nonunitary implementation. No polynomial-cost state-preparation, thermal-normalization or finite-temperature capability follows; exponential postselection examples are explicit. The theorem extends a source-cited PSD mechanism but originality is not claimed.
