The ideal expected acquisition-inclusive cost is O((C_X+d)(1+R^2)ln((1+R^2)/epsilon)); R=0 needs no oracle calls. This is internally reconstructed known mathematics, not a fresh capability claim.

Generate m independent accepted factors: propose y~N(0,I_d/m), draw J~Poisson(2), acquire J fresh X_j, and accept with product_j (1+clip[-1,1](y dot X_j))/2. The empty product is one. Averaging J gives acceptance exp(w_hat(y)-1), w_hat=E clip(y dot X). Thus accepted density is proportional to q_m exp(w_hat), acceptance >=e^-2 and expected acquisitions <=2e^2 per factor, without assuming J independent of acceptance. The unclipped tilt is N(mu/m,I_d/m). Gaussian projection tail estimates yield factor TV<=10 exp[-m/(8R^2)] and product/convolution contraction gives total TV<=10m exp[-m/(8R^2)]<=epsilon. Positivity, almost-sure termination, exact normalization and full-law scope are part of the source argument.
