ANGLE-INDEPENDENT RANGE COMPARISON
For injective maps M,N from the same r-dimensional domain,

    D_tr(unif Ran M, unif Ran N) <= rank(M-N)/r.

The report applies this rank-charge bound to truncate selected connected components in a graph construction, with logarithmic truncation length under its stated component assumptions. The claim concerns uniform states on the ranges themselves; it does not establish the state produced by applying a map to a uniform logical input. Preparing the target range, satisfying the logical constraints and achieving polynomial cost when local dimensions vary remain open.

GUARDED HISTORY LIFT — CONDITIONAL CONTRACT
For an old bad projector C and a fresh site B, the report bounds the low-source component by rank(P_lo)<=alpha d_B rank(C)/(1-eta), retaining the fresh guard on the input effect. A bounded-branch history construction is further conditional: branch count at most b, each lifted creation norm at most sqrt(alpha), alpha<=1/(16 b^2), and t=1/(2 sqrt(b)) give a reported weighted history-resolvent norm at most 2 and eraser norm at most 2/sqrt(3). An acquired common decoder/orthogonal logical chart block and the exact K-valued recurrence are required; with those premises the report states success probability at least 3/16. The recurrence, low-polar implementation, logical root preparation and disconnected-component composition are not supplied as a complete general encoder. This is a conditional acquisition result, not an end-to-end algorithm.

FAILURES, PREDECESSORS, AND NEXT GATE
Bell-projector chains have exponentially small Hilbert–Schmidt word weight but normalized source/range rank fraction 1/d^2 at every length; word weight cannot replace a normalized-state estimate. Keeping the original degree-zero row map and asserting a bounded contracting homotopy would imply a spectral gap, so higher source labels alone do not remove that requirement. The global product-chart argument is identified as existing R01 capital, not a new result. N98 already records that history erasure is not free in general; this continuation gives only a conditional case with acquired structure and does not invalidate that boundary. The decisive next step is an actual positive selected-range encoder/decoder for arbitrary low-rank noncommuting local rows, with normalization, source constraints, precision and variable-local-dimension cost polynomial.
