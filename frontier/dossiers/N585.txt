Claim: Let Lambda_0 be a full-rank symplectically self-dual k-mode lattice of volume (2pi)^k and chi:Lambda_0->Z_2 a nontrivial homomorphism. Choose t with exp(i t^T Omega lambda)=(-1)^chi(lambda), so 2t belongs to Lambda_0. The index-two lattice Lambda_n={(lambda_1,...,lambda_n) in Lambda_0^n: sum_j chi(lambda_j)=0 mod 2} is a valid commuting lattice encoding one qubit. For additive Gaussian noise with density f_v=N(0,v I_{2k}) per block and a fundamental cell C_0, define g_lat(s)=sum_{lambda in Lambda_0} f_v(s+lambda), f_chi(s)=sum_{lambda in Lambda_0}(-1)^chi(lambda)f_v(s+lambda), r(s)=f_chi(s)/g_lat(s), and g_t(s)=g_lat(s+t). Set rho(v)=integral_{C_0} f_chi(s)^2/g_lat(s) ds and beta(v)=integral_{C_0} sqrt(g_lat(s)g_t(s)) ds. For a uniform bit T with conditional iid syndrome densities g_lat or g_t, let H_shift(n)=H(T|Y_1,...,Y_n); let H_parity(n)=E_{S_j iid g_lat} h_2((1+product_j r(S_j))/2), with binary entropy in bits. The source-defined ideal analog stabilizer decoder obeys I_c(n)=1-H_parity(n)-H_shift(n) and I_c(n)>=rho(v)^n/(2 ln 2)-beta(v)^n, so rho(v)>beta(v) is sufficient for positive ideal coherent information at finite n. At bath occupation nu=1, sender preamplification maps v=2(1-eta); however, no lattice meeting the condition near v=1/2 is supplied.
Interface: The result concerns an ideal infinite-energy lattice-code family and analog syndrome decoding for a Gaussian additive-noise channel. The transfer to the thermal attenuator uses sender preamplification with v=2(1-eta) at nu=1. A physical finite-energy code additionally requires a charged envelope, Gram orthonormalization, syndrome instrument, finite precision and cutoff-error bound; these are not established by the integral criterion.
Proof spine: A nontrivial character of the self-dual base lattice defines a half-shift t. The parity-kernel sublattice has index two and its dual yields four logical cosets, giving a pure ideal qubit repetition family. Decomposing the decoded logical error into shift and parity bits gives I_c=1-H_parity-H_shift exactly. The binary entropy series lower-bounds 1-H_parity by rho^n/(2 ln 2), while a Bhattacharyya bound gives H_shift<=beta^n.
Costs: A block of n repetitions over a k-mode base uses kn physical modes per logical qubit. For the stated additive-channel simulation the source counts a sender preamplifier and a thermal-channel use per physical mode. No finite-energy preparation, decoder precision, outer coding or runtime bound is included.
Failure: The sufficient gate rho(v)>beta(v) is not met by the reported square, rectangular and tested hexagonal lattice seeds near v=1/2; the reported image-sum values are floating-point diagnostics, not a certified global search. No actual thermal-attenuator positive coherent-information witness near eta=0.75, finite-energy transfer, or complete decoder is proved. Failure of these examples is not impossibility for other lattices or codes.
