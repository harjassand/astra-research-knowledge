Claim: Assume the exact model and input-output germ of N594. If every physical node has the same unknown alpha>0, K is symmetric positive definite with K_ij<=0 for i!=j, and its off-diagonal graph is connected, then each active synchrony block B has reduced cubic coefficient alphabar_B=alpha/|B|, no physical coordinates are inactive, and |B|=alpha/alphabar_B is an integer. The one-port coefficient identifies alpha, so the exact germ determines the physical node count N=sum_B alpha/alphabar_B, even though hidden internal connections/eigenvalues can remain unidentifiable. This is a source-reported exact-data consequence, not a noisy census procedure.
Interface: Homogeneous positive cubic coefficient at every physical node, irreducible symmetric SPD M-matrix coupling (off-diagonals nonpositive and connected), same zero-initial single-port input/output and exact endpoint-germ interface as N594. Unknown alpha is allowed because it is identified at the port. No finite-noise observation contract is provided.
Proof spine: The canonical quotient of N594 has reduced block coefficient alpha/|B|. Irreducible SPD M-matrix positivity gives K^-1 e_1 strictly positive; K-invariance places this vector in W, excluding inactive coordinates and mixed-sign blocks. Thus all blocks are ordinary synchronized node blocks and their multiplicities are recovered as alpha/alphabar_B.
Costs: The count follows from the exact adaptive quotient/finite-jet certificate of N594 and exact identification of reduced cubic coefficients. This does not identify hidden within-block stiffness or transverse residual dynamics, and exact integer/rank decisions do not provide finite-noise sample costs.
Failure: The common-alpha and connected SPD M-matrix hypotheses are material. Signed off-diagonal couplings can leave inactive coordinates; heterogeneous alpha can produce the same harmonic coefficient sum for different block sizes. Weak coupling makes the exact census arbitrarily ill-conditioned. The formula is not a general node-count method.
Closest comparator: Local network-size estimation can return minimal realization order while merging synchronized nodes; Tang et al., New Journal of Physics 22 (2020), 093031, is cited in the source. Brovia et al. (2026), DOI 10.1063/5.0318179, is a close modern predecessor. The proposed distinction is using the homogeneous cubic coefficient to lift a one-port minimal quotient to physical multiplicities; priority remains UNKNOWN.
Open verification: Reconstruct the inverse-positivity and signed-block argument, check both necessity counterexamples (signed couplings and heterogeneous coefficients), and search prior work on inferring multiplicities from a nonlinear minimal quotient.
