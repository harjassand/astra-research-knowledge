Claim: For q_n(C)=sum_{S subseteq[n]}(-1/2)^|S| ||Tr_S C||_F^2 on complex matrices in M_(3^n), the universal target q_n(C)>=0 for all n and rank(C)<=2 remains unresolved. The source reports that for every fixed n>=1 there is a neighborhood within the rank-at-most-two variety of C0=|0^n><0^n|+(-1)^(n+1)|1^n><1^n| where q_n is nonnegative, with a smooth local zero manifold of real dimension 4*2^n-6+4n; all its points are critical and the Hessian is positive in normal directions. The radius is not uniform in n. An exact block lift shows that uniform local stability at all diag(R,R,0) rank-two zeros would encode the lower-copy global target; a separate high-rank witness defeats one proposed swap-sector concentration route but is not a rank-two counterexample.
Interface: The local theorem is fixed-n, complex rank exactly two near the specified C0 family (the local neighborhood is separated from rank<=1). The global question quantifies over every n and every complex rank<=2 C. The local radius has no uniform lower bound. q_n uses partial trace over the indexed qutrit sites and Frobenius norm.
Proof spine: Construct the explicit smooth zero manifold, prove stationarity, compute the full rank-two chart Hessian including its kernel and positive normal spectrum, then use a positive normal Hessian in adapted coordinates for higher-order local control. Separately derive q_(m+1)(C(t))=t^2 q_m(X) for the exact rank-two block lift and compute the high-rank swap-sector counterexample.
Costs: The argument is a fixed-n local analysis on a rank-two matrix variety of ambient dimension 3^n; its neighborhood radius and quantitative conditioning are not uniform or supplied as usable bounds.
Failure: This proves neither the global q_n inequality nor an NPT bound-entangled state. The exact lift is a barrier to an overbroad local-to-global argument; the swap-sector witness has high rank and is not a negative rank-two C. Two-copy positivity is cited prior art, not a result of this intake.
Closest comparator: The packet cites Song and Chen, https://arxiv.org/html/2607.23416v1 and https://arxiv.org/html/2607.24479v1 for two-copy work, plus Tran et al. 2017 (https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.03385), a 2024 paper (https://arxiv.org/html/2402.18037v1) and earlier local-minimum work (https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.2613). Citations and originality are not rechecked; no novelty claim is made.
Open verification: Independently reconstruct the full fixed-n Hessian/normal-coordinate proof and exact lift. Keep G-ALL-COPY-NPT-UNDISTILLABILITY open until a proof or valid complex rank-two counterexample covers the universal target.
