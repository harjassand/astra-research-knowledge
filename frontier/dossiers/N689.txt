N689-bounded-exchange-unbiased-rounding-classification | Bounded exchange width characterizes uniformly bounded unbiased rounding
status=source_derived_unreviewed. The pasted report presents a complete proof candidate; no independent proof reconstruction, formal certification, or priority review was done at intake.

For a nonempty Boolean-vertex polytope P=conv(V), V⊆{0,1}^n, define κ(P) as the largest number of coordinates changed by an edge of P. Let R(P) be the least D such that for every matrix A with Euclidean column norms at most one and every x∈P, there is a distribution on V with E[Y]=x and ||A(Y−x)||∞≤D almost surely. The report claims, for k=κ(P)≥1,

    k/2 ≤ R(P) ≤ 4096 k^3 + 48 k.

For a singleton R(P)=0. Thus a family has uniformly bounded R(P) exactly when its exchange widths are uniformly bounded. Exact marginals follow by objective-aware existence for each linear objective and a separation argument; at most n+1 support points suffice.

For matroid bases, k≤2, giving the source-reported existence bound 32864 under the stated column normalization. For a 0–1 matrix with at most Δ ones per column, the stated bound becomes 32864√Δ. The one-objective cost guarantee does not claim that every output of one unbiased distribution is cost-nonincreasing. No polynomial-time sampler or efficient constrained rounding algorithm is established: the proof uses a compact nonconvex minimization for existence.

The reported proof spine is a face-dimension lemma dim(F)≥s(F)/k, an energy-corrected spectral barrier and a finite face-descent argument, followed by separation for exact marginals. The lower bound uses an exposed edge changing k coordinates. A failed quartic minimization example defeats that proposed construction only. An even-cycle example shows why the stated extension does not cover arbitrary intersections of two matroids.

Scope relative to existing Astra records: N14 concerns small-mass cube rounding with supplied signing machinery and a stable-law support counterexample. N24 gives executable rational methods for sparse cube and partition-base settings with separate bounds and costs. N689 is a geometric classification for general Boolean-vertex polytopes with bounded exchange width; its upper proof is existential and does not supply N24-style implementation. It neither revises nor validates N14/N24, and it does not close P-round.

The report attributes the spectral-signing engine to Guo–Fang–Lu (https://arxiv.org/html/2609.23540v1) and compares the matroid application with Bérczi–Liu–Reis–Tarnawski (https://arxiv.org/html/2608.13983v1). These are source-reported prior-art links; exact novelty remains UNKNOWN.
