N543-action-compatible-stochastic-retention-contraction | An action-aware transfer operator bounds retention under positive stochastic composition

Claim: Let a compact group G act orthogonally on a finite-dimensional real inner-product space V by pi, with normalized Haar measure. For independent Haar controls and an m-state Markov simulator whose row-stochastic update P_t(g) may depend measurably on time and the current control, set b_i(t)=E[pi(g_t...g_1)x 1{S_t=i}] and R_t=sum_i ||b_i(t)||. Define kappa_m(pi)=sup_{||v||=1} sup_{||w_1||=...=||w_m||=1} integral_G max_j <w_j,pi(g)v> dg. Then R_t<=kappa_m(pi)R_(t-1), hence R_T<=kappa_m(pi)^T||x||. The adjoint action-compatible transfer is (A_P z)_i=integral pi(g)^(-1)(sum_j P_g(i,j)z_j)dg and has norm at most kappa_m(pi).
Interface: The controls are independent Haar draws; the representation is orthogonal; the state is the only charged persistent record of past controls. Fresh randomness is allowed. The transition rule can depend on time and the current control. The theorem bounds unnormalized conditional means, not only pure or initially orbit-valued states.
Proof spine: The conditional-mean recursion expresses each next state vector as a sum of group-averaged stochastic transitions. Align one unit vector with each resulting integral. Row-stochasticity bounds their sum by the orbit-code maximum defining kappa; the triangle inequality and summation over states give the one-step contraction. The transfer operator is the corresponding adjoint map.
Costs: A state-retention theorem only. The description size of P_t, gate acquisition, precision, barycentric compilation and sampling costs are not bounded. Persistent seeds, correlated external noise and external continuous controllers must be charged or are outside the model.
Failure: This does not lower-bound signed-coordinate or unrestricted numerical simulators. Correlated external noise changes the memory contract. The Haar one-step coefficient cannot be applied unchanged to arbitrary finite control alphabets; see N546.
Closest comparator: The representation-valued transform and spherical quantization ingredients are known mathematics. The source presents the operational contraction/composition interface as its candidate contribution; novelty is UNKNOWN. N507 is a scoped comparison only and is not used as a proved premise.
