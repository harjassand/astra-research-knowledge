N611-countable-stratified-networks-unique-stationary-law-local-mixing | Countable stratified networks have a unique stationary law with uniform local convergence

Symbols and resource contracts LOCAL to this card.

Claim: As a corollary of the stratified sampler, the source claims that a countable network with uniformly bounded parent incidence and the same finite-depth, strictly lower-level nonlinear forcing has a unique stationary probability measure on (F_q^c)^Lambda. Every finite-dimensional local marginal converges to stationarity uniformly over the initial configuration, with a source-stated exponential-in-time bound C exp(-gamma T); a finite list of stationary coordinates is sampleable with finite expected local oracle work. Constants may depend on the fixed observable batch and model parameters.
Interface: This is a local/fixed-observable result for countable sites, finite state alphabet, positive Haar-reset probability, uniformly bounded indegree and fixed finite level count. For multiple requested observation times, their prescribed separation must be accounted for separately; the simple terminal-time local mixing bound is not a global joint mixing guarantee.
Proof spine: Compactness of the countable product and the Feller property yield existence. The finite-depth rank/masking certificate closes before a random ancestry horizon with exponential tail and makes a target cylinder's law independent of all initial values on that event. The remaining total-variation discrepancy is bounded by the tail event; hence invariant measures agree on every finite cylinder and are equal.
Costs: The source reuses the fixed-batch oracle-work bound in N610. It gives no efficient algorithm for the entire infinite configuration and no system-size-independent cost for arbitrary global observables.
Failure: Uniqueness and convergence are restricted to the stratified model. The source explicitly distinguishes this from general positive-noise nonlinear cellular automata, where counterexamples to universal uniqueness are known. It does not imply O(log N) convergence of the full q^(Nc)-state finite network in total variation.
Closest comparator: The source cites existing ergodicity results for noisy algebraic cellular automata and known positive-noise obstructions. Its corollary's independent novelty is not established.
Open verification: Independently verify the compact-product Feller construction, the uniform-in-initial-configuration cylinder coupling/masking argument, and the parameter dependence of C and gamma; compare the exact class and conclusion against cited noisy-CA results.
