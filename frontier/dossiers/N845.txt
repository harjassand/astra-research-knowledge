The corrected blind fixed-dose result assumes one deterministic common cumulative fluence at each attempted site and unconditioned occupied output. It gives q*=p sigma_L/[p sigma_L+(1-p)sigma_H], while allowing vacancy-dependent refill timing. An independent bounded-adaptive counterexample shows why that bound cannot be extended to outcome-dependent stopping.

The ideal renewal operation screens one fresh atom to fluence d and observes either ejection or survival. Ejection triggers fresh refill and restart; a survivor is accepted and protected. Under the stated independent refill and fixed local-rate model, accepted heavy composition is q(d)=p exp(-sigma_H d)/[p exp(-sigma_H d)+(1-p)exp(-sigma_L d)]. At planning p=.01109 and central 95 kV cross sections sigma_L=56.6 mb and sigma_H=2.1 mb, the nominal 90% calculation gives EXPECTED costs of about 105 attempts, 104 refills and 1.959e13 target electrons/nm^2. Retry and dose tails are unbounded; finite caps reduce yield. These are conditional expectations at those inputs, not a general 90% cost. The 0.298 J/nm^2 value is incident kinetic energy per addressed area. The same-atom event, reset, repaired-site rate and protection interfaces are not jointly obtained. CVD/post-growth isotope methods remain substantial incumbents.
