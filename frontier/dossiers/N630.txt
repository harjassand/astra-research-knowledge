Proof spine: Choose a complex-kernel basis and assign free entries 0,1,omega,omega^2. A pointed-sector argument forces each incidence row to see either no nonzero types or multiple types. Types plus a base bit vector generate three uniform replicas with valid patterns and exact density 4^rank. For nonbinary alphabets, choose a distinct triple for one nonbinary latent in each affected coordinate.
Costs: Fraction-free rational elimination and exact Q(omega) comparisons have polynomial bit complexity in the explicit incidence matrix. Output and recoding cost remain explicit.
Failure: This assumes a supplied strong product representation; it does not learn one from arbitrary rows.
