Claim: Let the same smooth unknown drift b and constant symmetric D≻0 govern controlled diffusions on R^d with vanishing boundary flux/integrability, or on a flat torus. Under known smooth controls u_i, strictly positive stationary C² densities p_i and divergence-free currents J_i=(b+u_i)p_i−D∇p_i, set r=p_i/p_j, w=∇log r and δu=u_i−u_j. For every admissible scalar q for which the integrations are justified, the source derives D:E_{p_i}[q(r)wwᵀ]=E_{p_i}[q(r)δu·w]. Thus full rank of the collected symmetric matrix features M_{ij,q}=E_{p_i}[q(r)wwᵀ], in dimension d(d+1)/2, identifies the constant D uniquely.
Interface: All regimes share b and constant state-independent D; intervention fields are known; stationary laws are exact and fully observed for the theorem. The feature-rank condition is a hypothesis to check, not a consequence of having multiple regimes. The test-function class for q and the required boundary/integrability conditions are not fully enumerated in the source.
Proof spine: Use J_i=rJ_j+p_i(δu−Dw). Choose f with f′(r)=q(r)/r, multiply the stationary divergence equation by f(r), cancel the J_j term as a gradient against a divergence-free current, and integrate the remaining divergence by parts. The resulting identity is linear in the symmetric entries of D.
Costs: No finite-sample or numerical acquisition bound. The features require density-ratio gradients and stationary expectations; acquiring or estimating these, waiting for each regime to mix, and calibrating controls are uncharged. An algebraic perturbation bound is conditional on the exact design singular value and feature errors.
Failure: Rank deficiency prevents unique D recovery; two-regime weighted features can be ill-conditioned despite full rank. The exact identity does not supply a statistical estimator for general smooth densities or a physical snapshot-acquisition protocol.
Closest comparator: Fokker–Planck inversion and weak-form/Stein generator identities have established prior work. The source reports a partial literature check and leaves the exact weighted current-cancellation identity priority UNKNOWN.
