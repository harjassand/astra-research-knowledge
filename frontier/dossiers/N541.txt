N541-finite-equilibrium-witness-practical-acquisition-barrier | A finite discrete-time observable separates a positive-spectrum target from reversible hidden models at impractical sample cost
status=source_derived_unreviewed; Source-reported derived proof, adversarial review, exact finite algebra and portable replay. External/formal verification, priority and a practical test are not established.
Symbols and resource contracts LOCAL to this card.

Claim: For a supplied rational ten-state generator Q, changed discrete-time chains P_e=I+eQ (e=1,1/2) have stationary six-label observed laws that are time-reversal invariant and pass the stated centered reflection complete-positivity tests, yet an explicit rational statistic W_e on five consecutive letters has nonnegative expectation under every stationary reversible hidden Markov model with deterministic emissions and negative expectation under P_e. The e=1/2 target additionally has positive observable spectrum [0,1], hence completely monotone scalar correlations. A certified empirical-Bernstein test for this half-step target has sufficient budget 2,057,310,156,600,085,612,368 independent stationary windows. This is sufficient for the stated test, not necessary sample complexity.
Interface: The construction changes the continuous-time source process; P_e is not claimed to sample the original CTMC. Competitors are stationary reversible hidden Markov models with deterministic emissions and arbitrary hidden cardinality. The finite test assumes independent stationary five-letter windows and stationary preparation; it is not a guarantee for one correlated trajectory under arbitrary mixing.
Proof spine: Exact rational one-step features and a five-marker left inverse define a bounded five-letter statistic. A universal inequality forces nonnegative expectation for each stated reversible hidden model; exact target algebra makes it negative. Outward dyadic rounding and exact five-word recursion enclose the half-step target second moment, and empirical Bernstein yields the stated iid-window sufficient count.
Costs: Source reports 82 rational seed/geometry checks and exhaustive enumeration of all 6^5=7,776 words. The half-step certificate requires about 2.06e21 independent stationary windows, over 10^22 observed letters plus independent stationary preparation. A separate original-CTMC variance bound is about 2e64 windows but conditional on inherited mean/gap/range premises. Both are impractical.
Failure: Not a necessary sample lower bound, not a single-correlated-trajectory guarantee, and not an experiment or explanation of a natural process. The finite-state rational seed is supplied. It does not establish a general thermodynamic estimator or historic discovery.
Closest comparator: Related to Astra N509/N511/N512 finite-support and hidden-equilibrium work but uses a distinct five-letter statistic and a changed discrete-time source; no common interface or proof transfer is inferred.
Open verification: Independently audit the universal hidden-model inequality and exact certificate; materially lower the certified range-to-gap ratio or prove improved acquisition under explicit preparation and trajectory assumptions.
