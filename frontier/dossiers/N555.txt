N555-finite-source-unitary-permutation-rounding | A mixed-copy energy gap gives a charged exact permutation action from a supplied finite-dimensional unitary representation
status=source_derived_unreviewed; Source-reported complete proof candidate with a focused algebra, dimension-bookkeeping and primary-interface check; this intake did not independently reconstruct it. External correctness and historical priority remain unresolved. No 9-10/10 breakthrough is claimed.
Symbols and resource contracts LOCAL to this card.

Claim: Let G be a finite group, S a generating list sampled with weights mu, rho:G->U(d) an exact representation, and P_s prescribed permutation matrices. Put delta^2=E_s ||rho(s)-P_s||_F^2/d. On Hom(C^d,C^d tensor C^d), define R(g)A=(rho(g) tensor rho(g))A rho(g)* and let Pi project onto the invariant subspace. If E_s||R(s)A-A||_F^2 >= kappa||A-Pi A||_F^2 for every A, with kappa>0, then an exact action h:G->Sym(N) exists with d<=N<=(1+197316 delta^2/kappa)d and E_s d_h(P_s,h(s)) <= (1+330354/kappa)delta^2. Here d_h charges every added label: it is one minus the fraction of the N labels agreeing with the original d-label input.
Interface: An exact finite-dimensional representation rho and a uniform mixed-copy energy gap are supplied hypotheses. The original permutation matrices are compared with the representation in normalized Frobenius square. d_h counts added labels in the denominator N; no compact-group or Haar-selection step is used.
Proof spine: The three-factor estimate from N554 bounds the copying vector defect by 9d delta^2. The gap controls its distance from invariant vectors; exact invariance yields an approximate map from the finite group to permutations. The multiplicative defect is at most 18 eta^2. Apply the finite-source Becker-Chapman theorem and track both degree growth and generator error, retaining the added-sheet charge.
Costs: This is an existence theorem, not an efficient repair algorithm. Enumerating the finite group may be much more expensive than reading its presentation. It requires an exact representation and a gap; approximate permutation generators alone do not furnish that representation.
Failure: No efficient algorithm or polynomial presentation-time guarantee follows. The conditional rounding theorem does not itself produce rho or prove the energy gap for an arbitrary group. Compact/Haar extensions are unnecessary and are not part of the stated proof.
Closest comparator: The attached source invokes Becker-Chapman Theorem 1.2 only in its finite-source case. N554 supplies the quantitative three-factor quotient estimate used in the argument.
Open verification: Independently reconstruct the invariant-vector defect, constants, finite-source approximate-action repair and charged-degree bookkeeping; verify that every use of the supplied exact representation and energy gap is explicit.
