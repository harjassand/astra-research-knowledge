N540-bounded-energy-gaussian-heralding-perfect-matching-equivalence | A restricted low-energy Gaussian conditional sampler is polynomially equivalent to perfect-matching approximation
status=source_derived_unreviewed; Source-reported reconstructed reductions, adversarial model criticism and exact finite checks. External/formal correctness, novelty, an implemented conditional sampler and physical acquisition are not established.
Symbols and resource contracts LOCAL to this card.

Claim: For the explicitly constructed rational, nonnegative, pure undisplaced Gaussian pair kernels with operator norm<1/2 and mean photon number<5/12, condition on one photon in every graph mode and leave two output modes unheralded. A classical conditional sampler with adjustable TV error and polynomial runtime in exact input length and inverse error is polynomially equivalent, under the stated reductions, to an FPRAS for perfect matchings of arbitrary unweighted simple graphs, including exact zero detection.
Interface: Restricted supplied Gaussian family and graph; one unit-photon herald in every original graph mode, two output modes remain. The sampler must work uniformly over every nonzero herald. The reductions charge precision, encoded bit length, conditional calls, zero heralds and sample budget.
Proof spine: Attach two leaves to an edge uv. The two remaining modes output only 00 or 11 with q=R^2 p_uv^2/(1+R^2 p_uv^2), where p_uv is a uniform-perfect-matching edge marginal. Dyadic calibration and self-reduction recover the count from conditional samples; approximate counting supplies the reverse direction. Source replays check finite graph/hafnian identities and support.
Costs: The reduction uses O(n^2 log(n/delta)+n^3 epsilon^-2 log(n/delta)) conditional samples plus a coarse-search term; the finest requested TV error is Theta(epsilon/n). Literal herald rejection costs at least (63/128)4^n preparations per accepted calibrated event. The direct sampler is not implemented; proof constants request about 7.3e8 samples already at n=2, epsilon=.1, delta=.01.
Failure: This is an equivalence, not an efficient conditional sampler, matching algorithm or complexity separation. It applies only to the stated family and herald pattern. Variable-subset GBS has a different output law and does not supply the required conditional guarantee.
Closest comparator: Astra N417/N419 cover Gaussian samplers/reductions at distinct interfaces. Hafnian graph encodings and variable-subset GBS are prior art; this result does not claim the broad Gaussian/matching connection as new.
Open verification: Find a proved direct conditional sampler or a materially different resource contract; otherwise obtain expert review of both reduction directions and retain the capability barrier.
