Claim: For normalized initial and final nuclear states with J_i=3/2 and J_f=1/2, the stated one-body Gamow–Teller operator and reduced transition-density convention imply, for every bounded radial test function |h(r)|≤1, |M[h]|≤√((16/3)N_iZ_f), hence ∫_0^∞4πr²|ρ(r)|dr≤√((16/3)N_iZ_f). For 71Ga→71Ge with N_i=40 and Z_f=32, the source gives 82.6236447191. It also reports the pointwise relaxation |ρ(r)|²≤(16/3)p(r)n(r) and its associated weighted L² constraint. Under its conditional profile normalization, it reports that modified double- and triple-Gaussian fits exceed the bound; the single- and ordinary double-Gaussian fits do not.
Interface: The bound assumes normalized nuclear states, a one-body spin-isospin current with g_A factored out, the specified reduced-matrix-element normalization, and the 3/2→1/2 spin transition. It does not include effective multi-nucleon axial currents.
Proof spine: Removing a proton from the final state and a neutron from the initial state produces vectors in the same residual-nucleus space. Particle-number norms and Wigner–Eckart give a finite spin-coupling matrix whose reported KK† spectrum is {4/3,4/3,4/3,0}; Cauchy–Schwarz yields the coefficient. The source also derives a local density inequality and weighted norm constraint.
