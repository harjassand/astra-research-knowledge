Task and interface. For a finite positive frustration-free H=sum_j L_j*L_j with isolated zero-energy ground vector psi, Z=<psi,psi>, Hermitian local perturbation V and b=(V-<V>)psi, the response convention is chi=<b,H^+b>/Z and energy second derivative -2chi. The tested inputs are an exactly specified rational AKLT parent Hamiltonian and analytic exact MPS; neither a fitted material Hamiltonian nor certified approximate ground MPS was acquired. An exact feasible dual automatically verifies b is orthogonal to the whole kernel of H. Centering only against a selected psi does not suffice for a degenerate kernel.

Known variational identity. If sum_j L_j* w_j=b, then for any x, lower=(2 Re<b,x>-<x,Hx>)/Z and upper=sum_j ||w_j||^2/Z satisfy lower<=chi<=upper and upper-lower=sum_j ||w_j-L_jx||^2/Z. This is standard quadratic/Fenchel/Hylleraas/Thomson duality, not a newly discovered abstract dual principle. A small infeasible dual residual cannot by itself certify an upper bound: a component delta in a mode of eigenvalue epsilon can add delta^2/epsilon. Return UNKNOWN without exact completion or an independently acquired coercivity bound.

Conditional compact operation. Fix local dimension d, MPS bond dimension D, row support width R, dictionary size s and rational encoded tensors/operators. Require exact frustration-free checks L_j psi=0, Z>0 and a local Hermitian V for the physical curvature interpretation. For each physical row j, choose finite operators O_(j,a) whose support interval contains supp(L_j*L_j), and set w_j=sum_a c_(j,a)L_j O_(j,a)psi. Let columns of A be a_(j,a)=L_j*L_j O_(j,a)psi. Compute transfer-contracted K=A*A, g=A*b, h=<b,b> and physical row metric M_(j,a),(k,b)=delta_(j,k)<L_j O_(j,a)psi,L_j O_(j,b)psi>. Solve Kc0=g and rho=h-g*c0=||b-Ac0||^2. If rho!=0, this dictionary fails and the operation returns UNKNOWN. If rho=0, minimize c*Mc over c=c0+Tz, where columns of T span ker K, by the exact reduced equation (T*MT)z=-T*Mc0, after exact row-dependency removal or quotient. The resulting c, transfer contractions, equality Ac=b and norm c*Mc/Z provide an exactly checkable upper witness. M is neither K nor the identity: overlapping dictionary placements on the SAME physical bond have cross terms and must be merged before charging the bond norm. An initial numerical probe split those physical rows and spuriously halved the norm; all reported exact uppers use corrected M.

Locality and cost scope. Frustration freedom and disjoint supports make K block banded: if supp(L_i*L_i) is disjoint from supp O_(j,b), then (L_i*L_i)(L_j*L_j)O_(j,b)psi=0. Similarly g has source-near support, while M is block diagonal by physical rows. No gap, exponential clustering or inverse transfer is needed for these zero identities. Coefficients and null vectors can nevertheless extend across the chain. Exact MPS contractions and rational linear algebra have polynomial bit cost in N and input bit length for fixed d,D,R,s; the dense coefficient solve is polynomial, not claimed linear time. Exponential dependence on growing R or D, exact input acquisition, conditioning in practical arithmetic and ground-state verification remain costs. This does not prove generic feasibility, useful precision or performance superiority.
