N586-conditional-amplifier-entropy-photon-number-strengthening-counterexample | A finite-energy Gaussian reference refutes a conditional amplifier entropy-photon-number strengthening
status=source_derived_unreviewed; Source-reported interval-checked counterexample to a proposed conditional strengthening only. The reported five scoped replay checks were not rerun during intake. It neither promotes nor invalidates the separate unconditioned amplifier proof candidate.
Symbols and resource contracts LOCAL to this card.

Claim: Let g(x)=(x+1)ln(x+1)-x ln x and N(X|R)=g^{-1}(S(X|R)) when the conditional entropy is nonnegative. Prepare R,A by a two-mode squeeze of parameter 1/4 applied to tau_{1/2} tensor tau_{1/2}; take an independent vacuum B and apply a one-mode quantum-limited amplifier of gain G=6/5 to A,B. The source reports outward interval bounds N(A|R)>0.3895 and N(C|R)<0.6607, whereas the proposed conditional extension would require N(C|R)>=(6/5)N(A|R)+1/5>0.6674. Both conditional entropies are positive. Thus this finite-energy product-with-vacuum example refutes that reference-conditioned strengthening. The A and B input marginals remain independent and the unconditioned candidate inequality is saturated by the thermal marginals.
Interface: One mode in each amplifier port; ancillary input B is vacuum and independent of the reference-correlated A. The reference R is correlated with A by a two-mode squeezed thermal state. Vacuum covariance is 1/2 and entropies use natural logarithms. Only the proposed replacement S(X)->S(X|R) followed by g^{-1} is refuted; the independent-input unconditional theorem has no shared-reference premise.
Proof spine: The RA covariance has blocks aI,aI,cZ with a=cosh(1/2), c=sinh(1/2). Under gain 6/5, the output covariance blocks determine two symplectic eigenvalues. The source encloses S(A|R) and S(C|R) with outward intervals, then uses monotonicity of g to establish inverse bounds separated by more than 0.0067. The retained receipt reports an interval check; intake did not run the supplied mpmath code.
Costs: No algorithm or acquisition cost is claimed. The certificate is for one fixed two-mode Gaussian input family and one amplifier gain; finite-energy preparation is a mathematical input specification, not a demonstrated physical experiment.
Failure: This counterexample invalidates only the proposed reference-conditioned amplifier photon-number inequality. It does not contradict the unconditioned independent-input amplifier candidate N557, which is saturated here at the marginals; nor does it refute the weaker conditional entropy power inequality. It gives no thermal-channel capacity bound.
Closest comparator: The source identifies the established conditional entropy power inequality as compatible with these data and of a different, weaker exponential-entropy form. Existing N15 concerns a different reference-conditioned beam-splitter photon-entropy counterexample; it is related context, not the same theorem or witness.
