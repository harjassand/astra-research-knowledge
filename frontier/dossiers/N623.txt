The identity of late events changed under the fixed 200-ms guard and threshold sensitivities; counts across the six fixed settings ranged from zero to two of 16. The report therefore does not identify a robust excess of delayed post-pulse activation over ordinary recovery. This is a source-reported negative analysis, not evidence that mechanical priming is absent.
Interface: The observable is first sustained autonomous rotation during the OFF interval, not stator attachment or occupancy. The report uses selected 4-kHz long-axis/PCA angles, cell-level discovery/validation separation, a fixed Savitzky–Golay derivative, thresholds, persistence rule and edge guards. The 44 traces cover 43 unique cells; one cell appears under both forcing directions within the validation grouping, but no cell crosses the discovery/validation split.
Proof spine: The candidate distinguishes instantaneous activation from a latent mechanically prepared state by comparing first sustained autonomous-motion onset during OFF intervals. Extraction rules were frozen before validation outcomes were parsed, with a documented pre-validation correction. The reported late-event identities are guard/threshold sensitive and ordinary unforced recovery is sparse, so the source closes the candidate as unidentified rather than claiming a mechanism.
