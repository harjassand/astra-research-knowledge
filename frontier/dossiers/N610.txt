Symbols and resource contracts LOCAL to this card.

Claim: The source states an exact sampler for the joint stationary law of any fixed finite batch of raw coordinates at specified times in a finite-depth, finite-field network whose same-level update is affine, whose deterministic forcing depends only on strictly lower levels, whose parent count is at most k, and whose site-time category has Haar-reset probability s>0. If m coordinates are requested, c is the fixed depth, h=kc^2, and Q is the number of distinct update categories exposed, its bound is E[Q] <= (m/s) sum_{j=0}^{c-1}(1+h/s)^j. The category oracle is shared across levels so reset/gate correlations are preserved. The source also gives an exponential backward-depth tail for fixed c,m,k,s. These are source-reported theorem claims, not independently reconstructed here.
Interface: States lie in F_q^c. At a site-time one shared category chooses independent uniform Haar values for all coordinates with probability s, a locally known residual reset with probability r, or a gate with probability 1-s-r. At level ell the gate is linear in level-ell parents plus a deterministic function of strictly lower-level inputs; same-level nonlinear feedback is excluded. Parent acquisition, forcing descriptions and their evaluation are charged. Rational s,r support ideal exact random-bit draws; the reference implementation covers prime q, while the note states a mathematical finite-field generalization.
Proof spine: Process same-level dependencies in descending time while tracking coefficient vectors and the span V generated by independent Haar contributions. If a coefficient lies in V, an independent uniform vector U_V absorbs any unknown contribution in V in distribution (U_V+vZ has law U_V); this is distributional masking, not pathwise coalescence. Otherwise expose the category, propagate affine coefficients, retain lower-layer forcing terms without expanding them, and recurse on lower levels using the same memoized category field. A layer induction yields exact joint laws; negative-binomial domination gives the displayed expected-query bound.
Costs: At most cQ site-layer visits and kcQ parent incidences are reported. For fixed c the source states expected arithmetic/bit cost polynomial in m,k,1/s,log q and the acquired forcing-description/evaluation costs; its exponent and constants may grow sharply with c. The source reports a failed costly million-site run at k=5,s=.09 and a completed source-reported run at k=3,s=.20. These demonstrations were not rerun by intake.
Failure: No same-level nonlinear feedback, unbounded parent access, arbitrary nonlinear cellular automaton, full-state efficient sampling, or general full-chain O(log N) mixing theorem is established. Fixed-depth is essential to the recursive bound; low reset probability and larger depth can be practically prohibitive. Exactness assumes ideal independent randomness and does not follow from the source's pseudorandom statistical checks.
Closest comparator: The source cites coupling from the past, perfect sampling for probabilistic cellular automata, algebraic decomposition of nonlinear cellular automata, and noisy-CA ergodicity results as overlapping prior art. The exact novelty of this rank-absorbing layered construction is explicitly unverified; citations were not independently checked.
Open verification: Independently reconstruct the adaptive memoized-category and cross-level conditional-independence arguments, verify the negative-binomial and exponential-tail bounds including forcing acquisition costs, and perform a full prior-art review before treating this as a theorem or novelty claim.
