3. CANDIDATE CONNECTION: a representation or conceptual analogy suggests a bridge but its lemma is unproved. Preserve the missing lemma and a decisive falsifier. Shared topics, symbols or proof patterns never establish applicability.
4. INCOMPATIBLE ASSUMPTIONS: the proposed application lacks a hypothesis or changes the legal interface. State exactly what fails and whether an additional lemma could repair it. This is not a refutation of either source theorem.
