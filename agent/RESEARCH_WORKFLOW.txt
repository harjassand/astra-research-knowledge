4. DECISIVE PROOF. Open the source proof or derivation bearing on the blocker. Record assumptions, quantifiers, interface, and the exact proof span. Reconstruct the relevant steps in a linked artifact; mark incomplete steps and unknowns explicitly. Then state the connection obligation: what remains to connect this lemma/representation to the task’s requested conclusion.
5. BOUNDED CHECK. Choose one check that can change the next decision. Prestate the falsifier, comparator with equal information, input range, and cost accounting. Record command/method, environment, output, scope, and limitations. Finite checks, hashes, compilers, or another model’s agreement establish only their tested scope; shared context is not independent review.
6. DURABLE HANDOFF. For shared publication use state/CONTINUITY.md and a branch checkpoint linked to the session/bridge artifacts. Append a session record and any bridge record with source hashes, evidence references, inherited/acquired split, representation, blocker, comparator, next experiment, and unresolved items. Status is only `read`, `reconstructed`, or `reviewed` as a work-progress label; add a note and evidence refs. It never changes a claim’s scientific status or promotes evidence. Preserve failures and supersede by linking a new record, not editing the old one.

Static workspace study/retrieval can improve an answer to this task. It is not model training, persistent model-weight change, or proof of a fixed-weight capability. A reusable representation claim needs a held-out transfer test and full acquisition/execution cost accounting; record that test as future work unless it was actually performed.
