A significant result from the gauge work: we found and independently checked a counterexample to a proposed route for giving a lattice gauge model a positive physical interpretation. The calculation shows that one specific, finite, two-dimensional overlap model fails a required positivity test, even after the gauge constraint is accounted for. That rules out that particular shortcut. It does not show that the four-dimensional chiral theory fails, or that its continuum limit is impossible. I’m checking the result against prior research and looking for a construction that avoids the obstruction.
