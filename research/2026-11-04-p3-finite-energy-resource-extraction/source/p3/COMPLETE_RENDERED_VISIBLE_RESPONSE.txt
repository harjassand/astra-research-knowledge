7. What has been established

The completed construction connects four obligations:

Retention: a physical local filter preserves a quantitative witness while earning the tail needed downstream.
Acquisition: bounded observations certify the actual qubit resource and retain its control configuration for use.
Physical control: a smooth full-Rabi pulse can be chosen from the herald record, with explicit approximation error and duration.
Consumption: a finite local circuit converts the certified output into a fixed-quality Bell pair and a teleportation channel.

Astra’s N1580 machinery sharpened the treatment of actual admitted tails, complete apparatus exposure and physical compatibility. Its hardware claims were not imported into this construction. The package records the exact sources and their scope.

The remaining limits are material. The worst-case copy bounds are large, the conservative physical pulses can be slow, and fixed hardware noise can defeat arbitrarily weak-source extraction. Pump work and wall time for an unspecified source are not bounded by a source-attempt count. Historical priority for the complete theorem has not been established exhaustively.

The earlier PPT optical resource remains outside this distillation theorem. The three-versus-two representation theorem, the local-classical implementation boundary, and N1201’s charge-sector versus fully local scopes remain unchanged. No efficient-classical-simulation separation or computational speedup follows.

The substantive change is that the resource question now has a finite operational answer for an infinite-dimensional source class. A useful quantum channel can be obtained without first obtaining a uniformly accurate model of the source state, and the physical operations, observations, failures and precision costs needed for that answer are explicit.
