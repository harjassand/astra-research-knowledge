## 2. The mathematical mechanism: preserve a witness while changing the tail

The useful object is a **lower-bounded witness with a controlled change under energy filtering**.

Choose a unit vector \(v\) such that \(v^\dagger T_\rho v\le-\mu\). The corresponding quadratic operator \(H\) satisfies

\[
H\ge-\tfrac12 I.
\]

Introduce

\[
F_s=e^{-sN/2},
\qquad
G_s=\sqrt{I-F_s^2},
\]

and examine the remainder

\[
R_s=H-F_sHF_s-G_sHG_s.
\]

For this witness,

\[
\boxed{\|R_s\|\le s.}
\]

This estimate is the central retention calculation. The pair terms \(ab\) and \(a^\dagger b^\dagger\) change total photon number by only two. Their matrix elements grow linearly with photon number, while the filter’s mismatch across that two-photon step decreases sufficiently quickly to leave a bounded remainder.

Consequently,

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(HF_s\rho F_s)
\le
-\mu+s(E/2+1).
\]

Taking

\[
s=\frac{\mu}{E+2}
\]

gives a successful filtering probability \(p_s\ge1/2\), and the accepted state retains a witness gap at least \(\mu/2\).

The same operation earns the higher moments needed by the receiver. A final audit strengthened this part substantially by using the already-promised mean energy. Exponential weighting decreases the mean, and

\[
N^2e^{-sN}\le \frac{N}{es}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\begin{aligned}
M
&:=\left\langle(N+2)^2\right\rangle_{\rm accepted}\\
&\le 4+4E+\frac{2E}{es}\\
&\le \boxed{\frac{(E+2)^2}{\mu}}.
\end{aligned}
\]

This holds when the original \(\langle N^2\rangle\) is infinite. It concerns the **actual accepted state**; no inference from small trace distance to a finite moment is used.

The argument has a more general form. For an energy operator \(K\ge0\), a semibounded witness \(H\), and a bounded remainder

\[
\left\|H-e^{-sK/2}He^{-sK/2}
-\sqrt{I-e^{-sK}}\,H\,\sqrt{I-e^{-sK}}\right\|
\le Cs,
\]

finite mean energy controls both witness loss and the accepted higher moments. In particular, for \(q>1\),

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(K^q\rho_s)
\le
\frac{E}{p_s}
\left(\frac{q-1}{es}\right)^{q-1}.
\]

The remainder hypothesis is substantive. The package proves a sufficient condition based on finite energy transitions and also preserves counterexamples outside that condition.

## 3. From the retained moment to a fixed-quality quantum resource

\[

The diagonal errors help the negative Rayleigh quotient. The off-diagonal remainder is bounded using positivity of the original Fock-space matrix elements. No assumption that the output is an X state is required.

## 5. A separation between extracting a resource and approximating its state

The strongest existing theory changes how this result should be described.

Gaussian distillability and Gaussian extremality already imply a positive asymptotic distillation rate for the relevant covariance class. In particular, at a fixed entangled covariance, extremality already supplies a uniform positive asymptotic lower bound through the matched Gaussian state. The advance here is the explicit finite construction and acquired interface, rather than a new qualitative distillability criterion. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="6"}

Nevertheless, the natural state-approximation route has a precise obstruction.

Let \(\sigma\) be a pure two-mode squeezed state with squeezing parameter \(\lambda=1/2\). Let \(\tau_t\) be \(\sigma\) with independent Gaussian displacement noise adding \(tI\) to its covariance. Define

\[
d=\frac1{12},
\qquad
\rho_L=(1-d/L)\sigma+(d/L)\tau_L.
\]

Every member has exactly the same covariance, with

\[
E=\frac56,\qquad
T=
\begin{pmatrix}
5/12&2/3\\
2/3&5/12
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\mu=\frac14.
\]

Their covariance-matched Gaussian state is the same \(\tau_d\). Yet

\[
\|\rho_L-\sigma\|_1\le\frac1{6L}.
\]

For a standard passive \(m\)-copy central-limit Gaussifier, which fixes \(\sigma^{\otimes m}\) to \(\sigma\), contractivity gives

\[
\boxed{
\|\mathcal G_m(\rho_L^{\otimes m})-\tau_d\|_1
\ge
\frac{82}{185}-\frac{m}{6L}.
}
\]

Achieving trace-norm error \(1/10\) therefore requires

\[
m\ge\frac{381}{185}L.
\]

There is **no uniform copy count depending only on that exact covariance, \(E\), and \(\mu\)** for this Gaussian approximation.

In contrast, the extraction protocol uses the same setting \(s=3/34\) throughout the family and has the uniform reference seed bound

\[
\nu_0\ge\frac3{9248}.
\]

Its finite extraction bounds do not depend on \(L\).

This establishes a separation between two scientific objectives: producing a useful resource and reproducing a chosen state model. It is compatible with strong quantum central-limit convergence results, whose quantitative rates use higher-moment conditions. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="7"} It is not a lower bound against every Gaussian-based distillation method; filtering before Gaussianization is a legitimate competitor.
