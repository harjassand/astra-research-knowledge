REFERENCE-PRESERVING KINETIC PULSES AND FINITE ENTROPY INFERENCE
2026-10-31. Derived theorem and finite acquisition contract.

1. Target, interface and organizing construction

Let Q be an unknown finite irreducible all-even continuous-time Markov
generator, with stationary law pi and reciprocal edge support. Write
a_ij=pi_i q_ij. The target is the ZERO-FIELD STATIONARY MEAN entropy
production rate
 sigma_0=sum_{i<j} (a_ij-a_ji) log(a_ij/a_ji).
It is not realised entropy on the pulse, mean jump activity, maximum
escape rate, controlled-law identification or the entire device heat.
Stationary preparation and deterministic state labels are supplied.
The formula is the conventional Markov path-irreversibility rate. Its
physical entropy interpretation requires the appropriate all-even
time reversal and local detailed balance; unresolved physical channels
can add dissipation rather than being identified by this measurement.

A prescribed kinetic pulse has q^m_ij(t)=m_ij(t)q_ij off diagonal,
m_ij(t)=m_ji(t)>=0, and repaired diagonals. Its known dimensionless
amplitude envelope is m_ij(t)<=M(t). Define B=integral_0^T M(t)dt>0.
Q, pi, state count, mixing time and an escape-rate cap are not inputs.
The physical time T and amplitude envelope are inputs. Uniform
observed-label cut gating is one possible implementation, not an
operation whose microscopic qualification follows from two bits.

Construct a VIRTUAL reversible generator R by
 pi_i R_ij=sqrt(a_ij a_ji), i!=j,
and repair its diagonal. It is reversible with stationary law pi. Every
symmetric pulse R^m remains stationary at pi, even for a time-varying
prescribed symmetric protocol. This is the organizing fact: the
comparison law retains its initial measure during the full pulse.
R need never be fitted, implemented or observed. The geometric-mean
reversiblization and its fixed-pi information projection are established
tools, credited in Section 7. The finite-pulse use is the result derived
here; inventing this generator is not a contribution claimed here.

2. Exact path relative entropy, including waiting terms

For two finite CTMCs started with the same pi, the log path-density ratio
is the sum of log jump-rate ratios minus the integral of escape-rate
differences. Under the reference R^m, the expected number of i->j jumps
per dt is pi_i m_ij R_ij dt. Thus
 D(P_(R^m)^pi || P_(Q^m)^pi)
 = integral sum_{i!=j} pi_i [m_ij R_ij log(R_ij/q_ij)
                              -m_ij R_ij+m_ij q_ij]dt.
For a reciprocal pair set a=a_ij, b=a_ji, c=sqrt(ab). Its logarithmic
terms cancel: c log(c/a)+c log(c/b)=0. The remaining pair contribution
is m_ij(a+b-2sqrt(ab)). In particular, with
 H=sum_{i<j}(sqrt(a_ij)-sqrt(a_ji))^2,
 D(P_(R^m)^pi || P_(Q^m)^pi) <= B H <= B sigma_0/4.             (1)
Rates multiplied by zero simply remove the edge from both path laws.

The second inequality follows pairwise from
 (a-b)log(a/b)>=4(sqrt(a)-sqrt(b))^2.
For u=sqrt(a/b)>=1 it is equivalent to
(u+1)log u>=2(u-1), obtained by integrating the derivative of log u
or from the atanh series; interchange a,b for u<1. The constant 4 is
sharp for this edge inequality near equilibrium. No optimality of the
entire observable lower bound is asserted.

There is also an exact information-projection interpretation. Among
generators reversible with this SAME pi, choose any symmetric edge flow
c>=0. Its reference-to-Q path-KL rate contribution is
c log(c^2/(ab))-2c+a+b. It is minimized at c=sqrt(ab), with value
a+b-2sqrt(ab). Thus R minimizes this reverse path-KL rate within that
fixed-pi reversible class. This does not solve a fit over all possible
hidden models or prove a globally sharp entropy minimum from the data.

3. Observable consequences

Let r be the initial probability of an observed bit equalling one, and
s its probability at time T under the real pulse. Under the virtual
reference, the terminal probability is still r. Data processing in (1)
gives the finite-amplitude, finite-time inequalities
 sigma_0 >= (4/B) kl(r || s) >= (8/B)(r-s)^2.                  (2)
The direction kl(r||s) matters. Arbitrarily fast hidden transitions do
not enter this bound. Its stationary reference avoids a propagated
density-ratio estimate or a Taylor remainder at time zero.

For one constant pulse, the reference endpoint pair q_(x,y) has both
marginals r, and q_11>=r^2. The latter follows since exp(TR^m) is a
positive self-adjoint operator in L^2(pi); its centered bit covariance
is nonnegative. Time-varying reversible generators need not commute,
so that additional covariance premise is not used for general pulses.

Let p=(p00,p01,p10,p11) be the real endpoint pair law, r=p10+p11.
For positive z,w,v with v>=1, the variational KL inequality gives
 D(q||p) >= log(zw)r+log(v)r^2-log Z,
 Z=p00+w p01+z p10+zwv p11.                                  (3)
For v=1 only the two-marginal property is required. Setting z=1,v=1
recovers the ordinary one-endpoint exponential witness. For a positive
rational Z0 and rational r0>=0, use
 -log Z>=1-log Z0-Z/Z0,
 r^2>=2 r0 r-r0^2.
The resulting witness is linear in p. Minimizing it over a probability
box intersected with the simplex needs only an exact four-variable
greedy linear program. Enclosing all logarithms outwards produces a
rational certificate. A floating optimizer may propose z,w,v; it does
not certify the inequality. Taking any maximum over witnesses after
seeing the counts is valid on a single simultaneous confidence set.

4. Finite observations and honest coverage

Each trial is a NEW INDEPENDENT stationary preparation of the same
source Q, followed by the prescribed pulse and two bit readouts. These
are not successive windows of one unreset hidden trajectory. For n
trials the four cell counts have binomial marginal laws. Construct
Clopper-Pearson intervals with each of eight one-sided tails alpha/8.
The union bound gives a probability box containing the full true p with
probability at least 1-alpha, without assuming within-pair independence.
The certified outer CP endpoints are also intersected with a Hoeffding
envelope known to contain the EXACT CP intervals. This removes only
numerical endpoint-bracketing slack, using no additional risk budget.
Intersect with the simplex and apply (3). Also compute the ordinary
marginal kl and paired Pinsker versions of (2) on this SAME set.
The maximum is still a lower confidence bound for sigma_0 with risk
at most alpha. It is a fixed-n result; there is no uncharged repeated
stopping or anytime guarantee.

Binomial inverse-beta floating values are only proposals. The retained
receiver brackets them using directed rational binomial tails, including
a positive tail recurrence, geometric remainder and integer/log
enclosures. This reuses a previously retained helper, but here iid
BINOMIAL CELL INDICATORS follow from the supplied independent trials.
No exact binomial dominance for one correlated hidden record is assumed.

Independent preparations are the acquisition change. The earlier
one-preparation dormant-state obstruction is preserved: an extended
model can imitate a fixed one-preparation record with probability near
w while its stationary mean entropy is w sigma_0. With n independent
starts this overlap factor is w^n. The theorem makes no claim of a
uniform finite sample complexity over unknown arbitrarily slow Q, or
of obtaining independent stationary starts from one unknown source.

6. Important negative controls and limitations

A reversible two-state chain started nonstationarily responds to a
symmetric pulse despite sigma_0=0. Likewise an asymmetric energy or
source-clock pulse changes equilibrium occupation even from stationarity.
Either would invalidate (2) if its missing premise were ignored.
Independence, stationarity, reciprocal symmetry, actuator envelope and
timing must be supplied or independently qualified. A successful fit to
these endpoint data does not qualify them.

The fixture is a driven three-state ring, clockwise 10 and reverse 1,
pi uniform, bit one only at state 0. Its complete stationary PASSIVE
binary path is reversible: visits to the singleton open state regenerate
the path; open and closed sojourns form independent alternating renewal
sequences. Thus a passive time-reversal KL witness is zero. This does
not say its entire dwell-time law has a reversible hidden realization.
At our single passive endpoint horizon the covariance is positive, so
a reversible two-state chain matches that endpoint pair exactly. An
incumbent receiving complete continuous dwell times has a richer
interface and may infer additional hidden dissipation; it is not ruled
out by this pair-only comparison.

The finite source control uses m=16 on both directions of every
observed cut edge, with within-label rates unchanged, T=1/40 and B=2/5.
The source entropy is 9 log(10), about 20.7233 nats/model-time. The
matched equilibrium null uses both ring directions 11/2, so it shares
the baseline escape rate 11, pi, labels, pulse, duration and budget.
Its stationary mean entropy is zero and its pulse endpoint mean stays
1/3. Exact synthetic acquisition and cost receipts are separate files.
The sampler's known source matrix and its uniformization cap are NOT

7. Strong conventional comparators and claim status

The relative-entropy response method is conventional: Dechant and Sasa,
Fluctuation-response inequality out of equilibrium,
https://arxiv.org/html/1804.08250 , develops nonlinear response via KL
and path changes. We give the full waiting-term calculation above so
the particular direction of KL and finite pulse scope are checkable.

The reference generator itself, including its reverse-KL projection
property, is prior mathematics. Choi and Wolfer, Systematic approaches
to generate reversiblizations of Markov chains (2023),
https://arxiv.org/html/2303.03650 , Section 2 Eq. (2.4) and Section 3.7,
identify the geometric mean P_0 and its fixed-pi KL projection. Their
Section 3.7 explicitly recalls the earlier Diaconis-Miclo result. The
edge proof in Section 2 above reproduces this tool; it is not a claim
to have discovered geometric reversiblization or information projection.

Kinetic dissipation-response bounds also predate this work. The general
response thermodynamic uncertainty relation is proved in Aslyamov,
Ptaszynski and Esposito, Nonequilibrium Fluctuation-Response Relations:
From Identities to Bounds (2025), https://arxiv.org/html/2410.17140 .
The 2024 precursor https://arxiv.org/html/2406.08361 conjectures the
general relation and proves special cases. These concern steady current
precision and static linear response. We do not claim the broad idea
of using kinetic response to bound dissipation as a new discovery.

The current search also checked Zheng and Lu (2026), Spectral Duality
and Thermodynamic Bounds on Finite-frequency Fluctuation Responses,
https://arxiv.org/html/2602.18631v2 , setup and Eqs. (12)-(20). It treats
infinitesimal time-dependent probes, includes state occupation observables,
and obtains dissipation constraints from their spectral responses. Thus
time-dependent kinetic entropy inference and occupation observables are
also not generally new. The specific finite-amplitude endpoint contract
here is distinguished by its proof and resources, not by dismissing
this stronger recent conventional work.

Gu (2026), Spectral Fluctuation-Dissipation-Response Inequalities,
https://arxiv.org/html/2604.20362 , setup and Eqs. (1)-(3), compares
linear energetic susceptibility with a passive reference and includes
kinetic and relaxation prefactors. It was read as a related source;
its full proofs were not independently audited or imported here.

Clopper-Pearson, Hoeffding, KL variational duality, Pinsker and the
four-cell linear optimization are standard incumbents/shared methods.
They receive the same records and risks. No computational or sampling
advantage over an equally informed implementation of this same
information inequality is claimed. The candidate contribution is the
specific reference-preserving finite-pulse use and its explicit
hidden-rank/cap-free finite experiment contract, subject to a wider
novelty check. It does not make the reset or symmetric gate free.
