STATIC CONSERVATIVE CONTAMINATION AND THE AMPLITUDE BOUNDARY
Post-freeze mathematical extension, 2026-10-31. No new random records.

1. A useful extension of the same reference construction

Suppose the actual pulse is
 q^V_ij(t)=m_ij(t) q_ij exp[(V_i-V_j)/2],
where m is symmetric with known envelope M(t), and V is a STATIC
microscopic state potential with supplied dimensionless range <=epsilon.
The initial real state is still the zero-field stationary pi. V can
vary among hidden states; it need not be the observed bit. This covers
a specified conservative contamination model, not arbitrary asymmetric
actuator errors or an unknown change of cycle affinities.

Let R be the geometric-mean reversible generator from PROOF.txt. The
tilted reference R^V has stationary law
 rho_i=pi_i exp(-V_i)/Z, Z=sum_i pi_i exp(-V_i),
even while a prescribed symmetric m(t) changes with time. Start this
virtual reference at rho, rather than pi. Its pair edge flows are
 m_ij sqrt(a_ij a_ji) exp[-(V_i+V_j)/2]/Z.
The log jump-rate ratios relative to Q^V are still log(R_ij/q_ij),
so reciprocal logarithmic terms cancel. The path KL is now
 D(P_(R^V)^rho || P_(Q^V)^pi)
 =D(rho||pi)+integral sum_{i<j} m_ij exp[-(V_i+V_j)/2]/Z
                          *(sqrt(a_ij)-sqrt(a_ji))^2 dt
 <=D(rho||pi)+exp(epsilon)*B*sigma_0/4.                       (4)
The factor follows after shifting min(V)=0: Z>=exp(-epsilon)
and exp[-(V_i+V_j)/2]<=1. No escape cap enters.

To bound the initial cost define psi(t)=log E_pi exp(-tV).
 D(rho||pi)=psi'(1)-psi(1)=integral_0^1 t psi''(t) dt.
Since psi'' is a variance of a variable of range epsilon, it is at most
epsilon^2/4. Hence D(rho||pi)<=epsilon^2/8. This standard bounded-range
variance argument is given explicitly to fix the constant and direction.

If r is the initial real probability of bit one and u=rho(bit=1), then
 r/[r+(1-r)exp(epsilon)] <= u <=
 r exp(epsilon)/[1-r+r exp(epsilon)].
This follows by bounding the ratio of the conditional expectations of
exp(-V) in the two bit classes. Combining endpoint data processing with
(4), for real terminal probability s,
 sigma_0 >= 4/[B exp(epsilon)] *
        [ inf_{u in the above interval} kl(u||s)-epsilon^2/8 ]_+. (5)
For finite data use simultaneous confidence intervals for r and s from
the SAME four-cell set. The endpoints of the interval for u are monotone
in r, so an outward interval and a binary-KL rectangle minimum certify
(5). Directed rational exponential/log bounds suffice. There is no new
statistical failure budget: the same event containing the true four-cell
law controls all these deterministic conclusions. The physical epsilon
bound must actually hold; selecting a convenient value from the data
does not establish it.

If the potential is exactly V=h*bit, the sharper identities are
 u=r exp(-h)/(1-r+r exp(-h)), D(rho||pi)=kl(u||r).
One can use these in (4). The retained implementation uses only the
weaker range bound (5), and makes no optimality claim.

2. A small conservative error can defeat the pure-kinetic conclusion

Take the matched reversible three-state source, with baseline rates
11/2 in both ring directions and stationary uniform pi. Use m=16 on
the observed cut, T=1/40, and exp(V_open/2)=21/20, V_closed=0.
The potential range 2 log(21/20) is less than 1/10. The pulsed visible
chain is lumpable: open-to-closed rate is 176*(21/20), closed-to-open
is 88*(20/21). Its stationary open mean is 200/641, and its terminal
mean is
 200/641+(1/3-200/641) exp[-(176*(21/20)+88*(20/21))/40].
This is strictly less than 1/3. Ignoring the energy contamination
therefore gives a strictly positive population floor from (2), even
though sigma_0=0. Formula (5), with epsilon=1/10, correctly gives zero.
The exact control and the positive naive floor are in extensions.json.

3. An unknown amplitude envelope cannot be removed for free

Keep T=1/40 fixed. Scale the baseline driven-ring generator by s>0,
while the actual cut multiplier is 16/s. The controlled cut rates are
unchanged (160 and 16); only the within-closed pair rates scale with s.
All baselines are irreducible, have stationary uniform pi, and entropy
sigma_s=9s log(10). As s tends to zero, the controlled generator tends
to a fixed irreducible star K with nontrivial endpoint mean response.
The prescribed zero-field baseline itself is not held fixed here; it
is a family of possible unknown sources, as required for a uniform
inference counterexample.

Write the controlled generator K+sA, where A acts only on the closed
pair with exit rates 10 and 1. Extra-event coupling gives path TV at
most 1-exp(-10sT)<=10sT relative to K. The endpoint-pair TV is no
larger, and n independent preparations have TV at most 10nsT.
Thus for every fixed finite n, these response records can be arbitrarily
close while the zero-field mean entropy tends to zero. A lower band
honest over the class with no amplitude envelope has positive-threshold
power at the limiting response law at most alpha: apply honesty to
sigma_s below the threshold, then take s to zero in the TV bound.
This is a separate calibration obstruction, not a retraction of the
known-envelope theorem or of the prior one-preparation dormant-state
obstruction. Independent starts do not remove this amplitude issue.

The exact s=1/1000 fixture has baseline sigma about 0.02072. Treating
the actual multiplier 16000 as if it were bounded by 16 produces a
false positive rate floor above the true sigma; using its actual
envelope restores a valid smaller floor. No random data are drawn.

Likewise, without a physical time calibration, scaling the baseline by
s and the pulse duration by 1/s gives exactly the same endpoint laws
at the same dimensionless gate, while entropy per physical time scales
by s. This is consistent with N713's source-clock obstruction. The
present analysis does not acquire a clock from two-bit responses.

4. Scope of the practical opening

The extension can tolerate a quantified static conservative energy
shift while retaining freedom from a hidden rate cap, rank promise or
generator fit. The original supplied independent starts, amplitude/time
calibration and microscopic control qualification remain necessary.
All numerical outcomes are synthetic mathematical controls; these
