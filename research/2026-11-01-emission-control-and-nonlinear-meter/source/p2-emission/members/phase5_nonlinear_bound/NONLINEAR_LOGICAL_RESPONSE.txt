NONLINEAR LOGICAL RESPONSE AND THE NUMBER OF COMMUTATORS TO AN OUTLET
31 October 2026. Analytic qualification, reproducible checks, and limits.

MAIN RESULT

The norm of a common nonlinear gauge remainder is not the right quantity for
bounding logical information leakage. It can strongly change the gauge while
remaining identical on all coherent wells within the measured parity sector.
The appropriate objects are the actual branch-distinguishing perturbations,
their full common-Liouvillian correlation functions, and a separate localization
qualification of the physical coherent-well encoding.

A useful organising law is exact. Let O be a Hermitian nuisance observable and
O_j=(L_adjoint)^j O. If O_0,...,O_{r-1} commute with every physical Lindblad
outlet, then, away from stationary/singular spectral lines,

  S_O(omega) = omega^(-2r) S_{O_r}(omega).                 (1)

This is an exact stationary spectral identity, not a Gaussian approximation.
Under sufficient correlation differentiability and decay,

  S_O,sym(omega) ~ Gamma(O_r,O_r)/omega^(2r+2),
  Gamma(A,A)=sum_l Tr[rho [L_l,A]^dag [L_l,A]].             (2)

It counts the number of Hamiltonian commutators needed before a nuisance
observable reaches an irreversible outlet. For the full nonlinear cat meter,
storage-only parasitic Hamiltonians have r>=1: their leading bulk tail is
fourth order, with a coefficient determined by local storage moments. This
does not require the omitted cubic Hamiltonian to have a small total action.

Equation (1) does not supply a small finite-frequency gain unless the spectrum
on its right side is measured, solved, or bounded. Equation (2) does not remove
finite-window boundary excitation. A finite-time identity below exposes those
boundary terms and led to a concrete cycle-orientation proposal. The scalar
cancellation is proved, but the independent corrected-mean/Hessian joint test
found the proposed orientation alternation WORSE. That failure is preserved
below; the organising identities do not imply a gain from scalar closure alone.

1. EXACT STRONG SYMMETRY, AND WHY LOCALIZATION IS STILL REQUIRED

Take the full six-mode Hamiltonian, without dropping nonlinear terms,

 H0=sum_j g2[(a_j^2-alpha^2)b_j^dag+h.c.]
       +g3(a1 a2 a3+h.c.),
 L_j=sqrt(gamma)b_j; optional heating outlets sqrt(h)b_j^dag.

Every even product of physical storage parities commutes with H0 and all these
outlets. The even-flip group has four elements. Any two coherent product wells
with the same S=z1 z2 z3 are related by one of these physical unitaries.
Consequently their entire emitted-field reduced states are exactly equal at
zero branch-distinguishing perturbation, for all finite times. This includes
arbitrary common nonlinear gauge dynamics and all emission/no-jump histories.
The claim follows directly by commuting the physical symmetry through a common
unitary system-field dilation and tracing the storage.

This is not, by itself, a noiseless logical subsystem theorem. A symmetry-
preserving evolution can tunnel between wells, cause logical bit errors, or
reveal information in the conjugate basis. Equal environment marginals on a
set of classical basis inputs do not certify arbitrary superpositions.

A quantitative finite-domain localization construction is available. Let each
well retain fluctuation Fock levels 0,...,N about a common center A. Opposite-
well overlap is the operator

  M_N=P_N D(-2A) P_N,   sigma_N=||M_N||.

Parity factors in the local basis do not affect its singular values. Within a
fixed three-cat parity sector, distinct wells differ on two modes. The Gram
matrix of the four-well embedding W therefore satisfies

  ||W^dag W-I|| <= 3 sigma_N^2 = epsilon_N.               (3)

For all eight wells the row-sum bound is (1+sigma_N)^3-1. If epsilon_N<1,
V=W(W^dag W)^(-1/2) is an isometry, and

  ||W-V|| <= 1-sqrt(1-epsilon_N).

At |A|=4 and N=3, sigma_N=5.03501e-10 and the fixed-parity Gram defect is at
most 7.60540e-19. At N=8 these are 3.36457e-5 and 3.39610e-9. These are exact
finite-domain overlap calculations, not estimates of the probability of
leaving that domain. If the true branch state has tail probability tau beyond
the admitted fluctuation domain, truncating a purification costs sqrt(tau) in
vector norm. A coherent four-branch superposition can cost up to 2sqrt(tau)
by Cauchy-Schwarz, in addition to the Gram/polar-isometry defects. This tail
must be bounded from the true evolution, a verified truncation theorem, or a
physical moment envelope. A small last retained population or cutoff
convergence alone is not a rigorous omitted-tail bound.

The isometry is a mathematical decoder for qualifying the representation.
It is not a supplied perfect physical recovery or detector.

2. EXACT LOGICAL INFLUENCE EQUATION

Inside a localized sector, write branch coordinates a_j=z_j(alpha+c_j).
For fixed S, every term

 g2 sum_j[c_j^2 b_j^dag+h.c.],   g3 S[c1 c2 c3+h.c.]

is common to all four branches and can be retained nonperturbatively in the
common gauge generator L_s. Let V_z(t) denote the actual lower-weight drives
and the explicitly branch-dependent control contamination. For example,

 V_z=sum_R epsilon_R z_R[e^(-i theta_R(t)) product_(j in R)(alpha+c_j)+h.c.]
       +sum_j epsilon_odd z_j theta_dot_j(t) n_j.

The exact cross-density operator obeys

 dot sigma_zz'=L_s sigma_zz' -i V_z sigma_zz' +i sigma_zz' V_z'.  (4)

Its trace is the full gauge-plus-emission overlap. Common nonlinear evolution
drops out of the trace derivative:

 d Tr(sigma_zz')/dt = -i Tr[(V_z-V_z')sigma_zz'].

It still affects the state and susceptibility, so it cannot be omitted from
the calculation. The useful perturbation parameter is the nuisance coupling,
not the norm or integrated action of the common cubic remainder.

A direct non-Gaussian response hierarchy follows. Put V_z=lambda Vbar_z and
expand sigma=sum_n lambda^n sigma_n. Then

 dot sigma_0=L_s sigma_0,
 dot sigma_n=L_s sigma_n-i Vbar_z sigma_(n-1)
                             +i sigma_(n-1) Vbar_z'.      (5)

This retains all nonlinear common dynamics. At second order it computes the
irreversible response using genuine common-state two-time correlations. A
large bare Duhamel norm of the common nonlinear Hamiltonian does not appear.
Finite nuisance strength still requires control of the higher terms or an
actual nonlinear calculation; second order alone is not an all-orders bound.

To retain records, apply the same hierarchy to the physical record generator.
For photon counting, replace each observed recycling term L rho L^dag by
e^(i lambda_record) L rho L^dag. For an integrated homodyne record of phase phi,
weight w(t), and efficiency eta, add

 i lambda_record w sqrt(eta)[e^(-i phi)L rho+e^(i phi)rho L^dag]
                 -(lambda_record*w)^2 rho/2

to the characteristic-operator generator. Other outputs remain in L_s. This
retains both reported-record distributions and off-diagonal logical weights.
Equation (5) applied only at zero record tilt gives an unconditional logical
channel; it does not certify every conditional/no-click instrument branch.

3. STATIONARY SPECTRAL IDENTITY AND PROOF

Let A=L_adjoint be a time-independent Heisenberg Lindblad generator with a
stationary state rho. Use <X,Y>_rho=Tr(rho X^dag Y). Operators below must be in
the appropriate common domain; their listed moments must be finite. Remove
stationary components when discussing ordinary decaying spectra. Define

 R_O(omega)=(i omega-A)^(-1)O,
 S_O(omega)=2 Re<O,R_O(omega)>_rho.

This is one ordered two-sided spectrum for Hermitian O; averaging the two
frequency orderings gives the symmetrized spectrum. At regular nonzero
frequencies stationarity and the Lindblad product rule give

 S_O(omega)=sum_l ||[L_l,R_O(omega)]||_rho^2.              (6)

Indeed, O=(i omega-A)R and
2 Re<R,AR>_rho=-sum_l ||[L_l,R]||_rho^2.

The resolvent expansion through r terms is

 R_O=sum_(j=0)^(r-1) O_j/(i omega)^(j+1)
                         +R_(O_r)/(i omega)^r.

If [L_l,O_j]=0 for j<r, its first r terms disappear upon commuting with every
outlet. Substitution in (6) proves (1) exactly. The same statement holds for
the full matrix of cross spectra of several nuisance quadratures with the
same depth. Complex modulation therefore requires that matrix, including both
sidebands, rather than a favorable scalar response.

For a regularized resolvent (eta+i omega-A)^(-1), equation (6) has the additional
positive term 2eta||R||_rho^2. Nondecaying spectral lines cannot be discarded
by silently taking eta=0. Stationary zero-frequency delta weight is invisible
to the differentiated observable and must be treated separately.

4. HIGH-FREQUENCY COEFFICIENT, INCLUDING GENERAL DEPTH r

For Hermitian X,Y define B(X,Y)=Re<X,Y>_rho and the real bilinear Gamma by
polarization of Gamma(X,X)=sum_l||[L_l,X]||_rho^2. Stationarity gives

 B(X,AY)+B(AX,Y)=-Gamma(X,Y).

When [L_l,O_j]=0, Gamma(O_j,Y)=0 for every Y. Transfer A from the right to the
left r times. All lower odd moments vanish, and the first possible odd one is

 B(O,A^(2r+1)O)=(-1)^r B(O_r,AO_r)
              =(-1)^(r+1) Gamma(O_r,O_r)/2.               (7)

For C_sym(t)=B(O,e^(tA)O), repeated integration by parts in
S_sym(omega)=2 integral_0^infinity cos(omega t) C_sym(t)dt gives (2), with no
missing factor of two. Require sufficient differentiability, decay, and an
integrable remainder; an unbounded polynomial oscillator model is not exempt
from those requirements. Equation (1) remains preferable at finite frequency
because it keeps the remaining susceptibility and all resonances visible.

Passive examples with vacuum input and only the last mode damped are:

 directly damped probe mode:                 r=0, S~gamma/omega^2;
 storage--buffer coupling G:                 r=1, S~gamma G^2/omega^4;
 storage--intermediate--buffer, G1 and G2:    r=2, S~gamma G1^2 G2^2/omega^6.

These are susceptibility statements for a coherent Hamiltonian nuisance.
An intrinsic Markov jump on the logical storage is not thereby removed.

5. THE FULL NONLINEAR CAT COEFFICIENT

For any Hermitian storage-only O, [b_j,O]=[b_j^dag,O]=0, so
O1=i[H0,O]. The outlet commutator is exactly

 [sqrt(gamma)b_j,O1]=i sqrt(gamma) g2 [a_j^2,O].          (8)

The three-cat coupling, common self-Kerr, and other storage-only Hamiltonians
can change the finite-frequency spectrum, but do not enter this first outlet
commutator directly. For O_R=e^(-i phi) product_(j in R)a_j+h.c., with each mode
appearing once, the cold-buffer contribution is

 Gamma(O1,O1)=4 gamma g2^2 sum_(j in R)
                < n_j product_(k in R\{j})(n_k+1) >.     (9)

Buffer heating adds

 4 h g2^2 sum_(j in R)<(n_j+1) product_(k in R\{j})n_k>.

For proportional detuning contamination O=n_j, the corresponding coefficient
is 4g2^2[gamma<n_j(n_j-1)>+h<(n_j+1)(n_j+2)>]. These are local physical moments,
not norms of a discarded common Hamiltonian. A detuning error whose amplitude
itself grows with modulation frequency loses two powers of suppression when
converting this spectrum into its actual noise budget.

Direct storage loss sqrt(kappa1)a_j fails the initial commutation condition.
The actual Markov dissipator D[a_j] is invariant under a phase multiplier, so
the physical phase cycle does not suppress that intrinsic loss channel.

6. FINITE-TIME FILTER IDENTITY AND A LOCAL-MOMENT BOUND

The stationary law alone is insufficient. A stronger finite-time qualification
can be written directly in the full system-field dilation. For a real waveform
f(t), define F1(t)=integral_0^t f(s)ds and F2(t)=integral_0^t F1(s)ds. If

 F1(0)=F1(T)=F2(0)=F2(T)=0,

and O has [L_l,O]=0, quantum Ito calculus gives the exact operator identity

 integral_0^T f(t) j_t(O)dt
    =integral_0^T F2(t)j_t(O2)dt +integral_0^T F2(t)dM_(O1)(t). (10)

Here j_t includes the full emitted fields, O2=A O1, and M_(O1) is its quantum
martingale. No common Hamiltonian term has been dropped. Centering the drift
by its actual expectation, Cauchy-Schwarz and Ito isometry imply

 sqrt Var[integral f j_t(O)dt]
 <= integral |F2(t)| sqrt Var[j_t(O2)]dt
                  +sqrt[integral |F2(t)|^2 <Gamma(O1,O1)>_t dt]. (11)

For several quadratures, combine sum_mu F2_mu O2_mu inside the variance and
sum_mu F2_mu O1_mu inside Gamma. This can be much tighter than summing separate
norms. Equations (10)-(11) control the exact second-order nuisance response;
they are not an all-orders finite-amplitude logical-instrument theorem.

For a time-dependent common generator, use
O1(t)=A_t O and O2(t)=(partial_t+A_t)O1(t). At physical control discontinuities,
add sum_b F2(t_b)j_(t_b)[Delta O1_b] to (10) and its corresponding centered
norm contribution to (11). The shared CD miscalibration in the actual joint
model makes these terms relevant. They cannot be dropped.

If an endpoint primitive fails to close, its boundary operator remains.
The code supplies a concrete counterexample: drive a two-mode passive meter
with sin(omega t) over an integer number of cycles at fixed T. Its stationary
bulk term scales as omega^-4, but F2(T)=T/omega is nonzero and the complete
emitted-plus-ringdown distinguishability scales as omega^-2. For G=1,
gamma=10,T=8,omega=125.664, the actual total is approximately 1.019e-4 versus
the stationary bulk prediction 1.602e-7. Ringdown cannot undo emitted or final
receiver distinguishability; it only moves the latter into the field.

7. CONCRETE CYCLE-ORIENTATION PROPOSAL AND ITS EXACT STATUS

The existing sixteen-segment Eulerian-plus-reverse cycle has theta(T)=0 and
theta(t)=theta(T-t). Every admitted lower-weight parasitic phase has zero
average. Its first AND second primitives therefore close. This is stronger
than merely zero average force.

For proportional odd CD contamination f_j=theta_dot_j, however, F1_j=theta_j.
The current cycle has a nonzero integral of its unwrapped angle. For peak
detuning Delta and the actual A/B schedule,

 integral_cycle theta(t)dt=(64pi^2,-32pi^2,-32pi^2)/Delta.

Over sixteen same-orientation cycles at Delta=10, the CD second primitive ends
at approximately (1010.647,-505.324,-505.324), before multiplying the small
calibration-error coefficient. That boundary is not removed by theta(T)=0.

Alternate the orientation of consecutive COMPLETE closed cycles: theta_path,
then -theta_path. This preserves sum theta=0, the desired three-cat pump,
all existing durations, the peak detuning, and the number of rotations. With
an already even cycle count it adds no elapsed time. The second CD primitive
now closes every two-cycle pair. Its maximum magnitude over sixteen cycles
falls to approximately (63.1655,31.5827,31.5827). Ordinary parasitic first and
second primitives still close.

This is a physical waveform change, not a virtual relabeling of the record.
The scalar primitive identities are proved and numerically checked. A better
instrument does NOT follow automatically: common time-dependent gauge evolution,
finite-frequency resonances, branch-dependent calibration statistics, record
noise, and final gauge states must still be included.

8. RELATION TO THE ACTUAL LOGICAL INSTRUMENT

For weak nuisance amplitudes, the leading phase-stripped overlap satisfies

 -log|F_zz'| = (1/2) Var[ integral (V_z-V_z')_interaction(t)dt ]
                          +higher nuisance orders.      (12)

For a stationary reference and a long periodic drive this is the full
matrix-valued spectrum integrated against the real finite physical filter.
It includes irreversible within-parity information in every outlet. The
relevant spectral quantity is a quantum correlation function. In an active
nonequilibrium meter, a classical mean transfer function alone does not
determine it through an assumed equilibrium fluctuation-dissipation relation.

The hierarchy and finite-time identities establish the correct organising
quantity and a way to qualify it without small whole-gauge evolution error.
They do not yet certify the complete physical cat instrument. That requires:
the actual nonlinear finite-time correlations or response hierarchy; finite
nuisance remainder control; record-dependent statistics for parity assignment;
the coherent-well localization/tail qualification; and all loading, readout,
reset and elapsed storage-loss costs. These are specific remaining quantities,
not a claim that an ideal detector or outer recovery has been constructed.

9. PRIMARY CONTEXT AND MATHEMATICAL/PHYSICAL FREQUENCY WINDOW

Srivatsa and von Keyserlingk, "Operator growth hypothesis in open quantum
systems," Phys. Rev. B 109,125149 (2024), explicitly relate odd Lindbladian
moments to algebraic high-frequency spectral tails. Their generic omega^-2
discussion is direct precedent for the moment method. The commutator-depth
specialization and its application here are not claimed as literature-first.
https://journals.aps.org/prb/pdf/10.1103/PhysRevB.109.125149

Fang, Lu and Tong, "Mixing Time of Open Quantum Systems via Hypocoercivity,"
Phys. Rev. Lett. 134,140405 (2025), arXiv:2404.11503, separates Hamiltonian
transport and dissipative coercivity for mixing estimates. Its machinery may
help establish the remaining spectrum/gap bounds for an admitted model; its
conditions are not presumed satisfied by this nonlinear cat meter.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.11503

The adopted parity-meter interaction and noisy-computation baseline are from
Vuillot et al., "Quantum non-demolition measurements as a practical primitive
for fault-tolerant computation against biased noise," arXiv:2605.24262v1.
https://arxiv.org/html/2605.24262v1

A physical filtering window must lie above the actual relevant meter poles
and resonances but below breakdown of the Markov outlet, rotating-wave,
effective-Hamiltonian and control-bandwidth descriptions. A mathematical
omega->infinity statement alone does not demonstrate such a window. This
qualification is particularly important because the Lindblad tail itself
need not describe the arbitrarily high-frequency microscopic environment.

10. REPRODUCIBLE CHECKS AND CURRENT CONCLUSION

check_spectral_hierarchy.py verifies the exact resolvent/Dirichlet identities,
moment signs and factors on one-, two-, and three-spin non-Gaussian Lindblad
models with r=0,1,2. It independently checks passive oscillator chains, computes
the finite-window counterexample, displaced-Fock Gram bounds, and exact cycle
primitive closure. spectral_hierarchy_results.json preserves all outputs.

The organising law is established within its stated mathematical domain. It
explains why a common nonlinear remainder with integrated norm above seven
need not destroy the logical guarantee and identifies the actual nuisance
response that must be qualified. An equal-duration control proposal derived
from scalar closure was tested and rejected, strengthening the warning against
promoting filter moments to an instrument result. The nonlinear cat-device
improvement remains an
experimental/numerical qualification question; no full-device advantage or
new fault-tolerance threshold is inferred from these analytic checks.
