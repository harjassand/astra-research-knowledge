FAILED OR LIMITED MECHANISMS RETAINED
31 October 2026

1. Plain dual rail plus loss flags is not a new full construction.
Current publications already include universal gates, imperfect checks,
conditional partner dephasing, and delayed erasure decoding. The new
question must be more specific than rediscovering those components.

2. Calling lambda_max completion globally optimal is false.
It is optimal only among instantaneous positive loss additions that make
the hazard scalar on the entire stipulated manifold. Coherent echoes,
endpoint filters, gate redesign, and randomization can use less added loss.

3. Exact damped swaps with spectator attenuation fail under untreated
always-on chi. The saved simple implementation has conditional Choi
infidelity 0.337 and coupler leakage amplitude 0.738 at the illustrative
parameters. Turning chi off during swaps was a real resource assumption.

4. Small conditional infidelity does not prove a small accepted map in
trace/diamond norm. The raw illustrative SWS example has conditional Choi
infidelity 7.30e-6 but a pure-state trace-distance witness 0.00269.

5. A deterministic unknown-hazard lower bound is not a no-go theorem for
randomized computing. Fresh physical Pauli randomization of a
frame-independent channel produces O(p^2) stochastic nonidentity weight.
A lower bound conditioned on retaining the random key answers a stronger
question and cannot invalidate that computational improvement.

6. Two photons without a SAME-NUMBER symmetry check are insufficient for
a full accepted quantum-state guarantee. Differential loss generates a
Q amplitude of first order even though its code projection is scalar to
first order. Total-number sensing does not see this component.

7. Rotate live data to a simple number-parity readout, then undo, fails.
The temporary code has |20> and |02> distinguished by unequal losses.
No-jump filtering during the readout remains inside the restored code and
is first order. This fails with a perfect classical parity detector.

8. A direct controlled-BS symmetry map is not automatically a physical
primitive. Standard BS plus fixed dispersive hardware need not implement
the desired product Hamiltonian or preserve the required moving code.

9. The root-proposed identity-output C X C X^dag checker has an unprotected
initial physical X^dag. An angle error delta there is accepted with
probability one and logical error norm |sin(delta)|. The constructive fix
is C X C, tracking the known logical X byproduct. The one remaining middle
X pulse then changes only success probability through sin^2(alpha).

10. C X C alone does not detect photon loss. N=1 data have acceptance
probability 1/2 under perfect helper readout. Add a total-number/parity
measurement and pay its whole elapsed time.

11. The improved finite query still accepts a linear late Q amplitude.
For piecewise-changing modal rates at epsilon=.001 the saved accepted
Q norm is 5.56e-4, while the in-code residual is 5.39e-8. The stronger
stand-alone instrument bound is false. Streaming or terminal resolution
must be part of the operation's declared contract.

12. Calling detector misses p*q requires the joint-fault assumption.
At fixed q the error is first order in p. A common event that both damages
data and hides the flag is not independent and costs its own first-order
rate unless a later check localizes its full propagation cone.

13. Reset/replacement is not recovery. It does not reconstruct the lost
unknown logical state. Outer recovery must be noisy, syndrome-mediated,
and include replacement errors at retained known locations.

14. A qubit-helper version reduces resources but still needs compatible
controls. The middle BS must be unconditional while the g/f or dual-rail
helper is coherent. An always-on single-mode dispersive shift interferes
with that pulse. A qualified native synthesis or finite on/off control
must be included instead of silently dropping the term.

15. One exact native spin-1 endpoint construction fails the hazard audit.
With Hpm=(Jx +/- Jz)/sqrt(2) for time pi and the balanced code orthogonal
to the Jy=0 state, the difference of the two branch propagators is an
exact logical-X/projector instrument. Nevertheless its accepted
first-order differential-loss insertion kernel is non-scalar at interior
times, peaking at operator norm 1/sqrt(2). This failed native path is
saved in check_loss_algebra_and_native_failure.py. Root's separate
native-control agent found a different palindrome and its required
physical sign-randomization contract; the endpoint test alone proves
neither schedule's noise protection.

16. A bounded DIRECT fanout mask is not sufficient. Q or N=1 can damage
a partner within P; subsequent Hadamard and controlled-phase operations
spread that partner error to wires never touched by the original tagged
wire. The mask must cover the transitive forward cone. Finite-contrast
and missed-check tail estimates do not remove this architectural cost.
