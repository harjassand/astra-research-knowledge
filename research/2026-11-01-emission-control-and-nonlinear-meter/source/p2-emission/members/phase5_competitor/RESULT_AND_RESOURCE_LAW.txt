PHASE 5 COMPETING CONSTRUCTION AND RESOURCE LAW
31 October 2026

STATUS

This work is a constructive comparison, not a system-level advantage claim.
Its strongest objects are (i) a deterministic single-excitation lower bound,
(ii) an explicit two-photon gate family that escapes that bound, and (iii) a
finite symmetry instrument, improved jointly with the root agent to remove
an unprotected control pulse. The full operation still requires a finite
number check, readout qualification, late-fault coverage, and a noisy outer
fault-tolerant schedule. No perfect detector or perfect outer recovery has
been assumed as an earned physical component.

The central practical alternative is established physical randomization.
For a fixed, frame-independent noisy gate, Pauli twirling converts the
leading no-jump filter into a stochastic residual of second order. The
deterministic lower bound below does NOT rule that out. It concerns a
uniform accepted-branch guarantee against unspecified time-varying loss.

Existing components are explicitly not discoveries here: dual-rail erasure
qubits, bosonic binomial codes, controlled parity, beam-splitter control,
cross-Kerr gates, Knill-Laflamme conditions, error-detectable bosonic gates,
randomized compiling, Pauli-frame tracking, and erasure-aware outer codes.
No historical priority claim follows from this selective source review.

1. A RESOURCE LAW FOR A DETERMINISTIC SINGLE-EXCITATION QUBIT

Suppose one logical qubit is encoded in one excitation across M modes.
Allow arbitrary known number-preserving coherent control, any number of
initially empty helper modes, and a final code projection. Let

    W(t): C^2 -> C^M

be its ideal isometric encoding at time t, including all that control.
Each modal occupation compresses to a rank-one effect

    E_j(t) = W(t)^dag |j><j| W(t)
           = [w_j(t) I + r_j(t).sigma]/2,

with |r_j|=w_j, sum_j w_j=2, and sum_j r_j=0. A nonzero rank-one
effect cannot be a scalar on a two-dimensional code. This already proves
that more empty rails do not make independent unknown modal hazards
instantaneously invisible to the logical qubit.

The stronger time-integrated statement is as follows. Permit loss rates

    kappa_j(t) = gamma + delta_j(t),   |delta_j(t)| <= epsilon <= gamma.

No smoothness or bandwidth restriction on delta_j is imposed. To first
order the no-jump accepted logical map, in the ideal output frame, is

    K = scalar * [ I - A/2 + O(p^2) ],
    A = integral sum_j delta_j(t) E_j(t) dt,
    p = O(gamma T).

Write the traceless part as a.sigma. For a unit vector v choose admissible
rates delta_j(t)=epsilon sign[v.r_j(t)]. Then

    v.a = (epsilon/2) integral sum_j |v.r_j(t)| dt.

The spherical average of |v.r| is |r|/2. Therefore the spherical average
of the integral is T, and some v gives

    |a| >= epsilon T/2.

Choose a logical pure input whose Bloch vector is perpendicular to a.
After conditioning and normalizing, its trace distance from the intended
state is at least

    epsilon T/4 + O(p^2).

The adversary can also choose the negative of all delta_j. A fixed
first-order damping completion adds a vector b, and

    max(|b+a|, |b-a|) >= |a|.

Consequently fixed O(p) extra loss cannot cancel both admissible cases.
This statement also covers coherent endpoint balancing that has unit
success at zero noise and only O(p) extra rejection. A final code
projection removes the outside-code first-order AMPLITUDE but only changes
success probability at O(p^2); it cannot remove the displayed in-code term.

This is a deterministic minimax statement. The rates may depend on the
chosen known control schedule. It is not a statement that a physical bath
knows a fresh independent random frame. It does not cover deliberately
discarding a constant fraction of all ideal runs through arbitrary strong
filters, a larger excitation code, or additional information obtained from
environment sensors. Those are distinct resource/contract changes.

Numerical checks with 2, 4, and 8 modes, 16 independent control intervals,
and positive rates gamma=2 epsilon give conditional trace-distance slopes
0.307, 0.275, and 0.271 per epsilon*T. All exceed the proven 1/4 bound.
See check_excitation_bound.py and excitation_bound_results.json.

2. THE RANDOMIZATION ESCAPE IS REAL

For a Clifford ideal gate U, write its no-jump operator as K=U M and
M=sum_P m_P P in an orthonormal Pauli basis. If the same error map is
obtained independently of a freshly chosen physical Pauli frame, the
compensated randomization averages the accepted channel to

    rho -> U [ sum_P |m_P|^2 P rho P ] U^dag.

Its acceptance probability sum_P |m_P|^2 is state independent. If
M=cI+O(p), the total nonidentity Pauli weight is O(p^2). This is useful
protection of the computed channel, even though a branch conditioned on
the particular random key retains an O(p) filter. The stronger joint
classical-key/quantum-state norm is not the relevant operational target
when the key has already served its correction/compilation purpose.

Physical X rail swaps, randomization pulse duration, and frame-dependent
control errors must be included. Merely changing a software name does not
conjugate the bath. A fresh random physical encoding at a teleportation
boundary can sometimes absorb that cost, but the teleportation is a real
operation. Arbitrary current-frame-dependent noise invalidates the simple
twirling identity. Parent/root is treating that contract in the synthesis.

Independent loss-count calibration would require O(p^-3) trials to
estimate a per-cycle loss probability p to absolute O(p^2) precision. This
is only the cost of one exact deterministic compensation strategy, NOT a
necessary cost of reliable computation. Randomization is a counterexample
to interpreting it as a universal calibration lower bound.

3. EXACT LOSS COMPLETION: LOCAL OPTIMALITY ONLY

For cold loss operators L_j, put Gamma=sum_j L_j^dag L_j. On an invariant
protected manifold R, the no-jump generator is -iH-Gamma/2. If the demand
is exact scalar attenuation at EVERY prefix, then

    Gamma(t) R = lambda(t) R

is sufficient, and necessary for that same ideal controlled trajectory.
If Gamma preserves R, adding detectable cold jumps with

    sum_k J_k^dag J_k = lambda I_R - Gamma|_R

is possible iff lambda >= lambda_max(Gamma|_R). Thus lambda_max is the
minimum instantaneous scalar hazard within this specified completion
class. The added jumps must really go to a detectable erasure sector.

This is not globally optimal among echo, gate redesign, endpoint filters,
or randomization. If Gamma does not preserve the moving protected space,
matching only R Gamma R is insufficient: its off-diagonal coupling must
also be removed or subsequently detected. A full invariant manifold can
be flattened, at its potentially larger maximum eigenvalue.

For dual rails, adding cold loss to the slower rail makes their rates
equal. For a swap-wait-swap mediated entangler, matching both rails AND
the temporarily occupied coupler makes the complete no-jump trajectory
scalar, but can raise erasure substantially. In an illustrative 0.5 us
gate model, unbalanced mean no-jump code survival is 0.992166 with an
accepted-state trace-distance witness 0.002690. Full matching raises the
erasure rate to 0.016856 and makes the no-jump scalar identity exact to
1.7e-16. These are model calculations, not measured device rates.

The retained lower-cost endpoint variant tunes unequal-loss forward and
backward swaps and attenuates one spectator rail. It reaches erasure
0.013666 with exact no-jump output in the switched-interaction model.
It fails severely if the same chi is simply left on during swaps. This
failure is saved rather than hidden by the gate's nominal cartoon.

4. TWO EXCITATIONS ARE ENOUGH TO ESCAPE THE EXACT OCCUPANCY OBSTRUCTION

Use two bosonic modes with total photon number two:

    |0_L> = |1,1>,
    |1_L> = (|2,0>+|0,2>)/sqrt(2),
    |Q>   = (|2,0>-|0,2>)/sqrt(2),
    P = |0_L><0_L| + |1_L><1_L|.

This is a small member/variant of the established two-mode binomial
construction, not a newly invented code. Its relevant equalities are

    P n_a P = P n_b P = P.

Thus independent, unknown, arbitrarily time-varying mode loss rates have
scalar FIRST-ORDER no-jump compression whenever the moving code has this
property. The loss itself reduces total number and remains detectable.
One excitation cannot do this by the rank-one argument; two suffice.

This code does NOT correct an unlocated one-photon loss internally:
P a^dag b P is a logical X, so the full loss Knill-Laflamme conditions fail.
Losses are sent to the outer erasure code. It spends twice the excitation
exposure of a one-photon dual rail but not twice the number of data modes.

5. A UNIVERSAL GATE FAMILY WITH THE SAME FIRST-ORDER HAZARD PROPERTY

Define Jx=(a^dag b+a b^dag)/2. On the code, Jx=X_L and Jx|Q>=0.
Beam-splitter rotations exp(-i alpha Jx) preserve P at every instant.
The intra-pair Kerr n_a n_b is |0_L><0_L|, giving logical Z rotations.
Both preserve P and its scalar occupation compression.

For two blocks (a,b) and (c,d), use one intermode Kerr:

    C = exp(-i pi n_a n_c).

Within code x code it is diag(-1,1,1,1), an entangling controlled phase.
It returns both blocks exactly at this angle. During the excursion every
n_j commutes with its Hamiltonian, so the moving occupation compression
remains scalar at every time, although the instantaneous code need not be
the original tensor product. Hence unknown differential loss does not
produce first-order in-code filtering during this gate.

The finite 9-state calculation gives <5e-16 gate/leakage residuals and
<5e-16 moving-compression residuals. Independent amplitude damping at
any time lowers the total number of one of the participating blocks;
conservatively marking both operands covers its partner backaction.

A small Kerr overrotation delta has projected first-order generator
P(n_a n_c)P=I. Its leading accepted IN-CODE distortion is second order;
it can leave an O(delta) coherent leakage amplitude. Ordinary logical X
or logical Z rotation-angle errors are not generically detected by code
membership. They remain in the residual control budget unless addressed
by a separate fault-tolerant construction.

Direct cavity-cavity Kerr of useful strength, on/off isolation, higher
Fock-state control, and complete duration are paid resources. The MHz
transmon-cavity dispersive shift in a published SWS experiment is not a
measurement of equally strong cavity-cavity Kerr and must not be reused
as though it were.

6. SYMMETRY ACQUISITION: AN ACTUAL INSTRUMENT WITH A TRACKED BYPRODUCT

Root first proposed a symmetry query using two C gates and data X pulses.
Our independent finite-model audit found that an initial physical X^dag
would itself have an unflagged logical angle fault. Removing that pulse
and tracking a known logical-X byproduct gives the improved query.

Let a binary helper control a mode parity

    C_h = exp(-i pi n_a |1_h><1_h|).

Prepare helper |+>, apply the chronological sequence C_h, X, C_h with

    X = exp(-i pi Jx/2),

then read the helper in its X basis. On total data number two the exact
accepted and rejected Kraus operators are, up to a choice of signs,

    K_accept = i Jx = i X_L P,
    K_reject = Q.

The known X is carried in a Pauli frame. No physical undo is needed.
With an arbitrary middle pulse angle alpha,

    K_accept = i sin(alpha) X_L P,
    K_reject = cos(alpha) P + Q.

Consequently a pulse area error in this ONE middle BS pulse changes only
success probability, exactly. The removed initial X^dag would have left
an O(delta) logical rotation at acceptance probability one. Both versions
and that counterexample are retained in the scripts.

The helper may be a second N=2 block, a one-photon dual rail, or a g/f
qutrit with e as a decay flag. A two-photon helper is not required for
data protection. Helper number-diagonal phases and unequal no-jump
attenuation commute through both C_h gates and the data-only BS. On valid
data, they therefore act only on the final disentangled pointer. They
can spoil discrimination but do not create a logical data error alone.
Any initial helper state in its intended control subspace similarly
leaves valid data transformed by the same known X; helper preparation
need not be presupposed perfect.

For a g/f helper, a single f->e relaxation during a query is flagged if
the final three-level readout works. Data action while helper is e may be
arbitrary and is erased on that flag. Direct f->g transitions, e re-entry,
or a fault that both damages data and hides its flag are explicit residual
or correlated-fault terms, not silently assigned probability p*q.

Important physical compatibility condition: the middle BS must really be
unconditional while the helper is coherent. An always-on single-mode
dispersive interaction generally continues to act during it. A tunable
interaction that is off during the BS, or a qualified native synthesis,
is required. Simply citing BS plus dispersive hardware is not a proof of
this control schedule. The lower-level synthesis must preserve the
moving hazard property or have its extra residual computed.

The query does not certify total photon number. In N=1, a perfect helper
read can accept with probability 1/2. A total-number/parity check is an
additional operation. A helper controlled phase generated by (n_a+n_b)
can measure total parity; two single-mode parity interactions can realize
it if only local interactions are available. Both its duration and its
late faults must be paid.

7. THE FINITE-CHECKER BOUNDARY REMAINS REAL

Under piecewise-changing unequal data loss rates, the C X C query's
IN-CODE accepted residual scales quadratically. At epsilon=.001 in the
saved example it is 5.39e-8. However its accepted outside-code amplitude
is 5.56e-4, linear in epsilon. This is a late no-jump event in a finite
measurement and cannot be called a quadratic accepted-instrument diamond
bound. A direct controlled-BS checker has the same kind of boundary.

The useful streaming observation is that the specified gate endpoints
preserve P and Q. A first-order Q amplitude can remain pending and be
located by a later clean check; it cannot return to P at first order
without another fault. Loss flags can similarly be delayed by one check.
A circuit-level proof must carry that pending state and its propagation
support, rather than resetting the model to perfect code outputs after
every check. Terminal quantum outputs and terminal measurement records
must be explicitly covered. Root owns that full fault-tolerant synthesis.

The naive alternative of rotating LIVE DATA to an easy parity-measurement
basis is definitively worse: unequal loss during the readout produces a
first-order IN-CODE filter even under perfect parity readout. At epsilon
.001 the saved residual is 9.98e-4, versus 2.50e-7 for an ideal symmetry
projection. Rotating only an already disentangled HELPER for destructive
readout does not have this defect for valid data.

8. WHAT HAS AND HAS NOT BEEN WON

Won: an explicit minimal-excitation distinction; a universal low-degree
Hamiltonian gate family respecting first-order unknown modal loss; a
concrete symmetry instrument using two parity interactions and one BS;
exact immunity of its accepted logical operation to its middle pulse-area
error; pointer-noise and imperfect-preparation separation; and explicit
finite-duration and control-compatibility failure tests.

Not won: a hardware-qualified switched/native interaction implementation;
a numerical erasure/Pauli threshold for the full apparatus; a proof that
its increased photon exposure and measurement cadence beat randomized
one-photon dual rails; a complete noisy-recovery exRec; or a general
conversion of logical control, dephasing, heating, and correlated faults.
Those absent items prevent claiming the original physical-overhead target
has already been achieved.
