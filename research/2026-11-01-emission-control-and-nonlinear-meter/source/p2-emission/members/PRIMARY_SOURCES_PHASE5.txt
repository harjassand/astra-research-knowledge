Vuillot,Ruiz,Guillaud,Mirrahimi,
Quantum non-demolition measurements as a practical primitive for fault-tolerant
computation against biased noise (2026), arXiv:2605.24262v1.
https://arxiv.org/html/2605.24262v1
Adopted three-cat interaction, actual buffers, measured-ZZZ CNOT route.
Its separate ideal weight4noise simulations are not inherited as a threshold
for the actual weight3hardware/control cycle constructed here.

Huembeli,Nigg, Towards a heralded eigenstate-preserving measurement of
multi-qubit parity in circuit QED, PRA96,012313(2017), arXiv:1704.08734.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1704.08734
Prior dynamical decoupling during parity detection and explicit distinction
between repeated-label QND and preservation of degenerate-sector information.

Cohen,Smith,Devoret,Mirrahimi, Degeneracy-Preserving Quantum Nondemolition
Measurement of Parity-Type Observables for Cat Qubits, PRL119,060503(2017).
https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.119.060503
Established nonlinear Josephson parity construction; independent competitor
retains its Zeno, signal-strength and full-time limits.

Srivatsa,von Keyserlingk, Operator growth hypothesis in open quantum systems,
PRB109,125149(2024).
https://journals.aps.org/prb/pdf/10.1103/PhysRevB.109.125149
Close precedent connecting Lindbladian odd moments and algebraic spectral
tails. The present commutator-depth and finite-window derivations should not
be advertised as the discovery of Lindblad spectral tails generally.

Fang,Lu,Tong, Mixing Time of Open Quantum Systems via Hypocoercivity,
PRL134,140405(2025), arXiv:2404.11503.
https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.134.140405
Related separation of conservative and dissipative dynamics. No mixing-time
bound is borrowed without checking its hypotheses.

Putterman et al., Hardware-efficient quantum error correction via concatenated
bosonic qubits, Nature638,927-934(2025), arXiv:2409.13025v2.
https://arxiv.org/html/2409.13025v2
Demonstrated noise-biased cat/transmon operations, adjacentXXsyndromes,
distance5repetition memory and full2.8microsecond cycles. Actual lifetime and
pump figures are distinct from our illustrative kappa1/kappa2 design ratio.

Rosenblum et al., Fault-tolerant detection of a quantum error, Science(2018).
Source details and exact matched-qutrit instrument are in
phase5_parity_competitor/REPORT.txt. Frequency matching does not automatically
match dressed emission amplitudes or eliminate double decay.

Tsunoda et al., Error-Detectable Bosonic Entangling Gates with a Noisy Ancilla,
PRX Quantum4,020354(2023), arXiv:2212.11196.
https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/pdf/10.1103/PRXQuantum.4.020354
Strong same-control comparator. AppendixG treats no-jump backaction separately
from jump-error closure; AppendicesK/L address nonlinearities and bandwidth.

An entangling gate for dual-rail erasure qubits (2026).
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13441885/
Strong experimental SWS comparator: about500ns,.5% erasure,<.1% residual
Pauli errors. The present theoretical meter has not beaten its complete cost.

Halaski,Koch, Obstacles to Continuous Quantum Error Correction via Parity
Measurements, arXiv:2603.02106v2 / Physical Review Applied(2026).
https://arxiv.org/html/2603.02106v2
Live obstruction from within-parity and transient information leakage in
common continuous meters. Native higher-order coupling is an established
escape; this continuation adopts one and studies its imperfections.

Chuang,Leung,Yamamoto, Bosonic quantum codes for amplitude damping,
PRA56,1114(1997), doi:10.1103/PhysRevA.56.1114.
Michael et al., New Class of Quantum Error-Correcting Codes for a Bosonic Mode,
PRX6,031006(2016), arXiv:1602.00008.
Albert et al., Performance and structure of single-mode bosonic codes,
PRA97,032346(2018), arXiv:1708.05010, including two-mode constructionEq6.16.
https://jianggroup.yale.edu/sites/default/files/2018-v_albert_performance_physreva.97.pdf
These prevent calling the balanced N2or N4encoding itself a new invention.

Aliferis,Terhal, Fault-tolerant quantum computation for local leakage faults,
Quantum Information and Computation7,139-156(2007).
https://www.rintonpress.com/journals/doi/QIC7.1-2-9.html
Established coherent leakage handling/teleportation; the new inner instrument
does not replace the required noisy outer architecture.

Freedman,Hastings,Shokrian-Zini, Symmetry Protected Quantum Computation,
arXiv:2105.04649(2021), and Rudolph/Virmani2023 STP computation.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.04649
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10684540/
Constructive measurement/exchange comparators; ideal universality is distinct
from a fault-tolerant full operation and finite-input success contract.

Naor,Naor, Small-Bias Probability Spaces: Efficient Constructions and
Applications, SIAM Journal on Computing22(1993), doi:10.1137/0222053;
Alon,Goldreich,Hastad,Peralta, Simple Constructions of Almost k-wise
Independent Random Variables, Random Structures and Algorithms3(1992).
https://www.wisdom.weizmann.ac.il/~naor/PAPERS/bias.pdf
Established approximate-cancellation escape from the exact universal
phase-null action lower bound. Its physical finite-rate implementation and
complete output spectrum are not assumed for free.
