MEASUREMENT-BASED COMPETITOR: MOVE ANALOG FAULTS INTO CONSUMED PORTS
31 October 2026. Constructive independent route within the exchange branch.

Main disposition
----------------
The strongest completed route here adopts established measurement-based
and fault-tolerant machinery. It replaces analog entangling-angle control by
pair singlet/triplet measurements and maintained local spin controls. Its
specific useful derivation is that a complete DESTRUCTIVE Bell measurement
can turn all sector-scalar measurement histories into an ordinary classical
Bell-label channel. Finite measurement weakness then costs its wrong-label
PROBABILITY rather than its amplitude. This includes no-jump filtering and
arbitrary sector phases, because the measured pair is consumed.

This is a useful operational change. It does not establish a new universal
architecture, a device advantage, or a new threshold theorem. The stricter
proposal using only S/T measurements and maximally mixed spins still has a
nontrivial constant-resource fusion/interface gap. STP=BQP is not silently
used as a quantum-input, fixed-gadget threshold theorem.

The previous exact singlet checker is neither contradicted nor needed by
the selected complete Bell measurement. Its coherent helper cancellation
is bypassed. Its broad control-noise protection failures remain correct.

1. Why the measurement-only opening is real, but already established
------------------------------------------------------------------
Freedman, Hastings and Shokrian-Zini (FHSZ,2021) explicitly construct
universal computation from S/T measurements supplemented by local X,Z.
Their Lemma1 gives the complete Bell measurement used below. Their
construction prepares entangling gate resources offline, then teleports
the gates onto data. Rudolph and Virmani (2005) constructed measurement-
only cluster computation given three polarized state sources; their
2023 work proves STP=BQP even from maximally mixed spins.

The 2023 proof builds large symmetric ensembles and localizes their
relative directions. It explicitly states that its construction is not
yet explicitly fault tolerant, and suggests finite resource states and
Bell fusions as a promising alternative. It would be incorrect to claim
that mere equality of decision-problem classes already supplies a bounded
quantum-input gate compiler or a numerical threshold for a noisy device.

The actual reason S/T measurement helps is representation-theoretic. On
two spin-1/2 particles, SU(2) is multiplicity-free:

                 H_2 = spin0 direct_sum spin1.

Every FINE operation commuting with this spin action, with spinless
apparatus degrees included, has the form

                 M_r = s_r P_S + t_r P_T.                 (1)

Orbital/source operators can replace the scalar coefficients if retained.
Within either measured sector no logical vector is distinguished. A
Hamiltonian exchange error or no-jump term respecting this precise
symmetry is included. Covariance of an averaged channel alone is NOT
enough; the fine spin-independent coupling is the condition. Interactions
with an unmeasured third spin and symmetry-breaking fields need their
own fault budgets. This is the same distinction that matters in earlier
Astra theorems, now applied to a multiplicity-free operation.

2. Exact finite-strength S/T instrument
--------------------------------------
Consider the one-sided charge-capture instrument on a pair already in
single occupancy:

             N=a P_S+P_T,
             C_r=b_r P_S,       |a|^2+sum_r|b_r|^2=1.      (2)

The C_r are the actual distinct charge/emission records. The spin singlet
is unique, so a clicked pair may be consumed and replaced if a surviving
singlet output is wanted. The triplet remains in the separated charge
configuration. Repeat only on N, up to n times. Set q=|a|^2 and x=q^(n/2).
The total singlet miss probability is q^n. The no-click phase is arbitrary.

For the NONDESTRUCTIVE spin instrument, with a restored singlet on clicks,
the exact accessible half-diamond distance to a projective S/T instrument is

                      epsilon_QND = x/(2-x).               (3)

This equality holds including a reference. A pure input has singlet
weight p; the two sectors of its purification are orthogonal. The two
record blocks of the difference have total trace norm

     x^2 p + sqrt(x^4 p^2+4 x^2 p(1-p)).

Its maximum half-value occurs at p=1/(2-x), yielding(3). Refining the
click-time/charge record does not improve the operational comparison:
an ideal instrument may reproduce the same normalized click distribution.

This QND error is NOT q^n. The retained cross-sector coherence is the
amplitude burden already present in the earlier instrument work.

3. A complete destructive Bell measurement changes that burden
-------------------------------------------------------------
Order the four Bell states as

        Psi-, Psi+, Phi+, Phi-.

Use the exact FHSZ decision tree:

  test S/T;
  on no click apply Z to the first spin and test S/T;
  on no click apply X to the first spin and test S/T;
  the three click positions and final no-click identify the four labels.

Use n repeated attempts for each test of(2). All measured spins are
discarded after the tree. Let Pi_j be the four Bell projectors. Its POVM is

       E_j=(1-q^n) Pi_j,                         j=1,2,3;
       E_4=Pi_4+q^n(Pi_1+Pi_2+Pi_3).                         (4)

Therefore this physical spin instrument is exactly an ideal destructive
Bell measurement followed by a classical confusion channel. In particular,

                  epsilon_Bell = q^n.                       (5)

This is the full measurement channel on the consumed pair and any
unmeasured entangled reference, not merely a test on Bell input states.
The measured pair is traced out; off-diagonal Bell matrix elements vanish
in that partial trace. The remaining remote conditional state is precisely
the mixture specified by the Bell-label confusion matrix.

General form. Any fine history made of sector-scalar Kraus operators(1)
and exact Pauli permutations has a Kraus operator that is a permutation
times a Bell-diagonal matrix. Its effect is Bell diagonal. An adaptive
classical record decoder, including wrong labels, only sums such effects.
Thus the entire consumed-port channel remains

                      M_actual = C_classical o M_Bell.      (6)

Here a fixed classical channel means the apparatus starts independently
of the unknown data, or is refreshed/reset so that the displayed scalar
Kraus description is legitimate. With retained quantum memory, the most
general exact statement is a Bell-diagonal instrument with a conditional
quantum map on that memory. A classical Markov error channel and an
independent-fault threshold do not follow merely from diagonality. Use
fresh bounded apparatus packets, or separately prove conditional-history
noise bounds and include memory propagation. The earlier uniform-source
theorems can be useful for that qualification but are not assumed here.

Unknown strength, unknown sector phases, no-jump attenuation, and loss
of inter-sector apparatus coherence do not create an extra coherent
logical fault in this model. They alter the classical channel C. A
uniform conditional wrong-label bound p gives half-diamond error <=p.

Equation(6) is the specific organising observation of this route.
Destructive use is essential. A surviving triplet/code state cannot be
discarded in the proof of a QND operation. Approximate local Pauli pulses,
spin-breaking terms, an unqualified orbital extension, or a quantum charge
read that does not satisfy the stated fine operation domain are additional
faults; they are not absorbed by asserting Bell diagonality.

4. Use the existing finite Hubbard cycle, with its detector boundary
------------------------------------------------------------------
The earlier two-site single-orbital calculation used

 U=1, t=.0707106781, hopping ramps40/U, detuning sweep400/U to1.5U,

and retained all three charge-singlet states(D_L,S_11,D_R). Every triplet
remained exactly in(1,1). At the completed pulse, accepting BOTH double
charge records as singlet gives

 q = Prob(S_11 | S input) = 2.43723761742892e-5.             (7)

On a genuine(1,1) no-click result, the spin operator is exactly of form(2).
On either double-charge outcome, the initial spin sector is uniquely S;
the pair is consumed. A subsequent wrong classical decision on that already
resolved record does not reintroduce input Bell coherence. A final S read
needs no coherent orbital return. The final all-no-click branch is also
consumed. This is a substantial simplification relative to preserving a
four-spin logical vector through a reusable helper cycle.

Two tries per Bell label give intrinsic error5.9401272e-10 from finite
charge conversion. One try gives2.4372376e-5. These are not device errors.
The worst specified coherent control time is3n*480/U, plus at most two
local Pauli pulses. Add every charge-sensing interval, loading and reset
of fresh resources, routing, latency, and memory exposure. On ideal
uniform Bell inputs the decision tree uses2.25 S/T stages on average;
bounded worst duration, not this average alone, is needed by a fault proof.

The actual quantum charge instrument must be compared with the ideal
charge-resolved instrument used in deriving(7). Let its uniform accessible
error be delta_ch per call, including its finite signal collection time.
The earlier passive-receiver/output-mode theorem is an available way to
earn such a finite error in a specified spin-blind charge-coupled linear
receiver; it is not a claim that a laboratory charge detector is perfect.
Three charge values need either a calibrated three-level read or equivalent
binary probes. All incident fields, collection, initial-energy conditions
and sensor backaction remain resources.

A conservative full Bell-measurement budget is then

 epsilon_Bell <= q^n +3n delta_ch +2 delta_Pauli
                   +epsilon_orbital+epsilon_isolation
                   +epsilon_spin(T_elapsed).                (8)

Actual branch-dependent bounds can improve(8). No source reset or detector
independence should be inferred from it. The finite Hubbard calculation
has not supplied a single actual material's orbital spectrum, spin-orbit
coupling, charge-sensor quality, local spin-pulse quality and full lifetime.
Those are the remaining device-admission tasks.

5. Full reliable-computation route with noisy recovery, not an ideal oracle
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
For the strongest CLOSED comparator, deliberately admit maintained noisy
single-spin preparation, Clifford controls and readout, in addition to
the noisy S/T primitive. These are ordinary conventional resources; local
magnetic/ESR addressing and its reference must be paid. Global calibrated
Zeeman evolution can be treated in a rotating frame, whereas unknown
inhomogeneous or fluctuating fields remain faults.

Use the FHSZ offline CNOT Choi-resource preparation: two Bell pairs and a
prepared ancilla, their O_ZZ and O_XX parity constructions, and an ancilla
read. Its ideal joint acceptance probability is1/4. It is prepared before
unknown data arrive. Two complete Bell measurements then teleport a CNOT
to unknown data, followed by known Pauli corrections. All measurements,
local controls and resource preparations are noisy physical operations.
Their failure rates and durations are included; a failed offline attempt
is not free.

For example, cap the factory at R attempts. An ideal unavailable-resource
probability(3/4)^R becomes a flagged missing gate/resource. If a trial has
at most F relevant noisy locations and ideal success s0=1/4, conditioning
on acceptance can amplify its faulty fraction; a safe small-noise estimate
is Fp/(s0-Fp), not Fp. The exact stochastic bound should use the actual
successful and rejected distributions. Storage while waiting for factories
must be included or avoided by providing parallel factories and a bounded
queueing schedule. A permanent bad shared reference or detector is not a
local stochastic fault.

This supplies a noisy conventional gate set. Fault tolerance is then
implemented using actual syndrome circuits, for example the Steane[[7,1,3]]
code with Shor extraction:

 * Use the six weight-four CSS stabilizers (three X type, three Z type).
 * Prepare a four-spin cat by H on spin0 and CNOT chain0->1->2->3.
 * Verify Z0 Z3 using a fifth spin. Reject a failed cat before data contact;
   cap at two attempts, so two unavailable attempts require at least two
   faults in the idealized local-fault model.
 * Couple each accepted cat spin to one distinct data spin only. Read the
   cat in X. A dangerous X pattern in the cat is either detected by its
   verification or equivalent, modulo XXXX, to a single X. A cat phase
   fault affects its syndrome bit.
 * Repeat the whole six-bit syndrome until two consecutive syndromes agree,
   at most four rounds. On no agreement, flag the block. From a clean
   input, one fault can leave at most one data error. With no internal
   fault, one input error is corrected. Two consistent stale zero syndromes
   may leave a late single data error; that is allowed fault containment,
   not a demand to detect an error at the last instant.
 * H,S and CNOT are transversal at this code level. All their physical
   locations use the noisy gate set above. Destructive encoded Pauli reads
   use the classical code decoder. Magic-state injection/distillation uses
   the same noisy Clifford and readout circuits; FHSZ supplies a concrete
   nonstabilizer seed(3|0>+|1>)/sqrt(10), or a maintained conventional
   single-spin magic-state source can be used instead.

Thus recovery is an explicit repeated circuit made from the same faulty
resources. It is not supplied as a perfect map. The attached program checks
all94 elementary single Pauli faults in the verified-cat subcircuit;45
accepted cases give at most one data-error position and49 are rejected.
That is a deliberately scoped internal check, not an audit of the entire
Steane threshold construction. Standard extended-rectangle proofs and
their handling of overlapping recovery circuits are established machinery.

Leakage cannot simply be declared a Pauli error. Teleportation-based
leakage-reduction units consume old spins and output fresh prepared spins.
They are inserted before interactions where needed, using the same noisy
Bell instrument. A faulty resource can leave leakage; the next fault-free
reduction maps an arbitrary old input to a valid output qubit. Aliferis and
Terhal give the rigorous local-leakage extension of the threshold argument.
The isolated quantum-input Bell instrument supplies the required interface;
physical bad-occupation states must still be qualified as local faults on
the actual hopping/read graph, with no reservoir-mediated spread.

With bounded-size, bounded-time implementations, the standard local-noise
threshold is nonzero. This is an adoption of known fault tolerance, not a
new threshold result. A crude operation count for the cat schedule gives
at most914 macro locations per EC and3663 per two-block CNOT extended
rectangle BEFORE routing, leakage reduction, factory waiting and memory.
The inverse pair count is1.49e-7 and is only a scale illustration: it is
not a complete threshold claim. Modern optimized Steane/Knill/fusion
constructions are much stronger comparators than this conservative example.

In particular, the standard recursion p_next <= C p^2 applies only after
all macro faults, intrinsic approximation, rejection, memory, routing and
readout have been assigned a legitimate local-noise strength. For coherent
Hamiltonian faults use the corresponding norm-based theorem, not a squared
error probability inferred from a diamond norm. Large common-mode source
failure or shared reference errors need separate treatment.

6. S/T-only routing is constructive; S/T-only encoded fusion remains open
------------------------------------------------------------------------
There is an exact useful routing primitive. Given unknown psi_A and a
singlet B,C, measure S/T on A,B. A singlet transfers psi to C exactly. On
triplet, alternate a measurement on B,C and a new measurement on A,B.
Every fine successful history has the same input-to-C isometry up to a
scalar, because the total spin1/2 gauge is untouched. The first success
probability is1/4. Each following two-measurement recovery cycle succeeds
with probability3/8. After r cycles,

              Prob(no transfer)= (3/4)(5/8)^r.               (9)

This gives a finite-timeout transfer with a declared erasure on timeout.
Actual charge read return, noise on each S/T measurement, singlet loading,
routing geometry and storage time still count. It is established forced-
measurement machinery, with the small-spin probability worked out here.

A tempting extension FAILS. Prepare a logical Bell pair B,C of four-spin
singlet qubits and try teleporting A by measuring four physical cross pairs
A_i,B_i into singlets. All four singlets succeed with probability1/32 and
then teleport correctly. However, fine failure records are not harmless.
Already the first two cross-singlet outcomes have input-code effect

     E_A = I/16 - (s_A1 dot s_A2)/12
         = diag(1/8,1/24)                                  (10)

in the standard four-spin logical basis. That record distinguishes the
logical input. Classically forgetting it does not undo the measurement.
One cannot copy the three-spin forced-retry argument or treat a failed
encoded fusion as a clean retry. This is an explicit operational obstacle
to a simple S/T-only, no-reference, bounded fusion architecture.

A possible escape is to build a fresh bounded relational reference packet
for each SU(2)-invariant encoded macro-operation. Its random global frame
would cancel from an invariant target channel, and packet faults would be
local instead of global. However, a complete unknown-input encoded Bell
measurement/quantum-channel compiler must be constructed; the 2023 BQP
decision proof and offline state preparation do not alone provide it.
This remains a research opening, not a premise in the positive comparator.

7. What is actually added, and what it does not beat
--------------------------------------------------
The useful addition is the operational choice to consume the measured ports,
and the exact classicalization(6) with probability-level cost(5). It gives
a mechanism for handling the earlier no-jump/coherence failures without a
coherent helper cancellation. A physically noisy complete Bell detector is
then compatible with standard teleportation, erasure/fusion decoding and
noisy syndrome recovery.

This route can tolerate arbitrarily strong spin-independent exchange phases
inside a properly isolated S/T measurement. It does not tolerate arbitrary
spin anisotropy, between-pair crosstalk, a failed charge admission, a bad
global control reference, or unbounded detector memory for free. It trades
analog entangling control for charge-read duration, local spin controls,
resource factories and conventional fault-tolerance overhead.

The finite calculation does not demonstrate that this trade beats calibrated
exchange hardware or Knill/fusion architectures using equally good local
controls and detectors. It directly adopts their strongest available
operations. The strict STP-only fault-tolerant fusion problem remains more
interesting than another noiseless exchange pulse identity, but it was not
solved by the attempted four-cross-singlet construction.
