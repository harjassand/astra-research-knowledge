PHYSICAL PHASE CONTROL BEFORE IRREVERSIBLE EMISSION
Ordinary derivation, 31 October 2026. Units hbar=1.

Question and ownership
----------------------
Can a parity meter retain its desired signal while suppressing the information
it emits about individual constituents? This attacks an error source DURING
an operation; relabeling the final record cannot erase an earlier emission.
The current application adopts the measured-cat interaction of Vuillot et al.
(arXiv:2605.24262v1). It does not claim to invent cat parity measurements,
dynamical decoupling during readout, counterdiabatic following, or QEC.

1. The receiver's entire output is part of the disturbance
--------------------------------------------------------
For a stable passive linear meter with vacuum input, suppose logical basis
states z drive coherent output waveforms beta_z(t) and leave coherent meter
amplitudes x_z(T). Tracing all emitted fields and the meter multiplies an
off-diagonal logical element by an overlap whose modulus is

 exp[-(1/2) integral ||beta_z(t)-beta_z'(t)||^2 dt
     -(1/2)||x_z(T)-x_z'(T)||^2].                         (1)

There can also be a coherent phase. Omitting it, uncollected ports, or the
final receiver state gives an incomplete instrument. Finite collection
efficiency changes the reported measurement, not the total decoherence in
(1). Full emitted-field quantum erasure can change the resource contract;
ordinary coarse-graining of a classical label cannot.

For a stationary periodic force f(t), the all-port emitted power is

 sum_k ||H(i k Omega) f_k||^2,                           (2)

where H is the ACTUAL receiver transfer function, not an arbitrary filter
chosen in software. Zero average force only removes k=0; resonances can
still amplify higher harmonics. A Markov outlet with constant |H| cannot be
decoupled by this frequency-shifting mechanism: Parseval preserves its
emitted distinguishability. In a squeezed/active meter, (1) is not generally
valid. The companion cross-Gaussian calculation includes covariance and
phase differences rather than treating the output as a coherent state.

2. The adopted cat interaction and its real bandwidth
----------------------------------------------------
For three storage modes a_j and their lossy buffers b_j, take

 H0=sum_j g2[(a_j^2-alpha^2)b_j^dag+h.c.]
       +g3[a_1 a_2 a_3+h.c.],    L_j=sqrt(gamma) b_j.    (3)

In the displaced cat approximation a_j=Z_j(alpha+c_j), the desired
interaction depends on S=Z1 Z2 Z3, and the stabilization/buffer couplings
act only on the gauge variables. This explains within-S equality even for
buffer loss, heating and no-jump histories in THAT representation. Finite
cat overlap and nonlinear excursions require qualification.

The linearized gauge-buffer pair has G=2 alpha g2 and, at g3=0,

 c_dot=-i G b-iF(t),   b_dot=-i G c-gamma b/2,
 T(s)=-G/[s(s+gamma/2)+G^2].                             (4)

In the overdamped limit its slow amplitude bandwidth is
 k_conf=2 G^2/gamma. This is also the cat-confinement amplitude scale.
Adiabatically moving the cat much slower than confinement therefore cannot
move unwanted emission above this same response band. Taking gamma large
does NOT produce an independent fast-protection/slow-meter window.
In an underdamped example G=1,gamma=.5, omega=1, the normalized power is16:
blindly speeding up modulation can worsen the error.

The desired g3 coupling creates parity-dependent parametric terms. For
three equal cats, the common and two dark gauge modes have
 delta=2 alpha g3 S and delta=-alpha g3 S respectively. For real force,

 T_delta(s)=-G[D+i delta(s+gamma/2)] /
                  [D^2-delta^2(s+gamma/2)^2],
 D=s(s+gamma/2)+G^2.                                    (5)

Complex modulation requires both force and conjugate-force components.
The numerical checks retain all three output ports and these parametric
terms. They do not replace them by a favorable scalar linewidth.

3. A physical resource that separates those two speeds
-----------------------------------------------------
Let R(t)=exp[-i sum_j theta_j(t) n_j]. Physically implement

 H_lab(t)=R(t) H0 R(t)^dag + sum_j theta_dot_j(t) n_j.    (6)

Then R^dag|psi_lab> evolves exactly under H0. This is an exact moving-frame
identity for the Hamiltonian and buffer dissipators, not an adiabatic
approximation. Its resources are REAL storage-frequency shifts and tracking
the phase 2 theta_j of each two-photon exchange pump. The buffer drive stays
fixed. If sum_j theta_j=0, the desired three-mode pump can stay fixed too.
A virtual software phase change does not implement the extra term in (6).

An unwanted fixed-phase monomial M=product_j a_j^(sigma_j), where sigma=+1
denotes an annihilator and sigma=-1 a creator on the chosen support, acquires
the phase exp[+i sum_j sigma_j theta_j] in the moving frame (a sign convention
does not affect the power). It can therefore be shifted away from the meter
band without shifting the desired product. A nuisance pump that tracks this
same phase can remain resonant and is NOT suppressed. Broad-band storage
loss also remains: |exp(i theta)|=1 in its Markov jump dissipator.

The first constructive schedule is an Eulerian cycle over even flips,
implemented by opposite physical pi rotations of cat pairs, followed by its
physical time reverse. The reversal gives each actuator zero net detuning
area. The companion program includes finite-duration rotations, rather than
instantaneous, noiseless logical X operations.

An alternative constructed here is the balanced winding

 (theta1,theta2,theta3)=(1,2,-3) theta,
 theta:0 -> 2 pi -> 0, with constant absolute speed omega. (7)

It uses two simultaneous ramps, peak detuning3 omega and total duration
4 pi/omega=12 pi/Delta_max. Every one- or two-mode monomial, including mixed
creation/annihilation and with at most one factor per mode, has nonzero
integer winding frequency. Its DC average
vanishes on each full winding. This statement would fail for (1,1,-2),
because the exchange monomial a1 a2^dag has zero phase. That counterexample
matters if the admitted nuisance interaction includes exchange as well as
pure annihilation products.

There is a further, decisive degree qualification. The fixed-phase cubic
monomial a1^2 a2^dag has frequency2*1-2=0 for(7), yet acts with a proper
logical Z2 character. Thus(7) does NOT suppress every actual cubic nuisance.
For three modes, the alternative winding(1,D,-(D+1)) avoids every nonzero
proper-support integer relation of total degree at most D. Indeed a relation
on a pair first occurs at degrees D+1,D+2,or2D+1 (the pair coefficients are
coprime); a one-mode relation is impossible. For cubic nuisances use(1,3,-4),
which loses the short-cycle advantage: peak detuning4 omega and cycle16pi/
Delta_max. Nuisance pump phase tracking must be included before applying
either statement. The even-flip Eulerian schedule has a broader symmetry
contract and is retained as the central candidate.

At G=1,gamma=10,alpha^2=8,nu=g3 gamma/(4 alpha g2^2)=.2, the winding
calculation gives worst all-port leaked-power ratios over these weight1/2
monomials of .14115,.006006,.0001269 at peak detuning/k_conf=10,50,200.
The desired S drive is unchanged in the ideal moving frame. A separate
affine calculation of a COMMON1% proportional detuning error gives maximum
single-mode coherent gauge populations .2613,.3258,.3244 respectively.
These are two diagnostics, NOT a joint channel result or a nonlinear cat
fidelity. The 16-step balanced Eulerian schedule has a different resource
tradeoff and remains the main joint-channel test. No winner is inferred by
comparing unmatched nuisance algebras or separate favorable components.

4. A general cost law for universal phase cancellation
------------------------------------------------------
The following lower bound prevents promoting the three-mode construction to
cheap protection against arbitrary interactions at arbitrarily high weight.

Assumptions: there are w modes; each theta_j is absolutely continuous; the
control closes after T, theta_j(T)-theta_j(0) in2pi Z; sum_j theta_j(t)=0
up to a constant; and the time average of EVERY nontrivial proper-support
mixed creation/annihilation phase monomial is zero. This is a universal DC
nulling contract for phase controls, not a theorem about every QEC mechanism.

For each subset R of the first w-1 modes, define

 phi_R(t)=exp[i sum_(j in R) theta_j(t)],
 N=2^(w-1).

Distinct R,R' have proper-support phase differences. Hence the N functions
phi_R are orthonormal in L2([0,T],dt/T). Expand in the periodic Fourier
basis. Bessel's inequality says that the total occupation of each Fourier
mode, summed over these N orthonormal functions, is at most one. The sum
of squared derivative norms is therefore at least that of the N lowest
integer frequencies:

 sum_R <|phi_R_dot|^2> >= (2pi/T)^2 mu_N,
 mu_N=N(N^2+2)/12 for even N.                           (8)

The elementary minimum follows by filling k=0,+/-1,+/-2,...; for N=2m
one may choose k=-m+1,...,m, whose squares sum to(2m^3+m)/3.
On the other hand direct summation over all subsets gives

 sum_R |phi_R_dot|^2
 =N/4[(sum_(j<w) theta_dot_j)^2+sum_(j<w) theta_dot_j^2]
 =N/4 sum_(j<=w) theta_dot_j^2.                         (9)

Combining(8)-(9),

 T integral_0^T sum_j theta_dot_j^2 dt >=16pi^2 mu_N/N.  (10)

If each physical detuning has |theta_dot_j|<=Delta_max, then

 Delta_max T >= (2pi/sqrt(3w)) sqrt(N^2+2).              (11)

Thus a universal closed phase-null cycle has exponential control action in
parity weight. This statement holds for nonuniform smooth paths as well as
constant windings. It does not rely on a fixed number of pulse segments.
The proof is a Fourier dimension count, not a novelty claim for Bessel's
inequality or dynamical-decoupling lower bounds generally.

The distinction between actual and arbitrary nuisance interactions matters:
if only proper pure-annihilation products are admitted, the simple winding
(1,...,1,-(w-1)) gives zero DC with peak speed growing only linearly in w.
It leaves some mixed-creation exchange monomials unsuppressed. A true
bounded-degree physical error algebra can permit polynomial designs; it
does not inherit the full arbitrary-proper-support lower bound automatically.
Nor does an outer code require eliminating errors it already corrects.

The exactness qualification is also material. Approximate cancellation can
escape the exponential count. For M independent uniform even-flip masks,
Hoeffding's inequality and a union bound give

 Pr[max_(nontrivial character) |sample mean|>epsilon]
 <=2(2^(w-1)-1) exp(-M epsilon^2/2).

Thus M=O((w+log(1/delta))/epsilon^2) samples suffice for an epsilon-biased
set with failure probability delta. This is the established small-bias-set
idea (Naor/Naor1993; Alon/Goldreich/Hastad/Peralta1992), not a new derandomization
claim. It is a mathematical escape, not a free physical pulse schedule:
finite safe transitions, low-frequency power, actual readout and fresh key
independence must still be included. Uniform DC accuracy alone does not bound
the complete emitted field.

For comparison, a simple exact statement about finite-bandwidth receivers is
available. Let iid zero-mean unit-variance signs drive constant force slots of
duration tau, and include all final ringdown. If h(t) is the causal all-port
impulse response, define g_tau=h*1_[0,tau]. Cross terms vanish on averaging
over the physical signs, so the expected total emitted energy in M slots is
M||g_tau||_2^2. The mean emitted power is ||g_tau||_2^2/tau. For a first-order
meter h(t)=exp(-k t), the power relative to an unmodulated force is exactly

 1-(1-exp(-k tau))/(k tau) = k tau/2+O((k tau)^2).

For the passive two-mode meter(4), energy conservation gives
integral|h_b(t)|^2dt=1/gamma and |T(0)|^2=1/G^2, yielding the same fast-slot
limit k_conf tau/2. There is no dependence on the number of characters in
this expectation. Finite-rate safe switches and their faults are NOT earned
by this ideal sign-slot statement. The loss of exact cancellation buys a
potential polynomial-control route, at the price of a weaker spectral
suppression and additional statistical/control contracts. It is important
not to turn(11) into an impossibility claim for practical approximate QEC.

5. The useful scope, and the comparison still required
-----------------------------------------------------
The derived mechanism moves information BEFORE irreversible emission, using
a physical control that evades the adiabatic meter-bandwidth obstruction.
The actual receiver, all output ports, control-induced gauge excitation,
readout duration and final gauge state must be evaluated jointly. Successful
filtering of a signal amplitude alone is insufficient.

Established references already supply important parts: Huembeli/Nigg2017
studied dynamical decoupling during parity detection; Cohen et al.2017
engineered degeneracy-preserving nonlinear cat parity coupling; Vuillot
et al.2026 provide the measured-three-cat primitive adopted here. The present
extension is the explicit finite-bandwidth/physical-counterrotation analysis,
balanced controls, and stated scope/cost law. Publication-level novelty has
not been established by this targeted literature comparison.

The two-photon/binomial branch remains useful constructive capital, but its
extra sensing, doubled excitation exposure, native Kerr resource and delayed
fault propagation prevent claiming a cheaper architecture. The present
emission-control branch must clear an equally strict bar: useful S contrast
and reduced within-S disturbance in the SAME noisy model, followed by an
honest full-duration comparison with calibrated nonlinear parity and the
strong erasure architectures. No perfect recovery or detector is supplied.
