NATIVE TWO-PHOTON SYNDROME CHECK WITH ALWAYS-ON DISPERSION
31 October 2026. Constructive calculation; no device advantage or threshold claim.

RESULT AND SCOPE

A common, real +G,-G,+G beam-splitter pulse implements the needed pi/2
rotation in both helper branches despite an always-on chi n_a |f><f|
coupling. Its durations are explicit. Two dispersive C maps and known mode
and helper frame updates complete the ideal checker. The exact six-dimensional
no-jump instrument includes loss in every C map and every beam-splitter segment.

This earns compatibility inside the stated linear-mode/dispersive model. It
does not make the finite-time checker loss-transparent. A raw accepted logical
filter is first order in differential loss. A discarded random pump-sign coin
removes its first-order in-code channel term under a physical covariance
contract. Its residual error weight is quadratic, not quartic. Two matched
opposite-sign checks recover quartic in-code weight but double the complete
checking cycle. Both retain first-order accepted Q amplitude from late loss.

The strong same-resource comparator is Tsunoda et al., PRX Quantum 4, 020354
(2023), arXiv:2212.11196, https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.11196 . Its programmable
beam splitter, g/f/e ancilla, dispersive control, first-order ancilla error
detection, and treatment of photon no-jump errors are prior art. This note
supplies a particular native checker calibration and its loss/covariance
calculation; it does not claim invention of the general architecture.

1. PHYSICAL MODEL, CODE AND COMPLETE COHERENT SCHEDULE

Fix N=n_a+n_b=2 and use the data basis (|20>,|11>,|02>). Let

  Jz=diag(1,0,-1), n_a=I+Jz, n_b=I-Jz,
  Jx=(|20><11|+|11><20|+|11><02|+|02><11|)/sqrt(2).

Use |0>=|11>, |1>=(|20>+|02>)/sqrt(2), |q>=(|20>-|02>)/sqrt(2).
P=|0><0|+|1><1|, Q=|q><q|; Jx=X_L P. Define

  D=exp(-i pi n_a)=diag(1,-1,1),
  C=exp(-i pi n_a P_f), Ux=exp(-i pi Jx/2)=-iX_L P+Q.

The helper starts (g+f)/sqrt(2). The chronological schedule is C, middle
pulse, C. Each C takes t_C=pi/chi with the beam splitter off. The middle
beam-splitter pump is at the known midpoint mode frequency. In that frame,

  H_g(t)=-d Jz+u(t)Jx,
  H_f(t)=+d Jz+u(t)Jx+chi I,     d=chi/2.

The scalar f phase chi T_BS is removed by the final read basis phase. The
minus read bra is [<g|-exp(+i chi T_BS)<f|]/sqrt(2); plus has the other sign.
There is also a known physical mode frame W=exp(-i d T_BS Jz). The laboratory
output is W times the quoted instrument. It must be tracked by updating later
pump/code frames or physically compensated with its own cost. It is not an
unpaid arbitrary operation. C and D commute with W.

For actual chi drift, the implemented midpoint and durations remain nominal:
H_C=chi_actual n_a P_f and H_middle=chi_actual n_a P_f-d_nominal Jz+u Jx.
Replacing that error by symmetric detunings alone would omit a physical common
mode error and the wrong helper phase. The program uses the full expression.

2. EXACT COMMON THREE-SEGMENT CALIBRATION

Choose u=+G for t_o, -G for t_m, +G for t_o. Let

  r=sqrt(G^2+d^2), s=G/r, c=d/r, k=c^2-s^2,
  b=1/(2sqrt(2)s), beta=pi-arcsin(b), cb=cos(beta),
  w=-1/sqrt(2), h=-(1-k)b, D0=cb^2+k^2 b^2,
  cos A=(cb*w+k*b*h)/D0,
  sin A=(-k*b*w+cb*h)/D0,
  A=atan2(sin A,cos A) modulo 2pi,
  t_o=A/r, t_m=2beta/r, T_BS=(2A+2beta)/r.

This family exists for G >= chi/(2sqrt(7)). Both +/-d branches implement Ux.
In the spin-1/2 quaternion representation the palindrome has zero y component.
Writing alpha=A/2,

  B=sin(2alpha)cos(beta)-(1-cos(2alpha))k sin(beta),
  v_x=s[B-sin(beta)], v_z=c[B+sin(beta)].

The construction sets B=-sin(beta) and quaternion (w,v_x,v_y,v_z)=
(-1/sqrt(2),-1/sqrt(2),0,0). This is the negative of the desired spin-1/2
quaternion and therefore exactly the desired spin-1 rotation. No rotating-wave
approximation in G/chi is used inside this admitted Hamiltonian.

At G/chi -> infinity, let k0=arcsin(sqrt(2)/4). Then

  G t_o -> pi/4-k0,
  G t_m -> 2pi-2k0,
  G T_BS -> L=5pi/2-4k0 = 6.408513...,
  chi T_check -> 2pi, where T_check=2pi/chi+T_BS.

Helper preparation, readout, reset, number sensing, classical latency, loading,
and any wait for the next operation add to this coherent duration. A paired
checker has a high-drive coherent floor 4pi/chi and uses two helper cycles.
The rectangular switching also specifies a bandwidth resource. For a physical
envelope differing by delta u(t)Jx+delta v(t)Jy, a conservative propagator error
bound is integral(|delta u|+|delta v|)dt. Dispersive and midpoint calibration
errors add integral(2|delta chi|+|delta d|)dt on this finite domain. Thus finite
ramps can be admitted at a stated area error, or calibrated and recomputed
using their actual shape. They cannot be omitted while sending G to infinity.

At zero noise the full data Kraus operators are A=-iJx (accept) and B=Q
(reject). Thus valid data get a known logical X; a compensating physical X
should not be inserted for free. Track the logical frame.

3. FULL NO-JUMP INSTRUMENT AND ALL EMISSION HISTORIES

For independent Markov modal loss rates kappa_a(t), kappa_b(t), on N=2

  Gamma_data=(kappa_a+kappa_b)I+delta_kappa Jz,
  delta_kappa=kappa_a-kappa_b.

On the six-dimensional data x (g,f) space each segment propagates by
exp[(-iH-Gamma_data/2-gamma_f P_f/2)t]. The program contracts the exact
ordered product with the prepared helper ket and the two read bras. It
retains full 3x3 data A and B, including accepted leakage and Q-to-code return.
It never inserts P between successive checks.

For photon-loss and helper-decay jumps, the complete history operators have
the usual ordered form U_eff(T,t_m)L_m ... L_1 U_eff(t_1,0), contracted with
the final physical read. The modal jumps lower N; f->e leaves the g/f domain.
Those histories are erasures only if the actual number/helper read and record
qualify them. A parity check misses the N=0 two-photon-loss sector; either pay
that term or construct full number discrimination. The six-dimensional
no-jump calculation supplies the exact aggregate missing effect
I-A^dagger A-B^dagger B for pure modal loss. It does not construct a perfect
number detector, restore a lost state, or erase the measurement duration.

4. FIRST-ORDER LOSS: COMPLETE A AND B, NOT ONLY THEIR CODE PROJECTIONS

Remove only the known common attenuation amplitude
S=exp[-1/2 integral_0^T (kappa_a+kappa_b)dt]. This normalization is for comparing
code errors; S^2 remains a real survival/throughput cost.

Inside the middle pulse, let

  U_g(t)^dagger Jz U_g(t)=v_x(t)Jx+v_y(t)Jy+v_z(t)Jz,
  I_j=integral_middle delta_kappa(t) v_j(t)dt,
  a_first=integral_first_C delta_kappa(t)dt,
  a_last=integral_last_C delta_kappa(t)dt.

With hats denoting division by S, the full first-order data operators are

  Ahat = -iJx + (i/2)[ I_x P
             +(I_y+a_last)|q><0|+(I_z+a_first)|0><q| ] + O(delta_kappa^2),

  Bhat = Q - (1/2)[ (I_z+a_first)|q><1|
                         +(I_y+a_last)|1><q| ] + O(delta_kappa^2).

The accepted relative in-code operator is

  (-iX)^dagger P Ahat P = I-(I_x/2)X_L+O(delta_kappa^2).

Proof: H_f-chi I=R H_g R with R=exp(-i pi Jx)=Q-P. Inserting Jz changes sign
under R. The accepted insertion is proportional to B_g+DR B_g RD, where
B_g=Ux U_g(t)^dagger Jz U_g(t). In the (0,1,q) basis, DR=diag(1,-1,1).
Its even part gives exactly the three displayed accepted terms; its odd part
gives the rejected terms. The first C cannot create accepted Q from P at first
order; the last C can. Endpoint calibration alone cannot remove this kernel.

For constant delta_kappa, define c_X=-1/2 integral v_x dt. The exact integral
is computed independently of sampling by a block-matrix exponential. At high
drive,

  c_X = -A_inf chi/G^2 + O(chi^3/G^4),
  A_inf = 3pi/8+(sqrt(7)-1)/4 = 1.589535... .

The coefficient follows by expanding around d=0. With dimensionless pulse
angle theta(t), the relevant double sine integral is
I=integral dt integral_0^t dt' sin(theta(t)-theta(t'))
=-3pi/2+1-sqrt(7), and c_X=(chi/4G^2)I at leading order.

The exact numerical coefficient above is quasistatic. A useful stronger
uniform bound nevertheless holds in THIS linear-mode model: at d=0,
v_x(t)=0 pointwise; a rotation/Duhamel bound gives |v_x(t)|<=min(1,d t).
Consequently arbitrary branch-independent time-dependent modal loss satisfies

  |I_x|/2 <= ||delta_kappa||_infinity d T_BS^2/4.

This remains O(delta_kappa_max chi/G^2) for the fixed-shape high-drive pulse.
A covariance-only apparatus bound, branch-dependent loss, or additional
non-SU(2) Hamiltonians need not enjoy it; the generic bound is O(delta_kappa T).
The constant-loss signed coefficient must not be applied to an arbitrary
profile. Matching-profile echo cancellation also does not follow from this
bound.

At constant rates, integral v_y dt=integral v_z dt and both vanish as O(chi^2/G^3).
Therefore, even at arbitrarily large drive,

  ||Q Ahat P|| = |delta_kappa| pi/(2chi)+O(delta_kappa chi^2/G^3,delta_kappa^2).

Late accepted Q amplitude is not suppressed by the fast beam splitter. It must
be included as a pending fault in the next-check/outer-code analysis.

5. PUMP-SIGN RANDOMIZATION: PRECISE CONTRACT AND WHAT IT ACHIEVES

Flip every beam-splitter envelope sign, +G,-G,+G -> -G,+G,-G. Since
D Jx D=-Jx, D Jy D=-Jy, and D Jz D=Jz, the full native instrument obeys

  A_minus=D A_plus D, B_minus=D B_plus D

whenever the entire generator and apparatus noise are D-covariant. Modal loss
Lindblad terms are covariant; diagonal number drift is invariant. A common
relative amplitude error or pump phase error is conjugated by the selected
sign and obeys this condition. An additive pump component that does not flip,
sign-dependent reservoir noise, unmodeled cross-couplings, or noise correlated
with the random bit must be separately qualified. The random bit is generated
independently and discarded if a channel-level averaging claim is made.

The two ideal accepted gates differ only by a global sign. For a real
palindromic Hamiltonian and a palindromic loss profile, the accepted in-code
matrix is symmetric: K=aX+bI+cZ. Its sign-randomized accepted channel is

  |a|^2 X rho X + (bI+cZ) rho (bI+cZ)^dagger.

The first-order logical filter is now a second-order error subchannel. For
static differential loss its leading weight is

  p_check = A_inf^2 (delta_kappa/chi)^2 (chi/G)^4,
  A_inf^2 = 2.52662... .

For arbitrary time-varying branch-independent modal rates, the displayed
first-order kernel still lies in the canceled I component. Exact all-orders
scalar-even decomposition requires the extra symmetry just stated. General
covariance alone does not remove all higher-order coherent terms.

The coin-conditioned instrument does not exhibit this cancellation. The full
data instrument also still has its O(delta_kappa) accepted Q term: sign
randomization preserves that term after aligning the ideal global phase.
Do not report a code-projected quadratic result as a full instrument distance.

6. OPPOSITE-SIGN PAIR, RETAINING Q BETWEEN CHECKS

With a fresh/reset helper for the second check, accept twice and use
A_echo=A_minus A_plus. For matched quasistatic loss and control drift, the
first-order relative code error cancels. First-check Q can return only through
another small imperfection, so the in-code error amplitude is second order
and its Pauli weight fourth order. The program multiplies the full 3x3 A
operators, retaining this Q return.

This is not a free cancellation. It uses two complete syndrome cycles and
assumes matched profiles. Independent temporal variations leave a first-order
residual. The final accepted Q term remains first order, and a flagged first
check stops the pair rather than starting a new attempt on damaged data.

The high-drive limit is exactly soluble. Write z=delta_kappa*pi/(2chi) and
F=exp(-z Jz). Then A_plus=F(-iJx)F, A_minus=-A_plus, up to common attenuation.
The accepted code matrix of A_minus A_plus is

  diag(cosh(2z),cosh(z)^2).

Its relative Pauli error weight is

  p_echo = pi^4/64 (delta_kappa/chi)^4 + O(delta_kappa^6),

not zero. Its coefficient is the cost of Q return plus the two finite C maps.
The final Q amplitude remains approximately |delta_kappa|pi/(2chi).
Here "Pauli weight" is the nonidentity diagonal weight of the code-projected
Choi operator (equivalently squared relative Pauli coefficients), not an
automatically stochastic Pauli error probability. The echo Kraus operator
contains a logical Z filter of order delta_kappa^2, so its channel/diamond
deviation can remain order delta_kappa^2. A further physically qualified
randomization would be needed for a quartic stochastic-channel statement.
The existing pump-sign randomization does not remove this relative Z term.
The ideal-idle quartic benchmark below uses the same Choi-weight convention.

7. HELPER NOISE, READOUT, AND NONLINEARITIES

Pure helper dephasing commutes every admitted primitive. For
L=sqrt(gamma_phi)P_f the coherence factor is eta=exp(-gamma_phi T/2).
Writing q_phi=(1-eta)/2, the exact accepted map is
(1-q_phi) A rho A^dagger+q_phi B rho B^dagger; rejection interchanges A and B.
For otherwise clean valid data this only creates a false flag. It also makes
Q pass with probability q_phi. It is not an accurate syndrome on every input.

For f->e decay at rate gamma_f, the no-jump g/f factors give r=exp(-gamma_f T/2):
A_f=[(1+r)A+(1-r)B]/2 and B_f=[(1-r)A+(1+r)B]/2. With otherwise ideal valid
data, accept and no-jump reject are scalar multiples of -iX, and the e-jump
probability is (1-exp(-gamma_f T))/2. Any e history is a flag only while e stays
distinct and its readout is qualified. e->g plus a missed record is additional
physics; a first decay is not an automatically observed classical event.

A helper read-phase error phi gives
A_phi=[(1+exp(i phi))A+(1-exp(i phi))B]/2. On valid ideal input it attenuates
the good gate; on Q it creates accepted amplitude of size |sin(phi/2)|.
Classical read confusion mixes the true A, B, e and number-record channels.
For clean code a wrong accept/reject bit mainly causes a false flag; on pending
Q or an actual e/loss history it can create a missed flag. Independence must
be justified before multiplying those probabilities. All sensing and reset
faults, and decoherence during their elapsed times, remain in the construction.

The admitted Hamiltonian excludes self/cross Kerr and pump-induced higher
nonlinearities. They are material, not cosmetic: equal self Kerr
H_K=(K/2)[n_a(n_a-1)+n_b(n_b-1)] gives P H_K P=diag(0,K), a logical Z term.
It need not be detected by this symmetry check, and during a beam splitter it
need not commute with the gate. D covariance by itself does not remove every
such in-code term. Large G can increase these corrections or invalidity of
the effective model. A useful nonlinear entangler between encoded blocks,
or its rate, is not granted by the existence or magnitude of dispersive chi.

8. NUMERICAL TRADEOFF AND CONSEQUENCE

The program checks ideal endpoints, exact six-dimensional no-jump products,
first-order full P/Q coefficients, static control drift, helper decay,
phase covariance, and Q-preserving composition of opposite-sign checks.
Example: kappa_a/chi=0.002, kappa_b/chi=0.001. Rates are illustrative model
parameters, not claims about a demonstrated device. Common survival is kept
when reporting photon-loss probability; code-error weights remove it.

 G/chi   chi*T_check  randomized error weight   max photon-loss probability
   1       12.0295        1.19412e-6                       0.03754
   5        7.55866       3.91143e-9                       0.02254
  20        6.60351       1.57588e-11                      0.01962

At G/chi=20 the accepted Q amplitude is 0.0015554 with common attenuation
included. The opposite-sign pair has in-code Pauli weight 1.52232e-12, close
to its analytic high-drive floor, and consumes twice the coherent duration.
These quantities omit the additional helper/number read, reset and loading
time and faults; they are not end-to-end processor fidelities.

As a scale comparison, ideal instantaneous projection after a balanced-code
idle t has code Pauli weight approximately (delta_kappa*t)^4/256. Keeping a
single native randomized check's quadratic term below this ideal quartic term
requires

  G/chi >= 4 sqrt(A_inf)/[sqrt(|delta_kappa|/chi) (chi*t)]
         = 5.04307.../[sqrt(|delta_kappa|/chi) (chi*t)].

For delta_kappa/chi=0.001 and chi*t=1 this is about 159.5. For chi*t=10 it
falls to about 16, but the checker still adds roughly 6.6/chi coherent time
and about 2% photon-loss probability in the illustrative case. Even at
infinite drive, actually including both finite C maps around that idle gives
the in-code quartic coefficient delta_kappa^4 t^2(t+2pi/chi)^2/256. An
instantaneous-check benchmark therefore understates elapsed exposure.

The earned consequence is a native, fully specified checker with explicit
noise contracts and known limits. It is useful capital for a noisy-stream
construction. Its time floor, residual Q, sensing burden, nonlinear errors,
and the need for genuine logical entangling resources prevent a demonstrated
total-resource advantage over the established bosonic gate architectures.
