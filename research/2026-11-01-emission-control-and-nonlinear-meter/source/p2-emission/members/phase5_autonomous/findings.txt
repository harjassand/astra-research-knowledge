AUTONOMOUS / CONTINUOUSLY PROTECTED COMPETING ROUTE
Independent analysis, 2026-10-31. Intermediate research for the parent agent.

RECOMMENDATION

Adopt continuously stabilized cat qubits with direct multi-Z readout through
their existing buffers, following Vuillot et al., arXiv:2605.24262, rather than
inventing a new coherent cat CNOT. Add symmetry modulation only if physical
counterdiabatic frequency control is available and demonstrably cheaper than
the lower-weight leakage it suppresses. The nontrivial contribution here is an
actual buffer transfer calculation, a full-no-jump audit, and a finite-speed
counterrotation construction. These identify a credible robustness improvement
and a sharp feasibility boundary. They do not establish a new full-device cost
advantage or an experimental universal processor.

The proposed extra protection changes a specific failure mechanism: unwanted
lower-weight pump terms can reveal individual logical values while the intended
readout should learn only a product parity. Modulating the physical states before
the finite-bandwidth output responds can suppress that information flow. Echoing
an already emitted Markovian signal cannot do so.

1. STRONGEST BASELINE TO ADOPT

Vuillot et al. propose a directly measured Z1 Z2 Z3 primitive, implemented with
stabilized storage modes a_j and their existing damped buffer modes b_j. Its
Hamiltonian is

 H0 = sum_j g2_j [(a_j^2-alpha_j^2)b_j^dag + h.c.]
      + g3 (a1 a2 a3 + h.c.),
 L_j = sqrt(gamma_j) b_j.

The second term is generated by an extra sum-frequency pump on an ATS. Its
outgoing buffer field measures the triple parity while confinement stays on.
The circuit uses an extra |+> cat, M_ZZZ, then destructive X readout of the old
target. This is preferable to my initial M_ZZ/M_XX/M_Z construction. The paper's
best noisy-code simulations assume native M_ZZZZ; its explicit hardware
derivation is M_ZZZ. This difference must be charged when compiling QEC.

2. ONE COMPLETE PHYSICAL ENTANGLING OPERATION

Input: cats c and t, and a fresh cat a prepared approximately in |+>.

1. Prepare a by finite reset toward vacuum, followed by parity-preserving cat
   stabilization. A lost photon during preparation is a Z-type preparation
   fault; residual high excitation is separately bounded, not ignored.
2. Turn on the triple-mixing pump on c,t,a, retain all local two-photon pumps,
   and integrate the three buffer outputs for a finite sensing interval.
   The observed parity is s = +/-1. The physical measurement can be noisy.
3. Turn off the triple pump and allow a finite reconvergence interval. Use the
   actual slowest drift eigenvalue to bound residual cat-buffer correlations.
   This step occurs before that control/helper can touch another data cat.
4. Decouple t from active data and destructively measure X_t, obtaining m=+/-1.
   The new target is a. Apply or track X_a if s=-1 and Z_c Z_a if m=-1.
5. Reset the discarded old target before reuse, with measured reset rate and
   thermal floor. Continue faulty syndrome extraction on the output blocks.

For ideal projectors the four branch Kraus operators, after these frame
updates, are scalar multiples of CNOT(c -> new target a). This was checked
exactly in three_cat_gate_audit.py; maximum matrix residual is zero.

Every product of physical Z faults within this three-cat gadget remains at
most one phase error in each output block. Explicit mappings include:
 Z_c -> Z_c; Z_t -> Z_c Z_a; Z_a -> Z_a; Z_c Z_t Z_a -> I.
An erroneous M_ZZZ assignment causes X_a. An erroneous destructive X record
causes Z_c Z_a. These are materially different budgets: the ZZZ assignment
must be as reliable as the suppressed bit-error channel. No detector is assumed
perfect, but high-confidence parity discrimination must be paid for in time.

The constant logical part of g3(a1 a2 a3+h.c.) is proportional to the measured
S=Z1 Z2 Z3. Its area need not be an accurately calibrated unitary angle: after
projection it is only a global phase inside the measured sector. Measurement
strength and assignment probability, not a precise controlled rotation angle,
are the key calibration outputs.

3. FAULTY OUTER CORRECTION, EXPLICITLY

Use phase repetition stabilizers X_i X_(i+1) for the first demonstrator. A
concrete check using only the physically specified M_ZZZ primitive is:

 prepare a check cat h in |+>;
 CNOT(h -> data i), implemented by the three-cat teleporting gadget;
 CNOT(h -> data i+1), implemented by another such gadget;
 destructively read X_h.

The two data outputs are in fresh storage modules. A round is scheduled on
disjoint even edges followed by disjoint odd edges, with finite reset and
reconvergence before reuse. A bad X readout in the first teleport contributes
one data Z and a check-record error; Z on the check does not propagate to the
second data cat. This limits dominant phase-error spread. Leakage that survives
reconvergence is a separate correlated-fault probability, not a Pauli error
silently assumed to have reset itself.

Use repeated measured checks over a space-time window; infer the phase frame
from the full noisy history. A stable stream has at least d rounds on each side
of an isolated transversal logical CNOT when identifying its logical failure.
No physical ideal final syndrome or ideal recovery is inserted. Mathematical
ideal decoders may define a logical channel, but the implemented checks are the
faulty circuits above. The logical CNOT is d independent physical gadgets in
parallel between corresponding cats of two blocks. Its phase faults are bounded
to one location per block per gadget. Misassignments and X-type faults remain
the repetition-code floor; once that floor matters, use a thin surface code or
an additional high-rate bit-correction layer, not arbitrarily large repetition
distance alone.

The complete syndrome cycle in this conservative compilation has two M_ZZZ
slots and three destructive X readouts per weight-two check, plus preparation,
reset and reconvergence. It does not inherit the published threshold for one
native M_ZZZZ check. A native weight-four interaction could save these slots,
but its hardware cost must be demonstrated separately.

Universal operation can use the established cat Clifford/measurement machinery
and biased-noise magic-state factories, with their Clifford faults included in
the output error budget. There is no need to discover a new universal code to
test the proposed sensing robustness. At high bias, compare LDPC-cat memory
plus routing and current biased distillation as well as repetition cats.

4. WHY BUFFER FAULTS ARE DIFFERENT HERE

In the local shifted-Fock model write

 a_j = Z_j tensor (alpha_j+c_j).

Then the entire polynomial two-photon stabilization Hamiltonian acts only on
the gauge modes; the entire triple interaction is S tensor K_gauge. This is not
restricted to the leading linear term in c_j. Buffer operators b_j, b_j^dag,
n_bj, and correlated linear combinations of buffer operators are gauge-only.
Consequently, buffer loss, heating and dephasing, including their L^dag L
no-jump terms, cannot distinguish states WITHIN a fixed S sector in this model.
They can degrade the parity signal, heat the gauge system and cause loss of
confinement. Those effects are real and require finite sensing and tail bounds.
But they do not automatically create an individual logical Z simply because a
hot buffer photon decays, unlike the D[a b] coherent-feedback construction.

Storage single-photon loss or gain is Z_j times a gauge operator. It is a real
local phase fault, which the noisy outer code must correct. Unwanted lower-
weight pump terms produce Z_R times gauge operators and can leak which-state
information within a measured parity sector. They are the target of modulation.

Important limit: the shifted-Fock tensor factorization is approximate for finite
cats. Displaced local oscillator bases overlap:
 <k|D(-2alpha)|l> = exp(-2n) times an associated Laguerre polynomial and powers
 of 2alpha. A rigorous device claim requires a low-gauge-excitation cutoff, an
overlap/operator error bound on that cutoff, and a measured/computed tail bound.
An arbitrarily hot helper can drive the state out of this regime. We have not
proved a full unbounded-oscillator threshold or a full nonlinear thermal bound.

5. ACTUAL TRANSFER FUNCTION: THE KEY COMPATIBILITY RESULT

Take equal real alpha and g2, n=alpha^2, G=2 alpha g2, buffer decay gamma. A
small unwanted monomial of degree r gives, on each participating cat, a force
F_R=epsilon_R alpha^(r-1) times logical Z_R. Neglecting the desired interaction's
parametric mixing for a moment, the gauge/buffer equations are

 c_dot = -i G b - i F_R f_R(t) Z_R,
 b_dot = -i G c - gamma b/2.

Thus

 T(s) = b(s)/F_R(s) = -G/[s^2+(gamma/2)s+G^2],
 |T(i omega)/T(0)|^2 = G^4/[(G^2-omega^2)^2+(gamma omega/2)^2].

For gamma >> G, the slow cutoff is

 k_conf = 2 G^2/gamma = 2 n kappa2,  kappa2=4 g2^2/gamma.

It is NOT simply the bare buffer rate gamma. Suppression requires frequencies
above this confinement response. Adiabatic cat phase winding below confinement
does not work. In an underdamped device modulation near G can amplify leakage;
at G=1, gamma=.5, omega=1 the power is 16 times its dc value. One must use the
full transfer, not a generic low-pass slogan.

Include the desired cubic interaction. Its quadratic gauge term is a parametric
coupling proportional to K=ones(3)-I. K has eigenvalues 2 for the common mode
and -1 for each of two dark modes. Define delta=lambda_K alpha g3 S and
D(s)=s(s+gamma/2)+G^2. For a real force,

 T_delta(s) = -G [D(s)+i delta(s+gamma/2)] /
                [D(s)^2-delta^2(s+gamma/2)^2].

The poles solve D(s) +/- delta(s+gamma/2)=0. In the overdamped common mode they
are approximately -k_conf(1+/-nu), where
nu=g3 gamma/(4 alpha g2^2). Stability requires nu<1. The dc output reproduces
the source paper's parity pointer proportional to -(S+i nu)/(1-nu^2).

A degree-r unwanted drive has squared force weights r^2/3 in the common mode
and r-r^2/3 in dark modes. Add leakage from ALL output ports. Monitoring only
their sum does not remove unmonitored dark-port which-state information.
For real periodic modulation, both positive and negative frequency responses
are needed. For a complex winding phase f, the exact response includes the
Fourier coefficients of f and f*, as implemented in actual_buffer_transfer.py.

The analytic transfer agrees with a direct doubled-complex drift-matrix solve
to 2.4e-16 for the tested points. This is a meaningful check of signs, factors,
parametric coupling and output modes, not evidence for a fabricated device.

6. A FINITE-SPEED CONSTRUCTION THAT PRESERVES THE DESIRED SIGNAL

Let R(t)=exp[-i sum_j theta_j(t) n_j]. The exact rotating-frame implementation
of the baseline generator is

 H_lab = R H0 R^dag + sum_j theta_dot_j n_j.

Implement the two-photon EXCHANGE-pump phase as +2 theta_j, keeping the resonant
buffer drive fixed. This rotates the storage coherent states without rotating
the buffers, so no buffer counterdiabatic detuning is needed. The required
extra physical resource is a controllable storage frequency shift
H_CD=sum theta_dot_j n_j. A virtual Pauli-frame update is insufficient.

Use paired counterrotations: A rotates cats (1,2) by (+pi,-pi), and B rotates
(1,3) by (+pi,-pi). Their summed phase is zero at every time, so the desired
sum-frequency measurement pump can stay fixed. The generated endpoint group is

 G_even={I, X1X2, X1X3, X2X3}.

Its only trivial diagonal Pauli characters are I and S=Z1Z2Z3. It averages all
weight-one and weight-two Z_R terms to zero while preserving the desired parity.
An Eulerian cycle with bounded-duration rotations is

 A B A B B A B A.

Every group vertex sees each generator once. This averages the complete winding
paths, not just ideal instantaneous pulses. In the ideal CD rotating frame,
confinement and the desired parity signal remain stationary while parasitic
logical operators acquire fast phases. Eight paired rotations per cycle means
sixteen single-cat rotations; their control imperfections must be counted.

Same-pump parasitics: an operator with storage charge k_j=p_j-q_j transforms by
exp[-i k.theta]. Coefficients generated by integer combinations of two-photon
pump phases 2theta_j and the sum pump are unchanged at the even-flip plateaus.
Therefore their lower-weight odd-parity operator charges are still toggled.
An independently programmed parasitic drive whose phase tracks k.theta is not
suppressed. New near-resonances, finite pump bandwidth, extra Stark/Kerr terms,
and detuning-induced dissipation must be audited. The construction must not
quietly relabel all errors into the rotating frame and declare them modulated.

Numerical linear-response example: G=1, gamma=10, nu=.2, k_conf=.2. For the above
continuous Eulerian counterrotation schedule, the worst leakage-power ratios
over all six lower-weight Z subsets are:

 CD detuning / k_conf       worst leaked power / unmodulated power
 0.5                       0.9038
 2                         0.4876
 10                        0.05420
 50                        0.001648
 200                       0.00002146

At detuning 50 times confinement this is roughly 607-fold suppression of that
specific coherent information-leak channel, or a 24.6-fold relaxation of its
amplitude tolerance at a fixed dephasing budget. It is not a 607-fold gate or
hardware improvement. The numbers are stationary-periodic response rates;
finite start/stop transients and reset must be included in the final channel.

The engineering window is therefore physical CD bandwidth well above the
confinement response and well below unwanted mode resonances, with extra control
faults smaller than the saved leakage. Fixed high-Q storage cavities may not
offer this detuning resource cheaply. Adding a tunable element can degrade their
lifetime. If that cost does not close, reject this enhancement and use the
unmodified measured-parity architecture with calibration and noisy outer QEC.

The leading control faults should be classified physically. An erroneous
detuning delta_Delta*n_j and a two-photon pump-tracking error are gauge-only in
the same local subsystem model. Their danger is gauge excitation, not an
automatic generic logical Pauli error per rotation. Nevertheless an unbalanced
fractional detuning error epsilon on a large CD drive gives a residual epsilon
Delta, so epsilon*Delta << confinement would reintroduce a demanding calibration
condition. Append the physical time reverse of the eight-rotation Eulerian cycle
to make every storage's detuning area zero. This preserves the parity signal and
doubles the rotation count. It can also suppress proportional calibration errors.

cd_miscalibration_audit.py solves the complete periodic affine linearized drift,
including the residual delta_Delta*c frequency term, rather than just dropping
that term. With the same n=8, G=1, gamma=10, nu=.2 and a 1% proportional CD
miscalibration, at Delta=50*k_conf the maximum single-mode coherent gauge
population is 2.289 for the unbalanced cycle and .434 for the time-reversed
balanced cycle. At Delta=200*k_conf the values are 6.395 and .437. The ideal
baseline is .0903. Thus balancing prevents growth with modulation speed in this
linearized example. The large unbalanced excursions are already outside a
trusted small-excursion model and are a failure signal, not accurate device
predictions. These deterministic mean results do not bound nonlinear or
stochastic high-energy tails. The balanced protocol's ideal-CD leakage spectrum
was recalculated: worst lower-weight power ratios are .06172, .002041, and
.00003616 at detunings 10, 50, and 200 times confinement. Thus the matched
balanced schedule gives about 490-fold suppression at detuning 50, at a cost of
16 paired rotations per period. The leakage spectrum still assumes perfect CD;
the separate 1% CD-error gauge calculation does not constitute a joint noisy
channel simulation. Do not combine them into a certified gate fidelity.

7. NO-JUMP AND FINITE-DURATION AUDITS / INFORMATIVE FAILURES

The separate reservoir audit is an exactly solvable effective model:
 H=(theta/2T) A + lambda A(b+b^dag), A=Z1Z2, J=sqrt(kappa) A b.
Controlled buffer parity U=P_+(A) tensor I + P_-(A) tensor parity_b maps both H
and J to a tensor product logical gate plus ordinary damped buffer. This proves
full Lindblad/no-jump factorization, not only a jump correction statement.

At kappa=1, lambda=.25, T=8, the |++> probe-state infidelities are:
 ordinary buffer loss: .475558, unrecovered by ringdown;
 corrected loss: .191181 at gate end, 1.48e-6 after ringdown time 12;
 corrected loss plus physical heating rate .001: .020072 after that ringdown;
 corrected loss plus buffer dephasing .15: 1.30e-6 after that ringdown.
Waiting longer under heating worsens the error. A 10% identity contamination
J=(A+.1 I)b produces .010969 conditional infidelity in the no-click branch even
after ringdown 24. Monitoring every photon does not certify this branch.

A second finite-bandwidth Gaussian meter audit includes a desired parity and
unwanted single-Z drives. With H=lambda(S+uZ1+vZ2)(b+b^dag), u=.05, v=.03,
lambda=.5, kappa=1 and sensing T=20, a high-confidence parity result can coexist
with .1029 dephasing inside its even sector. The no-click branch can have .444
conditional infidelity, although its probability is small. Fast pre-emission
modulation suppresses this; coarse post-emission echo cannot. These simpler
models are conceptual audits, not substitutes for the three-cat transfer above.

For an effective output jump sqrt(Gamma)(S+uZ1+vZ2), inside parity s the unwanted
coefficient is u+s v. The averaged within-sector dephasing rate is
Gamma(u+s v)^2, while L^dag L has a first-order state-selective term
2 Gamma s(u+s v) Z1. This explicitly locates the correlated no-jump filter.
Its integrated first-order contribution can cancel for balanced waveforms, but
irreversibly emitted quadratic which-state information remains unless filtered
before emission. Finite start/stop/reset boundaries require explicit treatment.

8. DURATION, DETECTORS, AND COST CONDITION

Use the measured output discrimination statistics, not an assumed perfect
projector. For coherent pointers beta_s= s beta and homodyne efficiency eta,
the ideal matched-filter assignment probability is

 q_assign = .5 erfc(sqrt(2 eta sum_j gamma_j integral |beta_j(t)|^2 dt)).

Heterodyne costs an additional quadrature-noise factor, which can be included
in effective eta. Thermal receiver noise changes this distribution and must be
measured or calculated. The source paper's small-nu steady rate is
Gamma_meas=(3n/2)g3^2 gamma/g2^2=6 nu^2 n^2 kappa2. This is a signal-rate
scale, not proof of an arbitrarily fast measurement near nu=1.

Finite reset/reconvergence takes a logarithmic time in the residual-correlation
target, divided by the actual drift gap. Thermal occupation by itself is not
always a reset failure: the necessary criterion is that residual helper-data
correlation and dangerous gauge tails are below their budgets. A helper may be
reused in a known thermal state if the complete instrument accounts for it.

As an alternative, genuinely independent noisy parity readouts with error q
can be majority-decoded. At q=.001, five repetitions give 9.985e-9 assignment
error; at q=.01, nine give 1.219e-8. Every repetition increases data phase-loss
exposure and reset cost. Independent failure cannot be assumed for a drifting
common pump or a detector left correlated with the data.

A useful conditional improvement criterion is

 p_control,new + p_extra_time + p_transients
      < (1-F_modulation) p_lower_weight_leakage,old,

while the new X-type fault contribution remains below the rare-bit budget.
Intrinsic storage photon loss is a direct Markov jump: a phase multiplier from
the rotations cancels inside D[a_j], so this mechanism does not suppress it.
Likewise, a lower-weight operator already coupled directly to an irreversible
Markov bath is outside the proposed filtering mechanism. It suppresses coherent
parasitic information transfer through the explicitly retained finite-response
buffers.

Increasing readout time is also limited by the rare bit process: a practical
assignment budget contains the Gaussian discrimination tail plus roughly the
parity-flip hazard times the full sensing duration, and detector/pump drift.
An arbitrarily small Gaussian tail is not an arbitrarily accurate physical
measurement.
The relevant total hardware comparison includes spare cat/storage modules,
buffers, nonlinear mixers, detector/readout channels, physical detuning actuators,
pump synchronization, routing, resets, and magic-state preparation. Bare
oscillator counts or a quoted outer-code threshold do not establish cheaper
universal computation.

9. LIMITS OF LITERATURE SHORTCUTS

Error-transparent and error-detectable bosonic gates are useful comparators, but
one cannot transfer a jump-only theorem to the no-jump problem. PRX 14, 031016
(2024), Fault-Tolerant Operation of Bosonic Qubits with Discrete-Variable Ancillae,
explicitly neglects bosonic no-jump terms in part of its formalism; PRX Quantum
4, 020354 (2023), Error-Detectable Bosonic Entangling Gates with a Noisy Ancilla,
treats no-jump effects separately from error closure. They do not automatically
certify the faulty-meter channels studied here.

Likewise, a blanket autonomous-memory claim must earn its recovery rate. Shtanko
et al., Quantum 9, 1804 (2025), analyze rate/size constraints on autonomous
decoders. Our construction uses ordinary repeated faulty QEC for the surviving
phase channel; it does not posit a constant-rate magical autonomous global
decoder. https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2025-07-22-1804/
