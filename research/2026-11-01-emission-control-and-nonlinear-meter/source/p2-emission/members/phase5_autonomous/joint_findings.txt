JOINT FAULTY-CONTROL CAT PARITY METER: CURRENT RESEARCH HANDOFF
2026-10-31. This file supersedes the numerical feasibility discussion in
findings.txt. All numbers are dimensionless model calculations, not measured
hardware specifications. Later convergence and alternating-control results
will be appended as they complete.

OUTCOME

A physically specified finite-bandwidth parity meter can suppress unwanted
within-parity information leakage even when the counterdiabatic (CD) actuator
and the buffers are faulty. The joint calculation retains the desired cubic
interaction, six lower-weight parasitic drives, three independent output ports,
hot helper jumps, finite efficiency and duration of homodyne measurement,
proportional CD error, and an explicitly branch-dependent odd actuator error.

The corrected exact-classical-mean/Hessian Gaussian instrument reduces the
phase-oracle-calibrated CNOT entanglement infidelity from 0.00468133 to
0.000527032, or 8.88 times. Without any extra phase calibration, the modulated
value is 0.000706869, still 6.62 times below the generously phase-corrected
unmodulated baseline. These statements concern the simulated meter component.

This is now backed by an actual full-cubic six-mode quantum calculation for a
within-parity pair. With storage cutoff3 and buffer cutoff2, the loss of that
pair's logical coherence is 0.01186180546 unmodulated and 0.000916487829
modulated (12.94 times reduction). No cubic Hamiltonian term is dropped in this
calculation. It uses a common moving coherent displacement to keep the quantum
fluctuation cutoff economical. Storage-cutoff4 convergence is in progress.

There is no demonstrated total architecture-cost win yet. Storage photon loss,
finite preparation/readout errors, added physical detuning controls, repeated
faulty outer checks, nonlocal pump correlations, and finite oscillator tails
must be charged. The strongest absolute nonlinear Duhamel estimate remains
vacuous, although an exact-mean expansion improves it substantially. The full
nonlinear calculation currently validates a targeted coherence pair, whereas
the complete noisy CNOT instrument is Gaussian. No weight-four-code threshold
is assigned to this weight-three hardware.

1. PHYSICAL DEVICE AND EXACT LOCAL LOGICAL SYMMETRY

Adopt the three-cat buffer-readout proposal in Vuillot, Ruiz, Guillaud and
Mirrahimi, arXiv:2605.24262 (2026):

 H = sum_j g2[(a_j^2-alpha^2)b_j^dag + h.c.]
     + g3(a1 a2 a3 + h.c.),
 L_cold,j=sqrt(gamma)b_j.

The existing damped buffers supply the measurement fields and entropy outlet;
an extra sum-frequency mixing pump produces the cubic interaction. A fresh
|+> cat, one M_ZZZ measurement and destructive X readout of the old target
implement a teleporting CNOT. The source's best code simulations use native
weight-four measurements, which are not supplied by this weight-three device.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.24262v1 .

In the local cat subsystem coordinates a_j=Z_j(alpha+c_j), all of H2 is gauge
only, while H3 is S=Z1 Z2 Z3 times a gauge polynomial. Buffer jumps, heating,
dephasing and their no-jump terms cannot distinguish labels inside a fixed S
sector. This statement includes the cubic gauge terms; it is not a Gaussian
claim. Finite-cat basis overlap and escaping the localized gauge sector limit
the subsystem description and are quantified separately below.

The relevant parasitics are

 V_z(t)=sum_R epsilon_R z_R [exp(-i theta_R+i phi_R)
            product_(j in R)(alpha+c_j)+h.c.],
 R in {1,2,3,12,13,23}, theta_R=sum_(j in R)theta_j.

These terms reveal proper subsets of the desired parity. They are the target
of phase modulation. Storage photon loss remains a genuine Z_j fault and is
not erased by this scheme.

2. PHYSICAL CONTROL AND JOINT PARAMETER POINT

Let R(t)=exp[-i sum theta_j(t) n_j]. Realizing
H_lab=R H R^dag+sum theta_dot_j n_j requires actual storage-frequency control,
not merely a virtual frame update. Exchange-pump phases track 2 theta_j;
buffer drives stay fixed. Opposite pair rotations preserve sum theta_j=0,
so the intended sum-frequency measurement pump stays fixed.

Generators A=(1,-1,0), B=(1,0,-1) each perform a pi rotation at speed Delta.
The first half of a cycle follows ABABBABA. The second half physically retraces
that path with reversed order and negative directions. Each cycle has16 paired
rotations, duration16*pi/Delta, returns all phases to zero, and has zero net
detuning area for each storage. Proper annihilation-monomial characters have
zero cycle average. Mixed creation-annihilation noise is a broader issue and
is not all included in this joint test.

Exact parameters:
 n=16, alpha=4, G=2 alpha g2=1, gamma=10;
 g2=0.125, g3=0.005, nu=0.2;
 kappa2=4g2^2/gamma=0.00625, kconf=2G^2/gamma=0.2;
 Delta=10=50 kconf;
 sixteen full cycles, Tdrive=80.4247719319;
 finite ringdown60; total modeled meter duration140.424771932;
 independent hot buffer gain rate h=0.01 in h D[b_j^dag];
 initial thermal gauge occupancy0.001 for all six modes;
 homodyne efficiency eta=0.5;
 epsilon_R=0.01*g3*alpha^(3-|R|), all six parasitics simultaneously;
 CD residual delta_j(z,t)=theta_dot_j(t)*(0.01+0.0001*z_j).

The 1% common residual is ordinary proportional detuning miscalibration and
is gauge-only at zero parasitic coupling. The 0.0001*z_j term is an explicit
odd, phase-tracking actuator contamination used as a stress model. Ordinary
number-detuning noise alone does not acquire z_j; that identification would
be physically incorrect. The stress term represents an additional branch-
distinguishing imperfection, not a generic inevitable form of CD error.

This operation uses256 paired pi rotations, an integrated homodyne record and
one finite ringdown interval. No new readout ancilla is introduced, but the
physical detuning actuator and synchronization with three exchange pumps are
real extra resources. Instantaneous changes of detuning slope are idealized;
finite waveform bandwidth, additional resonances and actuator-induced loss
have not been modeled. The operating point is not claimed to be demonstrated
by an existing device.

3. FULL GAUSSIAN INSTRUMENT, NOT ONLY OUTPUT POWER

For logical branches z,z', propagate the gauge cross-density sigma_zz', not
just its branch means or emitted power. Its trace W_zz'=Tr sigma_zz' includes
both the final gauge overlap and the trace over every emitted field. This is
the logical Schur/Gram channel. Covariance changes and correlated no-jump
backaction are retained.

Write H_z=1/2 r^T G_z r+d_z^T r+h_z. For a complex Gaussian Wigner function
with normalized mean m, covariance V and trace w, set
 DeltaG=G_z-G_z', Deltad=d_z-d_z', Deltah=h_z-h_z',
 A=Omega*(G_z+G_z')/2+A_loss, v=Omega*(d_z+d_z')/2,
 D_cross=D_loss+i Omega^T DeltaG Omega/4.
Then
 Vdot=A V+V A^T+D_cross-i V DeltaG V,
 mdot=A m+v-i V(DeltaG m+Deltad),
 (log w)dot=-i[1/2 m^T DeltaG m+1/2 Tr(DeltaG V)+Deltad^T m+Deltah].

This is exact for quadratic branch Hamiltonians and common linear Lindblad
operators. A direct24-level single-oscillator Fock check, including unequal
quadratic Hamiltonians, linear forces, cold loss and hot gain, agrees to
1.4e-13 in trace and1.1e-12 in means/covariances.

Append the actual commuting integrated homodyne coordinate Y. The equal sum
of the three buffer quadratures is measured, while all individual Lindblad
ports remain in the generator. Dark output combinations are traced and can
still leak information. The collection weight is -1/sqrt(3) per port during
the drive and decays as exp(-kconf*t) during ringdown. For each cross block,
the final Y marginal is a complex Gaussian, yielding a physical binary
instrument

 W_plus= w/2 * erfc[-m_Y/sqrt(2 V_YY)], W_minus=w-W_plus.

All instrument matrices were positive semidefinite to numerical precision;
the resulting CNOT Choi matrices have trace1 and nonnegative spectra to
roundoff. Destructive X assignment errors and ancilla preparation Z errors
are propagated through the circuit as actual faults, not ideal final recovery.

The no-cold-click instrument is calculated separately by deleting cold
recycling and retaining -{sum L^dag L,sigma}/2. Hot jumps remain unobserved.
In the Gaussian equations this amounts to DeltaG -> DeltaG-iQ and
Deltah -> Deltah-iq0, with the cold damping/diffusion recycling contribution
removed. This is a photon-counting alternative audit, not the homodyne record
used to implement the gate. The no-click branch is not a scalar inside S when
the proper-subset parasitics and odd actuator term are present.

4. CORRECT NONLINEAR MEAN AND HESSIAN

The original fixed expansion around a=z alpha,b=0 retains desired H3 pairing
but omits the H2 c^dag b force of the same order at the driven operating point.
The source-paper linearized pointer must not be extrapolated near nu=1.

For a symmetric classical branch a_j=z_j A_s, b_j=B_s with h=0,
 B_s=-(g3*s/(2*g2))*A_s^*,
 A_s/alpha=sqrt(1+3nu^2/4)-i*s*nu/2.
For nu=.2 this gives A_+=4.059556626-0.4i and B_+=-0.081191133-0.008i.
The full classical residual is6.6e-17. Unlike the fixed-idle expansion, this
solution has no spurious1/(1-nu^2) mean-amplitude singularity.

The corrected approximation integrates all eight exact classical branch
trajectories under H2+H3+parasitics+CD errors and cold/hot damping. It expands
the full Hamiltonian to its Hessian at those time-dependent trajectories.
Local buffer-induced squeezing g2 B_j^* c_j^2 is included together with the
H3 pair terms and the phase-dependent storage-buffer exchange. The omitted
Hamiltonian is now cubic only in centered quantum fluctuations:

 deltaH=g2 sum(delta c_j^2 delta b_j^dag+h.c.)
          +g3 S(delta c1 delta c2 delta c3+h.c.).

The Hessian was checked against finite differences (max5.5e-8 at step1e-4).
Gaussian physical means agree with the independently integrated exact
classical reference to1.2e-14 at the end.

The phase oracle gives each measurement outcome an arbitrary free diagonal
output unitary. It is deliberately generous to the unmodulated comparison;
it removes coherent parasitic phases so that a large raw phase error cannot
be mistaken for irreversible information leakage. The modulated raw gate is
also reported to show the benefit without requiring this extra correction.
The oracle is not assumed to be a cheap available physical gate.

For the earlier fixed-idle Gaussian model, separate fixed parasitic phases
[.3,1.7,-2.2,.9,-.6,2.6] instead of all-zero phases give a phase-oracle baseline
.004918031 and modulated .000514217. This demonstrates insensitivity to those
six relative phase offsets. It does not model independent time-varying phase
noise. Spatially correlated pump faults and temporal control noise need a
separate locality/spectrum analysis before a fault-tolerance claim.

After the finite ringdown, normalized cross-Gaussian means are below4.76e-6
in magnitude. Their covariance differs from the common thermal covariance
by at most9.19e-7 in an entry. These are residual memory diagnostics, not a
diamond-norm decoupling certificate. Photon exposure includes the ringdown;
it is not charged only during signal integration.

At the illustrative ratio r=kappa1/kappa2, the first-order probability of any

7. FULL NONLINEAR SIX-MODE CALCULATION

For the pair z=(+++),z'=(-+-), both with S=+, choose ONE common classical
reference A_j(t),B_j(t) driven by ideal H2,H3 and common CD error, excluding
the branch-distinguishing parasitic and odd-CD terms. Apply that same moving
displacement to both sides of the logical cross density.

The exact transformed common Hamiltonian includes
 sum_j [2g2 A_j c_j b_j^dag+g2 B_j^* c_j^2+g2 c_j^2 b_j^dag+h.c.]
 +sum_j delta_common,j c_j^dag c_j
 +g3 S[sum_(i<j) A_k c_i c_j+c1 c2 c3+h.c.].
The parasitics and odd detuning are evaluated with a_j=z_j(A_j+c_j), keeping
their constants, linear terms and quadratic terms. Linear terms from the
common Hamiltonian cancel exactly against the moving-frame derivative and
the common coherent shift of the cold/hot jumps. The transformed jumps are
sqrt(gamma)b_j and sqrt(h)b_j^dag. Their common output displacement is a
unitary on the traced environment, so dropping it does not change the logical
Gram element. It would matter for a photon-counting conditional instrument.

The cross density obeys the full Lindblad equation with unequal left/right
Hamiltonians. No cubic H2 or H3 term is dropped. At storage levels0,1,2 and
buffer levels0,1 the Hilbert space dimension is216. At storage levels0--3 it
is512. These are finite quantum calculations, not Gaussian closures.

Full216-state result after16 cycles plus60 ringdown:
 unmodulated W=-.499881010994+.852370850249i,
 |W|=.988138194539, visibility loss .011861805461;
 modulated W=.998103993416-.044229883785i,
 |W|=.999083512171, visibility loss .000916487829.

The corresponding corrected Gaussian pair losses are .011946492723 and
.000921518888, respectively. Both differ from the full216-state result by

8. FINITE CAT AND REMAINDER QUALIFICATIONS

The exact classical branch amplitude stays above3.96039 in magnitude. For the
local branch basis |z,k>=z^k D(z A)|k>, the off-diagonal Gram block is
 K_kl=(-1)^l <k|D(-2A)|l>.
At the minimum radius, its largest singular value for cutoffs3,4,6,8 is
4.54e-11,8.89e-10,1.38e-7,8.74e-6, respectively. Thus finite-cat overlap on
the low fluctuation cutoffs is negligible compared with the measured leakage.
This does not bound the probability of leaving that cutoff.

Actual corrected Gaussian snapshots in the common reference predict maximum
storage population at or above levels3,4,6,8 of1.21e-4,1.76e-5,1.06e-7,
6.92e-10. Buffer population at or above level2 is7.42e-6. These are Gaussian
snapshot diagnostics, not certified nonlinear continuous-time tail bounds.

An ordered Wick calculation of the cubic remainder gives
 integral sqrt(<deltaH^2>) dt about7.54 for the original idle-cat expansion;
 about2.127 after correcting the exact mean and Hessian. The maximum centered
instantaneous rms is .01955. These coarse sampled integrals remain above one,
so the generic absolute Duhamel certificate does not close. A relative bound
that respects exact within-parity symmetry could be much sharper. The full
Fock convergence test supplies targeted numerical evidence but does not turn
that failed absolute bound into a proof.
