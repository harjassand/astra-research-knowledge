SELECTED OPENING: A PHYSICAL RECEIVER FOR SMALL QUANTUM PREDICTIVE MEMORIES
1 November 2026. First-stage solo construction and selection.

The best supported next target is to make the optical predictive-compression
advantage operational with a finite, costed receiver. The broader capability
would be useful inference from weak noisy light using small coherent memories.
This stage establishes a conditional finite construction, not a working device,
historical novelty, practical superiority or a civilisation-changing discovery.

The external starting point was Zixuan He's three specified Glasgow preprints,
at their latest versions checked today. Their mechanisms were read before
searching Astra. Predictive compression supplies the task and a strong classical
converse; coherent-light certification exposes the cost of acquiring a channel
contract; photon-optimal noise shows why mixing a marginal is not erasing its
information. Exact versions and decisive passages are in SOURCES_AND_COMPARISON.

Construction already attempted

I replaced the ideal optical head/tail encoder by an actual Jaynes-Cummings
write, amplitude-damping storage and a reverse write into a fresh optical port.
The complete optical higher-photon tail is charged. A heterodyne record from
the returned light controls a passive collector; a single on/off detector per
block and a count across blocks complete a specified finite decision rule.
Correlation-to-displacement reception is prior work by Shi et al. (2023),
including phase-only alternatives. The attempted contribution here is its
finite qubit-write bridge, explicit causal circuits and finite-test accounting.

At the displayed model budgets, 3000 blocks of 32768 pairs give:
  K = 98,304,000 source pairs; accept a match at 369 or more detector clicks;
  missed-match probability <= 0.09642320;
  log(false match) <= -2947.1962 for the constructed quantum receiver;
  log(false match) >= -2754.5105 for every allowed classical block encoder
  with missed-match probability <= .1 and the same source-information budget.
The strict log margin exceeds 192.68 by outward 60-digit interval arithmetic.
The classical statement is conditional on He's tensor contraction/Prop. 5;
I read the relevant proof dependencies but have not independently audited them.
The component loss/noise/phase contracts are also assumptions, not measurements.

The comparison allows arbitrary classical alphabets, collective encoding and
collective observation measurements; it is stronger than a heterodyne-only
baseline. The source-information cap is substantive. It does not imply equal
electricity, hardware cost or usefulness. Removing that cap changes the task.

What changed the selection

A streaming one-oscillator collector is exactly causal, but uniform .9 total
transmission over this window would allow only about 3.2 parts-per-million loss
per step if the entire loss budget were spent there. A parallel balanced tree
has 15 loss stages instead, allowing at most about .7% loss per stage on that
same generous allocation. It needs 32767 combiners, parallel read modes, and
qubits held until their controls are ready. Neither hardware contract is earned.
Finite ADC rounding and a bad-record allowance are included; arbitrary memory
dephasing and arbitrary control faults are not hidden inside amplitude damping.

I also attempted the tempting non-Gaussian-noise combination. The one-photon
optimal bath at transmission 1/2 retains a resolved quantum branch, verified on
a reference. In an exact binary-source restriction a legal classical timeshare
ties the quantum relevance at the same information cost. Full thermal finite
diagnostics give tiny positive differences against that one classical encoder,
not an all-classical separation. This route has no earned replacement converse.
Transfer of the certification method to unrestricted smooth nonlinear system
discovery fails without a paid regularity bound: an arbitrarily narrow smooth
response can evade every finite probing schedule. This rejects that proposed
transfer, not a different field as a whole.

Why this target ahead of more gate work

The preserved autonomous-gate programme aims at the larger capability, but its
remaining interfaces include physical reservoirs, layout and enormous finite
controller overheads. This receiver gives a smaller explicit decision task,
an unusually strong incumbent and a falsifiable finite parameter region. It
still cannot claim practical advantage over existing Gaussian C-to-D receivers
or conventional sensing. Astra's accepted output-mode/capture results retain
their actual acceptance; their conditional interfaces do not supply this device.

Decisive remaining question

Can a bounded-speed, finite-precision qubit-to-optical collector, including
actual write/read ports, phase coherence, cold-input preparation, all retained
and discarded information, detection and full elapsed time, meet ANY strict
finite all-classical separation after matched resource costs are imposed?

The next sustained construction should answer that question for one complete
physical model, comparing the balanced tree, the streaming accumulator and the
best existing C-to-D architecture with the same ingredients. The current
parameter point is a testable contract to improve or falsify, not a reason to
assume a useful device exists. Fixed detector/heating floors also prevent
inferring the preprint's n-log(1/n) limit from this finite certificate.
