FINITE OPTICAL CORRELATION TEST: CONDITIONAL CONSTRUCTION
1 November 2026. Solo derivation; no independent audit of this new composition.
Natural logarithms. Trace norm means ||.||_1; probability changes are bounded
by half that norm. The theorem below is conditional on the stated physical
channels and He's all-block classical contraction, not on a finite simulation.

1. Task and source contract

Prepare K independent two-mode squeezed purifications of thermal tau_n on A,
with actual purifier C. A passes through a thermal attenuator of transmission
eta and added output occupation b>0, giving B of mean s=eta*n+b. A source
encoder acts on C before access to B. Under H0 the encoded memory and B are
matched. Under H1 they have the SAME marginals but are independent. Retained
input-correlated flags, side registers and random seeds count as memory.

The source-information charge is I(A^K:M), before the A-to-B relevance channel.
For each C, first apply vacuum loss lambda, then the physical write below.
Data processing gives actual cost <=
 R = g(n)+g(lambda*n)-g((1-lambda)*n),
 g(x)=(1+x)log(1+x)-x log x.
Before receiver processing the product memory has K qubits, dimension 2^K.
Fresh source-independent measurement ancillas are allowed to both contestants.
B is common observation access outside this predictive-memory cap. Temporary
raw optical storage and actual apparatus are not made free by that convention.

He, predictive compression, Prop. 5 and Appendix R, using Lemma 12, supplies
for EVERY normal classical block encoder at total source cost <=K R:
 alpha_C<=epsilon =>
 log beta_C >= -(K*c_n*R+log 2)/(1-epsilon),
 c_n=eta*log(1+1/(eta*n+b))/log(1+1/n).
This even allows an unlimited classical alphabet and any joint B readout.
Its tensor statement is essential; a single-copy Gaussian optimum would not
prove it. No independent review of that external tensor proof is claimed here.

2. Actual finite-dimensional write, storage and read

An initially ground qubit interacts through
 U_w=exp[-i theta_w (a sigma_+ + a^dag sigma_-)];
discard the OLD optical C port and compensate its known swap phase. On the
full optical |0>,|1> operator algebra this is exactly amplitude damping with
transmission eta_w=sin^2(theta_w). No projection onto that head is performed.
All higher-photon inputs undergo the same physical unitary and discarding.
Amplitude-damping qubit storage eta_s and reverse JC into a FRESH VACUUM optical
mode, transmission eta_r, give eta_M=eta_w*eta_s*eta_r on the head. The decoded
head channel agrees with ordinary bosonic vacuum loss eta_M on all four head
matrix units, hence with an arbitrary reference. The output predictive memory
before readout is still a qubit. No reset/tail flag is retained.

The numerical full-channel control used theta_w=.47*pi and post-write damping
eta_M/sin^2(theta_w), with eta_M=.9. Reverse read can be split from that damping
by choosing eta_s,eta_r in [0,1] with the required product. These are conditional
amplitude-damping and known-phase models. Generic dephasing is not included.
The physical write and read take at least theta_w/g_w and theta_r/g_r at bounded
available exchange rates. No actual g, preparation time or return delay is known.

3. A virtual Gaussian reference and a paid full-tail comparison

Put y=lambda*n. A thermal C after this loss has tail probability
 p_tail=Pr(N_C>=2)=[y/(1+y)]^2.
On a block of ell copies, joint head projector P has discarded mass
 t<=ell*p_tail. If E is the actual write/storage/read channel and G is virtual
bosonic loss eta_M, E and G agree on every operator supported on this joint
head. For any reference state rho,
 ||E(rho)-G(rho)||_1
 <= 2||rho-P rho P||_1 <=4 sqrt(t).
Thus any block outcome under H0 differs by at most 2 sqrt(ell*p_tail).
This is a source-specific tail bound for the actual source, not a uniform
diamond approximation on arbitrary optical inputs or an ideal tail reset.

4. Fully specified block receiver

Heterodyne every B after vacuum detection loss eta_H, with complex outcomes
z_i. Their marginal law is iid circular Gaussian of variance 1+s', where
 s'=eta_H*(eta*n+b).
For the virtual Gaussian memory, write
 y_m=eta_M*y,
 c^2=eta_H*eta*lambda*eta_M*n*(n+1),
 a=c^2/(1+s'), nu=y_m-a >=0.
Under H0 the conditional optical idlers are independent displaced thermal
states with mean displacement mu_i=c*z_i^*/(1+s') and thermal occupation nu.
Under H1 they are independent tau_(y_m), independent of the z_i.

A normalized passive mode selector aims for
 a_out=sum_i z_i*m_i / sqrt(sum_i |z_i|^2).
Allow squared mode alignment >=A except on B records of total probability
delta_sel. The selected mode then passes through constant transmission zeta,
independent Gaussian added occupation u, and on/off detection with independent
dark-click probability d. The total zeta includes collection and detection;
it is not allocated independently to each component. These are supplied
channel contracts, including their conditional freshness across blocks.

The virtual H0 no-click probability is bounded by the exact Gaussian integral
 alpha_G <= (1-d)/(1+zeta*nu+u)
             *[1+zeta*A*a/(1+zeta*nu+u)]^(-ell).
Reason: conditional displacement squared is at least
 A*c^2*sum|z|^2/(1+s')^2; the sum normalized by 1+s' is Gamma(ell,1).
Therefore the ACTUAL JC receiver has block click probability
 p0 >= 1-alpha_G-2 sqrt(ell*p_tail)-delta_sel.

For H1 a sharper argument avoids a block-length tail penalty. Each physical
JC qubit is independent and number diagonal. Its post-read excitation obeys
 p_e <= eta_M*y/(1+y)^2 + p_tail.
For ANY normalized B-record-dependent passive row, the selected photon mean
is p_e, because the idlers have no first or cross moments and are independent
of that record. It is not ell*p_e. Markov's inequality for nonvacuum gives
 p1 <= d+zeta*p_e+u.
Clipping, arbitrary passive row choice on a bad record and source-independent
added noise do not invalidate this H1 bound. This retains the crucial rare
false-click rate without charging an unnecessarily loose union error to it.

5. Finite test rather than an unspecified Stein threshold

Use L independent blocks, K=ell L pairs. Accept H0 when the number of clicks
is >=h. More generally fresh conditional per-block bounds p0,p1 suffice by
conditional moment bounds, provided all recorded histories satisfy the model.
Choose epsilon=.1 and h=floor(L p0-sqrt(2 L p0 log 10)), with h/L>p1.
Standard Bernoulli Chernoff inequalities give
 alpha_Q <= exp[-(L p0-h)^2/(2 L p0)],
 log beta_Q <= -L D_Ber(h/L || p1).
These finite inequalities, together with the above classical bound, give an
explicit conditional finite separation. Tiny numerical binomial tails are
diagnostics only. No asymptotic limit is used to select an unspecified sample.

Displayed instance:

 eta_M=eta_H=zeta=.9, A=.95, u=d=1e-7, delta_sel=1e-8,
 ell=32768, L=3000, K=98,304,000, h=369.
 R=.00042275288893993076 nats/pair; c_n=.05963589050445058.

 Outward interval results:

The inequalities compare the same actual independence-testing task and source
budget. They are not measured probabilities, a hardware advantage, or a useful
application's error target. Ninety-eight million pairs is a substantial cost.

6. Two concrete causal mode collectors and finite precision

Streaming: one accumulator c, S_0=0. After the CURRENT z_i is known, set
 S_i=S_(i-1)+|z_i|^2,
 c_i=sqrt(S_(i-1)/S_i)*c_(i-1) + z_i/sqrt(S_i)*m_i.
Use the complementary beamsplitter output as a discarded port. The first
nonzero step is a swap, dumping the old accumulator state. Induction gives
the desired normalized row exactly, with no future outcome or final norm
needed. This does not claim free elimination of entropy: that old state exits
through a real discarded port. Every read mode still requires its fresh input.

Parallel balanced tree: phase each leaf by arg z_i, and merge two child modes
with positive amplitudes proportional to their known subtree norms. Induction
gives the same selected row. A block of 32768 leaves uses 32767 combiners and
depth 15, plus leaf phases. Controls are known after the corresponding B
records; retaining the qubits/read modes until then is an explicit delay cost.
The independently assembled 32-mode one-particle circuit is unitary to 5.93e-16
and has selected-row error 8.34e-17. This checks algebra, not an optical device.

Equal loss tau at every tree stage gives total tau^15 and vacuum loss ports;
equal shared-accumulator loss tau each step gives coefficients
 tau^((ell-i+1)/2)*z_i/sqrt(S_ell).
Record-uniform transmission >=.9 in the bus requires tau^ell>=.9. Allocating
the ENTIRE .9 budget to the collector allows loss <=3.2154e-6 per bus step or
<=.0069995 per tree stage. Real detector/propagation losses reduce those limits.
One cannot take the tree's shallow depth while omitting its paths, parallel
ports, memories, switching or classical control bandwidth.

Example finite-ADC accounting: clip complex heterodyne amplitudes at
 Z=sqrt((1+s')log(2ell/delta)), delta=1e-8. The probability that any amplitude
exceeds Z is <=delta/2. Meanwhile sum|z_i|^2>=(1+s')ell/2 fails with probability
<=exp[-ell(log 2-1/2)] (<exp(-6329) here).
Round both real coordinates with step Delta=2Z/(2^12-1). On the good event
 ||z_hat-z||/||z|| <=Delta/sqrt(1+s')=:e.
The normalized rows have squared overlap >=[(1-e)/(1+e)]^2 >.98944.
A remaining single-particle circuit-row norm error <=.020028 preserves A=.95.
A sufficient conservative bound is <3.0562e-7 operator error per phase/combiner
across all 65535 elements. The sum of rare-record charges fits delta_sel=1e-8.
This earns finite representation precision in the abstract circuit. It does
not acquire actuator accuracy, finite slew, control energy or dephasing bounds.

7. What the executable checks establish

Full JC matrix-unit agreement on the head: residual <=4.45e-16 with reference
stability following from equality of the four matrix units. A nontrivial
Gaussian B:C pair at n=.02 is propagated through the actual JC channel:
trace norm to the virtual channel .00233290, versus analytic upper .0159363.
The Fock-cut mass bound is 2.03e-19; positivity, completeness and conditional
heterodyne means are checked. The full quantum pair is not replaced by a
classical displacement sample. The causal 32-mode circuit is also checked.

The bound grid has nine qualifying finite points. Removing concentration,
large detector thermal occupation, and zero coherent alignment fail the
chosen sufficient inequalities. The phase-only prior's finite concentration
bound also fails at this selected point. None of these failures is a universal
impossibility theorem for an alternative encoder or improved receiver.

8. Physical and asymptotic limits of the statement

The chain assumes actual ground-state qubits, amplitude-damping storage with
controlled phase, vacuum read ports, Gaussian fresh added noise, the source
and relevance channel, isolatable discarded ports and qualified measurements.
All returning quantum registers belong in the physical account. An old write
port secretly returning as coherent memory would change the compression task.
JC readout ancillas cannot become a free source of purity for a reused device.

The additive bad-record and tail charges are local to the H0 block analysis;
H1 uses the moment bound. Arbitrary implementation/model error cannot simply
be added once to beta_Q without potentially destroying its tiny finite gap.
Loss, dark counts and thermal occupation must be justified structurally and
uniformly; this finite arithmetic does not statistically certify e^-2947 tails.

At fixed nonzero detector/heating floor, n->0 eventually leaves the regime
where false clicks scale with n. The preprint's n log(1/n) versus n information
limit therefore cannot be inferred for this noisy receiver at fixed hardware.
The earned assertion is the displayed finite conditional parameter region.
No autonomous universal computer, ordinary-cost advantage or device is proved.
