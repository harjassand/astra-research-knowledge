Strong incumbents and the selection

CLASSICAL, same mathematical task: all collective classical source encoders
at K R, arbitrary alphabet, full collective B readout, same source knowledge,
and every source-independent ancilla/control technique. The converse includes
them all. In fact the comparator gets undetected B while our receiver pays
eta_H=.9. It is not restricted to a convenient Gaussian/heterodyne algorithm.

The information cap matters separately from hard bits. For example, allowing
classical cost g(n) rather than R gives the known single-copy relevance
g(eta*n+b)-g(b); at n=.0001 this is about 5.49e-5 nats/pair, above our guaranteed
binary-receiver information per pair. That is not a same-budget refutation,
but prevents advertising a hardware or electricity advantage from the cap.
No application's need for that precise information cap has been acquired.

QUANTUM, existing optics: Gaussian optical-idler C-to-D architectures avoid
the JC head/tail approximation, while lacking a hard one-qubit-per-pair output
in their usual form. Give them the same improved paths, fields, controllers
and Astra techniques. Our current result establishes neither less full cost
nor better useful performance. The possible contribution is the finite qubit
interface and its attainable physical region, not the collector principle.

THEOREM/RESOURCES_AND_COMPARISON. It supplies a solo conditional finite-qubit
controller/latched-CNOT composition with generic modeled local faults, ideal

The native accepted memory/asynchronous-CNOT and scheduled-Clifford incumbents
retain their acceptance. Reusing our gate theorem would not make its fresh
reservoirs, encoded qubits or optical transduction free. The smaller optical
test is selected for a sharper finite falsification, not proven greater impact.

DIFFERENT FIELD genuinely considered: nonlinear response discovery / automated
experimental design. Try transferring the coherent certification multiscale
probe idea to testing zero response against a unit-height C-infinity bump
f_theta of half-width w, theta in an interval J of length one inside the
bounded-energy input domain. For any T adaptive probes, before the first hit
their law is the zero-response law. Averaging the union hit probability over
theta gives at most 2 T w, so some centre has total hit probability <=2 T w.
All inputs can have energy <=1. As w->0 no finite uniform query guarantee
follows from mean input energy alone. A derivative/regularity or structural
identifiability bound is an additional resource. CPTP/coherent analyticity in
He's proof is not a generic property of scientific black boxes. This closes
that particular proposed transfer; it is not an exhaustive comparison across
science or a hardness theorem for chemistry/system identification.
