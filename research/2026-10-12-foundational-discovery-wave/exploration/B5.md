## Candidate 1 — H10(ℚ) by a five-contact height obstruction

1. **North Star.** Settle the pure existential decision problem over ℚ: no terminating algorithm decides, for arbitrary (f\in\mathbb Z[X_1,\ldots,X_n]), whether (f=0) has a solution in ℚⁿ. This is stronger than undecidability of the full first-order theory of ℚ and than undecidability after adjoining height predicates.

2. **Exact question.** Does there exist a Turing machine that halts on every integral polynomial (f) and correctly answers “yes” iff ∃(x\in\mathbb Q^n\) (f(x)=0)? The pinned OpenAI/math family 004 claims the negative answer. This report treats it as a source claim, not a verified resolution.

3. **Importance.** A correct proof would close the central pure-existential undecidability problem for the rational field. Its exact quantifier boundary matters: Robinson's first-order undecidability, Koenigsmann-style definitions, and a result with extra height comparisons do not automatically yield an existential formula in the ring language.

4. **Obstruction.** The proof's key imported input is a five-rational-point height estimate. For fixed five points (C\subset\mathbf P^1(\mathbb Q)), finite exceptional primes (S), and (M_S(s)) the squarefree product of primes with an odd normalized contact order to at least one point of (C), it claims
   \[
   h(s)\le H M_S(s)^c\quad(s\notin C).
   \]
   The construction uses a quaternionic Shimura cover, a QM family of abelian surfaces, odd-contact ramification, a modular Galois representation, uniform conductor control, and Faltings/theta-height comparisons. The most consequential dependency is the companion claim of odd, regular 2-adic Fontaine–Mazur modularity for (G_{\mathbb Q}), including residual representations with no residual-image restriction. Neither this chain nor the companion theorem is in the Lean formalization catalogue. The repository-wide statement that a fraction of top-line results are formalized cannot be transferred to this theorem.

5. **Missing capability.** Independently establish the five-point estimate with fixed (H,c), or locate a precise counterexample in its interface. Audit especially: (i) the Shimura quotient really has the five required rational branch values and fixed level; (ii) odd contact controls every new ramified prime of the splitting field; (iii) the relevant 2-adic representation satisfies the exact modularity hypotheses and yields the weight-two factor; (iv) the local conductor exponent at 2 is uniformly bounded as the contact radical varies; (v) the isogeny-degree and Faltings-to-base-height bounds retain constants independent of (s). Separately audit that each finite rational test is genuinely a pure existential polynomial query over ℚ and that compactness does not strengthen the assumed oracle's quantifiers.

6. **Substantive first investigation (reconstruction and transfer check).** The reduction encodes a root tuple in a recursively axiomatized ring (R\hookrightarrow{}^*\mathbb Q), with an additive injection \(\eta:R\to{}^*\mathbb Z\) obtained from a rank-one elliptic subgroup; it deliberately does **not** assume \(\eta(ab)=\eta(a)\eta(b)\). Let \(B=\max(1,|\eta(a_j)|)\), \(D=\max(1,\deg f)\), and \(\delta=f(\eta(a_1),\dots,\eta(a_n))\). If \(\delta\ne0\), the transferred polynomial bound gives \(|\delta|\le C_f B^D\). Choose one ordinary precision \(K\ge cD\). For every (A\in R), its universal contact-representation axiom supplies three slopes; the local comparison makes each contact radical satisfy (M_K(s_i)^K\mid\delta), with the **same** \(\delta\) for every (A). Hence
   \[
   h(s_i)\le H M_K(s_i)^c\le H C_f^{c/K}B^{cD/K}=O_f(B).
   \]
   The three-fraction representation then gives (h(A)=O_f(B)) uniformly in (A). Applying this to the ring entries representing (x(T_{a_j})=x(\eta(a_j)P)), while the fixed nontorsion point has (h(x(nP))=2\widehat h(P)n^2+O(1)), forces (B=O_f(1)). Each nonstandard integer \(\eta(a_j)\) is therefore an ordinary integer, and the encoded root yields an ordinary integer zero. If every test succeeds, the assumed rational oracle would thus certify an integer zero; running the failed-test search in parallel with ordinary integer enumeration would decide H10(ℤ), contradiction. This is a coherent *conditional reconstruction* of the transfer, not independent verification of the arithmetic lemmas. I found no small counterexample in this pass; the decisive gate remains the new height/modularity input and the detailed finite-test audit.

## Candidate 2 — Hyperbolic failure of direct finiteness from Astra N129

1. **North Star.** Produce (or rule out) a concrete torsion-free hyperbolic group (G) and finite-support (a,b,c\in\mathbb F_2[G]) with (ab=1), (ac=0), and (c\ne0). Such a witness forces (G) to be nonsofic by the arbitrary-field direct-finiteness theorem for sofic group algebras.

2. **Exact question.** Does the N129 parity-graph and disk-filling construction preserve an explicit group-ring witness while yielding a torsion-free hyperbolic presentation? The algebraic interface is precise: (ab=1), (ac=0), (c\ne0) implies (e=1-ba=ab-ba) is an idempotent. Then (ec=c\ne0), so (e\ne0); and (e=1) would give (ba=0), whence (b=b(ab)=(ba)b=0), contradicting (ab=1). Thus (e) is proper and the ring is not directly finite. Elek–Szabó's theorem then rules out soficity ([primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0305440)).

3. **Importance.** A valid finite-support certificate plus explicit hyperbolic presentation would connect a concrete algebraic obstruction to a major open geometric-group boundary, rather than inferring nonsoficity from a different CAT(0) or rank construction. It would also give a clean test of how much group-ring structure survives hyperbolic approximation.
