# C2 — Interface-complete certificates for global scientific computations

**Stage I status:** a strong, mathematically precise baseline and a useful no-go boundary; no new algorithm or novelty claim established. The independent test cases are classical Ising ground states and electrical-network response, not the Astra program.

## 1. North Star

Replace a monolithic global solution by local summaries that retain exactly the information a downstream computation needs, together with a short, checkable proof that the summaries compose soundly. The representation must expose its interface state, approximation error, and witness-synthesis cost. “Local checks” alone are insufficient: the interface has to carry the global invariant that local pieces otherwise lose.

The most concrete target is an Ising ground-state energy on a graph whose global spin assignment is inaccessible by enumeration. A boundary energy table is a task-specific sufficient statistic: it records, for each separator spin assignment, the minimum energy achievable inside a region. A certified table can be composed with neighboring tables and can carry both the optimum value and a minimizing spin assignment.

## 2. Exact question

For a classical Ising instance with integer couplings,

\[
H(x)=\sum_{\{u,v\}\in E}J_{uv}x_ux_v+\sum_{v\in V}h_vx_v,
\qquad x_v\in\{-1,+1\},
\]

can we synthesize a compact, independently checkable family of boundary profiles that yields a certified interval \([L,U]\ni E_0=\min_x H(x)\), while avoiding the full \(2^n\) configuration search? For width \(w\), exact tree-decomposition messages already answer “yes” in \(O(n2^w)\) arithmetic operations. The research question is whether a **certified compressed profile** can extend that advantage to physically meaningful instances whose treewidth is large but whose separator energy profiles have smaller task-specific structure.

For a rooted tree decomposition with bags \(X_t\), parent separator \(S_t=X_t\cap X_{p(t)}\), and each unary/pair term assigned to one bag containing its scope, define

\[
F_t(\sigma_{S_t})=
\min_{x\,\text{in the interior of }t}
\sum_{a\text{ assigned in the subtree of }t} H_a(x_{\mathrm{scope}(a)}).
\]

The exact recurrence is

\[
F_t(\sigma_{S_t})=
\min_{\sigma_{X_t\setminus S_t}}
\left[H_t(\sigma_{X_t})+\sum_{c\text{ child of }t}
F_c(\sigma_{X_c\cap X_t})\right].
\]

At the root, \(F_r(\varnothing)=E_0\). Store an argmin for each table entry to reconstruct an optimal configuration. The recurrence is an explicit local certificate: a verifier recomputes each entry from its bag and child tables rather than trusting a claimed global optimum.

## 3. Importance

Ising ground states are a standard model for frustrated magnets and spin-glass optimization, and provide exact baselines for heuristic and annealing methods. General instances are hard: Barahona proves NP-hardness for 2D models with a magnetic field and for 3D models. Yet low-treewidth instances admit exact computation in time linear in the number of bags and exponential only in separator width. The representation therefore changes the relevant scale from \(2^n\) to \(n2^w\) on the promised class, while also producing a proof object and an optimizing witness.

The same interface principle has a second scientific-computing realization in resistor networks. For conductance matrix \(C\), incidence matrix \(B\), and Laplacian \(L=BCB^\top\), a feasible unit flow \(f\) from \(s\) to \(t\) and a potential \(\phi\) with \(\phi_s-\phi_t=1\) certify

\[
\frac{1}{D(\phi)}\le R_{st}\le U(f),\qquad
U(f)=f^\top C^{-1}f,\quad D(\phi)=\phi^\top L\phi.
\]

Indeed, \(1=(Bf)^\top\phi=f^\top B^\top\phi\le\sqrt{U(f)D(\phi)}\); Thomson’s and Dirichlet’s principles give the two bounds. If \(U(f)D(\phi)\le1+\epsilon\), the interval is multiplicatively \((1+\epsilon)\)-tight. For an unweighted graph, commute time is \(2mR_{st}\), so this certifies a global random-walk quantity from edge- and vertex-local values. This is an excellent auditability example, but existing Laplacian solvers already compute the quantity nearly linearly, so it is weaker as a complexity-breakthrough candidate.

## 4. Obstruction

Independent local marginals need not describe any global state. On the antiferromagnetic odd cycle \(C_{2k+1}\), let edge cost be \(1[x_u=x_v]\). Every edge wants opposite endpoint spins. The local pair distributions placing probability \(1/2\) on each of \((+,-)\) and \((-,+)\) have mutually consistent uniform one-vertex marginals and report total cost zero. But no global assignment can satisfy every edge: the number of sign changes around a cycle is even, so the true minimum cost is one. The missing information is cycle parity. Thus “locally consistent” is not “globally realizable”; an interface state must preserve the obstruction.

On general graphs, exact Ising ground-state computation is NP-hard, so no uniform polynomial-time certificate synthesizer can solve every instance unless \(P=NP\). Even on structured instances, the full separator profile has \(2^{|S_t|}\) entries. A decomposition supplied by the input is an assumption that must be charged separately from finding or verifying it. Finally, verification can be cheap relative to search only when witness synthesis is also cheaper; a short verifier does not provide the witness.

## 5. Missing primitive

The missing primitive is a **certified compressed boundary-profile compiler**. For each subproblem boundary \(S\), it would produce compact lower/upper functions \(L_S,U_S\) such that

\[
L_S(\sigma)\le F_S(\sigma)\le U_S(\sigma)\quad\text{for every }\sigma\in\{-1,+1\}^{S},
\]

with a small certified gap, without enumerating all \(2^{|S|}\) assignments. Composition is stable in the sup norm: replacing a child profile \(F\) by \(\widetilde F\) with \(\|F-\widetilde F\|_\infty\le\eta\) changes a parent’s min-plus recurrence by at most \(\eta\); errors from multiple children add at most \(\sum_c\eta_c\). Thus local interval profiles would yield a global energy interval with an explicit error budget. The unsolved part is a useful compact representation and a local proof rule that certifies its bound without first solving the original subproblem.

Potential structure parameters to test include sparse frustration across separators, low interaction rank across a cut, and repeated or symmetry-equivalent boundary patterns. Each must be tested in the bit model: low algebraic rank by itself may still leave exponentially many distinct weighted spin sums when couplings are binary-encoded. The primitive should return an energy interval and (when the upper bound is attained) a full spin witness; it should state `UNKNOWN` or widen the interval when the certificate cannot close.

## 6. First substantive attack

**Exact baseline.** On a width-\(w\) tree decomposition, the recurrence above uses at most \(2^{w+1}\) states per bag. It certifies the exact optimum, and stored argmins recover the global configuration. The message is a domain-specific sufficient statistic whose entries encode every possible boundary condition.

**Approximate extension lemma.** Suppose each child profile is replaced by an interval \([L_c,U_c]\) satisfying \(U_c(\sigma)-L_c(\sigma)\le\eta_c\) for all separator assignments. Apply the same bag recurrence separately to the lower and upper profiles. Since addition preserves order and pointwise minima are 1-Lipschitz in sup norm, the parent profile interval has width at most \(\sum_c\eta_c\). Recursing to the root gives a certified ground-energy interval of width at most the sum of local profile gaps. This is a direct proof of composition; it does not establish a compact profile representation.

**No-go diagnostic.** The odd-cycle example defeats edge marginals but is repaired by a boundary table carrying one spin (or equivalently a parity state) around the cycle. This separates a real representation failure from a generic failure of locality: the right separator state makes the computation exact in linear time. The decisive generalization is to find a similarly small, checkable state for a high-treewidth physical family.

**Secondary calibration case.** For effective resistance, the flow/potential certificate above has explicit local checks, but the full certificate is \(\Theta(m+n)\) rational values. Kron reduction already composes boundary response maps by Schur complements. A real follow-up would have to show a cost reduction in *certificate acquisition* or incremental updates, not merely restate this duality or Kron reduction.

Novelty assessment: the tree-decomposition DP, electrical primal/dual bounds, local resistance estimation, and Kron reduction are established. I found no novelty basis for claiming any of those mechanisms as new. The candidate gap is specifically a **compact proof-carrying boundary profile with efficient synthesis and compositional error bounds outside bounded-treewidth classes**. This is a research question, not a result.

## 8. Decisive next step

Pick one physical family with high treewidth but a plausible small interface statistic (for example, sparse-frustration Ising instances or repeated boundary couplings). Freeze a representation before benchmarking. Attempt a theorem with four explicit obligations: (1) a profile encoding of size \(o(2^w)\) on that family; (2) a local checker proving pointwise lower/upper bounds without optimizing the subproblem; (3) a composition theorem with a stated global error budget; and (4) a witness-synthesis and bit-cost bound charged from raw couplings through final spin assignment. First construct the smallest counterexample to the proposed compression—binary-encoded couplings and odd-cycle parity are immediate stress tests. Stop the line if checking the profile is as hard as the original ground-state problem, if the representation silently imports the optimizer, or if acquisition/precision costs erase the claimed speedup. A passing result would be a concrete proof-carrying separator primitive; otherwise retain the exact bounded-treewidth baseline and report the no-go boundary.
