# Retained section-cycle flag

30 October 2026. Clarifying convention for the frozen full closure candidate, not a new estimate.

The actual processor retains whether the initial high connector phase has been left. Starting at active B=q does not itself end a cycle, and a fast seed or dormant transition at B=q does not create a fictitious new section visit. The first connector event always changes B and sets the flag. Thereafter a new entry to ACTIVE B=q is a section return. Inactive B=q entries are designated and physically seeded as specified in the earlier V2 repair.

A dormant Green used inside the processor has exactly this same flag and active-section stopping convention. Its terminal bound follows from the larger reference reward counting all actual B=q jump entries and the true seed terminal reward. It does not rely on stopping immediately at the input B=q. A seed terminal carries B, the flag, and accumulated finite rare-degree counters to the positive-D processor.

This convention leaves every frozen source unchanged and prevents seed/reseed loops at the original B=q from being miscounted as completed cycles.
