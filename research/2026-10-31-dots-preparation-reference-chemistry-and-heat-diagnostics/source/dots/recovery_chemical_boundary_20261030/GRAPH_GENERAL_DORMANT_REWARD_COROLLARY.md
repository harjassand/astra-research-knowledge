# General dormant reward and the activation qualification margin

30 October 2026. Unreviewed algebraic corollary of the tagged-cohort proof; no original source is edited.

Under that note's exact assumptions and stopping convention, let

    V_p=E[(1+A_sigma)^(-p); dormant entry at sigma].

For every fixed p>=a0, the same predictable pre-depletion compensator gives

    V_p <= C_p (b)_q x^(a0-p) U + C n^M exp(-c x),
    x=1+a_in.

Indeed the dormant-entering jump only occurs at D=1. Its physical rate times reward is bounded by

    C (b)_q A^(a0+1-p)
      <= C_p (b)_q x^(a0-p) A

before cohort depletion, because A>=c x and a0-p<=0. The post-depletion reward is at most one and costs its actual probability. The initial-holding lower bound and x>=C log^2(2+n) absorb the error. Thus on that common good set

    V_p/[(b)_q U] <= C_p x^(a0-p).

The two required cases are p=m=a0+2 (ratio O(x^-2)) and p=m-1=a0+1 (ratio O(x^-1)). No fresh small-time lower occupation law is assumed.

If an independently earned dormant continuation has physical time <=C f_m/(b)_q and A-occupation <=C f_(m-1)/(b)_q, composition at the actual first dormant entrance gives additional time <=C U/x^2 and additional occupation <=C U/x. Therefore the full first-connector episode retains T_full/U_full<=C/x, and all but O(U_full/x) activation occupation belongs to the initial positive-D part (or a separately qualified initial high-A dormant part). This is exactly the qualification margin needed before constructing a negative phase word; it is not itself that word or a full-cycle proof.
