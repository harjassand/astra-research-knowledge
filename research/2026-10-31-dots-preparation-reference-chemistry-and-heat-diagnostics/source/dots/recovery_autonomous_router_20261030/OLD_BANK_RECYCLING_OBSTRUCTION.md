# Next substantive seam: can the old bank be reused while the receiver remains protected?
30 October 2026. Root new attack, not an accepted extension of preset transfer.

The preset-bank theorem leaves the whole paired native memory live. It has not given a detached output or an old bank freely usable by a later algorithm. The following exact algebra isolates the added cost rather than assuming decoupling.

Let a physical bounded Lindblad/ Hamiltonian operation act ONLY on old Q, without changing routing labels. In canonical G coordinates its operator is

 A_tilde = P0 A_Q + (Pe+P1) A_B,

where canonical B is spare. A canonical-memory observable F_Q (including native workspace/tags) is clock-independent. Since the clock projectors are orthogonal, the complete dissipative gain A_tilde† F_Q A_tilde has NO cross-clock blocks. The generator acts on F_Q exactly as

 L_old^*(F_Q) = P0 L_A,Q^*(F_Q).

The complementary branch is identity on canonical protected Q and cancels against its complete loss. The same identity holds for the Hamiltonian commutator. This is stronger than bounding the whole old-bank operation as weak noise: only unfinished0 contributes protected-data damage. It retains quantum coherence because the observable is block diagonal, not because the state was measured.

However L_old need NOT annihilate the ROUTER witness R, which has 0/e coherences. Strong old-bank operations entangle/dephase these branches and can slow settlement. An unbounded rate produces a quantum-Zeno obstruction; the new resource contract must bound old-bank operation strength. Moreover a product of a native memory witness and a clock witness is not justified by their marginal drifts. F and R commute as operators in canonical coordinates, but the controlled old-bank channel creates mixed gain terms in their product. This is the exact missing estimate.

Possible constructive route: use a local fast-return witness with all bounded old-bank operations included, and a positive credit charging their defect-supported canonical-energy injection. The existing bounded-support CP short-time/resolvent machinery may provide that witness. It must be proved on the actual full joint state, not by multiplying an independent mode probability by an error rate. If this closes uniformly at finite operation strength, continuous reuse of the old bank might be allowed at a paid noise/settling cost; that would be a more consequential interface than another fixed dressed gate.

Even such a theorem would not supply arbitrary repeated routing or gate composition. A new encoded input on the old bank creates an additional logical factor; it requires its own protection/causal contract. The identity above concerns protection of the original input, with any new source retained as auxiliary quantum information.
