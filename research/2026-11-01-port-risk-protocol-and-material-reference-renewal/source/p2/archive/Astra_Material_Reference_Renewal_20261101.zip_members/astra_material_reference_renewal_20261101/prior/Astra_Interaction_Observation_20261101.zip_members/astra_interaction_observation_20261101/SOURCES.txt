The following primary research supplies the strongest existing constructions and

1. Schuetz et al., High-fidelity hot gates for generic spin-resonator systems,
   Physical Review A 95,052335 (2017).
   https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.95.052335
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.01614
   Conventional spin-dependent geometric gate through a thermal resonator;
   initial hot-state independence is already established. Thermal decoherence
   and data storage costs remain. Our question is the continuing bath and observer.

2. Mueller, Luoma and Strunz, Geometric phase gates in dissipative quantum dynamics,
   Physical Review A 102,032611 (2020).
   https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.102.032611
   https://arxiv.org/html/1907.08033v2
   Full primary HTML read. Dissipation-compensating forces restore closure and
   geometric phase; this does not by itself remove all coherence lost to outputs.

3. Haddadfarshi and Mintert, High fidelity quantum gates of trapped ions mediated
   by a dissipative bus mode, New Journal of Physics 18,123007 (2016).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.05814
   Strong conventional polychromatic pulse design. The force/cost comparison
   cannot be made only against one unoptimized circular pulse.

4. Sabuncu, Filip, Leuchs and Andersen, Environment-assisted quantum-information
   correction for continuous variables, Physical Review A 81,012325 (2010).
   https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.81.012325
   https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.3546
   Established environmental measurement, optimized Gaussian feedforward,
   inefficient collection and thermal-excess-noise replacement. Thermal saturation
   of a conditional quadrature variance is not a new discovery here. A coherent-
   state information claim is not promoted into arbitrary-state unit fidelity.

5. Szigeti, Carvalho, Morley and Hush, Ignorance Is Bliss: General and Robust
   Cancellation of Decoherence via No-Knowledge Quantum Feedback,
   Physical Review Letters 113,020407 (2014).
   https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.113.020407
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.4493
   Actual monitored environment is a resource. General non-Hermitian coupling
   can require a conjugate engineered reservoir and access to its outputs.
   Monitoring a separate cold port does not supply those operations.

6. O'Connor, Albarelli and Genoni, Homodyne Unravelling and Parameter Estimation
   in Squeezed Thermal Baths, arXiv:2610.37438 (27 October 2026).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37438
   https://arxiv.org/html/2610.37438v1
   Full primary HTML read. Physical thermal-output homodyne separates monitored
   and unmonitored operators; it does not expose an arbitrary bath purification.
   For a suitable Hermitian dephasing displacement a no-knowledge quadrature can
   be fully informative about the random phase. The oscillator operator itself
   has different unmonitored terms. Our closed-loop reduction is derived first.

7. Frisk Kockum, Tornberg and Johansson, Undoing measurement-induced dephasing
   in circuit QED, Physical Review A 85,052318 (2012).
   https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.85.052318
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.2386
   Strong physical predecessor for phase reversal with finite detection and
   bandwidth. Already preserved as a comparator for the returning-error sensor.

8. de Lange et al., Reversing Quantum Trajectories with Analog Feedback,
   Physical Review Letters 112,080501 (2014).
   https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.112.080501
   Experimental analog correction of measurement-induced dephasing; observer
   efficiency and bandwidth are real costs. We claim no new feedback principle.

9. Wiseman and Milburn, Quantum theory of optical feedback via homodyne detection,
   Physical Review Letters 70,548 (1993).
   https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.70.548
   Feedback changing both drift and diffusion is established theory. The present
   question is the specified retained geometric phase and inaccessible thermal
   input at bounded interaction cost, not the existence of feedback Hamiltonians.

10. Grimsmo, Combes and Baragiola, Quantum Computing with Rotation-Symmetric
    Bosonic Codes, Physical Review X 10,011058 (2020).
    https://journals.aps.org/prx/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevX.10.011058
    https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.08071
    Self-/cross-Kerr logical gates and encoded correction are established. The
    explicit small binomial code in the supplement shows why continuous
    projection/HNLS reasoning is not a universal fixed-angle gate obstruction.
    Error-free auxiliary operations in a mathematical comparison are not assumed
    to be a physically completed recovery service here.

11. Cernotik and Hammerer, Measurement-induced long-distance entanglement of
    superconducting qubits using optomechanical transducers,
    Physical Review A 94,012340 (2016), arXiv:1512.00768.
    https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00768
    https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.00768
    Strong cold optical observation of a thermal mechanical interface. The task
    is postselected entanglement; its cooperativity includes thermal occupancy.
    It does not provide the same deterministic unknown-input gate at fixed
    resources as temperature diverges.

12. Barrett and Milburn, Quantum-information processing via a lossy bus,
    Physical Review A 74,060302(R) (2006), arXiv:quant-ph/0607206.
    https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0607206
    Coherent bus, conditional rotations and parity measurements under vacuum
    loss are different interactions/noise resources from a continuously hot
    linear-force mediator. Its loss tolerance is a real alternative, not a
    counterexample with the same allowed operations.

13. Lopez and Ratanov, On the asymmetric telegraph processes,
    Journal of Applied Probability 51,569-589 (2014).
    https://doi.org/10.1239/jap/1402578644
    Classical first-passage machinery underlying the unknown-interaction
    snapshot. Exact event observation and a physical stopping/handoff operation
    remain separately required.

14. Zhou, Zhang, Preskill and Jiang, Achieving the Heisenberg limit in quantum
    metrology using quantum error correction, Nature Communications 9,78 (2018).
    https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-017-02510-3
    Current checks also included Conlon et al. arXiv:2608.00171 and recent
    subsystem/finite-bias sensing constructions preserved in the prior archive.
    Signal-parallel sensor faults remain a physical obstruction; they cannot be
    hidden by relabeling controller operations as perfect.
