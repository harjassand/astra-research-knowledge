STRONGEST CONVENTIONAL COMPETITOR: A WARM, LOSSY GEOMETRIC BUS
1 November 2026, Brisbane. Independent competitor analysis for the root investigation.
This file preserves the previous sensing obstruction and then analyzes the NEW objective.
It does not assert priority for a new gate. The explicit comparator is a synthesis of
established longitudinal/geometric gates, Gaussian environmental correction, and
physical thermal-output homodyne filtering. Sources are in warm_competitor_sources.txt.

OBJECTIVE AND AVAILABLE OPERATIONS

Apply exp(i phi S^2) to arbitrary input data, including its entanglement with a
reference, using a harmonic mediator and bounded linear forces S(f a^dagger+f* a).
Charge all bath access, finite detector noise/bandwidth, control calibration and data
storage. Do not equate a Lindblad decomposition with access to its purification.
S is a fixed Hermitian sum of mutually commuting single-qubit operators. The simple
scalar-S model is essential: genuinely distinct noncommuting spin forces need a
separate analysis. There is no assumed protected ancillary qubit.

1. WHAT THE ORDINARY GATE ALREADY DOES

With H=Delta a^dagger a+g S(a+a^dagger), at T=2 pi m/Delta the oscillator displacement
closes and the exact propagator is exp[i 2 pi m(g/Delta)^2 S^2] times free oscillator
evolution. Initial thermal occupation does not itself spoil the gate. This is the
standard hot geometric/longitudinal gate; 'no ground-state cooling' alone is not an
opening.

For the lossy problem use the exact passive Gaussian dilation, rather than discard
the environmental modes. In a rotating frame, let

    alpha_dot(t) = -kappa alpha(t)/2 - i f(t),    alpha(0)=0.

The full propagator has the form exp(i phi S^2) D(S xi) U_free, where the final-bus
displacement is alpha(T), the bath-output displacement profile is

    beta(t)=sqrt(kappa) alpha(t),
    phi = -Re integral f(t) alpha(t)* dt
        = Im integral alpha(t)* alpha_dot(t) dt,
    ||xi||^2=|alpha(T)|^2+kappa integral |alpha(t)|^2 dt.

The sign of phi changes under force/phase convention; the area and error relations do
not. These identities follow directly by solving the driven passive Gaussian unitary.
They are not a perturbative adiabatic elimination.

An explicit closed, finite-strength comparator is

    alpha(t)=r[exp(i Omega t)-1],     T=2 pi m/Omega,
    f(t)=i[alpha_dot(t)+kappa alpha(t)/2],
    phi=2 pi m r^2,
    B:=||beta||^2=2 kappa r^2 T=2 kappa phi/Omega,
    max|f|=r[sqrt(Omega^2+(kappa/2)^2)+kappa/2].

The last equality is the exact maximum over a complete loop. It charges compensation
of damping. Switching this ideal envelope on/off has finite discontinuities in f;
specified actuator slew limits require smoothing and re-solving closure and area.
This is a conventional force-loop construction, not an instantaneous-pulse gate.

For fixed phase and force bound g, the weak-damping many-loop family has T proportional
to sqrt(m) and B proportional to 1/sqrt(m). Thus making the environmental exposure
small costs time. More generally Cauchy-Schwarz gives the exact resource inequality

    |phi| <= g sqrt[T integral |alpha|^2 dt],
    B >= kappa phi^2/(g^2 T).

It holds under the stated scalar-force model in this rotating frame, for any closing
path. The circle family is within a factor two of this bound in the weak-damping limit.
It is not a lower bound on all possible nonlinear, reservoir-engineered, or coded
architectures. A specified maximum modulation frequency additionally constrains the
ordinary loop family: B=2 kappa phi/Omega cannot fall below its value at Omega_max.

2. EXACT CHANNEL FOR A STATIONARY WARM OUTPUT

Assume the initial bus and every passive bath input mode have the SAME isotropic
thermal quadrature variance V=N+1/2, in conventions [X,P]=i and vacuum variance 1/2.
Then U_free preserves their product Gaussian state. At alpha(T)=0 the bus is fully
decoupled, while spin coherence between S-eigenvalues s and s' is multiplied by

    exp[i phi(s^2-s'^2)] exp[-V B(s-s')^2].

This is an arbitrary-input channel statement, not a Bell-state-only fidelity.
Closing the oscillator path does not remove bath leakage. Designing the compensated
force restores closure and phase, but tracing the bath still leaves this dephasing.

With data storage error of scale Gamma_q T, the unmonitored conventional comparator
has leading error of order

    V kappa phi^2/(g^2 T) + Gamma_q T.

After time optimization this is of order |phi| sqrt(V kappa Gamma_q)/g. Numerical
constants depend on S normalization, error metric and data-noise model. This is the
usual hot-cooperativity requirement. If no upper time penalty or modulation limit is
charged, ever slower/smaller excursions can conceal a major physical cost.

3. WHAT PHYSICAL ENVIRONMENTAL MEASUREMENT ALREADY ADDS

Because there is only one S, the bath displacement beta occupies one collective
complex temporal/spatial mode. Define its canonical generating quadrature P_beta.
For alpha(T)=0 the relevant interaction is

    U_env=exp[-i a S P_beta],      a=sqrt(2B).

An ideal measurement of P_beta produces only a known S rotation. Applying its inverse
restores the geometric gate exactly, for any mixed initial thermal state; no bath
purification is required. Indeed <p|exp(-i a S P)=exp(-i a S p)<p|. Purifying the initial
bath gives the same identity and shows that the remote purifier becomes independent
of S after this measurement. A shaped homodyne local oscillator measures this mode
without storing a coherent quantum record. A real apparatus must supply the matching
spatial access, envelope, phase reference, bandwidth and final rotation.

This is an application of established environmental correction/no-knowledge physics.
It should not be represented as a new principle. It is stronger than applying the
vacuum-bath no-knowledge formula blindly to a finite-temperature Lindblad operator.

For an inefficient measurement of the same physical output mode, write

    Y=sqrt(eta) P_beta+sqrt(1-eta) P_add+zeta,
    Var(P_beta)=V, Var(P_add)=W, Var(zeta)=E.

The added port and electronics are independent. Optimal feedforward removes the
conditional mean, leaving

    V_cond=V[(1-eta)W+E]/[eta V+(1-eta)W+E].

The corrected channel replaces V by V_cond in exp[-V B(s-s')^2]. This is ordinary
Gaussian conditioning/Wiener gain. If W and E stay bounded as V grows and eta>0,
V_cond tends to [(1-eta)W+E]/eta. Therefore increasing thermal occupation does not
increase this particular residual without bound. Sabuncu et al. explicitly obtained
replacement of environmental excess noise by vacuum noise through calibrated
environmental measurements and feedforward. The scalar variance formula itself is
not a scientific novelty.

Do not replace an unobserved hot port by this model without a dilation argument.
If W=V and E=0, V_cond=(1-eta)V and saturation vanishes. In the equal-temperature
product-output model, an inaccessible independent output component beta_perp causes
additional dephasing V||beta_perp||^2. Measuring a fraction of a mode through a cold
attenuator is different from missing an independent thermal mode.

For distinct-temperature input ports, the free output state need not be product.
Some unobserved thermal noise can then be inferred from correlated observed outputs.
The correct test is the full covariance conditional variance (Schur complement) on
physically commuting measured quadratures. An 'unmonitored damping fraction' alone
does not settle this more general case. A hot phase-noise direction in the null space
of the actual observation map leaves an error proportional to its hot variance.

4. EXACT CALIBRATION AND MISALIGNMENT COST

Consider one relevant bath mode, U_s=exp(-i a s P). Suppose the detector actually
measures Y=sqrt(eta)(P cos(delta)+X sin(delta))+sqrt(1-eta)P_add, and apply exp(i k S Y)
as classical outcome-conditioned feedforward. Tracing the outcome and environment
gives the exact multiplier for coherence s,s':

    exp[i a k sqrt(eta) sin(delta)(s^2-s'^2)/2]
    times exp[-(s-s')^2 R(k,delta)/2],

    R(k,delta)=a^2 V-2 a k sqrt(eta)V cos(delta)
               +k^2[eta V+(1-eta)W].

Derivation: insert the measured characteristic function exp[i k(s-s')Y] between
U_s and U_s'^dagger and use the Weyl relation. The commutator is a scalar, producing
the displayed S^2 phase. The remaining thermal characteristic function is Gaussian.
This phase is correctable only if the angle/gain is known or bounded sufficiently.

The optimal real gain is

    k_opt=a sqrt(eta)V cos(delta)/[eta V+(1-eta)W],
    R_min=a^2[V sin^2(delta)+V_cond cos^2(delta)].

Thus fixed angular error restores thermal growth; for small delta, the excess
dephasing scales as B V delta^2. Gain error Delta_k contributes exactly

    R(k_opt+Delta_k,delta)-R_min=[eta V+(1-eta)W] Delta_k^2.

To keep a fixed target error while increasing V, the relevant angle and relative
gain precision must therefore scale as V^(-1/2), unless B is also reduced at a
charged time/bandwidth cost. A fixed mode-overlap defect has the same O(V) problem.
Calibration resources, stable references, detector linearity and control drift are
material requirements. This is a resource requirement, not an impossibility theorem.
The simple exact expressions are independently checked in warm_competitor_check.py
using finite-dimensional oscillator matrices and thermal states, rather than by
substituting the same analytic variance formula into itself.

Final commuting S rotations can be deferred until the complete scalar record is
available. Consequently infinite-bandwidth Markov feedback is not intrinsically
required in this scalar-S gate. Finite measurement mode matching and finite final
rotation remain required. For S a sum of Pauli Z operators, these are local rotations.
Classical outcome dynamic range grows as sqrt(V); digitization must preserve the
required absolute phase precision, costing additional bits/calibration.

5. CURRENT PHYSICAL-THERMAL FILTER CHECK

O'Connor, Albarelli and Genoni (27 October 2026) give, for an unsqueezed thermal bath
and homodyne phase absorbed into c,

    A_m=sqrt[kappa/(2N+1)] [(N+1)c-N c^dagger],
    A_u=sqrt[kappa N(N+1)/(2N+1)] (c+c^dagger).

Their physical-output stochastic master equation has D[A_m]+D[A_u] and monitoring
H[A_m]dW. This differs from separately measuring the two Lindblad vacuum channels
sqrt[kappa(N+1)]c and sqrt[kappa N]c^dagger, which accesses a purification. Setting
c=-i S gives A_u=0, consistent with exact physical no-knowledge erasure of scalar
dephasing, while c=a leaves a nonzero unmonitored term. One must establish the full
closed-loop displacement reduction before treating the bus as that scalar channel.

6. RETAINED SENSING OBSTRUCTION FROM THE PREVIOUS OBJECTIVE

If the sensor Hamiltonian is H_omega=omega Z/2 and an additional parallel noise
channel L_z=sqrt(kappa_z)Z is unheralded, no parameter-independent adaptive controller
can recover indefinite T^2 information growth about omega. Represent the parallel
channel as the stochastic Hamiltonian (Z/2)dX with

    dX=omega dt+2 sqrt(kappa_z)dW.

Any allowed quantum/classical output, including other noise records and feedback,
is a parameter-independent processing of this drifted Brownian path. Its classical
Fisher information is T/(4 kappa_z), so output QFI is no larger. The same path gives
KL divergence (delta_omega)^2 T/(8 kappa_z), yielding a two-point minimax obstruction
without relying on local unbiasedness. This does not apply if the parallel noise
itself is physically monitored with an independent useful record.

For a merely static classical scalar frequency, estimate-then-synthesize on a
protected memory is also a strong ordinary competitor: |omega-omega_hat|<=2 eps/t
ensures half diamond distance at most eps for exp(-i omega t Z/2). It costs SQL
estimation time of order t^2/eps^2 (times the relevant noise scale), plus the fully
charged memory and synthesis. A genuinely coherent quantum-source task must preserve
source/reference correlations or improve the end-to-end resource cost. This helped
motivate the move to an entangling interaction.

DECISION

The complete existing route already supplies exact thermal-state-independent
geometric gates in a lossless bus, compensated closure under damping, and recovery
through correctly measured scalar environmental phase. Its costs identify the
surviving opening: build a physically specified architecture in which every hot
phase direction is observable through finite, imperfect outputs, with calibration,
bandwidth and data memory charged, and demonstrate a resource advantage over the
unmonitored hot-cooperativity baseline and cold/FT alternatives. Heat saturation
through a matched cold-noise detector is established physics; a physical accessibility
or resource theorem beyond that is the substantive question.
