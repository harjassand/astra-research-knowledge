# Coherent first-passage gates and finite-observer obstructions

Research continuation, 1 November 2026 (Brisbane) checkpoint. Mathematical claims below have explicit idealizations; no hardware result or novelty claim is asserted.

## Capability and exact success

The task is to implement a specified unknown interaction on an unknown quantum input, preserving its reference correlations. Let an inaccessible target T carry an arbitrary bounded Hermitian operator B. The available interaction is

\[
H=Z_S\otimes B.
\]

The sensor suffers returning jumps \(L_\theta=\sqrt\lambda\,\sigma_\theta^S\), with \(\sigma_\theta=\cos\theta X+\sin\theta Y\) and fixed unknown theta. A genuine acquired jump/parity record is assumed in this section. The target and B are not measured, inverted, or otherwise controlled.

Write \(y(t)=(-1)^{N_t}\), and signed exposure

\[
A(t)=\int_0^t y(s)ds.
\]

At an even jump count the trajectory operator is exactly \(e^{-iA(t)H}\). This follows because \(\sigma_\theta H\sigma_\theta=-H\), \(\sigma_\theta^2=I\), and all toggled Hamiltonians are scalar multiples of H.

For desired interaction time \(a>0\), stop at

\[
\tau_a=\inf\{t:A(t)=a\}.
\]

The continuous signed clock first crosses a positive upper level while its velocity is +1. Thus its jump parity is even at the crossing (apart from a zero-probability simultaneous jump), and

\[
\boxed{U_{\rm out}=e^{-iaZ_S\otimes B}.}
\]

This exact operator identity holds for any target dimension, any B, and any joint input/reference. It corrects the random clock by a stopping rule and never needs the spectrum of B. Physical duration is random and all waiting exposure is included. A composable delivered gate additionally requires an available way to halt or decouple the interaction, or hand off the state, at the stopping time. Scalar noise-rate control alone does not freeze an always-on unknown interaction. Without that endpoint operation, the equality is an exact snapshot theorem, not a completed processing primitive.

## Latency and its classical origin

Set rate alpha while y=+1 and beta while y=-1, with beta>alpha>0. Starting at y=+1, the first-passage Laplace transform is

\[
\mathbb E e^{-s\tau_a}=e^{-ak(s)},\qquad
k(s)=\frac{-(\beta-\alpha)+
\sqrt{(\beta-\alpha)^2+4s(s+\alpha+\beta)}}2.
\]

Derivation: for distance z to the boundary, write the two backward solutions as \(F_+(z)=e^{-kz}\), \(F_-(z)=c e^{-kz}\). The generator equations give \(c=(s+\alpha-k)/\alpha=\beta/(s+\beta+k)\), hence \(k^2+(\beta-\alpha)k=s(s+\alpha+\beta)\). Choose the nonnegative root and boundary \(F_+(0)=1\).

Differentiation gives

\[
\mathbb E\tau_a=a\frac{\alpha+\beta}{\beta-\alpha},\qquad
\operatorname{Var}\tau_a=\frac{8\alpha\beta a}{(\beta-\alpha)^3}.
\]

At equal positive rates the first-passage mean diverges; scalar rate bias provides the finite-cost implementation. First-passage formulas for asymmetric telegraph motion are established classical probability machinery, not a new stochastic-process theorem: López and Ratanov, *On the asymmetric telegraph processes*, Journal of Applied Probability 51, 569–589 (2014), https://doi.org/10.1239/jap/1402578644 .

For a hard time cap B and \(0<r<r_c=(\sqrt\beta-\sqrt\alpha)^2/2\), Chernoff gives

\[
\Pr(\tau_a>B)\le
\exp\left[-rB+
\frac a2\left((\beta-\alpha)-
\sqrt{(\beta-\alpha)^2-4r(\alpha+\beta-r)}\right)\right].
\]

An abort at B must be charged as a flagged erasure. The scheme is exact only on completed trajectories. Clock resolution, missed jumps, finite meter noise, helper errors, and the scalar-rate actuator remain real resources.

## Finite parity sampling loses quantum coherence even if readout is perfect

Consider constant rate gamma and perfect parity measurements only at times separated by Delta. No information about interior jump times is acquired. Let b be a spectral gap of H. For an interval whose parity changes, the normalized characteristic function of its signed exposure is

\[
c_o(b)=
\frac{\gamma\sinh(\Delta\sqrt{\gamma^2-b^2})}
{\sqrt{\gamma^2-b^2}\sinh(\gamma\Delta)}.
\]

The expression is analytically continued if \(|b|>\gamma\), and its limit is used at equality. It follows from the tilted two-state generator

\[
\begin{pmatrix}-\gamma-ib&\gamma\\\gamma&-\gamma+ib\end{pmatrix}.
\]

For b nonzero the conditional clock increment has continuous support, so \(|c_o(b)|<1\). Two odd endpoint transitions return the actual parity to even and therefore cancel the unknown theta exactly. Nevertheless their quantum coherence is multiplied by \(c_o(b)^2\). Returning errors and a returned syndrome do not return the unknown quantum state.

For gamma=1, Delta=0.6, b=0.8, the surviving coherence modulus is 0.9272081901. Against any desired unitary phase on that coherence, the conditional channel has diamond-norm error at least

\[
1-|c_o(b)|^2=0.0727918099.
\]

This is diamond norm, not half diamond norm. The latter lower bound is half as large.

### Why future scalar rate control cannot recover the lost timing

Conditioned on the sampled endpoint parity, later Markov dynamics carry no information about earlier hidden dwell times. A future protocol based only on parity records can add a further random signed clock, apply a known phase where available, or reject the record. It cannot increase the modulus of the past characteristic factor without new information about the target or its phase. Rejecting such records is an erasure cost, not deterministic coherent recovery.

More generally, a random-clock channel \(\int e^{-itH}\rho e^{itH}\mu(dt)\) equals \(e^{-iaH}\rho e^{iaH}\) for every unknown H with gaps in a nonempty interval only if \(\mu=\delta_a\). Equality forces \(\mathbb E e^{-ib(t-a)}=1\); applying this to two incommensurate gaps forces t=a almost surely. Thus an exact universal unknown-Hamiltonian gate requires the physical clock to be resolved, not just its mean.

This obstruction is specific to the stated observation and control algebra. It does not exclude full quantum error correction with additional protected subsystems, access to the hidden noise record, or data-control operations outside that algebra.

## Noncommuting target dynamics break the scalar-clock representation

If the physical target also has an inaccessible self-Hamiltonian H0, then

\[
H_{\rm phys}=I\otimes H_0+Z_S\otimes B
\]

becomes, in the jump frame,

\[
U_{\rm record}=\mathcal T\exp\left[-i\int_0^\tau
(I\otimes H_0+y(t)Z_S\otimes B)dt\right].
\]

When \([H_0,B]\ne0\), this depends on the order of the waiting intervals, not only on tau and A. Two paths can have the same physical time and the same first-passage signed time but different target channels.

An executable example uses \(H_0=X_T\), \(B=Z_T\). Both sign schedules have plus/minus/plus durations and first hit A=1 at the end:

- (0.2, 0.4, 1.2), total time 1.8;
- (0.6, 0.4, 0.8), total time 1.8.

Their output unitary operator-norm distance is 0.5745804059. No timestamp-based scalar clock correction identifies them as the same desired channel. If H0 commutes with B, the output factorizes as \(e^{-iH_0\tau}e^{-iaZB}\), but the physical free-evolution time remains tau. Calling this a fixed-time simulation at time a would change the target unless the free evolution is tracked or compensated by an available operation.

## Evidence and comparison

`warm_coherent_clock.py` verifies the unknown-target gate identity on 50 stochastic paths, with maximum operator-norm residual 1.24e-15. For alpha=1, beta=4, a=3, 100000 sampled paths gave mean duration 4.99704 with standard error 0.00594, versus exact mean 5; empirical variance 3.5224 versus exact 3.5556. Several Laplace-transform points agree numerically. These are algebraic and stochastic checks, not experimental results.

Relevant finite-observer primary competitor: *Imperfection analysis for random-telegraph-noise mitigation using spectator qubits*, Physical Review A (2026), https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/zchg-gcb3 . It analyzes parameter uncertainty, readout/reset efficiency and latency, and spectator decoherence for adaptive Bayesian mitigation. Its data correction controls must not be imported into an inaccessible-target comparison without charging them.
