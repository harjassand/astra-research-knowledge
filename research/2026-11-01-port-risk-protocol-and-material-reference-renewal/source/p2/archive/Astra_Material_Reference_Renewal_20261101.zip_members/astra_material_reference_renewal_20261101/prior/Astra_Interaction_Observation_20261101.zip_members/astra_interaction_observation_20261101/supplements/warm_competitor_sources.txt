PRIMARY SOURCE NOTES, checked 1 November 2026 Brisbane
Compact attributions only. Derived comparison formulas are identified as our own
applications/synthesis in warm_competitor_proofs.txt; no priority claim is made.

[1] M. J. A. Schuetz, G. Giedke, L. M. K. Vandersypen, J. I. Cirac,
High-Fidelity Hot Gates for Generic Spin-Resonator Systems,
Phys. Rev. A 95, 052335 (2017).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.01614
https://arxiv.org/html/1607.01614v2
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.95.052335
Established comparator: longitudinal/spin-dependent force creates an S^2 interaction
and exact stroboscopic spin-bus disentangling independent of initial thermal state.
Analyzes resonator rethermalization and qubit dephasing, including gate-time costs.
Physical longitudinal coupling and relevant noise model, not a general guarantee
for arbitrary warm devices. Main sections II-IV and appendices C,L,M are relevant.

[2] K. Mueller, K. Luoma, W. T. Strunz,
Geometric phase gates in dissipative quantum dynamics,
Phys. Rev. A 102, 032611 (2020).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.08033
https://arxiv.org/html/1907.08033v2
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.102.032611
Treats Markov damping and thermal fluctuations in geometric phase gates. Constructs
forces restoring zero-temperature phase and final motion despite damping; examines
fidelity and finite-temperature corrections. Restored closure and phase must not be
confused with removing environmental decoherence. Thermal corrections are evaluated
in the stated short-time rethermalization regime, not an arbitrary-temperature
unconditional high-fidelity theorem.

[3] S. S. Szigeti, A. R. R. Carvalho, J. G. Morley, M. R. Hush,
Ignorance Is Bliss: General and Robust Cancellation of Decoherence via No-Knowledge
Quantum Feedback, Phys. Rev. Lett. 113, 020407 (2014).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.4493
https://arxiv.org/html/1401.4493v3
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.113.020407
For monitored Hermitian L, orthogonal homodyne measures no system signal and permits
Hamiltonian feedback cancelling monitored noise. Efficiency eta leaves (1-eta)D[L]
in the paper's vacuum-output instrument. Non-Hermitian L requires engineering the
additional L^dagger reservoir and interfering both outputs before homodyne. This is
not free access to both mathematical channels of an ordinary thermal reservoir.

[4] M. Sabuncu, R. Filip, G. Leuchs, U. L. Andersen,
Environment-assisted quantum-information correction for continuous variables,
Phys. Rev. A 81, 012325 (2010).
https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.3546
https://arxiv.org/html/0909.3546v1
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.81.012325
Studies and experimentally tests thermal environmental correction by homodyne/
heterodyne plus feedforward, including imperfect access modeled by vacuum loss.
Section III.1 explicitly replaces environmental thermal noise by vacuum noise for
nonzero measured fraction; Section III.2 handles conjugate quadratures. Their
information-recovery statement is not unit-fidelity reconstruction of an arbitrary
CV state. Detector electronics are assumed negligible in the displayed equations;
stationary environmental/coupling/detection parameters are calibrated with probes.
This is direct precedent for heat-independent residual noise from calibrated
environmental measurements, so the variance saturation alone is not new.

[5] M. G. Genoni, S. Mancini, H. M. Wiseman, A. Serafini,
Quantum filtering of a thermal master equation with purified reservoir,
Phys. Rev. A 90, 063826 (2014).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.1435
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.90.063826
Distinguishes direct detection of a physical thermal bath from access to a larger
two-mode purification. In their oscillator steady-state squeezing problem, physical
bath detection does not attain the optimal general-dyne value; purification access
can. This is a governing access distinction, not a no-go for scalar spin dephasing.

[6] E. O'Connor, F. Albarelli, M. G. Genoni,
Homodyne Unravelling and Parameter Estimation in Squeezed Thermal Baths,
arXiv:2610.37438v1, 27 October 2026 (preprint).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37438
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.37438v1
Current physical thermal-output SME splits monitored and unmonitored collapse
operators, Eqs. 9-25. Explicitly warns that separately homodyning the two standard
thermal Lindblad channels represents joint access to a purification. Its physical
single-output formulas reduce, for unsqueezed noise, to the A_m,A_u expressions in
the competitor proof. This supplies a precise, current check on any claimed
warm-output instrument; it does not itself claim the spin-bus gate proposed here.

ADDITIONAL PREVIOUS-OBJECTIVE SOURCES, NOT EQUIVALENT TO A WARM GATE

[7] Conlon et al., Fault-Tolerant Heisenberg-Limited Quantum Sensing,
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.00171v1 (31 July 2026).
Biased bit-flip circuit-noise repetition-code construction. Appendix B7 charges
phase faults: fixed phase-error bias eventually limits large-distance improvement.

[8] Li et al., Fault-Tolerant Heisenberg-Limited Quantum Metrology with Bosonic Probes,
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.40405v1 (29 October 2026).
Bosonic displacement sensing under number dephasing with finite repeated recovery.
Appendix I3 charges cycle duty factor and ancilla phase faults; it does not supply
a uniform threshold against arbitrary controller noise. Different sensor/source
model from the present entangling-gate task.
