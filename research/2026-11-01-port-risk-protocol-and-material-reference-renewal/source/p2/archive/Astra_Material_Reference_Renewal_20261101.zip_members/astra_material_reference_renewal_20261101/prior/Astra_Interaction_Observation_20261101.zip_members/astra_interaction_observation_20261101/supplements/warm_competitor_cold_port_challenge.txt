COLD READOUT PORT: INDEPENDENT CHALLENGE OF THE PROPOSED OBSTRUCTION
1 November 2026 Brisbane

QUESTION
Can established Gaussian measurement/feedback methods refute the root's proposed
obstruction when an oscillator has a continuous, inaccessible intrinsic hot Markov
bath and only a separate cold readout output is observed? The task is a deterministic
arbitrary-input entangling gate, scalar S=Z1+Z2, bounded linear S-dependent forces,
Gaussian measurements and local linear S-phase feedforward. No native hot-output
access, encoded data, extra S^2 interaction, or direct measurement of classical bath
forces is supplied.

RESULT OF THE CHALLENGE
No counterexample under those same operations was found in the examined primary
methods. The strongest measurement alternative is a single force-weighted collective
homodyne quadrature, rather than heterodyne. It removes unnecessary heterodyne added
noise but leaves the root's coherent phase cancellation intact. Post-gate cold-output
smoothing also failed to evade it in the exact finite-collision comparison.
This supports pursuing the proposed theorem, but does not establish global priority
or a theorem against all warm mediators, active reservoir engineering, or non-Gaussian
controls. The root owns the general mathematical proof.

EXACT NUMERICAL CHALLENGE
Code: warm_competitor_cold_port_probe.py, independent new file; root probe untouched.
The intrinsic hot output is inaccessible. Additional cold outputs may be observed
after the force loop, while intrinsic hot input continues. We optimize reconstruction
of the leading hot phase directly, without a heterodyne-noise penalty.

Let U_H be the real symplectic columns of the passive free map corresponding to all
hot input quadratures, C select the measured cold output coordinates, and L be the
root's displacement generator. The best possible linear hot reconstruction solves

    minimize_k || U_H^T (L-C^T k) ||^2.

The coherent phase is evaluated separately as

    Phi_eff = Phi - L^T Omega C^T k / 2.

For the root's closed circle at T=2, kappa_h=.3, kappa_c=1 and nominal phase .1:

collision steps    actual Phi       Phi_eff          squared hot residual
20                 .09217292       4.0e-16          9.20870e-5
40                 .09640465      -4.2e-17          1.16690e-5
80                 .09828701       6.9e-17          1.46636e-6
160                .09916530      -1.4e-17          1.83709e-7

Adding 40 or 120 post-gate observation steps to the 40-step force loop changes
neither the residual nor the corrected phase, up to roundoff. Adding 80 post-gate
steps to the 80-step loop gives the same result. The post-gate outputs supply no
useful extra correction in this comparison. This does not substitute for an analytic
statement for all force shapes.

At fixed collision resolution, the squared hot residual is NONZERO. Consequently
the total residual variance eventually grows linearly with hot occupation. The
root's original 80-step finite-temperature probe gives .695 at occupation 1e4 and
2.153 at 1e6. A literal bounded-variance claim therefore needs a continuum limit or
explicitly growing discretization/measurement resources. The leading residual above
falls approximately as the third inverse power of collision resolution in this
particular smooth closed-loop example. Do not claim that rate universally.

A real vector C^T k is one Hermitian collective quadrature of the cold outputs.
It can in principle be measured by one temporally shaped homodyne observable. Thus
heterodyne added noise can be removed for this scalar-S objective. The reconstructed
hot phase and the symplectic coherent phase shift depend on C^T k and are unchanged.
Finite local-oscillator bandwidth and mode matching still have to be charged.

WHY THE APPARENT BYPASS FAILS
In the continuum physical model, the hot part of the cold output is proportional
to the instantaneous hot-driven oscillator coordinate. A force-weighted readout
therefore learns the relevant thermal phase. But the same observed oscillator
coordinate contains the displacement caused by S. That self-response is also what
creates the geometric S^2 phase. Subtracting the measured phase subtracts both.
The Gaussian conditional-variance calculation does not account for this by itself;
the Weyl commutator in the actual measurement/feedforward channel is essential.

The standard optimal linear filter still provides a useful constrained comparison:
if V is the physical output covariance and N the POVM added covariance, define

    Y=C V C^T+N,       b=C V L,       q=C Omega^T L/2.

The unconstrained variance minimizer is k0=Y^{-1}b. To retain a prescribed corrected
phase theta, impose q^T k=Phi-theta. For positive definite Y, the exact optimum is

    k=k0+Y^{-1}q[(Phi-theta)-q^T k0]/(q^T Y^{-1}q),
    Vres_min(theta)=Vres_min_unconstrained
       +[(Phi-theta)-q^T k0]^2/(q^T Y^{-1}q).

This is ordinary linearly constrained Gaussian estimation, with the physically
necessary coherent-phase constraint supplied by the exact quantum channel. It is
not a new control primitive. It supplies a clean benchmark for a proposed theorem.

PRIMARY EXISTING ROUTES CHECKED

1. Szigeti et al., PRL 113,020407 (2014), no-knowledge feedback.
https://arxiv.org/html/1401.4493v3
Exact cancellation requires monitoring the decoherence channel itself. General
non-Hermitian coupling a additionally requires engineering an a^dagger reservoir
and interfering the two accessible outputs. An inaccessible intrinsic hot bath
does not become monitored because another cold output is measured. Thus this
construction does not supply the prohibited hot-force record.

2. Sabuncu et al., PRA 81,012325 (2010), environmental CV correction.
https://arxiv.org/html/0909.3546v1
Thermal-noise replacement by vacuum detector noise assumes measurement of the
existing environment carrying the noisy signal. Its thermal-saturation mechanism
does not establish that a separate cold readout port provides the same physical
instrument or preserves an S^2 gate. This remains the closest precedent for the
earlier native-bath-output version of the root proposal.

3. Barrett and Milburn, PRA 74,060302(R) (2006), lossy bus processing.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0607206
The construction uses an initially coherent bus, conditional rotations Z_j a^dagger a,
unconditional displacements and homodyne to perform near-projective parity checks.
Its master equation contains vacuum loss 2 kappa D[a]. It is a strong established
route around photon-loss fragility, but does not use the same linear-force coupling
or continuously hot intrinsic bath, and does not provide a deterministic arbitrary
input unitary solely through local S-phase feedback.

4. Cernotik and Hammerer, PRA 94,012340 (2016), measurement-induced entanglement
of superconducting qubits via optomechanical transducers.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.00768
This is the strongest directly relevant physical cold-readout competitor: optical
homodyne observes hot mechanics coupled by a longitudinal qubit force. It postselects
Bell states, rather than enacting an arbitrary-input deterministic gate. Crucially,
its 'cooperativity moderately above one' includes thermal occupation:
C=4g^2/(kappa gamma nbar). Its effective thermal dephasing remains proportional to
gamma(2nbar+1); fixed-resource arbitrary-temperature protection is not claimed.

5. Wiseman and Milburn, PRL 70,548 (1993), optical homodyne feedback.
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.70.548
The governing established feedback framework includes coherent drift and added
diffusion, rather than merely subtracting a classical conditional variance. This is
prior framework for the necessity of charging a feedback-induced Hamiltonian.
The exact finite Gaussian gate/instrument identity in the root's model should be
presented as its own derivation; the primary abstract alone does not establish the
specific proposed cold-port no-go theorem.

6. O'Connor, Albarelli and Genoni, arXiv:2610.37438 (27 October 2026).
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.37438v1
Separates the monitored and unmonitored terms of the physical thermal-output SME
and distinguishes it from purification access. Useful current governing source,
but it does not analyze the proposed force-loop cold-port gate obstruction.

SCOPE THAT MUST REMAIN EXPLICIT

- Initial thermal occupation without ongoing thermal injection is insufficient for
  the obstruction: ordinary closed loops are already an immediate counterexample.
- The intrinsic hot bath must inject full-rank Markov thermal fluctuations during
  the force operation. Quiet intervals, controllable hot coupling, low-noise spectral
  windows of a colored bath, or an independently measured classical force change it.
- A finite number of ideal instantaneous force kicks between collisions can hide
  noiseless commutator loops; the bounded continuous interaction limit matters.
- Increasing cold damping/measurement strength with temperature is a resource change,
  as is cooling faster than hot rethermalization. Charge it rather than exclude it
  by unexplained language.
- Direct bus feedback, active/squeezed reservoir engineering, independent noncommuting
  qubit forces, adaptive non-Gaussian detection, encoded data, and heralded state
  preparation require separate analyses if they are to be ruled out.
- Conditional state-preparation entanglement does not refute a deterministic
  arbitrary-input gate bound. At Phi_eff=0 the averaged scalar-S dephasing channel
  is a mixture of local product rotations; it cannot create data entanglement.
- No exhaustive novelty claim is justified by this search. The useful possible
  contribution is a precise physical-access/resource theorem, not rediscovery of
  Gaussian estimation or feedback Hamiltonians.
