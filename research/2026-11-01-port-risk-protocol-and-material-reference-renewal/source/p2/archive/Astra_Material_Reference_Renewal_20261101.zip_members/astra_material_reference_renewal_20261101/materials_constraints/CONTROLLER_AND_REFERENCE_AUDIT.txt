1. NO PRISTINE GEOMETRIC TARGET ENTERS THE CONTROL LAW

The environment sets a target to prepare initial members and evaluate the result.
The actual loop uses sensor outputs, force-free zero measurements and relative
slope calibration. For exact linear records
  z_i=a_i[L-r_i]+b_i, a_i=g_i*k_i>0,
force-free measurements give b_i. Two shared gap states with the SAME natural
lengths give Delta z_i=a_i*Delta L. Hence normalized_i=(L-r_i)/Delta L and
  error_i = normalized_i - (normalized_j+normalized_k)/2
          = [(r_j+r_k)/2-r_i]/Delta L.
This cancels common L and the unknown separate sensor gains and stiffnesses.
Only the sign of Delta L is needed for feedback polarity. The initial target in
the simulation's calibration-gap choices does not leak into the controller.

With fixed references and positive Delta L, bounded-rate feedback has the correct
sign and converges to their mean in the noiseless continuous model. The gain sets
the convergence time, not the copied target. Unknown common displacement may vary
in time if the three records truly refer to the same instantaneous gap.

This theorem is linear and affine in each sensor. It must remain distinct from the
matched nonlinear force-law result. Two secant measurements do not calibrate three
arbitrary unequal nonlinear constitutive laws, especially when their operating
strains differ. Identical monotone laws allow zero/order comparison without knowing
their slope; arbitrary unequal nonlinear laws require additional calibration.

The present finite_controller() updates a PRESELECTED newcomer, branch index2.
The other two are supplied as reference members. It demonstrates scheduled renewal
or renewal after independent diagnosis, not by itself diagnosis of arbitrary
material versus sensor faults. Any broader inference claim belongs to the root's
separate joint-inference construction.

2. EXACT CYCLIC COPY DIFFUSION

For updates0,1,2 in that order, replacing each by the mean of the OTHER TWO CURRENT
copies plus independent zero-mean error eta_i of variance sigma^2, the full round is
  r'=A r+B eta,
  A=[[0,1/2,1/2],
     [0,1/4,3/4],
     [0,3/8,5/8]],
  B=[[1,0,0],
     [1/2,1,0],
     [3/4,1/2,1]].
The exact normalized left eigenvector is w=(0,1/3,2/3): wA=w.
The noise projection is wB=(2/3,2/3,2/3). Therefore the common reference coordinate
R=w r obeys
  R_next=R+(2/3)(eta_0+eta_1+eta_2),
and its variance increases by exactly4 sigma^2/3 each complete round. With
independent rounds, Var(R_N)=Var(R_0)+4N sigma^2/3. The other eigenvalues of A are
0 and -1/8, so agreement can improve while the retained common reference diffuses.
The root's stated coefficient and interpretation are correct for this protocol.

It is not a universal lower bound on reference noise. As a comparison, synchronous
averaging of OLD copies has A_sync=(11^T-I)/2, B_sync=I, w_sync=(1,1,1)/3 and adds
sigma^2/3 per simultaneous round. That requires old-reference acquisition/storage
and potentially different load-bearing backups, so it is not free. Correlated copy
noise, physical conservation, retained controller history or different update
schedules also change the coefficient. Translation covariance by itself does not
prove an impossibility theorem for every possible controller.

3. THE CURRENT SENSOR-NOISE AVERAGING NEEDS A PHYSICAL TIMING CONTRACT

As read, the finite controller divides raw sensor sigma by sqrt(64) on every2ms
control step, and calibration uses sqrt(256). The code does not assign an
acquisition window, sample rate or correlation model to these repetitions. They
cannot automatically be identified with the earlier OU meter having tau=1ms.
For that meter a2ms uniform average retains variance
  v*2(2-1+exp(-2))/4 = .567668*v,
not v/64. The two may represent different meters, but that must be stated and the
actual bandwidth/power/correlation provided. No issue exists with an illustrative
noise experiment if it is clearly separated from a complete physical estimate.

Force-free zero records likewise stand in for real unloading/zeroing. The finite
protocol should include load-bearing support during zeroing, settling, finite
readout time, restoration of load and any intervening drift. Two-gap calibration
requires rest lengths and gains to remain fixed through the probe, with the same
gap displacement seen by every path. Merely asserting an average count does not
earn those operations.

The simulation permits compressive forces in calibration and potentially early
parts of operation. Stable bidirectional flexures/springs are compatible with that;
tension-only cables need an explicit preload avoiding slack.

4. OVERLAP LIFETIME AND WORK CHECKS

For independent constant per-member fault rate lambda, fixed repair duration tau,
and failure on any second member fault before completion, the mean lifetime from
the healthy state is
  1/[3lambda(1-exp(-2lambda*tau))]+1/(2lambda).
It follows by a renewal equation: wait1/(3lambda) for the first fault, spend
E[min(Exp(2lambda),tau)]=(1-exp(-2lambda*tau))/(2lambda), and restart if repair
finishes first. The root formula is correct.

If repair itself is exponential with rate mu, the two-state transient generator
gives mean lifetime(mu+5lambda)/(6lambda^2), also as in the root code.

These exclude inaccurate diagnosis, copying errors, common faults, finite spares,
and sensor/controller/junction failures as stated. In addition, a real lambda may
depend on branch load and temperature. Removing one of three equal members raises
surviving force fromF/3 toF/2 and increases extension byF/(6k). Sensor Joule heating
can change local hazard. The baseline constant-lambda model must not silently be
promoted to a material-life prediction.

The positive quasistatic mechanical work accumulated by the controller is a valid
part of its resource record; it does not include sensor, computation, calibration,
actuator dissipation, fuel preparation or latch costs. The root is separately
adding those physical processes rather than treating this figure as total energy.

Longer observation trades lower copying uncertainty against overlap exposure. For
a white/OU-limited mean, sigma_copy^2 can scale asC/tau, while the second-fault
probability grows as2lambda*tau for short repairs. A low diffusion coefficient
therefore cannot be claimed by taking arbitrarily long observation without cost.

5. NOVELTY AND STATUS

The present positive mechanism combines established analytical redundancy,
differential force sensing, relative calibration, triple redundancy and servo
adjustment. Its exact protocol-specific diffusion coefficient is useful but does
not establish priority for noisy consensus or a new physical principle by itself.
The current result is best described as a conditional construction and a quantitative
boundary for geometric-reference renewal through constituent replacement without
an intact master. It does not yet demonstrate a resource advantage over a retained
coded specification plus calibrated metrology, or a fabricated renewable material.

The useful contribution of the integrated analysis is that reference retention,
measurement, calibration, actual replacement work and copying-induced drift now
belong to the SAME explicit objective. Further significance would require a
physically earned improvement in reference lifetime or complete resource cost,
not moving the stable reference into a sensor, digital controller or helper.
