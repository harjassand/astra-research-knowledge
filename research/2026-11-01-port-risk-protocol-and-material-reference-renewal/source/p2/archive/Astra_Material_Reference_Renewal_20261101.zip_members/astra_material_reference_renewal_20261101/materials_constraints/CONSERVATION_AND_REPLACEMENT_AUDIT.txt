CONSERVATION THROUGH PHYSICAL REPLACEMENT

Scope. This is an accounting and representation audit, not a universal
impossibility theorem. No new literature search or numerical experiment was
needed. A physical conservation law can correlate errors without measurement;
one must establish that the law survives export of the worn carrier and that
the conserved quantity actually determines natural length.

1. Exact live-carrier accounting

Before renewal, the live reference carriers are old a and survivors j. Let
S_minus = r_a + sum_j r_j. The incoming physical carrier b initially has natural
length r_b0. After all interactions, b has r_b_plus, the survivors have changed
by Delta_j, and a is exported. Then, identically,

S_plus - S_minus = r_b_plus - r_a + sum_j Delta_j.              (1)

Thus zero-sum changes among the survivors are insufficient. Conservation of
the live sum requires the new carrier plus the surviving changes to receive
the old carrier's contribution:

r_b_plus + sum_j Delta_j = r_a.                               (2)

Even if an ideal physical interaction conserves the sum of natural-length
coordinates of ALL carriers, incoming and outgoing included, its consequence
is only

S_plus - S_minus = r_b0 - r_a_out - Delta R_aux,               (3)

where R_aux denotes any additional carrier of that conserved coordinate.
With no auxiliary carrier, live-sum conservation requires r_a_out = r_b0.
For example, a perfect state SWAP r_b_plus = r_a, r_a_out = r_b0 works.
It transfers the unknown analog state; an explicit measurement is not a
logical necessity. Merely retaining conservation in the larger system while
discarding a does not work. The discarded carrier can export the reference.

2. What independent overwrite noise hides

If t is the desired newcomer value, r_b_plus = t + eta, then conservation
requires sum_j Delta_j = r_a - t - eta. If r_a = t, the write error eta must
be accompanied by exactly -eta in the surviving reference carriers. Such
correlation can come from a real pairwise exchange event or holonomic
constraint; projecting a sampled noise vector to zero sum does not construct
either. If the old member is damaged, r_a = t + d, conserving the CURRENT sum
transfers d into the surviving carriers instead of correcting it.

In particular, if damage already changed the sum by d, all subsequent
sum-conserving repairs preserve S_original + d. A conservation-based design
must protect the invariant through the damage itself, not only through the
renewal protocol.

3. A precise limited information statement

Condition on all retained state. Suppose the sole physical dependence of the
new/live output on r_a is a record Y; fresh local randomness is independent
of r_a given Y. Define C = r_b_plus + sum_j Delta_j. Equation (1) gives
the sum error C-r_a. Therefore

E[(S_plus-S_minus)^2] >= E[ Var(r_a | Y) ].                    (4)

This is the ordinary conditional-variance bound, not a claim about all
physical transfer mechanisms. A direct reversible analog state transfer or
a conserved-charge exchange need not fit an assumed noisy classical record
channel. With no r_a-dependent interaction at all, two indistinguishable
inputs whose old lengths differ by Delta have minimax mean squared sum error
at least Delta^2/4. Exact conservation for both is impossible.

4. Genuine exception: an intrinsic conserved representation

Let each member carry an extensive quantity q_i with local exchanges
(q_i,q_j) -> (q_i+delta,q_j-delta), and let natural length at a fixed physical
state be r_i = f_i(q_i). For arbitrary exchanges on connected open domains,
sum_i r_i is invariant only if

f_i'(q_i) = f_j'(q_j) for every allowed pair and state,

so each f_i(q) = alpha q + beta_i with the same constant alpha. Incoming
and outgoing physical carriers must also satisfy the charge balance, and
replacement must preserve the sum of the beta_i. A vector conserved quantity
gives the corresponding common affine map alpha dot q + beta_i.

Therefore a conserved particle count or amount of matter can in principle
protect a metric reference without a separate recorded value, if natural
length is fixed by that count. A serial assembly of N identical rigid units
with reference length a and exact one-for-one unit replacement has L0 = Na;
local replacements conserve N without first measuring N. This is a genuine
representation-level counterexample to a claim that ALL full constituent
turnover must cause reference diffusion. It stores an integer extensive
quantity, with a supplied physical unit a and topology-preserving exchanges.
It is not a demonstrated mechanism for arbitrary unknown analog rest lengths,
nor does it establish precision under changed bonding, strain, defects,
temperature, cross section, angular freedom, or material history.

For illustration, a closed ideal incompressible liquid can store a sum of
piston positions through equal-area chambers, r_i = V_i/A. Complete transfer
of the old chamber's liquid into a fresh empty one preserves the sum without
a readout. But this renews the chamber while retaining the liquid reference;
it also requires the same A and density/state. It is not complete renewal of
all reference carriers. Replacing liquid while preserving its amount requires
its own exact input/output balance. This example identifies the retained
resource rather than eliminating it.

5. Why the current adjustable-spring construction lacks this closure

Natural length is a parameter of a member's free-energy minimum. It is not
generically conserved by mechanics, force equality, or matter conservation.
For example, r_i = alpha_i q_i + beta_i with q transferred exactly from old
to new gives a replacement mismatch

(alpha_new-alpha_old) q + beta_new-beta_old.

If alpha or beta depends on wear, chemistry, temperature, or residual stress,
conserving q does not preserve the unknown original natural length. A fixed
fluid volume, calibrated screw/linkage, physical length unit, or fixed
stoichiometric count may furnish a different representation, but its own
physical identity and failure modes must be included.

Conclusion. The 4 sigma^2/3 cyclic-diffusion result remains exact for the
stated independent-write-error protocol. Conservation-correlated errors can
evade it. For the present adjustable-spring model, no physical invariant has
yet been shown that preserves the live original natural-length sum through
damage plus removal and replacement. Equations (1)-(3), rather than a broad
no-go claim, identify what such a new mechanism must supply.
