PHYSICAL COUNTEREXAMPLES AND A STRONG EXISTING COMPARATOR
2026-11-01. Contributor: physical_constraints. Root owns the main invention.

These are conditional model results and counterexamples, not an assertion of
historical priority or a universal impossibility theorem for self-healing matter.
The purpose is to reject a weak mechanism early and specify what a stronger one
must physically supply.

1. FUEL CANNOT BREAK A SYMMETRY IT DOES NOT COUPLE TO

Let a finite material/controller state space have controlled Markov generators
Q_u, and let a permutation group G act on the physical states. Suppose every
available Q_u obeys Q_u(gx,gy)=Q_u(x,y), the initial law is G-invariant, and all
physical observations/control choices respect the same symmetry. Then the law
at any time remains invariant. This follows directly by uniqueness of the
forward equation, and remains true under feedback by adjoining the physical
observer state to the material state. If the prescribed target lies in an orbit
O, its probability is at most 1/|O|. Unlimited fuel or repetitions do not change
this statement; a drive that does not couple to the missing distinction cannot
create it.

Concrete material: A and B functional payloads carried by otherwise identical
patchy particles, with identical lengths, angles, valence, stiffness, and binding
chemistry. The prescribed chemical pattern changes under A/B permutations while
all mechanical protection variables stay identical. With N sites and n_A A-type
payloads, |O|=binomial(N,n_A). A perfectly shaped, fully bonded wrong structure is
therefore possible at zero mechanical error. Species-specific binding faces,
an independent reference, or selectively acting chemistry can remove this
counterexample, but they are extra real resources, not consequences of geometry.

A more local information statement is also useful. Let P_W and P_C be the laws
of the complete physically acquired local record for a wrong and a correct site.
If a repair policy acts with probabilities >=1-delta on W and <=epsilon on C,
then TV(P_W,P_C)>=1-delta-epsilon and
  D(P_W || P_C) >= d_binary(1-delta || epsilon),
by applying data processing to the action/no-action event. This applies equally
when the record is a molecular conformation rather than a human-readable sensor.
The criterion does not provide a record or a transducer for free.

2. AN EXPLICIT LOCALLY DETAILED-BALANCED FUELED GATE

States: vacancy V; C_open,C_protected; W_open,W_protected. C/W are semantic labels
for correct/wrong payloads. Binding rates are a*x and a*(1-x); open-state removal
rates are d_C,d_W. Both payloads have the same two-channel O<->P gate. One gate
channel is passive, with p0/u0=exp(-Delta), and one is fueled, with
pF/uF=exp(A-Delta). A is the chemical affinity in kBT units. Therefore each channel
has local detailed balance, and circulating fuel is explicit rather than hidden.

Let p=p0+pF and u=u0+uF. Stationary weights obey
  P(C_open)=P(V)*a*x/d_C,
  P(C_protected)=(p/u)*P(C_open),
and similarly for W. Consequently
  P(C | occupied) = x*d_W / [x*d_W+(1-x)*d_C].
This is exactly independent of fuel affinity and gate speed. If d_C=d_W, accuracy
is the bath fraction x. The gate makes wrong structures long lived too.

Starting protected, the mean time to removal is
  tau_P(d) = (d+p+u)/(u*d).
It follows by solving two first-passage equations for O and P. The entropy
production rate is A*J_F, where
  J_F=pF*[P(C_open)+P(W_open)]-uF*[P(C_protected)+P(W_protected)].
In the numeric example x=.3, raising A from 0 to 10 increases residence time from
12.9575 to 150.183 and entropy production from 0 to 3.40949, while accuracy stays
.3 to numerical precision. Giving a real tenfold detachment selectivity raises
accuracy to .8108108, but the label-blind fuel cycle still adds no further gain.

This does NOT reject Hopfield-style proofreading generally: different identities
can couple differently to distinct reaction paths and discrimination can then be
amplified. It rejects the specific shortcut of adding a common protection/fuel
cycle and treating its expenditure as a source of missing chemical information.

3. GEOMETRIC COMPATIBILITY LEAVES A PRECISE BLIND SPACE

For an unstressed linear central-force network, let B map nodal displacements u
to bond-length changes, K>0 be diagonal spring stiffness, and d the erroneous
rest-length vector. The elastic energy is
  E(u,d)=1/2 ||K^(1/2)(Bu-d)||^2.
Write A=K^(1/2)B, z=K^(1/2)d, and let P project orthogonally onto ker(A^T). Least
squares gives the exact result
  min_u E(u,d)=1/2 ||P z||^2.
Thus internal-stress tests see incompatible defects, but every d=Bv is invisible
after the body relaxes to u=v. The body can change shape while showing no internal
stress. For bond forces t=K(Bu-d), their differential self-stress signal is
  P K^(-1/2)t=-P K^(1/2)d.
It rejects arbitrary compatible displacement changes in this linear model. This
could be a useful physical readout, but it requires an actual differential force
transducer and known/stable K and B. Large strain, changing connectivity and
unknown stiffness are not automatically removed.

Example: a planar square K4 cage with four side bars and two diagonals. B has rank
5 and one self-stress, proportional to (1,1,1,1,-sqrt(2),-sqrt(2)). A five-percent
affine horizontal stretch produces a nonzero compatible rest-length error with
zero differential self-stress. Exactly, replace its rest lengths by the lengths
of a 1.05-by-1 rectangle: (1.05,1,1.05,1,1.45,1.45). It relaxes to the rectangle with
zero nonlinear spring energy. Even perfect geometry-dependent stress protection
cannot restore the lost square reference from that relaxed state alone.

Scope: this counterexample allows errors to change intrinsic rest lengths. If
chemistry supplies a stable discrete length alphabet or chemically selective
constraints that exclude the wrong metric, that is precisely a reference resource
that can defeat it. The result must not be generalized to say that crystals cannot
heal local defects, or that all molecular length standards drift in this way.

4. EXISTING ASSOCIATIVE EXCHANGE ALREADY RETAINS BOND COUNT

Vitrimers are the strong existing construction for topology change while retaining
crosslink population. A new proposal cannot claim novelty merely for exchange
without network disintegration or for tuning stress relaxation independently of
instantaneous stiffness. Sources S1-S3 below explicitly realize/analyze these.

The remaining distinction is long-time reference-shape preservation. In the clean
single-rate limit a reference strand survives with probability exp(-beta*t),
giving G(t)=G0*exp(-beta*t), viscosity G0/beta, and Maxwell creep. To repair a wrong
site by one exchange before time tau with probability>=1-delta requires
  beta_W*tau >= ln(1/delta).
To retain >=1-epsilon of reference stress over service time T requires
  beta_C*T <= -ln(1-epsilon).
Thus an actual selective-rate contrast must satisfy
  beta_W/beta_C >= (T/tau)*ln(1/delta)/[-ln(1-epsilon)].
At epsilon=delta=.01, the final factor is 458.211. This is a conditional material
design test, not a universal bound: permanent subnetworks, explicit reference
chemistry, localization of repair to already-open cracks, or a different rheology
change its assumptions. Source S4 (15 Oct 2026) is especially important because
segmental diffusion can prevent bond exchange from determining network relaxation
on its own. Fast chemical events are not proof of fast repair or altered rheology.

5. SURFACE FUEL DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY SERVICE THE BULK

For a slab -L<x<L with externally maintained fuel concentration c0 at both faces,
effective diffusion D and first-order consumption k*c, the steady state solves
  D*c''-k*c=0,
  c(x)=c0*cosh(x/lambda)/cosh(L/lambda), lambda=sqrt(D/k).
Center fuel is c0/cosh(L/lambda); average fuel is c0*lambda*tanh(L/lambda)/L.
At L/lambda=10, the center has only 9.08e-5 of the boundary concentration. Fuel
cannot simply be assumed uniform while increasing sample thickness. For uniform
zero-order demand w, positivity requires L<=sqrt(2*D*c0/w). Distributed channels,
advection, light, electrons, or stored local fuel can change this, but their area,
volume, preparation, replenishment and effect on structural integrity must then
be counted. These formulas apply to this explicit slab model, not every delivery
technology. Real microvascular self-healing is already a strong alternative (S5).
