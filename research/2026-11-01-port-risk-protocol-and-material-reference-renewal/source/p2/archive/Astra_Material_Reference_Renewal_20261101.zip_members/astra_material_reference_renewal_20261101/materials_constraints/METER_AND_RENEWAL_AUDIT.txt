FINITE MECHANICAL OBSERVATION AND RENEWAL UNDER LOAD
2026-11-01. Independent audit for the root-owned renewable-geometry construction.

1. THE ROOT'S LINEAR HAMILTONIAN IS CORRECT, BUT IS NOT ITSELF A DEVICE

Root proposes
 H = 1/2 (Bu-d-ax)^T K (Bu-d-ax) + kappa*x^2/2,
 with Ka=s and B^T s=0.
Then k_eff=kappa+s^T K^-1 s,
 mean(x)=-s^T d/k_eff, var(x)=kBT/k_eff.
The cancellation of arbitrary u is exact in this linear model. The snapshot SNR
obeys (mean x)^2/var x <= 2 E_incompatible/kBT by Cauchy--Schwarz in the self-stress
subspace. This is one equilibrium-snapshot bound, not a bound on all time-averaged
observations of a long-lived static defect.

An ordinary linkage that only moves existing nodes gives a=Bv. Combining it with
B^T K a=0 implies v^T B^T K Bv=0 and hence a=0. A nontrivial instrument must change
internal natural lengths, or insert additional force-transducing internal degrees
of freedom. Calling ordinary boundary motion an arbitrary eigenstrain actuator
would silently supply an impossible operation.

2. SERIES FORCE TRANSDUCTION WORKS; AN ORDINARY PARALLEL PATCH CAN FAIL

For a damaged passive spring in mechanical series with a piezoelectric element,
let e be total extension, d the passive spring's changed rest length, and y the
piezo extension. A standard stable quadratic electromechanical model is
 U=1/2 kb(e-d-y)^2+1/2 kp*y^2-h*y*Q+1/2 b*Q^2,
 with b>h^2/kp. Eliminating y yields
 U_eff=1/2 k_eff(e-d)^2-h_eff*Q*(e-d)+1/2 b_eff*Q^2,
 k_eff=kb*kp/(kb+kp), h_eff=kb*h/(kb+kp),
 b_eff=b-h^2/(kb+kp).
Thus a series force transducer genuinely couples to the damaged member's elastic
extension. The remaining stability condition is
 b_eff-h_eff^2/k_eff=b-h^2/kp>0.

By contrast, a piezo patch mechanically parallel to the damaged spring has
 U=1/2 kb(e-d)^2+1/2 kp*e^2-h*Q*e+1/2 b*Q^2.
The electrical coupling is to e, not e-d. Weighting this coupling to cancel all
compatible e=Bu also removes the entire readout, including the desired signal.
This distinguishes physically different sensor placements.

A series transducer costs compliance and material. k_eff<min(kb,kp); preserving a
desired original stiffness requires stiffer/larger constituents or a different
transduction architecture. Calibration drift, transducer fracture, piezo fatigue,
nonlinear material response and added wiring remain physical fault channels.

For several series transducers with common charge Q, write their effective
couplings as theta_i and total electrical curvature b_tot. If B^T theta=0,
 mean(Q)=-theta^T d/b_tot, var(Q)=kBT/b_tot.
Passivity requires b_tot>theta^T K^-1 theta. Adding the electrical meter reduces
incompatible strain energy through a Schur complement. In the ideal linear
self-stress case it does not change B^T K B, but it does screen the defect stress.
Finite electromechanical coupling and readout capacitance cannot improve the
snapshot SNR without cost. If material coupling fractions are bounded by chi<1,
the corresponding energy bound tightens by at least that factor.

3. WHY THE GENERIC-FRAME CANCELLATION FAILS AT FINITE DEFORMATION

For exact Euclidean bar lengths,
 Delta ell=B0*u+h2(u)+O(u^3),
 h2_e=||P_perp,e Delta u_e||^2/(2 ell_e).
A fixed s in ker(B0^T) leaves the healthy-load term s^T h2. In the square K4 cage,
the normalized self-stress gives the following false syndrome under horizontal
strain epsilon:
 epsilon/sqrt(2)-[sqrt((1+epsilon)^2+1)-sqrt(2)]
 =-epsilon^2/(4sqrt(2))+O(epsilon^3).
At 1 percent strain it is -1.75896e-5 in unit-side-length coordinates; at 10 percent
it is -0.00168263. These are genuine healthy-material signals, not numerical noise.

Fabrication/calibration errors also turn large load into false defect signal. With
theta=s+delta_s and B=B0+delta_B, the leading leakage is
 (B0^T delta_s+delta_B^T s)^T u.
Arbitrary unknown load plus finite calibration error cannot imply bounded false
signal without an explicit load range. Prestress adds geometric stiffness and
changes B with configuration. Recalculating s from current geometry can fix some
of this, but acquisition of that geometry must not be free.

4. EXACT COMMON-LENGTH COMPARISON IS A STRONGER PHYSICAL ROUTE

Three load paths with exactly the same endpoints have a common actual length L.
For matched Hookean branches f_i=k(L-ell_i), so force differences give natural-
length differences independent of L. The stronger statement is nonlinear:
if every branch has the SAME strictly increasing force law f_i=G(L-ell_i), then
 f_i=f_j iff ell_i=ell_j,
 and force ordering is the reverse of natural-length ordering.
Thus median-force selection and a zero-force-difference copying servo can restore
one corrupted reference without knowing the force law's slope or the current
loaded length. Estimating the numerical error amplitude needs more calibration;
identifying order and reaching equality does not.

A mechanical differential balance is a DC-capable candidate. For two matched
springs, an internal mirror rocker shifts anchors by opposite amounts +/-h(theta):
 H=1/2 k[L-ell_1-h(theta)]^2
   +1/2 k[L-ell_2+h(theta)]^2+V_meter(theta).
The coupling between common L and h cancels exactly even if the rocker kinematics
are nonlinear. An actual finite pivot, geometry, elastic reference and displacement
readout are required. The balance is existing force-comparison machinery, not a
claim of novel meter physics. Meter backaction rescreens the force difference;
the equilibrium zero still occurs at equal natural lengths for a symmetric meter.

Physical common-length implementations require care. Parallel offset members see
different extensions if endplates bend or rotate. Coincident multiedges in a graph
are an idealization; coaxial paths or shared pin/guide assemblies must have finite
stiffness, finite bending error and matching tolerances. Slack, buckling, hysteresis,
different temperature, different constitutive history, or unmatched replacement
parts can destroy order/equality equivalence. For unequal known Hookean stiffnesses
compare f_i/k_i; blindly equalizing forces copies a load-dependent bias.

5. PIEZOELECTRIC CHARGE IS A FINITE-TIME RECORD, NOT A PERMANENT DC OBSERVER

The ideal series circuit gives a physical common-charge coordinate if intermediate
conductor charges are known and conserved over the measurement. With effective
capacitance C_eff and loop resistance R,
 dQ=-(Q-C_eff V_source)dt/(R C_eff)+sqrt(2kBT/R)dW,
 tau_m=R C_eff, var(Q)=kBT C_eff.
For stationary OU noise with variance v, a uniform mean over T has variance
 2v[tau_m/T-(tau_m/T)^2(1-exp(-T/tau_m))].
It can beat a single snapshot by spending time, provided the signal and calibration
last long enough. Amplifier, dielectric, cable, flicker and drift noise are extra.

Individual dielectric/electrode leakage eventually destroys the ideal charge
constraint. A simple direct-charge force channel obeys
 C*dV/dt+V/R_leak=d_p*df/dt.
Its response to a force step decays with tau_leak=R_leak*C. A long-standing static
defect is not recovered by merely averaging longer. Modulating the common applied
load does not help a perfectly differential linear channel: f_i-f_j is constant.

An actual protocol must either preserve/reset known charge offsets and read within
the window, generate a known force transient by physically unloading and reloading
the transducers (load-bearing clutches/bypasses then count), or use an alternative
DC force meter. Piezoelectric resonance/impedance/capacitance methods can measure
static forces using active interrogation; the leakage statement applies to the
direct-charge scheme, not all possible piezo sensing.

For a known input step followed by source decay, the scalar two-time-constant model
has unit-amplitude mean response
 m(t)=tau_leak/(tau_leak-tau_m)
      *[exp(-t/tau_leak)-exp(-t/tau_m)].
The executable averages this exact response and combines it with the OU variance.
For tau_leak/tau_m=100, the best uniform averaging window is126.655*tau_m and the
squared SNR is20.3624 in units where initial signal amplitude and equilibrium noise
variance are1. Longer averaging loses information from this statistic. This is
only an optimum over uniform averages, not over every estimator or reset policy.

6. REST-LENGTH COPYING REQUIRES WORK AGAINST THE ACTUAL LOAD

For one harmonic branch at fixed common length, a rest-length change delta costs
 Delta E=-f_j*delta+k_j*delta^2/2.
For a bundle at fixed external force F, allowing L to relax, it costs
 Delta G=-f_j*delta+k_j*(1-k_j/K_total)*delta^2/2.
For three equal branches, two at ell_0 and one at ell_0+Delta, correcting the latter
requires reversible work
 W_rev=F*Delta/3-k*Delta^2/3.
Restoring a slightly too-long branch under tension therefore consumes work even
though it reduces incompatibility. Simple energy relaxation or exchange that
rebonds at the current loaded length tends toward creep instead of reference repair.

For general convex branch energy V(L-ell_j), the quasistatic work is
 W_rev=-integral f_j d ell_j,
at either fixed L or fixed F (using the appropriate force along the path). A fueled
actuator taking a discrete forward step obeys local detailed balance
 ln(w_forward/w_reverse)=beta*(Delta mu-Delta G_step).
Its stall condition and reverse errors must be included. In the elementary
two-exponential race, reverse probability p requires effective affinity at least
ln[(1-p)/p]. This is an elementary step model, not a universal minimal cost for a
feedback protocol which can reject and retry mistakes.

The force-comparison signal must be acquired while a replacement/adjustable path
actually carries load. Detaching it and setting its rest length to the instantaneous
gap sets zero force, thereby copying the deformation rather than the reference.

7. COMMON FAILURES ARE NOT FIXED BY THREE COPY LABELS

Uniform shifts of all three natural lengths are unobservable without an additional
standard. Shared thermal expansion, substrate drift, common fabrication offsets,
correlated plastic damage, common controller errors, meter zero drift and endplate
deformation can produce precisely such errors. Independent sparse faults are a
useful but restricted statistical premise. Meter and actuator failure must appear
in the same fault budget as material degradation; a stable comparator cannot be
renamed a free immutable template. The root's complete controller must specify how
its reference carrier can itself be checked/replaced and what common mode remains.
