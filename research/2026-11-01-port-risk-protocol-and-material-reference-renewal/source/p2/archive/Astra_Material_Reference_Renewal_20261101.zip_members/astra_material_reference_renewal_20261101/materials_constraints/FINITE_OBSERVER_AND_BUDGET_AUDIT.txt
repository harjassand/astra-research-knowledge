1. EXACT FINITE-WINDOW OU SIMULATION IN THE CONTROLLER

Let stationary OU noise have variance v, correlation time tau, and let a=exp(-T/tau).
Conditional on initial state x0, endpoint noise eta and average noise zeta have
 Var eta=v(1-a^2),
 Var zeta=v[2tau/T-4tau^2(1-a)/T^2+tau^2(1-a^2)/T^2],
 Cov(eta,zeta)=v*tau*(1-a)^2/T.
The full interval mean has conditional mean tau*(1-a)*x0/T. Root's Gaussian
construction samples exactly this joint covariance and carries the endpoint
forward, so correlations between consecutive observation windows are retained.
The calibration now spends three0.5s zero episodes and two0.5s common-gap episodes,
followed by1.8s control observation. Actual unload/reload, probe travel, settling,
latching and part delivery remain explicitly additional time.

2. THE RECIPROCAL ESTIMATOR USES THE STRONG ORDINARY GAUSSIAN SOLUTION

Within held polarity s=+1 or-1, write
 x_j=rho*x_(j-1)+(1-rho)*s*m+eta_j,
 y_j=x_j+b+epsilon_j,
 with eta variance q=v(1-rho^2), independent readout variance R, and arbitrary
unknown initial state. Then
 z_j=y_j-rho*y_(j-1)
    =(1-rho)(s*m+b)+eta_j+epsilon_j-rho*epsilon_(j-1).
This eliminates the unknown initial state exactly. The covariance is
 C=qI+R*D*D^T,
with diagonal q+R(1+rho^2) and adjacent off-diagonal -rho*R. The tridiagonal solve
in finite_observer.py is correct. Defining h=1^T C^-1 1, the GLS estimate of each
phase's intercept has variance1/[(1-rho)^2 h]. Independent equal-design phases give
 Var(m_hat)=1/[2(1-rho)^2 h],
 with m_hat=(mu_plus_hat-mu_minus_hat)/2 and b_hat=(mu_plus_hat+mu_minus_hat)/2.

This is not a deliberately weakened conventional estimator. In the full Gaussian
record, each initial-state nuisance contributes the column(1,rho,...,rho^n)^T.
D annihilates that column and supplies a maximal set of linear invariants. GLS on
the invariants equals full-record GLS after profiling the two initial-state
nuisances. It is the BLUE/MLE for the stated linear Gaussian model with known
response and covariance. A prior, alternative meter, nonlinear inference or an
additional physical observation changes that model rather than being ignored.

The two phases require independent fresh endpoint readout errors. If an actual
implementation reuses the same noisy boundary sample as both the last positive
and first negative reading, its cross-phase covariance must be included. The
root's34-read implementation gives each phase its own readout, so no such term is
missing from the stated model. Correlation of the mechanical initial states across
phases does not itself matter because those states are eliminated.

3. RESPONSE CALIBRATION AND PHYSICAL POLARITY ARE REAL CONDITIONS

Sign reversal must change the physical force-to-meter coupling; multiplying one
already acquired trace by-1 does not supply a second experiment. The mechanical
reference, readout offset b and calibrated response must remain valid across both
holds. Meter gain, time constant, noise covariance, switch transients, latch leakage
and actuator accuracy are not acquired by the algebra.

In particular, exact annihilation uses calibrated rho. With actual rho different
from the employed rho_hat, an innovation includes(rho-rho_hat)*x_(j-1). Finite
calibration error and literally unbounded initial displacement cannot imply
bounded systematic error. The physical claim should therefore say unknown initial
states WITHIN a calibrated linear and amplitude range, or provide a bound on rho
error times initial amplitude. The existing exact-parameter theorem remains valid.

4. THE ALL-COMPLETED-ROUND BOUND IS CORRECT

For the root's fixed cyclic copying protocol, let R_n=w^T r_n with
w=(0,1/3,2/3), let bar r_n be the arithmetic mean, and D_n=bar r_n-R_n. Starting
with equal copies, independent Gaussian per-copy errors of variance sigma^2 give
 Var R_M=4M sigma^2/3,
 Var D_n=sigma^2[4/27-64^(-(n-1))/432], n>=1.
The second expression proves Var D_n<=4sigma^2/27 for every n, rather than relying
on a finite numerical check. It follows from q=(1/3,0,-1/3), qAB=(-1/96,1/48,-1/24)
and qA^j=(-1/8)^(j-1)qA for j>=1, with qB=(1/12,-1/6,-1/3).

R_n is a Gaussian random walk. Embedding it in Brownian motion and applying the
reflection bound gives
 P(max_(n<=M)|R_n|>z_R*sigma*sqrt(4M/3))<=4*Phi(-z_R).
For D_n, a union bound gives
 P(max_(n<=M)|D_n|>z_D*sigma*sqrt(4/27))<=2M*Phi(-z_D).
No independence between R and D or between different D_n is needed. The code's
z_R=isf(delta_R/4) and z_D=isf(delta_D/(2M)) therefore produce a valid sufficient
certificate for every completed round. It does not cover mid-repair geometry.

The per-copy error must be TOTAL copying error to apply the result to hardware:
measurement, write/actuator error, relevant calibration error and correlations.
The present example declares an observer-limited iid Gaussian case from GLS.
It should not silently be promoted to the finite motor/latch's complete error law.

The separately listed systematic-bias allowance reserves half the geometric
precision. The current zero-bias stochastic certificate uses the full precision.
They cannot be combined without allocating that half to the noise bound as well.

5. THE TERMINAL NECESSARY TIME IS ALSO CORRECT IN THIS ARCHITECTURE

Even ideal continuous observation of the OU path gives conditional Fisher
information I_m=T/(2v tau) over balanced held-polarity phases. The constant-offset
cross-information cancels between phases, and arbitrary initial states supply no
extra prior information. Thus unbiased estimation has variance at least2v tau/T.
For Gaussian independent copy errors and the fixed cyclic controller, combine it
with the exact terminal arithmetic-mean variance coefficient c_M to obtain
 T >= 2v tau*c_M*[Phi^-1(1-delta/2)]^2/precision^2.
This is the root's necessary-time expression. It distinguishes genuine failure of
the declared architecture from merely failing a conservative sufficient bound.
It is not an estimator-independent no-go for biased Bayesian strategies, different
update rules, correlated-error hardware, additional retained history or standards.

6. OVERLAP EXPOSURE AND THE CODED COMPETITOR

With three renewals per round, two trusted references at risk during each renewal,
constant independent hazard lambda, and duration Tobs+overhead, no-reference-fault
survival through M rounds is exactly
 exp[-6M lambda(Tobs+overhead)].
The resulting upper window is exact for the chosen no-overlap budget. It is not a
universal success bound for an architecture that can tolerate, identify or retry
some faults during a replacement. Stress-dependent hazards, between-round faults,
common failures and material delivery must be separate physical budgets.

The ordinary coded-reference comparator correctly avoids copy-to-copy accumulation
if each member is freshly set against retained correct data and a working physical
dimensional standard. Its arithmetic-mean error variance is sigma_code^2/3 at a
completed round, and the union-bound calculation is consistent. The standard's
acquisition, calibration, maintenance and hardware costs remain unpriced; this
comparison establishes a conditional performance difference, not a total resource
advantage for either construction.
