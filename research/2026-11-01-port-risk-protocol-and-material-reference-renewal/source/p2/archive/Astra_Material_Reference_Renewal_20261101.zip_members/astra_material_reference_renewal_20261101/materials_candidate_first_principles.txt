2026-11-01 -- first-principles candidate, before targeted literature or Astra retrieval

Capability: a precisely specified heterogeneous material that can remove assembly
mistakes and replace damaged constituents after growth, without per-site imaging
or sacrificing its mechanical integrity during every repair cycle.

Minimal candidate: reversible local bonds plus a geometry-dependent protective
state. A constituent first binds through reversible contacts. Closure of several
independent geometric constraints changes a real molecular conformation, which
changes access to a bond-exchange reaction. A correct neighborhood spends most
time protected; a strained or incompatible one remains exchange-active. Fuel
may power exchange or resetting, but cannot supply a semantic correctness oracle.

The information specifying the target must reside in physically prepared parts,
a reusable template, a boundary, or control history. It cannot be inferred from
the words correct and wrong. Count that information and repair of its carrier.
The selectivity must follow from an explicit energy/rate model with detailed
balance or a specified chemical affinity. Distinct rates cannot be stipulated
merely because a site is wrong. Exchange access and mass transport into the bulk
are part of the mechanism. Locally compatible wrong structures must be tested.

First discriminating question: does adding a driven protective state create a
repair/stability separation unavailable to reversible annealing or existing
kinetic proofreading once preparation, false protection, reverse reactions,
transport, and fuel are included? If not, reject it as the central opening.

Blind independent constructor received only capability and physical costs,
not this mechanism. Its early counterconstruction is DNA proofreading plus
self-healing tile systems, and origami staple replacement that cannot repair
the scaffold. This defeats novelty of the generic proofreading/healing claim.
