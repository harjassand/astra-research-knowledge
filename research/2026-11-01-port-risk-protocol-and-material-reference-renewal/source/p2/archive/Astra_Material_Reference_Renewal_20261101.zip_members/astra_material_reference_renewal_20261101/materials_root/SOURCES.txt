1. PRIMARY SELF-ASSEMBLY AND REPAIR RESEARCH

Soloveichik, Cook and Winfree, Combining Self-Healing and Proofreading in
Self-Assembly (2007/2008), DOI 10.1007/s11047-007-9036-x.
https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/7gj5t-g0873
The independent report records seed protection, idealized interactions and
kinetic/concentration conditions. This is a strong algorithmic construction,
not a generic chemistry-independent repair oracle.

Scheckenbach et al., Self-Regeneration and Self-Healing of DNA Origami
Nanostructures (2021), DOI 10.1002/anie.202012986.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7986372/
Replacement staples can restore portions of a DNA-origami structure; the original
scaffold remains a reference. The experimental scope matters for total turnover.

Notebook Git blob SHA1: 9e54620edd3104f2210375c9cd74458c45758364
Its programmed classifier and interaction switches do not by themselves earn a
molecular environment detector or a physically implemented fuel cycle.

Nonenzymatic DNA kinetic proofreading (2024):
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11258683/

2. PRIMARY MECHANICS AND STRONG CONVENTIONAL ALTERNATIVES

Adam and Smith (2007), Self-Diagnosis and Self-Repair of an Active Tensegrity
Structure, DOI 10.1061/(ASCE)0733-9445(2007)133:12(1752).
https://doi.org/10.1061/(ASCE)0733-9445(2007)133:12(1752)

Reksowardojo et al. (2025), Application of Self-Diagnosis and Self-Repair on a
Truss Prototype That Adapts to Loading Through Shape Morphing.
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1155/stc/8827609
The experiment supplies active diagnosis and stress compensation after removal
of members. It is not an experiment on complete matter turnover or recovery of
unknown original natural lengths. The root read the governing hardware and
analysis sections, and the independent report preserves their limitations.

Wang and Senatore (2020), Extended Integrated Force Method,
DOI 10.1016/j.ijsolstr.2020.05.029.
Xue et al. (2021), sparse prestress adjustment,
DOI 10.1016/j.engstruct.2021.112987.
These supply conventional self-stress/compatibility and rest-length actuation
machinery. They are not credited to this investigation as new principles.

Alfakih, Jung, Wolkowicz and Xu (2024), Single Element Error Correction in a
Euclidean Distance Matrix.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.15969v1
Known embedding dimension and exactly one corrupted distance are essential
conditions. The authors analyze ambiguity. Our independent K7 mechanics test is
a new small benchmark using ordinary numerical fitting, not an execution of the
paper's facial-reduction algorithm or a proof of global numerical convergence.

Wensrich et al. (2025), Well-posedness and trivial solutions to inverse eigenstrain
problems, DOI 10.1016/j.ijsolstr.2025.113505.
Compatible eigenstrains form an important inverse-stress nullspace. The root's
finite-dimensional proof makes the relevant obstruction explicit.

Gregg et al. (2024), ARMADAS, Science Robotics,
DOI 10.1126/scirobotics.adi2746.
https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20230005194/downloads/SciRobotics-ARMADAS_System_Paper_Distribution.pdf
Physical lattice assembly/disassembly, routing and reversible fastening are
valuable real ingredients; complete autonomous under-load renewal was not shown.

Moreno et al. (2026), distributed physical bricks,
DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-75166-7.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13365805/
Shape classification and damage localization do not by themselves preserve a
specific specimen's original metric or physically replace damaged matter.

Jin et al. (2024), Engineering error correcting dynamics in nanomechanical systems.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-71679-7
The logical-state correction model is a relevant existing mechanical-memory
construction; it is not natural-length restoration of a load-bearing structure.
