RENEWING A MATERIAL'S GEOMETRIC REFERENCE UNDER LOAD
1 November 2026. Fresh physical-technology investigation.

STATUS AND PRECISE OBJECTIVE

The intended consequence is precision structures that can replace worn material
without gradually forgetting their stress-free geometry. Such a capability would
matter for machines, instruments, manufactured infrastructure and eventually
actively maintained materials. This investigation does not establish a
civilisation-changing technology, historical priority, or a complete resource
advantage. It develops and tests a conditional physical construction, eliminates
several attractive shortcuts, and makes the remaining reference-maintenance
problem quantitative.

The positive construction copies a member's NATURAL LENGTH, while it is carrying
load, from two current surviving members. It uses force comparison, a finite
physical observation protocol, and powered rest-length adjustment. No particular
member must remain an intact geometric master. The initial target information
still resides in manufactured physical references; it has not been created from
fuel. Junctions, controller, delivery equipment, sensors and actuators have finite
costs and failure channels. Full autonomous replacement of all of that equipment
is NOT supplied by the member-level construction.

Three scopes must remain distinct:
  (a) exact conditional mathematics and finite-state/linear-model calculations;
  (b) executable numerical evidence using declared, mostly illustrative parameters;
  (c) experimental material performance, which was not acquired.

The earlier sensor and thermal-mediator research is preserved unchanged in the
prior archive. No quantum-information capability is the objective of this run.


1. FIRST CANDIDATE AND WHY IT WAS NOT SELECTED

Before targeted primary or Astra retrieval, the candidate was a heterogeneous
material with geometry-dependent protection of reversible bonds. Locally correct
closure would protect a part; strained or incompatible closure would leave it
exchangeable. Chemical fuel could drive replacement. The root recorded this in
materials_candidate_first_principles.txt. An independent constructor received the
capability and physical constraints, not this preferred mechanism.

Two failures defeated the general claim.

1.1 Isogeometric substitutions. Put different functional payloads on identical
binding faces. Swapping payloads leaves every geometrical measurement unchanged.
A controller whose transition law is invariant under that swap cannot select the
specified chemical arrangement. For fixed composition, a symmetry orbit can
contain binomial(N,n_A) different arrangements. Fuel does not break that symmetry.
Specific chemical recognition would be an additional physical resource.

1.2 Nonselective protection. The companion five-state Markov model has vacancy,
correct/open, correct/protected, wrong/open and wrong/protected states. Both
identities have identical geometry and protection kinetics; passive and fueled
protection channels individually satisfy local detailed balance. If x is the
correct feed fraction and d_C,d_W are open-state removal rates, then

 P(correct | occupied) = x d_W / [x d_W + (1-x)d_C].

The common protection cycle can increase residence time and dissipate fuel while
leaving accuracy unchanged. The explicit example increases affinity from 0 to
10 kBT and residence from 12.96 to 150.18 time units while accuracy remains 0.3.
This refutes that particular shortcut, not kinetic proofreading in general.

Strong existing constructions were allowed to change the question. DNA copying,
tile proofreading plus regrowth, replacement of damaged DNA-origami staples,
scaffold-free DNA structures and magnetic selective disassembly already supply
important versions of the proposed idea. Zhu et al.'s public 2024 notebook also
implements local-environment classification followed by stronger/weakening bonds;
its explicit classifier/species switches do not themselves instantiate a chemical
detector or fuel cycle. Liang et al.'s magnetic decoupling work provides an actual
physical discriminator for its small target assembly. The reports preserve their
actual conditions rather than dismissing them as weak competitors.

Associative covalent exchange also already preserves network connectivity during
rearrangement. In a simple transient-network model it loses reference stress,
G(t)=G0 exp(-beta t). That model is not universal: the October 2026 vitrimer work
shows that reaction and segmental diffusion can decouple. Neither retaining bond
count nor slowing stress relaxation establishes preservation of a specified shape.

This led to the sharper problem: repair the reference that tells material what
shape to return to, rather than just reconnecting it.


2. STRONG EXISTING ROUTES TO THE SHARPER OBJECTIVE

Compatibility-based passive damage detection, active tensegrity correction,
sparse prestress adjustment and robust distance-geometry reconstruction are
established ingredients. Their combination is a serious comparator.

An especially concrete 2025 truss experiment uses a 6.6 m, 20-node, 54-member
structure, 12 length actuators, member force sensors and eight infrared cameras.
It diagnoses removal of redundant members and redistributes stress. It does not
replace matter or recover unknown original natural lengths; it also neglects
several noise/model uncertainties in its analysis. It nevertheless defeats a
claim that active mechanical diagnosis and compensation is a new capability.

The independent constructor synthesized and executed a stronger conventional
route for the same natural-length problem. Measure actual length l_i and force
f_i; for a calibrated linear spring infer r_i=l_i-f_i/k_i. Decode sparse corruptions
of the inferred natural lengths with existing Euclidean-distance machinery, then
adjust the damaged member. A generic initially unstressed K7 framework in 3D can
uniquely correct one corrupted length because removing any two edges preserves
global rigidity. The decoder does not receive pristine coordinates. It pays for
length and force channels, calibration, an actuator bus and computation.

Its executed example restores one 30 mm length drift under a load of norm 762.9 N.
With finite illustrative measurement/calibration errors it identifies the same
member and leaves 2.85 micrometres of rest-length error and 6.86 micrometres of
loaded-geometry error. This is one simulated case, not a manufactured device or
a universal convergence guarantee. The method is not automatically applicable to
an intentionally incompatible/prestressed rest metric.

A still simpler comparator stores each intended rest length digitally and uses
calibrated metrology to reinstall it. Its code, dimensional standard and service
system must be protected, but it avoids accumulating the copying error analysed
below. This comparator remains stronger for long-term reference preservation in
the model unless its additional physical metrology is unavailable or too costly.


3. WHAT A GEOMETRIC COMPATIBILITY CHECK CAN AND CANNOT CORRECT

Let u be infinitesimal node displacements, B the bar compatibility matrix, K>0
the diagonal stiffness matrix, and d the vector of rest-length changes. Then

 E(u,d) = 1/2 (Bu-d)^T K (Bu-d).

Let P project onto ker(B^T K^(1/2)). Minimizing over u gives

 E_min(d) = 1/2 ||P K^(1/2)d||^2.

Every compatible error d=Bv is silent to internal-stress tests, although it can
change the body's unloaded shape. The K4 rectangle example in the companion
proof is exact beyond linearization: natural lengths chosen from another valid
rectangle have zero energy in that rectangle.

There is a useful exact LINEAR correction criterion. Let H have nullspace im B
(equivalently its rows span ker B^T). Every d with at most t nonzero components is
uniquely determined by Hd iff deleting any set of at most 2t rows of B preserves
its rank.

Proof. Two t-sparse errors have the same syndrome iff their difference is a
nonzero 2t-sparse vector z in im B. Write z=Bv with support S. Then B_(S^c)v=0
but Bv is nonzero, so deletion of S has enlarged the nullspace and reduced rank.
The converse reverses these steps. QED.

This is a coding/rigidity interpretation of existing compatibility algebra, not
a claim to invent either subject. Quantitative robustness needs a positive
minimum singular value on all admitted 2t-column syndrome submatrices. If that
minimum is gamma and the record error has norm <=epsilon, a feasible t-sparse
decoder has error <=2epsilon/gamma.

The linear criterion does NOT imply global nonlinear uniqueness. In planar K5,
reflect one vertex across the line through two neighbours. Its distances to those
two neighbours are unchanged; only two other distances change. A received vector
using one changed distance from each valid geometry is one-error corrupt relative
to either. The supplied test has tangent distance three, but exact one-error
recovery is ambiguous. A small-deformation basin or global rigidity after deletion
of 2t edges is required. This failure changed the physical construction.


4. CENTRAL CONSTRUCTION: COPY AN UNLOADED REFERENCE THROUGH CURRENT FORCE

Use three replaceable, independently adjustable load paths sharing the same two
kinematic endpoints. Let their natural lengths be r_i. They see a common actual
length L, which may change with an unknown load. Initially r_1=r_2=r_3=r_*.

For matched Hookean members,
 f_i = k(L-r_i),
 f_i-f_j = k(r_j-r_i).

Two agreeing surviving paths retain a reference for the third. The newcomer is
adjusted while loaded until its normalized force matches the references. Setting
its zero-force length to the currently observed gap would instead copy elastic
deformation into the material and cause creep.

This uses a physical reference encoded in the surviving members. It does not
deduce an arbitrary target from equilibrium. Every member can be replaced in
turn; no particular one is an immortal master. Copies subject to common damage
or simultaneous errors are outside the one-fault premise.

4.1 Nonlinear zero comparison. Suppose instead
 f_i = G(L-r_i)
for one common strictly increasing, memoryless constitutive law G. Then
 f_i=f_j iff r_i=r_j,
and force order is the reverse of natural-length order. No known slope is needed
to identify the zero or order. Thus the median-force rule identifies one outlier
when the other two agree. This result permits finite common deformation.

If m<=G'(z)<=M on the admitted range and a preselected newcomer obeys
 dr_i/dt = gamma [G(L-r_i)-G(L-r_*)],
then e=r_i-r_* satisfies
 d(e^2)/dt <= -2 gamma m e^2,
and |e(t)|<=|e(0)| exp(-gamma m t). This holds for any common L(t) within the
quasistatic constitutive regime. A bounded additive force-comparison error eta
gives the usual error floor |eta|/m. The executable verifies contraction for
G(z)=z+2z^3 under changing common deformation.

Hysteresis, slack, buckling, different temperatures, different material histories
or unequal nonlinear laws invalidate this premise. Two-point linear calibration
does not generally repair those departures.

4.2 What is actually corrected. The construction preserves natural lengths and
therefore the original loaded equilibrium under the same external conditions
within a stable mechanical basin. It does not hold unloaded coordinates fixed
while a load continues to act. Restoring stress-free geometry and suppressing all
elastic deflection are different physical objectives.


5. A FINITE PHYSICAL COMPARATOR, INCLUDING BACKACTION

An ordinary linkage that moves only original truss nodes has a=Bv. If it is also
required to satisfy B^T K a=0, then a^T K a=0 and a=0. It cannot provide a nontrivial
self-stress meter. Actual internal force transduction or eigenstrain actuation
must be introduced.

For two matched members, a finite internal rocker shifts their anchors by +/-x:

 U = k/2 (L-r_1-x)^2 + k/2 (L-r_2+x)^2 + kappa x^2/2.

The comparison coordinate has
 h=2k+kappa,
 mean(x)=k(r_2-r_1)/h,
 Var(x)=kBT/h
at thermal equilibrium. The total terminal force is k(2L-r_1-r_2), independent
of x. Thus the ideal differential meter changes incompatibility stress without
changing the bundle's terminal stiffness. Its pivot stiffness, inertia, damping,
endplate compliance and readout are real components, not costless projections.

For a nonlinear convex branch energy V with V'=G, the physical potential is
 V(L-r_1-x)+V(L-r_2+x)+kappa x^2/2.
Its derivative at zero is -G(L-r_1)+G(L-r_2); strict convexity gives the same
zero and sign test. With m<=G'<=M and d=r_2-r_1>0, the minimum lies between
 m d/(2m+kappa) and M d/(2M+kappa).
For equal references the potential is even, so its equilibrium mean is exactly
zero. Quantitative Gaussian precision formulas below use the Hookean case.

Finite damping gives an overdamped OU comparator,
 dx = -(x-m_s)dt/tau + sqrt(2 v/tau) dW,
 tau=zeta/h, v=kBT/h.
The equivalent natural-length variance for one equilibrium observation is
 h kBT/k^2. For a uniform average of duration T the multiplicative factor is
 2[tau/T-(tau/T)^2(1-exp(-T/tau))].
These are a sensor law and time cost, not 64 independent samples in an arbitrary
short interval. The corrected controller simulates the joint OU endpoint and
window-integral distribution exactly.

The one-snapshot signal-to-noise ratio is at most 2 E_incompatible/kBT. Longer
observation can improve it; that is not an absolute information ceiling. In the
long-window overdamped limit the equivalent variance is
 2 kBT zeta/(k^2 T).
Increasing pivot stiffness alone does not remove this fluctuation-dissipation
cost. Reducing damping until inertia matters requires a different finite response
model rather than continued use of the overdamped equation.

To obtain independent pair tests, unlock/read one comparison coordinate at a time
and hold unused ones at their neutral settings. Simultaneously adding several
rockers creates a coupled stiffness matrix; they are not independent ideal force
copies. Sequential tests cost time and current-reference validation.


6. A COMPLETE FINITE OBSERVER; INITIAL STATE AND OFFSET ARE NOT FREE

A direct-charge piezoelectric sensor forgets a static force through leakage.
Also, a parallel piezo patch senses total compatible extension, so cancelling that
extension can remove the entire signal. A series force transducer genuinely
couples to a damaged member's elastic extension; the companion audit derives its
Schur complement and added compliance. The central readout therefore uses a DC
differential balance, or an explicitly prepared finite-time alternative.

For the linear DC balance, physically reverse the differential coupling and use
two held polarities s=+1,-1. Reversal must actually change the interaction. Merely
negating a digitized reading does not remove its offset. During each held phase,
sample at spacing Delta, with rho=exp(-Delta/tau):

 x_j = rho x_(j-1) + (1-rho) s m + xi_j,
 y_j = x_j+b+epsilon_j,
 Var(xi_j)=v(1-rho^2), Var(epsilon_j)=r.

The offset b is unknown but constant across the two phases. Their initial x
values are unknown and separately observed with the same finite readout noise.
No stationary preparation at the unknown signal is needed. Exact cancellation
holds at the calibrated response rho. Physically, the unknown initial states
must lie within the calibrated linear and amplitude range.

Form z_j=y_j-rho y_(j-1). Within one phase,
 E z_j=(1-rho)(s m+b).
The entire initial-state contribution cancels. With D the difference matrix whose
rows contain (-rho,1), the record covariance is

 Sigma=v(1-rho^2) I+r D D^T.

It is tridiagonal: diagonal v(1-rho^2)+r(1+rho^2), adjacent entries -rho r.
The two phases have separate initial readings and disjoint process-noise
increments, so their innovation blocks are independent. Let
 w=Sigma^-1 1, W=1^T w,
 mu_s=w^T z_s / [(1-rho) W].
Then
 m_hat=(mu_+-mu_-)/2,
 b_hat=(mu_++mu_-)/2,
 Var(m_hat)=1/[2(1-rho)^2 W].

This is ordinary generalized least squares for the full specified Gaussian
experiment. It is unbiased, with exact finite covariance, and is the optimal
linear unbiased estimator; in this Gaussian location model it is the likelihood
estimator after eliminating the arbitrary initial states. It does not claim a
new statistical principle.

If the position readout were noiseless and each phase used n intervals,
 Var(m_hat)=v/[2n tanh(Delta/(2tau))].
There are 2(n+1) finite readings and 2n Delta observation time. Reversal, routing,
initial parameter calibration and physical delivery take additional time/work.
Unknown changes in b between phases cause bias and remain in the budget.

Response uncertainty is not cancelled for free. If the estimator uses rho_hat
while the actual response is rho, the innovation retains
 (rho-rho_hat)(x_(j-1)-s m).
If the mean transient amplitude in each phase is bounded by A, its signal bias
is at most |rho-rho_hat| A/(1-rho_hat), using positive GLS weights. Thus a finite
response-calibration error cannot support an unbounded initial displacement.
The reported Gaussian covariance is conditional on the stated response and
noise covariance; acquisition and drift of those parameters require a budget.

An executed 8,000-run check uses 34 readings over 16 ms, arbitrary initial states
10 nm and -7 nm, a 2 nm signal, unknown 8 nm offset, 1 nm equilibrium meter noise,
0.2 nm independent readout noise, and tau=1 ms. Predicted signal SD is 0.35977 nm;
the Monte Carlo SD is 0.35457 nm. These are illustrative model parameters, not
measured sensor specifications. The physical reversal is a conditional operation,
not an experimentally acquired nanomechanical commutator.


7. UNEQUAL LINEAR STIFFNESSES AND SENSOR GAINS CAN BE CALIBRATED RELATIVELY

Let a finite force channel report
 z_i=a_i(L-r_i)+b_i,
where a_i is the product of stiffness and sensor gain. Acquire b_i in a real
force-free state. Hold r_i fixed and visit two common lengths, whose actual
amplitude is not supplied to the controller. Then
 Delta z_i=a_i Delta L,
 (z_i-b_i)/Delta z_i=(L-r_i)/Delta L.

Subtracting the average of two reference channels produces exactly
 [(r_j+r_k)/2-r_i]/Delta L.
Only the sign of the probe motion is required. The root's finite controller uses
these actual records, not pristine r_* or external F.

Zeroing is not free: detach or unload one path while others support the load,
acquire the zero, then reload it without changing its natural reference. Its
clutch, reversible attachment, settling and actuator work remain requirements.
The code explicitly spends 0.5 s in each of three zero episodes and two shared
probe episodes: 2.5 s of observation. The control phase uses 900 windows of 2 ms,
for another 1.8 s, with exact OU correlation tau=1 ms. It does not compress
independent measurements into those windows. Mechanical operations add time.

In one declared example the preselected newcomer starts 120 nm too long; unequal
stiffnesses and sensor gains are present. The final copy error is 0.00977 nm in
4.3 s of observation. This is an algorithm/physics-model check, not device
performance. An 8 nm equivalent sensor-zero bias instead produces -7.989 nm
of repair error. The experiment renews a selected newcomer; it does not by itself
diagnose arbitrary sensor versus material faults.

For unequal linear branches an adjustable rocker can also null common-load
coupling. With anchor motions alpha x and -beta x its load coefficient is
 alpha k1-beta k2. A finite common deformation probe can null that coefficient
without knowing the probe amplitude. Reciprocal physical readout then cancels a
constant electronic offset. The illustrative bisection check includes finite OU
windows, transients and 36.72 s of calibration. Its 18 commanded bisection steps
are NOT a confidence certificate of 18 accurate mechanical bits. Actual achieved
error and its load leakage are reported; drift and noisy sign decisions limit it.


8. REPAIR WORK, STALL AND HOLDING THE RESULT

Changing one member's natural length by delta at fixed common L changes its
elastic energy by
 Delta E=-f_j delta+k_j delta^2/2.
At fixed total external force F, permitting the common length to re-equilibrate,
the relevant potential change is
 Delta G=-f_j delta+k_j(1-k_j/K_total)delta^2/2.

For three equal members, two at r_* and one at r_*+d, exact correction costs
 W_rev=F d/3-k d^2/3.
Thus removing mismatch energy does not imply that repair is downhill. Shortening
a slightly too-long member under tension usually requires positive work against
the applied load. A passive exchange process driven only by elastic relaxation
would select the current loaded length and creep.

The root code records positive quasistatic mechanical work, separately from
readout, actuator loss and calibration. Its example spends about 1.0121e-14 J
on positive correction work; that is not the total energy of the repair system.

A finite chemical/mechanical actuator cannot be supplied as a noiseless arbitrary
rest-length update. One explicit local-detailed-balance model has two opposing
fuel pathways on a mechanical edge. In units kBT, let A be chemical affinity,
w the mechanical energy increment, and c x a measured mechanical gate bias:

 a+ = nu exp[cx+(A-w)/2],   a- = nu exp[cx-(A-w)/2],
 b+ = nu exp[-cx+(-A-w)/2], b- = nu exp[-cx-(-A-w)/2].

Each pathway and its reversal have the correct affinity. Aggregating channels,
 log(w_forward/w_reverse)=-w+A_eff(x),
 A_eff=log cosh(cx+A/2)-log cosh(cx-A/2),  |A_eff|<=A.

The drive therefore cannot overcome an arbitrarily large load. At zero gate
signal A_eff=0: it is not an ideal holder against a load. A physical latch or
separately paid maintaining drive is required. In the elementary equal-occupation
edge contrast, an error ratio p needs A_eff-w >= log[(1-p)/p]. This is a local
rate relation, not the probability that an entire multi-step repair succeeds.
The code verifies the rate identities and retains the zero-signal creep failure.

A latch has finite force capacity and a finite barrier. Under an explicitly
assumed activated escape model nu0 exp(-E_b/kBT), duration t with probability
budget epsilon requires E_b >= kBT log(nu0 t/epsilon). Attempt frequency and load
dependence of the barrier must be acquired; this formula is not a material spec.
For nanometre strokes, useful loads can require far more than one chemical fuel
quantum per step. Mechanical advantage or many motors changes area, stroke and
speed costs rather than erasing the work requirement.

Removing one of three equal paths changes equilibrium extension from F/(3k) to
F/(2k), a transient difference F/(6k). Surviving member load rises from F/3 to F/2.
Either admit that elastic excursion and the corresponding hazard, or provide a
real load-transfer bypass. Instantaneous unchanged stiffness through replacement
is not silently assumed. A make-before-break matched handoff can in principle
preserve total stiffness, but its engagement law, capacity and work must be built.


9. REPEATED RENEWAL HAS A COMMON REFERENCE MODE

Let each new member copy the mean of the other two with an independent, zero-mean
write error eta_i of variance sigma_c^2. Replace members in fixed order 1,2,3.
At completed-round boundaries,

 r_(n+1)=A r_n+B eta_n,
 A=[[0,1/2,1/2],[0,1/4,3/4],[0,3/8,5/8]],
 B=[[1,0,0],[1/2,1,0],[3/4,1/2,1]].

The normalized left fixed vector is w=(0,1/3,2/3); wB=(2/3,2/3,2/3).
Thus the retained reference coordinate R_n=w r_n satisfies
 R_(n+1)=R_n+2(eta_1+eta_2+eta_3)/3,
 Var(R_N)=Var(R_0)+(4/3)N sigma_c^2.
The other eigenvalues are 0 and -1/8: disagreement decays while the common
reference wanders. A shared write bias b gives mean shift 2Nb.

The actual force-free length of three equal parallel members is their mean.
Starting from an exact shared reference, its variance coefficient is exactly

 C_N = 4N/3-4/9+(-1/8)^(N-1)/27-(1/64)^(N-1)/432.

Its shared-bias coefficient is
 2N-4/9+(-1/8)^(N-1)/36.
Furthermore
 Var(mean(r_N)-R_N)=sigma_c^2[4/27-64^(-(N-1))/432]
is at most 4 sigma_c^2/27 for every N>=1.
These formulas follow by summing the independent B-noise contributions through
the powers of A; the executable checks them against covariance recursions.

The 5,000-trajectory check with sigma_c=1 nm agrees with the predicted reference
variance to 0.8 percent after 1,500 rounds. Reference SD is about 44.72 nm, although
each copying operation has only 1 nm error. A coded-reference comparator with
independent 1 nm installation errors has mean-length SD about 1/sqrt(3) nm at a
completed round, conditional on retained code and working dimensional calibration.

This is NOT a universal impossibility theorem. Different copying schedules,
correlated errors, retained controller history, a conserved physical quantity or
an additional dimensional reference change the model. Synchronous averaging of
old references, for example, has a different coefficient and different storage
and load-support requirements. The exact coefficient belongs to this protocol.


10. FINITE OBSERVATION AND REFERENCE EXPOSURE MUST BE OPTIMIZED TOGETHER

The repeat-copying calculation permits a finite-horizon certificate. For M
completed rounds, let epsilon be the permitted error of the mean natural length.
Let Phi be the standard Gaussian CDF. Gaussian R_n increments can be embedded in
Brownian motion, giving
 P(max_(n<=M)|R_n-R_0|>epsilon_R)
   <=4 Phi[-epsilon_R/(sigma_c sqrt(4M/3))].
For D_n=mean(r_n)-R_n, a finite union bound gives
 P(max_(n<=M)|D_n|>epsilon_D)
   <=2M Phi[-epsilon_D/(sigma_c sqrt(4/27))].

With budgets delta_R,delta_D and z_R=Phi^-1(1-delta_R/4),
z_D=Phi^-1(1-delta_D/(2M)), it suffices that

 sigma_c [sqrt(4M/3) z_R + sqrt(4/27) z_D] + 2M|b| <=epsilon.

This covers completed-round boundaries. Transient loaded geometry, within-round
excursions and other physical failures require separate bounds.

The observer variance is not assigned independently of time. renewal_budget.py
uses the exact finite reciprocal GLS covariance in Section 6, including noisy
endpoint readings. It chooses the smallest integer number of readings meeting
the copy-precision requirement. It does not use the weaker uniform average when
declaring the limiting comparison.

The numerical example is explicitly OBSERVER-LIMITED with independent Gaussian
copy errors and zero systematic bias. A complete motor implementation must use
TOTAL write-error covariance, including measurement, actuator/write uncertainty,
calibration, latch motion and any correlations. Those extra errors cannot be
silently set to zero in a claim about an actual device. The separate JSON field
showing a bias allowance epsilon/(4M) reserves half the geometric tolerance for
bias; it cannot be combined with a noise certificate that already spends the
entire tolerance. Recompute the observation requirement using the remainder.

Suppose each of the two remaining references has independent failure hazard
lambda during a replacement. For 3M nonoverlapping replacement windows of total
duration t_op=T_obs+t_other, survival of all such exposures is
 exp[-6M lambda t_op].
Hence the overlap budget delta_o requires
 T_obs <= -log(1-delta_o)/(6M lambda)-t_other.

This hazard must be evaluated at the INCREASED surviving load and actual
temperature. Constant lambda is an explicit model, not a property of all materials.
Correlated failures, prior unnoticed faults and unavailable supply are additional.
The exponential is exact for the declared NO-REFERENCE-FAULT exposure contract,
not a universal failure probability of a controller that can tolerate or retry
some faults.

Even the ideal continuously read OU model, after eliminating an arbitrary initial
state, provides conditional information rate 1/(2v tau). In the specified unbiased
Gaussian overwrite architecture, the exact terminal variance therefore supplies
the optimistic necessary time

 T_obs >= 2 v tau C_M [Phi^-1(1-delta_ref/2)]^2 / epsilon^2.

The use of this necessary time is limited to the declared architecture and noise
model. It does not exclude a different physical memory, correlated correction or
an informed estimator with separately supplied target information.

An illustrative budget uses v^(1/2)=1 nm, tau=1 ms, independent readout SD=0.2 nm,
1 ms sampling, epsilon=10 nm, lambda=1e-5/s per remaining reference, 6 ms other
overhead, delta_R=delta_D=0.0025, and delta_o=0.01.

 Rounds   sufficient finite-GLS time   maximum exposure time
 100                  0.034 s                    1.6691 s
 500                  0.160 s                    0.3290 s
 700                  0.224 s                    0.2333 s
 1000                 0.316 s                    0.1615 s

At 1,000 rounds the optimistic necessary terminal time is already 0.2100 s,
larger than the allowed 0.1615 s. This is not merely failure of the sufficient
all-round bound. It excludes that particular renewal/observer contract. These
numbers are uncalibrated design values, not an empirical lifetime prediction.

The separately supplied coded-reference comparator avoids the common random walk
and satisfies the observation/exposure subproblem in this example. Its dimensional
reference, decoding and access costs are not included in that numerical comparison;
therefore no total hardware superiority is inferred in either direction.


11. PUBLIC RESEARCH CODE TESTED A REAL OBSERVER COST

The pinned PiezoD source (commit 021e5f96c3c65d709318a1062288e32fe54d3bb1,
version 0.13.2) is retained with MIT license and exact Git-blob checks. Unmodified
epitaxy, elasticity and noise modules were executed. Optional implantation data
and unused package-distribution metadata were not needed. This is a public-model
calculation, not calibration of the proposed compound structure.

The root tested a 100 x 10 x 1 micrometre silicon cantilever as the comparison
coordinate's DC displacement readout. Its model stiffness is 0.4225 N/m. With
1 N/m load-bearing paths, the displacement-to-rest-difference factor is 2.4225.
Removing the free-cantilever thermomechanical component avoids adding it twice to
the separately specified coupled-rocker thermal model.

 Over 1-1000 Hz:  resistor power     electronic rest-difference RMS   peak rise
 0.1 V              1.886 uW                  2.749 nm                0.040 K
 1.0 V            188.630 uW                  0.291 nm                4.028 K
 3.0 V           1697.673 uW                  0.132 nm               36.248 K

Only one sensing resistor's Joule power is listed. The bridge, amplifier,
controller, interconnect, thermal packaging and actuators add costs. Integrated
band noise is not a sequence of independent readings, and is not itself the OU
spectrum used in the inference example. The compound rocker has different
resonance and damping from a free cantilever. At the larger temperature rise the
ambient-property noise calculation needs further material closure.

The independent thin-beam case makes the failure sharper: 10 V predicts about
1.23 pN force noise but about 303 K peak temperature rise in the same model. The
appealing low-noise number cannot be selected while ignoring that consequence.

At one million simultaneously active 1 V readout resistors, their listed power
alone would be about 189 W. Duty cycling, sharing a reader, or mechanical logic
could reduce that cost, but changes monitoring latency, routing and failure
exposure. None is a free replacement for the specified measurement.

13. A REPRESENTATION CHANGE: CONSERVATION MUST INCLUDE THE REMOVED CARRIER

The diffusion result suggests a potentially better operation: correlate the
repair noise so that the retained reference is exactly conserved. This is a
real algebraic escape, not excluded by Section 9. For a linear update with left
fixed vector w, noises eta satisfying w B eta=0 do not diffuse R=w r.
For example, a mechanically enforced zero-sum exchange can protect the sum of
CURRENT carrier coordinates. Such a constraint must be physical; commanding two
nominally opposite noisy motors does not make their realized sum exact.

Turnover has a boundary term. Let S=sum_i r_i. Replace carrier a by a newcomer
whose prepared natural length is r_new, and let surviving carriers change by
delta_j. Then, identically,

 S_after-S_before = r_new-r_a + sum_(j != a) delta_j.

Zero-sum adjustment among survivors leaves r_new-r_a untouched. Preserving the
original S requires transmitting that missing quantity, retaining it elsewhere,
or conserving a physical carrier of it while the component is removed. An exact
conservation law for internal repair increments is therefore insufficient for
full replacement. This accounting is not a general impossibility theorem: a
genuine material-transfer operation can carry information without measuring it.

There is a second boundary: preserving the CURRENT sum is not necessarily repair.
If damage already changed that sum by d, every later sum-conserving operation
retains the error d. The invariant must survive the damage process as well as
the replacement process. A perfect transfer of a worn reference can faithfully
transfer the wrong value.

A conventional positive construction is a closed extensive inventory Q, with
fresh length realization r=F(Q,theta). If Q is transferred intact between
successive containers, fresh readout error does not get written back into Q.
Then repeated independent readout error stays a readout floor. Transfer leakage
does accumulate: Q_(n+1)=Q_n+ell_n, so independent leakage variance grows as
n Var(ell), and a mean leak grows linearly. The state theta includes the new
transducer's geometry, constitutive properties and environment.

The physical audit sharpens the constitutive condition. Suppose r_i=f_i(q_i)
and arbitrary local exchanges conserve q_i+q_j. Invariance of sum_i r_i requires
 f_i'(q_i)=f_j'(q_j)
for every allowed pair and state. On the connected open domain this requires a
common affine relation f_i(q)=alpha q+beta_i. Replacement must preserve both the
relevant inventory and the sum of offsets beta_i. A conserved amount of material
alone does not generally meet that metric condition.

Hydraulics makes the distinction explicit. Idealized r=m/[rho(T,p) A] stores a
conserved MASS m. It does not conserve volume or length independently of density,
temperature, pressure, cross-section A and dead volume. Replacing the cylinder
therefore renews a dimensional calibration obligation. A discrete register N with
a reproducible pitch a gives r=N a(T,p,composition,defects); conserving or
correcting N avoids analog copying drift, but it does not correct uncertainty in
a. To first order, delta r/r=delta N/N+delta a/a. At 1 mm length and 10 nm target
tolerance, pitch stability must already be within 10 parts per million before
allocating tolerance to attachment and load compensation.

One count also cannot encode arbitrary heterogeneous geometry. A general target
requires a distributed pattern of counts, identities and topology, or a compact
generating rule plus physical access to realize it. This route converges on the
strong coded-reference competitor rather than supplying a demonstrated cost
advantage over it. It is useful because it identifies a genuine way around the
particular diffusion result, and identifies exactly which physical costs replace
the repeated observer cost. The independent conservation follow-ups preserve
stronger qualifications and conventional implementations.


14. WHAT CHANGED AND WHAT REMAINS A REAL OPENING

The new construction changes the available OPERATION in the stated model: a
member can inherit another member's natural length while their common loaded
length is unknown. The finite observer, relative calibration, work calculation
and copying-noise propagation make that operation concrete and falsifiable.
It can also preserve an intentionally prestressed reference, for which simply
projecting to a stress-free Euclidean metric would be the wrong objective.

It does not establish that a microscopic renewing material is better than a
protected specification with calibrated replacement. The independent comparator
already achieves the central sparse-drift restoration task with conventional
metrology. Repeated analog copying loses its apparent simplicity once reference
diffusion, finite readout, sensor drift, surviving load and power are included.

The surviving research question is therefore not whether springs can vote or
whether a local residual can reject unknown load. It is whether a REAL material
can preserve and renew its constitutive reference at a lower total cost than
retained coded geometry plus calibrated maintenance. A successful new mechanism
must reduce the common reference noise or the actual calibration/delivery cost,
while also renewing the elements that provide that reduction. Simply naming a
perfect persistent reference would move the burden rather than solve it.

The present work is useful capital toward that question: exact constructions,
complete model observers, strong working competitors, conditional cost boundaries
and failures. It is not represented as the requested profound physical advance
already achieved.
