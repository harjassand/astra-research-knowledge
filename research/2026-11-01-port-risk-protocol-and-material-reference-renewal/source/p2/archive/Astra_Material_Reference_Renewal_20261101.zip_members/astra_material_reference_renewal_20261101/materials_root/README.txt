MATERIAL REFERENCE RENEWAL UNDER LOAD
Astra physical-technology investigation — 1 November 2026

RESULT

The capability sought is a precision material or structure whose load-bearing
parts can turn over without the object forgetting its original stress-free
geometry. This run develops a force-based reference-copying construction, its
finite observer, relative calibration, actuator work, accumulated copying error,
and a precision-versus-exposure boundary. It also constructs and executes strong
conventional alternatives.

This is not a demonstrated new material, a historical-priority claim, or an
established civilisation-changing advance. A complete resource advantage over a
retained coded specification plus calibrated replacement was not established.
The positive capability and the failures are preserved for further invention.

READ FIRST

PROOF.txt is the complete ordinary derivation, including assumptions and scope.
SOURCES.txt records primary literature, exact public-code provenance and the
selectively used Astra sources with governing conditions.

Principal conclusions:

1. Geometry-only repair cannot determine chemically distinct but geometrically
   indistinguishable target arrangements. Fuel without physical selectivity does
   not fix this.
2. In redundant elastic paths, force differences can identify natural-length
   differences under unknown common loading. A finite balance and relative
   calibration can realize a conditional copying operation.
3. A complete finite two-polarity Gaussian observer removes unknown constant
   offset and initial-state nuisances at the calibrated meter response. Its exact
   finite covariance includes endpoint readout noise and temporal correlation.
4. Repeated independent analog copying makes the common reference diffuse even
   when disagreement contracts. In the specified three-member cyclic protocol,
   Var(R_M)=(4/3) M sigma_copy^2. Bias accumulates linearly.
5. Observing longer reduces copying noise but exposes remaining load paths.
   The observer-limited model has a finite precision/exposure feasibility window;
   actual actuator/write noise, drift and routing require additional budgets.
6. Conserved inventories and corrected integer registers can avoid this specific
   diffusion. Their dimensional realization, leakage/defect control and physical
   transfer replace the observer costs. They strengthen the conventional baseline.

REVIEW RESOLUTIONS

The initial controller treated too many observations as independent inside short
windows. That failure is retained in the audit history. The current controller
uses exact joint OU window-average/endpoint noise, 2.5 s of calibration observation
and 1.8 s of feedback observation in its stated example. It does not compress
independent samples into those intervals.

The final budget uses the full finite reciprocal GLS estimator, not a deliberately
weak averaging competitor. The finite-observer audit checked its covariance and
the all-completed-round precision certificate. Known-response cancellation does
not remove response-calibration uncertainty. The numerical budget is explicitly
observer-limited and zero-bias; it does not silently certify motor/latch error.

The conservation follow-ups were added after the diffusion finding. They are a
representation challenge, not an assumed ideal escape mechanism.
