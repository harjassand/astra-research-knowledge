## The opening: matter that preserves its manufacturing precision while its parts are replaced

I chose a capability with a substantial physical consequence: **a material or structure whose load-bearing constituents can turn over without it gradually forgetting its original stress-free geometry**. That would support machines, instruments and infrastructure whose precision survives repeated repair.

This run produced a conditional physical copying mechanism, a complete finite observation model, and quantitative limits on repeated renewal. **The full comparison has not established an advantage over a protected specification with calibrated replacement.** I am preserving the construction and its failures as research capital, rather than presenting them as a civilisation-changing technology already achieved.

## 1. The first candidate failed for a substantive reason

Before targeted literature or Astra retrieval, I developed a candidate based on **geometry-dependent protection of reversible bonds**. A correctly closed neighborhood would enter a protected conformation; strained or incomplete neighborhoods would remain exchangeable. Fuel could support replacement and resetting.

The first obstruction was informational. Two components can have identical binding geometry and different functional payloads. A mechanism that senses only geometric closure receives identical evidence from the correct and incorrect payload. Driving that mechanism harder cannot create the missing discrimination.

I made this explicit in a finite reaction model. With correct feed fraction \(x\), and open-state removal rates \(d_C,d_W\), the occupied-site accuracy is

\[
P(C\mid\text{occupied})
=
\frac{x\,d_W}{x\,d_W+(1-x)d_C}.
\]

Adding the same fueled protection cycle to both identities increases residence time while leaving this accuracy unchanged. In the executed example, protection increased residence by more than an order of magnitude while accuracy stayed at \(0.3\).

The independent constructor also brought back strong existing constructions. Combined proofreading and regrowth already have a nontrivial tile-assembly theory; DNA-origami experiments already replace damaged staples and functional labels. Their protected boundaries, retained scaffolds, interaction specificity and replacement pools matter, but these are serious constructions. They defeated the generic claim that local protection plus replacement was the opening. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

The sharper physical problem emerged from that failure:

> **How can replacement preserve the reference geometry that the material should recover after the load is removed?**

For one elastic member,

\[
F=k(\ell-r),
\]

where \(\ell\) is its current length and \(r\) its natural length. Making a replacement stress-free at the current gap sets \(r_{\rm new}=\ell\). Under load, that writes elastic deformation into the permanent reference. Reconnecting material and preserving its manufactured geometry are therefore different achievements.

## 2. The strongest conventional construction changed the decision

The constructor initially received the capability and physical constraints without my preferred mechanism. For the refined problem, it developed a conventional route using measured lengths, measured forces, constitutive calibration and geometric redundancy.

For each calibrated linear member,

\[
\widehat r_i=\ell_i^{\rm measured}-\frac{f_i^{\rm measured}}{k_i}.
\]

The controller can then decode sparse corruption of the natural lengths using Euclidean-distance constraints. This has substantial mathematical precedent: existing research treats correction of an unknown corrupted distance when the embedding dimension is known. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}

The executed comparator used a generic seven-node complete framework in three dimensions. Its decoder received connectivity, calibration and current measurements, **without receiving the original coordinates**. The intact lengths carried the reference redundantly.

## 3. The physical operation that does work: copy natural length through force

Consider three parallel elastic paths sharing the same current length \(L\). For matched linear paths,

\[
f_i=k(L-r_i),
\qquad
f_i-f_j=k(r_j-r_i).
\]

The unknown common extension cancels. Two surviving paths can therefore guide the adjustment of a replacement’s natural length without supplying the external load or the absolute current length.

This cancellation survives beyond Hooke’s law in an important restricted case. If the paths share a strictly increasing constitutive law,

\[
f_i=G(L-r_i),\qquad G'>0,
\]

then equal force means equal natural length, and force ordering identifies the direction of correction. Constitutive mismatch, hysteresis, slack and buckling require additional treatment.

### A real comparator has stiffness, thermal motion and backaction

I constructed a differential mechanical balance with coordinate \(x\):

\[
U=
\frac{k}{2}(L-r_1-x)^2+
\frac{k}{2}(L-r_2+x)^2+
\frac{\kappa}{2}x^2.
\]

Here \(\kappa\) is the balance’s restoring stiffness. In the harmonic thermal model,

\[
\langle x\rangle
=
\frac{k(r_2-r_1)}{2k+\kappa},
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(x)=\frac{k_BT}{2k+\kappa}.
\]

This is a physical interaction with a calculable signal and thermal uncertainty. It requires a frame, flexure, attachment geometry, damping and readout. Multiple balances attached simultaneously interact through their stiffnesses; treating them as independent perfect force readings would be incorrect.

The audit also rejected an attractive transducer shortcut. A parallel piezoelectric patch can sense the compatible deformation that the proposed projection removes, leaving no useful defect signal. A series force transducer genuinely couples to the elastic extension, while adding compliance. Direct piezoelectric charge also leaks, so a static-force claim needs a finite preparation/readout protocol or a DC-sensitive alternative.

### Unequal paths can be calibrated relatively

Suppose a finite sensor reports

\[
z_i=a_i(L-r_i)+b_i,
\]

with unknown gain–stiffness product \(a_i\) and offset \(b_i\). A real force-free episode supplies an offset measurement. Two common-length probe states give

\[
\Delta z_i=a_i\Delta L.
\]

Consequently,

\[
\frac{z_i-b_i}{\Delta z_i}
=
\frac{L-r_i}{\Delta L}.
\]

Comparing these normalized channels removes the unknown common length. The controller needs the probe direction, while its exact amplitude can remain unknown.

The executed controller used finite correlated observations: **2.5 seconds of calibration observation and 1.8 seconds of feedback observation**. Under its illustrative assumptions, a preselected newcomer initially \(120\) nm too long ended about \(9.8\) pm from the reference. An \(8\) nm equivalent sensor-zero bias instead produced approximately \(8\) nm of repair error.

That result demonstrates the algorithm’s conditional operation. It does not establish picometre performance of a manufactured device. The unloading, reattachment, probe, actuator and sensor-drift requirements remain physical obligations.

### The finite estimator includes the initial state and offset

I replaced a preliminary averaging treatment with the full Gaussian estimator for the specified observation experiment.

The interaction is physically reversed between two held polarities \(s=+1,-1\). Within each phase,

\[
x_j=\rho x_{j-1}+(1-\rho)s\,m+\xi_j,
\qquad
y_j=x_j+b+\epsilon_j,
\]

where \(\rho=e^{-\Delta/\tau}\), \(m\) is the wanted balance displacement and \(b\) is an unknown offset constant across the two phases.

Forming

\[
z_j=y_j-\rho y_{j-1}
\]

eliminates the unknown initial mechanical state. Its covariance includes both thermal process noise and correlated differences of noisy readouts:

\[
\Sigma=v(1-\rho^2)I+\sigma_{\rm read}^{\,2}DD^\top,
\]

where \(D\) has adjacent entries \((-\rho,1)\). This covariance is tridiagonal, so the complete generalized least-squares estimator is straightforward to execute.

Writing \(w=\Sigma^{-1}\mathbf 1\) and \(W=\mathbf 1^\top w\),

\[
\mu_s=\frac{w^\top z_s}{(1-\rho)W},
\qquad
\widehat m=\frac{\mu_+-\mu_-}{2},
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(\widehat m)
=
\frac{1}{2(1-\rho)^2W}.
\]

An 8,000-run check using **34 readings over 16 ms** gave an estimator standard deviation of \(0.355\) nm against the exact prediction \(0.360\) nm.

The independent audit confirmed the calculation. It also identified the important physical qualification: initial-state cancellation is exact at the calibrated response \(\rho\). Response error times a large transient produces bias. Thus the experiment requires a bounded operating range, response calibration, stable offset across the holds and an actual reversal of the interaction. Negating one digital record does not perform that reversal.

## 4. Repair work and observer power materially affect the proposition

The actuator cannot be supplied as an arbitrary noiseless change of natural length.

For three equal paths, with two at the desired natural length and one too long by \(d\), correction under fixed total force \(F\) has reversible work

\[
W_{\rm rev}=\frac{Fd}{3}-\frac{kd^2}{3}.
\]

The first term can dominate: repair can require work against the load even while removing internal mismatch.

I also constructed a two-pathway chemical motor model obeying local detailed balance. Its effective directional affinity is bounded by the available chemical affinity. At zero comparison signal it does not automatically hold position against load. A latch or continuing holding drive is required, with finite force capacity, escape probability and energy cost.

Replacement changes the load envelope too. Removing one of three equal paths raises each survivor’s load from \(F/3\) to \(F/2\). A temporary bypass can address that change, but becomes another component to prepare, attach, operate and maintain.

### Public research code exposed the readout tradeoff

I ran the unmodified epitaxy, elasticity and noise formulas from a pinned version of **PiezoD**, verifying the retained upstream file hashes. The associated primary research explicitly makes bandwidth and design constraints central to sensor performance. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="3"}

For a declared \(100\times10\times1\ \mu\text{m}\) silicon readout beam, coupled in the model to two \(1\ \text{N/m}\) load paths:

| Bridge bias | Power in one sensing resistor | Electronic noise referred to natural-length difference, 1–1,000 Hz | Estimated peak temperature rise |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.1 V | 1.89 µW | 2.75 nm RMS | 0.040 K |
| 1 V | 189 µW | 0.291 nm RMS | 4.03 K |
| 3 V | 1.70 mW | 0.132 nm RMS | 36.2 K |

These are model predictions. The heating estimate uses constant properties; the highest-bias case especially requires a coupled thermal treatment. Full-band RMS noise also does not establish independent sample noise, an OU spectrum or a static-offset floor.

At the middle operating point, a million simultaneously active sensing resistors alone would dissipate about **189 W**, before the rest of the bridge, amplifiers, controllers and actuators. Sharing or duty-cycling sensors changes routing, observation delay and exposure during repair. Those costs prevent an accuracy number alone from selecting the technology.

## 5. The deeper failure: accurate agreement can preserve the wrong reference

The decisive long-term result came from carrying the construction through repeated turnover.

Let each replacement copy the average natural length of the other two paths, with independent zero-mean copying error of variance \(\sigma_c^2\). Replace paths in the fixed order \(1,2,3\).

At completed-round boundaries, the retained reference coordinate is

\[
R=\frac13 r_2+\frac23 r_3,
\]

and the exact update gives

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}(R_M)
=
\operatorname{Var}(R_0)+\frac43 M\sigma_c^2.
}
\]

Disagreement contracts, while the shared reference diffuses. A common per-copy bias \(b\) produces a mean shift \(2Mb\).

With \(1\) nm copying error and \(1{,}500\) complete renewal rounds, the predicted reference standard deviation is **44.7 nm**. The 5,000-trajectory simulation agreed with the variance prediction to about \(0.8\%\).

This is an exact result for the stated overwrite protocol, not a universal theorem about renewing matter. Retained history, different schedules, correlated transfers and conserved quantities can change it.

### Longer observation creates a competing exposure cost

The finite observer reduces copying variance with observation time. The two remaining reference paths must survive that observation and replacement interval.

For \(M\) rounds, three replacements per round, independent constant survivor hazard \(\lambda\), observation time \(T_{\rm obs}\) and other per-replacement overhead \(t_{\rm other}\), the probability of **no reference fault during any exposure** is

\[
\exp[-6M\lambda(T_{\rm obs}+t_{\rm other})].
\]

I combined this with an all-completed-round geometric-error bound and the exact finite estimator—not a deliberately weak averaging competitor.

An illustrative, zero-bias, observer-limited example uses a \(10\) nm tolerance, \(1\) nm equilibrium meter noise, \(1\) ms correlation time, finite readout noise, survivor hazard \(10^{-5}\ \text{s}^{-1}\), and \(6\) ms of other overhead:

| Complete renewal rounds | Sufficient finite observation per replacement | Maximum observation allowed by the exposure budget |
|---|---:|---:|
| 100 | 34 ms | 1,669 ms |
| 500 | 160 ms | 329 ms |
| 700 | 224 ms | 233 ms |
| 1,000 | 316 ms | 162 ms |

At 1,000 rounds, even the optimistic necessary observation time from an ideally read continuous meter is about **210 ms**, already above the allowed exposure. The failure therefore goes beyond a conservative sufficient bound.

## 6. I tested a representation change rather than treating diffusion as the endpoint

The natural response was to seek **physically correlated updates that conserve the reference**.

That can work algebraically. The physical accounting becomes decisive when an old carrier is actually removed. If \(S\) is the sum of live reference coordinates,

\[
S_{\rm after}-S_{\rm before}
=
r_{\rm new}-r_{\rm old}
+\sum_{\rm survivors}\Delta r_j.
\]

Zero-sum changes among survivors do not account for the outgoing member. The missing reference must be transferred, retained elsewhere or carried by a genuine conserved quantity.

There is a second obstruction: conserving the current sum also conserves any error already written into that sum by damage. A useful invariant must survive the damage process, or another mechanism must identify and correct its change.

### Conservation is nevertheless a legitimate escape

A closed inventory can carry a value through replacement without measuring and manufacturing a noisy copy. Likewise, an integer register can be corrected and then used to realize a fresh length:

\[
r_M=N a_M+\epsilon_M.
\]

Conditional on correct transfer of \(N\), this contains initial quantization error, current unit-realization error and current setting error. It does not inherit every previous analog setting error.

The independent constructor developed this stronger conventional route. Silicon lattice spacing is already recognized as a secondary realization of length, with explicit requirements concerning material impurities, environment and the measurement apparatus. A fresh crystal cannot simply be assumed to supply an identical ruler. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="4"}

Hydraulics illustrates the same distinction:

\[
r=\frac{m}{\rho(T,p,c)A}.
\]

Conserving mass \(m\) preserves length only through the density and effective-area relation. Leakage, discarded dead volume, temperature, composition and replacement-cylinder geometry all enter. Actual piston metrology models dimensional uncertainty and pressure-induced distortion explicitly. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="5"}

The audit yielded a useful constitutive condition. If members exchange an extensive quantity \(q\), and their natural lengths are \(r_i=f_i(q_i)\), invariance of the total natural length under arbitrary local additive exchanges requires

\[
f_i'(q_i)=f_j'(q_j).
\]

On the stated connected domain, this forces a common affine relation,

\[
r_i=\alpha q_i+\beta_i,
\]

with preservation of the relevant inventory and total offset. Generic spring natural lengths do not obey such a conservation law merely because matter is conserved.

This identifies a real way around the diffusion result. It also identifies the resource that replaces repeated observation: **a protected inventory or discrete specification, together with a renewable physical conversion from that representation into geometry**.
