The consequential methodological change is broadening from its fixed quartic/penalty family to all15 coefficients of a homogeneous quartic on the three-dimensional space Sym(2). For a homogeneous quartic, symmetry of its fourth-order tensor exchanges the two test-matrix slots with the two rank-one direction slots. The rank-one condition can therefore be expressed as positivity of the3-by3 Hessian matrix evaluated at `(a^2,ab,b^2)` for every a,b. The proposed9-by9 PSD Gram uses the binary-quadratic monomials times the three matrix coordinates.

The classical [matrix Fejer–Riesz theorem, Theorem1.2](https://zalara.github.io/Papers/MatrixFejerRieszArxivOriginal.pdf) supports this degree-bounded univariate matrix-SOS route; homogenization and real Gram receiving still belong in its completed derivation. This is a credible enlargement of the constitutive search with a continuum condition, rather than another angular sample. The working code is not a new accepted separation or an exact rational cone certificate.

The integrand cone can be complete while the tested field family remains restricted.
