## Strong alternatives and remaining consequence

Full digital target evaluation and the dense adaptive aggregate both use O(M) arithmetic. If a native target-value service is already available, it performs the same conditional operation and must be compared at its actual normalization/noise/cost. A hypothetical native service is not an independently available incumbent merely because it can be written as a formula. Conversely, exporting all V to implement the aggregate defeats the claimed acquisition saving. The theorem proves a measurement/control separation; no general computational speedup or total-cost advantage has been earned.
