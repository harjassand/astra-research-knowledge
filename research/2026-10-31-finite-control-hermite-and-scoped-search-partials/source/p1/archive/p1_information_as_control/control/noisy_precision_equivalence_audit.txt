INDEPENDENT AUDIT: NOISY FINITE-CONTROL LINEAR SENSING
2026-10-30. P1 frontier. New audit; earlier reports and control notes unchanged.

VERDICT

The proposed equivalence is correct under the protocol model below. Adaptive
interleaving, readout-dependent directions, randomized encoders, and readout-
dependent stopping do not improve the Bayes risk beyond an optimal static
finite codebook of feasible precision matrices. This is an information result;
the codebook search and coefficient acquisition are not supplied for free by it.

1. MODEL AND PRECISE STATEMENT

Let C be a measurable R^m-valued random vector with E||C||^2 < infinity. Let
V ~ N(0,I_m) be independent of C. The target is F=C^T V. Any normalization of
the target may be included in C. The controller knows C exactly at the outset.
The final estimator has C and the complete legal measurement transcript.

There are at most q measurements, where q is a hard nonnegative integer bound:

    Y_j = a_j^T V + xi_j,     ||a_j||=1,
    xi_1,...,xi_q independent N(0,s),    s>0.

All noise variables, public random seeds, and private random seeds are
independent of (C,V). The actuator chooses a_j using only prior measurement
readouts, received controller bits, and its independent random seed. The
controller can use C, all permitted preceding readouts, and its seed to choose
the next control bits. Feedback may be free, but it contains only functions of
these legal past readouts and independent randomness. The actuator has no
additional access to C or V.

The controller-to-actuator protocol is a binary decision tree with at most B
controller bits on each path. Its timing, termination, measurement requests,
and any other C-dependent actions are charged as control whenever they can
distinguish actuator behavior. Equivalently, after fixing readout randomness
and actuator randomness there are at most N=2^B possible controller branches.
A length cap alone without counting informative timing or unframed stopping
does not establish this property. All policies are measurable and are
totalized on counterfactual histories; every replay has at most q reads.

Define the feasible matrix family

    H_q = { sum_{j=1}^q a_j a_j^T : a_j in R^m, ||a_j||=1 }.

For q=0 this means {0}. One may instead allow up to q rows; the optimal risk is
unchanged since unused reads can be appended and ignored or optimally used.
Every matrix in H_q comes with a fixed implementable unit-row decomposition.
In particular, repeat directions are permitted and add precision.

Then the infimum of mean squared error over all the legal protocols is

    D(B,q,s;C)
      = min_{H_1,...,H_N in H_q}
          E_C [ min_{1<=w<=N} C^T (I_m + H_w/s)^(-1) C ].             (1)

Repeated codewords are permitted, so using fewer than N is included. For finite
m,q,N a minimizing deterministic codebook exists: the feasible family is a
continuous image of a compact product of unit spheres, and the expectation is
continuous by domination with ||C||^2.

2. CONDITIONAL GAUSSIAN POSTERIOR: WHY PATH DEPENDENCE IS HARMLESS

For a lower bound, reveal all independent seeds to the estimator. This can
only decrease its minimum risk. Fix C=c and the seeds. At each read, a_j is a
measurable function of past readouts and control messages, and those messages
are now functions of c and those same past readouts. Thus a_j is predictable.

For any realized terminal history with k<=q reads, write its realized
directions a_1,...,a_k and values y_1,...,y_k. Their conditional likelihood as
a function of v is, up to factors independent of v,

    exp( -||v||^2/2 )
      product_{j=1}^k exp( -(y_j-a_j^T v)^2/(2s) ).                 (2)

The observed messages, direction choices, and stopping decisions add only
consistency indicators. Conditional on c and the full observed numerical
history these indicators do not depend on the residual v. The same statement
follows by sequential Bayesian conditioning and avoids treating a zero-
probability exact continuous transcript as a positive-probability event.

Consequently, almost surely under regular conditional distributions,

    V | c, transcript, seeds ~ N(mu_H, Sigma_H),
    H       = sum_{j=1}^k a_j a_j^T,
    Sigma_H = (I_m+H/s)^(-1),
    mu_H    = Sigma_H sum_{j=1}^k a_j y_j/s.                        (3)

The matrix H, and therefore Sigma_H, generally depends on the observed path.
It is incorrect to replace it by an unconditional deterministic matrix at
this step. What is exact is the conditional Bayes residual variance

    Var(C^T V | C, transcript, seeds) = C^T Sigma_H C.              (4)

Conditioning on C is essential. Without it, controller messages can carry
information about C; the target posterior would not generally have this form.

3. REPLAY LOWER BOUND

Pre-sample the ordered noise sequence xi_1,...,xi_q, whether or not all reads
will actually be made. Fix a full value v of V, this noise sequence, and all
seeds. Replay the actuator for every possible branch of the binary controller
protocol. On a branch, whenever the actuator asks its j-th measurement in
direction a, return a^T v+xi_j. Follow the specified actuator operations until
the branch terminates. Fill unreachable or syntactically invalid histories
with a fixed legal action sequence if needed.

For these fixed values there are at most N=2^B terminal branches. Each gives
a matrix H_w with at most q unit outer products. Crucially, this construction
uses no C: whenever a controller bit is needed, branch on its possible values
instead of running the actual encoder. The actual protocol trajectory for
any C is one of these replayed branches. Therefore, pointwise,

    C^T Sigma_{H_actual} C >= min_w C^T Sigma_{H_w(v,xi,seeds)} C.   (5)

The replayed matrices can depend on v, noises, and seeds. This does not weaken
the argument. C is independent of all those quantities, so for each fixed
replay collection its conditional expected right-hand side is at least the
global optimum over static feasible codebooks in (1). Average over v, noises,
and seeds and use (4). Every protocol's MSE is at least the right-hand side
of (1). If seeds were initially hidden from the estimator, revealing them only
strengthened the estimator, so the lower bound still applies to the original.

This is not the invalid claim that conditioning on full V leaves a Gaussian
residual. Gaussian conditioning is done first, on the legal transcript, to
derive (4). Full V is fixed only afterward to compare the resulting numerical
risk functional with replayed codebooks, as in (5).

4. STATIC ACHIEVABILITY

Choose a minimizing finite codebook with fixed unit-row decompositions. The
controller calculates

    w(C) in argmin_w C^T Sigma_{H_w} C

with a deterministic tie rule, sends its index in B bits, and the actuator
makes all q predetermined reads for that codeword. The estimator computes
C^T mu_{H_w}. Since C is available at the estimator and V is independent of C,
the index-selection event introduces no additional V information. Its risk is
exactly the objective in (1). This proves equality, not only a lower bound.

5. ENDPOINTS AND INTERNAL CONSISTENCY CHECKS

(a) No control, B=0. The optimum is an ordinary deterministic Gaussian
experimental design minimizing E[C^T Sigma_H C]. Readout-dependent choices
without any C information cannot improve that Bayes objective.

(b) Full control. Since H <= q I_m, every legal protocol satisfies

    E(C^T Sigma_H C) >= s/(s+q) E||C||^2.

Knowing C at the actuator attains this by making q repeated reads in direction
C/||C|| (any direction on C=0). Thus the unlimited-control benchmark is exact.

(c) Vanishing noise. For a fixed codeword H,

    (I+H/s)^(-1) -> I-P_range(H) as s -> 0.

For 0<=q<=m the limiting optimal risk is the previously established optimal
q-subspace codebook distortion. A safe proof of the infimum limit is: every
noisy matrix risk is at least the corresponding projection residual, giving
the subspace optimum as a lower bound; use any optimal q-plane codebook with
orthonormal rows for the upper bound and let s decrease to zero. No unjustified
interchange of limit and minimization is needed. For q>=m the noiseless limit
is zero.

(d) Isotropic C. For C~N(0,I_m/m), B=0, and q<=m, orthonormal rows give

    D(0,q,s;C) = 1 - q/[m(1+s)].

Indeed, rank(H)<=q and trace(H)=q; concavity of lambda/(s+lambda) shows that
q eigenvalues equal to one maximize explained variance. This agrees with the
standard fixed noisy-measurement formula and accounts for repeat directions.

6. PRICED SCOPE AND NONCLAIMS

* Acquisition of the initially known C is an external cost. Formula (1) does
  not infer C, learn its representation, or construct a physical target read.
* B counts controller-to-actuator distinctions; q counts real noisy readings.
  Readout precision, return-channel bandwidth, local arithmetic, codebook
  storage, and matrix/direction programming need their own resource charges.
* A codeword H still requires implementation of its stored directions. The
  theorem does not make a dense read a constant-time physical operation.
* Brute-force nearest-metric encoding can cost exponentially in B. Neither the
  minimization over all codebooks nor its online encoding is claimed efficient.
* If the actuator already holds C, e.g. it retained the first measurement
  record locally, there is no externally communicated B-bit bottleneck of this
  form. One must instead charge local representation and actuator operations.
* If a public actuator context Z is correlated with C, codebooks must be
  conditioned on Z. When V remains independent of (C,Z), the corresponding
  value is E_Z D(B,q,s;law(C|Z)), subject to measurable implementation of the
  conditional designs. An unconditional C codebook lower bound can be false.
  If the context also changes the conditional law of V, that law needs a new
  audit. An actuator-only output which lacks C is a different estimation task.
* Hard q and hard B are substantive. Expected measurement or prefix-length
  budgets require separately proved statements; (1) is not such a statement.
* No generalization is asserted to non-Gaussian V, dependent noise, variable
  noise levels chosen without charge, nonlinear V observations, extra private
  access to V, or a target coefficient initially uncertain to the decoder.
* This proof is a finite-control extension of ordinary Gaussian posterior
  experimental design plus a replay argument. Its validity does not establish
  historical novelty of the equivalence; primary-source priority is separate.

AUDIT STATUS

Accepted with the model above. In particular, the dependence of posterior
covariance on observed history is real and explicitly retained; it is handled
by the replay lower bound, not discarded by an unjustified independence claim.
