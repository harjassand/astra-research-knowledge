INDEPENDENT AUDIT: EXPECTED PREFIX-LENGTH LOWER BOUND
2026-10-30. Exact-read Gaussian inner-product model only.

VERDICT

The proposed relative-entropy lower bound is valid with the conditioning and
low-risk qualification below. It complements the sharp hard-bit subspace
quantization theorem. It is not an exact expected-length characterization and
does not automatically extend the hard-q statement to expected measurements.

MODEL

U,V independent N(0,I_m), target F=U^T V/sqrt(m). The controller knows U and
the decoder retains U plus the legal readouts. The actuator sees U only through
counted controller bits, plus an independent random seed. It may make at most
q exact unit linear V readings, adaptively, with q<m a hard integer bound.
Readout feedback to the controller is allowed. Controller communication uses
a (possibly infinite) binary protocol tree whose terminal controller words
are prefix-free conditional on the actuator's simulated readouts and seeds.
All informative timing and stopping are included. Let W be the terminal word,
ell(W) its length, and E ell(W) <= B. All random seeds Z are independent of
(U,V). Policies are measurable and replay is defined on all finite branches.

Write d=m-q>0 and let R be the mean squared error of the optimal legal decoder
for the protocol. Giving all seeds Z to the decoder can only improve risk.
When proving a lower bound for an arbitrary original decoder, one may use
its larger actual risk in the final monotone lower-bound formula below.

CLAIM

For 0<R<d/m,

    B ln 2 >= (d/2) [ ln(d/(mR)) - 1 + mR/d ].                    (1)

For R>=d/m the universally valid version of this argument is the trivial
lower bound 0. For R=0 the right-hand side diverges, so no finite expected
prefix length can attain zero risk when q<m. The d=0 case is separate and
has no such control lower bound. If R denotes the globally optimal risk for
the given resources, R<=d/m since fixed orthogonal q readings are available.

PROOF

1. For the exact Gaussian experiment, conditional on U and the legal terminal
transcript, the residual target variance is

    ||Q_actual U||^2/m,

where Q_actual is the orthogonal projection onto the complement of the span
of the measured directions. Its rank is at least d. This is the previously
audited adaptive Gaussian-conditioning identity; it is an almost-sure
conditional statement.

2. Fix full V=v and all seeds Z=z. Replay the actuator for each possible
terminal word w. This defines a residual projection Q_w(v,z), independent of
U, of rank at least d. The actual transcript word selects the actual replayed
projection. The countably many words form a prefix code for these fixed
(v,z). Therefore Shannon entropy in natural units obeys

    H(W | V,Z) <= E ell(W) ln 2 <= B ln 2,

and consequently I(U;W | V,Z) <= B ln 2. The prefix property, not an arbitrary
expected transcript length without a framing rule, is what justifies this.

3. Let P be the actual joint law of (U,W,V,Z). Let P0 be the comparison law
which keeps (V,Z) and W|(V,Z) unchanged but draws U independently from its
N(0,I_m) prior. Since U is independent of (V,Z),

    D_KL(P || P0) = I(U;W | V,Z).

For t>=0 set f(U,W,V,Z)=-t||Q_W(V,Z)U||^2. Under P0, conditional on w,v,z,
the squared norm in f has a chi-squared distribution with rank(Q_w) degrees
of freedom. Hence

    E_P0 exp(f) <= (1+2t)^(-d/2).

The Gibbs variational inequality D_KL(P||P0)>=E_P f-ln E_P0 exp(f) gives

    I(U;W|V,Z) >= -tmR + (d/2)ln(1+2t).                           (2)

If the risk R supplied here is an actual non-Bayes risk, it is at least the
average residual variance, so (2) remains a valid weaker inequality with R.

4. For 0<R<d/m the maximizing t>=0 is

    t_* = [d/(mR)-1]/2.

Substitution gives (1). For R>=d/m the constrained optimum is t=0, giving 0.
For R=0 let t tend to infinity in (2).

WHY THE QUALIFICATION MATTERS

The expression ln(1/x)-1+x is positive also for x>1, but for x=mR/d>1 its
stationary point corresponds to t<0. Inserting it into (2), which was proved
for t>=0 using rank(Q_w)>=d, is invalid. State the low-risk regime or use the
piecewise lower bound. For optimal sensing risk the regime is automatic, but
it is not automatic for an arbitrary deliberately poor estimator or protocol.

SCOPE

This audit assumes U isotropic Gaussian; arbitrary coefficient laws do not
have the universal chi-squared transform used in step 3. Public context
correlated with U changes its conditional law and must be conditioned on.
The result charges only controller prefix bits and counts exact V readings;
it does not price real readout precision, acquisition of U, local coefficient
generation, physical read operations, or dense direction programming. No
noisy expected-prefix theorem or expected-query theorem is claimed here.
