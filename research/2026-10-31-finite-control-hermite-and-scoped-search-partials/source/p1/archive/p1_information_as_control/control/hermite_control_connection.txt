INDEPENDENT CHECK: HERMITES AS A STRUCTURED CONTROL ESCAPE
adaptive_structure, 2026-10-30 UTC.
This note records a quick mathematical audit requested by sequential_learning
and a connection to the control theorem. It is not a new model acquisition
or finite-bit implementation claim.

Let U~N(0,I_r), V~N(0,I_M) be independent, with
M=binomial(r+p-1,p). Let phi_a, |a|=p, be the orthonormal homogeneous Hermite
basis, and define

  G(U)=(phi_a(U)/sqrt(M))_a,
  F=G(U)^T V.

Then E||G(U)||^2=1, so Var F=1. Observing all r coordinates of U and one
unit projection of V along G(U) evaluates F exactly in r+1 reads.

1. Fixed mixed-direction lower bound: audited valid

Write d=p+1. Let A_a be order-p symmetric Wiener-chaos kernels with
<A_a,A_b>=delta_ab/p!, and let e_a denote the orthogonal V-coordinate
directions. With S=(1/sqrt(M)) sum_a A_a tensor e_a and T=Sym S, the product
formula on the orthogonal U/V coordinate spaces gives F=I_d(T).

For any fixed rank-q orthogonal projection P on the joint U,V space, the
explained variance is d!||P^(tensor d)T||^2. Symmetrization commutes with
P^(tensor d) and is an orthogonal projection, hence a contraction. Therefore

  EV <= d!||P^(tensor d)S||^2
     <= d!||(I^(tensor p) tensor P)S||^2
      = (d/M) sum_a ||P e_a||^2 <= d q/M.

Mixing the U and V coordinates does not invalidate either contraction or
the orthogonality of the A_a in the final norm. For q unit noisy reads with
common independent Gaussian variance s, set alpha=1/(1+s) and let C denote
the covariance of the posterior mean. Replace each P by C^(1/2). Since
0<=C<=I, the same argument gives EV<=d tr(C_V)/M<=d alpha q/M. The last
inequality follows by concavity of t/(t+s), trace H=q and rank H<=q for
H=M_read^T M_read. No assumption of orthogonal sensing directions is needed.

The adaptive noisy gain is alpha^d: posterior degree-p Hermite coefficients
after noisy U reads are alpha^(p/2) times orthonormal Hermites of the
standardized readouts, and the final V read captures the additional alpha
factor. These statements were independently reconstructed, not only checked
against the requesting agent's formulas.

2. Why ambient Gaussian control bounds do not apply

The vector G(U) is M-dimensional but lies in a known r-parameter nonlinear
feature image. It is not an isotropic Gaussian vector in R^M. The general
interactive replay theorem still applies, with source G(U), but its optimal
subspace quantization distortion is determined by that source law. Substituting
M into the isotropic Gaussian beta-cap lower bound would be invalid.

If the actuator knows the feature representation, sending an approximation
to r latent coordinates may program the M weights. The local evaluation and
hardware programming of those M weights remain costs. For fixed p, M is
polynomial in r; if p grows, those costs and the representation size can grow
very quickly. The spectral/chaos argument alone supplies no free physical
measurement primitive or unknown feature representation.

3. Exact sensitivity identities useful for a finite-control construction

The Hermite derivative identity d_i phi_a=sqrt(a_i) phi_(a-e_i) gives

  E||J_G(U)||_F^2=p.

If U'=rho U+sqrt(1-rho^2)W with independent standard Gaussian W, orthogonality
and the Ornstein--Uhlenbeck eigenvalue identity give

  E||G(U)-G(U')||^2=2(1-rho^p).

For the Gaussian rate-distortion test channel U=U_hat+Z, with independent
U_hat~N(0,(1-D)I_r), Z~N(0,D I_r), the conditional feature predictor is

  E[G(U)|U_hat]=(1-D)^(p/2) G(U_hat/sqrt(1-D)),

and its exact mean squared error is

  1-(1-D)^p <= pD.

The test-channel information is I(U;U_hat)=(r/2) log(1/D) nats. A line
through the reconstructed feature vector loses no more energy than this
vector reconstruction: dist^2(G(U),span(G_hat))<=||G(U)-G_hat||^2.
Consequently this suggests control proportional to r log(p/epsilon), rather
than M log(1/epsilon), at fixed degree and target risk epsilon.

IMPORTANT LIMIT: A continuous Gaussian test channel is not by itself a
one-instance B-bit encoder. A finite codebook, product quantizer with explicit
tail control, asymptotic block code, or paid shared-randomness channel
simulation must implement it. The average-Jacobian identity likewise is not
a global deterministic Lipschitz bound for the unbounded polynomial feature
map. These identities are exact resources for a construction, not a completed
finite-control theorem. Root owns the constructive continuation.
