P1 FRONTIER: CORRECTIONS, USEFUL FAILURES, AND CLAIM BOUNDARIES

1. A short control message is not automatically enough for the actuator to
   predict the target. The main theorem requires a final decoder retaining C
   or the full first-stage record H. The actuator may only implement patterns.
   Both independent noisy audits give simple counterexamples if this is lost.

2. A payload-length limit with free timing or termination is not a B-bit
   information limit. A fixed/prefix control tree with at most 2^B branches is
   required; every C-dependent request, address and timing distinction counts.

3. Adaptive Gaussian posterior covariance is conditioned on the legal record
   and algorithmic seeds, not the physical noise. Replay freezes that noise
   only AFTER the posterior risk expression is obtained. Revealing it to the
   predictor would make the physical measurement noiseless.

4. Public context correlated with the coefficient law requires conditional
   codebooks. Even public randomization of an earlier experiment can become
   correlated with its posterior coefficient. An unconditional law is then
   the wrong lower-bound problem.

5. Hard read/control budgets, expected read counts and expected prefix lengths
   are distinct. The expected-prefix entropy companion has a valid low-risk
   qualification R<(m-q)/m; above that regime it gives zero. It is not an
   expected-budget version of every exact noisy value identity.

6. Classical Grassmann rate-distortion bounds already price an unstructured
   Gaussian direction. The geometric bounds, high-rate exponents, and efficient
   structured quantizers are not new discoveries. Random-codebook existence
   does not supply a cheap codebook optimizer, storage scheme or encoder.

7. Equal covariance can preserve all fixed V-experiment risk while failing to
   preserve controlled value. This does not license the fixed-covariance
   equality for arbitrary mixed observations of both the coefficient source
   and V. That stronger Hermite comparator is proved separately by the tensor
   contraction argument.

8. The Hermite fixed-design bound is necessary for EVERY fixed experiment,
   including mixed directions and arbitrary decoders. Its constant is not
   asserted sharp. The 2697-read lower bound at MSE .03 is not a sufficient
   construction or an exact optimum. The 8384-coordinate full-input design is
   a separate sufficient benchmark.

9. The r=128 balanced theorem example and r=64 acquired experiment have different
   coefficients, variances and constants. Their numbers are not interchanged.
   Empirical Monte Carlo means and analytic expectations/bounds remain distinct.
   The later finite-pattern run's lower measured mean is not a quantization gain.

10. For a unit pattern the final noisy read has constant conditional variance,
    allowing the exact Gaussian-bin ADC formula. After finite rational pattern
    contraction, its variance depends on context. Only the uniform ADC/Minkowski
    bound is reused. Actual patterns have norm<=1, not necessarily norm 1.

11. The remote descriptor is 11r bits, but local dense pattern generation and
    application remain O(M). The explicit local pattern uses 20M bits. This is
    an encoding upper bound, not a universal necessary actuator-state lower
    bound. Exact rational power caps were checked; no physical actuator was
    built or hardware calibration inferred from a software simulation.

12. Coefficient acquisition assumes a declared feature layout and an explicit
    absolute calibration-error contract. One noisy deployment read per
    coefficient does not provide 10^-7 calibration precision. Generic unknown
    nonlinear representation discovery is not implemented or supplied free.

13. The cubic NP-hardness reduction concerns global affine-direction
    recognition with a specified Gaussian curvature gap. It is not a hardness
    theorem for all adaptive policies, all approximate representations, or an
    optimal observation count. Broad tensor-hardness context is not its proof.

14. A native target/value or quadratic-energy oracle is the ordinary final
    operation in a richer interface. Full-input dense evaluation uses O(M)
    arithmetic too. The linear-read separation is not a faster polynomial
    evaluation algorithm or a full-cost operational advantage.

15. Astra's configured-shunt identity requires actual new configurable native
    couplings. The source declares no demonstrated apparatus, and its bounded
    SPD law is not the Gaussian Hermite law. This route was examined and not
    credited as an acquired sensing capability.

16. Posterior convexification has an acquired certificate in its promised
    zero-hidden-bias class, but ordinary spectral design ties it. The elementary
    normalization budget is approximately 3.68e28 scalar values even on the 2D
    toy, was not executed, and does not beat established log-concave methods.
    Computing a conditional normalizer does not acquire the prior normalizer.

17. L2 model perturbation bounds are robust but do not certify that perturbation
    size. The holdout route needs fresh full-input target evaluations and a
    justified residual moment ratio. Rare errors defeat unqualified finite
    validation. Passing an audit does not prove exact class membership.

18. No inspected primary source resolved exact overlap with Jackowski's 1991
    multilinear average-case results. Blockwise, cross-block adaptive, and
    mixed linear access models must be distinguished. Unavailable source bodies
    and bot challenge pages are not read evidence. Historical novelty remains
    unresolved despite internally valid ordinary proofs.

Earlier campaigns and their corrections remain unchanged. This record adds
the current dispositions; it does not overwrite or silently broaden them.
