Independent audit of root finite_precision.py: exact final-ADC risk
30 October 2026. Existing files were read and left unchanged; no broad rerun.

The exact risk identity and its implementation are correct.

Define a(G)=c*phi(G)/sqrt(M), b(G)=c_hat*phi(QG)/sqrt(M), and
w=b/||b||, with an arbitrary unit fallback when b=0. Let alpha=1/(1+s).
The noisy-context Hermite identity gives E[phi(U)|G]=alpha phi(G).
Conditionally on G, the final noisy read is Z=w^T V+eta and
E[V|G,Z]=alpha w Z. Thus

    E[F|G,Z] = alpha^2 (a(G)^T w) Z.

The deployed finite-ADC output is

    F_hat = alpha^2 ||b(G)|| Q_z(Z).

Z has law N(0,1+s) independent of G and of the prepared model. Therefore,
writing cross=E[a^T b] and second=E||b||^2,

    E[F F_hat] = alpha^4 cross E[Z Q_z(Z)],
    E[F_hat^2] = alpha^4 second E[Q_z(Z)^2].

Since F has mean zero, its exact squared error is

    R_ADC = Var(F) - 2 alpha^4 cross E[Z Q_z(Z)]
                     + alpha^4 second E[Q_z(Z)^2].

The zero-amplitude case contributes zero to both terms and creates no issue.
Setting Q_z(Z)=Z yields E[Z^2]=1/alpha and reduces the formula exactly to
Var(F)+alpha^3(second-2cross), the earlier unquantized-output result.

Implementation inspection:

* coefficient_moments uses the correct diagonal Hermite cross moment
  (E[G^2 Q(G)^2]-E[Q(G)^2])/2 and diagonal second moment
  (E[Q(G)^4]-2E[Q(G)^2]+1)/2. Off-diagonal moments are the corresponding
  products (E[GQ(G)])^2 and (E[Q(G)^2])^2.
* adc_moments integrates each positive nearest-rounding bin, doubles by
  symmetry, ignores the zero-output bin correctly, and extends the final
  saturation bin to infinity. Its cross and second moments therefore match
  the actual clipped, nearest-grid int16 implementation.
* The decoder's direction and amplitude both depend only on decoded packed
  QG and the acquired coefficients. No exact context value enters prediction.
* The final read is genuinely serialized to two bytes per scalar and decoded
  before use. Float64 actuator arithmetic remains the explicitly stated model.
