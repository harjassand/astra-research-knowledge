# Unconditional reduction; conditional computational consequence
2026-10-29. Root synthesis of separately received physics/counting arguments. Read the actual receipts for their version scope. This synthesis introduces no claim of historical novelty.

## What is proved without importing a new SAT algorithm
For fixed local dimension2, localityk, incidenceDelta and local normJ, an explicitly supplied n-qubit local Hamiltonian can be reduced to TWO sparse3-CNF exact counts that determine an epsilon-accurate free-energy DENSITY estimate at fixed beta,epsilon. The number of Boolean variables is O(n), with constants depending on those physical/accuracy parameters. The CNF construction uses polynomial preprocessing bit work, and every integer has polynomial bit length. The two exact counts themselves are oracle tasks, not claimed polynomial-time computations. General bounded-degree graphs, noncommuting terms and complex signs are allowed.

More quantitatively, ground-energy-density accuracy epsilon uses
 N=O(n epsilon^(-3/2) log(2/epsilon))
Boolean variables and O(N) clauses, with constants only in fixed k,Delta,J. The original Hamiltonian's bit length is charged separately. This is an unconditional quantitative Turing reduction to exact classical counting. It does not say exact counting is easy.

The new ingredients in the reduction to check are the volume-uniform logarithmic product-formula estimate and the nearby-Hamiltonian interpretation of rational local-gate rounding. The signed-count compiler uses established exact Boolean circuit/counting mechanisms with every auxiliary uniquely constrained. No sampling sign cancellation, hidden matrix oracle or success-probability promise is used.

## What requires the released external theorem
Substitute the October25 OpenAI sparse#3SAT running time into the unconditional reduction. Then fixed-accuracy thermodynamics and ground-energy density have deterministic poly(input length)2^{o(n)} time and polynomial space. The explicit epsilon_n about(log n)^(-1/4) schedule still has time

These time consequences are conditional on the imported counting theorem at its actual scope. We acquired its original source and license but did not independently audit or execute its full asymptotic solver.

## Stronger output already constructed, with a different access meaning
The rational positive product defines one explicit low-energy mixed state sigma. Its energy guarantee follows unconditionally from the relative-generator bound. Given the counting subroutine, product-Pauli outcome probabilities can be computed exactly, and one can sample one selected measurement per distinct qubit in expected subexponential time. This supplies a classical operational description of a low-energy state; it does not physically prepare one.

At fixed accuracy, a constant-depth NONUNITARY positive-product description is not a shallow unitary preparation. Postselection cost has not been removed. Nothing here contradicts no-low-energy-trivial-state results, establishes small global trace error to the true Gibbs state, gives a specified ground state, or handles inverse-polynomial TOTAL-energy accuracy in subexponential time.

## Why this is a different research opening
The earlier recovery programme was limited by unacquired optimal quantum programs and expensive trusted global controls. This construction instead starts from the ordinary explicit local-Hamiltonian input of computational many-body physics and attacks a general noncommuting thermodynamic task, with no state-preparation assumption. It changes an asymptotic computational boundary if the counting premise holds. Its practical usefulness and historical novelty remain unestablished, and a worst-case asymptotic speedup alone is not claimed to meet the programme's civilisation-level objective.

The next worthwhile work should pursue a consequential access/output or genuinely better complete algorithm, rather than merely optimize another logarithmic exponent or append another Hamiltonian subclass.
