# Complete-port guard with fresh program calls during decoding

2026-10-29. Root additive scope extension, independent review pending. The earlier received transfer prepared its quantum program before role choice; this note does not silently rewrite that access contract.

A fixed candidate may make additional fresh-source queries WHILE the padded payload is being processed—for example, controlled sample simulation in a spectral filter or streaming decoder. Treat the entire finite adaptive algorithm, all such source calls, records, timeouts and total fallbacks as ONE logical input-output CPTP port L tensor M->L tensor M'. The algorithm must be identical for TEST and SERVICE, apart from the private logical input, endpoint Pauli conjugation, and terminal test readout. No direct current role/test label/pad-key side channel is supplied through query selection, timing or routing. The current test label is withheld until logical output is fixed.

For an arbitrary causal source with retained memory, a known finite controller interacting with that source still defines a CPTP map for the complete port. Thus the state2-design failure identity and full-record retired-Pauli expansion apply to this entire map, not individually to its internal calls. The previous hazard/aggregate-memory-effect proof can then be reused per COMPLETE trial. Input-dependent internal measurement records are retained inside M' as before; they are not treated as an independently learned classical model.

There must be a public bound Q_post on post-role source calls and a total known logical output on every branch. An internal failure after payload loading cannot be omitted from the test or called a harmless retry. It may return a declared logical fallback and flag; any ensuing guard abort is a memory/history event under the complete Pauli refinement. The same inner-failure behavior must be exercised on tests. Pre-role preparation has its separate cap Q_pre. A guard cap L therefore pays at most L(Q_pre+1+Q_post) source uses, plus any separately declared calibration. All known gates, idle interactions, buffers and simulation durations also remain paid.

This extends only MODEL-FREE ACCEPTED soundness of the finite guard. It does not make fresh program copies IID under a memoryful source, restore the Hamiltonian simulation/learning completeness proofs under drift, or establish availability. Those still require their own fixed-source/conditional-product contracts. In particular, a streaming architecture needs continued source access after target arrival; a prebank-only decoder cannot claim the same low live-memory bound by ignoring its stored samples.
