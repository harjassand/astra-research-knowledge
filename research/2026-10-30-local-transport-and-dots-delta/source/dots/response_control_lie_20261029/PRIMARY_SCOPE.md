# Primary controllability comparison actually checked
2026-10-29. No historical novelty claim.

D'Alessandro, Albertini and Romano, arXiv:1210.5449v2, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1210.5449, was read at the introduction, Theorem 2, and §4 Eqs. (45)–(47). It studies fully controllable accessors and indirect control, explicitly distinguishing accessor dimension at least three from dimension two. Its §4 uses commuting accessor directions with a noncentral anticommutator to obtain additional carrier commutator directions. Our two-accessor-qubit Pauli calculation is a transparent specialization of this established mechanism, with an explicit represented-algebra central-block/multiplicity statement tailored to the response-control application. Their rank-condition discussion also supplies the standard Lie-group controllability context. We do not identify this work as a new generic control theorem.

D'Alessandro and Romano, arXiv:1203.0887v1, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1203.0887, introduction and abstract read, separately studies two interacting qubits and the difference between indirect and full joint controllability. The ten-dimensional obstruction in our test is consistent with that established distinction; it is not a new classification claim.

These sources do not by citation establish the proposed source-sample collision implementation, unknown-matrix finite codebook computation, objective-preserving recovery reduction, memory resources, or measured practical advantage. Those require their own proofs and interfaces.
