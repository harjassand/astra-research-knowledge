## Exact statement

Let g be the REAL Lie algebra of anti-Hermitian matrices generated by iH⊗I and iH⊗Da, for every traceless Hermitian H on a FOUR-dimensional accessor. A finite real basis of these H suffices. Then exactly

Factors are ordered accessor/irreducible-carrier/multiplicity, up to a fixed mathematical reordering. There is no independent control on equivalent multiplicity copies, and no extra central u(1) phase per block. Both distinctions are essential.

## 1. Local spreading lemma
If iT⊗B belongs to g for one nonzero traceless Hermitian T, then iH⊗B belongs to g for every traceless Hermitian H. Indeed g is invariant under conjugation by U⊗I for U∈SU(4), because its local su(4) generators belong to g. The real span of the conjugation orbit of iT is a nonzero ideal of su(4), hence all of su(4) by simplicity. Exponentiating ad on a finite-dimensional invariant linear subspace is legitimate; this argument does not presuppose any carrier controllability.

Define V={B=B† : iH⊗B∈g for every traceless Hermitian H}. It is a real vector space containing I and all Da.

## 2. Both noncommutative products are available
Write X,Y,Z for conventional Hermitian Pauli matrices. All tensor products within H,K below refer to the TWO accessor qubits, not to the carrier.

For A,B∈V choose H=X⊗I2, K=Y⊗I2, and T=Z⊗I2. Since HK=iT and KH=−iT,
 [iH⊗A,iK⊗B] = −iT⊗(AB+BA).
The spreading lemma implies {A,B}∈V (the overall minus sign is an allowed real scalar).

Next choose H=Z⊗I2, K=I2⊗Z, T=Z⊗Z. H,K commute, HK=KH=T, and T is nonzero traceless. Then
 [iH⊗A,iK⊗B] = −T⊗[A,B] = iT⊗(i[A,B]).
Thus i[A,B]∈V as well. This is the direction that cannot be inferred from ordinary irreducibility or from Jordan closure alone. With a two-dimensional accessor, commuting traceless Hermitians are proportional, so their product is scalar; the same proof fails and the statement need not hold.

## 3. Associative algebra, including central projections
V+iV is a complex unital *-closed algebra: for Hermitian A,B∈V,

It contains all Da, hence contains A. Conversely every Lie word in the generators lies in M4(C)⊗A. Taking a nonzero accessor coefficient shows V⊆Herm(A). Therefore V+iV=A and V=Herm(A). In particular EVERY represented central projection Πj of A is available as a carrier coefficient. This gives H⊗Πj and H⊗Aj localized to each simple summand, rather than merely correlated rotations across different blocks. No numerical spectral gap or explicit matrix description of Πj has been assumed in this algebraic existence assertion.
