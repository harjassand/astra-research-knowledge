# Where the approximate-axis route stands
2026-10-29. Working continuation, not received. This is a preserved incomplete mechanism, not another accepted decoder theorem.

## Positive record weights avoid selected-branch normalization
For the ideal finite coarse-record instrument, each single-stage Kraus operator is a spectral function of its Hermitian B. Consequently its nonselective channel is unital as well as trace preserving. Sequential composition remains unital. Restricted to the response support P (dimension d), let M_r be the complete branch Kraus operators and H_r=M_r M_r*. Then sum_r H_r=P and sum_r Tr H_r=d.

Suppose the complete Stinespring intertwining bound for one Hermitian response Delta is a:
 E_r=M_r Delta-Delta M_r, sum_r E_r*E_r<=a^2 P.
Hilbert-Schmidt Cauchy-Schwarz gives
 sum_r ||M_r Delta M_r*-Delta H_r||_1 <= d a,
 sum_r ||[H_r,Delta]||_1 <=2d a.
Similarly, an output-side localization bound sum_r M_r*(B-g_r)^2 M_r<=zeta^2P implies
 sum_r ||(B-g_r)H_r||_1 <=d zeta.
There is no factor equal to the number of records and no division by a small branch probability. The exact unital statement applies to the ideal controlled-Hamiltonian instrument, not arbitrary finite fresh-program collision noise; the latter is charged separately in complete-instrument distance.

## Proposed, unclosed repair
1. Use the received coarse records and pending intertwining bounds to obtain two near-involutions on each weighted branch.
2. Replace them by exact reflections R,S using scalar functional calculus, with the propagated-support errors explicitly retained.
3. Analytically set S_odd=(S-RSR)/2 and round its polar part to an exact reflection anticommuting with R. A doubled auxiliary space can balance any zero-kernel sectors. This is a comparison construction, not a supplied native control.
4. Pauli-twirl H_r into the commutant of the two exact axes. Commutator trace bounds control the weight change.
5. Project the third response into the component odd under both axes, yielding Z tensor J. The weighted optimality lemma needs only J>=-bW, not [J,W]=0. A singular-safe weighted clipping could impose that order, but its aggregate trace cost remains to be proved from the original local Gram bounds.

The last step and a complete end-to-end error allocation have not been closed. The current preserved result is finite gentle coarse acquisition and supporting algebra, NOT efficient recovery under an approximate-centrality promise.

## Scientific continuation decision
A completed version would chiefly make the exact central streaming architecture robust to small response-algebra defects. It would still assume continued identical-source access, protected storage and trusted controlled permutations of whole ambient registers, with severe polynomial constants/exponents. That is not yet a demonstrated scalable advantage over realistic control alternatives. Before spending another research window closing this extension, the next mechanism should earn continuation by removing high-rank program suppression or an equally consequential operational bottleneck. Known local-lightcone tomography is a strong baseline; merely adding a locality promise is insufficient.
