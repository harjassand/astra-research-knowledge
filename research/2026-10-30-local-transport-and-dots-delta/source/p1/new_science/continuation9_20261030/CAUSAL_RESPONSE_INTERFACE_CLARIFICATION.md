# The spatial interface in the causal-response comparison

30 October 2026. Root clarification after the actual-file review of
`PREPARED_CORRELATIONS_AND_CAUSAL_RESPONSE.md`; the original input is retained.

The order-N conclusion in its Section 3 compares apparatuses on the **fixed
physical ring/ladder metric**, with every controller link having uniformly
bounded physical range and with uniformly bounded input/output gains. Then
the chosen source and receiver intervals have distance of order N even after
all permitted controller links are included.

Bounded degree on an arbitrary abstract graph is not enough: a matching,
shortcut, or other long physical link can reduce that distance. Such a link
is an additional resource, and the general bounds (2)-(4) use its actual new
distance. The theorem does not rule it out. The compared output is the change
in response to a fresh force relative to the same unforced preparation, not
an arbitrary preprogrammed absolute current.

The original factorial estimate, Duhamel bound, and ideal ring-2 response value
remain unchanged. This clarifies the quantified physical interface; it does
not turn the startup-step result into a sharp frequency-domain lower bound.
