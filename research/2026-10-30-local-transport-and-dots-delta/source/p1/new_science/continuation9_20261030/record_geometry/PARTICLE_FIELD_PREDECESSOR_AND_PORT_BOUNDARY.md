## Exact distinction that matters for a receiver

Let B be a discrete divergence and C the corresponding curl-type field transport. Actual Maxwell geometry has

    B C=0.

If particle current is J and the field evolves according to

    d eta=B dJ,       dq=g dJ+C p dt,

then

    d(Bq-g eta)=0.                                      (1)

The Gauss-law sector is conserved. Starting in a specified sector and integrating a quadratic field energy over that sector generally produces a nontrivial interaction in the particle marginal. In the Coulomb construction this is its purpose. It does not supply a uniform canonical exclusion marginal independent of the field.

The scalar-wave candidate communicated by root instead has

    dq=g dJ-a B^T p dt,       dp=a Bq dt,

and therefore

    d(Bq-g eta)=-a BB^T p dt.                            (2)

It preserves the spatial total of q apart from native current, but it does not preserve the Maxwell Gauss-law constraint. A product canonical particle/Gaussian-memory law can consequently be compatible with its separate reciprocal hop and wave operations. This algebra is a difference of supplied dynamics, not evidence that an ordinary electromagnetic field implements (2).

For the proposed hop `eta->eta^i`, `q_i->q_i+g delta`, the field-energy increment is `g delta q_i+g^2/2`; the actual Metropolis acceptance with that increment has the required marked reverse transition.
