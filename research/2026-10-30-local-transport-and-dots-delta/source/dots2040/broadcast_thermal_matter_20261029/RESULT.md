## New derivation: fixed-alphabet command cost is already order-optimal
For a fixed initialized deterministic device and K allowed fixed-duration commands, the number of words reachable with <=L commands is at most sum_{j=0}^L K^j < K^{L+1}/(K-1). Reaching every N-bit output therefore requires

    L > N/log2(K) + log_K(K-1) - 1.

Astra's O(N) command length cannot be asymptotically improved within that fixed alphabet/timing interface. This is NOT an advantage over ordinary serially addressed shift registers: they also have fixed alphabets and O(N) input length. Counting a continuously shaped waveform as one command moves the word information into shape precision/bandwidth rather than defeating the bound. Stop time is counted in the variable-length sum. Analog timing and measurement feedback are outside this elementary theorem unless charged explicitly.

## Thermal extension begun, deliberately not promoted
The source's conditional activated-escape estimate cannot establish fidelity without an acquired global barrier. A potentially sound alternative is direct additive Brownian-path control for

    dX = -grad Esoft(X,H) dt + sqrt(2 theta) dW.

With source delta=1/1024, choose r=delta and enlarged signed slabs s*[1-r,1+delta+r]. Local drift perturbation is bounded by 65(delta+r), independent of N. At the quarter-field holds, intended drift remains at least 1/4-65(delta+r)=63/512>0. Non-switching margins remain positive; the exterior quartic wall supplies inward drift. Brownian increments on blocks h=r can be bounded directly, without invoking any collective escape energy. A proposed sup-noise allowance eta=r/16 would have union-bound cost 4mB exp[-eta^2/(4 theta h)], with m=5N+2 and B=O(N/h). Thus a rigorous completed slab/arrival proof would produce a very conservative theta=O(1/log(N/epsilon)) sufficient condition, with prefactor about 10^-6. The stochastic invariance/arrival stitching has NOT been independently checked; this is a partial derivation, NOT a thermal theorem. No SDE simulation or device measurement has been claimed.

This potentially closes an explicit source gap, but it changes no decisive capability relative to known globally driven mechanical memories. It is therefore not a good civilisation-level opening by itself.

## Preparation, control, measurement and costs not silently granted
- Preparation: whole-device reset H=-64 and the explicit six-event arm/erase sequence, paid on every rewrite.
- Control: spatially uniform drive, 19-level soft protocol, rise-rate/mobility contract. Uniform scalar H is not fabricated hardware.
- Component: reciprocal signed springs and negative curvature plus a very stiff finite quartic wall. A mathematical decomposition is not a material realization.
- Measurement: output sign readout still requires non-destructive displacement sensing and calibration. No loaded useful actuator is acquired.
- Energy: hard-stop net-work formula 452N+200+26(p_old+p_new) does not transfer to the soft dynamics; unrecovered drive work can be quadratic. Temperature rescaling costs real energy.
- Ordinary comparator has the same target word and is allowed an ordinary serial register or physical dynamic-broadcast apparatus. No practical or full-resource advantage established.
