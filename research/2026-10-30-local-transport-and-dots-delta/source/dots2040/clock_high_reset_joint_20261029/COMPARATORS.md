# Conventional comparators checked early

Primary pages inspected 2026-10-29:

1. Cubitt, Lucia, Michalakis, Perez-Garcia, *Stability of local quantum dissipative systems*, CMP 337 (2015), 1275-1315. https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.4744 . Proves local-observable/correlation stability from rapid mixing and unique fixed point using Lieb-Robinson bounds. It is a relevant conventional interface, not a theorem that arbitrary small perturbations preserve mixing.

2. Lucia, Cubitt, Michalakis, Perez-Garcia, *Rapid mixing and stability of quantum dissipative systems*, PRA 91, 040302 (2015). https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.7809 . Short companion statement of local-observable stability under rapid mixing.

3. Montana-Lopez, Roos, Stengele, Capel, Trivedi, *Is rapid mixing stable?*, submitted 2026-09-28. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36016 . Reports counterexamples to general rapid-mixing stability, including weak local Hamiltonian perturbations, plus sufficient conditions. Thus the direct coefficient-norm proof here is important; importing generic stability would be unsafe.

These are comparator checks, not a novelty determination. The coefficient column-dominance argument is a standard high-noise/Dobrushin-type mechanism specialized to the guarded-reset algebra. The local Lyapunov product construction likewise should not be claimed historically new without a dedicated search.
