# End-to-end polynomial-bit verdict: mathematical theorem versus implementation

2026-10-29. Independent review conclusion, not publication/novelty claim.

## Decision

**At the mathematical existence level, yes:** for explicitly supplied rational positive periodic Toda initial data and rational target time, the repaired lemmas supply a polynomial-bit algorithm with original-state max error epsilon, including exact closed gaps. I do not identify a remaining difficult mathematical oracle to assume. A full executable is not a prerequisite to that theorem.

This conclusion uses the explicit perturbation/outer-matching repair in `INDEPENDENT_INTERFACE_AUDIT.md`, the inexact-domain/forward-error repairs in `LOCAL_INITIALIZATION_REVIEW.md`, the established Toda Lax/inverse-spectral facts already acknowledged in the governing draft, and the precise univariate root-isolation primitive below. The earlier draft *without* these repairs is not the complete proof. This is not a general raw-equation compiler, no practical efficient exponent is claimed, and an equally informed classical algorithm can use the same method.

## Precise root-isolation import, checked

Mehlhorn–Sagraloff–Wang, *From Approximate Factorization to Root Isolation with Application to Cylindrical Algebraic Decomposition*, [arXiv:1301.4870v2, Theorem 5](https://arxiv.org/html/1301.4870), states that an integer polynomial of degree n and coefficient bits tau has all distinct roots and their multiplicities isolated in soft-O(n^3+n^2 tau) bit operations; refinement to disk radius below 2^-kappa costs soft-O(n^3+n^2 tau+n kappa). Its proof computes the distinct-root count from p/gcd(p,p'), and normalizes by the exact leading coefficient. Thus there is no distinct-root-count or leading-coefficient promise in our rational-polynomial application. Clear denominators first. I inspected the theorem and proof, not just its abstract. The first supplied PDF URL failed with a 502; the accessible arXiv full text established the needed contract.

## Finite-size representation and bit ledger

Let B be total binary size of initial rational a,b, let L_T be rational time bit size, and let b=max(0,ceil(log2(1/epsilon))).

1. **Fixed spectral data.** Transfer recurrence, rational polynomial gcds, exact division, and squarefree root isolation compute p,F,K,Ftilde and the radical. Their degrees are O(N), heights polynomial(N,B). The initial Dirichlet roots are roots of an O(N)-degree rational polynomial. Their sheets are evaluated using the original transfer recurrence, and reduced sheets divide by K(mu), not |K(mu)|. Active roots are separated from closed nodes by the radical-derived separation. Exact closed-node multipliers are found by gcd with p-2C and p+2C. All of this is polynomial-bit, with no perturbation of closed gaps.

2. **Computable uniform constants.** Let S be an integer majorizing the logarithms of every explicit forward/inverse, chart, collar, coefficient, and physical decoder bound used in the proof, plus their reciprocals where appropriate. The displayed product/discriminant/Gram formulas make S polynomial(N,B). These are rationally overestimated bounds, not measured condition-number oracles. In particular the very small radical separation and a_* have polynomial logarithms.

3. **Fixed working accuracy.** Choose m=O(L_T+S) so tau=T/2^m is inside the initialization collar. Set a direct budget delta <=2^(-m-cS-b-c), for a sufficiently large absolute c after combining the displayed Lipschitz factors; cap the final accumulated error by a fraction of the collar radius as well as epsilon/kappa_dec. All needed angle/ambient/Abel tolerances then have negative logarithm O(m+b+poly(N,B)). Initialization is evaluated by scaled Taylor series and the repaired inexact contraction at this precision.

4. **Each arithmetic call.** The separation h, ambient matching radius d0, and chart existence radius rho all have negative logarithm polynomial(N,B,m,b). The residual theorem requires q=O(N(log(1/rho)+S))+O(log N) bits. The rounded evaluation matrix has O(N^2) rational entries of size O(q+S). Integer minor/Hadamard bounds give a kernel vector with numerator/denominator bits O(N(q+S+log N)), even if the exact rounded matrix has deficient rank. Normalizing by its largest coefficient preserves polynomial size. No pivot size is needed.

5. **Section bookkeeping.** Clearing denominators in A^2-Ftilde B^2 gives an integer polynomial of degree O(N), coefficient height polynomial(N,q,S). Exact gcds and squarefree decomposition are polynomial in those quantities. The imported theorem isolates/refines all its roots in polynomial work. Sheet quotients have denominator inverse <=2^poly(N,height) by the exact nonzero resultant after gcd removal. Common factors are handled separately, including branch multiplicity; infinity orders use only O(N) rational Taylor coefficients. Therefore the extra precision to produce ambient root balls is polynomial. The outer-radius inequality in the interface note charges *every selected occurrence*, not merely the guaranteed closest zero.

6. **Reset representations after every call.** Do not carry a product of all algebraic root fields, and do not retain the exact algebraic residual coordinates through repeated calls. After the collar and gap identification, compute zeta and its unit normalization. Rotate by a cardinal unit so its real part is at least 1/2 (certified approximate comparison with slack suffices), write t=Im(u)/(1+Re(u)), approximate t by a b_work-bit dyadic, and store the exact rational circle point ((1-t^2)+2it)/(1+t^2), rotated back. The derivative of this parametrization has modulus <=2. The corresponding physical point uses the original exact two gap endpoints and one square root. It is on the unchanged curve exactly and has polynomial-bit descriptive size. Each point's algebraic degree is O(N^2), not an exponentially large joint field. Independent interval evaluation suffices for the next rounded matrix. Precision depends on requested accuracy, not on previous exact section heights.

7. **Physical decoding.** Include the fixed closed nodes in the full finite Dirichlet polynomial. Evaluate stable reciprocal Floquet branches, the positive weight formula, and the moment Gram matrix. Its smallest eigenvalue has inverse <=2^poly(N,B), as proved from a_* and separated nodes. Cholesky/orthogonalization can use rational interval arithmetic: every leading Schur complement has smallest eigenvalue at least the full matrix lower bound, so all square roots/divisions have derived denominator margins. Moment magnitudes are <=2^poly(N,B); differentiating the O(poly N) arithmetic steps gives <=2^poly(N,B) error amplification. Set working precision to absorb that factor, and round a,b to epsilon/2. This charges the decoder algorithm, not merely its formal condition number.

8. **Composition.** Every call costs poly(N,B,m,b), and there are m calls. The direct torus error satisfies e_next<=2e+delta because every stored divisor is a true physical point of the same fixed curve. The fixed reference D0 may be evaluated to arbitrary interval precision from its original algebraic description; approximation errors in each evaluation must be included in delta, not treated as changing the reference silently. Since m=poly(N,B,L_T), total work is polynomial(N,B,L_T,b). All final error comparisons are made within the collar before applying the physical decoder bound.

## Residual obligations, classified honestly

**Not unresolved mathematical capabilities:** exact closed-gap detection; small initial step on the fixed curve; near-conjugate or edge divisor arithmetic without pivot promises; polynomial-height root/sheet/infinity bookkeeping; direct-error composition; original-state decoding. The above lemmas and primitive contracts address them at polynomial-complexity granularity.

**Still needed for an archival full proof:** consolidate constants and notation, explicitly state the classical spectral/Abel algebraic-geometric imports, insert the two local guards and the outer-selection budget into the main theorem, and spell out the input/output encoding and fixed-reference invariant. These are proof-writing/verification obligations; the present independent review is not equivalent to exhaustive peer review of every preceding imported Toda derivation.

**Engineering gaps:** certified arbitrary-genus interval implementation, actual precision admission tests, closed-gap and repeated-doubling end-to-end regressions, and practical operation counts. The existing genus-one numerical phase matches do not certify these. A low-precision case can have excellent apparent phase accuracy while failing zero-matching admission; a real implementation must reject it.
