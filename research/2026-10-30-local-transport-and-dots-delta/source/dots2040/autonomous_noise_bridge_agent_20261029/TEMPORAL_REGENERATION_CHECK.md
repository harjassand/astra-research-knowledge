q(t)^b=λ/κ+(1−λ/κ)e^(−bκt), λ/κ<1.
Then q(0)=1 and q'=−κq+λq^(1−b), so the derivative of q^r equals −rκq^r+rλq^(r−b), which dominates the source, including r<b. Thus arbitrary initial/conditional states obey m_S(t)≤q(t)^r.

This is a bound for the explicitly sampled measurement-instrument process. It does not justify treating the unmeasured continuous evolution as an equivalent classical spacetime flag history.
