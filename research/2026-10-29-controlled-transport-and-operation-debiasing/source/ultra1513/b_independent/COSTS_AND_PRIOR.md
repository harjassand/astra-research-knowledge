## Strongest ordinary construction was acquired first

The current official [small-matrix catalog](https://fmm.univ-lille.fr/4x4x4.html)
already supplies characteristic-zero rank-48 algorithms, and the primary
[Dumas--Pernet--Sedoglavic construction](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.13242v7)
explicitly transports AlphaEvolve's complex decomposition to a rational point
in its isotropy orbit. That is a real positive arithmetic transfer, not a
strawman. Its inverse-of-two coefficient setting differs from the integral
good-reduction hypothesis examined here. The primary
[2026 practical variants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12027) optimize actual
addition/basis overhead. Therefore obtaining rank48 again, or doing only a
tensor identity replay, would not establish a new algorithm or practical
advantage. Direct characteristic-zero Brent-equation optimization also
remains an equally informed ordinary competitor; it was not secretly given
a worse starting representation.

[Kauers--Moosbauer, Section5](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.01175v1) already
uses the ordinary mod-4/Hensel system, reports more than 100,000 nonlifting
rank-47 binary schemes, and also obtains positive integer algorithms for
other formats by lifting. The basic fixed-point mod-4 failure and Hensel
method were therefore not new discoveries here. The
[2026 meta-flip work, Section5.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.02480v1)
continues this ordinary ring-aware search and stresses lifting sensitivity.

The strongest mathematical comparator is ordinary orbit/deformation theory.
[Li--Bao--Zhang](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.09754), introduction and Sections2–3,
already compare Jacobian nullity with isotropy-orbit dimensions for Brent
equations, include the two trivial per-term scaling actions, and distinguish
scheme-theoretic issues. Their stated field there is complex, and inspected
examples include rank49 rather than this mixed-characteristic rank47
question. No novelty is claimed for the principle that exact gauge tangent
directions contain no physical deformation, or for Hensel lifting itself.
The new written derivation explicitly relates the acquired full binary
kernel to **all finite ramification orders** by finite exact gauge removal.
It is a direct local-deformation argument; this bounded source screen does
not establish historical priority for it or its native corollary.

An equally informed expert using those ordinary tools can obtain this same
negative conclusion once the gauge-complete rank equality is paid. There
is no comparison showing a practical advantage over an established general
deformation implementation. Conversely, the already-known mod-4
inconsistency alone does not justify the ramified conclusion: `x^2=2`
shows exactly why the new tangent premise is needed.

## Research decision

The proposed positive mechanism fails at the acquired seed. Its complete
139-dimensional infinitesimal kernel is exhausted by exact gauges. The
explicit mod-4 obstruction then excludes every finite ramification with
this integral reduction, even after residue-field extension or a lifted
unit/isotropy change of coordinates. This closes a real unexamined escape
from the earlier unramified failure; it does not produce a new algorithm.

The additional term-independence result also rules out individual poles
whose projective limiting factors are the SAME native factors. A genuinely
different bad-reduction boundary, allowing dependent leading term tensors,
remains outside the theorem. Even denominators alone are not automatically
an escape at this acquired projective point.

Do not spend more compute on ramified continuation of this same reduction.
The evidence that would justify a different modular porting attempt is a
new seed with a paid nongauge tangent space and an actually solved
higher-order obstruction, or a new characteristic-zero construction with
bad/different reduction. Those are missing acquisitions, not outputs of
this package. The general rank47 problem and any civilization-level
computational gain remain unacquired. Ordinary direct characteristic-zero
search/rational orbit transport remain the stronger positive mechanisms
actually in hand.
