6. Strong alternatives and remaining gaps

Several ordinary routes remain serious competitors.

Quantum program storage already provides exact heralded execution. A stored Choi program and Bell projection can execute the channel with success probability 
1
/
𝑑
2
1/d
2
. This is already in Nielsen–Chuang’s starting machinery, including the nonunitary extension. It retains quantum memory and can fail after interacting with the payload, so its contract differs substantially from classical instruction execution. 
arxiv.org

Physical approximate Petz implementation is prior work. Besides the dilation-access algorithm, Zhu and colleagues discuss a sample-access physical approximation through Uhlmann-transformation machinery, alongside their own virtual observable-estimation method. A complete cost comparison with that physical alternative remains necessary; the attempted full-source retrieval was unavailable and was not bypassed. 
arxiv.org

Structured promises can make conventional estimation much stronger. The parallel construction produced a finite phase-twirling compiler: on a calibrated qubit phase family, 66 fixed source calls convert a 50%-depolarized source into an exactly averaged 25%-depolarized target. Broad noisy-unitary purification is established research, including a recent optimal leading-order query law, so that construction is retained as a comparator rather than a novelty claim. 
arxiv.org

The decisive unresolved issues are now concrete:

A general certified recovery catalogue and derivative bounds have not been instantiated.
The displayed compiler does not establish manageable expected classical runtime.
No scalable advantage over strong approximate recovery methods has been demonstrated.
The exact batch converse has not been extended here to nonzero simulation error.
The precise novelty of the matrix construction, resource formula and stopping arguments still needs comprehensive review.

My scientific assessment is that this round establishes useful machinery and a consequential boundary. It supplies a route to a nonlinear physical operation, while ruling out generic dimension-efficient classical archiving under the stated acquisition interface. It does not yet establish a civilisation-changing capability.
