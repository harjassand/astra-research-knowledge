# Selection, primary predecessors and productive failures

29 October 2026. Brief research record for continuation 7. Earlier
scientific assets and accepted dependencies are unchanged.

## Chosen consequence

The target is a physically controlled family whose entire macroscopic
density/current action is known despite unknown frozen microscopic
kinetics. A conventional macroscopic-fluctuation solver can then
compute the cost of a specified transport history without learning
the hidden amplitudes. This is a population asymptotic capability,
conditional on the actual ports and structural bounds. No apparatus,
finite-system reliability certificate or practical speedup is supplied.

The round began with the inherited equilibrium response result. A full
hydrodynamic trajectory was a useful intermediate, but ordinary entropy
methods and deterministic phase cycling reach it. The harder and more
consequential target became rare histories at exponential volume scale.
Root developed the phase exponential estimate, an explicit scalar
circulation LDP, then the full action receiving construction. The
complementary roles reconstructed ordinary control and hydrodynamic
routes, rather than treating familiar methods as new inventions.

## Close primary routes, inspected early

1. Kipnis--Olla--Varadhan, *Hydrodynamics and large deviation for simple
   exclusion processes* (1989), DOI 10.1002/cpa.3160420202. This is the
   established weak-asymmetry and hydrodynamic-deviation framework.
   The published abstract was inspected; the detailed receiving
   martingale/action construction was read in the next source.

2. Bertini--De Sole--Gabrielli--Jona-Lasinio--Landim, *Large deviations
   of the empirical current in interacting particle systems*,
   https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0512394. The full paper was inspected,
   especially Lemma 3.1, Theorem 3.2 and Section 3's current LDP.
   Its SSEP action is exactly the target, after rescaling by s.
   Weak tilts, occupation replacement, action duality and smooth-path
   lower bounds are established. The source's language about continuous
   paths must be interpreted as limiting/interpolated support, not
   the literal absence of finite-N jumps.

3. Redig--Saada--Sau, *Symmetric simple exclusion process in dynamic
   environment: hydrodynamics* (2020), DOI 10.1214/20-EJP536,
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.01366. This is a strong path-space
   dynamic-conductance comparator. The self-duality method does not
   directly apply to rates depending arbitrarily on the occupancy
   configuration. That distinction is scope, not a lower bound
   against other conventional methods.

4. Yasodharan--Sundaresan, *Large deviations of mean-field interacting
   particle systems in a fast varying environment*,
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.06855. The full source's model and method
   were inspected: finite particle types, a fixed finite environment,
   coupled empirical-measure/environment-occupation LDP, stochastic
   exponentials. Rare-event averaging is established. Its stated
   framework is not a plug-in theorem for a growing collection of
   bond phases and spatially conservative, arbitrary nonlocal rates.

5. Kraaij--Schlottke, *A large deviation principle for Markovian
   slow-fast systems*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.05686. The full
   source's introduction/model was inspected. Its effective Hamiltonian
   incorporates fast occupation costs/eigenvalues. This is a direct
   conceptual predecessor to controlling a rare-event law by fast
   variables. The present estimate supplies a uniform-in-volume
   simplification for the specified conserving architecture; it does
   not invent slow-fast large deviations or the idea of averaging.

6. Deterministic exponential conjugation and averaging are also
   established. The actual complementary reconstruction in
   `physical_mechanism/` supplies global rotation/pi-cycle controls
   for bounded influence and independent local kicks for unrestricted
   contexts, including weak current comparison laws. Those controls
   are coequal ordinary alternatives when their ports are supplied.

Search coverage and this construction do not establish priority of the
assembled theorem. The candidate contribution is its stated physical
access, growing-system uniformity and receiving consequence, not the
classical action, a new learner, or a general exclusion of calibration.

## Productive failures and restrictions retained

- An equilibrium two-time compression estimate does not itself prove
  an empirical-density path limit, much less its rare-event action.
  The current round adds occupation, martingale and exponential inputs.

- Averaging only the drift of a typical trajectory is inadequate for
  a rare-history theorem. The phase potential must be negligible at
  speed N and under the weak comparison tilts used by the lower bound.

- A reversible variational formula cannot simply be applied to a
  nonreversible tilted generator. The exact likelihood second-moment
  identity and the retained-Dirichlet form estimate repair that step.

- An exp(hN) warm-up density ceiling transfers superexponential bad
  events. Used on a finite exp(-NI) probability it would shift the
  exponent. The receiving proof derives its finite action from the
  actual likelihood and actual initial law instead.

- The full current path needs a stated topology and compact containment.
  Pointwise tests alone do not supply it. In one dimension the exact
  conservation relation gives ||W_t||TV<=|Q_t|+1, allowing the current
  receiving argument to close in a negative Sobolev path space.

- An ordinary local-equilibrium assertion is a separate input from
  phase averaging. The exchange-form comparison, not an unproved
  claim that the full joint density factorizes, supplies it.

- A fixed confidence failure probability overwhelms exponentially
  small probabilities. The saved mixture example gives the actual
  altered exponent. Calibration selection and retained records remain
  in the deployment law.

- Vanishing reciprocal rate errors can be harmless at this scale.
  A directional bias of order 1/N changes typical macroscopic current.
  The distinction is proved in the implementation-symmetry note.

- Fast common control is not interchangeable with independent local
  actuation in unrestricted contextual families. The complementary
  heat-bath parity example gives a collective obstruction. It is
  explicitly not a sharp necessity theorem for the conserving ring.

- The numerical work checks finite population tilted generators and
  their finite-time MGFs. N=4 and 6 are far from the hydrodynamic
  exponent: their exact averaged-SSEP values are retained as the
  proper finite-size comparator. Neither matching an asymptotic
  formula prematurely nor adding larger easy fixtures proves the LDP.

## Conventional routes remain open

Per-device kinetic calibration, direct rare-event sampling, importance
sampling, additional initializers and ordinary deterministic controls
can all be useful when their actual experiments and costs are supplied.
No sample-complexity lower bound against all those routes is claimed.
A controller with the same selective ports can execute the present
construction. The intended gain is that the physical intervention
changes which microscopic information the desired prediction needs.

The theorem integrates over the actual fresh actuator randomness for
visible path events. Actuator records may be retained, but the joint
LDP of visible history and all those records is a stronger unproved
object. Arbitrary conditioning on a rare future actuator record can
change the visible law and is not licensed by the population result.

## Continued construction after the first completed action snapshot

The common-control challenge subsequently moved into the same
conserving ring. Root's parity amplitude alpha_i=alpha p delta_i gives
an exact physical current decomposition. For a shared Poisson clock
with gamma_N->infinity but gamma_N/N->0, it yields a strict rare-current
upper rate for arbitrary fixed-mass starts and a matching scalar LDP
under the separately stated canonical initial-law premise. The matching
lower uses a corrected-current tilt whose adjoint entropy source is
O(1), not the unproved full common-clock spatial action. At alpha_N->1,
nonzero circulation is superexponentially suppressed.

The complementary role also closed a deterministic-rotation current
FCLT obstruction inside the conserving ring, using an exact parity
inverse-energy bound and a time-dependent forward/backward estimate.
Its canonical odd-count initialization and CLT scope are retained.

Root then used the zero-rate form repair to construct the strong
ordinary alternative: groups of n_g bonds with clock rates gamma_g
recover the reference action when max_g n_g/gamma_g->0. One sufficiently
fast common clock is therefore an enabling implementation, not excluded
by the failure at lower shared bandwidth. Clock-event count and the
number of addressed bond flips remain separate physical costs.

These extensions make the relevant information more precise: the
individual clock laws and invariant weights do not determine the
macroscopic transport action; actual joint control correlations and
bandwidth matter. A candidate nonlocal current-mobility action is
recorded separately with its exact algebra and unresolved residual.
The full shared-clock spatial action is not claimed proved.
