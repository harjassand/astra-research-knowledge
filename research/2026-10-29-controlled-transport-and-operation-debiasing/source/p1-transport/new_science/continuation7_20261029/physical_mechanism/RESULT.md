\[
c[1+\alpha_i(\eta_{\rm outside},z)\cos\theta_i],\qquad
c=s/2,\quad |\alpha_i|<1,
\]

where each amplitude is unchanged by its own exchange and \(z\) is arbitrary frozen hidden information. The mean generator is SSEP with swap rate \(c\).

An **actual deterministic phase rotation** \(\theta_i(t)=\theta_i(0)+\Omega_N t\) makes the full empirical density trajectory on physical times \(N^2[0,T]\) converge to

\[
\partial_t\rho=c\,\partial_x^2\rho
\]

when \(D_N/\Omega_N\to0\). Here \(D_N\) bounds the number of current summands affected by one particle exchange. For fixed finite-range contexts, \(D_N=O(1)\), so \(\Omega_N\) may tend to infinity arbitrarily slowly. A real global pi-cycle supplier similarly works for \(h_ND_N\to0\), where \(2h_N\) is its physical period.

The result allows every deterministic visible initial configuration associated with the desired initial density profile, arbitrary initial phases, and arbitrary frozen hidden parameters and retained old records compatible with the declared future native law. Neither visible equilibration nor phase randomization is needed. The native dynamics need not preserve a linear density-observable sector.

An **actual independent local Poisson pi-kick** supplier reaches the same full density path for every \(\gamma_N\to\infty\), even when the amplitude at each bond depends on the entire unchanged visible context. Initial signs can all be deterministically +1 and that fact may be retained. Finite-state entropy/density domination pays for this initial condition instead of claiming a product equilibrium initialization.

## Mechanism and proof

For a smooth empirical density test \(F_N\), the oscillating microscopic current source \(V_N=NBF_N\) is bounded, and one exchange changes it by at most \(C D_N/N\). A time-dependent exponential gauge \(e^{aQ(t)V_N}\), with \(Q'=q\) and \(\|Q\|=O(h)\) or \(O(1/\Omega)\), removes the oscillating Feynman–Kac potential exactly. The remaining symmetric numerical-range cost is bounded by

\[
\sum_i r_i\,[\cosh(aQ\Delta_iV_N)-1].
\]

On physical time \(N^2T\), this yields a log-MGF cost of order \(a^2 h^2D_N^2NT\). Weak current tilting adds a cost \(C_E NT\), independent of the test multiplier \(a\), after its antisymmetric contribution is absorbed in native Dirichlet energy. Taking the volume limit for fixed \(a\), then \(a\to\infty\), proves superexponential replacement. Every visible initial law is dominated by \(2^N\) times the canonical uniform law, which does not alter superexponential negligibility.

Independent pi kicks give the elementary alternative bound \(C a^2NT/\gamma\) through sign-flip Dirichlet energy. Initial sign/visible laws are dominated by \(4^N\) times the stationary product reference. This is an actual cost of changing the initial law in the proof, not a hidden preparation operation.

An ordinary density martingale has quadratic variation \(O(1/N)\); Fourier-mode integral equations then give uniform convergence of the complete empirical measure path in an explicit metric for weak measures. No unverified general hydrodynamic theorem is needed for that receiving step.

## A substantive obstruction survives

The deterministic influence condition cannot be discarded just because the requested output is collective. In the broader reversible heat-bath family, let

\[
L_t=\sum_i[1+\alpha\cos(\Omega t)\prod_{j\ne i}y_j](E_i-I).
\]

The native rates are bounded per site and have common uniform equilibrium. The empirical magnetization \(m_N\) couples exactly to configuration parity \(p_N\):

\[
L_tm_N=-m_N-\alpha\cos(\Omega t)p_N,
\quad L_tp_N=-Np_N-\alpha N\cos(\Omega t)m_N.
\]

A bounded corrector and martingale estimate prove that if \(\Omega_N\to\infty\) and \(\Omega_N/N\to r<\infty\), the actual collective trajectory converges to

\[
m_0\exp\left[-\left(1-\frac{\alpha^2}{2(1+r^2)}\right)t\right],
\]

instead of the mean-generator prediction \(m_0e^{-t}\). Thus \(\Omega_N\gg N\) is genuinely required in this exact unrestricted-context family. This is a collective-output failure, not merely a demand to approximate the all-spin parity observable. It does not prove sharpness for the conserving nearest-neighbor ring; that narrower necessity question remains open.

The separate exact common-clock calculation strengthens the supplier distinction: with one common Poisson pi phase of rate \(\gamma_N\), the same empirical magnetization has decay rate \(1-\alpha^2N/(N+2\gamma_N)\). Thus a shared phase needs \(\gamma_N\gg N\) in this family, while genuinely independent local pi clocks need only \(\gamma_N\to\infty\).

## Real supplier and record limits

The deterministic actuator must actually impose phase translations while preserving the native model. Independent local pi flips are a different physical actuator, and their Poisson innovations must remain fresh conditional on the old deployment record. Retaining their outcomes as they occur is compatible with concentration under the same actual joint law; uniform guarantees after arbitrary rare-record postselection are not claimed.

On physical time \(N^2T\), deterministic pi cycling uses order \(N^2T/h\) global pulse events, each acting on \(N\) phases. Independent Poisson control uses \(\gamma N^3T\) expected local kicks. Continuous rotation acts on every phase for the full duration. Power, phase accuracy, pulse waveform, control isolation, and fabrication costs are not specified by these rates.
