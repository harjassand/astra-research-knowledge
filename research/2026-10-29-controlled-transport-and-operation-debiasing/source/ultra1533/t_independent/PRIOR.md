The governing closest constructive primary is Simon Buchholz, *Phase transitions
for a class of gradient fields*, PTRF179 (2021), DOI10.1007/s00440-020-01021-5:
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00440-020-01021-5 . Main representation
and Section5.6--5.7 were read. Its latent conductance law, attractive conditional,
and squared transfer-current calculation are paid predecessors. The present step
uses one-configuration column sums for a finite global coupling, instead of an
entrywise worst-environment sum. The paper's infinite-volume claims are not
silently strengthened by our finite result.

Propp--Wilson's exact CFTP mechanism and preservation of old maps are standard:
https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/206-RWG/RWGpapers/propp_wilson.pdf , Sections2.1--2.2.

https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~deloera/MISC/LA-BIBLIO/trunk/Dyer/Dyer2.pdf . Neither is
presented as new. Gaussian conditioning, determinant lemmas, leverage scores,
rejection sampling and Box--Muller output are also ordinary tools.

Strong conventional Gaussian-reference MCMC includes elliptical slice sampling,
Murray--Adams--MacKay2010: https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.0175 , and function-space pCN,
Cotter--Roberts--Stuart--White2013: https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.0709 . Their primary
abstracts were screened. Their Gaussian-reference treatment precludes claiming
generic removal of harmonic ill-conditioning as a new principle. They are not
automatically exact independent finite-word outputs after an unqualified burn-in.
This does not imply that the present CFTP implementation wins at a finite accuracy.

No identical finite-graph contraction/contrast theorem was located in this bounded
primary screen. That is NOT a novelty certificate. In particular a known

already encompass it. Historical priority is UNKNOWN. No literature-only ranking
is offered as the result: proofs, positive samplers, full quantized outputs and
the stronger conventional win have actually been constructed and executed.
