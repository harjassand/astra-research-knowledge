Here the proposed access is the demonstrated Ca+/CaOH+ recoil-cat apparatus,

## 5. Finite-history theorem: no known molecular Hamiltonian required

Assume the science interval is impulsive relative to the ion motion, and has
the position-dependent unitary (or trace-preserving isometric dilation)

    U(x,phi) = sum_{h=-r}^r exp(i h kappa x) M_h(phi).

It must be unitary/isometric for EVERY continuous x, not only on a sampled
position grid. Molecular/internal-reference dimension is not bounded. Initial
atomic-motion preparation is independent of the internal state rho; any bath
is included in rho/dilation. Controls and readout are independent of rho.

A sufficient finite-band model is four pulses between two bands G,E, with
Q=P_E and phase covariance
U_j(x)=exp(i q_j kappa x Q) U_j(0) exp(-i q_j kappa x Q), q_j in{-1,0,+1}.
Each pulse has just momentum orders0,+q_j,-q_j. Products with arbitrary
position-independent intermediate dynamics have degree<=4. This statement
allows arbitrary pulse strength and internal dynamics inside each assumed
band; it does NOT acquire a two-band approximation for CaOH+, a phase-locked
pulse train, or motion frozen over an arbitrary delay. Extra bands, spontaneous
emission recoil, motion during science, and field-dependent atomic backgrounds
are not silently contained in this finite band.

Define C_hj=Tr(M_h rho M_j*). It is PSD and, by pointwise unitarity,

    sum_{h-j=l} C_hj = delta_l0.                         (1)

Indeed the coefficient of exp(i l kappa x) in U(x)*U(x) is
sum_j M_j* M_{j+l}=delta_l0 I; tracing against rho proves(1). In particular
Tr C=1. No classical replacement of C by its diagonal is allowed.

Let v_h be the complete external output if the h-th momentum kick alone is
inserted at the science location, including parking, actual return, uncat and
analysis. For mean-bright observable O with -I<=O<=I define K_jh=<v_j,O v_h>.
For mixed preparation/noisy fixed instruments replace this by the corresponding
bilinear trace expression. Linearity gives the EXACT complete mean

    e(phi)=Tr[C(phi) K].                                (2)

Thus all unknown molecular dynamics enter a finite PSD coefficient matrix;
the instrument is a finite cross-history kernel. Loaded one-kick means give
only diagonal entries and generally do not determine(2). This is a standard
coherent-instrument/Gram representation, not molecular tomography.

If a residual D component receives a uniform phase z=exp(-i chi), split each
v_h=g_h+z d_h after return/readout. Relative to a reference instrument,

    Delta K(chi)=A+z B+conj(z) B*,
    A=Kgg+Kdd-Ktarget, B=Kgd.                           (3)

This phase may be arbitrary but must be fixed across a phase cycle and uniform
on the modeled D component. A position-dependent light shift, changing chi
with optical phase, or state-dependent backgrounds require a different model

There is no new generic Ramsey, BB1, STIRAP, phase-cycling, or SDP principle.

Standard coherent multidimensional/action spectroscopy is a strong incumbent

Statistical precision is still expensive. For independent balanced/randomized
