# Parity lifts: antecedents and the scope of a universal inference obstruction

Date: 2026-10-29. Targeted review for the root intervention-information branch. The root retains ownership of the prospective construction and its scientific interpretation. This is a literature and scope review, not a claim of historical priority.

## Finding

The doubled forward/adjoint construction belongs to established skew-detailed-balance lifting. The strongest precise collision is the arbitrary-adjoint-pair formulation of Andrieu and Livingstone (2021), rather than a superficially similar physical example. What may remain useful as a theorem in this project is a consequence for the set of possible observations: every stationary, time-reversal-invariant output law generated by a finite Markov chain lies in the finite-horizon total-variation closure of irreducible, generalized-reversible hidden models. That consequence needs the admissible parity, preparation, access, and horizon restrictions stated explicitly.

The search did not locate that exact universal inference statement. It was deliberately bounded and does not establish priority. It did locate closely related work showing why physical parity cannot be assigned solely to make an entropy formula vanish.

## 1. Exact mathematical antecedents

### Andrieu and Livingstone: arbitrary adjoint pair and a sign lift

[Andrieu and Livingstone, *Peskun–Tierney ordering for Markovian Monte Carlo: Beyond the reversible scenario*, Annals of Statistics 49, 1958–1981 (2021), DOI 10.1214/20-AOS2008](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10108137/1/Livingstone_20-AOS2008.pdf) is the most direct source. Section 3.3, equations (5)–(7), starts with substochastic kernels satisfying

\[
\pi(dx)T_+(x,dy)=\pi(dy)T_-(y,dx).
\]

It constructs a chain on a state space enlarged by a sign variable, with invariant measure \(\pi/2\), reversal involution \((x,v)\mapsto(x,-v)\), and sign-switch probabilities whose difference is fixed by the difference of row sums. The remaining switch freedom is explicit.

Take \(T_+=(1-a)P\), \(T_-=(1-a)P^*\), and equal switch probabilities \(a\). Their construction gives exactly

\[
\begin{pmatrix}(1-a)P&aI\\aI&(1-a)P^*\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Uniformization supplies the proposed continuous-time version. Section 4.1 separately develops involution-reversible semigroups and their generator criterion. Thus neither the two replicas, the use of the adjoint, nor equal sign switches should be presented as a new construction. The paper's objective is sampler comparison, not the proposed universal observation/inference corollary.

### Turitsyn, Chertkov, and Vucelja: earlier duplicate-state implementation

[Turitsyn, Chertkov, and Vucelja, *Irreversible Monte Carlo algorithms for efficient sampling*, Physica D 240, 410–414 (2011), DOI 10.1016/j.physd.2010.10.003](https://www.mit.edu/~turitsyn/assets/pubs/Turitsyn2011ik.pdf), section 4, equations (2)–(4), duplicates states into two replicas, reverses replica fluxes, and permits transitions between copies of the same state. Balance determines the difference of the two inter-replica switch rates, leaving a symmetric freedom. Its presentation starts from splitting a reversible base kernel; the arbitrary-adjoint-pair formulation above is the cleanest exact match to an arbitrary \(Q,Q^*\).

[Vucelja, *Lifting—A nonreversible Markov chain Monte Carlo algorithm* (author review, arXiv:1412.8762v4, 2016)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.8762), section V, equations (30)–(39), explicitly reviews the two-copy construction and switch freedom. This corroborates the established status of the mechanism; it is not the sole primary attribution.

### An explicit generalized-reversible hidden Markov formalism

[Zacher et al., *Annotation of genomics data using bidirectional hidden Markov models unveils variations in Pol II transcription cycle*, Molecular Systems Biology 10 (2014), DOI 10.15252/msb.20145654](https://link.springer.com/article/10.15252/msb.20145654) develops a hidden-state involution and an observation involution. Its mathematical conditions include

\[
\pi_i a_{ij}=\pi_{\bar j}a_{\bar j\bar i},\qquad
\pi_i=\pi_{\bar i},\qquad
\psi_i(o)=\psi_{\bar i}(\bar o).
\]

These imply invariance of the observation law under sequence reversal and conjugation. The source also gives an explicit symmetrization of an arbitrary transition matrix and stationary weights. This is a useful mathematical HMM antecedent to the implication from hidden involution reversibility to symmetric observations. Its reversal is genomic sequence orientation, not physical time reversal. It does not itself prove a thermodynamic realization or the exact law-preserving completion of an arbitrary symmetric output process. Only the mathematical passages were needed for this review.

## 2. Physical parity and hidden entropy: closest consequential precedents

[Shankar and Marchetti, *Hidden entropy production and work fluctuations in an ideal active gas*, Physical Review E 98, 020604 (2018)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1804.03099), especially the introduction, Table I, and the discussion of the time-reversal parity of propulsion, shows that different parity conventions yield different entropy expressions. The overdamped description can conceal entropy retained by the underdamped dynamics. Its point is precisely why an abstract transformation that makes one reversed path divergence vanish is insufficient to establish the physical dissipation of an already specified system. The microscopic interpretation of the force and eliminated variables matters.

[Degünther, van der Meer, and Seifert, *Fluctuating entropy production on the coarse-grained level: Inference and localization of irreversibility*, Physical Review Research 6, 023175 (2024); inspected preprint arXiv:2309.07665](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.07665), introduction and section IV A, explains the extra care needed for odd variables, including stationary-distribution factors in time reversal. Their stationary odd-variable models need not have a parity-symmetric stationary distribution. The proposed doubled model does, so those caveats do not invalidate its formal path-reversal identity; they do forbid silently transporting that identity to a physically different entropy convention.

[Dieball and Godec, *Perspective: Time irreversibility in systems observed at coarse resolution*, Journal of Chemical Physics 162, 090901 (2025), DOI 10.1063/5.0251089](https://www.mpinat.mpg.de/4960640/Dieball_2025_Perspective.pdf), introduction and section III, emphasizes the relation between microscopic assumptions, physical reversal, and the coarse record. In particular, hidden memory cannot automatically be assigned a convenient reversal rule. This is a closely relevant statement of scope, although not the universal doubled-completion theorem.

[Knight, Kaveh, and Pruessner, *Self-Propulsion Symmetries Determine Entropy Production of Active Particles with Hidden States*, Physical Review Letters 136, 198302 (2026), DOI 10.1103/xbk2-ggcf](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/xbk2-ggcf), together with the inspected first two pages of [arXiv:2507.22199v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.22199), relates reversal and sign symmetries of hidden self-propulsion to the partial entropy production of visible position trajectories. It gives physically specified examples where visible trajectories are reversible while the full model dissipates. A symmetry of the distribution of hidden propulsion under sign inversion must not be conflated with permission to assign a different physical time-reversal parity to a variable. This is a specific active-particle result, not an arbitrary finite-state hidden completion.

## 3. Scoped mathematical corollary of the established lift

Let \(Q\) be a finite irreducible row generator, \(\pi\) its stationary law, and \(Q^*=\Pi^{-1}Q^T\Pi\) its stationary adjoint. Consider

\[
\widehat Q_\epsilon=
\begin{pmatrix}
Q-\epsilon I&\epsilon I\\
\epsilon I&Q^*-\epsilon I
\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\widehat\pi=(\pi/2,\pi/2),\qquad
\theta(x,s)=(x,-s).
\]

Then \(\widehat\pi\) is \(\theta\)-invariant, and

\[
\widehat Q_\epsilon^*=\theta\widehat Q_\epsilon\theta.
\]

Consequently the stationary hidden path law is invariant under time reversal followed by \(\theta\). Its relative-entropy production under that stated reversal is zero. For \(\epsilon>0\), the lifted chain is irreducible.

Suppose the measured output ignores the sign. At \(\epsilon=0\), its stationary path law is the equal mixture of the original output law and its reverse. Thus an already reversal-invariant output law is preserved exactly. This exact version has two closed hidden classes.

For a finite interval of length \(T\), couple the lifted process at \(\epsilon>0\) with the zero-switch process until the first independent sign-switch event. This gives

\[
\|\mathsf P_{\epsilon,T}-\mathsf P_{0,T}\|_{\mathrm{TV}}
\leq 1-e^{-\epsilon T}.
\]

Therefore any such symmetric output law lies in the finite-horizon total-variation closure of irreducible, generalized-reversible hidden models. This is a closure statement, not an exact irreducible realization theorem, and it is not uniform over unbounded observation horizons.

For a fixed collection of independent stationary records with total duration \(B\), the same coupling gives total-variation distance at most \(1-e^{-\epsilon B}\). A test whose false-positive probability is uniformly at most \(\alpha\) over the zero-generalized-entropy model class consequently has power at most \(\alpha\) on the target symmetric law: apply the bound for each positive \(\epsilon\), then let \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) with \(B\) fixed. This is a finite-budget uniform inference obstruction. It does not say that two fixed, distinct models cannot eventually be distinguished.

## 4. Conditions that must remain attached

1. **Physical reversal.** The result compares different admissible model completions. It does not allow changing the physical parity of an already specified system in order to erase its entropy production. A generalized-reversible Markov representation alone is not a constructed zero-heat physical device.

2. **What information is absent.** Passive, stationary output laws do not specify the sign variable, its physical reversal, or a faithful operation implementing that reversal. Known interventions or resets impose additional constraints that this construction does not automatically preserve.

3. **Preparation and time scales.** Stationary preparation is an assumption. The invariant law is given mathematically, not acquired for free. As \(\epsilon\to0\), inter-replica mixing becomes arbitrarily slow. A known mixing or preparation bound may exclude this particular closure sequence.

4. **All-even is sufficient, not uniquely necessary.** The obstruction demonstrates the need for a restriction on admissible hidden reversal/dynamics if symmetric records are to certify the intended entropy. Requiring all hidden variables to be even is one such restriction. Other physically supplied parity, kinetic, control, or preparation information can serve that role.

5. **Resolved ports.** The whole-state lift duplicates every resolved port. If the interface promises that a port is one physical state, that lift does not satisfy the promise as stated. This is a substantive constraint, not merely relabeling.

## 5. A possible exact port-preserving refinement, assigned back to the root

For a model with resolved states \(B\) and hidden states \(I\), a candidate refinement keeps \(B\) once and duplicates only \(I\). Give the copies weights \(\pi_I/2\), retain weights \(\pi_B\), place \(Q_{II}\) and \(Q^*_{II}\) in the two interiors, use entrance rates \(q_{BI}/2\) and \(q^*_{BI}/2\), and use exit rates \(q_{IB}\) and \(q^*_{IB}\). Average direct resolved-to-resolved rates. The involution fixes \(B\) and swaps the interior copies.

The proposed weighted excursion kernel is then \((K(t)+K(t)^T)/2\), while port escape rates stay unchanged. If the native weighted kernel is symmetric, this suggests exact preservation of the endpoint/duration interface without an \(\epsilon\)-limit, potentially with irreducibility inherited through the shared ports. This refinement was sent to the root for construction and independent verification. It is not credited to the located literature, and this review does not certify additional intervention responses or arbitrary full-path observables.
