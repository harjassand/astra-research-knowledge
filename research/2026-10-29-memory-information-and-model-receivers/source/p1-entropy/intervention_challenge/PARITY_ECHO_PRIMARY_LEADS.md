## Stochastic echo

Jae Dong Noh, *Stochastic Echo Phenomena in Nonequilibrium Systems*,
J. Korean Phys. Soc. 64, 504 (2014), arXiv:1212.2706v2.

Primary URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.2706

Retrieval scope: primary metadata and abstract read, not the full proof.
The work studies control protocols producing reversed stochastic motion,
derives optimal protocols for Langevin dynamics, and identifies exact echo
examples and dual systems. This is a direct antecedent for the general
echo concept. The supplied abstract does not settle whether the present
finite-state midpoint CP certificate occurs inside its full argument.

## Entropy estimation with odd variables

Dong-Kyum Kim, Sangyun Lee and Hawoong Jeong, *Estimating entropy production
in a stochastic system with odd-parity variables*, Phys. Rev. Research 4,
023051 (2022), arXiv:2112.04681v2.

Primary URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.04681

Retrieval scope: primary metadata and abstract read, not the full proof.
The paper develops a neural estimation method for path entropy with odd
variables, illustrated by an underdamped bead-spring model and an odd-parity
Markov jump model. Its precise observation requirements have not been
audited here. It should not be called an equally informed competitor to the
coarse endpoint parity-pulse interface without reading those requirements.

## Current hidden active-state research

Jacob Knight, Farid Kaveh and Gunnar Pruessner, *Self-Propulsion Symmetries
Determine Entropy Production of Active Particles with Hidden States*,
Phys. Rev. Lett. 136, 198302 (15 May 2026), arXiv:2507.22199.

Primary URLs:

- https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/xbk2-ggcf
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22199

Retrieval scope: publisher abstract/metadata and primary preprint search
abstract, not the full proof. The study develops a perturbative treatment of
partial path entropy for active particles with hidden propulsion. Hidden
parity and time-reversal properties govern that partial entropy; examples
include a telegraph process and resetting diffusion. This is a consequential
current application lead, not an acquired realization of the present pulse.
