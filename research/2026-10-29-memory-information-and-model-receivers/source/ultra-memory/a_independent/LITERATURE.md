# Controlling primary comparison

* **Balasubramanian, Davydova, Lin (11 May 2026), A passive self-correcting quantum memory in three dimensions**, [arXiv:2605.10943v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10943v1). The abstract/main theorem already supply the generic passive-3D-storage existence consequence. Section 1.1 explicitly defers TQO-2 and arbitrary-local-perturbation gap stability. Sections 3.2.2–3.2.6 supply the actual cell/restriction primitives used here; Lemma 3.8 supplies local H1=0/connectivity, Lemma 3.14 supplies degrees, and Theorem 1.2 supplies bounded-density locality. These are imported source results, not consequences established by our small codes. Section 6's separate ell=8 deterministic embedding leaves its full thermal-lifetime argument to future work; it is not substituted for the qualified random construction.
* **Yin, Lucas (published 2025), Low-density parity-check codes as stable phases of quantum matter**, [arXiv:2411.01002v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.01002v2), [PRX Quantum 6, 030329](https://doi.org/10.1103/361k-nj4b). Definition of check soundness, Lemma 24/B25 and formal Theorem 2 are the ordinary sufficient stability operation. Any fixed polynomial soundness is allowed with polynomial interaction-shell growth. We do not claim that general theorem or infer finite-temperature stability from it.
* **De Roeck, Khemani, Li, O'Dea, Rakovszky**, [arXiv:2411.02384v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02384v3), [PRX Quantum 6, 030330](https://doi.org/10.1103/7x71-8j7k). A strong graph-local stability comparator. Its local-generation hypotheses are distinct from just code distance. Its current HTML build was unavailable; the PDF was acquired and the hypotheses in section 2.2 read. The final bridge here uses Yin–Lucas instead.
* **Bravyi, Hastings, Michalakis (2010), Topological quantum order: stability under local perturbations**, [arXiv:1001.0344](https://arxiv.org/html/1001.0344). Lemma 2.1 gives the stabilizer local-generation form of TQO-2. Section 2.4 already gives a same-groundspace/same-TQO-1 counterexample whose gap closes under vanishing fields. The initial boundary-pinned-repetition caution is an ordinary warning, not our new theorem or evidence against the actual recursive code.
* **Guo (26 Oct 2026), Self-Correcting 3D Surface Code with Growing Local Dimension**, [arXiv:2610.35099v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.35099v1). A separate contemporary route, with growing local dimension and elementary small-shift noise. It was an early correction against treating generic 3D passive memory as absent, not an input to our retraction proof.

Bounded exact-phrase checks for the 2026 code plus check soundness did not identify a controlling theorem already applying this retraction argument. That is **not** a priority/novelty proof. General homological retractions, stabilizer Gaussian elimination, local tree/cycle integration and the stability machinery are established tools. The scoped construction is their explicit norm-qualified composition for this alternating replacement family.
