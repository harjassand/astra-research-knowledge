## Matched comparator and the real limit

The ordinary comparator is allowed the exact same primitive, rational coefficients, known graph and Pauli controls. It can use Makur–Polyanskiy's Hessian comparison, standard positive-pencil functional calculus, graph spectral sparsification, matrix Poincare tools, and inverse perturbation bounds. It can reproduce the classical design and the scalar/binary pieces. No claim that a generic reference SDP or a support-blind method is the strongest baseline is made. A source-aware implementation of the SAME analytic decomposition may reproduce the entire computation; no speed advantage over it has been established.

For the actual selected-Pauli binary task, the strongest SAME-control ordinary route is even simpler: directly measure that Pauli. For rho_+/rho_-=(I±W_1/2)/d it succeeds with probability3/4, compared with approximately0.5069 for the deliberately noisy graph measurements in the q=2 family. Thus this construction is NOT a better predictor or sensor. The guessing reversal is a rigorous obstruction to interpreting entropy order as universal predictive capability.

The positive scalable result is instead a new derived DESIGN GUARANTEE at its exact mathematical scope: synthesize a strict, reference-uniform relative-information relation, including a global erasure and f-independent budget, from finite local graph data and a well-conditioned primitive. It produces a family in arbitrarily large dimensions with a constant guarantee and linear readout-description size. It is not another supplied covariance/moment fixture or a bounded-reference hierarchy.

Single-use implementation can be procedural with O(q) local measurement/arithmetic work; an explicit output table has O(d^2) size, exponential in q. Label support size must not be confused with a proved exponential per-shot control cost. Conversely, no calibration, error model, device, source preparation, useful target objective, sample count, or downstream advantage has been acquired. The primitive's absolute information may shrink with dimension, and independent flags/retention are not a generic block compression gain.

**Decision:** preserve the theorems, exact compiler and the scalable thinning construction as reusable foundational capital. World priority remains UNKNOWN. A consequential practical or civilization-level capability is NOT_ESTABLISHED. Do not allocate further work merely to widening the tiny qubit ball or increasing a finite example's dimension; the next useful question must attach a real objective whose value is not already delivered by the common primitive/direct task measurement.
