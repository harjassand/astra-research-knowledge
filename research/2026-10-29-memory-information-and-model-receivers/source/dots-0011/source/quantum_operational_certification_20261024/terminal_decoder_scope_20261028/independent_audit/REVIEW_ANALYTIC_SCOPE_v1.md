# Independent review of the bare Clifford decoder obstruction

28 October 2026. The analytic family classification and depolarizing obstruction are accepted at their restricted scope. The six noisy terminal-state diagnostic grids are not independently replayed by this receipt and remain author diagnostics.

A physical Pauli whose compression to the code is a nonzero logical Pauli must commute with every stabilizer: anticommutation would force the compression to vanish. Each output X or Z pullback of a Clifford/discard decoder with the intended logical action is therefore its logical normalizer times a signed stabilizer. The independent choices give 256 distinct reduced maps. Each pair anticommutes and generates a unital matrix-* algebra embedding, so its Schrödinger adjoint is CPTP. Symplectic extension supplies a Clifford realization. This class excludes nonlinear classical syndrome correction and arbitrary non-Clifford syndrome-decoding unitaries.

Every nonidentity Pauli output representative anticommutes with some single-site Pauli error. Hence no member of this class corrects all weight-one errors, despite each decoding an exact codeword correctly. The explicit syndrome-decoding unitary target and measured-syndrome circuit are not contradicted: the former need not be Clifford, and the latter includes outcome-dependent classical corrections.

The independent checker rebuilds stabilizers using binary symplectic vectors from the four literal generators, not the author's decoder routine. For each of 256 pairs, it enumerates all 1024 physical Pauli errors, counts those inducing logical identity by weight, and verifies the ENTIRE rational polynomial against [1+q^wx+q^wy+q^wz]/4, q=1−4p/3. Thus this check is stronger than matching five numerical/rational p values. The first-order coefficient histogram is 3:70, 11/3:90, 13/3:90, 5:6. Every representative weight is at least three.

The [[n,1,d]] extension follows directly from the definition of stabilizer-code distance: each nontrivial logical Pauli has weight at least d. For q in [0,1], q^wj<=q^d. The exact coefficient is sum_j wj/3>=d. The manuscript appropriately restricts the monotone inequality to p<=3/4 and does not claim the same comparison for negative q. The full ideal recovery's infidelity is bounded by the probability of at least two errors, at most 10p² for five independent sites; it is not an equality assertion.

This receives a standard-code interface obstruction, not historical novelty, an unrestricted decoder lower bound, a hardware threshold or a measured performance advantage. Choosing the best ideal extension from source-informed numerical data is diagnostic and is not an acquired decoder. A noisy compiled decoder is a different physical map and must be assessed separately.
