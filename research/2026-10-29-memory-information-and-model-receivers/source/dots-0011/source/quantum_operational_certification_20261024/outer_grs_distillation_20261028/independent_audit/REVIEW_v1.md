## Governing arguments checked

1. Write w_i as the reciprocal derivative of the evaluation-point polynomial, and v_i as its unique characteristic-two square root. The interpolation leading-coefficient identity gives weighted Vandermonde orthogonality through degree B-2. Hence H=C-perp and H is contained in C. Full Vandermonde rank gives the stated dimensions. C has minimum distance 2t+1; H has strictly larger distance in the positive-dimension regime, so the CSS distance is exactly 2t+1.
2. For H y=syndrome, y differs from an error of weight <=t by a codeword. The monic-degree-t Berlekamp-Welch locator can pad the actual locator. Any solution has Q-Ef of degree <B-t and at least B-w roots, so Q=Ef. Thus choosing free linear variables deterministically is safe even with fewer than t errors. Polynomial division and mismatch/syndrome validation make failure a total fallback branch rather than implicit postselection.
3. With row coordinates, Lin(T) maps x to xT. Decoder Pauli labels before Fourier are eT^-1 and fT^T. Fourier on the first s=2t coordinates swaps X/Z labels. Measured first-coordinate differences equal Hf, and last-coordinate differences yield He after multiplication by HR^T. Correction of the middle logical coordinates therefore removes every <=t-support error. Alice uses the complex conjugate decoder; these particular binary gates are real. No recursive-code or exhaustive-search decoder is silently assumed.
4. The field trace is essential: binary phase bits are trace-Gram times the field Z labels. Independent gate propagation tested this rather than substituting ordinary binary dot products. Saved elementary CNOT/SWAP circuits and every small-field Fourier amplitude sign were independently checked.
5. Whole-block bad indicators, not individual stripe or microscopic Pauli independence, control the binomial tail. Productness of complete inner blocks (including independent randomization where needed) is necessary. Marginal fidelity alone does not give this tail. Any inner rejection or variable-yield branch must already be included in a fixed payload CPTP inner protocol.
6. Twirl removal: for the full deterministic reduced-output protocol T, E=T*(Phi_out) obeys 0<=E<=I. Every corrected Bell basis vector has expectation one, so positivity forces E to be identity on their entire span, with zero cross terms. Consequently E>=P_good for arbitrary coherent input. For product blocks, the virtual Bell-projector outcomes are independent Bernoulli indicators. This proves the same fidelity tail without physical twirling. For coherent output the corresponding half trace distance is bounded by sqrt(tail), not automatically tail. The original genuinely Bell-diagonal output can have the stronger tail trace bound. Neither statement certifies an ideal joint transcript.

## Resources and limitations

The stripe construction avoids an uncharged exponentially large field search at h=m. Since 2^h<=2B in its nontrivial regime, trial polynomial construction is polynomial in B; per-side Clifford work is O(B^2 m log B), with actual syndrome measurement, classical forward communication and polynomial BW solves. Finite illustrative gate counts are not hardware timings or a noisy implementation. Inner acquisition/preparation cost is a separate dependency, not erased by this wrapper.

To take B to infinity one needs m>=ceil(log2 B); a fixed m with a single q=2^m code cannot support unbounded B. The intended m proportional to inner block length and B proportional to that length meets this requirement. If the parent inner claim applies only to contamination shrinking as 1/k, the composition remains a shrinking-contamination theorem, not correction of a fixed extensive noise density. Conventional concentration plus this same outer code is an equally relevant comparator. No historical novelty, superior equal-resource advantage, unknown-noise learning, laboratory robustness, fault-tolerance threshold or inner Schur theorem is accepted by this receipt.
