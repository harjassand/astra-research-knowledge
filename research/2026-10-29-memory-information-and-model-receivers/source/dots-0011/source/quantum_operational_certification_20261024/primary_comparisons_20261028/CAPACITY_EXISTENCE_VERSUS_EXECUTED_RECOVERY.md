The established method bounds one-shot quantum capacity from two complementary preparation/measurement bases without assuming independent noise. A second protocol intersperses tests and data. Its guarantee concerns capacity, with explicit typicality/abort qualifications, not an acquired executable near-optimal decoder. Therefore “no environment reconstruction” and “correlated-noise testing” alone are not new capabilities of the Astra construction.

Our continuing scientific obligation is different: acquire, compile and execute the physical encoder/recovery, with real boundaries, time and noisy controls. Existing operational certificates must not be compared against mandatory full process tomography as though it were the strongest alternative. The paper's capacity bound has NOT been imported into the present simulation; its full hypotheses and supplementary proof would need checking for that transfer. QKD/entanglement-distillation methods are further strong alternatives when retained entangled halves, local controls and a classical link are available.
