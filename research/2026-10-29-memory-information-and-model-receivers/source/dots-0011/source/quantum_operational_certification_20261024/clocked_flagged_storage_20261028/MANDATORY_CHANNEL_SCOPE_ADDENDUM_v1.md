# Mandatory-channel / no-bypass scope addendum

28 October2026. This narrows the physical interpretation of the ideal-idle and clocked numerical constructions; original numerical sources remain intact. No calculation changes.

The declared native amplitude-damping/frame channel acts on the five data ports, while the two local EC ancillas only receive their specified local control/background noise. Consequently the results do **not** establish a general passive-memory lifetime advantage: with seven freely interchangeable storage qubits, an equally resourced strategy might simply place the unknown state in a less-noisy ancilla, bypassing the mandatory native channel altogether.

The physically clean contract here is instead **repeated mandatory channel/link uses with local correction intervals**:

- The protected quantum information must traverse the stated native link/noise region at every required use.
- The coded strategy sends five carriers through its five declared ports; the single-carrier comparison sends one carrier through a declared port.
- Local EC ancillas are fresh processor resources and cannot transport the quantum information around the required link. No pre-shared entanglement or noiseless quantum side channel provides a bypass.
- Local correction intervals and their data/ancilla background exposure are charged by the explicit clock. Intermediate data operators remain physical across the full sequence; there is no ideal intermediate decode/re-encode.

One can interpret stations as receivers between successive mandatory link segments. This is a declared communication/processing task, not a proof that a given laboratory device enforces the no-bypass condition. An actual passive-memory claim would require noise/time contracts for **every eligible storage register**, and comparisons allowed to choose freely among those registers and strategies.

The single-carrier full-CPTP terminal-decoder oracle optimizes only its declared endpoint dynamics, not all intermediate controls or extra quantum resources. A separate all-controls ceiling requires its own proof. Equal elapsed time is not equal channel-use count or equal physical-qubit count. With the same seven-qubit resources, an equally informed conventional strategy can implement the very same standard flagged-code pipeline; no new-algorithm superiority follows from these demonstrations.

All prior phrases such as “storage advantage” in author findings must be read at this mandatory-channel/no-bypass scope. No general passive-memory lifetime, arbitrary-storage architecture superiority or environment-free protection theorem is asserted.
