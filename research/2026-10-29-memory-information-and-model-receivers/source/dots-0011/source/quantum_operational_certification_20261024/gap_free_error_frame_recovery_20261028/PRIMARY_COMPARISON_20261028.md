# Primary polar-recovery comparison

Root read Zhao and Xu, *Petz Recovery through a Single Polar Transformation for Approximate Quantum Error Correction*, arXiv2610.20515v1 (14 October2026), https://arxiv.org/html/2610.20515v1 , Introduction and equations1–4, on28 October2026. This recent work explicitly develops single-QSVT polar recovery, total non-postselected implementation, and spectrum-weighted error analysis. Therefore neither implementing recovery through a polar transform nor considering weighted spectral error is claimed new here. Its displayed uniform asymptotic iteration guarantee depends on the smallest nonzero scaled singular value. The present lemma instead bounds direct Bell loss by a source-weighted quadratic Gram defect and pays low singular directions as error; it does not promise diamond approximation to the exact Petz map. These are different targets. The main constructive opening is actual efficient access to the Schur reference frame, rather than assuming a source dilation or a dense matrix oracle. Full historical priority remains unassessed.
