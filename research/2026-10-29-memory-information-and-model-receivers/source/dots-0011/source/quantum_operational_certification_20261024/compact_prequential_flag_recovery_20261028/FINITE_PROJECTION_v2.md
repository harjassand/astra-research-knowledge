# Explicit finite projection slack accounting
2026-10-28. Clarification of CONSTRUCTION_v1 Section 3, not new source data. Candidate pending review.

Let m be the actual number of stored aggregate blocks and K0 the unseen multiplicity when present. Work in the weighted Frobenius norm of Section 3, with weights 1 or 1/K0. Each feasible aggregate block is <=I. The update changes one observed block by a r b W, of operator norm 1/sqrt N<=1. Thus each center Y_i has Frobenius norm <=2 sqrt r.

Suppose the finite rational convex solver produces Hermitian H_i with lambda_min(H_i)>=-delta, ||sum H_i-I||op<=delta, and weighted squared-distance objective <=the true minimum+xi/4. Require delta<=1/(m+1). Then H_i<= [1+(m+1)delta]I<=2I by subtracting the other lower bounds from the sum. The exact repair in Section 3 gives each ||M_i-H_i||F<=9m sqrt r delta. Consequently the weighted tuple difference is at most 9m^(3/2) sqrt r delta. The two objective vectors H-Y and M-Y have ordinary tuple norms at most 4 sqrt(mr) and 3 sqrt(mr), respectively. Their weighted objective difference is therefore at most 63m^2 r delta.

Choose delta<=min{1/(m+1), xi/(512m^2 r)}. Repair then adds less than xi/8. Reserve the rest of the xi allowance for the finite weak convex solve, rounding, and explicitly checked objective certificate. These are known-instance operations, not source acquisition.

To prevent recursive denominator growth, require pre-repair entries on a fixed dyadic grid of mesh u<=delta/(8mr), with Hermitian symmetry. Rounding changes any one operator norm by at most2r u, and the sum residual by at most2mr u<=delta/4. Start the solver at residual delta/2 and reserve an objective rounding allowance using the same bounded-norm inequality; decrease u further if required by that explicit bound. Dependence on the small metric coefficient 1/K0 is paid in bit precision (log K0=O(n)); one must not silently discard it. The known feasible interior center is I/m for the aggregate coordinates. Generic known-center convex quadratic/SDP weak optimization supplies the requested finite precision with rational encoding length polynomial in n,N,r and log(1/xi), provided its paid solver contract holds. The fixed-grid plus fixed-rational repair yields polynomial iterate encoding length without recursive denominator growth.

The mathematical regret bound needs only exact feasibility and the certified xi objective slack; it does not require an exact irrational projection. If a numerical implementation cannot produce that certificate, it must declare a failure/fallback within a predeclared implementation allowance rather than claim the bound from an approximate floating iterate. No such generic projection implementation has been executed by this package; the executed checks are exact rational pruning tests, separate from source experiments.
