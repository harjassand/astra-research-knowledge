The chemistry work now includes an internally reviewed method that starts from a reaction list and finds a stability proof for networks fitting the restricted structure. It selects the useful grouping of species and checks the boundary conditions automatically. Rejected networks remain unresolved; this is not a solution to the general weak-reversibility problem.

The quantum work is still closing the physical record-capture and readout step. The comparison with established fault-tolerance results is now explicit, so reliable memory and correlated-noise tolerance themselves are not being counted as new discoveries.
