A route to finite tolerance: preserve each block’s logical information throughout the gate

I constructed a conditional finite-tolerance architecture around four-electron, total-spin-zero blocks. Its central physical move is to use charge excursions for entangling gates while keeping each block separately in total spin zero. This makes the ideal computation insensitive to an arbitrarily fast, interacting collective magnetic bath, even when neighboring blocks experience different bath operators.

The difficult step was establishing what happens after something has leaked. Protection proved only for an entirely valid register is insufficient. The new charge construction has a stronger property: an ideal entangling gate preserves a valid block’s computational subspace even when its neighbor has leaked. Together with an explicitly supplied teleportation/reset cell, that permits a reduction to established leakage and non-Markovian fault-tolerance results.

This changes the response to the readout-exposure obstruction. We can choose a finite readout duration and fixed control precision, then improve computational accuracy through outer encoding. Increasing the accuracy of every raw measurement indefinitely is no longer the required improvement operation.

There are substantial qualifications. The coherent charge source needs calibration, and the capacitive interaction needs controlled activation or a sufficiently small residual outside its intended gate. I have not established a practical operating point or a numerical device threshold. The new derivations retain working status; the earlier accepted instruments, renewal procedures and correlated-history results remain intact.

9. What is already known, and what this construction adds

The relevant prior work is stronger than an isolated pulse-word comparator:

Prior construction	Established contribution	Comparison here
Decoherence-free universal computation	Encoded control and concatenation with outer correction	Supplies the organizing framework; the present gate retains separate block neutrality through charge excursions.
Fong–Wandzura and reset-if-leaked	Concrete exchange-only gates and leakage reduction	Serious alternatives with their actual initialized ancillas and noise assumptions; no raw-count superiority is claimed.
QUEX	Four-electron singlet encoding, electrical control, preparation/readout and charge sweet spots	Already addresses much of the same physical problem and may offer a better operating point.
Capacitive exchange-only sweet spots and SAGE analysis	Optimized entangling operations and explicit charge/magnetic-noise treatment	Must receive the same acquired information; ordinary optimized control may outperform the supplied unoptimized words.
Cavity-assisted scattering, Barrett–Kok, optical Zeno filtering	Robust or heralded entanglement and fidelity/success tradeoffs	Their actual sources, local operations and retained-memory models should be charged, without imposing an irrelevant long-photon survival penalty.
