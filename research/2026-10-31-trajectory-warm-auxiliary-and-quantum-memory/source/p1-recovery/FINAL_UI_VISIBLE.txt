1. The scientific opening

The question was whether acquired knowledge can change the computation needed to acquire a later capability.

The selected mechanism gives a concrete answer under explicit conditions. A previously learned function can reveal that two apparently different measurements contain the same unknown quantity. If the measurement errors are independent, the learner can combine those measurements to reduce noise. The resulting observations can support acquisition of another executable function.

The important object is therefore an acquired operation for interpreting future observations.
