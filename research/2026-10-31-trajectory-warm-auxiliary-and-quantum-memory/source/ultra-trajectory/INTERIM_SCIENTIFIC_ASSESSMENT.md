## A concrete plausibility check, not acceptance of the proposed lift

There is a valid-looking standard operator route behind the direction. For a finite stationary reversible Markov chain P with stationary pi and a fixed bounded antisymmetric edge function f(i,j)=-f(j,i), |f|<=1, put T_theta(i,j)=P(i,j)exp(theta f(i,j)). Pairing the two directions of each edge in the pi-weighted inner product gives

    |<z,T_theta z>_pi| <= cosh(theta)<|z|,P|z|>_pi
                        <= cosh(theta)||z||_pi^2.

The ordinary numerical-radius power inequality then gives, for the stationary path current S_n,

    E_pi exp(theta S_n)=<1,T_theta^n 1>_pi
                      <= cosh(theta)^n <= exp(n theta^2/2).

Thus fixed-horizon current tails can have a gap-free concentration bound without independent trajectories. This coordinator check uses a known numerical-radius theorem and finite reversible-chain assumptions; it is not a novelty claim or proof that Ultra's particular observed alternating statistic has the required representation. Berger's power inequality is stated in the primary mathematical article https://doi.org/10.1017/S0004972716000514 .

The load-bearing step is the lift: it must genuinely be reversible and stationary under every admitted hidden-equilibrium model, and its antisymmetric current must be computable from the actual record. A shifted-pair chain or an observation-history register is not automatically reversible. Correct sensing/time reversal and any clock-phase conditioning require explicit treatment. A bound for a fixed homogeneous tilted operator does not automatically cover a data-selected critic, products of different tilted operators or arbitrary stopping times.

## Compatibility with existing Astra obstructions

N185's governing one-trace metastability argument, and N186's stationary-start/mixing confidence construction, were read at their relevant proof ranges. N185 bounds uniformly honest substantial quantitative entropy-production lower bounds over an extension-closed hidden class. A stationary reversible-null rejection test is a weaker decision problem. Its existence need not contradict N185, and it must not be promoted to recovering a substantial dissipation rate. Hidden stationarity remains a physical/source assumption; no mixing-time bound does not mean arbitrary initial preparation is free.

N507 and N541 retain a different hard target. N541's governing five-letter witness source was read through its definition and universal inequality. Its enormous independent-window budget is a sufficient bound for that test, not a necessary lower bound for every acquisition method. A new gap-free null test could bypass independent windows for its own statistic while having no useful power on that particular positive-spectrum family. Power, full computation and comparison on the actual alternative therefore remain decisive.

Chikina–Frieze–Pegden already establish significance testing without a mixing bound: https://www.math.cmu.edu/~af1p/Texfiles/outliers.pdf . Its actual null is that a presented state is stationary for a specified reversible chain, with an outlier-rank test along that chain. That is relevant prior machinery, not automatically the same claim as rejecting every unknown hidden reversible explanation from a coarse record. The inspected paper's result cannot be counted as new, and the exact observed-record transfer remains to be earned.
