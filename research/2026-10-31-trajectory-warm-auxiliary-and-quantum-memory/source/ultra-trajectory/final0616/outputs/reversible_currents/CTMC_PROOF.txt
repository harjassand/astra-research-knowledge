AN ACTUAL CONTINUOUS-TIME, ONE-RECORD POWER CONTROL
Solo construction and proof, 31 October 2026.

This file instantiates the no-mixing reflection calibration on an actual
finite CTMC sampled on a known time grid. It supplies a mathematical source
and an exact synthetic sampler. It is not hardware, an acquired biological
record, or a new claim that time-symmetric data can reveal dissipation.
That broad capability is established prior art (Skinner-Dunkel 2021).

1. GENERATOR, OBSERVATION, AND TIME REVERSAL

States are i modulo 16. Put q_{i,i+1}=10, q_{i,i-1}=1, q_{ii}=-11 and
all other rates zero, in fixed inverse-time units. The chain is finite,
irreducible, reciprocal and stationary at pi_i=1/16. All states are even
under the supplied physical reversal. Observe ONLY the binary half-ring
label: Y=0 on {0,...,7} and Y=1 on {8,...,15}.

The stationary entropy-production rate is exactly

                         sigma=9 log(10) nats/time.       (C1)

This follows by pairing every forward flow 10/16 with its reverse 1/16.
It is the path-irreversibility rate of this CTMC; a physical thermodynamic
interpretation still needs its reservoirs, drive and readout model.

Let theta(i)=7-i modulo16. It preserves the observed label and satisfies
theta Q theta=Q^T. Uniform pi is also invariant under theta. Consequently
the complete stationary OBSERVED continuous-time process is reversal
invariant, including every sampled word and observed waiting-time event.
The hidden chain is nevertheless driven under ordinary all-even reversal.
theta is a label-preserving isomorphism to its reversed model, not a
physical odd-variable reversal that is silently substituted into (C1).

Sample at Delta=4/9 time units. The actual sampled kernel is exp(Delta Q).
It is not the earlier non-embeddable/periodic control relabelled as a CTMC.
The calculation below uses the actual generator and its exact semigroup.

2. A RATIONAL CERTIFICATE OF NEGATIVE LAG-TWO CORRELATION

Set f(Y)=+1 for Y=0 and -1 for Y=1. The desired source correlation is

       C2=E[f(Y_0)f(Y_2)]=<f,exp(2Delta Q)f>_pi.

Write R for the nearest-neighbor discrete walk with forward probability
10/11 and backward probability1/11; Q=11(R-I). At time 2Delta, mu=88/9,

 C2=e^-mu sum_{j>=0}(mu^j/j!)<f,R^j f>_pi.                (C2)

Each coefficient is a rational number in [-1,1]. The script computes the
first 101 coefficients by exact convolution on the ring. It encloses e^-mu
between the degree160 and degree161 alternating Taylor sums. The tail
after degree160 is alternating with decreasing magnitudes because
mu/(j+1)<1 there. These sums therefore give a rigorous ordered enclosure,
despite large cancelling terms earlier in the series.

If W=sum_{j=0}^{100}mu^j/j! and S is the corresponding signed correlation
sum, the omitted Poisson probability is at most 1-e_lower*W. Multiplying
S by the exponential interval with the correct sign and adding/subtracting
that tail encloses C2. All operations are rational; final dyadic rounding
is directed outwards. The resulting interval is contained in

                     C2 < -19/50 = -0.38,                (C3)

with decimal preview -0.38870628373067856. Floating matrix exponentiation
was used only to choose a promising source, not to establish (C3).

3. A CHARGED SOURCE DEPENDENCE BOUND FOR POWER ONLY

The Fourier eigenvalues of Q are

  -11(1-cos(2pi j/16))+9i sin(2pi j/16).

The generator is circulant and normal on L2(uniform pi). For P=exp(Delta Q),
on mean-zero functions ||P^k||<=rho^k, where

       rho=exp[-(44/9)(1-cos(pi/8))] < 7/10.              (C4)

Here is a simple exact bound avoiding transcendental root evaluation:
sqrt(2)<17/12, cos(pi/8)^2=(2+sqrt(2))/4<41/48<(37/40)^2.
Thus the exponent's magnitude exceeds 11/30; the rational alternating
series for exp(-11/30) is below7/10. The script verifies these inequalities.

This source-specific spectral information is used ONLY to prove power on
this inspectable model. The test's null calibration and evaluator do not
receive Q, rho, a state count, or a mixing envelope. Claiming uniform
alternative power over unknown hidden chains from (C4) would be invalid.

Let xi_t=f(Y_t)f(Y_{t+2}), T_n=sum_{t=0}^{n-1}xi_t. Its variance is bounded
by nV, V=29/3. To see this, Var(xi)<=1 and the one-overlap covariance is
at most1. For k>=2, condition the first window on X_2 and the second on
X_k. Conditional expectation is an L2 contraction; their centered L2 norms
are at most1. The intervening mean-zero transition operator has norm at
most rho^(k-2), so |Cov(xi_0,xi_k)|<=rho^(k-2). Therefore

 Var(T_n)<=n[1+2+2sum_{k>=2}rho^(k-2)]
          <=n[3+2/(1-7/10)]=(29/3)n.                      (C5)

No independence of overlapping observed windows has been assumed.

                Pr_rev{T_n<=-s}<=exp[-s^2/(8n)].          (C6)

Take n=10,800 windows, hence N=10,801 sampled transitions and 10,802

 Pr_source{T_n> -n delta/2} <=4V/(n delta^2)

The actual evaluator is reflection_trace.py. It accepts a binary record
and alpha, never the source's Q or Delta. At a fixed horizon it also gives

       L_step=4[alpha*A-h(alpha)]_+/N,
       A=((-T_n)_+)^2/(8n),

using an upper rational enclosure on h(alpha). For the actual sampled

Thus L_step/Delta is a (1-alpha)-confidence lower bound on sigma. The

The proof of (C6) is the ordinary reflection comparator. This example

5. SOURCE SAMPLER AND MATCHED CONTROL

The total jump rate is exactly11 in every hidden state. Consecutive
grid-interval microscopic jump counts are therefore iid Poisson(44/9),
and each microscopic direction is +1 with probability10/11. Starting
uniformly on16 states and applying those latent increments generates the
exact sampled CTMC law. Only its binary labels go to the evaluator.

work/ctmc_reflection_control.py samples each Poisson count by inverse CDF.
It uses directed rational enclosures for e^-mu sum_{j<=k}mu^j/j!, and
independent uniform binary prefixes. It returns a count only when the
entire uniform cell lies between consecutive certified CDF bounds. On an
ambiguous boundary it refines both the uniform bits and the exponential
precision; on a far tail it extends the CDF. Almost-sure termination follows
from continuous uniforms and convergent enclosures. Discarding unused
uniform bits after a certified count preserves its law. No finite tail is
silently truncated. A seeded PRNG supplies the synthetic randomness; this
is not a claim of physical random-bit acquisition or formal PRNG fidelity.

The matched reversible control has the SAME16 states, stationary law,
binary labels, total jump rate11, time grid, duration and observation
count, with each direction rate11/2. Its synthetic record is separately
initialized once. The exact-source mechanism has no expensive hidden
generator-fitting stage; the test does not get this advantage as input.
