FROM EQUILIBRIUM TESTS TO REALISED HIDDEN PATH ENTROPY
Solo proof, 31 October 2026. Historical priority unestablished.

1. A DIFFERENT TARGET, WITH AN EXPLICIT REASON

The uniformly honest stationary MEAN-rate endpoint is subject to the
metastable ceiling in PROOF.txt. This file does not withdraw that result.
Its target is the random entropy produced on the PARTICULAR realised
hidden trajectory, including its stationary system-entropy boundary term:

 Sigma_N=log[dP_{0:N}/dP^R_{0:N}](X_{0:N}).               (P1)

P is stationary all-even hidden Markov dynamics; P^R denotes its stationary
path law reversed in time. In discrete time,

 Sigma_N=sum_{t<N}log[dGamma/dGamma^T](X_t,X_{t+1}).

Its expectation is N e(P), but its value is NOT that expectation. In a
stationary finite CTMC over duration T, its expectation is T sigma; local
detailed balance is needed to interpret it as physical total entropy.
It is not medium heat alone when system-boundary entropy is nonzero.

Sigma can be negative. A confidence LOWER endpoint must retain negative
values, or abstain when it fails to certify positivity. Clamping it to
zero would be false: a one-step weakly driven cycle has negative Sigma
with probability arbitrarily close to one half.

2. UNIVERSAL TRANSFER OF ANY EQUILIBRIUM e-VALUE

Suppose E(Y_{0:N})>=0 is fixed and satisfies

        E_rev E <=1 for EVERY stationary reversible hidden
        standard-Borel Markov model of these observations. (P2)

Independent algorithm randomization may be included in both laws. Then
for ANY stationary hidden P at this fixed horizon,

                     E_P[e^{-Sigma_N/2} E]<=1,            (P3)

and consequently

        Pr_P{Sigma_N >= 2 log(alpha E)} >= 1-alpha.        (P4)

The inference algorithm does not need P or pi to evaluate the right side.
Its physical assumptions and the fixed/charged selection of E still matter.

Proof. Let K have stationary flow dH=sqrt(dGamma dGamma^T), using any
common dominating measure. H is symmetric. Its marginals are at most pi,
by conditional Cauchy-Schwarz on P and P*. Hence K is a sub-Markov kernel.
Complete it by self-loops:

           Kbar(x,dy)=K(x,dy)+(1-K1(x))delta_x(dy).

Kbar is an ACTUAL reversible Markov kernel with invariant pi. It is only
a mathematical reference, not a proposed physical model or an embedding.
The geometric mean of forward and reversed N-step path measures is exactly
the path submeasure of K started at pi. Pathwise K<=Kbar and E>=0 give

 E_P[e^{-Sigma_N/2} E] = int E sqrt(dP_path dP_path^R)
                      <= E_{pi,Kbar} E <=1.

Markov's inequality proves (P4). Extended infinite Sigma is harmless;
singular forward mass has e^{-Sigma/2}=0. Negative infinity occurs on
no positive forward mass. No mixing, hidden-count, ergodicity or repeated
stationary starts have entered this argument.

The same conclusion holds for the FULL hidden continuous-time trajectory
when E uses a time-grid record. Conditional Holder/Cauchy-Schwarz on the
fibers of the full-path -> hidden-grid map gives

 pushforward[sqrt(dP_full dP_full^R)]
                    <=sqrt(dP_grid dP_grid^R).

Apply the preceding domination to the stationary sampled kernel. Thus
(P4) bounds actual full-path stochastic entropy, not merely an assumed
discrete surrogate for it. This is a probability statement; it does not
replace an unacquired thermodynamic model or detector specification.

3. e-VALUES ALREADY SUPPLIED BY THE FINITE-DUAL DECOMPOSITION

For a frozen predictive dual, PREDICTIVE_DUAL_PROOF.txt gives
T_n>=Z-R/2, E_rev exp(lambda Z)<=exp(n lambda^2 C^2/2).
Therefore for any fixed lambda>=0,

  E_dual=exp[-lambda T_n-lambda R/2-n lambda^2 C^2/2]      (P5)

satisfies (P2). Its path entropy lower endpoint is 2[log E_dual+log alpha].
For ordinary binary lag-two reflection, omit the endpoint and use C=2:

                 E_ref=exp[-lambda T_n-2n lambda^2].       (P6)

These are fixed-horizon e-values, not proved supermartingales. Tuning
lambda after seeing the trace requires a predetermined mixture or an
explicit family penalty. Repeated horizons require alpha spending unless
a separate conditional supermartingale theorem has been established.

On the actual 16-state CTMC record in CTMC_PROOF.txt, lambda=1/10 is
fixed before data. T_n=-4176,n=10800 give log E_ref=1008/5=201.6.
At alpha=1/40, (P4) certifies at least

                  2(201.6-log40)=395.822241... nats

of realised full hidden path entropy, with 97.5% confidence uniformly
over the stated hidden model class. This is a random PATH-TOTAL target,
not a lower confidence bound of 395.8/T on the stationary mean rate.
The mean-rate endpoint in CTMC_PROOF.txt remains separately valid.

4. A SHARP UNIVERSAL ENTROPY-CURRENT EXPONENTIAL BOUND

For sharper bounds take an antisymmetric current |g|<=1, including any
reversible observer cover of PROOF.txt. Fix 0<theta<=1/2, c=1-2theta,
lambda>=0. Define

 C(theta,lambda)=sup_{u real} cosh(cu+lambda)/cosh u.

Then for arbitrary stationary P,

             E_P exp[-theta Sigma_N+lambda S_N]
                                  <=C(theta,lambda)^N.    (P7)

Proof. The positive tilted kernel has stationary flow

         dGamma^{1-theta} d(Gamma^T)^theta e^{lambda g}.

Without the current tilt both its row and column masses are bounded by
pi, by Holder. Hence it is an L2 contraction; the current tilt is bounded
by e^{|lambda|}, so the kernel is a bounded operator. Put
eta=(Gamma+Gamma^T)/2, t=(dGamma-dGamma^T)/(dGamma+dGamma^T), u=atanh t.
For any complex z, take absolute values inside its quadratic form and
average an edge with its transpose. The integrand multiplier becomes

       sqrt(1-t^2) cosh(c atanh t+lambda g)
                             <=C(theta,lambda).

Since eta has marginals pi, 2|z(x)z(y)|<=|z(x)|^2+|z(y)|^2 bounds the
quadratic form by C||z||^2. Thus its numerical radius is <=C. The
classical power inequality proved in PROOF.txt gives (P7), since the
path exponential moment is <1,K^N1>. Boundary densities follow by limits.
The bound is sharp on homogeneous biased cycles with a suitable bias:
their directional increments are iid and attain the maximizing u.

Applying (P7) at both signs of lambda yields

    E_P[e^{-theta Sigma_N} cosh(lambda S_N)/C^N]<=1.

For a binary alternating cover, cosh(lambda S_N) depends only on the
observed absolute score. No virtual seed must be physically generated.
Markov therefore gives the fixed-horizon confidence endpoint

  Sigma_N >= [log cosh(lambda S_N)-N log C+log alpha]/theta. (P8)

For full CTMC trajectories, conditional Holder on each hidden-grid fiber
gives pushforward[P_full^{1-theta}(P_full^R)^theta]
<=P_grid^{1-theta}(P_grid^R)^theta. Thus (P7)-(P8) also bound the
ACTUAL full CTMC path entropy when S uses sampled observations.

5. CLOSED TUNING AND CERTIFIED EVALUATION

An unbounded numerical maximization is unnecessary. Choose a fixed rational
v in [0,1), and set

 lambda=atanh v-c atanh(cv),
 log C=(1/2)log[(1-c^2 v^2)/(1-v^2)].                     (P9)

These exactly match the supremum: its log derivative is
c tanh(cu+lambda)-tanh u, which vanishes at u=atanh(cv).
Uniqueness follows because atanh(t/c)-c atanh t increases strictly from
zero to infinity for t=tanh u in [0,c); its derivative is
c(1-c^2)/[(c^2-t^2)(1-t^2)]>0 with respect to t. For c=0 the
maximum is at u=0 directly.
Negative u cannot improve the maximum when c,lambda>=0. Above atanh c
the derivative is negative. The value (P9) follows by cosh ratios.

path_entropy.py encloses these logs using the rational series already
checked in currents.py. It conservatively substitutes
log cosh(lambda S)>=lambda|S|-log2, LOWER lambda times |S|, and UPPER
log C. Its 96-bit rational endpoint is rounded DOWN, including negative
values. It implements a predeclared 42-member theta,v grid with alpha/42;
maximizing those endpoints is valid same-record selection. True source
parameters are not inputs to that grid evaluator.

6. FULL-RATE PATH RECOVERY ON THE SHARP FOUR-STATE SOURCE

For the earlier source P_b, the actual hidden entropy increments are
eta_t log[(1+b)/(1-b)], where eta_t are iid signs with mean b. The
observed absolute current satisfies |S_N|/N->b, and
Sigma_N/N->phi(b)=2b atanh b almost surely.

As theta decreases to zero, with v fixed in (0,1), (P9) gives

 lambda/theta -> phi'(v)=2atanh v+2v/(1-v^2),
 log C/theta -> 2v^2/(1-v^2)=phi*(phi'(v)).                (P10)

Choose a deterministic rational theta_N->0 with N theta_N/log N->infinity,
for example theta_N=1/[2ceil(N^(1/4))]. Use a predetermined dense rational
library v_j with weights w_j=2^-j, evaluate its first J_N->infinity entries,
and allocate alpha_{j,N}=alpha w_j/[N(N+1)]. The union bound makes the
maximal endpoint valid for Sigma_N SIMULTANEOUSLY at every N with risk
at most alpha. Its cost includes all these parameter evaluations and their
penalties; it is a mathematical procedure, not an automation request.

For each fixed j, (P8) divided by N tends to
phi'(v_j)b-phi*(phi'(v_j)). The confidence/log2 penalties vanish since
N theta_N dominates log N. The supremum over a dense library is phi(b),
so liminf of the selected endpoint/N is at least phi(b).

For the upper limit, let h=lambda/theta. Convexity of log cosh gives

 log C >= theta sup_u[(h-2u)tanh u]=theta phi*(h).

Thus EVERY candidate endpoint/N is <=phi(|S_N|/N), after dropping its
nonpositive confidence/log2 penalties. Eventually |S_N|/N stays away
from1 and phi is continuous. Hence limsup<=phi(b). The selected lower
endpoint/N therefore converges almost surely to the source's exact rate,
while remaining a uniformly honest bound on REALISED path entropy over
the entire stationary hidden model class. It is not uniformly honest for
the global mean rate. Extra hidden entropy invisible to the observations
is not recovered in arbitrary other models.

The source's observed law is exactly time-reversal invariant, so this
construction does not estimate a nonzero visible forward/backward KL.
For a finite v-library with the stated decreasing theta schedule, the
limit is the corresponding supported Fenchel bound. Exact source-family
recovery uses a dense library. The implemented sequential_current_bound
uses the dyadic order 1/2,1/4,3/4,1/8,3/8,..., weights 2^-j,
J_N=bit_length(N), and theta_N=1/[2ceil(N^(1/4))]. Thus it is an actual
finite computation at each horizon and implements the limit construction.
The separate 42-member fixed-horizon driver has fixed theta values and
should not be presented as that time-uniform algorithm.

Numerical correction: retaining a fixed logarithm enclosure width is NOT
sufficient to implement the exact limit as theta_N->0. Cancellation in
lambda followed by division by theta_N can amplify that error. The initial
fixed 40-term version therefore did not justify an exact implemented-limit
claim. The final sequential API uses 40+bit_length(N) series terms. On the
reduced interval the positive-series remainder is O(9^-terms), with the
power-of-two exponent factor explicitly included. For every fixed library
entry this error/theta_N tends to zero, as does the normalized log-alpha
error. Final 96-bit directed rounding contributes at most 2^-96/N per step.
These facts transfer the analytic limit to the implemented sequence.

Finite power is also certified, not only a limit or a single good draw.
For the four-state b=1/2 source, let M=sum eta_t and N=6000. Two-sided
Hoeffding gives

 Pr{|M-3000|>230}<=2exp(-529/120)<1/40.

On M in [2770,3230], actual Sigma=M log3 and |S|=M. With total current-
grid risk 1/120 and alpha divided by all 42 candidates, every candidate's
unrounded lower formula is affine in M. Exact rational log enclosures
check at BOTH endpoints that all 42 lie below M log3, and that the fixed
theta=1/16,v=1/2 candidate exceeds (3/4)M log3. The downward rounding
unit is charged in both checks. Affinity implies the same inequalities
throughout the interval. Hence the selected current-grid endpoint lies
between 75% and 100% of actual realised entropy with probability >=39/40,
on ONE stationary 6000-step trajectory of this source. This source-specific
power theorem is separate from its uniform all-hidden-model coverage.

The saved driven draw certified 3015.865903... nats against actual
3350.767480... nats, about 90.005%. Its three-method comparison splits
overall alpha=1/40 among current grid, reflection and predictive dual,
so selecting a maximum is charged. The additional reversible control
has actual entropy 0 and a negative endpoint, retained as required.
Both are synthetic records, each with one stationary preparation and
6000 transitions; their actual hidden entropy goes ONLY to the verifier.

The score scanner accepts an arbitrary iterable and retains one bit, a
count and a signed score. The from_score APIs reuse these statistics,
so the dense-library endpoint costs one pass plus O(log N) scalar
parameter evaluations per queried horizon, not O(N log N) rescanning.
Rational-log bit costs and parameter sizes remain additional. The JSON
convenience CLI reads O(N) observations into memory; this batch loader
is not itself an O(log N)-memory streaming interface. The iterable scanner
and from_score APIs provide that interface without storing the raw trace.

7. WHY THIS DOES NOT EVADE N185

In the dormant-copy construction, every active internal edge's forward/
reverse flow ratio is EXACTLY the base ratio; bridges have ratio1. On
an active no-bridge trajectory, Sigma'_N=Sigma_{A,N}, despite the global
stationary mean rate e'=w(1-delta)e_A. A large local path-entropy
certificate on that trajectory is compatible with a tiny ensemble mean.
Substituting its value divided by N as an honest GLOBAL-rate confidence
endpoint would be false and would contradict the proved ceiling.

The intended operational conclusion is finite-confidence lower bounds
on realised entropy along one stationary observation record. Keeping that
target explicit is load-bearing, not an optional interpretation.

8. PRIOR WORK AND LIMITS

Entropy-production martingales and single-trajectory thermodynamic inference
are established subjects. Examples include Neri, Roldan, Julicher,
Phys. Rev. X7,011019 (2017), https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.7.011019,
and Nadal-Rosa, Manzano (2025), https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08818 . Their
existence precludes a broad claim to first single-trajectory entropy
inference. The statements (P3)-(P10) above are supplied with their own
proofs; priority of this exact hidden-observation transfer and sharp
current specialization remains unestablished. They are not justified
by citing an unrelated martingale or applying Ville to fixed-time mgfs.
The v2 primary text, Appendix B around (48)-(50), explicitly discusses
the exponential entropy moments generally needed by excursion bounds
and the motor/efficiency conditions used to simplify them. Here the
current normalizer C is bounded universally without those entropy moments
or the hidden generator. This is a difference in supplied interfaces,
not a demonstrated numerical advantage over their motor experiment.

No physical experiment, detector calibration, reservoir realization or
thermodynamic device is supplied. Source samplers, proof arithmetic and
receipts are mathematical/implementation evidence at that scope only.
