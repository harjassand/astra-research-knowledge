REVERSIBLE OBSERVER COVERS, ONE-TRACE CURRENT TESTS, AND A RATE CEILING
Solo derivation and construction, 31 October 2026.

1. CONTRACT

X is a stationary discrete-time Markov chain on a standard-Borel space,
with invariant law pi and transition P. All variables are EVEN under the
specified reversal. Write Gamma(dx,dy)=pi(dx)P(x,dy), Gamma^T(dx,dy)=
Gamma(dy,dx), P* for the stationary reverse kernel, and

                         e(P)=D(Gamma||Gamma^T).

This is nats per discrete step. It is not device heat without a supplied
thermodynamic model. Reducibility and periodicity are permitted; no hidden
state count, mixing time, spectral gap or independent repeats are supplied.
Hidden stationary initialization is an ASSUMPTION and an acquisition cost.
It cannot be verified solely by running the evaluator on an observed trace.

The finite-valued deterministic observation Y_t=a(X_t) is available at each
step. The core binary evaluator uses only that record. Power computations
below use a fully specified mathematical source; those parameters are NOT
inputs to the null test. A future continuous-time claim must charge the
sampling interval and its physical implementation separately.

2. WHICH DETERMINISTIC OBSERVER UPDATES PRESERVE EQUILIBRIUM?

For every observed label a let C_a be a finite set of the same size m,
and let T_ab:C_a->C_b be bijections satisfying

                         T_ba=T_ab^{-1}.                    (1)

In particular T_aa is an involution. Start C_0 uniformly on the fiber,
independently of X, and update C_{t+1}=T_{Y_t,Y_{t+1}} C_t. The lifted
chain on (x,c) has kernel

          P~((x,c),d(y,d))=P(x,dy)1_{d=T_ab(c)}.

Its stationary law is pi(dx)/m: incoming transport maps a uniform fiber
to a uniform fiber for every x->y. The reverse of the lifted edge is
exactly the lift of the reverse microscopic edge, by (1). Consequently

       P reversible => P~ reversible,       e(P~)=e(P).    (2)

For the entropy identity each microscopic directed edge is copied m times,
each with flow divided by m and with exactly the original forward/reverse
Radon-Nikodym ratio. This also proves the extended-value identity. Averaging
P~ with its stationary reverse is the lift of P_s=(P+P*)/2.

A function g((x,c),(y,d)) that depends only on the observed labels/fibers
is available computationally. If |g|<=1 and g(z,z')=-g(z',z), its sum is
an antisymmetric current in the actual reversible lift under the null.
There is no physical intervention, hidden-state sampler or thermodynamic
observer device in this mathematical construction.

The inverse rule matters. A constant equilibrium record with observer
c->c+1 modulo THREE is stationary after uniform seeding, but its lift is
a directed cycle, not reversible. It produces current +n with certainty
and would falsely reject. This update fails (1). Stationarity of an
arbitrary finite-memory observer is insufficient.

3. A GAP-FREE CURRENT EXPONENTIAL BOUND

Theorem. For ANY stationary reversible Markov P and measurable antisymmetric
g bounded by one, S_n=sum_{t=0}^{n-1}g(X_t,X_{t+1}) satisfies

                   E exp(lambda S_n)<=(cosh lambda)^n       (3)

for every real lambda. This theorem needs no ergodicity or spectral gap.

Proof. On complex L2(pi) let

       (K_lambda z)(x)=int P(x,dy)e^{lambda g(x,y)}z(y).

This is bounded: pointwise domination by e^{|lambda|}P|z| and contraction
of P on L2 give its boundedness. For every complex z,

 |<z,K_lambda z>| <= int |z(x)||z(y)|e^{lambda g(x,y)}dGamma
   =int |z(x)||z(y)|cosh(lambda g(x,y))dGamma
   <=cosh(lambda) int |z(x)||z(y)|dGamma
   <=cosh(lambda)||z||_2^2.

The equality uses Gamma=Gamma^T and antisymmetry. The final inequality
follows from 2|z(x)z(y)|<=|z(x)|^2+|z(y)|^2 and equal marginals. Hence
the numerical radius w(K_lambda)<=cosh(lambda).

For completeness here is the CLASSICAL numerical-radius power inequality,
usually attributed to Berger, with Pearcy's elementary root-of-unity proof.
If w(A)<=1, fix n, a phase theta, B=e^{i theta}A, nth roots of unity omega_j,
and x_j=sum_{k=0}^{n-1}omega_j^k B^k x. Then

 (I-omega_j B)x_j=(I-B^n)x,       sum_j x_j=n x.

Every Re<x_j,(I-omega_j B)x_j> is nonnegative, since w(B)<=1. Summing
gives n Re<x,(I-B^n)x>>=0. Vary theta; thus |<x,A^n x>|<=||x||^2.
Scaling by any number greater than w(A) and taking a limit proves

                             w(A^n)<=w(A)^n.               (4)

There is no normality assumption. In particular the stationary mgf is a
QUADRATIC form, so

 E exp(lambda S_n)=<1,K_lambda^n1><=w(K_lambda^n)
                                      <=(cosh lambda)^n.

Using ||K^n||<=2w(K)^n would unnecessarily lose a factor two. (3) is
sharp: the uniform reversible walk on a three-cycle, with each clockwise
edge assigned +1 and anticlockwise edge -1, has iid fair sign increments.
Thus its mgf equals (cosh lambda)^n. This does not imply that every
individual tail of every reversible current equals a binomial tail.

Optimizing Chernoff and using a union bound gives

 Pr{|S_n|>=nt}<=min(1,2exp[-n I(t)]),  0<=t<=1,
 I(t)=[(1+t)log(1+t)+(1-t)log(1-t)]/2.                    (5)

The factor two here accounts for TWO signs. It is not a numerical-radius
loss. Endpoints use 0 log 0=0.

4. BINARY COVER AND AN EXACT RATIONAL TEST

Take a fiber h in {-1,+1} and flip it on EVERY step: T_ab(h)=-h.
For any fixed symmetric k(a,b)=k(b,a) in [-1,1], set

                g((x,h),(y,-h))=h*k(a(x),a(y)).

It is antisymmetric. For binary f=+/-1 and k(a,b)=f(a)f(b),

          S_n=h_0 sum_{t=0}^{n-1}(-1)^t f(Y_t)f(Y_{t+1}). (6)

Its absolute value is independent of h_0. Thus the two-sided test needs
NO random coin. The proof uses a uniform virtual h_0 to make the lift
stationary. One must not assert the signed version of (3) for a fixed
h_0: on a constant record the signed S_1 is +1.

For integer score s=|S_n| with n-s even, let k=(n+s)/2. Then the exact
rational conservative p-value is

     q_n(s)=min(1, 2*n^n/[2^n*k^k*(n-k)^(n-k)]),           (7)

with 0^0=1. It is the bound (5), not a binomial tail calculation under
an unknown null. Rejection when q_n<=alpha has null probability <=alpha
because q_n is decreasing and this is a nested tail test.

currents.py evaluates (6)-(7) using exact integers/Fractions. It returns
a conservative rational lower rate endpoint from Section 6. It never
uses the source's hidden transition matrix or a mixing estimate.

5. A STRONG ORDINARY MARTINGALE COMPARATOR

Let b(x)=E[g(x,X_1)|X_0=x], so |b|<=1. Under stationary reversibility,

 M=sum_t[g(X_t,X_{t+1})-b(X_t)],
 N=sum_t[g(X_t,X_{t+1})+b(X_{t+1})]

are forward and backward martingale sums. Their increments have conditional
range at most two. Also M+N=2S_n+b(X_n)-b(X_0). Conditional Hoeffding,
Cauchy-Schwarz, and |b(X_0)-b(X_n)|/2<=1 give

 E exp(lambda S_n)<=exp(|lambda|+n lambda^2/2),
 Pr{|S_n|>=s}<=min(1,2exp[-((s-1)_+)^2/(2n)]).            (8)

This is an ordinary forward/backward (Lyons-Zheng style) argument. Such
martingale ideas and no-mixing Markov significance tests are prior art.
The controlled comparison below uses this strong, same-information
baseline; it does not compare (7) only with an iid method inapplicable
to a dependent record.

6. A FIXED-HORIZON LOWER CONFIDENCE BOUND ON THE STATIONARY RATE

Put eta=(Gamma+Gamma^T)/2 and J=D(Gamma||eta). For the reversible
symmetrization P_s=(P+P*)/2, the full n-step path divergence is n J.
The following useful sharp near-equilibrium inequality holds:

                              e(P)>=4J.                    (9)

To prove it, set t=(dGamma-dGamma^T)/(dGamma+dGamma^T) in [-1,1].
Symmetry gives e=E_eta[2t atanh t] and J=E_eta[I(t)]. For t>=0,
the derivative of 2t atanh t-4I(t) is

             2[t/(1-t^2)-atanh t]>=0;

the bracket is zero at zero and has derivative 2t^2/(1-t^2)^2>=0.
Evenness and the zero value at t=0 prove (9); boundary/infinite cases
follow by limits. Both sides have ratio tending to equality for small t.

For A_s={|S_n|>=s}, let p=P(A_s), q=P_s(A_s). The lift in Section 2
preserves J as well as e; observed or computational coarsening decreases
path KL. Thus

                    kl(p||q)<=nJ<=n e(P)/4.               (10)

The reversible reference satisfies q<=q_n(s). For fixed alpha define

 L_n(s)=0                                  if q_n(s)>=alpha,
 L_n(s)=4 kl(alpha||q_n(s))/n                 otherwise.    (11)

Then, uniformly over ALL stationary hidden Markov P in Section 1,

                       Pr_P{L_n(|S_n|)<=e(P)}>=1-alpha.    (12)

Proof. The endpoint increases with s. If any attainable score threshold
has L_n(s)>e, its tail event must have p<alpha. Otherwise p>=alpha>
q_n(s)>=q implies kl(p||q)>=kl(alpha||q_n(s))>ne/4, contradicting
(10). The event {L_n>e} is the tail at its least attainable threshold,
so it has probability <alpha. This proof uses no estimate of a hidden
mixing coefficient or a stationary marginal.

currents.py encloses the logarithms by an explicit rational atanh series,
reducing each argument by powers of two to [1,2]. Its positive remainder
bound is 2z^(2m+1)/[(2m+1)(1-z^2)], z=(x-1)/(x+1). Directed dyadic
rounding gives a small lower rational certificate below (11). The upper
interval endpoint is an enclosure, not a confidence lower bound.

This is a FIXED n theorem. The evaluator is not a likelihood martingale,
and Ville's inequality cannot be applied to it. Predetermined alpha
spending over chosen horizons/fixed tests gives an anytime guarantee.
No automatic background task is created by this mathematical observation.

7. WHY HIGH-POWER FULL-RATE INFERENCE IS STILL IMPOSSIBLE

The governing Astra N185 proof establishes this obstruction for finite
CTMCs. Here is its discrete-time dormant-copy transfer with its own
stationarity and cost calculation, not an unproved change of time model.

Take a finite irreducible reciprocal-support base P_A with stationary pi
and e_A>0. Add a dormant copy B_i of every active state A_i with the SAME
visible label. Assign stationary weights w*pi_i and (1-w)*pi_i. For
epsilon>0 small enough put delta=epsilon(1-w)/w and define

 A_i->A_j=(1-delta)P_A(i,j),     A_i->B_i=delta,
 B_i->A_i=epsilon,              B_i->B_i=1-epsilon.         (13)

Rows sum to one. Active incoming internal mass is w(1-delta)pi_i, and
the bridge supplies (1-w)epsilon*pi_i=w delta*pi_i. Dormant stationarity
is equally direct. Bridges are detailed-balanced, so

                        e'=w(1-delta)e_A.                 (14)

Each positive epsilon gives an admissible irreducible reciprocal chain.
Its visible one-time stationary marginal is EXACTLY that of the base.
The active/no-bridge path submeasure is exactly

                         w(1-delta)^n P_A.                (15)

For any procedure with uniform coverage (12), choose w<c<1. On the event
L_n>=c e_A the extended model has a coverage failure, since e'<c e_A.
Consequently w(1-delta)^n P_A{L_n>=c e_A}<=alpha. Let epsilon decrease
to zero, then w increase to c. This proves the inherited ceiling

             P_A{L_n>=c e_A}<=min(1,alpha/c).              (16)

No inadmissible zero-bridge model is used. Splitting one path into pieces
does not supply independent preparations. Knowing the visible stationary
marginal does not evade (16). For ANY positive base, uniformly honest
lower bounds cannot converge in probability to its full rate.

This no-go does not forbid powerful equilibrium tests or small positive
rate certificates. It does forbid presenting (12) as consistent full-rate
inference. The certificate's dependence on alpha is scientifically real.

8. EXACT SOURCE POWER, SHARPNESS OF THE RATE SCALE, AND COSTS

Use the preserved four-state source (s,t) in {+/-1}^2, uniform pi, Y=s,

 (s,t)->(s,-t) with probability (1+b st)/2,
 (s,t)->(-s,t) with probability (1-b st)/2,       0<b<1.    (17)

Its exact minimum hidden e at the supplied full law is
e_b=2b atanh b, by predictive_flow/PROOF.txt. The ordinary sharp binary
triple bound is attained by this chain. All observed words are exactly
reversal invariant: theta(s,t)=(s,-t) preserves labels and theta P theta=P^T.
The chain is periodic; it is not claimed CTMC-embeddable.

Let q_t=s_t t_t=(-1)^t q_0, and k_t=s_t s_{t+1}. Then eta_t=q_t k_t
are iid signs with Pr(eta=+1)=(1+b)/2, independent of uniform q_0.
The statistic (6) is q_0 times sum eta_t, up to its irrelevant virtual
initial sign. Hence |S_n|=|2Z-n| in law, Z~Binomial(n,(1+b)/2).
This binomial law is proved for THIS source, not assumed for the null.

For b=1/2, alpha=beta=1/40, the FIRST horizon of the specific test (7)
with power >=1-beta is n=88 transitions, 89 observed bits. It rejects
at |S|>=28 and exact power is

 5861382907809101223963631666746918991044672645914833 /

                  = 0.9791310867706089... .

The equally informed elementary comparator (8) first reaches the target
at n=89, critical |S|>=29, power 0.9754737482752089... . Thus there is
essentially NO sample advantage over that ordinary comparator here.
Minimality refers only to these particular calibrated tests on this
particular source, not a minimax required sample size.

Adding holding probability r=1/100000 gives P_r=rI+(1-r)P_b, an actual

For (17), |S_n|/n->b almost surely, so (11) converges to 4alpha I(b).
Its fraction of the true rate is

                       4alpha I(b)/(2b atanh b).

As b decreases to zero this tends to alpha. Thus for every c<alpha,
some sufficiently weak-drive source has Pr{L_n>=c e_b}->1 as n->infinity.
This approaches the high-power ceiling (16) at its actual scale. The
limits are sequential: first n->infinity for fixed b>0, then b->0.
It is not full-rate consistency or a new general thermodynamic advantage.

9. NEGATIVE CONTROLS AND THE MORE GENERAL CONSEQUENCE

The receiver checks actual reversible hidden-memory nulls, exact current
mgfs on rational random reversible chains, the source's iid driving signs,
observer inverse identities, dormant-copy stationarity/cost factors and
the aperiodic no-hold power bound. It also retains two countermodels:
the forbidden modulo-three observer, and nonstationary preparation.

For the latter take the reversible birth-death chain on {0,...,m} with
right probability 3/4, left probability 1/4 and reflecting holds. Its
stationary law is proportional to 3^i. Start instead at zero, and label
states 00110011... . An all-right n-step history has current score n
and probability (3/4)^n, which exceeds 2^(1-n), the stationary-null
bound, already for n>=2. At n=8 this nonstationary false-rejection
subevent has probability >0.10 although the test level is 0.025.
Stationary preparation is therefore indispensable, not a cosmetic caveat.

PREDICTIVE_DUAL_PROOF.txt goes beyond the special current: it transfers
every exactly checked finite predictive-flow dual to a one-trace no-mixing
test through hidden forward/backward martingale decompositions, and gives
a statistically complete, possibly very expensive, semidecision against
stationary ergodic alternatives. It charges same-record selection explicitly.
It has not yet supplied a useful N507/N541 separation or a physical data set.
