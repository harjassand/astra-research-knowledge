PREDICTIVE-FLOW DUALS ON ONE DEPENDENT STATIONARY RECORD
Solo derivation, 31 October 2026. This is a mathematical proof, not a
physical experiment or a claim of historical priority.

1. THE ACQUISITION QUESTION

The preceding outputs/predictive_flow/PROOF.txt constructs complete finite
LP outer tests for a reversible hidden realization of a finite observed
word law. Its iid validation and count experiments have their own contracts.
Here the supplied sample is ONE consecutive stationary record. No mixing
time, spectral gap, hidden-state count, reset, or independent replicas are
assumed. Stationary hidden initialization is still required. It is not
established by calling a simulation stationary, or by an uncharged burn-in.

Hidden states are standard Borel; time is discrete and reversal is all-even.
P is a stationary Markov kernel with invariant probability pi. Observations
Y_t=a(X_t) lie in a finite alphabet of size s. The reversible null is P=P*.
All statements below permit reducibility and periodicity.

Conclusion. Every finite rational dual of the preceding LP, verified on
EVERY flow column, supplies a uniform no-mixing tail test on this record.
It also supplies a conservative confidence lower bound on e(P). A fixed
countable collection of such certificates yields an anytime equilibrium
test that is complete against stationary ERGODIC observed alternatives
with no reversible hidden realization. Computation and certificate selection
costs are explicit below; completeness supplies no useful universal budget.

2. PRECISE FINITE CERTIFICATE INTERFACE

Fix depth L>=1, mesh h=1/k, and the prefix-simplex grid used by
predictive_flow/flow_grid.py. A node i consists of a label and two leaf
distributions, each with k integer units on words of length L. Let r^q_w(i)
be the prefix probabilities, q in {+,-}; r_empty=1. A descriptor d=(q,b,w,tau)
has |w|<L and tau in {-1,+1}. For ordered nodes define

 a_d(i,j)=tau[1_{a(j)=b}r^q_w(j)-r^q_{bw}(i)]-h.          (D1)

The certificate consists of rational y_{i,d}<=0, rational word coefficients
c_v for |v|=2L+1, and rational z. Let

 F_v(i)=1_{a(i)=v_L} r^-_{reverse(v_0...v_{L-1})}(i)
                           r^+_{v_{L+1}...v_{2L}}(i),
 c(i)=sum_v c_v F_v(i),
 A(i,j)=c(i)+sum_d y_{i,d}a_d(i,j).

The receiver must verify exactly, for ALL node pairs including diagonals,

                  z+[A(i,j)+A(j,i)]/2 <= 0.               (D2)

The observable word statistic and its width are

             H(v)=-z-c_v,       R=max_v H(v)-min_v H(v).

Define the computable coefficient bound

 B=max_i sum_{b,|w|<L} |sum_{q,tau} tau*y_{i,(q,b,w,tau)}|. (D3)

Using max_i sum_d |y_{i,d}| instead also works and is usually looser.
Nothing about a proposed dual's floating solver status establishes (D2).
No coefficients are permitted to depend on the test record without the
selection charge in Section 8.

3. A POINTWISE CONTINUOUS-STATE CONSEQUENCE OF THE FINITE DUAL

Under the reversible null, put

 u_w(x)=Pr(Y_1...Y_|w|=w | X_0=x).

The same u is the conditional law of the past read AWAY from the anchor.
Disjoint past/future are independent conditional on X_0. Let R_x be the
unbiased prefix quantizer of predictive_flow/PROOF.txt Section 5B applied
to u twice, with independent seeds for the two groups. It preserves the
label, E_R r^q_w=u_w, |r^q_w-u_w|<=h, and
E_R[r^-_u r^+_v]=u_u u_v. This quantizer is a proof device; the observer
does not know x or u and need not acquire hidden states or run it.

Write c_*(x)=E_{i~R_x}c(i) and H_*(x)=-z-c_*(x). These are respectively
the conditional word costs given X_0=x. In particular H_* lies between
min H and max H. Average (D2) with i~R_x,j~R_y independently. The data
terms become c_*(x),c_*(y) EXACTLY. For each endpoint,

 E sum_d y_{i,d} a_d(i,j) = M(x,y)+E sum_d y_{i,d}
                {tau[u_{bw}(x)-r^q_{bw}(i)]-h} >= M(x,y),

where

 f_{bw}(x)=E_{i~R_x}sum_{q,tau} tau*y_{i,(q,b,w,tau)},
 M(x,y)=sum_{b,w}f_{bw}(x)
                       [1_{a(y)=b}u_w(y)-u_{bw}(x)].       (D4)

The inequality follows term by term: the brace is <=0 and y<=0.
Unbiased rounding at the NEXT endpoint is essential for (D4). Shared
rounding seeds for the two present groups would spoil c_*(x).
By the Markov prediction recursion, E[M(X_t,X_{t+1})|X_t]=0.
The conditional range of M(X_t,X_{t+1}), with X_t fixed, is <=B:
the varying part in each summand lies in [0,1], and sum|f|<=B by (D3).
It follows that

       H_*(x)+H_*(y) >= M(x,y)+M(y,x)                       (D5)

for every x,y at which the chosen conditional versions are defined.
The reverse sequence M(X_{t+1},X_t) is a backward martingale difference,
with the SAME conditional range, because P=P*. Neither martingale must
be observed to establish the tail bound.

Summing (D5) along n edges and keeping the two endpoints gives

 sum_{t=0}^{n-1} H_*(X_t)
   >= (1/2)sum_t[M(X_t,X_{t+1})+M(X_{t+1},X_t)]-R/2.     (D6)

Omitting the R/2 endpoint correction is not authorized by stationarity.
Let V denote the half sum in (D6). Conditional Hoeffding plus Cauchy-Schwarz
gives E exp(lambda V)<=exp(n lambda^2 B^2/8).

4. REPLACING HIDDEN CONDITIONAL COSTS BY OBSERVED WORDS

Observe Y_{-L},...,Y_{n-1+L}: n+2L letters and N=n+2L-1 transitions.
Let A_t=(Y_{t-1},...,Y_{t-L}), B_t=(Y_{t+1},...,Y_{t+L}); these are
ordered AWAY from the anchor. Set H^a(u,v)=H(reverse(u),a,v). Define

 V_t=H^{Y_t}(A_t,B_t)-sum_v u_v(X_t)H^{Y_t}(A_t,v),
 W_t=sum_v u_v(X_t)H^{Y_t}(A_t,v)-H_*(X_t).               (D7)

V_t has mean zero conditional on the ENTIRE hidden past through t.
It is measurable through t+L and has conditional range <=R. Therefore
each residue class of t modulo L is a forward block-martingale sequence.
Under reversal, W_t similarly has mean zero conditional on the entire
hidden future from t, is measurable back to t-L, and has range <=R.
This uses equality of conditional past/future word laws, not independence
of consecutive observed windows. The identity

             H(Y_{t-L}...Y_{t+L})=H_*(X_t)+V_t+W_t

is exact. Generalized Holder over the L residue classes gives

 E exp(lambda sum_t V_t)<=exp(n L lambda^2 R^2/8),

and the same bound for W. Combining V, W, and the half sum in (D6)
using Holder with weights proportional to B/2, sqrt(L)R/2, sqrt(L)R/2
(zero terms are omitted) gives, for

                   C=B/2+sqrt(L)*R,

 E exp(lambda [V+sum_t V_t+sum_t W_t])
                              <=exp(n lambda^2 C^2/2).    (D8)

Equivalently each term has a sub-Gaussian variance proxy; Holder adds
their square roots. No independence between these terms is presumed.
The computable rational replacement C=B/2+ceil(sqrt(L))*R is conservative.

Writing T_n=sum_{t=0}^{n-1} H(Y_{t-L}...Y_{t+L}), Chernoff now gives

 Pr_rev{T_n<=-s} <= exp[-((s-R/2)_+)^2/(2n C^2)]         (D9)

for s>=0. Clamp at one. If C=0 the statistic is constant and (D2) forces
it nonnegative; it cannot reject. (D9) is a fixed-horizon statement.

5. A DIRECT REFLECTION SPECIAL CASE

Let H^a be any fixed symmetric copositive matrix on length-L words,
so u^T H^a u>=0 for every nonnegative u. Then H_*(x)>=0 pointwise.
Equations (D7) alone apply: take B=0, omit the endpoint correction,
and use C=sqrt(L)R. Thus

 Pr_rev{T_n<=-s} <= exp[-s^2/(2n L R^2)].                  (D10)

For L=1, binary f=+/-1 and H(a,b)=f(a)f(b), R=2. Hence the ordinary
lag-two reflection statistic has the valid bound exp[-s^2/(8n)] on
one stationary dependent trace, without a mixing bound. The stronger
current construction in PROOF.txt has much better weak-drive power on
the periodic four-state fixture; neither construction establishes an
advantage over an equally informed optimum on all sources.

(D9) applies beyond copositive reflection witnesses: it uses the LP's
conditional consistency constraints. It supplies no new separation of
the actual N507/N541 all-reflection-CP example until a certificate for
that example is constructed and checked.

6. A STATIONARY ENTROPY-RATE CONFIDENCE LOWER ENDPOINT

Let P be any stationary hidden chain, e=D(pi P||(pi P)^T). Put P_s=(P+P*)/2.
Its invariant law is pi and it is reversible. Let eta=(Gamma+Gamma^T)/2,
J=D(Gamma||eta). Reversal symmetry gives the same divergence for Gamma^T.
PROOF.txt proves e>=4J, including infinite costs. The full N-step path
relative entropy between P and P_s is N J<=N e/4, by the conditional
chain rule. All initial distributions are the same pi.

For the nested tail event {T_n<=-s}, data processing gives

                 kl(p||q)<=N e/4,

where p is its probability under P and q under P_s. From (D9), q<=q_0(s).
For fixed alpha, if q_0<alpha define l(s)=4 kl(alpha||q_0)/N; otherwise
let l(s)=0. Then Pr_P{l((-T_n)_+)<=e}>=1-alpha. Proof: if an attainable
threshold has l(s)>e, its tail probability cannot be >=alpha, since
kl(p||q)>=kl(alpha||q_0)>N e/4 for p>=alpha>q_0>=q. Nested-event
inversion gives the assertion. Use limits for a continuum of thresholds.
There are finitely many word-count outcomes at each n in this interface.

A cheaper entirely rational/logarithmic certificate follows. Let

             A=(((-T_n-R/2)_+)^2)/(2n C^2),
             h(alpha)=-alpha log alpha-(1-alpha)log(1-alpha).

Then

              L= (4/N)[alpha*A-h(alpha)]_+                 (D11)

is conservative: kl(alpha||e^-A)>=alpha*A-h(alpha); positivity already
implies e^-A<alpha. The denominator is N, NOT the number n of windows.
Use a certified UPPER bound on h(alpha) to evaluate a lower endpoint.

This endpoint is uniformly honest at the stated fixed horizon, not
consistent for the full stationary rate. The metastable ceiling in
PROOF.txt explains that distinction. A positive bound has ordinary
all-even path-irreversibility units; physical heat requires a supplied
thermodynamic model. For a stationary all-even CTMC sampled every Delta,
sigma_CT>=L/Delta, by path data processing. There is no CTMC attainment
or physical validation claim.

7. EXACT FIXTURE AND COSTS

The frozen generic_triple_certificate.json of predictive_flow gives
L=1, k=8, 162 grid nodes and 13,203 unordered flow columns. Exact checking
gives R=10/37, B=33/148 after combining duplicate predictive descriptors,
and expected H=-3/128 on the preserved four-state b=1/2 source.
Consequently C=B/2+R=113/296. The unused looser B is 25/74.
The standalone receiver checks these values and every column again.

A deterministic sufficient simultaneous type-I/type-II guarantee can
be obtained without simulating the fixture. Under its exact construction,
the driving signs eta_t are iid, and each triple's H depends only on
eta_t,eta_{t+1}, a fair stationary phase, and the initial observed bit.
The frozen H is not to be presumed invariant under those latter variables:
the receipt evaluates its power or a bound using the actual state chain.
It is not legitimate simply to reuse the iid 1,258-window calculation.

Per test record: ONE hidden stationary preparation, N transitions,
N+1 observations; streaming word storage O(L log s), an exact rational
accumulator of polynomial bit length in N and fixed coefficient sizes,
and O(N) fixed-statistic evaluations. No mixing gaps or resets are used.
Obtaining/validating stationary preparation, detector fidelity, Delta,
timing, and physical energy costs is an external experimental obligation.
The synthetic exact fixture supplies only a mathematical power control.

Certificate production is additional: the earlier LP solver, grid memory,
rationalization, and every-column receiver cost remain. For general L,k,
the grid has s*binom(k+s^L-1,s^L-1)^2 nodes and quadratic flow columns.
Nothing here makes the full hierarchy polynomial or cheaply acquired.
The existing ordinary reflection and current tests must be compared using
the SAME one-trace contract; independent-window budgets answer a different
question. No test-source generator is input to the frozen evaluator.

8. SELECTION AND A COMPLETE STATISTICAL SEMIDECISION

For a predetermined finite family of K verified certificates, using alpha/K
in (D9) controls arbitrary same-record selection among them. Searching an
unpenalized continuum of duals using that record invalidates the guarantee.
Splitting a dependent trace into pilot and validation segments does NOT
make the selected certificate fixed or provide conditional stationarity.

All finite rational certificates (D1)-(D2), over all L,k, can be enumerated
in a fixed order j>=1, including zero/statistically useless ones if desired.
Their rational checks terminate individually. Assign a predetermined
positive summable weight, for example w_j=2^-j. At each integer horizon n
use false-positive allocation alpha*w_j/[n(n+1)]. A union bound gives
Pr_rev{any certificate ever rejects}<=alpha. This is a mathematical
procedure, not a request for a scheduled task, background process, or
unbounded runtime. Finite dovetailing describes its computability.

Suppose the observed stationary process is ERGODIC and has no stationary
reversible standard-Borel hidden Markov realization. Compactness in the
preceding predictive-flow theorem implies that some finite word law has
no reversible hidden realization. Otherwise a diagonal compactness limit
would realize the complete law. At some finite mesh its symmetric LP is
infeasible; strict rational separation yields a finite rational certificate
with E H=-delta<0. It has a fixed index j and finite B,R,L. The ergodic
theorem gives T_n/n -> -delta almost surely. Its log penalty is O(log n),
whereas the exponent in (D9) grows linearly in n. It therefore eventually
rejects almost surely. A dovetailing algorithm revisiting every fixed j,n
eventually finds such a rejection. No convergence speed is supplied.

Thus the exact hierarchy is also statistically complete, in this qualified
sense, on one stationary ergodic trace, with no supplied null mixing bound.
Under nonergodic alternatives a record can stay in a reversible component;
the stated power conclusion cannot be asserted for them. There is no
uniform finite sample/runtime bound or full-rate consistency claim.
Using a certificate's actual prefix-free description weight instead of a
fixed library also works, but charges its full description length; a large
LP dual may incur a prohibitive selection penalty. It is not a free fix.
