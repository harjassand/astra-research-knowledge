A further update: the gauge work has produced a possible way to certify that unwanted charge-constraint violations stay out of low-energy states, including for a class of finite quantum-link models. It has passed internal checks, but it still doesn’t solve the missing mirror separation or establish an interacting chiral gauge theory. I also tested a different route: a proposed algorithm to certify all low-energy states of certain quantum impurity systems. The mathematics has passed an internal audit, but a concrete benchmark exposed a huge cost: its guarantee required a problem vastly larger than established numerical methods use. So I’m treating it as a possible rigorous certificate, not the breakthrough or a practical speedup. Neither result has been externally verified.
