# Energy-restricted approximate passive compilation

Root corollary, 29 October 2026. This supplies a finite-error interface to the exact receiver theorem; its exact rank minimum is not a physical finite-error lower bound. Review is separate.

Let Y have complex-orthonormal columns representing the receiver's measured mode/quadrature axes, with unused modes discarded. In the W(f)=exp[i Phi(f)] convention define

    ftilde_i=Y Re(Y†f_i), Delta_i=f_i-ftilde_i.

The ftilde_i are isotropic. The same receiver, with local feedback coefficients Re(Y†f_i), exactly erases their controlled family. Y may be a refined causal receiver; its physical chronology still applies.

For each source label z let Ftilde_z=sum z_i ftilde_i, Delta_z=sum z_i Delta_i, phi_z=Im<Ftilde_z,Delta_z>. Suppose the common pre-displacement input, including source/reference correlations, has total field number moment <N><=E. Put

    d=max_z ||Delta_z||,
    b=inf_c max_z |exp[i(phi_z-c)]-1|.

Then the corrected source/reference/ACTUAL-record instrument is within complete half trace distance

    min(1, d(sqrt(E)+sqrt(E+1))+b)                         (1)

of the intended diagonal gate and the same common receiver instrument. A separate source-phase or common-background comparison is additive. This is an energy-constrained supremum, not unrestricted diamond distance.

## Proof

W(Ftilde_z+Delta_z)=exp[i phi_z]W(Ftilde_z)W(Delta_z). For any purification psi=sum_z |z>psi_z,

    sum_z ||a(Delta_z)psi_z||²<=d²E,
    sum_z ||a†(Delta_z)psi_z||²<=d²(E+1).

Integrating the unitary group generated by controlled Phi(Delta_z) bounds its vector difference from identity by d(sqrt(E)+sqrt(E+1)). Removing an arbitrary GLOBAL phase exp(ic) leaves additional error at most b. Pure-state half trace distance is at most vector distance. Purification and contractivity through the common detector/feedback prove (1). No per-branch energy assumption or source-field independence was introduced. The source-dependent Weyl phase was not silently treated as free local feedback.

## Computable data and a discontinuous exact rank

Let H_ij=<Delta_i,Delta_j>. Then d²=max_z z^T Re(H)z; a convenient computable sufficient estimate is d²<=n lambda_max(Re H). Let M_ij=Im<ftilde_i,Delta_j>, S=(M+M^T)/2. Taking c=Tr S removes only a global phase and gives b<=2sum_{i<j}|S_ij|. A sharper finite-source maximization is optional, not presumed free.

For the two-bin features in TWO_BIN_OPERATION.md, direct P homodyne of both packets keeps the A feature and drops the B feature. The omitted feature has norm sqrt(2)b and the total Weyl phase here cancels. With vacuum baseline input E=0, the complete record-retaining error is <=sqrt(2)b. Yet every b>0 and a>0 has exact prefix rank two and requires one mode for exact passive erasure. Taking a=1/4, b=1/10000 gives a sufficient zero-memory error <1/7000. Thus an exact mode-count jump is not a finite-error physical storage limit. The independent direct-homodyne formula in ../reviews/erasure_challenge/NO_MEMORY_COMPARATOR.md gives the stronger actual comparison for this family.

This example is a supplied-feature receiver test, not a free fixed-angle gate improvement. If the same interaction produces theta=2ab and theta is held fixed as b decreases, a grows as 1/b. The input vacuum moment does not count energy injected by the controlled displacements; output energy, phase-feedback precision and loss sensitivity then grow. Those costs remain in the source/control ledger.

Equation (1) also shows the next interface clearly: acquire a bound on actual feature mismatch, the common input moment and any extra source phase; then certify the delivered receiver against a nearby exactly erasable model. It does not claim that floating SVD tolerances or a small kernel residual automatically certify those quantities. The earlier number-moment machinery may supply E only after matching its physical interface and startup/supply assumptions.
