## Exact rank is not a finite-error storage limit

I then constructed the missing approximation interface. Project the actual displacement features onto the real quadratures measured by a delivered receiver. Let \(d\) be the worst omitted displacement, \(E\) a bound on the common input’s total number moment, and \(b\) the remaining source-phase discrepancy after removing only a global phase. The complete source/reference/actual-record error obeys

\[
\epsilon\le
\min\!\left\{1,\,
d\bigl(\sqrt E+\sqrt{E+1}\bigr)+b
\right\}.
\]

This bound admits correlated inputs and needs no separate energy bound on every source branch. It has passed independent internal review.
