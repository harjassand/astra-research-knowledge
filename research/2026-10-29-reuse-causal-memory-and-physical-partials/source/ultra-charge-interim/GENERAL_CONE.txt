GENERAL FINITE-STEP CHARGE-SECTOR GAUSSIAN INSTRUMENT
29 October 2026. Root internally supported construction; not yet independently
accepted; priority and useful runtime advantage unestablished.

This strictly generalizes the positive-diagonal measurement ansatz in
CONSTRUCTION.md at the level of allowed parameters. Whether it strictly
enlarges the feasible physical-noise set is an investigation, not assumed.

Retain H=.5 n^T U n+H0, physical dephasing Gamma PSD, and EXACT preserved
sector C^T n=q. Choose real symmetric X,Y,U' obeying

 X = diag(d)+C Vx^T+Vx C^T,
 U'= U+diag(s)+C Vu^T+Vu C^T,
 Q^T(X+Y)Q=Q^T Gamma Q,

              [ X       -U'/2 ] >=0.                       (G1)
              [ -U'/2       Y ]

There is no sign restriction on d. The covariance itself imposes X,Y PSD.
All these constraints are affine, hence minimize gamma with Gamma=gamma
Gamma0 by SDP. Singular X is permitted, so no artificial positive floor.
Let l=d+2Vx q and g=s/2+Vu q. Within the fixed sector n^T X n=l^T n and
.5 n^T(U'-U)n=g^T n.

FINITE-STEP IDENTITY. Under a reference JOINT REAL Gaussian law (a,b) with
mean zero and covariance h [[X,-U'/2],[-U'/2,Y]], use Kraus operator

       K_(a,b)=exp[(a+i b)^T n - h l^T n].                 (G2)

On an occupation coherence between r,t in the sector, let delta=r-t and
v=r+t. Integrating K_rr conjugate(K_tt) gives

 exp[-ih/2(r^T U'r-t^T U't)
     +h/2 v^T X v-h l^T v-h/2 delta^T Y delta]
 =exp[-ih/2(r^T U'r-t^T U't)-h/2 delta^T(X+Y)delta].     (G3)

The equality follows from r^T Xr=l^T r, t^T Xt=l^T t. The quadratic gauge
correction -g^T n in H0 and Q^T noise identity recover the target channel.
On diagonals r=t, (G3)=1, establishing trace preservation ON THE SECTOR.
Each K is a fermion Gaussian operator; no nonlinear state approximation.
The covariance need not define a trace-preserving instrument off-sector.

EXACT POSITIVE SAMPLING (not base-Gaussian importance weighting):
1. Sample an auxiliary occupation configuration s from diag(rho) of the
   CURRENT Gaussian trajectory, using ordinary determinantal point process
   sampling (or Gaussian occupation sampling). This is an auxiliary random
   variable; DO NOT replace the trajectory state by that configuration.
2. Sample a ~ N(2h Xs, hX). This is the correct tilted measurement-outcome
   law, since multiplying the base N(0,hX) by exp(2a^T s-2h l^T s) translates
   its mean by 2hXs; the total scalar factor is
   exp(2h s^T Xs-2h l^T s)=1 on the sector.
3. Set Z=-U'/2. Conditional on a, sample
   b ~ N(Z^T X^+ a, h[Y-Z^T X^+ Z]).
   PSD of (G1) supplies range(Z) subset range(X) and PSD residual. These
   are pseudoinverses only on the covariance support. The tilted norm
   depends only on a, so the reference b|a conditional law stays correct.
4. Update and normalize the Gaussian trajectory by (G2). All weights are
   nonnegative probabilities; matrix numerical precision remains a cost.

The DPP draw in step1 does NOT inject a configuration measurement into the
target evolution. Its sole purpose is to sample a continuous outcome law;
step4 is the actual state update. Discarding this distinction would destroy
quantum coherences and simulate a different process.

SCOPE. A supplied fixed-sector Slater determinant or efficiently sampled
mixture is sufficient. A generic strongly correlated initial state and
sampling its diagonal are not supplied for free. Noncommuting H0 requires
a paid product formula just as in CONSTRUCTION.md. Discrete outcome counts,
normalization/QR conditioning, finite precision, runtime, sample error,
and physically warranted Gamma remain separate obligations.

The cone is sufficient. No completeness for arbitrary positive Gaussian
unravellings is claimed. In particular, proving the most general possible
quadratic form linear on the support is a different question from proving
all physically simulable channels admit Gaussian exponential Kraus noise.

CONSEQUENCE. This is an explicit search operation over representations,
not simply a bound on an already supplied representation. It constructs
the covariance/sampler if feasible, and is allowed to fail. It has not yet
established a new general scientific principle or civilisation-level result.
