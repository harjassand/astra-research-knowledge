For physical dephasing Gamma=gamma I and fixed charges C^T n=q, define

 alpha(U,C)= min_s ||Q^T [U+diag(s)] Q||_op,              (O1)

where Q is any orthonormal basis of ker(C^T). Within the declared
Gaussian-exponential covariance cone (G1), the minimum gamma is EXACTLY
alpha(U,C). This is a characterization of that ansatz, not all positive
Gaussian decompositions, all classical simulators, or quantum dynamics.

NECESSITY. In any feasible covariance matrix, test the vectors (Qx,Qx)
and (Qx,-Qx). The two nonnegative quadratic forms imply

 x^T Q^T (X+Y +/- U') Q x >=0.

Projected X+Y equals gamma I and projected U' equals U+diag(s), since
the charge gauge vanishes there. Hence
 -gamma I <= Q^T[U+diag(s)]Q <= gamma I.
Taking the smallest possible s proves gamma>=alpha.

SUFFICIENCY AND ACTUAL CONSTRUCTION. Given s and gamma satisfying the
last two inequalities, put P=QQ^T and A=U+diag(s), and choose

 U'=PAP, X=Y=gamma I/2.

Then U'-A is a charge gauge CV^T+VC^T, because its quadratic restriction
to ker(C^T) is zero. One explicit formula, with G=C^T C, is

 V=-(A C)G^-1 + (1/2) C G^-1(C^T A C)G^-1,
 U'=A+CV^T+VC^T.

Block rotation by (I,I)/sqrt(2) and (I,-I)/sqrt(2) transforms covariance
[[gamma I/2,-U'/2],[-U'/2,gamma I/2]] into
(gamma I-U')/2 and (gamma I+U')/2, both PSD. The explicit sampler in
GENERAL_CONE.txt therefore applies, with simple real covariance X.
Use quadratic Hamiltonian correction g=s/2+Vq as before.
