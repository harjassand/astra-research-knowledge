Use its zero-start one-defect diffusion and controller premises: known SPD

No stationary-state sampler is granted.

1. Occupation moments need stopped Ito, not a factorization of
E[1_(t<tau) X_v(t)^6]. Write R=||X||^2. For m=1,2,3,
 L_t R^m<=-2m kappa0 R^m+2m theta(n+2m-2)R^(m-1).
The nonnegative shifts and local quartic add negative terms. Stop at tau
intersect a deterministic time cap and local state/controller cutoffs.
Ito/Dynkin and the NONNEGATIVE terminal R^m yield
 E integral_0^stop R^m dt
 <=theta(n+2m-2)/kappa0 E integral_0^stop R^(m-1)dt.
Use the same local stop for all m. Iterating from R^0=1 gives
 E integral_0^stop R^3dt
 <=(theta/kappa0)^3 n(n+2)(n+4) E stop.
Increasing caps/cutoffs and monotone convergence therefore give
 E_a integral_0^tau X_v(t)^6dt
 <=(theta/kappa0)^3 n(n+2)(n+4) E_a tau.              (S1)
Terminal R^3 was discarded before taking limits, so its uniform
integrability is not required for this occupation inequality.

No independence of tau and hidden fluctuations is assumed.

As T increases, the stopped-record sigma fields grow and determine the
