## Outcome and scientific choice

The chosen opening was to make a nonlinear retained quantum bath into a
controlled finite causal instrument. Success removes a broad obstruction
to testing computing operations: infinite bosonic memory whose accumulated
non-Gaussian correlations cannot safely be discarded, replaced by isolated
gate channels, or certified merely by two agreeing Fock cutoffs.

The completed bridge comprises output-tail regeneration, robust causal
windows, completely positive memory compression and a finite-Hamiltonian
compiler. It supplies a sufficient retained-memory dimension

\[
d=O\!\left(\log^2(J/\epsilon)\right)
\]

for J contacts and complete compression allowance epsilon at fixed
interface parameters. The same retained mode carries quantum correlations
between contacts; no physical reset is inserted. A separately enclosed
quantum prediction demonstrates the bridge for full conjugate-quadrature
sine interactions active during passive mixing.

This is a mathematical instrument and a specific causal regularization
principle, not an apparatus or a fault-tolerant computing advantage. Generic
bosonic finite approximation and polylogarithmic truncation are established
subjects. The contribution constructed here is the explicit repeated-port
instrument with bounded full Weyl vertices, complete causal records and
controlled finite dynamics. External novelty has not been adjudicated.

Three attacks were developed and compared:

| Attack | Constructed result | Scientific consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Thermal acquisition | Whole-product thermal comparison, count confidence, synthetic execution and restricted information bounds | Strong conventional improvement; the old visibility cost is methodological, but a count statistic does not acquire controls or couplers. |
| Fault locality / quantum mediator from the old contact | Exact commuting-field reduction with correlated initial states and records | Use the classical reduction for that source-only interface. Under its local-source assumptions it cannot create inter-source entanglement. |
| Complete retained quantum memory | Regenerated output algebra, causal lookback, CPTP compression, finite dynamics and a quantum witness | Committed route; it addresses noncommuting interfaces and is useful before hardware is available. |

The classical reduction is retained as the stronger solver where it
applies. Conjugate-quadrature coupling is a new stipulated interface; no
previous circuit or continuum-bath certificate is transferred to it.

## Decisive mechanism and load-bearing conditions

Pull the final retained oscillator backward through the prescribed passive
network. An operation on a departed mode that cannot return commutes with
this future output algebra. Complete causal detectors, source controls,
references and records can be absorbed into complementary dynamics. Each
nonlinear vertex still factors into an output displacement and a bounded
operator on the rest. A Dyson word of order k displaces the output by at
most kb.

At exact passive full swap the unperturbed output marginal is fresh
thermal, even if the old incoming memory has no finite energy moment.
Displaced-thermal tails plus the Dyson remainder therefore give an explicit
tail for the full nonlinear output. The nonlinear interaction need not be
switched off during mixing.

For partial swaps, a length-L window leaves at most cbar^L of its initial
retained mode. With exact boundary mean at most E,

\[
\delta_L=\arcsin(\bar c^L)\sqrt{(2\bar n+1)E+\bar n},\qquad
t_d\le\delta_L+T_d(\alpha L,b,\bar n).
\]

The same complete propagator acts on the two global comparison vectors.
This avoids an artificial exp(alpha L) from comparing Dyson terms
separately. Any fixed prescribed cbar<1 suffices asymptotically; the failed
condition cbar exp(alpha)<1 is not a physical threshold. Actual review
required explicit conjugation of the propagator into final-output
coordinates. That correction is incorporated and preserved.

A sufficient stable mean condition is

\[
\sqrt{\bar c^2E+\bar n}+\alpha b\le\sqrt E.
\]

Only the unconditional first mean is used. Rare normalized branches need
not obey it. Fresh thermal modes must be independent of incoming memory
and one another. Passive schedules and thermal supplies are prescribed,
not selected by records inside the window. First-contact robust compression
requires a thermal/vacuum initial marginal independent of future supplies,
or separately paid startup/burn-in. Finite mean alone gives no rapid
immediate partial-swap tail.

For P_d retaining occupations 0 through d-1 and Q_d=I-P_d, define

\[
\mathcal C_d(\rho)=P_d\rho P_d+
|0\rangle\!\langle0|\otimes\operatorname{Tr}_a(Q_d\rho Q_d).
\]

This is CPTP. An omitted tail amplitude t costs at most t+t² in complete
half-trace distance. Exact-prefix telescoping composes the bound without
assuming that artificial earlier compressions satisfy the original Weyl
control hypotheses. Logarithmic lookback and Dyson order give the
squared-logarithmic dimension.

Departed modes may undergo arbitrary common causal quantum processing,
but their separately retained detector memory is not compressed. Coherent
return, record-controlled passive mixing, correlated claimed-fresh supplies
and unadmitted oscillator controls require additional construction.

## Finite dynamics and computational reach

The compiler bounds displaced finite-number subspaces and uniform
intermediate tails, then compares exact dynamics with its finite
Hamiltonian using a projected forced equation. It uses P sin(X) P, never
sin(P X P). For finite input total number at most m and threshold M, its
complete Hamiltonian allowance is at most (1+A)T(M,m,A,kappa).
Restricting the simulator's fresh thermal state to B levels adds q^B,
q=n/(1+n). The exact finite map is CPTP. Its error ledger is

\[
\epsilon_{\rm initial}+\sum_j(t_j+t_j^2)
+\sum_j\epsilon_{{\rm contact},j}+\epsilon_{\rm finite\ solver}.
\]

Model uncertainty, statistical coverage and physical noise require their
own valid comparisons. Effective computation also needs computable matrix
elements and an effective control-integration rule. Integrability or
pointwise computability alone gives no numerical accuracy rate.

When departed quantum outputs are discarded, the contact-boundary temporal
operator bond is at most d², or (dh)² with other hidden apparatus of
dimension h. A fixed source dimension q gives a joint density matrix of
(qd)² entries with no additional hidden apparatus, or (qdh)² with that
apparatus retained. Actual records can be sampled without enumerating every
record string. This does not reduce a growing quantum register to a cheap
classical computation, bound retained detector outputs, establish finite
Markov order, or supply a local stochastic fault model.

For unit time, source I/2 and vacuum a,b, use

\[
H=\frac\pi2 i(b^\dagger a-a^\dagger b)
+\frac18\sigma_x\sin\!\frac{a+a^\dagger}{2}
+\frac18\sigma_y\sin\!\frac{i(a^\dagger-a)}{2}.
\]

Both full nonlinear terms remain active during passive swap. Here total
Weyl action A=1/4 and displacement bound b=1/2: this is a different numerical
parameter point from the dimension table, with its own certificates.
The accepted infinite-model expectation is

\[
0.009748778824845387583321769614292341
\le\langle\sigma_z\rangle\le
0.009748846972041638565154374216800806.
\]

The source channel moves I/2 and is therefore nonunital. It excludes an
initially independent classical random-Hamiltonian mixture, not every
classical state-space or dissipative model. An independent analytic
commutator derivation already gave the lower bound .0071349158228.

The enclosure uses total number below 24, dimension 600, rational matrix
enclosures, an exactly Hermitian 128-bit dyadic matrix and 64 degree-32
Taylor steps. Finite numerical error contributes less than 6.32×10^-34 to
the observable allowance. The roughly 3.41×10^-8 halfwidth is dominated
by the separate infinite-space cutoff allowance.

Root's independent review reconstructed all 3,548 nonzero entries from

This certifies one observable for one declared contact. It is not a full

or a scalable computing advantage. Coupling and phase delivery remain a

\[
d_{\rm output}\le d(\tau_n^{\otimes J},\tau_{\hat n}^{\otimes J})
=\operatorname{TV}(\mathrm{NB}(J,n),\mathrm{NB}(J,\hat n)).
\]

This implements established environment-parametrized-channel data
processing. Two million synthetic geometric draws were executed, with
zero physical starts. For the warm record and 10,000 future resources, the
global model allowance is about .1296844 while the raw per-contact sum is
3.2212565. That warm case is outside the old cold moment box; only the
global resource theorem is applied there.

For n≤10^-4 a hypothetical matched nonzero-temperature count plan needs
1,070,368,205 ideal counts for absolute error 10^-6 at failure .01. It was
not executed, covers thermometry only, and requires collection with known
efficiency. A restricted fully exposed future-resource task has a lower
bound of 52,030,186 calibration inputs; it is not a bound for every
sine-only operation. The separate all-zero vacuum-target plan is not a
matched substitute at nonzero nominal temperature.

Thus the old roughly 3.8-quadrillion visibility figure remains a method
cost. The new theory does not make that acquisition scheme a prerequisite
for foundational progress. Count access, signed mixing, waveform delivery,
independent preparation and model correctness still require acquisition.

Useful failures remain explicit: total incoming energy is the wrong

tail; termwise comparison invents an exponential; sin(PXP) is the wrong
restriction; artificial compression prefixes need not obey original
controls; isolated source channels lose memory; and cutoff agreement does
not certify arithmetic.

which useful noncommuting operation exploits this controlled memory under
a compatible coupling, source, phase and collection interface? The present
work does not preselect hardware. It makes joint-memory and complete-record
tests possible before a representation failure is mistaken for a physical
obstruction. Fully enclosed multi-contact channel propagation and
operation-directed acquisition are concrete extensions when a chosen
operation requires them.
