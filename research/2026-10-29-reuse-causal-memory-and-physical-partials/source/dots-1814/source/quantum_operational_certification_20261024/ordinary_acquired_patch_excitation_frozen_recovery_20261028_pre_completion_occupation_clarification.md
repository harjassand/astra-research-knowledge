    ||sign(C)-B||op<=2e/(2g-e)<=2e/g.                  (15)

One proof keeps the two positive/negative spectral subspaces. The true positive eigenvalues are >=g and the estimated negative ones <=-(g-e). Their overlap X solves a Sylvester equation with right side an off-diagonal block of C-Delta. Its convergent semigroup integral has norm <=e/(2g-e). The reverse overlap has the same bound, and the difference of the two sign operators is twice the larger principal-angle sine. No source degeneracy inside either sign sector matters. This proof concerns a deterministic perturbation of known-gap true matrices; the unknown g is certified below, not used as an inaccessible computational input.
