## A different opening worth a concrete test

Connect task-directed recovery to finite-memory predictive representations of controlled physical experiments. The consequential capability would be learning reusable controls/predictions without reconstructing all latent physics, including histories rather than independently reset channels. The actual bridge is not an analogy: it requires a physically acquired causal sufficient representation, admissible preparation/measurement policies, and a complete guarantee when those policies change. Known mean-state tomography is not reconstruction of the correlated archive; the Astra core explicitly preserves that distinction and correlated-source counterexamples.

Accepted complete-information-order undecidability prohibits a universal computable reference cutoff in its quantified variable-dimensional setting. It does not contradict the current finite-family recovery algorithms, whose objective, reference dimension and available control interface are bounded. That contrast suggests developing task-restricted predictive equivalence rather than claiming a universally sufficient description. This conceptual distinction is a direct scoped consequence, not claimed as a new discovery.

Before promoting the predictive-history opening, obtain the exact memory/classicality sources and primary controlled-process learning constructions, then build an actual acquisition instance or a decisive obstruction.
