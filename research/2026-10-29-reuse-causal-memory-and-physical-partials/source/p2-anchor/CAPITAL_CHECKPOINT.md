## Result and consequence

The completed capability is a structured **calibration-to-computation
instrument**. Finite model uncertainty now controls a family of adaptive
nonlinear-memory processes at two different, explicitly separated interfaces:

1. A complete source/reference/record interface from learned Weyl geometry,
   with the hidden bath traced.
2. A physically anchored retained-oscillator interface, extended to causal
   collected outputs, detector memory and actual detector feedback.

The second can reuse a non-Gaussian, source-correlated oscillator without
resetting it between contacts. Only its mean occupation must be stabilized.

This makes a calibrated model reusable across admitted source programs,
including programs chosen after calibration. It does not identify an
arbitrary unknown physical channel, solve the retained dynamics, or prove
a useful logical-computing advantage. The physical interface and noise

## The retained-memory construction

Each finite contact supplies an independent thermal b, retains physical a,
and uses

    H(t)=H_K(t)+A(t)S(t)sin[sqrt(v)(a+a^dagger)]
                           +g(t)i(b^dagger a-a^dagger b).

The sine remains active during collection. For the same canonical ports,
source program and declared passive waveform family, the reviewed bound is

    epsilon = alpha |sqrt(v)-sqrt(vhat)| C
              +Gamma ||g-ghat||_1
              +TV(tau_n,tau_nhat).

Here C bounds the current quadrature row and

    Gamma=sqrt((2nbar+1)P_*^2+nbar)+L C,
    L>=alpha sqrt(vmax).

The difficult step is domain preservation of D(J) intersect D(sqrt(N))
for J=i(b^dagger a-a^dagger b), and the fresh-product identity

    ||J psi_in||^2=(2n+1)<N_a>+n.

The exact Weyl commutators close this row during the nonlinear interaction.
It does not assert that generic quadratic generators are sqrt(N)-bounded.
With a first-moment invariant region, every finite sequence satisfies

    d_final <= min(1,sum_j epsilon_j),

while retaining actual a/source/reference correlations. The example
vmax=2.1, alpha<=0.01, nbar=0.0001, |theta-pi/2|<=0.1 has invariant
<N_a><=1/1600. Its conditional standalone parameter tolerances give a
1,000-contact bound <=0.005308353946532403. Those tolerances are not
measured device specifications.

The generator-domain construction strictly admits more inputs than an

Three accessible X probes identify v, signed near-swap angle and thermal n.
The two word experiments put the SAME passive transit on both Ramsey
branches, with local controlled-Weyl operations before/after it. No direct
control of a rotating field during the passive pulse is assumed. Their

An exact same-area/different-order counterexample shows why endpoint area
alone cannot identify a concurrent memory operation. The calibration means

in the explicit small-action witness. This identifies missing path

0.000860956. The hypothetical ordinary visibility plan needs
3,838,157,793,129,690 native-mode vacuum starts. None were performed.

1,000-contact certificate from that sufficient method. Neither result is
a physical noise rate or sampling lower bound. An additional terminal
zero/nonzero detector can certify a one-sided thermal upper bound much
more economically, but that is a different target and resource interface.

The pretrace contact comparison also supports complete causal collection
and detection. Departed modes can join the retained controller/detector
state, undergo common delayed or joint processing, and produce actual
feedback records. Those channels preserve the unconditional occupation
of a because they act trivially on it. Every new thermal input must be
independent of the entire retained state. No independent detector noise
model is assumed; the complete supplied detector process is compared on
both sides. Its own uncertainty and usefulness remain separate questions.
A common final readout on a is contractive without a future moment
condition. Direct-a operations followed by more reuse need such a condition.

The initial computation state is separately supplied in the moment region,
or compared to an identified in-region state with the reviewed preparation
allowance. An occupied state left by calibration is not a free fresh start.
The thermal purifier is inaccessible and is not an exposed reference.
Collection loss, logical loss/leakage, control delivery outside the family,
detector/reset errors, memory costs and fault-tolerant scheduling have not
been converted into a device advantage.
