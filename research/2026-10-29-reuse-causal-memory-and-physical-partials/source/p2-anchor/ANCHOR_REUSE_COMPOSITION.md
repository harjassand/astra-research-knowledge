failure of a sufficient certificate, not a device limitation. The narrow

inputs/passive transits. None were consumed. These are sufficient
fixed-design Hoeffding budgets under the supplied trusted experiment,
not sampling lower bounds, measured runtimes or guarantees of favorable
realized centers.
