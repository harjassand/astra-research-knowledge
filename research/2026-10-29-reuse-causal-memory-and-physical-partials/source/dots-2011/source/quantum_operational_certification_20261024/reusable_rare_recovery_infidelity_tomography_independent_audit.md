For the actual complete recovered channel, Uhlmann purifications have distance sqrt(2epsilon). Projecting onto acceptance leaves the ideal norm sqrt(p); the rank-one difference bound gives accepted trace error<=2sqrt(p)e+e². Partial trace contracts Hermitian trace norm. TP fixes the reference marginal, and the erased output is one-dimensional, so rejected-reference error is the negative partial trace of accepted error. Thus total Choi error<=4sqrt(2p epsilon)+4epsilon, and input dimension three gives diamond<=12sqrt(2p epsilon)+12epsilon. Without TP and a classical single erasure block this step would fail.
