The conclusion is deliberately stronger than the physical CP-suffix route's existence premise in one respect: finite operational rank plus a certified core can suffice for a PHYSICAL fixed-task recovery without a finite-dimensional physical realization of the entire source. The true environment may be infinite-dimensional; the signed quotient is used only for calculation, and physicality is restored on the actual finite accessible task. We do not claim that the quotient itself is a physical quantum memory.

Finite checks illustrate that example; they do not prove an infinite-dimensional theorem by truncation. General finite-rank CP realizability remains unresolved and unnecessary for (1)-(2).
