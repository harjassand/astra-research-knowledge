## Limits that must survive downstream composition

The global finite-Delta path rate is g², with a polynomial N cost in
the zero-initial version. Stationary and zero-start bounds are different
initialization experiments. Both remain valid after finite bandwidth,
instrument noise and passive readout processing by data processing.
No noiseless physical observer is claimed available. Native time units
have not been converted to laboratory seconds.

The g⁴/g⁸ Gaussian-chaos theorem concerns the stationary one-sided
tangent at a0. It does not bound fixed Delta paths, unknown-nuisance
Fisher information, nonlinear actuation, hidden-state measurements,
or zero-start local Fisher information after a finite burn-in. A
generic even-likelihood global-KL lift is explicitly false; the proof
contains a simple Gaussian-channel counterexample. That counterexample
does not show that a stronger finite-Delta theorem fails for this
particular quartic model.

The long-exposure temporal interface can improve a conservative
sufficient duration polynomially in N, subject to C's full receiver
qualification. It preserves the fixed-gap exponential depth cost.
Long integration, detector PSD/gain, stationary bath stability,
burn-in, fine calibration, read counts and clock duration are paid
physical requirements; none was executed here. Colored/1f noise,
unqualified bath fluctuations or an RC-noise covariance different
from the stipulated mobility require a different model/receipt.

No historical priority, economical physical implementation, practical
superiority over full nonlinear likelihood, or civilisation-level
capability has been established. The substantive completed outputs
are the actual global/local information scope and the finite temporal
