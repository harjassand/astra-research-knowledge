FINITE-TEMPERATURE SCALAR INTERFACE FOR DIAGONAL ANHARMONICITY
Root construction, 29 October 2026. Internal derivation; peer review pending.
This changes access: known K, calibrated uniform stiffness shifts, known
distributed mechanical Gibbs bath and scalar equilibrium variance. It is not
RC Johnson noise, unknown-K recovery, or an acquired physical instrument.

1. Finite-temperature affine enclosure, for ALL unknown quartics.
Let K be known SPD, alpha_i in [0,A], p a known port. At shift lambda>=0 put
R=(K+lambda I)^-1, b=R_pp, and a warranted curvature floor kappa>0.
V(x)=x^T(K+lambda I)x/2+sum alpha_i x_i^4/4. In its Gibbs law at theta>0,
x=sqrt(theta) z, Gaussian reference z~N(0,R), W=sum alpha_i z_i^4/4.
The EXACT rescaled density is e^(-theta W)/Z. For F=z_p^2, Wick gives
 C=Cov_0(F,W)=3 sum_i alpha_i R_ii R_pi^2 >=0.
Write mu=E_0 W, m2=E_0 W^2, mF2=E_0(F W^2).
The elementary 0<=e^-t-1+t<=t^2/2 for t>=0 gives
 Z=1-theta mu+rZ, N=E_0[F e^-theta W]=b-theta E_0(FW)+rN,
 0<=rZ<=theta^2 m2/2, 0<=rN<=theta^2 mF2/2.
If theta mu<=1/2, then Z>=1/2. Since R_pi^2<=b R_ii, C<=4b mu,
 N-(b-theta C)Z=rN-theta^2 C mu-(b-theta C)rZ.
Consequently
 |E_theta F-b+theta C| <=theta^2[mF2+3b m2+8b mu^2].
Gaussian Holder moments, R_ii<=1/kappa, give
 mu<=3nA/(4kappa^2), m2<=105 A^2 n^2/(16kappa^4),
 mF2<=945 A^2 n^2/(16kappa^5), b<=1/kappa.
Thus the ACTUAL finite-temperature variance v satisfies
 | (theta b-v)/(3theta^2)-sum alpha_i R_ii R_pi^2 |
       <= (111/4) A^2 n^2 theta/kappa^5 =: B(theta),       (1)
provided theta<=2kappa^2/(3nA). A=0 is trivial. No derivative-at-zero
observation or noiseless limiting-temperature oracle is supplied.

2. A finite exact rank guarantee for a known chain.
For an irreducible symmetric tridiagonal SPD chain, label nodes by distance
from endpoint p, i=0,...,n-1, with off-diagonal -b_i, b_i!=0. Set
D(lambda)=det(K+lambda I), L_i the leading i-node determinant, and T_i the
trailing (n-i)-node determinant, L_0=T_n=1. Ordinary continuants give
 R_pi=(product_(j<i)b_j) T_(i+1)/D,
 R_ii=L_i T_(i+1)/D.
Hence f_i(lambda)=R_ii R_pi^2 has numerator
 (product_(j<i)b_j^2) L_i T_(i+1)^3
over D^3, with distinct exact degrees 3n-3-2i. These n polynomials are
linearly independent. At ANY 3n-2 distinct positive shifts, the matrix
F_ji=f_i(lambda_j) has column rank n: a vanishing combination would be a
degree <=3n-3 polynomial with 3n-2 roots. Select n independent rows using
known K alone. For other known SPD matrices, explicit rank qualification
may succeed, but no universal rank theorem from cyclicity alone is claimed.

3. A genuine bounded-data receiver, rather than an ideal slope.
At qualified rows observe variance intervals [vhat_j-e_j,vhat_j+e_j].
Let y_j=(theta_j b_j-vhat_j)/(3theta_j^2),
 eta_j=e_j/(3theta_j^2)+B_j(theta_j).
The actual alpha belongs to the finite polytope
 {a in [0,A]^n: |F a-y|<=eta}.
Ordinary linear programming encloses any desired linear query globally.
If a nonsingular row subset has inverse L, the explicit point receiver
ahat=L y has |ahat_i-alpha_i|<=sum_j |L_ij| eta_j. All interval, model,
shift, temperature and K calibration errors must enter eta or F bounds.
No unknown K is silently treated as known physical calibration.

4. Exact GLOBAL finite-temperature injectivity, with a paid receiver.
The rank result can be strengthened beyond the affine approximation.
For common theta and selected n shifts write y(alpha) for the EXACT
finite-temperature vector in (1), L=F_selected^-1, G(alpha)=L y(alpha).
Differentiating the actual Gibbs integral at nonnegative alpha gives
 d y_j/d alpha_i = Cov_(j,alpha)(z_p^2,z_i^4)/12.       (3)
This is valid on the whole box, including one-sided boundary limits.
Here is a uniform bound without a perturbative convergence assumption.
For H>=0, Z>=1/2 and |e^-t-1|<=t give
 |E_alpha H-E_0 H|<=2theta[E_0(HW)+E_0 H * mu].
With H=F z_i^4, F, z_i^4, the Gaussian Holder estimates respectively
bound these differences by 495 A n theta/kappa^5,
9 A n theta/kappa^3, and 57 A n theta/kappa^4.
Also E_alpha F<=2/kappa, E_alpha z_i^4<=6/kappa^2.
Using (E_alpha F-E_0 F) E_alpha z_i^4
       +E_0 F(E_alpha z_i^4-E_0 z_i^4)
therefore bounds the covariance change by606 A n theta/kappa^5.
Its Gaussian value is12 R_pi^2 R_ii, so
 |d y_j/d alpha_i-F_ji| <=(101/2) A n theta/kappa_j^5.
Consequently ||dG-I||_infinity <=q, where
 q=(101/2) A n^2 theta max_i sum_j |L_ij|/kappa_j^5.   (4)
Choose actual theta>0 satisfying the prior Z floor and q<1. For any
a,b in the ENTIRE coefficient box, integrate the Jacobian along their
segment to obtain
 ||G(a)-G(b)-(a-b)||_infinity<=q||a-b||_infinity.
Thus G, and hence the n scalar finite-temperature variances, is globally
injective with inverse Lipschitz constant <=1/(1-q) in G coordinates.
This is an exact FINITE temperature statement, not a derivative oracle.
Together with section2 it works for every known irreducible endpoint
chain, using a known-K-selected subset of at most3n-2 supplied shifts.

It also supplies a concrete contraction receiver. Given noisy transformed
data c=L yhat, iterate
 a_(k+1)=clip_[0,A]^n[a_k-G(a_k)+c].                   (5)
Coordinate clipping is nonexpansive in infinity norm, so (5) has unique
fixed point in the box and contracts by q. If a* is the physical truth,
 ||a_fixed-a*||_infinity<=||L(yhat-y(a*))||_infinity/(1-q).
An implemented forward evaluation with transformed error <=e adds
e/(1-q) to the limiting radius; after k iterations initial error is
at most A q^k. G requires candidate Gibbs integrals. Those are a PAID
computational interface, not an oracle supplied by identifiability.
For each known candidate they can in principle be evaluated by Gaussian
rejection with acceptance Z>=1/2, qualified finite-precision Gaussian
draws and bounded/moment-controlled observables, or rigorous cubature.
The later section4a supplies a whole-box polynomial forward operator,
without new model observations. No inverse iteration on observed data
was executed. Ordinary global likelihood/membership inversion receives
the same data.

4a. Acquire the forward interface with finite Gaussian moments.
For EACH selected known R compile the exact Gaussian moments
 mu(alpha)=sum_i alpha_i E z_i^4/4,
 m2(alpha)=sum_ij alpha_i alpha_j E(z_i^4 z_j^4)/16,

 n2(alpha)=sum_ij alpha_i alpha_j E(F z_i^4 z_j^4)/16.
These are degree<=2 polynomials for ALL candidate alpha, not data at
one chosen alpha. All moments have degree<=10 and follow finite Wick
recursion from known R. Define
 Z2=1-theta mu+theta^2 m2/2,
 N2=b-theta n1+theta^2 n2/2,
 y2(alpha)=[b-N2/Z2]/(3theta).
Taylor's alternating finite remainder for nonnegative theta W gives
 0<=Z2-Z<=theta^3 m3/6,

This is a finite Taylor bound; the generally divergent infinite
quartic perturbation series is NOT assumed convergent. Both exact
Z and Z2 are>=1/2. Also
 0<=N2<=Nmax=1/kappa+theta^2 [945 A^2 n^2/(16kappa^5)]/2.
Thus uniformly on the entire box
 |y2-y|<=theta^2[n3+2Nmax*m3]/9.                     (6)
Multiplying these scalar errors by |L| bounds ||L y2-G||_infinity.
If this is below the paid forward tolerance in (5), the implemented

No high-dimensional candidate equilibrium sampler is now required

5. Conditional finite-noise cost, not an acquisition claim.
For stationary Langevin mobility I/noise sqrt(2theta) and a point read
Y+independent N(0,sigma^2), use score (Y+noise)^2-sigma^2. The above
Z>=1/2 gives E Y^4<=6theta^2 b^2 and E Y^2<=2theta b. Therefore score
variance <=6(theta b+sigma^2)^2. The global Gibbs spectral gap >=kappa
implies for reads spaced tau
 Var(mean score)<= [6(theta b+sigma^2)^2
                    +12theta^2 b^2/(exp(kappa tau)-1)]/Q. (2)
At tau=1/kappa this is <18(theta b+sigma^2)^2/Q (e>2).
Chebyshev and union bounds can pay any finite variance intervals.
Stationary preparation, finite exposure, absolute detector calibration,
classical bath validity as theta decreases, and physical time units are
additional admission costs. Section 4 does not grant pointwise white-noise
readout or a free independent Gibbs sampler. The explicit frame can be so
ill-conditioned that its finite-temperature and measurement budgets are
useless; fewer scalar summaries alone do not establish an advantage.
