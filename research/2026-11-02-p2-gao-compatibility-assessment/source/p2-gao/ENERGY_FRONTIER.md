# Energy-conditional frontiers and control-selection falsifier
**P2, 2026-11-02.** Reduction-only necessary conditions; no claim of full-system thermodynamic efficiency.

For CH4 made from CO2, 8F electrons per mole. At reduction-cell magnitude V_r, methane Faradaic efficiency f, the REDUCTION-PHASE electrical energy per newly synthesized methane mole is exactly E_r/n=8F V_r/f when f is consistently defined relative to reduction charge, all methane formed is correctly attributed and voltage is averaged charge-weightedly. Let chemical energy be LHV_CH4 * M_CH4,mol = 55.5e6 J/kg * .016043 kg/mol = 890386.5 J/mol. Hence reduction-only energy recovery eta_r = [890386.5/(8F)]*(f/V_r) = 1.1535257*(f/V_r) with V in volts. All added oxidation, pumps, capture, reforming, compression, coolant, refresh and downtime reduce system recovery if they consume net energy.

For even the reduction-only ratio eta_r>50%, it is NECESSARY that V_r/f<2.30705145 V. At f=.6 this means V_r<1.38423 V; at f=.8, V_r<1.84564 V; at f=.9, V_r<2.07635 V. The source-scale f=.6,V=3.9 gives V/f=6.50 V and eta_r=0.1774655; thus V/f must decrease by factor >2.817 for this 50% necessary gate, and still more once all other real process costs are included. This is an energy/selection diagnostic, not an achieved route to that efficiency. Never compare a reduction-only experimental ratio to a full-process modeled ratio without declaring different boundaries.

For r seconds reduction, o seconds oxidation, fixed reduction current and FE, methane output per wall time = I_red*r*f/[8F*(r+o)]. At baseline (r,o,f)=(5,5,.6), the output is proportional to .3 I_red/(8F). For (5,1,f_new), increased wall-time methane requires f_new>.36, but improved delivered-energy-per-mole needs measured oxidation and auxiliary energy. If f_new falls with age, short-term gain may not survive 500h. Oxidation is actual cathode Cu regeneration and cannot be treated as free downtime. Same-instrument ordinary waveform optimization can find this threshold.

The CEM primary source already improved on BPM by 2.1–3.6 V at matched current and sustained 500h with electrolyte service. Its 500h source output depends on 5s/5s cycling; no 5s/1s source result was established. The most consequential scientific front is to lower V_r/f while retaining true 500h or >100d stable output; extra sensors or copper recovery alone do not clear this bar.
