# Direct critical sectors through paired endpoints

29 October 2026. Work begun after the normal-fluid/critical-number proofs froze.

The attempted operation is a direct canonical, interacting pair description, avoiding the proved normalizable-grand count filter. In the conjugate-flavor sector the Hamiltonian vectorizes to the trace-decreasing CP generator

`Hcal(A)=T_K A+A T_K+sum_i U_i[n_i,[n_i,A]]`.

Its diagonal-to-diagonal imaginary-time endpoints are nonnegative. The missing constructive step is whether the eliminated off-diagonal excursions have nonnegative, computable first-return weights. Positivity at endpoints does not acquire those weights or a permanent/Gibbs-element oracle.

The analytic discriminator is the exact Schur/Volterra memory, not another Monte Carlo sign test. A fixed native frustrated trimer gives a negative OFF-DIAGONAL integrated return weight. I will test whether the failure persists on an acquired connected, bounded-range, arbitrarily many-mode family and at arbitrary finite U, and whether the bare paired space even retains the high-occupation critical Gibbs mass. A negative result here would exclude a specific positive-renewal/pair-elimination construction, not arbitrary canonical algorithms.

The equally informed alternatives include canonical ED, correlated AFQMC, number-conserving Bogoliubov/squeezed states, strong-coupling pair/rotor models and tensor methods. Kuklov--Prokof'ev--Svistunov 2003 already derives pair Hamiltonians and performs loop-current Monte Carlo. Feshbach/Nakajima--Zwanzig elimination and Schrieffer--Wolff effective Hamiltonians are established machinery. Their approximation ranges, occupations and physical interactions must be matched; a second-order pair hopping term is not a newly acquired exact positive operator.

No Gibbs matrix-element oracle, permanent oracle, mixing gap, microscopic cutoff or spectral gap beyond what is actually derived is supplied.
