The given choice was a genuine 3D reservoir layout for the sparse recursive
source, or a local multi-time microscopic error budget. The primary RX/RZ
source, arXiv2605.10943v1 Definition3.1/Lemma3.15, pays bounded-density3D cell

The Gaussian model supplied a stronger presently attainable analytic lever:
its exact correlation-based variation identity can retain useful relaxation
while isolating cross-block MEMORY faults. That is the construction pursued.

## Strongest equally informed ordinary methods

Ng--Preskill, PRA79,032318(2009), Eq27, already gives a non-Markovian Gaussian
Hamiltonian-noise threshold based on an integrated two-point correlation. It
handles Gaussian thermal baths, backreaction and arbitrary control without
assuming the evolved bath stays Gaussian. Applying it to our entire bath
as an unwanted perturbation is legitimate, but discards the controller's
useful relaxation. For a long cooling slot its parameter scales with the
slot duration. This is the controlling fault-tolerance prior, not a new
Gaussian-noise discovery in this packet.

Mascherpa et al., PRL118,100401(2017), proposed correlation-variation error
bounds. Liu--Lu, arXiv2408.04009v3, supplies their rigorous general Wick/
diagrammatic justification. In particular its Lemma1 resums the reference
environment into full unitary propagators, so the error does not inherit an
exponential prefactor from the strong reference dynamics. We use this actual
identity. Our local receiver explicitly builds a single consistent Mobius
fault expansion and pays its marked-coverage sum. It is not inferred from
Liu--Lu's global final-state error inequality alone.

Recent ancilla-train algorithms (Christensen--Agerskov--Nathan, PRA114,
042422,13Oct2026) and kernel-compression algorithms (Chen--Cai,
arXiv2610.29372,21Oct2026) are relevant strong ordinary finite-horizon
simulation comparators. Their primary abstracts/introduction describe
quantum circuits and horizon-dependent environmental resources; no theorem
about indefinite passive microscopic hardware or a positive fault process
is imported from them. They are not treated as absent prior simply because
their representation is different.

Burgelman et al., PRX Quantum6,010323(2025), already explicitly distinguishes
system resets from bath renewal, including exact quantum Gaussian dephasing
control histories. That warning is respected: the reused-bath process here
is expanded globally from its INITIAL Gaussian state, not re-assumed Wick/
thermal at each later time. Fault bounds allow history-dependent effects.
They are not constant independent gate-error probabilities.

## Concrete new receiver and why it matters

For every marked r seed/time slots, the inclusive coherent bad-map sum has
complete strategy norm at most (sqrt(2e eta))^r, with

    eta<=8alpha^2 sup_j integral_R |u C_j(u)|du.

This is independent of elapsed time AND the slot duration. All strong
within-slot dynamics belongs to the exact physical fresh-bath reference.
Thus one can have alpha^2*slot_duration of order1 while memory error per
slot is order alpha. The native compact band supplies the moment input from
the frozen scalar recurrence; no new unknown noise spectrum is estimated or
supplied free. The field/model calibration remains a physical gate.

TEMPERATURE_UNIFORM.md additionally pays one temperature-independent moment
constant A_0=4C_B by a fixed complex-neighbourhood bound on the gapped Bose
factors. The MEMORY coupling gate may therefore be fixed for all b>=1.
No same-temperature or all-temperature bare-KMS-controller accuracy follows
from this sharper memory estimate.

The proof norm retains arbitrary causal interventions, records and reference
systems. It is a multi-time operation, not merely a final-density bound.
The exponential-size formal fault decomposition is used for analysis; the
receiver requires only the scalar moment/gate, not an exponential table of
process maps or uncharged reconstruction.

Finite closed banks do not satisfy the needed all-horizon absolute-correlation
budget. The spacing-free Fejer bound gives a linear horizon cost in mode count
for this receiver. A finite-epoch alternative pays a first-moment/kernel gate
with M=O(epoch_duration+log(1/kernel_error)); its explicit renewal consumes
fresh banks/vacua and outputs entropy every epoch. Those are real resources.
The entropy inequality in FINITE_BANKS is a reset-capacity statement, not a
native heat/lifetime or universal dissipated-work bound.
