resource at its own declared interface. The signed-continuation theorem does
not question that preparation, establish its impossibility, or bootstrap it
from a later reuse claim.

finite-rate generator menu, with the specified unknown frozen parameter and

specimen, and any locality requirements must be paid. No such deployment
latency or addressed initializer is specified by the finite-rate family used
