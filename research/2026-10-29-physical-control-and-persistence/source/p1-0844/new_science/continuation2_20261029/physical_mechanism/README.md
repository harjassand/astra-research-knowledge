# Complementary ordinary-control construction and receiving review

29 October 2026. Existing Astra P1 physical-mechanism role; no new branch.
Predecessor files are unchanged. These files are proof/model calculations,
not physical observations or an apparatus demonstration.

- `ORDINARY_ADAPTIVE_CONTROL_AND_PER_DEVICE_CALIBRATION.md`: exact physical
  endpoint hitting control at random time; cheap robust waiting with explicit
  marked-record and timing scopes; same-specimen calibration to the same two

  read and duration records; optional continuous-path likelihood and costs.
- `check_ordinary_comparator.py`, `ORDINARY_COMPARATOR_CHECKS.json`: exact
  Bellman optimization over all depth-three marked feedback policies on finite

- `EXPOSED_STOPPING_TIME_OBSTRUCTION_AND_PRIMARY_COMPARISON.md`: the original
  narrow single/proportional-control timing obstruction and primary comparisons.
  Its stated narrow scope remains deliberate. Root's new signed-continuation
  theorem is stronger and is reviewed separately below.
- `SIGNED_CONTROL_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md`, `SIGNED_CONTROL_REVIEW_RECEIPT.json`,
  `reviewed_versions/`: actual input snapshot and SHA, full receiving review,
  accepted theorem scopes, and requested clock/latency clarifications.
- `check_signed_feedback_obstruction.py`,
  `SIGNED_FEEDBACK_OBSTRUCTION_CHECKS.json`: independent exact enumeration of
  40,805 bounded feedback trees and 1,298 heralded trees, plus sign checks.
- `check_signed_review_additions.py`, `SIGNED_REVIEW_ADDITIONAL_CHECKS.json`:
  independent rational four-site ring calculation and explicit counterexample
  to ignoring native evolution during controller latency.

The cheap waiting comparator matters: with an actual identity return, 100
macros at c_max=3/10 and L=9 native waits have proved complete endpoint/word
record TV at most 0.007564423, at most 540 expected native calls, and no
calibration. Native interiors are excluded. Calibration is separately useful
for short words or complete intermediate/path records; its conservative
confidence costs are much larger. Exact hitting is another strong comparator,
but its physical completion clock carries hidden-parameter dependence.
