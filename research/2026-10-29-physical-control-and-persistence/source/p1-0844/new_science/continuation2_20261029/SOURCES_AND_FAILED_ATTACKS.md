## Primary comparison

**Crank and Fill, Interruptible Exact Sampling in the Passive Case(2002).**
https://www.ams.jhu.edu/~fill/papers/passive.pdf
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0202136
Section3.2 and the impossibility arguments in Sections5-6 were inspected.
They already prove major interruptible unknown-chain sampling obstructions,
including reversible cases, using polynomial methods. Their returned sample
need not be the current physical state; the generic stationary law is unknown.
The present result fixes a known target but requires the actual terminal
physical state, and allows actively switched analytic families and continuous
monitoring. These are interface differences, not a first discovery that
interruptible exact sampling can fail.

**Goyal and Sigman, On Simulating a Class of Bernstein Polynomials(2012).**
https://www.columbia.edu/~vg2277/simulate_bernstein.pdf
https://doi.org/10.1145/2133390.2133396
Theorem2.3 characterizes fixed finite unknown-coin simulations by Bernstein
coefficients in[0,1], with independent auxiliary randomness. Finite transcript
polynomials are established. The extra restriction here is that a path's
terminal physical parity fixes its continued sign. Outputting an independent
label does not prepare that label as the device's actual state.

**Nacu and Peres, Fast Simulation of New Coins from Old(2005).**
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0309222v5
https://doi.org/10.1214/105051604000000549
Analytic Bernoulli factories can use random source-toss counts with exponential
tails. A deterministic-deadline obstruction does not exclude this strong
ordinary route. Its output coin is distinct from the current physical state
that supplied its observations.

**Lee and Valiant, Uncertainty about Uncertainty, SODA2021.**
https://arxiv.org/html/1904.09228v3
https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611976465.25
They distinguish within-coin and cross-coin adaptivity and give matching
bounds for population-fraction estimation with a bias gap. This is a close
statistical predecessor for returned-specimen acquisition. Our squared-error
certificate lacks that global gap and makes no statistical optimality claim.

**Jamieson, Haas and Recht, On the Detection of Mixture Distributions with
Applications to the Most Biased Coin Problem(2016).**
https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.08037
Rare versus weak alternatives and breadth/depth sampling are directly relevant
to the complementary returned-trial certificate and its lower bounds.

**Rebeschini and van Handel, Comparison Theorems for Gibbs Measures(2014).**
https://arxiv.org/html/1308.4117v1
https://doi.org/10.1007/s10955-014-1087-7
The introduction, classical comparison discussion, Proposition3.2 and Gibbs
sampler construction were inspected. Influence bounds, Wasserstein coupling
and dimension-independent observable comparisons are established. The new
program note proves its elementary dynamic receiver and supplies actual
per-device experiments and physical words that meet its premise.

**Glauber, Time-Dependent Statistics of the Ising Model(1963).**
https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1703954
The primary publisher page/PDF was located but unavailable through the web
tool. No inaccessible passage is used as a premise. The ring probabilities,
Gibbs measure and closed mean equation are directly derived and independently
checked here; they are not a new solution of Glauber dynamics.

Adaptive Markov control and ensemble control were also searched. Historical
priority is not established. The candidate addition is the controlled
physical-state signed continuation witness and its combination with actual
cross-response acquisition and synthesis.

## Failures that changed the mechanism

* **Monotonicity fails under feedback.** All source-B primitive flip slopes
  are positive. Nevertheless, using mode3 while the bit is0 and mode0 while
  it is1 makes the terminal flip derivative at h=0 negative after six calls.
  Exact polynomials are saved. The older open-loop monotonicity proof cannot
  cover feedback. The path sign survives and leads to the analytic witness.
* **Average error is not pointwise error.** Rare bad specimens and fitted
  deployment selection still defeat an unconditional population argument.
  The robust program instead obtains a confidence event uniform in each
  actual fixed device; the ensemble receiving theorem retains its conditional
  deployment-density requirement.
* **A better exponent can still be unusable.** The returned-trial certificate
  improves v^-2 to v^-1 up to logs only with a stronger repeated-specimen
  interface. Its saved v=1/1024 run requires786336 independent specimens and
 1390411776 returned trials, before return costs. This is not a practical
  control demonstration.
* **Waiting beats a weak comparator.** Mode3 has worst centered multiplier1/2.
  With a real identity branch and unobserved native interiors, a nine-call
  long branch gives100-macro marked TV<=0.007564423, using at most540 expected
  native calls. Calibration is unnecessary for that limited approximate task.
  Identity, selector runtime, sensing and timing premises are explicit.
* **Exact hitting retains a clock.** Feedback can prepare any target endpoint
  at an unbounded random time. It does not create a common full joint
  endpoint-clock law. A deterministic target can be heralded by a deadline;
  a nondegenerate target distribution cannot, within the witness family.
* **Controller latency is an operation.** A reviewer supplied an exact
  unprotected-delay counterexample. The final ring uses an ideal instantaneous
  boundary interface, or requires delay evolution inside the acquired kernel
  or protected by an actual hold. Five random bits do not prove an apparatus
  timing contract.
* **Unconditional equality does not preserve old information.** The general
  joint-record Wasserstein statement uses average conditional initial
  distance. Identical unconditional marginals can be correlated oppositely
  with the retained record. The same-start and worst-case preparation bounds
  are unaffected; the review preserves the counterexample.
* **The observable objective is narrower.** Uniform-in-time bulk expectation
  error is not full-record TV, adaptive stopping accuracy, arbitrary feedback
  or random-wall-time accuracy. The precision floor and loss of contraction
  near alpha=1 remain. Positive-flux repair stays a comparison object.
