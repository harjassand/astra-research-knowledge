# Result: bounded control of a stationary endpoint law

The new result is a general bounded operation on the actual current
microscopic state. A fixed, possibly unknown Markov kernel P preserves mu;
a noninvasive visible label specifies positive integer weights W. A finite
counter turns successive P calls into a kernel preserving nu=W mu/mu(W).
It uses at least one native call and never needs more than
ceil(max W/min W). Reversibility is unnecessary for invariance. When the
native kernel is reversible, a counter-direction mixture gives reversible
output without a physical adjoint operation.

The deterministic cap is sharp for the stated universal unknown-kernel
interface. At target-stationary macro starts the exact mean is
max W/mu(W); with a unique native stationary probability this is also the
least possible mean among positive-call stopped-current-state controllers.
The same mean is achieved by ordinary endpoint thinning. The counter's
candidate contribution is its bounded realization and matching limits,
not stationary reweighting or watched-chain mathematics themselves.

## Why this changes the opening

The invariant-law objective can be achieved without learning or eliminating
the hidden predictive state. The binary specialization has exact prescribed
odds while a frozen continuum of kinetic parameters leaves every finite
Hankel moment matrix positive definite. Infinite predictive rank and exact
target control coexist.

Local native operations sharing a stationary law can therefore be composed
into positive rational factor programs. With power-of-two energy weights
and bounded local energy changes, each local operation has bounded selector
bits and a bounded native-call cap independent of total system size.
Collective preparation and mixing costs remain. Prescribed or independent
state-independent scans are licensed; arbitrary adaptive schedules are not.

The continuous receiver uses coordinates x, a shared changing hidden
coordinate z, and a conserved hidden parameter h. Separately sign-even
energies give the same uniform visible baseline in every h-sector.
Addressed divergence-form diffusions with unknown mobility and conormal
reflection supply common reversible native kernels. Local compilation
preserves the desired full microscopic tilt and common visible factor law.
The family includes strong Ising interactions, frustration, and positive
soft-constraint factors beyond the previous contraction example. It remains

## Exact evidence and ordinary comparison

The root checkers use exact rational arithmetic for full hidden kernels,
including nonreversible examples, local composition, reverse identities,
occupation, native-call counts, and deliberately wrong controllers. The
two existing complementary roles supplied independent constructive routes,

One preserved comparison has native stationary masses (1,2,3,4)/10 and

lazy-kernel fixture, ordinary thinning exceeds 8 calls with probability
approximately 0.0155350056. The distinction is a universal hard cap, not a
smaller stationary mean or a blanket claim that every thinning source has
an unbounded tail.

## Material boundaries

- Completion-index law differs from ordinary clock-time equilibrium.
  Stationary occupation of the single repeated native kernel stays mu.
- Invariance does not prepare an arbitrary or calibration-conditioned start,
  remove retained records, guarantee convergence, or produce independent
  samples. The native state includes relevant hidden apparatus memory.
- Unique-law mean optimality does not apply unqualified to reducible sources.
  The universal cap lower bound does not cover arbitrary extra actuators.
- Rational integer weights have bounded native calls; exact randomizer time
  is automatically bounded by fair bits only for the stated dyadic cases.
  Bounded laboratory deadlines need actual compatible durations.
- Exact structural stationarity is not certified by finite observations.
  Measured baseline asymmetry, selector error, and sensing error need their
  own confidence and implementation bounds. Long-time phase observables
  can remain sensitive to coherent vanishing local errors.
- The closest predecessor is Andral et al.'s Rejection Markov chain; watched
  chains, occupation identities, correlated uniform updates, and time changes
  are established. Historical priority of the complete construction is open.
