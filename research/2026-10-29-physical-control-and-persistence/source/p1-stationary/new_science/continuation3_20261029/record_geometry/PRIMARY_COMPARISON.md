# Primary sources and scope of this continuation

Read early on29 October2026. These are close prior mechanisms, not a priority
endorsement. The preserved Astra scientific-acceptance governing source was
also consulted. No accepted predecessor or N675 dependency was reopened.
The companion proofs derive their claims directly.

1. Andrea Bertazzi and Giorgos Vasdekis, *Sampling with time-changed Markov
   processes*, arXiv:2501.15155v2,5 April2025.
   <https://arxiv.org/html/2501.15155v2>
   Sections2.1-2.2 connect state-dependent speed, invariant measures and
   importance-weighted occupation. Theorem2.3 relates base measure, speed
   and target measure. This is a direct conventional comparator. Our
   mandatory-call/hard-cap construction does not invent time change, and
   completion indices must remain separate from physical time.

2. Samuel Livingstone, Giorgos Vasdekis, Giacomo Zanella, *Foundations of
   locally-balanced Markov processes*, arXiv:2504.13322v1,17 April2025.
   <https://arxiv.org/html/2504.13322v1>
   Section2.1 defines g(t)=t g(1/t), builds jump rates from reversible flow
   ratios, and distinguishes the embedded jump chain from its holding
   times. Unknown symmetric mobility is ordinary balanced-kernel structure.
   The candidate physical contribution is a bounded actual excursion
   implementation at the declared interface, not balanced rates themselves.

3. Dootika Vats, Flavio Goncalves, Krzysztof Latuszynski, Gareth Roberts,
   *Efficient Bernoulli factory MCMC for intractable posteriors*,
   arXiv:2004.07471v3; Biometrika108(4),2021.
   <https://arxiv.org/html/2004.07471v3>
   Exact acceptance decisions need not numerically evaluate intractable
   target expressions; the paper also allows symmetric additions to the
   Barker denominator. Thus exact target preservation without estimating
   every transition probability is an established objective. This is a
   strong ordinary incumbent, not a weak baseline.

4. Dwija Kakkad and Dootika Vats, *Exact MCMC for Intractable Proposals*,
   arXiv:2410.10282v2.
   <https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10282v2>
   Section2 reviews the two-coin Barker construction from coin access to
   forward/reverse unnormalized flows and adapts it to unknown proposals.
   An acceptance variable does not itself move a physical specimen; actual
   proposal, rejection/return, records and clock costs remain part of the
   interface comparison. No superiority follows from exactness alone.

5. Serban Nacu and Yuval Peres, *Fast simulation of new coins from old*,
   Annals of Applied Probability15(1A),93-115,2005.
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0309222>
   The primary abstract was read. Analytic functions on a compact probability
   interval admit factories with exponential call tails, with a converse on
   open sets. Such a tail is different from a deterministic cap. Our first-
   coefficient argument adds actual-terminal-parity and mandatory-first-call
   restrictions; it is not a general factory existence theorem.

6. Giulio Morina, Krzysztof Latuszynski, Piotr Nayar, Alex Wendland,
   *From the Bernoulli Factory to a Dice Enterprise via Perfect Sampling
   of Markov Chains*, arXiv:1912.09229v2; Annals of Applied Probability32(1),
   327-359,2022.
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.09229>
   The primary abstract was read; HTML full text was unavailable. Rational-
   function factories are constructed using stationary distributions and
   perfect simulation, with exponential tails and uniformly bounded expected
   coin cost. This is close to using invariant laws instead of evaluating
   unknown functions. Reading the abstract cannot establish that our
   bounded physical assembly has no equivalent in this literature.

7. Laszlo Lovasz and Peter Winkler, *Exact Mixing in an Unknown Markov
   Chain*, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics2(1),R15,1995.
   <https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/v2i1r15>
   DOI10.37236/1209. The primary abstract gives a stopping rule for an
   unknown irreducible finite chain with exact stationary output and an
   expected-time bound using maximal mean hitting time. Exact stopping at
   an invariant law without identifying a transition matrix is established.
   The present target is a prescribed visible reweighting, with a hard cap
   and a coarse hidden-state interface, requiring a separate direct proof.

8. Pierre Alquier, Nial Friel, Richard Everitt, Aidan Boland, *Noisy Monte
   Carlo: Convergence of Markov chains with approximate transition kernels*,
   arXiv:1403.5496v3; Statistics and Computing26,29-47,2016.
   <https://arxiv.org/html/1403.5496v3>
   The introduction and guarantee setup were read. The invariant-law
   perturbation bounds use stability assumptions, initially uniform
   ergodicity and extensions. Our phase example explains why small row
   errors alone cannot yield a size/gap-independent observable guarantee.

9. Richard S. Ellis and Charles M. Newman, *The Statistics of Curie-Weiss
   Models*, Journal of Statistical Physics19,149-161,1978; and Richard S.
   Ellis, Charles M. Newman, Jay S. Rosen, *Limit theorems for sums of dependent
   random variables occurring in statistical mechanics*,1980.
   Primary publisher entry for the latter:
   <https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00536186>
   The entry connects Curie-Weiss asymptotics with multiple phases and
   metastability. Our exact small-field limit is proved directly by entropy
   maximization and bounded tilting, without importing an uninspected
   theorem's precise hypotheses.

10. J. W. Pitman, *Occupation measures for Markov chains*, Advances in
    Applied Probability9(1),69-86,1977, DOI10.2307/1425817.
    <https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-applied-probability/article/abs/occupation-measures-for-markov-chains/59E05999FA6DEA0F4D241327144705AC>
    The primary publisher abstract and metadata were read. The needed
    occupation identity is also proved in Aldous and Fill, *Reversible
    Markov Chains and Random Walks on Graphs*, Chapter2, Proposition2.4:
    <https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/users/aldous/RWG/Book_Ralph/Ch2.S2.html>
    If a positive finite-mean stopping time returns its initial distribution
    to itself, mean pre-stop occupation is the native stationary law times
    mean length. Our minimum-cost deduction uses this classical identity
    plus the mandatory initial visit. The bounded compiler's attainment is
    separately established by its explicit occupation calculation.

11. Radford M. Neal, *Slice Sampling*, arXiv:physics/0009028; Annals of
    Statistics31(3),705-767,2003.
    <https://arxiv.org/html/physics/0009028v1>
    The introduction and auxiliary-variable construction were read. A
    uniform auxiliary coordinate under the target density, with its
    conditional uniform refresh, is established slice-sampling structure.
    The governing counter uses a finite subgraph with a different actual
    source transition and a deterministic return cap. The augmentation
    itself is not a new principle.

12. Radford M. Neal, *Non-reversibly updating a uniform [0,1] value for
    Metropolis accept/reject decisions*, arXiv:2001.11950,2020.
    <https://arxiv.org/html/2001.11950v1>
    Primary PDF: <https://glizen.com/radfordneal/ftp/nrevu.pdf>
    Section2 was read. The paper keeps an auxiliary acceptance coordinate,
    applies wrapped nonreversible translations, and rescales that coordinate
    on accepted Metropolis moves. This is a close predecessor for retained
    uniform coordinates and wrapped translations. Its displayed scheme
    still has ordinary Metropolis proposal/rejection structure; the present
    proof separately constructs a bounded first return of the actual native
    operation. This limited comparison does not establish priority for the
    full bounded-return construction.

The search has not established priority for the bounded physical compiler.
Its present status is a self-contained, internally reviewable capability
theorem with explicit resources. Actual holds, spectators, physical return,
factory acceptance and unknown-chain stopping remain legitimate comparators.
