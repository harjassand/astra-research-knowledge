## 1. Literal counter and stationarity

The initial conditional phase distribution is essential and is correctly
specified: C is uniform on0,...,W(x)-1. Uniform on the whole clock would
not generally preserve the intended tilt. The root includes and verifies
that wrong-phase counterexample.

For either fixed direction, the translation by m moduloM preserves uniform
phase. Since the actual native P preserves mu, their product preserves
mu times uniform phase, even if P is irreversible or the visible process
has unbounded predictive rank. The accepted set has mass Z/M and conditional
native marginal W*mu/Z. A positive return to this set has a deterministic
bound because its universal accepted phase strip is0,...,m-1. This proves
the claimed watch kernel with no recurrence hypothesis or infinite series.

The backward direction's cap also holds, including initial phases already
in the universal strip: after the possible initial wrap, successive backward
steps enter that strip within ceil(M/m) total calls. The assumption that
m andM are actual global bounds is needed for the all-path physical cap;
the lower bound uses essential extrema with positive baseline mass as stated.

Refreshing the conditional phase at each macro yields a Markov kernel on
the complete native state. Retaining the old phase as a record does not
prevent the refresh, but the invariant native marginal is not a conditional
invariance claim given that entire old record. The manuscript preserves this
distinction and does not silently treat a measured label as the hidden state.

## 2. Reversal and microscopic parity

For an ordinarily reversible P, the adjoint of the augmented forward
rotation is the same actual P with the opposite counter rotation. Reversal
of a first-return path remains a first-return path, with the same path length.
Conditional phase sampling and forgetting are adjoints under the accepted
measure. Therefore K_+^*=K_-, and their independent equal mixture is
nu-reversible. No actual native adjoint actuator is being invented.

For P^*=Theta P Theta, extend Theta to the augmented space without changing
the counter. Because mu is Theta-invariant and W is Theta-even, the accepted
set and conditional phase kernel commute with this involution. Thus
K_d^*=Theta K_{-d} Theta, and the symmetrized K obeys the claimed generalized
reversal. The statement refers to the symmetrized kernel. It does not assert
ordinary reversibility of an irreversible native process or omit physical
odd variables. The full actual contract, including reversed controls where
required by the supplier, remains a hypothesis.

## 3. Occupation and the lower bounds

The killed augmented block is nilpotent. The stationarity equation in that
block determines the finite expected pre-return occupation exactly. Its
projection gives (M/Z)mu, so the stationary macro mean is M/Z. This conclusion
does not require uniqueness, but its GLOBAL OPTIMALITY argument does.

Under the unique-stationary-law assumption, any causal finite-mean controller
with matching start/end law nu has a finite positive occupation measure
Lambda satisfying LambdaP=Lambda. Normalizing it gives mu; the first native
call contributes at least nu to Lambda. Consequently the mean is at least
esssup(dnu/dmu)=M/Z. This is a correct application of the established
occupation-measure identity. It also explains why ordinary Markov rejection
thinning already attains the same stationary mean.

The unknown-family deadline lower bound is independent of that cost
argument. For P_p=(1-p)I+pQ, a finite endpoint-only controller gives a degree
at mostL polynomial kernel. A single occurrence of Q along the unchanged-
state prefix yields diag(t_x)(Q-I), including self transitions of Q. This
derivative calculation is correct. Invariance on an open unknown-p interval
then forces W(x)t_x constant when Q is irreducible, with1<=t_x<=L. Hence
L>=M/m and the integer ceiling is sharp. A fixed known-p controller,
unmodeled calibration oracle, different native menu, zero-call output,
or continuous between-call observation is outside this statement.

## 4. Physical and consequential scope

The theorem requires a repeatable actual full-state Markov operation with
exact native stationary law, including any bath or apparatus memory that
affects later calls. This structural premise is stronger than a few measured
equilibrium marginals; the manuscript says so. Hidden memory can evolve and
need not mix, but unspecified non-Markov environmental memory cannot be
silently discarded.

All selectors and counters are real resources. Rational weights imply a
finite native-call ceiling, while literal bounded fair-bit/wall-time
implementation is a separate issue. Powers of two provide the advertised
finite selector; variable numbers of bits and reads can remain informative
in the retained controller record. The controller phase is not absolute
laboratory time. A finite native-call cap does not imply a fixed physical
deadline unless all call and decision durations satisfy the supplied
boundary contract.

For local programs, common native stationarity plus preservation of all
other visible coordinates gives the needed conditional-context stationarity.
The local weighted kernels then preserve the same global W*mu law. General
history-selected updates, changing native operations within an excursion,
or state-dependent choices of individually invariant kernels need their own
balance proof; the manuscript does not grant them. Calibration can choose a
structurally valid family member, but the deployment start conditional on
that calibration record is not thereby stationary or prepared.

The visible target is W(y)mu(Y=y), and conserved-sector proportions remain
unchanged. The existing common-visible-baseline/within-sector convergence
condition is a valid sufficient route, while the7/10 versus3/4 example is a
real obstruction when that premise fails. Independent baseline initialization
or replacement remains a legitimate added supplier at its own latency and
locality costs. Protected holds and isolated spectators are also legitimate
alternatives when their full actual contracts are available.

The fixed native path remains unchanged when one P is repeated. The
occupation calculation therefore correctly rules out interpreting its
endpoint tilt as a newly imposed wall-time energy potential. A local network
changes its later actuation schedule, but its macro-index equilibrium still
does not imply arbitrary-wall-time Gibbs occupation, independence from
clock/selector/native-history records, or rapid low-temperature mixing.

## 5. Closest predecessors and honest candidate scope

Andral, Douc, Marival and Robert's2024 Rejection Markov chain, Section3,
already gives exact stationary reweighting by independent endpoint marking
of an arbitrary stationary native Markov chain. Its augmented uniform and
watched-set proof is explicit. Thus neither unknown-kinetic invariant-law
control nor auxiliary watched-set reasoning is new by itself.

Neal's2020 Section2 explicitly uses a wrapped translation of an acceptance
uniform; slice augmentation is older. In those reviewed algorithms the
native/simulated proposal can be rejected while retaining the previous
configuration, and the auxiliary update is tailored to that Metropolis
kernel. They do not state the root's arbitrary-native-kernel, actual-current-
state, mandatory-positive-call first-return compiler or its matched cap.
Andral's finite replication counts concern repeated stored outputs and do
not by themselves provide a positive-native-call physical operation for
each repetition. These differences are substantive at the declared interface.

Focused searches and actual passages have not established that the exact
finite counter assembly or its simultaneous cap/mean attainability already
appears elsewhere. That is a bounded comparison, not evidence of historical
priority. The physical theorem is an application and particular synthesis
of established trace, auxiliary-variable, and occupation machinery. Its
candidate addition should remain exactly the bounded assembly and scoped
capability, without an apparatus, thermodynamic advantage, or broad new
sampling-principle claim.
