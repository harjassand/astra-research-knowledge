## Scientific decision

The previous large calibration made full-record accuracy expensive and its
long-record bound vacuous. The completed contractive ring supplied a useful
finite-data objective but did not establish a broad interacting physical
capability. I changed the next objective to a prescribed invariant law of
actual operation endpoints. Unknown kinetic detail is allowed to survive.
This permits strong interacting factor laws beyond a contraction regime,
without demanding an exact finite predictive representation.

The strongest ordinary route changed the comparison. Independent endpoint
thinning already gives the desired stationary reweighting, including for
nonreversible hidden kernels. The claim cannot be stationary reweighting
itself. The new candidate is a bounded implementation on the current physical
state, with a sharp universal native-call cap and optimal stationary mean
under the stated uniqueness premise. It remains an endpoint operation, not
a change of one native kernel's clock-time equilibrium.

## Useful failures that constrain the consequence

- Changing a probability ratio does not produce a known transition speed.
  The signed-continuation obstruction remains intact.
- A deterministic return horizon does not replace the residual selector:
  the preserved binary fixture gives 175/202 rather than the intended 4/5.
- Starting the counter uniformly over every phase rather than conditionally
  over the current accepted phases gives 499/674 rather than 4/5 in the
  preserved exact fixture.
- Unknown frozen-sector weights cannot generally be repaired. The unequal
  baseline-sector example gives visible probability 7/10 instead of 3/4.
  The diffusion receiver supplies a common sectorwise visible baseline to
  resolve precisely that issue, without mixing the conserved parameter.
- Binary rejection-and-return works even with evolving hidden memory. An
  attempted objection based only on that memory was wrong. Its naive
  multilabel extension fails, as the exact physical-helper counterexample
  records.
- Finite local row accuracy is not a general long-time phase-observable
  guarantee. A coherent Curie–Weiss field of order 1/n has vanishing local
  row error and a nonvanishing magnetization limit. The error can be
  conservative, not merely an inconsistent collection of edge biases.
- Exact target invariance can coexist with infinite predictive rank. Hidden
  predictive information has not been erased, copied, or made unnecessary
  for every later observable.
- Mean native-call cost below two does not bound preparation time or rare
  path overhead. Repeating a single native kernel still has its native
  occupation law in its own clock.

## What remains open

The completed result is a bounded stopped-state control principle with a
continuous mathematical receiver. Its next substantive frontier is a real
compatible activation, isolation, sensing, and boundary-timing interface,
or a consumer for which bounded completion and structural target precision
change a consequential computation. A wall-time equilibrium claim needs a
separate actual controller analysis. Another easy fixture or a tighter
constant would not close those connections.
