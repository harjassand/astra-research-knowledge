The warm-controller argument has passed its first adversarial checks. A positive, bounded quantum gain gives the same worst-case photon-presence read efficiency as scalar gain uncertainty, even when the controller’s internal dynamics is fast and noncommuting. The guarantee covers detection, not faithful transfer of an arbitrary quantum state.

I also found a counterexample to a broader claim: number conservation alone is insufficient, because a pure auxiliary register can concentrate thermal excitations into the detector. The collective-spin symmetry supplies the missing constraint.
