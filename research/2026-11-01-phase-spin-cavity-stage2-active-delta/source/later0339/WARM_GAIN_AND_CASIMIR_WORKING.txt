A. Positive quantum gain gives photon-presence transfer without resetting it.

Two bosonic modes a (read cavity) and b (selected bright mode), a initially
vacuum. Source E may be arbitrarily correlated with b, dark modes and a
reference. Finite-dimensional E is enough; assume valid piecewise-smooth
propagators. During the ideal closed read,
 H(t)=I_ab tensor H_E(t)+(a^dag b+b^dag a) tensor G_E(t),
 (1-delta)g(t) I <=G_E(t)<=(1+delta)g(t) I,
 g(t)>=0, int g(t)dt=pi/2, 0<=delta<1.
There is NO bound on the speed or norm of H_E in the claimed probability
inequality, aside from existence; G and H_E need not commute at any times.

Let J=a^dag b+b^dag a=N_+-N_-, c_+=(a+b)/sqrt2,
c_-=(a-b)/sqrt2. On J eigenvalue j the source propagator is V_j for H_E+jG.
For R_j=V_j^dag V_(j+2), differentiation gives
 Rdot_j=-2i [V_j^dag G V_j] R_j.
Every continuously unwrapped eigenphase of R_j has derivative between the
extreme eigenvalues 2(1+-delta)g(t). (First prove for nondegenerate smooth
matrices by eigenvector differentiation, then approximate/remove degeneracy.)
At the end all phases lie in [pi(1-delta),pi(1+delta)]. Hence
 ||V_j+V_(j+2)|| <=2 sin(pi delta/2), uniformly in integer j and source E.

For input |0>_a |m>_b, the amplitude to finish again with a vacuum is
 A_m=2^-m sum_(k=0)^m binom(m,k) V_(2k-m).
For m>=1, Pascal's identity rewrites this as a convex combination of
 (V_j+V_(j+2))/2. Therefore ||A_m||<=sin(pi delta/2) UNIFORMLY in m>=1.
Total photon number conservation makes the no-transfer amplitude diagonal
in m; the bound works with arbitrary source/reference correlations and
arbitrary superpositions of bright-mode photon number. Consequently
 Pr(a_out nonvac)>=cos^2(pi delta/2) Pr(b_in nonvac).
This does not control output phase, coherences between photon sectors, or
the final joint source state. Do NOT call it a general quantum SWAP.

For an arbitrary positive gain interval g_-,g_+, choose the central pulse
duration/area with 2int average(g_-,g_+)=pi. The efficiency can be written
sin^2[pi*g_-/(g_-+g_+)] for constant proportional gain bounds. Positivity is
essential to the proof; G=Z and rapidly rotating source H_E=Omega X can
average the read interaction away. This is a useful failure control.

B. Source-specific physical spin-to-boson comparison.

Spin bank in the 0/1 oscillator subspace P, read cavity a, uniform bright
mode b=sum_i b_i/sqrt(ell), and actual collective spin interaction
 HQ=(a^dag sum_i sigma_i^-/sqrt(ell)+h.c.) tensor G_E(t).
The corresponding bosonic HB agrees with HQ on P except for creation of
a double occupancy at one site. Distinct doubled sites are orthogonal.
On total photon/excitation m,
 ||Q HB P||^2 <=2 ||G||^2/ell n_c N_spin
 <=||G||^2/ell m(m-1).
Thus Duhamel, with arbitrary source and reference, bounds the purified
vector difference after the pulse by
 epsilon_sat <=Theta_plus/sqrt(ell) sqrt(E[N(N-1)]),
 Theta_plus=int||G_E(t)||dt <=(1+delta)pi/2.
Using an outcome AMPLITUDE bound is important:
 sqrt(P_actual)>=sqrt(P_ideal)-epsilon_sat,
rather than subtracting a whole-state trace error from a rare probability.

For an inhomogeneous actual coupling (1+epsilon_i)/sqrt(ell),
|epsilon_i|<=epsilon_shape, the perturbing Hamiltonian has charge-m sector
norm <=||G|| epsilon_shape m (compress the bosonic one-particle star).
Thus epsilon_shape_state <=epsilon_shape Theta_plus sqrt(E[N^2]).
A bounded cavity detuning contributes epsilon_det<=Delta_max T sqrt(E[N^2]).
Only true symmetry-breaking terms enter the false-event comparison; arbitrary
common cavity detuning commutes the Casimir and does not create accessible
spin charge. Keep gain uncertainty delta distinct from shape error.

C. Casimir charge prevents passive thermal false clicks even with arbitrary
returning quantum controllers, without a source-purity assumption.

For ell spins define S_z=N_spin-ell/2 and
 Jop=sqrt(S^2+1/4)-1/2 (spin length, eigenvalue j),
 K=N_c+S_z+Jop >=N_c>=0.
Any Hamiltonian built collectively from S_+,S_-,S_z,a and arbitrary quantum
controllers, commuting total N=N_c+N_spin and S^2, conserves K. This includes
the positive quantum-gain read with arbitrary H_E. Controllers can be correlated
with the spins/reference and do not need resetting. In contrast, number
conservation ALONE is insufficient with a pure mutable entropy register.

For identical thermal spins with excitation p<1/2, q=p/(1-p), conditional on
each j the excess k=m+j has a truncated geometric distribution q^k. Hence
 E[K_spin]<=q/(1-q)=p/(1-2p), independent of ell.
For unequal populations p_i in [pmin,pbar], product diagonals are dominated
by the pbar product with factor
 R<=exp[ell(pbar-pmin)/(1-pbar)]. Therefore
 E[K_initial]<=nu_c+R*pbar/(1-2pbar).
This depends only on the actual spin/cavity marginal, not controller purity.

Homogeneous independent cold spin lowering cannot increase K. Proof: a
single sigma_i^- has angular-momentum selection j' in {j-1,j,j+1}, m'=m-1,
so k'<=k. The SUM over identical local lowering channels is permutation-
covariant and Sz-preserving, hence diagonal in j,m and scalar on multiplicity;
its adjoint applied to K is <=0. Cavity loss also lowers K.
Homogeneous spin raising has L_up^* K <=2 ell gamma_up.

For local dephasing L_i=sqrt(2Gamma_phi)n_i, the adjoint semigroup is unital,
leaves Sz fixed and satisfies
 T_t(S^2)=S^2+(1-exp(-2Gamma_phi t)) [ell/2-S^2+Sz^2].
Operator concavity of sqrt gives an upper bound for T_t(Jop). The quantity
inside the sqrt commutes with S^2,Sz. On Ns<=ell/4 its generator implies
 L_phi^* K <=4Gamma_phi Ns/ell.
(Use j>=|m|, numerator ell/2-j(j+1)+m^2<=Ns and denominator 2j+1>=ell-2Ns+1.)

Split the initial number distribution at N<=ell/4. Before any thermal upward
jump, N cannot increase, for ANY collective controller. On this subspace,
 E[K(T)]<=E[K(0)]+4Gamma_phi T E[N(0)]/ell.
The probability of a first upward event is bounded by
 delta_up<=T[kappa_up(E[N0]+1)+ell gamma_up].
The initial high-number tail can be bounded using product thermal spin
marginal and independent thermal cavity:
 delta_high <=(8e pbar)^(ell/8)+(nu_c/(1+nu_c))^(ell/8),
when ell is divisible by 8 and pbar<1/(8e).
Thus a simple structural bound is
 P_cavity_nonvac <=nu_c+R*pbar/(1-2pbar)
             +4Gamma_phi T E[N0]/ell +delta_up+delta_high.
Local dephasing enters with DENSITY p, rather than total excitations ell p.
Need verify the unital-Jensen generator argument and selection-rule argument
with full finite matrices; this is new solo mathematics, not yet an audit.

Uniform gain/coupling or exact permutation structure is important. With shape
error, compare actual to symmetric no-up dilation at amplitude level:
 P_actual <=(sqrt(P_symmetric)+epsilon_shape_state)^2+delta_up+delta_high.
Avoid counting delta_up/high twice if already inside P_symmetric. Common
detuning does not enter false-side epsilon. Dephasing/loss rates here are
homogeneous; generic local rate disorder needs a separate perturbation bound.

D. Actual no-jump read lower bound, with all loss/dephasing counted.

Kjump=sum L^dag L <=r N+s,
r=max(kappa_down+kappa_up,gamma_down+2Gamma_phi),
s=kappa_up+ell gamma_up (slightly overbounds the spin-up coefficient).
The no-jump propagator differs from the same closed Hamiltonian by vector norm
 epsilon_noise<=T/2 sqrt[r^2 E N^2+2rs E N+s^2].
All jump branches have nonnegative acceptance probability. Therefore a lower
bound on actual accepted photon probability is obtained by squaring
 max(0,sqrt(P_ideal)-epsilon_sat-epsilon_shape-epsilon_det-epsilon_noise).
There is no sqrt(probability of ANY loss) penalty on the accepted branch.
This holds with source/reference correlations because all generators preserve
N between jumps and the comparison is done on fixed input charge sectors.
