2. Remove the source cap and compare all classical-record parallel probes

Use ordinary quantum illumination: H0 is target-present thermal attenuator
Phi_(eta,b), H1 is the replacer tau_b, where b>0 is output background photons.
Let L be ANY retained classical label, of arbitrary alphabet, with the same
source-preparation distribution under both hypotheses. Conditional on L the
transmitted K-mode state can be arbitrary, non-Gaussian and entangled across
sent modes. No input-correlated quantum memory is retained by this comparator.
The expected TOTAL sent mean photons is E. The final measurement can be any
collective output-plus-classical-record measurement, with source-independent
ancillas. This is the parallel task, not a claim about every adaptive protocol.

The established multimode Gaussian minimum-output-entropy theorem says
 S(Phi^K(rho))>=K g(b), g(b)=(b+1)ln(b+1)-b ln b.
Since log tau_b is affine in photon number,
 D(Phi^K(rho)||tau_b^K)<=eta ln(1+1/b) E(rho).
Average over L. Binary data processing then yields, for miss alpha<=eps,
 log beta>=-[eta ln(1+1/b) E+ln 2]/(1-eps).
The exact source-information cap and He's stronger interpolation theorem
are unnecessary for this converse. Coherent-state optimality without a
quantum memory in this relative-entropy task is established prior; see
Giovannetti-Holevo-Mari 1405.4066 and De Palma-Borregaard 1802.02158.

4. A structural false-count bound with arbitrary quantum controllers

For ell two-level spins set Sz=Ns-ell/2,
 Jop=sqrt(S^2+1/4)-1/2, K=Nc+Sz+Jop>=Nc.
Any collective Hamiltonian commuting both total N=Nc+Ns and S^2 conserves K,
even with mutable quantum controllers of arbitrary purity and correlations.
For product thermal spins of common excitation p<1/2, each spin-j sector has
excess k=m+j distributed as a truncated geometric with q=p/(1-p). Therefore
 E K_spin<=p/(1-2p), independent of ell.
For p_i in [pmin,pbar], operator domination of diagonal products costs at most
 R=exp[ell(pbar-pmin)/(1-pbar)], giving E K<=nu_c+R pbar/(1-2pbar).

Homogeneous cold local spin lowering cannot increase K: its angular-momentum
selection rules have j'<=j+1,m'=m-1, hence k'<=k; the sum is permutation
covariant and diagonal in j,m. Cavity lowering decreases K. Homogeneous
dephasing L_i=sqrt(2Gamma_phi)n_i obeys the exact identity
 T_t(S^2)=S^2+(1-exp(-2Gamma_phi t))(ell/2-S^2+Sz^2).
Unital operator Jensen for sqrt then gives, on Ns<=ell/4,
 L_phi^* K<=4Gamma_phi Ns/ell.

Before the first upward event, N cannot grow. Split initial N at ell/4.
With initial M=E N, a conservative nonvacuum bound after time T is
 nu_c+R pbar/(1-2pbar)+4Gamma_phi T M/ell
 +T[kappa_up(M+1)+ell gamma_up]+delta_high,
where delta_high can be bounded by
 (8e pbar)^(ell/8)+(nu_c/(1+nu_c))^(ell/8)
for ell divisible by eight and pbar<1/(8e).
These are actual per-block conditional physical probabilities. Uniformity and
the preserved collective symmetry are assumptions, not a generic noise claim.
A coupling-shape perturbation of relative norm epsilon adds an amplitude
charge epsilon*integral||G||*sqrt(E N^2), inside the squared probability bound.

The previous reduced Gibbs-state proof cannot simply trace an arbitrary pure
mutable source. Explicit counterexample: ell=4 thermal spins with p=.01,
vacuum cavity and a pure five-state zero-charge controller. A total-number-
preserving unitary routes each distinct one-excited spin to a cavity photon
and records its position in the controller. False clicks are .03881196,
above p/(1-2p)=.01020408. It breaks the collective Casimir. Number conservation
alone does not prohibit entropy-assisted thermal concentration.
warm_gain_controls.py verifies the counterexample and full-spin generator
inequalities through ell=8. The bright/dark decomposition and dilute dark-to-
bright dephasing scaling are established prior (Shammah et al. 1704.07066).
The exact finite operator/controller extension is a solo scoped derivation.

5. Direct Lambda read: keep branching emission rather than eliminate it

There are ell identical atoms with levels g,s,e and one read cavity. Work
first in the EXACT zero/one-excitation manifold (vacuum, |s_i>,|e_i>,|c>),
with excitation Ns+Ne+Nc. Uniform finite control rotates s_i<->e_i; the cavity
couples the bright |B_e>=sum_i|e_i>/sqrt(ell) to |c> at g*sqrt(ell).
Common quantum operator gains and noncommuting source dynamics are retained.
No Raman effective Hamiltonian or effective loss substitution is made.

Define the accessible-position projector
 Q=|B_s><B_s|+|B_e><B_e|+|c><c|.
It commutes with both uniform Hamiltonians for ANY quantum source. For local
spontaneous branch L_i=sqrt(Gamma_es)|s_i><e_i|, exactly
 sum_i D^*_(L_i)(Q)=Gamma_es [P_e/ell-|B_e><B_e|].
For e->g emission the drift is -Gamma_eg |B_e><B_e|<=0.
For local position dephasing L_i=sqrt(2Gamma_phi)(|s_i><s_i|+|e_i><e_i|),
 the drift is 2Gamma_phi[(P_s+P_e)/ell
                        -|B_s><B_s|-|B_e><B_e|].
Cold s->g loss and cavity loss also decrease Q. Thus
 d E Q/dt <=(Gamma_es+2Gamma_phi)/ell in this manifold.

An initial uniformly incoherent single s excitation has E Q=1/ell, and so
P(cavity nonvac)<= [1+(Gamma_es+2Gamma_phi)T]/ell, uniformly in the source.
For product thermal spins p with no e excitation, sector one has probability
ell*p*(1-p)^(ell-1). Sector zero cannot produce a photon. Paying every
multi-excitation sector at worst one gives the full physical per-block bound

 P_false<=p(1-p)^(ell-1)[1+(Gamma_es+2Gamma_phi)T]
          +ell(ell-1)p^2/2+P(first upward event)+P(initial cavity nonvac).

No claim about dynamics in the other sectors is used. Their real probability
is charged in EVERY block, not as a once-per-experiment modelling failure.
Uniform spin/source correlations inside sector one do not weaken the Q drift
identity; it is an operator inequality, not an average over classical gain.

For a real coherent input, the exact initial bright-sector-one weight for
identical qubits with excited population p and coherence s is
 p(1-p)^(ell-1)+(ell-1)|s|^2(1-p)^(ell-2).
A uniform warm-gain s->e pulse transfers at least lambda_pulse of that bright
weight, and the subsequent cavity pulse at least lambda_read. Additional
no-jump damping is charged at amplitude level with rate
 max(Gamma_eg+Gamma_es+2Gamma_phi, gamma_sg+2Gamma_phi,kappa).
lambda_read_controls.py verifies the exact projector identities and evolves
the complete one-excitation+vacuum source-retaining GKSL dynamics for ell=2,4,8.

This construction confronts branching/spontaneous noise directly. It does
not yet solve the multiple-excitation optical receiver: with M=ell*p fixed,
the paid multi-excitation term is approximately M^2/2. Suppressing it by
making M smaller increases the number of blocks and detector exposure. A
full higher-excitation SU(3)/Young-sector extension is not proved here.
The control/cavity coefficients still need one compatible microscopic
platform, and the source/capture step is separate. Existing Lambda/Gaussian
ensemble memories use this same collective enhancement; no superiority over
them has been derived. This remains a physical bridge, not a mission win.
