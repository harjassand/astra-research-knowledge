QUANTUM CONTROL OF PHOTON COUNTS AND DESTRUCTIVE ENCODED READOUT
1 November 2026. New solo derivation with executable self-checks.
Independent audit NOT_PERFORMED. Historical novelty UNKNOWN.

The result concerns the measured observable and its fault sets. It does not
assert a calibrated bosonic SWAP, a small diamond distance for the whole
channel, preservation of a controller, or a complete quantum computer.

1. Exact exchange model and a sharper assumption than static gain noise

There are disjoint bosonic receiver/signal pairs (a_i,b_i). Every a_i starts
in vacuum. The signals, a finite quantum controller E, and a reference R may
have arbitrary correlations and arbitrary photon-number superpositions.
The closed read Hamiltonian, with hbar=1, is

 H(t)=I tensor H_E(t)+sum_i J_i tensor G_i(t),
 J_i=a_i^dag b_i+b_i^dag a_i.

All source propagators exist; for the core finite-E theorem, piecewise
continuous finite Hermitian matrices suffice. The operators G_i, G_j and
H_E need not commute. No estimate contains the norm, speed, temperature,
entropy, correlation length or initial preparation error of H_E or E.
This is NOT permission to omit other terms in an actual device Hamiltonian.

Choose real scalar reference pulses g_i with integral pi/2. Let

 A_i=integral ||G_i(t)-g_i(t) I|| dt < pi/2,
 r_i=sin(A_i), lambda_i=1-r_i^2.

A convenient physical sufficient condition is
 (1-delta_i)g_i I <=G_i<=(1+delta_i)g_i I,
 g_i>=0, integral g_i=pi/2, 0<=delta_i<1.
It gives A_i<=pi delta_i/2 and lambda_i>=cos^2(pi delta_i/2).
The integrated-deviation theorem itself does not require pointwise positive
gain. Short sign excursions can be charged in A_i. A positive interval is a
sufficient qualification, not a universally necessary condition.

The reference pulse is a finite control resource. Timing, ramps, bounded
waveform error and known scalar offsets must fit its integrated-deviation
budget. This theorem does not construct an autonomous timing device.

2. Finite differences of the full quantum source

First take one pair, fixed input |0>_a |m>_b. Normal modes
 c_+=(a+b)/sqrt(2), c_-=(a-b)/sqrt(2)
diagonalize J=N_+-N_-. On J eigenvalue j the full source propagator is V_j,
generated by H_E+jG. The exact receiver-vacuum amplitude on E is

 A_m^vac=2^-m sum_(k=0)^m binom(m,k) V_(2k-m).                 (1)

This identity keeps the controller as an operator. Replacing V_j by scalar
random phases would be an additional, unjustified restriction.

For complex z define W_z=exp(i z pi/2)V_z. It is the source propagator for
H_E+z(G-gI). It is entire, unitary on the real axis, and for z=x+iy

 ||W_(x+iy)|| <=exp(A |y|).                                  (2)

Indeed the Hermitian real-x generator cannot increase a vector norm; the
Hermitian part of the nonunitary evolution is bounded by |y| ||G-gI||.
Integrate this differential norm bound. Finite-dimensional Dyson series give
entireness. Nothing in this step bounds H_E or freezes source backaction.

The classical Bernstein finite-difference inequality, including its
Banach-valued version, is the imported mathematical tool:

 ||Delta_h^m F||_(infinity,R) <=[2 sin(A |h|/2)]^m
                             ||F||_(infinity,R), A|h|<=pi.   (3)

Primary source: Aleksandrov and Peller, arXiv:1003.3982v1, section 5.1,
equations (5.2),(5.3) and Lemma 5.1, PDF pp.17-18. This inequality and its
sharp constant are PRIOR mathematics, not discovered here.

Substituting V_j=exp(-ij pi/2)W_j turns the binomial sum (1), up to a scalar
phase, into 2^-m Delta_2^m W(-m). Therefore

 ||A_m^vac|| <= r^m.                                        (4)

For several mode pairs, use source propagators V_(z_1,...,z_s) of
H_E+sum_i z_i G_i and remove exp(-i sum_i z_i pi/2). The result is unitary
on real vectors and has norm <=exp(sum_i A_i |Im z_i|). Apply (3) one
coordinate at a time. Each finite difference preserves the other coordinate
types and its uniform real-axis bound. This gives

 ||A_(m_1,...,m_s)^vac|| <=product_i r_i^m_i.                 (5)

The G_i may share the same source and need not commute. The reads can overlap
completely in time. Source-only CPTP interventions also fit after finite
Stinespring dilation: intervening source unitaries preserve the exponential
type bounds. This extension will be used only with explicit fresh bath ports.

3. All photon counts, collection loss, and reference-uniform effects

For a fixed input photon number m, the two-mode bosonic sector is the symmetric
subspace of m virtual two-level particles, each recording a versus b. Its
Hamiltonian is (sum X_particle) tensor G+H_E. These virtual particles are a
proof device; no individually labelled-photon apparatus is supplied.

Pick k virtual photons. Move the other m-k photons into E. Their interactions
become part of an arbitrary source Hamiltonian, and G retains its operator
interval. Equation (4) now says that the probability all selected k photons
remain untransferred is <=r^(2k), as an operator effect on the original E.
The multivariable proof gives the product bound for selected photons from
several different signal modes. Source/reference correlations are retained.

Let t in [0,1]. In this virtual representation,
 t^(N_a)=product_particle [t+(1-t) P_untransferred,particle].
All coefficients in this expansion are nonnegative. Apply the subset bounds:

 <t^(N_a)> <=[t+(1-t)r^2]^m.

After independent vacuum collection loss of transmission eta, photon-count
generating parameter z corresponds to t=1-eta(1-z). Thus, for arbitrary input,

 Tr[rho_out product_i z_i^(N_detected,i)]
 <=Tr[rho_in product_i (1-eta_i lambda_i(1-z_i))^(N_bi)],
                    0<=z_i<=1.                             (6)

Equivalently the pulled-back measurement effect is bounded by the operator
on the right tensor I_E,R. The effect is diagonal in each input total charge:
the dynamics and measured photon numbers conserve that charge. Pinching input
charge sectors therefore changes no probability, proving (6) also for
superpositions of photon number, arbitrary input entanglement and arbitrary
correlations with E,R. A finite photon cutoff followed by monotone limits
extends the fixed-charge argument to all trace-class inputs; no mean energy
bound is required for this bounded observable.

At z_i=0 this is an exponential no-detection bound. Differentiating the
positive subset expansion also bounds all factorial moments of the number
of untransferred virtual photons. Equation (6) is a generating-function and
effect comparison, not a claim of stochastic ordering for every count event.
Markov applied to z^N for 0<z<1 gives a valid lower-count tail bound.

The constant is sharp for every photon number and all these generating
functions: choose G_i=(g_i+Delta_i)I with nonnegative Delta_i of integral A_i,
and any H_E. The signal then undergoes an ordinary scalar beamsplitter with
transmission cos^2 A_i. Its independent collection loss saturates (6).
No improvement is claimed over arbitrary composite-pulse control protocols.

Two limitations are witnessed explicitly in warm_gain_controls.json:
 * G=Z, H_E=100X and T=pi/2 can almost eliminate one-photon transfer.
   There is no qualifying small A around the central pi/2 pulse.
 * For scalar relative gain error .1, a 1000-photon vacuum/Fock coherence
   transfer factor is about 4.17e-6, despite excellent nonvacuum detection.
   The full channel is not a uniform high-fidelity quantum SWAP.
Also G=sqrt(N_pump) on an unrestricted quantum pump has a zero eigenvalue
and no bounded positive interval. An arbitrary thermal pump does not satisfy
the theorem. The identity part of an engineered coupler, fresh receiver
vacuum, timing and energy supply have not been removed as resources.

4. A complete destructive dual-rail instrument

Encode a physical bit as one excitation in data rails q_0,q_1. Read each into
its own initially empty receiver rail r_0,r_1. A correct single detected rail
is labelled 0 or 1; no click or two clicks are flagged erasure.
For a fixed bit l, the single-excitation Hamiltonian reduces to X tensor G_l
plus H_E. Write its source operators

 A_l=(V_+ + V_-)/2, B_l=(V_+ - V_-)/2.

Then A_l^dag A_l+B_l^dag B_l=I, ||A_l||^2<=r^2. With collection eta the
fine branches are sqrt(eta)B_l (correct click), A_l (untransferred data),
and sqrt(1-eta)B_l (lost photon). Residual data and loss ports are discarded.
Their rail labels are orthogonal, so the full source-retaining instrument
dephases the physical Z basis and has exactly the correct labelled outcome
or a flagged erasure, including arbitrary input/source/reference correlations.

The flag effect is <=p I with p=1-eta cos^2(pi delta/2).
For simultaneous reads, (5),(6) give every erasure-subset bound p^|S|, even
when all controllers are correlated or the same controller couples to all
reads. Sequential reuse also works by the single-read effect bound at every
actual preceding history. No controller restoration is part of the target.
This is destructive Z measurement, not a nondemolition unknown-state transfer.

encoded_readout_controls.py computes a full logical-plus-source Choi matrix
by carrying one source coherently through seven reads: 16*3^7=34,992 Kraus
histories, with two input logical dimensions and two source dimensions. It
checks trace preservation, positivity and the accepted-wrong-label blocks.
This is a finite control on the analytic statement, not an independent audit.

5. Compatible finite local dynamics with thermal errors

Here is an explicit effective open-system model, not a qualified material.
Each raw cell contains four hard-core two-level sites q_0,q_1,r_0,r_1. Its
controller E_i is a fifth two-level site. There are no bosonic truncations in
this construction. The read Hamiltonian is

 H=H_E+sum_(i,l) (q_il^dag r_il+r_il^dag q_il) tensor G_il,
 G_il=I+delta n_il dot sigma_Ei, |n_il|=1, T=pi/2.

The identity exchange is two-local; each source-dependent term is three-local.
Choose H_E=sum_i Omega_i X_Ei+J sum_i Z_Ei Z_E(i+1) for an explicit bounded-
degree, constant-width local example. This source network may start in ANY
state, including states correlated with the data and with other controllers.
Its noncommuting dynamics are included, rather than calibrated away.
All reads run during the same finite interval. Each cell's total charge N_i
is conserved by H, even though the source can correlate their read dynamics.

The supplied local Markov generator has, on EACH of the four sites,
 L_down=sqrt(gamma_down) sigma_minus,
 L_up=sqrt(gamma_up) sigma_plus,
 L_phi=sqrt(2 Gamma_phi) n.
Set gamma_up/gamma_down=p_R/(1-p_R). Each receiver starts independently in
the actual thermal mixture diag(1-p_R,p_R). Its cold preparation is a resource.
This is local detailed balance for the specified driven thermal ports, not
a claim of a Gibbs state for the whole interacting device. A microscopic
fabrication/reservoir derivation and drive/refrigeration work remain open.
The final detector has transmission eta and independent per-rail false event
probability d; its qualification is also an input, not inferred from this model.

Uniform cold loss or dephasing preserves the initially occupied rail label.
With no heating, initially empty wrong rail and no detector false event it can
therefore cause only a correct result or an erasure. Wrong labels require a
bad thermal receiver preparation, a local upward event, or a detector event.
No generic quantum fault is silently relabelled as erasure.

For ONE cell, the no-jump generator on its single-excitation sector has scalar
attenuation gamma_down+2Gamma_phi+3gamma_up. Thus a single-cell correct-label
effect lower bound is

 s_single=(1-p_R)^2 eta (1-d)^2 exp[-(gamma_down+2Gamma_phi
                                      +3gamma_up)T] lambda.

The wrong-label effect is at most 2p_R+4gamma_up T+2d by first-event bounds.
thermal_readout_controls.py evolves the full 4-spin+source GKSL generator,
extracts its 24x24 source-retaining Choi matrix, and checks these inequalities.
All source input states and a reference are covered by the matrix effects;
the numerical example does not sample just one favourable controller state.

The parallel joint bounds need more than these single-cell marginals.
The following explicit construction supplies them at somewhat worse constants.

Every input density operator commutes with every cell total N_i (the data has
one excitation; the receivers are thermal mixtures). H and the local noise
preserve this charge-block property, including arbitrary data/source entanglement.
For the cold/phase dissipator, K=sum L^dag L=(gamma_down+2Gamma_phi)N_i.
Take R_0=4(gamma_down+2Gamma_phi), and add a null jump sqrt(R_0 I-K).
On the invariant charge-block class, its jump map has trace one after division
by R_0, and R_0(JumpMap-Identity) is EXACTLY the original GKSL dissipator:
the null term equals (R_0 I-K)rho because [rho,N_i]=0.
For upward noise use R_u=4gamma_up and null jump sqrt(gamma_up N_i).

Consequently these specified dynamics have a representation by independent
classical Poisson clocks at rates R_0,R_u for each cell, with local CPTP maps
at their marks. This is exact on this invariant input class. It is NOT a
general Poisson representation for arbitrary non-charge-diagonal quantum states.
The null jumps are a mathematical noise representation, not additional physical
detectors or operations. The residual identity is checked by full finite matrices.

Mark all upward-clock events, thermal receiver defects and detector false
events as possibly wrong. Mark cold-clock events as possibly erased. These
marks are independent classical resources across cells, regardless of E.
For any fixed clocks at other cells, their CPTP maps can be dilated into the
source of the quiet cells. The multivariable theorem still applies uniformly.
It is crucial to condition on the independent clock marks, NOT postselect
unknown Kraus outcomes and then assume the source is unitary.

Let lambda=cos^2(pi delta/2). A rigorous joint local-stochastic pair of bounds is

 w=1-(1-p_R)^2(1-d)^2 exp(-R_u T),
 e=1-(1-p_R)^2(1-d)^2 exp[-(R_0+R_u)T] eta lambda.           (7)

For disjoint specified location sets S_e,S_w,
 P(all S_e flagged, all S_w wrong) <=e^|S_e| w^|S_w|.        (8)
To see this, replace wrong events by their independent bad marks. For flagged
locations expand over marked versus quiet cells. The quiet subset has joint
miss bound (1-eta lambda)^k by (6), uniformly in all other source/noise paths.
The expansion sums to (7),(8). No iid assumption on the actual measurement
outcomes, no spatial independence of quantum controllers, and no tiny additive
whole-device model-failure term enter this argument.

Additional cross-rail coherent terms, nonuniform unqualified baths, correlated
detector faults or source couplings outside the stated exchange form are not
included. They need their own conditional local bounds. A rare common violation
of the gain interval would not be suppressed by this code.

6. Standard Steane decoding supplies accuracy, not a new code

Let C0 be the eight words generated by 1111000,1100110,1010101, and
C1=C0 xor 1111111. The encoded basis is the uniform superposition over C0 or
C1. Physical Z measurements followed by nearest-codeword classical decoding
implement destructive logical Z measurement. A tie between logical labels flags.

An erasure-only pattern is logically ambiguous exactly when it contains one
of the seven weight-three supports of C1 (the Fano triples). Every one-error
pattern without erasure is correctable; every two-erasure pattern without
error is correctable. Any additional bad outcome has either a pair of wrong
locations or a wrong location paired with an erased one. Thus the union bound
and (8) give, for each concatenation level,

 e_next <=7 e^3+42 e w+21 w^2,
 w_next <=       42 e w+21 w^2.                             (9)

Witness sets for disjoint encoded blocks are disjoint, so the same joint bound
recurses despite arbitrary quantum source correlations. The raw instrument is
diagonal in physical Z and all coding words obey the classical decoding facts,
so the argument includes an encoded input entangled with a reference. The
controller may be disturbed. After discarding it, accepted labelled reference
outputs approach the ideal logical measurement as the error and flag bounds
vanish; preservation of the final controller-reference joint state is not promised.

This code structure and erasure correction are standard incumbents. The new
claimed derivation is the physical, source-operator joint bound that supplies
their correlated-noise input. The encoder is not built in this construction.

Take fixed dimensionless parameters
 delta=.1, eta=.95, p_R=1e-5, gamma_down=.002,
 Gamma_phi=.001, gamma_up=gamma_down*p_R/(1-p_R), d=1e-6,
and one simultaneous pulse T=pi/2. Receiver thermal beta*energy=ln(99999).
Equations (7)-(9) give raw e<.096270,w<2.2126e-5.
At level 1, f=e+w<.01. For f<=1/7, (9) implies f_next<=43 f^2.
Hence all later bounds tend doubly exponentially to zero, with N=7^level
data dual-rail qubits and constant simultaneous observation duration.
The worst general mixed-fault overhead is polylogarithmic in inverse error,
with exponent log(7)/log(2). No encoded-input acquisition cost is included.

Outward 80-digit interval arithmetic certifies the illustrative statements:
 level 3: N=343, each of flag/wrong <3.801e-8;
 level 4: N=2401, each <9.1e-14.
The level-4 local-spin implementation has 12,005 two-level sites including
controllers, 4,802 fresh thermal receiver preparations, and 4,802 detection
ports, besides baths, clock/control, encoding, routing and classical decoding.
This is a conditional model theorem, not a measured error rate or device budget.

7. What was and was not obtained

The model regime is arbitrary noncommuting, globally correlated,
unreset quantum control sources with fixed bounded exchange uncertainty,
while useful measured error sets still obey multiplicative bounds. The proof
uses an observable and an exact selection rule instead of whole-channel fidelity.
The one-pulse worst count statistics are already attained by scalar gain error.

This does not show that such a source is preferable to removing the noise,
or that an ideal/classical controller is costly in any actual application.
It supplies no novel code, universal gate set, long-time memory, passive source
reset, autonomous computer, microscope or civilisational application.
Source Hamiltonian engineering, microscopic unwanted terms, encoded-state
preparation and qualified detectors remain the decisive physical access gaps.
Novelty relative to all control/QEC literature remains UNKNOWN.

8. Stronger ordinary comparator: the qualitative regime is already covered

Aliferis, Gottesman and Preskill, quant-ph/0504218v3, section 11.1, PDF pp.47-49,
equations (53)-(59), explicitly allow a shared, non-Markovian quantum bath with
arbitrary bath Hamiltonian and no bath-locality assumption. Their standard
Hamiltonian fault-set bound is product integrated local perturbation norms.
Thus arbitrary quantum bath memory/correlations are not a newly allowed class
merely because they are present in the construction above.

In the virtual-photon representation, our perturbation at virtual location j
is X_j tensor (G-gI), of integrated norm A. If a specified photon remains
untransferred after the nominal pi/2 pulse, that selected location must have
at least one perturbation in a fault path. Apply the ordinary fault-set norm
bound and square after projecting onto the selected misses: their probability
is at most A^(2k). The same positive subset expansion used in section 3 gives
an ordinary generating-function upper bound with r^2 replaced by A^2 (when
A^2<=1). It has the SAME source-correlation scope, same components, same
thermal fault representation and same code. This is a matched comparator,
not an application of a pessimistic universal-gate threshold to a measurement.

At delta=.1, the ordinary single-photon missing bound is .0246740110; the
sharp bound is .0244717419. For the complete thermal example, the ordinary
and sharp formulas both first reach total flag+wrong <1e-8 at level 4, using
2401 data dual-rail qubits. The sharp constant improves the numbers but does
not produce the qualitative capability or reduce that encoding level.
The matched conclusion is also checked with outward interval arithmetic.

This is why the exact bound is retained as a scoped mathematical candidate,
not selected as a civilisation-changing principle. Prior dual-rail experiments
already demonstrate useful erasure-detected readout, and the current analysis
does not establish a practical disadvantage they suffer from quantum controllers.

9. A genuine full-qubit rescue and its limits

If both rails share exactly the SAME source operators A,B, a rail-blind
measurement of receiver occupation gives a heralded branch I_logical tensor B
and failure I_logical tensor A at the original location. For data/reference
INDEPENDENT of the controller this transfers an unknown qubit faithfully on
each success. Our whole-bosonic-channel counterexample does not forbid this
special encoded subspace. With pre-existing data/source correlations, a fine
success record can steer the data: I_logical tensor B_source does NOT imply an
unchanged conditional logical/reference marginal. The explicit positive-gain
countercontrol in matched_controls.json changes a logical-zero probability
from .5 to about .0872 merely by conditioning on success.

Repeated trials have failure effect <=r^(2L), for arbitrary correlations,
provided the location flag is actually implemented and no extra loss is hidden.
After ignoring the fine attempt number and retaining the coarse success flag,
the successful source effect is I-F_L with 0<=F_L<=r^(2L)I. Tracing out the
source then gives a logical/reference output approaching the ideal transfer
as L grows, also for correlated inputs. Fine-record conditional faithfulness
and this coarse approximate channel are different claims.

More generally a Feynman edge H=X_clock tensor G_E tensor U_data+H_E, with
supplied Hermitian unitary U_data, is gauge-equivalent to an exchange. The
transferred block is B_E tensor U_data and the failed block A_E tensor I_data.
matched_controls.py verifies these full source-operator identities for CNOT,
with residuals below 5e-16. Nonmatching rail-source couplings in its explicit
positive-gain countercontrol reduce conditional Bell fidelity to about .813.
The common-source symmetry is substantive.

Conclusive dual-rail transfer is established prior: Burgarth and Bose,
quant-ph/0406112 / PRA 71, 052315 (2005). The exact quantum-source extension
above is scoped; no invention of heralded transfer is claimed. Astra N1520's
accepted sector-scalar output measurement is a relevant possible flag interface,
with its fresh field, physical interaction and collection conditions intact.

Most decisively, the Feynman-edge gate already contains the desired CNOT in
its Hamiltonian. Its source-dependent interaction is four-local. It does not
synthesize the needed gate from our earlier allowed three-local apparatus,
construct the flag device, tolerate generic loss for free, or improve the
preserved latched-gate programme. This attempted capability rescue therefore
does not meet the mission criterion either.
