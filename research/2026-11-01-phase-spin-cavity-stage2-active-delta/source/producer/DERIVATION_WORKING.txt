9. Essential caveats/new failure tests:
 - Coupler phase-only/common cavity is prior spin-wave/cavity physics. No
   total-qubit slogan or collector invention claim.
 - Arbitrary non-number-conserving faults heat the output and can break the
   rare tail. Returning quantum controllers/pure entropy registers can also
   break the reduced Gibbs proof. Need explicit countercontrols.
 - Fixed detector/heating floor prevents unbounded n->0 gain. With ell~1/n,
   total signal photons fixed, parallel ports/hardware O(1/n), cold preparation
   noise O(n^2), detector false<=O(n), one can potentially get beta->0 while
   energy-only classical converse stays constant. This is known ideal QI/EA
   per-unit-cost behavior; new physical resource scaling is not yet proved.
 - Serial write with fixed spin lifetime requires storage through ell/P slots;
   cannot keep coherence fixed as n->0 without ports/bandwidth/lifetime growth.
 - Do not assume the illustrative independent rates form a real platform.
   Direct dipole g^2=3pi c^3 Gamma/(2 omega^2 V) ties g and Gamma. The chosen
   gamma/kappa=1e-7 with gw=.5 kappa implies cooperativity 2.5e6, probably a
   serious acquisition/physical bottleneck. Need confront strong Gaussian
   ensemble memories (Gorshkov) and Lambda/Raman dynamics rather than hiding it.
 - Raman elimination produces correlated lowering/scattering jumps and Stark
   nonlinearities, not just independent spin/cavity losses. Treat as new bridge,
   not automatic substitution. Full Lambda Gibbs reference can be too loose.
