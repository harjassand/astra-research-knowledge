# Astra investigation: Symmetry-protected collective response

**A substantially broader mathematical construction has emerged\:** heterogeneous local dynamics can sometimes be replaced by an exactly solvable, symmetry-averaged process while retaining a rigorous, nonperturbative bound on the original system’s entire long-time response.

The central mechanism is not another truncation of interaction histories. It uses the fact that **kinetic disorder cancels to first order on a symmetry-invariant observable**, even when that disorder changes every microscopic transition and the exact dynamics no longer closes on a collective variable.

For a reversible generator \(K\), let

\[
\chi_f=\langle f,K^{-1}f\rangle_\pi
=\int_0^\infty \operatorname{Cov}_\pi(f(X_0),f(X_t))\,dt.
\]

Suppose \(K_0\) is its permutation-averaged generator, both share the same equilibrium law, and all local kinetic multipliers differ from their group averages by at most a relative fraction \(\rho<1\)\. For an equilibrium-centered observable invariant under those permutations, I obtain

\[
\boxed{
\chi_{f,0}\leq\chi_f\leq
\frac{\chi_{f,0}}{1-\rho^2}.
}
\]

Unlike the ordinary first-order rate-comparison bound, the uncertainty is **quadratic in the residual kinetic heterogeneity**\.

The bound is independent of the physical spectral gap, does not enumerate interaction words, and includes all orders of the perturbation. It does not require the original collective observable to evolve as a Markov process.

A constructed 1,024-site cooperative model with 1,024 distinct microscopic clock rates, a strongly bimodal equilibrium and rational local transitions gives an exact-arithmetic interval for the full heterogeneous model\:

\[
\boxed{
3.7146990963\times10^{316}
\leq\chi_M\leq
3.7240091192\times10^{316}.
}
\]

The relative interval width is approximately **0.251%**\. The value's enormous magnitude is not the achievement; the important point is that the bound covers all the heterogeneous microscopic dynamics using a one-dimensional collective calculation, even though exact collective Markov closure is lost.

This is a conditional mathematical and computational capability, not a claim about an experimentally identified material or a proven historical discovery. Its physical scope is important: the disorder must affect kinetic activities without changing the common equilibrium.

## 2\. Why the bound holds to all orders

Let \(P\) project onto observables invariant under the physical site permutations, and let \(K_0\) be the averaged generator.

The essential identity is

\[
P(K-K_0)P=0.
\]

It says that the kinetic perturbation has no first-order component inside the collective sector. It does **not** say that the perturbation has no effect: it can move information into nonsymmetric modes and later return it.

Normalize the full generator by the reference\:

\[
T=K_0^{-1/2}KK_0^{-1/2}.
\]

The acquired relative rate bounds place its spectrum in

\[
1-\rho\leq\lambda(T)\leq1+\rho.
\]

For the normalized collective source, the spectral measure has mean exactly one. Its inverse moment is the ratio of the actual response to the reference response.

Convexity gives the lower bound. The elementary inequality

\[
\lambda+\frac{1-\rho^2}{\lambda}\leq2
\]

over that spectral interval gives the upper bound.

This construction sums the effects of **every possible excursion into the nonsymmetric dynamical sector** without enumerating those excursions.

The same argument applies to a whole matrix of collective observables and to every nonnegative Laplace frequency \(s\)\:

\[
\chi_f(s)=
\int_0^\infty e^{-st}
\operatorname{Cov}_\pi(f(X_0),f(X_t))\,dt.
\]

Thus the result is not confined to zero-frequency integrated response. It does not, however, establish individual time-point covariance bounds.

## 3\. A constructive compiler for interacting populations

The implemented family permits arbitrary cooperative and antagonistic count-dependent Gibbs energies across multiple modules. Each physical site has its own kinetic activity, and all microscopic transitions obey detailed balance.

For \(r\) modules containing \(n_g\) binary sites, the full system has \(2^n\) configurations. The averaged generator instead has

\[
D=\prod_{g=1}^{r}(n_g+1)
\]

collective states.

At fixed \(r\), that is polynomial in population size. It remains a complete stochastic model of the symmetrized dynamics, rather than a fitted moment-closure approximation.

For one population, I derived an exact birth–death current formula\:

\[
\chi_{M,0}
=
\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}
\frac{F_k^2}{p_k\lambda_k},
\]

where \(p_k\) is the exact equilibrium count probability, \(\lambda_k\) the upward rate and \(F_k\) the cumulative centered equilibrium source.

Every term is nonnegative. The executable evaluates the formula using integer arithmetic and outward rounding. It requires a linear number of large-integer divisions, with the arithmetic precision explicitly charged.

For multiple modules, I implemented a sparse count-state Poisson solver and a separate **rational a posteriori certificate**\. It derives a Poincaré bound from a spanning tree of the reduced physical transition graph, rather than assuming a spectral gap or trusting the numerical solver’s residual.

## 5\. What the strongest alternatives establish

Exact Markov lumpability is established mathematics, and existing methods already calculate collective dynamics when sites are genuinely interchangeable. citeturn418858search0

Coupled-chain Poisson methods also provide unbiased estimates of integrated Markov responses, with explicit meeting-time and moment conditions. citeturn801152search0

For conserved interacting particle systems, recent quantitative homogenization and established variational constructions are stronger than a claim that ordinary methods cannot access nongradient transport. citeturn801152search8turn801152search9

These comparisons change the significance of the present result.

**The new capability constructed here is not exact lumping. It is a controlled replacement of a non-lumpable physical process by an exact lumpable reference, with second-order relative uncertainty that does not deteriorate with collective relaxation time.**

The underlying operator convexity and Kantorovich inequality are also established tools. citeturn418858search4 Historical priority for the combined theorem and physical application remains unestablished.

There are hard limits. Different site-dependent equilibrium energies invalidate the symmetry argument. Noninvariant observables need not obey the bound. When the number of distinct interacting modules grows with system size, the quotient can again become exponentially large. Nonequilibrium generators require different mathematical treatment.

The model inputs are also complete supplied microscopic laws. The construction does not independently discover a material’s interactions or clock activities. I added a conservative interval-rate interface to show how uncertain acquired clock measurements affect the final bounds.

## 6\. Scientific decision

The investigation has produced a broader enabling principle than the previous kinetic-Ising dual\:

**A system's collective response can be much more stable under kinetic heterogeneity than its microscopic dynamics, provided its equilibrium symmetry and the relative kinetic perturbation are known.**

The previously obtained positive-dual work potentials control all-order sampling for a specific one-dimensional nonlinear family. The new construction uses a different resource—symmetry averaging and positive inverse-response bounds—to cover arbitrary cooperative count energies, multiple interacting modules, nonzero fields and individually distinct rates.

The remaining obstacle to the intended civilisation-changing generality is clear: **construct an economical analogue of this symmetry projection for spatial systems whose equilibrium has no useful global permutation symmetry.** That would have to acquire local effective structure, prove control over discarded dynamics, and keep the computational work bounded. Neither symmetry nor compressibility may simply be assumed.

This run has not solved that harder problem, but it has established a complete mathematical operation, its computational receiver, and a precise boundary for extending it.
