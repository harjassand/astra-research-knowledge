Outcome

I preserved the transport bounds, ordering-reversal construction, noisy-clock result, and failed complete-output comparisons unchanged. I then changed the target to distinguishing membrane transport from intracellular reaction by controlling nuclear-spin labels while holding chemical feeding fixed.

This allocation does not establish a superior physical assay or an efficient new separator. The tested spin-inversion construction loses its advantage against optimized conventional readout. Its preparation and delivery assumptions also fail to close into one demonstrated operating regime.

The strongest result worth retaining is an exact obstruction:

An unresolved precursor pool can hide finite changes in transport and reaction rates from every permitted longitudinal spin-control sequence—even with independently varied inputs and arbitrarily detailed downstream measurements.

I constructed an explicit family of positive kinetic models with this property, extended it to downstream networks, and derived a finite-polarization bound showing how the ambiguity persists approximately when reverse exchange is small. This is a scoped result about what physical information an experiment must supply. I have not established historical novelty or a transformative consequence from it.

1. Why the material-separation openings did not survive selection

I gave competing physical mechanisms room to develop before committing to the measurement problem. The important distinction from the earlier clock construction was that these mechanisms possess actual species-dependent interactions, so the common-equilibrium mobility bounds do not automatically dispose of them.

Quantum isotope discrimination: collected output changes the conclusion

The carbon-pore oxygen experiment reports a transient isotope selectivity near 60. However, the collected gas after ten minutes had a heavy/light ratio around 1.4 from a 50:50 feed—approximately 58.3% heavy oxygen. Natural oxygen also presents a different isotopologue mixture from the enriched homonuclear feed. The transient ratio therefore does not establish the collected product, recovery, or capacity required by the proposed separation consequence. 
Nature Communications

Graphene electrochemical pumping supplies a more substantial result: measured mixed-isotopologue collection with a separation factor around 8. I carried its reported flux and voltage into natural-feed mass and charge balances. The resulting batch calculation did not earn an energy or throughput advantage. That calculation is preserved with its limitations: it is not an impossibility proof for an optimized cascade, and conventional electrolysis comparisons require consistent energy credits. 
pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov

Lanmodulin: real affinity discrimination, insufficient demonstrated productivity

The lanmodulin route was more promising mechanistically. The 2026 screen demonstrated substantial sequence-dependent changes in rare-earth preference, including a Pr/La fraction exceeding 99.9% Pr purity at 83.2% recovery. This is a genuine energetic discriminant rather than a scalar mobility difference. 
Nature Chemical Biology

But the reported column conditions give a very different picture when converted into output:

Quantity	Calculation from the reported experiment
Recovered Pr per cycle	Approximately 0.0300 mg
Cycle including stated pre-equilibration, loading, wash and elution	Approximately 207 min
Demonstrated low-load productivity	0.261 kg elemental Pr/m³ resin/day
Generous full-capacity, lossless estimate on the same cycle	2.74 kg/m³/day

Those are my process calculations, not newly measured performance. The strongest relevant chromatography comparator already reports productivity above 116 kg rare-earth oxide/m³/day, with high purity and recovery and substantial ligand recycling. Elemental Pr and mixed rare-earth oxide productivity are different bases, so their quotient is not an exact process comparison. Nevertheless, the gap prevents a defensible bulk-superiority claim from the demonstrated protein result. 
ScienceDirect

I also inspected the public lanmodulin analysis implementation. Its normalized logarithmic retention scores cannot be treated as binding constants or competitive adsorption isotherms. The missing quantitative inputs therefore could not be supplied by turning its plotted selectivity map into an invented cascade model.

The retained physical lesson is that guest-specific free energy supplies a real escape from the earlier bounds. The retrieved evidence did not supply a new process that converted that escape into superior collected output.

2. The replacement target: measure transport and reaction separately

The useful output I pursued was a finite experiment that distinguishes:

how quickly a substrate enters cells;
how quickly it undergoes intracellular conversion;
how quickly the product exits.

A single observed lactate curve can combine all three. Separating them would improve the interpretation of metabolic interventions: a change attributed to an enzyme might instead arise from membrane transport or product export.

The proposed discriminant was the nuclear-spin state. Hyperpolarization provides a detectable carbon-13 label, and selective radiofrequency control can change pyruvate and lactate magnetization differently without necessarily changing their molecular concentrations. Pyruvate/lactate co-polarization has also been demonstrated. 
npj Imaging

The construction considered a composition-matched preperfusion, followed by controlled polarized feeding and chemical-selective readout. Its intended advantage was to probe dynamics while keeping the chemical environment stationary.

The obstruction survives downstream elaboration

The same construction works when the lactate pair is replaced by an arbitrary finite downstream linear network, provided there is no return into the unresolved precursor pair and its outgoing coupling rates remain unknown.

The transformation leaves the downstream network unchanged while rescaling the exits from the hidden precursor. Even measuring all downstream states does not resolve the upstream ambiguity under those conditions.

This gives a concrete construction rule: adding downstream complexity or more downstream measurements need not supply information about the hidden precursor decomposition. A compartment-specific observation, independently known hidden rate, intracellular preparation, or suitable feedback path changes the problem.

This is a specialization of established minimal-realization ideas, not a claim to have invented a general theory of observability.

6. What remains for Astra

I used Capital’s inventory and finite kinetic-contrast machinery to check separation claims against their conditions. N1079 and N1620 helped identify when a proposed selectivity had not supplied complete output. N1626 remained source-reported rather than an independently verified premise. N1198’s quantum-port assumptions do not match these unresolved longitudinal measurements, so I did not import its reconstruction conclusion.

The new spin results rest on the displayed model, explicit state transformations and independently checked calculations. Relevant Zenodo records were inspected for other polarization formulations, but they did not validate this construction; no Zenodo data replay is claimed.

I am closing the tested inversion route. The durable result is the precise distinction between an invisible kinetic decomposition, a structurally identifiable one, and one recoverable with finite polarization and noise. A future construction must obtain an actual compartment-sensitive observable, an independent hidden-state constraint, or a sufficiently strong return path—and then demonstrate useful output against conventional readout with preparation, delivery and total resources counted together. This allocation has not established that final capability.
