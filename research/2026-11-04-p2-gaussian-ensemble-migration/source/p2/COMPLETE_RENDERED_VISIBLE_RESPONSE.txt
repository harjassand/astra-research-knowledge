8. What this establishes

The completed consequence is that the same statistical information can support couplings with radically different costs in already completed downstream work. Ordinary mean translation spends no more statistical information than this construction, yet it destroys all literal input equalities. Coupling the conditional Gaussian ensembles preserves those equalities while maintaining the complete target law.

The accompanying foundational results explain the boundary. Exact adaptive conversion of a private Gaussian experiment obeys a pathwise information-matrix budget and a universal conditional residual law. Rich analytic noisy-response classes instead obey spatial acquisition restrictions; ordinary certified deterministic values supply a stronger interface. Those results and their strongest existing comparators remain in the report.

The selected ensemble result is complete at its stated scope: construction, joint response law, converse, acquisition accounting, finite implementation and executed validation. The assumptions that permit it are visible, and the ordinary methods that win with stronger information or more response structure remain available.

What remains unestablished is historical priority, optimality for the approximate finite-grid problem, validation of a particular physical source model, and a measured advantage over a particular scientific solver’s best incremental implementation. The achieved capability is a proved and executable reduction in expensive response evaluations under an explicit historical-access contract.
