## Direct architectural overlap

**Kerckhoff–Pavlichin–Chalabi–Mabuchi, Design of nanophotonic circuits for autonomous subsystem quantum error correction, arXiv1102.3143v1 (15 Feb 2011).** Full primary https://arxiv.org/pdf/1102.3143 . Nine-qubit Bacon–Shor network, continuous time-independent optical probes and physical relay-controller qubits, powered by CW fields. Probes accumulate joint parity, interference sets relay state, and relays route Raman correction beams. This is very close to the purpose of an autonomous physical parity latch; no external sampling clock or classical overseer is required. Equations are reduced input-output models after small-volume cavity limits (p4), not precisely the proposed finite-dimensional static edge Hamiltonian. Eq2 includes stochastic X/Y/Z data noise. Propagation loss and wider hardware robustness are postponed (p5); no size-uniform refresh/noisy-controller threshold. Appendix discusses subsystem protection against certain probe losses. Thus do not claim that a physical continuous parity cache or clockless Raman feedback is a new operation by itself.

**Reiter–Sørensen–Zoller–Muschik, Dissipative quantum error correction and application to quantum sensing with trapped ions, Nature Communications8,1822 (2017), DOI10.1038/s41467-017-01895-5.** Full primary https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-017-01895-5.pdf . Always-on multilevel ion drives, sideband coupling to motional modes, and engineered cooling implement three-qubit repetition correction. Effective-operator analysis and finite-system numerical master equations; correction scales quadratically against residual physical noise in the fast-correction limit. Methods 'External imperfections' explicitly includes excited-level decay/dephasing, imperfect initial cooling and ongoing mode heating, so describing this as a perfect-auxiliary-only proposal would be unfair. Strong auxiliary cooling is required, while interrogation-mode cooling must remain moderate relative to sideband coupling. It is not a finite-4w-level general commuting-check latch theorem, and no general coherent-noise/overlapping-network exponential-memory guarantee is supplied. Closest mechanism overlap: spectrally selective autonomous Raman dissipation and physically reset auxiliaries are already established design ideas.

**Kapit–Chalker–Simon, Passive correction of quantum logical errors in a driven, dissipative system: a blueprint for an analog quantum code fabric, arXiv1408.0959.** Full primary https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.0959 . Toric-code variant with propagating bosons and lossy shadow degrees of freedom: resonant energy transfer creates a rate barrier and autonomous repair. On-site/nearest-neighbor architecture, but its abstract explicitly says protection is not scalable to arbitrarily large systems. This is genuine autonomous reservoir-engineering prior, not a ready-made generic parity-cache primitive. Do not infer a threshold from its large finite lifetime enhancement.

## Decisive missing principle
The new finite-cell construction is a plausible alternative implementation of a heavily precedented operation. Its strongest possible contribution is not the Raman interference or cache-dark-state idea, but a composable local admission theorem for arbitrary bounded-degree overlapping checks with finite noisy apparatus and fixed rates.

Exact dressed logical-QND ideal dynamics is valuable: it may avoid secular approximation errors becoming logical errors. But it says nothing by itself about useful parity service. In the dressed frame, overlapping cells remain coupled by apparatus-dependent signs; a unique isolated dark state or isolated Liouvillian gap does not establish uniform local refresh in that interacting network. Prefix-undo dumps can remove an identified cleanup fault mechanism, yet must prove they do not create inaccessible dark/trapped sectors, slow modes, or unacceptable noise propagation.

Needed contract: for actual finite parameters and all admitted apparatus/data faults, either (a) a positive local Poisson/Foster certificate admitting cache lag, high-level occupation and coherent perturbations in the memory's weighted operator norm, or (b) a spatially composable fault-path/refresh estimate valid for perpetually overlapping operation. It must tolerate the actual reset noise and calibration drift, and local defect forcing—not only the unforced stationary cell. Exponentially accurate global effective-channel simulation is neither required nor sufficient as the right practical route.

A 16-level site with independently selectable Pauli-assisted transitions and selective dump decays is a substantial hardware primitive, even when every edge is formally two-body. Charge selection rules, drive phases, off-resonant transitions, reset selectivity and the physical noise budget. The J/g~eta^(w-1) slowdown remains. An active same-apparatus extraction baseline is entitled to the same transitions, controls and reset capability; exact ideal QND alone does not imply better useful memory.

Verdict: strong mechanism overlap, no complete dominator found in this focused pass. Do not promote a newly derived gadget or finite-cell spectrum as a new scientific capability until the network admission and native-hardware comparison close.
