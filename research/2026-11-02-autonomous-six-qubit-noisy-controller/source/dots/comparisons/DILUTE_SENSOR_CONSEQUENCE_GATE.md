## Closest prior contracts

**DST, Quantum Memory and Autonomous Computation in Two Dimensions, arXiv2601.20818v3 (25 Sep 2026).** https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20818v3 . Supplement formal Theorem1 proves the synchronous dissipative QCA threshold with sufficiently correct encoded input and composable local fault strength; noisy internal classical organization is not simply ignored. Main text continuous-time section explicitly leaves its threshold extension for future work (line124), repeated at Supplement VII opening (line1717). VII.1 discusses finite marching-soldier clocks, winding deadlock and local repair; VII.2 sketches continuous-time operation. Therefore its autonomous continuous-time architecture is strong prior, but it is not an established theorem with the candidate's actual continuously noisy physical sensors/clocks. Removing the unproved clock-to-faultpath bridge would matter; no claim that autonomous architecture itself is new.

**Midha–Abanin, Local autonomous inference machines for quantum LDPC codes, arXiv2609.38139v1 (29 Sep 2026).** https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38139v1 . Discussion explicitly confines the results to offline decoding: errors sampled once, no subsequent noise. Online syndrome/fault ingress is future work. Local BP processing and autonomous feedback therefore do not supply a perpetually noisy live decoder service. The proposed sensor/compiler contract is materially stronger on this axis.

**Gu et al., Single-Shot Decoding of Good Quantum LDPC Codes, CMP405,85 (14 Mar 2024).** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00220-024-04951-6 . Thm3.4: residual reduced weight bounded by alpha times old error plus beta times syndrome noise; parallel k iterations achieve alpha=2^{-Omega(k)}. Thm3.5 and Cor3.6 already prove repeated fresh-syndrome recovery with constant iterations and exponentially suppressed per-round tails. This earns exactly the useful correction contraction, not an autonomous noisy physical implementation of the decoder or an extraction service without trusted rounds. Do not sell constant-time partial recovery, accepting imperfect records, or lack of syndrome-history archive as novel in isolation.

**Bharti–Haug–Khu–Tanggara, Single-Shot Error Correction at Optimal Spacetime Cost, arXiv2610.03684v1 (2 Oct 2026).** https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03684 . Thm1.1: Tanner encoded memory with N=O(K+log(S/epsilon)), O(SN) quantum locations and O(N) classical work/cycle, counting noisy preparation, gates, measurement and waits. Section2.1 requires reliable classical records/processing, fixed-time parallel decoder communication, and no n-growing routing delay. Section2.2 assumes a classically sampled local-stochastic faulty-location set, with arbitrary coherent CPTP faults on it; it explicitly excludes coherent superpositions of fault paths and ubiquitous small coherent overrotations. Strong active benchmark, not same-resource fixed autonomous arbitrary-local-H theorem. Encoded input/output comparison maps are ideal. Optimality is asserted against independent-erasure spacetime lower bounds, not all noise models.

**Balasubramanian–Davydova–Lin, A passive self-correcting quantum memory in three dimensions, arXiv2605.10943v1 (11 May 2026).** https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10943 . Thms1.1–1.2: one logical qubit, exp(Theta(n^eta)) lifetime below fixed temperature, bounded-density geometrically local 3D stabilizer Hamiltonians. Dynamics assumes local jumps with Gibbs stationary state for specified H (also stated explicitly at §2.5); initialization is thermal encoding. This is stronger geometry and genuine passivity, weaker storage rate and different dynamics. Arbitrary local Hamiltonian perturbation stability is not established there: even spectral-gap stability is stated as future work pending TQO-2. It does not admit a fixed controller plus arbitrary non-Gibbs coherent noise on noisy auxiliaries. Conversely the candidate must not claim to solve passive 3D memory: it uses driven engineered GKSL and expander wiring.

## Consequence judgment
Conditional on the full join, the meaningful operation is a fixed, bounded-arity driven memory whose physical syndrome acquisition and control organization need not be trusted: accepted bad readouts, simultaneous probes and clock damage enter one error budget. That is an implementation-level closure of an actual missing theorem, not just a better decoder constant. No complete same-resource dominator established in this focused pass; this is not a historical novelty proof.

However useful fault-tolerant storage is already available with active scheduled extraction and classical control. The new operation only becomes a consequential scientific capability if eliminating that interface enables a regime the active baseline cannot economically reach. A deliberately faster noisy hardware hierarchy and arbitrary up-to-eight-qubit engineered GKSL terms may be more demanding than the ordinary controller they replace. Low arity alone does not establish physical realizability or advantage.

Next consequence gate, after proof closure: exhibit one physical primitive model realizing the allowed finite jump set at fixed rates/noise, charge all reset/drive/control dissipation and expander routing, and compare useful logical storage per physical spacetime/control resource against scheduled single-shot extraction using those same available interactions. No claim that active wins every platform without that test; currently no demonstrated advantage. Do not polish asymptotic constants before establishing this native implementation and matched-resource opportunity. Protected storage also does not yet supply in-place logical gates, arbitrary scientific algorithms, or cheap encoded input/output.
