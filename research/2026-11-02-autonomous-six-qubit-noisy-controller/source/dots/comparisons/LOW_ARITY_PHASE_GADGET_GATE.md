## Closest actual contracts

1. Harley et al., *Going beyond gadgets: the importance of scalability for analogue quantum simulators*, Nature Communications 15,6527 (2 Aug 2024), DOI10.1038/s41467-024-50744-9. Full published text read at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11297168/ . Proposition27/28, Eqs54–65: nonunitary locality reduction, ancilla measured every δt; effective channel error O(t δt), unwanted ancilla branch O(t δt²); interactions include O(δt^-1/2) and O(δt^-1). Thus exponential target time forces shrinking step/increasing strength under this guarantee. Discussion explicitly notes precisely engineered stroboscopic dissipation and multiple-gadget ancilla accumulation. Their generic Hamiltonian simulation lower bounds are NOT a no-go for phase-specific protection.

2. Brell–Bartlett–Doherty, *Perturbative 2-body parent Hamiltonians for projected entangled pair states*, NJP16,123056 (22 Dec 2014). Full published PDF https://d-nb.info/1215034334/34 . Thms6–8 (printed pp10–11), §5: isometric, quasi-injective, topologically ordered PEPS; local SW + topological stability keeps effective encoded ground state in the same gapped phase to any finite perturbative order, with sufficiently small intensive coupling. Eq56 uses local interaction-strength norm. This is genuinely phase-based rather than channel fidelity. Do not upgrade “any order” into a convergent exact all-orders open-system compiler. No noisy-auxiliary driven Lindbladian, Foster inequality, or finite-temperature logical lifetime is proved. Two-body qudits also do not imply two-qubit hardware.

3. Bravyi–Hastings–Michalakis, arXiv1001.0344v1 (3 Jan 2010), JMP51,093512: primary abstract https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.0344 . Zero-temperature, geometrically local commuting-projector Hamiltonians on D-dimensional lattices with topological-order assumptions: bounded local perturbations preserve separated low-energy spectral bands and exponentially narrow ground band. Spectral ground-band stability is not a bound on continuous noisy excursion/correction processes; expander geometry also needs fresh admission. Only abstract inspected in this pass.

4. Cubitt–Lucia–Michalakis–Perez-Garcia, *Stability of local quantum dissipative systems*, arXiv1303.4744v4 (28 Apr 2015), CMP337,1275–1315. Full primary https://arxiv.org/pdf/1303.4744 . Thm6.7 Eq39 (printed p21; theorem starts PDFp21): time-uniform perturbed-vs-unperturbed local observable bound, assuming local rapid mixing and unique fixed point. This is a genuine non-epsilon-times-T result. Its support-size dependent bound is not the required exponentially weighted extensive observable bound, and unique globally mixing steady state does not provide logical-sector retention. A fast auxiliary subsystem might use these ideas, but coupling it to a slow protected manifold requires a new sector-uniform argument.

5. Kessler, *Generalized Schrieffer-Wolff Formalism for Dissipative Systems*, arXiv1205.5440v1 (24 May 2012), PRA86,012126. Full https://arxiv.org/pdf/1205.5440 (PDF dateline June5,2018, arXiv footer v1/2012). §II nonunitary similarity separates slow/fast Liouvillian spectra; §III general ancilla elimination second order is Lindblad. End §III (printed pp8–9): obtaining Lindblad form at each higher order remains future work. Not a local, volume-uniform weighted-order admission theorem with physical noisy auxiliaries. Nonunitary similarity need not preserve positive observables or operator inequalities.

## Exact missing contract (assessment, not source claim)
A successful compiler can avoid exponentially accurate global simulation. It needs a positive physical corrected observable F_phys, and a local charge/sector encoding, such that the ACTUAL low-arity generator (including auxiliary faults) has the needed negative drift outside the low-potential floor, with a residual small in the same F-weighted order, uniformly in n. For example an error bound ±R*F_phys <= c eta n F_phys can be absorbed if c eta lies below the established extensive cooling margin. The correction must retain the barrier and logical transport properties, not only match a low-frequency spectrum.

If an effective generator is used, require: (i) a positive/order-valid lift of the witness; (ii) quasi-local remainder summable in a weight paying each term's potential change; (iii) charge transport or bounded correctable charge violation for every auxiliary/noise term; (iv) bounds on physical occupation outside the eliminated subspace, not permanently initialized ideal ancillas. Exact formal charge conservation alone supplies none of the drift/barrier bounds. At fixed coupling, rare auxiliary excitation has nonzero density; demanding no excitation anywhere for exponential time is the wrong contract. It must be admitted as locally correctable noise.

Scope conclusion: the candidate ingredient is a noise-tolerant phase-specific low-arity admission, not novelty of gadgets, SW, phase stability, or autonomous ancillas. No historical complete dominator established by this focused pass; no exhaustive priority claim.
