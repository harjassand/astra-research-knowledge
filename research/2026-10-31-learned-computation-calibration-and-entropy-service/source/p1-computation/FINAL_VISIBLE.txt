## The question I selected

**Can experiments discover an unknown representation that makes repeated inference and constrained sampling feasible—and determine which later questions preserve that advantage?**

I investigated energies on controlled binary configurations,
\[
f(x)=\theta_0+\sum_{j=1}^{s}\theta_j(-1)^{a_j^\top x},
\qquad x\in\mathbb F_2^n.
\]

The learner receives the input dimension, a valid total sparsity bound, and a paid operation that prepares a specified configuration and returns its scalar energy. It acquires the masks \(a_j\), coefficients, rank, algebraic dependencies, and useful computational structure.

This is a different interface from the earlier endpoint-return service. It is implemented as an executable software oracle here; availability and preparation cost on a physical platform remain explicit premises.

Two structural routes survived:

- **A hidden chain:** the learner discovers short XOR relations, verifies that they span every dependency, and constructs a two-state exact computation.
- **A hidden planar graph:** the learner reconstructs vertex stars from the acquired binary matrix, verifies the graph representation, and acquires an embedding. This supplies an exact planar partition routine.

The self-contained graph recognizer has a proved completeness scope: fixed degree, simple, 3-connected graphs, with an additional bounded-face condition for the implemented embedding construction. Failure outside that scope returns **UNKNOWN**. General constructive graph recognition is already available through Bixby–Wagner and CMR; I inspected that machinery without claiming to have executed it. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

## The strongest result: new evidence costs according to its overlap

Let \(A\) contain the acquired parity masks as rows. Suppose the acquired structure supports the exact **signed partition operation**
\[
\Phi(v)=\sum_{y\in\operatorname{im}A}\prod_j v_j(y_j),
\]
where the supplied rational factor values may be negative.

For new physical observations \(Cx=z\), define
\[
\boxed{
t=\dim\!\bigl(\operatorname{row}A\cap\operatorname{row}C\bigr)
=\operatorname{rank}A+\operatorname{rank}C
-\operatorname{rank}\!\begin{pmatrix}A\\C\end{pmatrix}.
}
\]

The construction proves and implements:

- **A conditional partition sum:** \(2^t\) signed partition requests.
- **Each conditional physical-parity numerator:** at most \(2^t\) requests.
- **An actual conditional input sample:** at most \((s+1)2^t\) requests, followed by a computed linear solve and a uniform draw from the remaining solution space.

Binary elimination, rational arithmetic, output precision, and sampling randomness are charged separately.

The reason is precise. Expanding the evidence indicator initially gives \(2^{\operatorname{rank}C}\) parity terms. Any term outside the learned row space cancels exactly within each set of inputs having the same learned features. Elimination constructs the surviving subspace, whose dimension is \(t\). Each surviving term changes only signs in the retained factor weights.

**The signed operation had to be earned.** A positive approximate partition oracle would not suffice because cancellation can destroy its accuracy. The exact trellis and elimination programs support signed arithmetic directly. For the planar implementation, I constructed and checked an interpolation procedure for exceptional zero denominators; its additional Pfaffian evaluations are counted.

This result is broader than conditioning only on learned features. It still pays exponentially when the overlap rank grows. It also does **not** establish efficient MAP optimization under general physical evidence.

## What was executed

| Experiment | Paid energy observations | Acquired computation | Later operation |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Hidden chain: 48 input bits, 61 interactions | **402,237** | Two states per largest layer; 122 total state cells; 182 transitions | 250 new weighting and feature-conditioning tasks |
| Hidden planar model: 32 input bits, 36 interactions | **135,663** | Recovered 24-vertex graph and embedding; retained elimination schedule with 399 table cells | 12 matched inference tasks and eight conditional samples |
| New physical evidence on that acquired planar model | **0 additional** | Eight independent observations, overlap rank \(t=2\) | Four signed sums per conditional mass; four conditional samples using 592 signed requests |

The chain had **30 independent dependencies**. A generic syndrome program would have \(2^{30}\) states, while the discovered order needed only two. Its exact computation also produced a feature-event probability of approximately
\[
9.7373\times10^{-37}.
\]
There is no inverse-event-probability rejection factor in these inference and sampling procedures.

The initial distributions use the **acquired energy coefficients**, at \(\beta=\ln 2\), with exact rational weights and the normalization scale restored. Later reweighting is restricted to the acquired interactions.

Both main acquisition runs requested a global failure allowance of \(0.01\); their finite caps were 1,608,948 and 542,652 scalar calls respectively. Their recovered representations were also checked exactly against the hidden synthetic fixtures. Those acquisition guarantees do not establish that an arbitrary real experiment can provide equally cheap or precise returns.

All **nine reproduction stages passed** using the Python standard library. Verification includes complete small conditional sampling laws, inconsistent evidence, signed cancellation, negative prefactors, and interpolation through singular factors. ## The conventional comparison changed the construction

**Ordinary retained variable elimination is the preferred executor on the compact planar examples.**

Across the twelve identical tasks in the integrated experiment, it took about **0.0057 seconds**, versus **2.03 seconds** for the exact rational Pfaffian implementation. Both received the same acquired graph and retained their reusable structural work. These are local implementation measurements, not a claim about the best production implementations.

The Pfaffian remains useful as a **proved polynomial fallback** when a small elimination table is unavailable. Classical planar inference already provides this machinery and structural reuse across changed weights. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}

The acquisition comparison also limits the claim. Cheraghchi–Indyk gives stronger bounded-bit sparse-Walsh recovery theory than the elementary quadratic-in-sparsity learner implemented here. I retained the elementary construction for its transparent finite budget and executable validation, without treating it as an acquisition improvement. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}

A broader novelty claim failed as well. **Minimally Complex Models** already learns useful high-order parity representations, and knowledge compilation already organizes representations by the queries and transformations they support. The present contribution is the checked path from a specified observation operation to an acquired structure, an exact signed computation, and a quantified family of later queries. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="3"}

## The boundaries are now sharper

**A fully known model can still leave planning hard.** Independent learned features plus unrestricted linear evidence already encode minimum-weight syndrome decoding. The general decoding problem is NP-complete. The overlap parameter measures a sufficient computational burden; it does not remove that obstruction. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="4"}

**An easy final model can still be hard to acquire from passive observations.** For a hidden mask \(a\), the integer-valued energy
\[
f_a(x)=\frac{1-(-1)^{a^\top x}}2
\]
has only one nonconstant Fourier term. At \(\beta=\ln 2\), an appropriate split of \(x\) makes its passive samples exactly learning-parity-with-noise examples. Recovering the mask therefore contains search LPN under the corresponding hardness assumption. In contrast, chosen energy queries at \(0,e_1,\ldots,e_n\) reveal it directly. This is a computational distinction between observation interfaces, not an unconditional impossibility result. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="5"}

**Small dependency rank is not a complete measure of tractability.** The hidden-chain construction defeats that measure, and planar structure supplies another route. Classical minimal trellises already explain why the arrangement of constraints matters. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="6"}

The result worth retaining is therefore specific: **learning can acquire a computational structure whose usefulness extends to new questions, and that extension can be quantified and executed.** Here, signed partition computation and evidence-overlap rank provide that connection. The learned representation earns its place; an additional special execution layer does not.
