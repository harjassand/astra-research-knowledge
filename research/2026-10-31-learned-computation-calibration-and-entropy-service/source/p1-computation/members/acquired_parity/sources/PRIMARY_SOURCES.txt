PRIMARY SOURCES AND THE CLAIMS USED
All retrieved during this investigation; original papers are not redistributed.

1. Cheraghchi, M.; Indyk, P. Nearly Optimal Deterministic Algorithm for Sparse
Walsh-Hadamard Transform. ECCC TR15-076(2015), later ACM TALG13(3)(2017).
https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2015/076/download/
Theorem1 and its bounded-bit qualification were read. Its l1/l1 result is exact
on exactly sparse spectra. It is a stronger asymptotic acquisition comparator
than our elementary quadratic-sparsity construction. It is not a promise that
its unspecified polynomial factors are small in the present experiments.

2. Scheibler, R.; Haghighatshoar, S.; Vetterli, M. A Fast Hadamard Transform for
Signals with Sub-linear Sparsity in the Transform Domain. IEEE TIT61(2015).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1310.1803
https://lcav.github.io/SparseFHT/
Subspace aliasing and shifted-sign support recovery are classical. The stronger
peeling analysis and continuous-coefficient collision arguments have assumptions
which are not silently applied to arbitrary rational supports here.

3. McEliece, R. J. On the BCJR Trellis for Linear Block Codes. IEEE TIT42(4),
1072-1092(1996).
https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/rr13a-7te87
The author archive describes the minimal trellis and minimal-span construction.
Our exact cut-space formula and construction are proved directly in the notes.
The result defeats any claim that total syndrome dimension alone measures the
best conventional inference cost.

4. Bixby, R. E.; Wagner, D. K. An Almost Linear-Time Algorithm for Graph
Realization. Mathematics of Operations Research13(1),99-123(1988).
https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/abs/10.1287/moor.13.1.99
https://discopt.github.io/cmr/graphic.html
https://discopt.github.io/cmr/graphic_8h.html
The primary abstract and full CMR1.3.0 implementation documentation were read.
The library returns an actual graph and aligned forest/coforest edge maps after
binary elimination. General recognition was not executed here. The restricted
bounded-degree recognizer is self-contained and executed.

5. Costalonga, J. P. Non-separating Cocircuits and Graphicness in Matroids.
https://arxiv.org/html/1211.5823v1
The introduction states the nonseparating-cocircuit/vertex-star relation in
3-connected graphic matroids. The scoped recognition proof is supplied here.

6. Schraudolph, N. N.; Kamenetsky, D. Efficient Exact Inference in Planar Ising
Models(2008).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0810.4401
The primary treatment gives planar inference and reuse of structural Kasteleyn
work across changed weights. These are established execution methods, not new
Astra primitives. Our exact rational implementation supplies its own four-port
matching identity, orientation check and signed interpolation.

7. de Mulatier, C.; Marsili, M. Bayesian Inference of Minimally Complex Models
with Interactions of Arbitrary Order. Physical Review E111,054307(2025).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.00520
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.111.054307
The strongest conceptual prior found: learned high-order parity coordinates,
gauge invariance, independent components, tractable inference and sampling.
The paper's passive-data search and heuristic regimes differ from our active
energy-query promise. It rules out broad novelty for useful parity-coordinate
learning; it does not remove the need to compare exact task/interface costs.

8. Darwiche, A.; Marquis, P. A Knowledge Compilation Map. JAIR17,229-264(2002).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.1819
Representations are already compared by succinctness and the queries and
transformations they support. The query-closure perspective belongs within this
established framework. The present work adds an explicit acquired source and
checked task family; it does not claim to invent knowledge compilation.

9. Berlekamp, E. R.; McEliece, R. J.; van Tilborg, H. C. A. On the Inherent
Intractability of Certain Coding Problems. IEEE TIT24(3),384-386(1978).
https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/aw9vs-ann16
Primary source for general decoding NP-completeness. The reduction from an
independent acquired model plus arbitrary evidence is given explicitly here.

10. Blum, A.; Kalai, A.; Wasserman, H. Noise-Tolerant Learning, the Parity
Problem, and the Statistical Query Model. JACM50(4),506-519(2003).
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/2003-Noise-Tolerant_Learning.pdf
Pietrzak, K. Cryptography from Learning Parity with Noise(2012).
https://research-explorer.ista.ac.at/record/3250
These supply the LPN problem and assumption context. The energy-model identity
is derived and checked in this work. The obstruction is conditional on search
LPN hardness, not an unconditional acquisition lower bound or a claim about
every possible approximate generative task.
