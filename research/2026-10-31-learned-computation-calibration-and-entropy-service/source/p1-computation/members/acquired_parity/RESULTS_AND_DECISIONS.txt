FAILED ATTACKS AND CORRECTIONS TO RETAIN

1. Total dependency d is not the right universal exponent. A connected hidden
   chain has arbitrarily large d but a two-state classical trellis. Planarity
   can retain polynomial inference beyond bounded treewidth as well.
2. A source model alone does not settle future questions. Syndrome decoding
   appears under unrestricted physical evidence. The final overlap theorem
   supplies a paid parameter t;it does not turn arbitrary t into a constant.
3. A positive approximate partition operation does not automatically support
   the signed cancellations required for physical observations. Exact signed
   extension and zero-a interpolation were acquired and charged explicitly.
4. A Boolean graphicness flag does not recover the graph needed by execution.
   A restricted constructive recognizer was implemented;the broader CMR
   constructive implementation was inspected and is identified as unexecuted.
5. Sparse Walsh learning,parity-coordinate modeling,and knowledge compilation
   have strong prior art. The broad novelty claim failed. The present result
   is a transparent end-to-end construction with unresolved historical priority.
6. The initial triangle emission order could give width two. Emitting the
   previous shared feature before the private feature repairs the width-one
   claim;the resulting cut dimensions are computed exactly.
7. A caller's mutable weight list could invalidate a saved sampling table.
   Retained evaluations now copy immutable weight pairs;the audit covers it.
8. Absolute energy partition functions need the factor
   2^[-(theta_0+sum|theta_j|)] after integerizing at beta=ln2. The JSON records
   this scale and the physical rational values. Probability ratios cancel it.
9. The simple acquisition implementation costsO(n S^2 log(1/delta)) calls.
   Existing sparse-transform theory is stronger in sparsity dependence. No
   acquisition-performance improvement is claimed.
10. Passive observations can contain searchLPN even for a one-mode energy.
    The chosen-energy interface materially changes the acquisition problem.
    This conditional hardness observation is neither an unconditional lower
    bound nor a claim that every approximate downstream task requires the mask.

DECISION

Retain the acquired representation,the deterministic structural certificates,
the exact signed-sum operation,and the explicit query contract. Adopt the
retaining conventional executor that wins. Do not retain an additional special
execution layer or a broad novelty claim. The substantive capability is that
paid acquisition makes a reusable family of exact computations available;the
meaningful burden for new linear evidence is its overlap with the acquired
feature space,with known special query families remaining cheaper.

The earlier cumulative-solver archive remains byte-for-byte unchanged;its
SHA256 has been verified during assembly. Capital handles integration.
