ASTRA: ACQUIRED STRUCTURE FOR REUSABLE EXACT COMPUTATION
Research checkpoint, 31 October 2026

The question retained in this investigation is:

Can paid observations acquire both an unknown representation and a computation
that stays useful as later scientific queries change?

This checkpoint constructs one such route. Global scalar energy experiments
acquire unknown sparse parity interactions. Binary algebra then discovers an
exact small trellis or a hidden planar graph and its embedding. Retained
classical programs use that acquired structure for repeated partition sums,
conditional probabilities, actual input samples, and trellis optimization.

The strongest final extension concerns NEW physical observations. Let A be the
acquired feature matrix and Cx=z be q independent new linear observations. If

    t = dim(row A intersect row C),

then an acquired exact signed partition kernel computes their conditional mass
using 2^t signed sums. Gaussian elimination constructs the contributing twists;
it does not enumerate all 2^q possibilities. Conditional parity readouts need
at most the same number, and an actual conditional input sampler uses at most
(s+1)2^t signed sums followed by a uniform joint-kernel lift. This result is
proved and executed. It is not a MAP guarantee under general physical evidence.

The signed operation is earned explicitly. Variable elimination and trellis
programs extend to exact signed arithmetic. The planar kernel handles zero
denominators by a charged finite polynomial interpolation, retaining the
acquired graph and orientation. An approximate positive-sum oracle would not
automatically supply this operation because cancellation matters.

EXECUTED RESULTS

1. Hidden chain: n=48, s=61, rank31, dependency30. Acquisition used 402,237
   scalar calls (finite cap1,608,948), plus19,307,376 configuration-bit writes.
   The learner found all masks/coefficients, all relations, and a width-one
   order: two states per largest layer,122 total state cells,182 transitions.
   The same template answered250 new reweighting/feature-conditioning tasks
   without new energy calls. A feature event had exact probability about
   9.7373e-37. The original energy minimum was -93 at beta-independent ground
   state x=3315964355, and its beta=ln2 physical partition is recorded exactly.

2. Hidden planar model: n=32, s=36, rank23. Acquisition used135,663 scalar
   calls (finite cap542,652), plus4,341,216 configuration-bit writes. The learner
   reconstructed24 graph vertices and an embedding without either being given.
   A cached96-dimensional rational Pfaffian and a cached classical width-six
   elimination schedule agreed on12 reweighted/conditioned tasks. The latter
   won and was adopted. Eight feature-conditioned input samples required232
   later partition calls and no new energy observations.

3. New physical evidence on that acquired planar model: eight independent
   observations had overlap rank two, hence four signed sums per conditional
   mass instead of the unreduced256 Fourier terms. Four actual conditional
   input samples used592 signed sums and no new energy observations.
   Separate tiny checks compare32 physical parity events, six three-observation
   events,128 conditional parity numerators, and a complete conditional sampler
   law against exact enumeration. Singular signed factors were deliberately
   included;174 Pfaffian eliminations were charged for112 signed requests.

All nine reproduction stages pass with Python3.12.14 and the standard library.
Single-run timings are in the JSON receipts; they are not physical measurement
costs or a hardware performance claim. No full 2^48 table or 2^31 syndrome
enumeration was run. Those numbers, where shown, are exact size comparisons.

WHAT THE RESULT DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

The input interface is powerful and explicit: arbitrary specified binary
configurations can be prepared and their scalar energies read. The valid total
sparsity bound is supplied. Neither physical preparation feasibility nor a
general passive learner is established. In fact, passive samples can encode
learning parity with noise even when the energy has only one nonconstant term;
the exact rational example uses energy values0 and1 and beta=ln2.

Unrestricted future evidence also remains computationally hard. Independent
features plus general linear evidence already contain syndrome decoding. The
positive theorems therefore specify a closed feature-query family, or pay the
2^t cost for general physical observations. No universal efficient planner is
inferred from successful prediction.

The elementary Walsh acquisition is not the strongest known sparse-transform
algorithm. Cheraghchi-Indyk gives a stronger bounded-bit asymptotic guarantee.
Minimal trellises, constructive graph realization, planar Ising kernels,
knowledge compilation, and learned parity coordinates have substantial prior
art. Minimally Complex Models is a particularly close conceptual predecessor.
Historical novelty of this exact end-to-end composition remains unestablished.
The strongest retaining conventional algorithm may use every acquired object;
there is no measured benefit from an additional Astra execution layer.

READING AND REPRODUCTION

TRACTABILITY_PROOF.txt is the integrated argument; Section11 is the final
overlap-rank extension. ACQUISITION_PROOF.txt supplies the complete finite
learning construction. GRAPHIC_ACQUISITION_PROOF.txt and
PHYSICAL_PARITY_ADDENDUM.txt give the independent graph/signed derivations.

and costs needed for noisy acquisition and uniform rare-event accuracy.
RESULTS_AND_DECISIONS.txt gives comparisons and failed attacks. Sources are in
sources/PRIMARY_SOURCES.txt; all claims are scoped by what was actually read.

Run from any location:

    python /absolute/path/to/acquired_parity/reproduce.py

The driver runs sequentially, writes results/*.json, and reports each stage.
Root modules are in experiments/. No external libraries, network, supplied
graph, or supplied latent basis are required. All fixtures keep their hidden
ground truth outside the learner and compare it only after acquisition.
