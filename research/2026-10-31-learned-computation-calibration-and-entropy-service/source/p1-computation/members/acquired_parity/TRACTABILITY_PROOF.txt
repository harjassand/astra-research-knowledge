ACQUIRING A REPRESENTATION THAT SUPPORTS A FAMILY OF EXACT COMPUTATIONS
Astra continuation, 31 October 2026

Status: a scoped acquisition-and-compilation construction, with exact executable
checks. The components have substantial classical precedents. No new general
learning theorem, general planning theorem, or superiority over the strongest
retaining conventional algorithms is asserted.

1. QUESTION AND CONTRACT

Can scalar experiments discover an unknown coordinate system together with a
tractable computation in it, and can the result remain useful after future
weights and evidence change? The intended capability is repeated exact
normalization, rare-event conditioning, sampling, and (on the trellis branch)
optimization of a structured discrete energy model whose interactions initially
look dense in the controlled coordinates.

The controlled configurations are x in F_2^n. An unknown function is

    f(x) = theta_0 + sum_{j=1}^s theta_j (-1)^(a_j dot x).

The distinct nonzero masks a_j, their coefficients, their rank, their relations,
a graph, an ordering, and an inference program are all unknown to the learner.
The supplied promises are the known input dimension, a valid total Fourier
sparsity cap S (including a nonzero constant), and the availability of a paid
chosen-configuration scalar-return operation. The exact main construction uses
rational returns with bounded description length. Preparing arbitrary x is a
substantive premise, separately charged; this is not a passive-sample theorem.

ACQUISITION_PROOF.txt proves and implements a conservative acquisition stage.
Set b=ceil(log2(2S^2)), T=ceil(log_4(2/delta)), and
L=ceil(2S ln(2T/delta)). When b<n, its worst-case scalar cap is

    Qmax = T[(n+1)2^b + L].

It returns the exact spectrum with probability at least 1-delta, counting both
incorrect acceptance and exhaustion. When b>=n, the full 2^n-point transform
is used instead. All following statements are conditional on correct
acquisition; structural certificates are deterministic relative to that model.
Reusing an accepted model for arbitrarily many later queries does not spend a
fresh statistical failure allowance per query. It also does not improve the
original probability that the model was acquired correctly.

The future weight contract is explicit: supply one positive rational pair
w_j(0),w_j(1) for each retained mask, defining

    W(x) = product_j w_j(a_j dot x).

This permits reweighting retained interactions. It does not grant prediction or
inference for arbitrary new interactions that were never acquired. An energy
with bounded integer theta can, for example, be used at beta=ln 2: multiply the
Boltzmann pair (2^-theta,2^theta) by 2^|theta| to obtain positive integers.
That common scaling cancels from probabilities and is explicitly recoverable
for normalization. Large integer energy magnitudes can cause large weight bit
lengths; rational input length, not a unit-cost real exponential, is charged.

2. THE ACQUIRED ALGEBRA AND THE COST OF FUTURE EVIDENCE

Let A be the s-by-n binary matrix with rows a_j. For evidence Cx=z, elimination
either detects inconsistency or constructs x=x0+N_C u, where columns of N_C
are a basis of ker C. Put B=A N_C, y0=A x0, q=rank C, and r_C=rank B.
The possible learned-coordinate vectors are precisely

    y in y0 + im B.

Each such y has exactly 2^(n-q-r_C) preimages satisfying the evidence. If H is
a full-row-rank basis of the left kernel of B, the same set is Hy=H y0.
Both the constraints and the linear map back to actual x are computed by
elimination. No useful latent basis or preimage solver is supplied.

The exact raw dependency count is

    d_C = s-r_C = s+rank C-rank([A;C])
        = (s-rank A) + dim(row A intersect row C).

Proof: restriction of row A to ker C quotients out exactly row A intersect
row C. Hence rank(A N_C)=rank A-dim(row A intersect row C). Substitute the
dimension formula for the intersection. This proves the displayed identity.

Before exponential work, zero projected masks are constants; equal projected
masks combine by multiplication, with pair swaps for affine offsets. Any
combined pair with equal entries is another constant. Removing it changes the
active rank and thus the preimage multiplicity. Recompute both. The supplied
conditional compiler performs these steps; its complete proof is retained in
CONDITIONAL_INFERENCE_PROOF.txt. If g active masks remain with rank rbar, its
simple positive syndrome DP has 2^(g-rbar) states. It supports exact partition,
maximum weight and traceback, and exact rational sampling with a uniform
kernel lift. This is an upper bound, not a lower bound on computation.

3. CONSTRUCTING THE SMALLER CLASSICAL TRELLIS

Fix an order of s feature coordinates and a full-row-rank H with d rows.
For a feasible affine constraint Hy=t, let h_j be its columns. At cut i define

    U_i = span(h_1,...,h_i),   V_i = span(h_{i+1},...,h_s).

All reachable and completable partial syndromes form

    S_i = U_i intersect (t+V_i).

Take one feasible y*, and let r_i=sum_{j<=i} h_j y*_j. Then

    S_i = r_i + (U_i intersect V_i),
    chi_i = dim(U_i intersect V_i)
          = rank H_prefix + rank H_suffix - d.

The dimension formula uses U_i+V_i=F_2^d. Elimination computes bases for these
spaces, and enumerating the intersection gives exactly 2^chi_i states. A bit b
at position i+1 adds b h_{i+1}. Keep the transition only if it lands in S_{i+1}.
This constructs the entire trellis; neither its states nor its decomposition
are an oracle. Its transition count is at most twice the sum of the preceding
layer sizes. Binary linear-algebra work and state allocation are charged before
running the inference recurrence.

Sum-product on this graph computes the partition. Max-product or min-plus
computes an optimum with traceback. Backward sampling uses exact nonnegative
integer branch masses after clearing rational denominators. Finally solve the
acquired linear system and sample uniformly in the residual kernel to produce
an actual input x. Pure partition evaluation needs two layers; traceback and
the implemented sampler retain the forward layers and/or predecessor choices.

For this fixed order, distinct reachable/completable partial syndromes have
disjoint sets of feasible suffix bit strings: a common suffix would sum to
t+sigma for two different sigma, an impossibility. They therefore cannot be
merged in a deterministic exact ordered trellis recognizing the same code.
This explains the classical minimal-state count. It is not a lower bound on
all algorithms. The planar Pfaffian branch below can avoid a large trellis.

One may compute chi_i for a proposed order before allocating states. A large
profile can cause a declared compilation budget failure; it does not prove the
problem hard. Finding a globally best order is not granted. An arbitrary order,
a paid heuristic, an exhaustive order search with its full cost, or the proved
recognizer in the next section are separate legitimate choices.

4. AN ORDER IS ACQUIRED FROM A HIDDEN CHAIN

Consider distinct nonzero masks. Enumerate all pairs and use a mask-to-index
dictionary to find every triple {i,j,k} with a_i+a_j+a_k=0. This costs O(s^2)
mask operations, each on n-bit words. View each triple as a binary relation
vector. Check that these vectors span all s-rank A dependencies.

Build the overlap graph on triples, joining two when they share a feature.
The restricted recognizer accepts when this graph is a single path (a single
triple is allowed), adjacent triples share exactly one feature, and all other
pairs are disjoint. Features outside every relation are allowed. Other cases
return UNKNOWN, not a claim of intractability.

Every triangle in an accepted path has a private feature, so its relation
vector is independent of the other triangle vectors. A feature cannot belong
to three triangles: they would form a triangle in the overlap graph. The rank
check proves these are all relations. Walk the path. In the first triangle,
emit its two features not shared with the next triangle. At each later
triangle, emit the previously shared feature first, then its private feature,
deferring the next shared feature. Emit the final remaining features and then
all features in no relation.

In the triangle basis, the resulting nonzero check columns have the form

    e_1,e_1,e_1+e_2,e_2,e_2+e_3,...,e_{t-1}+e_t,e_t,e_t,

with the natural three-column single-triangle case. At every cut the prefix
and suffix spans meet in at most one dimension. Zero check columns for
independent features do not change the bound. Thus the acquired order has
width at most one. The executable additionally computes every cut rank to
certify the actual width. It originally emitted a private feature before the
previously shared feature in some bags, which can give width two; the ordering
was corrected rather than assuming the desired width.

The intended hidden family consists of independent masks u_0,...,u_t and the
2t+1 interactions {u_i} union {u_i+u_{i+1}}. All these masks may be dense in
the controlled x coordinates. It has rank t+1 and t dependencies, yet the
recognizer acquires a width-one exact computation without being given the u_i,
the adjacency, or their ordering. This is a concrete representation-acquisition
result. The resulting transfer-matrix computation is classical.

5. A QUERY FAMILY THAT PRESERVES THE ACQUIRED COMPUTATION

Once an exact feature trellis is retained, future evidence specifying any
subset of the acquired feature bits only deletes incompatible transitions.
Its state space and edge count cannot increase. The same retained graph
therefore computes, for every positive rational reweighting and every such
evidence event F:

    Z_F = sum_{x in F} W(x),
    P(F) = Z_F/Z,
    a maximum-weight feasible x,
    and exact conditional samples when Z_F>0.

An inconsistent event gives Z_F=0. There is no factor 1/P(F) in this exact
computation. Output bit length remains a real cost: a probability with a large
denominator is not compressed into a free real number. Exact sampling of a
non-dyadic rational branch probability has finite expected random-bit cost but
generally no finite worst-case stopping bound. The programs use rejection from
fair random bits and report consumed bits in reproducible runs. A seeded PRNG
is used for reproducibility, while the theorem assumes independent fair bits.

For rational weights with L-bit numerator and denominator, clearing denominators
pairwise gives an integer product/sum bound O(n+sL+s) bits. Trellis arithmetic
therefore costs the number of edges times a polynomial in this bit length.
The graph is retained across weights/evidence; forward masses must be computed
again. Copying the weight pairs prevents later caller mutation from changing
the sampling law after its forward table was built.

This closure is specific to evidence on acquired features. An arbitrary new
linear observation is translated and analyzed separately using Section 2.
There is no assertion that all everyday input-coordinate conditions are easy.

6. ACQUIRING A GRAPH, RATHER THAN A SMALL TRELLIS

The next branch treats K=A^T as a generator matrix for the learned-coordinate
code L=im A. If its binary column matroid is graphic, a graph G with labelled
edges can have cutspace L. The vertex labels are immaterial; feature-to-edge
labels must be retained. A merely boolean graphicness test is insufficient:
an actual graph is needed for execution.

A complete self-contained restricted recognizer is supplied. Its promise is a
simple 3-vertex-connected graph, at least four vertices, of maximum degree at
most a fixed Delta. Enumerate every nonzero rowspace word of weight <=Delta.
For support D, the supported rowspace has dimension

    rank K - rank(K restricted to E\D).

Since 1_D is already a word, dimension one proves support minimality, hence a
cocircuit. Test whether deleting D leaves the represented matroid connected,
using fundamental-circuit components obtained by elimination.

For a 3-connected graph, the nonseparating cocircuits are precisely vertex
stars. A vertex deletion leaves a 2-connected graph and hence a connected cycle
matroid. Conversely, a non-star bond separates two connected edge-bearing
sides, and deleting it leaves a matroid direct sum. Thus all vertex stars are
found under the degree bound. Each edge lies in two stars. Regard the stars as
vertices and those two incidences as its endpoints.

Acceptance is gated by exact equality between the reconstructed graph's
incidence rowspace and rowspace K, along with explicit simplicity and
3-connectivity checks. This verifies the representation, even on inputs outside
the completeness promise. Failure returns UNKNOWN.

The executable generates the bounded-weight words by matching equal
annihilator syndromes of ordered half-subsets. For each target size k, split a
sorted support after floor(k/2), require all left indices precede all right
indices, and match the two syndromes. This enumerates each word once. Work is
polynomial for fixed Delta, with potentially large constants/exponents; the
actual half-subsets and candidate pairs are recorded. It is not generally
competitive with the classical general graph-realization algorithm.

For the self-contained planar branch, also recover a bounded-degree graph for
L^perp. Its vertex stars must form cycles in the primal graph. Orient these
cycles consistently, check that each edge has two incident faces, check the
local rotation around each vertex, and check Euler characteristic two. These
checks construct and certify an orientable sphere embedding. Completeness is
claimed for the stated 3-connected planar primal/dual degree promises. The
executed cubic examples have dual degree at most six.

The broader classical route is available: eliminate K to [I|M], retain the
column permutation, and run constructive graphic-matrix recognition. CMR 1.3.0
documents graph and forest/coforest outputs based on Bixby-Wagner, with
O(k alpha(k,r)) recognition time for k nonzeros after elimination. This is a
real published algorithm and implementation, not an assumed recognition
oracle. It was inspected, not installed or executed in this environment. Our
executed completeness claim remains the self-contained restricted branch.

7. THE EXACT PLANAR KERNEL AND ITS CLOSED QUERIES

Given the acquired connected cubic planar graph, write spin bits z_v and edge
features y_e=z_u+z_v. Each edge factor has the expansion

    w_e(z_u+z_v) = a_e + b_e sigma_u sigma_v,
    a_e=(w_e(0)+w_e(1))/2, b_e=(w_e(0)-w_e(1))/2.

Summing over spins leaves only even-degree edge subsets. Hence

    Z_spin = 2^|V| product_e a_e
             sum_{F even} product_{e in F} (b_e/a_e).

For nonnegative factors with at least one positive value, a_e>0. The expansion
is algebraic; b_e may be negative, so exact signed arithmetic is used.

Replace each cubic vertex by a planar four-vertex K4 gadget: one center and
three ports. Each center-port edge has weight one, each port-triangle edge
weight 1/3, and corresponding ports across an original edge are joined with
weight b_e/a_e. With zero external ports matched, the three internal perfect
matchings have total weight one. With two external ports matched, the remaining
center-port edge gives weight one. Odd numbers of external matched ports leave
an odd number of gadget vertices and give zero. Therefore the decorated
perfect-matching polynomial equals the even-subgraph polynomial exactly.

The acquired rotation constructs the decorated sphere embedding. A dual-tree
algorithm orients edges with odd face parity, and verifies every face condition.
The corresponding skew matrix has a Pfaffian whose matching signs agree. One
explicit internal matching fixes the common sign. This is the classical
Fisher/Kasteleyn mechanism. The implementation evaluates its Pfaffian by exact
rational skew elimination. A connected graph has two spin assignments per cut,
so Z_code=Z_spin/2, and the original configuration partition is

    Z_x = 2^(n-rank A) Z_code.

No cancellation-based floating-point certificate is claimed. Clearing input
denominators gives polynomial-bit integer entries. Schur complements are ratios
of minors/Pfaffians; standard determinant bounds give polynomial intermediate
bit lengths with reduced rational arithmetic. Thus cubic matrix arithmetic and
polynomial bit sizes give an exact polynomial algorithm. A detailed proof and
operation-specific distinctions are in GRAPHIC_ACQUISITION_PROOF.txt.

Pinning acquired edge bits sets disallowed factor entries to zero. The graph,
embedding, gadget, and orientation remain valid, since a_e stays positive.
Inconsistent pins yield partition zero. The same retained kernel therefore
supports all positive rational reweightings and all acquired-feature pinning
queries. Sequentially evaluating the mass with the next feature pinned to zero
and subtracting from current mass gives exact branch probabilities. At most s
additional partition evaluations per sample, followed by a uniform GF(2) lift,
produce a sample of the original input. This acquires and executes the sampler;
one is not supplied as an oracle.

The implementation also constructs and retains a conventional min-fill
elimination schedule on the acquired graph. Its compiled table-size profile is
known before execution. When it is small, this executor is much faster on the
recorded examples and is adopted. The Pfaffian is a polynomial fallback, not a
claimed compact-instance performance winner. Both methods receive the exact
same learned structure and retain their reusable work.

8. TWO DISTINCT BOUNDARIES

Evidence boundary. Let A=I and w_j(0)=1,w_j(1)=1/2. Without evidence the model
is independent. Given Cx=z, its MAP task is minimum-weight syndrome decoding.
The general decision version is NP-complete (Berlekamp-McEliece-van Tilborg).
Even ordinary coordinate evidence can encode this after a hidden invertible
change: extend a full-row-rank check matrix H to an invertible T, let learned
bits be y=T^-1 x, and pin the first rows of x=Ty. This imposes Hy=b. The
complete acquired predictive model does not remove the computational burden.

Observation boundary. For a=(s,1) and x=(u,b), the distribution

    p_a(x)=2^-n [1+rho (-1)^(s dot u+b)],  0<rho<1,

has uniform u and b=s dot u plus independent noise of rate (1-rho)/2. Passive
mask recovery contains search learning parity with noise. Its negative log
density has just one nonconstant Walsh coefficient. Under the standard LPN
hardness assumption, even this simplest passive acquisition problem is not
polynomially solved. That is a conditional computational obstruction, not an
unconditional lower bound. Exact chosen-energy queries at 0,e_1,...,e_n recover
the mask by equality/difference in n+1 calls. Our active interface changes the
available information operation in a material and explicit way.

This boundary also lies inside the exact rational-energy contract. At rho=1/3,
use f_a(x)=(1-(-1)^(a dot x))/2, an integer-valued energy with coefficients
theta_0=1/2 and theta_a=-1/2, and temperature beta=ln 2. Its normalized weights
2^(-f_a(x)) give precisely the same LPN distribution. The active service returns
only zero or one, and queries at the coordinate vectors reveal the mask bits.
Thus irrational logarithms are not essential to the access separation.

9. PRECISION AND RARE EVENTS

Exact coefficients under nonzero bounded noise require a separation mechanism;
a known coefficient lattice plus sufficiently accurate repeated returns is one
such mechanism. All repeated observations are charged. A separate theorem in
BOUNDED_NOISE_AND_QUERY_ROBUSTNESS.txt assumes an amplitude floor and proves
support recovery by a larger isolation batch. It retains its inverse-delta
cost and does not import exact-equality validation into noisy observations.

If an actually acquired approximation satisfies ||f-fhat||_infinity<=eta, then
for every nonempty feasible set F, simultaneously,

    exp(-beta eta) Z_F <= Zhat_F <= exp(beta eta) Z_F.

This follows term by term before summation. Consequently log partition error
is <=beta eta and log event-probability error is <=2 beta eta. An exact
optimizer of fhat has energy regret <=2 eta on the same feasible set. There is
no rarity factor, because the control is uniform. Small average prediction
error alone does not give this guarantee; a rare, deep energy spike defeats it.
This accuracy argument does not make arbitrary F computationally tractable.

10. WHAT IS EARNED, AND WHAT IS NOT NEW

The construction earns masks, coefficients, relation spaces, actual order or
graph/embedding certificates, a retained computation, and a map back to inputs.
It demonstrates repeated exact tasks after acquisition, including very rare
feature evidence. It also exposes two distinct failures: unrestricted future
evidence can encode hard optimization, and passive observations can conceal a
computationally hard acquisition problem even for an easy final model.

Sparse Walsh learning, minimal trellises, graph realization, planar Ising
inference, and knowledge compilation all have strong established algorithms.
Minimally complex spin models and finite-field ICA already develop the broader
idea of learning useful parity coordinates. The new work here is a scoped,
checked end-to-end construction and its task/interface accounting. Original
priority for this exact combination has not been established. The current
evidence justifies retaining this mechanism and the certified query family;
it does not justify an additional special execution layer or a claim of a
civilisation-changing discovery.

11. STRONGER EXTENSION: GENERAL PHYSICAL EVIDENCE AT PAID OVERLAP RANK

The final investigation extends Section 5 beyond feature pins. The needed
operation is an EXACT SIGNED partition routine

    Phi(v) = sum_{y in im A} product_j v_j(y_j)

for arbitrary rational pairs, including negative entries. A positive
approximation oracle alone does not give this operation: cancellations can
destroy relative accuracy. Both the retained exact trellis/elimination programs
and the acquired planar Pfaffian have a constructive signed extension.

Signed planar extension. The algebraic Fisher identity and Kasteleyn sign
identity hold for signed rational weights. Nonzero negative a_e are harmless.
When q0 nonzero factors satisfy w_e(0)+w_e(1)=0, add the same indeterminate t
to both entries of each of those factors. Its a_e becomes t and its b_e is
unchanged. The exact code partition P(t) is a polynomial of degree at most q0.
All other a_e remain nonzero. Evaluating at t=1,...,q0+1 avoids every division
by zero. Finite differences give

    P(0) = sum_{j=1}^{q0+1} (-1)^(j-1) binom(q0+1,j) P(j).

An identically zero factor returns zero immediately. This costs at most s+1
ordinary rational Pfaffian eliminations per signed request, and adds polynomial
bit length from the interpolation nodes and coefficients. The same acquired
graph, rotation, and orientation are retained. The positive and signed APIs
are separate, so this additional operation is explicit and paid.

Overlap theorem. Let Cx=z be consistent, reduce its rows to q independent
equations, and transform z accordingly. Define

    t = dim(row C intersect row A)
      = rank A + q - rank([A;C]).

Assume a signed Phi routine has been acquired. Then the partition under ANY
such Cx=z can be computed using 2^t signed Phi calls, plus binary elimination
and rational arithmetic. The acquired-feature pinning contract remains valid
as additional base evidence. This is a fixed-parameter result in t, not an
unrestricted polynomial planning theorem.

Proof. For a physical character c define

    T_c = sum_x W(x) (-1)^(c dot x).

If c is outside row A, some h in ker A has c dot h=1. Pairing x with x+h
within every feature fiber proves T_c=0. If c=lambda^T A, then

    T_c = 2^(n-rank A) Phi(w^lambda),
    w^lambda_j(b)=w_j(b) (-1)^(lambda_j b).

Any two choices of lambda differ by a code annihilator and therefore agree on
every attainable y. The result is independent of the chosen solution.

Character orthogonality expands the physical evidence indicator:

    1{Cx=z} = 2^-q sum_{u in F_2^q} (-1)^(u dot z) (-1)^((C^T u) dot x).

Only U={u:C^T u is in row A} contributes. Since C has independent rows, the
map u -> C^T u is injective and dim U=t. Elimination constructs U: compute
relations among the rows of [A;C], project them onto the C-coefficients, and
extract a basis. This is not a supplied intersection or a search over 2^q
possibilities. Enumerating its actual 2^t elements and solving
lambda_u^T A=C^T u yields

    Z(C,z) = 2^(n-rank A-q)
             sum_{u in U} (-1)^(u dot z) Phi(w^lambda_u).

Thus the parameter equals the INCREASE in raw syndrome dependency d_C-d_0,
but the base code's possibly large dependency d_0 is handled by the acquired
partition kernel. This is strictly more informative than paying 2^d_C for a
generic syndrome program. It is still only a sufficient bound: arbitrarily
many feature pins can be processed directly without an exponential factor.

Conditional readouts. For a new physical parity c, the contributing coefficient
set is U_c={u:c+C^T u is in row A}. A solve in span([A;C]) finds whether it is
empty and, if nonempty, a representative u0. Then U_c=u0+U and has 2^t elements.
The same formula with c+C^T u gives the conditional signed numerator. Dividing
by Z gives the exact parity expectation; one binary parity event probability
is (1+(-1)^b expectation)/2. This covers ordinary single input bits as well as
parities, under bounded-overlap physical evidence.

Conditional sampling. Sequentially pin the ACQUIRED features, computing each
branch's physical-evidence mass by the formula above. This leaves A and U
unchanged throughout the self-reduction. After choosing all feature bits y,
solve [A;C]x=[y;z] and choose uniformly from that joint affine fiber. The joint
fiber size is constant, so the resulting input law is exactly proportional to
W(x) under Cx=z and any initial feature pins. At most (s+1)2^t signed partition
requests are needed per sample; the planar interpolation multiplier, integer
arithmetic, and expected fair-bit cost remain charged. No rejection with
acceptance probability P(Cx=z) is used.

The executable physical_queries.py implements this intersection, the signed
extension, general physical-evidence masses, conditional parity readouts, and
the joint-fiber sampler. It verifies all of these against complete tiny
distributions. On the already acquired n=32, s=36 model, eight independent
physical observations have overlap rank two, so four signed sums replace the
unreduced 256-term evidence expansion. Four conditional samples use 592 signed
kernel requests and no further energy observations. MAP under general physical
evidence is NOT claimed by this extension; signed linear sums do not supply a
maximization identity.

This extension is another proved composition using character orthogonality and
the acquired exact kernel. Its historical novelty has not been established.
