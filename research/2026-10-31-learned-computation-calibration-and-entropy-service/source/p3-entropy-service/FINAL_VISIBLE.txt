## 1. The all-control resource law

Let \(\ell\) unknown logical qubits be encoded into \(n\) physical qubits, with an independent auxiliary source \(\sigma\). Its entropy deficit, in bits, is

\[
c_0=n-\ell-S(\sigma).
\]

Use the independent-dephasing convention

\[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{ind}}
=\sum_i\gamma_i(t)(\Delta_i-\mathrm{id}),
\qquad
\Delta_i(\rho)=\frac{\rho+Z_i\rho Z_i}{2},
\qquad
u=\int_0^T\min_i\gamma_i(t)\,dt.
\]

Thus a physical off-diagonal decays as \(e^{-\int\gamma_i}\).

Suppose the completed logical channel has **full diamond error** at most \(\delta<1\). Define

\[
\eta=h_2(\delta/2)+\frac{\delta}{2}\log_2(2^{2\ell}-1),
\qquad q=\ell-\eta>0.
\]

If admitted interventions can remove at most \(A\) bits of joint reference–memory entropy, then

\[
\boxed{c_0+A+\eta\ge 2u q.}
\]

This covers arbitrary coherent encoders and Hamiltonian controls, and an arbitrary final receiver. Nonunital preparation or intermediate operations must have their entropy reduction charged. Every quantum system that can return information belongs inside the boundary.

The decisive proof step is

\[
\dot S(RB)\big|_{\mathrm{ind}}
\ge 2\gamma_*D(\rho_{RB}\Vert\Delta_{\mathrm{all}}\rho_{RB})
\ge 2\gamma_* I_c(R\rangle B).
\]

The actual receiver forces every preceding coherent information to remain at least \(q\). Hamiltonian control changes no joint entropy. Integrating and closing the initial and final entropy budgets gives the theorem.

For an initially pure ancillary bank with no outlets,

\[
T\le \frac{n-\ell+\eta}{2\gamma_*q}.
\]

At fixed high whole-register fidelity, this is a **linear lifetime ceiling**, with the expected inverse dependence on logical width.
