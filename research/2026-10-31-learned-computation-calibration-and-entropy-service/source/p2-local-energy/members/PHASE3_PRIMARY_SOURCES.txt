SELECTIVE PRIMARY SOURCES AND INHERITED ASTRA GOVERNANCE
31 October 2026

The proofs in phase3_synthesis are derivations in this continuation, not
claimed quotations or priority results. The following primary research
grounds mechanisms, comparison, and limitations.

LOCALITY AND ENERGY ADMISSION

Abanin, De Roeck, Huveneers, Exponentially slow heating in periodically driven
many-body systems, Phys. Rev. Lett.115,256803 (2015).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.01474
Foundation: factorial nested-commutator bounds and exponentially small local
high-frequency response. The present finite-time operator estimate retains
the actual [H_E,V], and does not claim this general suppression is new.

Abanin, De Roeck, Ho, Huveneers, A rigorous theory of many-body prethermalization
for periodically driven and closed quantum systems, CMP354,809 (2017).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.05386
Foundation: local large-energy-scale block isolation/prethermal conservation.
No full-cycle exponential accuracy law is inferred here from that theorem.

Mozgunov, Lidar, Completely positive master equation for arbitrary driving and
small level spacing, Quantum4,227 (2020).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.01095
Relevant distinction: finite-level-spacing assumptions in Davies reduction
and nonsecular coarse-grained alternatives. A reduced-state bound is not
silently promoted to a complete emitted-field instrument bound.

Beaudoin, Gambetta, Blais, Dissipation and ultrastrong coupling in circuit QED,
PRA84,043832 (2011).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.3990
Foundation for the warning that an unqualified bare local dissipator can
produce unphysical excitation when the interacting Hamiltonian is retained.

PHYSICAL COMPONENTS AND STRONG CONVENTIONAL COMPARATORS

Scarani, Ziman, Stelmachovic, Gisin, Buzek, Thermalizing quantum machines:
dissipation and entanglement, PRL88,097905 (2002).
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.88.097905
Repeated two-qubit interactions with supplied reservoir qubits are established
thermalization/cooling physics. The present fresh energy carriers are such a
resource, not a newly invented source of entropy disposal.

Shinkai, Hayashi, Ota, Fujisawa, Correlated coherent oscillations in coupled
semiconductor charge qubits, PRL103,056802 (2009).
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.103.056802
Experimental precedent for coherent/correlated charge motion in coupled DQDs.
It is not cited as validation of this particular perpendicular form factor.

Goan, Milburn, Wiseman, Sun, Continuous quantum measurement of two coupled
quantum dots using a point contact: a quantum trajectory approach,
PRB63,125326 (2001).
https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0006333
Basis for QPC current records and quantum trajectories. Its physical
approximations remain distinct from the exact finite-contact detector proof.

Nurizzo et al., Complete readout of two-electron spin states in a double
quantum dot, PRX Quantum4,010329 (2023).
https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.4.010329
Demonstrated repeated spin-blockade readout with tunable detuning and
interdot coupling from GHz to sub-Hz. Full discrimination of spin states is
not the same task as preserving coherent vectors in the whole triplet.

Niegemann et al., Parity and singlet-triplet high-fidelity readout in a silicon
double quantum dot at 0.5K, PRX Quantum3,040335 (2022).
https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.3.040335
Strong speed/fidelity comparator: above 99.9% at 20 microseconds and 99% at
4 microseconds, with the measured task and state-preparation limitations
reported by the authors. No speed advantage is inferred for the new cycle.

Borjans, Mi, Petta, Spin digitizer for high-fidelity readout of a cavity-coupled
silicon triple quantum dot, PRApplied15,044052 (2021).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.03862
Strong charge-sensor component; near-digital response and average spin
readout performance do not themselves establish our full-instrument contract.

Weinstein et al., Universal logic with encoded spin qubits in silicon.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.03605
Calibrated exchange-only encoded operations are an established competing
consumer of unpolarized gauge degrees of freedom. Control/calibration,
routing, leakage and full duration remain part of a fair comparison.

Rudolph, Virmani, The two-qubit singlet/triplet measurement is universal for
quantum computing given only maximally-mixed initial states, Nat.Commun.14,7800
(2023).
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10684540/
The consumer theorem is prior art. The present work does not claim its
universality anew or infer a fault-tolerance threshold from it.

Duan, Cirac, Zoller, Geometric manipulation of trapped ions for quantum
computation, Science292,1695 (2001).
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0111086
Strong holonomic/geometric alternative; a failed uncalibrated coherent pulse
does not defeat its different controls and physical operating domain.

Verstraete, Wolf, Cirac, Quantum computation, quantum state engineering, and
quantum phase transitions driven by dissipation, Nat.Phys.5,633 (2009).
https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.1447
Dissipative computational power is established. An explicit jump mechanism,
fresh entropy outlet and physical time remain resources in either approach.

OPTICAL ORGANIZING RESULT

Haegeman, Cirac, Osborne, Verschelde, Verstraete, Calculus of continuous
matrix product states, PRB88,085118 (2013).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.3935
Foundation for field-wavefunction derivatives and coincidence regularity.
The explicit source-uniform photon bandwidth budget is an application of
this formalism. Additional optical component references are in the optical
note, with its collection, receiver saturation and stopping limitations.
