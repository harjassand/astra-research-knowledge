VALIDATION FOR THE LOCAL-ENERGY-TRANSFER CONTINUATION
31 October 2026

Runtime: Python 3.12.14, NumPy 2.3.5, SciPy 1.17.0. Standard-library rational
arithmetic is used by the preserved exact certificates. SymPy is not required
or installed. Run programs from the extracted archive root with python3 and
the relative path below. Every program writes its associated JSON beside it.

Proofs and tests have different roles. The norm/recurrence claims are proved
analytically and independently audited. Eigenvalue, Choi, lattice and spatial
quadrature checks challenge the formulas; they are not device evidence or a
substitute for complete-channel proofs.

1. phase3_joint_window/check_local_admission.py

Exact BCH remainder, complete-unitary comparison, source-size and large-
diagonal-energy checks, piecewise noncommuting common source, and a resonant
source counterexample. A four-site source has spectral diameter 3.46e6 while
||[H_E,G]||=.12; actual t=5 error .0878566 is below .138898. A resonant source
gives leakage .999980; retaining the commutator accepts the failure, while
dropping it would falsely certify error below .020223. Independent audit:
LOCAL_ADMISSION_AUDIT.txt.

2. phase3_synthesis/check_cold_carrier_cycle.py

Full finite singlet-charge/helper/source Hamiltonians, both double states,
all source input states via effect eigenvalues, and capture/return recurrence.
20 parameter cases cover Delta=16,32,64,128, source diagonal scale through
1e4 and one/two source spins. Bias jumps and the charge projectors are ideal
in this particular program; physical ramps and detector errors are separately
charged by the proofs, not silently validated by this simulation.

A full two-charge-read Choi diagnostic keeps all four outgoing helpers
COHERENT and includes the source in the input/reference. Trace distance
.009164861 is below the exact-effect certificate .022635465 and analytic
contact certificate .197547147. This is one Choi input, not a computed
diamond norm. The common-outlet D_minus survives with probability one;
separate fresh-helper edge contacts give ideal survival 4.93e-32.
The independent conceptual audit is COLD_CARRIER_AUDIT.txt.
The same program tests the scalar short-pulse transfer bound on 45 switched-
source cases, conditional detuning/gain ratios through 100 and common-source
scale through 1e4. It distinguishes full conditional detuning from the
centered swap perturbation, and checks the final-read square-root correction.

3. phase3_fast_detector/check_perpendicular_energy_carrier.py

Full six-dimensional direct Coulomb integrals for mutually orthogonal,
disjoint compact-support data/helper modes. All data differential-charge
coefficients vanish by analytic parity, below 1e-18 numerically. R=2a yields
lambda=-.0028091862 C/a and the stated complete nuisance coefficients.
The compatible y-field compensation is computed, not assumed. Quadrature
refinement changes the worst coefficient by 1.4e-13. The box spectrum does
not itself establish a wide orbital hierarchy or material operating point.

4. phase3_fast_detector/check_energy_carrier_coulomb.py

Parallel localized-helper alternative. Retains the larger charge-helper
diagonal coupling and demonstrates the dangerous Delta=2chi energy crossing.
It is kept as a valid but weaker physical design and a compatibility warning.

5. phase3_synthesis/check_record_isolation.py

36 exact recorded finite-contact Choi checks, source speed 0,5,500,10000,
three OFF hopping strengths, and 1,4,8 contacts. All diagnostic distances
lie below the complete norm bound; maximum diagnostic/bound ratio .580727.
Largest completeness residual 1.64e-12. A phase-only record example retains
coherence norm 1 although the unconditional norm is .4.

6. phase3_fast_detector/check_detector.py

Quantum-source count effects, completely bounded full-record contraction on
source/reference test blocks over 2048 exact histories, finite-carrier
Duhamel envelopes, finite-bandwidth current filters and initial-state
cancellation, Gaussian imprecision, and a colored-reservoir counterexample.
Both switched and continuously ON guard constructions are checked. A
metallic-lead finite-time Born-Markov error is not certified by these checks.

7. phase3_joint_window/check_screened_source.py

Actual screened Coulomb coefficient sums on growing two-dimensional source
lattices. From 81 through 16641 sites, the transition-gain, conditional-shift
and interaction sums approach finite values; the corresponding unscreened
comparison demonstrates the missing uniformity. Analytical far-field
summability is the proof; the finite table is only a check.

8. phase3_joint_window/explore_double_well.py

Numerical eigenfunctions of a quartic double well and actual projected
barrier-mode profile. Raising the specified barrier parameter .5 ->1.5
suppresses bare hopping about 1029 times and mode-assisted hopping about
1888 times. Equal onsite mode force is checked; a grid refinement is included.
This is one closed potential model, not a complete biased device simulation.

9. phase3_joint_window/check_resonant_valve.py

Finite oscillator/source non-RWA Hamiltonian, retained bands, coherent dressing
and exact source commutator. Demonstrates spurious heating of the exact dressed
ground state by an unqualified bare D[a] outlet. The properly lowering band
operator annihilates that state. No finite-band full-field bath approximation
is inferred; this is why finite cold carriers became the main construction.

10. phase3_photon_closure/streaming_decoder_checks.py

Exact integer relay/endpoint algebra, all 126 informative polarization
histories through six photons in the full split model with noncommuting
quantum gain and z recycling, global-parity-preserving temporal mode mixing,
spectral commutator budget through source Hamiltonian scale 1e6, an independent
one-photon FFT/Parseval check, and one-photon cavity scattering/loss bounds.
Maximum full-model corrected factorization residual 5.26e-16; global-parity
mode-mixing residual 1.12e-16; FFT agreement 4.5e-6. The script imports the
preserved next_recycling_optics/recycling_and_sidebands.py. Multi-photon
saturation of the receiving cavity remains outside that one-photon check.

11. phase3_synthesis/cold_carrier_resource_budget.py

Dimensionless joint certificate budgets, including cold-helper count, two
charge reads, preparation error, contact/supply time, spin exposure, and the
energy separation sufficient for the FIRST-ORDER admission bound. For an
illustrative a=.2 and target 1e-3, one selection uses 11 cold helpers and two
reads, but its chosen e=3.125e-5 requires a sufficient gap about 8.59e4 in the
script's normalized units. This is deliberately not advertised as a device
window or compared as a speedup over the old apparatus. The large gap exposes
the resource that a contact-count-only report would conceal.

12. phase3_synthesis/check_local_spectral_frame.py

Complete transition-effect identities and high-frequency moment bounds in
48 conditional-source models with different eigenbases and degenerate
eigenspaces. Moments zero through three are checked as matrices, rather than
on a sampled source state. This validates the algebraic implementation only;
no finite-time bath reduction or incoherent replacement of unresolved
frequency components is inferred.
