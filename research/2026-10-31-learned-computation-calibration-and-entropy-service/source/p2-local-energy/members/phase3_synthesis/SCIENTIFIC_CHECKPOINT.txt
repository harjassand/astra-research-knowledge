ASTRA: LOCAL ENERGY TRANSFER AND A COMPLETE FINITE-CARRIER CYCLE
Scientific checkpoint, 31 October 2026

Chosen question
---------------
Can a quantum device operate next to an unprepared, dynamically active, large
source without requiring its entire many-body spectrum to fit below a carrier
gap? Can the irreversible operation then be made from an actual interaction,
rather than an assigned jump operator?

The answer earned here is a local, finite-time admission law and an explicit
electrostatic energy-transfer cycle. It makes the source-domain requirement
less global. It does not establish a usable material operating point or a
competitive computer.

Main result
-----------
For H=H_c+H_E+W+V, where H_c contains known carrier energies, W preserves the
intended energy blocks, and V couples them, solve [S,H_c]=-V with S anti-
Hermitian. Define s=||S||, c=||[S,H_E]||, w=||W||, v=||V||. Then

 ||U(t)-U_ref(t)|| <=2s+t exp(2s)(c+2sw+sv).

For two ordered bare orbital bands separated by Delta,

 ||U-U_ref|| <=2v/Delta
   +t exp(2v/Delta)(||[H_E,V]||+2vw+v^2)/Delta.

The source norm, temperature, total energy and minimum level spacing do not
enter. The commutator still does. The bound is on the full unitary, so it
includes coherent outgoing helpers and an arbitrary reference.

This distinction is physically constructive. In a screened source layer,
summable electrostatic transition coefficients give finite source-coupling
norms independent of layer area. For a density gain G=t0+sum b_i n_i,

 [H_E,G]=sum_<ij> t_ij(b_j-b_i)(c_i^dag c_j-c_j^dag c_i).

Large diagonal source energies commute with this G. Local source hopping and
the spatial gradient of its coupling set the energy-transfer burden. Full
transition-density terms must still be retained or bounded. Unbounded local
occupation or unrestricted continuum source momenta are not covered for free.

The resonance counterexample is retained and sharpened: an explicitly
noncommuting source at the bare gap gives leakage 0.999980. Dropping the
commutator would incorrectly certify an error below 0.021 in the tested
example. Thus 'bare gap protection' is still false; global source bandwidth is
also unnecessarily strong as a universal sufficient condition.

Microscopic mechanism
---------------------
Put the data orbital pair along x and a prepared helper ENERGY doublet along
y, on the perpendicular bisector, in disjoint channels. Direct Coulomb
projection gives

 H_DH=c+mu_z Z_H+mu_x X_H+K_D(kappa+zeta Z_H+lambda X_H).

All coefficients multiplying the data differential charge q-1 vanish by x
reflection in this admitted basis. The useful lambda is nonzero: data
transition quadrupole times helper transition dipole gives lambda~R^-4.
No helper localized-orbital overlap factor is needed. Disjoint support removes
data-helper exchange in the exact mode example.

The larger common helper force mu_x is NOT discarded. A scalar electric field
along y cancels it in the helper doublet while having zero nonconstant
projection on the data doublet. This earns a compatible relative control.
Bare hopping, quantum-source hopping, K_D Z_H, off-resonant helper flips,
intradata pair hopping, higher orbitals and source response remain in the
admission/error calculation.

For explicit three-dimensional compact-support modes at R=2a, the complete
Coulomb integral gives lambda=-0.0028091862 C/a, kappa=0.0058391689 C/a,
zeta=-0.0005762605 C/a, and mu_x=-0.1789970132 C/a. This is an exact-form-factor
construction with a numerically checked integral, not a fabricated-device
parameter set. A box doublet does not itself provide a wide isolation gap;
the actual doublewell spectrum must be used for a device design.

The complete cycle
------------------
CAPTURE: tune a selected double charge D to -Delta and supply a helper in its
lower orbital. The resonant transition is S0 <-> D1. Remove that helper and
supply another lower-state helper. A captured D begins the next contact in
the off-resonant D0 sink. No source reset or intermediate charge read is used.

READ: after a finite number of contacts, measure charge. Double charge means
singlet. Charge (1,1) is reported triplet, with a uniform bounded singlet miss.

RETURN: reverse the energy bias, then use separate selective contacts for
D_L and D_R with DIFFERENT fresh helpers. They transfer D_j0 -> S1. This
resolves the old dark-charge obstruction because the two outgoing helpers
retain orthogonal route information. Their fields are not coherently merged
into the rank-one outlet that traps D_minus.

VERIFY: measure charge again. Charge (1,1) certifies a separated singlet;
failure remains a flag or uses the previous paid reload/recovery cycle.

If the reference contact's surviving amplitude is at most a<1 and its full
isometry differs from the actual contact by e, then, on every actual history,

 sqrt(p_N) <= a^N+e(1-a^N)/(1-a).

This includes all coherent past helpers. Applying it to capture and the
two-edge return macrostep gives the complete half-diamond bound

 epsilon_ST <= sqrt(p_miss)+p_miss+p_return+2 delta_Q+eta_other.

The final charge measurement is essential for the probability cost p_return.
Before that measurement, coherent return failure can cost sqrt(p_return).
Every fine triplet map is I_3 on the whole triplet spin factor; preserving only
the label would not suffice.

Two complete charge reads replace the old read-after-every-attempt structure.
Fresh cold helper preparations are still required. The full elapsed time is
charged: helper supply/removal, contact, bias and barrier ramps, both sensor
exposures, loading, routing and any recovery. At contact time ~1/|lambda|,
local source errors scale as M/(|lambda|Delta), and conservative virtual-shift
errors as v^2/(|lambda|Delta). A weak useful matrix element can make this slow.

Supporting detector result
--------------------------
A maintained charge detector both measures and inhibits residual hopping.
Its relevant rate is Hellinger distinguishability of the COMPLETE retained
record. Opposite jump amplitudes with equal magnitudes dephase the average
state but provide no such count-record contraction.

For d charge sectors, recorded contraction rate Gamma, and hopping commutator
bound v, mu=Gamma-v>0 gives

 E_iso(T) <=[(d-1)e^(-mu T)+d v/mu+v^2 T/mu]/2.

The boundary v/Gamma term is retained. An exact finite-carrier proof gives
the corresponding discrete bound without a detector white-noise assumption.
A signed finite-bandwidth current filter removes the initial receiver state;
the detector can stay on throughout the guard intervals.

Alternative optical capital
---------------------------
The split-manifold photon branch now has a more general organizing algebra:

 P_X P_Z=(-1)^N P_Z P_X.

For even informative-photon number N, the two global polarization parities
commute and determine a Pauli correction on the emitter. For odd N they
anticommute and carry the exported logical qubit. One prepared memory qubit,
the same controlled phase on every photon, and X-resolved detection implement
a streaming decoder. Aggregate photon count/parity suffice; no pairing or
matched temporal modes are assumed. Planned drive-off plus full drain leaves
the qubit in a KNOWN carrier, but a required original-emitter endpoint still
has a return cost.

A full-field derivative identity gives a source-uniform photon bandwidth
budget in the admitted optical model. First-photon loss remains irreversible:
if first export occurs with probability p and collection efficiency is eta,
entanglement infidelity is at least 3p(1-eta)/4 without extra encoding/access.
Single-photon cavity scattering does not certify overlapping-photon operation.
The optical route is retained as theory and physical capital, not claimed to
outperform direct storage in the same prepared memory.

Strong comparisons and outcome
------------------------------
Calibrated exchange already provides universal encoded control and can be
faster. Spin blockade and high-contrast sensors already provide strong charge
readout; their measured label fidelities alone do not prove full coherent
triplet preservation. Repeated-interaction cooling is established and can use
this very Coulomb component. Holonomic schemes can use the same maintained
controls where their actual manifolds qualify. STP universality with mixed
input spins and unpolarized gauge encodings is prior art.

The increment is a uniform admitted-source guarantee, a concrete common
interaction plus compatible control, and a complete two-read capture/return
cycle. No comparative speed advantage, material window or error-correction
threshold is claimed. The most consequential result to develop further is
local physical admission: source energy can be extensive while its ability
to inject energy through a particular coupling remains bounded.
