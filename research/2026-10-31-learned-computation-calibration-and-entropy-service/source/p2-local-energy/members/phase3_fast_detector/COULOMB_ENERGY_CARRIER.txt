AN EXPLICIT COULOMB ENERGY CARRIER AND ITS COMPATIBLE RELATIVE CONTROL
Complementary construction requested by root, 31 October 2026

Scope: earn the direct two-orbital transfer matrix element, the complete
projected Coulomb interaction, and an ordinary compensating control. The
root supplies the energy-filter/repeated-collision theorem. No device
window, finite-barrier error, or advantage over calibrated collisional
cooling is inferred from a nonzero matrix element alone.

1. ROOT'S STRONGER PERPENDICULAR GEOMETRY

The data occupy a symmetric spatial doublet along x near the origin.
The helper has an ENERGY doublet along y, centered at (0,R,0). Its transverse
x profile is even. The two channel supports are disjoint. Let real normalized
data parity modes be psi_D,e(x), psi_D,o(x), and define localized modes

 w_L=(psi_D,e+psi_D,o)/sqrt(2),
 w_R=(psi_D,e-psi_D,o)/sqrt(2).

The common normalized transverse data density is g_D(y,z). Define

 rho_D,av=(|w_L|^2+|w_R|^2)/2,
 delta rho_D=|w_L|^2-|w_R|^2=2 psi_D,e psi_D,o,
 eta_D=w_L w_R=(psi_D,e^2-psi_D,o^2)/2.

delta rho_D is odd in x; rho_D,av and eta_D are even. The transition density
eta_D has zero monopole and dipole, but need not have zero quadrupole.
Complete orthogonalization therefore removes a naive leading monopole or
dipole association term. The useful term derived below is the surviving
quadrupole response, not the cancelled leading term.

The helper ground/excited modes are phi_e(s), phi_o(s), s=y-R, with common
even transverse x,z profile. Its transition density eta_H=phi_e phi_o is
odd in s and has a nonzero transition dipole. In a genuine doublewell
energy doublet this dipole is approximately half the separation of the
localized wells; it is not suppressed by the overlap between those wells.

For the fixed two-data-electron, one-helper-electron domain,

 rho_data=2 rho_D,av+(q-1)delta rho_D+K_D eta_D,
 rho_helper=rho_H,av+(Z_H/2)delta rho_H+X_H eta_H,

where q=n_L, K_D=sum_sigma(c_L,sigma^dag c_R,sigma+h.c.),
Z_H=|0><0|-|1><1|, rho_H,av=(phi_e^2+phi_o^2)/2 and
delta rho_H=phi_e^2-phi_o^2. The direct electrostatic interaction is the
integral of these two densities against C/|r-r'|, C=e^2/(4 pi epsilon).

Because EVERY admitted helper orbital is even in global x, reflection
x -> -x makes ALL coefficients multiplying q-1 vanish exactly:

 (q-1)I_H, (q-1)Z_H, (q-1)X_H all have coefficient zero.

This does not rely on the helper remaining symmetric in local y under a
known y-directed control. The full retained interaction is

 H_C=c I+mu_z Z_H+mu_x X_H
                +K_D (kappa I+zeta Z_H+lambda X_H).         (1)

The desired coefficient is

 lambda=C int eta_D(r) eta_H(r')/|r-r'| dr dr'.              (2)

There is no data singlet projector and no omitted conditional helper force.
K_D annihilates the entire triplet in the two-data-orbital domain and couples
the (1,1) singlet to each doubly occupied singlet with magnitude sqrt(2).
Thus the selected S0 <-> D1 matrix element is sqrt(2)lambda.

2. ACTUAL ORTHOGONAL ORBITALS AND NONZERO COEFFICIENT

An explicit compact-support example uses a rectangular channel. On [-a,a],

 psi_e(x)=cos(pi x/(2a))/sqrt(a),
 psi_o(x)=sin(pi x/a)/sqrt(a),

and both vanish outside. In each transverse direction use the normalized
even box ground state on [-b,b]. The data channel is longitudinal in x;
the helper channel is longitudinal in y and displaced by R. If R>a+b,
their supports are disjoint. Each doublet is orthonormal, and all four
orbitals are mutually orthogonal without a later Gram correction.

For these longitudinal modes,

 Q_D=int x^2 eta_D(x)dx = -3 a_D^2/(4 pi^2),
 d_H=int s phi_e(s)phi_o(s)ds = 32 a_H/(9 pi^2).

At large R along y, the first nonzero term in (2) is

 lambda=(3C/2) Q_D d_H/R^4+O(R^-6)
       =-4 C a_D^2 a_H/(pi^4 R^4)+O(R^-6).                 (3)

The finite transverse widths affect the higher terms, not this leading
coefficient. The expansion follows from
partial_x^2 partial_y (1/r)|_(0,R,0)=3/R^4.
The other leading coefficients are

 kappa=-C Q_D/(2R^3)+O(R^-5),
 mu_x=-2 C d_H/R^2+O(R^-4),
 zeta=O(R^-5).

For a well-separated data doublewell, Q_D contains the data overlap factor.
The helper transition dipole does not contain a helper overlap factor.
The intended term therefore scales as S_D/R^4 rather than the
S_D S_H/R^5 of the parallel localized-orbital construction below.

Nonzero asymptotic coefficient proves nonzero lambda for all sufficiently
large finite R, and continuity gives an open neighborhood of useful
geometries. The executable calculation retains the full Coulomb kernel at
finite separation rather than trusting only the multipole truncation.

For a_D=a_H=a, transverse halfwidth b=0.15a, coefficients in C/a are:

 1.5    0.0159804790   -0.0025217561   -0.0094474529   -0.3159862926
 2      0.0058391689   -0.0005762605   -0.0028091862   -0.1789970132
 4      0.0006266000   -0.0000172153   -0.0001642624   -0.0449788767
 8      0.0000752285   -0.0000005311   -0.0000100858   -0.0112549283

All three coefficients multiplying q-1 are below 1e-18 numerically and
vanish analytically by parity. The independent quadrature refinement
changes the worst matrix entry by 1.4e-13. The integral uses

 1/r=(2/sqrt(pi)) int_0^infinity exp(-u^2 r^2)du,

which separates the six spatial integrations into one outer integral and
three two-dimensional integrals. Disjoint support provides an explicit
exponentially small upper bound for the truncated outer integral. The
spatial quadrature convergence is numerical evidence, not an interval-
arithmetic proof of every displayed decimal.

3. AN ORDINARY CONTROL THAT PRESERVES THE USEFUL TRANSITION

The common helper X force mu_x is much larger than lambda. Merely
diagonalizing a helper dominated by mu_x X can rotate the intended
lambda K_D X into a diagonal coupling, removing the R^-4 transition.
It is insufficient to call that diagonalization an automatic solution.

There is an explicit compatible scalar correction. A y-directed electric
potential F y projects on the helper as a common scalar plus

 F d_H X_H,

because both diagonal matrix elements of the local coordinate s vanish
by parity. Choose F=-mu_x/d_H. This cancels the known common X force.
On the data doublet, the SAME field has zero projected matrix because
the common data transverse y mode is even. It supplies neither data
charge bias nor data hopping in the admitted subspace. The helper gap
then includes its known common Z shift:

 Delta=Delta_bare-2 mu_z

with the convention that |0> is the lower Z=+1 level. An ordinary even
y-dependent potential or helper barrier control adjusts this gap.

At R=2a, the required projected compensation is F=0.4968646058 C/a^2.
This is a known scalar relative control, not a measurement or preparation
of E and not an arbitrary operator-valued counterterm. Its higher-mode
matrix elements and the response of E to the field remain part of the
joint physical admission. For large R, compensating the leading gradient
also removes that gradient from higher helper matrix elements; one should
bound the actual SUM of known potentials rather than unnecessarily charge
two large cancelling terms independently.

The exactly solvable box example earns the coupling and control algebra.
It does not provide a wide helper-doublet/high-orbital hierarchy. A genuine
doublewell energy doublet has a tunably small splitting below its higher
orbital gap while retaining an order-separation transition dipole. Its
actual wavefunctions, source-induced distortion and required compensation
must be used to establish a useful numerical window.

4. ALL REMAINING TERMS AND THE ABSORBING CHANNEL

After the scalar helper compensation, equation (1) contains the desired
lambda K_D X_H and the single-data-transition terms

 K_D [G_E+kappa I+zeta Z_H],

where G_E is the inherited retained active barrier source. These terms are
not dropped or assumed reset. The complete data charge energies, including
intradata exchange, pair hopping and source-dependent diagonal shifts, also
remain. The other double-occupation state must be included and detuned
or handled explicitly by the root's filter.

For the selected S/D pair, with actual calibrated helper gap Delta and
data D energy -Delta relative to S, the unperturbed energies are

 S0: 0,    D1: 0,    D0: -Delta,    S1: +Delta.

The desired pair S0<->D1 is resonant; the counter-rotating K_D X_H pair
D0<->S1 has gap 2Delta. Single-data K terms have gap Delta. A fresh helper
|0> followed by an interaction and removal is ordinary energy-selective
collisional cooling. In the ideal selected resonant model, a pulse of
angle theta=sqrt(2)|lambda|t/hbar gives

 S0 -> cos(theta) S0 - i sin(theta) D1,
 D0 -> phase times D0.

Tracing or collecting the outgoing helper gives a channel with a completed
transfer branch |D><S| sin(theta), and a no-transfer branch retaining
|D><D|+cos(theta)|S><S|. The data triplet is untouched except for its common
source/helper channel. A full pi/2 transfer is possible for calibrated
scalar lambda; repeated partial swaps are also possible. Fresh ground
orbitals, relative controls, helper routing/collection and any helper reset
are prepared or maintained resources. The process does not cool an
arbitrary unprepared helper for free.

The same lower-level helper can implement return. After a selected-D
capture, retune that D from energy -Delta to +Delta. Then D0<->S1 is
resonant and S0 is absorbing. The ramp supplies work; the outgoing helper
carries the released energy. This uses no prepared excited helper, but
requires the data-energy reversal, its finite ramp spectrum, its source
shifts and isolation of the other D to be qualified in both phases.

An absorbing state creates a collection issue: if only the first excited
outgoing helper heralds S, missing that helper leaves D trapped and causes
a false-T floor. More fresh ground helpers cannot make a perfectly
absorbing D re-emit. Collection error must be paid, final charge can be
read by the independent QPC construction, or controlled return/recapture
cycles can provide repeated herald opportunities. A label/stopping resource
is not supplied merely by the existence of an irreversible transfer.

ENERGY FILTER LIMIT. A large scalar Delta alone does not remove active
source sidebands. The offband G_E and source-helper terms must satisfy
the root's quantitative filter admission. Its effective second-order
shifts, approximately G_E^2/Delta in a benign limit, matter over the whole
transfer time t~hbar/|lambda|. A condition resembling h^2/(Delta|lambda|)
small can be more demanding than h/Delta small. Unknown helper gap/axis
terms B_z,E Z_H and B_x,E X_H must be retained. A bare norm envelope b
costs b t/hbar without an additional oscillatory/commutator bound; it does
not justify treating a rapidly changing source as static detuning.

Distance alone does not solve this burden. For a nearby localized source,
helper differential fields can fall as R^-2 and transition-curvature fields
as R^-3, while lambda falls as R^-4. Shielding, source geometry, maintained
relative control, or a stronger source admission is substantive.

5. SPIN AND ORBITAL ADMISSION

With disjoint data/helper supports there is no one-particle transfer and
every data/helper exchange Coulomb integral is exactly zero. The direct
Coulomb Hamiltonian acts only through densities and orbital transitions,
so it is invariant under DATA spin rotations alone. The source cannot be
declared harmless merely because data plus source together are rotationally
invariant. A Heisenberg exchange with source/helper spin would violate the
required multiplicity action.

For finite barriers the support idealization must be replaced by actual
tail and exchange bounds. Cross-channel exchange is controlled by overlaps
and can decay exponentially with barrier separation, whereas the useful
direct Coulomb coupling decays polynomially. This gives constructive
separation in a restricted orbital model, but accumulated spin-breaking
error still depends on the full elapsed loading, interaction, routing and
return duration. Higher data orbitals can admit triplet motion even when
data-only spin symmetry holds; they require their own joint-domain bound.
The compact-orbital calculation does not override that inherited correction.

6. PARALLEL LOCALIZED-ORBITAL ALTERNATIVE: VALID, WEAKER, WITH A RESONANCE TRAP

If both data and helper use localized modes built from even/odd pairs along
x, and their centers are separated in y, both transition densities are even
quadrupoles. Global x reflection removes (q-1)X_H and K_D Z_H, but permits
the stronger conditional diagonal interaction chi(q-1)Z_H. Then

 H_C=c+chi(q-1)Z_H+kappa K_D+xi X_H+lambda K_D X_H.

For symmetric orthogonalized Gaussian pairs, eta=A[ rho_center
-(rho_L+rho_R)/2 ], A=S/(1-S^2). The useful far-field coupling is

 lambda~9 C A_D A_H a_D^2 a_H^2/(64 R^5),

whereas chi~C a_D a_H/(2R^3). For the explicit compact box modes used in
check_energy_carrier_coulomb.py,

 lambda~81 C a_D^2 a_H^2/(64 pi^4 R^5),
 chi~2048 C a_D a_H/(81 pi^4 R^3).

All finite 3D coefficients are calculated, not just lambda. At R=a,
chi=0.1465417 C/a, kappa=0.0169119 C/a,
xi=0.0338237 C/a, lambda=0.00391189 C/a.

There is an important full-energy correction. With helper gap Delta and
selected D bare energy E_D, S0<->D1 resonance requires E_D=-Delta+chi.
The actual four energies become

 S0=D1=0,   D0=-Delta+2chi,   S1=Delta.

The counter-rotating pair has detuning 2(Delta-chi), while the single-data
and single-helper transitions have gaps Delta and Delta-2chi. At
Delta=2chi, the alleged absorbing state D0 is resonant with unwanted
one-body channels. A sufficient simple gap choice is Delta>2chi+g_min.
This trap is absent from the perpendicular construction because every
q-dependent helper matrix element vanishes by symmetry.

7. COMPARISON AND RESEARCH DECISION

Fresh low-energy ancillas performing resonant partial swaps are established
collisional thermalization, not a new irreversibility principle. Primary
examples include Scarani et al., Thermalizing Quantum Machines:
Dissipation and Entanglement, Phys. Rev. Lett. 88,097905 (2002),
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0110088
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.88.097905

Electrostatically coupled semiconductor charge qubits already support
correlated coherent dynamics; a relevant primary component comparison is
Shinkai, Hayashi, Ota and Fujisawa, Correlated Coherent Oscillations in
Coupled Semiconductor Charge Qubits, Phys. Rev. Lett. 103,056802 (2009),
https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.0805
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.103.056802

The useful increment here is an explicit data-spin-preserving Coulomb
implementation compatible with a retained active source, including the
full projected interaction and a scalar compensation that does not alter
the data doublet. Root's source/filter theorem, if its hypotheses can be
met jointly, is what could distinguish this from ordinary calibrated
cooling or exchange. The perpendicular geometry is the stronger opening:
it removes conditional helper shifts, avoids helper overlap suppression,
and makes the common helper-force correction an explicit one-particle
electric control. It remains necessary to establish the complete
source/orbital window and full elapsed-time error; these calculations alone
do not establish a fast gate or a practical advantage.
