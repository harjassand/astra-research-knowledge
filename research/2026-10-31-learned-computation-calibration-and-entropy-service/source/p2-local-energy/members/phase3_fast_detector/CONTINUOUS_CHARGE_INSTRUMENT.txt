CONTINUOUS CHARGE DETECTION WITH A RETAINED QUANTUM SOURCE
Research construction and domain audit, 31 October 2026
Authoring branch: fast_charge_instrument; root owns synthesis.

STATUS

This replaces the deliberately conservative reset--Ramsey--escape charge
meter with one maintained tunnel-contact exposure. It also identifies a
record-sensitive contraction that the root construction can use to suppress
OFF hopping. The probabilistic and instrument statements below are proved
on their admitted detector model. Ordinary Coulomb coupling and lead
tunnelling generate that model; a finite-bandwidth continuum qualification
is stated separately, not silently supplied by the theorem.

No superiority over equally informed conventional QPC, rf-SET, or resonator
readout is claimed. The advance is a guarantee that survives unprepared,
noncommutatively evolving retained source degrees of freedom, and an
explicit relation between usable record information and suppression of
coherence between charge sectors. Established fast sensors are components
to use, not weak alternatives to defeat.

1. PHYSICAL COUPLING BEFORE THE MARKOV MODEL

Let q=n_L=0,1,2 for two data electrons in two admitted spatial orbitals. The
projected data density is

 rho_D(x)=sum_(i,j=L,R),sigma w_i*(x)w_j(x)c_i,sigma^dag c_j,sigma.

Coulomb coupling to a nearby tunnel contact gives

 H_C=int dx dr rho_D(x) V(x-r) psi_contact^dag(r)psi_contact(r).

The diagonal density terms change the contact potential by

 U_q(r)=U_base(r)+q[Phi_L(r)-Phi_R(r)],

where the fixed total data charge has been absorbed in U_base. A contact
placed closer to L has a nonzero Phi_L-Phi_R on an open set of geometries.
Its three elastic tunnel amplitudes tau_q are different on an open set of
control settings. Their dependence on q need not be exactly linear. A
small-potential expansion often gives tau_q=tau_0+q chi. One explicit
scattering construction is a rectangular barrier U_q=U_0+q deltaU of width
a, with deltaU>0 and U_0 above all admitted incident energies E. Its
transmission is

 D_q(E)=[1+U_q^2 sinh^2(a sqrt(2m(U_q-E))/hbar)
                                      /(4E(U_q-E))]^-1.

For x=a sqrt(2m(U-E))/hbar, the logarithmic derivative of the second
denominator term is 2/U+[x coth(x)-1]/(U-E)>0. Thus all three transmissions
are strictly ordered throughout a finite energy window. Integrating over
occupied incident modes preserves the ordering. This explicit geometry
establishes a possible charge-dependent detector, not a semiconductor
device parameter window or a claim of exactly flat transmission.

In localized lead modes the transfer Hamiltonian is the ordinary bilinear

 H_contact=sum_kl [ A_kl(q,E)c_R,l^dag c_L,k+h.c. ],
 A_kl(q,E)=sum_q P_q tensor [tau_q,kl I_E+B_q,kl].

No spin or singlet projector is inserted. For spin-independent Coulomb
coupling and spin-independent lead tunnelling, every fine operation acts
identically on the three data-triplet orientations. The outgoing contact
electron spin is retained in the reservoir; it is not a measured data spin.
This requires symmetry under rotations of DATA spins alone. Exchange with
a spinful contact or source is a separate physical error.

The SAME Coulomb expansion also contains w_L* w_R transition densities.
They induce detector-assisted data hopping and transitions into higher
data orbitals. They cannot be discarded because the intended charge term
is capacitive. They are excluded from the exact charge-QND part and charged
as errors or treated by the root's monitored-OFF isolation construction.

Real, common-sign tau_q can be arranged in a time-reversal-symmetric,
single-barrier transfer model with nodeless localized lead modes, away from
a transmission zero. This is useful but NOT necessary below. Scattering
phases and extra environmental channels are retained. A dephasing rate
inferred without the observed record is not substituted for a measurement
rate.

2. THE ADMITTED COUNTING MODEL

In the tunnel, weak-coupling, reservoir Markov domain, a forward transfer
has a coherent charge-diagonal jump, not three independent projective jumps:

 L=sum_q P_q tensor L_q.

For a flat transport window, L_q=sqrt(gamma)(tau_q I+B_q). More generally,
there may be any number of charge-diagonal observed or unobserved channels,
and charge-diagonal source Hamiltonians. The ONLY signal condition needed
for counting is the uniform operator bound on the observed-event effect

 lambda_q^- I <= R_q(t,h) <= lambda_q^+ I,
 R_q=sum_(observed channels nu) L_q,nu^dag L_q,nu.             (1)

Bounds hold on the whole admitted source domain and after every actual
past h. Source Hamiltonians may be arbitrary and noncommuting in this
admitted model. Detector-source backaction is retained, never reset.

For the one-channel example, ||B_q||<=b_q gives

 lambda_q^- = gamma(max(|tau_q|-b_q,0))^2,
 lambda_q^+ = gamma(|tau_q|+b_q)^2.                           (2)

Relabel the charges if needed so that the intervals are strictly ordered:

 lambda_0^+ < lambda_1^- <= lambda_1^+ < lambda_2^-.          (3)

Given ANY actual past, the conditional count intensity is Tr(R_q rho_E|h)
and hence lies in (1). Conditional rho_E|h can be coherent, mixed, steered
by the record and correlated with a reference. Neither iid trials nor a
classical replacement of E is used.

Independent detection loss with efficiency eta changes observed bounds to
eta lambda_q^+/-; missed transfers remain in the source evolution. A known
independent dark-count rate adds to both endpoints. Source-dependent loss
can be bounded if its observed effect obeys (1). Dead time or an amplifier
filter is not automatically independent thinning; section 5 treats a
specific finite-bandwidth receiver.

3. FINITE-TIME THREE-CHARGE DISCRIMINATION

Write D(x||lambda)=x log(x/lambda)-x+lambda. For x>lambda_q^+,

 P_q[N_T/T >= x] <= exp[-T D(x||lambda_q^+)].                 (4)

For 0<x<lambda_q^-,

 P_q[N_T/T <= x] <= exp[-T D(x||lambda_q^-)].                 (5)

Proof: the point-process exponential martingale has conditional drift
(exp(s)-1)lambda(t,h). For s>=0 use the upper intensity endpoint; for s<=0
use the lower endpoint because exp(s)-1 is negative. Therefore

 E_q exp(s N_T) <= exp[T(exp(s)-1)lambda_q^+]  (s>=0),
 E_q exp(s N_T) <= exp[T(exp(s)-1)lambda_q^-]  (s<=0).

Chernoff optimization gives (4)--(5). The same proof follows from the
trace of the tilted quantum generator. It holds for every initial E plus
reference state, so it is an OPERATOR EFFECT bound, not only an averaged
probability assertion.

For two adjacent disjoint intervals, put a=lambda_q^+, b=lambda_(q+1)^-.
The threshold

 x(a,b)=(b-a)/log(b/a)                                      (6)

equalizes D(x||a) and D(x||b). Classify by the two thresholds. Let c be the
smaller adjacent exponent. In each charge sector the error effect obeys

 0 <= E_wrong,q <= p I,    p=2 exp(-cT).                    (7)

Thus one exposure has T=O(log(1/p))/c. There are no hundreds of separately
prepared, emptied and reloaded orbital probes. The reservoirs, bias,
receiver, collection and control switches are maintained resources.

COMPLETE INSTRUMENT. Assume q is exactly preserved. For every fine record h
the physical Kraus operator is K_h=sum_q P_q tensor K_q,h (allow extra
Kraus labels in h). Let m(h) be the reported charge. The ideal comparison
instrument first projects q, then applies the ACTUAL q-sector maps K_q,h,
and reports q. It retains the same source histories on each fixed-q input.

There is a direct Stinespring comparison with half-diamond error <=sqrt(2p).
Copy h and m(h) into output/environment registers for the real instrument;
copy h and q for the comparison. Correct-label amplitudes coincide. Wrong-
label amplitudes have orthogonal label copies and squared difference 2.
Different q remain orthogonal in the data output, so the dilation difference
norm is at most sqrt(2p). Tracing either copy gives the desired classical
record instruments. The inherited conservative 2 sqrt(p)+2p bound is also
safe. These statements do NOT assert identity on every vector of the whole
q=1 space: retained singlet/triplet-dependent exchange is allowed. Within
the triplet irrep, data-only SU(2) makes every fine map a scalar on its three
orientations, which is the property needed by the complete S/T cycle.

4. RECORD INFORMATION CONTROLS OFF-DIAGONAL CONTRACTION

This is stronger than the label-error statement and directly useful for
the root's monitored-OFF mechanism.

Fix two charge sectors q,p with lambda_q^+<lambda_p^-. Let Phi_qp^t be their
off-diagonal propagator, including the complete CLASSICAL observed count
record and retaining E and any reference. Then

 ||Phi_qp^t||_(1,cb) <= exp(-Gamma_qp t),
 Gamma_qp=1/2 (sqrt(lambda_p^-)-sqrt(lambda_q^+))^2.          (8)

Here the norm on classical records is the sum/integral of block trace
norms. This is not a claim that a coherent Stinespring environment loses
information; its isometry does not contract trace norm.

Proof. Factor any input off-diagonal X=A B^dag with
||A||_2^2=||B||_2^2=||X||_1 using the polar decomposition. For each record h,
the completely positive instrument Cauchy--Schwarz inequality gives

 ||Phi_qp,h(X)||_1 <= ||X||_1 sqrt(p_q[h] p_p[h]),            (9)

where p_q,p_p are the count-history laws from normalized A A^dag and
B B^dag. It is enough first to use a fine Kraus/Stinespring dilation and
the Hilbert--Schmidt product bound, then trace unobserved outputs. The
effect bounds are unchanged when an arbitrary reference is included.

Let F(t)=int sqrt(p_q[h]p_p[h]) dh be their classical Hellinger affinity.
For a shared past h, the next infinitesimal no-count and count probabilities
multiply the affinity density by

 sqrt((1-lambda_q dt)(1-lambda_p dt))+sqrt(lambda_q lambda_p)dt
 =1-1/2(sqrt(lambda_q)-sqrt(lambda_p))^2 dt+o(dt).

Both intensities are conditional on that SAME observed past, but their
quantum conditional source states can differ. The disjoint intervals imply
F'(t)<=-Gamma_qp F(t). Integration and (9) prove (8). Time-dependent interval
bounds replace Gamma_qp t by its time integral. This applies after any
actual earlier history, as required for composition.

CRUCIAL PHASE COUNTEREXAMPLE. For scalar amplitudes ell_q,ell_p the exact
record contraction is

 exp[-(|ell_q|-|ell_p|)^2 t/2],

while unconditional dephasing has rate |ell_q-ell_p|^2/2. If ell_p=-ell_q,
the counts are identical, each count adds a known relative phase, and the
full classical-record off-diagonal norm is unchanged. The unconditioned
density matrix can nevertheless dephase rapidly. Large ensemble dephasing
alone does not prove (8). Complex fine phases are allowed; distinguishable
rate magnitudes, not a chosen real gauge, supply the contraction.

OVERLAPPING INTERVAL COUNTEREXAMPLE. If the admitted class contains static
source states that yield the same scalar count intensity for two charges,
their entire count-history laws are equal. No universal count-only
classifier can distinguish those two allowed hypotheses. Interval overlap
is a sufficient warning, not a claim that every overlapping model fails:
temporal correlations, marked energies, phases, or another detector could
contain additional information.

5. ONE FINITE-BANDWIDTH CURRENT RECEIVER, WITH ITS INITIAL STATE REMOVED

Individual electrons need not be resolved by the electronics. Consider a
calibrated one-pole current receiver

 dr(t)=-r(t)dt/tau_a+(e/tau_a)dN_t.

Observe a central interval [0,T], a preguard [-t_b,0] and a postguard
[T,T+t_s]. Apply these signed integration weights:

 w=-1/(exp(t_b/tau_a)-1)                  on [-t_b,0],
 w=1                                    on [0,T],
 w=1/(1-exp(-t_s/tau_a))                  on [T,T+t_s].       (10)

First suppose the contact transfers only on [0,T]. Then EXACTLY, for every
event sequence and every initial receiver value,

 int_(-t_b)^(T+t_s) w(t)r(t)dt=e N_T.                        (11)

The coefficient of every arrival in [0,T] is e; the initial homogeneous
exponential has coefficient zero. This is an explicit instance of the
inherited temporal-filter construction. Choosing t_b=t_s=tau_a keeps the
weights finite; guard duration is paid.

STRONGER VERSION: KEEP THE DETECTOR ON THROUGHOUT BOTH GUARDS. This avoids
an unmonitored settling interval precisely where OFF hopping is to be
suppressed. The same filter gives

 int w(t)r(t)dt/e = Z_N = int_(-t_b)^(T+t_s) k(s)dN_s,

where d=-1/(exp(t_b/tau_a)-1), c=1/(1-exp(-t_s/tau_a)) and

 k(s)=d+(1-d)exp(s/tau_a)                      for -t_b<=s<=0,
 k(s)=1                                      for 0<=s<=T,
 k(s)=c[1-exp(-(T+t_s-s)/tau_a)]               for T<=s<=T+t_s.

In every interval 0<=k<=1. The initial receiver still cancels exactly.
The effective signal time is

 A=int k(s)ds=T-t_b/(exp(t_b/tau_a)-1)
                      +t_s/(1-exp(-t_s/tau_a)).             (11b)

For equal guards t_b=t_s=tau_a, A=T+tau_a while the actual monitoring
duration is T+2tau_a. Convexity gives exp(s k)-1<=k(exp(s)-1) for either
sign of s. Consequently the same point-process martingale proof gives

 E exp(s Z_N)<=exp[A lambda_q^+(exp(s)-1)]       for s>=0,
 E exp(s Z_N)<=exp[A lambda_q^-(exp(s)-1)]       for s<=0.

All the Poisson Chernoff statements therefore hold for Z_N/A with T
replaced by A, although Z_N is not an integer count. No unobserved gap or
reset is introduced; root's monitored-OFF bound can use the full actual
monitoring duration. This version is preferred for physical compatibility.

A quantum linear receiver needs the corresponding linear-dilation
treatment and fresh input field; the current equation by itself describes
the classical current chain, not arbitrary quantum backaction from
electronics.

With independent additive Gaussian current imprecision of two-sided
white-noise convention E[z(t)z(s)]=S_z delta(t-s), the filtered charge in
units e is Y=N_T+Z in the central-only variant, or Y=Z_N+Z in the
continuously-on variant, with

 Var Z=(S_z/e^2)[T+t_b/(exp(t_b/tau_a)-1)^2
                       +t_s/(1-exp(-t_s/tau_a))^2] = V_T.  (12)

For an upper threshold xT in the central-only variant the exponent becomes

 sup_(s>=0) {s xT-lambda_q^+ T(exp(s)-1)-V_T s^2/2},         (13)

and for a lower threshold use s<=0 and lambda_q^-. In the continuously-on
variant replace T by A everywhere in (13) except that V_T remains the
actual filter noise variance (12). Thus one low-pass analog record still
gives exponentially improving charge discrimination at fixed
noise spectral density and positive rate contrast. No artificial
count-resolving amplifier bandwidth is needed. For colored Gaussian
imprecision replace (12) by the calibrated quadratic filter variance.
Detector-independent bounded offsets can instead widen the thresholds.

The construction requires a calibrated impulse response, maintained
collection and a noise/energy envelope. In the central-only variant,
transport in the guards must be negligible or separately modelled; the
continuously-on version instead includes it in the useful signal. Opening,
closing, bias ramps and electronics latency count in the actual cycle time.
A merely unknown amplifier without any noise/energy bound is not admitted.

6. RESERVOIR AND ORBITAL ADMISSION: WHAT SOURCE SPEED DOES AND DOES NOT COST

The ordinary microscopic QPC derivation uses thermal free-electron leads,
small contact transparency, weak coupling, fresh/factorized incident
reservoirs, sufficiently short reservoir memory, and approximately flat
transfer amplitudes and lead density of states in the relevant transport
window [1,2]. At low temperature and forward bias,

 gamma_0=2 pi nu_L nu_R eV/hbar,
 lambda_q=gamma_0 |tau_q|^2.

Here tau has units of energy and gamma_0 has units 1/(energy^2 time).
Using a dimensionless transmission D_q instead gives lambda_q=eV D_q/h
per spin-resolved channel. Spin degeneracy can be included as two channels.

The exact theorem for an arbitrary H_E does NOT by itself justify replacing
every physical bath spectrum by gamma_0. The qualification has two useful
cases.

CASE A: DIRECT SOURCE-CONTACT COUPLING ABSENT. If A=f(q) tensor I_E, it
commutes with arbitrary charge-diagonal H_E^q(t). With hopping exactly OFF,
its interaction-picture coupling is constant independently of how rapidly
E evolves. A demand eV >> every internal frequency of E is unnecessary for
this particular detector reduction. With residual V_off,

 ||A_I(t)-A_I(t-s)|| <= int_(t-s)^t ||[V_off(u),A]|| du,

which is controlled by bounded hopping and bath memory, not ||H_E||. This
observation does NOT qualify source-assisted orbital leakage, detector
couplings outside the admitted orbital domain, or an arbitrary B_q.

CASE B: NONZERO DIRECT SOURCE-CONTACT AMPLITUDE. A flat-spectrum D[A]
model requires the frequencies actually carried by B_q in its interaction
picture to fit the flat bath domain, or a separate error bound. A bounded
||B_q|| is insufficient on an unrestricted source spectrum.

Explicit failure: A=tau I+b X_E, H_E=(hbar Omega/2)Z_E. At zero temperature,
the forward electron-hole phase space for source energy release hbar omega
is proportional to (eV+hbar omega)_+ until a lead-band cutoff. An excited
source therefore contributes b^2 gamma(Omega) to the initial count rate,
not b^2 gamma_0. With tau=1,b=0.1,hbar Omega=100 eV and a sufficiently wide
but finite lead band, the initial rate is 2.01 gamma_0 rather than the
white-model upper bound 1.21 gamma_0. A fast active source can reveal the
colored reservoir through an arbitrarily small direct coupling.

STATIC NONWHITE CONSTRUCTION. For a fixed source Hamiltonian in a weak-
coupling, frequency-resolved Markov domain, keep the source Bohr-frequency
components B_q,omega and their physical rates gamma(omega). The elastic
known amplitude remains in L_q,0=sqrt(gamma_0)(tau_q I+B_q,0); the inelastic
channels are L_q,omega=sqrt(gamma(omega))B_q,omega. If
0<=gamma(omega)<=gamma_max globally and ||B_q||<=b_q, then

 lambda_q^- >= gamma_0(max(|tau_q|-b_q,0))^2,
 lambda_q^+ <= gamma_0(|tau_q|^2+2|tau_q|b_q)+gamma_max b_q^2. (14)

Indeed sum_omega B_q,omega^dag B_q,omega is the energy dephasing of
B_q^dag B_q and is <=b_q^2 I. Thus finite lead bands give a conservative
global spectral-envelope construction that need not know the actual
source spectrum. Contrast pays gamma_max/gamma_0. This is a static-source
weak-coupling qualification, not a proof for an arbitrarily time-driven
non-Markov reservoir. It is useful because it retains actual inelastic
source changes instead of pretending they are forbidden.

If source energy release exceeds eV, REVERSE transfers can occur as well.
They must be retained. Formula (14) applies to forward counts alone when
that direction is resolved; it applies to total unsigned transfers after
gamma_max is replaced by the global bound on the summed forward/backward
spectral rates. An ordinary analog current instead measures N_+-N_-, and
its exponential generator is

 (exp(s)-1)lambda_+ +(exp(-s)-1)lambda_-.

For an upper tail use the upper forward and lower backward endpoints;
for a lower tail use the lower forward and upper backward endpoints.
One-way Poisson formulas cannot be used unchanged for this case. Both
directions may be retained as classical marks in the record contraction
proof; their Hellinger-rate contributions add. The source-decoupled scalar
case at zero temperature, the correctly qualified high-bias source window,
and the finite fresh-carrier construction avoid this extra directional
resource. At nonzero lead temperature, backward rates are small only with
an explicit thermal bound or a two-direction calculation.

Finite bandwidth and weak-coupling scale separation are physical admission
conditions, not a rigorous finite-time error constant. If quantitative
microscopic accuracy epsilon_BM(T) is needed, it must be established for
the specified lead spectral density, switching protocol and complete
domain. The inequalities lambda_max tau_B <<1 and flatness across the
coupled transition window are diagnostics, not replacements for that
bound. No universal finite-time continuum bound is claimed here.

Higher DATA orbitals require an additional joint excitation window. At
zero temperature an ordinary biased contact can deliver energy up to eV
in a forward event, but an active source can supply the rest. Therefore
eV below a bare orbital gap alone does not exclude leakage. The relevant
joint transition energies, source-assisted couplings, drive spectra,
thermal tails and contact noise have to be admitted or bounded. This
preserves the inherited sideband counterexample and finite-orbital scope.

7. EXACT FINITE-CARRIER REGULARIZATION, WITHOUT A WHITE-SOURCE PREMISE

A second, finite-mode route is useful when the reservoir Markov reduction
is the disputed step. Each fresh carrier occupies input mode a, mode b is
empty, and its ordinary spin-independent tunnel contact is open for Delta:

 H_q(t)=H_E(t) tensor I + (tau_q I+B_q(t)) tensor
                              (b^dag a+a^dag b),
 ||B_q(t)||<=b.

No carrier spin polarization is required. It is a spectator; output spin
is retained. Against B_q=0, exact interaction-picture Duhamel gives

 T_q=-i sin(tau_q Delta/hbar) U_E+R_q,
 ||R_q||<=b Delta/hbar.

Hence every conditional transmission probability lies between

 p_q^-=(max(|sin(tau_q Delta/hbar)|-b Delta/hbar,0))^2,
 p_q^+=min(1,(|sin(tau_q Delta/hbar)|+b Delta/hbar)^2).       (15)

Source speed and noncommutation are unrestricted in this bounded model.
Successive fresh incident modes produce Bernoulli records with conditional
probabilities in these intervals; use Bernoulli KL Chernoff exponents in
place of (4). The exact per-carrier off-diagonal record contraction is at
most

 r_qp=sqrt(p_q^+ p_p^-)+sqrt((1-p_q^+)(1-p_p^-))<1           (16)

for ordered disjoint intervals. N carriers give r_qp^N. The continuous
counting form follows in the small-transmission limit, but the finite-bin
statement is already exact.

The calibrated contact need not have a real amplitude or exactly zero
onsite detuning. More generally let

 H_q(t)=H_E^q(t) tensor I + I_E tensor h_q(t) + R_q(t),
 int_0^Delta ||R_q(t)||dt/hbar <= beta.

The known scalar one-particle Hamiltonian h_q can contain contact phases,
onsite shifts and finite control pulses. If its input-to-transmit amplitude
is t_q, Duhamel in the product interaction picture gives
T_q=t_q U_E^q+E_q with ||E_q||<=beta. Replace |sin(tau_q Delta/hbar)| by
|t_q| and b Delta/hbar by beta in (15). Thus an exact common real quadrature is not an
extra physical premise. The preparation is the incoming carrier orbital;
the retained source still need not be prepared or learned.

This is a single-pass tunnel detector, without the earlier Ramsey phase
hold, Hadamard, emptying and reloading of one local probe. Fresh occupied
incident modes and empty accepting modes, injection timing, collection,
and carrier throughput are explicit resources. A moving-packet continuum
realization still must bound packet overlap, transit tails, finite-band
effects, data-assisted carrier leakage and port errors. Strictly compact
time support and strict finite spectral support are not simultaneously
available. This regularization is a constructive bounded interaction
model, not a claim that an uncontrolled metallic Fermi sea is automatically
an exactly clocked product of two-mode packets.

8. DISTURBANCE AND THE FULL PAIR CYCLE

If the actual jump is L_0+R, ||L_0||<=ell, ||R||<=r, then

 ||D[L_0+R]-D[L_0]||_diamond <=4 ell r+2r^2.                (17)

This follows by expanding the jump product and anticommutator. A generic
unmodelled jump perturbation therefore costs half-diamond error at most
int(2 ell r+r^2)dt. Coherent unwanted terms cost int||H_bad||dt/hbar.
Charge-diagonal source noise that merely widens (1) is not automatically
charged by this pessimistic cumulative bound. Charge-changing or
spin-changing terms cannot be hidden inside the signal contrast budget.
When the root's monitored-OFF theorem applies, it can replace the linear
coherent hopping debit by a sharper detector-assisted estimate; genuine
spin-changing or orbital-loss jumps remain physical errors.

Increasing current is not free. For scalar amplitudes tau_q=tau_0+q chi
with large positive tau_0, the adjacent record contraction is exactly
gamma |chi|^2/2, independent of the baseline tau_0. The optimal Poisson
discrimination exponent tends to the same value as tau_0 grows, while
electron flux grows as gamma tau_0^2. Increasing the known baseline at fixed
electrostatic contrast wastes carriers and bias work; increasing bias or
barrier susceptibility can also increase detector backaction and tighten
the joint orbital window. Contact power and source shielding belong in
an actual device optimization.

For the inherited capture/return cycle, replace m t_probe by

 t_Q=T+t_b+t_s+t_switch+t_latency

with T chosen from (7), or (13) with amplifier noise. The full elapsed time
is

 T_pair <=(N_capture+N_return)(tau_pulse+t_Q),

plus any loading, final collection or routing operation not already included.
The complete inherited error decomposition remains

 sqrt(p_miss)+p_miss+p_return
 +(N_capture+N_return) delta_Q + eta_physical(T_actual).

Both q=0 and q=2 are resolved; the dark double-occupation superposition is
not left as an unobserved trap. The retained source is not reset at charge
measurements, phase changes, loading or recovery. At fixed positive count
contrast, the logarithmic charge-readout accuracy dependence remains; its
coefficient is a maintained current information rate rather than hundreds
of serial probe reset durations. Mean carrier use is at most lambda_max T
per exposure, and ideal bias work is eV times that expectation, before
electronics and refrigeration costs. Data loading and singlet separation
remain the separately proved reservoir-loading/return operations.

    This work separates singlet/triplet readout from parity readout caused
    by rapid T0 relaxation and controls the relevant relaxation rate over
    four orders of magnitude. Conventional hardware can target either
    regime; a reported parity result must not be counted as preservation
    of the entire coherent triplet sector.

An ordinary frozen-charge QPC or SET with the same data-only spin symmetry,
source bounds and orbital admission can satisfy this theorem. Conventional
full-triplet-preserving charge sensing is therefore a valid strong
comparator and can be the actual implementation of the construction. The
scientific
increment is the uniform statement about the retained quantum source,
actual conditional histories and logical multiplicity, and the explicit
information criterion usable for the root's OFF-isolation law. A qualified
hardware window with useful gain, leakage, contrast, spin preservation
and full elapsed-time exposure is still a separate obligation.
