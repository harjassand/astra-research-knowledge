QUANTUM ALTERNATIVE: ACTUAL CONSTRUCTION AND SELECTION DECISION
2026-10-31. Mathematical controls, no physical quantum experiment.

1. Resource question

Can a geometric or autonomous quantum gate remove an unknown physical
source clock, rather than merely rename supplied calibrated interactions?
N713's geometric perturbation is an exponential splitting of classical
transition rates. It is not Berry holonomy. The connection tested here is
the physical calibration burden, not a transfer of a classical theorem.

2. Genuine holonomic gate, and its missing resource

In a Lambda system let b=(|0>-|1>)/sqrt(2) and
H=|e><b|+|b><e|. With logical projector P, PHP=0 and H^3=H.
For pulse area A,
 U(A)=I+(cos(A)-1)H^2-i sin(A)H.
Since U commutes with H, the transported logical subspace has zero
compressed Hamiltonian at every instant. At A=pi it closes and its
logical action is exactly X. This is a real nonadiabatic holonomy.

For an unknown common coupling scale lambda the actual area is lambda*pi.
On input |0>, X fidelity is (1-cos(lambda*pi))^2/4 and leakage is
sin(lambda*pi)^2/2. Exact controls in receipt.json include lambda=1/2
(fidelity 1/4) and lambda=2 (fidelity 0). Specifying the desired geometric
path has not supplied a closed actual path or its pulse area.

More generally, if every available Hamiltonian is lambda H_nominal(t),
Duhamel's identity gives
 ||U_lambda(T)-I|| <= lambda integral_0^T ||H_nominal(t)|| dt.
A fixed finite action cannot realize a nonidentity gate uniformly as
lambda tends to zero. This does not exclude independently calibrated
counterdiabatic controls; those would be additional physical resources.

3. An autonomous heralded gate really can avoid a stopping-time calibration

Use clock states 0,1,2,3 and supplied gates U_0=H, U_1=S, U_2=H.
Forward Lindblad operators are sqrt(2 lambda)|j+1><j| tensor U_j;
reverse operators are sqrt(lambda)|j><j+1| tensor U_j^dagger.
Define W_0=I, W_{j+1}=U_j W_j and G=sum_j |j><j| tensor W_j.
Conjugation by G removes all logical gates from the jumps. Consequently,
every clock path reaching 3 for the first time has logical action W_3,
including paths with arbitrary backtracking. The completion channel is
exactly Ad_(H S H) for any lambda>0. The symbolic receiver checks the
forward and reverse gauge identities and the first-hit compositions.

The classical backward equations give E_0 tau_3=17/(8 lambda).
Uniformization at maximum escape rate 3 lambda implies
 P(tau_3<=T) <= min(1,(3 lambda T)^3/6),
because at least three updates are necessary. A heralded correct answer
therefore differs materially from a uniformly successful finite deadline.
First-hit monitoring/halting is an external primitive here. The full clock
has reciprocal edges; an absorbing detector is not silently substituted
for a thermodynamically reversible transition.

One thermal-gradient realization has E_j=-j kBT log(2). The net downward
steps at first completion are exactly three: clock bath entropy flow is
3 log(2) and lifting this clock back to 0 costs at least 3 kBT log(2).
Neither is the total instrument entropy. Detector memory, halt, controller,
logical noise and a physical reset implementation are unacquired.

4. A clock-only observation does not qualify the coherent interaction

Replace each forward jump by the two logical-state-resolving jumps
sqrt(2 lambda)|j+1><j| tensor U_j P_a, a=0,1, and use adjoints for
the paired reverse channels. The classical clock rates are unchanged.
The first forward hop dephases logical |+> to I/2. Subsequent unital
forward/reverse logical channels preserve I/2, so the target pure-state
fidelity at completion is 1/2. The same observed clock completion law
therefore does not acquire a coherent unitary-dressed bath channel.

5. Conventional comparators and decision

The Lambda construction reproduces the established nonadiabatic scheme:
Sjoqvist et al., Non-adiabatic holonomic quantum computation (2012),
https://arxiv.org/html/1107.5127 . The pulse area and actual closed
subspace path matter in that construction.

Unitary-dressed dissipative clock dynamics are an established resource,
as in Verstraete, Wolf and Cirac (2009),
https://arxiv.org/html/0803.1447 . Our supplied gates are already present
in the bath interactions and are gauge equivalent to an ordinary clock
transporting identity. No quantum-computing advantage is established.

This family received executable construction effort before selection.
It gives a useful distinction between heralded correctness and deadline
success, but it leaves the coherent gate interactions and detector
supplied. It is not selected for sustained depth in this turn. The finite
kinetic-pulse opening instead targets a specific mathematical burden:
stationary mean entropy inference without generator reconstruction,
hidden rank, a hidden escape cap or infinitesimal response estimation.

Artifacts: work/quantum_clock_opening.py and receipt.json. The exact
symbolic checks are internal controls, not an independent audit, a
novelty determination, an engineered reservoir or physical validation.
