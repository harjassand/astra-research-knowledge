# Classical source access versus an acquired coherent realization
31 October 2026

Start with law_preserving_lift_screen_20261031/UNIFORM_STATIONARY_LIFT_ORACLE_OBSTRUCTION.md and its separate independent review.

The strongest result is an access obstruction, not a new fast simulator. Any pure-reference-preserving quantum source lift with the full prescribed classical adjoint law contains the balanced classical transition kernel as an overlap. For an explicit forward-oracle permutation-plus-reset family, implementing that reversible coherent interface would invert the permutation. The established quantum inversion lower bound therefore charges at least order sqrt(d) total forward-oracle queries, despite trivial uniform stationary-state preparation and fast classical coalescence. The proof covers a small constant product-reference error while retaining the exact diagonal-law condition.

Acquisition and online work are both charged. This is not an online-only lower bound after expensive preprocessing, a lower bound on ordinary classical simulation, or an unconditional hardness result for inspectable structured circuits. An irreversible channel box alone is not the clean reversible interface used by the reduction. Known inverse access changes the resource model.

The same overlap and classical law need not give the same purification: bit dephasing and pure reset provide a decisive counterexample. Fast return still has to be earned separately.

Two constructive complements are preserved: a general edge-local continuous-time lift with an explicit potentially dimension-dependent return bound, and a reverse-random-map lift that requires an acquired coherent inverse-fibre operation and conditional coalescence certificate. Their limitations are substantive, not hidden implementation assumptions. The earlier Index Erasure example retains its large-range and unknown-support qualifications; the stronger uniform-stationary obstruction does not rely on that example.

All three mathematical constructions/obstructions have separate internal review scopes. Dense diagnostic scripts are small checks only. Established pure-state engineering and quantum-query lower bounds are credited, and historical novelty is not established. These results identify an access barrier and useful structural targets; no civilisation-changing capability or practical advantage is claimed.
