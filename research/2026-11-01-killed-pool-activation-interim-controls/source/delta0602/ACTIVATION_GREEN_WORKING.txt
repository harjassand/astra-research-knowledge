1. New candidate killed-pool theorem: full analytic derivation

Fix d species, ordinary homogeneous reaction degree m>=2, finite positive
rates and conservative reactions |y|=|y'|=m. Let Q_N be their EXACT falling-
factorial generator on total population N. Let A be a distinguished species.
Assume that from every pure initial composition, reaction closure creates A.
A sufficient check is a strongly connected complex graph containing all pure
complexes mX_i. Finite stoichiometric paths, not a mixing-time bound, are given.

Let D>=1 be a finite activation exponent such that the normalized deterministic
flow obeys u_A(t)>=c t^D uniformly for all u(0) in the probability simplex
and 0<t<=t0. This is acquired structurally: some initial coordinate >=1/d;
negative drift of each coordinate is at worst -C u_i on the compact simplex.
Following a complex path, a new coordinate with source exponents y_i has
lower exponent 1+sum y_i D_i. Starting with exponent zero at the populated
pure seed gives finite D for the target. There are finitely many seeds.
Strong positivity and quantitative comparison give one c,t0,D for all starts.
For the cascade 3C->3D->3A, D=4 (D_D=1,D_A=3*1+1).

Add a marked killing channel kappa*(A)_m and optional other nonnegative killing.
A common catalyst factor s>0 multiplying Q_N and the marked killing is kept
in the physical clock. Define
 eta=1/(mD+1), beta=m-1+eta.
Candidate conclusion:
 sup_(|x|=N) E_x tau <=C s^-1 N^-beta.                      (G1)

Proof, not just an ODE limit:
Use fast time theta=s N^(m-1) t and densities X/N. The drift differs uniformly
from the polynomial homogeneous ODE by O(1/N). Its Lipschitz constant, total
normalized jump intensity and jump bound are fixed on the compact simplex.
At theta_N=K N^-eta, the ODE target is >=c (theta_N/2)^D on the entire final
half interval. A bounded-jump exponential martingale inequality plus Gronwall
bounds the probability of density error >c' theta_N^D by
 C exp[-c N theta_N^(2D-1)].
The O(1/N) drift error is negligible compared with theta_N^D. Since
 1-(2D-1)/(mD+1)=((m-2)D+2)/(mD+1)>0,
the error tends to zero uniformly in the integer initial state.

On the good path A>=c N theta_N^D throughout that final half interval, so the
integrated ACTUAL marked-killing hazard is at least
 c N theta_N^(mD+1)=c K^(mD+1).
Choose K as a sufficiently large FIXED constant, then N sufficiently large.
The probability of surviving one block is <=1/2 uniformly over all starts.
Apply the strong Markov property at block endpoints to get a geometric tail
and (G1). Optional other killing only shortens the first terminal time.
The exponential probability above is a conditional event bound for the actual
CME in EACH block, not an additive once-per-run model-error allowance.
The pool is finite for each N, so this argument assumes no global recurrence
or full-process nonexplosion. Fixed graph, degree, rates and activity factor
are essential; constants are not claimed efficient/uniform over growing graphs.

2. Actual terminal rewards, not time-only averaging

For the first marked terminal consuming m A, let A'=A_tau- -m. Put
 w_j(a)=(1+a)^(-j). For 1<=j<=m,
 (A)_m w_j(A-m)<=C_m N^(m-j).
For j>=m it is bounded by a fixed C_(m,j). Therefore the ACTUAL killed kernel
has, for 1<=j<=m,
 E_x[1_marked w_j(A')] <= C N^(1-j-eta),                    (G2)
and for j>=m an upper bound C N^-beta. This is a direct compensator/Green
identity with forcing kappa*s*(A)_m*w_j(A-m), followed by (G1); the factor s
cancels. It keeps the true post-reaction A and the first terminal event.

At fixed high catalyst phase B>=q, add actual connector activation rate A B
and immigration rate 1; marked clearing rate is (B)_q (A)_m. Here s=(B)_q.
For m>=3, (G1) gives
 P_activation<=C N^(2-m-eta) B/(B)_q,
 P_immigration<=C N^-beta/(B)_q.
Thus P_clear>=1-o(1) uniformly over B>=q and all compositions. Consequently
the conditional law AFTER CLEARING also satisfies (G2), with at most a factor
two. This is a distributional reset bound, not a fictitious independent reset.

For q=m+1, the exact probability of q successive low-phase immigration steps
before any activation, starting B=0 with A=a, is
 U_m(a)=product_(b=1)^m 1/[1+b(a+b)].
It is <=w_m(a). The real incoming terminal reward is
 J=E[1_clear U_m(A')]. For B in any FIXED finite phase range, the pointwise
hazard comparison lambda_clear<=C N^(m-1)lambda_activation and the terminal
probability sum imply
 P_activation>=c N^(-(m-1))
(if P_clear is not >=1/2, activation itself has constant probability, since
immigration is small). Combining with (G2) gives
 J<=C N^-eta P_activation.                                 (G3)
This is the first genuinely useful population-small feedback estimate. It
does NOT follow from a mean holding-time bound without the reward calculation.

Network: 0->A+B->mA+qB->0 with q=m+1,m>=3, and ANY fixed homogeneous
conservative fast network on d species of degree m, gated by (B)_q, containing
all pure mX_i and joined at mA+qB. Full graph is single-linkage if the fast
complex graph is strongly connected. It need not be complex balanced.

Define h_B=m floor(B/q)+min(B mod q,m), r=N-h_B.
Clearing preserves r. Activation never increases it (it lowers r when the
phase remainder is zero); immigration changes r by zero or +1. For q=m+1,
both nonzero changes have magnitude one. All first nonzero-r exit states have
B>=q, including an up exit from B=m to B=q. This avoids demanding a false
uniform up-probability bound at arbitrary dormant low-phase entrances.

On B<q, fast reactions and clearing are absent. Immigration raises B by one;
activation followed by the normal clearing lowers the low-phase value by one.
Each clearing resets A with the G2 graded bounds. A run of l competitive
immigrations after a reset costs w_l(A), whose expected size is
O(N^(-(l-1+eta))). An internal low-phase death requires an extra birth to
reach the up boundary, so each extra reset/run gives an additional N^-eta.

up mass O(N^(-(m-1+eta))), versus down mass >=c N^(-(m-1)) from an activation
at high phase B=q. High-phase immigrations already have the smaller O(N^-beta)
mass; high-phase activations never raise r and have small total probability.

At the first slow terminal event in H, clearing decreases Z by m+q,
activation increases it by m+q-2, immigration by 2. The probabilities above
give E_x f(Z_T)<=rho f(Z), rho<1, for sufficiently small fixed a>0 and all
sufficiently large states in H. This is valid for every composition, with

 F(x)=E_x f(Z_T)+g(Z) E_x T <=f(Z) on H; F=f outside H.
