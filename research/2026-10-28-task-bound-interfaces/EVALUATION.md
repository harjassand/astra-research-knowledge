## Research-use comparison

For F05, the old broad N768 card leads through a large collection of quantum
calibration sources. Its first citation is not the fibre-regret proof. The new
`U768-fibre-gradient-regret` has a default complete packet and leads directly to
`research_note.md:67–101`. The actual passage proves

`F(u+h)-F(u) <= grad F(u).h - mu ||h||^2/2 <= G^2/(2mu)`.

The extracted premise makes the convex/whole-segment region, uniform gradient
bound, positive curvature and actual global exclusion explicit. If those
bounds are genuinely supplied with `G=2, mu=4`, a researcher obtains regret at
most `1/2`, which meets a supplied tolerance `3/5`, without identifying every
latent model. Those numbers are illustrative warranted inputs, not acquired
measurements. This can justify a robust policy-construction target. It neither
makes further data valueless for every objective nor supplies the needed
experiment, sensor or global certificate for free.

The current qualified-consumer route also makes exposed G06 reachable from
both returned N768 and U1000 families. Its exact premise is the existing
`U1069-signed-task-dual-and-no-value-observation`. Reading the actual governing
`ASTRA_PRO3_ACQUIRED_SPECTRAL_MEMORY_AND_TASK_SHAPING.md:97–126` supplies both
primal endpoint witnesses and the dual polynomials for
`[63/1024,33/512]`. Both endpoint witnesses retain `g3=27/256`, so that reading
leaves the sharp signed-task interval unchanged while narrowing the separate
`g4` interval eight-fold. A researcher can reject an observation chosen only
for nuisance-uncertainty reduction and seek one that distinguishes the actual
task witnesses. The physical readings and their costs remain unacquired/paid
as specified by the source. Sharing the consumer with the control result does
not make either result a theorem about the other's physical system.

For F01, the source route is
`EUCLIDEAN_HEAT_CERTIFICATE.md:1–77`, not the later dense-correlation convergence
theorem. Its actual finite inequality pays
`e_(1/4)+(2/3)e_(1/2)+(1/3)e_(1/8)` and the finite-common-mass loss. It enables a
conditional lower-bound calculation from three actual full-coordinate
imaginary-time correlations. It does not turn them into ordinary laboratory
measurements, a free sampler or a working cold-transport device.

## Assumption and unsupported-answer checks

F03 cannot be scored as an unqualified negative. The actual source
`ADAPTIVE_TERMINAL_READOUT_BY_REMAINING_TASK_VALUE.txt:52–121` gives a valid
branch-local `G(v)` bound on remaining **modeled posterior expected benefit**.
An early stop using the actual `G(v)<=epsilon` certifies that benefit at the
received branch. The separate fair-round capped policy theorem is a mean-regret
bound and need not hold with the same value at a forced cap leaf. Neither
quantity certifies small absolute logical error. The adapter preserves all
three distinctions; the unchanged question and development correction remain
in `evaluation/FRESH_SOURCE_ASSESSMENTS.md`.

For F04, direct `bridge` between the static-displacement and finite heat-bound
providers remains `NO SUPPORTED CONNECTION`, although they are navigation
peers. The actual no-heat obstruction and the heat unit's distinct complete
equilibrium-scattering/correlation contract remain visible. A shared scalar
consumer supplies no mechanical, real/imaginary-time or transport conversion.

For F06, the returned chemomotor, lattice-transport and retained-reaction
technology records are at simulation/model stages. The actual chemomotor
`README.md:4` says no catalyst meeting the decisive conditions was measured,
identified or fabricated; its efficiency discussion distinguishes model output
from full-cycle measured efficiency. No supported acquired experiment was found
in the inspected candidates. This is not a corpus-wide impossibility proof.

## Engineering checks and limits

The initial consumer packet exceeded the default budget because its bound
mappings appeared both in complete transfers and in navigation. Packet views
now reference the identical retained transfer mapping; conflicting assertions
are never collapsed. All conditions, reviews, corrections and source locators
remain. The complete consumer packet fits the usual 32 KiB envelope. Canonical
storage is linear in bindings, with explicit peer pagination and exact-version
guards. A consumer parent's route cannot silently disappear or inherit a
provider's whole claim.

The initial slice contains one new elementary consumer card, three new units
(including the consumer), six documented source adapters and six provider
families in physics/control/learning. It does not measure corpus-wide interface
coverage or scientific discovery. Previously tried operation-only lexical and
exhaustive unit-reranking approaches did not recover exposed G06 in the first
five; the source-proved routing mechanism addresses that navigation failure
without treating similarity as composition. Direct ranking still has misses.
Materials, chemistry, biology and other mechanisms need further supported
ports/extraction; ordinary full-content retrieval remains available throughout.

Ten independent finite exact controls check threshold signs, loose-enclosure
ambiguity, Bayes/support and benefit/absolute-error distinctions, heat-error
payment and illustrative premise use. They are arithmetic controls, not the
proof of the general claims, a source-program replay or empirical advantage.
