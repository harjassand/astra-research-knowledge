POSITIVE MECHANISM: TWO-BODY PAULI RAMAN INTERFEROMETER
For each check S_a=product_{j=1}^w P_aj use ONE (2w+2)-level apparatus: ground cache states g_0,g_1, excited states e_0,e_1, and w-1 intermediate levels v_cj for each c. For each c put v_c0=g_c,v_cw=e_c. Define
 H_a = Delta sum_{c,j=1..w-1}|v_cj><v_cj| + sum_{c,j=1..w}(g_cj |v_cj><v_c,j-1| tensor P_aj+h.c.) + sum_c (r_c|e_c><g_c|+h.c.) + endpoint Stark counterterms.
Every displayed interaction is one-site apparatus or two-body apparatus/data. Use local jumps L_ac=sqrt(kappa)|g_{1-c}><e_c| and optional apparatus dephasing that distinguishes the two c branches. Add weak intermediate cleanup R_acj=sqrt(r)|g_c><v_cj|, explicitly counted as faults; do not assume pure resets. Ordinary local physical noise is added separately.
For eta=g/Delta <<1, the virtual endpoint amplitude is J=(-1)^(w-1) product_j g_cj/Delta^(w-1), to leading order. Choose reference r_c=-(-1)^c J, correcting finite-chain shifts using its finite Schur complement. Effective drive is J[S_a-(-1)^c I]|e_c><g_c|. Thus an incorrect cache bit is bright and decays to the correct bit; correct bit is dark. At kappa~J, refresh Gamma=Theta(J). At kappa>>J, Gamma=Theta(J^2/kappa), not Theta(J). The continuous device never needs an external clock or classical readout to refresh its own noisy cache.

EXACT ALGEBRAIC PROTECTION AGAINST COHERENT OVERLAP
For each branch vertex let Q_a(v) be its prefix Pauli; Q_a(g_c)=I, Q_a(e_c)=S_a. Let U=sum_r |r><r| tensor ordered product_a Q_a(r_a), with a bounded-degree coloring order. Conjugating each microscopic edge by U removes its single-data Pauli up to apparatus-dependent Pauli commutation signs. A reference edge becomes apparatus operator times S_a, again up to apparatus signs. Therefore U^dagger H U lies in apparatus algebra tensor the COMMUTING STABILIZER ALGEBRA, even when microscopic check edges do not commute. Endpoint decay e_c->g_{1-c} similarly becomes apparatus operator times S_a and signs. This gives exact preservation of dressed logical observables for the ideal Hamiltonian and endpoint jumps, not merely an order-w assertion. It does not imply independent ideal syndrome meters, and does not establish the noisy threshold.
U is implementable as a constant-depth bounded-range circuit of check-controlled Pauli operations on a bounded-degree incidence graph. It therefore maps elementary physical errors to bounded-neighborhood errors. Intermediate cleanup maps to partial-check Pauli faults. At endpoint states, U is a conditional product of stabilizers and acts trivially on logical information. Bare data can have transient partial-Pauli dressing during operation; declaring bare data exactly protected would be false.
A small explicit numerical check, check_gauge.py, uses overlapping Z1Z2 and X1X2X3X4. It finds bare logical commutator norm 27.7128, dressed commutator 0, and every transformed Hamiltonian block exactly in span{I,S1,S2,S1S2} (floating arithmetic zero). This checks the algebra, not stochastic threshold or refresh rate.

HARDEST OPEN GATE / REUSABLE ADMISSION
Prove a uniform LOCAL refresh and fault-cluster lemma for the full overlapping finite-level network, rather than bound ||exp(tL)-exp(tLeff)|| by t*epsilon. For every separated set of spacetime boxes of size O(1/Gamma), bad-refresh/physical-fault/leakage histories should admit a local stochastic or coherent fault-path bound (C p)^|R|, allowing fixed-range dependence. In addition prove exponential tails for cache stale times and leakage bursts, conditional on good neighboring histories. Needed parameters include eta, gamma/Gamma, finite reset impurity, drift in the cancellation amplitude, and cleanup r/Gamma. A conservative design takes r=epsilon Gamma so cleanup is itself a small norm local fault process; physical leakage then persists O(1/(epsilon Gamma)), demanding gamma/(epsilon Gamma) small. A heuristic p~C[eta^2+epsilon+gamma/(epsilon Gamma)+relative cancellation error] is a TARGET, not a proved bound. Overlap-renormalized dark/bright rates and system-size-uniform conditioning are the decisive unresolved parts. Generic adiabatic elimination does not supply this lemma.
A coherent static drift should be admitted in local Hamiltonian norm/fault amplitude, not assumed stochastic with squared probability. Global correlated drift needs a uniform bounded-amplitude hypothesis or independent justification. Arbitrary fixed nonzero noise ratios are impossible; construction would at best establish an open sufficiently-small fixed-ratio region, not all fixed ratios. Acquisition alone also needs a separately justified decoder to imply exponential memory.

COST AND ACTIVE COMPARATOR
Apparatus dimension 2w+2 (10 for w=4), O(w) data links, fixed detuning ratio, fixed local reset rates and finite cache. J/g=Theta(eta^(w-1)) creates a serious slowdown. Compare against scheduled parity extraction using the SAME g,Delta,kappa,apparatus dimension, physical noise and reset impurity, including control faults. With fast resets the active protocol can run O(w/g+1/kappa), while autonomous rate is at best J and is J^2/kappa if over-damped. When kappa is deliberately set ~J both can be reset-limited; do not present this engineering choice as autonomous speed superiority. Positive claim is potential removal of precise schedules, not improved threshold or resource efficiency. Explicit same-resource numerics on two overlapping checks should test conditional cache refresh, logical channel, reset-induced hooks and all-axis drift first.
