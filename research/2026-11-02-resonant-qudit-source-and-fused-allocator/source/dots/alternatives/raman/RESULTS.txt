Reconciliation findings (new work after root architecture exposure; first mechanism.txt unchanged)

1. Exact isolated Raman latch, w=4,d=10, Schur-complement tuned at E0=0:
Without intermediate cleanup, unique correct dark stationary state exists, but gap/J=.000995 at eta=.1. Full faulty-apparatus recovery is NOT order J.
Ordinary local intermediate cleanup r=J repairs gap to .2576J, actual cleanup flux .01031J, wrong cache 1.04e-6. At eta=.03, gap .2507J and cleanup flux .0009024J.
However the centered Poisson observable for counting those partial-Pauli cleanup faults has positive spectral range tending to 1, not zero, as eta tends to zero. An arbitrary populated intermediate excitation incurs one fault. Small stationary occupation alone does not imply a small uniform storage charge. Root proposed an energy/debt Lyapunov admission to handle this; not tested here.

2. Exact two overlapping ZZ/XX latch network, endpoint jumps only:
Fixed stabilizer-sector reduction is valid because ideal transformed H and endpoint jumps preserve stabilizers. At eta=.1 the cache error floor is .01257 per cache; at .05 it is .000891. Coherent overlap does not break dressed logical protection but does impair cache acquisition. Never project ordinary partial-Pauli intermediate resets into a fixed syndrome sector: they change other syndromes. An initial temporary code attempt did that, caught the error, and removed that option before reporting results. Final test_overlap.py asserts cleanup==0.

3. NEW RECONCILED POSITIVE PRIMITIVE: PREFIX-UNDO CLEANUP
For each intermediate v_cj add dump d_c,j-1 with Q(d)=Q(v_c,j-1). Add two-body H_reverse=h |d_c,j-1><v_cj| tensor P_j+h.c. and one-site jump sqrt(kdump)|v_c,j-1><d_c,j-1|. The coherent step undoes P_j; the local jump connects equal-prefix vertices. In the controlled-prefix gauge ALL these Hamiltonian and dissipative terms are in the stabilizer algebra, so they cause no ideal dressed logical fault even at high occupation and under overlaps. Dimension rises from 2w+2 to 4w: d=16 for w4. No qubit-rail interpretation or uncharged excitation-sector constraint is made.
Tested h=sqrt(J*g)/2, kdump=g, endpoint kappa=J. At w4,eta=.03: gap=.04130J, wrong cache4.66e-12, dump flux2.04e-7J. eta=.1: gap=.04355J, wrong cache5.81e-8, dump flux2.69e-5J. This repairs slow leakage evacuation without introducing the preceding partial-Pauli cleanup fault channel.
The numerical implementation uses equal-energy intermediate/dump levels and a phenomenological directed local amplitude-damping bath. A truly physical reservoir construction must pay pumping/non-equilibrium work; a purely equilibrium zero-temperature bath cannot be silently presumed to supply arbitrary directed degenerate transitions. A downhill energy-staggered version is structurally possible but NOT tested here. Bidirectional cache refresh itself also consumes nonequilibrium resources.

The exact stationary cache Poisson equation (L*/J) Z=-(M1+M2-f I), centered by Tr(rho_ss Z)=0, has finite positive-shift spectral range: 7.493 at eta=.03, 7.852 at .05, 9.035 at .1. Stationary total mismatch f=7.93e-5,.000603,.009078 respectively. Thus multiplying this corrector by c/J gives storage cost ~7.5c/J at small eta; unlike the cleanup-count corrector, its size can be reduced by choosing a slower target contraction c. This is only a finite-cell numerical observation, not a uniform-graph positive operator drift theorem or a rigorous eigensolver certificate.

5. Remaining gates and comparison:
The exact logical algebra result removes ideal logical disturbance, not bare-data transient dressing, finite cache errors, thermal/upward faults, static drift errors, or failure under extra neighbors. Need a positive local cache/Lyapunov observable with its commutator against ACTUAL additional couplings bounded sufficiently tightly, and a decoder coupling that respects the dressed/bare distinction. Need a physical realization of the onsite directed reservoirs with finite impurity and noise; assumptions must specify norms/rates on all qudit transitions.
Same-resource active extraction can sequentially use O(w) interactions rather than a wth-order virtual amplitude J~g eta^(w-1), so this construction has no speed or noise-efficiency advantage. Fast dump reservoirs are an available resource to the active comparator too. An exactly matched control schedule for this same multi-level graph has NOT been simulated; one must not report conventional ideal CNOT performance as that simulation. Positive surviving capability is a concrete bounded-dimensional, clock-free, time-independent two-body/onsite-GKSL source with exact dressed logical symmetry, subject to charged reservoir engineering and a remaining uniform admission theorem.
