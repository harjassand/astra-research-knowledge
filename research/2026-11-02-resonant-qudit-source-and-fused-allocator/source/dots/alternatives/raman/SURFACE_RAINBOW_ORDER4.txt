SURFACE-CODE RAINBOW CO-DESIGN: ORDER-FOUR CHECK
New root-proposed construction, independently checked 2026-11-01 18:34 UTC. Does not overwrite the naive-ordering obstruction.

GEOMETRY
Horizontal edge from (x,y) to (x+1,y): color (x+y) mod2, in {0,1}.
Vertical edge from (x,y) to (x,y+1): color 2+(x+y) mod2, in {2,3}.
Each four-edge X star and Z plaquette contains all colors once. Use factor order X=[0,1,2,3], Z=[1,0,3,2]. Thus endpoint-adjacent data factors have colors {0,3} for X and {1,2} for Z. Shared X/Z data edges can NEVER be endpoint-adjacent for both apparatus cells.
Works globally in the square-lattice bulk and on compatible even periodic lattices. Truncated boundary checks require separate handling; padding missing colors with identity factors (apparatus-only chain transitions) retains fixed w4 without adding data action. Odd-periodic coloring is not automatically covered.

EXACT FINITE-ORDER ARGUMENT
Project each apparatus to its two endpoints g/e, retaining ALL endpoint configurations, not only cold ground. Count chain hops with scale g and intermediate denominators Delta. At fourth order:
(a) Four hops on one cell can traverse its full check, generating desired J S, or return and produce the single-cell Stark term already locally countertuned.
(b) A connected contribution involving two distinct cells must have two hops per cell. Each cell must leave an endpoint and return along the SAME first/last edge. The only data-dependent intercell effect is the signed virtual square of these two endpoint-adjacent Pauli factors. If they commute, its connected frustration coefficient vanishes; if they anticommute, the extra energy is 2g^4/Delta^3 for that endpoint pair. This is the previously observed obstruction.
(c) Three distinct cells cannot contribute a closed connected endpoint-return word in four chain hops: each needs at least two hops. Pure reference edges already have order four and cannot combine with a nontrivial other-cell excursion before order six. Counterterm insertions of order two only change same-cell/additive coefficients at this order; disconnected contributions are removed in the linked coefficient.
Same-type surface checks have commuting single-site factors. Opposite-type checks have disjoint endpoint color sets under the construction. Therefore every intercell connected fourth-order frustrated-square coefficient is exactly zero across the WHOLE graph. This statement is a local finite-order coefficient cancellation, not a global gap/threshold theorem.
The first possible cross-cell connected terms have six chain hops, scale g eta^5=O(J eta^2), eta=g/Delta, for w4. The number of fixed-order connected clusters touching a cell is bounded on this graph. A formal local linked expansion therefore has bounded order-six coefficient per cell. A convergent uniform remainder/actual GKSL admission is NOT proved here; do not invoke a globally isolated extensive low-energy band.

UNDO APPARATUS
The prefix-undo variant has d16 per check including both cache branches. In a fixed coherent cache branch it has d8. h=sqrt(Jg)/2, dump decay g. Coherent loop effects involving a dump require at least two h hops; since h/Delta=O(eta^(5/2)), their first endpoint-return effect is higher than the fourth-order chain contribution. Reservoir effects, their noise, and arbitrary high-level neighboring states still need actual-generator admission. The order-four argument does not declare all such effects harmless for cache acquisition.

EXACT FINITE-MATRIX TESTS
surface_rainbow_schur.py uses binary symplectic Pauli arithmetic to transform each actual edge to its exact fixed-stabilizer-sector sign. It constructs actual coherent H with single-cell counterterms, optional undo levels/couplings, and computes finite Schur complement at E=0 over every intermediate/dump state. All g/e endpoint configurations are retained. No effective pair interactions are inserted by hand.

WHAT THIS REOPENS / WHAT REMAINS
This graph/co-design directly removes the actual order-J Raman interference obstruction on the surface-code geometry without adding pair counterterm hardware. It merits reopening that restricted route. It does NOT rehabilitate arbitrary bounded-degree Pauli-check graphs, arbitrary Pauli ordering, or w>4.
Still required: actual positive local cache-service observable with controlled additional-cell commutators, energy-priced arbitrary high apparatus occupations, physical noisy directed-reservoir realization, drift bounds preserving the scale separation, and compatibility with a decoder/logical memory certificate. Exact dressed logical symmetry remains available but does not replace these service conditions. The existing strict-graph active benchmark and its limitations remain unchanged; no new autonomous performance advantage follows from this coefficient cancellation.
