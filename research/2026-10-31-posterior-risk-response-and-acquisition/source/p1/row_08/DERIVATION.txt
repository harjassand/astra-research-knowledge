ADAPTIVE LOCAL RELIABILITY DESIGN FROM RETAINED PRODUCT EVIDENCE
Working result; no novelty or priority claim. Ordinary baseline included.

Scope
-----
Unknown independent Bernoulli primitive sources. A fixed read-once hierarchy
has four children at each internal node. Each node may assign the distinguished
weight-two role to any one child; the other three children have weight one;
the threshold is three. The allowed design family has 4^G elements for G gates.
This does not optimize arbitrary graph topology, overlapping subcircuits,
component replacement under arbitrary costs, or dependencies among sources.

The central useful task is selecting a better local role assignment from
retained evidence. Its confidence certificate can need far less acquisition
than estimating the full root reliability. Every construction and maintenance
operation is also available to an ordinary product-count estimator. There is
no demonstrated cached-restoration advantage here.

1. Exact swap identity and recursive optimization
------------------------------------------------
At a weighted-threshold node, give reliabilities p_a,p_b to weights w_a>=w_b.
Let S be the total score of the remaining independent children. Compare this
assignment to exchanging the two children. The exact difference is

  q_ab - q_ba
    = (p_a-p_b) Pr(theta-w_a <= S < theta-w_b).

Proof: the two assignments differ only when exactly one of the two children
succeeds. Their probability difference is p_a-p_b, multiplied by the difference
between the two threshold indicators. The latter is the displayed interval
indicator. Thus sorting child reliabilities in the same order as the weights
is optimal by successive interchange. Strictness requires both p_a>p_b and
positive probability for the pivotal interval. Some different weights can be
equivalent if no other-child score lies in that interval.

For the four-majority gate, with distinguished child j,

  g_j(q) = q_j [1-product_(i!=j)(1-q_i)]
             +(1-q_j) product_(i!=j)q_i.

Exchanging distinguished child a with ordinary child b gives

  g_a(q)-g_b(q) = (q_a-q_b)(q_c+q_d-2q_c q_d).

The second factor is Pr(child c != child d), strictly positive for full-support
child probabilities. The best distinguished child therefore has the largest
reliability, with ties harmless.

Every four-majority gate is strictly increasing in every child reliability in
the open cube. Consequently maximizing all child-subtree reliabilities and
then selecting a maximum-reliability child for the distinguished role is
globally optimal over the entire hierarchical role-assignment family.
This induction needs no enumeration of the 4^G designs.

Build and retain each node's optimal reliability and selected role. Initial
work and memory are O(n). A single primitive probability change requires only
the ancestor path, O(h) constant-arity gate operations at height h. The
probabilities may be empirical source means, posterior means, or hypothetical
specified probabilities. If the means came from adaptively allocated data,
this optimization statement remains algebraically exact for that empirical
product law; it does NOT make the resulting plug-in estimator unbiased.

2. A confidence sequence compatible with the existing record factorization
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Suppose the nested complete records R_t have likelihood

  L_t(p;R_t) = k_t(R_t) product_i p_i^A_it (1-p_i)^B_it,

where k_t is parameter-independent. Ordinary predictable source sampling has
this property. So do the previously proved complete cylinder-certificate and
exchangeable-probe records under their stated disclosure/restoration rules.

For each coordinate i, change only p_i to a fixed q. The likelihood ratio
  (q/p_i)^A_it ((1-q)/(1-p_i))^B_it
is a nonnegative martingale in the complete-record filtration under p. Mixing
q uniformly over [0,1] gives the exact e-process

  E_it(p_i) = Beta(A_it+1,B_it+1) /
                 [p_i^A_it (1-p_i)^B_it].

The beta integral here is a proof device for frequentist coverage; it does not
assume that the unknown scientific p_i was drawn from a Bayesian prior.
Ville's inequality gives

  Pr_p(exists t: E_it(p_i)>=1/delta_i) <= delta_i.

Choose positive delta_i with sum_i delta_i<=delta. Let I_it=[l_it,u_it] be
the closure of {q:E_it(q)<1/delta_i}. Then with probability >=1-delta,
all true coordinates belong to all their intervals at every record time.
The intervals are computed from counts, with O(1) beta-normalizer updates
and two scalar root searches. For one new source count only that interval
and ancestor tree entries need updating.

This argument needs a genuinely nested, correctly normalized COMPLETE
likelihood record. It does not authorize exposing hidden orientations, new
cache values, or value-dependent maintenance logs while retaining a stale
conditional-cache guarantee. For ordinary raw source streams it applies
directly to the usual adaptive acquisition transcript.

On the one joint coverage event, every adaptively selected monotone circuit,
every adaptive role assignment, and every stopping time obeys its bounds
over the current source rectangle. No extra union bound over the number of
designs considered or the number of redesigns is needed. A location chosen
adaptively among many sources still needs simultaneous source coverage;
one cannot apply a two-preselected-source error budget retroactively to an
arbitrarily selected pair.

3. Exact recursive certificate for universally optimal roles
-----------------------------------------------------------
Let Q_v^*(p) be the maximum reliability achievable in subtree v over its
hierarchical role assignments. It is continuous and coordinatewise increasing.
For a rectangular source region, define

  L_v=Q_v^*(l), U_v=Q_v^*(u).

These bounds are exact: they are attained at the lower and upper corners
(or approached in the open full-support domain). Different child subtrees
have disjoint source scopes, so their extrema can be chosen independently.

Fix a full selected design D. At a leaf its universal-optimality flag is true.
At an internal node whose selected distinguished child is j, set

  CERT_v = [all children CERT] AND [L_j >= max_(k!=j) U_k].

THEOREM. For a nonempty source rectangle with full-support points, CERT_root
is true if and only if D is an optimal allowed design for every source vector
in that rectangle intersected with the open cube.

Sufficiency: child designs attain their respective optimum throughout the
region. The displayed interval dominance makes j a largest-reliability child
throughout the region. Apply the swap identity and induction.

Necessity: if a child design is not universally optimal, choose a parameter
vector improving it. Strict increase at every ancestor makes that improvement
strict at the root. Otherwise all children are universally optimal. If the
interval condition fails, some k has U_k>L_j. Put the j subtree at its lower
corner and the k subtree at its upper corner, choosing full-support points
arbitrarily close if necessary. Their source scopes are disjoint, so this
simultaneously makes child k strictly more reliable. Swapping j and k improves
the node and all its ancestors. This constructs an adverse parameter witness.

The same recursion maintains an actual selected design, its own [l_D,u_D]
reliability interval, and the optimal-value interval [L_root,U_root]. Thus

  true optimum - true selected value <= U_root - l_D.

That bound can be vacuous. CERT_root=true instead gives exactly zero regret
over the region even if the root-value interval itself is wide. The robust
maximum-minimum design is obtained by optimizing at l; its worst-case value
is exactly L_root. This is not a claim of minimax-regret optimality.

All these quantities can be updated along one ancestor path. The supplied
implementation uses five probability-gate evaluations per updated node.
Finding the next source to sample is a separate operation: the demonstration
uses an O(n) scan and does not hide it in the O(h) maintenance count.

4. Exact local comparison bands exploit context cancellation
-----------------------------------------------------------
Keep every design choice fixed except for exchanging distinguished role a
with role b at a single four-majority node. Write its four child reliabilities
as q_a,q_b,q_c,q_d. The root probability is affine in the local node value:

  mu = alpha_out + B_out q_local,

where

  B_out = product_(ancestors v) d_(path child) g_v(q_siblings).

Each derivative is a nonnegative pivotal probability. All sibling-source
groups appearing in different ancestor factors are disjoint. Hence

  Delta mu = B_out (q_a-q_b)(q_c+q_d-2q_c q_d).

The winner depends only on q_a versus q_b. Outside evidence is irrelevant to
the SIGN. It is needed to certify the effect magnitude unless outside source
restrictions are already part of the specified model.

Over a source rectangle, first obtain exact reliability intervals [L_i,U_i]
for the fixed child designs. The pivotal factor at an ancestor is multiaffine
in its at most three sibling reliabilities, so its extrema occur at the at
most eight sibling-interval corners. Its attainable range is exact because
the sibling source scopes are disjoint and each child probability spans its
interval continuously. Different ancestor factors use disjoint sources, so
the exact B_min and B_max are the products of the factor extrema.

Similarly gamma=q_c+q_d-2q_c q_d has exact extrema gamma_min,gamma_max at
four corners. Let

  A_min=B_min gamma_min, A_max=B_max gamma_max,
  D_min=L_a-U_b, D_max=U_a-L_b.

Then the EXACT contrast interval over the source rectangle is

  Delta_min = (A_min if D_min>=0 else A_max) D_min,
  Delta_max = (A_max if D_max>=0 else A_min) D_max.

All extrema can be attained simultaneously because the three factors depend
on disjoint source groups. In particular, the exact worst-case regret of
choosing role a rather than the better of roles a and b is

  Regret_box(a;b) = B_max gamma_max (U_b-L_a)_+.

The path computation is O(h 2^(k-1)) for arity k, hence O(h) for k=4.
It uses paired contrasts and a common context, and can be much tighter than
subtracting two separately computed root-value intervals. It remains a
confidence-RECTANGLE result; it is not an exact distributional confidence
interval or a claim that the rectangle is statistically optimal.

5. A nonvanishing-effect acquisition separation, with all costs stated
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Put the local gate under an OR with n outside primitive sources. Their laws
are unknown. Compare only the two allowed role assignments a and b.

Fixture values (harness only):
  p_a=.7, p_b=.3, p_c=p_d=.5;
  outside p_i=t/n with t=.5.

The local probabilities are q_a=.6 and q_b=.4. The root probabilities are

  mu_a=1-.4(1-t/n)^n,
  mu_b=1-.6(1-t/n)^n,

so the gain tends to .2 exp(-.5)=.121306..., with both root probabilities
bounded away from zero and one. This is not a disappearing-signal example.

With error budget .025 on each of the two preselected candidate sources,
sample predictably until their beta-mixture intervals are disjoint. In the
executed fixture, this took 135 observations (66 and 69); the intervals were

  p_a: [0.5084628031727817, 0.8697139434521024],
  p_b: [0.14496589470823007, 0.5045163594661759].

The resulting selected role is nonworse, simultaneously at the adaptive
stopping time, with probability >=.95 for every full-support source vector.
No data from c,d or the outside sources are required for this sign certificate.
For n=4096 the harness root gain is .12130242972836958.

This sample count is one realized run, NOT a universal expected-call bound.
For a fixed positive candidate gap Delta, standard bounds for these mixture
intervals give an acquisition scale of order Delta^(-2)
[log(1/delta)+log(1/Delta)], independent of n, under balanced sampling. The
algorithm need not use a full system cache to achieve this.

CONSTRUCTION BILL: the ordinary decision-only baseline uses 135 total draws.
If an entire n+4-coordinate simulator cache is instead required first, its
n+4 draws and n+1 initial gates remain charged. The two relevant initial
draws are already in the 135-call record, so n=4096 gives 4,100 initial draws,
4,097 initial gates, 133 additional decision draws, and 4,233 total draws.
The optional full outside cache is actually constructed by the executable.
An ordinary method allowed to skip irrelevant construction is stronger.

Why full root VALUE inference is harder in the same nontrivial fixture:
even give an estimator q_a=.6 and the knowledge that all outside p_i=t/n.
Only t remains unknown in a fixed open neighborhood of .5. One outside draw
has Fisher information

  I_one(t) = 1/[n t(1-t/n)],

and

  mu_a'(t) = (1-q_a)(1-t/n)^(n-1).

The adaptive van-Trees argument already proved in the parent record yields
the local asymptotic absolute-risk source-cost constant

  mu_a'(t)^2 / I_one(t)
    = n t (1-q_a)^2 (1-t/n)^(2n-1),

which is Theta(n) at t=.5. For n=4096 its value is
120.5540933958397. This is an asymptotic epsilon^2 times source-cost lower
constant, NOT a finite-epsilon numerical lower bound. Already-paid samples
subtract from the required additional information budget; they are not free.

For a finite explicit bound, take a smooth prior rho on [a,b] around .5,
vanishing at its endpoints, with J_rho=integral(rho'^2/rho). A uniform MSE
target epsilon^2 and worst expected total outside-call budget C imply

  C >= n a(1-b/n) [ (E_rho mu_a')^2/epsilon^2 - J_rho ].

This lower bound gives the ordinary root-inference baseline known common-t
structure and free computation. The separation is BETWEEN TASKS (choose the
better local design versus estimate root value), not between information-
equivalent retained and ordinary sampling architectures.

6. Executed adverse test and numerical scope
------------------------------------------
Exact rational tests:
  972 four-majority swap identities; one general weighted-threshold swap;
  six depth-two fixtures, all 1,024 allowed designs each;
  12 exact interval-dominance cases;
  every one of 128 source-box vertices for a two-gate seven-source contrast,
  repeated for all four outer roles, matching the factorized extrema exactly.

The dynamic consumer used 256 leaves, 85 initial probability-gate updates,
4,096 source observations, and exactly 16,384 ancestor updates. Each update
uses five probability-gate evaluations. It maintained the empirical optimum
over 4^85 designs and observed 295 role changes. Full recomputation checks
matched at every checked point.

The executed source-selection policy also made 979,200 scan comparisons.
Role-change auditing made another 348,160 comparisons, and 17 full consistency
checks used 10,115 further probability-gate evaluations. Confidence endpoint
root searches are additional arithmetic. These are recorded separately;
the O(h) claim describes representation maintenance, not the entire measured
consumer. No wall-time or end-to-end speed advantage is claimed.

ADVERSE RESULT: at this small sample budget, the root simultaneous confidence
rectangle was essentially [0,1], and global role optimality was NOT certified.
Cheap algebraic optimization does not make global statistical uncertainty
small. The local sign certificate is the meaningful inference consumer here.

Mixture-interval endpoints in the executable use 52 bisections and outward
endpoint choices in ordinary floating arithmetic. The displayed values are
not interval-arithmetic numerical proof certificates. The symbolic e-process
and exact confidence-set definition establish the probabilistic theorem.

7. Audit of the parent's fixed-batch Bayesian risk compiler
---------------------------------------------------------
For a read-once multilinear probability tree with independent current Beta
leaf posteriors, let m_j and v_j be current child means and variances. For a
node g define

  Phi_g(z) = sum_(nonempty S) [partial_S g(m)]^2 product_(j in S) z_j.

Orthogonal expansion around the independent child means gives exact current
target variance Phi_g(v). If future source counts are FIXED conditional on
current history, each leaf posterior mean after b future observations has
variance gain

  V_i(b) = v_i b/(s_i+b),

where s_i is current Beta concentration. Future child posterior means remain
independent, with current means m_j, so their root posterior-mean variance
gain is obtained by the same Phi recurrence. Its coefficients are nonnegative;
therefore maximizing child gains separately for each assigned child budget
gives an exact fixed-batch allocation DP.

Adaptive past sampling and query choice are fine after conditioning on their
complete history. The target must stay fixed through the contemplated batch.
Adaptive within-batch source allocation is a different, generally stronger
optimization problem.

EXACT COUNTEREXAMPLE TO A FULLY ADAPTIVE TWO-STEP CLAIM:
  g(p)=p_1 p_2+(1-p_1)p_3;
  p_1~Beta(1/10,1/10), p_2,p_3~Beta(1,1).

The exact gains of the six fixed two-call allocations are
  (1,1):0;
  (1,2),(1,3):61/5184;
  (2,2),(3,3):1/96;
  (2,3):1/72 (best).

Instead, observe source 1 first, then source 2 when its outcome is one and
source 3 when zero. The first observation has zero immediate target-mean
gain. Its selected branch coefficient becomes 11/12, so the total gain is

  (11/12)^2 (1/36) = 121/5184,

which exceeds the best fixed-batch gain by a factor 121/72. Exact predictive
enumeration in the executable verifies all these rational values.

8. Strong ordinary baseline and novelty boundary
------------------------------------------------
The strongest ordinary baseline here retains the identical sufficient source
counts, uses the same confidence sequences, compiles the same probability
tree, and runs the same local-contrast and design recursion. It achieves all
of the stated costs and certificates. Fresh simulation of every candidate
and recomputation of every global gradient are weaker baselines.

Exact variable elimination on the read-once tree is probability propagation;
it is already the evaluator used above. When the numerical primitive p_i are
known, this ordinary method optimizes and evaluates without new source draws.

The results identify a concrete consumer and exact structural certificates.
They do not establish superiority of a special retained-cache representation,
and no literature-priority claim is made. The weighted interchange argument,
likelihood-ratio martingales, confidence sequences, product sufficiency, and
dynamic tree evaluation are ordinary tools. The supplied derivations establish
the combined statements used here without relying on inaccessible papers.
