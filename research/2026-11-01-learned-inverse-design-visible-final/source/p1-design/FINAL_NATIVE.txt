## The strongest opening: acquire the logical variables of a design problem

I selected **repeated global inverse design**: learn from imperfect experiments, then reliably choose configurations as later costs or external fields change.

The construction now acquires more than an energy predictor. It discovers groups of physical variables that can be treated as logical variables, verifies the cost of restricting computation to those groups, and builds an actual optimizer on the resulting interactions. **Neither the groups, their number, the useful interaction graph, nor a downstream solver is supplied.**

The organizing principle is:

> **Learn a computational reduction together with its region of validity and an executable consumer.**

A large, dense, high-rank part of the physical energy can help justify that reduction: it makes departures from collective configurations expensive. It need not be approximated by the small model retained for later computation.

This is a completed theoretical and executable construction under an explicit experimental contract. I have not established historical priority or superiority over every strong conventional method. The strongest conventional acquisition substantially improved the construction, and the ordinary retaining optimizer succeeded on every small comparison case tested.

## 1. The consequential operation

Consider \(n\) controllable binary variables \(x_i\in\{-1,+1\}\), with unknown energy

\[
E(x)=c+b^\top x+\frac12x^\top A_{\mathrm{true}}x.
\]

An experiment prepares a complete configuration and returns a noisy scalar energy reading. The source supplies a warranted absolute noise bound; every observation, preparation, control write, and relevant arithmetic cost is charged.

Later requests supply fields \(h\) in a specified region,

\[
|h_i|\le H.
\]

The acquired service returns a physical configuration \(\widehat x(h)\) satisfying

\[
E(\widehat x(h))+h^\top\widehat x(h)
\le
\min_x\{E(x)+h^\top x\}+\epsilon n.
\]

The guarantee holds on **one acquisition event for every subsequent field in the region**, including fields chosen adaptively after seeing previous answers.

This addresses the planning obstruction directly. A good quadratic predictor can still describe a hard binary optimization problem. The new object includes a verified reduction of that optimization and the program that executes it.

The source contract is substantive. This branch assumes a globally quadratic mean response. It does not inherit the arbitrary resource-dependent nuisance function \(g(B)\) from the separate resource-quotient construction. Controlled inputs are literal for a computational experiment; laboratory preparation and measurement access would need to meet the stated contract, with their actual costs included.

## 2. The central theorem: quantify the price of a learned contraction

Suppose the learner proposes a partition of the physical variables into \(r\) groups. Let \(P\) be its indicator matrix and

\[
W=P(P^\top P)^{-1}P^\top
\]

the projector onto vectors constant within each group.

A logical configuration \(z\in\{-1,+1\}^r\) represents the physical configuration \(Pz\). The learner must earn the right to use these configurations.

From the acquired finite quadratic model \(\widehat E(x)=\widehat b^\top x+\tfrac12x^\top\widehat A x\), it constructs and verifies two quantities:

\[
(I-W)\widehat A(I-W)\succeq \gamma(I-W),
\]

and

\[
\|W\widehat A(I-W)\|_{\mathrm{op}}^2\le d^2.
\]

The first measures the energy curvature in discarded directions. The second measures how strongly those directions couple back to the retained variables.

These are **checked numerical certificates**. A floating eigensolver proposes the partition and bounds. Exact rational matrix checks authorize the bounds. A favorable-looking spectrum alone does not authorize a design.

Let

\[
C=P^\top\widehat A P,
\]

and, writing \(m_a\) for group sizes, define

\[
\alpha=
\max_a
\frac{(C_{aa})_+ + \sum_{b\ne a}|C_{ab}|}{m_a},
\qquad
\beta=\|\widehat b\|_\infty+H,
\qquad
g=\gamma-2\alpha-2\beta.
\]

We obtained the following uniform contraction bound:

\[
\boxed{
\min_z\{\widehat E(Pz)+h^\top Pz\}
\le
\min_x\{\widehat E(x)+h^\top x\}
+
\Lambda
}
\]

where

\[
\boxed{
\Lambda=
\frac n4\left(\sqrt{g^2+4d^2}-g\right).
}
\]

It holds throughout the field box, including when \(g\le0\). When \(g>0\),

\[
\Lambda\le \frac{nd^2}{2g}.
\]

### Why this goes beyond a small perturbation argument

The discarded energy itself can be very large. Its amplitude, dense interaction graph, and rank need not be small. The certificate uses its **lower energetic effect on departures from the learned groups**, together with the harmful cross coupling and the permitted future fields.

The derivation makes the distinction concrete. Given any physical configuration, round each group to its majority sign. If \(v=(I-W)x\), then

\[
\|Wx\|^2=n-\|v\|^2.
\]

The energy lost by rounding is controlled by

\[
\frac g2\|v\|^2
-
d\sqrt{\|v\|^2\bigl(n-\|v\|^2\bigr)}.
\]

Minimizing this expression yields \(-\Lambda\). Thus the theorem prices the actual computational restriction instead of demanding that an exact latent optimum set survive every perturbation.

A stronger discrete condition sometimes proves exact alignment for the acquired model:

\[
g>0,\qquad g^2>4nd^2.
\]

That shortcut is optional. The implemented service also accepts useful **approximate** contractions when this exact-alignment test fails.

For finite arithmetic, acceptance does not depend on an unchecked floating square root. A proposed rational loss \(L\) is verified through

\[
\psi=\frac{2L}{n},\qquad
\psi\ge0,\quad g+\psi\ge0,\quad \psi(g+\psi)\ge d^2.
\]

## 3. Acquisition was improved by adopting the stronger ordinary route

The first explicit acquisition used four fresh scalar measurements per quadratic or linear coefficient. It worked, but its conservative noise bill could become unnecessarily large.

We replaced that as the main route with **established orthogonal-array machinery**. Binary orthogonal arrays and their coding-theoretic constructions are longstanding tools; the accompanying proof reconstructs the particular design used here. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

The construction generates a deterministic set of fewer than \(8n^2\) binary input configurations. Its constant, unary, and pair features have exactly orthogonal columns:

\[
\Phi^\top\Phi=N I.
\]

Consequently:

- Every quadratic coefficient is acquired by a signed average of actual energy reports.
- An integer Walsh transform performs the reconstruction.
- No random conditioning assumption or useful physical representation is supplied.
- Report quantization, coefficient rounding, and received bits are explicitly counted.

For

\[
D=1+n+\binom n2
\]

features and \(M\) total observations, the source-noise contribution satisfies

\[
\sup_x |\widehat E(x)-E(x)|
\le
\sigma
\sqrt{
\frac{2D}{M}
\left(n\log 2+\log\frac2\delta\right)
}
\]

on one confidence event, before the separately charged deterministic precision terms.

The union bound over \(2^n\) configurations is a proof step; the acquisition program does not enumerate those configurations.

This gives a sufficient observation scale for **physical objective transfer** of

\[
O\!\left(
n^2+
\frac{\sigma^2[n+\log(1/\delta)]}{\epsilon^2}
\right).
\]

Discovery and acceptance can need additional accuracy. The proof separately charges precision for recovering a useful partition, preserving the contraction margin, and certifying the coarse interaction tail. It does not present the objective-transfer bound as the total completion cost.

The current construction still pays for a full quadratic acquisition. It has **not** earned acquisition cost proportional only to the small retained representation.

## 4. The optimizer is constructed too

After acquiring a partition, the learner computes the interaction matrix \(C\) between its logical variables.

It selects a maximum-weight forest from those acquired interactions. If some coarse interactions are discarded, it charges the directly computable oscillation bound

\[
B_{\mathrm{tail}}
=
2\sum_{\text{discarded }a<b}|C_{ab}|.
\]

For each later field request, the consumer:

1. Sums the physical fields inside each acquired group.
2. Runs two-state dynamic programming on the acquired forest.
3. Backtraces logical signs.
4. Returns the actual physical configuration \(Pz\).

The finite consumer uses integer arithmetic and exact comparisons. Its arithmetic cost is \(O(n+r)\), including reading all physical fields and producing the physical output. It does not enumerate \(2^r\) logical configurations.

The final guarantee combines three independently meaningful charges:

\[
\boxed{
\text{physical design regret}
\le
B_{\mathrm{source}}
+
L_{\mathrm{reduction}}
+
B_{\mathrm{tail}}.
}
\]

The service is issued only when this sum fits the requested budget.

The dense fitted matrix and calibration state can then be removed from the online service. They remain in the experimental record for comparison and reproducibility.

### The 128-variable run

The physical model had dense signed interactions and a locking component of rank 96. The learner discovered 32 groups and retained:

- **128 membership labels**
- **63 scalar coefficients**
- The field-region and error certificate

Twelve adaptive field requests produced **twelve different designs**, without additional energy observations.

The acquisition requested 4,194,304 input bits and received 2,097,152 binary64 report bits. The integer Walsh transform used 491,520 additions. Exact contraction verification used 305,792 rational products, with numerators reaching 3,868 bits. A recorded downstream query consumed all 128 fields and required 62 integer tree comparisons.

On this runtime, source acquisition took about 0.88 seconds, compilation about 9.04 seconds, and twelve subsequent queries about 0.0041 seconds. These are measurements of the synthetic executable, not physical preparation-time or broad scaling claims.

### What was independently checked

For \(n=12\), all 4,096 physical configurations were enumerated on each of twelve adaptive queries. The largest observed true regret was approximately **0.01306**, within the certified 0.200663 bound.

For the approximate \(n=24\) case, every aligned configuration was independently scored. Its certificate charges approximately **0.18914** for contraction itself. The exact-alignment sufficient test failed, while the approximate service passed.

The \(n=128\) ambient space was not exhaustively searched. Its guarantee comes from the acquired uniform source event, exact rational reduction checks, and the proved consumer—not an asserted enumeration of \(2^{128}\) states.

## 6. What the conventional comparison establishes

The relevant predecessors are substantial.

Relational persistency methods already discover variables that must agree or disagree and use those relations to reduce quadratic binary optimization. Ground-state-preserving contraction is therefore an established computational operation. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}

Minor-embedding work also establishes conditions under which physical groups synchronize into logical variables, with the embedding supplied as part of that construction. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"} Recovering indicator-like groups from a spectral subspace is established numerical machinery, which we use for proposals. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="3"}

There are also direct predecessors for learning energy models that preserve scientific decisions. Huang and colleagues’ constrained cluster-expansion construction preserves reference-set ground states and uses additional searches to address unseen configurations. Its treatment does not supply the finite-sample, all-future-field service proved here, but it prevents claiming that decision-preserving energy learning itself is new. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="4"}

The fair comparison therefore gives ordinary methods the same acquired quadratic, confidence information, retained data, and requested accuracy.

I implemented that comparison with a retaining mixed-integer optimizer, including presolve and cuts. Its stopping criterion uses an **absolute optimization-gap budget after reserving the same source uncertainty**. It was not forced to solve a harder exact physical problem.

**It met the requested gap on every tested small query.** In the noisy 24-variable comparison, six ordinary MILP queries took about 20.2 seconds; the six compiled queries took under a millisecond after the paid construction. These are implementation-specific benchmarks. Specialized persistency, SDP, or spectral preprocessing could also acquire a reusable reduction and should be allowed to adopt this certificate.

The defensible contribution is the complete connection:

**noisy scalar evidence → acquired logical variables → quantitative reduction certificate → actual coarse optimizer → uniform future-design guarantee.**

## 7. The failures sharpened the result

Several boundaries are decisive.

**Positive curvature alone is insufficient.** A preserved four-variable example has positive normal curvature but enough cross coupling that restricting to the proposed groups loses an order-one amount. This is why the certificate measures \(d\), and why the continuous contraction bound matters. An independent algebraic audit checked 80 dense instances, 12,800 complete binary assignments, and 960 field queries.

**The future field region is essential.** Any fixed proper subset of physical configurations omits some \(x^\star\). A sufficiently strong field favoring \(x^\star\) makes it uniquely optimal. The consumer therefore rejects fields outside its certified box; widening that box requires recertification.

**A favorable sample does not establish the constitutive class.** An unmodelled high-order spike can hide at an unobserved configuration and change the optimum. The globally quadratic source assumption is explicit.

**A failed proposed reduction is not a hardness result.** Wrong partitions were rejected. A grouped-forest acquisition also diverged in a preserved insufficient-data run. Neither failure authorizes an uncertified answer or proves that another algorithm cannot solve the task.

**Finite arithmetic does not calibrate an instrument.** Exact rational checks protect the finite certificate, and integer dynamic programming protects the consumer. The source’s preparation access, noise law, and readout precision remain explicit experimental obligations. The programs are synthetic demonstrations of that contract.

These limits are part of the construction, rather than qualifications added after a favorable numerical result.
