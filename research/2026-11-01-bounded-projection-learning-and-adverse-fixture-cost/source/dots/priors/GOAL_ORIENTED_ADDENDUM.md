1 November 2026. Read `scalar_return_acquisition_20261101/GOAL_ORIENTED_RETURN_CONSTRUCTION.md`.

## Finding

The primal/dual residual product and resulting quadratic output-error behavior are established goal-oriented reduction, not a new principle. The proposed larger ridge scale and improved sufficient epsilon exponent are a valuable application of that cancellation to the prior scalar acquisition construction. They do not alone establish a new enabling capability.

Feng, Chellappa, Benner, *A posteriori error estimation for model order reduction of parametric systems*, Advanced Modeling and Simulation in Engineering Sciences 11:5 (11 March 2024), Theorem 2 / equation 14 gives a product of accumulated primal and dual residual norms for corrected time-evolution outputs. It assumes zero initial error and SPD E and -A in that theorem; Remark 6 explicitly identifies the quadratic behavior. The setup provides full system matrices and projection bases. Therefore this is not an exact same-interface acquisition dominator. Avoid using its steady Theorem 4 displayed denominator as a formula reference: the displayed statement and proof appear inconsistent; Theorem 2 is sufficient here.

https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40323-024-00260-8

The official pyMOR v2022.2.0 DWR documentation (updated December 29, 2022) already implements corrected outputs and their estimator, requiring a full model and primal/dual bases; a dual problem is reduced for each output dimension. This confirms mature operational precedent, not just an abstract analogy.

## Same-interface credit that remains

Neither inspected source acquires primal and adjoint operations from noisy scalar return experiments with no original-Gram gap. The analytical tag's disappearance after the first action, empirical Galerkin cancellation, true residual Gram bounds, and measurable failed-task witness jointly adapt the established output estimator to that stricter interface. This join deserves verification/credit, but is best described as a goal-oriented corollary of the scalar acquisition theorem. Earlier kernel/automata source limitations remain unchanged; no need to repeat that literature pass to manufacture novelty.

## Crucial northstar gap

The construction still needs a consequential *actual* return source and a feasible cost crossover over strong retaining, goal-oriented kernel/operator learners. Specifically:

- Earn common stationarity, known adjoints, bounded unbiased reports and preparation cost physically, rather than assume that reversibility is available everywhere.
- Demonstrate that acquiring/reusing this model changes which valuable experiment/control tasks can be performed, after all calibration shots and failures are charged.
- The efficiency premise still concerns the primal/adjoint reachable family's low-dimensional tube. It is not yet a theorem in a smaller output-observable complexity such as Hankel/task rank. Thus “only learn what questions need” remains partly motivation, not a fully earned dimension advantage.
- A finite-horizon seed-return service is not arbitrary readouts, feedback/planning, a generator, or an upfront universally completed compiler.

## Decision

Do not start another incremental certificate/exponent optimization as a standalone research direction. Finish this short corollary only if needed to complete the already selected acquisition theorem, then require a concrete feasible source/application or a materially stronger task-complexity theorem before continuing under the user's northstar. A formal epsilon-exponent improvement is not evidence of civilization-level compounding by itself. Conversely, absence of a complete same-interface prior remains unresolved—not disproved by citing DWR.
