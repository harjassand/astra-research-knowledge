# A complete live-background neutral renewal without repeated Lp loss

Author construction using the precisely qualified P1 common-background
lead. This earns a polynomial actual neutral-renewal operation. It does
NOT yet prove that its final, competing-source output is inward, nor that
an interrupted neutral debt is fully repaid. Those are separate obligations.

## 1. The common background, at its actual scope

Freeze only the I anchor A=a as a GENERATOR DECOMPOSITION. Keep the entire
D-cycle and both core directions on Y=(B,C,D,E,F). Its generator G_a has
one full-support product-Poisson invariant probability mu for ALL a>=0.
The D/E/F means are the ordinary C3 complex-balance values d*,e*,f*.
Choose b*,c*>0 with the CORRECT balance

    b*c*=sqrt(kb/kf) e*f*.

Thus kf(b*c*)^2=kb(e*f*)^2. No incoming law is set equal to mu, and no
mixing across B-C or parity classes is assumed. The actual process is
recovered by the I source jumps, which change a and the background state.
This invariant-reference premise is P1's construction, used with the
coordinator's square-root correction. Same-p Poisson shifts are NOT used.

Let h_j^a(y) be the four genuine I propensities, and H_a=sum h_j^a>=alpha0.
Let R_a be the ACTUAL background killed at ALL these I hazards, integrated
over its physical time. Its adjoint acts on measures. Invariance and
nonnegative stopped iteration give the exact identity and domination

    R_a^*(H_a mu)=mu,
    R_a^*(h_j^a mu)<=mu.                             (1)

Indeed (H_a-G_a^*)mu=H_a mu, and the killed semigroup applied to mu is
bounded by exp(-alpha0 t)mu. Integrating its derivative proves (1).
Unbounded source hazards are integrable under mu; finite killed
localizations justify the identity. P3 nonexplosion is available for the
complete physical process as well.

## 2. Source transfer carries the next ACTUAL hazard

Follow the I word0,1,2,3, starting at A=a. Between its letters retain ALL
background evolution. Any other I source is a failure output, not omitted.
Let beta_B=b*,beta_C=c* in the following four shift constants:

 c0=alpha0/[alpha1(a+1)beta_B],
 c1=alpha1/[alpha2(a+2)beta_C],
 c2=alpha2/[alpha3(a+3)beta_B beta_C],
 c3=alpha3(a+3)(a+2)(a+1)beta_B^2 beta_C^2/alpha0.

Poisson falling-factorial identities give

    shift_j^*(h_j mu)=c_j h_(j+1)^new-anchor mu,
    c0 c1 c2 c3=1.                                  (2)

For example the I2 transfer turns BC mu into
(B)_2(C)_2 mu/(beta_B beta_C), with the correct actual A factor.
The terminal shifts to source0, which has full background support.
All constants in (2) are finite raw formulas; no polynomial multiplier
is being incorrectly declared bounded on the same Lp space.

Let Q_a be the substochastic kernel for SUCCESSFULLY following the full
word, from A=a back to A=a. Its physical background output is retained.
Starting with nu=alpha0 mu, apply (1),(2) four times. Exactly

    nu Q_a<=nu.                                     (3)

This is a bound on an actual path kernel with all D/core motion live.
It is not a reset to nu. The inequality, rather than an invariant physical
fiber, supplies the useful transport operation.

## 3. Every input has a polynomial abort chance

Write m=|y|. The event that the FIRST TWO genuine reactions are I0,I0
aborts this word, because its second expected I letter was I1. The two
I0 hazards are alpha0. Every full rate is a fixed polynomial of degree
at most7 and the first I0 adds only2 particles. Therefore for a raw c>0,

    1-Q_a 1(y)>=c(1+a+m)^(-14).                       (4)

One explicit choice is c=alpha0^2/[3^7 Ksum^2], where Ksum is the sum
of all ten fixed positive raw constants. Every propensity is bounded by
its constant times(1+M)^7, and after the first input 3+M<=3(1+M).

This is only a lower bound from one actual path. All other paths remain
in Q_a or its complete failure kernel B_a. No assumption on the current
D/E/F inventory or their conditional law is present.

## 4. A general polynomial-killing lemma

Suppose Q is sub-Markov, mu Q<=mu for a full-support finite measure with
Poisson-type tail, and 1-Q1(y)>=c(1+a+|y|)^(-K). Let L be the number of
successive Q attempts up to the first failure, starting at y.
Then for every integer r>=1,

    E_y L^r<=C_r(1+a+|y|)^(2Kr).                     (5)

Here is a direct proof rather than a supplied ergodicity premise.
Subinvariance implies delta_y Q^n<=mu/mu(y). If s_n=Q^n1(y), for any R,

 s_(n+1)<=[1-c(1+a+R)^(-K)]s_n
              +c(1+a+R)^(-K) mu{|z|>R}/mu(y).

Consequently

 s_n<=exp[-c n/(1+a+R)^K]+mu{|z|>R}/mu(y).          (6)

For a product Poisson,
mu(y)>=exp[-C(1+m)log(2+m)] and
mu{|z|>R}<=C exp[-c R log(2+R)]. Choose
R=ceil(C0(1+m)+n^(1/(2K))) with fixed C0 large enough to absorb mu(y)^-1.
The tail ratio in (6) is <=C exp[-c n^(1/(2K))log(2+n)]. Once
n>=C(1+a+m)^(2K), its first term is <=exp(-c sqrt(n)). Both tails have
finite moments of every order, with constants independent of a,y.
Summing them proves (5). No gap or stationary entrance law is used.

Applying K=14 gives E L^r<=C_r(1+M_input)^(28r), uniformly over the OTHER
unbounded anchor and all five background counts. This is deliberately a
crude proved polynomial, not an optimized exponent or practical bound.

## 5. Physical residence is actually paid

Repeat complete words until the first wrong I source. Call that actual
stopping time T and retain its complete wrong-source landing. There are
at most4L I reactions before T. The constant I0 channel is an independent
rate-alpha0 Poisson clock and is among those reactions. Hence

    E T<=4 E L/alpha0,
    E T^r<=C_r(1+M_input)^(28r).                      (7)

For the moment statement, {T>t,L<=alpha0 t/8} forces the independent
I0 Poisson clock to have at most alpha0 t/2 events by t. Its usual lower
tail is exponentially small. Thus P(T>t)<=P(L>alpha0 t/8)+exp(-c t),
which proves (7) from (5). P3 nonexplosion makes these actual physical
paths well defined. No expected count of all D/core activity is required.

## 6. Actual output moments, without an input-density fiction

The complete wrong-source kernel B_a includes every abort letter at each
of the four prefix stages and every background path. Starting from mu,
(1),(2) bound its source-weighted output density by a finite sum of

    C(1+a)^3 (1+|z|)^4 mu(z),                        (8)

at anchors differing from a by at most4. This follows by applying the
genuine Poisson source shift to each actual abort; it includes terminals.
Together with (3), delta_y Q_a^n B_a is bounded by (8)/mu(y) for EACH n.
To sum n, split at N=ceil[C(1+a+m+R)^(28)]. For output mass above R, the
first N terms have total bound N times the Poisson-polynomial tail in
(8)/mu(y); later failures have probability at most s_N. Equations (5),(6)
show that for R>=C0(1+a+m), the resulting output tail is <=C exp(-cR).
Therefore

    E(1+M_output)^p<=C_p(1+M_input)^p                 (9)

for every fixed p. In particular the actual new global service/envelope
receiver has a degree-preserving finite output bound. This is an acquired
polynomial statement, not a consequence of finite per-input Lp norms.

## 7. What this does NOT yet close

Q_a is a completed neutral-cycle kernel, but the final wrong-source output
in (7)-(9) can carry a new activation debt. A constant-C output moment
bound is not coefficient-one drift. This result does not identify that
landing with an invariant profile or declare its debt repaid.
The remaining construction must use these actual source kernels and
their complete competing outputs to pay joint neutral-debt completion,
or find a global invariant-law argument. The service receiver/envelope
remains replaceable. No full recurrence theorem follows from (3)-(9) alone.

The earlier marked-factorial neutral criterion and exact retag factorization
are preserved at their scopes. This proof uses a different, actual common
background and a source-hazard resolvent identity; it neither borrows a
same-p shift bound nor claims bounded-distortion composition sufficient.
