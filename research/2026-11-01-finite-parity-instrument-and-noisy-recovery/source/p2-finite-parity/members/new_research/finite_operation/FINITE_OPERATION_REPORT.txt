Root originated the final two-drive inverse construction after this collaborator

1. PHYSICAL MODEL AND ALL OUTLETS

Two data qubits have Pauli operators Z1,Z2; S=Z1 Z2. Two harmonic modes are
source b and corrector c. Their external linewidths are ke,kc. Source bypass
loss is kb, and corrector internal loss is ki. All inputs and both modes
initially are vacuum. The cascade is one-way from b to c, with a single
collected main output; there is no uncharged beamsplitter dark port.

 ae=(ke+kb)/2, ac=(kc+ki)/2,
 Lmain=sqrt(ke)b+sqrt(kc)c,
 Lbypass=sqrt(kb)b, Linternal=sqrt(ki)c,
 H=chi Z1 c^dag c+u(t) Z2(b+b^dag)+w(t) Z2(c+c^dag)
       +(i/2)sqrt(ke kc)(b^dag c-c^dag b).

The last Hamiltonian and the collective main dissipator jointly implement
unidirectional propagation, giving conditional coherent-amplitude equations

 b_z'=-ae b_z-i u z2,
 c_z'=-(ac+i chi z1)c_z-sqrt(ke kc)b_z-i w z2.            (1)

This is a physical cascaded-input-output model. Independent dissipators on b
and c without the collective main outlet do NOT implement it. A circulator,
chiral waveguide, or equivalent directional coupler, its insertion loss,
bandwidth, delay and isolation are actual apparatus resources. The Markov
zero-delay approximation is admitted here; a known pure delay can be included
by retiming the source, but a qubit error during transit cannot be ignored.

The actual longitudinal interaction of Z2 with BOTH modes is essential. A
classical subtraction of the output record, or an unconditional cavity drive,
does not supply w(t) Z2(c+c^dag). There is one Z1 dispersive link and two Z2
longitudinal links, each requiring its physical implementation and calibration.

2. EXACT INVERSE CONSTRUCTION

Let A(t) be real and sufficiently smooth on [0,T], with A,A',A'' zero at both
endpoints. Set

 B=-(A'+ac A)/chi,
 u=-(B'+ae B)/sqrt(ke),
 w=[B'-(kc-ac)B-chi A]/sqrt(kc).                         (2)

For nonzero calibrated chi the exact solution of (1) is

 b_z=i B z2/sqrt(ke),
 c_z=[A s-i B z2]/sqrt(kc),       s=z1 z2.               (3)

To prove this, substitute (3) into (1). Its coefficient of S requires
A'=-ac A-chi B, and its coefficient of Z2 requires
B'=(kc-ac)B+chi A+sqrt(kc)w. These are exactly (2).

The main output waveform therefore is

 beta_main,z(t)=sqrt(ke)b_z+sqrt(kc)c_z=A(t)s.           (4)

This identity holds throughout the finite pulse and at arbitrary admitted
pulse strength. It does not assume slow forcing, a stationary spectrum,
a fourth-order tail, a negligible transient, or a small discarded nonlinear
remainder. Both physical oscillators return to vacuum exactly at T. The
controls also start and end at zero because A,A',A'' do.

The mathematical claim is in the conditional-linear oscillator model (1).
Any breakdown of the dispersive or harmonic approximation at the actual
occupations, Kerr terms, saturation, or extra outlet is outside that claim.

In the lossless matched case chi=ac=kc/2,

 w=-2 A''/kc^(3/2).                                    (5)

Thus the key extra actuator carries a derivative counterdrive. This is an
inverse-engineered physical pulse. Generic pulse inverse engineering and
DRAG/counterdiabatic methods are established; they are not claimed as new.

3. FULL QUANTUM DILATION, NOT ONLY CLASSICAL POINTERS

A passive linear network driven conditionally by a fixed computational basis
state maps vacuum to a product of coherent oscillator/field states. Normal-
ordered quadratic passive evolution adds no amplitude-dependent scalar phase;
the linear drives add the phase

 phi_z(T)=-int Re[u z2 b_z+w z2 c_z]dt
         =z1 phi,  phi=-int w A/sqrt(kc)dt.              (6)

This is only a local Z1 phase. At T, the complete state of a superposition is

 sum_z psi_z exp(i z1 phi)|z>|0_b 0_c>
       |s A>_main
       |i sqrt(kb/ke) B z2>_bypass
       |sqrt(ki/kc)(s A-i B z2)>_internal.               (7)

Here each ket denotes the complete traveling temporal coherent field over
[0,T]. Equation (7) certifies every logical coherence, initial entanglement
with a reference, every no-click amplitude, and the final apparatus, within
the stated linear vacuum model. It is substantially stronger than equal
field marginals for selected basis inputs.

For kb=ki=0, after the known local phase correction the complete operation is

 |z> -> |z>|s A>.                                       (8)

Any detector inefficiency after the corrector loses information about S only.
It cannot distinguish two states in the same parity sector. Finite measurement
strength remains: the two parity field states have overlap exp(-2N),
N=int A^2 dt. The device is an adjustable weak parity measurement, approaching
a projective measurement with increasing N; it is not an exact projective
measurement at finite photon number.

4. DETECTION AND THE COMPLETE FINITE-STRENGTH INSTRUMENT

Use ordinary fixed-phase homodyne on the main output, with efficiency eta.
With the real A quadrature,

 dY=2 sqrt(eta) s A(t)dt+dW,
 Y_A=int A dY/sqrt(N) ~ Normal(2 sqrt(eta N)s,1).

Report sign(Y_A). Both parity assignment probabilities equal

 epsilon=Phi[-2 sqrt(eta N)].                           (9)

There is no within-sector stochastic measurement phase in the exact device,
since the main field is real and identical for both states in each sector.
No arbitrary diagonal feedback oracle is required. One calibrated local Z1
rotation removes the dynamical and hidden-port within-sector phase.

For the exact all-record-and-coarse-outcome instrument, use computational
basis ordering ++,+-,-+,--. Write s_i=z1_i z2_i, q_i=z2_i, N_B=int B^2dt,
C=int ABdt, r_b=kb/ke and r_i=ki/kc. After an optional local Z1 correction of
angle theta, the Schur multiplier for reported m=+1 or -1 is

 M_m[i,j]=Phi[m(s_i+s_j)sqrt(eta N)]
   * exp[-eta(s_i-s_j)^2N/2]
   * exp[-(1-eta+r_i)(s_i-s_j)^2N/2
         -(r_b+r_i)(q_i-q_j)^2 N_B/2]
   * exp{i[(phi-theta)(z1_i-z1_j)
           +r_i C(s_i q_j-s_j q_i)]}.                 (10)

This acts entrywise on the data density matrix and supplies the actual
classical report m. It is completely positive; the two branches sum to a
trace-preserving channel. Same-sector phase is removed with

 theta=phi-r_i C.                                      (11)

The full formula retains the physically nonzero cross-parity coherences of
a finite-strength measurement; it does not silently project them away.
Both positivity and trace preservation are independently checked numerically
in exact_cascade_parity.py. The matrices in instrument_gram() can be used
without modification to compose a concrete measurement-based gate circuit.

5. WHAT INTERNAL LOSS STILL DOES

For the two distinct states within either parity sector, after (11),

 coherence ratio = exp[-2 D],
 D=(kb/ke+ki/kc) int B^2 dt,
 p_Z=(1-exp[-2D])/2.                                   (12)

This is exact in the admitted model, not a first-order exposure estimate.
Source bypass sees uncorrected Z2 information. Corrector internal loss sees
an imaginary component proportional to Z2. The coherent main-output repair
does not undo either of these already escaped fields. The main detection
efficiency eta enters N required for a specified assignment error but does
not by itself cause within-sector dephasing.

At fixed N, equations (2) and endpoint closure imply

 int B^2dt=[int(A')^2dt+ac^2N]/chi^2.                  (13)

Thus slowing the gate removes the derivative term but does not remove the
internal/bypass loss floor ac^2 N/chi^2. Higher main-output collection loss
can be tolerated; pre-correction and internal loss must actually be low.
This is a redistribution of the loss requirements, not universal immunity
to loss.

6. FINITE PULSE, REAL CONTROLS, AND PAID RESOURCES

The executable choice is A=A0 sin^4(pi t/T). Then

 N=(35/128) A0^2 T,
 int(A')^2dt=(16 pi^2/(7T^2)) N,
 N_B=N[ac^2+16pi^2/(7T^2)]/chi^2,
 E_source=N_B/ke,
 E_corrector=(N+N_B)/kc.                               (14)

Here E are integrated physical photon occupations, not arbitrary abstract
operation counts. The program also gives peak photon occupations, both peak
conditional-drive amplitudes, and squared-drive action integrals. These do
not convert to pump electrical energy without a hardware model.

Illustrative dimensionless case, NOT a hardware performance claim:
ke=1, kc=10, chi=5, eta=.5, target assignment .001.
At T=8, ideal outlets:

 N=4.774767853041619,
 N_B=4.842089381350990,
 peak |u|=1.2606135232608175,
 peak |w|=.05763839224380869,
 peak source photons=2.23612565058746,
 peak corrector photons=.4392533416448295,
 source photon exposure=4.84208938135099,
 corrector photon exposure=.961685723439261,
 calibrated local Z1 phase=-.03366076415468539.

For equal nonzero ratios kb/ke=ki/kc=1e-5, with the actual linewidths used in
(2), p_Z=.00009683432. For ratios 1e-4, p_Z=.0009676713; for ratios 1e-3,
p_Z=.009609740. Increasing T from 8 to16 only modestly lowers those floors.
The source and corrector drives remain bounded and vanish at both endpoints.

Preparation is not an oracle. The admitted initial state is vacuum and
thermal input is negligible; the physical apparatus must earn that through
cooling/reset and a measured residual occupation. The ideal operation returns
both modes to vacuum, so an ideal repeated cycle does not need an independent
long reset. If initial states are product thermal states with occupations
n_b,n_c, their vacuum probability is p0=1/[(1+n_b)(1+n_c)]; the half-diamond
distance from the vacuum-initialized instrument is at most 1-p0 by convexity.
For arbitrary initial oscillator states with total mean occupation E, the
conservative bound is at most sqrt(E), from the vacuum overlap and trace
contraction. These are initial-state bounds only. Thermal noise entering
throughout the gate must be included in its dynamics or separately bounded;
equal thermal marginals do not imply preserved logical coherence.

Physical local Z1 correction duration/error, phase tracking, readout electronics,
latency and any basis changes for other parity axes are additional resources.
No encoded lifetime, fault-tolerant state preparation, ideal feedforward, or
arbitrary coherent multi-qubit gate is inferred from equations (1)-(14).

7. CHECKS THAT WERE ACTUALLY RUN

exact_cascade_parity.py and exact_cascade_results.json contain 16 finite
parameter cases, all instrument matrices, physical resources, CP/TP checks,
and two independent ODE comparisons. At T=4, eta=.5, source/corrector loss
ratios zero or .001, separately integrating the amplitude ODE gives maximum
field error below2.7e-11 and final oscillator population below4.6e-28.

check_exact_cascade_fock.py implements the full two-mode Lindblad equation
with the cascade Hamiltonian and every outlet, independently of the coherent-
amplitude solution. It evolves all six off-diagonal pairs of the four-qubit-
basis instrument, plus a diagonal density matrix. A deliberately weak pulse
N=.02 keeps this check inexpensive and isolates algebra/sign mistakes.
For loss ratios0 and.02, maximum errors relative to the exact full-output
coherent overlaps fall with the retained levels per mode:

  cutoff3: 3.46e-6;
  cutoff4: 2.84e-8;
  cutoff5: 1.71e-10.

All dynamical phases, bypass/internal output overlaps and no-jump evolution
match the ordinary analytic derivation. This is a small-model numerical check,
not a rigorous high-power Fock tail bound, a physical validation of the
Hamiltonian, or independent external review.

8. PRESERVED ALTERNATIVES AND USEFUL FAILURE

Before the exact two-drive construction, the source was driven by Z1+Z2.
With photon counting and complete vacuum return, every count contributes
Z1[g1+g2S]; tracking a local Z1 per count gives a correct parity instrument.
But missed photons leave random local Z phases. For a bright mean N and
collection eta, miss probability is exp(-eta N), while within-sector
coherence is exp[-2(1-eta)N]. Strong readout then needs nearly complete
collection to avoid destroying logical coherence. This counting-only route
is ordinary environment-assisted correction, not a new Astra mechanism.

A passive dispersive corrector controlled by Z1 can instead act on that
field before detection. At chi=ks/2, its reflection amplitudes obey

 |(r_+(omega)+r_-(omega))/2|^2
       =4omega^4/(ks^4+4omega^4).

With a finite closed smooth emitted waveform w(t), the exact complete
within-sector nuisance energy V satisfies

 V <= (4/ks^4) int |w''(t)|^2dt.

This includes the entire finite pulse, residual modes and return, rather than
using a stationary tail alone. The initial executable implementation used an
8-sine pulse in the three-real-dimensional terminal nullspace, with all
ports and a full homodyne instrument. Its programs/data are retained in
cascade_operation.py and cascade_results.json. At T8, eta.5, dark/bright
assignment.001 it had within-bright phase-flip probability.000823936 even
with ideal upstream outlets; T16 reduced it to.000053335. It required about
19.1 photons because dark-versus-bright detection has less separation than
the final +/-A construction. It is not the selected operation.

The exact counterdrive removes that bandwidth error within its calibrated
model; it does NOT remove internal loss or errors in the actuator. A
counterdrive following a wrong or delayed qubit variable, or a bit flip while
an emitted wave is traveling, breaks the cancellation. A correct final parity
report cannot certify such a history. Root's gate/fault analysis must carry
those processes, not silently treat this isolated measurement as a fault-
tolerant architecture.

9. PRIMARY PRECEDENT AND SCOPE OF ANY ORIGINALITY CLAIM

Kerckhoff, Bouten, Silberfarb, Mabuchi, Physical model of continuous two-qubit
parity measurement in a cavity-QED network, PRA79,024305(2009),
https://arxiv.org/abs/0812.1246
A sequential optical-cavity scattering network with fixed homodyne realizes
parity measurement; QSDE analysis proves convergence to the ideal model in
a physical limit. Cascade/parity homodyne is not new.

Nielsen, Fighting decoherence in a continuous two-qubit odd or even parity
measurement with a closed-loop setup, PRA81,012307(2010),
https://arxiv.org/abs/0908.0817
Repeated coherent scattering reduces selected within-parity decoherence.
Coherent feedback before readout is not new.

Motzoi, Whaley, Sarovar, Continuous joint measurement and entanglement of
qubits in remote cavities, PRA92,032308(2015),
https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.04766
Derives full cascaded-cavity dynamics and uses shaped pulses to impose
entanglement-preserving conditions throughout the measurement. This is a
particularly strong conventional competitor to the inverse pulse design.

Govia et al., Scalable two- and four-qubit parity measurement with a threshold
photon counter, PRA92,022335(2015), https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03340
Intensity-only detection as a parity mechanism is established.

Gregoratti and Werner, On quantum error-correction by classical feedback in
discrete time, https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0403092
Environment-assisted correction criteria precede the counted-meter framing.

No full literature-first claim is established here. The strongest defensible
result is the explicit finite-amplitude all-output parity instrument, ordinary
proof and resource/failure law for the specified physical control graph.
