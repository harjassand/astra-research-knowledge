Physical model
--------------
Let b,c be two auxiliary oscillators, m the engineered lossy readout oscillator,
and S=Z1 Z2. The Hamiltonian and outlets are

 H=g[(b^dag+c^dag)m+m^dag(b+c)]+chi Z1 c^dag c
       +u(t)Z2(b+b^dag)+w(t)Z2(c+c^dag),
 Lm=sqrt(Gamma)m, Lb=sqrt(kb)b, Lc=sqrt(kc)c.

All modes and input baths initially are vacuum. The physical resources are two
conditional longitudinal drives associated with Z2, one dispersive Z1-c link,
two finite beam-splitter couplings g, and one engineered lossy mode. Intrinsic
b/c loss is included as kb,kc. Nonlinear/saturation corrections, thermal input,
data-qubit noise, and implementation errors require their own budget.

Exact finite-time inverse construction
--------------------------------------
Define a real smooth U(t) with derivatives through the required order zero at
both endpoints. Root uses U proportional to sin^6(pi t/T). Set

 A=sqrt(Gamma)/g [U'+kb U/2],
 V=-(A'+Gamma A/2)/(g sqrt(Gamma))-U,
 B=[-kc V/2+g A/sqrt(Gamma)-V']/chi,
 u=-B'-kb B/2,
 w=B'+kc B/2-chi V.

The exact branch amplitudes are

 b_z=U s+iB z2,
 c_z=V s-iB z2,
 m_z=iA s/sqrt(Gamma).

Substituting directly into the THREE finite coupled oscillator equations proves
these identities. The engineered main output is exactly iA(t)S at every time.
All three modes return to vacuum, and the conditional drives vanish at both
endpoints. Initial/final limits follow from the sin^6 envelope; no final gauge
state is silently discarded and no ringdown interval is silently omitted.

The associated quantum dilation is exact in the conditional-linear model:

 |z>|vac> -> exp(i z1 phi)|z>|vac_modes>
      |iA s>_main |sqrt(kb)(U s+iB z2)>_b
                       |sqrt(kc)(V s-iB z2)>_c,
 phi=-int(uU+wV)dt.

Thus the only deterministic dynamical phase is a local Z1 phase. Even and odd
parity subspaces are both protected against the entire engineered main outlet.
Detection inefficiency downstream only changes how well S is read; it does not
leak additional information inside a parity sector. The hidden b/c outlets
remain harmful and are explicitly retained.

Full instrument and all hidden-port phases
-----------------------------------------
Let N=int A^2, IU=int U^2, IV=int V^2, IB=int B^2,
CU=int UB, CV=int VB, K=kc CV-kb CU. Homodyne in the iA quadrature at efficiency
eta with a matched filter has assignment probability Phi(-2sqrt(eta N)).

After a local Z1 correction of angle phi-K, the Schur coefficient of the
reported parity q in computational basis indices i,j is exactly

 exp[-(N+kb IU+kc IV)(s_i-s_j)^2/2
      -(kb+kc)IB(z2_i-z2_j)^2/2]
 * exp{iK[(z1_i-z1_j)+s_i z2_j-s_j z2_i]}
 * Phi[q(s_i+s_j)sqrt(eta N)].

This is the complete finite-strength binned quantum instrument, including
cross-parity coherences, every no-click amplitude, hidden loss, and the final
apparatus. The main photon field is coherent because the entire admitted
network is conditionally passive and linear; no Gaussian truncation of a
nonlinear oscillator is being substituted.

Within EITHER fixed parity sector, the exact coherence factor after that local
correction is

 exp[-2(kb+kc)IB],
 p_Z=(1-exp[-2(kb+kc)IB])/2.

The integral IB is the actual resource relevant to dangerous hidden emission.
Total oscillator occupation also contains U^2,V^2, which carry only the intended
parity information. A total-loss probability bound can therefore overcharge
logical damage, but dropping the imaginary B components would undercharge it.

What this does and does not earn
-------------------------------
This is a complete noisy parity operation within one explicit finite model,
with real finite controls, all modes returned, finite-efficiency homodyne,
nonzero internal loss, and a physically local phase correction. Root composes
this actual instrument into a CNOT and assesses useful operating costs.

It is not error-transparent to arbitrary DATA errors during the pulse. A bit
flip changes the source/corrector history and can leave the old hidden waveform
incompatible with the present data state. Consequently a correct final parity
bit does not certify the whole gate. Root's stress tests and their limitations
must remain part of the final result.

It is also not automatically fault tolerant to drive errors, thermal photons,
extra uncontrolled dispersive links, correlated pump noise, or deviations from
linear harmonic dynamics. Conditional drives cannot be replaced by software
record subtraction. Finite mode couplings and an ordinary engineered loss port
replace the earlier ideal directional-network component, but they remain real
resources requiring an actual physical implementation.

Closest established machinery includes cascaded parity measurements,
entanglement-preserving pulse shaping, environment-assisted correction,
longitudinal readout and control inverse engineering. No historical novelty
or total architecture advantage has been established by these internal checks.
