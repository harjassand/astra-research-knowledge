OPTIONAL EXACT CORRECTION OF RESOLVED AUXILIARY-PORT PHOTONS
1 November 2026. Consequence of the finite reciprocal mediator construction.

This is an additional compatible operation, not an assumption that intrinsic
absorption is observable. It applies only to physical b/c output ports whose
photons can actually be collected and time resolved. Main port remains under
homodyne parity readout; arbitrary hidden baths are not reclassified as
accessible detectors.

On the exact conditional coherent trajectories,

 L_b -> sqrt(kb)[U S+i B Z2],
 L_c -> sqrt(kc)[V S-i B Z2].

For real U,V,B and S=Z1Z2, set rb=sqrt(U^2+B^2), rc=sqrt(V^2+B^2),
 theta_b=atan2(B,U), theta_c=atan2(B,V). Then

 U S+i B Z2 = rb S exp(+i theta_b Z1),
 V S-i B Z2 = rc S exp(-i theta_c Z1).

Both normalized jump operations are PRODUCTS OF LOCAL UNITARIES: S is a
product of two local Pauli Zs, and the remaining exponential acts on qubit1
only. Their rates are kb rb^2 and kc rc^2, independent of the logical input
on the entire four-dimensional data space. No-click probabilities therefore
carry no logical information in this counting unraveling.

After a b-port count at the known time t, apply or retain the inverse frame
exp[-i theta_b(t) Z1]S; after a c-port count, apply
exp[+i theta_c(t) Z1]S. All of these diagonal operations commute with the
physical parity-measurement Hamiltonian, so they can be accumulated and
applied at the end; zero-latency feedback is not required. A zero-intensity
point has no count and needs no defined angle.

For known counting efficiencies eta_b,eta_c, divide each outlet into a
monitored fraction and an unobserved fraction. Apply the resolved corrections
and retain the actual main homodyne report. The exact within-sector error law
becomes

 coherence=exp[-2D_unseen],

The remaining deterministic hidden-output phase is computed with

 K_unseen=(1-eta_c)kc int VB -(1-eta_b)kb int UB,

in addition to the recorded jump frames. Main assignment probability remains
Phi[-2sqrt(eta_main N)]. This is a complete simultaneous homodyne-and-counting
instrument, because independent output ports are measured separately and
every factor is diagonal and explicitly known.

Costs and limitations
---------------------
Expected detected counts are eta_b kb int(U^2+B^2) and
eta_c kc int(V^2+B^2). The detectors must resolve the port and photon timing
well enough to know theta(t); a threshold detector that merges several counts
can lose the S-byproduct parity. Detector dead time, dark counts, frequency
conversion, and time uncertainty create real new errors. For a timing error,
the residual local rotation is exactly the difference between the true and
used theta; its weighted angle-error distribution must be charged.

This correction does not repair an unobserved material-absorption event,
thermal entry, an auxiliary Hamiltonian fault, or a DATA X/Y error during the
pulse. It relies on the actual conditional coherent trajectory being the one
used to calculate U,V,B. Unknown changing control errors require calibration
or a robust bound; one cannot simply reuse ideal jump angles.

Environment-assisted correction and random-unitary unravelings are established
machinery (Gregoratti/Werner). The contribution here is the exact compatible
local correction and no-click calculation for the final finite mediator,
showing precisely which additional monitoring resource can reduce its earned
hidden-port floor.
