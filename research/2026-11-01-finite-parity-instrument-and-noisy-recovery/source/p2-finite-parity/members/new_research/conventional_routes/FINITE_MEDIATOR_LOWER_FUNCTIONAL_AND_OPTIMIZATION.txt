EXACT FINITE-MEDIATOR EXPOSURE LAW AND THE INFORMED CONTROL COMPARISON
1 November 2026. Root owns the apparatus construction. This memo derives
its unavoidable intrinsic-loss exposure and optimizes its physical pulses.

1. Declared model and the scope of the result

The actual three oscillator modes are b,c,m. The Hamiltonian is

 H = g[(b^dag+c^dag)m + m^dag(b+c)] + chi Z1 c^dag c
     + u(t) Z2(b+b^dag) + w(t) Z2(c+c^dag).

The vacuum outlets are sqrt(Gamma)m, sqrt(kb)b, sqrt(kc)c. Parameters g,chi
are nonzero and time independent. The physical forces u,w are real. Data
Z1,Z2 are conserved throughout this derivation. Modes start in vacuum and
must return to vacuum at T. No eliminated mediator or chiral channel is
assumed. Write P=Z1 Z2, h=Gamma/2, ab=kb/2, ac=kc/2.

Root's exact all-time parity-output family is

 b = U P + i B Z2,       c = V P - i B Z2,
 m = i A P/sqrt(Gamma),

with real amplitudes and

 A = sqrt(Gamma)(U'+ab U)/g,
 V = -(A'+h A)/(g sqrt(Gamma))-U,
 B = [g A/sqrt(Gamma)-V'-ac V]/chi,
 u = -B'-ab B,          w = B'+ac B-chi V.            (1)

A smooth compact U with sufficiently many zero endpoint derivatives
constructs the full experiment. With U=sin^6(pi t/T) times any finite cosine
polynomial, U through U^(5) vanish at the endpoints. A,V,B and both drives
therefore start and end at zero. This also handles known kb,kc exactly.

Within the stated Hamiltonian and real-force class, (1) is necessary for
an exactly parity-only main outlet, not just a lucky ansatz. Branch fields
lie in the span of P and Z2 because the drives create Z2 and dispersion
multiplies c by Z1. Requiring no Z2 component of m at all times forces the
Z2 components of b,c to be opposite. The b equation forces its Z2 component
to be purely imaginary; the c equation then forces its P component to be
real and the m equation its P component to be imaginary. Their real
coefficients obey exactly (1). A broader actuator class, non-vacuum input,
an approximate output condition, or a different covariance changes this
necessity statement.

2. Exact intrinsic-loss functional

Inside a fixed P sector, the b,c fields for the two different Z2 values
differ by +2iB and -2iB. The inaccessible independent vacuum outputs thus
give, after allowed deterministic local Z1 phase correction,

 V_sector = exp[-2(kb+kc) I_B],       I_B=int_0^T B(t)^2 dt,
 p_Z = [1-exp(-2(kb+kc)I_B)]/2.                      (2)

The monitored main field is +/-iA. Define N=int A^2. A matched homodyne
receiver of efficiency eta has the exact equal-prior assignment

 q = Phi(-2 sqrt(eta N)),       N=[Phi^(-1)(q)]^2/(4 eta).       (3)

Unobserved main-port photons carry parity only in this model. Their loss
does not directly distinguish states inside a parity sector. For fixed q,
however, smaller eta raises N and therefore the intrinsic loss burden.

For arbitrary known intrinsic rates, eliminate A,V from (1). If D=d/dt,

 B = p(D)U/(chi g^2),
 p(D)=D^3+p2 D^2+p1 D+p0,
 p2=h+ab+ac,
 p1=h(ab+ac)+ab ac+2g^2,
 p0=h ab ac+g^2(ab+ac).

Because U,U',U'' vanish at both endpoints, integration by parts gives

 I_B = {||U'''||^2+(p2^2-2p1)||U''||^2
          +(p1^2-2p2p0)||U'||^2+p0^2||U||^2}/(chi^2 g^4),
 N = (Gamma/g^2)[||U'||^2+ab^2||U||^2].              (4)

The coefficients of derivative norms need not all be positive. Equation
(4) is nevertheless nonnegative because it is exactly ||p(D)U||^2.
Using the zero extension of U and Parseval gives the rigorous bound

 I_B/N >= inf_{omega in R}
 [(p0-p2 omega^2)^2+omega^2(p1-omega^2)^2]
 /[chi^2 g^2 Gamma(omega^2+ab^2)].                   (5)

At zero denominator use the appropriate limiting value; if p0 is nonzero
and ab=0 the ratio diverges at omega=0. This is a lower bound for the exact
parity-output realization, not for all approximate instruments or all
quantum hardware. Pulse duration, reset and force caps usually make it
strict. The spectral minimizer can require indefinitely long waveforms.

3. Particularly transparent equal-loss form

For ab=ac=a, one factor cancels algebraically and

 B=[A''+(h+a)A'+(ha+2g^2)A]/(chi g sqrt(Gamma)).       (6)

Put C=h^2+a^2-4g^2 and K=ha+2g^2. With A,A' zero at endpoints,

 I_B/N = [||A''||^2+C||A'||^2+K^2||A||^2]
          /[chi^2 g^2 Gamma ||A||^2].               (7)

The infinite-duration spectral lower bound is

 I_B/N >= [K^2-min(C,0)^2/4]/(chi^2 g^2 Gamma).       (8)

For C<0 the minimizing angular frequency is sqrt(-C/2); for C>=0 it is
zero. Thus increasing smoothness or making a pointer slow is not universally
the optimum: strong coherent g can favor a finite-frequency bipolar pulse.
The reset constraint also requires int_0^T exp(a t) A(t) dt=0. It follows
from U(T)=U(0)=0 and cannot be dropped when optimizing A directly.

For C>=0 a simple stronger finite-time bound follows from the clamped-beam
and Dirichlet inequalities for A=A'=0 at the endpoints:

 I_B/N >= [beta1^4/T^4 + C pi^2/T^2 + K^2]
          /(chi^2 g^2 Gamma),                      (9)

where beta1=4.730040744862704 is the first positive nonzero solution of
cos(beta) cosh(beta)=1. This bound is valid without exploiting the weighted
zero-area constraint. The separate derivative inequalities need not be
simultaneously saturated, so (9) is a sufficient lower bound only.

At a=0, zero area strengthens the exact variational derivative
bound to ||A''||^2/||A||^2 >= beta2^4/T^4, with beta2=7.853204624095838.
For completeness: on the centered interval, split A into odd and even
parts. The odd clamped eigenproblem gives tan(beta/2)=tanh(beta/2), whose
first nonzero admissible root is beta2. The mean-zero even eigenproblem
has a Lagrange multiplier constant; its characteristic equation is
coth(beta/2)+cot(beta/2)=4/beta, whose first admissible root is approximately
10.53531506 and exceeds beta2. There is no even root for 0<z=beta/2<pi:
the partial-fraction expansion gives
 coth(z)+cot(z)-2/z=4 z^3 sum_{n>=1}1/(z^4-pi^4 n^4)<0.
For pi<z<=beta2/2<3pi/2, cot(z)>0 and coth(z)>1>2/z, so there is also no
even root below the first odd one.
Orthogonality of even and odd parts proves the bound. The minimizing
clamped function need not satisfy the extra endpoint derivatives required
for smoothly switched finite-force realization; smooth approximants can
approach it when force caps are absent.

The actual nonzero-a weighted reset constraint can also be handled rigorously.
Let f(t)=exp[a(t-T/2)]A(t). Then f=f'=0 at endpoints and int f=0. Exactly,

 ||(D-a)^2 f||^2=||f''||^2+2a^2||f'||^2+a^4||f||^2.

Bounding the exponential weight above and below and applying the preceding
clamped mean-zero inequality gives

 ||A''||^2/||A||^2 >= exp(-2aT)
   [(beta2/T)^4+2a^2(pi/T)^2+a^4].                   (10)

For C>=0, combining (10), the Dirichlet inequality for A', and (7) gives

 I_B/N >= {exp(-2aT)[(beta2/T)^4+2a^2(pi/T)^2+a^4]
             +C(pi/T)^2+K^2}/(chi^2 g^2 Gamma).     (11)

Unlike the a=0 exact variational derivative bound, (11) is generally not
sharp because it relaxes exponential weights and separate derivative norms.
It is a rigorous finite-a, finite-T lower bound under the stated endpoint
and exact-output conditions. It does not charge the force caps and therefore
also lower-bounds the force-constrained problem.

4. The strongest tested ordinary pulse for the SAME apparatus

Use g=1, Gamma=4, chi=2, T=8, kb=kc=1e-4, eta=.5, q=.001 and force limits
|u|,|w|<=3. These are dimensionless illustrative values, not measured joint
hardware parameters. The required N is 4.774767853041621. The exact spectral
floor (8) is .250025000625. The stronger finite-T floor (11) is
.308015959370967, corresponding to p_Z>=.000294054438276. The original root sin^6 primitive has I_B/N
.454999022068781.

An ordinary informed controller is allowed to use the entire construction.
The code parameterizes U=sin^6(pi t/T) sum_{j=0}^n c_j cos(j pi t/T), enforces
the fixed N, and minimizes I_B with sampled force inequalities. It uses
several starts, increases n, refines actual local drive maxima, reserves a
1e-4 force margin, and checks a dense independent grid. The four data
branches are then solved independently as full finite-mode ODEs.

The objective ratios at n=6,14,22,30 are respectively .3573948234,
.3328957512, .3254342298, .3216755211. Multiple starts agree within numerical
tolerance at each tested size. The n=30 result is a feasible improvement
of approximately 29.30% in I_B, not a certified global optimum. Its I_B/N
is within 4.44% of the rigorous relaxed finite-T floor (11). Its smaller
logical-information exposure comes with greater total internal photon
exposure and greater corrector force action; it is not a total-resource

unconstrained value within each finite basis is also saved as a diagnostic;

Full ODE validation of the n=30 pulse gives maximum branch-amplitude error
6.25e-12 and maximum terminal total population 6.40e-26. The exact complete
coherent-state instrument remains determined by (1)-(3), including all
outlets; these ODE numbers verify implementation of the declared linear
model, not nonlinear circuit hardware or unknown data-error histories.

The finite cosine family includes angular frequencies through
(n+6)pi/T=14.14g. This is a complexity/drive-bandwidth obligation. No extra
slew, filter or maximum photon constraint was imposed in this optimization.
Mode populations and integrated exposures/actions are saved in the JSON
so that those constraints can be added without borrowing an ideal actuator.
Full control optimization allowing a small main-port nuisance and continuous
record feedback could find further tradeoffs; the exact-emission family is
not declared optimal for total noisy computation cost.
