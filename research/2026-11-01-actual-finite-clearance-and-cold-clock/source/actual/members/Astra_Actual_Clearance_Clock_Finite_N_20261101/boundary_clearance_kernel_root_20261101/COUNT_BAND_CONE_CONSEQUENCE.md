Status: conditional deterministic receiving consequence of ACTUAL_NARROW_COUNT_BAND.md, whose independent review is pending. No new stochastic event is assumed.

 |S-N| N/E² <=12/e0².

In particular E>=2T³ yields |S-N|N<=3E². This is substantially sharper near the mesoscopic entrance than |S-N|<=.01E, which throws away an entire scale.

since12/20²=.03<.04=1/25. If R=E²/(NL)<=2, this also gives |S-N|<=2L/25. The actual acquisition cost is the probability charge of the ORIGINAL count event, not measurement or preparation of a new total population.
