## Surviving operation

The substantive result is a constructive ideal forward-source recovery
interface for ONE unknown memoryless CPTP N:k->D, with separate actual
approximate-correctability and effective-mean-rank parameters. If some
mathematical recovery has Bell fidelity>=1-kappa and A=N(I/k) has top-d0
tail tau, then the completed operation has averaged half-diamond error

 epsilon+k sqrt(4kappa+6tau).

It does not need numerical kappa,tau, the source's matrix, an adjoint,
purification, support basis or exact reversibility as algorithm inputs.
The bound can be vacuous. Supplied d0 is a complexity/resolution index,
not an acquired recovery oracle. B's separately scoped pre-payload
qualification can test a resolution with an explicit yield/false-accept
contract; it is not asserted to certify every observed record or remove
all acquisition costs.

The basic route jointly stores/compresses actual filtered training outputs
and the payload, learns the small ACTUAL channel, and uses ordinary Petz
reversal with an approximate competitor. Its noise is not compared with
an ideal encoder independently on every training carrier. The direct
Bell-target filter lemma prevents a fourth-root loss in physical noise.

The stronger admitted ordinary route uses random dilation to acquire a
finite quantum PROGRAM before later payloads. Its common-projector copies
implement consistent controlled forward/inverse Stinespring calls;
finite copies of the actual mean supply a conditioned inverse signal.
Petz is invariant under the programme's hidden environment frame. A
predetermined finite T-payload stream has the explicit bound(O8), with
the programme consumed and all source/control/storage costs growing with T.

The change from N1291 is real but scoped: exact mixed-isometry structure
is replaced by actual approximate correctability plus effective rank;
the strongest ordinary programme also removes the need for the late
payload to join the training set's Schur frame. It does not provide an
unlimited reusable classical decoder or intrinsically bounded storage.

Physical noise belongs to the actual N and does not incur Q*kappa by an
invalid ideal-source substitution. Chosen implementation precision DOES

hybrid. All protected quantum memory, references, source copies, masks,
controlled swaps, finite rotations and resets are substantial assumed
computational controls, not acquired laboratory access or a noisy-gate
fault-tolerance theorem.

## Failed assumption and next capability gate

C_FIRST_MECHANISM's frame-memory lower bound is preserved but retired
as the pair's destination: it forbids intrinsic-only programme storage,
which N1291 never grants, and its one-query address decoder already
achieves the meaningful paid logarithmic-ambient scale. B's first generic
PGM mechanism was likewise absorbed by strong existing discrimination
and Petz priors. Neither is promoted as a discovery.

The surviving theory changes what is constructively possible in the
specified ideal source model, while civilization-scale leverage is
UNESTABLISHED. The next material gate is a low-full-cost implementation
or stronger usable physical source structure reducing coherent programme
and inverse-accuracy overhead. More fixtures, digits or a larger nominal
ambient Hilbert space would not pay that gate. No new physical experiment
or supplier robustness is inferred from this handoff.
