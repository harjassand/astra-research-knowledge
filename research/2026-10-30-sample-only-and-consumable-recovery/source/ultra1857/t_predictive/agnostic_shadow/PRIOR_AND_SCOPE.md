## Strongest consumed operations

Liu--Moitra, arXiv2411.07536v1, Theorem1.3 and §§3--7, is the constructive
same-target low-probability-rank/conditional-query predecessor. Its empirical
probability trees, density-mixture sketches, barycentric geometry, robust
truncation and KL convex inference are established machinery. The accepted
misspecified-teacher compiler and sample-only/unknown-KL compositions are the
immediate reusable inputs. Their reviewed source bytes, complete algebraic
assumptions and finite resource qualifications are retained in their own frozen
folders. The new argument is not a new spanner, KL identity or model-selection
principle.

Kakade--Krishnamurthy--Mahajan--Zhang, COLT2023/PMLR195, arXiv2302.14753, defines
the same-target conditional access and exact predictive-rank class. Its sample
theorem uses the fidelity resource; exact-value learning has a different access
contract. I checked its abstract, definitions and controlling theorem framing
from the official proceedings PDF. It is a real constructive ordinary comparator,
not a source for relabelling arbitrary Q as conditional P. No broad claim that
all agnostic or robust low-rank learning is new is made.

The current extension permits a hidden nonnegative low-rank row family at EACH
cut under ACTUAL P-prefix sampling. These families enter only the existence
and sample-compression/geometry proof. The full KL propagation always uses P
and the teacher's joint restrictions. This changes the exact-truth promise;
it is not a numerical budget/exponent improvement. Classical linear widths,
approximate rank and oracle inequalities are established mathematical notions;
historical priority of this particular conditional-teacher operation is UNKNOWN.

## Stronger prior discovered during the new access screen

Tomer Adar, *Tight simulation of a distribution using conditional samples*,
arXiv2506.18444v1, official HTML https://arxiv.org/html/2506.18444v1 . I read its
conditional model (Definition2.22), simulation contract (Definition2.25),
introductory guarantees, repeated KL chain rule and Theorem5.4/Corollary5.5.
It is a strong existing lazy distribution-simulation operation with O(n^2/e^2)
prefix samples per query/sample and an expected KL guarantee.

The direction is important: the explicit simulation contract is

    E D(Q_sim || Q) <=delta,

with expectation over the acquired random distribution. This is not the forward
D(P||R) needed in the current compiler when P is a different source. Its
conditional model also permits arbitrary behavior on zero-mass query sets
(output must merely remain in the queried set); the present input promises
only queries at positive-Q prefixes. Our accepted wrapper avoids such calls.
Thus this primary theorem is not silently inserted as a direct forward-KL
misspecification transfer.

Its supported empirical tree also exposes an ordinary simpler architectural
alternative to local flooring: raw empirical rows can give zero prefix mass
beyond an unobserved edge, stopping before querying its descendants; a SINGLE
global uniform mixture can then supply every conditional via its prefix posterior.
Charging omitted paths could yield the same required transfer. This is a prior/
architecture observation, not a new qualified replacement program or a reason to
rewrite the frozen valid wrapper. It reinforces that generic probability
simulation is established. No sample-budget or hardware advantage is claimed here.

## Why the current opening was chosen over an unqualified logit transfer

Golowich--Liu--Shetty, arXiv2512.09892v1, exact Definitions1.3--1.6/4.2--4.4,
Theorem5.14, Remarks5.8--5.9 and Problem6.2 were read live from the official
versioned primary page. Its logit matrices concatenate a history and a future
and evaluate a NEXT-token mean-centered log probability. That is different from
probability predictive rank of the full future law. The sample-to-logit remark
pays exponential logit-range cost, while high-temperature sampling is a stronger
service. Its approximation notion also has bounded-factor and specified
cross-marginal weighting assumptions. None is replaced by our arbitrary-source
conditional width without a proof. This read does not establish the global
October2026 status of its open question.

Root's explicit logit/probability-rank separation remains a valid exact boundary;
its very rare last bit does not alone prove a relevant approximate-rank separation.
The new width theorem does NOT claim low logit rank implies small width. Direct
censored-logit/Fisher geometry could be a later mechanism, but no basis-logit
oracle, exponential relative precision or high-temperature access is granted.
The chosen immediate construction instead removes an already load-bearing exact
model assumption using the accepted conditional-teacher machinery.

## Actual consequence and limits

The new proposed theorem applies to EVERY source P with a supplied model budget r;
performance depends on a cutwise nonnegative predictive approximation width, or
as a corollary distance to the best global rank-r law. It requires neither an
attaining low-rank model nor its samples, evaluation or approximation error as
inputs. The finite-domain compactness argument pays existence. Null shadow
prefixes are covered explicitly instead of assuming support inclusion.

The algorithm itself is unchanged from the frozen sample-only/grid compiler.
No fresh empirical execution is required to establish this analytical domain
extension. Whole internal challenge of the NEW proof is still pending. The
earlier adapter was AST/read-only qualified, not executed at formal huge budgets.
All prior successful/failing calculations and ordinary-trie/EM wins remain.

Tiny Holder tolerance, enormous polynomial resource degrees, actual source/
conditional-reset service, practical forecasting and historical novelty remain
unacquired. The guarantee is predictive under P's actual trajectory law. It does
not provide causal/interventional transfer to unsupported policies or arbitrary
rare histories. No physical world-model device, general scientific intelligence
or all-incumbent advantage follows from this statistical principle alone.
