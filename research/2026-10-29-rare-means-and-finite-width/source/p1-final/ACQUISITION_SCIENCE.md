# Acquired information for rare kinetic consequences

29 October 2026, Brisbane. Ordinary proofs, synthetic executions, useful
failures and scoped independent reviews. This continues the response,
eliminated-memory and mean-lifetime work without promoting its observation
counts into precision, its frozen-fibre data into dynamics, or its completed
fixtures into the scientific destination.

## The question selected after reopening the opportunity

A major success would let a scientist acquire a reusable record that
certifies consequential behaviour on timescales far beyond the observation
window. The difficult object is the information needed for the consequence:
an accurate stationary law need not determine the kinetic clock or the
return dynamics, and an accurate absolute survival curve need not control
a mean. This continuation asks which parts of that information really must
be acquired, how their cost depends on the later questions, and when a
different requested consequence removes a major computational obligation.

The materially different opening was a compact certificate for quantum
devices under energy, entropy and preparation-contrast budgets. Accepted
Astra N675 remained a foundation, without routine re-audit. A new resource
boundary construction shows that exact complete Umegaki order remains
coRE-complete under all those small positive budgets simultaneously. The
strict violation can be localized through its BKM Hessian and diluted into
a rare flag. The violation's magnitude shrinks, so this does not transfer
fixed-gap hardness. Existing fixed-resolution continuity results remain
available; composition-stable positive certificates are still missing.
The quantum proof and a small exact control are preserved in
`branches/opportunity_20261029/alternative/QUANTUM_RESOURCE_BOUNDARY.md`.

The classical opening earned the deeper attack because it supplied both
a positive acquisition construction and a matching information obstruction.
It then produced a more useful reformulation: **certify how local changes
to a rare target change its mean waiting time, without computing a global
partition function.** This is a consequence-specific capability; it does
not require that the first result be a universal new theory.

## Latest extension: strict contraction is no longer necessary for this consequence

After the acquired contrast construction closed, a further attack replaced
its uniform positive full-gap condition. For a known bounded-degree Ising
graph, prepare any vertex ordering and let B_N be the largest sum of absolute
couplings across a prefix cut. Ordinary canonical paths give

\[
m_N\ge(q/N)e^{-4B_N}.
\]

Meanwhile the target basis vector has exponentially small spectral weight
far below its extensive local generator energy. A self-contained even-moment
argument, with safe integer-floor constants, bounds that tail by
\(\exp[-cN]\), where c>0 depends on fixed q and local support overlap.
The relative inverse-moment contribution of slow modes is bounded by

\[
\frac{N^2(1-q)}q\exp(4B_N-cN).
\]

Thus **B_N=o(N) suffices for a uniform 1+O(1/N) kinetic-multiplier
certificate**, even though the available full relaxation bound can grow
subexponentially. On a square lattice B_N=O(sqrt(N)) for bounded couplings.
The protocol therefore no longer requires Dobrushin contraction. Local
conditional tables can acquire q and compatible coupling bounds under the
same declared pairwise/local-clock source contract; the partition function
still cancels from within-source contrasts.

This is an extension of the acquired certificate using established
canonical-path, energy-concentration and Green machinery, not a new mixing
theorem. Its consequence is the mean of hitting an individual COMPLETE
configuration. It does not predict transitions between macroscopic phases,
prepare equilibrium, or learn unknown graph topology. Rare-hitting theory
already distinguishes relaxation from a much longer rare mean; the added
work makes the local acquisition and finite consequence bounds explicit.

The conservative finite arithmetic is severe. For an open square grid with
zero field, |J|<=1/2, q=1/64 and support overlap 13, a sufficient checked size
is L=2^28, N=2^56. Exact scalar arithmetic then gives a kinetic factor
F<=(1+127/N)/(1-1/N)^2. No lattice of that size was acquired, allocated or
simulated. This enormous sufficient threshold makes the result a structural
scope extension, not a practical low-temperature performance claim.

The tail proof required a useful source correction. The optimization in a
printed concentration theorem did not establish its displayed constant.
Two independent checks and a self-contained word-count proof supplied a
weaker justified finite bound. None of the earlier fourth-moment numerical
certificates uses the questioned constant. The older prior-art note is
preserved with this explicit qualification.

A final corollary changes later execution cost as well. If two targets
differ only at k sites, their exit rates can differ at no more than (d+1)k
terms. A prepared reference and sparse edit list therefore support a
kinetic-ratio query using only k local probabilities and O(kd) state-bit
accesses, with an explicit additional exit correction. Reading a new full
N-bit reference still costs N. On the paid 128-site record, 36 such queries
used 66 probability lookups and 162 logical spin accesses in total, with at
most 4 and 10 respectively per query; no global exit scans or normalizations
were performed. The uniform distance-four factor is below 1.164764, slightly
wider than the earlier global-scan consumer's 1.155839. Original acquisition
costs remain paid. This finite execution uses the earlier acquired gap
certificate, not an unperformed square-lattice acquisition.

The full theorem, separate gap and tail proofs, exact controls, sparse-query
code and scoped final composition review are in
`branches/opportunity_20261029/geometric_extension`.

## 1. A normalization-free kinetic contrast

For a finite reversible binary heat-bath source, define

\[
T_a=\mathbb E_{\pi(\cdot\mid a^c)}\tau_a,
\qquad \ell_a=\text{native total exit rate at }a,
\qquad \kappa_a=T_a\pi(a)\ell_a.
\]

The specified initial law is true equilibrium conditioned outside the
target. Physical preparation of that ensemble is not established. A ratio
compares those specified means for two targets in the same generator; it
does not describe an arbitrary initial state or an intervention changing
the generator.

Classical Green theory gives

\[
T_a=\frac{Z_a}{\pi(a)(1-\pi(a))},\qquad
Z_a=\int_{x>0}x^{-1}d\nu_a(x),
\]

where \(\nu_a\) is the target spectral measure of the symmetric full
generator, including its stationary zero atom before that atom is removed
from the inverse integral. The new construction retains \(\kappa_a\)
explicitly. It does not substitute \(1/\pi(a)\) for a mean.

Suppose a known degree-d graph really specifies all conditional
dependencies, the process really is positive reversible heat bath, and
calibrated site clocks have rate one. Local clamps and marked refresh
observations acquire all neighbour-context probabilities. A simultaneous
confidence table bounds both the conditional odds and every native exit
rate. Comparing neighbouring contexts gives a symmetric influence envelope;
if its row sum \(\bar c<1\), standard Dobrushin contraction supplies the
full gap \(m\ge1-\bar c\). Numerical weakness of the influences can be
acquired. Locality, the refresh law, controllability and the clock scale
remain separate warranties.

The local generator moment bounds give

\[
1-\rho\le\kappa_a\le\frac{A}{1-\rho},\qquad
\rho=(1-q)^N,
\]
\[
A=1+\frac{V}{t(Nq)^2}
  +\frac{M_4}{m(1-t)^4(Nq)^3},\quad 0<t<1,
\]

with acquired/supplied full conditional bound q, \(V\le N/4\), and
\(M_4\le3N^2/16+N(1+\bar c)^2/4+|E|/4\). For fixed bounds and degree,
\(F=A/(1-\rho)^2=1+O(1/N)\). The finite formula, not its asymptotic
replacement, is used in every certificate.

If a and b differ at k sites, multiply conditional odds along a k-flip
path to obtain \(\pi(b)/\pi(a)\). The partition function cancels exactly.
Then

\[
\frac{T_b}{T_a}
=\frac{\pi(a)}{\pi(b)}\frac{\ell_a}{\ell_b}
 \frac{\kappa_b}{\kappa_a},\qquad
F^{-1}\le\frac{\kappa_b}{\kappa_a}\le F.
\]

The same local confidence event covers every such pair, including targets
selected after reading the record. No union bound over exponentially many
configurations is needed. Outer odds intervals need not themselves define
a reversible generator: the proof follows the single true compatible
source, and never treats incompatible endpoints as a new law.

For requested interval factor \(e^\eta\), fixed q, and an explicit
finite-N kinetic condition \(2\log F\le\eta/2\), the ordinary acquisition
construction uses

\[
O_q\!\left(N2^d(k+1)^2\eta^{-2}
\log\frac{N2^d}{\delta}\right)
\]

scalar refresh outcomes. It uses at most \(N2^d\) clamp contexts and
O(d) neighbour assignments per context. The count is nearly linear in N
for fixed d,k and accuracy above the kinetic floor. It does not remove
that floor by increasing sample count, and full unstructured context
tables retain their exponential dependence on degree. Expected serial
refresh waiting, precision and arithmetic costs are explicit in the proof.
The design uses the total probability-error radius, including statistical,
instrument/argument and interval-rounding allowances. The nonstatistical
allowance must satisfy its own budget; additional outcomes do not supply
that precision. This distinction was corrected following independent
review, while the released exact intervals were already using full widths.

This theorem extends beyond graphs with an exact partition-function
algorithm. It changes the consequence, so it does not contradict the
cubic information cost established below for all-target absolute means.
Conventional conditional-odds estimation is the equilibrium ingredient;
the acquired return certificate is what makes its kinetic use justified.

## 2. Absolute predictions: completed acquisition, with full costs

Two distinct synthetic acquisitions establish the absolute consequence.

| Source | Actually generated data | Charged scalar outcomes | Uniform later-target guarantee |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 64 independent binary sites | Materialized and streamed refresh bits; retained site counts and stream hashes | 33,554,432 | All \(2^{64}\) targets, factor below 1.277266, failure at most 1/64 |
| 128-site unknown weak Ising chain | 512 aggregate Binomial counts, not a raw-bit stream | 274,877,906,944 | All \(2^{128}\) targets, factor below 1.329539427485578, false-certificate probability at most 1/1024 |

The product source has supplied \(p_i\in[31/64,33/64]\), independence and
unit clocks. Exact positive polynomial arithmetic computes its actual
Green factor in O(N^2) operations, with integer precision paid. The count
record gives a simultaneous logit error bound. An independent consumer
checked the native record and exact target controls without importing the
producer. The acquisition itself took about 0.8591 measured wall seconds
in simulation; expected serial native waiting is the charged outcome count
in the supplied clock units.

The interacting source has 128 unknown fields and 127 shared couplings,
with supplied \(e^{2|h_i|}\le65/64\) and \(1\le e^{2J_i}\le65/64\).
Each of 512 batches represents \(2^{29}\) central refresh outcomes.
There are 512 context preparations and 1016 actual neighbour assignments;
the endpoint physical contexts are independently repeated, not supplied
with imaginary neighbours. Neighbours remain clamped within each batch.
Expected serial waiting is \(2^{38}\) model time units, in addition to
unspecified setup duration and holding work. No equipment was operated.

Outward probability and logit intervals produce coherent shared parameter
intervals. A rational-weight candidate has canonical log-weight error at
most approximately 0.05389983120473915. An exact chain transfer matrix
normalizes it. Direct acquired context intervals separately bound native
exit rates; the local-moment theorem then supplies relative mean bounds.
Candidate fitting does not turn inconsistent rate endpoints into a law.

A conventional shared-edge conditional logistic fit used the same data.
Under the same conservative confidence box it gave a larger certified
error radius than the midpoint candidate, so the midpoint was adopted
before reading private diagnostics. This is not a superiority theorem for
a new estimator: conventional methods may adopt the same confidence
record and a stronger joint statistical analysis.

The aggregate producer took about 1.8408 wall seconds. That is not physical
assay time. The retained public JSON occupies 650,777 bytes, rather than
255 machine words. It is a frequentist false-release guarantee under the
declared ideal sampling contract, not posterior certainty for one file.
Independent reviews checked counts, endpoint intersections, all 255 shared
parameter intervals, normalization, the uniform factor and thirteen
consumers by different rational arithmetic routes. No true 128-site mean
was directly measured or solved by enumerating the source.

These predictions concern means of order \(10^{36}\) to \(10^{37}\) in
supplied units. They demonstrate inference without waiting for a hit,
conditional on substantial source structure and acquisition cost. They
do not establish general metastable prediction or a hardware speedup.

## 3. The information requirement depends on the later questions

For product sources and predeclared separated target families of size K,
an adaptive selected-site lower bound gives, in the stated large-N class,

\[
n\ge\frac{N^2}{32\eta^2}
\big[(1-\delta)\log K-\log2\big].
\]

Adaptive site choice, resets and clamps do not avoid this scalar-outcome
cost. A conventional two-source refinement also supplies the matching
log(1/delta) dependence. This is a worst-case result for constructed
separated families, not for every family with K elements. Clustered
questions can have cheaper information geometry.

A complementary upper construction uses established inverse-binomial
log-likelihood estimation, median-of-means, and a separately acquired
Green-factor record. It obtains O_q(N^2 eta^-2 log(K/delta)) outcomes
without the earlier weak-bias approximation floor. It has a deterministic
outcome cap, with overflow included in the failure budget; native elapsed
time remains random. Exact controls and an independent review passed,
but no large stopped-run acquisition was executed.

For the entire target cube the separated-family lower bound is cubic in
N. Matching lower-bound claims require fixed q<1/2; at q=1/2 the product
law is already known. The nearly linear local-contrast construction in
Section 1 makes the consequence distinction concrete. The old lower
bound is a cost under a declared interface and task, not a ceiling on
what scientific discovery can change.

## 4. Failures that changed the construction

**Passive observation cannot acquire an invisible clock for free.** A
four-state reversible family has identical equilibrium information,
nonzero eliminated memory and a fast complement, but different slow
kinetic clocks. Even full continuous trajectories and free adaptive resets
need observation time of order 1/epsilon to distinguish the required
responses. Potential or frozen-fibre observations do not supply that
kinetic information. A separate form counterexample shows why preserving
the same dual load matters when a mass form is approximated.

**The first moment consumer discarded useful information.** A nonlocal
parity source has a density within a fixed factor of the product law,
bounded flip rates and a constant full gap, yet its fourth centered
spectral moment scales as N^3. The simple split bound has a genuine
nonvanishing error floor. At N=1024, bias 3/5 and positive target parity,
its best executed factor was about 13.1288.

The conventional Gauss/Radau consumer of the SAME moments gives factor
about 1.000610266 instead. An explicit affine trial has a coherent
operator-residual cancellation, proving a 1+O(1/N) bound for that actual
consumer. A known symmetry further reduces the problem to N+1 Hamming
states and gives an exact birth-death current formula. That formula and
an independently derived resolvent/binomial formula agree exactly in all
16 large cases; 24 small full-generator residual certificates also pass.
This is a consumer failure repaired by representation, not an information
lower bound. The complete scalar parity law and clock are supplied, with
zero statistical assays. Its unknown-law acquisition is not claimed.

**Stationary odds times exit rates still need a return correction.** Two
independent sites with refresh probabilities 1/4 and 3/4 have exact means
T_00=184/39 and T_01=104/63. Their ratio is 169/483, whereas omitting the
return multiplier gives 2/3. Exact backward equations preserve this small
counterexample; the contrast theorem explicitly pays the correction.

## 5. What is new, and what is established machinery

The focused primary challenge found that the Green identity, its basic
lower bound, the short-return interpretation and the many-body
1+O(1/N) scaling follow from established results under these hypotheses.
There is no claim to have discovered a new universal rare-event law,
Gauss quadrature, local-Hamiltonian concentration, likelihood estimator,
transfer matrix or Dobrushin contraction.

The concrete addition is the complete composition of acquired local
probabilities, compatible uncertainty, explicit return control, and a
simultaneous consequence certificate, together with its full acquisition
and reuse costs. The normalization-free variant adds a precise change of
task that removes global normalization and has a different proved
information requirement. Conventional adoption is welcome and does not
erase those constructions; no exclusive performance advantage over the
best equally informed conventional route has been established.

Relevant primary antecedents are Aldous–Fill's continuous-time Green and
hitting-time exposition; Manzo–Quattropani–Scoppola's First Visit Time
Lemma; Anshu's product-state local-energy concentration; Golub–Meurant's
matrix moment and quadrature bounds; Wu's Dobrushin spectral-gap theorem;
and van Opheusden–Acerbi–Ma's inverse-binomial likelihood estimator. Full
URLs, exact overlaps and reading limits are in SOURCE_NOTES.md and
`branches/opportunity_20261029/alternative/LOCAL_MOMENT_PRIOR_ART_CHALLENGE.md`.

## 6. Remaining scientific obligation

The direct next attack on coarse events produced an exact counterexample.
Take a reversible three-state star with two target leaves of mass epsilon
each, exit rates 1/2 and 1, and a non-target centre of mass 1-2epsilon. Its
full gap is at least 1/2 independently of epsilon. Replacing the singleton
in the Green formula by the normalized target-set indicator overestimates
the actual mean by exactly 9/8, even as the target mass tends to zero. Six
exact controls down to epsilon=2^-128 verify the Poisson and killed
equations. The source is supplied; no acquisition was performed.

Thus extending the current point result requires more than a stronger
concentration estimate for a different vector. The mean for a set is a
functional of its actual killed generator or a faithful boundary/entrance
representation. The earlier coarse-composition, Schur-memory and certified
killed-mean constructions are directly relevant capital. The failure,
ordinary proof and exact code are retained as COARSE_TARGET_COUNTEREXAMPLE
files in geometric_extension. It is not a claim that coarse-event
prediction is impossible: the strong conventional killed solve is exact
in this small example.

A stronger four-state control stays inside the binary unit-site heat-bath
primitive. Give 00 mass 1-3epsilon and each other configuration mass epsilon;
let the target be first-spin=1. The full gap is at least 1/2, but the wrong
normalized-indicator substitution approaches a 10/9 mean overestimate as
epsilon tends to zero. Exact eigendirections, Poisson equations and the
correct two-by-two killed solve are verified. The epsilon limit changes
the minimum conditional probability, so it does not contradict the fixed-q,
extensive point-target theorem. It refutes the attempted exact identity
for coarse targets. See HEAT_BATH_COARSE_TARGET_FAILURE.md and its code.

The present route predicts consequences far beyond observed times by
paying for a source representation that rules out hidden dynamical
alternatives. It has not acquired arbitrary locality, a clock, a global
nonlocal symmetry or a metastable return mechanism from finite data.
Those are distinct obligations, not omitted implementation details.

The stronger unresolved destination is an experimentally warranted class
of return representations that can be acquired and composed when neither
bounded local dependence nor a supplied global symmetry is available.
The passive clock lower bound constrains the access needed; the parity
repair shows that a failed local bound need not imply missing information.
These results identify the obstruction more sharply, while leaving the
earlier response, lifetime, prepared-law and coarse-composition capital
intact at its original scope.
