Boundary that should remain explicit

True-fidelity preservation under the diagonal measurement does not imply recovery or complete statistical sufficiency. Full-rank common-filter qubit states can have identical quantum/classical fidelity while quantum trace distance strictly exceeds diagonal total variation. Distinct filtered pure states make the relative-entropy failure particularly clear.

For replica quantities, Ekert et al., Hastings et al., and Humeniuk–Roscilde supply established cycle, SWAP and thermal-replica machinery. With cheap amplitude evaluation, the direct permutation-ratio estimator already has second moment at most one and achieves ordinary additive Monte Carlo scaling.

The consequential claim here is the paid conditional-sample receiver, including its variance-decaying bridge and exact physical target. The NPT construction gives that receiver a useful quantum-information output, while the completed purity execution establishes the present tested scope.
