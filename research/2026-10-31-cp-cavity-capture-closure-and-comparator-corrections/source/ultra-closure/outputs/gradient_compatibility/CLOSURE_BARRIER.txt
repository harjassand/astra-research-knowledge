1. AN EXACT GROUP-INVARIANT QUOTIENT METRIC

For cubic coefficients c_alpha use the Bombieri tensor norm

               ||C||_B^2=sum_(|alpha|=3)
                           (prod alpha_i!/3!) c_alpha^2.

This is the squared Euclidean norm of the associated symmetric
three-tensor, hence is preserved by orthogonal changes in the four
matrix coordinates. Output rotations and Gamma are orthogonal and
preserve F. Therefore the four-dimensional subspace {DF.h} of
translation cubics is invariant under these transformations.

Take the orthogonal quotient by that translation subspace in this
metric. In the retained eight charge coordinates it has an explicit
rational positive definite Gram matrix M. Let z0 be C0's charge vector
and m0=z0^T M z0>0. Every symmetry image g C0 has the same charge norm.

The exact construction uses the literal twenty monomials, their weights
prod alpha_i!/3!, the translation matrix and the unit charge section.
It orthogonally projects the section off translations, constructs M,
and verifies

            L^T M+M L=0,       Gamma_q^T M Gamma_q=M,

where L is the infinitesimal continuous output-rotation action. These
identities prove invariance for the whole continuous group, not only
the finite eight generators used for the earlier sufficient bound.

2. COMPLETED ROC SLACK AND ITS VARIANCE IDENTITY

For p=F+C+Q and rank-one R define its completed ROC slack

        s_p(R)=2Q(R)-kappa_R(C,C),

where kappa_R is the positive semidefinite completed cubic cost form.
It includes the acoustic jet defect at a singular ray. This is the
actual global ROC inequality, not just the root pseudoinverse test.

An argument translation by h changes cubic and quadratic parts to

 C'=C+DF.h,
 Q'=Q+DC.h+(1/2)F''[h,h].

The translation and mixed kernel blocks give s_(p(.+h))(R)=s_p(R)
exactly. This remains true at completed acoustic atoms, because the
additional charge term annihilates translations.

For weights w_i>=0 with sum w_i=1, C=sum w_i C_i and Q=sum w_i Q_i,

 s_(F+C+Q)(R)
   =sum_i w_i s_(F+C_i+Q_i)(R)
          +sum_i w_i kappa_R(C_i-C,C_i-C).              (1)

The last sum is nonnegative. The receiver checks a nontrivial rational
instance of this exact matrix variance identity; its general proof is
ordinary expansion of a positive semidefinite quadratic form.

Here 4474=2(4096-1859); the affine and determinant gauges cancel.
Adding a QC quadratic contributes a nonnegative value on rank-one R.

3. THE SLACK SURVIVES ALL THE ALLOWED CLOSURES

Let z=sum_i w_i lambda_i g_i z0 be the total charge of a finite
generated polynomial. The invariant quotient norm and Cauchy--Schwarz

             z^T M z <=m0 sum_i w_i lambda_i^2.

           s_p(R)>=4474 |R|^2 (z^T M z)/m0             (3)

All continuous symmetry images, arbitrary argument translations,

4. IMPLICATION FOR THE FOUNDATIONAL ATTACK

The eight-charge QC theorem remains valid and useful. Its convex
closure cannot recover the 1859 comparator, so it cannot prove the
whole exposed ROC face by this route. An attainable-capacity theorem
would require additional actual-gradient information or genuinely new
QC generators or operations. A merely larger list of symmetry images,
a change of basis, or repeating the same closures cannot remove (3).

The outer compatibility matrix still does not provide an attainable
allocation. This barrier neither rules out an allocation for any
actual field nor supplies a compatible field with a negative energy.
Strict ordinary certificates and closed boundary certificates retain
their previous distinction.
