The strongest new result is a general noise-to-circuit theorem. Under its specified local-noise and scheduling assumptions, it controls correlated quantum errors without an estimate that grows exponentially with the entire machine. It has passed internal review; the full connection to established fault-tolerant computation is still being developed.

The ZIP also includes reviewed physical-record capture/storage and a broader chemical stability construction, with their restrictions and failed approaches preserved. This is not yet an autonomous computer or a demonstrated practical advantage. Research continues.
