# True-face stopping overrides pooled z positivity

30 October 2026. Explicit stopping completion, following the independent local review.

An a+D birth at D=1 enters F={D=0,CE=0} in TWO cases:

1. E=0, so z=E+D-1=0 before the jump (C arbitrary).
2. C=0 and E>0, so z=E>0 before the jump.

The actual local stage stops at this jump in BOTH cases. A positive pooled z does not authorize continuing across the E-axis true dormant face. The event may be represented as its actual pooled z->a output when z>0, but then the process is stopped immediately and its face label F_E retained.

All references in the pooled-label construction to an 'extra z->a event when z>=1' are subject to this terminal-state check. The independent forcing comparison is applied only before the actual stage stop; an early F_E exit only shortens its horizon. The terminal reward/return compensators already used the exact condition D=1 and CE=0, so their estimates and the two-face labeled transfer are unchanged.
