# Coupled pooled-label and active-domain conventions

30 October 2026. Completion to the two new coupled-face files; prior reviewed sources unchanged.

## Pooled labels are abstract

Labels in the forcing proof describe (a,C,z), not persistent physical E or D identities. With z=E+D-1, set R=mu E+rho(D-1)>=c z. Give each z label an independent baseline clock of rate c. At a baseline event choose the physical E-consuming or D-consuming channel with probabilities mu E/R and rho(D-1)/R. The aggregate baseline contribution to each physical channel is <= its actual rate. Realize each nonnegative remainder as an adapted extra event, assigning any z label. The a+D birth is another extra z->a event when z>=1 and is the actual true-F stop when z=0.

A single protected pool particle can be reassigned before the physical update; no observable state changes during relabeling. This constructs the exact (a,C,E,D) generator while retaining independent per-label BASELINE Poisson clocks. It does not construct independent E/D particle chains. Extra pooled z->a moves cannot invalidate the forcing event when no a-departure clock rings.

The a and C pooled labels retain their identities through their baseline moves. After a seed removes a cohort label, its fictitious continuation can use its same baseline clocks with no extra moves. The difference between the actual surviving cohort and this fictitious reference cohort is bounded by twice the seed count.

## Physical active-domain gate

Every local M estimate is for nonnegative residual a, i.e. physical A>=a0, and n>=2. In a global physical state, the phrase 'active M input' means

    A>=a0, n>=2, and [D>0 or CE>0].

States with A<a0 have zero common fast factor even if D>0 or CE>0; they are inactive designated approaches, NOT local M inputs. At D=0,CE=0 the true dormant kernel includes all physical A>=0, with fast seed/clear enabled only when A>=m. Small n exceptions are handled in the finite connector head. The top-level set notation in COUPLED_TWO_FACE_SIGNED_TRANSFER is shorthand subject to this active gate, which its activity section already uses.

Inactive approaches are still bounded: if A>=1, activation raises A by m-1 and enables activity; if A=0, the first immigration is compulsory. A prescribed sequence of at most m immigrations enables a fast source regardless of hidden C,E,D. The actual output and initial-phase-left flag remain attached to every such approach.
