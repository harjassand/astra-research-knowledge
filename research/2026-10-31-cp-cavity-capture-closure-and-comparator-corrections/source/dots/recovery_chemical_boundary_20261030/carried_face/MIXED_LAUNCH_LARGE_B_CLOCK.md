# Initial mixed launch has a sufficient B-clock without an n-clock

Separate completion of the complementary Z trial in the carried construction.

There are finitely many positive restoring rate constants. On every mixed D=0 hidden state, at least one binary restoring source is enabled. Its falling factorial is a positive integer, so the aggregate operational restoration hazard h is bounded BELOW by a fixed h_min>0. No upper bound, or lower bound proportional to n, is required.

Inside the physical gate A>=a0 and at B=b>=q, the physical restoring rate is

    h (A)_a0 (b)_q >= h_min (A)_a0 (b)_q.

The actual competing activation and immigration rates are tAb and i. For b above a fixed head and every A>=a0, since a0>=2,

    tAb / [h(A)_a0(b)_q] <= C b^{-(q-1)},
    i / [h(A)_a0(b)_q] <= C b^{-q}.

The first mixed holding time is at most C b^-q in mean. Additional seed/clearing clocks only shorten it. Thus, before the first restoring/seed/clearing event, the probability of an unwanted activation is O(b^{-(q-1)}) and of immigration O(b^-q), uniformly in hidden mass AND A. A restoring or seed output enters the positive-D row at the actual unchanged b. A clearing output is passed to the existing actual clearing/inactive-output continuation, not asserted automatically to be a strict Z downstep.

This supplies a direct replacement for the initial mixed-clock premise in the Z trial: the accepted positive-D trial begins after the restoring/seed output, while the ordinary clearing branch follows its accepted clearing continuation. Actual activation errors are of exactly the already allowed b^{-(q-1)} scale. Pure true-F starts use the original dormant trial unchanged. Initial A<a0 is handled by the existing inactive approach, because the common restoring factor is then zero; this estimate is never applied outside its gate. B below the fixed head is not a large-Z estimate.

Consequently the proposed carried extension needs no claim that arbitrary killing itself improves a signed event probability. The B-clock comparison prices the actual additional launch branch and errors. The obstruction E tau>=1/251 at fixed b=5 is entirely compatible with this large-b estimate; it is an n-clock obstruction, not a B-clock obstruction.
