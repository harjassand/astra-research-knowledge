# Precision on phase factors in the connector-return completion

In Section1 of CONNECTOR_MEDIATED_LAUNCH_RETURN_COMPLETION, the phrase suggesting a neutral r change at every fixed input phase is too strong. The first connector is r-neutral from the original section input B=q. At arbitrary phases an immigration wrap can change r. What the estimate uses, and what is true, is that each connector's r change belongs to a fixed finite set independent of A,B,n. Hence its W multiplier is bounded by a fixed factor depending only on theta,lambda,m,q. The small epsilon_n estimate survives that factor. No general-phase neutrality is needed or asserted here.

For the regular leading contraction, a high ordinary clearing followed by a leading low-phase approach lowers B by at least one UNLESS it produces a designated section terminal. In particular B=q clearing gives B=0; the compulsory immigration and activation return to B=q and terminate the actual initial-phase-left cycle, rather than form a neutral-B continuation loop. Low-phase starting inputs contribute a bounded one-time approach factor and are not repeated unpriced leading blocks. The accepted phase flag convention is retained throughout.
