# Edge-energy phase weights for the spectral flag expansion
30 October 2026. Root completion/receiving specialization, not a complete capture theorem.

The flag identity itself needs no particular form of F_j. In ROOT_SPECTRAL_FLAG_EXPANSION.md, exp(s h_j) is one convenient atomic-majority example. For the strict external edge-energy native payload, use the ORIGINAL weighted disagreement energy U_j instead:

 F_j=exp(s U_j),
 P_j=1_{min(number of ones,number of zeros)<=delta n_j}.

Within this phase basin, expander edge confinement gives U_j>=h H_j, where H_j is distance to the nearer consensus. U_j<=d H_j as well. Any fixed-body physical leg changes H_j by at most q and U_j by at most qd. Thus a newly exiting source strip satisfies

 H_j>=delta n_j-q  => U_j>=h(delta n_j-q),
 ||R_j F_j^(-1/2)||<=exp[-s h(delta n_j-q)/2].

This supplies the same per-new-record barrier factor in the flag argument, with exponent adjusted by h. Remaining-weight relative congruences cost only exp(O(sqd)), independent of block size. The phase projection and energy commute as computational-diagonal observables.

Every strict external-majority flip lowers U_j by at least one. The set of vertices in the minority whose PADDED weighted majority would correct them is a subset of the external-energy correcting set: deleting self-votes that agree with a wrong current bit cannot prevent a correcting flip. Spectral mixing gives at least (1-c)H_j such vertices. Hence the actual external-energy payload has

 L_0^* exp(s U_j) <= -kappa(1-exp(-s))(1-c) U_j exp(s U_j)/d

on the small-phase basin. There is no need to assume the Hamming-error drift of the padded rule survives removal of self-votes. Extra external flips may increase H_j but still decrease the tracked U_j. This is precisely why phase ENTRY/EXIT must remain separate from energy monotonicity.

For complete-loss compression the nominal negative term may be dropped, and weak physical noise contributes C_s eta n_j F_j. Product weights on selected records therefore have the CP propagation budget used in the flag expansion. Strong QND reads commute the classical computational labels and cause no phase-energy injection; their deviations must stay within the actual declared noise/interface budget.

During growth, a global edge-energy parameter-sharpening step can use this strict negative U_j drift. Arbitrary s in a fixed finite interval is allowed nominally; larger s changes the finite noise constants and the required rate/noise hierarchy. The new-target reset, copy pullback and stage cut must still provide their ACTUAL global phase moment transport. The separately reviewed localized port cycle does not by itself prove that transport.

A union phase basin contains both correct consensuses. Inside it, bounded local legs cannot switch the encoded classical label when n_j(1-2delta)>q. Logical phase/dephasing is allowed for a classical record. Source-label/coherent-module accounting after a marked exit remains a separate receiving obligation.
