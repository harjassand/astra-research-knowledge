# Explicitly measured digital alternative — changed resource contract
30 October 2026. Informed alternative, not a claimed substitute for the coherent/native construction.

If each finite scheduled classical gate has a GENUINE instrument output and re-prepares a physical computational-basis output for the next gate, there is an actual positive sequence of recorded values. Conditional on prior real records, a fixed basis input is tensored with an arbitrary conditional retained bath. A bounded-depth gate/read/reprepare interval can then use the accepted positive subset-moment method to bound simultaneous wrong outputs uniformly in that bath. The exponent may be eta^a for fixed a>0; shared q-body noise need not give independent errors.

This is potentially the exact bridge to ordinary reliable noisy classical circuits: the per-layer bound is conditional on actual history, not a probability assignment to signed HP Dyson terms. All input-read, detector and reprepare faults must be included in the interval. The logical circuit itself still requires an explicit reliable encoder/memory/readout construction and bounded physical fanout/degree.

The final genuine instrument boundary is a declared extra resource. Finite-strength Lindblad dephasing for finite time is NOT an exact classical boundary; nor does an ideal measurement label authorize an immutable physical output latch. Repeated detectors, timing and feedforward must be counted for the full computation horizon. This setting differs materially from autonomous all-coherent/native control, especially if the timing/instrument boundary is externally supplied throughout retention.

No complete measured-digital implementation or uniform conditional gate-error theorem is certified in this note. Its value is to identify a lawful positive-history interface that could be proved, rather than continuing to assume one exists for arbitrary HP perturbations of autonomous hardware.
