## 1. Derive an ADDITIVE operator Foster bound without operator Jensen
Let T be a positive unital proof pullback, V>0 a raw positive weight, and F=T(V). Suppose on the declared quantum basin (with the actual complete-loss killed generator K)

and K^*I<=0. This is the form obtained from the actual native energy inequality by log V=sE+log G and log G<=gN; it is not the generally false replacement T(V log V)=F logF.

For any fixed c>rho/k, scalar functional calculus gives

 V log V >= cN V-exp(cN-1)I,

since the minimum of x log x-cN x over x>0 is -exp(cN-1). Positivity and unitality yield

 K^*F <= -(kc-rho)N F+k exp(cN-1)I.                (1)

The same argument works for a subunital pullback with the corresponding harmless upper bound on T(I); that property must be stated, not presumed.
