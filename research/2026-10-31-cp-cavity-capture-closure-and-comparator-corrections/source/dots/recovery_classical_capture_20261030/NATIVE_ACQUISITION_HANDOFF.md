# Scheduled native acquisition without a trusted all-ready signal
30 October 2026, 16:54 UTC. Receiving construction candidate; not covered by atomic acquisition PASS.

## 1. Why a handoff is necessary
Changing a hub's majority truth table/read neighborhood while its old C-prefix is in flight is not valid. Nor is a claim that every hub has drained after a constant wait. The construction below permits incomplete/failed controller resets and prices their residual labels. Acquisition remains declared finite scheduling; no autonomous scheduler is asserted.

## 2. Physical edge potential before controller erasure
In the classical majority forest, each clean hub's DATA prefix is either unchanged or has applied its single target flip c_v. This flip is zero whenever all canonical disagreement edges incident to v are zero. Thus each physical edge syndrome obeys a bounded-local support inequality

 P_(physical disagreement uv)
   <= sum_(canonical edge checks in a fixed neighborhood of uv) P_check
      +B_u+B_v.                                      (H1)

Only the unique writer of each endpoint can change its Z; other hub reads use it only as a control. This is a computational-basis operator statement, including coherent controller-label superpositions. Dirty histories pay arbitrary prefixes. It does not require that clean BUSY equals dirty.

Use a compatible mixed graph and sufficiently many actual history-token copies. By bounded multiplicity/dilation and rooted-animal preimage counting, (H1) should imply

 V_physical(root; ordinary parameters)
     <= C F_old-native(root; stronger parameters).     (H2)

The stronger old raw witness must include the positive-pullback lower-bound loss. This is a local syndrome dictionary, not a nonlocal claim that a sparse syndrome always has a sparse preimage. On a clean homogeneous record, every clean prefix leaves physical DATA homogeneous.

At this point switch the ANALYSIS observable to the physical-data potential. No physical gate or instantaneous repair occurs. Pause old controller Hamiltonians/dissipators by the declared acquisition schedule. Physical DATA and the actual port bit are unchanged.

## 3. Reset apparatus as arbitrary dirty targets
Old controller/lock/monitor systems can now be treated as auxiliary systems. Apply actual onsite resets toward a common padded ready label. The incoming state may be arbitrarily correlated with DATA/bath and may be partway through an old prefix. Introduce soft reset-defect weights r0>1 and ramp them to a hard penalty while the resets act, exactly in the spirit of NOISY_ACQUISITION_MIXED_ANIMALS.md. The physical DATA potential is diagonal and unchanged by these resets. Parameter reserve pays the fixed interval without old majority service, and read perturbations use the physical port rooted witness during this interval.

The reset systems attach to bounded local read/resource neighborhoods, not necessarily single leaves. Their bounded-degree/fiber attachment needs the finite-completion version of the soft-attachment bound; the exact four-leaf identity alone does not prove it. All controller dimensions and the maximal padded label alphabet are fixed across the prefix family.

No claim is made that all resets succeed. A nonready residual label is a reset defect, regardless of whether it happens to look like a clean label under the new table.

## 4. Enter the new native forest without a clean-ready promise
Install the new prefix's fixed truth tables while old dynamics is paused, after the actual reset preparation interval. For the NEW forest, a ready apparatus is identity on physical DATA. Any nonready residual apparatus/lock label can alter canonical DATA only in its bounded footprint under the new inverse-prefix map. Therefore new canonical disagreement checks and new B flags are locally covered by physical DATA disagreement checks plus the prepared reset-defect checks. A failed reset that accidentally satisfies a new clean-history predicate is still paid by its reset-defect source.

The accepted initialization comparison for the newly recomputed native instance bounds F_new above by a slightly stronger raw potential, including actual B tokens and activity margins. Combine it with this local dictionary and the prepared mixed reset potential. This is the desired receiving bound for starting new native dynamics on an arbitrary warm, not perfectly ready, state.

The NEW finite controller family, bad/isolated resolvents, monitor supports and uniform proof constants must be constructed for every local prefix pattern and padded to a common maximum. There are finitely many patterns because fixed-power neighborhoods reach only finitely many future layers. No old numerical resolvent is silently reused.

## 5. Phase cuts and what is not automatic
(H1)-(H2) do not mean every old state right up to its phase cutoff maps below the same new cutoff. If the new handoff fails, bounded support/multiplicity implies high old canonical error/history or high reset-defect weight; this supplies an endpoint barrier for the separate signed bad-record expansion. It must be proved with reserved cutoff margins. One cannot require cutoffs to shrink by a fixed factor at every growth step.

The whole receiving inequality must retain the quantum comparator during the finite pause and its source budget. The dirty/new-flag and physical-data factors may not commute after native pullback; choose the intermediate physical diagonal chart explicitly and use positive operator inequalities, rather than multiplying incompatible native witnesses.

At this checkpoint (H1) is being verified from the actual payload; (H2), the generalized soft apparatus attachment, and the new-forest warm receiving composition remain proof obligations. The construction avoids an immutable latch, a global all-ready test, and an unbounded-degree root-port switch, but is not yet a native acquisition receipt.
