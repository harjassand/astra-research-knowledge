## 2. Pooled generator and adaptive labels

For positive D, the proposed counts Y_i are nonnegative and total n-1. Every restoring edge is explicitly pool-neutral. A mixed excursion therefore inserts elapsed time without an unpriced change of Y; its exit seed is still a real seed and is counted as such.

For non-a targets, equality with q_ij Y_i is checked coefficientwise. For a targets, the selected baseline is the minimum source-pool coefficient. Subtracting that baseline leaves nonnegative coefficients after D=1+u_D:

- if the source pool contains D, its Y_i has zero constant after this substitution;
- otherwise the baseline has no constant;

Thus the code's remainder test is sufficient even for transfers caused by a catalyst belonging to a different pool. It is not merely a sign test on unshifted D. Baseline rates are nonnegative on every actual positive-D state.

A precise labeled coupling is available. Give each abstract label independent potential clocks for every baseline type edge. When an applicable clock rings, choose an actual physical channel with the corresponding rate split; choose/reassign physical identities inside pools as necessary. ## 3. Prescribed paths survive the allowed extra jumps

Reachability of a from every pooled type suffices; full baseline irreducibility is not needed. For each initial type, prescribe its shortest baseline path during successive subintervals of a fixed small horizon and suppress all undesired potential baseline rings, in particular all departures from a throughout the horizon. If an adapted extra jump reaches a early, the no-departure condition keeps the label there. Such a jump therefore cannot invalidate the prescribed event. With L the largest shortest distance (at least one), every label succeeds with probability at least c t^L. These events use independent potential clocks across labels before conditioning on survival.

The correct probability argument is an unconditional containment: on a low-a surviving trajectory with few seed marks, too few of the independent prescribed events can have occurred. It does not claim independence under survival conditioning. A seed removes at most two labels from a; choose a deterministic removal convention. The count of seed marks before clearing has a geometric tail because all seed rates and clearing share `(a)_2`. Restoring events are a-neutral. Hence the usual cohort loss allowance is valid for arbitrary accepted seed products, not only a+D and 2D.

For long horizons, apply a last-unit path event from each label's actual current type. Each has a uniform positive success lower bound. For short horizons, initial-a no-departure events suffice. Fixed n bounds all residual rates quadratically. Combining these concentration events with polynomial duration moments, using an exponential truncation time if necessary, gives the stated polynomial-prefactor exp(-c a_in) cohort bound. No independent restart-block assumption is required.
