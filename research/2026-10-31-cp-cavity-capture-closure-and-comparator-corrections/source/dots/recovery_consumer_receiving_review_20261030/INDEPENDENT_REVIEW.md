## 1. Governing raw algebra
I read GLOBAL_EXPONENTIAL_COMPILER.md and its actual use in the fixed-baseline receipt. There E>=0, G>=I, [E,G]=0, log G<=gN, and the positive unital proof pullback T is not asserted to preserve products or logarithms. The source's V=exp(sE)G therefore has log V=sE+log G. For k<=lambda/s,

 lambda E V-k V log V+k gN V
   =(lambda-ks) EV+k(gN-log G)V >=0.

Positivity of T proves the required quantum inequality. The record algebra is a separate tensor factor, so T acts as identity on it and R commutes V. Thus

 T((VR) log(VR)) = R T(V log V)+T(V)R log R.

This identity is the entire logarithmic composition step. It does not say log T(V)=T(log V), nor replace the raw logarithm by F log F.

The source slightly overestimates the root-vacuum source using |B| rather than |B\D|; this is harmless. The marked set contributes only through |D intersect B|. Bulk records cannot be added to B without changing the physical coupling contract and paying their actual resource count.

## 2. Actual mixed-noise receiving without a classical-volume charge
The rooted review §4 proves the local orbit variation of a record factor Z_j and its product extension. For record-only noise support S, the relative oscillation is bounded by

where T_(j,S) sums only animals containing a check touched by S. The complete HP term annihilates an orbitwise constant. Summing the actual per-site incident norms counts each animal through its |A| checks, producing C eta sum_j K W_j/Z_j. Tensoring with the independent quantum observable F_Q does not alter the estimate: the noise is identity on quantum DATA/controllers, although it may act on a common bath. The statement is completely bounded, so no bath dimension or independence enters.

For a term touching a quantum site as well as record sites, use the actual native local relative-norm/dilation estimate for F_Q and the bounded local orbit ratio for the at-most-q record factors. The combined relative bound is C times that term's cb norm. Charge the WHOLE term to one of its quantum legs. Every such term has at least one quantum leg; summing cannot exceed the total quantum-site incident budget, up to the fixed support multiplicity. This costs C eta N_Q, not eta times classical storage volume. The mixed support may contain distant legs; no graph edge between their locations is needed. The constant deteriorates with fixed local graph/body parameters but is independent of record block size and N.

This separation is already explicit in the frozen rooted review. It supplies the new source's weak-noise allocation, provided N_Q=O(N), the native local bound is for the actual padded controller family, and the full generator has CPTP propagators. Signed HP summands need not separately generate physical channels.

## 4. Softening and complete norm
For raw V_D>0, scalar functional calculus gives

 V_D log V_D-cN V_D+exp(cN-1) I>=0.

Apply the positive unital T and the joint drift. The resulting additive inequality is valid even when T(V_D) and T(V_D log V_D) do not commute. For Z=I+exp(-aN)F_D, the complete-loss generator has K*I<=0, and discarding the remaining negative F_D term gives K*Z+partial_t Z<=epsilon_N Z. Actual CP/TNI comparison implies

 Phi_(t,s)^* Z_t <= exp(integral_s^t epsilon_N) Z_s.

This is a complete weighted trace-norm bound: conjugate the CP map by the square roots of the positive weights. Its adjoint sends I below the displayed scalar multiple of I, so its completely bounded trace norm has that bound, also with arbitrary references. No signed-state Jensen argument is needed.

At a stage endpoint or mark drop, the outgoing observable is no larger than the incoming one in the appropriate actual frame. Thus the corresponding identity/frame transition is contractive. This does not bound the separate signed crossing superoperator; its source strip and left/right weighted norm are still required.

## 5. Exit and source scope
The quantum strip premise F_Q>=exp(beta N)P_exit is the sector-sensitive native lower bound with all old energy/baseline allowances included. Since R_D>=I, F_D>=F_Q. Therefore P_exit<=exp(-(beta-a)N)Z. The required interval nu,c<a<beta is real: none of its terms can be selected independently after accepting an arbitrary source.

The initial JOINT moment remains a substantive gap. Separate first moments of F_Q and R do not bound their product under correlated source noise. Further, during initial dirty seed reset, every active baseline read can be uncontrolled, so the strong-hot density may be order one. One possible declared schedule disables quantum read/correction service for a fixed seed window, pays its finite-time weak noise using the coherent local source bound, and enables the receiver after actual seed capture. That is a possible construction, not certified here; it must supply the initial joint moment and its frame/common-span interface.

The soft Q metric must retain a rooted factor for EVERY unmarked boundary port that drives it. A subset-family norm omitting one loses the credit for its read perturbation. Additional terminal-tail factors can be layered only after a separate joint product proof. Likewise multiplying selected-record phase weights with this Q metric needs an actual per-move joint estimate; two marginal Foster bounds do not prove it.

## 6. Checks
check_softening.py ran 1,000 random finite CP-unital pullbacks. The minimum eigenvalue margins were positive: 0.0842 for the raw log conversion, 0.253 for the additive scalar-Foster conversion, and 3.34e-6 for dropping factors. The pulled-back F and raw-log observable were deliberately noncommuting (maximum commutator norm about 2675), so the checks do not accidentally rely on simultaneous physical diagonalization. These are regressions for the algebra, not evidence of a physical threshold or substitutes for the proof.
