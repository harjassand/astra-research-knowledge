# Dominating-cover seed construction — candidate pending independent review

This local mathematical package joins two previously separate mechanisms: slow
finite seed inventories and overlapping fast hub activity. It is not operational
chemistry and does not claim general conservative one-port or weak-reversibility
stability. Nothing in a rejected graph proves instability.

## Result and earned information

The raw network has a single reversible port `0 <-> A`, square-free binary
satellite hubs, a reversible `2A` amplification pair at every hub, and reversible
`A+X` arms covering every satellite. The graph is acquired on **all** satellites.
It must exclude induced `triangle + isolated vertex` and `P4 + isolated vertex`.
This contains disjoint bicliques with free passengers, complete multipartite graphs,
and the new overlapping P4 and C5 geometries. All positive real rates are allowed
in the proof; exact construction uses positive rational rates.

The theorem acquires `R=A+minimum weighted vertex-cover count`, then constructs

    W=t^N [g_R + q A/((N+1)(R+1)^2) - epsilon A/(N+1)].

The cover collection is obtained by explicit subset enumeration, not supplied as
an oracle. A backward rational recurrence earns the entire low-inventory profile
and joins an analytic tail. A second finite scan pays its phase compensation.
The boundary-cone width pays the actual adverse-to-good profile slope ratio.
The interior correction has the opposite sign and pays activation through physical
A-death. No stationary fiber, independent success coin, fast latent clock or
favorable-cover premise is assumed. Whole physical birth/death costs remain.

`COVER_SEED_THEOREM.md` gives all joins, a sharp graph boundary for the particular
near-cone rate absorption, the Foster/occupation passage, and cost semantics.
Standard graph covers/domination, elementary pairing/Cauchy bounds, generator
corrections and Foster tools are credited as components. Historical novelty is
not established.
