# All-time passage law: costs, scope, ordinary priors and failures

## A different actual output

The operation is a UNIFORM absolute CDF approximation for the autonomous passage
time of the ordinary constant-SPD Langevin model, not another clamped scalar
CIR query. It returns one exact rational reference exponential rate and an
all-time Kolmogorov error bound, plus optional outward finite-time CDF intervals.
Actual dynamics need not have a Markov energy coordinate or a spectral gap.
The exact full Gibbs|A law remains mandatory; no fixed-ray shortcut transfers.

Neither tiny relative tail probabilities nor exponential moments are received.
The separate finite mean certificate is retained, but it cannot be deduced by
integrating a uniform constant CDF error to infinity. An externally verified TV
preparation error can affect bounded CDF probabilities additively; no such
preparation is implemented and it would not automatically control the mean.
Physical membership/calibration and the time-homogeneous model over the queried
horizon remain premises. A result in dimensionless model time is not a physical
claim that a clock or generator operates through10^38 units.

## Acquisition and all extra arithmetic

The wrapper re-executes the unchanged hash-pinned ordinary MFPT engine from the
original raw model and external task. Thus all positivity/coercivity/degree,
actual mobility, input reading/hashing, scalar quadrature/tails and old work are
paid again. It does not trust a previously displayed MFPT, a spectral measure,
a committor, a learned slow coordinate or an angular equilibrium sampler.
The exact model proof and the new spectral theorem jointly justify the output.

After receiving M/R, the wrapper computes the source spectral error and one
additional positive root for rho(1+rho), with outward integer arithmetic. The
root-call count is fixed; operand sizes depend on the acquired rational masses,
dimension, precision and input bits. There is NO dimension-free bit-cost claim.
The arithmetic-midpoint rate is exact rational; its numerical uncertainty costs
(3/16)(k_hi/k_lo-1) in the uniform error, not a hidden exact-rate assumption.

Finite external times must be nonnegative exact Fractions, with strict bit and
count budgets. Exp(-k_star*t) uses the already independently reviewed dyadic
range-reduction/Taylor/squaring primitive. All extra guarded products consume
the REMAINING external max_operations after MFPT products, not a fresh budget.
Precision, exponential terms and integer operand bounds remain finite. Optional
float displays never determine acceptance. The full KS and finite-query width
are checked against the original external requests, including all structural,
initial-law comparison, rate and exponential rounding contributions.

Work counters are diagnostics, not proof inputs. Verification recomputes all
work/parameters and compares every exact proof field, nested MFPT proof record,
rate, query and model/initial-law string. Altering diagnostic counters cannot
free extra arithmetic budget. A pre-freeze depth guard additionally refuses
native cyclic/recursively nested certificates finitely; JSON itself has no
cycles. Earlier model and source freezes remain unchanged.

## Strong ordinary priors, not a new generic exponentiality theory

Aldous and Fill, *Reversible Markov Chains and Random Walks on Graphs*, §3.5,
primary author-hosted text opened:
https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/RWG/Book_Ralph/Ch3.S5.html

It develops completely-monotone spectral mixtures and exponential approximation.
Proposition3.22 quotes a moment-based uniform approximation; Proposition3.23
uses relaxation time relative to mean hitting time. These are direct ordinary
priors for this organizing idea, not unrelated comparisons.

Fernandez, Manzo, Nardi and Scoppola, *Asymptotically exponential hitting times
and metastability: a pathwise approach without reversibility*, primary PDF:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1406.2637

This gives explicit exponentiality corrections using quantified recurrence
relative to passage time, without requiring reversibility. Thus absence of a
global gap is not by itself a novelty claim. Its required recurrence information
must be acquired rather than assumed as a free input.

The useful newly assembled component here is the finite received trial/current
energy and inverse-energy control plus exact A-to-trial initial-law comparison,
with no computed spectral measure, angular mixing or recurrence bound. Ordinary
spectral/variational implementations supplied the same derived certificate get
the same guarantee. No historical priority, optimized-runtime superiority or
proprietary algorithmic separation is established. Lu--Vanden-Eijnden's strong
prior on task-specific dynamical coarse-graining is separately retained in the
post-freeze comparison note of the preceding construction; it is not silently
substituted for this exact initialization or an uncharged coordinate solve.
