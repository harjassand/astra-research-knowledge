## New inventory geometry and the crucial sign/rate distinction

Let F be aggregate free count, U=A+rare mass, N=F+U, J=U+A and R=A+tau_B.

contains v free endpoints, v=0,1,2, a forward 2A->H has

- Delta A=-2, Delta F=v, Delta U=-v;
- Delta J=-(2+v), hence up to **four** resource units;

Its reverse never decreases either R or J. If the reverse leaves R unchanged,
tau_B must fall by two. A destination-optimal cover then gives an old optimal
cover containing both endpoints. Both are rare, their positive counts sum to at
most tau_B, and their product is at most R² (indeed tau_B²/4). This bounds the

absolute increments at most 20/(R+1)². For A/(J+1)² the corresponding safe
forward bound is 40/(J+1)². An enabled forward has A>=2 and J>=4; the destination
denominator can lose four, so the old two-step mass bound would not suffice.

are proportional to A(A-1), hence O(A²), rather than an uncontrolled adverse AF
or F² intensity.

profile/phase sign is paid individually. They are never declared absent merely
