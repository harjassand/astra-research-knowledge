No historical novelty or algorithmic separation is inferred from this bounded

## Direct exit-time variational principle (primary PDF opened)

Huang, Kim, Mao and Wang, *Variational formulas for the exit time of Hunt
processes generated by semi-Dirichlet forms*, arXiv:2011.04334v2 (26 Nov 2021):
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.04334

Theorem 1.1 provides an exit-time Laplace-transform variational formula and a
zero-parameter mean formula under transience. Corollary 1.3 gives its symmetric
Dirichlet minimization. Remark 1.2 identifies weak Poisson potentials and allows
compact test functions through form closure. This is a direct strong prior for
the general source/resolvent approach, not merely a loose conceptual analogy.
It does not, in the inspected result, acquire our conditional dilation current,
quartic moment bound, basin/slab estimates or exact raw finite arithmetic. Our
initial source is Gibbs restricted to A, whereas the target-killed domain also
contains S; the explicit cross-source argument remains needed. We have not
established that no other prior section or publication supplies those estimates.

## Spectral-gap route (primary PDF opened)

Huang, Mao and Wang, *Variational principles for asymptotic variance of general
Markov processes*, arXiv:2106.00324v1 (1 Jun 2021):
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.00324

Corollary 1.3 bounds stationary mean exit time using a spectral gap under
exponential ergodicity. Theorem 1.4 retains a reversible variational formula for
asymptotic variance without that assumption, allowing infinity. These locate
ordinary inverse-generator variational machinery. The gap-based corollary is
not a free finite certificate for this polynomial longitudinal marginal. Our
current proof uses positive resolvents and proves finite source energy before
passing to zero; it does not assert a bounded global inverse or import a gap.

## What is and is not new in this local construction

The general Poisson/Dirichlet/resolvent method is plainly existing machinery.
The useful assembled operation is a source-bound, finite full-state conditional
mean interval obtained from two scalar integrals plus explicit moment and basin
bounds, for the stated raw quartic/constant-mobility interface. An ordinary
implementation given this same constructed proof reproduces the interval; that
is not a located independent predecessor for the specific derived certificate.

The ordinary isotropic Gaussian has a radial energy closure and remains a
strong special case, but generic coupled quartics do not: the author suite
exhibits equal-energy states with different Euclidean energy covariance and
drift. Thus the new ordinary quartic consumer does not get a CIR reduction by
renaming the previous engineered model. A verified full elliptic PDE or barrier
solver, stochastic simulation and conditional averaging get the same model and
initial law; no controlled runtime win against their strongest implementation
has been demonstrated. Fast conditional averaging must acquire its own mixing
error. This construction does not learn empirical model membership.
