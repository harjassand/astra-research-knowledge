# Primary comparison at the repeated-clock boundary

Read directly 2026-11-01: Lee and Seo, *Non-reversible Metastable Diffusions with Gibbs Invariant Measure II: Markov Chain Convergence*, arXiv:2008.08295v3 (19 July 2022), https://arxiv.org/pdf/2008.08295 . Inspected Sections2–4, especially growth assumptions(2.1), Morse condition, Theorems3.4–3.5 and4.1. No mirror was used.

The paper already proves a complete successive-transition Markov-chain limit for nonreversible Gibbs diffusions. Its trace process removes off-valley excursions, whose time is separately proved negligible. Theorem3.5 permits arbitrary initial measures supported in a selected valley, stronger than our fixed Gibbs-basin initialization. Its main mechanism is a resolvent characterization, not independent exponential waits assumed after each hit. Gibbs-preserving skew-gradient circulation is also explicitly established prior machinery.

Its stated setting uses a Morse potential, specified coercive growth, and a small-noise limit. Our current F(y,z)=v(y)U(z) has a flat critical set z=0, is zero along that unbounded set, and takes a growing-dimension entropic limit. Thus those stated hypotheses do not directly admit this model. That is a scope distinction, not proof that their machinery cannot be adapted or that our result is historically new. Do not count repeated Markov limits, trace/last-visit distinctions, resolvent methods or Gibbs-preserving circulation themselves as inventions.
