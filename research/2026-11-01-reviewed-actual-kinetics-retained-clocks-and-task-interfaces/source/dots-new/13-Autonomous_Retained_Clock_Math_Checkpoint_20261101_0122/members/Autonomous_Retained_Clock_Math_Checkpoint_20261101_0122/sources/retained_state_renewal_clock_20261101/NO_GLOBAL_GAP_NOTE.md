# The global gap really is zero in this family

This separate elementary note supports the frozen candidate's stronger wording
that no global gap exists, rather than merely saying that no gap is assumed.
It is not needed to use its current or path bound.

The actual longitudinal marginal of pi is q(y)/Ztot, with
q(y)=[1+kappa(1-y^2)^2]^-alpha. Fix a nonzero smooth compactly supported
phi in (1,2), and f_R(y,z)=phi(y/R), R tending to infinity. On [R,2R],
q(y) is bounded above and below by fixed positive multiples of R^-4alpha.
Therefore

 int f_R^2 d pi is of order R^(1-4alpha),
 (int f_R d pi)^2 is O(R^(2-8alpha)),
 E(f_R,f_R) is O(R^(-1-4alpha)).

Since alpha>=4, the squared mean is negligible compared with the second
moment. Subtracting the mean yields a mean-zero trial with Rayleigh quotient
O(R^-2), tending to zero. The form membership of f_R follows directly from
these finite integrals, or by transverse cutoffs and closed-form convergence.
Longitudinal mobility is exactly one, and transverse derivatives vanish, so
this conclusion is independent of angular U and the finite constant SPD M_z.
The positive self-adjoint global mean-zero generator is injective but has no
strictly positive spectral lower bound. Finite inverse energy for the specific
odd observable is consequently a genuine task-specific statement, not a
hidden global mixing-gap assertion.
