# Primary matches, differences, and costs

Read-only primary sources inspected 2026-10-31. No claim of exhaustive historical novelty search.

* Douc, Jacob, Lee, Vats, **Solving the Poisson equation using coupled Markov chains**, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.05691 . Section 2.2, Definition 5 already sums observable differences until meeting to estimate a Poisson-solution difference. Section 2.1 imposes successful coupling/moment conditions for its undiscounted results. Our discounted cap is valid even without finite meeting, with a separately observed survival probability; that finite-confidence join is proved here. It is not a newly invented coupled estimator.
* Jacob, O'Leary, Atchade, **Unbiased Markov chain Monte Carlo with couplings**, https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.03625 . Coupling, retained trajectories, meeting-time analysis and unbiased differences are strong ordinary components.
* Biswas, Jacob, Vanetti, **Estimating convergence of Markov chains with L-lag couplings**, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.09971 . This is a direct prior on learning useful convergence information from coupling times, not evidence that experimental convergence diagnostics are new here.
* Maurer and Pontil, **Empirical Bernstein Bounds and Sample Variance Penalization**, https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.3740 . Theorem 4 supplies the sample-variance confidence inequality used after exact range rescaling. Our executable uses their range term as well as variance; zero observed variance is not a zero error certificate.
* Cao et al., **A Time-Aggregation Approach to Markov Decision Processes**, https://user.eng.umd.edu/~marcus/docs/cao.pdf . Time aggregation, sample-path policy optimization, bias/Poisson responses and available likelihood-ratio information are ordinary comparator capital. Ratios or known probabilities are not provided by this bare forward interface; if actually available they must be given to all methods.
* Morris, **An improved discount factor for the Markov decision problem**, https://math.gmu.edu/~wmorris/cubemdpmprev4.pdf ; Klaus, **A fresh look at the complexity of pivoting in linear complementarity problems**, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1405.1827 . The small canonical decision MDP remains ordinary hidden-K / LP machinery; accepted direct-canonical source and whole review are pinned separately.

## Equal-information comparison

The strongest ordinary coupled-Poisson or successor-response learner can implement this frontend and the same downstream compiler. It receives source-row tags, every intermediate microscopic label, all failed attempts, the acquired partition, exact confidence rules and canonical witness. This is not an exclusive algorithmic win against those methods. Direct row estimation, retained endpoint statistics, learned coupling proposals, trajectory reuse, empirical Bernstein/KL refinements and on-demand task-specific control are legitimate competitors. No d^N enumeration or discarded trajectory baseline is used.

An even stronger ordinary coupled-geometric baseline is implemented in `ordinary_coupled_geometric.py`. It uses an independent internal geometric stopping time shared by the two forward paths, stops at meeting, retains intermediate labels, and has mean U. Under E tau<=kappa its variance is O(kappa/(1-gamma)), so it too has inverse-linear discount acquisition. On the saved full path bank it requires zero additional forward calls. Its hypothetical shorter stopped run does not erase the actual bank's 2,142 calls. Selecting geometric coin seeds after looking for favorable outputs would require a new uniform event; the scientific fixture uses a fixed seed and does not authenticate iid physical coins. Both this learner and any ordinary learner may adopt the prefix certificate itself.

The earlier independently sampled geometric endpoint sufficient construction spent N~(1-gamma)^(-2) trajectories with expected length ~(1-gamma)^(-1), at fixed target tolerance and constant acquired canonical geometry. That inverse-cubic *sufficient* bill is improved by a locally fast paired response to inverse-linear up to logarithms. Ordinary retaining methods may already achieve this or better with additional structure. The task-level lower bound proves only that eliminating all discount dependence is impossible in this access/task language. It does not prove our full dimensional/sample/bit schedule optimal.

## Finite execution ledger

For a selected N,K, F=n(m-1): actual source calls are exactly the sum of 2+2 min(tau,K) over all generated trials. Every failed earlier grid point and retained extension is included. Under a named-row oracle each call also requests a named-state preparation; physical cost is source-specific and must be multiplied or separately reported, never assumed negligible. An implementation able to continue two physical trajectories needs two initial preparations per pair instead, but this is additional operational access, not a theorem that resets are free.

Full path storage is O(calls log n) bits plus row/action tags. The prototype deliberately retains all labels for replay; a prospective streaming version can retain sufficient statistics and unfinished paths, but cannot then claim authenticated full-record replay. Preparing the block lookup costs O(n log r) bits; it is not included in the r-sized consumer. Discount gamma=p/q gives prefix coefficients a paid common denominator q^K, O(K log q) bits. Exact sums/squares add O(log N) bits. Verification and empirical-variance arithmetic scale with all retained symbols plus F r, not only r. Upward square roots use integer arithmetic with externally fixed bits. Logarithms use verified integer upper bounds ceil(log2 x)>=ln x for x>=1; these conservative constants are exposed.

Once the received small model is retained, a new reward table has mr entries; ordinary small LP/canonical policy computation, numerical or exact checking, reward reading, policy storage, label sensing and partition mapping remain paid. Exact rational LP worst-case polynomial algorithms are a paper construction; the installed SymPy simplex is only a proposal backend, can return UNKNOWN or a rejected candidate, and has no asserted polynomial runtime. Source-provenance/independence cannot be authenticated by JSON consistency checks.
