hub and either `2A` or `A+X`; every hub has its amplification pair, and every
satellite has at least one arm. Duplicate directed rates are summed. Null channels
are discarded only after complete coordinate and rate validation. Rates are
strictly positive exact integers/Fractions at the executable interface; bool,
float, string and malformed dimensions/counts are refused, not silently coerced.
The ordinary proof covers arbitrary strictly positive real constants and every
finite real t>1; it does not identify finite rational tests with that universal
proof.

The parser tries isolated satellites in coordinate order, requires the chosen F

or merely trust a graph label. Core condition: no induced triangle-plus-isolate
or P4-plus-isolate. A deletion or coordinate permutation can change the acquired
role/circuit, so all receiving repeats raw acquisition.

be exactly one isolated satellite in the whole graph. The accepted remaining core

## 3. Inventory geometry, switching and graph facts

R=A+tau (minimum weighted cover of the acquired core graph), and

and then assume that same cover remains optimal after a jump.

For core R, each internal change is -1,0,+1. A hub removal decreases tau by one
or two; decrease two occurs exactly when some old optimal cover contains both
