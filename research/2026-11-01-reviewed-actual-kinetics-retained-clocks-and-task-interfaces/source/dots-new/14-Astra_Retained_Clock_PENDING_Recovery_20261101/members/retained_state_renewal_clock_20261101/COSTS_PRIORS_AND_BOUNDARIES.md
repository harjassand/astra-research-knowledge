# What the reusable-clock construction earns, and what it costs

## Consequence, rather than a new favorable profile

The raw model, original constant unit mobility, full Gibbs|A initial-law
premise and scalar integrals are exactly those of the accepted 513D example.
Raw SHA-256:
`e693d5c1ccf5fe43336f3e845d78fdad3a308d0fe88094cc8c4688a5caca76ff`.
No rethermalization or microscopic-state reset is made at a later crossing.
The new mathematical object is a **global mean-zero odd inverse-form current**,
not the old process killed on B. It receives a vector slow observable. Actual
history conditioning then controls a whole finite record, and a separate
stationary count/cluster proof earns continuous hysteretic time-jitter coupling.

This is not a logical consequence of a single Gibbs-start first-passage CDF.
The return journeys are charged in the same actual wall time. In particular,
unidirectional A-to-B output-to-output cycles include a return and converge to
two-exponential (Erlang-2) cycles, not to a freely reset exponential clock.
The angular state may retain information; its forgetting is never asserted.

The once-only exact full Gibbs|A law is a declared mathematical target, not an
implemented sampler. A genuine separately received TV preparation error would
add once to the full-path guarantee. Discrete samples are not automatically
TV-close to a continuous Gibbs law. Actual model identification, boundary
sensing, clock bandwidth and long physical exposure remain unpaid operations.

## All input and arithmetic costs remain visible

The canonical raw JSON is 1,079,871 bytes. The positive-quartic parser reads
1,023 monomials with 523,776 exponent entries. This concrete interface uses
full exponent vectors; it is NOT an implicit sparse-exponent input model.
Missing pure terms, negative coefficients, undeclared model fields and invalid
constant mobility are refused. Huge positive angular couplings remain admitted
only after their actual coefficient bits are read and checked.

Each production receive paid the inherited scalar engine anew:

- 131,075 distinct scalar points and 131,079 fixed-point integer-power calls;
- 2,228,343 guarded fixed-product operations, plus inherited raw acquisition;
- 3 inherited outward roots;
- roughly 3.5 seconds observed total on this execution environment (diagnostic,
  not a complexity guarantee or universal timing claim).

The 10-record query additionally paid 3 outward roots, 2 exponential calls,
84 exponential Taylor terms, 548 fixed products, and 52 capped-state DP updates
with 8 states. The continuous case used 424 additional fixed products and 26
updates with 4 states. The extra root operands reached about 6,034 bits: a
fixed number of roots does NOT make their bit cost dimension-independent.
Largest guarded product bits and operation fields are named engine counters,
not a claim that every Python Fraction addition, input parse, allocation or
output digit was metered by that one proxy. Integer bit limits and all other
finite budgets are independently checked; exhaustion yields UNKNOWN.

The global proof may be reused for future predetermined grids, and the same
raw scientific certificate suffices without new angular reconstruction. The
current public `verify` is deliberately a **fresh raw-input recomputation** on
every call, about the cost above. It does not pretend that a saved JSON claim
is authenticated retained state or implement a free trusted-cache service.
A later trusted in-process cache can remove repeated computation, but that
engineering operation is not silently counted as present in this interface.

The finite event is a bounded **finite-state** count DP, O(n J) arithmetic with
J=upper_count+2. Its finite precision, products, allocation bound and input
length are paid. Arbitrary predicates or bounded real-valued accumulators do
not receive that cost guarantee. Error is absolute event error, not relative
error for exponentially unlikely transcripts. An optional initial classical
record can be retained jointly in the theorem, but no quantum, feedback,
adaptive-time or arbitrary rare-subpopulation conditioning claim follows.

## Strong ordinary comparisons get the same raw information

1. **Two-state metastability and trace processes are established machinery.**
   Beltran and Landim's *Tunneling and metastability of continuous time Markov
   chains* ([primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0910.4088)) develops precise
   reduced Markov behavior and capacity/stationary-mass conditions. Their
   *A martingale approach to metastability* ([primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1305.5987))
   uses relaxation/mixing conditions to avoid stronger representative-point
   requirements. We directly inspected their introductions and stated
   frameworks; these are not new invented notions. Their supplied assumptions
   are not automatically a local angular-gap certificate for the present
   continuous unbounded-fiber class.

2. **Transition frequency/committor flux is ordinary potential theory.**
   Lu and Nolen's *Reactive trajectories and the transition path process*
   ([primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1303.1744)) supplies a strong primary
   treatment of reactive entrance/exit trajectories and committor boundary
   measures. Its displayed setup uses bounded smooth A/B and regularity
   hypotheses. We do not silently import it as a theorem covering our
   unbounded full-fiber basins; the finite-form/localization/count argument is
   explicitly supplied in the candidate. A publisher erratum was visible in
   search metadata but not read; no clearance claim is made about it.

3. **Effective one-dimensional/free-energy methods already propose this
   clock scale and two-state approximation.** Exact q=v^-alpha and scalar
   M,R are equally available to an ordinary implementation. The new burden is
   to receive whole continuing-record and hysteretic-path error without an
   angular mixing gap, not to invent the free-energy prediction. Computing a
   true full committor/eigenfunction might improve the bounds; neither is
   supplied free as an oracle. A numerical PDE or trajectory method receives
   the same raw model, and must pay its truncation, rare-event, discretization
   and full-model validation costs for a comparable rigorous certificate.

4. **Our construction uses ordinary primitives.** Explicit Thomson currents,
   Dirichlet forms, spectral/storage inequalities, conditional entropy chain
   rules, Pinsker, parity and union bounds construct the result. A comparator
   augmented with this newly derived global current and its proof can of
   course reproduce it. That is not an independently located prior theorem
   supplying the exact same guarantee. Historical novelty remains unresolved;
   no proprietary algorithmic separation or broad significance score is claimed.

5. **A stronger directly inspected diffusion prior must be retained.** Lee and
   Seo's *Non-reversible Metastable Diffusions with Gibbs Invariant Measure II:
   Markov Chain Convergence* ([primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2008.08295))
   already proves successive-transition Markov-chain limits by a resolvent
   characterization. Its Theorem 3.5 allows arbitrary initial measures in a
   selected valley, stronger than our fixed Gibbs|A premise. Its trace process
   charges removed off-valley time separately. The parent's direct Sections
   2--4 inspection is preserved in `retained_clock_primary_comparison_20261101/
   LEE_SEO_SCOPE_NOTE.md`, SHA
   `9879935d9f73fdaf4654d6ae98926e0aaa2576f7f54e265227a6d265f62d01c0`.
   Its stated Morse/coercive-growth/small-noise hypotheses do not directly admit
   our flat, unbounded z=0 critical set and growing-dimension limit. This is not
   evidence that those methods cannot adapt, or historical novelty clearance.
   A separate Project Euclid survey read returned an iframe wrapper only;
   article contents were not obtained or used.

## Scope and failure history

The fresh author suite checks algebra, exact finite-state analogues, interval
DP, input/budget gates, typed certificate mutation and actual production
recapture. Tests are not a replacement for the continuum proof. At initial
author freeze there has been no implementation repair or independently found
source correction; substantive **expected refusals** are saved separately in
`honest_refusals.json`. Future review corrections must preserve these bytes.

Continuous raw sign paths are excluded because Brownian crossings chatter.
The received object is the separated-basins hysteretic cadlag label. No
finite-grid result automatically extends to adaptive sampling. No continuous
hysteretic result here extends to time-dependent neutral processing: its
actual committor need not be fixed, and the stationary last-label count law
is not inherited. Those are distinct mathematical operations, not parser flags.
