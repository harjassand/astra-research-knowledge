# Pending next opening: robust source-current receiving under density uncertainty

Status: unreviewed root candidate, not a claim about any accepted code or model.
This is ordinary positive Dirichlet-form comparison assembled for a future
kinetic consumer. No historical novelty or physical acquisition is claimed.

Suppose the ACTUAL reversible diffusion has unnormalized density w, with
pointwise received bounds w_minus<=w<=w_plus, and an actual mobility whose
inverse quadratic form is bounded above by the one used to price a current.
The common normalizing scale is arbitrary but must be CONSISTENT in every
mass, source and energy expression. Work in the actual killed domain D=A union S.

For each source E=A or S, suppose a fully constructed current j_E satisfies
div j_E=w_plus*1_E distributionally and has finite bound

  beta int j_E^T D_actual^(-1) j_E / w_minus <=B_E.

Then Q_E=<1_E,(-L_B)^(-1)1_E>_w <=B_E. For a nonnegative compact trial f,
int_E f w <=int_E f w_plus =-int grad f . j_E; Cauchy bounds its square by
the actual Dirichlet energy times B_E. Positive-part contraction allows the
variational supremum to be restricted to nonnegative trials. Positive-resolvent
exhaustion can then earn the actual finite inverse form. No unknown true source
current or true conditional density is supplied as an oracle.

Suppose also a nonnegative scalar trial h(y), h=1 on A and0 on B, has actual
energy at most C0, earned from an UPPER density/mobility bound in its gradient
direction. For the present fiber setting h has only a longitudinal derivative
and that mobility is fixed at1, so no upper transverse-mobility bound is needed.
Let M_minus<=int_A w<=M_plus and int_S w<=S_plus be computed in the same scale.
The ordinary source/current bounds imply

  E_{actual Gibbs|A} tau_B
     <=[B_A+sqrt(B_A B_S)]/M_minus.

A Poisson trial c*h with source overlap at least M_minus gives
Q_A>=M_minus^2/C0, hence the mean lower bound

  E_{actual Gibbs|A} tau_B >= M_minus^2/(C0 M_plus).

The separately proved trial/spectral mechanism would also apply to this actual
process with reference k0=C0/M_minus, rho_upper=S_plus/M_minus and

  epsilon_upper=C0(sqrt(B_A)+sqrt(B_S))^2/M_minus^2-1.

These are deliberately sufficient bounds; no smallness is claimed until the
actual masses/currents are built and the output tolerance passes.

## Why this may matter for the current direction

Earlier robust radial envelopes supplied globally checked
U(l(y)z)<=F_actual(y,z)<=U(u(y)z) for genuinely nonfactorized rational potentials.
They give w_plus=exp[-beta U(lz)], w_minus=exp[-beta U(uz)] and calculable
mass bounds after a common unknown anchor normalizer cancels. A future current
would need source w_plus but be priced against w_minus, not accidentally use
the nominal source or reuse a capacity-only comparison for a mean time.

For homogeneous quartic U, conditional scaling/homotopy is explicit; its squared
density costs require positive differences such as2s-v_upper>0. For mixed
positive homogeneous components, the previously proved radial-concavity
inequality may bound the difference energy, but moments and global receiving
must be rederived. The current well-redistribution construction is only a
foothold; no complete robust finite receiver is written here.

Localized errors vanishing at the barrier and basin minima may avoid an
exponential-in-dimension global density penalty. That is a hypothesis to earn
by the scalar/current bounds, not permission to ignore model error. The actual
F's global diffusion, mobility, exact Gibbs-conditioned initialization and
