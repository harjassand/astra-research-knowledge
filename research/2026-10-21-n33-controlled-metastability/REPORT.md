## Derived result A: automatic controlled finite-volume exponential safety

**Theorem A (conditional only on N33 main proof and its stochastic appendix).** Let the finite ordinary-endotactic graph have integer nonnegative source and target complexes (actual molecular mass action), bounded predictable reaction rates k_e(t,past) in a fixed positive interval, and choose a rational c>0. Then there is a compact polytope Q inside the positive class P=(c+S) intersect positive orthant, with c in the relative interior of Q, and constants a,b,C>0 and integer V0 such that for every integer V>=V0 divisible by the common denominator of c, the molecular jump process with density-dependent falling-factorial propensities, started at Z_0=Vc, satisfies

P(tau_Q <= exp(aV)) <= C V exp(-bV),

uniformly over all predictable rate controls. tau_Q is first concentration exit from Q. The stopped system is nonexplosive. There is no asserted stationary distribution, general jump-process permanence, stochastic entrance from outside Q, efficient construction, sharp exponent, or physical actuation advantage.

**Proof.** Fix N33's sufficiently small h, its finite affine lower envelope F_h(x)=min_lambda(r_lambda dot x+b_lambda(h)), and ceiling M_h=F_h(c). The N33 plateau K_h={F_h=M_h} is compact, convex and inside (sqrt(h),h^(-1/2))^d. At c every active slope is zero, hence every nonzero-slope inequality defining the plateau is strictly satisfied. Therefore c is in the (relative) interior of Q=K_h intersect (c+S). Represent its proper relative facets by nonneutral affine inequalities g_i(x)=r_i dot x+b_i(h)-M_h >=0, after removing constraints constant on c+S. On a point of facet i, label i is active; N33's weighted essential-source bound gives r_i dot f(x,k) >= delta_h >0 for every rate vector in its compact box. Each facet is compact, and the field is jointly continuous in x and k on a positive neighborhood; a finite subcover yields collars 0<=g_i(x)<=ell_i inside Q with r_i dot f(x,k)>=gamma_i>0 for every admissible vector. There are finitely many proper facets. For S={0}, Q={c}, all jumps are zero and the exit bound is trivial.

For molecular volume V, jumps are nu_e/V and intensity V k_e(t,past) phi^V_{y_e}(x), where phi^V_y(x)=V^(-|y|) product_j (Vx_j)_{y_j}. N33's Appendix proves a uniform O(1/V) difference from x^y on Q and hence strict actual inward drift for sufficiently large V. Its stopped exponential test functions exp(-theta_i V g_i) give

P(tau_Q <= T) <= sum_i exp(-theta_i V g_i(c)) + 2 m V Lambda T exp(-c_* V).

Here m is the number of proper facets, Lambda is a finite hazard bound, and theta_i,c_*>0 depend on the supplied Q/collars/rates. Since c lies strictly inside every facet, choosing T=exp(c_* V/2) gives the theorem. Stopping at first exit avoids asserting global jump-process nonexplosion. The proof *supplies existence of the stochastic appendix's previously assumed strict-facet certificate from N33's own plateau*, without supplying an efficient numerical compiler.

## Derived result B: different-span mixtures with directional active-mode coverage

**Theorem B.** Let G_j, j=1..J, be finite endotactic diagrams, possibly with different spans S_j and union span S=sum_j S_j. Construct one N33 finite affine family using all vertices and comparison directions of all diagrams. Write its slopes as r_lambda. Each mode field f_j(t,x) has rate coefficients within the fixed positive box. For measurable convex weights alpha_j>=0 summing to 1, suppose there is a common eta>0 such that for every finite slope r_lambda not in S^perp,

sum_{j: r_lambda not in S_j^perp} alpha_j(t) >= eta

for almost every t. Then every positive union class has a compact convex set common to all such trajectories, invariant and absorbing in a finite time uniform over rate/weight histories and compact initial ranges. The component spans need not coincide and some alpha_j may vanish identically.

**Proof.** Fix a common finite comparison family and small h. For each active r_lambda and every component j, N33's weighted inward lemma applied separately gives r_lambda dot f_j>=delta_h>0 if r_lambda not in S_j^perp, while r_lambda dot f_j=0 identically if r_lambda in S_j^perp. Uniform delta_h exists by finiteness. The weight condition gives the mixture projection >=eta delta_h for every active union-nonneutral slope. On P outside the plateau no union-neutral slope can be active, because its affine label is constant on P and at least M_h. Hence the a.e. finite-min chain rule yields strict growth outside the plateau; its compact invariance, continuation and scale iteration produce a common absorber, exactly as in N33, with delta_h replaced by eta delta_h. Rates and weights need no continuity. The N33 counterexample of permanent switching to 0<->A instead of 0<->B violates directional coverage and prevents union-class absorption. The theorem does *not* assert sliding-window or average-duty-cycle excitation; those require a separate temporal argument. The finite slope family is existential and the condition is not an implemented controller algorithm. If all alpha_j>=eta, the conclusion is also a straightforward application of N33 to the union; the useful distinct scope is allowing zero weights on modes redundant for each finite relevant normal.

## Derived result C: fully explicit controlled three-species stochastic bound

**Model and attribution:** Use Mislan's six-reaction ordinary endotactic, non-weakly-reversible, non-strongly-endotactic three-species synthetic fixture, published in his paper:

C -> A+C; 2A+C -> A+C; A+B -> A+2B; A+3B -> A+2B; B+C -> B+2C; B+3C -> B+2C.

With independent predictable k_i in [1/2,2], deterministic mean-field equations are a'=c(k1-k2 a^2), b'=ab(k3-k4 b^2), c'=bc(k5-k6 c^2). The operating box Q=[2/5,5/2]^3 is the established deterministic fixture; we contribute the **finite-copy predictable-control risk bound**, not the network or its deterministic solution. For V>=100 integer, start molecular counts (V,V,V), and let tau_Q be first jump of concentrations outside Q. Then for any T>=0:

**P(tau_Q <= T) <= 6 exp(-3V/20) + 435 V T exp(-V/160).**

**Analytic proof and rational certificates.** For each of six faces, define g_{i,L}=x_i-2/5 and g_{i,U}=5/2-x_i, with collar width 1/40. Birth/death scaled jump intensities:
A: birth k1 c, death k2 a(a-1/V)c;
B: birth k3 ab, death k4 ab(b-1/V)(b-2/V);
C: birth k5 bc, death k6 bc(c-1/V)(c-2/V).
With insufficient molecules, the death propensity is zero. Within any lower collar u<=17/40, favorable birth/adverse death >=1/(4u²)>=400/289. Within any upper collar u>=99/40 and all V>=100, favorable death/adverse birth >=(u-1/V)(u-2/V)/4 >=242063/160000 (A death even larger). Both exceed exp(1/4), as follows *without floating point*: exp(1/4)<=113/88<4/3<400/289, using n!>=2*3^(n-2) for n>=2. Also exp(1/4)-1<1/3. These imply the generator of Phi_i=exp(-Vg_i/4) is nonpositive on its own collar for all predictable controls. Beyond each collar but inside Q, Phi_i<=exp(-V/160), and the positive generator contribution is <=V Lambda exp(-V/160)/3. The total scaled hazard bound on Q is Lambda=435/2 by direct source-monomial evaluation at the upper box coordinate 5/2. Summing six Phi_i gives L Phi <=435 V exp(-V/160). At the start all six gaps are at least 3/5, so Phi_0<=6 exp(-3V/20); at exit at least one gap is negative, so Phi_tau>=1. All jumps are size 1/V along one coordinate and pre-exit hazards are bounded, so stopped compensation proves the displayed inequality, including corners and overshoots.

Consequently, at T=exp(V/320), the bound is 6 exp(-3V/20)+435V exp(-V/320). For V=10000 this is 1.1662366824850844e-7 and log10(T)=13.5717025595, in the **synthetic nondimensional** time units. Not a simulation, sharp rate, chemical lab result, or permanent safety guarantee.

## Scientific interpretation, gates and continuation

N33 provides a conceptual pathway from polyhedral asymptotic chemical permanence to predictable-control finite-copy safety. The six-reaction test shows a concrete analytic result on a network outside weak reversibility and strong endotacticity. However no kinetic parameter in that *synthetic* fixture has been measured; positivity of rates over astronomical nondimensional times, chemical thermodynamic admissibility, feed and energy demands, and biological relevance are **not established**. Equal-species scalar compartment mixing was already treated in Astra N54; unequal diffusion requires separate work. N173 contains a conditional all-face generalized log-kinetic theorem; neither generalizes physical kinetics without checking exact exponents/inputs. Astra N181 cautions that a deterministic permanent ODE need not yield a positive recurrent molecular jump process.

Stop asserting practical usefulness if the physical models fail those hypotheses.
