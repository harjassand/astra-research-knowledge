    - heading "6. Selection decision" [level=2]
    - paragraph: The strongest remaining opening is genotype-controlled expression and evolution of abiotic functional matter, but with a much stricter experimental and economic claim than initially proposed.
    - paragraph: Its decisive missing operation is not another DNA gate. It is an integrated physical process that produces many different, reproducibly functional material architectures from a shared reagent inventory, preserves their genotype associations, and outperforms existing separately programmed assembly at matched total cost.
    - paragraph: Removing genotype-linked spatial translation must destroy that advantage; otherwise the new apparatus is unnecessary.
    - paragraph: The route to a genuinely transformative capability would extend selected designs into durable inorganic, catalytic or electronic materials. That requires proving that the desired physical function survives transfer out of the DNA-supported environment. No such transfer has been established here.
