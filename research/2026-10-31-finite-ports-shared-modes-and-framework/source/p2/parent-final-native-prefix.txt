I constructed a **finite local interface that turns an imperfectly known exchange coupling into a uniformly opaque optical port, even while the uninitialized medium evolves rapidly and does not commute with that coupling**.

That port can supply an established quantum-interrogation entangling protocol. The new analysis establishes three things beyond a correct output bit:

- A bound on the **whole accepted parity instrument**, including coherence inside each parity sector.
- A stronger, task-specific bound for **producing a Bell resource**.
- A composition law for **repeated production with correlated memory histories**, including finite retries and explicit erasure flags.

The strongest justified capability is a source of moderately accurate entanglement with controlled failures and reuse. Its physical cost is substantial and explicit. These remain **root-working results in a qualified model**; the earlier force theorem, sector factorization, dirty-ancilla identities, and accepted foundations retain their existing status.

## 1. The interaction is local; parity is constructed

The interface uses a stationary system with computational states \(|0\rangle,|1\rangle\), an auxiliary state \(|e\rangle\), and a local optical mode \(b\). In units with \(\hbar=1\), its effective interaction is

\[
H_{\mathrm{tap}}(t)
=
I\otimes H_M(t)
+
\left(
|e\rangle\langle1|\,b
+
|1\rangle\langle e|\,b^\dagger
\right)\otimes G_M(t),
\]

with a qualified operator interval

\[
0<g_-I\le G_M(t)\le g_+I.
\]

A probe photon can exchange its excitation with the transition \(|1\rangle\leftrightarrow|e\rangle\). The computational state \(|0\rangle\) is dark. A separate safe optical rail bypasses that transition.

The physical requirements are now local:

1. A bright transition with a sign-definite coupling.
2. A dark transition protected by an actual selection rule.
3. Common memory evolution on the idle computational levels.
4. A way to remove auxiliary excitation into fresh outgoing modes.
5. A qualified optical controller for rotations between the safe and risky rails.

There is **no native two-data-qubit parity Hamiltonian** in this model. Two spatially separate nodes may couple to the same interacting medium; their couplings need not commute.

A concrete origin for the operator-valued coupling is a cavity mode evaluated at an uncertain quantum position. For example,

\[
G=g_0\cos(k\hat x)
\exp[-(\hat y^2+\hat z^2)/w^2].
\]

On a genuinely confined domain satisfying \(|kx|\le a<\pi/2\) and \(y^2+z^2\le R^2\),

\[
g_0\cos(a)e^{-R^2/w^2}I
\le G\le g_0I.
\]

The position, motion, and interactions can remain unknown. The position-dependent coupling has the standard cavity-QED form; state-insensitive trapping is also established machinery. Actual experiments provide approximate state independence, however, so neither a finite-depth trap nor a small measured differential shift automatically supplies the exact all-state qualification used here. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

This makes the remaining physical issue concrete. **The protected dark and idle branches matter as much as the bright coupling.** An uncontrolled logical-state-dependent bath potential on an idle level can accumulate throughout the operation.

The original signed spin handles, the positive-force apparatus, and this optical exchange interface remain distinct resource settings. The optical ports and their controller have not been derived from the signed spin handles.

## 2. The central lemma: opacity without a slow bath

Consider the active block spanned by

\[
|1,1_b\rangle,\qquad |e,0_b\rangle.
\]

Let \(U_\pm\) be the propagators generated by \(H_M(t)\pm G_M(t)\) during a tap of duration \(\tau\). The active propagator has the exact form

\[
U_{\mathrm{active}}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
A&B\\
B&A
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
A=\frac{U_++U_-}{2},
\quad
B=\frac{U_+-U_-}{2}.
\]

Here \(A\) is the amplitude for the photon to remain in the risky mode; \(B\) transfers it into the auxiliary excitation. Unitarity gives

\[
A^\dagger A+B^\dagger B=I.
\]

I derive the following uniform bound:

\[
\boxed{
\|A\|
\le
\max\!\left\{
|\cos(g_-\tau)|,\,
|\cos(g_+\tau)|
\right\}
}
\]

whenever

\[
0<g_-\tau\le g_+\tau<\pi.
\]

Choose

\[
\tau_*=\frac{\pi}{g_-+g_+}.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\|A\|\le
r_*=
\cos\!\left(\frac{\pi g_-}{g_-+g_+}\right)<1.
}
\]

Every initial memory/reference state therefore transfers at least probability \(1-r_*^2\) into the auxiliary level.

### Why rapid memory motion does not spoil this bound

Define the relative propagator

\[
W=U_-^\dagger U_+.
\]

Its evolution is

\[
\dot W
=
-2i\left(U_-^\dagger G_M U_-\right)W.
\]

The generator remains between \(2g_-I\) and \(2g_+I\), regardless of the strength of \(H_M\) or its commutator with \(G_M\). The continuously unwrapped eigenangles of \(W\) therefore stay in the corresponding angular interval. Since

\[
A^\dagger A
=
\frac{2I+W+W^\dagger}{4},
\]

the cosine bound follows.

**The inverse appearing in \(U_-^\dagger U_+\) is part of the proof. No physical segment reverses the bath.**

The theorem also allows finite switching ramps. Replace \(g_\pm\tau\) by the integrated bounds \(\Gamma_\pm=\int g_\pm(t)\,dt\), requiring \(0<\Gamma_-\le\Gamma_+<\pi\).

There is a useful local optimality statement. Within the first-return window, \(\tau_*\) minimizes the worst survival amplitude of a single fixed-duration tap. Scalar couplings at the two interval endpoints already impose the two cosine terms. This is an optimality statement for this one-tap mechanism, not a lower bound on arbitrary control.

The width of the qualification matters. If \(R=g_+/g_-\) is large,

\[
1-r_*\sim \frac{\pi^2}{2R^2}.
\]

A very broad coarse box makes the port weakly opaque and increases subsequent overhead. The box is a physical resource.

### Which imperfections can be absorbed into this lemma?

A bounded perturbation confined to the active photon–auxiliary block changes \(A\) by at most its integrated Hamiltonian norm. Thus a coarse bound

\[
\|A_{\mathrm{real}}\|\le r+\xi_\tau<1
\]

is sufficient. Auxiliary detuning or other active-channel errors need not be calibrated to the final logical accuracy; they may simply worsen the opacity constant.

That argument does not cover a hidden interaction on the quiet computational levels.

## 3. From local opacity to a full quantum measurement

I use ordinary quantum interrogation: weak optical rotations interleaved with visits to the absorber. Entangling separate objects with this mechanism is established work by Gilchrist, White, and Munro. Azuma also analyzed imperfect scalar absorbers and their leading convergence cost. Those constructions changed the choice of useful task here. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}

Let

\[
R_\theta=
\begin{pmatrix}
\cos\theta&-\sin\theta\\
\sin\theta&\cos\theta
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \theta=\phi/N.
\]

For computational state \(1\), a no-loss stage has the form

\[
T_j=\operatorname{diag}(V_j,A_j)(R_\theta\otimes I_M),
\qquad \|A_j\|\le r.
\]

For state \(0\), the stage is \(R_\theta\otimes V_j\). The \(V_j\) include the common forward bath evolution during the tap and drain windows.

Crucially, **every \(A_j\) and \(V_j\) may change at every visit**. The proof does not assume a fixed scalar attenuation, commuting memory operators, independently sampled bath states, or memory reset.

Write the bright product as

\[
T_N\cdots T_1=
\begin{pmatrix}
u&v\\
w&z
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad V=V_N\cdots V_1.
\]

With \(c=\cos\theta\), \(s=\sin\theta\), \(q=rc\), and
\(L=(1-q^N)/(1-q)\), I obtain

\[
\|u-V\|\le D,
\qquad
\|v\|\le\alpha,
\qquad
\|w\|\le\beta,
\]

where

\[
D=N(1-c)+\frac{rs^2}{1-q}(N-L),
\quad
\alpha=sL,
\quad
\beta=rsL.
\]

In particular,

\[
D=
\frac{\phi^2}{2N}\frac{1+r}{1-r}
+O(N^{-2}).
\]

These are bounds on memory operators, not expectations in a selected memory state.

For parity measurement, use \(\phi=\pi\) at each of two nodes, plus a bypass reference rail. Route residual risky-rail amplitude to outgoing reject ports. The accepted signal is within

\[
d=D_1+D_2
\]

of \(Z_1Z_2\otimes V\). The two detected branches satisfy

\[
\boxed{
\left\|K_\pm-\Pi_\pm\otimes V\right\|
\le \frac d2.
}
\]

This is the needed quantum statement. A device could report even parity correctly while leaving

\[
|00\rangle\otimes V_{00}|\psi\rangle
+
|11\rangle\otimes V_{11}|\psi\rangle,
\]

with distinguishable memory tags. The branch bound instead controls departure from a common memory operation inside each sector.

For \(d\le1\), the heralded failure probability is at most

\[
d-\frac{d^2}{2},
\]

and a full flagged-instrument half-diamond bound is

\[
\frac d{\sqrt2}
+
\frac12\left(d-\frac{d^2}{2}\right).
\]

The lost-photon branch remains physically important: absorption and draining can change a data atom from \(1\) to \(0\). A failure flag does not authorize retrying on unknown live data.

## 4. The stronger capability is a Bell-resource factory

For a fresh resource, the same machinery gives a better accuracy law.

Prepare two logical operands in \(|++\rangle\), start the probe in the safe rail, use \(\phi=\pi/2\) at each node, and accept only a final safe-rail photon. These logical preparations are charged resources; the medium remains uninitialized.

The exact accepted memory blocks are

\[
\begin{aligned}
F_{00}&=-V_2V_1,\\
F_{01}&=v_2V_1,\\
F_{10}&=-V_2w_1,\\
F_{11}&=u_2u_1+v_2w_1.
\end{aligned}
\]

The ideal successful output is

\[
|\Phi_-\rangle
=
\frac{-|00\rangle+|11\rangle}{\sqrt2},
\]

with probability one half.

The decisive identity concerns the whole accepted state. Let \(S\) be its success effect on the memory, and \(B\) the effect for success accompanied by a bad Bell projection. Then

\[
S=\frac14\sum_{x,y}F_{xy}^\dagger F_{xy},
\]

and exactly, without commuting any memory operators,

\[
\boxed{
B=
\frac{F_{01}^\dagger F_{01}+F_{10}^\dagger F_{10}}4
+
\frac{(F_{00}+F_{11})^\dagger(F_{00}+F_{11})}{8}.
}
\]

For equal node bounds, define

\[
d_B=2D+\alpha\beta,
\]

\[
s_*=\frac{1+(1-d_B)_+^2}{4},
\qquad
b_*=\frac{\alpha^2+\beta^2}{4}+\frac{d_B^2}{8}.
\]

It follows that

\[
S\ge s_*I,
\qquad
B\le b_*I\le \frac{b_*}{s_*}S.
\]

Consequently,

\[
\boxed{
\text{conditional Bell infidelity}
\le e_*=\frac{b_*}{s_*}
}
\]

for every initial memory/reference state.

This is stronger for the resource task:

\[
e_*=O(N^{-2}),
\qquad
s_*\longrightarrow \frac12.
\]

It does not make the coherent joint error quadratic; that remains on an \(O(N^{-1})\) scale. Small Bell infidelity and small diamond error must remain distinct.

The leading infidelity coefficient is also informative. For \(\phi=\pi/2\),

\[
C_B(r)=
\frac{\phi^2(1+r^2)}{2(1-r)^2}
+
\frac{\phi^4(1+r)^2}{4(1-r)^2}.
\]

The allowed scalar case \(A_j=rI\), \(V_j=I\) attains

\[
e_N=\frac{C_B(r)}{N^2}+o(N^{-2}).
\]

Thus this leading bound is sharp for the unfiltered architecture. The overhead is not merely an artifact of treating the memory adversarially.

## 5. Meter renewal is finite and explicitly paid

An auxiliary excitation is drained through

\[
H_{\mathrm{drain}}
=
I\otimes H_M
+
\left(
|0\rangle\langle e|\,d^\dagger
+
|e\rangle\langle0|\,d
\right)\otimes G_d.
\]

Each drain mode starts in vacuum and leaves after use.

A fine-calibrated complete swap is unnecessary. If each drain collision has residual amplitude at most \(r_d<1\), then \(m\) fresh collisions leave residual amplitude at most \(r_d^m\).

There is a useful support-sensitive bound here. Replacing the finite drain by a comparison isometry that completely empties the auxiliary level costs at most

\[
\sqrt2\,r_d^m
\]

on excited input and costs zero on idle input.

The second Bell-factory node can receive the probe in its risky rail. Including that initial transient, the total two-node drain error obeys

\[
\boxed{
\delta_d\le
\sqrt2\,r_d^m
\left[
\frac{2N\sin\theta}{1-r\cos\theta}
+
\frac{\cos\theta\,[1-(r\cos\theta)^N]}
     {1-r\cos\theta}
\right].
}
\]

The bracket stays bounded as \(N\) grows at fixed \(r<1\). This avoids charging a full-size reset error on every cycle, while retaining the initially risky second-node branch.

If the total additional joint-isometry error is \(\delta\), including finite drains and qualified optical-control errors, the Bell bounds become

\[
p_*=
\left(\sqrt{s_*}-\delta\right)_+^2,
\]

\[
e_*
\le
\min\!\left[
1,\,
\frac{(\sqrt{b_*}+\delta)^2}{p_*}
\right].
\]

Freshness is essential. Two complete coherent swaps with the same retained drain return the excitation with probability one. The outgoing boundary condition supplies the irreversibility.

A real one-photon source can use the same local transfer mechanism: prepare a separate emitter excited, couple it to an empty probe mode, then isolate it. A vacuum component lowers the heralding rate. Multiple photons, late emission, memory-dependent mode release, and false detector clicks require separate bounds. Excitation preparation and source reset remain physical operations.

## 6. What survives repeated use

The reusable statement can be separated from the particular optical circuit.

Suppose every attempt, after every allowed preceding history, satisfies

\[
S_h\ge p_*I,
\qquad
B_h\le e_*S_h.
\]

Failed attempts may change the memory arbitrarily. Fresh logical operands are genuinely reset or supplied before the next attempt.

Then

\[
\Pr(\text{no success in }k\text{ attempts})
\le (1-p_*)^k,
\]

and

\[
\mathbb E[\text{attempts until first success}]
\le\frac1{p_*}.
\]

The first returned pair has conditional infidelity at most \(e_*\).

The proof pulls \(B_h\le e_*S_h\) backwards through the completely positive map for each failure history, then sums over first-success times. It never replaces the retained medium by an independent bath sample.

There is a finite-program version. Cap production at \(k\) attempts and return an explicit erasure flag on exhaustion. Counting either a bad Bell projection or an erasure as a defect gives

\[
\Pr(\text{defect})
\le e_*+(1-p_*)^k.
\]

If earlier outputs are held untouched, the same argument gives, for every subset \(A\) of production locations,

\[
\boxed{
\Pr(\text{defects at every location in }A)
\le
\left[e_*+(1-p_*)^k\right]^{|A|}.
}
\]

This is a joint probability bound with correlated memory. It is not an assertion that the output pairs are independent, and it is not yet a complete circuit-level fault model. Arbitrary later postselection on the entire factory record can bias earlier errors.

Holding also remains explicit. Previously produced pairs must be isolated from later active ports, or their storage disturbance must be included. Moving pairs into storage, supplying fresh addressable operands, and any subsequent twirling, purification, or error correction require their actual operations.

## 7. A finite example, including the cost that remains

Take a qualified gain interval \([1,2]\), a tap-time uncertainty of \(5\%\) around \(\pi/3\), and use the conservative bounds

\[
r=r_d=0.6.
\]

The timing interval actually gives \(r<0.588\); the larger value provides margin. Use \(m=20\) fresh drain collisions per tap.

The following analytic bounds include finite drain renewal. They initially assume qualified exact optical rotations, ideal logical preparation and holding, and no false accepts.

For an illustrative complete heralding rate, additionally assume:

- A one-pulse source emission probability at least \(0.64\).
- State-independent detector efficiency \(0.9\).
- Balanced, memory-independent transmission \(0.999\) per complete interrogation cycle.

| Visits per node \(N\) | Conditional Bell infidelity bound | Tap/drain success lower bound | Mean source attempts upper bound |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 32 | 4.405% | 0.3722645 | 4.9721 |
| 64 | 0.987% | 0.4291261 | 4.5985 |
| 128 | 0.236% | 0.4624699 | 4.8499 |
| 256 | 0.0588% | 0.4804210 | 6.0316 |

These are model calculations, not measured device specifications.

At \(N=64\), each attempt uses:

- 128 tap windows.
- 2,560 fresh drain windows.
- One source window.
- Two logical input preparations.
- Optical rotations, routing, release, and readout.

That is **2,689 interaction windows per attempt**. If all interaction windows use the same upper duration \(1.05\pi/3\), their total is at most \(2,956.71/g_-\), before the additional operations and holding time.

Allowing a total optical-rotation isometry error of \(0.02\) changes the \(N=64\) bound to:

\[
e_*\le 0.0179344,
\]

with mean source attempts at most \(4.8927\) under the stated source and loss assumptions.

A cap of 61 attempts then makes the erasure probability less than \(8.77\times10^{-7}\). That finite cap permits up to 164,029 interaction windows per requested output, plus the other operations. The guarantee includes this duration cost.

### Calibration has moved to an ordinary controller

The medium’s fine gains and Hamiltonian do not need reconstruction. The optical controller still needs qualification.

For example, with the tap switched off, a probe sent through \(N/2\) copies of a constant coupler measures

\[
p=\sin^2(\alpha/2),
\qquad \alpha=N\theta.
\]

On a separately qualified monotone phase branch, elementary concentration bounds give the sufficient detected-probe count

\[
M\ge \frac{4\log(2/\zeta)}{\eta^2}
\]

for total-angle error \(\eta\) with failure probability \(\zeta\).

For \(\eta=0.01\), \(\zeta=0.01\), and \(N=64\), that is:

- 211,933 detected probes.
- 32 coupler passes per detected probe.
- 6,781,856 detected-probe passes.

Under the illustrative source and detection assumptions, the mean number of source preparations is about 379,910. This is a sufficient ordinary method, not a lower bound. It assumes a qualified phase branch, bounded preparation/readout bias, and stability through reuse.

Corrected robust phase estimation is a serious alternative for this **meter-only** controller. Its long coherent probes are physically distinguishable from a hypothetical coherent oracle for the dirty medium. Its survival probability, duration, and the 2021 correction must enter the comparison. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}

### Fixed optical loss prevents a free arbitrary-accuracy limit

With fixed transmission \(\eta_{\mathrm{loss}}<1\) per cycle, the two-node success probability carries

\[
\eta_{\mathrm{loss}}^{2N}.
\]

The scalar endpoint example requires \(N=\Omega(\epsilon^{-1/2})\) for direct Bell infidelity \(\epsilon\). Consequently, this unfiltered architecture has a worst-case expected-interrogation cost growing at least as

\[
\Omega\!\left(
\epsilon^{-1/2}
e^{c/\sqrt{\epsilon}}
\right)
\]

under fixed nonzero cycle loss.

That obstruction selects the useful scope: a finite-quality resource factory, followed by a separately supplied and qualified improvement layer—or a better native interface. Increasing \(N\) alone does not establish a practical route to arbitrary precision.
