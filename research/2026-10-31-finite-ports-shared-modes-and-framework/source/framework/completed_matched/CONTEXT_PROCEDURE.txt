Briefly separate the scientific target from the objects and assumptions introduced by the previous approach. Use its most informative failure to identify what the representation fails to retain. If that is a contingent limitation, construct a materially different representation that preserves the original contract and derive a new consequence from it. Existing successful objects may remain the best choice; novelty is not compulsory. Spend the main tranche on the construction and its hardest test, rather than an inventory of possible frameworks. Do not weaken the experimental contract or substitute a more convenient target.
