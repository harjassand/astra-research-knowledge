# What the hard-frontier experiment actually supports

30–31 October 2026, Brisbane; source freeze dated 30 October UTC. The matched experiment is complete: four initial research states, twelve continuations, two task families, twelve local researcher contexts. Two fresh assessors examined scientific copies with policy labels withheld. A separate exploratory two-constructor transfer attempt is ongoing and is not part of the matched comparison.

The main finding is negative for a blanket prompting change: **the added representation procedure did not consistently outperform an equally fresh ordinary expert.** Fresh ordinary constructors produced strong reformulations without it. Warm contexts also produced important, different results. The evidence supports conditional context and information decisions, rather than either a universal restart rule or a new-theory checklist. No result here establishes a repeatable 90+ discovery policy, an optimal orchestration policy, or a civilisation-changing breakthrough.

## The hypothesis and the actual experiment

The tested procedure was concrete: separate the scientific target from assumptions introduced by the previous representation; identify what the failed approach loses; construct a different object retaining that obligation; derive and challenge an actual consequence. Every comparison arm already had permission to change representations and build new theory. Thus the test was not permission versus prohibition, or ordinary competence versus an artificially weak baseline.

Two independent ordinary constructors first attacked each of two real unresolved Astra frontiers:

* The exceptional s=1/3 nonhomogeneous quartic ROC/QC face, where four nontranslation cubic modes prevent applying the already completed generic rigidity theorem. Known factorisation, kernel dimensions and general Schur/moment criteria were supplied.
* Quantum entanglement using P2's original signed native handles, arbitrary retained correlations and only first local bath-motion control. Both initial constructors proved the privately randomized, exactly known-gain subproblem. Their sufficient costs were enormous. Under the predeclared solved-target rule, all subsequent physical continuations received the same harder unknown-static-gain extension, without calibration or higher bath regularity.

For EACH earned starting state, three continuations got the same factual capsule, proofs/code and predecessor rights:

W: retain the warm researcher. F: fresh ordinary expert. R: equally fresh expert plus the explicit representation procedure. All four starting states were retained, including successes. F versus R tests that procedure package within fresh contexts. W versus F tests restarting with compression and loss of conversational working state; it cannot isolate pure freshness.

All constructors used the inherited local model/effort without substitution. They were not native ChatGPT Pro. Each phase requested a substantive 15–25-minute tranche; exact hidden compute was not enforceably equal. There were sixteen matched research tranches, not twelve independent discoveries or tasks.

Task-first retrieval of the local canonical Capital index surfaced them. This was my source-preparation error. I preserved the initial packet, corrected attribution and gave ALL physical continuation arms the identical exact predecessor supplement before comparison. One neutral physical return had already completed when this was discovered. This limits the initial phase's source completeness and prevents treating it as discovery of general sign projection. The common supplement is not a controlled test of retrieval benefit. F and R still receive identical scientific sources; W/F remains reset plus compression, with the common information addition explicitly recorded. No native production policy was changed to rescue the study.

The corrective action is concrete: retrieve the strongest governing predecessor and its real failed boundary before freezing an experimental problem or a fresh handoff. Use task-first search and read the decisive proof, rather than rely on memory, a card title or a long archive dump. This is the coordinator's information-preparation job; Capital remains a fast integrator/retrieval architect, not a new scientific approval gate.

## Original synthesis from the programme data

The newest natural returns contradict a blanket diagnosis that Pro cannot reformulate. P1 now represents the decision's disclosure footprint rather than only a coupling; P2 constructs a full quantum instrument instead of merely calibrating a unitary; P3 acquires signed coordinates AND an original-variable observation order. Dots replaces an invalid noncommuting basin argument while keeping its actual noisy machine; Ultra replaces an insufficient cubic-moment span with positive covariance exchange. These are observed mathematical choices, not inferred hidden reasoning.

The distinction that matters is **freedom in the proof versus freedom in the receiving contract**. A proof representation can change radically while preserving the actual operation or phenomenon. Adding an unavailable physical interface can also define a legitimate new architecture, but the new theorem does not construct that interface. P2's positive parity force illustrates that remaining burden. P3's small-table access is different from global circuit admission. P1's decomposition acquisition and full disclosure contract still matter. Dots' continued success is especially informative where it changes the proof objects without replacing the actual generator by a convenient virtual machine.

Another useful check was attempted recombination. The warm conformal non-PC interval ends at about 0.187662, whereas the new proved global QC family begins at about 0.197169. Their parameter ranges do NOT overlap. Combining their titles would falsely claim a new non-PC QC example in that gap. Exact interface/range checks are an actual coordination operation, not a request for researchers to fill forms.

The better candidate policy is conditional:

* Preserve warm ownership when a construction is accumulating useful machinery and moving a consequential obstruction.
* Use a genuinely fresh ordinary constructor when independence can reveal a different construction, especially after a demonstrated method barrier. A compact scientific state is different from replaying an entire failed search history.
* Use the representation audit selectively to expose a specific contingent assumption. This study does not justify appending it to every prompt.
* Share an actual compatible construction or counterexample sparsely. New shared vocabulary, agent agreement and slot occupancy are not synthesis.
* Reassess the opportunity when complete success would still have limited reach, or serious distinct attacks leave the same central dependency untouched without new leverage. Preserve fundamental depth when a promising mechanism is genuinely developing; no immediate device demo is required.

These are provisional decisions, not a proven optimal policy. A local construction comparison does not test whether we select civilisation-level opportunities, whether a new physical theory is needed, or whether long-lived native Pro trajectories respond the same way. The scientific ambition remains fixed. There is no evidence that prompt/context tuning alone guarantees revolutionary discoveries on demand, and a correct restricted theorem is not relabelled 90+ to make a policy look successful.

Current production follow-ups are ordinary warm scientific continuations, separate from these treatments. No additional Pro run was used; Dots was not messaged or interrupted. Root continues source-based study of those natural returns without claiming a causal experiment from them.
