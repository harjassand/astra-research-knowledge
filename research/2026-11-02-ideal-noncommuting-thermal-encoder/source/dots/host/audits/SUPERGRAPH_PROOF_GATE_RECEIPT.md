# Independent supergraph proof-transfer gate

2026-11-01. **Conditional PASS for a compatible bounded-degree pair of supergraphs; not a literal application of the published theorem to arbitrary independently chosen supergraphs.** Actual hierarchical-host/filling certification and filtered-parent perturbation estimates remain separate gates.

Primary inspected source: De Roeck–Khemani–Li–O'Dea–Rakovszky, arXiv:2411.02384v3, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.02384v3. PDF and extracted full text are saved in this directory. Read the graph definitions, theorem hypotheses, word formalism, ghost estimate, relative boundedness, locality transfer and both norm-conversion proofs. No access to 2411.01002v3 was attempted.

## 1. Precise extension, with no Hamiltonian change

Keep exactly the original qubits Λ, actual nonidentity check list E, check supports, incidence sets supp_c(x), stabilizer group, projectors and Hamiltonian. No dummy check, fictitious ancilla, changed interaction, or physical shortcut gate is inserted.

Choose a graph Gc on E containing the original overlap graph and of uniformly bounded maximum degree D. Every qubit must meet a check. Define Gq on Λ to contain:

- the clique on each actual check support;
- all edges between the supports of each adjacent pair of Gc checks.

Then Gq has degree at most wq wc(D+1), up to an inessential subtraction of one. Both graphs have uniform exponential ball growth, with a new constant κ chosen from their actual degrees, not automatically the old wc wq bound.

This paired completion gives two essential facts:

(A) If M is Gq-connected, ext(M)=union_{x in M} supp_c(x) is Gc-connected, and |ext(M)|≤wq|M|. For an edge xy introduced from checks α,β, ext(x) and ext(y) contain α and β respectively; each incidence set is already a clique in the old overlap graph.

(B) If S is Gc-connected, the union of its actual qubit supports is Gq-connected and has at most wc|S| vertices. Adjacent check supports are directly joined by construction.

Merely enlarging both graphs independently need not imply (A) or (B). For example an extra edge between disjoint-support checks makes their check pair connected without making their qubit union connected in the unchanged qubit graph. This breaks the source Appendix B.2 connectivity argument, though not necessarily a spectral theorem obtainable by another route.

Physical finite-range perturbations can be admitted by first adding to Gc all pairs of incident checks associated to each desired physical qubit-neighbor edge. Bounded physical degree and LDPC incidence keep D bounded. The derived Gq then contains the physical graph. Geometry must supply a uniform bound on physical degree (including bounded-density assumptions), rather than infer it from the phrase 'three-dimensional'.

## 2. Required TQO hypothesis

Require in the enlarged Gc metric that every connected S with |S|<d_tilde=Theta(d) obeys

    {g in G: supp(g) subset supp(S)} subset group generated by checks
    at Gc-distance at most ell |S| from S,

with uniform ell. TQO-I remains the actual code-distance statement. Enlarging edges makes more sets connected, so TQO-II does not follow simply from the old condition by monotonicity. The proposed stronger anchored filling theorem for ALL weight-m stabilizers would suffice: such g has weight at most wc|S|, and an anchored O(log(1+m)) check-radius bound is at most ell|S| after adjusting a fixed constant. The host must bound distances to the ORIGINAL support/check set, not merely give small unanchored diameter. Disconnected physical support must be included.

Distance d*=min(d/wc,d_tilde) must still dominate c log N. This condition is unaffected by adding metric edges but is not replaced by a small host diameter.

## 3. Audit of every proof location using the metric

- Source §2: replace the defined overlap metrics by Gc,Gq and use their new ball-growth κ. Actual wq,wc remain incidence bounds. Perturbation locality is measured with the new Gq connected covers. This is a theorem extension, since the source explicitly fixes its original overlap graphs.
- §3.1–3.2: word classes, projectors, excitation bookkeeping and disjoint-word commutation retain the original incidence definitions. Added graph edges do not alter any of these algebraic identities. In particular disjoint-word commutation follows from the cores' true check incidences and commuting check projectors, not from the absence of a newly introduced graph edge.
- §3.3: the initial Pauli decomposition uses connected M and ext(M). Fact (A) proves the required word connectivity without enlarging actual support or replacing checks.
- §3.4 and Appendix B.1: |ext(M)|≤wq|M| and counting qubits within a Gq ball around a check still hold. The intensive Pauli-to-word estimate follows with the new κ. A direct derivation groups by r=|M(p)| and sums wc exp(κr) anchors against exp(−κ' r), avoiding any ambiguity in the displayed summation index in the source.
- §3.5: actual support size is bounded by wc|S|; TQO-I and the stipulated enlarged-metric TQO-II apply. No assertion that added edges are physical is needed.
- §§4,6,7: the KAM algebra and norm estimates use word sizes, intersections, excitation counts and unions of intersecting connected words. Such unions remain connected in Gc. New edges do not create new terms in algebraic commutators; overestimating connected admissible sets is harmless.
- §5.1: the only metric-sensitive ghost estimate uses the number of centers within radius ell r of a check and the TQO-II representation. That number is bounded using the new κ. All Pauli support counting stays wc r, not the enlarged neighborhood volume. The claim needed is physical support inside supp(S); one need not assert that every Pauli created by check projectors has literal check-incidence set inside S.
- §8: relative boundedness uses precisely the same enlarged TQO neighborhood and the exponential ball-count sum. It does not require adjacent checks to share qubits. The energy projectors remain actual checks; no artificial edge contributes energy or a gap.
- Appendix B.2 and §4.8: fact (B) converts connected word size to connected qubit-cover size and to distance. The resulting locality statement concerns Gq, not Euclidean distance. A manifestly safe conversion is

      |||O|||_mu <= wq ||O||_{wc(mu+log 4)}
                 <= ||O||_{wc(mu+log 4)+log(max(1,wq))}.

  This follows by expanding each word operator on at most wc|S| qubits. It avoids relying on potentially suppressed factors of wc in the source's displayed final constant. The spectral proof needs no sharp reverse-conversion constant.

The later example-specific Appendix A is not needed once the actual TQO-II hypothesis is independently established. Section 10's optional physical consequences do not supply additional spectral-proof assumptions and do not turn shortcut locality into physical locality.

## 4. What conclusion is earned, and what is not

Under these explicit graph, distance, TQO-II and intensive-smallness hypotheses, the source proof gives a uniform low-energy band gap and exponentially small ground-band width for the unchanged stabilizer Hamiltonian perturbed by sufficiently small graph-local terms. Constants must be recomputed from the new κ and ell; no claim of the original numerical threshold survives automatically. The ground band retains its original multiplicity; it is not in general an exactly degenerate ground eigenspace.

Fixed-body physical terms with bounded local incidence and constant connected Gq covers have finite intensive Pauli/word norms: expand only their constant-size supports, pay the constant Pauli coefficient count and word-projector count, and charge all cover-anchor multiplicities. A small global operator norm is neither assumed nor needed. Quasilocal filtered terms need an actual uniform convergent intensive-norm estimate; finite physical range of the original V alone does not establish it for an arbitrary filtered parent.

In particular this receipt does NOT yet prove: the hierarchical-host construction, an actual filtered-stabilizer parent gap, efficient quantum Gibbs preparation, logical-input protection, a local physical implementation of shortcut edges, or a new physical gap theorem without the stated extension. The parent application must identify its unperturbed projector Hamiltonian, prove the parent difference is small in the required norm uniformly in N and filtering time/parameters, and check multiplicity/sign-sector assumptions. Those are substantive remaining joins, not automatic consequences of this metric audit.
