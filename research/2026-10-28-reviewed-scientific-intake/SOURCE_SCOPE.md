# Governing scientific scopes

The completed curator source reviews are retained verbatim. Their actual
proof/audit versions, prior exposure and non-replay limitations govern; source
integrity and software CI are not new theorem or device acceptance.

* Exact serial CP/two-copositive maps retain arbitrary finite references,
  sufficient2(d-1), varying factors and no TP/unital premise. No bypass,
  optimality or broader cone proof is imported.
* Positive-star/full-Bose design retains strict interlacing, all bands, ideal
  scalar contacts and charged precision/stiffness. Compliance-only stiff
  escape is nonattainment at original mass, while joint-cap positive examples
  and exact optima have separate branches. Normalized identities retain m=K=1.
* Gaussian results distinguish strict noise-only and joint-source inverses,
  one-step weak/law results and acquired capped channels. Child gauges,
  full joint laws, reserve covariance, precision and every-execution caps
  are actual premises. Neither marginals nor covariance alone imply recursive
  law closure; architecture-specific obstructions are not general lower bounds.
* Predictive results preserve preparation versus test coordinates, supplied
  rank/stationarity/calibration, certified positivity and paid starts. Resetting
  read/global-abort and nondestructive absorbing-memory topologies differ.
  Rare-history and clocked reuse costs are charged. Matched synthetic losses,
  old source-call undercounts and missing historical diagnostic dependencies
  remain visible; N687/N952 remain NOT_ACHIEVED.
* Ladder density, local outgoing pole, ordered weak-g linewidth, coherent
  compensation and the finite all-sector form-stability box are distinct.
  A continued pole is not a nonreal eigenvalue of a self-adjoint Hamiltonian.
  Norm-defined positive radii are not computed tolerances; density alone is
  not a pole theorem. C31's sign/domain corrections retain original bytes.
* Conditional hopping transport retains complete rational norm inputs,
  actual current factors, every continuous exclusion cell and inherited
  ordered limits. It does not regenerate heavy norms or establish physical
  acquisition. Diagonal-model-error bounds retain their constant-hopping
  class; sufficient error budgets are not necessary hardware limits.
* Local readout uses the unrestricted full-rank Gibbs family and independent
  detector calibration, with independent pairs paid. N711 is not imported.
  State closeness alone does not control parameter derivatives. Current
  U712 v2 explicitly corrects necessity to sufficiency, with historical v1
  and current correction context. A reversible-refresh mean-procedure bound
  is not a bound on all trajectory information or all metrology.
* The quartic separator excludes the specified constant-SPD/closed-quadratic
  two-form/bilinear-SOS proof class with exact symmetries. It does not settle
  quasiconvexity, Morrey, all hierarchies or every nonlinear diffusion.

The method subset, both descriptive score judgments, full-cost unknowns and
N33 b91/bbe review-version gap retain their own narrower scopes. No measured
device, historical priority or practical advantage is acquired by publication.
