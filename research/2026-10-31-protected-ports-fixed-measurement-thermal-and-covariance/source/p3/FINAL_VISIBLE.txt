8. The consequential opening now isolated

The completed capability is a constructive reduction from an explicit interacting quantum model to acquired collective coordinates with a certified temporal information budget, coupled to a valid interacting conditional solver.

The unresolved computational problem is now specific: evaluate the retained, potentially multimodal Gaussian normalizer substantially better than the worst-case grid, while preserving absolute error and paying for every conditional solve. The present proof exposes that cost rather than concealing it in a sampling or integration oracle.
