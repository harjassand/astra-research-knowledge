Consequence and comparison

An equally informed conventional singlet-only controller may use the same Hamiltonian identification, Floquet response, filtered impedance and screened geometry. Ordinary exchange or capacitive gates may prove faster and simpler. Existing cavity gates already provide ideal hot-bus closure. Optical double-heralded entanglement remains a serious alternative with physical emission, local rotations, detectors and loss converted into retries; its waiting-time storage and source-renewal costs must also be counted. Barrett–Kok.

No hardware advantage follows from the present segment count or 
135
135 ns ideal duration.

The structural advance is more specific: a physically meaningful central source can preserve a quantum representation while interactions let that source remove information from the accompanying degrees of freedom. Charge motion through the source need not itself be a logical error. Two-sided screening then supplies a full incident-charge bound without demanding that neighboring blocks remain valid.

The missing bridge is now sharper. The active operation still selects a renewed gauge, so its usable regime depends on long source exposure, symmetry qualification and oscillator nonlinearities. The stronger next target is a logical interaction that acts on the permutation representation across a substantially larger gauge space, allowing the source to remain useful without placing frequent high-accuracy renewal on the critical path. Exact orbital permutations supply a finite set of such protected actions; a continuous entangling operation with that scope remains to be constructed.

The proofs, exact algebra certificates, executable counterexamples and numerical component checks are preserved in the linked work. They establish a reusable protection-and-accessibility law and identify the physical quantities that must improve before claiming a complete computational operating region.
