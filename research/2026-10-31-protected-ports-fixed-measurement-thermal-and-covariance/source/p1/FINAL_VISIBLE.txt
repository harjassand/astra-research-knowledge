P1 result: when a fixed measurement list can replace adaptive experimentation

I developed a constructive theorem about obtaining nearly the prediction accuracy of an arbitrary adaptive experimenter without constructing its decision tree.

The construction exchanges additional measurements for freedom from adaptive planning. Its central condition is precise: the target must have low interaction order, and the effects of individual coordinates must not be too concentrated on rare events. For quadratic targets of independent unbiased bits, that second condition holds automatically.

The work now includes a general proof, a stronger quadratic bound, executable representation and acquisition procedures, and a counterexample showing why the regularity condition matters.

8. Established machinery and the unresolved priority question

The construction uses classical ingredients. The source comparison is substantive:

Primary work	What it contributes to the assessment
Schramm–Steif revealment bounds	The closest methodological ancestry: query probabilities control Fourier mass, including an approximate-determination extension.
Golovin–Krause adaptive submodularity and Chen–Waggoner informational substitutes	Strong adaptive-selection guarantees under diminishing-returns conditions. Quadratic parity does not generally satisfy those conditions.
Gupta–Nagarajan–Singla stochastic probing	Established adaptivity-gap methods for submodular and XOS objectives, with different objective contracts.
Feldman–Vondrák junta approximation	Existing influence-based representation theory that must be considered when assessing the ranked-list result.
Eskenazis–Ivanisvili low-degree learning	A strong acquisition alternative under bounded-range and absolute-error assumptions.

The precise sorted-influence risk/budget statements were derived here and reconstructed independently. I have not established historical priority. A focused source search is insufficient to certify it.

The ordinary comparator is also preserved inside the theorem: it ranges over every adaptive policy with the stated information and budget. No weak planner is required to obtain the guarantee.

9. What was executed, challenged, and preserved

The final replay passed all six included scripts. The principal checks include:

60 exact rational degree-two/three models, each evaluated on all 128 inputs, with 480 top-influence sets checking the proof identities.
20 rational categorical pairwise models, checking the compiler against direct product-space calculations, including every fixed subset.
Exact fixed-set and adaptive-path checks for small tree witnesses, and ordinary knapsack checks for the rare-gate designs.
Exact reconstruction of a selected conditional predictor from Boolean evaluations.
A median-of-means diagnostic that explicitly paid 22,304 scalar calls; its maximum relative score error was about 0.06953. This is one finite diagnostic, not a confidence proof or an efficiency result.
An independent exact check of the implemented predictor and noisy-signal identity on 256 latent/noise assignments, all 16 fixed subsets, and a stopped adaptive policy.

Independent reviews covered the main theorem, its zero-risk and infimum cases, approximate ranking, the sharper quadratic bound, unequal costs, noisy channels, acquisition, precision, and the same-budget mixture argument. The corrections they identified are preserved.

The useful failures are preserved too. A shared-core/sign-pattern construction failed because a strong fixed design could complete many interactions. A generic predictive-state proposal left the acquisition and planning problem unresolved. A read-once reconstruction opening had substantial existing formula-learning antecedents. Those were retained as research evidence rather than promoted.

The resulting principle is:

Low interaction order, together with controlled concentration of coordinate effects, permits an explicit fixed design to approach the prediction accuracy of arbitrary adaptive computation after a quantified increase in measurements. Rare concentrated effects can make that increase necessarily large.

The next consequential requirement is now concrete: acquire a useful model and observation law economically, and tighten selection enough that the measurement increase is affordable. The present work establishes the structural guarantee and its obstruction; it does not yet establish that operational advantage.
