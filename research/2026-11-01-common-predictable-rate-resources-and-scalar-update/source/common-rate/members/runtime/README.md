## Files and receiving semantics

- `common_box.py`: standalone raw-box recognizer/scalar constructor; no candidate
  imports or external dependencies. Same-author canonical certificate and externally request-bound resource
  budget receivers are **not independent implementations**.
- `RateGuard`: typed in-process model-action admission; invalid vectors keep the
  previous positive admitted rates. It does not certify future predictability,
  physical calibration, actuator mappings, or resistance to private-state mutation.
- `RiskLedger`: positive rational risk reservation, rejecting overspend without
  resetting counts or granting unreceived episodes a guarantee.
- `resource_budget`: needs only a received upper bound on total count, not the
  full state or Psi; actual complete immigration counting plus an initial bound
  can earn this observation contract. Commands are not observations. It acquires
  actual binary count/firing levels for rational confidence,
  no independent-set enumeration or huge final-power expansion. Both ordinary
  and common-Foster caps and their elementary dyadic receipts are retained.
- `fixture_certificate.json`, `raw_box.json`, `resource_budget_examples.json`:
  canonical exact data. Fractions use tagged hexadecimal numerator/denominator
  pairs; large integers use tagged hexadecimal. Byte costs are real and charged.
- `foundation/`: byte-identical retained proof components and controlling historical
  source-access quarantine. Their scope and prior status are not enlarged.

`receive_wire` budgets bytes, tokens, depth, expanded tree nodes, scalar bits and
strings, and checks a canonical roundtrip. `receive_resource_budget` requires the
original client count-upper-bound/horizon/failure as external keyword arguments. Pointwise
profile evaluation separately budgets face masks, power exponents and rational
power bits. Subsequent exact rational operations, gcd and serialization also cost
work. Quantitative refusal does not negate qualitative raw-box matching. No claim
of efficient full Psi evaluation is made; its literal face sum can be exponential.
