1 November 2026. Solo analytic proof candidate; independent audit and

Claim at its actual scope
========================
Assume the code family and density/confinement lemma in
../tanner_global_phase_20261101/DENSITY_LEMMA.txt. There is a static,
bounded-graph-local Hamiltonian on n data and 8n controller qubits whose
ONLY nominal Lindblad jumps are 8n ordinary one-qubit lowering operators.
All rates and interaction neighborhoods are finite constants independent
of n and storage time. For sufficiently small, fixed local-generator
perturbation density, this model protects all k=Omega(n) logical qubits
for time exponential in n, in joint diamond norm with the controllers and
an arbitrary finite persistent bath. Controllers may start in ANY state,
including correlations with the logical input, bath, and reference.

This is a generator-model theorem candidate, not a laboratory realization
or a few-body Hamiltonian compiler. The large syndrome-dependent
Hamiltonian and expanding connectivity remain explicit resources.

A. Data inputs, selected instrument, and notation
================================================
Use the original notation c=Delta^2, n=c m/2, M=C_R n sector/centers,
C_R=8/c, and commuting integer potential U=U_X+U_Z. Every local U_a is
between 0 and c; every data qubit meets four such factors. The original
density lemma supplies constants s,rho>0 such that, on the safe syndrome
domain below, there are >=s U active sector/centers, and w<=c U.

At center v, Pi_vF selects the FIRST strictly improving nonempty subset F
in the original fixed ordering. J_vF=Q_F Pi_vF. Thus

 Pi_vF Pi_vH=0 (F!=H),  J_vF^dagger J_vF=Pi_vF,
 Pi_v0=I-sum_(F!=0)Pi_vF.                               (A1)

The Q_F are the original pure-X or pure-Z Pauli writes, of weight <=c.
The data support of the whole center table is <=R_0=c+2c^2.
No coherent sum of the J_vF is substituted for their instrument.

For r=floor(rho n), let P select data syndromes with minimum SUMMED CSS
filling weight w<r, including every logical coordinate and every
controller/bath state. Fix the original minimum-filling Pauli decoder and
tie-breaking. For all sufficiently large n, 2r+Q<d, where Q is the fixed
support bound specified in C. Multiplying a Q-qubit Pauli changes w by
at most 2Q. The original recovery-frame argument therefore makes every
safe-to-safe Q-local system operator scalar on the whole logical space.
Controllers and the persistent bath belong to the auxiliary subsystem.

B. Actual physical-model generator: loss and a static Hamiltonian
=================================================================
Give each center its own c record qubits, with computational basis |F>
indexed by subsets F of Q(v); |0> is the empty record. Define

 a_F=sqrt(|F|/c),
 S_v=sum_(F!=0) a_F J_vF tensor |F><0|,
 T_v=S_v+S_v^dagger,   N_v=sum_(j=1)^c n_vj,
 epsilon=g/Gamma,      nu=1/2.

S_v^2=0 and S_v^dagger S_v=sum_F (|F|/c)Pi_vF tensor |0><0|<=I.
Its ground/excited supports are orthogonal, so ||T_v||<=1, rather than
the number of branch labels. The nominal generator is

 L_phys = -i[nu Gamma sum_v N_v + g sum_v T_v, .]
          + Gamma sum_(v,j) D[sigma^-_vj] + L_B(t) + V(t),             (B1)

 V=sum_a V_a,   sum_a ||V_a(t)||_diamond <= delta n.                   (B2)

Each V_a preserves Hermiticity, acts on <=q DATA OR CONTROLLER qubits
and the same arbitrary finite B, and is identity on the other qubits.
Choose q large enough to cover a whole center support when including
arbitrary errors of the specified Hamiltonian couplers as single terms.
The full generator, rather than each signed V_a, must be GKSL/CPTP.
Bath-only L_B is free in the norm budget. Initial logical/C/B/reference
correlations are unrestricted; the input data are ideally encoded.
There is no fault-path probability model or time-variation budget.

There are M c=8n controller qubits and exactly 8n nominal loss channels.
The model is active repair at leading order. It is not the older completed
proposal instrument, and no inactive dephasing term is silently added.

C. A finite-depth LOCAL change of representation
================================================
Let D_v be sum_j D[sigma^-_vj]-i nu[N_v,.], at unit Gamma. Its unique
controller stationary state is |0><0|. On a data/controller coupling set

 G_v = integral_0^infinity exp(t D_v^*)(T_v) dt.                       (C1)

This Hermitian integral converges, D_v^*G_v=-T_v, and (G_v)_00=0.
For c independent amplitude-damping qubits the diamond distance to the
reset channel is <=4c exp(-t/2); free record rotation changes no norm.
For example this follows by the one-qubit Kraus formula, the bound
||AD_t-Reset||_diamond<=4 exp(-t/2), and tensor telescoping. Consequently

                         ||G_v|| <= M_G:=8c.                         (C2)

The support is unchanged: the same R_0 data and the same c record qubits.
The ground-to-F block, including detuning, is

 (G_v)_F0 = a_F J_vF / [ |F|(1/2-i nu) ].                            (C3)

Spectator record bits create additional EXCITED-to-EXCITED blocks in G_v.
They must be retained to obtain the FULL identity D_v^*G_v=-T_v.
An explicit coefficient for |F union A><A|, A disjoint from F, is

 a_F J_vF sum_(j=0)^|A| (-1)^j binom(|A|,j)
                        / (|F|/2+j-i nu |F|).                      (C4)

Form the intersection graph of the center supports, including records.
Its maximum degree z is finite: both original view size and incidence are
fixed. Properly color it with ell=z+1 colors. Same-color supports are
disjoint. Set

 W(epsilon)= product_(colors in a fixed order)
                product_(v of that color) exp(-i epsilon G_v).       (C5)

This is an ANALYTICAL UNITARY FRAME, not a physical pulse, circuit,
schedule, or reset applied to the memory. Its finite depth makes every
conjugated local generator exactly local, even at nonzero epsilon.

Here are deliberately loose, explicit constants that avoid an implicit
limit in n. Let h=R_0+c, Q_0=h^(ell+1), L_g=ell Q_0, and choose

 Q=max(Q_0, q h^ell), I_ov=(z+1)^(ell+2),
 B_3=64(c+1)(1+4M_G L_g)^3,
 C=2^16(1+c)^2(1+Q)^2(1+I_ov)(1+B_3)^2.                            (C6)

Q bounds physical support after conjugation; I_ov bounds how many center
cones contain any given record qubit. These bounds can be increased.
A color layer grows a support of size a by at most a factor h. At most
L_g gates occur in a primitive center's cone. Up to third derivatives of
its conjugated generator have diamond norm bounded by B_3 at unit Gamma
for |epsilon|<=1. Indeed each left/right unitary-conjugation derivative
costs <=2M_G; the product rule costs at most (4M_G L_g)^j; and the
unconjugated center has norm <=3c+2|epsilon|. This proves the stated
Taylor/remainder bound in the full CB norm, with no bath dimension.

D. Exact first-order cancellation and the second-order ground block
===================================================================
Use adjoint superoperators A_v(X)=i[G_v,X], H_v(X)=i[T_v,X]. On EVERY
data observable X (which may be global and include the safe projector),

 D_v^* A_v(X)=-H_v(X),   D_u^* A_v(X)=0 for u!=v.                    (D1)

Thus the coefficient of epsilon in (Ad_W L_phys Ad_Wdagger)^*(X) is
zero on ALL controller states, not just after tracing ground records.
The order-zero fast generator also vanishes on X.

For one center the coefficient of Gamma epsilon^2 on X is

                  (1/2)D_v^* A_v^2(X) + H_v A_v(X).                 (D2)

Sandwiching by the controller ground state kills its first term.
Using (C3), the remaining ground block is

 -<0|[T_v,[G_v,X]]|0>
 = k_0 [ sum_F D[J_vF]^*(X) - i nu[sum_F Pi_vF,X] ],
 k_0=4/[c(1+4nu^2)]=2/c.                                           (D3)

The imaginary part is the adjoint action of a syndrome Hamiltonian
-nu k_0 sum_F Pi_vF. It commutes with U, P, and the logical subsystem;
it is not an uncharged generic coherent error.

Different-center second-order terms contain one G/T from each of TWO
distinct controller registers. Their local all-ground blocks vanish.
Terms with two G_v and D_u vanish unless u=v. The color ordering may add
commutators of different G_v; these also have zero all-ground blocks.
Consequently, for EACH primitive center v, the second Taylor coefficient
minus its (D3) data generator has zero local record-ground block. The
remaining terms have support in its finite cone. No assertion that the
FULL second-order remainder is small on excited controllers is made.

Define the finite target rate

                  kappa = k_0 Gamma epsilon^2
                         =2 g^2/(c Gamma).                         (D4)

The symbolic controls check (D1)-(D3) on a full data operator basis, with
three distinct branch outcomes; a second control includes noncommuting,
overlapping X- and Z-repair couplers and the ordered frame (C5).

E. Why finite response is absorbed, rather than multiplied by n*time
===================================================================
Write L'=Ad_W L_phys Ad_Wdagger. For parameters chosen in G use

 F=exp(beta U+lambda N),   N=sum_v N_v,
 beta=1/(16cQ), 0<lambda<=1/(4Q), F_P=P F P.

The genuine killed comparison is K(X)=P L'(PXP)P. It is CP and trace
nonincreasing: compress retained jumps but retain their FULL escape
operators. Never normalize the surviving state. It factors as logical
identity times a syndrome/controller/bath process, by A and locality.

For any Q-local map R, split the exponent into potential factors meeting
its support and all remaining factors. All factors commute. The far
factor is outside the map's support and cancels from

 F^(-1/2) R^*(F^(1/2) X F^(1/2)) F^(-1/2).                         (E1)

This holds even when X=P is global. The near exponent has width at most
4 beta c Q+lambda Q<=1/2, so this weighted map has CB norm <2||R||.
Its derivative with respect to lambda is bounded by 4Q||R||. Compressing
by P changes neither bound. This also holds with the same arbitrary B.

The fast controller has EXACT negative drift

 Gamma sum_v D_v^*(F_P)
       =-Gamma(1-exp(-lambda)) N F_P.                              (E2)

The effective data part from (D3) has drift

       <=-gamma U F_P,  gamma=kappa s(1-exp(-beta)).                (E3)

This uses the original density lemma. Repair targets outside P only
strengthen killing. Its syndrome Hamiltonian contributes zero.

Here is the control of the actual remainder. At lambda=0 the first
Taylor coefficient applied to P exp(beta U) is EXACTLY zero. At nonzero
lambda its weighted norm is <=a fixed constant times Gamma epsilon
lambda per center, by (E1). The second coefficient minus (D3), at
lambda=0, is a Hermitian operator R_v of bounded weighted norm and has
zero local controller-ground block, by D. If its weighted norm is <=D_0
and Q_v denotes a non-ground record somewhere in its cone, then, for
any eta>0, the elementary two-block Schur bound gives

               R_v <= eta I+(D_0+D_0^2/eta) Q_v.                   (E4)

Proof: write a vector as ground plus excited; bound its cross term by
2D_0||x_0||||x_1||<=eta||x_0||^2+D_0^2||x_1||^2/eta, and its excited
diagonal by D_0||x_1||^2. Also Q_v<=sum_(j in cone_v)n_j, hence
sum_v Q_v<=I_ov N. The change from lambda=0 costs O(lambda), not a
volume exponential. The third Taylor remainder costs O(Gamma epsilon^3)
per center. These statements all apply to the GLOBAL input X=P in (E1).

Using (C6), summing the three bounds, and writing Gamma epsilon^2
=c kappa/2, yields the explicit sufficient operator inequality

 K^*(F_P) <= { kappa C[eta+lambda/epsilon+lambda+epsilon] n+3delta n
              -gamma U
              -[Gamma lambda/2-kappa C(1+1/eta)]N } F_P.            (E5)

Here 0<epsilon<=1, 0<eta<=1, lambda<=1/(4Q). The constant in (C6)
dominates the Taylor norms, c/2 conversion, lambda derivatives, center
density <=8, Schur square, and cone overlap; it was chosen for that
purpose, not estimated from the finite controls. The extra factor 3 on
noise is a convenient enlargement of the weighted bound <2. Signed
perturbation terms are legitimate because the FULL K is CP/TNI.

Set

 A=16 C(1+1/eta), lambda=A epsilon^2, A epsilon<=1.

Then Gamma lambda/2=c A kappa/4, so (E5) implies

 K^*(F_P) <=[a n-gamma U-mu N] F_P,
 a=kappa C[eta+(A+2)epsilon]+3delta,  mu=A kappa/8.                  (E6)

The excited-controller remainder has been paid for by the ACTUAL loss

approximation error proportional to n*time. The physical boundary term in

I. Boundary Duhamel estimate and joint logical conclusion
========================================================
Let P_b select safe data syndromes with w>=r-2Q. Confinement gives
U>=(r-2Q)/c there. For sufficiently large n, r-2Q>=rho n/2. Equations
F-H then give, for ALL THREE compressed proof segments,

 Tr(P_b sigma)<=exp[-beta(r-2Q)/c+b_0 n]<=exp(-b n),
                         b=beta rho/(4c)>0.                        (I1)

For a local generator term R, actual-minus-compressed action vanishes
on (P-P_b) X (P-P_b). With trace<=1 the omitted part of a positive state
has trace norm <=3sqrt(Tr(P_b sigma)); the difference map has norm
<=2||R||. Thus boundary Duhamel costs <=6||R||exp(-bn/2). Use the
actual CPTP suffix for contraction, including a reference and B. No
whole-history norm expansion is taken.

Each dressed nominal center has norm <=3Gamma c+2g, including its bare
record Hamiltonian. Bath-only dynamics have zero boundary action. Put

                     A_phys=C_R(3Gamma c+2g)+delta.                 (I2)

Telescoping the initial frame path, actual transformed storage, and final
frame path against their compressed processes gives total diamond
distance <=6n[A_phys T+256epsilon]exp(-bn/2). The physical composition is
EXACTLY Ad_Wdagger (Ad_W Phi_phys,T Ad_Wdagger) Ad_W=Phi_phys,T.
The compressed composition followed by the ordinary fixed DATA decoder
is Id_logical tensor Lambda_CB^0, CP/TNI. Its missing trace is an

 inf_(Lambda_CB CPTP)
 ||(Dec_data tensor Id_CB) Phi_phys,T (Enc_data tensor Id_CB)
                         -Id_all-k tensor Lambda_CB||_diamond
 <=min{2, 12n[A_phys T+256epsilon] exp(-bn/2)}.                      (I3)

The norm includes arbitrary initial logical/C/B/reference correlations;
the auxiliary map may depend on the actual generator and T, but not on

For example, choosing T_n=exp(bn/4)/[24 A_phys n] makes (I3) tend to zero
exponentially, including its constant frame-path term. All k=Omega(n)

J. Scope and proof checks
=========================
The finite controls verify exact algebra on small fixtures and diagnose
the full finite-speed weighted operator, including overlapping X/Z
couplers, a common bath, coherent noise, thermal gain and a mixed jump.
They do not establish the asymptotic theorem by extrapolation. The
analytic argument A-I is the proof candidate; it inherits the original
Tanner density lemma and family assumptions without re-proving them.

No claim of a few-body/static-Hamiltonian compilation, low-dimensional
layout, useful threshold, controlled microscopic reservoir derivation,
fault-tolerant acquisition/readout, priority, or practical advantage is
made. The precise new interface is finite-speed noisy branch controllers
with one-body loss, conditional on the explicitly specified Hamiltonian
and elementary GKSL loss resources. See RESOURCES_AND_BASELINE.txt.
