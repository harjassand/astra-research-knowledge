WHAT THE CONTROLLER EXTENSION EARNS AND WHAT IT STILL ASSUMES
1 November 2026. Read together with PROOF.txt and the preserved Tanner
family/density proof. No hardware, account, external research checkout,
or other chat was changed.

Concrete resource change
========================
The older selected-center ACTIVE generator could require as many as
8n(2^c-1)/c independently recorded, data-dependent jump operators. Their
total escape was <=kappa per center, not kappa per branch. That correction
to the older instrument remains essential and is preserved unchanged.

The new nominal model has:
  n data qubits;
  8n record/controller qubits, grouped as c bits at each of 8n/c centers;
  exactly 8n one-qubit lowering channels at rate Gamma;
  one record number Hamiltonian with gap omega=Gamma/2 on each bit;
  8n/c syndrome-dependent Hermitian couplers g T_v, ||T_v||<=1.

The coupler has <=c+2c^2 data and c controller support. Its branch weights
sqrt(|F|/c) equalize the leading correction rates, including the finite
record detuning. Bit-resolved loss records keep the leading F outcomes
separate. At finite Gamma the fields carry more complicated histories;
the proof covers that full process. One coherent reset is different and has
excited dark states. Independent bit loss has only the empty record as
its common dark state, and includes every intermediate flag pattern.

The actual protection statement is for the FULL model at FINITE
Gamma,g, with noisy controllers, not just its eliminated master equation.
No per-center completion/dephasing, perfect startup reset, healthy exterior,
or global recovery schedule is added. Controller initial states may be
arbitrary and correlated with the logical input and the persistent bath.
Initial data encoding and the final fixed data decoder remain ideal
mathematical interfaces, as in the earlier proof.

Rates, precision, and thermal excitation are paid for
====================================================
For a chosen effective repair scale kappa and epsilon=g/Gamma,

 Gamma=c kappa/(2epsilon^2),  g=c kappa/(2epsilon), omega=Gamma/2.

The proof chooses epsilon a very small but FIXED constant; it does not
send Gamma to infinity with n or storage time. This is an existential
finite-response result. Its explicit conservative constants are immense.
Calling them constants does not make them good engineering parameters.

All data/controller perturbations, including coherent miscalibration,
share one generator-norm density budget delta n. Examples of charges:

 * An error E_v in a center Hamiltonian costs <=2||E_v||. Thus a uniform
   relative coupler error f costs <=2 f g C_R per data qubit in density.
 * Replacing sqrt(Gamma) sigma^- by sqrt(Gamma)(sigma^-+E) costs
   <=Gamma(4||E||+2||E||^2) per channel. All 8n channels are charged.
 * Independent upward jumps sqrt(gamma_up) sigma^+ on all records cost
   <=16 gamma_up n. They are allowed when this and all other errors fit
   delta_* in PROOF.txt. There is no uncharged finite-temperature floor.
 * Errors of different branch coefficients must be combined into the
   actual local Hamiltonian/generator norm. An error tolerance per one
   of exponentially many coefficients is not a tolerance for the whole
   center table.

If a LOCAL thermal-gain model has gamma_up/Gamma=exp(-hbar omega/k_B T),
then 16Gamma exp(-hbar Gamma/(2k_B T))<=delta_* is a sufficient budget
condition before charging the other noise. This translates the assumed
one-qubit rates to a temperature scale. It is NOT a microscopic derivation
of those rates for the interacting Hamiltonian, or a claim that the full
device is in equilibrium. Any actual bath/pump realization has to meet
the local-channel accuracy budget at the specified finite bandwidth.

Explicit reset activity, rather than a free reservoir
=====================================================
Let N be the PHYSICAL record excitation number. Nominal lowering activity
is A_down(T)=Gamma integral_0^T <N> dt. When the named lowering channels
remain identifiable terms in the actual device, this is their expected
emitted-quantum count. With signed miscalibration differences it is a
nominal activity metric; the actual jump implementation must be specified
before interpreting it as a measured photon/phonon count.

A useful full-model bound does not require the analytical frame. At a
center, i[T_v,N_v] is ground/excited off-diagonal with norm <=c. Young's
inequality gives

 i g[T_v,N_v] <=(Gamma/2) N_v + (2g^2 c^2/Gamma) I.

For the signed perturbations in PROOF.txt, total trace preservation gives
V^*(I)=0. Splitting N into the <=q local record numbers and its complement
then bounds ||V^*(N)||<=2q delta n, even if the individual V_a are not
trace preserving. The far terms cancel in the sum. Hence

 d<N>/dt <=-(Gamma/2)<N> + (16c g^2/Gamma+2qdelta)n,

 A_down(T) <=2<N(0)>+(32c g^2/Gamma+4qdelta)nT
           <=16n+(16c^2 kappa+4qdelta)nT.                            (R1)

The cruder instantaneous ceiling is 8Gamma n. Thus the model does not
hide an infinite number of reset events in a finite time or require a
perfectly reinitialized c-bit register before each proposal. The emitted
fields provide entropy removal and branch information continuously.

Equation (R1) counts activity. It is not a free-energy, refrigerator,
power, or wall-plug estimate. A physically supplied loss channel needs
its reservoir spectral/correlation properties, cooling/pump hardware,
noise, heat removal and energy per reset characterized. If its qualified
cost per emitted quantum is E_loss and its idle power is P_idle per bit,
one can charge those supplied values to A_down(T) and 8nT respectively.
Neither value is acquired here. For a local bath with a bare gap, assigning
hbar omega per loss is only its bare-transition accounting; it does not
determine the work associated with the noncommuting coupler or the whole
interacting device. A microscopic/detailed-balance claim would need more.

Hamiltonian complexity and geometry: still the bottleneck
==========================================================
The T_v are arbitrary large, syndrome-dependent local tables. They have
up to 2^c-1 branch terms, and the projectors themselves depend on a large
neighborhood. The transformation replaces data-dependent MULTIBODY LOSS
by one-body loss plus a specified multibody Hamiltonian. It does not
replace the table by a compact two-qubit-gate or two-body-Hamiltonian
device. Expanding T_v into Pauli strings is not such a synthesis.

The finite-depth unitary W in the proof is not a physical compiler. Its
colors do not supply a scheduled recovery circuit. A perturbative gadget
or finite-duration circuit with a global error O(nT epsilon) would not
automatically preserve the exponential lifetime; its own local/global
controller certificate would have to close this same interface.

The Tanner input still needs sufficiently large fixed Delta and unspecified
primary base-code existence thresholds. The previous large-Delta controls
do not acquire useful concrete base codes or a hardware threshold. The
controller constants worsen that situation. The graph is expanding and
requires long-range connectivity at fixed density in ordinary 2D/3D space.
No bounded wiring length, propagation latency, bus noise, or thermodynamic
advantage follows from bounded graph degree.

Strong scheduled baseline, kept at its actual scope
==================================================
Bharti--Haug--Khu--Tanggara, Single-Shot Error Correction at Optimal
Spacetime Cost, https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03684v1 :
Section 2.2 uses an input-independent local-stochastic fault set. Fault
operations may be coherent and share an environment; coherent amplitudes
across different fault paths, including a small overrotation at every
location, are outside that model. Sections 3.1 and 3.5 give constant-depth
Tanner recovery, O(N) locations/live qubits/classical work per cycle,
counted decoder waits, and a 2AS exp(-gamma N) logical diamond bound.
Section 3.6 addresses destructive logical readout and distinguishes final
classical conversion from quantum waits. Reliable parallel classical
processing and declared connectivity/latency remain assumptions.

This baseline therefore supplies a serious costed scheduled alternative.
The new opening is robustness of a STATIC joint data/controller generator
to continuous local coherent and dissipative perturbations, not a better
proven resource scaling or a claim that scheduled QEC lacks coherent-fault
analysis. Our acquisition/readout and Hamiltonian-synthesis costs remain
less developed. No practical advantage over this baseline is established.

Related primary context, without a novelty assertion
=====================================================
Zanardi--Marshall--Campos Venuti, Dissipative Universal Lindbladian
Simulation, https://arxiv.org/html/1506.04311, directly constructs effective
Lindbladians with coherently coupled amplitude-damped qubits. Its
Proposition 1 is a controlled strong-dissipation simulation limit on a
scaled time interval. Kashyap--Styliaris--Mouradian--Cirac--Trivedi,
https://arxiv.org/html/2404.11081, analyzes the same basic Hamiltonian-plus-
loss simulator with hardware errors. Propositions 5 and 6 concern
geometrically local observables and, for time-uniform precision, their
rapid mixing. Neither statement is itself the required joint logical
diamond-norm memory theorem. They are closer existing results than a
claim that replacing jumps by damped ancillas is a new mechanism.

Reiter--Sorensen, Effective operator formalism for open quantum systems,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.2806, and Kessler, Generalized Schrieffer-Wolff
Formalism for Dissipative Systems, https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.5440, provide
standard perturbative/elimination context. Lossy ancillas and effective
repair are not new on their own. Pastawski--Clemente--Cirac,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.2901, already proposed engineered-dissipation
quantum memories. These sources were selectively consulted, not subjected
to a comprehensive priority review. The candidate's substantive step is
the direct finite-speed, all-time positive-moment transfer and its
boundary/control treatment, conditional on the preserved Tanner lemma.

Additional scope checks: https://arxiv.org/html/2308.16233v2 distinguishes
global Poisson recovery bottlenecks from local decoders. This construction
has 8n/c parallel centers, not one fixed-rate global recovery event.
https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38139v1 studies autonomous local inference with
perfect syndromes in the code-capacity setting. Its abstract and stated
scope do not supply a noisy quantum controller for (B1).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20537 establishes coherent-error code-capacity
thresholds for qLDPC codes; such a capacity result is not claimed new here
and does not alone imply a continual autonomous storage phase. These are
selective comparisons, not a comprehensive priority determination.

Next decisive physical question
===============================
A specified bounded-arity controller architecture must evaluate the table
and implement the coupler, with its own finite propagation and fault
spread, while preserving a local positive certificate. Alternatively, a
different code with a compact beneficial local rule could remove the
table at the source. Another small rate improvement does not close this
gap. No such qualified architecture is claimed by this extension.
