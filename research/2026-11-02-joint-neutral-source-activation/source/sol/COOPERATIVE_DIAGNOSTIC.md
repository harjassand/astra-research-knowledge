# A diagnostic that forces joint creation rather than a pinned controller

Species are (A,B,C,D). There is unit immigration and linear death at A. The internal conservative reversible pairs are

    2B <-> B+D,
    2D <-> B+D,
    B+D <-> 2C,
    2C <-> A+C.

Every rate is one EXCEPT 2B->B+D has constant two. No inventories are frozen. B and D must first mix, their joint source creates C, and C creates A. The support closures are B->D->C->A, D->B->C->A, C->A, and A itself. The uncatalyzed theorem applies.

The catalytic diagnostic translates EVERY internal source/product above by A, keeping the same port. Its total internal degree is three and its exact factor is A times the displayed quadratic residual rates. The entire A=0 face is inactive and infinite. The catalytic theorem explicitly pays the first real birth, actual cooperative amplification and every loss of the gate. In particular A+2B and A+2D do NOT directly increase A, and 3A is absent, so the accepted direct-amplification A-catalytic family does not automatically supply this construction.

## Strongest exact ordinary calibration

At all unit internal rates, both lists are complex balanced at c=(1,1,1,1); Anderson–Craciun–Kurtz therefore already give the product-Poisson law restricted to each actual irreducible class. That admitted locus is an ordinary baseline, not new recurrence evidence.

At the stated rate two, the internal complex graph is a reversible TREE. Complex balance on such a graph forces each edge flux to balance. The first pair requires c_D/c_B=2, while the second requires c_B/c_D=1, a contradiction. This proves failure of that exact ordinary Poisson premise, without asserting that all other established methods fail. There are five internal complexes, one internal linkage and rank three; adding the port gives seven complexes, two linkages and rank four: deficiency one. Translation by the common A leaves those quantities unchanged.

The true class containing a large B-only launch is not assumed to contain zero. Weak reversibility closes actual classes, and the global return contract closes their finite head trace. Trapped low-population classes, when present, keep their own scope.

The N1567 terminal-gap cone fails on the B-only divergence face: outside coordinates A,C,D are zero. In the uncatalyzed list the only available internal complex is 2B, and no allowed source has a larger B weight. In the catalytic list no internal complex is available at A=0, while the port linkage carries no B difference. The full stoichiometric span has rank four, so this divergence face is admissible. These failures are refusals of that sufficient architecture, not instability.

## Why a convenient pointwise creation correction does not substitute

Suppose a linear function phi=w_A A+w_B B+w_C C+w_D D had strictly positive quadratic creation drift on the entire A=0 face. At (0,n,0,0), its leading internal drift is

    2(w_D−w_B)n(n−1),

so positivity requires w_D>w_B. At (0,0,0,n) it is

    (w_B−w_D)n(n−1),

requiring the opposite inequality. No such linear creation correction exists. This does not prohibit nonlinear Lyapunov functions; the finite-time support method supplies the complete paid alternative.

In the translated model, all internal rates vanish at A=0, so the seeding cost cannot be hidden in a pointwise interior estimate. After the seed, the same two incompatible pure residual corners force cooperative B/D dynamics. Both versions have several actual unbounded inventories and no ordered source chain or +1/−k growth/degradation anchor.

The fixture is evidence for the acquired operation, not the programme's endpoint. The theorem's full scope is arbitrary finite dimension/degree and arbitrary conservative residual graphs satisfying the raw support condition, with arbitrary positive fixed rates. General weak reversibility outside that condition remains unresolved.
