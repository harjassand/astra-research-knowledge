## What materially changed

P3 has converted its field-uniform high-frequency covariance bound into an explicit conditional algorithm. For acquired `J = D0 + S + AA^T`, with `S` positive semidefinite of norm at most kappa and bounded-rank collective `AA^T` of norm at most Lambda, the contraction premise is `alpha = kappa chi_M + 4 Lambda/Omega < 1`. Alternating Gaussian augmentation and independent whole-spin cyclic-path updates have claimed spectral gap at least `1-alpha` and relative-entropy contraction at most alpha. This removes time-mesh size from the number of sweeps needed for contraction. Every sweep still pays for every bulk/fast coordinate and all time slices; total runtime is not mesh-independent.

The normalizer construction now has an explicit AIS second-moment bound using invariant intermediate kernels, without presuming intermediate equilibrium. The extensive refinement bounds energy variance by O(n) at fixed physical parameters and supports an O(n)-stage schedule. The completed small execution uses the earlier range-based schedule instead; the stronger extensive constants are less attractive on that fixture. Refining an AIS grid alone does not repair an identity kernel, as the retained counterexample demonstrates.

The thermal-state consequence earns a previously missing closure property: arbitrary compatible longitudinal pins preserve the covariance premise and tractable conditional path calculation. It uses the correct potential-outside symmetric product. Equality of unpinned traces does not imply equality of pinned probabilities. The operator-log error and mesh bounds distinguish bounded row strength from bounded spectral norms; the latter can require the more conservative O(n/sqrt(epsilon)) physical mesh.

P3 also obtains a sharp interface bound for positive prefix-mass estimates. With `m` bits and multiplicative log error eta, the resulting state error is at most `2 sqrt(1-sech(eta/2)^(2m)) <= sqrt(m) eta`. A product construction attains the order of this dependence. This improves the precision requirement for this particular prefix interface. It is not a lower bound against every state-preparation method. A frozen random tape and amplification accommodate all coherent prefixes with polynomial overhead, provided numerical errors receive the same stringent allocation. Quantum gates, reversible computation and a high-precision pinned counter are part of the construction; no quantum device or implemented quantum circuit is supplied.

## Executed result and comparisons

The full high-frequency method was executed on a constructed, acquired 12-spin connected frustrated model, with rank-one collective coupling and 415 integrated bulk/fast coordinates. Rational acquisition checks recover the specially constructed representation; this is not experimental acquisition or a general robust matrix-completion theorem. The negative triangle rules out a ferromagnetic gauge and the nonidentical parameters rule out the earlier exchangeable simplification.

The outward interval for log Z is `[9.6952767305, 9.7512047715]`, radius about 0.027964. Its 95.2084% confidence is conditional on the independent-uniform-word and arithmetic contract. A seeded pseudorandom stream does not itself prove independence. The main run used 16,965,200 sweeps and 450.55 seconds on two CPU threads. The reported deterministic assembly replay recomputed the certificate, not new trajectories; the coordinator did not rerun it.

The paid full spectral comparator gives `[9.72316227986329, 9.72316232656688]` in 38.70 seconds. It is decisively better on this finite instance. This does not settle the asymptotic comparison with exponential spectral computation. The earlier static-guide certificate misses the requested radius, but its actual bias is smaller than that radius; failure of that certificate is not an impossibility theorem for static approximations. Conventional whole-path Glauber, informed by the acquired representation and bounds, also has mesh-independent sweeps and receives the extensive AIS refinement. No asymptotic superiority of alternating Gaussian over this equally informed method is established.

## Established knowledge and retained contribution

[Margossian, Zhong and Mukherjee](https://arxiv.org/html/2110.10801v2) already supply Gaussian augmentation, whole-spin conditional sampling and low-rank Ising/Potts approaches. Their Theorems 5, 8 and 9 have distinct high-temperature, general and special Curie-Weiss scopes. P3 should not relabel this architecture as its invention. Its contribution is the qualified quantum high-frequency conditional construction, contraction/closure receiving and associated cost theorem.

[Vuong, Theorem 33](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.02079) already reduces positive thermal-state preparation to pinned partition functions, freezes the tape, unions over prefixes and uses normalized child masses with controlled rotations. P3's quantum-state architecture follows this established route; its acquired-class application, pinning premise, sharp prefix precision and associated accounting are the new claimed contributions. [Grover and Rudolph](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0208112) provide earlier distribution-to-superposition ancestry. None of these constructions implies quantum speedup merely from producing a circuit description; [Herbert](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.02240) gives a relevant warning in a specific Monte Carlo setting, not a universal prohibition.

The pinning counterexample is particularly useful: a three-atom feature law has variance at most 4/7 under every tilt of that feature, yet a compatible event pin gives variance 9/4. Restricted feature tilts alone cannot support arbitrary constrained outputs. P3's stronger arbitrary-longitudinal-field premise is therefore load-bearing. The useful general research observation is to test closure under the operations required by the desired output, rather than infer it from a successful unconstrained marginal.
