# Exact target gates from approximate finite gates: modified CP cavities
30 October 2026. Root construction candidate, independent review pending. This receives finite reset residuals without assuming fresh pure physical ancillas.

## Contract and construction
Use the accepted finite scheduled CP-cavity theorem. At location j, the intended finite-duration isolated channel is G_j, generated by its actual bounded intended generator. The computational TARGET channel is U_j, also CPTP on the same block, with ||G_j-U_j||_diamond<=delta_j. The target channel is a proof reference; it need not have an exact finite-time dissipative implementation. Let Delta_j=G_j-U_j. Intended unitary gates can have delta_j=0.

The original cavity T^R makes every selected location j in R exactly isolated from all system/bath disturbance during that location. Hence its G_j can be factored to the end of that interval. Replace it there by U_j, giving a modified cavity T_hat^R. This is CPTP: during the selected interval the complementary cavity generator is legitimate, and the inserted U_j is CPTP. At all unselected locations the original actual evolution remains. In particular T_hat^empty=T_actual.

The cavity's complementary generator does not depend on the selected intended generator: its partial trace contribution is zero by trace preservation. Replacing G_j by U_j therefore does not change that complementary generator. Repeated selected uses of the same site at different times are distinct wire-channel insertions. The factorization is temporal, not a claim that projections at different times commute.

Define E_hat_F by the same Möbius inversion of T_hat^R. Then sum_F E_hat_F=T_actual, and each E_hat_F lies in the ordinary linear fault class F with every other location its EXACT target U_j. Complete bath-only histories remain unrestricted as in the accepted bridge.

## Expansion at the already isolated locations
For A subset R let T^{R;Delta_A} denote the original cavity with G_j replaced by Delta_j at each j in A, leaving G_j at the other selected locations. This map need not be CP. Multilinearity of the isolated gate slots gives the exact identity

 T_hat^R=sum_(A subset R)(-1)^|A| T^{R;Delta_A}.

For a required location set S, the inclusive modified fault map is therefore

 sum_(F contains S)E_hat_F
 =sum_(R subset S)(-1)^|R| T_hat^R
 =sum_(A subset S) sum_(B subset S\A)(-1)^|B| T^{A union B;Delta_A}.   (AG1)

There is no approximation or independence claim in this identity.

## Bound the inner finite difference with A fixed
Force A to remain cavity locations. During their intervals use the complementary CP generator and put Delta_j into their isolated system slot at the endpoint. Interpolate the remaining cavities indexed by S\A exactly as in the accepted proof. All propagators between the fixed Delta_j insertions remain CPTP contractions, since the fixed-A projection and every convex combination of additional cavities are legitimate. Every Delta_j contributes norm at most delta_j once.

The mixed derivatives with respect to the remaining cavity variables have the same local incidence bound: fixed-A projections are contractions, cannot create new system support, and do not introduce differentiation labels. The ordered set-partition Duhamel bound is unchanged, with the fixed gate slots simply dividing its time integrals. Its norm is at most

 (product_(j in A)delta_j) x^|S\A|,
 x=[2*3^(q-1)*g*eta*tau]^(1/q),

with tau the largest ACTUAL intended location duration, including preparation. Summing AG1 gives

 ||sum_(F contains S)E_hat_F||_diamond
 <=sum_(A subset S)(product_A delta_j)x^|S\A|
 =product_(j in S)(x+delta_j).                          (AG2)

Thus a uniform delta gives target-gate local strength x+delta. No total-device or total-runtime penalty is introduced. All maps are complete, so arbitrary incoming correlations of dirty targets with the rest of the apparatus are included.

A proof audit should check the temporal factorization for its exact declared schedule. The simplest application is the strictly layered disjoint-block schedule already sufficient for the ordinary circuit alternative. This note does not assert the result for overlapping autonomous interactions.

## Actual finite dirty-target preparation
For one physical target bit or qubit, use the fixed onsite reset generator kappa(R-I), where R(rho)=|0><0| tensor Tr_target rho. Its nominal duration-tau_R map is

 G_R=e^(-kappa tau_R)I+(1-e^(-kappa tau_R))R.

Hence delta_R<=2e^(-kappa tau_R) in diamond norm, even for a target initially entangled with an arbitrary retained bath. The physical weak perturbations during this reset interval are already part of the accepted cavity noise bound; no independent residual-error assumption is added.

For a chosen positive fault-tolerance target strength x_star, first choose a finite tau_R so delta_R<x_star/2, with fixed kappa. Keep the gate family and schedule durations fixed, then choose eta small enough that x<x_star/2. This gives a strictly positive local-noise regime for finite preparation from arbitrary dirty targets. Its threshold may be very unfavorable. The reset must be an actually supplied onsite dissipative primitive, not a gratuitous entropy sink assumed for free.

This does not remove the unknown raw-latch ingress floor: a reset prepares a KNOWN state and intentionally destroys prior information. Nor does it provide an exact unencoded final detector, noisy external clock qualification, autonomous scheduling, actual fault-tolerant gadget correctness, or a historical novelty claim. It closes only the approximate finite-gate/preparation interface into the ordinary target-gate fault expansion.
