The noise theorem now connects to standard fault-tolerant computation, including finite resets of noisy auxiliary systems. We also have a reviewed scheduled route from encoded-state preparation into native memory, plus selectable CNOTs between banks without resetting their running controllers. Logical measurement and physical feedforward now have scoped proofs. Their full integration while protecting the remaining quantum data is still being completed. Chemistry gained broader stability certificates and guarantees for correlated population dynamics; the general weak-reversibility problem remains open. The ZIP contains proofs, programs, costs and failed routes. These are mathematical constructions under explicit assumptions, not demonstrated hardware or practical advantage. Research continues.
