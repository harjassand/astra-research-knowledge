# A checkable raw realization, and the uncolored obstruction

30 October 2026. Local acquisition design only; no integrated recurrence theorem.

The frozen-ordinary hypothesis is not earned by calling an arbitrary particle a token. In an uncolored mass-action phase chain, a transition enabled for an ordinary particle can change the ordinary fiber before restoration. Such changes must be rejected by the present rule or separately analyzed.

A sufficient raw certificate uses an ACTUAL conserved finite-control coordinate. Partition species into flagged F_p and ordinary X_i, with an integral conservation law sum_p F_p=1 on the selected closed class. Every residual reaction must preserve this law. Distinguish a reference flag state F_D. The class restriction sum F=1 is essential, not a statement about all initial states.

The following finite grammar makes the local hypotheses directly checkable:

* Entry: F_D -> F_p, optionally with a catalyst unchanged on both sides. No ordinary count changes.
* Internal phase: F_p -> F_q, with any declared unchanged catalyst; no ordinary count changes.
* Own restoration: F_p -> F_D, again no ordinary change.
* Exchange restoration: F_p+X_i -> X_j+F_D. This changes exactly one ordinary label i->j at the actual terminal event.
* Other exits may be retained as terminal marked outputs, but must not be silently collapsed to ordinary exchange or discarded.

There are no other internal reactions enabled while the flag is away from D, unless their ordinary action is separately certified as null. With exactly one flag, actual phase p is finite and ordinary counts are frozen until an exchange or other terminal exit. The exchange mark has exactly per-label rate k_{pij} (or a checked nonnegative frozen-count factor). Thus the marked uphill reward is sum_j k_{pij} 1{r(j)>r(i)}. This is read from actual products, not inferred from a pre-jump phase name. Entries and internal phase rates supply the actual finite killed Q_y. The preceding reward-potential rules now apply literally.

Acquisition is finite for a supplied finite species partition: check the flag conservation vector against every stoichiometric increment; check the selected class has flag total1; classify each reaction by its flag source/output and ordinary increment; reject all internal ordinary changes and all nonterminal multi-label changes; then construct Q_y and marked rewards exactly. A search can enumerate candidate nonempty flag subsets and reference states, or use conserved-support candidates. Enumeration may be expensive, but is sound and finite. Bounds on Q_y and ingress remain mathematical certificate obligations, not consequences of the grammar alone.

This avoids fictional physical distinguishability. It also does not claim to extend the accepted current raw compiler: its protected coordinate was not an actual conserved one-flag species system, and the connector/seed/total-count/signed-cycle interfaces for this new grammar are not established here. With no open population reactions such flagged examples may have only finite communicating classes, so mere realizability carries no recurrence significance by itself. The useful conclusion is a checkable local interface that can be tested in a genuinely open model later, rather than a claim of a new stable network.
