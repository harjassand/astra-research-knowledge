## Central result

The chosen opening was the quantum control supply: can lost energy be restored without making all of its which-number information irreversible? A finite construction now does so using a **forward Fock energy carrier, local coherent loss memory, an energy-conserving joint measurement, and classical feedback**. It preserves arbitrary inputs in the declared data-plus-battery encoding.

For physical survival eta and coherent collection xi, let `u=(1-eta)(1-xi)`. The actual corrected instrument is exactly

`K_(r,k)=sqrt(p_r(N-Q))/sqrt(d_r)`,
`p_r(m)=binom(m,r)u^r(1-u)^(m-r)`, `d_r=N-r+1`.

The uncaptured count r remains informative. The eraser outcome k is uniform conditional on r. At complete capture r vanishes, and the encoded input factors from every retained record/apparatus output. Multiple losses are included exactly. No infinitesimal collision approximation is used in this theorem.

Read `control/ENERGY_BALANCED_ERASURE.md` for the construction and `control/ANCESTRY_CORRECTION.md` for the governing attribution. Normalized photon replacement and square-root-number phase diffusion were already derived by Vitali-Tombesi-Milburn in 1998. General environment-assisted correction and no-knowledge feedback are also established ancestry. The work here is an explicit finite conserving interface, not priority for those mechanisms.

### What it enables, and what it costs

This constructs a way to erase captured-loss backaction without quantum return of the captured field or a supplied superposition of distinct total energies. The compensating quantum correlations travel forward in the energy carrier. The local loss register must remain coherent until the classical result returns. Its width is logarithmic in the number window, but its processing, holding and fault exposure are not thereby small.

The general measurement requires more than passive two-mode optics. Capture, charge-controlled arithmetic/transfer, fixed-energy mixing, local recovery, matched physical gaps, encoding/decoding, holding, control calibration and reuse are explicit ports. A coherent copy of the old count left inaccessible defeats recovery. A returning or thermal bath is not replaced by vacuum. A continuously driven noncommuting loss interval is not certified by this idle attenuator theorem.

Strong alternatives remain live. With coherent field return, passive recombination reaches the same residual-loss law more simply. With suitable qudit transduction, entanglement distribution and Bell/control ports, conventional teleportation can reproduce that access using forward quantum communication and classical return. Fixed-charge direct coupling may avoid the battery altogether. No universal communication, energy, or computing advantage is claimed.
