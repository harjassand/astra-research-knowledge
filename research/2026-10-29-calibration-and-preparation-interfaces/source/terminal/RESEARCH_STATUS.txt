Completed local result
----------------------
A finite autonomous stochastic program admits a terminal-posterior robustness
certificate based on the range of log-likelihood ratios of rooted forest
weights. The bound is tanh(spread/4), uniformly over possible terminal evidence.
The range is computably enclosed by integer directed-arborescence optimization.
Independent row calibration transfers to every controller within a specified
product-state count, even when the controller and evidence are selected after
the data. The raw-sample order K^2/(beta*epsilon^2) is necessary in the worst
case, up to logarithmic factors and coverage of multiple rows.

Acquisition and scope
---------------------
Positive finite kernel and source-row access must be supplied or acquired.
The theorem assumes a positive-cell floor, fixed homogeneous dynamics,
shared support, and all controller/monitor/clock configurations counted.
It is polynomial in explicit states, not state bits. Hidden resets, signed
low-rank representations, arbitrary drifting kernels, global controller
search, and full original histories are not supplied by the construction.
An external fixed deadline can amplify rare-conditioned errors unless its
clock is charged. The unconditioned fixed-time question remains unfinished.
