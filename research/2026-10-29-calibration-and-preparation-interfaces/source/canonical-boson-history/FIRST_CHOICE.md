# Chosen capability: ensemble-correct prediction for frustrated bosons

Input: an explicit finite frustrated hopping matrix, conjugate boson flavors, number-conserving interactions admitting conjugate auxiliary-field factors, and physically specified particle counts. Output: canonical partition functions and predictions without storing the many-body Fock state or imposing an artificial local occupation cutoff.

The first mechanism to construct is a positive, computable fixed-number auxiliary-field trace. The controlling new primary is Yu–Wei–Li, arXiv:2610.31541v1; its grand-canonical positivity result does not by itself make coefficient extraction positive. Its main text explicitly leaves canonical extension as future work. Shen et al., arXiv:2010.09813, already supplies canonical auxiliary-field recurrences and is the equally informed ordinary algorithmic comparator.

The operative hypothesis is that conjugate flavor histories admit a positive separately balanced K,K projection, and that a larger universally positive number-filter class can be characterized and compiled. Fixed total particle number, fixed numbers of each flavor, and thermodynamic stability must be kept distinct. A polynomial kernel does not provide a polynomial mixing or independent-sampling theorem.
