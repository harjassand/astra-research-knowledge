## Full mathematical resource contract

The complexes, seed image, factors and primal graph are constructed from the
nominal code. At fixed primary-qualified ell their degree/arity and worst
local-fill search 2^Ccell are fixed constants, possibly enormous. History
size N is <=2D n. Sparse local arrays use O(log N)-bit addresses. Literal
Python consumers instead use N-bit integers and pay an additional polynomial
large-integer cost; measured arithmetic is not a unit-cost scaling claim.

The finite-list engine has bounded work polynomial in n and 1/epsilon after
its charged rational-coin cap. At one still colder fixed temperature, the
rooted engine has polynomial EXPECTED bit work in n and log(1/epsilon), is
finite almost surely, and uses a fixed number of Markov updates. Rare large
enumeration depths need not have polynomial worst-case work in that logarithm.
A single-run work cap with fallback is charged at polynomial 1/epsilon;
restarting only successful fast runs is not used.

For epsilon=2^(-c 2^k), the distance-size inequality 2^k<=n/d_seed makes the
expected update count polynomial in n. This matches the ideal memory error
scale in expected computation. The unchanged corrected encoder then needs
its known classical Pauli correction and an ideal polynomial-size Clifford
encoding circuit. Circuit synthesis, gates and routing are additional paid
ideal resources. Noise during encoding, native gate access and physical
preparation time have not been acquired.

## Decision and remaining capability gate

The exponential-n rejection upper bound in the frozen nominal preparation
contract is replaced by a polynomial classical contract for this actual
family. The full quantum Gibbs sampler's slow logical mixing never supplied
a lower bound on this syndrome-only task. Ordinary methods, once given the
paid representation, do the sampling; no sampler superiority is claimed.

This removes an ideal computational premise from the accepted neutral-error
fixed-controller memory theorem. It does not remove its [V,H0]=0 restriction,
provide a fixed controller for arbitrary charged coherent errors, or prepare
and protect the unknown qubit during finite-duration noisy encoding. Those
are distinct operational gates, not consequences of polynomial syndrome
sampling. No hardware threshold or civilisation-scale advantage is claimed.
