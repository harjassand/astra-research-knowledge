## Native physical entropy and established current methods

Dechant and Sasa, *Entropic bounds on currents in Langevin systems*,
Physical Review E 97, 062101 (2018), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevE.97.062101;
primary text https://arxiv.org/pdf/1803.09447.
Read Section III's physical reversal and entropy-current square, Section
IV's thermal heat formula, and the magnetic-field discussion. Their
equations (7)--(8) supply the established current-square representation;
(26) gives the stationary kinetic-energy route. Their reversal changes
odd external parameters, including magnetic fields. The new physical
argument does not claim these ingredients or basic current inequalities.

Lyu, Ray and Crutchfield, *Learning entropy production from underdamped
Langevin trajectories*, Physical Review E 110, 064151 (2024), DOI
10.1103/PhysRevE.110.064151. Read the primary preprint
https://arxiv.org/html/2405.12305v2, Sections II--III and Appendix A.
The paper's irreversible-current formula and modified variational method
use phase-space trajectory information plus calibrated damping and
diffusion. Its stochastic current need not itself be directly observed;
the required variance can be expressed through trajectory integrals.
This is a strong comparator when sufficiently resolved phase-space
observations are available. It is not a construction acquiring all hidden
odd variables from an arbitrary coarse even readout.

## Heat from response and the bath contract

Harada and Sasa, *Energy dissipation and violation of the
fluctuation-response relation in non-equilibrium Langevin systems*,
Physical Review E 73, 026131 (2006), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevE.73.026131;
primary text https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0510723.
Read introduction, equations (5)--(8), and the stated physical source
assumptions. Heat can be evaluated from velocity fluctuations and their
linear response under the specified Langevin reservoir model. The paper
already distinguishes a Markov effective description from a justified
thermodynamic bath: fitting dynamics does not by itself identify heat.
This comparison supports retaining an explicit bath and probe contract.
