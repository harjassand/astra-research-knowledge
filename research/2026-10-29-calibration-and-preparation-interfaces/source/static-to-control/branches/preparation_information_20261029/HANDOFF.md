## Strongest new principle

In a fixed native kinetic class, low baseline physical entropy plus an accurate
COMPLETE position-law fit can select one same-bath Gibbs explanation reusable
across an entire bounded intervention interface. Exact path likelihoods can
remain separated. Memory matters through the information actually acquired to
prepare it, not just its eventual alphabet or stored bit count.

The native class is stationary Kramers motion on a fixed finite-dimensional
torus, unit masses, position-only smooth force |F|<=A, specified gamma,tau>0,
and directly thermalized momenta. No uniform derivative bound is imposed.
True physical parity and the entropy/Fisher identity are supplied physical
premises. Complete coordinates, baseline preparations and the calibrated
actuation frame must be warranted. Visible fitting does not establish them.

For ANY smooth Gibbs U with |grad U|<=A and complete position W1 error e,
baseline entropy upper bound s controls initial phase-space TV and a negative
kinetic force norm. The candidate receives the SAME bounded Borel Markov
force u(t,q,p), |u|<=B. The result is uniform over the whole interface and
uses one U chosen before the controls. Controlled heat can be large.
