A GLOBAL QUANTUM MOMENT THEOREM FOR AUTONOMOUS TANNER REPAIR
1 November 2026. Solo proof candidate, based on explicit primary code-family
inputs and DENSITY_LEMMA.txt. Independent audit and priority review pending.

What changes
--------------
This changes the architecture from geometric Toom erosion to a bounded-
connectivity quantum Tanner code. There is NO supplied healthy exterior,
periodic recovery layer, fictitious stochastic fault process or noise floor.
The representation is a positive exponential moment of the ACTUAL quantum
syndrome potential. A finite density of ordinary defects is allowed; only
the extensive decoding boundary must remain unlikely.

The result is graph-local, not geometrically local in 2D, 3D or 4D. Both
CSS sectors and all k=Omega(n) logical qubits are retained together. The
original active/completed Toom phase problem is not thereby solved.

1. Code, potential, local corrector and fixed decoder
------------------------------------------------------
Take the family and constants of DENSITY_LEMMA.txt. Put

 c=Delta^2,  s=1/(2 Delta^(3+2epsilon)),
 alpha=min(d_0/4,delta/(16 Delta^(3/2-epsilon))),
 rho=alpha/[4(1+2c)],                 r=floor(rho n).

For each CSS sector use the sum of local distances to its dual tensor code.
Let U=U_X+U_Z. Each nonnegative integer U_v is supported on Q(v), bounded
by c and computed from the local commuting stabilizers. All the U_v commute.
Each data qubit belongs to exactly four such potential terms in total.
The density lemma gives >=s U eligible sector/center pairs whenever a
syndrome has summed reduced CSS weight within alpha n. Its soundness bound
is w_X+w_Z<=c U for weights <=alpha n/(1+2c).

For each sector sigma and vertex v in V_0 union V_1, fix an order on the
nonempty subsets of Q(v). Select the FIRST subset F strictly reducing U.
Let Q_(sigma,F) be its X or Z Pauli write and define the projector

 Pi_(sigma,v,F)=1[F is the selected strictly improving subset].

The difference cancels all potential terms not meeting F. It is therefore
a function only of commuting checks in a bounded graph neighborhood of
Q(v), even when all earlier candidate subsets are tested. These projectors
are mutually orthogonal over F. Put Pi_(sigma,v,0)=I-sum_F Pi_(sigma,v,F).
The table measures neither the logical spin nor global U. Set
J_(sigma,v,F)=Q_(sigma,F) Pi_(sigma,v,F). The separately defined correctors are

 L_active=kappa sum_(sigma,v) sum_F D[J_(sigma,v,F)],
 L_completed=L_active+kappa sum_(sigma,v) D[Pi_(sigma,v,0)]. (G1)

Neither depends on time or a global update schedule. In the completed case
each sector/CENTER has one rate-kappa Poisson proposal channel, with Kraus
{J_(sigma,v,F)}_F and Pi_(sigma,v,0). The active case is its actual, different
GKSL generator. The proof below treats them separately; no active-to-
completed quantum transfer is used. Different F are different Kraus labels;
they must not be replaced by one coherent sum of Pauli writes.

There are C_R n sector/centers, where

                 C_R=8/c.                               (G2)

The active implementation has up to C_R n(2^c-1) jump operators, but their
source projectors are exclusive at a center, so its TOTAL escape is <=kappa.
Completed correction adds one inactive jump per center. This distinction
is essential for rates and resources, and does not erase the local table's
large complexity.

Choose once a Pauli CSS filling F_s for every valid syndrome s, with minimum
summed component weight and deterministic tie-break. The decoder has Kraus
V^dagger F_s^dagger Pi_s. A fixed local-potential descent decoder is an
equivalent option on the low-weight domain, by the density/soundness proof;
outside that domain use a fixed Pauli fallback. No decoder is chosen after
the perturbation. Ideal final decoding is an explicit readout assumption,
not a global decoder operating during storage.

2. General local perturbations, including a persistent bath
------------------------------------------------------------
Let B be any finite persistent bath, of arbitrary dimension and initial
state/correlations. Let R be an arbitrary untouched reference. The ACTUAL
joint generator is

 L(t)=L_corrector tensor Id_B+L_B(t)+sum_a V_a(t).         (G3)

Each V_a acts on at most q data qubits and the SAME B, and trivially on
the other data. Require it to preserve Hermiticity and obey

               sum_a ||V_a(t)||_diamond<=delta_noise n    (G4)

uniformly in time. The full L(t), rather than every V_a separately, must be
a valid bounded GKSL generator with a CPTP propagator. Piecewise continuity
is sufficient. L_B(t) is any bath-only Lindbladian and is not charged in (G4).
The constants are independent of bath dimension, input, n and horizon.

For ordinary bounded-incidence noise, O(n) terms of bounded local strength
give (G4) with a constant density. Pure unconditional Hamiltonian coupling
is included via ||-i[H_a,.]||_diamond<=2||H_a||, with arbitrary noncommuting
memory/bath operators. Local dissipators satisfy ||D[L_a]||_diamond<=2||L_a||^2.
Signed differences of nearby local Lindblad terms are also allowed if their
sum with the corrector is physical. For example perturbing a supplied jump
J to J+E costs at most 4||J||||E||+2||E||^2 in this interface. No positive
rare-fault decomposition, syndrome-scalar no-jump hazard, finite bath
correlation time, or total-variation-in-time budget is assumed.

Only the corrector is the storage controller; any time dependence in (G3)
may be adversarial noise. With static noise/bath the entire generator is
time independent. No physical measurement, reset, pulse or dephasing is
added to prove robustness.

3. Safe recovery frame and a genuine killed quantum process
-----------------------------------------------------------
Let P select syndromes whose minimum summed CSS filling weight w(s) is <r,
including the entire logical space at each such syndrome and the full B,R.
For sufficiently large n, 2r+max(c,q)<d. If a small Pauli E connects safe
syndromes s,t, then F_t^dagger E F_s has zero syndrome and weight <d, hence
is a stabilizer times scalar phase. Thus every such safe-to-safe Pauli
matrix element is scalar on ALL logical qubits. Local bath coefficients
and relative CSS Pauli phases remain in the syndrome/bath matrix elements.

This proves, in the fixed frame |ell,s> -> F_s V|ell>, that

 K_t(X):=P L(t)(PXP) P = Id_logical tensor k_t(X).         (G5)

For a local superoperator, expand its left and right memory operators in
Paulis; the preceding scalar statement applies on each side. No assumption
of independent CSS quantum states is used. The bath-only term also factors.

K_t generates a CP TRACE-NONINCREASING evolution on P. For any GKSL
representation of the actual L, its retained jumps are P L_j P, Hamiltonian
P H P and escape operator sum_j P L_j^dagger L_j P. The difference between
that escape operator and sum_j(P L_j P)^dagger(P L_j P) is positive. It is
an actual killing term; it is not discarded or normalized away.

Start from arbitrary encoded logical/B/R input and write sigma_t for this
unnormalized process. Its trace is <=1. Projection P is an analytical
comparison, never a physical intervention on the actual evolution.

4. Weighted locality handles arbitrary coherent and signed perturbations
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fix

 beta=1/(4q c),             F=exp(beta U),
 gamma=kappa s(1-exp(-beta)).                            (G6)

For a q-qubit patch A split U=U_near+U_far, where near contains potential
terms whose data supports meet A. There are at most 4q such terms, so
0<=U_near<=4qc. Far acts strictly outside A, all these terms commute, and
F=F_near F_far. Write Gamma_C(X)=CXC. Because a local V_a^* is a bimodule
map over memory operators outside A,

 Gamma_(F^-1/2) V_a^* Gamma_(F^1/2)
   =Gamma_(F_near^-1/2) V_a^* Gamma_(F_near^1/2).

Consequently its completely bounded operator norm is <=

 ||F_near^-1/2||^2 ||V_a^*||_cb ||F_near^1/2||^2
       <=exp(4beta qc)||V_a||_diamond<3||V_a||_diamond.   (G7)

This identity holds on ANY operator, including the global safe projector.
In particular, congruencing K_(V_a)^*(PFP)=P V_a^*(PFP)P by F^-1/2 gives
P [Gamma_(F^-1/2)V_a^*Gamma_(F^1/2)](P)P, bounded above by
3||V_a|| P. Thus the compressed perturbation contributes at most
3delta_noise n F_P. Neither the bath size nor all the far volume appears.
This also handles non-CP V_a: CP/TNI is required of the full compressed
process, not of a formal perturbation term.

5. Autonomous correction supplies extensive negative drift
------------------------------------------------------------
At a safe source syndrome, every active sector/center selects one jump
lowering the integer U by at least one. If its target is safe, its
contribution to K^*(F_P) is at
most -(1-exp(-beta))F_P. If the target is outside P, its retained jump
vanishes and its escape term contributes -F_P, a stronger negative bound.
There are at least s U such centers, by DENSITY_LEMMA.txt. Therefore

 K_t^*(F_P)<= [3delta_noise n-gamma U] F_P.               (G8)

The completed inactive terms commute with P and F and have zero adjoint
action on F_P. Equation (G8) is therefore separately true for BOTH (G1).
Bath-only evolution has zero action on F_P. This is a direct operator
inequality for coherent syndrome inputs, not a classical trajectory tail.

6. The nonlinear moment bound does not grow with storage time
--------------------------------------------------------------
Set m(t)=Tr(F_P sigma_t), tau(t)=Tr(sigma_t)<=1. Initially m(0)=1. For
tau>0, scalar spectral Jensen for x ln x gives

 Tr(F_P ln(F_P) sigma_t)>=m ln(m/tau)>=m ln m.

From (G8), F ln F=beta U F, and positive sigma_t,

 m'<=3delta_noise n m-(gamma/beta)m ln m,
 m(t)<=exp[(3beta delta_noise n/gamma)(1-exp(-gamma t/beta))]
      <=M_n:=exp(3beta delta_noise n/gamma).              (G9)

The zero-trace case is trivial. No normalized postselected state is used.
This allows an extensive small background. Its moment cost is O(n noise/
repair), rather than O(n*time). The negative drift continually repays it.

7. A dangerous background is charged before it can carry logical motion
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Let q_0=max(c,2q), and P_b select safe syndromes with w(s)>=r-q_0. Any
ideal Pauli repair that can leave P has its source in P_b. The same holds
for either side of a q-local perturbation, since multiplying a q-qubit
Pauli changes the summed CSS filling weight by at most 2q.

By confinement (D13), U>=(r-q_0)/c on P_b. Equation (G9) thus gives,
uniformly for every initial joint logical/B/R state and every time,

 p_b(t):=Tr(P_b sigma_t)
  <=p_n:=min(1,exp[-beta(r-q_0)/c+3beta delta_noise n/gamma]). (G10)

Large coherence inside a correctable background was never required to
decay fast. Reaching a boundary that can support a logical bridge costs
the extensive potential in (G10), including its creation under continual
coherent drive. This is the essential difference from the failed global
absolute history sum or the supplied healthy exterior.
