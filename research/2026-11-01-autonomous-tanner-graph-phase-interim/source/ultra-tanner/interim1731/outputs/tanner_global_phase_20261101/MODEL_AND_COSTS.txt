THE PHYSICAL ARCHITECTURE CHANGE AND ITS COSTS
1 November 2026. No hardware or external state was changed.

What is constructed mathematically
------------------------------------
A family of static, bounded-graph-local Lindbladians on positive-rate,
linear-distance quantum Tanner codes. Local writes are chosen by a fixed
syndrome truth table that lowers the Gu--Pattison--Tang potential. Both
CSS sectors run continuously with independent local generator terms.
No global round, schedule, repeated global decoder, perfect stored
syndrome register, free measurement, physical reset, or added noise floor
appears. One may choose the ACTIVE generator; completed proposal channels
are a separately counted alternative, not a free proof replacement.

The robust quantum conclusion covers every encoded qubit and arbitrary
initial logical/bath/reference correlations under the actual continuous
joint dynamics. It is stronger than the one-plane transverse-fault model
in noise class, but it changes the code and its interaction geometry.
The original Toom theorem, island return, delayed strip, native-bath result
and quantum-record alternative remain preserved and separate.

Mathematical resources, without calling them practical
------------------------------------------------------
* n data qubits; k>=R n and d>=d_0 n from the chosen primary code family.
* Fixed Delta, c=Delta^2 and O(n) local views in an expanding complex.
* 2|V|=8n/c sector/center proposal channels. Each chooses the first improving
  local subset from a fixed order. There are up to 2^c-1 active jump Kraus
  operators per center, with exclusive source projectors; completed
  correction adds ONE inactive Kraus operator per center. Their total
  center escape is <=kappa, and the total proposal-rate ceiling is
  8kappa n/c. The number of engineered jump operators can still be as
  large as 8n(2^c-1)/c. These distinct counts must not be conflated.
* A write touches <=c data qubits. Its eligibility depends on local
  potential terms at the neighboring opposite-type views touched by that
  write. A conservative data-support bound is c+2c^2 (at most 2c opposite
  views, each with c qubits); this is a CONSTANT only after Delta is fixed.
  It is not a single-qubit correction or an ordinary short-range gate.
* Each eligibility truth table can be precomputed by exhaustively evaluating
  the constant-size local classical codes and syndrome possibilities. This
  may cost exponential or worse powers of that very large constant. The
  proof does not acquire a compact hardware synthesis of these projectors.
* A generic local perturbation of these devices can be included by choosing
  the fixed support ceiling q large enough to cover their data neighborhood
  and charging its local generator diamond strength. There is no uncharged
  assumption that controller errors are independent stochastic Pauli faults.
  Errors across all jump components contribute to the budget; the theorem
  does not grant a fixed per-component error while ignoring 2^c components.
* One arbitrary finite persistent bath B is retained, including bath-only
  dynamics and noncommuting local couplings. The norm bound on memory/bath
  generator perturbations is essential; an unbounded physical bath operator
  is not made bounded by terminology.
* Ideal encoding and final fixed decoding remain mathematical interfaces.
  Decoding may use a fixed local-potential descent rule with global work-list
  coordination at READOUT, or an ideal Pauli filling. The ongoing memory
  protection uses neither. Fault-tolerant state acquisition and readout costs
  have not been supplied by this theorem.

Constants and geometry
------------------------
The conservative large-Delta conditions are extremely severe. The exact
parameter program verifies additional sufficient inequalities already for
Delta>=2^2000, but does NOT assert that this is beyond the primary source's
unspecified base-code existence threshold or acquire a code at that value.
The proof chooses Delta sufficiently large for BOTH requirements. More
efficient constants, base codes and correction-label selection need work.
No practical threshold or delivered performance is claimed.

Selecting one improving subset per center is important. Giving every
candidate its own independent rate-kappa clock would multiply the total
proposal rate by up to 2^c. Here they are outcomes of ONE local instrument.
They remain separate Kraus outcomes; a coherent sum can have non-diagonal
escape terms and is a different generator. Each outcome projector depends
only on stabilizers, and safe writes are logically scalar in the recovery
frame. Thus this grouping does not perform the orientation-resolved
logical measurement that invalidated the earlier split-NEC shortcut.

The expanding graph requires long-range connectivity when embedded at
fixed density in ordinary low-dimensional space. Bounded graph degree is
not bounded wire length, bounded propagation latency or 2D/3D geometric
locality. The theorem cannot absorb arbitrary growing wiring delays into
a free rate kappa. A realistic bus/network/controller implementation would
need its own latency, noise, dissipative work and fault-spread analysis.

This is therefore a code-level autonomous quantum-information phase proof
candidate, not yet a physically realizable low-dimensional memory or a
civilisation-changing device. No speed, resource or economic advantage over
strong conventional fault-tolerant architectures has been established.

Why this is a justified architectural choice
---------------------------------------------
The Toom strip obstruction showed that a small local disagreement can be
carried by a long common background. The Tanner construction does not deny
that phenomenon or supply a healthy exterior. Instead it earns extensive
repair drift and an extensive correctability boundary. The quantum moment
can allow many harmless defects, charge their maintained density, and still
suppress the dangerous background exponentially. Its weighted-locality
identity preserves the common bath and coherent cancellations without a
whole-history absolute-value estimate. This resolves the global receiving
obligation for the NEW graph architecture, not the original Toom generator.
