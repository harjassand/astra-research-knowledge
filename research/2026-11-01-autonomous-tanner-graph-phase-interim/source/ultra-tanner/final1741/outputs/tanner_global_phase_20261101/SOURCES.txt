Other primary technical comparators actually consulted:

1. Anthony Leverrier, Gilles Zemor, Decoding quantum Tanner codes,
   2208.05537v3, 8 December 2022.
   https://arxiv.org/html/2208.05537v3
   Read its mismatch pre/post-processing, sequential/parallel decoder
   distinction, Theorems 2,3,20 and the discussion of many simultaneous
   updates. That decoder's internal mismatch is NOT silently identified
   with a physical syndrome-only Lindbladian or the Gu potential.

2. Yifan Hong, Jinkang Guo, Andrew Lucas, Quantum memory at nonzero
   temperature in a thermodynamically trivial system, 2403.10599v3.
   https://arxiv.org/html/2403.10599v3
   Read the graph/nongeometric and thermal-memory scope and Appendix E's
   Gibbs sampler, ancilla/reset implementation and fault model, especially
   Eqs.(90)-(99). This is a strong existing passive LDPC-memory comparator.
   Its discussed hardware errors are incoherent Pauli faults, with rates
   retuned to detailed balance and deliberate positive-rate operations.
   This turn does not infer the persistent-bath, sustained arbitrary local
   generator perturbation result from that thermal construction.

3. Hongye Yu, Tzu-Chieh Wei, Universal energy-space localization and stable
   quantum phases against time-dependent perturbations, 2510.14160;
   published Nature Communications, 10.1038/s41467-026-75465-z.
   https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-75465-z
   Read Proposition II.1, the energy-barrier application and Methods
   Eqs.(14)-(16). The time-dependent localization guarantee has a total-
   variation budget, including integral ||V'(t)||. It is not a theorem for
   arbitrary sustained joint bath driving of bounded instantaneous local
   generator strength. The work is credited; no priority inference follows.

4. Oles Shtanko et al., Bounds on Autonomous Quantum Error Correction,
   2308.16233v2, Quantum 9,1804(2025).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.16233v2
   https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2025-07-22-1804/
   Version and abstract rechecked, with its global-recovery/rate discussion
   consulted. Constant TOTAL global-recovery rate differs from O(n)
   parallel local-center resources here. Our total proposal rate is
   8kappa n/c, not constant. No contradiction with its rate accounting
   or claim that the broad bounds already prove this architecture.

5. Zhengyi Han et al., Coherent error threshold for quantum LDPC codes,
   2609.20537v1.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20537v1
   Its primary abstract/version was rechecked; the earlier warm investigation
   read its coherent cluster-resummation interface. Its code-capacity
   threshold is credited. A one-shot or fixed-depth recovery result is not
   automatically continual time-independent protection with a shared bath.
