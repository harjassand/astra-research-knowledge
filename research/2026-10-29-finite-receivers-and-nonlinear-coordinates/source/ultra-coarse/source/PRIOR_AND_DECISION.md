# Native intervention opening: matched prior and disposition

## What survives

An actual public catalytic KMC catalog was acquired, parsed without
fitting, and consumed with all36directional processes. The true
computational control scales adsorption channels; it is not an
imaginary actuator on one microscopic configuration. From one native
baseline batch a STANDALONE receiver predicts three unseen simultaneous
controls using ONLY adsorption counts, total-vacancy residence times,
the supplied bounded outputs, a known graph and two arrival
coefficients. No learned background generator enters that receiver.

The new question differs materially from the N744 preview. We derive
positive likelihood envelopes instead of pretending a pressure channel
is rank one. The counterfactuals are finite-exposure quantities; the
original hidden stationary design problem is not solved.

  [Hoffmann, Engelmann and Matera, arXiv:1611.07554](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.07554)

* **Full nonlinear path reweighting.**
  [Warren--Allen, arXiv:1804.07614](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.07614)

  adding explicit partial-activity brackets for this native source.
  Neither Girsanov reweighting nor its long-path overlap problem is a

* **Confidence.**
  [Maurer--Pontil, arXiv:0907.3740](https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.3740),
  actual Theorem4/Corollary5, supplies the empirical-Bernstein constants.
  Hoeffding and bounded-KL Chernoff inversion are ordinary alternatives

  [Keidar etal., NatCommun2025 / arXiv:2409.14419](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14419)

## Matched comparison, rather than an artificial oracle gap

For the COMPUTATIONAL experiment both sides have the public local-rate
catalog. Ordinary direct KMC therefore has the same model information
and can simulate target controls directly. Its three20k-target batches
took .75845s of sampling/writing. The full acquisition/compiler/replay/
geometry/receiver cycle here is more than that. No small-query compute
speed advantage is established; the example is not a competition
against exponentially reconstructing a full3^32generator.

For the PROPOSED physical coarse-access task both sides would have
the same validated intensity scaling, graph, two arrival coefficients,
count/residence/reward records and error bounds. An informed ordinary
partial-likelihood method plus graph DP reproduces our receiver. The
full-activity comparator in COARSE_RESULT is given EXTRA vacancy-pair
activity and is labeled accordingly. Its narrower interval is not a
same-information algorithmic win.

The useful source-specific reduction is that total coverage need not
be promoted to a false Markov state: retain uncertainty in vacancy-pair
activity and propagate it to a positive counterfactual interval. This
is a constructive partial-observation interface, not a new generic
response theory or a proof of physical measurement availability.

## Scientific result and decision

The receiver gives nonvacuous ideal-law intervals for all three locked
mixed controls, agrees with independent native direct simulations, and
conditionally orders their SURFACE-OXYGEN coverages. Their CO2-event
intervals overlap. There is no delivered catalytic-product optimum.

N744 is not a route to a small physically acquired pressure-design
polytope on this native catalyst: its rank-one source premise fails
exponentially. Ordinary event-path reweighting supplies a genuine
nearby COMPUTATIONAL prediction operation, and the coarse envelope
shows exactly which weaker observations suffice mathematically.

A consequential PHYSICAL discovery multiplier, useful long-time/bulk
counterfactuals, a practical advantage and new generic theory remain
NOT_ESTABLISHED. A real event-resolved record and a warranted
control-invariance/calibration contract would be the missing resource.
That is a concrete experimental/data interface, not another synthetic
hidden generator or an existing LP to rebuild. This wave finishes at
that positive computational/negative physical boundary; no larger
pressure grid or extra convenient source fixture is warranted.
