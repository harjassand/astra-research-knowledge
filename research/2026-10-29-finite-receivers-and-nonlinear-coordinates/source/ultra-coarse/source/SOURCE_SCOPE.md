is identified, not silently equated to a calibrated physical pressure
actuator. No actual chamber/gas/temperature/pulse bandwidth is supplied.

conditions, not a guessed image. All64pairs are distinct and degree4;

The raw trajectory records are NEW COMPUTATIONAL data, not measured
RuO2 data or trajectories from a fitted emulator. Model parameters were
not fit to these outputs. All ideal-law independent-episode and exact
Gillespie probability statements are conditional; the actual host uses
binary64 and seeded mt19937 streams and is not formally distribution-
verified. A source hash or diagnostic agreement is not physical validation.
