# One weak Bob LOAD: actual fixed-word SUCCESS probability linear in its rate
2026-10-30. Quantitative native service interface, not a weighted useful-DATA theorem.

## Scope and rate choice

Keep all Alice LOAD/read, both laboratories' acquisition, payload, rollback, retirement, cleanup, monitor and measured-transport rates at fixed positive values. Set each Bob LOAD rate to lambda times a fixed type-dependent number in a positive bounded interval. Bob LOAD is an onsite COMPLETE source-resolved GKLS bundle; it is not replaced by an atomic Poisson CPTP attempt on DATA. Take 0<lambda<=lambda_0.

The actual one-SUCCESS word from the accepted native service theorem can be chosen to contain EXACTLY ONE selected Bob-h LOAD and NO selected Bob-neighbor LOAD. All other selected events have fixed positive rates. Therefore there are fixed T_0,c_0>0, independent of lambda and N, such that from every full local state/reference,

    probability(effect) of at least one actual Bob-h SUCCESS
       during [0,T_0] >= c_0 lambda I.                 (1)

The probability is an actual instrument lower effect, not an independently sampled success clock.

## Actual word and why only one weak event is needed

1. Retire h and all actual owners that could block its full Bob footprint; clear their own tags and reach idle. No LOAD is needed for this return. Choose no competing Bob LOADs throughout the remaining word, retaining all of their anticommutator losses. The upper bound lambda_0 makes that loss budget uniform.
2. Process the finite Alice-worker/FIFO backlog and obtain the full fresh purge. Old REQUEST can require Alice native READs before later SYNCs are processed; these are charged as actual finite macros. Their LOAD rates are FIXED in this lemma. Clear any blocking Alice owner by its actual complete retirement/cleanup path; park a finite proof patch by excluding its LOADs/I-watchdogs with losses retained. No recursive unbounded lock clearing is used.
3. Process one new READ through the actual Alice native circuit and measured response path. Bob's unconditional response receiver obtains WANT(a). All queue capacities and old potential replies are finite. Existing native monitors and complete erasures, not a perfect classical controller assumption, give the uniform full-state path lower effect.
4. In a fixed box select the SINGLE actual Bob LOAD gain. Its Kraus family has total intensity lambda times a fixed guarded source effect. The favorable preceding word supplies that guard. Select no further h LOAD.
5. Acquire all actually free Bob resources, commit, traverse the actual C rail and take the complete SUCCESS terminal before its C-to-B watchdog. All of these rates/amplitudes are fixed positive. Quantum gate path bounds include the retained background/loss norm; no strong H is switched off in the actual process.

A finite sum of label-conditioned records covers every starting phase/queue/monitor state. Define the selected record event to have EXACTLY one Bob-h LOAD and factor its actual gain as lambda times the normalized CP guarded gain J_LOAD. Perform the complete-instrument/projected-monitor/Dyson comparison on that NORMALIZED coefficient kernel BEFORE restoring lambda, retaining every lambda-dependent no-LOAD anticommutator loss. Its leading positive lower effect and its approximation error are then both multiplied by lambda. All normalized kernel norms are uniformly bounded for lambda in [0,lambda_0], so fixed monitor windows give error lambda O(epsilon), not an unscaled absolute O(epsilon) that would force the windows to shrink with lambda. This is an algebraic factorization of an actual selected gain, not replacement of the guarded LOAD generator by an atomic Poisson-CPTP attempt. Every other gain lower bound and local normalized remainder is uniform on the same interval. Restoring lambda proves (1). The word may be extremely unlikely at its fixed fast rates, but c_0 is positive and N-independent.

## Actual survival and a fixed number of successes

Deleting SUCCESS gains while retaining losses gives a CP killed semigroup. Equation (1) and conditional CP comparison imply

    ||no-SUCCESS effect by time t||
       <= (1-c_0 lambda)^floor(t/T_0)
       <= exp[-c_1 lambda t]

up to a fixed prefactor. No independence of successive quantum records is needed. An actual passive record latch counting m successes, for fixed m, has a positive m-success probability in a window C_m/lambda, uniformly over its initial alive states: divide that window into m consecutive subwindows and use the one-success lower effect in each. Its killed gap is therefore at least c_m lambda, although the naive single fixed-T word containing m weak LOADs has probability only order lambda^m.

The same rate scaling survives the analytic conservative raw tilt: complete reset effects are bounded above/below by fixed local raw-weight factors, monitor/H terms remain fixed, and the selected clean Bob LOAD is still proportional to lambda. This observation is not an assertion that a quantum record can be exported from an analytic tilted generator into the physical noise process.

## Why weak Alice LOAD is a different problem

If Alice LOAD also tends to zero, step 2 may need several weak Alice LOADs just to retire old pending READ requests before purge. Bob watchdog resets can discard a late DATA reply while Alice REQUEST blocks following SYNCs. Hence a lambda-independent purge/handshake resolvent or a linear-in-lambda_A useful service claim does NOT follow. A finite word may instead contain multiple weak Alice factors. The physical recipient port may still choose both laboratories weak because it recomputes its stochastic service constants and needs only positivity, not an asymptotic clean useful-rate normal form.

For the continuous-memory operator program, keep Alice fixed and weaken Bob only unless a genuinely new cancellation FSM is compiled/reviewed. The accepted Bob-only FREE moment permits precisely this rate choice; it does not itself establish useful correction drift.

## Remaining boundary

(1) concerns actual terminal occurrence. It does not say every terminal is semantically correct on dirty states, supply a scalar DATA channel for controller-correlated inputs, or bound instantaneous useful energy drift on an uncommitted hub. The actual semantic guard, weighted debt service, clean acquisition/FIFO credit and global energy coverage must still be composed. No logical lifetime follows from this rate lemma alone.
