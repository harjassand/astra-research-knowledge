## 2. Success-before-rollback operator, not an abort-free resolvent

Let K_C^* be the finite clock/monitor generator killed on BOTH exit classes, retaining their complete source losses. Write Gamma_s for the actual SUCCESS source intensity and Gamma_a for all C-to-B watchdog losses. Other C data-free gains/monitors are retained. Neither exit is replaced by a Poisson CPTP attempt on DATA.

Finite native monitor paths with no C watchdog until the terminal give a uniform positive SUCCESS-before-rollback effect on the whole C clock/monitor Hilbert space. The complete exit tail is finite. Hence

    P_s=integral_0^infinity exp(tK_C^*) Gamma_s dt,
    R=integral_0^infinity exp(tK_C^*) I dt

are finite positive matrices, with P_s>=p I>0 and 0<r_min I<=R<=r_max I. They obey

    K_C^*P_s=-Gamma_s,       K_C^*R=-I.

Choose 0<beta<p/(2r_max) and set

    A=P_s-beta R.

Then A>=pI/2>0 and A<=P_s<=I. Even if A has eigenvalue one, choose a separate interpolation 0<t<1/2. The survival coefficient I-tA is uniformly invertible. Crucially,

    K_C^*A=beta I-Gamma_s.                            (1)

If the actual equal watchdog rate is mu at every C position, P_s+mu R=I supplies an optional exact simplification. No equality of rates is required for (1).
