## 5. Exact obstruction to automatic multiplication

The original weighted generator is

    L_w^*(O)=L_c^*(O)-{Delta,O}/2.                      (7)

For O=I, the removed term is negative. For an arbitrary positive witness O, its anticommutator with Delta need NOT be positive. In particular one cannot omit it from an upper bound merely because Delta>=0. Nor can one sum separated subunital single-factor drifts with the usual unital product rule: killing terms outside a factor act on its identity and would be counted repeatedly.

For a local positive factor A, the exact identity is

    -{Delta,A^2}/2 = -A Delta A - [A,[A,Delta]]/2.       (8)

A near-identity A=I+O(delta) gives a second-order double-commutator remainder, but its support, energy weight and charge must be accounted for in a linked/local cavity proof. Overlapping factors or generic V-noncommuting witnesses do not come free. Establishing this weighted product/CP-credit step and actual useful GPT service remains open.
