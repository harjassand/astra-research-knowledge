## 1. Concrete actual combined projectors

For Bob read/engine hub h let S_h^A be the physical Alice DATA support of every raw stabilizer compared by that hub. Let

    P_h = {Alice g : R_g intersects S_h^A},

where R_g is g's FULL structural possible DATA footprint over all ownership masks and prefixes. Include Alice h itself. This set is bounded by fixed degree and footprint size.

Let D_h be the sum of false DATA tokens in h's entire private communication patch: Alice HOLD(DATA), all return-FIFO DATA slots, Bob WANT, and Bob phase-valid P (A/C/B or corresponding R parents). ACK/SYNC/READ/empty/REQUEST are not tokens. HOLD and WANT are counted in EVERY engine phase, including malformed coexistence. The definitions are the actual commuting operators of the reviewed raw-energy lemma, not hidden record marks.

Define

    Q_h = I - [product_(g in P_h) (I-B_g^A)] 1_{D_h=0}.    (2)

This is a bounded-support orthogonal projector. All Q_h and all Alice/Bob B_g commute, although later local witnesses need not commute when their supports overlap. For total debt, include each B_g itself as an additional member of the projector family. Since D_h has at most n_tok physical locations,

    D_false + sum_g B_g <= c_debt sum_i Q_i               (3)

for a fixed c_debt, where the index family on the right contains these combined projectors and the separate mechanical ones. Their support/incidence remains bounded.

## 2. Exact invariant zero range

- A dirty Alice g outside P_h acts only inside R_g disjoint from S_h^A. Thus it commutes every h comparison and cannot create false h tokens. It does not touch h's private worker/pipe/cache/P.
- Classical moves transfer-and-consume true values. Clean Alice h output is an actual true measured pointer. Every Bob Kraus operator commutes the Alice stabilizer projectors. Emptying Bob P/cache cannot create a false token, even for mechanically dirty Bob engines. This is a support/bimodule statement, not a claim that normalized postselection cannot change Alice syndrome probabilities.

The algebra is valid on coherent syndrome/reference states: true value a means support on the actual Alice projector Pi_h^A(a), not a sampled hidden syndrome.

Indeed its positive effect vanishes on Q_h=0 and is at most I. This is sufficient for the OWN-corner arguments below. It does NOT claim that an arbitrary operator commuting every Q_h stays in that joint commutant.

## 3. Actual finite healing word; full purge is unnecessary

## 4. Strictly local positive OWN-corner witness

For any projector Q_i in this family, (4) implies D_i=-L_0^*Q_i>=0 and D_i=Q_i D_i Q_i. Locality bounds D_i<=nu Q_i for a fixed nu. CP comparison gives

    E_t^*Q_i >= exp(-nu t) Q_i.

Hence G_i=integral_0^(T_d) E_t^*Q_i dt satisfies

    a Q_i<=G_i<=T_d Q_i,
    L_0^*G_i<=-p_d Q_i,
    a=(1-exp(-nu T_d))/nu.                            (6)

G_i need not be strictly local. Replace it by the finite-support telescoping polynomial construction used in the accepted local Lyapunov proof, now starting from the finite-support Q_i rather than I. Local Taylor errors therefore obey both their usual operator-norm bound and the corresponding relative bound inside Q_i. Use small fixed steps and degrees large enough that

    ||F_i-G_i|| <= a/4,
    ||L_0^*F_i-L_0^*G_i|| <= p_d/4.

The second bound is earned by the EXACT polynomial identity L_0^*F_i=O_M-Q_i, not by applying an extensive generator norm. Normalize conservatively to obtain strictly finite support and

    alpha Q_i<=Z_i<=Q_i,
    L_0^*Z_i<=-r Q_i<=-r Z_i,                         (7)

with alpha,r>0 independent of N. The support radius can be enormous but is fixed. This proof uses only own-corner invariance, not joint-mask commutant invariance.

For buffered separated supports, locality gives the exact product rule. It follows additionally that

    E_t^*(product_(i in A) Q_i)
       <= alpha^(-|A|) exp(-r|A|t) I                  (8)

for every separated A, with bounded packing weakening the exponent for arbitrary sets. This is a current-projector probability/effect statement, not a fictitious independently recorded quantum reset event.

## 5. Continuous joint-noise hypotheses

    L(t)=L_0 + L_bath(t) + sum_e N_e(t),

where the full propagator is well-defined and CPTP. L_bath acts on the bath alone. Each N_e is Hermiticity preserving, annihilates I in the adjoint, has fixed bounded physical support (it may also act on a shared bath), and obeys

    ||N_e^*(t)||_(infinity->infinity) <= eta c_e.

The sum of c_e incident to any one fixed witness support is bounded by J. This includes bounded local Hamiltonian and Lindblad perturbations under the declared joint norm model; individual N_e need NOT themselves be CPTP generators. There is no uncharged coupling. The required physical geometry/local incidence is explicit; no topology-free claim is made.

No effective generator or approximation is substituted. The witnesses act only on system registers. ## 6. Separated-product moment hierarchy

Color the buffered witness overlap graph with P colors, so all Z_i supports in one color are disjoint and every nominal term touches at most one. For a subset A of one color and beta>=0 define

    F_A = product_(i in A) (I+beta Z_i),     F_empty=I.

Equation (7) and the exact nominal product rule give

    L_0^*F_A <= -r |A| F_A + r sum_(i in A) F_(A\i).   (9)

For a noise term let B_e subset A be the factors it touches; |B_e|<=b for a fixed b. If B_e is empty its contribution is zero. Otherwise untouched factors commute through, N_e^*I=0, and

    N_e^*F_A
      = N_e^*(F_(B_e)-I) F_(A\B_e)
      <= eta c_e [(1+beta)^b-1] F_(A\B_e).

Choose any touched i. Since every other factor is >=I,

    F_(A\B_e) <= F_(A\i).

Assign each source e to one such i. The incident bound J then yields

    L(t)^*F_A <= -r |A| F_A
           + [r+eta C_beta] sum_(i in A) F_(A\i),
    C_beta=J[(1+beta)^b-1].                            (10)

All comparisons are joint operator inequalities. They use no independence, no classicalization of malformed masks, and no positivity of N_e separately.

For any joint state/reference, m_A(t)=Tr[rho(t)F_A] satisfies the corresponding scalar hierarchy. Starting from arbitrary initial apparatus/DATA/bath states, m_A(0)<=(1+beta)^|A|. Induction on |A| and scalar comparison give

    m_A(t) <= u_beta(t)^|A|,
    u_beta(t)=1+(eta C_beta/r)
              +(beta-eta C_beta/r) exp(-rt)
             <=1+beta exp(-rt)+eta C_beta/r.             (11)

This is valid also for time-dependent N_e with the same uniform budgets. It is a bound on positive subnormalized states after scaling by their trace, hence uniform with retained references.

## 7. Actual commuting debt moments and sparse density

Because alpha Q_i<=Z_i<=Q_i, Z_i is supported on Q_i and

    exp(theta Q_i) <= I+[(exp(theta)-1)/alpha] Z_i.

Within one color multiply these inequalities on disjoint supports and use (11). Across colors apply ordinary Holder to the JOINT SPECTRAL DISTRIBUTION of the commuting Q_i. Do not multiply overlapping Z_i or assume they commute. Taking

    beta_theta=(exp(P theta)-1)/alpha,
    n=number of projector indices<=cN,

gives

    Tr rho(t) exp[theta sum_i Q_i]
       <= u_(beta_theta)(t)^(n/P)
       <= exp[(n/P)(beta_theta exp(-rt)+eta C_(beta_theta)/r)]. (12)

This proves an exponentially small current-debt density tail after a fixed warm-up: for any fixed q>0 choose T_w so the decaying term is below theta q/(4c), then eta small enough for the noise term to have the same margin. For t>=T_w,

    Pr(sum_i Q_i>qN) <= exp(-c_q N).                  (13)

The probability is the quantum effect of the actual commuting projectors at observation time; no physical measurement is required by the dynamics. Equation (3) converts it to a bound on actual false-token/mechanical debt. It includes arbitrary dirty INITIAL apparatus, but explicitly does not claim that the DATA survived that dirty initialization.

## 8. Why this does not finish the continuous-noise memory

The result is current apparatus-debt cooling. It does not control accumulated logical DATA damage, prove enough useful corrections, or absorb local continuous noise in the full raw exponential DATA energy over exponential time. The Z_i may be nonmonomial on DATA in their bad sectors; multiplying them into exp(sE) is not justified by this scalar moment hierarchy.

In particular the stronger shortcut 'every nominal adjoint preserves the algebra of ALL joint-Q-block-diagonal operators' is false in general. A Bob reset can erase P without measuring Alice S; an arbitrary target operator mixing Alice-S sectors can pull back to good/bad Q cross terms. The present proof needs only own-corner invariance for E_t^*Q_i and separated supports, so that shortcut is neither assumed nor used.

The next continuous lifetime task remains an actual multiplicative useful-service/CP-credit construction for raw DATA energy, including weak clean acquisition and guard activation effects. The present pointwise contract can instead be used as a rigorously separate input to a finite native drain/handoff, once that drain's raw energy/noise comparison and endpoint are proved. No transfer to that interface is automatic.
