# Strong ordinary comparator for the global-budget question
2026-10-30. Targeted primary-source check; no separation claim.

Gu, Tang, Caha, Choe, He and Kubica, *Single-shot decoding of good quantum LDPC codes*, arXiv:2306.12470v2, https://arxiv.org/html/2306.12470v2, Definitions1.1/3.3 and Theorems1.3/1.4, explicitly handle adversarial DATA and syndrome errors. Their repeated-round residual inequality has the form r_next≤α r_current+β|D| after including new DATA errors. A fixed number of parallel decoder iterations can keep residual weight below a chosen linear radius when every round's error weights are sufficiently small fractions of N. These results do not require locating each bad DATA qubit first. Therefore unknown concentrated bad components are NOT automatically a new code-level capability.

Christandl, Fawzi and Goswami, *Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computation with Constant Overhead for General Noise*, PRX Quantum6,040334 (2025), https://doi.org/10.1103/k4cm-pp9p, also establishes a general circuit-level diamond-norm/coherent-noise constant-qubit-overhead theory. Its abstract and publication record were read; this note does NOT claim to have checked a complete conversion of its circuit noise assumptions into the present shared-bath/global generator model.

The constructive comparator should therefore use the same Tanner code, bounded-dependency parallel decoder and physical error budget, rather than apply every correction serially with an unprotected global minimum-weight solver. A fixed-depth scheduled syndrome/decoder round with bounded fanout has bounded error-support propagation. A finite-window common-span/Dyson argument is a plausible way to translate a small aggregate joint generator budget into a small global bad-support allowance for that round. This requires its actual gate/preparation/measurement/classical timing interfaces and a proof; it is not supplied by the decoder theorem alone.

The candidate native result's possible distinction is maintaining the actual distributed noisy quantum controller, without a reliable external correction schedule, and composing that with real finite readout. No practical yield advantage, better threshold or impossibility of an equally informed scheduled solution has been established. Report any global-budget theorem as an expanded guarantee for this concrete architecture, not as the invention of adversarial QEC.
