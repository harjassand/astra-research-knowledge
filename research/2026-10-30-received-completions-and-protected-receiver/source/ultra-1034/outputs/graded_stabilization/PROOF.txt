RESULT

Let F=F_* be the rational, non-aligned homogeneous quartic defined by the
acquired sibling certificate ../hopf_structure/certificate.json. Write
A=(a,b;c,d), T(A)=|A|^2, and let P3 be the FULL twenty-dimensional space
of homogeneous cubics in a,b,c,d. No output or domain invariance is imposed
on a cubic C in P3.

There is an explicit twelve-dimensional linear subspace V of P3 with
the following exact characterization:

 C in V
   <=> some finite nu makes F+C+nu T globally rank-one convex
   <=> some finite nu makes F+C+nu T globally quasiconvex.        (1)

For C=sum c_alpha A^alpha, put L=sum |c_alpha|. An explicit choice is

                         nu = 2^112 L^2.                      (2)

In fact F+C+(nu/2)T is already QC. Thus for any bounded Lipschitz domain
and phi in W^(1,infinity)_0, with normalized integrals,

  integral [F+C+nu T](M+Dphi) - [F+C+nu T](M)
                         >= (nu/2) integral |Dphi|^2.          (3)

Every polynomial here with positive leading coefficient F is non-PC,
regardless of its finite lower-degree terms. The bound is deliberately
conservative, and has no asserted numerical or physical advantage.

The four cubics DF:V0 obtained by translation form a proper subspace of
V. The new cubic directions have quotient dimension EIGHT modulo those
four translations. This is a twelve-dimensional admissible cubic space,
not twelve additional directions beyond translations.

The same computation exposes F as a ray of the FULL 35-dimensional
homogeneous quartic ROC cone, strengthening its previously established
exposedness in the six-dimensional even Hopf sector. In the full
70-dimensional polynomial space of degree <=4, the exposed face with
leading quartic a nonnegative multiple of F has dimension 28. Its QC
part has the same dimension and contains a relatively open subset of
uniformly strong QC, non-PC polynomials. Modulo affine functions and
the determinant, the face dimension is 22.

This does NOT classify all admissible lower quadratic terms or show
ROC=QC everywhere on that face. Nor does it extend the old local theorem
to a neighborhood in all 35 quartics. No general Morrey, historical
priority, external independent audit, or technology claim is made.

1. THE TWO ACOUSTIC ORBITS AND THE CUBIC CONDITIONS

Use the exact parameters from the base certificate:

 a0=-717794/773089, b0=3988013/795115, c0=4082259/878263,
 K1=(a0,b0;c0,1), K2=(-b0,a0;1,-c0).

For u=(x,y) define U_u=(x,-y;y,x), and

 R1(u)=u tensor e1, R2(u)=u tensor e2,
 Ki(u)=U_u Ki, i=1,2.

The acquired ROC certificate gives

                       F''(Ki(u))[Ri(u),Ri(u)]=0              (4)

for every u. The two orbits are related by output rotation and
Gamma(a,b,c,d)=(d,-c,b,-a). The base certificate has no further acoustic
zeros modulo these symmetries.

Define V by the eight linear equations

              grad C(Ri(u)) dot Ki(u) == 0 in R[x,y], i=1,2.  (5)

Each expression is a homogeneous binary cubic, hence four coefficients.
The literal 8 by 20 matrix is in certificate.json, in its stated monomial
order. The receiver REBUILDS the matrix by differentiating all twenty
monomials. Its exact rank is eight, so dim V=12. It checks an independent
literal rational basis spanning all twelve directions.

For a cubic, C''(K)[R,R]=2 grad C(R) dot K. If F+C+Q+affine is ROC,
evaluate its rank-one second derivative at the base s Ki(u) in the
direction Ri(u). The quartic term vanishes by (4), leaving

           2s grad C(Ri(u)) dot Ki(u) + Q''[Ri(u),Ri(u)].     (6)

This must be nonnegative for ALL real s. Its linear coefficient is zero.
Thus (5) is necessary for ANY finite lower quadratic Q, including any
nu T. QC implies ROC, so the necessity applies to QC as well.

For example C=a^3 is inadmissible: at u=e1, i=1 the cubic Hessian score
eta=6a0 is nonzero. For any finite nu choose

                       s=-(2nu+1)/eta.

Then (F+a^3+nu T)''(s K1)[E11,E11]=-1 exactly. Large quadratic repair
for an unrestricted cubic is false. This is an actual rank-one
obstruction, not an arbitrary minors-compatible measure.

2. WHY F IS EXPOSED IN ALL QUARTICS

For a homogeneous quartic G define the nonnegative ROC functional

 ell(G)=sum_{i=1,2} sum_{u in {(1,0),(0,1),(1,1),(1,-1)}}
                             G''(Ki(u))[Ri(u),Ri(u)].        (7)

It vanishes at F. Suppose G is ROC and ell(G)=0. For each i the
nonnegative homogeneous binary quartic

                     pi(u)=G''(Ki(u))[Ri(u),Ri(u)]

vanishes at four distinct real projective points. A nonzero nonnegative
binary quartic can have at most two distinct projective real zeros,
because every such zero has even multiplicity. Thus pi is identically
zero.

Quartic polarization gives G''(Ri)[Ki,Ki]=pi. For rank-one Ri, the FULL
matrix G''(Ri) is positive semidefinite: its value on any K is the ROC
quantity G''(K)[Ri,Ri]. Consequently

                          G''(Ri(u)) Ki(u)=0.                (8)

For fixed Ki(u), Ri(u)+t Rj(u) is rank one for every real t (j!=i).
Its nonnegative acoustic score has a zero at t=0, so

                    2 Ri(u)^T G''(Ki(u)) Rj(u)=0.            (9)

Expanding (8) gives sixteen coefficients per orbit, and (9) gives five.
The receiver reconstructs this 42 by 35 matrix for all quartic monomials.
Its exact rank is 34, and the coefficient vector of F spans its kernel.
Only its nonnegative multiples are ROC, since F(E11)=1. Therefore (7)
exposes precisely the ray R_+ F in the FULL quartic ROC cone. Because
F is QC, the same functional exposes that ray in the quartic QC cone.

4. THE GRADED FORM-BOUND PRINCIPLE

The mechanism is general: a positive quartic lift of the form (10)
controls a cubic density by a finite quadratic whenever the cubic
vanishes on the moving null line. Only FIRST-order vanishing is needed
for the cubic. This differs from the second-order vanishing needed to
continue the homogeneous quartic lift itself.

Fix C in V. After transporting a general gradient to the canonical chart,
the cubic becomes C'=C composed with an output rotation and possibly
Gamma. It need not be invariant. Its coefficient l1 norm is <=8L: each
of the three linear factors in a degree-three monomial has coefficient
l1 norm at most two. Gamma only permutes and changes signs.

Let B be the matrix of the UNCORRECTED cubic heat density

                  C' ''(Ahat)[B1,B1]+C' ''(Ahat)[B2,B2],

multiplied by t^2 and permuted as above. Write Bhat=Q^T B Q. The heat
null vector in the original Hessian order is (0,0,b0,0,0,1). Thus its
rank-one direction is Rhat=(0,b0;0,1) in the second spatial column.
Equation (5) implies

                            Bhat_00(eta0,zeta0)=0             (12)

for EVERY output rotation and both Gamma charts. The receiver verifies
this precisely: it reconstructs the eight coefficients of the two
rotated canonical-null scores, appends them to (5), and the rank remains
eight. This does not assume that C shares the quartic symmetries.

Every Bhat entry is a polynomial of degree <=5 in eta,zeta. By (12),

                            Bhat_00=w f+g k                  (13)

for polynomial f,k. For definiteness, divide first in eta, then in zeta,
using eta^i-eta0^i=(eta-eta0) sum_{j=0}^{i-1} eta^{i-1-j} eta0^j.
There is no denominator and no singular division at the acoustic zero.

Construct the 7 by 6 polynomial matrix N as follows:

  N00=f/2, N10=k/2;
  N_(j+1,0)=Bhat_(0,j) for j=1,...,5;
  N_(i+1,j)=Bhat_(i,j)/2 for i,j=1,...,5;
  all remaining entries are zero.

Then the exact graded factorization is

                            Bhat=D^T N+N^T D.                (14)

It is this identity that allows arbitrary admissible output-breaking
cubics. It requires neither a new cubic primitive nor a new quartic seed.

Here are explicit uniform bounds. Each Hessian entry of C' has coefficient
l1 norm <=6(8L)=48L. The two compatible-Hessian embeddings give <=96L
per cubic heat-matrix entry. The coefficient l1 norm of t^2 is nine.
Consequently each entry of B has coefficient l1 norm <=864L<2^10 L.
Thus ||B||<=6*2^10 L<2^13 L throughout the square.

Conjugating by Q gives at most (1+|b0|)^2<49 per entry, so each Bhat
entry has coefficient l1 norm <2^16 L. The division (13) of a degree-five
polynomial gives |f|,|k|<=5*2^16 L on [-1,1]^2. Therefore every N entry
is bounded by 2^18 L, and

                         ||N||^2<=42*2^36 L^2<2^42 L^2.     (15)

5. COMPLETING THE SQUARE AT ALL RADII AND ALL MEANS

At a gradient rho Ahat the quartic density is rho^2 S_F(Ahat), the cubic
density is rho times its value at Ahat, and the density of q T is
2q W, where W=diag(1,2,1,1,2,1)>=I. Multiply the entire density by t^2.
For the local chart use Q and (10),(14). For every h, with y=D h,

 rho^2 y^T M y +2rho y^T N h
              >= -mu^{-1} ||N||^2 |h|^2
              >= -2^106 L^2 |h|^2.                         (16)

The cleared quadratic density in the same coordinates is at least
2q |h|^2/49. Take q=nu/2 and nu from (2). Then

                    2q/49=2^112 L^2/49>2^106 L^2.          (17)

Away from the local box, the total cleared density is bounded below by

                 (mu rho^2-2^13 L rho+2q)|h|^2.

Its scalar minimum is nonnegative when q>=(2^13 L)^2/(8mu), which is
far below q=2^111 L^2. At A=0 the quartic and cubic densities vanish and
the quadratic density is nonnegative. Hence the completed density for
F+C+(nu/2)T is nonnegative at EVERY gradient and compatible Hessian.

The same alpha=P/T as in the homogeneous proof suffices. It is C^2 at
zero, d alpha is globally C^1, and its pullback has zero integral by
Stokes. Under ordinary heat flow of a periodic perturbation of any
affine map, the energy derivative is minus the uncorrected heat density.
The exact form contributes zero, so energy decreases to its value at
the mean gradient. This proves QC for every mean. Periodization and
bounded-gradient approximation give W^(1,infinity)_0 QC. Adding the
remaining (nu/2)T gives (3). Together with Section 1 this proves (1).

For a leading coefficient c>0, the same argument after division by c
gives the explicit choice nu=2^112 L^2/c. Affine terms and any further
QC quadratic summand may be added.

it is not a literature priority assertion or an exclusion of every

7. THE DIMENSION-28 POLYNOMIAL FACE

Extend ell from (7) to degree<=4 polynomials by applying it only to the
leading quartic. A globally ROC polynomial has ROC leading quartic,
by scaling its base to infinity and dividing its rank-one Hessian by
the squared scale. Thus ell is nonnegative and exposes a face. For QC
polynomials the leading quartic is QC by the same scaling in the QC
inequality. Section 2 forces the leading term on either face to be cF,
c>=0.

For c>0, Section 1 forces its cubic into V. For c=0, taking both signs
of the scale in its rank-one Hessian forces C''(K)[R,R]=0 for every K
and rank-one R. This implies C=0. One elementary finite verification is
to take K as the four coordinate matrices and R=u tensor xi with
u,xi in {(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)}: the resulting coefficient matrix on all
twenty cubics has exact rank twenty. The receiver checks that rank.

Consequently the face lies in the linear space

             span{F} + V + P2 + P1 + P0,

of dimension 1+12+10+4+1=28. Conversely fix c near one, C in a small
open ball of V, and Q in an open neighborhood of a positive multiple
of T. The bound in Section 5 leaves a uniformly positive convex
quadratic margin. Thus all these polynomials, with arbitrary affine
terms, are QC. They give a relatively open subset of this 28-dimensional
space. Both exposed faces therefore have dimension exactly 28.

The null-Lagrangian subspace is affine functions plus lambda det,
of dimension six and contained in both faces. The quotient dimension
is therefore 22. We have not determined the sharp boundary in P2 for
a prescribed admissible C, so equality of the entire ROC and QC faces
is left OPEN.

8. NON-POLYCONVEXITY SURVIVES ALL FINITE LOWER TERMS

The acquired two opposite-determinant support cuts have a STRICT
rational incompatibility for F. Their minimizing increment scales are
fixed finite rational numbers. At the base tA and those increments
scaled by t, divide the necessary polyconvex minor-support inequalities
by t^4. A cubic contributes O(1/t), a quadratic O(1/t^2), and an affine
term cancels exactly. The two bounds on the normalized determinant
support slope converge to the incompatible bounds for F. For all
sufficiently large t they remain incompatible. Thus any polynomial
with leading part cF, c>0, is non-PC, including (18). This uses the
actual strict support cuts rather than an unsupported closure claim.

Run from the project root:

  python3 outputs/graded_stabilization/receive.py

The first uses only the standard library and the acquired sibling
receiver/certificate. It reconstructs the full constraint maps, all
rank statements, the canonical cancellation and the FULL base density,
then checks all shifted Bernstein matrices. It writes replay.json.
The mathematical heat and graded-factorization arguments are above;
the receiver does not replace those arguments with a numerical test.

The second constructs (18). It also accepts a JSON list of twenty

  python3 outputs/graded_stabilization/stabilize.py --coefficients PATH

For an inadmissible cubic it returns an exact rank-one Hessian witness
of value -1 for the requested --nu (default one), and the proof excludes
ALL finite quadratics. For an admissible cubic it returns the explicit
stabilizing coefficient; --quartic-scale accepts any positive rational.
This is a bounded algebraic operation with exact output, not an assumed
closed selector or an invitation to infer QC from a finite sample.
