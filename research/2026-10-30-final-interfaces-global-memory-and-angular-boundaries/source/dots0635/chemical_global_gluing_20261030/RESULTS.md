# Global-gluing attack: earned results and decisive remaining obstruction

## Earned construction: one measure throughout the high-count bulk
For a single strongly connected complex graph, a new finite killed-generator inequality yields one fixed alpha>0 such that

    mu(x)=product_i(x_i!)^(−alpha)

has strictly negative adjoint outside a class-dependent finite set whenever every population exceeds a fixed graph-dependent K. This is arbitrary-dimensional and uses no log-sector partition or supplied barrier. The finite inequality even has eta(alpha)/alpha→infinity. See FRACTIONAL_FACTORIAL_BULK.md and its child audit.

## Earned construction: exact genuinely leaky boundary-block elimination
For a fixed finite phase strip whose EVERY lifted complex can reach killing, the same inequality gives relative coercivity of the factorial-tilted phase matrix. Principal-minor lower bounds plus M-matrix cycle bounds and positive simple-path formulas prove C/u logarithmic derivatives of its positive resolvent. This earns actual fixed-shift control and absorption of explicit incoming forcing. See LEAKY_STRIP_RESOLVENT.md and audit. It does not assume positive polynomial coefficients for signed determinants.

## Structural obstruction: the general boundary is not a finite background
A fixed rank-three single-linkage WR network was constructed whose conservative two-population composition layer makes EVERY fixed-alpha, fixed-fugacity frozen boundary-phase matrix unstable on some escaping ray. Its positive eigenvalue persists for every larger finite strip. The escaping states lie in one actual communicating class. See FROZEN_BOUNDARY_COMPOSITION_INSTABILITY.md and audit. This does not contradict the leaky theorem: a closed lifted complex component remains.

## Changed representation, rather than weakened constants
For that conservative component, an exact growing-shell birth–death stationary profile sigma_N was constructed. Its fast adjoint is exactly zero, and neighboring-shell ratios were bounded directly using its positive product formula, without mixing, time-scale separation, or an assumed infinite-fiber comparison. See EXACT_CONSERVATIVE_SHELL_REPAIR.md and its child audit.

The stationary-shell product itself was then falsified under actual connector forcing. A genuinely killed-shell Green kernel was constructed instead; endpoint Schur complements and positive forest weights give polynomial neighboring-shell entry comparisons. A direct test function further yields a derived N^(-9/4) absorption scale, not an assumed mixing time. These repairs still do not close the coupled N/B dynamics.

## Actual global attempts that failed
* A positive residual softmax divided by activity matches leading fast-cycle scales but has a provably positive next-order adjoint along an infinite boundary sequence even in a complex-balanced network.
* The uncorrected fractional-factorial measure fails at bounded coordinates even at deficiency zero.
* Multiple linkages can have a neutral fast closed linkage whose positive adjoint dominates slower confinement; reducing alpha does not fix it.

## What is still not proved
No complete recurrence theorem follows. Remaining obligations include conservative-profile construction/comparison for arbitrary multidimensional fast components, connector/interface feedback, closed-component elimination, and a compatible common choice of alpha and strip scales. The exact shell repair addresses the demonstrated counterexample, not those general obligations.

All audits here are separate child mathematical checks, not independent whole-project certification or novelty clearance. The earlier independently accepted finite-phase theorem remains intact. No publication or external mutation was performed.
