# The exact shell profile is necessary machinery, not a supplied global certificate

For the rank-three obstruction network, try the simplest global use of the exact fast profile:

    mu(A,C,B)=sigma_(A+C)(A) / [((A+C)!)^beta (B!)^beta],
    0<beta<1.

This cancels the fast conservative chain exactly, but still fails globally. At x=(A=0,C=N,B=0), all fast reactions and the activation/clearing reactions are disabled. The only outgoing rate is1 from0→A+B. The clearing reaction3A+4B→0 supplies incoming flux from(3,N,4), with rate(3)_3(4)_4=144.

Hence its incoming/outgoing ratio is

    144 [sigma_(N+3)(3)/sigma_N(0)]
        [N!/(N+3)!]^beta (4!)^(−beta).

The exact product formula gives sigma_(N+3)(3)/sigma_N(0)≥c N³ for a fixed c>0: the binomial factor is of order N³, the three P/Q factors are≥1/2, and three neighboring normalization ratios are bounded by the proved constants. Therefore the displayed ratio is≥c' N^[3(1−beta)]→infinity. The adjoint is positive eventually.

These corner states belong to the class of0: first reach(3,N,4) by the explicit path in the instability proof, then fire the clearing reaction.

Thus the exact stationary shell profile plus a subfactorial radial envelope does not by itself close the connector problem. The next receiving object would have to be a killed/forced conservative-shell profile that includes clearing and actual incoming connector forcing, with its own neighboring-shell estimates. No stationary averaging or free boundary forcing can replace that construction.
