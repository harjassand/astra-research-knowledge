# Primary scope refresh, 2026-10-30

Targeted primary searches still locate the public claim of general two-species weakly reversible recurrence on ConStRAINeD, with manuscript in preparation:
https://constrained.polito.it/publications/

Agazzi–Laurence, arXiv:2610.31550 (submitted2026-10-22), supplies planar bimolecular bulk criteria plus separately compatible boundary estimates, not a general all-dimensional recurrence theorem:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31550

Anderson–Cappelletti–Kim, arXiv:1904.08967v2, Theorem4.1 covers binary single-linkage networks with each species appearing as S or2S. Anderson–Cappelletti–Kim–Nguyen, arXiv:1808.05328v2, Theorem7.2 uses a high-order pure-species draining family. These do not establish the arbitrary-order boundary mechanism attempted here:
https://arxiv.org/html/1904.08967
https://arxiv.org/html/1808.05328

Wiuf–Xu, arXiv:2312.07590, proves stationary MEASURES, not their normalizability. It is not imported as recurrence:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.07590

The closest stochastic reaction-network parameter-comparison theorem (Campos et al., arXiv:2302.03091, Theorem3.5) assumes both chains positive recurrent; that hypothesis is not used circularly. Its exact scope is recorded in the previous chemical_cycle_comparison_20261030/PRIMARY_COMPARISON_CHECK.md.
