## Strongest completed result

**A passive physical system can support learning and a stopping certificate for a specified control task while its hidden state retains memory and its relaxation rates remain unknown.** The construction replaces a model of how that memory decays with energy accounting, fresh experimental randomization and a measurement schedule that limits exposure to old fields.

## 1. The principle: bound encounters with memory

The task is to choose a command \(u\) solving
\[
\min_{u\in D}
F(u)=\frac12\|Au-b\|^2+\frac{\lambda}{2}\|u\|^2,
\]
where \(A\) is a fixed unknown incremental response, \(D\) bounds the commands, and \(\lambda>0\). The same specimen is used throughout. Its internal state may depend on every preceding experiment.

Let:

- \(B_i\in\{0,1\}\) select whether round \(i\) contains an active experiment, with fresh probability \(p_i\);
- \(e_i\) measure the old-field energy exposed by the round’s two measurement windows;
- \(E_0\) bound initial stored energy;
- \(S\) bound incident energy per active pair.

For deterministic, nonincreasing activity probabilities, the completed theorem gives
\[
\boxed{
\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbb E[B_i e_i]
\le
p_1E_0+S\sum_{i=1}^{N}p_i^2 .
}
\]

The argument starts from the conditional passive-energy balance
\[
e_i\le E_i-\mathbb E[E_{i+1}\mid\mathcal H_i]+Sp_i.
\]
Multiplying by a nonincreasing weight makes the stored-energy terms telescope with favorable signs. Choosing the weight \(p_i\), and using the freshness of the current activity decision, produces the squared-probability term.

This gives a precise consequence. If
\[
\sum_i p_i=\infty,
\qquad
\sum_i p_i^2<\infty,
\]
then infinitely many active experiments occur, but only finitely many actual saturation-warning encounters occur, almost surely, under the specified instrument margins. Hidden coordinates can remain frozen, and observable modes can have arbitrarily long memory.

There is no known last-warning time. Observing a final warning on a particular trajectory does not justify declaring subsequent conditional bias zero.

### Learning needs less than finitely many warnings

A useful distinction emerged: finite total warnings are stronger than the optimization task requires.

For schedules \(p_i\asymp i^{-a}\), every \(0<a<1\), the completed argument establishes convergence of a projected learner indexed by **actual active experiments**. Its measured certificate eventually reaches each positive tolerance under continued valid operation.

| Schedule | Expected active pairs by calendar round \(N\) | Expected accumulated warning bound |
|---|---:|---:|
| \(a=1/3\) | Order \(N^{2/3}\) | Order \(E_0+N^{1/3}\) |
| \(a=1/2\) | Order \(N^{1/2}\) | Order \(E_0+\log N\) |
| \(1/2<a<1\) | Order \(N^{1-a}\) | Bounded uniformly in \(N\) |

The first regime allows warnings to continue while their contribution per experiment vanishes. The last regime buys finitely many warnings at a greater time cost. These are expected-count and energy-budget statements, not complete finite-confidence wall-time bounds.

Every inactive window remains a real physical operation. Waiting is charged.

## 2. The operation and its assumptions

The enabling experiment uses two sequentially fresh signs:

1. Drive the forward operation with a signed command and measure its response.
2. Form the residual from that measurement.
3. Draw a **new** sign and execute the adjoint residual operation.

The second sign is chosen after the first measurement. Reusing the first sign leaves a demonstrable inter-pass memory bias, even in a simple passive system initially at rest.

With full signed readout, the two signs produce a conditionally unbiased task gradient. With bounded analogue-to-digital converters, clipping introduces bias; actual recorded warning flags provide a conservative allowance for that bias. Saturated observations are retained.

The main premises are:

- **Fixed linear incremental response.** Operator drift and state-dependent gain are not covered by treating them as old additive fields.
- **Actual forward and adjoint access.** A reciprocal mechanical-port construction was developed, with loading, finite waveforms and energy accounting. This is a mathematical physical construction, not built hardware.
- **Bounded commands and warranted stored energy.** A task chosen from an old record must use its current conditional energy bound.
- **Calibrated bounded measurement channels.** Their mean condition must hold given the complete pre-read history, current signs and commands, with fresh independent dither.
- **Valid operation for the required duration.** Almost-sure eventual completion does not supply a deterministic deadline or guarantee operation through an arbitrary finite device lifetime.
- **No unaccounted continuing material-energy supply.** Ongoing injection adds a further term to the energy bound. A persistent thermal or apparatus energy source is not covered for free.

The target is the fixed incremental response. The construction does not cancel every raw warm field, prepare a prescribed microscopic state, or qualify all future interventions.

## 5. Strong ordinary alternatives and contribution scope

The strongest conventional alternatives substantially narrow the claim.

An ordinary optimizer can use the same physical operation and energy principle; the active-index solver above already does so. The other complementary construction also supplies forward-only gradient estimates. For a single-input, single-output time-invariant system, waveform reversal can provide the required adjoint through the same forward plant. Multichannel random coding provides an established extension, with actual recording, replay, mixing and variance costs. Physical adjoint iterative learning is therefore a direct predecessor. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

Reset-free iterative learning, identification without mixing, and identification with binary sensors likewise precede this work. The contribution cannot be “learning without reset,” “learning without mixing,” or “learning with finite-bit measurements” in general. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}

The candidate addition is the combined bridge from **passive energy accounting, through experiments on a persistent specimen, to bounded task learning and confidence computed from its retained record**. External priority remains unestablished.
