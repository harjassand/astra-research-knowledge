## 1. The operation and its central mechanism

A physical carrier consists of two fermionic modes containing one particle:

\[
|0\rangle=|10\rangle,\qquad |1\rangle=|01\rangle.
\]

Four such carriers pass through the medium. Each use has the **same fixed \(2\times2\) transfer block \(T\)**. Define its worst capture defect by

\[
\delta=1-\lambda_{\min}(TT^\dagger).
\]

The medium’s bath may be hot, non-Gaussian, correlated and retained between calls. Its coupling to a current carrier must still be a state-independent passive linear transformation. That is a substantive structural assumption.

The logical qubit uses the two-dimensional four-carrier singlet subspace, with basis

\[
\begin{aligned}
|0_L\rangle&=
\frac{|0101\rangle-|0110\rangle-|1001\rangle+|1010\rangle}{2},\\
|1_L\rangle&=
\frac{|0011\rangle+|1100\rangle
-\tfrac12(|0101\rangle+|0110\rangle+|1001\rangle+|1010\rangle)}
{\sqrt3}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Writing its encoding isometry as \(V\), the key identity is

\[
T^{\otimes4}V=(\det T)^2V.
\]

Thus a common unknown orientation contributes only a scalar on the code. Four-qubit reference-frame-free encoding is established machinery; the candidate contribution here is the explicit retained-bath error bound, acquisition connection and finite endpoint construction. Originality has not been established. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

### Why hidden errors become fourth order

There are three steps.

1. **Particle-number acceptance rejects a single exchange with the bath.** The accepted single-carrier nonidentity amplitude is bounded at order \(\delta\).
2. **The four-carrier code rejects every single-carrier Pauli error.** The surviving nonidentity logical amplitude begins at order \(\delta^2\).
3. **A private average over six physical carrier permutations removes coherent interference.** The surviving error becomes a positive Pauli-fault component with weight beginning at order \(\delta^4\).

The resulting bounds are explicit. Set

\[
\begin{aligned}
Q(\delta)&=\frac92\delta^2+\sqrt3\,\delta^3+\frac{21}{16}\delta^4,\\
H(\delta)&=3\delta^2+\sqrt3\,\delta^3+\frac34\delta^4,\\
s(\delta)&=\left[\max\{0,(1-\delta)^2-Q(\delta)\}\right]^2,\\
b(\delta)&=\min\{1,H(\delta)^2\}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Then the native joint instrument has:

- acceptance effect at least \(s(\delta)I\);
- erasure effect at most \([1-s(\delta)]I\);
- accepted nonidentity-Pauli effect at most \(b(\delta)I\).

In particular,

\[
b(\delta)=9\delta^4+O(\delta^5).
\]

These are **joint system–bath operator bounds**. Their derivation keeps bath operators in their physical order and does not assume that the bath resets or that successive faults are independent. The proof and finite coefficient table are in :chatgpt-content-reference{index="4"}[four_carrier_transport.md](producer-workspace/four_carrier_transport.md).

### The private average gives an exact Pauli interface

For the accepted logical/bath amplitude

\[
K=I\otimes C+X\otimes D_x+Y\otimes D_y+Z\otimes D_z,
\]

the six permutations of the first three carriers, applied before the block and undone afterward, produce exactly the Kraus representation

\[
I\otimes C,\quad Y\otimes D_y,\quad
\frac{X\otimes D_x}{\sqrt2},\quad
\frac{X\otimes D_z}{\sqrt2},\quad
\frac{Z\otimes D_x}{\sqrt2},\quad
\frac{Z\otimes D_z}{\sqrt2}.
\]

This is a full map identity, including correlated references. The Pauli label is a mathematical positive decomposition; the service does not reveal which hidden fault occurred.

Because the effect bounds are uniform on the current joint state, specified disjoint sets of \(r\) erased blocks and \(k\) hidden-fault blocks have probability at most

\[
[1-s(\delta)]^r b(\delta)^k.
\]

This is the useful compositional property. It supplies a fault description that conventional codes can consume despite retained bath memory.

The current permutation key must remain private and be discarded after correction. Averaging only the three even permutations fails. Publishing the current key, or allowing it to change the native interaction beyond the intended endpoint conjugations, changes the claim.

## 2. What a quantum recipient can actually use

The completed finite application is a **fresh Bell-pair service**. Each attempt starts with a new Bell pair. A rejected attempt discards its reference and starts again.

On acceptance, the proof gives a positive decomposition into an ideal Bell component and a bounded bad component. Consequently, the same error bound controls both:

- the delivered pair’s distance from the intended Bell state; and
- its joint distance from an ideal Bell state tensored with the **actual accepted bath marginal**.

The second statement supplies approximate decoupling. A payload introduced after resource preparation can consume the Bell pair by ordinary teleportation. That consumer’s further gates, signaling and holding remain additional costs.

This scope matters. The result does not recover an unknown payload that was erased, and acceptance can steer a payload that was already correlated with the bath before heralding.

## 3. Acquisition without reconstructing the channel

The strongest acquisition mechanism came from the ordinary alternative and was generalized in the root construction.

For \(k\) independently labelled fresh source modes, a passive transfer block \(T\), and occupation parity \(\Pi\), the signed ensemble obeys

\[
2^{-k}\sum_{x\in\{0,1\}^k}(-1)^{|x|}
\Phi(|x\rangle\langle x|)
=
|\det T|^2\frac{\Pi}{2^k}.
\]

The full operator identity requires a parity-invariant bath marginal. With a mathematically unrestricted parity-coherent bath, the identity remains valid on the receiver’s **even observable algebra**, which includes the occupation-parity readout used here.

The statistic measures a transport determinant—a squared transport minor—without recovering the bath state or the complex entries of \(T\).

For two modes, let \(Z_t\) report disagreement between the private input parity and the receiver’s reported parity. Under the stated interface,

\[
\mathbb E[Z_t\mid\text{past}]
\ge \frac{1-|\det T|^2}{2}.
\]

A valid one-sided upper endpoint \(u\) from the mismatch count therefore gives

\[
\delta\le 2u.
\]

With a **separately supplied equal-singular-value promise**, the stronger conclusion is

\[
\delta\le 1-\sqrt{1-2u}.
\]

The counts establish neither balance nor common orientation.

### What makes the calibration sound

Numerical preparation and detector fidelities are not needed for this test’s soundness: under the specified independent encoding and receiver-only measurement, their imperfections can reduce the contrast. They nevertheless determine whether the test has useful statistical power.

The essential interfaces are:

- fresh current source modes independent of the retained bath;
- each private input bit encoded separately into its own mode;
- current labels unavailable to the readout and bath except through the intended source;
- receiver-only measurement;
- branch-isolated preparation and padded timing;
- a fixed transfer word during acquisition and subsequent reuse.

These are stronger than knowing source occupation marginals. A saved counterexample routes the XOR of both labels through one perfect rail and fakes perfect parity transfer while the other rail has zero capture. Finite relaxation of a source still correlated with the bus does not automatically supply the required independence.

The proof uses a conditional exponential-supermartingale bound, so history-dependent bath states do not require an IID replacement. Details are in :chatgpt-content-reference{index="6"}[parity_minor_theorem.md](producer-workspace/parity_minor_theorem.md).
