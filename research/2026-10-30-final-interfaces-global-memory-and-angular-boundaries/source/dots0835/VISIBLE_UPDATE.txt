I’ve removed a substantial idealization: the memory now has a reviewed finite physical procedure for destructive logical readout, without an ideal controller-frame inversion or waiting for every recovery circuit to finish.

The key proof controls the number of unfinished circuits despite quantum coherence and a shared environment. Their remaining effects become correctable errors. The readout construction explicitly pays for finite switching, imperfect pointer preparation and detector noise.

This is theoretical progress, not a demonstrated device. Encoded initialization, specified local hardware and reliable classical control remain requirements; practical thresholds and a nondestructive quantum output are still open.

The ZIP contains the proofs, internal reviews, reproducible checks and corrections. The separate chemistry investigation also advanced, but general recurrence remains unresolved. I’m continuing toward a usable quantum receiver and the harder chemical boundary problem.
