## Eligibility and timing qualification
'At a selected storage time' means a deterministic or exogenously scheduled time, or an independent random selection to which the uniform pointwise bound may be averaged. No noise-dependent stopping-time or continuous-monitoring guarantee is inferred. The base native tail is uniform at each such time. A tracker still needs its final target held long enough for the separately proved program-settling tail, and pointers need their finite preparation interval. Removing the native retirement dwell does not remove those distinct resources or permit arbitrary adaptive use of measurements without a new protocol proof.
