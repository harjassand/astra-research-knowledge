LOCAL CONTINUATION ALONG THE NON-POLYCONVEX CURVED BOUNDARY
30 October 2026. Supplement to PROOF.txt.

Theorem C. Put

 beta(tau)=(1-3 tau^2)/(1+3 tau^2),
 r(tau)=4-12 tau/(1+3 tau^2), tau0=1/30.

There exists delta>0 such that, for every real tau with
 |tau-tau0|<delta,
F_(beta(tau),r(tau)) is quasiconvex and not polyconvex. This is an OPEN
ARC ON the curved rank-one-convex boundary, not just an open region
obtained by adding a convex quartic. One now verified choice is
delta=2^(-206), by the conservative exact bounds in Section 4 below.
The whole curved boundary and the whole quartic cone remain open here.

An explicit rational primitive family, not a closed-selection oracle,
is defined by arc_data.json. Its 10 coefficient functions v_i(tau) are
given as ratios of univariate integer polynomials in ascending powers.
For each primitive component its monomials are given by `powers`; the
coefficient is sum_(i,n,m in weights) (n/m) v_i(tau). Divide the resulting
degree-5 numerator by T as in PROOF.txt. Seven coefficient functions are
constant. The other three are acquired rational functions. Their
denominators are nonzero at tau0, and their values there match the actual
104-term B1 primitive exactly.

The direct verifier verify_arc.py reconstructs this family, verifies its
generic Lie/anti-pullback symmetries, reconstructs its exterior derivative,
and verifies the generic acoustic-zero equations below. It does not
import the acquisition script, solve an SDP, or infer an identity from
parameter samples. It uses exact symbolic arithmetic with SymPy. The
separate standard-library receiver verifies positivity at tau0.

1. A FINITE ALGEBRAIC ZERO FAMILY

Write B=1-3 tau^2, H=1+3 tau^2. A rank-one direction is
 R=[[1,t],[0,0]]. Define the quartic in t

 m(t,tau)=B^2(1-t^2)^2-(1-4 tau+3 tau^2)H(1+t^2)^2.

Removing a nonzero rational content does not change its roots. The actual
kernel gradient can be represented by the polynomial vector

 K0=-B[tau(1-t^2)+H t^2](1+t^2),
 K1= B t[H-tau(1-t^2)](1+t^2),
 K2= B^2 t(1-t^2)-tau H t(1+t^2)^2,
 K3= B^2 t^2(1-t^2)+tau H(1+t^2)^2.

The verifier checks F_tau''(R)K=0 modulo m. The acquired primitive family
also satisfies Ctilde_tau(K) h_R=0 modulo m for
 h_R=(1,t,t^2,0,0,0).
These are rational-function identities, not just vanishing scalar values.
The latter alone would not make a completed matrix positive semidefinite.

The zero geometry is

 kappa=2 tau/B,
 mu=(1-4 tau+3 tau^2)/H,
 sigma=4 mu/(1+mu)^2.

The verifier checks X(K)-kappa Z(K)=0 and
 Z(K)^2-sigma T(K)^2=0 modulo m. At tau0 the roots are simple. The four
real acoustic directions yield the four Hopf sign choices, and their
canonical gradients are the four simple points of

 I_tau=(w_tau,g_tau),
 w_tau=b^2+d^2+2 kappa d-1,
 g_tau=7059192361 [d^2-sigma(1-kappa d)^2].

The fixed nonzero factor makes g_tau at tau0 equal to the literal B1
factor g, so the continuing division convention agrees with the verified
certificate. It has no effect on the zero ideal.

The symmetries transport the acquired zero-vector equation to all these
canonical points. All four points remain simple in a neighborhood of
tau0: g has distinct roots and its resultant with 1-d^2-2 kappa d is
nonzero there. These conditions are checked exactly at tau0.

For 0<tau<1/3 the given beta,r lie on the already derived upper curved
ROC boundary. We choose delta inside this interval. In particular the
acoustic quadratic form is nonnegative and has zero first derivative
at each of its zero gradients.

2. CONTINUING THE ACTUAL FACTORIZATION

In the four-dimensional quotient algebra Q(tau)[b,d]/I_tau, solve for a
polynomial vector k_tau whose first component is 1 and whose other
components have basis (1,b,d,bd), such that Ctilde_tau k_tau=0.

This is a finite rational linear operation. The lower 5 by 5 block is
invertible in this quotient at tau0: the verifier constructs its full
20 by 20 multiplication operator and checks its nonzero exact
determinant. Its determinant stays nonzero nearby. The solution is
therefore rational in tau with no pole at tau0. The acoustic-zero
equations guarantee consistency of the remaining row. At tau0 it equals
the actual kernel polynomial in the literal B1 factorization.

Let Q_tau have first column k_tau and the other five standard columns.
The first row of Q_tau^T Ctilde_tau Q_tau belongs to I_tau. Its first
entry belongs to I_tau^2. To see the extra order, its value and first
derivatives vanish at each zero: Ctilde k=0 cancels derivatives of k,
and the remaining directional scalar is the acoustic quadratic form at
its zero minimum. The zero ideal is a finite simple complete intersection.
Equivalently, its maximal ideals are comaximal, so vanishing to first
order at every point means membership in their squares, hence in I_tau^2.

Perform the same exact polynomial divisions by w_tau and g_tau as in
PROOF.txt Section 3. They produce a polynomial matrix M_tau with
rational coefficient functions of tau, analytic at tau0, and

 Q_tau^T Ctilde_tau Q_tau=D_tau^T M_tau D_tau.

This operation does not supply or assume positivity. It supplies an
actual continuing identity. At tau0 the divisions reproduce the literal
M already verified in seed_299_301.json.

3. WHY AN OPEN ARC IS PROVED

Use the SAME fixed rational rectangles and the SAME local M box as the
129-rectangle certificate for B1. Every matrix Bernstein coefficient of
M_tau on the local box and Ctilde_tau on the other leaves is a continuous
function of tau. At tau0 all 6,321 coefficient matrices are STRICTLY
positive definite, as checked by the standard-library receiver. This is
a finite collection of strict inequalities. All persist on some common
open interval about tau0. The unchanged cover and the continuing
factorization therefore prove Ctilde_tau>=0 on the complete square in
that interval. The retained orientation, global-cover and heat/Stokes
argument in PROOF.txt then prove quasiconvexity.

The two non-PC support cuts at A=diag(36,35), Rplus=[[0,55],[-4,0]],
Rminus=diag(1,-2) have a strictly positive rational gap at tau0. Their
coefficients and denominators are continuous and finite nearby, so the
same contradiction persists after reducing the interval if necessary.
Thus the arc is also non-polyconvex.

4. AN EXPLICIT CONSERVATIVE RADIUS

continuing_matrices.json gives the actual rational polynomial matrices
Ctilde_tau, M_tau and kernel k_tau, rather than merely asserting their
existence. verify_arc.py reconstructs the WHOLE continuing density from
the actual primitive and checks the full continuing factorization.

quantify_arc.py computes an explicit sufficient interval with exact
rational arithmetic. It uses reference radius R=1/1000 about tau0.
For every rational coefficient f=p/q, write its polynomials in powers
of z=tau-tau0, with p0=p(tau0), q0=q(tau0). Put

 S_q=sum_(j>=1) |q_j| R^(j-1),
 S_f=sum_(j>=1) |p_j q0-p0 q_j| R^(j-1).

For |z|<=h<=min(R,|q0|/(2S_q)), the denominator stays nonzero and
 |f(tau)-f(tau0)| <= h (2 S_f/q0^2).
The zero S_q case needs no denominator restriction beyond R. These
bounds are applied to the primitive and kernel denominators, every
monomial coefficient of both continuing matrices, and the non-PC gap.

The Bernstein transform on any of the degree-(6,6) rectangles amplifies
an entry's sum of coefficient errors by at most 3^6=729: the b interval
lies in [0,1], and |left_d|+width_d<=3. Thus a common exact constant L
bounds the change of EACH Bernstein coefficient matrix entry by L h.

For each positive definite coefficient matrix A at the center, the exact
lower bound det(A)/trace(A)^(n-1) is valid for its smallest eigenvalue.
Let ell be the minimum of these positive rational bounds over all 6,321
matrices. A matrix of size at most 7 changes in operator norm by at most
7 L h. The script selects h small enough that 7 L h<=ell/2, all
denominators stay nonzero, and the change of the non-PC support gap is
at most half its positive central value. It then rounds downward to a
dyadic radius. Every required inequality is checked with Fractions.

The resulting certified radius is 2^(-206). The exact intermediate
bounds are in arc_radius_certificate.json. This is deliberately very
conservative, not an optimized interval, a claim about a substantial
fraction of the curved boundary, or a complexity bound for general
certificate acquisition. In particular no wider interval is inferred
from numerical sampling.

To replay the checked family identities, after replaying the center:
 python3 receive_boundary.py seed_299_301.json
 python3 verify_arc.py
 python3 quantify_arc.py

The latter two commands require SymPy. The proof of continuation is the
finite strictness/continuity argument above, not parameter sampling.
This establishes a local structural extension of the new boundary
construction. It does not settle the rest of the curved boundary,
establish historical priority, or provide external independent review.
