2. ACTUAL CLOSED ANGULAR CORRECTIONS AND THEIR REGULARITY

For each seed the four lists named P in its JSON file define polynomials
P_i on R^4. A row [e0,e1,e2,e3,n,m] means exactly
 (n/m) a^e0 b^e1 c^e2 d^e3.
All numerator monomials have total degree 5. Define, for A!=0,

 alpha=sum_(i=0)^3 P_i(A)/T dA_i, and Omega=d alpha.

This is an acquired primitive; closedness is not supplied by a pointwise
matrix-selection oracle. Every coefficient of alpha is smooth off zero
and homogeneous of degree 3. On the compact unit sphere all its first
two derivatives are bounded. Its derivatives of orders j<=2 are therefore
O(|A|^(3-j)). Set alpha(0)=0. It is C^2 globally, and Omega is C^1 globally
with degree-2 coefficients. This is the same legitimate origin regularity
as the accepted predecessor angular certificate; an origin-C^2 two-form
is not claimed.

Let h=(h0,...,h5) and use the compatible Hessian pair
 B_1=(h0,h1,h3,h4), B_2=(h1,h2,h4,h5).
Let C_F(A) represent

 F''(A)[B_1,B_1]+F''(A)[B_2,B_2]+Omega_A(B_1,B_2).

The receiver reconstructs it from the actual primitive, using

 T^2 Omega_ij = T(partial_i P_j-partial_j P_i)
                   -2(A_i P_j-A_j P_i).

It then reconstructs Ctilde=T^2 C_F, a symmetric polynomial matrix of
degree 6. In forming its wedge contribution, the receiver divides each

also checked by the reconstruction, rather than silently changed.

3. THE ACOUSTIC-ZERO FACTORIZATION LEMMA

Here is a general sufficient positivity operation, used concretely below.
Let C be a symmetric 6 by 6 polynomial matrix in two variables. Suppose
k=(1,k1,...,k5)^T and w,g are polynomials. Let Q have first column k and
remaining columns e1,...,e5, with coordinate numbering starting at zero.
Thus det Q=1. Set D to be the 7 by 6 polynomial matrix with

 D_00=w, D_10=g, D_(j+1,j)=1 for j=1,...,5,

and all other entries zero. If a symmetric 7 by 7 polynomial matrix M
satisfies

                 Q^T C Q = D^T M D,                    (L)

then M>=0 on a region implies C>=0 there. This follows immediately by
evaluating the quadratic forms and using the invertibility of Q.
At w=g=0, D loses its first column and allows the original density to
retain its null vector k. Strict positivity of M is compatible with this
necessary degeneracy of C.

The factorization can be acquired by exact division. In the present
cases, k is first found in the finite quotient algebra of the zero set.
One then divides k^T C k by w twice. The remainder of degree zero in w
is divisible by g^2; the coefficient of degree one in w is divisible by g.
For j>=1, k^T C e_j is divisible by the ideal (w,g). These exact identities
define the first two rows of M; its lower 5 by 5 block is the corresponding
block of C. All divisions and the full identity (L) are checked exactly.

This lemma is sufficient, not an assertion that every rank-one-convex
quartic admits such a positive lift or a closed angular correction.

For C=Ctilde(1,b,0,d), the actual pairs of factors are

 B0: w=b^2+d^2+(8/11)d-1,
     g=8761d^2+5720d-7865;

 B1: w=b^2+d^2+(40/299)d-1,
     g=7027767961d^2+939589560d-7023431961.

The kernel polynomials k and all entries of M are given literally in the
respective JSON file. Their entries have degree at most 6 in each of b,d.
The receiver checks (L) by its component identities:

 k^T C k = w^2 M00+2wg M01+g^2 M11,
 k^T C e_j = w M_(0,j+1)+g M_(1,j+1),
 C_ij=M_(i+1,j+1), i,j>=1.

For orientation of the zero factors, write n=(X,Y,Z)/T. The acoustic-zero
orbits obey X/T=kappa Z/T and (Z/T)^2=sigma, with

 B0: kappa=4/11, sigma=65/81;
 B1: kappa=20/299, sigma=78561/78961.

On w=0, T=2(1-kappa d), so the other equation is precisely
d^2-sigma(1-kappa d)^2=0. Clearing its positive denominator gives g.
The factorization proof does not rely on approximate root locations.

5. FULL MATRIX COVER, ORIENTATION, AND THE HEAT IMPLICATION

The receiver checks the Lie identity for left output SO(2) rotations and
the ANTI-pullbacks of the actual primitive under A->C A C and A->A S,
where C=diag(1,-1), S exchanges columns. F is invariant under these maps.
Taking d preserves the primitive's pullback identities.

The minus sign under each domain reflection is essential: reversing the
two spatial derivative directions contributes the other orientation sign
in the wedge evaluation. For column swap, B'_1=B_2 S, B'_2=B_1 S, so the
primitive's anti-pullback and the interchange of B_1,B_2 cancel. The same
reflection accounting applies to C A C. Output rotation gives its ordinary
compatible-Hessian congruence. Hence positivity transports under all these
maps, with the required signs, as in the governing predecessor proof.

For any A!=0, swap columns if needed so the first column has greater or
equal norm. Rotate its output to make that column (a,0), a>0, and divide
by a. The representative is [[1,b],[0,d]], b^2+d^2<=1. The reflection C A C
can make b nonnegative. This canonical disk lies in the certified square.
Homogeneity and the congruences therefore prove C_F(A)>=0 for ALL A!=0.
At zero it follows by continuity, with C_F(0)=0. Clearing T^2 did not
change a sign away from zero.

To give the analytic step explicitly, work first on a torus with
u(x)=M x+phi(x), phi smooth periodic. Evolve phi_t=exp(t Delta)phi and let
E(t)=integral F(M+Dphi_t). Integration by parts gives

 E'(t)=-integral sum_j F''(M+Dphi_t)[partial_j Dphi_t,
                                             partial_j Dphi_t].

The pullback of d alpha by the periodic gradient map has integral zero
by Stokes. Adding it to the integrand proves E'(t)<=0. The heat flow
converges to its mean, so Dphi_t->0 and E(t)->volume*F(M). Thus
integral F(M+Dphi)>=volume*F(M). A compactly supported smooth test can be
periodized in a box containing its support, proving ordinary
quasiconvexity; the usual bounded-gradient smooth approximation gives
W^(1,infinity)_0 tests. The C^2 primitive makes Stokes legitimate even
where a gradient attains zero.

This uses the Bruno-Pasqualotto heat/exact-form mechanism. Their published
Theorem 2 and Appendix A require BOTH left and right rotational invariance:
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06367v1
The qX term here is anisotropic under right rotation, so that classification
is not silently applied to these two seeds. No historical-priority claim
for the present certificates or lemma is made.

6. EXACT UNRESTRICTED NON-POLYCONVEXITY OF B1

For any polyconvex quartic F and any base A there must be a real scalar
lambda such that

 F(A+H)-F(A)-DF(A):H-lambda det H >=0 for every H.

Indeed a convex lift on (A,det A) has a supporting hyperplane there. Such
a lift is finite everywhere: any point (B,s) is the average of graph
points B+Y,B-Y with det Y=s-det B. Differentiating the touching support
along the graph fixes the matrix coefficient and leaves the scalar lambda.

If F(R+tA)=c0+c1 t+c2 t^2+..., quartic polarization and minimization of
the support inequality along real multiples of R require

       c2-c1^2/(4c0)-lambda det R >=0, provided c0>0.

For B1 use A=diag(36,35), Rplus=[[0,55],[-4,0]], Rminus=diag(1,-2).
The receiver expands the actual polynomial on both lines and obtains

 lambda <= 917884501/33110,
 lambda >= 227527416931/8195929.

The lower bound exceeds the upper by exactly
                 34938784681/901552190 >0.

Thus B1 admits no scalar minor support at this A and is not polyconvex.
This excludes arbitrary convex minor lifts, not just polynomial lifts.
The witness is an exact evaluation of the known opposite-cut mechanism
at the new polynomial. It is not a new general characterization of PC.

           (2448b-2068)/101,       99/100<=b<=299/301;
           60b-56,                299/301<=b<=1.

Theorem B. Every F_(beta,r) with r>=U_new(|beta|) is quasiconvex.

Each line is exactly the segment between consecutive vertices
 (0,1/2), (11/13,28/13), (99/100,88/25),
 (299/301,1084/301), (1,4).
At a point of a segment use its two nonnegative affine barycentric
weights to combine the corresponding QC quartics. Above the segment add
4(r-U_new(b)) det^2, which is polyconvex by determinant Jensen. Negative
beta is obtained by an orthogonal column reflection. This proves the
whole region without assuming det^2 dissipates the ordinary heat flow.

At b=299/301, the previous admitted threshold was 48b-44=1108/301;
the new seed is lower by exactly 24/301. The entire region between the
old and new envelopes is the new certified coverage relative to that

Where a positive weight of Q or C0 occurs, its previously established
