9. Prior machinery, remaining gaps and evidence scope

The ordinary alternatives deserve their full strength.

Aarnoudse and Oomen already construct randomized experimental gradient estimates for adjoint iterative learning control, including a single dedicated experiment per stochastic gradient iteration. Their work remains an important predecessor for physical gradient acquisition. 
pure.tue.nl

Identification without mixing is also established. Simchowitz and coauthors analyze ordinary least squares from a single observed state trajectory without standard mixing-time arguments. Their observation interface differs from the hidden-state, scalar-comparator interface used here, but their result rules out treating “no mixing assumption” alone as a new foundational contribution. 
proceedings.mlr.press

The strongest completed contribution here is the combination of:

a returned binary law that cancels additive memory exactly within a guard range;
a sharp energy charge for departures from that range;
a guarantee for the full adaptive returned process;
a finite threshold implementation with its precision cost retained;
a complete nontrivial decision certificate and a faster task-specific alternative.

The remaining physical premises are substantial. The response must stay fixed and additive in the current signed command. The stored-energy and input-energy bounds must be warranted. Signs and thresholds must satisfy the required conditional freshness. The comparator must operate validly across the permitted input amplitudes. Threshold placement, sign-control errors and unmodeled sensor effects require their own allowances; their acquisition and calibration costs have not been demonstrated here.
