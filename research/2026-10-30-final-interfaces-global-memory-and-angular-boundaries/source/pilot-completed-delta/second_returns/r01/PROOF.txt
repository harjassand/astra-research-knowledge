ORIGIN REGULARITY IS AN ESSENTIAL RESOURCE IN THE ANGULAR HEAT PROOF
30 October 2026. Local derivation and exact algebraic verification.

This tranche continues the first result without network access or new
external inputs. It proves a necessity statement at the actual accepted
non-polyconvex seed, and an exact regular-origin classification for a
rotationally invariant comparison family. These are statements about the
heat/exact-form mechanism, not a new Morrey theorem.

MAIN RESULTS

(A) For the accepted seed B1=F_(299/301,1084/301), NO closed two-form
correction that is C^2 near the zero matrix can make the componentwise
heat Hessian density pointwise nonnegative for every matrix and every
compatible Hessian pair. This excludes arbitrary polynomial primitives
and arbitrary smooth or analytic primitives, not just a finite ansatz.

The supplied acquired primitive DOES certify this quasiconvex,
non-polyconvex energy. Its two-form is in fact C^(1,1) globally, while its
primitive is C^(2,1). Thus the loss of a continuous second derivative for
the two-form, or a continuous third derivative for the primitive, cannot
be removed by replacing the certificate with a smoother one in this heat
mechanism. The energy itself remains a smooth quartic polynomial.

(B) For

                  G_gamma(A)=|A|^4+gamma (det A)^2,

a C^2-near-zero closed heat correction exists if and only if

                   -2 <= gamma <= 12+8 sqrt(3).              (1)

Equivalently, for the supplied notation F_(0,r), its exact range is

                    1/2 <= r <= 4+2 sqrt(3).                (2)

An explicit cubic primitive realizes the upper endpoint. Every member
with gamma>=-2 is already polyconvex, so (1) is an exact classification of
a proof mechanism under its stated regularity, not a QC classification.
Above the endpoint, a less regular angular correction is not excluded by
this theorem. The first tranche's periodic heat witness excludes all
regular global exact-form corrections in a larger-parameter range.


1. THE ORIGIN TAYLOR REDUCTION

Let F be any homogeneous quartic on R^(2x2). Use coordinates (a,b,c,d),
and, for h in R^6, the compatible matrices

 B1=[[h0,h1],[h3,h4]],       B2=[[h1,h2],[h4,h5]].

Let C_F,Omega(A) denote the symmetric 6x6 matrix of

 F''(A)[B1,B1]+F''(A)[B2,B2]+Omega_A(B1,B2).                  (3)

Lemma. Suppose Omega is closed and C^2 in a neighborhood of A=0, and
(3) is nonnegative for all compatible h and all A in that neighborhood.
Then there is a closed two-form Omega2 with homogeneous quadratic
polynomial coefficients for which C_F,Omega2(A)>=0 for EVERY A.
It has a homogeneous cubic polynomial primitive.

Proof. For any fixed alternating form W, its contribution K(W) to (3)
vanishes on every wave vector

 h=(z1 xi1^2,z1 xi1 xi2,z1 xi2^2,
                         z2 xi1^2,z2 xi1 xi2,z2 xi2^2).

Indeed B2 is proportional to B1 there, with the cases xi1=0 handled
directly. These wave vectors span R^6: use z=e1,e2 and xi=(1,0),(0,1),
(1,1). If K(W)>=0, each zero scalar value forces the associated wave
vector into its kernel. Hence K(W)=0. The map W -> K(W) is injective,
as seen from the six independent wedge quadratics

 h0 h2-h1^2,   h0 h4-h1 h3,   h0 h5-h1 h4,
 h1 h4-h2 h3,  h1 h5-h2 h4,   h3 h5-h4^2.

At A=0, F''(0)=0, so positivity forces Omega(0)=0. For fixed A and
s>0 tending to zero, divide C_F,Omega(sA)>=0 by s. Since F''(sA)=s^2
F''(A), the limit is K(DOmega(0)[A])>=0. The same spanning argument
forces DOmega(0)=0.

Now define Omega2(A)=(1/2)D^2 Omega(0)[A,A]. Dividing the original
inequality at sA by s^2 and taking the limit gives C_F,Omega2(A)>=0
for each arbitrary A. Expanding the first derivatives in dOmega=0 shows
dOmega2=0. For the radial field E=sum_i A_i partial_(A_i), Cartan's
identity gives

              alpha2=(1/4) i_E Omega2,  d alpha2=Omega2,

because the scaling weight of a degree-two coefficient two-form is four.
This is an actual polynomial primitive; no pointwise selection is used.
The proof also works if Omega is C^1 nearby and its first derivative is
Frechet differentiable at zero. In particular C^2 regularity suffices.

Consequently no higher-degree polynomial terms or nonhomogeneous smooth
terms can repair the failure of every quadratic-coefficient closed form.
The obstruction is stronger than failure of a chosen polynomial profile.

Symmetrizing is legitimate. For any symmetry A -> U A V of F, transport
the two-form with the spatial orientation factor det(V). The compatible
Hessian density transforms by a congruence. Averaging over the compact
symmetry group therefore preserves positivity and exactness. On cubic
polynomials this is a finite-dimensional linear projection. Thus a
certificate implies a symmetry-adapted cubic primitive. Reflection signs
here are the same orientation signs checked in the supplied proof.


2. AN EXACT OBSTRUCTION AT THE ACCEPTED B1 SEED

For B1 use its actual symmetry group: left output SO(2), and the
anti-pullbacks under A -> C A C and A -> A S, with C=diag(1,-1) and
S exchanging columns. Do not impose right rotational invariance: the
qX term is anisotropic.

Write L=a^2+c^2, R=b^2+d^2, N=ab+cd, X=L-R, q=ad-bc.
The complete space of symmetry-adapted cubic primitives has basis

 P0=(-b L, a R,-d L, c R),
 P1=( c L,-d R,-a L, b R),
 P2=q(b,a,d,c),
 P3=N(-a,b,-c,d),
 P4=(-b R, a L,-d R, c L),
 P5=( c R,-d L,-a R, b L).

Here alpha=sum_i z_i P_i dot dA. Completeness is checked directly:
all four cubic coefficient polynomials have 80 unrestricted monomials;
the exact Lie and two anti-pullback equations have rank 74, and these
six independent vectors lie in their kernel. No optimizer premise enters.

The acoustic zero information supplies necessary equations, not a
selection oracle. Put R(t)=[[1,t],[0,0]] and

 m(t)=t^4-(83981/2710)t^2+1,

 K0=761510-25254181 t^2,
 K1=1655520 t^3+761510 t,
 K2=-1710280 t^3+810290 t,
 K3=810290-23400039 t^2.

This is the supplied exact acoustic kernel at tau=1/30 after clearing a
common nonzero scalar. The verifier checks from the actual B1 quartic
that F''(R(t))K(t)=0 modulo m. Since 83981/2710>2, m has four distinct
real roots: its quadratic equation in t^2 has two positive reciprocal
roots.

Quartic polarization gives

 F''(K(t))[R(t),R(t)]=F''(R(t))[K(t),K(t)]=0

at every root. For h_R=(1,t,t^2,0,0,0), the compatible pair is
(R(t),t R(t)), so every two-form contribution to its scalar value is
zero. Positive semidefiniteness of C_F,dalpha(K(t)) therefore REQUIRES

                    C_F,dalpha(K(t)) h_R=0.                 (4)

Reduce (4) in the four-dimensional quotient Q[t]/(m). All four roots are
real, so vanishing at them is exactly vanishing of the remainder. This
gives 24 rational linear coefficient equations of rank three in z0,...,z5.
Their COMPLETE solution is

 z0=-220423 z3/180402-310624 z4/90201-139667048/9050167,
 z1=-81029 z3/180402-81029 z4/90201-87237436/9050167,
 z2=598 z3/321+1196 z4/321+2 z5+1344304/32207,

with z3,z4,z5 free. The only effective density parameter is

                             w=z3+2 z4.

Indeed -P0-2P3+P4=d(XN), and 2P2+P5=d(qN), so the other freedoms are
exact primitive gauges. Every admissible two-form is represented by
z3=w, z4=z5=0 in the displayed solution.

Two exact pointwise tests now contradict positivity.

At A1=[[1,1],[0,0]], h1=(2,-1,2,0,-1,0), (3) is

                 -311815872/1292881-(883948/90201) w.

Nonnegativity requires

                         w <= -233861904/9502441.

At A2=diag(1,1/2), h2=(0,-1,0,-1,0,2), (3) is

                  254490123/9050167+(872897/721608) w.

Nonnegativity requires

                         w >= -6107762952/262741997.

The lower bound exceeds the upper by exactly

                    79225269437592/58062565691039 > 0.

Hence no cubic primitive can work. Symmetry averaging plus the origin
Taylor lemma proves result (A) for arbitrary closed forms that are C^2
near zero. This is not merely a certificate-family obstruction.

The working acquired primitive attains the remaining regularity.

The accepted primitive has coefficients P5/T, smooth off zero and
homogeneous of degree three. Its third derivatives are homogeneous of
degree zero and bounded on the unit sphere. Its second derivatives
extend continuously as zero at the origin. Integrating the bounded third
derivatives along line segments, splitting at zero if necessary, makes
the second derivatives globally Lipschitz. Thus alpha is C^(2,1).
Likewise Omega=dalpha is homogeneous of degree two, smooth off zero;
its second derivatives are bounded there and its first derivatives extend
continuously through zero. Hence Omega is C^(1,1) globally.

The source's exact certificate proves that this form actually works;
its defining P5/T is not replaced by an oracle. Result (A) proves that
no alternative C^2 two-form or C^3 primitive can certify B1 by this
ordinary heat density. The source's weaker origin regularity is therefore
an essential resource of this construction. No conclusion about the
possibility of smoother proofs using different flows or different
mechanisms is made.


3. THE EXACT REGULAR-ORIGIN CONE FOR THE ISOTROPIC COMPARATOR

Now let F=G_gamma=T^2+gamma q^2, with T=|A|^2. Its full independent
orthogonal output/domain symmetries allow a larger averaging group than
B1. Define the invariant one-forms by their coefficient vectors

 theta=(-b,a,-d,c),           chi=(-c,-d,a,b).

All averaged cubic primitives are exactly

                    alpha=u T theta+v q chi.                (5)

This is verified on all 80 cubic coefficients: left and right SO(2) Lie
identities, ordinary output-reflection pullback, and anti domain-reflection
pullback form a rank-78 linear system; the two displayed vectors span its
kernel. Equation (5) is therefore necessary after averaging, not a
chosen sufficient ansatz.

At A=diag(1,rho), reconstruct the full compatible density from the actual
exterior derivative of (5). It splits into the two coordinate blocks
(h0,h2,h4) and (h1,h3,h5). Three finite PSD tests give

 gamma>=-2,
 u^2<=12,
 (u-v/2)^2<=8 gamma+16,
 2 gamma+2u+v<=8.                                           (6)

The first three follow from entries and 2x2 principal minors at rho=0;
the last is one quarter of the density at rho=1 and
h=(1,0,-1,0,-1,0). All four identities are checked exactly in
origin_heat_cone.py.

Let s=sqrt(3). Since u>=-2s and v>=2u-2sqrt(8 gamma+16), (6) implies

                    gamma-4-4s <= sqrt(8 gamma+16).

If gamma>12+8s the left side is positive. Squaring gives

               (gamma-4)(gamma-(12+8s)) <= 0,

a contradiction. Together with the first inequality, this proves
necessity of (1). By the origin Taylor lemma the bound applies to every
closed correction C^2 near zero, including arbitrary polynomial or
analytic profiles.

An actual primitive attains the endpoint.

Set

       gamma*=12+8s,   u*=-2s,   v*=-16-12s,
                   alpha*=u* T theta+v* q chi.              (7)

For A=diag(1,rho), its first 3x3 block is

 [ 12+(28+16s)rho^2,  (8+4s)rho^2-4s,  16(s+2)rho ]
 [ (8+4s)rho^2-4s,   4(1+rho^2),      0            ]
 [ 16(s+2)rho,        0,                (16+8s)(1+rho^2) ].

Its three diagonal entries are positive. Its 2x2 principal minors are

 128(2+s)rho^2,
 (832+480s)(rho^2+3-2s)^2,
 (64+32s)(1+rho^2)^2,

and its determinant is identically zero. Thus this block is PSD for
every real rho. The second block equals rho^2 times the reverse of the
first block at 1/rho, so it is PSD for rho!=0 and at zero by continuity.
All these are symbolic identities over Q(sqrt(3)).

Signed singular-value decomposition, the verified symmetries, and
homogeneity transport positivity to every real 2x2 A. At zero the
polynomial density is zero. The ordinary heat/Stokes argument therefore
applies with the actual cubic primitive (7).

For gamma=-2, F=Tr[(A^T A)^2] is convex. Indeed, for any direction B,

 F''(A)[B,B]=2 Tr[(A^T B+B^T A)^2]+4 Tr[(A^T A)(B^T B)] >=0.

For every gamma between -2 and gamma*, interpolate the energies and
their corrections between this convex energy with zero correction and
(7). This produces a literal closed polynomial correction and proves
sufficiency throughout (1). Translation gamma=4(r-1) gives (2).

The strongest ordinary QC comparison is immediate:

 G_gamma=Tr[(A^T A)^2]+(gamma+2)(det A)^2

is polyconvex for EVERY gamma>=-2. No heat certificate improves that QC
range. The new calculation instead exactly locates the limitation of
the regular-origin heat mechanism.


4. A SERIOUS FAILED REPAIR: POINTWISE POSITIVITY WITHOUT CLOSEDNESS

A natural attack after the first heat obstruction is to complete the
pointwise Hessian density directly. For G_gamma with gamma>=0 this is
particularly easy. Put

 sigma=da wedge dc+db wedge dd,       Omega_nc=-4 gamma q sigma.

The determinant chain rule and compatibility give the EXACT identity

 C_F,Omega_nc(A)[h,h]
   =sum_j [8(A:Bj)^2+4T|Bj|^2+2 gamma(cof A:Bj)^2] >=0.

It even has a positive margin for A!=0 and h!=0. Thus pointwise
completion is not the missing resource in this comparison.

But dOmega_nc=-4 gamma dq wedge sigma is nonzero in general: its
da wedge db wedge dc coefficient is -4 gamma A21. The correction cannot
be discarded under integration. The exact code checks both the sum of
squares identity and this exterior derivative. Repairing closedness
while retaining positivity is a substantive constraint; it is not
provided by the pointwise formula. The regular-origin classification
shows that no smooth-at-zero repair exists beyond gamma*, while the
first tranche's heat-increase witness excludes even angular repairs for
sufficiently large gamma. Increasing polynomial degree cannot fix this.

This failed attack changes the conclusion: the acquired angular form's
origin regularity is part of what the method needs, rather than a
removable consequence of a numerical profile. At B1 this statement is
proved by the finite obstruction, while existence at C^(1,1) is supplied
by the already accepted exact certificate.


5. EXACT SCOPE, ORDINARY ALTERNATIVES, AND REPLAY

The B1 result is a necessity theorem for the ordinary componentwise
heat/exact-two-form proof. It does not classify all proofs of its QC,
exclude nonlinear auxiliary flows, or convert certificate failure into
a QC counterexample. Its working acquired angular certificate remains
accepted at precisely its original scope. Its non-polyconvexity rules
out the ordinary convex-minor alternative at that seed; it does not make
the present regularity obstruction a general Morrey result.

The isotropic calculation gives a complete comparison where ordinary
polyconvexity already settles QC. This prevents treating a richer heat
ansatz as a universally necessary route. The inherited local lift
completeness theorem still begins after acquiring a legitimate closed
correction and does not resolve that enabling operation.

The central broader obligation remains a global mechanism for the
unclassified anisotropic rank-one-convex quartics, including the rest of
the curved boundary. These regularity and completeness results do not
establish that mechanism. No historical priority or physical capability
is claimed. Phase two used no external literature comparison, so novelty
is not inferred from the calculations.
