## Result: a physical interface for learning rare risks without identifying the hidden dynamics

I constructed a conditional route to the intended capability: **make the probability accounting observable at a controlled source, and use a global physical inequality to cover the receiver’s hidden failure paths.** I also constructed a mechanical family in which one endpoint displacement supplies the failure observable, and a physical seating operation supplies a finite-precision preparation bound.

This is a substantive acquisition mechanism. It does not establish a new superior importance-sampling algorithm, a validated device, or a civilisation-changing advantage. The strongest conventional sampler given the same source access performs the same experiment and ties it exactly. That comparison determines what we should retain: the physical interface and its information requirements.

## 1. The consequential use chosen first

The use is **qualifying unintended release or loss of a stored state in an elastic mechanical memory or latch under a specified stochastic loading environment**.

Mechanical bits are a real experimental family: recent work demonstrates targeted writing of five mechanical bits using global rotation and acceleration. Experiments on coupled mechanical hysterons also show that returning an external control to a previous value need not restore the internal state. These results support both the application and the seriousness of the preparation problem. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

The scientific question became:

> Can controlled loading experiments establish a useful deadline-risk bound without learning the assembly’s internal modes, transition pathways or microscopic forces—and without waiting for failures under its nominal loading law?

I restricted the target environment to a **specified, physically generated stochastic source with passive coupling and back-reaction**. This is an environment we can reproduce and alter exactly. Transferring the answer to arbitrary ambient vibration or uncontrolled thermal forcing requires additional information.

The experiment needs:

- A known scalar energy reservoir and a logged randomization command.
- Physical isolation while that command is applied, followed by passive reconnection.
- A bound on initial stored energy and on unaccounted switching work.
- A physical lower bound on the energy crossed by every counted failure.
- A failure detector and the same actual preparation protocol across nominal and accelerated experiments.

It does **not** need a microscopic transition model or an operation that prepares arbitrary hidden states.

## 2. The mechanism: put the likelihood where we can measure it

Consider an auxiliary rotor or mass with observed momentum \(y\) and known energy

\[
E_r(y)=\frac{y^2}{2m}.
\]

While isolated from the receiver, program its next momentum. For \(0<a<1\), let

\[
s^2=m\theta(1-a^2).
\]

The nominal and accelerated programs are

\[
P_R:\quad y'\sim N(ay,s^2)\ \text{conditioned on } |y'|\le R,
\]

\[
Q_R:\quad y'\sim N(y/a,s^2/a^2)\ \text{conditioned on } |y'|\le R.
\]

Then reconnect the reservoir passively to the unknown assembly.

The Gaussian law now belongs to **random numbers we generate**. The receiver can be nonlinear, have many hidden coordinates, and exhibit unknown passive dissipation.

### Exact observable probability accounting

The ratio of the two programmed densities is

\[
\frac{p_R(y'\mid y)}{q_R(y'\mid y)}
=
c(y)\exp\!\left[-\frac{E_r(y')-E_r(y)}{\theta}\right],
\qquad c(y)\le a^{-1}.
\]

The normalizing factor \(c(y)\) is computable from one-dimensional Gaussian interval probabilities. It includes the truncation; clipping an unbounded experiment and retaining its old likelihood would be wrong.

Across an experiment, multiply these observable ratios. All unchanged conditional receiver dynamics cancel—even when those dynamics depend on an unobserved history.

Now suppose total recoverable storage satisfies \(H\ge0\), preparation satisfies \(H_0\le E_0\), and every counted failure crosses \(H\ge B\). Let \(W\) bound additional supplied work, including the relevant measurement allowances. With at most \(N\) delivered source updates,

\[
p_N=\mathbb E_{Q_R}[Y],
\qquad
Y=L_K\,\mathbf 1_{\{\text{failure}\}},
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
0\le Y\le
w_N:=a^{-N}\exp\!\left[-\frac{B-E_0-W}{\theta}\right].
}
\]

The proof is ordinary energy accounting. At failure,

\[
\sum_{j\le K}\Delta E_{r,j}\ge B-E_0-W.
\]

Insert that inequality into the product of source likelihoods. **Every hidden path is covered by the same bound.**

This separates two achievements:

1. Logging the changed random source makes the likelihood observable.
2. A physical barrier and preparation budget make the failure contributions uniformly bounded.

The second does not follow from the first.

### Finite hardware and imperfect servos

The reservoir endpoints are bounded, so the construction does not require arbitrarily energetic source commands. The protocol can also stop amplification once the source itself reaches the barrier energy; the subsequent nominal evolution preserves the bounded contribution.

An imperfect servo need not have a known microscopic response density. Randomize and log its **command**, and keep its actual response channel identical under both programs. That channel cancels too. What remains necessary is an energy-error allowance: the difference between commanded and actually delivered energy must fit inside \(W\).

Fixed timing, genuine isolation, switching work and compliant coupling are physical obligations. In particular, bounded energy alone does not bound the peak force of a rigid impact.

## 3. Earning the barrier and preparation in the same physical family

The main constructive advance beyond a supplied port model is a family of elastic linkages.

Take a planar chain of \(J\) rigid links with torsional springs, mounted horizontally. Hold its axial span at \(D<L\). Its right endpoint moves on a transverse slider, and the stored state is the sign of its actual transverse displacement

\[
Q=\sum_i\ell_i\sin\vartheta_i.
\]

Assume geometric stops impose \(|\vartheta_i|\le\Theta<\pi/2\), and component information bounds the joint elastic energy above and below by quadratic forms with constants \(k_{\max}\) and \(k_{\min}\). Individual joint laws, inertias, modal frequencies and damping coefficients can remain unknown.

Every continuous change of the stored sign crosses \(Q=0\).

### A barrier covering every internal configuration

For equal link lengths \(\ell\), with shortening \(\Delta=L-D\), I proved

\[
\boxed{
B=
\frac{k_{\min}}{\ell}\,
4\sin^2\!\left(\frac{\pi}{J+1}\right)
\left(\frac{\sin\Theta}{\Theta}\right)^2
\Delta.
}
\]

Every admissible configuration with \(Q=0\) has elastic energy at least \(B\).

The proof applies a cycle Poincaré inequality to \(u_i=\sin\vartheta_i\). The endpoint condition supplies its zero-mean constraint; fixed shortening supplies the required amount of angular displacement. The report gives the unequal-length version and the penalty for finite endpoint-readout accuracy.

This covers all internal configurations of the declared linkage family. Fracture, joint slip, unaccounted prestress and additional hidden storage require separate treatment.

### A physical preparation operation

Extend the linkage nearly to its full length, engage a fixture that brakes the relevant joints, and measure the remaining closure defect \(\delta_{\rm seat}\). Geometry and the stiffness upper bound give

\[
\boxed{
E_{\rm reset}
\le
\frac{\pi^2k_{\max}}{\ell_{\min}}\delta_{\rm seat}
+
E_{\rm residual}.
}
\]

Then restore the operating shortening under a prescribed bias while measuring work at the axial and transverse ports:

\[
E_0\le E_{\rm reset}+W_{\rm prep}.
\]

This is an energy-bounded preparation without reconstruction of the internal state. The global brake is real additional hardware; endpoint quietness does not establish its action.

For a 64-link example, the executable geometry calculation gives:

| Quantity | Value in the declared dimensionless units |
|---|---:|
| Barrier at every endpoint sign crossing | \(0.333987\) |
| Barrier with endpoint uncertainty \(0.001\) | \(0.333970\) |
| Reset allowance at closure defect \(10^{-7}\) | \(0.004447\) |
| Upper bound on a low-energy stored template | \(0.106023\) |

A complete geometrically admissible sign-flip path also exists. Thus the barrier does not obtain its apparent success by excluding every possible transition.

If actual measured preparation work were at most \(0.11\), with negligible additional residual allowances, the preparation bound would be \(E_0\le0.114447<B\). **That work limit is an apparatus requirement, not a measured result.** Geometric path existence also does not prove that a scalar actuator can execute it.

## 4. What the executable evidence establishes

I tested the source estimator on a separate nonlinear passive mechanical fixture with four or sixteen hidden elastic coordinates. This is not a simulation of the linkage above.

The selected confirmation used a fresh random stream, separate from the exploratory runs that selected the coupling.

| Experiment | Trials | Nominal-risk estimate | Conservative 95% interval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Moderate risk, nominal sampling | 40,000 | \(4.650\times10^{-3}\) | \([4.007,5.366]\times10^{-3}\) |
| Same moderate-risk task, accelerated sampling | 12,000 | \(4.556\times10^{-3}\) | \([4.178,5.720]\times10^{-3}\) |
| Rare-risk task, fresh accelerated confirmation | 24,000 | \(3.894\times10^{-13}\) | \([1.959,6.621]\times10^{-13}\) |
| Rare-risk task, smaller integration step | 12,000 | \(3.740\times10^{-13}\) | \([1.391,8.250]\times10^{-13}\) |

The fresh experiment observed **7,338 accelerated releases**. Those are not nominal failures. The confidence interval comes from a bounded-weight construction: independently retain each weighted contribution \(Y\) with probability \(Y/w_N\), producing an ordinary Bernoulli observation with mean \(p_N/w_N\). The confirmation produced 12 such retained observations; an exact binomial interval then supplies the reported conservative interval.

For this fixture,

\[
w_N=7.583\times10^{-10}.
\]

The energy certificate alone therefore fails the example requirement \(p_N<10^{-12}\). The experiment supplies consequential information beyond that certificate.

I also proved a coupling bound transferring the bounded-source result to its specified unbounded nominal Gaussian source. Its allowance here is \(1.407\times10^{-16}\), giving an upper confidence endpoint of approximately

\[
6.623\times10^{-13}<10^{-12}.
\]

The entire archived numerical programme used **187,000 trials**, including all pilots, failed variants, comparison runs and fresh confirmation. The selected confirmation used 4,435,755 delivered source updates.

The numerical dynamics use an explicitly energy-projected finite integration map. Step refinement changed the selected point estimate by about \(4\%\), within sampling uncertainty. These intervals concern that map; they do not certify a continuum solver or a physical device.

## 5. The strongest ordinary construction ties

The relevant comparator is controlled source importance sampling, including directionally negative-friction sampling. That literature already contains directional acceleration, path-observable reweighting and adaptive termination. The continuous white-noise precursor developed during this run belongs to that established family. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}

Earlier proposals for recovering low-force kinetics from accelerated single-molecule experiments explicitly encountered difficulties associated with microscopic forces and temporal resolution. Generating and logging the changed source addresses that acquisition issue in the present architecture. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}

With the **same source, reset, observations and interventions**, a conventional importance sampler uses the same kernels and likelihoods. Its statistical performance is identical.

For perspective, nominal testing would require approximately \(3.0\times10^{12}\) independent trials merely to place a one-sided 95% upper bound below \(10^{-12}\) **if it observed zero failures**. But the resulting enormous trial-count comparison is a comparison with nominal testing. It is not an advantage over the strongest equally informed method, nor a measured hardware speedup.

The retained result is therefore an experimental architecture that makes known probability machinery usable with a hidden receiver.

## 6. The failures that constrain the next advance

### Almost straight is not an energy certificate

The continuum predecessor had a valid all-shape barrier, but its finite-precision reset failed.

For

\[
y_k(x)=\frac{A}{k}\sin(k\pi x/D),
\]

the slope distribution and arclength excess remain fixed as \(k\) increases, while bending energy grows as \(k^2\). Displacement amplitude tends to zero.

Thus arbitrarily small displacement or extension defect can conceal arbitrarily large continuum bending energy. The finite-feature linkage repairs this specific failure by paying for a minimum feature size and a global brake.

That payment matters: at fixed total length with continuum-like spring scaling, the barrier stays of order one, while the reset allowance scales as

\[
E_{\rm reset}\sim J^2\delta_{\rm seat}.
\]

Removing modal identification does not remove the precision cost.

### Thermodynamic work does not identify hidden kinetics

The complementary construction produced a hidden-gate counterexample: systems can share equilibrium and accessible work information while having different deadline risks. If the available control does not act on the hidden gate, identifying its rate still requires exposure on its own rare timescale.

The reservoir construction does not invalidate that obstruction. It changes the available experiment by making the changed randomness observable. A weakly coupled or slowly opening hidden gate can still make its statistical cost prohibitive.

### An exact likelihood does not guarantee useful overlap

The variance satisfies

\[
\operatorname{Var}(\widehat p_N)
\le \frac{w_Np_N-p_N^2}{n}.
\]

Relative learning cost depends on \(w_N/p_N\). Hidden dissipation, unfavorable coupling, long delays or narrow successful input directions can make that ratio enormous.

The retained unfavorable-coupling run observed no accelerated releases. It supplies an upper bound, not a relative-accuracy estimate or proof of zero risk. An earlier unbounded-source pilot accumulated excessive energy without useful performance; it is preserved as a failed proposal.

### Deadline risk is not an uncontrolled lifetime

The factor \(a^{-N}\) deteriorates with mission length. The construction does not determine an unrefreshed device’s mean lifetime or justify exponential extrapolation.

If the operating device itself has independent identical reset cycles, a cycle-risk estimate can determine a geometric lifetime. A laboratory reset does not establish that operating property.

## What should survive this run

The general principle worth carrying forward is:

> **Engineer the point where randomness enters so that its likelihood is observable; construct a global physical constraint that every counted failure must cross; and acquire preparation through an operation whose energy cost can be bounded.**

The synthetic experiment shows that the resulting bounded contributions can resolve a deadline risk far below what nominal testing could practically measure.
