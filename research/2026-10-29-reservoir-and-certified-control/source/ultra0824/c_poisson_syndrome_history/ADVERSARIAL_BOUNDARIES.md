# Adversarial checks and exact failure controls

29 October 2026. No additional trajectories or source cases were acquired.

**The finite control is not a native programme source.** Its explicitly
constructed XZZXI/IXZZX/XIXZZ/ZXIXZ checks define the standard non-CSS
[[5,1,3]] code. The programme uses a different planar CSS [[5,1,2]] seed;
the frozen source masks were read to correct an earlier informal SAME-seed
claim. SOURCE_SCOPE.json records this correction. The general theorem
and analytic SAME_CONTROLLER instantiation are unchanged; the numerical
control is not evidence of native memory performance. No substitute
native run was made merely to preserve the earlier wording.

**Coherence is not replaced by the physical Pauli twirl.** The exact
standard non-CSS [[5,1,3]] consumer applies two rational rotations at one site with
no intervening read cut. The final nonzero syndrome has probability
576/625, while independently twirling the two physical events would give
288/625. The logical channel happens to be identity in this correctable
case; its record probabilities still retain interference. The theorem
does not preserve physical twirl probabilities or produce independent
classical labels.

**Syndrome measurement alone is not an exact universal Pauliization.**
Three uncut single-site rational rotations on the seed's minimum logical
word XYXII produce an actual logical chi entry

    chi_(I,Y)=3456 i/15625 !=0.

This is not a failed numerical tolerance: it is exact rational arithmetic.
The fault component has a noncorrectable three-site union. The SAME three
events separated by the check cuts give a diagonal logical chi matrix,
exactly matching the equally informed full Pauli-mixture comparator.
Thus the component-correctability condition is substantive, and the
positive construction changes the dynamics rather than merely renaming it.

**The nominal controller can move common errors without moving their
quantum difference.** A second exact control uses a coherent fault at
site0, a nonunital amplitude-damping channel at site1, and an intervening
nominal check measurement/conditional correction at site2, outside their
two-site union. The resulting logical chi is exactly diagonal, consistent
with the recordwise normalizer proof. This checks the difficult distinction
between common nominal propagation and relative Pauli support.

**The exploration needs more than an average clock speed.** It reveals a
new check interval only once, and counts already known faults as already
discovered. A newly consulted read process has not been consulted to
choose the center time. Its two exponential gaps are truncated by old
boundaries; overlapping fault-process portions are filled only by a
dominating independent process, not counted twice. These deferred-decision
facts pay the conditional joint moment (4). Inserted roots enter the
initial queue and are not Poisson offspring. The exact single-check
Palm control has T=1+K_left+K_right, where the two K are geometric; its
probability and tail identities are replayed without simulation. The
general graph tail and multipoint supermartingale are analytic proofs,
not extrapolations from that control.

**Nominal jump statistics cannot be reweighted for free.** We use
state-independent attempt maps with deterministic syndrome decisions and
independent finite coins, all included in the nominal schedule. The
thermal seed conversion in SAME_CONTROLLER.md actually adds syndrome
dephasing to obtain that form. A merely formal Pauli-times-function Kraus
description of arbitrary state-dependent jumps would not by itself give
the asserted support-respecting classical path law.

**The reference process can be noncausal.** Representatives are selected
after a coherence component has sealed. Their distribution can depend
on later cuts and environmental records, even though the real physical
channel is causal. The proof therefore imports no causal Markov process,
no hotter Gibbs law, and no ordinary Pauli threshold whose adversary is
only past-adapted. A true pathwise eroder bound, uniform over all labels
on a given actual fault pattern, is sufficient. That is still unpaid for
the actual nominal controller. The comparison Pauli channels at different
horizons need not form one semigroup.

**Classical clock failure is not repaired by this lemma.** Treating noisy
controller bits as trivial Z-check ancillas permits the same quantum
analysis, but does not make their classical state reliable. The frozen
six-cell absorbing-core counterexample remains a classical persistence
failure. Dephasing a corrupted clock bit does not restore its correct value.
This consistency check is why the separate delta_cl term is retained.

**Static energy/Peierls is not the missing dynamical proof.** The added
syndrome dephasing leaves the nominal Gibbs state and its Pauli rates
unchanged. The unknown CPTP events do not preserve that state. Pointwise
rate bounds do not give attractive-process domination on a general joint
syndrome graph, and a global path-likelihood comparison can cost order
n*t. No such argument is substituted for delta_cl. Both CSS sectors are
kept jointly; no sector-separable reduction is asserted.

**The ideal local generator is a resource, not a laboratory result.** The
construction adds O(n) bounded-support dephasing channels at finite
per-check rate Gamma, and possibly the fixed error-admission ballast.
Their own local Markovian implementation errors must lie in the warranted
error class; a microscopic bath, finite-reservoir approximation, non-Markov
controller memory or hardware bandwidth is not acquired by this proof.
No repeated external perfect syndrome apparatus or physical history log
is supplied. The ideal final decoder and nominal initializer retain their
separate input/cost/error contracts.

For clarity, the sufficient classical quantity is

    delta_path=E_(schedule,s)[max_(supported Pauli words) 1_failure],

not merely the supremum of expected failure over past-adapted strategies.
The maximum is inside the expectation. Initial syndrome knowledge is
also allowed to that adverse labeling, while its law remains the actual
fixed encoder law. Exchanging these two orders without proof would be a
real additional gap; no such exchange is used here.
