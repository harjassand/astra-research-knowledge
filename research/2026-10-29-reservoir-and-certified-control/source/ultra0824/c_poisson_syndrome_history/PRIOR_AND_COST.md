29 October 2026.

The controlling ordinary coherence prior is
[Beale--Wallman--Gutierrez--Brown--Laflamme1805.08802v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1805.08802v2).
The primary SectionsIII--V and Theorem2 were read. It proves logical
off-diagonal suppression for tensor-product local noise under ideal
encoding, syndrome measurement, correction and decoding, with a convex
mixture extension to certain correlated channels. It also explicitly warns
that the logical Pauli channel need not be the one obtained by twirling
the physical noise. We do not claim the generic decoherence principle
as new. Our finite exact repeated-event control enforces that warning.

[Tamiya--Koashi2609.20708v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20708v1), primary
setting and Theorem1 read, is a stronger static threshold comparator than
a numerical coherent-error example. Its theorem concerns products of
coherent Z rotations, ideal full syndrome readout and maximum-likelihood
Pauli recovery. It supplies no constant-rate autonomous schedule or the
unknown-CPTP-event classical persistence contract needed here.

and the original [primary report](https://open.bu.edu/items/4477fcb8-0eff-4eff-aaaa-f388ca82c7be)

stabilization without further errors. These are strong conventional
alternatives; neither is silently treated as a ready quantum-data

## What was actually constructed

The fixed controller modification is explicit: finite-rate stabilizer
dephasing plus a local measurement-and-Pauli attempt form for each nominal
thermal seed. It preserves the nominal classical rates, nominal Gibbs
encoder and same syndrome-defined decoder. No timing register is added.
Noisy measured classical bits can alternatively be included through
trivial Z-check ancillas; their reliability is not assumed.

The quantum-to-Pauli theorem is all-orders at finite Gamma, all input
states/references, and all volumes under the stated bounded support/
incidence conditions. It gives a small-fault-rate component tail and an
explicit multipoint exponential exposure bound. It therefore handles the
coherence part of the proposed finite stabilizer-dephasing strategy
without a finite-order Zeno remainder. It does not provide delta_cl,
the full all-input memory threshold, an efficient classical simulator,
or a physical device.

## Actual executable work

`exact_controls.py` ran once successfully on a standard non-CSS [[5,1,3]]
code, reused unchanged across its diagnostic comparisons,
with no imported producer code. Its receipt EXACT_CONTROLS.json records
0.691764666 seconds after imports, 74,727,424-byte peak RSS and zero
samples. The arithmetic is exact SymPy1.14 rational/complex arithmetic.
All32-dimensional code/projector/instrument matrices, the16 Pauli decoder
representatives, the minimum normalizer support and the logical chi
entries were constructed literally. This finite quantum matrix calculation is a diagnostic; no stochastic
quantum/bath trajectory or physical state was acquired.

Controls comprise a nonunital correctable-union instrument, an uncut
coherent logical-support failure, its exact same-event cut repair, the
full-information Pauli-mixture comparator, and the same-site physical-
twirl probability failure. The single-check Palm probability/tail
identities are exact fractions. They validate the finite controls, not
the all-volume proof by extrapolation.

Primary HTML acquisition for the two coherence papers used direct public
HTTPS; cached bytes/hashes are in source_entry/SOURCES.json. Other cited
primary comparator scopes are explicitly limited above. No paid tools,
physical action, external writes or new source trajectory/sample pool
were used. Previous frozen packages were preserved.

The actual remaining resource gate is now sharply classical: a sustained-
noise, pathwise Pauli eroder/persistence theorem for the joint syndrome
and any noisy classical controller, with real local-bit rates and the
same decoder. The quantum reduction does not acquire such a theorem by
applying Gibbs stationarity, a mean clock velocity, a synchronous active
threshold or a record-preserving perfect-measurement fallback.
