## Governing prior and strongest ordinary construction

Scandi and Alhambra's corrected primary theorem already supplies a microscopic
Gaussian-bath derivation of the exact finite Gaussian-averaged KMS generator,
including Lamb renormalization and a linear-time comparison. The mechanism,
the canonical tanh correction and the avoidance of a rotating-wave limit are
theirs. A reservoir compiler alone would not be an invention of a new general
thermalization mechanism. Their general theorem does not furnish the mixing
gap needed for this clock family; the accepted weak-product all-cold result
and the bare-seed/positive-average transfer supply that extra input here.

Woods, Cramer and Plenio's particle-chain construction and Theorem1 already
pay finite-horizon truncation of passive oscillator reservoirs. The bath-state
positive coherent mixture, matrix-function computation and orthogonal-polynomial
recurrence are ordinary Gaussian/numerical tools. Our explicit Gram floor,
moment algorithm and driven-protocol occupation bound pay the required
interfaces instead of treating them as free inputs. No advantage over an
equally informed use of these same tools is claimed.

The formal Davies generator has the same parent gap by positive infinite-time
averaging. It is not an equally complete microscopic receiver at polynomial
coupling when its derivation requires resolving exponentially close many-body
Bohr frequencies. The stronger ordinary alternative here is the finite-coarse-
graining theorem, which we use, rather than an artificially weak Davies bound.
Generic quantum Gibbs algorithms/circuit simulation remain different admitted
operations; they do not pay an analogue apparatus merely by existing.

## Full-cycle theoretical resources

With fixed admitted clock family, E=O(n), and U=O(n(1+b)+log epsilon^-1),
one loose receiver has alpha^-1=O(epsilon^-1 m^5(1+b)^40 U) and
tau=O(epsilon^-2 m^10(1+b)^80 U^3). The finite bath uses m independent
chains of length M=O(tau+log(m/epsilon)); its own Gaussian thermal state is
prepared before each use. A finite Gaussian amplitude clip plus classical
matrix/force computation pays this preparation. Both the initialization
forces and subsequent cooling are included in the local-occupation cutoff.
Finite calibration and switching errors are bounded for the whole horizon.

The resulting mode count, total occupation capacity, peak force/energy
upper bounds, inverse tolerances and classical work are polynomials in
n,b,epsilon^-1. They are not polynomial in log b/log epsilon^-1. Bath
coupling alpha is deliberately size/temperature/accuracy dependent. Data
lambda and penalty Delta are fixed family constants. Native lambda_B,T_B
and useful hardware constants have not been evaluated. Extremely small
family constants can therefore make this construction impractical.

Initialization consumes explicit one-mode vacuum resources and local
calibrated classical forces. The construction does not obtain these from an
unspecified ambient bath or make a universal full-cycle thermodynamic-work
claim. It also does not grant ideal controlled system-Hamiltonian evolution
or a many-body spectral oracle. Analogue evolution uses the supplied local
Hamiltonian table and the separately acquired bath-only table.

## Boundaries that change the research decision

The positive result removes the ideal quantum-gate and spectral-controller
prerequisites from CONDITIONAL preparation at a declared microscopic model
level. It does not establish economical native couplings or a real device.
Prefix-pulled source couplings are constant-body; a two-body-only apparatus
is not supplied. A bounded-density physical 3D layout of the polynomially
long independent bath tails is not supplied. These are genuine outstanding
resources rather than mathematical confidence qualifications.

The global weak-coupling comparison pays polynomial-time preparation only.
It does not imply a volume-uniform local multi-time fault budget, and cannot
be multiplied out to stretched-exponential storage at fixed bath coupling.
No full-syndrome Gibbs initialization is inferred from a conditional sampler
without the separate full free-energy syndrome weights. No experiment,
physical calibration, quantum state, hardware benchmark, historical novelty,
or practical advantage has been acquired.
