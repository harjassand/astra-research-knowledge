The accepted source reports a normalized-Hilbert--Schmidt relaxation theorem
under its positive conditional flip floor, actual bounded neighborhood,
positive dephasing and full-support interaction conditions. It does not assert
arbitrary pure-state trace mixing or a dimension-independent relative-entropy
contraction theorem. We retain that scope and do not routinely re-audit the
accepted underlying construction from unavailable attachments.

Attempted stronger consequence: show arbitrary-state entropy deficit decays
at a dimension-independent rate, which would control genuinely nontracial
information loss. Directly copying an instantaneous modified log-Sobolev
argument fails, for a concrete algebraic reason.

Consider generator
   d rho/dt = -i[H,rho] + sum_j gamma_j (Z_j rho Z_j-rho),
where the Z_j are diagonal phase flips. Choose a full-rank nonuniform diagonal
rho. Its entropy deficit relative to the maximally mixed state is positive.
At t=0 all dephasing terms vanish. The Hamiltonian contribution to the entropy
derivative is zero because
   Tr([H,rho] log rho)=Tr(H[rho,log rho])=0.
Thus the initial entropy-deficit derivative is zero. No inequality asserting
an immediate strictly negative slope bounded by a fixed positive multiple of
the deficit can hold for every such state.

This does not disprove delayed entropy contraction: the Hamiltonian can first
generate coherences which the dephasing then removes. A potentially useful
statement would require, for some dimension-independent tau,c>0,
   integral_0^tau sum_j gamma_j D(rho_s || Z_j rho_s Z_j) ds
      >= c D(rho_0 || I/d).
The present work did not prove this for the full inherited model class.
Moving a quadratic tracial argument into the state-dependent BKM geometry
does not follow merely by replacing a norm symbol. Diagonal-unitary conjugates
of independent driven dephasing admit ordinary tensorization arguments, but
that known special structure does not close the general question.

This was retained as a useful failure and did not become the selected main
opening. There is no claimed entropy theorem, physical reset protocol, or
quantum-device capability from this unfinished line.
