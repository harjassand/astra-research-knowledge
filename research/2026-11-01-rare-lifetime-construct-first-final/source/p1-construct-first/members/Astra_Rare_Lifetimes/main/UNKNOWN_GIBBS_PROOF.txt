1. A useful general sensitivity theorem
=======================================
Let L=-Q be a finite irreducible generator with positive unnormalized invariant
weights w. Write D=diag(w)L. The directed flows w(x)q_xy are balanced, so D has
zero row and column sums. Decompose this flow into positive directed cycle
coefficients c_j on a specified common cycle architecture. A cycle has length
at most ell. Its ordinary bilinear form B_j and energy E_j obey
   |B_j(f,h)| <= K sqrt(E_j(f) E_j(h)),   K=csc(pi/ell).
This follows from the Fourier modes of a cycle. Two- and three-cycles have
K=2/sqrt(3)<=7/6. This concerns STATIONARY-FLOW cycles; state-dependent accepted
moves need not themselves be permutation kernels.

Let the fitted model have weights what and coefficients chat_j, satisfying
   exp(-u) w <= what <= exp(u) w pointwise,
   exp(-v) c_j <= chat_j <= exp(v) c_j for every cycle.
For any nonempty proper target A, let M and Mhat be mean hitting times from
the respective stationary laws conditioned on B=A^c.

THEOREM 4 (weights and circulation).
   exp(-3u)/F(v,K) <= Mhat/M <= exp(3u) F(v,K),
   F(v,K)=exp(2 asinh(K sinh(v/2))) <= exp(Kv).              (G1)
Thus
   |log(Mhat/M)| <= 3u+Kv.                               (G2)
The same event covers every target, including targets selected afterward.
No gap, rarity, state count or time horizon occurs in this comparison itself.
The gap is separately earned for the computational receiver.

Proof of flow comparison. First keep w fixed while changing the balanced
flow. The normalized forward and true-adjoint potentials of MAIN_PROOF.txt
give the exact capacity identity
   Chat-C=<h*,(Lhat-L)hhat>_pi,
with true adjoint energy C and fitted forward energy Chat. Coefficients obey
   |chat_j-c_j| <= 2 sinh(v/2) sqrt(c_j chat_j).
Apply the individual cycle form bound and Cauchy--Schwarz over cycles:
   |Chat-C| <= 2K sinh(v/2) sqrt(C Chat).
Solving the quadratic inequality for sqrt(Chat/C) proves the F bound, with
no restriction that v be small. The inequality asinh(K sinh(t))<=Kt for
K>=1 follows by differentiating, so F<=exp(Kv).

Proof of the weight comparison. At any fixed balanced flow D, the killed
matrix D_B has entrywise nonnegative inverse. The hitting equation is
   D_B t_B=w_B,
so, writing W_B=sum_B w,
   M=w_B^T D_B^(-1)w_B/W_B.
Replacing w by what at fixed D changes the positive numerator by at most
exp(2u) in either direction and the denominator by at most exp(u). Combine
this exp(3u) comparison with the preceding flow comparison. End proof.

The proof handles the change in the conditioned initial law; simply applying
a common-pi theorem after changing the weights would not be valid. The bound
is conservative and gauge dependent: weights and flows must use the same
chosen normalization. In the explicit model w(all-zero)=1 fixes that gauge.

2. Concrete unknown path model
==============================
The state is x in {0,1}^n. Unknown local factors satisfy
   0.9<=a_i,b_i<=1.1,
   w(x)=product_i a_i^(x_i) product_i b_i^(x_i x_(i+1)).
Unknown positive directed-cycle coefficients satisfy 0.5<=alpha_i<=1.

There are unit-rate heatbath redraws at each site. Its conditional odds are
   r_i(l,r)=a_i b_(i-1)^l b_i^r,
with missing boundary factors omitted. There is also a directed three-cycle
on each neighboring bit pair, alternating orientation by pair index. With
little-endian pair code x_i+2x_(i+1), the forward map is [1,3,2,0] and its
inverse [3,0,2,1]. Code 2 is fixed. Let v_i be the product of all factors
touching the pair. The cycle transition rate is alpha_i/v_i.

For fixed outside bits, w=w_outside*v_i. Every directed edge on that orbit
therefore has unnormalized stationary flow alpha_i*w_outside, proving balance.
Heatbath flow on an edge joining endpoint weights w0,w1 is
   w0*w1/(w0+w1).
The full source has invariant law w/Z, while its directed currents generally
break detailed balance. This is an engineered stochastic architecture. A
thermal implementation or its entropy production is not supplied here.

3. What the calibration service actually exposes
================================================
An intervention can hold at most three local bits and observe the selected
native candidate-transition counter for a calibrated duration T. It must leave
the native local kernel and clock law unchanged. This modularity is a stated
source interface, not a consequence of the Gibbs stationary distribution.
Physical clamp setup, reset, holding and instrument work are extra resources;
no physical source was operated in the present execution.

At each site measure both flip directions with existing neighbors zero.
Their rate ratio is a_i. For i<n also set the right neighbor to one and left
neighbor to zero: the ratio is a_i b_i. Divide the two RAW context ratios to
obtain b_i before rounding anything. At pair state 00, v_i=1 regardless of
outside neighbors, so its directed rate directly measures alpha_i.

There are 4n-2 heatbath counts and n-1 cycle counts, H=5n-3 in total. With
each count Poisson(T lambda), every measured positive rate exceeds 1/3.
For relative tolerance eta, ordinary Poisson Chernoff gives
   failure <= 2H exp[-T eta^2/9].                        (G3)
The raw estimates are count/T, and all factor ratios are rational. On this
common event, putting t=-log(1-eta), the factor log errors are at most 2t for
a, 4t for b, and t for alpha. Therefore all full-state weights obey
   |log(what/w)| <= (6n-4)t.

In the execution, clip a,b to their known range [0.9,1.1], alpha to [0.5,1],
and round each independently to denominator 100000. Clipping reduces log error;
nearest-grid rounding costs at most q=1/50000 in each log parameter. For
eta=1/10000 use the rational upper bound t<=1/9999. Then
   u=(6n-4)/9999+(2n-1)/50000,
   v=u+1/9999+1/50000.                                  (G4)

Why v suffices: heatbath conductance is coordinatewise increasing and
homogeneous of degree one in (w0,w1), hence its log error is at most u. Each
directed-cycle flow contains alpha times a SUBSET of the weight factors, so
its error is at most u+t+q. No unknown normalization constant enters these
unnormalized flow coefficients.

At n=80, u=25389841/499950000 and v=35347/694375. A positive Taylor series
with a geometric remainder proves
   exp(3u+(7/6)v)<5/4.
Thus a single calibration event gives a source mean factor 5/4 for every
target. No true rare target mass or true conditional sampler is passed to
the fitted receiver. The diagnostic private source factors are stored for
reproducibility and are not used in the certificate.

The elementary acquisition cost for fixed global accuracy is O(n^3) local
observations, up to accuracy and logarithmic failure factors. This is not
claimed to improve Astra N1213's existing acquisition scaling. If background
channels run during serial clamps, their operations add another factor O(n).

4. Gap, counting and conditional initialization
================================================
Changing a neighboring bit changes heatbath log odds by log b. The largest
change in its redraw probability is tanh(|log b|/4). With b in [0.9,1.1],
the sum of neighbor influences is at most 2tanh(log(10/9)/4)<0.053.
Synchronous heatbath coupling contracts Hamming distance at the complementary
rate. Since the heatbath component is reversible, a nonconstant eigenfunction
with smaller eigenvalue would contradict this contraction. Its gap is at
least 12sqrt(10)-37>0.947. Every added stationary cycle has nonnegative
symmetric energy, so the true and fitted full additive-symmetric gaps remain
at least this value. The implementation uses the deliberately coarse g=1/2.
The independent LOCAL_PATH_CALIBRATION.txt gives a detailed coupling proof.

For a target fixing arbitrary specified bits to a pattern, two-state transfer
messages compute Z_A and Z exactly from the fitted rational factors. Reverse
messages give sequential exact conditional bit probabilities. Rational integer
rejection samples these bits without waiting for the rare event. O(n) rational
operations suffice per sample, with polynomial integer bit lengths. The actual
implementation pays for 640000 free-bit draws per target and writes its 40000
initial states. These initialize a numerical model, not a physical rare state.

The analytical sensitivity theorem applies to every target, but efficient
counting and sampling are claimed only for this cylinder grammar or another
explicitly tractable representation. Arbitrary membership access does not
supply conditional initialization. A hidden singleton among 2^n strings needs
Omega(2^n) membership queries for constant-success identification, even if its
stationary mass 2^-n is supplied.

5. Exact rational short excursions and the true deadline law
============================================================
Each heatbath channel has proposal rate 1. Each pair cycle has proposal rate 2:
alpha/v_i<=1/(0.9^5)<2. Accept its proposed move with exact rational probability
alpha/(2v_i). The global proposal rate is 3n-2. Add the killing category s=1/40.
All categorical and acceptance choices are rational and finitely implemented.
Each excursion has expected duration at most 40 and at most 40(3n-2)+1
proposal categories. Null moves are included in the reported event count.

The boundary trial draws x from the computed fitted conditional law, chooses
a potentially exiting channel proportionally to its DOMINATING proposal rate,
and executes its acceptance rule. An initial null or a move staying in A gives
zero. Otherwise it runs to return or kill. Consequently r_s=s+R theta still
holds, with R the sum of those dominating proposal rates. The existing screened
mean theorem then applies to the fitted model. Expand its interval by 5/4 for
source uncertainty. This exact composition is implemented in run_gibbs.py.

For deadlines, the TRUE target mass is also uncertain. The weight bound gives
   p_true in [p_fit exp(-2u),p_fit exp(2u)].
If these endpoints are p_lo,p_hi, a true mean interval [M_lo,M_hi] implies
   b_true M_true in [(1-p_hi)M_lo,(1-p_lo)M_hi].
Use delta_max=1/[g_lo(1-p_hi)M_lo] in LIFETIME_LAW.txt. Its conditional error
is bounded by [D(delta_max)+p_hi]/(1-p_hi). This prevents replacing a true
stationary quantity silently by the fitted one. deadline_gibbs.py implements
this outward calculation for all retained results. The same confidence event
supports every later deadline without another sampling union bound.

7. Interpretation
==================
This supplies a complete conditional route from local acquisition to rare
coarse-target means and additive deadline probabilities in driven networks.
Its important mathematical feature is that calibration error enters through
weights and stationary circulation, without multiplication by the rare mean.
Its practical scope is limited by genuine source knowledge, local intervention
validity, tractable target representation and the earned fast relaxation.

The pinned Astra source already supplied local learned Ising weights and rare
complete-configuration means. Standard literature already supplies nonreversible
capacity principles, local recrossing methods, learning from dynamics and
exponential hitting laws. The contribution of this work is its explicit
weights/flows transfer, finite nonreversible screening and lifetime inequalities,
and complete executed composition. Broad novelty and physical consequence
remain unestablished; the comparison notes preserve that finding.
