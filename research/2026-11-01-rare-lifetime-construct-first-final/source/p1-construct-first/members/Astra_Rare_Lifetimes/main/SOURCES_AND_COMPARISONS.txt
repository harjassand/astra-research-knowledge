Primary ordinary predecessors actually consulted
=================================================
1. David Aldous, Probability Approximations via the Poisson Clumping Heuristic,
   Chapter B, especially B17--B19.
   https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/Book2/book.pdf
   Its outward stationary flux times short no-return factor already predicts
   rare coarse-target hitting means, including nonreversible examples. Our
   positive recrossing experiment is not the invention of that idea.

2. Aldous and Fill, Reversible Markov Chains and Random Walks on Graphs,
   Chapters3.5 and3.7.
   https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/RWG/Book_Ralph/Ch3.S5.html
   https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/RWG/Book_Ralph/Ch3.S7.html
   Standard Green/variational formulas and Proposition3.23. The reversible
   lifetime error delta is sharper than our general nonreversible
   O(delta log(1/delta)) certificate. It must not be claimed as improved here.

3. Alexandre Gaudilliere and Claudio Landim, A Dirichlet principle for
   non-reversible Markov chains, Annals of Probability42(2014),857--897.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.2445
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.2445
   Established nonreversible forward/adjoint capacity theory and sector bounds.

4. Claudio Landim and Tiecheng Xu, Metastability of nonreversible finite state
   Markov chains, ALEA13(2016),633--659, especially section4.
   https://alea.math.cnrs.fr/articles/v13/13-29.pdf
   Cycle decomposition and comparison are established machinery. Our constant
   is proved independently by exact Fourier modes. A printed constant was
   questioned in the independent note; no broad invalidity claim is made.

5. Lu-Jing Huang and Yong-Hua Mao, Variational principles of hitting times
   for non-reversible Markov chains, JMAA(2018).
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmaa.2018.08.036
   Primal/dual mean formulas are predecessors, not newly invented identities.

6. Alessandra Bianchi, Alexandre Gaudilliere and Paolo Milanesi,
   On soft capacities, quasi-stationary distributions and the pathwise
   approach to metastability, 2018 preprint.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.11233
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.11233
   Exponential killing and soft potential theory are established ideas; the
   precise finite nonreversible bracket in this work is proved separately.

7. Pierre Mathieu, Hitting times and spectral gap inequalities,
   Annales de l'Institut Henri Poincare B33(1997),437--465.
   https://www.numdam.org/item/AIHPB_1997__33_4_437_0.pdf
   Includes non-symmetric processes, Laplace hitting potentials, gap bounds and
   exponential-law approximation (sectionIV and Theorem7). Our bounded reading
   did not locate the exact finite inequality proved here; this is not a
   historical novelty proof.

8. Philippe Flajolet and Robert Sedgewick, Analytic Combinatorics,
   TheoremIX.5, citing Feller volumeII p.538.
   https://algo.inria.fr/flajolet/Publications/book.pdf
   Standard CDF smoothing inequality with constants1/pi and24/pi, used in
   LIFETIME_LAW.txt. It is not a new smoothing result.

9. Jackson Loper, Guangyao Zhou and Stuart Geman, Capacities and efficient
   computation of first-passage probabilities, Physical Review E102,023304(2020).
   https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.102.023304
   https://stanniszhou.github.io/papers/diffusion_manuscript.pdf
   Local simulations already compute certain target-first probabilities in
   reversible diffusions. Short local rare-event simulation itself is not new.

10. Arkopal Dutt, Andrey Lokhov, Marc Vuffray and Sidhant Misra,
    Exponential Reduction in Sample Complexity with Learning of Ising Model
    Dynamics, ICML2021.
    https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/dutt21a.html
    Dynamics-based graphical-model acquisition is established.

11. Jason Gaitonde, Ankur Moitra and Elchanan Mossel,
    Learning Ising Models from Evolutions (Extended Abstract), COLT2026.
    https://proceedings.mlr.press/v336/gaitonde26a.html
    An especially strong comparator: learns bounded-degree Ising structure and
    parameters from actual state evolution without failed-update observations,
    for a broad reversible single-site class. Its observation model is weaker
    than our clamped marked service. It does not automatically cover the full
    driven-pair generator used here. Our acquisition is not a better general
    Ising-learning algorithm.

12. Claude Godreche, Dynamics of the directed Ising chain,2011.
    https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0141
    Gibbs invariance with broken detailed balance is established; our local

What changes relative to the equally informed construction
===========================================================
The conventional comparator is given the SAME learned model, target grammar,
calibration data, clocks and gap proof. It may use direct killed solves on
small systems, sparse or structured solvers where appropriate, local return
simulation, importance sampling, capacity estimates and all acquired Astra
ingredients. The comparison is not restricted to naive waiting or a dense
2^n-by-2^n matrix.

The explicit additions developed here are:
 - a finite two-sided nonreversible screening factor1+s/g, using the symmetric
   Poincare gap without conversion to a worst-case total-variation mixing time;
 - an all-target relative-rate/flow comparison, including a changed stationary
   law, that does not accumulate error over the rare lifetime;
 - a sector-free complex-potential estimate and a fully specified additive
   CDF envelope, then composition with learned mass and mean uncertainty;
 - executable positive boundary trials, exact rational conditioning and
   simulation, outward arithmetic and complete observation/operation accounting.

These precise inequalities and their composition are the candidate technical
contribution. This work has not established that an equally informed ordinary
expert could not assemble them, that no prior source contains them, or that
the resulting implementation outperforms the strongest existing software.
The broad aspiration remains consequential experimentally warranted rare-event
prediction. The achieved result is a conditional mathematical/computational
construction with unusually explicit obligations and limitations.
