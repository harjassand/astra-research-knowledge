## 1. Main result: local information can certify uniform quantum relaxation

Consider
\[
L(O)=i[H,O]+\frac12\sum_i\gamma_i(Z_iOZ_i-O),
\qquad
0<\gamma_{\min}\le\gamma_i\le\gamma_{\max}.
\]

Group the supplied Hamiltonian by the sites its Pauli terms flip:
\[
H=V(Z)+\sum_i\bigl[X_iF_i(Z)+Y_iG_i(Z)\bigr]+H_{\ge2}.
\]
Here \(F_i,G_i\) contain **all** terms flipping only spin \(i\), while \(H_{\ge2}\) contains terms flipping at least two spins.

The acquired condition is
\[
F_i(z)^2+G_i(z)^2\ge a_*^2>0
\]
for every spin and every assignment of its conditional neighborhood. Physically, every spin retains a nonzero flip amplitude in every surrounding configuration covered by that neighborhood.

Let \(d\) bound the actual neighborhood size, and acquire
\[
J=\max_j\sum_{t:j\in\operatorname{supp}P_t}|h_t|,
\]
counting the **full support** of every Hamiltonian term, including its \(Z\) factors. Define
\[
\Phi_i=\frac{Y_iF_i-X_iG_i}{2(F_i^2+G_i^2)},
\qquad
M=\max_i\|\Phi_i\|_{\mathrm{Pauli},1}.
\]
The coefficient norm \(M\) can be computed from the local tables; it also obeys
\[
M\le \frac{2^{(d-1)/2}}{a_*}.
\]

Set
\[
g=2\sqrt{2(1+d)}\,JM+\frac{\gamma_{\max}}{2a_*},
\qquad
\lambda=
\frac13\min\!\left\{
a_*,
\frac{\gamma_{\min}}{2J/a_*+g^2}
\right\}.
\]

Then every traceless operator satisfies
\[
\boxed{
\|e^{tL}O\|_2\le \sqrt3\,e^{-\lambda t}\|O\|_2,
}
\]
in normalized Hilbert–Schmidt norm.

**The rate is independent of system size when the acquired local bounds remain fixed.** The theorem permits strong signed and complex interactions, including multiple-spin flips. Their actual strength enters \(J\) and therefore the rate. It requires no weak-interaction expansion.

This gives a physical structural conclusion: within this verified class, increasing system size cannot by itself produce arbitrarily slow Hilbert–Schmidt modes. Every local tracial autocorrelation inherits the same decay tail. This is an infinite-temperature statement; it does not imply rapid trace-distance mixing from arbitrary pure states.

### The mechanism that made the theorem possible

The successful repair averages over the occupied sites of the **input** operator:
\[
T Z_S=\frac1{|S|}\sum_{i\in S}\Phi_i Z_{S\setminus\{i\}}.
\]

Writing \(P\) for the traceless diagonal sector, \(Q\) for the off-diagonal sector and \(B=Qi[H,\cdot]P\), this construction satisfies
\[
B^\dagger T=I.
\]
The decisive additional estimate is
\[
\boxed{\|Qi[H,\cdot]T\|\le 2\sqrt{2(1+d)}\,JM.}
\]

The distinction matters. Even for independent spins, the earlier first-site repair has a Hamiltonian image growing as \(\sqrt{n-1}\). The averaged repair’s corresponding squared norm is at most \(2\). Averaging therefore resolves an actual obstruction left by exact constraint satisfaction.

The proof uses weighted Schur bounds that count the possible Hamiltonian contributions to each output Pauli word. Conditional multiplication and support cancellation are included explicitly. A separate implementation checked nine adversarial rational fixtures and all 75 exact repair identities. Exact rational positive-LDL checks also verify the finite metric inequalities at two and three spins.

The full ordinary proof is :chatgpt-content-reference{index="6"}[CONDITIONAL_AVERAGE_DECAY.md](producer-workspace/CONDITIONAL_AVERAGE_DECAY.md).

Hypocoercivity and its modified-energy method are established machinery. Fang–Lu–Tong and Li–Lu both give explicit Ising applications whose displayed estimates carry a system-size penalty. ## 3. The complete response construction—and its cost

For a local Pauli source \(A\), the target remains
\[
R_A(\omega)=
\left\langle A,(-i\omega-L)^{-1}A\right\rangle,
\]
with **zero extra linewidth** and positive physical dephasing.

The earned decay bound controls the full inverse. Locality then allows a finite patch to approximate the response, provided the patch preserves the theorem’s hypotheses.

That proviso exposed an important failure. For
\[
H=X_1Z_2+X_2,
\]
deleting the crossing interaction on the one-spin patch leaves \(Z_1\) exactly conserved. Instead, fixing one consistent exterior \(Z\) configuration gives
\[
H_W=\langle z_{\mathrm{out}}|H|z_{\mathrm{out}}\rangle.
\]
This preserves the conditional amplitude floor and the acquired coefficient bounds.

The pin is an auxiliary numerical boundary condition. Its effect is paid through the complete residual:
\[
A-(-i\omega-L)x_W=(L-L_W)x_W.
\]
The locality calculation bounds this entire omitted action, including return from the exterior. Standard Lieb–Robinson machinery supplies the propagation argument; the new input is the acquired relaxation tail. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}

A finite dyadic Richardson construction then supplies a qualified patch solution. At fixed positive physical parameters and finite range, the resulting work is polynomial in inverse absolute accuracy in one dimension. In spatial dimension \(d_{\rm space}\), the stated bound is

\exp\!\bigl[O(\log^{d_{\rm space}}(1/\epsilon))\bigr].
\]
There is no joint efficient weak-noise claim.

## 4. The independent opening produced a second physical theorem

The fresh-context branch initially pursued short coherence memory and static population reduction. It reached a useful physical conclusion that does not require acquiring the static inverse:

> **For a Hamiltonian real in the dephasing basis, every nonreal mode decays at least at half the weakest local dephasing rate.**

Precisely,
\[
\operatorname{Im}\lambda_L\ne0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
-\operatorname{Re}\lambda_L\ge\gamma_{\min}/2.
\]
Every eigenvalue strictly slower than that threshold is real and semisimple. The threshold is sharp for one spin.

The proof uses established transpose pseudo-Hermiticity: a nonreal eigenvector has half its squared norm in the transpose-odd sector, where dephasing costs at least \(\gamma_{\min}\). The symmetry identity has clear primary precedents; the coercive consequence is derived in the retained proof. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}

The reality condition is essential. A connected two-qubit chiral model has an exactly certified eigenvalue within \(10^{-9}\) of
\[
-0.06011710989914+0.00291817504136\,i,
\]
despite \(\gamma_{\min}/2=4.5\). An integer characteristic polynomial and rational Rouché bound certify the counterexample.

This is a theorem about modes, not every visible spectral peak or nonnormal transient. Its proof and counterexample are in :chatgpt-content-reference{index="10"}[MODAL_SEPARATION.md](producer-workspace/MODAL_SEPARATION.md).
