## Decision after reading predecessors

This is a working sparse native-state preparation alternative. It is
not a new general law of equilibrium engineering. Internal pistons
with the same tagged positions, particle counts and force access
implement the same mechanism. The positive distinguishing task is
preparing a multiband native material with sparse internal access,
without learning reciprocal kinetics. Static exterior pistons alone
do not hold those internal bands at zero current. That restricted
comparison does not exclude dynamic boundary control or stronger
spatial force ports.

The continuous preparation bound assumes an actual overdamped thermal
mobility interface. No finite-bandwidth inertial replacement of that
entire native bath was constructed. The force schedule itself can
have finite ramp duration at the stated compatible force port.
