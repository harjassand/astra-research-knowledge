# Reachable-ensemble learning and its physical information cost

30 October 2026. Astra P1, same research role, model and two complementary
roles. Earlier scientific assets and acceptance boundaries remain intact.

## 1. The completed consequence

An unknown reciprocal material can, at the stated actual preparation and
operation interface, **learn and qualify a replacement preparation for
every later common use of the included physical state**. It does not need
to identify its hidden transition law or close a finite predictive model.
The same construction produces an actual distinguishing continuation when
the two reachable ensembles differ.

The change from the earlier finite-error path predictors is substantive:
acquire the distance between genuine prepared hidden ensembles, rather
than approximate every later observation branch. Total-variation
contraction then handles any common later intervention and any well-defined
record length, without an observation-depth multiplier. Preparation and
calibration still have their real duration and cost. This applies to
specified reachable preparations on one common physical state space, not
arbitrary unknown channels or unrelated hidden devices.

A second result explains the precision price. A reversible four-state
source with uniformly positive rates and a uniform spectral gap has
hidden preparation error of order epsilon, but its entire original
future observation/control interface sees only order epsilon squared.
Even the strongest ordinary estimator, with continuous visible jump times,
adaptive switching and stopping, needs order epsilon to the minus fourth
active time to qualify the hidden preparation. An actual extra hidden
readout reduces the achievable exponent to minus two. This separates a
reusable preparation objective from a fixed visible-use objective.

These are mathematical receiving and information-cost results, accompanied
by actual simulated chronological acquisition and pinned internal reviews.
Historical priority, a manufactured apparatus, practical speedup and
civilisation-scale impact are not established.

## 2. The physical acquisition operation

Let mu be an actually preparable baseline law on the full included state X.
The visible label Y is noninvasive. Fixed operation calls P_j are genuinely
reversible with respect to mu and remain the same conditional operations
between calibration and deployment. Their hidden kinetics need not be
known. The input f mu is actually available, with known visible
0<=f(Y)<=B and integral f dmu=1.

For a chronological word w, its actual output density is rho_w=P_w* f.
A single echo trial starts in mu, reads f, executes u followed by the
reversed list of v, and reads f again. Every call uses fresh forward noise;
the experiment neither undoes a trajectory nor copies a microscopic state.
Its bounded report acquires

    K(u,v)=<rho_u,rho_v>_mu=<f,P_u P_v* f>_mu.

Three entry types therefore acquire

    D(u,v)=K(u,u)+K(v,v)-2K(u,v)=||rho_u-rho_v||_2^2.

Every common later causal receiver C satisfies

    TV((rho_u mu)C,(rho_v mu)C) <= min(1,sqrt(D)/2).

The receiver can include later interventions never enumerated during
learning, adaptive choices and a complete well-defined later history.
The conditional future apparatus law must actually be common. Any memory,
retired bath, clock or selector that may reenter must be included and
qualified, or have its real isolation/reset operation supplied and paid.

Failure of equality has a literal witness. A fresh fair branch selects
reversed u with final acceptance f/B, or reversed v with acceptance 1-f/B.
Its conditional response is 1/2+(rho_u-rho_v)/(2B), and the probability gap
is D/(2B). Thus on this reachable family, exact acquired zero, equality
of the full hidden ensemble and indistinguishability within the compatible
adjoint menu coincide. The statement does not make arbitrary dark hidden
inputs observable. Finite probability-synthesis error zeta can reduce the
gap by 2 zeta.

The chronological adjoint construction is inherited Astra machinery.
Hilbert norm comparisons, kernel witnesses, statistical testing and
observability duality have close primary predecessors. The candidate
addition is this actual acquisition/preparation/continuation bridge and
its controlled information law; it is not a new generic kernel method.
See `CONSEQUENCE_SELECTION_AND_PRIMARY_SOURCES.md` for exact primary-read
scope and the equally informed ordinary alternatives.

## 3. Retained history without a hidden conditional-state sampler

An old visible history usually leaves a hidden conditional density, so it
cannot simply be inserted into an endpoint report as a known function of
the present label. The record-geometry role supplied a stronger operation.
For an actual finite prefix instrument A_r with available adjoint factors,
define the unnormalized joint outputs

    b_r=P_(u_r)* A_r* f,    c_r=P_(v_r)* A_r* f.

Choose fixed positive design weights q_r summing to one on replaced
branches. They are numerical weights, not assumed deployment frequencies.
Execute the reference unchanged on excluded branches. Then

    D_q=sum_r ||b_r-c_r||_2^2/q_r,
    TV(retained R, output, common later record) <= sqrt(D_q)/2.

The actual echo runs the real prefix, retains its record r, executes u_r,
reversed v_r and the adjoint prefix branch with its real visible checks
and known selector coefficients. The forward branch event is counted once.
Division by q_r completes the report. No conditional hidden specimen is
sampled or copied, and no individual deployment probability p_r must be
known. The echo range is B squared over q_min; rare contexts and large
record alphabets can still be expensive.

Known visible reweightings extend the construction through actual endpoint
weight ratios. They incur their real weight growth and do not create a
physical Markov inverse. A raw input g with unknown global normalizer Z
needs one acquired positive lower bound on Z; it does not need all p_r.
All of these statements preserve the actual source and record interfaces.

## 4. A precision law that survives the strongest ordinary estimator

The source is (a,b) in {+1,-1} squared, with uniform equilibrium; only a is
visible. Its native transitions have rates

    (a,b)->(a,-b): 1/4,
    (a,b)->(-a,b): 1/4,
    (a,b)->(-a,-b): 1/4-theta a b/2.

The actual menu switches between theta=0 and the device's unknown
theta in [0,1/4]. The minimum rate is at least 1/8 and the gap at least
3/4, so the bottleneck is not slow hidden mixing. From the actual visible
preparation f=1+a, a one-time-unit call gives

    rho_theta=1+exp(-1)[cosh(theta)a+sinh(theta)b],
    rho_0=1+exp(-1)a.

Full hidden TV is exp(-1)sinh(theta)/2, of first order. Under theta=epsilon,
the exact continuous-observation filter h=E[b|complete visible history]
satisfies |h|<=2 epsilon on every history under every permitted predictable
controller. The visible intensity differs from 1/2 by at most epsilon
squared while the active operation runs. Consequently the complete
observed likelihood satisfies

    KL(P_epsilon_record || P_0_record)
       <= 2 epsilon^4 E_epsilon T_active.

A useful certifier accepting an exact match and rejecting the separated
alternative with probability at least 1-alpha must therefore use

    E_epsilon T_active >=
       (1-2 alpha) log((1-alpha)/alpha)/(2 epsilon^4).

This includes continuous visible jump times, arbitrary adaptive switching,
stopping, the exact known parametric family, its filter and unlimited
ordinary computation. It sums native active time over all attempts and,
if independent parallel specimens are supplied, over all specimens. It
does not lower-bound wall time against unlimited hardware or total work.

The complete initializer instrument must be hypothesis-independent:
its timing and exposed records cannot reveal theta, the old b, or the new
hidden generation seed. Equal returned state marginals alone are not
enough. This is an actual binding correction from the receiving review.

The companion proof goes further. For the same fixed source and one
prepared state, every available common future controller obeys

    exp(-1)(cosh(epsilon)-1)/2 <= d_available
       <= exp(-1)[cosh(epsilon)-1+2 epsilon sinh(epsilon)]/2.

Thus d_available is second order even for complete infinite histories,
whereas full-state TV is first order. The proof transposes fixed transcript
matrices, keeping the original pre-jump controls; it does not assume that
reversed feedback is causally executable. An added a/b swap followed by
the visible read attains the full-state TV exactly and allows ordinary
second-order-in-inverse-precision mean estimation.

The elementary three-echo upper bound has the same fourth-order precision
and logarithmic confidence exponents as the lower bound at fixed word
durations and bounded B. Constants and total apparatus costs are not
matched. This is an interface-specific worst case, not a universal
thermodynamic law or a newly discovered generic singular statistical rate.

## 6. What the full physical cycle still owes

The actual source must supply fresh conditional baseline preparation,
the intended noninvasive label, stable reciprocal calls, switching and
timing, independent fresh noise at the stated interface, probability
synthesis and any required weighted endpoint readings. Sensing reaction,
physical communication, finite actuation precision and apparatus memory
belong to that supplier. The proofs include explicit TV/report-bias budgets
when such errors are actually bounded; the simulation does not measure them.

A later accepted-input preparation has its rejected specimens and records,
completion time, restoration or replacement, bath capacity and energy bill.
The three-call candidate does not make those costs vanish. Its differing
preparation command log is not jointly equal to the reference log. That
log must be included in the comparison or physically isolated/reset if the
later consumer could use it. The full apparatus bill has not been supplied.

Ordinary kernel selection using exactly the acquired oracle ties this
learner. Ordinary finite-word fluctuation-response learning is a strong
alternative for a fixed endpoint loss. The independent sparse internal-load
construction is a real way to prepare native profiles, but ordinary internal
pistons with the same ports match it. These constructive comparisons shaped
the target and remain saved at full strength.
