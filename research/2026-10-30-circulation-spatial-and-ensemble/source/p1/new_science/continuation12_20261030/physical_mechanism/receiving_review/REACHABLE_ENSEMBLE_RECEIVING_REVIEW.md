## 1. Chronological identity reconstructed

Use row-law/function notation consistently. A physical chronological word
u has function operator P_u. Starting from f mu, its output density is
P_u* f. Since each actual primitive is mu-self-adjoint, P_u* is the
same list of forward Markov calls in reverse order. Thus

\[
 \langle P_u^*f,P_v^*f\rangle_\mu
 =\langle f,P_uP_v^*f\rangle_\mu.
\]

The corresponding observed experiment is u followed by reversed v,
not reversed u followed by v. Reading f at the two ends and multiplying
does give this expression. I checked the distinction because products
of individually reversible noncommuting kernels need not be reversible.

Since mu is invariant, every adjoint is positive, preserves constants
and integrates densities to one. The common B ceiling is therefore
preserved. The three-echo difference equals the full L2(mu) density
distance, not merely a norm of visible projections. Equality is equality
of probability measures modulo mu-null sets, which is the appropriate
statement. A new continuation can act arbitrarily on those null sets
without changing the conclusion because both prepared laws are mu-absolutely
continuous.

The distinct-input extension replaces the second endpoint function and
is correct with both actual normalized input preparations available.

## 2. The strongest consequence survives the attack

For every common Markov kernel C from the included physical state to a
complete later record, contraction gives

\[
 \|(\rho_u\mu)C-(\rho_v\mu)C\|_{\rm TV}
 \le \tfrac12\|\rho_u-\rho_v\|_{L^1(\mu)}
 \le \tfrac12\|\rho_u-\rho_v\|_{L^2(\mu)}.
\]

Thus the same certificate covers new, previously unmeasured interventions.
Their individual kinetics need not be inferred. This does not contradict
the earlier intervention-identifiability obstruction: the theorem compares
two initial laws on the same complete state space feeding the same
physical C. It does not infer an unknown C, compare two unmatched devices,
or predict C's common output value.

An arbitrarily long well-defined record is also legitimate: either it
is already the output space of C, as assumed, or one passes from all
finite-cylinder differences to the product sigma-field. No union bound
over later observation depth is used.

The claimed horizon independence concerns the subsequent discrepancy
bound, not acquisition/preparation duration, future execution time or
all-apparatus cost. The input states this correctly.

## 3. Actual witness reconstructed

The conditional success probability of the reversed-u branch is
(P_u* f)(x)/B=rho_u(x)/B. The complementary reversed-v branch has
probability 1-rho_v(x)/B. A fair fresh selector therefore gives

\[
 \phi(x)=\tfrac12+\frac{\rho_u(x)-\rho_v(x)}{2B}.
\]

It is in [0,1] because both densities lie in [0,B]. Since their difference
delta integrates to zero,

\[
 \int\phi\,\delta\,d\mu
 =\frac{\|\delta\|_2^2}{2B}.
\]

This is a genuine finite-amplitude measurement gap; it does not require
evaluating an inaccessible density at x or realizing the sign of that
density difference. The continuation uses new stochastic paths. No
hidden trajectory replay is being smuggled into the word reversal.

The displayed sandwich for operational distance follows. In particular,
zero distance implies equality of the full states, while nonzero distance
has a menu witness. This observability conclusion is confined to the
reachable densities P_w* f (or the declared family of inputs). An
arbitrary initial hidden population remains outside it.

### Finite coin precision

If the actual conditional probability implemented by the whole binary
test differs from phi uniformly by at most zeta, then its gap is at least

\[
 D/(2B)-2\zeta.
\]

For endpoint coins with uniform probability error zeta and an exact
fair selector, this same bound applies. Word implementation or sensor
errors can be added to the test probability error at their actual
conditional norm interface. An acquired lower bound D_low>0 therefore
gives a constructive finite-precision witness by choosing
zeta<D_low/(4B), provided the needed calibrated coin/readout operation
is physically available. Coin randomness, precision and duration are
paid. An arbitrary known real-valued f is not automatically an exact
finite-bit coin synthesizer.

This also separates population witness existence from a finite-data
statistical declaration that the source distributions differ. A positive
point estimate alone is insufficient; use the lower confidence bound.

## 5. Retained records and the rejection supplier

Rejection from fresh independent mu preparations gives the unconditional
accepted state f mu and mean attempt count B when mu f=1. It need not
give that state conditional on the accepted visible label. Retaining
that label and letting the future consumer use it changes the joint
problem. The failed labels, timing and preparation control memory also
have to be treated at their actual interface.

The current input warns that adaptive instruments and transcripts are
separate. Root has stated that a subsequent receiver will handle
per-label conditioned inputs and actual joint records. This review
accepts only the unconditional or correctly conditionally reinitialized
interface written here, not a future blanket retained-record extension.

The same warning applies to private mixtures of candidate words.
Deterministic candidate selection conditional on a calibration record
avoids the new mixture-selector problem. A hidden-key mixture can use
the convex QP in the companion ordinary comparison. A retained selector
instead requires the branchwise or joint certificate, and unequal word
durations do not come with free physical padding.
