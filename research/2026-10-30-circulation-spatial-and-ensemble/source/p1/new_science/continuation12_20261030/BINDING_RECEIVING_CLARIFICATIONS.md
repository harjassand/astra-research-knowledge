## 2. The information lower bound covers the complete initializer

In `ADAPTIVE_INFORMATION_COST_OF_HIDDEN_ENSEMBLE_VALIDATION.md`, every
initialization or replacement operation must have the same complete
conditional exposed law under theta=0 and theta=epsilon. This includes
completion time, failed attempts, controller records and any apparatus
output. It must not disclose the old hidden bit or the random mark that
generated the new hidden b. A separate theta-independent, fixed-protocol
uniform initializer with unobserved thermal b-generation marks is one
explicit ideal supplier. Its work, time, precision and restoration costs
are additional; the lower bound does not construct it from native waiting.

Equal returned state marginals alone are insufficient. An initializer
that reports theta in its completion time identifies the source at zero
native active time. One that reads old b before replacing it adds the
excluded sensor. These are actual counterexamples to an unqualified claim,
not negligible bookkeeping details.

For multiple simultaneous specimens, native noises and qualified fresh
initializations are independent conditional on the fixed source hypothesis.
Arbitrary common adaptive commands are permitted. Alternatively restrict
the result to a sequential single specimen. The bound sums native active
time across every specimen and attempt; unlimited parallel hardware does
not turn it into a wall-clock bound. No inference is made from individual
marginal generators to a correlated multi-specimen bath.

The ordinary learner is granted the exact parametric family, filter,
continuous visible trajectory, jump times, adaptive switching, stopping
and arbitrary computation. It is subject to the same initializer and

## 3. Exact witnesses and finite probability synthesis

An exact witness presumes its classical endpoint acceptance probabilities
are actually executable. If the entire conditional response is implemented

2 zeta. This applies to the common-equilibrium and finite-instrument
witnesses as well as the weighted version that already states the budget.
Known irrational weights are not physically exact coins by declaration.

## 4. Which records are qualified

The preparation receiver qualifies the included physical output state,
the declared retained prefix record R, and any subsequent common causal
receiver. A full calibration history C may be attached identically to both
laws when the required conditional source law holds after every C. With
failure probability alpha and successful conditional TV bound eta, the
unconditional joint (C,R,later-record) bound is at most alpha+eta.

This does not equate the actual candidate and reference preparation logs:
their different commands or durations can reveal which recipe ran. If
those logs, retired baths or selection keys can be used by the later
consumer, include and qualify their joint laws or supply and pay for their
actual isolation/reset. A matched bookkeeping label is not a physical
erasure. The full-cycle cost ledger retains those operations even when
they lie outside the receiver's observation interface.
