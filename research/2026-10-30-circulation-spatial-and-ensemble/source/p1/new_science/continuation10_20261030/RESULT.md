## Main gain

The new theoretical operation changes the actual circulation history of a
composite interacting conductor while preserving its complete macroscopic
density history. It supports prescribed nonlinear cumulative-current maps
and does not learn the native generator.

Add R_N real controlled carriers to each of a fixed number of isolated
circular lanes, with 1<<R_N<<N, and observe for physical time N^2T. The
reference and deployment contain the same auxiliary population. Its mass is
at most R_N/N at every time, yet repeated winding supplies order N^2
displacement. Conservation makes its nonconstant integrated current vanish,
leaving a controllable circulation coordinate.

For a supplied smooth computable phi, the executed law is exponentially
equivalent at speed N to

\[
(\rho,W)\longmapsto
(\rho,\;W+[\phi(t,\overline W)-\overline W]dx).
\]

The joint theorem retains auxiliary current Y, so native macroscopic current
is W_out-Y. The native branch current is unchanged; additional carriers
supply real flow. Information and work are not erased by showing a marginal.

The native interface is a bounded-drift, bounded-activity aggregate current
whose path law is preserved by sensing. Homogeneous exclusion with unknown
bounded positive ring rates supplies it. Generic heterogeneous reciprocal
rates do not automatically qualify. Hidden memory need not be reconstructed
or mixed. A strong ordinary stationary-reversible alternative covers a
different equilibrium class under its own initial-law premises.

## Consequence beyond passive constraints

The native density-path action is preserved by contraction, even with
nonlinear density-dependent mobility. For two circuits mapped to common
circulation, the output action is native current cost minus its optimized
difference-current component. A passive restriction lacks that subtraction
and can increase the cost of the same nonflat density history.

Leave the drive off until b_N=N^(3/2)log N is crossed. For unforced native
martingales and phi(t,0)=0, arming probability is at most C exp(-c log^2 N),
yet the same macroscopic map executes. Controlled and zero-drive composite
trajectory laws have that total-variation bound. Ordinary fluctuations remain
unchanged while rare-current laws can change completely. Polynomial passive
trials may miss the difference; a sustained active force probe can reveal it.

An explicit exclusion example has noncommuting response limits. Define
J_N(F)=E_F[B+A]/N^2 for native per-step load F/N and phi=0. Then

\[
\lim_NJ_N(F)=0\ \text{for every fixed }F,
\qquad \lim_NJ_N'(0)=D m(1-m)T>0.
\]

A shrinking force boundary layer explains the difference. Finite-system
response theory is respected. The result separates bulk histories, ordinary
circulation noise, rare-current laws and macroscopic load response at their
actual scales.

## Stronger actual carrier supplier

Independent bounded-rate walkers supply a port with unknown positive clocks,
costing O(NR_N) separate-track hardware unless a noninteracting shared lane is
supplied. A generic exclusion auxiliary lane fails through clustering.

The interacting mechanical alternative has actual charged positions, local
velocity/neutral-coordinate coupling, finite transport and bath intervals,
and fresh unread thermal sources. Unknown translation-invariant relative
forces cancel from its aggregate equations. Root then removed exact quarter
rotation and complete reset: fixed unknown angles in a positive-response
range and partial refresh suffice. Its true-current cell has

\[
\Delta A+k^T\Delta z=R\mu F+\sqrt R\sigma\zeta,
\quad \mu=\frac{1-r}{2(1+r)}[2/g+b\cot(\theta/2)]^2,
\quad\sigma^2=2\mu.
\]

The controller never learns k or mu. Compact bounds control retained memory
and supply positive inward response. Stability alone fails: theta=3pi/2,
g=1,b=2,r=0 gives a stable cell with zero transport mobility.

## Costs and scope

Local held counts, synchronized timestamps and delayed arithmetic/broadcast
give O(N) or conservative O(N log N) delay. Current acquisition is real;
one cut count cannot replace total displacement without a polarization
correction. Force range, readout noise, quantization, mechanical relative
energy and logical reliability are explicit interfaces. A finite-band
detector is not silently an exact counter. Fixed calibration-failure
probability does not certify a speed-N rare-event law.

Mechanical carriers pay O(R_NN^2) fresh thermal preparations and retirements.
Separate tracks pay O(NR_N) hardware and O(N^2R_N log N) bit operations in
the given local protocol. Native sensing may process O(N^3) events. Only a
fixed finite number of circulation coordinates is programmed; arbitrary
spatial flow fields are not supplied.

Rare arming makes expected carrier-drive work vanish unforced. It does not
pay for continuously operating sensors, clocks, thermal preparations or
memory. Sustained order-one counterflow has a different Omega(N^2/R_N) work
bill. Per-step force can vanish while whole-loop affinity grows. Fixed total
loop voltage is insufficient. Work retains actual momentum/switching terms;
partial refresh leaves an exponentially small old-state correction in the
delayed loaded-work bound. Exact finite-block positivity is not claimed.

## Evidence and preserved corrections

The existing roles reviewed actual root files and kept exact input snapshots.
Separate corrections retain three distinctions: quadratic LDP action alone
does not prove projected central-limit noise; derivative localization needs
an exhaustion with diverging exit exponents; partial-carrier rare arming
requires phi(t,0)=0 and its smaller observation-error bound.

Executed `verify_circulation_operation.py` checked actual interacting motion
and work, nonlinear exclusion-action minimization, and delayed feedback with
real native and auxiliary hops. Cell residual was at most 2.15e-11; action
minimization agreed within 2.23e-16; integer continuity residual was zero.
The small loaded execution retained sampled errors around 0.15 and substantial
drive work. It is population execution, not practical performance or an
asymptotic-tail fit.

Executed `verify_partial_thermal_carrier.py` checked three untuned partially
refreshed interacting carriers. Corrected-current residual was at most
5.38e-11; noise/mobility identity error was at most 7.11e-15. It also verified
the stable zero-response example. Neither script acquires preparation,
detector, clock or calibration confidence.
