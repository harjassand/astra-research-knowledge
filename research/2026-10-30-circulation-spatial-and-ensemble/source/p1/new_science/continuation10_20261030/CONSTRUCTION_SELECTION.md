Let the unchanged background have empirical density rho_N and integrated
current W_N. Let Y_N be the added carrier current. Since the auxiliary mass
is o(N), continuity forces every fixed nonconstant spatial mode of Y_N to
vanish at the macroscopic scale, regardless of the carrier trajectories.
Its harmonic component can remain of order one.

superexponentially small at speed N, while the unprotected auxiliary current
is superexponentially negligible. Then the actual measured law should be
exponentially equivalent to a geometric projection of the unchanged
background current, jointly with its density. This is a pushforward law,
not an unnormalized restriction of a native action and not the previous

actual apparatus estimates can be proved.

exclusion walkers can cluster into a block, destroying an asserted uniform
capacity proportional to R_N. Independent walkers supply such capacity but
require a real noninteracting lane. An alternative interacting Langevin
carrier population has a closed center-of-mass port only with homogeneous

requires actual sensing and counters. It is not instantaneous global access.

cost of order at least N^2/R_N at fixed mobility per carrier. That is distinct
from the expected work needed merely to stabilize equilibrium fluctuations:
a weak linear current governor may have subextensive mean drive work.
Preparation, counter storage, communication, force changes and clocking are
additional actual costs.
