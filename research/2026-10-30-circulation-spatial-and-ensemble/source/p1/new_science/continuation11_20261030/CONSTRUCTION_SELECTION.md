# Round 11: selecting a physical consequence

30 October 2026. Root's working selection record. Earlier completed files,
actual reviews and binding corrections are unchanged. The same two
complementary roles continue; there is no new branch or worker pool.

The question selected is whether a material's **entire macroscopic density
history law can coexist with a separately programmed spatial circulation
law at extensive carrier-drive work**. This is stronger than suppressing one
current, and different from transporting native material through a gear.
It tests how much constitutive information is actually fixed by bulk
fluctuations once an active physical layer is allowed.

## Selection changed by constructive alternatives

The record-geometry role constructed an ordinary driven tracer in a
single-file native material. One tracer transmits displacement to all native
particles and produces order-one bulk restructuring with order-volume work.
Sparse reciprocal gates also change a native diffusion coefficient through
their resistance. Sparse contact is therefore not generally too weak.

The physical-mechanism role constructed an ordinary Brownian gear and then
a finite-state single-file tight-coupling cell. They transfer actual input
work to an opposing load without sensing or runtime global communication.
They defeat a claim that current locking or load transfer requires auxiliary
counterflow. They change native state histories and do not do the selected
bulk-history-preserving task. Their derivations and direct predecessors are
retained in their own directories.

## Main mechanism personally developed by root

Dilute independent carriers pay N^2/R per lane for sustained displacement
N^2. Instead put N carriers in a locally linked elastic train. Its translation
mode carries actual charge with mobility proportional to N. Its relative
coordinates have increasing stiffness, so a macroscopic density deviation
has a cost proportional to N times that stiffness. The added density is a
known uniform *nonzero* background, present in both reference and deployment.
No charge or work is subtracted from the measured system.

Place such trains on all N rows of a two-dimensional native material. This
allows an actual horizontal shear-current field, not only finitely many
circulation coordinates. The two new technical requirements are joint
volume-speed control, rather than a union of insufficient row bounds, and
joint concentration of the physical train densities.

A nonlinear function of an unsmoothed row-current field is not continuous
in the weak topology of the native current LDP. The intended operation
therefore includes a specified smoothing kernel and its actual finite
communication/evaluation. This is a scientific change to the program, not a
topological assumption silently added to the native theorem.

## What success would and would not establish

Success would establish a local-interaction architecture in which the
native bulk path law does not fix the spatial transport law, even when the
mean **carrier force work** is of thermodynamic volume order. Ordinary
governors can use the same carrier layer. Belts, elastic trains, feedback
traps, thermal auxiliaries, concentration inequalities and current LDPs have
direct predecessors; none is claimed as a new basic component.

This is a foundational apparatus theorem before a device. The total work
also includes preparation, readout, synchronization, computation,
communication, thermal-source supply, switching and reliability. Those
terms will be kept explicitly. The force-work improvement is not a claim
that total operating work is extensive or that this beats a practical
conveyor. Fixed stiffness, unsupplied perfect sensors, exact zero-delay
forces, arbitrary unequal carrier couplings and a fixed calibration failure
probability do not supply the theorem.

The unfinished round10 spatial argument remains preserved as unfinished;
only a new, actually proved joint argument can replace it for this changed
architecture.
