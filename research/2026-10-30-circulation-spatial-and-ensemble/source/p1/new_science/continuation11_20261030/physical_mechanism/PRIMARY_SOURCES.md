1. **Lathouwers, Lucero and Sivak (2020)**, *Nonequilibrium Energy Transduction in
   Stochastic Strongly Coupled Rotary Motors*, J. Phys. Chem. Lett. 11,
   5273–5278. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpclett.0c01055);
   [actual primary PDF including supporting derivations](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.01837).
   Read the model and output-power sections and supporting Eqs. (7)–(30).
   The barrier-free sum/difference separation, periodic relative potential,
   useful output work, slippage and finite-coupling power tradeoff are direct
   predecessors. The unequal-unknown-mobility and whole-path bounds in this
   folder do not turn that established mechanism into a new basic operation.

2. **Kalnin and Berezhkovskii (2013)**, *Note: On the relation between
   Lifson–Jackson and Derrida formulas for effective diffusion coefficient*,
   J. Chem. Phys. 139, 196101.
   [Actual full primary text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4108624/);
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4832035).
   Read Eqs. (2)–(19), including the continuum cell formula and equilibrium
