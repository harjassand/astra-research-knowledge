# Primary sources actually checked in round 11

All sources below were read through public primary PDFs or primary
publication metadata. No generic novelty claim is made.

1. Arvind Ayyer, A simple symmetric exclusion process driven by an asymmetric
   tracer particle, Annales de l'Institut Henri Poincare D 10 (2023), 687--713.
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.02425
   https://doi.org/10.4171/AIHPD/157
   The actual PDF introduction, stationary factorization, current theorem,
   and profile discussion were read. This is a direct predecessor. The
   present result does not claim novelty for the gap mapping or tracer
   density control.

2. Ivan Lobaskin and Martin R. Evans, Driven Tracers in a One-Dimensional
   Periodic Hard-Core Lattice Gas (2020).
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/2002.02841
   The actual PDF introduction and zero-range mapping were read. It identifies
   the no-passing coupling and solves totally asymmetric tracer cases.
   It does not supply a finite-affinity thermodynamic port by setting q=0.

3. Claudio Landim, Stefano Olla and Sergio B. Volchan, Driven Tracer Particle
   in One Dimensional Symmetric Simple Exclusion, Communications in
   Mathematical Physics 192 (1998), 287--307.
   https://doi.org/10.1007/s002200050300
   The primary abstract and bibliographic identity were checked. A request
   to open the indexed full PDF timed out. No theorem with unexamined
   initial-law hypotheses is imported into the finite-ring construction.

4. Thierry Bodineau, Bernard Derrida and Joel L. Lebowitz, A Diffusive System
   Driven by a Battery or by a Smoothly Varying Field, Journal of Statistical
   Physics 140 (2010), 648--675.
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1003.5838
   The actual PDF introduction and Sections 2--3 were read, including the
   asymmetric native bond, exact linear stationary mean relation, nonlinear
   boundary matching, and moving-battery/zero-range comparison. This is the
   strongest direct native-contact comparator. Its fluctuation formula is
   not used as a proved law for the unknown-rate extension.

5. Tertuliano Franco, Patricia Goncalves and Adriana Neumann, Hydrodynamical
   behavior of symmetric exclusion with slow bonds, Annales de l'Institut
   Henri Poincare B 49 (2013), 402--427.
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1010.4769
   https://doi.org/10.1214/11-AIHP445
   The actual PDF opening and the finite-number-of-slow-bonds classification
   were read. The paper supplies the beta=1 threshold, not a proof of every
   growing sparse periodic gate construction mentioned as an ordinary
   homogenization comparator.

6. Massimiliano Esposito and Christian Van den Broeck, Second law and
   Landauer principle far from equilibrium, EPL 95 (2011), 40004.
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.5165
   https://doi.org/10.1209/0295-5075/95/40004
   The actual PDF's free-energy inequality, entropy balance, Markov
   formulation and initial-information qualifications were read.
   The native-profile lower bound is an explicit specialization of an
   established thermodynamic principle.

No new source export, transfer, worker, branch or external message was used.
