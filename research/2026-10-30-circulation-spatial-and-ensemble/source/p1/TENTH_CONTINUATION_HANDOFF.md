## Resources, alternatives and unresolved claims

Sensing uses synchronized held counts, actual pickup noise and quantization,
finite local aggregation/broadcast and warranted whole-horizon reliability.
The mechanical detector additionally needs its relative-energy envelope.
Native event sensing may cost O(N^3); separate tracks cost O(NR_N) hardware
and O(N^2R_N log N) bit operations; thermal carriers use O(R_NN^2) fresh
coordinates and retirements. These costs are not paid by retained records
or by small mean drive work.

Rare-arming expected carrier work can vanish unforced. Sustained mean
counterflow instead costs Omega(N^2/R_N). The partial-refresh loaded bound
retains an exponentially small old-state work correlation; exact finite-block
positivity is not claimed. Whole-loop drive range generally grows even when
per-step affinity vanishes. Fixed total-loop voltage is not a supplier.

ensemble control, Ceriotti auxiliary thermostats, and partial-refresh GHMC
are explicit primary predecessors. Ordinary controllers can use the same new
carrier port. No new basic governor, Gaussian refresh, LDP machinery,
historical priority, built device or practical/total-resource speedup is claimed.
