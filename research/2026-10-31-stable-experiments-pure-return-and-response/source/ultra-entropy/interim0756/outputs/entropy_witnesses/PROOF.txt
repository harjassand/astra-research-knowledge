2. THE POINTWISE ENTROPY-WITNESS CONE

Define phi(t)=2t atanh t on (-1,1), phi(0)=0, with the extended endpoint
values +infinity, and phi*(h)=sup_t[ht-phi(t)]. For an edge payoff H(x,y),
write H_s=(H(x,y)+H(y,x))/2 and H_a=(H(x,y)-H(y,x))/2. Require

                    H_s+phi*(H_a)<=0                      (E1)

pointwise. Equivalently a H(x,y)+b H(y,x) is at most
a log(a/b)+b log(b/a) for all nonnegative a,b. Diagonal payoffs must be
nonpositive. The cone is convex: H_s is affine and phi* convex.

For any stationary flow, E_Gamma H<=e(P). Indeed, relative to
eta=(Gamma+Gamma^T)/2 let t=(dGamma-dGamma^T)/(dGamma+dGamma^T). Then
e=E_eta phi(t), while E_Gamma H=E_eta[H_s+t H_a]. Fenchel proves the
claim, including zero/infinite cases. This expectation inequality alone
would not justify inference from dependent finite data.

3. THE REALISED-ENTROPY EXPONENTIAL TRANSFER

Suppose (E1), H bounded and |H_a|<=M. For 0<theta<=1/2,

 E_P exp{theta[sum_{t<N}H(X_t,X_{t+1})-Sigma_N]}
                                      <=exp(N theta^2 M^2/2).             (E2)

Proof. The positive tilted kernel has stationary flow
dGamma^{1-theta}d(Gamma^T)^theta e^{theta H}. It is a bounded L2(pi)
operator: before tilting, both its marginals are <=pi by Holder; bounded
H preserves boundedness. Taking absolute values in a quadratic form,
then averaging an edge with its transpose, gives the multiplier

 e^{theta H_s} cosh((1-2theta)u+theta H_a)/cosh u,
                                                   u=atanh t.

Put C(theta,theta h)=sup_u cosh((1-2theta)u+theta h)/cosh u. For h>=0
its maximum is at u in [0,h/2]. The derivative equation is
tanh u=(1-2theta)tanh((1-2theta)u+theta h); it implies
u<=(1-2theta)u+theta h. The zero/end cases are immediate. Taylor's
inequality for log cosh, whose second derivative is <=1, therefore yields

 log C(theta,theta h)
   <=theta sup_u[(h-2u)tanh u]+theta^2 h^2/2
   =theta phi*(h)+theta^2 h^2/2.

Evenness handles negative h. By (E1), the quadratic-form multiplier is
at most exp(theta^2 M^2/2). Its numerical radius has that bound. The
classical numerical-radius power inequality, proved in reversible_currents/
PROOF.txt, bounds the Nth power on the constant unit vector. This is (E2).
No conditional observed martingale or spectral gap is assumed.

Markov now gives, with failure probability <=alpha_e,

 Sigma_N >= sum H -N theta M^2/2-log(1/alpha_e)/theta.      (E3)

The negative baseline may use a genuinely conditional martingale argument:
e^{-Sigma_N} is the stationary reverse-kernel likelihood ratio. Under P,
its conditional next factor has mean at most1 (reverse mass outside P's
support is discarded). It is a nonnegative supermartingale starting at1.
Ville therefore gives Sigma_N>=log(alpha_0) simultaneously at all N
with failure <=alpha_0. This is the standard entropy-infimum argument,
not optional stopping applied to the observed fixed-horizon witness.

For FULL CTMC trajectory entropy, conditional Holder on each hidden-grid
fiber gives pushforward[P_full^{1-theta}(P_full^R)^theta]
<=P_grid^{1-theta}(P_grid^R)^theta. Thus the same bound applies to the
full CTMC entropy when the observable payoff uses time-grid samples.
The actual sampling interval and thermodynamic interpretation stay charged.

4. A FINITE PREDICTIVE-FLOW ENTROPY CERTIFICATE

Use the prefix-simplex grid of predictive_flow/flow_grid.py, alphabet s,
depth L and h=1/k. Node i has a label and separate plus/minus prefix
probabilities r^q_w(i). Let F_v(i) be its full-word feature. Choose rational
word coefficients a_v, normalization z, a state potential p_i and row
multipliers y^q_{i,b,w,tau}<=0, tau in {+1,-1}, |w|<L. Define

 A^q_{i,b,w,tau}(j)=tau[1_{label(j)=b}r^q_w(j)-r^q_{bw}(i)]-h,
 D(i)=sum_v a_v F_v(i),
 H(i,j)=z+D(i)+p_i-p_j
       +sum_{b,w,tau}y^+_{i,b,w,tau}A^+_{i,b,w,tau}(j)
       +sum_{b,w,tau}y^-_{j,b,w,tau}A^-_{j,b,w,tau}(i).     (E4)

The receiver must check (E1) for EVERY ordered pair, or every unordered
pair with its antisymmetric part. This is a FULL entropy certificate,
not merely a feasibility/Farkas witness for equilibrium.

For this interface backward residuals use the COLUMN marginal, while
forward residuals use the ROW marginal. Stationarity makes these the same
feasible program as the earlier driver. Its old row-marginal backward
dual needs a corresponding potential correction; dropping that correction
would change the payoff and is not allowed.

The observable word score is W(v)=z+a_v. Define
R=max W-min W, O=max p-min p, H_min=min_ij H(i,j), M=max_ij|H_a(i,j)|,
B_q=max_i sum_{b,w}|sum_tau tau*y^q_{i,b,w,tau}|,
C=(B_++B_-)/2+sqrt(L)R. Use ceil(sqrt L) instead for a rational upper C.

Quantize the actual true predictive fields u^+(x),u^-(x) independently,
with the earlier unbiased prefix quantizer R_x. Average H(i,j) over
i~R_x,j~R_y to obtain Hbar(x,y). Convexity of (E1) preserves the entropy
cone; |Hbar_a|<=M and Hbar>=H_min. No hidden field is acquired by the
algorithm: this is a proof device. Independence of the present plus/minus
rounding preserves the observed cross moments EXACTLY.

Let W_*(x) be the true conditional word score. Unbiased next rounding and
y<=0 give, pointwise,

 Hbar(x,y)>=W_*(x)+P_*(x)-P_*(y)+M_+(x,y)+M_-(y,x),       (E5)

where P_*=E_R p_i and
M_q(x,y)=sum f^q_{bw}(x)[1_{label(y)=b}u^q_w(y)-u^q_{bw}(x)],
f^q_{bw}(x)=E_R sum_tau tau*y^q_{i,b,w,tau}.
The discarded quantization correction is
sum y{tau[u^q_{bw}(x)-r^q_{bw}(i)]-h}>=0. The forward M_+ is a
martingale difference for P with conditional range <=B_+. The reverse
M_- is a backward martingale difference for the ACTUAL P*, with range
<=B_-. Unlike the reversible-only proof, u^+ and u^- are kept distinct.

Observe Y_0,...,Y_N. There are n=N-2L+1 full windows, centered at
L,...,N-L. Their data scores differ from W_*(X_t) by one forward and
one backward block innovation of length L, each conditional range <=R.
Past/future independence given X_t proves the exact decomposition, using
u^- for the past and u^+ for the future. Partition centers modulo L.
Conditional Hoeffding and Holder imply that the sum Z of the two M
terms minus the two data innovations obeys

                    E exp(lambda Z)<=exp(n lambda^2 C^2/2).

No independence of overlapping windows is used. The potential telescopes,
costing at most O. The central n edges leave 2L-1 edges outside; each has
payoff at least H_min. Combining their path payoff with (E3) gives

 Sigma_N >= sum_windows W +(2L-1)H_min-O
            -C sqrt(2n log(1/alpha_m))
            -N theta M^2/2-log(1/alpha_e)/theta             (E6)

with failure probability <=alpha_m+alpha_e. All constants are supplied
by the finite checked certificate, not an unknown hidden count or mixer.
Negative endpoints must be retained. Theta may depend on N and the
allocated confidence but not on the record without a selection charge.

5. A CLOSED ANALYTIC CERTIFICATE: THE ORDINARY LAG-TWO BOUND

Let f(Y)=+/-1, u(x)=E[f(Y_1)|X_0=x], v(x)=E[f(Y_-1)|X_0=x],
t=(u-v)/2, a=(u+v)/2. Then |t|+|a|<=1. Put r(x)=t(x)f(Y_0),
d(x,y)=t(x)f(Y_1)-t(y)f(Y_0). Define

 H0=2d-d^2/2,
 G=[r(x)+r(y)]^2/2+a(x)^2+a(y)^2,
 H=H0-G.                                                  (E7)

Here G>=0 is symmetric, H_a=2d and H_s=-d^2/2-G. Since phi(t)>=2t^2,
phi*(h)<=h^2/8. Hence (E1) holds exactly. Also |H_a|<=4, H0>=-6,
and G<=2, so H>=-8. For G<=2, let x=|t(x)|,y=|t(y)| in [0,1];
G<= (x+y)^2/2+(1-x)^2+(1-y)^2<=2 by x^2<=x,y^2<=y,2xy<=x+y.

There is the EXACT polynomial identity

 H(x,y)=W_*(x)+Phi(x)-Phi(y)
               +2t(x)[f(Y_1)-u(x)]-2t(y)[f(Y_0)-v(y)],   (E8)
 W_*(x)=-2u(x)v(x),
 Phi(x)=(u(x)^2-v(x)^2)/2+u(x)v(x).

The potential lies in [-1,1]: Phi=u^2-(v-u)^2/2<=1 and
-Phi=v^2-(u+v)^2/2<=1. Each forward/backward residual has conditional
range <=4. The observed word score W=-2f(Y_{t-1})f(Y_{t+1}) has width4.
Thus the constants for (E6), with L=1, are

             M=4, H_min=-8, O=2, C=8.                    (E9)

This converts the ordinary population inequality e>=-2C2 into a valid

6. FINITE ENTROPY DUALS ARE COMPLETE AT THEIR GRID LEVEL

The finite program minimizes e(J)=D(J||J^T) over nonnegative normalized
flows, equal marginals, the two directed row-residual families and exact
word moments. Its value v_{L,k}(p) equals

       sup_certificates [z+sum_v a_v p(v)].                (E11)

Here certificates are (E4) satisfying the pointwise cone, and the
supremum need not be attained with finite multipliers. No Slater premise
is smuggled in. A compact penalty argument proves equality: add K times
the sum of absolute equality violations and positive inequality violations
to e(J), on the compact normalized simplex. The minima increase to the
constrained minimum: any bounded subsequence has violations tending to
zero and a feasible limit of no greater limiting entropy. Each penalty
is the maximum of a*(p-DJ)-y*FJ-potential*balance(J), with a,potential
in [-K,K] and y in [-K,0]. On the convex domain where e(J) is finite,
the Lagrangian is real-valued, convex/lower-semicontinuous in J and
affine/continuous in the COMPACT multiplier box. Sion minimax exchanges
the infimum and maximum. Excluding infinite-cost J changes no finite
penalized infimum; the uniform flow ensures each is finite. The inner
infimum is evaluated by the unordered-edge calculation below. Thus no
interior feasible flow or Slater assumption is required.

The minimax theorem used here is Sion (1958), Corollary 3.3, p.174:
https://msp.org/pjm/1958/8-1/pjm-v8-n1-p14-s.pdf . Only ONE of the two
convex spaces must be compact. The compact space here is the multiplier
box; the finite-entropy flow domain is convex and need not be compact.

For any oriented payoff H, the least e(J)-sum JH over normalized J is

          -max_{i<=j}[H_s(i,j)+phi*(H_a(i,j))].

To see this, choose one diagonal or one unordered edge pair and optimize
its imbalance t; pair costs are exactly phi(t). This proves the local
cone form and the normalization adjustment used by the exact receiver.

Rational multipliers suffice densely: approximate any finite coefficients
by rationals and decrease z slightly. Phi* is continuous on every bounded
h interval, so a strict local margin absorbs rationalization errors.
The exact checker uses certified rational upper bounds on phi*. Increasing
their precision makes the adjustment arbitrarily small. Thus finite
encoded rational certificates form a computably enumerable complete family.
A numerical solver proposes coefficients only; every column is checked
after rationalization, and failure to get a positive bound proves nothing.

7. UNIVERSAL CONSISTENCY AT THE OBSERVABLE MINIMUM

Let P_Y be a stationary ERGODIC finite-alphabet process and define

 I(P_Y)=min{e(Q): stationary standard-Borel hidden Markov Q
                         has COMPLETE observed law P_Y}.  (E12)

The earlier predictive-flow proof establishes attainment, compactness and
I=sup_L E_L(p_L)=sup_{L,k} v_{L,k}(p_L). This is not an exact finite-state
attainment statement or a fixed hidden-count assumption. From (E11),
I=sup over finite certificates E_{P_Y}W.

Fix one encoding of all rational certificates and their numerical proof
budgets; invalid encodings contribute a harmless zero witness. Assign
weights w_j=2^(-j-1) for j>=1, reserving weight1/2 for the baseline.
At record length N allocate
alpha_{j,N}=alpha w_j/[N(N+1)] to (E6), split in two. Evaluate increasing
finite prefixes J_N->infinity, ignoring witnesses whose windows do not
fit. Include the baseline Sigma_N>=log(alpha/2), using the hidden
likelihood-ratio supermartingale just proved, to retain a finite negative
endpoint at short records. Its entire time-uniform risk is alpha/2.
Choose deterministic theta_N->0 with N theta_N/log N->infinity. All
logarithms and square roots are enclosed in the conservative directions.

The maximum endpoint L_N has

 inf_Q Pr_Q{Sigma_N(Q)>=L_N for EVERY N}>=1-alpha,          (E13)

over ALL stationary hidden models of the stated class. Moreover

                      L_N/N -> I(P_Y) almost surely.      (E14)

Lower limit: for every fixed certificate the ergodic theorem gives its
empirical W average. All boundary terms divided by N vanish; the martingale
term is O(sqrt(N log N))/N, the kernel term theta_N M^2/2 vanishes, and
log(1/alpha_{j,N})/(N theta_N) vanishes. Every fixed index is eventually
included. Taking the supremum gives liminf>=I, including I=+infinity.

Upper limit when I is finite: take the attaining hidden model Q_min.
Its stationary Markov ergodic components remain Markov with the same
transition kernel restricted to invariant sets. To justify this fact,
a shift-invariant event A has h(x)=Pr(A|X_0=x), Ph=h. Stationarity gives
E[h(X_1)-h(X_0)]^2=0; the future conditional martingale h(X_t) converges
to1_A, so A is determined by X_0 and preserved by transitions. Conditional
components therefore retain the Markov property.
Because P_Y is ergodic, almost every hidden component has observed law
P_Y. Each component's entropy cost is >=I, and their average is I, so
each has cost I. The negative part of log(dGamma/dGamma^T) is integrable
(bounded by the usual x log x bound); finite I then gives absolute
integrability. Birkhoff yields Sigma_N(Q_min)/N->I almost surely.
The certificate-part horizon failure probabilities are summable, so
Borel-Cantelli gives their maximum <=Sigma_N(Q_min) eventually almost
surely, even allowing early failures. The constant baseline/N tends to0,
which is <=I. Thus limsup<=I. L_N is observation-only, and its observed law
is exactly P_Y under every compatible model, proving (E14).

The entropy mixture identity here is exact: invariant state components
are disjoint, and relative entropy disintegrates over their common
component law. It is not merely the convexity upper bound for overlapping
mixtures. This justifies the equal component costs used above.

Component corollary. For a nonergodic stationary observed law, the same
procedure converges to I of the record's OBSERVED ERGODIC COMPONENT.
An observed invariant event lifts to a hidden invariant event and hence,
by the argument above, an invariant state partition. Conditional on that
partition, the hidden chain is still stationary Markov, with the same
edge entropy ratios. Applying (E14) conditionally and integrating proves
the corollary. A single record identifies this component minimum, not
the stationary ensemble mean across unvisited components.

This recovers the IDENTIFIABLE MINIMUM, not arbitrary invisible excess
dissipation. Coverage is for realised paths, not the global mean rate.
In N185's dormant extension an active no-bridge path has the base entropy
despite a much smaller ensemble mean. There is no contradiction.

Optimality at the same confidence contract. Let A_N be ANY observation-only
band satisfying (E13), with no computational restriction. For finite I,
under the attaining model Sigma_N/N->I almost surely. On its simultaneous
coverage event A_N<=Sigma_N at every N. Consequently

     Pr_{P_Y}{limsup_N A_N/N>I(P_Y)}<=alpha.                (E15)

Thus (E14) reaches the largest possible asymptotic observation-only rate
at confidence 1-alpha, over this entire hidden-model class. In particular,
if A_N/N has a deterministic limit at this ergodic observed law, that
limit cannot exceed I when alpha<1. This is a limit characterization,
not a claim that any finite certificate attains I or a finite-time

8. COMPUTATION, ACQUISITION, AND SCIENTIFIC BOUNDARIES

(E13)-(E14) are a finite-at-each-stage mathematical algorithm with potentially
enormous certificate generation, verification, storage and library-index
costs. The grid has s*binom(k+s^L-1,s^L-1)^2 nodes and quadratic flow
columns. No polynomial runtime, universal onset, or practical complete
compiler is claimed. Searching an uncharged data-dependent dual is invalid;
a predetermined library or full description-length selection charge is
required. Splitting a dependent trace does not supply independent validation.

A fixed finite library can be evaluated with bounded word buffers and its
sufficient statistics, as the implemented iterable APIs do. The complete
growing library has a different resource contract: later witnesses can use
longer words and must score the OLD prefix as well. One may retain the raw
prefix and rescan it, or maintain a growing exact word-count structure.
The fixed-witness two-bit buffer does not supply that growing history for
free. Memory, rescanning, certificate search and exact precision costs must
all be charged; no bounded-memory complete inference claim is made.

For the analytic witness, one stationary preparation and N+1 observed
bits suffice for evaluation. Obtaining stationarity, correct physical
reversal, calibrated time, sensor fidelity and a thermodynamic model is
still a real external obligation. The new comparison uses fresh matched
CTMC records after freezing (E10); the older record is a retrospective
diagnostic only. All source durations and latent simulation costs are
charged separately in the receipt. No physical experiment is supplied.
