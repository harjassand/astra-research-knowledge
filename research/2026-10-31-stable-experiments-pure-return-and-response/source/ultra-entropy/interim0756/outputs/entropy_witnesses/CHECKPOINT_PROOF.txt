TIME-UNIFORM PATH ENTROPY FROM SPARSE FIXED-HORIZON CERTIFICATES
Solo derivation, 31 October 2026. Uses the established entropy martingale.

1. THE GAP

The direct per-sample alpha/[N(N+1)] construction is valid but expensive.
On the 10,802-bit CTMC control its finite-family band returned only the
negative baseline. That result is retained in entropy_receipt.json.
One cannot retain the maximum of uncorrected path-entropy endpoints:
realised entropy can decrease. The observed fixed-horizon e-values have
not been proved supermartingales, so applying Ville to them is invalid.

2. A CONDITIONAL ENTROPY-DESCENT LEMMA

For stationary all-even Markov P, let r=P*/P on forward-supported edges.
The product M_N=exp(-Sigma_N)=product_{t<N}r(X_t,X_{t+1}) is a nonnegative
supermartingale: conditional integration of the next factor is at most1.
For any deterministic checkpoint T, the CONTINUATION product from T to
T+k has the same property conditional on the entire hidden history F_T,
starting at1. Ville therefore gives

 Pr{some N>=T: Sigma_N-Sigma_T<log delta | F_T}<=delta.    (S1)

Use the sum of new local entropy increments to handle an infinite Sigma_T;
if the prefix entropy is already infinite a finite lower bound is trivial.
In finite reciprocal CTMCs exp(-Sigma(t)) is the stationary reversed-
generator likelihood-ratio martingale. The same argument applies to every
future physical time, not merely samples. Correct reversal and unchanged
dynamics are indispensable. This is established entropy-martingale
machinery, used at its actual conditional scope.

Let C_T be ANY observation-measurable fixed-horizon lower endpoint with
Pr{C_T>Sigma_T}<=a. Combining its coverage with (S1) gives

 Pr{some N>=T: Sigma_N<C_T+log delta}<=a+delta.             (S2)

No independence between the inference and future event is assumed. The
conditional bound holds for every past. Hidden states and Sigma are not
inputs to the observation-only endpoint C_T or the final algorithm.

3. A TIME-UNIFORM BAND AND A VALID STOPPING RULE

Fix deterministic checkpoints T_j and error allocations

       a_j=d_j=alpha/[4j(j+1)],       j>=1.

At checkpoint j compute a valid fixed-horizon C_j with risk a_j, splitting
it among any PREDECLARED entropy-witness library and its internal error
terms. Use the universal baseline log(alpha/2). Then

 B_N=max{log(alpha/2), max_{j:T_j<=N}[C_j+log d_j]}         (S3)

satisfies

          Pr{Sigma_N>=B_N at EVERY N}>=1-alpha.           (S4)

The baseline costs alpha/2; checkpoint inference and future-descent costs
each sum to alpha/4. Every selection, including the best past checkpoint,
is charged. Thus one may stop when B_N reaches a specified positive
entropy total. The endpoint never silently extrapolates a rate from a
past record: it carries a certified TOTAL with a negative descent allowance.
It remains valid while the same stationary dynamics continues. Resetting
or changing the generator starts a different contract.

For sampled CTMC observations, C_j may be the bound on full CTMC entropy
proved in PROOF.txt. Then (S4) also holds at all intervening physical times
if B is held constant between updates. No unobserved microscopic jump is
treated as an acquired measurement.

4. AN EXACT INTEGER SCHEDULE WITH A COMPLETE LIMIT

Within doubling epoch k>=1 use

       T_{k,r}=2^k+floor(r 2^k/k),   r=0,...,k-1,

in increasing order and number them j=1,2,... . They are distinct,
T_{j+1}/T_j->1, and the number through N is O((log N)^2). Hence
log(1/a_j)=O(log log T_j), plus fixed witness-description penalties.
The entropy and martingale calibration terms at a checkpoint are
O(sqrt(T_j log log T_j)) for fixed witness constants and optimized
horizon-only tuning. This is NOT a uniform convergence rate for empirical
word laws. Between checkpoints there is an additional staleness cost:
the certified total is held constant, and the relative checkpoint gap
is O(1/log N). These costs must not be suppressed.

Combining (S3) with the complete entropy-dual library of PROOF.txt gives

       B_N/N -> I(P_Y) almost surely                       (S5)

for stationary ergodic observed laws, with uniform coverage (S4) over all
stationary hidden models. At checkpoint j use growing finite prefixes
of the certificate library with prior weights. The lower limit follows
from the ergodic theorem, vanishing calibration terms and density of the
full entropy duals. The upper limit uses the attaining least-cost hidden
model: its path entropy/N tends to I, and sum_j a_j<infinity makes
checkpoint coverage failures finite almost surely by Borel-Cantelli.
Earlier finite endpoints divided by N vanish. Later endpoints satisfy
C_j/T_j<=I+epsilon; because T_j<=N and I>=0 they cannot make the limsup
exceed I. The latest checkpoint, T_j/N->1, supplies the matching lower
limit. Negative descent allowances divided by T_j vanish. Infinite I
and the observed-component corollary follow as in the main proof.

This is the discrete standard-Borel observable minimum. Full CTMC coverage
does not turn it into an attained CTMC minimum or reveal within-grid
excess dissipation.

5. IMPLEMENTATION AND AN ACTUAL SOURCE POWER COMPARISON

checkpoint_band.py implements (S3) for the analytic entropy witness, full
grid entropy duals, or the previous reflection e-value with a fixed lambda.
It updates sufficient statistics in one pass. It retains a two-bit buffer
for the analytic witness; a finite generic library needs its word buffers
and lookup tables. Only the largest transported endpoint is needed unless
history output is requested. The JSON CLI loads the full record; the
iterable API avoids storing it. Certificate verification, rational-log
precision, all checkpoint computations and any retained history are
additional explicit computation costs.

The same transport is applied to BOTH compared methods. The older
reflection approach is not handicapped by leaving it on the weaker
per-sample spending schedule. An optimistic oracle envelope permits any
lambda in its original exp(-lambda T_n-2n lambda^2) e-value family with
no fitting penalty; its log endpoint still cannot exceed

                         ((-T_n)_+)^2/(4n).               (S6)

Any properly mixed or selection-charged member is no larger. The new
comparison is therefore against this entire earlier reflection e-value
family, not against all conceivable inference methods.

For the SAME specified16-state CTMC source, a separate exact power proof
uses source information only for validation: the lag-two conditional
mean h(x)=f(x)(P^2f)(x) has standard deviation<1/5 and is8-periodic on
the ring. P restricted to its mean-zero8-periodic subspace has norm<1/4.
The Poisson solution V=sum P^k(h-Eh) consequently has oscillation<=32/15.
The two-step future innovation has conditional range2. Hoeffding on its
two residue classes, the Poisson martingale and Holder give the SOURCE
tail bound

 Pr{|T_n-n mu|>=s} <=2exp[-((s-32/15)_+)^2/((25/2)n)],

with the factor2 omitted for either fixed sign, and -0.39<mu<-0.38.
This known-source bound is not supplied to either inference algorithm.

At T_j=16384, j=92, the new band can certify1000 nats with high power.
For every earlier checkpoint, a reflection-family1000-nat crossing would
require -T_n>=2sqrt(1000n), by (S6). The same source tail bounds its
probability. The exact arithmetic, stronger one-sided bounds, power/risk
accounting, fresh sampled records and full preparation/time/jump costs are
in checkpoint_receipt.json. These are source-specific mathematical
guarantees and synthetic validation, not physical experiments or a claim
of superiority over all ordinary methods.

6. A FAILED SHORTCUT RETAINED

An attempted positive-kernel resolvent f=sum_{n>=0}nu^-n K^n1 gives
Kf<=nu f and E f<=nu/(nu-rho) from fixed-time moment bounds rho^n.
It does produce a time-uniform boundary, but pays log[nu/(nu-rho)].
This is exactly the geometric-discount union bound in another form;
choosing small slack does not remove its logarithmic cost. It supplied
no missing maximal inequality. The useful improvement above instead
uses the conditional martingale for actual entropy increments. It does
not assert that an arbitrary finite-memory observer is reversible.
