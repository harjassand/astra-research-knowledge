# Acquired pure-return compiler: result and reading guide

## Result

The full finite-bit receiving construction passed independent mathematical-existence review on 31 October 2026. Given actual succinct source operations and a certified return horizon, it avoids a loop proportional to rapid source rate and avoids explicit source-state/Kraus enumeration. This is not an implemented circuit, novelty claim, practical crossover, or learned-source result.

Input: controlled Stinespring W and inverse, prepared psi,c with W psi=c tensor psi, acquired reflection about P=|psi><psi|, and (Phi^dagger)^m(I-P)<=(I-P)/16. The system coupling is bounded, acquired and exactly centered, PKP=0. Source starts once in psi. Controls are system-only at prescribed times; their implementation and final estimation costs remain paid.

Output: an unconditional finite CPTP simulation of the retained private source-system process generated by -i[H0+sqrt(lambda)K,.]+lambda(Phi-I), uniformly over allowed controls/references, to the requested channel error. Intermediate actual source outputs are retained, including after jumps. Private artificial jump times and colors are not exposed.

Cost: polynomial in return horizon m, 1+b^2T+alpha0 T+p, input/register lengths, log(2+lambda T) and log(1/epsilon), with actual acquired-operation costs multiplied in. The construction pays O(sqrt(m)) complete-isometry amplification, finite virtual sources and a physical count cap O(b^2Tm^2+m log(1/epsilon)). No optimized practical exponent is claimed.

## Concrete example and essential qualification

The acquired signed top-to-random shuffle has factorially many classical labels but polynomial-size known operations and a coupon-collector/symmetry return certificate.
