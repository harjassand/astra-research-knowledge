2. A RETURNING TWO-PHASE KERNEL LEMMA

Adjoin an auxiliary bit c which flips at every HALF step. Its stationary
law is uniform, its flip is a reversible involution, and its entropy is zero.
The product chain has stationary law pi times uniform and transition R times
flip. Let K be its positive entropy-tilted kernel for a bounded payoff H
satisfying the entropy cone H_s+phi*(H_a)<=0, phi(t)=2t atanh t.
The earlier proved kernel lemma gives w(K)<=exp(theta^2 M^2/2), |H_a|<=M,
for0<theta<=1/2, where w is the complex numerical radius.

The observation protocol fixes c_0=0. This initial clock law is not stationary,
but there is NO factor2 loss at an EVEN horizon: K^(2N) preserves clock phase.
For the unit vector v0=sqrt2*1{c=0} in L2(pi times uniform),

 E_{pi,c0=0} exp{theta[sum H-Sigma_halfgrid]}
      =<v0,K^(2N)v0><=w(K^(2N))<=w(K)^(2N).                (V1)

The last step is the numerical-radius power inequality already proved in
reversible_currents/PROOF.txt. This is a quadratic-form return identity,
not arbitrary conditioning of a stationary moment inequality. An odd number
of half steps does not have this return identity. The receiver includes an
exact one-step counterexample to dropping the even-return condition.

The auxiliary bit is a mathematical lift, not a physical clock that must
be manufactured. Its entropy cancels identically. A deterministic3-cycle
would NOT have zero all-even entropy; see Section8 before generalizing.

3. A CONE PAYOFF THAT ELIMINATES EVERY UNOBSERVED MIDPOINT

Write u(z)=R f(z), v(z)=R* f(z), t=(u-v)/2, a=(u+v)/2 at physical midpoint
states z. Then |t|+|a|<=1. On the augmented chain put t=a=0 at phase0 and
use these physical predictions at phase1. Put F(x,0)=f(x), F(x,1)=0 and
d(x,y)=t(x)F(y)-t(y)F(x). On cross-phase edges define

 H(x,y)=4d(x,y)-2[t(x)^2+t(y)^2+a(x)^2+a(y)^2].             (V2)

Set H=0 on same-phase pairs (they have no transition mass). On each
cross-phase pair only one t,a is nonzero. Thus |H_a|=|4d|<=4 and
H_s=-2(t^2+a^2). Since phi*(h)<=h^2/8 and d^2=t^2 on these binary edges,
H_s+phi*(H_a)<=-2a^2<=0. The ENTIRE augmented entropy cone holds.

For one interval with left state x, midpoint z, right state y, abbreviate
f_x=f(x),f_y=f(y). Exact algebra gives

 H(x,z)+H(z,y)=-4f_x f_y
       +2(u+f_x)(f_y-u)+2(v+f_y)(f_x-v).                  (V3)

The first residual is a forward martingale difference when conditioned on
the midpoint and all its past. The second is a backward difference for the
ACTUAL stationary R*, conditioned on the midpoint and all its future. Each
has conditional range at most8. Intervals are disjoint forward and backward
martingale blocks; no observed independence or mixing bound is assumed.
Conditional Hoeffding and optimized Holder give, for their total Z,

 E exp(lambda Z)<=exp(N lambda^2*8^2/2).                   (V4)

Potentials and flanks are absent: every interval has exactly one complete
left-midpoint-right block. All midpoint states and predictions in (V2)-(V3)
are proof devices, never acquired inputs.

Combining (V1), M=4 and the full-path conditional-Holder projection with
(V4), with failure probability at most alpha_m+alpha_e,

 Sigma_full(N Delta)>=-4S_N-8sqrt(2N log(1/alpha_m))
                          -16N theta-log(1/alpha_e)/theta. (V5)

Take alpha_m=alpha_e=alpha/2 and a horizon/confidence-only theta in(0,1/2].
The implementation uses directed rational logs/square roots and a nearby
rational optimum. Negative endpoints remain negative. There is no source
generator, hidden count, spectral gap or guessed intermediate label input.

At population level this implies Delta*sigma>=4(-C(Delta)) when C<0.
That population consequence can already be obtained by applying the ordinary

Use a4-state ring with clockwise p=1, counterclockwise q=1/1000, uniform

 Delta0=pi/omega=1000pi/999,       r=exp(-gamma Delta0).

For f=(1,1,-1,-1), g=(1,-1,-1,1), the generator acts as

 K_Y=[[(1-r)/2,(1+r)/2],[(1+r)/2,(1-r)/2]].                (V6)

     Pr_source{ever produces a POSITIVE endpoint}<=alpha.  (V7)

6. AN EQUALLY INFORMED CONTINUOUS-TIME COMPARATOR

For stationary reversible CTMCs the sampled kernel P is a positive
semidefinite self-adjoint contraction. With F multiplication by the binary
label and lambda>=0, its tilted operator is
K_lambda=cosh(lambda)P-sinh(lambda) FPF <=cosh(lambda)I.
It has nonnegative transition kernel and is self-adjoint. Positivity in
the order sense implies its norm is bounded by its largest quadratic-form
value: |<h,Kh>|<=<|h|,K|h|>. Consequently

 E_revCTMC exp(-lambda S_N)<=cosh(lambda)^N.                (V8)

This is a stronger ordinary comparator than a generic Gaussian lag-two
reflection bound. The PSD premise matters; a reversible discrete flip
violates (V8). The receiver checks that counterexample exactly.

For a general reciprocal stationary CTMC define reversible geometric rates
g_ij=sqrt(q_ij q_ji pi_j/pi_i). Cauchy-Schwarz and stationarity give
g_i=sum_j g_ij<=q_i. Its full reversible path measure dominates the
geometric mean of the original forward/reversed path measures, since
holding factors contribute exp[-int(q_i-g_i)dt]<=1. Thus any nonnegative
e-value valid for EVERY reversible CTMC transfers as

 E_P[e^{-Sigma_full/2} E(Y)]<=1.

Using (V8) gives the fixed-horizon path endpoint

       2[-lambda S_N-N logcosh(lambda)+log alpha].         (V9)

Apply the same checkpoint transport and budget to (V9). Grant even a free
record-dependent supremum over ALL lambda, with no fitting penalty. Its
endpoint is bounded by2N psi(((-S_N)_+)/N), where
psi(v)=[(1+v)log(1+v)+(1-v)log(1-v)]/2. This envelope dominates every fixed
lambda, static mixture and properly charged selection in THIS family.
It is an optimistic upper comparator, not itself valid inference.

Since2psi(v)<=v^2/(1-v^2), a target b crossing needs
 -S_N>=sqrt(b N^2/(N+b)). For2Nlog2<b it is impossible even for S_N=-N.

8. A POPULATION DIVISIBILITY HIERARCHY AND AN UNFINISHED BRIDGE

For a complete stationary observed discrete law P_Y define V_m as m times
the least e(R) over all stationary standard-Borel discrete hidden Markov
kernels R whose observation sampled every m steps has law P_Y. Intermediate
labels are unconstrained. This is a population object, not an acquired law.
The earlier compact predictive-flow construction applies with depth mL and
linear word constraints summed over all unobserved intermediate symbols.
It gives attained compact convex problems, complete finite-grid lower
approximations and a complete-law minimum, at this unrestricted Borel scope.

Data processing gives V_m<=V_n when m divides n: an n-stride realization
coarsened to its n/m-step kernel realizes m-stride observations, with edge
entropy at most(n/m)e(R). For every CTMC realization,

 V_1<=V_2<=V_4<=...<=Delta*sigma_CT.                       (V10)

Here V_1 is the previously constructed discrete observable minimum; the
source (V6) has V_1=0 and V_2>=4r>0. Thus the hierarchy can STRICTLY gain
physical-model information. No equality of its limit with the finite-CTMC
minimum, CTMC attainment, useful generic computation bound or complete
single-record calibration for every m is proved here.

Two failed shortcuts explain the remaining gap. First, coarsened pair
flows at several rational times need not compose as one Markov semigroup;
the relation T_{s+t}=T_s T_t is not a free linear constraint on those flows.
Second, a deterministic m-phase clock for m>=3 has a one-way cycle under
ordinary all-even reversal and infinite entropy. It cannot mark virtual
times for free. The special2-phase returning construction above avoids
both shortcuts at its actual square-root scope. Further complete CTMC
inference needs an earned compatibility/tightness argument.
