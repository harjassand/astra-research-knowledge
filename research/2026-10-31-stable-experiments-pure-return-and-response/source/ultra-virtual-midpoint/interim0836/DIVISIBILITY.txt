Let P_r be the COMPLETE stationary balanced binary Markov law with transition
eigenvalue-r,0<r<1: its flip probability is(1+r)/2. Define V_m as in PROOF.txt:
m times the minimum entropy per edge of a stationary standard-Borel hidden
kernel R whose observations every m steps have law P_r. Then

          V_1(P_r)=0,
          V_2(P_r)=2 phi(sqrt r)=4sqrt r atanh(sqrt r)>0.   (D1)

The second equality is an EXACT unrestricted-hidden-model result, including
the complete observed law, not a moment diagnostic or fixed hidden-count fit.

Lower bound. For any such root R, let C_j=E[f_0 f_j] at its fine grid.
C_2=-r, while its C_1 is unconstrained. Write u=Rf,v=R*f,a=(u+v)/2,t=(u-v)/2.
Then C_2=E(a^2-t^2), C_1=E(fa), and E t^2>=C_1^2-C_2>=r.
One direct derivation of e(R)>=phi(sqrt(E t^2)) uses the symmetrized edge
measure eta=(Gamma+Gamma^T)/2 and edge imbalance z=(Gamma-Gamma^T)/(Gamma+Gamma^T).
Conditional expectation gives t(x)=E_eta[z(x,Y)f(Y)|x]. Thus E t^2<=E_eta z^2.
Also e(R)=E_eta phi(z), and phi(sqrt s)=2sum_{k>=1}s^k/(2k-1) is convex
and increasing. Jensen gives e(R)>=phi(sqrt(E_eta z^2))>=phi(sqrt r).
Extended zero/support/infinite cases use the same limiting conventions as
the original entropy cone. Multiplying by2 proves V_2>=2phi(sqrt r).

Attainment. Put b=sqrt r and use the four-state kernel already constructed
in predictive_flow/PROOF.txt, states(s,t) in{+1,-1}^2, uniform stationary law:

 R((s,t),(s,-t))=(1+b s t)/2,
 R((s,t),(-s,t))=(1-b s t)/2.

It has Rs=b t, Rt=-b s and hence R^2s=-b^2s=-r s. Strong lumpability makes
the ENTIRE stride-two observed process the Markov law P_r, not just its
lag-two moment. Pairing the reciprocal edges gives e(R)=phi(b), proving
attainment in (D1). The root itself is periodic and is not claimed to be a
finite-CTMC sampled kernel. Its admissibility is at V_2's discrete-root scope.

Consequently every stationary all-even CTMC realization of P_r obeys the
