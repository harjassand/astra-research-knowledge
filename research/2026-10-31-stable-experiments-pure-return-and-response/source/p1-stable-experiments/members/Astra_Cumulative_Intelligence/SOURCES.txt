Established primary methods used or compared
-------------------------------------------
Guglielmi, Protasov, Invariant polytopes of linear operators with applications
to regularity of wavelets and of subdivisions.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1502.01192
https://doi.org/10.1137/15M1006945
Fixed coordinate matrices are the supplied input; generator-image expansion
and symmetric-hull membership are direct structural predecessors. The root
opened the primary PDF; the comparison agent read the algorithm passages.

Bach, Lacoste-Julien, Obozinski (ICML 2012), On the Equivalence between Herding
and Conditional Gradient Algorithms.
https://icml.cc/2012/papers/683.pdf
Hilbert/kernel quadratic approximation and conditional-gradient machinery are
established, including implicit feature access. Root and comparison agent read
the primary source. No claim that coordinate-free convex projection is new.

Pola, Girard, Tabuada (Automatica 2008), Approximately bisimilar symbolic models
for nonlinear control systems.
https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.0246
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0706.0246
Symbolic abstraction and approximation relations are established. Their
construction uses state grids/reachable-set information and stability premises.

Devonport, Saoud, Arcak, Symbolic Abstractions From Data: A PAC Learning Approach.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.13901
Data-driven abstraction is also established; the stated source evaluates
successors of given states. It is not the same hidden Gram interface.

Liu, Netrapalli, Szepesvari, Jin, Optimistic MLE—A Generic Model-based Algorithm
for Partially Observable Sequential Decision Making.
https://arxiv.org/html/2209.14997v3
Strong model-based and reward-free baselines; its PSR contracts and known core
tests/conditioning cannot be omitted. This run does not claim to improve its
general POMDP guarantees or computational oracle costs.

Wang, Zhang, Vinayak (ALT 2026), Bridging Lifelong and Multi-Task Representation
Learning: An Algorithm and a Complexity Measure.
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v313/wang26c.html
Task-eluder and multi-task ERM already treat shared representations across
unknown future tasks. Generic 'novel task adds a direction' is not claimed new.

Wagenmaker, Simchowitz, Jamieson (ICML 2021), Task-Optimal Exploration in Linear
Dynamical Systems.
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/wagenmaker21a.html
Task-dependent experiment design and instance-optimal acquisition are strong
ordinary comparators in the fully observed linear setting.
