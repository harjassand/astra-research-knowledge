## Main scientific decision

A static exact-linear "innovation dimension" or retained-uncertainty theorem
would substantially overlap N997. Its exact rank optimality theorem and equal
retaining learner already give that principle. A linear operator-law-to-program
compiler would overlap N996. A generic count-to-Hankel-predictor argument would
overlap N952 and ordinary PSR/OOM/spectral realization.

The strongest source-grounded new direction appears to be acquiring a useful
*intervention-closed* representation, with the closure tests and exploration
operation actually obtained and paid. N1220 identifies the exact failure:
complete baseline data only identify the source on an excited invariant span;
a new intervention may take the state/effect out of that span. A useful
construction would have to discover and pay the new directions, rather than
assume a closed model or a universal counterfactual oracle. This is a candidate
opening, not a demonstrated novelty or advantage.
