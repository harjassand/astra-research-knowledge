# A finite-capacity packet mark for normal interleavings

Live representation construction, not a global recurrence theorem. It removes a spurious debt caused by erasing an unfinished packet when another cycle starts; it does not yet pay genuine repeated-source loans.

## Exact augmented dynamics

Take a fixed number K of auxiliary packet slots, each carrying a raw complex or the empty zero placeholder. Let tau be the sum of the carried complexes. Define the admissible augmented state by the condition that the lattice class x-tau+L has a nonnegative representative, and set

    Psi(x,packets)=Phi_L(x-tau), U=(1+Psi)^2.

Here Phi_L is defined on ANY signed integer representative whose L class meets the nonnegative orthant. A packet mark is auxiliary bookkeeping, NOT a claim that its molecules remain physically segregated or that the physical process has the carried-target mark of the earlier theorem.

Each genuine raw channel is counted ONCE with its actual propensity. There is no rate multiplication by the number of matching slots.

* For a conservative-core channel, retain the packet modes. Its physical displacement is in L, so Psi is exactly unchanged.
* If a noncore source s equals one occupied packet complex, update one deterministic matching slot s->u. Then physical x and tau change by the same vector; Psi is exactly unchanged. A zero-source reaction may advance an empty slot when one exists.
* For every other actual channel, reset to one slot carrying its actual product u and all other slots empty. The new residual x'-u=x-s is genuinely nonnegative. Its actual output is always represented and its receiving value is Phi_L(x-s); it is not dropped or supplied externally.

The physical marginal is EXACTLY the original chain. Normal source steps and arbitrary conservative-core interleavings preserve the same quotient energy. In particular, two cycles' simultaneous normal packets do not lose the first packet's credit merely because the second zero-source input fired.

Since K is finite and the raw mark sizes are bounded, |tau| is uniformly bounded. Every nonnegative representative of x-tau+L has total N(x)-|tau|, so Psi is normlike uniformly over all packet modes. Allowing UNBOUNDED packet capacity would destroy that implication: an indefinitely growing population could be stored entirely in the marks at constant residual energy. That is a decisive constraint on an unbounded-packet proposal, not a reason to assume a finite controller for the physical population.

## Earned local cell, and its unpaid exit

Fix a residual lattice class and K. All normal states have total population at most its residual total plus K times the largest raw complex size. Hence the complete normal state set is finite, including all actual conservative-fiber compositions. Its genuine normal generator and ALL nonnormal exit channels can be acquired from raw counts. An actual Green solve, when transience/absorption is proved, gives the physical residence and the complete source/mode-indexed exit law. A projected neutral component is not automatically a closed physical class; the accepted N1396 warning applies.

For a fixed packet mode tau and unexpected source s, every physical state in its normal lattice fiber has the SAME quotient receiving value Phi_L(x-s), by the proved lattice invariance. The exact output mode is the actual product u. Thus a common receiver exists at those exits without freezing the internal composition.

This is not yet a return construction: the first nonnormal exit can have positive energy debt. The remaining operation must continue through those genuine repeated-source loans until its negative repayment dominates the physical time and the complete squared increment in (Q1), or produce another common energy. Concatenating the isolated cycle theorems would not prove that bound.
