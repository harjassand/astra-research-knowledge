The last stop pays a real protected-region exit.

Every hot excursion beginning at D=0 uses its first genuine zero-source input. Those inputs are a constant-rate Poisson clock beta_0, independent of the cold autonomous trial. The number of hot excursions begun before T_c is therefore bounded by N_0(T_c). It has all polynomial moments uniformly in the initial hot population.

## 5. Actual conservative activation from the clean input

While A=D=1, run the genuine core until a narrow reference-central band or its FIRST noncore event. Write S=B+E, and fix the two hidden-pair differences, which are bounded in terms of R. Core B increases by2 at rate g=(E)_2(F)_2 and decreases by2 at rate f=2(B)_2(C)_2. Its reference ratio is exactly g(B)/f(B+2); hence its modal point and a narrow central band are acquired by finite integer comparisons.

Choose a band a fixed small relative distance eta>0 below and around that modal point. Starting at the cold end, until the lower edge is hit,

    L_core B=2(g-f)>=c_eta S^4

for all sufficiently large S. This follows directly from the quartic rates: after dividing by S^4 their difference is positive on the compact interval a fixed distance below the unique interior root. Bounded pair differences produce only O_R(1/S) errors. Stopping the B martingale therefore gives

    E pure_core_hitting_time <=C_eta/S^3.                   (C5)

The full noncore hazard at A=D=1 is alpha_0+beta_0+alpha_1 B+beta_1 E<=C S. Thus the ACTUAL probability of interruption before the band is <=C_eta/S^2. This is a finite-rate estimate with its actual clock; no infinite-rate limit or gap is invoked. At ANY such interrupting noncore event Delta Z=0, since its phase1->2 correction increases by the same2 as the physical population. At the band, Z has also stayed exactly fixed.

For any specified finite K, choose eta small enough that the reached point lies in a reference band of deficit at most cN/(8K), where c is a fixed cold-barrier constant. Exact factorial ratios show this: near the modal fraction the normalized log weight has a continuous strictly convex limit, and the finite-buffer discrepancy is O_R(log N). Uniform rational bounds on its one-dimensional derivatives acquire such an eta and a finite threshold. Thus successful clean activation enters an ACTUAL deep reference band with probability 1-O_R,K(N^-2). Failed activation already has its actual value paid.

## 6. A fixed number of slow episodes repays ALL launch loans

Use the completed normal-cell episode repeatedly after clean activation, for at most K unexpected-source exits. Stop immediately if an exit has an anchor4 or fails the next reference-band test. All outputs still receive (C1).

First, each episode has mean physical time <=C(N+15), bounded |Delta Z|<=3, and expected Delta Z<=-d<0, with d depending only on the fixed rates and a fixed warm fraction. This d can be chosen independently of R; R changes the finite threshold. Second, for a fixed bounded raw buffer change the reference log-deficit changes by at most C log(N+15). This is the already proved finite-conformal-generator buffer argument applied to the tilted objective H-k log2; its rank-one generators are acquired explicitly. Phase weights add only constants. It is an earned comparison, not a neighboring-profile assumption.

At each stage allow an extra deficit cN/(4K). The actual killed-occupation bound makes its violation polynomial times exp(-cN/(4K)). Bounded buffer transport fits inside that allowance for sufficiently large N. The K-stage budget remains below the cold barrier. Pair differences change by at most2 per stage, so R+2K suffices; after a good exit the cell total falls by1, and it otherwise stays fixed until an abort. ## 7. Earned scope and remaining global work

The operation pays a random cold launch, its actual interruptions and unbounded population tail, then repays that launch by a fixed but possibly enormous number of slow actual episodes. The bounded-cold-head theorem no longer assumes a receiving law or a uniformly fast conservative fiber. It combines the banked autonomous physical return/occupation theorem with a newly derived raw normal-cell operation; it does not merely concatenate their statements.

It is still a theorem for a FIXED finite cold head. D_R and K can depend on R. Letting R grow with the hot population without new uniform control would silently reinsert the missing global receiver. The other important uncovered regions include unbounded cold populations/anchors and hidden-pair differences comparable to the total. The normal-cell graph rule is reusable beyond one ordered source chain, but acquiring a reference policy and compatible face coverage for arbitrary weakly reversible lists remains open.
