# A constructive productive-exit synthesis rule

The new star launch suggests a reusable operation, rather than an enlarged ordered-cycle constant. This statement is a proof rule, not a claim that every weakly reversible list has its certificate.

Fix one proper receiving quantity z>=1 whose normal transitions are neutral, and whose genuine stopped increments are bounded. Partition a complete actual launch's exits into direct repayment G, harmful B, neutral C, clean activation A and other neutral U. Suppose its RAW finite mode flow acquires

    P_B<=C_B P_A,
    P_C+P_U<=C_0 P_A,
    E launch_time<=C z^s.

Every exit is included, so P_G+P_A>=p_*>0. A harmful launch need not have vanishing probability. The key is a proved matching of its actual flux to an activation opportunity, before receiving those outputs. Such a matching can be acquired by finite crossing potentials and exact raw propensity ratios, as in the seven-state star; it is not an assumption of an exit profile.

For each clean activation input, PERSONALLY construct a continuation at the same z, including its failures, with

    E Delta z <=-L d+o(1),
    E(Delta z)^2<=C_L,
    E continuation_time<=C_L z^s.

Require a fixed bound on each continuation's final increment (or prove all higher moments needed by a chosen power). An activation kernel or expectation supplied without its physical construction does not satisfy this requirement. In the completed instance it is actual quartic core hitting, paid noncore interruption, followed by a fixed number of actual normal-cell episodes whose finite adjoint occupation and raw crossing debt comparison are proved.

If launch increments satisfy Delta z<=-a on G, <=b on B and <=0 elsewhere, choose the fixed L with Ld>b C_B+a. Then the complete actual episode has

    E Delta z <=-a(P_G+P_A)+o(1)<=-a p_*/2,
    E(Delta z)^2<=C,
    E episode_time<=C z^s.

An integer power p>=max(2,s+1), with a fixed scalar multiplier, pays physical time by the SAME receiving function z^p. When s=1 the receiver is quadratic. This uses bounded increments at the final actual stop; it does not require exponential return, a bounded word length, stationary activity or a uniform fiber gap.

This is the joint synthesis principle earned by the new construction: match debt to productive exit BEFORE paying it, then choose a fixed amount of earned downstream repayment. It avoids the nonuniform clean-zero route completely. Global recurrence still requires covering every outside state at that same receiver and earning every continuation. A raw-policy finder and arbitrary-network coverage have not been proved.
