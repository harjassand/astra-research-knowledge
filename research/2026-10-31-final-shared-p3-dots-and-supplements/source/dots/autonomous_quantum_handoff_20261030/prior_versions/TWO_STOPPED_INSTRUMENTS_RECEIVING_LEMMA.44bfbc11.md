# Two actual stopped-instrument comparisons: the simpler endogenous-selection interface
2026-10-30. Root-suggested simplification, written here as an explicit receiving lemma. Candidate, not a physical autonomous controller construction. The companion joint-cut argument remains a more general fallback; it is not needed for this route.

## Contract: a real trigger, not a decoded past or a nominal noise unraveling

Fix a horizon H. The native memory starts in its admitted encoded/ready (or certified warm) source. Adjoin an actual controller/record subsystem with a declared normal quantum instrument. Its nominal operations are DATA-independent in the fixed representation; any DATA sensing/coupling backaction is included in the physical bounded-body joint perturbation contract. At its selected trigger, an ACTUAL terminal CP map acts at that physical time and exports the stipulated quantum output/record. An equivalent alive/terminal instrument representation is allowed.

For the waiting analysis require the actual trigger source effects and controller-only instrument legs to commute with EACH of the system witnesses/cuts: the canonical-memory pair (F_C,P_C) and the BUSY pair (G_B,P_B). A sufficient concrete case is a trigger on an actual retained classical controller/bath register only, not a strong DATA measurement. The trigger can be wrong, premature, delayed, correlated with noise, or never occur. Its semantic truth is not a premise, but its actual CP instrument and support/propagator model are premises. Generic signed HP noise pieces are NOT assigned imaginary nominal SUCCESS/fault records.

One cannot reconstruct a past quantum state from a final classical record. The terminal quantum map must be present at the actual selected time. If a theorem describes only an ideal snapshot, it must say so; it has not implemented a transfer or a noiseless post-trigger holding device. A real receiver subsequently evolves by its stated physical CPTP dynamics.

## 1. Canonical stopped comparison

Remove the trigger gain from the positive alive evolution. Its effect contributes a negative source loss. Since that effect commutes F_C and P_C, it only subtracts a positive term in the witness drift. The accepted canonical killed moment and boundary estimate therefore hold on the subnormalized alive process, uniformly over the actual controller/bath correlations. Terminal output records are retained; no rare branch is normalized.

The complete full-versus-canonical-killed argument, including its terminal CP output legs, gives

    ||I_actual^[0,H]-I_C^[0,H]||_diamond
       <=epsilon_C(H)=C polynomial(N)(1+H)e^(-c_C N).      (1)

The instrument includes a no-trigger-by-H outcome. I_C is positive and all its outputs have the common canonical logical-identity module. Its DATA output may still carry native prefix dressing. Initial source-bad mass, if present, is separated first exactly as in the continuous port.

The extra controller/trigger terms do not enter a new large boundary norm: they preserve the cut. Sensing backaction that does not commute it is already counted in the declared physical local perturbation budget. Actual normal interval CP/TNI existence remains explicit.

## 2. Independent BUSY stopped comparison

Use the ACTUAL G_B of the direct singleton theorem. It commutes M_BUSY and P_B=1_(M_BUSY<=bN). The full-Hilbert log-moment drift survives complete-loss BUSY killing and the commuting trigger loss. Bounded local M bandwidth puts every outgoing system leg in M>=bN-K. The exponential barrier and standard finite-system-leg/shared-bath boundary estimate give

    ||I_actual^[0,H]-I_B^[0,H]||_diamond
       <=epsilon_B(H)=C polynomial(N)(1+H)e^(-c_B N).      (2)

I_B is a positive instrument whose output at its ACTUAL trigger has BUSY count at most bN. The trigger itself preserves that predicate. This is an unnormalized finite-horizon instrument comparison, not a pointwise tail conditioned on a noise-selected time and not a claim of an underlying classical coherent-clock exit path.

No commutation of F_C with P_B, or of G_B with P_C, is used. There is no product of the two witnesses.

## 3. Transfer then gentle cut

Since I_B has zero mass outside P_B, (2) bounds the actual stopped instrument's bad-BUSY effect by epsilon_B(H), completely uniformly over reference input. By (1), the canonical-killed stopped instrument has bad-BUSY effect at most

    epsilon_B(H)+epsilon_C(H).

Apply the physical controller/tag cutoff positively to I_C AFTER this transfer. A gentle instrument estimate gives a positive comparison I_safe with both the canonical common module and sparse physical prefixes, and

    ||I_actual^[0,H]-I_safe^[0,H]||_diamond
       <=epsilon_C(H)+2sqrt(epsilon_B(H)+epsilon_C(H))
          +a harmless trace-deficit term.                (3)

The cutoff commutes the forest and the outer common module. It need not commute every original proof observable. The finite number of square roots only reduces the positive exponential rate. Thus a controller-correlated physical trigger can supply the same correctable physical coefficient-span input that the earlier exogenous port required, as an UNNORMALIZED instrument up to horizon H.

A fixed ordinary decoder on that span has identity logical action in every kept coefficient. A later actual receiver CP channel propagates comparison errors contractively, but its intended logical action, mode switching and protection require their own proof.

## 4. Exact exclusions

- No lower bound on trigger probability or arrival time; the no-trigger outcome remains.
- No normalized rare-postselection guarantee: conditioning on a branch of mass p can cost error/p.
- No proof that a request/DONE bit is truthful, or that a fixed B bank has finished routing.
- No autonomous global broadcast, all-ready detector, finite waveform distributor, physical decoder/re-encoder or uninitialized consumer gate resource.
- No free strong DATA measurement: noncommuting sensing must be paid physically.
- No reconstruction of environment histories or hidden past quantum snapshots.

The lemma closes the specific *endogenous selection of a still-protected memory output* seam once an actual commuting-source trigger instrument is supplied.
