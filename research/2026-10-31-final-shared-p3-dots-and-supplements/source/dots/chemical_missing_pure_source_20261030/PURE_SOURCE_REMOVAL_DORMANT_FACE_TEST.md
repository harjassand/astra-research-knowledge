# Removing residual pure sources: a dormant-face obstruction to the current clock bound
Status: explicit local counterexample to an assumption, not to recurrence.

In operational variables take the strongly connected degree2 residual chain

    2A' <-> A'+D <-> C+D,

with positive fixed rates and killing at (A')_2. It contains2A' but neither2C nor2D. Start at A'=2,C=n-2,D=0. Only source2A' is enabled; its fast rate and the clearing rate are both fixed constants. Thus the probability of clearing before the first fast reaction is bounded below by a positive constant independently of n, and the operational clearing mean has a positive constant lower bound. No N-power fast-clearing estimate can hold uniformly.

After the first fast transition creates D, the source C+D has rate of order n, so there is a real graph-driven acceleration mechanism. The problem is not absence of a path to clearing, nor evidence of transience. It is a dormant initial face whose finite-time return law cannot be replaced by a vanishing worst-case output envelope.

Adding a common a0 A seed and restoring physical connector0->A+B->(a0+2)A+qB->0, q>a0+2, embeds this test in the same full weakly reversible architecture. The earlier time-change profile transfer cannot be applied, because its residual pure-source premise is false. Initial low-A, D=0 returns need a nonvanishing but transient phase operator; profiles must retain both A and the newly created D reserve. Pure A-based profile transport and scalar p0->0 alone do not settle that operator.

This is the concrete next multidimensional boundary test if the current graph-path transfer is validated. It is not being labeled a completed recurrence family or a novelty claim.
