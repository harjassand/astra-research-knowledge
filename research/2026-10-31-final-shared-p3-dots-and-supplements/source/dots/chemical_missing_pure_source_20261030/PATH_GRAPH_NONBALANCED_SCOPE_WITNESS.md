# Non-complex-balanced scope witness for the graph-path transfer
Status: algebraic witness only; not a novelty claim or an independently reviewed extension.

Use residual degree v=2 on A',C,D with directed cycle

    2A' -> 2C -> C+D -> 2D -> 2A',

and rate constants1,1,2,1 in that order. This residual graph is strongly connected and contains every pure source. Neither2C norC+D has an immediate A'-producing edge, so it is outside the directional source contract of the already-reviewed profile theorem. It meets the separate pathwise transfer contract.

Add common seed3A, so m=5 and the actual fast composition complexes are5A,3A+2C,3A+C+D,3A+2D. Attach common qB with q>5 and connector0->A+B->5A+qB->0. There are six complexes, one linkage class, and stoichiometric rank4 (two independent conservative composition directions plus the two independent connector directions), so deficiency1.

At a positive complex-balanced point, the three non-anchor cycle vertices force equal fluxes

    C^2=2CD=D^2,

after canceling the common positive A^3 B^q factor. The first equality requires C/D=2 and the second C/D=1/2, impossible. Connector rates at the anchor cannot repair those non-anchor balance equations. Thus the new pathwise scope is not exhausted by deficiency-zero/product-form members.

This does not rule out other established recurrence criteria. The existing strongest-primary comparison still applies, and exact novelty remains unverified.
