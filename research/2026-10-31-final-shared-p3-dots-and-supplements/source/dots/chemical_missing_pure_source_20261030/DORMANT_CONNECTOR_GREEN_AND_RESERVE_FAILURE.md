# Dormant-face Green kernel earned by an ordinary comparator; a reserve-profile obstruction
Status: local construction plus explicit failure, not a global extension.

## Exact dormant D=0 comparator
For residual2A'<->A'+D<->C+D with common a0 A seed, while D=0 the reservoir C is frozen. The only fast reaction that can fire is the D-creating edge from2A', and its physical propensity is k_seed(A)_m(B)_q, with m=a0+2. It has EXACTLY the same monomial as connector clearing.

Until that seed fires, the active variables (A,B) therefore follow the ordinary three-complex connector0->A+B->mA+qB->0, except that each would-be clearing of rate(k_D+k_seed)(A)_m(B)_q is marked as a seed/exit with fixed probability k_seed/(k_D+k_seed). The reference connector is deficiency-zero with stoichiometric rank2 when q!=m. Its complex-balanced parameters solve

    ab=k_I/k_T,
    a^m b^q=k_I/(k_D+k_seed),

and are positive. Its invariant law on each closed lattice class is the corresponding conditional product Poisson law. Thus its return-cycle occupation of every exp(theta(A+B)) is finite. Independent marking of the clearing events earns a finite killed Green occupation before the seed, from every fixed bounded input phase. This is an ordinary exact comparator, not an assumed recurrence theorem for the full three-composition network. The frozen C contributes only an overall exponential factor.

A finite-phase exponential row sum can exceed1 here, but that alone is not an obstruction: the dormant residual tilt is a conservation-induced similarity, as recorded separately. The actual weighted Green kernel is earned from the reference connector's regenerative occupations and the fixed positive marking probability.

## Attempted two-coordinate reserve and its precise limitation
In operational residual variables choose R=A'+2D. For the four directed fast edges, its jumps are +1,-1,-1,+1 respectively. Hence

    Q R=k_1(A')_2+k_4 C D-(k_2+k_3)A'D.

When R<=epsilon N and N is large, C is a positive fraction of N, so choosing epsilon small gives QR>=c[(A')_2+ND]. Thus R is a real graph-derived reserve near the C-dominant face, even though A' alone may initially decrease. It captures the D seed rather than forgetting it.

However this is not a global profile. Near the D-dominant face, C and A' may be tiny; the same R is large, Q R can be negative, and clearing can be disabled. A killed inverse-R supersolution cannot rely on clearing domination there. Replacing all low-A states by a single R profile would therefore import the missing multifacet control. A separate C-reserve chart or an actual joint boundary return operator is needed.

The useful outcome is an earned dormant-face Green kernel and a locally valid upstream reserve, together with a sharp failure of its global extension. Neither the exact reference connector nor this reserve calculation settles the outgoing seeded-state return law across both faces.

Primary comparator checked directly: Anderson–Craciun–Kurtz, [Product-form stationary distributions for deficiency zero chemical reaction networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.3042), v4. Its complex-balance/product-form theorem is the ordinary input for the local two-species reference chain. The marked-killing Green argument above additionally uses nonexplosion and regenerative occupation, not just a formal stationary formula.

## Weighted outgoing seed law is also earned locally
The seed jump is not the reference clearing jump; only the PRE-jump killed path is identified. At the marked event, use the actual fast seed output: A decreases by1, D becomes1, B is unchanged, and C stays frozen. Its rate is a fixed multiple of (A)_m(B)_q. The expected exponential output weight is the killed occupation integral of this polynomial propensity times the exponential pre-state weight. The reference chain's regenerative occupation is finite for every polynomial times exp(theta(A+B)), since its conditional product-Poisson law has those moments. Consequently the actual outgoing seed law has all exponential moments in A+B, uniformly in the frozen C for every bounded initial phase set. This earns the local Green's weighted exit forcing, not merely its mean lifetime.

The outgoing residual A' is at least1 and D=1. The remaining global obligation is its seeded excursion through the C/D-dominant faces; the local Poisson comparator does not control that excursion.
