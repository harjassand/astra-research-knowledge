# Tail/product-form scope check, 2026-10-30
Limited primary-source search; no novelty clearance and no general tail theorem asserted.

[Classification and threshold dynamics of stochastic reaction networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.07954) explicitly establishes CMP-like tails for its one-dimensional endotactic/closed-class scope. It does not supply an all-dimensional weak-reversibility tail theorem.

[Any Stochastic Reaction Network has a Stationary Measure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.07590) concerns a stationary measure, not necessarily a normalizable probability distribution. It therefore does not remove the recurrence/tightness obligation of the present construction.

[Derivation of stationary distributions via structure transformation](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-021-02117-x) and [Stationary distributions via decomposition](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00285-021-01620-3) are additional ordinary comparators. In particular, failure of ordinary complex balance alone is NOT a proof that no translated/decomposed stationary representation exists. The current non-CB witnesses establish scope separation from the simple deficiency-zero product-Poisson theorem, not exact novelty or exclusion of all conventional methods.
