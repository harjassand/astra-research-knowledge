# A containment guard earns the isolated counter-root boundary range

Status: new deterministic structural construction for ONE connected counter-fault seed, plus a bounded-hole repair interface. It is not the full noisy controller theorem. Arbitrary dirty beacons, multiple overlapping seeds and joint inclusive norms remain open.

## 1. Change the ordinary attempted channel explicitly

Use the oriented event-counter model of `VALID_CLOCK_CUT_SPLICE.md`. Fix a seed-size budget s and a constant radius r≥s+1 (enlarge by the fixed DATA/dependency footprint if required). In addition to its ordinary causal increment test, update h refuses whenever ANY counter-constraint violation occurs within graph distance r of h. This is a finite classical predicate read only after the native transaction acquires its complete read/write footprint. Rejection is the actual identity channel, not a disabled physical timer.

If the whole counter field is valid, the additional predicate is always true: fault-free asynchronous computation is unchanged. All clock reads use the read-only construction; quantum DATA is not swapped into a large inspection hub merely to test the guard.

## 2. Immediate frozen halo after one connected seed

Let the pre-root field n^0 be a valid integer event-counter cut. Change clock labels arbitrarily on one connected set S of at most s vertices. Take the counter modulus large enough to distinguish the local lifts used below. If the new field is still valid, the finite-cut splice lemma applies directly and no invalid-clock purge is needed.

Otherwise let E_bad be its invalid edges. Every invalid edge has an endpoint in S. Let F be the radius-r neighborhood of the endpoints of E_bad. Then:

* S⊂F: a path inside the connected S from any of its vertices to an invalid edge has length at most s−1;
* every vertex in F is frozen by the new ordinary guard;
* all invalid edges remain inside F, and no outside ordinary update creates an invalid edge;
* every accessible boundary vertex of F is outside S, hence its frozen value is exactly its uncorrupted pre-root value;
* F has graph diameter bounded by a fixed function of s and r, independent of M and of the magnitude of the corrupted clock residue.

Thus the accessible frozen boundary has height span D_c≤diam(F), because n^0 was a valid cut and changes by at most1 along each dependency edge. This is an earned boundary-range statement. It does not infer that an arbitrary wrong clock has a small value; the arbitrary value is buried inside the frozen core and is not a boundary source.

The claim is initially for an atomic counter root. In the native implementation, transactions already in flight across that event must be included in the finite leading rectangle. Extra freeze-predicate readers are nondemolition; a stale containment decision is not permission to ignore their actual DATA writes or retirement. A full native seed-size constant must be proved before replacing s by a physical-noise support bound.

## 3. Exterior relaxation and bounded target

Outside F, the only nominal operations are the genuine accepted circuit updates. Their clocks are feasible relative to F and increase toward the maximal directed envelope. The directed constraint adapter proves reachability in exactly the total deficit. For a synchronization neighborhood with O(M²) vertices and O(M) deficit per vertex, this is O(M³) increments; actual time is paid through the fresh native service windows, not inferred from fairness.

Choose a repair target H bounded by a directed-distance sphere satisfying the directed extension inequality

    R>a+L(D_c+a+1),

where a=max_(v∈F)d(o,v) and L is the fixed brickwork layer count. These quantities depend on s,r,L, NOT on M. After exterior relaxation, the target boundary has a feasible oriented-causal extension.

The envelope values on this boundary and the interior extension differ from the old healthy local phase by only a fixed function of H. In particular, the new valid clock cut does not skip O(M) virtual layers merely because the initially corrupted residue was arbitrary. The bad residue is discarded, not used as a reliable phase reference.

## 4. Quantum cost during the wait

All gate clocks in F stay fixed. A neighboring ordinary clock can make only a bounded number of accepted increments before the incident causal guard blocks it (the general three-increment bound is conservative). Hence there are only finitely many actual quantum boundary-channel occurrences during the ENTIRE waiting interval. Leave their full quantum/classical/ancilla ports open, together with the initial fault and every native straddling prefix.

The exterior is the exact ordinary circuit on its valid cut. The bounded internal repair can be an arbitrary tensor on those ports; its small virtual-height change adds only a bounded number of event vertices. This is an open-network structure statement, not an automatic decoder theorem. The common full-amplitude decoder identity and signed norm bookkeeping remain required.

Physical storage time is a separate cost. DATA in the frozen region is not magically protected against bath noise while waiting for clock repair. Every such perturbation is charged over the actual finite window; after M and all rates are fixed, its incident norm budget scales with that window. A single clock root with no further physical faults does not acquire extra noise merely from idling.

## 5. Actual bounded-hole consumer and exact missing cases

The small-write fill, bounded-hop dirty-minimum flushing, real slow-fill timer and actual PAUSE/retire/live-ACK adapter are candidate physical consumers of the feasible target. No instantaneous clock reset, quantum rewind or global all-ready oracle is supplied.

This note does NOT prove:

* recovery of an arbitrary corrupted purge beacon/phase/collar protocol;
* a bound for multiple disconnected or overlapping counter roots;
* semantic fault-weight bounds for every native hardware fault;
* inclusive norm sparsity after the long-lived classical control process;
* a universal continuous QCA threshold.

The contribution is narrower and concrete: an explicit guard makes the isolated-root frozen boundary genuinely healthy, so the extension radius is independent of M, while bounded-lag causality controls the quantum frontier during its paid physical waiting time. The remaining cluster and hardware-root obligations cannot be replaced by calling every healthy native attempt computational progress.
