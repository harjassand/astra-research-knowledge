\[
\langle p(M+G)\rangle-p(M)
=c(f-E)+\frac12\sum_iw_i p''(M+A_i)[R_i,R_i].

The Aᵢ need not form a gradient; they are finite explicitly constructed evaluation backgrounds in this exact identity. No pointwise minimizing field or correction-selector oracle is assumed.
