Exact interface/range checks are an actual coordination operation, not a request for researchers to fill forms.

The better candidate policy is conditional:

* Preserve warm ownership when a construction is accumulating useful machinery and moving a consequential obstruction.
* Use a genuinely fresh ordinary constructor when independence can reveal a different construction, especially after a demonstrated method barrier. A compact scientific state is different from replaying an entire failed search history.
* Use the representation audit selectively to expose a specific contingent assumption. This study does not justify appending it to every prompt.
* Share an actual compatible construction or counterexample sparsely. New shared vocabulary, agent agreement and slot occupancy are not synthesis.
* Reassess the opportunity when complete success would still have limited reach, or serious distinct attacks leave the same central dependency untouched without new leverage. Preserve fundamental depth when a promising mechanism is genuinely developing; no immediate device demo is required.

These are provisional decisions, not a proven optimal policy. A local construction comparison does not test whether we select civilisation-level opportunities, whether a new physical theory is needed, or whether long-lived native Pro trajectories respond the same way. The scientific ambition remains fixed. There is no evidence that prompt/context tuning alone guarantees revolutionary discoveries on demand, and a correct restricted theorem is not relabelled 90+ to make a policy look successful.

## What this does and does not say about new theories

There is no evidence here that stronger axioms are the missing ingredient. A new representation or branch of mathematics can work within existing axioms. Changing axioms changes the conditional statement being proved; an unsolved question does not by itself demonstrate independence. These tests do not settle whether a particular historic problem needs a new framework, or whether a new physical theory could reconcile existing phenomena.

The right fixed boundary depends on the scientific task. For engineering with specified hardware, new mathematical language cannot supply unavailable operations. A proposed new architecture may introduce different hardware, whose construction then becomes part of the claim. For fundamental physics, the current model's postulates should be revisable; established observations, internal consistency and discriminating consequences constrain the replacement. For mathematics, preserve the claimed quantifiers and label any stronger axiomatic premise. Thus 'preserve the target' must not become 'preserve every assumption of the predecessor.' The present comparison freezes native operations to make a precise control question, and cannot test wholesale replacement of physical postulates.

The experiments also do not test civilisation-level opportunity selection. The tasks were unresolved selected frontiers, not representative scientific opportunities; a short successful theorem construction does not measure the rate of historic discovery. The initial quantum constructors developed a supplied conjectural foothold. Hidden compute was not hard matched, source completeness needed correction, and native Pro was not the experimental model. These limits prevent identifying a universal optimal policy or promising 90+ on every return. The meaningful aim is a more reliable programme that closes genuinely consequential problems; grade inflation or a new framework name is not progress toward that aim.
