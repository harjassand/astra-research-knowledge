# Finite physical controls for growing-selector active effect tomography

28 October 2026. Independent constructive controls note. No source sampling, publication, hidden-channel evaluation, or modification of received files. The object is a frozen receiver's actual ordinary Bernoulli test

`Pr(y=1 | x)=x^T A x`, `x in R^m`, `||x||=1`, `0<=A<=I`.

This is an unknown quadratic effect, not an arbitrary circuit-optimization objective. The decoder and the definition/distribution of its global test are frozen before this stage. Every query below prepares one actual selector and executes one actual test; there is no task-value or coefficient oracle.

## 1. Exact finite design and sampler

Choose a basis vector uniformly with probability `2/(m+2)`. Otherwise choose m independent fair signs and put `z=s/sqrt(m)`. The distribution has exactly

`E z_i z_j = delta_ij/m`,

`E z_i z_j z_k z_l = (delta_ij delta_kl + delta_ik delta_jl + delta_il delta_jk)/[m(m+2)]`.

The diagonal fourth moment is `3/[m(m+2)]`; the distinct squared-coordinate moment is `1/[m(m+2)]`; other even-fourth entries vanish. These follow directly from the two finite branches. Unsigned basis vectors give the required **even/projective fourth-moment design**, not literally all odd moments of a spherical 4-design. An additional fair global sign on the basis branch makes the latter terminology literal without changing a selector's physical pure state or any moment used here.

An exact sampler first draws a uniform integer from `{0,...,m+1}` and takes the basis branch on two designated labels. It then draws a uniform basis index if needed, or m fair signs. A uniform integer in a set of size r is obtained by drawing `ceil(log2 r)` bits and accepting values below r. At most R attempts are allowed. Each attempt accepts with probability at least 1/2, accepted outputs are exactly uniform, and exhaustion invokes the prescribed safe abort; it is not an unbounded hidden stopping rule.

For a deterministic maximum of T Bernoulli calls there are at most 2T such integer draws. Choosing

`R=ceil log2(2T/nu_rng)`

bounds total exhaustion probability by nu_rng. The draw with r=1 is deterministic and uses zero bits. A sufficient primitive random-bit cap is

`T*R*[ceil log2(m+2)+ceil log2 m] + mT + T`.

The final T term allows the optional global basis sign. Global Clifford-test randomness is additional and retains its separate received cap. The promised law is unconditional apart from the known pre-bank history; capped failures are charged, not conditioned away for free.

## 2. Canonical rational preconditioner: no inverse-root moment bias

Let the current stored B be exactly rational symmetric with certified

`tau I <= B <= 4I`, `0<tau<=1`.

Set `Q=I-B/4` and choose an integer K satisfying

`K+1 >= (4/tau)*ceil log2(32/tau)`.

Define the exact rational matrix

`C=(1/2) sum_(k=0)^K [binom(2k,k)/4^k] Q^k`.

For each eigenvalue of Q in `[0,1-tau/4]`, the positive binomial series yields `0<C<=B^(-1/2)`. Since each coefficient is at most one, its operator-norm remainder is at most

`(2/tau)(1-tau/4)^(K+1) <= (2/tau) exp[-tau(K+1)/4] <= 1/16`.

Also `B^(-1/2)>=I/2`, so `C>=(7/8)B^(-1/2)` and

`C^(-2)<=64B/49<2B`.

C commutes with B and `CBC<=I`. The strict positive floor here is generated by the learner's B, not a spectral assumption on the source or its gauge. No unknown operator is diagonalized or inverted.

Prepare the actual ideal-reference selector

`x=Cz/||Cz||`.

After its actual Bernoulli test gives y, record

`Y=y ||Cz||^2 [m(m+2)zz^T/2 - mI/2]`.

Writing `F=CAC`, the exact fourth-moment identity gives `E Y=F`. There is no approximation to an inverse-square-root-defined experiment and hence no moment-bias allowance from C. The original effect is reconstructed as `C^(-1) F C^(-1)`.

All recorded matrices are rational: for the sign branch, both `zz^T=ss^T/m` and `||Cz||^2=||Cs||^2/m` are rational. Preparing the normalized vector uses C times a rational basis/sign vector; the factor sqrt(m) cancels. The output y is the observed bit, not a probability substituted by the learner.

## 3. Explicit first-order source rate with a finite rational estimator

On the statistical good-history invariant `A<=B`, one has `F<=CBC<=I`. Let

`L_m=m^3(m+3)/4`.

The bracketed kernel has squared Frobenius norm L_m for every z. Since `||Cz||^2<=1/tau`,

`E||Y-F||_F^2 <= E||Y||_F^2 <= L_m/tau`.

This uses the actual importance-weighted Bernoulli sample and is not a variance-one binary surrogate for a spectrum-valued observation.

Here is a concrete conservative amplification implementation, independent of any numerical geometric-median solver. Suppose there are S fixed stages and desired per-stage operator error `c/4`, `0<c<=1/8`. Use b odd, at least `ceil log2(2S/beta_tom)`, independent blocks, each of size

`N(tau)=ceil[9216 L_m/(tau c^2)]`.

A block mean has Frobenius error exceeding c/12 with probability at most 1/64 by its exact second-moment bound and Markov's inequality. Choose the first block mean having a strict majority of the b block means within Frobenius distance c/6, including itself. Compare squared rational distances exactly. If no such mean exists, safely abort.

When a strict majority of blocks have error at most c/12, such a candidate exists, and every accepted candidate is within c/4 of F. The probability that at least half the independent blocks are bad is at most `(1/4)^b`. Union over stages therefore fits within beta_tom. Freshness is conditional on the preceding history; within a stage B and C are held fixed. No inter-block adaptive whitening is inserted.

For `tau_k=2^(-k)`, `k=0,...,S-1`, a concrete raw-test cap is

`T=b sum_k N(tau_k) <= b[S + 18432 L_m/(c^2 tau_min)]`.

This is a conservative `1/tau_min` source rate; it is not an optimal dependence on gamma. T counts actual Bernoulli queries. A final payload, earlier decoder acquisition and any other qualification stage are additional. A tighter independently proved amplification constant can replace 9216 without affecting this controls construction.

## 4. Rational reconstruction and safe update, instead of unproved clipping

With an estimate `Fhat` satisfying `||Fhat-F||<=c/4`, set the exact rational

`Ahat=C^(-1) Fhat C^(-1)`.

The preceding comparison gives

`-(c/2)B <= Ahat-A <= (c/2)B`.

Round the entire candidate `Ahat+cB+tau_next I` symmetrically to a common dyadic entry grid with operator residual at most `c tau/4`. Since `tau I<=B`, the total deviation from `A+cB+tau_next I` is at most `3cB/4` in Loewner order. Therefore on good histories

`A+(c/4)B+tau_next I <= B_next <= A+(7c/4)B+tau_next I`.

If `tau_next<=1`, `A<=I`, `B<=4I`, and `c<=1/8`, this lies in `[tau_next I,4I]` with a strict upper reserve (`1+7c+tau_next<4`). Exact PSD tests accept the rounded matrix on every such history.

The implementation tests `B_next-tau_next I>=0` and `4I-B_next>=0` using exact rational elimination. A positive diagonal pivot reduces PSD to its Schur complement. A zero diagonal with a nonzero row is a rejection witness; an all-zero residual is PSD. Negative diagonals are rejected. This is polynomial-time exact rational arithmetic with a replayable certificate, not a floating eigenvalue sign assumption. If the tests fail, the protocol terminates with its prescribed physical replacement/default encoder, or uses a separately specified feasible B=I fallback while retaining the paid failure event. It does not resample until favorable.

Spectral clipping must not be assumed to preserve arbitrary noncommuting Loewner comparisons. The certificate/abort version avoids that issue. On bad estimation histories the output remains a physical experiment or physical replacement, even if Ahat is indefinite. Ahat itself is an empirical proposal, not a claimed physical PSD effect.

## 5. Concrete bit-length and arithmetic bounds

Choose a dyadic common precision

`b_grid >= ceil log2[2m/(c tau_min)]`.

Nearest symmetric entry rounding then has operator error at most `m*2^(-b_grid)/2 <= c tau_min/4`, sufficient at every stage. Accepted B entries have O(b_grid+1) bits since `||B||<=4`. Rounding after each update prevents uncontrolled bit-length accumulation across stages.

Let K_max be the maximum series degree above. As Q has dyadic denominator `2^(b_grid+2)`, C has a common dyadic denominator dividing

`2^[1+K_max(b_grid+4)]`.

Put `d_C=1+K_max(b_grid+4)`. Its entries are at most `1/sqrt(tau_min)` in magnitude. Integer determinant/cofactor bounds imply that exact C inverses need only

`O(m[d_C+log(m/tau_min)])`

bits per entry, apart from supplied rational-input bit lengths. Within a bank, all importance-weighted matrices share a known denominator; a block mean adds only `O(log N+log m)` bits. Exact reconstruction and the rational majority-distance comparisons therefore have polynomial bit complexity in m, 1/tau_min, log T, log(1/c), and the input bit lengths.

A naive implementation uses `O(K_max m^3)` rational arithmetic operations to form C, `O(m^3)` for its inverse, and `O(m^2)` per query for vector/weight accumulation, plus the polynomial block-selection and PSD-certificate work. These conservative classical costs are real resources. They are not polylogarithmic in m and are not absorbed into source-call complexity.

## 6. Actual selector states and whole-process gate error

The selector occupies `ell=ceil log2 m` qubits (or a specified m-level register), with unused basis states outside its m-dimensional subspace. Preparing an arbitrary known vector in this subspace is not free merely because ell is logarithmic. A direct constructive route uses at most m-1 known two-level real rotations. Gray-code reductions and reversible control tests implement them with `O(m*poly(log m))` elementary controls and `O(log m)` paid workspace. All inverse/uncomputation work and workspace resets count. No QRAM or amplitude-loading oracle is assumed.

The normalized basis/sign vector C z is known rational up to an ordinary scalar normalization. Its norm is at least 1/2 for unit z, so normalized state approximation is stable. Known rotation parameters can be obtained by finite certified arithmetic. Zero branches are handled exactly or discarded only within the explicit norm-error allowance. Gate approximations must be budgeted across all T preparations, the final selected encoder, the frozen decoder uses, and the global Clifford input/readout circuits.

Allocate a complete operational discrepancy `nu_ctrl` to the entire compiled process, for example by making each of at most G physical preparation/measurement/control gates accurate enough that their telescoping sum is at most nu_ctrl. Per-gate precision is at worst inverse-polynomial in the declared finite G and nu_ctrl, with finite classical bit precision `O(log(G/nu_ctrl))` plus known arithmetic conditioning. Adaptive histories are compared as a complete process, not by pretending the finite compiled selectors have exactly the ideal moment law. The rational C experiment is the exact reference; universal-gate implementation error is paid separately.

The actual global test also consumes its known q-logical input preparation, source call, frozen receiver, inverse Clifford and q readouts. Those counts are multiplied by T, with selector-input traffic `ell*T`, logical-input traffic `q*T`, and physical source-output exposure `n*T` when composed with the joint-recovery source. Full setting/outcome records or exactly sufficient statistics are ordinary classical data; they do not reveal an environment or supply effect entries.

## 7. Failure ledger and final known-matrix selection

- `beta_tom`: statistical stage failures, union-bounded using the first-failed-invariant argument. The variance assumption `A<=B` is used only before the first statistical failure.
- `nu_rng`: capped exact-integer sampler exhaustion, with all attempts/calls charged and a prescribed physical fallback.
- Deterministic arithmetic and PSD certificates: no invented numerical failure probability. The chosen finite bounds guarantee valid output or the safe branch on every normalized input history.
- `nu_ctrl`: one linked operational discrepancy for all finite actual gates, state preparations, random Clifford compilation, adaptive branches and payload.

Loss of the final physical service is bounded by one, so exceptional histories contribute their allocated probabilities, not the potentially large importance-weighted matrix norm. No good-event conditioning refunds source calls. The outer iterative invariant, stopping rule, final Rayleigh tolerance and relative-risk comparison must be stated by the governing algorithm; this note supplies its concrete measurement/finite-control implementation.

The final known symmetric rational proposal can be minimized to a certified Rayleigh tolerance by a known-matrix backward diagonalization. An eigenvalue gap is unnecessary: an exactly referenced orthogonal frame with operator residual delta and its least diagonal label gives a Rayleigh witness within 2delta. This is conventional finite linear algebra on acquired data, not a task-value oracle or generic optimization over an unknown circuit family. Its selected normalized vector also pays state-preparation/compilation cost before holdout or payload.

## 8. End-to-end anchor factors must change with m

If the original encoder has m real coordinates, the uniform anchor state uses m coordinate inputs. For every gauge test vector the positive quadratic form obeys `x^T L x<=Tr L`; therefore the operator-valued source satisfies

`G(x)<=m Gbar`.

Every previous four-anchor transfer factor 4 must become m, and all anchor preparation/acquisition controls must use the new dimension. The factor is not an extra free dimension-independent source property.

A perfect encoder does not alone imply a positive uniform operator calibration margin when gauge states vary. For m=2, take a perfect branch on gauge `|0>` and a completely depolarizing branch on orthogonal gauge `|1>`. Then `Hbar=|0><0|/2`, while `Gbar=I/2`; no positive kappa satisfies `Hbar>=kappa Gbar`. The scalar nonnegative-quadratic trace argument needs its additional common-gauge/scalar-factor assumptions. In the general mixed-gauge source, kappa must remain separately supplied or proved by the literal family.

The strongest equally informed conventional learner has this same effect-tomography procedure. Growing selector dimension is a substantive but structured quadratic extension, not a claim about arbitrary nonlinear encoders or scalable arbitrary-state loading at logarithmic cost.
