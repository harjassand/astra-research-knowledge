## Bounded receiving operation

Run from a fresh checkout containing this change, with Python standard library:

```sh
python3 -I research/2026-10-21-n33-source-recovery/receiving_check.py --output-dir /absolute/path/new-receipt
```

The operation follows the source map, checks the restored original and fetches
the exact public PR #112 proof/code by revision and hash. It uses only the
unchanged owner's `soft` function, not the checker's already-completed default
1200/10000 grids. It challenges the scalar generator at volume 2400 and applies
the source's Section 5 bounds to volume 7500 at failure budget `1e-6`.

The use question is whether the same resource point certifies capture by time 8
and residence for `exp(V/1024)` after that deadline. The capture bound is below
the budget, but the longer-residence bound is above it. This is insufficiency of
the supplied certificate, not evidence that actual residence fails. An all-off
rate counterexample shows why removing the positive-rate hypothesis is illegal.
All times are nondimensional. Floating evaluations are numerical, not rigorous
interval enclosures; the finite generator challenge is not the general proof.

This demonstrates source retrieval followed by an actual scoped mathematical/code
application, including rejection of an unsupported stronger transfer. It does not
demonstrate a new theorem, an owner research advance, reduced research cost or an
effective higher-dimensional stochastic certificate. The scientific owner's next
near-active/stopping/effective-construction obligations remain theirs.
