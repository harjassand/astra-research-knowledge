Conditional on the complete mixture-selector sequence, the reference visible
record is iid: its first swap value is the independent fair pre-swap hidden
bit, and later swap positions receive fresh reference signs. Thus the reference
record and its stopping time are independent of the selectors. Actual and
reference histories agree until a second swap. If E tau is finite, the chance
of two swaps before the reference stop is o(delta), by domination with tau.
No second moment or analytic unbounded stopped likelihood is assumed.

On an exactly-one-swap path, the deposited visible sign has terminal hidden
mean lambda^(tau-s)Y_(s-1). Zero-swap paths have conditional hidden mean zero.
The resulting absolutely summable response coefficients are independent of
lambda at the iid-visible source. Exactness throughout the actual lambda
interval makes them vanish; optional sampling makes their sum -a. The
contradiction applies to arbitrary randomized causal stopping, including an
in-protocol calibration prefix. External parameter-dependent advice changes
the interface. Positive-delta stopping only needs to be almost surely finite.

The family has all entries at least 3/32. It is a uniformly mixing partial-
observation/universality obstruction, not a small-gap or sector example.
Since P Y=delta H, the completion-label mean and one later native-label mean
already state the failed service without demanding a hidden measurement.

The independent run passed 16 kernel fixtures, all 676 deterministic visible

The arbitrary-time theorem is analytic; finite enumeration does not prove it.
The ordinary first-target-label absorbing law is correct. The N=2 compiler's

## Reversible scenery bound: accepted at its distinct interface

Read the complete proof at SHA256
`c4f5ab7d84a9fd3af60ee71cdff82be0ffe5fdebe2f980c958c64e6507977908`.
Finite expected stopping gives an integrable pre-stop occupation density.
Pairing its Poisson equation with each independent scenery bit yields a bounded
discrete harmonic sequence except for a nonzero point source. Bounded affine
half-lines and their adjoining equations contradict that point source.

For finite precision, stationary transport across either boundary of an
n-block has expected mass E min(n,|J|). The native random-walk increments remain
martingale differences even with the entire initial scenery known. Stopped
L2 isometry uses only E tau finite and gives E|J|<=sqrt(E tau). These facts and
iid block fluctuations prove the finite-n inequality and epsilon^(-2) lower
order. No matching upper bound is accepted.

This infinite scenery source is ergodic under its stationary law, with
zero-mass conserved translation orbits. Harris irreducibility is not claimed.
Commanded displacement, reset, stored-state output or extra actuation changes
the actual operation menu and can escape the result.

across its preserved label pair. The actual kernel therefore preserves that

and finite-rate gamma q_j(Y) mode selection. Hence the physical-time marginal
has the target law in this supplied joint generator model. The selector,
compatible native semigroups and preserving mode are actual premises; a

two 64-state joint-generator checks and coded-family selector identities.
