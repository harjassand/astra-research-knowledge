# Support-dimension clarification (receiving review)

In GOVERNING_v1.md section1, the sentence about occupied support dimension below8 means: **if all eight labels are supported in one common SINGLE-LABEL subspace of dimension less than8, the exterior success probability is zero.**

It does not refer to the rank or dimension of the joint eight-register density operator's support. A pure antisymmetric eight-label state has joint rank1 and can succeed with probability1. The governing proof uses the common single-label subspace and remains unchanged. Arbitrary correlations among records and with source memory remain allowed.
