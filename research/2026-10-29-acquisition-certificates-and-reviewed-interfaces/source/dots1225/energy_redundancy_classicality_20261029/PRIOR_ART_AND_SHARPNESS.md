# Prior-art boundary and next load-bearing obligation
2026-10-29. Mandatory companion to the initial construction.

After the initial freeze, primary Knott–Tufarelli–Piani–Adesso, Generic emergence of objectivity of observables in infinite dimensions, PRL121160401 (2018), arXiv:1802.05719, Theorem1 and its scope were read. It proves energy-constrained diamond approximation of most fragments by measure-and-prepare channels with a common POVM, at an explicit power rate in the number of fragments. For a single oscillator with finite fixed mean energy its displayed rate is N^(-1/17). Applying that result to the symmetric binary tree already gives an O(b log(1/b)) approximate-broadcasting-to-classicality implication at fixed energy: choose tree depth proportional to log(1/b). Thus the order stated in the initial packet is NOT evidence of a new foundational rate. The finite spectral-cutoff/channel-de-Finetti derivation remains valid and explicit, but historical novelty is unestablished and substantial prior coverage is now identified. The stronger scope of the primary result (arbitrary fragments and energy-constrained reference-assisted channel comparison) must not be conflated with our promise-family reconstruction metric.

The real unresolved quantitative question is the minimum physical resource needed for persistent nonclassicality at small binary broadcast error. The present sufficient inequality requires oscillator energy at least exp(Omega(1/b)) for fixed reconstruction gap. Astra's N61 dyadic copy ladder gives an explicit far more expensive mechanism. For L levels, m=2^(L-1), d=Lm, its Hilbert dimension is D=sum_j binom(d+2^j-1,2^j), with ln D=Theta(2^L log L) from the dominant top binomial. Embedding it into the first D number states of a single oscillator uses energy at most D-1 and retains b<=1/L and e bounded away from zero. This is only a double-exponential-energy UPPER construction, not a lower bound on all possible embeddings or all mechanisms. The exponential versus double-exponential gap is not closed.

A related open information-loss interface remains in current N371/N553: the reservoir counterexample's actual-prior Holevo loss diverges, and therefore does not refute a dimension-free small-loss implication. Any transfer to that question must read its governing proof and must distinguish worst-case state-family reconstruction from prior-average loss; rare-parameter states immediately obstruct unsupported changes of quantifier.
