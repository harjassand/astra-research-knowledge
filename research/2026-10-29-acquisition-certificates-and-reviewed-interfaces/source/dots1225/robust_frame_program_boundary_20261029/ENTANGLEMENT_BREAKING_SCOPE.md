# Irreversible source versus recovered frame operation

The physical collective twirl source in PHYSICAL_TWIRL_LABEL_DRAFT is entanglement-breaking: it measures the two-outcome POVM {P_s,P_t} and prepares P_s or P_t/3. Local conjugation preserves this property. Its convex mixture with the completely depolarizing channel is also entanglement-breaking. Thus its ordinary unassisted quantum capacity is zero.

Acquiring a frame-dependent program and using that program to act on a later protected payload does not transmit a qubit through this source. Coherent known controls, protected labels, program storage and a separate protected payload interface are supplied. They constitute a bypass for communication/storage and must not be omitted from the resource comparison. The nontrivial intended operation is learning and executing a gate associated with the source's unknown internal frame, without an oracle for that gate or environment tomography.

At exact rank-deficient support the accepted program construction may herald this operation exactly. At any positive depolarizing admixture, the full-rank source boundary rules out finite exact distinct-frame gates; the approximate acquisition/purification/processor construction is the appropriate continuation. A classical estimate of the small9-dimensional Gram matrix also avoids complete environment reconstruction and is a mandatory comparator. Neither zero source capacity nor successful frame extraction establishes quantum communication through a zero-capacity channel, computational speedup, or practical superiority.
