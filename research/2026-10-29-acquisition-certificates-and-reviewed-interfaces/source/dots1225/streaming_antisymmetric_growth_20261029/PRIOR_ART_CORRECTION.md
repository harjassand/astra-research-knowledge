# Priority correction: streaming growth is prior work
2026-10-29. Additive correction; frozen governing mathematics is unchanged.

Watts, Bostanci, Caro, Grier, Liu and Mahajan, *Anti-symmetrization is cheap and useful*, arXiv:2610.06737v1 (5 October 2026), Algorithm 2, Theorem 5 and equation (35), already give the fresh-copy antisymmetric growth algorithm, discard-on-failure strategy, and the same minimum-eigenvalue growth bound used here. Algorithm 1 supplies an efficient controlled-SWAP implementation. These are direct predecessors, not merely related work. No novelty is claimed for that mechanism, hazard inequality or generic streaming sample complexity.

The Astra packet specializes this machinery to its retained-memory commutator-record interface, exact frame-program conversion, and explicit finite ordinary-source-call accounting. Whether these transfers have historical novelty is unestablished. The earlier final paragraph of GOVERNING_v1.md must be read with this correction; independent derivation does not establish priority.
