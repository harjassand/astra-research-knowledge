# Primary-source freshness and scope check
Read 2026-10-29.

- Nielsen–Chuang, Programmable quantum gate arrays, https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/9703032 . The deterministic orthogonal-program theorem and later probabilistic Choi-program teleportation have different scopes. The latter explicitly allows mixed programs for nonunitary operations. The same-support argument here is not being presented as a new general programming principle.
- Pavličko–Ziman, Robustness of optimal probabilistic storage and retrieval of unitary channels to noise, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.07079 and https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.106.052416 . The study concerns noisy storage/retrieval of phase gates and contrasts depolarization with dephasing; its apparatus is more specialized than the general elementary CP-order argument.
- IMPORTANT correction: https://journals.aps.org/pra/pdf/10.1103/PhysRevA.111.049902 , published21 April2025. The erratum corrects calculations in sectionVI B1 following equation23, including two-use and general N-use retrieval expressions. It says the conclusions are unaffected. Do not quote the old implementation formulas as current without the correction. The no-go/support review and the elementary one-query teleportation witness do not rely on those corrected formulas.

No literature completeness or historical novelty determination is claimed.
