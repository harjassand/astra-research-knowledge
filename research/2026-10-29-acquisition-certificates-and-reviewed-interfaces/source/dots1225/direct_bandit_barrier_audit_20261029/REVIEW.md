## Reviewed claims and independent derivations

1. **Physical mean.** For the candidate swap involution S, the uniform four-state Bell ensemble, tensored with maximally mixed spectators, satisfies E[s rho]=S/D. A final S measurement gives E[st]=Tr(S N(S))/D; hence the flip probability is delta/2. This uses trace preservation, not unitality. With memory, the fresh prepared carrier is independent of the actual conditional memory marginal; the resulting reduced CPTP map has the same identity. This is a measurement identity, not a tomography or recovery theorem.

2. **Rational implementation.** With c_i>=0 and min c=0, f(nu)=sum 1/(c_i+nu) is decreasing, f(1)>=1 and f(M)<=1. Bisection therefore brackets the unique root (the M=1 endpoint is harmless). The derivative magnitude is at most one on [1,M]. For bracket width <=1/D, floor(D lower_i)+2 lies between D p_i and D p_i+2. Consequently D<=W<=D+2M, q_i>=D p_i/(D+2M), and q_i>=2/(D+2M). The domination implies TV<=2M/(D+2M)<=1/(4T), and p_i/q_i<=5/4. The rational program implements these formulas rather than sampling an algebraic ideal point.

3. **Stability and feedback.** Write p_i=1/(eta L_i+lambda) without the irrelevant minimum shift. Along L+s g e_j, differentiation of sum p_i=1 gives lambda'=-eta g p_j^2/sum p_i^2. Thus -p_j'=eta g p_j^2(1-p_j^2/sum p_i^2), nonnegative and at most eta g p_j(0)^2. Integration gives the stated coordinate stability for arbitrary nonnegative update magnitude; a small-step assumption is unnecessary. Bernoulli Y is essential: E[Y^2]=ell. Observation probability rho q_j and update Y/(rho q_j) give E[g_i]=ell_i and expected stability <=(eta/rho)sum p_i^2 ell_i/q_i <=25/64 sum q_i ell_i for eta=rho/4. Missing the edge factor rho would invalidate the result; it is present.

4. **Regret.** The minimum-potential increments bracket g.p before/after the update, giving the stated FTRL inequality. The smoothed fixed comparator has barrier difference <=(M-1)log T and cumulative loss at most L_j+1; action mismatch contributes at most 1/4. Hence (39/64)L_alg<=L_j+5/4+4(M-1)log T/rho. Since 64/39<2 and 80/39<3, the advertised conservative bound is valid, including T=1 and M=1. Comparator selection is fixed independently of fresh randomness, not hindsight selection over random adaptive histories.

5. **Coverage and completeness.** A unit quaternion can be signed to q0>=0; stereographic coordinates q_vec/(1+q0) lie in the unit ball within [-1,1]^3. Grid rounding has error <=sqrt(3)/(2r); inverse stereographic parametrization is 2-Lipschitz and quaternion-to-rotation Frobenius distance is at most 2sqrt(2) times quaternion distance. This yields the advertised covering radius 2sqrt(6)/r and (2r+1)^3 arms. The cited conservative quadratic defect constant 9/2 is sufficient: for any CPTP map, delta(S)=sum_a ||[K_a,S]||_HS^2/(2D). Under exact common symmetry the comparison involution commutes with all Kraus operators; bounding the remaining commutator by the operator norm of S-S* already gives a conservative quadratic bound. For two-site swaps S_C-S_C*=sum DeltaC_ab sigma_a tensor sigma_b/2, the full-carrier operator norm is bounded by its two-site Frobenius norm ||DeltaC||_F, giving delta<=2||DeltaC||_F^2, stronger than the used 9/2. Thus the stated 108/r^2, 216T/r^2 and fixed-port exponent 2/5 follow. The approximate-Gamma expression follows from the same commutator triangle inequality and Cauchy-Schwarz across edges/time/expectation. It is a sufficient symmetry-loss comparison, not fidelity optimality.

## Causality, stops, and costs are load-bearing restrictions

The current source map must be fixed before fresh arms and role/edge/Bell randomness. A quantum source may react through that fixed interaction to the physical input; it may not use an unauthorized classical leak to select a different current map. Arbitrary past quantum memory is allowed. Loss/comparator vectors are evaluated on the actual learner-induced conditional history, not counterfactual policy trajectories. This review does not cover the separate public-candidate extension.

For integer weighted rejection sampling, P(index=i, first success=a)=(1-W/2^b)^(a-1) w_i/2^b. Conditioning on success (or its attempt count) retains q_i. A cap therefore preserves the arm law if all draws use independent randomness and ranges determined by the past. The prospective role/edge/Bell draws must be preflighted neutrally; abort before the source call with zero subsequent losses. Their ranges cannot covertly depend on a freshly selected arm or role. The union bound controls availability, not a promised amount of useful service. Neither the primitive nor its tests implement a complete privacy-preserving fixed-clock scheduler.

Cost includes M stored arms per edge, exact rational arithmetic of growing bit length, all edge draws, the inverse observation factor rho=p_w/(n-1), candidate rotations, Bell preparation/readout, routing, spectator mixing, fresh private randomness, and trusted control/calibration. One source call observes only one star edge. Exact classical rational arithmetic does not establish exact physical rotations or feasible hardware clock/runtime. Preparation/readout error is not silently absorbed in the ideal completeness claim.
