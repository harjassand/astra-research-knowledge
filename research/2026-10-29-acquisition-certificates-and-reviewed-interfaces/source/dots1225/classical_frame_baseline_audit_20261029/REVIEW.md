## Mathematical checks
1. All 45 real matrix-entry observables have estimator outcome range length one: diagonal projectors take 0/1; off-diagonal observable Aij/2 takes -1/2,0,1/2. Independent m samples per setting give two-sided failure at most 2exp(-2me²); a union bound gives 90exp(-2me²). Independent fresh records and the promised real state are essential.
2. A symmetric entry error bounded by e gives operator error at most maximum absolute row sum 9e. For sigma=alpha I-g cc^T, let v be the estimated bottom eigenvector and lambda its eigenvalue. Projecting the perturbed eigenvalue equation gives (alpha-lambda)P_perp v=-P_perp Delta v. Weyl gives alpha-lambda>=g-E, proving sin(angle)<=E/(g-E). E<g/2 also ensures the estimated bottom eigenvalue is simple.
3. Choose the mathematical sign with c^T v>=0. Then ||sqrt(3)v-vec(R)||<=sqrt(6)sin(angle). Under the chosen epsilon<=1/2, this is at most sqrt(2)epsilon<1. Reshaping therefore preserves determinant sign relative to R; the algorithm's determinant-positive sign equals this mathematical choice. Nearest SO(3) projection doubles the Frobenius error by triangle inequality. For two qubit unitary channels whose Bloch rotations differ by angle theta in [0,pi], half-diamond distance is sin(theta/2)=||Rhat-R||_F/(2sqrt(2)). Consequently the asserted bound 2sqrt(3)E/g is correct.
4. e=g_L epsilon/(18sqrt(3)) gives E<=g epsilon/(2sqrt(3)), and m>=486 log(90/delta)/(g_L² epsilon²). Multiplication by 45 settings is correct. g_L must be valid, not inferred without its own confidence cost. IID normalized accepted records and q=(8+eta)/18 give the expected source waiting cost. Actual finite-cap failures must be separately charged.
5. One calibration reused K times has good-event adaptive hybrid error <=K epsilon. Since the same bad calibration event is shared, the unconditional averaged channel has half-diamond bound <=min(1,K epsilon+delta), not a mandatory K delta penalty. Any uncertainty in the contamination bound, control synthesis, source waiting cap and storage adds its own applicable error/abort budget. This statement concerns a fixed underlying frame and trusted controls, not arbitrarily drifting future frames.

## Reproducible diagnostics
check_baseline.py performs 300 synthetic IID measurements using exact multinomial sampling of the specified three-outcome observables, and 1,000 independently generated symmetric perturbation tests. It constructs random rotations, estimates the lowest eigenvector, fixes determinant sign and applies the explicit SO(3) SVD projection. All numerical tests pass. eta=.2, epsilon=.2, delta=.01 yields 11,622,593 records per setting and 523,016,685 total records under the conservative analytic bound. Maximum sampled gate error is .0017369248605; no failures among the 300 trials. This is not a physical acquisition experiment, a confidence-interval certificate for the floating arithmetic, or a claim that this sample budget is necessary. The elementary bound is substantially conservative.

## Consequence and remaining scope
The comparator avoids environment reconstruction just as the quantum-program route does. It produces a reusable classical frame and, for fixed source gap, has a sufficient O(K² epsilon_total^-2 log(1/delta)) one-time calibration cost. Quantum consumptive-port results cannot be called superior merely for avoiding full tomography. Exact finite-query kernel programs at eta=0 remain a different capability; finite noisy classical data do not generically determine a continuous frame exactly. No practical advantage, optimal rate or noisy-generic-source readiness is accepted here.
