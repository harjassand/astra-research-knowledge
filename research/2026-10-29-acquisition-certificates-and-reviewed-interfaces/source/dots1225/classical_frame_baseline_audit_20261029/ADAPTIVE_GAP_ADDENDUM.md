# Optional gap-adaptive comparator: no externally supplied eta upper bound
2026-10-29. Independent algebraic extension by receiving reviewer; not part of root's original reviewed snapshot. Assumes the same fixed IID family sigma=alpha I-g cc^T, g>0, and trusted measurements. It does not test whether the family promise itself is true.

At stage l>=1 use m_l=2^l independent samples per setting (fresh stage banks suffice). Set delta_l=delta/[l(l+1)], e_l=sqrt(log(90/delta_l)/(2m_l)), E_l=9e_l. Let Delta_hat_l be the difference between the two smallest eigenvalues of the observed symmetric matrix. Define g_lower_l=max(0,Delta_hat_l-2E_l).

With probability at least 1-delta, every stage's operator error is <=E_l simultaneously. This follows from the same 45-setting Hoeffding argument and sum_l delta_l=delta. On that event Weyl gives Delta_hat_l<=g+2E_l, hence g_lower_l<=g. Whenever g_lower_l>0 and 2sqrt(3) E_l/g_lower_l<=epsilon<=1/2, the original sign/projection proof applies and yields gate half-diamond error <=epsilon. Therefore the data-selected stopping stage is valid without an independent confidence estimate of eta. The complete returned classical frame still has averaged channel error <=epsilon+delta; for K uses, <=Kepsilon+delta.

For g>0 on the simultaneous good event, Delta_hat_l>=g-2E_l and g_lower_l>=g-4E_l. Since E_l tends to zero, stopping eventually occurs. For instance E_l<=g epsilon/(2sqrt(3)+4epsilon) suffices. The total fresh-stage sample count up to a stopping stage is less than twice its last-stage count. This gives the usual additional iterated-logarithmic confidence overhead relative to the fixed known-gap bound. No finite uniform stopping bound holds as g approaches zero. A public finite call cap gives abort when the criterion has not yet passed; it cannot be replaced by assumed successful readiness.

Numerically computing a certified lower eigenvalue gap would require interval/eigenvalue residual allowances in a rigorous finite-arithmetic implementation; a floating eigengap alone is a diagnostic, not a machine-certified lower bound. The algebraic theorem treats exact arithmetic or rigorously enclosed eigenvalues. Source waiting, controls, storage, and frame drift restrictions remain unchanged.
