# Actual live-bank diagnostic: optimization success is not statistical certification

The input is the author's actual 120-call three-role simulated trajectory, not an offline query mixture. It has 38 product probes, 59 matching validations and 23 service calls, retained coherent bath evolution, and fixed-work rational candidate certificates. Neither a verified outer code nor unknown payload was deployed.

`evaluate_live.py` independently reconstructs the empirical score exactly from the final 35-coefficient banks and retrospective rational quaternions, checks each reported gap sum against the retained per-query raw gaps, and evaluates the accepted retrospective high-probability formula. Public gap cap 340050 is used (not the slightly smaller analytical cap 337890), consistently with conservative dyadic reporting.

At individual failure allowances 2^-20, the score is -1000.033, but the selected-loss upper bound is about 1,934,951, larger than the trivial maximum 3T=360. Width 60,637.5 and observed stability 45,393.7 already overwhelm the empirical benefit; predictable-gap concentration dominates further. Small total reported optimization gap (~0.0773) is not a substitute for an expected/predictable budget.

A useful obstruction within THIS bound: for any R>0, mdR/4+Vobs/(4R) >= sqrt(md Vobs)/2 > 104000, with md=105 and Vobs=419437440. Therefore even deleting every other nonnegative confidence term and tuning R after this fixed bank cannot make this formula certify a nontrivial selected-loss bound. This is a formula-level diagnostic, not permission to choose R retrospectively and reuse its theorem, nor a claim about a different trajectory run with different R. The short-horizon bottleneck is broader than solver precision.

The two-role acquisition-only protocol is separately accepted, but its changed service rule was NOT executed here. This script does not feed three-role records into its acceptance evaluator. Source propagation is floating simulation; rational arithmetic checks only the classical supplied counters. No practical advantage, acceptance probability, physical clock or hardware claim is established.
