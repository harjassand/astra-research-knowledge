# Observable fixed-clock gap certificates — 2026-10-29

New high-probability proof, not an observed-sum substitution into the accepted expectation theorem. `GOVERNING_v1.md` constructs: (i) a nonnegative-gap supermartingale, (ii) a true-action/feedback regret martingale, (iii) a validation-count martingale, and (iv) an observable joint false-readiness event. Independent receiving review is pending.

The conclusion is **Pr[reject AND diagnostic passes]** bounded by declared failure allowances under the specified fixed-comparator/pathwise-defect promise. It is NOT Pr[reject | diagnostic passes], a new conditional accepted-error bound, or an unconditional high-yield claim. Unknown arbitrary noise may make the threshold fail. Actual source memory/outer correlations are retained through the reviewed orbit interface. Current neutral preflight count events are explicitly separated from candidate/role indices; candidate-dependent aborts are not silently treated as harmless.

`check_martingales.py/.json` checks exact adaptive-history centering, potential steps, bias bounds, a nontrivial adaptive observed-gap tail and validation martingale range. These are scalar finite proof diagnostics, not a quantum experiment.

`exact_certificate.py` evaluates the conservative inequality with rational arithmetic and integer upper square roots. It requires genuine observed solver-gap/feedback counters supplied by a caller; it does not acquire them. Its JSON uses SYNTHETIC scaling inputs only. With current worst-case constants even zero observed solver gap does not pass at small scales; the shown fixed-port synthetic sequence first passes at h120. This exposes a serious conservatism/practical-sample-cost limitation, rather than claiming an implemented useful certificate.

The solver, physical fixed clock, current-control secrecy, quantum buffers, endpoint gates and actual online integration are separately required. All earlier accepted sources remain unchanged. A possible later empirical-comparator extension must receive its own proof; it is not included in this version.
