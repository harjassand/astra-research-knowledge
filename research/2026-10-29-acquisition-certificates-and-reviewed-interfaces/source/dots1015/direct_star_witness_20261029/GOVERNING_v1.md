# Direct bounded star-Dirichlet witness (candidate-dependent acquisition)

This is an additive interface construction, not an improvement to the already executed product-probe trajectory. It is a conventional signed input/output correlation experiment tailored to the existing star-loss certificate. No novelty or unique quantum advantage is claimed.

Let S be the candidate twisted two-site swap, tensored with identity on other carrier sites, D the full carrier dimension. Let N be any current CPTP carrier map (or the carrier reduction of the current joint carrier/memory interaction with its actual conditional memory marginal). The existing objective is delta(S)=1−Tr[S N(S)]/D.

Prepare one of the four Bell states in the candidate's two-site local frame, uniformly at random. Its S eigenvalue s is −1 for the singlet and +1 for each triplet. Prepare the remaining n−2 carriers maximally mixed, by private random Pauli eigenstates or another paid implementation. Measure S after the source and record its eigenvalue t. The signed prepared ensemble satisfies E[s rho_in]=S/D. Therefore E[s t]=Tr[S N(S)]/D and X=1−s t has expectation delta(S), with X in {0,2}. Neither unitality nor a microscopic noise model is required.

For m=n−1 stars, independently sample an edge uniformly before its preparation; m X is an unbiased estimator of the current sum of star losses, with range [0,2m]. Conditional on the actual past and currently chosen candidate, the same identity holds for causal retained source memory. The input is freshly prepared and independent of that memory. Arbitrary existing outer-reference correlations are relevant to service transfer, not replaced by assuming fresh memory here. Existing all-input CP-effect transfer must still supply the service-error implication; this scalar witness alone is not that proof.

This probe is candidate-dependent and does NOT return the full-information 35-coefficient bank for alternative candidates. A separate probe role, or a justified mixture of learning probes and these witness probes, is needed if full-information learning is to continue. Its range advantage over the product-probe estimator is paid for by candidate-frame rotations, Bell preparation, Bell/singlet-triplet readout, pair routing, classical randomization, and private role choice. One cannot retroactively infer these measurements from old product-probe data. The source must not adapt to the current role/preparation randomness through an unauthorized classical side channel; the physical quantum input itself remains part of the same preselected interaction map.

The source need not share a hidden exact algebra for unbiasedness. Guaranteeing a useful rate or showing the witnessed loss becomes small remains separate. At a fixed source port, direct full-path logical testing can be a stronger conventional baseline; this star witness is useful only where the existing low-order-to-service transfer makes it economical.
