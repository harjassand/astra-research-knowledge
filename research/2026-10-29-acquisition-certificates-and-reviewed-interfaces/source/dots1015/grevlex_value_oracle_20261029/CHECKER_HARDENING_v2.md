# Additive verifier hardening

`verify_value_v2.py` supersedes `verify_value.py` for standalone candidate checking. Before any modular check it additionally checks lengths41, monic rational leading coefficient1, positive primitive integer leading coefficient, gcd of integer coefficients1, and exact agreement of the rational coefficient list with integer coefficients divided by their leading coefficient. Thus the integer height bound applies to precisely the rational candidate being verified. The generated frozen candidate already satisfies these conditions; this is fail-closed checker hardening, not a correction to its polynomial or global bound.
