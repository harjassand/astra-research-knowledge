## Governing algebra

Write q=(w,x,y,z), G for the perturbed homogeneous quartic. On w=1, form the exact rational ideal generated by ∂iG−q_i∂0G for i=1,2,3. Compute its grevlex Gröbner basis. The monomials not divisible by any leading monomial give the standard basis of A. In this instance there are40. Multiplying every basis monomial by x,y,z and reducing exactly gives the columns of Mx,My,Mz. There is no conversion to lex order.

Set Q=1+x²+y²+z², f0=∂0G(1,x,y,z), and λ=f0/(4Q). At each admissible prime, evaluate these polynomials in the three commuting multiplication matrices and invert M_Q. Gaussian elimination on the40 Krylov columns (1,λ,…,λ^39) gives the monic relation of degree40. All reported primes have full rank and invertible Q; the first relation is explicitly squarefree. All rational entry denominators are checked through modular inversion. These facts imply over Q that Q is invertible, λ is primitive, and its degree40 characteristic polynomial is squarefree. In particular A is reduced: its regular multiplication by λ has distinct eigenvalues and spans the algebra.

The height proof is recomputed from G, not from a shape. The220×220 cubic Macaulay matrix determinant is nonzero (at λ=0 modulo1000000007 it is333279776). Each of the40 distinct normalized values annihilates this determinant by its projective degree-nine monomial kernel vector. Hence the primitive value polynomial divides the integer determinant. Gauss, Mahler measure and rowwise Hadamard give the exact6613-bit coefficient bound H. The source file VALUE_POLYNOMIAL_HEIGHT_BOUND.md gives the accepted detailed bound. We also check candidate coefficients≤H. At every prime, the actual rational characteristic coefficients are integral in the localization defined by the quotient entries and invertible norm matrix. The candidate relation agrees there. Product M>2H² forces equality of the rational monic coefficients by cross multiplication. This is deterministic exact reconstruction, not probabilistic confidence from fresh primes. Matrix arithmetic uses int64 only after asserting40(p−1)²<2^63; primes are about ten million.

Three freshly computed q0=0 boundary-chart ideals equal(1), excluding missing projective stationary rays. A real λ has exactly one complex stationary point because λ is primitive and A reduced. Conjugation must fix that unique point, so its chart coordinates are real. Conversely every real sphere stationary ray occurs in these charts; Q>0 on real chart points. Euler's identity identifies λ with G(q)/||q||^4. Therefore the least real value root is exactly the global sphere minimum of G. Subtracting the exact coefficient-l1 perturbation bound19/1600000 gives the reported original-objective lower bound.
