# Required physical qualification to the pre-role runtime relaxation
2026-10-29, agreed in independent receiving review.

A protected common-map boundary requires more than hiding an explicit classical role label. Role-dependent preparation delays AFTER this boundary can generate different source-memory evolution/noise maps for LEARN and SERVICE even if no timing metadata is disclosed. Such differences must be equalized, quantitatively budgeted by a separate valid extension, or represented within a genuinely common joint operation. The present theorem does not silently absorb distinct role-conditioned operations into one map.

Variable learner computation/runtime BEFORE this boundary is allowed and may be public. The relaxation removes a fixed private solver-computation clock requirement, not the physical timing, preparation, storage or role/settings/key privacy obligations of the post-boundary quantum interface. Learner/public runtime depends only on allowed classical controller history; it must not introduce an extra unknown-payload measurement or leak.
