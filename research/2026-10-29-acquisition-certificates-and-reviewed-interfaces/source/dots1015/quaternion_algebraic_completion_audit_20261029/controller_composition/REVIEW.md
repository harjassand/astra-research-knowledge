# Independent review of expected-bit controller composition
2026-10-29. Reviewer sharpen_noreset_acquisition_certificate.

ACCEPTED at the stated theoretical fixed-template scope, conditional on the already accepted generic quartic oracle and finite FTPL contracts. This review does not implement or independently re-prove the universal resultant template.

For fixed rational p, each of the35 feedback coefficients is a sum of T bounded rational increments with a fixed common denominator. Its numerator needs O(log(T+2)) bits. A conservative dyadic R within factor2 of the prescribed square-root scale, midpoint count M>=140T² and tolerance R/(4T²) all admit O(log(T+2))-bit descriptions. Adding the finite perturbation and the factor1/2 for the quartic score preserves that bound. Substituting these uniform bit-size bounds into the accepted expected-polynomial-bit oracle contract gives expected poly(log(T+2)) bit work per edge/query, with fixed potentially astronomical constants. Conditional expectation over adaptive earlier source history is legitimate because the accepted oracle complexity bound is uniform over all inputs of the bounded size. This is not an empirical runtime benchmark. Feedback storage, current candidate representation and temporary solver workspace are distinct resources.

Extra private optimizer coins do not require stable minimizers. Every returned additive-epsilon action satisfies the coordinate finite-difference inequalities pointwise. They therefore hold after averaging over the oracle coins and finite-perturbation coupling. The source/feedback independence premise must include those new coins and their clocks. The accepted finite FTPL proof then supplies the same720sqrt(35T/p)+720 per-edge fixed-comparator bound. The dyadic amplitude slack is covered: writing R=a R_opt with a in[1/2,2], the exact smoothing contribution is (a+1/a)/4 times720sqrt(35T/p), at most5/8 of that displayed leading term. The rounded-oracle tolerance contributes at most720. This is actual-history external regret, not policy regret, hindsight selection or a drift-tracking replacement.

The oracle is almost-surely terminating, not pointwise terminating on every infinite tape. No retry timeout is treated as neutral. A finite complete-grid totalization requires its separate explicit bit-cost cap contract. Candidate-dependent runtime can leak a private perturbation or change the current source; without genuine clock/control isolation, deterministic padding can erase the expected wall-clock improvement. These physical limitations are correctly retained. Gate synthesis, calibration, output storage and informed conventional alternatives are not removed by the bit-cost statement.

These checks test the composition arithmetic, not the missing universal solver, a quantum experiment or practical performance.
