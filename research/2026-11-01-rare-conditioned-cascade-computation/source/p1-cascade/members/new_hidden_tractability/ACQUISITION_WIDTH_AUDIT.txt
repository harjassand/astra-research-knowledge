1 November 2026

Reviewed without editing:
  new_cascade/acquire_local_rates.py
  new_cascade/acquire_width.py

Disposition: no mathematical or implementation-scope defect found in the
reviewed deterministic acquisition and width mechanisms. The strengthened
approximate-junta theorem is valid at the constants below. The assay's stated
simultaneous confidence event follows from its fresh Bernoulli experiment
contract and Hoeffding bounds. The implementation itself uses ordinary floating
arithmetic; this review does not promote it to validated interval arithmetic.

1. Assay confidence and its actual information contract
-------------------------------------------------------

The source accepts a node v, a fully specified clamped state of the other nodes,
a duration t, and a number m of independent fresh resets. It returns only the
number of threshold crossings. For unit-scale Weibull shape k_v>=1, the success
probability is 1-exp(-(a_v(S)t)^k_v). Shapes and positive global exposure bounds
0<lower<=a_v(S)<=upper are supplied scalar promises. The graph and rate tables
are not supplied. Fresh reset independence and the known threshold family are
substantive physical premises.

Write D0=max(1,d), alpha=rho/(64D0), and M for the deterministic max_calls cap.
Each log-rate assay starts at t=1/upper and increases duration by 2^(1/k_v).
Thus the Weibull dose doubles at each stage. Let each coarse estimate be within
1/16 of its true probability. At the first observed probability >=1/4, the true
probability is at least 3/16. If this is the first stage, it is at most 1-e^-1.
Otherwise the preceding probability was below 5/16, so doubling dose gives

  p <= 1-(11/16)^2 = 135/256 < 1-e^-1.

By stage ceil(k_v log2(upper/lower)), even the slowest admitted exposure has
dose at least one. The declared additional stages therefore suffice.

The code uses

  m0=ceil(128 log(4M(jmax+1)/delta)).

Hoeffding gives coarse failure probability at most delta/[2M(jmax+1)] per
stage. The allocated stage failures sum to at most delta/(2M) per distinct
configuration assay, even though its stopping time is adaptive.

For the final estimate, set beta=min(1/64,k_v alpha/32). The inverse log-rate map

  g(p)=log(-log(1-p))/k_v-log(t)

has derivative at most 9/k_v on [1/8,3/4]. The bracket and beta bound place both
the true and estimated probabilities inside that interval. Therefore the final
log-rate error is at most 9 beta/k_v <= 9alpha/32 < alpha. With

  m1=ceil(log(4M/delta)/(2 beta^2)),

its failure probability is at most delta/(2M). A union bound over the capped
adaptive calls gives simultaneous |h_tilde(S)-h(S)|<=alpha with probability at
least 1-delta. The proof conditions on each adaptive configuration before using
the fresh independent trials. Cached estimates cause no extra union bound.

All represented independent resets and total clamped exposure time are charged
by the code. Aggregate simulated binomial counts do not mean those physical
resets occurred cheaply. Bracketing uses O(k_v log(upper/lower)+1) stages, not
just O(log(upper/lower)) uniformly in shape. Calibration/time precision and
floating transcendental error require their own implementation allowance.

2. Deterministic acquisition on the simultaneous confidence event
----------------------------------------------------------------

Let h=log a be monotone. For current discovered parents J, each C subset J is
tested at the two opposite unknown-coordinate corners C and C union U, where
U is every undiscovered coordinate other than the target node.

If all observed corner gaps are <=rho/2, true monotonicity gives, for every
original context S with S intersect J=C,

  h(C) <= h(S) <= h(C union U),
  h(C union U)-h(C) <= rho/2+2alpha.

The returned lower monotone table is

  h_hat(C)=max_(B subset C) [h_tilde(B)-alpha].

It obeys h_hat(C)<=h(C)<=h(S), and h_hat(C)>=h(C)-2alpha. Thus

  0 <= h(S)-h_hat(C) <= rho/2+4alpha <= rho.                   (2.1)

This output accuracy statement does not require an exact or approximate junta
promise. It follows from the actual stopping tests, monotonicity, and the
simultaneous assay event. The junta condition is used to earn efficient
discovery/termination and a sparse graph, not to certify (2.1) after stopping.

3. Robust acquisition near an unknown sparse representation
-----------------------------------------------------------

Assume an unknown monotone d-junta h0 exists with

  ||h-h0||_infinity <= b <= rho/(64D0).

The true h remains monotone and may have dense exact dependency. No parent
gap or the identity of the latent scope is supplied. The learner is unchanged.
For d=0, the true function varies by at most 2b and the corner test immediately
stops on the confidence event. Now assume d>=1.

The split threshold is kappa=rho/(8d). If an interval in the sorted-coordinate
chain is not pruned, its observed contrast exceeds kappa; hence

  Delta h > kappa-2alpha = 3rho/(32d) > 2b.

Therefore Delta h0>0 on that interval. In particular, an accepted singleton is
a genuine parent of h0 (and has a genuine positive effect on h). No latent
nonparent is added. At each depth, disjoint unpruned intervals can number at
most d, since each contains a distinct positive h0 chain increment.

It remains to show that a triggered corner gap yields an accepted singleton.
A trigger has true contrast greater than rho/2-2alpha >=15rho/32. Suppose the
recursive search reaches no accepted leaf. Its terminal pruned intervals
partition the full chain. At most d of them contain any h0 change. Each such
interval has true contrast at most kappa+2alpha, totaling at most 5rho/32.

The other intervals form at most d+1 contiguous runs separated by those
change-containing intervals. Each run has constant h0 between its endpoints,
so its total true contrast is at most 2b. Monotonicity of h makes interval
contrasts nonnegative and permits this aggregation without cancellation.
Consequently the full chain contrast is at most

  d(kappa+2alpha)+2b(d+1) <= 5rho/32+rho/16 =7rho/32,

contradicting the trigger. A relevant singleton is therefore found.

At most d new parents can be found. Once all latent parents have been found,
the endpoint contrast for any remaining unknown-coordinate interval is at most
2b; the observed corner contrast is at most 2b+2alpha<rho/2, so the learner
stops. It may stop earlier, which is safe by (2.1).

All learned local scopes are subsets of the latent sparse scopes. Their moral
graph is therefore a subgraph of the latent moral graph. Adding the same event
factors on both sides preserves this inclusion. Hence a bounded width promise
on the latent computation graph survives dense monotone perturbations of the
true physical dependency graph.

The latent approximation can even be initially nonmonotone: if an arbitrary
d-junta g is uniformly b-close to monotone h, its isotone closure
g0(S)=max_(B subset S) g(B) is still a d-junta and remains b-close to h.
Thus existence of a monotone latent approximation costs no extra error in that
case. This observation is not needed by the reviewed implementation.

4. The distinct-configuration cap is safe
-----------------------------------------

Over at most d discoveries, the corner tests use at most
2 sum_(j=0)^d 2^j <2^(d+2) evaluations per target before caching savings.
Let L=ceil(log2(max(2,n-1))). At each search depth at most d intervals are
unpruned; their children give at most 2d visited intervals. At most two endpoint
evaluations occur per visited interval. Across at most d searches this is
bounded by 2d+4d^2 L, again before caching savings.

The implemented per-node allowance

  2^(d+3)+8 max(1,d)^2(max(1,L)+1)

dominates these counts with slack. Failure of a model promise or the confidence
event can raise an explicit exception; the cap prevents an unbounded adaptive
query sequence from invalidating the union bound.

5. Complete width search: soundness and completeness
---------------------------------------------------

For width k, a state (S,C) has C a connected component whose entire outside
neighborhood is exactly S. It asks whether G[C union S] has a width-k tree
decomposition with a bag containing S. The root uses each connected component
of G with S empty.

The algorithm selects nonempty X subset C with |S union X|<=k+1, sets B=S union
X, and recursively handles every connected component C_i of C-X with its actual
outside boundary S_i. The invariant gives S_i subset B. The code explicitly
checks this boundary condition.

Soundness. Glue each child decomposition to the common root bag B. Each edge
either lies in B or in one child graph. Occurrences of a shared boundary vertex
connect through B. This is a valid width-k tree decomposition. Eliminating child
orders before X gives the returned elimination order: when X is eliminated,
all live neighbors are in B, so its width is at most k. The implementation
independently verifies the returned order and width.

Completeness. Take a width-k decomposition of G[C union S] with a root bag
containing S. The union of bags meeting C is connected because C is connected.
Let B be its bag nearest the root. For every s in S, some vertex of C is
adjacent to s. The running-intersection property along the root-to-edge-bag path
forces s to belong to B. Thus B contains all S and at least one vertex of C.
Put X=B intersect C. This is one of the algorithm's enumerated choices.

For a component C_i of C-X, its boundary S_i is contained in B. Restricting the
original decomposition to C_i union S_i gives a width-k decomposition with a
bag containing S_i. Induction on |C| therefore proves that every child call
can succeed. The search consequently finds a width-k order whenever one exists.

In particular, a state with |S|=k+1 and nonempty C is correctly rejected: the
nearest bag meeting C would have to contain S plus another vertex.

6. Width-search cost and scope
------------------------------

Every cached C is a whole component of G-S, not an arbitrary connected vertex
subset. For each S there are at most n possibilities for C. Separators have
size at most k+1, so there are at most O(n^(k+2)) states. Enumerated X choices
and explicit component/boundary work give a finite n^O(k+1) total bound. A loose
more concrete counting bound is polynomial(k)*n^(k+4) per tested width: for
|S|=s, at most n^s choices of S, n components, and O(n^(k+1-s)) choices of X
occur, each with O(n^2) set/graph work.

This is an elementary XP algorithm, not an efficient modern FPT treewidth
implementation. Its completeness earns the decomposition interface. Its actual
cost can dominate for growing width; replacing it by an established faster
algorithm would strengthen the baseline, not invalidate the construction.
The relevant n here is the number of variables including event auxiliaries.
Domains and factor construction costs are charged separately from graph width.

The code assumes ordinary valid scopes with distinct variable indices. No claim
is made for malformed scopes containing repeated variables or out-of-range
indices, nor for a zero-variable graph unless explicitly handled by a caller.

8. Prior-art boundary
----------------------

Finding relevant junta variables through membership-query contrasts and paths
in the Hamming cube is established machinery. The audit does not attribute that
idea to the current construction. Primary routes include:

  Bshouty and Costa, Exact Learning of Juntas from Membership Queries.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.06934
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2017.12.032

  Mossel et al., Learning Juntas, author manuscript, Section 5.2.
  https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~mossel/publications/learning_k.pdf

The specifically established contract here is the noisy real-valued uniform
one-sided envelope, without an edge gap, when a monotone true function may have
dense exact dependency but is uniformly close to an unknown sparse junta.
Historical priority for that complete contract is not certified by this audit.
