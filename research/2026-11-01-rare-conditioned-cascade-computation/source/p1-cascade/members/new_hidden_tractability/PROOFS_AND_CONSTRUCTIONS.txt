HIDDEN TRACTABILITY, EVIDENCE CLOSURE, AND AN ACQUIRED PLANAR REFERENCE
1 November 2026 — independent complementary construction for Astra

Scientific conclusion
---------------------

An experimentally acquired product representation does not, even with moderate
sensor noise, make arbitrary subsequent inference feasible. The soft-observation
reduction below turns a completely known, trivially sampleable product model into
a bounded-degree antiferromagnetic Ising posterior in the computationally hard
regime. This is a computational obstruction, stronger in kind than failure of a
particular representation certificate.

There are constructive escapes. The strongest one developed here acquires a
different representation of the requested conditional computation: recover a
sparse parity energy, absorb hard linear evidence, recognize a hidden planar
graphic matroid, recover its graph and coordinate map, and execute planar Ising
counting and sampling. A bounded residual log weight can be restored by rejection
with cost exp(oscillation of the residual), independent of evidence probability.
This covers graphs of unbounded treewidth and is different from the inherited
noisy linear-solver family.

The principal components are established. Sparse Fourier acquisition, graphic
matroid recognition, planar Ising/Pfaffian computation, and bounded-likelihood
rejection already deliver the assembled operation. I do not claim a new main
theoretical breakthrough or a new efficient general intervention compiler here.
The new work retained is the explicit soft-observation reduction, the fully
stated assembly/acquisition contract, a sharp uniform conditional envelope,
and a finite executable check. This branch is a useful alternative and failure
analysis, not grounds for inflating a solved special case into Astra's destination.

1. Exact access and output contract
----------------------------------

The ordinary coordinates are x in F_2^n. A chosen experiment returns

    Y(x) = f(x) + xi,

where f is real valued, |f(x)| <= B, and fresh noises have conditional mean zero,
|xi| <= sigma, and are independent across distinct repetitions used in a product.
The experimenter can prepare any requested x and read its scalar energy. This is
a strong access model; passive equilibrium samples are not silently substituted.
Its experimental feasibility is a premise, not earned by the mathematics.

Initially f has at most s nonzero Walsh coefficients:

    f(x) = sum_a theta_a chi_a(x),  chi_a(x)=(-1)^(a dot x).

The vectors a and coefficients theta_a are unknown. No graph, latent chart,
partition function, sampler, elimination order, or planning oracle is given.
This exact sparse model is only the acquisition baseline. Section 5 explains
how residual terms can be retained without demanding exact planar structure.

The requested later task supplies some combination of:

  (i) hard affine evidence Bx=b;
  (ii) nonnegative unary weights on explicitly specified parity observables;
  (iii) inverse temperature beta within a declared finite range.

The desired operations are a multiplicative partition estimate and a sample
from the corresponding conditioned/reweighted Gibbs law in original x
coordinates. An exponentially rare evidence event is allowed. Arbitrary
nonlinear evidence or arbitrary optimization objectives are not promised.

Future query choices may depend on the acquired model and previous computations.
The acquisition error event will be uniform across all those choices; the cost
certificate is recomputed for each requested operation.

2. Strong conventional acquisition baseline, written out
-------------------------------------------------------

This is a direct noisy real-valued version of the Kushilevitz–Mansour Fourier
prefix-energy search, not a new learning algorithm.

Let tau=epsilon/(2s). For a prefix u of length l define

    W_u = sum_v theta_(u,v)^2.

Split an input into (z,w), with z in F_2^l. Fourier orthogonality gives

    W_u = E_(w,z,z') [ f(z,w) f(z',w) (-1)^(u dot (z+z')) ].       (2.1)

Two fresh noisy evaluations give an unbiased estimate of the product in (2.1).
The estimate lies in [-(B+sigma)^2, (B+sigma)^2]. With

    O((B+sigma)^4 tau^-4 log(ns/delta))

independent products, Hoeffding's inequality estimates W_u within tau^2/4,
with the allocated failure probability. Starting with the empty prefix, test
both children and retain a child if its estimate is at least tau^2/2.

On the simultaneous accuracy event:

  * Every coefficient of magnitude at least tau survives all n levels.
  * A retained prefix has W_u>0, so it contains a genuine nonzero coefficient.
  * At most s prefixes survive any level; at most 2ns candidates are tested.

Cap each retained list at s and return acquisition failure if it exceeds s.
This makes the number of experiments deterministic even off the good event.

At the leaves, estimate each retained coefficient directly from
E[Y(x)chi_a(x)] within tau. Another

    O((B+sigma)^2 tau^-2 log(s/delta))

fresh evaluations per retained coefficient suffice. Discarded coefficients
have magnitude below tau, so their total l1 contribution is at most s tau.
Retained coefficient errors also have total l1 at most s tau. Consequently

    ||f-f_hat||_infinity <= epsilon                               (2.2)

with probability at least 1-delta.

A deliberately loose completely explicit source bound is

    O(n s^5 (B+sigma)^4 epsilon^-4 log(ns/delta)
        + s^3 (B+sigma)^2 epsilon^-2 log(s/delta)).                (2.3)

Ordinary arithmetic, bit operations, preparation/readout, and coefficient
precision still cost work. This proof is not advertised as the best sparse
Fourier learner; stronger established implementations and bounds may replace
it. Its purpose is to show that the useful Fourier interface is acquired, not
assumed. Persistent adversarial energy bias does not satisfy the fresh
mean-zero premise. A separately certified uniform bias can instead be charged
in (2.2); arbitrary unknown bias cannot be averaged away.

3. A product model becomes hard under fixed noisy observations
--------------------------------------------------------------

Theorem 3.1 (finite-noise conditional hardness construction).
For every graph G=(V,E), construct binary variables x_v and z_e. Define

    y_e = z_e xor x_u xor x_v,  e={u,v}.

The transformation (x,z) <-> (x,y) is an invertible triangular F_2 chart.
Take x_v independent uniform bits and y_e independent with

    P(y_e=0)=2/3,  P(y_e=1)=1/3.

Thus the entire joint prior is explicitly known, its partition normalization
is 2^|V| 3^|E| in integer-weight convention, and exact samples cost O(|V|+|E|).
Its energy is a sum of |E| three-bit parity terms with a fixed nonzero gap.

Now observe each ordinary auxiliary coordinate z_e through an independent
binary symmetric channel with error probability 1/3. Request the posterior
given that every observation is one. For a single edge, its base numerator
is a(y)=2 if y=0 and 1 if y=1. Its observation-likelihood numerator is
b(z)=1 if z=0 and 2 if z=1. For s=x_u xor x_v,

    sum_z a(z xor s)b(z) = 4 if s=0 and 5 if s=1.                 (3.1)

Therefore

    P(x | all observations=1)
      = Z_G^-1 4^(number of equal edges) 5^(number of unequal edges). (3.2)

The evidence probability is exactly

    P(all observations=1)=Z_G / (2^|V| 9^|E|).                   (3.3)

In the convention exp(K sigma_u sigma_v), the effective coupling is
K=(1/2)log(4/5), with tanh(K)=-1/9. The zero-field Ising uniqueness threshold
on the d-regular tree is |tanh(K)|=1/(d-1). Thus d=11 is strictly in the
nonuniqueness regime: (d-1)|tanh(K)|=10/9>1.

The Sly–Sun approximate-counting/sampling hardness theorem applies on that
bounded-degree graph family. A uniformly polynomial relative evidence estimator
for the product-model construction would, by (3.3), give the forbidden Ising
partition approximation unless RP=NP. A uniformly efficient accurate posterior
sampler would also give an accurate sample from the hard model simply by
discarding z. The latent product chart can be given for free in this reduction;
more accurate learning cannot repair the computational gap.

This is not a theorem that typical sensor data are hard. The prescribed all-one
observation can have exponentially small probability. If a task permits failure
on rare observation outcomes, that quantifier requires a different analysis.
Equivalently, b(z) can be a deliberately chosen finite local reweighting: then
the same obstacle concerns an explicitly requested intervention rather than an
unlikely stochastic observation. Both likelihood values are positive; neither
exact pins, vanishing coefficients, nor unstable division is involved.

Comparison with Astra N1132.
The pinned source SAMPLING_CONDITIONING_OBSTRUCTION.tex proves that a legal bit
pin can create a radical in a formerly semisimple matrix multiplier algebra.
It explicitly concerns closure of that certificate, not count hardness. The
construction above addresses a different issue: a fully known easy positive
measure can have computationally hard conditionals under noisy ordinary
observations. No unavailable predecessor proofs or broader Astra guarantees
are imported.

4. Conditional numerical robustness has a useful stronger geometry
------------------------------------------------------------------

Lemma 4.1 (uniform log-weight envelope).
Let p and q be finite positive unnormalized weights and assume

    |log q(x)-log p(x)| <= t  for every x.

For every nonnegative likelihood h with sum_x p(x)h(x)>0,

    exp(-t) <= Z_(qh)/Z_(ph) <= exp(t),
    TV( normalize(qh), normalize(ph) ) <= tanh(t/2).             (4.1)

Both bounds hold simultaneously over the entire nonnegative likelihood cone,
including adaptively selected likelihoods and hard restrictions. They have no
inverse-evidence-probability factor. If the evidence is impossible for p, it is
also impossible for q because p and q have the same support.

Proof. Set r=q/p, m=exp(-t), M=exp(t), and take P=normalize(ph).
Let z=E_P r. Then m<=z<=M, proving the partition bound. For r in [m,M],
convexity of |r-z| and interpolation between its endpoint values give

    E|r-z| <= 2(M-z)(z-m)/(M-m).

Hence TV(P, rP/z) is at most

    (M-z)(z-m)/((M-m)z).

This is maximized at z=sqrt(mM), with value

    (sqrt(M)-sqrt(m))/(sqrt(M)+sqrt(m))=tanh(t/2).

A two-state distribution placing the appropriate mass at r=m and r=M attains
the bound. This proves sharpness. QED.

Applied to (2.2), t=beta epsilon. Additional bounded bias, finite exponential
rounding, or any other proved uniform log-weight error adds to t. The result
does not earn a conditional sampler: Theorem 3.1 remains valid even for t=0.
Thus numerical robustness and execution complexity need separate certificates.
This is a simple likelihood-ratio fact, not claimed as new mathematical theory.

5. A genuinely different constructive escape: acquire a Pfaffian reference
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 Absorb the requested hard evidence before compiling.

Given Bx=b, Gaussian elimination either certifies inconsistency or constructs

    x=x0+Nu,  u in F_2^d,  d=n-rank(B),

with N of full column rank. A learned parity theta_a chi_a(x) becomes
theta_a (-1)^(a dot x0) chi_(aN)(u). Combine equal parity forms and extract
the constant. This is an acquired, exact affine chart for the requested query;
it is not a posterior sampler or an inference oracle.

Known soft parity likelihoods can be multiplied into the corresponding factor
weights. A factor with one zero weight is another hard linear equation and is
absorbed in the same way. Zero weights on both values certify impossibility.

5.2 Recognize the hidden tractable representation.

Let a_1,...,a_m be the remaining factor parity rows on u, and let
M=[a_1^T ... a_m^T] over F_2. Compute its rank r, a column basis, and a row
equivalent systematic representation [I_r | D], keeping column identities.

Use established graphic-matroid recognition on D and cographic recognition
on D^T. Their success certifies that the represented binary matroid is planar
graphic. Graphic recognition constructs a graph G and a spanning forest whose
fundamental circuit matrix is D. Planarity testing supplies an embedding.

This step has implemented polynomial algorithms; for example CMR's cmr-graphic
constructs the graph and forest. Its documented graph-realization method is
Bixby–Wagner, with almost-linear cost in the nonzeros of the already acquired
systematic matrix. Gaussian elimination and finite-bit bookkeeping are added.
No hidden graph or favorable coordinate chart is a premise.

Why graph reconstruction gives the correct computation: the equal systematic
matrices imply equality of the binary row codes. Thus

    C={ (a_i dot u)_i : u in F_2^d }

equals the graph cut code. If G has c connected components, its incidence rank
is r=|V(G)|-c. Each y in C has 2^(d-r) original-u preimages and 2^c graph-spin
preimages. For arbitrary nonnegative parity weights w_i,

    sum_u product_i w_i(a_i dot u)
       = 2^(d-r-c) sum_(graph spins sigma)
                         product_(e={v,w}) w_e(sigma_v xor sigma_w). (5.1)

Constants and original affine offsets are separately included. The right side
is a zero-field planar Ising partition function, computable by established
Pfaffian/Fisher or Kac–Ward machinery. Signs of couplings may vary; the weights
themselves remain nonnegative. This permits low temperatures and unbounded
treewidth, unlike a generic bounded-separator dynamic program.

5.3 Earn sampling rather than infer it from counting.

The edge-parity family is closed under its own pins. If an edge is pinned equal,
contract it. If it is pinned unequal, flip one endpoint's spin gauge, swap the
incident weights as required, and contract it. Process all pins with a
parity-aware disjoint-set structure. A contradictory parity cycle has zero
mass. Loops become constants and parallel edges multiply. Graph minors remain
planar and no vertex field is introduced.

For each edge parity, compute both pinned partition functions by the planar
counter. Their ratio gives a nonnegative exact branching probability. At most
O(m) partition calls generate a full edge-code word y. Solve a_i dot u=y_i by
Gaussian elimination, and draw uniformly from its affine kernel. Restoring
x=x0+Nu gives a sample in the original variables with the required law.

This is a fully specified standard self-reduction inside a proved conditional
family. It avoids the incorrect inference that an arbitrary exact counter
automatically permits unrestricted original-bit self-reduction.

For rational factor weights, exact integer/rational Pfaffian evaluation and
exact integer random draws make this an exact arithmetic algorithm with
polynomial bit cost. If real exponentials are first approximated by rational
weights, the induced additional log-envelope error must be charged in (4.1).
The floating Kac–Ward demonstration in check_constructions.py does not certify
that arithmetic guarantee by itself.

5.4 Restore a generic small residual without closing it into the representation.

Suppose the acquired conditional model is

    p_target(u) proportional to p_reference(u) exp(-R(u)),

where the preceding algorithm constructs the reference partition and sampler.
From the explicitly retained residual factors, compute a deterministic interval

    L <= R(u) <= U,  Omega=U-L.

For residual Fourier terms sum_j rho_j chi_(b_j), the simple interval
[-sum|rho_j|,sum|rho_j|] is valid. Other interval bounds may be tighter.

Draw u from the reference and accept with probability exp(L-R(u)). Then

    acceptance >= exp(-Omega),
    expected proposals per accepted sample <= exp(Omega).        (5.2)

The accepted law is exactly the acquired target law. This is ordinary rejection
sampling with a computed bound, not a perfect-sampling oracle. Its cost includes
each Pfaffian reference sample, residual evaluation, and random-bit precision.

Moreover

    Z_target/Z_reference = exp(-L) E_reference exp(L-R(u)).        (5.3)

The expectation in (5.3) is the acceptance probability. Its value is at least
exp(-Omega), so Bernoulli averaging gives a relative-epsilon estimate with
O(exp(Omega) epsilon^-2 log(1/delta_query)) proposals. The probability guarantee
for this Monte Carlo computation is separate from the one acquisition event.
For an unlimited adaptive sequence, allocate a summable delta_query schedule;
unbiased estimates alone are not a simultaneous high-probability theorem.

The reference can contain arbitrarily small evidence probabilities. They do not
appear in (5.2): evidence is compiled into the reference before correction.
In contrast, rejecting from the unconditional prior would pay inverse evidence
probability. This is a real operational difference, earned by the planar
conditional sampler just constructed.

5.5 The tractable reference is found, not supplied.

A simple constructive search sorts the explicit residual candidate factors by
their worst-case log-weight oscillation. For each prefix, or by a binary search
using minor closure, test whether the retained parity matrix is planar graphic.
Choose a successful reference and compute the exact oscillation budget of all
omitted factors. Constants and hard equations are handled first. Every test,
coordinate change, retained factor, and residual bound is explicit.

This is not an algorithm for the globally optimal reference. It makes a
meaningful conditional claim: if a successful tested prefix leaves residual
oscillation O(log input size), it gives polynomial expected execution. Weak
generic nonplanar terms do not force a full-dimensional algebra or get silently
dropped; they are charged in exp(Omega). An extensive collection of fixed-size
weak terms can still make Omega extensive. The construction does not hide that
exponential cost or prove a better local-resampling mechanism.

Because minor closure implies that deleting factors preserves planar graphicness,
prefix validity is monotone. All unknown Fourier support entries, coefficients,
and the graph realization have already been acquired. In a more flexible
implementation, greedy insertion rather than sorted prefixes could find useful
references, but no optimality or approximation guarantee is claimed.

6. Stronger conventional methods and failed next attacks
-------------------------------------------------------

6.1 Sparse Fourier plus branchwidth already solves much.

For M=[a_i^T], the cut connectivity of a factor partition S and its complement is

    lambda(S)=rank(M_S)+rank(M_complement)-rank(M).

A bounded-branchwidth decomposition can be acquired by established finite-field
matroid algorithms. Exact weighted code enumeration and original-coordinate
sampling follow from finite positive dynamic programming. This depends on a
coordinate-invariant width, not the density of each parity in the measured basis.
It is an excellent standard baseline, not an original Astra theorem. Recent
primary work even supplies FPT decomposition for general connectivity oracles.
The planar route was pursued precisely because its treewidth need not be small.

6.2 Planarity is not the strongest known Ising tractability class.

There are exact inference/sampling algorithms beyond planar graphs, including
K5-free and K3,3-free graph classes using decompositions. Ferromagnetic Ising
partition functions admit an FPRAS on arbitrary graphs; regular-matroid
extensions also exist. High-temperature/Dobrushin or correlation-decay routes
can make many nonplanar cases efficiently approximable. A proposed deployment
must run these equally informed competitors rather than compare only with
brute force or a bad coordinate ordering.

These facts make hidden planar compilation a credible construction but a weak
novelty claim. The notes do not assert a practical advantage over those routes.

6.3 Why independent components are an inadequate invariant.

The soft-observation construction starts with independent y_e and x_v. A new
factor dictionary can connect those components into a graph with a genuine
hard inference phase. Predictive compression of the source measure does not
certify its closure under external evidence. The full computation's interaction
structure, or a probabilistic domination certificate, has to be acquired too.

6.4 Why small total log perturbation is not the final opening.

Bounded-residual rejection has a clean proof and survives generic perturbations,
but it pays exp(total residual oscillation). Merely obtaining that bound would
be a routine robustness extension, insufficient as the requested main advance.
The consequential remaining operation would be to acquire and execute local
correction or a different stochastic proof representation whose cost depends on
an extensive system's local dependence rather than total residual amplitude.

Product-reference high-temperature sampling and partial-rejection/LLL methods
already solve important versions. A planar reference has correlated edge-code
coordinates: conditioning unaffected edges can rigidify the rest through cycle
constraints. Simply importing product resampling rules is invalid. No new
general local correction theorem is proved here. This is the genuinely missing
operation left by the attempted planar-reference attack.

6.5 Why a better chart cannot bypass hard inference.

Theorem 3.1 grants the entire chart and product sampler for free. Another
representation learner cannot remove its computational lower bound without
restricting the queried interventions, allowing failure on the relevant data,
changing the computational assumption, or obtaining a genuinely stronger
physical experiment. This separates information acquisition from execution.

7. Executable finite checks
--------------------------

Run:

    python new_hidden_tractability/check_constructions.py

Dependencies: Python standard library and NumPy. No network or external sampler
is used. check_results.json contains the actual run.

Checks:

  * All 1,024 states of the K4 auxiliary construction match the exact product
    normalization and every posterior marginal weight in (3.1).
  * Sixteen bounded-error energy experiments recover eight parity factors of
    a five-vertex wheel hidden behind an invertible dense binary chart.
  * The graph is reconstructed from the parity matrix by an intentionally
    exponential small-instance cocircuit enumeration, not given to the inverse
    compiler. The production theorem uses polynomial graphic recognition.
  * An explicit planar drawing is found by finite coordinate search. The
    Kac–Ward partition agrees with direct enumeration to floating roundoff.
  * Adding the two missing K5 edges destroys planar graphicness. They are
    retained as residual factors rather than dropped. With multiplicative
    residual range 1.44, the expected proposal count is about 1.2354; the actual
    20,000-sample run used about 1.2369 proposals per accepted sample.
  * A two-state extremizer attains the sharp tanh(t/2) envelope.

These are mechanism checks, not benchmarks, practical scaling evidence,
numerical conditioning certification, or tests of a complexity assumption.
