# Prior comparison and opportunity decision

29 October 2026. The original fully autonomous charged-error quantum-memory
target remains NOT_ACHIEVED. This assignment produced an exact event-noise
admission operation and a real obstruction to one common autonomization
architecture. Neither is marketed as a new general gauge, channel,
unison or entropy theorem.

## Controlling ordinary sources, acquired early

1. [Dunnweber--Styliaris--Trivedi, 2601.20818v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20818v1).
   Direct primary full HTML acquired; relevant Sections II--IV and the
   final noise-scope discussion were read. The discrete QCA admits local
   adversarial fault-path amplitudes. The continuous implementation in
   Section IV.2 specifies local unitary jump errors and local reset/control
   updates. It is therefore a much stronger ordinary comparator than a
   passive code-capacity statement. Its final paragraph explicitly leaves
   general nonunitary Lindblad perturbations open.

   The exact Pauli-ballast construction in our PROOF fits the stated
   unitary-event noise class at the level of generator representation.
   It does not independently establish the proposed continuous threshold.
   The text infers the required effective local-noise condition from a
   positive mean successful-update density. For our quantifiers, a
   size-uniform multipoint physical-time restoring-history bound is still
   needed. The section also writes p as a sum of jump rates; that notation
   is not an already acquired per-site extensive-noise contract of the
   form (3) in our PROOF. We do not infer a paper-wide error from those
   source/admission debts, nor claim that every intended implementation
   has our deliberately **unrepaired** handshake module.

2. [Lake, 2510.08056v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.08056v2).
   Direct primary relevant threshold and desynchronization sections read.
   The proved architecture uses reliable classical storage growing
   polylogarithmically with system size. Its bounded-wait asynchronous
   theorem explicitly pays that timing promise. Poisson updates are used
   numerically; Section IV.2.3 already explains why marching-soldier
   computation velocity is not a fault-tolerance theorem. We do not
   claim to have discovered the generic timing concern.

3. [Drechsler--Oreg--Aharonov, 2610.05589v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05589v1).
   The earlier accepted branch's source scope was retained, and the
   present relevant stochastic-adversarial/SPCA and continuous conversion
   statements were read. Sector separability and Pauli/noisy classical
   histories are actual hypotheses. Its Reset--Read--Execute conversion
   pays a periodic schedule. Those are not silently replaced by a
   threshold for arbitrary CPTP events at fixed physical times.

4. [Devismes--Ilcinkas--Johnen--Mazoit, STACS2025](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol327-stacs2025/html/LIPIcs.STACS.2025.30/LIPIcs.STACS.2025.30.html).
   Direct primary theorem and Sections5--6 read. Modular deadlocks,
   cyclic winding and the neighboring-increment bound are established
   unison mechanisms. Their self-stabilizing construction is a strong
   ordinary repair comparator: Theta(log B) local bits, B>=2diam(G)+2,
   O(diam(G)) rounds and polynomial moves after arbitrary transient
   corruptions. It is not a sustained-noise, fixed-local-dimension quantum
   restoring-history theorem. A quiet-window proof whose window grows
   with diameter cannot be used for free under ongoing positive per-site
   faults: the probability of a globally fault-free interval of length T
   is exp(-constant*n*T). This statement rejects that inference only;
   it does not prove the ordinary unison algorithm cannot run usefully
   amidst faults.

5. [Albert--Bradlyn--Fraas--Jiang, PRX6,041031](https://journals.aps.org/prx/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevX.6.041031).
   Primary AppendixA's gauge statement read. Our exact identity is its
   elementary completing-the-square specialization. No new Lindblad
   gauge freedom is asserted.

6. [Duer--Hein--Cirac--Briegel, PRA72,052326](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0507134).
   The primary abstract and publisher statement were acquired. It already
   studies randomized standard forms, added decoherence and their use in
   error thresholds. Its detailed control recipes were not imported as
   clock-free autonomous access. This is an equally informed warning
   against claiming a generic new noise-tailoring principle.

7. [Watrous, QIC9,406--413](https://www.rintonpress.com/xxqic9/qic-9-56/0406-0413.pdf).
   Primary Theorem1 was read: mixing any unital channel with complete
   depolarization at weight <=1/(d^2-1) makes it mixed unitary. It controls
   our all-unital extension. Choi positivity/interior and finite Poisson
   channel semigroups are standard. The nonunital boundary is exact and
   cannot be bypassed by invoking that theorem.

8. [Aharonov--Ben-Or--Impagliazzo--Nisan, quant-ph/9611028](https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/9611028).
   Direct primary quantum-noise/entropy sections read. The role of fresh
   entropy extraction and logarithmic-depth forgetting without it is
   established. The post-clock-halt entropy argument is an elementary
   continuous-time/reference-sensitive instance, not a new universal
   dissipative no-go.

9. [Nelson--Rajakumar--Gullans, CCC2026](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.CCC.2026.38)
   and [Shtanko et al., Quantum9,1804](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.16233)
   were screened at primary abstract/intro scope. The former forbids
   long-term noisy measurement-free Clifford memory even with fresh
   states; the latter pays potentially size-dependent recovery rates
   in global autonomous-decoder models. Neither stronger theorem was
   imported beyond that acquired scope, and neither is asserted to
   settle the source-specific local-controller target.

## What survives the strongest comparison

The operational normal form is useful **within this programme**:

* A fixed local Pauli/depolarizing ballast can replace an unknown coherent
  generator by an exact low-rate event model. It sacrifices noise budget,
  with support-dimension constants, but requires no estimation or clocked
  randomized-compilation sequence. It is not a reinterpretation of the
  unmodified Hamiltonian. No practical advantage over equally informed
  noise-tailoring methods has been shown.
* A threshold for arbitrary local CPTP events is sufficient, after this
  fixed ballast, for the unknown local Markov-generator robustness target.
  Conversely that event class is necessary. This gives a precise target
  for a restoring-history theorem without a Zeno or O(n*time) remainder.
* A raw finite modular handshake is not such an operation when its own
  registers can suffer weak unbalanced reset errors. The six-cell source
  gives polynomial-time failure even with an optimal final quantum
  decoder. This is an actual architectural negative, not the failure of
  a chosen correlation or a claimed classical population reduction.

The source-aware conventional response is to use a genuinely repairing
classical/quantum control structure, or to pay reliable/growing registers,
external cycles and a fault-tolerant noisy extraction/feedback apparatus.
Those may be good methods. They are not resources already delivered by
attaching an unrepaired clock to the prior perfect-measurement fallback.

## Next gate and final disposition

The consequential unpaid operation is a **fault-contained restoration of
consumed quantum history and noisy clock state**, with a physical-time
threshold for arbitrary local CPTP events. Merely resynchronizing classical

the resulting data operations must themselves be proved correctable.
