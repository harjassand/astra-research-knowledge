# Neutral sampler scheduling for literal actual-history oracle guarantees
2026-10-29. Operational clarification from the independent fixed-port reviewer. Additive to frozen robustv4; no governing mathematical inequality changes.

The proof that capped candidate rejection sampling is a neutral stopping event is necessary but not sufficient if OTHER key samplers are capped only after their role is chosen. A validation-only sampler failure could then stop a round in a role-dependent fashion; simply zeroing that round's hypothetical losses would invalidate the literal1[LEARN]/p unbiasedness claim for those stopped histories.

Use the following finite schedule whenever actual-history Gamma and capped physical key generation are claimed:
1. At each active round, before selecting/exposing its role or making the source call, sample the candidate frames and ALL potential role-specific classical keys/settings: the prospective probe input/output axes and signs, validation matching key, service permutation key, and any other declared finite key. If any capped sampler fails, stop neutrally without a source call or feedback.
2. Generate the role by exact rational sampling. For a capped binary-rejection role sampler its rejection count is independent of its accepted role, so its cap is also a neutral pre-role stop. Equivalent ordering may put this independent abort/count event first.
3. Conditional on all required generation success, execute only the chosen role. The accepted indices of exact rejection samplers have their intended laws independent of their counts. All pre-role cap events therefore leave the required conditional candidate/role/setting laws unchanged.
4. Retire unused keys under the same trusted private-record scope. They are not disclosed to the source and do not stand in for physical probes or source calls.

For the listed protocol, prospective probe/matching/permutation key laws and rejection ranges depend only on the past and fixed dimensions, NOT on the freshly selected candidate frame. That restriction is required: a general candidate-dependent key sampler can have a candidate-dependent cap probability even when its accepted index is independent of its own rejection count conditional on the candidate. Such extra samplers are not covered without a separate neutrality or bias proof.

This adds finite private randomness and classical work for unused prospective roles. It does not add physical source exposures. Charge the cap budget over ALL attempted sampler invocations, not just selected roles: with at mostL(n) such rejection samplers per round, each capped atr attempts with success probability at least1/2, a union bound gives at mostT L(n)2^(−r), plus separately generated code/endpoint resources. Exact fair-bit keys need no rejection cap. Rational gate descriptions still require the separate physical synthesis/error budget.

Under this explicit schedule, the fixed-comparator Gamma in robustv4 and any predictable tracking extension is evaluated on the actual stopped physical history, with zero losses/feedback after a neutral abort. No ideal unbounded-sampler continuation or conditional-success loss expectation is substituted. If an implementation instead samples capped keys AFTER role selection, this literal statement must not be applied unchanged; it needs a different coupled reference process or an explicit bias allowance.
