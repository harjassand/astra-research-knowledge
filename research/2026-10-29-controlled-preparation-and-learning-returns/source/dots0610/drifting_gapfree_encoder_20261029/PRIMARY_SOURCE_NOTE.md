# Prior learning machinery and scope of contribution
2026-10-29. Primary source actually retrieved and read: Herbster and Warmuth, *Tracking the Best Expert*, Machine Learning32,151–178(1998), author-hosted PDF https://mwarmuth.bitbucket.io/pubs/J39.pdf . Figure1(page6), section5 Theorem1(pages10–11) and Corollary1(page12) contain the fixed-share construction, switch-dependent path bound and binary-entropy interpretation. Their displayed share excludes the current expert from the redistributed pool; our uniform-reset variant includes it and its transition probabilities are proved directly in the draft. The rational affine loss update is also proved directly rather than substituted into the source's exponential update without justification.

Fixed-share and expert-over-expert aggregation are established online-learning machinery, not newly invented scientific principles here. The proposed quantum bridge concerns the actual shared physical feedback, current operational loss transfer under causal quantum memory, predictable hidden-frame paths, consistent local encoder actions, finite unbiased sampling and guarded recovery. Historical novelty of that complete bridge has not been established by this targeted source check. A conventional adaptive full-path controller can reuse the same learning mechanism.
