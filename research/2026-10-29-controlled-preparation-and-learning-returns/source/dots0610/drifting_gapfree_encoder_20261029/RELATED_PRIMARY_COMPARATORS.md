# Targeted primary comparison, not a novelty determination
2026-10-29. These sources were actually retrieved to challenge the apparent opening; this is not a comprehensive literature review.

1. Chen, Hazan, Li, Lu, Wang and Yang, *Adaptive Online Learning of Quantum States*, Quantum8,1471(2024), arXiv2206.00220v2: https://arxiv.org/html/2206.00220v2 . The abstract, introduction and section structure explicitly treat dynamic/path-length and adaptive/switching regret for changing quantum states. Dynamic online learning in quantum information is therefore established prior work. Its stated object is online shadow tomography/measurement prediction; the present proposed transfer concerns native probe feedback driving an actual encoder and a guarded accepted recovery map. That distinction needs full comparison before any historical novelty claim.

2. Guatto et al., *Real-time adaptive quantum error correction by model-free multi-agent learning*, arXiv2509.03974v2, revised14July2026: https://arxiv.org/html/2509.03974 . SectionsII–III and AppendixD.3 were read. They combine offline code/circuit discovery with online low-dimensional variational retuning and a keep/retrain bandit; the modeled source interpolates between error axes. AppendixD.3 studies regret through a nonstationary differential equation and describes its simulations as effectively infinite-sampling. Thus model-free adaptive QEC under changing noise is not a new mission or capability claim by itself. Our finite shared-feedback/predictable-path theorem with complete causal-memory guard has a different stated guarantee, but no dominance follows: their practical low-dimensional controller may be much cheaper. Compare equal information/control resources before asserting advantage.

These papers are not load-bearing dependencies of the new proofs. They constrain novelty and comparator language. The strongest operational alternative already identified internally can use the SAME full-information learner with full-path logical tests, so the present construction does not establish unique access to the capability.
