# Central gap-free construction: task loss rather than frame identification
2026-10-29 05:18 UTC. Working synthesis; independent review is active. This is not yet an accepted theorem or a historical novelty claim.

The current positive-gap learner identifies a full hidden local frame. That is unnecessarily demanding for identity or one-axis noise. The new attack learns only enough to keep cumulative operational loss small, using a fixed hidden frame common to all source operations but permitting arbitrary causal quantum memory and changing excitation.

For each star edge i-root, candidate relative rotation C_i specifies a swap S_i(C_i). Define the nonnegative Dirichlet loss delta_i=1−Tr[S_i N†(S_i)]/D. The full-array product-Pauli acquisition bank already gives an unbiased estimate of this quadratic function for EVERY candidate C_i, from the same shot. Its coefficient statistics are fixed-size (identity row and9×9 correlation block), not an environment description. The true relative frame has exactly zero loss at every source history under the structural promise.

Independent choices of star rotations define one consistent global local frame (fix root gauge, take O_i=C_i). The star-transposition Poincare inequality controls full-singlet raw Bell loss by (D/d) sum_i delta_i. Crucially D/d is polynomial in n, whereas direct arbitrary-subcode localization would have D/K. The reviewed physical-permutation orbit then transfers this full-sector loss to the simple logical subcode with no d/K factor. This is the current proposed link between scalable feedback representation and actual encoded protection.

Per-edge full-information online learning on a rational SO3 net yields expected cumulative loss of order sqrt(T log M/p), plus discretizationT epsilon². Choosing epsilon=T^(-1/4) and M polynomial inT gives sublinear cumulative bad-service weight without any positive excitation gap. The controller's finite arithmetic, physical random settings and nonanticipating source boundary must be explicit. Current source operations may depend on every past private record and quantum memory, but not on leaked current controls outside the declared quantum input channel.

Three actual roles are needed: acquisition probes, matching validation and service. Outer correction tolerates cumulative service faults; matching validation provides model-free soundness; online regret supplies conditional high yield only within the hidden collective-algebra family. Expected fault bounds alone are not high-probability certificates: availability needs an explicit tail/Markov argument and role/cap costs. Resource-first use avoids risking an unknown payload on a rejected attempt; postselection does not create a normalized fidelity guarantee.

Strong alternatives remain open. The simple product four-spin code has a cheap blockwise-matching witness with coefficient2, and local S4^h twirling, without global routing. Product logical six-state testing has coefficient3/2. Those verification facts do not automatically supply a polynomial transfer from the SAME unweighted star-loss statistics to the small product code. Neither an impossibility for that direct learner nor a general practical separation has been proved. Explicitly distinguish the proposed learning bridge from already available constant-gap verification.

Open review items: exact star gap/normalization and operator-valued memory bound; rational net and finite sampling; adaptive quantum-memory regression; three-role outer-code composition; actual nonstationary quantum-source fixture; strong comparator; end-to-end source/control/storage/arithmetic resources. No claim of arbitrary-noise positive capacity, fixed future-channel prediction, or hardware fault tolerance.
