# Fast exact full-field CSS/RS decoder
2026-10-29. New integration and executable classical completion, pending independent receiving review. Berlekamp–Massey, Chien search and Forney error evaluation are established coding algorithms, not claimed discoveries.

## Completed operation
The B2048 outer code now has an executed classical decoding path from its actual simulated coefficient-basis readout to the logical correction bits. It avoids the previous large dense Berlekamp–Welch linear solve. Tests cover t99 and t147, including maximum-weight errors, coordinate zero, nonzero-only support, equal amplitudes and subspace supports with long syndrome cancellations. The source of the decoder is the readout record, not the hidden injected error pattern.

Across24 paired X/Z cases at each radius, every correction was exact. Sixty small cases agree with the inherited Berlekamp–Welch decoder; all56 nonzero weight-one GF8 errors were also checked. Two timing runs are preserved rather than selecting the faster run. Observed median complete classical decoding and logical correction preparation were approximately55–70ms at t99 and70–80ms at t147. The largest observed times were437ms and206ms respectively. Peak process RSS was approximately29MiB. These are walltimes on this execution host, not guaranteed latency or quantum hardware performance.

Field lookup arrays occupy32,760 bytes at q2048. The degree-basis matrices occupy78,408 bytes for t99 and172,872 for t147. No q-by-q multiplication table or dense q-by-q decoding matrix is allocated. The classical FFT stores20,481 field-addition records. A decoder-specific copy generates those records without generating or storing the651,264 quantum CNOT list. Exact equality of its field records with the received compiler was checked.

## Actual syndrome interface and trace conventions
The received inverse quantum FFT yields last2t coefficient registers in the computational basis and first2t registers in the binary X basis. The latter are packed binary phase bits, not field elements by assumption. Field.phase_codes implements a->(Tr(a beta_j))_j; the inverse lookup converts the measured phase word to its field label. Its bijectivity is checked exhaustively. This preserves the earlier trace-dual convention.

Let L be the lower-triangular matrix of coefficients of the known novel polynomials B_i in the monomial basis, for i<2t. Its diagonal is nonzero. If Y_i=sum_alpha e_alpha B_i(alpha), then Y=L S, with S_j=sum_alpha e_alpha alpha^j. Exact forward substitution therefore obtains the standard power syndromes.

For the X error, the observed last coefficient vector z first requires the old H R^T map. The implementation avoids materializing that matrix: pad z with leading zeros, apply the classical FFT to obtain y=zR, then apply the FIELD-linear transpose FFT and retain its first2t outputs. This equals H R^T z. The transpose FFT reverses the field multiply-add sequence and swaps source/target using the SAME field constant. It is not a bare transpose of the binary CNOT matrix without trace-dual conversion. For the Z error, the first phase-field vector already is the novel-basis syndrome. Both are converted by L to power sums.

The benchmark independently checks these converted power sums against direct sums over the injected errors. That direct computation is a test oracle only and is not supplied to the decoder. Following decoding, inverse/transpose FFTs compute the middle-register logical X labels and binary Z phase correction bits.

## BM/Chien/Forney construction including the zero field point
For nonzero error locations alpha_i and amplitudes a_i, S_r=sum_i a_i alpha_i^r. The sequence is generated by the locator Lambda(z)=product_i(1-alpha_i z). Berlekamp–Massey determines its shortest recurrence from at least twice the number of nonzero nodes. Chien search evaluates the locator at every inverse nonzero field element and requires exactly the declared number of distinct roots.

For v roots, compute Omega=[Lambda(z) sum_r S_r z^r] below degree v. At z_i=alpha_i^-1, the amplitude is
 a_i=alpha_i Omega(z_i)/Lambda'(z_i)
in characteristic two. The derivative is checked nonzero at every root. This Forney evaluation replaces a cubic Vandermonde solve. BM, the small convolution and verification cost O(t²) field operations; vectorized Chien costs O(qt). O(q) log/exp tables provide exact arithmetic. These are finite-field operation counts; word sizes, table construction and physical CPU latency remain separate.

Full-field length includes alpha=0, which cannot be located by inverse-root search. The decoder explicitly tries two cases:

A. Assume the zero-position amplitude is absent. Run BM on S0,...,S(2t-1), permit at most t nonzero roots and reconstruct amplitudes. Verify every original power syndrome and support at most t.

B. Permit a zero-position amplitude, so there are at most t-1 nonzero nodes. Run BM on S1,...,S(2t-1). These are power sums with weights b_i=a_i alpha_i. Recover b_i, divide by alpha_i, then set a0=S0-sum_nonzero a_i. Again verify ALL2t original power sums and total support at most t.

Every true error of weight at most t is covered: either zero is absent, or removing it leaves at most t-1 nonzero nodes and the shifted sequence has more than enough samples. A candidate failing degree, root-count, derivative, support or exact syndrome verification is rejected; if neither case succeeds, return a declared total fallback. Uniqueness of any verified weight<=t representative follows from RS distance2t+1. The X and Z corrections are also checked for combined support at most t in this implementation.

## What successful verification does NOT certify
An error above radius can share a syndrome with a valid low-weight representative. The decoder cannot detect all such events. check_totality_and_alias.py preserves an exact GF16,t3 counterexample: a nontrivial logical novel-basis row has zero syndrome, the decoder returns the zero representative with a valid flag, and a nonzero logical X remains. Consequently, a decoder flag alone is not an unknown-noise certificate. The guard's accepted bad-tail bound supplies that protection.

For arbitrary syndrome inputs the returned flag distinguishes a found verified representative from the fallback, not whether the physical error was truly small. One may choose an additional abort on fallback, but this would change acceptance accounting. The earlier guard-only yield calculations are not silently redefined as decoder-postselected yields.

## Executed cases and dependencies
benchmark_decoder.py generates48 large paired-error instances, stores errors and actual simulated readout records in benchmark_cases.npz, and times the complete classical decode plus production of logical correction bits. Readout-generation times are evaluator computation and are reported separately. Physical readout, applying the quantum correction gates and maintaining the stored data are not included in these CPU timings.

The source folders include unchanged received copies of the exact additive-FFT compiler and earlier total GRS/BW decoder, used for independent small comparisons. The new ClassicalFFT field list agrees exactly with the received h11 compiler. The uint16 field implementation explicitly restricts h to2..16; this deliverable tests h3,4,5,11 and does not claim arbitrary-size serialization safety. CSS use requires1<=t and4t<q.

The construction removes the previously unexecuted large classical interpolation step. It does not remove the1,302,528 ideal encoder/decoder CNOTs,22,528 trusted buffer qubits, quantum measurement/control errors, routing or storage costs. It supplies no fault-tolerant endpoint implementation. The same decoder benefits all compared guard tests equally.
