# Equally informed high-rate full-path alternative
2026-10-29. Independent comparison for the gap-free online construction.

The online local-swap learner is not exclusive to matching/orbit validation. For every candidate frame, choose a known singlet subcode Q of power-of-two dimension K'=2^floor(log2 d), so d/2<K'≤d. Use its actual high-rate singlet encoder and total decoder and the same logical coordinate convention at each changing frame.

The same group Poincare projection gives, for any source and current independent memory state,
 p_raw,Q ≤(1/K')sum_a||(K_a−Ecomm K_a)Q||F²
          ≤(D/K')sum_i delta_i ≤2(D/d)sum_i delta_i.
To justify the first inequality, Ecomm K_a acts as a scalar on the whole singlet P and hence on Q; minimizing the Q scalar residual gives its raw entanglement loss. As before, testing every memory state lifts the appropriate logical-twirl bad effect to an operator bound. The exact common-zero online regret applies unchanged.

Full-path computational/Hadamard product-basis tests on the bare logical register satisfy e≤2q and q≤e; fresh private bare-logical Pauli randomization before E and after D gives the full-memory CP closure. Thus the same three-role compiler obtains gap-free cumulative protection and the same polynomial readiness order, up to constants, without physical permutation. Logical Pauli cost remains only O(log K') one-qubit gates and2log K' random bits.

This baseline needs the known high-rate singlet/Schur encoder/decoder on EVERY service and full-path test. The matching/orbit route instead supports a much smaller simple tensor-four-spin code with fixed-size block encoders and matching tests that bypass E/D, while paying physical permutations on services and additional endpoint trust/calibration. A high-rate code can carry more logical qubits per native source use, but its control cost differs. Outer Reed–Solomon/CSS codes may use a field large enough for the selected logical alphabet; shorter evaluation lengths remain possible.

No advantage claim follows without counting those actual controls, storage, compiler precision and endpoint calibration on equal terms. The potentially consequential new bridge is gap-free online loss learning under the fixed joint algebra and actual interleaving, not a claim that orbit is the sole means to obtain it. The earlier direct SMALL four-spin-product-code comparator for accuracy-based learning does not automatically prove a comparable loss-transfer bound here; naively using K=2^(n/4) in the global residual inequality produces an exponential D/K. That distinction must be kept visible.
