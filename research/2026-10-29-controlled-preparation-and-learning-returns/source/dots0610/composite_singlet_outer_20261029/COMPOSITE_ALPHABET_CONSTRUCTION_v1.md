## Factor and group
Factor d=product_p p^(a_p). This factorization is not an assumed hard integer oracle: d=(n)!/[(n/2)!^2(n/2+1)], and each prime exponent follows factorial valuations with primes p<=n. Choose integer t>=1, B>=8t and u=ceil(log_2 B). Then every q_p=p^(a_p u)>=B. Group u consecutive native d-level blocks into one B-position outer symbol. Regroup their computational basis registers by prime factor, giving an EXACT tensor identification (C^d)^(tensor u) = tensor_p C^(q_p), with no dimension discard. A group is 'faulty' if any constituent native block is bad. Thus <=t native faults imply <=t faulty outer positions, simultaneously across all prime-factor codes. Total native service calls J=uB.

## An explicit CSS code over every field q_p
Fix B distinct field elements alpha_1,...,alpha_B in F_q (q>=B). Let C1 be evaluations of polynomials degree<B-2t, and C2 evaluations of degree<2t. They are ordinary Reed-Solomon linear codes with C2 subset C1 because B>=4t. The CSS code has computational basis states

 |[c]> = |C2|^(-1/2) sum_{v in C2} |c+v>,  c in C1/C2.

Its dimension is q^(B-4t). Stabilizers are X(v) for v in C2 and Z(w) for w in C1^perp under field dot product and additive character chi(x)=exp(2pi i Tr_(q/p)(x)/p). They commute since w.v=0. Field-trace orthogonality to all scalar multiples is equivalent to field dot-product orthogonality.

Generalized Pauli X(x)Z(z) on F_q^B commutes with all checks exactly when x in C1 and z in C2^perp. Both C1 and C2^perp are MDS with minimum distance 2t+1. Therefore every nonidentity normalizer outside the stabilizer has support at least2t+1. Arbitrary errors on <=t positions satisfy Knill-Laflamme, including coherent superpositions and shared source memory. The argument uses nested C2 subset C1; it does NOT require a self-dual or dual-containing RS code and does NOT require characteristic2 or square-root multipliers.

An explicit encoder is obtained by choosing a generator basis of C2, extending it to C1, and then to F_q^B. Prepare 2t registers in the uniform field superposition, B-4t registers with the message, and 2t in |0>. Apply the invertible B-by-B field-linear basis matrix using Gaussian elimination into additions, nonzero scalings and swaps. Uniform field superposition is a product of p-level Fourier states after choosing a field basis. These operations are finite, known endpoint controls, not source calls.

An explicit decoder measures a basis of the X(C2) and Z(C1^perp) checks via fresh ancillas and field-character Fourier readout. The X-error syndrome identifies a coset modulo C1; the Z-error syndrome a coset modulo C2^perp. Recover the unique weight<=t representative when it exists using Reed-Solomon bounded-distance decoding (C2^perp is a generalized RS code with known nonzero weights); otherwise return a fixed declared total fallback. For an entirely self-contained finite implementation, enumeration of all weight<=t representatives is already an exact terminating decoder and establishes construction but costs exponential in t. Polynomial-time efficiency requires the explicit RS algorithm implementation, not just naming it. Apply the inverse errors, reverse the linear encoder, and discard check registers. Correlated errors are corrected by the same syndrome operation by linearity, not by assuming errors are classical.
