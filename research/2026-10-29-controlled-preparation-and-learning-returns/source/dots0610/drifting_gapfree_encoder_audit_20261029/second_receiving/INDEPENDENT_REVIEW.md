## Fixed-share core
The integer update exactly implements affine loss multiplication followed by a uniform-reset Markov transition on EVERY round. It is the forward recursion of a path prior. A path with S switches has probability at least M^(-1)(alpha/M)^S(1-alpha)^(T-1-S). For eta*ell<=1/2, the elementary logarithmic upper/lower estimates yield the displayed regret K/eta+eta sum ell_path². The comparator may depend on earlier actual observations, because its current index is chosen before the current private record and so the conditional feedback expectation and second moment remain valid. A hindsight path cannot be inserted into that expectation step.

Post-stop equal/common artificial feedback preserves the relevant loss DIFFERENCES; fixed-share transitions may continue mathematically and generally change the weights. No actual continuation calls are used. The neutral pre-role abort schedule from the separately accepted clarification is required. No IID memory assumption enters this reasoning.

A predictable anchor replacement consumes more than epsilon total max-edge variation, so each edge path has at most ceil(Pbar/epsilon) switches. Anchor rounding adds at most another epsilon, and the squared commutator approximation gives18(n-1)T epsilon². For alpha near S/(T-1), the entropy complexity is (S+1)ln M+(T-1)h(S/(T-1)), not the looser S ln T. With epsilon on the declared scale, both K/T and the approximation cost/T vanish for EVERY deterministic sublinear variation envelope. This includes envelopes approaching linear variation slowly; replacing entropy by SlnT would not establish that full claim.
