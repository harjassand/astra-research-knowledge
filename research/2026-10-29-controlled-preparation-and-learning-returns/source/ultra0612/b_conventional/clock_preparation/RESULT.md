# Conditional cold-clock Gibbs preparation

For an explicitly supplied weak product-clock Hamiltonian
H_s=N+lambda V_s with known classical signs, bounded native interactions,
and fixed polynomial-growth geometry, the packet constructs an EXACTLY
Gibbs-stationary algorithmic Lindbladian and a finite quantum circuit
receiver. For every fixed a,B there are sufficiently small FIXED lambda
and T0 such that it mixes from any clock state in O(log(n/epsilon))
parallel update time, uniformly in signs and volume, for

    T0<=t<=B lambda^(-a).

The complete circuit has explicitly charged polynomial(n,t,1/epsilon)
work. The native t=beta/(4lambda^8) window at chosen fixed physical beta
is included after a new, UNEVALUATED coupling gate. All clock levels and
the full thermal state remain; there is no global ground-band truncation.

The enabling composition is finite-order integer-number dressing, a fixed
flat downward-transition band, exact KMS heating, TOTAL-N cancellation of
the tanh coherent correction, and a temperature-independent oscillator
proof with complete anchor-GKLS deletion. Ordinary SW/prethermal, KMS,
oscillator, LCU and Hamiltonian simulation methods are credited. Priority,
classical hardness and practical advantage remain UNKNOWN/unclaimed.
