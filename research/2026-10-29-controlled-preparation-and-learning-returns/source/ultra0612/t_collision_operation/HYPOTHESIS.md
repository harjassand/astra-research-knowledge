# A positive angular dual for anisotropic collision laws

29 October 2026. Private computational research. No physical action.

The proposed capability is exact one-particle output from a nonlinear,
anisotropic Maxwell collision model at any elapsed time, without building
all of its Wild ancestry. This is a model-side sampler, not a particle path,
spatial gas solver, experiment, or finite-bit exact-output claim.

The existing Astra N787 construction already performs the scalar-variance
version. Its full contract and governing source were read first. Classical
Wild, Maxwell moment hierarchies, time-relaxed Monte Carlo, positive Gaussian
mixtures, Gaussian quadrature, and Huber's linear Bernoulli factory are
established operations, not inventions here.

Changed question: for a supplied centered Gaussian COVARIANCE mixture
`a I <= Sigma_0 <= b I`, an unseparated mark still rotates. It cannot be
discarded as in the radial source. Can the whole interval of such rotations
be replaced by a positive finite quadrature that preserves exactly the
polynomial information consumed by the rest of the genealogy?

The candidate bridge uses zonal spherical-harmonic eigenvalues to do this.
A complex-direction polarization and shifted Fourier contour would then
convert directional moments into a bounded full-density-ratio query.
Whether the resulting positive dual, full-law receiver, and actual work
contracts all compose must be proved and executed; a small moment check
alone is not completion.

Ordinary comparisons: exact Wild sampling; the deterministic Maxwell
moment hierarchy; recursive/time-relaxed MC with its actual truncation or
equilibrium error; and a late-time Gaussian approximation with calibrated
energy. No general practical superiority or historical priority is assumed.
