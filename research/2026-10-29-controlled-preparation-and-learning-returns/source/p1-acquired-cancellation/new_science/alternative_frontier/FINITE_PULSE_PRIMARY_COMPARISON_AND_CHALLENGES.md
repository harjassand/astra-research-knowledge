## Main judgment

The residual, Gram inversion, finite invariant-subspace propagation, resolvent reconstruction and control-family formalism all have strong established counterparts. The promising addition is the PHYSICAL ACQUISITION AND COMPLETENESS bridge:

1. Hidden future-test functions are not evaluated at microscopic states.
2. A stationary reversed past, a physically applied pulse and an ordinary future report acquire their true weighted operator inner products.
3. Positivity turns those measured inner products into a certificate of leakage outside the finite predictive space.
4. A finite family of warranted label-bias operations, together with certified label multiplication, determines an entire declared intervention family.

If those steps and a robust finite-data receiving theorem are completed, ordinary ResDMD, observable-operator learning or reduced solvers adopting the construction would not erase that addition. The certificate is instance-dependent; it does not promise a small closing dictionary for every physical system.

## 1. The closest algebraic comparator is stochastic residual DMD

Colbrook, Li, Raut and Townsend, *Beyond expectations: residual dynamic mode decomposition and variance for stochastic dynamical systems*, Nonlinear Dynamics112,2037–2061 (2024), arXiv:2308.10697.

[Official primary article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11071-023-09135-w)

Sections1.3,3.2–3.3, especially equations24–26, estimate H_ij=<K psi_j,K psi_i> using two independent stochastic futures from the SAME starting state. This separates true Koopman residual from trajectory variance. Algorithms1–2 and finite-sample concentration results are existing machinery.

Thus R=D−K^T G^-1 K is a standard projected-operator residual Gram; its zero criterion is not by itself new. The access difference matters: the root obtains the analogous squared operator norm from biased reversibility and actual past/pulse/future records, without preparing two independent continuations of an inaccessible microscopic state. Unbatched trajectory squared error would include stochastic variance and would not certify the same closure.

## 2. Chapman–Kolmogorov tests already assess reduced-model closure

Paul, Wu, Vossel, de Groot and Noe, *Identification of kinetic order parameters for non-equilibrium dynamics*, J.Chem.Phys.150,164120 (2019), arXiv:1811.12551.

[Primary full article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6486394/)

SectionII.C compares direct longer-lag covariance estimates with powers of a reduced Koopman model, extending the usual Chapman–Kolmogorov test beyond indicator-state models. This is a strong baseline for testing memory introduced by projection. The root should not call the two-lag comparison itself a new scientific operation.

For a reversible source, a discrepancy at twice the lag has a particularly strong positive leakage interpretation. The proposed addition is the inaccessible-feature acquisition route, weighted physical pulse contract and finite-family completeness under later interventions.

Older primary work by Hachigian–Rosenblatt (1962), *Functions of reversible Markov processes that are Markovian*, is a historical lead. Its direct full text was unavailable. Do not claim an inspected exact historical theorem or priority comparison from that lead.

## 4. Resolvent-based generator learning is established

The paper avoids time derivatives and operator logarithms by learning resolvents. It develops finite-horizon integration, dictionary approximation, quadrature and a Yosida/resolvent formulation. Clock generation, censoring and finite precision remain separate costs.

## 5. Hidden-state-free spectral prediction is established

Hsu, Kakade and Zhang, *A Spectral Algorithm for Learning Hidden Markov Models*, COLT2009.

[Primary paper](https://www.learningtheory.org/colt2009/papers/011.pdf)

This estimates an observable-operator representation from low-order observation statistics using SVD and matrix multiplication, with separation/conditioning conditions. It predicts observation laws without recovering a unique latent labeling. More general observable-operator and predictive-state approaches use past/future moment structure.

Thus “predict without identifying the hidden state” and past/future moments are not sufficient novelty claims. The possible advance is a physically measured POSITIVE completeness witness for a candidate space, with no supplied hidden-state number, and a native bias identity supplying unseen interventions. That is stronger than fitting a finite observational model under an assumed sufficient rank.

## 7. Concrete physical-interface counterexample

Reversibility and a changed equilibrium weight do NOT determine microscopic pulse kinetics.

Take baseline two-state rates q_12=q_21=1, with pi=(1/2,1/2). For h=(1,2), the proposed native pulse rule gives

    q^h_12=2,   q^h_21=1/2,

and stationary law pi_h=(1/5,4/5). Multiplying BOTH off-diagonal rates by any a>0 gives the same stationary law and still satisfies detailed balance, while relaxation and finite-time responses change by a.

More generally,

    q^h_ij = a_ij(h) q_ij h_j/h_i,
    a_ij(h)=a_ji(h)>0,

has the same pi_h proportional to pi h^2 for every symmetric edge factor. A measured energy/free-energy shift therefore does not establish the canonical a_ij(h)=1 operation. It fixes rate ratios but leaves symmetric dynamical activity free.

The root must retain the actual symmetric-barrier/rate-tilt rule as a construction, warranted physical source class or independently acquired operation contract. A finite library of test pulses cannot infer that untested h obey the same rule from equilibrium weights alone. If the actuator changes barriers unpredictably, all-bias completion fails.

This self-contained example does not challenge the root's explicitly stipulated native interface.
