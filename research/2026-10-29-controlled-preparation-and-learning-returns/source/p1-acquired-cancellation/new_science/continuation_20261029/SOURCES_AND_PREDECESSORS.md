## Primary work read

**Colbrook, Li, Raut and Townsend, Beyond expectations: residual dynamic mode
decomposition and variance for stochastic dynamical systems**, Nonlinear
Dynamics112,2037--2061(2024), DOI10.1007/s11071-023-09135-w.
https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/mjc249/pdfs/stochastic_ResDMD.pdf

The batched-data definition and equations(24)--(27) separate conditional-mean
residuals from trajectory variance using independent continuations at shared
initial states. Those matrix concepts are established. Our reversible loops
supply different physical access, followed by cross-operation synthesis.

**Winick et al., Concepts and conditions for error suppression through
randomized compiling**, arXiv:2212.07500v1(2022).
https://arxiv.org/html/2212.07500v1

Theorem3 and equation(2) retain environment-dependent stochastic Pauli
coefficients. This need not make the whole record memoryless. Their following
key-leakage example is a close prior failure. Here the whole classical
next-visible-row dependence is cancelled using acquired cross responses.
No quantum fault-tolerance conclusion is imported from the analogy.

**Choi, Lim and Wang, Group-averaged Markov chains II: tuning of group action
in finite state space**, arXiv:2512.13067v2(1 September2026).
https://arxiv.org/html/2512.13067v2

Orbit kernels, GPG sandwiches, information projections and exact-sampling
constructions are strong predecessors of the companion phase-scrambling route.
Its initial comparison used v1; root checked v2. Group averaging is not claimed
new. Root compilation uses a selectable menu and measured cross responses.

**Cardelli et al., Lumpability for Uncertain Continuous-Time Markov Chains**,
QEST2021,391--409, DOI10.1007/978-3-030-85172-9_21.
https://backend.orbit.dtu.dk/ws/files/262406426/qest2021.pdf

Read ordinary/uncertain lumpability and Section3.4/Proposition1 on convex
extremal rate choices. Controlled lumpability and convexification are
established. Their supplied microscopic model differs from hidden conditional
response acquisition. Endpoint mixtures here remain separate from generators.

**Bian and Abate, On the Relationship between Bisimulation and Trace Equivalence
in an Approximate Probabilistic Context**,2017.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.04547

Approximate bisimulation supplies finite-horizon trace-TV bounds. Complete-path
comparison is established. The companion flux-repair proof instead starts with
acquired equilibrium L2 leakage and a positive label projection. It does not
claim to invent Markov aggregation or path coupling.
