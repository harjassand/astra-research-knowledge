# Common-equilibrium operations remove acquisition-weight growth with history

29 October 2026. Root corollary addressing the weighted-depth obstacle.

Let actual Markov kernels T_j on an arbitrary hidden standard Borel space be
reversible for the SAME stationary probability pi. These can be fixed-duration
semigroups of equilibrium jump processes or diffusions. A finite visible
partition supplies multiplication projectors E_a. Observations are classical
and nondemolition for the declared state process; switching includes no extra
unmodelled state map. Each independent acquisition trial genuinely begins at pi.
Its density and the hidden dimension need not be known. Reversibility here
means SELF-ADJOINT detailed balance. An odd-parity kinetic thermostat does not
automatically satisfy it; a needed parity/momentum operation is extra physical
access. The predecessor Kramers theorem is not transferred by terminology.

Every word O in T_j,E_a has adjoint equal to its REVERSE WORD. Hence, for
controlled future features f_i=O_i1,

    <f_i,T f_l>_pi=<1,reverse(O_i) T O_l1>_pi.                (1)

The right side is the probability of an actual chronological experiment. Reverse
the order of the earlier feature's physical calls; do not reverse a bath or a
trajectory. All recorded factors are visible indicator tests and the result
is in[0,1]. Operations can be different and noncommuting. No reweighting,
microscopic restart, clone, or conditional-force oracle is required.

In particular, two successive calls T_j^2 acquire <T_j f_i,T_j f_l>. Together
with the one-call moments this certifies the true squared operator leakage

    R_j=D_j-K_j G^(-1)K_j >=0.

Squaring a single stochastic future output would add intrinsic trajectory
variance and would not measure this object.

Build a label-local dictionary. Zero residual for every actual call gives
one finite representation of every causal program of those calls and visible
endpoint observations. Positive residual supplies an executable new feature
T_j f_i; append its label-local components and acquire its moments by(1).
This call-level result uses no hidden rate ceiling or generator inverse, and
applies to continuous/infinite hidden spaces. It does not supply unseen
between-call paths, arbitrary call durations, or a discarded clock transcript.
The separate bounded-rate/resolvent theorem gives a stronger full-path interface
when its physical clock conditions hold.

For r features, s operations and maximum word length L, at most
(1+2s)r(r+1)/2 moment settings suffice. Every datum is bounded by1 regardless
of L. Therefore N>=2 eta^(-2) log(2M_entries/delta) independent preparations
per setting suffice for scalar confidence radius eta. Calls and label reads
per trial still grow as O(L); durations, preparation/reset, work, precision,
storage, Gram conditioning, and feature search are paid separately.

The general differing-equilibrium construction has range <=R^(2L+2), hence
a conservative statistical factor R^(4L+4). This corollary removes that factor
because the COMPLETE common equilibrium is warranted, not because an estimator
normalizes unknown weights. It does not guarantee favorable predictive rank,
good conditioning, mixing, or inexpensive iid preparation.

Barrier or mobility changes that preserve the complete equilibrium measure
and retain detailed balance are relevant source classes. Their actual effect
on hidden energies must be warranted; an unchanged visible marginal is not
enough. Switching may consume work even with common equilibrium. No zero-work
claim is made. The addition is a physical acquisition route for conditional
Gram information used by strong conventional residual/observable-operator
methods, rather than a claim to invent their matrix algebra.
