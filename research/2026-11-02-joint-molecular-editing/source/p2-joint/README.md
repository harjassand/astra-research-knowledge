## Physical-control law without a thermal-noise oracle
For a real external digital light command A_k chosen with logged propensity pi(A_k|observed past), and arbitrary physical chemistry kernel K(dhidden_next,doutcome|hidden,A_k), comparing another randomized external light policy q with the same chemistry K gives
`dP_q/dP_pi = PRODUCT_k q(A_k|observed past)/pi(A_k|observed past)`
on complete action/outcome histories (under positivity). All hidden reaction innovations cancel; they are NOT actuated or logged. At one actual observed bound-state checkpoint W, independently randomize A=0/1 at pi=1/2. Horvitz–Thompson `n^-1 SUM W*(2 A Y - 2(1-A)Y)` estimates the **population that reaches this checkpoint** with contribution range [-2,2], so a valid two-sided Hoeffding radius is `4*sqrt(log(2/delta)/(2n))` on n independent full-specimen episodes. This does not identify responses of never-reached rare modes or chemical product fidelity.

## Exact same-scaffold repair theorem (conditional—not novel alone)
Let actual chemical site bits x_i and prescribed bits b_i define `D_t = SUM 1[x_i!=b_i]`. After a chemically faithful inspect, choose a genuinely wrong site and perform an actual address-specific physical write or erase. On EVERY reachable full hidden-state/history when D_t>0, suppose (i) chosen error corrected with conditional probability at least s, (ii) expected new wrong sites elsewhere at most c. Then the exact change in chemical error count obeys
`E[D_(t+1)-D_t | F_t] <= -(s-c)`.
For delta=s-c>0, the first true completed chemical configuration tau satisfies
`E[tau | D_0] <= D_0/delta`
by stopped supermartingale drift. Arbitrary hidden mode persistence is allowed, but the uniform s,c MUST come from actual chemical physics; finite negative assays cannot establish universal worst-case coverage. Structural destruction or undetectable false completion is outside the bare theorem.

With at most q_N collateral risk per other correct site, the simple sufficient condition is `s>(N-1)q_N`. If external illumination/invader architecture provides a **verified bounded influence graph** of degree d with risk q per exposed neighbor, then `s>dq` is size-independent. This is the pivotal physical question: global light plus diffusion may kill graph locality. If false site choices occur with conditional probability at most r and cause up to a new errors, a conservative repaired drift is `(1-r)(s-c)-r*a`. Release still requires a chemically valid stopping assay.
