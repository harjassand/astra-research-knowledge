EXACT ALIAS-FREEDOM DOES NOT SUPPLY FINITE KINETIC RESOLUTION
Solo finite-CTMC construction, 31 October 2026.

Interface: known independent Poisson inspection rate nu, ordered finite-
alphabet labels, with individual inspection timestamps NOT supplied to
inference. A protocol may use independent stationary preparations, a
finite total budget N of inspected intervals, and rates bounded by nu_max.
The theorem does not claim indistinguishability when exact timestamps or
controlled readouts at a prescribed delay are supplied.

Fix a physical target time t>0, reset rate gamma>0, even hidden size M>=4,
and a rotation rate a>0. On the M-cycle put

                   Q=a(S-I)+gamma(Pi-I),                 (U1)

where S moves one state clockwise and Pi resets to the uniform law. Start
uniformly and observe the binary half-cycle label. This is a FINITE,
irreducible CTMC with reciprocal support: every off-diagonal rate is at
least gamma/M. All states are even. Its escape rate and entropy rate are

 exit=a+gamma(1-1/M),
 sigma=a log(1+aM/gamma).                                 (U2)

Only clockwise/reverse nearest-neighbor pairs contribute to sigma; reset
flows on every other pair are symmetric. Both cost and kinetic bandwidth
are large and are charged. Nothing is powered by an uncharged clock.

1. PHYSICAL RESPONSE REMAINS DIFFERENT

For the balanced binary label f, a deterministic rotation by j states has
correlation A(j)=1-4d_M(j)/M, where d_M is distance to a multiple of M.
Also A(j+M/2)=-A(j). The reset part gives

 C(t)=e^(-gamma t) E[A(J)],       J~Poisson(at).            (U3)

Choose a_+=nM/t and a_-=(n+1/2)M/t, with n a positive integer. Their mean
rotation counts are an exact whole number and a half number of cycles.
Using E|J-EJ|<=sqrt(EJ),

 C_+(t)>=e^(-gamma t)[1-4sqrt(n/M)],
 C_-(t)<=-e^(-gamma t)[1-4sqrt((n+1/2)/M)].                (U4)

If M>=256(n+1/2), then C_+>=3e^(-gamma t)/4 and
C_-<=-3e^(-gamma t)/4: a fixed nonzero separation.

2. EVERY FINITE ORDER-ONLY POISSON RECORD BECOMES INDISTINGUISHABLE

The hidden inspection kernel is B_nu=nu(nu I-Q)^(-1). Its nonconstant
Fourier eigenvalues are

 nu/[nu+gamma+a(1-exp(2pi ik/M))], k=1,...,M-1.

Their magnitudes are <=nu/[2a sin(pi k/M)]. Concavity of sine on[0,pi/2]
gives sin(pi k/M)>=2min(k,M-k)/M. Since sum_{k>=1}1/k^2<=2,
sum_{k=1}^{M-1}sin(pi k/M)^(-2)<=M^2. Parseval and Cauchy-Schwarz give
the UNIFORM row bound

                   TV(B_nu(x,.),uniform)<=nu M/(4a).      (U5)

Thus an N-interval hidden inspection record is within Nnu M/(4a) of
independent uniform states. Its binary label record is within the same
distance of independent fair bits. Across independent preparations the
same bound sums over the TOTAL number of intervals. For the two models
in(U4), their record laws therefore obey

                      TV(P_+,P_-)<=N nu t/(2n).            (U6)

It tends to zero as n grows, while(U4) stays separated by choosing the finite
M accordingly. An adaptive order-only protocol with rates bounded by
nu_max and total intervals<=N has the same coupling bound with nu_max:
its choices remain identical before the first coupled-label mismatch.
Independent preparations do not remove this high-frequency obstruction.

If an estimator uniformly estimated C(t) within epsilon<3e^(-gamma t)/4
with error probability alpha<1/2, its sign would distinguish these models.
Le Cam's elementary two-point bound requires2alpha>=1-TV(P_+,P_-),
contradicting(U6) as n grows. Hence no universal finite readout budget over
all finite hidden CTMCs provides this accuracy at this clock interface.

Every model here has a reset clock of rate gamma, giving physical mixing
TV<=e^(-gamma s), independently of a and M. The one-time observed marginal
is always exactly uniform. The failure is therefore neither an unvisited
dormant component nor absence of a physical mixing bound; it is unbounded
kinetic bandwidth at an order-only observation interface.

3. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

The coherent capped-generator theorem remains exact, and complete infinite
inspection laws still identify the continuous observed law. This theorem
shows why that exact-law result cannot be turned into a uniform finite-
resource capability while ignoring kinetic information.

The example has growing hidden size M, exit rate a+gamma and entropy(U2).
Those are explicit resources, not claims that finite observational ambiguity
is free. finite_clock_control.py checks exact rational inspection kernels
and finite Fourier/rotation controls at stated moderate sizes. The general
argument above, not a finite fixture, supplies the no-uniform-budget result.

Timestamped inspections, a controlled measurement at precisely0 and t,
a supplied kinetic cap, finite hidden-size constraints, or other physical
priors change the interface and may restore useful finite inference. No
universal impossibility for those richer interfaces is claimed. Finite
timestamp precision and its errors would require their own analysis.
