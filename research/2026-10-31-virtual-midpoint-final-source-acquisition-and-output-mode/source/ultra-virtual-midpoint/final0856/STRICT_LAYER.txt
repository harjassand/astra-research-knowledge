1. WHY ATTAINING THE FIRST ROOT MINIMUM FORCES A PERFECT LATENT PARITY

Let K be ANY stationary standard-Borel Markov root attaining V_2. Its
stride-two observed law is P_r and e(K)=phi(sqrt r). In the notation of
DIVISIBILITY.txt set u=Kf,v=K*f,a=(u+v)/2,t=(u-v)/2. Let
eta=(Gamma+Gamma^T)/2, z=(Gamma-Gamma^T)/(Gamma+Gamma^T). The exact chain
of lower bounds is

 e(K)=E_eta phi(z)
     >=phi(sqrt(E_eta z^2))
     >=phi(sqrt(E_pi t^2))
     >=phi(sqrt r).

All values are finite and equal. The last step uses
E t^2=r+E a^2. Strict increase of phi on[0,1) forces a=0 almost surely
and E t^2=r. Strict convexity of s->phi(sqrt s) forces z^2=r almost surely.
Equality in the conditional square inequality
t(x)=E_eta[z(x,Y)f(Y)|x] forces

                  z(x,y)f(y)=t(x) eta-almost surely.

It follows that |t(x)|=sqrt r pi-almost surely. By antisymmetry of z,
t(x)f(y)=-t(y)f(x). Hence the measurable binary state function

                  h(x)=t(x)f(x)/sqrt r

satisfies h(y)=-h(x) on every supported K edge, modulo null sets. Thus

                             Kh=-h.                      (T2)

This is not an assumption that all minimizers equal the explicit4-state
model. It is a forced deterministic parity factor of EVERY minimizer.

2. A FINITE-ENTROPY SQUARE ROOT CANNOT GENERATE THAT PARITY

Suppose K=R^2 for a stationary Markov R with finite e(R). Then(T2) gives
R^2h=-h, so E[h(X_0)h(X_2)]=-1. Apply the already-proved dimension-free
binary lag-two bound to h under R:

 e(R)>=phi(sqrt((C_1(h)^2-C_2(h))_+))=+infinity.

To spell out the boundary without evaluating a square root outside[0,1]:
C_2=-1 forces E a_h^2=0 and E t_h^2=1 because |t_h|<=1, and therefore
the entropy lower limit is phi(1)=infinity. This contradicts finite e(R).

An equivalent physical picture is the forced4-phase rotation. Jensen
equality for the L2 contraction R gives g=Rh with |g|=1 and supported
R edges mapping(h,g) to(g,-h). A reversed edge would require h=-h.
Those edges have no reciprocal support, so their entropy is infinite.
The argument uses an auxiliary factor to identify a forbidden root;
it does not charge a fictitious physical clock in the actual experiment.

3. WHY THIS PROVES A STRICT INFIMUM GAP

An infeasibility-of-exact-equality argument alone would not rule out an
unattained infimum. Here V_4 ATTAINMENT is available at its proper scope:
the compact predictive-flow construction uses the fine observed alphabet
and all finite word coordinates, with LINEAR constraints summing out the

discrete-root problem, not unsupported CTMC compactness/attainment.

If V_4=V_2<infinity, take such a minimizing R. Data processing yields

 V_2 <=2e(K)<=4e(R)=V_4=V_2.

Thus K attains V_2, forcing(T2), which Section2 makes impossible for finite
e(R). Contradiction. If V_4 is infinite, V_4>V_2 is immediate. This proves(T1).
