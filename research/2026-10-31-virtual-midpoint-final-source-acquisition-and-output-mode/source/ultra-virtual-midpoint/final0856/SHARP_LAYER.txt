STREAMING, TIME-UNIFORM ATTAINMENT OF THE EXACT FIRST ROOT LAYER

1. THE MISSING NONLINEAR WITNESS

The Gaussian midpoint cone recovers rate4r for the law P_r in DIVISIBILITY.txt.
Its exact first root-layer minimum is V_2=2phi(sqrt r), slightly larger.
The gap can be closed analytically without acquiring midpoints or solving
a predictive grid. Let g(s)=phi(sqrt s),0<=s<1. For any fixed rational
0<b<1, put

 lambda_b=g'(b^2)=atanh(b)/b+1/(1-b^2),
 c_b=lambda_b b^2-phi(b)=b^2/(1-b^2)-b atanh(b).             (S1)

Also allow b=0 with lambda_0=2,c_0=0. Convexity of g gives
phi(z)>=lambda_b z^2-c_b. Hence for every real t,

 phi*(2lambda_b t)<=sup_z[2lambda_b tz-lambda_b z^2+c_b]
                                       <=lambda_b t^2+c_b. (S2)

Replace the cross-phase payoff(V2) by

 H_b=2lambda_b d-lambda_b(t_x^2+t_y^2+a_x^2+a_y^2)-c_b.    (S3)

On cross-phase pairs its entropy-cone margin is at most-lambda_b a^2 by(S2).
Set it to0 on unsupported same-phase pairs. The antisymmetric bound is
M=2lambda_b. The exact two-edge identity is

 H_b(x,z)+H_b(z,y)=-2lambda_b f_x f_y-2c_b
       +lambda_b(u+f_x)(f_y-u)+lambda_b(v+f_y)(f_x-v).      (S4)

The two residuals have ranges<=4lambda_b, giving C=4lambda_b. The returning
kernel and full-path transfer therefore yield the finite-confidence bound

 Sigma_full>=-2lambda_b S_N-2N c_b
       -4lambda_b sqrt(2N log(1/alpha_m))
       -4N lambda_b^2 theta-log(1/alpha_e)/theta,           (S5)

for0<theta<=1/2, failure<=alpha_m+alpha_e. b is fixed before the record or
its selection is charged. (V5) is the b=0 specialization. The state-space
predictions remain different forward and backward fields and are not inputs.

2. A COMPLETE FAMILY FROM ONE SUFFICIENT STATISTIC

Predeclare b_1=0. Enumerate the remaining dyadic b's by increasing denominator:
1/2,1/4,3/4,1/8,3/8,5/8,7/8,... . Give witness j weight2^-j. At deterministic
checkpoint k with total inference risk a_k, evaluate the first
J_N=bit_length(N) witnesses at risks a_k 2^-j. Transport their maximum with
the SAME single future-descent budget d_k. All selections are charged.

sharp_midpoint.py encloses the log in(S1) with exact rational series. For
the observable term it uses the smaller endpoint of -2lambda_b S_N, an
upper c_b, and upper lambda_b in both noise costs. theta depends only on
N,b and allocated risk. Its log precision grows with bit_length(N), so
every fixed witness's coefficient enclosures converge to the exact values.
No numerical fitted source parameter enters the inference algorithm.

Unlike the complete general word-critic library, EVERY critic here uses
the same adjacent-product sum S_N. Later witnesses can score the old prefix
from this sufficient statistic. There is no hidden raw-prefix retention or
rescan. The streaming input needs one previous bit and O(log N)-bit counters.
Temporary rational arithmetic, J_N witness evaluations at each of O(log^2 N)
checkpoints, growing precision, and optional history are additional costs.

3. EXACT ASYMPTOTIC RECOVERY AT THIS LAYER

For each P_r,0<r<1, the observation-only sharp checkpoint band satisfies

             B_N/N -> V_2(P_r)=2phi(sqrt r) almost surely.  (S6)

Lower limit: S_N/N->-r. Every fixed witness is eventually included, its
coefficient precision converges, and all calibration/boundary costs divided
by N vanish. Its score limit is2lambda_b r-2c_b. Dense dyadics and convex
duality give the supremum2g(r)=2phi(sqrt r). The checkpoint gaps/N vanish.

Upper limit: the attaining four-state root R_sqrt(r) from DIVISIBILITY.txt
has this SAME complete sampled law and fine path entropy divided by N
converging to2phi(sqrt r). The cone/kernal proof applies to every stationary
Markov root, including this periodic discrete root; it need not itself be
a CTMC. Checkpoint errors are summable, so Borel-Cantelli gives eventual
upper coverage under that root, up to the constant baseline. Transported
earlier finite totals/N vanish; later totals have limsup at most V_2.
The observation law is exactly P_r, so this almost-sure statement transfers
to any CTMC or other root realization with that sampled law.

The same attaining model proves that ANY band honest for EVERY stationary
Markov square-root realization has Pr{limsup B_N/N>V_2}<=alpha. Thus(S6)
is the exact asymptotic ceiling for this information contract. A CTMC-aware
method using more semigroup structure could do better; no full CTMC optimum
has been computed. No uniform onset or finite-width optimum follows.
