## 1. The constructive result

Consider independent dephasing,

\[
\mathcal L(\rho)=\frac{\gamma}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{n}(Z_i\rho Z_i-\rho),
\qquad a=e^{-\gamma t}.
\]

The input contains \(\ell\) arbitrary logical qubits, possibly entangled with an external reference. The other spins are maximally mixed, with no preparation record or purification available to recovery. Storage is passive after encoding; final recovery can be any quantum channel.

Set \(n=2^\ell-1\). A conventional perfect Hamming code partitions the physical computational basis into disjoint balls of radius one. I use that partition as a reversible quantum encoding:

\[
U|s,u\rangle=|c(u)+e_s\rangle.
\]

Here \(u\) identifies a codeword, \(s\) is the logical label, and \(e_s\) is either zero or a single-bit displacement.

**The useful geometry is that any two logical labels in the same fibre differ in at most two physical bits.** Their coherence therefore encounters at most two dephasing factors, even as the logical register grows.

After inverse encoding, the entire output factorizes exactly into a logical channel and the unchanged mixed auxiliary state. The logical channel multiplies matrix entries by

\[
K_{ss}=1,\qquad K_{0i}=a,\qquad K_{ij}=a^2
\quad(i\ne j,\ i,j\ne0).
\]

This holds for arbitrary reference-entangled inputs.

I proved the optimal recovery for this channel under both entanglement fidelity and diamond error. More generally, for every positive-entry correlation kernel \(K\),

\[
F_e^{\mathrm{opt}}=\frac{1}{k^2}\sum_{i,j}K_{ij},
\qquad
\Delta_\diamond^{\mathrm{opt}}
=
2\left(1-\min_{p\in\Delta_k}p^TKp\right).
\]

The canonical inverse encoding attains both optima. Thus the result includes the complete receiver, rather than assuming a recovery oracle.

### Comparison with every Clifford encoder

For any Clifford encoder under the same source and noise contract,

\[
F_C^{\mathrm{opt}}
\le
\left(\frac{1+a}{2}\right)^\ell,
\qquad
\Delta_C^{\mathrm{opt}}
\ge
2\left[1-\left(\frac{1+a}{2}\right)^\ell\right].
\]

Bare storage attains these bounds. The proof allows an arbitrary final decoder, including non-Clifford operations and measurements.

For the Hamming encoding, as \(\ell\) grows,

\[
F_H^{\mathrm{opt}}\longrightarrow a^2,
\qquad
\Delta_H^{\mathrm{opt}}\longrightarrow2(1-a^2).
\]

Consequently, a fixed small whole-register error permits a fixed storage time. The optimal Clifford storage time at that same error shrinks as \(1/\ell\).

The broader importance of non-Clifford operations for memory already has strong primary results. Nelson, Rajakumar and Gullans study noisy Clifford circuits with depolarization and fresh-qubit resources. The separation here concerns a different, explicitly fixed task: passive dephasing, a fully mixed auxiliary source, and arbitrary final recovery. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

## 2. A universal limit that makes the comparison consequential

The construction emerged from a failed conjecture: that each additional logical qubit necessarily incurs another additive unit of dephasing loss. The Hamming encoding disproves that conjecture.

The replacement theorem controls a **complete logical algebra** through the quantum blocks of the dissipator’s undamped algebra.

Suppose

\[
\mathcal L_t=-i[H_t,\cdot]-D,
\qquad
D\succeq\gamma(I-E_{\mathcal N}),
\]

where

\[
\mathcal N=\bigoplus_\alpha
\left(M_{r_\alpha}\otimes I_{m_\alpha}\right).
\]

This requires a gap of the dissipator outside its fixed algebra. It requires no mixing or spectral gap of the full dynamics. The Hamiltonian \(H_t\) can be arbitrary predetermined coherent control on the fixed physical system.

For a \(k\)-dimensional logical register encoded with a maximally mixed auxiliary system, define

\[
r_{\mathrm{eff}}
=
\sum_\alpha
\frac{r_\alpha m_\alpha}{d}\min(k,r_\alpha),
\qquad
c=\min\{kr_{\mathrm{eff}}-1,\dim\mathcal N-1,k^2-1\}.
\]

Then every encoder and every final recovery obey

\[
\boxed{
F_e^{\mathrm{opt}}(t)
\le
\frac{
1+\sqrt{(k^2-1)\left[c+(k^2-1-c)e^{-2\gamma t}\right]}
}{k^2}.
}
\]

The mechanism is simple enough to reuse. Complete positivity limits how much of an entire logical operator basis can fit into each undamped quantum block. The remaining squared norm dissipates; Hamiltonian motion contributes zero to its real norm derivative. A second complete-positivity bound covers arbitrary recovery.

For local dephasing, the undamped algebra is enormous but entirely classical: \(c=k-1\). The fidelity ceiling approaches \(e^{-\gamma t}\) as \(k\to\infty\). Therefore a fixed whole-register diamond-error target \(0<\delta<2\) requires

\[
\limsup_{k\to\infty}t
\le
-\frac{\log(1-\delta/2)}{\gamma}.
\]

The Hamming construction achieves asymptotically

\[
t_H=
-\frac{\log(1-\delta/2)}{2\gamma}.
\]

**It reaches one half of the universal ideal storage-time upper bound**, even though that upper bound allows arbitrary encodings and coherent control.

This uses established operator-algebra and noiseless-subsystem foundations. Approximate correction of entire algebras also already has general complementary-channel criteria. The contribution developed here is a necessary finite-time bound acquired directly from the dissipator, plus a representation whose complete receiver is explicitly computable. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}
