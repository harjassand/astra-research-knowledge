ACQUIRING COMPACT STABLE PREDICTION MACHINERY
31 October 2026. Working proof and complete cost contract.

Status: ordinary mathematical derivation, constructive algorithms and numerical
diagnostics. No claim of formal verification, historical priority, physical
measurement, general polynomial-in-rank identification, or advantage over a
strong retaining spectral/model learner. The earlier operation-rule theorem,
its physical interface, failures, ordinary wins and recovery-control theorem
are preserved unchanged in the preceding research bundle.

1. THE FUTURE TASK DETERMINES THE CLAIM

Unknown fixed linear operators T_a, a=1,...,m, act on a real Hilbert space H.
They are contractions in ONE common Hilbert norm. Seeds and the requested
readouts g_j have norm at most one. The task is to predict <g_j,T_w f> for
arbitrary subsequently specified words w of length at most H, to additive
error epsilon, on the same source. Words may be selected after acquisition.
An operation is a genuine source operation, not a free hidden-state vector.

Available scalar experiments acquire <T_u f,T_v f> with conditionally unbiased
bounded reports and a known range bound R_uv. Every actual preparation,
primitive in the chronological return word, weight, readout and arithmetic
operation is charged. Readout moments <g_j,T_v f> need their own warranted,
paid experiment. No physical preparation of a synthetic vector is assumed.

For bit-complexity claims, reports must have finite encodings or a warranted
digitization-error bound. A mean bias of at most eta_digit/N in each Gram
entry adds at most eta_digit to the operator error. Reserve that budget before
using (3), and charge the required report precision. Repetition does not
remove unknown quantization or systematic calibration error. The concrete
four-state experiment uses binary endpoint-product reports.

Common-stationary reversible Markov kernels supply a sufficient physical
interface: stationary preparation, actual forward adjoint words, and bounded
endpoint products acquire these moments in L2(pi). These are source premises,
not conclusions from fitting data. The broader Astra weighted-adjoint interface
need not give a common contraction norm; its report range can grow exponentially
with return depth. Neither difference is erased by the construction below.

The conclusion concerns the specified linear readouts and controlled words.
It does not automatically predict a complete temporal record, arbitrary new
observation operations, conditional rare events, or an optimal feedback plan.
The accompanying four-state counterexample makes this distinction exact.

2. THE ALREADY ACQUIRED REFERENCE IS AN OPERATION, NOT A SUPPLIED BASIS

Run the preserved raw-word compiler, including its atomic guard and confidence
allocation, at local tolerance rho. It discovers N actual word vectors

    V=[v_1,...,v_N],       v_i=T_(w_i) f,

and rational N by N matrices M_a with

    ||M_a||_(1->1) <= 1,
    max_i ||T_a v_i - V M_a e_i||_H <= rho.                 (1)

The empty seed word is one retained column, indexed i0. All unsuccessful
candidate fits and raw-word experiments needed to obtain (1) remain charged.
The compiler does not receive a basis, rank, Gram floor or useful probe set.
Its good-event finite-rank bound is a packing bound, not polynomial in rank.

For c_w=M_w e_i0, (1) gives

    ||c_w||_1 <= 1,     ||T_w f - V c_w||_H <= |w| rho.    (2)

An important distinction: (1) is a certificate on the raw columns. It does not
assert a small normalized operator defect on every vector in span(V).

The new learner uses V only through raw-word descriptors, scalar Gram reports,
and the acquired matrices M_a. The final predictor will not retain N runtime
coordinates. The acquired reference can be expensive; that expense is never
subtracted from the lifetime bill.

3. ACQUIRE A TASK-RESOLUTION GEOMETRY WITHOUT INVERTING A GRAM MATRIX

After the dictionary is chosen, obtain fresh estimates of its Gram matrix
G=V*V. Suppose their symmetric matrix Ghat satisfies

    ||Ghat-G||_op <= eta.                                  (3)

Section 7 constructs this event from scalar reports and pays for it. Set all
eigenvalues of Ghat that are <=2 eta to zero and keep the others. Call the
result B=X*X, with X having k rows and full row rank if k>0. Write x_i=X e_i.
No inverse eigenvalue occurs in this step or in the main fitting algorithm.

Then

    ||B-G||_op <= 3 eta,       k <= rank(G).                (4)

The first assertion follows by truncation and the triangle inequality. For
the second, every eigenvalue of Ghat beyond rank(G) is at most eta by Weyl's
inequality, so none passes the threshold 2 eta. The true rank need not be an
input. If the entire reachable space has dimension r, k<=r.

There is an isometric embedding Q:R^k -> H' into an enlarged Hilbert space,
with the true source extended by zero on the added orthogonal directions,
such that

    max_i ||v_i-Q x_i|| <= alpha,   alpha=sqrt(3 eta).      (5)

Proof. Enlarge H if necessary so the polar factor of V extends to an isometry
J:R^N->H', with V=J G^(1/2). If B=W Lambda W* on its retained eigenvectors,
put X=Lambda^(1/2) W*, Q=J W. Then QX=J B^(1/2). For positive semidefinite
matrices the square root obeys

    ||A^(1/2)-C^(1/2)||_op <= sqrt(||A-C||_op).

For completeness, if d=||A-C||, then A<=C+dI. Operator monotonicity of the
square root gives sqrt(A)<=sqrt(C+dI)<=sqrt(C)+sqrt(d)I.
Exchange A and C to obtain the opposite order bound and hence the norm bound.
Operator monotonicity itself follows from the positive integral representation
of sqrt(t) and order reversal under inversion. Apply this to G,B and (4).

Thus even a singular or arbitrarily poorly conditioned true Gram matrix gives
an acquired compact geometry at the requested absolute resolution. The weak
directions are discarded, not asserted to have been normalized for free.

A useful finite-numerical certificate avoids trusting an eigenvector routine.
For ANY computed rational factor X, certify

    e_B >= ||Ghat-X*X||_op,        D=eta+e_B.

Then (5) holds with alpha=sqrt(D), regardless of the fitting method. If k>0
and a certified lower bound on lambda_min(XX*) exceeds D, then k<=rank(G):
G is strictly positive on the k-dimensional range of X*. Rational row-sum
bounds, exact LDL arithmetic, or certified singular values can check these
claims. The executable uses such final checks, so a floating decomposition is
only a proposal. This certificate can be conservative and may require more
precision or a different proposal; all those attempts are charged.

It is not a universal acceptance test for a plain truncated factor: if
Ghat=G=diag(2.01eta,1.99eta), that factor has smallest retained eigenvalue
2.01eta while D=2.99eta. Arbitrarily fine computation will not make the
sufficient test pass. A bounded constructive repair is to raise every kept
eigenvalue below 4eta up to 4eta. The change from Ghat is still at most 2eta,
so (4)-(5) are unchanged, while the kept squared singular values are at least
4eta>D. Fixed numerical margins and rational PSD certificates can then verify
the result. Row-sum bounds may still be conservative; a certified operator
bound or exact positive-definiteness test is the prescribed fallback, rather
than unlimited retries. On a confidence-failure event a fixed resource cap
may return failure; it must not launch an unbounded acceptance search.

4. FIT CONTRACTIONS; THEIR FEASIBILITY IS PROVED, NOT SUPPLIED

For each operation solve, to additive residual tolerance zeta,

    minimize F_a(A)=max_i ||A x_i-X M_a e_i||_2
    subject to ||A||_op <= 1.                              (6)

This is a finite convex optimization in k by k entries. Section 8 supplies an
explicit projected-subgradient algorithm; an efficient oracle is not a premise.
All targets X M_a e_i are computed from already acquired rules. No additional
K_a=V*T_a V, normalized synthetic probes, or hidden coordinate reads are needed.

The true, unobserved compressed operator A_a^*=Q*T_a Q is a feasible contraction
with F_a(A_a^*)<=rho+2 alpha. Indeed,

 A_a^* X-X M_a
   = Q*T_a(QX-V) + Q*(T_a V-V M_a) + (Q*V-X)M_a.

Each term has maximum column norm at most alpha, rho, alpha respectively.
The last bound uses ||M_a||_(1->1)<=1, not a Gram eigenvalue. Consequently the
algorithm can return stored contractions A_a with a measured fit certificate

    beta=max_(a,i) ||A_a x_i-X M_a e_i||
         <= rho+2 alpha+zeta.                              (7)

A numerical proposal such as clipped least squares can be tried first. It is
accepted only if its actual residual and contraction certificates pass. Its
unsuccessful work is charged; it is not presumed to solve the minimax problem.

5. TWO STABLE REPRESENTATIONS GIVE A LINEAR, NOT QUADRATIC, HORIZON BILL

Start the new predictor at y_empty=x_i0 and update y_(wa)=A_a y_w. Compare it
to the old projected rollout X c_w. The discrepancy satisfies

    y_(wa)-X c_(wa)
      = A_a(y_w-X c_w)+(A_a X-X M_a)c_w.

Euclidean contractivity controls the first term; the old l1 stability controls
the second. Hence

    ||y_w-X c_w||_2 <= |w| beta.                           (8)

Combining (2), (5), (8) gives the physical comparison

    ||T_w f-Q y_w|| <= |w|rho + alpha + |w|beta.            (9)

The tempting proof that projects each TRUE prefix separately accumulates an
unnecessary O(H^2 rho) error. Equation (8) removes it by comparing the two
stable executable models directly. We preserve that failed proof route as a
correction: the quadratic horizon factor was not an information lower bound.

For a retained dictionary readout v_j, predict x_j* y_w. A sharper scalar bound
avoids the alpha term in (9):

 |<v_j,T_w f>-x_j*y_w|
   <= |w|rho + D + ||x_j|| |w|beta
   <= |w|rho + D + (1+alpha)|w|beta.                       (10)

Proof: compare first with (G c_w)_j, then with (B c_w)_j, then use (8).
The l1 norm of c_w converts the maximum entry error <=D to the same bound.
For the exact spectral factor D=3eta; a rounded factor must use its larger
certified D, not silently keep the exact-factor constant.

For an additional norm-at-most-one readout g, acquire each
d_i=<g,v_i> within tau_g, with an explicit joint confidence event. Fit

    minimize max_i |h*x_i-dhat_i|,     ||h||_2<=1,

to tolerance zeta_g. The unknown h*=Q*g is feasible with residual at most
alpha+tau_g. If the achieved measured residual is kappa, its true residual is
at most chi=kappa+tau_g<=alpha+2tau_g+zeta_g. Therefore

    |<g,T_w f>-h*y_w| <= |w|(rho+beta)+chi.                (11)

One acquired event covers every later word in the declared task family, even
when selected after the acquisition data. There is no union bound over future
words. Increasing H or asking for smaller epsilon can require new acquisition.
With old-reference failure allowance delta_old, fresh Gram allowance delta_G,
and total readout allowance delta_R, the overall success probability is at
least 1-delta_old-delta_G-delta_R. Finite deterministic numerical certificates
need no statistical failure allowance of their own.

6. ONE COMPLETE FINITE-ACCURACY SCHEDULE

For H>=1 and 0<epsilon<=1, a conservative choice is

    s=epsilon/[16(H+1)],
    rho=s, alpha<=s, zeta=s,
    tau_g=zeta_g=epsilon/16.

Then beta<=4s, chi<=s+3epsilon/16, and (11) is less than epsilon/2.
Reserve the remaining epsilon/2 for numerical representation and execution.
For exact spectral truncation, eta=s^2/3 suffices for alpha=s. For a genuinely
finite numerical construction use eta=s^2/16, and reserve a per-column factor
approximation error <=s/4. Computing eigenvalues to absolute eta/4, retaining
only eigenvalues confidently above 3eta/2 and discarding those confidently
below 5eta/2, leaves all ambiguous threshold choices harmless. Kept values
exceed eta and discarded values are <=5eta/2. Thus k<=rank(G), while the
square-root and factor-rounding error is <s. No eigengap is assumed or tested
to unbounded precision. A near-threshold eigenspace may be kept or discarded.

The chosen model is compact at task resolution, not necessarily the minimum
possible dimension for that task. No instance-optimal dimension claim follows.

7. GRAM ACQUISITION: FRESH SHOTS, DEPTH, WEIGHTS, AND ALL FAILURES

Condition on the selected dictionary, which is fixed before this new stage.
For each unordered pair (i,j), take n_ij fresh conditionally unbiased reports,
bounded by known R_ij. Use their mean symmetrically. Let

    L_N=N log(9)+log(2/delta_G),
    n_ij=ceil(16 R_ij^2 eta^(-2) L_N).                    (12)

Then (3) holds with probability at least 1-delta_G. No entrywise union bound
and no independence of adaptive historical dictionary choices is needed.

Proof. For a fixed unit vector x, x*(Ghat-G)x is a weighted sum of the fresh
centered reports, with pair weights x_i^2 on the diagonal and 2x_i x_j off it.
The sum of squared pair weights is 2-sum_i x_i^4 <=2. Hoeffding's exponential
bound, or its conditional-martingale version, gives

 Pr(|x*(Ghat-G)x|>t) <= 2 exp[-t^2/(4v)],
    v=max_ij R_ij^2/n_ij.

A 1/4-net of the unit sphere has at most 9^N elements, and a symmetric matrix's
operator norm is at most twice the maximum quadratic form on that net.
Set t=eta/2 and apply the union bound. This proves (12).

For constant range R, the fresh Gram stage uses

    O(R^2 N^2 eta^(-2) [N+log(1/delta_G)]) reports.

At eta=Theta(epsilon^2/H^2), this is

    O(R^2 N^2 (N+log(1/delta_G)) H^4 epsilon^(-4)).        (13)

Let d_i be actual primitive length of word i. The exact primitive bill is

    sum_(i<=j) n_ij (d_i+d_j),                            (14)

with any additional central stages included for the actual experiment. Add
sum n_ij stationary preparations and the actual readouts, clock, actuation,
energy, settling or variable-time costs. A report with range growing as
R_ij~C^(d_i+d_j) pays the corresponding squared factor in (12); a short retained
model does not erase it. In the old one-seed compiler, d_i<=N, so (14) is at
most 2N times the report count. This is a conservative polynomial in N, not r.

The old compiler costs remain: O(mN^2) scalar jobs, roughly
O~(mN^2 rho^(-4)) reports, O~(mN^3 rho^(-4)) primitive steps, plus preparation,
weights and its O(mN^2 rho^(-2)) fitting arithmetic. Its rank-r packing bound
can grow like (1+C/rho)^r. The atomic guard gives almost-sure termination and
finite moments of N under the old finite-rank premises, including confidence
failure paths. Adding the polynomial stage (13) therefore preserves finite
expected work in the common bounded-report case. None of the unsuccessful
old fits, rejected candidate words, fresh refinement shots or failed numerical
proposals is omitted. Exponentially depth-weighted interfaces require their
own expected-cost check; finite moments of N do not by themselves pay them.

8. THE FINITE OPTIMIZATION IS CONSTRUCTED

For (6), write L=max_i ||x_i||<=1+alpha and y_i=X M_a e_i, so ||y_i||<=L.
If k=0, return the zero-dimensional zero predictor; the readout and prediction
bounds still apply. No 0/0 subgradient step is attempted.
Use the smoothed convex objective

    F_mu(A)=max_i sqrt(||A x_i-y_i||^2+mu^2),  mu=zeta/4.

It differs from F_a by at most mu and is L-Lipschitz in Frobenius norm. For an
active index i a subgradient is

    g=[(A x_i-y_i)/sqrt(||A x_i-y_i||^2+mu^2)] x_i*.

Its Frobenius norm is <=L. Start A_0=0. For T iterations take the usual
projected subgradient step with step size h=sqrt(k)/(L sqrt(T)); project onto
the spectral-norm unit ball by taking an SVD and clipping each singular value
at one. Return the arithmetic mean of the iterates. Projection is a finite
matrix computation, and convexity preserves contraction of the average.

The squared-distance telescope gives

    F_mu(A_average)-min F_mu <= L sqrt(k/T).

T>=16 k L^2/zeta^2 leaves ample room for smoothing and numerical error.
Each iteration explicitly scans N residual columns (O(Nk^2) arithmetic) and
computes a k by k SVD (O(k^3) conventional dense arithmetic). Thus a simple
conservative bound is O(m T (Nk^2+k^3)); computing X M_a costs O(mkN^2).
The analogous unit-ball subgradient procedure fits each readout. No planning
or optimization oracle is an unpaid input.
The dense geometry proposal adds O(N^3) arithmetic; rational or interval
certificate arithmetic and report accumulation must also be counted at their
actual bit precision. All optimization budgets use the observed L on every
input, rather than assuming its good-event upper bound on failure paths.

For finite precision, choose the active index within objective tolerance gamma,
compute a bounded subgradient to norm error e_g, and compute a feasible projected
point within Frobenius distance nu of the exact projection. The same telescope
adds at most gamma+2sqrt(k)e_g+(4sqrt(k)nu+nu^2)/(2h) to the averaged objective
bound. For example take gamma<=zeta/32, e_g<=zeta/(64sqrt(k)), and
nu<=min(1,h zeta/(128sqrt(k))). Increasing T by a fixed constant covers these
errors. The smoothing denominator is >=mu, so the required gradient precision
is explicit rather than dependent on an unknown residual gap.
Radially clip each approximate subgradient to Frobenius norm <=L before the
step. This cannot increase its error relative to the true bounded gradient
and avoids an omitted squared-gradient term in the inexact telescope.

Certified singular-value computation to that finite tolerance, rational
rounding and a conservative shrink give a feasible approximate projection.
An exact rational PSD/LDL check of I-A*A, or interval singular-value upper
bounds followed by shrink, can verify the stored contraction. Near-zero
singular values need no inversion. The needed bit precision is polynomial in
the input bit sizes and log(N+k+H+1/epsilon+1/zeta); fixed absolute margins
control spectral truncation and projection. The executable diagnostics use
NumPy floating point and record margins; they are not an outward-rounded
implementation of this universal certificate.

9. STORAGE, LATER EXECUTION, AND WHAT CUMULATES

The final reusable numerical model contains m matrices of size k by k, seed
coordinates, and q readouts of size k. Its runtime storage is O(mk^2+qk),
with k<=r on the acquisition event. Raw words, the N-dimensional reference and
the scalar cache may be retained as acquisition capital for new operations
or finer accuracy, but are not consulted for an ordinary later prediction.

Length-L prediction uses O(Lk^2+qk) dense arithmetic and zero new source calls.
If stored matrices are certified contractions and each step's coordinate
rounding is <=2^(-b), state error is <=L sqrt(k)2^(-b); final dot-product
rounding adds <=2^(-b). Choose b from the actual H, k and numerical error
allowance. Exact rational products instead have growing numerator/denominator
length and must pay that bit cost. Counting only arithmetic operations is not
an exact-rational runtime claim.
For a dictionary readout x_j, multiply the propagated state-rounding error by
its actual certified norm, bounded by 1+alpha. The unit coefficient above
applies to the separately fitted readouts h with ||h||<=1.

The old l1 dictionary can require arbitrarily many atoms even on a rank-two
rotation orbit. This construction compiles its acquired knowledge to at most
two Euclidean coordinates at every finite task tolerance. It removes that
representation-size obstruction without asserting cheap generic discovery.
An ordinary retaining spectral/control learner can perform the same conversion.
The contribution under examination is the complete acquisition-to-execution
contract and its proofs, not an architecture separation or a novelty claim.

10. LIMITS THAT REMAIN LIVE

The following are not proved here:
  * polynomial total acquisition in (r,m,H,1/epsilon) from this general oracle;
  * instance-optimal discovery, dimensions, precision allocation, or stopping;
  * uniformly accurate infinite-horizon prediction from finite noisy data;
  * efficient optimization over all future words or arbitrary feedback policies;
  * general full-record prediction from endpoint-only moments;
  * a common stable Hilbert norm for arbitrary differently weighted controls.

The companion adaptive phase construction addresses the rank-two source much
more efficiently and is adopted there as the strong ordinary comparator. The
four-state temporal-task construction isolates an actual information-contract
obstacle, which no coordinate change fixes. Neither result overwrites the
previous fully charged recovery-control theorem or its retaining learner wins.

11. ADOPT THE DIRECT ORDINARY LEARNER WHEN ITS CERTIFICATE IS CHEAPER

The reference conversion above is not a reason to first build an expensive
dictionary on every source. A direct spectral learner can earn its own basis
and geometry. This route is implemented, with complete failed-stage costs, in
notes/direct_baseline.py and proved in notes/direct_baseline.txt.

Start from the actual seed word. For current distinct actual landmarks W,
form the literal-word pool Z=W union {wa:w in W, a in the menu}. Deduplicate
literal words and acquire the entire pool Gram by the fresh stage in section 7.
Obtain a computed factor X and a certified error D>=||G_Z-X*X||_op. The
square-root argument in section 3 supplies the stronger GLOBAL coupling

    ||V_Z-QX||_op <= alpha=sqrt(D).                       (15)

Let X_W denote the landmark columns and Y_a the corresponding successor
columns. If X_W has full row rank, acquire a lower bound

    sigma <= sigma_min(X_W),    sigma>0,

by a finite numerical proposal followed by exact rational positive-definiteness
checks. Sigma is neither supplied nor inferred from low rank. Fit each
Euclidean contraction A_a directly to these actual successor targets and
certify the attained operator residual

    r_a >= ||A_a X_W-Y_a||_op.

Again feasibility is proved: Q*T_a Q is a contraction and its residual is at
most 2alpha. Minimizing the spectral residual is a finite convex problem.
A subgradient is u (X_W v)* when u,v are top singular vectors of the residual;
its Frobenius norm is at most ||X_W||_op. The same explicit spectral-ball
projected method and finite final certificates apply.

The measured certificate gives an actual physical operator bound

    ||T_a Q-Q A_a||_op <= (2alpha+r_a)/sigma =: kappa_a.   (16)

Proof. Both landmark and same-action successor selections from Z have norm
one: all literal words are distinct and w->wa is injective. Therefore

 (T_a Q-Q A_a)X_W
   = T_a(QX_W-V_W)+(T_a V_W-QY_a)+Q(Y_a-A_aX_W)

has operator norm at most 2alpha+r_a. Multiply by a right inverse of X_W of
norm 1/sigma. No unmeasured normalized source experiment occurs in this proof.
The right inverse is used only to justify the certificate; its measured floor
and all precision needed to establish it are paid.

For the seed readout f, let x0 be its computed coordinate and S=||x0||.
Starting at x0, use the learned contraction matrices and predict x0*A_w x0.
With kappa=max_a kappa_a, all words of length L satisfy

    |<f,T_w f>-x0*A_w x0|
       <= alpha(1+S)+L kappa S.                          (17)

Indeed the physical state discrepancy is at most alpha+L kappa S. The readout
replacement contributes another alpha S. Add the separately certified actual
floating or rational execution error before accepting the requested task.
This is a generic acceptance theorem for unknown Hilbert contractions; exact
rank, the hidden states, useful words and the frame floor are not premises.

Discovery is also an operation. If retained pool dimension exceeds the current
landmark count, inspect actual successor columns and append a word only when
the acquired principal Gram submatrix minus eta I is certified positive
definite. This proves physical independence on the same confidence event.
Otherwise try (16)-(17). If rank, independence, conditioning or task error is
unresolved, refine the pool moments (the implementation divides eta by four).
Allocate delta_s=delta/[s(s+1)] to the entire s-th Gram stage. All adaptive
choices, candidate tests and final predictions share these stage events; no
extra confidence is manufactured by selecting the best-looking certificate.

On the joint good event, an uncapped version with growing numerical budgets
terminates for finite reachable rank: independent additions number at most r;
at each fixed independent W, sufficiently accurate Gram measurements either
certify an independent actual successor, or resolve its finite nonzero frame
floor and arbitrarily small closure error. This is NOT a useful rank-only
cost bound. Very small measured floors can make it exorbitant. Nor does this
good-event argument alone prove finite expected work on confidence failures.

The executable has explicit stage, landmark and optimizer caps and returns
failure when they are exhausted. A complete always-continuing procedure can
make one such bounded direct attempt and, on failure, run the earlier guarded
compiler followed by sections 3-9. Its complete cost includes the failed
attempt. The earlier finite-expected-work guarantee is not replaced by a
silent assumption that the direct search eventually succeeds on every path.

For stage s with p_s pool words and largest actual depth d_s, its constant-range
shot cost is

 O(R^2 p_s^2 [p_s+log(s/delta)] eta_s^(-2)),

and each shot costs its actual pair depth, at most 2d_s, plus preparation and
readings. Sum this bill over EVERY attempted stage. Add factorization,
independence tests, contraction fitting, rational certification and readout
arithmetic. Longer-probe methods, such as the acquired phase anchor, may
reduce precision debt but must pay for those longer probes instead.

This ordinary direct learner decisively beats the reference conversion on the
executed four-state comparison. It discovers (), (2), (2,0) from scalar returns,
while the reference conversion first acquired 45 atoms. Both end with three
Euclidean coordinates. It is the adopted compact-operation learner for that
source. The numerical ratios concern the recorded conservative calibrations,
not an optimality claim or an architecture-specific advantage. For the short
finite prediction task itself, a retained table is cheaper still, as follows.

12. THE FINITE TASK TABLE IS ALSO A STRONG RETAINING LEARNER

For m operations and horizon H, directly enumerate the

    Q=sum_(h=0)^H m^h

literal words. Query <f,T_w f> directly, estimating each to epsilon with
failure allowance delta/Q. Retain all observations and means. Bounded reports
of range R require O(R^2 epsilon^(-2) log(Q/delta)) shots per word, with any
report rounding or simulation bias subtracted from epsilon first. The complete
bill is Q times that preparation count and

    shots_per_word * sum_(h=0)^H h m^h

primitive controls for the endpoint-return interface. Readings, query setup,
storage and computation are additional actual quantities. Later lookup uses
no fresh source calls; no forgetting is imposed on this learner.

For the executed H=8, m=3 task, Q=9841. This direct table needs only millions
of source preparations/controls and beats both compact acquisitions by a very
large margin. It uses 9841 retained means instead of a three-coordinate
operation model. If only this finite task is requested and its storage is
acceptable, the table is the adopted method. The compact fixture demonstrates
acquisition and representation conversion, not cheapest complete task cost.
The accompanying table_baseline program executes this comparison rather than
merely naming it after the fact.

The reflection-family promise also strengthens the table baseline: every word
is determined by cos(k theta), with |k|<=ceil(H/2). Only ceil(H/2) nontrivial
cosine means need be retained. Its full primitive bill is O(H^2 epsilon^-2)
up to confidence factors, rather than exponential in H. At H=256, the exact
cost calculation beats the recorded conservative two-coordinate phase runs
for the interior-angle fixtures. The phase algorithm is therefore a favorable
scaling theorem and a constructed quadrature operation, not the practically
best calibration at those numerical parameters.

13. TASK-SENSITIVE SCALAR REFINEMENT: THE STRONGER ADOPTED COMPACT ROUTE

QUADRATIC_TASK_PROOF.txt supplies a subsequent direct acquisition theorem for
self-adjoint primitives in one common geometry. A scalar endpoint task sees
an omitted component only after it returns to the retained span. If forward
and adjoint leakage are both at most e, its projection error is at most
choose(H,2)e^2. Fitted-operator, endpoint-coordinate and execution errors are
charged separately and remain first order. The quadratic leakage factor is
sharp even for bounded endpoint reports from a reversible Markov source.

That proof constructs a numerical whitener from the acquired raw-word Gram,
checks its relation to the actual Hilbert geometry, forms an exact empirical
Schur defect, and certifies the squared leakage directly. The complete bound
is choose(H,2)e2 + H d + 2s + numerical execution error, where every quantity
is acquired or finitely certified. At a fixed acquired frame this improves
the bounded-report accuracy dependence from epsilon^-4 to epsilon^-2. Frame
conditioning, discovery, return depths, weighted report ranges and every
unsuccessful stage remain explicit costs.

The executable notes/quadratic_scalar_baseline.py is now the adopted compact
learner for the self-adjoint scalar fixture. Section 11 remains a broader
forward-contraction certificate, not an incorrect result. The H=8 direct table
still wins total acquisition for that finite task. The same scalar learner
also acquires a three-coordinate H=64 predictor and explicitly continues an
H=8 retained state to H=64, preserving its global confidence stage indices and
paying three new precision stages. No new word types or deeper returns are

All finite capped learners use a joint soundness statement:
Pr(any returned certificate is false)<=delta. This does not by itself imply
the same error probability conditional on acceptance. Distinct independent
diagnostics have separate ledgers; the warm continuation uses one shared
ledger for both accepted models. No practical cost or optimality claim is
inferred from aggregate simulation CPU time.

These comparisons do not erase the general compact-model consequence. For
fixed physical rank r in the common bounded-report interface, the earlier
packing bound and the new conversion give acquisition polynomial in H/epsilon
with an exponent depending on r, followed by O(Hr^2) dense runtime and O(mr^2)
operator storage. They do not give a polynomial-in-r theorem. The phase
construction has nearly H/epsilon primitive scaling under its precise family
and bounded-report contract, versus the simple family's H^2/epsilon^2 table.
Strong retaining learners may choose either strategy, or stronger statistical
methods. A representation's existence, its acquisition cost, and the cheapest
method for a specific future task remain separate questions.
