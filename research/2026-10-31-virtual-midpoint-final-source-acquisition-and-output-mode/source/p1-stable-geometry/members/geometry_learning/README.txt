ASTRA: ACQUIRED STABLE GEOMETRY FOR LATER PREDICTION
31 October 2026

MAIN RESULT

The finite-l1 dictionary obstruction is representation-specific. A learner
can acquire actual landmark words, their numerical geometry, stable operators
and a finite-horizon prediction certificate from paid scalar return reports.
The strongest construction here is an ordinary direct spectral learner whose
certificate is designed for the requested endpoint scalar. It does not first
recover a hidden state or normalize every reachable direction.

For self-adjoint controls in one common contraction geometry, an omitted
component affects an in-span endpoint scalar only after leaving the span and
returning. If its normalized leakage is at most e, the projection error for
every length-H word is at most choose(H,2)e^2. The implemented learner acquires
the squared leakage from a measured Gram/Schur certificate. This changes the
bounded-report accuracy dependence of this certificate from epsilon^-4 to
epsilon^-2 at a fixed acquired frame. Conditioning and discovery are paid;
no general polynomial-in-rank acquisition result is proved.

The program then explicitly continues retained H=8 knowledge to H=64. It first
finds that the old certificate is insufficient, pays three further precision
stages on the same discovered experiment set, and returns a three-coordinate
model covering every subsequent word of length at most 64. All earlier reports,
failed stages, confidence allocations and numerical artifacts are retained.

This is a constructive capability and a precise cost contract. It is not a
new general principle of spectral learning or primal-dual error estimation,
not a practical acquisition-efficiency claim, and not an architecture advantage
over ordinary learners that retain their knowledge. Those ordinary learners
are the constructive mechanism here.

THE ACTUAL TASK AND SOURCE

The task is to predict <f,T_w f> for any later supplied word w up to the
declared horizon, with a uniform additive error certificate. The learner has
an operation alphabet, a bounded seed observable, and actual scalar inner-
product return experiments. No rank, latent transition matrices, useful word
basis or Gram floor is supplied. The source contract warrants common-stationary
self-adjoint contractions; the learner does not infer those source properties
from a finite fit.

The mixed fixture is a hidden four-state uniform-stationary system with three
symmetric Markov controls and a binary observable. It is an intentionally small
constructive test. The acquisition code receives only noisy empirical scalar
means. Latent arrays appear only inside the source simulator and in explicitly
post-acquisition truth audits. All preparation counts, actual return lengths,
endpoint reads and failed stages are recorded. Physical reports have range one.

The broader weighted-adjoint interface remains distinct. Its weight ranges can
grow with depth, and differently weighted controls need not share a contraction
norm. Preparation, clocks, weights and a warranted adjoint interface do not
become free merely because the stored model is small. The endpoint theorem
also does not identify all visible temporal records or an efficient planner.

NUMERICAL AND FAILURE ACCOUNTING

The four-state source computes ideal moments as exact rational numbers, then
uses aggregate binomial simulation with an explicitly reserved binary-coin
mean rounding budget. Exact count/total pairs retain the empirical reports.
Confidence shot counts are rounded upward by integer/rational calculations.
The saved matrices are dyadic and contraction-certified. Exact rational
Schur and error checks accept the model, while floating factorizations are
only numerical proposals. A separate bound covers binary64 model execution.

At H=64 selected certified matrix entries reach 434 bits; the final exact
error-bound fraction reaches 10148 bits. These are separate recorded quantities,
not a measure of every intermediate integer operation or memory peak. The
empirical means' maximum denominator bit length rises from 35 to 47 in the
warm upgrade. The theoretical cost charges bit precision; it does not count
arbitrary rational arithmetic as a constant-cost operation.

The direct implementation caps stages at 24, landmarks at 12, and preparations
at 10^17. Its numerical checks are bounded. Exhausting a cap returns a paid
failure. Its fixed 1e-7 stored-proposal rounding allowance is adequate for the
recorded tasks; finer tasks require finer computation or an explicit failure.
The proof gives rational alternatives and a guarded prior-compiler fallback
with separately allocated confidence, rather than assuming unlimited search
will terminate on a bad statistical event.

Aggregate-simulator CPU times around tenths of a second do not stand for the
physical time of trillions or quadrillions of episodes. Preparation latency,
actuation time and energy would multiply the explicit physical counts for a
specified apparatus; none is inferred from CPU time. These bills establish
that the demonstrated calibration is not yet practically efficient.

OTHER RESULTS RETAINED IN THIS INVESTIGATION

The general conversion proof can discard directions below task resolution
without paying an inverse Gram floor to normalize them. On a weak-direction
fixture of exact reachable rank three it returns one task-sufficient coordinate.
Its acquisition may nevertheless pay the old dictionary's exponential-in-rank
packing cost. No rank-only efficient-discovery theorem follows.

The circle-reflection family supplies a constructive resolution of the
rank-two polygon obstruction. The learner searches actual dyadic return depths
until it acquires a well-conditioned anchor, then derives sine quadrature from
offset cosine returns. Its long-word primitive scaling is, up to confidence
factors, O(H/epsilon + q log(H/epsilon)) with the acquired anchor depth q.
Physical unit-L2 endpoint reports have range TWO. The stored phase construction
does not supply a sine experiment, a small q, or a generic rank-two certificate.
On the H=256 interior-angle calibration, a retaining cosine table with
ceil(H/2) unknown means beats the recorded phase constants. That win is explicit.

The rank-one noisy decay example has an actual source-information cost of order
H epsilon^-2 log(1/delta), matched by an ordinary retaining learner in its stated
family. The phase lower bound of order H epsilon^-1 log(1/delta) applies to the
specified randomized +/-2 product service, not richer raw continuous reports.
Its corrected horizon index and factor are retained.

The four-state temporal counterexample has identical full endpoint-pair laws
at every lag but different triple probabilities (0.145 versus 0.125 for +++
at c=0.1). It applies to independent reset episodes without a continuing hidden
trajectory handle. If intermediate labels and continuation are available, the
information contract is richer. In the specified two-model family a paid triple
estimator acquires the missing sign at order c^-4 log(1/delta), matching the
retaining adaptive learner lower bound. General observation multiplication is
already present in Astra N1251 and observable-operator research; it is credited.
