PRESERVED FAILED ATTACKS, CORRECTIONS, AND CLAIM BOUNDARIES
31 October 2026.

1. Finite l1-stable polygons are not a general intelligence-acquisition limit.
The previous irrational-rotation obstruction remains correct, but its scope
is that representation. A finite Euclidean contraction model is acquired here.

2. Normalizing every reachable direction is an unnecessarily strong objective.
The earlier exponentially weak-chain example obstructs normalized all-state
operator recovery. It does not obstruct additive finite-horizon prediction.
The new weak-direction fixture deliberately retains one task-sufficient
coordinate although the source's exact reachable rank is three.

3. A first compression proof lost H^2 rho by projecting true prefixes.
That loss was unnecessary. Comparing the two stable executable rollouts gives
H beta and restores a linear horizon bill. This was independently found and
checked by root and the spectral helper.

4. Plain truncated factors do not always pass a conservative rank certificate.
Ghat=G=diag(2.01eta,1.99eta) gives a retained eigenvalue below eta plus the
discard error. More precision does not fix that sufficient-test failure.
The proof now gives a bounded eigenvalue-flooring repair or certified spectral
fallback. Actual fixture programs use final exact rational checks and return
failure under explicit caps; they do not assume every proposal passes.

5. Floating SVDs and optimization results are proposals.
Exact rational reconstruction, positive-definiteness, matrix-norm and residual
checks certify the stored numerical inputs. Source confidence is separate.
Computed Gram products must be formed exactly for those checks, rather than
certifying an already rounded residual matrix. The k=0 branch, positive
frame-floor guard, bounded numerical failure and readout-norm factors are
explicit. The theoretical inexact optimizer clips approximate gradient norms.

6. Report encoding and shot-count arithmetic are part of the contract.
The common four-state simulator now computes ideal moments in exact rational
arithmetic. Its binary64 coin probability has a reserved mean bias <=2^-52.
The final root/direct shot schedules use integer/rational upper bounds
ln(9)<3 and ln(x)<=ceil(log2(x)); no rounded logarithm chooses too few shots.
The stored-model execution error has an explicit binary64 rounding bound.
The earlier floating-mean diagnostic output is retained in
initial_compact_diagnostic_results.json as a SUPERSEDED diagnostic, not the
final confidence/cost record. compact_geometry_results.json is controlling.

7. The reference conversion is not the preferred learner on the tested source.
It first discovers 45 atoms and then compresses to 3. The ordinary direct
successor-pool learner discovers 3 actual landmarks and obtains its own
geometry and contraction certificate much more cheaply. It wins and is
adopted there. The complete cost of every failed precision stage is retained.
Matched requested-accuracy and tighter-certificate comparisons are recorded
separately; their ratios are calibration-specific, not optimal separations.

A still cheaper retaining comparator was identified late: enumerate all 9841
words of the H=8 finite task and store directly estimated responses. Its full
program is now executed and paid, and it wins that task. The late comparison
corrects the interpretation of the compact fixture; it cannot be presented as
evidence of cheapest total acquisition. The reflection-family promise likewise
permits a cosine table with only ceil(H/2) unknown means, which beats the
recorded phase constants at the interior H=256 fixtures. These wins are kept
explicit rather than defeated by an artificial compact-output requirement.

8. A useful sine probe was not given to the phase learner.
Cosine powers alone do not justify the usual atan2 unwrapping proof. An actual
longer-word anchor first acquires a conditioned two-vector Gram matrix; offset
cosine returns then construct sine up to a harmless orientation. Both failed
anchor tests and increased word length are paid. The no-anchor one-coordinate
fallback uses the stated reflection-family promise, not a generic rank test.

9. Physical circle reports have range TWO for the unit-L2 seed.
Replacing this with unit-range physical reports would undercharge sampling.
Using the bounded observable cos instead of sqrt(2)cos requires rescaling
the moments and restores the same effective report-range bill. Broad weighted
adjoint returns can have exponentially growing range and remain distinct.

10. A phase lower bound initially used an inaccessible horizon index.
For H primitive reflections, cos(H theta) is not generally a task response
at that length. The corrected proof uses n=ceil(H/2), attainable by the word
B(AB)^(n-1), and obtains cost >=3H/(16epsilon) kl(1-delta,delta), rather than
the previous factor-two stronger constant. Scaling is unchanged. Sixteen
explicit word-reachability/separation checks cover the correction.

11. A small stable endpoint model does not identify every temporal task.
The four-state witness has identical complete endpoint-pair laws at all lags
but different triple-event probabilities. Observation multiplication is a
separately required operation, already identified by Astra N1251 and OOM/PSR
research. Its general principle is not rediscovered as new here. Endpoint
impossibility requires reset episodes with no continuing trajectory handle;
if shared endpoints can be retained across continued calls, intermediate
observations are already available and the richer interface applies.

12. Lower bounds are source-contract specific.
The rank-one decay lower bound covers its actual endpoint experiment service.
The rank-two phase lower bound covers randomized +/-2 product reports, not
the richer raw continuous product law or both endpoint values. The missing
temporal sign costs Theta(c^-4 log(1/delta)) only for the explicitly stated
two-model family and the richer full-label observation interface.

13. Compact inference does not establish cheap general discovery or planning.
The general packing-based acquisition can be exponential in rank. Direct
normalized closure can pay heavily for small acquired frame floors. No
polynomial-in-(r,H,1/epsilon) general theorem or impossibility has been proved.
No efficient optimizer over all future action words or feedback policies is
assumed or claimed. All executed simulations are software experiments, not
physical measurements of the astronomical billed episode counts.

14. Earlier results are not rewritten.
The previous acquired operation rules, chronological return interface, strong

15. The later task needs a scalar, not whole-vector fidelity.
The first direct certificate charged H times normalized omitted-vector error.
For a scalar with both actual endpoints in the retained span, an omitted
direction must leave and then return. With common self-adjoint contractions,
the correct projection bound is choose(H,2)e^2. The strengthened acquisition
certifies e^2 from a measured Schur defect. It reduces the required moment
precision from quadratic to linear in epsilon at a fixed acquired frame.
This is ordinary primal-dual/projection reasoning, not a novelty claim for a
general new error principle. A bounded-report reversible Markov example shows
the H^2 e^2 dependence is attainable. It is not a statistical lower bound.

16. The scalar square needs reverse leakage and appropriate task endpoints.
Forward leakage alone can be e while the length-two scalar error is e, not
e^2. Additional readouts outside the span produce explicit first-order boundary
terms. Numerical operator fitting and execution errors remain first order.
The improvement is not exported to arbitrary weighted controls or full-record
prediction. A rational-whitener/Schur proof checks the geometry instead of
assuming a supplied stable basis or unknown exact Gram inverse.

17. Capped acceptance proves joint soundness, not selective confidence.
The precise statement is Pr(returned certificate is false)<=delta. It does
not automatically imply Pr(false | accepted)<=delta. Each separately executed
diagnostic has its own ledger. The explicit warm H8-to-H64 operation preserves
the original stage indices and delta_s schedule, so both returned models share
one joint event. It first checks the retained Gram at the new horizon; that
check fails, and all three additional precision stages are paid.

18. Numerical metadata and warm reuse are stated literally.
The initial largest_checked_rational_bit_length label counted selected matrix
entries, not all intermediates. It is now largest_selected_matrix_entry_bit_length;
the final error-bound fraction is recorded separately (10148 bits at H=64).
Neither is a global peak-memory or all-integer-operations measure. No experiment
was rerun to make this metadata correction. Independent H8/H64 runs remain
independent-run records; the added warm run implements actual retained-state
continuation and verifies its ledger separately. Its earlier return descriptors
include 60 ordered (u,v) pairs but only 55 unordered Gram pairs, because the
landmark ordering changed. Neither orientation nor its cost is erased.
