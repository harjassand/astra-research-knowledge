DIRECT ORDINARY ACQUISITION FROM SUCCESSOR-POOL GRAM RETURNS
31 October 2026. Companion to direct_baseline.py.

Result
======

A direct ordinary spectral/contraction learner acquires the useful geometry
on the root's common-stationary four-state fixture much more cheaply than
first acquiring the 45-atom l1 model. It discovers three actual landmarks,
constructs its Euclidean coordinates, measures the needed frame floor, fits
three contraction matrices, and certifies every later word through the
requested horizon. The useful basis, norm matrix, probes, and operation
matrices are not provided to this learner.

This is an implemented constructive alternative, not an optimality theorem.
The large cost ratios below compare the selected conservative calibrations.
They do not establish a universal efficiency separation. The large absolute
sample bills also preclude describing this simulation as practically cheap.

1. Interface and acquired objects
================================

Let T_a be contractions in one Hilbert space and let ||f||<=1. For the actual
fixture, controls are symmetric four-state Markov kernels sharing the uniform
stationary law. The physical observable is +/-1; its L2 coordinates are the
root source's vector (+1/2,+1/2,-1/2,-1/2). A stationary endpoint-product report
is +/-1 with the requested Gram mean. Both the direct and teacher routes use
the identical StationaryEndpointSource and acquire_geometry implementations.

The learner only receives the alphabet size, seed-word name, task horizon H,
accuracy epsilon, confidence budget, and scalar report interface. It does not
inspect hidden matrices, state coordinates, rank, or source mixing strength.
The wrapper supplies the latter only to instantiate the source, and inspects
truth only after acquisition, for separately marked audits.

Maintain actual landmarks W=(w_1,...,w_n), starting with the empty word. Form
the actual-word pool

  Z = W union {w_i a : i in [n], a in alphabet}.

Literal duplicate words are removed; unobserved semantic equalities are not
assumed. Acquire the complete symmetric pool Gram matrix freshly at an
operator-norm tolerance eta. Stage t receives delta_t=delta/[t(t+1)], so all
stages together consume at most delta, including adaptive landmark choices
and failed precision attempts. This is the sphere-net sampling schedule
proved in spectral_baseline.txt, not an uncharged spectral oracle.

The source computes its physical Gram mean in exact rationals before forming
a binary64 binomial probability. The root's implementation reserves at most
2^-52 report-mean bias for this coin rounding. The pool schedule subtracts
|Z|*2^-52 from eta before allocating shots. The simulation batches the law of
the many reports; it still charges every preparation, primitive control and
endpoint reading. Batching is not a physical speedup.

The final shot schedule is outward and integer-only. Stage delta is the exact
Fraction delta/[t(t+1)]. Use ln(9)<3 and ln(x)<=ceil(log2(x)) for x>=1, then
take the rational ceiling of

  16 [3|Z| + ceil_log2(2/delta_t)] / statistical_eta^2.

The helper gram_shot_schedule performs integer comparisons for ceil_log2 and
exact rational bias subtraction/ceiling. The preparation-budget preflight
calls this same helper, so it cannot undercount a stage due to a different
rounding convention. Superseded floating-log calibrations are discussed at
the end of this note; every table below uses the corrected integer schedule.

2. Geometry and landmark discovery are checked from those reports
=================================================================

Retain eigenvalues of Ghat above 2 eta and let X be the proposed square-root
factor. Numerical eigensolver output is only a proposal. The program forms

  E = Ghat - X*X

in exact rationals using the exact recorded report means and binary input
entries, then certifies an operator-norm upper bound e on E. Thus, on the
sampling confidence event,

  ||G_Z-X*X||op <= eta+e = alpha^2.

The polar/square-root argument in spectral_baseline.txt produces an isometry
Q into an enlarged physical Hilbert space such that

  ||V_Z-QX||op <= alpha.                                    (1)

This is a GLOBAL operator-norm coupling, stronger than a bound on individual
columns. The isometry Q is a proof witness and is never supplied to the
learner. A separate exact rational positivity check using the numerical
eigenvectors proves that the retained dimension is no larger than the actual
pool rank. It does not assume that the pool already reaches global rank.

When the pool's retained dimension exceeds the number of landmarks, the
learner ranks actual successors by their numerical residual outside the
current coordinate span. It does not blindly accept that numerical score.
For a proposed augmented landmark set J, it verifies

  Ghat[J,J] - eta I > 0                                    (2)

by exact rational LDL' elimination. On the same confidence event, (2) proves
that the corresponding actual physical vectors are independent. A failed
candidate is recorded. If no candidate passes, the learner refines eta and
pays another complete stage. This prevents a redundant noise direction from
silently becoming a promised basis.

Once X_W has full row rank, the learner obtains a numerical estimate of its
smallest squared singular value and verifies a slightly smaller lower bound
lambda by exact positivity of

  X_W X_W* - lambda I > 0.

The resulting sigma=sqrt_lower(lambda)>0 is an ACQUIRED frame floor. Its
discovery, unsuccessful checks, numerical precision, and all measurements
needed to make it useful are charged. No particular floor is a premise.
If finite square-root precision yields a zero lower bound, that stage cannot
divide by it or pass; it must refine or return bounded failure.

3. Direct operation acquisition and its normalized certificate
=============================================================

For generator a, let Y_a consist of the columns of X corresponding to the
actual successors T_a w_i. Fit an ordinary Euclidean contraction A_a to

  minimize over ||A||op<=1: ||A X_W-Y_a||op.                  (3)

The inaccessible comparator Q* T_a Q has residual at most 2 alpha. This
follows directly from (1) and the contraction of T_a. The executed program
first proposes a least-squares fit, projects its singular values onto the
unit interval, then performs exact rational final norm and residual checks.
If necessary it runs an explicitly bounded projected-subgradient method for
(3). No efficient optimizer is assumed as an oracle.

For F(A)=||A X_W-Y||op, a subgradient is

  u (X_W v)*,

where u,v are leading singular vectors of the residual. Its Frobenius norm
is at most ||X_W||op. Starting from zero and projecting onto the spectral
unit ball gives objective excess at most

  sqrt(k)||X_W||op/sqrt(T)

after T iterations. A spectral residual SVD, an A SVD, and the matrix products
are all actual computations. The implemented fit uses a declared 200,000
iteration cap and returns failure if its final certificate does not pass.

Let r_a be the exact-input certified bound on ||A_a X_W-Y_a||op, and let
r=max_a r_a. Landmark columns in the pool are distinct. Successor columns
for a fixed a are also distinct literal words. Therefore their column
selection matrices have operator norm one, and (1) gives

  ||(T_a Q-Q A_a)X_W||op <= 2 alpha+r_a.

Since X_W has full row rank, X_W X_W^+=I. Hence

  ||T_a Q-Q A_a||op <= (2 alpha+r_a)/sigma,
  kappa := (2 alpha+r)/sigma.                              (4)

The tempting sqrt(n)(2alpha+beta)/sigma bound is unnecessarily loose.
The global coupling removes sqrt(n) from the two geometry terms. If only
maximum-column fit residual beta is available, use
(2alpha+sqrt(n) beta)/sigma. The executable certifies r directly.

Equation (4) is sound without a global rank promise and without asserting
that an untested infinite family of raw words is exactly closed. All inputs
to its certificate are acquired from this finite pool and physically valid
return experiments.

4. Every later word, including numerical execution
=================================================

Let x_0 be the acquired coordinate of the actual seed f, and S=||x_0||. The
stored predictor is x_0* A_w x_0. Its physical state error obeys

  ||T_w f-Q A_w x_0|| <= alpha+|w| kappa S,

because all stored A_a are certified contractions. The readout f has norm at
most one and differs from Q x_0 by at most alpha, so

  |<f,T_w f>-x_0* A_w x_0|
     <= alpha(1+S)+|w| kappa S.                            (5)

All square-root upper/lower bounds and the final task comparison are formed
as exact rationals. Stored A matrices are inward-shrunk dyadic matrices with
exact contraction certificates. Stored X entries are the binary64 numbers
against which the geometry certificate was evaluated, not unrecorded exact
eigenvectors. Exact-rational residual calculations include multiplication
rounding rather than treating a rounded residual array as exact.

For the binary64 matrix-vector implementation, the program adds an explicit
roundoff allowance to (5). With unit roundoff u=2^-53 and
gamma_k=ku/(1-ku), one step has relative Euclidean disturbance bounded by
q=gamma_k sqrt(k). The program bounds repeated growth by (1+q)^H and adds
an absolute underflow reserve based on k*2^-1074 per coordinate operation.
The final dot product is also charged. The H=8 allowance is approximately
4.95e-15 for the rank-three run and 9.99e-16 for the rank-one run. Normal
finite IEEE arithmetic is used; no exact-real deployment is presumed.

This is a prediction theorem for arbitrary supplied later words. It does
not construct an efficient optimizer over the exponential space of words.
Any future planner or control search still has to be executed and paid.

5. Bounded attempts and complete failure scope
=============================================

The implementation stops successfully only when the acquired bound, including
numerical evaluation, is no larger than the requested epsilon. Otherwise it
refines eta by a factor of four, preserving the entire bill and trace.

Declared limits are:
  - at most 24 acquisition stages;
  - at most 12 actual landmarks;
  - at most 10^17 stationary preparations in one direct attempt;
  - at most 200,000 projected-subgradient iterations per fit;
  - at most 80 lower-frame-floor checks;
  - the source's signed-64-bit binomial batch capacity and binary64 coin
    precision, checked before they can invalidate a claim.

Before executing a Gram stage, its complete preparation charge is calculated.
If it would exceed the preparation cap, that stage is not run and the attempt
returns failure. The prior spent work is still returned. With three controls
and the prefix-connected landmark set, the largest permitted pool has 37
literal words and return depth at most 24. Thus the preparation cap also
bounds primitive controls by 24*10^17 and endpoint readings by 2*10^17.
Actual observed bills are far below these caps.

No expected-work theorem is claimed for an unbounded direct refinement
procedure. A conditional finite-rank progress argument is not an expected
runtime proof, especially off the confidence event. The bounded direct
attempt may precede the earlier finite-work teacher construction as a
fallback. Such a portfolio pays all failed direct work and splits a common
failure budget between both learners; it cannot reuse the standalone .01
budgets and silently retain an overall .01 guarantee. The results below are
standalone runs, each with total failure budget .01.

7. Interpretation and relation to earlier capital
=================================================

This ordinary learner wins the head-to-head example. It should be presented
constructively as the direct route, not hidden behind a weak comparator.
The teacher-to-Euclidean theorem remains useful as a general conversion with
no unknown positive-Gram-floor cost, and as a possible bounded-attempt
fallback. It is an expensive route on this particular fixture.

The new direct operation does acquire a measured stability geometry and the
frame on which its task consequence depends. It pays for making that frame
useful rather than supplying it. The normalization factor 1/sigma is visible
and can be arbitrarily expensive on other sources. No general rank-polynomial
sample claim or condition-number-free direct-discovery claim follows.

There is also a decisive ordinary TABLE comparator for the declared small
horizon. Enumerate all J=sum_{h=0}^8 3^h=9,841 words and directly estimate
G(empty,w) for each. Uniform Hoeffding confidence at epsilon=.5 and delta=.01,
using the same outward integer logarithm and the same 2^-52 coin-bias reserve,
requires only 169 reports per word. Store their exact rational empirical
means. This constructs a predictor for every length-at-most-eight word with
the same requested uniform guarantee and no learned operator matrices.

Its complete analytical bill is:
  1,663,129 stationary preparations;
  12,474,228 primitive controls;
  3,326,258 endpoint readings;
  9,841 scalar jobs, maximum return depth eight;
  9,841 retained scalar predictions instead of three Euclidean coordinates.

The function acquire_exhaustive_table implements this comparator. Its bill
was first calculated in direct_baseline_table_bill.json and then one complete
source run was executed by table_baseline.py. The exact-count model contains
all 9,841 predictions. A truth audit performed only after acquisition found
maximum error .27076923076923076 over the entire declared word set; the
probabilistic guarantee remains epsilon=.5, delta=.01. The executed report
and finite-count model are table_baseline_results.json and
table_baseline_model.json. This comparator is far cheaper than BOTH
compact learners on the H=8 task. Accordingly the fixture demonstrates the
acquisition of compact compositional machinery and a direct win over an
expensive representation route; it does not demonstrate the cheapest complete
acquisition/execution cost for the declared small-horizon prediction task.
The table's storage and acquisition grow exponentially with horizon, while
the direct compact route's measured-frame cost can grow polynomially with
precision/horizon on favorable instances. That larger-horizon advantage is
not an operational achievement established by the current H=8 experiment.

Both learners are ordinary learners retaining knowledge. Neither these
results nor the earlier rank-two exact ordinary win establish a privileged
architecture advantage. All earlier acquired-operation, physical-return,
rank-two l1 obstruction, and fully charged recovery-control conclusions remain
unchanged. The weighted-return interface still requires its own report-range,
preparation and common-stability justification; the common-stationary bounded
case here cannot be exported to it for free.

Reproduce the main runs:
  python geometry_learning/notes/direct_baseline.py

Outputs are direct_baseline_results.json and direct_baseline_models.json.
The matched run is preserved separately as direct_baseline_matched_results.json
and direct_baseline_matched_model.json. It calls run_case with mixing=.4,
horizon=8, seed=20261102, and epsilon=.27368364266349654. The models preserve
actual word names, stored numerical matrices, and every raw binomial batch
(word pair, number of reports, count of positive reports), so failed stages
and exact recorded empirical moments can be reconstructed.

8. Numerical correction preserved
=================================

The first diagnostic used math.log followed by math.ceil for shot counts.
Although its counts were numerically conservative to displayed precision,
that implementation did not prove outward logarithm rounding. An independent
review identified this as a certification gap. Both root and direct schedules
were replaced by the exact rational/integer upper schedule described above,
and the two main sources plus the one matched comparison were rerun.

Superseded mixed-source preparation counts were 19,240,310,406,237 for the
direct route and 782,911,196,463,435,869 for the teacher route; the matched
direct count was 310,341,384,251,852. These are not the final certified bills.
The current tables and JSONs use 27,064,840,963,173; 1,081,795,391,722,246,958;
and 433,073,468,347,808 respectively. The qualitative comparison survived the
correction. No new source family or optional experimental extension was added.
