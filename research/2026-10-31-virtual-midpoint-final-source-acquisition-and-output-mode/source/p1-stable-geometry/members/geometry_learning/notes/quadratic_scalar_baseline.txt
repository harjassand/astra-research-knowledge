TASK-SENSITIVE DIRECT ACQUISITION: SCALAR ERROR PAYS FOR LEAVING AND RETURNING
31 October 2026. Companion to quadratic_scalar_baseline.py.

Finding
=======

The retaining-table win exposed an unnecessarily strong objective in the
previous compact learner: controlling the entire physical state vector. For
scalar tasks whose initial state and readout lie in an acquired subspace,
self-adjoint physical controls give a sharper certificate. A scalar error
requires a departure from that subspace and a return. The physical leakage
contribution is quadratic in the normalized subspace residual.

The new direct learner acquires its actual landmarks, their Gram geometry,
their conditioning, the transition matrices and the squared leakage bound
from paid successor-pool returns. It does not assume a global rank. It uses
ordinary spectral and control calculations constructively; no architecture
advantage is claimed. The result is a task-sensitive representation theorem
and an executable same-source acquisition, not a minimum-cost theorem.

1. Scalar compression lemma
===========================

Let P be an orthogonal projection, let ||f||<=1 and Pf=f, and let each T_a be
a self-adjoint contraction. Define

  e_a = ||(I-P) T_a P||op.

For a word w=(a_1,...,a_H), compare the true product with stepwise compression
S_a=P T_a P. A telescoping expansion has one term for each first departure
from the projected prefix:

  <f,T_H...T_1 f> - <f,S_H...S_1 f>
    = sum_j <f,T_H...T_{j+1}(I-P)T_j S_{j-1}...S_1 f>.

The right departure in term j has norm at most e_{a_j}. The left vector's
component outside P has norm at most sum_{i>j} e_{a_i}: reverse the suffix,
use self-adjointness, and telescope its distance from the stepwise projected
suffix. All intermediate products contract. Consequently

  |scalar error| <= sum_{j<i} e_{a_j} e_{a_i}
                 <= H(H-1) e^2/2,                            (1)

where e=max_a e_a. The same proof works for distinct unit-norm seed/readout
f,g when both belong to P. Common stationarity alone is insufficient for
this self-adjoint version. More general controls need separately acquired
backward leakage for their actual adjoints; that additional access and its
cost are not silently assumed here.

The actual four-state source has symmetric Markov kernels and a common
stationary law, so the self-adjoint contraction premise is physically valid.
The subspace P will be the span of actual acquired raw-word landmarks. It
contains the actual seed exactly, rather than only approximately.

2. Finite observations, relative coordinates and their numerical check
=====================================================================

For independent actual landmarks V=[v_1,...,v_n], including the seed, acquire
from one actual successor pool the blocks

  G=V*V, K_a=V*T_a V, D_a=(T_a V)*(T_a V).

One operator-norm confidence bound eta for the pool Gram controls all these
blocks. Symmetrize the noisy K estimate; since true K is symmetric, its
operator error remains at most eta. Independence is checked by exact rational
positive definiteness of Ghat-eta I, not supplied as a basis promise.

Let R be an invertible rational coordinate proposal. The code obtains it as
the exact rational inverse of a dyadic Cholesky proposal for Ghat. It checks

  B0=R*Ghat R,
  nu >= ||B0-I||op,
  z >= eta ||R||op^2,
  t=nu+z <= 1/2.                                            (2)

All operator-norm upper bounds use a numerical SVD only as a proposal; exact
rational reconstruction and orthogonality residuals certify the bound.
Thus E=R*G R satisfies ||E-I||<=t. The inaccessible Q=V R E^{-1/2} is an
isometry onto the actual landmark span. It is only used in the proof.

For Atilde_a=R*Khat_sym R, the true compressed operator in this aligned gauge
is A_a^*=E^{-1/2}(R*K_a R)E^{-1/2}, a self-adjoint contraction. Its difference
from Atilde satisfies

  ||A_a^*-Atilde_a|| <= 3t.                                 (3)

For completeness, writing B=R*K_aR=E^{1/2}A_a^*E^{1/2}, the square-root
perturbation contribution is at most

  t [sqrt(1+t)+1]/[1+sqrt(1-t)] <= 1.5t

when t<=1/2. The observation contribution is at most z<=t. Thus 3t is a
conservative convenient bound. No unknown minimum positive Gram eigenvalue
is supplied: large ||R|| is measured and increases the required acquisition
precision and physical sampling charge.

The stored matrices A_a are symmetric dyadic contractions, obtained by
symmetric spectral clipping followed by exact norm certification and inward
rounding. Let d_a be the checked norm ||A_a-Atilde_a||. Then

  ||A_a-A_a^*|| <= 3t+d_a.                                  (4)

3. Acquired squared leakage, without taking a noisy square root
==============================================================

Set

  C_a=B0^{-1}Atilde_a,
  Dtilde_a=R*Dhat_a R,
  Rhat_a=Dtilde_a-Atilde_a C_a.

These matrices and inverses are calculated as exact rationals. Rhat is
symmetric. Consider the actual candidate residual T_a V R-V R C_a. Its
squared Gram differs from Rhat by at most

  z(1+||C_a||)^2.                                           (5)

This follows by expanding the two cross terms and the Gram term. Separate
block error bounds suffice even after symmetrizing Khat. There is no need
to presume that the noisy block matrix itself is positive semidefinite.

Projecting this candidate residual outside P can only decrease its norm.
Normalizing by E>= (1-t)I therefore proves

  e_a^2 <= [lambda_max(Rhat_a)+z(1+||C_a||)^2]/(1-t).         (6)

The implementation uses a checked ||Rhat_a||op in place of lambda_max, a
conservative upper bound that avoids an unchecked eigenvalue sign. This
confidence upper bound is linear in the raw moment error. It does not first
estimate a global state-vector error and then square an asserted estimate.

4. Seed, deployment and completed scalar certificate
====================================================

Let b0=R^{-1}e_seed. The actual seed coordinate is b_true=E^{1/2}b0 and has norm
at most one. For t<=1/2,

  ||E^{-1/2}-I||<=t,

so ||b0-b_true||<=t. The code stores a certified unit-ball dyadic seed b and
checks d_b=||b-b0||. Combining (1), (4) and the two endpoint seed replacements
gives the acquired all-word bound

  |<f,T_w f>-b^T A_w b|
    <= binom(|w|,2) max_a e_a^2
       + |w| max_a(3t+d_a) + 2(t+d_b).                       (7)

An explicit binary64 matrix-vector/dot-product roundoff allowance is added
before checking the requested task tolerance. It uses the same norm-stable
bound and underflow reserve as direct_baseline.py. At H=64 that allowance is
3.73e-14. All comparisons of the total bound against epsilon are rational.

The fixed 1e-7 stored-matrix rounding budget is adequate for these displayed
tasks. Arbitrarily smaller requested errors require refining that budget and
the proposal/report arithmetic, or returning explicit bounded failure. The
current binary64 and source-coin limits are not advertised as an arbitrary-
precision implementation.

5. Actual acquisition and complete work scope
============================================

Start with the empty-word landmark. At every stage, execute a fresh complete
Gram experiment on W union {w a}. First attempt the scalar certificate (7).
If it does not pass, use the acquired pool factor to propose an actual new
successor and certify physical independence by Ghat[J,J]-eta I>0. If no
useful new landmark passes, refine eta by four and pay another stage.

The learner never receives source matrices, latent coordinates, a useful
frame, global rank, or a planner. It only receives the physical source
conditions and task H,epsilon. It is capped at 24 stages, 12 landmarks and
10^17 preparations. Each planned stage is charged before execution and is
rejected if it would exceed that cap. Failed certificate stages remain in
the saved trace. No expected-work claim for an unbounded adaptive search is
inferred from a conditional finite-rank argument.

Stage s receives exact delta/[s(s+1)], and sampling uses the root's outward
integer/rational Hoeffding-net schedule, including exact coin-bias reserve.
The guarantee is joint soundness:

  P(any returned certificate is false) <= delta.

It is not a claim that the conditional error probability given acceptance
is necessarily delta. In the warm run, both accepted horizons share one
original confidence budget and continued stage indices.

At a fixed acquired exact-invariant frame, bounded conditioning and fixed
pool size, the dominant squared-leak uncertainty is O(eta). Hence scalar
accuracy needs eta=O(epsilon/H^2), rather than the O(epsilon^2/H^2) required
by the global-vector coupling route. The conservative report count scales
as H^4 epsilon^-2, with explicit conditioning, pool-size, confidence and
physical-depth factors. This local cost statement does not erase basis
search, failed acquisition, norm weights, or numerical arithmetic. When the
actual subspace has nonzero leakage, its irreducible term also remains.

6. Executed H8 and one new H64 task
==================================

Both use the same mixed-control four-state source, epsilon=.5, and standalone
failure budget .01. Three actual words were discovered: (), (2), (2,0).
No global rank was supplied. Maximum native return depth stayed six.

  H=8:
    six paid stages; three learned coordinates; 258 scalar jobs;
    1,689,301,760,258 preparations;
    6,081,268,320,879 primitive controls;
    3,378,603,520,516 endpoint readings;
    all-word certificate 0.24575992547793948.

    Its error terms are .199797196343 (quadratic leakage),
    .042465214307 (estimated operators), .003497514828 (endpoints),
    plus 4.95e-15 (binary64 execution). The acquired e^2 upper bound is
    .007135614155 and relative Gram t is .001748735062.

  H=64:
    nine paid stages; the same three coordinates; 423 scalar jobs;
    7,076,851,016,432,793 preparations;
    25,476,663,441,142,005 primitive controls;
    14,153,702,032,865,586 endpoint readings;
    all-word certificate 0.23059191452012578.

    Its error terms are .225246819135 (quadratic leakage),
    .005290404468 (estimated operators), .000054690916 (endpoints),
    plus 3.73e-14 (binary64 execution). The acquired e^2 bound is
    .000111729573 and relative Gram t is .000027323106.

There are 5,150,525,730,438,768,726,986,773,633,921 literal words through H=64.
The compact certificate covers all of them; they were not enumerated during
learning or verification. Post-acquisition heldout audits found maximum
task errors 6.55e-5 and 3.89e-6 respectively. These diagnostics do not replace
the all-word certificate.

The H8 route improves preparation cost by roughly a factor of 16 over the
preceding global-vector compact calibration, while certifying tighter error.
The ordinary retaining H8 table still wins: 1,663,129 preparations and
12,474,228 controls for the same requested .5 task. This win is preserved.
At H64 the explicit literal table is exponentially large, but this does not
establish superiority to every strong spectral, PSR or task-sensitive learner:
the construction itself is an ordinary such learner. Nor are quadrillions
of physical preparations a practical experimental result.

7. Explicit cumulative continuation, using retained capital
=========================================================

The separate --warm operation uses ONE source object and ONE delta=.01
allocation. It first acquires H8, retains its exact Gram means, stages,
landmarks, geometry and stored model, and re-evaluates those exact observations
for H64. Their H64 bound is 14.72861736596122, so old precision is insufficient.
It then executes only refinement stages 7,8,9. Their confidence allocations
are exactly 1/5600, 1/7200 and 1/9000, continuing the original schedule.

Marginal H8->H64 bill:
  7,075,161,714,672,535 preparations;
  25,470,582,172,821,126 primitive controls;
  14,150,323,429,345,070 endpoint readings;
  165 new scalar batch jobs.

Total cost equals the independent H64 report above. The old queries are not
executed a second time in this warm operation. Finer stages use fresh cohorts
whose full costs are included; the learner does not claim a statistically
optimal pooling of old and new precision cohorts.

There are no new raw-word types, no new ordered return-pair descriptors, no
new unordered Gram pairs, and no greater return depth. Ten word types and
depth six suffice throughout the continuation. The old history contains
60 ordered (u,v) experiment descriptors because earlier pool orderings used
both orientations of five pairs; the final ten-word Gram has 55 unordered
entries. Both distinctions are explicit in the metadata and every ordered
experiment remains paid.

The acquired Gram tolerance changes from .00015625 to .00000244140625, a
factor of 64. Maximum denominator lengths of the recorded empirical means
change from 35 to 47 bits. Selected matrix-entry rational widths are tracked
separately from final-bound arithmetic: the H8 and H64 final bound fractions
have 2,698 and 10,148 bits respectively. These labels are not a claim to have
instrumented the maximum bit length of every temporary arithmetic object.

The warm raw-batch prefix exactly matches the preserved independent H8
experiment. Its final raw batches and stored matrices/seed exactly match the
independent H64 experiment. This verifies the continuation against the
separate executions without another source family or horizon.

On this machine the warm aggregate simulator used .08818 seconds for initial
acquisition and .05805 seconds for the continuation, including the retained-
data certificate check. End-to-end time before serialization was .15813
seconds. These CPU figures are NOT the physical time represented by the
preparation/control/readout bills.

8. Reproduction, files and limits
================================

Reproduce the independent comparisons:
  python geometry_learning/notes/quadratic_scalar_baseline.py

This command took approximately 7.70 seconds in the recorded invocation,
including post-acquisition diagnostics and serialization. It writes
quadratic_scalar_baseline_results.json and quadratic_scalar_baseline_models.json.

Then reproduce explicit cumulative reuse:
  python geometry_learning/notes/quadratic_scalar_baseline.py --warm

The recorded command completed in approximately .16 seconds; its internal
end-to-end timer was .158 seconds. It writes quadratic_scalar_warm_results.json and
quadratic_scalar_warm_model.json. The latter preserves retained exact Gram
means, old/final stored models, the full stage history and all raw batch counts.

Earlier direct, teacher, phase and table results remain preserved. The new
bound changes which prediction error is acquired; it does not revoke their
proofs, ordinary-learner wins, representation obstruction, source requirements
or numerical corrections. Weighted-return settings still need their own
stability/adjoint access and report-range accounting. No global cheap-basis
theorem, free long-horizon precision, or efficient arbitrary planner is claimed.
