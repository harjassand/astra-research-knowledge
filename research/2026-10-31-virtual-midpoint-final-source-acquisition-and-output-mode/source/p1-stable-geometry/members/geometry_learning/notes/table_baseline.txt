RETAINING TABLES: DECISIVE COMPARATORS AND A PRESERVED CORRECTION
31 October 2026. See table_baseline.py and its saved reports/models.

1. One executed table on the same four-state source
==================================================

The declared task is prediction of every word of length at most H=8 over
three controls, within epsilon=1/2, with failure probability at most 1/100.
There are J=sum_{h=0}^8 3^h=9,841 words. An ordinary learner can simply request
G(empty,w) for every word and retain its empirical mean. The source is the
identical common-stationary endpoint-product source used by both compact
learners; no hidden state, basis, rank or operator matrix is consulted.

The report range is [-1,1]. Reserve the source simulator's at-most-2^-52
report-mean bias. An exact integer bound on ln(2J/delta) is
ceil(log2(2J/delta))=21. Hence

  n = ceil[2*21/(epsilon-2^-52)^2] = 169

reports per word suffice by Hoeffding and a union bound. The table stores
exact rational means (2p-n)/n, so no prediction-rounding allowance is hidden.
The common denominator is 169; each positive-report count lies in [0,169]
and fits in one byte if packed. This uses 9,841 scalar entries, compared with
the rank-three compositional model's small matrices.

Complete executed bill:
  stationary preparations: 1,663,129;
  primitive controls: 12,474,228;
  endpoint readings: 3,326,258;
  scalar jobs: 9,841;
  maximum return depth: eight;
  later source calls for declared tasks: zero.

The primitive bill uses sum_{h=0}^8 h*3^h=73,812, multiplied by 169. Every
lookup can compute its breadth-first lexicographic array index in O(|w|)
integer operations. Both entry storage and output precision are explicit.

After acquisition, an exhaustive source-truth audit over all 9,841 words found
maximum error 0.27076923076923076. This observed error does not replace the
acquired .5-confidence guarantee. Truth was never used by the acquiring
routine. The saved model contains all 9,841 integer counts, sufficient to
reconstruct every exact empirical mean and all job counts.

For comparison, the corrected direct compact calibration uses
27,064,840,963,173 preparations and 97,433,209,451,373 controls. Its attained
certificate .372444328386 is tighter than the table's requested .5 guarantee,
but both meet the same declared task. The table wins complete task acquisition
cost by approximately 16.27 million in preparations and 7.81 million in
primitive controls. These are comparisons of these implemented conservative
calibrations, not information-theoretic lower bounds on compact learning.

The correction is substantive: the H=8 fixture demonstrates acquisition of
small stable compositional machinery, not minimum total task cost. Both the
large teacher dictionary and the direct compact route must be compared with
this ordinary retaining learner. A memory-size or future-horizon advantage
must be separately earned; it cannot be silently substituted for the stated
finite-horizon task.

2. Analytical table for the explicit reflection family
======================================================

The companion phase construction assumes the known family
  A f_k=f_{-k}, B f_k=f_{1-k}, <f_k,f_l>=cos((k-l)theta),
with unknown theta. The table comparator uses precisely that same promise.
For every primitive word of length at most H, its integer phase index satisfies
|k(w)|<=ceil(H/2), and it is computed exactly by k->-k or k->1-k. Cosine is
even. Thus at H=256 only c_1,...,c_128 must be acquired; c_0=1 is the known
normalization. Enumerating 2^H words would be an unfairly weak comparator.

Use the same physical endpoint reports in [-2,2], rounded to ten fractional
bits. Their deterministic rounding bias is at most b=2^-11. The retained
mean of rounded reports is an exact rational. For epsilon=1/50 and delta=1/100,
the statistical allowance is

  epsilon-b = 999/51200.

There are 128 independently queried moment types. A rigorous rational upper
bound on ln(2*128/delta) is (7/10)*15=21/2: ceil_log2 gives 15, and ln2<7/10
follows, for example, from a positive Taylor lower sum proving exp(7/10)>2.
The required per-moment sample count is therefore

  n = ceil[8*(21/2)/(999/51200)^2] = 220,643.

No new phase-source run was performed; this is a constructive complete-cost
calculation from the same physical report contract and family promise.

The actual word B(AB)^(j-1) reaches phase index j in 2j-1 primitive reflections.
Its report has the requisite cosine mean. Therefore:

  preparations: 28,242,304;
  endpoint readings: 56,484,608;
  scalar jobs: 128;
  shortest-word primitive reflections: 3,615,014,912;
  maximum shortest-word return depth: 255;
  retained entries including known c0: 129.

If one uses the existing PaidSource.mean(j) API, which executes a length-2j
rotation word instead, the control bill is 3,643,257,216 and depth is 256.
Both bills are preserved in phase_table_baseline_bill.json. With preparation,
reading and primitive unit costs all equal to one, the respective complete
totals are 3,699,741,824 and 3,727,984,128.

The preserved theta=2 atan(1/64) phase diagnostic uses 32,993,880 preparations,
65,987,760 endpoint readings and 98,499,424,956 primitive reflections, with
unit-cost total 98,598,406,596. This ordinary moment table beats that selected
calibration in each physical resource, not only in the optional unit-cost
sum. For the near-zero rank-one phase diagnostics, the table uses more
preparations/readings but fewer controls; those resource vectors should not
be collapsed without a specified cost convention.

The phase algorithm has a better asymptotic primitive-depth law as horizon
grows under its family assumptions. Its implemented constants do not produce
a finite-cost win at the displayed H=256 experiment. The task table's
O(H^2 epsilon^-2 log(H/delta)) primitive scaling and O(H) stored moments are
part of the strong ordinary comparison. Future horizon extensions can retain
old moments, but newly required moments and confidence allocation are paid.

3. Scope
========

The first comparison is an actual same-source run; the second is a proved
analytical bill. Neither is a new application or additional source family.
They preserve the operation-rule and geometry theorems while correcting an
overly favorable efficiency interpretation of their selected demonstrations.
They also identify a concrete next issue: global vector-normalization loss
can be much stronger than the scalar future tasks actually require.
