ACQUIRE THE GEOMETRY NEEDED BY THE OUTPUT TASK
31 October 2026. Ordinary proofs and a finite acquisition operation.

This is the main task-sensitive refinement of PROOF.txt. It uses a direct
retaining spectral learner, rather than first acquiring an l1 dictionary.
The geometric idea is established projection/duality reasoning. No historical
priority, architecture separation, practical sample efficiency, or general
polynomial-in-rank acquisition theorem is claimed. The new executable is
notes/quadratic_scalar_baseline.py. Its saved ledgers include unsuccessful
stages, all physical repetitions, actual return lengths and numerical checks.

1. TASK AND PHYSICAL CONTRACT

There are m unknown self-adjoint contractions T_a on one real Hilbert space.
The seed f satisfies ||f||<=1. A later task supplies any word w of length at
most H and requests the scalar <f,T_w f>, to additive error epsilon. A single
accepted model must work for every such word, including words chosen after
seeing the acquisition data. It need not identify the full hidden state,
every trajectory law, conditional rare events, or a best control word.

For actual words u,v, the learner can obtain independent or conditionally
unbiased scalar reports of <T_u f,T_v f>, with a known bounded range. A known
digitization or calibration bias can be reserved separately. These reports
are acquired by actual physical experiments. Neither synthetic hidden-state
preparation nor a normalized vector-query oracle is present.

A sufficient realization is a finite menu of reversible Markov kernels with
the SAME stationary distribution pi, stationary preparation, and bounded
observable f. Each primitive is self-adjoint and contractive in L2(pi).
A chronological return word realizes the inner product with |u|+|v| actual
primitives and two endpoint readings. All preparations and primitives are
paid even when the resulting report is deterministic. The finite-state
fixture has f in {-1,+1}, so every report has magnitude one.
With the code convention T_w f=T_(last)...T_(first)f, a chronological
Markov experiment executing u followed by reverse(v) has the required mean.

This is narrower than Astra's weighted-adjoint interface. Differently weighted
controls do not automatically have one common contractive Hilbert norm.
Known report weights must still be paid at their actual range. In a genuinely
nonself-adjoint common-contraction case, a two-sided variant needs separate
forward AND adjoint leakage certificates; see section 2. A fitted model does
not certify any of these physical source premises.

2. THE OUTPUT SEES AN EXCURSION ONLY AFTER IT RETURNS

Let P be an orthogonal projection and P_perp=I-P. For a sequence of arbitrary
contractions T_1,...,T_H, put

    a_i=||P_perp T_i P||,
    b_i=||P T_i P_perp||=||P_perp T_i* P||.

Then

 ||P T_H...T_1 P - (P T_H P)...(P T_1 P)||
       <= sum_(1<=i<j<=H) a_i b_j.                         (1)

Proof. Start at x in range(P), ||x||<=1. Write the true trajectory as
p_t+q_t, with p_t in range(P), q_t in its orthogonal complement. Its norm
never exceeds one. The outside recursion gives

    ||q_t|| <= a_t ||p_(t-1)|| + ||q_(t-1)||
             <= sum_(i<=t) a_i.

The projected comparison y_t=(P T_t P)y_(t-1) obeys

    ||p_t-y_t|| <= ||p_(t-1)-y_(t-1)|| + b_t ||q_(t-1)||.

Summing proves (1), uniformly over x. Equivalently, the exact telescoping
identity in notes/quadratic_leakage.md separates first departure and later
return. Commutativity is unnecessary.

If each primitive is self-adjoint, a_i=b_i=e_i. For unit f,g in range(P),

 |<g,T_H...T_1 f>-<g,(P T_H P)...(P T_1 P) f>|
       <= sum_(i<j) e_i e_j <= H(H-1)e^2/2,                (2)

where e=max_i e_i. The omitted-vector error is generally first order in e;
this particular scalar output error is second order. In-span operator fitting
errors and numerical execution errors remain first order.

For endpoints not wholly in the span, let alpha=||P_perp f|| and
beta=||P_perp g||. The complete bound is

 ||Pf|| ||Pg|| sum_(i<j) a_i b_j
 + beta ||Pf|| sum_i a_i + alpha ||Pg|| sum_i b_i + alpha beta.  (3)

Thus the containment of the actual task endpoints matters. The acquisition
below retains the actual seed as a landmark; it does not estimate away this
condition. Other readouts need acquired containment or the terms in (3).

The H^2 e^2 dependence can be attained inside the bounded physical interface.
Let the state be uniform (x1,x2) in {-1,+1}^2, f=x1, and
T_e=(1-e)I+e SwapCoordinates, with 0<=e<=1/2. Project onto f. The leakage is
exactly e, the true H-step scalar is [1+(1-2e)^H]/2, and the compressed scalar
is (1-e)^H. Their nonnegative difference D_H satisfies

 choose(H,2)e^2(1-e)^(H-2) <= D_H <= choose(H,2)e^2.        (4)

For the lower bound, expand by the number of swaps and retain exactly two;
the upper bound is (2). This is sharpness of a projection inequality, not a
statistical lower bound: an ordinary learner can exploit that known family.

Forward leakage alone cannot justify a square. With P projecting onto e1 and
T=[[0,1],[e,0]], ||P_perp T P||=e but ||P T P_perp||=1. The length-two scalar
error is e. Weighted adjoint access, where warranted, must acquire and pay for
the missing reverse control; it cannot be supplied by a change of notation.

3. DISCOVER ACTUAL LANDMARKS AND PAY FOR A POOL GRAM

Let W=(w_1,...,w_n) be the currently retained actual words, with w_1 empty.
Their unknown feature columns are V=[T_(w_1)f,...,T_(w_n)f]. Form the literal
pool Z=W union {wa:w in W,a in the menu}, removing literal duplicates only.
The learner starts with the seed, not a useful basis or a rank promise.

Acquire a fresh symmetric pool Gram estimate Gamma_hat with

    ||Gamma_hat-Gamma|| <= eta.                            (5)

For each primitive, extract the blocks

    G=V*V, K_a=V*T_a V, D_a=(T_a V)*(T_a V)=V*T_a^2 V.

Every corresponding estimated block has operator error <=eta, because row
and column selections are isometries. The same-action successor words are
distinct. Symmetrizing the K estimate does not enlarge its error since the
true K is symmetric. The full pool, not unmeasured normalized states, supplies
all three blocks.

Append an actual successor only after its enlarged principal estimated Gram
minus eta I is certified positive definite. This proves physical independence
on (5). The current implementation uses a spectral factor to order candidate
successors; that ordering has no authority to certify independence. If no
candidate or task certificate passes, refine eta and pay for a new stage.

At stage s use delta_s=delta/[s(s+1)]. Conditional on the previously selected
pool, bounded independent reports and the sphere-net argument in PROOF.txt
give (5) with failure probability at most delta_s. The union event covers
every adaptively selected basis, all certificates and every later word.
No extra confidence is obtained by selecting the best-looking fit.

For p pool words and known report magnitudes B_ij, a sufficient fresh count is

    N_ij=ceil(16 B_ij^2 eta_stat^(-2)
                   [p log(9)+log(2/delta_s)]),             (6)

where eta_stat=eta-p b if b bounds each entry's systematic mean error.
It must be positive. Bias is not reduced by repetition. The code uses exact
integer/rational bounds log(9)<3 and log(x)<=ceil(log2(x)), and reserves a
2^-52 mean error for its binary64 simulation coin. Reports themselves are
binary; measured means are retained as exact rational count/total pairs.

4. CONSTRUCT AND CHECK THE NUMERICAL GEOMETRY

Use the measured positive-definite G_hat to propose ANY invertible rational
matrix R. A numerical whitener is convenient, but its correctness is checked.
Define the exact rational matrix and certified scalar bounds

    B0=R^T G_hat R,
    nu >= ||B0-I||,
    z >= eta ||R||^2,
    t=nu+z <= 1/2.                                        (7)

If (7) fails, reject/refine; do not assume a favorable frame floor. On (5),
the unknown actual metric E=R^T G R obeys ||E-I||<=t. It is positive definite,
so these n physical landmarks are independent. For proof only,

    Q=V R E^(-1/2)                                        (8)

is an isometry onto their actual span. The learner never receives Q or E.
Its stored Euclidean metric has been related to the physical metric by the
measured inequality (7), and ||R||^2 records its conditioning price.

The executable takes an ordinary binary64 Cholesky proposal U, interprets its
entries as exact dyadic rationals, and sets R=U^(-1) by exact rational
elimination. Exact norm and positivity checks then certify (7). A floating
factorization failure produces a charged unsuccessful attempt.

A universal finite alternative needs no trusted floating decomposition:
compute a rational LDL^T factorization of positive G_hat, approximate the
positive diagonal inverse square roots by rational bisection, and form R.
Check the desired nu explicitly. Norm upper bounds can be computed to any
positive additive tolerance by bisection on u and exact positive-definiteness
checks of u^2 I-M^T M. A rational upper bound on the Frobenius norm, plus a
positive rational margin, initializes the upper endpoint. A raw row-sum norm
alone is not a sufficient spectral bound for a general nonsymmetric M.
No unknown eigengap is used; equality can be classified as failure
of a strict upper certificate and a slightly larger rational endpoint used.
Precision and exact arithmetic bit lengths are part of the acquisition cost.

5. ACQUIRE STABLE OPERATORS AND THE SEED IN THESE COORDINATES

Set, using the symmetric K estimate,

    Atilde_a=R^T Khat_a R,    Astar_a=Q*T_a Q.

The latter is an unobserved self-adjoint contraction. Since

    R^T K_a R=E^(1/2) Astar_a E^(1/2),

the triangle inequality gives

    ||Atilde_a-Astar_a|| <= 3t.                            (9)

Details: for t<=1/2, ||E^(1/2)-I||<=t/(1+sqrt(1-t)); multiplying by
sqrt(1+t)+1 bounds the two-sided square-root perturbation by less than
1.305t. The measured K error is at most z<=t. Their sum is below 3t.

Construct and store a certified contraction A_a, and measure exactly

    d_a >= ||A_a-Atilde_a||.

Then its error against the true compression is at most 3t+d_a. The code
clips a symmetric spectral proposal, rounds and shrinks to a dyadic matrix,
and verifies the stored contraction and its exact residual. No unpriced
optimizer is needed. A simpler universal algorithm is radial scaling:
certify u>=||Atilde_a|| within xi, and set A_a=Atilde_a/max(1,u).
It is a rational symmetric contraction and d_a<=3t+xi. This gives an explicit
finite arithmetic construction with arbitrarily small numerical allowance.

Let e0 select the actual seed column. Compute b0=R^(-1)e0. The true coordinates
are bstar=Q*f=E^(1/2)b0 and ||bstar||<=1. Hence

    ||b0-bstar|| <= ||E^(-1/2)-I|| <= t.                   (10)

The last inequality holds for 0<=t<=1/2; in particular
1/sqrt(1-t)-1<=t. Store any certified unit-ball vector b with measured
d_b>=||b-b0||. Its seed/readout error is at most t+d_b. Unit-ball scaling
using a certified norm bound is another finite rational operation. The code
uses a checked dyadic unit-ball projection proposal.

The program's fixed 1e-7 contraction-proposal rounding allowance is adequate
for its recorded H<=64, epsilon=1/2 tasks. It is not an arbitrary-accuracy
promise: a general finer task must refine that allowance too, or return failure.

6. MEASURE SQUARED LEAKAGE WITHOUT TAKING A NOISY SQUARE ROOT

Compute the exact rational quantities

    Dtilde_a=R^T Dhat_a R,
    C_a=B0^(-1) Atilde_a,
    Rhat_a=Dtilde_a-Atilde_a C_a.                          (11)

B0 is positive definite. Thus C_a minimizes the empirical matrix residual
quadratic, and Rhat_a is symmetric. Let

    r_a >= max(0,lambda_max(Rhat_a)),  c_a>=||C_a||.

The implementation uses the stronger, possibly conservative, upper bound
r_a>=||Rhat_a||. Its calculation is checked against the exact rational matrix,
not a previously rounded residual.

For the actual feature matrices, the residual at this computed C_a is

    F(C_a)=(T_a V R-V R C_a)*(T_a V R-V R C_a).

Expanding the four terms and applying (5) shows

    ||F(C_a)-Rhat_a|| <= z(1+c_a)^2.                     (12)

Here the two cross terms each contribute z c_a and the Gram term z c_a^2.
Projection onto the actual span minimizes the true residual. Moreover
V R=Q E^(1/2) and ||E^(-1/2)||^2<=1/(1-t). Therefore the actual normalized
leakage satisfies

    e_a^2 := ||(I-QQ*)T_a Q||^2
       <= [r_a+z(1+c_a)^2]/(1-t).                        (13)

This is the acquired operation certificate. The physical unknown Q only
justifies it. Every quantity on the right is measured or finitely certified.
The same global event (5) controls these data-dependent choices, so selecting
R, C_a and A_a from the data does not invalidate the confidence calculation.

7. THE COMPLETE SCALAR PREDICTION CERTIFICATE

For a later word w of length L, run y_0=b and y_(j+1)=A_(w_(j+1)) y_j, then
return b^T y_L. Put

    e2=max_a [r_a+z(1+c_a)^2]/(1-t),
    d=max_a (3t+d_a),    s=t+d_b.

The acquisition certificate is

 |<f,T_w f>-b^T A_w b|
       <= choose(L,2)e2 + L d + 2s.                      (14)

Proof. The true seed lies in the actual span. Apply (2) to the physical
compression, telescope the product of two families of contractions to obtain
L d, and replace its two unit-ball endpoint vectors at cost at most 2s.

Add a certified bound for the actual stored-model evaluation before accepting
epsilon. For entrywise fixed-point rounding at precision 2^-b, a sufficient
state-roundoff budget is L sqrt(n)2^-b, followed by a final inner-product
rounding budget; choose b from the requested error and observed n. The code
uses a conservative binary64 dot-product bound with an underflow reserve.
All accepted fixture matrices and seed vectors are exactly dyadic inputs.
One word needs O(L n^2) arithmetic and no new source calls. Arithmetic counts
are not a claim of constant-cost exact rational multiplication.

The exact code accepts only if its rational total, including the numerical
evaluation bound at H, is <=epsilon. It stores the full bound, all matrices,
the actual word descriptors and the retained report counts. No future-word
union bound is necessary. Statistical wording is joint soundness:

    Pr(any returned certificate is false) <= delta.

Because resource caps can return failure, this does not by itself say
Pr(false certificate | success)<=delta. A success-probability denominator
has not been supplied. For a shared warm sequence of accepted tasks, retain
the original global stage numbering and confidence allocation.

8. WHY SMALL CERTIFICATES ARE ATTAINABLE, AND WHAT REMAINS EXPENSIVE

Suppose the currently acquired physical span is exactly invariant. This is
used for a yield argument only, not for acceptance. The true coefficient
operator Cstar=E^(-1/2) Astar E^(1/2) has norm at most
sqrt((1+t)/(1-t)). Its true residual is zero. Empirical least squares thus
gives, in positive-semidefinite order,

 lambda_max(Rhat_a)
       <= z [1+sqrt((1+t)/(1-t))]^2
       <= (1+sqrt(3))^2 z.                               (15)

This is a consequence of the same measurement event, not a separate assumed
small-residual condition. For the implementation's norm-based Schur bound,
F(C_a)>=0 and (12) also give
lambda_min(Rhat_a)>=-z(1+||C_a||)^2. In addition
||C_a||<= (1+t+z)/(1-nu)<=4 when t<=1/2. Hence ||Rhat_a||<=25z
under exact closure. Sufficiently accurate norm calculations preserve an
O(z) bound, with their explicit additive numerical allowance.
An arbitrary conservative reported bound c_a need not itself be <=4; it may
be tightened by taking the minimum with the analytic bound just established.

Choosing a numerical whitener with nu=O(z), numerical operator/seed error
O(z), and z=O(epsilon/H^2), makes (14) O(epsilon). Since

    z >= eta ||R||^2,

the required Gram error is of order epsilon/[H^2 ||R||^2] at a fixed acquired
frame. This certificate needs moment accuracy linear in epsilon, whereas
using a first-order vector leakage bound with noisy squared residuals requires
accuracy of order epsilon^2. With bounded reports, the corresponding accuracy
dependence changes from epsilon^-4 to epsilon^-2. It does not remove the
H^4, report-range, pool-size or conditioning factors in the shot bill.

For p pool words, a sufficient stage preparation count is

 O(B^2 p^2 [p+log(1/delta_s)] ||R||^4 H^4 epsilon^-2),     (16)

under the stated closure/yield conditions and proportional numerical budgets.
Actual precision is selected and checked from observations; ||R|| is not a
supplied favorable constant. To this add every preceding discovery/refinement
stage and the exact physical cost in section 9.

On the joint good event and with growing finite numerical budgets, the
uncapped successor procedure eventually resolves any finite nonzero reachable
rank: each accepted addition increases true rank; at a fixed independent W,
either a successor is outside its span and sufficiently precise moments
certify it, or the span is invariant and (15) makes the scalar certificate
arbitrarily accurate. Unknown tiny frame floors and tiny new directions can
make this very expensive. A zero seed needs no predictor; an optional certified
small-norm stop follows from |<f,T_w f>|<=||f||^2. The concrete binary seed is
nonzero. No uniform rank-only acquisition bound follows from this argument.

Good-event eventual progress does not prove finite expected work on failed
confidence paths. The executed learner caps stages, landmarks, preparations
and certificate iterations; it can return a charged failure. A general
always-continuing fallback can make one bounded direct attempt, then run the
earlier guarded compiler and the representation conversion in PROOF.txt,
splitting its failure budget and paying both costs. The earlier finite-expected-
work theorem remains the justification for that fallback in its own source
class; it is not silently transferred to an uncapped new search.

9. PHYSICAL, NUMERICAL, FAILED-ACQUISITION AND LATER COSTS

For every attempted stage s, the exact primitive bill is

    sum_(i<=j) N_ij (|z_i|+|z_j|),                        (17)

plus the actual preparation and readout costs. There are sum N_ij preparations
and two readings per sample for the endpoint-product realization. The maximum
return depth and every report's weight bound are retained. For common bounded
reports this is at most 2 max_i |z_i| times the preparation count. For a
weighted interface B_ij may grow exponentially in depth; (6) pays B_ij^2.
No finite-moment bound on the number of landmarks automatically pays such
exponential weights.

The computational bill includes accumulation of counts, the p-by-p factor
proposal, successor independence tests, rational inversions, norm bisections,
contraction proposals, and final error certificates. Rational inversion costs
O(n^3) rational arithmetic operations by elimination, with operand bit lengths
charged separately. A norm certificate uses finitely many exact matrix tests;
its count depends logarithmically on the requested absolute norm tolerance.
Raw acquisition storage includes all word descriptors, measured counts and
failed-stage records. Runtime predictor storage is m n^2+n numbers.

The saved software fixtures use aggregate binomial sampling of the endpoint
law. It saves simulation CPU, not physical episodes. The H=64 acquisition
record's roughly 7.08e15 stationary preparations were NOT physically carried
out. Time, energy and monetary cost are not inferred from the simulated CPU
time. Numeric accounting records selected matrix entries of up to 434 bits
and a final error-bound fraction of up to 10148 bits in the H=64 diagnostic;
neither statistic measures every arithmetic intermediate or a global memory
peak. Those integers are not unit-cost machine scalars.

Finite tables and other retaining models are valid competitors. For the
H=8 fixture a directly measured table is much cheaper than this compact
learner. For H=64 enumerating all literal words is enormous, while this
acquired model covers them by one uniform certificate. This establishes the
capability and a comparison with enumeration, not optimality against every
spectral, predictive-state, control or family-specific algorithm.

10. WHAT THIS ADDS TO THE ACCUMULATED RESULT

The old finite-l1 obstruction belongs to the chosen representation. Actual
return observations can acquire a different stable finite numerical model.
The task-sensitive result further separates state fidelity from output
fidelity: an unresolved direction costs its entry times its return strength
for the endpoint task. That difference changes the required measurement
precision and the paid acquisition procedure, not merely its interpretation.

The measured Gram geometry, actual landmark descriptors, rational stability
certificate and retained report batches are reusable capital. Later words
within the certified task class require only numerical model execution. A
harder horizon can first reevaluate the existing certificate, then refine the
same acquired experiment set if needed; it must retain the old confidence
ledger and pay additional shots. The explicit warm H=8 to H=64 operation is
reported separately from the independent-run diagnostics.

Endpoint closure still does not identify all temporal questions. The separate
four-state task counterexample has identical endpoint-pair experiments and
different triple-event probabilities. That is an information-contract
obstruction, requiring an additional observation operation. It is not repaired
by a better coordinate norm. The original operation-rule theorem, weighted
return interface, ordinary learners' wins, rank-two obstruction and recovery-
control lifetime result remain unchanged in the preserved prior archive.

Established overlap: spectral observable-operator learning, local operator
loss optimization, constrained Koopman regression, and primal-dual output
error bounds all provide ingredients. Feng--Chellappa--Benner (2024) explicitly
review residual products for output error and their stability constants;
Harlim--Li (2015) give projection-memory identities and explain why evaluating
the resulting hidden memory is not automatically cheap. Here the proof is
elementary and independent. The examined claim is the finite acquisition,
certificate and cost contract at the stated physical information interface.
