### 1. The task-sensitive theorem

Let \(P\) project onto an acquired physical subspace containing the actual seed and readout. For contractions \(T_1,\ldots,T_H\), define the forward and reverse leakage

\[
a_i=\|(I-P)T_iP\|,
\qquad
b_i=\|PT_i(I-P)\|.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\left\|PT_H\cdots T_1P-
(PT_HP)\cdots(PT_1P)\right\|
\le \sum_{i<j}a_i b_j.
}
\]

The proof is short. The component outside the retained space after step \(t\) has norm at most \(\sum_{i\le t}a_i\). Its contribution back into the retained space at the next step is multiplied by that step’s reverse leakage. Summing those contributions gives the bound.

For self-adjoint primitives, \(a_i=b_i=e_i\). Therefore, for unit seed and readout in the retained space,

\[
\boxed{
\text{endpoint scalar error}
\le \sum_{i<j}e_i e_j
\le \binom H2 e^2.
}
\]

This dependence is attainable within the bounded-report physical interface. Take a uniform state \((x_1,x_2)\in\{\pm1\}^2\), observable \(f=x_1\), and the reversible Markov operation

\[
T_e=(1-e)I+e\,\mathrm{SwapCoordinates}.
\]

Projection onto \(f\) has leakage \(e\). The exact scalar response and projected response differ by

\[
D_H(e)=\frac{1+(1-2e)^H}{2}-(1-e)^H,
\]

with

\[
\binom H2e^2(1-e)^{H-2}
\le D_H(e)\le \binom H2e^2.
\]

Thus the quadratic bound is sharp to leading order. This example establishes sharpness of the projection bound; a learner exploiting this particular family can still do better than a generic acquisition procedure.

Two boundaries matter. Forward leakage alone does not justify the square: a contraction can have forward leakage \(e\), reverse leakage one, and length-two scalar error \(e\). Also, fitting errors in the retained operators and numerical execution errors remain first order.

The underlying projection and duality idea overlaps established output-error estimation. The work here is the acquisition procedure and finite certificate at the stated physical return interface. Primal-dual model-reduction research already obtains products of residuals, while retaining the relevant stability constants and access assumptions. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

### 2. The geometry and operations are acquired

The learner begins with the actual seed word. For its current landmarks \(W\), it forms the pool consisting of \(W\) and every one-step successor. It measures that pool’s Gram matrix through actual return experiments.

A candidate landmark is added only when an exact numerical certificate establishes its physical independence on the measurement-confidence event. The learner receives no rank, useful basis, Gram floor, or latent transition matrices.

From the measured pool it obtains estimates of

\[
G=V^*V,\qquad K_a=V^*T_aV,\qquad
D_a=(T_aV)^*(T_aV),
\]

where \(V\) contains the actual landmark features. It constructs a rational coordinate transform \(R\) and checks

\[
\|R^\top\widehat G R-I\|\le \nu,
\qquad
z\ge \eta\|R\|^2,
\qquad
t=\nu+z\le \tfrac12.
\]

Here \(\eta\) is the acquired Gram-error bound. **The factor \(\|R\|^2\) records the conditioning cost.** It is measured and paid, rather than assumed favorable.

An empirical Schur residual then certifies squared physical leakage. With

\[
B_0=R^\top\widehat G R,\quad
\widetilde A_a=R^\top\widehat K_aR,\quad
C_a=B_0^{-1}\widetilde A_a,
\]

the certificate is

\[
e_a^2\le
\frac{
\left\|R^\top\widehat D_aR-\widetilde A_aC_a\right\|
+z(1+\|C_a\|)^2
}{1-t}.
\]

All quantities on the right come from acquired observations or finite numerical checks. Stored matrices are certified contractions. The final acceptance test includes

\[
\boxed{
\binom H2\widehat e^2
+H\,\xi_{\mathrm{operator}}
+2\,\xi_{\mathrm{endpoint}}
+\xi_{\mathrm{execution}}
\le \epsilon.
}
\]

At a **fixed acquired invariant frame**, with proportional numerical precision, this needs moment accuracy of order

\[
\eta=O\!\left(\frac{\epsilon}{H^2\|R\|^2}\right).
\]

Consequently, the accuracy dependence of this bounded-report certification stage improves from \(\epsilon^{-4}\) to \(\epsilon^{-2}\). Pool size, conditioning, return depth, report range, and earlier discovery costs remain. This is not a uniform end-to-end rate when a harder task changes the acquired frame.

I adopted ordinary spectral and predictive-state methods as the construction.
