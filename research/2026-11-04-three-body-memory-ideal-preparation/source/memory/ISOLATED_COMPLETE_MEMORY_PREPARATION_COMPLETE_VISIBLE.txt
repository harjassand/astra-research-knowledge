A meaningful update on the memory: we now have an internally checked ideal preparation procedure for the complete isolated memory state, not just its auxiliaries. It combines a way to sample the required error patterns with the auxiliary preparation, and is designed to preserve a qubit entangled with another system. This closes a mathematical loading step I previously said was missing. It still assumes ideal active controls and extremely demanding fixed operating conditions. Reliable noisy writing, preparation together with the actual bath, and a practical advantage over active error correction remain unproved. The result has not been externally reviewed.
