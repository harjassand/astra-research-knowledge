Strong prior art and comparator boundaries

The closest algorithmic antecedent is Koehler–Lee–Risteski. Its classical low-rank Ising algorithm already uses constant-oscillation cells and statistical cell integration, rather than an accuracy-refined grid. I preserved a corresponding alternative for the quantum slow marginal. Gaussian quadrature is cleaner here because of the additional positive Laplace structure.

Three direct physical precedents materially narrow the novelty claim:

Malpetti–Roscilde, arXiv:1608.01205, equations (29)–(31), replaces intercluster temporal interactions with interactions between imaginary-time averages while retaining conditioned quantum paths. Retaining thermal fluctuations through static averages is established.
Okuyama–Ohzeki, arXiv:1808.09707, evaluates its “exact” static optimal-control solution in the thermodynamic limit. It does not supply arbitrary finite-
𝑛
n, absolute-log-normalizer accuracy.
Knysh, Nature Communications 7, 12370, already derives a low-dimensional dynamical particle description for competing valleys of the two-pattern Gaussian Hopfield model. Its effective kinetic term uses a slow-angular-mode expansion. Generic two-pattern examples therefore have a serious existing scientific comparator. Koh, arXiv:1803.08632, also develops dynamical-path finite-size corrections.

Generic mixture-tempering theorems retain minimum-component-weight costs. A formal Gaussian mixture over exponentially many spin histories does not satisfy those costs for free.
