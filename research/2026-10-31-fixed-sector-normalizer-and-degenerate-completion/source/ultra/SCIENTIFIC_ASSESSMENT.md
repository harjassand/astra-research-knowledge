## What changed

1. **One free-law candidate is decisively excluded.** The exact affine pullback of a known quasiconvex polynomial has negative expectation on the previously orbit-gated twelve-atom law. Its actual-gradient realizability changes from unknown to impossible. This excludes that law, not all free laws or actual-gradient counterexamples. Source: `.astra-dispatch/sources/Review_1913_20261030/ULTRA_AFFINE_PARTIAL/`.

2. **Ordinary capacity is constructed on two actual-field classes.** Every covariance-rank-at-most-two gradient and every pointwise rank-one-valued gradient has an ordinary allocation and a uniform `c/6` quartic Jensen gap in the fixed-leading family. The latter class includes full-covariance examples. The rank-two Fourier argument gives independent one-dimensional profiles; qualitative Jensen also follows directly by sequential rank-one convexity. Thus the explicit capacity construction and quantitative gap are the useful refinements, not a newly established general ROC/QC principle. Neither theorem covers arbitrary rank-three/full-rank fields or nearby fields merely because a covariance eigenvalue is small.

3. **A restricted local repair is impossible; a conic receiving problem is exact.** The no-go excludes a positive covariance correction quadratic in the local jet with the required plane-wave symbols. It does not exclude nonlocal or different-degree constructions. The 13-by-13 Gram formulation is lossless for the stated fixed rank-three face with invertible annihilator; singular-annihilator faces remain outside it. Its equality with global optimization concerns infima after an epsilon extension, not zero-epsilon attainment or elimination of the universal field quantifier. Source: `.astra-dispatch/sources/Review_1934_20261030/ULTRA_COVARIANCE_FRONTIER_PARTIAL/`.

4. **A new degenerate quadratic family is classified.** For this fixed non-aligned Hopf quartic and `Q0=b²+c²`, an explicit four-dimensional cubic space is exactly the set admitting **some finite ROC completion** and exactly the set admitting **some finite QC completion**. The theorem supplies a loose sufficient coefficient `2^86` times the squared coefficient norm. It does **not** prove that ROC and QC hold at the same coefficient, identify the optimal threshold, or classify other quadratics. The nontranslation cubics and kernel-preserving closed-form heat lifts extend the earlier construction beyond the positive quadratic seed's closure.

5. **An actual-field limit defeats a tempting strategy.** An explicit smooth, uniformly convergent full-rank gradient family has capacity cost bounded strictly above zero for every positive parameter, although the limiting rank-two field has zero cost. The construction retains a uniform positive quartic ROC gap and vanishing residual correlations. Thus strong field convergence, positive gap and small residuals do not by themselves justify continuity of optimal capacity at rank loss. The diverging optimizing cubic coefficient explains the boundary effect. This is neither nonattainment nor a negative variational gap: ordinary positive-budget allocations already exist for these fields. Source: `.astra-dispatch/sources/Review_1954_20261030/ULTRA_DEGENERATE_COMPLETION_PARTIAL/outputs/degenerate_completion/PROOF.txt`, SHA256 `105b2f2581590cdc2ccbe4e80a7c47f9e821ce7064e65a6660ab55b37f02536a`.
