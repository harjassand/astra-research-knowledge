1. A GENERAL PRINCIPLE ABOUT CUBIC RIGIDITY

Let F be a nonzero QC homogeneous quartic on R^(2x2), exposed as a ray in
the full homogeneous ROC cone. Suppose its four derivatives are linearly
independent, and the cubics satisfying

                grad C(R) dot K=0                           (3)

for every acoustic pair rank R=1, F''(R)K=0, are precisely DF:V.
Then the entire degree<=4 exposed face over F has the normal form (2),
and is common ROC/QC.

Proof. Scaling a globally ROC polynomial's base to infinity proves its
leading quartic ROC. Exposure fixes it to cF, c>=0. For c>0, evaluate
the polynomial's rank-one Hessian at the base tK in direction R. Quartic
polarization and the cubic identity give

               p''(tK)[R,R]=2t grad C(R) dot K+2p2(R).

Both signs and all sizes of t force (3). Hence C=c DF:V uniquely.
Subtract cF(A+V); the remainder is Q+L+d. At the base A=-V,
p''(-V)[R,R]=2Q(R), so Q is ROC. Conversely translated F is QC and
a ROC quadratic is QC by its direct Fourier mode decomposition; sums
with affine terms are QC. QC implies ROC, proving equivalence.

If c=0, the same both-sign argument at arbitrary bases forces the cubic
Hessian to vanish on every rank-one direction, hence C=0. Indeed all
quadratics partial_i C then vanish on det R=0 and are multiples of det.
Equality of mixed partials forces all those multiples to be zero.
Thus only a ROC quadratic and affine terms remain. This completes the
general argument; no correction-selector assumption is used.

6. DIMENSION, THE ACTUAL QUADRATIC BOUNDARY, AND NON-PC

The face lies in the linear space with leading coefficient c (one),
translation cubic coefficients (four), homogeneous quadratics (ten),
and affine terms (five). Normal form (2) contains a relatively open
subset of this space, by choosing c>0 and a positive definite reduced
quadratic. Its dimension is therefore exactly twenty. Affine functions
and lambda det form a six-dimensional lineality space, giving quotient
dimension fourteen.

For an arbitrary p in the face, solve the four exact linear equations
for V from its cubic, then subtract cF_s(A+V). The remaining Q must,
and need only, be ROC. This decides its SHARP boundary. It does not
replace Q by a large isotropic quadratic.

The runnable classifier works at rational s. For Q(u tensor (1,t)),
write the 2 by 2 output matrix as [[a(t),b(t)],[b(t),c(t)]]. ROC holds
exactly when a,c and ac-b^2 are nonnegative on the real projective line.
These are univariate polynomials of degrees <=2,2,4. The program checks
nonnegativity exactly by square-free decomposition and Sturm counts,
including even real roots and identically zero minors. This is an
