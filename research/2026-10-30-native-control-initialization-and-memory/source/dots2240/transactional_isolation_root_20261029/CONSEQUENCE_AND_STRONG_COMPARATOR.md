# Consequence gate for the autonomous-controller programme
2026-10-29 22:14 UTC. Root scope decision, not a novelty certificate.

A controller-integrity lemma, a graph-state factory, or a count of malformed episodes is insufficient as the destination. The intended consequence is a quantum memory with constant encoding rate, bounded per-site control/reset resources and finite rates, robust to broad local noise without reconstructing the environment or assuming a faultless classical decoding computer. Graph-local connectivity is not fixed-dimensional geometric locality, and the resource distinction remains material.

Current reviewed bridge: an ideal atomic asynchronous SSF decoder under continuously arriving local Pauli faults has a finite-physical-window support-tail proof. Its threshold is existential, not a useful measured engineering threshold. Controller compilation, coherent-noise witnesses and operational output remain open.

Strong contemporary comparator: Dünnweber–Styliaris–Trivedi, arXiv:2601.20818v2, develops autonomous two-dimensional noisy-control computation. Its discrete-time theorem already includes local non-Markovian fault amplitudes. Its continuous-time section III.2 uses channel-minus-identity Poisson updates and local unitary noise jumps. The discussion explicitly says the detailed continuous-time proof is left for future work. Constant rate and more general continuous-time noise are identified as extensions. This source defeats a claim that autonomy or fallible control alone is a new capability. Source inspected: https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20818v2 (sections Main theorem, Discussion, II.2, III.2).

A second comparator, Selub–Bhardwaj–Lake, arXiv:2609.11457v2, constructs spatially local fault-tolerant computation with bounded resource density, while explicitly retaining noiseless classical processors. Source inspected: https://arxiv.org/html/2609.11457v2 (Introduction and stated noise/control interface).

These are primary author claims and inspected mechanisms, not a fresh independent audit of their entire proofs. Our work does not refute them. The quantum direction earns further depth only by closing an actual missing noise/control/data interface or a comparably consequential principle. Existing clean Stinespring/Feynman clocks are the equally informed conventional baseline, not an invention attributed to this programme.
