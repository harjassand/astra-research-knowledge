# Initial primary-literature screen (29 October 2026)

This is a targeted screen, not an exhaustive novelty search and not priority clearance.

1. **Anderson and Kim, Some network conditions for positive recurrence of stochastically modeled reaction networks**: https://web.math.wisc.edu/~dfanderson/papers/AndersonKimSIAP2018.pdf . General weakly reversible positive recurrence is formulated as a conjecture; principal sufficient classes are restricted. Tier arguments are prior methodology and must be credited rather than advertised as invented here.

2. **Anderson, Cappelletti and Kim, Stochastically modeled weakly reversible reaction networks with a single linkage class**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.08967 . The abstract states binary and special pure-species-complex assumptions and an n-step embedded-chain method. These are not the same as arbitrary reaction order with finite internal modes.

3. **Wiuf and Xu, Classification and threshold dynamics of stochastic reaction networks**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.07954 . Establishes recurrence for weakly reversible systems with a one-dimensional stoichiometric subspace, with CMP-like stationary tails. A strip with two or more independent internal-mode directions need not have one-dimensional stoichiometry. A full-paper scope comparison is still needed.

4. **Xu, Non-explosivity of endotactic stochastic reaction systems**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.05340 . Includes all bimolecular weakly reversible systems as nonexplosive. Nonexplosion is not positive recurrence, and bimolecular order is different from our arbitrary finite order.

5. **Cappelletti, Howells and Xu, Stability of Reaction Networks with Randomly Switching Parameters**, primary author conference slides: https://easychair.org/smart-slide/slide/HZgd and https://easychair.org/smart-slide/slide/7wpr . The available abstract describes sufficient conditions for finite-regime environments under linearity assumptions and feedback extensions. SIAM primary conference abstract https://www.siam.org/media/13rgukxr/ag25_abstracts.pdf includes switched systems that can be transient despite individually recurrent networks. This is a warning not to infer stability from modewise recurrence; the candidate here instead requires weak reversibility of the full expanded complex graph. Slides' full content has not yet been audited for overlap.

6. **Wiuf and Xu, Any Stochastic Reaction Network has a Stationary Measure**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.07590 . Explicitly distinguishes a stationary measure from a normalizable distribution. Our summable dual measure is not itself asserted stationary; the embedded-chain excursion argument is essential.

7. **ConStRAINeD project results**: https://constrained.polito.it/publications/ reports a two-species recurrence solution while arbitrary dimension remains open. This is primary project status, not an independent audit of the reported theorem.

Searches specifically for weak reversibility plus bounded species, one unbounded species, finite phase, strips and regime switching did not identify the exact arbitrary-order finite-strip statement in the returned primary records. That negative search outcome does not establish novelty. Before any novelty claim, inspect full relevant papers, their later versions, citations, and matrix-analytic/Markov-renewal work on polynomial-rate strips.
