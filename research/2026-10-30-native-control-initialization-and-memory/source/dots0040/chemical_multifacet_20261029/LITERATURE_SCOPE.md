# Exact primary overlap (2026-10-29; no novelty clearance)

Anderson–Cappelletti–Kim, arXiv:1904.08967v2, Theorem4.1 requires a weakly reversible binary network with one linkage class, and for each species S at least one of S,2S among the complexes. This is not an arbitrary-order single-linkage recurrence theorem. The upper-frontier72-order example fails binary.

Anderson–Cappelletti–Kim–Nguyen, arXiv:1808.05328v2, Theorem7.2 starts with a strongly endotactic network of maximal source order m and adds, for each species, a nonempty family aS_i→y' with a≥m−1 and |y'|≤a−1; additionally the maximum target order in this draining family is strictly smaller than its minimum source order. Optional extra reactions have source order≤m−2. The upper-frontier72-order cycle has no required high-order pure-A drain, so this theorem does not directly apply. Strong endotacticity alone is not their recurrence condition (they give explosive strongly-endotactic examples).

General two-species weakly reversible recurrence has already been publicly announced by Agazzi, Anderson, Cappelletti, Laurence, Mattingly; manuscript listed in preparation by ConStRAINeD. Consequently the restricted2D candidate is not claimed as a new recurrence endpoint. The exact-dual proof technique and tail conclusion would need separate priority review.
