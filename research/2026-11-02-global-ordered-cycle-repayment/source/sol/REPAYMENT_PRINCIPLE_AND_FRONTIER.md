## Actual unrestricted obstruction

The chosen full destination remains recurrence for arbitrary finite weakly reversible mass-action lists at arbitrary positive rates, on their true irreducible classes. The present result is a complete structural theorem beyond the terminal-gap family, with unbounded deficiency; it is meaningful evidence for repayment as a mechanism. It does not complete that destination.

For a general list there may be no scalar +1/−k anchor, no total source ordering, and no strict bounded-jump normalizer on the complement of a manageable slow section. Several neutral activation layers can exchange inventory on comparable scales. Weak reversibility supplies finite reverse words and the preceding stage supplies coordinate deletion templates, but those facts do not bound the aggregate probability and physical occupation of all competing paths before their compatible final landings. Nor do they identify a profile whose feedback is small in every other receiving chart.

The missing operation is a raw-network construction of a shared finite-return/reward potential when these first-order debts themselves interact across unbounded sections. It must simultaneously control actual occupation and all outgoing rewards. A theorem may obtain such a potential through a proved existential construction; a numerical compiler cannot be declared to possess it merely because a stopped expectation has been written down. Our stopped expectation was first proved bounded by an independently earned strict contraction, and the certificate does not numerically evaluate it.

The exact slow-cell calibration also rules out a tempting stronger target: even the ordinary complex-balanced k=3 member has no positive exponential physical return moment and no positive Poincare gap. General recurrence cannot be obtained by requiring either one as the common interface. The physical occupation contract used here remains compatible with those earned constraints.

No claim of an unrestricted theorem, a complete face algorithm for all weak reversibility, a practical numerical crossover, independent audit, historical priority or civilisation-scale capability follows from this stage.
