## Reproducible quantities

`last_refresh_operation_ledger.py` uses90-digit directed interval arithmetic. It imports the SAME sharp fixture generator; no earlier JSON is overwritten. Let S,A,dA,K be its exact caps. Put e=epsilon/(1000S), beta=e/(128A), eta=beta/10000, D=K+4, and factory failed-probability target xi=eta/(100D). Choose odd YLC degrees above

    acosh(1/sqrt(xi))/acosh(1/sqrt(1-pmin)),

with two integer slack units, pmin1/4 for the event race and1/2 for atom/gap. The exact finite-grid success floors use the structural complementary coins in the bound option. Every ideal phase is acquired classically; numerical phase/gate errors remain an additional paid allocation.

The inner time grid obeys the whole source's eta/[10^7 D^3(Qrate+Lword)] allocation, and is reduced further to satisfy beta/[100(1+Lout)] and e/(100Lphys). It is rounded DOWN to a power of two. W is a power of two above twice the maximum window and all three fixed race thresholds, with two mesh units of slack. Thus the clock register length Bclock=log2(W/h) is explicit. It measures timing discretization, NOT rotation-synthesis precision.

A whole path PREP uses at most Le+2Lh fixed-clock preparations inside the three bounded factories, K+1 visible-prefix clocks and2K+2 QQ clocks. The source's inverse is included. Visible-prefix clocks choose between rates s and d coherently; charge TWO controlled rotations per bit (base rotation and conditional difference), and two tail rotations. The other fixed clocks use at most one rotation per bit and a tail coin. Each factory proposal has at most four additional known-coin rotations besides its tail. The displayed conservative controlled-rotation INVOCATION proxy is therefore

    2 S dA { (K+1)(2Bclock+2) + (2K+2)(Bclock+1)
               + (Le+2Lh)(Bclock+5) }.

This counts controlled rotation invocations before decomposition. It excludes reflection/zero-test gates, leading-bit logic, comparisons/addition, routing, phase/reflection synthesis and actual operator words. No claim is made that a multi-controlled rotation costs one elementary gate. In particular the fixed-point reflection cost must be added, not hidden inside the word count.

For the separate Gaussian binary attenuation proxy choose eta_k=beta/[10000(K+1)]. Its time-rounding mesh is a power of two below eta_k/[100(h_source+1)], and the active normalized time range is 2log(1/eta_k). This fixes Bkernel. The straightforward received filter uses at most2n Bkernel controlled attenuation rotations per kernel slot. Multiply by S dA(K+1). This is the TIME-DISCRETIZATION/filter invocation budget, not a complete normal-form/frequency/rotation-synthesis certification. Gaussian basis transforms, certified small-matrix diagonalization and residuals, marker logic, work-qubit compression, physical couplings/jump-stack operations, H0, controls and state preparation remain additional real costs. The code does not assert that these omitted costs are small.

## Consequence and ordinary comparison

The last-refresh construction genuinely removes endpoint-conditioned recursive sampling and coherent transcendental arithmetic from the main path source. The Gaussian component genuinely replaces generic source functional calculus by acquired quadratic-mode operations. Neither improvement establishes practical usefulness at these conservative parameters. Billions of controlled rotation invocations remain even before decomposition and all omitted costs. The earlier millions-of-slots improvement was not a gate count; this ledger makes that distinction concrete rather than erasing the improvement or overselling it.

The ordinary small dense CQ solver and the n-by-n spin-correlation/effective-noise computation remain the strong same-task comparators already preserved in the frozen audit. They are not displaced by this table. No comparative error-certified gate/runtime crossover is established. A meaningful further advance would need to remove or amortize the padded operation burden, acquire a directly useful observable without full process synthesis, or demonstrate a task where these paid costs beat an equally informed retained ordinary method. Merely broadening the source family would not answer that question.
