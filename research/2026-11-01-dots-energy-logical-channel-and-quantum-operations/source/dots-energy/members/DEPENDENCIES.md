# Dependency and version boundaries

This is a bounded scientific checkpoint, not a flattened claim that every
historical source is newly reviewed. Original relative paths and citations
inside copied source files are unchanged.

## Included operational dependency chain

* The energy-resolved receiver depends on the native whole receiver, its count
  lemma, the energy-weighted activity addendum, and their respective receipts
  in `native_lyapunov_source_20261031`.
* The concrete chain binding additionally uses the native geometric Gibbs
  binding, the corrected open-chain port and tail-correction receipt, and the
  direction-merged local-energy certificate/review in
  `local_gibbs_lift_20261031`.
* The native receiver's finite ordinary LCHS kernel and its review are included
  under `pure_return_comparison_20261031`. This remains the actual baseline
  kernel premise, not a free exponential oracle.
* Sharp assembly receives the native whole construction. Its scalar ledger
  imports `native_resource_audit_20261031/resource_ledger.py`; that code, bound
  baseline report/review, frozen comparison versions and dense comparator are
  included. All 29 baseline science files match the earlier native ZIP's bytes.
* Last-refresh receiving uses sharp assembly plus fixed-window and coherent
  dyadic clock receipts. Its final fixed-point option supplies the explicit
  finite-grid success-floor qualification without relabeling historical
  receipts. The operation audit separately binds the source-clock and Gaussian
  binary-attenuation interfaces. It does not certify all omitted gates.
* The expected-work corollary receives an already acquired whole operational
  algorithm. Its final-source binding is necessary alongside the earlier
  qualified review. The older expected-execution JSON is not this corollary's
  fully priced final numerical ledger.
* The two-state and domain-wall diagnostic-only folders have no standalone
  whole-compiler acceptance. Their mathematical context is supplied by the
  relevant bound sources above.
* The parity extension imports actual N1077 `fields.py`, `local_recognition.py`
  and `fisher.py`; all five N1077 modules and its full proof/review are included.
  `N1077_INCLUDED_SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` pins their exact original bytes and
  source URLs. N1077's generic theorem and older review are not transferred
  without qualification to the new all-weight/singular-query extension.

## Historical references not expanded into this checkpoint

The native proof mentions an older pure-return finite-bit receiver by hash
`1a097458af52e4b89746b2f5e38e8ba4ea9f8a835db731daef39b8e9ea71e032`.
The native source explicitly rebinds its own bridge/amplification operations
and does not substitute the earlier generator/jump stack. That historical body
is not added here. Likewise the included ordinary-comparison notes cite earlier
families and external papers without importing each historical package.

The N1077 proof/review cites REPORT/README, integration checks and earlier
mathematical audits in its pinned repository. The actual governing proof and
executable modules are included; every earlier work file is not. The original
intake manifest's administrative packet and mixed SOURCES note are deliberately
reference-only. Their omission is not disguised as a complete repository export.

Preserved pre-amendment receipts sometimes refer to older source hashes whose
bytes were not retained locally. They remain historical scope evidence, not
claims that an unavailable old version is included. The final-source bindings
and current exact-byte manifest are authoritative for the versions copied here.
