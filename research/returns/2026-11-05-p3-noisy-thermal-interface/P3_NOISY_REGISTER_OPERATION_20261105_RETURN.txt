## 1. The resolved question and its guarantee

The question I pursued was:

> Can a processor with fixed component noise prepare the required thermal background around an unknown reference-entangled qubit, cease active quantum correction for a long interval, and subsequently decode, use, reload or deliver that qubit through the same noisy hardware?

The answer is affirmative in the following specified model.

The active primitives have independent stationary Pauli faults. For the **fully commissioned instance**, one unknown homogeneous Pauli channel determines the faults in all known gate, preparation, reset, measurement and record-capture templates. The architecture supplies paid active links with specified latency, error and cost, plus constant-size terminal cells that couple locally to the memory. The holding system is the accepted commuting stabilizer family with positive bounded couplings and its specified local Pauli Davies bath. The BDL construction supplies the underlying three-dimensional passive-memory family; the new work supplies the noisy interface to it. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

There is a sufficient threshold \(p_*>0\), independent of memory size \(N\), requested duration and interior accuracy, such that \(p<p_*\) permits the complete operation. Its process error obeys

\[
\boxed{
d_{\mathrm{proc}}
\le
C n_{\mathrm{raw}}p
+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{total}}
+C\bigl[RNq+\Gamma NT\bigr]b_N,
\qquad
b_N\le C'e^{-cN^\eta},
\quad q=O(p).
}
\]

Here:

- \(d_{\mathrm{proc}}\) includes arbitrary logical references and retained external records, and takes the worst case over the allowed adaptive logical protocol.
- \(n_{\mathrm{raw}}\) counts unknown logical raw entries and raw deliveries.
- \(R\) bounds offered source/access opportunities, including candidates considered or rejected using retained records. For ordinary sequential operation, it is proportional to writes and reads.
- \(T\) is the sum of deterministic, source-specific caps on intrinsic passive time.
- \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{total}}\) pays protected computation, classical regeneration, source approximation, numerical sampling, parameter uncertainty and acquisition failure.

The constants depend on temperature, check incidence, the accepted history representation and the chosen control gadgets. They can be extremely unfavorable.

For any fixed number of raw ports and target error above their unavoidable \(O(p)\) contribution, increasing \(N\) supports holding times of stretched-exponential order. The source approximation is paid once for the commissioned source or finite source stream. Each refresh still pays its actual preparation and execution costs.

There is an essential endpoint distinction: protected internal work can achieve arbitrarily small \(\varepsilon\), whereas an unknown qubit crossing a fixed noisy raw port generally incurs an irreducible \(O(p)\) error. The construction earns the former and explicitly retains the latter.

## 2. Why ordinary fault tolerance did not automatically close the interface

Existing fault-tolerant input/output machinery already permits noisy encoding and decoding at fixed component error. It also exposes the relevant difficulty: logically correct output blocks can retain auxiliary syndrome systems correlated with one another and with previous records. Their final physical errors need not be independent merely because the subsequent decoder faults are independent. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}

That distinction matters here. The accepted passive theorem starts from a particular thermal syndrome law. Applying a constant amount of independent noise to that law can produce an extensive global discrepancy.

A simple example makes the issue concrete. Independent thermal bits with excitation probability \(r=0.1\), followed by independent flips of probability \(0.01\), acquire excitation probability \(0.108\). In the finite check included in the package, the total-variation distance between the two product laws at \(N=100{,}000\) is approximately \(0.999966\). A small local perturbation is therefore insufficient as a global initialization guarantee.

Earlier quantum error-precompensation work studies inverse-channel preparation and positivity for specified targets and channels. The additional requirements here are positivity uniformly in system size, efficient sampling under the actual syndrome constraints, preservation of unknown logical information, and implementation using noisy controls. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}

The route I constructed supplies these requirements together.

## 3. The hardest operation: removing old control-history dependence

### A noisy classical regenerator

All protected computation—including syndrome arithmetic and classical control—uses one measurement-free Aharonov–Ben-Or scheme. Its use avoids an ideal classical processor hidden beside the noisy quantum computer. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="3"}

For each intended physical output, prepare an independent encoded Bell resource \(A_iB_i\). Perform the logical Bell transform on the incoming data and \(A_i\). Copy its two logical classical outcomes into protected pools, then discard the consumed systems. **No old coherent controller acts on \(B_i\).**

Disjoint actual descents of the protected classical copies give raw bits with a local-stochastic error bound:

\[
\Pr[S\subseteq \text{wrong copies}]
\le q_c^{|S|}.
\]

The errors may remain correlated with each other and with old quantum references. The bound does not claim independence.

Feed those copies into a fixed ternary majority tree. Every wire and voter remains noisy. A native voter is the finite circuit

\[
\operatorname{CNOT}(a\!\to b),\qquad
\operatorname{CNOT}(a\!\to c),\qquad
\operatorname{Toffoli}(b,c\!\to a),
\]

retaining \(a\). Its ideal output is the majority of the three inputs. Under every fixed Pauli bit-fault pattern, complementing all three input bits complements the output. This symmetry is established for the actual gadget.

The proof couples the physical tree to a reference tree whose leaves are exact copies of the logical bit. Those exact leaves exist **only in the proof**. Let \(d_\ell\) bound disagreement between the two trees, and \(u_\ell\) bound an error in either tree. With fresh wire-error probability at most \(e\) and voter-fault probability at most \(v\),

\[
u_{\ell+1}\le v+3(u_\ell+e)^2,
\qquad
d_{\ell+1}\le [3v+6(u_\ell+e)]d_\ell.
\]

A mixed local-stochastic bound makes these recurrences valid for correlated inputs. If

\[
q_c,e,v\le s\le 0.01,
\]

then

\[
d_L\le q_c(21s)^L.
\]

The reference root has a computable binary-symmetric error of order \(p\), independent of all old records and references. Separate reference trees have independent errors. Thus the real roots approach independent, calibrated noisy controls, with an explicitly budgeted coupling error.

This operation has a substantial price:

\[
M=3^L
\]

protected copies per control bit. Suppressing history dependence to accuracy \(\varepsilon\) costs a polynomial in inverse accuracy. It must not be reported as merely the usual polylogarithmic fault-tolerance overhead.

### Bell export produces independent Pauli boundary channels

The surviving Bell half \(B_i\) follows its own independent protected waiting and descent path. Its raw output and the two regenerated classical controls arrive at the **same prescribed clock**. Final correction uses always-scheduled native CNOT and controlled-Z gates. There is no perfect classical gate selector.

Let \(E_i\) be the independent uncorrected descent channel. The uniform Bell outcome supplies a Pauli twirl:

\[
K_i
=
Q_{\mathrm{terminal},i}
\circ Q_{\mathrm{tree},i}
\circ
\frac14\sum_b
\mathcal P_b^{-1}\circ E_i\circ\mathcal P_b.
\]

Each \(K_i\) is an exact Pauli channel in the comparison, with strength \(O(p)\). Independent resources give their product. Protected-circuit replacements and tree-coupling errors account for the approximation to the physical operation.

Fine internal Bell and tree histories are discarded and reset at paid cost. Arbitrary **pre-existing** references and records remain. The proof never conditions on a globally successful factory event, which could reintroduce correlations.

This is a broader consequence than thermal loading: under the declared independent-noise model, it is a compiler that turns history-dependent quantum output errors into a product of calibrated local boundary channels.

## 4. Positive thermal precompensation and its sampler

Let the actual valid syndrome space be \(L\), including all check relations, and write

\[
\pi(s)=Z^{-1}\exp\!\left(-\sum_a\lambda_a s_a\right),
\qquad
\lambda_a=\beta J_a.
\]

A physical Pauli translates the syndrome within \(L\). The local channels \(K_i\) therefore act as commuting convolutions. Define

\[
\mu_I=\left(\prod_{i\in I}K_i^{-1}\right)\pi.
\]

An inverse channel is not generally positive. The central theorem proves positivity **for this thermal family**, simultaneously for every subset \(I\), uniformly in \(N\).

Suppose one physical site touches at most \(w\) checks, and one check touches at most \(d\) sites. The graph joining sites that share a check has degree

\[
D\le w(d-1).
\]

If each local Pauli channel has nonidentity probability at most \(q<1/4\), a sufficient condition is

\[
\boxed{
\theta
=
\frac{2q}{1-2q}e^{w\lambda_{\max}}
\le
\frac{1}{32e^3(D+1)}.
}

The proof expands the inverse law into signed connected-noise clusters. A cluster of \(k\) sites has absolute activity at most \(\theta^k\). Nonadjacent clusters touch disjoint checks, so their energy ratios factor even when the syndrome space has global constraints.
