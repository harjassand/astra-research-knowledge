Independent branch: analytic rank-one inverse-design acquisition obstruction.

Endpoint actually earned: for d>=17 there is an entire analytic one-latent-variable response family on the cube for which any adaptive Gaussian sensor policy that returns a design of response >=1/2 with probability >=1-delta needs total exposure at least (2 sigma^2/e) 2^d kl(2^-d || 1-delta), despite unlimited computation. Exhaustive corner sensing achieves response >=1/2 with exposure 32 sigma^2 2^d log(2^(d+1)/delta). Thus the nearly matching endpoint is an operation lower bound, not a proposed application.

Construction: hidden a in {+1,-1}^d, f_a(x)=exp[-gamma(d-a.x)], gamma=(log d)/4. High-response regions are pairwise disjoint. The mean-square response over hidden a is <=q^d, q=(1+exp(-4 gamma))/2. Compare every adaptive transcript to the zero-signal experiment: averaged KL <= T q^d/(2 sigma^2). Data processing and binary-KL convexity give the bound above. Fixed-order noisy derivative measurements improve it by at most sum_(k=0)^K(gamma^2 d)^k.

Complete constructive contrast: if f(x)=g(a.x/d), g is L-Lipschitz and |g(1/d)-g(-1/d)|>=Delta_0, 2d noisy axis measurements recover a up to sign with exposure (8 sigma^2 d/Delta_0^2) log(2d/delta). Then x=t a traces every latent state; an explicit grid of <=ceil(4L/epsilon)+1 settings and exposure (32 sigma^2/epsilon^2)log(4K/delta) per setting gives epsilon global regret. Control, output, memory and reset costs are explicit in the full note.

Decisive gap: low latent dimension/analyticity does not supply accessible contrast. An exact gradient or direct log-response oracle removes this obstruction, but those are stronger acquisition primitives. No new transformative capability or priority claim is established. Generic ridge acquisition obstructions and conditional recovery already have strong prior literature (Doerr-Mayer; Fornasier-Schnass-Vybiral; Holodnak-Ipsen-Smith; Jun-Jain-Mason-Nassif). This is a rigorous branch-selection filter, not a breakthrough claim.
