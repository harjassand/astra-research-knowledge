This directory holds the joined physical-protocol demonstration, its finite-error
resource ledger, and numerical diagnostics. The microscopic bath used by the
simulator is never passed to the model-fitting routine.

The theorem is analytic. Numerical ODE solutions are illustrations, not validated
interval integrations of the unknown physical apparatus. The positive-model
fitter independently verifies its emitted finite rational model using exact
rational exponential enclosures. Confidence is conditional on the declared
physical model and calibrated meter/control bounds, not inferred from the
synthetic fixture.
