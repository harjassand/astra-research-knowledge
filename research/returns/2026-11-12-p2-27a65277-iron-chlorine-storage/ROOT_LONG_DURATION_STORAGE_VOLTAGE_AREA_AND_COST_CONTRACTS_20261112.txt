This is useful electrode manufacture/depassivation evidence, not a rechargeable air-cathode device,100hcalendar test, complete RTE or factory cost.

A finite sulfide reservoir and capacity restoration therefore need an actual inventory/lifetime balance. No replenished chemical energy is a free recharge. Air/O2 electrodes, CO2 ingress, water management and the long standby interval are new interfaces, not supplied by an iron working-electrode result.

7. Decisive later observables
A later authorized small prototype should integrate both electrodes or the complete alternative mechanism, realistic usable loading, actual air/water conditions, terminal energy, standby after charge, net product/species return and full chemical/gas inventory. Half-cell/reference/counter-electrode tests can identify a mechanism but do not establish a complete store. Measure hydrogen, oxygen, carbon/sulfur and electrolyte/additive changes where relevant, blank/formation energy and residual SOC. No physical test is executed here.
