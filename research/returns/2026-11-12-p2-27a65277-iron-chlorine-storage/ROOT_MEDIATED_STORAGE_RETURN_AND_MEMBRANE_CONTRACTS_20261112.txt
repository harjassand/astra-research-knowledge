Mediated storage: terminal iron return, mediator reversal and oxygen/ion transport
ROOT original conditional engineering calculations, 12 November 2026 Brisbane

Physical operation and actual source boundary

A dissolved mediator can carry charge between a compact electrochemical stack
and bulk solid inventory, avoiding the need to make all stored metal a thick
conducting electrode. That useful operation does not itself recharge the solid,
isolate oxygen, restore chemical inventories or establish useful AC efficiency.

Distinct primary: Gao et al., Advanced Energy Materials 2023, 2301868,
https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/aenm.202301868 .
ROOT read Sections 2.1--2.2, equations 1--9 and Section 3. The aqueous system
uses AQDS or a sulfonated viologen for iron oxidation, dissolved Fe(III/II)
for oxygen reduction, Fe(II) membrane transport and FeOOH precipitation.
The long runs feed iron powder and bubble oxygen; they are fuel-cell discharge,
not complete electric recharging. Reported iron utilization is 73% in the
roughly 300-hour AQDS run and 53% in the over-350-hour viologen run at
2 mA/cm2. Oxygen crossover, membrane fouling and powder clogging limit the
experiments. The paper's redox-potential and SOC discussion supplies a concrete
mediator/solid interface; its material-only price arithmetic is not a store
cost. No supporting information, cited original or alternative provider was
followed. These facts do not supply a reversible oxygen electrode, charging
path, qualified separator, AC round-trip efficiency or calendar life.

1. The terminal phase changes the complete return charge

Combine the reported reaction pathway while keeping all mediator and dissolved
inventory states equal at the two accounting boundaries. The net discharge is

  2 Fe + (3/2) O2 + H2O -> 2 FeOOH.

The external circuit carries FOUR electrons for these two iron atoms: metal
first becomes Fe(II), then the later oxidation to Fe(III) occurs chemically
in the oxygen/contact tank. The terminal FeOOH is Fe(III). A purely Faradaic
return of all that Fe(III) to Fe(0), paired with oxygen evolution and restoring
all other inventories, requires SIX electrons per two iron atoms. Therefore
the original discharge paired with that return has an ideal external charge
ratio 2/3 before side reactions. A two-electron Fe(II)-to-metal charging ledger
would leave a third-electron return operation unpaid.

This charge ratio is NOT a universal 2/3 energy-efficiency ceiling. Define
Vdis = Wdis_DC/(2 F NFe) and Vch = Wch_DC/(3 F NFe). For that specified
Faradaic cycle, its DC energy ratio is (2/3) Vdis/Vch, with actual loss factors
and inventories still to be paid. Vch need not equal Vdis or an isolated
electrode voltage. A different terminal phase, collection of the otherwise
bypassed electron, or a chemical/thermal regeneration route changes the
equation and must include that route's work and material return.

An independent HYPOTHETICAL screen uses 0.75 V mean discharge, 0.97 power
conversion in each direction, 0.99 hold retention and 0.98 auxiliary factor.
It assumes ideal return Faradaic efficiency and no other loss. Reaching 65%
AC efficiency would require Vch <= 0.70220 V averaged over ALL three charging
electrons. This is a requirement, not a measured charging voltage or an
all-architecture obstruction. The 53% experimental fuel utilization is not
used as a charging efficiency or transplanted as a 144-hour hold result.

An implementable reversible route must choose an actual FeOOH collection,
reduction, metal morphology and return path, and account for the counter
reaction, dissolved iron, acid/buffer, water and gas inventories. A separate
oxide-return reactor is an engineering candidate, not constructed hardware.
Off-site reduction or sale of rust changes the useful operation and cannot
quietly make this an electricity-in/electricity-out store.

2. Reversing a mediator is a chemical-potential operation

For an ideal n-electron mediator with oxidized/reduced activity ratio r,

  Em = Em0 + (R T / (n F)) ln r.

To oxidize a solid at potential Es with margin dd requires Em >= Es + dd.
To reduce it with margin dc requires Em <= Es - dc. Thus the two operating
activity ratios must satisfy r_dis/r_ch >= exp[n F (dd+dc)/(R T)]. Merely
naming the discharge mediator does not give the reverse reaction at the
same liquid composition. Surface passivation, potential shifts with solid
phase, reaction rates and nonideal activities are separate operations.

At 298.15 K, symmetric ideal minority fractions epsilon and 1-epsilon span
2 R T ln[(1-epsilon)/epsilon]/(n F). For n=1 the windows at epsilon=0.1,
0.05, 0.01 and 0.001 are 112.90,151.30,236.12 and354.90 mV. For n=2
they are half as large. These are activity screens, not a prepared electrolyte
or an experimental concentration law. Two distinct mediators or staged
reactors may change the topology, with separation, mixing and return paid.

3. Oxygen isolation and useful ion conductance must coexist

Consider a static homogeneous separator of thickness l and area A. Let its
actual useful-ion conductivity be kappa, its oxygen permeability P, and the
oxygen partial-pressure difference dp. Assume constant properties, a perfect
oxygen sink on the negative side and four electrons lost per oxygen molecule.
Define reference discharge current I=j A, duration td and Qref=I td. Over an
idle hold th the nominal lost electrical-equivalent charge relative to Qref is

  f = 4 F P dp th / (l j td),     Vohm = j l/kappa,
  f Vohm = 4 F P dp th/(kappa td) = K.

This is an exact product within the declared surrogate. The first-order sum
of nominal hold loss and operating voltage loss is f + Vohm/Vdis, minimized
over l at 2 sqrt(K/Vdis), with Vohm=sqrt(K Vdis). It is not an exact full-cycle
efficiency formula. If f exceeds one, the reference inventory would exhaust;
it is not a physical retention fraction greater than 100%. There is no
capacity/area improvement from changing j or l alone in this product at fixed
properties and th/td. This does not prohibit a different separator topology.

Numerical HYPOTHETICAL joint states: th/td=144/100, dp=0.21 atm, Vdis=0.9 V,
and 1 Barrer defined as 3.35e-16 mol m/(m2 s Pa). At P=1 Barrer and
kappa=0.1 S/m, K=3.96154e-5 V. A 25 mV membrane drop gives nominal hold loss
0.0015846; the minimum first-order combined loss is1.3269%, at5.9711mV.
At j=100 A/m2 that optimum needs a5.9711um uniform layer. With the SAME
conductivity but100 Barrer permeability, the minimum is13.269%, at59.711mV
and59.711um. No such measured joint material is supplied. Mechanical support,
Fe-ion transport rather than proton-only conductivity, fouling, concentration
polarization, manifold loss, aging and cost are missing from this screen.

For a10MWh useful AC reference at0.9V DC and97% output conversion,
Qref=4.12371e10C. A1% four-electron oxygen-loss allowance is1068.48mol O2,
equivalent to124.48m3 ideal ambient air at298.15K and0.21atm oxygen. The
100W/10kWh module is one-thousandth of this reference. These inventories do
not supply oxygen-free processing: trapped gas, dissolved oxygen and leaks
must be measured and paid under the actual iron/mediator reaction network.

Physical isolation of the charged bulk tank during the hold can change dp
or remove the exposed separator area. Valves, trapped liquid, seals, purge,
oxygen inventory, restart and maintenance must then be actual components and
costs. Their useful operation is preserving the charged bulk state while the
power interface is idle, not granting an ideal selective membrane.

Scope and executable calculation

ROOT_MEDIATED_STORAGE_RETURN_AND_MEMBRANE_SCREEN_20261112.py uses only the
Python standard library and writes the same-basename JSON. It executes the
explicit conditional arithmetic above. It does not simulate the source
experiment, fit a reaction law, recover its SI, qualify a material or test a
complete invention. The charge-return, chemical-potential and oxygen/ion
interfaces remain concrete missing physical operations for candidate designs.
No physical experiment or acquisition was executed.
