17. Broad deductions and exact boundaries

A reusable structural principle

After the accepted ensemble is supplied, no thermal identity enters the compiler. Any described ensemble of uniformly full-rank bounded factors over a bounded-round catalogue of finite-support cells admits this replacement/product-noise architecture. Gibbs chains provide a broad noncommuting family. Other geometries need their own catalogue and ensemble theorem.

Accuracy can be carried by classical probabilities

For these known mixed targets, arbitrary global trace accuracy does not force larger coherent error-correcting blocks, longer quantum data lifetime or increasingly precise analog gates. Fixed quantum templates suffice; sampling, metrology and reliable classical control carry increasing precision. This is a sufficiency result. An equally informed preparer can make the same choice.

Restrictions removed by construction

No dynamically perfect block mask, ideal final selector, growing quantum controller or passive holding bath is used. Wrong within-call coupling across intended blocks is included. The explicit cluster family additionally avoids the enormous universal quantum library and uses fixed nearest-neighbor quantum gates.
