## 1\. The exact Maxwell–Stefan entropy metric

Let

\[
f_-=\min_{i\ne j}f_{ij},\qquad
f_+=\max_{i\ne j}f_{ij},\qquad
\lambda=\frac1{f_+}.
\]

All three quantities are computable positive rationals.

For positive concentrations, write

\[
J_i=c_i v_i,\qquad \sum_i c_iv_i=0.
\]

The friction equations become

\[
-\nabla\log c_i
=\sum_{j\ne i}f_{ij}c_j(v_i-v_j).
\]

Define the weighted friction operator

\[
(L_cv)_i=\sum_{j\ne i}f_{ij}c_j(v_i-v_j).
\]

Its quadratic form satisfies

\[
\begin{aligned}
\langle v,L_cv\rangle_c
&=\frac12\sum_{i,j}f_{ij}c_ic_j|v_i-v_j|^2,\\
f_-\sum_i c_i|v_i|^2
&\leq\langle v,L_cv\rangle_c
\leq f_+\sum_i c_i|v_i|^2,
\end{aligned}
\]

where \(\langle v,w\rangle_c=\sum_i c_i v_i\cdot w_i\), and the inequalities use \(\sum_i c_iv_i=0\)\.

Thus the friction operator is uniformly positive in its natural weighted tangent space, with bounds independent of the concentrations' distance from the simplex boundary.

For a smooth positive solution, incompressible advection cancels exactly from the relative-entropy balance\:

\[
\frac{dH}{dt}=-\mathcal D(t),
\]

where

\[
\mathcal D(t)=
\frac12\int_{\mathbb T^3}
\sum_{i,j}f_{ij}c_ic_j|v_i-v_j|^2\,dx.
\]

Spectral comparison of \(L_c\) gives two useful estimates\:

\[
\boxed{
\sum_i\|\nabla c_i\|_2^2
\leq f_+\mathcal D,
\qquad
\sum_i\|J_i\|_2^2
\leq f_-^{-1}\mathcal D.
}
\tag{1}
\]

The stronger Fisher-information bound is

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal D\geq
\frac1{f_+}
\sum_i\int_{\mathbb T^3}
\frac{|\nabla c_i|^2}{c_i}\,dx
=
\frac4{f_+}\sum_i\|\nabla\sqrt{c_i}\|_2^2.
}
\tag{2}
\]

These estimates are valid by approximation when concentrations vanish.
