Pair occupation under adaptive transport and local marked chemistry

1. Fixed-count transport lemma

Work on the unit three-torus. During an interval without chemical events,
there are n point particles, with fixed species. Let A0(t) be the block
diagonal 3n by3n matrix with deterministic UNKNOWN symmetric diffusivities
D_s(t) on the diagonal. Assume d0 I<=D_s(t)<=I. Measurable time profiles
suffice. The nominal joint generator is sum_i D_s(i)(t):D_i^2.

Allow any predictable symmetric covariance A(t) with
 ||A(t)-A0(t)||op<=delta d0, 0<delta<=1/4,
and predictable drifts v_i(t) with |v_i(t)|<=delta. Controls may depend
on the entire position/chemical history and external randomness, without
future driving noise. In lifted coordinates define actual motion by
 dX=v dt+sqrt(2A)dW,
with the positive matrix square root. Define a comparison process from
the SAME starting positions and SAME W by dY=sqrt(2A0)dW. This is a proof
coupling, not a physical observation, reset or an alternate noise source.

Put S=sqrt(A),S0=sqrt(A0),E=A-A0. The exact Sylvester identity is
 S(S-S0)+(S-S0)S0=E.
The integral inverse gives
 S-S0=int_0^infinity exp(-tS)E exp(-tS0)dt,
 ||S-S0||op<=||E||op/(sqrt(lambda_min A)+sqrt(lambda_min A0))
             <=C delta sqrt(d0).
This bound is dimension independent. For an individual pair i!=j,
the 3 by3n difference-coordinate projection has fixed norm and rank3.
Ito isometry and the drift bound therefore imply, at any elapsed g,
 E|[(X_i-X_j)-(Y_i-Y_j)]_lift|^2
 <= C delta^2(g+g^2).
No derivative of A, drift, time profile or history-dependent policy is
used. Correlations between different particles are permitted in A.
The expression concerns a pair displacement, not full-configuration TV.

The reference difference Y_i-Y_j has deterministic covariance integral
2(D_s(i)+D_s(j)). Its torus heat density has
 ||p_g-1||TV<=C exp(-c d0 g), sup p_g<=C
for g>=d0^-1, uniformly in starting positions/time/species. Fourier
summation proves this directly from the lower matrix bound. At shorter
times use the bound one. Independence of different reference PAIRS is
neither true nor needed.

2. Discontinuous local contacts can still be compared

Let k_r(x)=1_[-r,r]^3(x)/(2r)^3, 0<r<=1/8, v=(2r)^3. For a tube of
width w<=1 around the periodic cube boundary, its Haar volume is <=Cw.
For g>=d0^-1 the reference density bound gives probability <=Cw of
being in that tube. Off the tube and off the event of displacement>w,
the actual and reference contact indicators agree. Thus
 |E k_r(X_i-X_j)-E k_r(Y_i-Y_j)|
 <=C v^-1[w+delta^2(g+g^2)/w^2].
Take w proportional to [delta^2(g+g^2)]^(1/3), using a trivial bound
when that quantity exceeds one. For all g>=0 this gives the uniform
conditional contact-occupation estimate
 |E[k_r(X_i-X_j)|starting full history]-1|
 <=C v^-1{min(1,C exp(-c d0 g))
            +min(1,[delta^2(g+g^2)]^(1/3))}.             (A)
The short-gap case is included by increasing the absolute constants.
No independence between the displacement error and the reference
boundary event was assumed: the proof uses a union bound. In particular,
neither an assumed invariant density for the controlled process nor
Girsanov equivalence of different-diffusivity path laws is used.

The same argument applies after conditioning on a chemical-free proposal
gap: choose the proposal clock independent of the driving innovations
and external randomness, and use the admissible bounded policy during
that interval. The reference starts from the actual positions at its
beginning. This changes a PROOF reference only. It does not reinitialize
or smooth the physical particles.

3. Why pair marginals can suffice for the entire chemical mark law

Consider a conservative elementary network using2->1,1->2,1->1,0->0
channels, with coefficients c_e<=1 and integer conserved mass w.z=N,
w_s>=1. Its local implementation uses the accepted ordered-pair binding
kernel k_r, anchored fusion and matching local release. Slow all chemical
coefficients by h. Arbitrary initial positions and zero extra particles
are permitted. Suppose fine chemical multipliers also lie in1+/-delta
and can see the continuous position history and every hidden/null mark.

Let M be the number of directed fine channels and put
 L0=2M(1+N)^2/v, lambda=L0/h.
This dominates total actual chemical intensity for delta<=1/4.
Uniformization is only a proof construction. Between proposals no
chemical event can occur. Conditional on the full past at the previous
proposal and the next gap g, let p_e be the ACTUAL probability that the
next proposal has chemical channel mark e. Marginalize current positions
and any intervening transport randomness, without conditioning on their
future value. The local intensity is a sum over ordered pairs, unary
particles or an empty-source mark. Consequently (A), summed linearly
over these tuples, gives
 sum_e |p_e-[c_e(z)_alpha_e/L0]|
 <=C{b(g)+[delta^2(g+g^2)]^(1/3)+delta},                (B)
where b has integrated majorant I=C/d0 and is bounded by1 after a
harmless rescaling of constants. Rejection is an additional mark and
has the same total-variation bound. The chemical multiplier term is
paid pointwise; its correlation with contacts is allowed.

It is unnecessary to couple the FULL n-particle position vector to a
fresh product-uniform state. Couple the conditional mark distribution
in (B) directly to the averaged count chain's proposal mark. Draw the
actual reactant tuple and subsequent placement under their correct
conditional law. Given success, count states and chemical marks match;
the actual post-event positions and policy history may be arbitrary.
At the next gap use the same uniform conditional estimate. The averaged
mark has the correct marginal conditional on the PAST enlarged history;
conditioning instead on the current hidden configuration would be an
invalid replacement. This distinction is essential when faults inspect
all particle positions.

For logical fine-count horizon T and physical horizon hT, summing the
first and later independent Poisson proposal gaps yields
 TV(complete local chemical timed path at clock h,
    nominal well-mixed timed path on[0,T])
 <= C (L0 I/h)(1+L0 T)
  +C(1+L0 T){[delta^2(hT+(hT)^2)]^(1/3)+delta}.        (C)
On agreement, chemical marks occur at EXACTLY identical rescaled
proposal times, including null/parallel channels. Rejections delete no
physical residence time. The kernels and chemical process never stop.
The clock and conditioning argument need to be retained explicitly in
any full compiler assembly; a pair expectation alone is not a blanket
claim about full position-path TV.

There are rational choices with fixed polynomial costs. For example,
enlarge an absolute computable rational constant C0 covering (C), set
 h=1+ceil[C0 epsilon^-1 L0 I(1+L0 T)],
 K0=1+L0 T,
 delta<=min(1/4,epsilon^2/[C0 K0^2(1+hT)]).
Increasing C0 gives error<=epsilon/2. Indeed delta^2 times
hT+(hT)^2 is at most a fixed multiple of epsilon^4/K0^4, whose cube
root is <=epsilon/K0 for epsilon<=1,K0>=1, after the constant choice.
No logarithm, time-profile value, inverse tiny contact probability or
dimension-dependent exponent is required. Rational larger majorants
for I and ceilings suffice. This is an actual conditional comparison
interface, not a simulated trajectory or completed variable-order
compiler implementation.

4. Nominal balance and actual movement/work limits

At zero faults/no drift, the accepted anchored binding/release rule has
equal forward/reverse Poisson-point Palm flux measures whenever
c_e a^alpha=c_bar(e) a^beta. Ordered DISTINCT identical reactants are
counted with falling factorials, without an inserted symmetry factor.
Unknown deterministic anisotropic D_s(t) preserves the uniform position
factors; each instantaneous nominal diffusion generator is symmetric.
Time-varying profiles do not imply ordinary path reversibility of the
whole non-autonomous process. Fault-controlled marginals need not be
stationary or reversible.

For all actual histories, n<=N pathwise. Aggregate chemical intensity
is <=L0/h, so nonexplosion and E F_all(t)<=L0 t/h are direct. Integrated
particle count is <=Nt. The covariance bound gives total coordinate
quadratic variation <=C N t; sum of drift-path variations is <=delta N t.
The quadratic variation counts actual lifetimes and is not energy.
Brownian total variation remains infinite. Local reaction placement is
a discontinuous jump; its distance is bounded by r per released/fused
pair and is charged through the ALL-chemical-event count. There is no
assertion of a bit-exact continuous-path sampler or molecular device.

Composing the conservative paired variable-order chemical compiler
with this local comparison requires the exact fine interface, actual
clock multiplication, split error budgets, activity/decoder/rate bit
costs, all original null labels and the canonical zero-auxiliary graph
contract. Those should be stated as one full output operation rather
than inferred from an averaged generator. The conservation premise is
not a solution to the independent general-open-tail programme.
