Newton certificates, numerical choices and fixed physical libraries

Scientific scope. Consider the full reversible elementary compiler question
with variable d,D, prescribed activities, exact decoded graphs, zero
auxiliaries, complete timed marks and all predictable fine faults. The
following are additional mathematical interfaces. They do not construct
the general stabilizer or a universal negative.

1. A paid rational form of the Newton condition

Combine identical original source complexes exactly and write
 b_y=sum_(r:y_r=y) k_r omega.(z_r-y),
 L N(x)=sum_y b_y (x)_y.
The supplied upper drift and independent Poisson factorial moments imply
 F(c)=sum_y b_y c^y<=A for every c>=0. No invariant distribution is used.
For each nonzero y with b_y>0,
 y belongs to conv({0} union {z:b_z<0}).
A strict separating vector would make a positive monomial dominate all
negative monomials along c_i=exp(t v_i), contradicting bounded upper F.
Every vertex used in a convex representation is supported in supp(y):
nonnegative exponents cannot cancel a coordinate outside that support.

Put h=|supp(y)|<=min(d,D). There is a representation
 q y=sum_(j=1)^r p_j z_j,
 p_j>=1, p_0>=0, sum_(j=0)^r p_j=q, r<=h+1,
with a positive integer q and nonnegative integer p_j. The optional zero
vertex has coefficient p_0. Choose a basic convex representation and
independent rows of its augmented vertex matrix (1,z). Its square minor
is a nonzero integer. Cramer's rule makes q its absolute determinant;
each numerator p_j is an integer between zero and q. A column has
Euclidean norm at most D+1, so
 q<= (D+1)^(h+1) <= (2+2d)^(2D+2) < Xi.
For the last inequality it suffices to consider h=1 separately and, for
h>=2, use that log(t)/t decreases for t>=3. Thus each positive convex
coefficient is at least Xi^(-1). Numerators, denominators and an explicit
support certificate have O(D log(D+1)+log m) bits per entry. Verification
is exact rational arithmetic polynomial in the complete input length.
This is an existence and verification bound, not an executed LP or a
paid search through all bases. No population/class enumeration occurs.

Weighted AM--GM now gives the exact deterministic inequality
 c^y <= p_0/q + sum_j (p_j/q)c^z_j.
The sign classification depends on numerical data; the exponent vectors
come from the original stoichiometric list. The simplex conditioning is
therefore numerically affordable in the stated Xi charge. This removes
one possible numerical obstruction, not the physical closure problem.

Important boundaries. Substitution of falling factorials into this
AM--GM inequality is invalid: y=1,z=2,q=2,p_0=p_1=1 gives
 (1)_1=1 > [1+(1)_2]/2=1/2.
This already occurs for the admitted reversible X<->2X example. The
original all-state drift does not turn a monomial inequality into an
enabled physical reaction or into a pointwise finite-speed drift.
Also q<Xi is a NUMERICAL bound. A compiler with O(q) species or O(q)
expanded output can still violate polynomial binary cost, since Xi may
be exponentially large in the input length. Nor may coefficients p_j
be encoded by changing a decoder that is required to depend only on R.
A physical compressed realization, valid reverse enabling and paid
activity for that realization remain separate mathematical operations.

2. A fixed-library completion lemma

The following separates numerical selection of useful channels from
the actual requirement that all emitted structural data depend only
on R. Fix a finite dictionary P and a reversible finite channel library
E determined solely by R. Every library channel must already be hidden
and P-preserving, or carry an enabled original label with the correct
increment and reverse. Suppose an existing base subnetwork already has
the full all-state decoder graph property. Adding any such channels
preserves that property: each new fine edge projects to an original
edge, and the old base paths still connect full preimages.

Let a_tilde be assigned positive rational activities and
 G=max(1,max_s a_tilde_s,max_s a_tilde_s^(-1)).
Assume a numerically chosen subset of E has, for every instantaneous
fault vector in [1/2,3/2], a raw drift certificate
 L V <= a0+a1 n_active-a2 Q, a2>0,
 V=sum_s w_s z_s, 1<=w_s<=wmax,
 Q>=n_active(n_active-1)/2.
The coefficient bounds and a2^(-1),wmax,G are to be polynomially paid.
Require Q to dominate the source monomial of EVERY binary channel of
the whole library, including every 2->2 mark. For any unused channel
whose unary source is inactive, require its V increment <=0. These
coverage/sign requirements are substantive, not consequences of graph
faithfulness or of a nominal detailed-balance certificate.

For each of the J unused reverse pairs alpha<->beta, assign nominal raw
flux zeta a_tilde^alpha a_tilde^beta; equivalently coefficients
 k_alpha,beta=zeta a_tilde^beta,
 k_beta,alpha=zeta a_tilde^alpha.
These are exactly balanced, lie in [zeta G^-2,zeta G^2], and are strictly
positive. Null self-reverse channels can use the same formula once.
No selected edge has been physically switched off or software-selected
during the run. The actual emitted library is always the same E.

Because a jump of V has absolute value <=2wmax, the unused channels add
at most
 6J zeta G^2 wmax (1+n_active+Q)
to its upper drift throughout the enlarged rate box. Choose, for J>=1,
 zeta <= min( G^-2,
   a2/[24J G^2 wmax(1+a2)],
   epsilon/[1024J G^2 U^2(1+tau)] ).
Then the combined raw drift is at most
 (a0+a2/4)+(a1+a2/4)n_active-(3a2/4)Q.
The bounded-drift/martingale/counting argument therefore pays the actual
all-time population supremum and all firings. No stationary assumption,
conditional reset or uniform mean bound is needed.

All additional reciprocal coefficients are bounded by zeta^-1 G^2;
the choice has fixed polynomial cost in the displayed quantities.
If nominal coefficients are divided by the same existing H>=1, these
bounds and the drift constants are rescaled with a paid factor H.
The total added raw intensity before N(Pz)>=B is at most
 3J zeta G^2 U^2,
since each fine column is nonzero and omega>=1. Over physical H tau,
normalization by H cancels the clock. Coupling until the first added
firing costs at most 3epsilon/1024. Every added channel continues to
operate on actual excursions; the global certificate pays its work.
Rational minima/ceilings and expansion of a POLYNOMIAL-SIZE fixed
library cost polynomially many bit operations when all its inputs have
polynomial bit length. This does not assert such a library has been
found for every R, or that finding the drift certificate is free.

Thus a data-dependent choice of a favorable forest, source signs or
potential need not by itself violate the R-only structural rule. A
fixed admissible dictionary/library can retain the unused choices with
small positive balanced rates, PROVIDED their source drift/activity
is covered. It is precisely the inactive-source and binary coverage
conditions that prevent this lemma from hiding the general problem.
Including all elementary transitions indiscriminately is not justified.

3. The current common-cost interface and its noncoercive boundary

For an elementary process with every coefficient<=1, a common drift
 L V<=C*-(c2/2)Q
and coverage of binary 2->2 sources gives
 E integral_0^t Q <=2(V0+C*t)/c2,
 E integral_0^t n <=V0 t+C*t^2/2.
The pathwise count
 F_all<=n0+3F_zero+2F_unary+F_22
pays the full expected firing count quadratically in 1+t. Each V jump
is at most 2wmax, so the compensated martingale has expected quadratic
variation <=4wmax^2 E F_all. Doob gives
 E sup_(s<=t)(1+n_s)
 <=1+V0+C*t+4wmax sqrt(E F_all(t)),
which is linear in 1+t. This is the completed return's new interface,
not an additional theorem credit assigned to this note.

The actual admitted network A+B<->2A+B, B<->2B with unit data has
 L(a+b)=b(2a-a^2+2-b)<=2.
Every nA state is a singleton original class. Any finite allowed lift
has a finite closed fibre above nA. Averaging a putative globally
strict negative quadratic drift in the ENTIRE fine population over a
stationary law of that fibre contradicts positivity for large n.
Consequently neither the completion lemma's coverage nor a whole-
population quadratic Foster bound can be supplied universally for
free. This does not obstruct a partial/activity-sensitive certificate
or the full allowed design class. In particular, inactivity cannot be
discarded by declaring every fine species part of the controlled set.
