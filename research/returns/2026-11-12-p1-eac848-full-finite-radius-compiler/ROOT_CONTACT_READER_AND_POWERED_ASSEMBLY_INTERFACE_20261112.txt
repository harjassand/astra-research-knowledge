Contact readers, bounded-force contact work, and powered geometric assembly

1. What the completed hard-ball construction provides

The full conservative finite-radius compiler supplies a polynomial geometric
Poincare comparison after a paid dilute radius choice, all-start conditional
channel averaging, exact marked-path compilation and actual wall/pair work.
Its inward buffer, rank separation and one-three-dimensional-coordinate paths
are analytical proof devices. They are not physical actuators or assembly
instructions. The compiler has no persistent bond graph, fixed-palette shape
interpreter, local contact reader or powered growth/repair theorem.

The new distinction is between sampling a conserved reaction programme and
building a retained geometry. Conditioning uniform volume on nonoverlap does
not manufacture a target. Adding attractive pair energies can change its
Poincare constant exponentially; the dilute uniform theorem cannot simply be
relabelled a mixing theorem for every assembly energy.

2. An actual pair-energy two-contact reader, with held inputs

Let a,b,y be binary conformation states. Assume a,b are held at the two input
contacts during one read. In thermal units use the energy
 E(a,b,y)=24y-16ay-16by.
This has unary and PAIR terms only. Conditional on a,b, the energy difference
between y=1 and y=0 is Delta=8(3-2a-2b). Thus the energetically favoured y is
a AND b, with energy margin at least8. This is an elementary cooperative
threshold-energy construction, not a claim that such gates are new ideas.

For the actual continuous-time conformation rates choose
 q(0,1)=exp(-Delta/2), q(1,0)=exp(Delta/2).
Their ratio is exp(-Delta), so they satisfy the exact local detailed-balance
identity for these two states. The largest rate is exp(12), a fixed finite
constant. A rational energy description and a computable exponential specify
these constants; no increasingly strong coupling is used as a device grows.

Independently perturb these rates and an analytical sampling-clock rate1 by
arbitrary predictable factors in[15/16,17/16]. Throughout any held-input read,
the rate toward the correct state is at least
 alpha=(15/16)exp(4)>750/16,
and the rate away is at most beta=(17/16)exp(-4)<17/800.
The sampling hazard lies between15/16 and17/16. Start in either sensor state.
If it starts wrong, consider the event that it corrects before sampling and
is sampled before returning wrong. The competing-hazard bounds, valid under
arbitrary predictable switching, give
 P(correct sample | full preceding history)
 >=[alpha/(alpha+17/16)]*[(15/16)/(15/16+beta)]
 > (750/767)^2 >3/4.
Starting correct only improves this lower bound. Additional irrelevant marks
do not change the hazard comparison. The read has finite expected duration
and a fixed expected number of sensor transitions. Every sensor transition
has absolute energy change at most24, so its actual heat-exchange magnitude
summed over transitions is bounded by24 times the actual transition count.

The inputs are HELD premises here. The pair energies exert backaction on them.
A physical output latch, fuelled sampling/counter, input stabilization and
reset have not been constructed by writing this two-state generator. Nor
does the model identify a diffuse approaching particle with an already
formed input contact. Orientational capture, ports and bond recognition
remain physical operations to implement.

3. A conditional repeated-read reliability certificate without independence

For a fixed unchanged truth bit let X_j=+1 for a correct sample and -1 for a
wrong sample, and Y_j=sum_(h<=j)X_h. The conditional result above gives
 P(X_j=1 | complete sample history)>=3/4.
Therefore 3^(-Y_j) is a nonnegative supermartingale: its conditional multiplier
is at most (3/4)/3+(1/4)*3=1. Stop at Y=+m or -m. The probability of reaching
the wrong boundary is at most3^-m. The conditional drift of Y is at least1/2.
Stopping first at j=K gives E[min(tau,K)]<=2m because Y_(tau wedge K)<=m;
letting K increase gives E tau<=2m. No independent-read assumption is used.
For at most N decisions, m=ceil(log_3(N/epsilon)) pays a union bound with a
fixed sensor energy/rate margin. The counter uses O(m) state/space if embodied
as a unary record; its reliable autonomous embodiment and reversible fuelled
clock are NOT supplied by this probability calculation. Likewise, the
argument fails if an input can change unnoticed during a sequence of reads.

4. The contact-work potential admits a bounded-force extension

Use the accepted nearby inverse-metric core potential Phi_eta for a fixed
hard-ball configuration domain. Its estimates include
 |grad Phi_eta|<=C eta/(alpha d0),
 grad Phi_eta . A0 grad g_j<=-eta/2 at every active contact.
If an additional continuous drift b has |b(Q)|<=K sqrt(n), its Ito contribution
has magnitude at most C eta K sqrt(n)/(alpha d0) dt. Dividing by the contact
coercivity eta/2 adds at most
 C K sqrt(n)/(alpha d0) t
to the expected total clock bound, with the existing potential/end-point,
quadratic-variation and chemical-jump terms retained. This calculation does
not need b to be small. With bounded mobility and per-particle force K, a
bounded-valence pair-energy model satisfies precisely this drift bound.

Thus actual push work remains polynomial after adding FIXED bounded local
interaction forces. It does not follow that the interacting energy has a
polynomial mixing time or grows a prescribed shape. Changes in mobility or
radius retain their paid potential-jump terms. On a growing physical cube,
rescale space/time consistently and charge the resulting polynomial powers
of the side length, inverse ellipticity and force bound; no uncharged
zero-radius or fast-mobility limit is used.

5. What must be specified for a geometric assembly theorem

Actual bonded components, occupied volume, stable neighbouring-block bonds,
finite temperature, finite internal states/valence and finite fuel are new
model data. An initially supplied straight programme seed is information
and prepared matter, not a target-shaped scaffold. Loose feedstock is not
already placed at lattice sites. A software evaluation of neighbours cannot
serve as a physical conformation transition unless its local energetic or
chemical mechanism is supplied. Reverse channels, spent fuel, unsuccessful
attachments, spurious clusters, repairs and all actual costs remain.

At fixed temperature a microscopic defect need not vanish. A functional
block criterion can tolerate defects while still requiring genuine occupied
geometry and inter-block connectivity. This differs from counting the
number of particles of each type, or merely decoding a shape from a word.
Finite fuel can support a specified finite fabrication/retention horizon;
it is not an eternal powered reservoir. Fixed energies cannot be replaced
by energies growing as log(N/epsilon) without charging that different model.
