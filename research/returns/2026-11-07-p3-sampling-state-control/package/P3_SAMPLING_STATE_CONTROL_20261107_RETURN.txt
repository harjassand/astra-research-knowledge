1. RESULT AND SCIENTIFIC QUESTION

The question resolved here is whether whole-state control in the accepted noisy
native family requires learning every unknown local noise rate to a precision
that improves with system size. The answer is no. Fresh source outcomes can be
used directly to randomize physical commands, so that the complete quantum
output has the desired density operator. This is a statement about the full
physical output, including its joint distribution over sites and repeated
outputs, not just about a mean observable.

The construction uses two fixed native settings whose stationary populations
strictly bracket the target. Their populations may be unknown and different at
every site. A calibrated, possibly noisy, measurement supplies source outcomes.
An established Bernoulli factory turns those outcomes into the correct command
distribution without numerically identifying the populations. I supply the
physical mixture identity, its complete-reference and repeated-output lift, a
fully bounded integer-arithmetic approximation, an explicit way to obtain its
fair bits from additional probe outcomes, and a finite native realization.

At fixed positive margins, observation contrast and local relaxation rate:

* The exact command sampler needs O(K) expected control-source outcomes for K
  site-output slots. Its exactness does not imply a hard runtime or a bounded
  expected bit complexity. Those costs are kept separate.
* The bounded compiler uses O(K log(K/epsilon)) source outcomes with a shared
  budget, including a bounded supply of extracted fair bits. A per-site deadline
  schedule uses O(K log^2(K/epsilon)) outcomes. Its logical work and memory are
  explicitly finite. No enumeration of a 2^N-dimensional Hilbert space occurs.
* Finite physical probe resets and output resets follow from the accepted native
  restoration bound. Their time is logarithmic in the relevant accuracy. The
  total physical cost includes the activity of idle resident cells, readout,
  digital control, routing, recovery after measurement, and retained outputs.
  Parallel probe throughput is an input, not inferred from the query count.
* The necessity results identify an exact-sampling information cost, a boundary
  obstruction, a finite-query obstruction, a repeated-output correlation cost,
  and an observation-calibration obstruction. In particular, an observation
  anchor cannot be inferred from these same ambiguous probes even with
  unlimited data.

The complete selected question is resolved at this interface. The full programme
is not relabelled as complete for arbitrary unknown many-body apparatus. This
return removes precise identification of the modeled local noise rates from one
substantial operating regime. It does not acquire an unknown native law, a
calibrated physical reference, arbitrary many-body couplings, or a universal
constant threshold for unknown residual errors.

3. THE INTERFACE, INCLUDING THE OBSERVATION ANCHOR

State error means one-half trace norm. Complete channel error means one-half
diamond norm, hence includes arbitrary finite untouched reference systems.

Start with one two-level factor. Let

    tau(p) = diag(1-p,p),      0 <= p <= 1.

There are two physical settings, cold and hot. Their stationary states are
tau(p_c) and tau(p_h), with unknown fixed real populations. The target tau(q)
lies strictly between them. A supplied calibrated measurement effect E has

    Tr[E tau(p)] = alpha + chi p,       chi > 0.

Thus perfect readout is unnecessary. A symmetric readout flip probability r has
alpha=r and chi=1-2r. More generally the two diagonal entries of E determine
alpha and chi; off-diagonal entries do not affect the stationary probabilities.
If the contrast is negative, complement the outcome. Zero contrast does not
identify this population coordinate.

Write

    c = alpha + chi p_c,    h = alpha + chi p_h,
    x = alpha + chi q.

The controller receives q, alpha, chi, and certified rational bounds

    0 <= c <= c_bar < x < h_under <= h <= 1.

It receives fresh binary outcomes of means c or h on request. It does not
receive c, h, lambda or the actuator-noise parameter. Define

    eta_c = x-c_bar,    eta_h = h_under-x,
    eta = min(eta_c,eta_h),    d = h_under-c_bar = eta_c+eta_h.

The ideal source outcomes are independent, conditional on the fixed apparatus
parameters, and independent of untouched references and previously released
outputs. Finite physical realization replaces this ideal with an explicit
complete error budget below. The parameters need not be known, homogeneous,
rational, or averaged over a prior. The supplied controller constants in the
emitter are rational; arbitrary exact real constants are not free inputs.

The source law and the output law must match. A probe on a different device, an
unwarranted common coefficient, drift between probing and output, or an unknown
measurement effect does not provide this interface. The measurement is an
available instrument with its own reset/recovery, time and activity costs; a
calibrated effect alone does not grant an instrument or free carrier replacement.

4. EXACT CONTROL BY SAMPLING THE COMMAND

For one slot, choose the hot setting with probability

    w(c,h) = (x-c)/(h-c) = (q-p_c)/(p_h-p_c).                    (1)

Then

    (1-w) tau(p_c) + w tau(p_h) = tau(q).                      (2)

There is no estimate in (2). The question is how to sample its unknown weight.

One proposal works as follows. Propose hot with known probability x and cold
with probability 1-x. If hot was proposed, use a linear Bernoulli factory to
produce a rejection bit of mean c/x. If cold was proposed, use a factory on
complemented hot-source outcomes to produce a rejection bit of mean
(1-h)/(1-x). Accept the proposed setting when the rejection bit is zero; otherwise
make a new proposal with fresh source outcomes and fresh auxiliary randomness.

The probabilities of accepting hot and cold in a proposal are exactly

    x-c  and  h-x.

The total acceptance probability is h-c >= d. Therefore the accepted command
has probability (1), and the race terminates almost surely.

For the delivered Huber emitter, set

    e_c = min(eta_c/x,1/2),  e_h = min(eta_h/(1-x),1/2).

The expected number of control-source outcomes per accepted command is at most

    B = 9.5 (1/e_c + 1/e_h) / d.                              (3)

This follows from the governing linear-factory query bound and the renewal
equation for the outer race. The expected query cost of a proposal can correlate
with its acceptance, but the renewal equation still gives E[cost]/P(accept)
because the next proposal uses independent fresh resources. No independence of
cost and acceptance within one proposal is assumed.

The exact emitter uses exact rational probabilities for its known coins. It can
create very large integers on rare paths. Almost-sure finite termination and
the expected unknown-coin count in (3) are not claims of a hard wall-clock bound,
small maximum memory, finite expected bit work, or cheap physical execution.
The next construction, rather than a claim about that tail, supplies those
finite budgets.

5. COMPLETE MANY-BODY AND REPEATED-OUTPUT GUARANTEE

Let the desired state be

    sigma = U [ tensor_i tau(q_i) ] U^dagger,                 (4)

where U and its native dressed implementation are supplied at their actual
contracts. The economical local regime is fixed depth and bounded light cones.
It includes entangled thermal graph states and commuting Hamiltonians obtained
by conjugating independent on-site energies. It does not include every commuting
Gibbs model, all finite-range systems, or generic canonical states.

For example, with two factors at q=1/4 and U=CZ(H tensor H), the partial
transpose has the exact negative eigenvalue -1/16, with normalized eigenvector
(1,-1,-1,-1)/2 in the computational basis. The controlled state is entangled;
the required dressed actuators and probes, rather than classical mixing alone,
supply that physical resource.

Run an independent command sampler for every factor and every newly delivered
copy. Conditional on the fixed unknown parameters, the command distribution
factors. Applying the selected independent reset channels and conjugating by U
then gives exactly (4) in the ideal replacement-channel limit.

For an arbitrary initial rho_SR, an ideal complete replacement produces

    sigma_S tensor rho_R.

The source/controller records are internal. Source queries can be made on the
work register before the final reset: ideal local replacement followed by its
measurement supplies a fresh outcome independent of the untouched reference.
The work state may depend on every earlier command; the final complete
restoration bound is uniform in that conditional work state. Finite departures
from ideal source replacement are paid by a hybrid argument.

For M retained outputs, fresh independent source streams and command samplers
give sigma_1 tensor ... tensor sigma_M, tensored with the untouched reference.
With approximate operations, complete channel errors add under sequential
composition, including when earlier outputs are part of that reference. Holding
those outputs requires their own physical capacity and holding contract; their
continued dynamics is not erased by preparing a later output.

This guarantee is unconditional on newly generated internal source records and
command labels, just as a mixed-state preparation is unconditional on its
discarded classical randomness. It is not the assertion that every conditional
state given a disclosed command label equals sigma. Disclosing those labels as
part of the output changes the specified output channel.

There is also a macroscopic per-realization consequence. The commuting dressed
occupations U |1><1|_i U^dagger in (4) have the product Bernoulli distribution.
For a homogeneous target, the probability that their mean differs from q by
at least u is at most 2 exp(-2 N u^2). A complete trace-error epsilon adds at most
epsilon to this event probability. Independent site commands therefore control
the whole-state fluctuations; a random choice of one hot or cold phase for the
entire system would not have this property.

6. A HARD FINITE-BIT COMPILER

Replace each exact linear factory by the following bounded operation. To
approximate a rejection coin with probability mu/v, where mu <= v-eta, take
n fresh source bits with mean mu and let S be their sum. Generate a rejection
bit with conditional probability min(S/(n v),1). Before clipping, its mean is
exactly mu/v. Its bias is confined to the event S/n > v, whose probability
decreases exponentially with n under the supplied margin.

For the hot proposal, mu=c and v=x. For the cold proposal, mu=1-h and v=1-x.
With exact auxiliary probabilities, the two acceptance masses are

    hot:  b + D_h,       b=x-c,
    cold: a + D_c,       a=h-x,

where

    D_h = E[(S_c/n-x)_+],
    D_c = E[(S_(1-h)/n-(1-x))_+].

Both are nonnegative. Hoeffding gives

    D_h + D_c <= 2 exp(-2 n eta^2).                           (5)

The accepted hot weight is (b+D_h)/(a+b+D_h+D_c). The resulting physical
population differs from q by

    [a D_h - b D_c] / [chi (a+b+D_h+D_c)].                   (6)

Consequently its absolute error is at most

    (D_h+D_c)/chi <= (2/chi) exp(-2 n eta^2).                 (7)

There is no inverse gap in this physical-output bias bound. The gap still
controls how many proposals are needed. Formula (6), rather than an assumed
pointwise accuracy of the sample mean, is the crucial cancellation.

The compiler uses L fair bits for every known decision and rounds its desired
probability down to an L-bit dyadic rational. A proposal has two decisions.
Coupling to the exact known probabilities costs at most 2 T 2^(-L) for a hard
total cap T on the number of proposals. Probabilities equal to zero or one are
also handled by the same integer rule. The finite timeout path always emits a
physical command; it is not postselected away or silently retried.

Let K count all site-output slots being jointly certified. Choose positive
budgets epsilon_c, epsilon_r, epsilon_b for clipping, race timeout and dyadic
rounding. Define integer exponents by exact rational comparison:

    H_c = ceil(log2(2K/(chi epsilon_c))),
    n   = ceil(H_c/(2 eta^2)).                               (8)

Because e > 2, this certifies clipping error at most

    2K / (chi 2^H_c) <= epsilon_c.

Shared-budget schedule. Take

    H_r = ceil(log2(1/epsilon_r)),
    T   = ceil((2K+8H_r)/d),
    L   = ceil(log2(2T/epsilon_b)).                          (9)

In the ideal clipped race, every next proposal succeeds with probability at
least d, including when different sites have different unknown populations.
The number of successes stochastically dominates a binomial count with mean
T d. Since T d >= 2K+8H_r, the Chernoff lower-tail bound gives probability at
most exp(-T d/8) <= 2^(-H_r) of failing to obtain K accepted commands. This is
a bound on the shared cap, not an assumption that all sites finish together.

Thus the complete state-output error from the classical controller alone is

    epsilon_alg <= 2K/(chi 2^H_c) + 2^(-H_r) + 2T/2^L.       (10)

At most nT unknown-source outcomes and 2TL fair bits are requested. For fixed
chi, eta and d, this is O(K log(K/epsilon)) outcomes and fair bits. No unknown
population appears in the emitted controller program.

Per-site-deadline schedule. Alternatively choose

    H_r = ceil(log2(K/epsilon_r)),
    R   = ceil(H_r/d),       T=KR,
    L   = ceil(log2(2KR/epsilon_b)).                         (11)

Each slot makes at most R proposals. The union probability of race timeouts is
at most K (1-d)^R <= K 2^(-H_r). Replace the middle term in (10) accordingly.
This gives O(K log^2(K/epsilon)) source outcomes, with a fixed per-slot deadline.
Its extra logarithm buys a schedule that can be parallelized without assuming
that one very slow site can borrow all of another site's time.

Integer work. Suppose input rational constants have at most B input bits. The
largest threshold arithmetic in the bounded emitter has O(B+L+log n) bits.
With schoolbook arithmetic, a conservative work bound is

    O(nT log(n+1) + T (B+L+log(n+1))^2
                   + Q_f log(Q_f+1)),                     (12)

where Q_f is the separately counted fair-bit source work below. Input reading,
compilation and output are additional; writing K command bits alone costs K.
The logarithmic exponents and ceilings in (8)–(11) are computed using integer
bit lengths and rational comparisons, without uncertified floating-point logs.
Memory can be O(K+B+L+log n+log T) by streaming counts and random bits and
retaining only the current command vector. It is unnecessary to retain the
entire source transcript. Repeated output holding may require more command
memory as described below. Concurrent per-site controllers instead require
their concurrent counters and working storage; that hardware is not supplied
by the serial Python emitter.

7. THE RANDOMNESS SUPPLY IS CONSTRUCTED AND CHARGED

If a separate warranted fair-bit source is supplied, its acquisition and 2TL-bit
consumption are charged directly. It is not necessary to assume one. Use disjoint
pairs of additional cold-source outcomes. Map 01 to zero and 10 to one; discard
00 and 11. For any fixed unknown source probability z in (0,1), the accepted bit
is exactly fair, and its value is independent of the accept/discard pattern.
Successive extracted bits are independent. The expected source-outcome cost per
fair bit is 1/[z(1-z)].

For a hard bound, supply an interval 0<z_under<=z<=z_bar<1. Let

    v = min(z_under(1-z_under), z_bar(1-z_bar)),   s=2v.

For B_f required fair bits, H_f=ceil(log2(1/epsilon_f)) and

    m = ceil((2 B_f+8H_f)/s)                                (13)

source pairs suffice except with probability at most 2^(-H_f). If the cap is
exhausted, the controller emits a declared fallback command vector; it does not
claim an exact fair-bit supply on that path. The complete error budget includes
this probability. All 2m extra source outcomes are charged.

For the per-site schedule, give each slot its own pair cap, put
H_f=ceil(log2(K/epsilon_f)), and use B_f/K bits per slot in (13). The total
failure bound is K 2^(-H_f). This allows distributed extraction using existing
probe capacity. For the pooled implementation, one designated source supplies
the bits; its throughput is explicitly a bottleneck and cannot be counted as
parallel work for free.

This extraction uses the actual noisy probe outcome law. Known readout noise
does not bias the extracted bits. The source must still be independent over
trials, or have a complete approximation to that contract. Reusing observations
as both control samples and randomness is not the construction.

8. NATIVE PHYSICAL REALIZATION WITHOUT A NEW ANALOG CONTROL ORACLE

Use the accepted native family, in its warranted U frame:

    L_i(a) = lambda_i D[X_i]
             + a (D[down_i] + e_i D[X_i]),

with a positive amplitude a constrained by the actual cap. The stationary
population and the accepted complete convergence bound are

    p_i(a) = (lambda_i+e_i a)/(2 lambda_i+(1+2e_i)a),
    distance_complete(e^(t L_i(a)), R_tau(p_i(a)))
                  <= 2 exp(-a t/2).                       (14)

Choose two fixed legal amplitudes a_c>a_h>0. For lambda_i>0 the population
decreases strictly with a. Every target strictly inside the population interval
between those settings has a sampling construction. Thus this applies to the
interior of a native attainable interval, not only to the numerical example.
Approaching an amplitude cap, the population floor, a zero gap, or a zero
restoration rate has an explicit cost. Lambda_i=0 can remove this population
controllability. No negative dissipative rate or infinite switching bandwidth
is introduced.

Fixed unknown offsets in the two realized amplitudes can also be absorbed into
the actual sampled endpoint populations, provided their positive lower bounds,
rate caps, matching source/output settings and convergence certificates remain
warranted. Time-dependent or unmatched offsets are not absorbed by this argument.

The selected setting is held fixed during output preparation. Rapidly switching
between the two settings would average generators and generally give the wrong
stationary state. This construction instead randomizes which entire native
relaxation experiment is carried out at each site. Native simultaneity in the
same U frame is substantive; a list of separately calibrated generators does
not warrant their joint law.

A source outcome is physically acquired by running the requested setting on a
usable carrier for time t_p and applying the supplied measurement instrument.
Arbitrary prior initialization and reference entanglement are covered by (14).
Recovery from a destructive readout, fresh carriers if needed, and routing are
part of the per-trial source contract. No coherent inverse of an unknown
physical evolution is used.

Let Q count all probe outcomes, including random-bit extraction. With
g=min_i a_h,i/2, choose

    H_p = ceil(log2(2Q/epsilon_p)),     t_p=H_p/g,
    H_o = ceil(log2(2K/epsilon_o)),     t_o=H_o/g.             (15)

Then the total complete source-reset error is at most 2Q 2^(-H_p), and the
output-reset error is at most 2K 2^(-H_o). The source error estimate is a
complete instrument hybrid bound: source records may affect later commands,
so a state-only comparison of an isolated trial would not suffice.

For the whole operation,

  epsilon_total <= epsilon_alg + epsilon_f
                    + 2Q 2^(-H_p) + 2K 2^(-H_o)
                    + epsilon_acquisition + epsilon_measurement
                    + epsilon_native + epsilon_logic + epsilon_holding.   (16)

Each additional term must be obtained from its actual interface. For arbitrary
per-call instrument discrepancy delta, a conservative contribution is Q delta.
An integrated complete generator discrepancy contributes its time integral.
These are sufficient budgets, not a constant hardware threshold. Correctly
modeled nonzero lambda_i, e_i and readout errors are already inside the operation;
unknown residual errors are not silently included in those modeled rates.

The existing gate, routing and target-representation costs remain. If physical
U-dressed actuators and probes are absent, a logical circuit for U does not by
itself supply them. The general supplied-circuit restoration compiler remains
available at its earlier, different cost; no efficient native realization of
arbitrary circuits follows from (14).

9. HOLDING, RECOVERY AND REPEATED OUTPUTS

After preparation, keeping each selected command fixed makes its conditional
state tau(p_b) stationary. Therefore the unconditional mixture tau(q) is also
stationary in the joint system-plus-classical-controller model. This is a
holding operation under the same fixed native law, with continuous activity,
stable command memory and constant apparatus parameters charged.

It is not a marginal Markov semigroup with the averaged amplitude. Nor is it
an auxiliary-uniform restoration guarantee for an old controller correlated
with an arbitrary reference. If an intervention exposes or correlates with the
old label, reusing that label can preserve unwanted reference correlations.
Start a new restoration cycle with fresh source outcomes and a fresh command
vector independent of the new input/reference. This reset of the controller
information is part of the repeated-output operation.

Reusing one random command across two emitted qubits gives the correct one-copy
population but introduces covariance

    Cov(n_1,n_2) = w(1-w)(p_h-p_c)^2.

The two-copy trace distance from tau(q) tensor tau(q) is exactly

    2 w(1-w)(p_h-p_c)^2.                                   (17)

Fresh independent labels remove this correlation. Reusing a finite calibration
dataset as an indefinitely repeatable random-controller seed likewise does not
automatically provide independent outputs.

To retain M physical copies, the apparatus must provide the associated carriers
or a separately warranted transfer/storage operation. If their hold times are
h_j, a conservative ongoing activity charge is sum_j N A_max h_j, plus control
memory, readout/routing overhead and residual-error budgets. The labels needed
to keep a retained output stationary must also be retained reliably. When
released, their memory is reset or discarded at its actual physical cost.
Nothing here assigns zero work to record erasure or a universal energy per bit.

10. FULL RESOURCE REGIME AND A COSTED NUMERICAL INSTANCE

    q_site = nR + 2m_site

source outcomes, including its fair-bit supply. Let at most P sites be probed
concurrently by the supplied apparatus. A conservative batched wall time is

    T_wall <= ceil(N/P) q_site (t_p+t_readout+t_recovery+t_routing)
                 + T_digital + t_o + T_extraction.          (18)

All terms are prices of specified operations, not assumed equal or negligible.
The known native activity per resident cell can be bounded by

    A_max = lambda_max + (1+e_max) a_c.

This is a sum-of-squared-jump-norm bound. Its time integral is an activity price,
not a conversion to energy and not a deterministic cap on a realized jump count.

Charging idle as well as actively probed resident cells gives

    activity <= N A_max T_wall + activity_of_other_interfaces

11. HOW THE BRACKET AND CALIBRATION ENTER

The coarse population bracket can be acquired from the supplied probe law. With
n_0 independent records per setting, a two-sided Hoeffding interval of radius r
has a union failure bound at most 4N exp(-2 n_0 r^2) across two settings at N
sites. Therefore a fixed-width bracket can be certified with

    n_0 >= log(4N/zeta)/(2r^2)

records per setting, plus the already charged finite-reset and instrument
errors. Certification requires that the true apparatus have enough interior
margin to pass the selected conservative bounds. Data cannot certify that an
arbitrary outside-bracket apparatus is controllable. A pass flag also does not
turn an unconditional failure probability into the same conditional probability;
either retain the flag in the contract or include the failure/fallback event in
the total operation.

The structural native law, the matched source/output interface and absence of
hidden memory are not proved by population records alone. They remain warranted
physical input. The previous Hamiltonian/noise-learning and calibration results
are credited for their own acquisition tasks.

This construction removes the requirement to estimate each modeled p_c,p_h to
O(epsilon/sqrt(N)) before every large-state preparation. It does not prove that
all estimation-based control strategies cost N^2, nor that all approximate
state controllers obey the exact-query lower bound below. A certify-and-freeze
strategy requiring simultaneous narrow per-site confidence intervals is a
different information product from the unconditional state output here.

An uncertain observation anchor still matters. If the actual effect has
alpha_true, chi_true and the controller uses x=alpha_0+chi_0 q, exact sampling
from the actual observed source populations produces

    q_actual-q = [alpha_0-alpha_true+(chi_0-chi_true)q]/chi_true.  (20)

Similarly, if the source populations differ from the output populations by
fixed offsets u_c,u_h, the population error is the corresponding convex
combination of those offsets. Stable scalar mismatch need not be charged Q
times; its structured bound is sharper. But a fixed nonzero residual population
bias can still make many-copy or many-site states distinguishable.

For product outputs with local errors z_i and a variance floor v_i along the
interval between actual and target populations, the accepted KL/Pinsker argument
gives the complete stationary-output bound

    distance <= sqrt(sum_i z_i^2/(4 v_i)).                   (21)

Unitary dressing preserves this bound. Thus whole-state observation calibration
precision remains substantive. If it is a warranted common calibration law,
that shared parameter can be acquired once at its actual sample cost; if every
detector has an independent unknown bias, their acquisition costs all remain.
No independent detector has been declared calibrated merely because another
one was measured.

12. INFORMATION LOWER BOUND AND EXACT-SAMPLING LIMITS

The selected-reset interface has a useful uniqueness property. The two columns
of its classical population channel are (1-p_c,p_c) and (1-p_h,p_h). Its
determinant is p_h-p_c, which is nonzero. The K-fold tensor channel is therefore
invertible as a linear map on classical command distributions. If the final
unobserved reset calls are independent conditional on their labels, an exact
product physical output tau(q)^tensor K forces the command distribution to be
Bern(w)^tensor K. Correlated labels cannot secretly improve the exact product
state within this interface.

Now compare two source parameter points theta=(c,h) and theta'=(c',h'). Include
all stopping information, source results and auxiliary coins in the transcript.
For any adaptive algorithm with finite expected query counts,

    K D(Bern(w(theta)) || Bern(w(theta')))
       <= E_theta T_c D(Bern(c)||Bern(c'))
          + E_theta T_h D(Bern(h)||Bern(h')).                (22)

This is data processing and the likelihood chain rule for a stopped sequence;
bounded likelihood increments at interior parameters justify the expectation.
It does not assume an unbiased numerical estimator, asymptotic efficiency,
pointwise Cramer–Rao regularity, or a particular factory algorithm.

Put a=h-x, b=x-c, Delta=h-c. Vary one parameter locally in (22). Since
partial_c w=-a/Delta^2 and partial_h w=-b/Delta^2,

    E T_c >= K c(1-c) a/[b Delta^2],
    E T_h >= K h(1-h) b/[a Delta^2].                         (23)

For interior fixed parameters this is a linear expected information cost in K,
even if the same two unknown populations are common to every site. Near one
attainability boundary it diverges inversely with that margin; when both sides
approach x at comparable distance eta it is of order K/eta^2. The factory upper
bound has the same margin orders away from degeneracies. These are lower bounds
for exact output through this selected-reset interface, not for every
approximate estimator, every measurement architecture, or all possible control.

Boundary obstruction. If c=x is included while h>x is an interior coin
probability, exact output requires zero hot commands. Any almost-surely finite
factory that outputs hot with positive probability at some c<x must have a
finite observed cylinder on which it outputs hot. That cylinder has positive
probability at c=x as well. This contradicts exactness. Strict margin is not
just a numerical convenience. Outside [p_c,p_h], convexity already forbids the
target for this one-selected-reset menu. Other native operations can change
that menu and are not ruled out.

Fixed-query obstruction. An algorithm using at most Q unknown-source outcomes
has command probability polynomial in c,h, even with adaptive source selection
and arbitrary parameter-independent randomness. It cannot equal
(x-c)/(h-c) on an open two-parameter region: multiplying by h-c and using
polynomial identity would force x-c=0 along the whole line h=c. Hence exactness
generally requires an unbounded stopping time. The bounded approximation is a
different theorem, not a hidden truncation claimed to stay exact.

13. WHY SELF-CALIBRATION CANNOT BE ASSUMED

Here is an explicit indistinguishability obstruction. Both apparatuses return
the same complete iid probe distributions, with observed cold population 1/10
and hot population 2/5. The physical target is q=1/4.

  Apparatus A: perfect readout; physical populations 1/10 and 2/5.
               Required hot probability: 1/2.
  Apparatus B: symmetric readout flip 1/20; physical populations 1/18 and 7/18.
               Required hot probability: 7/12.

No number of these source observations distinguishes the two apparatuses.
Therefore every source-only controller has the same command distribution in
both. Minimizing the worst one-site physical population error over its hot
probability gives w=31/57 and unavoidable error 1/76. This is a physical-state
error, not merely uncertainty in a reported parameter.

Additional calibrated references, different informative instruments, or a
warranted model can resolve the ambiguity, at their actual acquisition costs.
The example does not prohibit self-calibration with such additional resources.
It establishes that the present observations alone do not earn the missing
anchor. This limit is central to the full programme and prevents the sampling
construction from being promoted to generic unknown-apparatus control.

14. EXECUTABLE EVIDENCE AND VERIFICATION

The package contains two distinct implementations.

  sampling_control.py: the fully bounded rational compiler, finite controller,
    calibrated-contrast accounting, native bracket calculation, and bounded
    source-derived random-bit supply. Its callbacks are actual interface
    obligations: count n fresh observations at an identified site and setting.
    This emitter uses a common rational target/readout coordinate and uniform
    interval bounds, while the site-indexed actual source populations may vary.
    Heterogeneous target coordinates in the theorem use separate plans or the
    displayed formulas with uniform minimum margins and contrasts; the common-
    coordinate executable is not represented as a generic quantum compiler.
  exact_factory.py: the exact rational Huber-based command sampler, with counts
    of source outcomes, fair bits, rational coins and largest integer size.
    Its unbounded-runtime contract is not substituted for the bounded compiler.

run_audits.py performs mathematical and synthetic checks, not experiments:

* Nine rational native-parameter cases verify the exact population identity and
  the same identity through the noisy calibrated readout.
  A separate exact rational partial-transpose witness verifies an entangled
  two-qubit target at the same q=1/4 operating point.
* Four small bounded-controller distributions are enumerated with Fraction
  arithmetic, including clipping, dyadic probabilities and timeout fallback.
  One includes the unsharp calibrated readout. Their actual physical output
  error satisfies the derived bound.
* Six finite resource plans at N=100, 10,000 and 1,000,000 satisfy all rational
  compiler inequalities. The million-slot plans are compiled and checked;
  their billions of source calls are not executed.
* A seeded 2,500-command exact-algorithm fixture produced hot frequency 0.2876,
  compared with 13/45. It consumed 426,185 synthetic control-source outcomes,
  10,753,361 synthetic fair bits and reached a 30,721-bit integer. This illustrates
  why query complexity cannot stand in for all digital costs. The frequency is
  a smoke check, not a proof of exactness; the inputs are pseudorandom fixtures.
* The bounded controller and source-derived randomness both run on small seeded
  fixtures with every source outcome charged. The binomial callback in the
  audit accelerates synthetic generation only; it is not an available physical
  count oracle. The controller itself receives no population parameter.
  A forced all-zero source transcript separately exercises the fair-source
  exhaustion path and verifies that it returns the declared bounded fallback.
* A three-qubit dressed native channel was applied to half of a maximally
  entangled system-reference state. At t=24, its observed trace error is
  0.00054445431, below the analytic complete bound 0.01487251306. This checks
  the implementation on a reference-entangled input; it is not a numerical
  evaluation or proof of the diamond norm.
* At the central parameters, averaging the two amplitudes gives 37/10 and the
  wrong stationary population 137/644. Reusing one label gives two-output trace
  error exactly 1/52. These are explicit tests of the two tempting invalid
  substitutions discussed above.
* The observation-anchor obstruction and its minimax error 1/76 are checked
  by rational arithmetic.

The complete proofs, not empirical frequency agreement or a small Hilbert-space
fixture, establish the scalable result. The exact compiler and mathematical

15. CONSEQUENCE FOR THE RETAINED PROGRAMME

Precise identification and controlled output are different information tasks.
The positive result supplies a repeatable physical density operator, its
reference decoupling, its joint output statistics and its modeled holding law
without first producing a precise numerical description of each unknown noise
rate. It applies throughout a strict interior native population interval and
to bounded-depth dressed many-body targets, with heterogeneous local rates.

The construction pays fresh observations, probabilistic control, carrier reset,
finite relaxation, command storage, and continued activity. Its strongest
economical consequence is a near-linear information/digital regime and, when
parallel probes are actually warranted, a near-linear polylogarithmic native
relaxation-activity regime. The explicit large conservative example is not a
claim of inexpensive laboratory execution.

The negative results also matter. Exact state control cannot obtain arbitrary
numbers of independent outputs from a fixed finite calibration record in this
interface; cannot include a nondegenerate zero-margin boundary with finite
termination; cannot be exact with a uniform finite unknown-query cap on an open
parameter set; and cannot reconstruct an unobserved physical calibration anchor
from indistinguishable source laws. These are structural limits of the actual
operation, rather than gaps concealed by a simulator or an ideal inverse.

The full programme remains repeatable many-body physical-state control under
real information and resource contracts. This return supplies a complete new
operation and a corresponding information boundary within it. It neither
changes that endpoint to estimating a few observables nor asserts that arbitrary
many-body thermodynamics, generic fault tolerance, generic unknown-apparatus
commissioning, or P=NP has been established.
