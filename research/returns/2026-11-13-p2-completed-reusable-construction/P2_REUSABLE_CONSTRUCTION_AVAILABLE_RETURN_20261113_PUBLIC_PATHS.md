1. The specific mechanical proposal

The proposed change is to give each field connection two physically different separation interfaces:

Ordinary interface: retract reusable bearing pins after individually observing their load return and separation.
Exceptional interface: remove the failed contact, its opposing counterhold and the attached member together, leaving the internal jam closed.
Restrained-rack operation: subsequently dissipate the captured internal energy while mechanically retaining the assembly.

The intended benefit is to stop making routine reconfiguration consume sacrificial wafers, while keeping an escape route that does not depend on a jammed ordinary connector becoming reversible.

9. Scaling and the resulting design judgment

Ten or one hundred times as many parts cannot be obtained by extending the same unsupported span. With fixed depth and sections, self-weight bending demand grows rapidly with span, and deflection grows faster still. More roots, deeper structure or different members change the assembly family and access problem.

Replicating separately supported reference frames is a different scaling route. Ten copies of the largest represented output would already contain roughly 4.64 tonnes above the roots; one hundred would contain 46.4 tonnes, before extra machines, roots and depots. Shared machines could reduce duplication but would increase travel, support occupation and recovery queues.

Calibration and prepared interfaces grow with the physical stock. Larger finite-element models can use sparse methods, but the present dense calculation cannot inherit unchanged memory or solution time. More importantly, additional computation does not create a missing extraction corridor or loaded dock.

The most consequential finding from this attempt is that the support and service machinery dominates the design. Replacing sacrificial wafers with reusable pins moved the difficulty into own-tool clearance, reaction accommodation, independent support access, calibration and renewal. The current architecture expanded to a million-dollar, multi-tonne cell while still failing those operations.

The delivered result is therefore a documented, reproducible rejected candidate—not a completed autonomous construction invention. The local energy and observation constructions are useful components of further work, but this revision supplies no complete reusable release, no complete loaded escape across the required family, and no matched whole-service advantage. That conclusion applies to this design; it establishes no general obstruction to autonomous construction and repair.
