ONE-QUBIT COVARIANT CPTP RECEIVERS HAVE SHARP QUADRATIC VISIBILITY

SCOPE. The actual source is the homogeneous partial-SWAP qubit emitter,
U_SE=cI+is SWAP, c=cos(theta),s=sin(theta),0<theta<pi/2, with fresh independent
tracial carriers. The receiver retains one qubit M, initially tau_M, and at
encounter n applies ANY SU(2)-covariant CPTP channel Lambda_n:ME->M.
The map may vary deterministically with n and use fresh pure ancillas,
resets, arbitrary implementation work, and measurements whose outcomes are
discarded. It retains no quantum workspace besides M and no measurement
outcome correlated with the specimen. External specimen-independent random
choices do not help, by convexity.

This does NOT cover trace-decreasing covariant instruments with retained
outcomes/feedback, noncovariant controls with an oriented pure memory, a Bell
purifier retained beside M, or growing-memory receivers. A retained branch
can steer the conditional source signal, invalidating the recurrence below.

CONCLUSION. Uniformly in N and all such time-dependent maps, the acquired
qubit channel is depolarizing with visibility B_N satisfying
  |B_N| <= min{1,s^2[1+8/(3-c)]}.                         (1)
Every final decoder consequently has half-diamond error at least
  epsilon >= (3/4)max{0,1-s^2[1+8/(3-c)]}.               (2)
The optimal visibility is Theta(theta^2): retaining the first emitted carrier
already gives visibility s^2, with no source full SWAP or bath purifier. The
single retained carrier can then be protected and later carriers ignored.
An arbitrary number of collisions cannot turn this nonselective covariant
one-qubit architecture into an accurate arbitrary-input/reference reader.

1. COMPLETE CONTROL REGION

Every covariant TP adjoint has the form
  Lambda^*(sigma_i)=alpha sigma_i^M+beta sigma_i^E
                    +gamma sum_jk epsilon_ijk sigma_j^M sigma_k^E,
with real alpha,beta,gamma. These are the three vector copies in the two-qubit
operator representation. Complete positivity is equivalent to
  alpha+beta<=1,
  3(alpha-beta)^2+12gamma^2<=1+2(alpha+beta).              (3)
The second inequality implies alpha+beta>=-1/2.

In output-first Choi order J=(I+sum_i sigma_i tensor[Lambda^*(sigma_i)]^T)/2.
Time reversal on both input spins makes it invariant. Its spin-3/2 block is
(1-alpha-beta)/2 times identity. In the spin-1/2 multiplicity basis where
the input pair has spin0 or spin1, its block, up to basis phases, is
  (1/2)[[1,-sqrt(3)(alpha-beta)+2i sqrt(3)gamma],
        [-sqrt(3)(alpha-beta)-2i sqrt(3)gamma,1+2(alpha+beta)]].
Positivity of these blocks is exactly (3); TP is already built in.

Fixing the other input at tau gives depolarizing channels, so alpha,beta>=-1/3.
Parallel and antiparallel product inputs give
  |alpha|+|beta|<=1.                                      (4)
Moreover (|alpha|,|beta|,|gamma|) is also in the CP region. Flipping a negative
alpha increases the determinant polynomial by4|alpha|(1+3beta)>=0, and
similarly for beta; (4) preserves the first CP constraint.

2. EXACT SOURCE/RECEIVER RECURRENCE

Write the image of input Z/2 on S,M as
  delta_n=[A_n Z_S+B_n Z_M+C_n(X_S Y_M-Y_S X_M)]/4.
The baseline remains tau_S tensor tau_M. Initially (A,B,C)=(1,0,0).
Direct Pauli multiplication gives
  A'=c^2 A,
  B'=alpha B+s^2 beta A-2s^2 gamma C,
  C'=-cs beta A+(c^2 alpha+cs gamma)C.                    (5)
The same equations hold on all axes. In particular A_n=c^(2n), and discarding
S leaves visibility B_n.

3. CORRELATION LYAPUNOV BOUND

Put kappa=(3-c)/4. Every point in (3) satisfies
  |c alpha+s gamma|+kappa|beta|<=1.                       (6)
By absolute-value closure it suffices to maximize c alpha+kappa beta+s gamma.
At fixed t=alpha+beta the support value is
  (c+kappa)t/2+sqrt((c-kappa)^2+s^2)sqrt((1+2t)/3)/2.
It increases in t, so its maximum is at t=1 and equals one, since
  sqrt((c-kappa)^2+s^2)=2-c-kappa.

For D_n=C_n/c^n, (5) becomes
  D_{n+1}=(c alpha+s gamma)D_n-s beta c^n.
Equation(6) makes |D|<=s/kappa invariant, starting from zero. Hence
  |C_n| <=(s/kappa)c^n.                                  (7)

4. UNIFORM QUADRATIC ACQUISITION BOUND

In addition to (4), (3) implies
  gamma^2<=1-|alpha|.                                    (8)
Indeed, put a=|alpha| and use absolute-value closure. Maximizing gamma^2 at
fixed a over 0<=beta<=1-a gives
  gamma^2<=(1+3a)/9 for0<=a<=1/3,
  gamma^2<=a(1-a) for1/3<=a<=1.
Both are at most1-a.

Unroll the B recurrence. Set u_j=|alpha_j|,
w_j=prod_{l=j+1}^{N-1}u_l. The direct forcing obeys
  sum_j w_j|beta_j|c^(2j)<=sum_j w_j(1-u_j)<=1.
For the correlation forcing, Cauchy-Schwarz and (8) give
  sum_j w_j|gamma_j|c^j
   <=sqrt(sum_j w_j(1-u_j))
     sqrt(sum_j w_j gamma_j^2 c^(2j)/(1-u_j))
   <=sqrt(sum_j c^(2j))<=1/s.
When u_j=1, gamma_j=0 and the quotient uses its zero convention. Therefore
  |B_N|<=s^2+(2s^3/kappa)(1/s)=s^2(1+2/kappa),
proving (1). This bound has no horizon or running-time dependence.

For a final decoder, covariance averaging preserves entanglement fidelity;
the acquired channel is already depolarizing. Recovered Pauli correlations
have magnitude at most|B_N|, so entanglement fidelity is at most
(1+3|B_N|)/4. Half diamond is at least the Bell-input trace error, proving(2).
For B_N<0 a physical qubit decoder cannot flip all axes perfectly; the
absolute-value bound only weakens the converse and is safe.

5. MATCHING SCALE, INCLUDING A WEAK RECEIVER CONTROL EXAMPLE

The simplest matching construction retains the first actual emitted carrier
as M. Its visibility is s^2. This requires no source full SWAP, no bath
purifier, no repeated payload preparation, and no local measurement record.
The receiver may implement it by rerouting/retaining that carrier and then
ignoring the rest. Thus the best visibility in the stated class is
Theta(theta^2), even though the coefficient in (1) is not claimed sharp.

If receiver encounter actions are ALSO restricted to angle theta, choosing
the same partial SWAP at every receiver encounter gives
  alpha=c^2,beta=s^2,gamma=cs,
  C_N=-N c^(2N-1)s^3,
  B_N=N s^4 c^(2N-2)[1+(N-1)s^2].                         (9)
For N~x/s^2, B_N/s^2->x(1+x)e^(-x), maximized at
  x=phi=(1+sqrt(5))/2,
  phi^3 e^(-phi)=.8399620946571751.
This uses one retained qubit, an incoming carrier and one bounded-angle
two-body receiver gate per encounter. It is a construction for weak
visibility, not an accurate reader, and is not better than retaining the
first carrier when simple retention is available.

At theta=.1 the upper visibility is .0497342194473404 and
  epsilon>=.7126993354144947.
The matched weak-action construction at N=162 has B=.008355721358594689.

6. EXECUTION AND PRECISE LIMITS

verify_covariant_qubit.py constructs the full8x8 channel Choi matrix, extracts
Kraus operators, and compares actual three-qubit source/channel evolution
with(5). Across295 channels, including interior and boundary CP points, the
maximum recurrence residual is1.33e-15 and TP residual is0; minimum Choi
eigenvalue is-3.83e-16, consistent with roundoff. Time-dependent trajectories
and matched-angle trajectories check(6)-(8). The receipt is
covariant_qubit_verification.json. These are finite algebraic checks, not
physical experiments or a universal instrument theorem.

A covariant trace-decreasing branch may have
  Lambda_y^*(I)=a_y I+b_y sigma_M dot sigma_E.
Conditioning on it can change the source Pauli coefficient through existing
correlations, even when its probability is independent of the unknown input
direction. Then A_n=c^(2n) and(5) do not describe each retained history.
Retained classical outcome feedback is therefore genuinely outside this
proof. Fresh pure implementation ancillas are included only when discarded
within the CPTP update; retaining one coherently enlarges the memory model.
