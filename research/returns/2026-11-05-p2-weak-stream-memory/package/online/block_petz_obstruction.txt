DISJOINT-BLOCK PETZ COMPRESSION CANNOT REDUCE THE ACQUISITION MEMORY SCALE
New restricted-architecture result for the fixed homogeneous weak stream.
The accepted exact partial-SWAP channel identities are premises, not revalidated.

1. Actual operation under consideration

A qubit source interacts chronologically with independent fresh tracial qubits
through U(theta)=cos(theta) I+i sin(theta) SWAP. The original source is discarded.
Partition the outgoing stream into predetermined consecutive blocks. On a block
of b outputs the receiver prepares an independent tracial dummy, applies the b
inverse collisions in reverse order, discards those b outputs, and retains the
dummy. It may keep every resulting dummy and apply an arbitrary collective
final CPTP decoder, with arbitrary independent initialized workspace.

Thus this is an actual causal block compression, not an assumption that block
outputs are independent. The original source remains correlated with earlier
block outputs, and those correlations are propagated in the proof. The theorem
does not concern arbitrary block instruments or a receiver allowed to retain
additional registers carrying information from a completed block.

Errors are half diamond distance from the identity qubit channel.

2. The block obstruction

Write r_b=(1-sin(theta)^4)^b and alpha_b=1-r_b. If every compressed block has
length at most B, every final decoder obeys

    epsilon >= (3/4)[1-sqrt(1-r_B)].                           (1)

The bound is uniform in the number of blocks, even with unlimited storage and
processing of their one-qubit compressed outputs. Therefore epsilon<3/4 requires

    B >= log(1/[1-(1-4epsilon/3)^2])
         /[-log(1-sin(theta)^4)].                             (2)

At small epsilon this is Theta(theta^-4 log(1/epsilon)), the original acquisition
scale. A block decoder retaining all b physical outputs to reverse them must
therefore retain an original-scale block. Dividing into small Petz-decoded
blocks and combining their outputs does not provide genuinely sublinear peak
quantum storage. This is not a universal memory lower bound for this stream.

3. Proof without replacing correlations by marginals

Use normalized Hilbert--Schmidt operator norms at the tracial reference. Track
the Heisenberg-encoded image of one initial Pauli, decomposing it as P+Q, where P
is identity on the actual source and Q has zero source partial trace. Old-output
operator coefficients are arbitrary. Let p=||P||_2^2 and q=||Q||_2^2; initially
(p,q)=(0,1).

For a b-collision block, the accepted exact Gram calculation exports alpha_b of
every Q norm into its new source-identity component and leaves r_b in Q. Its
normalized output channel is B_b(X)=Tr_S[U_b(X tensor I)U_b^*]/2. Let Dhat_b be
the physical reverse-dummy decoder rescaled between the normalized operator
spaces. The accepted recovery identity is

    Dhat_b B_b(X) = alpha_b X

for every traceless X. Therefore, after the block is replaced by its dummy,

    p_new = p + alpha_b^2 q.                                  (3)

The old P term is unchanged and identity on the new dummy. The exported term is
traceless on that dummy, so the two terms are orthogonal. This remains true with
arbitrary old-output coefficients.

Dhat_b is a normalized-trace-preserving unital completely positive map, hence
an L2 contraction. It acts only on bath outputs and commutes with the source
P/Q projection. Thus

    q_new <= r_b q,       q-q_new >= alpha_b q.                (4)

Combining (3)-(4), for alpha_max=max_blocks alpha_b,

    p_new-p <= alpha_max (q-q_new).

Telescoping gives p_final<=alpha_max(1-q_final)<=alpha_max. The same bound holds
for each input Pauli and its polarized Gram form. The entire retained-dummy
baseline is tracial. Consequently each encoded Pauli has trace norm at most
2 sqrt(alpha_max). Applying the accepted Weyl/Bell-fidelity converse (or its
direct two-dimensional proof) yields

    epsilon >= (3/4)(1-sqrt(alpha_max)),

which proves (1). A collective final operation cannot restore the lost norm.
Intermediate processing on previously retained dummies can be deferred to the
end when the subsequent block schedule and block operation are predetermined.
Input-independent random schedules obey the same bound when B is a uniform
cap. Measurement-adaptive block selection is not claimed in this note.

4. Exact source/dummy channel: diagnostic strengthening

For one block, write c=cos(theta), s=sin(theta), and
K_z=X_S Y_M-Y_S X_M. The unnormalized Pauli deviation maps to

    A_b Z_S + B_b Z_M + C_b K_z,

where

    A_b=c^(2b),   B_b=1-(1-s^4)^b,   C_b=-b c^(2b-1)s^3.       (5)

The source/dummy baseline is I/4. Joint SU(2) covariance leaves only these
three vector components. Adding the last source collision and the first
reverse-dummy collision, then contracting the older block, gives

    A_(b+1)=c^2 A_b,
    B_(b+1)=B_b+s^4(1-B_b),
    C_(b+1)=c^2 C_b-c s^3 A_b,                                (6)

with (A_0,B_0,C_0)=(1,0,0).

One way to verify the last contraction analytically is to expand the older
encoded Pauli as J_z=I tensor B_z+sum_a sigma_a tensor A_az. The Pauli identity
J_y J_z=i J_x, traced against sigma_x, determines the antisymmetric coefficient
Tr(A_yy A_zz-A_zy A_yz)/dim(E)=A_b. The apparent cross terms cancel: covariance
makes Tr(B_a A_bc) proportional to epsilon_abc, with opposite signs in the two
terms. This supplies the coefficient A_b multiplying the new K_z contribution.
The B recurrence follows by contracting sum_a sigma_a tensor A_az=J_z-I tensor B_z
under the inverse older circuit. Solving (6) gives (5).

For equal blocks the EXACT source-traceless norm multiplier is

    q_b=A_b^2+2 C_b^2,

and after K blocks the retained-dummy Pauli Gram parameter is

    p_(K,b)=B_b^2 (1-q_b^K)/(1-q_b).                          (7)

Thus even the remaining source/dummy correlations need not be guessed.
For b=Theta(theta^-2), B_b=Theta(theta^2), q_b stays bounded away from one,
and p_(infinity,b)=Theta(theta^4). This attempted compression destroys the
source/past relational information on the source-forgetting timescale.

Equation (1) does not rely on (5)-(7); its proof uses only the accepted exact
block Petz identity and complete-positive-map contraction.

5. Implementation and scope

Each block uses b receiver inverse two-qubit gates and one tracial dummy. With
a physical stack or appropriate direct-address buffer, processing can be linear
in the original collision count, but a successful operation requires an
original-scale block buffer by (2). Storing only one dummy per block is not a
source-sufficient statistic. The information discarded with the rest of a block
cannot be treated as refreshed noise or replaced by an independent state.

General quantum instruments, pure initialized receiver workspace, adaptive
subsystem encodings, or a different causal compression are not excluded. The
full homogeneous-stream target remains unresolved by this result.
