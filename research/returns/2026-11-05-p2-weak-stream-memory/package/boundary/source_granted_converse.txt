GENERAL PREFIX CONVERSE FOR THE HOMOGENEOUS TRACIAL PARTIAL-SWAP STREAM
Working mathematical result; 5 November 2026

STATUS AND SCOPE

This file proves a general, source-specific receiver converse in terms of
the exact bath-scattering singular spectrum.  It strengthens a minimum-gap
bound to a density-of-states bound, including adaptive memory tails.  It
also gives an exact finite transfer-matrix calculation of every spectral
moment and two analytic trial families.

It does NOT establish the required large-prefix density-of-states estimate,
an asymptotically sharp causal memory lower bound, or a new causal decoder.
In particular, no one-percent memory claim at theta=0.1 follows from the
proved minimum-gap result.  The distinction is substantive.

All warm partial-SWAP acquisition and converse results are accepted premises.
Their files are not modified.  Half diamond means ||A-B||_diamond/2.
There is no bath purifier, preparation label, return link, or source control.

1. PHYSICAL PREFIX AND THE FREE-SOURCE RELAXATION

Let c=cos(theta), s=sin(theta), r=1-s^4.  At prefix length ell put D=2^ell,
B=E_1 ... E_ell, U_ell=U_{S,E_ell} ... U_{S,E_1}, and
U_{S,E_j}=c I+i s SWAP.  The carriers enter in I/2.  Define

  J_a=U_ell (sigma_a tensor I_B) U_ell^*, a=x,y,z.

The actual source-plus-prefix encoding is

  E_ell(rho)=U_ell(rho tensor I_B/D)U_ell^*.

Any causal receiver's action before this cut is a channel/instrument

  C : B -> direct_sum_y M_y,

with arbitrary classical outcomes y and branch quantum dimensions m_y.
Every coherent register, unprocessed carrier, delay line, and ancillary
system crossing the cut belongs to M_y.  Pure initialized workspace,
arbitrary measurements and feedback, and arbitrary earlier block operations
are allowed.  The classical outcome space need not have bounded cardinality.

Grant the decoder the actual source S at this cut for free.  Its later
operation can simulate all subsequent source collisions and receiver actions
with fresh tracial carriers.  This is a relaxation because the original
source is in fact discarded.  It is valid because the emitter follows the
fixed known program and the receiver has no return interaction that changes
the emitter.  Thus any final recovery of the original payload/reference with
half-diamond error epsilon also gives a decoder of

  (id_S tensor C) E_ell

with the same error.  Assume 0<=epsilon<=1/2 throughout.

2. THE EXACT SPECTRAL OPERATOR

Define the bath channel and its Hilbert--Schmidt adjoint by

  T_ell(A)=(1/2) Tr_S U_ell(I_S tensor A)U_ell^*,
  T_ell^*(A)=(1/2) Tr_S U_ell^*(I_S tensor A)U_ell.

They are unital, trace preserving, and contractions in Hilbert--Schmidt norm.
On the D^2-dimensional Hilbert space of bath operators put

  H_ell=I_super-T_ell T_ell^*.

Its eigenvalues satisfy

  0=lambda_0 <= lambda_1 <= ... <= lambda_(D^2-1) <= 1,

where the identity is a zero eigenoperator.  There may be other zero modes
at exceptional angles; no unproved exact irreducibility claim is needed in
the theorems below.  Define kappa_ell=(8/3)lambda_1.

The logical-Pauli commutator map is exactly

  Phi(K)=(1/6) sum_a Tr_S[J_a(I_S tensor K)J_a]
        =(4 T_ell T_ell^*(K)-K)/3.

To verify this, apply sum_a sigma_a X sigma_a=2 I_S tensor Tr_S X-X
inside conjugation by U_ell.  Therefore, for every complex bath matrix K,

  (1/3) sum_a ||[I_S tensor K,J_a]||_2^2
       =(16/3) <K,H_ell K>
       >=2 kappa_ell ||K-(Tr K/D)I||_2^2.

Norms in this identity are unnormalized Hilbert--Schmidt norms.

3. THE COMMON PETZ WITNESS AND ITS ENERGY

Put sigma=C(I_B/D).  On its support define

  R(X)=C^*(sigma^(-1/2) X sigma^(-1/2))/D.

This is the transpose/Petz recovery for the maximally mixed bath reference.
Each classical branch has its own trace-preserving extension on the unused
support.  The bath channel W=R C is trace preserving and unital, and it
factors through precisely the same hybrid memory as C.  R is an analytic
witness, not a decoder supplied to the physical receiver.

Apply the actual successful decoder to the two logical eigenstates for each
Pauli a.  Their optimal distinguishability after id_S tensor C is t_a, where

  t_a=||(id_S tensor C)(J_a)/(2D)||_1 >=1-2 epsilon.

The transpose recovery for id_S tensor C factors as id_S tensor R.  Its
Pauli correlations are

  q_a=Tr[J_a (id_S tensor W)(J_a)]/(2D) >= t_a^2.

For completeness, writing x=(id_S tensor C)(J_a)/(2D) and
sigma_tilde=I_S/2 tensor sigma, the correlation is
Tr[x sigma_tilde^(-1/2) x sigma_tilde^(-1/2)].  Weighted Cauchy--Schwarz
with sign(x), followed by sqrt(sigma_i sigma_j)<=(sigma_i+sigma_j)/2,
bounds ||x||_1^2 by this correlation.  This proves the inequality without
requiring that the physical decoder reconstruct every bath output.

Let K_alpha be Kraus matrices of W.  Define its normalized Choi state by

  Z=(1/D) sum_alpha |K_alpha>><<K_alpha|.

Both marginals of Z are I/D, because W is trace preserving and unital.
Using the commutator identity and trace preservation gives exactly

  E := Tr(H_ell Z)
     =(1/D) sum_alpha <K_alpha,H_ell K_alpha>
     =(3/4)(1-(q_x+q_y+q_z)/3)
     <=3 epsilon(1-epsilon)=:e_epsilon.

This is the central conversion: successful payload recovery forces a
bistochastic Choi state with small energy for the bath singular operator.

4. UNIFORM BRANCH CAPACITY: GAP AND SPECTRAL-PROFILE THEOREMS

Assume m_y<=m<=D in every classical branch.  Every composite Kraus matrix
K_alpha through that branch has rank at most m.  Thus Z has Schmidt number
at most m.  Two consequences follow.

4.1 Minimum-gap version

H_ell>=lambda_1(I-|I>><<I|/D).  The entanglement fidelity
F_e(W)=<<I|Z|I>>/D is at most m/D by Kraus rank.  Consequently

  m/D >=1-e_epsilon/lambda_1
       =1-8 epsilon(1-epsilon)/kappa_ell,

whenever kappa_ell>0.  The right side may be negative, in which case the
inequality has no nontrivial content.

4.2 Full spectral-profile version

A rank-m vector |K>> satisfies the reduction inequality

  |K>><<K| <= m I tensor Tr_left(|K>><<K|).

Proof: in its Schmidt decomposition, Cauchy--Schwarz bounds
|sum_{i=1}^m sqrt(p_i) z_ii|^2 by m sum_i p_i |z_ii|^2, which is at
most the quadratic form of the right side.  Sum this inequality over a
Schmidt-rank-m decomposition of Z and use its right marginal I/D:

  0<=Z<=(m/D) I,  Tr Z=1.

Let a=m/D and k=floor(1/a).  Filling the lowest eigenspaces of H_ell up to
this density cap yields the necessary condition

  a sum_{j=0}^{k-1} lambda_j +(1-ka)lambda_k <= e_epsilon.

If D/m=L is an integer, this simplifies to

  (1/L) sum_{j=0}^{L-1} lambda_j <= 3 epsilon(1-epsilon).

Thus a small number of very slow modes need not invalidate a strong memory
bound.  Their total number, and not only their smallest gap, now matters.
The density-cap relaxation discards the detailed marginal and Schmidt-number
constraints, so it is necessary rather than an exact characterization of
compressibility.

4.3 Mode-count and heat-trace corollaries

Let n_ell(a)=rank 1_[0,a)(H_ell), for a>0.  Then

  m >= (D/n_ell(a)) [1-e_epsilon/a]_+.

Let Z_ell(t)=Tr exp(-t H_ell), t>0.  Jensen's inequality and the density
cap give

  m >= D exp(-t e_epsilon)/Z_ell(t), for every t>0.

These are explicit targets for a density-of-states calculation.  A useful
result requires an upper bound on n_ell or Z_ell, not just a trial operator
with a small Rayleigh quotient.

5. VARIABLE QUANTUM MEMORY, UNLIMITED CLASSICAL RECORDS

Let p_y be the branch probability under I_B/D.  This is the branch law of
the physical Bell-input experiment, since the source-plus-bath marginal is
maximally mixed before compression.  The Petz Choi state decomposes into
positive Z_y with Tr Z_y=p_y and Schmidt number at most m_y.

For a threshold m, put Z_low=sum_{y:m_y<=m}Z_y and
p_high=P(m_y>m).  Its relevant marginal is at most I/D.  The same reduction
inequality therefore gives Z_low<=(m/D)I, even though the individual
conditional branch marginals need not be uniform.

For every a>0:

  P(m_y>m) >= [1-e_epsilon/a-m n_ell(a)/D]_+.

For every t>0:

  P(m_y>m) >= [exp(-t e_epsilon)-(m/D) Z_ell(t)]_+.

The first follows by bounding the low-memory weight in the slow subspace
and charging at least a for its remaining weight.  The second follows from
Tr[Z exp(-tH)]>=exp(-tE), from the density cap on Z_low, and from the
bound exp(-tH)<=I on high-memory branches.  Thus there is no assumption of
a fixed branch dimension, bounded classical entropy, or a fixed instrument
history.

If q_y is the actual number of coherent qubits, summing these threshold
bounds at m=2^j, j=0,...,ell-1 gives a lower bound on E[min(q_y,ell)].
The parameters a or t may be optimized separately at each threshold.
Summing such bounds over physical time intervals gives qubit-time.  It is
essential to use the actual branch distribution and actual fixed cuts;
adding maximum capacities of mutually exclusive branches is not an
expected-occupancy calculation.

The simpler gap form also gives

  E[2^(min(q_y,ell)-ell)] >= 1-e_epsilon/lambda_1,

and hence by the convex chord

  E[q_y] >= ell [1-e_epsilon/lambda_1-2^(-ell)]_+/(1-2^(-ell)).

6. EXACT MOMENTS AS FINITE TRANSFER MATRICES

Use the normalized Pauli basis sigma_0=I,sigma_1=X,sigma_2=Y,sigma_3=Z.
Let G be the real orthogonal 16 by 16 matrix of Ad_(cI+i s SWAP):

  G_(a',q;a,p)=Tr[(sigma_a' tensor sigma_q)
                    U (sigma_a tensor sigma_p) U^*]/4.

For fixed bath input/output p,q define the 4 by 4 source block
G^{qp}_{a'a}=G_(a',q;a,p).  Then

  (T_ell)_(q_1...q_ell,p_1...p_ell)
       =<0|G^{q_ell p_ell} ... G^{q_1 p_1}|0>.

For an integer k>=1, define the 16^k by 16^k matrix

  M_k = sum_(x_1,...,x_(2k)=0)^3
         tensor_(r odd) G^{x_r x_(r+1)}
         tensor_(r even) G^{x_(r+1) x_r},

with tensor factors in the order r=1,...,2k and x_(2k+1)=x_1.  Direct
contraction of the cyclic physical indices in Tr[(T T^*)^k] gives

  mu_k(ell):=Tr[(T_ell T_ell^*)^k]
            =<0|^(tensor 2k) M_k^ell |0>^(tensor 2k).

Also mu_0=D^2, and exactly

  Z_ell(t)=exp(-t) sum_(k>=0) (t^k/k!) mu_k(ell).

Truncation after k=K incurs absolute error at most

  D^2 P(Poisson(t)>K),

because 0<=T T^*<=I.  This makes finite-precision truncation explicit.
Constructing/contracting M_k directly is exponential in k, not in ell for
fixed k.  Dense repeated squaring has arithmetic cost O(16^(3k) log ell)
and O(16^(2k)) storage, before exploiting tensor structure.  The local
matrix G and the power calculation must be evaluated at adequate precision;
near-cancellation is not automatically controlled by a floating-point run.

The first moment has a closed expression:

  mu_1(ell)=4^(ell-1)(1+3 c^(4ell)).

Proof: T_ell has a Stinespring environment comprising the physical source
and a mathematical purifying source qubit, of dimension four.  On a
maximally mixed bath input, the environment density matrix is the Choi
state of the source depolarizing channel with visibility c^(2ell).  Its
eigenvalues are (1+3c^(2ell))/4 and (1-c^(2ell))/4 three times.  The Choi
state of T_ell has the same nonzero spectrum.  Its purity is
(1+3c^(4ell))/4, while superoperator squared norm equals D^2 times that
Choi purity.  This mathematical purification supplies no physical bath or
source access to the receiver.

An important cost obstruction follows already from rank at most four:

  mu_k(ell)>=D^2/4^k.

Indeed mu_1/D^2>=1/4, and Jensen applies to the D^2 nonnegative eigenvalues
of T T^*.  Consequently the elementary moment bound
n_ell(a)<=mu_k/(1-a)^k cannot reduce the count below D unless k is of order
ell (for a fixed useful threshold a<3/4).  A fixed-replica transfer
calculation is not by itself an efficient large-ell solution.  Likewise
the heat parameter must be of order ell before a heat-trace bound can
certify a linear number of qubits.  A new analytic bound uniform in this
replica/length regime is still required.

7. EXACT QUARTIC EXTENSION OF TRIAL MODES

For a normalized traceless bath input A on an old prefix of length j,
append identity on all later carriers.  Decompose the evolved operator into
source identity and source traceless parts.  The accepted exact collision
recursion contracts the squared norm of the latter by r=1-s^4 per future
collision, including arbitrary old-output coefficients.  Thus

  1-||T_ell(A tensor I)||_(2,normalized)^2
       =r^(ell-j) [1-||T_j(A)||_(2,normalized)^2].

In particular

  kappa_ell <= r^(ell-j) kappa_j.

This is an UPPER bound on the minimum gap.  It is not the lower bound that
would be needed for a positive memory converse.  It also implies ordinary
monotonicity kappa_(ell+1)<=kappa_ell.

At two bath sites the SU(2)-scalar traceless swap operator
K=SWAP-I/2 is an eigenoperator of both T_2 and T_2^*, with eigenvalue r.
Therefore, for every ell>=2,

  kappa_ell <=(8/3)(1-r^2)r^(ell-2).

At weak action the prefactor is (16/3)theta^4+O(theta^6).  This proves why
the minimum-gap version alone cannot address a fixed one-percent error
when theta=0.1: even its best possible right side is already vacuous at
ell=2.  The spectral-profile or cumulative-loss problem cannot be avoided
by a more accurate computation of the smallest gap.

8. AN EXACT RESTRICTED LOSS GRAM

For one fixed Pauli axis consider A=sum_i v_i sigma_a^(i) on ell sites.
Its normalized squared input norm is sum_i |v_i|^2.  The final source
traceless squared norm is v^* L v, where exactly

  L_ii=(1-c^4) r^(ell-i),
  L_ij=-s^4 c^(2|i-j|) r^(ell-max(i,j)), i!=j.

Reason: the i-th site's Pauli produces source-traceless norm 1-c^4 when
first met, followed by r-contraction.  For i<j, the component with identity
on all earlier bath sites is s^2 c^(2(j-i-1)) times a source Pauli just
before collision j.  Its source-traceless overlap with the Pauli arriving
at j is -c^2 s^2.  Later collisions contract this cross Gram by r as well.

Writing x=s^2, q=c^2/sqrt(r)=sqrt((1-x)/(1+x)), and
D0_ii=r^((ell-i)/2), the matrix factors as

  L=D0 [2x I-x^2 K_ell(q)] D0,
  K_ell(q)_ij=q^|i-j|.

The Toeplitz norm bound ||K_ell(q)||<=(1+q)/(1-q)=(1+sqrt(r))/x gives

  lambda_min(L)>=r^(ell-1) x(1-sqrt(r)).

This last lower bound concerns ONLY this restricted single-Pauli trial
space.  It is NOT a lower bound on the full commutator gap.  In the full
space it only supplies variational upper bounds
kappa_ell<=(8/3)lambda_min(L).  Scalar and higher-weight operators are
already slower at short prefixes.

9. EXECUTED FINITE DIAGNOSTICS AND THEIR LIMITS

commutant_probe.py computed the dense logical-commutator map for ell<=4.
singular_gap_profile.py applies the exact bath superoperator in the Pauli
basis without forming a D^2 by D^2 dense matrix.  Its eigensolver estimates
the largest nonidentity singular value squared; it reports the residual.
The local-transfer implementation reproduced the dense ell=4 value.

At theta=0.3 the computed kappa values for ell=4,...,8 are
  .00336428021407, .00147815258686, .000739554956982,
  .000408527485701, .000244986390525.
Reported eigenvector residuals are at most 9.8e-12.

At theta=0.1 the values for ell=4,...,8 are
  .0000589258899124, .0000262317715638, .0000134495655188,
  .00000760107763457, .00000463566645514.

At theta=0.6 the values for ell=4,...,8 are
  .0192285627059, .00773113881798, .00350185866106,
  .00175760653475, .000961369269619.

These short-prefix profiles resemble polynomial suppression in length.
They do not determine the joint weak-angle/long-prefix asymptotic regime.
They are not interval-certified spectral bounds, and a small eigensolver
residual by itself does not prove no missed eigenvalue lies closer to one.

The restricted Gram in section 8 was checked against dense scattering at
theta=.3, ell=1,...,4, with maximum entry error 3.4e-16.
transfer_witness.py checks another constant-bond transfer-operator trial;
those trials did not resolve the gap or produce a receiver construction.

10. PRIMARY MACHINERY AND WHAT REMAINS

The transpose recovery and rank/Schmidt-number methods are existing
machinery.  A primary reference for Schmidt number and its witnesses is:
  B. M. Terhal and P. Horodecki, A Schmidt number for density matrices,
  Phys. Rev. A 61, 040301(R) (2000), arXiv:quant-ph/9911117.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9911117

The relevant homogeneous partial-SWAP bath transfer structure has substantial
integrability prior art:
  P. W. Claeys, J. Herzog-Arbeitman and A. Lamacraft,
  Correlations and commuting transfer matrices in integrable unitary circuits,
  SciPost Phys. 12, 007 (2022), arXiv:2106.00640.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.00640

Its homogeneous transfer operators are generally nonnormal and have growing
Jordan blocks.  Eigenvalue Bethe formulas are not singular-value profile
bounds.  The parameter/wire convention must be checked explicitly before
importing a formula; the one- and two-site spectra match the identification
lambda=cot(theta).  No new proof here relies on that identification.

What is completed here is the exact conversion from arbitrary causal hybrid
memory to a low-energy, bounded-Schmidt-number Choi state for this physical
stream, its spectral-profile and branch-tail consequences, and a finite
moment contraction with explicit exponential replica cost.  What remains is
a useful analytic profile estimate or a constructive causal compression
showing that the converse is loose.  Exact closure of a large operator
algebra, a small MPO bond, or a small source Hilbert space supplies neither
of those conclusions by itself.
