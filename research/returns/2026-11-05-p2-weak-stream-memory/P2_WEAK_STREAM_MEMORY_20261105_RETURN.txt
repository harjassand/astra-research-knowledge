7. Relation to the strongest applicable existing machinery

Efficient Schur circuitry addresses implementation of a representation change. The lost-information calculations above explain why that change alone does not yield the required compression.

Coherent “roll-up” methods can gather pure finite state families, including suitable bounded-bond-dimension states, into small quantum memory. Their mixed i.i.d. extension uses permutation symmetry. The present outputs are ordered, correlated mixed states; those assumptions do not establish a sufficient quantum statistic for this stream.

arxiv.org

Against the accepted all-output decoder, this work has not yet improved the acquired arbitrary-input operation. Its completed contribution is the general prefix converse and the exact identification of several information-destroying reductions.

The remaining endpoint is precise: either construct a CPTP causal receiver on the actual once-through carriers with reference-uniform recovery at the quartic collision scale and a genuinely sublinear total coherent-memory bill, or prove a general lower bound for this source with a matching operation. The spectral theorem provides a route to the latter, but its decisive estimate is still missing. None of the restricted failures above substitutes for it.
