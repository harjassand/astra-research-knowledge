Low-return shear loading and its actual coefficient-blind boundary

Scope. The nonlinear flux-response question retains its full twelve-force,
unknown-data/history, all-disturbance/all-energy-weak and universal-cost
contract. Prepared weak packets now have an actual joint-background
readout/clearing theorem. This note investigates the remaining loading
operation. Ideal operators below are NOT additional physical controls.

1. An exact ideal register-to-packet identity

Let kappa=2pi, F_Q=e1 sin(kappa Q x2), Q a positive integer. Its shear
flow is x1->x1+a sin(kappa Q x2). Let M_a be its induction pushforward.
For an unknown scalar mean-zero register r(x3), initially
 B=H+A0 r(x3)e2,
the exact ideal result is
 M_a B=H+A0 r(x3)e2
       +a kappa Q cos(kappa Q x2)[H2+A0 r(x3)]e1.
There is no observation, division by r or separate experiment. The old
register remains and an unknown-value packet is produced. The known
coefficient a can be rational; kappa can be enclosed as in the original
compiler. Taking a=sigma/A0 makes the leading packet amplitude Q sigma
times the register, independently of how large Q is. This is distinct
from a kinetic reload whose amplitude requires A0 proportional to Q.

The identity is only an ideal single-register loader. It does not route
all registers, produce arbitrary output polarizations or prove physical
memory preservation. F_Q is not one of the twelve actuators for Q>1.
Actual generation/lowering of this shear and its full coupled reference
must be paid. In particular it cannot be implemented by declaring a
magnetic pushforward to be an allowed actuator.

2. The exact Euler low-frequency second return is uniform in Q

For L_F v=P[(F.grad)v+(v.grad)F], the ideal linearized velocity coordinate
is E_a=exp(-a L_F). Write a complex solenoidal velocity mode u_m, m.u_m=0,
and temporarily use F_Q=e1 cos(kappa Q x2). A first application sends it
to k_s=m+s Qe2, s=+/-1, with coefficient i kappa A_s(m)u_m, where
 A_s(m)u=(1/2)P_(m+sQe2)[m1 u+sQ u2 e1].
Thus the exact return to m after TWO applications is
 (L_F^2 u)_m=-kappa^2 C_Q(m)u_m,
 C_Q(m)=sum_s A_(-s)(m+sQe2) A_s(m).
Set t=|m|^2 and D_Q=(t+Q^2)^2-4Q^2 m2^2. Direct projection gives
 C_Q(m)u=(m1^2/2)u
  +[Q^2 m1 m2(t-Q^2)/D_Q]u2 P_m e1
  -[Q^2 m1^2(t+Q^2)/D_Q]u2 P_m e2.
For Q>=4|m|, D_Q is bounded below by a positive absolute multiple of
Q^4. Consequently
 ||C_Q(m)u|| <= C |m|^2 |u|,
uniformly in Q. The same low return applies to the sine quadrature.
If m1=0 the complete second application vanishes on this input mode.
As Q tends to infinity the displayed expression tends to
 (m1^2/2)u-m1 m2 u2 P_m e1-m1^2 u2 P_m e2.

This shows why the first high velocity sideband of size aQ need not
produce a low memory distortion of size a^2 Q^2. The leading low return
is instead O(a^2 |m|^2). For a low band N, its SECOND-ORDER Taylor
contribution costs O(a^2 N^2). This is an exact finite Fourier identity,
not an operator estimate for the entire exp(-a L_F), for arbitrary rough
incoming modes, or for physical MHD. No uniform all-orders Taylor radius
or full prefix bound is asserted here. Some intermediate wave numbers
are near Q; a low-band computation alone does not control those errors.

An exact Fraction diagnostic compared the two projection products with
the formula on solenoidal projections of all three coordinate vectors,
at m=(1,0,0),(1,2,3),(2,-1,1), Q=32,64,256:27 identities agreed exactly.
This diagnostic checks the algebra; it does not replace the displayed
general estimate or establish a finite-angle/rough-data theorem.

3. A noncircular scale opening, conditional on the missing physical law

Let the desired weak magnetic packet size be sigma, with Nw polynomial
in the target envelope and sigma^-2. If a physical load-and-clear law
had low velocity distortion O(a^2 poly(Pu,N)) and high velocity packet
size O(Q a poly(Pu)), the above choice a=sigma/A0 would give total low
distortion and weak-window Reynolds contamination controlled by
 Nw sigma^2 poly(Pu,N)/A0^2.
Choose A0 from the VELOCITY/target envelope, not from a joint envelope
that already contains A0 times its bank frequency. This distinction is
needed to avoid a circular amplitude inequality. A0 polynomially large
would pay that putative cumulative cost.

One can then choose code frequency F polynomially larger than A0, so
the parallel bank has small critical norm A0/F and admits a weak final
clearing regime. Choose Q last, polynomially larger than F sqrt(Nw)
and all reciprocal errors, so the bank's diffusion and low velocity
evolution during Nw high-carrier windows remain small. All these scalar
inequalities admit fixed-depth polynomial choices. This scale arithmetic
does not prove the hypothesized loading law, all-orders low-return
estimate, register routing or accumulated perturbation estimate.
The actual joint state contains BOTH loaded magnetic packets and Euler
velocity sidebands; the prepared-window theorem was stated for initially
magnetic packets. Extending it to this joint preparation, including its
Reynolds and induction signals, is another required calculation.

The ideal identity thus gives a specific constructive opening whose
algebra does not require A0 proportional to Q. It does not close the
compiler, and the hypothetical errors above are not accepted services.

4. A real viscous phase obstruction to a naive implementation

Already in the exact scalar Stokes shear sector, with magnetic field
zero and velocity v(t)F_Q, one has for constant unknown viscosity nu
 v'+nu kappa^2 Q^2 v=g(t).
Therefore
 v(T)-v(0)+nu kappa^2 Q^2 integral_0^T v
 =integral_0^T g.
If endpoints vanish and the applied force has one coefficient-blind
integral, a common NONZERO phase integral v cannot be independent of
nu. More quantitatively, for two viscosity values and endpoint errors
at most e, phase errors at most d around the same a imply
 |nu1-nu2| kappa^2 Q^2 |a|
 <=2e+(nu1+nu2)kappa^2 Q^2 d
when both initial endpoints are zero. This is a restriction on that
single-shear closed-endpoint implementation, not all mechanical words.

In the coordinate u=qF_Q+v with force q'F_Q and integral q=a, the
momentum equation contains the affine term nu q Delta F_Q. Its endpoint
integral has size nu kappa^2 Q^2 a. Compressing the time does not remove
it. Dropping that affine correction while using E_a as the actual
velocity endpoint would manufacture a physical loader.

A phase depending internally on 1/nu is not forbidden by the full
question, but it must be handled by actual operations and their errors,
not by evaluating nu in a force. Closed multi-shear words, physical
normalizers or different loading mechanisms can evade this particular
single-shear requirement. The completed coupled internal operation
encoding (nu+rho)r, together with rho r, supplies an actual formal signal
for nu r from a prepared sector; its routing and t^-2 precision costs
remain physical requirements. It is not permission to read nu.

5. Why one passive-bank scale choice cannot be combined with weak
critical cleanup for free

For the different kinetic-reload scaling A0=Q sqrt(s), a weak cell gives
a useful fourth-order response of size s^2, so an order-one output uses
Nw of order s^-2 cells. If a bank mode of frequency F is only passively
diffusing between cells, and each clearing cell occupies at least a
positive multiple of Q^-2, preserving its relative amplitude within
beta forces F/Q <= C sqrt(beta/Nw). Hence
 A0/F >= c/[sqrt(s) sqrt(beta)].
For small s this is incompatible with requiring A0/F to be a fixed
small critical norm for the final cleanup. This is a proved conflict
between THESE chosen sufficient regimes. It is not instability of the
actual bank, a lower bound on every cleanup operation, or a negative
for the full compiler. Regeneration, a different readout, a new robust
large-bank theorem, or the shear-loading amplitude scheme in Sections
1--3 could avoid it. Merely increasing Q in both inequalities does not.
