## What familiar regularity fails to control

Every fixed-size marginal has a volume-independent eigenvalue floor: for bounded incident energy `J_S`, `rho_S>=exp(-2 beta J_S) I_S/d_S`, with `J_S<=4|S|` here. Even the isolated root marginal has a fixed positive tolerance. The global correlated state nevertheless loses a uniform physical preimage.

A root flip changes only boundedly many terms. Ordinary relative entropy remains bounded by `3 beta` in an isolated cell, or `4 beta` with a connector, while max-relative entropy grows extensively. Local mixedness and bounded average thermodynamic disturbance therefore do not certify global delivery feasibility.

A product ground state, uniform ground gap and stoquastic basis also do not establish finite-temperature clustering or mixing; neither is inferred here. The result concerns the declared unencoded full-state trace-distance endpoint rather than only local observables.

## The strongest baseline narrows the novelty

The source credits Pettinari's finite-range translation-invariant weak-Gibbsianity counterexample with an existing exponential exact local-output fragility corollary. It also credits Bouch's higher-dimensional imaginary-time growth mechanism and established precompensation/SDP methods. Bare exponential fragility is not claimed as new.

The retained additional conjunction is positive bounded local terms, unique uniformly gapped product vacuum, counted planar geometry and a normalized witness preserved under fixed-strength physical connections. Those ingredients yield the connected approximation converse, fixed-pattern quantifier and precision deductions. The separate translation-invariant note does not acquire the connected amplification by implication.

Optimal precompensation, ideal sources, full FT/qLDPC interfaces, unlimited upstream acquisition/cooling and global entangled or postselected preparation are all granted. Their internal capabilities are not disputed. None changes the compulsory last unobserved channel. Encoded output, post-port recovery, useful fault flags, target-preserving final thermalization or a local-observable target change the operation and need their own complete construction.

Known-state positive bounded-factory preparation and the autonomous-processing branch with classical output remain unchanged. No lower bound on ideal sampler complexity, generic preparation hardness, laboratory performance or total-resource advantage is established.

The [failure record](package/new_consequence_work/root/scope_and_failed_routes.md) retains a generator-uniformization route that did not pay finite gate/idling/scheduling costs. A proposed fermionic quantum-layer lower bound failed under measurement-generated records and teleportation; the surviving scoped bound charges total local causal time, including classical propagation. Neither failed route is promoted to the new endpoint theorem.
