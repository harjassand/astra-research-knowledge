## 1. The question and the completed theorem

The question was whether finite temperature and otherwise benign many-body structure can guarantee a nonzero, size-independent tolerance to imperfect physical output.

The endpoint must be specified precisely. On each selected output qubit, the last operation is an independent, unobserved bit-flip channel

\[
\Lambda_p(\sigma)=(1-p)\sigma+pX\sigma X,
\qquad 0<p<\frac12.
\]

Everything before that channel is unrestricted. The preparer may have exact knowledge of the Hamiltonian and noise, unlimited computation, perfect calibration, arbitrary cooling and reset, ideal quantum error correction, nonlocal links, ancillas, postselection and entanglement across the entire device. Every other output port may be perfect.

The target is the **actual unencoded Gibbs state at release**, measured in global trace distance

\[
D(\rho,\tau)=\frac12\|\rho-\tau\|_1.
\]

### Connected delivery theorem

For every fixed \(p>0\), I constructed a connected planar qubit Hamiltonian family at the fixed inverse temperature \(\beta=12\) such that:

- Every interaction is nearest-neighbor on a square-lattice layout.
- The interaction graph has degree at most three.
- Every local term is positive and has norm at most one.
- The ground state is the unique product vacuum.
- The spectral gap is at least one, uniformly in size.
- Every fixed-size reduced Gibbs state has a positive lower eigenvalue bound independent of total volume.

Nevertheless,

\[
\boxed{
\inf_{\sigma}
D\!\left(
(\Lambda_p^{\otimes M}\otimes\mathrm{id})(\sigma),
\rho_{\beta,M}
\right)
\longrightarrow 1.
}
\]

The infimum includes **every globally entangled upstream state**. Thus this is not a limitation of a particular sampler, circuit architecture, code, controller or preparation schedule.

There is also a stronger quantifier statement: **one fixed computable aperiodic interaction pattern, chosen independently of \(p\), has this failure for every fixed \(p\in(0,1/2)\)** along its growing finite-volume restrictions.

A separate quantitative family forces, for any fixed desired accuracy \(\epsilon<1\),

This last statement is a necessary physical precision law. It does **not** say that growing fault tolerance suffices: if the same fixed noisy physical channel remains last, arbitrarily good internal fault tolerance still cannot overcome it.

## 2. Why this asks something beyond preparation complexity

## 3. The exact criterion: a local port probes a global operator-order ratio

Let \(U=U^\dagger\), \(U^2=I\), and consider

\[
\Lambda_p=(1-p)\mathrm{Id}+p\,\mathrm{Ad}_U.
\]

For a full-rank target \(\rho\), the unique linear preimage is

\[
\sigma_p=
\frac{(1-p)\rho-pU\rho U}{1-2p}.
\]

Its trace is one. It is a physical state exactly when its numerator is positive. Conjugating by \(\rho^{-1/2}\) gives the sharp criterion

\[
p\le p_{\max}(\rho,U)
=
\frac{1}{1+\kappa(\rho,U)},
\]

where

\[
\kappa(\rho,U)
=
\left\|
\rho^{-1/2}U\rho^{1/2}
\right\|^2.
\]

For a Gibbs target,

\[
\rho=\frac{e^{-\beta H}}{Z},
\]

the normalization cancels:

\[
\boxed{
\kappa(\rho,U)
=
\left\|
e^{\beta H/2}Ue^{-\beta H/2}
\right\|^2.
}
\]

This connects noisy output feasibility to imaginary-time operator growth. Equivalently,

\[
\log\kappa
=
D_{\max}(U\rho U\|\rho).
\]

The relevant quantity measures the largest probability ratio in any quantum direction. Local marginal mixedness and average thermodynamic disturbance do not control it.

Complex-time growth in higher-dimensional quantum lattice systems is established machinery. Bouch constructed a two-dimensional example with finite-imaginary-time norm divergence, using lattice trees with many admissible growth histories. That mechanism is prior art. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}

The construction below retains such growth while imposing a product ground state, a uniform gap and an explicit physical geometry. Its normalized witness then turns the exact inverse obstruction into a global approximation theorem.

## 4. The cell: positive local terms, a trivial ground state and an exponentially shrinking radius

### Actual planar geometry

The basic cell is a recursively embedded tree.

Start with one root. At level \(h\), draw horizontal paths of length \(2^{h-1}\) to the left and right, then vertical paths of the same length to four corners. Attach four translated copies of the preceding tree. Every path is subdivided into unit lattice edges.

The cell has

\[
n_h=3(4^h-2^h)
\]

edges and

\[
N_h=n_h+1
\]

qubits. Its containing square has

\[
(2^{h+1}-1)^2=\frac{4N_h-1}{3}
\]

lattice sites. Maximum degree is three.

Thus the tree does not hide long-range interactions or an exponentially expensive embedding. Physical occupied volume and containing-square area are proportional.

A useful geometric feature is that the tree intersects its root’s vertical axis only at the root. That provides a corridor for the later connecting wire.

### Hamiltonian

Orient every edge away from the root and write

\[
n_v=\frac{I-Z_v}{2}.
\]

Define

\[
\boxed{
H_h=
\sum_v n_v+
\frac12\sum_{u\to v}n_u(I+X_v).
}
\]

Each term is positive, has norm one and annihilates the vacuum. Consequently,

\[
H_h\ge\sum_v n_v.
\]

The all-zero state is therefore the unique ground state and the spectral gap is at least one.

The interactions do not commute. A product of onsite \(Z\) transformations changes the off-diagonal signs and makes the Hamiltonian stoquastic. The obstruction therefore does not require a sign problem.

### Activation histories

Let \(|s\rangle\) have only the root occupied and let \(|F\rangle\) have every vertex occupied. All computational-basis matrix entries of \(H_h\) are nonnegative.

Every order that activates parents before their children contributes \(2^{-n}\) to

\[
\langle F|H_h^n|s\rangle.
\]

If \(s_v\) is the descendant-subtree size, the rooted-tree hook formula counts these orders as

\[
\frac{n!}{P_h},
\qquad
P_h=\prod_{v\ne\mathrm{root}}s_v.
\]

The explicit tree recurrence gives

\[
P_h\le16^n,
\]

and hence

\[
\langle F|H_h^n|s\rangle
\ge
\frac{n!}{2^n16^n}.
\]

### A normalized eigenvector supplies the quantitative bound

Root occupation commutes with \(H_h\). In the root-one sector, let \(\psi\) be the normalized positive Perron eigenvector, with eigenvalue \(E\).

The Hamiltonian preserves nonnegative coordinatewise increasing functions of the descendant bits. Power iteration from the constant vector therefore makes \(\psi\) increasing, so

\[
\psi(F)\ge2^{-n/2}.
\]

The all-occupied diagonal and the total norm give

\[
1+\frac32n\le E\le2n+1\le3n.
\]

Combining this with the activation count yields

\[
r:=|\langle s|\psi\rangle|^2
\ge A^{-2n},
\qquad
A=96e\sqrt2.
\]

Since the vacuum has energy zero and a root flip maps it to \(|s\rangle\),

\[
\kappa
\ge r e^{\beta E}
\ge
\exp\!\left[
\beta+
\left(\frac32\beta-2\log A\right)n
\right].
\]

At \(\beta=12\), put

\[
g=18-2\log(96e\sqrt2)
=6.1781564365\ldots.
\]

Then

\[
\frac{1}{1+e^{12(2n+1)}}
\le p_{\max}
\le
\frac{1}{1+e^{12+gn}}.
\]

Thus the exact permissible root error decays exponentially in physical cell volume:

\[
-\log p_{\max}=\Theta(N).
\]

The lower bound is an inverse-positivity statement, not a synthesis algorithm for the preimage.

## 5. The central completion: approximate delivery fails on a connected target

Exact inverse negativity can occur in a very small spectral direction. By itself, that need not prevent a useful approximation. The next operation closes that gap.

Let

\[
P=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|,
\qquad
Q=XPX,
\]

and define

\[
O=(1-p)P-pQ.
\]

The two projectors lie in opposite root-occupation sectors. The outcomes of \(O\) are \(-p,0,1-p\), an interval of length one. Direct calculation gives

\[
\Lambda_p^*(O)=(1-2p)P\ge0.
\]

Therefore **every obtainable output has nonnegative expectation of \(O\)**.

The Gibbs target has a negative expectation. The \(P\) contribution is \(e^{-\beta E}/Z\), while the vacuum component of \(X\psi\) contributes at least \(r/Z\) to \(Q\). Moreover,

\[
Z\le(1+e^{-\beta})^{n+1}.
\]

This partition-function inequality follows from eigenvalue min-max; it does not use the false assertion that matrix exponentiation is operator monotone.

We obtain

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(\rho O)\le-\delta,
\]

with

\[
\boxed{
\delta=
\frac{
pA^{-2n}-(1-p)e^{-\beta(1+3n/2)}
}{
(1+e^{-\beta})^{n+1}
}>0
}
\]

for a sufficiently large cell. At fixed \(\beta=12\), an eligible cell has \(n=O(\log(1/p))\), although the conservative constants are very poor.

### Physical connections preserve the witness

Place the cells in a row. Run occupation-qubit wires from their roots through the empty vertical corridors to a shared horizontal spine. Add onsite \(n_w\) terms and positive diagonal couplings \(n_un_v\).

These are actual nearest-neighbor interactions of fixed strength. The full graph is connected, degree remains at most three, and

\[
H_{\mathrm{total}}\ge\sum_{\mathrm{all}\ v}n_v
\]

preserves the product ground state and gap.

All wire occupations commute with the Hamiltonian. Conditional on the **complete wire configuration**, the cells are independent Gibbs states with Hamiltonians

\[
H_h+t_i n_{\mathrm{root}},
\qquad t_i\in\{0,1\}.
\]

The Perron vector remains unchanged because it lies entirely in root sector one. If

\[
u_1=\operatorname{Tr}(Pe^{-\beta H_h}),
\qquad
u_0=\operatorname{Tr}(Qe^{-\beta H_h}),
\]

the conditional witness mean is

\[
\frac{(1-p)e^{-\beta t_i}u_1-pu_0}{Z(t_i)}
\le-\delta.
\]

The numerator becomes more negative and the partition function decreases. Thus the same negative margin holds for **every** wire record. The correlations introduced by the connections are retained exactly.

### Arbitrary entangled preparation cannot defeat amplification

Measure the cell witnesses successively on an obtainable output. Conditional on earlier outcomes, an unmeasured cell remains in the image of its own final root channel: operations elsewhere commute with that channel and only condition its input.

Hence

\[
\mathbb E_{\mathrm{out}}[Y_i\mid\mathrm{past}]\ge0
\]

even when the initial state entangles every cell and every wire.

For the target, conditional on the wires, the outcomes are independent with means at most \(-\delta\). Conditional Hoeffding concentration on both sides gives

\[
\boxed{
\inf_\sigma
D\!\left(
(\Lambda_p^{\otimes M}\otimes\mathrm{id})(\sigma),
\rho_{\mathrm{connected}}
\right)
\ge
1-2e^{-M\delta^2/2}.
}
\]

This is the decisive strengthening. It handles approximate delivery, connected interactions and arbitrary upstream entanglement simultaneously.

## 7. Broader deductions

The construction separates properties often treated as evidence of robustness.

### Uniform local full rank does not certify a global preimage

For any fixed set \(S\), split a bounded local Hamiltonian as

\[
H=I_S\otimes H_{\mathrm{far}}+K,
\qquad
\|K\|\le J_S.
\]

Jensen’s inequality and eigenvalue min-max give

\[
\rho_S
\ge
\frac{e^{-2\beta J_S}}{d_S}I_S.
\]

Here \(J_S\le4|S|\), independently of total volume. Every fixed-size marginal therefore has a positive uniform eigenvalue floor, while the full-state root-noise radius tends to zero.

Even the root marginal has its own fixed tolerance. In an isolated cell,

\[
\frac1{1+e^{3\beta}}
\le
\Pr_\rho[n_{\mathrm{root}}=1]
\le
\frac1{1+e^\beta}.
\]

Its isolated bit-flip preimage remains positive on a size-independent interval. The failure belongs to the correlated many-body state.

### Bounded thermodynamic disturbance does not imply robust delivery

A root flip changes only the root onsite term and two outgoing terms:

\[
\|XHX-H\|\le3.
\]

Thus

\[
D(X\rho X\|\rho)
=
\beta\,\operatorname{Tr}\rho(XHX-H)
\le3\beta.
\]

Yet

\[
D_{\max}(X\rho X\|\rho)
\ge\beta+gn.
\]

The connector raises the first constant to at most \(4\beta\). Ordinary relative entropy and local energetic disturbance stay bounded while the worst-direction ratio grows extensively.

### A trivial ground state does not settle finite-temperature output feasibility

The vacuum can be prepared as a product state, the ground gap never closes, and a local basis change makes the Hamiltonian stoquastic. None of these facts supplies a uniform global output-noise radius.

No finite-temperature clustering or mixing theorem is inferred from the ground gap. Those would be additional properties requiring their own proof.
