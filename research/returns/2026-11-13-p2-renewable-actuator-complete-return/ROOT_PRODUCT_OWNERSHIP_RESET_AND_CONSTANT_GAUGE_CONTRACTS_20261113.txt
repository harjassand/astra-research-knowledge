1. Product ownership can replace a separate mobile completion signal, but ownership and chemical identity are different.

For ordinary polymerase extension, the newly incorporated covalent residue carries the next reactive chain end. A released catalytic signal is not necessary for progression. This is existing chemistry. Base-matched incorporation with an incorrect remote substituent may still progress; a correctly copied natural sequence does not by itself authenticate all chemical functions on the modified product.

In a declared well-mixed excess-substrate competition, let q be the mole fraction of a wrong chemical variant with the same base identity, and rho its relative incorporation specificity. Then f=rho*q/(1-q+rho*q) is the conditional wrong-incorporation fraction. Under the stated constant-pool, conditionally identical step model, full chemical correctness after n additions is (1-f)^n. At n=20, 90% requires f<=0.0052541741. For rho=100, feed q must be at most0.00005281647 in this model. At q=.001 and rho100, whole correctness is.148373. No actual purity, relative specificity, proofreading discrimination or depletion law is supplied by these numbers.

Finite wrong stock gives a separate counting limit, without constant-pool assumptions: the number of chains that contain at least one wrong unit cannot exceed the number of incorporated wrong units. It does not prove the wrong stock is incorporated or specify which program receives it. In an introduced16-program,1nmol-each,20-addition case,320nmol stoichiometric growth stock multiplied by eightfold excess and.001 incorrect fraction contains2.56nmol incorrect input. 2. A physically different candidate is covalent carrier-to-carrier handover.

Consider the schematic topology
old-carrier--leaving-group--acyl(prefix) + amine--new-unit--new-carrier
 -> old-carrier--leaving-group + prefix--amide--new-unit--new-carrier.
If actually implemented, the prefix changes its carrier because its new product bond is made; mere release of the old carrier is not the continuation observation. Hydrolysis can instead release a failed prefix without putting it on the next carrier. Correct local docking, the next reactive end, suppression of background reactions, positional register, usable chemical functionality, activation and carrier renewal remain actual construction problems. This topology is not a supplied reagent, a ribosome replacement, a new chemical reaction or a claim that an arbitrary non-natural backbone can be made this way. Acyl transfer and autocleaving tethers already exist.

INTRODUCED finite-window handover model:
At each of n states, correct transfer has rate a, wrong but continuing transfer rate w, and absorbing hydrolytic loss rate h. A nonreactive undocking/retry can be folded into effective waiting rates only if it really preserves the same state and its time/cost is paid. A wrong transfer continues; the model is deliberately not a perfect product detector. Let lambda=a+w+h and G_n(lambda W) be the Erlang completion CDF. Then
correct_complete=(a/lambda)^n*G_n(lambda W),
all_complete=((a+w)/lambda)^n*G_n(lambda W),
correctness_among_complete=(a/(a+w))^n.
Unfinished and hydrolyzed populations are explicit and conserve total probability. The controller cannot observe these plant-state labels.

3. Repeated clearing is not an independent new chance for a persistently inaccessible site.

If a fraction z of extended sites is persistently inaccessible and the other sites are cleaved with per-pass probability p, remaining stock after k passes is
z+(1-z)*(1-p)^k.
For introduced p=.95,32nmol and k3, z0 gives.004nmol; z.005 gives.16398nmol. Six passes still leave.16000050nmol in the latter case. The model does not infer any actual inaccessible fraction, independence of accessible sites or particular defect. It distinguishes a repeated stochastic chance from a shared structural blockage. Real access-changing/refolding/removal operations may change z but must be constructed; more nominal dose alone is not that operation.

This matters for primer reset because an uncleaved covalent product remains attached after fluid exchange. A functional ready-state observation must distinguish retained product, damaged primer and properly restored end at actual sensitivity. An intact scaffold mass or cleared liquid inventory is not automatically a ready population.
