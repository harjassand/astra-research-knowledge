# N1823 — current mathematical operations

ROOT distinguishes actual pumped vascular fracture repair, bounded rapid-adhesion materials and pressure compensation from renewed useful actuator function. Introduced models show that a feed throat can seal before filling a defect and that small local deposits can dominate later access. A hypothetical pressure-body screen does not inherit tested actuator strength from tensile specimens. Earlier local-heating and observation results remain unchanged; no compatible autonomous repair material, mold, complete force-work cycle, renewal life or whole-cost advantage is supplied.

Prepared epoxy fractures with pumping, support, manual cleanup, long cure or clamping are not autonomous soft actuators. Manually realigned tensile cuts and limited contact tests do not qualify a useful pressure body or repeated damaged-device life. External sealing, recovered shape or compensated force can coexist with missing material or depleted redundancy. All numerical laws below are introduced; no actual cure window, renewed conduit or restored useful-load life is established.

Construct actual delivery, containment, mixing, cure and load transfer while preserving or restoring access after each repair. Qualify pressure, force through useful stroke, work, damage, consumed stock and return to readiness. Proposed discriminating experiments are not physical execution or spending authority.
