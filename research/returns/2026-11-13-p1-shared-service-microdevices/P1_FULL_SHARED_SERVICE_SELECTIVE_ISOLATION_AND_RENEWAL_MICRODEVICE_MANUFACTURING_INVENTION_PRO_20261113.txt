Can a programmable manufacturing system construct, diagnose and selectively renew useful three-dimensional heterogeneous microdevices with economical receiving interfaces, moving the difficult isolation, retention and metrology operations into common service tooling where that improves the whole useful operation?

The physical capability is construction and renewal of functional matter from common component stock across different device geometries. Software may choose designs, plan actual motions and interpret finite observations. It must not supply an unconstructed contact, holder, material release, thermal boundary, extraction route, calibrated function detector or externally restored ready state. A concrete proposed construction separates component ownership, electrical connection and access.
