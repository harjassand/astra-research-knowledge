2. A dimension obstruction for homogeneous full/dual products

This is the strongest general algebraic conclusion obtained in this turn.

Let
$R_t=\mathbb Q[x_0,\ldots,x_d]_t,
\qquad
H_t=\dim R_t=\binom{t+d}{d},$
and let
$P_{a,b}:R_a\times R_b\longrightarrow R_{a+b}$
be homogeneous polynomial multiplication. Here $d=1$ means binary forms and $d=2$ means ternary forms.

Write $P_{a,b}^{23}$ for the dual obtained by exchanging the right-input and output legs while retaining the left leg.


A finite necessary inequality

Suppose a shared-left border packing computes:


- $F$ full targets $P_{a,b}$;
- $H$ dual targets $P_{a,b}^{23}$;

using $q$ full sources $P_{a,b'}$. Then
$F H_b+H H_{a+b}\le qH_{b'},
\tag{2}$
and
$F H_{a+b}+H H_b\le qH_{a+b'}.
\tag{3}$
The proof is by the two matrix flattenings that separate the right-input and output legs. Polynomial multiplication is concise on both: a nonzero right polynomial cannot annihilate every left polynomial, and products of the prescribed degrees span $R_{a+b}$. Sharing only the left leg leaves the other two legs disjoint, so their flattening ranks add. Linear maps and degenerations cannot increase those ranks.

Consequently, with $m=F+H$,
$\boxed{
\frac mq
\le
\frac{H_{b'}+H_{a+b'}}{H_b+H_{a+b}}.
}
\tag{4}$
This applies to arbitrary linear or border packings with those source and target types, not merely the canonical binary-form gadgets.

\le

\tag{5}$

The effort exponent $d$ is attainable by rational interpolation: the scalar rank of $P_{a,b}$ is exactly $H_{a+b}$. A rational evaluation matrix can be constructed using integer lattice points after dehomogenization, with a multivariate Newton basis, or by exact elimination. Limit: this theorem does not cover arbitrary heterogeneous tensor types, a different extraction architecture, or unrestricted matrix-multiplication algorithms.


3. The proposed ternary kernel does give an explicit degeneration

The kernel idea is mathematically valid and yields more than a dimension calculation. Its fourth piece is, however, a binary boundary product.

S_e=\mathbb Q[y,z]_e,\qquad
D_e=\binom{e+2}{2},$
and define
$\sigma_e:R_e^3\to R_{e+1},
\qquad
\sigma_e(v_1,v_2,v_3)=xv_1+yv_2+zv_3.$
Let $K_e=\ker\sigma_e$.

For $e\ge1$, introduce
$i_1(h)=(-yh,xh,0),\qquad
i_2(h)=(-zh,0,xh),\qquad
i_3(q)=(0,-zq,yq),$
where $h\in R_{e-1}$ and $q\in S_{e-1}$.

The filtration
$0
\subset
i_1(R_{e-1})\oplus i_2(R_{e-1})
\subset
K_e
\subset
R_e^3$

$R_{e-1}^{\oplus2},\qquad S_{e-1},\qquad R_{e+1}.
\tag{8}$
Multiplication by any $A\in R_a$ acts by $A$ on the free pieces, by $A(0,y,z)$ on the binary piece, and by $A$ on the final quotient. Thus the compatibility holds uniformly for every left input.


Explicit encoders and decoders

For $w\in R_{e+1}$, define coefficient-shift maps
$s_1(w)=\frac{w-w(0,y,z)}x,
\quad
s_2(w)=\frac{w(0,y,z)-w(0,0,z)}y,
\quad
s_3(w)=\frac{w(0,0,z)}z.$
These divisions are exact shifts of monomial indices.

Given
$h_1,h_2\in R_{e-1},\quad q\in S_{e-1},\quad w\in R_{e+1},$

E_1&=-yh_1-zh_2+\varepsilon^2s_1(w),\\
E_2&=xh_1-\varepsilon zq+\varepsilon^2s_2(w),\\
E_3&=xh_2+\varepsilon yq+\varepsilon^2s_3(w).

\frac{W_3(0,y,z)-W_3(0,0,z)}y,\\

U_2\equiv Ah_2,\qquad

5. The integer-bit transfer can be completed conditionally

The missing exponent cannot be replaced by a tensor oracle. But once the requisite finite identities are proved to exist, uniform construction and bit accounting can be supplied.


Certificate format and exact verification

$\sum_sW_{ij,s}U_{s,\alpha\beta}V_{s,\gamma\eta}

\tag{18}$

For a fixed rational $\delta=p/q>0$, seek

\tag{19}$
The inequality is decidable exactly by
$r^{2q}<v^{4q+p}.$

A modulus that keeps every intermediate bounded

$L=\lceil\log_v n\rceil,\qquad N=v^L,$

$K=2b+\lceil\log_2(2N)\rceil,

\tag{20}$

$\gcd(D,Q)=1,

$|(AB)_{ij}|<n2^{2b}<Q/2,$

6. Other bit-level possibilities checked

Large coefficient precision can change the complexity substantially. Harvey and van der Hoeven give a deterministic bit algorithm with near-quadratic dimension dependence when the coefficient precision is sufficiently large relative to dimension—for example, dimension $O(\log b)$. That is a useful algorithm at its actual parameter scope, not an answer for every $b$. texmacs.org <https://www.texmacs.org/joris/zmatmult/zmatmult.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com>

A direct integer-packing route also needs care. In a single uncancelled monomial encoding, exact matching forces
$a_{ik}=\alpha_i+\beta_k,\qquad
b_{kj}=\gamma_j-\beta_k,\qquad
c_{ij}=\alpha_i+\gamma_j.$
If $H=\{c_{ij}\}$ contains the $n^2$ distinct output positions, avoiding terms with unequal inner indices forces the $n$ translates $H+\beta_k$ to be disjoint. They all occur in the product support, so this encoding needs at least $n^3$ positions. Carry spacing then produces cubic-size packed integers.

That argument excludes this particular encoding/readout model. It does not exclude cancellation, multiple products, a different representation or a general bit algorithm.
