Common control of three-dimensional Navier-Stokes with polynomial physical resources

FULL QUESTION. Does there exist ONE fixed finite family G_1,...,G_r of mean-zero divergence-free real trigonometric polynomial fields on the UNIT three-torus, with integer wavevectors and ordinary rational sine/cosine vector coefficients, and a total deterministic compiler with the following property?

For EVERY binary rational M>=1, 0<nu,tau<=1 and 0<epsilon<1, the compiler outputs ordinary rational piecewise-polynomial coefficients c_j(t) on [0,tau] and a positive rational eta. The SAME prescribed force f=sum_j c_j G_j must make

satisfy ||U(tau)||2<=epsilon for EVERY real mean-zero divergence-free ||U0||H1<=M, EVERY mean-zero divergence-free additive disturbance ||h||_(L-infinity_t L2_x)<=eta, and EVERY corresponding Leray-Hopf realization. Use the one-sided energy trace at the fixed endpoint. The target is small TOTAL velocity. No observation, feedback, initial-dependent force, selected weak solution, extra advection, distributional impulse or parameter-dependent spatial family is supplied.

The new requirement is POLYNOMIAL resources in numerical input size. Put R=2+M+nu^-1+tau^-1+epsilon^-1 and let L be the complete binary input length. There must be fixed explicitly computable constants A>=1 and integer p>=1, chosen once with the spatial family, such that:
 * compiler bit operations, working-memory bits, total output bits, number of time intervals, polynomial degrees and the bit lengths of every rational numerator/denominator are <=A(L+R)^p;
 * eta^-1, sup_(0<=t<=tau)||f(t)||_(W1,infinity_x), and integral_0^tau ||f(t)||_(W1,infinity_x) dt are <=A R^p.
Fixed-family selection and its complete description, all constants, searches, rational rounding and PDE estimates must be paid within a specified finite precomputation and these bounds. A and p cannot depend on the variable inputs. Numerical rational heights may be exponential in their stated bit lengths; they are not free real numbers. No PDE, optimization or norm oracle is supplied.

A positive answer must construct and prove this full compiler. A negative answer must obstruct the stated existence claim over all admitted fixed finite families and compilers; showing that one word, one transfer estimate or one forcing ansatz has exponential cost is not a full negative.
