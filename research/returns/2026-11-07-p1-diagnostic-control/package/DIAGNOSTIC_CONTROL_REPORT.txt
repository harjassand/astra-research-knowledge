## 1. Result and exact meaning of completion

For every factor, sample a cold/hot setting with a probability determined implicitly by the two unknown stationary populations. No point estimate of lambda_i, e_i, p_c,i, or p_h,i is required. Then apply the warranted simultaneous U-dressed native reset for the sampled setting vector.

In the ideal independent-coin and ideal replacement-channel interface, the preparation channel is exactly

    R_sigma,   sigma=U [tensor_i tau(q_i)] U^dagger,
    tau(p)=diag(1-p,p).

The exact setting sampler stops almost surely and has an explicit expected count of Huber source-coin calls. That expected-query statement is not a hard runtime or a total bit-work theorem.

A separate, completely specified finite controller below always returns a setting vector after bounded probe calls and bounded bit work. Combined with finite native reset times, it gives a deterministic-deadline preparation channel P with

    (1/2)||P-R_sigma||_diamond <= epsilon_cert,

where every classical, source-reset, instrument, final-reset and residual term is explicit. An error target is certified only when this total bound meets it. Unknown fixed residual error is not assumed to average away.

Private command records must be included in the interpretation. Their joint state with the output is generally correlated. The advertised target channel is the channel on the delivered quantum system after those private records are traced out; a clean-record implementation additionally pays its actual memory recovery/erasure and handover costs. Keeping conditional holding commands private is a valid delivery convention, but it does not make the output independent of those commands.

## 2. Frozen native premises used, without reproof

The source law for factor i is

    L_i(a)=lambda_i D[X_i]+a(D[|0><1|_i]+e_i D[X_i]),
    lambda_i>0, e_i>=0,
    p_i(a)=(lambda_i+e_i a)/(2 lambda_i+(1+2e_i)a).

Both selected amplitudes are legal and positive, a_c>a_h. Every physical generator is dressed by the same supplied bounded-depth U. The joint implementation is warranted; it is not inferred from scalar measurements and it does not grant a free new implementation of U.

The accepted complete estimate is

    (1/2)||exp(t L_i(a))-R_tau(p_i(a))||_diamond
       <= min(1,2 exp(-a t/2)).                              (1)

The exact unknown modeled lambda_i and e_i remain in the source law. They are not residual error. The coarse bounds, calibration, native probes, positive rate bounds, amplitude caps and physical cost schedules are supplied premises. Arbitrary drift, unknown readout anchors, different probe/output stationary laws, and arbitrary additional couplings are not part of this construction.

The derivative of p_i(a) is negative, so p_c,i<p_h,i; the supplied rational coarse bounds obey

    p_c,i <= c_i^+ < q_i < h_i^- <= p_h,i.

The measurement response is r=alpha_i+chi_i p with known rational constants and chi_i>0. A genuine binary measurement on a two-level factor has 0<=alpha_i<alpha_i+chi_i<=1. A physical stationary population lies in (0,1/2). The strict bracket therefore puts the measured target strictly between the two observed source probabilities.

## 3. An exact setting coin without estimating the unknown populations

Suppress i. Define observed-coordinate quantities

    r_c=alpha+chi p_c,  r_h=alpha+chi p_h,
    t=alpha+chi q,
    d_c=chi(q-c^+)>0,  d_h=chi(h^--q)>0,
    d_0=d_c+d_h>0.

Let A=t-r_c, B=r_h-t and D=A+B=r_h-r_c. Then A>=d_c, B>=d_h and D>=d_0. The required probability of choosing the HOT output setting is

    w=A/D=(q-p_c)/(p_h-p_c).                               (2)

If S is that label, then

    (1-w) tau(p_c)+w tau(p_h)=tau(q).                       (3)

Equation (2) is a mathematical identity. The controller never substitutes estimated values for its unknown numerator or denominator.

### Exact Bernoulli race

Use the supplied linear factory BF(C,eta; source), whose output has mean C p when C p<=1-eta. Repeat independent trials:

1. Draw a known t-coin.
2. If it is HOT, call BF(1/t,d_c/t; cold observed source). Accept HOT exactly when that factory outputs zero.
3. If it is COLD, call BF(1/(1-t),d_h/(1-t); complemented hot observed source). Accept COLD exactly when that factory outputs zero.
4. On rejection, repeat with fresh source outcomes and auxiliary randomness.

The two factory calls satisfy their actual promises:

    r_c/t <=1-d_c/t,
    (1-r_h)/(1-t) <=1-d_h/(1-t).

In one race trial the probabilities of accepting HOT and COLD are respectively

    t(1-r_c/t)=A,
    (1-t)[1-(1-r_h)/(1-t)]=B.

Hence the returned label has probability A/(A+B)=w. The number of completed trials is geometric with success probability D, so it has mean 1/D<=1/d_0. Every individual Huber call stops almost surely. Therefore the complete exact race stops almost surely.

Huber's expected source-coin bound gives

    E Q_Huber <= 9.5 [ t/d_c+(1-t)/d_h ] / d_0.             (4)

This counts the unknown-probability calls made inside the factories, not uncharged auxiliary randomness. It is uniform over the coarse bracket. Renewal of independent trials justifies dividing the per-trial expected cost by D, even though a trial's cost and acceptance event can be correlated.

### Actual exact randomness and digital scope

A known rational coin can be generated by comparing fresh unbiased binary digits with the binary expansion of its rational probability. It stops almost surely and uses at most two fair bits in expectation. Rational arithmetic is finite on every terminating path. In Huber section 2, the geometric variates can be generated by repeated known rational coins, and the other displayed rational-power coins can also be generated exactly. Thus an ordinary fair-bit implementation is available; it is not a real-number oracle.

Fair bits can themselves be extracted from native samples as in section 6. The exact extractor stops almost surely. This supplies an actual randomness route for the ideal exact race. Equation (4), however, does not by itself bound total expected bit work or the expected number of native samples used to produce all of Huber's auxiliary randomness. No such bound is imported from Huber's expected source-query theorem. The finite controller below avoids needing it and has a hard total resource budget.

There is no use of a convenient unprovided Huber tail estimate. The geometric tail of the OUTER race concerns completed trials, not their durations.

## 4. Complete many-factor preparation and arbitrary references

Sample independent setting labels S_i with probabilities w_i, using fresh independent source streams and auxiliary bits. The source parameters are fixed, though unknown. Probe replacement before measurement makes the ideal coin records independent of any previous state or untouched reference. Finite deviations are charged later.

For a fixed label vector s, let

    sigma_s=U [tensor_i tau(p_i(a_s_i))] U^dagger.

The warranted joint implementation and (1) give, with a_*=min_i a_h,i>0,

    (1/2)||E_s(T)-R_sigma_s||_diamond
      <= min(1,sum_i 2 exp(-a_s_i T/2))
      <= min(1,2n exp(-a_* T/2)).                           (5)

No learned scalar equation is used to establish coherence erasure or joint locality: those come from the accepted native law and common U dressing.

For every initial rho_AR, including arbitrarily entangled A and R, the ideal limit is

    sum_s P(s) sigma_s tensor rho_R
       = U [tensor_i ((1-w_i)tau(p_c,i)+w_i tau(p_h,i))]
           U^dagger tensor rho_R
       = sigma tensor rho_R.                              (6)

Convexity of the diamond norm gives the same uniform bound (5) after averaging over s. Thus the preparation is complete, not only correct on diagonal test inputs.

Compute the classical labels first and then start the simultaneous dressed reset. No already-prepared quantum output needs to wait while a later factor's setting is still being sampled. The output may be arbitrary before the final reset; (5) covers it.

For the finite implementation, early classical completion can be padded to a predetermined command deadline, followed by the fixed reset interval T and scheduled delivery. This keeps the delivery time from becoming an unreported conditioning variable. Padding, idle supply and command-memory holding use their actual scheduled resources; the output is not postselected on a favorable stopping time.

## 5. A finite controller with an explicit deterministic budget

The next construction is a finite approximate coin factory using clipping, binomial means and the supplied strict margins. It is not an estimator of the native rates.

### 5.1 A finite race trial

For a HOT proposal, draw N_c fresh cold-source outcomes and let Cbar be their mean. Accept with the known rational probability

    a(Cbar)=(t-Cbar)_+/t.

For a COLD proposal, draw N_h fresh hot-source outcomes and let Hbar be their mean. Accept with

    b(Hbar)=(Hbar-t)_+/(1-t).

The proposal is a known t-coin. The acceptance probabilities always lie in [0,1]. The HOT proposal deliberately samples the COLD source, and conversely.

With exact known coins, the unconditional trial acceptance masses are

    A'=E[(t-Cbar)_+]=A+e_c,
    B'=E[(Hbar-t)_+]=B+e_h,

where

    e_c=E[(Cbar-t)_+]>=0,
    e_h=E[(t-Hbar)_+]>=0.

Hoeffding concentration gives

    e_c <= (1-t) exp(-2N_c d_c^2),
    e_h <= t exp(-2N_h d_h^2).                             (7)

If both exponentials are at most beta, then e_c+e_h<=beta. The infinite repetition of this finite trial returns HOT with probability w'=A'/(A'+B'). The resulting physical population q' satisfies the EXACT identity

    q'-q=(B e_c-A e_h)/[chi(D+e_c+e_h)],

and therefore

    |q'-q| <= (e_c+e_h)/chi <= beta/chi.                    (8)

This is an expectation identity of the randomized preparation. A sample mean need not be close to its unknown mean at whole-state precision. Linearity is correct away from the clipping boundary, and reaching that boundary is exponentially unlikely under the supplied fixed margin.

### 5.2 Bound the number of trials

Allow at most K trials. If none accepts, return COLD unconditionally. There is no success-only output or conditioning on a hidden success event.

Since A'+B'>=D>=d_0,

    P(all K trials reject)<=(1-d_0)^K<=exp(-K d_0).         (9)

Coupling to the infinite finite-trial race bounds the additional population and total-variation errors by this probability.

### 5.3 Bound known-coin bit work

Represent each known probability theta by

    theta_b=floor(2^b theta)/2^b,

and compare a b-bit uniform word with the integer threshold. Each such coin differs from the desired coin by less than 2^(-b). There are at most 2K known coins per factor, so this contributes at most

    2K 2^(-b).                                             (10)

No unbounded exact-comparison loop is used in this finite controller. Counts, clipping, thresholds and probabilities are computed by integer/rational arithmetic.

## 6. Auxiliary randomness from the actual native source

No independent ideal random-bit oracle is silently supplied. Select an available cold probe with a warranted positive lambda lower bound lambda_- and known calibration alpha,chi. Its stationary physical population obeys

    p_c >= lambda_-/(2lambda_-+a_c)>0,    p_c<1/2.

Hence its observed probability r lies between the known strict bounds

    r_- = alpha+chi lambda_-/(2lambda_-+a_c)>0,
    r_+ = alpha+chi/2<1.

Set

    kappa=r_-(1-r_+)>0.

Two independent outcomes differ with probability 2r(1-r)>=2kappa. Ignore 00 and 11; map 01 to one fair bit value and 10 to the other. The exact extractor gives independent fair bits using fresh disjoint source samples, with expected native outcomes per bit

    1/[r(1-r)]<=1/kappa.

For a deterministic deadline, try at most L pairs per requested bit. If they are all equal, return zero and charge that event. For at most B_tot requested bits,

    extraction discrepancy <= B_tot(1-2kappa)^L
                            <= B_tot exp(-2kappa L).        (11)

At most 2 L B_tot extra native outcomes are used. Source outcomes assigned to entropy extraction are never reused as factory input outcomes. Thus ideal independence is preserved. Actual finite-reset and instrument discrepancies are charged for these extra outcomes too.

If an independently warranted random-bit service is available, its actual bit, timing and error costs may replace this extractor. That would be a different explicit resource contract; it is unnecessary for existence here.

## 7. One complete finite error bound

For factor i choose N_c,i,N_h,i,K_i,b_i and beta_i as above, and let

    B_tot=sum_i 2K_i b_i,
    Q_signal=sum_i K_i max(N_c,i,N_h,i),
    Q_max=Q_signal+2L B_tot.                                (12)

For each of the at most Q_max probe outcomes, let epsilon_j^probe bound the complete reset/read/recovery discrepancy from a fresh ideal calibrated source outcome, uniformly over the previous history and any untouched reference. Such a bound follows by composing the accepted complete reset estimate with the warranted measurement/recovery contract. A finite-output procedure is defined for every outcome string, so its error can be bounded by sequential channel replacement; actual imperfect samples need not be treated as exactly iid.

Only the classical outcome of the probe measurement is retained. A full tomography contract for that measurement is unnecessary: after an ideal complete reset, its input is tau(p) tensor the unchanged reference, so the stated affine response makes the outcome an independent Bernoulli(r). Contractivity charges the finite reset error, and a warranted stationary response error xi adds at most xi in total variation. Any additional apparatus memory or recovery discrepancy still follows its actual supplied contract; it is not inferred from a scalar fit.

After tracing private work records, the delivered channel satisfies

    (1/2)||P-R_sigma||_diamond
    <= sum_i [beta_i/chi_i + exp(-K_i d_0,i) + 2K_i 2^(-b_i)]
       + B_tot exp(-2kappa L)
       + sum_(j<=Q_max) epsilon_j^probe
       + 2n exp(-a_* T/2)
       + epsilon_implementation + epsilon_handover.         (13)

The native probe reset part of epsilon_j^probe can be made small with the known selected positive amplitude a_j:

    epsilon_j^reset <=2 exp(-a_j t_j/2).

Other instrument or controller residuals remain explicit. A fixed residual rho per probe yields the conservative term Q_max rho. This bound does not claim that repeated sampling removes a fixed calibration error.

### A rationally computable budget for any requested epsilon

Let 0<epsilon<1. Allocate epsilon/8 to each of six terms: clipping, race truncation, known-coin rounding, entropy extraction, probe reset, and final output reset. Reserve epsilon/4 for the remaining warranted implementation, instrument, handover and holding errors.

For each factor set

    beta_i=chi_i epsilon/(8n),
    z_i=ceil(log2(1/beta_i)),
    N_c,i=max(1,ceil[z_i/(2d_c,i^2)]),
    N_h,i=max(1,ceil[z_i/(2d_h,i^2)]),
    z_tail=ceil(log2(8n/epsilon)),
    K_i=max(1,ceil[z_tail/d_0,i]),
    b_i=max(1,ceil[log2(16nK_i/epsilon)]).

Compute B_tot, then choose

    z_entropy=ceil(log2(8B_tot/epsilon)),
    L=max(1,ceil[z_entropy/(2kappa)]).

After computing Q_max, use

    z_probe=ceil(log2(16Q_max/epsilon)),
    t_j=2 z_probe/a_j,
    z_out=ceil(log2(16n/epsilon)),
    T=2 z_out/a_*.

These times are rational. All log2 ceilings of rational numbers are computed by integer comparisons with powers of two. Since e^(-x)<=2^(-x) for x>=0, the selected integer exponents rigorously imply the desired bounds; no floating logarithm is an acceptance oracle.

This gives nominal error at most 3epsilon/4. If the sum of the separately warranted remaining residuals is at most epsilon/4, the full operation meets epsilon. If it is not, the actual reported guarantee is (13), not an unsupported arbitrary-accuracy claim.

## 8. Physical, digital, and holding costs

### Logical and source costs

The hard native-outcome cap is (12), including every sample used for auxiliary randomness. Under fixed positive coarse gaps, calibrated contrast, entropy-extraction margin and fixed input parameter sizes, let Z=1+log(n/epsilon). Then

    N_c,i,N_h,i,K_i=O(Z),
    b_i,L=O(Z),
    Q_signal=O(n Z^2),
    B_tot=O(n Z^2),
    Q_max=O(n Z^3).

The hidden constants depend explicitly on d_c,i^(-2), d_h,i^(-2), d_0,i^(-1), chi_i and kappa^(-1). Their inverse dependence is retained in the exact budget. This is not a polynomial-bit-complexity claim uniform over arbitrarily tiny binary-encoded physical margins.

For rational input bit size H, counters and coin thresholds have bit sizes bounded by H+O(log Q_max+b_max). Using schoolbook rational/integer arithmetic, a conservative digital bound is polynomial in H, Q_max and b_max; for example O((Q_max+B_tot+sum_i K_i) W^3), with W the preceding explicit bit bound, covers the streamed controller and exact threshold calculations. Planning uses only rational arithmetic and integer log2 ceilings. The logical storage can be streamed: the n labels, input descriptions, a few counters, and one random word. Recording the entire history instead uses O(Q_max+B_tot) bits and pays the corresponding storage and cleanup costs.

No exponential offline catalogue, unknown-population table or instance-dependent advice is used.

### Actual native durations and activity

Every raw coin needs the actual amplitude switch, reset time t_j, measurement, recovery and instrument throughput cost. A safe serial schedule sums these costs over at most Q_max outcomes, then pays the classical controller time and the final simultaneous reset duration T. Parallel speedups require the supplied probe capacity and throughput; simultaneous readout does not make scalar outcomes free.

With t_j=2z_probe/a_j, the preparation amplitude dose for that probe is a_j t_j=2z_probe. The total probe reset dose is at most 2 Q_max z_probe, plus any measurement/recovery actuation charged by its own contract. The output reset dose is at most n a_c T for common amplitudes. Actual power, jump activity and work use the supplied native cost functions, including actuator-generated noise e_i a and continuing lambda_i. They are not converted to joules or seconds without the warranted physical conversion.

At fixed parameters, the conservative serial probe-reset time/activity scales as O(n Z^4); the final parallel reset takes O(Z) model time and O(n Z) amplitude dose. This explicit finite construction is not claimed resource-optimal. The ideal exact race has the different expected source-call law (4).

The controller must pay its program/counter initialization, reliable arithmetic, timing, signal selection and memory cleanup costs. Unknown failures of those operations are part of epsilon_implementation. No free trusted physical implementation is inferred from an abstract source-sampling bound.

### Holding and delivery

Retain each selected setting on the corresponding output during holding. Conditional on s, sigma_s is exactly stationary under its own warranted native generator. Therefore ideal holding does not add target bias, and the finite initialization error contracts. Its continuing amplitude dose is h sum_i a_s_i<=h n a_c, with the supplied sustaining power and capacity costs.

The retained setting record remains correlated with the output. Replacing the conditional setting by an averaged amplitude is not the same stationary operation. If labels are erased and the output is transferred or left under a different law, that handover and storage channel needs its actual error bound and cost. It is not supplied by the sampling identity alone.

For a residual generator with (1/2)||Delta(t)||_diamond<=eta_hold(t), the conservative Duhamel contribution during a specified holding interval is at most integral eta_hold(t) dt. The stronger accepted native stability bounds can be used under their own conditions, but are not needed for this completion. Permanent fixed residuals do not disappear by declaring the intended state stationary.

## 9. Internal records and repeatable independent outputs

### Records are correlated

With complete transcript C and branch s(c), the ideal joint state is

    Omega_CA=sum_c P(c)|c><c| tensor sigma_s(c).

For every initially untouched reference R, the ideal full preparation is Omega_CA tensor rho_R. Thus the initial reference is decoupled. In general Omega_CA is not Omega_C tensor sigma.

For one factor, write X for its excited-state measurement and S for the hot label. Then

    Cov(S,X)=w(1-w)(p_h-p_c)>0.

So retaining or releasing a hot/cold label cannot be described as retaining an independent innocuous log. The target claim is made for A after the private work records are discarded. If a final reset of accessible controller records is part of delivery, that reset and its error/resources are paid. Discarding records does not claim absence of correlations with the wider thermodynamic environment.

### Reusing a label does not produce independent copies

If the same S is used for two fresh conditional resets, their energy bits obey

    Cov(X_1,X_2)=w(1-w)(p_h-p_c)^2>0.

The joint state is a mixture of tensor powers, not the tensor power of the target mixture. Therefore each new output needs a fresh independent setting vector and fresh random/source samples.

With that renewal, the ideal joint output of R preparations is sigma^(tensor R), decoupled from arbitrary initial reference systems. Fixed unknown apparatus parameters do not spoil this: the target is the same sigma for every allowed fixed parameter value.

For finite operations with complete errors epsilon_1,...,epsilon_R, sequential channel composition gives joint preparation error at most sum_j epsilon_j. Stored earlier outputs additionally pay their actual holding/transfer errors, capacity and continuing supply. To target a specified R-output error epsilon, allocate suitable per-output budgets, such as epsilon/R after reserving storage costs. A single reusable register is a sequential preparation resource; simultaneous retained copies require actual output storage/transfer capacity and cannot be obtained for free by notation.
