- On a **HOT proposal**, take \(N_c\) fresh cold-source outcomes with empirical mean \(\bar C\). Accept with the known rational probability

- On a **COLD proposal**, take \(N_h\) fresh hot-source outcomes with empirical mean \(\bar H\). Accept with
  \[
  \frac{(\bar H-t)_+}{1-t}.
  \]

The unconditional accepted masses are
\[
A'=\mathbb E(t-\bar C)_+=A+e_c,
\qquad
B'=\mathbb E(\bar H-t)_+=B+e_h,
\]
where
\[
e_c=\mathbb E(\bar C-t)_+\ge0,\qquad
e_h=\mathbb E(t-\bar H)_+\ge0.
\]

Concentration at the **known clipping margins** yields
\[
e_c\le(1-t)e^{-2N_cd_c^2},\qquad
e_h\le t e^{-2N_hd_h^2}.
\]
If both exponentials are at most \(\beta\), then \(e_c+e_h\le\beta\).

The infinitely repeated finite trial would select HOT with probability
\[
w'=\frac{A'}{A'+B'}.

\[

\frac{B e_c-A e_h}
{\chi(D+e_c+e_h)}.

\boxed{|q'-q|\le\frac{\beta}{\chi}.}
