Global regularity for the finite-mass two-species Landau-Coulomb system

FULL QUESTION. Resolve global classical well-posedness of the spatially HOMOGENEOUS two-species Landau-Coulomb equation on velocity space R^3, for arbitrary positive finite particle masses and arbitrary smooth nonequilibrium data, as follows.

Fix ANY m_1,m_2>0 and positive symmetric coefficients c_ij=c_ji>0, i,j in {1,2}. Define P_z=I-z tensor z/|z|^2 and
 Q_ij(f_i,f_j)(v)
 = (c_ij/m_i) div_v integral_(R3) [P_(v-w)/|v-w|]
     *[(f_j(w)/m_i) grad_v f_i(v)
       -(f_i(v)/m_j) grad_w f_j(w)] dw.
The equation is partial_t f_i=sum_(j=1,2) Q_ij(f_i,f_j).

For EVERY pair of strictly positive Schwartz initial densities f_i^0 on R^3, with nonzero finite masses n_i=integral f_i^0 and finite mass-weighted Fisher information
 I_m(f^0)=sum_i (1/m_i) integral |grad f_i^0|^2/f_i^0,
does this Cauchy problem have a unique global nonnegative classical solution, smooth for positive times and attaining f_i^0 at t=0 in the usual finite-mass/energy strong-solution class?

The result must include finite-time a priori bounds sufficient for classical continuation for every finite T, with their dependence on the finite masses, coefficients and actual initial norms specified. In particular, a Fisher-information or other continuation bound must be proved rather than assumed. Retain each species mass, total momentum sum_i m_i integral v f_i, total kinetic energy (1/2)sum_i m_i integral |v|^2 f_i and the entropy inequality. No near-Maxwellian smallness, radial/even symmetry, infinite-mass species, frozen background, special mass ratio, artificial velocity cutoff or modified collision operator is part of the question.

A full affirmative must prove the stated global existence and uniqueness for the whole class. A negative must exhibit a genuine obstruction to that contract for admissible FINITE masses and data; growth of one proposed Lyapunov functional, failure of a particular estimate or an infinite-mass limiting example is not such an obstruction. This is an analytic mathematical question, not a requested numerical simulation. Any computation or approximation used must have its actual hypotheses, errors and costs accounted for; no solution, optimization or real-number oracle is supplied.

Useful starting mathematics and contrary results, not a prescribed method:

1. Single-species Fisher monotonicity does not automatically extend to Coulomb mixtures. For the mass-weighted I_m, the known general cross-species spherical criterion in dimension3 reaches |gamma_ij|<=sqrt(8), whereas Coulomb has gamma=-3. Diagonal same-species terms retain particle-exchange symmetry; cross terms generally do not. The exact lifted derivative separates a negative squared derivative of the angular collision field from a positive radial-coefficient term. The absence of a sign from that criterion is not a global blowup theorem.

2. There is an actual compensation mechanism in a distinct limiting problem. For one species interacting with a frozen infinite-mass ion background, the equation contains the spherical diffusion c_ei div(|v|^-1 P_v grad f). Write
 J(f)=integral |v|^-1 |P_v grad log f|^2 f.
An existing theorem constructs a nonnegative Lyapunov functional I(f)+a J(f)+R H(f|M) and obtains global regularity. In the Coulomb case its theorem includes the additional data/coupling condition
 2+J(f^0)/I(f^0)^(3/2)+H(f^0|M)<=c_ei/K
for a specified sufficiently large constant K. This is not an arbitrary-finite-mass or arbitrary-coupling result, and that extra condition is not imposed on the full question here.

3. The finite-mass pair geometry is explicit. For a pair (v,w), put z=v-w, Z=(m_i v+m_j w)/(m_i+m_j), mu_ij=m_i m_j/(m_i+m_j), and F_ij=f_i(v)f_j(w). At fixed Z the relative derivative is
 D_z=mu_ij(grad_v/m_i-grad_w/m_j).
The pair angular quantity
 J_ij=integral_(R6) F_ij |z|^-1 |P_z D_z log F_ij|^2 dv dw
satisfies the exact entropy-dissipation identity
 -partial_t sum_i integral f_i log f_i
   =(1/2)sum_(i,j) c_ij mu_ij^-2 J_ij.
This identifies a finite-recoil counterpart of the limiting angular quantity. It does not supply a sign for its derivative: both marginals evolve, and differentiating introduces mixed collision terms. A compensation built from these quantities, a different representation or another mechanism is open.

4. Do not assume entropy dissipation is itself monotone. Even for the single-species Landau-Coulomb equation there are smooth near-Maxwellian examples where its time derivative is positive. Thus adding entropy dissipation to Fisher information needs a genuine estimate; thermodynamic entropy decrease alone does not provide it.

Optional primary starting points, in any order:
 * Zhu, https://arxiv.org/html/2512.17905v1, Theorem1.1 and Proposition2.2: mass-weighted mixture Fisher information, exact lifted derivative and the different diagonal/cross-species thresholds.
 * Junne, Winter and Yoldas, https://arxiv.org/html/2512.17082v1, Theorems1.1-1.3 and Sections2-3: finite-mass local theory, the contrary Fisher example, and the compensated infinite-ion theorem with its Coulomb condition retained.
 * Junne, Winter and Yoldas, https://arxiv.org/html/2609.03847v1, Theorem1.1: entropy dissipation can increase even near equilibrium. This concerns a Lyapunov shortcut, not failure of classical solutions.
