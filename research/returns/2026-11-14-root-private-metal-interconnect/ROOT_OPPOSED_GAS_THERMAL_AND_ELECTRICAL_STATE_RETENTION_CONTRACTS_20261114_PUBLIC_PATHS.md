1. A DIFFERENT PHYSICAL PRESSURE ORGANISATION
Two separately sealed gas pockets oppose the displacement of one private liquid-metal inventory. Displacing volume q compresses one pocket and expands the other. The useful wide conducting bridge remains distinct from a narrow SIDE feed. A common external head would supply the displacement and withdraw after a pinned state is reached. It must positively own and reverse the fluid displacement, leave separate electrical nets, and provide actual mechanical/thermal/electrical interfaces.

Let Va=Va0-(1+epsilon)q and Vb=Vb0+(1-epsilon)q. Fixed mole inventories give pi=ni R Ti/Vi. The generalised restoring pressure is
 (1+epsilon)(pa-Pambient) - (1-epsilon)(pb-Pambient) + k_total q/A^2.
The ambient-reference terms REQUIRE actual ambient pressure forces on the complete outer moving surfaces with the declared virtual-work geometry. Subtracting atmosphere numerically does not construct those surfaces, seals or load paths. Total membrane stiffness includes ALL relevant flexible walls; adding a second diaphragm is not free.

For equal effective areas, matched initial pressure and q=0, the common-temperature pressure cancels EXACTLY for any common T in this ideal-gas model. A larger single sealed pocket does not have that cancellation. At nonzero q the gas spring survives: for equal volumes V, its pressure is P0*(T/T0)*(V/(V-q)-V/(V+q)). Temperature differences, fill mismatch, asymmetric permeation and geometric asymmetry do not cancel.
