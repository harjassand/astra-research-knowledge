# Finite reversible geometry certificates do not turn capacity into arbitrary-start passage time

The retained transfer route acquires a restricted reversible model and constructs exact finite spatial brackets. It also preserves the decisive correction: **capacity divided into basin mass is not generally the requested mean first-passage time from an arbitrary preparation**. Read the [transfer construction](package/next_science/transfer/construction_checkpoint.txt), [finite geometry completion](package/next_science/invention/CAPACITY_GEOMETRY_COMPLETION.txt), [Poisson/access repair](package/next_science/root_construction/POISSON_AND_ACCESS_NOTES.txt) and [counterexample record](package/next_science/observation/poisson_conditioning_counterexample.json).

For a fully acquired strip/cylinder with flat Dirichlet end faces, periodic transverse directions and positive log-Lipschitz scalar conductivity, Q1 primal and RT0 divergence-free flux approximations give finite upper energy and lower reciprocal-energy bounds for continuous capacity. With acquired positive rational conductivity enclosure `exp(-eta)ahat<=a<=exp(eta)ahat`, the certificate is `[exp(-eta)/Rhat,exp(eta)Uhat]`. Domain topology, both periodic paths and coefficient metrology must be included; an extra route cannot be discarded.

At fixed dimension d and geometric aspect ratios, a relative bracket has cell count of order `((K+ell^-1)/epsilon)^d`, plus coefficient/geometry bits and exact finite algebra. It is exponential in dimension. Rational weights representing a finite potential span H need bit length polynomial in H and inverse-accuracy logs rather than waiting `exp(H)` for a rare crossing, but physical observation, holding, diffusion/force estimation, sensor error and conditioning remain costs. No global high-dimensional solver, reaction-coordinate discovery or arbitrary geometry oracle is supplied. [Exact finite-element code](package/next_science/invention/capacity_geometry_exact.py), [recorded enclosures](package/next_science/invention/capacity_geometry_exact_results.json).

Even reflecting Brownian motion separates capacity-based quantities from a specified preparation's mean passage time. The mixed Poisson formulation targets the actual lifetime/initial-law functional and requires its own acquired coefficients, geometry and conditioning. A good capacity bracket alone is not that operation. The transfer notes also repair a circular-increment cut-locus mistake and account for finite observation/readout and diffusion estimation; they do not convert exact coefficient knowledge into a free physical measurement.

This is completed conditional analysis and finite geometry, not a general efficient rare-event prediction capability. Mathematical inputs, state preparation, noise model and exact target remain substantive.
