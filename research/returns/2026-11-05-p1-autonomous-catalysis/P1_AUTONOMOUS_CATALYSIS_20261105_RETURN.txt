Scientific judgment

The cross-challenge changed the result materially. A short-pulse argument had to be restricted after a reversible counterexample. A general entropy certificate had to be reinterpreted after an exact matched relay. The relay’s impressive gain had to be charged for lifetime, stalls, and current noise. The first-principles routes lost novelty under close prior art.

What survives is useful: a complete way to calculate signed catalytic consequences, a finite conditional certificate, and explicit autonomous mechanisms that show which observations are insufficient for causal attribution.

What does not survive is a claim that the mission has been achieved. The actual PtCo mechanism remains unresolved, and the constructed relay is ordinary thermodynamically consistent kinetics rather than a demonstrated new foundational principle. The full record preserves both the deductions and the operations still separating them from the consequential endpoint.
