Preserved authors' causal account
Exothermic O2 adsorption excites anchored flexible calixarene; phonon
coupling stores .126eV/molecule, passes excitation to an adjacent Pt OH*
site, and lowers the OH*->H2O-associated quoted barrier .375->.183eV.
The .126eV/.132=~.9545eV adsorption-energy calculation defines the
reported13.2% utilization. It is not a measured overall fuel-cell
thermodynamic efficiency. The reported catalyst performance remains a
real observation; the alternative constructions do not erase it.

Final empirical status
The available evidence establishes improved catalyst performance and
reports a specific energy-transfer interpretation. This construction
does not resolve how much of the measured gain is caused by autonomous
chemical pumping. The strongest ordinary alternatives include static
electronic/binding changes, changed active-site normalization, oxygen
transport, and thermal gating in an enlarged reaction-state model. A
uniform rate gain3.3 corresponds to kBT log(3.3)=.03066eV at298K; that
does not mean a single-step shift always suffices for a multistep cycle.
The titration ratio .04175/.079=.52848 means an intrinsic TOF gain3.3
would correspond to current gain1.74399 on the same catalyst-mass basis;
it does not erase the intrinsic gain. These normalizations must be kept
distinct. The accessible SI reports a35% change in the pressure-independent
oxygen transport resistance; kinetic-current correction is intended to
remove the measured RDE diffusion limitation, so transport cannot simply
be subtracted from every performance metric.

FigS16's gain is U-shaped, about3.48 at260mV,3.14 at300mV,and3.49 at340mV.
Root separately digitized this figure with pixel uncertainty. Do not
apply the separately fitted Tafel slopes globally over this range or
classify an already-observed shape as a new prediction. FigS4 is computed
modified-catalyst microkinetics, not a measured bare-catalyst waiting
fraction; its water-desorption rate-control contribution is negative
over much of the range and its exact perturbation convention is not
specified in accessible SI. It cannot be inserted uncritically into
our simple irreversible cycle.

The exact irreversible pulse bounds and the reversible counterexample
must be presented together. A short pulse can be ineffective in one
complete cycle and effective in another whose reactive phase is rare
and correctly targeted. The difference is earned by complete signed
turnover response, not by counting accelerated elementary jumps as
independent positive offspring. The event-conditioned chemical/gate
generator and actual thermodynamic capture/reverse paths remain missing
for an empirical attribution of this material.

All numerical scenarios are labeled. None is fitted as the real material's
generator. No result here proves a new foundational mechanism or a
civilisation-changing physical capability. The strongest earned science
is a complete conditional kinetic account and concrete countermodels
that identify the causal operation still required for that endpoint.
