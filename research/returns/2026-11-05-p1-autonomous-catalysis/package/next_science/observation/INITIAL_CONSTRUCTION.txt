EXACT CARBON ISOTOPE ACCOUNTING
After known minor terms are removed, a well-mixed box has light/heavy
burdens X,Y, R=Y/X, source fluxesE_j and ratiosR_j, OHlossk, alpha=k13/k12:
X'=sumE_j-kX, Y'=sumR_jE_j-alpha*kY. Therefore exactly
J=Y'/R-alphaX'=(1-alpha)X'+X(logR)'=sumE_j(R_j/R-alpha).
Arbitrary uniform OH history cancels. PureOH conservesY/X^alpha.
For two signaturesRm<Rf, k->k+h together with
Em->Em+hX(Rf-alphaR)/(Rf-Rm), Ef->Ef+hX(alphaR-Rm)/(Rf-Rm)
preserves the complete record. IfRm<=alphaR<=Rf,positiveh adds positive
sources; negativeh is allowed until a baseline source reaches zero.
This does not claim every spatial observation is matched.

In a heterogeneous atmosphere L12=integral kX,ROH=integral kY/L12,
Rbulk=Ytot/Xtot, and the true global identity is
J=sumE_j(R_j/Rbulk-alpha)-alphaL12(ROH/Rbulk-1)+other-sink terms.
Spatial OH/isotope covariance survives. Surface global mean does not
acquireROH. EffectiveKIEuncertainty is separate.

After known pyro/minorloss terms, b=Em*a_m+Ef*a_f-L*s, where
a_m=(1,cm,dm),a_f=(1,cf,df),s=(1,cOH,dOH) andb contains three burden
derivatives. For EXACT linearity use affine atomic-isotope-fraction
coordinates rather than uncorrected delta ratios. Ordinarydelta converts

With knownsource-line n=(-a,-beta,1) andDelta, only the time-weighted
scalar n dot b is needed after knownminor terms. But endmember changes,

Linearisingq=r'eta, any acquired operatorH closesq iff r lies in its

mathematical information; arbitrary chemical kernels are not thereby
physically implementable. Historical measurements cannot automatically
be acquired by measuring today's atmosphere; an archived-sample replay
would need actual sampleavailability, calibration and representativeness.
