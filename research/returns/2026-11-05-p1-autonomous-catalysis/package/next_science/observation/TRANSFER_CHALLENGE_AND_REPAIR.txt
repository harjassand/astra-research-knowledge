PEER CHALLENGE AND REPAIR: THE FIRST-PASSAGE TARGET
Observation constructor, 2026-11-04. Mathematical peer check; no novelty claim.

1. CAPACITY IS NOT THE RECIPROCAL CONDITIONED-START MFPT

Reflected Brownian motion on [0,1], generator L=D d^2/dx^2, target B={1},
start set A=[0,a], has stationary density uniform. Its equilibrium
potential is 1 on A and linear to 0 at B. Hence cap(A,B)=D/(1-a) and
mu(A)/cap=a(1-a)/D. But -Lu=1, u'(0)=0,u(1)=0 gives u(x)=(1-x^2)/(2D),
so E_{mu(.|A)}tau_B=(1-a^2/3)/(2D). At a=1/2 these are 1/(4D) and
11/(24D). Thus capacity/equilibrium-reactive-flux certificates cannot
silently become conditioned-start MFPT certificates. A metastability
approximation requires an earned error bound.

2. DIRECT MIXED POISSON PRIMAL/DUAL CERTIFICATE

Let Omega be a connected bounded domain, B an absorbing boundary or
interior target, remaining boundary reflecting. Work on Omega\B. Assume
boundary regularity and positive bounded density sufficient for a
coercive killed inverse and integration by parts. The finite reversible
graph version has identical algebra with sums.

Use unnormalised pi=exp(-V/D), c=D*pi, Lw=pi^{-1}div(c grad w), K=-L_B,
<r,s>_pi=integral r*s*pi, and a(r,s)=integral c grad r dot grad s.
The energy space has zero trace on B. For nonnegative sources f,g let
u=K^{-1}f, v=K^{-1}g, Q=<f,K^{-1}g>_pi=a(u,v).
For the MFPT from pi conditioned on A, take f=1_A,g=1 and divide by
mu(A)=integral_A pi. Normalisation of pi cancels.

Choose conforming primal fields p,q and finite-energy flux fields j_f,j_g
such that -div j_f=f*pi, -div j_g=g*pi and j dot n=0 on the reflecting
boundary. Flux on B is free. These j have the sign of c grad u, opposite
to the physical outward probability current. Their integrated outward
boundary flux is negative for positive sources; the sink current is -j.

Define E_f=integral |j_f-c grad p|^2/c, E_g analogously, and
S=<f,q>_pi+<g,p>_pi-a(p,q). Then exactly

                  |Q-S| <= sqrt(E_f E_g).

Proof: integration by parts gives integral j_f dot grad w=<f,w>_pi.
Write j_f=c grad u+k_f. Conservation implies integral k_f dot grad w=0,
so E_f=||u-p||_a^2+integral |k_f|^2/c >= ||u-p||_a^2. Similarly for g.
Direct expansion gives Q-S=a(u-p,v-q); Cauchy-Schwarz completes the proof.

This avoids the structural cancellation in polarising Q(f+g,f+g) and
Q(f-g,f-g). At exact primal solutions each of the three terms in S equals
Q. Arbitrary approximations can still be poor. A relative certificate
requires S-sqrt(E_f E_g)>0; numerical quadrature, roundoff, discretisation
and source-normalisation errors must be charged. For uncertain mu(A),
divide the positive Q lower bound by its upper mass bound and vice versa.

3. FEASIBLE POSITIVE-SOURCE CURRENTS

On a connected finite graph with positive conductances, choose a spanning
tree rooted at absorbing B. Route each vertex's accumulated subtree
source f_i*pi_i to its parent, leaves first. This takes linear work in
tree edges and yields exact conservation. Reverse sign for j convention.
Initial energy can be enormous across low-conductance edges. Feasibility
is not a small-relative-error complexity guarantee. Cycle corrections
and primal solves can tighten the bound. Continuum discretisation needs
independent validation.

4. THE CROSS-FUNCTIONAL CONDITION NUMBER CAN BE LARGE

A generic inverse-form perturbation bound naturally contains
chi=sqrt(Q(f,f)Q(g,g))/Q(f,g). It cannot be replaced by one merely because
sources are nonnegative. Take two transient states with invariant masses
1, symmetric coupling eps^2 and killing rates 1,eps. Then
K=[[1+eps^2,-eps^2],[-eps^2,eps+eps^2]]. With f=(1,0),g=(1,1), this is an
actual conditioned-start MFPT. As eps->0, Qfg->1,Qff->1,Qgg~1/eps and
chi~eps^(-1/2). At eps=1e-4 the executed values are
Qfg=1.0000999800010002, Qff=0.999999990001, Qgg=10000.000299970001,
chi=99.99000399920014. See poisson_conditioning_counterexample.json.

This proves the conditioning factor is not identically one. It does NOT
prove every physically structured scalar-potential perturbation attains
the general symmetric-form worst case. Stronger structure may allow a
better bound, but capacity Rayleigh comparison alone is not that proof.

5. ACQUISITION AND EXECUTION REMAIN SEPARATE

The certificate presupposes known generator, sources and boundaries.
Uniform potential error gives coefficient intervals, but robustifying
the mixed target over them is a separate operation. Bounded feedback
needs drift/controllability premises before it can promise reaching and
holding all acquisition patches. A full d-dimensional cover can cost
exponentially many patches. Exact variational duality removes none of
these acquisition or discretisation costs.
