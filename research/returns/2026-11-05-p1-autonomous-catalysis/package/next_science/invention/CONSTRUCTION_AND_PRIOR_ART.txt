Precise assumptions and deductions
---------------------------------
Let Y be a stationary finite-alphabet process with entropy rate h in nats.
Let S be an m-state predictive sufficient memory with stationary law and
unifilar update S_next=f(S,Y). Each local operation accesses only its current
memory and a fresh standard-state output cell. Earlier output is inaccessible.
Logical energies are degenerate at step boundaries; temperature is Theta.
Work is positive when supplied. The work statements are quasistatic infima,
not finite-power device guarantees.

For the local map (S,blank)->(S_next,Y),
 H(S_next,Y)-H(S)=h-H(S|S_next,Y),
so its ideal local work is
 W=-k_B Theta h+k_B Theta H(S|S_next,Y).
The extra term is an access-dependent modularity cost. Access to the past
output can enable reversible cleanup, so this is not a universal cost of
predictive algorithms independent of their physical interface.

Take a finite unifilar predictor of the reverse output process, with states
X, stationary law pi, and reverse transition R^y(x|x'). Define a forward
sampler G^y(x'|x)=pi(x')R^y(x|x')/pi(x). Stationarity normalizes each joint
distribution over (x',y). Telescoping pi factors in path probabilities shows
that the forward sampler generates the original process. It requires an
initial stationary random draw, not an external future oracle. Reverse
unifilarity makes X=f_reverse(X_next,Y), while reverse predictive sufficiency
gives H(Y|X_next)=h. Thus H(X_next,Y)-H(X)=h and ideal work is -k_B Theta h.
The finite reverse-machine assumption is substantive: finite forward memory
need not imply finite exact reverse memory.

Binary example: a stationary symmetric Markov chain switches with probability
p. Past-memory S=previous output updated to the emitted new output has ideal
local work zero. Retaining the next output X, emitting it first, then sampling
X_next with switch probability p has the same output law and work -k_B Theta
h_2(p). At p=.05, h_2=.1985152433 nats=.2863969571 bits. Garner's published
perturbed-coin example is literally this example; it is not a new result.

For a block B of L outputs, unifilarity similarly gives
 W_block=-k_B Theta Lh+k_B Theta H(S_initial|S_final,B).
The excess is <=k_B Theta ln m, or <=k_B Theta ln(m)/L per output. One can
retain S_initial, generate B using the original machine, compute S_final
reversibly, clean work space by recomputation, and erase S_initial conditional
on (S_final,B). Its posterior can be computed by enumerating m initial states
and following deterministic transitions through B: O(mL) likelihood arithmetic.
This is not a bit-complexity or finite-control energy guarantee. Retained
block, likelihood precision, pure ancillas, clock and conditional control all
remain resources. No exponential-arity physical stochastic map is supplied.

Resource interpretation and unearned extensions
----------------------------------------------
Negative work randomizes initially pure output cells and consumes their free
energy. Preparing/resetting a fresh a-ary cell from uniform equilibrium costs
at least k_B Theta ln a in the idealization. There is no one-bath net-energy
device in a closed cycle. Stationary memory initialization, matching an
existing correlated measured past, and end-of-run cleanup are distinct tasks.
The construction does not learn an unknown model, acquire conditional
probabilities, provide zero-cost clocks, or anticipate future interventions.
Finite-time bounded-control realization requires its own speed, reliability,
barrier, precision and controller cost analysis. Appending such a refinement
solely because this representation is warm would not establish the mission.
