QUESTION AND EXACT CONSTRUCTION
For a probability-measure-valued martingale P_t, define M_t=P_t f,
V_t=P_t f^2-(P_t f)^2 and Q_t=Var(P_{t+1}f|F_t), for bounded f.
Conditional unbiasedness on every measurable set gives
E[V_{t+1}|F_t]=V_t-Q_t. Thus expected within-model diversity is transferred
exactly into between-history disagreement. For deterministic P_0,
E[V_t]+Var(M_t)=Var_{P_0}(f).

If Q_t >= kappa_t V_t, with deterministic kappa_t in [0,1] and divergent
sum kappa_t, then E[V_t] tends to zero. On a compact metric state space,
the probability-measure martingale converges almost surely; applying the
identity to a countable separating class makes the limit a random Dirac
mass with location law P_0. This is not a theorem about arbitrary biased
neural estimators, arbitrary unbounded observables or noncompact state
spaces without tightness/uniform-integrability assumptions.

For empirical resampling of N_t independent outputs, Q_t=V_t/N_t and the
expected diversity is multiplied by (1-1/N_t). Fixed N and N_t=O(t)
collapse in this model. Summable 1/N_t preserves positive expected
diversity but does not recover an unknown true distribution. Exact copy
has Q_t=0 and is outside the collapse premise.

For independent synthetic and fresh-real empirical measures,
P_{t+1}=(1-alpha) Emp_N(P_t)+alpha Emp_m(P_*), with E[P_t]=P_*, let
a_t=E[(P_t f-P_*f)^2]/Var_{P_*}(f). Direct total-variance calculation gives
a_{t+1}=(1-alpha)^2(1-1/N)a_t+(1-alpha)^2/N+alpha^2/m.
The constant-parameter stationary error is the additive term divided by
1-(1-alpha)^2(1-1/N). A constant fresh fraction prevents the absorbing
collapse of the closed finite empirical population; it does not make the
error vanish. In finite alphabets positive immigration generally yields
an irreducible finite Markov chain, so individual rare categories can be
absent repeatedly even when aggregate diversity remains positive.

ACQUISITION/RETENTION ENDPOINT AND LIMIT
For a fixed finite alphabet K, retain the actual-real-data count vector
and any time-uniform confidence set derived from it. This uses O(K log n)
count bits after n observations; arbitrary synthetic proposal/model
updates do not erase the sufficient evidence. Sample complexity for a
specified error and confidence remains ordinary multinomial estimation.
For arbitrary future query classes, a statistic that merges distinct
real distributions can fail on a query separating them; acquisition or
retention of the discarded distinction is necessary. This is ordinary
statistical sufficiency/Blackwell comparison, not a new representation
that earns a civilisation-changing endpoint. Model-based approximate
sample amplification can help under specified families; one must not
assert that synthetic computation has no statistical value (see
Axelrod, Garg, Sharan and Valiant, Sample Amplification, ICML 2020,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.12053 ).

DECISION
The proposed identities are exact under their stated assumptions, but
the closest public research already supplies both collapse mechanism
and weighted fresh-data stabilization. No further novel fundamental
operation was earned in this route. Preserve it as an independently
originated but rejected construction. No Astra scientific theorem was
imported; the repository was consulted after the pre-literature
checkpoint, but nearest primary literature settled the novelty issue.
