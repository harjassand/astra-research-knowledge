SECOND FIRST-PRINCIPLES ATTEMPT, 2026-11-04. Recorded before nearest literature
on recursive model training/model collapse is consulted in this route.

Question
========
Can an indefinitely recursive scientific/AI model ecosystem preserve and
improve its contact with real observations while using its own generated
data, and what fresh-measurement/retention resources are indispensable? The
target is preservation and accumulation of reality-linked knowledge through
unlimited model generations, including rare outcomes, rather than improved
one-generation test accuracy.

Mechanism and representation
============================
Separate the distribution inside one trained model from the distribution
over model histories. An unbiased synthetic refit can preserve the mean model
across histories while converting within-model diversity into between-history
disagreement. Track this transfer for every bounded observable f using a
random probability measure P_t, not only one nominal model prediction.

Exact deduction before literature
=================================
Assume E[P_{t+1}(A)|F_t]=P_t(A) for every measurable A. Then
M_t(f)=integral f dP_t is a bounded martingale. Define V_t(f)=Var_{P_t}(f)
and Q_t(f)=Var(M_{t+1}(f)|F_t). Total variance gives

 E[V_{t+1}(f)|F_t]=V_t(f)-Q_t(f).

Therefore E V_t+Var M_t is conserved for deterministic P_0. This transfers
diversity to disagreement; it need not change the ensemble mean. If
Q_t>=kappa_t V_t for deterministic kappa_t, then
E V_t<=V_0 product_{j<t}(1-kappa_j). When sum kappa diverges, within-model
variance of all functions in a countable separating class vanishes. On a
compact metric state space, the measure-valued martingale converges to a
random point mass; its location has law P_0, because all means are preserved.
This is a complete collapse theorem for this model class, not all neural
learners.

For exact empirical refitting from N_t independent synthetic samples,
Q_t=V_t/N_t and E V_t=V_0 product(1-1/N_t). Fixed N or N_t=O(t) destroys
all within-model diversity. Superlinear growth with sum1/N_t finite retains
positive expected diversity but leaves a random model, not convergence to
truth. Exact copying of the measure has Q_t=0 and avoids this mechanism.

Fresh-data deduction
====================
Let P_* be the fixed real observation law and use independent conditional
draws in
P_{t+1}=(1-alpha_t) empirical_N_t(P_t)+alpha_t empirical_m_t(P_*).
If E P_t=P_* initially, then for bounded nonconstant f, set
sigma_f^2=Var_{P_*}f and a_t=E[(P_t f-P_*f)^2]/sigma_f^2. Exactly,

a_{t+1}=(1-alpha_t)^2(1-1/N_t)a_t
          +(1-alpha_t)^2/N_t+alpha_t^2/m_t.

This same scalar recurrence applies to every such f. Constant alpha,N,m give
a_infty=[(1-alpha)^2/N+alpha^2/m]
        /[1-(1-alpha)^2(1-1/N)].

An improvement operation must control both fresh-data bias contraction and
variation introduced by regenerated samples. Arbitrarily many nominal
synthetics are not independent real information. Retained real observations
can also be preserved directly: their empirical law has a paid acquisition
error, but an exact copy does not generate further drift.

Next challenge and unearned endpoint
===================================
This may already be population-genetic drift, martingale model collapse, or
recursive-learning theory. No novelty is asserted until primary literature
is checked. The full endpoint requires resource laws beyond unbiased refits,
biased learners, controlled updates, rare-event fidelity, and an implemented
retention/innovation protocol for a stated observable class. A new label or
the scalar recurrence alone is not that capability.
