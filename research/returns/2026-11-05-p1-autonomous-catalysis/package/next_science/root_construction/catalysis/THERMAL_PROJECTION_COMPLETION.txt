CONSTRUCTIVE FINITE-GATE COEFFICIENT (invention constructor)
For a supplied irreducible bath generatorB and nu-centered corrected
observablew, solveB f=w. For every distributionp,
 E_p w=sum_(x<y)(p_x b_xy-p_y b_yx)(f_y-f_x).
With logarithmic meanL(a,b)=(a-b)/(log a-log b), weighted Cauchy-Schwarz gives
 |E_p w|^2<=D_B(p)sum_(x<y)L(p_x b_xy,p_y b_yx)(f_y-f_x)^2.
SinceL<=(a+b)/2, a finite computable uniform choice is
 C=(1/2)max_x sum_y b_xy(f_y-f_x)^2.
A sharper coefficient maximizes the concave logarithmic-mean sum over the
probability simplex. Bath rates must describe the actual thermal mechanism;
any driven auxiliary must have its resource affinity and entropy counted.
The general-gate theorem now has a finite linear-algebra construction, not
an assumed functional-inequality oracle. These are established inequalities
composed with the complete-cycle Poisson corrector, not new thermodynamics.
