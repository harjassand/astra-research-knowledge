1. EXACT TWO-STATE COMPETITION CALCULATION
During a reaction-ready chemical state, let a gate switch0->1 at ratea and
1->0 at rateb. Reaction ends the wait with ratesk0,k1. Start in gate0.
Backward equations give
 T0=(a+b+k1)/(k0(b+k1)+a k1),
 k_eff(a)=[k0(b+k1)+a k1]/[a+b+k1].
This includes switching during the wait, not the entire catalytic cycle.
If an independent memoryless pump addslambda to the SAME0->1 edge, other
rates fixed, andk1>=k0, then
 1<=k_eff(a+lambda)/k_eff(a)<=1+lambda/a.
The nonnegative affine numerator and increasing denominator prove the bound.
For bath gate states a/b=exp[-DeltaF/(kBT)], b=1/tau, gainG requires
 lambda*tau*exp[DeltaF/(kBT)]>=G-1,
 DeltaF>=kBT log[(G-1)/(lambda*tau)].
Necessary, not sufficient; conditional on this independent gate interface.

4. LIMITS THAT MATTER
(a) Net current subtracts forward/reverse counts; near stall their ratio can
be arbitrarily sensitive. Positive gross-count bounds do not automatically
bound net current. Compute ORR as a reversible chemical network.
(b) One physical mode may perturb several chemical-state-specific edges.
Support size, not the name of a mode, governs multichannel sensitivity.
(c) Simultaneous donor adsorption/recipient excitation changes the joint
state. It is not the same edge as recipient bath excitation. The single-
channel theorem cannot be applied without a valid coarse-graining.
(d) State-conditioned pumping can select rare ready configurations. Its
average frequency does not bound the conditional hazard. Spatial/phase
correlations and persistent structural changes need explicit parameters.
(e) Barriers require common potential, solvent free energies, entropy and
prefactors.10meV uncertainty changes a rate by1.476 at298K. This is a
sensitivity, not an experimentally supplied barrier error bar.
(f) Repeated exothermic adsorption followed by desorption is not repeated
independent fuel consumption. The reverse coupled transition and energy
withdrawal must be included or an equilibrium bath can be spuriously pumped.
(g) Mean stored energy is not nonequilibrium free energy. For classicalp and
thermalq on the same Hamiltonian,F[p]-F[q]=kBT D_KL(p||q), a standard identity.
Entropy, coherence where applicable, and preparation cannot be discarded.

5. ADVANCED EVENTS VERSUS NET ADDITIONAL TURNOVER
Baseline generatorL0, signed reaction incrementsd_xy, instantaneous reward
r0(x)=sum_y q0_xy d_xy, stationaryj0=pi0 r0. SolveL0 h=j0-r0.
For modifiedL=L0+deltaL with stationarypi, stationaritypi Lh=0 gives
 j-j0=sum_x pi(x)sum_y deltaq_xy[d_xy+h(y)-h(x)].
This is the exact standard Poisson-equation performance-difference identity.
The bracket counts immediate reaction plus the changed future production.
It includes depletion and refractory delay omitted by positive offspring
counting. For added nonnegative channels at total stationary rateLambda,
j-j0=Lambda times their average full-cycle reward. General reversible
perturbations require the signed-channel expression. This is useful known
machinery, not an invented physical law.
