THERMAL PROJECTION AND FULL-CYCLE TURNOVER

SETTING
Finite irreducible Markov jump process with state(i,g): chemical configuration
i and binary gate g=0,1. A distinguished set of bath-only edges changes g at
fixedi, with rates a_i=gamma_i q_i>0 and b_i=gamma_i>0. Thus the conditional
thermal gate distribution has p_i*=q_i/(1+q_i). q_i can depend on i.
All remaining edges are arbitrary chemical/gate transitions, including joint
adsorption/excitation, correlated donor/recipient changes, reverse reactions,
and gate consumption. Resolve parallel channels in entropy accounting.
Let k_ij^(g) be the total rate to chemical statej starting at(i,g), summed over
destination gate and channels. Let K_g be the resulting rowwise chemical
generator, and r_g(i) its signed target-product reward rate (include counted
self-transitions in r, though their contribution to Kh iszero). Bath-only
edges carry no target-product count. All quantities are finite.

COMPARATOR
Define the thermal projection row by row:
 Kbar_ij=(1-p_i*)k_ij^(0)+p_i* k_ij^(1),
 rbar_i=(1-p_i*)r_0(i)+p_i* r_1(i).
This is the same chemical-channel architecture and rates with the gate
instantaneously conditionally thermally equilibrated. It is NOT necessarily
the capture-off physical catalyst, and it is not the bare unmodified catalyst.
Joint reverse-channel changes that alter Kbar are retained as changes to the
comparator. They must not be smuggled into a claimed nonequilibrium benefit.
Let mubar Kbar=0, Jth=mubar rbar, and solve
 Kbar h=Jth-rbar.
Irreducibility follows if the full process is irreducible and both bath gate
directions are positive. An additive normalization ofh is arbitrary.

EXACT IDENTITY
Let pi_ig be the actual stationary distribution, mu_i=sum_g pi_ig,
p_i=pi_i1/mu_i and delta_i=p_i-p_i*. Define
 w_i=r_1(i)-r_0(i)+(K_1-K_0)h(i).
Stationarity of h(i), which is unchanged by bath-only edges, gives exactly

  J-Jth=sum_i mu_i delta_i w_i.                         (1)

Proof: average r_g+K_g h-Jth over pi. The K_g h term has zero stationary
mean. Its conditional thermal average at everyi vanishes by the Poisson
equation. Only the conditional population deviations remain. The w_i are
complete-cycle response contrasts, not instantaneous hot/cold rate differences.

ENTROPY AND BOUND
The entropy production on the bath-only gate links, in kB per unit time, is

 sigma_gate=sum_i gamma_i[pi_i1-q_i pi_i0]
                         log[pi_i1/(q_i pi_i0)]
   =sum_i gamma_i(1+q_i)mu_i J_Bern(p_i,p_i*),          (2)

where J_Bern is the sum of the two Bernoulli relative entropies. Every resolved
edge pair's stationary entropy production is nonnegative, so sigma_gate<=
sigma_total. Pinsker on each KL gives J_Bern(p,p*)>=4(p-p*)^2. Cauchy-Schwarz
applied to(1) therefore proves

  |J-Jth|^2 <= sigma_total V_mu,
  V_mu=sum_i mu_i w_i^2/[4gamma_i(1+q_i)]
       <=Vmax=max_i w_i^2/[4gamma_i(1+q_i)].           (3)

Equation(3) allows arbitrary gross adsorption frequency, readiness correlations
and reversible donor loops, provided the finite-state model, bath links and
thermodynamic bookkeeping are correct. It does not infer those physical inputs.
It measures departure from the thermal projection, not all catalyst improvement.

For one chemical affinity A>0 whose conjugate current is exactly the counted
total product currentJ, local detailed balance and stationarity imply
sigma_total=A J. If Jth>0 and kappa=A Vmax/Jth, then

  (G-1)^2<=kappa G,
  G=J/Jth<=1+kappa/2+sqrt(kappa+kappa^2/4).             (4)

With multiple reservoirs use the full sigma=sum_l A_l J_l. If donor fuel
current differs from target current, do not set it equal toJ. Heat, optical,
mechanical or feedback work must be included. At equilibriumsigma=0 forces
conditional thermal gates and J=Jth, as required.

SHARPER BINARY CONSTANT
Pinsker is optional. Define
 c(p*)=sup_{0<p<1}(p-p*)/[logit(p)-logit(p*)],
 with the continuous valuep*(1-p*) atp=p*. It is positive and<=1/4,
because the derivative oflogit is>=4. The supremum is a bounded scalar
optimization; endpoints give0. Then replace1/4 inV_mu by c(p_i*).
This follows directly from the exact Bernoulli Jeffreys expression in(2).
Still not an identified physical free energy or a measured lifetime.

GENERAL FINITE GATE
For conditional bath generatorB_i with stationarynu_i, define its link entropy
functionalD_i(p). Let w_i(g)=r_g(i)+(K_g h)(i)-Jth, whose nu_i average iszero.
If an independently valid constantC_i satisfies
 |sum_g[p_g-nu_ig]w_i(g)|^2<=C_i D_i(p) for everyp,
the same proof gives |J-Jth|^2<=sigma_total sum_i mu_i C_i.
This formulation requires the actual gate generator or an acquired functional
inequality. It does not turn an unresolved vibrational bath into a binary mode
by definition. Establishing finiteC_i, computing it and paying missing bath
states are part of using the bound.

WHY THIS HELPS AND WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
The exact identity separates chemical architecture (thermal projected cycle)
from sustained conditional nonequilibrium in the gate. The coefficientw_i
contains the entire later chemical response, including depletion and trapping.
The bound then ties the allowed response to actual dissipated chemical power.
A large stored-energy flow can coexist withJ~Jth. Conversely a large response
is possible if a state has a large full-cycle response contrast or a slow gate;
that is a material/kinetic requirement, not a contradiction of thermodynamics.

The theorem does not establish a new microscopic mechanism or a functioning
fuel cell. Exact governing prior art and the strongest response/coarse-graining
results must be compared before a novelty statement. The independent
constructor has been asked to challenge this proof and scope.
