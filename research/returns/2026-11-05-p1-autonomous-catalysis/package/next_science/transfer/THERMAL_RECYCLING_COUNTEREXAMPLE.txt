EXACT AUTONOMOUS ENERGY RELAY WITH THERMAL CONDITIONAL OCCUPATIONS
Transfer constructor,2026-11-05. A scope theorem and conditional mechanism,
not a reconstruction of the calixarene/PtCo catalyst.

1. COMPLETE FINITE MODEL
Four states ordered00,01,10,11, where first index is chemical empty/adsorbed
and second is a binary energetic mode. State free energies are0,E,-E,0.
At temperatureT define q=exp(-E/kBT), gamma>0, chemical affinityA>0 and
z=exp(-A). Thermal edges at each fixed chemical state are g0->g1 atgamma*q
andg1->g0 atgamma. Captured adsorption is00->11 at ratea with reversea.
Product is11->00 at ratec with reversec*z; this channel counts+1/-1 net
chemical product and receives chemical workA*kBT per forward event.
The two00<->11 channels are distinct and must not be merged thermodynamically.
Every forward/reverse pair satisfies log(k+/k-)=beta(work-DeltaF).
The collapsed product channel represents a full driven two-stage chemical
cycle; itsA may be a net ORR affinity only as a coarse-grained illustrative
choice. It is not claimed to be a literal elementary four-electron step.

Row-generator Q is
 Q00,01=gamma*q, Q01,00=gamma;
 Q10,11=gamma*q, Q11,10=gamma;
 Q00,11=a+c*z, Q11,00=a+c;
 all other offdiagonal entries0, each diagonal minus its row sum.
The channel ledger retains captured adsorption and product separately.

2. EXACT STATIONARY SOLUTION AND ENERGY FLOW
Let u=a+c*z, v=a+c, t=u/v. Then
 pi00=1/[1+q+t(1+1/q)], pi01=q*pi00,
 pi11=t*pi00, pi10=pi11/q.
Both conditional hot probabilities are EXACTLY p*=q/(1+q). Both thermal
edges have zero net flux and zero entropy production. Nevertheless,
 J_capture=a(pi00-pi11)=J_product=c*pi11-c*z*pi00
 =a*c*(1-z)/[(a+c)(1+q)+(a+c*z)(1+1/q)]>0.
Capture putsE into the mode per net event; product removesE. Net internally
relayed energy isE*J. Total chemical power=A*kBT*J and total entropy/kB=A*J.
All are exact steady-state identities, with no positive offspring reduction.
The conditional thermal-projection current Jth equals this J exactly.

3. MATCHED CAPTURE-OFF COMPARATOR WITH THE SAME THERMAL PROJECTION
Keep the product and bath edges unchanged. Replace captured adsorption
00<->11 with ordinary adsorption00<->10 at ratesa,a*q. This is the same
chemical free-energy decrease without putting its released energy in the
mode. The product then waits for thermal10->11 excitation. Both paired
models have the SAME Kbar and the SAME thermal-averaged signed reward:
source average forward ratea/(1+q), reversea*q/(1+q), independent of routing.
The product averaged rates also remain fixed. Thus this comparison isolates
energetic routing within the supplied finite-state model.
Define
 D0=(1+q)(1+z/q)/a+(1+q)(1+1/q)/c,
 C=1+q+1/q+z.
Then exact long-run currents and causal routing gain are
 J_on=(1-z)/D0=Jth,
 J_off=(1-z)/(D0+C/gamma),
 J_on/J_off=1+C/(gamma*D0).
The gain can diverge as gamma decreases although the ON system remains
conditionally thermal with zero bath maintenance entropy. This DOES NOT
violate the thermal-projection bound, which controls J-Jth for one model,
not J_on/J_off. The OFF model falls far BELOW Jth. Two-sided certificates
for both models, or their full Poisson comparison, are needed for that ratio.

4. RETAINED ENERGY ORIGIN IS A TRAJECTORY QUANTITY
For forward product events, the exact fraction whose latest mode excitation
was the captured adsorption, rather than thermal excitation or the reverse
product channel, is
 f_capture=pi00*a/[pi00*(a+c*z)+pi10*gamma*q].
The label is attached upon entry to11 and cleared at any exit. Competing
memoryless exits make the origin distribution among product exits equal
to its distribution among entries. This is a precise lineage statement
within the finite model, not an inference from net mode-energy flux alone.
Conditional gate populations, net mode capture and total current do not
by themselves reveal this lineage. The ON mean current is independent of
gamma, while its retained-energy fraction and long-time noise change greatly.

 It is a conditional parameter regime, not an available molecular device.

A large mean gain is not a low-cost precise steady output. For signed product
countN(t), solve Qh=J-g. Its exact asymptotic variance rate is
 v=sum_channels [pi_i*k+ + pi_j*k-]*(r_ij+h_j-h_i)^2.
The Poisson martingale proves this formula; bounded endpoint terms vanish
in the asymptotic variance/t. For the ON cases above, Fano=v/J is.985641
(fast bath) and19137.8985 (100s memory), respectively. Corresponding single
unit asymptotic run times for1% relative standard error are13621s and
2.64476084e8s (~8.38years). These are asymptotic precision estimates, not
finite-sample confidence guarantees. Observation must also exceed mixing
and rare cold-state residence times. Independent parallel units divide the
sampling time at proportional physical material/resource cost; interacting
units cannot be assumed independent. Extremely slow gamma creates long
inactive periods and fast bursts, explaining the large noise penalty.

6. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT EARN
This supplies a fully reversible autonomous cycle, explicit energy source,
net product count, matched capture-off comparator, finite positive rates,
exact gain, mode-energy bookkeeping, a trajectory retention observable and
an explicit steady-output uncertainty bill. It proves the mathematical
possibility of beneficial internal energy routing and the insufficiency of
conditional thermal populations to identify it. It does not establish a new
physical law or novelty over molecular-machine/energy-relay theory. The
nearby dynamic catalysis and maintenance-cost literature is strong.
It cannot explain the reported nanosecond source gain: its source-like case
predicts only4ppm routing benefit. To claim that this mechanism describes
the real catalyst would require acquiring the actual full chemical/routing
rates, reverse adsorption traffic, mode free energy/lifetime and topology.
The source's complete reaction cycle has additional intermediates, site
couplings and a continuum of vibrational states absent here.

The optional different-pathway comparator in the code uses ordinary
adsorption/product rates in both gate states and a cold product rate
100*exp[-.192/(kBT)]. It gives12.8842x, but changesKbar. It is retained only
to illustrate static/pathway ambiguity and is NOT the matched comparator
used for the same-Kbar counterexample above.
