EXACT NET TURNOVER REWARD OF A FINITE PULSE
Let Q0 be a finite irreducible row-generator on complete chemical states.
A transition i->j has signed product reward r_ij (reverse reward -r_ij).
Let g0_i=sum_j q0_ij r_ij, pi0 Q0=0, J0=pi0 g0. Solve
    Q0 h = J0*1 - g0,  pi0 h=0.
This is the standard Markov reward Poisson equation, not a new identity.
For a finite perturbation Qt=Q0+deltaQt, starting from any distribution,
compare with the unpulsed system from the same initial distribution and let
both relax to the common baseline stationary state. Then exact integrated
net product excess is
 R = integral_0^infinity sum_i p_i(t) sum_j deltaq_ij(t)
                          [r_ij+h_j-h_i] dt.
Proof: d(p h)/dt=J0-p g0+p deltaQ h; integrate and subtract unpulsed
baseline. Identical initial and final distributions cancel the endpoint.
This charges displacement of later events, reverse cycles and refilling.
The edge value w_ij=r_ij+h_j-h_i can be negative or exceed one when an
edge unlocks a trapped state. Therefore neither positive offspring counting
nor a universal one-pulse/one-turnover bound follows from extra-clock count.

In an irreversible serial n-step cycle, each state has one exit at rate k_i,
J0=(sum_i 1/k_i)^-1 and w_i=J0/k_i=pi_i. For a short pulse changing only
step i with negligible refill and integrated excess hazard A, its leading
net-turnover reward from stationary arrival is pi_i^2(1-exp(-A)). One pi
is readiness; the other is the long-run phase advance per accelerated jump.
The expression is a short-pulse limit, not exact for arbitrary pulse width.

AUTONOMOUS STATIONARY EXTENSION
Lift h from chemical state i to every joint chemical/vibration state (i,e).
For any stationary autonomous joint process, aggregate actual transition
rates over the ending vibration state. Then exactly
 J-J0 = sum_(i,e) mu(i,e) sum_j [q_actual^e(i,j)-q0(i,j)] w_ij.
Energy-only transitions have zero reward and zero h difference. All changed
chemical channels, including coupled adsorption and its reverse, must be
included. This identity avoids an assumption that each adsorption creates
an independent net offspring. It is useful after the full generator has
been acquired; it does not acquire the generator from bulk currents.

A DEPLETION-COMPLETE TWO-PHASE MEAN-FIELD CONSTRUCTION
As a controlled irreversible reduction, empty->OH-like occupied has rate a,
occupied->empty/product has rate b. Every successful refill emits a pulse;
a pulse can remove each of M occupied targets with probability p. In the
well-mixed population limit, c=Mp and
 dtheta/dt=a(1-theta)-b theta-c a(1-theta)theta.
Product rate at steady state is J=a(1-theta), not the number of hot jumps.
Baseline theta0=a/(a+b), J0=ab/(a+b). Gain G gives
 theta=1-G(1-theta0), c=1/theta-1/(G theta0).
For illustrative G3.3 and promoted theta.3: theta0=.7878788,c=2.948718,
M=245.84 for the conditional pulse p=.01199443. This is a constructed
cycle, not an acquired site count. Source theta.3 is itself microkinetic
output, not measured independent coverage. Gross adsorption can be faster
than this irreversible refill and requires a reversible source model.

Let x=e*eta/(kBT), alpha_a=dlog(a)/dx, and similarly b,c. Exact local
log-current sensitivity in this reduction is
 alpha_eff=[alpha_a(1-theta)+alpha_b theta(1-c theta)
             +alpha_c c theta^2]/(1-c theta^2).
If weak-pulse probability scales with b, alpha_c=alpha_b, so numerator
reduces to alpha_a(1-theta)+alpha_b theta. A ratio alpha_a/alpha_b about.63
can match the measured modest slope change at G3.3,theta.3 with ordinary
positive transfer coefficients. Thus66.1/3.3=20mV/dec is a prediction only
of the restrictive single-RDS positive-feedback limit, not a general
contradiction of all finite-cycle excitation mechanisms.

GROSS ADSORPTION IS NOT FREE DRIVING
Forward gross O2 adsorption can be much faster than net ORR turnovers. It
cannot be replaced by z*TOF without a bound on reversibility/recycling. In a
full local-detailed-balance network, an adsorption/excitation edge and its
reverse must both be counted. A purely adsorption-desorption loop coupled
to an isothermal vibration bath has zero chemical affinity; it cannot
maintain a nonequilibrium steady gate by itself. Rapid equilibrium traffic
can supply thermal gate fluctuations and new static reactive pathways.

A GENERAL PAID THERMAL-GATE RESOURCE BOUND
Assume a two-level gate of genuine free-energy splitting E, state-independent
thermal decay gamma and excitation gamma*q with q=exp(-beta E). For joint
chemical state i, let x_i=mu(i,1), y_i=q*mu(i,0). The bath-edge contribution
to total dimensionless entropy production is exactly
 sigma_gate=gamma sum_i (x_i-y_i)log(x_i/y_i)>=0.
If the only externally driven chemical cycle is ORR with affinity A per net
turnover, full steady local detailed balance gives sigma_total=A*J, hence
 sigma_gate<=A*J.
For multiple sites per gate J is the total net current supplying that gate;
all other chemical affinities/resources must be added if present. This is
stronger than a gross pump-count argument because reverse loops are paid.
Log-sum gives the marginal necessary condition
 gamma[p-q(1-p)] log[p/(q(1-p))] <= A*J.
It bounds sustaining p away from thermal p_eq=q/(1+q). State-resolved form
also charges conditional gate/readiness correlations. It does not imply
that .126eV, a single-point molecular energy gain, equals E. That missing
identification is crucial. Nor is heat dumped by one internal subsystem
always bounded by total chemical power; use entropy production, not an
unjustified one-way energy-flux inequality.

THE LARGE-REWARD TRAP DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY RESCUE A SHORT BARRIER PULSE
For an off-cycle leaf P with only escape P->i at cold rate delta, g(P)=0,
the baseline Poisson equation gives h_i-h_P=J0/delta exactly. This edge can
have a very large net-turnover reward: release unlocks many later fuel-driven
cycles. But a fixed barrier pulse multiplies its cold rate by1+f(t), hence
 R=J0 integral p_P(t) f(t)dt <= J0*Tact,
where Tact=integral f(t)dt. The larger release reward cancels the smaller
cold escape rate. For the source-conditioned .192eV/41ns/298K pulse,
Tact=11.531141366 microseconds. The same exact bound holds for a step of an
irreversible serial cycle, because its exit-edge weight is J0/k_i. It is a
bound on expected NET turnover after full relaxation, not one hot jump.
A genuinely new release pathway whose rate is not a fixed multiple of the
cold escape need not satisfy this particular bound; its energetics and
reverse pathway then require separate acquisition.

A further exact trap restriction: a dead-end edge has zero stationary net
flux even if the rest of the chemical network is driven. Accelerating both
its directions symmetrically preserves the full stationary distribution and
has ZERO integrated net reward from stationary initialization. A trap-rescue
explanation therefore needs an energy-consuming channel, a changed state
free energy, or acquired state-conditioned pulse correlations; ordinary
symmetric barrier lowering is insufficient.

For a stationary population with at most z effective pulses per net
turnover, one-way serial/leaf barrier multiplication and a nonoverlapping
or peak-reset pulse envelope, gain obeys
 G-1 <= lambda*Tact <= z*J0*G*Tact.
Consequently G3.3 requires z >= .6969697/(J0*Tact). At baselineJ0=.2,1,4/s
this is302212,60442,15111. This deliberately conditional bound is invalid
if gross adsorption is substituted for net turnover without proof, if
energy pulses stack beyond the assumed envelope, if the step's quoted
barrier is not at the operating potential, or if a different path is opened.
It is not a claim those assumptions have been acquired in this catalyst.

CONDITIONAL THERMAL-PROJECTION CURRENT CERTIFICATE (ROOT COMPOSITION CHECKED)
Let i denote the complete chemical configuration and g the binary gate.
Thermal bath edges at fixed i have up/down gamma_i*q_i,gamma_i. Let
pstar_i=q_i/(1+q_i), K_g the marginal chemical generator after summing all
ending gate states, and r_g its signed product reward rate. All chemical
gate-changing source edges and their reverses belong in these quantities.
Rowwise Kbar=(1-pstar)K0+pstar K1, rbar likewise. If Kbar is irreducible,
solve Kbar h=Jth-rbar. Then define
 w_i=(r1-r0)_i+[(K1-K0)h]_i.
For any stationary joint process, mu_i=Pr(i), p_i=Pr(g=1|i), exact identity
 J-Jth=sum_i mu_i (p_i-pstar_i)w_i.
Thermal-bath entropy contribution is
 sigma_bath=sum_i mu_i gamma_i(1+q_i)(p_i-pstar_i)
                          [logit(p_i)-logit(pstar_i)].
As derivative logit>=4, Cauchy gives
 |J-Jth|^2 <= sigma_bath * sum_i mu_i w_i^2/[4 gamma_i(1+q_i)]
            <= sigma_total * max_i w_i^2/[4 gamma_i(1+q_i)].
If every cycle affinity is A times net ORR product reward, sigma_total=A J.
Writing V for the maximum above and kappa=A V/Jth gives
 G=J/Jth <=1+kappa/2+sqrt(kappa+kappa^2/4), for Jth>0.
The square-root absolute shift is valid without a positive-gain premise.
This is a finite comparison, not a statement of infinitesimal response.

SHARPER COMPUTABLE VERSION
Set c_i(p)=gamma_i(1+q_i)(p-pstar_i)[logit(p)-logit(pstar_i)]. For every
lambda>0, J-Jth<=lambda*sigma+max_i sup_p{w_i(p-pstar_i)-lambda*c_i(p)}.
With sigma=A J and lambda*A<1 this yields a direct rational upper bound on J.
Use sum_i mu_i of the inner sup if stationary chemical masses are known.
The inner scalar costs are convex. No physical optimal-controller attainment
is implied: stationarity and allowed chemical transitions can obstruct it.

COMPARATOR CAUTION
Jth is the same complete chemical pathways under instantaneous conditional
thermal equilibration; it is not capture-off and is not the unmodified
catalyst. Zero excess over Jth does not prove there is no internal energy
recycling. For example, a four-state loop00->11 (charged adsorption),
11->00 (consuming product), with both reverse channels and only thermal
leaf states01/10, has conditional thermal gate occupations by leaf balance.
It can carry equal positive capture/consumption currents while J=Jth.
The chemical pathways already embody that coupling. Causal gain still
requires a like-for-like capture-off comparison, with all channel changes.

FINAL SCIENTIFIC SCOPE
The observed bulk TOF/Tafel gains, source simulations and spectroscopy do
not uniquely acquire an autonomous energy-recycling causal generator.
Finite pulse action, exact net-turnover response, complete reversible
cycles, conditional thermal comparison and counterexamples have now been
constructed and executed. They distinguish static pathway changes, finite
cycle timing, thermal gate traffic and actual stored-energy lineage.
The remaining operation is physical identification: mode free energy and
state-resolved decay/transfer channels, voltage-specific chemical rates
and reverses, conditional adsorbate populations, reactive site count and
source-target topology. Without these, neither reproducing3.3by parameters
nor rejecting one restricted pulse model resolves the real catalyst.
No economical or noneconomical civilization-changing physical capability
has been established by this branch. The complete rare-event alternative
is preserved separately; its actual arbitrary-basin MFPT gap remains open.
