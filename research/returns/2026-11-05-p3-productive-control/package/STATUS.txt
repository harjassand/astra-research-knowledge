SCIENTIFIC STATUS AND EXACT SCOPES
5 November 2026

PROVED IN THE MAIN REPORT

1. A positive-reward finite-cycle nearest-neighbor process has the displayed
   forward optimal-work recursion. The policy is optimal among all admissible
   randomized history-dependent controls for every safe start and finite depth.
   This is an explicit specialization of established skip-free dynamic programming.

2. A finite prefix and invariant tail interval certify a common exact optimum for
   every fixed state-dependent Markov kernel inside uniform action envelopes.
   The certificate itself requires neither common invariant laws nor numerical
   homogeneity. Acquiring those envelopes at one state does require homogeneity
   or independently earned physical transfer guarantees.

3. The explicit family has a nontrivial stable policy (A at zero, B elsewhere),
   while exchanging kinetic profiles reverses the policy. The same action rule
   maximizes a stated resource-priced objective in the nominal equal-energy model.
   The executed raw-work certificate is not automatically a certificate for every
   energy price; reward intervals must be recomputed.

4. Stable policy acquisition has an evidence budget independent of depth N.
   Relative absolute-value forecasting has an Omega(N^2 epsilon^-2 log(1/delta))
   lower bound at the same transition-sampling access. The hard pairs also lie in
   the precise acquisition success family for N>=100. This compares different
   statistical outputs; ordinary direct policy methods can match the decision.

5. A separate scale-function bound certifies finite useful missions under conditional
   local drift bounds, including history dependence. This is not an optimality
   theorem for every changing environment, and a low-risk mission needs safe reserve.

6. If all distinguishing evidence is in rare hazards, an adaptive and parallel
   experiment still needs aggregate exposure of order epsilon^-1 log(1/delta).
   Event races or finite holds with persistent failure readout attain that order,
   subject to the explicitly charged timer, reset, meter and failure-flag contract.

7. An exponentially small omitted direct-failure transition can make exact optimal-
   policy certification exponentially costly. This exact-policy statement is not
   promoted to a lower bound for every approximately optimal adaptive policy.

8. Work-conserving pooled repair attains a sharp useful-output capacity boundary
   within the continuous-time repair contract. Repair stock, actual energy cost and
   indispensable support hazard bound achievable work. The classical repair model
   is an independent physical companion, not a proven realization of the cycle model.
