# 2. The decisive operation: certify actions through forward differences

Consider an operating system with damage count

\[
i\in\{0,\ldots,N\},
\]

where \(N\) is service failure. Each safe operating cycle lasts a fixed finite time \(\Delta\). An allowed action \(a\):

- completes useful output \(r_i(a)>0\);
- increases damage by one with probability \(p_i(a)\);
- decreases damage by one with probability \(q_i(a)\);
- otherwise leaves damage unchanged.

Downward movement reflects at zero. Assume \(p_i(a)\ge p_{\min}>0\). The reward is delivered before the transition, so even the output assigned to the terminal cycle has actually been completed.

This is a declared **actual cycle kernel**. It cannot be justified by taking a small time step and silently discarding multiple physical events.

Let \(V_i^{(N)}\) be the largest expected useful work before failure, allowing every admissible randomized controller that can use the entire observed history. Define

\[
c_i(a)=\frac{r_i(a)}{p_i(a)},\qquad
\rho_i(a)=\frac{q_i(a)}{p_i(a)}.
\]

The exact recursion is

\[
d_0=\max_a c_0(a),
\]

\[
d_i=\max_a\left\{c_i(a)+\rho_i(a)d_{i-1}\right\},
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
V_i^{(N)}=\sum_{j=i}^{N-1}d_j.
}
\]

An action attaining the maximum in the recursion is optimal at state \(i\).

The important structural fact is that **the action recursion does not contain the terminal depth \(N\)**. One rule on the nonnegative integers, restricted to the states below \(N\), is optimal for every finite \(N\) and every safe initial state.

The verification is short. Writing \(d_i=V_i-V_{i+1}\), the action inequality becomes

\[
p_i(a)d_i\ge r_i(a)+q_i(a)d_{i-1}.
\]

This is precisely the Bellman inequality. Accumulated reward plus remaining value is a supermartingale under any allowed policy; maximizing actions give equality. Absorption is integrable because any sequence of \(N\) upward moves has conditional probability at least \(p_{\min}^N\), giving the uniform bound

\[
\mathbb E T_N\le \frac{N}{p_{\min}^N}.
\]

Forward recursion for processes of this kind is established machinery. Wijngaard and Stidham explicitly developed maximum-total-reward forward recursion for skip-free controlled processes; Serfozo’s earlier work covers controlled random walks, birth–death processes, and queues. The present proof fixes the reward, initialization, and policy quantifiers needed for this construction. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}

## How a finite certificate covers infinitely many depths

Suppose acquisition and physical transfer guarantees supply bounds

\[
p_a^-\le p_i(a)\le p_a^+,\qquad
q_a^-\le q_i(a)\le q_a^+,\qquad
r_a^-\le r_i(a)\le r_a^+.
\]

These bounds may contain different fixed transition rows at every state. Define

\[
c_a^-=\frac{r_a^-}{p_a^+},\quad
c_a^+=\frac{r_a^+}{p_a^-},\qquad
\rho_a^-=\frac{q_a^-}{p_a^+},\quad
\rho_a^+=\frac{q_a^+}{p_a^-}.
\]

A finite prefix is certified by interval comparisons of the competing affine expressions. For the remaining states, suppose one action \(*\) satisfies

\[
\rho_*^->1,
\qquad
\rho_*^->\max_{b\ne *}\rho_b^+.
\]

Set

\[
D_{\mathrm{box}}
=
\max_{b\ne *}
\frac{(c_b^+-c_*^-)_+}
{\rho_*^--\rho_b^+}.
\]

If the certified lower bound on the preceding difference has reached \(D_{\mathrm{box}}\), action \(*\) dominates every competitor thereafter. Its update keeps the difference above that threshold.

Thus a finite prefix and one invariant interval certify the entire policy.

The certificate does not need an accurate numerical value for the exponentially large \(V_i^{(N)}\). It needs enough accuracy to settle action inequalities.

**Acquiring uniform bounds remains a separate obligation.** A homogeneous port can be tested at one safely prepared interior state. Arbitrarily varying state-dependent rows require coverage of those rows, or independently earned physical bounds that apply to them. The theorem does not turn a few measurements near zero into knowledge of an unrestricted tail.

# 5. A separate guarantee for useful missions

Mean useful-work optimality does not establish a small probability of failure before a deadline. I derived a separate mission bound.

\[
p_i\le p_i^+,\qquad q_i\ge q_i^-.
\]

\[
b_0=1,\qquad
b_i=\frac{q_i^-}{p_i^+}b_{i-1},
\qquad
H(i)=\sum_{j=0}^{i-1}b_j.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\Pr_x(T_N\le t)
\le
\frac{H(x)+t p_0^+}{H(N)}.
}
\]

The proof uses the nonpositive conditional drift of \(H\) at positive states. It does not require equilibrium preparation or approximation of the complete trajectory law.

For the example, let

\[
R=\frac{q_B^-}{p_B^+}>1.
\]

Starting from zero,

\[
\Pr_0(T_N\le t)
\le
\frac{t p_0^+(R-1)}{R^N-1}.
\]

Survival through \(t\) cycles guarantees at least \(2t\) useful units.

The physical failure budget must also include calibration error, initialization error, and additional catastrophic interface failures:

\[
\boxed{
\delta_{\mathrm{cal}}
+\eta_{\mathrm{init}}
+\frac{t p_0^+(R-1)}{R^N-1}

}
\]

With relative timing error at most \(\omega\) and endpoint-report bias at most \(\beta\), the surviving contrast is at least

\[
g=e^{-(1+\omega)/2}-e^{-(1-\omega)}-2\beta.
\]

For a fixed positive \(g\), taking

\[
m\ge2g^{-2}\log(1/\delta)
\]

paired trials identifies the better action. Aggregate hold exposure is at most

\[
\frac{m(1+\omega)}{\varepsilon},
\]

with \(2m\) resets and endpoint measurements additionally charged.

The endpoint measurement has a substantive physical meaning: **failure must remain observable until readout**. That requires deferred recovery or a protected retained first-failure flag. Reading only “working now” is insufficient if recovery could have occurred. Reset fidelity and common metrology failures also require their own guarantees.

This gives a boundary with an attaining operation at a finite timer-and-readout interface.

## An omitted catastrophic channel can make exact policy certification exponentially costly

A second construction begins with a local model whose unique optimum is always action \(A\). Another action \(B\) has mean useful work

\[
M_B(N)=24(1.5^N-1)-8N.
\]

Add only to \(A\) a direct catastrophic transition with per-cycle probability

\[
h=\frac{10}{M_B(N)}.
\]

That exponentially small omitted transition limits always-\(A\) work to at most \(M_B/10\), while \(B\) retains \(M_B\).

Exact optimal-policy certification across the two possible models requires

\[
\mathbb E K
\ge
\frac{M_B(N)}{20}\operatorname{kl}(1-\delta,\delta).
\]

Reset-to-zero probes attain the exponential dependence: a direct jump from zero to failure is impossible in the nominal model and occurs with probability \(h\) in the alternative.

This is an **exact-policy certification** obstruction. It does not establish that every approximately optimal adaptive controller faces the same lower bound.

The consequence is precise: finite local evidence can settle the action inequalities while remaining insufficient to exclude a rare bypass mechanism that changes the correct decision.

# 7. Productive repair supplies the resource boundary

The independent repair construction makes the useful-output and resource accounting explicit.

There are \(N\) modules. With \(K\) failed modules, output is

\[
\nu(N-K).
\]

Each healthy module fails at rate \(\lambda\). There are \(c_N\) repair channels, each completing repairs at rate \(\mu\). Service fails when \(K=m<N\).
