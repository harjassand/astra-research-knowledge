ROOT P3 MATHEMATICAL PREPARATION: EXACT CONDUCTANCE TRADEOFF FOR TWO CATALYTIC LINKAGES
10 November 2026 Brisbane. A direct design calculation for two cut families, not a proved embedded gap, general compiler or new allocation.

Use the accepted network 0<->A+B, B<->2B at lambdaA=lambdaB=1. For the pair linkage use every catalyst of total degree<=K, and for the B linkage use every catalyst of total degree<=K+h, where K,h are fixed nonnegative integers. Use equal positive rational circulation scale c<=1 for the four directions, with coefficients c/w!. Each separate reversible translated linkage is complex balanced at(1,1), so the WHOLE graph-preserving process has pi=Pois(1) tensor Pois(1). The actual class containing the origin is N^2. No species or edges are added.
Write N=A+B and F_j(n)=sum_(l=0)^j binom(n,l). The four rates are
 q_pair+=c*F_K(N), q_pair-=c*A*B*F_K(N-2),
 q_B+=c*B*F_(K+h)(N-1), q_B-=c*B*(B-1)*F_(K+h)(N-2),
with disabled rates0. Let a be their total. The embedded firing chain has invariant probability rho(x)=pi(x)*a(x)/(pi a), since all moments of pi and pi a are finite. Define cut conductance Phi(C)=sum_(x in C,y outside C)pi(x)q(x,y)/sum_(x in C)pi(x)a(x). It equals the embedded boundary-flow/mass ratio, independent of c and its stationary normalisation.

FIRST CUT: THE KNOWN FAST PAIR. Let u_n=(n,0),v_n=(n+1,1),C_n={u_n,v_n}, and
 H_n=F_K(n+2)+F_(K+h)(n+1).
The ONLY exit contributions are from v_n. Since pi(v_n)=pi(u_n)/(n+1), exact summation gives
 Phi(C_n)=H_n/[2*(n+1)*F_K(n)+H_n].
For each fixed j, F_j(n+t)~n^j/j! as n->infinity. Hence:
 h=0: Phi(C_n)~1/n ->0;
 h=1: Phi(C_n)->1/(2K+3)>0;
 h>=2: Phi(C_n)->1.
Increasing the B-linkage degree by ONE already removes this particular vanishing-conductance mechanism. Making it arbitrarily faster is not automatically harmless.

SECOND CUT: THE SLOW A TAIL. Let D_n={A>=n}. Its only exits decrease A at A=n by the pair-death reaction. Thus its unnormalised boundary flow is
 c*pi_A(n)*n*E_(B~Pois1)[B*F_K(n+B-2)]
 ~c*pi_A(n)*n^(K+1)/K!.
At A=a->infinity, Poisson B averaging gives total-activity asymptotics:
 h=0: E_B a(a,B)~c*a^(K+1)/K!;
 h=1: E_B a(a,B)~c*a^(K+1)*[1/K!+2/(K+1)!];
 h>=2: E_B a(a,B)~2c*a^(K+h)/(K+h)!.
These follow from E B=E B(B-1)=1 and the fixed-degree leading terms; domination by a fixed Poisson moment justifies passage of the limit. For any fixed positive polynomial P,
 sum_(a>=n)pi_A(a)P(a)~pi_A(n)P(n): its j-th tail ratio is bounded by a polynomial in j times(n+1)^-j. Therefore
 h=0: Phi(D_n)->1;
 h=1: Phi(D_n)->(K+1)/(K+3)>0;
 h>=2: Phi(D_n)~[(K+h)!/(2K!)]*n^(1-h) ->0.

RIGOROUS CONSEQUENCE AND LIMIT. rho(C_n),rho(D_n)->0. The indicator test has Rayleigh quotient Phi(C)/(1-rho(C)) for the embedded stationary Dirichlet form. Thus h=0 has zero embedded gap by C_n, whereas EVERY fixed h>=2 has zero embedded gap by D_n. The same tests obstruct the activity-weighted nominal inequality needed by136C at these parameters.
