Finite-affinity chemical memory and the feedback resource interface

1. Why exact nominal balance does not supply a powered controller

The current arbitrary-network detailed-balanced kinetic question remains
open at its actual generality. Its resource and thinning constructions
do not automatically implement thermodynamic fuel or an endogenous
error-correcting control loop. A different physical question must state
those resources explicitly rather than silently import a chemostat.

For example, consider the antithetic reference/measurement reactions
 0 -> Z1 at rate mu, X -> X+Z2 at rate theta*X,
 Z1+Z2 ->0, Z1 ->Z1+X.
The ideal controller has L(Z1-Z2)=mu-theta*X. Add positive reverse
coefficients gamma1 for Z1->0 and gamma2 for X+Z2->X, with the other
pairs also reversed. Then EXACTLY
 L(Z1-Z2)=mu-theta*X-gamma1*Z1+gamma2*X*Z2.
At stationarity, assuming the stated moments exist,
 theta E X=mu-gamma1 E Z1+gamma2 E[X Z2].
The ideal integral identity is lost by this particular finite-reverse
implementation. This is not an all-design no-go for finite-affinity
adaptation or a theorem that every useful controller must be driven in
the same manner. It identifies the terms a proposed physical controller
must actually pay. A controller can instead target a finite tolerance,
use a different conservation relation, or exploit an explicitly powered
and measured physical signal.

2. An actual robust bistable memory in a charged ideal CRN

Here is a complete single-module calculation, not the discovery of
bistability itself or a universal elementary/fuelled compiler. Let V be
an integer>=2^20. Use four labelled channels, with aggregate rates
 lambda(n)=6n(n-1)/V+6V,
 mu(n)=n(n-1)(n-2)/V^2+11n.
The constant-source birth is positive even at zero. Every falling
factorial has its exact stochastic meaning. The density drift at the
formal large-volume limit is
 -x^3+6x^2-11x+6=-(x-1)(x-2)(x-3),
with stable wells1,3 and unstable separator2.

The fuel-resolved reactions are A+2X <->3X with raw coefficients1 in
both directions, and B <->X with raw coefficients11 in both directions.
Chemostats of concentrations A=6 and B=6/11 give the rates above.
The closed full reaction system has exact detailed balance at unit
activities. The unequal supplied chemostats drive it with finite cycle
affinity log(11), not zero chemical work. The first full reaction has
three molecules per side; this calculation is NOT an elementary binary
implementation. No open detailed-balance compiler is invoked on the
non-detailed-balanced chemostatted reduction.

Independently multiply each of the four actual channels by ANY
predictable factor in[1-delta0,1+delta0], delta0=2^-14. The following
estimates are pointwise throughout that rate box and need neither an
invariant faulty law nor independent fault multipliers.

3. Whole-run population and all-channel work, including rare tails

For all integer n>=0 and V>=1, straightforward polynomial inequalities
give, with x=n/V,
 L n <=1024 V-n^3/(4V^2),
 lambda_fault+mu_fault<=32[V+n^3/V^2].
For the first bound use
 L n/V<=-(3/4)x^3+(21/2)x^2+15/2
and max_(x>=0)[(21/2)x^2-(1/2)x^3]=686.
For the second use n(n-1)<=n^2, n(n-1)(n-2)<=n^3 and
x,x^2<=1+x^3. These conservative constants cover the whole fault box.

Stopping at population K and using the first inequality proves
nonexplosion by P(sup_(s<=t)n_s>=K)<=(n0+1024Vt)/K. Bounded-state
rates are finite. Monotone convergence then yields
 E integral_0^t n_s^3/V^2 ds<=4n0+4096Vt,
 E F_all(t)<=128n0+131104Vt.
For n0<=4V this is <=2^18 V(1+t), and
 E sup_(s<=t)n_s<=2^19 V(1+t).
All four reaction labels and all faulty excursions are paid. The
calculation never conditions on successful memory operation.

4. A fixed positive fault margin and exponential barrier estimate

Put r=1+1/1024. Throughout the density band[5/4,7/4], the worst
downward rate exceeds r times the worst upward rate. Throughout
[9/4,11/4], the reverse inequality favors the upper well. The finite-V
falling-factorial corrections preserve both inequalities for V>=2^20.

One exact certificate for the leading density polynomials uses
 (1-delta0)*good-(1+delta0)*r*bad.
For the lower band good=x^3+11x, bad=6x^2+6; for the upper band swap
them. Expand each cubic into Bernstein coefficients on eight equal
rational subintervals. The respective minimum coefficients are
27860925/134217728 and209007351/1073741824, both positive. The
finite-V correction has magnitude at most100/V over these bands, so
the claimed margins remain strict. The exact rational calculation was
performed; it is a certificate for these two intervals, not a network
enumeration or a general positivity oracle.

On the lower band r^n is a supermartingale, since
 L r^n=r^n(r-1)[lambda_fault-mu_fault/r]<=0.
On the upper band use r^-n. Conditional optional stopping shows that
one excursion across its protected band has probability at most
exp(-V/4096) of crossing toward the separator. The number of possible
excursions is at most F_all+1. This conditional argument remains valid
when the fault rule depends on the entire past.

Starting at n0=V or3V, a wrong-bit crossing of n=2V must cross one of
these bands. Consequently, for every t>=0,
 sup_(admitted fault policies)
 P[wrong-bit separator crossed by t]
 <=2^20 V(1+t)exp(-V/4096).

For a requested finite horizon t and error epsilon, choose
 K=1+ceil(log2((1+t)/epsilon)), V=2^20 K.
The displayed bound is below epsilon. This uses only a paid integer
bit-length/logarithm comparison; it is not an exact-real oracle.
Microscopic coefficients, density wells and delta0 are fixed. Material
volume and molecule count grow logarithmically with duration/error.
The expected actual firing and chemical-work costs grow as V(1+t).
One fixed finite module is not claimed to remember a bit forever at
positive intrinsic noise.

5. Explicit fuel and a finite-reservoir interface

With chemical potentials measured relative to unit activities, the
chemostats have mu_A=log6 and mu_B=log(6/11); their difference is log11.
Each labelled reaction transfers at most one A or B. Its absolute
chemical work has a fixed bound<3 per event in these units, so the
entire absolute work is <=3 F_all. Parallel chemical mechanisms must
retain their actual labels: the one-dimensional aggregate birth/death
chain can be reversible while marked A-to-B cycles dissipate work.

An infinitely maintained chemostat is NOT free. A finite-time material
implementation can instead use explicit A,B inventories and choose
their reaction coefficients to keep initial effective concentrations
at6 and6/11. Conditional on at most K_fuel firings, their relative
change is at most C K_fuel/N_res. Taking
 K_fuel>=E F_all(t)/epsilon and N_res>=C K_fuel/epsilon
pays a failure probability and a relative-rate error using finite
material. Reserve part of the rate box for that error: use base faults
<=delta0/2 and reservoir variation<=delta0/4, choosing the fixed C at
least4/delta0. Their product remains inside the delta0 box above; no
margin is spent twice. The reservoir amounts are polynomial in
V,t,epsilon^-1.
This is a finite-horizon reservoir approximation; eventual depletion
and loss of the powered memory are retained, not renamed all-time
autonomy.

The explicit fuel-resolved network is conservative with positive
weights. Its known detailed-balanced elementary/conservative lowering
is therefore a COMPATIBLE possible interface at finite horizon, after
paying its physical clock, all fuel material, normalization and allowed
path approximation. It is not the current arbitrary open all-time
theorem. Spatial finite-density realization, the lowering's inverse
precision, actual reservoir tracking and primitive work transfer still
need their complete contracts; no prior point or hard-core result is
silently extended to those conclusions.

6. What remains for a general consequential controller

The single-module memory has no WRITE, COPY, nonlinear gate, spatial
communication or output actuation theorem. Adding catalytic controls
can move the density drift but must preserve stable margins in idle
modules and pay backaction, actual work and error rates. A programmable
chemical machine would need those operations to compose with local
physical error repair and an explicitly bounded fuel supply. Scalar
metastability alone is neither a universal controller nor a new physical
advantage. An all-network quantitative stability law could help its
implementation, but is not logically the same scientific question.

The constructed operation is a fixed-affinity/fixed-rate-error module
with paid finite-time
reliability and whole faulty tails, plus a finite-fuel composition
interface. It makes the larger thermodynamic programming hypothesis
more concrete without claiming the remaining molecular gates or
spatial architecture exist.
