1. The operation to preserve

Take the completed singleton-to-zero species-forest interface. Each species i
has a parent p(i), possibly empty. The analytical weights obey1<w_i<2 and
Delta_i=w_i-w_parent>0, with empty weight0. Put
 F_i=1+sum_(j:p(j)=i) F_j, 1<=F_i<=s,
where s is the number of fine species, and let a_i>0 be its nominal activity.
Its catalytic pairs are
 2S_i <-> S_i+S_parent,
or2S_i<->S_i at an empty parent. Raw coefficients are
 eta F_i/(a_i^2 Delta_i),
 eta F_i/(a_i a_parent Delta_i),
using a_empty=1. Their nominal coefficient/activity balance is exact. At a
well-mixed count state the quadratic drift of V=sum_i w_i z_i is
 eta[-sum_i F_i x_i^2+sum_(p(i)!=0)F_i x_i x_parent], x_i=z_i/a_i.
The finite forest and descendant SUMS pay a strictly negative quadratic
margin. No product along a long word is used.

An arbitrary local encounter kernel does NOT automatically preserve this
common drift. Pair occupancies are not the products of global counts.

2. A compactly supported positive kernel makes the same proof pointwise

On the unit three-torus let0<r<=1/8, chi_r=r^-3 1_[-r/2,r/2]^3 and set
 K_r=chi_r*reflected(chi_r)
    =r^-3 product_(j=1)^3 (1-|x_j|/r)_+,
periodically extended. It is nonnegative, normalized to integral1, supported
in[-r,r]^3 and K_r(0)=r^-3. Its description is finite rational piecewise
polynomial. Equivalently its Fourier coefficients are |hat chi_r|^2>=0.

For an ACTUAL finite particle configuration define the positive empirical
measure mu_i=a_i^-1 sum_(particles l of type i) delta_(q_l), and
 E_ij=int int K_r(x-y) dmu_i(x)dmu_j(y).
Then E_ij is the Hilbert inner product of chi_r*mu_i and chi_r*mu_j.
Consequently
 E_ij<= (E_ii+E_jj)/2,
 E_ii>= (z_i/a_i)^2.
The second inequality is ordinary Cauchy--Schwarz against the constant1
on a torus of volume1. These statements hold at every configuration,
including coincident particles and species segregation. No diffusion,
equilibrium, concentration of measure or joint-position TV is needed.

Use this same kernel on every catalytic pair. For nonempty parent, a
designated S_i at x changes to S_parent, while its S_i catalyst at y stays
put, at the forward ordered-pair rate. Reverse parent-at-x/catalyst-at-y
changes the parent back to S_i. At an empty parent the forward removes
the designated particle and retains its catalyst; reverse release retains
that particle and places the new S_i at displacement density K_r. Unary
word channels act at the same point; empty-source births use uniform
position measure. These are local elementary stochastic rules.

Their forward/reverse Poisson-point Palm flux measures agree whenever
the displayed coefficient/activity products agree. For identical forward
reactants count ordered DISTINCT pairs; the diagonal has already been
removed by the factorial convention. No extra factor2 is inserted.
The count graph, payload/label rules and zero-auxiliary initialization
are not changed by this spatial rule.

The ACTUAL quadratic local contribution, before falling-factorial diagonal
corrections, is now exactly
 eta[-sum_i F_i E_ii+sum_(p(i)!=0)F_i E_i,parent].
The same subtree identity and the Hilbert inequality give at most
 -(eta/2)sum_i E_ii.
For arbitrary independent instantaneous chemical multipliers1+/-delta,
the diagonal coefficient after Young's inequality is at most
 -(1-delta)F_i+(1+delta)(F_i+sum_children F_j)/2
 =2delta F_i-(1+delta)/2.
Thus delta<=1/(8s) gives at most-1/4 at every species. The whole rate-box
quadratic contribution is bounded above by

Subtracting self particles from E_ii adds only the affine correction
 <=(1+delta)eta K_r(0) sum_i F_i z_i/a_i^2.
Empty-parent catalytic reverses are unary and also affine. Base binary
bindings have negative V increment since each w_i<2 and both reactant

unaffected by adaptive covariance, drift or other motion that preserves
particle types between chemical events and defines a nonexplosive
continuous motion. Completing the square gives an affine Foster bound
and a uniform polynomial mean count. Integrating the negative n^2 term
pays every actual binary2->2 cycle. Their intensities are bounded by a

not cut off, restarted or replaced by equilibrium samples.

4. An exact diagnostic shows why the box kernel is different

Take a three-node forest g->i->p->empty, with F_g=1,F_i=2,F_p=3 and
unit activities. Put no g particles, M i particles at EACH of the eight
corners(+/-3r/4,+/-3r/4,+/-3r/4), and8M/3 p particles at the centre,
where M is a multiple of3. Different corners do not encounter under the
box kernel, whereas each corner encounters the centre. Writing K0 for
its central value gives
 E_ii=8M^2 K0, E_pp=(64M^2/9)K0, E_ip=(64M^2/3)K0,
 -2E_ii-3E_pp+2E_ip=(16M^2/3)K0>0.
Falling-factorial and unary corrections are only affine in M. Thus the
forest's well-mixed certificate is not a spatial certificate for that
box kernel. This is a counterexample to that TRANSFER, not to every box-
kernel compiler or to the currently posted conservative local question.
