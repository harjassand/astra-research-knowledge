## 2. The finite structure behind the result

The central structural result is a rank criterion.

Write a channel \(\Phi:M_d\to M_b\) using a minimal Kraus representation,

\[
\Phi(X)=\sum_{i=1}^{k}K_iXK_i^\dagger,
\qquad
k=\operatorname{rank}J_\Phi,
\]

where \(J_\Phi\) is its Choi matrix. Let

\[
S=\Phi(I_d).
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\Phi\text{ preserves the identity channel’s complete maximal profile}
\iff
\operatorname{rank}S
=
d\,\operatorname{rank}J_\Phi.
}
\]

For exact rational input, this requires two finite matrix-rank computations.

This is a criterion for equality with the **identity’s profile**. It does not replace N1690’s general complete maximal-order evaluator, and it supplies no generic SLD/Umegaki comparison algorithm.

### Why the criterion works

Form the horizontal Kraus matrix

\[
W=[K_1\ \cdots\ K_k].
\]

It maps \(\mathbb C^k\otimes\mathbb C^d\) into the output space, and

\[
\Phi(X)=W(I_k\otimes X)W^\dagger,
\qquad
S=WW^\dagger.
\]

The rank condition says exactly that \(W\) is injective. On its range, the channel therefore has the form

\[
\boxed{
X\longmapsto W(I_k\otimes X)W^\dagger,
}
\]

with an invertible congruence after restriction to the effective output.

For positive definite \(A,B\), the maximal operator perspective is

\[
P_f(A,B)
=
B^{1/2}
f(B^{-1/2}AB^{-1/2})
B^{1/2}.
\]

Perspectives respect invertible congruences. Consequently,

\[
P_f(\Phi_R(A),\Phi_R(B))
=
\Phi_R(P_f(A,B)).
\]

Taking the trace proves maximal-divergence preservation. Taking the physical-output partial trace proves the stronger reference-valued identity. Tensor products preserve injectivity, which earns the statement for every tensor power without a reference cutoff.

The reverse implication comes from a finite equality argument. Normalize the channel by its output on the identity:

\[
\pi(X)=S^{-1/2}\Phi(X)S^{-1/2}.
\]

This is unital and completely positive. Its Schwarz defect satisfies

\[
\operatorname{Tr}S\bigl[\pi(H^2)-\pi(H)^2\bigr]
=
\operatorname{Tr}H^2-
\operatorname{Tr}\bigl[\Phi(H)S^{-1}\Phi(H)\bigr].
\]

Perfect RLD preservation makes the right-hand side zero for every Hermitian \(H\). Positivity forces the Schwarz defect itself to vanish. Thus \(\pi\) is a representation of the whole input matrix algebra, giving the displayed normal form and rank condition.

\[

\operatorname{Tr}H:
H\otimes I_d\preceq G

\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathbb C^d}Z\succeq I_k

Both optima are attained, with no duality gap in this positive-definite case.
