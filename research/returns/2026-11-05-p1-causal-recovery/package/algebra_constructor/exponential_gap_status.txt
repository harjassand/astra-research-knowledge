EXPONENTIAL SINGULAR-GAP ATTEMPT: AUDIT, CLOSEST INEQUALITY, AND LIMITATION
5 November 2026.

STATUS. The desired estimate gamma_n(theta)>=a(theta) exp[-b(theta)n] has NOT been proved here. Operational_constructor's crossing/interpolation proof establishes exact scalar kernel and an exp[-O_theta(n log n)] lower bound. I found no flaw in that proof or its stated constants. The full sublinear-memory endpoint and the joint weak-angle regime remain unresolved. The estimate below is genuinely exponential for a different, fixed-point defect; it must not be substituted for the one-step singular defect.

Definitions. Phi_n(X)=Tr_S[V_n(I_S tensor X)V_n^dagger]/2, V_n=U_Sn ... U_S1, U=c I+i s SWAP. All Hilbert-Schmidt norms use normalized trace. Let q=1-s^4 and r=sqrt(q), with 0<|s|<1. Gamma_n is 1 minus the squared largest singular value on traceless bath operators, not the ordinary eigenvalue gap.

1. Exact causal triangular block form.
Decompose the last bath site's operator space into identity and traceless Pauli components. Phi_n preserves the subspace with a traceless last site, because tracing both source and the last site after their unitary equals tracing both before it. Thus

 Phi_n = [ Phi_(n-1)   0 ; B_n   C_n ].

The first diagonal block acts on X tensor I. The lower block B_n records the newly emitted last-site traceless part.

2. Uniform norm bound on the new diagonal block.
Let P_S select source identity and Q_n select a traceless last bath site in operator Hilbert space. The local map P_S Ad_U Q_n has range in source identity and last-site traceless operators. Its three nonzero rows are orthogonal and each has squared norm q=1-s^4. Indeed, Ad_U^dagger(I_S tensor sigma_a) has last-bath-identity component s^2 sigma_a^S tensor I, of squared norm s^4. Full conjugation is an isometry, so the remainder has squared norm 1-s^4. Orthogonality follows directly from the Pauli formulas. The preceding prefix map is also an isometry. Therefore

 ||C_n||_(2->2) <= sqrt(1-s^4)=r

uniformly in n. The identity-padding loss relation yields the exact operator equality

 B_n^dagger B_n = s^4 [I-Phi_(n-1)^dagger Phi_(n-1)].

3. Exponential resolvent estimate.
Order the traceless operator space by the position of its last nonidentity factor. The above decomposition makes Phi_n block triangular with n diagonal blocks C_j, all of norm at most r. Hence every nontrivial ordinary eigenvalue satisfies |lambda|<=r, uniformly in n. Write T=Phi_n restricted to traceless operators as D+L, with D block diagonal and L strictly block triangular. Then ||D||<=r, ||L||<=1+r, and [(I-D)^(-1)L]^n=0. Setting kappa=(1+r)/(1-r) gives

 ||(I-T)^(-1)|| <= [kappa^n-1]/(2r).

Equivalently, ||X-Phi_n(X)||_2 >= [2r/(kappa^n-1)] ||X||_2 for traceless X. This has exponential dependence on n at fixed theta.

4. Precise obstruction to using this for gamma_n.
The required quantity is ||X||^2-||Phi_n(X)||^2. The resolvent controls ||X-Phi_n(X)||, which can be large even when the former is zero. A nilpotent shift has ordinary spectral radius zero and nonzero fixed-point defect on every nonzero vector, yet singular value one. The causal block norm and B_n identity alone also do not remove this issue: for scalar A=a, B=s^2 sqrt(1-a^2), and C=r, the matrix [[a,0],[B,r]] is a contraction with singular value one, although a<r<1 and B^*B=s^4(1-a^2). Thus an induction using only these structural inequalities cannot prove even strict singular contraction. Additional information about the actual alignment of the range of B_n with C_n is needed.

5. Audit of the crossing/interpolation loss.
The crossing route propagates an intertwiner defect to z=u-2ki, k=0,...,n, where u=cot(theta). The direct norm bound grows as k^(2n). Interpolation to z=0 costs exp[O(n log n)]. Although the true defect polynomial has degree n, replacing k^(2n) by k^n alone would still leave n^n growth when n+1 interpolation nodes have radius O(n). This degree observation is not an exp[O(n)] improvement. The sharpened cyclic-shift Poincare constant alpha_n=4sin^2(pi/[2(n+1)]) changes only polynomial factors. The proved asymptotic remains gamma_n>=exp[-4n log n+O_theta(n)], and hence a general Omega_theta(N/log N) memory obstruction at the acquisition benchmark, not Omega_theta(N).

6. Exact identification with an existing integrable transfer matrix.
P. W. Claeys, J. Herzog-Arbeitman, and A. Lamacraft, "Correlations and commuting transfer matrices in integrable unitary circuits", SciPost Physics 12, 007 (2022), arXiv:2106.00640, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.00640.

Their folded transfer matrix, normalized to fix the identity operator, is this Phi_n after ket/bra index ordering, with lambda=cot(theta). The explicit component trace in their Eq.24 is divided by the auxiliary dimension two for this channel normalization. Their Eq.24 uses a locally rotated check-R tensor. Explicitly,

 checkR(-lambda)_(a i,j b)
  = [delta_(a j)delta_(i b)-i lambda delta_(a b)delta_(i j)]/(1-i lambda)
  = [(lambda I+i P)/(lambda+i)]_(a i,b j).

The other half is its conjugate; the closed auxiliary line is exactly the maximally mixed source trace. They prove commuting transfer matrices at different lambda (Eq.25), and study homogeneous Jordan blocks and spin-one Bethe equations. Numerical matrix-level commutators for n=1,...,4 are below 2e-15. For n=2 their Eqs.76-79 give eigenvalues 1, c^2, 1-s^4, and c^2(c^2-s^2), with three size-3 Jordan blocks at c^2. Dense matrices agree; n=3 also matches Table 1. This source is closer to the deterministic bath channel than random angle-twirled eSWAP-chain results, but does not give the needed singular spectrum or conditioning bounds.

At lambda=0 (theta=pi/2), Phi_n is the bath shift, nilpotent on traceless operators. Thus every Phi_n(theta) commutes with that shift. Commutation with a nilpotent contraction still does not imply the needed one-step singular-gap estimate.

CONCLUSION. The crossing theorem and the sharp all-instrument profile audit are sound. No exp[-O_theta(n)] lower bound for gamma_n was established. The exponential resolvent estimate above, the ordinary eigenvalue bound, and the integrable-transfer identification are auxiliary results only; the unresolved logarithmic and joint-angle gaps remain explicit.
