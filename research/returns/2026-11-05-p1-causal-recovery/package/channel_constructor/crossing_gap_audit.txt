INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE ALL-PREFIX CROSSING GAP
5 November 2026. Channel constructor, for root's final synthesis.

VERDICT
The crossing/interpolation argument is valid. I find no missing factor of two,
dimension factor, or partial-transpose norm factor. With the normalized
Hilbert-Schmidt conventions stated below, it proves the advertised explicit
positive gap at every finite prefix, and the fixed-theta general memory lower
bound with asymptotic coefficient 1/4. The optional stronger Dirichlet-cycle
estimate in operational_constructor/crossing_gap_proof.md is also valid.

The result is a general growing-memory obstruction, but not the requested
matching linear-memory obstruction: Omega_theta(N/log N) still permits o(N).
The explicit finite bound is valid jointly in n and theta; its asymptotic
coefficient 1/4 is claimed only with theta fixed.

1. NORMALIZATIONS AND THE PHYSICAL CHANNEL

Let n>=1, 0<theta<pi/2, u=cot(theta)>0, and let site 0 be the source. Write

  L_j(z)=z I+i P_(0j),
  M(z)=L_n(z)...L_1(z),
  W=M(u)/(u^2+1)^(n/2),
  T(X)=(1/2)Tr_0[W(I_0 tensor X)W^dagger].

For this theta range, W is exactly the physical chronological partial-SWAP
circuit, with no additional normalization factor. Write Xbar=I_0 tensor X.
All norms ||.||_2 below use normalized trace on the indicated full matrix
algebra, so ||Xbar||_2=||X||_(2,bath). Operator norms ||.||_infinity have their
ordinary meaning. Multiplication inequalities involving these two norms retain
their usual constants under normalized trace.

For traceless bath X with ||X||_2=1 put Y=T(X). The source-trace map is the
orthogonal projection P_0(Z)=I_0 tensor Tr_0(Z)/2 in this Hilbert space. Thus

  delta^2=||W Xbar W^dagger-Ybar||_2^2
         =1-||T(X)||_2^2.

Equivalently delta=||W Xbar-Ybar W||_2. There is no sqrt(2) factor here.

2. THE PARTIAL-TRANSPOSE IDENTITY AND ITS EXACT SCOPE

Define C(A)=sigma_y^(0) A^(t_0) sigma_y^(0). This is a complex-linear
Hilbert-Schmidt isometry, and C^2=I. The identity
sigma_y sigma_a^T sigma_y=-sigma_a for all three Paulis gives

  C(P_(0j))=I-P_(0j),
  C(L_j(z-i))=z I-i P_(0j).

CAUTION: C is not an anti-automorphism on arbitrary bath-valued matrix
products. The order reversal needed here is nevertheless valid: the bath
coefficients of L_j and L_k commute whenever j!=k. Transposing their source
matrix indices therefore reverses the order of these distinct local factors.
It follows exactly that

  C(M(z-i))=(z I-iP_(01))...(z I-iP_(0n))
           =(z^2+1)^n M(z)^(-1).

This is used only where the inverse exists. At every point z=u-ki considered
below, u>0 ensures z+i and z-i are nonzero, so every local factor and M(z) are
invertible.

Bath multiplication has a DIFFERENT, also exact, property:

  C(Ybar A)=Ybar C(A),     C(A Xbar)=C(A)Xbar.

It follows directly from source-index transposition and does not require Y or
X to commute with the bath coefficients of A. This distinction resolves the
main potential partial-transpose pitfall.

3. ALTERNATING INTERTWINERS AND NORM PROPAGATION

Use D(z;X,Y)=M(z)Xbar-Ybar M(z), the negative of root's E convention. The
crossing identity and C^2=I give

  D(z-i;Y,X)
    =-(z^2+1)^n C[M(z)^(-1)D(z;X,Y)M(z)^(-1)].       (1)

The sign is immaterial for the norm, but the X,Y alternation is essential.
At z=u, ||D(u;X,Y)||_2=(u^2+1)^(n/2) delta.

At z=u-ki, k>=0, each local factor has singular values |z+i| and |z-i|, so

  ||M(z)^(-1)||_infinity
     <= [u^2+(k-1)^2]^(-n/2),
  |z^2+1|^n
     =[(u^2+(k-1)^2)(u^2+(k+1)^2)]^(n/2).

C is used only in Hilbert-Schmidt norm, where it is an isometry. No assertion
that partial transpose preserves operator norm is needed. Applying
||A B C||_2<=||A||_infinity ||B||_2 ||C||_infinity to (1) yields precisely

  [(u^2+(k+1)^2)/(u^2+(k-1)^2)]^(n/2)

as the stepwise upper multiplier. The products telescope. If D_r denotes D
with the arguments in their parity-dependent order, then for every r>=1,

  ||D_r(u-ri)||_2
    <= [((u^2+(r-1)^2)(u^2+r^2))/u^2]^(n/2) delta. (2)

The same formula reproduces the initial norm if r=0 is interpreted literally.
For r=2k, k=0,...,n, the arguments have returned to X,Y. With

  B_n=(u^2+4n^2)/u,

equation (2) gives ||D(u-2ki;X,Y)||_2<=B_n^n delta. The initial k=0 point also
satisfies this because B_n>=sqrt(u^2+1) for n>=1.

4. OPERATOR-VALUED LAGRANGE INTERPOLATION

D(z;X,Y) is a matrix-valued polynomial of degree at most n. Its leading
coefficient is Xbar-Ybar. At nodes z_k=u-2ki, k=0,...,n,

  |product_(j!=k)(z_k-z_j)|=2^n k!(n-k)!,
  sum_(k=0)^n 1/[2^n k!(n-k)!]=1/n!.

Coordinatewise Lagrange interpolation followed by the Hilbert-Schmidt triangle
inequality therefore gives

  ||X-Y||_2<=B_n^n delta/n!.

For evaluation at z=0, each numerator factor obeys
|z_j|=sqrt(u^2+4j^2)<=u+2n. There are n numerator factors at each node. Thus

  ||D(0;X,Y)||_2<=B_n^n (u+2n)^n delta/n!.

Both bounds are independent of the bath Hilbert-space dimension. The n+1 even
nodes, rather than n+1 consecutive single-shift nodes, are necessary because
the arguments alternate under crossing.

5. CYCLIC PERMUTATION AND VARIANCE

At z=0,

  M(0)=i^n C_n,     C_n=P_(0n)...P_(01),

and C_n is the (n+1)-cycle sending site 0 to 1, 1 to 2, ..., n to 0. Combining
the two interpolation bounds gives

  ||[C_n,Xbar]||_2<=K_n delta,
  K_n=B_n^n[(u+2n)^n+1]/n!.                            (3)

Let E_j be the orthogonal conditional expectation replacing site j by identity.
E_0 fixes Xbar. Moreover E_j fixes Ad_(C_n^j)(Xbar), and the latter is within
j||[C_n,Xbar]||_2 of Xbar by a telescoping sum of unitary conjugations. Hence

  ||(I-E_j)Xbar||_2<=j||[C_n,Xbar]||_2.

The E_j commute. Their product is the full trace projection. For commuting
orthogonal projections, I-product_j E_j<=sum_j(I-E_j). Thus

  ||Xbar-tr(X)I||_2^2
   <=sum_(j=0)^n ||(I-E_j)Xbar||_2^2
   <=[n(n+1)(2n+1)/6] ||[C_n,Xbar]||_2^2.

There is no extra factor two from the projections, and the E_0 term is zero.
For the traceless unit-norm X used above, the left side is exactly one.

It follows that

  gamma_n:=min_(tr X=0, ||X||_2=1) [1-||T(X)||_2^2]
      >=g_n:=6/[n(n+1)(2n+1)K_n^2] >0.                (4)

Consequently identity is the only singular-value-one direction of T. The
operators I-T^*T and H_n=I-TT^* have the same singular-value defects and both
have a one-dimensional kernel, so this is the gap needed by the earlier
general prefix-witness theorem.

6. OPTIONAL SHARPER CYCLE CONSTANT ALSO PASSES

The operational proof's optional estimate

  ||[C_n,Xbar]||_2^2
    >=alpha_n ||X-tr(X)I||_2^2,
  alpha_n=4 sin^2[pi/(2(n+1))],

is valid. A normalized Pauli-string basis makes Ad_(C_n) a permutation of
orthonormal basis vectors. Partition into cyclic orbits. Each nonidentity orbit
contains at least one string with a nonidentity source Pauli, whose coefficient
in Xbar must be zero. The remaining coefficients therefore form disjoint
Dirichlet paths, each with at most n allowed vertices. On a path of r vertices,
the energy sum |a_(j+1)-a_j|^2 with zero boundary values has minimum eigenvalue
2-2cos[pi/(r+1)], which is at least alpha_n for r<=n. Shorter-period orbits cause
no problem; their allowed paths are shorter. The identity orbit is removed by
the trace condition.

Thus the stronger finite bound gamma_n>=alpha_n/K_n^2 also holds. It improves
only a polynomial prefactor, not the leading 4n log n exponent. The simpler
bound (4) is already sufficient for the advertised memory consequence.

7. FIXED-THETA ASYMPTOTICS: WHAT IS AND IS NOT EQUAL

At fixed u>0, Stirling's formula gives

  log K_n=2n log n+n log(8e/u)
          -(1/2)log(2pi n)+u/2+O_u(1/n).

In particular log K_n=2n log n+O_theta(n), and the EXPLICIT LOWER BOUND in (4)
satisfies

  log g_n=-4n log n+O_theta(n).

The correct conclusion is gamma_n>=g_n=exp[-4n log n+O_theta(n)]. No matching
upper bound on gamma_n, nor equality of gamma_n with that asymptotic scale,
has been established here. The constants implicit in this asymptotic statement
are not uniform as theta tends to zero. The finite formulas (3)-(4) remain
explicit and valid in the joint parameters.

8. CONVERSION TO GENERAL MEMORY QUBITS

Use the independently audited all-instrument prefix theorem. For a prefix of
n carriers, a receiver with retained quantum dimension at most m has a Choi
witness Z with Tr Z=1, Z<=(m/2^n)I, and

  Tr(H_n Z)<=eta(epsilon)=2epsilon-(4/3)epsilon^2<=2epsilon

when the final half-diamond error is <=epsilon<=3/4. Since ker H_n has rank one,

  Tr(H_n Z)>=gamma_n(1-m/2^n).

Let q_peak denote the maximum retained number of qubits, including every
persistent quantum ancilla. If q_peak<N, take n=q_peak+1<=N. Then m<=2^q_peak
and m/2^n<=1/2, so

  gamma_n/2<=eta(epsilon)<=2epsilon,
  epsilon>=gamma_n/4>=g_n/4.                            (5)

This applies to arbitrary instruments, classical records, initialized
workspace, feedback and block processing. The source grant and refined labels
enter only as converse relaxations. For non-power-of-two memory dimensions,
rounding log_2 m upward changes the prefix by at most one carrier.

With L=log(1/epsilon) and theta fixed, (5) gives

  L<=4(q_peak+1)log(q_peak+1)+O_theta(q_peak+log q_peak).

Along any successful small-error sequence at the stated acquisition benchmark,
either q_peak>=N already gives a stronger linear bound, or this inequality
implies

  liminf_(epsilon->0)
    q_peak loglog(1/epsilon)/log(1/epsilon) >=1/4.

An equivalent direct prefix choice is
n=floor[(1-nu)L/(4log L)] for any fixed nu>0. The explicit gap then satisfies
eta(epsilon)/g_n->0, and the cap bound forces log_2 m>=n-o(1). Letting nu tend
to zero gives the same coefficient. This also makes clear that qubits, not
Hilbert-space dimension, appear in the conclusion.

At N=Theta(theta^(-4)log(1/epsilon)) with theta fixed, N=Theta_theta(L), so the
result is q_peak=Omega_theta(N/log N). It is a necessary memory condition and
does not establish a matching processing-time obstruction.

9. OPTIONAL PROBABILITY STRENGTHENING

The earlier branch-tail witness gives a useful strengthening when memory
allocation depends on the classical record. At any prefix n whose explicit
gap obeys eta(epsilon)/g_n->0, for every fixed integer k>=1,

  Pr[q_at_prefix<=n-k] <=2^(-k)+eta(epsilon)/g_n.

These probabilities are under the Bell/maximally mixed logical input, as in
the earlier branch-tail theorem. Thus the lower bound need not be witnessed
only on an arbitrarily rare high-memory branch. This is optional; it is not
needed for the peak-memory statement and does not close the o(N) endpoint.
