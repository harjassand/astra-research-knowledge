SHARP GENERAL PREFIX PROFILE, FINITE GAP CERTIFICATES, AND ERASURE AUDIT
5 November 2026. Independent channel-constructor audit for the root synthesis.

STATUS AND ATTRIBUTION
The general low-Schmidt Choi-witness/profile mechanism is prior machinery in
weak_stream_memory.pdf. The all-instrument rank lemma was derived by the algebra
constructor. This note verifies the exact logical-channel Petz identification,
replaces the older energy budget 3 epsilon(1-epsilon) by the sharper budget
eta(epsilon)=2 epsilon-(4/3)epsilon^2, and supplies exact finite-prefix gap
certificates. The sharper recovery inequality itself is established prior work:
Joseph M. Renes, "Better bounds on optimal measurement and entanglement recovery,
with applications to uncertainty and monogamy relations," Phys. Rev. A 96,
042328 (2017), arXiv:1707.01114, Eq. (13),
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1707.01114 . A short proof is included below.
This note does not establish the required growing-prefix spectral estimates and
does not resolve the general sublinear-memory/near-linear-processing endpoint.

1. CHANNEL, PREFIX, AND MEMORY CONVENTIONS

The logical input L and physical source S are qubits. At a prefix ell>=1, the
emitted carrier space A has dimension d=2^ell. Let U=U_ell ... U_1 be the actual
collision circuit, and let tau_A=I_A/d. Its encoding channel is

  J(rho)=U(rho tensor tau_A)U^dagger.

Every original chronological receiver induces, at the cut, an instrument on A
whose outputs are its retained quantum memory and its classical record. All
persistent quantum workspace, including initialized ancillas retained across
the cut, is included in the memory dimension. Operations on earlier carriers
commute with later source collisions, so the chronological receiver can be
represented by this prefix instrument acting after U. This does not assert that
every abstract instrument has an efficient causal implementation; enlarging the
class is legitimate for the converse.

Refine each instrument branch to individual Kraus operators K_x:A->Q_x and
reveal the refined label. Revealing it can only help recovery. If every retained
quantum space has dimension <=m, then

  A_x=(K_x^dagger K_x)^(1/2)>=0,
  rank(A_x)<=m,                 sum_x A_x^2=I_A.

Arbitrary initially mixed workspace and all discarded environments are already
included in the Kraus decomposition. Continuous records are handled by the
corresponding operator-valued integrals; no outcome-count bound is used.

Grant the receiver the actual source S immediately after the prefix. From the
joint state of S and its retained memory it can create fresh tracial carriers,
simulate every remaining source collision and receiver operation, and reproduce
the original final channel. Thus this grant and the Kraus refinement can only
increase achievable recovery fidelity. The resulting qubit-input channel is

  N(rho)=direct_sum_x (I_S tensor K_x)J(rho)(I_S tensor K_x^dagger).

Define the bistochastic bath channel and its Hilbert-Schmidt adjoint by

  T(X)=(1/2)Tr_S[U(I_S tensor X)U^dagger],
  T^*(X)=(1/2)Tr_S[U^dagger(I_S tensor X)U],
  H=I-T T^* >=0.

The inequality H>=0 follows from Hilbert-Schmidt contraction of a bistochastic
channel. It holds in ordinary or normalized Hilbert-Schmidt inner products,
since the input and output bath dimensions are equal.

2. EXACT LOGICAL PETZ CHANNEL: NO GAUGE-NORMALIZATION GAP

Write K_x=V_x A_x for the polar decomposition, with V_x a partial isometry.
The maximally mixed logical input produces the branch average state

  sigma_x=N(I_L/2)_x=I_S tensor (K_x K_x^dagger)/(2d).

This factorization is exact: U preserves the completely mixed joint input.
Using pseudoinverses on the supports, the Petz channel of N relative to I_L/2 is

  P_N(Y)=Tr_A[U^dagger sum_x (I tensor V_x^dagger)Y_x
                                  (I tensor V_x) U].

Indeed N^*(Y)=(1/d)Tr_A[U^dagger sum_x K_x^dagger Y_x K_x U], while
sigma_x^(-1/2)=sqrt(2d) I_S tensor (K_x K_x^dagger)^(-1/2).
The two factors (I_L/2)^(1/2), the gauge factor 1/d, and the two inverse-square-
root factors cancel exactly. Moreover,
K_x^dagger(K_x K_x^dagger)^(-1/2)=V_x^dagger on the output support.
An arbitrary trace-preserving completion outside that support does not change
P_N N, since every N(rho) is supported in N(I_L/2).

Consequently, if L_A(X)=sum_x A_x X A_x is the bistochastic Lueders channel,

  P_N N(rho)=Tr_A[U^dagger (id_S tensor L_A)
                         (U(rho tensor I_A/d)U^dagger) U].

This is the Petz channel for the LOGICAL QUBIT channel with its tracial gauge
initializer, not merely the Petz channel of a different gauge-recovery task.

3. EXACT FIDELITY / CHOI-WITNESS IDENTITY

Let F_P be the squared entanglement fidelity of P_N N at the maximally mixed
logical input. Put W_mu=U(sigma_mu tensor I_A)U^dagger for mu=0,1,2,3, where
sigma_0=I. In normalized Hilbert-Schmidt inner products,

  F_P=(1/4) sum_mu <W_mu,(id tensor L_A)(W_mu)>.

Twirl each Y_x=U^dagger(I_S tensor A_x)U over the four logical Paulis. The
orthogonal projection onto source-identity operators is

  P_S(Y)=(1/4)sum_mu (sigma_mu tensor I)Y(sigma_mu tensor I)
        =I_S tensor Tr_S(Y)/2.

Cyclicity of trace and the orthogonality of this projection give

  F_P=sum_x ||P_S(Y_x)||_(2,tracial)^2
     =sum_x ||T^*(A_x)||_(2,tracial,A)^2.

Use ordinary, unnormalized vectorization |B>> with norm squared Tr B^dagger B.
Define the normalized Choi witness

  Z=(1/d)sum_x |A_x>><<A_x|.

Then Tr Z=(1/d)Tr(sum_x A_x^2)=1 and, EXACTLY,

  F_P=Tr[(T T^*)Z],
  E:=Tr(HZ)=1-F_P.

Both marginals of Z are I_A/d, so Z is the normalized Choi state of the
bistochastic channel L_A (up to the chosen vectorization order). Each vector in
the displayed decomposition has Schmidt rank rank(A_x)<=m. Thus Z also has
Schmidt number at most m.

4. OPERATOR-NORM CAP, INCLUDING ARBITRARY CLASSICAL BRANCHING

For any bath operator B and the support projector P_x of A_x, Hilbert-Schmidt
Cauchy-Schwarz yields

  |Tr(B^dagger A_x)|^2
   =|Tr(P_x B^dagger A_x)|^2
   <=rank(A_x) Tr(A_x^2 B B^dagger).

One may first apply Cauchy-Schwarz to Tr(P_x B^dagger A_x), then drop P_x in the
resulting squared norm. Summing over x gives

  <B|Z|B> <=(m/d)Tr[B B^dagger sum_x A_x^2]
           =(m/d)||B||_HS^2.

Therefore

  0<=Z<=(m/d)I,      Tr Z=1,      Tr_1 Z=Tr_2 Z=I/d.

No bound on the number of classical outcomes or on the discarded environment
dimension enters this proof.

5. SHARP RECOVERY INEQUALITY AND THE IMPROVED ENERGY BUDGET

For completeness, here is a direct proof of the known sharp recovery inequality.
For any k-dimensional-input channel N, let

  rho_RB=(id_R tensor N)(Phi_k),
  tau_B=N(I/k),   sigma_RB=(I_R/k) tensor tau_B,
  C=sigma^(-1/4) rho sigma^(-1/4).

Inverses are restricted to support. Expanding rho in matrix units and using the
Petz formula P_N(Y)=(1/k)N^*(tau^(-1/2)Y tau^(-1/2)) gives

  Tr C^2=k^2 F_P.

For any decoder R, define its Bell-test effect

  M=(id_R tensor R^*)(Phi_k),   F=Tr(M rho),   q=Tr(M sigma)=1/k^2.

Here 0<=M<=I because R^* is unital. Set G_1=sigma^(1/4) M sigma^(1/4) and
G_0=sigma^(1/4)(I-M)sigma^(1/4). Their Hilbert-Schmidt Gram matrix equals

  diag(q,1-q)-h [[1,-1],[-1,1]],     h=Tr(G_1 G_0)>=0,

because G_1+G_0=sigma^(1/2) and the row sums of the Gram matrix are q,1-q.
Thus this Gram matrix is bounded above by diag(q,1-q).
Choose a=F/q and b=(1-F)/(1-q). The overlap of C with aG_1+bG_0 equals
Q=F^2/q+(1-F)^2/(1-q), while ||aG_1+bG_0||_HS^2<=Q. Cauchy-Schwarz implies
Q^2<=Tr(C^2)Q, hence

  F_P >= F^2+(1-F)^2/(k^2-1).

For k=2 this is Renes's Eq. (13), with F denoting squared entanglement fidelity.
No assumption about an unobserved gauge or the output dimension is needed.

If the original complete receiver has half-diamond error <=epsilon, the Bell
test gives F>=1-epsilon for the simulated decoder after the source grant.
The function F^2+(1-F)^2/3 is increasing for F>=1/4. Therefore, for
0<=epsilon<=3/4,

  E=Tr(HZ)=1-F_P <= eta(epsilon):=2epsilon-(4/3)epsilon^2.       (SHARP BUDGET)

This improves 2epsilon-epsilon^2 throughout this range. It improves the older
profile budget 3epsilon(1-epsilon) for 0<epsilon<3/5, in particular throughout
the requested epsilon<=1/2 regime. The leading small-error change is 3epsilon
to 2epsilon. For other ranges where the old theorem applies one may use the
minimum of the two valid budgets.

6. GENERAL SPECTRAL-PROFILE AND HEAT-TRACE CONSEQUENCES

Let 0<=h_1<=...<=h_(d^2)<=1 be the eigenvalues of H, including multiplicities.
For 1<=m<=d put kappa=m/d, r=floor(1/kappa), and alpha=1-r kappa.
The exact capped-density minimum is

  Phi_H(kappa)=kappa sum_(i=1)^r h_i + alpha h_(r+1).

This follows by dephasing Z in an eigenbasis of H and allocating its unit trace
to the lowest eigenvalues at the cap kappa. Every receiver under consideration
must satisfy

  Phi_H(m/d)<=eta(epsilon).

This relaxes the additional bistochastic and low-Schmidt constraints, so it is
a necessary condition, not a characterization of implementable receivers.

For a>0 define n_H(a)=rank 1_[0,a)(H). Since
H>=a(I-1_[0,a)(H)), one obtains, for a>eta(epsilon),

  m >= d [1-eta(epsilon)/a]/n_H(a).

Let Z_H(beta)=Tr exp(-beta H). Jensen's inequality in the spectral measure of H
under Z and the cap give, for every beta>=0,

  exp[-beta eta(epsilon)] <= Tr[exp(-beta H)Z]
                          <= (m/d) Z_H(beta).

Thus, writing q=log_2 m for quantum-memory bits,

  q >= ell-log_2 Z_H(beta)-beta eta(epsilon)/ln 2.

If ker H is one-dimensional, spanned by |I>>/sqrt(d), and h_2=gamma>0, the
spectral-gap consequence is

  m/d >= 1-eta(epsilon)/gamma.

Whenever the right side is positive this is a lower bound on dimension; the
corresponding qubit lower bound is its base-2 logarithm plus ell. Dimension and
number of qubits must not be interchanged.

7. VARIABLE-MEMORY BRANCH TAILS

Suppose branch x retains dimension m_x. Its probability for the maximally mixed
logical input (equivalently for the Bell-input experiment) is

  p_x=Tr(A_x^2)/d.

For any collection G of branches with m_x<=m_0, put
Z_G=(1/d)sum_(x in G)|A_x>><<A_x| and p_G=sum_(x in G)p_x.
Exactly the same cap proof, using sum_G A_x^2<=I, gives

  Tr Z_G=p_G,    0<=Z_G<=(m_0/d)I,    Tr(H Z_G)<=eta(epsilon).

Consequently the exact fractional profile with trace p_G obeys

  (m_0/d)sum_(i=1)^r h_i + [p_G-r(m_0/d)]h_(r+1) <= eta(epsilon),
  r=floor[p_G/(m_0/d)].

In particular, for every a>0,

  Pr[m_x<=m_0] <= (m_0/d)n_H(a)+eta(epsilon)/a.

For qubit thresholds q_0 this reads

  Pr[q_x>q_0] >= 1-2^(q_0-ell)n_H(a)-eta(epsilon)/a,

with a negative right side replaced by the trivial zero bound. These are branch
probabilities under the Bell/maximally mixed input, not a claim uniform over all
unknown pure input states. Refinement does not change the probabilities of a
coarse event specified by the original receiver's allocated memory.

The corresponding heat inequalities, for p_G>0, are

  p_G exp[-beta eta(epsilon)/p_G] <= (m_0/d)Z_H(beta),
  p_G <= (m_0/d)Z_H(beta)+beta eta(epsilon).

The first uses normalized Jensen on Z_G/p_G; the second follows from
exp(-beta H)>=I-beta H. Either can be optimized over beta>=0.

8. EXACT TWO-CARRIER GAP: ANALYTIC RANGE, NOT A NUMERICAL CLAIM

Let c=cos(theta), s=sin(theta), t=tan(theta), with 0<theta<pi/2. The two-carrier
bath operator space decomposes under collective SU(2) rotations into two scalar
copies, three vector copies, and one rank-two tensor copy (dimensions 2+9+5=16).

On I the map T_2 acts as 1. On the normalized traceless scalar
(sigma_1 dot sigma_2)/sqrt(3), it acts as

  lambda_0=1-s^4=(1+2t^2)/(1+t^2)^2.

On the five-dimensional rank-two tensor sector it acts as the scalar
lambda_2=c^2(c^2-s^2), so |lambda_2|<=c^2<=lambda_0.

For each Cartesian index a, take the orthonormal vector-sector basis
sigma_a tensor I, I tensor sigma_a, and
(sum_bc epsilon_abc sigma_b tensor sigma_c)/sqrt(2).
Direct Pauli multiplication gives the same multiplicity matrix for each a:

  M=(1+t^2)^(-2) [[1+t^2, 0, 0],
                  [t^4, 1+t^2, -sqrt(2)t^3],
                  [-sqrt(2)t^3, 0, 1+t^2]].

Let M_num=(1+t^2)^2 M and
Q=(1+2t^2)^2 I-M_num^T M_num. Set z=t^2. Its three leading principal minors are

  Delta_1=t^2(2+3z-2z^2-z^3),
  Delta_2=2t^4(2z+1)(2+2z-2z^2-z^3),
  Delta_3=4t^6(1-3z^2)(z^3+7z^2+7z+2).

For 0<z<=1/sqrt(3), the first two are strictly positive. For example,
2+3z-2z^2-z^3=2+z(1-z)(z+3)>0, and, for z<=1,
2+2z-2z^2-z^3>=2-z>0. Delta_3 is positive in the interior and zero at the upper
endpoint. Sylvester's criterion and continuity therefore give Q>=0 throughout
this interval. Thus ||M||<=lambda_0. The scalar mode attains lambda_0, proving

  gamma_2=1-||T_2|_(I^perp)||^2=2s^4-s^8
  whenever tan(theta)^4<=1/3.                              (EXACT)

This contains the originally stated sufficient range s<=sqrt(2)-1. It also
identifies the maximal interval for this formula in 0<theta<pi/2: beyond
tan(theta)^4=1/3, Delta_3<0, so ||M||>lambda_0.

For a one-qubit persistent memory, take m=2,d=4 in the sharp gap bound. Every
receiver, including arbitrary instruments and unlimited classical records,
satisfies the finite error floor

  epsilon >= (3/4)[1-sqrt(1-(2/3)gamma_2)]
          =theta^4/2+O(theta^6).

9. THREE-CARRIER GAP: EXACT LEADING COEFFICIENT CERTIFICATE

Let P inject source-identity operators and Q_S project onto source-traceless
operators. On normalized Pauli coordinates put

  R(t)=Q_S Ad_(U_3 U_2 U_1) P=t A+t^2 B+O(t^3),
  A=Q_S(sum_j L_j)P,
  B=Q_S(sum_j S_j+sum_(j>k)L_j L_k)P,
  L_j=i[SWAP_(S,j),.],      S_j=Ad_SWAP_(S,j).

This follows from Ad_Uj=(I+tL_j+t^2 S_j)/(1+t^2). The constant numerator term
has zero Q_S projection, so the denominator does not affect A or B.
The matrix R^*R=I-T_3^*T_3 has the same eigenvalues as H_3=I-T_3T_3^*.

A^*A is the total-spin adjoint Casimir. Its eigenvalues are 0,2,6,12, and its
kernel has dimension five. Besides I, use the traceless scalar basis

  K_1=sigma_1 dot sigma_2,
  K_2=sigma_1 dot sigma_3,
  K_3=sigma_2 dot sigma_3,
  K_4=sum_abc epsilon_abc sigma_(1,a)sigma_(2,b)sigma_(3,c).

The normalized Hilbert-Schmidt Gram matrix is G=diag(3,3,3,6). After allowing the
first-order correction outside ker A, the second-order leakage metric is

  S=K^* B^* [I-A(A^*A)^+A^*] B K
   =[[6,3,-3,0], [3,18,3,0], [-3,3,6,0], [0,0,0,12]].

This is an exact integer/rational coefficient calculation, not a fit. A useful
certificate for reproducing it is

  (A^*A)^+=p(A^*A),
  p(x)=x^3/192-7x^2/72+61x/144,

which takes values 0,1/2,1/6,1/12 at 0,2,6,12 respectively. Exact SymPy code is
channel_constructor/leading_gap.py.

The eigenvalues of G^(-1)S are (7-sqrt(33))/2, 2, 3, (7+sqrt(33))/2.
The other 59 nonidentity modes have positive leading coefficients at order
theta^2. Finite-dimensional analytic perturbation, or the equivalent Schur
complement, consequently gives

  gamma_3=((7-sqrt(33))/2)theta^4+O(theta^6).

The absence of an odd theta^5 term follows because theta->-theta complex
conjugates the channel, while the minimum small-theta branch has a unique
leading coefficient and hence maps to itself. In particular, ker H_3 is exactly
one-dimensional for all sufficiently small nonzero theta.

For a two-qubit persistent memory m=4,d=8, the improved finite-gap floor is

  epsilon >= (3/4)[1-sqrt(1-(2/3)gamma_3)].

The lower-bound function has expansion
((7-sqrt(33))/8)theta^4+O(theta^6). This excludes any proposed fixed two-qubit
deterministic receiver whose error is asserted to vanish as N increases at a
fixed weak theta, including a rotated-tensor/Pauli-frame realization of the
global reverse decoder. It does not exclude memory that grows with N.

10. INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF ROOT'S RETAINED-CARRIER THEOREM

Audit result: the root derivation in root_analysis/erasure_theorem.txt is valid
under its stated restriction that carrier erasure occurs before receiver
processing, with the erasure pattern independent of the input and flagged.
There is no identified substantive correction.

Here are the checked points and normalizations. Propagate a source Pauli in
normalized Hilbert-Schmidt norm and split it into source-identity signal a and
source-traceless squared norm q. For a retained fresh carrier, old source-
identity terms remain unchanged and the new source-identity contribution is
s^2 times each source-Pauli coefficient on the fresh carrier. It is orthogonal
to the old contribution because that carrier Pauli is traceless. The new
contribution has squared norm s^4 q, and unitarity leaves (1-s^4)q in the
source-traceless part. For an erased fresh carrier, partial trace induces
visibility c^2 on the source, so q is multiplied by c^4 and a is unchanged.
Thus the stated recurrence is exact, including operator coefficients on
previous retained carriers.

If d_A is the retained-output dimension and C_A the retained-output channel,
put B_k=(d_A/2)C_A(sigma_k). Then tau(B_k^2)=a_N, so

  ||C_A(sigma_k)||_1=(2/d_A)||B_k||_1<=2sqrt(a_N).

Trace-norm contraction by an arbitrary decoder R gives
F_e(R C_A)<=(1+3sqrt(a_N))/4 and hence the stated half-diamond lower bound
(3/4)(1-sqrt(a_N)). The Petz channel is (d_A/2)C_A^*, since the input and output
averages are tracial. Its physical reverse schedule has the correct factors:
inverse partial SWAP for retained positions, and the self-adjoint source
depolarizing channel with visibility c^2 at erased positions. Polarization of
the Pauli Gram gives the exact recovered visibility a_N and error 3(1-a_N)/4.

For flagged iid retention p, independence gives
E q_j=[p(1-s^4)+(1-p)c^4]^j and the stated geometric series for E a_N.
The flagged branchwise Petz channel has that average visibility. Averaging the
branchwise decoder converse and using concavity of sqrt gives the stated
unconditional converse. The same converse also follows by applying the sharp
recovery inequality to F_P=(1+3 E a_N)/4.

The adjacent exchange (erase,retain)->(retain,erase) adds s^4(1-c^4)q to a and
does not change subsequent q. Hence placing all retained carriers first is
optimal at any fixed intact-carrier budget. The logarithmic lower and upper
budget bounds follow with the stated factors; an integer budget takes the
corresponding ceiling. Randomization independent of the input does not improve
the worst-budget conclusion. None of this extends an erasure operation to an
arbitrary prior coherent compression or state-dependent instrument.

11. WHAT IS STILL MISSING FOR THE FIXED ENDPOINT

The witness now has a rigorously sharper energy constraint, and the finite gap
calculations rule out fixed one- and two-qubit memories without architectural
restrictions. They do not bound the relevant low-energy spectral profile of
H_ell for ell growing with N=Theta(theta^(-4)log(1/epsilon)). No general
sublinear-memory impossibility or sublinear-memory near-linear-time recovery
construction is claimed. Initializations, resetting, protection, routing and
control costs of any proposed construction still require their own physical
accounting; the converse cannot supply those missing operations.
