1. The specific mechanism developed

The candidate uses four stages of directly driven opposed liquid pistons, with the power machinery on shore. A new pressure pipeline connects them to individually isolatable gas cells at approximately 1,200 m depth.

The proposed operation combines three specific features:

Mechanical pressure matching: changing gas pressure acts directly on a balanced water displacer and reversible electric drive.
Pressure-dependent heat-transfer geometry: chamber volume, plate gap and plate thickness change together between stages.
Independent barrier renewal: the gas liner can, in principle, be exchanged while the load-bearing sleeve, ballast and main pressure connection remain installed.

These features must work simultaneously. Their names alone do not establish novelty or performance.

Measurements and acceptance

The complete-duty attempt is 3 kW net for 100 hours after a 144-hour isolated hold, charging at no more than 6 kW for 100 hours. Achieving 65% would require no more than 461.54 kWh total import for 300 kWh export.

The experiment meters:

Common-boundary AC import/export and every auxiliary.
Shaft torque, speed, pressure, displacement and valve timing.
Gas mass, composition, humidity, condensate and dissolved gas.
Water stocks and distributed thermal states.
Battery, actuator, DC-link and receiver inventories.
Bag volume and buoyant reaction, as well as pressure and temperature.

It must restore all declared inventories and repeat after a real liner exchange and idle/cold restart. Pressure alone cannot establish retention in a pressure-compensated bag.

A million mechanical cycles takes about 23 continuous days at 0.5 Hz. It is only approximately 0.6% of a 165-million-cycle lifetime duty. What this work establishes

The direct mechanical load path is a credible way to avoid the specified throttled-actuator error. The pressure-dependent plate geometry produces a testable thermal/mechanical hypothesis. The separate liner supplies a definite maintenance operation to attempt.

The complete construction developed around those mechanisms remains expensive, and several physical boundaries are still qualified only by assumptions. A successful liner test or a few percentage points of converter improvement would not remove the approximately $10.9 million central capital gap.

The deliverable is therefore a reproducible engineering candidate and a decisive experiment, accompanied by an explicit failure to establish the requested economical installation. It does not establish an all-design obstruction, and it should not be represented as the completed invention.
