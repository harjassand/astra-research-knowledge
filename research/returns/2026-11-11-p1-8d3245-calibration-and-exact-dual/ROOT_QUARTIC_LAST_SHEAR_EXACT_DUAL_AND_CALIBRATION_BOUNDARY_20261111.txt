1. THE PAID COERCIVITY IS STRONGER THAN A BARE QUARTIC BOUND

The accepted slice identity gives <v>>=1 and <v^2>>=1. Cauchy--Schwarz gives

2. THE EXACT CONSTRAINED MINIMUM HAS A ONE-SCALAR DUAL

For each real lambda there is a unique real root p_lambda(x) of
 4a(x)p^3+3b(x)p^2+2c(x)p+B(x)+lambda=0.                  (2)
It is measurable. By (1),
 |p_lambda-p_mu|<=3|lambda-mu|/2.
The map lambda -> integral p_lambda is continuous and strictly decreasing.
At lambda=-ess sup B every root is nonnegative, while at lambda=-ess inf B
every root is nonpositive. Hence a unique lambda_* in that closed interval
satisfies integral p_lambda_*=0; the constant-B case has the same conclusion.
Also |p_lambda_*|<=3 osc(B)/2. There is no unbounded last-shear minimizer.
