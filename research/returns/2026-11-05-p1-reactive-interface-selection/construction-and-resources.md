# The proposed operation, decisive transfer and full resource boundary

The [completed selection](README.md) nominates construction of a reusable interface-to-bulk physical clock. The operation below is a **proposed target**, not an implemented or validated constitutive law.

## What must be constructed

A local law would map temperature, electrochemical potentials and a physically specified interface state to fluxes, phase-creation/dissolution rates and the state change caused by the reaction itself:

\[
\mathcal R_{\alpha|\beta}:
(T,\widetilde{\boldsymbol\mu},z_{\alpha|\beta})
\mapsto(\mathbf J,\text{phase rates},\dot z_{\alpha|\beta}).
\]

The important missing object is a sufficiently complete, measurable and reusable `z`, together with composition that remains accurate as interfaces appear, evolve or disappear. Local laws share material inventories and chemical-potential fields. They cannot be independent reaction scores. Elemental conservation, nonnegative amounts and equations such as `n_dot=S r`, `A S=0` are established constraints, not the missing constitutive physics.

The state cannot be defined as the full new specimen's microscopic trajectory. Its acquisition and computation must be practical and amortize across uses. Pairwise interfaces may prove insufficient; junction state, connectivity, supply or longer-range context may be required. Additional state earns value through successful transfer at acceptable cost, not a suggestive name or an unconstrained fit.

The current evidence does not establish existence, identifiability or sufficiency of that state. If measured bulk trajectories do not identify a unique operator, the result should be a justified prediction set or a precise obstruction, rather than invented rates. The [materials audit](package/opportunity_materials/materials_evidence_audit.md) and [mechanics assessment](package/opportunity_dynamics/assessment.md) retain this challenge.

## The selected construction test

The final report proposes starting with the audited correlated-transport system and replacing arbitrary macroscopic scaling with a unitful assembly. It then requires changed processing/geometry and held-out chemistry, with local laws frozen before the new whole-system prediction.

| Transfer | Fixed input | Changed input | Required consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chemical assembly | Independently determined local laws | Precursor combination/family | Composition beyond a fitted recipe |
| Processing | Chemistry and constitutive parameters | Heating, cooling or atmosphere | Predicted intervention in physical time |
| Geometry | Chemistry and local laws | Measured particles/contacts | Preparation treated as a physical input |
| Constructive control | Predictions fixed before synthesis | Model-selected procedure | Delivered useful material at the stated cost |

These are future tests, not reported successes. Strong comparators must receive the same microscopy, inventory, calibration and physical input data. Responses should include full phase trajectories, intermediate identities, appearance/consumption times, unresolved material, delivered quality/quantity and costs. Tolerances come from measurement precision and application requirements before held-out evaluation. A fitted explanation of one route reversal, a random endpoint split or improved diffraction score alone does not earn the full capability.

## Complete resources and useful endpoint

| Stage | Costs the proposed capability must earn |
|---|---|
| Precursors | Production, purity, purchase and embodied preparation |
| Powder preparation | Milling, drying, particle/contact measurements and variability |
| Thermal processing | Furnace losses, actual energy, atmosphere control, holds and cooling |
| Recovery | Separation, waste and delivered mass meeting specifications |
| Scientific inputs | Potentials, atomistic calculation, interface calibration, metrology and uncertainty |
| Reuse | Demonstrated amortization across new recipes and interventions |

The source's nine-composition, five-temperature, five-replicate, 5 ns microscopic protocol corresponds arithmetically to 225 trajectories and 1.125 microseconds of aggregate MD time, plus specialized potential preparation. This is not a GPU-hour, monetary or global energy-saving estimate. The interface library is a substantial input, not a free oracle.

Success would turn a materials specification into a transferable physical preparation procedure, including usable quality and quantity. Neither this release nor its conditional shell calculation demonstrates that result, a new functional material or a quantified global energy benefit. The mission remains distinct from the completed opportunity-selection task.
