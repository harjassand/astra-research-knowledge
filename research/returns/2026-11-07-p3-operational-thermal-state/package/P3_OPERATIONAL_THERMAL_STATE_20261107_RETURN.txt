FINITE-SYSTEM THERMODYNAMIC STATE PREPARATION
Hard macroscopic outcomes, complete thermal states, and the price of access
P3 independent research return — 7 November 2026, Brisbane

RESULT AND SCOPE

This return resolves the following operational question. Given a warranted
finite collective-spin Hamiltonian, can we construct the finite-system
minimum-free-energy state with a specified collective-magnetization outcome
window, rather than merely choose fields or report an equilibrium mean?

The answer is constructive under an explicit active preparation interface.
The complete canonical Gibbs state is the special case with no restriction
on the outcome. The construction preserves noncommuting interactions, every
total-spin sector, all representation multiplicities, and the entire N-spin
density operator. It has polynomial classical and logical-circuit cost at
fixed degree. It requires neither a spectral-gap lower bound nor a thermal
mixing oracle. A classical description of a state is turned into an explicit
randomized family of finite-dyadic Ry/Rz/CNOT circuits, with error bounds.

This is a mathematical and executable logical preparation result. An active
device with the stated gate, reset, randomness and calibration contracts was
not supplied or run. The interface is stronger than the fixed-tag access
used for the accepted Hamiltonian acquisition. A separate theorem shows why
that distinction matters: on a loaded product-bath sample, collective
operations and one fixed tagged spin can conserve a bath-sector probability
that a thermal target needs to change. Heralding and fresh-sample restarts
have a different contract; their success and acquisition costs are bounded
explicitly below.

Two information regimes emerge. Uniform approximation of the complete true
thermal state can require Hamiltonian accuracy proportional to 1/N, even at
a fixed finite temperature. A hard magnetization outcome plus a near-optimal
free-energy density does not require that stronger model accuracy. Every
ideal constructed component lies in the requested window, and coefficient
uncertainty nu costs at most 2 nu in free-energy-density regret. Hardware and
gate calibration still have their own size-dependent costs.

The earned capital is an operational bridge and a separation of its access,
information, fluctuation and thermodynamic costs. Schur representation,
Clebsch-Gordan circuits, generic state synthesis, entropy variational
principles and divergence inequalities are established machinery and are
credited. No historical-priority assertion is made.


1. PRECISE INPUT, OUTPUT AND INTERFACE

The model is the accepted normalized collective family on N labelled qubits:

  H_N = N sum_(|alpha|<=K) c_alpha A_(N,alpha),       ||A_(N,alpha)|| <= 1.

The identity coefficient may be omitted: it changes absolute free energies
but not normalized states or the constrained-versus-unconstrained penalty.
The nonconstant A's are averages of distinct-site Pauli words. K is fixed,
N>=K, the common coefficients and normalization are warranted, and a known
bound C>=sum |c_alpha| gives ||H_N||<=NC. Rational real coefficients are the
delivered input representation. Y terms produce complex Hermitian blocks.
Arbitrary requested precision is part of the mathematical algorithm; the
delivered implementation uses declared finite arithmetic and checks its
error bounds. Known static controls have already been included in H_N.

For an acquired model Hhat_N, the accepted coefficient certificate supplies

  ||H_N-Hhat_N|| <= N nu

on its calibrated confidence event. This premise includes the physical
identification of axes, the common coefficients and size normalization.
Unbounded symmetry-breaking errors are not covered. Bounded departures can
be included in the same operator-norm uncertainty. A justified supplied
model is also a legitimate input; its acquisition and description cost is
not zero merely because it is supplied.

Let beta>0 be finite and supplied, with the same energy units as H. Set

  M_z = (1/N) sum_i Z_i,
  P = 1_[a,b](M_z),                    -1 <= a <= b <= 1.

The rational window must contain at least one eigenvalue of M_z. Otherwise
the requested output subspace is empty and the compiler rejects it. The
logical measurement axis and its physical calibration are part of the
input contract. A reporting measurement with POVM error delta_read adds
that error to a reported outcome guarantee.

The target is

  B = (P H P)|_(ran P),
  Z_P = Tr_(ran P) exp(-beta B),
  omega_P = P exp(-beta B) P / Z_P.                         (1)

All logarithms are natural, and k_B=hbar=1. A physical energy calibration
is required before the model's inverse-energy times become clock times.

The embeddings in (1) mean that omega_P is zero on the forbidden subspace.
For P=I, it is the full canonical Gibbs state rho_beta(H). For noncommuting
H and P, it is generally different from the postselected state

  P exp(-beta H) P / Tr[P exp(-beta H)].

The output contract is the N-spin marginal after the purifier and classical
sector/path record are discarded. Keeping the classical record gives a
labelled ensemble, not a product of that record with the Gibbs state.
Fresh randomness and resets are required for independent copies. Repeating
one fixed component circuit does not produce the thermal mixture.

The positive preparation interface consists of:

* fresh or reset labelled qubits in a known computational state;
* calibrated programmable one-qubit Ry and Rz rotations and CNOTs;
* classical control, fresh random bits, and auxiliary discard/reset;
* a specified connectivity or a paid routing implementation;
* an initialization error and a composable error bound for every executed
  gate, including native drift, timing, leakage, crosstalk and context.

This can instead be compiled mathematically into a fixed universal finite
alphabet using the accepted synthesis machinery. That adds its gates,
ancillas, classical work and errors. The delivered emitter stops at exact
finite-dyadic logical rotation angles. It is not a native pulse compiler or
a fault-tolerant implementation. An always-on native H must be included in
the gate contract or compensated at its actual cost. No unknown physical
inverse is assumed. No passive bath law, fast equilibration law or reusable
energy reference is inferred from a gate list.


2. WHAT THE TARGET OPTIMIZES

Write the total Helmholtz functional as

  Fcal_H(rho) = Tr(rho H) - beta^(-1) S(rho),
  F_P = -beta^(-1) log Z_P.

For every density operator supported in P,

  Fcal_H(rho) - F_P = beta^(-1) D(rho || omega_P).             (2)

Proof. On ran P, log omega_P=-beta B-log Z_P. Also
Tr(rho H)=Tr(rho B). Substitution in relative entropy proves (2).
Relative entropy is nonnegative and vanishes only when the states agree.
Thus omega_P is the unique constrained optimum at finite beta. This is a
direct application of the Gibbs variational principle, not a new principle.

Consequently, the output operation has an unambiguous finite-system
meaning: it prepares the least-free-energy state consistent with the hard
outcome condition. This does not promise a simultaneous sharp value for
noncommuting macroscopic observables. It does not preserve a previously
chosen mean of another observable when the window changes the state.

Trace distance is T(rho,sigma)=||rho-sigma||_1/2. If an output sigma is
epsilon-close to omega_P, measuring M_z returns a forbidden outcome with
probability at most epsilon. A stronger support observation used later is
that model and matrix-normalization errors need not enter this particular
probability bound: the ideal implemented mixture can be supported in P
even when it is not the exact true thermodynamic optimum.


3. THE COMPLETE PREPARATION CONSTRUCTION

3.1 Accepted representation and its executed exact-input form

Use the accepted all-sector decomposition

  (C^2)^(tensor N) = direct sum_J V_J tensor C^(m_(N,J)),
  H_N = direct sum_J H_J tensor I_(m_(N,J)),
  m_(N,J) = binom(N,N/2-J)-binom(N,N/2-J-1).

There are O(N) blocks, dim V_J=2J+1<=N+1, and O(N^3) total block entries.
The window P is diagonal in magnetic quantum number m and acts identically
on every multiplicity copy. Let B_J be its nonempty compressed block, with
dimension d_J. Empty blocks are omitted, not assigned missing probability.

The predecessor's distinct-site Newton recurrence is used without a new
proof. This implementation executes it in the unnormalized Dicke basis.
Its matrices have Gaussian-rational entries and Gram matrix
G=diag binom(2J,k). Thus G B_J is exactly Hermitian rational-complex.
The orthonormal matrix is sqrt(G) B_J sqrt(G)^(-1). Integer square-root
brackets and dyadic rounding produce an exactly Hermitian dyadic Htilde_J
and an explicit operator-norm error delta_J. Exact rational row-sum bounds
enclose the change of basis; an unverified floating eigensolver is absent.

3.2 A lower spectral shift without an eigenvector or gap oracle

Obtain rational lower and upper bounds on the least eigenvalue of B_J with
width at most 1/(2 beta). At a rational x, the statement x<lambda_min is
equivalent to positive definiteness of G(B_J-xI). An exact LDL/Sylvester
test decides this: positive pivots continue, a nonpositive pivot rejects.
This test only needs positive definiteness, so a zero pivot is correctly
rejected rather than perturbed. Bisection starts from the supplied norm
bound. Exact rational arithmetic and polynomial-size minors give polynomial
bit cost. No lower bound on a difference between distinct eigenvalues is
used. The contiguous window preserves the fixed-degree band structure.

If lo_J is the exact lower bound, choose a_J=lo_J-delta_J and make the
dyadic precision sufficient that

  0 <= lambda_min(Htilde_J)-a_J <= 1/beta.

Define

  A_J = exp[-beta(Htilde_J-a_J I)/2],
  T_J = ||A_J||_F^2.

Then

  1/e <= T_J <= d_J,                  ||A_J|| <= 1.           (3)

This explicit normalization is important. A tiny physical probability of
the desired magnetization window does not become a tiny success amplitude
for the block-purification algorithm. The window changes the target and
has a thermodynamic penalty; it is not acquired by rejecting physical Gibbs
samples.

3.3 Certified exponential and normalization

Let X=beta(Htilde_J-a_J I)/2^(s+1), with s chosen so ||X||<=1.
Then X is positive semidefinite and A_J=(exp(-X))^(2^s).
The implementation rounds the entries of X to a dyadic grid 2^(-p), uses a
Taylor polynomial of degree M, and rounds after every term multiplication
and every squaring. NumPy object products use exact Python integers.

Here is the conservative bound actually used. Let d=d_J and r=d 2^(-p).
Entrywise rounding of real and imaginary parts has operator norm at most
r. The rounded X differs by delta_X<=r. Its norm is at most 2. The Taylor
tail is bounded by

  tail <= 8 * 2^(M+1)/(M+1)!.

Term-rounding errors obey e_k<=2 e_(k-1)/k+r, so their sum is below
8 M r. The perturbation of exp(-X) is at most 2 delta_X when delta_X<=1/2;
Duhamel's formula and the contraction of exp(-tX) prove this even if a
rounding makes the approximate generator slightly non-Hermitian.
After a squaring, error delta becomes at most (2+delta)delta+r.
As long as the advertised final bound is below 1, the following bound is
sufficient for all intermediate steps:

  E_J = 4^s [2 delta_X + (8M+1)r + tail].                    (4)

The code asserts E_J<1/100 for its chosen finite precision. A mathematical
arbitrary-precision implementation increases p and M until its requested
budget is met. Since log(4^s)=O(log(2+beta NC)), this does not hide an
exponential dependence on beta NC in the working precision.

Let Ahat_J be the dyadic output. Equation (4) certifies
||Ahat_J-A_J||<=E_J. Therefore its squared Frobenius norm differs from T_J
by at most d E_J(2+E_J). These are outward rational trace intervals.
The vectorized normalized matrices obey

  T( |vec(Ahat)/||Ahat||_F><...|,
     |vec(A)/||A||_F><...| ) <= 4 d E_J.                    (5)

For (5), the unnormalized vector error is at most sqrt(d) E_J, normalization
changes a vector by at most twice its relative error, and (3) gives
||A||_F>1/2. The displayed d bound is deliberately conservative.

3.4 All sector weights and exponentially many multiplicity copies

The correct sector law is

  q_J = m_(N,J) exp(-beta a_J) T_J /
                sum_L m_(N,L) exp(-beta a_L) T_L.            (6)

The shifts in (6) are retained; dropping them would change the state.
To avoid overflow, subtract min_J a_J in every scalar exponential. The
smallest-shift block has T_J>=1/e, so the common denominator is bounded
away from zero. Very small scalar terms may be clipped only with a paid
upper bound. Directed Decimal exp/log enclosures and exact rational
normalization provide intervals [q_J^-,q_J^+]. A finite dyadic cumulative
distribution is constructed, and its total-variation error is bounded by

  (1/2) sum_J max(|qhat_J-q_J^-|, |qhat_J-q_J^+|).

Draw J from this finite distribution. Draw an integer uniformly in
0,...,m_(N,J)-1, using at most R rejection trials of ceil(log2 m) fair bits.
Each trial accepts with probability at least 1/2. On exhaustion, output
rank zero. This introduces at most 2^(-R) total-variation error and bounds
the randomness cost. It never asks for a physical postselection success.

The rank is converted into a valid angular-momentum branching path by the
integer recurrence

  m_(k,L) = m_(k-1,L-1) + m_(k-1,L+1),       L=2j.

At each backward step select the branch interval containing the rank.
This takes N steps on O(N)-bit integers. No exponential list of paths is
constructed. Randomizing those paths supplies the maximally mixed
multiplicity factor in (6). Its O(N) classical entropy is paid explicitly.

3.5 Purify a small block and decode it into N spins

Prepare vec(Ahat_J)/||Ahat_J||_F on two logarithmic-size registers, using
the standard binary-tree amplitude rotations and phase rotations. Exact
integer squared magnitudes supply the conditional probabilities. A
uniformly controlled rotation is decomposed by the standard Gray-code
Walsh transform into Ry or Rz rotations and CNOTs [3]. The Walsh transform
of the finite-dyadic angles is done exactly, with no additional rounding.

The delivered angle computation uses outward 60-digit Decimal arithmetic.
Arctan arguments are reduced by three half-angle steps to below 0.1;
twelve alternating terms and an explicit 10^(-24) enclosure suffice for
the declared 48-bit angles. A Machin rational interval supplies pi.
Quantization checks each angle's error is at most 2^(-48). Larger requested
precision requires raising these arithmetic parameters; an unmet bound is
rejected. This differs from a binary64 circuit proposal. Binary64 is used
only in the separately labelled diagnostic simulator.

Use an r=ceil(log2 d_J) register for the block index and add the beginning
of the contiguous magnetic window into a b=ceil(log2(N+1)) spin register.
The second register is the purifier. The complete matrix A, not merely its
diagonal, is encoded; hence coherences and entanglement are retained.

Now invert the qubit Clebsch-Gordan chain along the sampled path [2]. Let
ell_k=j_k-m_k, Lprev=2j_(k-1), and s=2(j_k-j_(k-1)). The kth emitted physical
spin has computational amplitudes:

  s=+1: up=sqrt((Lprev+1-ell)/(Lprev+1)),
        down=sqrt(ell/(Lprev+1));
        then subtract one from ell if the emitted spin is down.

  s=-1: up=-sqrt((ell+1)/(Lprev+1)),
        down=sqrt((Lprev-ell)/(Lprev+1));
        then add one to ell if the emitted spin is up.

The relative minus sign is retained. Invalid register values receive an
arbitrary unitary completion, because the ideal vector has zero support
there. Controlled additions are reversible. Multi-controlled NOTs are
decomposed into clean-ancilla Toffoli chains, then Ry/Rz/CNOT gates. The
working spin register ends at ell_0=0. Tracing out the purifier gives the
selected thermal block in its physical multiplicity copy. Averaging the
fresh sector and path choices gives the entire target state.

This establishes the central operation. It does not first prepare a
whole-system phase mixture, sample a rare thermal event, invert an unknown
apparatus, or wait for inter-sector relaxation.


4. RESOURCE AND ERROR THEOREM

At fixed K, with an explicit rational coefficient description of bit length
L_in, the construction has classical bit cost polynomial in

  N, L_in, log(2+beta NC), log(1/epsilon).

The dense block-exponential part uses O(sum_J d_J^3)=O(N^4) matrix-arithmetic
work times the stated precision/scaling factors; representation and exact
inertia work are also polynomial. This statement concerns a supplied model.
Acquiring that model and calibrating an apparatus have separate costs.
The result is polynomial in N, not in log N; producing N physical spins
already costs at least N output resources.

One draw needs N+b+r+max(0,b-2) qubits, including output, spin register,
purifier and clean carry ancillas. Thus auxiliary space is O(log N).
Generic state synthesis and the CG chain use

  O(N^2 + N log^2 N + log^3 N)

logical rotations/CNOTs. A conservative count valid for every path is

  G <= 16 d_max^2 + 34 b^3 + N[2^(b+1)+34 b^2+4].           (7)

This bound includes the explicit Toffoli decomposition and constant shift.
It is not a depth bound or a claim of gate optimality. Finite-alphabet
synthesis multiplies the appropriate gate count by its established
polylogarithmic precision overhead. On a line, moving a CNOT control to the
target and back costs 1+6 max(0,|c-t|-1) native CNOTs. The delivered example
counts include this optional explicit routing, rather than assuming free
all-to-all connectivity on every platform.

With B sector bits and R multiplicity trials, randomness is at most B+RN
bits per copy and has expected cost at most B+2N bits. The executable uses
B=96 and R=80, so N=64 has a 5,216-bit worst-case cap. Output entropy is
not created for free: the classical record/purifier and their reset/discard
costs are part of the interface.

For a supplied exact model let delta_H=max_J delta_J, eta_q be the certified
sector TV error, eta_A=max_J 4d_J E_J, and eta_ang the logical circuit bound.
The output obeys

  T(output,omega_P(H)) <= beta delta_H/2 + eta_q + eta_A
                         + 2^(-R) + eta_ang
                         + eta_init + eta_rng + sum_g eta_g.              (8)

Here eta_g bounds half the diamond distance between the actual contextual
gate and its advertised gate. A uniform context-free randomized benchmark
number does not automatically supply that stronger bound. Telescoping
channels proves the sum in (8), including correlated environmental effects
only when the supplied contextual bounds actually cover them.

For the ideal finite-angle emitter, a uniform sufficient bound is

  eta_ang <= 2^(-48)[(2b+N)/2 + 2 + G].                    (9)

The binary-tree controlled rotations cost one half-angle error per level;
phase reconstruction costs at most twice the phase error up to global
phase; the N CG multiplexers cost another N half-angle errors. Fixed-pi
arithmetic rotations are counted explicitly. Their total weight is below
G, giving (9). Per-component certificates are substantially tighter.

All claims refer to the system marginal. A fixed input reference untouched
by a preparation that starts from independent resets remains untouched;
this is not a cloning or independent-copy acquisition claim.


5. SHARP THERMAL MODEL ACCURACY AND A MATCHING SIZE BARRIER

For arbitrary finite-dimensional Hermitian H and K at the same beta,

  T(rho_beta(H),rho_beta(K))
      <= tanh[ beta osc(H-K)/4 ],                           (10)

where osc(X)=lambda_max(X)-lambda_min(X). The same statement holds after
compression to the same exact nonempty window. Scalar energy shifts cancel.
Temperature uncertainty is handled by replacing beta(H-K) with the
dimensionless generator difference beta H-beta_hat K.

Proof. Put t=T(rho,sigma). Measure the Helstrom projector, preserving this
trace distance in the two-outcome distributions. Relative-entropy data
processing and the established tight Jeffreys/TV inequality [4] give

  D(rho||sigma)+D(sigma||rho)
       >= 2t log[(1+t)/(1-t)].

The classical binary inequality can also be checked directly: for fixed
difference t the sum is minimized by probabilities (1+t)/2 and (1-t)/2.
For Gibbs states the left side equals

  -beta Tr[(rho-sigma)(H-K)] <= beta osc(H-K) t.

Cancel t, with t=0 immediate, and invert the logarithm to obtain (10).
The commuting qubit pair H=+aZ/2, K=-aZ/2 attains equality. This sharp
modulus is a deduction from established divergence machinery, not a claim
of priority for that machinery or for Gibbs-state continuity in general.

Thus the predecessor coefficient uncertainty contributes

  eta_model <= tanh(beta N nu/2).                          (11)

For a target eta_model<1, it is sufficient to acquire

  nu <= 2 artanh(eta_model)/(beta N).

The 1/N scale can be necessary at coexistence even with fixed local
dimension, fixed degree and fixed temperature. Consider the commuting
collective ferromagnet

  H_h = -N A_zz - h sum_i Z_i,             beta=2.

Its exact energy at total Z value M is
-(M^2-N)/(N-1)-hM, with binomial multiplicity. At zero field,

  Pr(|M|<=aN) <= 2^(N-1) exp[-2(1-a^2)N^2/(N-1)].        (12)

The denominator contains the two fully polarized configurations; the
numerator is bounded by 2^N configurations at the maximal allowed energy
weight. Equation (12) is an elementary finite-size proof, not an assumed
thermodynamic-limit phase theorem.

Take h_+=1/(beta N), h_-=-1/(beta N). Under the zero-field law the two
Gibbs distributions are tilted by exp(+M/N) and exp(-M/N). Symmetry yields

  T(rho_+,rho_-)=E_0 sinh(|M|/N) / E_0 cosh(M/N)
     >= (1-delta_N) sinh(1/2)/cosh(1),
  delta_N=2^(N-1) exp[-(3/2)N^2/(N-1)].                    (13)

For N=64 the certified lower bound exceeds 0.33769; the directly evaluated
exact binomial formula is about 0.76131055. These two fields differ by only
2/(beta N). A single output cannot approximate both states to less than
half their distance. Uniform whole-state guarantees therefore require
resolving an O(1/N) field ambiguity in this family. This is a model
information lower bound, not an exponential-time hardness theorem.

The older reference-L2 continuity result N52 has a different input norm.
The warning in N652 about relative-operator approximation and normalized
states is respected: (3)-(6) pay the normalizations explicitly.


6. A HARD OUTCOME DOES NOT NEED WHOLE-STATE MODEL ACCURACY

The actual ideal circuit before angle/hardware errors prepares a mixture
supported in the chosen P. This remains true with an imperfect model,
rounded matrix entries, dyadic sector weights and a capped-sampler fallback:
each component was explicitly embedded in the allowed magnetic subspace.
Consequently,

  Pr(forbidden actual outcome) <= eta_ang+eta_init+sum_g eta_g,             (14)

with any error in the measurement/interface definition added separately.
The model term (11), the sector-weight error and matrix-normalization error
need not be included in (14). They matter for thermal optimality and full
state closeness, not for support. This is a probability guarantee for every
fresh run; it is not fault-proof enforcement with exactly zero failures.

For the exact constrained optimum of an acquired model,

  Fcal_H(omega_P(Hhat)) - F_P(H)
      <= osc((P(H-Hhat)P)|_(ran P)) <= 2N nu.               (15)

Proof. Apply the variational optimality of omega_P(Hhat) against
omega_P(H), then compare the two expectations of H-Hhat. Their difference
is at most its spectral oscillation on the allowed subspace. Dividing by
N gives the free-energy-density regret 2 nu, independent of system size.

Finite implementation error can also be priced without an unacquired
entropy oracle. If two states have trace distance epsilon, write their
Jordan difference as epsilon(tau_+-tau_-). The common state
(rho+epsilon tau_-)/(1+epsilon)=(sigma+epsilon tau_+)/(1+epsilon), entropy
concavity and the binary mixture-entropy upper bound imply

  |S(rho)-S(sigma)| <= epsilon log D
                +(1+epsilon)h2(epsilon/(1+epsilon)).

For D=2^N and ||H||<=NC this bounds additional free-energy-density error by

  2C epsilon + [epsilon log 2
          +(1+epsilon)h2(epsilon/(1+epsilon))/N]/beta.       (16)

Equations (14)-(16) provide a physically meaningful alternative to asking
for an extremely accurate global canonical state. They preserve a definite
finite-system response window and thermodynamic near-optimality within
that window. They do not assume fluctuations vanish because a mean was
specified. Acquisition of common coefficients can retain its original
precision for this task, although gate and apparatus calibration must
still meet (14).


7. FREE-ENERGY PENALTY, RARITY AND HOLDING TIME

Let F=-beta^(-1) log Tr exp(-beta H) and p=Tr(P rho_beta(H)). Then

  Delta F = F_P-F = beta^(-1) log(Z/Z_P)
           >= beta^(-1) log(1/p) >= 0.                    (17)

For any state supported in P, relative entropy to the full Gibbs state
equals beta times its excess free energy. Applying the two-outcome
measurement {P,I-P} gives relative entropy at least -log p. Minimize using
(2). This proves (17). If [H,P]=0, the constrained optimum is precisely the
postselected Gibbs state and equality holds. For noncommuting H and P the
penalty can be strictly larger.

Thus the active compiler avoids repeated rare physical sampling, but it
does not remove the free-energy increase or the resources for coherence,
control and reset. Delta F is an intrinsic state-function difference. In
an isothermal work-accounted process it supplies the usual average-work
lower bound; this return does not construct a reversible work-optimal
process or identify Delta F with a complete single-shot work price.

For the Curie-Weiss example, zero field has exactly zero mean magnetization.
Yet (12) with a=1/4 bounds the probability of an approximately zero outcome
exponentially in N. At N=64 the exact-formula diagnostic is about
3.7867e-36, while a certified analytic upper bound is 1.0528e-34 (rounded
up). The hard-window free-energy penalty from the exact binomial formula
is about 40.7808 total energy units. Mean zero gives no high-probability
claim about an individual macroscopic realization.

Any state epsilon-close to the canonical state has window probability at
most p+epsilon. In particular, a supported output must be at least 1-p
away in trace distance. This is an actual incompatibility between two
specifications, not a failure of an estimator or optimizer.

The window is guaranteed at the preparation endpoint. If the original
noncommuting H is resumed without extra protection, define V=(I-P)HP.
For an initial state supported in P, compare exp(-itH) with the evolution
of PHP+(I-P)H(I-P). Duhamel's formula bounds the forbidden amplitude by
|t| ||V||. Therefore an epsilon-close prepared state obeys

  Pr(outside at time t) <= min(1, epsilon+t^2 ||V||^2).      (18)

An acquired-model bound can replace ||V|| by ||Vhat||+N nu. With [H,P]=0
the ideal support is invariant. Otherwise long holding, repeated
measurement, a Zeno implementation or a protecting Hamiltonian requires
its own interface and cost. No such additional operation is assumed.


8. THE FIXED-TAG INTERFACE OBSTRUCTION, INCLUDING HERALDING

Suppose every allowed operation on a loaded sample has Kraus operators
commuting with a projector Q. A trace-preserving protocol preserves
Tr(Q rho), since sum K_a^dagger Q K_a=Q. Adaptive compositions obey the
same statement. Hamiltonians and Lindblad jumps that commute with the
sector projectors are examples. Mere covariance of a channel under
permutations does not imply this strong symmetry. Independent local noise
or independent resets on all bath spins can change sector weights and are
outside this obstruction.

Split off one fixed tagged spin and let S_B be the total spin of the other
N-1 spins. Every collective polynomial in S_B+S_tag and every operation
on the fixed tag commutes with S_B^2. Thus these controls preserve the
untagged bath's total-spin probabilities on a loaded sample.

For N even and bath initialization rho(r)^(tensor (N-1)), the probability
of bath spin 1/2 is exactly

  m_(N-1,1/2) [det rho(r)]^((N-2)/2)
       <= b_N := m_(N-1,1/2)/2^(N-2).                     (19)

To see this, in the spin-1/2 Schur block the tensor power is
(det rho)^((N-2)/2) rho, with the stated multiplicity. Its trace is the
displayed expression; det rho<=1/4. Classical mixtures of these initial
product baths retain the same upper bound. The tagged initial state is
arbitrary. Total spin J=0 of all N spins requires bath spin 1/2. Therefore
every unconditional output of the stated loaded-sample controls has

  Pr(J=0) <= b_N.                                          (20)

Consider the legitimate degree-two collective target

  H_AF = N(A_xx+A_yy+A_zz)
       = [4 S^2-3N I]/(N-1).

Its exact thermal J=0 weight is obtained from sectors with energy
[4J(J+1)-3N]/(N-1), dimension 2J+1 and multiplicity m_(N,J).
The delivered rational lower certificate uses
exp(-x)<=(1+x/128)^(-128) for positive gaps and outward dyadic sums.
It gives:

       N    beta     target J=0 probability >    b_N          trace error >
       8      20          0.9999999955           0.21875         0.7812499955

Heralding qualification. A success-conditioned operation can change sector
weights. If a successful output is epsilon-close to a target with singlet
weight p0, conservation before conditioning implies

  Pr(success) (p0-epsilon) <= b_N.                          (21)

This is a success bound, not an impossibility theorem for postselection.
For independent restarts from the same source, expected fresh preparations
are at least (p0-epsilon)/b_N. Replacing or resetting the untagged bath is
another operation, not a commuting operation on the same loaded sample.
Neither (20) nor (21) establishes an optimal protocol for that broader
restart interface. They identify the assumption needed by a deterministic
preparation claim and price the simplest conditioning loophole.

  zeta proportional to nu/Lambda_K,
  W <= 4R^2/T0,
  gamma = zeta/(2W),
  M = 3K^3(R+1),
  shots/record = ceil[8 gamma^(-2) log(2M/alpha)].

All timing, state-preparation and measurement systematic errors must fit
the original record budget. At fixed structural parameters, substituting
nu=O(epsilon/(beta N)) gives a sufficient preparation-acquisition schedule
scaling as (beta N/epsilon)^2 times explicit conditioning and logarithmic
factors. This is not logarithmic dependence on temperature or precision
once physical learning is included. The existing short-time local-readout
lower bound also retains its original scope; stronger coherent acquisition
resources have not been silently inserted.

9.36e29 fresh preparations for its stated nu and confidence. This return

Per independently prepared output, the physical account includes fresh
N+O(log N) initialized spins, the complete gate and routing/synthesis list,
per-gate durations and contextual errors, randomness generation, reset and
