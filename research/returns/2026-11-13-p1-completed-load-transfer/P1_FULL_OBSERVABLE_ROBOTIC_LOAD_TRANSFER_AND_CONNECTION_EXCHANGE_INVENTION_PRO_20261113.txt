Invent a reusable robotic load-transfer and connection-exchange module that lets a machine remove, replace and reattach a structural connection while the useful load remains supported, with an actual way to detect an ineffective connection and recover from a jam.

The useful operation begins with an installed, loaded connection and ends with a qualified replacement carrying the declared load, the temporary support recovered, and the tool ready for another exchange. This is a complete maintenance operation, not merely a strong connector, a low insertion force, a gripper, a load sensor or a nominal assembly script. It could enable machines to maintain and reconfigure equipment frames, manufacturing fixtures and larger robot-built structures. Construct the actual mechanical interface, load path, sensing, controlled transfer and access sequence that makes that operation possible. Commercial tool changers, reversible fasteners, passive alignment, temporary shoring, proof loading and series elasticity are existing ideas; naming or combining them does not by itself establish an invention. All methods and representations remain open.

Use an Earth-gravity structural-maintenance application with no human occupancy claim. Specify a useful reference envelope capable of transferring a resultant force up to1kN and resultant moment up to250Nm simultaneously, including the weight and applied forces of the tool and machinery. Declare the load reference frame and admissible directions, combinations and changes during the operation. An individual support does not receive the entire envelope for free: construct its actual lever arms, section, contact and fastener loads. Loads may be introduced and maintained by a paid test fixture, but that fixture may not silently substitute for the module's temporary support or accepted replacement.

Demonstrate the operation on at least two materially different connection geometries, such as a bolted face joint and a strut-end joint, from an explicitly bounded family of equipment frames. You may design repeatable interfaces and adapters, paying their manufacture, installation and occupied space. Distinguish a new structure using those interfaces from retrofitting an inaccessible legacy joint. Arbitrary hidden access and universal operation on every existing machine are not supplied. Preserve a usable tool path throughout support installation, connection release, part removal, replacement, tightening, inspection, load return and recovery of the support itself. A permanent gripper that blocks the last bolt, cut or withdrawal path has not completed the exchange.

The module must hold the useful load mechanically during each represented state, including a declared one-hour loss of external electrical power. If it uses a pneumatic, hydraulic, thermal or magnetic stock, include its finite preparation, leakage, restoration and loss-of-power behavior. Design the retained-load function, not a free emergency lock. At useful-load handoffs, seek no uncontrolled movement greater than1mm or1degree, with tighter requirements where the destination needs them. State the actual elastic movement, stored-energy release, contact pressure and permissible permanent set. Explain which loads and geometry these limits support; a stiff final joint does not bound a transient release.

Construct the complete sensing and control operation. It must distinguish enough physical states to avoid accepting a seated-but-unlocked connection, a jam or unintended friction path that imitates seating, and a partly engaged connection unable to carry the intended wrench. Test an initially declared finite envelope that includes at least1mm placement uncertainty,2degree orientation uncertainty and measured or conservatively bounded changes in friction, wear and sensor bias. Fault category, location and time are not provided to the controller. It may use an action that safely handles several indistinguishable states, but may not read a simulator's hidden fault label. Unbounded arbitrary damage need not be recoverable; define the assumptions needed to remain supported while a fault is detected.

The reference service is at least500 complete load-transfer, connection-exchange and return-to-ready cycles, including at least20 unannounced challenge events drawn from the supported envelope and more than one loading direction. Include an obstructed or persistent jam for which repeating the same command is ineffective, a false appearance of completed engagement, and a loss of power during an exchange. Every counted cycle ends with the useful load carried by the accepted connection and all temporary supports and tools in their specified reusable or paid disposition states. Report failures and interventions; discarding them from the denominator does not establish a working service. The short campaign does not grant a long wear or fatigue life.

Find a new useful mechanical or control primitive that changes this entire operation. One possible opening is a mechanically bounded, observable handoff: establish a second finite load path, verify its reactions, progressively unload the original path, obtain actual access, exchange the connection, verify the new path under relevant loading and recover the bypass. Independent force limits or geometry may sometimes replace expensive sensing, but their friction, backlash, hysteresis, energy and failure modes are physical claims. A different interface, kinematic organization, inspection method or support relay may be stronger.
