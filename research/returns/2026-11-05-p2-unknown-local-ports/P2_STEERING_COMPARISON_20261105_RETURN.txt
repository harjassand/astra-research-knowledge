## 1. The question I resolved

The useful question was:

> Can interventions cover a physically defined family of unknown local mechanisms so that the aggregate effect of all still-unobserved mechanisms is bounded, while the cost avoids the inverse probability of system failure?

Simply measuring recovery and applying detailed balance would not answer this. That strategy already has strong precedent: Trendelkamp-Schroer and Noé showed how equilibrium information and downhill trajectories can estimate rare-event kinetics, including multistate models and uncertainty. The reversal idea itself is established. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

The missing step was a guarantee that survives **unknown transition support**. A fitted model can contain only transitions that appeared in the data, while the real device contains additional channels. Relative errors on fitted rates say nothing about those omitted channels.

I resolved that problem by retaining an operating mechanism whose mixing strength is known. This provides a reference against which the *total absolute uncertainty* in additional local mechanisms can be compared. An unknown channel need not be observed or estimated relatively if its possible contribution is already bounded tightly enough against that reference.

This leads to three linked results:

1. A physical intervention exposes the complete input–output response of each bounded-size port, summing over its unobserved internal pathways.
2. A local error-envelope theorem bounds every still-possible transition within those ports.
3. A fixed-start first-passage argument converts that bound into an interval for the full mean lifetime, with explicit computation costs.

The last two steps are necessary. A bound on an equilibrium probability or a principal decay rate alone does not generally establish the mean from the all-working state.

## 2. The physical setting and its reach

### The register

There are \(m\) binary occupations. Write \(x_i=1\) for a failed or excited component and \(x_i=0\) for a working component. Their prescribed equilibrium law is

\[
\pi(x)=\prod_{i=1}^{m}p_i^{x_i}(1-p_i)^{1-x_i},
\qquad 0<p_i<1.
\]

A known graph assigns components to edges. The failure set \(B\) consists of disconnected configurations.

The dynamics contain an independent refresh mechanism:

\[
Q_0 f(x)=
\sum_i\gamma_i
\left[
\mathbb E_{Z_i\sim\operatorname{Bern}(p_i)}
f(x_{-i},Z_i)-f(x)
\right],
\qquad \gamma_i>0.
\]

Additional ports act on known blocks \(S_\alpha\), each containing at most \(k\) components:

\[
Q=Q_0+\sum_\alpha Q_\alpha.
\]

Inside each block, the additional dynamics may contain arbitrary unknown transitions, including simultaneous component changes and rates equal to zero. Blocks may overlap. Each port’s response depends only on its block; any necessary context must be included in that block.

Thus the dynamics can have correlated failures even though the stationary distribution is a product distribution.

The retained refresh mechanism gives a full-system spectral-gap lower bound

\[
\gamma_*=\min_i\gamma_i.
\]

**It must remain in the operating device.** Adding refresh during calibration and removing it during deployment would invalidate the result.

### Why the local response is reversible

A port couples its register block to a fresh auxiliary block. It then discards the auxiliary block and dephases the register into the occupation basis.

For a thermal implementation, assume energy conservation and reciprocal transition probabilities:

\[
[U,H_S+H_E]=0,
\]

\[
|\langle v,b|U|u,a\rangle|^2
=
|\langle u,a|U|v,b\rangle|^2.
\]

The measured local kernel is

\[
K(u,v)=
\sum_{a,b}\tau(a)
|\langle v,b|U|u,a\rangle|^2.
\]

Energy conservation, thermal weights and reciprocity then imply

\[
\pi_S(u)K(u,v)=\pi_S(v)K(v,u).
\]

This derivation sums over all internal auxiliary pathways. Those pathways do not need to be individually identified.

Reciprocity is a substantive condition. A sufficient implementation is a single pulse generated by a real symmetric, energy-conserving Hamiltonian in the occupation basis. An arbitrary sequence of pulses need not retain it. Gibbs stationarity alone does not imply detailed balance. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}

### A version that avoids estimating tiny equilibrium populations

I also constructed a stricter digital version. Each port conserves the occupation sum of every matched register–auxiliary pair:

\[
n_i^{S}+n_i^{E}.
\]

Retaining reciprocal amplitudes then permits prescribed rational auxiliary probabilities \(p_i=a_i/d_i\). The detailed-balance identity follows directly from the conserved occupations.

An exact Bernoulli request with probability \(a_i/d_i\) can be generated using rational rejection sampling with expected random-bit work \(O(\log d_i)\). It does not require observing a naturally rare occupied state.

The physical write of the requested bit is a separate cost. Its errors are addressed below; digital sampling does not make preparation perfect.

### What the architecture actually covers

The complete local table is covered because the block is finite, all its input occupations can be prepared, and its output occupations can be measured. Fresh auxiliary systems and endpoint dephasing provide the Markov description.

This does **not** derive the absence of arbitrary external baths, leakage states or hidden memory from a short experiment. Those processes are outside this constructed architecture unless separately included and bounded.

The target is also precise: **first failure at a registered classical endpoint**. During a coherent interaction pulse, the register need not have a definite classical network configuration. The theorem does not certify the absence of an in-pulse transient disconnection.

## 3. The main acquisition theorem: unseen transitions retain a finite budget

For every port and every local input, the protocol is:

1. Isolate the port.
2. Prepare the chosen local occupation.
3. Remove preparation control.
4. Apply one actual operating interaction with a fresh auxiliary block.
5. Dephase and read the complete local output.
6. Reset the acquisition apparatus.

The interaction being measured is the operating interaction. There is no extrapolation from arbitrary stress-modified transition rates.

For each unordered pair of local states, measure the direction from smaller equilibrium weight to larger equilibrium weight. Detailed balance reconstructs the reverse direction with a multiplier at most one. Consequently, absolute measurement error is not amplified when reconstructing the rarer direction.

With \(n\) trials per input and \(J\) unordered local pairs across all ports, simultaneous probability radii can use

\[
h=\sqrt{\frac{\log(3J/\alpha)}{2n}}.
\]

For a zero count, a valid upper bound is

\[
K(u,v)\le \frac{\log(3J/\alpha)}{n},
\]

intersected with the ordinary confidence interval.

**A zero count remains a positive uncertainty bound.** It is never converted into a statement that the transition is absent.

### The certificate

For each port, build a reversible *error generator* whose rates are the absolute confidence radii. Let \(r_\alpha\) be its maximum total exit rate. Define

\[
\boxed{

### Why it works

The proof has three short ingredients.

First, the error generator dominates the absolute conductance error on every local transition.

Second, any reversible local generator with maximum exit rate \(r_\alpha\) satisfies

\[
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{error},\alpha}(f,f)
\le
2r_\alpha\operatorname{Var}_{\pi_{S_\alpha}}(f).
\]

Third, product variance tensorization bounds a block variance by the sum of its conditional single-coordinate variances. Those are exactly the quantities paid for by the native refresh form.

The local inequalities therefore add into a global bound without enumerating \(2^m\) configurations.

Dirichlet-form comparison is classical machinery. The contribution here is the acquisition construction that feeds **absolute, unknown-support confidence intervals** into a usable global comparison. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}

### Why the refresh mechanism is necessary

Without a reference mechanism, a small absolute rate can still determine the whole lifetime.

For example, a two-state system with downhill rate \(\theta\) and uphill rate \(\theta e^{-h}\) has mean uphill waiting time

\[
M=e^h/\theta.
\]

Relative inference then requires information about \(\theta\), potentially costing exposure of order \(1/\theta\).

The refresh mechanism changes the situation: sufficiently small additional uncertainty can be bounded against a known operating recovery scale. The theorem does not eliminate that physical requirement.

## 5. Computing the mean without waiting for failure

The acquired model still has exponentially many configurations. A statistically efficient experiment would be insufficient if evaluating its consequence required enumerating them.

The computation extends the accepted [P2 governing construction](https://github.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/tree/5cfc803599d9f95cfa0a55896fb580e77567e162/research/returns/2026-11-05-p2-first-failure) to correlated reversible dynamics with product equilibrium.

The key observation is that the static counting step needs product equilibrium, not independent component trajectories. Karger’s all-terminal unreliability algorithm therefore remains applicable to \(\pi(B)\). Its general counting subroutine is invoked as published machinery; it is not implemented in this package. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="3"}

### Rare branch

The algorithm:

- Estimates the stationary failure probability \(p=\pi(B)\).
- Constructs a sampler approximately distributed as \(\pi(\,\cdot\,|B)\), using contraction/deletion self-reduction with at most \(2m\) counting calls per draw.
- Runs a short ordinary trajectory from each sampled failed configuration.
- Records whether the trajectory returns to safety and remains safe through the prescribed window.

For a window of order

\[
g^{-1}\log(1/\varepsilon),
\]

the useful-trial probability is bounded below by a constant multiple of \(g\). The exponentially small factor is supplied by static counting; the sampled event itself is not exponentially rare.

A spectral argument relates this short return-avoidance flux to the killed decay rate and then to the specified-start mean, including the initial correction.

Excluding counting calls and per-update work, the update count has the form

\[
O\!\left(
g^{-2}\varepsilon^{-2}
\log(1/\varepsilon)
\log(1/\alpha)
\right).
\]

The counting precision, conditional-sampler error, local-update cost and graph-connectivity checks are all included in the full argument.

### Nonrare branch

When the rare-regime test fails, the static failure probability has an inverse-polynomial lower bound in the declared parameters. A spectral survival estimate then bounds the mean and second moment of direct first-failure trajectories.

Median-of-means estimation and an explicit trajectory cap provide a finite polynomial work bound. This branch does not require monotonicity of the dynamics or an assumption that the all-working state maximizes lifetime.

The resulting computation is polynomial in graph and table size, probability/rate bit length,

\[
2^k,\quad g^{-1},\quad\kappa^{-1},
\quad\varepsilon^{-1},\quad\log(1/\alpha).
\]

There is no additional inverse-failure-probability or lifetime factor in the rare branch. Very small \(g\), very small \(\kappa\), or large local blocks can nevertheless make it expensive.

## 6. Finite physical execution and error costs

### A clocked implementation

An ideal continuous-time clock is unnecessary.

Let one operating tick have duration \(T\), including bounded port selection, controller work, auxiliary preparation and verification, the interaction, dephasing, reset and endpoint service. Select a port with prescribed rational weights and pad every branch to the same duration.

If the actual endpoint kernel is \(P\), define the analytical generator

\[
Q=(P-I)/T.
\]

For the number \(N\) of ticks to first failed endpoint,

\[
\boxed{
M_{\mathrm{lab}}=T\,\mathbb E N=(-Q_A)^{-1}\mathbf1.
}
\]

This is an exact identity for the mean. It does not assert equality between the physical discrete trajectory and a continuous-time trajectory.

Serial throughput is paid: if refresh \(i\) has selection weight \(w_i\), then \(\gamma_i=w_i/T\). Maintaining a fixed refresh rate per component as the system grows requires faster cycles or additional parallel hardware.

### Finite auxiliary preparation

A tiny intended population can be overwhelmed by small additive preparation error. I therefore derived a separate closure rather than treating preparation fidelity as harmless.

The sufficient hardware assumptions are strong and explicit:

- A requested occupation write has success probability bounded below.
- Repeated readout errors are conditionally independent and bounded below one-half.
- Readout is nondemolition and leaves the occupation stable.
- Storage remains protected while other preparation occurs.
- Reset removes factory history.
- Auxiliary states are dephased before interaction.

Majority verification then reduces label error exponentially with the number of checks. Write retries and random-number rejection are capped. **A timeout produces a null operating tick**, so successful preparation is not conditioned on without charging time.

Under these conditions, the actual off-diagonal rates can be bounded by

\[
(1-\zeta)q(x,y)
\le q^{\mathrm{act}}(x,y)
\le(1+\zeta)q(x,y).
\]

The actual auxiliary distribution may contain correlations, and the actual operating process need not remain reversible.

For \(n\) safe states, positive tree–forest representations yield

\[
\frac{(1-\zeta)^{n-1}}{(1+\zeta)^n}
\le
\frac{M^{\mathrm{act}}_x}{M_x}
\le
\frac{(1+\zeta)^{n-1}}{(1-\zeta)^n}.
\]

The underlying positive forest machinery is established; the note applies it to this finite-factory uncertainty. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="4"}

Although \(n\le2^m\), taking \(\zeta=O(\varepsilon/2^m)\) requires only logarithmic error precision. Under the stated independent QND error model, the verification count scales with

\[
m+k\log(1/p_{\min})+\log(k/\varepsilon),
\]

along with the readout margin and write-success factors. Capped retries can make service time quadratic in these quantities, but do not require \(2^m\) physical checks.

Finite auxiliary preparation

A tiny intended population can be overwhelmed by small additive preparation error. I therefore derived a separate closure rather than treating preparation fidelity as harmless.

Reset removes factory history.
Auxiliary states are dephased before interaction.

The comparison is narrower than “a new rare-event method.”

9. The scientific consequence and its boundary

Within the constructed class, an unobserved local channel has a quantitatively bounded possible effect. Its forward event need never appear naturally. Its reverse response may expose it directly; if that response also remains unobserved, the absolute upper interval still contributes to the global uncertainty budget.

That is stronger than supplying a complete generator and calculating its lifetime. It is also stronger than checking only the mechanisms that happened to appear in a calibration dataset.

The physical assumptions identify where that ambiguity has been controlled:

All admissible port inputs are reachable by preparation.
All registered outputs are observed.
Internal auxiliary pathways are summed in the measured kernel.
Reciprocity connects the suppressed direction to its accessible reverse.
The retained refresh mechanism bounds the influence of remaining absolute uncertainty.
Finite preparation errors are controlled under a specified verification model.

Unknown global faults, leakage, ageing, hidden history, interacting equilibrium laws and arbitrary symmetry-breaking noise remain outside the completed theorem.

The earned result is therefore a conditional rare-failure inference capability for an engineered physical class, with consequential correlated mechanisms demonstrated in simulation. Extending that capability to a natural material or deployed reliability system would require a comparably strong derivation of its actual observation and control interface. This work supplies one complete instance of that derivation; it does not yet establish the broader civilisational consequence.
