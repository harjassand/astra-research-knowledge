CONSTRUCTIVE COLLISION INTERFACE FOR RARE-FAILURE INFERENCE
5 November 2026

STATUS AND SCOPE

This is a mathematical construction for a specified engineered collision
architecture, not a laboratory realization or a theorem about unrestricted
natural noise. Reverse-rate inference itself is established prior art. The
candidate contribution combines finite-confidence acquisition of unknown
correlated local channels, including unseen channels, with a native independent
refresh backbone and a complete first-failure computation.

1. EXACT PHYSICAL CLASS AND DERIVATION

There are m occupation bits. x_i=1 denotes a failed/excited bit. Initially use
additive energy E(x)=sum_i Delta_i x_i, Delta_i>=0, and product Gibbs law
pi(x)=Z^-1 exp[-beta E(x)]. There is a specified list of J local collision ports.
Port j has support B_j of size k_j<=k, known attempt rate lambda_j, and a fixed
interaction pulse. Each port can be isolated, triggered once, and read at its
endpoint. Its internal scattering amplitudes are unknown.

For every collision bring a fresh ancillary copy of B_j in its Gibbs state.
The joint unitary has exact support on B_j and that ancilla, conserves combined
uncoupled energy, and obeys probability reciprocity:

  [U_j,H_B+H_A]=0,
  |<v,b|U_j|u,a>|^2=|<u,a|U_j|v,b>|^2.

The register is dephased in its occupation basis at the endpoint. Ancillas are
not reused or correlated with any past system, ancilla, or controller state.
The register has no other dynamics while ports are disabled.

A sufficient microscopic realization is one fixed pulse U=exp(-it V), with V
real symmetric in the joint occupation basis and commuting with H_B+H_A. Then
U^T=U. A generic sequence of real symmetric Hamiltonians is insufficient;
noncommuting pulse products need not be symmetric. Energy conservation alone
also does not imply general detailed balance.

The observed local kernel is

  K_j(u,v)=sum_{a,b}tau_A(a)|<v,b|U_j|u,a>|^2.

On every nonzero term, energy conservation gives
pi_B(u)tau_A(a)=pi_B(v)tau_A(b). Reciprocity and summation therefore give

  pi_B(u)K_j(u,v)=pi_B(v)K_j(v,u).

This includes ALL microscopic ancilla pathways. They need not be separately
resolved, named, or enumerated. A port can flip an arbitrary simultaneous subset
of its k_j bits. Product equilibrium does not imply independent dynamics.
Fresh ancillas and endpoint dephasing make the occupation dynamics Markov:

  Q=sum_j lambda_j(K_j-I)+Q_0.

Completeness concerns the declared architecture. It does not exclude a new
uninstrumented bath, leakage state, ageing mechanism, environmental memory,
or coupling beyond the declared port support.

2. NATIVE REFRESH BACKBONE

A full SWAP between bit i and a fresh matching ancillary bit, attempted at rate
gamma_i, gives q_i(0,1)=gamma_i p_i and q_i(1,0)=gamma_i(1-p_i), where
p_i=1/[1+exp(beta Delta_i)]. A resonant exchange pulse implements this occupation
swap; its phases are irrelevant. Define b_*=min_i gamma_i(1-p_i)>0.
Since p_i<=1/2, b_*>=min_i gamma_i/2. This is a physical clock-and-swap operation,
not an assumption that every unknown correlated channel has a fast reverse.
Unknown correlated rates may be arbitrarily small or zero.

3. EXACT DIGITAL FACTORY VIA PAIRED OCCUPATION CONSERVATION

A useful stricter implementation avoids inferring rare Gibbs populations from
occupancy data. Require U_j to conserve each matched register-plus-ancilla
number N_i=x_i+a_i individually. A fixed real symmetric Hamiltonian inside each
joint-number sector can still produce arbitrary correlated local flips. The
term exchanging |00>_S|11>_A and |11>_S|00>_A is one example.

Choose arbitrary rational 0<p_i<=1/2 and prepare independent ancillary bits with
those probabilities. Define pi(x)=product_i p_i^x_i(1-p_i)^(1-x_i). Conservation
of each N_i implies pi(x)pi(a)=pi(y)pi(b) on every permitted transition, so the
same reciprocity proof gives exact detailed balance. Actual gaps need not be
known for that identity: the pair-number symmetry conserves every additive
physical energy. Effective dimensionless energies are log[(1-p_i)/p_i].

To sample p_i=A_i/D_i exactly, sample a uniform integer 0,...,D_i-1 using unbiased
bits and rejection and output 1 iff it is below A_i. Expected random-bit work is
below 2 ceil(log2 D_i), with O(log D_i) integer storage. It is not O(1/p_i).
Perform the requested physical occupation write and verify it. This is an
engineered thermal-like reservoir, not a claim about all natural reservoirs.

For finite write/readout accuracy assume: requested writes succeed with
probability at least w_0>0; a QND sensor has independent conditional label errors
at most e<1/2; the bit stays fixed while checked; checking has no effect on later
collision dynamics; and a factory reset removes all memory. Majority voting over
r checks has error nu<=exp[-2r(1/2-e)^2]. Accept only the requested verified label
and retry otherwise. Expected attempts are at most 1/[w_0(1-nu)], and conditional
mispreparation is at most nu/[w_0(1-nu)].

Make the accepted-label error O(zeta_i p_i). Then the actual excitation
probability p_i' obeys

  |p_i'-p_i| <= (1-exp(-zeta_i))min(p_i,1-p_i),

so both actual populations are within exp(+-zeta_i) of their targets. This needs

  r=O([log(1/zeta_i)+log(1/p_i)+log(1/w_0)]/(1-2e)^2)

checks, not O(1/p_i). Each check/retry costs actual hardware time. If every port
uses the same fixed per-site factory with independent fresh draws, its ACTUAL
distribution is a shared product pi', and pair-number reciprocity holds exactly
for pi'. Also |log(pi'(x)/pi(x))|<=sum_i zeta_i.

Independent, nondemolition, nonbackacting label errors and protected idle storage
are substantive premises. A systematic sensor floor, measurement-induced flips,
factory history, or a new unobserved excitation process is not fixed by voting.

4. ONE-COLLISION ACQUISITION, INCLUDING UNSEEN CHANNELS

For each port j and each input u, prepare u; remove preparation control; bring a
fresh ancilla; trigger exactly ONE collision; dephase/read its output v; reset.
Other ports are disabled. This is not a clamped-rate extrapolation and uses no
small-dwell or multiple-jump approximation.

Orient each unordered local pair from higher to lower effective energy (ties
lexicographic). Estimate its downhill probability with n_j independent outputs
from that input. One row's observations estimate all its destinations. Let
L=sum_j 4^k_j. Hoeffding plus union bound gives simultaneous absolute entry errors
<=h_j with probability at least 1-alpha if

  n_j >= log(2L/alpha)/(2h_j^2).
  N_acq=sum_j 2^k_j ceil[log(2L/alpha)/(2h_j^2)].

Known input-preparation/output-classification TV contamination <=rho_j adds
rho_j to the entry interval. Clock-rate uncertainty is added to the rate
interval. A zero-count channel receives its upper bound; it is never silently
discarded. Infer each uphill rate from its downhill rate d_j=lambda_j K_j(u,v):

  q_j(v,u)=d_j exp[-(h(u)-h(v))].

Only the last expression is exponentially small. Any nonnegative reversible
table consistent with the intervals can be used. Local linear constraints may
also impose the stochastic row bounds. Direct empirical downhill reconstruction
still defines a valid generator if a port's inferred row sum slightly exceeds
lambda_j; uniformization then uses the correspondingly enlarged bound.

5. STRONGER LOCAL-VARIANCE UNCERTAINTY CERTIFICATE

This is the main form bound, supplied independently by the theory collaborator.
It improves the canonical-path bound below. Suppose the true and reconstructed
local generators are reversible for the same product law. Bound their local
absolute entry errors by a reversible nonnegative error generator A_j. Let

  r_j=max_u sum_{v!=u} A_j(u,v).

The largest eigenvalue of -A_j is at most 2r_j (or use the direct squared-difference
bound). Since constants are killed,

  E_Aj(f,f) <= 2r_j Var_pi_Bj(f).

Product variance tensorization bounds the right side by 2r_j times the sum of
the conditional one-bit variances in B_j. The native independent refresh form
at bit i is exactly gamma_i times that conditional variance. Consequently

  |E_Qhat(f,f)-E_Q(f,f)| <= rho E_0(f,f),
  rho=max_i [2 sum_{j:i in B_j}r_j/gamma_i].

This includes every allowed unobserved channel and needs no positive minimum
unknown rate. It remains valid for killed forms by setting f=0 on failure states.
Uniform downhill probability error h_j+rho_j gives upward error no larger
because the uphill factor is at most one, and therefore

  r_j <= (2^k_j-1)lambda_j(h_j+rho_j).

Data-dependent entry intervals can substantially improve r_j. In particular,
unseen channels should use their zero-count confidence intervals, not a claim
that their rate is zero. For lambda_loc=max_i sum_{j:i in B_j}lambda_j and
gamma_*=min_i gamma_i, common h and negligible instrument contamination, choose

  h=epsilon gamma_*/[2(2^k-1)lambda_loc].

The resulting bound is

  N_acq=O(J2^k [(2^k-1)lambda_loc/(epsilon gamma_*)]^2
             log(J4^k/alpha)).

It is exponential in physical port size k, polynomial at fixed k and bounded
local clock ratios, and has no inverse failure probability, lifetime factor,
or inverse unknown-channel rate. Costs of specifying/sampling the p_i and
readout precision remain explicit.

6. INDEPENDENT CANONICAL-PATH DERIVATION (WEAKER, OPTIONAL)

Use E_Q(f,f)=(1/2)sum pi(x)q_xy(f(y)-f(x))^2. Route a local transition through
x -> x AND y -> y: turn differing 1s off first, then turn differing 0s on, in a
fixed order. Every intermediate energy is at most max(h(x),h(y)). Any backbone
edge e along this <=k_j-step path has conductance

  c_0(e)>=b_* min(pi(x),pi(y)).

The uncertain port conductance is at most lambda_j(h_j+rho_j)min(pi(x),pi(y)).
Cauchy-Schwarz gives a path-length factor k_j. For a fixed backbone edge at site
i and a fixed changed support S containing i, at most 2^(|S|-1) unordered
endpoint pairs differ on S and could use that edge. Summing over supports within
B_j gives sum_{S contains i}2^(|S|-1)=3^(k_j-1). Therefore

  |E_Qhat-E_Q| <= kappa E_0,
  kappa<=(1/b_*)max_i sum_{j:i in B_j}
                k_j3^(k_j-1)lambda_j(h_j+rho_j).

This is an independent proof of lifetime-independent control of unseen local
channels. The local-variance bound is quantitatively better and should lead the
main theorem. Both rely on product equilibrium and a physically available
independent refresh backbone; neither is generic for interacting equilibrium
weights or arbitrary nonthermal common-cause faults.

7. FINITE COLLISION PULSES AND WALL-CLOCK TIME

Use a serial event scheduler. Let Lambda sum all attempt rates, including
backbone ports. Draw port j independently with probability lambda_j/Lambda,
wait an independent Exp(Lambda) time, execute its fixed-duration pulse and
endpoint operations of total duration t_j, then continue. Pause all other
ports while busy. The register has no additional idle dynamics. Factory,
reset and readout overhead paid serially can all be included in t_j.

Let N be the first collision endpoint satisfying the Boolean failure rule.
The ideal Poisson-chain mean is M_CTMC=E[N]/Lambda. Wald's identity gives

  M_lab=[1/Lambda+sum_j(lambda_j/Lambda)t_j]E[N]
       =(1+sum_j lambda_j t_j)M_CTMC.

The event type and wait at event n are independent of survival through events
1,...,n-1. The last event causing failure does not invalidate Wald's identity.
If duration depends on input/outcome, use a state-dependent holding-reward
calculation or conservative min/max duration bounds. Failure during a coherent
pulse is not the registered endpoint Boolean failure considered here. A
different observation convention requires another model.

8. COMPLETE RESOURCE AND ERROR BILL

The acquisition wall-clock bill is

  T_acq=sum_j 2^k_j n_j
       [t_prepare,j+t_factory,j+t_collision,j+t_dephase,j+t_read,j+t_reset,j]
       + clock/factory/energy metrology costs.

Each trial prepares at most k_j system bits, uses k_j fresh matching ancilla
bits in the simple implementation, triggers one addressed collision, applies
k_j dephasings/random-Z operations, and reads k_j output labels. Exact rational
factory randomness costs less than 2 sum_{i in B_j}ceil(log2 D_i) bits per
factory attempt. Verification and retry factors are given in section 3.
A forced input write can inject at most sum_{i in B_j}Delta_i into the register,
in addition to actual control and reset dissipation. No claim of minimum
dissipation is made. Clocks and pulse durations require their actual timing
metrology; infinitely accurate event timing is not assumed.

For a truly thermal factory, gap/temperature uncertainty can be stated as
|delta(beta Delta_i)|<=eta_i. This gives
|log(pihat(x)/pi(x))|<=sum_i eta_i. Balanced gate-bias populations or direct
spectroscopy can acquire gaps without waiting for rare excitations, but
physical thermal equilibrium and reciprocity remain substantive conditions.

Separate two error types:

(i) Errors preserving support, a common product factory, reciprocity, and
fresh-reset structure change the unknown K_j and are directly acquired.

(ii) An unmodelled nonthermal/symmetry-breaking transition of probability nu
per actual operating cycle can add an order-Lambda*nu hazard. For an independent
catastrophe clock, limiting relative mean-lifetime error to epsilon requires
nu=O(epsilon/(Lambda M)). There is no short-exposure shortcut unless that process
also has an accessible symmetry/reverse certificate or a derived domination
bound.

A generic small ancilla trace-distance error has the second problem: an excess
excited population 10^-9 can overwhelm a desired 10^-24 population. Shared
product factory errors with controlled per-population multiplicative bounds
have the first structure, plus a stated product-weight uncertainty. Readout
majority voting cannot eliminate an unknown systematic floor or non-QND noise.

9. EXPLICIT TWO-BIT UNKNOWN-COMMON-CAUSE EXAMPLE

Two redundant bits are independently refreshed at rate gamma with equilibrium
failure probability p. Failure is first registered state 11. An unknown pair
port of rate lambda mixes |00>_S|11>_A with |11>_S|00>_A with probability
s=sin^2(theta), leaving other joint basis states fixed. It obeys paired-number
conservation and reciprocity.

Its failure rate is c=lambda s p^2 and reverse rate lambda s(1-p)^2. With
a=gamma p and b=gamma(1-p), grouping 01 and 10 gives

  (2a+c)M0-2a M1=1,
  -b M0+(a+b)M1=1,

hence, with repairs continuing,

  M=(1+2p)/[p^2(2gamma+lambda s)].

An unknown pair port with lambda s=2gamma halves the lifetime while leaving
stationary pi exactly unchanged. At p=2^-40, gamma=1/s, lambda=4/s, s=1/2,

  natural pair hazard =1.6543612251060553e-24/s,
  true M              =3.0223145490420705e23 s,
  independent-only M  =6.044629098084141e23 s.

Preparing register 11 makes the observed reverse probability s(1-p)^2,
approximately one half. A 95% Hoeffding interval of radius .01 for s requires
18,445 forced reverse trials, independent of p's rarity.

The reproducible SIMULATION in physical_collision_example.py uses exact
rational Bernoulli probabilities and seed 20261105. It produced 9,312 reverse
returns in 18,445 trials, yielding the 95% interval

  s in [.49485242687386144,.5148521001000543],
  M in [2.9780837510649093e23,3.037952632370945e23] s,
  mean upper/lower ratio =1.020103155690174.

The true mean lies inside. This is simulation, not laboratory evidence. The
two-state exact solver is standard, not the novelty claim. Per-trial hardware
duration, factory cost, and finite-pulse slowdown are explicit inputs.

11. FINITE FACTORY ERRORS WITHOUT AN ACTUAL PRODUCT DISTRIBUTION

This extension closes finite factory errors under the stated independent QND
verification and protected-idle assumptions. Unlike section 3's simpler case,
the actual ancillary bits may have arbitrary within-batch correlations. The
actual collision process need not be reversible. It is enough that factories
reset between uses so that a fixed Markov operating kernel results. An explicit
complete occupation dephasing is applied to the ancilla before its collision.

Let tau(a) be the exactly digitally sampled ideal product probability for a
k_j-bit ancillary pattern. Let p_min=min_i p_i>0, with p_i<=1/2, so
tau(a)>=p_min^k_j>=p_min^k. Suppose the accepted physical write of every requested
bit is wrong with probability at most xi, uniformly over the desired pattern.
The ideal digital pattern A and actual prepared pattern A' can be coupled using
the same requested label. A union bound gives

  Pr(A'!=A)<=k_j xi<=k xi.

No independence of the bit errors is needed in this step. Therefore each pattern
probability obeys |tau_actual(a)-tau(a)|<=k xi. Taking

  xi <= zeta p_min^k/k

gives the multiplicative sandwich

  (1-zeta)tau(a)<=tau_actual(a)<=(1+zeta)tau(a).

The actual distribution need not be product. For a fixed U, each register
transition probability is a nonnegative linear combination of these ancillary
probabilities. Thus every offdiagonal rate, including backbone rates, obeys

  (1-zeta)q_xy <= q_actual,xy <= (1+zeta)q_xy.

The transition support is unchanged. This conclusion needs no actual detailed
balance. Ancillary coherences must not be silently ignored: a mere label-error
bound xi can allow O(sqrt(xi)) coherent amplitude. Explicit dephasing gives the
classical argument above; alternatively use the stronger trace/operator error
bound, obtainable with xi=O(zeta^2 p_min^(2k)/k), at twice the logarithmic
verification precision order.

GENERIC KILLED-CHAIN SENSITIVITY. Let B contain n safe states and collapse all
failed states to one absorbing root. A=-Q_B is a nonsingular M-matrix because
the native refresh backbone makes every state communicate with the nonempty
failure set. The directed matrix-tree formula expresses det A as a positive
degree-n polynomial in transition rates: each term is a directed spanning tree
rooted at the absorbing state. Cramer's rule and the all-minors tree theorem
express each numerator (A^-1 1)_x det A as a positive degree-(n-1) forest
polynomial: the forest has roots at the absorbing state and one safe state j,
and x belongs to j's component. Summing over j preserves positivity.

Ratewise multiplication by numbers in [1-zeta,1+zeta] therefore implies

 (1-zeta)^(n-1)/(1+zeta)^n
       <= M_actual(x)/M_ideal(x)
       <= (1+zeta)^(n-1)/(1-zeta)^n.

In particular,

 |log(M_actual/M_ideal)| <= (2n-1)[-log(1-zeta)].

This applies to an arbitrary initial safe state and arbitrary nonempty failure
rule; neither reversibility nor independence of the actual generator is needed.
Killing rates, which sum transitions into the failed set, obey the same bounds.
For n<=2^m and 0<epsilon<=1, zeta=epsilon/(4*2^m) is sufficient for logarithmic
mean error below epsilon. Allocate a smaller constant fraction if other error
sources also consume the accuracy budget.

WHY THIS DOES NOT REQUIRE EXPONENTIAL VERIFICATION EXPOSURE. Under the explicitly
independent QND label-error model, accepted-write error xi is exponentially small
in the number of repeated checks. The target above requires

 log(1/xi)=O(m+k log(1/p_min)+log(k/epsilon)).

Thus the number of checks is

 O([m+k log(1/p_min)+log(k/epsilon)+log(1/w_0)]/(1-2e)^2),

not O(2^m) or O(1/p_min^k). This is conditional on a known error bound e<1/2,
independence, exact complete dephasing, a nonbackacting sensor, and protected
idle storage. An unknown systematic floor or uncontrolled operational noise
does not satisfy this exponential error-suppression model.

FINITE WORST-CASE SERVICE TIME. Cap each write/verify retry count at R. A correct
accepted write occurs per attempt with probability at least w_0/2 for majority
error <=1/2. Therefore timeout probability is at most exp(-Rw_0/2). Choose R so
that the entire port's timeout probability is <=zeta/4 uniformly in the
requested pattern. This costs R=O([log(k/zeta)]/w_0) attempts per bit. Cap the
exact integer-rejection sampler similarly: acceptance is at least 1/2 each
round, so O(log(k/zeta)) rounds suffice for the same uniform timeout budget.

On any factory timeout, execute a NULL collision (identity) at that clock
attempt. Do not condition the physical attempt clock on success or redraw a
new desired pattern for free. Pad all branches to a fixed port service time.
With xi<=zeta p_min^k/(4k), the successful-pattern subprobabilities and null
timeouts together keep all offdiagonal rates within 1+-zeta of the ideal after
allocating the stated constant error fractions. One way to see this is that
each desired pattern has timeout probability <=zeta/4, contributing a relative
loss of at most zeta/4, while all wrong-pattern mass is <=kxi<=zeta tau_min/4.
The lower and upper pointwise pattern bounds then each fit inside 1+-zeta.

Worst-case port service time is polynomial. Its write/check part scales as

 O(k [log(k/zeta)]/w_0
       [t_write+t_check log(k/(xi w_0))/(1-2e)^2]),

plus capped random sampling, actual collision, dephasing, reset, and endpoint
readout. This can have a quadratic m dependence, but not exponential exposure.
The exact finite-pulse law now applies to the actual generator:

  M_actual,lab=(1+sum_j lambda_j t_j)M_actual,CTMC.

Without padding, the same Wald identity holds with mean service times if fresh
factory service/ancilla marks are iid independently of the state before the
event. Correlation between a service duration and that event's ancilla or final
outcome is allowed. State-dependent or history-dependent service requires
separate holding-time bounds.

BOUNDARIES OF THE CLOSURE. This closes the factory-imperfection contribution,
not arbitrary pulse errors, idle flips, hidden baths, or false declarations of
failure by a noisy observer. The forecast target remains the true occupation
state at an endpoint. Acquisition-label errors are already part of the
additive experimental confidence bands. A noisy operational failure indicator
is a different observable and must not be substituted without accounting for
its false-positive hazard. Actual loss of dephasing or hidden environment
memory invalidates the fixed finite-state Markov premise used by the tree
formula unless an additional conditional-rate argument is supplied.

12. FULLY DIGITAL FIXED-TICK ALTERNATIVE

The implementation need not synthesize ideal exponential waiting times. Choose
a fixed tick duration T at least the largest padded port/factory duration.
At each tick sample a port index with specified rational weights w_j and execute
that port, padding to T. Include an identity/null index if necessary. Exact
integer sampling produces the port mark; sampler timeouts can be handled as
null ticks with the preceding error budget. The kernel is P=sum_j w_j K_j.

Define the ANALYTICAL generator Q=(P-I)/T. For the first failed endpoint at tick
N, the discrete first-step equation is (I-P_B)E[N]=1. Thus

  M_lab=T E[N]=(-Q_B)^-1 1

exactly. The actual trajectory is a discrete-clock process; the CTMC is an
equivalent mean-hitting-time computation, not a claim about its continuous
path law. Detailed balance, Dirichlet-form comparison and tree/forest sensitivity
all apply to the analytical Q. Native refresh rates are gamma_i=w_i/T.

This removes any need for infinite-precision exponential timing. Actual clock
tolerance gives a direct multiplicative time uncertainty. Serial throughput is
explicit: sum_i gamma_i<=1/T before allocating unknown ports, so keeping a
fixed per-site refresh rate while m grows would require parallel resources or
a faster tick. Neither resource is free. For mean first failure alone, this is
the cleanest fully finite execution model.
