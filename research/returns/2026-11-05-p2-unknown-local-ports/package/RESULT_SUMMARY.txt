INTERVENTIONS FOR UNKNOWN LOCAL RARE-FAILURE MECHANISMS
Completed mathematical construction, 5 November 2026

QUESTION
Can preparation/control expose otherwise unobserved failure mechanisms and give
a finite relative confidence interval for a very long system lifetime?

EARNED CLASS
A clocked register of m binary components has a constructed/independently
calibrated product invariant law pi. An independent native refresh backbone
with rates gamma_i remains active in operation. Each additional unknown port
acts only on a declared block S_alpha of at most k bits, with no outside-state
dependence, fresh ancillary memory and reciprocal transition amplitudes. Its
positive transition support is not assumed known. The failure rule is known;
the polynomial computation specializes to all-terminal graph disconnection.

PHYSICAL INTERFACE
Prepare every local input, remove preparation control, apply one actual native
port collision, and read the local output. Each unordered pair is measured
from its lower-stationary-weight endpoint; detailed balance supplies the other
direction. Positive upper intervals are retained for zero counts. Unknown
microscopic routes between the same local endpoints are aggregated.

MAIN UNKNOWN-SUPPORT THEOREM
The absolute local rate errors define a reversible error envelope H_alpha.
Let r_alpha be its maximum exit rate. Then simultaneously for every full-state
function f,

 |E_true(f,f)-E_fit(f,f)| <= rho E_backbone(f,f),
 rho=max_i [2 sum_{alpha containing i} r_alpha/gamma_i].

Proof: E_Halpha<=2 r_alpha Var_block, followed by conditional product variance
tensorization. A uniform downhill rate radius delta_alpha gives
r_alpha<=(2^|S_alpha|-1)delta_alpha. This does not require identical additional
transition support, a positive lower bound on every channel, or observing rare
forward failures. The operational backbone is indispensable to this bound.

FIRST-FAILURE UNCERTAINTY
For killed conductance Laplacian L and fixed safe x0,
M_x0=e_x0^T L^{-1}pi_A. If rho<1,

 |M_true/M_fit-1| <= [rho/(1-rho)] B_x0.

If pi(x0)>=kappa, full gap>=gamma, and R is a lazy uniformization bound,
g=gamma/R gives B_x0<=4/(sqrt(g) kappa). In the rare regime
lambda_killed/gamma<=kappa/16, B_x0<=2/sqrt(kappa). Product-weight and backbone
calibration have a separate proved conductance/mass envelope. There is no
all-deadline relative CDF claim.

COMPUTATION
Karger's all-terminal static FPRAS and the supplied P2 conditional failure
sampler still apply because pi remains product despite correlated kinetics.
Short reverse return-avoidance trials estimate p E[Z], with useful-trial
probability >=g(1-2xi). A new spectral starting-state correction handles the
absence of monotone coupling. Rare and nonrare branches have polynomial work
in graph/table size, 2^k, g^-1, kappa^-1, accuracy^-1 and log confidence^-1.
The generic Karger component is invoked, not implemented here.

A distinct prepared equilibrium-safe initial law pi(.|A) removes kappa from
the rare branch; when pi(B)<=1/2 it is sampled by at most two independent
configuration draws on average. It is not the all-working initialization.

FINITE PHYSICAL IMPLEMENTATION
Fresh Gibbs ancillas plus energy conservation AND reciprocal amplitudes imply
local detailed balance. A stricter paired-occupation-conserving collision
allows rational digital ancillary probabilities: exact Bernoulli bit cost is
logarithmic in denominators. Fixed finite ticks, null timeouts and padding
give P=sum_j w_j K_j and analytical Q=(P-I)/T. The actual first failed endpoint
mean is exactly T E[N]=(-Q_A)^-1 1. No instantaneous-pulse or continuous
intra-pulse safety claim is made; serial throughput and full service time are
paid.

FINITE FACTORY ERROR
Under explicit independent, nondemolition, nonbackacting verification and
protected-idle assumptions, tiny prepared-label error needs logarithmically
many repeated checks. Dephased ancillary distributions within relative zeta
of the desired product give all operating rates within 1+-zeta, even if actual
ancillary bits are correlated and reversibility is slightly broken. A positive
tree/forest bound for n safe states gives log mean error at most
(2n-1)[-log(1-zeta)]. Set zeta=O(epsilon/2^m). Verification effort remains
polynomial: O(m+k log(1/p_min)+log(k/epsilon)) checks up to sensor and retry
factors. This covers ancillary mispreparation, not arbitrary extra noise.

EXECUTED EVIDENCE
Two-bit example: unknown cooperative port halves the independent prediction.
Four million represented collision trials; no forward cooperative event;
99% ideal mean interval [2.9261012,3.1177482]e23 contains the true
3.022314549e23 in native-refresh model time units.
K4 example: four overlapping three-bit ports, all 28 unordered pairs per port
admitted. 256 million represented collision trials; no forward cooperative
event; 99% ideal interval [5.6122300,5.9168462]e16 contains true
5.764615769e16. Independent prediction is 1.666665 times too large.
These are directly sampled multinomial counts and high-precision small-state
solves, not physical experiments or that many executed simulated trajectories.
The exact conventional small-state solver is the better computational method
for these examples.

BOUNDARIES AND PRIOR ART
Reverse-kinetics inference, TAD, collision models, form comparison, tree/forest
formulas and Karger's counting are prior machinery. Candidate addition is the
full finite observation/control interface, zero-support-tolerant global risk
bound, and succinct computation with explicitly paid costs. Historical priority
and practical advantage remain unestablished. No civilization-changing device
has been demonstrated. Arbitrary hidden baths, leakage, larger ports, ageing,
memory, systematic sensor floors, and unobserved in-pulse failures remain
outside the class; silence does not exclude an independent catastrophe clock.
