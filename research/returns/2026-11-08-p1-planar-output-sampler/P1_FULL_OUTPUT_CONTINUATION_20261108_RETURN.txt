## 3. Full sampling needs less than pointwise control of every rare prefix

A positive resolution need not construct uniformly accurate relative estimates of every exponentially small prefix probability.

For an arbitrary output distribution \(p\), let \(c_i(\cdot\mid x_{<i})\) be its exact sequential kernels. If a sampler uses kernels \(k_i\), telescoping the stochastic kernels gives
\[
\boxed{
\left\|p-\prod_i k_i\right\|_{\rm TV}
\le
\sum_i
\mathbb E_{X_{<i}\sim p}
\left[
\left\|
c_i(\cdot\mid X_{<i})
-k_i(\cdot\mid X_{<i})
\right\|_{\rm TV}
\right].
}
\]

The expectation is over prefixes actually drawn from \(p\). Rare prefixes can consume correspondingly little error budget. Thus the earlier additive-marginal division obstruction does not establish that full sampling must pay inverse probabilities on every prefix.

There is also a useful exact conditional-information identity. Choose \(B_i\subseteq\{1,\ldots,i-1\}\), and define
\[
q(x)=\prod_i p(x_i\mid x_{B_i}).
\]
With natural logarithms,
\[
D(p\|q)
=
\sum_i
I_p\!\left(
X_i:X_{<i}\setminus B_i\mid X_{B_i}
\right).
\]
Consequently,
\[
\|p-q\|_{\rm TV}
\le
\sqrt{\frac12
\sum_i I_p\!\left(
X_i:X_{<i}\setminus B_i\mid X_{B_i}
\right)}.
\]

This identifies one sufficient route: prove a summable conditional-information bound and construct the required kernels at polynomial cost.

Neither operation has been established here for arbitrary \(H,\rho_0\). Unconditioned locality estimates do not directly establish the conditional-information bound. A compact representation of the unconditioned state does not automatically make its conditional kernels cheap to compute. These are remaining proof obligations for this route, not extra premises and not necessary conditions on every possible sampler.

## 4. Why the checked current sources do not settle the endpoint

### Discrete unital-circuit sampling

Nelson et al.’s theorem retains a fixed layer-noise parameter and a depth threshold. Its stated parameter dependence is not uniform under a fine continuous-time discretization. [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00548v2?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Indeed, for a step \(\Delta\),
\[
p_\Delta=1-e^{-\gamma\Delta}\sim\gamma\Delta,
\qquad
d_\Delta\asymp T/\Delta.
\]
Although \(p_\Delta d_\Delta\) remains of order \(\gamma T\), the theorem’s threshold and runtime also depend on \(p_\Delta\) separately. Holding \(\gamma/J\) fixed does not hold \(p_\Delta\) fixed. Replacing one with the other would change the argument.

### Mixed-state tensor-network bounds

The current Shao–Zhao–Cheng–Liu paper gives a constructive purified-distance result in one dimension. Appendix F explicitly limits its higher-dimensional conclusions to cutwise rank and average boundary-bond estimates; it does not construct efficiently contractible PEPO tensors. [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.00474v2?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The norm conversion remains substantive:
\[
\operatorname{TV}(\operatorname{diag}\rho,\operatorname{diag}\sigma)
\le
\frac12\|\rho-\sigma\|_1
\le
\frac12\,2^{n/2}\|\rho-\sigma\|_2.
\]
A fixed absolute Hilbert–Schmidt tolerance therefore does not supply fixed TV accuracy. Relative Hilbert–Schmidt accuracy also requires an appropriate purity or flatness estimate before this inequality becomes useful operationally.

### Noiseless Hamiltonian hardness

Quek’s proved high-precision counting result, the stronger precision conjecture, and the sampling-to-evaluation reduction remain distinct. They do not establish hardness under fixed positive continuous replacement depolarization. [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06321v1?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

For the native generator, the exact Pauli-jump representation has total jump rate \(3n\gamma/4\). Hence
\[
p_\gamma
=
e^{-3n\gamma T/4}p_0
+
\left(1-e^{-3n\gamma T/4}\right)r
\]
for another output law \(r\).

This identity does not give an efficient extraction of the noiseless distribution. Its no-jump weight is exponentially small at fixed positive \(\gamma T\). Requesting exponentially small \(\epsilon\) also would not itself contradict the proposed complexity bound, which explicitly permits polynomial dependence on \(\epsilon^{-1}\).

### Strong-noise and fault-tolerance results

The retained strong-noise sampling results cover their stated parameter section. The Trivedi–Cirac hardness constructions use non-unital cooling, programmed interactions or additional dissipative resources and different output contracts. Those resources cannot be imported into this static-H, replacement-noise-only class. [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.10638v2?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
