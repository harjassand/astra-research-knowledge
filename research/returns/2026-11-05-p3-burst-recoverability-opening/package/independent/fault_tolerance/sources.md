# Primary source record

These are comparator sources, not evidence that the proposed sufficiency theorem is proved. URLs were inspected on 2026-11-05. Root should open the desired sources itself before citing their web-reference IDs.

1. Peter Gacs, Reliable computation with cellular automata (1986).
   https://www.cs.bu.edu/faculty/gacs/papers/GacsReliableCA86.pdf
   Scope: reliable computation is existing machinery.

2. Peter Gacs, Reliable Cellular Automata with Self-Organization; corrected and strengthened arXiv version, 2024; original journal paper 2001.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0003117
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0003117
   https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1004823720305
   Scope: asynchronous one-dimensional fault-tolerant self-organizing hierarchy. Latest abstract explicitly states that the initial configuration need not encode an infinite hierarchy.

3. Jan M. Swart, Renata Szabo, Cristina Toninelli, Peierls Bounds from Toom Contours (2026).
   https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10959-026-01508-2
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13161273/
   Earlier author version: https://staff.utia.cas.cz/swart/papers/Toom_Peierls_2022_02_22.pdf
   Scope: deterministic and intrinsic-randomness cellular-automaton stability via Toom contours; not the proposed marked-burst finite-seed theorem.

4. A. L. Toom, Stable and attractive trajectories in multicomponent systems (1980), Multicomponent Random Systems, Advances in Probability and Related Topics 6, 549-575.
   Primary author archive: https://cs-web.bu.edu/faculty/gacs/toomandre-com-backup/my-articles/engmat/index.html
   Scope: foundational eroder/stability method. Do not confuse its local-stochastic noise condition with arbitrary unbounded spatial burst laws.

5. Sergey Foss, Takis Konstantopoulos, Thomas Mountford, Power law condition for stability of Poisson hail.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.0911
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1410.0911
   https://doi.org/10.1007/s10959-016-0723-3
   Journal of Theoretical Probability 31 (2018).
   Theorem 1: finite d+1+epsilon moments of service time and diameter imply small-arrival-intensity stability.

6. Thomas Mountford, Zhe Wang, Stable Systems with Power Law Conditions for Poisson Hail.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.07406
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.07406
   https://doi.org/10.1017/apr.2023.23
   Advances in Applied Probability 56(1), 315-353 (2024; online 2023).
   Scope: more complete moment regions up to boundaries; includes infinite propagation cases. Workload stability is not automatically information recovery.

7. Francois Baccelli, Sergey Foss, Poisson hail on a hot ground (2011).
   https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-applied-probability/article/poisson-hail-on-a-hot-ground/91D96533DCACE7702983E1D0DDA35089
   Scope: origin of spatially overlapping-job workload model.

8. Jean-Baptiste Gouere, Subcritical regimes in the Poisson Boolean model of continuum percolation (2008).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0611369
   Scope: finite dimension-th radius moment and subcritical Boolean percolation. Requires a valid bridge before applying to interacting repair dynamics.

9. Nicolas Lanchier, Contact Process with Destruction of Cubes and Hyperplanes: Forest Fires Versus Tornadoes (2011).
   https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-applied-probability/article/contact-process-with-destruction-of-cubes-and-hyperplanes-forest-fires-versus-tornadoes/AF05B57DBFBCE078ED3969E1198AF992
   https://doi.org/10.1239/jap/1308662632
   Journal of Applied Probability 48, 352-365.
   Scope: proves catastrophe geometry matters beyond a per-site death-rate comparison. Does not supply unflagged fault-tolerant unknown-information storage.

## Literature caution

This search supports the stated comparators and identifies a substantive gap between their conclusions and the proposed full operation. It is not an exhaustive proof of historical novelty. In particular, the d+1 geometry also appears in hail and continuum percolation; the selected consequence must include the actual information-preserving local construction and sharp resource contract.
