Candidate considered: a full entropy/energy law for reliable useful computation
with faulty local components, potentially replacing the previous common-support
hazard premise by locality and bounded physical resources.

Decisive elementary obstruction

For N simultaneous component opportunities let Z be Bernoulli(p). Compare:
  independent faults: F_i are independent Bernoulli(p);
  common burst: F_i=Z for every i.
Every single-component marginal is identical, while
  P(all N faulty)=p^N versus p.
Thus each marginal fault bound supplies no exponential suppression of fault sets.
Local finite coupling alone does not imply initial independence: pre-correlated
local timers can trigger together without post-preparation long-range signals.
Bounded energy density permits a total burst cost proportional to N. A total
energy budget independent of N is stronger; a damage cost must itself be earned
before using that premise to exclude a burst.

Candidate control mechanism and its missing operation

Independent private scheduling samples oblivious bad-time sets. If gate i samples
U_i uniformly on a frame, and its predetermined bad-start set B_i occupies at most
p of the frame, then for every finite S,
  P(U_i in B_i for every i in S) <= p^|S|.
This earns a joint local-stochastic bound. However, useful computation stores live
data between gates. Sampling gate start times does not protect storage, pay for
synchronization, or prevent an environment responding to actual control signals.
A perfect off/holding state would insert part of the desired reliable operation.
Randomized decoupling and fault-tolerant compilation are existing comparators.
This elementary sampling lemma does not establish a new computer.

Literature kill-test

Gacs (1986) already constructs reliable computation with faulty local components
and self-repairing organization. Asynchronous constructions (1991 and 2001)
already address the absence of an ideal global clock.
https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-0000(86)90002-4
https://doi.org/10.1109/SFCS.1991.185379
https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1004823720305

Taati (2021) proves rapid forgetting for reversible/surjective cellular automata
under positive additive noise. Claiming a new generic necessity of irreversibility
for scalable reliable computation would therefore fail.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.00725
https://doi.org/10.4230/OASIcs.AUTOMATA.2021.3

Minimal-work and finite-time-computation results also prevent treating an erasure
entropy bound alone as a new thermodynamic computer architecture.
https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms8669
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-36020-2
