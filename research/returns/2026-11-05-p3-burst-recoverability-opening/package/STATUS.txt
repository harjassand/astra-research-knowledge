SCIENTIFIC STATUS - QUESTION-SELECTION ROUND, 2026-11-05

SELECTED QUESTION
Is alpha > d+1 the sharp low-intensity boundary for uniformly reliable,
finite-seed local information recovery under spatial Poisson burst corruption
with radius tail P(R>r)=r^(-alpha), alpha>d, when commissioning, all mutable
control state, finite physical readout, hardware and execution are included?
The two-dimensional case alpha=3 is the first concrete construction target.

The core target permits a uniform family of finite-horizon local operations.
Initial input-dependent seed extent is fixed independently of the horizon.
Finite input-independent hardware may be designed for that horizon and is
charged in full. Autonomous self-fabrication and translation invariance are
not imposed as unnecessary extra requirements. A reliable immutable local
update law and bounded-rate input-independent schedule are declared model
premises; an apparatus must additionally realize them. All mutable decoding
and control registers are in the burst-fault support.

PROVED OPENING
- Exact architecture-independent causal-cone erasure probability, total
  variation contraction and binary recovery-error lower bound.
- Finite d-th radius moment with infinite (d+1)-st moment forces forgetting.
- Exact Pareto coefficients, critical logarithm, necessary support-growth
  exponent, initial-extent cost, and fixed-device lifetime ceiling.
- Linear isolated-error erosion for the NEC Toom rule and exact failure of
  superposing independent recovery regions.
- Exact scale of large bursts intersecting a spacetime repair block.
- Finite iid radius samples cannot certify the moment class without tail
  assumptions. This is an acquisition limit, not an estimated physical law.

CONDITIONAL OR OPEN
- The two-separated-bad-subblocks implication for an actual local decoder.
- The proposed multiscale recurrence and matching recovery bound.
- Finite-seed recovery with corruptible metadata and charged local readout.
- Positive sufficiency at alpha>d+1 or at the general finite (d+1)-moment
  endpoint; neither is established here.
- A physical platform realizing the entire burst, control and metrology
  contract, historical priority, or a demonstrated civilisational capability.

ACCURACY CONVENTION
The minimum target is a fixed nontrivial recovery advantage (for example
total binary error at most 1/4) at every finite requested time. It does not
promise infinitely many readouts are jointly correct. Any stronger accuracy
target includes seed and output-interface costs. A fixed bare input has an
early-loss floor; a fixed noisy final output cell has a readout-error floor.

PRESERVATION
All prior productive-control, repair, direct acquired-prevention, grouped-wait,
mean-only initialization, interacting Gibbs and first-event work is preserved
byte for byte in the complete preceding package. No physical reduction from
those classes or P2's loaded-LTI preparation class to this burst model is
assumed. Independent rejected constructions are retained separately.

INDEPENDENCE
Selection was based on independent internal derivations and public primary
literature, without consulting other Pro researchers' new tournament proposals.
