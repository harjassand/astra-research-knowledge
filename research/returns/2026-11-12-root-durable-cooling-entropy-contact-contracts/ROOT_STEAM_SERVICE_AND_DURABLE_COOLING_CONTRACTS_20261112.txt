One concrete optional mechanism
Two mechanically opposed active beds could load and unload in counterphase through a direct crank/cam or other reversible force path, while separate fluid manifolds alternate heat rejection and useful cooling. Buckling support, bearings, motion guidance, thermal isolation and finite valve/heat-transfer paths have to coexist. The recovered work must physically reach the bed being loaded or the electrical boundary.
A low-loss regenerative arrangement must transport useful heat while limiting warm/cold fluid mixing, dead-volume heat shuttling, contact resistance, parasitic solid heat capacity and friction. Existing opposed drives and active regeneration are credited concepts, not a new chiller by arrangement names.
Bending or rolling can reduce actuator force, but changes transformed volume, strain gradients, available phase heat, contact area and material preparation. A few-gram low-force result is not a free force reduction for the same10kW thermodynamic point. All material/loading/working-fluid representations remain open.
