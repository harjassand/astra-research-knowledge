Internal work cannot be chosen independently of heat ports
Specialize to one material loop whose net cold heat q=5kJ/kg/cycle enters isothermally at4C and whose hot heat leaves isothermally atTh. The second law gives
  w_internal >= q*(Th/Tc-1), Tc=277.15K.
If loading is3kJ/kg, recoverable unloading cannot exceed3-w_internal. At40Chot this reversible ceiling is2.350532kJ/kg, rather than2.4; the0.6kJ nominal loading-minus-unloading would be0.049468kJ below this specialized thermodynamic minimum. At45Chot, minimum internal work is0.739672 and maximum unloading2.260328kJ/kg. Actual hysteresis, heat-transfer entropy and incomplete phase conversion lower those unloading ceilings further.
A motor, bearing or external coupling loss cannot repair an internally impossible material cycle by heating an unrelated part of the apparatus. The active loop needs its own heat/work closure. Conversely, these isothermal bounds must not be applied to a distributed regenerative bed while ignoring its many local heat ports. Its integral of deltaQ/T, material state restoration and useful net cold output are the governing quantities.

Finite solid contact: a deliberately limited surrogate
An apparent-capacity slab diagnostic uses INTRODUCED density6450kg/m3, conductivity15W/m/K, basecp500J/kg/K and10000J/kg phase heat spread across an introduced4Kwindow. Its apparentcp is3000J/kg/K and thermal diffusivity7.75194e-7m2/s. These are not claimed NiTi measurements. The originally exposed1.43mmstock thickness is a geometric starting point, not a transfer of its microstructure to a new strip.
For a symmetric slab with both faces suddenly exposed to fixed fluid temperature and constant film h, the analytic mean response uses roots mu*tan(mu)=Bi and the usual symmetric diffusion modes. The program solves time to90%mean step response. At1.43mm, even infiniteh gives0.5593s; h5000/10000W/m2/K gives6.8818/3.6998s. At0.1mm, h10000 gives0.2253s, compatible with a quarter-second thermal blow only within this surrogate. At1Hz a quarter-cycle blow is0.25s; this timing choice is introduced, not mandatory.
Apparentcp is not a moving phase-front/stress-state/adiabatic-reset model. No cycle rate or useful heat is established by this step test. A real stress-driven phase pattern, finite fluid heat capacity, dead volumes and contact geometry can differ. These results expose why simultaneously quoting bulk phase heat, high frequency and finite contact is a new construction rather than a supplied material property.

Compression support is not free
For an introduced pinned weak-axis strip withE50GPa at500MPacompression, elastic Euler scaling gives maximum unsupported length/t=pi*sqrt(E/(12*stress))=9.069. Thus0.1mmstock needs a support pitch no larger than0.907mm in this simplified screen;1.43mm needs12.969mm. The unbraced50mm examples buckle below the target stress under those assumptions. This is not a statement about the exposed bending experiment, phase-dependent modulus or a completed constrained device. Lateral guides, tension, curvature, different cross section or other material can change the mechanism. Their friction, thermal shorting, displaced flow area, fabrication, contact and fatigue costs remain paid.

Constructive freedom and remaining operation
A spatially staged bed with finite separately routed hot/cold flows and direct force return could replace the overly restrictive two-isothermal-port picture. Thin active sections could share low-loss support or use another strain geometry. Such changes need an actual periodic field, useful heat and work integral, flow network, force path and manufacturable service interface. The calculations supply no mechanism that already meets all those conditions. They do not set a new reporting or reproof requirement or authorize changing an active scientific question.
