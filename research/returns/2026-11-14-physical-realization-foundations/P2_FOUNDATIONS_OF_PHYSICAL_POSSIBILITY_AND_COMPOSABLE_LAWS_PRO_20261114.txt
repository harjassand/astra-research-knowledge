Can a new foundational principle explain which laws of physical response finite matter can realize, and make those laws predictively and constructively composable?

The scientific destination is a new way of understanding physical possibility that earns substantial explanatory, predictive or constructive consequences. The basic assumption to challenge is that a desired behavior, an abstract transformation, or a closed equation for selected variables already specifies a physically meaningful operation independently of its realization and its interactions with the rest of the world. Investigate whether different primitive concepts can close that gap. Here is a concrete opening. A collection of local physical elements may have many metastable states and a response that depends on the history of driving. We can write a transition graph, a causal response rule, a phenomenological equation, or a desired transformation. Yet some formally consistent descriptions require an unprovided energy source, inaccessible internal control, exponentially many prepared parameters, an unmodeled reservoir, or a state reset after every use. Conversely, microscopic restrictions can conceal powerful collective operations. Is there an organizing principle that tells us what behaviors can actually be created, what resources they require, and how their capabilities survive composition and repeated use, without first resolving or commanding every microscopic state?

The full question includes the forward and inverse directions. A proposed foundation should connect a specified physical class to meaningful constraints on its possible responses, and should enable either a constructive realization of a substantial family of responses or a new obstruction that changes what a consequential physical class can do. The connection must carry information that existing dynamics, hysteresis theory, dissipativity, control and realization theory, resource theories or ordinary computation do not already supply at comparable cost. You are free to choose and replace the formal language. A general principle with strong new deductions is the aim; a restricted result is useful when it supplies an indispensable operation for that principle.

A possible test arena, rather than a compulsory model, is growing networks of locally coupled mechanical, electrical or other nonbiological degrees of freedom. For example, a classical finite-temperature starting model could be

  dx = -mu * grad_x V_N(x, lambda(t)) dt + sqrt(2*mu*k_B*T) dW,

with constant positive mobility mu, temperature T>0, a specified confining local potential, finite-range interactions, and driving through a stated set of accessible global or boundary variables lambda. This model is only a conditional physical premise: it does not supply a material, its coefficients, a noise qualification or the means to manufacture and measure it. Inertia, active elements, different physical models and different foundations are available when their energy sources, interactions and assumptions are explicit. An apparently powerful update rule should not receive independent internal actuators or a hidden measuring device for free.

To give a finite scientific meaning to a response law, one can describe legal driving histories with a finite alphabet and finite precision, specify accessible observables and loads, and ask for their output law after each legal history. Let N index a genuinely growing family, L a history length or use horizon, epsilon an output tolerance, delta a failure probability, and R a number of repeated uses. The physically meaningful claim concerns the stated class of legal histories and reachable internal states, with its actual quantifiers. Selected successful trajectories cannot substitute for that class. These parameters are available for posing the theory, not a requirement to use automata, probabilities or this notation if another formulation is more revealing.

Physical composition is central. Connecting components can change their loads, energy flows, fluctuations, available stable states and even the variables that can sensibly be treated as inputs and outputs. A serial or parallel diagram of logical tasks need not establish that the corresponding mechanisms can run together at their claimed accuracy, speed and reuse. Seek a principle that predicts this interaction or replaces the usual way of stating the problem. It should recover established successful physical descriptions in their domains and have a definite consequence where the new description matters. Consistency with known observations is essential; the existing choice of concepts is open to challenge.

Any constructive claim pays for the description and acquisition of its physical parameters, preparation and initialization, work and heat, material or stored free energy, physical time, precision, observations and feedback, retained memory, rejected outcomes, and restoration needed for reuse, as applicable. Resource requirements must scale with the actual family and accuracy, rather than hide an exponentially large truth table in a material or an infinitely accurate real parameter. Reuse means that the reachable post-use states retain the claimed capacity, or that the actual renewal operation and resources are included. A mathematical state variable representing remaining fuel is not replenished by restarting a simulation.

The decisive transfer is to understanding, predicting, designing or controlling a consequential physical class. It could establish a new capability, reveal an unexpected limitation and a way around it, or unify previously separate phenomena in a way that produces new calculations or constructions. A physical device need not already exist for a major foundational result to matter. The physical and computational assumptions supporting the earned consequence must nevertheless be explicit. Universal computation, a change of notation, an already-known conservation argument, or one small state graph alone does not establish that consequence. Pursue an actual scientific result, with theory and executable constructions where informative, rather than stopping at a proposal for future research.

One elementary diagnostic illustrates why the distinction matters. In the restricted small-angle equal-pair spring mapping J_ij = g_ij*k_ij*C*d_j/k_i, with positive spans d_i and symmetric signed pair couplings, the positive weights w_i=k_i*d_i satisfy w_i*J_ij=w_j*J_ji. Thus directed-looking threshold interactions can have a consistent weighted energy description. Conversely, an arbitrary directed threshold table need not be compatible with that particular construction. This is a restricted algebraic observation, not a general obstruction to passive matter; finite-angle geometry, hidden state and other mechanisms can change the conclusion.
