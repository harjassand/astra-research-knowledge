## 2. An exact mean representation without Markov lumpability

Let \(k(x)\) be the number of plus spins in configuration \(x\), and set
\[

\]

For a configuration \(x\), define its total physical upward and downward rates:
\[

\qquad
d(x)=\sum_{i:x_i=+1}c_i(x).
\]

Define the expected occupation measure before the physical hitting time:
\[

\]
where \(a\) is all-minus. Its mass at level \(j\) is
\[
H_j=\sum_{x:k(x)=j}G_a(x).
\]
Every \(H_j\) is positive. Normalize within that level:
\[
\nu_j(x)=\frac{G_a(x)}{H_j},
\qquad k(x)=j,
\]
and define
\[

\qquad
d_j=\mathbb E_{\nu_j}d(X).
\]

These averages use the **physical occupation law before hitting**, including returns and holding times.

### Exact flux identity

For every \(0\le j<K\),
\[
\boxed{g_jH_j-d_{j+1}H_{j+1}=1,}
\]
where the second term is zero for \(j=K-1\).

The proof is pathwise. A completed path starts below each cut
\[
\{0,\ldots,j\}\mid\{j+1,\ldots,K\}
\]
and finishes above it. Its number of upward crossings minus downward crossings is exactly one. Taking expectations and compensating the jump-counting processes gives the identity.

Consequently,
\[
H_{K-1}=\frac1{g_{K-1}},
\qquad
H_j=\frac{1+d_{j+1}H_{j+1}}{g_j},
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{\mathbb E_a\tau=\sum_{j=0}^{K-1}H_j.}
\]

Expanding the recurrence gives the positive-product formula
\[
\boxed{

=
\sum_{0\le j\le l<K}
\frac1{g_l}
\prod_{i=j}^{l-1}\frac{d_{i+1}}{g_i}.

\]

No claim that \(k(X_t)\) is Markov enters this proof. The associated birth-death chain reproduces the mean occupation of every level from the specified start.

\[
