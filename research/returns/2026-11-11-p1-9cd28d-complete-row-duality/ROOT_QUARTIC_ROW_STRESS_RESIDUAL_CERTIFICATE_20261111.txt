A natural-exponent stress certificate for the original quartic
ROOT, 11 November 2026 Brisbane

This note uses the actual ORIGINAL J_k and the attained row duality, without
changing it to an adapted-Hessian problem or assuming quartic regularity of
the constitutive stress. It constructs an explicit dual lower bound. It is
not a proof of the full all-mean/all-gradient inequality.

1. A UNIFORM FOURTH-POWER ROW BREGMAN RESERVE

Let ell=3k-1,n=k+1,k>1/3 and a_k=ell/(8n). Write F by its two output rows
x,y in R2. For fixed x the leading homogeneous part in y is EXACTLY
 Q_k(y)=a_k[k^2 y1^4+2 y1^2 y2^2+y2^4]
       >=a_k min(k^2,1)|y|^4.                       (1)
Use the accepted rank-one convexity: a row variation has rank one, so
f_x(y)=J_k(x,y) is convex for EVERY fixed x.

For arbitrary x,y,z, the scalar g(t)=f_x(y+t z) is a convex quartic with
leading coefficient Q_k(z). If z!=0 its second derivative is a nonnegative
quadratic on the ENTIRE real line, hence
 g''(t)=12Q_k(z)(t-t0)^2+h0, h0>=0.
Taylor's exact integral remainder and
 min_s integral_0^1(1-t)(t-s)^2 dt=1/36, attained at s=1/3,
give
 f_x(y+z)-f_x(y)-D_y f_x(y).z>=Q_k(z)/3
                              >=gamma_k |z|^4,
 gamma_k=ell min(k^2,1)/(24n)>0.                    (2)
This is uniform in x and the row background y. No false lower bound of the
full pointwise Hessian by a multiple of |y|^2 is needed; the shifted
quadratic g'' is what pays the Bregman integral. The reserve is from ONE
original row energy, not an additional independent piece of c_G.

2. ANY SOLENOIDAL STRESS GIVES AN ACTUAL VALUE CERTIFICATE

Let x=a+Df and y0=b+Dg0 be actual periodic L4 gradient rows. Put
 s=D_y J_k(x,y0) in L^(4/3).
For the partial conjugate L_k(x,sigma)=sup_y[sigma.y-J_k(x,y)], (2) implies
 L_k(x,tau)<=tau.y0-J_k(x,y0)+C_k|tau-s|^(4/3),
 C_k=3/(4^(4/3)gamma_k^(1/3)).                      (3)
Indeed maximize |tau-s|r-gamma_k r^4 over r>=0. This supplies the stated
constant without a smooth inverse constitutive map or a numerical optimizer.

For ANY periodic tau in L^(4/3) with div tau=0, pairing with every gradient
b+Dg gives integral tau.(b+Dg)=b.integral tau. Fenchel's inequality and (3)
therefore prove
 min_g integral J_k(a+Df,b+Dg)
 >=integral J_k(x,y0)-C_k integral|tau-s|^(4/3).      (4)
In particular if the right side is>=J_k(a,b), the original inequality holds
for EVERY second-row gradient at this first row. This is an explicit
field-dependent stress operation, not a fixed pointwise touching minorant.

The same derivation works with the rows exchanged. It retains the complete
weighted minor in the constitutive stress; no determinant coefficient
depending on x or the field has been discarded.

3. FINITE FOURIER HODGE SELECTION IS EFFECTIVE

For finite Fourier candidate rows, s is a finite Fourier polynomial. For
each nonzero integer frequency xi choose
 rhat(xi)=xi[xi.shat(xi)]/|xi|^2, rhat(0)=0,
 tau=s-r.                                          (5)
Then div tau=0 exactly. This is the actual Fourier Poisson/Hodge correction
to the constitutive stress, not a request for a PDE oracle. Its coefficient
formation and nonzero-mode division are finite and explicit. The certificate
(4) uses its actual L^(4/3) norm. Numerical evaluation must pay integral,
frequency/coefficient and power-approximation errors and retain a strict
margin; no unexecuted optimizer or integral is claimed computed.

For arbitrary rough rows (4) holds with ANY admitted solenoidal L^(4/3)
stress directly. One may also minimize the L^(4/3) distance to that closed
subspace: reflexivity and coercivity give an attained analytic minimum.
This existence statement is not a free algorithm for the minimum. The note
does not require quartic regularity of that stress or a Hessian source map.

As an exact example at k=1, x=(1,beta(y)),y0=(0,1), integral beta=0, the
actual stress is s=(beta^3/2-3beta,3). It is already divergence free, so
r=0 and (4) certifies the exact original row minimum. This remains true for
beta in L4 outside L12: s needs only L^(4/3), not L4. The orientation of
the mixed map (first potential,stress potential) is irrelevant to (4).

4. LIMITS AND THE FULL MEAN BUDGET

The stress distance in (4) has NOT been bounded by the original first-row
Jensen reserve for every first row. Thus it does not pay the full original
quartic endpoint. A negative value of this lower bound is no negative
compatible witness. Its merit is an actual natural-exponent, field-dependent
stress choice with a quantitative paid defect, bypassing the invalid
constant-stress concavity and adapted-Hessian shortcuts.

For compatible G, the exact original affine-mean gap remains
 c_G+b_G:A+(1/2)H_G[A,A],
 c_G=integral J_k(G), b_G=integral DJ_k(G),
 H_G=integral D^2J_k(G).
The acoustic Fourier argument from rank-one convexity gives H_G>=0.
Minimizing this polynomial over ALL independent A requires BOTH
 b_G perpendicular to ker H_G AND b_G^T H_G^dagger b_G<=2c_G. (6)
The pseudoinverse inequality alone would not handle a nonzero kernel
component. This note assumes neither condition for general G. Any automatic
kernel orthogonality must be proved or retained as an accepted scoped fact.

The new finite-positive-affine Burkholder touching obstruction does not
exclude (4), which selects its stress from the ACTUAL field. It also does
not exclude nonlinear graph cores or unattained infinite representations.
Conversely no such representation is assumed constructed. All earlier
