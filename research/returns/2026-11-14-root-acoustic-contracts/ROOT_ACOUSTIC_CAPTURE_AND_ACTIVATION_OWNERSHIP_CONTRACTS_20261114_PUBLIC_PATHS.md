2. Worked one-dimensional specialization and the ownership conflict

The following specialization assumes an isolated homogeneous solid sphere of
radius a, fluid density rho, viscosity mu and sound speed c. ALL numerical
values in this note are INTRODUCED, not selected from a qualified feedstock.

For source coefficient f1=1-kappa_particle/kappa_fluid and the real part of
the source's viscous f2, define the algebraic combination
  Phi = f1/3 + Re(f2)/2.
In a prescribed standing pressure p=p_a*cos(k*x), use
  <p^2>=p_a^2*cos(k*x)^2/2,
  <v^2>=p_a^2*sin(k*x)^2/(2*rho^2*c^2),
  E=p_a^2/(4*rho*c^2).
Inserting these into the read potential(20) and differentiating gives
  F(x)=4*pi*a^3*Phi*k*E*sin(2*k*x).
For positive Phi, a pressure antinode x=0 is unstable and a pressure node is
stable. Thus a node trap and a pressure-antinode activation region cannot
simply be called the same retained state.

Introduce a trapping mode with pressure node at x=0 and a DIFFERENT-frequency
activation mode with pressure antinode at x=0. Assuming the linear field and
time averaging remove cross-frequency terms,
  F_total =
    -A_T*sin(2*k_T*x) + s(t)*A_C*sin(2*k_C*x) + F_b,
  A_i = 4*pi*a^3*Phi_i*k_i*E_i.
Here s(t) is a prescribed activation envelope. Constant F_b contains the
introduced buoyant weight plus Stokes drag from a constant imposed stream.
The one-dimensional overdamped equation is
  6*pi*mu*a*x' = F_total.
This is not a model of acoustic streaming generated by these fields; that
streaming is an external introduced disturbance in this screen.

The local restoring stiffness at x=0 is
  K_local = 8*pi*a^3*(Phi_T*k_T^2*E_T -
                     s*Phi_C*k_C^2*E_C).
Two modes can therefore retain a local well while one contributes pressure
at the target, but only for a compatible force budget. Independent local
controls do not automatically construct three-dimensional confinement.

For a constant stream the implemented tilted potential is
  W(x) = A_T/(2*k_T)*(1-cos(2*k_T*x))
       - s*A_C/(2*k_C)*(1-cos(2*k_C*x)) - F_b*x.
It is a convenient one-dimensional force diagnostic. It is not the acoustic
source's electrical energy or the general work of a surrounding flow.

6. What was advanced, and what remains physical

The new worked mechanism is a staged, two-frequency location-retention
sequence with a finite activation interval. It demonstrates why capture
before activation and preservation of a finite basin are distinct jobs.
It is more specific than asking for better field control, and provides a
conditional positive trajectory as well as counterexamples.

It does not supply the next indispensable physical operation: a real
particle-to-product handover under an actual locking chemistry or phase

transmission through an opaque housing, material identity, renewal and
complete useful output remain open. A prescribed clock is not that handover.
