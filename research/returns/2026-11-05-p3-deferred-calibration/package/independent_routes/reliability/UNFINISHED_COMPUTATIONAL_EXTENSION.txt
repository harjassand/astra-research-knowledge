UNFINISHED FULL-CDF COMPUTATIONAL CONSTRUCTION
Preserved 2026-11-05; this is not a completed algorithmic theorem.

COMPLETED_RESULTS.txt governs the completed sensitivity and finite-
implementation results. The earlier DIRECTION.md assertion of completion
at theorem level was preliminary and is superseded by this status.

Target. For a succinct lazy reversible chain P, known invariant pi,
failure mass 0<p=pi(B)<=1/2, gap lower bound g, and initial law pi(.|A),
produce one finite confidence certificate of relative-width 1+epsilon
for every integer deadline n>=1, without inverse-p or lifetime cost.
Necessary supplied computational access: a relative estimator of p,
a TV-accurate sampler of pi(.|B), and ordinary P updates. The accepted
all-terminal/product-law instance supplies the first two from existing
Karger machinery; arbitrary failure predicates do not provide them.

EXACT DERIVED CORE

Put a=1-p, K=I-P_A, j=sum_{x in A,y in B}pi(x)P(x,y), and v=1_A.
Poincare, zero extension, Rayleigh quotient and interlacing give

  gpa <= j <= p,
  gp <= lambda=lambda_1(K) <= j/a <= p/a,
  lambda_2(K)>=g.

For the spectral expansion F_n=sum_i alpha_i[1-(1-lambda_i)^n],
alpha_i>=0 and sum alpha_i=1. In the rare regime lambda<g,
r=1-alpha_1<=j/(ag)<=p/(ag).

Short-time Palm operation: sample y~pi(.|B), take P(y,.) to x, and
accept x in A. Acceptance c=j/p>=ag>=g/2. By reversibility the accepted
pair x->y has the stationary A->B crossing law. Put it uniformly at
one of n step positions, simulate backwards and forwards ordinary P
paths, and return W=1{X_0 in A}/N, where N>=1 counts A->B crossings.
The forced-path law is N/(jn) times stationary path law. Therefore

  a F_n = p c n mu_n,  mu_n=E W,
  mu_n >= ag/(1+ngp) >= g/[2(1+ng)].

This is an adaptation of existing inverse-multiplicity/Palm sampling,
not a claim that inverse-multiplicity estimation is new.

Long-time relative CDF approximation, f_n(lambda)=1-(1-lambda)^n:

  f_n(lambda)<=F_n,
  (F_n-f_n(lambda))/f_n(lambda)<=r/(n lambda)<=2/(g^2 n).

Short no-return eigenvalue statistic: sample Y~pi(.|B), take one P
step and set Z=1 iff it enters A and then survives ell further steps.
Reversibility gives

  D_ell=p E Z=<v,K P_A^ell v>
       =a sum_i alpha_i lambda_i(1-lambda_i)^ell.

Set ell=ceil[g^-1 log(4/(g xi))], p_gate=xi/(2ell). If p<=p_gate,
lambda ell<=xi, r<=xi and the fast contribution is at most xi/2
relative to lambda. Thus

  (1-2xi)lambda<=D_ell/a<=(1+xi)lambda,
  E Z>=g/4.

These formulas require only short, bounded-mean computational trials;
the static rare factor p is estimated separately. They do not supply a
physical rare-failed-state preparer.

PROPOSED ASSEMBLY

Use xi=epsilon/200 and a static interval [p_L,p_U] of multiplicative
width r_xi=(1+xi)/(1-xi). Rare branch: p_U<=p_gate. Otherwise p>p_gate/2.
Set

  H=max{ceil[2/(g^2 xi)],
        ceil[(2/(g p_gate))log(1/xi)], ell+1}.

In the rare branch the single slow mode gives relative CDF error <=xi
after H. In the nonrare branch S_n<=exp(-gpn)<=xi after H.
Before H, estimate mu_n on a finite integer grid containing all points
through ceil(1/xi), then n_next=ceil((1+xi)n). Adjacent ratios <=r_xi;
the grid has G=O(xi^-1 log(H+1)) points. Concavity of F, with F_0=0,
gives F_m/F_n<=m/n, so grid bounds extend to all intermediate deadlines.

Candidate intervals of width <=r_xi for p,c,mu at each node give node
CDF width <=r_xi^4 and between-node width <=r_xi^9. A width-r_xi interval
for E Z gives a lambda interval of width <=r_xi^5, after the explicit
(1-2xi),(1+xi) correction. Its tail CDF interval has width <=r_xi^6.
All intervals must be certified on ONE finite probability event.

PRECISE UNFINISHED PART

No central identity above was found false. The outstanding mathematical
task is to close the full randomized-oracle algorithm into one proof:
allocate approximate conditional-sampler bias, per-step simulation
bias, capped boundary-rejection failure, bounded-variable concentration,
positive eigenvalue confidence endpoints, and directed finite arithmetic
together while retaining the claimed uniform relative width and work.
The detailed ingredients are present, but that end-to-end argument has
not been completed or independently checked. There is no implementation
or numerical diagnostic for this computational route.

Provisional bias scales: with mu_min=g/[2(1+gH)], a conditional sampler
TV error tau creates O(tau/c) accepted-pair error; a per-step simulator
error e creates O(He) path error. Tolerances tau<<xi mu_min g and
e<<xi mu_min/H should suffice with explicit smaller internal confidence
budgets. This needs a checked interval construction, not separate
informal assertions that each error is small.

For binary n in the tail, use outward evaluation of
f_n(lambda)=-expm1[n log1p(-lambda)], not a huge exact rational power.
When n lambda>=log(1/xi), saturation [1-xi,1] suffices. A proof of
polynomial bit complexity in input length, log n and accuracy, including
very small positive lambda endpoints, remains to be written.

Nominal scales before oracle and rounding costs:

  ell=O(g^-1 log(1/(g xi))),
  H=O(g^-2 xi^-1 log(1/(g xi))log(1/xi)),
  G=O(xi^-1 log(H+1)),
  ordinary updates=O(G H^2 xi^-2 log(G/delta)).

These costs can be large. Dependence on g^-1 is on its value, not its
bit length. No practical advantage is demonstrated.

SCOPE AND PRIOR

The actual endpoint clock must be lazy, with null ticks explicitly
paid if used. Q=(P-I)/T does not preserve its CDF. A genuine CTMC
variant would need actual uniformization/Poisson Palm sampling and
appropriate continuous-time initialization; it is not completed here.

Nakayama-Shahabuddin 2004 already combine short-horizon simulation and
large-horizon regenerative bounds, including broader dynamics and
all-working starts. Aldous-Brown already give reversible rare-event
exponential approximation; Karp-Luby already give inverse-multiplicity
estimation. A completed version could establish a specific nonasymptotic
succinct-state uniform relative certificate under the above static-access
and gap promises. It could not claim first all-horizon risk estimation
or general dominance over those approaches. Full sources and the
completed achieved consequence are in COMPLETED_RESULTS.txt.
