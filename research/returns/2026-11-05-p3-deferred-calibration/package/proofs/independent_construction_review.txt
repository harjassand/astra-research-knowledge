OUTPUT-ONLY HAMILTONIAN CALIBRATION AND RETROSPECTIVE RECOVERY
Independent construction/proof review, 2026-11-05

STATUS

The mixed-derivative calibration construction is valid under the stated pure,
coherent, calibrated output access. The Chebyshev/Markov estimator has finite
all-trial cost proportional to eta^-2 up to logarithms at fixed locality.
Two useful strengthenings are proved below:

  (i) Calibration need not be statistically independent of the stored payload.
      A uniform failure probability and branchwise decoder diamond bound suffice.
 (ii) Fresh randomized interpolation nodes admit a variance-sensitive martingale
      estimate, including without burn-in. Finite dyadic sampling suffices.

This reviews the NEW calibration/composition operation. The existing continuous
pure-output acquisition theorem and its finite collector are accepted premises,
not results re-proved here. No hardware experiment, optimality theorem, or claim
of historical priority is supplied. The separate convex-capacity investigation
remains incomplete and is not a premise of this construction.

1. MODEL AND THE TWO CALIBRATION REGIMES

Let S contain n qubits. Write a static Hamiltonian in a known complete list of
distinct, canonically signed, nonidentity Pauli strings:

  H = sum_{a=1}^M theta_a P_a,   |supp(P_a)| <= k,
  sum_a |theta_a| <= B_H.

Identity energy is unidentifiable and irrelevant to the recovered channel.
The source has actual pure-vacuum, calibrated nonidentity Weyl ports, all needed
fields coherently and losslessly available, giving the averaged generator

  L(rho) = -i[H,rho] + r sum_i (E_i(rho)-rho),
  E_i(rho) = (I_i/2) tensor Tr_i(rho).

The inherited noise-only-bin collector and its fixed endpoint isometry expose
the instrument

  |psi> -> sqrt(1-p)|psi>|empty>
             + sqrt(p)|Phi_2>_{S_i A_i}|psi>_{B_i},
  p = 1-exp(-r Delta).

Its flags are state independent; A is the physical accessible purifier of the
replacement, and B exports the previous source qubit with arbitrary references.
This is an acquired Kraus decomposition of the pure coherent output, not an
interpretation of ordinary photon counts or of an unspecified depolarizing bath.
It uses a known environment isometry and an ancillary embedding of its canonical
Kraus space. Discarding an unavailable bath purifier cannot be repaired by naming
it A. On the actual continuous fields, the inherited full-output approximation
and finite receiver error are charged to the entire unconditional protocol.

Set Lambda = 2 B_H + 2 n r. All relevant finite record-machine generators below
have diamond norm at most Lambda. Assume 0 < eta <= Lambda.

There are two separate sufficient regimes.

  A. Support-only, arbitrary k-local candidate geometry: before each trial,
     replace the source approximately by I/2^n using passive burn-in. A front
     window need require resets only on supp(P), so its size is at most k.

  B. No burn-in: for a chosen output site i, let C_i be the union of supports of
     every candidate term containing i. Require front resets on all of C_i.
     Then the target derivative is independent of every starting source state.
     Polynomial efficiency of this version requires bounded |C_i|, a stronger
     condition than bounded interaction arity. Arbitrary all-to-all k-local
     systems can have |C_i|=n; regime A remains available in that case.

Neither regime applies a source-side preparation, Hamiltonian pulse, swap, or
measurement. Measuring outgoing A can steer a conditional source state, which is
an explicitly supplied indirect operation. Its average is the original L.

2. THE PHYSICALLY ACQUIRED SIGNED RECORD

For a target P choose i in supp(P) and a one-qubit Q_i anticommuting with P_i.
Let R = i P Q_i, a signed Hermitian Pauli string. Its support is supp(P), and

  2^-n Tr[R i[H,Q_i]] = 2 theta_P,
  Tr[R Q_i] = 0.

Choose the front reset set C=supp(P) in regime A, or C=C_i in regime B. Put
c=|C| and s=c+1. On C outside supp(P), the measured factor of R is identity.

Trial (front width w, middle duration t, back width w):
  - Front: retain the last A from each site in C, and its seen flag.
  - Middle: discard outputs while the actual source evolves for t.
  - Back: retain B_i from the first reset of i, and its seen flag.
  - If any required flag is missing, output X=0. Otherwise measure R^T on the
    retained A registers and Q_i on B_i, and output their signed product X=+/-1.

The identity factors need flags but no nontrivial measurements. Transposition
and the overall sign of R must be included: Y^T=-Y in the calibrated lab basis.
One may measure each outgoing A immediately in its fixed local R_j^T basis and
keep its last classical outcome. One may measure each B_i immediately and keep
its first back-window outcome. Earlier overwritten outcomes are unused and
cause no feedback; deferred measurement gives the same signed distribution.

The source need not stop at t=0 or at a window boundary. Receiver-side records
can be tagged by retarded source time, with adjacent windows at t=0. Fixed local
A and B measurement bases and finite delayed processing avoid a hypothetical
instantaneous source operation. Readout, refresh, propagation and selection
latency are included in wall time; sufficient fast receiver processing or the
inherited extra-bank fallback is a supplied paid resource.

For every initial state and H, the ideal independent reset clocks give

  E[X^2] = p(w) = (1-exp(-r w))^s.

No accepted-sample or normalized postselection probability appears in the data.
Every trial, including X=0, is counted.

3. EXACT MIXED-DERIVATIVE IDENTITY

Let f_sigma(w,t)=E_sigma[X]. There are no terms in its w expansion below order
s: at least c front resets and one back reset are necessary. At order w^s,
each required reset occurs exactly once and there are no other reset or
Hamiltonian insertions inside the front/back windows. Additional such insertions
cost another factor of w. Summing all c! front reset orders cancels the ordered
simplex factor 1/c!. The front's signed source operator is

  r^c w^c (R_C/2^c tensor Tr_C sigma) + O(w^(c+1)).

Here the identity on C outside supp(P) is part of R_C. The first back export
contributes r w times the Q_i expectation. Thus

  [w^s] f_sigma(w,t)
    = r^s Tr[Q_i exp(t L)(R_C/2^c tensor Tr_C sigma)].                 (1)

In regime A, sigma=I/2^n and this is
r^s 2^-n Tr[R exp(t L^dagger)(Q_i)]. Since

  L^dagger(Q_i)=i[H,Q_i]-r Q_i,

Pauli orthogonality gives

  (partial_w^s partial_t f)(0,0) = 2 s! r^s theta_P.                 (2)

In regime B, every term of [H,Q_i] is supported in C_i. Taking the trace of
(1) over C_i gives the same Pauli orthogonality, while Tr(Tr_C sigma)=1.
Consequently (2) holds for EVERY starting sigma, including a source correlated
with the original input reference, early output bank, and prior observations.
Only the single derivative in the middle duration needs this neighborhood;
higher middle-time coefficients may depend on the exterior state.

4. A FINITE RECORD MACHINE GIVES UNIFORM ANALYTIC BOUNDS

For analysis, append at most c A qubits, one B qubit, and seen bits. A front
reset overwrites A_j with the new Bell purifier; a back reset freezes the first
B_i; all later resets act ordinarily. Each reset/update is CPTP. Hamiltonian
evolution acts only on S. The resulting generators G_F,G_M,G_B satisfy

  ||G_F||_diamond, ||G_M||_diamond, ||G_B||_diamond <= Lambda.

For a norm-at-most-one final observable O and an initial state rho_sigma,

  f_sigma(w,t) = Tr[O exp(w G_B) exp(t G_M) exp(w G_F) rho_sigma].

The matrices are a proof representation; the acquisition algorithm never
constructs their exponentially large source-space entries. Expanding the three
exponentials gives an entire scalar function with

  f_sigma(w,t)=sum_{a,b>=0} c_ab w^a t^b,
  |c_ab| <= (2 Lambda)^a Lambda^b/(a! b!).                           (3)

The bound is uniform in sigma, including conditional states in regime B.
For nonnegative real times, CPTP contraction also gives

  |partial_w f_sigma| <= 2 Lambda,   |partial_t f_sigma| <= Lambda.   (4)

5. EXPLICIT CHEBYSHEV/DCT ESTIMATOR: ROOT'S CONSTANTS WORK

Let h=1/(8 Lambda), D>=max(s,2), and use the tensor Chebyshev--Lobatto grid of
degree D on [0,h]^2. Let I_D interpolate the grid values. The rectangular Taylor
polynomial T_D through degree D in each variable has exactly the desired
derivative at (0,0). From (3), on the entire square,

  ||f_sigma-T_D||_infinity <= 4*4^-D.                               (5)

One-dimensional DCT-I interpolation has sup norm at most 2(D+1) times the
largest nodal magnitude. Tensoring and repeated Markov inequalities yield

  |partial_w^s partial_t I_D(v)(0,0)| <= W_Ds max_j |v_j|,
  W_Ds = 4(D+1)^2 (2 D^2/h)^(s+1).                                 (6)

Therefore the linear functional

  L_D(f) = partial_w^s partial_t I_D(f)(0,0)/(2 s! r^s)
         = sum_j a_j f(w_j,t_j)

has A=sum_j |a_j| <= A_0=W_Ds/(2 s! r^s), and deterministic bias

  |L_D(f_sigma)-theta_P| <= 2 W_Ds 4^-D/(s! r^s).                   (7)

Select the first D>=max(s,2) with

  8 W_Ds 4^-D <= eta s! r^s.                                      (8)

Then bias <=eta/4. The search terminates: its exponential decay beats the fixed
power of D. For fixed s the degree is logarithmic, up to log-log terms, in the
dimensionless quantity Lambda^(s+1)/(eta r^s). There is no exact derivative
oracle, zero-duration experiment, analytic continuation oracle, or hidden
source-space matrix exponentiation.

For regime A with independent ideal source replacements, estimate each
nontrivial grid mean to accuracy

  zeta = eta s! r^s/(2 W_Ds) = eta/(4 A_0).

The w=0 values are known exactly to be zero and need no trials. Round all
physical durations INSIDE [0,h], with individual error <=zeta/(12 Lambda).
Equation (4) makes this a nodal bias at most zeta/4; clipping preserves the
variance bound p_max=(r h)^s. At each nontrivial node take

  N_node = ceil[8(p_max/zeta^2 + 1/zeta)
                   log(2 M(D+1)^2/delta)].                         (9)

Bernstein's inequality applies because |X-E X|<=2 and Var X<=p_max. A union
bound makes all nodal statistical errors <=zeta with failure at most delta.
The coefficient statistical error is <=eta/4; timing adds <=eta/16. Certified
final coefficient-weight rounding with l1 error <=eta/8 adds at most eta/8.
Together with (7), these remain strictly below eta. The sample schedule is
fixed; there is no unaccounted repeat-until-success or confidence stopping.

For s<=k+1, the resulting all-trial bound is

  O_k[M (Lambda/eta)^2 (Lambda/r)^(k+1) D^(4k+14)
          log(M(D+1)^2/delta)].                                  (10)

This checks the exponent and rare-clock factor in root's working note. The
linear Bernstein term is dominated under eta<=Lambda and D>=s; keeping it in
(9) is simplest. The exponent in eta is 2 up to logarithms. This is not a claim
of optimal metrology, or a claim that the norm/locality factors are practical.

6. STRONGER RANDOMIZED-NODE ESTIMATOR, WITH FINITE RANDOMNESS

This is essential in regime B, where the source state changes between trials.
Fixed batches of different nodes would evaluate different functions f_sigma;
their Taylor cancellations would not be justified. Instead select the node
freshly and independently after the current trial's conditional state is fixed.
Any selection latency must be common to all node choices (or padded to be so).

With exact weights, choose node j with probability |a_j|/A and return

  Z = A sign(a_j) X_j.

For each preceding classical history F, one and the same current conditional
state sigma_F appears throughout the conditional expectation:

  E[Z|F] = sum_j a_j f_sigma_F(w_j,t_j),
  |E[Z|F]-theta_P| <= eta/4,
  E[Z^2|F] <= A^2 p_max,   |Z-E[Z|F]| <= 2 A.                      (11)

Thus a variance-sensitive martingale Bernstein/Freedman inequality gives

  N = O[(A^2 p_max/eta^2 + A/eta) log(M/delta)]                    (12)

raw trials per coefficient. The same proof is uniform over arbitrary initial
source/reference states. It does not assume a Markov chain has mixed.

Here is an explicit finite implementation, avoiding exact real sampling.
Compute rational weights a_tilde_j with sum |a_tilde_j-a_j|<=eta/16, let
A_tilde=sum |a_tilde_j|, and delete identically zero-data w=0 nodes. For K
remaining nodes choose a power of two N_q>=2K and integers

  m_j = ceil(N_q |a_tilde_j|/(2 A_tilde)).

Their sum is at most N_q. Assign the remaining integer slots arbitrarily and
put q_j=m_j/N_q. Using log_2(N_q) fair bits samples these probabilities exactly,
and q_j>=|a_tilde_j|/(2 A_tilde). Return

  Z = a_tilde_j X_j/q_j.

Then E[Z|F] is exactly the rounded-weight interpolation functional,
|Z|<=2 A_tilde, and E[Z^2|F]<=2 A_tilde^2 p_max. If A_tilde=0, the rounded
functional is identically zero and no sample is needed. Choose clipped node
timing error at most eta/(48 Lambda A_tilde); its weighted bias is <=eta/16.
Together with (7) and weight rounding, the conditional mean bias is <=3eta/8.

The centered increments have absolute value <=4 A_tilde and conditional
variance <=2 A_tilde^2 p_max. Therefore the explicit sufficient schedule

  N = ceil[32(A_tilde^2 p_max/eta^2 + A_tilde/eta)
                    log(2M/delta)]                               (13)

makes the average centered error <=eta/2 except with probability delta/M.
Final arithmetic error <=eta/16 still leaves total error below eta. The usual
exponential-moment proof uses
E[exp(uD)|F] <= exp(u^2 v/[2(1-u b/3)]) for a centered increment |D|<=b
and E[D^2|F]<=v, followed by multiplication and Chernoff optimization. Thus no
independence beyond the freshly chosen node is being smuggled into (13).

With s fixed, (12) scales as

  O_s[(Lambda/eta)^2 (Lambda/r)^s D^(4s+8) log(M/delta)]

per coefficient. In regime B set s=|C_i|+1. The same randomized estimator can
also be used after each burn-in in regime A, reducing the conservative degree
factor in (10) by (D+1)^2. It retains a SINGLE inverse-success-probability
factor, rather than the squared factor that a variance-blind Hoeffding estimate
would introduce. Exact constants and the full expression (13) are preferable
to calling this a metrological optimality result.

7. PASSIVE COMPLETE BURN-IN: VALID AND USEFUL, BUT NOT NEEDED FOR COMPOSITION

For any joint rho_SR, put tau_S=I/2^n and

  D(rho_SR || tau_S tensor rho_R) = n log 2 - S(S|R).

The Hamiltonian on S contributes zero to its derivative. For each replacement,

  -dD/dt contribution
    = r[D(rho||E_i rho)+D(E_i rho||rho)]
    >= r[log 2-S(S_i|S_except_i R)].

Strong subadditivity, applied in a chain order, gives
sum_i S(S_i|S_except_i R)<=S(S|R). Hence D'<=-rD. The initial conditional
entropy lies between -n log2 and n log2, so D(0)<=2n log2. Pinsker yields

  ||(exp(t L) tensor id_R)(rho)-tau_S tensor rho_R||_1
       <= 2 sqrt(n log2) exp(-r t/2).                              (14)

The statement is uniform in the reference, including all stored output and
prior trial records. Singular states follow by regularization. During burn-in
the output is discarded; this is an averaged source-channel bound, not a
historywise claim. If J calibration trials are scheduled, per-trial replacement
error mu is achieved by

  t_b >= (2/r) log[2 sqrt(n log2)/mu].

Choosing mu=epsilon_burn/J gives total joint hybrid error <=epsilon_burn.
Regime A thereby obtains the I/2^n exterior needed for support-only coefficient
isolation and, if desired, independent ideal trial distributions. Regime B
uses (11)-(13) instead and requires no such burn-in.

8. REFERENCE-UNIFORM RETROSPECTIVE COMPOSITION WITHOUT INDEPENDENCE

Let B be the entire stored early bank, including any acquired classical flags,
and R an arbitrary original reference. For a later calibration record y, let
sigma_y be the subnormalized B,R state. Later operations act only on the
remaining source and later outputs, so no-signalling gives

  sum_y sigma_y = rho_BR,

the original archived marginal. Suppose a fixed known-H decoder D_H exists and
every good estimate gives a decoder satisfying

  ||D_hat_y-D_H||_diamond <= e.

For good y, the corresponding trace-norm contribution is at most e Tr sigma_y;
for bad y it is at most 2 Tr sigma_y. Therefore

  ||sum_y (D_hat_y tensor id)(sigma_y)
           -(D_H tensor id)(rho_BR)||_1 <= e + 2 Pr(bad).           (15)

No assumption about independence, lack of entanglement, or conditional
disturbance of B is used. A failure probability bounded uniformly over all
initial states is sufficient. The concentration proof in regime B has exactly
that uniformity; regime A obtains it by (14) and unconditional hybrid bounds.

For the accepted P1 full-history inverse decoder, compare H and H_hat on the
same recorded reset history and same dummy-source registers. Duhamel plus
unitary-channel telescoping yields

  ||D_hat-D_H||_diamond <= 2 T0 ||H_hat-H||
                         <= 2 T0 sum_a |theta_hat_a-theta_a|
                         <= 2 T0 M eta.                           (16)

The sum of Hamiltonian interval lengths is T0. Reset swaps are identical.
For a finite product-formula or any pruned implementation, the coefficient-l1
bound is the robust justification: fix a common layout from candidate supports
and supplied bounds, and change only gate angles. Subsets of terms need not
preserve cancellations in ||H_hat-H||. Use a uniformly compiled circuit rather
than changing a stopping rule discontinuously with H_hat. H_hat itself need
not satisfy an export threshold; the P1 recovery bound concerns the TRUE H.

If the accepted early collector/known-model decoder has error epsilon_P1 and
all physical/finite comparison errors total epsilon_phys, (15)-(16) give

  ||recovered_channel-id||_diamond
    <= epsilon_P1 + 2 T0 M eta + 2 delta + epsilon_phys.            (17)

For example, choose T0 so 2 sqrt(n exp(-kappa_0 T0))<=epsilon/4,
eta<=epsilon/(8 M T0), delta<=epsilon/8, and epsilon_phys<=epsilon/4.
All constants are merely sufficient allocations. Calibration can be correlated
with the very first arbitrary payload and still obey (17).

9. FINITE PHYSICAL EXECUTION AND TOTAL COSTS

Fix the total number J of trials before execution. For regime A, source-time
calibration duration is at most J(t_b+3h); for regime B it is at most 3hJ plus
paid common waiting intervals. The archived payload must survive the full
wall time: early capture, propagation, random selection, finite measurements,
receiver reinitialization, classical arithmetic and inverse-decoder runtime.
The apparent sample advantage does not remove Q_buffer*T_wall*nu_memory or the
declared fault-tolerant replacement for that storage cost.

Apply the inherited physical collector error to the COMPLETE source+output
channel before classical record processing. With R_ph=3nr/4 and a supplied
local commutator scale chi_0, a segment of duration u and bin Delta has

  epsilon_split <= 2 chi_0 Delta sqrt(R_ph u+(R_ph u)^2).

Allocate O(epsilon_phys/J) per calibration trial. The finite record reset maps
are idempotent and act on disjoint local memories, so a noise-only bin gives
their exact independent Bernoulli updates. Splitting those updates from the
Hamiltonian in a total active duration T_act costs at most
2 Lambda^2 T_act Delta in diamond norm, by bounded CPTP Lie splitting; choosing
Delta <= epsilon_record/(2 Lambda^2 T_act) is sufficient. Also choose Delta
below the timing tolerance above. Allocate local collector error z to the
deterministic number of processed site-bins, e.g. z=O(epsilon_phys/(J n K))
for K bins per trial. The inherited sufficient controls are finite:

  peak <= 16 sqrt(r)/z,
  slew = O(sqrt(r)/(z Delta)+r^(3/2)/z^4),
  mesh = O(1+r^2 Delta^2/z^5).

Readout/refresh/routing must meet the bin deadline, or extra banks are paid.
Fixed bins and explicit queue/workspace caps avoid an unbounded Poisson-event
execution promise. Quantum calibration data can be measured on arrival; the
early payload bank still remains coherent. All these costs are polynomial in
the actual numerical bounds at fixed locality, but can be very large.

The full unconditional instrument comparison, followed by any deterministic
postprocessing and estimated decoder, is contractive in trace distance. It
therefore needs no division by rare reset success. A normalized heralded Choi
or remote-teleportation query is different: any unconditioned interface error
used for THAT conditional guarantee must be divided by its actual success
probability. The estimator here never makes that substitution.

The rate r and lab port axes/phases remain calibrated premises. If r is only
known to logarithmic relative error xi, the coefficient factor r^s creates
bias at most |theta_P|(exp(s xi)-1). A sufficient additional allocation is
xi=O(eta/(s B_H)) when B_H>0; the case B_H=0 is already known. Use certified
rate upper/lower bounds in h, burn-in and p_max.
State-independent flags allow ordinary paid rate calibration, but exact rate
knowledge is not proved by the Hamiltonian estimator itself.

The known complete Hamiltonian candidate list, static parameters throughout
the experiment, calibrated pure inputs, no omitted interactions, controlled
loss and receiver accuracy are substantive premises. Unmodeled source noise,
drift, missing purification, or finite fixed bandwidth are not excused by the
proof. For small additional generators a safe error is their integrated norm
over the relevant early/correctly modeled calibration duration. Complexity is
polynomial in values such as Lambda/r, kappa_0^-1 and hardware norm bounds,
not automatically in their logarithmic bit descriptions.

10. WHAT THE OPERATION ADDS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

The accepted P1 theorem already captures and decodes an arbitrary unencoded
payload when H is supplied. The additional operation physically obtains the
local Hamiltonian information from later coherent ports, with finite precision
and a uniform probability guarantee, and uses it to decode the earlier stored
payload. No second specimen with that original unknown state is needed. This
removes the preknown-H premise under the other declared resources.

The replacement purifiers carry essential information for this particular
compiler. Starting from I/2^n, discarding all A and retaining only exported B
registers and measured reset flags gives a product maximally mixed B stream
independent of H: unitary source histories preserve the joint identity and
each reset exports identity while replacing identity. This does not say every
alternative coherent or homodyne output measurement is uninformative.

Quantum early storage is likewise substantive. If ALL payload information must
cross a classical entanglement-breaking cut, with no remaining quantum route
to the receiver, later classical calibration cannot restore an arbitrary
reference-entangled input. The normalized Choi test gives distance at least
2(1-1/d) from the d-dimensional identity channel. The qualification about all
remaining quantum routes matters; the newly proved correlated-calibration
composition should not be confused with an unrestricted classical-memory no-go.

Prior components include Stinespring/Kraus transformations, Choi correlations,
Hamiltonian derivative learning, interpolation and martingale concentration,
and P1 inverse-history recovery. The new proof is a finite physical compiler
and its uniform first-payload composition. Historical novelty and superiority
over every output-identification or controllable-probe method need separate
primary-literature comparison; no such blanket claim follows from this review.
