# Prior and uncertainty

## Direct primary comparisons

1. Jan Kristensen, On the non-locality of quasiconvexity, Annales IHP C 16 (1999), 1–13.
   https://www.numdam.org/item/AIHPC_1999__16_1_1_0.pdf
   Definitions on pp.3–4 distinguish small-gradient weak quasiconvexity from agreement with a globally quasiconvex function nearby. Proposition1 gives the classical strict Legendre-Hadamard case. The present contribution addresses degenerate contacts, not that ordinary case.

2. Bruno–Pasqualotto, Quasiconvexity for the Dacorogna–Marcellini Energy, arXiv:2608.06367v1.
   https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06367v1
   Its global bi-SO(2) quartic theorem and heat/exact-form method are strong prior. The symmetry class differs from the original one-sided invariant J_r. No automatic transfer is claimed.

3. Sergio Gutierrez, A Necessary Condition for the Quasiconvexity of Polynomials of Degree Four, Journal of Convex Analysis13 (2006), 51–60.
   https://journalofconvexanalysis.com/articles/jca13004/jca13004.pdf?v=2
   The reviewed cubic acoustic cancellations and resonant necessary conditions are relevant antecedents. The inspected statements do not supply the current sufficient bound for every compatible small-gradient test.

4. Bandeira–Pedregal, Quasiconvexity: the quadratic case revisited, and some consequences for fourth-degree polynomials (2008).
   https://matematicas.uclm.es/omeva/wp-content/preprints/preprint10-08.pdf
   Independently read during review; its Fourier characterization and quartic criteria concern zero-mean questions rather than the specific full-rank degenerate-background radius.

5. Agazzi et al., Sharp Higher Integrability Theory Part I: The Lp-norm of the Beurling–Ahlfors transform, arXiv:2609.28311v2 (8 October 2026).
   https://arxiv.org/html/2609.28311v2
   Theorem1 and Corollary2 state global Burkholder quasiconvexity and sharp Beurling norms; Lemma4 uses projection onto a convex scalar-gradient constraint. The displayed primary theorem/proof was read during final packaging, but not independently audited here. No automatic transfer to the anisotropic original J_r is established. It is a potentially stronger global method comparison, not a dependency of the present proof or a historical novelty clearance.

These public primary papers are linked, not reproduced wholesale. This was a targeted comparison, not an exhaustive novelty search.
