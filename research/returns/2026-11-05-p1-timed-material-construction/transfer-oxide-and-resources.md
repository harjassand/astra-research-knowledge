Blocking a front, finite interface resistance or a different transport law can change it, but are additional inputs. An internal target-phase island is not another Cu reservoir.

A separate 200 C incremental measurement converts to about `1.03e-17 m^2/s`. Combined with the old activation slope, this hybrid sensitivity gives roughly 30/144/469 hours; the middle preparation still disagrees. It does not acquire an independent 175 C law or repair validation.

## Oxide assembly and actual—but incomplete—input acquisition

The Ba–Ti–O calculation uses established correlated Onsager transport and joint interface balances. Closed fixed-valence, zero-current projection gives `L_closed=L-(Lz)(Lz)^T/(z^T Lz)`, `z=(2,4,-2)`. A computationally specified seeded planar BaO/TiO2 assembly at 1000 K evolves through phase loss and precursor exhaustion; mean library inputs predict BaTiO3 disappearing in favor of Ba2TiO4/BaTi2O5, with conditional endpoint about `.543 microseconds`.

That is not a measured powder clock. Nanoscale layers, supplied seeds, liquid-like/amorphous transport assigned to growing phases and negligible interface resistance are assumptions. Shared material flux and a genuine model endpoint are implemented; their applicability to bulk preparation is not validated.

Positive-semidefinite, mass-conserving matrices within the published componentwise mean-plus/minus-SD boxes can have zero constrained open-circuit mobility. Feasible shifts are about `.034,.109,.406` marginal SD for the three modeled products. This deterministic feasible-set result says the marginal uncertainty description alone cannot guarantee a positive mobility floor or finite robust upper time. The boxes are not a joint confidence region and do not show actual zero transport.

The source also acquired a real public 100-atom, 1,251-frame, 1500 K trajectory with declared 4 ps spacing over 5 ns. After mass-center removal and joint displacement analysis, conditional projected mobilities at 100–1500 ps lags are about `(2.60–3.29)e16 (eV cm s)^(-1)`. Nine 500 ps blocks give `2.69e16`, with delete-one-block range `(2.30–2.92)e16`, a sensitivity diagnostic rather than confidence interval.

Sparse periodic coordinates need an unwrapping assumption; unsampled winding cannot be excluded. The example is not the full replicate collection and differs substantially from its aggregate table. It does not replace the published coefficient, prove a unit error or supply the needed 1000 K law.
