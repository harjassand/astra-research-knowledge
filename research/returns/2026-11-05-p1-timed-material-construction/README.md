## The actual ordinary interface operation

The Cu / Cu3Sn / Cu6Sn5 / Sn model uses physical layer thicknesses, finite component inventories per area and prescribed temperature history. Integrated interdiffusion coefficients measured under the actual neighboring phases determine coupled Wagner/Stefan growth, reservoir consumption and subsequent layer disappearance. They are not interchangeable with tracer diffusivities or a whole-recipe fitted coefficient.

The independently measured 2011 coefficients give, at 473 K and 225 hours, calculated Cu3Sn/Cu6Sn5 thicknesses of 8.346/18.315 micrometres against reported 9.0 plus/minus 3.6 and 16.4 plus/minus 2.1. The Cu3Sn coefficient is extrapolated below its original 498–623 K measurement interval. This positive control belongs to the established method, rather than a new constitutive invention. Similarity/numerical agreement checks the implementation; an interpolated curve is not a measured phase trajectory.

With elemental Sn exhausted, the outer through-flux closes, remaining Cu6Sn5 is consumed and its disappearance removes an interface. The endpoint is actual complete conversion inside the model, with amount fixed by Sn inventory, rather than an arbitrary maximum phase fraction.

The state and inputs are physically accessible through cross-sectional phase mapping and independent diffusion couples. The operation assumes existing product layers, local interfacial equilibrium, planar quasistationary transport and intact contact. It does not supply nucleation, porosity/grain evolution, finite interface resistance or mechanical quality.
