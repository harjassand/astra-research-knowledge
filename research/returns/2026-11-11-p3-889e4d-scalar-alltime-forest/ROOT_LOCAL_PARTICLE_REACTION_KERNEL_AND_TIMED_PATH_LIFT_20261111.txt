1. Exact input and scope

falling-factorial propensities c_e (z)_{alpha_e}, and 0<c_e<=1. The only channel
types used here are 2->1, 1->2, 1->1 and 0->0. Reverse labels, including actual
null and parallel marks, remain distinct. Suppose positive rational activities
a_s satisfy c_e a^{alpha_e}=c_bar(e) a^{beta_e}. Suppose also that an explicitly
given integer weight w_s>=1 is preserved by EVERY channel. Start at exactly the
given integer populations z0, with arbitrary deterministic positions of the
actually present particles and no additional particles. Let N=w.z0.

The paired-prefix compiler in P3_68FE8F_FULL_FINAL_20261111.md supplies precisely
this chemical interface when the original supplied positive integer weight is

nonzero payload columns and canonical zero-auxiliary initialization are retained
at the chemical-count level. The accepted A=0 implication can supply that
conservation premise. No general positive-A conservation premise is assumed.

Work on the unit flat three-torus. Each particle diffuses independently between
reaction events, with generator D_s(t) Delta for its current species. The unknown
profiles are deterministic measurable functions with d0<=D_s(t)<=1, where d0>0
is supplied rational. They are not state-dependent or chosen by an adversary.
Allow arbitrary predictable chemical-channel multipliers in [1-delta,1+delta],
which may depend on all prior marks and particle positions. Diffusion itself is
not perturbed by those multipliers. Particles are points; no hard-core exclusion,

Fix rational 0<r<=1/8. Let v=(2r)^3 and

An additional integer chemical slowdown h>=1 will be chosen. Diffusion is not
accelerated. Continuous random placement is a specified stochastic kernel, not

2. A local placement rule with exactly the right reverse kernel

For A+B->C, each ordered eligible reactant pair at positions x,y reacts with
coefficient c_e k_r(y-x)/h. Put C at x. For its reverse C->A+B, each C at x
reacts at rate c_bar(e)/h, leaves A at x and puts B at x+R, with R drawn from
density k_r. The displacement is local and has a finite rational description.
For a unary A->B, change the species at the same position, at rate c_e/h.
A 0->0 channel is an actual marked Poisson event at rate c_e/h. It needs no
particle position. Apply this rule separately to every private reverse pair.

The chemical-count convention is IMPORTANT. For 2A->C, use ordered DISTINCT
particles at rate c_e k_r(y-x)/h per ordered pair, rather than dividing by two.
The reverse release still has total rate c_bar(e)/h per C. These conventions
agree with (z_A)_2 and with the usual activity certificate, without a hidden
symmetry factor. One ordered particle is designated first only to define the
placement kernel; the actual configuration remains an unordered configuration.

Proof of spatial balance. Let mu be the independent Poisson point process for
each species, with intensity a_s on the unit torus. Its ordered factorial Palm
measure for the reactants has density a^{alpha_e}. Removing those reactants
leaves the same background Poisson process. The forward marked reaction flux
therefore has the common spatial measure
  h^{-1} c_e a^{alpha_e} dx k_r(R)dR times that background law.
The reverse release flux has exactly the same measure with c_bar(e)a^{beta_e}.
Their coefficients agree by the given certificate. This also proves the claim
for identical reactants: the ordered factorial Palm formula has already paid
the two orientations and the Poisson factorial. Unary and null pairs follow
directly. Independent diffusion preserves the uniform spatial factors.

Consequently all instantaneous nominal generators have the common invariant
Poisson-point measure and balanced reactive edge measures. With constant
diffusivities the complete time-homogeneous process is reversible. For varying
D_s(t), common invariance is retained; ordinary time-homogeneous path
reversibility is not asserted. Conditioning on an actual invariant chemical
class is permissible but no class normalizer is evaluated by a compiler.

3. A uniform integrated spatial mixing bound, with no initial entropy premise

At fixed chemical counts z, there are n=|z|_1<=N particles. In labelled
coordinates, the invariant position law pi_z is product uniform. Quotienting
by within-species permutations cannot increase total variation.

For one particle with elapsed integrated diffusivity a>=d0 s, Fourier expansion
of the torus heat kernel gives
  ||p_a(x,.)-1||_TV <= (1/2)[sum_{k!=0} exp(-8 pi^2 a |k|^2)]^{1/2}.
For a>=1, bound the one-dimensional nonzero sum by 3 exp(-72a), using pi>3
and j^2>=j. The three-dimensional sum is at most 63 exp(-72a), so the TV norm
is at most 4 exp(-36a). For a<1 the trivial bound one suffices. In particular,
at every a>=0 it is at most min(1,4 exp(-a)).

Product coupling now gives, from ANY initial positions and any starting time,
  b_N(s):=sup ||F_{z,t}(s;p,.)-pi_z||_TV
          <= min(1,4N exp(-d0 s)).
For N>=1,
  integral_0^infinity b_N(s) ds <= [1+log(4N)]/d0 <= I0,
  I0=(4+bit_length(N))/d0.
This rational majorant is computable directly from a binary integer. For N=0,
the actual mixing error is zero; the harmless choice I0=4/d0 is still valid.
The estimate is independent of reaction radius, individual count denominators,
unknown diffusivity values and actual particle placement. No product fibre,
position net or high-dimensional heat kernel is enumerated.

In particular, a deterministic initial configuration's infinite relative
entropy with respect to a continuous uniform position law is NOT an obstacle
to this mixing argument. The first physical waiting gap is included below.
No equilibrium initialization, burn-in interval or deleted residence time is
inserted.

4. Exact averaged rates and complete timed marked-path comparison

Let m_f be the number of fine directed chemical labels. If m_f=0, there are no
marks and the conclusion is exact. Otherwise set
  L0=m_f (1+N)^2/v.
All nominal chemical event intensities together are bounded by L0/h, at every
spatial state, since n<=N and k_r<=1/v. The same bound covers unary and null
labels. Under product uniform positions, averaging the local pair kernel gives
integral k_r=1. Thus the averaged fine-count generator has EXACT propensities
  h^{-1} c_e (z)_{alpha_e}.
This is an averaged generator, not a claim that the spatial count process is
Markov at finite h.

Fix a desired fine-network logical horizon T>0. In a coupling proof, use a
Poisson proposal clock of rate lambda=L0/h in physical time. Between proposals
only diffusion acts. Conditional on the previous joint history and the NEXT
proposal gap s, couple the actual positions after that gap to a fresh pi_z
sample, with failure probability at most b_N(s). Use the same chemical
acceptance and original fine label after a successful coupling. Average over
the fresh equilibrium positions to obtain exactly the stated averaged rates.
Both rejected proposals and arbitrary post-event positions are allowed.

Summing failures over the first proposal and subsequent proposals gives
  TV(nominal spatial complete fine marked path on [0,hT],
     nominal well-mixed complete fine marked path on [0,T],
     with the deterministic clock rescaling)
    <= lambda I0 (1+lambda hT)
     = (L0 I0/h)(1+L0 T).
The first term is at most lambda integral b_N(s)ds. For later proposals, their
mean number is at most lambda hT and each next exponential gap contributes at
most lambda integral b_N(s)ds. On coupling success, all accepted labels and
their rescaled event times coincide EXACTLY, including null and parallel marks.
The proof's proposal clock and equilibrium couplings are not physical devices.

For predictable chemical faults, first couple the faulty spatial process to
the nominal spatial process with the same Brownian motions, configurations and
entire fine marked histories up to the first mismatch. The aggregate mismatch
intensity is at most delta L0/h. Therefore, even if faults use all particle
positions and hidden marks,
  TV(faulty spatial path, nominal spatial path on [0,hT]) <= delta L0 T.
No conditional-equilibrium claim about the fault-controlled process is made.

For 0<epsilon<=1 choose
  h=1+ceil[8 epsilon^{-1} L0 I0 (1+L0 T)],
  delta=min(1/4, epsilon/[8 L0 (1+T)]).
The two errors above sum to at most epsilon/4. An existing well-mixed timed-path
compiler run with error epsilon/2 can therefore be composed with this lift,
with total error below epsilon and total physical clock h times its own clock.
Its observation map may hide private chemical labels but never deletes time.

6. Open boundaries of the result

here. For a general open positive-A network, stopping below a decoded population
cutoff gives the same spatial timed-path comparison, but does not pay the work
