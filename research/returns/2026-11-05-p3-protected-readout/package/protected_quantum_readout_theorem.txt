ACQUIRED PROTECTED QUANTUM READOUT
Complete construction, proof and costs
4 November 2026

RESULT AND SCOPE

An instrument can acquire the code of a specified family of protected quantum specimens and implement their entire logical Bell measurement using fixed destructive physical measurements followed by classical decoding. Known encoded reference states make the same reader a programmable logical Pauli detector.

The family consists of m disjoint square toric blocks of side L>=5, obscured by a fixed unknown physical permutation and fixed unknown single-qubit Clifford frames. There are k=2m logical qubits and N=kL^2 physical qubits. The promise concerns a family of already protected specimens; the reader does not create redundancy in an arbitrary unknown exposed input.

For local-stochastic support noise q<1/36 on paired sites, the corrected measurement channel differs from ideal logical Bell measurement by at most

  beta(N,L,q) = (8N/3) (6 sqrt(q))^L / (1-6 sqrt(q))

in half diamond norm, conditional on successful code acquisition. The statement includes arbitrary logical inputs and inaccessible references.

For an efficiently preparable hard family, the complete operation identifies a hidden signed logical Pauli using O(k) specimen copies asymptotically at fixed target error, polynomial classical work and polynomial noisy state-preparation work. An arbitrary adaptive single-copy learner retaining only classical information between specimens needs at least (2^k+1)/3 copies for distinguishing success 2/3 in the worst case. The physical-size lower bound is exp[Omega(N/log^2 N)], not exp[Omega(N)].

The components have substantial prior art. The contribution assembled and checked here is the full acquisition-to-measurement construction and its costs, including the improvement from exhaustive four-wire search to parity collisions. It is not a claim that every component is new, that all quantum experiments have the promised structure, or that an economical hardware implementation has been demonstrated.

1. PRECISE ACCESS AND NOISE

The reader knows wire labels, the structural family and a noise bound. The unknown code is fixed across calibration and data. Calibration states may have arbitrary logical payloads; their code must be the same. The inference application uses independent fresh copies of the same density operator.

Two specimen banks have matched physical interactions between identically labelled wires. The reader can perform calibrated one-qubit Pauli measurements and a fixed Bell measurement on each corresponding pair. This is an explicit on-chip access model. It is not supplied automatically by a travelling measurement field or by a communication-capacity theorem.

Use the local-stochastic CP-support model of [CFG, Definition39]. A channel W admits CP summands W_B supported on B, with

  W = sum_B W_B,
  ||sum_(B containing A) W_B||_diamond <= q^|A| for every A.

The complete sum is trace preserving; individual summands need not be. One site is a single physical qubit in calibration, and a corresponding physical pair in the Bell reader. Write q_c and q for these parameters.

Independent rare faults are a special case. Coherent or collective action within a faulty support is allowed. Arbitrary coherent drift on all sites, or an arbitrary continuous amplitude-damping channel, is not automatically a rare-support channel of small parameter. Additional device errors require their own justified budget.

All statistical claims assume fresh independent shots, or uniform conditional versions of the same bounds. The proofs do not certify unrestricted drift from a finite calibration sample.

2. IDENTIFY THE CODE WITHOUT ASSUMING A LOGICAL STATE

Let Pi project onto the underlying code. Distance greater than four implies that every Hermitian Pauli P of weight at most four obeys

  Pi P Pi = 0 or +Pi or -Pi.

The nonzero cases are exactly signed stabilizers. Thus every logical payload has the same low-weight signature. The distance promise excludes low-weight logical stabilizers of a selected codeword.

For weight w, let W_hit sum branches touching supp(P). Then
||W_hit||_diamond<=w q_c. All other Kraus operators commute with P, so

  W^dagger(P)-P = W_hit^dagger(P)-P W_hit^dagger(I).

The expectation bias is therefore at most 2w q_c. Genuine weight-four checks have magnitude at least 1-8q_c; nonchecks have magnitude at most 8q_c. Threshold 1/2 has gap g=1/2-8q_c.

For square tori with L>=5, the only nonidentity weight-four stabilizers are elementary stars and plaquettes. There are N of these and they generate the code. A four-edge cut of either square-torus graph isolates a single vertex; combining a nontrivial star and plaquette has support at least six. Products across disjoint blocks have weight at least eight.

An exhaustive acquisition is possible with
M_4=sum_(j=1)^4 3^j binom(N,j) candidates and

  S >=1296 g^(-2) ln(3M_4/delta_cal)

random product-measurement copies. Each candidate matches at least S/162 shots except with probability exp(-S/648); conditional Hoeffding error g/2 is at most 2 exp(-Sg^2/1296). Union bound proves the claim. This has O(log N) copies at fixed noise, but O(SN^4) classical work. The earlier exhaustive audit is preserved separately.

3. SPARSE ACQUISITION BY PARITY COLLISIONS

The selected algorithm reduces classical search by using more calibration copies. Put

  a=1-4q_c, b=1/2+4q_c, g=1/2-8q_c,
  t=ceil[2 ln N / ln(1/b)],
  R=ceil[81 a^(-t) ln(N/delta_m)],
  H=ceil[R(3N+N^2/4)/delta_t],
  u=ceil[2 g^(-2) ln(2H/delta_v)].

In each of R rounds, choose a random product Pauli basis and collect t+u fresh rows in that same basis. The first t rows are for discovery; the remaining u are held out for verification.

Let c_i be the t-bit outcome column at wire i. For every unordered wire pair form c_i XOR c_j. Sort using the smaller of this string and its complement as key. Equal keys on disjoint wire pairs propose a signed weight-four check. The complement allows either stabilizer sign.

A genuine check matches the chosen four axes with probability 1/81. Each discovery parity is correct with probability at least a. Hence its discovery probability in a round is at least a^t/81, and the chance that some check is never proposed is at most N exp(-Ra^t/81)<=delta_m.

A noncheck has either parity with probability at most b, so its collision probability is at most 2b^t. Overlapping pairs reduce to weight-two nonchecks and obey the same bound. Identical pairs are not compared. There are fewer than N^4/8 distinct unordered pair-pairs, and at most 3N true-check pairings per round. Since b^t<=N^(-2),

  expected collision comparisons <=R(3N+N^2/4).

Enforce the cap H, including overlapping comparisons. Exceeding it causes an explicit abort. Markov's inequality bounds this event by delta_t. There is no assumption that a hash bucket is small.

For every disjoint proposal, test its fresh u-row parity mean at absolute threshold 1/2 and retain its sign. Conditional on discovery, the chance of a wrong verification is at most 2 exp(-ug^2/2). At most H proposals are checked, giving total verification error <=delta_v. Thus

  delta_acq <=delta_m+delta_t+delta_v.

Source copies are R(t+u). Sorting and verification require

  O(RN^2 t log N + H(u+log N))

classical bit operations. Raw records require O(NR(t+u)) bits; rounds can be streamed. Sorting avoids an assumed constant-time hashing oracle.

At fixed noise and error budgets define

  alpha(q_c)=2 ln(1/a)/ln(1/b).

Copy cost is O(N^alpha polylog N), and classical acquisition is O(N^(2+alpha) polylog N). For q_c<1/36, a^4>b, hence alpha<1/2; the limiting exponent is approximately .4783295821. This is an explicit sparse-parity search, not an optimality claim over the classical sparse-parity literature [PARITY].

4. ACQUIRE GRAPHS, LOGICAL OPERATORS AND DISTANCE

Join learned checks that locally anticommute on a shared wire. Each block's resulting graph is connected and bipartite; two-coloring separates stars and plaquettes, up to their exchange. At every wire the two checks of each color have one common local axis, with different axes for the two colors. This gives the local Clifford interpretation.

One color supplies primal vertices, the other faces; physical wires supply edges and the dual graph. Verify incidences, commuting checks, ranks, simple face and vertex-link cycles, orientability and Euler characteristic. Compute a symplectic normalizer basis by binary elimination, block by block. Stabilizer signs are retained, because known-reference preparation requires the correct signed codespace.

Distance is also acquired. Label each edge by its intersections with the two opposite logical representatives. Build the four-sheet F_2^2 homology cover, whose edges update this two-bit label. BFS from every (vertex,0) to (same vertex,nonzero sheet) finds the shortest nontrivial cycle. Run this on both syndrome graphs. No decoder or distance oracle is supplied.

The result is in the learner's acquired logical frame. A manufacturer's private logical naming convention is not identifiable from the code alone.

The asymptotic choice L=Theta(log k) describes the constructed specimen family. The reader cannot enlarge an arbitrary existing specimen's distance. It measures the actual distance and uses the resulting error bound.

5. COMPLETE LOGICAL MEASUREMENT FROM A FIXED PHYSICAL READER

On each corresponding pair apply CNOT, then H to its first qubit, then two Z readouts. This measures laboratory XX and ZZ. If their outcome bits are x,z,

  YY = -(XX)(ZZ).

The outcome bit for P tensor P, for P=i^(v dot w)X^vZ^w, is

  v dot x+w dot z+v dot w (mod 2).

Every learned local Clifford is therefore applied as an affine classical relabeling of fixed laboratory outcomes. No quantum unpermutation or learned quantum rotation is required.

In the acquired CSS frame ideal rows satisfy H_X x=0 and H_Z z=0. Correct observed syndromes by minimum-weight T-joins on the acquired graphs, implemented through shortest paths and minimum-weight perfect matching. Pair the corrected strings with the acquired logical representatives to get a 2k-bit logical Bell label.

Call support B good if no nontrivial simple cycle gamma in either graph has at least half its positions in B. This is a simultaneous criterion for every Pauli error on B. If E is supported in B and C is a minimum-weight correction with the same boundary, a nontrivial cycle in E XOR C would satisfy |C intersect gamma|<=|gamma|/2 by minimality. At least half the cycle would therefore lie in E, a contradiction.

For a length-l cycle the CP norm of supports occupying at least half its sites is at most 2^l q^(l/2). Counting degree-four nonbacktracking walks and summing l>=L gives the conservative bound beta stated above. Peierls decoding and stochastic-support toric correction are established machinery [DKLP, CFG AppendixD].

For the measurement interface, let J embed two logical registers, Q_y be a logical Bell projector, and Pi_y sum physical Bell outcomes decoded as y. On a good support every Pauli E satisfies

  Pi_y E J Q_z = delta_(y,z) E J Q_z.

By Pauli expansion the same holds for every supported Kraus operator K, giving Pi_y K J=K J Q_y. Let B_bad sum bad CP supports and D=J^dagger B_bad^dagger(I)J. Then 0<=D<=beta I. The good instrument has effects Q_y(I-D)Q_y; D commutes with Q_y. The missing ideal instrument and the actual bad instrument are both CP of norm at most beta. Therefore

  (1/2)||M_corrected-M_logical_Bell||_diamond<=beta.

This proves the entire measurement channel, including conditional states of any inaccessible reference, for arbitrary joint logical states of the two banks. No Choi-state-only inference is used.

6. PROGRAMMABLE PAULI MEASUREMENT AND COMPLETE SIGNED LEARNING

Prepare a known encoded stabilizer reference sigma_P with P eigenvalue +1. Coarsening logical Bell outcomes by the P tensor P eigenvalue has joint effects (I +/- P tensor P)/2. Tracing the reference gives input effects (I +/- P)/2. Thus the same fixed reader implements logical Pauli measurement, with its channel error bounded by beta plus the budgeted factory approximation.

For the learning separation compare rho_0=I/d with

  rho_(sP)=(I+sP)/d, d=2^k,

where P is an unknown nonidentity unsigned logical Pauli and s=+/-1. The alternative has one bit less entropy. Its purity difference is only 1/d; no constant-shot SWAP-mean estimate is claimed.

Write P=i^(u dot v)X^uZ^v. Identical-copy Bell rows are uniform on the affine hyperplane

  u dot x+v dot z=u dot v (mod 2),

whereas the null produces uniform rows in F_2^(2k). The sign s cancels in this stage.

Take M rows and subtract the first from the others. Binary elimination distinguishes full rank from a codimension-one span and identifies unsigned P. The rank-failure bound is 2^(2k-M+1). After identifying P, prepare sigma_P offline; request a fresh unknown specimen only when this reference is ready. One further corrected Bell row yields s. The reference can be the product of +1 eigenstates of the individual logical Pauli factors, with identity factors assigned |0>. It is a known stabilizer preparation.

With all parameters uniform, complete failure is bounded by

  delta_acq+(M+1)beta+2^(2k-M+1)+delta_factory.

Total prepared banks are C=R(t+u)+2M+2, including the one known reference. At fixed target error choose M=O(k), L=Theta(log k), N=kL^2. Since alpha<1/2, calibration copies are o(k) up to logarithmic factors. Total copies are O(k), and all execution costs below are polynomial.

For the converse, allow arbitrary adaptive single-copy POVMs and unlimited classical computation, but no quantum information retained between specimens. Give the entire code for free. Choose P uniformly among d^2-1 Paulis and s uniformly +/-1. Refine POVMs to rank-one outcomes. At a fixed transcript put a_t(P)=<phi_t|P|phi_t>. Adaptivity does not change that these vectors are fixed at that transcript. Pauli completeness gives E_P a_t^2=1/(d+1).

The alternative/null likelihood ratio satisfies

  L=E_P[(product_t(1+a_t)+product_t(1-a_t))/2]
   >=E_P product_t sqrt(1-a_t^2)
   >=1-sum_t E_P a_t^2
   =1-T/(d+1).

AM-GM and product(1-z_t)>=1-sum z_t prove this even at a_t=+/-1. Transcript total variation is at most T/(d+1); equal-prior success 2/3 needs T>=(2^k+1)/3. This supplies the exact rank-half endpoint within the established learning-tree framework [CCHL, CGK].

The lower bound also survives any fixed hypothesis-independent output channel, by pulling physical POVMs back to logical POVMs. Hypothesis-dependent preparation faults could leak P. The comparison is consequently minimax: the quantum upper bound is uniform over allowed noise, while the lower bound already holds on a noiseless or fixed-channel subfamily. It is not a pointwise assertion about every faulty source.

7. KNOWN-STATE FACTORIES AND FULL EXECUTION COST

The hard family is efficiently preparable. Choose eigenvalue bits of P's nonidentity logical factors uniformly subject to their product being s; use fresh independent bits for every specimen. Identity factors are uniformly mixed. Averaging the product eigenstates gives exactly rho_(sP). This needs O(k) classical random bits and parity operations, not a Haar-state oracle.

Each block's specified physical state is a known stabilizer state: its signed checks plus two logical eigenvalues. Clifford synthesis using H,S,CNOT prepares it from known local states, including logical Y [AG]. Ideal synthesis size is Theta=O(kL^4) before any routing; use the actual compiled Theta including routing, resets and idles. Reference preparation uses the acquired signed checks and logical operators. The source must support the chosen factory's gate/access model; no nearest-neighbour hardware threshold is invented.

A governing physical output interface is [CFG, Corollary45, Eq138]. Under independent circuit-level stochastic noise p<1/c it gives a physical factory of size Theta P(r), where P is a fixed polynomial, with

  ||Phi_physical-W Phi_ideal||_diamond<=epsilon_f=4Theta(cp)^r,
  W is local stochastic with parameter 2cp.

The compiled construction includes physical output interfaces. An ideal proof decoder is not substituted for a physical device.

For C prepared banks the total variation contribution is at most C epsilon_f/2. Choose

  r>=ceil[ln(2 C Theta/delta_factory)/ln(1/(cp))].

Total source locations are at most C Theta P(r), polynomial in the problem size. This is an approximation budget, not necessarily a heralded failure probability. Classical choices may give different output-noise channels; the upper bounds are uniform and extend to their mixtures.

Two factory outputs contribute at most 4cp on paired sites. Local-stochastic channels compose additively. If the readout has b independent noisy locations per pair, its contribution is at most 1-(1-p)^b<=bp. Require q<=4cp+q_reader<1/36 and q_c<=2cp+q_calibration<1/36.

One explicit finite reader has matched-pair Hamiltonians J Z_A Z_B, independent local controls of norm at most Omega/2, and terminal Z measurement. Use

  CZ=e^(-i pi/4)e^(+i pi Z_A/4)e^(+i pi Z_B/4)e^(-i pi Z_A Z_B/4),
  CNOT=(I tensor H)CZ(I tensor H).

The Bell circuit has four successive pulse groups: initial H_B; parallel Z phases; ZZ interaction; final parallel H_A,H_B. Fixed-axis trapezoids with ramp time tau_r<=min{pi/(4|J|),pi/(2Omega)} preserve the exact areas. Their finite duration obeys

  tau_B<=5pi/(2Omega)+pi/(4|J|)+4tau_r+tau_read,

and slew is at most max{Omega/2,|J|}/tau_r. There are N physical pair couplers; total hardware and aggregate control grow with N.

Finite control precision can be charged separately from stochastic faults. If
there are G implemented pulses, a uniform bound nu on each integrated
Hamiltonian error contributes at most G nu to final half diamond distance by
Duhamel's inequality and circuit telescoping. A control budget delta_ctrl is
therefore met by nu<=delta_ctrl/G, requiring O(log(G/delta_ctrl)) parameter bits
at bounded pulse scales. This specifies the required calibrated accuracy; it
does not infer it from a reduced master equation or from these simulations.
If this extra error is present, add delta_ctrl to all final error budgets.

For actual independent lambda D[Z] on the two qubits during this window, L^dagger L is scalar. The no-jump branch is the ideal controlled evolution times a scalar, while the branch with at least one jump is an arbitrary pair-supported CPTP map. Its probability is 1-exp(-2lambda tau_B). Thus this specified physical noise fits the support model directly; detector error is added separately. Pure idle dephasing has Z-error probability (1-exp(-2lambda tau))/2. Cavity leakage a is not relabelled as Z noise.

Two synchronized preparation banks, or protected buffering inside factories, keep the bare release skew bounded. A first output cannot be left unprotected through a growing second preparation time. At rate Gamma a delay tau contributes at most 1-exp(-Gamma tau). Classical acquisition can finish before new data are requested, or all raw measurements can be collected first. No quantum memory is required during classical decoding.

Each Bell row costs N CNOTs, N Hadamards and 2N readouts at the abstract circuit level; the pulse decomposition above pays their physical realization. Calibration costs O(N) local gates and N readouts per specimen. Reset and fresh preparation are charged C times. Source wall time is bounded by the compiled circuit depth and its physical gate/measurement/feed-forward times; conservative serialization gives at most C Theta P(r) gate-time units. Factory width is that of the explicit compiled circuit, with two-bank hardware paid separately. Reliable classical computation is a declared conventional premise.

Blockwise dense blossom decoding uses O(kL^6) operations per row; final elimination uses O(Mk^2) bit operations. Acquisition costs are stated in section3. Integer arithmetic suffices for graph decoding; the rational certificates avoid floating-point assertions.

8. EXECUTED CONSTRUCTION AND FINITE LIMITS

The provided programs generate actual product-measurement records through exact stabilizer algebra. On a hidden [[50,2,5]] torus, 1000 random bases and 256 copies per basis give 256000 calibration copies. Parity collisions use 12 discovery rows and 244 fresh verification rows per basis.

The recorded sparse run uses 1225000 pair records, 367551 collision comparisons and 113269 quartet verifications, recovering all 50 checks with no false positives or misses. The preserved exhaustive run used 230300000 pattern/support tests. The acquired graph has 25 vertices, 50 edges, 25 faces, genus one, ranks 24+24 and distances five computed from its homology covers.

The default decoder is polynomial integer weighted blossom, with the upstream BSD license preserved. The small and replicated-block logical-data audits and the additional signed-reference audit are recorded in the JSON. Reference stabilizer specifications are checked, while the noisy physical factory itself is supplied by the cited theorem, not numerically simulated.

The larger analytic sparse-acquisition certificate uses N=11552, k=32, L=19, q=q_c=.003, R=1848, t=28, u=348 and collision cap 61717345536000. Its unsigned stage has 74 Bell rows and 694996 total prepared specimens, with error below .007220 plus the factory budget. Signed completion uses 75 corrected rows, 694998 prepared banks and 866400 physical Bell gates, with certified learning/readout error below .007251 plus factory and any separate control-approximation budgets. These large resource counts are explicit; this is not an economical-device claim.

Finite runs do not establish a threshold, a physical noise model or performance beyond their stated stochastic simulation. Exact rational tail bounds and the analytic channel proof provide the uniform guarantees. The full-register data audit reuses an acquired small block; full large-register calibration is not reported as executed.

9. COMPARISON AND PRESERVED SCOPE

[LPTO] already learns stabilizer codes from product measurements. [HG] already recognizes Bell-measurement compatibility with stabilizer codes. [CGK] already demonstrates a protected two-copy advantage using a known HaPPY code under erasures. [KPC] supplies general uploading after an experiment-dependent transduction. [CFG] supplies physical faulty output interfaces and toric correction. The present operation uses these at their stated scopes and earns the intervening acquired decoder and fixed measurement interface.

The consequence is a programmable logical measurement device for an unfamiliar protected representation, with a rigorous full signed-learning application. It is applicable to protected quantum processors, memories and simulators meeting the physical access and noise assumptions. The theorem supplies no protection promise for arbitrary natural specimens.

The previous lossy assisted-communication theorem, direct-acquisition obstruction, finite receiver, explicit purification/teleportation and finite-cavity certificate are preserved in the included original archive. The finite-cavity point is not used to infer uniform small-efficiency bandwidth performance. P1's exposed-input resource law remains intact: known resources are prepared before fresh unknown data arrive, and the logical information is already protected. Actual intrinsic Z noise and cavity a leakage remain distinct.
