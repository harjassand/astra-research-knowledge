Native renewal resolvents and zero-mean corridor transport

1. Conditional resolvent closure for update damage

Let A be physical space-time update sites. Suppose an ACTUAL recurrent
module theorem, relative to the rule on actual incoming decoded symbols,
establishes a nonnegative damage inequality
 E_a<=X_a+sum_b L_ab E_b,
where L is a deterministic nonnegative causal matrix. This hypothesis
concerns complete off-code physical state, not only a finite list of ports.
If both Schur sums of L are bounded by q<1, finite causal iteration gives
 E<=R X, R=sum_(n>=0)L^n,
with both Schur sums of R at most1/(1-q). Thus a genuine physical reset
margin can turn a one-step kernel into a whole-run kernel. Small integrated
linear response alone does not provide the q<1 renewal hypothesis.

For a fixed degree d>1 and damage X_a=sum_z K_az N_z^d, ordinary Schur
summability is insufficient for the root-weight probability argument.
Assume instead BOTH Schur sums of L_ab^(1/d) are<=q_d<1 and those of
K_az^(1/d) are<=W_d. Subadditivity of the1/d power, expansion of each L^n,
and matrix product bounds yield
 sup_a sum_z[(R K)_az]^(1/d),
 sup_z sum_a[(R K)_az]^(1/d) <=W_d/(1-q_d).
Then E_a>=beta implies
 sum_z[(R K)_az]^(1/d)N_z>=beta^(1/d).
The exact weighted Poisson theorem pays a local-stochastic update error
parameter with all rare count clusters retained. No positive exponential
moment of N_z^d is assumed. These are explicit sufficient PHYSICAL interface
conditions, not properties already proved for MHD. A different cluster
representation may succeed when these strict root budgets fail.

Logical errors and defective updates must be separated. A bit flipped to
another valid symbol can affect arbitrarily many later correct updates;
its difference from the original fault-free word need not fade. E must
therefore compare to a renewed code for the ACTUAL incoming word. It cannot
be defined as distance from one eternal fault-free computation and then
assumed summable. The completed return's nonlinear backward-port identity
has exactly this remaining off-code renewal gap.

2. A fixed zero-mean lane flow implements an ideal neighbour translation

For a fixed spatial period a, choose a smooth a-periodic scalar v with
mean zero, equal to1 on a strip around the codelet support and negative on
an otherwise unused strip. Let u(t,x)=c(t)v(x_l)e_j with j!=l. This is
solenoidal and has mean zero, and its exact flow is
 x_j -> x_j+v(x_l)integral c, all other coordinates fixed.
If integral c=a, every codelet supported inside the positive plateau is
translated by exactly one cell. Its deformation gradient is the identity
there, so the ideal magnetic pushforward is just that translation. Pick
l!=1, and the constant bias H_*=e1 is unchanged by the whole shear because
H_*.grad u=0. This works for j=1,2,3 by choosing an allowed transverse l.
The inverse lane has zero code support and supplies the compensating mean
fluid displacement. No constant-mean velocity or external cell actuator is
being supplied by this kinematic identity.

This proves an exact spatial representation of a neighbour operation with
a fixed periodic geometry. It does not implement it through the allowed
momentum-only coupled MHD dynamics. A prescribed velocity field is not an
additional control. Magnetic Lorentz feedback, rough errors and cleanup
of the empty lanes remain in that native realization problem.

3. Fast mechanical ramping does not by itself renew the lanes

The previously identified Stokes phase limitation persists. Take a smooth
ramp U with U(0)=U(1)=0 and integral_0^1 U=1, and apply only the mechanical
force (a/T^2)U'(t/T)v e_j from rest. For the linear Stokes shear,
 u(T)=a[ integral_0^1 U(s)nu(Ts)ds] Delta(v e_j)+O(aT)
in each fixed smooth norm, uniformly over the supplied coefficient range.
This follows by heat Duhamel expansion and integration by parts. The main
residual does not vanish by decreasing T. A flat corridor moves this leading
residual outside the code support but does not erase its future diffusion
back into it. This is the earlier affine-viscosity/phase obstruction at its
actual route scope, not a new all-control negative.

A native phase can instead include that residual in a controlled recurring
state, pay a different physical cleanup, or use a different representation.
It must prove the renewal bound, not assume that velocity and unused lanes
have been returned to zero after an ideal magnetic map.

4. Actual scope of the newly available MHD operation

The complete unequal-viscosity/resistivity propagator now has a summable
port kernel. Its heat-time analytic/Dyson expansion retains the distribution
of alternating Alfven translations rather than taking a supremum over a
ballistic segment. Compact port moments and zero-mean kicks give a t^-3/2
spatial-sum tail. Testing the FULL nonlinear weak equations backward with
solenoidal ports removes Leray exactly from the observable identity and
leaves actual stresses bounded by sqrt(E_ref,j)e_j+e_j^2. Those full e_j
are not already controlled by the ports or clock counts.

Predictable fault weights are admissible only with pathwise row/column
budgets and no retrospective future amplification. Higher fixed probe
moments allow root-weight summability for a supplied degree-d count bound
when r>6d-4; r=9 covers a quadratic count bound. The PDE being quadratic
does not prove that fixed-degree damage bound for a recurrent processor.

The explicit scalar masked-heat/polynomial reshaper is a useful canonical
target across every material word and Bloch sector. Its masks, translations,
copying, heat action and polynomial operations still need the actual MHD
mechanical realization. Low bulk modes can be invisible to high-moment ports
while carrying unbounded energy density; native logical renewal and the
uniform nonlinear bulk-energy enclosure are separate physical obligations.
No new source, native gate, sensor or full threshold theorem is imported.
