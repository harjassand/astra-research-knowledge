   https://doi.org/10.1007/s10107-016-1009-3
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.05666
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1502.05666

   The primary PDF was inspected, including Theorem 4, the constructive
   conjugate remark, Corollary 1, and cyclic Remark 3. These supply the
   exact finite-data criterion and explicit global witness. Their
   mathematical attribution is retained. The use of negative mu here
   is justified by shifting the declared potential class to a convex
   beta-smooth h, not by changing the source theorem's assumptions.

5. Saugata Basu. Algorithms in Real Algebraic Geometry: A Survey.
   https://www.math.purdue.edu/~sbasu/raag_survey2011_final.pdf

   The primary author's survey of algorithms was inspected, especially
   Theorem 2.4 on cylindrical algebraic decomposition and the effective
   quantifier-elimination/bit-complexity discussion around Theorem 2.18.
   Ordinary complete CAD is used as a charged accepted algorithm. The
   main antecedents are Collins and Basu--Pollack--Roy, credited in the
   proof. No CAD executable was installed or run here; it is not
   represented as an observed numerical result or a unit-cost oracle.

6. David Angeli, Eduardo D. Sontag. Monotone Control Systems.
   IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control 48(10), 1684--1698 (2003).
   https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.2003.817920
   https://sontaglab.org/FTPDIR/angeli-sontag-monotone-TAC03.pdf
   https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0206133

   Author-hosted primary PDF retrieved; the order/positive-flow conditions,
   cooperative characterization and variational positivity were inspected.
   Classical comparison is credited. Our explicit divided-difference

   The simple comparison argument used here is supplied in full and does
   not invoke the paper's more elaborate feedback/small-gain theorems.
