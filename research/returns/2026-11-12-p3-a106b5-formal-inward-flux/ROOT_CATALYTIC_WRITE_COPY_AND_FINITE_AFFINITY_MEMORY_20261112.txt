Catalytic WRITE and COPY interfaces for the finite-affinity memory

1. Scope and actual ports

This calculation extends the explicit ideal stochastic memory with birth and
death rates lambda(n)=6n(n-1)/V+6V and
mu(n)=n(n-1)(n-2)/V^2+11n. It constructs chemical WRITE and non-destructive
source-to-target COPY under specified catalytic gate occupations. It is not
an autonomous clock, spatial circuit, binary lowering or finite closed fuel
reservoir. In particular, setting a gate concentration for a stated interval
is a declared input port; its endogenous generation, removal and timing have
not been constructed. Every actual fuel transfer is nevertheless counted.

Use distinct source and target memory species X,Y, with their respective
memory channels. Put x=n_X/V,y=n_Y/V. The underlying density drift is
 f(y)=-(y-1)(y-2)(y-3), f'(y)=1-3(y-2)^2<=1.
All actual directed-channel coefficients may independently have arbitrary
predictable factors in[1-delta0,1+delta0], delta0=2^-14. The policy may use
the entire source/target/gate history. No faulty equilibrium or independence
between module errors is assumed.

2. WRITE through a finite-affinity catalytic port

For a requested bit b, use a fixed fuel species F_b of held concentration
c_b=1 if b=0 and c_b=3 if b=1. Hold its supplied catalyst C_b at count16V
during the write interval and use
 C_b+F_b <-> C_b+Y,
with equal mass-action coefficients1/V in the two directions. The added
aggregate rates are16 c_b V and16 n_Y. In the density limit the target drift
is f(y)+16(c_b-y). Its derivative is at most-15 globally. For c_b=1 this
drift is (1-y)(y^2-5y+22); for c_b=3 it is
(3-y)(y^2-3y+18). Both bracket factors are at least63/4. There is one stable
well at the requested value, with a fixed rate margin.

On0<=y<=4 the total target rate is bounded by400V; finite-V drift corrections
are at most80/V and rate faults add at most400delta0 in density drift.
Density martingale bounds plus the strictly contracting drift show that a
fixed write interval1 brings any y0 in[1/2,7/2] within1/16 of c_b except
with probability<=2 exp(-V/2^28). This conservative fixed constant follows
from the stopped Freedman inequality and the scalar comparison below;
no horizon-dependent rate precision or increasing chemical potential is used.
When the actual catalyst is turned off, the original memory retains the bit.
The turn-off mechanism remains an unconstructed physical port operation.

3. A COPY reaction that does not consume the unknown source

Use fixed fuel concentrations F=25/4 and G=1/1024. Hold a supplied catalyst
C at countV during a copy interval and use the reversible channels
 C+X+F <-> C+X+Y, equal coefficients128/V^2,
 C+G   <-> C+Y,   equal coefficients512/V.
The first has THREE carriers per side; no binary implementation is claimed.
The additional target rates are
 lambda_copy=800 n_X+V/2,
 mu_copy=128 n_X n_Y/V+512 n_Y.
Every copy channel leaves n_X EXACTLY unchanged. Its clocks and the target
may affect the fault policy, but the source's own drift and barrier estimates
already cover such full-history rate policies.

The leading target drift during COPY is
 f(y)+128[x(25/4-y)+4(1/1024-y)].
The clamped target value from the second term is
 (25x/4+1/256)/(x+4).
For x<=5/4 this is below3/2; for x>=11/4 it is above5/2. Thus the source
need not be numerically read or known exactly. Low and high source basins
select different target basins through the actual catalytic propensities.
The two fuel concentrations are fixed, independent of the unknown bit.

For a source remaining in[3/4,5/4] or[11/4,13/4], stop the target at4V.
Its rate is at most2^13 V. The faulty density drift differs from the displayed
drift by at most1/2+80/V. On the low-source comparison, at y=3/2 the nominal
drift is -63/8; on the high-source comparison, at y=5/2 it is327/8. The
fault and falling-factorial corrections leave strict margins. The derivative
of the comparison drift is at most-607 on the stated source range.

Let M be the stopped target density martingale. On a copy interval of length1
it has jumps of magnitude1/V and predictable quadratic variation<=2^13/V.
Freedman's inequality gives
 P(sup|M|>=1/64)<=2 exp(-V/2^28).
For clarity, the exponent2^28 is a conservative fixed choice, not an optimized
constant. Compare y-M with the deterministic upper or lower scalar equation
using x=5/4 or x=11/4 and the extra drift bound1/2+80/V. Monotonicity gives
an additional deviation at most2 sup|M|. For V>=2^20, the comparison equilibria
remain below3/2 and above5/2, and the initial discrepancy contracts by at most
exp(-607). Starting y0 in[1/2,7/2], the target stays below4 and at time1 obeys
 y(1)<=13/8 for a low source, y(1)>=19/8 for a high source,
apart from the displayed failure event or a source-band exit. The stopping
bootstrap is valid because the comparison and the martingale margin stay
strictly below4. This is a conditional component theorem on actual chemistry,
not an invocation of a supplied logical COPY gate.

4. Source confinement and subsequent target retention

The source can be kept in the bands needed above using the original memory's
pointwise rate-box barriers. Put r=1025/1024. Four protected intervals are
 [3/4,7/8] upward, [9/8,5/4] downward,
 [11/4,23/8] upward, [25/8,13/4] downward.
For the density leading rates lambda=6x^2+6 and mu=x^3+11x, form
 (1-delta0)good-(1+delta0)r bad,
where good is lambda on upward intervals and mu on downward intervals.
An exact degree-three Bernstein certificate on eight equal rational
subintervals gives these minimum coefficients, respectively:
 2469597033/8589934592,
 102075405/536870912,
 1238758713/8589934592,
 122321325/536870912.
All exceed100/2^20. The finite-V correction is bounded by100/V, so the
strict barriers persist for V>=2^20. This exact rational certificate was
checked for these four intervals; it is not a general polynomial oracle.

The corresponding r^n or r^-n supermartingales bound each crossing attempt
by exp(-V/32768), with room for integer rounding. Count attempts using the
actual memory-firing budget, even under a policy that inspects copy events.
Starting at n_X=V or3V, source exit by time t is at most
 2^23 V(1+t)exp(-V/32768).
After COPY ends, a lower target at<=13V/8 or upper target at>=19V/8 is also
separated from its wrong-bit separator by a protected width at leastV/8.
The original rate-box argument pays its later wrong-well crossing in the
same way. A target observed within its wider basin is an actual density
memory state; it is not a requirement of exact microscopic restoration.

Consequently a COPY plus a finite idle retention interval has an error bound
of the form
 4 exp(-V/2^28)+2^26 V(2+t)exp(-V/32768).
A fixed choice V=2^40[1+ceil(log2((2+t)/epsilon))] pays this with volume
logarithmic in duration/error. The factor2^40 is deliberately conservative.
It changes material amount rather than chemical affinity or microscopic
precision. A single finite module is not reliable forever at positive noise.

5. All actual tails and marked fuel work

Let S=n_X+n_Y. During COPY, the only added positive contribution to LS is
bounded by (1+delta0)(800x+1/2)V. Absorb its linear x term into one half
of the source's cubic negative drift. Using the original memory inequalities
gives, conservatively,
 LS<=2^17 V-(n_X^3+n_Y^3)/(8V^2),
 total actual channel rate<=2^14 V[1+x^3+y^3].
The same bounds cover a supplied WRITE port and idle phases with at most one
listed port enabled, and arbitrary predictable switching within these stated
occupations. Stopping and integrating prove nonexplosion and
 E integral_0^t(n_X^3+n_Y^3)/V^2<=8S0+2^20 Vt.
Therefore all memory/copy/write firings have expectation<=C V(1+t) for fixed
computable C when S0<=8V. Population supremum is bounded by S0+F_all and has
the same linear-time bound. Gate catalysts have explicitly supplied fixed
occupations and are separately counted as prepared matter; this estimate
does not account for an unspecified controller changing their occupation.

All paired reactions have equal nominal forward/reverse coefficients in a
common volume reference. Their closed fuel-resolved systems have exact
detailed balance at unit concentrations. The held A,B,F,G values drive finite
marked chemical affinities. Absolute bath work per event is bounded by8 in
these units since |log(25/4)|<2 and |log(1/1024)|<7 and the original memory
fuels retain their previous bounds. Thus the actual absolute marked chemical
work is<=8F_all, including every rare failure, reverse and futile cycle.
The aggregate density law is not a substitute for the individual fuel labels.

The maintained baths, port timing and molecular preparation are declared
resources. Marked chemical work is not the unspecified actuator work of an
external apparatus implementing arbitrary rate faults. Finite-reservoir, endogenous timing, repeated circuit composition,
spatial communication, binary lowering and physical error containment need
their own paid constructions. In particular this COPY is well-mixed and
species-addressed, and is not a native spatial wire or a new generic
all-network theorem. It was derived from the explicit memory rates rather
than importing an unavailable source or a controller theorem.
