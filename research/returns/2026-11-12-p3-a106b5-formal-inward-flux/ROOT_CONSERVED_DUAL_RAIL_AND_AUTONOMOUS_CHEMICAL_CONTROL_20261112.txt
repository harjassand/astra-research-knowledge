Conserved dual-rail memory, powered copying and autonomous chemical control

1. A different population contract

The arbitrary-network all-time compiler remains open at its stated generality.
Its new full-support inward-cone certificate is algebraic, not an enabled
physical flux. A different device can instead conserve its computing carriers
exactly. If every dynamic species has an integer weight w_s>=1 and every
emitted reaction preserves sum w_s n_s=M, population and nonexplosion are
immediate. For r elementary channels, fixed bounded microscopic coefficients
and vessel volume Omega>=1, the total actual marked rate is bounded by
 C r(1+M+M^2/Omega).
Fuel chemostats of fixed concentration only modify the fixed constants; an
emitted channel must still have at most two molecules per side BEFORE their
elimination. Actual all-event and marked bath-work costs are therefore linear
in time on EVERY path law, including failed computations. This operation
does not solve the arbitrary open-network compiler and needs no such theorem.

2. A complete stochastic conservative memory at fixed finite reverse bias

Let n_A+n_B=V, n=n_A. At module volume V use the exact birth/death rates

           +(V-n)(V-n-1)(V-n-2)/V^2+(V-n)/64,

Negative-looking factorial products are zero at their disabled integer
states. These are two reversible autocatalytic pairs and an unbiased A/B
interconversion. All physical transitions change n by one and conserve V.
All six directed channels may have arbitrary predictable factors within
1+/-2^-14. The total rate is at most64V, so
 E F_all(t)<=64Vt with no population-tail exception.

The leading density drift at x=n/V is
 f(x)=(2x-1)[17x(1-x)-65/64].
There are stable outer roots and an unstable root1/2. The outer roots solve
x(1-x)=65/1088 and lie near0.064 and0.936. No first invention of bistability
or dual-rail restoration is claimed.

The positive reverse coefficient1 is physically important. The coarse
majority ratios16:1 in both directions cannot simultaneously be equilibrium
detailed balance on A,B. Resolve them with separate fixed fuel pairs:
 2A+B+F_A <->3A+W_A,

with equal raw forward/reverse constants and concentrations F_A=F_B=16,

4. A conserved, non-destructive COPY at a held gate

For source fraction x and target fraction y, add two biased catalytic pairs
that leave both source rails unchanged. Choose k=128 and u=1/16. With a gate
held fully ON, their density drift contribution is exactly
 k[(1-u)x+u-(1+u)y].
For example, source A catalyses B_target->A_target with rate k and its reverse
with rate ku; source B catalyses A_target->B_target with rate k and reverse
ku. The explicit fuel-resolved versions use the source rail, target rail,
and one F/W pair, so are order THREE; an additional conserved gate catalyst
raises this to order FOUR. Equal raw constants and held F/W=16 realize the
finite ratio. All carrier conservation relations survive this coupling.

For x<=1/8 the imposed target centre is<=23/136, while for x>=7/8 it is
>=113/136. The intrinsic memory drift has derivative<=207/32, so combined
with the COPY term the scalar comparison has derivative<-129. At y=13/64
and x=1/8 the copy drift is exactly-37/8 and the intrinsic drift is also
negative; the reflected high-bit statement follows by symmetry.

Within the invariant unit interval, total target rate is bounded by256V.
Rate faults change density drift by at most256delta and finite factorial
corrections by at most100/V. A stopped density martingale M on an interval1
has jumps1/V and quadratic variation<=256/V. Freedman at threshold1/256
gives P(sup|M|>=1/256)<=2exp(-cV) for fixed computable c>0. Monotone scalar
comparison for y-M adds at most2sup|M|. The low comparison equilibrium stays
below13/64, so after the fixed interval, from either initial bit basin,

except for this failure or a source-band exit. This gives an actual
population-level COPY certificate at the stated held gate. It retains
source backaction through its unchanged catalytic counts rather than
assuming an observed or numerically supplied unknown input bit.

An autonomous gate is NOT supplied. Idle leakage, fanout sequestration,
phase changes, input stability and completion acknowledgements must be
proved for an actual coupled controller. Holding a port exactly ON/OFF is
not a thermodynamic clock or a noiseless classical scheduler.

5. Physical concentration and capacity are charged

In a single well-mixed vessel, K modules each holding V carriers cannot all
have unit concentration in volume V while total concentration is bounded.
Take vessel volume proportional to KV. Third-order dynamic propensities then
slow by a factor of order K^2. This is a polynomial collision-time cost, not
a reason to increase microscopic rate constants with capacity.
The unary symmetric flip can be replaced by 2Z+B<->2Z+A with an explicitly
conserved reference species of count V and fixed coefficient1/64. Its time
scale then matches the third-order feedback; its finite-V multiplier1-1/V
is covered by the same margin. A supplied gate species of count V similarly
makes the copy reaction third-order in dynamic carriers. This is a concrete
mass-action scaling observation, not a completed common-clock or elementary
architecture. Fast reversible intermediates can carry actual computing
carrier mass; a proposed lowering must keep that conservation and control
sequestration instead of hiding extra independent populations.

There is also a simple information constraint. With m well-mixed species and
at most M molecules, there are at most(M+1)^m count states. Storing arbitrary
S input bits in one such initial count state requires m log2(M+1)>=S. A
fixed-species, polynomial-molecule device cannot secretly provide arbitrary
linear-size digital memory by infinitely precise concentrations. An actual
chemical architecture may instead pay a growing number of species addresses,
or use spatial/bonded memory. The former is a species-addressed chemical
computer, not a fixed physical alphabet or spatial wire already obtained.
