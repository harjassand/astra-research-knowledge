2. The outer gas/water barrier is not the inner ionic separator
A gas-permeable covering of a wet capture interface sees only tens of pascals of atmospheric CO2 driving pressure, but thousands of pascals of water-vapor driving pressure. A separate carbon-pump membrane can see nearly one bar of CO2 backpressure. These different functions cannot be assigned the same favorable permeability without a real architecture.
For constant permeances, a perfectly reactive CO2 sink and dilute plug flow, J_CO2=Kc*p_CO2 and J_water=Kw*Delta_pw. Integrating 50% capture gives a log-mean CO2 pressure p_lm=P*x/2/log(2). Thus m_water/m_CO2=(Mw/Mc)*(Kw/Kc)*Delta_pw/p_lm.
This is a favorable, isothermal screen: finite reactive-interface CO2, gas films, support resistance, humidity changes, defects and nonlinear sorption are omitted. It is not an all-architecture water bound.
At1atm and425ppm, p_lm=31.06348Pa. Taking an ILLUSTRATIVE internal water activity0.9, the CO2/water permeance ratio required to allocate only1kg evaporation/kgCO2 is15.41 at20C/40%RH,25.05 at25C/30%RH and39.16 at30C/20%RH. Allocating the ENTIRE2kg budget to evaporation halves these ratios but leaves no startup, preparation or service-water allowance. Water saturation pressure uses a declared Buck-type engineering fit.
A net target flux0.19mol/m2/h would require about5080GPU CO2 permeance in this ideal covering. Neither the water selectivity nor that permeance is supplied by contact angle or liquid-water rejection. Making the same layer thinner changes both gas fluxes together. Lowering internal water activity could change the problem, but its actual carbonated chemistry, conductivity, regeneration and retention must be established together.

3. A conditional conductivity--backdiffusion relation
Let a homogeneous inner separator have thickness L, CO2 permeability Pc, ionic conductivity sigma and CO2 pressure difference Delta_p. Set a=Pc*Delta_p. Let J be NET product molar flux, eta charge efficiency, and z the electron-equivalent count per gross forward CO2 for the ACTUAL mechanism. With F Faraday's constant, steady molecular backflux is a/L and j=(zF/eta)*(J+a/L). Ohmic areal power is j^2 L/sigma.
Consequently membrane ohmic work per NET mole is
 (zF/eta)^2/sigma * [J*L+2*a+a^2/(J*L)]
 >=4*(zF/eta)^2*a/sigma.
The identity is [J^2L+2Ja+a^2/L]-4Ja=(JL-a)^2/L. Equality occurs at L=a/J, at which molecular backflux equals net flux. That equality need not minimize TOTAL electricity, which also pays the extra forward transport, interfaces, voltage losses, supports and chemistry. There is no universal optimum thickness or zero-thickness design.
At fixed L one must evaluate backflux, charge, voltage and cost jointly. A hypothetical z=2/eta=1, J=0.19mol/m2/h, L=50um, Pc=3.43Barrer, Delta_p=95000Pa and sigma=0.25mS/cm gives0.04130 back/net,10.605A/m2,0.02121V membrane loss and0.02690kWh/kg NET ohmic work. At sigma0.01mS/cm, that same conditional term becomes0.67252kWh/kg. THESE ARE NOT SIMULTANEOUSLY MEASURED MATERIAL PROPERTIES or a prediction for a named membrane in humid air.
The only new governing material read used here is Papchenko--Kentish--DeAngelis2025, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12157859/ , Sections2.1--2.2,3.2--3.3, equation5/Table3. After KOH exchange and overnight105C vacuum drying, the approximately50um commercial Fumasep/Sustainion samples were tested at30C; their2bar CO2 permeability estimates are3.43/0.72Barrer, derived from sorption transients. They do NOT measure coupled20--40%RH carbonated through-plane conductivity, water leakage, a complete capture cycle or device durability. Those dry numbers are used only as hypothetical scale inputs above; extrapolating them to a humid working separator is unvalidated. No linked supplementary ZIP or cited original was followed.

6. Decisive observable interface
A later authorized experiment can measure an actual candidate's gas flux, water inventory/evaporation, through-plane resistance under its carbonated working state, terminal energy and startup/final chemical inventories in the SAME cyclic operating condition. Wet versus dry blanks, inactive-electrode/zero-current controls, leaks, CO2 concentration/purity and carbon/water closure distinguish regenerated transport from a finite alkaline or battery inventory. It is not executed here. A component result would still leave packaged area, product collection, lifetime and full-system costs open.
