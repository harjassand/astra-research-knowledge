The proof uses elementary polynomial identities, Sylvester's criterion, rational Sturm certificates, the real-cubic discriminant criterion, compactness, left SO(2) invariance, and the null integral of a periodic scalar Hessian determinant. The certificates are exact rather than numerical samples. The large degree-28 rational Sturm record is included in full; its representation cost is not hidden.

The unsuccessful three-term allocation is not part of the final proof. The final four-power cubic uses the adjacent positive coefficient exactly once. Initial serialization and checker-normalization failures are retained with their successful narrowly corrected replays. They are not mathematical counterexamples and are not represented as successful checks.

This package records a mathematical result for the stated density and class, not an exhaustive literature comparison. Related rank-one-convex/quasiconvex quartic and Burkholder results do not justify a claim here about historical priority or full quasiconvexity. No novelty claim is made. The independent disposition is a scoped reconstruction, not formal proof-assistant verification.

Dates in source filenames are stable research identifiers. Local file timestamps and the later independent-review filename can differ; no theorem depends on those timestamps.
