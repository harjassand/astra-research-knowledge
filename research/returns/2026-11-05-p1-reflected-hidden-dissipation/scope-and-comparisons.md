# Physical reversal, strongest comparisons and remaining endpoints

The [completed theorem](README.md) and [finite-data procedures](inference-and-resources.md) concern a broad but specified stochastic model. The [final report](package/reflection_report.pdf) is authoritative over alternative formulations in the preserved audit notes.

## Hidden physical reversal is substantive

The same four-state construction has `Q^T=R Q R`, where `R` exchanges hidden states 3 and 4 without changing the binary observation. If `R`, rather than identity, is the physical reversal, the model satisfies generalized detailed balance and has **zero physical entropy production**. The visible recording cannot determine which hidden parity is physical.

Therefore the positive rate floor is relative to finite stationary CTMC realizations with all microscopic states even under reversal. It is not a model-free positive physical power certificate for systems with hidden velocities or other odd variables. Ordinary path irreversibility is a mathematical quantity; local detailed balance and a justified isothermal interpretation are needed to identify heat/work dissipation. No energy technology or laboratory dissipation measurement is supplied.

Observations are deterministic, noninvasive instantaneous labels at specified times. Stationarity, access and protocol-specific preparation assumptions remain necessary. Independent calibrated bit flips are a particular supported extension, not a treatment of arbitrary sensor backaction or hidden instrument memory.

## Limits that change the endpoint

A positive certificate lower-bounds dissipation within the declared class. A zero certificate is inconclusive. No finite reflection table is claimed to characterize every equilibrium embedding, and the exact minimum dissipation compatible with the full observed process remains unsolved.

“Three observations” means three snapshots per scored block, repeated many times. It does not mean three total readings, free stationary preparation or free discovery of an informative score. Full word-table acquisition can be exponential. The independent-triple sample lower bound is scoped to its observation model. Fixed-budget statements do not authorize arbitrary optional stopping.

The [auxiliary aliasing note](package/auxiliary/physics_aliasing_note.md) is a separate result retained as source evidence, not a premise of the main reflection theorem or an experiment. The report's figure and fixed-seed outputs illustrate exact-model deductions; numerical discrepancies and synthetic tests do not substitute for the universal proofs.

The source explicitly states that civilisation-changing discovery has **not** been established. No microscopic reconstruction, universal minimal-realization solution, physical sensor, real-device preparation or demonstrated energy advantage is promoted by this publication.
