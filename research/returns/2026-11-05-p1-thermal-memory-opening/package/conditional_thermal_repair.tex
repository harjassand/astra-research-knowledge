\section*{A conditional thermal transport lemma}

Let $\rho_s=e^{-\beta H(s)}/Z_s$ for a supplied differentiable Hamiltonian path.
Quantum belief propagation gives
\[
 \partial_s\rho_s=-\frac\beta2\{\Phi_{H(s)}(V_s),\rho_s\}
 +\beta\tr(V_s\rho_s)\rho_s,\qquad V_s=\partial_sH(s).
\]
The filter has a positive real-time representation
\[
 \Phi_H(V)=\int_{\mathbb R}f_\beta(t)e^{-itH}Ve^{itH}\,dt,
 \quad f_\beta(t)=\frac{2}{\pi\beta}
 \log\coth\frac{\pi|t|}{2\beta}.
\]
Its Fourier multiplier is $\tanh(\beta\omega/2)/(\beta\omega/2)$,
the kernel integrates to one, and its first absolute moment is
$7\beta\zeta(3)/\pi^3$.

Write $V=\sum_jv_jP_j$ in bounded-support Pauli terms and set
$v=\sum_j|v_j|$. Randomly choose $j$ with probability $|v_j|/v$, and a time
$t$ from $f_\beta$. Use $\Pi_j=(I+\operatorname{sgn}(v_j)P_j)/2$.
The two measurement operators
\[
 K_{\rm success}=\sqrt{I-\delta\Pi_j},\qquad
 K_{\rm fail}=\sqrt\delta\,\Pi_j
\]
form a complete instrument. Conjugate the success branch by the sampled
real-time unitary. Because $\rho_s$ commutes with $H(s)$, the failed branch
can be repaired in the original constant-size projector support, before
undoing the real-time conjugation. This avoids asking a recovery map to act
on a spatially expanded measurement operator.

Assume a CPTP outcome-conditioned repair map is supplied for each local
projector, with the unnormalized guarantee
\[
 \|\mathcal R_j(\Pi_j\rho_s\Pi_j)
       -\tr(\Pi_j\rho_s)\rho_s\|_1\leq\eta.
\]
Let $S$ be the averaged success subchannel and $F$ the averaged failed-then-
repaired subchannel. Then $S+F$ is trace preserving, $\tr F(X)\leq\delta\tr X$
for positive $X$, and $F\rho_s=q\rho_s+e$ with $q\leq\delta$,
$\tr e=0$, $\|e\|_1\leq\delta\eta$.

Repeating until success defines the CPTP channel
\[
 T=\sum_{r\geq0}S F^r,
\]
with expected attempts at most $(1-\delta)^{-1}$. Since $T=S+TF$,
\[
 (1-q)T\rho_s=S\rho_s+Te,
 \qquad
 \left\|T\rho_s-\frac{S\rho_s}{1-q}\right\|_1
 \leq\frac{\delta\eta}{1-q}.
\]
The bound follows from trace-norm contraction on the Hermitian error $e$.
With $\delta=2\beta v\,ds$, the normalized averaged success state agrees
with the QBP derivative to first order. If the path has a supplied uniform
second-order bound, the discretization error is $O(\sum\delta_i^2)$ and
the repair contribution is $O(\sum\delta_i\eta_i)$ for $\delta_i$ bounded
away from one. Each actual step is CPTP, so inherited state errors telescope
rather than being amplified by repeated normalization. Expected repair calls
are $O(\beta\int v_s\,ds)$, although success attempts include all discretization
steps. Time truncation, Hamiltonian simulation and finite loop cutoffs have
their own accuracy budgets.

This conditional lemma does not produce the repair maps. Chen--Rouze,
\emph{Quantum Gibbs states are locally Markovian}, arXiv:2504.02208v1, and
Chen, \emph{Note on Strong Quantum Markov Properties}, arXiv:2605.02877v1,
are the relevant primary comparisons. Their available strong-recovery bound
can carry a factor exponential in the recovery-region volume. Replacing it
by a polynomial-cost, outcome-conditioned repair remains the actual missing
operation. Ordinary clustering, correctness of a thermal chain, and small
relative entropy do not establish that stronger operation.
