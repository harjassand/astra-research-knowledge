The useful output is a reusable manufactured component, not merely recovered rare-earth mass, a liberated fragment, powder, a transient flux measurement or an attractive debonding coupon. The central challenge is to separate an existing strong joint while preserving a valuable brittle magnetic body, then restore and qualify all the properties that make it useful. Find a new mechanism or an integrated construction that changes the full useful operation; intact reuse, thermal demagnetization, solvent cleaning, mechanically removable retainers, remagnetization and hydrogen-based powder recycling are existing ideas and do not become inventions by being named together.
