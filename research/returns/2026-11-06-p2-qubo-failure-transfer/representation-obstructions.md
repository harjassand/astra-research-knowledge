One-bit elimination is exact: `min_y[E₀(x)+y(a+Σb_ix_i)]=E₀(x)+min(0,a+Σb_ix_i)`. For `E_k=y(1−Σ_i x_i)`, the eliminated function is `1−Σ_i x_i−∏_i(1−x_i)`. Its unique multilinear expansion contains exactly`2^k−k−1` nonzero monomials and degree`k`, after eliminating a bit from a small ±1-coefficient star. This defeats an explicit monomial list. The factored product stays short and the original star's minimum is`1−k`. The identity is credited to established pseudo-Boolean quadratization/Boros–Gruber machinery, not claimed novel or an obstruction to all elimination orders/compact representations.

A stronger restriction concerns separated minima `H(x_A,x_B)=min_(s≤r)[f_s(x_A)+g_s(x_B)]`, whose least state count is the established min-plus/Barvinok factorisation rank of the explicitly indexed energy matrix. If distinguished pairs satisfy `M_ij+M_ji−M_ii−M_jj≥2`, uniform error`≤ε<1/2` preserves strict diagonal-versus-cross inequalities. One separable state cannot attain two distinguished diagonal entries: additivity would contradict that inequality. Hence`r` is at least the number of distinguished pairs. Factors may be arbitrary real functions; the count lower bound holds before their construction/evaluation costs.

For `E_(n,T)=(Σ_i2^(i−1)x_i−T)²`, `0≤T<2^n`, expanded coefficients have`O(n)` bits and total input length`O(n³)`. Every partition has distinct side subset sums. Pair increasing row sums with decreasing column sums to obtain cross gap at least2. Thus exact or uniformly`<1/2`-accurate separated representation requires `r≥2^min(|A|,|B|)`; exact rank is sharp. Every variable ordering encounters at least`2^floor(n/2)` states at its balanced middle split. The continuous quadratic matrix has rank1 and the split energy matrix ordinary real rank≤3, yet min-plus rank is exponential. **The optimum is simply zero at the binary expansion of`T`.** Representation width is not intrinsic optimisation difficulty.

Bounded coefficients do not save this representation. On an even-side`k×k` grid use `E_G=x_corner+Σ_edges(x_u−x_v)²`, with`N=k²`, `|a_v|≤5`, `|b_uv|≤2`, and sparse binary length`O(N log N)`. Setting other bits to zero leaves Hamming distance plus row/column terms, so the collision bound forces `r≥2^ceil(k/32)` even with uniform error`<1/2`. This is superpolynomial in input size for the specified representation. Its unique minimiser is all zeros, and negative pair coefficients put it in standard submodular minimisation. It is not a hard optimisation family.
