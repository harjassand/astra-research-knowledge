INDEPENDENT TRANSFER OPENING: FIXED-LAW LONG-TIME CERTIFICATES
Status: mathematically sound constructive route; NOT SELECTED as new capability.
No historical novelty or scientific breakthrough is claimed. The strongest
applicable existing auxiliary-function theory supplies the central mechanism.

QUESTION AND DESIRED CONSEQUENCE
Can finite local measurements certify a long-time observable simultaneously
for every statistically compatible fixed physical law, even when trajectories
are chaotic, without sampling the invariant law or assuming a mixing gap?
The desired output is a finite globally checkable inequality for mean power,
heat or transport, not a simulated trajectory. The missing operation was a
finite certificate search that retains unknown parameters as fixed in time,
instead of allowing an adversary to switch laws during a trajectory.

KNOWN BACKBONE AND ACTUAL THEOREM
Let K be a known compact set forward invariant under every smooth polynomial
ODE x'=F(x,theta), theta in a known compact semialgebraic Theta. All input
coefficients are rational. Let f(x,theta) be polynomial. The physical parameter
theta is constant. Define
 beta = sup_(theta,x0) limsup_(T->infinity) T^-1 int_0^T f(x_t,theta) dt.
For every C1 auxiliary function V(x,theta), the pointwise certificate
 f + F dot grad_x V <= b on K x Theta
implies every indicated long-run average is at most b. More strongly,
 T^-1 int_0^T f <= b + osc_K V(.,theta)/T.
This follows by integrating dV/dt=F dot grad_x V. A lower certificate follows
by applying the same construction to -f. Parameter derivatives are absent
because theta'=0. No invariant measure is queried and no mixing rate appears.

Apply Tobasco-Goluskin-Doering's auxiliary-function strong duality to the
augmented flow (x',theta')=(F(x,theta),0). It gives
 beta = inf_V sup_(K x Theta) [f+F dot grad_x V],
with the standard compact-flow and smooth-extension premises. This may also
be understood through invariant measures: theta's marginal is conserved, and
disintegration conditions invariant measures on their fixed theta fibers.
Every invariant measure therefore averages fixed-law invariant measures.
The maximization is over all trajectories/invariant measures, including
unstable exceptional trajectories; it is not the value for a selected
physical or typical invariant measure.

A direct derivation also explains why strict certificates exist. For the
augmented flow Phi_t, write A_T f(y)=T^-1 int_0^T f(Phi_t y)dt. Compactness and
empirical-measure limits imply lim_T sup_y A_T f(y)=beta (continuous-time
subadditivity, followed by an invariant empirical-measure subsequence).
Set V_T(y)=T^-1 int_0^T (T-t) f(Phi_t y)dt. Differentiation and integration by
parts give L V_T=A_T f-f, hence f+LV_T=A_T f. Thus for every b>beta there is
finite T with sup_y(f+LV_T)<b. Smooth extensions near the compact state set
and C1 polynomial approximation then give a polynomial V with strict slack.
This derivation does not provide an effective bound on the required T,
polynomial degree, coefficient magnitude or positivity certificate size.

FINITE SEARCH, WITHOUT AN OPTIMIZATION ORACLE
Enumerate rational polynomials V and rational b. At each finite stage use
real quantifier elimination to decide the exact polynomial assertion
 forall (x,theta) in K x Theta: b-f-F dot grad_x V > 0.
This is a fully specified finite-arithmetic semidecision procedure. Whenever
b>beta is fixed in advance, the preceding strict approximation argument
ensures eventual discovery under those compact smooth-extension premises.
For practical search one can instead run bounded-degree SOS programs and
verify a rational/interval certificate; an Archimedean positivity module
is an additional premise for a complete Putinar hierarchy. Merely trusting
floating-point SDP feasibility is not a certificate. This procedure has no
uniform degree or useful runtime bound supplied here. The elementary count
of degree-D monomials in n+p variables is binom(n+p+D,D), before Gram matrices
and positivity constraints. This is already a substantial cost.

FINITE LOCAL DATA CAN BUILD Theta
Suppose F(x,theta)=F0(x)+B(x)theta and theta lies in a known compact Theta0.
At prepared state x_j, measure an endpoint coordinate after duration h.
Assume known uniform speed bound M and Lipschitz bound L in the infinity norm,
exact initial preparation, and independent zero-mean sigma-subGaussian
endpoint readout. The difference quotient has deterministic bias at most
LMh/2 and noise scale sigma/h. Repeating each of s selected scalar rows m
 times gives, simultaneously with probability at least 1-delta,
 |y_j-a_j dot theta| <= LMh/2 + (sigma/h)sqrt(2 log(2s/delta)/m).
Add the known readout-quantization error divided by h to this width. The
intersection of these rational confidence strips with Theta0 is a finite
semialgebraic confidence region. If target row error is tau, h=tau/(LM)
and m>=8 sigma^2 (LM)^2 tau^-4 log(2s/delta) suffice before quantization.
Direct derivative sensing would instead give tau^-2 scaling, but is a
stronger instrument contract. Preparation error contributes a paid Lr term.
No unknown-invariant-distribution primitive has been assumed.

Conditioned on true theta belonging to the region, every subsequently chosen
and exactly verified certificate is valid. Adaptive certificate search does
not require a union bound over all candidates: the same model-coverage event
supports them all. Adaptive data acquisition requires an appropriate joint
confidence sequence or a preallocated failure budget. If the region leaves
radically different long-time values compatible, no narrow result follows.

EXACT SEPARATION: FROZEN VERSUS SWITCHED UNCERTAINTY
On x^2+y^2=1 let x'=-theta*y, y'=theta*x, with theta in {-1,+1}. Let f=x.
Every fixed-law trajectory has time average zero. The polynomial auxiliary
function V=-theta*y gives f+F dot grad_x V=x-theta^2*x=0 exactly.
A parameter-independent auxiliary V is insufficient: at the point (1,0),
the two vector fields are opposites, so max_theta(f+LV)>=1. A common-function
bound can therefore be no smaller than 1, although the fixed-law value is 0.
The switching interpretation can approach 1 by repeatedly reversing the
rotation near that point. A parameter-dependent auxiliary function correctly
preserves a time-constant epistemic parameter. This is an instructive exact
separation; no claim that parameter-dependent storage is new is justified.

INFORMATION-THEORETIC OBSTRUCTION TO A GENERIC BREAKTHROUGH
On [-1,1], x'=theta(1-x^2), x0=0, f=x. For every theta>0, x_t=tanh(theta*t)
and its infinite-time average is +1; for every theta<0 it is -1; for theta=0
it is 0. Thus an arbitrarily small local-model error can change the limiting
observable by 2. In a derivative-query experiment at chosen x_j with iid
Gaussian noise of variance sigma^2, the mean difference between theta=+a and
-a is 2a(1-x_j^2), at most 2a. The KL divergence of any N-query adaptive
experiment is at most 2N a^2/sigma^2 by the conditional KL chain rule.
Distinguishing the two hypotheses with fixed high confidence therefore
requires N=Omega(sigma^2/a^2). No sample count uniform over arbitrarily small
|theta| can deliver a narrow interval for the infinite-time answer. Known
Lipschitz and compactness bounds do not remove this obstruction. It already
occurs in one dimension without chaos. Robust separation margins or stronger
physical structure are necessary for uniformly efficient useful output.
The all-trajectories objective gives beta=1 even for negative theta because
the unstable equilibrium +1 is a permitted initial condition, underscoring
the distinction between worst-trajectory and specified-initial predictions.
