COMPLETED MATHEMATICAL ENDPOINT
A finite simultaneous relative first-failure CDF for a declared correlated
Gibbs network under reversible dynamics and its exact equilibrium-safe
start. Rare static counting and conditional failure sampling are constructed,
not imported as an unspecified correlated-reliability oracle.

The key hypotheses are positive explicit rational weights, fixed logarithmic
conditional-failure contrast, and efficient sampling under every coordinate
pinning. A finite Dobrushin heat-bath implementation supplies the last
operation. Heavy-prefix partition estimation, weighted cut contraction and
inverse multiplicity supply static rare-event access. The preserved finite
CDF algorithm then applies with explicit replacements and revised safe-start
sampling errors and caps. The record includes the stronger rare-field family
with fixed contrast c=2, fixed mixing margin, and unbounded conditional odds
and declared interaction degree.

SCOPE
This is a finite-bit computation theorem for an explicit model. Numerical
inverse gap/mixing margins and v^O(c) work are paid. It is not a physical
measurement of unknown rare rates or an inference that a natural-system
failure model is complete. Exact equilibrium-conditioned safe initialization
is the target. The prior independent-edge all-working point-query extension
does not become an interacting all-working whole-CDF theorem.

The new Gibbs changing-law corollary uses relative factor/rate bands and a
common support. The earlier product-law unknown-support acquisition theorem
is preserved at its scope and is not silently generalized.
