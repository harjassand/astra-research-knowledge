VERDICT
The central construction passes. One essential clarification is required:
a following buffer is bounded as a PRE-BLOCK JOINT event with failure of
the current block, not conditionally on the complete failed block. This
gives the stated constant without an extra denominator.

The continuous-law structural TV bound is
  eta/(1-eta)
  + [(1+eta)/(1-eta)] [q(H)+q(b)]/q_G
  + (1+eta)q(b).                                        (A)
With the checkpoint constants, (A)<epsilon/60. Finite implementation and
estimation are additional budgets, checked separately below when available.

1. DEFINITIONS AND STATIONARY LOWER BOUND

Let Q=P-I be the finite-state normalized generator, pi invariant, pi>=zeta,
and the additive-symmetrization gap lower bound be g in (0,1].
Let tau_B=inf{t>=0:X_t in B}, p=pi(B)>0,
q(t)=Pr_pi(tau_B<=t), G={H<tau_B<=L}, and q_G=q(L)-q(H).
Initial occupation of B is an event at time zero.

The stationary influx into B equals outflux and is at most p, so
  q(t)<=p(1+t).
For Y=integral_0^L 1_B(X_t)dt, EY=Lp. With f=1_B-p,
  |<f,P_t f>_pi|<=e^{-gt}p(1-p),
hence
  EY^2<=(Lp)^2+2Lp/g,
  q(L)>=Pr(Y>0)>=(EY)^2/EY^2=gLp/(2+gLp).
If Lp<=1 and L>=16(H+1)/g, then
  q_G>=gLp/3-p(H+1)>=13gLp/48>=gLp/4.                  (B)
No reversibility or independent-visit approximation is used.

The checkpoint choices eta=epsilon/128,
b>=g^{-1}log[1/(eta*zeta)], and
L>=1024(H+b+2)/(g epsilon), integer,
imply the needed inequalities for 0<epsilon<=1.
The binary-log definition of b is conservative since log_2 r>=log r
for r>=1. The rare-branch certificate p<=1/(8L) implies Lp<=1.

2. THE RELATIVE OUTCOME-KERNEL LEMMA

For a block-start law rho with (1-eta)pi<=rho<=(1+eta)pi, let nu and nu_rho
be the stationary and rho-started success submeasures on COMPLETE paths:
  nu(A)=Pr_pi(path in A and G),
  nu_rho(A)=Pr_rho(path in A and G).
The whole-path likelihood ratio is rho(X_0)/pi(X_0), so
  (1-eta)nu<=nu_rho<=(1+eta)nu.                         (C)

Write q=nu(Omega)=q_G and q_rho=nu_rho(Omega). Aggregating a failed block
into one atom bottom gives probability measures
  M=(1-q)delta_bottom+nu,
  M_rho=(1-q_rho)delta_bottom+nu_rho.
Their total variation is
  TV(M_rho,M)
   = (1/2)[|q_rho-q|+integral_G |dnu_rho-dnu|]
   <= eta q.                                          (D)

This is the crucial q factor. Comparing complete unconditional blocks
would give only eta; comparing success indicators alone would lose the
precursor-path and terminal-mark guarantee.

3. HISTORY AND STOPPING

Before each observed block, condition on the initial observed history and
previous complete failed block paths, but not the fresh preceding buffer.
The block start is P_b(x,.) from the preceding terminal state x, or a
mixture of such distributions. Pointwise multiplicative mixing gives
(C) after every such history. It also holds conditional on extra past
coupling randomness, since the current buffer uses fresh Markov increments.

Do not condition that buffer on avoiding B. Ignored visits are charged
later. Imposing such survival would invalidate the mixing argument.

Let E_k mean that blocks 0 through k-1 failed, and K be the first success
index (the number of failures). Then
  Pr(G_k|surviving history)>=(1-eta)q_G,
  Pr(E_k)<=[1-(1-eta)q_G]^k,
  E(K+1)<=1/[(1-eta)q_G].                              (E)

Sequential maximal coupling of each aggregated actual outcome with M has
conditional mismatch probability <=eta q_G. The reference marginal is M
conditional on the entire past joint history, hence reference outcomes
are iid. A match on success matches the ENTIRE success block path.
Summing mismatches up to actual success and using (E) gives
  TV((K,successful block),
     Geom_failures(q_G) tensor Law_pi(block|G))
  <= eta/(1-eta).                                      (F)
Independence is asserted only for the reference pair.

4. THE BUFFER PITFALL AND EXACT REPAIR

A complete failed block can end in B. Conditional on that history, a
buffer including its start hits B with probability one. Therefore the
bound (1+eta)q(b) is not valid conditional on an exposed failed block.

Before exposing an active block, let its start law be rho. For
h_b(x)=Pr_x(tau_B<=b), pi h_b=q(b). Then
  Pr_rho(prefix hits B)<=(1+eta)q(H),                   (G)
and the required JOINT estimate is
  Pr_rho(G^c AND following buffer hits B)
   <=Pr_rho(following buffer hits B)
    =rho P_L h_b
   <=(1+eta)pi P_L h_b
    =(1+eta)q(b).                                      (H)

No conditioning on current block failure is performed; a restriction is
dropped before normalization. Positivity of P_L and stationarity justify
the inequalities. Buffers are counted only after failed blocks.

Summing prefix indicators and failed-block/buffer joint indicators over
active cycles, using (E), gives
  Pr(any omitted B hit before selected success)
  <=[(1+eta)/(1-eta)] [q(H)+q(b)]/q_G
  <=8(H+b+2)/(gL)<=epsilon/128.                         (I)
Here eta<=1/3 and (B) were used. Multiple hits or shared endpoints merely
overcount in this union bound and cause no problem.

If no omission occurs, the selected G block's first entry is the actual
global first entry. Its local entry time exceeds H, so its last-H path
lies wholly inside the block. This yields full-record equality pathwise.

5. ARBITRARY INITIALIZATION

For arbitrary mu, simulate [0,b] and return the actual event immediately
if it occurs there. Let
  nu_b(y)=Pr_mu(X_b=y,tau_B>b)
be the UNNORMALIZED survivor measure. Then
  nu_b<=mu P_b<=(1+eta)pi.                              (J)
No division by the survival probability is made.

The first actual event occurring in the initial unobserved extra buffer
has unconditional probability
  nu_b h_b<=(1+eta)q(b)
           <=(b+1)/(4L)<=epsilon/4096.                 (K)
The rare-branch certificate p<=1/(8L) and eta<=1 justify the middle bound.

After any particular initial survivor history, X_b is known; the next
block starts from P_b(X_b,.), multiplicatively mixed regardless of that
history. Sections 2-4 therefore hold uniformly conditional on every
initial survivor history. Their unconditional contribution is multiplied
by the survivor probability, at most one.

The late branch must retain all initial survivors. It does not condition
the additional initial buffer to be event-free; (K) is exactly the price
for ignoring those possible events. If initial survival is zero, the late
branch is never used.

6. CALENDAR INDEXING AND TOTAL ERROR

For K failed blocks and local successful first-entry time s,
  T=2b+K(L+b)+s
is correct. There are two initial b intervals, followed by K complete
failed-block/buffer cycles and then the local successful event.

Combining (F), (I), and (K) yields (A). Numerically,
  (A)<=epsilon/127+epsilon/128+epsilon/4096<epsilon/60.
This leaves ample room for independent finite precision and estimation
budgets. Rounding/encoding the ideal coupled records cannot increase TV.

If an initial event occurs before time H, its target precursor must be
the available path from time zero to entry, unless negative-time history
is an additional supplied input. The late branch always provides a full
H window.

7. CONDITIONS TO PRESERVE IN THE FINITE COMPILER

- Execute one encoded geometric-integer draw; do not simulate K failed
  cycles. The coupling proof's conceptual K cycles are not runtime.
- Integer L,b keep the potentially huge calendar offset in integer
  arithmetic. Local clock precision must not depend on the VALUE of K.
- Analyze clock approximations under the Palm proposal with acceptance
  >=g/8, not by conditioning an ordinary stationary proposal on G and
  dividing by q_G.
- Conditional on proposal count and state sequence, clocks are ordered
  iid uniforms independent of the states. First-entry index is then
  fixed by the state sequence. Compare unordered clocks and their pair
  differences for threshold and quantization errors; do not use a joint
  order-statistic density bound proportional to N!.
- The auxiliary chain continues after ignored B visits. If the physical
  model is actually absorbed at B, an explicit ergodic continuation whose
  pre-hit law is the desired one must be part of the model. A full-support
  invariant pi for a truly absorbing chain is outside the assumptions.
