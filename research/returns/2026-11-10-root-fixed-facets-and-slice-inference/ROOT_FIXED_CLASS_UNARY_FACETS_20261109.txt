Conversely each anchor is in Gamma. Each period b is a recession direction of closedconv(Gamma): for any x in this closed convex hull and t>=0,

The local-witness theorem puts EVERY point of closedconv(Gamma) in conv(Gamma), so P=conv(Gamma)=Q is CLOSED. This last step is material: general semilinear sets need not have closed convex hulls.
