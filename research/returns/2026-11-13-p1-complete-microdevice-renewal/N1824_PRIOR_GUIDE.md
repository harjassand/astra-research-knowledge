# N1824 — Programmable microdevices: real connections and selective local rework

[Canonical card](https://github.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/blob/main/cards/N1824-programmable-microdevices-and-selective-joint-rework.txt) · [Scoped status](https://github.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/blob/main/frontier/cards/N1824-programmable-microdevices-and-selective-joint-rework.json)

ROOT credits actual magnetic/capillary part assembly and maker-configurable circuits, then derives introduced coupled electrical/thermal rework cases. Low electrical resistance can heat neighboring parts during release and later cooldown, but a higher-resistance alternative can pass the introduced temperature limit without a new isolator. A proposed thermal disconnect requires real contacts, retention, access and restoration. No useful heterogeneous 3D device, selective-rework apparatus, novel algorithm, qualified material or whole-operation advantage is established.
