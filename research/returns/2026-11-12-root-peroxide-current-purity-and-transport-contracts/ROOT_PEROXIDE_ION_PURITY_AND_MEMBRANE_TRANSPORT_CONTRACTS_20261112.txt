2. A passive separator can trade peroxide loss for voltage without an independent thickness escape
Consider only a homogeneous layer of thicknessl, fixed effective ionic conductivitykappa(S/m), and neutral-peroxide permeabilityP=D_eff*K(m2/s), whereK is the specified partition factor. Let upstream neutral peroxide concentration minus downstream concentration beDeltaC(mol/m3). Assume steady Fick transport, uniform Ohmic current densityj, no convection/electro-osmosis/electromigration of peroxide, fixed properties and a downstream sink that actually destroys all crossing product. For a cathode gross sourcej*f/(2F), define r=lost/gross product. Then
 J=P*DeltaC/l; r=2F*P*DeltaC/(l*j*f);
 DeltaV_separator=j*l/kappa;
 r*DeltaV_separator=K0=2F*P*DeltaC/(kappa*f).
At fixedP/kappa andDeltaC, changing thickness or current density alone cannot reduce both loss fraction and this voltage. Increasingj permits a thinner layer for the same r but leavesDeltaV unchanged. This is a coupled operating contract of this restricted family, not a universal separator bound. Lower neutral fraction, different partitioning, a non-destructive receiving stream, matched product concentrations, selective collection, multiple paths, active transport or actual concentration gradients can change the premises. Pinholes and material attack can invalidate the homogeneous layer favorably or adversely; they are not supplied improvements.
For the examples only, all3wt%peroxide is treated as neutral, solution density is1kg/L, the sink concentration is zero, soDeltaC=881.9716mol/m3. Real speciation, membrane partitioning and the concentration at the active interface must replace this assumption. Bulk product concentration is not automatically the membrane-side concentration.
WithP=1e-11m2/s,kappa=1S/m,f=.90,j=2000A/m2 and illustrative r=.02, l=47.2763micrometres andDeltaV=.0945526V. AtP=1e-10 the corresponding thickness is472.763micrometres andDeltaV=.945526V. These are NOT property claims for any polymer or ceramic. Forj=1000/2000/5000 in the first case, thickness is94.5526/47.2763/18.9105micrometres, butDeltaV andK0 are unchanged. Area, inactive frame fractions, cooling and replacement still change withj and cannot be declared free.

4. Internal crossover cancels in whole inventory until a real reaction or rejection loses product
For two product compartments with volumesVa,Vc, gross sourcesSa,Sc, withdrawalqa,qc, first-order destructionka,kc and internal neutral transferg*(Cc-Ca),
 d(Va*Ca)/dt=Sa-qa*Ca-ka*Va*Ca+g*(Cc-Ca);
 d(Vc*Cc)/dt=Sc-qc*Cc-kc*Vc*Cc-g*(Cc-Ca).
Adding them cancels the transfer exactly. New collected product plus destruction plus accumulated stock equals independently generated product. A transported molecule cannot become a second anodic product, but movement alone is also not automatically loss. Never subtract a modeled destruction term again from a Faradaic yield already measured as NET recovered product at the same boundary.
The executed toy takesI=2000A,fa=.8,fc=.9,Va=Vc=1L and choosesqa=Sa/Ctarget,qc=Sc/Ctarget forCtarget=881.9716mol/m3. This illustrates a representation freedom: paired collection can in principle remove a destructive concentration difference rather than merely suppress all permeability. It supplies NO compatible paired catalysts, common pH, pure-product membrane, startup inventory, stability or cheap flow equipment.

The anode concentration becomes a sink, so stronger transfer exposes cathode product to destruction. Simply insisting on equal3%bulk reservoirs does not neutralize an actual reactive surface. In this introduced high-ka case even that necessary condition fails. No physical material is asserted to have this decay constant.
For an electrochemical destruction mechanism, its partial current, electrode potential and gas/ion products must enter the same current and voltage balance as the production reactions. Independently chosen gross source yields, destruction rates andV0 are not automatically jointly attainable. The sink formula can describe chemical loss or a separately closed electrochemical sink; it does not grant an extra unmetered redox path. Equal bulk concentrations also need not give equal neutral chemical activities across different pH, hydration and partition states. Those distinctions can defeat the favorable equal-concentration illustration.
These are steady balances, not a complete100-hour campaign. Their nonzero final inventories must be paid on startup and accounted on collection/shutdown. A useful first comparison would measure ionic resistance, neutral-peroxide transfer AND leaching on the same actually prepared coupon under the intended product concentration, hydration, temperature, current/polarity and pressure history. Measure both receiving streams; compare a non-destructive receiver with a separately characterized reactive receiver. Measure the actual layer thickness and area, include contact/solution resistance separately, and test repeated wet/dry/current cycles appropriate to the chosen service operation.

Equal-concentration two-stream tests can discriminate diffusion from convective drag and show whether a proposed common collector actually protects peroxide near active surfaces.
