2. Explicit completion.

For k>1 put c_*=(k+1)/[2(k-1)] and K_k(F)=J_k(F)+c_*(det F)^2. Direct expansion gives

 K_k(F)=ell/(8n) {
   [ku+v/k+2j]^2
   +(1-1/k^2)[v-2k j/(k-1)]^2 }.                     (1)

The right side is a convex QUADRATIC in the three features (u,v,j). It is not asserted convex as a function of the four matrix entries.

At any actual matrix A, with u0,v0,d its three features, the derivatives in u and v are
 alpha_A=ell/(4n)[k^2 u0+v0+2kd]>=0,
 beta_A=ell/(4n)[u0+v0-2d]>=0.                        (2)
For the first sign use k^2u0+v0>=2k sqrt(u0v0)>=2k|d|; for the second use u0+v0>=2|d|. The j derivative is
 gamma_A=ell(ku0-v0+2d)/(2n)+2(1+c_*)d.

Feature convexity therefore supplies a global polyconvex support
 p_A(F,s)=K_k(A)+alpha_A(|F_col1|^2-u0)
                   +beta_A(|F_col2|^2-v0)+gamma_A(s-d).
Each p_A is convex in the independent variables (F,s). Also p_A(F,det F)<=K_k(F), with equality when A=F. Consequently
 Pi_k(F,s)=sup_(A in R^(2x2)) p_A(F,s)
is a polyconvex extension: Pi_k(F,det F)=K_k(F).

The supremum is finite, not an uncharged computational oracle. Euler homogeneity in the quadratic features gives the constant term -K_k(A). The inherited coercivity K_k(A)>=J_k(A)>=mu_k|A|^4, and the quadratic growth of alpha,beta,gamma, bound every support above by
 C_k|A|^2(|F|^2+|s|)-mu_k|A|^4.
Thus Pi_k is a finite convex function. Formula(1) and the supports prove existence without solving a numerical maximization. No efficient evaluation claim is needed or made.

3. Exact compatible-gap identity.

Fix ANY real A and periodic phi in W1,4(T2;R2), G=Dphi,F=A+G. Write
 du=u-u0, dv=v-v0, dj=j-d,
 S1=integral|G_col1|^2, S2=integral|G_col2|^2,
 Vj=integral(j-d)^2.
Compatibility gives integral dj=0 and integral du=S1, integral dv=S2. Quadratic feature polarization of(1) yields EXACTLY

 integral J_k(F)-J_k(A)
 =alpha_A S1+beta_A S2-c_* Vj
  +ell/(8n) integral {
       [k du+dv/k+2dj]^2
       +(1-1/k^2)[dv-2k dj/(k-1)]^2 }.              (3)

There is no small-gradient or determinant-sign assumption. In particular
 integral J_k(F)-J_k(A)>=alpha_A S1+beta_A S2-c_*Vj. (4)
The original inequality is thus paid whenever its ACTUAL total-Jacobian variance does not exceed the complete positive quantity in(3) divided by c_*. No such universal compatible-variance estimate is supplied here. When Vj=0, (3) recovers the inherited constant-determinant positive slice k>1. At k=1, that slice instead follows directly from the positive semidefinite (u,v) feature Hessian; taking c_* to infinity is not a proof or a useful uniform bound.

The positive squares and column reserves in(3) belong to this SAME identity. They cannot be spent again as an independent extra convex reserve from the older Psi decomposition. The negative term is the variance of the TOTAL determinant, including cof(A):G, not merely integral(det G)^2.

4. Exact optimality of this FEATURE completion, not a global obstruction.

For J_k+c j^2, the feature matrix after multiplication by8n/ell is
 [[k^2,1,2k],
  [1,1,-2],
  [2k,-2,4+8n(1+c)/ell]].
For k>1 its leading two-by-two block is positive definite. Its Schur complement is
 (8n/ell)(c-c_*).
Hence c_* is the LEAST c making this quadratic feature extension convex. This does not prove that a different polyconvex extension requires c>=c_*, or that the uncorrected J_k is nonquasiconvex.

For1/3<k<1 the leading block is indefinite, so no finite determinant-square correction can convexify these same three independent features. At k=1 its kernel(1,-1) is not orthogonal to the j coupling(2,-2), again ruling out every finite c for this feature-convex construction. These are method limits, not compatible negative fields. The apparent large-k tail is not a pointwise feature-convex theorem about J_k: the uncorrected feature determinant stays negative, whereas the genuine accepted global tail uses compatibility.

For rational k>1 all coefficients and Schur/sign identities require a fixed number of rational operations with polynomial bit work in its encoding.
