3. Report \(D=1\) precisely when

\[
\ell_D(U)=\frac{x+s(1-x)}{N},
\qquad
p_D=\frac{s+1}{2N}.

\begin{aligned}

&=
\frac{\int_0^1(1-x)[x+s(1-x)]\,dx}

\boxed{\frac{2s+1}{3(s+1)}}.
\end{aligned}

\[
s=0 \Longrightarrow r_s=\frac13,
\qquad
s\ge1 \Longrightarrow r_s\ge\frac12.
