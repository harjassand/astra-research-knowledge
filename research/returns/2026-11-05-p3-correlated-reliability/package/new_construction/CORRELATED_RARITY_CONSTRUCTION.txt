MODEL
G=(V,E) connected undirected graph, v>=2 vertices, m binary edges.
x_e=1 means FAILED. Full-support Gibbs law pi(x) proportional to a product
of positive rational bounded-arity local factors. The dependence graph on
edge variables is distinct from G. Explicit local factors, bounded incidence
and neighborhood sizes permit all local conditional probabilities to be
evaluated and their extrema to be enumerated in polynomial time.

Let O_e(z)=pi(x_e=1|x_-e=z)/pi(x_e=0|x_-e=z).
Supply fixed K>=1 with max_z O_e(z)/min_z O_e(z)<=K for every e.
Fields can be arbitrarily rare in binary encoding; K bounds interaction
distortion, NOT the inverse rare field. Let q_e^+,q_e^- be conditional extrema.
Then q_e^+/q_e^-<=K and q_e^->=(q_e^+)^K:
write q^+=1/(1+t), q^->=1/(1+Kt)>=(1+t)^(-K).

Supply a uniformly efficient sampler for pi under arbitrary coordinate
pinnings. An explicit sufficient construction is Dobrushin uniqueness with
row influence sum chi<1: random-scan heat bath reaches TV tau in
O(m/(1-chi)*log(m/tau)) updates, uniformly over every pinning. Actual bit
evaluation and Bernoulli generation need finite caps/accuracy allocation.

RATIONAL CUT WEIGHTS
Construct positive rational w_e with
  w_e <= -ln q_e^+ <= 2w_e.
This has polynomial bit cost; it is not an additive approximation that
loses tiny weights when q_e^+ is close to one.
Put C=ceil(2K), lambda=min_nontrivial_cut sum_e_in_cut w_e.
For any edge set D, sequential conditioning gives
  exp(-C w(D)) <= pi(all D failed) <= exp(-w(D)).
No conditional independence is used. Thus p=pi(disconnected)>=exp(-C lambda).

KEY LOCAL RARE-PROBABILITY OPERATION
For a pinning A and an unpinned e,
  pi(x_e=1|A)=E_pi(.|A)[pi(x_e=1|x_-e)].
The normalized integrand f_e=r_e(x_-e)/q_e^+ lies in [1/K,1], regardless of
how small q_e^+ is. Estimate this bounded expectation, multiply by the exact
known q_e^+, then telescope over the edges of D. With per-factor relative
error O(epsilon/m), the total product has relative error O(epsilon), using
polynomially many pinned draws. TV approximation is divided by the lower
mean 1/K, NOT by the rare marginal. This is the essential Rao--Blackwell
step; raw Bernoulli occupancy would reintroduce inverse rare local fields.

TWO STATIC REGIMES
Let L_v=ceil(log2(2v)).
If lambda<4L_v, the explicit rational bound
  p >= p0=2^(-8 C L_v)
permits direct pi sampling/rejection with polynomial cost v^O(C), including
finite conditional-output error. Small p can still be polynomially small;
no input-value reciprocal-rare-field factor is silently hidden here.

Otherwise lambda>=4L_v. Weighted cut counting gives at most v^(2a) cuts of
weight <=a lambda. For integer A, cuts heavier than A lambda have union mass
at most
  2 exp(2 ln(2v) -(lambda-2 ln(2v))*A).
Relative to p>=exp(-C lambda), choose
  A=ceil(2C+2+2*ceil(log2(32/epsilon))/lambda).
The tail is <=epsilon*p/16 with surplus slack. The number of relevant cuts
is at most v^(2A)<=64 v^(4C+6)/epsilon.

Finite weighted random contraction enumerates every <=A lambda cut with
joint confidence: contract to ceil(4A) vertices, then enumerate remaining
partitions. Each designated cut survives with probability at least
(ceil(4A)/v)^(O(A)); use the standard explicit contraction product to price
repetitions. If ceil(4A)>=v, direct cut enumeration is still polynomial in
1/epsilon in this parameter regime. No cut-list oracle is supplied for free.
Weights are rational; all edge choices can be implemented with finite
integer randomness and explicit capped rejection. Deduplicate cuts.

UNION ESTIMATION AND CONDITIONAL FAILURE SAMPLING
For each retained cut D_j estimate p_j=pi(D_j failed) by the local operation.
With exact p_j, select j proportional p_j, sample pi(.|D_j failed), and
compute c(x)=number of retained cuts failed. The proposal has density
pi(x)c(x)/Z, Z=sum_j p_j. Thus E[1/c]=p_union/Z>=1/Jcuts, yielding relative
estimation by bounded means and exact conditional-union sampling by accepting
with probability 1/c. Approximate weights give multiplicative bias; pinned
sampling TV error is amplified by at most O(Jcuts), not by 1/p. Cap all
rejections and budget errors. The omitted-cut probability controls both
relative probability and TV of the full conditional failure law.
