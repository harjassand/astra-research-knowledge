The construction is now complete at the theorem level for a succinct lazy reversible chain P, known invariant pi, failure set B of mass 0<p<=1/2, certified gap g>0, and deliberately prepared initial law pi(.|A). It needs an efficient static estimator of p and sampler of pi(.|B), plus ordinary P updates. It produces one finite confidence certificate covering every integer deadline n>=1, with work polynomial in inverse gap, inverse relative accuracy, input length and log inverse confidence, and no inverse p or rare mean factor. Queries at binary encoded n cost polynomial in log n. The complete proof and diagnostic are being written next.

The short-time operation is an explicit boundary-Palm path sampler. Draw y~pi(.|B), take one P step to x and condition x in A (acceptance >= g/2), reverse that crossing, choose its position uniformly in the n-step window, and simulate backward/forward ordinary paths. Weight by 1{X0 in A}/number of entrances into B. Its expectation is pi(A) F(n)/(n j), where j is stationary crossing flux. Through a polynomial horizon H its mean is at least g/[2(1+gH)].

The long-time operation estimates the smallest killed eigenvalue lambda using a bounded reverse no-return statistic of length ell=O(g^-1 log(1/(g epsilon))). If p is below an explicitly estimated gate, this statistic is within relative O(epsilon) of lambda and has success probability >=g/4. After H=poly(g^-1,epsilon^-1), the exact CDF differs from 1-(1-lambda)^n by relative at most epsilon. If p is above the gate, the CDF is already in [1-epsilon,1]. A geometric grid plus concavity gives a finite uniform certificate rather than separate per-deadline promises.

The main new stability lemma is stronger than the proposed interpolation bound. If killed positive contractions K0,K1 obey exp(-eta) K0 <= K1 <= exp(eta) K0 as forms, then exp(-eta) F0(n) <= F1(n) <= exp(eta) F0(n) simultaneously for every n, despite noncommutation. Use the path Ks=K0^(1/2)(K0^(-1/2)K1K0^(-1/2))^s K0^(1/2). It remains a positive contraction by scalar weighted arithmetic-geometric inequality, and |dKs|<=eta Ks. Duhamel and the energy identity give |dF|<=eta F. Continuous-time heat content has the same theorem. Accepted local unknown-support P2 form control instantiates this directly.

There is also a separate finite-factory theorem, so exact physical reversibility is not silently required. Initial total-variation error delta_init contributes at most delta_init/(g p) relative error for every n>=1. Thus choose both errors O(epsilon g p_L).
