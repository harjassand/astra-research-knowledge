WHAT WAS EXECUTED

construction/check_output_calibration.py
   Ideal finite reset/export simulation on a two-qubit noncommuting model;
   Pauli-transpose and finite-window checks; synthetic accepted Bernoulli
   data; a reference-entangled retrospective decoder on one selected record.
   The counts are generated by binomial sampling from calculated probabilities.
   Rejected physical attempts, noisy fields and protected factories were not run.

construction/check_finite_derivative.py
   A triangular moment ODE computes small-model signed-output probabilities.
   A 48th-order Taylor evaluation and 70-digit finite Chebyshev interpolation
   test the mixed-derivative identity. The analytic Taylor truncation allowance
   is recorded separately from un-certified floating-point roundoff.
   Grid means are calculated, not physically acquired.

construction/evaluate_sharper_schedule.py
   High-precision arithmetic for the available factorial-tail / actual-weight /
   randomized-node schedule, with a conservative factor-two norm inflation.
   This computes an upper resource bound; it does not execute its trials.

construction/plot_finite_derivative.py
   Reproduces the scientific convergence plot from the saved diagnostic.

The corresponding JSON files contain complete settings and results.
At the explicit n=2, M=15, epsilon=0.1 point, a loose sufficient raw-trial
schedule is about 10^62.54; the sharper evaluated schedule is about 10^39.12.
Both precede continuous receiver compilation. They are upper bounds, not lower
bounds. They emphatically do not demonstrate a practical implementation.
