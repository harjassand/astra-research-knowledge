WORKING CONSTRUCTION -- chronological derivation notes, 2026-11-05
The final report and independent construction note govern over these exploratory
notes. Independence of later calibration and archived payload, required in the
initial draft below, is unnecessary; the final branchwise lemma supersedes it.

Question: can a single arbitrary unencoded many-body payload be captured before
the apparatus Hamiltonian is known, and decoded after learning that Hamiltonian
from only its continuing outgoing fields? No later copies of the original input,
no direct source preparation, control, or measurement.

Accepted baseline: P1 at revision 324acb589443002bf8d529cc15e160129c5e36d2.
Known local H, calibrated pure vacuum Weyl ports with complete lossless coherent
access, r > beta(H): explicit reverse-reset output decoder, diamond error
2 sqrt(n exp(-(r-beta)T)), polynomial finite local collector and inverse circuit.
Do not claim bounded hardware. P1 is developing that separately.

Inherited local environment isometry:
V|psi> = sqrt(1-p)|psi>_S|empty>
       + sqrt(p)|Phi_q>_{S,A}|psi>_B,  p=1-exp(-r Delta).
Orthogonal flags, state-independent reset probabilities. A is the accessible
purifier of the newly replaced source; B exports the preceding source state
including arbitrary references. Keeping A, rather than discarding it as the
generic payload decoder does, is central to the prospective new operation.
P1 already uses A for basis-state preparation in its diagonal-H companion.

Premises:
n qubits, fixed locality k, static H=sum_{a=1}^M theta_a P_a, known complete
candidate Pauli list, no identity. M can be sum_{j<=k} 3^j binom(n,j), or smaller
known geometry list. Known B_H >= sum |theta_a| and beta_0>=beta(H), chi_0.
Known calibrated port operators sqrt(r) W_mu/q and labels/phases. Rate
r>beta_0 with known kappa_0=r-beta_0>0. All other source interactions absent or
bounded as error. H/ports stationary through early collection and calibration.
Protected receiver memory, growing finite receiver control and calibrated

1. EARLY CAPTURE
Choose T0=kappa_0^-1 log(C n/epsilon^2). Use P1 noise-only bin collectors,
which depend on r and the port calibration but not H. Store reset flags and B
exports from [0,T0]; discard A in this early record, as in P1. The analysis
splitting uses H-dependent kicks, but these are not physical source controls.
Store the early quantum record throughout the subsequent calibration.

2. COMPLETE SOURCE BURN-IN
L_H(rho)=-i[H,rho]+r sum_i (E_i(rho)-rho), E_i replaces site by I/q.
For any joint rho_SR, including early output or prior trials in R, define
D(rho_SR || (I_S/d) tensor rho_R)=n log q - S(S|R).
Source Hamiltonian contributes zero. For each E_i its entropy production is
D(rho||E_i rho)+D(E_i rho||rho) >= log q-S(S_i|S_except_i R).
Conditional Shearer/SSA yields sum_i S(S_i|S_except_i R)<=S(S|R).
Thus D'<=-rD and D(0)<=2n log q. Quantum Pinsker gives
|| (exp(t L_H) tensor id_R)(rho)-I/d tensor rho_R ||_1
 <= 2 sqrt(n log q) exp(-rt/2).
Discard output during burn-in. This is averaged source replacement, NOT
historywise decoupling or a new weak-r efficiently decoded output theorem.
Use burn-in before every capped trial to make ideal trials independent of all
retained input/reference and history, with total error budget telescoped.

3. LOCAL CHOI ORACLE
After burn-in source I/d. For selected X, |X|=b<=k:
- front window w: keep A from last reset of each site in X; accept all reset;
- let source evolve actual exp(tau L_H), discarding intermediate outputs;
- back window w: keep B from first reset of each selected output site.
Omitting H in front/back windows produces exact normalized Choi state of
T_X^tau(rho_X)=Tr_except_X exp(tau L_H)(rho_X tensor I_except_X/d_except_X).
With every ideal reset record, front/back timing perturbation <=4 B_H w.
Reason: flag probabilities independent of input; conditional resets are CPTP;
telescoping bounded Hamiltonian kicks gives 2 B_H per total window time.
For back exports of all b sites acceptance is (1-exp(-rw))^(2b).
This uses random windows paid explicitly, not controlled source resets.

Optional remote channel query: Bell-project arbitrary receiver input with A,
accept identity Bell outcome. Success q^(-2b) independent of the input/reference.
Output then is T_X^tau on that input/reference. Failures consume unknown input;
cannot repeat-until-success on that same state. Deferred Bell measurement is
ordinary teleportation, not backwards signalling. Total probability
q^(-2b)(1-exp(-rw))^(2b). For normalized postselected accuracy, unconditional
instrument/interface error must be <= success_probability * target_error.

4. COEFFICIENT LEARNING USING ONLY ONE BACK EXPORT
For P in candidate list choose i in support(P) and single-site Q_i anti-
commuting with P_i. R_P=i P Q_i is a signed Hermitian Pauli.
Front retain A on support(P), back retain only B_i. Clock success
p_b=(1-exp(-rw))^(b+1), >=(rw/2)^(k+1) for rw<=1.
Measure R_P^T on A and Q_i on B_i, get Y in {-1,+1}.
In zero-window ideal:
c_P(tau)=2^-n Tr[R_P exp(tau L_H^dagger)(Q_i)],
c_P(0)=0, c_P'(0)=2 theta_P.
Pauli orthogonality isolates coefficient; known -r Q_i is orthogonal to R_P.
Let Lambda=2 B_H+2 n r (conservative global superoperator norm).
Unital CP contraction and Taylor integral remainder give
|c_P(tau)-2 theta_P tau|<=Lambda^2 tau^2/2.
Estimate theta by sample mean/(2 tau).

Conservative candidate parameter choices, constants to finalize:
tau=eta/(4 Lambda^2), w=eta tau/(32 Lambda)=eta^2/(128 Lambda^3), eta<=Lambda.
Taylor coefficient bias <=eta/16. Window coefficient bias <=2 B_H w/tau.
Required accepted samples per coefficient N0=O((eta tau)^-2 log(M/delta))
 =O(Lambda^4 eta^-4 log(M/delta)).
Use a fixed cap L=ceil((2N0+8log(M/delta))/p_*) attempts per coefficient,
p_*=(rw/2)^(k+1), then first N0 successes or explicit failure.
All attempts paid; no adaptive confidence stopping.
N_attempts=O(M Lambda^(3k+7) r^(-(k+1)) eta^(-(2k+6)) log(M/delta)).
This deliberately loose polynomial is not a practical parameter claim.

STRONGER FINITE-ACCURACY ESTIMATOR (2026-11-05, in construction)

The preceding short-time estimator is deliberately elementary but very costly.
Replace it by mixed-derivative acquisition from UNCONDITIONAL records:
f_P(w,tau)=E[Y * 1{all b front sites reset and back i resets}].
All rejected attempts output 0; no normalized rare conditioning in the theorem.
Front width w, middle tau, back width w. Set s=b+1. Then
  (partial_w^s partial_tau f_P)(0,0)=2 s! r^s theta_P.
Each of the b+1 required resets supplies one power of w. On the first nonzero
w order, front/back Hamiltonian insertions are absent, so the coefficient is
r^s * 2^-n Tr[R_P exp(tau L_H^dagger)(Q_i)]. Any H acting in front/back costs
an additional w. No derivative at an unrealistically exact short time is assumed:
use finite tensor-product Chebyshev interpolation to acquire this derivative.

Actual receiver can measure each newly emitted A immediately in its fixed
R_i^T basis and retain the LAST classical outcome in the front window. Measure
each back B_i in Q_i and retain the FIRST. Deferred measurements prove agreement
with the Choi picture. Long-lived quantum calibration storage is unnecessary.
The early original-payload buffer still must stay quantum throughout.

Finite Markov description for analysis only: retain at most b A qubits, overwrite
A_i at each front reset and set a classical seen bit; back freezes first B_i.
Each elementary reset is CPTP, Hamiltonian same source H. Generators have
diamond norm at most Lambda=2B_H+2nr. Hence the scalar unnormalized f is entire,
f(w,t)=sum c_ab w^a t^b, with |c_ab| <=(2Lambda)^a Lambda^b/(a!b!).
Dimensions do not enter this norm estimate. The large matrices are NOT computed
by the acquisition algorithm; the apparatus produces observations.

Choose h=1/(8 Lambda); tensor Chebyshev--Lobatto nodes on [0,h]^2 with degree
D>=max(s,2). The rectangular Taylor polynomial through degree D in each variable
has the EXACT desired derivative; nodal remainder <=4*4^-D (loose safe bound).
For DCT-I interpolation, sup norm amplification <=2(D+1) per variable.
Repeated Markov polynomial inequality yields the conservative data-to-derivative
norm W_Ds=4(D+1)^2 (2D^2/h)^(s+1).
Thus coefficient bias <=2 W_Ds 4^-D/(s!r^s).
Choose D as first integer satisfying 8 W_Ds 4^-D <= eta s!r^s.
For each nodal mean use accuracy zeta=eta s!r^s/(2 W_Ds), giving coefficient
statistical error <=eta/4. Hoeffding sample count per nontrivial node
N_node=ceil(2 zeta^-2 log(2 M (D+1)^2/delta)); w=0 nodes known exactly zero.
All trials (zeros included) are counted; no hidden factor 1/p is omitted.
For fixed k, degree is logarithmic up to loglog and total attempts
O_k(M * Lambda^2/eta^2 * (Lambda/r)^(2k+2)
    * polylog(Lambda^(k+2)/(eta r^(k+1)), M/delta)).
An explicit loose log-degree exponent is 4k+14 before the confidence logarithm.
This improves the eta exponent from 2k+6 to 2, up to logarithms, at the price of
high polynomial norm/locality factors. It is standard derivative estimation
machinery applied to a newly acquired physical observable, not a claim to invent
Hamiltonian derivative learning.

Variance improvement: for each raw X in {-1,0,+1}, E[X^2]=p(w)=(1-exp(-rw))^s
regardless of H. Let pmax=(r h)^s. Bernstein allows
N_node=ceil(8*(pmax/zeta^2+1/zeta)*log(2M(D+1)^2/delta)).
The leading full-trial bound improves to
O_k(M * (Lambda/eta)^2 * (Lambda/r)^(k+1) * D^(4k+14)
    * log(M(D+1)^2/delta)), for eta<=Lambda and fixed k.
The 1/p clock cost is thus explicit rather than squared by a Hoeffding bound.
The derivative estimator uses no normalized postselected accuracy assumption.

Finite timing and arithmetic: |partial_w f|<=2Lambda and |partial_tau f|<=Lambda
on nonnegative times. Approximate both nodal durations within zeta/(12Lambda)
to limit mean timing error to zeta/4. DCT-I weights are computable with certified
rounding; require the total l1 error in final coefficient weights <=eta/8.
All values lie in [-1,1], so this is an explicit sufficient output bound.
Finite clock/timing precision and arithmetic are paid and remain polynomial.

Useful exact small-system diagnostic representation (not needed by algorithm):
Let F_J be the source density matrix weighted by the product of the latest front
A measurement outcomes on J, with missing outcomes valued zero. Start F_empty=I/d,
F_J=0 otherwise. For i in J, reset sends F_J <- R_i/2 tensor Tr_i F_(J\i);
for i notin J it sends F_J <- I_i/2 tensor Tr_i F_J. Other sites reset ordinarily.
This is a triangular 2^b-block linear ODE with common -i[H,.] terms.
After middle exp(tau L), the back scalar is
r * integral_0^w Tr[Q_i exp(t (L-r E_i)) F_all] dt,
because no reset on i has occurred before its first export. This gives an exact
finite-dimensional calculation of the emitted signed correlation for diagnostics.

Two resource boundaries:
(a) At source I/d, discarding all A and retaining only B+reset flags gives a
globally maximally mixed B stream independent of H: unitary histories preserve
the joint maximally mixed state. No amount of that particular data can learn H.
(b) If all initial payload information crosses an entanglement-breaking classical
record cut and later calibration is independent, the recovered channel remains
entanglement breaking. Distance from identity is at least 2(1-1/d), by testing
its normalized Choi state against Phi_d. Quantum early storage is indispensable.

INDEPENDENT CONSTRUCTIVE UPGRADE (continuous_quantum collaborator)

Late calibration need NOT be independent of the archived quantum payload.
Let sigma_z be its subnormalized bank/reference state given late record z.
If every good decoder satisfies ||D_hat_z-D_H||_diamond<=e, then
||sum_z (D_hat_z tensor id)(sigma_z) -(D_H tensor id)(sum_z sigma_z)||_1
 <= e + 2 Pr(bad).
Later source/output operations leave the unconditioned archived marginal fixed.
A uniform calibration failure bound suffices even with reference correlations.
The source mixing lemma is useful for an I/d exterior in the support-only
k-local theorem, not a prerequisite for the adaptive-decoder composition.

For bounded candidate interaction neighborhoods C_i, front-reset all sites of
C_i (observable identity on C_i outside P). With s=|C_i|+1 fixed, the derivative
identity holds for EVERY correlated source start. Every term in [H,Q_i] lies in
C_i, whose first nonzero reset term exactly replaces it. No burn-in is required.
Freshly randomize the interpolation node each trial with probability |a_j|/A,
where a_j is its final coefficient weight and A=sum |a_j|. Output
Z=A sign(a_j)Y. Conditional on all past records, its mean is the interpolation
functional of ONE current-state function, and differs from theta_P by the
uniform bias bound. Fixed nodes on changing source states would be invalid.
Bounds |Z-E(Z|past)|<=2A and E(Z^2|past)<=A^2(rh)^s give a Freedman/Bernstein
schedule O((A^2(rh)^s/eta^2+A/eta)log(M/delta)) raw trials. The general k-local
result and this stronger fixed-neighborhood premise must remain separate.
