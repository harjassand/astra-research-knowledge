## Exact model and the useful contrast condition

A known connected undirected graph has v vertices and m visible binary edge variables, with additional explicitly declared latent bits allowed. The full-support invariant law is `pi(x)=W(x)/Z`, `W=product_f psi_f(x_Sf)`, with positive explicit **rational** factors. Input length includes every table and bit length; exponentially large implicit tables are not free.

For each visible edge supply certified conditional-failure bounds `0<q_e^-<=pi(x_e=1|x_-e)<=q_e^+<1` and `q_e^->=(q_e^+)^c`, with fixed `c>=1`. This bounds **logarithmic surprisal contrast**, not ordinary odds/probability ratio. Ordinary contrast can be exponentially large. Deriving exact conditional extrema for an unrestricted succinct graph is not assumed cheap.

An explicitly costed sampler must work uniformly under **every coordinate pinning**. The supplied sufficient implementation has certified Dobrushin row sum at most `1-alpha`, `alpha>0`. Fast unconditioned sampling alone does not provide this interface. Numerical inverse alpha is paid. A finite systematic dyadic heat bath starts from any pin-compatible assignment, with explicit mixing and finite random-bit approximation; rare pinnings are imposed computationally, not waited for physically.

The actual operating dynamics must be rational, succinct and reversible for the same pi, with ordinary updates, exit-rate bound and certified gap gamma. A conditional Gibbs heat-bath backbone plus other pi-reversible moves gives `gamma>=alpha r_min`; spreading a fixed total rate over N coordinates pays `r_min=r/N`. An unconditional independent bit-refresh backbone would generally preserve the wrong invariant law and is not silently substituted.

## Constructing rare static access

Heavy-prefix sampling-to-counting estimates each pinned partition Z_D without observing its rare event: successively estimate an unpinned marginal, select its empirically heavier branch, and evaluate the final exact weight divided by the product of those branch probabilities. The sampled branch masses are bounded away from zero, and adaptive-prefix confidence, approximate sampler bias, rational products and bit work are allocated. This is explicit use of established Jerrum–Valiant–Vazirani self-reduction, not a new general counting primitive.

Rational weighted-cut geometry produces a finite list of failure witnesses. The proof includes the stop-at-`2a` contraction argument, a direct small-vertex branch, actual finite randomness and a bound on omitted failure mass. The retained-cut partition ratios give proposal weights. Inverse multiplicity removes overlapping witness counts and supplies a failure-conditioned law. All partition, cut, proposal, rejection, random-bit and conditional-error budgets are priced. Work has cut dependence **`v^O(c)`**, polynomial at fixed c, not jointly polynomial in a growing contrast parameter.

The final theorem deliberately improves on an [early development note](package/new_construction/CORRELATED_RARITY_CONSTRUCTION.txt) that used bounded ordinary odds and a tentative contraction paragraph. That dated note is preserved for provenance, not used as the governing final theorem. [Source/status explanation](package/new_construction/SOURCE_AND_STATUS.txt).

## Finite simultaneous curve and its physical boundary

For `0<p=pi(disconnection)<=1/2`, target the exact initial law `nu=pi(.|safe)`. The [preserved whole-CDF mechanism](../2026-11-05-p3-passive-calibration/reliability-curve-certificate.md) now receives the constructed correlated static operations. The new proof explicitly replaces failure-sampler and safe-rejection tolerances, boundary conditioning, confidence caps and finite arithmetic. It does not assume exact computational Gibbs draws or free physical preparation.

One finite certificate contains a relative interval of endpoint ratio at most `1+epsilon` for **every positive rational deadline**, simultaneously with probability at least `1-delta`. Preprocessing is polynomial in explicit input size, numerical inverse sampling margin/gap, inverse accuracy and log inverse confidence at fixed c. Queries pay certificate and binary-deadline bit length. There is no inverse global failure-probability or mean-lifetime trajectory factor. Genuine Poisson uniformization preserves the continuous-time deadline law; the discrete endpoint version retains its own positive-semidefinite kernel conditions.

The changing-Gibbs-law corollary uses positive relative factor/rate bands and common transition support, together with the earlier conductance/mass comparison. It does **not** extend the old product-law unknown-support acquisition operation without a new proof. Factor/rate measurement, exact physical safe initialization, local reach, model completeness and implementation precision are still independent costs. The earlier independent-edge all-working point query does not become a correlated all-working whole curve.

An explicit rare-field family has fixed `c=2` and `alpha>=1/2` while conditional odds distortion and declared interaction degree grow without bound. Actual factor bit length and degree-dependent conditional evaluation remain paid. In this family, replacing dependence by independent edges can be exponentially wrong. This is a sufficient correlated tractability class, not a characterization of every dependent reliability model.
