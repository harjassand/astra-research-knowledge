## 2. The main theorem and its actual contract

Begin with \(N\) physical output qubits and target \(\lvert0^N\rangle\). Put every auxiliary system, controller memory and untouched reference into \(A\), and define

\[
P=\lvert0^N\rangle\!\langle0^N\rvert_W\otimes I_A,
\qquad
F(t)=\operatorname{Tr}[P\rho_{WA}(t)].
\]

The auxiliary dimension can grow arbitrarily with \(N\). Its initial state can be mixed and correlated with the output. The proof does not assume freshly initialized or independent controller ancillas.

The physical noise contains the stable additive component

\[
\mathcal N=\lambda\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathcal D[X_i],
\qquad
\mathcal D[L](\rho)
=L\rho L^\dagger-\tfrac12\{L^\dagger L,\rho\},
\qquad \lambda>0.
\]

The controller may use joint Hamiltonians and dissipators,

\[
\mathcal K_t
=-i\!\left[\sum_S H_S(t),\,\cdot\,\right]
+\sum_{S,\alpha}\mathcal D[L_{S,\alpha}(t)].
\]

A term with work support \(S\) may interact with arbitrary auxiliaries. Auxiliary-only operations have zero action on \(P\), so they can be arbitrarily strong without weakening the theorem.

The relevant caps are the strengths of operations that touch the physical output. A sufficient contract is

\[
\max_i\sum_{S\ni i,\alpha}\|L_{S,\alpha}(t)\|^2\le r,
\]

\[
\max_i\sum_{S\ni i}
\left(
\|H_S(t)\|
+\frac12\sum_\alpha\|L_{S,\alpha}(t)\|^2
\right)\le b,
\]

uniformly over time and allowed control choices. Directly warranted bounds on the target’s return and off-diagonal blocks can give smaller constants.

These are **instantaneous strength caps**. An average-power budget alone is a different assumption: increasingly strong short pulses can escape the theorem by paying increasing peak capacity and bandwidth.

Under this contract, I proved

\[
\boxed{
\dot F
\le
-N\lambda F
+(\lambda+r)(1-F)
+2b\sqrt{N F(1-F)}.
}
\tag{1}
\]

Define

\[
C=
\frac{b+\sqrt{b^2+\lambda(\lambda+r)}}{\lambda}.
\]

The resulting fidelity barrier is

\[
\boxed{
F_*=\frac{C^2}{N+C^2}.
}
\tag{2}
\]

It governs the dynamics in two ways:

\[
F(t)\le \max\{F(0),F_*\},
\qquad
\limsup_{t\to\infty}F(t)\le F_*.
\]

If the initial fidelity is below \(F_*\), the controller cannot raise the unconditional fidelity above it. A restoration operation promised for arbitrary initialization must include such inputs.

Consequently, with fixed \(\lambda,r,b\),

\[
F_*=O(N^{-1}).
\]

The complete physical-state error becomes large, because

\[

\rho_{WR},
\lvert0^N\rangle\!\langle0^N\rvert\otimes\rho_R
\right)
\ge 1-F.
\]

Thus this is a necessary obstruction to the full physical-output/reference contract.
