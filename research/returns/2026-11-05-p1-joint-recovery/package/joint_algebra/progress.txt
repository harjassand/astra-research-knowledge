CONCRETE DERIVATION (current partial result; no complete joint law)

For fixed prefix length n and theta -> 0, restrict bath operators X to the SU(2)-invariant algebra, [sum_j sigma_j^a,X]=0. Define Q_a = sum_{i<j} (sigma_i cross sigma_j)_a, the ordered level-one Yangian charge. BCH gives source leakage to second order proportional to i theta^2/4 sum_a sigma_0^a [Q_a,X], so the leading singular-loss quadratic form is
  <X,H_n X> = theta^4/16 sum_a ||[Q_a,X]||_2^2 + O_n(theta^5).
HS normalization can be normalized or unnormalized consistently. Reversing the collision order changes Q to -Q and leaves this form unchanged.

SU(2) covariance eliminates the potential Schur-complement correction in the zero eigenspace of the theta^2 term: that zero eigenspace is exactly invariant bath operators; their second-order leakage has total spin zero, whereas the first-order leakage of every non-invariant input has nonzero total spin. Consequently A* B P_ker A=0. Thus H4 is exactly this Yangian commutator Laplacian on the invariant algebra, rather than an unproved compression of H.

Numerical diagonalization using an exact Clebsch-Gordan invariant basis gives H4 gaps for n=2..7:
  2, .627718676731, .231572115444, .103183430160, .0530761416913, .0300907239598.
n=3 equals (7-sqrt(33))/2. n^4 times these values increases from 32 to 72.25; this suggests n^-4 but is not a theorem. Pair-only restriction gives much larger n=7 gap .11754, and the lowest full mode has about 89.9% pair-operator weight, so higher-body dressing matters.

Exact local identity [Q_a,P_{j,j+1}] = 2 i (sigma_j^a-sigma_{j+1}^a). Thus X=sum_{j=1}^{n-1} a_j(P_{j,j+1}-I/2), with a_0=a_n=0, has leading Rayleigh quotient sum_{j=1}^n (a_j-a_{j-1})^2 / sum_{j=1}^{n-1}a_j^2. This gives an explicit upper bound 4 sin^2(pi/(2n)) on the H4 gap, but not a converse nor an instrument.

This is consequential as a corrected weak-angle algebra: first-order collective noise is blind to the entire Catalan-dimensional invariant algebra, and the next singular-loss order is governed by ordered Yangian charges. It does NOT yet imply the joint operation/matching boundary, since the expansion is fixed-n and optimal N is theta^-4 log(1/epsilon); positivity/rank structure remains necessary.
