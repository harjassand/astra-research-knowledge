# Independent channel/sufficiency construction

## Status and scope

I obtained (i) a rigorous fourth-order weak-angle obstruction at every fixed prefix, identified with a Yangian Dirichlet form; (ii) an exact symmetry reduction useful for computing that obstruction at finite angle; and (iii) a finite, attaining Bellman characterization of the entire arbitrary-instrument chronological receiver class, including a dual converse and a physical realization with two additional work qubits. The Bellman characterization removes unbounded classical branching and gives a genuine finite optimization boundary, but I have **not evaluated it into the requested sharp asymptotic joint memory law**. The Yangian result is **not uniform in the prefix length** and therefore does not establish the endpoint at N proportional to theta^-4 log(1/epsilon).

I did not read any other new constructor's files. I used only the old files explicitly allowed by the parent and primary published research. I found no callable collaboration/send-message tool in the available tool registry; this report is the first actual derivation/status to return to the parent.

## 1. A fixed-prefix theorem: all nontrivial invariant directions leak at order theta^4

Write P_j=SWAP_(S,j), W_n(theta)=exp(i theta P_n)...exp(i theta P_1), and use normalized Hilbert-Schmidt inner products. Let E_S be the trace-preserving conditional expectation onto I_S tensor B(E^n), and define

    R_n(theta)X = (I-E_S) W_n(theta)(I_S tensor X)W_n(theta)^dagger.

Then

    ||R_n(theta)X||_2^2 = ||X||_2^2 - ||T_n(theta)X||_2^2.

Thus R_n^*R_n=I-T_n^*T_n. Its spectrum equals that of the parent's H_n=I-T_n T_n^*, including multiplicity. Define collective Pauli spin and the ordered spin current by

    J^a = sum_j sigma_j^a,
    Y^a = sum_(j<k) (sigma_j cross sigma_k)^a.

Let A_n={X:[J^a,X]=0 for a=x,y,z} be the full SU(2) commutant, including multiplicity operators; it is much larger than the algebra generated by J^2.

### 1.1 Direct leakage expansion

Because P_j=(I+sigma_S dot sigma_j)/2,

    sum_j P_j = n I/2 + (sigma_S dot J)/2,
    [P_k,P_j] = -(i/2) sigma_S dot (sigma_j cross sigma_k),  j<k.

Consequently BCH gives

    log W_n = i theta sum_j P_j + (i theta^2/4) sigma_S dot Y + O_n(theta^3).

For X in A_n the first term commutes with I_S tensor X, hence

    R_n(theta)X = (i theta^2/4) sum_a sigma_S^a tensor [Y^a,X] + O_n(theta^3)||X||_2.

Therefore, as quadratic forms on A_n,

    theta^-4 (I-T_n^*T_n)|_(A_n) -> L_n,
    <X,L_n X> = (1/16) sum_a ||[Y^a,X]||_2^2.                 (1)

Equivalently,

    L_n = (3/16) P_(A_n) ad_(Y^z)^2 P_(A_n).                (2)

The factor 3 in (2) follows from rotational invariance of X. This is a Dirichlet form on the full multiplicity algebra, not a scalar total-spin statistic.

### 1.2 Its kernel is exactly the scalars for every finite n

The operators J^a and Y^a are, up to nonzero constants and a common evaluation-parameter multiple of J^a, the level-zero and level-one generators of the Yangian Y(sl_2) on the tensor product of n identical spin-half evaluation modules. Drinfeld's first realization is generated by these two levels. The corresponding Y(gl_2) center acts as scalars.

Molev's irreducibility criterion gives an especially short check. His Theorem 1.1 says two evaluation modules L(lambda), L(mu) are irreducible in tensor product when A_lambda\\A_mu and A_mu\\A_lambda are noncrossing. For two identical fundamental highest weights lambda=mu=(1,0), both differences are empty. Theorem 1.2 says the n-fold tensor product is irreducible iff every pair is irreducible. Hence the n-fold homogeneous spin-half evaluation representation is irreducible. Schur's lemma then gives

    [J^a,X]=[Y^a,X]=0 for all a  =>  X is scalar.

By (1), ker L_n=C I. Define, for n>=2,

    lambda_n = min { (1/16) sum_a ||[Y^a,X]||_2^2 :
                     X in A_n, Tr X=0, ||X||_2=1 } > 0.    (3)

The irreducibility input here supplies strict positivity, **not a quantitative lower bound in n**.

Sources:

- A. I. Molev, *Irreducibility criterion for tensor products of Yangian evaluation modules*, math/0009183, Theorems 1.1 and 1.2: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0009183 ; full text https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0009183 .
- F. Loebbert, *Lectures on Yangian Symmetry*, 1606.02947, first realization (3.19), coproduct (3.75), and RTT relation (3.107): https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.02947 . The irreducibility statement above uses Molev, not a numerical inference.

### 1.3 Why this is the full gap for small angle at fixed n

The emitter, T_n, and R_n are globally SU(2)-equivariant. The scalar input sector A_n cannot mix with a nonscalar input sector under H_n. On an operator-spin-l sector, l>=1, the leading form is

    (theta^2/4) sum_a ||[J^a,X]||_2^2
       = theta^2 l(l+1)||X||_2^2.

These eigenvalues begin at order theta^2. The nonidentity scalar sector begins at order theta^4 and has positive coefficient (3). Thus for every fixed integer n>=2,

    gamma_n(theta) = theta^4 [lambda_n + O_n(|theta|)],
    lim_(theta->0+) gamma_n(theta)/theta^4 = lambda_n.      (4)

For n=1 there is no nonidentity scalar sector and gamma_1=1-cos^4(theta), of order theta^2. I deliberately use O_n(|theta|), rather than claiming an even sixth-order remainder without checking possible degeneracies.

An explicit but poor uniform remainder follows by expanding the product of n conjugations, each generated by an operator of Hilbert-Schmidt superoperator norm at most 2. For n|theta|<=1, the leakage remainder in 1.1 is at most C n^3 |theta|^3 and the quadratic-form remainder in (4), restricted to A_n, is at most C' n^5 |theta|^5. Hence an explicit small-angle range can be written using lambda_n, e.g. theta <= c lambda_n/n^5 together with a bound keeping the nonscalar sectors above it. This range cannot cover the critical growing-n regime.

### 1.4 Consequence for the parent's general prefix witness

Let eta=2epsilon-(4/3)epsilon^2, d=2^n, and memory dimension m. The witness constraints and scalar kernel imply

    eta >= gamma_n(theta)(1-m/d),
    m >= 2^n max(0, 1-eta/gamma_n(theta)).                  (5)

Combining (4) and (5), for every fixed n and epsilon=o(theta^4), m>=2^n[1-o(1)]. This is an arbitrary-instrument result, including initialized pure workspaces and unlimited classical information. It improves the angle exponent of a crude fixed-prefix polynomial-interpolation estimate, but is **not a growing-prefix memory theorem**.

## 2. Exact scalar-sector reduction and reproducible numerical evidence

Schur-Weyl decomposition gives

    (C^2)^tensor n = direct_sum_j (C^(r_j) tensor V_(j/2)),
    X in A_n iff X=direct_sum_j (X_j tensor I_(j+1)),

where j is twice the spin and r_j is its multiplicity. The commutant dimension is the Catalan number C_n, versus 4^n for the full operator space. Append the source spin as the last spin. In a full total-spin-K sector, K=j+/-1, the collision unitary is W_K on its multiplicity space. There is an exact formula

    T_n(X)_j = [1/(2(j+1))] sum_(K=j+/-1) (K+1)
                     [ W_K (direct_sum_(j'=K+/-1) X_(j')) W_K^dagger ]_(j,j).    (6)

T_n^* is obtained by reversing W_K and W_K^dagger. This formula follows from the partial trace of the Clebsch-Gordan projector and contains all multiplicity coherences. It is not the lossy operation of measuring total spin and discarding multiplicity.

The script `yangian_gap.py` computes L_n using recursive Clebsch-Gordan transforms and (2); `full_scalar_gap.py` computes (6) with Young's orthogonal representation of transpositions. Both are in this directory.

## 3. An exact, attaining finite boundary for the full causal instrument class

This construction is independent of the prefix spectral witness. It directly imposes compatibility across all cuts and preserves the reference. It is an implicit computational boundary, not a derived asymptotic scaling law.

### 3.1 Diamond error reduces exactly to entanglement fidelity

Let F_m(N,theta) be the maximum entanglement fidelity for any chronological receiver with a retained quantum memory Q of dimension at most m between arrivals and arbitrary classical control. The emitter is SU(2)-covariant. A receiver can choose a uniformly random single-qubit Clifford g, rotate every arriving carrier by g^dagger, run its receiver, and rotate its final qubit by g. This realizes the Clifford twirl of its effective logical channel using classical randomness and no additional retained coherent system.

The twirl is depolarizing and preserves entanglement fidelity. For a qubit depolarizing channel its half-diamond distance to identity is exactly 1-F. Every channel, twirled or not, has half-diamond error at least 1-F. Consequently

    epsilon_m^*(N,theta) = 1-F_m(N,theta).                 (7)

This equality includes inaccessible references of arbitrary dimension through the diamond norm; the optimization can use the two-dimensional Choi reference R.

### 3.2 The Bellman state retains R and the inaccessible emitter

The offline control state is a subnormalized rho_RSQ on dimension 4m. Neither R nor S is granted to the receiver. They are included only in the numerical description used to synthesize the allowed policy. Define

    G_theta(rho) = U_SE (rho_RSQ tensor I_E/2) U_SE^dagger,
    Xi_K(rho) = (I_RS tensor K) G_theta(rho) (I_RS tensor K^dagger),

for K:Q tensor E -> Q. A refined instrument has matrices K_a with

    sum_a K_a^dagger K_a = I_(2m).

Refining Kraus outcomes and retaining the refined label classically never reduces the attainable fidelity. This accounts for arbitrary CPTP instruments, not just isometries or unitary receivers.

Let V_L(rho) be the optimal unnormalized entanglement fidelity with L carriers left. Its terminal value is the small SDP

    V_0(rho) = max_(D:Q->B CPTP) Tr[Phi_RB (id_R tensor D)(Tr_S rho)]
             = (1/2) min { Tr Z_Q : I_R tensor Z_Q >= Tr_S rho }.    (8)

Then the exact recursion is

    V_L(rho) = max_(sum K_a^dagger K_a=I) sum_a V_(L-1)(Xi_(K_a)(rho)),       (9)
    F_m(N,theta) = V_N(Phi_RS tensor |0><0|_Q).                           (10)

For an arbitrary initialized mixed workspace, convexity in its initial state shows that some pure initial state is at least as good. Any pure state is unitarily equivalent to |0>. A classical mixture of initial policies cannot beat the best component. Thus (10) does not assume a tracial or uncorrelated-purifier workspace accidentally; it optimizes all allowed initialized workspaces.

The conditional-min-entropy identity used in (8) is the standard operational characterization of singlet fraction: R. Koenig, R. Renner, C. Schaffner, *The operational meaning of min- and max-entropy*, IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory 55, 4337-4347 (2009), https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2009.2025545 .

### 3.3 Unlimited classical histories can be reduced to a finite, attaining tree

Each V_L is continuous, convex, positively homogeneous, and bounded between 0 and Tr rho; it is a supremum of bounded fidelity effects.

Normalize nonzero Kraus matrices to ||K||_F=1 and put their squared norms in weights. For a fixed state rho, (9) is a linear optimization over positive measures mu on the compact unit sphere of complex m-by-2m matrices:

    integral K^dagger K dmu(K)=I_(2m),
    maximize integral V_(L-1)(Xi_K(rho)) dmu(K).

The trace of the moment constraint fixes mu's total mass to 2m. The feasible measure set is compact, so a maximum exists. Conic Caratheodory applied jointly to the Hermitian moment matrix and the objective gives an optimum with at most

    B=4m^2+1                                                       (11)

outcomes at each node. (An extreme-measure refinement can reduce this to 4m^2; the +1 bound is enough and avoids relying on that refinement.) Induction gives an attaining finite tree of depth N and branching at most B, with an optimal terminal CPTP decoder at each leaf.

Equivalently, for fixed N,m the optimum is a finite compact polynomial optimization over all node Kraus matrices plus terminal decoder Choi matrices. The tree can be enormous, but this proves that unlimited classical histories and continuous outcomes do not cause an unattained supremum or an unbounded classical-outcome loophole. For algebraic cos(theta),sin(theta), the finite threshold problem can in principle be decided by real quantifier elimination; numerical implementations can instead use globally convergent polynomial-optimization bounds. No efficient algorithm is claimed.

### 3.4 A local dual gives converse certificates compatible across time

The measure optimization has the dual

    V_L(rho) = inf_H Tr H,
    Tr(H K^dagger K) >= V_(L-1)(Xi_K(rho))
               for every K with ||K||_F=1,                         (12)

where H is Hermitian on Q tensor E. A sufficiently large scalar H is strictly feasible. Compact moment duality supplies equality; any feasible H gives an upper bound on attainable fidelity. One can replace V_(L-1) by any certified upper function to obtain a recursive converse. This is stronger in form than testing unrelated prefix compressions: every continuation depends on the actual reachable rho_RSQ and a realizable next instrument.

This is consistent with the global memory-decomposition criterion for quantum combs in A. Bisio, G. M. D'Ariano, P. Perinotti, M. Sedlak, *Memory cost of quantum protocols*, Phys. Rev. A 85, 032333 (2012), https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.85.032333 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.3853 . Their global compatibility requirement is why a collection of independent good prefix channels is not by itself a streaming construction.

### 3.5 Physical realization and what memory is being counted

Equations (7)-(12) measure retained quantum memory between arrivals. They can be translated to an explicit peak-workspace bound without hiding a large Stinespring environment.

Pad m to a power of two. Keep Q, the current carrier E, and one binary measurement pointer. Any finite Kraus instrument with K_a:QE->Q can be implemented by a sequential binary POVM on QE using one resettable pointer qubit, outcome-dependent polar isometries, and discarding the unused two-dimensional factor after selecting an outcome. Thus an attaining instrument policy can be realized with at most

    ceil(log_2 m)+2 coherent qubits at any instant,                  (13)

including the incoming carrier and pointer. All histories and the finite policy tree are classical. The terminal decoder can be implemented with the same constant overhead. Conversely a receiver with M total coherent qubits has retained dimension at most 2^M, so the cut-memory optimization is a valid relaxation for its converse. In particular, for M>=2,

    1-F_(2^M)(N,theta) <= epsilon_peak^*(M;N,theta)
                       <= 1-F_(2^(M-2))(N,theta).                   (14)

This specifies the possible additive overhead rather than silently identifying retained and physical peak memory.

A direct binary-POVM implementation uses O(B) generic unitaries on a space of dimension at most 4m per carrier. With arbitrary continuous two-qubit gates, a crude synthesis bound is O(N m^4) elementary gates, plus terminal decoding and classical policy lookup, before finite-gate-set approximation overhead. This is a finite physical attainment bound, **not an optimal processing law**, and its classical storage may be exponential in N. The already-known store-all inverse reader remains vastly more practical.

## 4. What is not established

1. No quantitative lower bound lambda_n >= c/n^4, or any comparably strong bound, was proved.
2. No finite-angle full-gap/profile estimate uniform in n,theta was proved.
3. No causal compression policy with sublinear retained memory and vanishing critical-regime error was found.
4. The Bellman boundary is exact and attainable but still has to be analyzed or solved. Writing it down does not prove linear memory, N/log N optimality, or a processing lower bound.
5. In particular, the known Ω(N/log N) versus O(N) fixed-angle memory interval is not closed by this report. Nor is the joint theta,epsilon boundary at N=Theta(theta^-4 log(1/epsilon)).

The most promising concrete bridge is a uniform coercivity/profile estimate for the SU(2)/Yangian transfer channel, potentially resembling theta^4 q^n times a polynomial in n. The numerical evidence motivates looking for it, but is not the estimate itself. The Bellman formulation provides an independent exact benchmark for any proposed general receiver or converse and includes the initialized-workspace/classical-history cases explicitly.
