## Remaining gap

This package does not show that the specified shape family covers all important actual states, current, or occupation. Generic rough/overhanging, multiple-droplet and other winding configurations remain. No efficient edge-sampling oracle for the aggregated flow is supplied; the scalar analytic occupation bound does not require enumerating its edges. No full mean-hitting-time predictor, polynomial physical simulation, generic engineering capability or civilization-level breakthrough is claimed.

Potential theory, Peierls methods and Ferrers partition functions are established machinery. Novelty of this specific finite construction relative to the entire literature is not established. Its demonstrated contribution here is a reusable source-faithful occupation certificate in an explicitly high-Gibbs-mass thermal family.
