# Prior methods and the contribution claimed here

The underlying tools are standard: Dirichlet's variational upper bound on capacity, Peierls contour counting and full-domain flips, positive transfer/dynamic-programming recurrences, exponential tilting, exact rejection sampling, and directed numerical enclosure. This package does not claim to invent those principles or to establish novelty against all literature.

The reusable work here is their explicit source-faithful assembly: a global M_s trial with complete topology/target/source terms; physical magnetic-walk moments and a finite tail; removal of actual root/order/gate multiplicities; a specific finite upper receipt sharper than the previously executed bound; and an exact validated inverse-flip proposal identity with its missing-energy and acceptance costs. The same-cut Ferrers subset is used only for proposal acceptance, not falsely as a capacity lower.

The original program's primary literature and prior-art comparison are supplied in the authorized source and `03_thermal_core_dependency/PRIOR_ART.md`. In particular, existing low-temperature metastability/overlap theory is not treated as an acquired polynomial oracle for the full critical canonical law. No universal mixing, near-optimality or fully implemented large-instance sampler is imported from an asymptotic result.

Independent review and finite checks increase confidence in the stated result; they are not peer review, a formal proof assistant verification, or evidence of a civilization-changing breakthrough.
