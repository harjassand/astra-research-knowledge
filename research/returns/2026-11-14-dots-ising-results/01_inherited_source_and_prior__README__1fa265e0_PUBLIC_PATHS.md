# Candidate reusable partial result: actual M5 entrance-current reduction

## What this package establishes, subject to its stated inherited interfaces

For the actual periodic heatbath Ising model and soft thermal-core source, explicit finite current bounds reduce structural entrances outside M5 to a single large, relatively dense precursor class. The bounds retain source-clock normalization, shape entropy, thermal interiors, mergers, hole closure and winding. They do not establish subsequent physical growth, weighted residence, or the full first-passage predictor.

At theta=4, R=200, L=1600, r=5, epsilon=.01 and the acquired source clock kappa=epsilon/(64D0), the union of excluded structural entrance edges has current at most exp(-75) C_kappa. This is normalized by capacity, NOT necessarily by total structural entrance current, which can be smaller.

The remaining class has a nonwinding new component, one joined precursor of filled area in (4R²,5R²], fewer than25% minus spins inside that precursor, and every other joined component of filled area <R². Unchanged exterior configurations remain unrestricted.
