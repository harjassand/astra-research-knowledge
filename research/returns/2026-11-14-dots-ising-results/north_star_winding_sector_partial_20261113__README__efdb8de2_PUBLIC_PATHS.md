# Original-clock winding-sector major partial

This package consolidates the previously reviewed axis result with a new native, retained-wall construction for intermediate diagonal walls. It is a substantial mathematical partial result, NOT a full exit/residence theorem or a civilisation-changing engineering breakthrough.

## New result
For exactly two actual diagonal winding walls, with arbitrary contractible defects and unchanged original source, core, majority target and physical heat-bath clock:

| R | occupation divided by original core partition Z_A |
|---|---|
|80| at most exp(-47)|
|200| at most exp(-440)|
|1000| at most exp(-3196)|
|10000| at most exp(-34695)|

The native construction covers each selected actual diagonal wall shorter than2.5L. It uses a one-pin-band periodic-walk envelope, freezes initial bounded pockets, and orders two-neighbor births to preserve essential-minus connectivity. It reaches the ORIGINAL majority target and retains the actual wall as an observable label. The thermal source preimages are summed with a paid conditional partition, not replaced by a new Gibbs entrance law.

The complementary total-essential-perimeter tail covers the remainder of the exactly-two-diagonal class. The original axis/global topology result and this diagonal result together bound ALL configurations with at least one essential wall by exp(-137) and exp(-4355) at R1000 and10000 respectively. No all-winding claim is made for the smaller instances from these constants.

## What remains open
Configurations with ZERO essential walls, including large contractible high-mass geometry, are not controlled by this package. This is not full source coverage, a complete rare-transition algorithm, or proof of rapid physical progress. All finite constants retain the original site-clock normalization.

Use Python3 standard library only:

    python run_checks.py
    python verify_manifest.py

The runner can be launched from another working directory. It reruns the full earlier original-source/capacity/axis chain, exact envelope arithmetic, finite geometry diagnostics, rational diagonal tail/partition and linked union receipts. Diagnostics corroborate the written proofs; they do not replace topology or infer global coverage from small grids.
