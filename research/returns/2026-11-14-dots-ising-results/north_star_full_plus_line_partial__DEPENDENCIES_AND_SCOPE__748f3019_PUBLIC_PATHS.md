# Exact dependency and review scope

## Original process
The original periodic rate-one-per-site ferromagnetic heatbath, decreasing core A=C_r, increasing target B={M>=0}, and original accepted rate-kappa core clock remain unchanged. The prior package's authorized source snapshot specifies one zero-coupling diagonal per plaquette for topology only. Physical rates and perimeters remain four-neighbor. The full original acquisition archive was not reconstructed; inherited source/contour/core interfaces remain explicit assumptions/results.

## New proof chain
1. Original voltage is decreasing by attractive coupling and shared accepted source clock. This does NOT assume attractiveness of reflected-outside-core dynamics.
2. Each coordinate's total upward native current is at most the actual capacity; direct source/target overlap is paid.
3. Upward prefix paths stop at first B, retaining an unprocessed opposite column at both endpoints.
4. Fixed-edge source patterns on the overwritten prefix are summed, not claimed uniquely recoverable. Worst-boundary comparison is pointwise; no Gibbs entrance law is substituted.
5. One fixed winding wall is removed; another winding wall is selected and counted. Every remaining wall, including nested holes and additional winding loops, is included in a positive contour-product upper.
6. The original conservative4^p contour count, interface entropy4^L and volume pressure are paid. A proposed sharper3^p improvement is NOT part of this package.
7. The2L full-line labels are paid by a positive union bound. No line uniqueness is assumed.

## Inputs actually included
- Complete previous high-mass package, including its optimized capacity proof/check and authorized source snapshot.
- Original-current proof plus independent derivation and exact rational three-spin tests including source-target overlap.
- Fixed SW-NE triangulation wall decomposition and prefix/boundary diagnostics.
- Independent full-line review and exact diagonal-band count.
- Headline scalar code that imports regenerated optimized-capacity data.

The headline scripts use a floating logarithm only to propose an integer; exact positive Taylor sums then certify that integer. Their accepted comparisons are rational. The diagonal lower baseline uses the included full-wall pressure receipt, not an optional external density file.

## Package-specific edits
Copied proof documents preserve the reviewed theorem. The scalar script's capacity input was changed from frozen numerical constants to reading the LOCAL regenerated dependency, with an assertion that the reviewed four constants match. The diagonal review's optional external singleton-density reference was replaced by the weaker LOCAL full-wall pressure bound. The diagonal count uses exact Fraction arithmetic for its weight ratio. These changes remove stale/external-input risks without strengthening the theorem.

## Limitations
No full complementary-set occupation, general rough-winding coverage, full hitting-time enclosure or civilization-changing capability is proved. The explicit diagonal band is a coverage warning, not a lower bound on its voltage or a counterexample to a future theorem. Large-L pressure costs are present, so the finite examples must not be generalized to arbitrary thermodynamic limits without rechecking every term.
