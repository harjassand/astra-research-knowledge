# Same-encoding extension of the global perimeter tail

The original RESULT.md was phrased for a fixed zero-coupling triangulation. The consolidated winding union instead defines EVERY wall and homology using the SAME deterministic physical-plus adaptive resolution. The identical bound and constants hold for that encoding; no identification of homology across two different resolutions is made.

For each actual configuration resolve all checkerboards by the deterministic physical-plus rule, and contract zero-coupling dual edges. Every resolved cycle becomes a closed physical square-dual nonbacktracking walk of its physical length. No physical edge is repeated immediately backwards: a simple resolved cycle cannot traverse that disagreement edge twice. At a square-dual vertex there are at most three next physical edges after excluding the entering edge. Thus the enlarged universe of ALL rooted physical nonbacktracking walks has at most100 n 3^s candidates at length s, independently of which triangulation produced a particular walk. Physical lengths are at least4 for the present L, and an essential walk of primitive homology(a,b) has length at least(|a|+|b|)L.

A collection of these actual resolved physical walks specifies its union of physical disagreement edges. On the connected physical torus graph, that union determines the spin configuration up to one global spin sign. The adaptive encoding is a deterministic function of that spin configuration. Therefore each physical-walk collection has at most TWO spin-configuration preimages: the collection together with one reference-spin sign reconstructs a configuration uniquely. This is precisely the single global factor2 in the positive upper-bound sum. There is NO factor for independently choosing plaquette diagonals, nor a factor two per contour. Allowing invalid, intersecting, differently resolved or repeated candidate walks in the product upper bound only enlarges a nonnegative sum.

Consequently the entire exponential-moment proof of RESULT.md applies verbatim with P_w defined by the adaptive physical-plus walls, the same100 n 3^s count, the same global factor2, ordinary full-contour pressure, and original non-target field cap. All scalar receipts are unchanged: occupation/Z_A for adaptive P_w>=5L is at most exp(-14R+3) on the stated finite range.

The topological classification also uses only THIS adaptive encoding: its cycles are embedded and disjoint before zero-edge contraction, so essential cycles are parallel and their number is even. We do not substitute the essential set of a fixed triangulation. The original core A, physical Hamiltonian, source, voltage and clock remain unchanged.
