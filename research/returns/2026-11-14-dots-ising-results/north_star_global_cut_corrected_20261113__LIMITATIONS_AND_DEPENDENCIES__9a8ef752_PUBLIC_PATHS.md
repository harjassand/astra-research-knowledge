## Arithmetic contract

This is not an all-integer proof assistant certificate. Magnetic and convolution recurrences rely on correctly implemented directed binary floating-point arithmetic and compiler semantics under the recorded flags. Scalar calculations rely on Python Decimal correctly rounded exp/ln plus outward neighbors and explicit rounding modes. The executed platform used 64-bit long-double mantissa and passed runtime directed-rounding checks. Tests independently compare small recurrences against exact Fractions, but tests alone do not prove compiler semantics.

Large outputs use exact hexadecimal floating values and exact powers-of-two scaling. No libm exp/log is used inside the magnetic recurrence. All three original large runs reported no underflow or overflow. The proof and finite-tail terms remain necessary even if every test passes.
