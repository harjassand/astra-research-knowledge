A variable-network bottleneck not removed by graph-preserving catalysis

Question distinguished. The completed catalytic compiler has constants depending on a FIXED weakly reversible reaction structure R. This note excludes a uniform polynomial-time/size analogue over arbitrary variable reaction descriptions, even on reversible bounded-locality examples with0/1 populations and constant activities. It does not contradict the fixed-R theorem.

Graphs. For infinitely many n divisible by300 there is an undirected labelled multigraph of maximum degree20 such that every S with n/3<=|S|<=2n/3 has at least n/100 crossing edges. An elementary existence proof uses ten independent uniform random permutations, interpreted as undirected labelled edges (loops harmless). For n/3<=s=|S|<=n/2, put m=n/100. For one permutation, if at most m vertices of S map outside S, some subset T of size s-m maps entirely into S. The probability is at most
 binom(s,m)(s/n)^(s-m)
 <=(50e)^(n/100) 2^(-(1/3-1/100)n)
 <exp(-0.17n).
If the TOTAL outgoing crossing count of ten permutations is at most m, each permutation satisfies this event. Independence gives probability <exp(-1.7n). A union bound over fewer than2^n sets is <1 for large n. Complementation supplies the upper half of the balanced range. Delete every loop before the reaction construction; this preserves all cut sizes and the degree bound. Parallel labelled edges may remain. Thus suitable graphs exist; selecting one by finite search introduces no hypothesis of a polynomial expander construction.

Reaction class. For each vertex i introduce two mathematical species X_i^+,X_i^- with total population1. For each labelled graph edge e introduce E_e^same,E_e^different, also with total1. Start with all spins plus and each edge marked same. For each vertex and each possible assignment of its bounded number of DISTINCT neighbours, include an original reaction that flips its spin and updates every incident labelled edge-agreement bit, retaining each distinct neighbour's spin species ONCE as a catalyst, even if parallel edges join it to i. Include its reverse. There are O(n) such reactions with a fixed (possibly large) constant and bounded molecularity, because degree<=20. Each source and product has0/1 entries. The structure is reversible. Its actual communicating class is exactly the2^n consistent spin/edge configurations: every spin flip is enabled in its matching local pattern, and every reaction preserves consistency. Its full description has O(n log n) bits.

Poisson target. Assign activity1 to spin species and different-edge species, and the fixed rational activity K=2^400 to same-edge species. Since all populations are0/1, factorials are1. Conditional Poisson on the actual class is exactly
 pi(sigma)=K^(number of agreeing labelled edges)/Z.
Spin reversal is a symmetry. Let B be the balanced-spin states n/3<=number_minus<=2n/3. Relative to either all-equal state, every state in B has weight at most K^(-n/100). Hence
 pi(B)<=2^(n-1)K^(-n/100)=2^(-3n-1).
The all-plus initial state u satisfies pi(u)>=2^-n, since every configuration has weight at most that of u and there are2^n of them.

Every allowed catalytic augmentation leaves every enabled population-changing edge a SINGLE vertex flip; it cannot add a jump across B. Catalyst multiplicities beyond1 in any coordinate make that channel disabled on this0/1 class. Every enabled intensity with nominal coefficient<=1 is therefore at most1. If the augmentation has J physical labelled channels, total intensity at every state is at most J, including null channels.

Take ANY such nominal generator with exact stationary law pi; its choice may depend on the all-plus initial condition. For its transition kernel, stationarity and nonnegativity give
 P_t(u,x)<=pi(x)/pi(u).
The expected number of firings whose pre-state is in B by time T is at most
 T J pi(B)/pi(u)<=TJ 2^(-2n-1).
Starting from all plus, reaching a negative-spin phase with number_minus>2n/3 must use such a firing, because a single spin flip cannot bypass the balanced band. Therefore its probability by time T is at most that bound. In pi the negative phase has mass at least(1-pi(B))/2. If TV(P_T(u,.),pi)<=1/8, then for large n it is necessary that
 TJ>=2^(2n-3).

Consequences. The input numerical size W, at fixed accuracy1/8 and fixed activityK, is O(n) with a large constant. The initial population is O(n). A uniform polynomial compiler cannot output more than polynomially many channels, and a uniform polynomial physical horizon would violate the last bound. The obstruction already holds at nominal coefficients, hence also for any error-robust contract that includes them. Arbitrary state-dependent catalyst degrees do not evade it on0/1 populations, and parallel channels only increase J, which is charged. The proof uses no complexity-theoretic assumption, counting oracle, physical experiment or biological realization.

Limits. This is not a lower bound on the fixed-R constants or on the newly completed finite-R compiler: for each graph those constants may be exponentially large in n. It does not exclude new graph edges, block moves, auxiliary computational states with a different target contract, larger coefficient bounds, or restricted high-temperature families. It identifies an exact scalability obstruction, rather than treating successful fixed-R preprocessing as a dimension-uniform efficient physical program.
