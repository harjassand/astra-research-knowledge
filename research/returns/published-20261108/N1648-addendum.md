# N1648 addendum — two-linkage marked-state gluing failure

For the native binary two-linkage network0<->A with rates k_plus,k_minus and A+B<->A+C with rates u,v>0, class A=n>=0,B+C=1, the exact invariant probability is Poisson(k_plus/k_minus) times B/C weights(v,u)/(u+v), with detailed balance on every edge. Native immigration-death A is nonexplosive and finite-interval switch intensity is integrable. Nevertheless, at reachable last-target state((n,1,0),A+B),n>1, the carried-target factorial V=sum_i log((x_i-t_i)!) has birth/death/switch increments log n,0,0 when each fired reaction updates the last mark to its target. Its embedded one-step mean is k_plus log n/[k_plus+(k_minus+u)n]>0; the C-mark analogue uses v. Every designated within-link target-following episode has positive expected drift, so the single-last-mark finite-exceptional-set construction cannot be glued naively over linkage classes on infinitely many marked states, despite positive recurrence. Ordinary unmarked log(n!) has CTMC drift k_plus log(n+1)-k_minus*n*log n<0 eventually.
