## Claim
A bounded local ownership/short-lease/deadline module has a joint sparse failure bound after causal eligibility. An exact fully secular Davies model with distinct ordered nonzero gaps and uniform local bath rates has an arbitrary-encoding/decoder entanglement-fidelity bound. Specified affine perturbations establish applicability almost everywhere. Cold/thermal domination and an exact mediator example obstruct proposed transfer arguments. The global autonomous quantum threshold and noisy-write-to-passive-retention bridge remain OPEN; the north star was NOT_ACHIEVED.

## Inputs and assumptions
Service: bounded-degree conflict graph, clean compatible ownership/tag states, continuously eligible requests and independent fixed-rate local CP-event proposal clocks. Bath: N qubits, simple spectrum and distinct ordered nonzero gaps, sup_i sum_{X contains i}||h_X||<=J, independent onsite X/Y/Z baths and all rates>=g>0 on |omega|<=4J uniformly in N. Affine genericity requires perturbation directions containing onsite X,Z and ZZ along one spanning nearest-neighbor path; uniform Jbar and g must persist. The other two results use the separately stated commuting-Pauli or two-qubit model.

## Costs
All service supports, rates and local dimensions are independent of device size for a chosen finite deadline H. Set h=H/(4 nu_d), m=max(0,floor(h/S)-2), beta_H=exp[-H(log2-1/2)]+(2h/S+4)(1-p)^m and chi=3(d^2+1). Any fixed marked witness set F has probability<=beta_H^(|F|/chi). Bath time is the displayed generator time: F_ent(t)<=(1+exp(-Ngt))/2; target error epsilon<1/2 needs t<=-log(1-2epsilon)/(Ng). Fixed physical coupling and uniform secular rates are not interchangeable. Driven resets consume power/export entropy; encoding/readout have separate contracts.

## Limits
No theorem preserves the quantum causal frontier and protected lower-level idles during noisy controller reconstruction. Classical dwell/heartbeat bounds do not imply NEXTOP progress. No joint diamond-norm induction for actual hierarchical macro-locations under general local GKLS noise is proved. The service CP-event representation is explicit and is not generic GKLS uniformization. Fully secular frequency resolution may require microscopic coupling decreasing with N, so physical g_N can vanish. Restricted geometry/symmetry needs a separate nonresonance witness. Neither entropy domination failure nor the bare-logical mediator example proves a protected physical memory impossible. No usable device, matched practical advantage or historical priority is established. Earlier Gibbs constructions remain unchanged.

## Open obligation
Prove quantum strong simulation preserving causal order and protected idles through controller repair, and the joint diamond-norm recursion for actual macro-locations. Bath-resolution-aware memory transfer and paid noisy-write basin entry remain separate openings.
