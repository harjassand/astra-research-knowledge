## Claim
Construct a uniform family with 3N/2<=m<=2N and combinatorial gap>=1/3 against every entangled/mixed state, but 0<lambda_min(H)<=288/sqrt(N), witnessed by an explicit rational product state. Thus fixed alphabet, precision, dimension, locality and overlap do not GENERICALLY convert violation density into a constant normalized energy gap. The unrestricted quantum-PCP compiler remains OPEN; this does not reject a specially structured amplifier output family.

## Inputs and assumptions
Normalized projector Hamiltonian H=m^-1 sum_e h_e on N=K^2 qubits, integer K>=720000. The principal construction uses two-qubit rank-one projectors from at most four fixed matrices with fixed rational real/imaginary entries, site degree<=4 and term-overlap degree<=6. Combinatorial energy is inf over ALL entangled/mixed states rho of the fraction of terms with Tr(h_e rho)>0, an exact zero-versus-positive statistic rather than expected energy.
