Uniform pin-Gram sampling requires its explicit representation; unpinned strong injectivity/BP fixed-depth strong-noise results do not establish arbitrary native pin stability. The Bell obstruction needs the stronger ALL-positive-endpoint-effect/common-linear-scalar/geometrically bounded physical representation; it does not rule out a representation only for one classical measurement law or establish sampling hardness.
