2. A catalyst is not automatically an equilibrium switch

For an introduced two-state crosslink fraction x with fixed forward and reverse
rates kf and kr, suppose the catalyst multiplies BOTH by a nonnegative c(t):
  x' = c(t) [kf*(1-x) - kr*x].
Then x_eq=kf/(kf+kr), and
  x(t)-x_eq = (x(0)-x_eq)*exp[-(kf+kr)*integral c(t)dt].
No catalyst schedule of this type changes which side of equilibrium is approached.
It can freeze a nonequilibrium state when c=0; reactivation moves in the same
direction. With kf=4, kr=1 and x(0)=0.2, activation reaches 0.795957; deactivation
freezes it and reactivation approaches 0.8. It cannot return below an introduced
gel threshold 0.5 without changing another physical operation.

This is not an all-photochemistry theorem. A photoswitch can change relative
reactant energies, a bath can change water/chemical activities, temperature can
change equilibrium, and a driven reaction can couple energy into the cycle.
Those operations must actually be supplied.

Bond EXCHANGE gives a different representation. A connected network may preserve
crosslink count while changing which chain pairs are connected and thus relaxing
stored strain. The controlled state is a rest metric, not necessarily the amount
of solid. The read thioester construction supplies this distinction at its actual
scope, with activation/inhibition ingredients and preparation still paid.

3. Coherent introduced rest-curvature writer

Use a homogeneous beam model:
  M = B*(kappa-kappa0),
  kappa0' = a(t)*(kappa-kappa0).
Here B is instantaneous bending stiffness and kappa0 is stored rest curvature.
External mechanical tools impose kappa. Optical or thermal input may control a,
but that law and the physical tools are NOT provided by the equations.

For a constant-rate ramp kappa'=v lasting T with a=a_on:
  e(T)=kappa(T)-kappa0(T)=v*(1-exp(-a_on*T))/a_on.
Holding the imposed shape for H then gives e(T+H)=e(T)*exp(-a_on*H).
The writer's moment is B*e and work is B*L*integral[e*kappa' dt].
Dissipation B*L*integral[a*e^2 dt] plus final stored energy B*L*e^2/2 equals
the applied work. Thus relaxing strain does not erase the forming work or the
tool's moment reaction.

Introduced E=500 MPa, 40x35 mm solid section, density1200 kg/m3 and length0.5 m
give B=71.4583 N m2 and body mass0.84 kg. These are NOT a material point from any
source, and exclude writer, attachment, optics, sensors and all preparation.
At target curvature2/m, a_on0.1/s, 60 s ramp and30 s hold, the model leaves
kappa0=1.983445/m. Exact planar centerline integration gives2.01294 mm endpoint
error, while the writer supplies23.7604 N m peak and19.8594 J mechanical work.
Peak introduced surface stress is2.90944 MPa. Final residual elastic energy is
0.00489585 J; most input work is dissipated. No physical thermal map follows.
Reversal from the stored positive curvature toward -2/m is a further signed
state-writing operation, not a fresh initial condition.

The calculation assumes externally maintained uniform planar curvature. It does

6. Physical opening and unresolved operation

A useful positive route is to write stored geometry through controlled exchange,
then physically retain the formed body until its active state has decayed or been
reversibly switched off. This preserves a connected material load path and can
avoid replicated powered joints. It has finite forming moments and energy in the
introduced model. The required combination of high stiffness, fast addressable
exchange, very slow service creep, rapid readiness and regenerable switch is
UNCONSTRUCTED. Other mechanical, thermal, layered or hybrid states are legitimate
alternatives and must receive the same available tools in any whole-operation
comparison. No named existing material receives favorable properties from an
unrelated source.

A complete writer also needs actual stock feed, spatial activation, transport,
supports, observation, end fixtures, load return, access and continuous wear
history. Thermoplastic forming, ordinary adjustable fixtures, layer jamming and
prepared-part exchange are strong existing operations. This note supplies a
physical representation and quantitative interface for investigating that full
operation; it does not establish a practical advantage, a newly qualified
material, physical production, experimental execution or unlimited renewability.
