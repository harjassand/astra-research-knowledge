The pure states in the ensemble optimization on the left may be entangled across copies.

### The Gaussian optimization is additive

\[

3. Approximate their entropy function uniformly by a rational polynomial.
4. Use exact semialgebraic feasibility and sample-point algorithms to optimize that polynomial.
