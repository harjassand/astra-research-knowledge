P3 — LOCAL STATE RESTORATION HAS A PRECISION COST
Independent research return, 7 November 2026, Australia/Brisbane

STATUS AND THE QUESTION RESOLVED

The endpoint selected for this continuation is the resource relation between
efficient state preparation and reliable local state restoration: can a supplied
preparation procedure become a fixed, continuously reusable process that restores
the actual output system from arbitrary initial conditions, and can it retain the
preparation procedure's accuracy dependence?

The answer has two parts, with different accuracy contracts made explicit from
the outset.

1. A supplied q-qubit, T-gate preparation circuit can be converted into a fixed
   random-scan process of channels acting on at most five retained qubits per
   event. It uses q+T retained qubits, has a unique stationary history state, and
   restores that state from every initial state, including invalid clock states
   and states correlated with an untouched reference. Its physical output is
   within a chosen clock error delta of the circuit's output without heralding.
   A sufficient event count is polynomial in q, T, 1/delta and log(1/epsilon).
   The process has an explicit, uniform perturbation and continuing-maintenance
   budget. It does not assume an efficient Gibbs oracle or an unknown inverse.

2. A universal conversion polynomial in log(1/delta) is impossible within this
   local, restoring interface. A finite Curie–Weiss family has polynomial-size
   active preparation, yet every local process with an exactly canonical
   stationary physical marginal has an exponential mixing obstruction. The
   obstruction survives arbitrary finite quantum auxiliaries. More strongly,
   an autonomous process that promises the correct physical output from every
   initial condition must pay an inverse-accuracy cost even if its auxiliaries
   need not converge or have a unique stationary state.

These statements resolve the qualitative equivalence and precision-separation
question. They do not determine the optimal exponent of inverse accuracy for
general quantum auxiliaries. They do not establish practical commissioning,
constant-rate physical fault tolerance, passive equilibration under the original
Hamiltonian, or thermodynamic work cost from a logical event count.

The broader programme remains directed at reproducible state control with its
information and physical resources charged. This result identifies a genuine
boundary within that endpoint; it does not rename a driven approximate process
as an exact equilibrium bath, or declare an economical physical machine obtained.

PRIOR SCIENCE AND EVIDENCE ORDER

The general circuit/dissipation connection is established science. Verstraete,
Wolf and Cirac constructed local dissipative computation whose steady state
encodes a circuit's output [1]. Quantum bottleneck machinery was developed by
Gamarnik, Kiani and Zlokapa [2] and by Rakovszky, Placke, Breuckmann and Khemani
[3]. No historical-priority claim is made for universality, history clocks, or
quantum bottlenecks. The contribution constructed here is the combined interface
and precision theorem, its explicit weighted and self-initializing process, the
complete-input restoration proof, finite event certificate, perturbation budget,
and auxiliary-robust application to the existing thermal-state capital.

Before selecting this destination I checked the current Astra record, including
the N90/N95/N96/N98 counting and coherent-kernel line, Chen–Liu's v2 original [4],
and the all-temperature local Markov work of Chen–Rouze [5] and Kuwahara [6].
The counting route already contains a conditional coherent compiler; the
global versus local Markov distinction remains substantive. These findings
favoured the operational precision question over rebuilding a nearby counting
compiler. None of those results is an efficient oracle in the proof below.
The source and access ledger records unsuccessful originals without recovering
them by an alternative route. No peer operation, worker, or experiment was used.

1. INPUT, OUTPUT, AND COST CONTRACT

Write D(rho,sigma)=||rho-sigma||_1/2. The circuit input is an actually supplied
finite description

    C = U_T ... U_1,     U_t acts on at most two of q work qubits,

starting from |0^q>. Some work qubits are the physical output S; the remainder
are source workspace or purification registers. Let tau_C be the circuit's
output on S. A desired state tau is connected to the input by a separately
warranted source bound D(tau_C,tau)<=eta_src.

The theorem takes exact logical unitaries. Native synthesis and calibration
errors enter the perturbation budget below. The source description and its
adjoint gates are known: U_t^dagger is the inverse of a specified logical gate,
not access to the inverse of an unknown apparatus. Reset to |0>, independent
random bits, labelled controlled gates, and discarded temporary environment
registers are paid resources. Their absence is not repaired by this theorem.

A preparation algorithm that includes classical coins or resets can first be
made into a finite unitary preparation with explicit ancillary records. Bound
its running time, truncate any rejection routine with a charged failure budget,
implement the deterministic computation reversibly, and retain its environment
until the output is discarded. All of that work contributes to q, T and
eta_src. A single sampled branch of a mixed-state preparation is not the full
preparation circuit. In particular, the earlier N=64 sampled Schur circuit
cannot be substituted for the complete all-sector source algorithm.

An event of the new process chooses a channel from a fixed finite list. Each
channel acts on at most five retained qubits; different events may address
different qubits. There is no geometric nearest-neighbour promise. Routing,
addressing, controller storage and local instrument realization are separate
costs. The bound counts one local event, not a parallel sweep or a free global
update. The same random-scan rule is repeated at every event.

The primary output is the reduced state on S at a declared time, with the
clock and workspace ignored. It is not postselected on a successful clock
reading. The complete restoration theorem also bounds correlations with any
finite untouched reference R. It therefore supports paid repeat preparations,
not just a correct one-system marginal.

2. THE WEIGHTED LOCAL PROCESS

Assume T>=1; an empty source can be padded with an identity gate. Choose rational
0<delta<=1/2. Define

    b = ceil(log_2[T(1-delta)/delta]),
    K = 2^b,       r=1/K,       a=1/(K+T).

Use T unary clock qubits. Its legal words are

    |t> = |1^t 0^(T-t)>,       0<=t<=T.

Let V_t=U_t...U_1, V_0=I, and |psi_t>=V_t|0^q>. The proposed stationary state is

    Omega = a sum_{t=0}^{T-1} |psi_t><psi_t| tensor |t><t|
            + K a |psi_T><psi_T| tensor |T><T|.                 (1)

The total unfinished-clock weight is

    delta_c = T/(K+T) <= delta.                               (2)

In fact a>=delta/(2T), so the reverse clock probability is not exponentially
small in the ordinary inverse-accuracy parameter. K's bit length is
O(log T + log(1/delta)); it is a dyadic coin, not an ideal real-number oracle.

There are q+3T-1 channel types:

(i) q input-reset channels. If the first clock bit is zero, reset a specified
work qubit to zero. Otherwise leave the work register alone. Dephase the
tested clock bit. On the legal clock, the reset condition is exactly t=0.
The channel acts on two retained qubits.

(ii) T history-edge channels. For edge t, 1<=t<=T, inspect clock bits t-1,t,t+1,
using fixed boundaries c_0=1 and c_{T+1}=0. Dephase the inspected actual bits.
If the left neighbour is one and the right neighbour zero, then:
  - a zero centre is flipped to one and U_t is applied to the work register;
  - a one centre is flipped to zero and U_t^dagger is applied, with probability
    one for t<T and probability r for t=T;
  - the rejected final reverse move leaves the work state unchanged.
Otherwise leave the work register and the classical clock word unchanged.
The support is at most three clock qubits plus two work qubits.

(iii) T individual clock dephasers.

(iv) T-1 clock-cleanup channels. Dephase an adjacent pair and replace pattern
01 by 00. The other three classical patterns are unchanged. These channels
have support two and are inactive on every legal clock word.

Choose M to be the smallest power of two at least 2(q+3T-1). Pad the list with
identity channels to M slots. At each event select a uniform slot using log_2 M
fresh bits. The terminal reverse coin uses b fresh bits and succeeds only on
the all-zero word. No unbounded rejection sampler is used by the clock.

The clock inspections above denote unread local measurements, or equivalently
dephasing followed by classically controlled local operations. They can be
implemented with local temporary registers, controlled gates and discard/reset;
there is no need to expose a measurement record to an observer. At the logical
level these are explicit bounded-dimensional CPTP maps. A physical implementation
must implement or approximate those maps and pay for the discarded records.

Every listed channel fixes (1). At an early edge the two clock weights are both
a. At the last edge the stationary flows agree because Ka*r=a. Resets at t=0
fix the all-zero source input. Clock dephasing fixes the classical history
mixture, and cleanup annihilates every illegal pattern. Thus the average channel
Phi fixes Omega exactly for the exact source gates.

The output is

    Omega_S = (1-delta_c) tau_C + delta_c chi,                 (3)

for an explicitly determined mixture chi of intermediate output states. Therefore
D(Omega_S,tau)<=eta_src+delta_c. This is a full-state bound and consequently
controls every measurement distribution in total variation. It does not assert
that an individual run has a particular macroscopic outcome when tau itself
has phase fluctuations.

3. COMPLETE RESTORATION FROM THE ENTIRE HILBERT SPACE

There are three difficulties: clock coherences, illegal clock words, and
uninitialized work qubits. They are handled separately without assuming a
prepared clock or a spectral-gap oracle.

3.1 Clock coherences

Every channel is covariant under conjugation by each clock Z and commutes with
every individual clock-dephasing channel. A selected explicit dephaser can
therefore be moved through a fixed event sequence without changing its map.
The probability that a particular clock dephaser has not appeared in m events
is at most exp(-m/M). A union bound gives a complete trace-distance error at
most T exp(-m/M) when replacing the channel by one with a fully dephased clock.
This bound holds with an arbitrary untouched reference because it is a convex
mixture bound on channels, not a dimension-dependent state estimate.

3.2 Invalid clocks

For a classical clock word c, define

    W(c) = sum_{i=1}^{T-1} (T-i) 1{c_i c_{i+1}=01}.           (4)

W is zero exactly on the legal unary clock words, and W<=T(T-1)/2. Every
history-edge move preserves every 01 interface and hence preserves W. A cleanup
move at an 01 interface either removes it or moves it one position to the
right, reducing W by at least one. No other channel increases W.

Whenever W>0, at least one effective cleanup channel is selected with probability
at least 1/M per event. Thus the expected time to enter the legal clock space
is at most M T(T-1)/2. In a block of M T(T-1) events the chance of not having
entered is at most 1/2. Repeating blocks yields a geometric tail uniformly over
the initial clock word. Once legal, the clock stays legal. Work states may have
been arbitrarily modified during cleanup; the next argument accepts them all.

3.3 Mixing on the legal clock

For analysis only, conjugate by the history unitary

    V = sum_{t=0}^T V_t tensor |t><t|.                        (5)

This large unitary is not an implemented oracle. The physical operations are
the already specified two-qubit gates and their adjoints. In this frame, all
history-edge channels move only the classical clock. Work resets occur only
at t=0 and reset a single input qubit to zero. Consequently, after every input
qubit has been reset at least once, the entire work register is exactly zero
and decoupled from any reference in this frame.

The clock is a lazy reversible birth–death chain P. For edges before the last,
the two transition probabilities are 1/M. The last forward probability is 1/M
and its reverse is 1/(KM). Its stationary distribution is

    pi_0=...=pi_{T-1}=a,       pi_T=Ka.

Every edge has stationary conductance a/M. For any function f on the clock,

    sum_t pi_t |f_t|^2
       <= (T+1)( |f_0|^2 + sum_{j=1}^T |f_j-f_{j-1}|^2 ).   (6)

To bound failure to reset one specified work qubit, kill the clock trajectory
when its reset channel is selected at t=0. The resulting substochastic matrix
is Q=P-(1/M)|0><0|. Its Dirichlet form is

    <f,(I-Q)f>_pi
      = (a/M)( |f_0|^2 + sum_{j=1}^T |f_j-f_{j-1}|^2 ).     (7)

The padding M makes Q and P nonnegative-spectrum lazy matrices. Equations
(6)-(7) give the explicit killed-chain gap

    kappa = a/[M(T+1)] = 1/[M(T+1)(K+T)].                    (8)

Starting at any clock position, the probability that this work qubit has not
been reset after m legal-clock events is at most

    a^(-1/2) exp(-kappa m).

The ordinary clock mixing bound is at most half this expression: its gap is
at least a/(MT), and the smallest stationary weight is a. A union bound over
the q input qubits therefore gives

    (q+1/2) a^(-1/2) exp(-kappa m)                           (9)

for the full history-state error after entering the legal, dephased clock.

For completeness with a reference, condition on the classical clock path.
Resetting every input qubit is an exact replacement channel on the work
register in frame (5), and it leaves the reference marginal untouched. Replace
the work state by this replacement channel also on the failed paths, paying
their total probability. Ordinary clock mixing then removes its correlation
with the initial clock label and its reference. This proves the same bound
for arbitrary initial work-clock-reference states without a reference-dimension
factor. The earlier dephasing and cleanup comparisons are also complete channel
comparisons, so the entire conclusion is complete.

3.4 An integer event certificate

Let ell_2(x) be the smallest nonnegative integer l with 2^l>=x. For rational
0<epsilon<1, define

    m_D = M ell_2(3T/epsilon),
    m_C = M T(T-1) ell_2(3/epsilon),
    m_L = M(T+1)(K+T) ell_2(3(q+1)(K+T)/epsilon),
    m(epsilon) = m_D+m_C+m_L.                               (10)

The first two estimates follow from the preceding exponential and geometric
tails. For the last estimate, a^(-1/2)<=a^(-1)=K+T and e^(-l)<=2^(-l).
These choices assign at most epsilon/3 to each stage. In particular,

 D((Phi^m tensor id_R)(rho_QR), Omega tensor rho_R)<=epsilon  (11)

for m>=m(epsilon), every initial state, and every finite untouched R. Uniqueness
of the stationary state follows immediately. All integers in (10) are obtained
by exact rational arithmetic and bit-length comparisons; no numerical
eigenvalue or unacquired mixing certificate is used.

The resulting event count is

 O((q+T) T^2 delta^(-1) log[(q+1)T/(delta epsilon)]).         (12)

The source circuit length may already contain a large compilation or acquisition
cost. Formula (12) is not a promise that any Hamiltonian has a small source C.

4. PERTURBATIONS, MAINTENANCE, AND REPEAT PREPARATION

Set m_*=m(1/4). Equation (11) implies that Phi^{m_*} contracts trace distance
between states by a factor at most 1/2. One can see this by decomposing an
arbitrary traceless Hermitian difference into its positive and negative parts
and applying the 1/4 bound to the resulting two states. The complete form of
(11) supplies the analogous replacement bound with a reference.

Suppose that event j is instead a CPTP map Phi_j on the retained register,
with a time-dependent but uniform half-diamond bound

    ||Phi_j-Phi||_diamond/2 <= xi.                           (13)

Telescoping over a block costs at most m_* xi in trace distance. Thus, for any
initial state and every n,

    D(rho_n,Omega) <= 2^(-floor(n/m_*)) + 2 m_* xi.           (14)

This bound is valid at all event times, not only along a subsequence: apply the
block recurrence backwards from the requested time. If a single perturbed map
is repeated and m_*xi<=1/8, its m_*-step contraction coefficient is at most 3/4.
It then has a unique stationary state at distance at most 2m_*xi from Omega.

For the actual output,

 D(rho_{S,n},tau) <= eta_src + delta_c
                     + 2^(-floor(n/m_*)) + 2m_*xi.          (15)

This is a finite precision requirement, not a constant hardware noise threshold.
For a fixed target maintenance error eta_hold, a sufficient event error is
xi<=eta_hold/(2m_*). Persistent time-dependent Markovian faults within (13)
are allowed; arbitrary unmodelled memory-bearing noise is not certified.
Noise on idle registers, resets, random selection, native gates, controls and
physical storage must all be included in xi. Gate errors cannot be counted
only on the currently addressed five qubits while ignoring the rest of the
device during the event.

The process can be interrupted by output extraction or a destructive measurement.
Those operations need not satisfy the small-error budget. After that intervention,
run the complete restoration period again. The untouched-reference statement
then bounds correlation with earlier retained outputs. For L repeat samples a
telescoping joint error bound sums the L preparation errors; requesting total
error eta consequently requires each error budget to scale as eta/L. This is
paid repreparation, not amplification of unknown copies or independent clones.

The same construction gives a time-independent local Lindbladian

    L = Phi-I = (1/M) sum_j (Phi_j^{ideal}-I),                (16)

where the sum includes identity padding. Its semigroup is the compound-Poisson
mixture exp(tL)=exp(-t) sum_s t^s Phi^s/s!. Each local channel has a bounded-size
Stinespring representation, so (16) is a local Lindblad description. A sufficient
normalized time is

    t >= max{2 m(epsilon/2), 8 ln(2/epsilon)}.

This statement specifies a generator, not access to an engineered reservoir.
The explicit digital realization is repeated execution of the same random-scan
channel using the labelled gate/reset/randomness interface. Event durations can
be padded to a common physical duration. A native analog realization would
require its own warranted couplings, rates, noise and calibration.

5. WHY EXACT EQUILIBRIUM DOES NOT FOLLOW

The distinction can be stated without detailed balance, without classical
dynamics, and without restricting the auxiliary dimension.

Let Phi be a channel on physical spins S and finite auxiliary A with stationary
state sigma. Let P be a physical phase projector (tensored with I_A), and B a
physical boundary projector. Suppose that for some Kraus representation,

    [P,K_alpha](I-B)=0 for every alpha.                      (17)

A channel whose Kraus operators each act on at most k physical spins satisfies
this for a magnetization cut P=1{sum Z_i>0} and B=1{|sum Z_i|<=2k}. Arbitrarily
many auxiliary qubits can participate in an operation; condition (17) concerns
its effect on the actual physical order parameter. More general operators
with the same displacement bound also satisfy (17).

Write p=Tr(P sigma), b_sigma=Tr(B sigma), and sigma_P=P sigma P/p. For 0<p<1,

    D(Phi(sigma_P),sigma_P) <= 2 sqrt(b_sigma/p).              (18)

Here is a direct complete proof of the needed form. Let V be a Stinespring
isometry and Delta=(P tensor I_env)V-VP. By (17), Delta=Delta B and ||Delta||<=2.
Set A=VP sqrt(sigma) and C=(P tensor I_env)V sqrt(sigma). Stationarity gives
||A||_2=||C||_2=sqrt(p), while ||A-C||_2<=2 sqrt(b_sigma). Therefore

    ||AA^dagger-CC^dagger||_1
         <= (||A||_2+||C||_2)||A-C||_2 <=4 sqrt(p b_sigma).

Partial trace and division by p yield (18). This argument keeps the coherences
between phase and auxiliary registers; a small physical boundary probability
does not justify deleting those coherences for free.

Since sigma_P is entirely on one side of the phase cut, telescoping (18) gives

 D(Tr_A Phi^m(sigma_P),sigma_S)
       >= 1-p-2m sqrt(b_sigma/p).                            (19)

In particular, to reach physical error epsilon_mix relative to sigma_S,

    m >= (1-p-epsilon_mix)/2 * sqrt(p/b_sigma).               (20)

If [sigma,P]=0, there is a stronger result. Then sigma_P<=sigma/p, and positivity
and stationarity preserve that domination at every event. The operator
E=Phi^dagger(P)-P is supported on B and has norm at most one. Hence the change
in phase probability per event is at most b_sigma/p, proving

    m >= (1-p-epsilon_mix) p/b_sigma.                        (21)

This stronger statement allows quantum dynamics; its extra hypothesis is phase
block diagonality of the joint stationary state, not classical evolution.

For a continuous local Lindbladian with Hamiltonian terms h_l and jumps J_l,
define the paid activity norm R=sum_l ||h_l||+sum_l ||J_l||^2. Let each term
act on at most k physical spins, so h_l, J_l and J_l^dagger J_l all obey the
same phase-boundary condition. Write D_B=I-B and sigma'=D_B sigma D_B. The gentle
truncation bound is ||sigma-sigma'||_1<=2 sqrt(b_sigma). On sigma', locality
gives L(P sigma' P)=P L(sigma')P. Since L(sigma)=0 and ||L||_diamond<=2R,

    ||L(P sigma P)||_1<=8R sqrt(b_sigma).

Contractivity of exp(tL) then yields

    t >= (1-p-epsilon_mix)p/[4R sqrt(b_sigma)].               (22)

If [sigma,P]=0, the supported phase-current operator instead gives
t>=(1-p-epsilon_mix)p/[2R b_sigma]. This derivation uses the completely positive
semigroup directly, not a generally unjustified positive Euler step I+dt L.

6. A FULLY COSTED SEPARATION FAMILY

Take odd N and the supplied collective Hamiltonian

    H_N=-(sum_i Z_i)^2/(2N),       beta=2.                   (23)

Its canonical state pi_N is an input-model instance of the accepted collective
preparation construction. It also has a direct classical implementation using
the N+1 magnetization weights and uniform combinatorial unranking. Active
preparation to error eta uses polynomial work in N and log(1/eta), including
the finite probability arithmetic and bounded-error random-bit generation.
No laboratory learning or Gibbs oracle is assumed for this supplied model.

Let P=1{M_z>0}. Since N is odd, Tr(P pi_N)=1/2. For B=1{|M_z|<=2k}, a simple
bound using the two fully ordered configurations in the denominator is

    b_N=Tr(B pi_N)
       <= 2^(N-1) exp(-N+4k^2/N).                           (24)

For fixed k, and indeed k=o(N), this is exponentially small. It is a deliberately
conservative finite-N bound, not an optimal critical temperature or block-size
threshold.

If any stationary extension sigma has exactly this physical marginal, then
p=1/2 and b_sigma=b_N regardless of auxiliary dimension or phase coherences.
For physical mixing error 1/4, (20) gives

    m >= 1/[8 sqrt(2 b_N)].                                 (25)

For a phase-block-diagonal stationary extension, (21) gives m>=1/(8b_N).
These lower bounds concern local events. Multiplying all rates or calling a
whole circuit one event changes the charged activity and does not evade them.

The package encloses the exponential in (24) by exact rational Taylor bounds,
reciprocal bounds, outward dyadic rounding and repeated squaring. The following
integer lower bounds are conservative consequences, not floating estimates:

The full exact integers and rational enclosures are in BOTTLENECK_CERTIFICATES.
The table uses k=5, allowing an event to affect any five physical spins. No
geometric locality, bath detailed balance, or classical auxiliary restriction
is needed. The example is a counterexample to a universal implication; the
positive construction above covers every supplied circuit, not only this model.

7. THE PRECISION OBSTRUCTION WITHOUT A JOINT MIXING ASSUMPTION

Suppose a fixed local channel on S and arbitrary finite A promises

    D(Tr_A Phi^m(rho),pi_N)<=delta_out for every initial rho,  (26)

at some m, with 0<delta_out<=1/8. The controller need not have a unique
stationary state or converge jointly. Every finite-dimensional CPTP map has
at least one stationary state sigma. Applying (26) to sigma gives

    p >=1/2-delta_out,       b_sigma<=b_N+delta_out.

Apply (26) also to sigma_P. Its initial phase probability is one; its output
phase probability is at most 1/2+delta_out. Combining this required change with
(18) proves

    m >= (1/2-delta_out)^(3/2) / [2 sqrt(b_N+delta_out)],

or, equivalently,

    b_N+delta_out >= (1/2-delta_out)^3/(4m^2)
                   >= 27/(2048 m^2).                        (27)

If the chosen stationary extension commutes with P, the stronger consequence is

    b_N+delta_out >= (1/2-delta_out)^2/m >=9/(64m).           (28)

Thus a controller with an arbitrary stationary extension cannot provide a
universal precision-efficient shortcut merely by hiding a clock, retaining
coherences, or avoiding convergence of its internal state. It must change the
physical displacement contract, pay enough activity, spend sufficient output
error on the boundary, or give up restoration from every initial condition.

For delta_out=exp(-cN), any fixed positive c, equations (24) and (27) force
exponentially many local events. The active source circuit still has size
polynomial in N because log(1/delta_out)=O(N). Consequently no universal
conversion polynomial in circuit size and log(1/delta_out) exists with these
restoration and locality contracts. At inverse-polynomial accuracy, (12)
provides a polynomial construction. This is the claimed precision separation.
The remaining gap between inverse-square-root and inverse-linear accuracy
dependence with coherent auxiliaries is not claimed solved.

No exact finite-alphabet preparation of irrational Gibbs weights is assumed in
this separation. Choose a finite source circuit within delta_out/10 of pi_N.
A proposed conversion with comparable error to that circuit's exact output
would still approximate pi_N to exponentially small error by the triangle
inequality, and (27) would still apply.

A zero-boundary corollary makes the mechanism particularly transparent. Replace
pi_N by the exact GHZ state (|0^N>+|1^N>)/sqrt(2), and take N>2k. Its phase
weights are 1/2 and b_N=0. Equation (27) then directly requires
delta_out>=27/(2048m^2), although an exact finite-alphabet source circuit has
only O(N) gates. An exactly restoring local process is impossible even with
arbitrary finite auxiliary registers. Exact-local GHZ stabilization obstructions
are established in the quasi-local control literature [9]; the quantitative
approximate-output statement here follows from the auxiliary-uniform proof.
The small Bell audit does not meet N>2k: its gate may act on both output qubits.

8. BROAD DEDUCTIONS AND BOUNDARIES

8.1 Computational preparation and equilibration are different resources.

The accepted all-sector thermal compiler supplies an active source under its
labelled-gate/reset/randomness assumptions. It does not imply that the original
Hamiltonian plus a local equilibrium-preserving reservoir mixes rapidly. The
same state family demonstrates this distinction with all activity counted.

8.2 Approximate restoration is an ordinary computational resource.

Given a supplied circuit, the fixed local process is constructible with explicit
polynomial work at inverse-polynomial accuracy. Conversely, a supplied finite
list of constant-size local channels with a polynomial restoration time is
implemented by applying those channels, including their synthesis and reset
costs. This is the operational circuit/open-process equivalence under the
declared precision contract. The established universality result [1] is credited;
the present bounds specify the physical output and initialization conditions.

8.3 Full-state accuracy does not preserve rare-event kinetics.

Equation (3) spends at most delta_c probability on intermediate computation.
A boundary whose exact Gibbs probability is exp(-cN) may therefore acquire
inverse-polynomial mass while the entire output state remains close in trace
distance. Every bounded measurement probability remains accurate additively.
The relative probability of an exponentially rare boundary, and the associated
mixing obstruction, need not be accurate. This is an actual quantitative reason
that even a global state certificate does not by itself certify metastability.

For the ideal full-rank canonical target tau_C=rho_beta(H), relative-entropy
convexity gives an additional free-energy statement for (3):

    F_H(Omega_S)-F_H(rho_beta)
       <= delta_c [osc(H)+log(dim S)/beta].                  (29)

Indeed beta times the left side is D(Omega_S||rho_beta), which is at most
delta_c D(chi||rho_beta), and the latter divergence is bounded by
beta osc(H)+log(dim S). Thus the approximate output can also have small
free-energy density regret while its boundary occupation is qualitatively
different. No available-work claim follows from (29).

8.4 Hard windows require an explicit distinction.

The source may be the predecessor's hard-window minimum-free-energy state.
The new output approximates it in trace distance, but intermediate circuit
states can leave the window. Their occupation is part of delta_c. Strict zero
window violation and finite constrained relative entropy are not inherited
from approximate state closeness. In particular, a small weight outside the
support of a hard-window state gives infinite relative entropy to that state.
Equation (29) was stated for the full canonical state for this reason.

8.5 Restoring a state is not preserving unknown information.

The device intentionally erases its initial work state. It does not store an
unknown quantum input or clone an unavailable source. The reference contract
ensures independent reset/preparation after the paid delay. Preparing a useful
encoded information state or protecting an unknown logical state is a different
task and requires its own interface and noise analysis.

8.6 The precision separation is not a P=NP result.

All general positive results start with an acquired preparation circuit. If
constructing that circuit for a generic Hamiltonian is hard, this conversion
inherits that cost. It neither solves generic Gibbs preparation nor computes
generic partition functions or ground energies in polynomial time.

9. FULL RESOURCE ACCOUNTING

Classical compilation: read and validate the entire source circuit and its
gate descriptions; form O(q+T) local-instrument descriptions; compute K, M and
(10) using exact integer arithmetic. Source synthesis, reversible classical
control, coherent probability generation, and model acquisition remain in
the source ledger. No efficient oracle is introduced in their place.

Retained quantum storage: q work qubits, T unary clock qubits, plus bounded
temporary environment storage per local instrument. Classical program and
selector storage contain the full source gate list and the b-bit coin rule.
The clock need not be initialized. Resetting temporary registers and producing
independent bits are continuing resources.

One event: log_2 M independent selector bits, at most b additional coin bits,
one bounded-size local channel, and paid addressing/routing and storage during
the event. A safe implementation can scan the entire finite source table to
locate the selected instruction; random-access memory is not an unpriced
assumption. This still gives polynomial classical work. A known gate may be
replaced by a certified native synthesis, with its adjoint similarly charged.

If an event needs at most g native gates, u reset/discard operations, random-bit
cost C_rand per bit, routing/control cost C_ctl and duration tau_event, then a
run of m events has the explicit ledger

    C_source + C_compile
    + m [g C_gate + u C_reset + (log_2 M+b) C_rand + C_ctl]
    + C_storage(q+T, m tau_event).                          (30)

Prices in (30) may be monetary, energy, duration or an appropriate resource
vector; incompatible resources are not silently summed as a physical law.
Actual hardware parameters were not supplied, so no numerical physical cost
or practical deployment claim is made. The formula states what must be paid.
Maintaining the state for an additional hold interval costs additional events,
power and storage. The infinite-time stationary theorem is not infinite free
holding from a finite battery. Constant per-gate physical error does not
automatically satisfy (13) as the computation grows.

For the earlier collective models, eta_src includes the accepted model mismatch
and finite logical approximation costs. The sharp thermal model modulus and
the supplied common-coefficient and size-normalization premises are unchanged.
The approximately 9.36e29-preparation unknown-source learning schedule is still
a costed sufficient construction, not an experiment performed or practical
commissioning. The labelled-gate interface is stronger than the earlier
collective-plus-fixed-tag interface; its sector and heralding limits remain.

10. DELIVERED CONSTRUCTION AND VERIFICATION

local_stabilizer.py emits the finite local-instrument program from a supplied
one/two-qubit circuit. Its resource certificate uses exact rational arithmetic
and integer comparisons. It does not emit a native hardware pulse sequence.
Its dense matrix helpers are only small-system audit tools, and their
exponential storage cost is not the cost of the symbolic compiler.

run_audits.py constructs two explicit sources and evaluates the resulting maps.
All matrix diagnostics are binary64. The thermal source's supplied matrix gates
have a measured unitarity residual about 1.03e-15; the mathematical theorem
requires exact unitaries or a separately charged implementation error. The
floating diagnostics do not replace that contract.

The structural audit exhausts all 8,190 clock words of lengths 1 through 12,
90,114 edge updates and 20,481 effective cleanup updates. It checks legal-space
invariance and the potential decrease independently of the work circuit.

The full channel audit uses a two-gate Bell-state circuit with two work and two
clock qubits. It expands the complete 256-by-256 superoperator, including illegal
clock space and coherences. Maximum trace-preservation residual was 6.32e-16;
the stationary-history residual was 3.48e-18. There was one unit eigenvalue at
the stated numerical tolerance; the second eigenvalue modulus was about
0.99936017. The exact worst-case certificate gives 21,520 events for complete
mixing error 1/16. The tested initial states included all-zero, all-one,
invalid-clock, coherent valid/invalid-clock, random entangled work-clock and
maximally mixed inputs. Their largest measured joint error at that event count
was about 7.48e-7. A maximally entangled untouched reference was also checked.
Its measured joint replacement error at the same time was about 8.22e-7.

The perturbation audit mixes each nominal Bell-process event with a single-qubit
reset of weight 1e-6. With m_*=18,160, (14) bounds the stationary joint error by
0.03632. The observed binary64 stationary error was about 0.00131158. This is
a check of the perturbation mechanism, not a physical noise calibration.

The thermal audit uses the rational two-qubit model

    H=-Z0 Z1 -(7/10)X0 -(2/5)X1 -(1/5)Y0,     beta=3/2.

It builds a four-gate purification circuit on four work qubits, then adds four
clock qubits. The source state differs from a direct matrix exponential by
about 3.46e-16. The stationary-history residual is about 2.43e-16. The physical
stationary output is 0.03454740294 from the canonical state, below its exact
clock duty bound 1/17. The complete mixing certificate is 154,880 local events
for error 1/16. For a 0.001 maintenance-error budget, the sufficient per-event
half-diamond error is 1/264,576,000, approximately 3.78e-9. These conservative
numbers expose the price of the guarantee even in a small fixture.

The finite fixtures validate the delivered map and arithmetic; they do not
supply the theorem's broad scope. That scope follows from Sections 2–7.
The exact-rational bottleneck enclosures are separate from binary64 diagnostics.
