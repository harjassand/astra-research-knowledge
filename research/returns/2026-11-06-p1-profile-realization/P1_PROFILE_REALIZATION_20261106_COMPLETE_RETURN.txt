\[

\bigoplus_jT_j(M_a)

For \(A\in\mathcal A\), with block projection \(\pi_j\), define
\[
\tau_j(A)=\frac1a\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(\Omega_j\pi_j(A)\right),

\ell_c(A)=\sum_jc_j\tau_j(A).
\]

These functionals are generally **nontracial**. The order of matrix products must therefore be retained.

The manuscript proves the following equivalence:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\text{Some finite-dimensional CPTP channel realizes }\Pi_f^{(c)}
\\
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\ell_c\text{ is positive and }
\mathcal N_c=\{A:\ell_c(A^*A)=0\}
\text{ is a two-sided ideal of }\mathcal A.
\end{aligned}}
\]

An equivalent density-matrix formulation is particularly informative. Write
\[
\ell_c(A)=\frac1a\operatorname{Tr}(Z_cA),
\qquad Z_c\in\mathcal A.
\]
Then realization holds exactly when
\[
Z_c\ge0
\quad\text{and}\quad
p_c=\operatorname{supp}Z_c
\text{ is central in }\mathcal A.
\]

Here \(p_c\) is the **actual support projection**. Replacing it with its central carrier would erase the obstruction.

### Why positivity alone is insufficient

Positivity permits a GNS representation with a cyclic state. A normalized physical channel supplies an additional property: its output state is faithful on its actual output support.

That difference becomes decisive when a nonzero polynomial \(P\) satisfies
\[
\ell_c(P^*P)=0
\]
but right multiplication by an input-generated operator produces
\[
\ell_c\!\left((PT(H))^*PT(H)\right)>0.
\]

In a proposed physical realization, faithfulness would force \(P\) to represent the zero operator. Then \(PT(H)\) would also be zero, contradicting the second equality.

This supplies a finite, directly checkable certificate of physical impossibility. It also identifies precisely why a positivity-only construction stops too early.

### Why the condition is sufficient

When \(Z_c\ge0\) and its support \(p_c\) is central, define on that support
\[
\Gamma(X)=Z_c^{1/2}T(X)Z_c^{1/2},
\qquad
T(X)=\bigoplus_jT_j(X).
\]

Complete positivity follows from composition and conjugation. Trace preservation follows from the original channels:
\[
\operatorname{Tr}\Gamma(X)
=
a\ell_c(T(X))
=
\sum_jc_j\operatorname{Tr}\Phi_j(X)
=
\operatorname{Tr}X.
\]

Centrality ensures that compression to \(p_c\) is the appropriate algebra quotient. The normalized channel and its faithful state then reproduce the required ordered moments, and hence the entire target profile.

The necessity argument goes in the reverse direction: a hypothetical realizing channel induces an input-preserving algebra homomorphism, and its kernel must be exactly the null space above. Thus the condition is forced by physical realization.

The underlying finite-algebra facts are standard. The construction developed here connects them to complete profile realization, provides both kinds of obstruction, and removes irrational matrix operations from the executable compiler.
