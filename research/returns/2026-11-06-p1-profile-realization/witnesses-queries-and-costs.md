# Distinguishing states, exact queries and paid implementation

The [available prefix](P1_PROFILE_REALIZATION_20261106_RETURN.txt) claims a polynomial construction of one rational faithful pair, a finite untouched reference and one comparison orientation separating two unequal complete maximal profiles for **every** nonaffine operator-convex `f`. The compiler needs no evaluations of `f`. The same pair is separable across the input/reference cut and has eigenvalue bounds `I/(2ar)≤ρ,σ≤3I/(2ar)`.

The rational positive certificate `μ` gives divergence gap at least `f''(1)μ/2`, rather than an absolute gap independent of generator scale. It may be exponentially small. A distinguishing ordered word has length at most `D−1` in the generated joint algebra; finite matrix generation and weighted-automata equivalence methods are credited. The proposed addition converts that word into normalized positive faithful separable inputs with a common controlled gap. This does not retroactively assign a polynomial witness theorem to corrected N1698.

The finite-family procedure computes all affine profile relations. If the constant-plus-moment matrix has rank `p`, the affine span has dimension `p−1` and `p−1` common scalar queries determine every profile there. Coefficients apply across faithful pairs, finite references and nonaffine operator-convex generators. The minimality is for the **whole affine span**, including an open mixture simplex, not the number of tests to label a finite list. Reconstructing a target requires exact values and a supplied span-membership promise; the algorithm does not establish that promise from arbitrary noisy data.

The original [maximal-divergence/integral-representation machinery](https://real.mtak.hu/166018/1/1604.03089.pdf) and [weighted-automata equivalence methods](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/james.worrell/fossacs12-journal.pdf) are source-credited foundations. The proof uses the full operator-convex representation on `(0,∞)`, without requiring a finite value at zero. These are supplied source comparisons, not a fresh Capital literature audit; the return's later comparison/status discussion is truncated.
