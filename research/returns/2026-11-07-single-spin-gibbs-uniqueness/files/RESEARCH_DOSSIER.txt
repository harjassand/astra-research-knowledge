4. ARBITRARY PHYSICAL STATES AND FULL LOCAL DLR

4.1 Conditioned group mixing

Existence is not left implicit: finite-alphabet finite-range physical Gibbs kernels are continuous and strictly positive. Compact subsequential limits of increasing finite-volume laws satisfy each fixed local DLR identity once its boundary has receded. Thus at least one physical Gibbs state exists, also for bounded inhomogeneous fields. Its projection supplies the required nonempty canonical class.

Fix all spins after summing multiplicities. Each group factor f_v=1+a_beta F_v lies in [1-u,1+u]. Only edges sharing a vertex can influence an edge conditional, and there are at most six. With R=(1+u)/(1-u), changing one neighboring edge changes the unnormalized density by a ratio in [R^-1,R]. The elementary likelihood-ratio TV bound is (R-1)/(R+1)=u. Therefore the Dobrushin row sum is

  alpha=6u<0.0006.                                                    (4.1)

Influence paths reaching distance d have total weight <=alpha^d/(1-alpha). This gives a unique conditioned group law Q_s^g, uniform free/fixed boundary convergence, exponential conditional covariance and exponential dependence on distant spins. These bounds are independent of group size and the complete spin configuration.

4.2 Projection of EVERY physical Gibbs state

Let mu be any physical Gibbs state, without TI or extremality. Conditional on all spins its group law satisfies the conditioned group DLR and hence equals Q_s^g almost surely. For a finite change s to s' in Lambda, define the positive local ratio

  L_{s,s'}(g)=product_{v in Lambda}f_v(g,s')/f_v(g,s).

Disintegration of the exact physical spin-change identity gives the spin marginal nu the conditional odds

  nu(s'|outside)/nu(s|outside)

uniformly for the fixed change region and all complete spins. The ratios define a quasilocal spin specification directly. Infinite formal tilings never enter this passage.

4.4 Why separate conditionals really imply local spatial DLR here

Encode an object by its tile labels, vacuum otherwise. The proof specification has nearest-neighbor hard color matching, occupied-tile factor b, marked-minus factor r, Ising bonds and fields. The proof energies -log b and -log r are distinct from the integer physical energies.

A finite edit preserves the finite-component sector. For any admissible replacement remove all old objects meeting the edit, change its spins, then insert all final objects meeting it. These ratios identify the finite-region conditional among every compatible filling, including split/join edits. The limit follows, is independent of exhaustion, and is TI. The finite covariance swap also yields (5.7) in this selected state. QED.

Physical version: freely sum all group edges incident to V, include site factors centered in V, omit other site factors, and use plus Ising exterior. The exact spin marginal is rho_V's. These are legitimate finite positive boundary models; extra stored-edge coordinates occupy only a fixed collar. Every weak limit is physical Gibbs. Auxiliary boundary comparison and quasilocal truncation of conditional physical observables transfer (6.1) to a computable uniform exponential rate for the full physical selected limit.

This supplies a TOTAL algorithm on every input: choose a radius from the explicit error bound, enumerate the finite positive physical model, and evaluate to certified precision. It selects the plus lift on nonhalting inputs, so it cannot decide uniqueness. With fixed support geometry and fixed beta, brute force costs q^{O(R^2)} configurations, R=O(log(1/epsilon)) up to observable-dependent constants; coefficient evaluation and compilation also cost resources. The alphabet q can be enormous. No matched practical advantage or rapid-dynamics claim follows.

The boundary has been SPECIFIED. Conditioning on rare opposite boundaries can completely invalidate the soup domination. Neither arbitrary-boundary weak mixing, strong spatial mixing, a Glauber spectral gap, nor a uniform minus-boundary rate is proved by (6.1).

7. SMOOTH PRESSURE AND THE COMPUTABLE RIGHT JET

THEOREM C (PROVED, including for M0 itself).
For a halting model and its h_*>0, pressure is C-infinity in the uniform dimensionless field h throughout (-h_*,infinity), with (1.1). Pressure at zero is not differentiable on nonhalting inputs. Nevertheless all one-sided right derivatives at zero exist and are computable uniformly from the input and the derivative order.

7.1 Uniform inhomogeneous control is the additional hypothesis

An isolated infinite-volume clustering estimate would not suffice for the response argument. Here Section 5 independently proves uniqueness and the SAME all-local covariance bound for every bounded inhomogeneous field profile H with inf_x H_x>-h_*. Compare to the homogeneous positive field inf_x(H_x+h_*). All object and auxiliary bounds are unchanged. On any compact interval of uniform fields inside (-h_*,infinity), every finite-support interpolation between two of these fields lies in this class. This is the needed uniformity, not finite-volume SSM.

For finite Lambda, the unique state at H_x=h+t1_Lambda(x) is exactly mu_h tilted by exp[t sum_{x in Lambda}s_x]; a finite perturbation of the DLR interaction gives this identity. Differentiation therefore gives

  d/dt mu_{Lambda,t}(F)=sum_{x in Lambda}Cov_{mu_{Lambda,t}}(F,s_x).   (7.1)

As Lambda exhausts, every local limit at fixed t is mu_{h+t}: the locally limiting DLR equations pass by finite-range continuity, and uniqueness identifies the limit. Compactness gives local convergence uniformly in t on compact subintervals (otherwise take a convergent t-subsequence). The uniform covariance tail justifies dominated integration. Thus

  mu_{h+epsilon}(F)-mu_h(F)
    =integral_0^epsilon sum_x Cov_{mu_{h+t}}(F,s_x)dt.                 (7.2)

Local moments are continuous by the same uniqueness/compactness argument, and their covariance tails are uniformly summable. Hence local expectations are C^1. The pressure derivative equals the unique TI magnetization by the finite-range full-shift variational principle, so p''(h)=sum_x Cov(s_0,s_x). Formula (5.7) and

  sum_{x in Z^2}2^-||x||=1+8 sum_{m>=1}m2^-m=17

give |p''|<=68; convexity gives nonnegativity.

7.2 All cumulants: the missing higher-order proof

For n>=2 spin variables at x1,...,xn, choose a minimum spanning tree with infinity-distance edge weights. Let L be its largest edge. Removing it partitions the index set into I,J. Every cross-distance is >=L, since a shorter cross-edge would reduce the tree weight, and L>=diam{x_i}/(n-1).

Compare the moment-cumulant polynomial with that of the product of the I and J marginal laws, whose mixed cumulant vanishes. For any block crossing the cut its moment changes by the covariance of two bounded spin products, at most 4n^2 2^-L by (5.7). Products of block moments telescope. For partition pi the cumulant coefficient is (|pi|-1)!, with at most |pi| block errors. Therefore the explicit uniform bound

  |kappa_n(s_x1,...,s_xn)|<=C_n 2^{-diam{x_i}/(n-1)},
  C_n=4n^2 sum_{partitions pi of {1,...,n}}|pi|!                      (7.3)

holds for all allowed inhomogeneous fields. If diameter is zero use the trivial bounded-variable cumulant bound; repeated sites otherwise cannot lie across a positive-distance cut.

Anchoring x1=0, this is absolutely summable over every fixed number of relative coordinates. Tuples with maximum radius m number at most (2m+1)^{2(n-1)}, dominated by the geometric factor in (7.3). A computable tail bound is

  T_n(R)=C_n sum_{m>=R}(2m+1)^{2(n-1)}2^{-m/(n-1)}.                  (7.4)

Finite exponential tilts differentiate a cumulant by adding one spin and summing its position in Lambda. All derivatives of f_Lambda(t)=mu_{Lambda,t}(s_0) are thus finite sums of higher cumulants. Uniform local convergence of mixed-field moments and the fixed-order tails (7.4) give uniform convergence of each derivative on compact t-intervals. Repeated use of the fundamental theorem of calculus proves f=mu_{h+t}(s_0) is C-infinity, with precisely the sums (1.1). This also justifies all sum/integral/differentiation interchanges. No factorial growth bound in n has been proved, so analyticity does not follow.

This is a classical correlation-to-smoothness method. The exact predecessor is Lebowitz (1972), CMP 28, Section I equations (1.12)–(1.13), Section II equations (2.4a,b) and the smoothness argument on pp. 317–318. Its FKG/finite-volume hypotheses do not directly match the target; our inhomogeneous infinite-volume tilt argument supplies the required replacement bridge. The method is not a novelty claim.

7.3 Right jet on EVERY input

The selected-box proof is uniform for all nonnegative fields, including nonnegative inhomogeneous profiles. At h>0 every input has full uniqueness by the comparison argument, even without a marked component. The selected local laws converge continuously to the plus-selected law as h decreases to zero, by (6.1) uniformly in h. Uniform cumulant tails extend every derivative sum continuously to zero from the right. Repeated integration gives every right derivative p^(n)(0+), including on nonhalting inputs. Compute it by truncating (7.4) and computing finitely many selected local moments with (6.1). This is a TOTAL uniform computation in the input, n and precision. It says nothing about convergence of the Taylor series.

For nonhalting, p(h)=constant+p_Ising,K(h), because all object weights are spin-independent. Its slopes at zero are -m_K and +m_K, m_K>0.99. For halting the two slopes coincide and exceed 0.99. Thus differentiability, and also C-infinity near zero, are equivalent to halting within this family. The right slope alone has no computable separation gap from the nonhalting right slope; the LEFT slope has a fixed sign gap and is not uniformly computable.


8. STABILITY UNDER SMALL ARBITRARY SPIN-ONLY PERTURBATIONS

THEOREM D (PROVED).
Add to the exponent h sum_xs_x+sum_X Psi_X(s_X), with finite-range real spin potentials, possibly multibody, nonferromagnetic and spatially inhomogeneous. Put

  d_X=osc(Psi_X), delta=sup_x sum_{X contains x}d_X,
  p_X=1-exp(-d_X),
  B_Psi=2 sup_x sum_{X:x in X^+}|X^+|p_X2^{diam X^+}.

If h+h_*>delta/2 and B0+B_Psi<1, where B0 is the base four-soup constant, the full physical model has exactly one Gibbs state and all local physical observables cluster exponentially. Physical energy perturbations W_X correspond to Psi_X=-beta W_X. This theorem covers SPINS ONLY; group/multiplicity perturbations are not covered.

Proof. The spin odds change by at least exp(2h-delta); comparison is therefore to the positive Ising field h+h_*-delta/2. The base physical conditional group law and canonical object kernel given spins are unchanged.

For each X write exactly

  exp(Psi_X)=exp(min Psi_X)[1+q_X(s_X)],
  q_X=exp(Psi_X-min Psi_X)-1>=0.

Introduce an activation eta_X with conditional probability q_X/(1+q_X)<=p_X. Conditional on spins these variables are independent and independent of the original object gas. In a finite local update, their Bernoulli denominators cancel the original exp(Psi_X); local augmented weights contain q_X only when active and the constant one when inactive. This cancellation, or a remove/change/reinsert proof, is essential because q_X can be zero; a globally positive Radon–Nikodym derivative of one activation kernel against another cannot be assumed.

Fresh uniforms independent of the entire original coupling produce eta_X and independent Bernoulli(p_X) upper variables. Two replicas therefore add TWO independent activation soups to the original four. Include whole active supports in the bad hypercomponents, even if X is disconnected. If B=B0+B_Psi<1 the same weighted chain sum makes these hypercomponents finite. Swap spins, whole occupied objects, and active factors in components meeting A. An active support cannot cross the swapped set; a crossing inactive support has factor one. Every product Gibbs weight is invariant, including support zeros. Countable finite-event partitioning justifies the random swap. Thus uniqueness and

  |Cov(f,g)|<=2||f||||g|| |A||C| B/(1-B)2^-dist(A,C)

follow. The unchanged conditioned auxiliary Dobrushin law transfers clustering to all physical observables. QED.

Explicit range threshold: if diam X<=R, let

  v_R=min{5(R+1)^2,(R+3)^2}, C_R=10v_R2^{R+2}.

Since p_X<=d_X, |X^+|<=v_R, and an inflated support through x contains a site of the five-site neighborhood of x, B_Psi<=C_R delta. It suffices that

  delta<min{h+h_*,1/(6C_R)},                                        (8.1)

which gives B<5/6 and covariance prefactor <=10. At h=0 a finite certificate supplies an effective positive radius for every FIXED R. This is compatible with the absence of a family-wide radius for general physical perturbations in Section 10.


9. COMPUTABILITY: WHAT IS AND IS NOT OBSTRUCTED

9.1 Every unique computable specification has a computable valid modulus

THEOREM E (PROVED; elementary effective compactness).
For any computable finite-alphabet, finite-range, finite-energy specification on Z^d, under the promise of uniqueness among ALL Gibbs states, its unique measure and an all-boundary finite-volume convergence modulus are uniformly computable. No clustering assumption is needed.

Proof. For finite A inside Lambda_n let D_n(A) be the maximum TV distance of its finite-volume A-marginals over all assignments on the finite interaction collar. These finitely many positive normalized sums are computable. If D_n(A) did not tend to zero under uniqueness, take separating boundary pairs along a subsequence and pass to compact weak limits. Local continuous DLR kernels pass to both limits, giving two different Gibbs marginals, a contradiction.

Search n and numerical precision until a strict certificate D_n(A)<epsilon is obtained. This terminates under uniqueness. Every infinite Gibbs marginal is a mixture of these boundary marginals. Every larger-volume marginal is also a mixture, by conditioning on the collar of Lambda_n. Hence the certificate is a VALID modulus for all larger boxes and all boundaries. A fixed boundary marginal computed accurately enough approximates the unique law. QED.

For a fixed unique model the algorithm is total in A,epsilon. As a procedure on all input models it is partial and may diverge on coexistence inputs. 'No supplied uniqueness proof' does not change its termination on unique inputs. Exact minimal onset indices may present equality issues; existence of a computable VALID upper modulus is the claim.

9.2 The correct no-total-bound theorem

In the natural physical boxes freely sum all incident group edges, omit outside site factors, and fix minus Ising spins. Their spin law is exactly exp(-H_Ising,V^-)*Xi_V(s), normalized. On nonhalting inputs Xi_V is constant and every box is bare minus-boundary Ising, with mean at the origin <-0.99. On halting inputs all weak limits are physical Gibbs, so full uniqueness forces these boxes to converge to mean >0.99.

Suppose a TOTAL recursive N(M) guaranteed accuracy 1/4 for every halting input, with any behavior on nonhalting inputs. Compute the finite mean at N(M) with error 1/8. It is >0.615 on a halting input and <-0.865 on a nonhalting input, so its sign decides halting. Contradiction. The same argument applies to a total uniform all-boundary bound or to a total bound by description length. For unbiased/free-spin boxes the nonhalting finite mean is exactly zero and the halting limit is positive, giving the analogous contradiction. None of this applies to the specified selected-plus sequence, which already has the total bound (6.1).

9.3 Arithmetic classifications

For exact finite tables, all-Gibbs uniqueness is equivalent to

  for every finite support A_j and every k, there exists n:
      D_n(A_j)<2^-k.

Attach a finite rational-enclosure precision witness to each strict inequality to obtain a decidable matrix. Thus general uniqueness has a Pi^0_2 UPPER BOUND on a valid effective class; general completeness is unproved. All finite supports matter: single-site equality need not determine a general Gibbs measure.

On the MACHINE-INDEXED family of Theorem A or M0, uniqueness equals halting, hence is Sigma^0_1-complete; coexistence is Pi^0_1-complete. The robust magnetization gap also makes a single-site boundary-diameter threshold a c.e. certificate on this family. The full-clustering-versus-coexistence promise, and smooth-versus-cusp pressure promise, have the same classification. This is not a claim that every table satisfying uniqueness is c.e. recognizable.

9.4 Pressure values are computable

For an L-by-L free PHYSICAL box, include supports wholly inside. When tiling a larger square by such blocks, each block has at most 2L crossing site factors of norm one and 2L crossing mismatch factors of norm twenty. All crossing terms are nonnegative. Thus for p_L=L^-2 log Z_L,

  p_L-42 beta/L <=p_beta<=p_L.                                    (9.1)

Uniform field terms are on-site and introduce no crossing cost. This directly computes pressure in BOTH branches; no uniqueness decision is required. It also matches Gayral–Sablik–Taati Proposition 14 for computable full-shift potentials.

The finite-range full-shift variational principle gives

  p'(0-)=min{mu(s_0):mu TI Gibbs},
  p'(0+)=max{mu(s_0):mu TI Gibbs}.                                 (9.2)

To see the directional formula, write pressure as the supremum of entropy-minus-energy plus h*magnetization over compact TI measures. An equilibrium state at zero gives one inequality. Maximizers at h tending to zero have equilibrium subsequential limits by entropy upper semicontinuity, giving the reverse inequality. Gibbs/equilibrium equivalence applies to the finite-energy full shift. Therefore p'(0-) has a fixed negative/positive gap encoding halting and is not uniformly computable; (6.1),(7.4) compute p(0) and the entire RIGHT smooth jet uniformly. A computable convex function can have an undecidable differentiability property.

9.5 The independent architecture-free construction and its limit

Set h_M=0 if M never halts and h_M=2^{-(T+2)} if its first halt is at time T. A finite program computes this real: for accuracy 2^-n simulate n steps, return the dyadic if a halt was found and zero otherwise. Any later positive value is below the error tolerance. There is no noncomputable oracle.

Use ordinary square-lattice Ising with J=10, exponent field h_M and beta=1; physical potentials may be written 10(1-s_xs_y) and h_M(1-s_x), in [0,20]. If h_M>0, standard positive-field Ising gives all-Gibbs uniqueness and exponential local clustering. If h_M=0, the elementary K=20 bare-Ising contour bound already checked in Section 5 gives plus/minus coexistence and mixture correlation >0.99. Projection/lifting are identity. Lebowitz–Penrose (1968), Section VII, equations (90)–(93), supplies finite-range nonzero-field Ursell exponential decay; finite Walsh expansion extends it to all bounded local observables. The initial independent note used J=1 and the exact Ising magnetization formula; choosing J=10 here avoids needing that extra input.

Every coefficient VALUE is rational, but no compiler can output its exact numerator/denominator uniformly: testing the numerator for zero would decide halting. This alternative proves the phenomenon for computable-real NAMES, not for exact integer/rational tables. The representation difference is the decisive weakness. Its all-state uniqueness proof genuinely uses standard Ising machinery rather than the new Fourier/object architecture.


10. STRONGEST OBSTRUCTIONS AND COUNTEREXAMPLES

10.1 Exact small physical perturbations restore coexistence

THEOREM F (PROVED).
There is no positive real-potential stability radius valid for ALL compiled halting models, even with the perturbation restricted to the original physical site-factor supports. For every epsilon>0 a halting instance has an epsilon-close real local perturbation with physical coexistence. This does not disprove a neighborhood for each fixed model, and says nothing about a topology allowing only integer-valued perturbations.

Proof for M0. Let t be its tile count, m the marked count, and a its beta=1 parameter. Write U(g)=sum_unmarked Chi_z, V(g)=sum_marked Chi_z. The two summed factors are f_+=1+a(U+V), f_-=1+aU, both in [1-u,1+u]. Put f_sym=(f_++f_-)/2 and add to each microscopic site energy the b_v-independent perturbation

  Delta(s,g)=log[f_s(g)/f_sym(g)].                                  (10.1)

The multiplicity sum becomes EXACTLY C f_sym, independent of s. Its group model remains Dobrushin. Hence both zero-field K=20 Ising phases lift to physical Gibbs states. This is an exact restoration of coexistence, not an inference from a tiny lower bias.

The logarithm is 1/(1-u)-Lipschitz on the factor interval, giving

  ||Delta||_infinity<=a m/[2(1-u)]
                    <m/[20000(t+1)(1-10^-4)].                     (10.2)

Padding with unreachable machine states drives m/t to zero without changing halting. To check this quantitatively, let P be the number of tape-symbol pairs and Q the state count including HALT/REJECT. One origin-bit half of the cell alphabet has sigma0=P(1+2Q)+1 letters; sigma=2sigma0. Unrestricted interior-status tiles give t>=sigma^2 sigma0, while top-left marked types in M0 number m=3P sigma. Their visible edge data distinguish them even after deduplication. Thus m/t<=3/(8P Q^2). Add unreachable completed self-loop states to a one-step halting machine: Q grows effectively and the computation is unchanged. Equations (10.1)–(10.2) make the perturbation arbitrarily small.

Each site belongs to at most three translated site supports, so the summed interaction norm costs a factor at most three. If nonnegative energies are desired, add a spin-independent site constant, costing at most another factor two. Real energies are permitted in this perturbation statement.

The soft variant has the same symmetrization: average marked coefficients 2a and a. With the top DFA removed, a fixed one-step halting base machine with unreachable-state padding has m/t<=8P/sigma+16P/sigma^2, again tending to zero. The full counting and exact perturbed physical lift are in the symmetrization note. QED.

This does not prove that a tiny UNIFORM NEGATIVE FIELD restores coexistence in every fixed delayed model. Forcing plus at one field and minus at another does not rule out continuous interpolation. A phase-stability or additional contour theorem is required for that claim; it remains open here.

10.2 Why naive positive softening of an accepting tiling fails

Suppose an auxiliary finite-alphabet finite-range layer has strictly positive finite energies, and a plus-favoring pin is attached pointwise nonnegatively to a locally marked pattern. Finite energy gives that finite pattern a uniformly positive conditional probability, whether or not it can extend to a valid accepting computation. If the marked factor is 1+epsilon*1[spin=+] on that event, the spin-flip partition ratio is >1 on EVERY input. Invalid local certificates therefore leak a positive bias even in nonhalting cases. Finite error penalties do not repair an exact zero test.

The obstruction applies to pointwise nonnegative one-sided local acceptance, not to every conceivable positive physical simulator. M0 escapes it through signed character contributions BEFORE summation, despite strictly positive physical Boltzmann factors. The soft construction retains that exact cancellation. It softens the spin preference after a valid component has been selected; it does not replace matching by an error-tolerant local marker.

10.3 Fixed finite-data alphabet obstruction

Fix dimension, a bound q on alphabet size, finitely many support shapes, and a fixed finite set E of allowed TOTAL energy-table values. There are at most |E|^{sum_i q^{|F_i|}} such tables (sum over smaller alphabets if needed). Every subset of this finite set is decidable by a finite hard-coded list. No halting reduction to a semantic property of these tables can exist.

Thus a fixed alphabet cannot be added while retaining ALL the original fixed-data restrictions. Some unbounded input channel—range, energy magnitudes/values, spatial nonuniformity, alphabet or coefficient representation—must remain. An unbounded number of duplicate summands on one support would itself be such a channel; normalization into total tables is essential. This is a counting obstruction, not a quantitative optimal-alphabet theorem.

10.4 One dimension and the simple two-state ferromagnet

Every one-dimensional finite-alphabet finite-range finite-energy model has a positive transfer matrix after blocking. Perron–Frobenius gives a unique stationary Markov state and uniform exponential boundary convergence. Arbitrary-boundary DLR limits coincide. Hence the coexistence branch cannot be moved to one dimension at finite temperature within that class.

The homogeneous square-lattice ferromagnetic two-state nearest-neighbor subclass with exact rational couplings and field is also not an exact-data solution. Nonzero field gives uniqueness. At zero field the anisotropic critical expression is sinh(2J_x)sinh(2J_y)-1. For positive rationals J_x=p/q,J_y=r/q, equality would give a nonzero integer polynomial in exp(2/q), contradicting Hermite–Lindemann. Refining enclosures therefore decides the side of the known critical line; zero couplings give one-dimensional products. This is an obstruction for THAT ferromagnetic subclass, not for arbitrary binary finite-range or multibody models.

10.5 A concrete noncanonical hard-tile sector

The blank headless bit-zero letter x satisfies F(x,x,x)=x. The tile with h=I,v=I,triple(x,x,x),trivial DFA state zero is unmarked and matches itself on every edge. It fills the entire plane, even on a halting input. Its all-interior outside pattern actually freezes finite tile fillings: the incoming bottom vertical colors force h=I and center x in the first row; the left horizontal colors force v=I and the blank triples across that row. Its north colors repeat the condition for the next row. Induction fixes every row of any finite box, with no possible vacuum hole.

Consequently either bare Ising phase times this frozen tile plane is a Gibbs law of the PROOF spin/tile specification: the occupied-tile energy is a fixed local constant and no mark couples the spin. These are additional infinite-component sectors, including when the PHYSICAL model is unique. This is an explicit reason the full local DLR bridge must be combined with the canonical finite-component restriction. Arbitrary hard-tile boundary mixing is false; it cannot be substituted for physical Gibbs uniqueness.

13. DECISIVE OPEN PROBLEMS AND CONSTRUCTIVE ATTACKS

1. An independent exact-integer-table construction. The two-state computable-real route does not meet this objective. The rigorous positive-marker leakage obstruction explains why simply putting finite penalties on a hard computation layer fails. A new route must preserve an EXACT nonhalting zero or replace it by a quantitatively robust two-sided computation mechanism. Possible attacks are alternative signed orthogonality/tensor identities, or fault-tolerant hierarchical computation with a proof that invalid local marks cannot bias the phase. Fourier cancellation has not been proved necessary; only the naive pointwise-positive local-marker route has been excluded.

2. Full microscopic perturbation stability for EACH fixed halting model. The family-wide version is false by Theorem F. A constructive model-dependent route is to integrate perturbed auxiliary factors while retaining uniform hidden-variable Dobrushin control, then prove a bound on the resulting spin interaction in the weighted oscillation norm used by Theorem D. Precisely, one needs an effective bound on the induced perturbation's delta and diameter-weighted activation sum tending to zero with the microscopic perturbation. A spin-ratio bound alone is insufficient for the replica swap: long-range effective interaction supports must also be controlled. If this missing norm theorem is proved, Theorem D provides the remaining uniqueness/clustering step.

3. Analyticity, not just C-infinity. The current n-dependent diameter bound is summable at fixed order but gives no n!C^n control. The useful next target is a tree-decay bound for all cumulants or a complex-parameter dependency expansion. Direct target FKG/Lee–Yang is blocked by the explicit two-object determinant. A stable plus-phase contour/object expansion with controlled normalization could work, but nested contours and overlapping computation supports need a complete treatment. No convergent Taylor series is claimed.

4. Arbitrary PHYSICAL auxiliary boundaries and strong spatial mixing. Uniform group mixing removes distant auxiliary influence from local ratios, but turning those boundary corrections into controlled local spin potentials is still needed for a full finite-volume comparison theorem. Canonical hard-tile boundaries are a different problem: forced infinite components are unrelated sectors and cannot be treated as physical counterexamples. The selected-plus theorem is insufficient for an arbitrary-boundary or SSM claim. The reference-Ising boundary attack in the final supplement gives a useful intermediate result for free-group boxes only.

5. Opposite-field first-order crossover. Strict bias gives a plus interval. A sufficiently negative field can force minus, but this does not prove a coexistence field between them. Seek a Pirogov–Sinai theorem for the exponentially local induced interaction with an explicitly checked Peierls condition and stable pressures. An exact uniform negative-field transition estimate remains open here; the symmetrization counterfamily avoids assuming one.

6. Alphabet/range tradeoffs. A bounded alphabet with every other finite-data restriction fixed is impossible. Reducing the VARIABLE alphabet while allowing another controlled unbounded parameter is a meaningful problem. The soft compiler is a proof simplification, not an alphabet improvement: the one-state sample rises from 3,712 to 4,896 tiles and from about 1.95e13 to 2.05e14 physical states per site. A binary block simulation needs a finite-temperature exactness or error-correction proof; a zero-temperature gadget or soft equality constraint is not enough.

7. General classification and dynamics. Pi^0_2 is only an upper bound for unrestricted exact finite-table uniqueness. A completeness reduction would require a new construction, not merely re-indexing halting. No lower bound or efficient upper bound for physical Glauber mixing has been obtained. Fast conditional object/auxiliary algorithms do not imply fast spins. A meaningful next dynamics attack would analyze the witness-dependent metastable minus-to-plus escape separately from intraphase decorrelation.

These are precise remaining proof obligations, not results hidden behind qualitative terminology.
