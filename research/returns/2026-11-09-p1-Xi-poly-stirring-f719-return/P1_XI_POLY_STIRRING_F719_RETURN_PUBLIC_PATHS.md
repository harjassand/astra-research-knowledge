The fixed trigonometric inequalities used in the proof can be established symbolically, using elementary rational bounds on \(\pi\)\. No input-dependent approximation to \(\pi\), no spectral conditioning parameter, and no adjustable numerical tolerance is needed.

Thus the full construction and its resource verification remain finite-word Turing computations.

## 9\. Physical actuation and continuing service

The theorem establishes an algorithmic and kinematic control primitive. It does not establish an energy-efficient physical stirring mechanism.

For the final controller,

\[
\|b\|_2^2\leq4R^{600},
\]

and

\[
\|\nabla b\|_2^2\leq10^{10}R^{1012}.
\]

Maintaining the same prescribed field over any service horizon \(H>0\) gives the bound

\[
\int_0^H\|\nabla b\|_2^2\,dt
\leq10^{10}H R^{1012}.
\]

For example, in a Newtonian-fluid model with constant viscosity \(\nu\), the corresponding viscous-dissipation expression is \(\nu\|\nabla b\|_2^2\), subject to the model's dimensional normalization.

Actual actuation energy, pressure requirements and external forcing depend on the physical fluid system, which the input does not specify. No claim of a low-energy realization follows from this theorem.

In particular, the deliberately enormous exponents in \(A_0\) and \(n\) trade physical practicality for a simple uniform polynomial guarantee.
