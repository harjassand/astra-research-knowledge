Can a growing physical network with inaccessible interior and genuine loops be taught a substantial family of retained useful responses through its boundary, with a global constructive guarantee and useful total observation, control and physical costs? Discover the principle that converts information available at the boundary into an actual writing operation. The target is a predictive and constructive account of physical programmability, rather than another local rank certificate, an inverse problem with freely adjustable internal parameters, or a proposed unqualified memory circuit.

Preserve the useful tree construction: conservation of imposed boundary currents can select a hidden branch independently of its resistance, a nonlinear local response can turn full/half selection into a retained update, and unloaded branches can expose a relevant hidden voltage. This closes a real access problem under the stated local switching and observation contracts. Its sharp factor-two instantaneous margin, series-state equivalence, and sensitivity to a cross-connection also define its limits. The teacher knows paid generic layout and qualification bounds. Its actual initial retained states and component parameters are unknown throughout a declared domain. It observes only finite-accuracy boundary voltages, currents, forces, flows or other stated quantities, and issues only supported boundary fields and any physically supplied common teaching cue. It cannot clamp hidden junctions, read hidden gradients, independently set every link, receive an exact hidden state or obtain a free full reset. It may infer aggregate information from observations, but must pay for the acquisition, inverse problem, conditioning and precision. One promising bridge is the duality between boundary energy measurements and local physical writing fields. Here is a conditional starting construction, not a required method or a claim of a qualified material.

Let r be positive aggregate branch resistances and Z(r) the grounded boundary impedance of a known network. For a boundary-current pattern q, define F_q(r)=q^T Z(r)q. Kirchhoff/Thomson minimization gives the envelope identity

partial F_q/partial r_e = i_e(r,q)^2,

where i_e is the actual local branch current. Choose patterns q_l whose boundary energies determine the desired response, and let V(r) have columns i(r,q_l)^2. Thus DF(r)=V(r)^T. Static recoverability with a differentiable inverse implies full row rank for V in the corresponding aggregate coordinates. This is a connection to classical inverse-network theory, not a supplied robust teacher.

Suppose, additionally, that a local medium obeys the IDEAL signed time-sharing law

dr/ds = A(r,hidden) V(r) u,

where A is unknown positive diagonal, bounded above and away from zero, and the SAME gain at a given physical state applies to all patterns and both cue signs. If exact boundary observations allow rhat=r, then choosing

u = V(rhat)^+ (rstar-rhat)

gives dr_e/ds=a_e(s)(rstar_e-r_e). Each coordinate remains between its initial and target value and converges at the gain floor. This is a global conditional feedback construction, beyond a local rank argument. It uses a paid boundary inverse, not a hidden-state oracle. It can concern aggregates rather than individual microscopic memory cells.

Its missing operations are substantial. Unknown current- or polarity-dependent gains need not factor as A. A physical local mechanism must supply the update, cue, retention and noise bounds. Your earlier binary relay does not supply this continuous signed square-current law. Finite pulses are not ideal averaging: their duration is proportional to the sum of absolute coefficients, and fields change as the state moves. Finite-error observations must actually support the inverse or an alternative functional controller. Static recoverability can have severe conditioning. Compactness gives a finite inverse bound, not a useful one as the hidden system grows. None of those costs can be hidden in the pseudoinverse symbol.

An exact small cyclic example shows both the opening and its limits. Boundary vertices are0,1,2,3 with3 grounded; hidden vertices are4,5; edges are(0,4),(1,4),(4,5),(2,5),(3,5),(0,2). Use the three unit boundary injections into0,1,2 and their pairwise sums. The six boundary energy measurements determine the grounded3x3 impedance. Removing the boundary chord in admittance form and imposing the tree identity Ztree_02=Ztree_12 gives an aggregate inverse. At all six resistances1, the six squared-current fields have determinant-1/32. With chord resistance rho and the other five resistances1, the determinant is-8*rho^2/(rho+3)^4.
