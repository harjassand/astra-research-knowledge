ACQUIRED INTERFACES FOR GENUINELY NEW INTERVENTIONS
A complete transfer theorem for finite static quadratic interfaces
7 November 2026

STATUS AND CENTRAL RESULT
=========================

This report resolves the following chosen formulation of the transfer question.
A collection of unknown reciprocal static quadratic modules has a warranted,
complete, finite interface. Calibrated macroscopic energy readings can be made
at that interface. What information permits uniformly reliable prediction and
design after new passive couplings, new wiring, and new assemblies are chosen,
without returning to the original sources?

The answer is the full interface quadratic form, acquired with a relative
quadratic-form certificate. The main result is exact: the worst logarithmic
relative response error over every finite passive assembly equals the largest
Thompson distance between the individual module laws. Consequently a library
of intervals L_i <= K_i <= U_i <= gamma L_i transfers with the SAME factor gamma
through arbitrary finite wiring and elimination. There is no multiplicative
factor for assembly size, loops, or elimination depth. Every new intermediate
approximation, if used, would need its own accounted error; the compiler here
uses exact rational elimination.

The report also constructs acquisition, a finite compiler, and a global
continuous design algorithm. It proves dimension/precision information lower
bounds and the impossibility of size-independent absolute-error transfer from
a record with residual uncertainty. These are full statements for the stated
source and intervention class, not a claim about arbitrary interventions in an
unknown heterogeneous continuum. No historical-priority claim is made for
Schur reduction, port models, spectral composition, tomography or optimization.

The new operation combines established mathematical machinery into an explicit
acquisition-to-new-assembly contract. A numerical source is fully constructed;
52 scalar source solves support three subsequently specified assemblies and a
new continuous design, with all rational certificates retained. This is not a
physical experiment or evidence of an advantage over directly available source
coefficients. The broad consequence earned here is universal compositional
reuse within the stated class, with exactly identified information and cost.

1. SOURCE, INTERFACE, AND ALLOWED CHANGES
=======================================

1.1 Complete interface
----------------------

Module i has p_i accessible, grounded port coordinates z in R^{p_i}. Its
minimized static energy is

    E_i(z) = z^T K_i z,             K_i = K_i^T > 0.       (1.1)

Grounding, coordinate units, the energy convention, and which ports can be
independently set are supplied. A common factor 1/2 in physical energy changes
no relative assertion, but it must be used consistently in calibration.
Different module copies have the same law only under an explicit identical-
response prior. Otherwise each copy needs an interval covering its own law.

A concrete source is an explicitly listed rational positive definite quadratic
form in port and microscopic internal coordinates. Each requested reading is
obtained by a charged rational linear solve and finite rounding. This source,
including its calibration computation, is implemented in run_transfer.py.

More generally, let H be a real Hilbert energy space, H_0 a closed subspace of
internal variations, and J:R^p -> H a supplied finite-energy lifting. Then

    inf_{h in H_0} ||Jz+h||_H^2 = ||(I-P_0)Jz||_H^2

is a finite quadratic form. The promise K>0 means no nonzero port setting is
energetically invisible. This includes suitably formulated rough anisotropic
conductivity energy problems when their trace/lifting, grounding, closed
internal space, and complete port boundary model are actually warranted.
The accepted rough-energy theory is useful at precisely that scope.

A finite list of measured boundary modes does not establish that unmeasured
modes are absent. A homogenized tensor requires a separately justified
assembly model before it can be used to join finite pieces. New contacts may
excite additional boundary modes. Neither a small complete port space nor its
physical adequacy follows automatically from exponential coefficient moments.

1.2 Every future assembly in the chosen class
-------------------------------------------

A new assembly supplies a finite explicit list of module placements, rational
wiring maps T_a, and a known rational positive semidefinite quadratic form G.
Partition the global variables into externally imposed e and internally
relaxed y. The energy is

    Q_K(e,y) = sum_a (R_a e+S_a y)^T K_{t(a)}(R_a e+S_a y)
               + [e;y]^T G [e;y].                       (1.2)

Here T_a=[R_a S_a] and t(a) identifies a module type. Shared coordinates model
connections; general rational linear maps also permit warranted linear
constraints or coordinate transformations. All variables, placements, maps,
and coefficient bits count in the input. A binary integer is not an implicit
license to supply exponentially many unlisted components or output entries.

The assembly response is

    e^T F_K e = inf_y Q_K(e,y).                          (1.3)

G must be nonnegative and identical when two possible source libraries are
compared. Its entries and their calibration are supplied information. A new
unknown component requires acquisition; calling it a passive component does
not reveal its coefficients. Wiring imperfections, contact effects, and
setting errors need justified enclosures or a revised interface model.

This class allows genuinely new interventions. The assembly dimension, number
of copies, topology, and positive coupling directions may all be chosen after
acquisition. For example a new edge adds w(e_j-e_k)(e_j-e_k)^T and creates
off-diagonal coupling absent from diagonal shunts. More generally, no finite
collection of rank-one positive directions generates all rank-one positive
directions by nonnegative combinations in dimension at least two: a positive
sum with rank one can contain only vectors parallel to its range. The learned
record is therefore not interpolation over a prelisted actuator family.

All changes still act through the complete retained interface. Internal
constitutive changes, newly exposed microscopic coordinates, hidden cross-talk,
nonlinear response, hysteresis, dynamics and resonance are outside (1.1)-(1.3).
The predecessor's indistinguishable-actuator-family example remains an
accepted premise establishing why unrestricted transfer needs more information.

2. EXACT COMPOSITION AND THE TRANSFER METRIC
===========================================

2.1 Elimination is constructed
------------------------------

For a finite rational positive semidefinite matrix, write

    Q = [ A  B^T ; B  H ].

Positivity implies ker(H) is contained in ker(B^T). Indeed, if Hz=0, the
quadratic form on [te;z] is nonnegative for both signs of arbitrarily small t;
its linear term forces z^T B e=0. Hence H X=B is consistent. Rational row
reduction, with free coordinates zero, produces X. Then

    F = A-B^T X,            y_min(e) = -Xe.              (2.1)

Completing the square shows that (2.1) attains the infimum, including singular
H. The result is independent of the chosen consistent solution X. No inverse,
ground-state, relaxation or minimization oracle is being left unevaluated.
The code includes this singular case and a floating unused-coordinate check.

Eliminating each module's private internal variables before forming (1.2)
gives the same value as assembling the complete microscopic energies and
minimizing all internal variables together. This is simply the associativity
of infima over the independent private coordinates and the global y. For
attaining quadratic source problems it is also associativity of the actual
minimizing construction. This is the standard Schur/static-condensation/Kron
principle, credited below. It does not identify the module's microscopic form.

2.2 One uncertainty factor for any assembly
-----------------------------------------

Suppose simultaneously for every module type

    0 < L_i <= K_i <= U_i <= gamma L_i,     gamma >= 1.    (2.2)

The orders are quadratic-form orders. Congruence, summation, and G>=0 imply
pointwise in every global variable

    Q_L <= Q_K <= Q_U <= gamma Q_L.

For the last inequality, the module contribution is at most gamma times its
lower version and G <= gamma G. Taking the infimum over exactly the same y
therefore proves

    F_L <= F_K <= F_U <= gamma F_L.                       (2.3)

This proves the statement for every finite assembly at once. Choosing the
next assembly adaptively from the acquired library does not require new
statistical coverage events, provided (2.2) was a simultaneous deterministic
enclosure or was already established on one stated simultaneous event.

Repeated use of the same type does not multiply gamma. Correlation between
copies with identical response causes no difficulty, since the proof is
pointwise in the common K_i. Known additional passive components and arbitrary
loops cause no difficulty either. If one later approximates a compiled model
again, that separate approximation can accumulate error. This report retains
exact rational compiled matrices and does not make that additional step.

For the declared rectangular uncertainty class C=product_i[L_i,U_i], its lower
and upper libraries are both admissible. Consequently (2.3)'s endpoints are
the exact energy envelopes over C, for every assembly and loading. If C is an
outer compression of more informative raw measurements or additional physical
constraints, the envelopes remain valid but need not be the sharp envelopes
for that smaller consistency set. In particular an arbitrary SPD interval
endpoint need not be a resistor-network Laplacian of a specified topology.

2.3 An exact necessary-and-sufficient error criterion
----------------------------------------------------

For positive definite A,B define the Thompson distance

    d_T(A,B)=inf{s>=0: exp(-s) A <= B <= exp(s) A}.

For two module libraries K,K', define d_task as the supremum, over all finite
assemblies (1.2) and loadings with positive energy, of

    | log( e^T F_{K'} e / e^T F_K e ) |.

Then

    d_task(K,K') = max_i d_T(K_i,K'_i).                  (2.4)

Proof. Let s be the right-hand side. The two-sided relative comparison
propagates through each congruence, sum, common G, and infimum, exactly as
above, giving the upper bound s. For the converse select one module, no G,
and no internal assembly coordinates. The extremal generalized Rayleigh
quotient gives d_T for that module. Rational loadings approximate the extremal
loading arbitrarily closely, so restricting settings to rationals leaves the
supremum unchanged. Taking the maximum proves equality. Relative comparison
also ensures common zero-energy kernels; zero/zero is omitted from the ratio.

Thus two libraries have the same responses in every allowed new context if
and only if every corresponding port law agrees. A data representation need
not literally store a dense matrix, but it must preserve these port laws to
the required metric accuracy if it promises uniform transfer to every context.
Equation (2.4) identifies the appropriate information object and precision.

2.4 Further response consequences
---------------------------------

When the assembled law is positive definite, inversion reverses order:

    F_U^{-1} <= F_K^{-1} <= F_L^{-1} <= gamma F_U^{-1}.

Consequently the corresponding demand-controlled quadratic compliance also
has the same multiplicative guarantee. Force/energy conventions must be
converted consistently. Relative error of individual signed current entries,
especially entries close to zero, is not implied by an energy ratio.

For a controlled absolute statement, (2.3) gives

    |e^T(F_K-F_L)h| <= (gamma-1)
                      sqrt((e^T F_L e)(h^T F_L h)).       (2.5)

This follows from positivity and Cauchy-Schwarz in the normalized quadratic
form. Entire tensors and bilinear response predictions can therefore be
certified without pointwise microscopic field reconstruction. Hierarchical
assembly and later exact elimination preserve the same library certificate.

3. FINITE ACQUISITION, SETTINGS, AND CALIBRATION
================================================

3.1 Scalar-energy construction
------------------------------

Fix one module of dimension p. In fixed supplied coordinates assume a
warranted lower scale K>=alpha I with rational alpha>0. For setting-error
accounting and output magnitudes an upper bound K<=beta I may also be supplied.
To achieve gamma=1+delta, 0<delta<=1, put

    epsilon_m = delta alpha / (2(1+delta)),
    nu = epsilon_m/(2p).                                 (3.1)

Acquire a calibrated scalar energy record (y_v,r_v) with

    |y_v-v^T K v| <= r_v <= nu

at the p settings e_i and the p(p-1)/2 settings e_i+e_j. Thus exactly

    D = p(p+1)/2                                         (3.2)

scalar readings suffice. All settings are rational, with at most two unit
entries. Reconstruct the diagonal from the first p readings and put

    Khat_ij = (y_{e_i+e_j}-y_{e_i}-y_{e_j})/2.

The diagonal error is at most nu and the off-diagonal error at most 3nu/2.
The symmetric error therefore has operator norm at most 3p nu/2, which is
at most 3epsilon_m/4. The implementation also keeps the sharper row-sum
bound obtained from the individual calibrated radii.

Define the rational matrices

    L = Khat-epsilon_m I,       U = Khat+epsilon_m I.      (3.3)

Then L<=K<=U. Moreover L>=(alpha-2epsilon_m)I
=alpha I/(1+delta), and U=L+2epsilon_m I<=(1+delta)L.
This proves (2.2). The program additionally verifies L>0 and (1+delta)L-U>=0
by rational symmetric elimination, giving replayable finite certificates.
The unknown inequality L<=K<=U is earned from the source calibration, not
inferred solely from an internally consistent matrix calculation.

3.2 What the access contract costs
----------------------------------

A scalar reading is not assigned unit cost. Its cost C_acq(v,nu) includes
preparation, reset, setting, waiting, receiver work, calibration, and all source
computation required to justify the radius. If energy is computed from several
physical channels, their costs also belong here. Acquisition of the complete
interface prior and scale bounds is either paid or explicitly supplied as
valid input; these premises are not generated by a successful finite fit.

For example, if the actual setting is v+h with ||h||_2<=xi and K<=beta I,

    |E(v+h)-E(v)| <= beta(2||v||_2 xi+xi^2).              (3.4)

Here ||v||<=sqrt(2). This contribution must be included with measurement and
rounding error in nu. Equation (3.4) prices required setting fidelity; it
does not manufacture that fidelity. A positive noise or control floor may
prevent the requested certificate. All units are fixed at the port level;
there is no pointwise normalization of heterogeneous coefficients.

For fixed valid alpha,beta, dyadic reading precision can be chosen with

    O(1+log p+log_+(1/delta)+log_+(1/alpha))

fractional bits and O(1+log_+ beta) integer bits. After fixed-unit scale
encoding, a dense record is of order

    p^2 [1+log p+log_+(beta/alpha)+log(1/delta)]

bits, with the actual scale descriptions, calibration metadata and all
instance-dependent information added. The conditioning/units qualifications
are essential. No information in analog settings or auxiliary state is free.

For many module types the complete source cost is the sum of (3.2) actual
reading costs, plus the supplied or acquired interface and calibration costs.
There is no source experiment for an additional assembly once the library
and the new assembly's components/maps are validly specified. Physical
deployment, fabrication and setting of a later design, if wanted, are separate
costed operations; they are not performed by this numerical construction.

4. INFORMATION THAT TRANSFER NECESSARILY RETAINS
================================================

4.1 Exact scalar-query dimension
--------------------------------

Over unrestricted real positive definite p-by-p port laws, at least
D=p(p+1)/2 adaptive deterministic exact scalar-energy queries are necessary
for exact responses to every future passive task. The same statement holds
for rational port laws when the allowed probes are rational.

Run the protocol on K_0=I. Every queried v supplies one linear functional
H -> v^T H v on the D-dimensional space of symmetric matrices. Fewer than D
functionals leave a nonzero H in their common kernel. Rescale H so that
||H||<1. Then I+H and I-H are both positive definite and produce exactly the
same complete adaptive transcript: at each step the previous answers, hence
the next setting, coincide. Their port laws differ, so (2.4) supplies a future
task distinguishing them. With rational probes H can be found by rational
homogeneous linear algebra. This is not a bound for full-vector observations,
coefficient access, extra structure, or hypothetical infinite-precision real
encodings on a restricted discrete source class.

3.2's acquisition therefore matches the exact scalar-query dimension. It
does not obtain a generic operator for fewer observations than tomography.
Its benefit is certified reuse after the intervention has changed.

4.2 Finite binary record lower bound
------------------------------------

Let the source class contain every K=I+H with ||H||_op<=1/4. Suppose a reusable
source-dependent binary record of at most B bits, with no further source
access, permits predictions within multiplicative factor 1+delta for every
future passive assembly and loading. All source-dependent settings, advice,
state and stored encodings are included in B. For 0<delta<1/32,

    B >= D log_2(1/(16delta)) - 1.                       (4.1)

Proof. In the D-dimensional symmetric-matrix normed space, take a maximal
set of points in the radius-1/4 operator-norm ball separated by more than
4delta. Balls of radius 4delta around the set cover that ball. Comparing
volumes gives at least (1/(16delta))^D points. If two corresponding sources
have the same record, their common prediction for every isolated-module
loading forces K' <= (1+delta)^2 K and the reverse inequality. Since both
operator norms are at most 5/4, this gives

    ||K-K'|| <= (5/4)((1+delta)^2-1) < 4delta,

a contradiction. There are fewer than 2^{B+1} binary strings of length at
most B, proving (4.1). A rational packing with adjusted absolute constants
gives the same order when a dense rational source class is desired.

This is an information bound for the generic SPD class, not an unrestricted
runtime lower bound. Special graph, sparsity, symmetry or rank priors can
change storage requirements. In particular graph spectral sparsification
cannot be dismissed by a packing theorem for all symmetric positive forms.

4.3 A broad absolute-error obstruction
--------------------------------------

Suppose one acquired record is compatible with K and K' whose port laws
differ. Some rational loading v has a nonzero energy gap Delta. Connect N
identical-response copies in parallel at that loading. The two possible
assembly energies differ by N Delta. For N>2epsilon/Delta a single prediction
cannot be within absolute error epsilon for both sources.

Thus a record with residual source uncertainty cannot promise a fixed
absolute error for every arbitrarily large later assembly. All N copies are
explicitly listed and costed; this is an information assertion even when
unlimited digital computation is allowed. It is not a succinct-input or
algorithmic hardness argument.

In the full continuous SPD class, finite calibrated observations with strictly
positive radii cannot guarantee removal of all uncertainty in the worst case.
Take an interior K_0 and let the source return readings centered at its exact
energies. The finite positive-radius constraints contain a sufficiently small
nonzero symmetric perturbation. This argument concerns a permissible worst-
case transcript, not a claim that every possible positive-radius consistency
set has interior. Exact records, discrete coefficient priors, bounded task
size/gain, or additional source access alter the question.

Relative certification in (2.3) remains valid under replication. If a task
supplies a finite energy scale U_task, it immediately converts the uncertainty
to absolute error at most (gamma-1)U_task. The demand for a size-independent
absolute guarantee is precisely what fails.

5. FINITE COMPILER AND COMPLETE BIT COST
=======================================

5.1 Implementation
-------------------

port_transfer.py acquires the law, checks rational positivity, forms the full
new assembly matrices from the explicit maps, computes both response endpoints
and minimizing lifts by RREF, and verifies the final relative interval. It
also normalizes new actuators and invokes the unchanged accepted global
decision code. All matrix calculations use fractions.Fraction. No floating
eigenvalue test, semidefinite optimizer, physical ground-state operation or
unevaluated global bound is required by the algorithm.

Positive semidefiniteness is checked by symmetric positive-pivot elimination.
A negative diagonal rejects the matrix. If all remaining diagonals vanish,
positivity requires that remaining block to vanish. Otherwise eliminating a
positive diagonal gives an equivalent lower-dimensional PSD test. This
supplies a finite rational witness, including singular cases.

5.2 Prediction complexity
-------------------------

Let L>=2 be the complete binary input length of a finite call, including all
explicit coordinates, module intervals, maps, known components, and rational
loading/output specification. Matrix construction uses a polynomial number of
arithmetic operations; a simple dense implementation uses at most O(L^4).
Let D be the product of input denominators. Its bit length is at most L.
Compiled entries are sums of terms of degree at most three in the supplied
rationals, so a common denominator divides D^3 and entry bit lengths are
O(L). Gaussian/Schur intermediates are ratios of minors of explicitly formed
integer matrices. Hadamard's determinant bound gives O(L^2) bits. Rational
gcd and schoolbook integer arithmetic therefore give a deliberately loose
O(L^10) bit bound for compilation, PSD checks, endpoint matrices and lifts.
The output has polynomial bit length as well. Acquisition postprocessing has
the same type of bound in its complete received-data length.

Full cost is

    C_interface/calibration + sum_{i,v} C_acq(i,v,nu_i)
        + O(L_record^10) + sum_new_tasks O(L_task^10).     (5.1)

Every new component's acquisition/calibration, if it is not already supplied
with a justified law, must be added. Source costs may grow far faster than
the number of requested digits. There is no efficient measurable-continuum
input engine here. Expanded matrices and all global coordinates are actual
input, not a supposedly cheap succinct description.

The fully implemented rational microscopic source itself has polynomial
reading cost in its explicit matrix length and requested precision. Its
internal variables are listed and solved. Directly using that source code
as an incumbent is legitimate and may be cheaper than acquiring its port
law. The example's information separation demonstrates the interface
contract, not a computational advantage against that incumbent.

6. GLOBALLY CERTIFIED DESIGN AFTER NEW INTERVENTIONS ARE CHOSEN
================================================================

6.1 Objective and robustness
----------------------------

After library acquisition choose a new assembly and new PSD directions C_j.
Let u lie in a nonempty explicit rational polytope P=conv{v_1,...,v_q} with
nonnegative coordinates, and let the new known contribution be sum_j u_j C_j.
For a supplied loading e and rational price vector ell the utility is

    f_K(u) = m_K(u)-ell.u,
    m_K(u) = inf_y Q_K(e,y;u).                           (6.1)

This is imposed-field energy benefit minus price. It is concave in u because
it is an infimum of affine functions. No general discrete topology optimum
is claimed. The topology and positive directions can be new; continuous
amplitudes within the now explicitly supplied decision set are optimized.

For the declared outer uncertainty rectangle C, monotonicity gives exactly

    min_{K in C} f_K(u)=f_L(u),
    max_{u in P} min_{K in C} f_K(u)=max_{u in P} f_L(u).  (6.2)

If the rectangle contains models excluded by raw data, (6.2) is the optimum
for that conservative rectangle, not the sharper raw-data minimax problem.
It still supplies a guarantee for the true source and a uniform true-regret
bound below. No unknown physical source is queried during this optimization.

6.2 Normalization into the accepted global solver
-------------------------------------------------

Require the assembled lower baseline Q_0 to be SPD, or supply a valid fixed
quotient on which it is SPD. This condition is checked. General PSD prediction
remains covered by Section 2; the following decision implementation uses this
explicit coercivity condition and does not silently regularize singular input.

Let R be the largest vertex coordinate, C_sum=sum_j C_j. If R=0 or all C_j=0,
the energy is constant in the controls and a best vertex for the linear price
term solves the problem directly. Otherwise construct

    mu=1/||Q_0^{-1}||_infinity,
    W=||C_sum||_infinity,        T=max(1,W/mu),
    b=R/2+1/T,                 r=(R/2)/b,
    Q_c=Q_0+(R/2)C_sum,         D_j=b C_j.               (6.3)

The inverse in (6.3) is obtained by paid exact rational elimination. Symmetry
implies Q_0>=mu I and C_sum<=W I. Hence C_sum/T<=Q_0 and

    sum_j D_j <= Q_c,        0<r<1.

The new variables theta_j=(u_j-R/2)/b lie in [-r,r]. Thus

    Q_0+sum_j u_j C_j = Q_c+sum_j theta_j D_j

satisfies exactly the predecessor's positive relative affine contract. A
valid baseline bound is S=e^T (Q_c)_{ee} e from the trial y=0. Prices become
b ell, with the constant objective offset (R/2)sum_j ell_j subtracted on
return. All transformed vertices remain explicit rationals.

The unchanged predecessor ellipsoid_design constructs finite-precision scalar
secants, rational separating cuts, bounded rational ellipsoid updates, and a
global gap certificate. finite_quadratic_reader constructs every requested
model value by a new rational solve and dyadic enclosure. Consequently this
application earns both the value calls and the optimizer; it does not invoke
a source oracle or posit an optimization certificate without evaluating it.
The output rational u_hat is feasible, and f_L^*-f_L(u_hat)<=epsilon.

6.3 Actual reading and bit bounds
---------------------------------

For q>=2 write m=q-1. Let D_theta be the infinity-norm diameter of the explicit
theta vertices, eta=epsilon/2, and use the predecessor's computable constants

    C=2(1+r) S D_theta^2/(1-r)^2,       Cbar=max(C,eta),
    h=eta/(16 Cbar),                   nu_d=eta h/256,
    L_theta=(1+r)S/(1-r)+sum_j |b ell_j|,
    L_s=max(m D_theta L_theta,eta),
    rho=eta/(16 q^2 L_s),
    B=ceil(log_2(1/rho)),              N=4q(q-1)(B+1).

There are at most (q+1)N constructed scalar model evaluations. Each has
requested source radius nu_d/4; the source here is the known finite model,
not the unknown original module. Convex weights are rounded on a dyadic
lattice with L_theta D_theta(q-1)2^{-b_act}<=nu_d/4. Returned actual settings
remain in P, and the gap includes this rounding loss. The full predecessor
contract and proof are retained in its unchanged archive; they are accepted
machinery rather than a recurring proof obligation.

For an explicit polynomial composition bound, let L>=2 contain all original
finite data for this design and ceil(log_+(1/epsilon)). Normalized entries
have O(L^2) bits; their total parameter length is O(L^5). Allowing conservative
generic rational sums, the scalar constants above have O(L^3) bits, so
N=O(L^5), the evaluation count is O(L^6), and an actual control/rounded-value
record has O(L^4) bits. The predecessor's Z=1+B_par+B_rec+N+q+k+d is therefore
O(L^10). Its accepted O(Z^10) digital bound, the normalization work, and all
finite rational source solves are covered by O(L^110) bit operations. This is
an intentionally loose explicit bound, not a proposed practical scaling law.
The displayed counts and arithmetic lengths are more informative in use.
No coefficient oracle, exact-real record or instance-dependent advice is free.

6.4 Guarantee for the actual system
-----------------------------------

Let U_task bound m_L(u) throughout P. A finite rational choice is the maximum,
over listed vertices, of the trial y=0 energy; that trial is affine in u.
Equations (2.3) and (6.1) imply

    f_L(u) <= f_K(u) <= f_L(u)+(gamma-1)U_task.

For the returned lower-model epsilon optimum,

    f_K^*-f_K(u_hat) <= epsilon+(gamma-1)U_task.           (6.4)

The same proof uses the smaller actual returned gap in place of epsilon.
There is only one uncertainty term. Prices cancel in the comparison of a
given u and are fully retained in optimization. New tasks demanding a much
larger energy scale or much finer absolute accuracy can require a finer
initial library; Section 4.3 proves why no fixed uncertain record avoids that.
