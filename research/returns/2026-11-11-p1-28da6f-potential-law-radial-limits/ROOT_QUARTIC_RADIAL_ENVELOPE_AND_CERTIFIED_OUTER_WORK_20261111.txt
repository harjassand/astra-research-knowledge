An actual radial envelope for the original quartic and a paid outer-work certificate

Premises and notation. Use the original J_k, k>1/3, ell=3k-1,n=k+1, its accepted rank-one convexity, the natural row Bregman reserve gamma_k|z|^4, and the attained conditional row minimizer in the complete mixed-curvature continuation. Means a,b are arbitrary real rows. Fix p=Df in L4 and write x_t=a+tp, 0<=t<=1. Let y_t=b+Dg_t be the UNIQUE conditional minimizing gradient row, and M(t)=integral J_k(x_t,y_t). Uniqueness concerns gradients; potentials have their usual constant freedom.

1. Uniform actual envelopes along the whole path.

Put A_k=ell/(8n),B_k=ell/4 and
 mu_k=(min(k^2,1)/2) min(A_k/2,1/8,B_k/(2k))>0.
For column norms u,v and determinant j, set S=ku-v+2j. Then
 k^2u^2+v^2=(ku-v)^2+2kuv<=2S^2+8j^2+2kuv.
Consequently J_k(F)>=mu_k|F|_F^4 for EVERY real F. This is a crude explicit coercivity bound, not the sharp shared reserve.

Let C_k be a positive coefficient-sum majorant for the degree-four polynomial J_k and its first two derivatives, so
 J_k(F)<=C_k|F|^4,
 |DJ_k(F)|<=C_k|F|^3,
 |DJ_k(F)-DJ_k(G)|<=C_k(|F|+|G|)^2|F-G|.
Increasing a rational coefficient sum gives such a C_k for rational k; no pointwise norm oracle is needed. Define P>=1 to majorize
 |a|+|b|+||p||4+(C_k/mu_k)^(1/4)(|a|+||p||4+|b|).
The competitor y=b and coercivity give ||x_t||4+||y_t||4<=C P uniformly in t. Rational upward fourth-root enclosures can replace the displayed radicals in a finite calculation.

2. Strong continuity at the natural exponent, without a positive row-Hessian floor.

At fixed x_t, the minimizing stress is solenoidal. The original Bregman bound therefore gives
 gamma_k ||y_t-y_s||4^4
 <=E(x_t,y_s)-M(t)
 <=2 C P^3 ||p||4 |t-s|,                                    (1)
where C is a coefficient majorant depending only on k. The second inequality follows by comparing the two minimizing values through their respective admissible competitors and using the first-derivative polynomial bound. Thus y_t is globally Holder continuous of exponent1/4 in L4 along this fixed path. No pointwise invertibility, L12 stress assumption or adapted-Hessian representation is used.

3. Exact outer work, with an envelope derivative.

The upper comparison using y_t at t+h and the lower comparison using y_(t+h) at t sandwich the difference quotient. Polynomial derivative continuity and (1) give
 M'(t)=integral D_x J_k(x_t,y_t).p,                           (2)
with one-sided endpoint derivatives. This derivative is continuous. Since x_0=a is constant, row convexity gives M(0)=J_k(a;b). Therefore
 M(1)-J_k(a;b)
 =integral_0^1 integral_(T2) D_xJ_k(a+tDf,b+Dg_t).Df dx dt.    (3)

The complete original question is precisely nonnegativity of (3) for EVERY admitted k,a,b,f. This is a true compatible nonlocal path representation. It does NOT make its sign automatic. Ordinary convexity of M as a functional of f is stronger and is now disproved by an actual compatible example; (3) asks only about the total work from the constant first row.

The derivative in (2) has a computable Holder modulus. Increasing C if necessary, one may take
 L=C P^2||p||4 [||p||4+(2 C P^3||p||4/gamma_k)^(1/4)]
so that |M'(t)-M'(s)|<=L|t-s|^(1/4). This follows from the second-derivative polynomial bound, (1), and Holder's inequality in space. The case p=0 is immediate.

The envelope is paid by its energy certificate and the same coercivity bound, not assumed separately.

For fixed compatible G, let q=cof G,C=int q tensor q,r=int(det G)q,d=int(det G)^2. At k0=1/3 the all-mean gap is
