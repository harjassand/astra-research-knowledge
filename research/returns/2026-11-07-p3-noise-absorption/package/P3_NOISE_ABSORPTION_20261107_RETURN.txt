P3 — CALIBRATED LOCAL NOISE ABSORPTION AND ITS OPERATING BOUNDARIES
Research return, 7 November 2026

Programme endpoint retained: repeatable many-body state control with information,
initialization, reset, storage, calibration, noise, and physical resources paid.

0. RESULT AND STATUS

The question resolved here is whether a nonvanishing, supplied local Markovian
noise process can be incorporated into exact state restoration, and how to decide
whether the necessary correction belongs to an actual finite control menu.

The answer has four parts.

(1) For a faithful Gibbs state of a supplied commuting local Hamiltonian, each
supplied local noise jump has a positive local completion. The construction is
an application of established KMS detailed-balance machinery. It gives exact
stationarity; it does not promise fast mixing through an arbitrary phase diagram.

(2) For a state U (tensor_i tau_i) U*, where U is a supplied bounded-depth local
circuit and each tau_i is faithful, there is a stronger construction. Any
supplied bounded-support Lindblad noise can be completed by another Lindblad
generator so that their SUM is a set of commuting, dressed block resets. This
gives unconditional complete physical-output restoration from arbitrary initial
states, including correlations with any finite untouched reference. Its exact
mixing certificate is a coverage probability. There is no unfinished-clock
weight or intrinsic stationary-output error in this construction.

(3) There is an exact optimal-rate criterion for embedding a given noise process
into one specified reset generator. It is a generalized eigenvalue of two
projected Choi matrices, with a kernel criterion at deficient rank. A simpler
positive Choi subtraction supplies explicit correction jumps. The optimal rate
is for this reset embedding, not for every conceivable stabilizing controller.

(4) For a finite, warranted affine menu of local physical generators, exact
stationarity is a finite linear feasibility problem, and realizing the stronger
reset construction is a second finite linear feasibility problem. Both can be
formed from local matrices without full-system Hilbert-space enumeration in the
bounded-depth product representation. Infeasibility certificates refer to the
stated menu and to the stated task. They do not rule out different interfaces.

This resolves a mathematical operating regime with bounded noise and bounded
activity per site. It also resolves why that regime is not a hardware threshold
for arbitrary unknown error. Exact compensation uses information about the noise
and controls. Residual unknown error has a separate precision budget; global
trace-distance accuracy can require increasingly accurate calibration with N.

The full programme endpoint is NOT declared complete. The short-circuit target
class is a substantive change from arbitrary supplied preparation circuits and
from generic collective Gibbs states. No native dissipative apparatus, calibrated
affine actuator law, or experimental record has been acquired here. The supplied
models in the executable audits are declared synthetic mathematical inputs.
The results below make the interface and information obligations decidable or
quantitative where their inputs are supplied. They do not erase those obligations.

All accepted predecessors remain intact without reproof. In particular, the
five-local restoration compiler remains the general supplied-circuit conversion;
its inverse stationary-accuracy cost, logarithmic mixing-accuracy cost, auxiliary-
uniform obstruction, and shrinking implementation budget are unchanged.

1. NOTATION, DATA, AND PHYSICAL INTERFACE

All generators below act on density matrices. Put

 D[F](rho) = F rho F* - (F*F rho + rho F*F)/2,
 R_omega(X) = omega Tr(X),
 T(rho,sigma) = ||rho-sigma||_1/2.

The supplied noise is N = -i[H_n, .] + sum_j D[F_j]. Amplitudes and physical rates
are included in F_j. All output guarantees concern the actual retained physical
system, not an unperformed ideal decoder. The reference is untouched.

There are two distinct access contracts.

Mathematical synthesis contract: finite matrices for a target and for local noise
are supplied. The output is a finite list of positive jumps, a Hamiltonian, rate
certificates, locality bounds, and an error certificate. Being a Lindblad
generator is a mathematical implementability condition, not evidence that the
user has a native actuator for it.

Native-menu contract: an additional finite list G_a, admissible rate/capacity
constraints, and costs is supplied, together with a warranted simultaneous law

 L(u) = N + sum_a u_a G_a,       u in a supplied rational polytope.

The law includes control-dependent noise, ongoing idle noise, reset, selection,
and controller effects to the stated accuracy. Independent access to each G_a
does not establish this joint law. Costs are supplied for the actual native
operations, routing, replenishment, and holding. A successful menu certificate
then specifies admissible commands and a physical-time restoration bound within
that law. The current work does not acquire this law for an unknown apparatus.

For finite-bit statements, coefficients can be rational or computable with
certified enclosures. Exact rational LP statements below use rational data.
For finite-bit real data at a boundary, strict feasibility/PSD margins or exact
algebraic representations must be retained. A floating-point solver's status
alone is not an exact certificate.

2. LOCAL COMPLETION FOR COMMUTING GIBBS TARGETS

Let sigma>0 and let F be one supplied noise jump. Set

 J = sigma^(1/2) F* sigma^(-1/2),
 K = F*F + J*J.

In an eigenbasis of sigma with eigenvalues s_i>0 define

 G_ij = -(i/2) [(sqrt(s_i)-sqrt(s_j))/(sqrt(s_i)+sqrt(s_j))] K_ij,
 G_ii = 0.

Then G is Hermitian and

 [-i[G,.] + D[F] + D[J]](sigma) = 0.                         (2.1)

This is the established KMS modular completion, specialized to keeping F as an
unavoidable jump and adding its modular partner. Credit belongs to the KMS
representation and coherent-correction machinery of Fagnola-Umanita,
Chen-Kastoryano-Gilyen, and Ding-Li-Lin; see the latter's governing v5 Theorem 10,
Eq. (2.33), and paired-coupling discussion. No priority claim is made here.

A direct identity needed for this application is

 F sigma F* + J sigma J* = sqrt(sigma) K sqrt(sigma).

Its off-diagonal dissipative defect is
 -(sqrt(s_i)-sqrt(s_j))^2 K_ij/2; the commutator in (2.1) cancels it.
Dropping G is generally incorrect. The audit includes complex examples where
the omission gives defects between 0.268 and 0.849, while including G gives
roundoff-scale residuals.

Now suppose sigma=exp(-beta H)/Z with H=sum_a h_a, [h_a,h_b]=0, and F supported
on a finite region E. Let H_E be the sum of terms that do not commute with F.
Every omitted h_a commutes with H_E, F, F*, J, and K. Consequently

 J = exp(-beta H_E/2) F* exp(beta H_E/2),

and the functional calculus defining G can use ad_(H_E) in place of ad_H.
Both added operators live on the union B_E of the selected interaction supports.
Bounded range, degree, and support size make this a bounded-size calculation.
No partition function, global Gibbs matrix, spectral oracle, or physical inverse
is required. Conservative norm bounds are

 ||J|| <= exp(beta osc(H_E)/2) ||F||,
 ||G|| <= ||K||_F/2 <= sqrt(dim B_E) ||K||/2.

The Hamiltonian term of supplied noise can be compensated by adding -H_n when
that Hamiltonian is in the actual control set; its cost must be paid. For many
noise jumps, sum the completions. This proves local stationarity for the complete
Gibbs state at any finite beta. It does not prove uniqueness, a spectral gap,
logarithmic mixing, inter-sector mixing, or a native implementation. In particular,
coexisting phases and the previously established local bottleneck remain relevant.

The construction uses exact commutativity. Applying imaginary-time conjugation
to a general noncommuting many-body Hamiltonian need not preserve strict local
support or an economical finite representation. The broad noncommuting samplers
in the literature have their own access, filter, locality, and mixing conditions.

3. COMPLETE FINITE-DIMENSIONAL RESET-EMBEDDING CRITERION

This section answers a stronger, sharply specified question. Given N and omega,
when is there a Lindblad controller C such that

 N + C = kappa (R_omega - id)?                               (3.1)

Let |Omega> = sum_i |i,i>, let P=I-|Omega><Omega|/d, and use the unnormalized Choi
matrix J(T)=(T tensor id)(|Omega><Omega|). On Omega-perpendicular define

 A = P(omega tensor I)P,
 B = P J(N)P = sum_j |P vec(F_j)><P vec(F_j)|.

The equality for B shows that it is positive and independent of Hamiltonian
and jump-gauge choices. Conditional complete positivity of a trace-annihilating,
Hermiticity-preserving generator gives the exact criterion

                  kappa A - B >= 0.                         (3.2)

Indeed, J(id)=|Omega><Omega| and the Hamiltonian and anticommutator contributions
vanish under projection by P on both sides. Conversely, factor the positive
projected Choi matrix into traceless jump vectors. Subtracting their dissipators
from C leaves a trace-annihilating Hermiticity-preserving map with zero projected
Choi matrix, hence a Hamiltonian commutator. This is the standard GKSL structure
used as an algorithmic test, not a new form of quantum dynamics.

For omega>0, A is positive on this subspace and therefore

 kappa_* = lambda_max(A^(-1/2) B A^(-1/2))                    (3.3)

is finite and is the exact minimum rate for (3.1) over all Lindblad controllers.
For possibly deficient rank, a finite solution exists precisely when

                 ker(A) is contained in ker(B).             (3.4)

If (3.4) holds, use (3.3) on ran(A). Otherwise a vector in ker(A) with positive
B quadratic form is an immediate infeasibility witness. For a chosen kappa below
the threshold, a negative quadratic form of kappa A-B is a certificate.

These statements optimize the TOTAL reset rate kappa. They are not minimum power,
minimum control jump activity, minimum state-preparation time, or the optimum
over every generator with stationary state omega. A hardware rate cap or power
budget still has to be checked independently.

Explicit positive construction. Let Psi(X)=sum_j F_j X F_j* and Q=J(Psi). For

 kappa >= kappa_CP = ||(omega^(-1/2) tensor I) Q
                             (omega^(-1/2) tensor I)||,

the matrix M=kappa(omega tensor I)-Q is positive. Factor M=sum_l vec(K_l)vec(K_l)*.
Choose C=-i[-H_n,.]+sum_l D[K_l]. Since

 sum_l K_l X K_l* = kappa omega Tr(X)-Psi(X),
 sum_l K_l* K_l = kappa I-sum_j F_j*F_j,

the sum N+C is exactly (3.1). This supplies only positive additional dissipators;
it does not ask for an inverse noise channel or a negative dissipative rate.
In general kappa_CP >= kappa_*. The delivered generic emitter uses this explicit
CP construction and reports both rates as binary64 diagnostics.

A readily certified sufficient value is

 kappa >= [sum_j ||F_j||_F^2]/lambda_min(omega).              (3.5)

The correction norm activity satisfies

 ||H_n|| + sum_l ||K_l||^2
 <= ||H_n|| + Tr(M)
 = ||H_n|| + kappa d - sum_j ||F_j||_F^2.

Activity in this mathematical normalization does not substitute for energy,
bandwidth, reset throughput, or wall-clock costs of a specified physical actuator.

Exact qubit example. For N=lambda D[X] and omega=diag(1-p,p), 0<p<1,

                 kappa_* = kappa_CP = lambda/[p(1-p)].       (3.6)

The relevant 2x2 Choi block has diagonal kappa(1-p)-lambda and kappa p-lambda,
off-diagonal -lambda, and determinant kappa^2 p(1-p)-kappa lambda. Thus (3.6)
is both necessary and sufficient. At p=1/5, lambda=1, kappa_*=25/4. The package
contains an exact rational LDL witness and a negative determinant below this rate.
This is a full quantum reset requirement. Classical population balance alone
does not capture the coherence constraints.

4. MANY-BODY CONSTRUCTION AND COMPLETE PHYSICAL-OUTPUT CERTIFICATE

Let

 tau = tensor_(i=1)^N tau_i,       sigma = U tau U*,

where tau_i>0 are supplied fixed-dimensional single-site states and U is a
supplied circuit of depth D with bounded gate arity and bounded geometric range.
The worked implementation uses diagonal qubits and Clifford gates, but the
mathematical statement does not require Clifford gates. The probability spectrum
of each tau_i and the circuit itself are part of the source, not learned for free.

Transform each physical local noise term by U*. A k-site noise term lies on at
most k 2^D sites for two-site layers. On its logical light cone B, apply Section 3
to omega=tau_B. Conjugate the resulting controller back by U. The physical support
lies on the forward light cone of B, at most k 4^D sites by this crude count.
Circuit geometry gives a finite range bound as well. The conjugations here are
explicitly calculated local descriptions; an unperformed global unitary is not
silently inserted into the hardware schedule.

For uncovered sites add a paid single-site thermal reset in the logical frame,
again conjugated by U. The completed total generator is

 L_0 = U [sum_B kappa_B (R_B-id)] U*,                        (4.1)

where R_B replaces B by tau_B and leaves the other factors untouched. Positive
coverage requires r_i=sum_(B containing i) kappa_B >0 for every site i. Let
r=min_i r_i. These rates have units of inverse physical time in a warranted
native continuous-generator model.

The block resets commute and satisfy R_B R_C=R_(B union C), even when B and C
overlap. Each exponential is

 exp(t kappa_B(R_B-id)) = exp(-kappa_B t) id
                           +(1-exp(-kappa_B t)) R_B.

Expand their product as independent block-reset occurrences. On the event that
all sites have been covered, the channel is exactly replacement by tau, with all
correlations to any external reference removed. An uncovered site i has
probability exp(-r_i t). A union bound therefore proves

 (1/2)||exp(t L_0)-R_sigma||_diamond
 <= f_N(t) := min(1, sum_i exp(-r_i t))
 <= min(1,N exp(-r t)).                                     (4.2)

Consequently t >= log(N/epsilon)/r suffices for complete physical-output accuracy
epsilon. The retained register may start arbitrarily and be correlated with any
untouched auxiliary/reference. The native generator law already includes its
required controller/bath initialization and renewal; arbitrary unqualified bath
initialization is not promised. The source target
is sigma itself, with no unfinished-history admixture or heralding condition.

For fixed D, local degree, block size s, target minimum single-site eigenvalue
p_*>0, and bounded local noise amplitudes, (3.5) bounds each rate independently of
N: with qubit blocks, kappa=(2/p_*)^s sum_j ||F_j||^2 is a sufficient choice. The
number of blocks is O(N). Matrix work is exponential in s, not N; dense Choi
factorization uses O(2^(6s)) arithmetic and O(2^(4s)) storage per block. Thus
supplied-model compilation is linear in the number of bounded blocks up to local
matrix and precision costs. Native-menu optimization adds its stated LP cost.

If the native costs per bounded block and per unit activity are uniformly bounded,
parallel preparation time is O(log(N/epsilon)); total ongoing activity and supply
use through preparation are O(N log(N/epsilon)). Holding for additional time h
requires O(N h) continued activity in that regime. These are conditional resource
statements. The constants can be large, and inverse p_* or growing depth destroys
the useful uniform bounds. Equation (3.6) already forces divergence toward a pure
state. A generic deep state-preparation circuit can have an N-site light cone,
leading to exponential block computation, rates, and nonlocal actuation.

5. NATIVE-MENU DECISION, INCLUDING NEGATIVE CERTIFICATES

The previous sections do not grant arbitrary engineered jumps. Here is the
finite procedure that checks the supplied actuator set.

Stationarity task. Express each local defect G_a(tau) and N(tau) in the tensor
basis {tau_i,X_i,Y_i,Z_i} for qubits, with tau_i full rank and trace one. Each
local term has at most 4^s coefficients. Identity outside the term is replaced
by the outside product state. Distinct global tensor basis strings are linearly
independent, so gathering equal sparse strings gives exactly

                    A_stat u = b_stat.                     (5.1)

Together with the supplied affine capacity/rate constraints, (5.1) is necessary
and sufficient for stationary tau within that fixed finite generator menu.
It allows cancellation among overlapping local terms. No local-termwise
stationarity assumption is imposed. The representation has at most O(M 4^s)
rows before gathering, for M local input terms. For non-diagonal tau_i use any
computable local basis containing tau_i and traceless matrices.

Restoration task. To obtain the explicit mixing guarantee, introduce nonnegative
reset rates kappa_B and require the superoperator equality

 N + sum_a u_a G_a = sum_B kappa_B(R_B-id),
 sum_(B containing i) kappa_B >= gamma for every i.           (5.2)

Expand local superoperators in a tensor basis consisting of identity
superoperator plus 15 independent alternatives per qubit. Gather sparse strings.
The number of candidate rows is O(M 16^s). Equality of the coefficient strings
is equality of the FULL many-body generators, including coherences and reference
action; it is not a sample-state fit. Equations (5.2), rate caps, and additive
resource inequalities form an LP. An admissible solution gives (4.2) with
r>=gamma. Rational LP feasibility and Farkas duality supply finite certificates;
cost minimization is permitted when the native cost law is supplied and affine.

The basis is not the Pauli basis for operators alone: a generator equality uses
the full local superoperator basis. Mistaking the stationarity equations for
generator equality would lose the mixing and physical-reference guarantee.

The two LP questions must remain separate. Failure of (5.2) proves no embedding
of this reset family in the given menu. It does not prove failure of (5.1), or
failure of all stabilizing dynamics. Failure of (5.1) proves impossibility only
within the stipulated stationary affine controller family; it does not cover
arbitrary time dependence, feedback, auxiliaries, or different native hardware.

An exact example checks these boundaries. Let the unavoidable noise be D[X],
target p=1/5, and let native controls be rates y,z,a,b multiplying D[Y],D[Z],
D[|0><1|],D[|1><0|], respectively. Reset equality forces

 y=1, z=kappa/4, a=4 kappa/5-2, b=kappa/5-2.

Nonnegativity gives kappa>=10 and a>=6. With caps y<=6/5, z<=3, b<=1 and a<=7,
the optimum reset embedding is kappa=10, (y,z,a,b)=(1,5/2,6,0).
If only the cap on a changes to 5, reset embedding is impossible. Nevertheless
the target is stationary with a=3 and y=z=b=0. If the cap changes to 2, even
stationarity is impossible: stationarity forces a=3+3y+4b>=3. The package includes
these exact rational certificates, as well as numerical LP runs reproducing them.

For uncertain matrices, exact feasibility at estimated coefficients is not a
robust certificate. Use certified enclosures and a residual bound, or solve the
appropriate robust constraints when their uncertainty model is warranted.
An arbitrary unknown dependence of the apparatus on the chosen controls cannot
be replaced by an affine law because the LP is convenient.

5A. A COMPLETE OPERATING REGIME WITH ACTUATOR SELF-NOISE

An explicit noisy native law makes the positive result more concrete than an
unrestricted engineered-dissipator assumption. In each product-frame factor let
the warranted native generator be

 L_i(a_i) = lambda_i D[X_i] + a_i (D[|0><1|_i] + e_i D[X_i]),
 lambda_i>0, e_i>=0, 0<=a_i<=a_i,max.                        (5.3)

In the physical system every displayed operator is dressed by the SAME supplied
U. Thus the actual native operators have bounded support when U has bounded
depth. This is a particular physical class and joint actuator law. It is not a
claim that arbitrary lab amplitude damping or arbitrary physical Pauli noise has
this alignment. The parameter e_i prices noise generated by running the actuator
itself. It is not dropped when its control rate increases.

For a desired tau_i=diag(1-p_i,p_i), with 0<p_i<1/2, define

 d_i=p_i(1+2e_i)-e_i.

The target is attainable by (5.3) with finite rate precisely when d_i>0 and

 a_i = lambda_i(1-2p_i)/d_i <= a_i,max.                      (5.4)

Equivalently, the strict infinite-rate floor is p_i>e_i/(1+2e_i), and the finite
cap raises the minimum attainable population to

 (lambda_i+e_i a_i,max)/(2lambda_i+(1+2e_i)a_i,max).

Proof and complete output. Put w_i=lambda_i+e_i a_i. The Bloch equations are

 x'=-a_i x/2,
 y'=-(a_i/2+2w_i)y,
 z'=a_i-(a_i+2w_i)z.

Their unique fixed state has p_i=w_i/(a_i+2w_i), giving (5.4). The slowest decay
is g_i=a_i/2. For any initial state with any untouched reference, expand it as
one half of I tensor rho_R plus X,Y,Z tensor Hermitian coefficient operators.
Each coefficient has trace norm at most one. The three decay factors and the
fixed-state displacement give the complete one-factor bound

 (1/2)||exp(t L_i)-R_(tau_i)||_diamond <= min(1,2 exp(-g_i t)).

Telescoping tensor products and conjugating by U therefore proves, with
g=min_i a_i/2,

 (1/2)||exp(t sum_i L_i)-R_sigma||_diamond
 <= min(1,2N exp(-g t)).                                   (5.5)

This is a physical-output guarantee with the actuator's nonzero modeled noise
retained. An arbitrarily initialized source needs t>=log(2N/epsilon)/g. Exact
maximally mixed p_i=1/2 is excluded from this sole-actuator result: it gives
a_i=0 and the remaining X noise does not erase X coherences. A second native
dephasing direction would be an additional, separately specified control.

If lambda_i, e_i, the distance above the floor, the distance below 1/2, native
costs, and the depth/support parameters are uniformly controlled, this is an
O(N)-description, O(N)-command computation, O(log(N/epsilon))-parallel-time,
O(N log(N/epsilon))-activity regime. It covers entangled physical mixed states
under U; it does not cover arbitrary long-range, nearly pure or collective
targets by a change of notation. Reset/supply, control electronics and storage
costs must still satisfy the native contract (5.3).

For the exact rational model p=1/5, lambda=1 and e=1/10, a=30/7, g=15/7, the
self-noise floor is 1/12, and total jump norm activity is 40/7 per factor. A cap
of 5 permits the target; a cap of 4 does not. A requested p=1/20 is impossible
even without a cap. For N=10^6 and epsilon=0.01, (5.5) gives time 8.919786365
in the declared model units, using approximately 5.097e7 total norm-activity
units. Neither number has been converted to seconds, joules, or device cycles.
No warranted hardware conversion or experimental law is supplied in this run.

The same variation-of-constants proof as Section 6 gives continuing residual
floor 2 eta(1+log(2N))/g for this family; use this expression, not the reset
family's specific r. Known e=1/10 in (5.3) is a retained nonzero noise parameter;
unknown deviations from (5.3) are still part of eta. It is not a universal ten
percent hardware threshold.

6. RESIDUAL ERROR: GLOBAL ACCURACY AND LOCAL STABILITY

Let the actual evolution have generator L_0+Delta(t), and suppose its propagator
is CPTP. Include every residual in Delta: model mismatch, calibration drift,
approximate synthesis, control timing, ongoing storage, reset and selection.
Suppose

 eta = sup_t (1/2)||Delta(t)||_diamond < infinity.

Variation of constants, trace annihilation of Delta, and (4.2) give uniformly
for arbitrary references and initialization

 T(rho_SR(t), sigma tensor rho_R)
 <= f_N(t) + 2 eta (1+log N)/r.                             (6.1)

Proof. In the integral comparison, the perturbation output has zero partial
trace on the system, so replacement by sigma annihilates it. The remaining
ideal propagator has diamond norm at most 2 f_N(s). Integrate f_N(s) <=
min(1,N exp(-rs)); its integral is at most (1+log N)/r. This proves (6.1)
also for time-dependent errors without assuming the perturbed dynamics has a
stationary state. The estimate is conservative; it is a sufficient certificate,
not a claim that every hardware error has this worst scaling.

If Delta=sum_E Delta_E, then eta<=sum_E eta_E, with eta_E bounding each half-
diamond generator error. A fixed per-site residual does not give a fixed global
trace-distance tolerance as N grows. For M=O(N) bounded local terms, (6.1) permits
eta_E=O(epsilon r/(N log N)), whereas the modeled noise itself can remain O(1).
This is the essential distinction between modeled noise absorption and an
unknown-error hardware threshold.

There is also an economical LOCAL output guarantee, separately labelled. Work
in the product frame and let A contain a sites. Its ideal Heisenberg evolution
stays on A. Let eta_A=sum_(E intersecting A) eta_E, after transforming the errors
into this frame. The same calculation, with all disjoint error terms vanishing,
gives a complete local-output bound

 T(rho_AR(t), tau_A tensor rho_R)
 <= min(1,a exp(-rt)) + 2 eta_A (1+log a)/r.                (6.2)

In the physical frame, enlarge a requested observable's support by the supplied
U light cone. At bounded degree, eta_A is bounded independently of N for a fixed
local observable. This direct no-spreading proof is a special case of the broad
local-stability principle studied by Cubitt, Lucia, Michalakis, and Perez-Garcia;
their held full original is not used as a premise here. Equation (6.2) is not
substituted for the global physical-output requirement (6.1). It does not by
itself promise vanishing macroscopic fluctuations in a perturbed N-body state.

7. TWO EXACT LIMITS ON THE LARGER ENDPOINT

Pure-output obstruction in the presence of outgoing noise. Let P=|psi><psi|
be a desired exact physical state, with arbitrary controller auxiliaries. Any
joint state with this physical marginal has form P tensor rho_A. For an additive
unmonitored physical jump F, its instantaneous leakage out of P is

 Tr[(I-P) D[F](P)] = <F*F>_psi - |<F>_psi|^2 >= 0.

Every additional positive dissipator has nonnegative leakage from that support;
a Hamiltonian has zero first-order leakage there. Thus exact stationarity of
this pure physical output is impossible whenever a supplied noise jump has
strictly positive variance on psi. Auxiliaries do not change this conclusion.
Encoded logical recovery and approximate physical output are different tasks.
Monitoring access that changes the effective noise instrument is also a change
of resource contract. This support argument does not rule out useful finite
accuracy or fault-tolerant encoded computation.

Noise information is necessary. A single fixed controller C that exactly fixes
sigma for both N_1 and N_2 must obey

                    (N_1-N_2)(sigma)=0.                    (7.1)

Conversely, if (7.1) holds, any controller stabilizing one also makes the target
stationary for the other, though mixing still needs its own proof. Therefore
unknown noise directions that do not preserve sigma cannot be absorbed exactly
by one fixed uncalibrated controller. Online inference is possible only with
its observation, identifiability, time, and intervention costs paid.

A simple global-accuracy obstruction already occurs in product states. For
omega_p=diag(1-p,p), omega_q=diag(1-q,q), put

 b=sqrt(pq)+sqrt((1-p)(1-q)).

The root fidelity of their N-fold products is b^N, and

 1-b^N <= T(omega_p^tensor N,omega_q^tensor N)
          <= sqrt(1-b^(2N)).                                (7.2)

For any fixed q!=p, the global distance tends to one while each single-site
distance remains |p-q|. Conjugation by any supplied U preserves this global
distance. Fixed local calibration tolerance therefore does not entail fixed
whole-state accuracy, even in the favorable rapidly mixing family. This is an
exact statistical distinction, not a complexity-theory conjecture.

8. ACQUISITION AND FINITE INFORMATION COST

A justified supplied model is legitimate. It still has a provenance and reading
cost: O(M) local records of their stated dimensions and precision, the target
probabilities, the full U description, the native joint law, and cost/cap data.
The present examples are supplied mathematical models, not measured records.

An optional finite acquisition route can be costed without asserting an unknown
apparatus oracle. Suppose the apparatus is known to have a finite affine local
generator model L(theta)=sum_j theta_j L_j, with warranted bounds on local
activity, available calibrated preparations rho_l and measurements O_l, and
resolved local support. For actual allowed probes form the response design

 A_lj = Tr[O_l L_j(rho_l)].

Compute its rank and a left inverse on the identifiable quotient. Nonidentifiable
directions or an unbounded conditioning cost are explicit acquisition failures,
not parameters to be assumed away. No claim of universal identifiability from
one restricted preparation/measurement interface is made.

Measure f_l(h)=Tr[O_l exp(hL)(rho_l)] and f_l(0), with ||O_l||<=1. A supplied
bound B_2 on ||(L*)^2(O_l)|| gives derivative bias at most B_2 h/2. For bounded
local norms, degree and observable support, B_2 is computable without an N-body
matrix: only pairs of generator terms whose supports successively intersect the
observable contribute. If every term has norm at most ell, each site meets at
most nu terms, terms have size at most s, and the observable has support a,
the conservative bound B_2<=nu^2 a(a+s) ell^2 is sufficient.

For desired derivative error r_d and B_2>0, choose h=r_d/B_2. Estimate both bounded
means with sampling error at most r_d h/8 and separately warrant systematic mean
error at most r_d h/8. Hoeffding's inequality and a union bound over m settings
allow

 R >= 128 B_2^2 r_d^(-4) log(4m/zeta)

fresh trials per mean, with failure probability at most zeta. Thus at most 2mR
preparations, readouts and paid h-duration evolutions suffice under these probe
conditions. A computed norm C_A of the chosen left inverse translates derivative
error to generator error, eta<=C_A r_d. Known preparation, readout and timing
systematics must fit the stated systematic mean-error budget; reducing h amplifies them.
The routine cannot certify a systematic error below its warranted SPAM floor.

If B_2=0 the derivative bias vanishes and any warranted positive probing time can
be used with its corresponding sampling budget. Trials require independent fresh
preparations and the stated stable Markov model. Finite tomography alone does not
establish absence of environmental memory, time homogeneity, or the simultaneous
native affine law; these remain warranted model premises with residual budgets.

This is a finite conservative acquisition schedule, not a practical commissioning
claim. For global output accuracy, (6.1) can make r_d extremely small, with a
quartic sample penalty in this elementary schedule. Common coefficients may
reduce the number of independent unknowns only when their commonality is a
physical premise. Uncalibrated spatial homogeneity is not supplied by a drawing
of a repeated circuit. No acquisition experiment was performed in this work.

There is a better finite-time acquisition route for the fully specified family
(5.3), when its calibrated preparation/readout probes are actually available.
At one fixed command, let u=P(1 at h | initial 0) and v=P(0 at h | initial 1).
Then, writing s=u+v, the exact model equations are

 R=-log(1-s)/h,  p_infinity=u/s,
 w=R u/s,       a=R(v-u)/s.                                (8.1)

At the off setting, a=0 and R=2lambda. At a nonzero setting, (8.1) and that
lambda give e=(w-lambda)/a. Thus two population preparations at two command
settings identify these parameters within the warranted model. Coherence
behaviour is part of the full model premise; population data alone do not
establish it. If it must be checked, additional calibrated X/Y probes, channel
model validation, and their costs are required.

This acquisition uses finite h, so there is no finite-difference bias or h->0
amplification. Keep explicit conditioning margins s>=s_0>0, 1-s>=m_0>0 and
active a>=a_0>0. They are substantive; near s=0, s=1 or a=0 the inverse is
ill-conditioned. Given population-estimation error delta<=min(s_0,m_0)/4,
the inverse stays in s>=s_0/2, 1-s>=m_0/2. For example, writing
R_max=-log(m_0/2)/h, direct derivatives give

 |delta R| <= [4/(h m_0)] delta,
 |delta p_infinity| <= (4/s_0) delta,
 |delta w| <= [4/(h m_0)+4 R_max/s_0] delta,
 |delta a| <= [4/(h m_0)+8 R_max/s_0] delta.

The quotient for e then has its explicit inverse-a conditioning; provided the
estimated a stays at least a_0/2, use
 |delta e| <= 2(|delta w|+|delta lambda|+e_max |delta a|)/a_0.
For k independently estimated probabilities, Hoeffding permits

 R_trials >= [log(2k/zeta)]/(2 delta^2)

trials per probability for sampling error delta, with any systematic error
budget allocated separately. This is an O(delta^-2) schedule at fixed margins,
not the generic derivative schedule's O(r_d^-4). It requires actual fresh
preparations, qualified readouts, timing, and a stable affine noise law. The
provided executable checks (8.1) and its sampling-cost formula on synthetic
population probabilities; it performs no physical acquisition.

At a fixed commanded a, the exact stationary population is
 p=(lambda+ea)/(2lambda+(1+2e)a).
Its derivatives with respect to lambda,e,a are a/R^2,a^2/R^2,-lambda/R^2.
Thus calibration and actuation sensitivity are computable in this native regime.
At the rational example they are 0.084,0.36,-0.0196, respectively. Whole-state
accuracy still has the information distinction (7.2). Common parameters may be
estimated with a common record only when commonality is warranted; otherwise
each independent local record and its union confidence must be paid.

A sharper whole-state calibration certificate is available within (5.3), so the
generic O(1/(N log N)) per-term residual budget need not be imposed on these
structured parameter errors. Suppose simultaneous confidence intervals give
lambda_i in [l_i,L_i], e_i in [e_i^-,e_i^+], and actual actuator rate in
[a_i^-,a_i^+] with a_i^->0. The stationary population q increases with lambda
and e and decreases with a. Its exact interval is therefore

 q_i^- = (l_i+e_i^- a_i^+) / (2l_i+(1+2e_i^-)a_i^+),
 q_i^+ = (L_i+e_i^+ a_i^-) / (2L_i+(1+2e_i^+)a_i^-).

Let z_i=max(|q_i^--p_i|,|q_i^+-p_i|) and
v_i=min(p_i(1-p_i),q_i^-(1-q_i^-),q_i^+(1-q_i^+))>0. The elementary Taylor
bound D(Ber(p)||Ber(q))<=(p-q)^2/(2v), using second derivative 1/[x(1-x)]
between p and q, additivity for product states,
Pinsker's inequality and unitary invariance give the COMPLETE output bound

 T(rho_SR(t),sigma tensor rho_R)
 <= min(1,2 sum_i exp(-a_i^- t/2))
       + min(1, sqrt(sum_i z_i^2/(4v_i))).                  (8.2)

Additional errors outside the calibrated model receive their own implementation
or continuing-perturbation budget. The second term is a stationary target-model
bound; it is not multiplied by the mixing time. With rational confidence
endpoints, its SQUARED bound is rational and can be certified exactly, without
forming any 2^N matrix. It gives sufficient population precision O(epsilon/
sqrt(N)) when the v_i are bounded below. Equation (7.2) shows why a constant
population bias is inadequate. This is a structured improvement over the fully
adversarial generator-norm budget, not a generic estimator lower bound.

At fixed inverse-design conditioning, (8.1) and (8.2) give O(N/epsilon^2) trials
per independent probability parameter, with the stated logarithmic confidence

9. COMPILATION, EXECUTION, RESET SUPPLY, AND HOLDING

The mathematical compiler uses only finite local linear algebra. For rational
data, choose a rational rate above a certified PSD bound and use exact LDL
factorization to emit positive rational weights and rational jump vectors.
The weights may be kept outside the jumps, so a square root need not be encoded
as an exact hardware amplitude. A specified finite gate alphabet approximates
the resulting small channels with a separately allocated error budget. For
computable real data use interval enclosures and a positive feasibility margin.

The delivered generic emitter uses binary64 eigenvectors and arithmetic. Its
floating residuals are numerical audits, not interval proofs or a universal
finite-alphabet physical compiler. The exact qubit certificate uses Fraction
arithmetic independently. The code explicitly distinguishes kappa_CP from the
general projected-Choi optimum and distinguishes stationarity from reset equality.

There are three physical implementation possibilities, none interchangeable:

* A warranted native affine generator menu realizes the successful LP commands.
  Its local rates, continuous bandwidth, cooling/reset supply and activity costs
  are the physical resource input. Arbitrary computed jumps are not automatically
  members of this menu.
* A known labelled gate/reset interface can simulate finite local dissipative
  maps, with Stinespring/ancilla preparation and synthesis charged. This is a
  logical simulation route. Noise acting during gates, ancilla storage, random
  selection and reset remains in its implementation error; ideal simulation
  does not establish physical addition of the computed generator to unknown noise.
* A repeated-interaction or weak-collision construction needs fresh ancillary
  systems and sufficiently fast coupling. For a jump rate a and time step h,
  the interaction angle is O(sqrt(a h)); treating that angle as instantaneous
  while letting h tend to zero hides a strength O(sqrt(a/h)). We do not use that
  limit to claim bounded physical bandwidth.

In a digital simulation, if Q implemented operations contribute half-diamond
errors e_j, their total is bounded by sum_j e_j. Gate synthesis, reset, random
selection, classical branching, probability generation and idle intervals all
contribute; the actual physical implementation need not obey the ideal numerical
generator model. Rational target probabilities can be sampled with paid random
bits/rejection, or approximated with a certified tail/error. Neither random-number
generation nor a continuous Poisson clock is free.

Repeated output. By (4.2), after each extraction or disturbance one can restore
again from an arbitrary state, including correlations with previously retained
outputs. For m restorations with complete errors epsilon_j, the joint delivered
state differs from the product target by at most sum_j epsilon_j, before adding
transport, swaps, and subsequent storage errors. Producing m separate N-site
outputs requires the corresponding fresh physical output capacity or a warranted
replacement procedure. Old outputs are not protected merely because the source
register continues restoring. They need their own qualified storage/holding costs.

A full cost expression therefore includes

 C_source + C_model_acquisition + C_compilation + C_native_setup
 + integral_0^(t_prep+t_hold) C_activity(u(t),reset,selection,idle) dt
 + C_output_transfer + C_output_storage.

An abstract O(N log(N/epsilon)) activity bound becomes an economical physical
operation only when these native terms and the required calibration accuracy
are actually warranted in the regime of use. They were not measured here.

11. SCIENTIFIC CONSEQUENCES AND PROGRAMME CONTINUITY

Faithful target weight creates room for a positive completion of known noise.
The exact local criterion identifies the necessary rate, and the local
representation can prevent this calculation from inheriting the full Hilbert-
space cost. The pure limit, local support growth, and actuator cone each remove
that advantage in a different, explicit way.

Exact target stationarity and rapid restoration are different mathematical
questions. Commuting Gibbs structure suffices for local completion of arbitrary
supplied local jumps, but the general phase/mixing problem remains. The bounded-
depth product representation supplies a complete rapid-restoration mechanism
with an unconditional output and external-reference guarantee.

Full physical state control and decoded logical protection remain distinct.
The inspected Astra N517 card assumes supplied encoded resources, decoder
contraction, a whole fault-cylinder norm condition, and charged resets/idle/
controllers. No such encoded result was promoted to a raw physical-output
guarantee. Shtanko et al.'s autonomous correction bounds likewise have an explicit
logical-decoder contract. No new peer operation was assumed.

The favourable operating regime is real as a mathematical conditional capability:
bounded-depth faithful targets, warranted bounded-support Markovian data, and
an attainable local control cone with paid rates and supplies. It is not a
universal economical commissioning theorem. Unknown residuals, native actuator
availability, and long-range or nearly pure target structure remain substantive.

The retained programme now has an explicit interface question: which warranted
native noisy processes have a sufficiently rich, calibrated local control cone
at acceptable global information and holding costs? This return resolves the
finite supplied-menu decision and restoration operation, and proves why neither
an uncalibrated arbitrary apparatus nor arbitrary-state physical storage follows.
The broader endpoint remains owned and unfinished; no narrower output contract
or synthetic fixture is used to declare it closed. No P=NP claim follows.
