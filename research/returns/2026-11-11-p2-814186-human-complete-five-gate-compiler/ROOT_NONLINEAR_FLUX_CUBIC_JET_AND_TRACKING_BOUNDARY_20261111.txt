Nonlinear flux response: an actual cubic jet and a common-tracking boundary
ROOT, 11 November 2026 Brisbane

This note constructs a nonzero cubic Fourier response of the coupled MHD
equations, at the level of an actual third Taylor coefficient. It does NOT
construct a finite-amplitude coefficient-blind cubic gate or a polynomial
response compiler. It also proves why arbitrary uniform-in-time tracking of
both fields is an unsuitable automatic successor to common endpoint control.

1. THE SYMMETRIES OF THE FULL INPUT-OUTPUT QUESTION

For a mechanical force independent of initial magnetic flux, the equations
are invariant under (u,B,h_u,h_B)->(u,-B,h_u,-h_B). After a common eraser has
removed unknown mean-zero initial information, a prescribed deterministic
response of the conserved H must therefore have velocity even in H and
magnetic field odd in H, if it is to work for the entire symmetric input class.
The magnetic output must have mean H and be divergence-free. At H=0 the
zero magnetic realization is invariant, so a nonzero magnetic target at zero
flux is impossible. These necessary conditions do not establish sufficiency.

A linear response Pi(x)H has no nonlinear dependence on H. A
prescribed passive velocity, even when it is very complicated, still gives a
linear induction map. Nonlinear response must use the actual Lorentz feedback
or another genuinely nonlinear operation, rather than rename passive gates.

2. AN EXACT HIGH-TRIAD CUBIC COEFFICIENT

Set kappa=2pi, m=e3. For an integer Q>=2 use
 k1=Q e1, k2=Q e2, k3=-Q(e1+e2)+e3,
 b1=e2, b2=e3, b3=e1-e2.
Then k_j.b_j=0 and k1+k2+k3=m. Consider the REAL smooth initial field
 B(0,x)=H+theta Q sum_(j=1)^3 [H_j b_j exp(i kappa k_j.x)+conjugate],
 u(0,x)=0,
with zero nominal force and disturbance. Its mean is H and it is solenoidal.
The amplitudes are linear in H, so this is a legitimate matrix-response
reference. Actual H1 preparation of this reference is a separate service;
an L2 endpoint theorem alone is not silently strengthened to supply it.

First take constant positive nu,rho. The calculation below isolates the
third coefficient in theta, NOT a uniform bound on the finite-theta remainder.
In the high-frequency limit the constant-H Alfven coupling is smaller by
O(|H|/Q) on the diffusive fast time kappa^2 Q^2 t. It therefore does not
change the leading coefficient. With that coupling omitted only in this
leading limiting calculation, the first magnetic coefficient is scalar heat.
For an unordered pair a,b of the three high modes define
 F_ab=P_(k_a+k_b)[(b_a.k_b)b_b+(b_b.k_a)b_a],
 D_ab,c=(b_c.m)F_ab-(F_ab.m)b_c,
 S=|k1|^2+|k2|^2+|k3|^2.
The quadratic velocity coefficient at k_a+k_b has the exact convolution
 i kappa Q^2 H_a H_b F_ab
 times int_0^s exp[-kappa^2 nu|k_a+k_b|^2(s-r)]
              exp[-kappa^2 rho(|k_a|^2+|k_b|^2)r] dr.

The low induction derivative uses m, not Q: solenoidality gives
 b_c.(k_a+k_b)=b_c.m and F_ab.k_c=F_ab.m.
Its second time integral, with negligible low-mode heat over a vanishing
physical window, is exactly
 J_ab,c=1/{kappa^4 [nu|k_a+k_b|^2+rho|k_c|^2] rho S}
when the fast upper time is extended to infinity. This follows by setting
s=r+v and integrating the two positive exponentials separately. Consequently
the contribution at m is
 -kappa^2 Q^3 H1 H2 H3 sum_(pairs ab) D_ab,c J_ab,c.       (1)

Choose an observation window T_Q=J_Q/Q^2 where J_Q tends to infinity
logarithmically, so T_Q tends to zero. Truncation of the fast integrals is
exponentially small in mu J_Q, mu=min(nu,rho). The retained output m is
changed by only O(T_Q) low heat. Thus the stated infinite-fast-time integral
is the actual limiting third coefficient of the finite-time PDE response.
For each finite Q it is obtained by differentiating the ordinary smooth
local solution at theta=0; no hidden velocity assignment is used.

After division of F_ab by Q, the limiting pair values are
 F_12/Q=e3, F_13/Q=-e1, F_23/Q=-e3.
The corresponding D/Q values are
 -(e1-e2), -e1, +e2,
and |k3/Q|^2=2, |k2/Q|^2=|k1/Q|^2=1. Their weighted sum is
 -(3/2)(e1-e2).
Since S/Q^2 tends to4, (1) gives the NONZERO limiting coefficient
 [theta^3 exp(i kappa x3)] B(T_Q)
   -> 3 H1 H2 H3 (e1-e2)/[8 kappa^2 rho(nu+rho)].          (2)
The real cosine coefficient is twice (2), with the conjugate convention above.
No other choice of signs of these three high carriers gives the same slow
m at third order. The coefficient is a genuine trilinear product of the
three unknown flux components, not a linear matrix response in disguise.

The small constant-H coupling can be retained in every finite-dimensional
Fourier Duhamel coefficient. In fast variables its norm is O(M/Q), and
finite products of its integral perturbations tend to zero on these windows.
For unknown Lipschitz profiles on a window beginning at t0, freezing each
coefficient in these FINITELY MANY third-order integrals changes its
diffusive exponent by at most C Lambda Q^2 T_Q^2. The leading coefficient
therefore has nu(t0),rho(t0) in (2), uniformly on supplied positive intervals.
This does not compute or measure either coefficient in an emitted programme.

An independent exact rational calculation of (1), factoring out kappa^-2,
at nu=rho=1 gives first components 0.1855629618,0.1873779930,
0.1874923709,0.1874995232 for Q=8,32,128,512, tending to3/16.
This checks the finite-dimensional arithmetic and signs only; it is not a
PDE simulation, a finite-amplitude error theorem or an additional audit gate.

3. WHAT THIS OPERATION DOES NOT YET SUPPLY

The coefficient 1/[rho(nu+rho)] is positive and bounded on the supplied
rectangle, but UNKNOWN. Positivity is not coefficient-blind normalization.
Signed controllable composition or another actual ensemble operation would
have to remove this nuisance dependence without measuring the material.

At critical packet amplitude theta Q, fifth and higher odd low interactions
are not automatically killed by Q->infinity. Increasing Q is not a proof of
an isolated finite cubic gate. A uniform nonlinear remainder bound, treatment
of a pre-existing smooth memory, actual H1 preparation/reset services,
unknown-profile compensation and physical twelve-actuator lowering all
remain necessary. Repeating a tiny cubic operation requires paid accumulated
error/population/amplitude/time budgets. A formal polynomial Lie algebra
does not give a universal polynomial degree in a variable target degree.

The carrier amplitudes, frequencies and required preparation tolerances can
all be explicitly charged; no coefficient inverse depending on an unknown
H_j is used. The expression (2) itself vanishes when any input component is
zero. Additional polarization/representation work is needed for every cubic
monomial and for arbitrary even velocity outputs. A restriction to the
displayed triad would not answer that whole nonlinear output question.

4. WHY GENERAL COMMON CONTINUOUS TRACKING IS A FALSE NEXT STEP

Suppose a family of ordinary common controls, as epsilon->0, made both fields
converge in L-infinity([t0,t1];L2) to smooth targets V,W independent of the
unknown coefficients, for EVERY admitted constant pair nu,rho. Take zero
disturbances. Products converge in L1, since the L2 norms remain bounded.
Passing to distributions in the induction equation for two different rho
values gives
 W_t=curl(V cross W)+rho1 Delta W
    =curl(V cross W)+rho2 Delta W.
Hence Delta W=0. On the torus W is spatially constant, and conserved mean
requires W=H. This already rules out arbitrary Pi(t,x)H tracking.

Use two different nu values and subtract their momentum equations. The
COMMON applied force cancels exactly, even if its norm grows with epsilon.
All quadratic terms have the same L1 limit, so (nu1-nu2)Delta V=0. Mean-zero
velocity then forces V=0. These arguments apply on any nontrivial interior
time interval and do not need a bound on the size of the applied force.

This is an obstruction to that STRONGER simultaneous uniform-time target,
not to the current endpoint theorem, stroboscopic operation, separately
specified compatible trajectories or a nonlinear ENDPOINT response compiler.
It is a substantive reason to investigate nonlinear endpoint programming
rather than automatically ask for arbitrary tracking of the two outputs.
