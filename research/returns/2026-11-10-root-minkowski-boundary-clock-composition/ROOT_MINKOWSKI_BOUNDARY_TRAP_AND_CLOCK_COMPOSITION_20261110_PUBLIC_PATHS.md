3. BOUNDED REACTION WORDS HAVE PAID PROBABILITIES, BUT NO FREE INWARD DRIFT
Fix a finite allowed design with J labelled channels, maximum source degree D, maximum jump L1 size B_R, and a length-m enabled word e1,...,em from x. The conditional probability that the next m ACTUAL labelled firings are exactly the word is therefore at least
 [kappa_min/(4J(1+N+mB_R)^D)]^m.
For fixed m this is a reciprocal polynomial in V_lambda and N. Conditional expected holding time for those m firings is <=2m/kappa_min, since exponential holding time and jump label are independent given the current state and total intensity is at least one enabled intensity. These are genuine finite-bit/numerical resource bounds for an ALREADY supplied bounded word.

They are not a recurrence theorem. A failed trial can change the population, enablement pattern and future failure cost; the negative successful displacement may be outweighed by the other paths.
