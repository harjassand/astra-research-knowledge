# Finite early- and late-record readers with explicit selector families

These implementations earn the [causal payload-memory theorem's](README.md) encoding family, early arbitrary-reference recovery and complete resource bill. Read [small-bias instantiation](package/proofs/small_bias_instantiation.txt), the separate [local-expander construction](package/proofs/local_expander_instantiation.txt), [independent audit](package/proofs/late_selector_independent_audit.txt) and [emission-error refinement](package/proofs/emission_error_refinement.txt). Errors are half diamond distance.

## Constant-gap family with a logarithmic control record

For n gauge qubits, choose `k=ceil(log2(4n-2))`, `q=2^k`, and uniform a,b in `GF(q)`. Bits `x_l=tr(a b^l)`, `l=0,...,2n-1`, with `b^0=1`, form a small-bias Pauli program: every nontrivial character has absolute bias at most `(2n-1)/q<=1/2`. Pair the bits into a Hermitian Pauli string P. Combine a `Z_flag tensor I` anchor with weight `1/5` and `X_flag tensor P` with total weight `4/5`. The full complex-matrix commutator gap is **exactly `4/5`**. The anchor is indispensable; X-only families do not have the claimed gap.

A padded physical record has `2k+1` bits. It is an emitter encoding program, not a supplied bath purification or hidden initialization key. The source retains one coherent flag, applies one specified controlled Pauli to each fresh tracial carrier and releases that carrier once, then uses a paid full SWAP to export the flag. Classical field/seed/counter state costs `O(log n)`; calling the entire source state space constant-dimensional would be inaccurate. Gate slots/output timing and routing are padded independently of selector, with no other program side channel supplied.

With the record early, measure each nonidentity Pauli factor of each incoming carrier in its local eigenbasis, accumulate the product eigenvalue as one parity bit, discard that carrier, and apply `H Z^b` to the arriving flag. This returns the arbitrary input and reference exactly in the ideal model. Outcome probabilities are payload-independent. The early reader works for any independent gauge state, even internally correlated with an inaccessible purifier; the **lower-bound experiment** uses the specified tracial gauge. No particle returns to the source. The late reader simply stores the same stream and performs the same local measurements/correction after the record arrives.

The early receiver has one current carrier and constant meter/output workspace, a parity bit and `O(log n)` classical program state. It performs `O(n)` quantum operations/readouts/resets, `O(n log^2 n)` elementary classical work, and `O(n)` coherent receiver qubit-time at fixed cadence. Finite field setup can additionally cost `O(n log^3 n+log^4 n)` classical work. The source flag remains protected through the stream. Strong finite controlled gates, finite peak/slew, routing, readout, reset, flight, settling and actual timing are resources. If the source can instead be commanded to export the original payload immediately by full SWAP, that simpler operation changes the stipulated fixed-emitter program.

## Separate five-bit fixed-register family

The supplied [local-expander note](package/proofs/local_expander_instantiation.txt) imports sixteen explicit local Hermitian Pauli/CNOT layers with absolute gap `1/(2*10^8)` from its pinned primary theorem. Adding the Z anchor gives seventeen labels, five record bits and `kappa0=16/(17*10^8)`. At `epsilon<=kappa0/16`, approximately `5.88235e-10`, the stated extensive-capacity consequence follows. **This is not the one-percent constant-gap small-bias family.** Its tiny certified threshold cannot be replaced by `4/5`.

Ten coherent source registers suffice for the supplied fixed-order boundary/look-ahead/flag implementation, plus fixed classical selector and an external `O(n)`-instruction time program. Internal timing instead costs an `O(log n)` counter. Actual ordering and look-ahead are supplied, not inferred from logical depth one. CNOT eigenmeasurement uses its product control-Z/target-X basis, so the early receiver need not keep a coherent pair buffer just to measure it.

## Precision, qualification and a score-only certificate

Preparation, record mistakes, loss, local gate/readout error, storage and timing add compositionally. A sufficient uniform per-operation error allocation is `O(epsilon/n)`; fixed raw error per gate is not a scalable constant-error theorem. Fault-tolerant protection adds its physical resources. Independent tracial factories and inaccessible keys require a whole-state error contract; good local marginals alone do not suffice. The supplied independent-qubit preparation bound `.5 sqrt(product_i(1+beta_i^2)-1)` is sufficient with `beta_i=O(nu/sqrt n)`.

Generic stationary gate qualification at the declared precision costs `O(n^2 epsilon^-2 log(1/delta))` trials up front. End-to-end averaged qubit-channel qualification uses `O(zeta^-2 log(1/delta))` full source/receiver runs with trusted Bell/reference resources. These conditional calibration contracts do not rule out unmodelled quantum side channels or certify source timing merely from final-channel agreement.

A separate trusted binary-probe experiment `(I+-H_j)/N` with late selector and average signed score s yields `m/N>=1-(2/kappa)(1-s^2)`. For `kappa=.8`, the supplied calculation gives 300 independent zero-failure trials as sufficient for a `.9` dimension-fraction certificate at confidence `.95`, assuming zero trusted preparation/score bias. It is a **memory witness**, not full arbitrary-payload recovery, and each trial pays the whole `O(n)` preparation/stream. This point was calculated, not physically executed.

At n=1000 the field polynomial `x^12+x^3+1` and one 1000-site program were generated classically; no 1000-qubit channel was simulated.
