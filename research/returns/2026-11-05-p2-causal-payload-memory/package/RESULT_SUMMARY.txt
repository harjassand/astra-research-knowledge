FACTUAL RESEARCH RETURN: CAUSAL MEMORY FOR ONE QUANTUM PAYLOAD
5 November 2026

COMPLETED CONSEQUENCE
====================

One arbitrary qubit of payload can require essentially the entire quantum
output stream to be retained until a short classical source-program record
arrives. The same record delivered before that stream enables exact causal
recovery with constant quantum workspace and linear quantum processing.

The result is a dimension-uniform finite-error memory theorem plus a complete
physical stream/receiver construction. It concerns recovery of the payload
and arbitrary reference, not compression of every mixed-output correlation.
The homogeneous weak partial-SWAP receiver remains open. No general
finite-memory impossibility for that channel is established here.

CONVENTIONS AND INTERFACE
=========================

d_diamond(A,B)=||A-B||_diamond/2 throughout.

Let B have dimension N=2D. A selector j is independent of the arbitrary qubit
payload Q and reference R. The emitted encoding is

  E_j(rho)=V_j(rho tensor I_D/D)V_j^dagger.

All B arrives before j in the hard interface. A common compression C may
retain an unlimited dephased classical record and quantum dimension at most
m in each branch. Every coherent ancilla, entanglement half and delay line
crossing the cut counts. No source-receiver initial entanglement is supplied.
After j arrives the reader may use an arbitrary different decoder D_j.

Only selector-averaged recovery is needed:

  d_diamond(sum_j p_j D_j C E_j, id_Q)<=epsilon, 0<=epsilon<=1/2.

A flagged or worst-selector guarantee implies this premise. Discarded
failure outcomes count. The fixed source does not accept feedback changing
its future program or record schedule. Receiver-internal adaptive processing
and unrestricted collective compression are covered.

MAIN THEOREM
============

Let H_j=V_j(Z tensor I_D)V_j^dagger be balanced Hermitian involutions. Suppose
for every COMPLEX N by N matrix K, in unnormalised Hilbert-Schmidt norm,

  sum_j p_j ||[K,H_j]||_2^2
    >= kappa ||K-Tr(K)I/N||_2^2.

Then

  m/N >= max{0, 1-8 epsilon(1-epsilon)/kappa}.

The proof uses only the average discrimination bias of the binary encoded
states (I +/- H_j)/N. Set sigma=C(I/N), x_j=C(H_j)/N, t_j=||x_j||_1.
Recovery implies sum_j p_j t_j>=1-2epsilon. The single ANALYTIC witness

  R(X)=C^*(sigma^(-1/2) X sigma^(-1/2))/N

is CPTP on the output support, with a TP extension outside it, and satisfies

  q_j=Tr[H_j R C(H_j)]/N
     =Tr[x_j sigma^(-1/2) x_j sigma^(-1/2)] >= t_j^2.

The commutator gap implies

  F_e(RC)>=1-(2/kappa)(1-sum_j p_j q_j)
          >=1-8epsilon(1-epsilon)/kappa.

Every composite Kraus operator through a hybrid dimension-m memory has
rank at most m, hence F_e(RC)<=m/N. No physical implementation or efficient
compilation of the Petz witness is assumed. No common whole-output recovery
is required of the actual selector-dependent decoders.

EVERY PREFIX AND ACTUAL EXPECTED MEMORY EXPOSURE
===============================================

Let B consist of s qubits in a fixed one-way output order. Deferring a
prefix receiver operation algebraically past future source operations and
granting the untouched suffix free gives, after ell outputs,

  m_ell/2^ell >= r,  r=1-8epsilon(1-epsilon)/kappa.

If r>1/2, a whole-qubit implementation needs maximum/provisioned capacity ell.
This does not say every classical branch holds ell qubits.

For branch-dependent quantum dimension m_y=2^(q_y), under the maximally
mixed payload / Bell-input marginal the branchwise Kraus rank bound gives

  r <= E[2^(min(q_y,ell)-ell)],
  E[q_y] >= f_ell
    = max{0, ell(r-2^(-ell))/(1-2^(-ell))}.

These are actual input-dependent branch probabilities, not maxima collected
from incompatible histories. Applying the bound at each instant and
integrating, arrival spacing at least tau gives

  E[retained qubit-time] >= tau sum_(ell=1)^(s-1) f_ell
                         = Omega(tau s^2) for fixed r>0.

The bound covers intervals before any selector information arrives.
An additional delay t_header with NO selector information costs at least
t_header*f_s. Progressive disclosure of header bits changes the conditional
ensemble and is not charged by the unconditional gap without further proof.

USEFUL EXPLICIT FAMILY: LOGARITHMIC RECORD, KAPPA=4/5
==================================================

For n gauge qubits, let k=ceil(log2(4n-2)), q=2^k. Choose a,b uniformly
in GF(q), set x_l=tr(a*b^l), l=0,...,2n-1, with b^0=1. Every nontrivial
binary character has bias

  E(-1)^(v.x) = Pr_b[sum_l v_l b^l=0] <= (2n-1)/q <=1/2.

Pair the bits into a Hermitian n-qubit Pauli string P_(a,b). Its average
conjugation has absolute contraction at most 1/2 on traceless matrices.
Use a Z tensor I anchor with weight 1/5 and X tensor P_(a,b) with total
weight 4/5. This gives kappa>=4/5 (indeed the anchor sector attains it).
The Z anchor is essential: X tensor U alone has a nontrivial commutant.

Encoders are V_0=I and V_j=controlled_0(P_j)*(Hadamard tensor I).
The record has L=2k+1 bits, including an anchor branch bit, padded uniformly.
It is a physical source encoding program, not a bath purification/preparation
label. The declared distribution and record accuracy are paid.

Source operation: one unknown coherent flag, fresh independent tracial
carriers, one specified controlled Pauli per carrier, each carrier released
once, and a final paid full SWAP exporting the flag to a fresh carrier.
The original source is left tracial and discarded. Source coherent workspace
is constant; classical field/seed/counter storage is O(log n).

With j early, measure each arriving nonidentity Pauli factor in its local
eigenbasis, accumulate the negative-eigenvalue parity b, and discard it.
When the flag arrives apply H Z^b (first Z^b, then H).
The instrument identity

  (I tensor Pi_z)V_j = [diag(u_z,1) H] tensor Pi_z

gives exact arbitrary-reference recovery. Outcome probabilities are independent
of payload/reference for an initially independent gauge. The positive reader
works for any such gauge, even internally correlated and mixed with an
inaccessible purifier. The lower-bound experiment requires the specified
tracial gauge. CNOT factors in the separate local family have a product
control-Z/target-X eigenbasis, so they also need no quantum pair buffer.

EXECUTION BILL
==============

The early receiver uses one current carrier, a constant meter/output
workspace, a parity bit and O(log n) classical program state. It performs
O(n) quantum operations and meter resets, O(n log^2 n) elementary classical
work, and O(n) coherent qubit-time at fixed cadence. The source flag must
remain protected through the full stream. Finite control peak, slew, routing,
settling, readout, detector reset, flight and clock costs are given in the
paper. Classical field setup is conservatively O(n log^3 n+log^4 n).

The strongest simple late reader keeps all carriers and applies the SAME
O(n) local decoder after the record. Its memory is extensive; its processing
need not be quadratic or a generic inverse circuit. The theorem quantifies
the memory/protection bill, not a fictitious computation disadvantage.

The encounters are strong finite gates. If one can instead command immediate
full SWAP of the original payload, that is simpler. This construction fixes
the emitter program and varies record timing; it is not a reason to build
the encoder solely to transmit one qubit.

Errors add compositionally in half diamond: preparation, record, loss,
sum of local gate/readout errors, storage and timing. A sufficient uniform
local allocation is O(epsilon/n); fixed raw gate error is not a scalable
constant-error result. Fault-tolerant hardware, when used, costs additional
physical resources. Generic stationary gate qualification to that precision
costs O(n^2 epsilon^-2 log(1/delta)) trials upfront. End-to-end averaged
qubit-channel qualification has O(zeta^-2 log(1/delta)) runs, each paying the
whole source/receiver operation and trusted Bell/reference resources.

If flagged emission error is nu and an actual reader has error epsilon, use
epsilon+nu in the capacity bound, under the same fixed arrival/record schedule.
For actual expected occupancy, branch probabilities may change: the valid
bound is E_actual q_y >= max{0,f_ell(r_(epsilon+nu))-ell*nu}, with f_ell as
above and epsilon+nu<=1/2. Apply the ideal chord bound to bounded
z_y=min(q_y,ell), then transfer its expectation using prefix-record TV<=nu.
The same instrument, capacity assignment and physical cut are required.
Integrating this corrected bound gives actual retained qubit-time.
Final-channel closeness alone does not control earlier metadata arrival or
unbounded timing changes. The hard tracial factory has
paid independence and precision. The independent-qubit sufficient trace
error bound is .5 sqrt(product_i(1+beta_i^2)-1); beta_i=O(nu/sqrt n) suffices.

SEPARATE FIXED-REGISTER VERSION
===============================

Imported Anshu et al. arXiv:2609.01605v1 Theorem 2.2 supplies sixteen local
Hermitian depth-one Pauli/CNOT layers with absolute gap 1/(2*10^8).
Adding the Z anchor uniformly gives seventeen labels, a five-bit record,
and kappa_0=16/(17*10^8).

At epsilon<=kappa_0/16=5.88235294...e-10, at least n qubits of capacity are
needed after B. Ten coherent source registers suffice for the fixed-order
boundary/look-ahead/flag construction, with a fixed classical selector and
explicit external O(n)-instruction time program. Internal timing costs an
O(log n) counter. This is not the one-percent small-bias construction.

SCORABLE MEMORY CONSEQUENCE
===========================

Trusted binary probes (I +/- H_j)/N and a late j yield average signed score s.
The proof gives m/N>=1-(2/kappa)(1-s^2). This tests memory, not universal
payload recovery. At kappa=.8, 300 independent zero-failure trials with zero
trusted preparation/score bias certify m/N>=.9 at confidence .95.
Every trial pays the full O(n) preparation and stream. This qualification
point is calculated, not physically executed; unmodelled quantum side
channels and untrusted preparation are outside the certificate.

PRIOR MACHINERY AND CLAIM BOUNDARY
=================================

Ballester-Wehner-Winter already proved a one-Boolean-answer/full-memory exact
phenomenon. Bluhm-Rauber-Wolf supplied measurement-relative compression with
unlimited classical memory and fixed-family compactness stability.
Ioannou et al. supplied the closer late-measurement framework, low-rank
channel characterization and robust bounds for the entire unitary-invariant
projective-measurement family.

The claimed derivation here is the explicit finite-binary-family score /
commutator-gap implication, applied to arbitrary-payload encodings and
causal prefix/exposure costs, with a short-record physical implementation.
No first late-information phenomenon or exhaustive priority claim is made.
Early recovery is environment-assisted correction; small-bias sets and Pauli
randomization are existing machinery. Equally informed early readers can
use the same local instrument.
