# Late-selector payload theorem: primary input and physical implementation

Independent follow-up, 5 November 2026. The expander theorem is accepted input;
this note records its exact primary statement and constructs the resulting
local implementation. It does not re-prove or numerically validate expansion.

For the final fixed-clock and fixed-order implementation use
../root/local_expander_instantiation.txt. It adopts the conservative ten-qubit
source cell and common order 5,...,n,1,...,4. The earlier source scheduling
discussion below is exploratory and is not the canonical execution bill.

## Exact expander input

Anshu, Balasubramanian, Haferkamp, Harrow and Tan, *Depth-1 expanders on the
unitary group and applications*, arXiv:2609.01605v1, Theorem 2.2, equations
(2.1)-(2.6):

https://arxiv.org/html/2609.01605v1

Use n=3s, s>=1, and uniform weights on 16 indexed generators. Their average
conjugation channel Phi has Hilbert--Schmidt contraction <=1-gamma on traceless
operators, with gamma>=1/(2*10^8). With C_ab denoting CNOT(control a,target b):

    U1=X1, U2=Z1,
    U3=C12, U4=C21, U5=C23, U6=C32;
    {U7,...,U12}={product_(i=0)^(s-1) C_(3i+a,3i+b):
                                        a,b in {1,2,3}, a!=b};
    U13=product_i C_(3i+4,3i+2);
    U14=product_i C_(3i+5,3i+1);
    U15=product_i C_(3i+5,3i+3);
    U16=product_i C_(3i+6,3i+2).

Indices are modulo n in {1,...,n}. The products are disjoint-pair layers,
including the periodic pairs. Ring interaction range is at most four. Every
generator is a real Hermitian involution. At small s, indexed generators may
coincide; uniform sampling retains their multiplicities.

Source retrieved directly through the web tool as turn116view0 / turn117view1;
the parent must open the source itself before using those refs in its final
citations. The general Definition 1.1 specifies an absolute operator contraction,
not just an upper bound on the second-largest signed eigenvalue.

## A 17-selector balanced lift (new derivation for this operation)

This optional lift is sufficient for root's abstract theorem. Let D=2^n and
N=2D. On a flag F and n carriers define

    H0=Z_F tensor I_D,
    Hj=X_F tensor Uj,  j=1,...,16,
    p_j=1/17.

Every Hj is balanced and squares to I. Write

    K=I tensor A+X tensor B+Y tensor C+Z tensor E.

The H0 commutator has squared norm 8(||B||_2^2+||C||_2^2). Averaging the other
commutators gives twice the sum of commutator energies for A,B and
anticommutator energies for C,E. Expansion yields

    average_j ||[A,Uj]||_2^2 >=2 gamma ||A-Tr(A)I/D||_2^2,
    average_j ||{A,Uj}||_2^2 >=2 gamma ||A||_2^2.

The second bound uses the absolute gap; the scalar component has energy 4.
Combining the Pauli blocks therefore proves the root assumption with

    kappa=min(32 gamma/17,4/17).

For the stated certified gamma this is kappa=16/(17*10^8). This small numerical
constant must be disclosed. A dimension-independent constant is not a practical
one-percent certificate. Amplifying the gap changes selector, circuit and
memory costs and is not free.

Take V0=I and Vj=controlled-Uj (H_F tensor I). Then
Vj(Z_F tensor I)Vj*=Hj. The output is the carriers in temporal order 1,...,n,
followed by F, followed by the selector. Original preparation labels and bath
purifiers are unnecessary. This constructed encoded stream is distinct from
the once-through partial-SWAP source stream; no solution to that open receiver
is inferred.

## Early selector: exact local eigenmeasurement and payload recovery

CNOT has the product eigenbasis

    |c>_Z tensor |b>_X,   c,b in {0,1},
    CNOT |c>_Z|b>_X = (-1)^(c b)|c>_Z|b>_X.

Thus no entangled two-qubit eigenmeasurement is needed. For a CNOT-layer
selector, measure each control in Z, each target in X, and compute the parity
of the products c*b over its pairs. For U1 or U2, measure the corresponding
Pauli on the first site; other sites can be discarded. The measured full
product eigenbasis can refine the eigenspaces: that does not harm this input.

For an outcome with Uj eigenvalue lambda=(-1)^b, the late flag/reference state
is exactly

    (Z_F^b H_F tensor I_ref) rho_(F,ref) (H_F Z_F^b tensor I_ref),

and the outcome probability is its product-basis rank divided by D, independent
of the payload/reference. Apply H_F Z_F^b to the flag to recover rho exactly.
For selector zero, simply retain the flag. This is an instrument-level,
reference-preserving claim, not just preservation of Z data.

Receiver cost: O(n) one-qubit measurements and Boolean operations; no two-site
quantum gates; one arriving flag qubit retained for its correction. At most
three matching edges cross any linear stream cut for these layers, so at most
three pending classical outcomes and one parity bit suffice. A wrapping edge
keeps one such bit for O(n) slots. A general address counter costs O(log n)
classical bits; a known periodic finite controller plus an end marker can avoid
the counter if that timing interface is physically supplied. The selector uses
ceil(log2 17)=5 classical bits. Outcomes need not all be retained.

## Source initialization, bounded controls and timing

The source knows j and receives the arbitrary input qubit in F. It prepares
actual independent tracial carriers, not pure states whose labels are given to
the receiver. To realize controlled-Uj, when the earlier output position of a
matched pair is due, create both fresh tracial partners, apply the flag-controlled
CNOT (a Toffoli), emit the earlier partner, and retain the later partner until
its output position. Every gate pair is disjoint. At most three pairs straddle
any output cut; at most two newly prepared carriers are needed for a gate.
Together with F, six source qubits suffice as a conservative peak bound.

A fixed-size local gate cell can compile each Toffoli into a constant number
of one- and two-qubit gates; transporting a constant-size register layout costs
only a constant number of swaps per emitted carrier. This uses bounded local
control amplitudes and a nonzero gate time. Total gate count and elapsed
preparation/emission time are O(n), with the actual hardware constants paid.
The flag and a constant number of wrapping partners must remain protected for
O(n) slots. Their protection is a supplied resource or separately compiled cost.
All n carrier preparations, measurement/readout slots, fresh-state errors and
classical control operations are part of the bill.

The all-data memory decoder after a late selector can use the same product
eigenmeasurements and correction, but retains n+1 quantum outputs across the
selector cut. The dimension theorem permits arbitrary better processing and
arbitrary classical records; it does not restrict the comparator to this decoder.

If a G-operation compiled protocol has worst-case half-diamond error budgets
e_1,...,e_G, telescoping and contractivity give total error <=sum e_i. A fixed
total target therefore requires per-operation accuracy O(1/n) absent a better
structured noise analysis or fault-tolerant compilation. Fixed local physical
noise is not silently assumed to give a fixed reference-uniform total error.

## Strongest applicable prior comparison

### Ballester--Wehner--Winter: the exact phenomenon already exists

*State Discrimination with Post-Measurement Information*, quant-ph/0608014v2
(2006), journal version IEEE TIT 54, 4183-4198 (2008), DOI
10.1109/TIT.2008.928276:

https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0608014v2

Lemma 5.1 and equation (11) characterize exact memory through the commutant of
the support projectors. Theorem 6.6 constructs three bases for which even one
balanced Boolean answer requires the full d-dimensional memory after the basis
is disclosed. Their paragraph following the proof leaves the approximate
success question open. Consequently a claim that late selection can make a
single answer require full quantum memory is not new. Root's possible addition
is an explicit dimension-uniform error law, a local constant-selector physical
construction, and an arbitrary-reference payload operation. The qubit lower
bound follows already from its one-bit subtask; reference preservation does not
create the obstruction by itself.

### Bluhm--Rauber--Wolf: exact compression and nonuniform stability

*Quantum compression relative to a set of measurements*, arXiv:1708.04898v4,
Ann. Henri Poincare 19 (2018), DOI 10.1007/s00023-018-0660-z:

https://arxiv.org/pdf/1708.04898

Their setup admits unlimited classical side information and a common
decompression preserving all-state statistics of a chosen observable family.
Theorems 6.1/7.1 give algebraic bounds, including full incompressibility for a
full matrix algebra. Theorem 5.1 supplies a positive small-error threshold by
compactness; it is not a dimension-uniform numerical error law for root's growing
family. Root cannot directly apply its common-reconstruction theorem to the
weaker selector-dependent payload task. The Petz witness supplies that missing
bridge; it need not be an implemented decoder.

### Petz and Barnum--Knill: recovery machinery is established

D. Petz, *Sufficient subalgebras and the relative entropy of states of a von
Neumann algebra*, CMP 105 (1986), 123-131:
https://math.bme.hu/~petz/pdf/29suff.pdf

H. Barnum and E. Knill, *Reversing quantum dynamics with near-optimal quantum
and classical fidelity*, J. Math. Phys. 43 (2002), 2097-2106:
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0004088

Transpose/Petz recovery and near-optimal recovery of quantum or classical
information are prior machinery. Root's direct weighted Cauchy--Schwarz proof
can stand on its own. Its contribution is the combination of a common witness,
the commutator gap and hybrid-memory Kraus rank, not a new recovery map or an
efficient algorithm for implementing that witness.

### Recent robust orbit incompressibility: related but inapplicable directly

Hayden, Maloney, Wang and Yang, *Quantum minimum description of density
matrices*, arXiv:2610.06742 (2026), authors' exact proposition page:
https://wangjinzhao.com/Quantum-Minimum-Description-Length/results/propositions/orbit-sector-compression/

For a Haar orbit in an irreducible representation with a simple largest
state-eigenvalue gap gamma, this gives dim M>=dim H(1-delta_bar/gamma) under
common whole-state reconstruction. Root's balanced probes have a degenerate
top eigenspace; their relevant gap is a commutator-expansion gap. Also the orbit
proposition counts total Hilbert-space memory, not the per-branch quantum
dimension with free classical records. Thus it does not replace root's bound.

## Scientific claim that is supportable

If root's abstract proof is accepted, the defensible advance is a quantitative
and physically compiled version of postmeasurement-information incompressibility:
a known constant family of shallow local controls can encode one arbitrary
qubit into a tracial stream such that disclosing the selector before the stream
admits immediate local measurement and one output qubit, whereas sufficiently
accurate late-selector recovery forces almost the entire stream's quantum
dimension across the cut. The certified numerical robustness constant is very
small. This is not a positive low-memory decoder for the natural partial-SWAP
stream, and it does not establish a broad practical advantage by itself.
