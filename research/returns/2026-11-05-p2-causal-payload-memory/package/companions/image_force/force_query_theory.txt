# One optical moment opens the translational-force query

Independent constructive branch, 5 November 2026.

## Status and consequence

This branch changes field and representation. It constructs a direct raw-image operation for a translational hydrodynamic-force functional. It does not decode the quantum stream and does not identify a thermodynamic covariance model.

Astra's accepted N1199/N1200 identify a useful boundary: a quadratic displacement query immune to every unknown radial point-spread function must use a harmonic incompressible virtual field, and a single-valued planar field of that kind has zero resultant virtual force on each closed component. The escape here is small but exact: calibrate one scalar, the start-image PSF second moment. The virtual field may then be any Stokes field. Five real cross-image covariances acquire its planar quadratic current, and a sixth supplies the positive normalizer. The Stokes field may carry a nonzero translational load.

This is a completed conditional measurement construction, including a finite execution and error contract. The full force consequence requires the declared fluid, optical, seeding, boundary and finite-lag assumptions below. It is not an experiment, a proof that ordinary images meet those assumptions, or a major performance separation from an equally informed query-specific implementation. Pizzetti's finite mean-value formula, reciprocal force identities, moment-based blur correction and direct image-correlation stress inference are existing machinery. This branch supplies their exact interface and the calibration threshold for this admitted query family. Historical novelty is not established. This assessment is material when selecting the strongest branch.

## 1. The finite calibration hierarchy

Let a scalar function g be defined on an open neighborhood of every closed radius-rho ball under consideration in R^d. Let B be convolution with a centered radial probability law supported in that ball. Write m_(2j)=E|xi|^(2j), m_0=1. For Delta^r g=0, Pizzetti's formula terminates exactly:

    B g = P(Delta) g,
    P(z) = sum_(j=0)^(r-1) m_(2j) z^j / [4^j j! (d/2)_j].       (1)

Here (a)_j is the rising factorial. First take the spherical mean, whose polyharmonic expansion terminates, and then average the radius. In particular, for a biharmonic function,

    B g = g + mu Delta g,           mu = E|xi|^2/(2d),
    B(g - mu Delta g) = g.                                   (2)

This needs no knowledge of the radial PSF profile. If r>2, compute the polynomial Q satisfying P(z)Q(z)=1 modulo z^r by q_0=1, q_n=-sum_(j=1)^n p_j q_(n-j). Then B Q(Delta)g=g. For independent blurs of several variables, multiply the corresponding finite inverse polynomials.

There is an exact local converse. Suppose Bg(x), for all radial laws supported in a sufficiently small ball and at every admissible center x, is determined by the first r radial moments 1,m_2,...,m_(2r). Then Delta^(r+1)g=0, provided g is C^(2r+2). Indeed, the spherical mean M_x(t) must be a polynomial of degree at most r in t^2. Fix r+1 radii and use Lagrange interpolation coefficients to form a signed measure with zero first r moments. Its positive and negative parts, normalized by their common mass, are two probability laws. The hypothesis forces its integral against M_x to vanish, so M_x equals its interpolating polynomial at every additional radius. Its t^(2r+2) coefficient is a nonzero constant times Delta^(r+1)g(x), hence vanishes.

This is a calibration hierarchy for the linear radial-blur response, not an impossibility theorem for every nonlinear estimator or active experiment. The mean-value content is classical. Below, the PDE selects an adjoint query at the next level of this hierarchy.

## 2. One moment suffices for a force-carrying Stokes field

Let v,pi solve the unit-viscosity auxiliary Stokes equations in the required neighborhood:

    div v=0,          Delta v=grad pi.

Then Delta pi=0. Put S=sym grad v and D=y-x, and define f(x,y)=D^T S(x)D. The exact derivative identities are

    tr S=0,
    div S=(1/2) grad pi,
    Delta S=Hess pi,
    Delta_y f=0,
    Delta_x f=D^T (Hess pi) D - 2 grad pi . D,                 (3)
    Delta_x^2 f=0.

For the last line, the first term in Delta_x f has a matrix coefficient with zero trace, divergence and Laplacian; grad pi is harmonic and divergence free. These identities hold in any dimension and do not require a polynomial Stokes solution.

For the calibrated start-image moment mu, define

    k_mu(x,y)=f(x,y)-mu Delta_x f(x,y)
             =D^T [S(x)-mu Hess pi(x)]D+2mu grad pi(x).D.       (4)

If start blur xi is radial and its whole supporting ball lies in the Stokes neighborhood, while endpoint blur eta is independent and centered with isotropic covariance and finite second moment, then

    E k_mu(x+xi,y+eta)=f(x,y).                                (5)

Endpoint radiality is sufficient but stronger than necessary: quadratic harmonic dependence on y only uses zero mean and isotropic covariance. Start radiality supplies the local mean-value identity. The PSFs may differ. No latent particle matching or labels are read by the receiver; those enter only the admitted image-generation law below.

If mu is replaced by mu_hat, the residual is exactly

    E k_mu_hat(x+xi,y+eta)-f(x,y)=(mu-mu_hat) Delta_x f(x,y).    (6)

This finite correction avoids an infinite deconvolution. Its weights can still be large, and their statistical and precision costs remain. Calibrating mu supplies additional information, so (5) does not contradict the unknown-radial-blur zero-load obstruction or grant arbitrary hidden-PSF coverage.

## 3. A literal five-covariance receiver

In two dimensions write H=S-mu Hess pi=[[h1,h2],[h2,-h1]], ell=-2Hx+2mu grad pi, and c=x^T Hx-2mu grad pi.x. Then k_mu=sum_(j=1)^5 a_j(x)b_j(y), with

    a=(h1,h2,ell_1,ell_2,c),
    b=(y_1^2-y_2^2, 2y_1y_2, y_1, y_2, 1).                 (7)

Use N1199's fixed matched-pair Poisson imaging law: particle pairs have finite intensity measure nu(dx,dy); particles carry independent brightness marks with positive mixed moment m01; images are linear sums of translated PSFs plus deterministic backgrounds and independent between-frame zero-mean electronics. The relevant image sums are in L^2. Then

    Cov(integral a I0, integral b I1)
       =m01 integral E[a(x+xi)b(y+eta)] nu(dx,dy).              (8)

Sum the five covariances from (7) to Q, and compute N=Cov(total I0,total I1). Under full capture and the declared brightness law, N=m01 nu(total)>0 and

    Q/N = integral f dnu / nu(total).                        (9)

This is the normalized finite-lag displacement current, not yet force. It needs six start-image real sums (five a weights and total), five endpoint sums (four nonconstant b weights and total), and six covariance accumulators. Processing costs O(P) arithmetic per P-pixel frame pair, with constant retained accumulation memory. A coordinate table costs O(P) memory, or weights can be evaluated while streaming. No velocity field, pair identities, PSF shape or full image-covariance tensor is constructed. In d dimensions, d(d+3)/2 numerator covariances suffice.

The covariance law does not hold for arbitrary particle populations, changing gains, common electronic noise, nonlinear cameras or an unrestricted random-flow/Cox mixture. Uniform seeding transported by a volume-preserving, no-flux flow on the fully observed domain is sufficient to make both conditional image means independent of a random flow and remove the unwanted between-flow covariance. A source model or calibration must warrant that fact.

## 4. An explicit nonzero translational virtual load

For a<r<R, put

    L=log(R/a), Z=(R^2+a^2)L-(R^2-a^2),
    C=1/(2Z), B=-Ca^2R^2, D=-2C(R^2+a^2),
    A=C(a^2-R^2)-D log R,
    G(s)=A+B/s+Cs+(D/2)log s,
    psi(x,y)=y G(x^2+y^2),
    v=(partial_y psi,-partial_x psi),
    pi=8Cx-2Dx/(x^2+y^2).                                  (10)

Direct differentiation gives div v=0, Delta v=grad pi, v=e1 at r=a and v=0 at r=R. The virtual fluid-on-inner-body force is

    F_virtual=4pi D e1,
    D=-1/[log(R/a)-(R^2-a^2)/(R^2+a^2)] < 0.                 (11)

For a=1,R=2 the executed value is -134.90875986602757 e1. This crosses the accepted zero-resultant boundary for harmonic virtual fields.

All weights in (7) are rational functions of x,y with fixed geometry constants; differentiation removes the spatial logarithm from S. They extend throughout r>0. A compact start PSF of radius rho<a causes no mathematical boundary-strip loss even next to the inner wall, provided the optics capture the PSF and remain shift invariant there. A transparent, index-matched inclusion and complete aperture are possible physical contracts. Ordinary opaque walls do not provide that access. Multiplying (4) by a crop mask destroys the identity and needs a separate omitted-volume bound.

## 5. Mechanical consequence

Let the physical flow be incompressible Newtonian, with constant density rho and viscosity eta, in a fixed domain Omega. The selected fixed body boundary Gamma_b is impermeable. The auxiliary v equals e_k there and zero on other boundary components. Put tau_v=-pi I+2S. Suppose

    rho partial_t E u + rho div E(uu)=div E sigma+rho E b,
    sigma=-p I+2eta sym grad u.

Integration by parts followed by the Stokes reciprocal identity gives the fluid-on-body component

    F_k = rho integral_Omega E(uu):S
          -eta integral_boundary E u . tau_v n
          +rho integral_Omega v . E b
          -rho d/dt integral_Omega v . E u.                  (12)

Here n is outward from the fluid, and the body feels minus integral_(Gamma_b) sigma n. The outer convective boundary term vanishes because v=0; the body term vanishes because u.n=0. For a stationary mean the last term is zero; for fixed no-slip walls the boundary term is zero. Otherwise these terms, forcing, and their errors must be supplied or acquired. A planar image does not supply out-of-plane flow or endcap forces. The literal annular case is force per axial length in two dimensions, or an appropriate extruded/periodic experiment with its full volume contract.

Under uniform start seeding and pair lag h, |Omega|Q/(Nh^2) tends to the first integral. If speed <=U and material acceleration <=A0 on particle paths,

    |D/h-u(0)| <= A0 h/2,
    |D^T S D/h^2-u^T S u|
        <= ||S||[U A0 h+A0^2 h^2/4].                         (13)

The corresponding force bias is bounded by rho |Omega| ||S||_infty times the bracket. The operation acquires finite-lag current with a paid dynamic conversion. Brownian tracers, inertia, finite-exposure motion blur, projection and illumination need their own models or bounds.

## 6. Optical, precision and statistical execution costs

The following errors are in normalized current units before multiplication by rho |Omega|/h^2, except the already converted mechanical and finite-lag terms.

**Calibration.** An isolated stationary point emitter with known coordinate and warranted aperture/background gives the PSF second central moment. Emitter size, spatial variation, photon exposure, camera response and tails must be paid. Calling a bead pointlike is not calibration. If |mu_hat-mu|<=e_mu, (6) bounds bias by e_mu[||Hess pi|| U^2h^2+2||grad pi||Uh]. If the pair law has identical uniform start/end marginals on a closed incompressible domain, E[pi(Y)-pi(X)]=0. Taylor's theorem improves the bound to 2e_mu ||Hess pi|| U^2h^2. Thus the closed-domain force calibration error can be bounded independently of lag. This population cancellation is not a variance guarantee.

For the improved bound, the Hessian supremum is taken on all straight segments joining paired endpoints, within the certified Stokes continuation. In the annulus, hU<a/2 is one sufficient way to keep these segments away from the pole. For an explicit bounded-photon calibration contract, suppose N_cal independent detected photon locations come from an emitter at known z0, every displacement is <=rho, and pixel/background/finite-size contributions to the second moment are already bounded. The estimator mu_hat=(1/(2d N_cal))sum |Z_i-z0|^2 obeys

    P(|mu_hat-mu|>=e_mu) <= 2 exp[-8 d^2 N_cal e_mu^2/rho^4].

Thus N_cal >=rho^4 log(2/delta_cal)/(8d^2 e_mu^2) suffices, plus the actual emitter setup, collection and readout time. Unknown centroid must also be estimated with its own error. A compact-support assumption is not certified merely by observing no exterior photons. Spatially invariant radial shape is still a source/optics premise, not implied by this one scalar estimate.

**Support and tails.** Exactness uses certified compact PSF support inside a Stokes neighborhood. With tails, use a calibrated radial good-event law, its conditional second moment, and a warranted tail probability e<1. If visible corrected weights are bounded by M and the true kernel by M0, normalized query tail error is at most e(M+M0)/(1-e). Good-event probability must be independent of the true pair. Unknown light outside a finite camera cannot be excluded by that camera alone.

**Omitted particles.** If a strip E is excluded and uniform seeding is warranted, its instantaneous force-current contribution is at most rho U^2 integral_E ||S||. Small area alone does not bound high shear. An arbitrary crop mask cannot be inserted into (4) without paying this change.

**Pixels.** Let Lx,Ly bound the partial gradients of k_mu on all true-to-pixel-center segments. Integrated square pixels of sides h0,h1 incur at most (Lx h0+Ly h1)/sqrt(2) normalized error. The explicit rational formulas yield computable interval bounds on every compact guarded region.

**Arithmetic.** Evaluate every weight in a dyadic interval. An error e_w gives a weighted-sum error <=e_w T on a nonnegative frame of total intensity T. Propagate intervals through covariance sums and the ratio. Admit a normalized result only if its denominator interval is positive. Geometry constants, logarithms and their conditioning are also paid.

**Statistics and exposure.** For iid paired records, group consecutive records into independent two-record blocks. Use Z=(A_2-A_1)(B_2-B_1)/2 for each covariance; it is unbiased. If E Z^2<=V, a median of g block means, each using at least 8V/t^2 independent Z samples, has error <=t with failure <=exp(-g/8). Use odd g>=8 log(2q/delta), q=6, and a union bound. Supplied fourth-moment bounds E A^4<=M_A and E B^4<=M_B imply the conservative V=4 sqrt(M_A M_B): (a-b)^4<=8(a^4+b^4), followed by Cauchy-Schwarz. Each Z costs two paired image records. Known moment bounds, paid clipping bias or another justified heavy-tail procedure are needed; existence of second moments alone gives no distribution-free finite confidence radius.

If numerator error is t_Q=sum_j t_j, normalizer error is t_N<=N_min/2, N>=N_min>0 and |Q/N|<=M0, ratio error is <=2(t_Q+M0 t_N)/N_min. Prefer dividing the actual simultaneous intervals. A negative empirical normalizer never permits a sign flip or replacement denominator.

Independence is an acquisition resource. A certified reseeding, reset or mixing protocol and its elapsed time must be supplied. For independent resets costing T_reset and lag h, K records cost at least K(T_reset+h), plus illumination and readout. Correlated time series cannot simply use the iid formula. Intensity and brightness moments affect V and N_min.

At fixed optical noise, individual raw covariances need not shrink with h even though their signed sum is O(h^2). Fixed force accuracy can therefore require O(h^-4) records with the direct estimator. Pixel and arithmetic tolerances may tighten as h^2. O(P) processing and constant accumulator memory do not erase this acquisition bill. Any better control variate must prove its variance law. N1199's difference-image control has separate symmetrization and unmatched-particle conditions and is not imported as a free improvement.

## 7. Executed checks and strongest comparison

`force_query.py` verifies the Stokes derivative identities symbolically, evaluates the correction for several distinct radial PSFs, and checks (12) on a manufactured incompressible steady flow with prescribed body force and zero wall velocity. It also integrates tracer paths to check the actual finite-lag current. `receipt.json` records outputs. The script uses NumPy and SymPy; in this session SymPy was supplied in `/tmp/force_query_deps` and the command was

    PYTHONPATH=/tmp/force_query_deps /opt/codex/runtimes/codex-primary-runtime/dependencies/python/bin/python3 output/next_openings_stream/alternative/force_query.py

The executed blur correction residual was below 2e-17 in all three cases. The manufactured flow has psi=.03(s-1)^2(4-s)^2(1+.3x+.4y), rho=1.2, eta=.7, zero pressure, and known b=(u.grad)u-(eta/rho)Delta u. Its inertial current is 0.5690162400949328; its body-force virtual work is 1.0143464573143213. The reciprocal force 1.9000352368911049 agrees with the directly integrated wall stress 1.9000352368911066. For lags .008,.004,.002,.001 the finite-lag errors are .00627483,.00314030,.00157087,.000785612, consistent with the proved first-order bound.

These are algebra and deterministic quadrature, not observed images, a physical experiment, or a certified statistical force measurement. The earlier 500-image N1199 record retains its negative normalizers and failed physical normalization.

The strongest relevant ordinary baselines already include reciprocal hydrodynamic-force formulas, PIV pressure/force inference, direct image-correlation Reynolds-stress inference, and blur-invariant moments. An equally informed implementation can use (7) and has the same O(P) processing. This branch establishes a narrower definite capability: one moment moves an exactly blind harmonic zero-force interface to a translational-force interface without fitting the full radial PSF or reconstructing a velocity field. It does not establish lower exposure than the best correlation fit or higher accuracy than a direct force balance. A body-mounted force balance is a strong simpler baseline when such access is available. This interface matters when that access is unavailable or perturbative and the stated optical-volume/calibration contract is available.

The strongest counterweight to selecting this as the main scientific result is therefore substantive, not a proof gap: the new composition is useful, but its underlying finite calibration and force identities are classical and its measurement conditioning can remain expensive. No civilization-scale consequence or historical priority is justified by the current result.
