ONE-QUBIT PARTIAL-SWAP RECEIVERS DO NOT ACCUMULATE A TRACIAL PAYLOAD
Construction note, 2026-11-05. This is a restricted-architecture theorem,
not a no-go for arbitrary finite-memory receivers.

1. Operation and convention

S is a source qubit carrying an arbitrary input, possibly entangled with
an inaccessible reference. M is a single receiver qubit initially I/2.
Round j introduces E_j independently in I/2, applies

    U_SE(theta) = cos(theta) I + i sin(theta) SWAP_SE,

then, without a return to S, applies the receiver gate

    V_ME(phi_j) = cos(phi_j) I + i sin(phi_j) SWAP_ME,

and discards E_j. The source is discarded at termination. The receiver
angles phi_j are arbitrary real predetermined numbers; input-independent
randomized schedules and a recorded seed are also covered. An arbitrary
final CPTP decoder on M and that seed is allowed. Intermediate receiver
measurements, non-SWAP controls, pure auxiliary memories, and adaptive
measurement feedback are not covered.

Errors use HALF diamond distance: epsilon = ||R C - id||_diamond / 2.
Take 0 < theta < pi/2, c=cos(theta), s=sin(theta).

2. Exact three-dimensional evolution

For a source Z deviation, define

    P=Z_S tensor I_M,  Q=I_S tensor Z_M,
    K=X_S tensor Y_M - Y_S tensor X_M.

The evolved deviation is A_j P + B_j Q + C_j K, beginning at
(A_0,B_0,C_0)=(1,0,0). This subspace is invariant. With
a_j=cos(phi_j), b_j=sin(phi_j), the exact recurrence is

    A_(j+1) = c^2 A_j,
    B_(j+1) = s^2 b_j^2 A_j + a_j^2 B_j - 2 s^2 a_j b_j C_j,
    C_(j+1) = -c s b_j^2 A_j
                + (c^2 a_j^2 + c s a_j b_j) C_j.

One derivation uses [SWAP_SE,Z_S]=i(X_S Y_E-Y_S X_E), expands the
two partial SWAPs, and traces E/2. The accompanying script independently
checks this against full 8-by-8 matrices, rather than another recurrence.

The source-to-M channel is exactly depolarizing with visibility B_N:

    C_N(rho) = B_N rho + (1-B_N) I/2.

This follows from joint SU(2) covariance and the tracial initialization.

3. Uniform correlation envelope

Let K0=4s/(3-c). For every receiver-angle sequence and all j,

    A_j=c^(2j),       |C_j| <= K0 c^j.

For the induction, bound the recurrence by

    |C_(j+1)| <= c s b^2 c^(2j)
                   + (c^2 a^2+c s |ab|) K0 c^j.

Since c^(2j)<=c^j, it suffices that

    K0 >= s b^2 / (1-c a^2-s |ab|).

The denominator, using a^2+b^2=1, is the positive quadratic form

    (1-c)a^2 + b^2 - s|ab|.

Its 2-by-2 matrix has determinant (1-c)(3-c)/4. The maximum generalized
Rayleigh quotient of b^2 divided by this form is 4/(3-c), establishing
the envelope exactly. No small-angle approximation enters this bound.

4. Accumulation bound

The recurrence and Cauchy-Schwarz give

    B_(j+1)^2 <= B_j^2 + s^4 (A_j^2+2 C_j^2).

Indeed B_(j+1) is the scalar product of
(B_j,s^2 A_j,sqrt(2)s^2 C_j) with
(a_j^2,b_j^2,-sqrt(2)a_j b_j), and the latter vector has norm one.
Summing the geometric envelopes yields, uniformly in N,

    |B_N| <= L(theta),

    L(theta)^2 = s^2/(1+c^2) + 32 s^4/(3-c)^2.

In particular L(theta)=theta/sqrt(2)+O(theta^3) as theta tends to zero.
The bound can be replaced by min(1,L) when it is larger than one.

For any final qubit decoder R, its Bell-state entanglement fidelity
after a depolarizing channel of visibility B satisfies

    F_e(R C_N) <= (1+3|B_N|)/4.

For B>=0 this follows from
F_e(R C_N)=B F_e(R)+(1-B)/4 and F_e(R)<=1. For B<0, F_e(R)>=0 gives
the stronger upper bound (1-B)/4. A Bell-projector test lower-bounds
half diamond distance by 1-F_e. Therefore every allowed receiver has

    epsilon >= (3/4) max(0,1-L(theta)).

At theta=0.1, L=0.0761514353682639 and
epsilon >= 0.6928864234738021, even with unlimited rounds and arbitrary
receiver partial-SWAP angles. At weak action the error floor tends to
3/4. Input-independent random angle schedules cannot improve the bound:
apply it conditionally and average the entanglement fidelity.

5. Meaning and limits

The accepted all-output decoder can acquire the arbitrary input at the
fourth-order collision scale. This theorem excludes a particularly
natural attempted implementation of a small sufficient statistic:
one tracial receiver qubit with scalar partial-SWAP updates. It does not
exclude a receiver with larger or pure initialized memory, general local
quantum channels, measurements, a subsystem code, or block compression.
It is not a memory lower bound for the homogeneous tracial stream.

The theorem is exact in the ideal stipulated model. A physical receiver
whose whole induced channel is at half diamond distance delta from an
allowed ideal receiver has error at least the displayed floor minus
delta. This is a stability statement, not a supplied calibration or
fault-tolerance protocol. The rejected architecture has O(1) retained
qubits and O(N) gates, but fails the required acquisition operation.

The three-dimensional recurrence and the uniform control envelope are
derived here. No novelty claim beyond this restricted diagnostic has
been established by a comprehensive literature search.
