INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE LATE-SELECTOR PAYLOAD THEOREM
2026-11-05. Audits root/late_selector_theorem.txt as read during this run.

Verdict: the algebraic theorem, its half-diamond convention, and factor 8
are correct under the displayed commutator-gap premise. The physical
instantiation must still supply that premise for its actual emitted
operator family and count its source buffering/output ordering.

1. Independent recomputation of the Petz bridge

Let C map N-dimensional B to a direct sum of memory blocks of dimension
at most m. This includes arbitrary retained dephased classical records.
Put sigma=C(I/N), x=C(H)/N, where H is a balanced involution.
The two equally likely probe states map to sigma +/- x. Their optimal
distinguishing advantage is t=||x||_1. The Petz map at I/N is

    R(Y)=(1/N) C^*(sigma^(-1/2) Y sigma^(-1/2)).

It is trace preserving on supp(sigma). Every C input lies in that
support because its reference input is full rank. Extend R arbitrarily
and trace-preservingly off the support. For T=R C,

    q=Tr[H T(H)]/N
     =Tr[x sigma^(-1/2) x sigma^(-1/2)] >= t^2.

To see the last inequality, apply ordinary HS Cauchy-Schwarz to
sigma^(1/4) sign(x) sigma^(1/4) and sigma^(-1/4) x sigma^(-1/4).
The squared norm of the first factor is at most 1: in a sigma eigenbasis
use sqrt(lambda_a lambda_b)<=(lambda_a+lambda_b)/2 and sign(x)^2<=I.

This common R is valid for every H_j. It need not be a physically
executed decoder and it need not be the task's selector-dependent decoder.
Thus the proof does not assume that the receiver preserves all B.

2. Gap and memory bound

The premise is

    sum_j p_j ||[K,H_j]||_2^2
       >= kappa ||K-(Tr K/N)I||_2^2

for every complex K. For any Kraus representation T(X)=sum_a K_a X K_a^*,
trace preservation gives the exact identity

    sum_a ||[K_a,H_j]||_2^2 = 2N(1-q_j).

The gap therefore yields

    F_e(T)>=1-(2/kappa)(1-sum_j p_j q_j).

Each composite Kraus operator through the hybrid memory has rank at most
m, even if the classical output alphabet is arbitrarily large. Consequently
|Tr K_a|^2<=m Tr K_a^*K_a and F_e(T)<=m/N.

If the selector-averaged decoded qubit channel has half diamond error
epsilon, its average computational-basis test succeeds with probability
at least 1-epsilon. Therefore sum_j p_j t_j>=1-2epsilon. Jensen and q_j>=t_j^2
give precisely

    m/N >= 1-8epsilon(1-epsilon)/kappa.

No hidden worst-selector requirement was used. The restriction epsilon<=1/2
makes the squaring step valid. At epsilon<=kappa/16 the right side is at
least 1/2. With N=2^(n+1), retained quantum qubits are at least n when
that error condition holds. All coherent delay lines and ancillary
registers crossing the selector cut belong to this memory.

3. Early-selector eigenmeasurement decoder

For U_j=product_l u_(j,l) on disjoint gauge blocks and

    E_j(rho)=controlled(U_j) (H rho H tensor I_D/D) controlled(U_j)^*,

measure every gauge block in an eigenbasis of its known u_(j,l).
If the outcomes have eigenvalues exp(i phi_l), their probability is
the eigenprojector dimension divided by that block's dimension,
independent of the arbitrary input/reference. The conditional late flag
is exactly

    Z(phi) H rho H Z(phi)^*,    phi=sum_l phi_l mod 2pi,

including its joint state with an untouched reference. Apply Z(-phi)
then H to the late flag. This is exact recovery using a classical phase
accumulator and at most the maximum local eigenmeasurement block size
of quantum buffer. For Pauli/CNOT involutions only a parity bit is needed.

CNOT has a product eigenbasis: measure the control in Z and the target
in X. Thus CNOT eigenmeasurement itself need not retain its two inputs
coherently together, provided their classical roles and pairing are known.

4. Necessary instantiation checks

If every U_j is Hermitian, then the displayed encoding has
H_j = X_flag tensor U_j. All these H_j commute with X_flag tensor I.
They therefore have zero full commutator gap by themselves. A supplied
construction must include additional flag-axis anchors or an equivalent
modification, with the stated final kappa derived for the combined set.

The source must implement each controlled local gauge gate before those
gauge particles leave. Disjoint gates do not by themselves imply a
constant source-side buffer in a fixed stream order. Any selector-dependent
reordering must be included in the actual emitted H_j and must not leak
the selector through labels or timing. This concern is distinct from the
early decoder, whose local eigenmeasurements can be especially simple.

The early and late settings must have the same physical emitted channel
except for the explicitly changed metadata time. Purifiers, preparation
labels, or source return are not needed by the eigenmeasurement decoder.
This construction can use strong source gates; it is not a theorem for
the homogeneous weak partial-SWAP stream.

5. Close prior work

Bluhm, Rauber and Wolf, Quantum compression relative to a set of measurements,
Annales Henri Poincare 19 (2018), arXiv:1708.04898,
DOI 10.1007/s00023-018-0660-z, is a close primary comparator.
Full paper: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1708.04898.
Their model allows arbitrary classical side information. Their
Theorem 5.1 establishes an error threshold for each fixed measurement
family by compactness. Their main definition requires one common
decompressor to preserve all specified measurement statistics for every
input. Exact generic incompressibility for sufficiently rich small
observable sets is already in that work. Any new claim here should be
restricted to the explicit dimension-uniform gap bound under the weaker
encoded-payload premise, and the fully specified metadata-timing operation.
The Petz bridge, not generic measurement-relative compression, is the
specific link supplied by the present proof.
