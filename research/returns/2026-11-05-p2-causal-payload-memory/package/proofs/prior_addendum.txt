PRIOR ADDENDUM: IOANNOU ET AL. (2022)

Source: Simulability of High-Dimensional Quantum Measurements,
PRL 129, 190401; https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.12980.

Verdict: this is the closer operational framework, and it contains genuine
quantitative analytic bounds. Those bounds do not imply our finite-family
expander/payload bound.

Equation (1) already permits measurement-specific late POVMs after a common
quantum/classical compression. Claim 5 relates simulability to Kraus-rank
bounded channels. These concepts must not be claimed as new here.

For ALL isotropically noisy rank-one projective measurements, their Eq. (14)
gives

  eta <= [d sqrt((m+1)/(d+1))-1]/(d-1).

This uses full unitary symmetry. Their finite-assemblage SDP constructs
simulations using selected compression instruments.

Our premise concerns only a finite binary family and its average discrimination
score on (I +/- H_j)/N. It neither specifies isotropic noise for every input
nor requires every projective measurement. A lower bound for a larger
measurement family cannot be imported for this smaller one.

The specific additional implication is

  m/N >= 1-(2/kappa)(1-score^2),

obtained through the common-Petz correlation witness and the finite family's
commutator gap, then linked to the explicit payload encoding and metadata
timing operation. The examined paper supplies no such gap-dependent bound.
This is a scoped comparison, not a claim of exhaustive novelty verification.
