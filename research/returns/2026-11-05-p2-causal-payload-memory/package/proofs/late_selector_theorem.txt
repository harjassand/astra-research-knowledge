LATE SELECTOR, ONE QUANTUM PAYLOAD
Completed algebraic derivation, independently audited, 5 November 2026

CONVENTIONS. Half diamond d_diamond(A,B)=||A-B||_diamond/2.
Hilbert--Schmidt norm is unnormalised. Memory has quantum dimension m
in EACH classical branch; arbitrary classical records are allowed.
Every coherent register and delay line crossing the cut counts.

ALGEBRAIC INPUT. N=2D. H_j are balanced Hermitian involutions, p_j>0,
sum p_j=1. Assume for every complex K:
sum_j p_j ||[K,H_j]||_2^2 >= kappa ||K-(Tr K/N)I||_2^2.
Choose V_j with H_j=V_j(Z tensor I_D)V_j*. The arbitrary qubit payload,
possibly entangled with a reference, is encoded as
E_j(rho)=V_j(rho tensor I_D/D)V_j*.
All B (dimension N) arrives before a classical selector j is revealed.
j has distribution p, independent of payload/reference. A common
compression C retains quantum dimension at most m per classical branch.
After j arrives the decoder D_j may depend on j. Assume
d_diamond(sum_j p_j D_j C E_j,id)<=epsilon, 0<=epsilon<=1/2.
No common reconstruction of the whole B is required.

THEOREM. m/N >= 1-8 epsilon(1-epsilon)/kappa.
For epsilon<=kappa/16 this implies m>=N/2. Exact recovery requires m>=N.
Keeping all B and applying V_j* after j arrives attains exact recovery.

PROOF. Put sigma=C(I/N), x_j=C(H_j)/N, t_j=||x_j||_1 in [0,1].
The two inputs |0>,|1> become (I+/-H_j)/N. A late Z measurement on
D_j's output has average success at least 1-epsilon by the assumed
half-diamond bound. Optimal discrimination is no worse, so
sum_j p_j t_j >= 1-2epsilon.

On supp(sigma) define R(X)=C*(sigma^(-1/2) X sigma^(-1/2))/N.
This Petz map is CP and TP on the support, and has a TP extension.
T=R C factors through the same hybrid memory as C. Its correlations are
q_j=Tr[H_j T(H_j)]/N=Tr[x_j sigma^(-1/2) x_j sigma^(-1/2)].
For A=sign(x_j), weighted Hilbert--Schmidt Cauchy--Schwarz gives
t_j^2 <= Tr[sigma^(1/2) A sigma^(1/2) A] q_j <= q_j.
The last factor is at most 1: diagonalise sigma and use
sqrt(sigma_a sigma_b)<=(sigma_a+sigma_b)/2 and A^2<=I.

For Kraus operators K_a of T, trace preservation gives
sum_a ||[K_a,H_j]||_2^2=2N-2 Re Tr[H_j T(H_j)].
Apply the commutator gap and sum over Kraus operators to obtain
F_e(T)>=1-(2/kappa)(1-sum_j p_j q_j)
      >=1-(2/kappa)(1-(sum_j p_j t_j)^2)
      >=1-8epsilon(1-epsilon)/kappa.
Every composite Kraus operator through a hybrid dimension-m memory
has rank<=m. Hence |Tr K|^2<=m Tr K*K and F_e(T)<=m/N. QED.

The common Petz reconstruction is an ANALYTIC WITNESS, not a task
requirement. This is the step missing from simply applying the warm
whole-output preservation theorem to a payload decoder.

MEASUREMENT FORM. A late-selector +/- probe test with average error q
or nonnegative signed score s=1-2q gives
m/N>=1-8q(1-q)/kappa=1-(2/kappa)(1-s^2).
It certifies memory, not universal recovery from the score alone.

TWO EXPLICIT INSTANTIATIONS. For Hermitian expander involutions U_j,
put H0=Z tensor I and Hj=X tensor U_j. Give H0 weight alpha and the
others total weight 1-alpha. If the average conjugation by U_j has
absolute contraction <=1-gamma on traceless matrices, flag-Pauli
decomposition gives kappa>=min(4alpha,2(1-alpha)gamma).
Encoders are V0=I and Vj=controlled_0(U_j)(Hadamard tensor I).
The extra Z anchor is essential; X tensor U_j alone has nontrivial
commutant X tensor I.

Version A (percent-error constants): take all n-qubit Pauli strings
P uniformly. Pauli twirling has gamma=1. Choose alpha=1/3, so
kappa=4/3 exactly. N=2^(n+1). The late classical record needs 2n+O(1)
bits, and the source needs one coherent payload qubit plus O(n)
classical control storage. At epsilon=.01, m/N>=.9406. A memory made
of whole qubits must have capacity n+1 after the flag arrives,
or n immediately before that flag. These are maxima across classical
branches, not required occupancy in every branch. Constant quantum
source memory is not constant TOTAL source dimension in this version.

Version B (constant total source dimension): import Anshu et al.,
arXiv:2609.01605v1 Theorem2.2: 16 Hermitian depth-1 Pauli/CNOT layers
on n=3s qubits, ring range<=4, gamma>=1/(2*10^8). Uniform weights on
the 16 lifted involutions plus the Z anchor give
kappa>=min(32gamma/17,4/17)>=16/(17*10^8), about9.4117647e-9.
There are 17 labels, requiring five bits. For epsilon<=kappa/16,
about5.88235294e-10, at least n retained qubits are necessary.
This does NOT provide a one-percent lower bound for that local family.

EARLY SELECTOR OPERATION. If j is known before the gauge particles,
measure each local factor of U_j in an eigenbasis as it arrives.
Let its eigenvalues multiply to u. Conditional on the eigenlabels,
the flag experiences W=diag(u,1) Hadamard. Once the flag arrives,
apply W*. This restores arbitrary input/reference states exactly on
every branch. For Pauli and CNOT factors u=(-1)^b, so the correction
is Hadamard Z^b: first Z^b, then Hadamard. The Z-anchor branch uses
V0=I and needs no correction.

CNOT has a product eigenbasis: measure its control in Z and target
in X. If their outcome bits are c and b, its eigenvalue is (-1)^(cb).
Thus no coherent pair buffer is needed at the receiver. It needs
one arriving-qubit meter, a parity bit, and a bounded number of
pending CLASSICAL outcomes for fixed-range matching pairs.

The positive decoder works for any gauge state initially independent
of payload/reference, including correlated mixed states with hidden
purifiers. Spectral projectors reduce each controlled factor to a
known scalar on the flag. Tracial gauge is needed for the memory
lower-bound experiment, not for positive recovery.

This is an actual causal CPTP operation: local measurements, classical
accumulation, final one-qubit unitary, and discard of used carriers.
It does not use the original source, a coherent return link, bath
purifiers or bath preparation labels. The early record is the SOURCE
ENCODING PROGRAM; its earlier delivery is a paid timing resource.
