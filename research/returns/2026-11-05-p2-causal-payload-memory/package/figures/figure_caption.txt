Analytic quantum-memory bounds for the late-selector payload theorem. (A) The required dimension fraction 1-8 epsilon(1-epsilon)/kappa for kappa=4/5 and 4/3; the marked one-percent values are 0.901 and 0.9406. The dashed horizontal line is one-half of the full dimension. (B) The every-prefix expected-qubit lower bound f_l=max{0,l(r-2^{-l})/(1-2^{-l})} for r=0.901, compared with direct retention of all l outputs. The early-selector O(1) line is schematic and does not assert an exact meter count. For n=1000 gauge outputs and one final flag, the expectation of retained quantum exposure before the final output is at least 450,950.22834065 tau qubit-time, versus 500,500 tau for direct storage. Delay t_hdr before any selector information becomes available adds at least 901.901 t_hdr; a progressively disclosed header needs a conditional analysis. The mean bounds use the maximally mixed/Bell-input marginal and remain valid with branch-dependent memory. Parameters q=4096, k=12 give a 25-bit header. No device or 1000-qubit state was simulated.
