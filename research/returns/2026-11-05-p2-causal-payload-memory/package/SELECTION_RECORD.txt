The user's suggested positive weak-stream receiver was treated as a candidate,
not a required architecture. Initial constructors pursued materially different

1. Once-through weak partial-SWAP receiver
----------------------------------------

Question: can one recover an arbitrary unknown source/reference at the warm
quartic collision scale with little retained quantum memory and near-linear
processing, from the actual tracial stream without source/purifier/return?

Construction attempted: a downstream single tracial qubit absorbing the
outputs through individually chosen partial SWAPs, including a final arbitrary
decoder. A closed three-variable recurrence enabled a complete uniform
obstruction for this architecture, not a successful receiver. General
instruments, pure workspace, larger memories, feedback and block compression
are not excluded. Coherent-absorber priors use pure incoming units and/or
parameter-estimation objectives; their existence does not close this task.

Decision: preserve the exact restricted result and keep the general positive
operation unresolved. Do not inflate that no-go to all causal receivers.

2. Finite thermal access to hidden linear dynamics
-------------------------------------------------

Question: can a physically specified noise/heating intervention expose native
irreversibility hidden from partially observed force-response records?

Construction completed: a finite raised-cosine common thermal pulse yields an
invertible covariance transfer for every Hurwitz drift. Random hot/control
tagging yields martingale-unbiased moments on a continuously running specimen.
Finite observable models can be identified up to similarity with known order,
anti-aliasing, readout observability and calibrated all-mode thermal reach.
The entropy functional is then determined under the all-even physical bath
model. Finite conditioning, drift, exposure and sampling costs are supplied.

Decisive limitation: strong stochastic-realization and covariance-identification
baselines already exist; the new port is substantive, all-mode reach is not
learned from short records, and conservative sample costs can be very large.
The displayed million-cycle point is not certified by its conservative radius.

Decision: complete and retain as a positive companion. It does not overturn
the warm force-only nonidentifiability example.

3. Mechanical force from raw blurred image pairs
------------------------------------------------

Question: can a consequential mechanical quantity be obtained directly from
optical records without first reconstructing every particle and velocity?

Construction completed: a Stokes virtual field, exact radial-PSF moment
correction, five weighted covariances and positive normalization yield a
translational-force query with explicit reciprocal and finite-lag terms.
Symbolic, deterministic-quadrature and tracer-lag examples were executed.

Decisive limitation: Pizzetti/Faxen, reciprocal-force and correlation-estimator
machinery are strong existing alternatives. Positive normalization, optical
volume/support, particle-pair law and calibration are substantive premises.
Direct small-lag variance can cost O(h^-4); a direct force balance can be
better when accessible. No physical optical experiment or general efficiency
advantage was established.

Decision: complete and retain as the second new positive companion.

4. Payload-specific memory and the timing of a control record
------------------------------------------------------------

Question selected: is a small recoverable payload (or small programmed source)
enough to ensure small causal quantum memory from mixed physical outputs?
If not, can an actual accessible record change that memory requirement, with
an exact receiver and quantified finite-error lower bound?

Initial obstacle: the preserved whole-output compression obstruction does not
by itself constrain a reader that needs only one unknown qubit. Its hypothesis
must not be silently substituted for payload recovery.

Construction completing the gap: discrimination of the payload's two basis
states creates a common analytic Petz correlation witness even though each
late selector may choose a different decoder. A commutator gap forces that
witness to preserve high-dimensional entanglement fidelity. Kraus-rank
factorization through hybrid memory yields a dimension-uniform finite-error
bound. Deferring causal prefix maps extends it to each prefix and to actual
expected retained qubit-time, including variable branch sizes.

An explicit small-bias Pauli program gives a logarithmic classical record and
useful gap. Delivering that record early permits actual local eigenmeasurements
and a final one-qubit correction, with constant quantum workspace and linear
quantum work. Delivering it late forces extensive memory even though only one
arbitrary qubit is requested. A separate imported local expander gives a
fixed-register source and a five-bit record at a much smaller error threshold.

An essential construction repair added a Z anchor: X tensor U alone has a
nontrivial common commutant. Independent audits checked the Petz bridge,
classical-branch treatment and causal expected-occupancy refinement.

The prior-art comparison includes Ballester-Wehner-Winter, Bluhm-Rauber-Wolf,
Ioannou et al., environment-assisted correction, small-bias sets/Pauli
randomization and the supplied explicit local expander. Late-choice memory,
Petz recovery and the early environment-assisted protocol are not claimed
as newly invented. The explicit score-gap bound and its physical payload /
record-timing composition are the completed derivation offered here.

Selection reason: this branch closes a general structural question that
directly constrains proposed compact receivers, supplies both a quantitative
converse and a complete positive interface, and yields a small-output trusted
memory certificate. Its memory/exposure statements survive unrestricted
receiver computation and unlimited classical records. The other branches did
not establish a stronger closed consequence against their actual baselines.
