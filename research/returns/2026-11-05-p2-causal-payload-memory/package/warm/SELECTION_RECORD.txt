CONSTRUCTIVE INDEPENDENCE AND QUESTION SELECTION
5 November 2026

The rare-failure intervention task was treated as completed machinery. Four
initial constructions pursued materially different scientific questions before
one architecture was selected. This record distinguishes completed statements
from obstacles and unproved possibilities.

A. MIXED-BATH QUANTUM ACQUISITION (SELECTED)
Question: after an unknown quantum source has relaxed, what has its actually
accessible, initially mixed bath acquired, and can a finite receiver recover
the input after the source is discarded?

The independent constructor derived an exact partial-SWAP acquired-channel
Gram recursion, an output-only maximally-mixed-dummy inverse receiver, and an
all-decoder clock. Root then constructed a universal weighted-norm action law
with genuine classical feedback; the theory collaborator independently proved
it and strengthened it to path-dependent bounded action budgets. The physical
collaborator supplied a finite local register implementation and error bill.

Reason for selection: both sides close. There is a universal obstruction for
every interaction in a stated physical access class, and a complete finite
operation reaching its action and accuracy scaling. The same exact source
dynamics can coexist with different output acquisition clocks. This resolves
an operational distinction that a reduced noise model alone cannot supply.

B. THERMODYNAMIC IDENTIFICATION FROM CONTROLLED PARTIAL OBSERVATION
Question: can complete active experiments through a known conjugate force port
certify equilibrium or determine entropy production in an observable physical
linear system?

The independent physical construction supplies a three-dimensional Ornstein-
Uhlenbeck family with identical stationary output/force-response transcripts
but different entropy production, even though deterministic state-space
observability and controllability hold. It identifies the covariance ambiguity
through Z C^T=0 and Z A^T C^T=0, and the resulting rank criterion. The companion
states initialization, force and noise limitations. Divergent dissipation in
its limiting family requires diverging physical forces; bounded positive
ambiguity already survives.

Reason not selected: it supplies a sharp and useful identification boundary,
but broad hidden-dissipation/partial-observation obstructions and inference
approaches are established. A new large positive experimental capability was
not supplied. The exact construction is retained in the companion rather than
inflated into the main consequence.

C. COMPUTATIONAL PREDICTION FROM SIMPLE LOCAL DYNAMICS
Question: when does an apparently favorable dynamical spectrum fail to make
long-time physical prediction tractable?

The independent theory construction encodes a deterministic polynomial-space
tape in directed local dynamics. A path generator has eigenvalues 0 and -1
while traversal and relaxation can be exponentially long; large Jordan blocks
expose the missing nonnormality information. The note develops the prediction
hardness implication and its strong known predecessors.

Reason not selected: strong complexity/mixing prior art and an idealized error-
free local compiler limit the new physical consequence. No fault-tolerant
experimental prediction separation was constructed.

D. WEAK-EXPORT RECOVERY FROM PURE CONTINUOUS PORTS
Question: can the supplied many-body pure-port collector's strong-export
threshold be removed while retaining an efficient finite decoder?

Root examined entropy tensorization and decoupling as a route to rapid source/
reference forgetting. A local-light-cone recovery strategy did not resolve
overlapping output recovery and boundary correlations. Generic recovery
existence or an abstract Petz map does not establish efficient implementation;
known decoders can retain a source-dimension cost.

Reason not selected: no complete efficient decoder was established. This is a
decisive construction gap, not a negative theorem about all weak-export models.
No new weak-export recovery result is claimed or used as a premise.

AFTER SELECTION
Collaboration concentrated on the selected complete question. Existing Petz
recovery, physical inverse implementations, Barnum--Knill and Renes bounds,
homogenization, no-hiding, and repeated-interaction limits were reused explicitly.
The main increment is the finite physical acquisition recursion and its matched
cost law. Historical priority, broad hardware advantage, and a civilization-
scale application have not been established by this construction.
