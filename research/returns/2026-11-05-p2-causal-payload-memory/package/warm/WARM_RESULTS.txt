1. UNKNOWN LOCAL PORTS AND RARE FIRST FAILURE
research/returns/2026-11-05-p2-unknown-local-ports

Unknown-support acquisition: the operating register has a constructed or
independently calibrated product invariant law pi, an independent refresh
backbone with rates gamma_i that remains active during operation, and fresh
reciprocal ports with declared local reach S_alpha of size at most k. Local
transition support is not presumed known. Prepared lower-weight endpoints,
one actual collision, and local readout supply downhill rate intervals;
reciprocity supplies reverse rates. Zero counts retain positive upper bounds.

For reversible local error envelopes with maximum exit rates r_alpha,
  |E_true(f,f)-E_fit(f,f)| <= rho E_backbone(f,f),
  rho=max_i 2 sum_{alpha containing i}r_alpha/gamma_i.
This holds for every full-state function f. It does not silently exclude
outside-block dependence, inaccessible additional baths, or unobserved
catastrophes. A testing-only backbone does not satisfy the premise.

Fixed-start mean: for safe x0 and killed conductance Laplacian L,
  M_x0=e_x0^T L^{-1}pi_A,
  |M_true/M_fit-1| <= rho B_x0/(1-rho).
The starting mass kappa<=pi(x0), refresh/gap contrast g, and calibration costs
remain explicit: B_x0<=4/(sqrt(g) kappa), with B_x0<=2/sqrt(kappa) in the stated
rare regime. This is not an all-deadline relative failure-CDF claim.

Correlated product-invariant computation: overlapping local ports can create
correlated kinetics while preserving product pi. The all-terminal static
counting/conditional failure sampler, reverse return-avoidance trials, and
starting-state spectral correction yield the stated polynomial mean-time
inference. Costs include graph/table size, 2^k, inverse gap/refresh contrast,
inverse starting mass, accuracy and confidence. The generic Karger FPRAS is
invoked machinery, not an implementation supplied by the small examples.

Finite collision/factory realization: energy-conserving fresh Gibbs collisions
also require reciprocal amplitudes for detailed balance. The stricter paired-
occupation construction supports rational digital ancillary probabilities.
Finite ticks, null timeouts, padding and serial service give an actual clocked
register with P and Q=(P-I)/T and exactly equal first failed ENDPOINT means
T E[N]=(-Q_A)^{-1}1. Intermediate coherent-pulse failures are not monitored.
Factory precision requires its independent nondemolition verification,
nonbackaction, dephasing and protected-idle contract. Its exponentially small
allowed preparation error has a paid polynomial verification cost under that
contract; it is not a cure for arbitrary extra hardware noise.
In particular, relative ancillary-distribution error zeta yields the ratewise
tree/forest mean bound exp(plus/minus (2n-1)[-log(1-zeta)]) for n safe states.
The conservative zeta=O(epsilon/2^m) must be retained. Under the stated sensor
and retry contract its repeated-check cost depends logarithmically on this
precision: O(m+k log(1/p_min)+log(k/epsilon)), with the sensor/retry factors
and actual service times still charged.

Separate equilibrium-safe initialization: deliberately prepare pi(.|A). If
pi(B)<=1/2, rejection takes at most two independent full-register preparations
on average. The rare branch has no kappa parameter, and its mean-form
perturbation interval is Mhat/(1+rho) to Mhat/(1-rho). This does not replace
all-working initialization. Write/verification, initial error, safe-set test,
and protected storage costs remain paid, and rare safe-set preparation is
outside the claimed efficient regime.
