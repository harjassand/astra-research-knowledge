INDEPENDENT AUDIT: PLANAR CONVEX RELAXATION, HOLONOMY AND THERMAL NOISE
2026-11-05. Internal mathematical audit; no new experiment or browsing.

1. DECLARED MODEL AND TARGET

Controls A(t) are symmetric 2x2 matrices with 0 <= A(t) <= Lambda I.
They are prescribed in advance, or randomly selected independently of the
unknown input and bath trajectory. The deterministic fundamental matrix obeys
Mdot=-A M, M(0)=I. The target is M(T)=exp(-s) R_theta, with |theta|<=pi.
This is an attenuated rotation of every unknown input, not a lossless rotation
or the movement of one already known point. Feedback is a different resource.
No slew-rate or acceleration constraint is included. Endpoint changes of A can
be ideal quenches. Such a model is not a fabricated physical apparatus.

2. DETERMINISTIC FRONTIER: INDEPENDENTLY CHECKED

Let a=tr(A)/2, B=A-aI, k=||B||op. Then a>=k and a+k<=Lambda.
Normalize Y=exp(integral a) M. It obeys Ydot=-B Y in SL(2,R).
The right-invariant symmetric-traceless distribution is horizontal for the
left SO(2) quotient, with projection P=Y^T Y. In the metric
ds^2=(1/2)tr(P^-1 dP P^-1 dP), this is hyperbolic space of curvature -1.
Its projected speed is 2k. The connection holonomy is one-half signed area,
up to orientation convention. Thus a loop ending at R_theta has signed area
2theta modulo 4pi, and projected length 2K, K=integral k.

Hyperbolic isoperimetry gives length >= sqrt(A^2+4pi|A|). This extends from
Jordan loops to all rectifiable loops: decompose finite self-intersections
into signed simple loops, apply each inequality, then use the subadditivity
of f(x)=sqrt(x^2+4pi x) on x>=0 and |sum A_i|<=sum |A_i|. Approximation handles
general rectifiable loops. Multiple windings or signed-area cancellation
cannot improve the bound. Choosing the minimal area modulo4pi yields

  K >= L_theta := sqrt(theta^2+2pi|theta|).

Consequently s>=L_theta and T>=(s+L_theta)/Lambda. At theta=pi, either
orientation of the minimal circle is possible. At theta=0 the construction
reduces to isotropic contraction, with no nontrivial loop.

Attainer: S=diag(1,-1), J=[[0,-1],[1,0]],
  A(t)=a I+k R_(omega t) S R_(-omega t),
  a=s/T, k=L_theta/T, omega T=theta+sign(theta)pi.
For Z=R_(-omega t)Y, Zdot=(-kS-omega J)Z. The constant generator squares to
(k^2-omega^2)I and sqrt(omega^2-k^2)T=pi. Hence Z(T)=-I and Y(T)=R_theta.
Both eigenvalues a+-k lie in [0,Lambda] at T=(s+L_theta)/Lambda.

The underlying SL2 distance and hyperbolic geometry are established prior
mathematics, as the root literature audit confirms. The PSD-generator
reduction is short and should not be advertised as a new foundational theory.

3. GENERAL TARGET REDUCTION

For any F in GL^+(2), put s=-log(det F)/2 and N=exp(s)F. If d_CC(I,N) is the
standard SL2 symmetric-traceless horizontal distance with norm ||B||op,
then F is reachable precisely when s>=d_CC, and its minimum time is
(s+d_CC)/Lambda. Necessity follows by the same scalar split; sufficiency
uses a constant-speed minimizing horizontal curve and a=s/T. A minimizer
exists by bounded-control compactness on matrix balls and lower semicontinuity.
This is a reduction to established geometry, not by itself a new primitive.

4. THERMAL CHANNEL AND A POSITIVE COMPOSITION QUANTITY

Let dx=-A(t)x dt+sqrt(2D)dW, D>0. For a prescribed schedule the channel is
x_T=F x_0+xi, with xi centered Gaussian, input-independent, covariance Q.
Write m=D/Lambda. Direct Lyapunov integration gives

  E := Q-m(I-FF^T)
     =2m integral F(T,t)(Lambda I-A(t))F(T,t)^T dt >=0.

For channel composition, E_21=E_2+F_2 E_1 F_2^T. Moving an isotropic maximal
OU segment (cI,m(1-c^2)I) from before a channel (F,Q) to after it reduces Q by
(1-c^2)E. This is a valid Loewner comparison, not a claim that arbitrary
physical cooling or reset is free.

5. COMPLETE NOISE ORDER AMONG TIME-OPTIMAL PROTOCOLS

At T=(s+L)/Lambda, every admissible path has K=L and a+k=Lambda almost
everywhere. Its normalized shape is a minimizing circle, up to orientation.
Let ell=integral k, and d(t)=integral(a-k). Final d is delta=s-L.
Let G_ell denote the remaining transition of the fastest rank-one boundary
rotator, A=Lambda P, parameterized by its shape arclength. Then

 E_T=4m integral_0^L exp[-2(delta-d(ell))]
             G_ell P_perp(ell) G_ell^T dell.

Since d(ell)>=0, this is at least exp(-2delta) E_rot. Thus, for each protocol,

 Q >= exp(-2delta) R_alpha Q_rot R_alpha^T
                  +m(1-exp(-2delta))I

for one optimal-path orientation alpha. The fastest boundary rotator followed
by isotropic maximal-rate cooling attains equality.

IMPORTANT CORRECTION: there is no single Loewner-minimal covariance across
all orientations. The minimal covariances form a rotation orbit and are
generally pairwise incomparable. Their trace and eigenvalues coincide.
The orbit minimizes every rotation-invariant Loewner-monotone functional
among deterministic time-optimal protocols.

For u=|theta|, L=sqrt(u^2+2pi u), rho=L/(pi+u), f=1-exp(-2L), the boundary
rotator covariance eigenvalues are

 q_plus/minus = 2m f [1+rho^2 +- rho sqrt(1+rho^2)].

The final optimal eigenvalues are exp(-2delta)q_plus/minus
+m(1-exp(-2delta)). Constants agree with direct integration in the rotating
frame. In that frame Q_rot=alpha I+v_S S+v_K K, K=[[0,1],[1,0]], where
alpha=2m f(1+rho^2), v_S=-2m f rho^2,
v_K=sign(theta)2m f rho. The lab covariance is R_theta Q_rot R_theta^T.

Anderson's centered-Gaussian inequality gives a stronger quality statement.
If Q>=R_alpha Q_* R_alpha^T, then for every radius r,
P(||xi_Q||<=r)<=P(||xi_*||<=r). All minimal orientations have the same radial
law. Therefore the same all-radius optimality survives arbitrary mixtures
of independently preselected time-optimal schedules with the same F.

For eigenvalues q1,q2, the optimal squared error is q1 Z1^2+q2 Z2^2.
This specifies the entire radial error distribution, not only a mean score.

6. RANDOMIZATION AND FEEDBACK LIMITS

Mixing two orientations separated by pi/2 makes the covariance isotropic,
with value tr(Q_*)I/2, while preserving F. The error is a Gaussian mixture,
not generally Gaussian. Thus a claimed optimum of the largest covariance
eigenvalue over randomized protocols would be false. The radial-law optimum
above remains valid and attained by that same mixture.

For preselected random schedules, fixed F is also necessary if the required
additive-error covariance is independent of every unknown x0: covariance is
E Q_i+Var(F_i x0). Evaluating all x0, including0, forces F_i=F almost surely.
If only E(F_i)=F is required, the target is weaker and input-dependent error
is being hidden. Adaptive bath-correlated controls invalidate the conditional
Gaussian argument and are not covered by these claims.

7. EXPECTED PHYSICAL-MODEL WORK, INCLUDING QUENCHES

Take mobility1/gamma, D=k_B T_bath/gamma, and potential
U(x,t)=gamma x^T A(t)x/2. Let S(t)=E[x_t x_t^T] include the mean contribution.
Expected heat to the bath is
 gamma integral[tr(A^2 S)-D tr A]dt.
If the initial and final apparatus potential is A_ref=Lambda I, including
all endpoint quenches, energy balance and the trace covariance equation give

 W = gamma integral tr[(A^2-Lambda A)S]dt
       +2 k_B T_bath (Lambda T-s).

For the rank-one fastest rotator followed by isotropic maximal cooling,
A^2=Lambda A throughout. Its expected modulation work is therefore exactly

 W = 2 k_B T_bath L_theta,

independent of the initial finite second moment. This is not work-optimality:
other time-optimal protocols with interior eigenvalues can have smaller work
but more output noise. It is also not a pathwise work bound. Real actuator
friction, energy recovery inefficiency, observation, and initial preparation
are not supplied by this ideal potential-work calculation.

The number W is NET expected potential-modulation work. It credits recoverable
negative work during quenches or parts of the rotation. It does not bound gross
positive supply, peak power, an energy-recovery buffer, or stochastic work tails.
For unrestricted input amplitude those resources need not be uniformly bounded;
Gaussian thermal excursions also preclude a finite pathwise energy cap.

The fastest rotating rank-one potential has interior slew
||Adot||op=Lambda^2(pi+|theta|)/(2L_theta), diverging as theta tends to0;
endpoint quenches have unbounded ideal instantaneous slew/power. Finite slew
and actuator costs are additional resources. The time optimum concerns only
the declared matrix-amplitude cap.

8. STRICT THRESHOLD FOR EXACT ISOTROPIC GAUSSIAN NOISE

This extra result is stronger than an isotropic-covariance assertion and is
not needed for the all-radius time-optimal theorem.

For theta!=0 at s=L, PSD saturation forces a=k. Every equality-circle noise
integrand has, in one fixed rotating-frame traceless direction, a component
with sign(theta) times a positive multiple of sin^2(pi tau). Arbitrary
positive time weighting, zero-control pauses, or reparameterization cannot
cancel it. The amplitude cap supplies a positive minimum component. Thus
the added Gaussian noise cannot be isotropic at the contraction boundary.
A mixture cannot become an isotropic Gaussian: a centered Gaussian mixture
is Gaussian only when its covariance is almost surely constant (e.g. fourth
cumulant in each direction is three times the variance of that conditional
variance). Randomization can make only its covariance, or radial symmetry,
isotropic without producing a Gaussian law.

Conversely, for every delta=s-L>0 there is a finite explicit construction of
the exact channel with isotropic Gaussian added noise. Use the fastest
boundary rotator with Q_g=alpha_g I+B_g, ||B_g||op=b_g>0. Choose 0<eta<=delta
sufficiently small that

 w=b_g/[D(exp(2eta)-1)]-eta/Lambda >=0.

After the rotator, apply A=Lambda P_v for eta/Lambda, wait w with A=0,
then A=Lambda P_(v-perp) for eta/Lambda. Choose v along the smaller-noise
eigenvector of Q_g. This excursion has mean exp(-eta)I and adds traceless
noise exactly opposite exp(-2eta)B_g. Finish with A=Lambda I for
(delta-eta)/Lambda. The exact final mean is exp(-s)R_theta and added noise is
q_iso I, where

 q_iso=exp(-2delta)[alpha_g+b_g coth(eta)]
                         +m(1-exp(-2delta)),
 T_total=(2L+delta)/Lambda+b_g/[D(exp(2eta)-1)].

All operating matrices are zero or Lambda times an orthogonal projector.
With the endpoint reference trap Lambda I, expected potential work is
2k_B T_bath(Lambda T_total-s).

This proves the exact existence threshold s>L for a finite-time isotropic
Gaussian-noise channel when nonzero rotation, D>0 and finite Lambda are fixed.
For this construction, taking eta=delta near the boundary gives time, noise,
and expected work of order1/delta. That divergence has NOT been proved optimal.
Allowing arbitrary extra additive force/noise controls changes the problem.

9. CONSEQUENCE AND NOVELTY LIMITS

The SR distance result is known. The thermal minimal orbit, simultaneous
radial-tail optimum, pulse isotropization and resource accounting are exact
deductions for this specified classical linear-Gaussian control family; root
must challenge their priority against stochastic-control literature.

No new law of nature, observed anomaly, manufactured device, universally
reusable physical primitive, or civilian-scale consequence has been earned.
Attenuation is severe: a pi/2 rotation requires s>=pi*sqrt(5)/2 and retains
only about3% of input amplitude; a pi rotation retains about0.43%. Reamplifying
the result is an additional operation and cost, not included here.

10. TWO QUANTITATIVE DETAILS FOR THE EXTRA ISOTROPIZATION RESULT

The fixed-sign K component at s=L is at least
2(D/Lambda)(1-exp(-2L))*L/(pi+|theta|)>0. Every traversal must spend at
least the fastest-path time at each shape position; additional waiting has
the same sign in that component.

No unspecified small-parameter choice is needed in the construction. Put
beta=Lambda*b_g/D and choose
 eta=min(delta,1,0.5*sqrt(beta/(exp(2)-1))).
For eta<=1, eta*(exp(2eta)-1)<=(exp(2)-1)eta^2<=beta, guaranteeing w>=0.

APPENDIX A. INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE ALL-TARGET EXTENSION

Task: sections3 and6 of invention_variation/proof_and_claim_boundary.txt.
Verdict: the mathematical scalar solver, cut-locus classification and common
radial-noise law of all shortest shapes are sound, subject to two explicit
qualifications below. There is a real numerical conditioning defect in the
current floating implementation near a rotation; it is not a counterexample
to the exact mathematical formulas.

A1. EXPLICIT ENDPOINT AND EXHAUSTION OF CANDIDATES

Represent N as z -> u z+v conjugate(z), where
u=(N11+N22+i(N21-N12))/2 and v=(N11-N22+i(N12+N21))/2.
Then |u|^2-|v|^2=1. For the normal geodesic
Y=R_(alpha+w) exp[-d E-w J] R_(-alpha), direct exponentiation gives
 u=exp(iw)(C-i w F),  v=-d F exp(i(2alpha+w)).
Here C=cosh z,F=sinh(z)/z,z^2=d^2-w^2 in the hyperbolic case; replace
C,F by cos(delta),sin(delta)/delta for delta^2=w^2-d^2.

For a nonrotation minimum, delta<pi. At delta=pi the endpoint is a rotation
independent of alpha; replacing that prefix by a different equal-length
prefix produces a corner if a purported minimizer continued beyond it.
Contactness excludes nonconstant abnormal minimizers, and normal extremals
are smooth, so this cannot be minimizing. Hyperbolic and parabolic paths
have F>0; for0<delta<pi the elliptic F is also positive. Therefore q=|v|=dF
and alpha=(arg(v)-w-pi)/2 modulo pi. These cover all candidates.

The scalar equations printed in section3 follow directly. Their derivatives
are correct. There is also a particularly useful check on cost monotonicity:
along either nonzero-angle branch,
 dd/d|theta_unwrapped|=b/q>0,
where b=sqrt(q^2-sinh(z)^2) in the hyperbolic branch and
b=sqrt(q^2+sin(delta)^2) in the elliptic branch. Thus principal absolute theta
minimizes over all windings. At theta=pi precisely the two signs tie.

A2. NEGATIVE SYMMETRIC TARGETS, INCLUDING HYPERBOLIC BRANCHES

For q>0, theta=pi means u is negative real, so N is symmetric negative
definite. These endpoints can lie on EITHER the elliptic or hyperbolic
parameter branch. For example q=6,20,100 put pi below h=q-atan(q), so their
minimizers are hyperbolic. The two minimizing signs are still the only two
and are related by an orthogonal reflection H commuting with N.

Let arg(v)=phi and choose H=R_(phi/2) diag(1,-1) R_(-phi/2). Then HNH=N.
Conjugating the positive-w path by H negates w and changes its initial angle
to the negative-w solution modulo pi. Consequently the covariance satisfies
Q_minus=H Q_plus H^T. The two paths have identical covariance eigenvalues and
radial error distributions. No assumption of elliptic type is needed.

For q>0 and principal theta strictly between -pi and pi, the scalar root and
alpha determine one minimizing shape. For q=0, nonidentity rotations have the
free orientation family already audited. Thus the claimed common radial law
for all shortest shapes of each fixed arbitrary target does follow.

A3. SCOPE CORRECTION TO THE RANDOMIZATION EXAMPLE

The statement that a fair coin between orientations differing by pi/2 yields
isotropic covariance WITH THE SAME MEAN MAP must be restricted to ROTATION
TARGETS (and their scalar attenuation). For a generic target N, conjugating
the program rotates N too. Its minimizing shape is usually unique. At a
negative symmetric target there are only the two reflected shapes; averaging
their covariances generally leaves unequal diagonal entries and does not
produce an isotropic covariance. This qualification does not damage the
all-target radial-tail optimality theorem.

A4. NUMERICAL CONDITIONING DEFECT IN THE CURRENT COMPILER

shape_distance solves for elliptic delta directly with absolute root tolerance
1e-14 and subsequently divides by sin(delta). At fixed nonzero theta and
q approaching zero, pi-delta is O(q), so tiny absolute root error produces
large endpoint error even though every entry of the requested N is O(1).
This is not excluded by a description saying 'ordinary-scale input matrices'.

With theta=.7 and arg(v)=.6, independent checks of the current implementation
gave normalized endpoint errors approximately:
 q=1e-8: 5.13e-7;
 q=1e-12: 1.04e-2;
 q=2e-14: 5.35e-1.
Below its hard1e-14 threshold the routine instead substitutes a rotation and
returns small absolute error. A stable near-rotation chart or arbitrary-
precision root is needed before claiming a uniformly accurate compiler.

One stable chart is epsilon=pi-delta=q*z. Compute
 t=sin(q*z)/q=z*sinc(q*z),
 d=(pi-q*z)/t, w=d*sqrt(1+t^2),
 phi=atan2(q*sqrt(1+t^2),-cos(q*z)),
 and solve w-phi=|theta| for z near pi/L_theta.
This avoids evaluating sin(pi-small) or dividing q by a numerically unresolved
distance to pi. For other regimes retain the original charts, with suitable
endpoint limits; certified all-scale accuracy would still require an explicit
precision/error policy rather than a fixed floating tolerance.

A5. EXECUTED AUDIT CHECKS

Independent samples included nonrotation elliptic endpoints, principal angles
approaching pi, and both geodesics at negative symmetric endpoints with
q=.01,.4,1,3,6,20,100. At ordinary conditioning the endpoint formulas agreed
within1.1e-11 absolute norm (negative-cut sample up to1.4e-10 at q=100).
The reflected covariance identities agreed within4e-15, with eigenvalue
agreement within3e-15. The two-path averages remained anisotropic, confirming
the scope correction above. all_target_results.json records all values.

These are checks of exact model formulas, not new physical evidence. They
support the proof but do not replace the global minimizing-path argument.

APPENDIX B. FIRST-VARIATION AUDIT (COMPLETED)

The analytic first-variation construction in
invention_variation/slower_loop_derivation.txt is correct for
0<|theta|<=pi, D>0, finite Lambda>0, s>L_theta.

Control checked independently: with H(z)^2=rho^2 I and
B=(I+H)/sqrt(1-rho^2), differentiation gives
B'B^-1=[H'-rho^2 psi' J]/(1-rho^2).
Thus beta'=rho^2 psi'/(1-rho^2) cancels the skew part exactly. The symmetric
part has norm sqrt(rho'^2+rho^2)/(1-rho^2)=dl/du. Hence the stated S_hat has
eigenvalues+/-1, A=(Lambda/2)(I+S_hat) has eigenvalues0,Lambda, and the stated
time change gives M=e^-l Y. The holonomy-constrained endpoint is exact.
For sufficiently small epsilon all denominators and speeds stay nonzero.

The implicit area condition and reflection make a(epsilon) and K(epsilon)
even. I independently re-derived
l1'=4a sin(u)cos(u)(1-w+a^2w^2)/(1-a^2w)^2,
where w=sin^2(u), and therefore the printed integral for l1 is correct.
At fixed arclength u1=-(1-a^2w)l1/a, so
delta rho=cos(u)[2aw-(1-a^2w)l1]
 =cos(u)[-2(1-a^2w)/a^3][a^2w+log(1-a^2w)].
All constants and signs match. This is positive in the first half and
negative in the second, giving h1(L-l)=-h1(l).

The trace functional is also correct: the remaining response has normalized
squared Frobenius norm tr(B^-2)=2(1+rho^2)/(1-rho^2). Its bath integration and
final isotropic cooling give the printed formula. K'(0)=0 removes both the
moving-endpoint and cooling derivatives. Pairing l and L-l proves strict
negative trace derivative for every positive theta through pi. Reflection
proves the same statement for negative theta. No unresolved analytic issue
was found in this proof.

Finite-penalty corollary: for J=alpha trQ+lambda T+mu W_net with alpha>0 and
finite lambda,mu>=0, the perturbation changes noise by -c epsilon+o(epsilon),
c>0, but time and net expected work by nonnegative O(epsilon^2). Therefore
J decreases for sufficiently small positive epsilon with K<=s. This also
holds for locally Lipschitz time/work penalties. Do NOT phrase it as covering
all finite-valued penalties: sqrt(T-T_min) has singular slope and can change
the conclusion. Hard deadlines and lexicographic time priority remain valid.
The fastest point remains Pareto efficient, since the improvement takes
longer and costs more net work. The proof concerns trace noise; it does not
show improvement of every radial tail or solve the full slower-time optimum.

At s=L, positivity forces K=L and a=k. Every shape is shortest; only speed
and A=0 pauses vary. The remaining response at each shape position is then
timing-independent, so
Q-Q_fast=2D integral F_remaining F_remaining^T [dt-2dl/Lambda]>=0.
The extra-time measure is nonnegative. For any genuinely longer finite
duration it has positive mass, making the difference positive definite.
The previously proved orientation and randomization arguments give global
radial-noise optimality across all durations at s=L. For theta=0, maximal
isotropic OU likewise attains the bath floor for every s; this is another
exception to the nonzero-angle surplus-attenuation improvement.

Work means NET EXPECTED potential-modulation work, with counted quenches and
recoverable negative work. Gross supply, peak power, control resolution and
work tails are not bounded. Arbitrarily small epsilon is a mathematical
control resource; no physical accuracy claim follows.
