SOFT-CONSTRAINT ORDER PRESERVATION AND A SHARP COMPONENT BOUND
Internal theorem, 2026-11-05. No literature priority claim. Parent is auditing
Gortler/Holmes-Cerfon/Theran 2025 and Tarnai/Lengyel 2011; the basic work-order
organizing primitive must not be presented as new in light of those sources.

1. Soft-constraint lemma

Let x be one imposed scalar coordinate and q in R^m the internal coordinates.
Let F=(F_1,...,F_m) and g be real analytic near (0,0), with F(0,0)=g(0,0)=0 and
J=partial_q F(0,0) invertible. Let W(x,q) be an analytic positive definite
matrix and w(x,q)>0 analytic. Define

 E(x,q)=1/2 F(x,q)^T W(x,q) F(x,q)+1/2 w(x,q) g(x,q)^2.

The hard constraint F=0 has a unique analytic solution q_h(x). Write
h(x)=g(x,q_h(x)). There is a unique local analytic minimizing branch q_*(x),
and its locally relaxed energy U(x)=E(x,q_*(x)) satisfies:

 * If h is identically zero near zero, U is identically zero.
 * If h(x)=c*x^r+o(x^r), c!=0, then

   U(x)=[w_eff/2]*h(x)^2+o(h(x)^2),
   w_eff=[w_0^(-1)+A W_0^(-1) A^T]^(-1)>0,
   A=(partial_q g)(0,0) J^(-1).

In particular ord_0 U=2r. Finite positive compliance changes the leading
coefficient but does not change this vanishing order.

Proof. Use the analytic coordinate change (x,q)->(x,z=F(x,q)); its inverse
is q=phi(x,z). The output can be written exactly

 g(x,phi(x,z))=h(x)+a(x,z) z,

where a is analytic and a(0,0)=A (integrate its z-derivative along the straight
segment). The z-Hessian of E at the origin is W_0+w_0 A^T A, positive definite,
so the analytic implicit function theorem gives a unique analytic stationary
and minimizing branch z_*(x).

For rigor in the small residual, temporarily replace h(x) by an independent
parameter h in the energy. At h=0 the unique minimum is z=0 for every small
x, because E is a sum of nonnegative squares and W is positive definite.
The implicit function theorem in (x,h) yields

 z_*(x,h)= -w_0 W_0^(-1) A^T h/(1+w_0 A W_0^(-1)A^T)+o(h)

as x,h->0. Substitute this into the energy; a scalar Schur complement gives
w_eff above. Setting h=h(x) gives the claim. No assumption about the unknown
future of the specimen enters; this is a constitutive-model deduction.

An elementary order-only proof is also available: by bounded derivatives,
|h(x)| <= |g(x,phi(x,z))|+M|z|. Positive quadratic penalties therefore give
c*h(x)^2 <= E(x,phi(x,z)), uniformly locally, while z=0 supplies the matching
upper bound C*h(x)^2. Thus the energy and h^2 have the same order.

2. Ordinary Hookean axial-spring specialization

Consider N axial springs and N generalized coordinates (x,q_1,...,q_(N-1)).
All coordinates describe points constrained by declared affine guides, so
each spring's squared length is a quadratic polynomial in those coordinates.
Every rest length l_i is strictly positive, and all springs are unstressed at
the reference point. Their actual energy is

 E=1/2 sum_i K_i (r_i-l_i)^2, K_i>0.

Let f_i=r_i^2-l_i^2, a polynomial of degree at most 2. Near the reference,

 E=1/2 sum_i [K_i/(r_i+l_i)^2] f_i^2.

Suppose the Hessian in the N-1 internal coordinates is positive definite.
Equivalently the internal Jacobian of the strain vector has rank N-1. Choose
N-1 independent strain rows as F and the remaining one as g; then the lemma
applies with analytic positive weights. Let h be the remaining squared-length
residual along F=0.

Either h is identically zero, giving a true local zero-energy motion, or
ord_x h <= 2^N. To prove the latter, the zero of all N quadratic polynomials
is locally isolated in the complexified smooth curve F=0; local Bezout bounds
the intersection multiplicity by the product of the N degrees, at most 2^N.
Because x is an analytic local parameter on this curve, that multiplicity is
exactly ord_x h.

Therefore an isolated zero-energy reference has local relaxed work order

   ord_x U <= 2^(N+1).

This is a bound for the stated regular internal-relaxation family. It is not
claimed for arbitrary mechanisms with several zero modes, singular internal
relaxation, nonaffine guide constraints, preload, unilateral contact or general
nonanalytic constitutive laws.

This is the minimizing branch near the prepared reference. Remote alternative
zero-energy configurations can change an unrestricted global minimum. In the
explicit attainer below the only zero-energy configuration is the origin;
coercivity therefore makes its local minimum global for small imposed x.

3. Exact attainment with N finite ordinary springs

Let e_i alternate between orthogonal coordinate unit vectors. Put O_0=(0,0)
and O_(i+1)=O_i-l e_(i+1). There are N movable guided points

 P_i=O_i+q_i e_i, i=0,...,N-1, with q_0=x,

and a fixed final anchor P_N=O_N (equivalently q_N=0). Join adjacent points
with N ordinary axial springs of rest length l and arbitrary positive finite
stiffnesses K_i. The exact spring lengths are

 r_i=sqrt(q_i^2+(l-q_(i+1))^2), i=0,...,N-2,
 r_(N-1)=sqrt(q_(N-1)^2+l^2).

Take the first N-1 squared-length constraints as F. Their internal Jacobian
at zero is diagonal with entries -2l. The hard curve has

 q_i=Fmap^i(x), Fmap(t)=l-sqrt(l^2-t^2),
 q_i ~ x^(2^i)/(2l)^(2^i-1).

The remaining squared-length residual is h=q_(N-1)^2, so its order is 2^N.
Its internal derivative at the origin is zero, hence A=0 in the lemma. Thus
the finite-spring relaxed energy is

 U(x) ~ (K_(N-1)/2) * x^(2^(N+1))/(2l)^(2^(N+1)-2).

The sharp upper bound is attained with all K_i=l=1. There are N springs,
N ideal frictionless guides, and a fixed anchor. Reference coordinates are
integers of magnitude O(N), so an explicit list costs O(N log N) bits. This
does not mean that the real manufacturing tolerances cost only that many bits.

Every zero-energy state must have q_(N-1)=0 by the final spring; working
backwards then forces every earlier q_i=0. The equilibrium is therefore
isolated. Positive definiteness of the internal Hessian holds already from
the first N-1 spring terms. The boundary work is positive at every sufficiently
small nonzero imposed x despite all its derivatives below order 2^(N+1)
vanishing. The leading restoring force has order 2^(N+1)-1.

4. Angular perturbation of the attained construction

If adjacent guide directions have delta_i=e_i dot e_(i+1) instead of zero,
each nonterminal spring has linearized strain delta_i q_i-q_(i+1). The final
spring has linearized strain delta_(N-1) q_(N-1). With all delta_i nonzero,
the complete strain Jacobian is invertible and the boundary energy is
quadratic. In terms of nonterminal errors
e_i=q_(i+1)-delta_i q_i, the final displacement/strain is a linear form, so
the exact quadratic boundary stiffness is

 k_lin = A^2 / sum_(j=0)^(N-1) B_j^2/K_j,
 A=product_(i=0)^(N-1) delta_i,
 B_j=product_(i=j+1)^(N-1) delta_i.

For the final index the product is empty, B_(N-1)=1. This is the stiffness
of N compliant transformations in series. In particular if all K_i=1 and
all delta_i=delta, k_lin=delta^(2N)/(1+delta^2+...+delta^(2N-2)).
Generic manufacturing errors destroy the exact high order, even though the
resulting quadratic stiffness can be very small.

To resolve the ideal final transverse-spring nonlinearity at amplitude x,
its angular error must be small compared with q_(N-1)/(2l). At fixed 0<|x|/l<1,
this requires exponentially many precision bits in N, by the exact iteration
bounds in geometry_primitive.txt. The construction consequently cannot earn
a robust manufacturing advantage merely by counting components.

5. Novelty and consequence boundary

The soft-constraint lemma is a standard kind of Lyapunov-Schmidt/Schur
complement reduction; no claim is made that the technique is new. The order
bound follows from classical Bezout once that reduction is recognized. The
specific sharp finite-component law and explicit attained geometry require
comparison with primary literature, including the direct 2025 higher-order
rigidity/energy and 2011 linkage-chain antecedents identified by the parent.

If not already present there, the earned mathematical consequence is a sharp
resource law for the vanishing order of relaxed work in this nondegenerate
one-input family. It is not a general new theory of mechanical objects, and
it does not establish a physically prepared device or a civilization-scale
capability. Ordinary nonlinear mechanics with the same inputs remains fully
capable of calculating the result.
