FINITE-STAGE CONTRACTION / ROTATION LAW
Internal verification, 2026-11-05. Exact model mathematics, not a physical
actuator implementation. No external browsing by this agent.

PROBLEM

The planar linear evolution is x_dot=-A(t)x, with A symmetric and
0<=A(t)<=Lambda*I. Permit at most N constant-control stages, instantaneous
switches, and freely chosen nonnegative stage durations. Seek the full mean
map M=exp(-s)*R_theta, where theta is principal, -pi<=theta<=pi.
The nontrivial case is 0<abs(theta)<=pi; identity is a separate trivial case.

Define

  s_N(theta)=N*acosh[cos(pi/N)/cos((pi+abs(theta))/N)].

A finite-stage realization exists if and only if

  N > 2+2*abs(theta)/pi, and s>=s_N(theta).

For a finite cap Lambda>0, the minimum total evolution time is

  T_N=(s+s_N(theta))/Lambda.

Among determinant-one positive factors whose product is R_theta, the minimum
sum of logarithmic anisotropies is s_N. The minimum possible worst condition
number among N factors is exp(2*s_N/N), attained by the same equal-factor
construction. These are distinct from heat, work, switching costs and noise.

STAGE NORMALIZATION

Every physical stage B_j=exp(-tau_j*A_j) is positive definite. Write

  B_j=exp(-a_j)*H_j, det H_j=1,
  eigenvalues(H_j)=exp(+/-k_j), k_j>=0.

Positive-semidefinite A_j implies a_j>=k_j. The cap gives
tau_j >= (a_j+k_j)/Lambda. Product determinant fixes sum a_j=s.
Hence contraction s>=sum k_j and time T>=(s+sum k_j)/Lambda.
The normalized factors obey H_(N-1)...H_0=R_theta.

THE HYPERBOLIC POLYGON

Let G_j=H_(j-1)...H_0 (G_0=I) and P_j=G_j^T G_j. The positive definite
unit-determinant matrices P form the hyperbolic plane of curvature -1 under

  distance(P,Q)=acosh[trace(P^(-1)Q)/2].

A constant symmetric stage gives a geodesic segment between P_j and P_(j+1),
of length 2*k_j. Since G_N=R_theta, the polygon closes at P_N=P_0=I.
Its perimeter is P=2*sum k_j. Zero-length stages are allowed and can be
regarded as degenerate vertices.

Its signed algebraic area A_s satisfies

  A_s/2 = theta (mod 2*pi).

One way to check the factor 1/2 is to parameterize
P=R_(phi/2) diag(exp(r),exp(-r)) R_(-phi/2). Write the lift
G=R_psi P^(1/2). Requiring G_dot G^(-1) symmetric gives

  psi_dot=(cosh(r)-1)*phi_dot/2.

The hyperbolic area form is sinh(r) dr wedge dphi, with smooth primitive
(cosh(r)-1)dphi. Integration around the closed polygon yields the claim.
Thus abs(A_s)>=2*abs(theta) for a principal theta. Additional winding cannot
reduce this required absolute algebraic area.

WHY SELF-INTERSECTIONS DO NOT INVALIDATE THE BOUND

Lemma. Every oriented closed geodesic N-gon in the hyperbolic plane can be
replaced by a convex polygon with at most N vertices, no larger perimeter,
and area at least the absolute algebraic area of the original polygon.

Proof by induction on N, initially for general position. If the polygon is
simple, its convex hull suffices: hull area is larger, hull perimeter no
larger, and hull vertices are a subset of the original vertices.

Otherwise split at a transverse crossing of two nonadjacent sides. The two
closed loops have vertex counts N_1,N_2<N with N_1+N_2=N+2, perimeters adding
to the original perimeter, and signed areas adding to A_s. Convexify each
loop inductively. If one resulting area is zero it can be discarded. For
positive areas, choose a vertex of each convex polygon. Place these vertices
together by hyperbolic isometries and point the two interior-angle bisectors
in opposite directions. Each polygon lies in its angle cone of width <pi;
the two interiors are disjoint. Their shared vertex lies strictly inside the
convex hull of their union, because the two opposite nonzero-width cones are
not contained in any half-plane through that vertex.

The hull therefore has at most (N_1-1)+(N_2-1)=N vertices. It contains both
disjoint interiors and has area at least |A_1|+|A_2|>=|A_s|. Its perimeter
is no more than the sum of the two original perimeters: the hull boundary
shortcuts the two closed polygonal walks through their common vertex, and
geodesic triangle inequalities cannot increase length. This completes the
induction. Repeated vertices, overlapping edges and tangencies follow by
small generic perturbations and continuity of perimeter and algebraic area.

This lemma removes the simple-polygon/winding-one assumption from the lower
bound. It does not assert that the original control path itself is convex.

CONVEX POLYGON ISOPERIMETRY (CLASSICAL INPUT)

For a convex hyperbolic N-gon of perimeter P and area A, the polygonal
isoperimetric inequality is

  A <= A_N(P)
    =2*N*acos[cos(pi/N)/cosh(P/(2*N))]-2*pi.

Equality is attained by a regular N-gon. At most N vertices are covered by
adding degenerate vertices and taking limits. This classical theorem needs
primary-source attribution; the root agent is acquiring that source.

The formula itself follows by dividing a regular polygon into 2N congruent
right hyperbolic triangles. With interior angle alpha and half-side P/(2N),

  cos(pi/N)=sin(alpha/2)*cosh(P/(2N)),
  A=(N-2)*pi-N*alpha.

Using convexification,

  2*abs(theta) <= abs(A_s) <= A_N(2*sum k_j).

The maximum area of a finite N-gon is strictly less than (N-2)*pi, giving
the strict factor-count condition. Inverting the inequality gives
sum k_j>=s_N(theta), and therefore the contraction and time lower bounds.

EXPLICIT ATTAINMENT

Let l=s_N/N and delta=sgn(theta)*(pi+abs(theta))/N. Set

  E=diag(exp(-l),exp(l)),
  H_j=R_(j*delta) E R_(-j*delta),  j=0,...,N-1.

Put X=R_(-delta) E. Then det X=1 and
trace X=2*cos(delta)*cosh(l)=2*cos(pi/N). Its distinct eigenvalues are
exp(+/-i*pi/N), so X^N=-I. Telescoping the factors gives

  H_(N-1)...H_0=R_(N*delta) X^N=R_theta.

For prescribed s>=s_N, let a=s/N and choose

  B_j=exp(-a)*H_j,
  tau_j=(a+l)/Lambda,
  A_j=(a*I-log H_j)/tau_j.

The eigenvalues of A_j are Lambda and Lambda*(a-l)/(a+l), so they are
nonnegative and at most Lambda. The factors multiply to the required
exp(-s)R_theta and their total duration is exactly (s+s_N)/Lambda.
At minimum contraction a=l, every stage is rank-one damping with eigenvalues
(Lambda,0) and duration 2l/Lambda. All N stages have positive duration for
nonzero theta. Strictly positive A would make minimum contraction an infimum;
the statement permits PSD A and so attains it.

The largest factor condition number is exp(2l). Any competing factorization
has max k_j>=sum k_j/N>=s_N/N, which proves the worst-conditioning optimum.

LIMITS AND SCOPE

For theta=0, exp(-s)I is realized by isotropic A=Lambda*I for time s/Lambda;
there is no nontrivial minimum-factor-count condition. For |theta|=pi, either
orientation realizes the same -I and N>=5 is necessary. At the excluded
boundaries (e.g. N=3,|theta|=pi/2 or N=4,|theta|=pi), l diverges and no finite
factorization exists.

As N->infinity,

  s_N -> sqrt(theta^2+2*pi*abs(theta)),

matching the continuous-control hyperbolic isoperimetric law. Finite N prices
an actual restriction on the control schedule, instead of silently assuming
an arbitrary continuously turning control direction.

This model permits costless instantaneous switching and assumes the complete
linear restoring law and its coefficient cap. It does not earn a physical
actuator, preparation, temperature control, measurement, useful signal/noise
endpoint, actuator work, reset/reuse or material throughput. Contraction is
not entropy production or heat. A physical claim must pay those separately.
