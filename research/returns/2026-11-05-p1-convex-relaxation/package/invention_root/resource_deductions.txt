RESOURCE DEDUCTIONS FOR THE DECLARED CLASSICAL CONTROL MODEL
5 November 2026. Mathematical deductions, not a physical demonstration.

1. A SUPPLIED UNKNOWN INPUT IS A RESOURCE

For dX=-A(t)X dt+sqrt(2D)dW, 0<=A<=Lambda I, let m=D/Lambda.
The exact noise identity gives Q>=m(I-FF^T). For the planar rotation target
F=exp(-s)R_theta, every prescribed protocol, of any duration, therefore has
Q>=m(1-exp(-2s))I. Independent preselected mixtures obey the same radial
probability bound. Adaptive feedback is outside this statement.

Suppose an unknown input has radius R and the desired error is at most a
fraction epsilon of the desired output radius exp(-s)R, with probability p.
The isotropic Gaussian baseline and the centered-ball Gaussian inequality
give the necessary condition

  epsilon^2 R^2 >= 2m(exp(2s)-1) log(1/(1-p)).

If the initial apparatus potential is U0=gamma Lambda |x|^2/2, its stored
input energy at that radius is E0=gamma Lambda R^2/2. With D=kBT/gamma,

  E0/(kBT) >= (exp(2s)-1) log(1/(1-p))/epsilon^2
           >= (exp(2L_theta)-1) log(1/(1-p))/epsilon^2.

This is a necessary stored-energy bound, not the work required to prepare
an unknown state and not a bound on the wall-plug energy of a trap. For a
rotation the baseline is not attained, so the bound is not claimed sharp.

At the exact fastest, least-noisy boundary protocol, if r_p is its radial
noise quantile in units sqrt(m), the exact input-radius requirement becomes

  E0/(kBT) >= exp(2L_theta) r_p^2/(2 epsilon^2).

Thus the small net mean modulation work 2kBT L_theta does not provide a
complete operating-cost account. It is compatible with a large initially
stored signal energy and with much larger gross positive work and power.

2. RESET IS NOT A FREE SUPPLIED OPERATION

Holding A=Lambda I after an operation gives
  mean(t)=exp(-Lambda t) mean(0),
  Cov(t)=mI+exp(-2Lambda t)(Cov(0)-mI).
For a covariance spectral-norm tolerance eta>0 and initial excess norm H,
  t >= max(0, log(H/eta)/(2Lambda))
suffices for the covariance tolerance. A separate mean tolerance is needed.
This statement is about moments, not total-variation equilibration of every
non-Gaussian input. Exact equilibration of an arbitrary input is generally
not achieved at finite time. The initial unknown signal is destroyed by reset.

3. A DIMENSION-INDEPENDENT COST THAT SURVIVES EXTRA COORDINATES

Consider the same isotropic bath and positive drift cap in R^n. Let B0 be
an n-by-k isometry encoding an unknown k-dimensional input, with the other
coordinates initially known. Put B(t)=M(t)B0. Contractivity gives
B(t)B(t)^T<=I. If the exact terminal signal map is B(T)=cU for an n-by-k
isometry U and 0<c<=1, then Phi(T,t)B(t)=cU. Consequently

  U^T Phi(T,t)Phi(T,t)^T U >= c^2 I_k,
  Q_out >= 2D c^2 T I_k.

The noise referred back to the input is at least 2DT I_k. Thus adding
auxiliary coordinates cannot turn arbitrarily slow passive transport into
a noiseless operation in this model, even if the planar contraction bound
does not extend to that larger access class. This is not a sharp all-resource
optimization theorem in higher dimensions.

There is also an elementary necessary time/loss bound for any individual
input direction whose initial and final directions differ by beta in [0,pi].
Write its deterministic trajectory as x=r u, |u|=1, and alpha=u^T A u.
Then -d(log r)/dt=alpha and
  |u_dot|^2=u^T A^2 u-alpha^2 <= alpha(Lambda-alpha).
If r(T)/r(0)=exp(-s), Cauchy-Schwarz and angular path length give
  beta^2 <= s(Lambda T-s),
  Lambda T >= s+beta^2/s  (s>0).
This applies in every dimension. It is not sufficient for simultaneously
rotating every unknown direction in a plane, and is not asserted sharp for
the encoded-plane operation. At s=0, a nonzero direction change is impossible.

4. PRECISE STATUS

The underlying Langevin, fluctuation-dissipation and contraction identities
are standard. These deductions account for resources in the constructed
operation; they are not claimed as a new microscopic law. No input preparation,
extra coordinate, force calibration, measurement, amplifier, or reset apparatus
has been physically supplied or operated in this research turn.
