PRIMARY-SOURCE CHALLENGE AND CLAIM BOUNDARY
5 November 2026. Root literature role only; no contact or new experiment.

1. DIRECT DEFEAT OF THE GEOMETRY PRIMITIVE

T. Tarnai and T. Lengyel, A remarkable structure of Leonardo and a higher-order
infinitesimal mechanism, Journal of Mechanics of Materials and Structures 6
(2011). https://msp.org/jomms/2011/6-1/jomms-v6-n1-p36-p.pdf
The exact circular displacement map 1-sqrt(1-x^2), exponentially increasing
order in a chain, and elastic-bar behavior occur directly in this paper.
Our construction does not earn a new organizing primitive.

S. Gortler, M. Holmes-Cerfon and L. Theran, Higher Order Rigidity and Energy,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.03108. Acquired PDF v2 is dated 1 July 2026.
Energy-growth order is explicitly developed there. Our bounded-component
deduction is retained as mathematics but is not the selected discovery.

R. Connelly and H. Servatius, Higher-Order Rigidity - What Is the Proper
Definition? (1994), DOI 10.1007/BF02574003.
https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~connelly/pdf/10.1007_BF02574003.pdf
This is relevant prior higher-order rigidity, not a new concept introduced here.

2. DIRECT DEFEAT OF THE HOLONOMY PRIMITIVE

U. Boscain and F. Rossi, Invariant Carnot-Caratheodory metrics on S3, SO3,
SL2 and lens spaces, SIAM Journal on Control and Optimization 47 (2008).
https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.3997
This supplies explicit geodesics, global cut loci and distance through an
elementary inverse function. The normalized SL2 geometry is prior art.

V. Berestovskii and I. Zubareva, Geodesics and shortest arcs of a special
sub-Riemannian metric on SL(2), https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.07221; related
distance article DOI 10.1134/S0037446617010037 (2017).
The rotation distance and topology are not new geometric laws.

M. Abdelgalil and T. Georgiou, The Holonomy of Optimal Mass Transport:
The Gaussian-Linear Case, https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14707v2 (2025 revision),
DOI 10.1109/TAC.2025.3598675. It explicitly retains particle labels and the
full transition map beyond covariance endpoints. Its displayed dynamics are
deterministic and its metric/cost differs from our bounded-contraction problem.
Neither label-preserving transport nor gradient-flow holonomy is new here.

The same authors, The factorization of matrices into products of positive
definite factors, https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.12560v2, 8 January 2026.
It gives constructive factorizations, minimum factor counts and conditioning
tradeoffs. Its displayed five-factor -I example has condition number about 30.
Our proved 25.377002107... optimum is an exact resource-law refinement, not a
claim that their method cannot find the same factors. Scaling their factors
to positive contractions is allowed in the comparison.

3. STRONG ORDINARY STOCHASTIC INCUMBENT

P. Abiuso et al., Thermodynamics and optimal protocols of multidimensional
quadratic Brownian systems (2022), DOI 10.1088/2399-6528/ac72f8.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.00764
The Langevin model, covariance dynamics, arbitrary quadratic controls and
work/heat accounting already exist. The model used here is an instance of
that ordinary framework. Its principal optimization is thermodynamic cost
of distribution paths, including partial control; our problem fixes the full
unknown-input map and a spectral rate cap, then lexicographically optimizes
duration and radial error. Numerical integration of the same ordinary model
agrees with our endpoint formulas. No predictive superiority over it is claimed.

4. CLASSICAL INPUT TO THE FINITE-STAGE PROOF

B. Sanki and A. Vadnere, Isoperimetric Inequality for Disconnected Regions,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.07697v2; DOI 10.1007/s10711-024-00961-9.
The introduction states the hyperbolic polygon isoperimetric theorem and
attributes it to K. Bezdek (1984), Ein elementarer Beweis fuer die
isoperimetrische Ungleichung in der Euklidischen und hyperbolischen Ebene,
Ann. Univ. Sci. Budapest, Eotvos Sect. Math. 27, 107-112.
The regular-polygon area formula and its monotonicity with the number of sides
are used as known geometry. The source's separate disconnected fixed-N problem
is not confused with our splitting argument, where total vertex counts change.

5. MEASURED BEHAVIOR THAT LIMITS A PHYSICAL INTERPRETATION

I. Martinez et al., Engineered swift equilibration of a Brownian particle,
Nature Physics 12, 843-846 (2016), DOI 10.1038/nphys3758.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.07821v2
This is an actual optical-trap experiment, with silica spheres of radius
1 micrometre in water. Reported stiffness endpoints were 0.5 and 1.0 pN/micrometre,
and the accelerated protocol lasted 0.5 ms. Methods report a 200 kHz switching
controller, 20 kHz acquisition, 2x10^4 cycles, absolute calibration errors
0.03 pN/micrometre and 0.1 nm, and 99% statistical intervals. It supports
controlled Brownian relaxation, not availability or validation of our rotating
rank-one two-dimensional trap, arbitrary input transformation or full costs.

T. Franosch et al., Resonances arising from hydrodynamic memory in Brownian
motion, Nature 478, 85-88 (2011), DOI 10.1038/nature10498.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.6161v2
The authors measured melamine-resin spheres in water and acetone. Records were
about 50 s at 1 MHz, roughly 5x10^7 samples; plotted spectral error bars are
standard errors after binning. In the strongest plotted acetone case the fitted
times were tau_f=2.4 microseconds and tau_K=19.2 microseconds. A resonance and
negative position correlations defeat the memoryless exponential model there.
The times are authors' fitted parameters, not independent new measurements by
us. Raw time records were not reacquired or reanalyzed. This evidence blocks
an unrestricted high-speed physical claim from the ideal white-noise theorem.

6. WHAT THE SEARCH DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

The accessed sources directly defeat novelty of the proposed energy-order and
holonomy primitives. I did not locate the exact combined bounded-time,
all-radial-error scheduling theorem, the finite-N optimal contraction and
worst-conditioning formula, or the all-angle slower-loop first variation in
these sources. That is not an exhaustive priority proof. These are recorded
as proved deductions from established geometry and stochastic dynamics, not
as an established new physical law or a civilisation-changing capability.
