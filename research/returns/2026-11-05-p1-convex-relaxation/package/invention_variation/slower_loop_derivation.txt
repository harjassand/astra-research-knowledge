PRESERVED FAILED STRENGTHENING: MINIMUM-TIME NOISE OPTIMALITY DOES NOT EXTEND
TO ALL LONGER OPERATIONS

This file proves a local all-angle failure of the stronger claim. It does
not claim a global optimum of the slower-time problem. The finite epsilon
example in the companion script is a converged floating computation; the
existence result below is analytic.

Use the same two-dimensional overdamped OU model, 0<=A<=Lambda I and isotropic
diffusion D. Fix a positive rotation angle 0<theta<=pi, its shortest shape
length L=sqrt(theta^2+2pi theta), and a total attenuation s>L.

1. EXPLICIT LOOPS, WITH NO UNKNOWN DISTANCE INPUT

Represent the normalized shape in the Poincare disk by z=rho exp(i psi).
Take u in [0,pi], psi=u-pi/2, and
 rho_epsilon(u)=a(epsilon) sin u [1+epsilon sin(2u)].
The scalar a(epsilon) is chosen to make the exact holonomy equal theta:
 integral_0^pi rho_epsilon^2/(1-rho_epsilon^2) du = theta.
The left side is strictly increasing in a near zero perturbation, so the
implicit function theorem gives a smooth unique a(epsilon). At epsilon=0,
 a=L/(pi+theta), b=sqrt(1-a^2)=pi/(pi+theta).

The shape arclength element and total length are
 dl=sqrt(rho'^2+rho^2)/(1-rho^2) du,
 K(epsilon)=integral dl.
The zero-perturbation curve is a hyperbolic circle through the origin, with
K(0)=L. Reflection u->pi-u exchanges epsilon with -epsilon without changing
the area or length. Hence a'(0)=K'(0)=0 and K(epsilon)=L+O(epsilon^2).
Hyperbolic isoperimetry gives K>=L. For sufficiently small epsilon the fixed
surplus s-L ensures K(epsilon)<=s.

2. ACTUAL CONTROL AND ITS EXACT RESPONSE

Let H(z)=[[Re z,Im z],[Im z,-Re z]],
 B(z)=[I+H(z)]/sqrt(1-|z|^2), and
 beta(u)=integral_0^u rho^2/(1-rho^2) dv.
Y(u)=R_beta B(z) has determinant one and a symmetric right logarithmic
derivative. Its normalized derivative S_hat=-Y'Y^-1/(dl/du) is symmetric,
traceless and has eigenvalues +/-1. Thus
 A(u)=(Lambda/2)(I+S_hat(u)), dt/du=2(dl/du)/Lambda
is a prescribed admissible rank-one restoring force. Its response after the
loop is exactly e^(-K)R_theta. Following it by isotropic Lambda I cooling
for (s-K)/Lambda gives the EXACT same target e^(-s)R_theta for every epsilon.

Total time is (s+K)/Lambda. With starting/ending control Lambda I and counted
quenches, net ideal actuator work is 2 k_B T_bath K. Both extra time and extra
work are O(epsilon^2). These assertions concern the stated ideal model, not
physical realization at bounded actuator slew.

3. THERMAL TRACE FUNCTIONAL

Let h(rho)=tr(B^-2)=2(1+rho^2)/(1-rho^2). The exact final covariance trace is
 tr Q(epsilon)=(4D/Lambda)e^(-2s) integral_0^K e^(2l) h(rho(l)) dl
                  +(2D/Lambda)[1-e^(-2(s-K))].
Since K'(0)=0, only the first variation of h at fixed arclength contributes.

Write w=sin^2 u, d=1-a^2 w. At zero perturbation,
 dl/du=a/d,
 l(u)=(a/b) atan2(b sin u,cos u),
 rho_1(u)=a sin u sin(2u).

The arclength first variation at fixed u is
 l_1(u)=2a integral_0^w (1-t+a^2 t^2)/(1-a^2 t)^2 dt.
Correcting to fixed arclength gives
 (delta rho)|_l = cos u [2a w-d l_1(u)]
 = cos u [-2d/a^3][a^2 w+log(1-a^2 w)].

Because x+log(1-x)<0 for 0<x<1, this is strictly positive for 0<u<pi/2,
strictly negative for pi/2<u<pi, and changes sign under u->pi-u. The induced
h_1(l)=8rho/(1-rho^2)^2 (delta rho)|_l has the same signs and satisfies
h_1(K-l)=-h_1(l).

Therefore
 d(tr Q)/d epsilon at 0
 =(4D/Lambda)e^(-2s) integral_0^(L/2)
       [e^(2l)-e^(2(L-l))] h_1(l) dl < 0.

This proves that for EVERY nonzero rotation angle and EVERY surplus s>L,
the minimum-time noise-optimal operation admits nearby, slightly longer
operations with strictly lower thermal mean-square error. The improvement
is first order in epsilon while extra time/work are at most second order.
This is an explicit constructive failure of the stronger optimality claim.
It does not identify the globally best slower curve or a whole Pareto front.

4. A COMPLETED POSITIVE COROLLARY AT THE MINIMAL-ATTENUATION BOUNDARY

At s=L there is no surplus: every admissible path has K=L and a=k almost
everywhere, so its shape is a shortest curve. Relative to shape arclength,
dt/dl=1/k>=2/Lambda. Its pulled-back noise is an integral of positive matrices
weighted by dt/dl. Traversing at maximum speed therefore minimizes covariance
for that shape even if ANY longer duration is permitted; idle intervals add
positive noise. The same free-orientation/radial-tail argument applies.
Thus the fastest protocol is globally optimal in radial thermal error at
the minimal attenuation boundary. The longer-time improvement requires s>L.

5. EXECUTED EXAMPLE

Companion script slower_loop_counterexample.py fixes theta=pi/2,
s=L+0.05, Lambda=D=gamma=1 and compares epsilon=0 with epsilon=0.05.
It checks the actual fundamental matrix, accumulated attenuation, holonomy,
control eigenvalue cap, noise by two independent evolution formulas, and
work including endpoint changes. Three numerical tolerances expose solver
convergence. JSON records all residuals; those are not interval enclosures.
The analytic proof above supplies the existence result independently.
