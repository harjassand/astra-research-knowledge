## A random completion event changes the operation

The proof concerns fixed-time marginals. Replacing the deterministic deadline by a random stopping time is not licensed by the argument. Immediate delivery after a causally detected completion event may escape this bound, but must earn the receiver, detector response, timing records, error probabilities and full costs. Leaving the quantum state at the same raw port until a predetermined pickup remains covered.

Unbounded-dimensional oscillators and uncounted non-Markovian reservoirs with persistent information also change the model and accounting. The result is neither a universal autonomous-computation impossibility nor a no-go for every receiving interface.
