RETAINED FAILED INFERENCES, SCOPE BOUNDARIES, AND OPEN OPERATION
5 November 2026

This is an operational continuation of P2. The pinned accepted records are
preserved verbatim. No comparison of researchers, contexts, or discovery
performance is inferred from this continuation.

1. RANDOMLY FIRING THE ACCEPTED SYNCHRONOUS CIRCUIT
Not accepted as a construction. The sparse two-track birth/disposal
identity depends on the synchronous cohort relation. Independently timing
its gates does not establish that relation, safe quantum-operation order,
clock recovery, or the actual-source bad-event recurrence. No iid law for
coarse decoder faults is substituted.

2. NAIVE MARCHING-RULE DEADLOCK
Not accepted as a general obstruction. The specified prior work already
proposes finite-neighborhood clock repair. Failure of a simpler modulo
clock does not refute that machinery or autonomous computation generally.

3. DIRECT POISSON REPRESENTATION OF COHERENT DYNAMICS
False without an additional argument: a noiseless Hamiltonian generator
need not equal a(Phi-id) for any finite rate a and CPTP Phi. This gap is
resolved for the converse by an exact allowed local replacement-noise
assignment and the proved local Choi-positivity regularization lemma.
The resulting representation is algebraic, not a hardware proposal.

4. A ROBUST ENCODED MEMORY AS A RAW PORT BUFFER
Insufficient for the requested fixed-time compact output. Keeping the
unknown state locally available while an uncertain timer finishes incurs
the proved conditional-information drain. A final large-region decoder
and a fixed compact raw port are different access operations.

5. COMPLETION FLAGS AND STOPPING TIMES
The deterministic-time theorem cannot be evaluated at a random stopping
time by substitution. A completion-event interface may escape this
particular bound, but needs its own causal receiving and timing model.
Producing a done flag and leaving the state at the same raw port until a
public pickup time remains covered by the theorem.

6. COINCIDENCE WITH THE ARBITRARY-JUNK HARDWARE EXPONENT
No equivalence of contracts is inferred. The new converse grants known,
even perfectly prepared controllers and initial encoding. The accepted
arbitrary-junk restart theorem is a separate result with a stronger
startup requirement. Its access cost is not charged to this theorem.

7. WHAT REMAINS UNRESOLVED
No positive continuous-time acquisition/computation/local-delivery
processor is constructed. No matching N=O(T) upper bound is proved.
The open construction must establish corrupted clock/control repair,
causal quantum-operation history, liveness under the actual Pareto
source, unknown-input/reference semantics, useful protected work,
local output, and a complete resource bill. At a predetermined late
time and fixed compact port, it must also meet the new linear-hardware
lower bound. Changing to a completion-event interface must be explicit
and paid for; it is not an earned construction here.

The retained mathematical result is a general converse for the full
input/output requirement at the inherited sublinear resource bill.
It does not stand in for a positive solution of the wider operation.
