2. The complete mechanical cycle

The proposed sequence preserves a real clearance path:

Ready: Every head is recessed 0.2 mm behind the active face.
Seat: The robot supports the payload and brings the datum faces together.
Extend: The carriage advances 5.7 mm, placing each head’s underside 0.2 mm beyond the receiver lip.
Rotate: Each head turns 90° inside its rotation pocket.
Draw: The carriage retracts 0.2 mm to contact the lips, then compresses each disc approximately 0.1875 mm.
Retain: The crossbolt and rotational index engage. Holding does not depend on motor torque or gearbox friction.
Qualify: Observe actual head orientation, local spring travel, seating, retention and the load/displacement signature.
Hold: Remove the tool and all power.
Unload: A paid support takes the useful wrench.
Release: The tool takes the carriage’s spring reaction, withdraws retention, relaxes the discs, advances the heads clear of the lips, rotates them back and retracts them.
Restore: Inspect and clean, verify subflush heads and known mechanism positions, and confirm the temperature is inside the readiness window.

The important mechanical feature is removing head/lip pressure before rotating the heads. It avoids deliberate sliding of those load-bearing contacts under the retained preload. It does not eliminate guide, gear or cam wear.

Decision

The construction makes a useful distinction explicit: the load-bearing contacts can be unloaded before the mechanism moves, so return to readiness can be a geometric operation with retained solid parts. That is a practical direction, but it is already substantially represented in mechanical-connector prior art.

In this particular implementation, recessing and distributing the mechanism introduces precision guidance, gears, a translating carriage, multiple observed states and service obligations. Those costs outweigh the reset savings. The guide correction also prevents a defensible full-motion claim.

I would not describe this result as the economical reusable interface you requested, or fund the present geometry as though that result had been achieved. The report and model establish why this candidate loses, preserve the parts that are physically useful, and specify the experiment that would distinguish a repaired mechanism from another favorable nominal calculation.
