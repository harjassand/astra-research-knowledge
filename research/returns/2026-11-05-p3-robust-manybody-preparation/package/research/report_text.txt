A uniform local-noise preimage theorem for one-dimensional Gibbs states at every fixed positive temperature

<b>Completed consequence.</b> Every bounded-range one-dimensional qubit Gibbs family at fixed finite inverse temperature admits a constructive ensemble of bounded contiguous blocks with a uniform positive spectral floor. A nonzero amount of independent local output noise can therefore be precompensated, uniformly in system size. The accepted noisy Bell/tree boundary compiler turns this representation into unencoded global-state preparation to arbitrary accuracy at fixed component noise in its stated Pauli apparatus model.

MATH: \rho_\beta=\mathbb{E}_{\mu}\bigotimes_{A\in\mathcal{P}_{\mu}}\tau_{\mu,A},\qquad |A|\leq B,\qquad \tau_{\mu,A}\succeq \eta I_A/2^{|A|}

B and η depend on temperature and interaction range, not chain length or requested accuracy. The proof modifies a recent pure-block Gibbs preparation theorem, preserves its classical sampling mechanism, and removes the rank-deficient factors that obstruct noisy delivery.

3. Preserve positive slack at the spectral step

The original cut decomposition can lose rank by subtracting the entire cut operator and by resolving a remainder with sharp projectors. Halve the cut operator instead:

MATH: F_j=G'_j/2,\qquad a=e^{-7m}/2,\qquad \Delta_j=G_j-F_j

MATH: aG_j\preceq F_j\preceq G_j/2,\qquad G_j/2\preceq\Delta_j\preceq(1-a)G_j

Now Aⱼ=Gⱼ⁻¹ᐟ²ΔⱼGⱼ⁻¹ᐟ² has eigenvalues in [1/2,1−a]. Choose a rank-one orthogonal spectral resolution with eigenvalues λⱼₜ and projectors Pⱼₜ. Let dⱼ be its dimension, s=a/2, and λ̄ⱼ its mean eigenvalue.

MATH: Q_{j,t}=(1-s)P_{j,t}+sI/d_j,\qquad b_{j,t}=\frac{\lambda_{j,t}-s\overline{\lambda}_j}{1-s}

MATH: \sum_tQ_{j,t}=I,\quad\sum_tb_{j,t}Q_{j,t}=A_j,\quad 0\leq b_{j,t}\leq1-a/2

The equalities follow by collecting spectral coefficients and identity terms. The bounds follow from the admitted eigenvalue interval. Any orthonormal basis in a degenerate eigenspace works. Define

MATH: E_{j,t}=G_j^{1/2}Q_{j,t}G_j^{1/2}\succeq(s/d_j)G_j

MATH: \sum_tE_{j,t}=G_j,\qquad\sum_tb_{j,t}E_{j,t}=\Delta_j

Every factor is full rank, yet its scalar remainder coefficient is uniformly below one. This exact replacement for the sharp spectral factors is the central new operation. A fixed sufficiently long superblock makes the residual central perturbation small enough to resolve with mixed product states.

11. An exact attained entangled-state boundary

MATH: H=\frac{XX+YY+ZZ}{3},\qquad\rho_\beta=\frac{I-t(XX+YY+ZZ)}{4},\qquad t=\frac{e^{4\beta/3}-1}{e^{4\beta/3}+3}

Let both compulsory final channels be depolarizers with Bloch contraction λ. A Werner input with parameter u becomes one with parameter λ²u, and its positivity range is −1/3≤u≤1. Thus exact delivery is possible precisely when t≤λ²; the attaining input has u=t/λ².

MATH: \inf_{\sigma}D\left[(\mathcal{D}_\lambda\otimes\mathcal{D}_\lambda)(\sigma),\rho_\beta\right]=\frac{3}{4}(t-\lambda^2)_+

To prove optimality over every input, twirl it by U⊗U. This fixes the target, commutes with depolarization and cannot increase trace distance. Minimizing over Werner inputs gives 3|t−λ²u|/4. A singlet input attains the residual floor if t&gt;λ². At λ=0 the sole output is maximally mixed.

The target is entangled for t&gt;1/3, so λ²&gt;1/3 gives a nonempty entangled interval with exact positive precompensation. At λ=0.8 this is 0.823959&lt;β≤1.569926. λ is an endpoint parameter; this is not a claim that the full compiler tolerates 20 percent primitive error.

For target r=1/2, actual m₁,m₂∈[0.8,1] and outer box [0.7,1.1]², the numerator polynomials m₂±rm₁ have positive corner coefficients. The affine/Bernstein race samples the desired sign law without estimating κ or meter visibility A to output accuracy. The final quantum outputs are freshly reset after the classical assay computation finishes, at a common deadline.

Constants remain substantial: the conservative checked schedule for R=1000 and total ε=0.001 uses 222,912 assay-template invocations per output. Each template's operations, resets and captures count. This proves an accuracy-scaling distinction from point estimation, not practical economy or optimality against every competing method.

A general six-palette application needs actual output vectors to be identifiable functions of finite observable assay parameters. Arbitrary SPAM has gauge ambiguities: a shared rotation of sources and meters can be invisible internally while changing the external target. The scalar construction does not solve that problem. Competing preparation methods are free to use the same probability-programming technique.

19. Useful failures, preservation and reproduction

A finite-cell shortcut remains unproved

A uniform block floor can place every target inside the convex hull of an isolated finite cell's noisy preparations. This alone does not schedule a random partition on a fixed chain. Faulty masks, selectors and couplers can create cross-block interactions. The completed all-temperature operation retains protected scheduling and routing. Elimination of growing error-correcting codes is not claimed.

The boundary conditions remain substantive

The general mathematical channel radius does not validate arbitrary coherent or correlated apparatus errors. Digital target input does not identify an unknown material. Finite calibration does not certify indefinite stationarity. A retained source label changes the conditional target. Known mixed-state preparation does not remove the unknown-input raw-port floor or supply a canonical TFD. Postselection on very rare outcomes can amplify an implementation error.
