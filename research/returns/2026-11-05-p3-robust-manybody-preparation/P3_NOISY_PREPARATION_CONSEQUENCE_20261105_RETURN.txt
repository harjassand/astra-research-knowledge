## 2. The new structural theorem

Let

\[

act on an \(N\)-qubit chain or ring. Each \(h_j\) is supported on at most \(r\) consecutive sites and satisfies \(\|h_j\|\le 1\), with bounded term density. Fix finite \(\beta\ge 0\) and \(r\). The terms may be noncommuting and spatially inhomogeneous.

Write

\[

### Uniformly mixed blocks

There are computable constants

\[

\qquad
\eta=\eta(\beta,r)>0,

independent of \(N\) and requested accuracy, such that

\[
\boxed{
\rho_\beta
=
\mathbb E_\mu
\bigotimes_{A\in\mathcal P_\mu}\tau_{\mu,A},
\qquad
|A|\le B,
\qquad
\tau_{\mu,A}\succeq
\eta\,\frac{I_A}{2^{|A|}}.
}
\]

Each \(\mathcal P_\mu\) is a partition into contiguous blocks. The partition and mixed block states are classically describable and can be sampled to arbitrary delivered trace accuracy using the source’s polynomial sampler with the modifications below.

\frac34(t-\lambda^2)_+.
