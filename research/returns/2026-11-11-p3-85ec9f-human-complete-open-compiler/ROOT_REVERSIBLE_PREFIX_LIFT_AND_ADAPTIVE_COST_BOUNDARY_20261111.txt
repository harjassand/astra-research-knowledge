Paired elementary kinetic lift, equilibrium activities and the adaptive tail boundary

Model. Let the finite labelled mass-action input have an involution r->bar r with y_bar_r=z_r,z_bar_r=y_r. Fixed labels may be allowed only for null reactions. Let rational c_i^*>0 satisfy k_r(c^*)^y_r=k_bar_r(c^*)^z_r for each pair. Propensities use falling factorials at unit volume, with no extra symmetry factors. The input may be open and of variable order. This note constructs an exact nominal detailed-balance graph lift and identifies what its algebra does NOT pay.

1. A paired lift with no product-prefix duplication.

For EVERY directed label r with q_r=|y_r|>0, create a private source-prefix chain S_r,j,2<=j<=q_r, and a private pending species C_r with P C_r=y_r. All prefixes decode to their actual source prefix; original species decode identically. Set s_r to the last prefix or the original source species when q_r=1. All auxiliaries initially vanish. Fix rational U>=1,K>0,theta>0,Q=theta^(-1).

For q_r>=2 use first assembly coefficient K theta k_r U^(2-q_r), later assembly coefficients KU and all release coefficients K. At the last prefix use s_r<->C_r with coefficients K,K. For q_r=1 use s_r<->C_r with coefficients K theta k_r,K. These are hidden and binary on each side.

When y_r,z_r are both nonzero, make the observed pair
 C_r -> C_bar_r with label r, coefficient Q,
 C_bar_r -> C_r with label bar_r, coefficient Q.
Each physical channel is emitted once; the ordered input labels specify its direction. When y_r=0,z_r!=0, use
 0 -> C_bar_r with label r, coefficient k_r,
 C_bar_r -> 0 with label bar_r, coefficient Q.
If a null label is its own reverse, use an actual observed null C_r->C_r at coefficient Q, or0->0 at coefficient k_r when its source is empty. Distinct null/parallel labels retain their distinct channels and reverse labels.

Every fine decoder column is nonzero, and every fine reaction has at most two molecules on each side. Hidden assembly/release/pending paths give u<->j(Pu) for ALL fine states. The observed central channel consumes a pending species containing its entire source; it is enabled only on an actual admitted original edge. The canonical lifted word for every original edge gives the exact all-state equivalence
 u leads to v iff Pu leads to Pv,
including the actual directed communicating classes. This is a graph lift, not an externally executed reset.

2. Exact rational nominal equilibrium certificate.

Define relative weights
 w(S_r,j)=theta k_r U^(j-q_r), w(C_r)=theta k_r.
For a prefix p_r,j let its absolute activity be
 a(S_r,j)=w(S_r,j)(c^*)^p_r,j,
 a(C_r)=theta k_r(c^*)^y_r,
 a(A_i)=c_i^*.
Each hidden pair satisfies coefficient_forward a^alpha=coefficient_reverse a^beta by substitution. The observed nonzero-source pair does too, because
 a(C_r)=theta k_r(c^*)^y_r=theta k_bar_r(c^*)^z_r=a(C_bar_r).
The zero-source pair satisfies k_r=Q a(C_bar_r). A common clock division preserves all equalities. Thus this is ACTUAL nominal chemical detailed balance, with rational activities and the prescribed original activities, not just reversibility of the reaction graph.

All activities and reciprocal activities can be charged explicitly. For example let
 Xi=2+(2+sum_i(c_i^*+(c_i^*)^(-1)))^(2D+2).
Every source-prefix activity factor and its reciprocal contributed by c^* is bounded by Xi. The source coefficient/weight powers U^q are paid by the input source activity Gamma>=k_r U^q and k_*^(-1), as in the open compiler. Xi may be numerically large; its logarithm has polynomial input bit length because the complexes and D are explicitly expanded. This is not a free uniform bound independent of thermodynamic activities.

3. The exact finite-fibre equilibrium and kinetic interface.

Inside Pu=x, the nominal hidden invariant law has relative weights w and the same partition polynomial
 q(x)=sum_(Ea<=x) w^a(x)_(Ea)/a!.
This is a finite irreducible fibre by nonzero decoder columns and release paths. The exact marginal is
 E_nu_x C_r=theta k_r(x)_y_r q(x-y_r)/q(x).
Thus the central averaged marked rate is
 bar lambda_r(x)=k_r(x)_y_r q(x-y_r)/q(x),
and a zero-source birth has rate k_r. Detailed balance survives averaging with the pushforward weights proportional to (c^*)^x q(x)/x!, not the original Poisson weights without q.

For a supplied cutoff U=1+B and sufficiently small theta, source-prefix and pending activities satisfy the same finite partition and tagged-lineage release estimates as the complete open kinetic compiler. In particular q(x)<=exp(theta(D+1)Gamma) below the cutoff. The proof-only Poisson uniformization estimate Lambda I(1+Lambda tau) compares ENTIRE exact timed labelled paths, including null and parallel labels, when an actual uniform hidden mixing integral I is supplied. It applies to the paired lift as well: a central event leaves an arbitrary fine encoding in its next fibre, and the coupling uses the next proposal gap. No physical equilibrium sampler or externally executed proposal clock is introduced.

These facts give an opening for a nominally detailed-balanced compiler. They do NOT establish universal polynomial costs for its actual uncontrolled tails under arbitrary predictable rate faults. The exact new open compiler used irreversible collision returns to obtain that global estimate; those returns are absent here.

4. Why simply reversing the collision returns does not inherit the old credit.

The open compiler's extra channel C_r+S->s_r+S with coefficient eta d_S gives per-pending return hazard eta(V-q_r) and a bounded credit chi_r(V)=G/[G+K/2+(eta/2)(V-q_r)_+]. For the paired nominal lift above, detailed balance would require its reverse coefficient eta d_S a(C_r)/a(s_r). This ratio is1 for q_r>=2 in the displayed lift. The reverse channels create pending molecules with rate quadratic in the decoded population. The old proof paid initiation by a LINEAR intensity, so its pointwise bounded-credit drift no longer follows.

Changing the activity ratio may reduce that reverse coefficient, but for any fixed positive ratio its quadratic influx persists at sufficiently large population. This is a failure of this inherited estimate, not an impossibility for every reversible design. A different reversible controller, common Lyapunov function or actual pathwise population/event budget could resolve the interface.

5. Common equilibrium for frozen generators is not arbitrary-feedback invariance.

Take states0,1 and nominal rates1 in both directions. For each b in [1-delta,1+delta], the frozen generator with BOTH directional rates b is reversible for the uniform law. Choose b=1+delta whenever the current state is0 and b=1-delta whenever it is1, using its left-limit state for predictability. The resulting controlled process has rate0->1 equal1+delta and1->0 equal1-delta, and stationary mass at1 equal(1+delta)/2. Thus even selecting among generators EACH individually reversible for one common equilibrium can change the marginal law under state/history feedback. This two-state example proves no unbounded-tail obstruction; it prevents importing an autonomous equilibrium argument as a history-adaptive guarantee.

For the detailed-balanced compiler question, nominal detailed balance is required only at zero faults. Faults may bias the physical affinities. Accuracy and global costs still require a pointwise or conditional argument for the entire admitted predictable rate box. No claim of equilibrium thermodynamics under arbitrary independent rate perturbations is made.

6. What a nominal product-Poisson entropy bound actually gives.

Suppose the nominal fine process is already known nonexplosive and has stationary product-Poisson law pi_a. The global mixture over classes is permitted. From canonical initialization, its finite relative entropy is
 D0=-log pi_a(j(x0))=sum_s a_s+sum_i[log(x0_i!)-x0_i log(c_i^*)].
Markov relative-entropy contraction gives D(P_t||pi_a)<=D0. The entropy variational inequality with observable n=|Z| gives
 E n_t<=D0+log E_pi_a exp(n)=D0+(e-1)sum_s a_s.
When the emitted activities and reciprocals are paid, this is a computable polynomial NOMINAL mean-population bound. One may bound log(x0_i!) by x0_i log(1+|x0|), so no factorial evaluation oracle is needed.

It does not directly pay E sup_(s<=t)n_s, an all-firing count with quadratic propensities, or arbitrary-feedback faults. A product-Poisson law does not have a finite exp(n^2) moment, so the same entropy inequality cannot simply be applied to n^2. Formal detailed balance alone is also not a prior proof of nonexplosion. These are the actual remaining global-cost interfaces, not missing source counts or reporting steps.

7. A stronger exact stationary-output requirement would be false in general.

For an UNCONDITIONED product-Poisson fine law with activities a_s>0, the decoded probability-generating function is
 E z^(PZ)=exp(sum_s a_s(z^(P_s)-1)).
If the original-species activities are c_i^* and there is ANY positive auxiliary activity with |P_s|>=2, its higher monomial occurs with a strictly positive coefficient in the logarithm. It cannot cancel. Therefore this pushforward cannot be EXACTLY the original product-Poisson(c^*) law on all states. Even unit-payload auxiliaries add positive linear coefficients unless the original activities are changed. This is an unconditional-law statement, not a blanket assertion about every class-conditioned law. A finite-horizon approximate kinetic compiler need not demand this impossible stronger identity; its partition correction is paid by small activities.
