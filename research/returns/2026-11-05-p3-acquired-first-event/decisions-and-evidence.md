# Finite-menu intervention, safe preparation and actual evidence

The [acquisition/decision proof](package/next5_root/acquisition_composition.tex) and [experimental composition](package/next5_decision/experimental_composition.tex) turn the [joint event record](README.md) into a bounded-loss decision among K supplied **constant** interventions. The loss may combine timing, terminal damage, precursor attributes and known scaled intervention cost.

With conditional record error epsilon_law, finite loss-evaluation error epsilon_eval and preparation-abort budget delta_prep, n fresh records per menu item with `n>=log(2K/delta_dec)/(2sigma²)` give the stated regret bound `2(epsilon_law+sigma+epsilon_eval+delta_prep)`. Conditional concentration is used where cells/records are reused; iid physical experiments are not invented.

The decision occurs **after acquisition and before paid deployment/preparation**. It is not instantaneous control of an unheld live network or unrestricted adaptive policy optimization. Acquisition-period exposure and any safe holding/shutdown remain separate.

## Safe mass is a real preparation resource

The inherited repairable-evolution/safe-inspection routine needs stationary safe mass at least **1/2** for each menu item to retain its constant-cost form. A smaller floor s* pays an explicit `O(s*^-1 log(1/delta_prep))` attempt bill; learned safe-state rejection also pays its corresponding floor. Certification exactly on a threshold can need strict slack.

For fitted weights, a physically prepared density ratio `exp(+-beta)` relative to the true equilibrium-safe law adds `tanh(beta/2)+tanh(E)` to the initial-law comparison, plus numerical safe-sampling error. True experimental static draws can avoid the fitted-weight term. Ramp, inspection, repairability, timing and work are not supplied by a low event probability or a cheap digital sampler.
