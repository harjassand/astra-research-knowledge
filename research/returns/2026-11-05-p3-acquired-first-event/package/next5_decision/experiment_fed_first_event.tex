\begin{abstract}
We construct the missing physical interfaces of a rare first-event generator
from faithful local copies, additive fields and priced state pins. Fresh local
experiments implement the true Gibbs and operating-chain updates to any paid
finite accuracy. Relative probabilities of rare coordinate-pinning events are
obtained by heavy completions and locally measured weight ratios; a fine table
of energy factors is optional and a fine table of operating rates is
unnecessary. The completed static disconnection construction and first-event
sampler can consequently be used for the true physical law, with a total
variation certificate for the joint quantized event time, terminal state and
precursor record. All physical errors are conditional-on-history kernel errors,
so reusing an uncontrolled local cell does not assume independent records.
Finite-menu bounded-loss decisions follow by martingale concentration.
The result consumes fresh experiments on every generated record. It is not an
unlimited offline simulator learned from a finite data set. Its premises include
faithful local copies and complete local model closure, certified mixing and
fixed static contrast, calibrated and reproducible fields, finite detector and
timer precision, and paid local state pins. The sufficient cost is polynomial
without an inverse global-rarity factor, but may be large.
\end{abstract}

\section{Endpoint and accepted machinery}

The endpoint is a quantitative law and decision about a first event in a
physical system, earned from local experiments without observing its global
rare event. The output record is the one defined in the completed first-event
construction: a finite quantized event time, the terminal configuration, and a
requested finite preceding window of the path. Its error is joint total
variation, not relative error for arbitrarily tiny subevents or deadlines.
The acquired relative whole-CDF certificate remains a distinct operation.

We use, at their exact accepted scopes:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The correlated all-terminal disconnection Count/SampleB construction:
positive Gibbs weights, coordinate-pinned sampling, and fixed conditional
failure-surprisal contrast. Its operations are cut witnesses, relative masses
of coordinate pinnings, pinned draws, and inverse-multiplicity correction.
\item The finite first-event sampler: forward and stationary reversed updates,
relative stationary counting, unconditional-TV failure-conditioned sampling,
a stationary mass floor and an additive-symmetrization mixing bound. Compilation
and a record draw have deterministic finite polynomial caps in the supplied
interface costs, inverse gap, log inverse mass floor, requested window length,
clock digits, and inverse error. There is no inverse stationary failure
probability or mean-lifetime simulation factor.
\item The faithful-cell physical access and paid equilibrium-safe preparation
of the accepted active-acquisition construction.
\end{enumerate}
The exact governing sources are the public first-event package at revision
\[
\texttt{719e201332992002528c221c49f80f69431485f6},
\]
especially its first-event
report and block completion, active-acquisition certificate, and preserved
correlated-reliability report \cite{accepted}.
We construct their interface here; in particular an unknown factor table is
not silently treated as a supplied numerical weight oracle.

\section{Actual access and finite parameters}

There are $N$ binary coordinates, known interaction neighborhoods
$\partial i$ of maximum degree $d$, and a finite allowed intervention menu
$\mathcal U$. Write
\[
 W^u(x)=\exp\!\left(\sum_i(h_i+u_i)x_i+
                    \sum_{\{i,j\}}J_{ij}x_ix_j\right),\qquad
 \pi^u(x)=W^u(x)/Z^u,\qquad
 \ell_i(s)=h_i+\sum_{j\in\partial i}J_{ij}s_j .
\]
The fields and couplings are unknown. Known conservative bounds are
$|\ell_i(s)|\le B_0$ and $|u_i|\le U$. The operating dynamics are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Q}
 Q^u(x,x^{(i)})=r_i^u(s)\sig\!\big((1-2x_i)(\ell_i(s)+u_i)\big),
 \qquad 0<r_*\le r_i^u(s)\le r^* .
\end{equation}
The clock $r_i^u(s)$ may depend on the neighborhood and on the whole menu
environment $u$. It is the same for the two focal-bit values at fixed
$(i,s,u)$, as the declared heat-bath form requires. It need not equal a clock
observed at an artificial field. These are complete declared mechanisms;
finite observations do not infer the absence of external catastrophes.
The uniform bounds $r_*,r^*$ must be supplied by a justified operating
backbone/specification or actually acquired uniformly over their declared
scope. A finite set of sampled contexts does not certify them at
unvisited contexts of an arbitrary clock function. The polynomial-degree
alternative below relies on these supplied uniform kinetic bounds; it
does not infer them from its $D_E$ energy measurements.

A faithful local cell can realize each requested neighborhood $s$, the full
operating environment $u$ when used, and the focal two-state process. A held
artificial field $z$ has stationary bit probability
$q_z=\sig(\ell_i(s)+z)$ and total relaxation rate at least $r_{\rm cal}>0$.
The artificial-field clock can otherwise be unknown and field dependent.
The local cell may be reused from an arbitrary prior focal state. After a
hold $t$, its bit law, conditional on the entire previous transcript, differs
from $\operatorname{Bernoulli}(q_z)$ by at most $e^{-r_{\rm cal}t}$.
A priced focal pin sets either starting bit before an operating first-exit
experiment. The first exit is observed without an unpriced missed-event
channel. Response delays, dead times, bit-read errors and timestamp errors
must either satisfy the budgets below or be charged as additional
conditional error. An absorbing physical event requires a faithful ergodic
continuation with the same pre-hit law, as in the accepted construction.

We charge the following actual services as functions, rather than assigning
them zero cost:
\[
 C_{\rm context},\ C_{\rm field}(\xi),\ C_{\rm slew},\
 C_{\rm settle},\ C_{\rm pin},\ C_{\rm read}(\beta),\
 C_{\rm event}(\beta),\ C_{\rm timer}(\Delta).
\]
They include context realization, additive-field calibration to absolute
accuracy $\xi$, the available slew schedule, settling, state pins,
readout/detection and reference timing. Parallel cells change the scheduling,
not the sum of exposures or number of pins. A maximum allowed slew rate is
not a guaranteed available schedule. The bounds below specify the precision
and number of services required; a platform has a finite operation only when
it supplies those services at the charged precision.

\subsection{Coarse structural certification and fine field metrology}

The accepted compensating-field search, using bounded conditional-bias
observations and martingale Hoeffding, locates a balance field $z_{i,s}$ with
true $q_{z_{i,s}}\in[1/4,3/4]$. It can also supply coarse intervals for
$\ell_i(s)$. The number of contexts is
$C_0=\sum_i2^{|\partial i|}$ unless a proved pairwise reconstruction reduces it.
We do not call this polynomial in $d$; it is polynomial at fixed degree.

A stronger pairwise alternative avoids this enumeration when its explicit
sufficient certificate succeeds. The accepted learner uses
$D_E=N+2|E_{\rm int}|$ energy contexts to estimate $h_i$ to error
$\eta_0$ and $J_{ij}$ to error $2\eta_0$. Choose
$\eta_0\le[8(1+2d)]^{-1}$ and a paid actual field-setting error at most
$1/8$. For every adaptively requested context $s$,
$|\ell_i(s)-\widehat\ell_i(s)|\le(1+2d)\eta_0\le1/8$.
Thus the implemented balancing request $z=-\widehat\ell_i(s)$ gives
$|\ell_i(s)+z|\le1/4$, placing its actual coin in $[1/4,3/4]$.
The finite energy acquisition is polynomial in $N,d$ and inverse coarse
accuracy, rather than a scan of $2^d$ contexts. The sufficient all-field,
all-pinning influence certificate
\[
 \max_i\sum_{j\in\partial i}
       \tanh\bigl((|\widehat J_{ij}|+2\eta_0)/4\bigr)\le1-\alpha
\]
is valid because the maximum variation of a logistic conditional when its
logit changes by $J$ is $\tanh(|J|/4)$. Interval sums of positive and
negative coupling endpoints give full conditional bounds for each menu
environment. This certificate can be stricter than exact context
enumeration; both options are retained. Neither option acquires a table
of $r_i^u(s)$: adaptively encountered operating contexts are handled
directly by the censored-exit experiment.

The intervals must certify a pinned Dobrushin contraction margin $\alpha>0$
for every menu environment and full conditional failure-probability bounds
\begin{equation}\label{eq:contrast}
 0<q_i^-\le\pi^u(x_i=1\mid x_{-i})\le q_i^+<1,\qquad
 q_i^-\ge(q_i^+)^c
\end{equation}
with fixed $c$. Coarse interval propagation provides such a certificate if
the true system has strict margins and refinement reaches them. With full
conditional logit error at most $\nu$, each probability changes by at most
$\nu/4$, hence each row's influence uncertainty is at most $d\nu/2$.
For $a\in\mathbb R$, the derivative of
$\log[-\log\sig(a)]$ has absolute value at most one; thus a true surprisal
contrast $c_0$ is certified by $c$ if $c_0e^{2\nu}\le c$, using the
corresponding worst-case bounds. All refinements have declared caps. Failure
to certify by a cap returns \emph{premises unresolved} and is not evidence
that the premise holds. Supplied valid certificates may instead be used.

This coarse localization does \emph{not} eliminate fine physical metrology.
The Gibbs coin below uses the actual shift $u-z$. To achieve absolute coin
error $\theta$, the additive fields used in that transformation must be
known and reproduced to $O(\theta)$, costing $C_{\rm field}(O(\theta))$.
Likewise deployment must reproduce the chosen actual field environment.
Without arbitrary required precision, the construction stops at the
corresponding attainable error floor.

For convenient conservative common bounds define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gap}
 b_E=\lceil2(B_0+U)\rceil,\qquad
 \zeta=2^{-N(b_E+1)},\qquad
 \rho=2r^*,\quad R=N\rho,\quad g=\alpha r_*/R .
\end{equation}
Every full conditional bit probability is at least
$\sig(-(B_0+U))\ge2^{-(b_E+1)}$, and successive conditioning preserves this
bound by the tower property. Thus $\pi^u(x)\ge\zeta$.
The operating generator dominates the heat-bath Dirichlet form with rate
$r_*$, whose certified gap is at least $\alpha$ in the accepted normalization.
Consequently $P=I+Q^u/R$ has gap at least $g$. Notice that $\rho$ uses
\emph{twice the upper clock bound} $r^*$, not $r_*$.
