INDEPENDENT ENDPOINT: EXPERIMENT-FED FIRST-EVENT GENERATION
5 November 2026

Question: Can a true first-event record and a bounded-loss intervention choice
be generated from faithful local experiments without learning a fine operating
rate table, or assuming that an energy-biased clock equals the operating clock?

Constructed route:
1. Coarsely balance each required local conditional energy using calibrated
   fields. A physical hold supplies a q_z coin uniformly conditionally close
   to its stationary q_z in [1/4,3/4], with time logarithmic in coin error.
2. For a=exp(u-z), select branch 1 with probability a/(1+a). On branch 1 accept
   a q_z success and output 1; on branch 0 accept a q_z failure and output 0.
   Repeat otherwise. Acceptance >=1/4 and accepted law is sigma(ell+u).
   Finite rejection caps, field calibration, dyadic selectors and hold bias
   give a finite true-Gibbs update to any paid precision, without estimating ell
   finely and without inverse conditional rarity.
3. For an actual operating flip rate lambda <= r^*, pin the requested source
   bit, release, and race its first exit against an independent Exp(rho)
   deadline, rho=2 r^*. The ideal bit has p=lambda/(lambda+rho)<=1/3.
   A +/-1 walk with up probability p, started at 0, ever hits +1 with probability
   p/(1-p)=lambda/rho. Absorb at -M and cap n steps. Boundary error <=2^(-M-1).
   For n>=6M, survival beyond n is <=exp(-n/72), by Hoeffding. Thus logarithmic
   M,n suffice. Each race is censored by a capped external deadline; finite
   deadline precision and detector ties have a bound proportional to rho*Delta.
   No inverse lambda dwell is paid, but every requested pin is explicitly paid.
4. Random scan over N sites gives the true P=I+Q/(2Nr^*). Local detailed balance
   makes P*=P, even with unknown clocks depending on context and full menu u.
5. No static-factor oracle is assumed. Pinned Gibbs sampling uses step 2 and
   the certified Dobrushin margin. For any pin event D, use the previous
   heavy-completion construction for D and for no pin. Their terminal states
   x_D,x_0 and heavy conditional probabilities give
       pi(D)=[W(x_D)/W(x_0)] prod(h_0)/prod(h_D).
   A path of at most N bit flips joins x_0 to x_D. Its log weight ratio is a
   sum of full local log odds, measured from balanced coins. This estimates
   a rare pin probability relatively without ever observing the pin event.
   It closes the unknown-W interface in the existing cut-counting/sampling
   construction. Costs are polynomial in N and inverse requested accuracy,
   not inverse pin probability; each actual experimental query is charged.
6. These true experimental Count/SampleB/P/P* interfaces feed the completed
   first-event construction. Approximate physical calls are treated as kernels
   uniformly close conditional on every earlier experimental transcript. A
   predictable query-count cap permits ordinary adaptive coupling. This avoids
   unproved iid assumptions when test cells are reused.
7. The output is a true-law generative capability consuming fresh physical
   queries, not an unlimited offline simulator from one fixed fitted table.
   A finite menu of constant interventions can be selected for a bounded
   first-event record loss with additive regret, using conditional Hoeffding.
   Existing relative-CDF guarantees remain distinct for tiny deadline risks.

Scope: complete pairwise/local model closure and faithful copies; calibrated
additive fields and certified available slew; biased relaxation lower bound;
priced state pins; known/coarsely certified operating clock bounds; finite
first-exit detector and reference-timer precision; pinned contraction and fixed
conditional log-surprisal contrast; repairable finite safe preparation where
used. No physical closure is inferred from finite observations. Live-system
exposure during experimental acquisition remains separate.

Main parent is independently completing a reusable fitted-model Hellinger
marked-law bridge. The two routes expose an actual experiment-versus-calibration
resource choice; they are not silently identified.
