# Active thermodynamic nonidentifiability in a minimal, fully controllable Langevin system

Status: completed mathematical construction and sharp identification criterion within an explicit linear isothermal class. Novelty against the entire literature is not established. This is a decisive observation/control obstruction, not yet a new useful engine or transport capability. No physical experiment was performed.

## Question

Does arbitrary force control and arbitrarily accurate continuous observation through a dynamically controllable/observable port suffice to infer whether a connected isothermal device dissipates energy internally?

Answer: no. Even with the physical drift matrix known exactly, every causal experiment through the same conjugate force/position ports can have exactly the equilibrium transcript law while the true entropy-production rate is unbounded across observationally equivalent devices. The obstruction is not an uncoupled hidden subsystem or a limited observation bandwidth.

All coordinates below are even under time reversal; temperature units are k_B T = 1. An isothermal overdamped diffusion with symmetric positive mobility D is

    dX = [A X + D C^T u] dt + sqrt(2D) dW,    Y = C X.

Here u is the externally applied mechanical force conjugate to Y. The native force is D^{-1} A X, which need not be conservative. The allowed controls may use the entire previous Y record and independent randomness. Initial conditions are stationary for each device. The controller does not reset or measure the hidden state, alter the native mobility, or access the hidden drive's power supply. Those would be additional ports.

## 1. Exact identification criterion with known drift

Fix stable A and known C with (C,A) observable. For a positive stationary covariance Sigma, let

    D = -(A Sigma + Sigma A^T)/2 > 0.

The stationary output covariance at positive lag is

    R(t) = C exp(A t) Sigma C^T,

and the conjugate-force impulse response is

    H(t) = C exp(A t) D C^T.

For two covariances Sigma and Sigma+Z, where Z is symmetric and both resulting diffusion matrices are positive, their full stationary Gaussian output laws and force responses agree if and only if

    Z C^T = 0,    Z A^T C^T = 0.                 (1)

Proof: equality of R and all its derivatives at zero says C A^j Z C^T=0 for every j. Observability gives Z C^T=0. The same argument for H gives (D'-D)C^T=0. Substituting the Lyapunov relation and Z C^T=0 gives the second equality in (1). Conversely these equalities directly preserve R and H. Stationary Gaussian means and covariances specify the entire uncontrolled output path law.

Adaptive experiments do not add information. The input-output identity is

    Y(t) = Y_0(t) + integral_0^t H(t-s) u(s) ds.

The two devices have the same law for Y_0 and the same kernel H. They can be coupled by the same Y_0 and controller random seed. Every causal control law with a unique well-posed solution therefore gives the same Y, u, stopping decisions, and observed work functionals on the two devices. Arbitrarily nonlinear feedback does not change this conclusion.

Consequently the stationary covariance and mobility are identifiable from these ideal data precisely when

    span{range(C^T), range(A^T C^T)} = R^n.      (2)

If that span is deficient, any nonzero v perpendicular to it gives an admissible local perturbation Z=theta vv^T for sufficiently small theta. The vector space of ambiguities consists exactly of symmetric matrices supported on that orthogonal complement. With r independent position/force ports, n>2r necessarily leaves an ambiguity, even if ordinary controllability and observability have full rank. Conversely n<=2r and full rank in (2) remove it in this known-drift model. This is a statement about complete covariance/mobility identification, not a universal claim that every thermodynamic functional needs all those parameters.

## 2. General equilibrium-to-unbounded-dissipation family

Take A=-K for a known symmetric positive K, and assume (C,K) observable. Baseline Sigma_0=K^{-1}, D_0=I is equilibrium. Suppose (2) is deficient. Choose a unit v orthogonal to range(C^T) and range(K C^T), and define

    a = v^T K v,
    b = ||K v||^2-a^2,
    w = (K v-a v)/sqrt(b),
    c = w^T K w.

Observability forces b>0: an invisible eigenvector would contradict observability. For

    theta_- = -2/(a+sqrt(a^2+b)) < theta <= 0,

set

    Sigma_theta = K^{-1} + theta vv^T,
    D_theta = I + (theta/2)(K vv^T + vv^T K).

In span{v,w}, D has matrix

    [[1+theta a, theta sqrt(b)/2],
     [theta sqrt(b)/2, 1]],

with determinant Delta=1+theta a-theta^2 b/4. Elsewhere it is identity. Delta>0 on the stated interval. Sigma is positive because 1+theta a>0, including at the limiting mobility singularity. Equation (1) is satisfied, so all stationary observed force experiments have exactly the baseline equilibrium law.

The stationary current matrix is

    Q = K Sigma-D = (theta/2)(K vv^T-vv^T K).

For even overdamped coordinates the entropy production rate is

    sigma = tr(Q^T D^{-1} Q Sigma^{-1}).

Direct rank-two calculation gives the closed form

    sigma(theta) = theta^2 b(a+c)/(4 Delta).     (3)

To verify (3), Q restricted to span{v,w} is a rotation of magnitude theta sqrt(b)/2. For a symmetric 2x2 D, J^T D^{-1} J=D/det(D). Sherman-Morrison gives Sigma^{-1}=K-theta(Kv)(Kv)^T/(1+theta a); multiplying its projected 2x2 block by D has trace a+c. Hence (3).

Thus sigma(0)=0, sigma(theta)>0 for theta!=0, and sigma(theta) diverges as theta decreases to theta_-. Every member before that boundary is a finite-dimensional, stable, strictly elliptic isothermal diffusion with the same deterministic A, same observed law and same input-output response. There is no invisible deterministic mode.

Physical resource limit: the native force matrix is -D_theta^{-1}K. Its norm diverges as the mobility approaches singularity. Unbounded hidden dissipation is therefore obtained by allowing an unbounded hidden-force/mobility condition number across the model class; it is not free energy or an unbounded effect at fixed total hardware budget. Any stated lower mobility bound or native-force bound must be included in a quantitative finite upper certificate. The stronger binary obstruction (equilibrium versus positive dissipation) already holds for arbitrarily small bounded perturbations around theta=0.

## 3. Explicit three-coordinate example

Let

    K = [[2,-1,0],[-1,2,-1],[0,-1,2]],
    C = [1,0,0],
    v = [0,0,1].

For 0<=t<sqrt(5)-2, set theta=-2t and

    D_t = [[1,0,0],[0,1,t],[0,t,1-4t]],
    Sigma_t = (1/4)[[3,2,1],[2,4,2],[1,2,3]] - 2t vv^T.

Then

    dX = [-K X + e_1 u] dt + sqrt(2D_t) dW,
    Y = X_1.

The physical conjugate force gives the displayed input term because D_t e_1=e_1. Both controllability of (-K,e_1) and observability of (e_1^T,-K) are full rank; the corresponding determinant is one. The system is a connected three-node chain in its deterministic dynamics.

For all nonnegative lag tau,

    Cov(Y(tau),Y(0)) = e_1^T exp(-K tau) K^{-1} e_1,
    H(tau) = e_1^T exp(-K tau) e_1,

independently of t. The entropy production is

    sigma(t) = 4t^2/(1-4t-t^2).

Examples in the normalized time and temperature units:

| t | minimum eigenvalue of D | entropy production rate |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1 | 0 |
| 0.10 | 0.5763932023 | 0.0677966102 |
| 0.20 | 0.1527864045 | 1 |
| 0.23 | 0.0257043652 | 7.8081180812 |
| 0.236 | 0.0002879573 | 732.842105263 |

The formula, Lyapunov equation, covariance equality and controllability determinant were checked numerically with NumPy. The construction and proof are algebraic; those numerical checks are only sanity checks.

## 4. What additional access resolves this example?

Observing X_3 reveals Var(X_3)=3/4-2t. Measuring X_1 and X_2 while continuing to force only X_1 still gives identical joint stationary path laws, because the covariance perturbation is supported only on e_3e_3^T. Applying a true conjugate force at X_2 changes its drift input from e_2 to D_t e_2=e_2+t e_3, so the resulting response resolves t. Known full-state resets can also expose transient noise covariance. These are concrete changes to the observation/control interface.

Repeated finite observations cannot defeat exact equality. For a confidence set that covers the true sigma uniformly over this class, the same data law occurs at sigma=0 and at arbitrarily large sigma. Therefore no finite upper endpoint with a nontrivial uniform coverage guarantee is possible. More exposure, higher precision, faster sampling, or randomized/adaptive forcing at the original port do not alter that conclusion. Additional independent sensor noise can only preserve or worsen it.

This does not deny extracting accessible work. Since the complete observed force-position transcript agrees, every admissible force protocol also has the same accessible work distribution. Total internal dissipation and port-accessible useful work are distinct inference targets here, and no claim is made that hidden dissipation can be harvested through the unchanged port.

## 5. Prior work and novelty boundary

The general phenomenon that partial observation loses entropy production is established. This note should not be sold as discovering hidden dissipation. Harada-Sasa relates dissipation to fluctuation-response violation under its resolved-dynamics assumptions; coarse-grained trajectory methods supply lower bounds, and sophisticated waiting-time statistics can expose hidden irreversibility in some systems. Their existence does not imply an upper bound when the entire observed path law and response match equilibrium exactly.

1. T. Harada and S.-i. Sasa, "Equality connecting energy dissipation with a violation of the fluctuation-response relation," Physical Review Letters 95, 130602 (2005). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16197127/ . Strong response-based benchmark; complete-state assumptions matter.
2. I. A. Martinez, G. Bisker, J. M. Horowitz and J. M. R. Parrondo, "Inferring broken detailed balance in the absence of observable currents," Nature Communications 10,3542 (2019). https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-11051-w . Waiting-time information yields nontrivial lower bounds even when visible currents vanish. Our equality concerns the full controlled path law, which includes all waiting-time statistics.
3. K. Kawaguchi and Y. Nakayama, "Fluctuation Theorem for Hidden Entropy Production," Physical Review E 88,022147 (2013), arXiv:1209.6333. https://arxiv.org/abs/1209.6333 . Hidden entropy under coarse graining is prior machinery.
4. G. T. Landi, T. Tome and M. J. de Oliveira, "Entropy production in linear Langevin systems," Journal of Physics A: Mathematical and Theoretical 46,395001 (2013). https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8113/46/39/395001 . Standard linear-diffusion entropy-production machinery.
5. A. Roman, "Certifying Hidden Dissipation from Observed Current Fluctuations," arXiv:2609.12598 (2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12598 . Claims cycle-observability conditions in finite-state current geometry; it concerns long-time observed currents and a different physical model. This manuscript has not been technically assessed here, so no dominance claim is made.
6. L. Muruga, F. Ginot, S. A. M. Loos and C. Bechinger, "The extraction of work from hidden degrees of freedom," Nature Physics (2026). https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-026-03442-w . A strong existing positive benchmark for recovering work using bath-memory information. An independently proposed memory-work construction would need to beat this, not merely reproduce the idea.

The potentially distinguishing mathematical package here is the exact covariance ambiguity under arbitrary adaptive *conjugate-force* interventions, a fully controllable/observable counterexample, an unbounded closed-form entropy range, and the necessary/sufficient two-step span criterion with known drift. Whether this package is new needs a focused literature check. At present it is a completed obstruction suitable for comparing research openings; it is not a completed civilization-changing positive capability.
