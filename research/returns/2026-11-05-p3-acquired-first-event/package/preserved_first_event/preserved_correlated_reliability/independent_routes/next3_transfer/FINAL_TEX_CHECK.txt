1. DYADIC CONTRACTION: PASS
For a target cut of weight at most a lambda, set r=2a. At h>r contracted vertices, every singleton cut has weight at least lambda; thus the total non-loop edge weight is at least h lambda/2. Conditional on survival, the probability of contracting a target edge is at most r/h. Therefore survival to r vertices is at least 1/binom(v,r). The uniformly chosen final unoriented partition has target probability 2^(1-r), so the target output probability is at least

  2^(1-r)/binom(v,r) >= r!/(2^(r-1) v^r) >= v^(-r).

The v<=r case gives probability 2^(1-v)>=v^(-r), also valid. Count is proved from disjoint output events and does not assume the count it is trying to establish.

For V_a=v^(2a), each weighted edge choice has TV error <=1/(4vV_a), and there are fewer than v choices. Coupling the entire run to its ideal version costs at most 1/(4V_a). The actual output probability is therefore at least 3/(4V_a), and the report safely relaxes to 1/(2V_a). Fixed b=ceil(log2(4mvV_a)) gives m 2^(-b)<=1/(4vV_a). The error bound covers adaptive changes to the contracted graph because it is uniform over each current graph.

R_a=ceil(2V_a ceil(log2(V_a/beta))) then gives

  P(any designated light cut is missed) <= V_a exp[-R_a/(2V_a)] <= beta.

The graph edge count never exceeds m after aggregation; exact rational weight sums retain polynomial bit length. The always-included computed minimum cut makes J>=1 on all randomness outcomes, which is necessary for the subsequent always-terminating sampler. This is correctly included.

2. TAIL AND LIST SIZE: PASS

For the size bound,

3. APPROXIMATE UNION SAMPLER: PASS
For fixed retained cuts and positive estimated masses, all exact and finite implemented component draws satisfy their selected cut because the sampler obeys coordinate pins exactly. Thus d(x)>=1 for EVERY proposal, even on bad preprocessing events. This support fact is what makes the pointwise acceptance lower bound and all-failed fallback safe.

On the good preprocessing event, let a_j=phi_j/p_j in [exp(-s),exp(s)]. Exact proposal density is pi(x) A(x)/Zhat, with A=sum a_j 1_Ej. Acceptance probability 1/d gives the unnormalized accepted density pi(x)1_U [A(x)/d(x)]/Zhat. The bracket lies in [exp(-s),exp(s)], so after normalization the density ratio relative to pi|U lies in [exp(-2s),exp(2s)]. For s=tau/32 and tau<=1/4, exp(2s)-1<=tau/8 is valid.

Categorical-plus-component approximation contributes at most tau/(128J) to joint proposal/acceptance TV. Downward dyadic acceptance rounding contributes at most another tau/(128J). The joint perturbation is <=tau/(64J). Since the ideal success probability is at least 1/J, a conservative conditioning bound multiplies by <=2J, giving accepted-law TV <=tau/32.

The implemented acceptance chance at every proposal is at least 1/J-tau/(128J)>=1/(2J), independently of the mass-estimation event. Therefore the deterministic cap M_B=ceil(2J ceil(log2(16/tau))) fails with probability <=tau/16. Bad preprocessing adds <=tau/8 in unconditional TV; the omitted-cut contribution is <=tau/16. Adding these, the tau/8 ideal mixture bias and tau/32 finite implementation error gives <=13tau/32<tau. The fresh-preprocessing convention makes separate calls independent and removes the random-cache conditional/unconditional ambiguity.

5. OTHER CORE PREMISES: PASS
The systematic-scan proof uses the maximum coordinate disagreement probability. During a sweep no coordinate exceeds its initial bound d; every update contracts its own mismatch to at most (1-alpha)d; after a full sweep all free coordinates obey that improved bound. Individual heat-bath updates preserve the pinned law, so coupling to a stationary chain is legitimate even though the full systematic-scan kernel is not reversible. Random scan is used separately for the reversible spectral-gap proof, as it must be.

Heavy-prefix sampling preserves absolute pinned weights, uniformly controls each adaptive stage conditional on history, and keeps every chosen true branch probability at least 3/8. Its product estimate and bit-length claims are valid. No rare event must occur in a trial.

The exact rational weights w=(2/3)s+2z obey w<=-ln q<=9w/8 and are positive even near q=1. The choice C=ceil(2c) is safe; ceil(9c/8) is an optional sharpening. The input assumption about certified conditional extrema is explicit, so no unearned extrema oracle has been introduced.

The state mass floor zeta=2^(-N) product(min psi/max psi) is valid; for a positive conditional event E under pin D, pi(E|D)>=pi(E intersect D)>=zeta because the intersection contains at least one state. Only its bit length is used.
