PRESERVED UNSUCCESSFUL OR INSUFFICIENT ROUTES
- Absolute error per independent-block replacement loses a rarity factor.
  The governing construction compares the collapsed failure/success-path
  outcome instead, obtaining error proportional to success probability.
- Conditioning a following buffer on an exposed failed block is invalid;
  the correct proof charges the joint event before exposing that block.
- Ordinary stationary path rejection would pay inverse global rarity;
  the Palm proposal cancels the visit multiplicity and has a gap-scale
  acceptance floor.
- Exact rational powers at huge geometric exponents have prohibitive bit
  length. Fixed-point powers and finite-uniform coupling replace them.
- A nonnormal eigenvalue-real-part bound is not used as a mixing certificate.
- Coherent/noisy physical memory, transport and thermal observations do not
  silently grant missing purifiers, state resets or hidden thermal access.
  Earlier useful failed attempts remain in the untouched original package.
