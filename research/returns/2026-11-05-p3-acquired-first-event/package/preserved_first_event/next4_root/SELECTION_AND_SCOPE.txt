SELECTED ENDPOINT — 5 November 2026

Construct a finite sampler of the JOINT first-event record: event time, terminal state and a chosen recent path window, for a supplied succinct ergodic continuous-time Markov process, from ANY efficiently supplied initial law. The process may violate detailed balance. Target accuracy is total variation of a finite, explicitly quantized record. Computational cost is polynomial in the declared model/access length, numerical inverse symmetrized gap, log reciprocal stationary mass floor, requested window VALUE, inverse accuracy and clock digits. Rare static counting and failure-conditioned sampling are priced. There is no inverse stationary failure probability or mean-lifetime trajectory factor.

Why this endpoint was selected: the completed correlated construction already gives scalar first-failure risks for equilibrium-safe reversible dynamics. It does not generate the joint event and precursor law, does not give an arbitrary-start whole event sampler, and does not cover driven irreversible stationary currents. The new endpoint addresses those three operations together. It is a theoretical computation capability; model acquisition or a physical predictor is not silently inherited.

The independent destinations were allowed to choose and construct their own full questions. Active bias calibration, exact fixed-law rollout envelopes and active thermodynamic identifiability are retained with their actual completion/overlap status. They did not set the main theorem's endpoint or replace it with a simpler nearby statement.

Strong established comparators: transition path sampling and weighted ensemble, reverse-time SMC, optimal Jackson overflow path sampling, quasi-stationary parallel replica, exact finite-state graph reduction, classical rapid-mixing exponential laws. No historical firstness is inferred. The exact new-to-this-project composite guarantee and the access paid for it must be stated against these approaches.
