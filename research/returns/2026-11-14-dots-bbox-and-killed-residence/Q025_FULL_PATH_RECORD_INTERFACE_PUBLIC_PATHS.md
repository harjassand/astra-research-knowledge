# Canonical-path reconstruction records and remaining numerical interface

Candidate full algorithm: scan ORIGINAL augmented components in a fixed deterministic root order. Classify each by deterministic connected exploration with local lifts, stopping immediately when either lifted span reaches400. If exploration exhausts the component first, it is contractible small with both spans<400; erase it using direct erosion. This definition avoids presuming a global bbox for winding components. At the first component admitting a connected-prefix lifted span400 (including winding components), stop deletions and begin addition-only construction. A deterministic connected exploration, with incremental bbox filling, reaches a width400 seed with other span<=400. Extend the other dimension one row at a time until wrapping; then thicken the full-height cylinder until FIRST majority. If no large component exists, erosion reaches all-minus. Check the target after EVERY non-null spin addition and stop immediately on either target; never wait for a later rectangle completion. Already-plus sites are bookkeeping no-ops, not native transitions. Deletions<=n and additions<=n, so total path length<=2n.

Geometry / winding: until the connected prefix first reaches span400, its lifted bbox has both spans<=400<L/2. Thus its original vertices have unique local lifts. Winding of the full original component does not obstruct this seed. All coordinate/orientation choices must be deterministic; proof can overcount their finite records.

## Bootstrap record
Old rectangle lower-left anchor(n), its two dimensions(each<=L), one of8 extension directions, original witness offset(<=L;0 for diagonal), and fill progress(<=2L) specify overwrite region U. Use a fixed lexicographic order among new-bbox sites with at least two neighbors in U, based on U alone (not the unrecorded exterior). Include already-plus original sites as bookkeeping no-ops in this same order. Thus the recorded progress determines U without knowing the original exploration history. At most16n^3 records. For any fixed edge state z and such record, original remaining configuration is (z outside U) union S, where S is its original subset of U. The connected explored prefix lies in S and spans bbox(U); its restricted augmented component is the marked component. The other restricted components form the unmarked gas. All omitted current exterior original-plus sites are retained in z outside U, not silently discarded.

## Earlier erased history
Encode the FULL collection T of earlier erased ORIGINAL components, even if they cross U's boundary. First remove T from the ORIGINAL configuration. Original component nonadjacency gives exact Gibbs activity factorization. Apply the conditional exterior-plus deletion comparison to the remaining initial configuration and current state, then sum T independently by the small-component polymer gas, dropping compatibility only after factorization. Reconstruction is x=(z outside U) union S union T. Do not split T at U or restore T to current state and assume its energy unchanged.

## Prewrap growth record
Seed/rectangle anchor(n), orientation(<=4), current full height(<=L), and active row-prefix length(<=L) determine U=width400 full rectangle plus a contiguous added-row prefix. At most4n^2 records. The original seed remains in S and spans all400 columns. Its restricted component need NOT span the current height. Strip-confined marked-contour mass is needed, summed over possible vertical root/translation. An extra factorL (or conservatively n) pays vertical root positions; it must not be omitted if the walk bound fixes its root at vertical0.

## Postwrap record
Anchor(n), orientation(<=4), full cylinder width(<=800), and active next-column prefix length(<=L) determine U. At most4n^2 records. The enclosing cylinder has width<=800 before first-majority stopping. Its full relative Gibbs partition has log<446. The partial-column-to-enclosing-cylinder energy difference is <=79.96. Arbitrary outside plus spins only improve the conditional deletion bound. All subset preimages may be summed; no seed or topology restriction is needed in this phase.

## One deliberately loose common envelope
Each phase uses fewer than10n^4 records for actual n=2560000. Multiply weighted preimage bounds by2n path length. Bootstrap rates are k>=2 additions, inverse rate<2; erosion inverse rate<3 for the only uphill k2 deletes. Growth inverse rate may cost e^15.96+1 (first k1 protuberance), unless using an endpoint-energy conductance bound instead. Keep this factor explicit. Summing phases introduces an additional small finite factor, not a maximization without accounting for the sum.

This is an injection/record interface; final congestion depends on every invoked contour/gas bound being established. In particular, pending prewrap strip-walk numbers must pay both bounded-perimeter and full high-perimeter tails. No restriction on INITIAL perimeter from a static occupation bound may be used to narrow an otherwise uniform congestion sum.

## Final wrap-completion edge (important)
Do NOT compare the unfinished height1600 strip directly with the full cylinder: their physical energy difference can approach6400. Instead keep the planar marked-strip reference through the entire last-row filling. Cut the periodic seam between the growth-origin row and the final newly added row; the original locally lifted seed stays intact on the planar side of this cut. If B(A) is the number of occupied-occupied bonds crossing that seam, E_open(A)=E_periodic(A)+16B(A). For a subset preimage x inside current overwrite U,
 E_periodic(x)-E_periodic(U)=E_open(x)-E_open(U)+16[B(U)-B(x)]>=E_open(x)-E_open(U).
Thus the open height1600 rectangle/prefix bound is conservative. Switch to the full-cylinder envelope only after the wrap is complete. This keeps the6400 topology change from becoming an unbudgeted factor.
