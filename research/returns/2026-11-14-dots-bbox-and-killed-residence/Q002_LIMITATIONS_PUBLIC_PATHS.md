# Scope, limitations, and no-claim boundaries

1. The full goal is predicting the actual physical rare-transition mean to majority with controlled relative error and useful scaling. It is NOT achieved by this package.
2. The exp20 result concerns a finite parameter tuple and a changed trial energy divided by capacity. It is not a uniform polynomial-inR theorem, a runtime guarantee, an experimentally measured speedup, or a civilization-changing capability.
3. The new trial's R200 energy-weighted edge sampler has not been fully acquired/run. A proposal for the previous area cut cannot automatically be reused for a different bbox support. Toy or conditional channel samples do not prove full coverage.
4. Original physical decision-time work, all returns/growth, and Nκ/full residence acquisition remain open. A good capacity ratio alone does not determine the mean transition time.
5. Geometry depends on the recovered fixed-augmentation filled-domain interface: disjoint outermost domains, collar separation, injective whole-domain flips, and resolved topology accounting. The bbox-specific transfer and independent small tests are included. Tests are not an exhaustive proof over all R200 spin configurations; inherited interfaces have not been independently rebuilt from scratch here.
6. Lower-flow geometry, preimage recoverability, weighted positive-step fibers and phase-overlap accounting remain explicit proof premises. Directed scalar arithmetic certifies the formula conditional on those premises, not the premises themselves.
7. Arithmetic contracts include correctly rounded Decimal functions, directed IEEE long-double operations, compiler/runtime checks and supplied upward magnetic-moment inputs. Source and correction history are retained. Default reproduction rebuilds downstream convolution but not large moments; --full does.
8. The authoritative original source excerpt says the complete earlier thermal-core archive was uncollected. This release includes the available original source and inherited interfaces, not an invented missing archive. “Self-contained” here means the finite computational reproduction and documents need no absent local files, NOT a fully formalized proof of all upstream research.
9. The original phrase “r by r rectangles” does not resolve site count versus coordinate span. Exact thermal samplers expose box_sites5/6 explicitly. The new supplied-diagonal sampler also exposes the actual graph instead of silently assuming a uniform orientation. Conservative analytic source bounds retain the previously documented49-site envelope.
10. No source law is replaced by all-minus, no source edge is translation-discounted, no target overlap is silently removed, and no topology/merger/tail sector is discarded in the stated new upper.
11. Float finite-state comparisons and Monte Carlo receipts are labeled diagnostics. An exact random-bit event simulator does not by itself guarantee useful importance variance. The included seed example rigorously demonstrates infinite variance for one aggressive local tilt.
12. No novelty claim beyond the documented finite constructions/combinations is established by these calculations; no claim of uninterrupted activity, external peer review, or solved North Star is made.
