Can useful physical behaviour be taught to the inaccessible interior of a material through a limited set of boundary ports, with a constructive principle that respects the material's actual local adaptation, observations, retention and physical costs?

The intended discovery is a substantial physical account of what structured matter can LEARN under its real controls, together with a way to make it learn useful responses. This differs from counting states a material could possess, solving a fully observed inverse-design problem, or choosing an independent actuator setting at every internal link. A successful principle could make internal force, flow or wave organisation teachable after fabrication without installing a separate control and sensing system at each degree of freedom. Establish that consequence under explicit physical assumptions; a claim of abstract universality or a list of future applications is insufficient.

Pursue the full constructive question. Choose the physical system, representations and methods. Passive elastic networks, fluidic networks, adaptive electrical media, dissipative structures, nonlinear geometry, history-dependent elements and other organisations are legitimate starting points. The central scientific result should be establishable by mathematical analysis and bounded executable work, with a concrete transfer to a useful physical operation. An unconstructed material, arbitrary local state setter or ideal observer must not silently supply the missing operation. A conditional result must make its constitutive domain and the physical bridge it establishes explicit; it is not an experimental demonstration merely because trajectories are simulated.

The physical operation

Choose and define a family of nonliving physical structures with inaccessible interior degrees of freedom and a genuinely smaller set of externally accessible boundary ports. Specify the bulk geometry or network, its preparation, internal state variables, read/use law, local adaptation and retention mechanism. The family must contain internal nodes or regions that the teacher cannot directly clamp, read, replace or address by hidden dedicated lines. A direct crossbar whose every state lies between independently controlled exposed terminals is a strong prior construction, not the inaccessible-bulk endpoint. One tunable lumped resistor, one trained zero-load pose or one isolated target at a favourable initial state does not establish the family-level operation.

Boundary commands may apply physically available forces, displacements, pressures, flows, voltages or other declared fields. A shared teaching cue or read/write mode can be allowed when its actual physical role, range, timing, work and effect on retention are supplied. It cannot be shorthand for independently choosing every internal update. Local evolution must depend on the actual local state and physical signals that the chosen medium has. Identify which laws follow from established mechanisms, which are derived physical hypotheses and which remain unsupported. In particular, an assumed cubic plasticity or perfect mode gate is a possible mathematical model, not a qualified material.

The teacher observes only declared boundary measurements with finite accuracy, bandwidth, loading and cost. Its policy receives no true hidden state, individual internal gradients, defect labels or free full-network reinitialisation. Known generic layout information may be paid in preparation. Address a meaningful specified domain of initially unknown internal states and constitutive variation, rather than supplying a separately identified exact instance to the teacher. Complete identification of all internal parameters is not required if useful behaviour can be learned without it; do not introduce a harder tomography task unnecessarily.

Give the input and desired output a useful physical meaning. For example, teach a retained force-transfer or compliance response under several loads, a passive flow-distribution response across operating inputs, or a wave-transmission/attenuation function over a nonzero range. Choose the operation and scales; none of these examples is prescribed. Define a family of target functions before judging success. It must have genuine independent variation as the number of useful boundary channels grows and include different useful responses on the SAME physical structure. The physical admissibility conditions matter: positive transport coefficients, reciprocity where it applies, stability, loads, noise, finite amplitudes, losses and energy sources cannot be replaced by arbitrary signed matrices.

The central result sought

Establish a constructive physical-learning principle that tells when and how the family can be taught those useful target behaviours through its boundary access. The procedure must work throughout its stated initial-state and constitutive domain and reach a declared functional error under its actual observations. After teaching ends, useful operation must depend on the retained material state and the declared application input. Supply and price any continuing power, load, field, environmental control or memory-maintenance operation. If the claimed function is passive, continuing external state control cannot be omitted.

Distinguish at least three scientific objects: physically admissible target behaviour, behaviour reachable through the permitted adaptation, and behaviour a boundary-observed teacher can reliably achieve. A local rank calculation alone addresses none of global reach, reliable learning or finite physical cost. Conversely, inability to identify an internal parameter need not prevent useful-function learning. Seek a principle or mechanism that closes a central gap between these objects, rather than another isolated controllability certificate.

Use full quantifiers and give explicit resource dependence on bulk size, boundary channels, target accuracy, observation uncertainty, constitutive range, heterogeneity and the relevant conditioning or barriers. Count both mathematical computation and physical teaching: preparation and identification; boundary instruments and channels; all probes and repeated readouts; control stages, settling and elapsed time; source work and dissipated heat; changes in stored state energy; training-induced wear, drift, irreversible stock or renewal; and retargeting the SAME structure. A polynomial operation count with unbounded pressure, exponentially weak signals, arbitrary precision, hidden field resolution or a freely reset state is not a useful learning principle. Do not equate hydraulic or electrical dissipation alone with the complete work of writing material memory.

Connect the general argument to finite physical scales where the consequence would matter. Use executable cases to expose conditioning, noise, multiple equilibria, history or failure modes where relevant, without treating selected successful simulations as a proof over an entire class. Retain histories within each teaching/retargeting example. A laboratory build is not assumed as the means of establishing the central scientific claim, but the transfer must identify a real physical operation and the constitutive conditions that make it possible. If a critical material operation remains an unconstructed assumption, preserve that limitation instead of promoting the model to an achieved capability.

Compare with the strongest relevant existing approach to the SAME operation and access: conventional inverse design with paid modelling and measurements; boundary-adaptive control and system identification; directed aging; coupled/contrastive learning; in-situ adjoint methods; direct nonlinear crossbar writing; and separately controlled adaptive elements when their installed cost is actually justified. Existing methods may use the same boundary instruments, offline computation, common teaching equipment and competent calibration or recovery. Establish a consequential difference in useful reach, observability burden, robustness, preparation, training cost or accessible operation. Do not invent human waiting, grant the candidate privileged data, or call a standard optimiser with freely programmable internal parameters a new physical learning mechanism.

Concrete starting constructions and their limits

For positive passive fluid resistors joining each of two output ports to two input ports and a reference, the read map is W_ij=(1/R_ij)/sum_k(1/R_ik). With a common linear write law dr/dt=Bp, B the oriented edge-by-node incidence matrix, every cycle functional z^T r with B^T z=0 is conserved under arbitrary boundary-pressure programs. Starting all six resistances equal, the passive target W=[[0.4,0.1],[0.1,0.4]] is unreachable by that update: every positive realization has resistance rows s1*[2.5,10,2] and s2*[10,2.5,2], whose four-edge circulation is -7.5*(s1+s2), while the initial circulation is zero. This is a restriction of the declared adaptation, not an obstruction to all physical learning.

If ALL nodes are pressure-controlled during writing and dr_e/dt=phi(d_e) is an odd state-independent local rule, let v and w select the two endpoints of one edge. Three whole-port fields v+w, -v+w and -w with duration ratios1:1:2 cancel every nonselected edge. The selected change is proportional to 2*phi(1)-phi(2): zero for a linear rule and -6b for phi(d)=a*d+b*d^3. This is related to existing nonlinear selective addressing, not a new material or a supplied bulk-learning method. Unknown gains, state-dependent kinetics, finite bandwidth, gating and retention are separate operations.
