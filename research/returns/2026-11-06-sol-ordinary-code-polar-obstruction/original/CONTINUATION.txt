1. THE OPERATIONAL REPRESENTATION TESTED

For a channel N and logical dimension M, write

  F(N,M)=max_{enc,dec} <Phi_M|(id tensor dec o N o enc)(Phi_M)|Phi_M>.

Only ordinary local CPTP encoders and decoders are allowed. Shared
randomization does not change this maximum because the objective is linear
in a mixture of codes.

Let K(N's input/output dimensions,M) be the convex hull of pairs of normalized
code matrices E tensor D, with the separate exact trace-preserving affine
constraints. The notation K depends on the dimensions, not on the numerical
channel entries. The full-transpose convention on E preserves positivity and
the same affine constraint. With the canonical subsystem reorder,

  H(N,M)=a b (J_N tensor Phi_M)>=0,
  F(N,M)=h_K(H)=max_{Z in K} Tr[H Z].

The precise convention is the original Berta-Borderi-Fawzi-Scholz Lemma 4.2,
equations (111)-(112), https://arxiv.org/html/1810.12197v3 . Its Theorem 4.8
converges to this fixed-channel, fixed-M fidelity as the symmetric extension
level increases. Here a channel N may already be an n-use channel. The
extension level is not the communication block length n.

For positive polars, K^o_+={X>=0:h_K(X)<=1}. The tensor-polar condition needed
in the contemplated divergence route would imply

  K_2^o_+ tensor K_2^o_+ subset K_4^o_+,

where the two-use logical dimension is 2 and the four-use logical dimension
is 4, and the spaces are canonically regrouped. I now give an explicit
rational X in the left factor whose tensor square violates the right side.

2. A UNIVERSAL TWO-USE UPPER BOUND

Take the ordinary qubit erasure channel

  E_p(rho)=(1-p)rho direct_sum p |e><e| Tr rho.

It has input/output dimensions 2,3, and a 6-by-6 normalized rational Choi
matrix for every rational p. In input-first ordering its nonzero entries at
p=1/10 are

  J[0,0]=J[0,4]=J[4,0]=J[4,4]=9/20,
  J[2,2]=J[5,5]=1/20.

Consider ANY ordinary two-use encoder for a logical qubit. Before erasure,
the reference R and two physical qubits A,B have some state rho_RAB, with
rho_R=I/2. Let f_A and f_B be the entanglement fidelities of the respective
decoders used when just A or just B arrives. These two local decoders can be
applied simultaneously to rho_RAB, yielding a state sigma_R Ahat Bhat with
both the correct individual marginals.

Set P=Phi_R Ahat tensor I_Bhat and Q=Phi_R Bhat tensor I_Ahat. Then

  P Q P=P/4,
  ||P+Q||_infinity=3/2,
  f_A+f_B=Tr[(P+Q)sigma]<=3/2.

For the norm identity, P,Q are projections and ||PQ||=1/2. The map
T:psi -> (P psi,Q psi) has TT* with identity diagonal blocks and off-diagonal
blocks of norm 1/2, so ||P+Q||=||T||^2<=3/2. Equality is attained on the
nonzero principal-angle subspace. The executable artifact also gives an exact
8-dimensional PSD certificate and an exact eigenvector for eigenvalue 3/2.

If both physical qubits arrive, the fidelity is at most 1. If neither arrives,
Bob's state is independent of R=I/2 and the fidelity is exactly 1/4. The
erasure flags have input-independent probabilities. Therefore every ordinary
two-use logical-qubit code obeys

  F(E_p^tensor2,2)
  <= (1-p)^2+p(1-p)(3/2)+p^2/4
  = U(p):=1-p/2-p^2/4.

This includes mixed encoders, arbitrary ancillas, and arbitrary decoders. It
uses no presumed optimality of a chosen code. At p=1/10, U=379/400.

3. A FOUR-USE CODE, ITS COMPLETE RECOVERY, AND ITS EXACT FIDELITY

Use the four-qubit, two-logical-qubit erasure code

  |00_L>=(|0000>+|1111>)/sqrt2,
  |01_L>=(|0011>+|1100>)/sqrt2,
  |10_L>=(|0101>+|1010>)/sqrt2,
  |11_L>=(|0110>+|1001>)/sqrt2.

Its stabilizers are XXXX and ZZZZ. Let V=W/sqrt2 be its encoding isometry,
where W is the 16-by-4 zero-one matrix given by the displayed complement
pairs. W*W=2 I_4. Each of the twelve single-site nonidentity Paulis obeys
W* P W=0, establishing the Knill-Laflamme condition for any one erased qubit.

The decoder is explicit on EVERY erasure branch:

  - No erasure: on the code subspace use V*, and send its orthogonal
    complement to logical |00>. This is CPTP and inverts V exactly.

  - Exactly position j erased: let W_{j,x} be the 8-by-4 integer matrix
    obtained from W by retaining its bit-j=x rows and deleting that bit.
    These satisfy

       W_{j,x}* W_{j,y}=delta_xy I_4,
       sum_x W_{j,x} W_{j,x}*=I_8.

    Bob uses decoder Kraus operators W_{j,0}*, W_{j,1}*. Composing them
    with the actual erasure-branch encoder gives delta_xy I_4/sqrt2, so
    the decoded channel is exactly the logical identity.

  - Two or more erasures: Bob discards the surviving systems and prepares
    logical |00>. This deterministic replacement has entanglement fidelity
    1/16 for the four-dimensional logical reference.

No postselection or discarded failure branch is used. The probability of at
most one erasure is

  G(p)=(1-p)^4+4p(1-p)^3
      =1-6p^2+8p^3-3p^4.

Thus this particular complete ordinary four-use code has exact fidelity

  L(p)=G(p)+(1-G(p))/16
      =1-(45/8)p^2+(15/2)p^3-(45/16)p^4.

Subtracting the square of the proven TWO-use upper bound yields

  L(p)-U(p)^2
    =p(1-p)(23p^2-35p+8)/8.

On 0<p<=1/4 the quadratic decreases and is at least 11/16. Hence this
difference is strictly positive throughout that interval. This is an
analytic interval theorem, not an extrapolation from a numerical fixture.

In particular, at p=1/10,

  F(E_p^tensor2,2)^2 <=143641/160000,
  F(E_p^tensor4,4)  >=152155/160000=30431/32000,
  strict gap >=4257/80000.

5. CONSEQUENCE FOR THE FULL COMPUTABILITY ATTEMPT

The attempt to replace regularized coherent information by a regularized
convex-code divergence with automatically tensor-stable positive polars fails
on a simple channel family. The finite symmetric-extension fidelity hierarchy
remains valid at each fixed communication block; the counterexample does not
challenge that theorem. It specifically prevents importing a different
theorem's polar tensor hypothesis for the actual ordinary code sets.

Replacing these sets by a tensor-polar relaxation supplies an upper bound,
but equality to ordinary Q must then be proved. I found no such equality or
certified vanishing relaxation gap. Regularizing the exact support functions
instead merely restores the original asymptotic coding problem.

The erasure family is itself degradable for p<=1/2: Bob can obtain the
environmental erasure output by erasing his surviving state with the
appropriate extra probability. Its coherent information is
(1-2p)S(rho), and the standard degradable-channel coding theorem gives
Q(E_p)=1-2p. Thus the demonstrated polar failure is compatible with a
perfectly computable ordinary capacity and cannot imply global hardness.

I also considered using unbounded symmetric helpers as a continuous resource
parameter. Capacities with a fixed rational helper-use ratio can be expressed
using Q on a finite tensor-product channel. Taking the helper ratio to infinity
still introduces a supremum with no obtained effective tail bound. Concavity,
monotonicity and a known bounded range do not provide one: functions
f_R(r)=h r/(r+R), with unbounded R, can hide almost all their total increase
beyond any prescribed finite r. I did not turn this resource representation
into an ordinary fixed-gap reduction or an upper evaluator.
