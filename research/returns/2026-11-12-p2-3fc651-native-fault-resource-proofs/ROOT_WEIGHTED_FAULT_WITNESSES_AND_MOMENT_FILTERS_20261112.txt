Weighted fault witnesses and local readout moment cancellations

1. A Poisson-to-local-stochastic theorem with spatial tails

Let z range over elementary cell-period boxes in a finite periodic spatial
lattice and nonnegative discrete time. Let independent N_z have Poisson means
at most p. A mark within a box may be chosen adaptively; the following result
does not assume independent directions. Let w(a,z)>=0 be DETERMINISTIC causal
weights with both bounds
 sup_z sum_a w(a,z)<=W, sup_a sum_z w(a,z)<=W,
where0<W<infinity is independent of device capacity and runtime. Suppose an
actual native construction proves that a defective update a can occur only
if sum_z w(a,z)N_z>=beta, with beta>0. Deterministic preparation, coefficient
and approximation errors must already have been subtracted from the margin
used to define beta. This is a substantive PHYSICAL premise, not a consequence
of an expected energy bound.

For any finite set A of updates put s_z=sum_(a in A)w(a,z). Then s_z<=W and
sum_z s_z<=W|A|. If every a in A is defective, sum_z s_z N_z>=beta|A|.
Poisson exponential moments, monotone convergence if needed, and the chord
bound for the convex exponential give
 P(A all defective)
 <=exp[-theta beta|A|+p sum_z(exp(theta s_z)-1)]
 <=exp{[-theta beta+p(exp(theta W)-1)]|A|}.
Take theta=log(1/p)/(2W), for0<p<1. It follows that
 P(A all defective)<=[exp(sqrt(p))*p^(beta/(2W))]^|A|.
The logical local-stochastic parameter therefore tends to zero with a fixed
positive physical fault density. No exact finite propagation or bounded
integer witness is required. Every rare high-count Poisson cluster remains
in the exponential moment, rather than being conditioned away. A classical
reliability result can be used only after matching its actual error and
initialization contract to this bound.

The two Schur-sum hypotheses are essential. One finite expected displacement
or a per-site mean energy estimate does not imply them. A cluster whose
damage has superlinear total space-time weight can violate this interface.
This theorem is a useful sufficient route, not a necessity for every possible
physical architecture or every reliability theorem.

2. An actual linear heat/Alfven readout kernel with finite space-time weight

There is a concrete way to obtain the required summability for one linear
comparison problem. Work on the three-torus of side aM, with fixed a. Let
phi be a fixed smooth compactly supported solenoidal readout vector field
within a cell, with
 integral phi=0, integral x_j phi=0 for j=1,2,3.
Such a nonzero field is available as phi=partial_1 curl A for a suitable
smooth compactly supported vector potential A. Integration by parts proves
both moment identities. Translates have disjoint support if the support is
chosen smaller than the cell. Their self-inner products can encode a fixed
signal; vanishing moments do not force the code signal to vanish.

Let a kick xi have the stipulated fixed support, norm and zero integral.
For the scalar heat propagator, pair phi_i against the evolved xi_j. Expand
the heat kernel once against the zero moment of xi and twice against the
zero and first moments of phi. The resulting bound uses THIRD spatial
derivatives of the heat kernel. For elapsed time t>=1 and every q>3, it has
the form
 |<phi_i, exp(s Delta)xi_j>|
 <=C_q sum_(ell in Z^3)t^-3
 [1+|a(i-j)+aM ell|/sqrt(t)]^-q,
where s is between fixed positive multiples of t. Fixed compact-support
sizes only change C_q. This follows from the integral Taylor remainder and
the ordinary Gaussian derivative formula; it is not a finite-mode cutoff.

Summing over all spatial cells of a periodic device unfolds the periodized
sum to the infinite a-lattice. Thus the bound is C t^-3/2, uniformly in M.
For bounded t use the fixed smooth norms and rapid off-diagonal heat decay.
Taking suprema over a time box and summing over integer positive time boxes
gives a finite constant W independent of M and the total observation time.
The reverse Schur sum has the same bound by translation invariance. At an
observation with a jump, use the declared post-jump convention; its same-box
direct pairing has a fixed finite contribution to W.

For the linearization of MHD about(0,H_*), if nu=rho=chi(t) with chi in a
fixed positive interval, the Elsasser variables u+b and u-b satisfy heat
equations translated by +H_*t and -H_*t. Translation does not change the
lattice-sum estimate: use the Gaussian lattice bound uniformly in its shift.
Unknown common diffusivity enters only through integral chi, retaining the
same fixed constants. No value of chi is read or estimated.

This is an actual all-frequency LINEAR comparison result at EQUAL viscosity
and resistivity. It does not yet prove the estimate for independently
varying nu,rho, around a driven nonlinear code reference, or for the actual
state-dependent faulty dynamics. In particular the weighted superposition
of kicks is a linear property that cannot be silently inherited by MHD.

Without the readout moment cancellations, one input cancellation gives a
spatial L1 heat bound of order t^-1/2, not integrable over long time. One
additional readout cancellation gives a t^-1 borderline logarithm. The two
readout moments above make the combined three derivatives integrable. This
explains a concrete choice of local ports that changes the long-tail issue,
instead of requiring the fluid itself to have finite propagation.

3. Full nonlinear resource scopes from the completed return

For the actual MHD system with fixed periodic mean-zero drive, centred bounded
zero-integral local kicks, and every energy-admissible realization, the
completed return supplies
 E E(t)+.5 E integral_0^t D <=C V(1+t),
 E integral_0^t D<=C V(1+t), E W_kick(t)<=C V(1+t).
The absolute kick-work estimate subtracts each local field average before
pairing with its zero-integral mark, then uses bounded overlap and the actual
dissipation. It includes every failed computation and rare cluster.

The uniform-in-time bound sup_t E E(t)<=C V and absolute prescribed-drive
work are still unproved. A symmetric sign kick can generate an averaged
Reynolds or Maxwell stress at second order; conditional centering removes
the linear energy cross term, not every nonlinear mean response. A vanishing
global spectral gap is an inadequate estimate, not a universal obstruction.

The weighted-witness argument requires actual native module renewal,
neighbour transfer, buffer cleanup and a nonlinear localization estimate.
It neither supplies those operations nor resolves the uniform energy bound.
An architectural proof could instead use different ports or a different
reliability representation; moment filters and this kernel condition are
optional constructions with the stated limits.
