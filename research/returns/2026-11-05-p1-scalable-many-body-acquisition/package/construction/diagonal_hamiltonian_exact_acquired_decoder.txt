1. CAPABILITY, MODEL, AND QUANTIFIERS

There are n source sites of fixed local dimension q. Their arbitrary initial
joint state may be entangled with an arbitrary reference inaccessible to the
receiver. The receiver cannot operate on the source or turn off its Hamiltonian.
It receives specified calibrated output ports coherently and without loss.

The known Hamiltonian is

  H(t) = sum_(X in E) h_X(t),       |X| <= k,

where q and k are fixed. EVERY h_X(t) is diagonal in ONE known fixed product
basis, at all times. This includes arbitrary strengths and arbitrary signs of
local diagonal interactions. It does not include every commuting Hamiltonian:
commutation alone need not give a common product eigenbasis.

Let m_H=|E| and D=max_i #{X in E: i in X}. The term list has polynomial size and
there is an efficient numerical procedure to integrate any diagonal table entry
of h_X(t) on specified time intervals to specified absolute accuracy. A static
local Hamiltonian meets this promise directly. Arbitrary uncomputable time
functions are not an efficient input description.

The ideal reset/export instrument has independent rate-r clocks at each source
site. An event at i implements the pure isometry

  V_i |psi>_(S_i) = |Phi_q>_(S_i A) |psi>_B,
  |Phi_q> = q^(-1/2) sum_(a=0)^(q-1) |a>|a>.

The receiver obtains the event's coarse time/site label and quantum registers
A and B. Equivalently, its reduced action is replacement of source site i by
I/q, with the OLD source qudit exported coherently into B. All source-reference
correlations in that qudit are carried by B. The receiver's operations below act
only on acquired output registers and its own hardware.

Section 5 earns a finite approximation to this instrument from actual continuous
vacuum ports. Exact event times and instantaneous abstract source SWAPs are not
assumed as uncharged physical access.

THEOREM, IDEAL INSTRUMENT.
For every r>0, every Hamiltonian in this class, and every record in which all n
sites have reset at least once, the initial arbitrary n-qudit state is exactly
recoverable from one retained first-export qudit per site by a known diagonal
unitary. This includes reference entanglement. No comparison of r with interaction
strength is required.

At deterministic time T, treating an incomplete record as failure gives a CPTP
transfer channel with diamond error at most

  epsilon_ideal <= 2[1-(1-exp(-rT))^n] <= 2n exp(-rT).

Its retained payload memory consists of n q-level registers. The full reset
history can be processed into polynomial-size phase tables and then discarded.
There is one final <=k-local diagonal correction gate per Hamiltonian term,
before ordinary fixed-dimensional gate synthesis and hardware routing.

2. EXPLICIT DECODER AND EXACT PROOF

At every event, measure A in the specified product basis. Its outcome a is
uniform and independent of the payload and previous outcomes. Conditioned on
a, the new source site is exactly |a>. Retain B if this is the FIRST event at
that site. Later B registers from that site are known basis states and can be
discarded. Never measure the retained B registers in the payload basis.

A measurement of A can be performed later at the receiver. It commutes with all
subsequent operations on the source and other output systems, so the proof may
place it immediately after its event. This is deferred measurement, not an
assumption of instantaneous communication or feedback to the source.

Let gamma denote the event record and the measured reset labels. Let tau_i be
the first event time at site i, and let a_i(t) be the most recent reset outcome
at i after tau_i. For an initial basis string x define

  z_i(t;x) = x_i                  if t < tau_i,
             a_i(t)              if t >= tau_i.

Define the real phase function

  f_gamma(x) = integral_0^T sum_X h_X(t; z_X(t;x)) dt.          (1)

Write a purification of the arbitrary input as

  |psi>_(SR) = sum_x |x>_S |phi_x>_R,
  sum_x <phi_x|phi_x> = 1.

The reference vectors need not be orthogonal. Between events, the diagonal
Hamiltonian multiplies each branch x only by its known branch-dependent phase.
At a first reset at i, the original label x_i is exported into its retained B_i;
the source site becomes the known basis label a. At every later reset at i,
the old source state is its known current basis label and is uncorrelated with
the quantum payload conditioned on the record. Diagonal interactions leave a
known-basis source site in that same basis state. They can change phases on the
other unknown labels, but cannot reintroduce an unknown quantum label into the
already reset site.

An induction over intervals and resets therefore gives, once every site has
reset, the conditional joint state

  [sum_x exp(-i f_gamma(x)) |x>_(B_1...B_n) |phi_x>_R]
      tensor |known source and discarded-export basis states>.

All factors other than the displayed payload/reference state are specified by
gamma and carry no x dependence. The diagonal receiver unitary

  U_corr,gamma = sum_x exp(+i f_gamma(x)) |x><x|                (2)

on the retained B_i recovers |psi> exactly. Linearity establishes the result for
mixed inputs as well. This is an actual quantum operation; no amplitudes of the
unknown input are estimated or classically represented.

The event clocks have state-independent rates. Each A measurement has uniform
conditional distribution because the reset produces a fresh maximally entangled
pair independently of the old payload qudit. Thus the whole classical record is
independent of the unknown input. At fixed time T, the probability of completion
is exactly (1-exp(-rT))^n. On failure the receiver can output any fixed state,
giving diamond error at most twice its probability. In the ideal continuous
instrument it can instead wait until every first reset, which happens almost
surely and has expected waiting time H_n/r. The finite implementation below uses
a fixed timeout, so it has deterministic finite clock and control resources.

3. PHASE CIRCUIT, STREAMING ALGORITHM, AND PRECISION

Equation (1) is a sum of functions on at most k ORIGINAL logical labels:

  f_gamma(x) = sum_X F_X,gamma(x_X),

  F_X,gamma(y_X)
      = integral_0^T h_X(t; z_X(t;y_X)) dt.                   (3)

In (3), the unreset sites in X use the symbolic entries y_i and the reset sites
use their most recent known classical labels. For each X, break the integral at
reset events touching X. There are at most

  S <= m_H + sum_(events at i) deg(i) <= m_H + D M             (4)

such local integration pieces, where M is the number of processed reset events.
For each piece, add its diagonal table to F_X. Since q and k are fixed, each table
has at most q^k entries. All the final diagonal functions commute, so

  U_corr,gamma = product_X exp(+i F_X,gamma(B_X)).              (5)

There is ONE final <=k-local diagonal gate per term X, not one quantum gate per
reset interval. The reset dependence is handled by classical integration and
phase-table accumulation. The total classical table work is O(q^k S), measured
in numerical integrations/additions at the stated precision. A receiver can
maintain the current reset labels, first-reset flags, last-update time for each
term, and its accumulated phase table. It need not store the full record.

For phase-channel error epsilon_phi, one sufficient allocation is:

  each scalar integration/arithmetic error <= epsilon_phi/(4S),
  each compiled local correction's operator-norm error
      <= epsilon_phi/(4m_H).

Uniform total phase error is then at most epsilon_phi/4, hence channel error at
most epsilon_phi/2; the compiled gate errors contribute at most the remaining
half. If m_H=0, no phase gates are needed. A deterministic bound S_max may replace
S when allocating precision before collection. For the finite-bin scheme below,
M<=nL, so S_max=m_H+D nL is a valid polynomial choice.

If B0=sup_t sum_X ||h_X(t)||, a conservative arithmetic word length is

  O(log((1+T B0) max(1,S_max) / epsilon_phi)) bits,

in addition to the bits needed for the supplied time dependence. Phases can be
reduced modulo 2pi during accumulation. Numerical integration cost depends on
the explicit regularity/computability promise for h_X; it is not declared free.

With fixed q,k, each gate in (5) has constant dimension. It can be decomposed into
ordinary few-qudit gates and synthesized with a polylogarithmic precision
overhead using a fixed universal gate set. If receiver connections implement the
source interaction hyperedges, this preserves locality. Otherwise ordinary SWAP
routing must be counted; even a simple chain gives only polynomial overhead.

4. STROBOSCOPIC IDEAL INSTRUMENT

For a bin width Delta=T/L, the local noise-only channel is

  D_p = (1-p) id + p R_tau,       p=1-exp(-r Delta),
  R_tau(rho)=I/q tensor Tr_i(rho).

A convenient nonminimal pure dilation is

  |psi> -> sqrt(1-p)|psi>|empty>
             +sqrt(p)|Phi_q>_(S_i A)|psi>_B.                 (6)

Measure empty versus reset, and in the reset branch measure A in its basis.
This produces independent Bernoulli(p) flags and uniform reset labels. At each
global bin, perform the product noise channels, then the known unitary

  U_j = exp[-i integral_(j Delta)^((j+1)Delta) H(t) dt].       (7)

The time-ordered exponential has become an ordinary exponential because the
Hamiltonians are diagonal in the same fixed basis. The same induction as above
is exact in this model. The phase function is

  f_gamma,Delta(x)
    = sum_(j=0)^(L-1) sum_X
        integral_(j Delta)^((j+1)Delta)
             h_X(t; z_(X,j)(x)) dt,                         (8)

where z_(i,j)(x) uses x_i if no reset flag has occurred at i through bin j,
and otherwise the most recent measured reset label. Noise occurs before U_j;
this convention matters when constructing the phase tables.

The probability that any given site has no reset flag in all L bins is
(1-p)^L=exp(-rT), so the same exact completion and error formulas hold. The local
piece count and streaming algorithm remain valid, counting each flagged reset
as an event. Coarse flags, not individual physical photons, are the events in
this compressed instrument. M is binomial(nL,p), with E M<=nrT and M<=nL.

No assumption about tiny binwise Boolean dependency cones is used. The phase
proof exploits the diagonal structure exactly. The fictitious instantaneous
unitaries in (7) are an analysis model; they are not operations imposed on the
physical source.

5. EARNING THE FINITE INTERFACE FROM CONTINUOUS VACUUM PORTS

5.1 Actual dilation and full-history comparison

One permitted physical input consists of actual calibrated local vacuum ports

  L_(i,ab)=sqrt(r/q)|a><b|_i,             a,b=0,...,q-1,

all delivered coherently. They generate r(R_tau-id) on each source site and have
state-independent total intensity R=nr. Their coherent mark is the reset
isometry in section 1.

Another permitted input consists of actual nonidentity Weyl ports

  L_(i,mu)=sqrt(r) W_mu/q,              mu!=0.

Here the Weyl operators satisfy sum_(all mu) W_mu rho W_mu^dagger=q Tr(rho) I.
The reduced noise is the same r(R_tau-id), while the PHYSICAL total jump
intensity is R_phys=nr(1-q^-2). The improved comparison below uses this smaller
physical rate directly. A reduced generator without calibrated coherent output
access is not sufficient to claim either input interface.

Let V be the actual continuous joint source-and-field isometry on [0,T]. Let
W be its comparison dilation in which noise evolves alone inside each bin and
the exact integrated Hamiltonian unitary (7) is applied at that bin's end. Define

  chi = sup_t max_i sum_(X containing i) osc(h_X(t)),
  osc(h)=lambda_max(h)-lambda_min(h).

For a normalized local coherent jump-mark isometry J_i, disjoint Hamiltonian
terms intertwine exactly. Center every touching h_X at its spectral midpoint.
Because ||J_i||=1,

  ||H_out(t)J_i - J_i H_in(t)|| <= chi.                       (9)

Duhamel's identity bounds moving a Hamiltonian segment through J_i by chi times
that segment's duration. For a COMPLETE history gamma, move Hamiltonian pieces
only inside their original bins, preserving their original time labels and
order. Each event crosses at most Delta of Hamiltonian evolution, and intervening
operators are norm-one isometries or unitaries. Thus

  ||V_gamma-W_gamma|| <= chi Delta m_gamma.                  (10)

The arrival-time/site histories are orthogonal sectors of the same output Fock
space; mark labels remain coherent. Since total intensity is R I, their weights
are input independent and the total event count m_gamma is Poisson(RT). The
direct-integral norm estimate therefore gives

  ||V-W|| <= chi Delta sqrt(E m_gamma^2)
           = chi Delta sqrt(RT+(RT)^2).

It holds for the whole coherent output and every reference. Consequently

  epsilon_split <= 2 chi Delta sqrt(RT+(RT)^2).               (11)

This improves the valid but looser estimate obtained by summing separate bin
isometry norms. It needs no condition R Delta<=1. A sufficient choice is

  L = max{1,ceil[2 chi T sqrt(RT+(RT)^2)/epsilon_split]}.

For RT>=1, L=O(1+chi R T^2/epsilon_split). These are GLOBAL bins. The number of
local collector uses is N_loc=nL.

For the nonidentity Weyl implementation, use R=R_phys in (9)-(11). Its noise-only
local channel is still D_p. Applying the local collector and environment isometry
below therefore produces the reset flag probability p=1-exp(-r Delta), although
that flag rate differs from the physical photon count rate. Coherent conversion
of the environment must precede measuring the desired coarse reset flag.

Alternatively, add a source-independent coherent identity port sqrt(r)I/q at
the receiver and use a fixed port unitary to obtain the matrix-unit representation.
That implementation pays mean reference photon number nrT/q^2. Appending the same
pure reference and port unitary to both sides of the comparison preserves the
bound with R_phys. This augmentation is optional when the canonical-environment
route below is used directly.

5.2 Constant-dimensional local acquisition

In every site/bin, apply the inherited N1732 chronological receiver to the actual
calibrated ports, calibrated for that site's NOISE-ONLY D_p channel. It uses a
q^2-level receiver, hence fixed local dimension. It acquires the entire local
canonical Stinespring channel, including source and reference correlations, to
chosen full-channel error eta. Source inputs across different sites may be
arbitrarily entangled; complete-channel approximation handles this.

The acquired canonical environment has dimension at most q^2. A known
fixed-dimensional environment isometry maps it to the q^2+1-dimensional
nonminimal empty/reset dilation (6). This follows from Stinespring equivalence
AFTER acquisition, and can be compiled directly from the constant-size Kraus
matrices. Extra constant-dimensional workspace makes it a finite unitary.

Apply the same receiver postprocessing to V and W. Channel contraction preserves
(11), and a telescoping bound over N_loc local uses contributes at most
N_loc eta. The actual approximate flag record need not have exactly the ideal
input-independent distribution. The entire instrument is compared in diamond
norm; no conditioning or free reweighting of approximate record probabilities
is needed. The diagonal decoder on its acquired registers is fixed physical
postprocessing of that instrument.

The inherited receiver's unital-guide regularization has mu=alpha/q^2 with
alpha=Theta(eta^2). Its explicit finite controls give the conservative local
bounds, for fixed q:

  peak port coupling             O_q(sqrt(r)/eta),
  peak receiver Hamiltonian      O_q(r/eta),
  analytic port slew             O_q(r^(3/2)/eta^3),
  analytic Hamiltonian slew      O_q(r^2/eta^3),
  executed control pieces/bin    O_q(1+(r Delta)^2/eta^5).

These follow by substituting mu and zeta=Theta(eta) into the governing formulas
||C_j||<=l_j mu^-1/2, ||H_M||<=g mu^-1/2,
||dot C_j||<=l_j b0 mu^-3/2, ||dot H_M||<=g b0 mu^-3/2, and
N_segments>=2b0(g+5s)Delta^2/(zeta mu^2), with g,s,b0=O_q(r).

Endpoint ramps, finite setting precision, and inter-bin export/reinitialization
must also be paid. A total ramp duration O(min(Delta,eta^3/r)) per local use is a
sufficient conservative choice, because the joint source/collector generator
norm is O_q(r eta^-2). Its change over that duration costs O(eta), absorbable in
the local error allocation. If compiled pieces are made continuous by smoothing
all their transitions, divide that total duration among the polynomially many
transitions. Peak divided by the resulting transition duration gives a finite
polynomial slew bound. Alternatively implement the smooth analytic controls
directly to their assigned uniform accuracy. No infinite-slew resource is needed.

Two alternating collector banks per site allow one bank to receive while the
other exports its register, converts and measures the flag/A, and reinitializes.
Those constant-dimensional operations must complete within Delta; the required
rate, or extra pipeline banks, is a charged resource. Fresh local pure-state
preparations/reinitializations number O(N_loc), and any entropy-removal or fresh
ancilla supply needed to realize them belongs to the receiver implementation.
The same analytic local waveform can be compiled once and reused; this reduces
classical design work but does not remove N_loc physical executions.

These are finite-control bounds in the inherited calibrated Markov model. They
do not assert an adequate fixed experimental bandwidth or a bounded microscopic
field Hamiltonian outside that model's validity range.

6. COMPLETE ERROR AND RESOURCE BILL

For any specified error budget, choose positive allocations obeying

  epsilon >= epsilon_fail + epsilon_split + epsilon_col
             + epsilon_phi + epsilon_memory + epsilon_cal.

For example, any fixed fractions of epsilon suffice. Set

  T >= log(2n/epsilon_fail)/r,
  L as in section 5.1,
  Delta=T/L,
  N_loc=nL,
  eta <= epsilon_col/N_loc.

Then the actual deterministic transfer channel obeys

  ||N_actual-id||_diamond
      <= 2n exp(-rT) + epsilon_split + N_loc eta
         +epsilon_phi+epsilon_memory+epsilon_cal.             (12)

All terms concern the initial arbitrary input, including a reference. There is
no new payload introduced after resource preparation and no postselected restart.

Collection duration: T=O(log(n/epsilon)/r).

Interface bins: L=O(1+chi T sqrt(RT+(RT)^2)/epsilon_split).
For fixed q, R<=nr and the displayed T imply the conservative scaling
L=O(1+(chi/r) n log^2(n/epsilon)/epsilon_split) in the nontrivial RT>=1 regime.
Local collector uses are N_loc=nL, including the extra factor n.

Quantum working space: n first-export payload qudits plus O(n) constant-size
collector/conversion workspaces. No n-qudit dummy source and no storage of every
later export are needed. Reusable banks require O(N_loc) initializations over
time. Fixed-dimensional gate decomposition may use a constant number of
additional ancillas per concurrently processed term.

Physical field traffic: mean RT photons in the specified jump-port basis, with
R=nr for matrix-unit ports or R=nr(1-q^-2) for nonidentity Weyl ports. Optional
identity-reference augmentation costs a further nrT/q^2 mean photons.

Classical processing: N_loc coarse flags and at most M<=N_loc reset labels;
E M<=nrT. Streaming table updates take O(q^k[m_H+D M]) numerical integrations
and additions, plus flag processing, at the precision in section 3. A deterministic
polynomial implementation may provision the upper bound m_H+D N_loc.

Classical working memory: O(m_H q^k) phase-table entries, O(m_H) last-update
bin indices and O(n) current labels/first-reset flags. Each bin index uses
O(log L) bits; numerical entries use the explicitly bounded word length.
Keeping a full audit record is optional and costs O(M log(nLq)) additional bits.

Final quantum computation: m_H fixed-dimensional diagonal gates, followed by
ordinary gate synthesis and, where needed, routing. There is no global q^n by
q^n matrix calculation or unitary synthesis. All dependence on Hamiltonian
strength in phase calculation is charged through the numerical precision and
integrability promise; all dependence in field acquisition is charged through
chi and the collector parameters.

Control execution: N_loc times O_q(1+(r Delta)^2/eta^5) control pieces, with
peak, slew, finite ramps and pipeline rates specified in section 5. This is a
polynomial upper bound and can have a large degree. It is not an economic estimate.

Storage and gate noise: if an implementation has per-stored-qudit diamond
error-generator bound nu, uncorrected storage contributes at most
nu n(T+T_decode), where T_decode includes its actual correction/routing time.
It must satisfy the assigned epsilon_memory budget, or ordinary error correction
must supply the required effective memory with its own gate, ancilla and time
overhead. Physical gate errors add by the usual channel telescoping bound and
must be included in the correction/hardware error allocation; numerical synthesis
precision does not by itself certify a laboratory gate.

Calibration: the theorem assumes the specified calibrated coherent ports and a
known Hamiltonian description. If the actual Hamiltonian differs by delta H(t),
a sufficient additive full-channel error bound is
2 integral_0^T ||delta H(t)|| dt; for local calibration errors, sum local norms.
Any port, receiver, timing or loss discrepancy must likewise be covered by an
acquired full-channel interface bound in epsilon_cal. Knowing only a reduced
source decay rate does not establish that bound. Model characterization from
an unknown single source is not claimed as a free operation here.

For fixed q,k and a polynomial-size efficiently integrable Hamiltonian input,
all displayed resources are polynomial in n, 1/epsilon, 1/r, charged strengths
and input-description complexity. Large chi/r can be expensive. The theorem
says every fixed positive r is permitted, not that arbitrary interaction strength
can be handled with one fixed bandwidth or power ceiling.

7. STRONGEST ORDINARY COMPARISON AND LIMITS

With the SAME ports and H=0, retaining the first reset export at each site already
gives ordinary coupon-collector transfer in O(log(n/epsilon)/r) time. The theorem
shows that arbitrary known diagonal interactions preserve this architecture:
all their influence is a computable phase correction on the captured payload.
The collection-time scaling is unchanged, while phase and acquired-interface
costs are added explicitly. For decoupled sources, individual full-interval local
collectors may be a better implementation than the conservative bin scheme.

If direct source-side SWAP operations are available, ordinary parallel SWAP
transfer can be faster and simpler. Such access is not supplied by output ports
and is not included in this theorem. The abstract reset-isometry representation
must not be mistaken for permission to pulse the physical source.

The theorem does not recover from an inaccessible hidden noise bath. In
particular, adding hidden Z dephasing would remove payload phase information
without exporting its record, and would require a separate quantitative error
or coding analysis. The earlier acquisition resource law for such noise remains
applicable to its own physical interface.

The exact diagonal family proves that a reset-versus-spreading inequality such
as r>beta in the general noncommuting-Hamiltonian decoder is a sufficient
constructive regime rather than a universal acquisition threshold. It does not
prove efficient output-only decoding for every noncommuting Hamiltonian at every
r, nor does it claim a compact receiver for every observable of the full global
canonical Stinespring output.
