Invent a practical modular process that makes useful ammonia from air, water and electricity.

Can a new physical mechanism or reactor architecture make small, distributed ammonia production economically useful without relying on a supplied hydrogen factory, sacrificial high-energy reagents or a tiny analytical ammonia signal? The sought capability is a complete air-and-water-to-fertilizer operation. An abstract chemical computer, isolated catalyst selectivity or a cathode-only efficiency does not provide it.

Use a nominal 1 kg NH3 per day module as the worked complete design. Inputs are ordinary air at 1 bar and 20--35 degrees Celsius, water of a specified attainable quality, electricity, and explicitly charged replaceable materials. Deliver ammonia as an aqueous product containing at least 10 wt-percent NH3-equivalent, with a stated impurity specification suitable for a concrete fertilizer use. Express output and all costs per mass of actual NH3, not mass of dilute solution. The design target is continuous useful production over a 100-hour run, net purchased energy at most 12 kWh/kg NH3, and no process component above 250 degrees Celsius or 20 bar. State startup/shutdown, cycling and ambient tolerances. These are invention targets, not assumed existing performance.

At a credible modular scale of 1 tonne NH3 per day, aim for a full production cost at most US$1/kg NH3 at electricity US$0.05/kWh. Include the complete plant and actual preparation/replacement/maintenance: air separation, gas purification and drying, water treatment, reactor and membranes, catalysts/mediators/electrolytes, heat management, pressure equipment, controls, product recovery/concentration, losses, containment and waste handling, manufacturing yield, labour and financing. Give explicit utilization, capital-life and material-life assumptions and sensitivity. Separate the first demonstrator budget from a production-scale cost model. No component price, long lifetime, regeneration efficiency or industrial access is gifted.

You may choose electrochemical, thermochemical, plasma-catalytic, staged chemical-looping or another physically consistent representation. A different approach that better supplies the same useful operation is welcome. Do not assume that low temperature is automatically low energy or that an electrochemical label is necessary. The entire route must have actual constituents, attainable preparation, dimensions and operating conditions. A newly proposed material may be the discovery, but its required properties, synthesis, stability and governing evidence must be explicit.

The net sustainable chemistry is nitrogen plus water to ammonia and oxygen, with every intermediate inventory restored or its replacement charged. If hydrogen is produced first, include its electrolyzer, purification, drying, storage and energy. If a metal, nitride, proton donor, electrolyte or organic species provides reducing equivalents or hydrogen, close its regeneration and material balance. An initial stock of lithium metal, hydride, alcohol or precharged membrane is not a renewable power source. Count long-time net solvent/salt consumption and side products. Show where the oxygen and unused nitrogen go. An aqueous ammonium product obtained by consuming acid has a different reagent balance from recovered ammonia and must be accounted consistently.

Some useful actual openings and limits:
- A monolithic flow-cell experiment has coupled aqueous water oxidation to a nonaqueous lithium-mediated nitrogen-reduction compartment through an electrically isolated palladium membrane. The membrane transfers hydrogen while suppressing bulk-water crossover. This supplies an actual aqueous/nonaqueous interface, not a complete cheap ammonia plant. The measured regime includes low current density, finite prehydridation, modest Faradaic efficiency, multi-volt full-cell loss and membrane/solvent degradation concerns.
- A dissociative metal-mediated thermodynamic analysis separates electrodeposition, nitride formation and protonation. A less costly deposition step is useful only if the complete subsequent cycle remains possible. Shifting a half-cell reference, changing ion solvation or consuming a proton donor cannot by itself remove whole-cycle energy.
- For an electrochemical route with three useful electrons per NH3, the charge requirement is about 4721 Ah/kg divided by the actual ammonia Faradaic efficiency. Thus 12 kWh/kg with 90-percent Faradaic efficiency permits at most about 2.29 V charge-weighted complete-cell voltage before auxiliaries. A 1-kg/day module at that efficiency carries about 219 A on average. These are physical diagnostics, not supplied catalyst, membrane or power-density achievements.
- Whole-cell energy is the integral of actual terminal voltage times current, plus all auxiliaries and preparation, divided by measured recovered NH3. An iR-corrected cathode potential, Faradaic efficiency, a brief peak rate and energy for only one compartment cannot replace that quantity.

Construct the complete process and its central new mechanism. Give the active-material or catalyst choice and preparation, reactor geometry, actual feed compositions and circulation, charge/heat/mass-transfer and kinetic model, separation/recycle, startup and control, and a numerical operating point. Use experimental rate/stability inputs where available and transparent bounded assumptions where not. Provide an executable complete-cycle model and the sensitivities that could destroy output, energy or cost advantage. Include finite mass transfer, current distribution, gas/water crossover, contaminant accumulation, product inhibition and loss of active material. A drawing with freely assigned selectivity or a DFT barrier alone is not the full reactor.

Compare the same useful product, scale, energy boundary and utilization with the strongest relevant existing routes, including water electrolysis plus Haber-Bosch and delivered fertilizer where relevant. Credit actual prior nitrogen-fixation inventions. Explain which load-bearing physical operation is genuinely new. If an existing matched technology already supplies the target, the proposed invention must show a consequential additional advantage rather than retitle known work.

Develop a concrete smaller decisive prototype for later physical execution. Specify necessary components and paid access, measurements, power and complete material balances, ordinary controls, isotopic nitrogen tracing, blank/contamination controls, analytical calibration and numerical pass/fail thresholds. Distinguish freshly fixed atmospheric nitrogen from pre-existing ammonia/nitrogen-containing materials and hydrogen from a depleting initial reservoir. Quantify the expected signal above blanks and total prototype cost. The prototype must test the central proposed new operation and its greatest failure mode. Design the experiment here; do not execute it. Its possible success must not be represented as proof of production lifetime or a 1-tonne/day factory.

Please work on the full practical invention, not a proposal-only survey or another ideal universality theorem. Seek a mechanism that could make the requested useful chemistry work, pay its actual resources and state which central operations are constructed, supported by measurements or still untested. You may challenge the framing or use a different mechanism. Failure of one metal, interface or route is not an all-ammonia-production obstruction.
