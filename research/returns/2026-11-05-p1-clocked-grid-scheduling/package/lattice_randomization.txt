Irregular task intervals do not imply incoherent physical forcing
================================================================

This elementary signal-theory calculation explains a mitigation failure
already reported by Hartmann et al. (2026). Neither the calculation nor the
physical mechanism is claimed as historically new.

Let launches remain confined to nT+phi. Let Q_n be stationary ergodic
nonnegative energy marks with mean e. All tasks share a real integrable
kernel h with H(f)=integral h(t) exp(-2*pi*i*f*t) dt. Form

    p(t)=sum_n Q_n h(t-nT-phi).

After removing boundary tails, the long-record complex coefficient at k/T is

    C_k=(e/T) H(k/T) exp(-2*pi*i*k*phi/T).

At f=k/T every lattice factor exp(-2*pi*i*k*n) is one. The coefficient over
L periods equals H(k/T) exp(-2*pi*i*k*phi/T) times sum(Q_n)/(LT), plus a
vanishing boundary term. Ergodicity gives the displayed limit. Thus variance
in inter-event gaps, or skipping many cycles, does not itself remove a line.
A spectral zero of H, phase diversification, or mean energy reduction is
needed to remove that line in this model.

For iid Q_n of variance sigma_Q^2, the time-averaged two-sided PSD is

    S_p(f)= (sigma_Q^2/T)|H(f)|^2
          + sum_{k in Z} (e^2/T^2)|H(k/T)|^2 delta(f-k/T).

Here f is cycles/second and R_p(tau)=integral S_p(f) exp(2*pi*i*f*tau) df.
The cyclostationary process is averaged over one clock period. Correlated
marks can change the continuous term and add further structure.

If tests are retained independently with probability r and energy e/r,
Q_n=(e/r) B_n with B_n Bernoulli(r), then sigma_Q^2=e^2(1-r)/r. Mean work per
period remains e. With a fixed kernel the line is unchanged and broadband
noise increases as r falls. If compensation lengthens the pulse, the new
H_r must be used. The line may then change. This does not claim that the
published firmware update left harmonic amplitude unchanged. Random skipping
also cannot meet a finite hard maximum inter-test interval almost surely.

The public after-update trace has starts at elapsed 86,145,506 s, or 26,25,26 s
modulo 60. It confirms phase concentration at one-second sample resolution,
but does not establish iid marks, ergodicity or an asymptotic PSD. Supplementary
Note 9 already documents phase locking, longer pulses and partial mitigation.
