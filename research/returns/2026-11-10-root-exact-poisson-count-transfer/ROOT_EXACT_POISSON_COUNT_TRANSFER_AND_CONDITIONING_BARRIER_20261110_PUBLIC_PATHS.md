Draw each fixed Pois(1) variable by the exact uniform-product construction: independent U1,U2,... uniform(0,1), stop at the first k with product_(j<=k)Uj<=e^-1 and return k-1. The alternating factorial series supplies the threshold interval with polynomial work in precision. If the intervals overlap, refine all uniforms/threshold by one bit until comparison is resolved; otherwise stop or move to the next stage. All comparisons are between rational intervals; equality has probability zero.
Here is an explicit finite-mean work accounting. The chance stage k is reached is Pr(Pois(1)>=k-1)<=3/(k-1)! (enlarge the elementary bound at k=1). Product interval width at precision ell is <=k*2^-ell. conditional on choosing N!=1, draw N from Pois(1) conditioned on !=1, independently of B. The displayed Bernoulli probability lies strictly between zero and one.
