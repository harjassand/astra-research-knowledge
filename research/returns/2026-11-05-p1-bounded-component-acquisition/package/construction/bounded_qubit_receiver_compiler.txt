BOUNDED TWO-LEVEL COMPILATION OF A CAUSAL PASSIVE FIELD RECEIVER
Research note, 4 November 2026
Agent: independent_opening; companion to the continuous-Hamiltonian acquisition construction

STATUS AND CLAIM

This note proves a receiver compiler. A bosonic cell with L=sqrt(kappa) a has
fixed linewidth but an unbounded material-system operator. Each physical
capture cell here is instead a two-level system with

  L(t)=g(t) sigma_minus,             ||L(t)||=|g(t)|<=g_max,

where g_max and its slew bound do not grow with source size or accuracy.
Accuracy is paid through rails, cells, precision, memory, transport, and bounded
quantum gate time. The construction preserves arbitrary input-reference
entanglement; it uses no photon-number measurement, collision postselection,
source operation, or ideal field buffer. The field is processed as it arrives.

The physical model is a coherent dispersionless chiral field with local Markov
couplings. Fixed local response, amplitude, slew, and material-system operator
norm are earned. A fixed hard aggregate optical passband, fixed propagation
loss, fixed memory noise, and a microscopic ultraviolet completion are not
conclusions. The companion first_ingress_bandwidth_loss_converse.txt proves
sharp first-loss limits, including a separately audited actual-Weyl-source
converse. The original expander and reverse-reset notes are unchanged.

1. INPUT AND ERROR CONVENTIONS

Let A be an arbitrary unknown input, possibly entangled with a reference R.
An input-independent isometry V emits J bosonic field ports during the finite
source-time window [0,L] and leaves an arbitrary remaining system E. Unused
receiver field inputs are independent vacuum. Let N_j count photons in port j
during [0,L]. Assume uniform operator inequalities

  V^dagger N_j(N_j-1) V <= F_j I_A.                            (1)

These inequalities allow arbitrary temporal entanglement, photon-number
superpositions, and inter-port correlations. They hold after tensoring any
reference, unlike a check on one input state's average photon number.

Suppose a proposed passive linear receiver B has N_j^cell oscillator memory
modes for port j, initially vacuum. Couplings are g_m(t)a_m with bounded
coefficient, feed-forward field connections, actual finite delays, and known
source-time schedules. It freezes memory by setting coupling to zero. Its
one-particle transformation and later passive mode conversion are computable.
The compiler retains any complete-channel approximation already proved for B.

The norm ||C-D||_diamond is the unhalved diamond distance. Two isometries obey

  ||Ad_U-Ad_W||_diamond <= 2||U-W||.                           (2)

Tracing output systems and later quantum processing contract this distance.

2. PHYSICAL CONSTRUCTION

For each original port j choose P_j rails. A static lossless splitter sends

  b_j^dagger(f) -> P_j^(-1/2) sum_{p=1}^{P_j} b_jp^dagger(f).  (3)

Orthogonal spatial inputs are vacuum. A balanced binary tree implements this
with P_j-1 two-port splitters when P_j is a power of two. Each rail carries an
identical copy of the N_j^cell-cell receiver, but each oscillator is replaced by
a ground-state qubit and each coupling by g_m(t)sigma_minus,m.

Local material dimension is exactly two, coupling norm <=g_max, and local
material Hamiltonian can be zero during capture. Bounded detunings are also
allowed. Coupling modulation remains excitation-conserving: vacuum and ground
are dark. Each cell sees only its local waveguide and later processor; graph
degree is bounded. The implementation retains actual transmission links and
retarded schedules. The large cross-Hamiltonian obtained by formally eliminating
a zero-delay cascade is not an implemented all-to-all material control.

3. COLLISION LEMMA FOR ARBITRARY QUANTUM FIELDS

Fix one port with m photons and P rails. The temporal state may be arbitrary
and entangled with all other systems. In first quantization the splitter puts
each photon in the same spatial vector u=(1,...,1)/sqrt(P). The symmetrized
wavefunction has spatial factor u^tensor m. Thus rail occupations are
multinomial, independently of temporal correlations. Each pair shares a rail
with probability 1/P. A union bound yields

  Pr[some rail has >=2 photons | m] <= m(m-1)/(2P).             (4)

The exact good probability is (P)_m/P^m when m<=P, and zero otherwise. This is
a statement about the coherent splitter isometry, not a distinguishable-photon
or classical-light approximation.

Let S include all splitters and vacuum ancillas. Let Pi_bad project onto the
union of input-field rail-collision sectors. It is a proof device, never a
measurement. Resolving by total photon number gives the operator inequality

  S^dagger Pi_bad S <= sum_j N_j(N_j-1)/(2P_j).

Consequently

  V^dagger S^dagger Pi_bad S V <= p_bad I_A,
  p_bad=min{1, sum_j F_j/(2P_j)}.                             (5)

The uniform operator statement is what makes the subsequent bound valid for
every unknown input and arbitrary reference.

4. EXACT SINGLE-EXCITATION EQUIVALENCE AND COMPLETE CHANNEL ERROR

If a rail has at most one incoming photon over its entire active source-time
window, passive excitation conservation keeps that rail's joint field and all
memories in vacuum plus one excitation. On the reachable subspace,

  a|0>=sigma_minus|0>=0,       a|1>=sigma_minus|1>=|0>.

The creation terms agree as well on the states actually reached. Therefore
oscillator and qubit waveguide interactions, time-dependent couplings, free
evolution, finite propagation, and freezing agree exactly as joint isometries.
Many photons may be present globally; only one per rail is required. Entanglement
between rails and the unknown reference is unrestricted.

Embed every qubit's |0>,|1> in oscillator occupations 0,1. The copied linear
network U_lin and qubit network U_q then satisfy

  (U_q-U_lin)Pi_good=0.

Both are contractions, so (5) gives

  ||(U_q-U_lin)SV|| <= 2sqrt(p_bad),
  ||C_q-C_lin||_diamond <= 4sqrt(p_bad).                       (6)

The compared channel may retain the source, receiver memories, and all output
fields. Hence the inequality survives any later processing or discarding.
It is unconditional: no success probability or conditional normalization occurs.
This exact subspace statement is stronger than a weak-excitation mean-field
approximation.

The proof describes the emitted field as an intermediate subsystem to simplify
notation. It does not posit a device that stores the whole field first. In the
chiral cascade the receiver never influences the source, and its isometry is
implemented chronologically on the arriving field, with the declared delays.

5. RECOVERING THE UNSPLIT PASSIVE RECEIVER

Identical passive rail networks commute with a change of spatial basis. In the
oscillator comparison model, inverse-split the P captured memory modes for each
fixed mode index. The populated original spatial mode is restored exactly and
the other P-1 modes are vacuum. An inverse spatial transformation can also be
made virtually on the field before tracing it; no physical access to discarded
output is needed. Thus the retained memory channel becomes precisely the
unsplit passive receiver B, up to known transport delay.

The actual qubit implementation performs this inverse transformation digitally
after capture. It does not apply a physical bosonic beam splitter directly to
two saturable qubits after photons have been concentrated. Section 8 gives the
finite occupation-register construction.

If B and its desired processing approximate a target with error eta_lin, the
compiled result has error at most

  eta_lin+4sqrt(p_bad)+eta_cut+eta_gate,                        (7)

where cutoff and bounded-gate errors are specified below. Source-dilation
comparison and hardware calibration errors are added separately by the channel
triangle inequality.

6. ACTUAL COHERENT DEPOLARIZING PORTS

For n q-level source sites, the actual nonidentity Weyl ports are

  L_{i,mu}=sqrt(r)/q W_{i,mu},           mu=1,...,q^2-1.        (8)

Every W is unitary. Arbitrary known time-dependent source Hamiltonians,
including inter-site interactions, are allowed. One physical port has counting
rate a=r/q^2. The one-site physical rate is c=r(1-q^(-2)); total physical rate
is R=nc. These must not be confused with the effective reset rate r.

For a fully specified marked photon history, its source Kraus operator is a
scalar amplitude times a product of unitary propagators and Weyl jumps.
Therefore K_history^dagger K_history is the scalar history probability times
identity. Counting histories are the state-independent marked Poisson process,
uniformly over source and reference inputs. In particular,

  V^dagger N_j(N_j-1)V=(rL/q^2)^2 I.

There are J=n(q^2-1) actual field ports. With P rails per port,

  p_bad <= n(q^2-1)r^2 L^2/(2q^4P) <= nr^2L^2/(2P).          (9)

Thus a sufficient rail count for dilution error eta_dilute is

  P >= 8 nr^2 L^2 / eta_dilute^2,                             (10)

rounded up to a power of two. Keeping the q-dependent factor improves the
constant. These bounds establish polynomial resources; they are not economic
estimates or optimized hardware counts.

L includes every source-time interval during which a capture coupling is
nonzero: startup, useful prefix, and trailing switch-off. Photons in a guard
still count even if their temporal modes are later discarded. A long physical
propagation delay does not enlarge L when all windows are properly retarded:
each cell selects the same source-time window and is off outside it.

7. ACQUIRED STATIC SPLITTER AND TRANSPORT COST

In the declared dispersionless chiral-field model, a static splitter is acquired
with two co-propagating fields of group velocity v and a finite interaction
region of length ell:

  H=-iv integral dx psi^dagger partial_x psi
    +integral_0^ell dx J(x)(psi_1^dagger psi_2+psi_2^dagger psi_1),
  |J(x)|<=J_0.                                               (11)

Along characteristics the field obeys a two-dimensional rotation:

  psi_out(t)=exp[-i theta sigma_x] psi_in(t-ell/v),
  theta=integral_0^ell J(x)dx/v.                              (12)

Theta=pi/4 gives an equal splitter with known phases. Constant J_0 permits
ell=pi v/(4J_0). The component is static, with fixed local coefficient and
length, and requires no switching whose rate grows with accuracy.

Equation (11) bounds a local one-particle interaction coefficient. It does not
bound the unrestricted Fock-space norm of a field Hamiltonian, which is already
unbounded for free fields. The strict finite material-system norm claim is
L=g sigma_minus. Continuum components must be specified by their scattering
coefficients, transport, and actual accessible field model.

A balanced tree has P-1 splitter cells and graph depth log_2P. Graph depth is
not automatically physical latency at fixed component density. A safe planar
layout uses level-k edges of length O(P/2^k), with total root-leaf path O(P),
total tree wire length O(P log P), and equalized leaf arrival times. Appending
P rails of N fixed-spacing cells gives additional delay O(N/v). This explicit
bounded-degree realization has polynomial footprint and wire cost, with total
transit O((P+N)/v), times fixed geometric length constants.

Every local envelope is shifted by its actual path delay. A cell that has
frozen waits while more distant cells finish and the digital decoder executes.
That material storage lifetime is counted; it is not set to zero.

8. FINITE OCCUPATION REGISTERS AND BOUNDED GATES

Each frozen physical memory is already a qubit. Appending ell_reg-1 zeros embeds
it exactly as binary occupation 0 or 1. No flying-field transducer, oscillator,
or strong Jaynes-Cummings swap is needed.

Choose a total emitted-photon cutoff M. In (8), total count is Poisson with
mean mu=RL. For any zeta in (0,1), take

  M=ceil[e mu+log(1/zeta)].                                   (13)

Markov's inequality for exp(N) gives Pr[N>M]<=exp[mu(e-1)-M]<=zeta.
This is a uniform input-operator bound. Passivity implies retained excitation
number cannot exceed emitted photon number.

For total occupancy <=M, use ell_reg=ceil(log_2(M+1)) bits per mode. A passive
two-mode unitary is a known direct sum of fixed-total-number SU(2) blocks. It
acts on O((M+1)^2) pair-register basis states. Define it as identity on the
unused pair-total-number >M blocks. A deliberately loose explicit compilation
uses QR into O((M+1)^4) two-level rotations; Gray-code controlled rotations then
use O(log^2(M+1)) bounded one-/two-qubit gates per two-level operation with
polynomial ancillas. Thus each abstract pair gate has polynomial cost

  O((M+1)^4 log^2(M+1))                                      (14)

before the chosen gate-precision overhead. Its matrix is computed from finite
SU(2) blocks with polynomial classical work. No full many-mode Fock matrix is
ever formed or diagonalized.

For J ports, P rails, and N capture modes per rail, inverse spatial splitting
requires JN(P-1) pair gates. Any N-mode unitary basis conversion requires
O(JN^2) more. Hence

  G_pair=O(JNP+JN^2).                                        (15)

Multiply (15) by (14) and the chosen rotation-synthesis overhead. Give each
elementary gate error at most eta_gate/G_total. A fixed universal bounded gate
family, or smooth bounded continuous rotations with explicitly paid angle
precision, suffices. Accuracy increases gate count, time, and parameter bits,
not maximum norm or slew.

Bounded-degree processor geometry can be enforced by nearest-neighbor SWAP
routing. With K_reg=O(JPN log(M+1)) register qubits, the crude bound O(K_reg)
extra swaps per arbitrary-pair gate is sufficient. Fixed-duration smooth
pulses with fixed Hamiltonian norm and slew then give a polynomial runtime.
This is intentionally conservative but fully finite.

The finite circuit need only agree with ideal processing on photon count <=M;
extend it arbitrarily outside. The same subspace-isometry argument as (6)
charges cutoff error

  eta_cut=4sqrt(zeta).                                       (16)

No count measurement or abort is required.

In the application's normalized zero-/one-physical-photon-per-site-per-bin
source comparison, final selected temporal modes have occupation 0 or 1. Their
low-order bits feed the explicit local Kraus-basis conversion and reverse-reset
decoder. That source-specific decoder is supplied in the companion construction.
This compiler does not assert efficient inversion of an arbitrary exponentially
specified field isometry.

9. INDEPENDENT INITIALIZATION AND FINITE STARTUP

Vacuum source INPUT ports do not imply vacuum emission before t=0. Each material
cell is independently initialized in its ground state with coupling zero for
retarded source time s<=0. Earlier emitted light crosses inactive cells without
correlating them with the source. Chirality, path matching, and retarded windows
prevent earlier light from arriving after activation.

A bounded-slew envelope may rise over a fixed tau_on by

  g(s)=sqrt(kappa_0)sin^2(pi s/(2tau_on)),  0<=s<=tau_on,       (17)

then remain on a plateau and fall with a fixed-duration cosine-squared ramp.
It is exactly zero afterward. Amplitude and slew are fixed. Each schedule is
retarded by its actual cell's path delay. Freezing therefore requires no
accuracy-dependent strong transfer pulse.

In the passive-mode approximation, the source-time prefix [0,delta_0] may be
dropped analytically at channel error O(sqrt(R delta_0)); its photon probability
is uniformly <=R delta_0. No physical switch occurs at delta_0. Since
sin(pi x/2)>=x for 0<=x<=1, the retained prefix obeys

  kappa_min>=kappa_0(delta_0/tau_on)^4.                        (18)

Inverse-polynomial delta_0 therefore gives inverse-polynomial kappa_min. The
companion warped-Laguerre approximation proves polynomial mode count with this
startup. It does not assume an instantaneous leading-edge absorber. All photons
in the nonzero trailing ramp remain included in L for (9), regardless of how
their modes are used later.

10. POLYNOMIAL CALIBRATION PRECISION

If two one-particle isometries differ by delta, their m-particle second
quantizations differ by at most m delta: telescope the tensor powers and
restrict to the symmetric subspace. Thus on photon count <=M, full-channel
error is at most 2M delta. A network of B passive components, each with
one-particle error <=nu, has error <=2MBnu. Inverse-polynomial component
precision suffices for a prescribed eta_cal.

For example, a real coupling g on [0,L] has stored-amplitude kernel

  k_g(s)=-g(s)exp[-(1/2)integral_s^L g(u)^2 du],

and output-field Volterra kernel

  K_g(t,s)=-g(t)g(s)exp[-(1/2)integral_s^t g(u)^2du], s<=t.

If |g|,|h|<=g_max and sup|g-h|<=delta_g, elementary exponential and
Hilbert-Schmidt estimates yield

  ||k_g-k_h||_2 <= sqrt(L)(1+g_max^2L)delta_g,
  ||K_g-K_h||_op <= (2g_max L+g_max^3L^2)delta_g.               (19)

This is a polynomial stability bound for one cell. Delay calibration also
needs the derivative/bandwidth control of the particular finite temporal-mode
subspace in use; arbitrary high-frequency states admit no uniform small-delay
bound. That cost belongs to the source-mode approximation.

An imperfect known splitter with rail probabilities p_a has pair collision
probability sum_a p_a^2. If max p_a<=C/P, equations (4)-(10) change only by factor
C. Known phases and weights are included in the inverse spatial transform;
remaining imperfections can be charged by the one-particle norm bound above.
No exponentially precise matrix of the entire many-body Hilbert space is
required. These statements pay calibration precision, not fixed stochastic
noise or memory decoherence.

11. RESOURCE SUMMARY AND STRONGEST ORDINARY COMPARISON

For J ports, N modes per rail, P rails, active window L, and cutoff M:

  Capture qubits:       JPN.
  Static splitters:     J(P-1).
  Capture local dim.:   exactly 2.
  Capture ||L||:        <=g_max, independent of n and accuracy.
  Control slew:         the chosen fixed ramp bound.
  Expanded registers:   O(JPN log(M+1)) qubits plus polynomial ancillas.
  Passive pair gates:   O(JNP+JN^2).
  Elementary gates:     (14) times (15), accuracy and routing overhead paid.
  Transport:            actual finite delays; a conservative planar layout
                        has O(P+N) longest path and polynomial total wire.
  Time and precision:   polynomial at fixed local strength/slew.

In the positive application J=n(q^2-1), fixed q,r, and bounded local Hamiltonian
coefficients. The companion mode approximation gives polynomial N and the
reverse-reset decoder is polynomial when r exceeds the proved local
operator-growth coefficient. This compiler preserves polynomial total cost.
It does not claim a small exponent or practical device economics.

The strongest same-access ordinary comparator is a linear passive memory
network at fixed linewidth followed by standard quantum processing. It is more
economical in count: JN oscillator modes rather than JPN qubits. This compiler's
purpose is to earn the stronger strict finite-dimensional material-coupling
claim, explicitly paying the hardware factor. It does not improve oscillator
engineering by assertion.

For a known single-photon envelope, a conventional tailored impedance-matched
absorber may be superior. Its initial condition, pulse-dependent peak control,
and wavepacket shape must be supplied. Direct source SWAPs, source pulse
shaping, initial encoding, and pre-established source-receiver entanglement are
extra interfaces, not same-access comparisons when only already-emitted fields
are granted. If those operations are allowed, ordinary transfer may be simpler.

12. PRECISE BANDWIDTH AND NOISE SCOPE

Fixed local material parameters: two-level dimension, maximum ||L||, resonance
linewidth, slew, and component geometry are fixed. More rails, cells, paths,
and stored modes are added. Aggregate collective spectral/temporal response
can therefore improve with hardware. A fixed hard aggregate optical passband
is not claimed.

A compactly supported nonzero envelope cannot also be strictly bandlimited.
The finite ramps above have bounded amplitude and slew, not compact Fourier
support. If bounded bandwidth means a literal hard Fourier cutoff on controls,
this construction does not establish that different requirement. A fixed-pole
controller with exponential tails would require a separately charged tail
error, not a relabeling of the compact ramp.

The local scattering and coupling operations are acquired within the declared
dispersionless Markov-port model. Real dispersion, propagation loss, scattering
into other modes, control noise, and finite reservoir correlation time must be
bounded and added as interface errors. They cannot be inferred from a reduced
Lindblad generator alone.

The rail encoding is vacuum plus distributed single excitation; it does not
correct photon loss. Material T1 during loading/transport is also unprotected
by this proof. Fixed-noise fault tolerance has not been established. The
companion loss note proves a nonzero one-site fidelity floor, and exponential
many-site Bell-fidelity ceiling, for fixed loss in the actual H=0 Weyl-source
subclass even with arbitrary later decoding.

The earned consequence is precise: under actual ideal coherent-port access,
fixed local finite-dimensional receiver strength does not force a nonzero
acquisition-error floor. Polynomially many causal weak cells can replace
accuracy-dependent strong capture. Permanent inaccessible ingress modes and
fixed first loss are separate resources that this compiler does not remove.
