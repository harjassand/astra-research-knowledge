Consider 2D Navier-Stokes with arbitrary time-dependent force f(t) in E, with no bound on its amplitude or switching, and an L2 spatial disturbance h. Write omega=curl u and
 Q(u)=||omega||2^2-lambda1||u||2^2
     =sum_(k!=0)(lambda_k-lambda1)|u_hat(k)|^2,
 D(u)=sum_(k!=0)lambda_k(lambda_k-lambda1)|u_hat(k)|^2.
For smooth solutions the exact energy and enstrophy identities cancel both the Euler quadratic term and the forcing f in this difference, since -Delta f=lambda1 f. Thus
 (1/2)Q'(t)+nu D(u)=<(-Delta-lambda1)u,h>.
By spectral Cauchy-Schwarz,
 |<(-Delta-lambda1)u,h>|<=sqrt(D(u))*||h||2.
Consequently Q'+nu D<=||h||2^2/nu. The next square-torus eigenvalue is 2*lambda1, so D>=2*lambda1*Q. If ||h||LinfL2<=eta,
