All product water flows from the anode side through the bridge toward the cathode. Forward water motion is intended to oppose peroxide diffusion back toward the anode, where peroxide would be oxidized or decomposed. Separate drainage paths remove liquid while preserving air access to the cathode.

The additional engineering proposal is to divide each cell into small hydraulic tiles, each fed through a dominant inlet restriction. This makes tile flow less sensitive to small differences in the porous bed. A carbon cathode leaf can then be replaced independently of the expensive anode assembly.

Generic through-flow, solid-electrolyte peroxide collection and separate gas/liquid paths are prior art. Faita/De Nora disclosed closely related water-perfused microporous-ionomer arrangements.
