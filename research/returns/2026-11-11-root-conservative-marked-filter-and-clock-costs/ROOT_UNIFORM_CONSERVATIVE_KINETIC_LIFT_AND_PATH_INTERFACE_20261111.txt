Purpose. The completed predictable-fault compiler concerns fixed reaction
structures and conditional-Poisson laws. This note investigates a DIFFERENT
output: full timed labelled trajectories of a variable conservative reaction
network, lowered to at most two molecules on each side. The intended polynomial

For each distinct complex c of order>=2, introduce its private ordered binary

Replace an original label r by rep(y_r)->rep(z_r), retaining the label even
for a null or parallel channel. The decoder P is identity on original species,

2. A FAST FIBRE WITH POLYNOMIAL MIXING IN NETWORK SIZE AND CAPACITY

For the fast assembly system alone set first assembly/reverse coefficients
to K theta,K and later coefficients to K,K,0<theta<=1. Temporarily disable
central conversions. The decoded x is fixed. Let M<=2m be the number of
complexes with order>=2. Tag prefix copies analytically, not physically.
A token at depth j changes depth by one; release has rate K per token and
attachment has rate at most KB per token. Last release at depth2 kills it.
Even with instantaneous multipliers in[1/2,3/2], the probability of its next
move being a release within time1/K is at least1/[8U]. At most D-1 such
conditional events kill a token. Therefore its conditional lifetime has mean
 <=C_D(B)/K, C_D(B)=D[8U]^(D-1),                        (1)
and a geometric-block tail. This bound is independent of d,m and the number
of fibre states. Other tokens can change free counts but cannot exceed B,
so no independence of their service times is assumed.

Fresh first-assembly tokens arrive with total intensity<=2K theta M U^2.
There are initially at most B tokens. Let
 h=16B C_D(B)/K,
 theta<=1/[1024(1+M)U^3 C_D(B)].                       (2)
The union/Markov bound makes the chance that an initial token survives h at
most1/16. The probability of any fresh token in[0,h] is at most1/32.
Consequently, from ANY state in the fibre, the fast process is canonical at
time h with probability at least1/2. These are conditional service bounds;
they do not presume an arbitrary adaptive process is Markov or has an invariant
law. For the NOMINAL homogeneous fast kernel, this is an actual common-atom
minorization. Its Dobrushin coefficient satisfies
 alpha_x(t)<=2^(-floor(t/h)), integral_0^infinity alpha_x(t)dt<=2h. (3)
This proves a uniform polynomial fast-fibre mixing interface, without the

6. WHY A FREE CLOCK WOULD BE FALSE

Take the fixed conservative original network with one genuine null label
 D A->D A, k=1, x0=N A and tau=1. Its target marked count is Poisson with
mean lambda_N=(N)_D. In ANY allowed elementary realization with m_f channels,
all rates<=1 and positive decoder columns, population<=N. The expected total
fine count to physical time H is at most m_f(N+1)^2 H. To approximate the
target marked law within TV1/8, Chebyshev applied to its count>=lambda_N/2
and then Markov applied to the fine count force, for sufficiently large N,
 H>=3 N^(D-2)/(2^(D+5)m_f).                            (8)
The network description is fixed before N. Thus a population-independent
clock bound is impossible for D>=3 under these conventions. This is a lower
bound against all such elementary realizations, NOT against polynomial
slowdown. Erasing the original null label would wrongly conceal this cost.
