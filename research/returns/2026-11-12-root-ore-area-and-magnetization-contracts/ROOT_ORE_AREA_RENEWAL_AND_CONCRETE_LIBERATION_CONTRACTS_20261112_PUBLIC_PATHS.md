A genuinely different useful separation operation
Demolition concrete contains valuable original aggregate and already-processed binder. Their useful recovery requires distinguishing original paste from limestone filler/aggregate and preserving two material streams. A fine calcium-rich fraction is not automatically original cement paste or suitable kiln feed.
The already-read initial main results of Dunant/Joseph/Prajapati/Allwood2024, DOI10.1038/s41586-024-07338-8, show adjusted recovered-paste slags formed over molten steel and subsequent mortar behavior. Their specific recovered paste contained substantial aggregate-derived impurities. This supplies a downstream experimental construction, not a free furnace, complete cost or scalable clean-paste separation.
