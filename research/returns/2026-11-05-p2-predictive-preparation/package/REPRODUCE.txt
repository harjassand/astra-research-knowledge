SCIENTIFIC SCOPE
  Analytic theorems assume the declared physical LTI, storage, energy, clock,
  metrology and delivery contracts. They do not infer the model class.
  The source/sensor simulation is floating point, with no interval validation.
  Reproducible PRNGs emulate private physical clocks; they do not certify them.
  Finite rational verifiers prove emitted numerical identities and parameter
  implications. Source plots and sampled agreement are diagnostics.
  The LP execution is counterfactual planning on a saved readiness record.
  A new physical deployment must pay fresh preparation and its energy budget.
