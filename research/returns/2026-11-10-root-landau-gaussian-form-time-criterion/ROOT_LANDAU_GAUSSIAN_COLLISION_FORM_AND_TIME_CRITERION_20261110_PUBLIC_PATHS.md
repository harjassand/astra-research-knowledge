Gaussian heat-collision benchmark and the precise time-growth interface

1. Available actual-evolution control.
For the homogeneous finite-mass Coulomb system in the current full question, the return proves with all physical n_i,m_i,c_ij retained:
 int_0^T E_(7/8)(f(t))^(8/7)dt<=C_T,
on every finite maximal classical interval, from total entropy dissipation, positive self-collision coefficients and propagated21st moments. It also proves
 E_s^(m)(h)<=C_s m^-s N_(3s/(1-s))(h)^(1-s)(W(h)+n)^s,
 W(h)=int<v>^-3|grad sqrt(h)|^2.
The unconditional moment mechanism and weighted Fisher coercivity are imported foundations, not new global regularity. The dyadic heat-entropy interpolation and its exact physical constants are additional scoped content.

The return's fractional Sobolev consequence lies on2/r+3/q=3 with r=1/s,q=3/(3-2s). The direct reaction-energy closure requires2/r+3/q=2. At s7/8 the available time exponent8/7 is less than the required8/3. No interpolation within this same family removes that deficit. Finite time integrability of E alone permits terminal spikes and is not a continuation theorem.

2. A complete scalar criterion, with the coefficient's time norm paid.
Let y>=1 be locally absolutely continuous on[0,T), int_0^T y^p<infinity, and
 y'<=a(t)y^q,
with q>1, a>=0 and a in L^r(0,T),1<r<=infinity. If q-1<=p(1-1/r), y cannot blow up at T.
Proof: in a hypothetical terminal blowup, integrate (y^(-(q-1)))'>=-(q-1)a from t to T. Then
 y(t)^p>=[(q-1)int_t^T a]^(-p/(q-1))
 >=[(q-1)||a||r]^(-p/(q-1))(T-t)^[-p(1-1/r)/(q-1)].
The last function is not integrable under the stated exponent condition, contradiction. The argument at r=infinity uses its usual convention. Oscillations before the terminal limit are handled by applying this bound to an unbounded sequence of later levels and passing to the endpoint. At r=1, the linear bound y'<=a y gives Gronwall directly; no superlinear exponent is licensed solely by a in L1.

For p8/7 a bounded a permits q<=15/7; an L2 coefficient permits only q<=11/7. Thus replacing a bounded coefficient by entropy dissipation D(t), known only in L1, changes the sufficient contract. A bound E_s'<=C(1+D)(1+E_s) would suffice, but is NOT constructed here. So would other actual regularity mechanisms. These are conditional mathematical interfaces, not proposed accepted collision estimates.

3. Fully integrated Gaussian benchmark.
Take finite positive masses and n_i and a common temperature Theta>0 but distinct drifts u_i:
 f_i(v)=n_i[m_i/(2pi Theta)]^(3/2) exp[-m_i|v-u_i|^2/(2Theta)].
For mass-weighted heat P_tau^i, temperature becomes Theta+2tau while n_i and u_i remain fixed. Hence, with N=n_1+n_2,
 E_s(f)=(3N/2)s(1-s)int_0^infinity log(1+2tau/Theta)tau^(-1-s)dtau
       =(3N/2)(1-s)(2/Theta)^s*pi/sin(pi s),0<s<1.
The integration by parts has vanishing endpoints because tau^(1-s) at0 and log(tau)tau^-s at infinity vanish; int_0^infinity x^-s/(1+x)dx=pi/sin(pi s).

For each ordered pair and rotation score q_k, the return's physical Gaussian calculation gives
 B_(ij,k)(tau)=[2tau/(Theta+2tau)] int alpha F q_k^2,
 alpha=|v-w|^-3,F=f_i(v)f_j(w).
Define the integrated collision form by the convergent expression
 C_s(f)= -s(1-s)/2 sum_(i,j) c_ij sum_k
             int_0^infinity B_(ij,k)(tau)tau^(-1-s)dtau.
Using D(f)=(1/2)sum_(i,j)c_ij sum_k int alpha Fq_k^2, direct integration gives
 C_s(f)=-s(1-s)(2/Theta)^s*pi/sin(pi s)*D(f)
       =-[2s/(3N)] E_s(f)D(f).
Near tau0 the integrand is O(tau^-s); near infinity it is O(tau^-1-s), so this integrated form is finite for every0<s<1. This is an instantaneous identity at these profiles, not a claim that the nonlinear collision flow remains Gaussian or that a full-range derivative identity has already been justified for arbitrary data.

The same data have negative posterior covariance, even after summing rotations, on an open set of observation centers. Their integrated form nevertheless has the favorable sign. Taking an absolute value before integrating over centers loses the exact cancellation. Conversely this Gaussian calculation proves no favorable sign for general product densities.

4. Distinct nonlocal functionals and domains.
For a probability density h, convexity gives at each tau
 H(h)-H(P_tau h)<=int(h-P_tau h)log h.
The heat-entropy E_s therefore differs from the entropy dissipation i_s of the fractional heat generator. A lifting theorem for i_s does not identify these functionals or supply E_s collision monotonicity. For example h(v)=C exp(-|v|^4) is a strictly positive Schwartz density with finite ordinary Fisher and finite E_s for0<s<1, but its fractional-jump Fisher i_s is infinite: restrict one integration variable to a fixed ball of positive h-mass and let the other variable tend to infinity. The symmetrized integrand dominates C|y|^4/|y|^(3+2s), whose radial integral int r^(3-2s)dr diverges. This is a domain obstruction to substituting that functional for ALL initial data, not a counterexample to a suitably modified functional or the Cauchy question.
