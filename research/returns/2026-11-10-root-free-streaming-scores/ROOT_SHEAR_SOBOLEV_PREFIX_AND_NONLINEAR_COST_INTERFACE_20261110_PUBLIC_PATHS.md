Sobolev prefix stability for physical shear loops, and the nonlinear cost interface

Mathematical premises. On the unit three-torus let H^m_sigma be the real mean-zero divergence-free Sobolev space. Let L_F v=P[(F.grad)v+(v.grad)F]. Take the fixed rational rotated shear family and fixed finite macro-word W(s) from the supplied rotated-shear construction. Its underlying vector-pushforward transport is exactly the identity. Its expansion is W(s)=I+s^4 D+O_(L2->L2)(s^5), where D is Fourier-diagonal and Sym D(k)<=0 on k-perp. All spatial fields, rotation denominators, word multiplicities and the finite word description are computably fixed. These are mathematical premises; the individual companion verifier is not used here.

Sobolev extension. For EACH fixed nonnegative integer m there are computable fixed b_m,c_m>0 and s_m>0 such that
  ||W(s)-exp(s^4 D)||_(H^m->H^m)<=c_m |s|^5,  |s|<=s_m,
and every partial physical macro-word, including its inverse, has H^m operator norm <=exp(b_m |s|). Consequently, for a path consisting of n complete copies, EVERY forward subinterval propagator has norm
  ||S(t,r)||_(H^m->H^m)<=exp(2 b_m |s|+c_m n |s|^5),  0<=r<=t<=1.
Here prefixes/suffixes are parts of the actual physical word, not Fourier truncations. In particular, choosing the exponent <=log 2 gives ||v(t)||H1<=2||v(0)||H1 throughout the whole repeated path. This replaces an exponential in the total absolute strain for the inviscid reference v. It does not yet replace that exponential in nonlinear all-weak stability.

Proof of the extension. Decompose L_F=C_F+B_F with C_F v=[F,v] and B_F v=2P[(v.grad)F]. On each fixed H^m, B_F is bounded, since multiplication by the fixed smooth grad F and P are bounded. The vector-pushforward group of C_F and its inverse have norm <=exp(b_(F,m)|t|), by differentiating the integer Sobolev energy, using div F=div v=0, and the finite Leibniz estimates with derivatives of F through m+1. Multiplying over the fixed word gives the stated prefix estimate, after enlarging b_m.

For the endpoint expansion, use the interaction picture with these pushforwards. A finite composition g(s) of the explicit shears is smooth in s and tends to the identity with all the finitely many required spatial derivatives. Conjugating P gives the divergence-form elliptic inverse with a_g=Dg^-1 Dg^-T. Its H^m bounds and first five parameter derivatives are computable near s=0, as follows. Work on scalar mean-zero periodic functions. Write A_g=div(a_g grad)=Delta+div((a_g-I)grad). Choose a rational s_m small enough that
  ||Delta^-1 div((a_g-I)grad)||_(H^(m+1)->H^(m+1))<=1/2
for every prefix. Finite Leibniz bounds reduce this to explicit bounds on finitely many derivatives of a_g-I; the explicit finite shear compositions provide those bounds and an effective choice of s_m. A Neumann series then bounds A_g^-1:H^(m-1)->H^(m+1). For m=0 use the coercive H^-1->H^1 bound. Derivatives R_j of R=A_g^-1 satisfy
  R_j=-R sum_(r=1,...,j) binom(j,r) (partial_s^r A_g) R_(j-r).
For j<=5 this is a finite recursion of bounded maps between the displayed Sobolev spaces. Any constant-mode correction is finite-rank and smooth. The physical mean-zero inputs are preserved by the shear flows.

Thus the conjugated B_F is C^5 in bounded H^m operators. Its finite interaction equations have five norm derivatives bounded by finite integral/Gronwall recursions. The underlying transport of each fourth commutator is EXACTLY identity: for F=e_i f(x_j), G=e_j g(x_l), i,j,l distinct, the second commutator is an e_i shear depending on x_j,x_l; the next remains in that abelian shear subgroup and commutes with the final F. Rotations, finite concatenation and inversion preserve this endpoint identity. Therefore the endpoint interaction is the endpoint physical operator and is C^5 in H^m operator norm. On smooth trigonometric inputs its derivatives through degree four agree with the given formal word: zero at degrees one through three and coefficient D at degree four. Density identifies the bounded derivatives. Taylor's integral remainder supplies a computable c_m, after including the O(s^8) difference between I+s^4D and exp(s^4D).

Because D is Fourier-diagonal, the H^m weight is a scalar on each polarization block. Its negative symmetric part therefore makes exp(tD) a contraction in H^m, as well as L2. Hence ||W(s)^j||Hm<=exp(c_m j|s|^5). A forward subinterval of the repeated path is a final partial block, complete copies, and an inverse initial prefix. Applying the three bounds proves the claim. No inverse of the entire contracting repeated word is used.

What remains for physical polynomial costs. For a fixed macro-word with one shear active at each instant and signed impulses proportional to s, the total absolute strain is EXACTLY
  K=n |s| K0=K0 T/|s|^3,  n=T/s^4,
where K0>0 is the fixed sum of absolute single-pulse gradient costs. Rescaling time does not reduce K. The accepted all-weak comparison still contains exp(K), even after its inviscid reference H1 bound is replaced by 2M. Making its displayed error small by choosing the fast physical duration delta thus gives exponentially small sufficient delta and exponentially large sufficient force. This is a limitation of that sufficient estimate, not a lower bound on all possible controllers.

A possible cancellation interface must be treated carefully. On a partial block let S be the inviscid propagator from its beginning and set Q=S^-* S^-1. Q is uniformly coercive and close to I for small s. Inside that block its time derivative cancels the inviscid linear generator exactly. At a block boundary the near-contractive endpoint controls the metric reset. This is a legitimate linear mechanism and does not require inverting the full path. It is NOT by itself an all-weak nonlinear energy estimate. The extra nonlinear term contains <(Q-I)e,P div(e tensor e)>. The generic order-zero estimate in three dimensions is only
  |<(Q-I)e,B(e,e)>| <= C s ||e||2^(1/2) ||grad e||2^(5/2),
using H1->L6, interpolation to L4, and an H1 bound for Q-I. This is not absorbable by the Leray energy dissipation uniformly over arbitrary energy-class e. A special cancellation, a sufficiently smoothing correction, a strong-solution/parabolic argument valid for the full input class, or another mechanism is still required. The failure of this generic estimate proves no impossibility for the actual Q or for polynomial-cost control.

Separate representation check for kinetic alternatives. For free streaming f_t+v.grad_x f=0, put X=grad_x log f and V=grad_v log f. Along characteristics, X'=0 and V'=-X. Hence I_v'=-2 integral f V.X, (integral f X.V)'=-I_x, and I_x'=0. A time-independent positive-definite quadratic form in (X,V) cannot be non-increasing for every free-streaming solution: V(t)=V(0)-tX(0) makes it unbounded for nonzero X. This excludes a constant coercive mixed-score transport correction as an automatic extension of homogeneous Fisher monotonicity. It excludes neither a time-dependent hypocoercive functional nor the full inhomogeneous kinetic problem.
