Can a renewable molecular manufacturing system make useful, sequence-defined abiotic oligomers with a genuinely synthetic backbone, by coupling each intended product bond to the physical handover of the growing product and the next usable reactive state?

The useful operation is construction of chemically diverse functional matter from a common stock of building blocks, with molecular control of order, failed attempts and renewal. The final useful product must have a non-nucleic backbone: a protein, a translated biological product, DNA/RNA, or a merely base- or sugar-modified nucleic-acid analogue does not meet this new endpoint. Choose an actual benign synthetic backbone and at least four distinguishable building blocks with at least two useful chemical functionalities. Peptoids, oligoureas, oligoesters or another family are possible starting points, not required choices. DNA, enzymes, supports or other components may be tools if their chemistry, preparation, compatibility, life and replacement costs are real. A new name for natural polymerase extension or conventional solid-phase synthesis would not establish the capability.

A concrete opening is to give the growing covalent product physical ownership of progression. In ordinary polymerase extension the new residue carries the next hydroxyl, so there is no separate reusable key that can escape and activate a neighbour. That is established chemistry, but its substrate range and base-pairing specificity do not authenticate every remote substituent or supply a general synthetic backbone. Investigate whether an actual transfer chemistry, moving local register, reaction-driven assembly or a different organisation can retain the useful ownership while enabling the chosen material. You may replace the framing if another physical mechanism gives a stronger complete construction.

Use this first full production challenge. Sixteen specified programs, each with at least twenty ordered covalent growth operations, run together in one shared reaction environment. Include adjacent repeats, alternating regions, long runs and at least one pair with identical composition but different order. Reusable local compartments or supports and global timed feeds are allowed, with their fabrication, occupation and transport paid. Separately addressed reaction sequences, independently chosen monomer feeds or off-stage selective coupling for every product are not free molecular operations. If introduced, build and cost them and compare with a competent parallel synthesis platform.

Starting from prepared but fully costed stock, seek at least1nmol independently authenticated correct released product per program within24h, at least90% correct full sequences in each accepted population before sequence-selective salvage, and at least50% initiator-to-correct-released-product molar yield for every program. Include all handling and analytical time in the useful turnaround. Separate modeled populations, measured signals, isolated correct product and the actual accepted output. An average dominated by easy programs or a full-length band does not meet the per-program specification.

The central missing operation is the physical transition from an actual intended bond to the next construction state. Draw and analyse the actual covalent topology before and after the event, the old and new attachments, the next reactive end, consumed leaving groups and the location of the product. A controller may not observe an assigned successful-reaction flag. If the product transfers from one carrier to another, show why hydrolysis, loss of an old carrier or an unrelated cleavage cannot impersonate that transfer. If it remains attached, construct the next local selection without a mobile catalytic signal or a freely supplied positional oracle. Another organisation is welcome if it solves the same operation more effectively.

One unconstructed acyl-transfer topology is:
old-carrier--leaving-group--acyl(prefix) + amine--new-unit--new-carrier
 -> old-carrier--leaving-group + prefix--amide--new-unit--new-carrier.
Here actual new bond formation moves the prefix onto the incoming unit's carrier. This is only a topology, not a supplied monomer, catalyst, reaction window or self-advancing register. The new unit must carry a usable next group without uncontrolled cyclisation or bulk polymerisation; productive docking must beat hydrolysis and reactions with free or wrong carriers; spent carriers must not re-enter; and all activation, protection, recharge and clearing remain chemical operations. Acyl transfer itself is old chemistry. You may ignore it and choose a different family.

Locality and order are indispensable. Construct the next-position register when the same building block occurs at many positions and programs share feeds. A long tether reaching every instruction may also reach the wrong instruction. A product can move onto a chemically correct monomer at the wrong position. A record of intended carrier order is not the product's covalent sequence. Specify how skipped positions, exchange, misregistered transfer, branching, end-to-end coupling, premature termination, terminal extra units and permanently stalled sites arise or are physically excluded. No unbounded fault-detection requirement is assumed; define the actual finite chemical and contamination envelope and the cases that remain observationally indistinguishable.

Recoverable noncoupling should leave a physically specified state available for retry. Hydrolysis may instead destroy a reactive stock or release an unfinished chain; a wrong but stable bond can continue and remain hidden. Include those branches explicitly. Capping and rejection are allowed and sometimes useful, but discarding a failed chain is distinct from restoring its original end. If purity is obtained by salvage, retain the pre-salvage denominator, losses and extra preparation in the comparison.

An introduced finite-window model illustrates a possible operating region without supplying chemistry. At each of n positions let correct handover, wrong continuing handover and absorbing loss have rates a,w,h. With lambda=a+w+h and the Erlang completion CDF G_n(lambda*W), correct completion is (a/lambda)^n*G_n, all completion is ((a+w)/lambda)^n*G_n, and correctness among completed chains is (a/(a+w))^n. At n20,a.1/s,w.0003/s,h.0002/s,W600s and hypothetical95% release,2nmol initiator gives1.71962nmol correct released and94.1849% correctness. Raising w to.003/s leaves1.01195nmol correct but only55.3676% correctness. Raising h to.005/s instead leaves94.1849% correctness but only.67637nmol correct released. All constants, release and local selection are introduced. The positive window is not a measured material, a detector or a whole factory. Identify which actual physical operation could deliver the required ratios, or challenge this model.

Also distinguish backbone/register fidelity from chemical functionality. A correct-base or correct-carrier reagent with a damaged side group may still form the backbone. In a declared constant-pool competition, wrong reagent fraction q and relative acceptance rho give f=rho*q/(1-q+rho*q), so twenty correct additions have probability (1-f)^20. At q.001 and rho100 this is.14837. Real depletion, stock purification, side-group-specific chemistry, rejection and replenishment may change the result. Do not assign the same purity to stock, incorporation, surviving full chains and accepted output. Investigate actual attainable selectivity rather than requiring an impossible detector of every hypothetical impurity.

Sequence authentication belongs to the physical operation. Resolve incomplete products, isomers, wrong linkages, wrong order, chemically damaged groups and mixed-program contamination at actual minority sensitivity. Equal-composition products can share parent masses; pooled fragment-composition ladders can be consistent with mixtures that contain little or none of an intended full sequence. This does not prohibit mass spectrometry, chromatography, standards or other methods. Construct the actual analytical discrimination, including fragmentation response, separation, calibration, recovery, samples and time. Copying a program into ordinary DNA cannot by itself authenticate all abiotic chemistry. Production control and destructive post-run certification are different resources.

Product release must leave the construction machinery ready in a specified chemical state. Design ten consecutive complete production cycles on the same platform, retargeted across at least four sixteen-product libraries. The last cycle should retain at least90% of the first cycle's accepted output rate and fidelity. At least95% of each critical stock claimed reusable should remain functional after the ten-cycle campaign, or its replenishment must be constructed and charged. Identify program-bearing stock, carrier, catalyst, support, recognition part and every consumed activator, monomer, adaptor or protecting group. Freshly synthesized replacement machinery is allowed as paid stock, not a free reset.

Do not assume repeated washing or cleavage restores an inaccessible covalent state. If fraction z is persistently inaccessible while the remainder is cleared with probability p per pass, residual fraction after k passes is z+(1-z)*(1-p)^k. For introduced p.95,32nmol,k3, z0 gives.004nmol, while z.005 gives.16398nmol; six passes still leave.1600005nmol in the latter case. Actual refolding, access-changing or physical removal may alter that population, but nominal extra dose is not automatically a new independent chance. Include old-product carryover, the fate of retained chains and finite ready-state observations. Distinguish loss per event from loss per production cycle. Examine100cycles and100growth operations without assuming the short case transfers.

Connect the resulting library to one useful non-biological materials operation. It could be selective non-biological recognition, charge transport, an optical response, controlled assembly, wetting, catalysis for a benign materials process or another useful physical function. Choose the function and actual operating conditions. At least one same-composition pair should have a predicted or source-supported independently measurable response difference of at least twofold. Explain why sequence control supplies useful value against a competent ordinary material; merely adding chemical diversity or producing a different analytical spectrum is insufficient. Include any derivatization, folding, complementary support, purification or interface preparation needed for that function.

Compare the whole operation with the strongest relevant existing construction: automated solid-phase or flow synthesis, iterative or convergent assembly, template-directed chemistry, parallel enzymatic synthesis where it can make the chosen product, and other competent material preparation. Give the alternatives automation, capping, purification, standards and equal access to ordinary primitives. A single robot can already run many wells. Cheap monomers, faster common assays and an existing reaction benefit competitors too unless a new physical organisation gives an exclusive useful advantage. Conversely, an incumbent's ability to adopt a proposed new primitive is not proof that the complete primitive already exists.
