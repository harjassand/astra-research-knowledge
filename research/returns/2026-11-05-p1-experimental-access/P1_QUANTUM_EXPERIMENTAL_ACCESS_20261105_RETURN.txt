## 2. The exact access model

The source consists of \(n\) initially unencoded qubits. From the moment the unknown input is present, it experiences actual inaccessible noise

\[

With this convention, a component transverse to \(Z_i\) decays at rate \(2\lambda\).

The apparatus includes every accessible memory, output, controller, measurement outcome, and feedback record. Its resources may be arbitrarily large and entangled among themselves, but initially independent of the unknown source.

The joint evolution is

\[
\mathcal L_t
=
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{hid}}
+\mathcal G_t
+\sum_a\mathcal K_a(t),
\qquad
\mathcal K_a(t)(\rho)=-i[H_a(t),\rho].
\]

The assumptions are:

- **Free operations preserve source parity sectors.** The background evolution \(\mathcal G_t\) commutes with conjugation by every source \(Z_i\). This applies to the full retained operation, including its accessible records.
- **Paid interactions have bounded source order.** Each \(H_a\) can fail to commute with \(Z_i\) only on a set \(B_a\), with \(|B_a|\le k\).
- **Strength is charged per source site:**
  \[
  h_a(t)=2\|H_a(t)\|,

  \sum_{a:i\in B_a}h_a(t)\le\Omega.

- The source is eventually discarded. There is no final free noncommuting measurement of it.

The receiver part of an interaction may act on an arbitrarily large protected register. Parallel operations are allowed. Extensive diagonal dependence on other source \(Z_i\) is also allowed. Thus \(k\) counts the source sites on which an interaction can change the relevant parity, rather than imposing a geometrical distance restriction.

**The summed term budget is an additional assumption.** A bound on the total commutator \(\|[H,Z_i]\|\) does not automatically imply it: terms can cancel. The new theorem does not silently import locality from the predecessor’s commutator law.

### Why the proof survives arbitrary receiver processing

The proof does not assume an initial noise layer before control. Control and noise act simultaneously from time zero.

Fix a final time \(T\). For each nonempty unresolved set \(R\), remove all Hamiltonian terms touching \(R\), and call the remaining propagator \(\mathcal V_R\). It is still completely positive and trace preserving. Its action on the selected sector factors exactly:

\[
\mathcal V_R(u,t)\mathcal P_R
=
e^{-2\lambda |R|(u-t)}
\widetilde{\mathcal V}_R(u,t)\mathcal P_R,
\]

where \(\widetilde{\mathcal V}_R\) is also a quantum channel.

If no interaction ever touches \(R\), tracing out the source annihilates this sector. A Duhamel identity at the first interaction touching \(R\) therefore gives a recursion. Writing \(F_R(t)\) for the diamond norm of the future acquired map restricted to this sector,

\[
F_R(t)
\le
\sum_{a:B_a\cap R\ne\varnothing}
\int_t^T
h_a(u)e^{-2\lambda |R|(u-t)}
F_{R\setminus B_a}(u)\,du.
\]

Every set on the right is smaller. Dynamics on sites already resolved remain inside full quantum channels, whose diamond norm is one.

Let \(j_a=|B_a\cap R|\). The strength budget gives

\[
\sum_a j_a h_a(u)\le\Omega |R|.
\]

The definition of \(x_k\) implies

\[
\frac{x_k^{-j}}{j}\le\frac1r,
\]

and consequently

\[
\sum_a h_a(u)x_k^{-j_a}\le2\lambda |R|.
\]

Induction from \(F_\varnothing\le1\) now yields

\[
F_R(t)
\le
x_k^{|R|}
\int_t^T
2\lambda |R|e^{-2\lambda |R|(u-t)}\,du
\le x_k^{|R|}.
\]

The other bound follows by charging one factor \(r\) per resolution step, with at least \(\lceil w/k\rceil\) steps required.

This is a finite recursion over unresolved sets. It avoids an unsigned expansion whose estimate grows indefinitely with acquisition time.
