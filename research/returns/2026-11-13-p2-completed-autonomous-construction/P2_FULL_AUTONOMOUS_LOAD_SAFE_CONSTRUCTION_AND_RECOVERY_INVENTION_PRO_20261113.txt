Invent an autonomous construction system that can build, load, reconfigure and repair useful structures without a human taking over when an assembly operation fails.

The central useful operation is sustained, load-safe physical construction and recovery, rather than successful execution of an ideal sequence of placements. Start with finite stocks of manufactured structural parts, fasteners, tools, replacement parts and electrically powered machines. Produce a functioning structure, diagnose faults using obtainable measurements, recover or replace failed connections or components, and continue useful construction. Design the mechanics and software together when that changes what can actually be accomplished. Local proof loading, passive alignment, reversible connectors, modular robots, motion planning and temporary supports are existing tools; their names alone are not an invention. All architectures and representations remain open.

Use an Earth-gravity equipment-support application that can be tested without claiming human occupancy or space qualification. A reference useful output is a freestanding or explicitly anchored modular equipment frame with at least a2m clear horizontal span carrying a100N movable equipment load in addition to self-weight and the loads of every robot and tool. Declare the support and anchoring conditions, load locations and directions, permissible elastic deflection and the mechanical consequences of a failed joint. Use obtainable materials, actual cross-sections and manufactured joint geometry, including clearance, preload, friction, compliance and finite strength. The100N load is a minimum useful challenge, not a supplied joint capacity. No external fixture may quietly carry it after acceptance.

Use the same part family and construction machinery to build at least three materially different load-bearing geometries, including a span, a cantilever or offset support, and a frame that requires changing access paths. Specify a finite admissible family of designs and loads; not every imaginable shape must be buildable. Assembly prefixes, reconfiguration and repair must preserve equilibrium, strength, stability and robot access under their actual loads. Graph connectivity alone is not structural stability. Include buckling, contact opening or slip, fastener and member failure where relevant. A nominal final finite-element calculation does not certify an unsafe intermediate operation.

The full reference service consists of at least1000 actual attachment, detachment or component-transfer operations, covering construction, two changes of useful geometry, repeated loading and at least ten recoveries of unannounced faults. It must have no human physical correction, manual fine alignment, task-specific remote command, manual part reload or repair during that interval. Initial setup, programming, feed preparation and maintenance before or after the interval are paid. Parts may be presented by finite dispensers or bins, but their preparation, replenishment mechanism and limited capacity are part of the system. Choose the number and type of physical machines and justify them; no ideal external manipulator, infinite spare robot or instantaneous replacement is supplied.

Define an indispensable, finite fault envelope from actual mechanisms. Include placement or alignment errors, a connection that appears seated but is not carrying its intended load, a degraded or damaged structural component, and a persistent tool or locomotion fault that retrying the same command does not remove. Give the displacement, strength, sensing and timing bounds that make recovery possible. Fault location, category and time are not supplied to the controller. It must infer enough from its own finite sensors to act. Distinguish a damaged but temporarily supported part from complete loss of an indispensable support; do not promise recovery after an uncontrolled collapse. Account for the time during which a fault remains undetected and for additional failure risk while repairing it. A fault flag from a simulator is not a physical sensor.

Find a genuinely new mechanism or organization that makes this whole service possible with a useful advantage against competent current construction and repair systems. One possible opening is a recoverable load path: install and verify a finite bypass, unload the suspect connection, replace or repair it, verify the restored connection and retrieve the bypass while retaining safe support and access. That sequence is only a hypothesis, and temporary shoring is old technology. The invention would require an actual autonomous, inspectable, repeatable construction and recovery capability. A different connector, robot organization, perception strategy, compliant mechanism, part organization or method may be stronger.

A local force or motor-current trace can be ambiguous. For example, an introduced healthy spring and an introduced jammed fixture can both present1MN/m stiffness at a probing port, giving the same50micrometre displacement under50N, while carrying force through different paths. Do not equate a tight motor, low displacement or closed electrical contact with a correctly completed structural joint. Construct the actual measurement, controlled unloading, geometry or other witness needed for your claimed fault envelope. Test what remains indistinguishable and how it affects useful operation. Conversely, do not demand that every possible fault be identifiable when a conservative recovery action can handle several indistinguishable states.

There are strong physical starting points and important existing constructions:
- NASA's ARMADAS work physically built a256-voxel structure from roughly305mm modular units with transporting and fastening machines, passive alignment features and reversible captive fasteners. Its reported operation categories total15970 primitive operations,307 mechanical faults and162 recoveries involving human touch. The experiment also used manual depot loading, a supplied build order and operator recovery; the fault summary excluded tether/communications interference. Those counts are not independent trials or a mission-reliability model. This establishes real construction machinery, not the present unattended service or autonomous repair.
- Cooper and colleagues, Test Results for Autonomous Assembly of Modular Space Structures, NASA20230013537, actually describe a UR10e, a three-prong Grapple Tool, capture features, servo-driven bolts tightened to8.4Nm and a100N axial test load. Their three-module ground experiment used manual fine alignment and a known worksite. Proof loading and compliant capture are prior constructions, not new merely because they appear in your design. Its readable main paper is an optional starting point:
https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20230013537/downloads/main.pdf
- Caltech's5November2026 account of its ASTRA orbital demonstration reports assembly of a third truss bay after two prebuilt bays. Engineers examined images, reproduced unforeseen problems on a ground spare and sent workarounds. Its actual demonstration should be credited without treating astronaut-free assembly as fully autonomous fault recovery or a completed11-bay antenna:

An independently executed small structural example exposes a constructive distinction. In an introduced1m square pin-jointed frame with its two left nodes fixed, four bars including one diagonal support100N on the lower-right node. Removing that diagonal leaves a connected but singular linear stiffness matrix. Adding the opposite diagonal as a bypass, removing and replacing the original diagonal, then recovering the bypass preserves positive stiffness in every represented state; the largest displacement component is below0.483mm at introduced axial rigidityEA=1MN. This is a simple static illustration, not a new structural theorem, a realizable robot motion, a validated connector or an available manufacturing result. Real preload, installation access, actuator forces, temporary contact, buckling and damaged-joint dynamics remain to be constructed.

Reliability must be organized around recovery rather than an unsupported exponential of average component success. For illustration only,100000 independent operations with95% probability of no unrecovered failure would require per-operation residual probability below5.13e-7. The observed ARMADAS touch rate cannot be extrapolated as an independent probability. In an introduced mixture with1% transient failure and0.02% persistent failure, four independent transient retries leave essentially all persistent failures unresolved. Replacement changes that calculation only if diagnosis, access, load transfer, replacement stocks and the replacement operation actually work. Correlated wear, common-mode errors, sensing mistakes and failed recovery of the recovery machine need their own finite account. Do not infer large-scale reliability from a short nominal run.

Construct the actual mechanical and computational system and execute an end-to-end model of construction, measurements, fault detection, recovery and useful acceptance. Use measured component behavior and obtainable hardware where available, and label every introduced joint claim. Include finite sensing rate, uncertainty, latency, motion dynamics, control and planning time, computer memory, communications, calibration and every preparatory computation. A controller that receives exact hidden state, perfect friction or a pre-labelled fault from the simulator has not performed the reference operation. Mathematical guarantees are useful when their hypotheses can be connected to the actual parts, sensors and operating envelope.

Compare against a competent industrial manipulator with appropriate fixtures, perception, force control, retries and replacements; modular robotic construction and repair; and straightforward human-assisted assembly using the same parts and acceptance rules. Give each competitor the same task knowledge, access, load, part preparation and fault conditions. Seek at least a tenfold reduction in human intervention hours per accepted construction-and-repair service relative to a strong human-assisted implementation, without increasing complete service cost by hiding setup, machines or consumed parts. Compare the strongest unattended alternative directly, even if it needs a different architecture. If autonomy provides an operation unavailable at the same deployment mass or access rather than lower Earth-site cost, quantify that distinct useful advantage at the actual boundary instead of inventing a remote-labor price.

Pay the entire fabrication and service ledger: structural stock, joint preparation and rejects, every robot, bearings, drives, force and vision sensors, tools, dispensers, power, batteries or tethers, controllers, safety support fixtures, transport, calibration, assembly, software preparation, replacement inventory, fault recovery, cleaning, wear, maintenance and retirement. State first-build and repeat-manufacturing costs separately, with an actual production quantity and attainable fabrication route. Price estimates are estimates unless supported by procurement evidence. Count throughput over the entire interval, including diagnosis, recovery, changing geometry and restoration; nominal fastening speed is not useful construction throughput. Assess how cost, structural mass, robot inventory, intervention risk and computation change between the reference task and structures with10to100times as many parts.

Design the smallest decisive physical experiment for later execution, with itemized fabrication/access and campaign costs, matched controls, actual load and independent metrology. It must distinguish load-safe recovery from a nominal placement script and expose an apparently seated but ineffective connection. Specify the active loads and support reactions during bypass installation, unloading, removal, replacement and return to service. Make repeated fault recovery, false acceptance, wear and restoration measurable. Explain exactly what this test could establish and which central operations would remain unconstructed at larger scale. Pursue the complete construction-and-recovery capability, not only a proposal, a graph planner, one successful connection, or ideal universal assembly.
