LOCAL RULES, UNIT CLOCKS, AND THE LIMIT OF EIGENVALUE-BASED PREDICTION
Internal selection note. Status: complete elementary obstruction and complexity reduction; not claimed as a new foundational theorem.

1. Exact stochastic construction
For any deterministic computation with distinct successive configurations c_0,...,c_L and a halting final configuration, attach independent exponential holding times of rate one to successive steps. On the reachable component its generator is Q(j,j+1)=1, Q(j,j)=-1 for j<L, Q(L,L)=0. It has unique stationary distribution delta_L. Its characteristic polynomial is z(z+1)^L. Hence every nonzero eigenvalue equals -1 even when L is exponentially large in the physical description.
The absorption time H is Gamma(L,1): E H=L, Var H=L. The law at time t is c_min(N_t,L) for N_t Poisson(t). Therefore TV(P_t(c_0,.),delta_L)=P(N_t<L). For t=L/2, Chernoff's bound gives P(N_t>=L)<=exp[-(log 2-1/2)L], and TV is at least 1 minus this quantity. A unit real-part eigenvalue gap does not control this transient. The killed generator is a single Jordan block; the polynomial multiplying exp(-t) has degree L-1.

2. Local physical compiler
Use a fixed deterministic one-tape Turing machine, a finite alphabet, one distinguished head-state symbol, and a finite interval of O(s) cells with end markers. Each TM transition can be expressed as a nearest-neighbor rewrite of two cells: the current head cell and its left or right neighbor. A stay transition is one-cell. Put rate one on every permitted local rewrite. Exactly one rewrite is enabled on each valid nonhalting configuration, because there is exactly one head and the machine is deterministic. A halting configuration enables none. This is a continuous-time asynchronous finite-alphabet network with bounded interaction size and a constant local rule table. The input resides in the initially prepared cells. A fixed polynomial-space decider has no repeated configuration on a halting run; a repeat would force an infinite deterministic loop. Thus its reachable component is exactly the path above. No claim is made about arbitrary invalid configurations or uniqueness of the stationary law on all physically representable strings. The target is prediction from the specified prepared valid input.

3. A robust decision gap in the ideal model
Take a fixed polynomial-space decider for TQBF. For input length n it uses s(n)=poly(n) cells. Let U(n)>=1 be an explicit integer bound on its number of possible configurations, enlarged by any polynomial local compilation overhead. U has poly(n) bits. Arrange that a designated output cell is zero until acceptance is decided, becomes one if accepted, and remains zero if rejected. This can be done with a final head-return-and-write routine, retaining polynomial space and exponential configuration bound. At t=8U, for an accepting input the output-one probability is at least P(Poisson(8U)>=U)>=1-exp[-8U(1-1/8)^2/2]>=1-exp(-49/16)>0.95. For a rejecting input the output-one probability is exactly zero. Therefore polynomial-time additive-1/4 prediction for this class, given the local rule table, prepared input, and binary t, decides TQBF. A deterministic such predictor implies P=PSPACE; a randomized bounded-error such predictor implies BPP=PSPACE. This is a hardness result, not an unconditional superpolynomial time lower bound.
For this restricted path-clock class the promise problem is in PSPACE: simply run the deterministic polynomial-space decider until halt. This establishes PSPACE-completeness of the restricted accept-versus-reject prediction promise problem. It is unnecessary to claim PSPACE membership for every arbitrary local stochastic generator.

4. Access and cost ledger
The finite transition table is exactly supplied, not inferred. Calibrating allowed rate-one clocks to relative tolerance eta with failure probability alpha uses O(eta^-2 log(r/alpha)) completed jumps per rule for r constant rules, under separately prepared contexts. This does not establish the exact absence of omitted rules. In the ideal model the only stochasticity is timing, so per-rule rate errors bounded by 1+/-eta merely change the total expected completion time by those reciprocal factors. An output prediction gap remains at t=O(U) if all allowed rates have a constant lower bound. However, allowing wrong-symbol transitions of total hazard delta per enabled step changes the claim: over L steps, even independent per-step error can accumulate probability O(L delta). A finite local calibration cannot certify delta=0, and a fault-tolerant compiler has not been supplied here. Ordinary physical execution takes Theta(L) mean serial clock time and Theta(L) local updates; a driven irreversible realization has a fuel/reset/resource cost per update which cannot be hidden. No thermodynamic equilibrium implementation with bounded local energies has been established.

5. Why the obstruction does not contradict reversible prediction bounds
For an irreducible finite reversible chain with gap gamma and known stationary distribution pi, TV(P_t(x,.),pi)<=0.5*pi(x)^(-1/2)*exp(-gamma*t). Thus a lower bound log(1/pi(x))=poly(n) plus gamma^-1=poly(n) gives a polynomial relaxation bound. Our irreversible absorbing path has neither a positive full-support pi nor self-adjointness. A biased reversible path can have constant eigenvalue gap and long relaxation from its unlikely endpoint, but then log(1/pi(endpoint)) is Theta(L); a bounded-energy local equilibrium device on O(n) sites cannot silently supply that energy range. General nonreversible analysis must account for transient norm growth, singular values, a justified contraction/coupling criterion, or other appropriate mixing certificates, not just real parts of eigenvalues.

6. Existing strongest alternatives and selection judgment
Bhatnagar, Bogdanov and Mossel already established PSPACE-completeness of succinct Markov-chain convergence testing, and SZK-hardness even under rapid-mixing promises: https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.0089 . Chatterjee's nonreversible spectral-gap theory explicitly replaces eigenvalue-based intuition with a singular-value definition and distinguishes convergence of empirical averages from mixing: https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.10876 ; published Annals of Applied Probability 35(4),2644-2677 (2025), DOI 10.1214/25-AAP2183. Reliable computation under persistent local noise has substantial machinery going back to Gacs: JCSS32(1),15-78 (1986), DOI10.1016/0022-0000(86)90002-4. Such machinery is not included for free.
The contribution of this note is an explicit operational connection: constant-size local rules, no rare clock rates, and a trivial eigenvalue list can coexist with exponentially expensive physical execution and PSPACE-hard prediction from a prepared input. This is useful as a design constraint on proposals for scientific prediction. It is not a demonstrated new capability beyond these strongest approaches, and the absence-of-errors premise is substantial. I do not recommend presenting it as the mission's principal scientific result. The backward-dependency perfect-sampling positive lane was also screened out because classical perfect simulation and Astra N1627 already provide stronger relevant constructions.
