MIXED-BATH ACQUISITION: EXACT OUTPUT-ONLY RECOVERY AND ITS OPTIMAL CLOCK
Construction note, 5 November 2026. Mathematical result and finite-matrix
diagnostics; not a hardware experiment, priority claim, or claim of practical
superiority over an available strong SWAP.

1. QUESTION AND OPERATIONAL SETTING

A source S of dimension d initially contains one arbitrary unknown state,
possibly entangled with an inaccessible reference R. It sequentially interacts
with N fresh d-dimensional bath registers E_1,...,E_N. Each register is initially
I/d, independent of every other register and of SR. The initial purifiers of
these mixed states are inaccessible. Register E_j is delivered to a receiver
after its single interaction and never returns to the actual source. The
receiver receives all emitted registers, can preserve all their correlations,
and knows their chronological order and the implemented interaction sequence.
The final source is unavailable to the receiver and is discarded.

The j-th native source interaction is

  U_j = cos(theta_j) I + i sin(theta_j) W_{S,E_j},                 (1)

where W is SWAP. Arbitrary known source-only unitaries may occur before,
between, or after these collisions. They are included in the full known
unitary U. There are no additional source/bath couplings, hidden payload noise,
or losses in the ideal mathematical model. These physical premises are not
deduced from observing source relaxation.

Let e=d^N. The entire accessible output channel is

  C(X) = Tr_S[ U (X tensor I_E/e) U^dagger ].                     (2)

All tensor factors E_1,...,E_N are retained in (2). In particular, this is not
a channel that separately measures or replaces the individual bath marginals.
Since the total maximally mixed state is invariant,

  C(I/d)=I_E/e.                                                  (3)

For a channel Lambda on a d-dimensional input, define the worst-case,
reference-uniform error by half the diamond distance,

  epsilon(Lambda) = (1/2)||Lambda-id||_diamond.                   (4)

Every reference dimension and every input entangled with that reference are
covered by this norm. A physical implementation must pay for the precision,
calibration, storage, routing, and bath preparation assumed in (1)-(3).

2. AN EXACT FINITE RECEIVER

The receiver supplies one independent maximally mixed dummy S' of dimension
d, applies U^dagger to the dummy and all received registers in reverse
chronological order, and discards those registers. Its output is the dummy.
The corresponding CPTP map is

  D(Y) = Tr_E[ U^dagger (I_S/d tensor Y) U ].                     (5)

The actual source and every initial bath purifier are absent. There is no
postselection and no repeated access to the unknown input. Known source-only
rotations are reversed on the dummy as part of U^dagger.

With the Hilbert-Schmidt adjoint in ordinary trace normalization,

  C^dagger(Y) = (1/e) Tr_E[ U^dagger (I_S tensor Y) U ],
  D = (e/d) C^dagger.                                           (6)

Because of (3), this is exactly the Petz/transpose channel for reference input
I/d. The reverse circuit implements an established recovery operation. The
calculation below gives its exact channel and output-only acquisition clock.

THEOREM 1 (exact acquired channel followed by recovery).
Put r_N=product_j(1-sin^4(theta_j)) and alpha_N=1-r_N. Then, including
arbitrary known source-only rotations,

  D C = alpha_N id + r_N R_{I/d},                              (7)

where R_{I/d}(X)=Tr(X)I/d. This is an equality of linear maps and remains exact
after tensoring with the identity on any reference.

PROOF.
Use normalized Hilbert-Schmidt inner products: on a D-dimensional system,
<A,B>=Tr(A^dagger B)/D. Define the isometric embedding

  J(X)=U(X tensor I_E)U^dagger

and the orthogonal projection onto operators that are identity on S,

  P(Z)=I_S tensor Tr_S(Z)/d,       Q=I-P.

The normalized-Hilbert-Schmidt adjoint is
J^*(Z)=(1/e)Tr_E[U^dagger Z U]. Substitution of (2) and (5) gives

  D C = J^* P J.                                                (8)

At any intermediate stage an operator on S and previous outputs has a unique
decomposition into an old P-part I_S tensor A and a Q-part. After appending a
fresh identity bath factor, the P-part commutes with U_j and is unchanged.
Expand a Q-part as a sum X_a tensor A_a, where every X_a on S has trace zero
and A_a is an arbitrary operator on all previous outputs. For each term,

  U_j(X_a tensor I)U_j^dagger
   = c_j^2 X_a tensor I + s_j^2 I tensor X_a
       + i c_j s_j [W, X_a tensor I].

The first term and commutator have zero partial trace over S. Therefore

  P Ad_{U_j}(X_a tensor I)=s_j^2 I_S tensor X_a.                 (9)

This is a scaled isometry from the entire Q-space, including arbitrary old
output coefficients, to the source-identity space whose new E_j factor is
traceless. The new contribution is orthogonal to the old P-part, whose E_j
factor remains identity. Unitarity preserves the total normalized norm. Hence

  ||P Z_after||^2=||P Z_before||^2+s_j^4||Q Z_before||^2,
  ||Q Z_after||^2=(1-s_j^4)||Q Z_before||^2.                      (10)

Source-only unitary rotations preserve P, Q, and both norms. Initially every
traceless X has P(X tensor I)=0. Iteration gives
||P J(X)||^2=alpha_N||X||^2 for every traceless X. Polarization yields
J^*PJ=alpha_N id on the traceless space. The identity is fixed and orthogonal
to that space. Equations (8) and (7) follow. This proof explicitly propagates
correlations with all older outputs. END PROOF.

3. EXACT DECODER ERROR AND A CONVERSE FOR EVERY DECODER

Write c_d=1-d^(-2). Depolarization is a uniform generalized-Pauli/Weyl mixture.
Equation (7) has identity-Weyl probability 1-c_d r_N and each nonidentity
Weyl probability r_N/d^2. On half of a maximally entangled state these errors
produce orthogonal Bell states. The resulting diamond-distance lower bound
equals the elementary mixture upper bound. Thus

  epsilon(D C)=c_d r_N,                                        (11)
  F_e(D C,I/d)=1-c_d r_N.                                      (12)

THEOREM 2 (all-decoder optimum within a factor two).
Let epsilon_opt be the infimum of (4) over every CPTP receiver decoder from
the entire emitted bath, including arbitrary receiver ancillas, measurements,
and computation. Then

  c_d[1-sqrt(1-r_N)] <= epsilon_opt <= c_d r_N,                  (13)
  (c_d/2) r_N <= epsilon_opt <= c_d r_N.                        (14)

PROOF OF THE DIRECT LOWER BOUND.
Take an orthogonal unitary Weyl basis W_a with W_0=I and
Tr(W_a^dagger W_b)=d delta_ab. Set B(X)=Tr_S[J(X)]/d, so C(X)=(d/e)B(X).
For a nonidentity W_a the Gram identity gives
Tr[B(W_a)^dagger B(W_a)]/e=alpha_N. Consequently

  ||C(W_a)||_1 <=sqrt(e)||C(W_a)||_2=d sqrt(alpha_N).

CPTP maps contract the trace norm, also for non-Hermitian operators by duality
with operator-norm contraction of unital CP maps. Thus, for every decoder R,
|Tr[W_a^dagger R C(W_a)]|<=d sqrt(alpha_N). The superoperator-trace formula
for entanglement fidelity gives

  F_e(R C,I/d)=d^(-3) sum_a Tr[W_a^dagger R C(W_a)]
             <=[1+(d^2-1)sqrt(alpha_N)]/d^2.                    (15)

The identity term is d because R C is trace preserving. Testing the recovered
normalized Choi state against the original maximally entangled projector gives
epsilon(R C)>=1-F_e(R C,I/d). This proves (13); the upper bound is the actual
decoder. Finally 1-sqrt(1-r)>=r/2 proves (14). END PROOF.

Independently, established Barnum-Knill transpose near-optimality gives

  [sup_R F_e(R C,I/d)]^2 <=F_e(D C,I/d)=1-c_d r_N.               (16)

It implies epsilon_opt>=1-sqrt(1-c_d r_N)>=c_d r_N/2. The direct Weyl bound
(13) is stronger for this isotropic model. Transpose near-optimality itself
is existing machinery, not a new theorem claimed by this construction.

More sharply, Renes (Phys. Rev. A96,042328(2017), Eq.13) already proves
F_transpose>=F_opt^2+(1-F_opt)^2/(d^2-1). Solving this quadratic with (12)
gives exactly the lower bound (13). Thus the model-specific Weyl proof above
is a direct derivation of an existing sharper recovery relation after the
new physical Gram calculation, not an independent novelty claim.

4. INDEPENDENT MODULES WITH ARBITRARILY ENTANGLED INPUTS

Suppose m independent source/bath architectures have dimensions d_i and
residual parameters r_i. The unknown input may be entangled across modules
and with an external reference. Independence means that the acquisition
channel factorizes, C_total=tensor_i C_i; it does not constrain the input.
Put f_i=1-(1-d_i^(-2))r_i. Then

  epsilon((tensor_i D_i)(tensor_i C_i))=1-product_i f_i,         (17)
  inf_R epsilon(R C_total)>=1-sqrt(product_i f_i).              (18)

The converse allows R to process all outputs jointly across modules. The
explicit decoder is at most a factor two worse than this unrestricted optimum.

PROOF.
The recovered channel is a product Weyl channel, with no-error probability
product_i f_i. The orthogonal-Bell-state lower bound and mixture upper bound
give (17). The transpose channel for the maximally mixed joint input
factorizes, and its entanglement fidelity is product_i f_i. Apply (16) to the
entire channel, without restricting its optimal decoder to a product map,
then use epsilon>=1-F_e. This proves (18). The factor two follows from
1-sqrt(f)=(1-f)/(1+sqrt(f))>=(1-f)/2. END PROOF.

For equal d,theta,N, write ell=-log(1-sin^4(theta)) and r_N=exp(-ell N).
The explicit decoder achieves global half-diamond error at most epsilon if

  r_N <=[1-(1-epsilon)^(1/m)]/c_d,                             (19)

whereas every decoder necessarily obeys

  r_N <=[1-(1-epsilon)^(2/m)]/c_d.                             (20)

For small error, fixed finite d, and weak theta this yields

  N=Theta(sin(theta)^(-4) log(m/epsilon)).                     (21)

The O(mN) count is for native source collisions and direct-address inverse
gates. Spatial routing, physical memory, precision, and gate compilation must
be paid separately. This acquires an arbitrary m-module input with a uniform
channel guarantee; it is not a communication-capacity theorem permitting a
smaller payload encoded across modules at the source.

5. SOURCE FORGETTING AND BATH ACQUISITION HAVE DIFFERENT CLOCKS

The reduced source after one partial SWAP with I/d is

  X ->cos^2(theta)X+sin^2(theta)Tr(X)I/d.

Its retained visibility is beta_N=product_j cos^2(theta_j). Known source-only
rotations accompany this by a known net conjugation. For equal theta,

  (1/2)||source_channel-R_{I/d}||_diamond=c_d cos^(2N)(theta).

Source forgetting to error epsilon requires order sin(theta)^(-2)
log(1/epsilon) collisions; every bath-only decoder requires order
sin(theta)^(-4) log(1/epsilon). A full SWAP at theta=pi/2 exports perfectly
in one collision. If the source permits that operation, it is the simpler
baseline. This construction concerns a supplied weak fresh-once port; the
receiver may use stronger hardware without invalidating the converse.

6. IDENTICAL EXACT SOURCE DYNAMICS, DIFFERENT OUTPUT CLOCKS

Choose collision duration Delta and rate gamma with
cos^2(theta)=exp(-gamma Delta). At EVERY grid time T=N Delta the source has
exactly the depolarizing visibility exp(-gamma T). In contrast,

  r_N=[1-(1-exp(-gamma Delta))^2]^(T/Delta),
  alpha_N=1-r_N=gamma^2 T Delta+O(Delta^2)                       (22)

for fixed gamma,T. The ENTIRE bath channel obeys

  ||C-R_{I_E/e}||_diamond<=min(2,sqrt[(d^2-1)alpha_N]).         (23)

For any pure SR input, its centered weighted chi-square norm relative to
I_S/d tensor rho_R is at most d^2-1. The source-identity projection captures
exactly alpha_N of that norm: Theorem 1's Gram identity applies to arbitrary
spectator coefficients. After tracing S, weighted Cauchy-Schwarz bounds
trace distance. Purification covers all inputs and references, proving (23).

At a fixed coarse time h, M fresh weak collisions with
cos^2(theta_M)=exp(-gamma h/M) and one collision with
cos^2(theta_1)=exp(-gamma h) give EXACTLY the same source channel. The former
has alpha_M~gamma^2 h^2/M and approaches an input-independent entire bath
channel as M grows. The latter has alpha_1=(1-exp(-gamma h))^2 and explicit
receiver error c_d[1-(1-exp(-gamma h))^2], small for large gamma h. Repeated
coarse blocks preserve the equality of source dynamics at every coarse time.

Both source and entire emitted-bath marginals can become input independent
in a simultaneous limit: take Delta->0 and
T approximately gamma^(-1)log[1/(gamma Delta)], rounding N=T/Delta. The source
visibility is order gamma Delta; alpha_N is order
gamma Delta log[1/(gamma Delta)]. Both vanish. At each finite Delta the
joint source-plus-bath inverse still recovers the input perfectly. Input
dependence can therefore remain in joint correlations when neither marginal
reveals it. No-hiding is respected because the pure dilation also contains
the inaccessible initial bath purifiers.

The physical cost is essential. Holding gamma fixed requires
theta~sqrt(gamma Delta) and square-pulse strength
theta/Delta~sqrt(gamma/Delta), which diverges. The output acquisition exponent
per time is asymptotically gamma^2 Delta. Collection time is order
log(1/epsilon)/(gamma^2 Delta). At bounded strength J with theta=J Delta,
source forgetting instead takes order 1/(J^2 Delta), and output acquisition
order 1/(J^4 Delta^3), up to accuracy logarithms. The limit does not supply
unbounded coupling, switching speed, or coherent memory for free.

7. MATCH TO THE UNIVERSAL ADAPTIVE TRACIAL ACTION LAW

The companion theorem covers any finite source and fresh finite maximally
mixed bath registers, arbitrary receiver instruments on earlier outputs, and
genuinely classical feedback selecting subsequent source/fresh-register
interactions. At stage j every record branch has action at most
a_j=integral ||H_int,j(t)||dt. There is no coherent feedback, returning bath
register, source measurement/reset, or additional source port. Put
q_j=min(1,4a_j^4), b_N=product_j(1-q_j). It gives

  ||C-R_{C(I/d)}||_diamond<=min(2,sqrt[(d^2-1)(1-b_N)]),
  inf_R (1/2)||R C-id||_diamond>=c_d[1-sqrt(1-b_N)].

The root's adaptive_tracial_law.txt and the independent theory audit provide
the full proof: a baseline-weighted chi-square operator norm, CPTP contraction,
and orthogonality of old and new source-identity contributions. The first
commutator has zero source partial trace for a fresh tracial bath; export
amplitude starts at second order and squared information transfer at fourth
order. The recovery converse uses a Petz-versus-weighted-norm comparison and
Renes's established sharper transpose-recovery inequality. Barnum-Knill also
supplies the same small-error scaling.

The partial-SWAP transfer is exactly sin^4(theta), so the finite construction
matches the fourth-power action dependence and logarithmic accuracy cost in
the universal law. This supplies both a constraint on every allowed receiver
and a concrete operation at that scale.

8. PAID RESOURCES AND PHYSICAL BOUNDARIES

One unknown input is consumed. There are N fresh mixed d-level registers,
N source collisions, N retained outputs, one mixed dummy, and N inverse
two-register interactions, plus inverse known source rotations. The receiver
stores N d-level systems; for qubits that is N coherent qubits. This is an
attainable memory requirement, not a proven optimal memory requirement.

Chronological labels, coherent retention, and inverse-gate availability are
real access assumptions. Direct addressing permits O(N) fixed-dimensional
decoder gates. A local register can require O(N^2) routing SWAPs; the physical
supplement specifies a layout and bill. Collection, delivery, and decoder
durations must be paid. The earliest register must survive through collection
and its use in the inverse circuit. A source pulse with norm at most J takes
at least |theta|/J when theta is in its principal weak range.

Ordinary channel hybrid bounds pay finite errors by summing per-operation
diamond differences, including preparation, source gates, inverse gates,
routing, and storage. A common control bias does not average away. Partial-
SWAP unitary mismatch is at most |delta theta| in operator norm, and channel
mismatch at most 2|delta theta| in diamond norm. A crude worst-case angular
budget is therefore O(epsilon/N) per collision if other errors are controlled
separately. Fixed-dimensional trusted process calibration has polynomial
precision cost, but uses paid known states and measurements. It does not
certify independence, absent hidden couplings, or purifier inaccessibility.

N outputs waiting O(N) serial collision times have O(N^2) integrated memory
exposure. At nonzero noise this must be budgeted or corrected. If fault-tolerant
receiver memory is supplied, its factory, gates, latency, and threshold are
additional physical premises and costs. None was simulated here.

Traciality is substantive. The theorem does not cover a polarized Gibbs
reservoir, an infinite-dimensional bosonic bath, fresh ancillas entangled with
accessible receiver memory, accessible bath preparation labels, bath return,
or coherent source/receiver feedback. Small independent bath defects can be
paid by a global preparation trace-distance estimate or a hybrid sum; an
unknown persistent nontracial defect is not negligible merely because native
couplings are weak. The strongest simpler baseline remains one full source
SWAP when that operation is allowed.

9. EXECUTED EVIDENCE

check_hot_bath.py explicitly builds the full source-plus-bath unitary and
all emitted correlations in 48 qubit cases: N=1,...,6, four base angles,
half with noncommuting known source rotations. It computes the full bath
operator Gram matrix and actually applies the dummy inverse decoder. Largest
Gram error: 9.992007221626409e-16. Largest decoder-map discrepancy:
1.1102433744052141e-15.

Five additional dense runs use d=3 or4, N<=3, noncommuting source rotations,
and random mixed source/reference inputs with reference dimension d+1. They
evolve the full joint input and bath, trace out the real source, append an
independent dummy, and apply the inverse receiver. Maximum entrywise error
against the reference-tensored identity (7): 5.56203128037865e-17.

Eighty generic two-qubit Hamiltonians test the export-amplitude bound 2a^2;
the largest observed ratio to that bound is 0.39759133314529693. Twelve
selected measured-output feedback chains retain all records and select the
next interaction from a preceding record. Their Bell-input trace distances
obey the general law. These floating-point checks do not prove diamond-norm
or all-Hamiltonian statements. No SDP optimization or hardware run is claimed.

Large-N rows are formula evaluations, not large bath simulations. For a qubit
with theta=0.1 and target half-diamond error 0.01, source forgetting takes
432 collisions. Every bath-only decoder needs at least 36,485 collisions by
(14), and the explicit decoder suffices after 43,462. That separation has a
corresponding coherence and precision cost; it is not evidence of economical
quantum storage. Full records are in hot_bath_checks.json.
