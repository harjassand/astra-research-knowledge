PRIMARY AND ASTRA COMPARISON: MIXED-BATH ACQUISITION
5 November 2026. This is a scoped comparison, not an exhaustive priority claim.

The claim worth carrying is the universal finite-action, all-output tracial
collision law with classical feedback, together with an exact partial-SWAP
clock and a concrete decoder achieving that clock. Neither partial SWAP,
homogenization, Petz recovery, physical inverse implementations, near-optimal
transpose recovery, nor mixed-dilation ambiguity is newly introduced here.

1. Quantum homogenization: strongest same-dynamics predecessor.
M. Ziman et al., Quantum homogenization, published as Diluting quantum
information: An analysis of information transfer in system-reservoir
interactions, Phys. Rev. A 65, 042105 (2002).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0110164
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.65.042105

The partial-SWAP model, source relaxation, correlation bookkeeping, and
classical-order quantum-safe inverse are established. Section VII.B explicitly
retains N+1 particles, identifies the original source, and reverses their
interactions. Our physical receiver has only N emitted particles: it discards
the original source, substitutes a maximally mixed dummy, and incurs the exact
residual product(1-sin^4 theta). The resource distinction is decisive.

2. Recent homogenizer protection claim.
A. Yosifov, A. Iyer, D. Ebler, V. Vedral, Quantum homogenization as a quantum
steady-state protocol on noisy intermediate-scale quantum hardware,
Phys. Rev. A 109, 032624 (2024).
https://arxiv.org/html/2412.14544v1
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.109.032624

Section IV uses generalized Beny-Oreshkov conditions and a stipulated
approximately k-forgetful complement condition (Eq.15) for correctable
reservoir subspaces. The reviewed argument does not give the finite tracial
output-only sin^4 clock or our all-Hamiltonian adaptive action law. We neither
refute its conditional protection result nor identify its assumed complement
with the inaccessible initial bath purifiers in our model.

3. Recovery near-optimality: machinery, not new discovery.
H. Barnum, E. Knill, Reversing quantum dynamics with near-optimal quantum and
classical fidelity, J. Math. Phys.43,2097-2106(2002).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0004088
https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1459754
The current arXiv version includes a corrected proof. The inequality
F_opt^2<=F_transpose supplies our product-module collective-decoder converse.

J. M. Renes, Better bounds on optimal measurement and entanglement recovery,
with applications to uncertainty and monogamy relations,
Phys. Rev. A96,042328(2017).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1707.01114
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.96.042328
Equation13 is the stronger inequality
F_transpose>=F_opt^2+(1-F_opt)^2/(d^2-1). It yields our sharp-centered
single-module lower bound once the physical export Gram norm is calculated.
Our direct Weyl/Cauchy-Schwarz proofs do not create a new recovery inequality.

4. Physical Petz inverse implementations: strong existing operation.
C. C. Aw, L. H. Zaw, M. Balanzo-Juando, V. Scarani, Role of Dilations in
Reversing Physical Processes: Tabletop Reversibility and Generalized Thermal
Operations, PRX Quantum5,010332(2024).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.13909
https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.5.010332
Theorem3 gives product preservation as a sufficient condition for an inverse-
unitary Petz implementation with a replacement ancillary state. The fully
mixed reference in our collision architecture is product preserving. Thus our
dummy-and-inverse formula is an elementary instance of an existing physical
recovery principle. The unresolved quantity addressed here is how much
recoverable information that finite bath channel has acquired and how that
quantity scales for every allowed interaction/feedback protocol.

M. Song, H. Kwon, V. Scarani, Exact and approximate conditions of tabletop
reversibility: when is Petz recovery cost-free? (2025 preprint, 2026 update).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.26895
https://doi.org/10.1088/2058-9565/aea92d
This work develops exact/approximate inverse-implementation conditions and
collisional Lindbladian implementations. Those conditions concern how to
implement the reverse of a given channel; our acquired output channel and
action law concern when the actual emitted mixed reservoir can encode an
arbitrary unknown source state. We do not describe inverse gates as cost-free.

5. No-hiding and repeated-interaction limits.
S. L. Braunstein, A. K. Pati, Quantum information cannot be completely hidden
in correlations: Implications for the black-hole information paradox,
Phys. Rev. Lett.98,080502(2007).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0603046
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.98.080502
No-hiding concerns the full pure dilation. In our mixed-bath implementation
that includes inaccessible initial purifiers. No violation follows when the
source and the accessible bath separately become input independent.

S. Attal, Y. Pautrat, From repeated to continuous quantum interactions,
Annales Henri Poincare7,59-104(2006).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0311002
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00023-005-0242-8
Repeated-interaction/quantum-stochastic limits and the distinct Hamiltonian
scalings are established. The full physical mixed output need not be the
entire purified/GNS noise space. Our finite diamond bound distinguishes that
access explicitly and charges the diverging coupling in fixed-rate limits.

6. Homogenizer reuse and erasure.
M. Violaris, C. Marletto, Irreversibility beyond Landauer's principle: is it
possible to perform erasure using the quantum homogenizer?
New J. Phys.24,113030(2022).
https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/ac9e13
The paper compares pure-to-mixed and mixed-to-pure transformation under repeated
reuse of a reservoir, emphasizing deterioration and cyclic performance. That
is distinct from retrieving one unknown source from all first-use outputs
after discarding the source. The publisher endpoint was blocked; the published
full-text copy indexed by ResearchGate was inspected. This is not used as a
mathematical premise of our proof.

COMPLETED INCREMENT TO PRESENT CAREFULLY

The source partial trace of the first-order commutator vanishes for every
fresh tracial ancilla. A weighted operator norm turns that local cancellation
into a finite bound on ALL outputs, ALL correlations, arbitrary receiver
processing, and genuine classical feedback. This is the missing acquisition
calculation; ordinary Petz inequalities only operate once its acquired channel
has been characterized. Partial SWAP then admits an exact Gram calculation,
source-discarded reverse implementation, and collective-decoder converse that
match the fourth-power/log-accuracy law. Identical exact depolarizing source
dynamics at every sampled time can coexist with arbitrarily different actual
output acquisition clocks. Its architecture, preparation errors, memory,
timing, and calibration are explicit conditional resources.

The literature search did not establish that no prior theorem contains some
equivalent formulation. The manuscript should present a proved construction
and its precisely delimited consequence, not an unverified priority claim or
claim of a civilization-scale scientific transformation.
