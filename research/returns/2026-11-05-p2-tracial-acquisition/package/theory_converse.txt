ADAPTIVE TRACIAL COLLISIONS: AUDITED CONVERSE AND PRODUCT-MODULE LAW
Independent proof supplement, 5 November 2026

AUDIT VERDICT
The root proof is valid under the fresh-tracial, one-pass, classical-feedback architecture. Its second-order transfer estimate, weighted-norm data processing, arbitrary-reference normalization, Petz comparison and Barnum--Knill consequence check out. Branchwise integrated action bounds must be applied blockwise: the integral of the maximum branch norm need not equal the maximum integrated branch norm. A product-module fidelity law requires product emission channels; cross-module feedback can destroy that condition. This supplement strengthens the root converse and adds a pathwise variable-action law. The dimension-aware recovery inequality is inherited from Renes (2017), Eq. (13), and is proved here rather than claimed as new.

CONVENTIONS
All operational channel errors below are HALF diamond distances delta(C,D)=0.5||C-D||_diamond, in [0,1]. Entanglement fidelity is the squared overlap with a normalized maximally entangled state. hbar=1. Source dimension d>=2; d=1 is trivial. Protocols have finitely many rounds and finite-dimensional systems/records, of arbitrarily large dimensions. Constants do not depend on receiver dimension. Inverses mean Moore--Penrose inverses on support. No infinite-dimensional extension is asserted without its usual domain and limit hypotheses.

1. PHYSICAL AND OPERATIONAL MODEL
The source S has dimension d and an arbitrary unknown input, possibly entangled with a reference R. Receiver memory is initialized in an arbitrary fixed state independent of that input/reference. Round j introduces E_j in tau_j=I/e_j, fresh and independent of S, earlier outputs, receiver memory, and every accessible reference. Its purifiers are inaccessible.

A collision, conditional on a genuinely classical history h, is a unitary on S E_j generated by H_{j,h}(t), with action
  a_{j,h}=integral ||H_{j,h}(t)||_infinity dt.
Known source-only and fresh-ancilla-only unitaries can be removed in interaction pictures. The receiver may apply arbitrary channels/instruments to retained systems and use the resulting classical record to choose later controls. It may retain arbitrary coherent output correlations. Source-only unitaries are allowed. Emitted systems never return to S, retained quantum memory never couples coherently to S, and incoming E_j cannot be replaced by a purified or pre-correlated state. Bounded adaptive stopping is included by zero-action padding. At the end S is discarded; let C denote the source-to-receiver channel and sigma=C(I/d).

These are actual architecture premises. Local tracial marginals alone do not certify freshness, missing interactions, or absence of an accessible purifier. No short calibration theorem for those premises is asserted.

2. WEIGHTED HILBERT SPACE LEMMA
For positive omega and a supported operator X, define
  ||X||_omega^2=Tr(X^dagger X omega^{-1}).
The weight can be unnormalized. Every CPTP map Phi satisfies
  ||Phi(X)||_{Phi(omega)}<=||X||_omega.               (2.1)
Proof: the variational identity is
  ||X||_omega^2=sup_Y{2 Re Tr(X^dagger Y)-Tr(omega Y^dagger Y)}.
For the output expression pull Y back by the unital CP adjoint Phi^dagger. Its Schwarz inequality
  Phi^dagger(Y^dagger Y)>=Phi^dagger(Y)^dagger Phi^dagger(Y)
makes the output supremum at most the input supremum. This applies to arbitrary X, not only positive states or Hermitian differences. Restriction to supports, or regularization and a limit, handles singular baselines. Supported deviations remain supported under channels.

Appending a fixed state, with the same state appended to the baseline, is an isometry for this norm. A unitary commuting with omega is an isometry. When Tr omega=1,
  ||X||_1<=||X||_omega,                             (2.2)
by the Hilbert--Schmidt Holder bound on (X omega^{-1/2})omega^{1/2}.

For omega=I_S/d tensor sigma_M define
  P X=I_S/d tensor Tr_S X,  Q=I-P.
P is an orthogonal projection: when Tr_S Z=0,
  <I/d tensor A,Z>_omega=Tr[A^dagger(Tr_S Z)sigma^{-1}]=0.
Its norm is exactly
  ||P X||_omega^2=||Tr_S X||_sigma^2.                (2.3)
A receiver map acting only on M commutes with P,Q and contracts both components separately. Include any spectator reference in M when applying this statement.

3. BASELINE FACTORIZATION UNDER CLASSICAL FEEDBACK
If the source initially equals I/d, its joint state with the receiver always has the form I_S/d tensor sigma_M. Each fresh collision sees I_S/d tensor I_E/e, so leaves that product invariant. In a classical record block h, the weight is I_S/d tensor sigma_{M,h} tensor I_E/e, and the controlled collision commutes with it. Receiver maps update only sigma_M. Source unitaries leave I/d fixed. This proves the product property inductively, including arbitrary classical feedback.

Classical records are dephased before controlling source operations. If an operation later erases or merges such labels, copy them first to an inert proof archive. Copying an already dephased record does not change the actual process when the archive is ignored. A bound for the enlarged receiver bounds the actual one by data processing. The archive permits a classical history tree for the variable-budget argument.

4. ONE COLLISION: SECOND-ORDER AMPLITUDE, FOURTH-ORDER SQUARED NORM
In a single history block append I_E/e and write X=P X+Q X. The old P X commutes with the collision U and is unchanged. For every H on S E,
  P[H,(Q X) tensor I_E/e]=0.                        (4.1)
Indeed expand Q X in source matrices A_l and old-memory coefficients B_l, and H in source matrices C_k and fresh-ancilla matrices D_k. Each source partial trace contains Tr_S[C_k,A_l]=0. Old memory is a spectator. This uses exact traciality of the fresh ancilla and the stated coupling reach.

Write L_t=-i[H(t),.] and V_t=Ad_{U_t}. The exact Volterra expansion is
  V_T X=X+integral_0^T L_s X ds
       +integral_{0<=r<=s<=T} L_s L_r V_r X dr ds.
The V_r are isometries, and ||L_t Z||_omega<=2||H(t)||_infinity||Z||_omega, since H commutes with omega. Apply P with input Q X. Its zeroth and first-order terms vanish by (4.1). If a=integral||H||, then
  ||P Ad_U(Q X tensor I/e)||_omega<=2a^2||Q X||_omega.
Projection and unitarity also bound this amplitude by ||Q X||. Set
  q(a)=min{1,4a^4}.
The transferred squared norm t obeys
  0<=t<=q(a)z,  z=||Q X||^2.                        (4.2)
It is orthogonal to the old P component, because
  <P X,P Ad_U Q X>=<P X,Ad_U Q X>
                 =<Ad_{U^{-1}}P X,Q X>=0.
Thus the exact collision bookkeeping is
  y'=y+t, z'=z-t, y=||P X||^2.                      (4.3)
The collision preserves total norm. Receiver operations may decrease y and z separately.

If every history obeys deterministic per-round caps a_j, set q_j=q(a_j). Apply the above estimate within each history block, then sum. With initial total squared norm K and initial P X=0, induction gives
  y_N<=K alpha,
  alpha=1-b, b=product_j(1-q_j).                    (4.4)
Use y'<=y+q_j(K-y). The proof never exchanges an integrated branch maximum with the maximum integrated action.

5. GENUINELY VARIABLE ACTION: PATHWISE BUDGET LAW
Suppose each branch action has q(a_{j,h})<1 and assign its charge
  c_{j,h}=-log(1-q(a_{j,h})).
Assume EVERY complete classical history satisfies
  sum of its collision charges <= C_*.             (5.1)
Then the export estimate (4.4) holds with
  b=e^{-C_*}, alpha=1-e^{-C_*}.                     (5.2)

Proof: use the archived history tree. At node h let R_h=C_*-spent(h)>=0, and let y_h,z_h be its unnormalized weighted squared norms. Define
  V=sum_h[y_h+(1-e^{-R_h})z_h].
An instrument splits a parent into children with unchanged R_h. The direct-sum channel data processing inequality contracts y and z separately, so V cannot increase. A collision of charge c changes R to R-c and (y,z) to (y+t,z-t), where t<=(1-e^{-c})z. Its potential change is
  e^{-R}[e^c t-(e^c-1)z]<=0.
Initially V=(1-e^{-C_*})K. Finally all remaining-budget coefficients are nonnegative, so sum_h y_h<=V. This proves (5.2), including stopping early.

If every action is <=a_max, with q_max=4a_max^4<1, and EVERY history has sum_j a_{j,h}^4<=A_*, then
  C_*<=kappa A_*, kappa=4/(1-q_max),
so one may take
  b=exp[-kappa A_*].                               (5.3)
This follows from -log(1-x)<=x/(1-q_max) for x<=q_max. A q=1 branch gives an infinite charge and a trivial bound. The law uses a worst-case path budget. An expected-action version is not claimed: quadratic information weight need not follow ordinary baseline branch probabilities.

6. ARBITRARY-REFERENCE CHANNEL BOUND
For arbitrary rho_SR set rho_R=Tr_S rho_SR and
  X_0=rho_SR-I_S/d tensor rho_R,
  omega_0=I_S/d tensor rho_R.
Then P X_0=0 and
  ||X_0||_{omega_0}^2<=d^2-1.                       (6.1)
Proof: source-basis pinching gives rho_SR<=d I_S tensor rho_R=d^2 omega_0. Therefore rho_SR omega_0^{-1}rho_SR<=d^2 rho_SR. Expand the centered norm to get Tr(rho_SR^2 omega_0^{-1})-1<=d^2-1. Appending fixed initial receiver memory leaves this norm unchanged.

At the final time tracing out S has exactly the norm of P X. Equations (2.2),(4.4) show, for every reference and input state,
  ||(id_R tensor C)(rho_SR)-rho_R tensor sigma||_1
      <=sqrt[(d^2-1)alpha].
The state characterization of diamond distance for a difference of channels gives
  delta(C,Tr(.)sigma)<=min{1,0.5 sqrt[(d^2-1)alpha]}. (6.2)
This is reference-uniform already; there is no Choi-to-diamond dimension multiplier.

7. ALL-DECODER CONVERSE, WITH THE SHARP DIMENSION NORMALIZATION
Let Phi_d be normalized maximally entangled on A S, and set
  rho_AB=(id_A tensor C)(Phi_d), omega=I_A/d tensor sigma,
  E=||rho_AB-omega||_omega^2<=(d^2-1)alpha,
  p=d^{-2}, c=1-p.
For any CPTP decoder R:B->S', define
  X_R=(id_A tensor R^dagger)(Phi_d).
Unital complete positivity gives 0<=X_R<=I. Also
  Tr_A X_R=I_B/d and Tr(omega X_R)=p.
Its entanglement fidelity is F_R=Tr(rho_AB X_R). Weighted Cauchy--Schwarz, centered at pI, gives
  F_R-p<=sqrt(E) sqrt[Tr omega(X_R-pI)^2]
       <=sqrt(E p(1-p))<=c sqrt(alpha),             (7.1)
because X_R^2<=X_R. Thus
  F_R<=p+c sqrt(alpha).                             (7.2)
Testing the decoded Choi state with the maximally entangled projector yields
  delta(R C,id)>=1-F_R
      >=c(1-sqrt(alpha))
       =c(1-sqrt(1-b))>=c b/2.                     (7.3)
This improves the root note's uncentered Barnum--Knill expression 1-sqrt(1-cb), recovers the exact best constant-channel obstruction c when b=1, and has the same small-b leading coefficient cb/2.

Under a pathwise quartic action budget A_* with q_max<1,
  delta_opt(C):=inf_R delta(R C,id)
      >=(c/2) exp[-kappa A_*].                      (7.4)
Therefore target half-diamond error epsilon<c/2 requires
  A_*>=(1-q_max)/4 log[c/(2epsilon)].                (7.5)
The sharper (7.3) applies away from the high-accuracy regime. Nothing in this proof infers export or recovery from the reduced source channel. Whether that source has relaxed is irrelevant.

8. PETZ NORMALIZATION, RLD COMPARISON, AND EXISTING RECOVERY INEQUALITIES
This section applies to any finite-dimensional channel C, independently of the collision architecture.

On sigma's support the transpose/Petz decoder for input I/d is
  D(Y)=C^dagger(sigma^{-1/2}Y sigma^{-1/2})/d.         (8.1)
It is CP, and Tr D(Y)=Tr(Pi_sigma Y). Complete it to a TP map off that support by mapping the unused block to a fixed state. All C outputs lie in this support, and D(sigma)=I/d. Its exact entanglement fidelity is
  F_P=F_e(D C,I/d)
     =(1/d)Tr[rho_AB(I tensor sigma^{-1/2})
                     rho_AB(I tensor sigma^{-1/2})]. (8.2)
This follows by computing (id tensor D^dagger)(Phi_d), whose supported block is (I tensor sigma^{-1/2})rho_AB(I tensor sigma^{-1/2})/d.

For Hermitian Z and positive sigma,
  Tr[Z(I tensor sigma^{-1/2})Z(I tensor sigma^{-1/2})]
      <=Tr[Z^2(I tensor sigma^{-1})].                (8.3)
In sigma's eigenbasis this is 1/sqrt(lambda_i lambda_j)<=(1/lambda_i+1/lambda_j)/2, summed against |Z_ij|^2 with reference indices included. Expansion of the centered Choi energy gives
  d Tr[rho_AB^2(I tensor sigma^{-1})]=1+E.
Therefore
  F_P<=(1+E)/d^2<=p+c alpha=1-cb.                   (8.4)
For comparison with the root basis calculation: if Hermitian F_mu obey Tr F_mu F_nu=d delta_mu, the fidelity equals d^{-4}sum_mu Tr[C(F_mu)sigma^{-1/2}C(F_mu)sigma^{-1/2}]. The identity contribution is precisely d^{-2}.

A self-contained proof of the needed Barnum--Knill bound is short. Define
  A=(I tensor sigma^{-1/4})rho_AB(I tensor sigma^{-1/4}),
  B=(I tensor sigma^{1/4})X_R(I tensor sigma^{1/4}),
  S=I tensor sigma^{1/2}.
Then Tr A^2=d F_P and Tr AB=F_R. Since X_R<=I,
  Tr B^2=Tr X_R S X_R S<=Tr X_R S^2=1/d.
Cauchy--Schwarz gives
  F_R^2<=F_P.                                      (8.5)
This verifies the singleton-state convention directly.

For the stronger centered inequality put A_0=I_A/d tensor sigma^{1/2}. Direct calculation gives
  Tr A_0^2=Tr A A_0=1/d, Tr A_0 B=1/d^2,
  ||A-A_0||_2^2=d(F_P-p),
  ||B-A_0/d||_2^2<=c/d.
Thus
  |F_R-p|<=sqrt[c(F_P-p)],                         (8.6)
equivalently
  F_P>=F_R^2+(1-F_R)^2/(d^2-1).                   (8.7)
For the optimal decoder F_opt>=p, hence
  F_opt<=p+sqrt[c(F_P-p)].                         (8.8)
Combining (8.8) with (8.4) recovers (7.2).

Equation (8.7) is exactly Joseph M. Renes, Better bounds on optimal measurement and entanglement recovery, with applications to uncertainty and monogamy relations, Physical Review A 96,042328 (2017), Eq.(13), DOI10.1103/PhysRevA.96.042328, https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.01114 . That paper gives a Bell-diagonal equality example. The simpler square bound is inherited Barnum--Knill machinery. Neither inequality is a new claim here; the proof fixes all conventions and avoids reliance on an unchecked recovery citation.

9. INDEPENDENT MODULES, WITH ARBITRARY COLLECTIVE FINAL DECODING
Assume the emission channel is exactly C=C_1 tensor ... tensor C_m, with source dimensions d_i>=2. Each C_i may use its own local classical feedback and memory. Cross-module active controls/shared memories are excluded unless the final emission channel remains a product. Arbitrary joint coherent processing and decoding after emission is allowed. Inputs can be arbitrarily entangled across modules and with a reference.

Let p_i=d_i^{-2}, c_i=1-p_i and let b_i be any valid residual bound from Sections 4 or 5. Then
  F_{P,i}<=u_i=1-c_i b_i.
The baseline is sigma=tensor_i sigma_i, and the Petz map factors on its support as tensor_i D_i. The normalized maximally entangled test state factors too, so
  F_P(C)=product_i F_{P,i}<=U=product_i(1-c_i b_i).  (9.1)
Every collective decoder therefore obeys
  delta_opt(C)>=1-sqrt U.                          (9.2)
For total input dimension D=product_i d_i and p_D=D^{-2}, the sharper bound is
  delta_opt(C)>=1-p_D-sqrt[(1-p_D)(U-p_D)].         (9.3)
Since U>=p_D the square root is defined. These are whole-register diamond bounds, not a sum of presumed individual decoding errors.

For target half-diamond error epsilon<1, a necessary condition is
  sum_i c_i b_i<=eta_epsilon,
  eta_epsilon=-2 log(1-epsilon).                    (9.4)
Indeed (9.2) requires U>=(1-epsilon)^2, while U<=exp[-sum_i c_i b_i]. With identical source dimensions and b_i=e^{-C_i}, Jensen gives
  sum_i C_i>=m log[c m/eta_epsilon].                (9.5)
Replace a negative right-hand side by zero. For heterogeneous dimensions one can replace c by the geometric mean of the c_i, using arithmetic--geometric mean.

If each module has a pathwise quartic action budget A_i, every action is <=a_max with q_max=4a_max^4<1, and kappa=4/(1-q_max), take b_i=e^{-kappa A_i}. Then
  sum_i A_i>=(m/kappa)log[c m/eta_epsilon].          (9.6)
Since eta_epsilon=2epsilon+O(epsilon^2), total quartic action is Omega(m log(m/epsilon)). This does not apply to an arbitrary architecture with cross-module adaptive feedback: such feedback can invalidate (9.1).

10. EXACT PARTIAL-SWAP CONSTRUCTION MATCHES THE REGISTER SCALING
Let every fresh ancilla in one module have dimension d, and apply
  U_j=cos(theta_j)I+i sin(theta_j)W_{S,E_j},
where W is SWAP. Set e=d^N and let U be the full circuit. Retain all bath outputs coherently. Then
  C(X)=Tr_S[U(X tensor I_E/e)U^dagger], sigma=I_E/e,
and the Petz decoder is the physical reverse circuit
  D(Y)=Tr_E[U^dagger(I_S/d tensor Y)U].             (10.1)
It needs one independently maximally mixed dummy source and reversal of the known collision circuit, but no inaccessible bath purifier. Physical inverse implementations of Petz maps for product-preserving reference states are existing machinery (see the principal constructor's prior-art discussion of Aw et al., PRX Quantum 5,010332 (2024)); the identity below supplies the exact acquired-information clock for this collision family.

The exact identity retaining every output correlation is
  D C=(1-r)id+r R_{I/d},
  r=product_j(1-sin^4 theta_j).                     (10.2)
Self-contained proof: use normalized Hilbert--Schmidt inner products Tr X^dagger Y/d on source operators and Tr X^dagger Y/(de) on source-bath operators. Define the isometry
  J(X)=U(X tensor I_E)U^dagger
and the orthogonal projection P(Z)=I_S/d tensor Tr_S Z. For every old source-traceless deviation Z, direct partial-SWAP expansion gives
  P Ad_{U_j}(Z tensor I_{E_j})
      =sin^2(theta_j) I_S tensor Z_{S->E_j}.
The zeroth term has zero source trace, and the commutator has zero source trace by (4.1). Relabeling S as E_j is an isometry in normalized Hilbert--Schmidt norm. Thus the transferred squared norm is exactly sin^4(theta_j)||Z||_2^2. This transfer is orthogonal to the old P component, which has identity on the new ancilla. The remaining hidden squared norm is multiplied by 1-sin^4(theta_j). Induction gives
  ||P J(X)||_2^2=(1-r)||X||_2^2
for every traceless X. Polarization shows J^dagger P J=(1-r)I on that subspace. The identity operator is fixed and orthogonal to it. Since
  J^dagger(Y)=Tr_E[U^dagger Y U]/e,
one has exactly J^dagger P J=D C, with (10.1)'s normalization. This proves (10.2). Arbitrary known source-only unitaries between rounds preserve the recursion and are reversed by D.

For independent partial-SWAP modules define their residuals r_i and
  V=product_i(1-c_i r_i).
The explicit reverse product decoder has exact half-diamond error
  delta_reverse=1-V.                               (10.3)
A d-dimensional depolarizing channel (1-r)id+rR is a generalized-Weyl mixture with identity probability 1-c r and nonidentity probabilities r/d^2. The product's identity probability is V. A product maximally entangled input yields an orthogonal Bell mixture and half trace distance 1-V; the triangle inequality gives the same channel upper bound. This proves the equality, not merely an entanglement-fidelity bound.

Because this reverse decoder is exactly Petz, every collective final decoder satisfies
  1-sqrt V<=delta_opt<=1-V.                         (10.4)
The reverse decoder is therefore within factor 1+sqrt V<=2 of optimal half-diamond error. Equation (9.3) supplies a tighter finite-dimension lower bound. No efficient implementation of a generic abstract Petz map is asserted: efficiency here comes from the known reverse collision circuit.

For m identical modules, constant angle 0<|theta|<pi/2, and c=1-d^{-2}, set
  lambda_theta=-log(1-sin^4 theta), r=e^{-N lambda_theta}.
A half-diamond target epsilon has the collision-count bounds
  N>=log[c m/eta_epsilon]/lambda_theta              (necessary),
  N>=log[c m/epsilon]/lambda_theta                 (sufficient, rounded up).
The necessary condition follows from (9.4); the sufficient one follows from 1-(1-c r)^m<=m c r. Negative necessary counts are replaced by zero. These agree in the coefficient of log(m/epsilon). Since lambda_theta=theta^4+O(theta^6) as theta->0,
  N=Theta(theta^{-4} log(m/epsilon)) per module,
and total quartic action is Theta(m log(m/epsilon)), matching the universal bound (9.6) up to constants.

11. FULL EXECUTION COSTS, CLOCK LIMITS, AND IMPERFECTIONS
For fixed d the construction uses mN source-bath gates for emission, mN inverse gates for recovery, mN retained d-level bath systems, and m maximally mixed d-level dummy sources. Coherent memory and circuit size are linear in mN, conditional on the supplied pairwise collision and inverse-gate interface. Routing, storage faults, and synthesizing that interface from a particular platform are separate costs. All retained output correlations are used; independent measurements of individual outputs are not the decoder.

A partial-SWAP gate has action |theta|. If its Hamiltonian norm is bounded by Omega, it takes at least |theta|/Omega. With m modules operated in parallel, each forward or reverse pass takes at least N|theta|/Omega, or Theta(Omega^{-1}|theta|^{-3}log(m/epsilon)) in the weak-angle regime. Fully serial operation multiplies elapsed time by m.

A formal Markov scaling theta^2 approximately gamma Delta with collision interval Delta holds the reduced-source relaxation rate nonzero as Delta->0, but requires peak coupling of order sqrt(gamma/Delta). At bounded Omega, theta<=Omega Delta instead implies theta^2/Delta<=Omega^2 Delta->0. Strength and clock costs must accompany this limit.

For fixed finite d and sum_j a_j^4->0, (6.2) proves convergence of the entire accessible output channel to a constant-output channel. A separately specified reduced-source channel can simultaneously have a nontrivial relaxation limit. No conclusion about accessibility follows solely from source forgetting. A pure Stinespring field or an accessible purifier is a different interface.

If actual emission C_tilde has half-diamond distance at most nu from ideal C, then
  delta_opt(C_tilde)>=delta_opt(C)-nu.
A specified ideal decoder's upper error changes by at most nu, plus that decoder's implementation error. Distance from C_tilde to its OWN constant-output baseline is at most (6.2)'s bound plus 2nu. Independent bath-preparation and gate errors telescope using their actual half trace/diamond errors. Bounds on local bath marginals alone do not control correlated bath preparation errors. If each of 2mN implemented gates has adversarial half-diamond error <=zeta, the crude uniform contribution is <=2mN zeta. Error correction or improved error models can improve this, but are not supplied for free.

12. CONTRIBUTION AND ATTRIBUTION BOUNDARY
The interface-specific construction is the weighted information-transfer recursion with arbitrary receiver processing, genuine classical feedback, and branch-dependent pathwise action budgets. Its fourth-order action scaling follows from zero first-order source-to-bath transfer together with orthogonal accumulation in the correct weighted norm. The exact partial-SWAP Gram recursion supplies a matching clock, and the inverse circuit provides an executable decoder. Product emission structure plus recovery-fidelity multiplicativity yields the independent-register m log(m/epsilon) law.

Weighted chi-square/RLD data processing, the Petz map, Barnum--Knill near-optimality, Renes's sharper inequality, physical Petz inversion under product-preserving reference states, and Weyl-channel diamond calculations are inherited machinery or elementary instances of it. The new consequence is obtained by connecting those tools to the stated mixed-output acquisition interface; source forgetting is never used as a recovery certificate.
