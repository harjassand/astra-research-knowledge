# N1825 — Finite exact-integer grid sampler and contextual coherence removal

[Canonical card](https://github.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/blob/main/cards/N1825-exact-integer-grid-sampling-and-contextual-coherence-removal.txt) · [Scoped status](https://github.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/blob/main/frontier/cards/N1825-exact-integer-grid-sampling-and-contextual-coherence-removal.json)

The supplied package constructs an output-equivalent removal of bridge-Q coherence, a binary-coloop generalization and an implemented exact-integer conditional-component sampler for its specified noisy grid circuit. At 32 by 32 sites, 100 matching layers, r=3/100 and epsilon=1/50, its frozen 1000-draw holdout reports zero resource aborts and a one-sided 99% upper abort bound 919/200000=.004595. Conditional on the mathematical reduction and ideal unbiased random bits, fixed fallback has full-output TV at most abort probability. This is finite-setting statistical evidence, not deterministic certification, independent verification or polynomial scaling. Supplied matched direct MPS is faster on the five favorable-rate records; the harder rate remains unsolved.

## Proof and scope

[Complete supplied source](../2026-11-13-exact-finite-grid-sampler/README_PUBLIC_PATHS.md)

The bridge identity preserves final Z-output plus reset-record law, not local quantum channels or arbitrary intermediate conditional states. Preserve parallel occurrences and all classical feedforward. Diagonal gates can couple different cycle blocks; neither block count nor cycle rank certifies an efficient full sampler. Tiny exact checks do not verify every implementation path. The favorable-rate 99% bound is statistical and assumes the source protocol, fixed algorithm and independent ideal trials; the supplied PRNG experiment is distinct from ideal unbiased randomness. At epsilon=1/20 all twenty 1024-site pilot draws abort, and five-record matched exact/MPS trials abort five/four times. No speed superiority, general noisy-circuit result, polynomial tail theorem, hardware advance or novelty claim.
