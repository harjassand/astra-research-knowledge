# N1828 — Remote acoustic writing: renewable chemical contrast and capture before activation

[Canonical card](https://github.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/blob/main/cards/N1828-remote-acoustic-material-conversion-and-capture-contracts.txt) · [Scoped status](https://github.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/blob/main/frontier/cards/N1828-remote-acoustic-material-conversion-and-capture-contracts.json)

Two separate ROOT models give a conditional renewable pre-conversion barrier and a staged two-frequency capture/activation route. They preserve full-history stocks, leakage and finite capture-basin limits. Neither model constructs a compatible formulation, actual particle-to-product lock or useful opaque-housing manufacturing service.
