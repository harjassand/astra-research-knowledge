# N1824 — current mathematical operations

ROOT gives a conditional conductance-difference certificate after physically opening only diagnostic contact B while retaining the same part and functional A contact. A state-dependent shunt counterexample shows why that independent opening and shunt-change bound must be physically established. Existing Kelvin contactor constructions are credited at their own scope. No fixture, installed uncertainty, qualified microcontact or economical production system is supplied.

The certificate assumes invariant or independently bounded shunt conductance during B opening. Motion of contamination, A-load/frame changes or unverified conductor separation can violate it. Counterexamples concern the specified linear ports, not every sensor. Full paired-contact qualification, tooling, wear, service recovery and production advantage remain open.

Construct independent B opening, positive part/A-load retention, contamination-motion bounds, nonperturbing installed calibration and function after all tools withdraw. Publication does not authorize experiments, source recovery or a new audit.
