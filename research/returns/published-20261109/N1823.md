# N1823 — Local heating and reset: terminal ambiguity, cooling and functional recovery

[Canonical card](https://github.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/blob/main/cards/N1823-local-heating-electrical-reset-and-functional-recovery.txt) · [Scoped status](https://github.com/harjassand/astra-research-knowledge/blob/main/frontier/cards/N1823-local-heating-electrical-reset-and-functional-recovery.json)

ROOT credits an existing puncture-created liquid-metal heater that seals a depressurized actuator and electrically resets damage indication. Independent introduced circuits have identical terminal histories and opening while their wound temperatures differ; optional real local-voltage taps would distinguish that pair. A finite-gas calculation shows that warming and compliance can hide a nominal leak signal below endpoint uncertainty. These are scoped model results, not a new working repair device or an all-sensor impossibility theorem. Electrical reset, pressure observation, cooling, useful force and repeatable life remain separate.

The terminal-only counterexample depends on its stated ohmic and thermal assumptions. Optional taps require actual surviving wiring and calibration; local power still does not prove repair. The closure law is not measured polymer kinetics and cooldown is not a prescribed readiness limit. Surrogate holes are not calibrated cracks. Real creep, force, fatigue, adhesion and stock renewal remain unresolved. The existing source already combines detection, heating, sealing and electrical reset. No new material, sensor algorithm, working device, whole-cost advantage or all-sensor obstruction is established.
