## 3. The theorem

For a set \(R\) of \(w\) source sites, define the transverse-sector projector

\[
\mathcal P_R
=
\prod_{i\in R}\frac{\mathrm{id}-\mathcal Z_i}{2}.
\]

It retains operator components that are odd under every \(Z_i\) in \(R\). A Pauli product transverse on those sites is an example. GHZ off-diagonal coherence is another.

Let \(\mathcal N\) be the eventual acquired-output channel, including every retained quantum system and classical outcome, and set

\[
A(t)=\int_0^t\Omega(u)\,du.
\]

### All-strength acquisition bound

For any selected \(t>0\) satisfying \(kA(t)\le 1\),

\[
\boxed{
\|\mathcal N\mathcal P_R\|_\diamond
\le
\left[
e^{-2\lambda t}
+
(1-e^{-2\lambda t})(kA(t))^{1/k}
\right]^w.
}
\]

The diamond norm makes this a statement that remains valid in the presence of an arbitrary reference. The bound is uniform in eventual acquisition duration, apparatus size, subsequent computation, and the allowed feedback.

For a constant peak envelope \(\Omega>0\), choose

\[
t=\frac{1}{2k\Omega}.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\|\mathcal N\mathcal P_R\|_\diamond\le q_k^w,
\qquad
q_k=
e^{-\lambda/(k\Omega)}
+
\left(1-e^{-\lambda/(k\Omega)}\right)2^{-1/k}<1.
}
\]

The strict inequality holds for **every finite positive \(\Omega/\lambda\)**. There is no weak-control threshold in this statement.

The report also proves a sharper coefficient under a global Hamiltonian-decomposition budget. That stronger coefficient is kept separate from the more permissive model with only branchwise classical-feedback budgets.

### Why the proof works

The useful representation is a collection of **first-dephasing deadlines**.

First, write

\[
E_i=\frac{\mathrm{id}+\mathcal Z_i}{2},
\qquad
\lambda(\mathcal Z_i-\mathrm{id})
=
2\lambda(E_i-\mathrm{id}).
\]

The actual Markov noise is therefore exactly representable as independent rate-\(2\lambda\) Poisson clocks applying full dephasing maps \(E_i\), interleaved with control.

These clocks are mathematical variables used in the proof. Their records are not supplied to the reader.

Fix a clock trajectory on \([0,t]\). Let \(S\subseteq R\) be the sites hit at least once, and let \(\tau_i\) be their first hit times. A transverse component that remains unresolved at site \(i\) is killed at \(\tau_i\).

For unresolved sites \(U\subseteq S\), remove all paid terms touching \(U\) until the earliest deadline

\[
\tau_U=\min_{i\in U}\tau_i.
\]

The remaining evolution preserves every unresolved parity. Its contribution is consequently killed at that deadline. Restoring the removed interactions through an exact Duhamel expansion gives a recurrence of the form

\[
F_U(u)
\le
\sum_{a:B_a\cap U\ne\varnothing}
\int_u^{\tau_U}
h_a(v)\,
F_{U\setminus B_a}(v)\,dv,
\qquad
h_a(v)=2\|H_a(v)\|.
\]

Each insertion can resolve at most \(k\) sites. Everything else remains inside physical, trace-norm-contracting propagators.

Put

\[

\[
\sum_a j_a h_a(v)\le s\Omega(v),

\[
\frac{b^{(s-j_a)/k}}{j_a}

\[
s\Omega(v)b(v)^{s/k-1}
=
-\frac{d}{dv}b(v)^{s/k}.

\|\Phi_\omega(t)\mathcal P_R\|_\diamond
\le (kA(t))^{|S|/k}.

The classical-feedback extension uses the direct-sum trace norm

\[
\sum_m\|X_m\|_1

over retained records. The budget is applied **within the current branch before summing branches**. Support choices enter at their physical insertion times. This chronological structure avoids using a future record to justify an earlier control choice.

### Complete calibration of the local binary measurements

Scalar visibility alone does not certify the whole local measurement channel.

Write its positive-outcome effect as

\[
E=c_0I+\boldsymbol c\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma.
\]

Six trusted Pauli eigenstates determine its coefficients. If the measured probabilities are known to accuracy \(u\), the resulting operator-norm uncertainty is at most

\[
(1+\sqrt3)u.

For a binary measurement channel, diamond error is at most twice the effect’s operator-norm error. Thus the full local measured channel can be estimated and certified to tolerance \(\eta_{\mathrm{loc}}\), with a constant-factor margin, using

\right)
