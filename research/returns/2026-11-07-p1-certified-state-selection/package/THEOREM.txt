FINITE PREPARATION AND RETENTION OF ACQUIRED COLLECTIVE ORDER
7 November 2026, Australia/Brisbane

This is a complete mathematical write/retain operation for the class specified
below. It is not an unrestricted control or global-optimization theorem. The
implemented experiment is a paid finite software realization, not a physical
or hard-real-time demonstration. No priority claim is made for classical
control ingredients or for their synthesis here.

0. THE QUESTION RESOLVED

Given independently acquired stationary dissipative components, a prescribed
collective port pattern, a certified nearby equilibrium of a later conservative
interaction, a declared uncertain preparation, and a calibrated sampled reader
and first-order actuator, construct a finite procedure which (i) enters a
certified basin of that equilibrium, and (ii) remains within a prescribed tube
for any requested finite retention duration. Count every acquisition,
initialization, read, computation, command, actuation time, amplitude, slew and
energy term. Competing stable states may remain present in the retention law.

The procedure is a temporary globally stable write drive, followed by an
explicitly controlled handover to the original nonlinear retention law. Its
proof does not require observing hidden states or finding a path through the
basins of the unchanged nonlinear system. The ability to program the total
port drive, including replacing the retention law during writing, is a real
actuation premise. A permanently hardwired force that cannot be switched or
overridden is outside this implementation contract.

1. FINITE INPUT AND ACCESS CONTRACT

Work on a real Hilbert space X, possibly infinite-dimensional. The source is

    x' = -H x + B v,                y = B* x.                         (1)

H is stationary, self-adjoint, H >= a I, a>0. B:R^n -> X is a fixed normalized
bounded port map, B*B=I, ran B subset Dom H. Let P=BB*, Q=I-P. In port/hidden
coordinates write H in blocks A,V*,V,D. The supplied numerical bounds are

    ||A|| <= A0,       D >= aI,       ||V||^2 <= c0.                  (2)

These are warranted model assumptions, not conclusions learned by fitting the
finite histories. Known orthogonal gauges of port signs do not change norms.
The already-earned acquisition operation provides rational S_hat and E>=0 with

    S = A - V* D^{-1} V,    ||S-S_hat|| <= E,
    a I <= S <= A0 I.                                                (3)

All source uncertainty, instrument errors and acquisition approximation
residuals used to obtain E remain included. Nothing here replaces a general
SDP proof by the smaller LP used in the predecessor execution. Direct sums of
independently acquired sources have a=min a_i, A0=max A0_i, c0=max c0_i and
E=max E_i. No hidden dimension appears in these norm estimates. Any more
general assembly must separately carry its dynamical bounds; a static Schur
complement alone does not warrant (2).

An ideal desired retention law F:R^n -> R^n is finitely specified. In the
executed family it is the rational polynomial

    F(y)=K y - beta .* y^3 + h,     K=K^T, beta_i>0.                 (4)

The accepted ordering calculation supplies a rational reference p (with any
known sign gauge), a true equilibrium q_* of S q_*=F(q_*), an upper bound
epsilon_* on ||q_*-p||, and a convex port neighborhood O containing that
equilibrium. The source is not asked for q_*; only its certified enclosure is
used. For the attractive/balanced construction, q_* is the unique global
ordered equilibrium selected by the positive field. In more general laws the
operation prepares the certified intended stable equilibrium; global
optimality requires its own certificate.

Required, checkable local data are:

  * S - DF(y) >= m I throughout O, for every allowed S, with m>0;
    for a non-gradient extension replace DF by its symmetric part;
  * ||DF(y)|| <= L on O enlarged by the measurement-error ball;
  * a full-state radius r>0 such that ||x-x_*||<=r implies B*x in O,
    where x_* is the unknown full equilibrium lifting q_*;
  * a rational bound C_F >= ||F(p)|| and a finite uniform algorithm that
    evaluates F on the measured region to error epsilon_c at its charged
    bit cost. Here this is a polynomial evaluated by integer arithmetic.

A cubic instance obtains these data directly from the retained pattern
formula. In gauge coordinates q, let p_i>0 and

    T_hat=S_hat-K=L_p+diag(f_i/p_i-beta_i p_i^2),   L_p>=0.

For |q_i-p_i|<=theta_b p_i,

    m = min_i [f_i/p_i + beta_i(3(1-theta_b)^2-1)p_i^2] - E.         (5)

If the retained equilibrium enclosure is |q_*i-p_i|<=theta_e p_i,
take epsilon_* = theta_e ||p|| and
    r <= (theta_b-theta_e) min_i p_i.
All square roots used in actual certificates are rational upper bounds.
This m is sufficient, not asserted to be the best possible curvature bound.

Uncertain preparation is only

    ||x(0)|| <= R0,       ||v(0)|| <= V0.                           (6)

No hidden-state measurement, exact reset, initialized equilibrium, or zero
actuator state is assumed. The reader returns y(t_k)+nu_k, ||nu_k||<=nu.
Its error includes calibration, quantization and numerical source residuals.
Readings are synchronous vectors, or nu also includes the effect of the
individual coordinates' time skew. Synchronization is an instrument premise.
Neither independence nor a zero mean is assumed. It does not average away.

The actual actuator, present during both phases, obeys

    tau v' = -v + c(t) + e_a(t),     ||e_a(t)|| <= zeta, tau>0.      (7)

c(t) is a held digital command. tau, force calibration and its error bounds
are supplied, including any relative-gain error converted using the certified
command range. All drive channels share the stated scalar tau. This is a
specified actuator class, not an assertion about arbitrary hardware. There is
no saturation on the proved command/force ranges; a device with smaller
capacity fails the contract. Commands can jump; actual v is continuous and
has a finite slew bound derived below. The actuator state is not reset at
handover.

The timestamp, sample-spacing and command-latency specifications give a bound
h on the age of the newest applied reading. They also give a positive minimum
sample interval h_min for counting calls. A physical realization must finish
the bounded controller work before its stated deadline; a simulation running
in virtual source time does not establish this hardware property. Clock rate
and unit calibration must be propagated into actual source-time bounds. If
a phase has duration in [T_minus,T_plus], use T_minus in its decay guarantee
and T_plus in the time, energy and call-count bill. The displayed execution
uses exact virtual phase durations; it does not assume a free exact physical
clock. A physical stop rule may extend a phase until its lower duration bound
reaches the required time, charging the resulting upper duration.

The input also contains a requested finite retention duration H_ret, desired
dynamic error delta, all relevant costs and calibration constants. Rationals
are encoded with their full binary lengths. Arbitrarily refinable precision
is used only when the actual source/reader/actuator specification warrants it.
Fixed native noise and fixed bandwidth can prevent the inequalities below.

2. TWO HIDDEN-STATE ESTIMATES FROM THE ACQUIRED INTERFACE

The full equilibrium with port value y is

    LIFT y = B y - Q D^{-1} V y,
    ||LIFT|| <= ell := sqrt(1+c0/a^2).                             (8)

The port time derivative has the dimension-independent estimate

    y' = -(HB)*x + v,
    ||HB|| <= b_H := sqrt(A0^2+c0).                               (9)

The Dom H port assumption justifies the identity even when the hidden source
generator is unbounded. Mild solutions with arbitrary finite-norm initial
states satisfy the associated integral inequalities; the energy calculations
can equivalently be obtained by bounded-generator regularization and passage
to the limit. No bounded global source frequency is needed.

A local port curvature bound m yields a full-state contraction rate. Choose a
rational alpha with 0<alpha<a and

    (m-alpha)(a-alpha) >= alpha c0/a.                             (10)

Such an alpha exists for every m>0. Indeed, subtract alpha I from the full
Hessian. Its hidden block D-alpha I is positive, and its port Schur complement
is bounded below by

    m-alpha - alpha V* D^{-1}(D-alpha I)^{-1} V
       >= m-alpha - alpha c0/[a(a-alpha)] >=0.

Consequently, in the certified region the ideal drift -Hx+BF(B*x) contracts
toward x_* at rate alpha in the full-state norm. With actuator mismatch
d_v=v-F(y) and w=||x-x_*||,

    D^+ w <= -alpha w + ||d_v||.                                (11)

The ordinary differential inequality and its integrated form also hold at
w=0. This is local strong monotonicity, not a claim that the multistable
retention dynamics is globally contracting.

3. A COMPLETE HANDOVER AND RETENTION THEOREM

Suppose at the handover time, relabeled t=0, certified bounds are

    w(0)<=w0,          ||v(0)-F(y(0))||<=D0.                       (12)

Let

    R_h = ell (||p||+epsilon_*) + r,
    F_h = C_F + L(epsilon_*+r+nu),
    V_h >= max{the actuator norm at handover, F_h+epsilon_c+zeta},
    M_y >= b_H R_h+V_h.                                          (13)

If a reading used by the first command predates handover, include its preceding
write interval in the derivative bound M_y and verify that the corresponding
port values lie in O. The constant-write procedure below gives this check
directly. Continue writing while the first retention command is computed; the
actuator is never given an uninitialized or stale unbounded command.

Define

    d_inf = L nu + epsilon_c + zeta + L M_y (h+tau),
    b = d_inf/alpha,                  A = w0+2 tau D0.             (14)

THEOREM. If

    alpha tau <= 1/2,             A+b < r,                        (15)

then every allowed source, preparation satisfying (12), measurement sequence,
actuator error, and timing sequence stays in the full-state basin for all
times during which the channel contract is maintained. In particular,

    ||x(t)-x_*|| <= A exp(-alpha t) + b.                          (16)

For any delta>b, choose a finite T_set satisfying

    A exp(-alpha T_set)+b <= delta.                              (17)

Then for every t in [T_set,T_set+H_ret], the dynamic error is <=delta. Port
errors are <=epsilon_*+delta in Euclidean norm. In the cubic componentwise
enclosure they satisfy |q_i(t)-p_i|<=theta_e p_i+delta. This is a continuous-time
guarantee between readings, not only a bound on sampled states.

PROOF. Work up to a hypothetical first exit from the ball. On that interval
(13) bounds the command, hence (7) bounds actual v and (9) bounds y'. The
latest reading is at most h old, so its held command differs from F(y(t)) by
at most L nu+epsilon_c+L M_y h. Differentiating d_v almost everywhere gives

    tau d_v' = -d_v + [c-F(y)] + e_a - tau DF(y)y'.

The stable actuator filter therefore gives
    ||d_v(t)|| <= D0 exp(-t/tau)+d_inf.
Inserting this into (11), integrating, and using alpha tau<=1/2 gives

    w(t) <= w0 exp(-alpha t)
      + D0 (exp(-alpha t)-exp(-t/tau))/(1/tau-alpha)
      + (d_inf/alpha)(1-exp(-alpha t))
      <= A exp(-alpha t)+b < r.

This contradicts the first exit. Bounded input and state on each finite
interval supply continuation of the mild source/actuator trajectory. The
sample times have a positive minimum spacing, so no finite-time accumulation
of switches occurs. This proves invariance, the transient and retention.

The proof does not use global equilibration or absence of other attractors.
It also shows exactly how an imperfectly aligned actuator can safely cross
the handover: its contribution is the finite term 2 tau D0, not zero.

4. GLOBAL PREPARATION BY A TEMPORARY CONSTANT DRIVE

Let c_w be a finite-bit rounding of S_hat p, with

    rho_w >= ||c_w-S_hat p||,       C_w >= ||c_w||,
    e_w = (E||p||+rho_w)/a.                                       (18)

During writing set the digital command c(t)=c_w. The true write equilibrium
is x_w=H^{-1}B c_w=LIFT S^{-1}c_w. It need not be known by the controller.

Its distance from the intended full equilibrium obeys

    ||x_w-x_*|| <= ell(epsilon_*+e_w) =: Delta_w.                  (19)

For a tau<=1/2, the actual uncertain preparation and actual actuator satisfy

    ||x(T)-x_w|| <= M_w exp(-aT)+zeta/a =: xi(T),
    M_w=R0+C_w/a+2 tau(V0+C_w),
    ||v(T)-c_w|| <= (V0+C_w) exp(-T/tau)+zeta.                    (20)

PROOF. Solve the first-order actuator equation and use its decaying initial
error plus the persistent bound zeta. The source semigroup has norm at most
exp(-at). The convolution of exp(-at) and exp(-t/tau) is at most
2 tau exp(-at) when a tau<=1/2. Also ||x_w||<=C_w/a. This proves (20).
The inverse static law ||S^{-1}||<=1/a and (8) prove (18)-(19).

At a write duration T_w the handover bounds are

    w0 = xi(T_w)+Delta_w,
    D0 = (V0+C_w)exp(-T_w/tau)+zeta
          + ||c_w-F(p)|| + L[xi(T_w)+e_w].                        (21)

For the retained direct pattern formula F(p)=S_hat p, the middle norm is just
rho_w. More generally it is evaluated from the supplied law, not assumed zero.
The optional pre-handover reading uses the same xi(T) bound at
T=T_w-latency. This verifies the local reading region without measuring x.

All inputs during writing obey

    ||v||<=V_w=max(V0,C_w+zeta),
    ||x||<=R_w=max(R0,V_w/a),
    ||v'|| <= (V_w+C_w+zeta)/tau.                                (22)

No reader is needed during the constant write except the reading used to
prepare the first retention command. Any diagnostic observation is counted
separately. Writing is uniformly effective on the whole declared initial
state ball, not just a small set of convenient preparations.

The resulting finite algorithm checks (5), (10), (18)-(22), (13)-(15), then
computes a finite T_set using (17), loads the fixed-point controller, executes
the constant command for T_w, and executes the sampled retention law for
T_set+H_ret. Its output includes an explicit schedule, all budgets, and the
computed inequalities. For a fixed channel which fails the inequalities it
reports that this sufficient certificate failed; it does not claim a general
impossibility result.

5. OPTIONAL GLOBALLY STABLE FEEDBACK WRITE PRIMITIVE

When constant writing leaves too large a static mismatch, a second proved
primitive uses only port readings:

    c(y)=c0-k y,       c0=(S_hat+kI)p,       k>0.                  (23)

Its ideal equilibrium obeys
    y_w=(S+kI)^{-1}c0,
    ||y_w-p|| <= E||p||/(a+k),
    x_w=LIFT y_w,                 v_w=S y_w.
Coefficient rounding is included in the residual numerator as in (18).
This primitive replaces the programmable retention law during writing; it is
not a free cancellation of an unmodeled permanently active interaction.

Put e_x=x-x_w, e_v=v-v_w and
    W^2=||e_x||^2+(tau/k)||e_v||^2.
For ideal continuous feedback, the source/actuator cross terms cancel. If the
sampled read, command evaluation and actuator errors in the actuator equation
have combined norm at most epsilon_w, Young's inequality gives

    d(W^2)/dt <= -2 lambda W^2 + epsilon_w^2/k,
    lambda=min(a,1/(2tau)).

Hence
    W(T)^2 <= exp(-2lambda T) W(0)^2
                    + epsilon_w^2/(2k lambda).                   (24)

Every constant is finitely computable. For example bound x_w by
X_w=ell(||p||+E||p||/(a+k)), bound v_w by A0 times the same port bound, and
bound W(0)^2 using R0,V0. Choose a rational W_bar greater than W(0) and the
claimed noise floor. Within W<=W_bar the source norm is at most X_w+W_bar.
Bound the command by ||c0||+k(X_w+W_bar+nu)+epsilon_cw, and the actuator by
the maximum of V0 and that bound plus zeta. This gives M_yw through (9).
The actual finite sample condition is

    epsilon_w >= k nu + epsilon_cw + zeta + k M_yw h_w.            (25)

Equations (24)-(25) close the same first-exit argument. A sufficiently long
finite write yields W<=xi_w. The state is then within
    xi_w + ell(epsilon_*+E||p||/(a+k))
of the target full equilibrium. A handover bound can use either
    ||v-v_w||<=sqrt(k/tau) xi_w
or simply the certified actuator-amplitude bound. With either bound the
retention theorem applies unchanged.

For clarity, existence of finite specifications does not require an implicit
unbounded search. If mu=r-ell epsilon_*>0, select finite k so that
ell E||p||/(a+k)<mu/4, select a write-state budget xi_w<mu/8, and take a finite
global W_bar as above. Choose finite tau, read age and precision such that
(24)'s noise floor is below xi_w^2/2, 2tau D0<mu/8, and d_inf/alpha is below
min(mu/8,delta/2). With tau<=1/(2a), lambda=a. The amplitude bounds can first
use nu,epsilon_cw,zeta<=1 and therefore do not depend on subsequently shrinking
tau or the error budgets. All requirements are strict inequalities in finite
rational upper bounds; dyadic choices satisfy them whenever the channel is
actually refinable to those specifications. A finite duration makes the
exponential term smaller than xi_w^2/2. If a fixed device cannot supply the
resulting precision, slew, sampling or computation rate, this existence
argument makes no claim about that device. This general primitive is proved
and costed; the executed six-port case uses the simpler constant write.

6. A COMPETING ATTRACTOR IN THE SAME SIX-PORT FAMILY

For the supplied six-port pattern, theta_e=1/100, theta_b=1/5,
r=19/200, m>2.78319 and alpha=7/8. At the opposite nominal pattern -p the
port potential gradient has norm at most

    R_minus = E||p||+2||f|| < 0.095272.

In the Euclidean port ball of radius rho=1/10 about -p the Hessian is >=mI,
since the squared-amplitude bounds in (5) are identical on both sides.
On its boundary the radial derivative is at least
    rho(m rho-R_minus)>0.
The compact-ball minimum is therefore interior, unique in that ball, and
has positive Hessian. Its displacement from -p is at most R_minus/m<0.034231.
All of its coordinates in the sign gauge are negative, so sign reflection
lowers its energy strictly when every field f_i>0. It is a strict nonglobal
local minimum. Lifting gives a full equilibrium with norm <2.729; its actual
equilibrium drive has norm <3.433. Both are inside the declared preparation
bounds R0=V0=4.

This is also an operational trap, not only a critical-point count. Around
the negative equilibrium choose a full-state radius
    r_minus = rho-R_minus/m >0.065769.
The same sampled-actuator proof, starting at this equilibrium and its correct
actuator state, gives a continuing error floor below 0.006794. Thus without
the write phase the implemented retention law has a wrong-pattern invariant
neighborhood even under the declared continuing bounded errors. Initial
history can be the constant negative equilibrium for the first delayed read.
The positive write theorem covers this preparation along with every other
preparation in the declared balls. It therefore resolves actual basin
selection, while preserving the finding that ordinary retention dynamics
does not itself select the global state.

7. EXACTLY THE SAME STATIC LAW CAN HIDE ARBITRARILY SLOW WRITING

This is a scoped lower bound for release into a specified retention law, not
an impossibility of all actively controlled port tracking.

For 0<d<=1 set
    H_d=diag(d,2-d),
    B_d=(sqrt(d/2),sqrt(1-d/2))^T.
Then B_d*B_d=1 and
    B_d* H_d^{-1} B_d=1,        S_d=1 for every d.
Let t=1-d. Its port compression and coupling variance are
    A_d=1+t^2<=2,
    c_d=t^2(1-t^2)<=1/4.
Thus exact static impedance, leading normalization, A0=2 and c0=1/4 are
identical or uniformly bounded. Only a uniform lower decay bound is missing.

Start from x=0. Allow ANY write force satisfying |v|<=V, V>=1, for duration T.
Split the output into slow and fast parts y_s,y_f. At release,
    y_s(T)<=d V T/2,          y_f(T)<=V/2.
After release require the original ideal retention law
    v=F(y)=2y-y^3
and require y(t) in [0.9,1.1] at all subsequent times. On this interval
    0.869 <= F(y) <= 1.071.
At t0=log(5V) after release, using the fast decay 2-d>=1,
    y_f(T+t0)<=0.1+1.071/2=0.6355,
    y_s(T+t0)<=d[VT+1.071 t0]/2.
Retention therefore requires

    T >= 0.529/(d V) - 1.071 log(5V)/V.                          (26)

It diverges as d decreases even though the static ordered states +/-1 and
the exact static impedance remain unchanged. This rules out a uniform
static-only write-and-release time estimate at bounded force. It does not
rule out indefinite auxiliary active clamping, unbounded-force preparation,
or a controller which is supplied the missing dynamical information.

The positive operation uses precisely such declared dynamical information:
the decay prior pays for erasing uncertain hidden preparation. Its possible
dependence on 1/a is a real physical-time cost, potentially exponential in
the binary length of a small rational a. There is no polynomial bit/time
claim for arbitrary physical preparation encoded by such parameters.

8. FULL RESOURCE CONTRACT

8.1 Acquisition and information.
Add the complete retained acquisition bill, including its source generation,
finite waiting/actuation histories, scalar records, precision and convex
optimization. For the executed two-component inheritance this is 1,536
finite trials and 4,608 scalar observations, with the full original budgets
and software source cost preserved unchanged. The new write/retain operation
does not reacquire H or query an exact transfer function. Its extra online
information is n scalars per sampled retention step (and sampled write steps
if the optional linear feedback writer is used).

8.2 Finite preprocessing.
For rational cubic data, matrix-vector products, row-sum Jacobian bounds,
componentwise ordering brackets, norms with rational square-root enclosures,
and (10)-(25) are uniform finite computations. A rational PSD certificate can
be checked by symmetric Schur elimination. Computation is polynomial in n,
the complete data/precision lengths and the numbers of explicitly considered
candidate schedules. This does not count physical time as a unit-cost real
oracle. No nonuniform table, advice or hidden solver is supplied. General
semialgebraic identification/optimization retains the earlier CAD or conic
cost appropriate to that task; it is not made polynomial by this controller.

To avoid relying on exact transcendental values, a schedule may use
e^{-j}<=2^{-j} for integer j>=0 and choose j by integer comparisons. Finite
T_w=j/a and T_set=j'/alpha then suffice whenever the strict budgets have
positive slack. The instantiated planner instead uses outward rational
Taylor/range-reduction enclosures for exponentials. Their arithmetic precision,
coefficient sizes, number of terms and squarings are counted in PLAN.json.

8.3 Runtime controller.
At p fixed fractional bits, store rounded K,beta,h. For each n-vector reading
of integers y_i (interpreted as y_i/2^p), compute

    N_i = 2^(2p) sum_j K_ij y_j - beta_i y_i^3 + 2^(3p) h_i,
    command_i = nearest_integer(N_i/2^(3p)).                     (27)

Here K_ij,beta_i,h_i also denote their stored integers. The coefficient
rounding error on the declared read region plus the final half-unit rounding
is bounded in Euclidean norm; it is epsilon_c in (14). Integer arithmetic
introduces no further unspecified numerical solver error. Per read this uses
n^2+3n integer multiplications, O(n^2+n) additions and shifts. If all signed
intermediates fit b bits, a conservative schoolbook implementation takes

    C_step = 8(n^2+3n+1)b^2+8(n^2+16n)b

bit operations, with O((n^2+n)b) working storage. Here b also covers the finite
clock, sample counter and addressing widths; otherwise add their explicit
bit costs. It is a finite worst-case
operation count, not a measured CPU cycle estimate. Hardware with a latency
budget ell_cpu must warrant at least this much work within ell_cpu (or carry
a tighter implementation-specific worst-case timing certificate). Average
runtime does not prove that deadline.

If the retention-law duration is T_h=T_set+H_ret and samples are at least
h_min apart, charge at most 2+floor(T_h/h_min) vector reads/commands, n times
as many scalar readings, and this number times C_step. The periodic executed
schedule has its exact, smaller count. For fixed noise floors, accuracy
below b is not certified. With refinable channels, meeting b=O(delta)
typically needs h,tau=O(delta) at fixed other constants, and hence order
H_ret/delta reads. Logarithmic digit count is not logarithmic physical cost.

8.4 Amplitude, slew, supply and physical energy.
Use (22) during writing and (13) during retention. The actual slew is bounded
by (V+C_command+zeta)/tau, where the corresponding phase command bound is
inserted. Hence the actuator's transient energy and bandwidth are not free.
The source passivity supply satisfies

    integral |y^T v| dt <= R_w V_w T_w + R_h V_h T_h.             (28)

At an exact nonzero equilibrium, v=S q and y=q, so y^T v=q^TSq>=a||q||^2.
This is a continuous sustaining supply requirement even after writing ends.

There is also a lower bound for the actual disturbed retention interval.
Let q_bar bound ||q_*||, q_low^2 bound ||q_*||^2 from below, and let
d_hold=D0 exp(-T_set/tau)+d_inf. When w<=delta,

    y^T v >= a q_low^2 - delta A0 q_bar
                    - (q_bar+delta)(L delta+d_hold).             (28a)

This follows by writing v=S q_*+[v-F(y)]+[F(y)-F(q_*)] and applying the norm
bounds. A positive right side certifies a genuine continuous sustaining
supply for the disturbed operation, not only its ideal equilibrium. The
executed family has a positive bound above 3.744 in the declared supply units.

To call these units watts/joules requires a warranted power calibration. If
the abstract coordinates do not already give physical conjugate power ports,
include the known conversion. An unknown force/displacement-to-power map is
not silently treated as one. The model alone cannot determine sensor, driver,
processor or refrigerator dissipation. Those are charged through the actual
device specifications. For example an explicitly supplied certified driver
envelope

  P_driver <= P0+k_v||v||^2+k_s||v'||^2+eta^{-1}|y^Tv|

with known P0,k_v,k_s>=0 and eta>0 gives the total energy bound

  E_prepare + sum_phases T_phase(P0+k_v V_phase^2+k_s S_phase^2)
  + eta^{-1}(R_w V_w T_w+R_h V_h T_h)
  + N_reads E_read + C_bits E_bit.                               (29)

The input must actually warrant these device costs, or an alternative explicit
upper-cost model. They are not inferred from static impedance. Finite H_ret
has a finite bill; an all-time invariant-tube statement is not free perpetual
retention. The present execution records software wall time and operation
counts and does not invent unmeasured physical joules or a real-time device.

8.5 Software-source realization.
The retained sources have 512 total states, 6 ports and rational diagonal
rates in [1,2]. The code stores the source privately and exposes only reading
and command methods to the controller. It evolves a held command and actual
first-order actuator by the exact linear formulas. For a rate lambda,

 v_new = e^(-Delta/tau) v + (1-e^(-Delta/tau)) d,
 x_new = e^(-lambda Delta) x + B d (1-e^(-lambda Delta))/lambda
         + B(v-d)(e^(-Delta/tau)-e^(-lambda Delta))/(lambda-1/tau),

where d is command plus the executed bounded actuator bias. Exponentials
are enclosed with rational arithmetic; chosen binary64 coefficients are
compared back to the exact intervals. Explicit roundoff bounds use
gamma_j=ju/(1-ju), u=2^-53, including dot products and gradual underflow.

Compare the exact source/actuator with the native source driven by the SAME
emitted command sequence. Their difference is a stable linear recurrence,
so its uniform state error is bounded by the one-step errors divided by

10. AN ADDRESSABLE FAMILY WITH EXPONENTIALLY MANY COMPETING STATES

There is a uniform family-level consequence beyond the executed six-port
instance. It is a theorem with a complete finite compiler, not a claim that
the existing two component records already meet the finer accuracy required
by this corollary.

Let n>=2, s=ceil(sqrt(n)), and take any connected undirected weighted graph
with rational nonnegative edge weights, graph Laplacian L_G and maximum
weighted degree d>0. Put eps=1/(100d), f_i=1/100. Assume the source priors
a>=1,A0<=2,c0<=1/4 and an acquired certificate

    ||S-S_hat||<=E<=1/(1000n).                                   (31)

This requires the actual acquisition accuracy and its full paid cost. If the
instrument's fixed noise prevents (31), the corollary is not available.
Define ONE fixed retention law, used for every address,

    F(y)=(S_hat+I-eps L_G)y - y^3 + f.                            (32)

For each n-bit sign address sigma in {-1,+1}^n, define the box
    C_sigma={y: |y_i-sigma_i|<=1/10 for all i}.
The true port potential, up to a constant, is

    Phi(y)=sum_i (y_i^2-1)^2/4 + (eps/2)y^T L_G y
                    - f^T y + (1/2)y^T(S-S_hat)y.               (33)

Each of the 2^n disjoint boxes contains exactly one local minimum q_sigma.
To see this, the scalar derivative y_i^3-y_i points outward at every box
face by at least 171/1000. The remaining terms in that derivative have
absolute sum at most

    (11/5)eps d + 1/100 + (11/10)E sqrt(n)
       <= 22/1000+10/1000+11/10000 = 331/10000.

    Hess Phi >= (143/100-E)I >= (7/5)I.
The minimizer is unique in its box and is a strict local minimum. The
componentwise equilibrium equation and the scalar slope >=143/100 show

    |q_sigma,i-sigma_i| <= 331/14300 < 0.024.                     (34)

Lift each minimum using (8). These are distinct full equilibria; no enumeration
of them is needed to address one of them. They need not all be global minima.

round each coordinate to a finite dyadic grid, then project again. The exact
map contracts by at most 157/300. If the Euclidean rounding error is gamma,
the certified distance recursion is e_next <=(157/300)e+gamma. Choose gamma
small enough to give a limiting error <=1/2000 and take enough iterations to
make the decaying initial error <=1/2000. All bounds are rational apart from
a rational upper bound on sqrt(n). Thus ||p-q_sigma,0||<=1/1000 is obtained in
O(log(n+1)) iterations with O(log(n+1)) fractional bits at this fixed target
accuracy, and polynomial work in n and the graph's complete coefficient bit

11. SCOPE OF THE ENABLING CONSEQUENCE

The operation makes an acquired component library usable for deterministic
selection and retention of certified collective patterns, without complete
internal identification. The retained balanced-cubic compiler supplies an
arbitrary finitely described sign/amplitude target with a global-order and
local-curvature certificate when its stated robust inequalities hold. The
present compiler supplies the missing preparation, switching and retention
schedule from bounded unknown preparations, even when the same family has
wrong-pattern traps. Known additional damped states and independently
acquired components can participate when their direct-sum dynamical bounds
are included. A finite bank of target programs can be compiled against the
same acquired static data and reused; each rewrite pays its own preparation
and retention bill and uses the previous source and actuator norm bounds
as R0 and V0.

The barrier heights and number of other retention equilibria do not enter the
constant-write convergence bound because the programmable field is changed
during writing. The result does not claim barrier-independent switching under
an unchanged hardwired potential, free global optimization of frustrated
networks, or applicability to arbitrary biological/mechanical devices. It is
a complete, finite, checked operation under a specified model and access
contract. Its breadth is compositional and independent of hidden dimension;
its uncertainty, actuation, time and energy restrictions remain explicit.

The collective-order, port-acquisition, memory, inference, experimental-accuracy,
mechanical and SAT predecessors remain intact at their original scopes. In
particular, unrestricted SAT/P versus NP remains unresolved here.
