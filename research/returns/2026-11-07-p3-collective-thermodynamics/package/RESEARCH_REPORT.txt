Finite information, all equilibrium responses, global decisions, and the

RESULT

For a warranted fixed-degree, permutation-invariant, extensively scaled
qubit interaction family, phase-robust thermodynamic prediction and a useful
class of global thermodynamic decisions admit a complete construction whose
information and computation costs do not enumerate the 2^N-dimensional
many-spin Hilbert space. A finite record of positive-time, single-tagged-spin
probes identifies the interaction coefficients. That record is reusable for
all particle numbers in the warranted family, temperatures, and calibrated
affine control settings. Finite systems can be treated by all-sector Schur
blocks; thermodynamic-limit calculations reduce to a certified optimization
over the three-dimensional Bloch ball. No unique phase, spectral gap,
mixing-time bound, unknown inverse, or Gibbs-state acquisition is required.

The information step matters. Fully symmetric dynamical probes can be
exactly blind to a traceless interaction that changes pressure extensively.
One tagged preparation/readout suffices to remove this blind spot in the
declared family. The physical sample count is explicit and polynomial in
inverse coefficient error at fixed degree, with substantial conditioning
constants. The deliberately conservative executed schedule would require
approximately 9.36e29 physical preparations at its requested calibration
precision. It is not an economical laboratory protocol. The executed record
is a calibrated digital construction, whose source computation is included.

The computational improvement is real and much less pessimistic in the
executed example: two globally certified inverse thermodynamic decisions,
including coexistence, take about 17.5 seconds after acquisition. A separate
all-sector, 64-spin interval calculation takes about 20.3 seconds. These are
observed runtimes in this session, not universal performance guarantees.

The structural boundary is also explicit. Allowing an input-sized number of
independently distinguishable collective ensembles recovers unweighted
MAX-CUT with commuting quadratic interactions. Polynomial cost uniformly in
that number and inverse accuracy would imply P=NP. Phase coexistence itself
is therefore not the obstruction removed here: the positive result uses a
fixed-dimensional, acquired macroscopic description.

This report makes no historical-priority claim. Mean-field variational
principles, Schur compression, Hamiltonian learning, entropy variational
bounds, interval optimization, linear programming, and quantum estimation
are established machinery. The contribution of this return is the explicit
information-to-prediction-and-decision construction, its proofs and costs,
the symmetry-information obstruction, and the executed certificates.


1. QUESTION AND COMPLETE RESOURCE CONTRACT

1.1 Chosen question.

Can one acquire an unknown collective quantum interaction using a finite
specified physical record, and then predict every relevant equilibrium
response or choose a globally certified equilibrium control, at costs that
scale polynomially rather than with Hilbert dimension, while retaining
noncommuting interactions, criticality and coexistence?

The answer proved below is affirmative for the following family. The
restriction is structural, rather than an assumption that the desired
equilibrium phase is easy to prepare or that an efficient pressure oracle
already exists.

1.2 Hamiltonian family.

Fix an integer K>=1. For N>=K and alpha=(a,b,c), 1<=|alpha|<=K, define

  A_(N,alpha) = the average of all distinct-site Pauli strings containing
               a copies of X, b copies of Y, c copies of Z, and identities
               on the remaining sites.

The number of strings in this average is

  M_(N,alpha) = N! / [(N-|alpha|)! a! b! c!].

Each A is Hermitian and has operator norm at most one. Set

  H_N(0) = N sum_alpha c_alpha A_(N,alpha),
  P(r)   = sum_alpha c_alpha r^alpha,
  D      = binomial(K+3,3)-1.

The unknown real coefficients satisfy a supplied bound sum|c_alpha|<=C.
The same coefficients, degree bound, and normalization govern every N in
the family. This scaling law and permutation symmetry are warranted model
premises. The finite measurement record does not certify them against an
arbitrary unknown apparatus or a different size-dependent interaction law.

The operators include complex matrix entries through Y and need not
commute. Examples include collective transverse-field Ising, Lipkin-type
and higher-p-spin interactions. The operation is not restricted to a
diagonal classical polynomial or a single exactly solvable instance.

A scalar multiple of identity is dynamically unidentifiable. We fix the
traceless Pauli gauge above. An unknown, control-independent energy zero
must be added to absolute free energies; it cancels in response and
control comparisons. A control-dependent scalar shift must be calibrated
and included. The negative example in Section 2 is already traceless.

1.3 Controls and objectives.

The main contract supplies calibrated static affine Hamiltonian controls

  H_N(u)=H_N(0)-N sum_i u_i Q_(i,N),
  Q_(i,N)=sum_alpha q_(i,alpha) A_(N,alpha).

Their polynomial symbols Q_i(r) and a bounded feasible rational polytope
are supplied. In the executable, the polytope is the convex hull of an
explicit list of q vertices. Reading that list is paid; q can itself be
exponential in another description of the control set. There is no hidden
vertex-enumeration or separation oracle.

The decision class is

  maximize over feasible u: F_beta(u)-c.u,

for a declared linear term c. An important interpretation is c=-m_target:
this is a bounded-field Legendre decision for a specified vector of mean
responses. It does not assert that a chosen field selects one unique phase
or realizes that response in every individual run. At coexistence, the
associated convex weights are probabilities of whole-system phases, not
spatial volume fractions or independent cloned preparations.

If the affine control operators are also unknown, the same acquisition can
be run at every supplied feasible vertex. Under a warranted affine law,
H(sum_j lambda_j v_j)=sum_j lambda_j H(v_j). A coefficient error at most nu
at every vertex therefore gives error at most nu throughout the convex
hull. This costs q acquisitions, with their confidence probabilities
budgeted jointly. Affineness itself is not established by finitely many
vertex measurements. No conductivity response law is imported from P2.

1.4 Acquisition interfaces.

The physical acquisition contract consists of:

* fresh product preparations tau on a tagged spin and rho(r) on every
  other spin, at a chosen N0>=K;
* calibrated Pauli preparation and readout on that tagged spin;
* positive-time evolution under the unknown Hamiltonian at rationally
  specified times, without an inverse;
* calibrated record error, timing and SPAM budgets, and the coefficient
  bound C in calibrated energy/time units (hbar=1);
* either an exactly warranted model family, or additional errors already
  included in the record and model budgets by a separately justified law.

These are access requirements, not supplied experimental hardware. No
physical experiment, apparatus acquisition or infrastructure deployment
was performed. The construction counts preparations, readouts, evolution
time, record size and digital work. Preparation/reset time, device cost,
calibration cost and digital infrastructure remain separately priced.

Physical temperature and the actual static affine field law require their
own calibration. Command feasibility refers to the supplied feasible
polytope. A physical actuator must preserve feasibility and have a declared
error or margin contract; numerical simplex coordinates do not enforce it.

1.5 Equilibrium meaning.

For beta>0 let

  F_(N,beta)(u)=-(beta N)^(-1) log Tr exp[-beta H_N(u)],
  F_beta(u)=lim_(N->infinity) F_(N,beta)(u).

At beta=infinity, use the minimum energy per spin. Pressure in energy units
is p_beta=-F_beta. Some cited papers instead call beta*p_beta pressure.

The phase-response statement covers limits of states whose canonical
free-energy-density deficit tends to zero. Exact finite-N canonical Gibbs
states are included. Arbitrary mean-field stationary or KMS solutions are
not automatically included. Mean-field interactions are nonadditive; this
is not a transfer of the predecessor's finite-range equilibrium theorem.

The physical questions retained are canonical free-energy densities,
averaged fixed-degree observables, all coexisting responses in the declared
equilibrium sense, and globally certified affine-field free-energy/Legendre
decisions. Spatial interfaces, domain-wall costs, transport, thermalization
times and general short-range materials are outside this structural class.


2. AN EXACT INFORMATION OBSTRUCTION FOR SYMMETRIC PROBES

Let S=(1/2)sum_i sigma_i, N>=2, and compare

  H0=0,
  H1=-g N [A_xx+A_yy+A_zz]
     =-g [4 S^2-3 N I]/(N-1),       g>0.

Both Hamiltonians are traceless. H1 is scalar on each total-spin sector.
Every permutation-invariant density matrix commutes with it. Consequently
the following entire class of records is identical under H0 and H1:
permutation-invariant preparations, intervening controls and conditional
instruments that preserve permutation invariance, positive-time evolution,
and any final measurement. The proof is induction over the operations:
the state entering each free-evolution step commutes with H1, so this step
acts identically to H0. Collective spin controls obey this restriction.
A measurement that prepares a tagged conditional state is an additional
resource and is not covered by this blindness statement.

The pressure difference is macroscopic. At beta=2/g,

  p0=(g/2) log 2,
  p1>=g,

where the second inequality follows by using a fully polarized product
state in the Gibbs variational principle. Thus

  p1-p0 >= g[1-(log 2)/2].

This is neither an energy-gauge ambiguity nor a finite-precision problem.
The entire symmetric dynamical record can be exactly uninformative about
thermodynamically important sector weights. Calibrated energy measurements
or a bath with supplied inter-sector transition information are different
resources. Collective-bath thermodynamics also requires attention to
sector restrictions; the relevant caution in [4] has its own stated model
scope and is not imported as a general bath theorem.

For a tagged +X spin, bath Bloch vector r, and Y readout, H1 instead gives

  d<Y_tag>/dt at t=0 = -4 g r_z.

The signal has no 1/N suppression. This is the opening used next.


3. EXACT TAGGED-SPIN IDENTIFICATION

THEOREM 1 (tagged derivative identity).
Prepare tau on site 1 and rho(r)^tensor(N-1) on the bath, where
rho(r)=(I+r.sigma)/2. The effective tagged Hamiltonian in the initial
commutator is exactly grad P(r).sigma. In particular:

  prepare +Y, measure Z: f_x'(0)=2 partial_x P(r);
  prepare +Z, measure X: f_y'(0)=2 partial_y P(r);
  prepare +X, measure Y: f_z'(0)=2 partial_z P(r).

This holds for every N>=K, including small acquisition systems.

PROOF.
In A_(N,alpha), the fraction of strings with sigma_j at site 1 is
alpha_j/N. A term avoiding site 1 commutes with the tagged observable.
For the remaining terms, tracing the other sites in the product state
gives r^(alpha-e_j). Multiplication by the N in H cancels the fraction's
denominator. Summing yields sum_j partial_j P(r) sigma_j. The three
commutator signs give the displayed identities. No mean-field limit or
factorization approximation occurs in this derivative calculation. END.

Let a=1/4 and take K equally spaced rational nodes in [-a,a] in each
coordinate (the node 0 when K=1). The K^3 product grid is physical:
|r|<=sqrt(3)/4<1. Each component partial_j P has degree at most K-1 in
each coordinate, so tensor Lagrange interpolation reconstructs it.

For alpha!=0, let the notation [r^b] denote a polynomial coefficient. Then

  c_alpha=(1/|alpha|) sum_(j:alpha_j>0)
                   [r^(alpha-e_j)] partial_j P.

This defines an explicit rational matrix R_K from 3K^3 gradient values to
the D interaction coefficients. Define

  Lambda_K=sum_alpha sum_record |(R_K)_(alpha,record)|.

If every acquired gradient coordinate has error at most zeta, the recovered
coefficient vector has l1 error at most Lambda_K*zeta. This norm, rather
than a unit-cost interpolation oracle, is computed by polynomial_tools.py.
For K=2, Lambda=21. For K=4 and this grid,

  Lambda_4=442467/64 = 6913.546875.

The K dependence and the conditioning are paid. This is a fixed-degree
efficiency statement, not a uniformly well-conditioned tomography result
as K grows.

If sum|c_hat-c|<=nu, then simultaneously for every N>=K and every known
control setting,

  ||H_hat_N(u)-H_N(u)||/N <= nu.

It follows from the variational principle that the free-energy-density
error is at most nu at every beta, including the ground-energy limit.
No union bound over the infinitely many downstream N, beta or u is needed.


4. FINITE POSITIVE-TIME ACQUISITION; NO DERIVATIVE ORACLE

Write the Hamiltonian as a sum of supported Pauli interactions. Its local
absolute interaction incidence obeys

  max_i sum_(terms containing i) ||h_term||
       =sum_alpha |alpha| |c_alpha| <= J:=K C.

A commutator starting at one site can grow its support by at most K-1
sites at each step. Expanding the nested commutator and summing absolute
weights gives, for a tagged norm-one observable O,

  ||ad_H^r(O)|| <= (2J)^r product_(l=0)^(r-1)[1+l(K-1)]
                <= (2KJ)^r r!.

Hence the scalar expectation f(z) is analytic and has |f(z)|<=2 on the
complex disk |z|<=1/(4KJ). The bound is independent of N.

Set T=1/(16KJ). Use R+1 rational times t_0=0<...<t_R=T approximating the
Chebyshev-Lobatto nodes within T/(16R^3), with exact endpoints. Exact
rational interpolation derivative weights w_l then satisfy

  W:=sum_l |w_l| <= 4 R^2/T.

For completeness: ideal node separation is at least T/R^2 and the ideal
endpoint derivative row has l1 norm 2R^2/T. Perturbing each node by at most
T/(16R^3) changes each product of Lagrange ratios by less than a factor
two; the t_0 row is a sum of reciprocal distances and obeys the same
bound. The executable constructs the rational nodes from alternating
Machin arctangent bounds and bounded cosine Taylor polynomials. It uses no
exact irrational-time primitive.

Cauchy's interpolation formula on |z|=4T gives the derivative remainder

  |f'(0)-sum_l w_l f(t_l)| <= b_R:=1/(2 T 3^R).

Indeed the product of nonzero nodes in the numerator is at most T^R,
while the contour denominator contains 4T(3T)^R and the extra derivative
factor 4T. Combining these with |f|<=2 gives the displayed constant.

Choose R so b_R<=zeta/2 and require each scalar record to have radius

  gamma=zeta/(2W).

Then the gradient reading (1/2)sum_l w_l f(t_l) has error at most
(b_R+W gamma)/2 <= zeta/2. The code uses this actual bound, not just the
looser requested budget.

There are M=3K^3(R+1) scalar records. If each record's systematic error is
at most gamma/2, independent bounded Pauli trials and a union bound give
all record radii simultaneously with probability at least 1-alpha using

  s=ceil[8 gamma^(-2) log(2M/alpha)] shots per record,
  S=M s fresh N0-spin preparations.

This is a sufficient sample bound, not an asserted practical optimum.
At fixed K,C and interpolation grid, S is nu^(-2) times logarithmic factors
and explicit conditioning constants. N0-spin preparation count is N0*S;
evolution time is s * 3K^3 * sum_l t_l. Reset, calibration and readout
durations are additional. Record arithmetic, including the bit lengths of
the weights and times, is part of the digital cost.

Timing error delta_t contributes at most 2J*delta_t to a scalar record.
State-preparation and readout errors must fit the remaining systematic
budget. A conservative global product-state trace-error bound can grow
with N0; no per-site calibration cost is silently held constant as N0
grows. The learned coefficient confidence covers true records only when
these record conditions hold.

The inverse-square precision dependence is not gratuitous in this short-
time local-readout resource model. The subfamily H_c=c sum_i Z_i reduces
each observed tagged trial to a one-qubit phase experiment of duration at
most T. Distinguishing c=+nu from c=-nu with bounded error requires order
1/(T^2 nu^2) such product-prepared single-tag readouts, by the two-point
state-overlap bound (equivalently bounded one-qubit Fisher information).
This lower bound does not cover long coherent interrogation, entangled
input acquisition, collective readout or other stronger resources. It
does not justify the large constants of the particular schedule above.


5. EXACT THERMODYNAMIC LIMIT AND WHAT ITS PHASES MEAN

The governing imported pressure result is Manai-Warzel [1], Proposition
1.1 and its Appendix A.7 justification, for self-adjoint Weyl polynomials
in the rescaled total spin. It has no unique-minimum or spectral-gap
condition. Their subsequent spectral-gap theorems have additional
hypotheses and are not used here. Earlier mean-field equilibrium work is
credited in [2], without importing an unread state-classification theorem.

Our distinct-site normalization is connected to that governing result by
an explicit operator-norm comparison. In a degree-k rescaled total-spin
monomial, choose site labels with replacement. Conditional on distinct
labels, the Weyl-ordered average is A_(N,alpha). Collision probability is
at most k(k-1)/(2N); both the collided and noncollided products have norm
at most one. Thus the per-spin Hamiltonian difference is bounded by

  K(K-1) C/N.

The variational norm bound transfers the limiting free energy. With
s(r)=h_2((1+|r|)/2) and natural logarithms,

  F_beta(u)=min_(r in B^3) Phi_beta(r,u),
  Phi_beta(r,u)=P(r)-u.Q(r)-s(r)/beta.                 (1)

At zero temperature the entropy term is omitted. This follows also by
sandwiching finite-temperature and ground energies within (log 2)/beta.
No rate of approach in N is inferred from an unspecified asymptotic
constant; Section 9 supplies a finite-N operation instead.

THEOREM 2 (complete equilibrium response set).
Let M_beta(u) be the full argmin in (1). The limiting controlled-response
vectors of all states whose canonical free-energy-density deficit tends
to zero are exactly

  conv{ Q(r): r in M_beta(u) }.                       (2)

This statement permits every coexisting minimizer and arbitrary mixtures.
It covers averaged fixed-degree diagnostic observables by adding their
formal fields to the mathematical pressure family. Such formal fields
need not be physically actuated merely to prove the response statement.

PROOF, INCLUDING BOTH DIRECTIONS.
Put p=-F. For a finite-N state with response q_N and free-energy-density
deficit delta_N, the Gibbs variational principle gives for every v

  p_N(v)>=p_N(u)+(v-u).q_N-delta_N.

Along any convergent response subsequence with delta_N->0, taking the
pressure limit gives q in the subdifferential of p at u. Formula (1) is
a maximum of affine functions for p, so that subdifferential is exactly
the convex hull in (2).

Conversely, for each minimizing r the product sequence rho(r)^tensor N
has exact energy density P(r)-u.Q(r), entropy density s(r), and response
Q(r). Its deficit tends to zero by (1). A finite mixture realizes any
convex combination in (2): entropy concavity gives the required upper
bound on its free energy, and the mixture entropy excess is at most
the finite mixing entropy, which is o(N). Compactness and finite-dimensional
convexity complete the description. At zero temperature the same argument
uses the energy variational principle. END.

Equation (2) is a response-set result, not a trace-norm approximation to
the canonical Gibbs state and not a statement about every locally
stationary mean-field solution. Mixture probabilities are ensemble
probabilities. A fixed magnetization in every realization is a stronger
constraint and can differ in a nonadditive model.


6. CERTIFIED PRESSURE AND ALL-PHASE COMPUTATION

6.1 A finite algorithm and worst-case cost.

Take the rational net B^3 intersect (1/M) Z^3, M>=2. Rounding each coordinate
toward zero remains inside the ball and moves it by at most 1/M in each
coordinate. If the total controlled polynomial has coefficient l1 bound
C_total, telescoping monomials and the binary-entropy continuity bound give

  |Phi(r,u)-Phi(z,u)| <= omega_M,
  omega_M=K C_total/M + beta^(-1) h_2(1/M).           (3)

The entropy term is omitted at beta=infinity. The factor sqrt(3)/2 in
the one-qubit trace distance is at most one, which justifies the argument
1/M in (3). Thus a net of O(M^3) states suffices. Choosing M with
omega_M<=epsilon gives polynomial work in K, C_total/epsilon,
beta^(-1)/epsilon and logarithmic accuracy factors at fixed dimension.
This is not a polynomial-in-log(C_total/epsilon) claim.

At each point, evaluate the polynomial exactly and entropy with outward
rational intervals of width rho. If G^-(u),G^+(u) are the minima of the
lower and upper affine intercepts, coefficient uncertainty nu gives

  F_true(u) in [G^-(u)-omega_M-nu, G^+(u)+nu].        (4)

All costs are finite: the net is generated, scalar transcendental
precision is chosen, and every evaluation is performed. No efficient
pressure oracle is assumed as an input.

6.2 Executed adaptive implementation.

The delivered implementation uses dyadic boxes in [-1,1]^3, discards boxes
whose minimum squared radius exceeds one, and bounds the energy polynomial
by rational interval arithmetic. Entropy is decreasing in radius, so the
minimum-radius point gives a valid lower bound for the negative entropy.

A stronger lower bound uses a Taylor bound for P and the exact variational
inequality, valid for every rational b,

  -s(r)/beta >= b.r - log[2 cosh(beta|b|)]/beta.

For a box center c, radii d_i, polynomial gradient g at c and certified
Hessian-entry bounds H_ij, put a=g+b. Then

  lower >= P(c)-g.c - (1/2)sum_ij H_ij d_i d_j
           -log[2 cosh(beta|b|)]/beta
           +max{min_box a.r, -|a|}.

Every term is enclosed outward. Floating point only proposes a rational b
and optional candidate states; the inequality is valid for every such b.
The exact rational point norm and all final bounds are checked. The
negative-entropy bound, rather than a derivative extrapolation through
the pure-state boundary, handles that boundary safely.

Natural interval bounds alone imply termination: once every side width is
small enough for (3), a box cannot force a further split to meet the value
tolerance. The adaptive search therefore has a polynomial uniform-grid
worst-case bound at fixed dimension, although that bound can be large.
The observed adaptive costs are reported separately from it.

For all-phase prediction with coefficient uncertainty nu, retain every box
whose learned lower bound is at most the best learned upper bound plus
2nu. If r minimizes the true potential then

  Phi_hat(r)<=F_hat+2nu,

so its box is retained. Mapping the retained boxes through the supplied
Q polynomials and taking a convex outer hull encloses every response in
(2). The cover can be refined geometrically and may remain broad near
coexistence or under significant coefficient uncertainty. No phase is
discarded by assuming the optimizer found a unique minimum.

The executable has 44-bit outward rational entropy outputs and 60-digit
Decimal internal exp/log calculations, under the documented correct-
rounding contracts [7]. The stated executed tolerances are much larger
than this rounding floor. The mathematical algorithm chooses arbitrary
precision as needed; the fixed-precision emitter is not claimed to handle
arbitrary requested tolerances or unbounded numeric ranges unchanged.


7. GLOBAL CONTINUOUS DECISION AND INVERSE THERMODYNAMICS

Concavity here follows from the infimum of affine Hamiltonian energies in
(1). There is no assumption that a magnetization or another observable is
a concave function of its field. Nondifferentiability at a transition is
allowed.

For net states z_s, supplied control vertices v_j, and lower energy/entropy
intercepts a_s^-, solve the rational linear program

  maximize t
  subject to lambda_j>=0, sum_j lambda_j=1,
             t<=sum_j lambda_j [a_s^- -v_j.Q(z_s)-c.v_j]
                for every state s.

The command is u=sum_j lambda_j v_j. If the LP objective gap is eta,
equations (3)-(4) give

  decision regret <= omega_M + rho + 2nu + eta.      (5)

This is polynomial in the explicit LP size and rational bit lengths.
There is no exponential control-grid search in the number of fields.
The number of state-grid points can nevertheless be large. Arbitrary
nonlinear utilities of the response are not included in this decision
theorem.

The executed exchange method uses a stronger certificate that does not
trust a binary64 LP solver's optimality assertion. SciPy/HiGHS proposes
probabilities lambda on feasible vertices and w on candidate states.
They are rounded to exactly nonnegative rational simplices. Any such w
gives the following valid global upper bound:

  U=max_j sum_s w_s [a_s^+ -v_j.Q(z_s)-c.v_j] + nu.

At the exact feasible rational u, the global state oracle gives a lower
bound L on F_true(u)-c.u. Consequently U-L certifies global regret.
More states are added until the requested certificate is obtained. The
executable returns an error if its floating-point proposal stage stalls;
it does not label an uncertified numerical candidate a solution. The
general polynomial existence/termination statement uses the explicit net
and rational LP, not an unproved complexity bound for HiGHS.

The simplex rounding idea is compatible with P2's supplied operation [5].
Its conductivity concavity and relative actuator law are not used. The
Lipschitz bound here is derived directly from the Hamiltonian:

  |[F(u)-c.u]-[F(v)-c.v]|
       <= (sum_i ||Q_i|| + ||c||_1) ||u-v||_infinity.

The rational primal/dual certificate already includes numerical command
rounding. An additional feasible physical actuator error delta_u adds at
most the displayed Lipschitz constant times delta_u. An error in inverse
temperature adds at most log(2) times its size to free energy. These errors
are not silently absorbed into the executed ideal-control certificate.

7.1 Useful inverse task and phase probabilities.

With c=-m_target, maximizing F(u)+u.m_target is the dual problem for a
constraint on mean response. At an interior optimum, m_target belongs to
the convex response set (2). At coexistence the target can require a
random mixture of entire phases. With bounded fields, a boundary optimum
can instead leave a residual target mismatch; that mismatch must be
reported, not declared solved by the objective certificate alone.

The executed certificates report the actual rational mixture response,
its error relative to the target, and a free-energy-density deficit bound.
For any finite list of rational Bloch states and mixture weights, the
mathematical preparation recipe is

  rho_N^mix=sum_s w_s rho(r_s)^tensor N.

For all N>=K it has exact averaged polynomial responses sum_s w_s Q(r_s).
Entropy concavity gives its true free-energy density at most

  sum_s w_s Phi_hat(r_s,u)+nu.

Subtracting a finite-N free-energy lower bound, or a limiting lower bound,
therefore certifies its deficit with the appropriate additional nu.
This is a useful approximate-equilibrium-density preparation contract.
It is not a guarantee of canonical-state fidelity, phase switching time,
small macroscopic fluctuations or fixed response in every realization.
An actual implementation requires the declared local preparation interface
and its additional costs; no physical mixture was prepared here.


8. FINITE-N COMPUTATION WITHOUT A LIMIT-ERROR ORACLE

The all-sector Schur decomposition and multiplicities are established [1]:

  H_N acts on one spin-J block of dimension 2J+1 for each allowed J,
  multiplicity m_(N,J)=binom(N,N/2-J)-binom(N,N/2-J-1).

There are O(N) blocks and O(N^3) stored matrix entries. Every sector and
its full multiplicity must be retained for a finite-temperature trace.
Keeping only J=N/2 would lose thermodynamically essential entropy.

Here is an explicit finite-degree compiler for the distinct-site inputs.
Introduce commuting formal variables t=(t_x,t_y,t_z) and set

  product_i [I+t.sigma_i]=sum_alpha E_alpha t^alpha.

Then A_(N,alpha)=E_alpha/M_(N,alpha). The homogeneous degree-k matrix
polynomials E_k are obtained by Newton's recurrence

  E_0=I,
  k E_k=sum_(j=1)^k (-1)^(j-1) E_(k-j) p_j(t),
  p_j(t)=N(t.t)^(j/2) I                         for j even,
  p_j(t)=2(t.t)^((j-1)/2) t.S                   for j odd.

The site operators commute across sites; the displayed power sums follow
from (t.sigma)^2=(t.t)I. Substituting each spin-J representation yields all
block coefficients with polynomial arithmetic at fixed K. This also
provides the correct lower-degree collision corrections at finite N.

A mathematical finite-N certificate need not use unverified binary64
eigenvalues. In an unnormalized Dicke basis of L=2J spins, S_+, S_- and
S_z have rational entries, and the positive diagonal Gram matrix has
entries binomial(L,k). Thus G H_J is rational complex Hermitian and G is
positive rational diagonal. Exact inertia of G H_J-lambda G counts
eigenvalues below a rational lambda. Hermitian congruence elimination
uses a nonzero diagonal pivot or, if necessary, a nonzero off-diagonal
2x2 pivot; an all-zero remainder is handled as a null space. Bisection
over the supplied spectral norm range gives eigenvalue intervals to the
required energy-density precision without a minimum-gap assumption.

There are polynomially many blocks, matrix operations and bisections in
N at fixed K. Rational bit sizes are polynomially bounded by the input
and elimination minors. Multiplicity integers have O(N) bits. Certified
scalar exp/log, with energy shifting and small-term bounds when needed,
turn the intervals into a partition-function/free-energy interval. At
zero temperature take the least eigenvalue interval. This is polynomial
in N, not in log N: the particle number and preparation cost remain real.

11. A SHARP BOUNDARY: MANY COLLECTIVE VARIABLES RESTORE NP HARDNESS

Let G=(V,E) be a simple unweighted graph with m>=1 edges. Give every
vertex i an ensemble of L qubits, and let z_i be its averaged Z. Define
the commuting quadratic polynomial

  P_G(z)=(1/(2m)) sum_{(i,j) in E} (1+z_i z_j).

The full Hamiltonian is (|V|L)P_G of the group-averaged operators. It is
invariant under permutations inside each ensemble and has a compact
supplied degree-two description. Its exact minimum energy density is

  e0(G)=1-MAXCUT(G)/m.

PROOF.
The polynomial is affine in each z_i separately, so a minimum on
[-1,1]^|V| occurs at a vertex. At z_i=+/-1, an edge contributes zero if
cut and 1/m if uncut. These vertices are attained by fully polarized
states in every ensemble. Since the Hamiltonian is diagonal, no quantum
state improves its minimum. The identity holds at every L, not just in
a thermodynamic limit. END.

An additive e0 approximation finer than 1/(3m) recovers MAXCUT by rounding.
At finite temperature,

  e0-(log 2)/beta <= F_beta <= e0.

For example beta=16m and free-energy error at most 1/(16m) suffice to
recover the same integer optimum. The inputs, beta and inverse accuracy
are polynomial in graph size. Unweighted MAX-CUT is NP-complete under
the governing unit-weight condition in the original published abstract
of Garey-Johnson-Stockmeyer [6].

Therefore a deterministic algorithm with polynomial full cost uniformly
in the number of distinguishable ensembles and inverse accuracy for
this larger task would imply P=NP. This is a conditional implication,
not a proof of P!=NP. It already holds with known coefficients and
commuting degree-two interactions; the difficulty is not caused by
Hamiltonian-learning access, quantum synthesis or a laboratory gate.

The positive operation has a fixed three-dimensional macroscopic state
space. Increasing that dimension is a substantive change of scientific
contract. This explains why the construction is a useful broad family
result without supplying a generic efficient thermodynamic solver.
