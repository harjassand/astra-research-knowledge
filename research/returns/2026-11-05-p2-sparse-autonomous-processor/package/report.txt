SPARSE AUTONOMOUS PROCESSORS AND THE INFORMATION COST OF INITIALIZATION
Research result and proof record — 5 November 2026

STATUS
The results below are new derivations relative to the completed strong
burst compiler and reusable processor. They are not claimed to be an
already published or independently peer-reviewed theorem. Primary
sources listed at the end support imported machinery and comparisons.
Finite checks validate the new adapter and transaction identities;
they do not simulate the complete physical code or measure a threshold.

MAIN RESULT
Let d >= 2 be the ambient spatial dimension, alpha > d+1, and
r = alpha-d > 1. Fix the Pareto burst law and a sufficiently small
positive physical intensity lambda before choosing horizons.

In the static synchronous local-channel model described below, a
uniform family of finite input-independent layouts can acquire an
unknown qubit from a fixed compact raw seed, retain it through a
requested time T, and deliver it at a fixed local port at a declared
tick t_T = T(1+o(1)), with reference-inclusive error at most 1/4, using

    diameter L(T) <= T^(1/r+o(1)),
    installed registers N(T) <= T^(1/r+o(1)),
    all-register-time U(T) <= T^(1+1/r+o(1)).

Matching lower polynomial exponents hold in the same static bounded-range
register model. This improves the preceding dense reusable construction
from N=T^(2/r+o(1)) and U=T^(1+2/r+o(1)). The diameter exponent was
already attained by that construction.

A second theorem shows that accepting the same fixed prescribed seed
from EVERY possible outside mutable state forces N=Omega(T).
Thus known, noisy controller initialization is a real resource:
the prepared and arbitrary-junk restart contracts have different
polynomial hardware costs for every r>1.

1. MODEL AND ACTUAL OPERATION

Spatial marks form a homogeneous Poisson process of intensity

    lambda dx dt nu(dR),   Pr(R >= u) = u^(-alpha),  u >= 1.

Each mark permits an arbitrary CPTP corruption of registers inside its
ball or cube. Changing the norm changes constants only. Mark geometry
is independent on disjoint source sets. Fault values may be correlated,
non-Markovian and mediated by a common environment. Marks are
instantaneous spatial damage; persistent damage durations would change
the model.

The device is a static finite graph with bounded degree, bounded
interaction range, bounded local dimension and bounded local register
density. The construction embeds in two dimensions, also within any
ambient d>=2. Each tick applies the same constant-depth local channel.
The finite patterned layout may depend on T but is input-independent.
The local primitive alphabet is fixed.

The immutable patterned gate law, static wiring and synchronous tick
remain premises. This is not a translation-invariant, asynchronous,
Hamiltonian or thermal realization. Every mutable data, program,
syndrome, vote, phase, counter, flag and I/O record is fault-exposed.

Known controller words are initialized by actual noisy local product
writes. Their error, number of writes and spatial access are charged.
Their prescribed region can depend on the horizon. Only the unknown
input-dependent seed is fixed and compact. Distributed initialization
access and finite layout fabrication are declared resources; autonomous
fabrication is not required.

The input is one raw qubit Q, possibly entangled with an arbitrary
reference R. The operation performs actual noisy ascent, protected
retention/computation, and actual noisy descent to a constant-size raw
port. It does not start with an encoded unknown answer. Error is half
the diamond distance from the ideal operation, or equivalently the
uniform declared reference-inclusive trace-distance guarantee.

A fixed nontrivial error target is essential: raw input/output retain a
fixed O(lambda) contribution. The theorem does not claim arbitrarily
small error at one fixed noisy output cell.

Sparse positions are physically absent hardware, not mutable vacuum
symbols in a filled lattice. All wire storage and accessible persistent
records capable of carrying the input count. Bounding-box area does
not equal installed-register count. All-register-time charges every
installed register on every tick, including idles and backward tracks.
It is not an energy law.

2. IMPORTED MACHINERY

The included accepted_reusable_report.tex contains the preceding full
reusable construction. Its accepted ingredients are reused here.

For fixed code, correction budget and padding B, write

    H = B^m,  A = C B,  kappa = log_B A,
    theta = A B^(-r) < 1.

The actual separated-source recurrence gives sufficient geometric bad
events with

    p_m <= S_B^2 p_(m-1)^2 + c_B lambda theta^m,
    p_m <= K_B lambda theta^m

at the corresponding fixed-family threshold. It does not assume
independent repair cones or independent effective faults.

The strong compiler has correctness on valid inputs, validity on
arbitrary physical inputs, and semi-correctness retaining syndrome and
environment correlations. A good protected normalization Norm_m maps
ANY physical block into the valid class with bad probability <=p_m.
It has no extra raw-interface sum over lower levels. AGP Section 10,
Lemma 6 and the accepted Aharonov--Ben-Or gadget machinery supply the
strong semantic framework; the Poisson-source compatibility is the
separate completed construction.

A reusable period contains sacrificial I/O I, persistent encoded data
and work D, and protected controller C. It performs actual ascent of I,
strong normalization, guarded first-pass admission into normalized D,
the protected kernel and controller update, normalization and guarded
final-pass release into I, and actual descent. Retained data undergo
protected identities throughout the other stages.

Intermediate I/O histories may be arbitrary. Good normalization and
disabled guards prevent them from damaging retained D. Only the first
useful ascent and final useful descent must succeed. Counter, loaded
and done flags, program, and private vote records are all corruptible
and protected, rather than reliable external control.

Known controller bits have a spatial noisy-preparation failure bound
C_init lambda H^(-r) each. Private-vote classical/quantum interfaces
avoid a single vulnerable decoded control word.

For logical width w and kernel depth b, let bar_b=b+w+1 and
G=O(w bar_b). The source circuit period has

    width W=O(wH),   duration P=O(bar_b H^kappa).

For K periods its error is at most

    eta_in + eta_out + C_io lambda
    + C_init lambda w_c H^(-r)
    + C_bulk lambda K G H^(kappa-r)
    + epsilon_synth.                                      (1)

Raw apparatus errors and whole-computation synthesis error remain
explicit. Exact programs over the declared finite gate set need no
synthesis allowance.

3. THE NEW LIVENESS LEMMA

There is a computable constant Q_* independent of B such that a
level-m block needs at most c Q_*^m simultaneously live physical
registers. A width-w circuit therefore has live width <=c w Q_*^m.

Proof.
Define a value as live from input/preparation through its last
SYNTACTIC read. Count all code coordinates, syndrome, scratch, private
votes, program and controller records. Keep all possible controlled
gate operands. Liveness never depends on a control value being correct
or on an ideal branch being taken.

A fixed finite gadget has only finitely many child code/work blocks.
Localization routes these through B positions, increasing path length,
not the number of simultaneous tokens. Route serially by local swaps;
retain the source, current destination and fixed workspace. Prepare
or normalize the current destination just before use instead of
initializing all B destinations concurrently.

This uses only a fixed number of extra child blocks and child
operations per hop. O(B) hops still have duration CB, with a changed
fixed C if necessary. Every arbitrary-input operand required by the
strong contract remains present at its use.

Unused routing partners have no later syntactic use before reset.
Their old states can be discarded after the transport. This does not
assert that garbage or syndromes are independent: the proof retains
their environmental correlations. Swaps relocate rather than copy
errors, and each fixed primitive contacts boundedly many lineages.

Let Q_* be the maximum live child-block count over the fixed finite
library, including normalization workspace and protected identities
on waiting blocks. Then Q_m <= Q_* Q_(m-1). Induction proves the bound.

4. EXPLICIT SPARSE AUTONOMOUS EXECUTION

Create a vertex v=(x,phi) for each live source-register occurrence at
source microtime phi modulo P. Preserve its original spatial coordinate
x. Join each value to its next live continuation. Continuation edges
have bounded displacement; each vertex has at most one predecessor
and one successor. Two-live-operand gates are retained. A transport
swap with dead padding can instead be represented by the live
operand's diagonal continuation.

The continuation graph consists of paths and cycles. Values retained
across the period seam are persistent cycles. Scratch prepared and
discarded within a period gives an open path. Every ancilla's reset
precedes its first use in the selected cohort; initial useful inputs
are actual input or prescribed prepared registers.

At every retained vertex install two mutable registers A_v and B_v.
At every tick apply the SAME fixed local layers:

    (i)   SWAP A_v and B_v on site;
    (ii)  SWAP B_v and A_successor(v) on every continuation edge;
    (iii) RESET A_v at open-path sources;
    (iv)  apply fixed gates between equal-phase A vertices.

The edge layer is a matching: each edge uses its source's B factor
and destination's A factor. A fixed number of microphases suffices
for the finite gate library. Resets are actual noisy local channels.

For an open path v_0,...,v_l, the exact transport law is

    A'_v_j = A_v_(j-1)  (j>0),     A'_v_0 = |0>,
    B'_v_j = B_v_(j+1)  (j<l),     B'_v_l = A_v_l.

Discarded states travel backward. Their only re-entry to the useful
forward track passes through a source reset. Backward registers count.

At global tick t, forward phase phi has cohort label t-phi modulo P.
Continuation preserves that label; equal-phase gate inputs have a
common cohort; reset births enter the correct cohort. Every cohort
therefore executes the intended source circuit.

This is a tensor-factor channel identity, not an independence
assumption. Arbitrarily entangled unselected cohorts and arbitrary
CPTP operations on them cannot change the selected marginal unless
a corruption touches an actual selected interaction operand.
Such interactions are counted as faults.

5. LOCAL GEOMETRY AND NOISE COMPATIBILITY

Keep original source coordinates and use the inherited annular map,
with P>=cW:

    (x,phi) ->
      (-R0+(R0+x)cos(2pi phi/P), (R0+x)sin(2pi phi/P)),
    R0=c1 P.

After constant geometric refinement, density, degree, continuation
length, gate length and phase-seam length are bounded. Crossings use
constant tracks. Diameter is O(P+W)=O(P).

At each microphase the selected cohort, including both operands of
transport swaps, lies in the same bounded-thickness radial tube as
the accepted full adapter. A rigid time-dependent straightening map
preserves homogeneous Poisson source geometry. The inherited wire
domination has tail exponent alpha-d+1=r+1, up to fixed constants.

Deleting unused hardware does not enlarge sufficient bad-source sets.
Equivalently extend the sparse source circuit by virtual discarded
padding in arbitrary unused states. Every good-source geometry still
satisfies the strong source contracts on the retained active operations.
Actual source resets and all active transport/gate operands remain.
No independence is assigned to off-cohort junk.

Thus (1) survives sparse compilation after fixed geometric constants
and a once-only small-intensity choice.

6. RESOURCES AND ONE FIXED INTENSITY

Let zeta=log_B Q_*. The retained occurrence count satisfies

    V=sum_phi S_m(phi) <= c w Q_*^m P,

so

    N=O(w bar_b H^(kappa+zeta)),
    L=O(bar_b H^kappa),
    U_all=O(N K P).                                      (2)

The layout builder recursively enumerates retained occurrences and
edges in O(N polylog N) description work; it does not scan the deleted
dense W-by-P rectangle. Local components and wire segments cost O(N).
Known controller preparation uses <=c w_c Q_*^m noisy writes, with
bounded depth under declared distributed access; their span can still
be H. Raw acquisition, all resets/disposal and local readout are paid.

For memory, the counter and its circuit have w,bar_b=O(log T). Set
K=ceil(T/P) and choose H satisfying

    H^r >= C lambda T w / epsilon,
    H^(r-kappa) >= C lambda w bar_b / epsilon.

The second condition pays the rounded extra period. Initial known-word
failure is also controlled. Each fixed padding family gives

    L=tilde O(T^(kappa/r)),
    N=tilde O(T^((kappa+zeta)/r)),
    U_all=tilde O(T^(1+(kappa+zeta)/r)).

One cannot simply increase B while letting the threshold shrink.
Reuse the completed fixed-intensity boost: fix baseline B0,A0, code,
alphabet and physical lambda once. Before an upper padding B use
ell0 baseline levels:

    L0=B0^ell0, tau0=A0^ell0,
    mu=lambda tau0 L0^(-r) -> 0.

The actual-source seam recurrence is

    p_j <= S_B^2 p_(j-1)^2 + c_B mu (A B^(-r))^j.

A finite boost enters each upper family's threshold and controls its
interface suffix. The baseline raw-interface sum is uniformly bounded.
These are source estimates, not a new independent coarse-noise law.
Sparse compilation adds only the finite family factor Q_*^ell0.

The accepted computable slow horizon diagonal absorbs family constants,
baseline dimensions, logarithmic factors and selection work into
T^o(1). As B_j increases,

    kappa_j+zeta_j = 1+log(C Q_*)/log B_j -> 1.

This proves the stated upper exponents at one fixed positive intensity,
with the same finite raw unknown seed. Constants and subpolynomial
factors can be enormous. No useful numerical threshold or practical
hardware estimate follows from this asymptotic statement.

7. MATCHING STATIC-HARDWARE LOWER BOUND

Let C be the input-reachable component in the graph of all possible
ideal interactions. If each edge length is at most a and there are N
registers, its radius is at most c(aN+diam(seed)+1)=O(N).
Count all persistent input-bearing wire, buffer, environmental and
readout storage. Disconnected apparatus components cannot receive
input dependence through ideal local CPTP evolution.

A fixed Poisson substream covers C at rate

    nu_C >= c lambda N^(d-alpha)=c lambda N^(-r).

For example choose a center region of linear size O(N) and radii
between two and three times a covering radius. Let those marks
trace out and replace C while acting identically elsewhere; let
all other fault maps be identity. Discard erased information,
with no later restoration source.

For a Bell input independent of apparatus preparation, the exterior
apparatus/reference marginal remains a product before the first reset,
by no-signalling. This remains true with pre-existing entanglement
among apparatus components. Resetting C makes the entire accessible
device independent of the reference. Future evolution cannot restore it.

The reset event has probability p=1-exp(-nu_C T). On that branch
final Bell overlap is 1/4; otherwise it is at most one. Therefore

    delta >= (3/4)(1-exp[-c lambda T N^(-r)]).

For delta<=1/4 this forces N>=Omega((lambda T)^(1/r)).
The same argument for the actual device bounding radius gives
L>=Omega((lambda T)^(1/r)). Under the all-register-time convention
U_all>=N T, giving the matching work exponent.

The event depends on fixed geometry; no conditional future-noise
argument is needed. The result excludes uncounted long wires,
external cached input/output, accessible input-bearing environments,
moving-carrier models and changing layouts. It is not a universal
energy lower bound.

8. A USEFUL ADAPTIVE QUANTUM OPERATION

Two adjacent protected processors can perform one internally requested
handoff through constant-width raw ports. A stores the unknown state
in protected D_A. B's own protected causal computation chooses a request
frame. All request, majority, frame and admission records are corruptible
protected registers.

Use an odd M complete sample intervals per frame. Each interval includes
protected classical copying, actual descent, local raw transfer, actual
ascent, normalization and a protected vote update. Their complete
physical source-time windows are disjoint. Latch a request at frame
start and repeat it; queue mid-frame decisions. A decides only at
frame end.

Let F_i be sufficient geometric sample failure with probability <=q<1/2.
The F_i are independent by disjoint Poisson time windows, though faulty
values need not be. Keep global bulk success G_bulk unconditioned:

    {wrong frame decision} intersect G_bulk
       is contained in {at least M/2 sample failures}.

Over J candidate frames this has probability <=J exp(-c_q M),
where c_q=D(Bern(1/2)||Bern(q)). Thus M=O(log(J/epsilon)) suffices
even for a causally selected request frame.

For this corollary, require the chosen frame end tau to be a stopping
time for the actual Poisson-source filtration. Causal fault mechanisms
and causal requests have this property. If future-aware fault values
may steer the request, the once-paid selected-window claim needs an
additional ideal-request coupling and is not asserted here. Begin the
entire charged quantum interface AFTER tau. Fresh future geometry gives

    Pr(F_Q(tau) | F_tau) <= epsilon_Q=O(lambda)

plus the fixed suffix budget. A successful request prefix is measurable
at tau. Future bulk failure is charged separately; do not condition
on the entire future bulk-good event.

Guarded protected swaps and actual raw transport implement

    D_A -> I_A -> I_B -> D_B.

All earlier I histories can be arbitrary; normalization and disabled
guards keep them away from retained quantum data. There is no raw
quantum exposure on every null frame. Only the selected quantum
handoff needs succeed.

B knows its selected admission phase internally. An outside observer
does not automatically know it. B can instead hold the received state
and perform one final descent at a public fixed deadline.

Total error is the two bulk/initialization budgets, J exp(-c_q M),
the selected quantum-interface error and any final raw-readout error.
Latency after request is O(MP), and vote/phase counters use O(log M)
logical bits. Polylogarithmic logical width/depth leaves the principal
sparse exponents unchanged; all waiting updates are charged.

This is a fixed finite number of quantum handoffs. An unbounded number
of raw quantum exchanges would accumulate interface error. A wide
encoded link is a legitimate alternative if that access is available.
Classical logarithmic synchronization cost is established communication
theory; the new bridge is to corruptible controllers and a once-paid
nonclonable transfer.

9. RESTART-CAPACITY THEOREM

Strengthen initialization as follows. A fixed prescribed finite seed S
contains x in {0,1} and all bootstrap constants. Starting from
sigma_x tensor tau, for EVERY outside mutable state tau independent
of x, the same time-homogeneous device must return x after T ticks
with error <=delta<1/2 under every allowed fault assignment.
The decoder is fixed.

Suppose an independent reset-compatible slot has probability q>0
of replacing S by sigma_x while leaving the outside arbitrary.
Interleaved noise slots satisfy this in the synchronous channel model.
An arbitrary continuous-time model needs a separately earned restart
compatibility.

THEOREM:
    log_2 dim(H_device) >= q T [1-h_2(delta)].              (3)

Proof.
Generate independent fair bits X_i and independent flags E_i~Bern(q),
1<=i<=T. At slot i, when E_i=1, an allowed fault resets the seed to
sigma_(X_i). Discard X_i from the fault source afterward; all future
bits and randomness are fresh. Other fault maps can be identity.

Conditional ONLY on E_i=1, the outside is independent of the fresh
X_i. The restart guarantee applies and promises delivery at i+T,
with all other/future geometry still averaged under its original law.

At checkpoint T the full device Q_T is an encoding. To decode X_i,
run its future averaged channel for i more ticks and perform the
fixed output. This is a CPTP decoder from Q_T, with conditional error
<=delta and no restoration source for X_i.

Give E only to the mathematical lower-bound decoder. These are not
reliable physical flags. Fano and data processing imply

    I(X_i:Q_T | E) >= q [1-h_2(delta)].

The X_i remain independent conditional on E. Chain rule and strong
subadditivity give

    I(X_1...X_T:Q_T | E)
        >= sum_i I(X_i:Q_T | E)
        >= q T [1-h_2(delta)].

Classical/quantum Holevo information in dimension D is <=log_2 D.
This proves (3). Individual bit decoders suffice; simultaneous
recovery is not required. The proof is an operational reduction to
the established quantum random-access bound.

For a prescribed seed in radius s>=1, marks with centers in a cube
of side s and radii [2s,3s] have rate

    nu_s=(2^(-alpha)-3^(-alpha)) lambda s^(-r).

For a compatible duration Delta, q=1-exp(-nu_s Delta). With bounded
local dimension,

    N s^r >= c lambda T.

Fixed s forces N=Omega(T), while the prepared sparse device has
N=T^(1/r+o(1))=o(T) for every r>1.

For bounded-range bursts, the recurrence eventually has no tail term:
p_(m+1)<=S^2 p_m^2. Depth O(log log T) gives polylogarithmic dimensions.
Prepared reuse then has N=polylog T and U=T polylog T.
Arbitrary-junk restart has lower bounds N=Omega(T), U=Omega(T^2);
the accepted one-pass construction achieves N=T polylog T,
U=T^2 polylog T. These polynomial exponents match.
The exact full-Pareto arbitrary-junk optimum is not settled.

The obstruction does not exclude a typical initial distribution,
charged commissioning before unknown-input admission, a growing
prescribed region, or a persistent external source. Those change
the initialization/information-access contract.

10. STRONGEST EXISTING METHODS AND LIMITS

Autonomous quantum processing itself is not the novelty. The 2026
Dunnweber--Styliaris--Trivedi construction gives a fixed,
translation-invariant two-dimensional QCA under its local-noise
assumptions. Its spatial symmetry is stronger than this patterned
hardware model.

A single Pareto mark can corrupt arbitrarily many specified locations
with polynomially suppressed probability. It therefore does not
directly satisfy a local-noise condition exponentially small in the
number of specified faulty locations. The accepted actual-source
recurrence is the required bridge; this report proves preservation
under sparse execution. It does not rule out adapting that other
architecture.

A naive asynchronous conversion fails: the mod-3 marching-soldiers
rule forbids advancing when a neighbor is one phase behind. Phases
0,1,2,0,1,2 around a 2-by-3 lattice rectangle block every perimeter
cell; the middle chord connects equal phases. A bounded burst can
create this permanently frozen loop, with all later fault maps
identity. This is the cyclic inconsistency already discussed by Gacs,
not a general asynchronous impossibility theorem.

No result here combines Pareto corruption with the independent
thermal-intervention or source/bath memory theorems. Actual cooling,
fabrication speed and energetic expenditure require a specified
physical realization. The present claim is a local-channel operation
and resource theorem with declared access and charged operations.

11. FINITE IMPLEMENTATIONS

sparse_adapter.py:
12 geometry/cohort cases and 4 quantum cases pass. The latter use
8 hardware qubits plus reference, arbitrary initial junk, actual reset
births, a two-CNOT ancilla lifetime and arbitrary CPTP channels on
unselected cohorts. Maximum reference trace-distance discrepancy:
1.485e-15.

Illustrative B=31, P=62, K=17:
128 sparse registers versus 3968 dense registers;
134912 all-register updates versus 4182272.
This factor-31 case validates the adapter identity/count, not a
physical noise threshold.

framed_transfer.py:
1280 exhaustive three-frame classical cases pass, including causal
request policies and every pattern with <=1 wrong sample per
three-symbol frame. All release/admission actions occur at frame end.
24 six-qubit handoff checks with correlated states and arbitrary
port/environment channels have maximum numerical discrepancy 1.667e-15.

The small harness's logical controllers stand for protected operations
whose failures are charged analytically. Past records provided to
a test policy do not create free physical feedback.

At illustrative independent bit-flip rate q=0.05, a three-symbol
majority has exact error 0.00725 and Chernoff bound 0.0828191.
This is a probability example, not an inferred physical threshold.
