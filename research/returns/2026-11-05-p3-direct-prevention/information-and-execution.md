# Information cost, actual execution and commissioning bill

This page accompanies the [direct acquired prevention theorem](README.md) and [finite local assays and deployment](assay-and-commissioning.md). It distinguishes an acquired mean-lifetime decision from absolute prediction, and supplied synthetic execution from a physical commissioning run. Original sources and saved results are preserved unchanged.

## Absolute prediction can require more local exposure

The [prediction lower bound](package/next6_decision_theory/prediction_lower_bound.tex) grants arbitrary calibrated local fields, perfect clocks, complete continuous released-cell records, adaptive stopping, parallel cells with summed exposure, and free parameter-independent pins in its relaxed information bill. For uniform log-lifetime accuracy epsilon with confidence 1-delta, it proves

```
E W >= N^2 / (512 e epsilon^2) * kl(1-delta, delta).
```

Two nearby models have local logits separated at order epsilon/N, a fixed relative global lifetime change, and local information rate of order epsilon squared/N squared. This is a same-access local exposure lower bound under the stated model. It is not a lower bound on every hardware service or every decision problem.

At fixed degree, menu size, margins and accuracy, the supplied direct construction has coarse acquisition of order N up to logarithms, a boundary decision whose local trial count is independent of N after certificates, and ordinary mean-preparation with order N summed exposure. Uniform absolute prediction has the quadratic lower bound above. Calibration, installation, field setting, propagation and other services remain paid. The separation concerns requiring an absolute forecast; an equally informed ordinary direct cancellation method can match the decision construction.

The [comparison source](package/next6_root/comparison_and_results.tex) retains capacity, product-formula, likelihood and direct boundary-flux comparators, including prior local-capacity arguments with explicit flatness. Rare probability can cancel from a decision without yielding absolute lifetime. No historical-priority claim follows.

## Executed local decisions

The supplied [execution record](package/next6_root/direct_decision_execution.json) uses heterogeneous interacting rings at N=64, 256, 1024 and 4096 with three context-dependent crossing clock menus. Local binary outcomes and coarse bounds are supplied to the decision; numerical energy/clock tables, the rare probability and absolute lifetime are not supplied to that decision.

Each size uses 12,288 finite holds and 3,061.76 active exposure units. The recorded simultaneous confidence is 98% under the declared law and coarse guarantees.

The warm-up window is [2,2.001]. Its failure-probability bound is below 1.727e-6 at N=64 and decreases in these examples. Summed warm-up exposure is 2.001N, with installation and other services additional. These are seeded synthetic local records, not a hardware calibration or an executed global first-catastrophe experiment. The mean guarantee comes from the supplied proof and certificates rather than observed rare failures.

A separate canonical grouped-rate execution covers N=64, 4096 and 262144 and six assays: 49,152 groups per menu, 294,912 groups total, 1,179,647 pin/release operations and 1,597,823 active exposure units. One censored group's cost is retained. Observed rate error below 0.54% does not replace the issued 12.5% finite-confidence accuracy guarantee.

## Full coarse calibration is expensive

The [calibration ledger](package/next6_root/calibration_costs.json) is a sufficient-cost evaluation and deterministic margin diagnostic, **not an executed calibration**. Floating margin checks have strict slack; the report supplies the actual certificate formulas.

| N | Contexts | Reads per start and context | Pin/read count | Active exposure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 64 | 768 | 66,643 | 102,363,648 | 516,936,422.4 |
| 256 | 3,072 | 73,574 | 452,038,656 | 2,282,795,212.8 |
| 1024 | 12,288 | 80,505 | 1,978,490,880 | 9,991,378,944 |
| 4096 | 49,152 | 87,437 | 8,595,406,848 | 43,406,804,582.4 |

The ledger uses nominal holds of 5, deadline tolerance +/-0.05, conditional bias 0.0005, statistical radius 0.01 and confidence allocation delta=0.005. Pin/read/context setting, timer, field, slew and installation services are extra. These conservative constants are enormous; the result does not become a cheap physical procedure by omitting calibration.

The preserved [stronger-timer variant](package/next6_root/stronger_timer_contract_unresolved.json) has a different, more conservative timer contract. Its N=64 example ratios differ from the preferred-timer final report. That branch's filename does not make the whole final construction unresolved. The current [report source](package/next6_root/direct_prevention_report.tex) and current execution govern their declared timer assumptions. Capital ran no new scientific script, solver, simulation or proof audit.
