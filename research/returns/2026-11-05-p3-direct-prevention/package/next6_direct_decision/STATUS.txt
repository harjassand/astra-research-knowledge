kinetic acceleration for a rare desired completion event, from an

Scope:
The actual reciprocal kinetic multiplier response law is an additional
declared intervention resource. It is not inferred from arbitrary field
response or from finite local observations. If it is unavailable, this
is a mathematical design theorem for that generator family, not a
physical intervention capability on the original object.

The result minimizes desired completion time. It does not solve the
general maximization problem of catastrophe prevention.

Bounded gains c<=C improve mean by at most C. Actual completion exposure
can remain exponentially large. The fully evaluated sufficient schedule
is enormous (~10^61.65 potential updates for the stated N=128 instance).
This is not executed and not a lower bound. The theorem earns a global
optimization deduction beyond finite-menu record-based comparison, not
an economical prototype or a separation from all possible prior methods.
