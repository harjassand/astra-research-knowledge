Can rare static conditional access plus an acquired/certified mixing bound compile into a sampler of the first-event time, terminal mark and an actual bounded precursor path, with no inverse-rarity trajectory cost, on a succinct NONREVERSIBLE process and from an arbitrary efficiently sampled initial law?

Potential central operation: stationary blocks of length L separated by an unobserved mixing buffer b. Use stationary failure-conditioned samples and the time-reversed local kernel to construct an inverse-multiplicity Palm proposal for a whole block conditioned on hitting. Approximate regeneration only on the aggregated {no event, marked event path} kernel costs eta*q per block, not eta, so geometric number of blocks has O(eta) aggregate error independent of q. Ignored buffer and short-prefix hits are bounded by their stationary hitting mass divided by block success mass, controlled via an additive-symmetrization gap and occupation second moment. Estimate block success probability by a bounded Palm observable; sample the geometric wait in binary, not by looping over failures.

Candidate broad payoff: generate a probability-controlled failure mechanism and recent trajectory after an exponentially long operating time; full exact path has intrinsically large output size, which is not hidden. Target TV law of a finitely encoded/rounded event record, rather than claiming finite-valued outputs have TV-small distance to continuous real times.

A preliminary ordinary burn-in followed by one unobserved mixing buffer may handle arbitrary initial laws: observed early failures are returned exactly; the unnormalized surviving distribution after burn-in is pointwise bounded by (1+eta)pi. This prices first hidden-buffer failures without dividing by the probability of surviving the initial transient.
