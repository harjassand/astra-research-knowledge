INDEPENDENT ROUTE -- NOT SELECTED; SHARP TARGET INCOMPLETE

The collaborator investigated whether a harmonic well maximizes finite-time
mutual-information capacity among all uniformly convex overdamped thermal
systems at fixed preparation variance:
 dX=-grad V dt+sqrt(2theta)dB, Hessian V>=mI, Tr Cov(X0)<=P.

It obtained, using established log-Harnack and Bayesian Fisher-information
machinery, the bound I(X0;Xt)<=d/2 times s for s<=1, or (1+log s) for s>=1,
where s=mP/[d theta(exp(2mt)-1)]. This lies within d/2 nats of the harmonic
capacity d/2 log(1+s); the sharper harmonic bound was obtained for Gaussian
preparation. A complete sharp theorem for arbitrary preparations was not proved.
No sufficient novelty or major capability was established for selection.

The missing step was a sharp comparison for contraction interleaved with
thermal noise. Aishwarya--Li's entropy Kneser--Poulsen work,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.03664, concerns contraction before Gaussian noise
and was not treated as settling this step.

The code records 45 exploratory discretized capacity calculations with no
nonlinear convex-well counterexample. Those calculations are not a proof.
Neither this incomplete target nor its calculations are premises of the main
quantum result. The source files are retained as an independent research record.
