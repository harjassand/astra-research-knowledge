# Route selection

Question: Can finite-time, noisy equilibrium observations certify a material's macroscopic transport coefficient at arbitrarily long scales?

Empirical mismatch: slowly decaying hydrodynamic current correlations and anomalous transport make finite-time Green–Kubo predictions unreliable.

Indispensable operation: certify the integral of the unobserved correlation tail (equivalently the zero-frequency response) from finite-resolution observations.

First construction: positive exponential relaxation mixtures with a small spectral weight at an arbitrarily slow rate produce nearly indistinguishable finite-time correlations but arbitrarily different DC transport. Both models can be passive reversible OU processes, so energy conservation / equilibrium / fluctuation-dissipation assumptions alone do not remove the ambiguity.

I will attempt a sharp minimax observation theorem and exact constructive identified interval when a physical relaxation-rate bound is independently supplied. The original scale-independent certification endpoint will be marked unclosed if it requires that bound; I will not relabel the restricted result as the original consequence.
