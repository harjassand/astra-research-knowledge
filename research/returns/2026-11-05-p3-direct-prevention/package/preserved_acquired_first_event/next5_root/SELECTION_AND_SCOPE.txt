ACQUIRED JOINT FIRST-EVENT CAPABILITY — 5 November 2026

Selected full question
Can local controlled evidence support the joint first-disconnection time,
terminal state and paid recent precursor window of an interacting reliability
system, and a quantitative decision among actual permitted interventions,
without observing a global failure or simulating its entire lifetime?

Completed endpoint
1. A reusable fitted-model route: acquired local energy/rate confidence
   envelopes imply a full marked-record TV bound through short Palm proposals,
   accepted-submeasure Hellinger distance, and a separate geometric-clock
   comparison. The theorem covers a common arbitrary supplied initial law.
   A shared rare/direct branch and explicit sufficient finite precision close
   the algorithmic interface. Paid safe preparation and bounded-loss regret
   are composed explicitly. There is no 1/pi(B) or mean-lifetime factor.
2. An independently constructed experiment-fed route: balanced observations
   supply true Gibbs coins; actual censored release races and a bounded walk
   supply operating P=P* coins. Heavy completions plus measured local odds
   replace an unknown numerical weight oracle. Conditional errors, conditional
   concentration, finite clocks/caps and safe preparation close the global
   first-event and decision operation. Fresh physical queries are consumed by
   every draw. This is not an unlimited offline model acquired once.
3. The two routes can be mixed by acquiring static weights finely and querying
   true operating kinetics. No route is declared universally cheaper.

Exact scope
- The general marked-law bridge is a finite-chain theorem with common support,
  event set, lazy uniformizer, positive numerical gap and stationary mass floor,
  local proposal divergence bounds, and the accepted static/reverse interfaces.
- The acquired physical instantiation uses the declared pairwise Gibbs binary
  model and all-terminal network-disconnection predicate. Model closure,
  local detailed balance, faithful local copies, fixed/reproduced operating
  environments and the known local graph are substantive premises.
- Uniform positive clock bounds r_*,r^* for every queried context, a biased-cell
  relaxation bound, fine field/timing metrology, priced state pins, and certified
  pinned Dobrushin/static contrast margins are supplied or acquired within their
  declared finite schedules. Energy-only measurements do not certify arbitrary
  clocks at unvisited contexts. Unknown discontinuous clock dependence on a
  slightly changed field cannot be controlled by field accuracy alone.
- Physical equilibrium-safe preparation uses a certified safe stationary mass
  at least 1/2 for every considered intervention. A smaller floor s_* is possible
  only with an explicit inverse-safe-mass preparation/rejection bill. Statistical
  certification at an exact boundary may remain unresolved without strict slack.
- Decisions choose a constant menu intervention after acquisition and before
  deployment/preparation. No unpriced instantaneous feedback, live-system hold,
  arbitrary adaptive policy optimization, or protection during acquisition is
  granted. A repairable continuation is required during the selected preparation.
- The record retains a specified finite precursor window. Its value, aggregate
  physical update rate, timestamp digits, state encoding and memory are paid.
  TV is for the same quantized record; no discrete law is TV-close to an
  unquantized continuous clock.
- Absolute joint TV and bounded-loss regret do not give relative control of
  every tiny subtype or unbounded lifetime loss. The acquired relative whole-CDF
  operation is preserved separately.

Hardest interfaces actually closed
- CDF agreement does not imply marked-law stability: a three-state chain gives
  identical first-event CDFs and terminal-state TV 0.98.
- Comparing full trajectories up to a rare hit can pay an unnecessary enormous
  stopped-path information cost. Short marked proposals are compared instead;
  their acceptance floor is g/8, not the rare stationary mass.
- A supplied-weight heavy-prefix algorithm could not simply be invoked for an
  unknown physical Gibbs law. The exact identity pi(D)=[W(x_D)/W(x_0)]H_0/H_D
  and a path of <=N locally measured log odds supply the missing ratio.
- Artificial-field clocks do not identify actual operating clocks. Every
  dynamic factory races an actual operating exit against a known deadline.
- Conditional-TV coins from reused cells need not be iid. Conditional bounded
  means and conditional accepted-mark laws supply the concentration/rejection
  interfaces, preserving log(1/delta) in abstract query counts.

Independent destinations, completed and retained
- next5_fixedlaw: a complete finite rational compiler to a globally certified
  1/2-contraction in R^4 with known unique fixed point; exact event reachability
  remains undecidable. Positive absolute margins admit a finite logarithmic
  horizon. This is an explicit quantitative boundary; general PWA reachability
  is established and homogenization may make the contraction result a known
  corollary. It was not selected as a stronger historical breakthrough.
- next5_physical: all causal conjugate-force experiments obey a quantitative
  finite-time/finite-effort transport information bound despite arbitrarily
  large transport. A finite-slew specified-pair pulse achieves the active
  visibility scaling. Exact equilibrium initialization, model-order qualification,
  force/readout/clock/work-reserve bills remain explicit. This does not replace
  the earlier fixed-mechanics thermal or effusion constructions.

Executed here
- Exact-rational conservative root uncertainty certificates and a small analytic
  three-state reference calculation. It is a certificate diagnostic, not a
  general event engine or a speed comparison; the small chain has a better exact
  solver. Its precursor is trivial, so the general path assertion is from proof.
- Independent exact-rational finite-factory and pin-mass identity audits.
- Independent finite contraction compiler and explicit precision diagnostics.
- Independent controlled-transport symbolic checks, specified-pair Gaussian
  endpoint simulations and a directed-rounding finite-meter/work certificate.
No physical experiment, hardware factory, or full general correlated cut engine
was executed in this continuation.

Useful failed attempts and repairs preserved
- Full-lifetime stepwise coupling; CDF-to-mark transfer; assumed unknown weights;
  clock transport from biased experiments; independent-output assumptions for
  reused cells; high-level delta/D calibration coupling that would worsen static
  confidence costs; and omission of a safe-mass floor were all rejected or fixed.
- The first lambda=10^-6 root certificate check correctly failed the rare-branch
  premise Lp<=1. The direct-branch theorem covers that regime; no false rare
  certificate was issued. Displayed executions use 10^-12,10^-30,10^-100.
- Finite clock arithmetic is kept separate from physical response/timing access.
- The sufficient factory example has 1656 pins and 33120 units of censored dwell
  at theta=2^-20, rho=2 before ancillary services. It is not an economical device
  or a lower bound. The prior thirteen-year acquisition example is unchanged.
