Retained useful development stages, 5 November 2026.

1. The initial moment bound used E[Y^2|past] <= 4 lambda_max^2,
   leading to a valid conservative median-of-means schedule with
   m=ceil(256 kappa^2 eta^-2). Its complete synthetic execution is
   retained in rate_assay_initial_mom_execution.json, with its original
   reproduction program audit_rate_assay_initial_mom.py. It remains a
   valid sufficient schedule, not the final recommended one.

2. Averaging before applying the maximum-rate bound gives the sharper
   E[Y^2|past] <= 4 lambda_max mu. The final source therefore supplies a
   median-of-means schedule with m=ceil(256 kappa eta^-2), and a smaller
   direct arithmetic-mean schedule from a fully proved conditional
   Bernstein inequality. The final growing-family execution uses this
   direct-mean option, with an exact integer log2 ceiling for confidence.

3. The original lower-bound example used independent unbiased bits,
   p=2^-N, symmetric operating rates lambda_+/- and field-independent
   attempt clocks 2 lambda_+/-. It already gave the valid bound
   KL <= 64 lambda0 eta^2 E[total local exposure]. That derivation is
   retained here but is NOT the final hard family: q=1/2 need not lie
   in the chosen endpoint's uniformly reliable class.

   The final theorem instead permits any known q_star in (0,1/4].
   The invariant target mass is q_star^N. Boundary recovery rates are
   lambda_+/-, while failure rates are lambda_+/- q_star/(1-q_star).
   Attempt clocks are lambda_+/-/(1-q_star), unchanged by calibration
   fields. Choosing q_star <= qbar/2 places the hard pair strictly
   inside every stated reliable family with qbar<1/2. The same displayed
   divergence bound remains valid, with slack. The current lower-bound
   numerical checks use q_star=1/4. The historical initial MoM record
   contains the earlier q=1/2 algebra and is labeled as historical.

4. The final execution replaces ordinary floating reciprocal quantization
   and interval calculation by exact integer quotients and rational
   aggregate/interval endpoints. A dyadic timestamp grid and half of the
   declared output-rounding allowance leave explicit arithmetic slack.
   The same latent synthetic waits were rerun with that final service.
   Rate estimates changed by about 0.00012; dwell and pin counts did not.
   Current PDF tables and the canonical execution JSON use the final
   exact-arithmetic service.

No accepted prior package or completed independent continuum file was
edited during these changes.
