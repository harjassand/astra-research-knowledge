Invent a practical way to manufacture programmed three-dimensional optical interconnects in parallel.

Can a genuinely new fabrication mechanism make useful three-dimensional optical routing substantially cheaper than the best suitable existing fabrication process, while preserving optical performance and manufacturing yield? The output sought is a concrete, physically implementable invention with a worked design and quantitative evidence. Mathematical programmability of an ideal assembly model is not the manufactured capability.

Use the following full operation as the target. A digital route description is converted into a finished, packaged passive optical connection. Choose one operating wavelength lambda0 between 600 and 1600 nm and a common material/process platform. A route has length 50--100 micrometres, lies in a 40 by 40 by 20 micrometre routing volume, joins specified input/output ports, and contains at least two 90-degree changes of direction and an out-of-plane displacement of at least 5 micrometres. The input is a list of centreline segments or smooth control points, with bend radius at least 2 micrometres and separation at least 4 micrometres between non-neighbouring portions. You may specify a finite fabrication grid and finite geometric tolerances, provided they still admit a substantial family of distinct routes under this contract. This is an optical interconnect, not a structural-colour sample or an unaddressed periodic crystal.

The useful optical output is a single guided connection with end-to-end insertion loss at most 3 dB, including the actual two port couplers, and at least a 1-percent fractional bandwidth around lambda0. The same fabrication procedure must handle different admitted digital routes without hand-adjusting individual building blocks after assembly. You may choose the input/output mode and coupler technology, but their preparation, alignment and losses belong to the device. Specify allowable optical input power and the temperature/environmental range. A three-dimensional bandgap crystal with an intentional defect is one possible representation, not a required method. Other physical architectures, material systems, replication methods or routes to the same useful operation are welcome.

The manufacturing challenge is a batch of 10,000 devices containing at least ten different route designs, with at least 90 percent working-device yield under the stated performance test. The central invention must use parallel assembly, parallel pattern transfer, replication or another actual mechanism that avoids writing every final three-dimensional voxel serially. A patterned master or seed is allowed; all its design, manufacture, reuse lifetime and amortized cost must be paid. Addressable building blocks are allowed; every unique sequence, surface treatment, encoding step and supply stream must be produced by an explicit attainable process. A supplied perfect geometric template, a free local controller, exact arbitrary pair potentials or a finished programmed scaffold cannot stand in for that process.

Aim for at least a tenfold reduction in total cost per working device against the strongest suitable existing process for the SAME batch, route diversity, optical ports and tolerances. Identify that comparator rather than choosing a deliberately weak serial implementation. Include direct laser writing, multilayer lithography and replication where they are relevant competitors, and credit any existing process already providing the claimed operation. A reduction in an isolated assembly step does not meet the target if synthesis, templates, conversion, inspection or packaging consume the saving. If the absolute tenfold target is defeated by a stronger incumbent, that is substantive information: the invention must then acquire a different demonstrated manufacturing or optical capability that the incumbent lacks, rather than merely relabel known work as new.

Construct the physical mechanism far enough to specify actual constituents and dimensions; how the route description changes the fabrication inputs; how growth, pattern transfer or assembly produces that particular route; how the optical material survives the complete processing sequence; and how the finished device is coupled and tested. Use measured or defensibly bounded complex refractive indices at the chosen wavelength for the FINAL processed material, not only a lossless bulk index. Include absorption, scattering, finite-size leakage, bends, surface roughness, disorder, shrinkage, drying/conversion damage and relevant correlated defects. Explain which defects are harmless, detected, repaired or fatal. A union bound is valid without independent defects, but a high yield cannot be inferred by multiplying single-site yields without evidence about the actual correlations.

All preparation and full-cycle costs matter: precursor and scaffold/monomer manufacture, sequence diversity or master fabrication, purification, folding/annealing, solvent and buffer, functionalization, coating/infiltration or material conversion, thermal treatments, drying, alignment, packaging, inspection, rejected batches, equipment occupancy, energy, labour, waste, maintenance and consumables. Distinguish a first demonstrator from the 10,000-device batch. Give numerical processing times, throughput, material quantities and a plausible costing range, with dated evidence or explicit engineering estimates. Do not treat existing specialized laboratory equipment or low-yield conversion as free. Model sensitivity to the few physical uncertainties that could eliminate the advantage.

Useful starting constructions and their limits are available. DNA-origami tetrapods have actually produced diamond-lattice photonic crystals, with material conversion and optical characterization; this does not supply prescribed defect routes or a working optical interconnect. More recent torsion-controlled assemblies show competing diamond/hexagonal/twinned/clathrate structures, so a designed local contact does not guarantee a unique macroscopic phase. Hybrid three-dimensional laser-printed polymer/air waveguides already provide compact bends and confinement transitions, so three-dimensional routing alone is not new.

Optional primary starting points:
- Posnjak and collaborators, DNA-origami photonic crystals, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.10884v2, particularly the crystal-growth, conversion and optical-property experiments.
- Yin, Kostyurina, Nickel, Liedl and Posnjak, https://arxiv.org/html/2607.07877v1, particularly competing binding torsions and phase selection.
- Sharma, Grabulosa, Jung and Brunner, https://arxiv.org/html/2605.24990v1, particularly the actual fabrication process and measured loss definitions for three-dimensional hybrid waveguides.

For an inexpensive first physical discriminant, develop a smaller prototype that exercises the claimed NEW routing/manufacturing mechanism and its most dangerous failure mode. Specify an actual build sequence, necessary components and access, measurements, ordinary controls, expected signals, numerical pass/fail conditions and total experimental cost. This experiment is to be designed, not performed here. A successful small optical or assembly component must not be presented as the complete 10,000-device manufacturing result.

Please pursue the full invention question. Produce a concrete design and work through its governing optical/fabrication calculations with executable models where useful; distinguish measured inputs, derived consequences and untested physical assumptions. The aim is to discover and develop a new capability that could work, not a survey, a proof of ideal universality or a proposal-only answer. You may find a different mechanism, challenge the targets or show that a proposed route fails, but a route-specific failure is not a general impossibility for manufacturing.

The suggested papers and supplied premises are starting points, not a limit on your research. You may read them, seek additional papers, literature, available Astra work or other sources, change mathematical representations, introduce different ideas and methods, and challenge my framing while pursuing the full question. You choose your sources and approach without further permission.
