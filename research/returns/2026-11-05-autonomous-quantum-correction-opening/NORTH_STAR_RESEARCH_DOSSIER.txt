1. THE HARD TARGET AND THE ACTUAL MISSING OPERATION

Desired capability: fixed local time-independent driven/dissipative dynamics
in 2D or 3D, finite site dimension/density/range/rates, preserving unknown
encoded quantum information for exp(c L^a) time under nonzero local noise on
data AND controllers/ancillas. No perfect global clock or noiseless decoder
operates during storage. Driven resets consume power and export entropy;
autonomous correction is distinct from passive equilibrium storage.

A decisive proof would construct the physical rule and establish at every
encoding level a joint fault recursion eta_(j+1)<=C eta_j^(1+delta), delta>0,
for the actual quantum macro-locations. It must preserve quantum gate order
and control idle exposure while classical history is repaired. A natural noise
contract is a per-site bounded local GKLS/cb-norm perturbation independent of
L and storage duration. Encoding and readout costs/noise require their own
contracts. No such complete theorem was proved here.

The most promising concrete mechanism uses fixed main quantum registers,
per-register NEXTOP tags, one owner per gate, atomic all-lock grant, a short
reservation lease, one-shot in-place U and a longer independent deadline.
COMMIT advances tags once even if U was omitted. Gate hazard is independent
of the reference-entangled input. Controller progress never waits for quantum
data. This repairs several precise failed approaches, but not the hierarchy.

The remaining operation is WAIT TO CAUSAL ELIGIBILITY. A repaired classical
history must yield the intended physical worldline frontier outside sparse
damage. Waiting for a predecessor must execute protected lower-level idles or
have a sparse fault witness. Bounded classical dwell can be a heartbeat while
NEXTOP makes no progress. Unknown quantum data cannot be restored by copying
classical old/new histories. The appended proofs stop at this boundary.

proposals implement the explicit owner module. For clean continuously eligible
requests, a fixed local Poisson word executes U with probability p>0 regardless
of outside state. Thus P(no U by t)<=(1-p)^floor(t/S).

An H-stage final deadline commits a local omitted gate if U did not fire.
With h=H/(4nud), m=max(0,floor(h/S)-2),
 beta_H=exp[-H(log2-1/2)]+(2h/S+4)(1-p)^m,
each adaptive-start omission is dominated by a fixed proposal-clock event in

 P(all fixed marked service witnesses F)<=beta_H^(|F|/chi).
All dimensions, rates and supports are independent of device size. Full

frontier, conflicting eligible operations on one worldline are already excluded:

PROVED B — A SECULAR-BATH OBSTRUCTION COVERING EVERY ENCODER AND DECODER.
Let H=sum h_X on N qubits have distinct ordered nonzero energy gaps and
sup_i sum_(X contains i)||h_X||<=J. Use the exact fully secular Davies generator
with independent one-site X,Y,Z baths, whose rates are >=g>0 for |omega|<=4J.
Then every qubit encoding and every final decoder satisfy
 F_ent(t)<=(1+exp(-Ngt))/2.

Essential proof: nonzero-frequency jumps are rank one. Per site/eigenstate,
offdiagonal Pauli weight is >=2 and its energy-weighted second moment <=12J^2.
At least one unit lies in the rate-bounded window; total rank-one jump loss
R>=NgI. The branch without such a jump has trace <=e^-Ngt; all other branches
are entanglement breaking. Bell overlap after arbitrary recovery is therefore
at most (1+e^-Ngt)/2. The complete proof is appended.

APPLICABILITY IS PROVED on affine perturbation spaces containing X,Z onsite
and ZZ along a spanning nearest-neighbor path. Huang's nonresonant witness Hw
and a homogeneous gap-discriminant polynomial give
p(H0+tHw)=t^deg(p)p(Hw+H0/t)!=0 for large t, for ANY fixed H0.
The exceptional perturbations thus have measure zero in EVERY small open box.
Fully occupied rectangular cubic lattices qualify. Local fields alone and
arbitrary restricted supports do not automatically qualify.

This excludes an open robust memory region in that exact-secular class with
UNIFORM generator rates. It does not exclude physical fixed-coupling quantum
memory: resolving shrinking splittings may require a microscopic bath coupling
decreasing with N, making physical g_N vanish. Finite-resolution/quasi-local
baths are different models. These time-unit and geometry qualifications are
essential, not optional disclaimers.

PROVED W — EXTENSIVE COLD/THERMAL DOMINATION COST.
For a commuting Pauli model with check weight<=w, qubit degree<=D and no
unprotected qubit, pack M>=N/[D(w-1)+1] qubits with disjoint check neighborhoods.
Their independent valid syndrome excitations yield
 Z_syn>=(1+e^-betaD)^M.

PROOF B — GENERAL BATH OBSTRUCTION AND AFFINE APPLICABILITY

PRINCIPAL NORTH-STAR ATTACK: A UNIFORM BATH MODEL IS LOAD BEARING
5 November 2026. Direct proof, independently audited by researcher B.

Target: robust passive quantum storage under microscopic perturbations. The
principal independently reached the split-frequency toy mechanism reported by
B, then constructed the general bound below. This is an obstruction to a bath
model/transfer argument, not a physical quantum-memory impossibility theorem.

THEOREM. Let H on N qubits have simple spectrum and distinct ordered nonzero
energy gaps. Write H=sum_X h_X with sup_i sum_(X contains i)||h_X||<=J, J>0.
Use the exact fully secular Davies generator with independent one-site X,Y,Z
bath couplings, and suppose all bath rates gamma_(i,a)(omega)>=g>0 for
|omega|<=4J, uniformly in i,a,N. A Lamb-shift Hamiltonian commuting with H
is allowed. Then for ANY encoding channel of one qubit and ANY decoding
channel, entanglement fidelity at time t is bounded by
  F_ent(t)<=[1+exp(-N g t)]/2.                               (1)
Fidelity >=1-epsilon, epsilon<1/2, requires
  t<=-log(1-2epsilon)/(N g).

The positive-window bath hypothesis includes KMS absorption at fixed finite
temperature when spectra are positive continuous on that window. It can fail
for super-Ohmic spectra vanishing at zero. No assertion that arbitrary local
Hamiltonian families have the required nonresonance is made; it must be proved
for any claimed application. Exact gauge-protected degeneracy evades it.

PROOF. For a nonzero frequency, distinct gaps make each Davies jump a scalar
multiple of |m><n|, hence rank one. For one energy eigenstate |n> and site i,
 sum_a sum_(m!=n)|<m|sigma_(i,a)|n>|²
   =3-sum_a<n|sigma_(i,a)|n>²>=2.
The site's Bloch vector has length at most one. Also
 sum_a sum_m(Em-En)²|<m|sigma_(i,a)|n>|²
   =sum_a||[H,sigma_(i,a)]|n>||²<=12J².
Thus at most3/4 of the offdiagonal spectral weight lies outside |omega|<=4J;
at least one unit lies inside. The loss operator R of all nonzero-frequency
jumps is diagonal and obeys R>=N g I.

Separate the generator into L0 and the CP nonzero-frequency jump recycling.
L0 contains Hamiltonian evolution, zero-frequency dissipators and -{R,.}/2.
It generates a CP trace-decreasing map F_t with
  Tr F_t(input)<=exp(-Ngt)Tr input
for positive inputs, including an external reference. The Dyson/jump expansion
is exp(tL)=F_t+G_t. Every branch in G_t contains a rank-one jump and is therefore
entanglement breaking; arbitrary earlier/later system CP maps and mixtures
preserve this fact. G_t is an entanglement-breaking subchannel.

Encode half a Bell pair. If the F_t branch has trace p<=exp(-Ngt), its final
Bell overlap after decoding is <=p. The separable G_t branch has trace1-p
and overlap <=(1-p)/2. This proves (1), uniformly over mixed encodings and
global decoders. No ordinary correlation-decay argument is used. QED.

EXACT SMALL TEST. H_epsilon=(Delta I+epsilon Z_L) tensor|1><1|_g,
with coupling I tensor X_g and 0<epsilon<Delta, has the SAME ground code as
H0, gap>=Delta-epsilon, and Hamiltonian norm error epsilon. At epsilon0,
the jumps I tensor sigma+/- preserve the logical qubit. At every positive
epsilon, frequencies Delta+epsilon and Delta-epsilon split into separate
P+ tensor sigma+/- and P- tensor sigma+/- jumps. Ground-encoded Bell fidelity,
even after the best decoder, is exactly(1+exp(-Gamma_epsilon t))/2,
Gamma_epsilon=[gamma_up(Delta+epsilon)+gamma_up(Delta-epsilon)]/2.
It tends to a positive rate as epsilon->0 at fixed positive temperature.
B's independent note proves the optimal-decoder assertion via negativity.
This toy's bare local logical qubit is NOT a protected quantum code.

GENERIC LOCAL-FAMILY COROLLARY (subsequent exact literature check).
The earlier caution about unproved nonresonance can be discharged on specified
regular-lattice coefficient spaces, rather than on arbitrary constrained models.
Huang, arXiv2104.02053v1, Theorem2 and AppendixC.2, proves distinct gaps almost
everywhere for independently parametrized nearest-neighbor qubit Hamiltonians,
including onsite X,Z and ZZ bonds. The polynomial witness embeds along a
nearest-neighbor Hamiltonian path through a rectangular lattice. Alternatively,
Lee--Pilatowsky-Cameo--Choi arXiv2609.26885v1, PropositionS2 (Proposition1),
includes all onsite X,Z and nearest-neighbor ZZ,XZ in its parameter space and
proves all-order nonresonance; only order2 is needed here.

Let P be a finite fixed-range Pauli family on a rectangular regular lattice
containing those path terms, and let coefficients range over any bounded open
set. Bound the per-site interaction norm uniformly by Jbar using the fixed
range and coefficient bounds. The exceptional polynomial-zero set has Lebesgue
measure0 in EVERY open sub-neighborhood. If bath rates are uniformly >=g on
[-4Jbar,4Jbar], (1) holds for almost every coefficient tuple there. Thus no open
coefficient region within this precisely defined fully secular bath model can
uniformly support long-lived quantum storage. For cubic nearest-neighbor
families these are two-body interactions plus onsite fields.

This does not establish the same statement in symmetry-restricted, translation-
invariant, irregular-support or exact-gauge families without a separate witness.
It also does not change the physical warning about the infinite secular limit.
The genericity machinery is established prior art; the corollary is its direct
composition with (1), not a new spectral-genericity discovery.

AFFINE PERTURBATION STRENGTHENING (B's subsequent applicability proof).
Let H0 be ANY fixed Hamiltonian, even outside the chosen perturbation span.
Suppose the allowed perturbation directions contain all X_i,Z_i and the ZZ
bonds ALONG ONE SPANNING NEAREST-NEIGHBOR PATH of the physical qubits. Huang's
Lemma7 supplies a nonresonant witness Hw in that span. Let p(H) be the symmetric
product of all forbidden equal-gap differences, a homogeneous polynomial in
matrix entries, with p(Hw)!=0. Then
  p(H0+tHw)=t^degree(p) p(Hw+H0/t)!=0
for sufficiently large positive t. Thus the polynomial in perturbation
coefficients p(H0+sum u_j P_j) is not identically zero. Its zero set has
measure0 in EVERY arbitrarily small coefficient box. This proves distinct
gaps for almost every arbitrarily weak perturbation in this affine family,
not merely for almost every base Hamiltonian.

With uniform Jbar and rate g as above, (1) follows for those perturbations.
This covers arbitrary finite-range multi-body H0 on a fully occupied rectangular
lattice. A physical support lacking such a path needs a separate witness;
local fields alone do not suffice (take H0=0). The spectral-genericity input
is established; the affine application is proved here rather than assumed.

TIME-UNIT CAVEAT. The bound uses rates in the displayed generator. Deriving a
fully secular Davies equation resolving increasingly small splittings may
require a microscopic system-bath coupling decreasing with N. In physical time
the resulting g_N can vanish, violating uniform g. This is NOT a fixed-
microscopic-coupling no-go. It is an order-of-limits obstruction to inferring
physical robustness from the exactly secular semigroup alone.


PROOF W — COLD/THERMAL COMPARISON



Question: can a fault-tolerantly prepared cold encoded state enter the proved
passive 3D memory's long-lived sector without globally thermal initialization?
The bridge remains open. The following obstruction is complete and rules out
a common attempted proof; it is not a no-go for the physical task.

Let H=sum_a (I-S_a)/2 be a commuting Pauli stabilizer Hamiltonian on N qubits,
with check weight <=w and <=D checks meeting any qubit. Assume every qubit
meets a nonidentity check (in particular any family of distance >1 has this).
Let Z_syn=sum_(valid syndromes s) exp(-beta |s|). All syndrome multiplicities
are equal, so the thermal syndrome probability of zero is exactly 1/Z_syn.

Put Delta=D(w-1). The qubit graph joining qubits that share a check has degree
<=Delta. Greedily choose an independent set of M>=N/(Delta+1) qubits. At its
j-th qubit choose a Pauli P_j anticommuting with some incident check. The
nonempty syndrome sets T_j have size <=D and are pairwise disjoint. Every
product over A of these P_j has valid syndrome disjoint-union_(j in A) T_j.
These 2^M syndromes are distinct. Therefore
 Z_syn >= product_j [1+exp(-beta |T_j|)]
       >= [1+exp(-beta D)]^M.                              (A1)
At every fixed finite beta, log Z_syn=Omega_beta(N).

In the decoded logical/syndrome factorization, a cold logical state is
rho_cold=rho_L tensor |0><0| and thermal encoding is rho_th=rho_L tensor pi_beta.
The least C with rho_cold<=C rho_th is EXACTLY C=Z_syn, by restricting to zero
syndrome. Any initial-state operator-order/path-probability comparison must
pay exp(Omega(N)). Applying it to exp[-Omega(N^eta)] thermal leakage, eta<1,
gives no useful growing retention bound. This persists under an allowed iid
p end-noise write: the no-fault event has probability (1-p)^N, so its optimal
domination factor is >=Z_syn(1-p)^N, still exp(Omega(N)) for sufficiently
small fixed p. Cleaner writing does not fix this comparison argument.

This lower bound does not imply that a cold state actually loses information
quickly. A dynamics-specific cold-start survival/committor theorem remains
necessary. Generic FT encoders and equilibrium witnesses do not supply it.
The earlier Astra CP-compression programme is not relabelled as a new result.



EXACT MEDIATOR COUNTEREXAMPLE WITH OPTIMAL DECODER



Let L,a be qubits, P_+=|0><0|_L, P_-=|1><1|_L, n_a=|1><1|_a. Set

  H_e=(Delta I_L+e Z_L) tensor n_a,
  Delta>0, 0<=e<Delta.

This is a one-site field plus a two-body term on a two-vertex graph. For every e the ground space is EXACTLY C=H_L tensor |0>_a, with gap at least Delta-e. Also ||H_e-H_0||=e. The Hamiltonians commute, so comparison of Boltzmann weights yields

  ||rho_beta(H_e)-rho_beta(H_0)||_1<=min(2,exp(2 beta e)-1).

Their closed-system channels differ in diamond norm by at most 2te. Thus the ground code is unchanged, the gap is uniform for e<=Delta/2, and spectrum, Gibbs states and fixed-time unitary dynamics are arbitrarily accurate approximations as e tends to zero.

Use the SAME bath coupling S=I_L tensor X_a. At frequencies near Delta choose downward rate d(E)=r>0 and upward rate u(E)=r exp(-beta E); extend the rates outside this interval in any positive detailed-balance manner. This is an explicit Davies rate choice. Omit a Lamb shift, or choose one vanishing on C.

At e=0, degenerate transitions share a frequency, so the jump operators are sqrt(r) I_L tensor |0><1| and sqrt(r exp(-beta Delta)) I_L tensor |1><0|. They preserve L exactly for all times and initial mediator states.

At EVERY e>0, the gaps E_+=Delta+e and E_-=Delta-e differ. Full secularization produces FOUR separate jumps:

  D_+=sqrt(r) P_+ tensor |0><1|_a,
  D_-=sqrt(r) P_- tensor |0><1|_a,
  U_+=sqrt(u(E_+)) P_+ tensor |1><0|_a,
  U_-=sqrt(u(E_-)) P_- tensor |1><0|_a.

THEOREM 1. Encode a qubit into C, evolve by this Davies generator for time t, and allow ANY CPTP decoder from L,a to a qubit. Its optimal entanglement fidelity with a reference is exactly

  F_opt(t)=(1+exp(-Gamma_e t))/2,
  Gamma_e=(u(E_+)+u(E_-))/2
         =r exp(-beta Delta) cosh(beta e).

In particular Gamma_e tends to r exp(-beta Delta)>0 as e tends to zero, although at e=0 the optimal fidelity is one forever.

PROOF. Start with a maximally entangled reference-logical pair and mediator |0>. The logical populations evolve separately and preserve their labels. In the cross-logical block every recycling term vanishes, because P_+|0><1|P_+=P_-|0><1|P_-=0. The only nonzero mediator cross block remains |0><0| with coefficient exp(-Gamma_e t); the Hamiltonian vanishes there. Thus the reference-system state is

  omega(t)=1/2[|00><00|_RL tensor rho_a^+(t)
              +|11><11|_RL tensor rho_a^-(t)
              +exp(-Gamma_e t)(|00><11|+|11><00|)_RL tensor |0><0|_a].

Its partial transpose on R has exactly one negative eigenvalue -exp(-Gamma_e t)/2. Its negativity is exp(-Gamma_e t)/2. A decoder cannot increase negativity, by trace-norm contraction of CPTP maps on Hermitian matrices. Any two-qubit state sigma obeys

  <Phi|sigma|Phi><=||sigma^(T_R)||_1 ||Phi^(T_R)||_infinity
                =(1+2 Neg(sigma))/2,

because Phi^(T_R) is half the swap. This proves the upper bound for every decoder. Tracing out a attains it: the logical channel preserves populations and multiplies the off-diagonal entries by exp(-Gamma_e t). QED.

For target error eta<1/2, retention is at most -log(1-2eta)/Gamma_e=O(exp(beta Delta)/r), uniformly as e approaches zero. Evaluating the derivative of logical X at |+><+| tensor |0><0| also gives a Davies-generator norm change at least Gamma_e. Hence no modulus of continuity in Hamiltonian norm can support a GENERIC thermal-generator transfer theorem of this kind.

LIMIT. The toy logical qubit has no distance against direct logical-site noise. It is a counterexample to an unrestricted simulator/Davies continuity claim, not a demonstrated failure of the 3D incumbent under every local gadget encoding. The same microscopic coupling operator is used in both compared models; the change is the bath model's exact frequency resolution.

SERVICE MODULE — COMPLETE PHYSICAL UPDATE RULE



Let a set of elementary gates have fixed finite main-data footprints and a
conflict graph of degree <=d. Main data remain in fixed finite-dimensional
registers. Each gate g has one classical owner located within fixed distance
of its whole footprint. A lock at each main register names one of its O(1)
nearby owners or FREE. All channels below have fixed local support and rates.

A pending transaction has a command U_g, a done bit initially0, and one long
transaction-deadline counter. Its internal reservation lease is SHORT and
independent of that deadline. The pure classical machine is:

* GRANT_g: when pending, done=0 and all footprint locks are FREE, atomically
  assign ALL those locks to g and set ARM_g=1 with lease phase0.
* U_g: while pending=1, ARM_g=1, done=0 and every footprint lock is owned by g, a rate-kappa
  channel applies U_g to the main data and atomically sets done=1.
* The lease advances through J exponential-rate-nu stages, independent of U or
  done. Expiry clears ARM_g and releases g's locks. If done=0, retry GRANT;
  if done=1, no further grant or U is enabled for this transaction.
* A separate long deadline atomically sets pending=0 and ARM_g=0, releases
  g-owned locks, publishes COMMIT and advances NEXTOP tags exactly once. The
  pending guard disables every later U from this transaction. If done=0 the
  intended gate was OMITTED, i.e. identity acts instead of U. This is one
  local quantum-gate fault. It never makes the classical controller wait.
* A new transaction begins with an atomic IDLE initialization that clears all
  private flags/queues and g-owned locks, preserves main payload, sets done=0.

The lock acquisition is atomic on a constant footprint; there is no partial
reservation or hold-and-wait. The common ARM interval is one owner state, not
an assumed intersection of independently drifting participant windows.
No quantum ACK controls the deadline. The in-place U jump has a state-independent
hazard kappa for EVERY unknown input, including inputs entangled with a reference.
Its classical guard turns off after firing, so it cannot repeat in a clean
transaction. There are no delayed queued GKLS jumps: guards are tested at firing.

This primitive is for clean, compatible requests and lock states. No full
arbitrary-program self-stabilization claim is hidden in that phrase. Illegal
flags/locks caused by faults require IDLE cleanup and a bound on controller
damage duration in the full compiler.

APPENDIX A: EXACT JOINT POISSON SERVICE BOUND AND ITS LIMIT

1. THE CONCRETE MODULE AND THE CAUSAL GUARD

Main registers always have H_q. A request starts its deadline ONLY when all
per-main-qudit NEXTOP tags match. Successor tags advance once, atomically, on
COMMIT whether U succeeded or was omitted. A gate owner acquires ALL footprint
locks atomically; there is no partial acquisition or quantum backpressure.
The done bit survives short-lease retries and disables further U attempts.
At the long H-stage deadline the controller atomically clears pending/ARM,
releases its locks and commits regardless of done. Every U guard includes
pending=1; COMMIT cannot leave an old U enabled. NEXTOP advances exactly once.

Let the conflict degree be <=d. Independent GRANT, lease-tick, U-attempt and
deadline proposal clocks have rates alpha,nu,kappa,nud. Short leases have J
stages. These are fixed local CP-event channels, identity when their guard is
false. They can be constructed exactly; no generic GKLS uniformization is used.
For example Phi_U(rho)=V rho V^dagger+Q rho Q, with
V=|done><armed| tensor U, Q=I-P_armed, is TP and u(Phi_U-Id) is GKLS.
The flag dephasing is intentional and does not measure the unknown payload.

This appendix concerns clean compatible ownership/tag states. A noisy invalid
owner record requires the separately counted controller-damage/IDLE-cleanup
contract. Nothing here claims recovery from arbitrary global scheduler states.

2. PRODUCTIVE REGENERATION AFTER ELIGIBILITY

Choose a,b,c>0, S=a+b+c, and qJ=P(Poisson(nu*a)>=J). In any length-S window:
 suppress all d competing GRANTs; suppress target GRANT for its first a;
 require >=J lease ticks at all d+1 possibly holding owners in that first a;
 suppress target lease ticks thereafter; require target GRANT in middle b;
 require target U attempt in final c.
These are independent proposal-clock restrictions with probability at least
 p=exp[-alpha(d*S+a)] qJ^(d+1) exp[-nu(b+c)]
       (1-exp[-alpha*b])(1-exp[-kappa*c]) >0.              (S1)
The first part clears every target lock from any clean lease phase. No outside
owner can hold a target lock. The target then grants and keeps its single ARM
until U fires, unless it already succeeded. Eligibility is unchanged until its
own COMMIT. Thus, ignoring the final deadline temporarily,
 P(no U by time t | full eligible-start past)<=(1-p)^floor(t/S).
All rates, dimensions and supports are independent of total system size.

3. FIXED ANALYSIS BOXES HANDLE ALL ADAPTIVE STARTS

Use a fixed mathematical grid of S-windows; this is not a physical clock.
Set h=H/(4nud). For each owner g and integer k use coarse start box
I_k=[kh,(k+1)h] and witness box J_k=[kh,(k+2)h].

A continuously eligible, clean request starting anywhere in I_k and receiving
no U for h contains at least m=max(0,floor(h/S)-2) whole S-grid windows. Each
must miss its forcing pattern. There are at most 2h/S+4 possible consecutive
runs in J_k. Therefore a fixed event Eslow(g,k), depending only on its local
proposal clocks in J_k, dominates EVERY such adaptive slow start, and
 P(Eslow)<= (2h/S+4)(1-p)^m.                               (S2)
For small h the bound may exceed one; using min(1,right side) is harmless.

A clean phase-zero H-stage deadline expiring within h of ANY start in I_k
requires >=H deadline ticks in J_k. Their mean is 2nud*h=H/2, so the fixed event
Efast(g,k) has probability <=exp[-H(log 2-1/2)]. The factor four in h is vital:
using H/(2nud) before doubling the box would give mean H and lose the bound.

An omitted eligible transaction implies Efast or Eslow in its start box.
Thus E(g,k)=Efast union Eslow is a single fixed-clock witness with
 beta_H=exp[-H(log 2-1/2)]+(2h/S+4)(1-p)^m.                (S3)
This tends to zero exponentially in H, apart from the displayed linear factor.
The implication assumes the request stays causally eligible until its COMMIT,
as enforced by the NEXTOP guard; it does not condition a probability on a
retrospectively selected failed service window.

The proposal neighborhood is the closed conflict neighborhood. Two such
neighborhoods can overlap only at conflict-graph distance <=2. Its square has
degree <=d^2. Color that square with d^2+1 colors and use time color k mod 3.
Events with the same combined color use disjoint Poisson increments. Thus
chi=3(d^2+1) is a valid fixed overlap bound, independent of H. For ANY fixed
set F of marked owner/start boxes, choosing its largest color class gives
 P(intersection_(g,k in F) E(g,k))<=beta_H^(|F|/chi).       (S4)
Omissions in all those boxes are dominated by the same bound. This is an
actual joint local-stochastic service estimate, not a marginal mean bound or
an independence assertion about adaptive starts. Short leases and done across
retries are essential to (S1); holding every lock for the growing H deadline
would not give the same p independent of H.
