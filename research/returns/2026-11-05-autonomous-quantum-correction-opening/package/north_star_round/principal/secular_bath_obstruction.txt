THEOREM. Let H on N qubits have simple spectrum and distinct ordered nonzero
energy gaps. Write H=sum_X h_X with sup_i sum_(X contains i)||h_X||<=J, J>0.
Use the exact fully secular Davies generator with independent one-site X,Y,Z
bath couplings, and suppose all bath rates gamma_(i,a)(omega)>=g>0 for
|omega|<=4J, uniformly in i,a,N. A Lamb-shift Hamiltonian commuting with H
is allowed. Then for ANY encoding channel of one qubit and ANY decoding
channel, entanglement fidelity at time t is bounded by
  F_ent(t)<=[1+exp(-N g t)]/2.                               (1)
Fidelity >=1-epsilon, epsilon<1/2, requires
  t<=-log(1-2epsilon)/(N g).

The positive-window bath hypothesis includes KMS absorption at fixed finite
temperature when spectra are positive continuous on that window. It can fail
for super-Ohmic spectra vanishing at zero. No assertion that arbitrary local
Hamiltonian families have the required nonresonance is made; it must be proved
for any claimed application. Exact gauge-protected degeneracy evades it.

PROOF. For a nonzero frequency, distinct gaps make each Davies jump a scalar
multiple of |m><n|, hence rank one. For one energy eigenstate |n> and site i,

Thus at most3/4 of the offdiagonal spectral weight lies outside |omega|<=4J;
at least one unit lies inside. The loss operator R of all nonzero-frequency
jumps is diagonal and obeys R>=N g I.

GENERIC LOCAL-FAMILY COROLLARY (subsequent exact literature check).
The earlier caution about unproved nonresonance can be discharged on specified
regular-lattice coefficient spaces, rather than on arbitrary constrained models.
Huang, arXiv2104.02053v1, Theorem2 and AppendixC.2, proves distinct gaps almost
everywhere for independently parametrized nearest-neighbor qubit Hamiltonians,
including onsite X,Z and ZZ bonds. The polynomial witness embeds along a
nearest-neighbor Hamiltonian path through a rectangular lattice. Alternatively,
Lee--Pilatowsky-Cameo--Choi arXiv2609.26885v1, PropositionS2 (Proposition1),
includes all onsite X,Z and nearest-neighbor ZZ,XZ in its parameter space and
proves all-order nonresonance; only order2 is needed here.

Let P be a finite fixed-range Pauli family on a rectangular regular lattice
containing those path terms, and let coefficients range over any bounded open
set. Bound the per-site interaction norm uniformly by Jbar using the fixed
range and coefficient bounds. The exceptional polynomial-zero set has Lebesgue
measure0 in EVERY open sub-neighborhood. If bath rates are uniformly >=g on
[-4Jbar,4Jbar], (1) holds for almost every coefficient tuple there. Thus no open
coefficient region within this precisely defined fully secular bath model can
uniformly support long-lived quantum storage. For cubic nearest-neighbor
families these are two-body interactions plus onsite fields.

This does not establish the same statement in symmetry-restricted, translation-
invariant, irregular-support or exact-gauge families without a separate witness.
It also does not change the physical warning about the infinite secular limit.
The genericity machinery is established prior art; the corollary is its direct
composition with (1), not a new spectral-genericity discovery.

Let H0 be ANY fixed Hamiltonian, even outside the chosen perturbation span.

  p(H0+tHw)=t^degree(p) p(Hw+H0/t)!=0
for sufficiently large positive t. Thus the polynomial in perturbation
coefficients p(H0+sum u_j P_j) is not identically zero. Its zero set has

not merely for almost every base Hamiltonian.

With uniform Jbar and rate g as above, (1) follows for those perturbations.
This covers arbitrary finite-range multi-body H0 on a fully occupied rectangular
lattice. A physical support lacking such a path needs a separate witness;
local fields alone do not suffice (take H0=0). The spectral-genericity input
is established; the affine application is proved here rather than assumed.

TIME-UNIT CAVEAT. The bound uses rates in the displayed generator. Deriving a
fully secular Davies equation resolving increasingly small splittings may
require a microscopic system-bath coupling decreasing with N. In physical time
the resulting g_N can vanish, violating uniform g. This is NOT a fixed-
microscopic-coupling no-go. It is an order-of-limits obstruction to inferring
physical robustness from the exactly secular semigroup alone.
