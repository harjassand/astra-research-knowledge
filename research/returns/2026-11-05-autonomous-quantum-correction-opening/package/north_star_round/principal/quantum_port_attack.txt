PRINCIPAL CONSTRUCTIVE ATTACK: IRREVERSIBLE QUANTUM PORTS FOR NOISY CONTROL
5 November 2026. Post-release proposal, being challenged by A/B/C.

North-star operation: convert a rigorous noisy asynchronous classical control
simulation into a continuously running local quantum fault-tolerant machine.
Gacs supplies strong sparse classical damage and bounded dwell times. Ordinary
classical history reconstruction cannot copy or roll back unknown quantum data.
The following representation attacks that specific gap.

SLOT. Each wire register is |vacuum> direct-sum H_q. For a local unitary gate U
with separate input and output slots, define a partial isometry V_U mapping
 |x>_inputs |vacuum>_outputs to |vacuum>_inputs U|x>_outputs,
and zero on other occupation sectors. All inputs must be occupied and all
outputs empty. Then V_U^* V_U is exactly the corresponding occupation
projector, independent of x. A jump sqrt(gamma)V_U moves the state coherently;
its time distribution carries no information about the unknown quantum input.
After a jump the input is vacuum, so replaying the same command is inactive.
Stinespring ancillas and flagged reset/discard operations treat general channels.

PROVED IDEAL BASE LEMMA. A finite acyclic circuit with a once-only classical flag
per gate can be implemented by a time-independent local sum of these jump
dissipators. Each jump flips its gate flag as well. All wires initially have
the prescribed input/vacuum occupations, and flags are initially unfired.
Every causal firing order implements EXACTLY the original circuit, including
on inputs entangled with an arbitrary reference. Independent Poisson hazards
of rates gamma_g give
 T_g=max_(h preceding g)T_h+Exp(gamma_g).
For m gates, depth D, maximum fan-in r>=1 and gamma_g>=gamma,
 P(T_finish>t)<=m(2r)^D exp(-gamma t/2).
Proof: each critical path has at most D independent exponential waits, whose
mgf at gamma/2 is at most2^D. At most m*r^D paths suffice by tracing backwards
from sinks. A union/Chernoff bound proves the claim. Gates with no predecessor
start immediately. State-independent hazards and commuting independent gates
prove the channel assertion. The flag makes preparation gates once-only too.

This ideal lemma is established dissipative/dataflow-computation machinery in
spirit, not claimed as a new computation model or a threshold theorem.

REGENERATION PROPOSAL. Each fixed finite synchronization neighborhood has a
rare independent watchdog reset of control flags/workspace, preserving quantum
data and intended virtual macrostep labels. Mid-gadget resets can be charged as
constant-support quantum faults at rate lambda. If a reset guarantees legal,
boundary-compatible controller recovery, a poisoned region has an exponential
healing-time tail at scale1/lambda. Noise epsilon then produces fault-volume
of order epsilon*lambda^(-(Dspace+1)), while reset interruptions scale lambda.
Choosing fixed small lambda and then epsilon sufficiently small could leave a
positive threshold. This is a candidate operation, NOT a proved global scheme.

EXACT TESTS REQUIRED.
1. A legal replay must not operate on a future packet after an epoch wrap.
2. Missing/spurious occupations or prematurely flipped gate flags must become
   local erasures, not permanent deadlock or unbounded rollback.
3. Every repaired port must align to the correct command boundary; quantum
   payloads may be replaced as local faults, never copied by classical repair.
4. Watchdog resets must not themselves generate persistent boundary clock
   defects. Rate lambda times repair time1/lambda would give order-one defect
   density and defeat the construction.
5. Fault-volume tails must hold jointly for adaptive overlapping neighborhoods,
   including coherent/non-CP fault insertions from general GKLS perturbations.

WHY A SIMPLE FIX FAILS. For once-only circuit flags f_p,f_q, a valid edge has
occupation f_p(1-f_q). Locally forcing occupancy to match flags repairs a missing
token, but a prematurely fired flag can violate causal order. Closing the fired
set backwards can skip an unbounded ancestor cone; closing forwards can replay
an unbounded future cone. Classical error correction of the flags and an actual
bounded port-recovery contract are indispensable. The ideal lemma alone earns
no noisy autonomous-computation theorem.

Naive modulo3 marching-soldier clocks also admit finite deadlock defects: a
six-cell cycle with phases012012 has a behind-neighbor at every cell, so none
can advance. Noiseless asynchronous simulation or fair activation is not a
clock-fault healing proof. Local eroders/strong classical simulation are the
constructive incumbents to use, with their exact quantum-port gap retained.
