APPENDIX A: EXACT JOINT POISSON SERVICE BOUND AND ITS LIMIT
5 November 2026. Independent check of C Sections 2--4.1 and principal refinement.

1. THE CONCRETE MODULE AND THE CAUSAL GUARD

Main registers always have H_q. A request starts its deadline ONLY when all
per-main-qudit NEXTOP tags match. Successor tags advance once, atomically, on
COMMIT whether U succeeded or was omitted. A gate owner acquires ALL footprint
locks atomically; there is no partial acquisition or quantum backpressure.
The done bit survives short-lease retries and disables further U attempts.
At the long H-stage deadline the controller atomically clears pending/ARM,
releases its locks and commits regardless of done. Every U guard includes
pending=1; COMMIT cannot leave an old U enabled. NEXTOP advances exactly once.

Let the conflict degree be <=d. Independent GRANT, lease-tick, U-attempt and
deadline proposal clocks have rates alpha,nu,kappa,nud. Short leases have J
stages. These are fixed local CP-event channels, identity when their guard is
false. They can be constructed exactly; no generic GKLS uniformization is used.
For example Phi_U(rho)=V rho V^dagger+Q rho Q, with
V=|done><armed| tensor U, Q=I-P_armed, is TP and u(Phi_U-Id) is GKLS.
The flag dephasing is intentional and does not measure the unknown payload.

This appendix concerns clean compatible ownership/tag states. A noisy invalid
owner record requires the separately counted controller-damage/IDLE-cleanup
contract. Nothing here claims recovery from arbitrary global scheduler states.

2. PRODUCTIVE REGENERATION AFTER ELIGIBILITY

Choose a,b,c>0, S=a+b+c, and qJ=P(Poisson(nu*a)>=J). In any length-S window:
 suppress all d competing GRANTs; suppress target GRANT for its first a;
 require >=J lease ticks at all d+1 possibly holding owners in that first a;
 suppress target lease ticks thereafter; require target GRANT in middle b;
 require target U attempt in final c.
These are independent proposal-clock restrictions with probability at least
 p=exp[-alpha(d*S+a)] qJ^(d+1) exp[-nu(b+c)]
       (1-exp[-alpha*b])(1-exp[-kappa*c]) >0.              (S1)
The first part clears every target lock from any clean lease phase. No outside
owner can hold a target lock. The target then grants and keeps its single ARM
until U fires, unless it already succeeded. Eligibility is unchanged until its
own COMMIT. Thus, ignoring the final deadline temporarily,
 P(no U by time t | full eligible-start past)<=(1-p)^floor(t/S).
All rates, dimensions and supports are independent of total system size.

3. FIXED ANALYSIS BOXES HANDLE ALL ADAPTIVE STARTS

Use a fixed mathematical grid of S-windows; this is not a physical clock.
Set h=H/(4nud). For each owner g and integer k use coarse start box
I_k=[kh,(k+1)h] and witness box J_k=[kh,(k+2)h].

A continuously eligible, clean request starting anywhere in I_k and receiving
no U for h contains at least m=max(0,floor(h/S)-2) whole S-grid windows. Each
must miss its forcing pattern. There are at most 2h/S+4 possible consecutive
runs in J_k. Therefore a fixed event Eslow(g,k), depending only on its local
proposal clocks in J_k, dominates EVERY such adaptive slow start, and
 P(Eslow)<= (2h/S+4)(1-p)^m.                               (S2)
For small h the bound may exceed one; using min(1,right side) is harmless.

A clean phase-zero H-stage deadline expiring within h of ANY start in I_k
requires >=H deadline ticks in J_k. Their mean is 2nud*h=H/2, so the fixed event
Efast(g,k) has probability <=exp[-H(log 2-1/2)]. The factor four in h is vital:
using H/(2nud) before doubling the box would give mean H and lose the bound.

An omitted eligible transaction implies Efast or Eslow in its start box.
Thus E(g,k)=Efast union Eslow is a single fixed-clock witness with
 beta_H=exp[-H(log 2-1/2)]+(2h/S+4)(1-p)^m.                (S3)
This tends to zero exponentially in H, apart from the displayed linear factor.
The implication assumes the request stays causally eligible until its COMMIT,
as enforced by the NEXTOP guard; it does not condition a probability on a
retrospectively selected failed service window.

The proposal neighborhood is the closed conflict neighborhood. Two such
neighborhoods can overlap only at conflict-graph distance <=2. Its square has
degree <=d^2. Color that square with d^2+1 colors and use time color k mod 3.
Events with the same combined color use disjoint Poisson increments. Thus
chi=3(d^2+1) is a valid fixed overlap bound, independent of H. For ANY fixed
set F of marked owner/start boxes, choosing its largest color class gives
 P(intersection_(g,k in F) E(g,k))<=beta_H^(|F|/chi).       (S4)
Omissions in all those boxes are dominated by the same bound. This is an
actual joint local-stochastic service estimate, not a marginal mean bound or
an independence assertion about adaptive starts. Short leases and done across
retries are essential to (S1); holding every lock for the growing H deadline
would not give the same p independent of H.

4. EXACT REMAINING OPERATION

The bound starts AFTER causal eligibility. Let W_j be the wall time a qudit
waits for its next operation's predecessors. Neither (S1) nor Gacs's reliable
classical computation implies a uniform tail for W_j. Generic bounded-degree
DAGs can contain arbitrarily long predecessor chains. A recurring FT schedule
may have special bounded frontiers or protected idles, but that must be proved.

Gacs math/0003117v2, Def.7.10/Prop.7.12, Thm.7.3, Thm.7.6/Cor.7.21 provide
strong classical window bounds and bounded primitive variable periods.
Computation Property10.16 bounds state dwell, not advancement of every chosen
NEXTOP field: a valid heartbeat can continue while the scheduler waits.
Theorem6.1 permits a time-growing simulation slowdown. Reliably simulating a
purely classical causal scheduler does not improve the scheduler's own wait.

The remaining coupled quantum induction is: at each hierarchy level, prove
that waiting executes protected lower-level idle rectangles, or charge an
unprotected delay to sparse enlarged bad rectangles, while preserving physical
quantum causal order through controller reconstruction. Then prove the joint
DIAMOND-NORM fault-sum bound for those ACTUAL macro-locations, including generic
GKLS perturbations. (S4) supplies the local service contribution only.

Verdict: the fixed-box refinement closes the adaptive-start service-counting
gap. The full asynchronous autonomous quantum threshold, and the original
noisy-write-to-passive-retention target, remain unproved. Stop at this boundary;
no new committor or quantum hierarchy is claimed.
