5 November 2026. Independent opening; subsequent joint attack after release.

not a missing numerical estimate for a single gate. No device or priority claim.

2. THE PROVED FINITE PRIMITIVE: SINGLE OWNER, ALL LOCKS, SHORT LEASE

Let a set of elementary gates have fixed finite main-data footprints and a
conflict graph of degree <=d. Main data remain in fixed finite-dimensional
registers. Each gate g has one classical owner located within fixed distance
of its whole footprint. A lock at each main register names one of its O(1)
nearby owners or FREE. All channels below have fixed local support and rates.

A pending transaction has a command U_g, a done bit initially0, and one long
transaction-deadline counter. Its internal reservation lease is SHORT and
independent of that deadline. The pure classical machine is:

* GRANT_g: when pending, done=0 and all footprint locks are FREE, atomically
  assign ALL those locks to g and set ARM_g=1 with lease phase0.
* U_g: while pending=1, ARM_g=1, done=0 and every footprint lock is owned by g, a rate-kappa
  channel applies U_g to the main data and atomically sets done=1.
* The lease advances through J exponential-rate-nu stages, independent of U or
  done. Expiry clears ARM_g and releases g's locks. If done=0, retry GRANT;
  if done=1, no further grant or U is enabled for this transaction.
* A separate long deadline atomically sets pending=0 and ARM_g=0, releases
  g-owned locks, publishes COMMIT and advances NEXTOP tags exactly once. The
  pending guard disables every later U from this transaction. If done=0 the
  intended gate was OMITTED, i.e. identity acts instead of U. This is one
  local quantum-gate fault. It never makes the classical controller wait.
* A new transaction begins with an atomic IDLE initialization that clears all
  private flags/queues and g-owned locks, preserves main payload, sets done=0.

The lock acquisition is atomic on a constant footprint; there is no partial
reservation or hold-and-wait. The common ARM interval is one owner state, not
an assumed intersection of independently drifting participant windows.
No quantum ACK controls the deadline. The in-place U jump has a state-independent
hazard kappa for EVERY unknown input, including inputs entangled with a reference.
Its classical guard turns off after firing, so it cannot repeat in a clean
transaction. There are no delayed queued GKLS jumps: guards are tested at firing.

This primitive is for clean, compatible requests and lock states. No full
arbitrary-program self-stabilization claim is hidden in that phrase. Illegal
flags/locks caused by faults require IDLE cleanup and a bound on controller
damage duration in the full compiler.

3. EXPLICIT UNIFORM PRODUCTIVE-SERVICE TAIL

Choose identical proposal rates alpha for GRANT, nu for lease ticks and kappa
for quantum U attempts. These can be realized by local Poisson-attempt CPTP
channels (identity when a classical guard is false). Let
  a>0,b>0,c>0, S=a+b+c,
  qJ=P(Poisson(nu*a)>=J)>0,
  p=exp[-alpha(d*S+a)] qJ^(d+1) exp[-nu(b+c)]
       *(1-exp(-alpha*b))*(1-exp(-kappa*c)) >0.              (1)
All parameters are fixed constants independent of the total system size and
the long transaction deadline. A gate's whole closed conflict neighborhood is
of constant size and diameter.

Suppose target g is pending with done=0 and remains eligible, ignoring its final
deadline temporarily. In any length-S window impose this local Poisson word:
  (i) no competing GRANT throughout the window and no g-GRANT in its first a;
  (ii) at least J lease ticks for every possibly conflicting owner, including
       g, in that first a;
  (iii) no g lease tick after the first a;
  (iv) at least one g-GRANT in the middle b, then at least one g-U attempt in
       the final c.
The probability is at least p. Conditions (i)--(ii) free every target resource,
whatever the clean initial lease phases. Owners outside the conflict neighborhood
cannot own a target resource. Condition (iv) grants the whole footprint, (iii)
keeps its common ARM active, and the U attempt succeeds exactly once unless it
already succeeded earlier. Other quantum payloads and outside controller states
do not alter this argument. If g was active initially and succeeds while its
lease is being cleared, the desired event has already occurred.

Fresh proposal increments in consecutive windows give, conditional on any
past clean compatible state and continued eligibility,
  P(no productive U by time t)<=(1-p)^floor(t/S).           (2)
This is an actual constructive service bound, not a bounded-mean assumption.
The short lease is essential. If every competitor holds its locks until the
same growing final deadline K, increasing K can also increase blocking times;
small omission probability would not follow from that version of the protocol.

Use a separate H-stage Erlang transaction deadline of rate nud, initialized at
phase0 and independent of the payload. At expiry the machine publishes completion
even if U never fired. Couple it to the unbounded-pending process until expiry.
With t=H/(2nud), the omission probability is at most
  zeta_H=exp[-H(log(2)-1/2)] +(1-p)^floor(H/(2nud*S)).        (3)
The first term is the lower tail of Gamma(H,nud); the second is (2). Thus
zeta_H tends to zero exponentially with H. H is chosen once for the desired
fixed base fault strength and remains independent of L. The owner state alphabet
and all rates are finite. Ideal service failures are local omitted gates.

4. A JOINT CLOCK-FAULT BOUND THAT IS ACTUALLY PROVED

For a transaction requested at a prescribed time, omission implies either early
deadline or failure of every forcing word in its first t=H/(2nud) time interval.
This witness event depends only on independent proposal clocks in a fixed-radius,
fixed-duration box, and has probability <=zeta_H. It dominates omission without
needing to condition on the quantum payload or outside clock evolution.

For ANY FIXED family of such boxes whose intersection graph has a proper coloring
with chi colors, the probability that all designated service faults occur is
  <=zeta_H^(|F|/chi).                                      (4)
Proof: omission implies its local witness. In each color class the proposal
clock restrictions are disjoint and independent. Taking the largest color class
(or Holder across color classes) proves (4). This is a strong joint local-
stochastic bound for fixed, bounded-overlap windows, not only a marginal rate.

Equation(4) is NOT silently applied to arbitrary adaptively selected stopping-
time boxes. Such selection can reveal or bias future clock increments. Gacs's
Def7.10 provides a stronger admissible stopping-rectangle framework; its precise
selection/overlap hypotheses must be verified for actual quantum macro-locations.
This verification, together with causal pending requests, is the remaining
hierarchy interface. Ordinary independence of Poisson proposals does not prove it.

General local noise within these fixed boxes has an elementary Poisson-event
bound only for actual added CPTP event noise. Arbitrary local GKLS perturbations
require the separate norm argument described below; probabilities cannot simply
be assigned to non-CP Dyson insertions.

4.1 FIXED ANALYSIS BOXES REMOVE ADAPTIVE STARTS FOR SERVICE

The principal suggested a stronger domination, independently verified here.
Use a fixed analysis grid of length-S service-word windows. No physical global
clock is introduced. A continuously eligible clean request that receives no U
for wall time h contains at least m=floor(h/S)-2 whole grid windows, every one
missing its forcing word. If its arbitrary start lies in [kh,(k+1)h], the entire
failure interval lies in [kh,(k+2)h]. There are O(h/S+2) possible consecutive
runs to test there. Define Eslow(g,k) by the existence of such a run, solely
from local proposal clocks. Then
  P(Eslow)<=C(h/S+2)(1-p)^max(0,floor(h/S)-2).              (5)
It dominates ALL adaptive clean failure starts in that coarse box. There is
no conditioning on failure to choose a supposedly independent future window.

The long Erlang deadline also needs an adaptive-start witness. Choose
  h=H/(4nud).
If its H-stage counter expires within h of a clean phase0 initialization
anywhere in [kh,(k+1)h], the fixed doubled box [kh,(k+2)h] contains >=H counter
ticks. Its mean is H/2, so
  P(Efast)<=exp[-H(log(2)-1/2)].                           (6)
Using h=H/(2nud) and THEN doubling would instead give mean H and would lose
this exponential bound; the factor4 is intentional.

An omitted clean transaction starting in that coarse box is dominated by
Eslow union Efast. Let beta_H be the sum of bounds (5)--(6). It tends to zero
exponentially in H, up to a polynomial prefactor. Each marked box uses only
a fixed spatial conflict neighborhood and two adjacent time boxes. In coarse
h-units their overlap coloring number chi is CONSTANT, independent of H.
Thus for every fixed set F of marked boxes,
  P(all marked)<=beta_H^(|F|/chi).                        (7)
This is the earned adaptive-start service-failure geometry. It does not yet
charge causal predecessor stalls, controller reconstruction or general quantum
fault-norm insertions. Those remain the full compiler's separate obligations.

5. WHY THE FULL HIERARCHY DOES NOT YET FOLLOW

Classical controller correctness, mutual exclusion and successful gate service
are not enough: overlapping quantum gates must obey causal order. Intended
H_A then CNOT_AB sends |00> to a Bell state; CNOT_AB then H_A sends it to |+0>.
Both owners can have exclusive locks, report done=1 and have no omitted gates;
their final classical receipts agree. Correct classical snapshots therefore
cannot substitute for a physical quantum circuit-order invariant.

Each quantum instruction needs a causal frontier/epoch on its worldlines.
A successor becomes pending only after every predecessor's ARM guard is inactive
and COMMIT is published. A missed predecessor counts as an omitted gate and
still commits by its deadline. One must prove that Gacs reconstruction and
self-simulation preserve this frontier outside enlarged sparse damage, at every
encoding scale. Unknown payloads cannot inhabit classical old/new copy buffers.
One-shot packet transfer avoids copying but flag noise can create permanent
missing-packet backpressure. Fixed main registers avoid that particular failure.

The missing full lemma is quantum strong simulation: after marking controller
damage, service failures and local quantum noise insertions, all other extended
rectangles enact the intended encoded channels in causal order, and their bad
fault sums satisfy a product DIAMOND-NORM bound for actual macro-locations.
Gacs supplies classical damage technology; Section3 supplies productive quantum
service. This composition and its quantum frontier invariant have not been
constructed here. Computing a finite classical scheduler is not itself a proof
that its physical quantum side effects survive reconstruction and rollback.

6. GENERAL GKLS NOISE: EXACT REPRESENTATION OBSTRUCTION

A local Lindblad generator need not be a finite-rate Poisson CPTP event
lambda(Phi-I). For amplitude damping, A=|0><1| and
  N(rho)=A rho A^dagger-(1/2){|1><1|,rho}.
For every s>0, the Euler map I+sN has Choi principal block
  [[1,1-s/2],[1-s/2,1-s]],
with determinant -s^2/4. It is not CP. Thus N=lambda(Phi-I) would require a
non-CP Phi=I+N/lambda for every finite lambda.

Generic amplitude damping, coherent perturbations and imperfect engineered
channels cannot simply be renamed independent rare unitary jump faults. A
valid route is local diamond-norm Duhamel/Dyson insertions with intervening
actual CPTP evolutions grouped to retain norm<=1. Raw absolute sums over all
unselected faults can grow with total space-time volume. Fixed-time Trotter
convergence does not establish exp(L^c) stability. This is a missing proof
operation, not a universal impossibility theorem.

7. CLOCK COUNTERTESTS AND A MEMORY-ONLY ALTERNATIVE

For the naive modulo-q marching rule (q>=5), take a periodic square lattice
of side divisible by q and kappa(x,y)=x+y mod q. Every nearest-neighbor clock
difference is +/-1, yet every site has a neighbor one step behind, so increasing
creates difference2 and is forbidden. Repair acting only on locally invalid
differences leaves this configuration frozen. This known winding problem is
already acknowledged by Dunnweber et al.; it does not refute their proposed
finite-neighborhood repair.

Likewise a valid absolute height field can have a descending path through an
arbitrary patch boundary, making an interior update depend on an outside blocker.
Our owner/short-lease machine avoids that specific problem: all owners of its
target resources lie in a bounded conflict neighborhood and have independent
release clocks. No release waits on outside gates or quantum ACKs.

A different memory-only representation is to use commuting local QND stabilizer
measurements and keep all correction decisions in a protected classical Pauli
frame, applying no quantum commands during storage. It eliminates physical
quantum gate replay/rollback, but requires a proved high-throughput noisy online
input/timestamp/history interface to Gacs and a bounded-memory sliding-window
decoder. Static-input reliable computation does not supply that automatically.
This alternative has not been promoted to an autonomous-memory theorem here.

8. EARNED RESULT AND NORTH-STAR STATUS

Earned: a concrete fixed-alphabet, bounded-rate local quantum-service machine:
one owner, atomic all-lock grant, fixed main payloads, short reservation leases,
long deadline, no data-dependent backpressure, uniform exponential productive
service, and a joint sparse fixed-box witness dominating adaptive clean service
failures. Rates and fields may be huge constants; none scale with L. This has
established asynchronous-computation/dissipative-circuit ancestry; priority is
not claimed.

Not earned: a complete low-dimensional autonomous quantum-memory threshold,
the coupled quantum causal-frontier/strong-simulation lemma under noisy Gacs
reconstruction, or general local GKLS fault-norm domination for that hierarchy.
Those are the next decisive operation. Neither the finite primitive nor a
conditional invocation of an existing threshold theorem is the north-star result.
No practical, asymptotic resource or civilization-scale advantage is established.
