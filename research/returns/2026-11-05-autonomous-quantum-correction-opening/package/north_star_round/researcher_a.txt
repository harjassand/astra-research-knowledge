1. INITIAL QUESTION AND ACHIEVED OBSTRUCTION

Question: can a fault-tolerantly prepared cold encoded state enter the proved
passive 3D memory's long-lived sector without globally thermal initialization?
The bridge remains open. The following obstruction is complete and rules out
a common attempted proof; it is not a no-go for the physical task.

Let H=sum_a (I-S_a)/2 be a commuting Pauli stabilizer Hamiltonian on N qubits,
with check weight <=w and <=D checks meeting any qubit. Assume every qubit
meets a nonidentity check (in particular any family of distance >1 has this).
Let Z_syn=sum_(valid syndromes s) exp(-beta |s|). All syndrome multiplicities
are equal, so the thermal syndrome probability of zero is exactly 1/Z_syn.

Put Delta=D(w-1). The qubit graph joining qubits that share a check has degree
<=Delta. Greedily choose an independent set of M>=N/(Delta+1) qubits. At its
j-th qubit choose a Pauli P_j anticommuting with some incident check. The
nonempty syndrome sets T_j have size <=D and are pairwise disjoint. Every
product over A of these P_j has valid syndrome disjoint-union_(j in A) T_j.
These 2^M syndromes are distinct. Therefore
 Z_syn >= product_j [1+exp(-beta |T_j|)]
       >= [1+exp(-beta D)]^M.                              (A1)
At every fixed finite beta, log Z_syn=Omega_beta(N).

In the decoded logical/syndrome factorization, a cold logical state is
rho_cold=rho_L tensor |0><0| and thermal encoding is rho_th=rho_L tensor pi_beta.
The least C with rho_cold<=C rho_th is EXACTLY C=Z_syn, by restricting to zero
syndrome. Any initial-state operator-order/path-probability comparison must
pay exp(Omega(N)). Applying it to exp[-Omega(N^eta)] thermal leakage, eta<1,
gives no useful growing retention bound. This persists under an allowed iid
p end-noise write: the no-fault event has probability (1-p)^N, so its optimal
domination factor is >=Z_syn(1-p)^N, still exp(Omega(N)) for sufficiently
small fixed p. Cleaner writing does not fix this comparison argument.

This lower bound does not imply that a cold state actually loses information
quickly. A dynamics-specific cold-start survival/committor theorem remains
necessary. Generic FT encoders and equilibrium witnesses do not supply it.
The earlier Astra CP-compression programme is not relabelled as a new result.

sectors, but needs new factorization/locality proofs and would not satisfy the
strict request to avoid global thermal encoding. No such operation is claimed.

3. RELEASED CROSS-CHALLENGE: GACS'S POSITIVE CLASSICAL PRIMITIVE

Peter Gacs, Reliable Cellular Automata with Self-Organization,
arXiv:math/0003117v2 (25 January 2024 corrected/strengthened version),
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0003117v2 . Full primary text (231 pages) is saved
in sources_a/gacs2024.pdf/.txt with PDF page markers.

Exact import inventory, v2 numbering:
 - Definition 7.10, Proposition 7.12: strong trajectories multiply failure bounds
   for disjoint nonanticipatively chosen spacetime rectangles; CCA is strong.
 - Theorem 7.3, PDF pp.72--74: finite-alphabet nearest-neighbor noisy CCA realizes
   primitive variable-period media with sparse timing/computation failures.
   Its Erlang counter has fixed 3n stages and total rate 3n.
 - Theorem 7.6, Corollary 7.21: continuous-time reliable computation under small
   entrywise perturbations of local transition rates.
 - Definition 10.3, Lemma 10.5: canonical damage-island removal gives
   epsilon* >= cDam[(B*/B)(T*/T)epsilon]^2.
 - Computation Property 10.16: bounded dwell periods and atomic classical

This is positive rigorous timing/program machinery, stronger than readout
marginals or noiseless asynchronous emulation. Its perturbation model is
classical rates. Arbitrary local GKLS controller/data noise is not directly an
instance. Classical restoration does not by itself specify quantum side effects.

4. PRECISE SUFFICIENT QUANTUM COMPOSITION INTERFACE

Supply quantum gadget locations j and random local port rectangles R_j.
The chosen controller must satisfy the following physical contract.

(P1) On a clean enlarged rectangle, actual quantum channels implement exactly
the intended gadget once and in a causal order compatible with its dependencies.
All temporary data, gates, resets and idles lie in R_j.
(P2) Clean service has bounded wall time and total physical volume, uniformly
in size and time. An unbounded wait must count as a failed service event.
(P3) An unclean R_j has a witness among <=v damage windows. The expanded-window
incidence graph has degree <=b-1, and windows are selected nonanticipatively.
Parameters v,b and the volume bounds are fixed.
(P4) Quantum faults obey their own local fault-path bound in the full history
filtration, including syndrome outcomes/controller events. For GKLS norm noise,
prove a continuous-time norm expansion or instrument reduction; do not assume
generic finite-rate CP-event uniformization.

Composition lemma: if every disjoint collection of damage windows has joint
probability <=epsilon^(number of windows), then for any set S of locations,
 P(all j in S have controller-induced failure)
    <=[v epsilon^(1/b)]^|S|.                               (A2)
Proof: choose a witness per j (<=v^|S| choices). Greedy selection using the
incidence bound leaves >=|S|/b disjoint windows. Apply the strong product bound
there and sum. Stopping-time selection is allowed by the source interface.
Gadget overlap can be included in b. The selected quantum code's extended-
rectangle theorem could then combine (A2) with its quantum fault-path bound.

Equation (A2) is standard coarse-graining, not a new quantum threshold. It
makes the missing task exact: prove (P1)--(P4) for an explicit physical port
controller. Gacs supplies strong classical windows, but not quantum semantics.

5. AUDIT OF THE PRINCIPAL'S ACTUAL VACANCY-PACKET REPRESENTATION

This was supplied after independence release. A physical slot has space
|vac> direct-sum C^2. For disjoint input/output slots define
 V_U=sum_(a,b) U_ba |vac>_in|b>_out <a|_in<vac|_out.
Then
 V_U^dagger V_U=P_in,occupied P_out,vacant.                 (A3)
This is independent of the unknown data state. A ready packet has exponential
waiting time at the chosen jump rate, and its no-jump attenuation is scalar
on its data space. One firing consumes inputs, applies U, and fills outputs.
Immediate replay is blocked by input vacancy. No quantum data is copied.
A general channel can use a Stinespring ancilla and discard/reset its output.

On a correctly occupied acyclic circuit graph with unique outgoing operations,
enabled competing operations have disjoint data. Their firing order therefore
gives a valid topological circuit ordering. This core algebra is sound.

Finite epoch wrap is not automatically a counterexample for periodic QEC:
if slot and tag uniquely identify the same required operation in the next
cycle, a future matching packet can legitimately receive that operation. An
arbitrary changing program needs a separate binding between packet epoch,
instruction and operation. Coherent occupancy errors, extra packets and erased
packets are allowed quantum faults; classically restoring a clock does not
restore an erased unknown state. Local destructive packet repair is permissible
only if its quantum fault neighborhood is explicitly counted.

The exact unresolved condition is BACKPRESSURE:
 - If classical control advances without a gate-fired acknowledgment, a command
   can no-op while an input is missing, yet the classical history is correct.
 - If control waits for that acknowledgment, one missing quantum packet can
   impose zero effective transition rates and unbounded waiting. This is not
   a small rate perturbation of the imported bounded-dwell controller.

Thus one needs a bounded-time timeout/destructive repair protocol together
with a fault-volume induction charging all resulting bad extended rectangles
to bounded-overlap physical damage. A deterministic cascade of timeout resets
from one missing packet must not be silently counted as independent faults.
Gacs 10.16 guarantees bounded classical dwell once the reference service
contract applies; it does not prove that coupled packet service contract.
This is a precise missing composition lemma, not a quantum no-go theorem.

Principal's subsequent fixed-main-register watchdog repairs this particular
backpressure problem: IDLE flushes flags/ancillas while preserving the main
payload, ARM attempts one U with a done flag, and COMMIT acknowledges by a
controller-only deadline regardless of success. On timeout it erases a constant
gate footprint. Quantum data can no longer impose permanent zero control rates.
This restores the bounded-dwell import provided the timer itself is implemented
with the sparse timing-fault contract. It is a substantive repair of the earlier
packet-service gap, not an already complete quantum threshold proof.

The remaining lemma is PRODUCTIVE COMMON ARM. Bounded classical dwell and atomic
snapshots alone do not guarantee simultaneous compatible requests: two noiseless
three-phase loops offset by one phase can have disjoint ARM windows forever,
while every dwell time is bounded. Watchdogs would then erase every cycle with
zero physical faults. This does not refute a correctly specified synchronizing
transition rule, but that rule must exclude the history. Prove that every clean
enlarged gate rectangle contains a same-epoch common-ARM interval of length
>=tau_good, with competing data operations locked out. The correct U event then
misses that interval with probability <=exp(-gamma tau_good). Only after this
positive service/overlap bound is proved can the timeout erasures be charged
to sparse fault volumes. Deadline liveness alone is insufficient.

Principal's later atomic all-lock GRANT removes the window-intersection issue:
one owner controls ARM over the whole constant gate footprint, operates U in
place, and releases every lock by its own deadline. It has neither data vacancy
nor hold-and-wait. This is a substantive improvement over pairwise ARM.

Mutexes still need causal operation tags. For a circuit U_AB then V_BC, merely
granting the first available owner can execute V first with positive probability
in a completely fault-free run. A sufficient fault-free guard is that each main
qudit records its next required operation, GRANT checks all such tags, and one
completion atomically advances them. Correctly initialized periodic circuits
then admit an abstract unbounded-counter lift: each physical completion is a
topological step of the unrolled circuit; a repeated modular label alone does
not make a stale replay. This statement assumes that tags are advanced only
once per completion and that no command is queued after its guard expires.

For an already eligible request, bounded-degree contention gives a legitimate
local regeneration event: suppress interfering grants in its constant expanded
footprint, let current leases release, acquire all locks atomically, and fire U.
If its probability in a fixed interval is >=p uniformly in prior port state,
then K consecutive failures have probability <=(1-p)^K. Strong versions for
disjoint adaptively chosen windows follow from the underlying fresh Poisson
clocks, and bounded-overlap selection gives (A2).

This does not manufacture CAUSAL ELIGIBILITY. If a deadline starts before the
required predecessors complete, timeouts can systematically omit unavailable
operations. Starting the deadline at eligibility avoids that problem but leaves
quantum idle exposure while waiting to be bounded. A finite predecessor-closure
or protected-idle construction must connect these service windows to actual
extended rectangles. The source's bounded classical dwell alone is not that
productive-progress proof. No explicit full eligibility/idle/fault-volume
induction was completed in this audit.

B's proposed Pauli-frame receipt ports simplify memory corrections: an atomic
update P(p+a), a<-p telescopes and repeating p is idempotent. Syndrome extraction,
ancilla reset and bounded service/repair exposure still require (P1)--(P4).

6. MATCHED QUANTUM INCUMBENT AND FINAL STATUS

Dunnweber--Styliaris--Trivedi, arXiv:2601.20818v3,
https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20818v3 : the synchronous dissipative-QCA threshold
is proved. Sections VII.1--VII.2 outline local asynchronous clocks, repair,
and a continuous-time realization. The main text leaves a formal continuous-
time threshold proof to future work, and the outlined noise reduction treats
local unitary jump noise. General nonunitary GKLS perturbations are another
open extension. Synchronous universal autonomous QEC is therefore incumbent;
these precise asynchronous/noise-composition operations remain legitimate targets.

Search coverage: asynchronous quantum CA, self-stabilizing classical control,
autonomous-QEC clock thresholds, BDL initialization and BGL sector mixing. No
theorem satisfying the full (P1)--(P4) interface was found in the targeted
search. This is not a comprehensive historical-priority statement.

Achieved: extensive cold/thermal domination obstruction; exact positive Gacs
strong-clock imports; sufficient fault-volume composition inequality (A2);
and the sound packet algebra with an exact coupled-service gap. Not achieved:
noisy-write passive retention, a positive global committor, an explicit robust
quantum port compiler, or a new asynchronous quantum threshold theorem.
