RESEARCHER B — TWO-BODY PASSIVE QUANTUM MEMORY: DYNAMICAL TRANSFER
5 November 2026. The two-qubit result below was obtained independently before reading other researchers' new-sprint results. The principal's subsequent many-qubit generalization is attributed separately.

STATUS

No three-dimensional, bounded-degree, finite-strength two-body Hamiltonian with a proved quantum memory lifetime diverging at fixed nonzero temperature is constructed. The positive target remains unachieved. The proved capital is an exact obstruction to transferring that capability through finite-size spectral/Gibbs approximations. It is not a no-go for physical two-body quantum memory. An exact gauge representation or a uniform finite-resolution open-system transfer remains a viable replacement, but neither has received a new positive thermal certificate here.

1. INDEPENDENT EXACT MEDIATOR COUNTEREXAMPLE

Let L,a be qubits, P_+=|0><0|_L, P_-=|1><1|_L, n_a=|1><1|_a. Set

  H_e=(Delta I_L+e Z_L) tensor n_a,
  Delta>0, 0<=e<Delta.

This is a one-site field plus a two-body term on a two-vertex graph. For every e the ground space is EXACTLY C=H_L tensor |0>_a, with gap at least Delta-e. Also ||H_e-H_0||=e. The Hamiltonians commute, so comparison of Boltzmann weights yields

  ||rho_beta(H_e)-rho_beta(H_0)||_1<=min(2,exp(2 beta e)-1).

Their closed-system channels differ in diamond norm by at most 2te. Thus the ground code is unchanged, the gap is uniform for e<=Delta/2, and spectrum, Gibbs states and fixed-time unitary dynamics are arbitrarily accurate approximations as e tends to zero.

Use the SAME bath coupling S=I_L tensor X_a. At frequencies near Delta choose downward rate d(E)=r>0 and upward rate u(E)=r exp(-beta E); extend the rates outside this interval in any positive detailed-balance manner. This is an explicit Davies rate choice. Omit a Lamb shift, or choose one vanishing on C.

At e=0, degenerate transitions share a frequency, so the jump operators are sqrt(r) I_L tensor |0><1| and sqrt(r exp(-beta Delta)) I_L tensor |1><0|. They preserve L exactly for all times and initial mediator states.

At EVERY e>0, the gaps E_+=Delta+e and E_-=Delta-e differ. Full secularization produces FOUR separate jumps:

  D_+=sqrt(r) P_+ tensor |0><1|_a,
  D_-=sqrt(r) P_- tensor |0><1|_a,
  U_+=sqrt(u(E_+)) P_+ tensor |1><0|_a,
  U_-=sqrt(u(E_-)) P_- tensor |1><0|_a.

THEOREM 1. Encode a qubit into C, evolve by this Davies generator for time t, and allow ANY CPTP decoder from L,a to a qubit. Its optimal entanglement fidelity with a reference is exactly

  F_opt(t)=(1+exp(-Gamma_e t))/2,
  Gamma_e=(u(E_+)+u(E_-))/2
         =r exp(-beta Delta) cosh(beta e).

In particular Gamma_e tends to r exp(-beta Delta)>0 as e tends to zero, although at e=0 the optimal fidelity is one forever.

PROOF. Start with a maximally entangled reference-logical pair and mediator |0>. The logical populations evolve separately and preserve their labels. In the cross-logical block every recycling term vanishes, because P_+|0><1|P_+=P_-|0><1|P_-=0. The only nonzero mediator cross block remains |0><0| with coefficient exp(-Gamma_e t); the Hamiltonian vanishes there. Thus the reference-system state is

  omega(t)=1/2[|00><00|_RL tensor rho_a^+(t)
              +|11><11|_RL tensor rho_a^-(t)
              +exp(-Gamma_e t)(|00><11|+|11><00|)_RL tensor |0><0|_a].

Its partial transpose on R has exactly one negative eigenvalue -exp(-Gamma_e t)/2. Its negativity is exp(-Gamma_e t)/2. A decoder cannot increase negativity, by trace-norm contraction of CPTP maps on Hermitian matrices. Any two-qubit state sigma obeys

  <Phi|sigma|Phi><=||sigma^(T_R)||_1 ||Phi^(T_R)||_infinity
                =(1+2 Neg(sigma))/2,

because Phi^(T_R) is half the swap. This proves the upper bound for every decoder. Tracing out a attains it: the logical channel preserves populations and multiplies the off-diagonal entries by exp(-Gamma_e t). QED.

For target error eta<1/2, retention is at most -log(1-2eta)/Gamma_e=O(exp(beta Delta)/r), uniformly as e approaches zero. Evaluating the derivative of logical X at |+><+| tensor |0><0| also gives a Davies-generator norm change at least Gamma_e. Hence no modulus of continuity in Hamiltonian norm can support a GENERIC thermal-generator transfer theorem of this kind.

LIMIT. The toy logical qubit has no distance against direct logical-site noise. It is a counterexample to an unrestricted simulator/Davies continuity claim, not a demonstrated failure of the 3D incumbent under every local gadget encoding. The same microscopic coupling operator is used in both compared models; the change is the bath model's exact frequency resolution.

KNOWN MECHANISM. Near-degenerate secularization is a known problem. Trushechkin, Phys. Rev. A 103,062226 (2021), https://arxiv.org/html/2103.12042v3 , rigorously groups Bohr-frequency clusters in a joint weak-coupling/near-degeneracy scaling. No novelty of that physical issue is claimed. The exact ground-code-preserving example and arbitrary-decoder fidelity calculation make the obstruction concrete for the proposed memory transfer.

2. AUDIT OF THE PRINCIPAL'S NONRESONANT DAVIES NO-GO

The principal independently obtained the same mechanism and proposed this generalization after the initial report. It survives the present audit.

THEOREM 2. Let H=sum_X h_X on N qubits, with nonempty supports and sup_i sum_(X contains i)||h_X||<=J, for fixed J>0. Assume simple energy spectrum and simple nonzero Bohr spectrum: E_m-E_n=E_p-E_q!=0 implies (m,n)=(p,q). At every site use three independent bath couplings X_i,Y_i,Z_i, with rates gamma_(i,alpha)(omega)>=g_0>0 for |omega|<=4J. Include Hamiltonian evolution and an optional diagonal Davies Lamb shift. For ANY qubit encoding and ANY final CPTP decoder,

  F_ent(t)<=(1+exp(-N g_0 t))/2.

Time is measured in the stated Davies rate units. No dimension or two-body assumption is needed beyond the local strength bound.

PROOF OF ESCAPE. Fix an energy eigenstate |n> and site i. The one-qubit Bloch vector has norm at most one, so

  sum_alpha sum_(m!=n)|<m|sigma_(i,alpha)|n>|^2
    =3-sum_alpha|<n|sigma_(i,alpha)|n>|^2>=2.

Also ||[H,sigma_(i,alpha)]||<=2J, whence

  sum_alpha sum_m (E_m-E_n)^2 |<m|sigma_(i,alpha)|n>|^2
      =sum_alpha||[H,sigma_(i,alpha)]|n>||^2<=12J^2.

Weight outside |E_m-E_n|<=4J is at most 3/4. Nonzero transition weight inside that interval is at least 5/4, hence at least one. The outgoing nonzero-frequency jump rate from EVERY |n> is at least N g_0 after summing sites.

PROOF OF OPERATIONAL BOUND. Every nonzero-frequency jump is a scalar times |m><n|, hence rank one. Let D be the sum of their L^dagger L; the escape bound gives D>=N g_0 I. Keep Hamiltonian evolution, zero-frequency jumps, and all anticommutator terms, but omit nonzero-frequency recycling. This defines a completely positive trace-decreasing evolution T_0(t). For positive rho,

  d/dt Tr T_0(t)(rho)=-Tr[D T_0(t)(rho)]
     <=-N g_0 Tr T_0(t)(rho).

Thus its surviving trace p_0 is at most exp(-N g_0 t), also with an entangled reference. Expand the full channel in nonzero-frequency jump histories. Every other history contains a rank-one jump; its complete Kraus product has rank at most one even after earlier/later operations. This positive subchannel is entanglement breaking. The reference-system output is a positive branch of trace p_0 and a separable branch of trace 1-p_0. After any decoder their Bell fidelities are at most p_0 and (1-p_0)/2. This proves the theorem. QED.

QUALIFICATIONS. Simple Bohr spectrum is stronger than no three-term arithmetic progressions. Section 8 establishes its almost-everywhere applicability in full cubic nearest-neighbor parameter spaces, and in affine perturbation families containing a stated spanning-path witness. It is not inferred from onsite fields alone or arbitrary symmetry restrictions. A finite-temperature bath need not have g_0>0: spectra vanishing near frequency zero fail it. Independent coupling axes and rate normalization are stated resources. If the total N-site noise rate is normalized to one, rescale the exponent. This theorem is not a size-uniform derivation of a Davies model from a fixed-strength microscopic bath.

DIRECT PRIMARY PREDECESSORS. Temme, https://arxiv.org/html/1305.5591 , Lemma 8 and Theorem 11, provides the Bohr-block structure and coherence-decay estimates. Basso–Ganguly–Sinclair–Srivastava–Stier–Vuong, https://arxiv.org/html/2510.07267v1 , Remark 1.18, Proposition 1.20 and Appendix A, explicitly treats simple energy/simple Bohr spectra and bounds a coherence decay rate by average outgoing classical rates. The rank-one/coherence mechanism is therefore established prior machinery. Their Theorem 1.16 only gives absence of three-term progressions for generic local fields, and explicitly permits exponentially degenerate Bohr blocks; it does not establish Theorem 2's nonresonance premise. The useful additional composition is the any-encoding/decoder entanglement-breaking trajectory bound and the uniform N-site variance/commutator estimate. No claim of absent prior art for that composition is made.

3. THE RESOLUTION-TIME RESOURCE

The above are exact fully secular Davies statements. A finite averaging window gives

  (1/T) integral_0^T exp(i delta t)dt
    =exp(i delta T/2)sinc(delta T/2).

If |delta|T<=1 its magnitude exceeds .95; deleting such a cross term is not a small finite-window operation. In Theorem 1 unresolved frequency branches merge and remove the discontinuous measurement.

For a simple-Bohr N-qubit spectrum, M=2^N(2^N-1)/2 distinct positive gaps lie within width 2NJ. Pigeonhole gives a pair separated by at most 2NJ/(M-1)=O(NJ 4^(-N)). Resolving every frequency class can require exponential averaging time. A particular coupling may have zero amplitude for the close pair, so a microscopic uniform approximation must use its actually coupled frequencies and amplitudes rather than this count alone. Burgarth–Facchi–Gramegna–Yuasa, https://arxiv.org/html/2603.26606v3 , gives explicit secular approximation bounds with time and spectral-separation dependence; it is not a uniform gadget-memory transfer.

4. MATCHED INCUMBENTS AND CONSTRUCTIVE REPAIRS

Balasubramanian–Davydova–Lin, https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10943v1 , Theorems 1.1,1.2,2.6 and Section 5, is the strongest starting point in three dimensions: bounded-body CSS stabilizers, bounded range/density and a thermal encoding retain one qubit for stretched-exponential time. Its exact factorization H=I_L tensor H_else is central. Section 1.1 leaves full local-perturbation gap stability/TQO-2 for later work. This is not already a two-body theorem or a generic perturbation-stability theorem. The current note uses the stated incumbent result without claiming a complete independent audit of the construction.

Cubitt–Montanaro–Piddock, Universal quantum Hamiltonians, https://arxiv.org/html/1701.05182 , Proposition 28 and Sections 8.4–8.5, provides low-energy spectral, Gibbs and unitary simulation with explicit accuracy/cutoff dependence. The thermal error includes high-sector dimension against exp(-beta Delta); coherent error grows as t times the spectral error. Noise transfer assumes small leakage from the simulated subspace and does not identify the simulator's perturbed Davies generator with the target one. Fixed gadget penalties do not meet that asymptotic interface automatically.

Ma–Khemani–Sang, PRX Quantum 7,033052 (2026), https://arxiv.org/html/2509.15204v3 , Theorem 3, is a stronger CONDITIONAL phase route. A Gibbs state connected to a macroscopic quantum code by a uniformly stable ANNULAR clustering path retains information under a QUASI-LOCAL Lindbladian having it as a FRUSTRATION-FREE steady state. Ordinary two-point clustering or a ground gap is insufficient. Section VI and Appendix G leave a rigorous stable-clustering proof even for low-temperature 4D toric code desirable. No such path is proved here for a two-body simulator. Its dynamical locality condition excludes calling generic globally resolved Davies jumps local just because their original coupling operators were local.

REPAIR A: require every mediator to remain clean. For M independent two-level mediators of gap Delta, the equilibrium clean-sector weight is (1+exp(-beta Delta))^(-M). At fixed Delta this vanishes. Keeping it above 1-eta requires Delta at least logarithmic in M. This rejects a globally clean-sector proof, NOT a local proof tolerating finite defect density. That local proof is the needed repair and was not obtained.

REPAIR B: replace approximate gadgets by exact noncommuting gauges. Bare logicals of a subsystem code commute with all gauge generators, so a gauge Hamiltonian acts as I_L tensor H_else in every sector. Its logical spectral degeneracy genuinely avoids Theorem 2 and Theorem 1's distinguishable logical excitation gaps. Arbitrary local noise still acts nontrivially and must be corrected on a large thermal stable subspace. An imposed symmetry restriction on all bath Kraus operators would instead give a noiseless subsystem; that is a different resource, not generic thermal protection.

The viable finite-strength operation is an EXACT low-weight gauge compiler with a uniform Gibbs-weight bound on decoder-unstable CENTER syndromes. For a gauge Hamiltonian, center-syndrome projectors and decoder-dressed logicals commute with H. If a local Pauli error is protected on a stable projector, its Davies components remain protected because that projector and the logicals commute with every spectral projection of H. The stable-subspace theorem of Chesi–Loss–Bravyi–Terhal, https://arxiv.org/html/0907.2807 , can then turn an exponentially small unstable Gibbs weight into retention. No such two-body compiler plus positive thermal certificate has been constructed here.

A concrete first cell fails before any asymptotics. For data Z_1,Z_2,Z_3 and one mediator define two-body gauges g_1=Z_1 X_a, g_2=Z_2 Y_a, g_3=Z_3 Z_a and h=-j(g_1+g_2+g_3). Their product is iZ_1Z_2Z_3, so the desired parity is central. But h^2=3j^2I: both parity sectors have partition function 8cosh(beta sqrt(3)j). There is EXACTLY zero parity free-energy penalty at every temperature. Having the target center relation does not provide the required thermal confinement. This attempted repair is rejected.

The commuting two-body alternative is independently limited by Bravyi–Vyalyi's algebra decomposition, https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0308021 , and the Marvian–Lidar error-suppression no-go used in Jiang–Rieffel, https://arxiv.org/html/1511.01997v3 . The latter gives useful finite noncommuting two-body code penalties, not scalable fixed-temperature self-correction. Bacon's original 3D two-body subsystem proposal, https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0506023 , supplies mean-field evidence rather than this thermal proof. Baspin–Williamson's Wire codes, https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10194v2 , compiles arbitrary codes to weight/degree THREE with an active error-correction threshold, rather than a two-body passive thermal theorem. These existing routes are not relabeled as new constructions.

6. CONSTRUCTIVE QUANTUM-PORT OPERATION AFTER INDEPENDENCE RELEASE

The following is an audit/refinement of the principal's token port and researcher C's erasure transaction. It is a positive finite-service primitive, not an autonomous threshold theorem or a passive thermal memory.

Let every packet slot have Hilbert space span{|vac>} direct-sum C^2. For an m-qubit gate U on disjoint input/output slot sets, define the partial isometry

  V_U= sum_(x,y in {0,1}^m) U_(y,x) |vac^m,y><x,vac^m|.

It consumes occupied inputs and vacant outputs. Exactly

  V_U^dagger V_U=P_(inputs occupied)P_(outputs vacant)=P,
  V_U V_U^dagger=Q, V_U^2=0, P Q=0.

The CPTP attempted-operation channel E_U(rho)=V_U rho V_U^dagger+(I-P)rho(I-P) is idempotent: E_U^2=E_U. On the valid occupation sector its first firing transfers the entire input state, including all reference entanglement, and applies U. All later attempts are inert until a genuinely new packet is installed. Both the guard and its firing rate are independent of the quantum payload. The continuous generator kappa(E_U-Id) is a local time-independent GKLS generator; on the valid sector its waiting time is exponential of rate kappa. These are exact algebraic statements, not a low-energy approximation.

For a finite causal DAG of at most m_0 such actions, with correct flags and no recycled slots, every nonterminal configuration has an enabled action of rate at least kappa. Serializing a chosen enabled action after each completed action stochastically upper-bounds completion time by Erlang(m_0,kappa). Therefore

  Pr(T_complete>T)<=2^(m_0) exp(-kappa T/2).

This can be made an arbitrarily small FIXED constant by a fixed waiting budget; no device-size-dependent rate is needed. It says nothing about corrupted controls or data-dependent backpressure.

PRIVATE-OUTBOX TRANSACTION. Give each gadget a private output buffer. Its quantum operation is guarded by ACTIVE(e) and atomically changes the flag to COMMITTED(e) together with V_U. A watchdog cancellation, also guarded by ACTIVE(e), atomically discards the constant-size input/work/private-output slots, installs known dummy output states, and changes the flag to COMMITTED(e). Thus successful completion and cancellation cannot both happen in one correct epoch. A single atomic HANDOFF moves the committed outbox to the receiving gadget's vacant input, sets the producer CONSUMED(e), and sets the receiver HAS(e). Neither gadget otherwise writes the other's private slots. The old operation cannot fire after cancellation because ACTIVE(e) is false; GKLS jump attempts are evaluated at firing and do not queue a pre-cancellation payload operation.

The watchdog can use a fixed Erlang counter. For serial m_0 rate-kappa actions and an independent H-stage rate-nu watchdog, the probability that the watchdog finishes first is

  sum_(j=0)^(m_0-1) binom(H+j-1,j)
      [nu/(kappa+nu)]^H [kappa/(kappa+nu)]^j.

It tends to zero exponentially in H for fixed m_0. Gacs' continuous-time reliable computing theorem already supplies robust finite-alphabet timing primitives; the counter is not claimed as new. A cancellation is one arbitrary quantum fault on a fixed number of slots, rather than a hidden successful quantum gate. These engineered resets use dissipative resources and are not a two-body equilibrium Hamiltonian.

WHAT STILL NEEDS PROOF. A missing late input must be discarded AND acknowledged under its cancelled epoch; otherwise upstream backpressure survives the local cancellation. Stop-and-wait with at most one outstanding packet prevents old packets surviving epoch reuse in a proper history. Finite modulo tags alone do not prove correctness from arbitrary corrupted receipts. For a periodic QEC program, however, a later matching phase may legitimately require the same operation; wraparound by itself is not an impossibility proof. Arbitrary local unitary noise also creates coherent vacant/occupied leakage or spurious packets. Controller-only regeneration does not clear those faults: private quantum slots need a destructive reset or a proved leakage-reduction mechanism.

The required global contract is a bounded-overlap fault charging rule: outside sparse damaged spacetime regions, every packet action is a correct causal action and each qubit's idle dwell is bounded; all actions influenced by an erased/corrupt transaction must remain within those same charged regions. Merely decoding the classical controller correctly does not imply that contract. Gacs' classical history reconstruction may replay already committed quantum side effects, or waiting for an acknowledgement may create endogenous zero update rates outside the existing service assumptions. This is the decisive remaining composition, rather than the finite-DAG port algebra proved above.

REGENERATION TAIL CAUTION. If one poisoned controller survives Exp(lambda) time and influences a cone of radius vT, its spacetime volume grows as T^(D+1). The resulting volume tail is stretched exponential, exp(-c lambda V^(1/(D+1))), not the product local-stochastic fault bound p^V. A small mean density epsilon lambda^(-D-1) alone does not prove a quantum circuit threshold. Exponential diameter tails might instead be handled by a new cone/percolation renormalization. If a poisoned controller infects neighbors at order-one rate while each heals only at rare rate lambda, even the single-cone lifetime assumption can fail; quarantine or one-event neighborhood repair is necessary. No such global fault-volume theorem is asserted here.

MEMORY-ONLY SIMPLIFICATION. An absolute Pauli-frame port is idempotent: with desired frame p and receipt a in F_2^2, atomically apply P(p+a) and set a=p. Repeating the same command does nothing, and a sequence telescopes to its final frame up to phase. Receipt corruption a->a+e is a local Pauli error in the framed state P(a)rho P(a)^dagger. This can simplify storage corrections, whose syndrome measurements are commuting idempotent channels, but does not by itself give a noisy asynchronous decoder or a bounded idle-volume proof. Pauli-frame bookkeeping is established fault-tolerance machinery, not a new memory capability.

PRIMARY CLASSICAL INTERFACE. Gacs, Reliable Cellular Automata with Self-Organization, https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0003117 , Theorem 7.3 constructs a finite-alphabet continuous-time counter/simulation with small fixed timing failures; Theorem 7.6 and Corollary 7.21 transfer reliable computation to small entrywise rate perturbations. Condition 10.16 provides bounded dwell and off-damage classical snapshot atomicity. The retrieved proof was read at these statements. It reconstructs classical histories and does not state a quantum packet side-effect theorem. That distinction is exactly the outstanding composition above.

FINITE AUDITS. check_b.py and checks_b.json verify 18 Lindblad evolutions of Theorem 1, including the discontinuous e=0 case; its fidelity and negativity formulas agree to below 1e-15 and its Gibbs stationarity residual is below 6e-17. One- and two-qubit random-unitary transfer matrices verify the port guard, nilpotence and channel idempotence. Twenty-five exact rational watchdog-race recurrences agree with the negative-binomial formula. These checks support finite algebra only, not the unresolved autonomous fault-volume theorem.

7. A POSITIVE CAUSAL REPAIR: ERASURE MAY FINISH BEFORE INPUTS ARRIVE

The preceding missing-input obstruction has a precise partial repair. A cancellation should cut the quantum dependency, rather than require all predecessor quantum operations to finish before emitting a dummy.

ERASURE REORDERING LEMMA. Consider any finite directed acyclic quantum circuit of CPTP gates, with arbitrary input/reference entanglement. At a selected collection of gates replace the gate by the replacement channel E_v(X)=Tr(X) sigma_v, where sigma_v is a fixed output state. Replace each such vertex v by two vertices: P_v prepares sigma_v with no input, and D_v discards the original input factors with no output. Remove the ordering edge from D_v to P_v. Every execution respecting the remaining wire dependencies implements EXACTLY the same final channel as the original circuit with those replacement faults.

PROOF. On a joint state rho_IR of the gate's inputs I and all other systems R,

  (E_v tensor Id_R)(rho_IR)=sigma_v tensor Tr_I(rho_IR).

The preparation acts on fresh output factors and the discard acts on I, so these two maps commute. Every downstream operation using the new output factors also commutes with discarding I, even if I is entangled with other live factors acted upon downstream. This is the standard tensor-product commutation of maps on disjoint factors; entanglement does not change it. Splitting the vertex therefore changes only an unnecessary causal ordering. Any two linear extensions of the resulting DAG are related by exchanges of adjacent independent vertices, whose channels commute. Contracting all vertices proves the asserted channel identity, including a reference. QED.

APPLICATION. A watchdog may publish fixed dummy outputs immediately and retain a cancellation receipt that later discards and acknowledges the missing incoming packets. An already advanced downstream consumer then need not be rolled back. If an upstream producer also has other live outputs, it still performs its gate; only the output wire cancelled by this receipt is discarded. Skipping the entire upstream gate would generally be wrong. The same receipt must not erase a later legitimate epoch: stop-and-wait retirement/fencing is still needed.

This lemma removes a quantum-causality objection to erasure transactions and is a concrete constructive operation. It does not prove the global threshold. In particular, a missing input can create a cascade of timeouts if every downstream timer starts too early. One needs a sparse bound for such cascades and bounded service/dwell outside damaged regions. Classical snapshot correctness alone does not establish that timing bound. No claim of a complete autonomous quantum compiler follows from the lemma.

8. PRIMARY GENERICITY CHECK: EXACT SCOPE OF THE BATH-MODEL OBSTRUCTION

The initial qualification that nonresonance had not been established is now resolved for the following concrete coefficient spaces. This is a material applicability check, not a physical no-go or a new claim about spectral genericity.

PRIMARY INPUT. Huang, Extensive entropy from unitary evolution, https://arxiv.org/html/2104.02053v1 , Definition 4, Theorem 2, Appendix B Lemma 7 and Appendix C.2, proves DISTINCT nonzero ordered Bohr gaps, not merely absence of three-term progressions. Lemma 7 constructs, for every N, a chain witness

  H_w=Z_1+sum_(j=1)^(N-1)[h_j X_(j+1)+J_j(2+Z_j)Z_(j+1)].

Its finite-N inductive perturbation proof separates the remaining repeated gaps and terminal Z expectations; it makes no uniform lower-gap claim. Appendix C.2 embeds the chain through a spanning nearest-neighbor path. Lee–Pilatowsky-Cameo–Choi, https://arxiv.org/html/2609.26885v1 , Proposition S2 and Lemma S1, gives a stronger all-orders no-resonance theorem for coefficient spaces containing onsite X,Z and nearest-neighbor ZZ,XZ. Its proof likewise uses a one-dimensional witness and a polynomial zero set. For the present second-order condition Huang's smaller set of witness directions suffices.

POLYNOMIAL LEMMA. Let V be a finite real vector space of Hermitian matrices containing one H_w with nondegenerate energy gaps. For ANY fixed Hermitian H_0, almost every v in EVERY nonempty open subset of V makes H_0+v have simple spectrum and simple nonzero Bohr spectrum. The exceptional set is the zero set of one nonzero polynomial, hence has measure zero and empty interior. This is also an open dense condition for each fixed finite N.

PROOF. Put d=2^N and let H^(2) be H tensor I+I tensor H restricted to the symmetric square of C^d. Its eigenvalues are the unordered pair sums E_i+E_j, i<=j. Let p(H) be the discriminant of its characteristic polynomial. Matrix entries, characteristic coefficients and this discriminant are polynomial functions of the real coefficients of H. The discriminant is nonzero exactly when all unordered pair sums differ. This is equivalent to simple energies and distinct nonzero ordered gaps: a gap equality rearranges to a pair-sum equality, and the only pair-sum equalities corresponding to trivial gap equalities have identical unordered index pairs.

The polynomial p is homogeneous under H->tH, of degree M(M-1), where M=d(d+1)/2. Since p(H_w)!=0, for sufficiently large t,

  p(H_0+tH_w)=t^(M(M-1))p(H_w+H_0/t)!=0.

Thus p(H_0+v) is not identically zero on V, even though H_w need not lie near H_0. A nonzero real polynomial has a zero set of measure zero: apply the one-variable root bound to fibers outside the zero set of its leading coefficients and use induction/Fubini. Its zero set has empty interior. Its complement is open by continuity. Translation to ANY small parameter neighborhood now follows; no witness near the desired memory Hamiltonian is required. QED.

CUBIC NEAREST-NEIGHBOR COROLLARY. Take N qubits at all sites of a rectangular cubic box, allowing independent onsite X,Y,Z coefficients and all nine two-Pauli coefficients on every nearest-neighbor edge. The box has a nearest-neighbor Hamiltonian path: order rows alternately forward/backward and reverse successive layers. Its coefficient space contains the embedded Huang witness. Hence almost every point of every full-dimensional open coefficient neighborhood, including neighborhoods centered at any candidate memory Hamiltonian in that space, meets Theorem 2's spectral premise.

If every coefficient has magnitude at most B, decompose each Pauli term separately. Since the site degree is at most six,

  sup_i sum_(X contains i)||h_X|| <=(3+9*6)B=57B=:J_bar.

Alternatively, in a coefficient-l_infinity neighborhood of radius delta around a Hamiltonian with local strength J_0, take J_bar=J_0+57delta. Assume the SAME per-site Pauli-bath family has a uniform rate lower bound g_min>0 on [-4J_bar,4J_bar], independent of the chosen coefficient point and system size. Then almost every Hamiltonian in every such neighborhood obeys

  F_ent(t)<=(1+exp(-N g_min t))/2

for every encoding and decoder. At target error eta in (0,1/2), useful retention necessarily satisfies

  t<=-log(1-2eta)/(N g_min).

This rules out a nonempty open robust quantum-memory phase WITHIN THIS EXACT SEMIGROUP CLASS with a uniform per-site rate bound. The encoder and decoder may depend on every perturbed coefficient; the bound already optimizes over them.

FAMILY QUANTIFIERS. For each size take any nonempty open neighborhood U_N, however small, subject to a common local-strength bound. A coefficient distribution with an absolutely continuous marginal on each U_N avoids the exceptional set at every size simultaneously with probability one, by a countable union of null events; independence across sizes is unnecessary. In particular, a bounded independent-disorder perturbation of a fixed infinite cubic interaction has absolutely continuous finite-volume marginals, so this is not merely an isolated bad finite-size Hamiltonian. Conversely, this statement does not assign an almost-everywhere probability to a discrete finite-energy table.

AFFINE AND RESTRICTED SCOPES. More generally, the same proof works around any fixed H_0 whenever the permitted perturbation directions contain onsite X,Z and ZZ couplings ALONG A SPANNING PATH, because they contain H_w. H_0 itself can have other bounded local terms; its local bound must then be included in J_bar. A full two-body parameter space is one sufficient case. Merely being a connected irregular occupied-site graph does not establish a spanning path. No theorem is inferred for an irregular subset without a witness, for a translation-invariant or symmetry/gauge-restricted subspace, for onsite fields alone, or for fixed discrete coefficient lists. A restricted family may lie entirely on the exceptional algebraic set. For an individual exact table, Theorem 2 still applies if its spectral premises are verified separately.

PHYSICAL TIME AND ORDER OF LIMITS. The time t above is in GENERATOR/RATE UNITS. A microscopic Davies derivation resolving exponentially close frequencies may require a bath coupling lambda_N that shrinks with N. If the displayed rate functions are defined in rescaled time tau=lambda_N^2 t_physical, then the physical bound is

  F_ent(t_physical)<=(1+exp(-N lambda_N^2 g_min t_physical))/2.

Its useful physical time can increase as lambda_N decreases; the uniform physical-rate premise has then failed. At a fixed nonzero microscopic coupling, fully resolving all these gaps may instead be an invalid nonuniform secular approximation. Therefore this corollary rules out an open robust phase for exact fully secular semigroups WITH UNIFORM WINDOW RATES. It does not rule out fixed-coupling physical quantum memory. The missing operation remains a uniform weak-coupling/coarse-graining or finite-resolution dynamical transfer theorem.
