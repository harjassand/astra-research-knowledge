INTERFACE. Unknown strongly connected directed Q on n>=2 resolved states, known

THEOREM. For every delta,alpha in (0,1), and maintenance duration T<infinity,

No rate identification or exact instantaneous distribution sensor is used.

1. SAFE ENLARGED-BOX POPULATION CONSTANTS

Put r=min rho_i, R0=(n-1)qmax, A=R0+(k/2)log(1/r),
B=2(n-1)log(A/(r*qmin)). The deterministic p feedback has |ui(t)|<=B,
and its unique equilibrium (rho,u*) has |u*i|<=B. This uses C's exact identity
 V=F+(k/2)D(p||rho), F=sum_E rho_i qij exp((ui-uj)/2),
 V'=-(k/2)sum_E rho_i q^u_ij
       [ri log(ri/rj)-ri+rj], ri=p_i/rho_i,
and F>=r*qmin exp(osc(u)/(2(n-1))).

A noisy path need not have F<=A. Instead use |ui|<=B+1 and set
 Qb=R0 exp(B+1), m=r*qmin exp(-B-1)/[4n(n-1)],
 Mh=Qb/2, Cq=2Qb, a=k*r*m,
 eps=min(m/2,k/4,a/[2(k/r+Cq^2/(4m))]),
 cW=min(m/4,k/8), CW=max((Mh+eps)/2,k/(2r)+eps/2),
 lambda=min(a/2,eps*m)/CW.
All are positive and computable without Q. Let x=p-rho,y=u-u*,
 W=F(u)-F(u*)+(k/2)D(p||rho)+eps*x.y.
Throughout this box (and all p in the simplex),
 cW(||x||²+||y||²)<=W<=CW(||x||²+||y||²), W'<=-lambda W.       (1)
Indeed Hess F[v,v]=(1/4)sum rho_i q^u_ij(vi-vj)²>=m||v||²
for rho.v=0 by a directed max-to-min path. Trace Hess F=F/2<=Qb/2,
||Q^u||2<=2Qb, and the scalar relative entropy curvature yields V'<=-a||x||².
Then (x.y)'<=[k/r+Cq²/(4m)]||x||²-m||y||². Taylor, Pinsker,
D(p||rho)<=sum x_i²/rho_i and the stated eps prove (1).

2. BASIN AND FINITE-DIFFERENCE ENTROPY

Choose h=min(1,cW/4,cW*r²/16,cW*delta²/(4n)). Use the connected basin W<h
around (rho,u*), intersected with |ui|<B+1,p_i>r/2. In it (1) implies
||x||<=r/4,||y||<=1/2,||p-rho||1<=delta/2. A first exit through either
auxiliary boundary already requires W>=h, so no extra exit is hidden.

Before exit and for N>=4/r, every jump Delta p=(ej-ei)/N and its interpolation
stay at p_i>=r/4. Set H=2k/r. Since Hess_pp W=(k/2)diag(1/p_i),
 0<=Delta W-grad_p W.Delta p<=H/N².                          (2)
The tangent part of grad_p W is (k/2)log(p/rho)+eps*y, with norm at most
G sqrt(W/cW), G=k/r+eps. Put K=sqrt(2/cW)*G. Thus
 |Delta W|<=K sqrt(W)/N+H/N²,
 (Delta W)²<=2K² W/N²+2H²/N⁴.                               (3)
Total jump intensity is at most N Qb. The finite-N generator therefore obeys
 G_N W<=-lambda W+H Qb/N.                                  (4)
Feedback destroys unconditional independence; only conditional intensities are
used. Entropy at an empty empirical state is never Taylor-expanded.

3. EXPONENTIAL EXIT BOUND

Put s=min(1/(K+H),lambda/(2e Qb K²)),
b0=s H Qb+s² e Qb H², Cstar=b0 exp(2b0/lambda), Z=exp(sNW).
Equations (3), h<=1 give |sN Delta W|<=1. Using
exp(z)-1-z<=z² exp(|z|)/2 in the jump generator and (4),
 G_N Z/Z <=sN[-lambda W+H Qb/N]
              +s² e Qb[K² N W+H²/N]
           <=-(s lambda/2)N W+b0.
For z=sNW>=0, exp(z)[b0-(lambda/2)z]<=Cstar, so G_N Z<=Cstar,
independent of N. Stop at tau=first exit W>=h. Bounded coefficients and
controlled jump overshoots justify Dynkin/optional stopping. If W0<=h/4,

 P(tau<=T)<=exp(-3sNh/4)+Cstar*T exp(-sNh).                  (5)

Taking N>=4/r and
 N>= (4/(3s h))log(2/alpha_m),
 N>= (1/(s h))max(0,log(2 Cstar*T/alpha_m))
gives maintenance failure <=alpha_m. For T=exp(s h N/2), failure is
exponentially small in N. This is a finite-horizon bound; fixed N does not
give exact forever regulation. Constants can be extremely poor in n and rates.

4. COMPUTABLE BURN-IN AND BOUNDED HARDWARE

Initially ideal W0<=Wmax=CW(2+nB²). Choose
t0=max(0,lambda^-1 log[64 CW Wmax/(cW h)]),
eta=min(1/4,sqrt(h/(64 CW))). Then the ideal Euclidean error at t0 is at most
sqrt(h/(64 CW)), and actual/ideal combined error <=eta puts actual W<=h/16.

Use a smooth component actuator clamp, identity on [-B-1,B+1], bounded by B+2.
Multiply the feedback integrator velocity by a smooth scalar radial cutoff,
identity for ||u||<=sqrt(n)B+1, zero for ||u||>=sqrt(n)B+2. Gauge is preserved;
ODE uniqueness makes the outer ball invariant. Neither clamp acts during a good
burn-in or maintenance path. Both hardware ranges are independent of T.

Let Qg=R0 exp(B+2), L=2Qg+k/r. The empirical martingale has
E sup_{t<=t0}||M_t||²<=8Qg*t0/N. Up to first eta separation, the unchanged
local drift is L-Lipschitz in ||P-p||+||u-u_ideal||. Stopped Gronwall gives
P(uniform separation>eta)<=8Qg*t0 exp(2L*t0)/(N eta²).
It suffices to take
 N>=8Qg*t0 exp(2L*t0)/(alpha_b eta²).                       (6)
Conditionally on the good endpoint, apply (5) and the augmented Markov property.
Set alpha_b=alpha_m=alpha/2. Equations (5)-(6) prove the theorem. Burn-in can be
prohibitively expensive, but its bound is independent of the later duration T.
No global Lipschitz assertion for the radial cutoff is silently used: the
burn-in comparison is stopped while both cutoffs are inactive.
