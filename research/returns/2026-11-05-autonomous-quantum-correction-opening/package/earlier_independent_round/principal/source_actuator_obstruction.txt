PRINCIPAL COUNTEREXAMPLE: SOURCE-ENERGY ACTUATION LOSES ALL-GAIN STABILITY
5 November 2026. Direct finite-dimensional proof. Priority unestablished.

The proved controller uses q^u_ij=qij exp((ui-uj)/2). One might replace this
restrictive actuator by q^u_ij=qij exp(ui), which is the fixed-transition-barrier
Arrhenius response to changing a source state's energy. Keep exactly the same
integral feedback u'=k(p/rho-1). The tempting all-gain extension is FALSE.

COUNTEREXAMPLE. Let Q be the unit-rate directed 3-cycle, q12=q23=q31=1 and
all other off-diagonal entries zero. Set rho=(1/3,1/3,1/3), gauge sum u=0.
The equilibrium p=rho,u=0 is linearly asymptotically stable for 0<k<6,
has eigenvalues +/-2sqrt(3)i at k=6, and has two eigenvalues with positive
real parts for EVERY k>6. No limit-cycle claim is needed or made.

PROOF. Put x=p-rho and z=diag(rho)u. The linearization on the two zero-sum
subspaces is
 x'=(x+z)Q, z'=k x.
Each nonzero left eigenmode mu of Q therefore has characteristic equation
 lambda^2-mu lambda-k mu=0.
The two mu are -3/2 +/- i sqrt(3)/2. Multiplying the conjugate factors gives
 lambda^4+3lambda^3+(3+3k)lambda^2+6k lambda+3k^2.
The Routh first column, for k>0, has signs corresponding to
 1, 3, 3+k, 3k(6-k)/(3+k), 3k^2.
Thus 0<k<6 has no right-half-plane roots and k>6 has two. At k=6 the polynomial
factors as (lambda^2+12)(lambda^2+3lambda+9). The imaginary roots are simple,
and d Re(lambda)/dk=1/13>0 there. This proves a transverse instability.

More generally a generator mode mu=-a+ib, a>0,b!=0, crosses at
 kcrit=a(a^2+b^2)/b^2, with omega=kcrit*b/a.
For real negative mu, lambda^2-mu lambda-k mu has both roots in the open left
half-plane for every k>0. Reversible generator spectra therefore do not reveal
this obstruction; nonreversible circulation does. The 3-cycle is admissible
in the proved symmetric-tilt theorem, whose entropy identity remains valid
for every k. The two actuator laws cannot be conflated.

This is a counterexample to the proposed extension, not a new general control
instability principle. It does not rule out a different source-actuated feedback
law, sufficiently small-gain regulation, extra sensing, edge-rate control, or
model identification. Those are constructive alternatives. It pinpoints a
load-bearing physical assumption in the strongest achieved regulation theorem.
