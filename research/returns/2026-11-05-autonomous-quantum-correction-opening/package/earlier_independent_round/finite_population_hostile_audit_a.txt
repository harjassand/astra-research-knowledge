VERDICT

The principal's finite-horizon theorem is sound at its stated exact actuator,
continuous controller interface. The proof handles the material issues: noisy
paths need an enlarged box; entropy is expanded only at positive empirical
coordinates; jump overshoots are included; and a scalar integrator cutoff bounds
the internal controller on every path without changing the successful paths.
The maintenance population is logarithmic in duration for fixed other inputs.
The principal's original burn-in bound scales as 1/alpha_b. C's subsequently
audited Bernstein burn-in in Section 8 below upgrades the total population to
O(log((1+T)/alpha)) for fixed model data, gain and target accuracy.

The stochastic operation is established small-noise stability/metastability
machinery. The specific unknown-generator, conservative state-potential feedback
and its joint entropy Lyapunov remain a candidate specialized extension. I found
no exact matched predecessor in the documented targeted search. This establishes
neither priority nor a better sample/time/work standard than calibration followed
by a static approximately optimal field.

1. PRECISE STOCHASTIC MODEL

N particles have conditional rates qij exp[(chi(ui)-chi(uj))/2], qij in the given
positive bounds on a strongly connected support. A simultaneous jump of two
particles has probability zero. Hence the empirical-controller pair is Markov,
with jump P -> P+(ej-ei)/N at rate N Pi qij^{chi(u)} and continuous drift
u'=k xi(u)(P/rho-1). Here chi is the component actuator clamp and xi the scalar
radial integrator cutoff specified by the principal. Independence is conditional
on the current shared controller; unconditional particle independence is unused.
The cutoff is scalar, so rho.u remains zero. Globally bounded rates give
nonexplosion; locally Lipschitz bounded controller dynamics have unique paths.

2. EXACT LOWER-BOX CONSTANTS

The deterministic identity bounds F(u)<=A and osc(u)<=B. On the deterministic
reachable set and segments to u*, one may improve C's constants to
 m_det=r qmin exp(-B/2)/[4n(n-1)], M_det=A/2, C_det=2A/r.
The factor exp(-B/2) follows from the oscillation, not an absolute-coordinate
bound. F<=A bounds rho-weighted activity and each row out-rate by A/r; it does
not by itself bound the actual p-weighted jump activity by A.

The principal correctly does NOT import F<=A for noisy paths. On |ui|<=B+1,
qmin exp(-B-1)<=qij^u<=qmax exp(B+1), F<=Qb, and the absolute row/column sums of
Q^u are <=2Qb. Thus its enlarged-box m, Mh=Qb/2 and Cq=2Qb are valid.
For v with rho.v=0, min(v)<=0<=max(v), so ||v||^2<=n osc(v)^2. A directed path
of length <=n-1 from a maximum to a minimum gives
 sum_E(vi-vj)^2>=||v||^2/[n(n-1)].
This proves the claimed Hessian lower bound on the correct gauge space. The
Euclidean gradient is -(rho Q^u)/2, although it belongs to sum(v)=0 rather than
rho.v=0; the proof needs only its dot product with y=u-u*, so no projection is
missing. The strictification and norm comparisons of Section 1 pass.

3. ENTROPY BOUNDARY AND BASIN BOOTSTRAP

The h choice gives ||P-rho||<=r/4, ||u-u*||<=1/2 while W<h. Therefore
Pi>=3r/4 and |ui|<=B+1/2. A first departure through Pi=r/2 or |ui|=B+1 is
impossible before W reaches h. Defining the exit via the auxiliary box as well
is harmless and avoids circular reasoning.

For N>=4/r, each interpolation between a pre-exit empirical point and its
one-jump successor remains at Pi>=r/4. Since
 Hess_pp W=(k/2)diag(1/Pi),
its norm is at most H=2k/r on that interpolation. The cross term eps*x.y is
linear in p, so it creates no second-order jump term. Taylor's remainder is
between zero and H/N^2 because ||Delta p||^2=2/N^2. This proves (2).

The irrelevant constant k/2 in the ambient entropy gradient drops out against
Delta p. On the pre-exit region, log(Pi/rho_i) has Lipschitz constant <=2/r
along the segment to rho. Thus the displayed G=k/r+eps and K are safe. The
first-order jump generator plus integrator drift is the deterministic derivative
at the current (P,u), giving GW<=-lambda W+H Qb/N. No derivative or Taylor
expansion at an empty empirical state has been used.

The optional connected-component wording causes no endpoint problem: W is
strictly convex in (p,u) on the local box. Its diagonal Hessian blocks are at
least (k/2)I and mI, and cross blocks are eps I; eps<=k/4,m/2 makes their quadratic
form positive. The small sublevel containing the good endpoint is connected.

4. EXPONENTIAL GENERATOR AND STOPPING

For Z=exp(sNW), the exact PDMP chain rule is
 GZ/Z=sN GW+sum_(jumps) rate*(exp(sN Delta W)-1-sN Delta W).
The chosen s, N>=1, W<h<=1 ensure |sN Delta W|<=s(K+H)<=1. Inserting the square
bound and exp(z)-1-z<=e z^2/2 gives precisely
 GZ/Z<=-(s lambda/2)NW+b0,
 b0=sH Qb+s^2 e Qb H^2.
The ignored factor 1/N in the last positive term only makes this looser.
For z=sNW>=0, exp(z)(b0-lambda z/2)<=b0 exp(2b0/lambda)=Cstar.

Stop at the first W>=h. Continuous exits hit h; jump exits may overshoot, which
only raises Z at exit and strengthens the lower bound Z_tau>=exp(sNh).
The stopped pre-exit region plus all one-jump successors is compact, has
Pi>=r/4 and controlled overshoots, so the stopped test function can be extended
smoothly there and Dynkin's formula applies. Starting W<=h/4 yields
 P(tau<=T)<=exp(-3sNh/4)+Cstar*T*exp(-sNh).
The stated sample thresholds therefore pass. The argument is for a prescribed
finite horizon and does not assert forever regulation at fixed N.

5. BURN-IN AND THE FINITE INTERNAL MEMORY

Wmax=CW(2+nB^2) is valid at (e1,0). The principal's t0 ensures
 ||(p(t0)-rho,u(t0)-u*)||_2<=sqrt[h/(64 CW)].
The combined tracking tolerance eta is no greater than this same quantity, so
the actual Euclidean error is at most twice that radius. The upper W comparison
then gives W_actual(t0)<=h/16, safely within the required h/4.

Until the first actual/ideal separation reaches eta<=1/4, actual components
satisfy |ui|<=B+1/4 and its Euclidean norm is <=sqrt(n)B+1/4. Consequently both
clamps are inactive throughout this stopped comparison. This proves, rather
than assumes, that the original local drift applies. On that region the direct
exponential-rate bounds give the stated combined Lipschitz constant L=2Qg+k/r.
The global clamped rate bound gives the Hilbert martingale estimate
 E sup_(t<=t0)||M_t||^2<=8Qg*t0/N.
Stopped Gronwall and Markov's inequality prove (6), including possible jump
overshoot of the separation threshold. No global Lipschitz bound for the
radial cutoff is needed.

The scalar radial cutoff is one on the ideal and every pre-exit good path and
zero outside radius sqrt(n)B+2. At the outer boundary the continuous velocity
vanishes, and uniqueness forbids crossing it. Its scalar nature preserves the
gauge. Therefore internal memory is bounded on ALL paths, independently of T;
this statement is stronger than merely bounding actuator outputs. It introduces
no unknown-F or unknown-u* detector. After burn-in, the augmented Markov property
and the exit estimate apply conditionally at the good endpoint. Union of the
two failure events proves the advertised theorem.

6. CLOSEST PRECISE PRIMARY STOCHASTIC COMPARATOR

Adrien Prodhomme, "Strong Gaussian approximation of metastable density-dependent
Markov chains on large time scales", arXiv:2010.06861v3, 11 December 2020,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.06861v3 . Proposition 3.8, Eq. (38), gives
 P(sup_(s<=t)||X_x^N(s)-phi_x(s)||>eta(N))
   <=2|E|(t+1)exp[-V0 N eta(N)^2].
Standing assumptions (A),(B): finite jump set; differentiable rate functions
with locally Lipschitz gradients and square roots on an open neighborhood;
nonexplosion; a Hurwitz equilibrium of the fluid ODE. Initial x ranges over a
compact subset of its basin. The proposition requires N^(-1/2)<<eta(N)<=eta0
and N sufficiently large, allowing constant small eta. It already implies a
log-duration population requirement at fixed accuracy. Theorem 1.1 gives a
stronger Gaussian coupling, and Proposition 2.1 sharp moderate-deviation exits.

Exact applicability obstruction: Prodhomme's state is a lattice pure-jump
process; our integrator has continuous deterministic drift. His theorem is
therefore a close primary comparator, not a theorem directly imported here.
The principal's proof supplies the elementary PDMP extension with explicit
constants uniform over unknown Q satisfying the bounds. Method-level novelty
for exponential residence or logarithmic population is excluded by this source.

7. ACTUATOR-INTERFACE COMPARISON AND SEARCH LIMITS

The exact single-trajectory controller is factorized ECMC, as identified in
researcher_a.txt Section 11 and accepted by C. Michel--Kapfer--Krauth,
J. Chem. Phys. 140 (2014)054116, arXiv:1309.7748v2, Section IV Eq. (15) and the
paragraph after Eq. (16), are the exact directed-factor event-rate predecessor.
This is not a novelty claim for the finite-ensemble mean-field feedback.

Elamvazhuthi--Biswal--Berman, Automatica 124 (2021)109351,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.automatica.2020.109351, supplies density-feedback
stabilization ancestry, with separately actuated edge rates. The publisher's
primary preview gives its Theorems 4.3,4.7, Proposition 5.1, Lemma 5.4 and
Proposition 5.5. I have not imported or claimed to audit its unavailable full
author PDF. C's note specifies the differences from conservative potentials.

An additional recent primary check is Cohen--Priya, arXiv:2610.24883v1,
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.24883v1 . Theorem 3.3, Eq. (3.1), uses the value
gradient of a known control problem. Theorem 3.5 assumes Assumptions 2.2--2.3
(regularity/joint convexity of the corrected flux Lagrangian, positive symmetric
reference rates) and 2 beta K<1. It does not provide the present
unknown nonreversible integral-feedback theorem. It reinforces that free-energy
stability and conservative exponential actuation are established themes.

Search coverage: primary density-feedback control, unknown-rate Markov control,
integral feedback, adaptive bias, ECMC, finite-state entropy control, and
small-noise stable-population bounds. Several broad "unknown transition rates"
hits control a linear plant switched by an exogenous Markov chain; those do not
control the chain's occupation and were rejected. No comprehensive-history or
first-theorem claim is justified by this search.

A matched model-based incumbent remains available at this same resolved-state
interface: estimate Q from transition counts and occupation exposures, then
solve the finite convex matrix-balancing/DV problem and apply a static field.
Known positive rate bounds and strong connectivity give a computable finite
calibration/mixing contract. Neither this audit nor the construction proves
the adaptive route cheaper. Its earned operation is a finite, autonomous
unknown-Q regulation theorem at the exact ideal actuator, with explicit and
potentially enormous total resources and a direct proof for finite ensembles.

FINAL SCOPE

All counts and controller variables are resolved across n states; n can be
exponential in a many-body problem. Continuous control precision and the exact
multiplicative rate response remain explicit assumptions. This audit does not
establish robustness to a physically calibrated finite-precision apparatus,
independent novelty, efficient prediction/sampling, or a device advantage.

8. FINAL BERNSTEIN BURN-IN UPGRADE: C'S LONG-HORIZON NOTE

C's population_long_horizon_c.txt passes, including its use of tighter original
deterministic constants on a newly justified noise neighborhood. The choice
ru=min(1/2,(1/2)log(A/R0)), r<=ru/4 implies F(u*+y)<=R0 exp(ru)<A whenever
||y||<=4r; r<=r0/8 gives pi>=r0/2 there. Every segment to u* remains in this
neighborhood. There is no assumption that the noisy process preserves F<=A.
The h=cW r^2 basin, jump-size requirement M>=sqrt(2)/r, entropy Taylor remainder,
theta, b and stopped exponential calculation all pass by the same algebra as
Sections 3--4 above. Its (L2) is a valid slightly weaker combination of the two
terms in the principal's exit estimate.

The Bernstein replacement is exact. Each coordinate martingale has jumps
bounded by 1/M in absolute value. Its predictable quadratic variation is at
most Qbar*T0/M: total conditional event rate is <=M Qbar and each coordinate
jump has squared size <=1/M^2. The jump-compensator exponential and
exp(z)-1-z<=z^2/[2(1-|z|/3)] give the upper-tail maximal bound
 exp[-M a^2/(2(Qbar*T0+a/3))].
Apply it also to the negative martingale and union the two tails. Taking
a=zeta/sqrt(n), then unioning n coordinates, gives exactly
 2n exp[-M zeta^2/(2(n Qbar*T0+sqrt(n)zeta/3))].
No independence between coordinate martingales is required. Its displayed
Mburn therefore gives failure <=alpha/2.

The settling arithmetic also passes: deterministic combined error is at most
sqrt(2U0/cW)exp(-lambda T0/2)<=s0/2, and martingale sup norm <=zeta makes tracking
error at most exp(LT0)zeta=s0/2. Hence actual combined error <=s0 and W<=
CW*s0^2=h/4. Since s0=r sqrt(cW/(4CW))<=r/2, this endpoint lies in the valid
neighborhood independently of the W inequality; there is no endpoint bootstrap
gap. Global clamped drift is L-Lipschitz during burn-in because the integrator
cutoff cannot activate on ANY burn-in path: its radius exceeds the worst
possible travel k sqrt(n)T0/r0. It is also inactive throughout the stopped
maintenance basin. A scalar cutoff preserves the gauge and freezes the
continuous velocity at its outer boundary, so memory stays bounded forever.

T0, zeta, local radii and all amplitude bounds are independent of alpha and
the maintenance duration T. Combining Mburn proportional to log(4n/alpha) with
the maintenance threshold proves the asserted joint
M=O(log((1+T)/alpha)), with fixed model data and accuracy. The constants may be
extremely poor. This repairs the earlier inverse-confidence resource statement;
it does not change the established small-noise methodological comparator in
Section 6 or demonstrate an efficient physical implementation.

9. PRINCIPAL'S SOURCE-ONLY ACTUATOR OBSTRUCTION

Keep the same integral law but change the actuator to qij^u=qij exp(ui).
For the unit directed 3-cycle and rho=(1/3,1/3,1/3), u*=0 is an equilibrium.
With x=p-rho, z=diag(rho)u, its linearization on the normalized/gauge space is
 x'=(x+z)Q, z'=k x.
For each nonzero eigenvalue mu=-3/2 +/- i sqrt(3)/2 of Q, the corresponding
characteristic equation is lambda^2-mu lambda-k mu=0. Multiplying the conjugate
factors gives
 P_k(lambda)=lambda^4+3lambda^3+(3+3k)lambda^2+6k lambda+3k^2.
Its Routh first column is
 1, 3, k+3, 3k(6-k)/(k+3), 3k^2.
Therefore 0<k<6 is linearly asymptotically stable; k>6 has exactly two roots
in the open right half-plane. At k=6,
 P_6=(lambda^2+12)(lambda^2+3lambda+9),
so the critical pair is +/-2sqrt(3)i and the other pair has real part -3/2.
For a reversible Q the nonconstant modes have real mu=-a<0; then
lambda^2+a lambda+ak=0 has both roots in the left half-plane at every k>0.
Thus circulation really matters to this failed actuator relaxation. This is a
rigorous local-instability counterexample to the all-gain claim for the precise
source-only actuator with the unchanged integral law. It does not rule out
other controllers or claim a nonlinear limit cycle.
