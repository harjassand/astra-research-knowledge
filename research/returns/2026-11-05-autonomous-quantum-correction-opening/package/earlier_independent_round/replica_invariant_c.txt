Assume n>=2, strongly connected finite directed support, fixed unknown positive
q on that support, positive target rho and k>0. There are N labelled particles,
each with conditional rates q^u_ij=q_ij exp[(u_i-u_j)/2]. Shared controller:
  u'=(k/N)sum_a(e_(X_a)/rho_(X_a)-1), rho.u=0.
No actuator or memory cap is imposed in this exact identity.

Set F(u)=sum_(i,j)rho_i q^u_ij. Its gauge coercivity and strict convexity are
established in researcher_c.txt; the endpoint is the known DV/matrix-balancing
optimum. Define
  phi_N(u)=Z_N^(-1)exp[-2N F(u)/k],
  Psi_N(X,du)=(prod_a rho_(X_a))phi_N(u)du.
The density is normalizable, smooth and positive on the whole gauge space, with
all polynomial moments finite. Velocities are bounded, making the uncapped
process nonexplosive on each finite time interval.

For a configuration X, the transport contribution divided by Psi_N is
  -u'(X).grad log phi_N
    =2sum_a partial_(X_a)F/rho_(X_a),
since sum_i partial_i F=0. The forward jump divergence for replica a, divided
by Psi_N, is
  (rho Q^u)_(X_a)/rho_(X_a)
    =-2partial_(X_a)F/rho_(X_a).
The terms cancel. Integration by parts for compactly supported smooth tests,
normalizability, finite flux and the nonexplosive characteristic construction
make Psi_N an actual invariant law, not a formal ansatz.

Consequently at one stationary time, the labelled particles are EXACTLY iid
rho and are independent of u. This does not assert independence over time or
Markovian single-particle marginals after forgetting the controller/other paths.
Accessibility follows on the finite product graph as in researcher_c.txt:
it is strongly connected, and reachable configurations with all particles in
state i supply the original affine-spanning velocities. Positive dwell-time
maps give counting-times-Lebesgue irreducibility and uniqueness/ergodicity in
stationarity. AC initial memory laws inherit almost-sure ergodic conclusions;
no quantitative burn-in or universal deterministic-start theorem is imported.

The shared endpoint identity controls ONLY aggregate occupations:
  (1/T)integral_0^T P_i^N(t)dt-rho_i
    =rho_i[u_i(T)-u_i(0)]/(kT).
It cannot be assigned to the individual labelled-particle occupation errors.

Retain all replica/controller records. Relative to N base-Q particles with
the SAME memory rule and initial joint law, the stationary joint path KL per
particle per time is
  J_N=E_phiN sum_(i,j)rho_i
       [q^u_ij log(q^u_ij/q_ij)-q^u_ij+q_ij].
Gauge integration by parts gives
  E[(u-u*).grad F]=k(n-1)/(2N), E grad_H F=0.
Thus
  J_N=F(0)-E F+k(n-1)/(2N).
Convexity bounds 0<=E(F-F*)<=k(n-1)/(2N), and the established stationary
optimum is I_Q(rho)=F(0)-F*. Therefore
  0<=J_N-I_Q(rho)<=k(n-1)/(2N).

This is an exact lifted-product invariant / equipartition consequence in the
established factorized-ECMC class. It is not a new generic invariance principle,
