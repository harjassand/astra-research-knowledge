Short leases, one-shot execution and fixed-box Poisson witnesses prove sparse **service after eligibility**. Each repair earns that particular operation; none proves causal-frontier preservation throughout noisy reconstruction.

A recurring fault-tolerant schedule may have special frontiers, but this must be proved for the actual physical worldlines. Every wait must be covered by protected lower-level idle rectangles or charged to sparse enlarged damage. Reliable classical history cannot roll back an unknown quantum payload.

The decisive construction is a quantum strong simulation: preserve repaired NEXTOP/worldline tags, physical gate order and protected idles at each level, then prove a joint diamond-norm recursion such as `eta_(j+1)<=C eta_j^(1+delta)` for the **actual** macro-locations.
