# Autonomous quantum-memory threshold: construction analysis

**Status: The full theorem is not yet established.** There is a credible route through three-dimensional passive quantum memory that avoids the noisy classical-controller problem altogether, but I cannot currently prove that it survives the full class of arbitrary local GKLS perturbations required in the question.

An important alternative is the May 2026 construction by Balasubramanian, Davydova and Lin, *A Passive Self-Correcting Quantum Memory in Three Dimensions*\. It constructs geometrically local three-dimensional stabilizer Hamiltonians with exponentially long quantum-memory lifetimes under suitable thermal Lindblad dynamics. This is stronger than simply producing a fault-tolerant synchronous decoder. citeturn986505view0

However, its lifetime theorem relies on a Gibbs-stationary noise generator and a thermal encoded initialization. Neither a constant threshold against arbitrary local Hermiticity-preserving, trace-annihilating perturbations nor a polynomial-cost physical preparation of the required thermal encoded state follows from that theorem. citeturn500389view0turn382389view4

The distinction matters: **exponential memory lifetime under a specified thermal dynamics is not equivalent to exponential lifetime under every sufficiently small local Markovian perturbation.**

## 1\. A rigorous improvement: reference-entangled inputs

One part of the requested conclusion can be obtained without assuming independent Pauli errors.

**Lemma.** Let \(\Lambda\) be any quantum channel on one qubit, and suppose

\[
\sup_{\rho\in\mathcal D(\mathbb C^2)}
\|\Lambda(\rho)-\rho\|_1\leq u.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{\|\Lambda-\mathrm{id}\|_\diamond\leq 3u.}
\]

This uses the ordinary, unhalved diamond norm.

### Proof

Write \(\Delta=\Lambda-\mathrm{id}\)\. For every pure state \(|v\rangle\),

\[
\|\Delta(|v\rangle\langle v|)\|_1\leq u.
\]

In any orthonormal qubit basis \(\{|0\rangle,|1\rangle\}\), express the off-diagonal operator as

\[
|0\rangle\langle1|
=\frac12(X+iY).
\]

Each of \(X,Y\) is the difference of two orthogonal pure-state density operators. Therefore

\[
\|\Delta(|0\rangle\langle1|)\|_1\leq 2u.
\]

Now take an arbitrary logical qubit entangled with an arbitrary reference. Every pure joint state has Schmidt form

\[
|\psi\rangle=
\sqrt{\lambda}|0\rangle|r_0\rangle+
\sqrt{1-\lambda}|1\rangle|r_1\rangle.
\]

Expanding its density operator into two diagonal and two off-diagonal terms gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\|(\Delta\otimes\mathrm{id}_R)
(|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|)\|_1\\
&\qquad\leq
u+4u\sqrt{\lambda(1-\lambda)}
\leq3u.
\end{aligned}
\]

Convexity extends the result to mixed joint states, and the channel-discrimination characterization of the diamond norm completes the proof.

Consequently, whenever an ideal encoded-storage result establishes a uniform single-qubit trace-distance estimate \(u_L(t)\), it immediately gives

\[
\left\|
\mathcal D_L\circ e^{t\mathcal L_L}
\circ\mathcal E_L-\mathrm{id}
\right\|_\diamond
\leq3u_L(t),
\]

provided \(\mathcal D_L\circ\mathcal E_L=\mathrm{id}\)\.

For the thermal-encoding result above, the published dressed-observable bound has the form

\[
u_L(t)\leq
8\|\mathcal L_L\|_{1\to1}\,t\,
\operatorname{Tr}[(I-P_{\mathrm{stable}})\rho_\beta].
\]

Its Peierls estimate makes the final factor exponentially small in a power of system size. Thus the reference-entangled diamond-norm requirement follows for that narrower thermal model, up to a fixed factor of three. citeturn873549view2turn873549view1

This resolves the reference-entanglement conversion; it does **not** extend the result to arbitrary perturbations or certify the physical boundary procedures.

## 2\. A direct lemma for arbitrary local HP/TA perturbations

There is also a useful way to avoid assuming that perturbations are Pauli channels, or even that the individual perturbation terms are GKLS generators.

Let

\[
\mathcal K_L=\sum_{X\in\mathcal T_L}\mathcal K_X
\]

be the complete local physical generator, with every \(\mathcal K_X\) Hermiticity-preserving and trace-annihilating. Only the total generator needs to be GKLS.

Let \(\mathcal E_L,\mathcal D_L\) be ideal encoding and decoding channels satisfying \(\mathcal D_L\mathcal E_L=\mathrm{id}\)\. Define the dressed decoded observables

\[
O_j=\mathcal D_L^*(\sigma_j),
\qquad j=x,y,z.
\]

Suppose a projector \(P_L\) has the following *local logical-safety* property\:

\[
[O_j,A_X]P_L=0
\]

for every operator \(A_X\) supported on any permitted local generator support \(X\), and all three \(j\)\.

Write \(Q_L=I-P_L\), and define

\[
q_L(s)=\sup_{\rho\in\mathcal D(\mathbb C^2)}
\operatorname{Tr}\!\left[
Q_L e^{s\mathcal K_L}\mathcal E_L(\rho)
\right].
\]

**Proposition.** Under these assumptions,

\[
\boxed{
\left\|\mathcal D_L e^{t\mathcal K_L}\mathcal E_L
-\mathrm{id}\right\|_\diamond
\leq
3\sqrt3\,M_L\int_0^t q_L(s)\,ds
}
\]

where

\[
M_L=\sum_X\|\mathcal K_X^*\|_{\infty\to\infty}.
\]

### Proof of the key step

Every local linear superoperator has an operator-sum representation, not necessarily with positive coefficients\:

\[
\mathcal K_X(\rho)=\sum_{a,b}c_{ab}A_a\rho A_b^\dagger,
\]

where \(A_a,A_b\) are supported on \(X\)\. Trace annihilation implies

\[
\sum_{a,b}c_{ab}A_b^\dagger A_a=0.
\]

By the local logical-safety condition,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal K_X^*(O_j)P_L
&=\sum_{a,b}c_{ab}A_b^\dagger O_jA_aP_L\\
&=\sum_{a,b}c_{ab}A_b^\dagger A_aO_jP_L\\
&=0.
\end{aligned}
\]

Because \(\mathcal K_X\) preserves Hermiticity, \(\mathcal K_X^*(O_j)\) is Hermitian. Hence

\[
\mathcal K_X^*(O_j)
=Q_L\mathcal K_X^*(O_j)Q_L.
\]

Thus the rate of change of any dressed logical Pauli expectation is bounded by \(M_Lq_L(s)\)\. Integrating, bounding the three output Bloch components, and applying the previous diamond-norm lemma gives the proposition.

### Consequence

For a bounded-density lattice with bounded local generator strength, the local perturbation budget implies

\[
M_L\leq C_0|\Lambda_L|+|\Lambda_L|\epsilon
=O(L^D)
\]

for a device contained in a \(D\)\-dimensional box of side \(O(L)\)\.

Therefore, a robust confinement estimate of the form

\[
\sup_{0\leq s\leq e^{cL^a}}q_L(s)
\leq A e^{-\kappa L^a}
\]

would imply

\[
\|\mathcal D_L e^{t\mathcal K_L}\mathcal E_L
-\mathrm{id}\|_\diamond
\leq C_1L^D t e^{-\kappa L^a}.
\]

Choosing \(2c\leq\kappa\) gives the requested storage-error form, with a sufficiently small \(c\) also ensuring the desired time window.

**What this achieves\:** the logical-error conversion works for arbitrary local HP/TA terms, coherent faults, reference entanglement, and non-GKLS individual perturbations.

**What it does not achieve\:** the required uniform exponential bound on \(q_L(s)\)\. The thermal Gibbs Peierls estimate controls the equilibrium syndrome distribution; it does not prove this confinement estimate for the non-equilibrium states produced by arbitrary perturbations.

## 3\. A concrete autonomous local generator

The passive route can be made more explicit at the level of its nominal dynamics.

Take a geometrically local commuting Pauli Hamiltonian

\[
H_L=-\sum_{a\in\mathcal A_L}S_a,
\]

where every stabilizer has bounded support and each qubit participates in a bounded number of stabilizers.

For each qubit \(v\), one-qubit Pauli \(P\in\{X,Y,Z\}\), and local stabilizer-sign configuration \(s=(s_a)_{a\in A(v,P)}\), define

\[
\Pi_{v,P,s}=
\prod_{a\in A(v,P)}
\frac{I+s_aS_a}{2},
\]

where \(A(v,P)\) comprises the stabilizers anticommuting with \(P_v\)\. The energy change from applying \(P_v\) is

\[
\Delta E(s)=2\sum_{a\in A(v,P)}s_a.
\]

Fix an integer \(h\geq1\), and put

\[
r(\Delta)=\min\{1,2^{-h\Delta}\},
\qquad
J_{v,P,s}=\sqrt{r(\Delta E(s))}\,P_v\Pi_{v,P,s}.
\]

Then define the time-independent Lindbladian

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{th},L}(\rho)=
\sum_{v,P,s}
\left(
J_{v,P,s}\rho J_{v,P,s}^{\dagger}
-\frac12\{J_{v,P,s}^{\dagger}J_{v,P,s},\rho\}
\right).
}
\]

Every jump has fixed-radius support and norm at most one. The number of jumps per qubit is bounded independently of \(L\)\. Because \(\Delta E\) is even, the jump coefficients are rational powers of two, giving a finite rational description with constant coefficient precision.

The rate identity

\[
\frac{r(\Delta)}{r(-\Delta)}
=e^{-\beta\Delta},
\qquad \beta=h\log2,
\]

establishes detailed balance and hence stationarity of

\[
\rho_{\beta,L}
=\frac{e^{-\beta H_L}}{\operatorname{Tr}e^{-\beta H_L}}.
\]

This construction requires no runtime clock, message passing, counters, measurement records, or externally replenished ancillas. It has an \(O(n_L)\)\-term specification for \(n_L\) physical qubits.

**But this only constructs a local thermal dynamics.** Applying the published memory-lifetime proof still requires an appropriate code, a thermal encoded initial state, and its specified decoding observables. It does not establish robustness when arbitrary local perturbations break detailed balance.

### Paying for arbitrary perturbations

Let \(d_*=\max_X\dim\mathcal H_X\), where the maximum ranges over the fixed local support template. Add the actual, paid generator

\[
\gamma\sum_{X\in\mathcal T_L}(\mathcal T_X-\mathrm{id}),
\qquad
\mathcal T_X(\rho)=\frac{I_X}{d_X}\otimes\operatorname{Tr}_X\rho.
\]

For \(0<\epsilon_0\leq\gamma/d_*^2\), the accepted local channel-completion lemma rewrites each admissible perturbed term exactly as

\[
\gamma(\mathcal T_X-\mathrm{id})+\mathcal E_X
=\gamma(\Phi_X-\mathrm{id}),
\qquad
\Phi_X=\mathcal T_X+\frac{\mathcal E_X}{\gamma},
\]

where \(\Phi_X\) is CPTP.

This supplies independent Poisson **event times** at rate \(\gamma\) for the programmed supports, with arbitrary local CPTP payloads. It pays the depolarization and its nonzero rate, rather than treating them as free proof machinery.

The resulting physical generator is

\[
\mathcal K_L
=
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{th},L}
+\sum_X\gamma(\Phi_X-\mathrm{id}).
\]

It remains local, bounded-rate, finite-dimensional, and time-independent.

The obstruction to declaring a theorem is exact: even at \(\epsilon=0\), the added depolarization generally destroys Gibbs stationarity. The published thermal memory estimate therefore cannot simply be reused. One must prove long-time logical stability for the *new* driven semigroup, including the paid depolarization.

Independent Poisson locations alone do not prove that the resulting quantum errors are correctable over exponentially long storage times.

## 4\. Extending one-qubit protection to every fixed-radius perturbation

There is a further quantitative reduction that closes a potential loophole in Section 2: protection against individual Pauli jumps must extend to **every operator on a permitted bounded-size support**, including multi-qubit coherent errors.

Suppose \(P_{\mathrm{stable}}\) is a stabilizer-syndrome projector satisfying

\[
[O_j,\sigma_v]P_{\mathrm{stable}}=0
\]

for every single-qubit Pauli \(\sigma_v\) and logical dressed observable \(O_j\)\.

Let \(k_*\) be the largest number of qubits in any permitted perturbation support, fixed independently of system size. Define

\[
P_{\mathrm{safe}}^{(k_*)}
=
\prod_{e\in\mathcal P_{\leq k_*}}
e^\dagger P_{\mathrm{stable}}e,
\]

where \(\mathcal P_{\leq k_*}\) is the set of phase-free Pauli strings of weight at most \(k_*\), including the identity.

These projectors commute because they are all diagonal in the stabilizer-syndrome decomposition.

For any Pauli string \(e=\sigma_1\cdots\sigma_m\) with \(m\leq k_*\), use

\[
[O_j,e]
=
\sum_{\ell=1}^m
\sigma_1\cdots\sigma_{\ell-1}
[O_j,\sigma_\ell]
\sigma_{\ell+1}\cdots\sigma_m.
\]

Every suffix maps \(P_{\mathrm{safe}}^{(k_*)}\) into \(P_{\mathrm{stable}}\)\. Each term therefore vanishes on the safe subspace\:

\[
[O_j,e]P_{\mathrm{safe}}^{(k_*)}=0.
\]

By Pauli-basis linearity,

\[
\boxed{
[O_j,A_X]P_{\mathrm{safe}}^{(k_*)}=0
}
\]

for **every** operator \(A_X\) supported on at most \(k_*\) physical qubits.

### The thermal cost is only polynomial

Suppose every physical qubit belongs to at most \(d_0\) Hamiltonian stabilizers, and let

\[
q_\beta=
\operatorname{Tr}[(I-P_{\mathrm{stable}})\rho_\beta].
\]

A weight-\(m\) Pauli changes at most \(d_0m\) stabilizer energies, giving

\[
e\rho_\beta e^\dagger
\leq e^{2\beta d_0m}\rho_\beta.
\]

Taking a union bound over all phase-free Pauli strings of weight at most \(k_*\),

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Tr}[(I-P_{\mathrm{safe}}^{(k_*)})\rho_\beta]
\leq
q_\beta
\sum_{m=0}^{k_*}
\binom{n_L}{m}3^m e^{2\beta d_0m}.
}
\]

For fixed \(k_*\), the multiplying factor is \(O(n_L^{k_*})\)\. Hence a Gibbs bad-syndrome bound of the form \(q_\beta\leq e^{-\kappa n_L^\alpha}\) remains stretched-exponentially small.

This proves that enlarging the error alphabet from single-qubit Paulis to arbitrary fixed-support local operators does **not itself** destroy the thermal suppression exponent.

It does not, however, bound the non-equilibrium occupation of the unsafe subspace under the perturbed generator. That remains the decisive dynamical gap.

## 5\. Boundary maps, noise budgets, and the exact remaining theorem

For a stabilizer code, an ideal encoded thermal boundary can be specified as

\[
\mathcal E_{\beta,L}(\rho)
=
U_L\bigl(\rho\otimes\rho_{\beta,\mathrm{syn}}\bigr)U_L^\dagger,

where \(U_L\) identifies the logical qubit and syndrome subsystem. The corresponding ideal decoder traces out the syndrome subsystem after applying \(U_L^\dagger\)\.

A stabilizer description permits \(U_L\) to be specified by symplectic elimination using polynomially many classical operations and Clifford gates. A local implementation can be described using SWAP routing with polynomial overhead.

That is not yet a certified noisy physical boundary. In particular, polynomial-cost preparation of the requisite correlated thermal syndrome state is not established by the 3D self-correction paper. citeturn356318view3

\leq\eta_{\mathrm{enc}},

\|\widetilde{\mathcal D}_L-\mathcal D_L\|_\diamond

then channel contractivity gives the explicit budget

\boxed{
\delta_{\mathrm{boundary}}

uniformly for \(0\leq s\leq e^{cL^{D\alpha}}\), with sufficiently small fixed \(c>0\)\.

\left\|

## 6\. Final assessment

The most relevant recent asynchronous advance I found is Chakraborty and Lee, *Renormalization for Robust Memory without Synchronization*, submitted October 29, 2026. It proves stability of certain continuous-time classical memories in two and three dimensions without synchronized updates. That is relevant to autonomous record protection, but it does not automatically supply commuting quantum correction sectors or a threshold against arbitrary local quantum perturbations. citeturn367177view0

- A uniform single-qubit trace-distance error converts to an unhalved reference-entangled diamond error with factor three.
- Arbitrary bounded-support HP/TA generator terms admit a logical-error bound through occupation of an unsafe syndrome subspace, without assuming Pauli or independently distributed payloads.
- Single-qubit Pauli safety extends to every fixed-size local operator with only a polynomial loss in the thermal bad-syndrome probability.
- A rational, local, autonomous thermal Lindbladian and a paid depolarization-based Poisson representation can be specified explicitly.

**The requested full threshold remains unproved.** There is no established positive \(\epsilon_0\) for the complete construction, no proven uniform non-equilibrium confinement estimate, and no certified polynomial-cost noisy thermal encoded-input boundary. Therefore I cannot supply the requested constants \(a,c,C,p\) as unconditional guarantees for one uniform explicit family.

Nor have I established a model-wide impossibility theorem. The failure to close these steps is a limitation of the construction and present proof, not evidence that autonomous quantum memory under the stated model cannot exist.
