## Reversal, preparation and physical entropy

1. For arbitrary stationary hidden parity, the full stationary path KL is
   B+T sigma, with B=D(pi||piTheta). It is not T sigma unless B vanishes.
   The intrinsic rate contains escape-rate differences as well as jump
   logarithms. Stationary edge-flow entropy formulas from the all-even
   setting cannot be substituted silently.
2. B is distinguishability from the reversed preparation. In the new lift,
   a single orientation bit has unbounded B while its KL to an unbiased
   orientation stays below log 2. No unbounded work, stored-bit count or
   necessary external preparation time follows from B alone.
3. A fixed maximum microscopic escape clock does not bound hidden memory.
   The orientation construction keeps exactly 4n states and a common fixed
   clock while small switch rates, small stationary weights and the
   orientation relaxation time remain uncontrolled.
4. All results labeled entropy are path-reversal divergence results under
   the stated reversal contract. A heat or physical thermodynamic claim
   needs the actual reversal, relevant odd external fields, reservoirs and
   local detailed balance. No universal thermal realization of the abstract
   completions has been constructed.
5. The older generalized-equilibrium ambiguity keeps its parity-invariant
   equilibrium requirement. The new small-rate completion uses asymmetric
   stationary preparation and slow relaxation; it does not turn arbitrary
   irreversible data into an exact irreducible equilibrium law.

## Native record and exact matching

6. The native entropy floor assumes resolved singleton microscopic ports
   fixed under reversal. A return to a coarse symbol need not reset hidden
   orientation. The fourfold completion is inadmissible at the resolved
   singleton interface and therefore does not erase its J floor.
7. Histogram Erlang components overlap. Their microscopic component KL is
   generally larger than their observed duration KL. Exact common-mixture
   representation alone does not prove exact entropy attainment.
8. Narrowing Erlangs at fixed bin centers does not prove L1 convergence.
   The root construction refines log bins faster than kernel narrowing and
   proves convergence in L1 weighted by 1+t, preserving the total mean.
9. Exact finite attainment for phase-type records is iff every directional
   ratio is C exp(delta t). With equal reversed escape clocks, delta=0.
   The reverse PH occupation vector is beta_minus(-T_minus)^-1, generally
   not the tempting z*h/C formula.
10. The exact-record infimum when this attainment criterion fails remains
    unclassified in general. The one-phase/two-phase example has infinite
    exact finite cost but finite closure floor. Exact short-time zeros are
    additional structural information, not a conclusion from finite samples.
11. Native flux E, entropy per departure E/R, and per-time sigma=E/W are
    distinct normalizations. R>0 is assumed for departure/cycle quantities;
    an event-free singleton is handled separately.

## Equilibrium compatibility and inference

12. A common CP factorization of every reflected-history table is not a
    complete equilibrium-realization condition. Current Astra N507 supplies
    the stronger counterexample; its completed internal evidence is retained
    at its source-reported scope without an external-validation upgrade.
13. The metric-tree theorem describes rational-cylinder CLOSURE of finite
    reversible CTMC outputs. Positive observed continuity additionally gives
    an exact standard-Borel reversible strongly continuous L2 semigroup,
    not necessarily a finite model, finite activity or regular hidden paths.
14. Separate constant-control continuity does not imply common controlled
    continuity. The exact four-state insertion gap 1/64 is preserved. No
    passive regularity corollary has been silently promoted to all controls.
15. The tree hierarchy is complete for finite separation and strict positive
    robust entropy detection, not a tight numerical entropy optimization or
    an efficient acquisition algorithm. Its tables may be very large.
16. In the projected KL relaxation, all OTHER path tables as well as tree
    extension tables must remain free. Fixing them to the target can exclude
    the arithmetic reversibilization and invalidate the bound.
17. Joint minimization over data and model laws remains convex only for a
    convex data-confidence region. A plug-in empirical KL is not a certified
    population bound. One valid fixed-grid region can cover all hierarchy
    levels, while grid selection and repeated data updates need coverage.
18. Eventual detection requires a dovetailed schedule that revisits every
    valid witness with shrinking error, not one test per witness. An explicit
    summable schedule is in COMPLETE_EQUILIBRIUM_INFORMATION.md.
19. The stronger linear entropy transfer needs an algebraically reversal-even
    statistic. It does not require finite data to establish reversibility of
    the entire target. Target symmetry is used for a completeness argument.
20. A numerical Dobrushin contraction at a specified time for the forward
    or physically reversed HIDDEN dynamics is sufficient for the recovery
    lemma. Finite irreducibility, visible correlation decay and a rate ceiling
    do not acquire a uniform contraction premise.
