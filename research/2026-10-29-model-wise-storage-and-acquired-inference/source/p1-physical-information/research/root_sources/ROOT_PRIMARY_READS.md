literature search. Detailed source comparisons and exact Astra payloads

- Kim, Lee and Jeong (2022), Physical Review Research 4, 023051;
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.04681. Root read the primary paper and its
  odd-jump ring/likelihood section; the complementary review read the
  boundary estimator and rate definitions in detail. Known ring/sign and
  stationary parity-boundary ingredients are explicitly credited.
- Ehrich (2021), Journal of Statistical Mechanics 083214;
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.08803. Root inspected the known-state-count
  hypothesis and the question about adding hidden states/cycles. The new
  arbitrary-parity approximate-record result does not resolve his exact
  all-even fitting problem.
- Degunther, van der Meer and Seifert (2024), Physical Review Research 6,
  023175; https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.07665. Root inspected the Markovian
  event framework and the physical versus parity-reversed endpoint
  boundary in Section IV. The full path KL boundary is kept separate
  from the intrinsic additive rate.
- Nitzan, Ghosal and Bisker (2023), Physical Review Research 5, 043251;
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.01783. Root inspected the known-topology
  optimization scope and the stated limitation on simpler hidden models.
- Wolfer and Watanabe (2021), Information Geometry 4, 393–433;
  https://doi.org/10.1007/s41884-021-00061-7. The primary paper and
  complementary Theorem 7 reading identify additive reversibilization as
  an existing information projection, not a new algorithmic ingredient.
- Navascues and Wolfe, complete inflation hierarchy;
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06476 and DOI 10.1515/jci-2018-0008.
  Root opened the primary version history/abstract; the complementary
  review read the theorem and tensor constraints. Complete LP compatibility
  with unrestricted latent cardinality is established antecedent machinery.

  https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/Papers/me22.pdf. The primary author's
  indexed theorem/remarks were retrieved. Full PDF fetching timed out;
  no full-PDF acquisition is claimed. The uncolored tree representation
  is classical; the root direct compact-bush proof independently supplies
  the representation used here.
- Schwarz, Kolomeisky and Godec, Consistent time reversal and reliable and
  accurate inference in the presence of memory;
  https://journals.aps.org/prl/accepted/10.1103/gwf7-6kvr and
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.11819. Root verified the primary publisher's
  acceptance notice dated 25 September 2026 and abstract. The complementary
  review read the ordinary-reversal and specified-memory assumptions in
  the manuscript/supplement. This is a current strong conventional
  memory-aware comparator, not a uniform guarantee of hidden mixing or
  parity preparation from finite coarse samples.
