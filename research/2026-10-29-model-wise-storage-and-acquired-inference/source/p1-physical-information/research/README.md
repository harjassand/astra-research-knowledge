## Scientific destination and chosen attack

conclusion across admissible hidden explanations, and which preparation
or dynamical information changes that answer. Entropy is the constructed
case here; a general solution for every physical claim is not established.

An accessible-response route was tested first. Conservative perturbations
that only change departure clocks preserve hidden reversal ambiguity even
under time-dependent feedback protocols. An equilibrium-looking response
identity can coexist with a dissipative all-even source. This failure
motivated a full compatible-explanation theory rather than another

| Information and physical class | Established consequence |

These are different interfaces, not contradictory estimates of one model.

## 1. The strongest obstruction: a persistent hidden orientation

Read **ORIENTATION_MEMORY_AND_SUSTAINED_ENTROPY.md**.

For any irreducible n-state source Q with stationary pi, make two hidden
orientations running Q and its stationary adjoint Q*. Physical reversal
swaps the orientations while preserving each visible configuration. The
stationary orientation weights are (1-epsilon,epsilon), and switches have
rates kappa epsilon and kappa(1-epsilon). The sustained rate and boundary
are exactly

    sigma_2n=kappa(1-2epsilon)^2,
    B=(1-2epsilon)log[(1-epsilon)/epsilon].

The full visible path law on [0,T] differs from Q's by at most

    epsilon+(1-epsilon)(1-exp(-kappa epsilon T))

in total variation. Duplicating every state with an invisible bit makes
EVERY microscopic escape rate equal the same fixed Lambda. This 4n-state
model preserves the preceding visible path law exactly and still has
sigma tending to zero. Neither state count nor an upper clock bound
repairs the missing information.

The uncontrolled resources are increasingly small switching rates and
stationary weights, slow orientation relaxation, and unbounded divergence
from the reversed preparation. The orientation is still one bit. Its KL
to an unbiased orientation is at most log 2, so B is not a memory-size or
automatic work-cost claim.

The full stationary path KL is B+T sigma. A supplied B bound, or a supplied
uniform contraction bound for the forward or physically reversed hidden
dynamics, restores an observable time-arrow rate bound. Ordinary visible
correlations do not acquire either premise. The recovery lemma follows
the failure through to a precise sufficient memory condition.

## 2. Resolved native events have a matching entropy converse

Read **ENTROPY_COMPLETION_CONVERSE.md** and
**entropy_completion_review/EXACT_ATTAINMENT_CLASSIFICATION.md**.

For fixed even singleton ports, let K_ij(t) be positive-duration excursion
flux densities and d_ij separate direct fluxes. Their reversal KL is

    J=sum_ij integral K_ij log(K_ij/K_ji)
                              +sum_i!=j d_ij log(d_ij/d_ji).

Among finite CTMC sequences with arbitrary hidden parity fixing the ports,
J is exactly the infimum of limiting intrinsic entropy under weighted-L1
convergence of K. The construction preserves port weights, all integrated
endpoint fluxes, direct fluxes, native port clocks and total hidden mean
exactly. Paired hidden escape rates can be equal. Hidden dimension and
rates may grow; coefficient/instrument precision is not free.

For a finite-source phase-type record, exact finite attainment E=J occurs
if and only if every nonzero directional duration ratio is C_ij exp(delta_ij t).
Equality in KL data processing gives necessity; phase-type reversal and
exponential h-transforms give sufficiency. Under equal reversed escape
clocks the ratios must be constant in time. This is an attainment
criterion, not the exact-record infimum when attainment fails.

The sharp failure is K_12=e^-t and K_21=t e^-t. Observable J=1, but every
exact finite completion fixing the ports has infinite reversal divergence:
a one-phase forward route forces a one-phase reverse route. Finite
approximants preserve the total mean and have E tending to 1. The infinity
is ideal path-support failure, not infinite laboratory heat. Exact short-
time zeros are extra information not established by finite samples.

The rational approximation control reaches 1,986,690 hidden states and
phase rate about 127.657 at its largest checked parameter. Its E lies in
(0.976018403402799998,0.976018403402799999), with total weighted-L1 record
error below 0.0369256608. Those execution costs are retained. Direct
evaluation of the supplied source already gives J=1; no speedup is claimed.
