# Constant-control continuity does not determine continuity of a common hidden realization

29 October 2026. New scoped obstruction discovered while checking the metric-tree proof's extension to multiple controls.

The passive proof has one semigroup. Its zero-time spectral projection fixes the sensor by observed stochastic continuity; its range is an algebra containing every branch feature, so it is the identity on the canonical state space.

For several actions, a projection for action a need not preserve features created by action b. The following exact four-state example shows why continuity of every constant-control observed law is insufficient.

## Exact model

Use four abstract states, uniform stationary distribution, and the deterministic labels

    g = (0,0,1,1).

Let P be conditional averaging inside the two visible cells:

    P = [[1/2,1/2,0,0],
         [1/2,1/2,0,0],
         [0,0,1/2,1/2],
         [0,0,1/2,1/2]].

Action a has K_a(0)=I and K_a(t)=P for all t>0. This is a stochastic selfadjoint semigroup, but it is not strongly continuous at zero. Since P leaves every label unchanged, the observed law under constant action a is a static random binary label and is stochastically continuous.

Action b is an ordinary symmetric CTMC: state 1 exchanges with state 3 at rate log(2)/2, and state 2 exchanges with state 4 at rate log(2). The two channels do not communicate. Its unit-time transition matrix is

    B = [[3/4,0,1/4,0],
         [0,5/8,0,3/8],
         [1/4,0,3/4,0],
         [0,3/8,0,5/8]].

Every b-only observed finite-dimensional law is generated by this ordinary finite CTMC, so it is stochastically continuous. Both action kernels have the same invariant uniform law and are individually reversible.

## An intervention of arbitrarily short positive duration has a fixed consequence

Let f=(1,1,-1,-1), a bounded function of the observed label. The correlation under b for two time units is

    <f,B^2 f>_pi = ( (1/2)^2 + (1/4)^2 )/2 = 5/32.

For b for one unit, then a for any epsilon>0, then b for one unit,

    <f,B P B f>_pi = ((1/2+1/4)/2)^2 = 9/64.

The difference is exactly 1/64, independent of epsilon. At epsilon=0 the inserted operator is I and the first correlation is recovered.

Any common strongly continuous hidden Markov realization with a fixed memoryless observation has continuous bounded-word expectations when an action duration decreases to zero: strong continuity of that action acts between two fixed bounded transition/observation products. Such a realization cannot reproduce this family of protocols.

This is not an obstruction to a discrete action or instantaneous intervention model. It excludes the stronger interpretation of every action as a finite-duration strongly continuous hidden evolution.

## Yet it belongs to finite-CTMC common-control closure

For M>0, replace action a by

    Q_a^M = M(P-I).

This is a finite symmetric CTMC generator, with escape rate M/2. Its semigroup is exactly

    exp(t Q_a^M) = P + exp(-Mt)(I-P).

As M tends to infinity it approaches K_a(t) for each fixed t>0. Keep Q_b unchanged. Every approximating family has one common uniform stationary law, individually reversible generators, and the same sensor.

Finite products therefore converge for every fixed protocol with positive action durations. In the displayed protocol the correlation is

    9/64 + exp(-M epsilon)/64.

Achieving a small error uniformly down to epsilon=0 would require an unbounded rate. This is the resource hidden by closure; it is absent from the exact strong-continuity claim.

## Consequence for the controlled metric-tree theorem

The common-control finite-CTMC closure construction by separate Poissonization and one common finite partition remains valid. The passive exact-semigroup regularity corollary cannot be imported using only constant-control observed continuity.

A condition governing insertion of vanishing-duration controls into the relevant contexts is necessary. Whether ordinary controlled path-word insertion continuity alone suffices for an exact common strongly continuous realization, or whether branch-level compatibility is needed, is not proved here. Merely adding a continuity phrase without stating its observation interface would leave that gap unresolved.

The model is an exact abstract example. It is not a claim about a native physical system, a supplied experimental pulse, or the thermodynamic cost of switching.
