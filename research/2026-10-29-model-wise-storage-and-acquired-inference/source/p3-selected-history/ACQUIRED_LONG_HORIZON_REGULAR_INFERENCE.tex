\section{Destination and barrier removed}
The destination is exact inference from an ordinary computation whose hidden
trajectory is neither inverted nor unfolded. The needed object is an acquired
requested law and constructive original-input fibers. A small decoder or large
count alone is insufficient. The earlier graph route was challenged by its
all-order width obstruction even for cheap ordinary Gaussian inversion. This
route instead discovers the stochastic information the query uses.

The acquired quadratic channel escaped affine-uniform laws but its completed
history operation initially admitted only prescribed words. Here temporal
evidence is supplied as an explicit query automaton and binary horizon. It
compiles into a positive integer arithmetic DAG. Certified random-bit decisions
draw short exact conditional observations without materializing that DAG's
possibly enormous integer value. The hidden source channel, unlike the query
automaton, is recovered from actual gates.

Weighted automata and certified random generation have established antecedents.
The result is the integrated acquired-source capability and its exact operation
boundaries, not a priority claim for those general mechanisms or an algorithm
acquiring positive count DAGs for arbitrary circuit predicates.

\section{An explicit positive temporal count}
Let $D=(Q,\delta,q_0,F)$ be the supplied deterministic binary event automaton,
with $d=|Q|$ states. It reads future observations $O(X_1),\ldots,O(X_H)$,
not the initial observation. Define
\[
 M_{ij}=\sum_{b:\delta(i,b)=j}N_b,\qquad
                       Z_H=e_{q_0}^TM^H\mathbf1_F.              \tag{2}
\]
\begin{theorem}
For every actual hidden initial state, $Z_H$ is the exact number of accepted
original noise tapes and $Z_H/2^{pH}$ is their probability. Binary powering
constructs an explicit nonnegative integer $+,\times$ DAG for $Z_H$ of size
$O(d^3\log(H+1))$, with constants of at most $p+1$ bits.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each future word $b_1\cdots b_H$, the causal local bijections give exactly
$\prod_tN_{b_t}$ original tapes, independently of the actual initial state.
Determinism gives one query path per word, so the matrix product sums disjoint
original populations exactly once. Repeated squaring stores shared powers.
\end{proof}
The DAG is an exact compact count expression, not an expanded integer output
of the same cost. Zero propagation in a positive DAG decides emptiness exactly.
Ambiguous nondeterministic automata could count runs rather than words and are
not accepted. No free polynomial determinization is assumed.

\section{Exact choices without materializing enormous weights}
Consider an actual nonnegative integer circuit of size $s$ with binary
constants of maximum length $B$. Zero nodes are recognized structurally.
For positive nodes define
\[
 E_{\rm leaf}=1,\quad E_{a+b}=1+\max(E_a,E_b),\quad
 E_{ab}=1+E_a+E_b.
\]
These budgets have $O(s)$ bits. Value bit lengths are bounded by
$O((B+1)2^s)$, so normalized binary exponents have $O(s+\log(B+1))$ bits.

The implementation rounds significant bits outwards with unbounded binary
exponents. For addition it rounds both operands onto a common leading grid,
then rounds the sum; large exponent gaps are never expanded into zero strings.
Multiplication uses bounded significands and added exponents. A deliberately
loose one-operation relative error is $\eta=16\,2^{-P}$ for precision $P\ge6$.
Induction gives certified intervals
\[
       (1-\eta)^{E_w}w\le L_w\le w\le U_w\le(1+\eta)^{E_w}w.    \tag{3}
\]
Addition uses the larger incoming error budget, multiplication their sum.

\begin{lemma}
For two positive roots $A,B$, an exact fair-bit algorithm chooses zero with
probability $A/(A+B)$ in expected time polynomial in the circuit size and
literal constant lengths. It does not expand $A,B$. Worst-case time is
unbounded. A zero weight is handled exactly; two zero weights give no law.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Refine one common uniform bitstream. A $k$-bit prefix $r$ describes the cell
$[r/2^k,(r+1)/2^k)$. Evaluate (3) with
\[
 P=k+\operatorname{bitlength}(\max(E_A,E_B))+10.
\]
Here $\gamma=E\eta<2^{-k-6}$ and the chosen $P$ is at least eleven.
Bernoulli's inequality and $(1+\eta)^E\le e^\gamma\le1+2\gamma$
put every weight interval between $(1-\gamma)w$ and $(1+2\gamma)w$.
The true threshold is enclosed by
$[L_A/(L_A+U_B),U_A/(U_A+L_B)]$ with width less than $2^{-k}$;
indeed its width is at most $3\gamma/(2+\gamma)$, with the worst bound
attained for equal positive weights.
Return zero only if
\[
                   (r+1)U_B\le(2^k-r-1)L_A,
\]
and one only if
\[
                         rL_B\ge(2^k-r)U_A.
\]
These place the entire uniform cell on the certified side of the exact
threshold. They use exact comparisons of bounded-significand dyadics, with
polynomial-length binary exponents, not probability division.
Otherwise read another fair bit and increase precision. Unresolved cells
cover measure at most $3\,2^{-k}$, giving almost-sure termination and bounded
polynomial moments of the required precision. Polynomial work at each
precision gives expected polynomial total work. The output agrees almost
surely with one exact uniform-real comparison, establishing its exact law.
\end{proof}

Repeated squaring can make $E$ exponentially large, but only its binary length
enters precision. A finite user cap must return unresolved, never an
approximate choice. The lemma does not give a cheap worst-case exact comparison
of two nearly equal weights. Subtraction and cancellation invalidate the
structural zero and relative-error arguments.

\section{Short observations versus full original histories}
For requested positions $t_1<\cdots<t_k$, replace the relevant transfer factors
by one-label matrices and use powers of $M$ on the intervening gaps.
For each successive requested label, the two positive roots count disjoint
original populations summing to the current population. Apply the exact
choice lemma and continue.

\begin{theorem}
For $Z_H>0$, any $k$ specified future observation positions can be sampled
jointly from their exact conditional marginal in expected time polynomial
in $d,p,k,\log(H+1)$ and the actual source acquisition parameters. No hidden
initial-state reconstruction or actual time update is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Sequential ratios of genuine branch populations give the exact marginal.
A conservative bound for their shared arithmetic construction is
$O((k+1)^2d^3(\log(H+1)+1))$ gates; cached powers can cost less.
The lemma gives expected polynomial bit work. Equation (1) makes the same
weights valid for every microscopic initial state.
\end{proof}
The emitted labels are generally not uniform over feasible label assignments:
they are the marginal of uniform accepted original histories.

To emit a full original tape at actual state $x$ and query state $q$, with
$h$ steps remaining, choose $b$ with weight
$N_b(M^{h-1}\mathbf1_F)_{\delta(q,b)}$ and draw an independent uniform local
ticket in its $N_b$-element fiber. Decode original noise at $x$, execute the
literal transition, and repeat. Probabilities telescope to $1/Z_H$ for each
accepted original tape. A witness instead chooses any nonzero branch and a
deterministic valid local ticket.

This operation executes $H$ transitions and emits $H$ original noise words.
Even for zero noise bits the actual API emits an $H$-entry tape and performs
the updates. Its explicit output guard prevents accidental huge unfolding.
The wrapper exposes no new full-history rank/unrank. Sampling a distant
\emph{original noise word} is also not supplied: its local normalization needs
the actual hidden state reached by the omitted prefix. It is a different task
from sampling the corresponding observation bit.
