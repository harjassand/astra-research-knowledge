Universal counting:
exact constructions and remaining obstructions
Astra P3
27 October 2026
Abstract
The target is exact counting for arbitrary Boolean circuits in time polynomial in their
description length. This target is not achieved here. We construct a polynomial-size circuit-
to-cubic compiler without expanding the output polynomial. Constant nonsingular Gaussian
fibers and a two-value alphabet can retain the entire counting problem. A sharper affine
matrix-pencil construction locates the same problem one nilpotency level beyond a verified
polynomial case: with constant sources, an affine pencil with A3 =0 reduces to a quadratic
count,whereasA4 =0alreadyrepresentseveryBooleancubicgap. Conditions,normalization
factors, original input counts and arithmetic costs are explicit. Additional attacks examine
recursive replacement summaries and finite-field packing. Positive repairs acquire cubic
and quartic etale norm geometry from raw coefficients, integrate coupled Gaussian radicals,
and count verified field-linear programs at unbounded nilpotency depth. A Hessian Pfaffian
manufacturesnormcoefficientserasedbytheabsolutetrace,withafixed-degreereconstruction
principle beyond quartics. None of the architecture-specific obstructions is a lower bound for
arbitrary counting algorithms. The previous acquired chain-algebra theorem and its source
files are preserved.
