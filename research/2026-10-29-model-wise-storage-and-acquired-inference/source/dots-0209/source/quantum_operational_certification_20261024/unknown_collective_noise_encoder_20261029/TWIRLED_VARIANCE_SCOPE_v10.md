# Review clarification for v9
2026-10-29. Originals remain frozen.

The reference-complete Pauli-channel equality in v9 uses the logical-output CPTP decoder with a fixed logical-state replacement on leakage. The author numerical program calculates projected code Bell overlap (equivalently exact overlap for an orthogonal-erasure completion). Its reported Bell loss is therefore a conservative upper bound on the reset-completed decoder's Bell loss; it is not asserted to be exactly the latter. A retained erasure output requires an explicitly extended twirling representation and a separate covariant-erasure argument before importing a square-output Pauli-channel formula. No such extension is needed or claimed for v9's stated reset-completed service.

The local short SU(2) logarithms are chosen in a fixed comparable global gauge and up to physically irrelevant local signs. Subtracting their arithmetic mean in the exact singlet covariance identity is NOT an optimization over all noncommuting gauge transformations. Compatible common logarithms give zero variance for a common collective rotation. No gauge-invariant minimization claim is made.
