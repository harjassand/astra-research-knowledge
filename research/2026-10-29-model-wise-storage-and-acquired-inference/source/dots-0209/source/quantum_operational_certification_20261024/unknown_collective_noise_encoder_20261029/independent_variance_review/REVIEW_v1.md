Bell input provides a half-trace-distance lower bound 1-Fe, and convexity over nonidentity Pauli branches gives the matching half-diamond upper bound. Thus the equality is rigorous for the averaged channel after key retirement; it is not an assertion that an untwirled channel has diamond error equal to its Bell loss.
