# v3 boundary correction and remaining conditioning failure

2026-10-29. Independent exact verification accepts both frozen v3 boundary certificates in `boundary_certificates_v3.json`; hashes and exact gaps are recorded by `check_boundary_v3.json`. The unchanged dual kernel is used. An exactly feasible center has objective zero; for PSD underloaded seen centers, adding a PSD residual to the default block with dual zero supplies a cheap candidate whose gap remains checked at finite K. The latter positive gap may underflow to displayed float zero; exact rational gap is authoritative.

A separate v2 public-interface test exposes a broader remaining limitation, also inherited by v3: for the previously used overloaded centers, K=2^20,2^30,2^39 all fail closed after the 30,000-iteration Dykstra cap with gaps approximately .5821,.6425,.6426 at requested xi=1e-12. K=2^10 passes at 12,388 iterations. The `K>10^12` routing cutoff bypasses this ill-conditioned regime only at still larger K. Inputs, reproducible program and outcomes are retained in `check_moderate_K_v2.py/.json`. This failure does not affect the accepted recorded banks (K<=16), nor invalidate accepted exact certificates. It rules out describing v3 as a generally reliable finite-call oracle across compressed multiplicities. A direct finite-weight dual mechanism, rather than tolerance relaxation, is a plausible next construction; it has not been established by this review.
