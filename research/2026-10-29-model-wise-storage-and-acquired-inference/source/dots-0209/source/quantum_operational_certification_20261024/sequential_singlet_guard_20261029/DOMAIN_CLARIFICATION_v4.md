# Finite-code domain inherited by the sequential theorem
2026-10-29. Review clarification; governing v1 unchanged.

The sequential guard uses even n≥4 and K=2^k≥2, as inherited from the weighted witness and nontrivial logical-code construction. Its stated positive outer correction regime additionally requires integers t≥1 and4t<J≤K; if these inequalities have no solution for a selected small n, increase the inner dimension or do not claim that outer regime. GF(1) is never used. The t=0 special case may be interpreted with a trivial outer identity code, but is not the nontrivial fault-correcting capability.

The n=4 memory/count fixture intentionally tests the Pauli-instrument and stopping argument separately from the q=8 [[5,1,3]] outer fixture. It is not an impossible claim that the n=4 singlet logical dimension2 supports a length5 GRS code over GF(2). The separate mathematical fixtures and their exact scope are labeled in their data.
