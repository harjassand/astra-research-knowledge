## Governing construction
`GOVERNING_v1.md` combines the reviewed weighted local-symmetry witness, actual private logical Pauli pads, arbitrary retained-memory CP instruments, a finite negative-binomial fault guard, and an explicit total CSS/GRS outer decoder. It proves a SUBNORMALIZED accepted-operation error bound. It does not guarantee acceptance against arbitrary noise or a small normalized error on a rare accepted run.

The new all-history soundness argument does not assume a collective deployment algebra, iid source, reset, or memoryless source. It DOES assume a real common source port with private current settings, exact private-pad boundaries, accurate trusted controls, and isolated endpoint holding buffers. The older fixed-joint-algebra theorem remains useful for horizon-uniform passive protection and yield, not a soundness premise here.
