# Exact two-stream proof of the keyed negative-binomial bound
2026-10-29. Clarification of NEGATIVE_BINOMIAL_FAULT_TAIL_v1.md, preserving its frozen text. This is scoped to the exact direct-pad comb.

Let p=1-q. Pre-sample two independent infinite iid Bernoulli(p) streams U_1,U_2,... and V_1,V_2,..., also independent of all source/instrument sampling randomness. Success denotes probe. At physical round t, conditioned on all previously consumed coins and latent instrument outcomes, use the exact common instrument to sample the next physical label and Kraus branch before assigning the role. Compute opportunityO_t=1[v_t notin S_t]. IfO_t=1 consume the next unused U coin; otherwise consume the next unused V coin. Use that coin as the actual role.

At every finite t, the next unused coin of either stream is independent Bernoulli(p), independent of current instrument output and previous consumed data. This follows by induction: every earlier stream-consumption decision was a function only of already consumed coins and separately sampled instrument outcomes, never any unused coin. Thus the conditional joint law of(current instrument branch,current role) is exactly the original law with an independent Bernoulli role. The construction therefore reproduces every finite-dimensional distribution of the actual padded adaptive protocol, including stopping rules based on services, probes or a call cap. This is a coupling, not conditioning on the eventual existence of another opportunity.

The actual sequence of opportunity-role coins is a prefix U_1,...,U_K, where K may be finite, random, or infinite. Each successful U coin in the consumed prefix is an actual probe innovation and increases dimS_t by1. There are at most m*=dimS* such successes. If m* are reached, S_t=S*, so the common support promise makes every subsequent opportunity impossible. Therefore the consumed prefix ends at or before the m*th success in U. If it ends earlier, or the physical procedure has arbitrarily long intervals with no further opportunities, inspect the unused tail of U only mathematically. The complete U stream reaches its m*th success almost surely because p>0. LetF_U be the number of U failures preceding that success. Every actual opportunity-service is one such failure in the consumed prefix, and each actual service fault requires an opportunity-service. Hence pathwise

  actual service fault count <= F_U,

and F_U has NB(m*,p) law. Replacingm* by the knownm>=m* gives an additional pathwise domination by using the same U stream and waiting for its mth success. For m*=0 there are no opportunities at all. Nonopportunity role coins V are included specifically to preserve the original physical-time role law and its finite acquisition cap. No iid assumption is made about labels, source memory, inter-opportunity delays or source interactions.

If the source could inspect currentrole before choosing its instrument, the pre-role instrument sampling identity would be false. If keys leak to the source later, the key-retired comb can fail and so can this coupling. These are interface restrictions, not exceptions concealed by optional stopping.
