## 1. Weighted rectangular representation: proof checked
Let W be the singlet sector, P its projector, and d0 its dimension. On Hom(W,H), R(pi)X=U_pi X(U_pi|W)^dagger is a unitary representation in Hilbert–Schmidt norm. Thus the same complete-transposition gap n from v1 applies; there is no dimension-weighted or nonunitary inner-product step to justify. Averaging K_a|W yields G(K_a)|W.

By Schur–Weyl decomposition, the singlet partition (n/2,n/2) appears in H with SU(2) multiplicity one. Consequently its invariant intertwiner space Hom_{S_n}(W,H) is one-dimensional, generated by inclusion, so the average is alpha_a inclusion. Other spin sectors carry inequivalent permutation irreducibles and contribute no invariant intertwiners. This is the load-bearing fact; it would fail for a generic domain with larger equivalent-irrep multiplicity.

For a Hermitian unitary transposition S commuting with P, expand the squared commutator and use sum_a K_a^dagger K_a=I:

    sum_a ||(S K_a-K_a S)P||_F^2
       = 2 d0 - 2 Re Tr[P S N^dagger(S)]
       = 2 d0 delta^P_S.

The trace is real because PS and N^dagger(S) are Hermitian. No unitality assumption occurs. The gap therefore gives sum_a ||(K_a-GK_a)P||_F^2 <= (d0/n) sum delta^P. Restricting the domain to E can only decrease the unnormalized squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm. Dividing by K, then using v1's correct orthogonal-Bell-component argument, gives

    1-Fe <= (d0/(K n)) sum delta^P = (d0/K)(n-1) q_P.

The completed decoder and retired-key Pauli equality are inherited from the already-reviewed v1 proof. The scalar is not incorrectly identified with the trace on a proper subcode. For the largest power-of-two K<=d0, 1<=d0/K<2. Thus C_P<2(n-1), with nontrivial code for even n>=4. The n=4 endpoint works: d0=K=2 and both signed branch ranks are one.

Independent explicit rectangular Laplacian matrices have one-dimensional kernel and gaps 4 and 6 at n=4,6. The analytic argument establishes general n; these matrices are only checks. Independent arbitrary nonunital channels and near-identity mixtures satisfy the commutator identity, Poincare inequality, completed-decoder Bell bound, and actual branch transition formula.

## 2. Full singlet ensemble and the rejected input shortcut
Uniform permutation of any fixed singlet-pair product has density average P/d0 by irreducibility. Classical random permutation/matching generation implements this mixture without coherent group summation or n!-size execution. This assertion is exact at ideal gate/randomness precision, not an assumption of access to a thermal state.

V3's initial binary SWAP instrument must be Lüders: its signed subnormalized states are Pi_s P/d0. A fully resolving pair Bell measurement changes the triplet correlations with spectators, so v2's physical-input shortcut cannot be reused here. Author numerical leakage checks corroborate this rejected route. This rejection is correctly preserved rather than being silently removed.

## 3. Direct signed branches: proof and interface checked
For n=2r, the pair-singlet branch has rank C_{r-1}; the total sector has rank C_r. Their ratio is

    p_minus=(n+2)/(4(n-1)), p_plus=3(n-2)/(4(n-1)).

These are known rational probabilities. They are neither free parameters nor the 1/4,3/4 signs of the fully mixed physical state. Uniform integer sampling/rejection can implement them exactly in the mathematical random-bit model; finite randomness bias must otherwise be budgeted.

Minus branch: pair singlet times a randomized spectator singlet matching averages to Pi_minus P/(p_minus d0).

Plus branch: the fixed four-qubit Omega1 is the unique spin1-pair/spin1-spectator total singlet. Multiplying by additional spectator singlets gives a fixed spectator spin1 multiplicity vector. Permuting ALL spectators acts trivially on the spin1 carrier and irreducibly on that multiplicity, hence averages exactly to Pi_plus P/(p_plus d0). Retaining the original seed spectators outside the permutation would not justify this result.

The combined classical sign ensemble is precisely Pi_s P/d0. Final ordinary Bell sign measurement therefore yields

    Pr(s,t)=Tr[Pi_t N(Pi_s P)]/d0,
    Pr(s!=t)=delta^P/2.

No input nondemolition measurement, controlled-SWAP, or long-lived quantum reference is necessary for v4. The retained sign is classical and must not independently control the source. Same channel for every branch, except its declared quantum input, remains essential.

Independent ensemble construction checks n=4,6,8 using a different enumeration: minus branch sums uniform spectator perfect matchings; plus branch uniformly chooses an unordered spectator seed pair and then a perfect matching on the rest. The number of terms is respectively (1,3,15) and (1,6,45), and all subnormalized density matrices agree with exact projected singlet densities to below 1e-12. This enumeration is equivalent to the permutation ensemble because each matching/seed-pair pattern has the same permutation multiplicity. It is a verification method, not a claim that larger dense matrices must be constructed during calibration.

## 4. Explicit seed circuit checked
V5's Ry angle followed by controlled-on-zero Hadamard produces the equal three-label state. Two copying CNOTs correlate the labels without claiming to clone arbitrary unknown states. The listed U_A has orthonormal columns, U_B=(X tensor X) U_A(I tensor Z) has the stated occupied-column actions, and their application gives exactly Omega1. The optional U_A construction as the specified CNOT followed by odd-parity rotation also checks.

The mathematical gate identities prove the target; these numbers are floating checks. A fixed known four-qubit unitary is a constant-size compilation task, not an n-dependent state-preparation oracle. Finite synthesis/pulse error must still enter the test bias.

## 5. Statistics and actual control scaling
For a prespecified validation block with frozen frame and independent same-channel resets, random edges and signed preparations give iid Bernoulli(q_P). The v1 exact binomial endpoint transfers without alteration. A zero-defect block of m>=C_P log(1/beta)/epsilon calls suffices to certify epsilon; it does not guarantee a source will be accepted. Repeated candidate searches or stopping require confidence spending/a valid time-uniform method. No bound is inferred from arbitrary drifting bath history.

The finite-control bound is still

    service half-diamond <= min(1, C_P(q_U+b_test)+b_service).

The physical preparation burden is not removed by the smaller witness coefficient. If L imperfect preparation/routing/readout primitives each admit uniform half-diamond error <=eta relative to specified operations, a telescoping channel bound gives b_test<=L eta, plus separate sampler/preparation/readout biases. A sufficient per-primitive accuracy is consequently eta=O(epsilon/(n L)) when a constant fraction of the budget is allocated to testing. Thus L=O(n) direct-routing control requires O(epsilon/n^2) per-primitive precision; L=O(n^2) line routing requires O(epsilon/n^3). This is a conservative sufficient budget, not a proved necessity or an experimentally attained threshold. Actual correlated/unmodelled control faults require a justified complete-circuit budget instead.

With O(n/epsilon) accepted low-error sample calls, O(n) preparation work per call is O(n^2/epsilon) control volume before geometry/log factors. O(n^2) line routing raises this to O(n^3/epsilon). Each N_T call separately costs T storage exposures. Classical sampling, fixed seed synthesis, local frame gates, final pair readout, encoder/decoder execution in service, and private Pauli key retirement remain real operations.

## Accepted change and open obligations
The weighted construction reduces the sufficient-certificate shot coefficient from O(n^(5/2)) to less than 2(n-1), using physically specified singlet matching/constant-seed preparations and local final readout. It does not require the Schur encoder on every calibration shot. That is a substantive mathematical/interface improvement within this programme.

Direct encoded logical 2-design survival remains the strong comparator with O(1/epsilon) samples and full encoded operations each shot; no general overall advantage over it follows. The theorem is sufficient, may reject channels good on the code, does not invent the optimal code, and has no future bath guarantee. Current-channel identity across calibration/service and independently justified preparation/control error budgets remain obligations outside the witness itself.
