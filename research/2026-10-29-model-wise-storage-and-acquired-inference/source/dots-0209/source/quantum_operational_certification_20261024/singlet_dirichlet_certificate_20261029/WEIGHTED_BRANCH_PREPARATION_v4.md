# Root branch-prepared weighted witness: no input measurement or long-lived ancilla
2026-10-29. Supplements v3's preparation interface; proposed for independent review. The weighted certificate and statistical bound do not change.

Fix the selected pair i,j. Let P be the full n-qubit singlet projector in the frozen frame, and Π±=(I±S_ij)/2. The required subnormalized signed ensemble is Π_s P/d0. It can be prepared directly rather than generated by a nondemolition input SWAP measurement.

Write n=2r≥4. The minus subspace consists of the pair singlet tensored with the (n−2)-qubit spectator singlet space, with rank C_{r−1}. The plus subspace couples the pair's spin1 irrep to the spectator spin1 irrep to form total spin0, one state for each spectator spin1 multiplicity. Its rank is d0−C_{r−1}. Therefore draw a classical sign with probabilities
p−=C_{r−1}/C_r=(n+2)/[4(n−1)],
p+=1−p−=3(n−2)/[4(n−1)].
These are NOT generally the3/4,1/4 weights for maximally mixed physical input from v2. They can be sampled exactly using integer random draws with ordinary rejection; no unknown noise parameter enters.

## Minus preparation
Prepare a singlet on i,j. Prepare (n−2)/2 singlet pairs on spectators and uniformly randomly permute all spectator positions. The spectator S_(n−2) representation on its total-singlet space is irreducible, so its ensemble average is the spectator singlet projector divided by C_{r−1}. Thus this branch prepares Π−P/C_{r−1} exactly.

## Plus preparation
Choose two designated spectators a,b. Prepare the four-qubit total-singlet seed
|Ω1> = (|t+>_ij |t−>_ab − |t0>_ij |t0>_ab + |t−>_ij |t+>_ab)/sqrt(3),
where |t+>=|00>, |t−>=|11>, |t0>=(|01>+|10>)/sqrt(2).
Prepare singlet pairs on the other n−4 spectators. Then uniformly permute ALL n−2 spectator positions, including a,b. Do not permute only the remaining singlet pairs.

The spectator system has total spin1. Under Schur–Weyl decomposition it is Q1 tensor M1, where Q1 is three-dimensional spin space and M1 is the irreducible permutation multiplicity space. The seed couples pair Q1 with spectator Q1 into their unique total singlet, and occupies a fixed vector in M1. Spectator permutations act trivially on Q1 and irreducibly on M1, so their randomization replaces the multiplicity state by I_M1/dimM1 while preserving the spin coupling. This is exactly Π+P/rank(Π+P).

The seed is a fixed four-qubit state, requiring a constant-size state-preparation circuit to a chosen synthesis precision, not an n-qubit Schur encoder. Explicit computational amplitudes are +1/sqrt(3) on0011,1100 and −1/(2sqrt(3)) on0101,0110,1001,1010. Other amplitudes vanish. A generic known four-qubit state compiler is constant in n; its gate precision and physical error must enter b_test. An alternative is a paid pre-source triplet heralding preparation from two crossed singlets, with acceptance3/4; the deterministic seed construction avoids invoking this alternative as an unpriced oracle.

Finally apply the learned local frame to every site in either branch. Keep only sign s as needed for the test; other preparation randomness must not independently influence the source. Apply N and measure the pair's twisted Bell sign t. The input average subnormalized states are exactly Π_s P/d0, so Pr(s≠t)=δ^P/2 as in v3.

## Resource and scope comparison
This preferred weighted test uses O(n) Bell-singlet preparation gates, a fixed four-qubit seed in the plus branch, randomized spectator routing, known local rotations, a classical retained sign, and a final pair Bell readout. There is NO input parity measurement, no controlled-SWAP instrument, no full Schur encoder in validation, and no coherent ancilla surviving N. Routing can cost O(n²) nearest-neighbor SWAPs. Approximate state synthesis/randomization/calibration is paid in b_test, amplified by C_P<2(n−1).

It retains O(n ε^(−1)log(1/β)) low-error validation calls, one entire N_T call per trial, and all current-channel/reset/frozen-frame/confidence caveats. It is an interface tradeoff against direct logical 2-design validation, not an established aggregate-resource or historical advantage. v3 remains valid when its nondemolition input measurement is available; v4 replaces that requirement with the explicit signed branch preparations above.
