ALL-TIME PROTECTED-PHASE TRANSFER UNDER DAMPED ARITY REDUCTION
1 November 2026. Solo analytic proof candidate. Independent mathematical
audit and historical priority are not claimed. Earlier files are unchanged.

Precise purpose
==============
This transfers a LOCAL POSITIVE MEMORY CERTIFICATE, not a global
Hamiltonian/channel simulation guarantee. A finite Hamiltonian gadget
approximation with error O(nT epsilon) does not prove this statement.
The excited gadget sectors, their damping, ordinary input/output frame,
and physical controller noise are included directly in the certificate.

The construction uses standard subdivision interactions. The candidate
principle is that a protected dissipative phase with the premises below
is closed under their damped, finite-speed implementation. Its consequence
is an autonomous Tanner memory model with only THREE-QUBIT Hamiltonian
terms, at most THREE coupling terms per qubit, and ONE-QUBIT nominal
losses. The bounded-degree step is derived in section 10.
The enormous overhead, energy/precision
hierarchy, and expanding connectivity are explicit remaining limitations.

1. Input class and required slack
================================
There are n original DATA qubits and at most nu_0 n existing controller
qubits. Let H_0 be a sum of O(n) bounded-norm Pauli terms, of maximum
weight k, on a graph of bounded incidence. All constants may depend on
k and the chosen code/controller, but not on n or storage horizon.
The nominal losses are one-qubit sqrt(r_j) sigma^-_j with finite rates
and O(n) channels. Bath-only dynamics L_B(t) may be arbitrary on a finite
persistent B. Unknown system/B interactions are not inputs to a compiler;
they enter the physical local perturbation budget later.

The data carry a linear-distance code and fixed minimum-Pauli decoder.
Use the Tanner safe projector P (w<r=floor(rho n)) and confinement
w<=c U, with U a nonnegative commuting local syndrome potential. Every
Q-local operator between safe syndromes is scalar on all logical qubits
when 2r+Q<d. Here Q can be any fixed support allowance, for sufficiently
large n. The theorem does not assert geometric locality in ordinary space.

Assume a finite-depth LOCAL analytical unitary frame W_0 and a commuting
exponential weight

 F_0=exp(beta U+sum_i lambda_i N_i),

where the N_i count existing controller excitations. Let
K_0(X)=P (Ad_W0 L_0 Ad_W0dagger)(PXP) P. Require the OPERATOR inequality

 K_0^*(F_0P) <=[a_0 n-zeta_0 log(F_0)] F_0P, zeta_0>0.              (1)

F_0P means P F_0 P; logarithms are on P's support. The process is CP/TNI,
with full escape retained. Let Theta=beta rho/(8c). Require

 a_0/zeta_0<=Theta/4,
 maximum initial log moment density + BOTH frame-path growth charges
                                      <=Theta/4.                  (2)

Frame paths are analytical interpolations, not actual physical gates.
Initial logical/controller/B/reference correlations may be arbitrary.
Only the DATA input is ideally encoded. Equation (2) includes the worst
possible initial controller excitation; it is not a ground-state premise.
The finite-response Tanner construction satisfies these strict margins:
its b_0=Theta/2, 8lambda_0+768epsilon_0<=Theta/4, and its moment source
a_0/zeta_0<=Theta/4. See the preserved controller PROOF.txt sections E-H.

2. The actual subdivision Hamiltonian and new loss
=================================================
Take a target term h P, h real and nonzero, with Pauli weight s>=4.
Split its support into two disjoint parts A,B, each of weight >=2 and
as evenly as possible, so P=A B, A^2=B^2=I and [A,B]=0. Add one mediator
qubit a. Write t=sqrt(|h|/2), u=-sign(h), K=t(A+u B). Replace hP by

 H_gad=Omega n_a + sqrt(Omega) K X_a,
 Omega=epsilon^(-2),
 new loss: sqrt(Gamma) sigma^-_a.                                  (3)

Thus the two nontrivial couplers are sqrt(|h|Omega/2) A X_a and
u sqrt(|h|Omega/2) B X_a, each of weight <=ceil(s/2)+1. The detuning
Omega and loss Gamma will be finite, different scales. All other target
terms are retained. Add a separate qubit and loss for each compiled
Pauli term. There is no outcome postselection, pulsed clock, measured
record, or operation resetting mediators between proposals.

This is a concrete Hamiltonian, not a notation for a desired effective
interaction. Its overlaps with OTHER gadgets are allowed and included.

3. Local unitary normal form, including overlapping gadgets
==========================================================
At one layer use the same Omega=epsilon^-2 for every new mediator.
Let G_a=K_a Y_a, V_a=K_a X_a, N=sum_a n_a, V=sum_a V_a.
Then

                    [G_a,N]=-i V_a.                               (4)

Color the graph of intersecting gadget supports with finitely many
colors and define R as the ordered product of exp(-i epsilon G_a),
one disjoint layer per color. This is a proof frame only. Same-color
gates commute; its finite depth bounds all supports and all local Taylor
coefficients independently of n. Let H_low denote retained terms. The
ACTUAL Hamiltonian has an exact local normal form

 R H_gadget-layer R^dagger
   =epsilon^-2 N + H_0 -sum_a |h_a| I + H_exc + E_epsilon,
 local norm density of E_epsilon <=C epsilon.                       (5)

Here C is finite and independent of epsilon,Gamma,n,T. It depends on
the fixed old term bounds and incidences, gate norms and color count.
The identities establishing (5) are as follows.

The entire order-epsilon^-1 coefficient is V-i[sum_a G_a,N]=0,
on ALL controller states. This is Hamiltonian cancellation, not a
ground-state average. For one gadget the order-one coefficient is

 -(i/2)[G_a,V_a] =-K_a^2 Z_a
                  =(h_a P_a-|h_a|I) Z_a.                          (6)

Subtract h_a P_a-|h_a|I. Its residual is
2 n_a (|h_a|I-h_a P_a), with zero local mediator-ground block.
Every different-gadget order-one term contains a nontrivial off-diagonal
Pauli on EACH of two distinct mediator qubits, so its all-ground block
is zero. For example, for R=exp(-i epsilon G_b)exp(-i epsilon G_a),
the cross term is -i[G_a,V_b]. It need not be small or vanish on excited
controllers, including when target Pauli terms do not commute.
Order effects from the color product have the same two-mediator form.
Group these residuals into bounded local Hermitian terms H_exc,a with

              <0_cone|H_exc,a|0_cone>=0.                           (7)

There are O(n) such terms and bounded cone incidence. Each has bounded
norm independent of epsilon,Gamma. Taylor order three of epsilon^-2 N
and order two of epsilon^-1 V both leave O(epsilon) local remainders.
Retained H_low is changed by O(epsilon) locally. No global operator norm
O(n epsilon) is substituted for this local decomposition.

For clarity on the constants: a primitive support enlarged through ell
color layers has at most h^(ell+1) sites if initial support size <=h;
the number of relevant gates is <=ell h^(ell+1). Derivatives of an
adjoint gate cost <=2||G_a||. Product-rule bounds on derivatives up to
order three, applied separately BEFORE multiplying by epsilon^-2 and
epsilon^-1, give the claimed uniform local constants. Disconnected
gates commute with the primitive map and never enter its cone.

4. The transformed losses cannot be omitted
===========================================
Old one-qubit losses conjugated by R change their generators by a sum
of local maps of total diamond norm <=C epsilon n. Their old finite
rates are included in C. New losses satisfy the local expansion

 Ad_R (Gamma D[sigma^-_a]) Ad_Rdagger
       =Gamma D[sigma^-_a]+Gamma epsilon M_1a+Gamma M_2a,
 ||M_2a||_diamond<=C epsilon^2.                                    (8)

M_1a is a commutator of the loss superoperator with the single G_a
adjoint action; G_b for b!=a commutes with this primitive loss.
On ANY old-system observable X and any diagonal new excitation weight,

 <0_cone| M_1a^*(X tensor exp(lambda N)) |0_cone>=0.                 (9)

Each term has exactly one off-diagonal mediator factor. X can include
the GLOBAL safe projector and all old-system coherence. Equation (9)
does NOT say M_1a vanishes on all excited states, or is a free extra
noise channel. Its size is Gamma epsilon and must be paid for below.

5. Direct positive certificate for finite mediators
==================================================
Conjugate (5)-(8) also by the OLD frame W_0, which acts on no new mediator.
The zero-ground properties (7),(9) are unchanged and supports remain
bounded. Use F=F_0 exp(lambda N), 0<lambda<=1, and the genuine compressed
generator K on the same original-data safe projector P.

Weighted locality is used before taking norms. For a map supported in a
fixed cone S, only commuting factors in log F meeting S enter

 F^-1/2 M^*(F^1/2 X F^1/2) F^-1/2.                                (10)

All far factors cancel by the outside-S bimodule property, even for
X=P. The weighted norm is <=exp(width_near(log F))||M||_diamond.
This is a finite local constant, uniform for lambda in [0,1], independent
of n,B,reference. It may be extremely large. No small bath or classical
syndrome input is assumed.

For every Hermitian weighted residual B_a with local record-ground block
zero and norm <=D, let Q_a be a non-ground new mediator somewhere in
its cone. For any eta>0,

 B_a <=eta I+(D+D^2/eta) Q_a,
 Q_a<=sum_(j in cone_a)n_j.                                       (11)

This is the elementary two-block Young/Schur inequality. Summing it costs
bounded cone overlap times N. The global input P in (10) commutes with
all new mediator ground projectors, so the same zero-block statement and
bound hold AFTER safe compression and weighting. This is the step that
handles excited gadget sectors without a global adiabatic-channel error.

Bare detuning epsilon^-2 N commutes with F and P exactly. The actual
nominal new loss has negative drift

           -Gamma(1-exp(-lambda)) N F_P<=-Gamma lambda N F_P/2.    (12)

The target generator has its inherited drift (1) multiplied by the new
weight. Apply (11) separately to H_exc and the first loss correction in
(8). Charge E_epsilon, old-loss changes and M_2a by (10). For a single
sufficient constant C>=1 absorbing local term densities, norms, squares
and overlaps, one obtains the operator bound

 K^*(F_P) <= { [a_old+C(eta+epsilon+Gamma epsilon^2)] n
              -zeta_0 log F_old
              -[Gamma lambda/2
                -C(1+1/eta+Gamma epsilon+Gamma^2 epsilon^2/eta)]N }
                                                               F_P. (13)

C is independent of epsilon,Gamma,n,T; old rates/strengths and frame
geometry are fixed before choosing the new scales. One can take the
same C for all lambda in [0,1], using the larger near width with lambda=1.
Each operation in this derivation is either a local derivative bound,
the exact Hamiltonian identities (4)-(7), or the two-block bound (11).
The full K is CP/TNI because the full physical generator is GKSL. Its
formal Taylor coefficients need not individually be CP.

6. A nonempty finite hierarchy, with no n- or T-dependent rate
============================================================
Let d>0 be the allocated increase in source density. Choose lambda>0
small enough for the initial moment budget in section 8. Choose eta>0
so that C eta<=d/4, and then choose a FINITE new loss rate

 Gamma>=max(4zeta_0, 8C(1+1/eta)/lambda).                           (14)

Finally choose epsilon>0 small enough that

 C(Gamma epsilon+Gamma^2 epsilon^2/eta)<=Gamma lambda/8,
 C(epsilon+Gamma epsilon^2)<=d/2,
 and the frame-path cost in section 8 meets its allocated budget.    (15)

Every constraint in (15) is an upper bound with a positive solution.
Omega=epsilon^-2 and all coupler strengths sqrt(|h|Omega/2) are finite.
Equations (13)-(15) give

 K^*(F_P)<=[(a_old+d)n-zeta_0 log F_old-(Gamma lambda/4)N]F_P
          <=[(a_old+d)n-zeta_0 log F]F_P.                           (16)

The increase d can be arbitrarily small. The new controller's response
and faults have not been sent to an n- or T-dependent ideal limit.
No assumption g<<Gamma is needed: this compiler uses detuning, with
large energy gap Omega and coherent coupler ~sqrt(Omega), then finite
loss to control occupied mediator sectors. Loss dressing is paid for
by (13), rather than dropped on the basis of a gap argument.

7. Recursive reduction terminates at Hamiltonian arity THREE
===========================================================
At a layer compile all Pauli terms above the new maximum weight.
The weight recursion is k_next<=ceil(k/2)+1, so after at most
L=ceil(log_2(k_initial-2)) layers all Hamiltonian terms have weight <=3.
Bare gaps are already one-body and are not subdivided. The ONLY new
Lindblad jumps at every layer are one-qubit lowering operators.

For one weight-s Pauli monomial, every split conserves the sum of
child weights minus two: (|A|+1-2)+(|B|+1-2)=s-2. Its final leaves have
weight three, so the complete subdivision tree has exactly s-2 leaf
couplers and s-3 new mediators. Each new mediator occurs in two final
three-body couplers, its one-body gap, and its one-body loss. Original
sites still occur in as many Pauli trees as their Pauli expansion needs.

Thus bounded arity is actually achieved by the declared physical terms.
The very large number of trees is not concealed: in the Tanner
application, with h=R_0+c, at most (8/c)4^h n initial Pauli monomials
give at most (h-3)(8/c)4^h n new mediators. The underlying table has not
been compactly synthesized. This is an enormous but n-independent
overhead; all support incidence remains n-independent.

At layer i the target is the ACTUAL physical generator of the previous
layer, including its finite one-body loss rates and strengths. Its frame
is W_i=W_(i-1) R_i and its moment adds lambda_i N_i. Equation (16)
therefore iterates without a global simulation error. Coefficients may
grow enormously with the number of layers; no polynomial or useful
dependence on k_initial is asserted.

8. Arbitrary initial mediators, local faults, and ordinary output
===============================================================
For the full bounded-degree result let L now count ALL compiler stages,
including the port-isolation and fork stages of section 10. Their number
is finite and independent of n; the same budget argument applies.
Let layer i add <=nu_i n mediators. Choose lambda_i so that

 sum_i nu_i lambda_i<=Theta/16,
 d_i<=zeta_0 Theta/(16L),
 total growth charge of ALL new frame paths, input AND output,
                                                <=Theta/16.        (17)

The last choice is possible by further decreasing each epsilon_i after
Gamma_i is fixed. A frame path has integrated local-generator norm
O(epsilon_i n); weighted locality gives growth <=exp(C_i epsilon_i n).
The constants C_i are finite local constants. Such paths are analytical
comparisons only. Mediators can begin fully excited, entangled, or
correlated with logical/C/B/reference inputs: max N_i=nu_i n is already
paid by (17). There is no physical mediator preparation requirement.

All physical faults may act on a fixed q original-or-controller qubits
and the SAME arbitrary finite persistent B, with sum diamond norms
<=delta n uniformly in time. The terms may be signed differences of
local generators; the actual total generator must remain GKSL/CPTP.
The whole finite-depth frame preserves these norms and enlarges their
supports only to a fixed cone. Use (10) once at the end, and choose
delta>0 so its weighted charge is <=zeta_0 Theta/16 per n.
This charges fault spread through every compiler layer. Miscalibration
of large gap and coupling terms, loss errors, thermal gain, and coherent
bath couplings are all subject to this SAME actual-generator budget.

From (1),(2),(16),(17), the final source/zeta_0 is <=3Theta/8. Its
initial moment plus all proof-frame path charges is <=3Theta/8. For
sigma a positive killed state with trace tau<=1, spectral Jensen gives

 Tr(F log F sigma)>=m log(m/tau)>=m log m,
 (log m)'<=a_final n-zeta_0 log m.

Consequently at every storage time and throughout the final frame
paths the moment is <=exp(Theta n), with room to spare. No normalized
postselection or total-variation-in-time assumption enters.

Let Q_final bound supports after the entire frame. The dangerous safe
boundary has w>=r-2Q_final, hence U>=rho n/(2c) for sufficiently large n.
Its probability is at most exp(-3Theta n). Every local actual-minus-
compressed generator term vanishes on the complementary low-weight
block. CP/TNI Duhamel with the actual CPTP suffix bounds its norm by
six times its local generator norm times the square root of that tail.
All physical nominal strengths, frame costs, and noise density are finite
constants with total norm O(n). Bath-only terms have zero boundary action.

After all compressed segments, the ordinary fixed DATA decoder factors
the comparison as Id_all-k tensor Lambda_CB^0, CP/TNI. Complete its
auxiliary-only trace defect by a square-root Kraus operator, as in the
preserved proof. This gives constants C_final,b>0 independent of n,T,
input, reference and finite bath dimension such that

 inf_(Lambda_CB CPTP)
 ||(Dec_data tensor Id_CB) Phi_compiled,T (Enc_data tensor Id_CB)
                   -Id_all-k tensor Lambda_CB||_diamond
       <=min(2,C_final n(1+T) exp(-b n/2)), b=2Theta.                (18)

C includes ALL existing/new controllers. The physical channel contains
no W_i gates. Input R paths, transformed actual storage, and inverse
output paths compose algebraically to the actual physical channel.
Their compressed comparisons earn the same ordinary data decoder;
there is no hidden gadget decoder or final active frame correction.

9. Consequence and limits
=========================
Applying this principle, including section 10, to the preserved Tanner/
branch-controller candidate gives a STATIC graph-local generator with
<=3-qubit Pauli Hamiltonian couplings, at most THREE nontrivial coupling
terms per qubit, and one-qubit nominal loss, a nonzero FIXED local
noise threshold, k=Omega(n), and exponentially long joint quantum storage.
If N_phys=nu_total n including all mediators, nu_total is finite and
n-independent, so rate and distance remain positive constants in N_phys
and (18) remains exponential in N_phys. Those constants may be unusable.

This is a stronger arity-constrained GENERATOR-MODEL consequence than
writing the original syndrome-table Hamiltonian. It is not a delivered
device: overhead is exponential in the table support, the number of
controller stages and scale separation/calibration are severe, primitive
three-body couplers and one-body reservoirs remain supplied resources,
and no fixed-dimensional layout or microscopic bath has been acquired.
Fault-tolerant DATA acquisition/readout and practical advantage remain
unearned. See CONSTRUCTION_AND_COSTS.txt for the exact costs and prior-art
boundary. The theorem is a solo proof candidate, not independently audited.

10. Uniform degree reduction: the same certificate, not a new import
====================================================================
The normal-form and loss argument has a useful more general input. For
ANY fixed-support Hermitian K on old qubits, a mediator with

                 Omega n_a +sqrt(Omega) K X_a                       (19)

has the same G=K Y_a and order-one Hamiltonian -K^2 Z_a. Its ground block
is -K^2, and its residual after subtracting that block is 2 n_a K^2.
All cross-gadget order-one terms still have zero new-ground block.
Equations (8)-(16) therefore apply whenever the intended old Hamiltonian
is H_low-sum_a K_a^2, up to scalar identities. Known bounded local
counterterms may be placed in H_low. No bound is inferred from a bare
effective Hamiltonian calculation alone: damping and the Schur bound
are still required. Pauli subdivision also works for weights TWO and
THREE with nonempty disjoint A,B; it simply need not lower arity there.

10a. Isolate every old site behind private ports
------------------------------------------------
Start after the arity-three stage, and call ALL its qubits old sites.
Leave their one-body fields and one-body losses unchanged. For each
two- or three-body Pauli coupling, subdivide sequentially to isolate
each old Pauli factor. For h P_j P_k P_l the support pattern is

 P_j P_k P_l
    -> (P_j X_a) + (P_k P_l X_a)
    -> (P_j X_a) + (P_k X_b) + (P_l X_a X_b)
    -> (P_j X_a) + (P_k X_b) + (P_l X_c) + (X_a X_b X_c).           (20)

Each arrow denotes a separately damped subdivision stage, with its
actual coefficients given by (3), not equality of those displayed
unweighted sums. All intermediate couplings have arity <=3. For a
two-body coupling use two ports and a final X_a X_b internal coupling.
All old endpoints now have only two-body P_j X_port interactions to
private ports; the ports have degree two, one old endpoint and one
internal coupling. All original one-body fields remain one-body.
There are at most three global stages and <=3m ports for m initial
nontrivial couplings. The stage graph need not be geometrically local.

10b. Merge two equal-Pauli endpoint edges with a damped fork
----------------------------------------------------------
At an old site j, group the port edges by P_j=X,Y,Z. For two edges
h_1 P_j X_b + h_2 P_j X_c with distinct private ports b,c, use

 K=(P_j-h_1 X_b-h_2 X_c)/sqrt(2),
 H_low includes the counterterm +h_1 h_2 X_b X_c.                   (21)

Then -K^2+h_1 h_2 X_b X_c equals the two desired old edges, minus the
scalar (1+h_1^2+h_2^2)/2. The physical new mediator f has gap Omega n_f
and sqrt(Omega/2)(P_j-h_1 X_b-h_2 X_c)X_f: THREE two-body couplings.
Add h_1 h_2 X_b X_c, also two-body, and one-body loss on f.
The old site j now meets only ONE edge P_j X_f in place of the pair.
The new f has three incident edges. The shared-ground Hamiltonian is
correct, and the occupied/cross-controller remainders are controlled
by the general K version of (13)-(16).

10c. Keep every intermediate vertex's degree bounded
-----------------------------------------------------
A naive repeated fork would accumulate extra degree at earlier fork
vertices. Before EACH fork round, first subdivide each edge that will
be paired, inserting a fresh private port u on P_j--b:

                       P_j--u--b.                                 (22)

This is the weight-two subdivision, with only two-body couplers.
The old endpoint b loses P_j--b and gains u--b, so its degree does not
increase. The fresh u has degree two. Fork a pair of such fresh ports.
After the fork each u has its old b edge, one f edge, and the counterterm
edge to its partner: degree three. A new f has degree three. If f is
paired in a later round, (22) keeps its degree three. No counterterm
touches another old site, so degree at other old sites cannot increase.

In parallel pair each old site's X, Y and Z groups. Every subdivision
and fork phase is one finite certificate-transfer stage; overlaps are
permitted and color-grouped in the proof. After at most ceil(log_2 D)
rounds, D the initial endpoint degree, each old site has at most one
edge of each Pauli type: at most THREE couplings. Ports internal to
an original term have one internal triple/pair and ONE external two-body
coupling: fresh subdivisions ensure they are never directly forked.
Every other new vertex has at most three two-body couplings.
Thus ALL physical sites meet at most three coupling hyperedges, every
hyperedge has size <=3, and the ordinary graph degree is also <=3:
an internal triple gives two neighbors plus one external neighbor; all
other vertices meet at most three pairwise neighbors. One-body
fields and loss are separately counted local resources.

The primitive alphabet is also uniform: two-body P tensor X with
P in {X,Y,Z}, controller-only XXX triples, one-body fields, and one-body
lowering loss. Port isolation puts every remaining triple entirely on
NEW controllers with X on each port. Forks/subdivisions create only
two-body P tensor X or XX, so they preserve that fact. The original DATA
qubits themselves therefore need only PAIRWISE couplings, at most one
for each Pauli type. No syndrome-table projector is a primitive operation
in this final Hamiltonian. Its large information content is instead
encoded in the number, coefficients and scale hierarchy of the network.

There are m_j-1 forks for an initial group of m_j edges, and two fresh
subdivisions per fork. Sum_j m_j<=3m after port isolation, so this step
adds <=9m mediators, beyond <=3m ports in 10a. The number of stages is
at most 3+2ceil(log_2 D), in addition to arity reduction. D can be huge
but is n-independent. All new scales are chosen stage by stage using
(14)-(17), with budgets divided by this TOTAL finite stage count.

The resulting bound on the Tanner physical-qubit multiplier is, for
p_0=(8/c)4^h and h=R_0+c, the deliberately loose

             N_phys <=[9+13h p_0] n.                              (23)

This exposes rather than removes the exponential table overhead. The
new consequence is uniform primitive arity AND uniform coupling degree,
with a direct all-time noisy-controller proof candidate. It is not an
efficient lookup compiler, a two-body-only result, or an acquired
low-dimensional device. The scale and precision hierarchy may worsen
dramatically through these additional stages.
