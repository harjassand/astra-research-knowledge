4. Strong mathematical precursor
Pantazis, Katsoulakis and Vlachos (2013), Parametric sensitivity analysis for
biochemical reaction networks based on pathwise information theory.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4015035/
DOI 10.1186/1471-2105-14-311.
Path Fisher information and score sensitivity are prior machinery. Our local
control projection is ordinary linear algebra applied to those quantities.
No novelty of the generic score or Fisher method is claimed.

5. Our scope
The observationally identical controlled chains, exact selectivity/MFPT formulas,
finite probe comparison and combined reuse tests are constructed for this run.
They discriminate the proposed use; they are not a realistic chemical device.
No experimental catalyst, force field, field-response function or molecular
observation protocol was silently provided by the small model.
