BOUNDARY FLUCTUATIONS AS A LOCAL MECHANICAL RESPONSE MAP
Stage-one calculation, 1 November 2026

This is an ordinary fluctuation-response construction, independently checked
in a physical scattering model. It is not a priority claim for fluctuation-
dissipation theory, system identification, collective wave cooling, or physical
learning. The complete competitor and physical restrictions change the stage
selection: this branch is preserved, but it has not earned a total advantage.

1. Lossless scattering identity

Let S(q) be the complete unitary scattering matrix at fixed boundary reference
planes, using the time convention for which Q_i=-i S^dagger partial_i S is
Hermitian. All relevant propagating output channels must be included. For a
power-normalized input coherence C>=0, tr C=P, the mechanical force is

  f_i(C)=tr(C Q_i)/omega.

The sign and amplitude normalization are fixed by the chosen time/power
convention; other primary sources use the opposite phasor convention.
The executable checks this particular convention against direct momentum flux.

Choose a real orthonormal basis of Hermitian port matrices. Let B have columns
vec(Q_i), and let M be a positive symmetric mechanical mobility matrix. Then

  K=B M B^T >= 0,
  controlled scattering-velocity increment = K vec(C)/omega.

This is a response of observables to physical forces. It does not require
separate reconstruction of all hidden coordinates or the individual Q_i.

For thermal overdamped motion, write the local covariance law as

  Cov(dq | q)=2 kBT M(q) dt.

The infinitesimal Hermitian scattering increment is most cleanly expressed as
  dH=-i S^dagger circ dS = sum_i Q_i circ dq_i,
where circ denotes the Stratonovich differential. Equivalently use the local
Hermitian matrix logarithm of S(q)^dagger S(q+dq). To leading order,

  Cov(vec dH | q)=2 kBT K(q) dt+o(dt).

Thus thermal fluctuations can supply the local action Gram matrix. This is a
standard FDT consequence, not a new physical information source. In Itô
coordinates the observable mean includes curvature and noise-induced drift;
those terms must not silently be identified with mechanical load alone.

2. Deterministic load cancellation and its scope

In a locally deterministic drift experiment, let f0 be an unknown external load
and y0=B M f0. The drift-induced viscous dissipation is

  D(C)=D0 + 2<vec C,y0>/omega + (vec C)^T K(vec C)/omega^2.

The unknown constant D0 does not affect the optimizer. This is NOT the square
of an instantaneous Brownian velocity (which is undefined). Using this formula
with measured stochastic mean velocity requires removing or modeling the
noise-induced observable drift, or an appropriate deterministic/local regime.
The code validates the deterministic formula; it does not acquire that full
separation in an actual thermal specimen.

For a motion v=M B^T c induced by an allowed control, Bv=0 implies v=0: multiply
by c^T to obtain (B^T c)^T M(B^T c)=0. This collocation fact says that an
actuated direction cannot be locally dark to the full response. It does not
make arbitrary external motion observable or controllable.

3. What the physical executable actually establishes

port_response.py constructs the exact lossless two-port scattering matrix of
three movable delta interfaces in the scalar 1D Helmholtz equation. It computes
momentum transfer directly and compares it with the Q_i force identity.

Results:
  unitarity residual: 4.10e-16
  largest force identity absolute error: 1.97e-9
  largest local induced-response relative error: 2.07e-5
  thermal covariance relative K error with 8000 local samples: 0.00645

The 8000 samples use independent local starts at the same simulator position.
They validate a kernel. They do NOT supply an experimental reset or an observer
that acquires those samples before geometry changes.

A load chosen to be cancelable within the PSD power cone gives residual
deterministic dissipation fraction 1.05e-4 with estimated K. This does not prove
that arbitrary loads lie in the cone. Four ordinary positive spanning probes
recover the same local K to numerical precision, so the headline cancellation
does not select the fluctuation method over its informed competitor.

Changing positions by normalized distances 0.001,0.01,0.05,0.1 changes K by
approximately 0.72%,6.20%,23.25%,62.60%. Locality is a real acquisition cost.

4. Electrical finite-port version

For positive input conductance G(q), low-frequency conduction-dominated media,
and the force identity f_i=kappa tr(C partial_i G), let J=d vec(G)/dq. Then

  controlled d vec(G)/dt = kappa K vec(C),
  K=J M J^T=Cov(d vec G)/(2 kBT dt)+o(1).

For peak phasor voltages with current into the device, virtual work and an
independent small-inclusion Maxwell-stress calculation give
  kappa=+epsilon_medium/(4 sigma_medium).

The 2026 DGEM preprint is useful experimental machinery but still acquires
particle-position derivatives using optical guide stars. Its printed negative
coefficient requires a different sign convention to match the positive-input
identity. The independent derivation and finite-frequency limitations are in
stage1_wave/DGEM_SIGN_AUDIT.txt, not assumed away in this formula.

An active block with other electrodes grounded need not have row sum zero.
Only a complete isolated terminal matrix has the common-mode row-sum rule.

5. Obstructions and stronger conventional routes

The root and independent constructors preserved four distinct restrictions:

* Partial channels: two reciprocal unitary scatterers can have exactly the same
  observed histories and 98% collected power, yet opposite work sign. Lost power
  alone does not bound the derivative carried by hidden channels.
* Positive actuation: C is PSD. Digital subtraction of two measurements does
  not create negative physical voltage covariance. For G(q)=q[[1,-1],[-1,1]],
  all allowed forces have the same sign, even when K is known exactly.
* Finite observation: thermal and detector fluctuations differ; increment
  detector errors are correlated. A four-read positive on/off protocol on one
  unreset trajectory cancels unknown affine drift and may beat a passive
  covariance oracle, while paying in real displacement and work.
* Reachability: a strict circular-planar conductor cannot realize arbitrary
  passive terminal responses. Circular-minor inequalities survive arbitrary
  hidden model complexity and unlimited controller intelligence.

For a particular loss L(G), the strongest conventional controller need not
identify K at all. It learns the scalar loss-rate quadratic u^T A_L u from
positive rank-one probes, then selects an admissible voltage. Contracted
terminal power reads reduce its acquisition burden. A callback-only active
controller was executed without hidden coordinates or resets and reached a
feasible complete-response target in a simple inclusion model.

These are genuine reasons to change direction. They do not discard the local
response identity or the finite-observer machinery.
