The root's current central question is whether one can acquire the mechanical
actions available inside a scattering body WITHOUT resolving its internal
coordinates or supplying a positional guide-star. Full success would enable
controlled physical interventions in optically/acoustically complex matter from
boundary measurements. This is stronger than another imaging score and different
from the existing position-free collective cooling operation.

Working ingredients:
  - a measured coherent scattering matrix S(q) at a fixed wave frequency;
  - classical wave-force operators Q_i=-i S^dagger partial_i S for lossless
    adiabatic scattering, with fixed boundary reference planes;
  - existing experimental wavefront actuation;
  - overdamped local thermal mechanical motion with mobility M and bath T.

Central missing operation: the Q_i and M are not separately supplied. Learn the
observable mechanical response directly. For power-normalized coherence C>=0,
tr C=P, force is f_i=tr(C Q_i)/omega. If qdot=M(f0+f), then the Hermitian boundary
velocity is
  Y=-i S^dagger Sdot = Y0+K(C)/omega,
  K(C)=sum_ij Q_i M_ij tr(C Q_j).
The same positive self-adjoint operator K appears in thermal fluctuation covariance:
  Cov(vec_Herm[-i S^dagger dS])=2 kBT K dt + higher-order terms.

Thus spontaneous finite mechanical fluctuations are a candidate calibration
operation for all locally available wave-induced responses, without microscopic
coordinate recovery. This follows fluctuation-dissipation theory; the claim to
investigate is the complete scattering-to-action construction and its attainable
physical tasks, not invention of FDT or generic system identification.

Important discriminating questions:
  1. Does this acquire a useful intervention beyond the strongest existing
     camera-free cooling and single-particle stochastic trap optimization?
  2. Does a physical multiscatterer model obey the inferred operation when the
     controller receives only noisy boundary S records?
  3. The allowed C form a positive power-bounded cone. Arbitrary signed force
     operators and scalar identity shifts are not free physical controls.
  4. Partial/lossy boundary data need not identify work, even with tiny measured
     loss. Unknown absorption, photothermal motion, nonthermal diffusion and
     detector noise can invalidate the covariance-to-response conversion.
  5. The local response may expire before it is acquired; global motion can have
     invisible internal degrees of freedom and S need not be a Markov state.
