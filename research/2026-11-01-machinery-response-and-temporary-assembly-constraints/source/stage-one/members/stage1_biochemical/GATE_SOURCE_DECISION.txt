SOURCE-TO-CONSTRUCTION DECISION: TEMPORARY RNP ASSEMBLY GATES
2026-11-01. Parent investigator owns the finite kinetic construction.

WHAT IS AVAILABLE

Sheng et al. 2025, Nature Communications 16:6642,
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-61640-1
provides real ASO interventions in S150-based iSAT. DNA-RA15 facilitates the GFP
readout and targets a domain II junction. PNA-RA20 disrupts H1 and produces an
observed misdocked III/VI arrangement, including RA20-B-c. Biotinylated probes
co-localize with intermediate fractions. The reported antidote rescue neutralizes
the probe BEFORE the assembly reaction; it does not remove a prebound gate.
S150 supplies modification, processing, assembly and translation functions.
This is direct access to relevant physical states, but its helper inventory is
not defined. Exact conditions and missing sequence inputs are in
ASO_GATE_INPUTS.json. Sequences have not been invented or reverse-engineered
from helix names.

Smart et al. 2024, DOI 10.1093/nar/gkae143,
https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/52/10/5950/7624078
provides a distinct real operation: a DNA splint is displaced from ligated 16S
rRNA using complementary DNA and terminal unpaired tails, at 46 C. The authors
preserve higher-order RNA structure. It supports removal from folded naked RNA;
it does not establish access, branch migration or post-release recovery in a
protein-coated, ASO-stalled 50S assembly intermediate. The source operation was
read independently by the parent investigator and is recorded with that scope.

Huang and Karbstein 2021, DOI 10.1073/pnas.2101164118,
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8237570/
supplies the biological mechanism worth abstracting: factors and snoRNAs bind
the tertiary partner l31 rather than the j34-35-38 junction itself, withholding
a docking interaction that would trap an incorrect local fold. Release then
allows the folded junction to dock. This is a rationale for selective partner
withholding, not a demonstration of a synthetic large-subunit guide.

PRIOR CONSTRUCTIONS THAT REMOVE BROAD NOVELTY CLAIMS

Lei et al. 2020, DOI 10.1016/j.giant.2020.100008,
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666542520300084
already constructs catalytic nucleic-acid chaperones for RNA/ssDNA hairpin
refolding, with large acceleration. Pokhrel et al. 2024,
DOI 10.1021/jacs.3c14350, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11135164/
uses short DNA chaperones to relax mechanically prepared hairpin-array traps.
Generic nucleic-acid-assisted folding is therefore established. A distinctive
claim would require the complete large-RNP gate, its actual release, product
function and resource improvement, rather than a general folding metaphor.

ACTUAL EXECUTABLE MACHINERY AND ITS LIMITS

The pinned upstream repository and Zenodo release are preserved in
public_code/PNA_50S/PROVENANCE.json. Five files have exact Git blob hashes.
audit_pna_sources.py executes the upstream contact/quadrant-count functions and
audits the deposited 158-by-158 mature-contact matrix. There are 622 undirected
off-diagonal contacts. H1 contacts H2, H94, H98, H99 and uL13 in this matrix.
The code can constrain candidate structural neighborhoods and distinguish
local from distant contact dependencies; it cannot identify kinetic rates.

The public code is an exploratory analysis script with external data paths.
Raw density masks, threshold tables and occupancy tables used in the complete
workflow are not in this repository. A source-data ZIP is linked from the paper;
its contents were not obtained. The graph-building script explicitly drops
RA20-B-c1 and RA20-B-c2 before dependency inference. The native DAG therefore
does not represent the misdocked failure state that motivates the proposed gate.
The audit constructs two kinetic chains with identical binary occupancy support
and very different completion times. Thus occupancy order alone does not earn
a release schedule, an escape rate or a real-time observer.

SCIENTIFIC DECISION

The actual gap is postbinding, recoverable, selectively timed withholding of a
specific RNA docking partner in a protein-containing assembly. Its experiment
must establish both that the prebound guide leaves and that useful assembly
resumes; an antidote rescue caused only by intercepting unbound probe does not
pass. The first target can be selected from structurally resolved RA20 states,
but strong inhibition is not evidence that this target is a useful chaperone.
RA15 is a facilitative comparator. An unmodified RNA that immediately re-enters
an unstable PTC conformation after release cannot be saved by kinetic timing
alone. Mature modified RNA, synthetic patches or separately earned modification
chemistry remain explicit requirements, not gifts from the guide.

Compare with the strongest full-protein assembly route under the same supplied
RNA/protein/factor inventory. Control both guide and antidote consumption and
their postreaction removal. A protein-free release result, structural rescue
without protein synthesis, or a gain only against deliberately poor assembly
conditions would not establish the intended technology. Conversely, a specific
failure to release, loss of RNA or proteins upon release, or rapid post-release
misfolding is informative and should redirect chemistry or target selection.

No exhaustive novelty claim is made. The narrow literature search found the
three complementary operations above, not a demonstrated reversible ASO gate
that releases from a pre-50S complex and beats optimized conventional assembly.
