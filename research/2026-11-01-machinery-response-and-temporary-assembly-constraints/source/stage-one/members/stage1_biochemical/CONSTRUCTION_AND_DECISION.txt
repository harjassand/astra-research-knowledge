BIOCHEMICAL MACHINERY CONTENDER — STAGE ONE, 2026-11-01

Decision
========
Keep the operations and exact obstruction, but do not select generic PURE
self-regeneration as the principal sustained-construction opening. Existing
research already owns the apparent composition, and the decisive service is
still missing: active ribosome/accessory-factor renewal in a defined system.
The exact source-network audit and maintenance bound below explain why.

Capability and proposed physical mechanism
==========================================
A seed quantity of sequence-programmed molecular manufacturing machinery could
in principle support enduring, reprogrammable synthesis from small-molecule
feed, while making its own replacement catalysts. That would reduce reliance
on repeated production/purification of a large enzyme inventory and could allow
chemistry incompatible with growing cells. Full success would be consequential;
no complete cost advantage over fermentation or ordinary cell-free manufacture
has been earned here.

The proposed construction was a retained-catalyst factory: small-molecule feed
and waste exchange through dialysis; immobilized or retained genetic templates;
shared transcription/translation machinery divided between product manufacture
and replacement of the active machinery; explicit assembly and folding services;
ordinary functional assays to measure replacement rather than reporter mass alone.

The strongest informed conventional approach changed the decision. Dialysis
PURE already produces for at least 12.5 days, and recent self-regeneration papers
already propose combining longer feeding with catalyst regeneration. A generic
controller or a few improvements to yield would not establish a new capability.
The 2025 ribosome construction uses soluble E. coli S150 proteome; it does not
supply a defined, self-renewed ribosome-assembly service. Combining paper titles
would move the hardest work into that helper.

First construction: maintenance feasibility, not reproduction slogans
===================================================================
Let c_i be a desired active catalyst concentration. Let delta_i be its intrinsic
active loss rate, D the small-molecule exchange rate, and R_i the experimentally
measured rejection/retention coefficient for catalyst i. At a fixed setpoint its
required active replacement flux is

    ell_i = [delta_i + D(1-R_i)] c_i.

This simple distinction matters: a factory need not double itself. It only needs
to replace actual losses. Retention can make the protein-replacement requirement
far smaller than the one-inventory-per-generation requirement. This is a useful
engineering route but is already latent in dialysis and retained-enzyme reactors.

Necessary translation-service bound
-----------------------------------
If i has length L_i, and eta_i is the NET yield of correctly folded, assembled,
active i per completed translated chain (not fluorescence or total protein mass),
then at a stationary target and with residue-production capacity B,

    sum_i L_i ell_i/eta_i + L_p J_p/eta_p <= B.

Proof: producing active flux ell_i consumes at least ell_i/eta_i completed chains,
each with L_i residues. Sum all chain costs and add exported product. The same
bound applies to time averages of a bounded inventory when coefficients and
capacity are constant: integrate its material balances and divide by elapsed
time; the inventory boundary term vanishes. Pulsed or feedback schedules cannot
beat this integrated budget. A fixed allocation attains it in the ideal linear
service model, so a generic adaptive controller earns no advantage there.

This is only a necessary physical bound. It omits, and therefore cannot certify,
rRNA production, cofactor regeneration, ATP/GTP and phosphate flux, folding,
ribosome assembly, membrane leakage/fouling, template preservation, pump work,
purification losses and measurement/calibration costs. Those services must be
included explicitly before calling the factory complete.

General resource-complete form and obstruction witness
-----------------------------------------------------
Let nonnegative mode flux u produce active catalysts with matrix Y and consume
bounded services with matrix C. Feasibility requires

    Y u >= ell,  C u <= b,  u >= 0.

For any nonnegative prices p,q satisfying Y^T p <= C^T q, feasibility implies
p^T ell <= q^T b. Proof: multiply the two primal inequalities and insert the
dual inequality. Thus p^T ell > q^T b is an exact impossibility certificate.
This is ordinary linear resource duality, not a claimed new theorem.

If no available mode makes active ribosomes, choose p to select their positive
replacement requirement and q=0. Then Y^T p=0, but p^T ell>0, proving that every
positive ribosome-loss rate defeats indefinite renewal. Small loss delays failure;
it does not remove the missing operation. If an unreplaced active ribosome stock
has loss at least delta>0, R(t)<=R(0)exp(-delta t). With per-ribosome elongation
capacity at most v, its cumulative translated residues are <=v R(0)/delta.
This bound holds however the templates, feed or other factors are controlled.

Actual executable source audit
==============================
The public PURE_CRN_models repository supplies a sequence-resolved transcription
and translation mechanism. We preserved a small MIT-licensed source subset at
commit 59f8c7b7c10b3a925d0be511e5fa8b633db7af46, including authors' SBML networks,
initial conditions and rates. Every file is checked against its exact Git blob
hash in PROVENANCE.json; README.md is stored as README.txt with identical bytes.

audit_renewal.py parses the actual 3,852-species, 4,564-reaction translation SBML
and verifies exact left-null vectors of its stoichiometric matrix. Both 30S and
50S subunit material and all 35 non-polymerase protein-factor moieties are
conserved. Its listed degraded species participate in no reaction. In the
separate transcription-containing network, T7 RNA polymerase is also conserved.

This is not a defect in a model designed for finite reporter synthesis. It is an
exact limit on the scientific claim one can earn from executing it. Replacing the
reporter sequence with a factor's sequence would still require an explicitly
validated active-product/assembly connection; a peptide is not automatically an
active machine. The authors' Bioscrape trajectories were not rerun: the audit is
an independent exact stoichiometric calculation on their declarative model.

The script also solves a deliberately idealized maintenance LP. A single
nonribosomal replacement channel with capacity 0.08 inventories per interval is
used only as a conditional example inspired by published recovered mass. It is
NOT a fitted active production rate. Losses of 0.01, 0.02 and 0.04 inventories
would leave 87.5%, 75% and 50% of that linear service for product if ribosomes
were immortal. Adding any positive ribosome loss and zero renewal makes the
complete LP infeasible. Supplying an invented ribosome-renewal channel restores
feasibility: this is shown expressly to expose the unearned-helper assumption,
not as a proposed real experiment. Fixed allocation already attains the bound.

Why the empirical 8% is not the missing parameter
================================================
Mottaghi/Maerkl report 28 micrograms recovered from 350 micrograms initial
nonribosome protein, i.e. 8% total mass. The products can reconstitute function,
but activity ratios, recoveries and per-component active yields remain distinct.
The method includes separate purification, buffer exchange and concentration.
It does not measure a universal renewal rate inside an indefinitely operating
retention reactor. It also cannot prove functionality of every nominally
regenerated factor when a contaminating ribosome preparation can rescue dropouts.

What would justify concentrating on this family
================================================
The decisive question is whether the necessary ribosome-assembly services can be
reconstituted from a finite, chemically defined and renewable set at a favorable
active-yield/turnover ratio. This is a concrete physical bottleneck, not permission
to assume a lysate. The existing orthogonal-ribosome activity assay and stable-
isotope tracing provide useful operations for distinguishing new activity from
seed carryover. They would have to be calibrated under the actual reactor's
retention and feed conditions. Total protein mass or persistent GFP production
does not answer that question.

A stage-two effort would need a genuinely new assembly/folding/retention
mechanism, or a quantitative minimal-service construction with real measured
activity. Merely optimizing templates, feedback or simulation scores remains
below the user's ambition. This audit does not supply that missing mechanism.
Therefore this contender is presently preserved as useful capital and a strong
negative comparison rather than recommended for the main sustained attack.
