EXACT SOURCE OPERATIONS, CONDITIONS, AND LIMITS
Retrieved 2026-11-01. These are concise paraphrases, not copied papers.

1. Mottaghi SS, Maerkl SJ. PURE makes PURE: reconstitution of the PURE cell-free
system from self-synthesized proteins. Nature Communications 17, 6756 (2026).
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73337-0
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-73337-0
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13385756/
Operation: synthesize all 36 nonribosomal proteins separately or together,
purify, exchange buffer, concentrate and reconstitute functional second-generation
PURE. Simultaneous reaction gives 28 ug recovered /350 ug original =8% protein
mass; authors identify roughly tenfold missing capacity for nondegenerative
renewal. Individual functionality can be confounded by contamination in ribosome
preparations. Purification/control and concentration changes matter; this is not
indefinite in situ closure. Authors explicitly propose dialysis among next steps.
Source data: https://zenodo.org/records/20268751 (version 3, 22 May 2026).
Includes raw fluorescence/gels. Record inspected; spreadsheets not analyzed here.
Web refs available to parent: turn187search0, turn187search1, turn183view2.

2. Kosaka Y et al. Autonomous ribosome biogenesis in vitro.
Nature Communications 16, 514 (2025).
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-55853-7
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-55853-7
Operation: coupled transcription/translation of rRNA and 54 ribosomal-protein
genes and functional assembly of both subunits. Orthogonal translation plus
femtoliter droplet readout separates nascent function from seed translation.
Critical condition: optimized E. coli S150 extract supplies soluble proteome,
including many accessory biogenesis factors. Seed ribosomes are supplied. It is
not a chemically defined self-renewing assembly machine. New ribosomal proteins
were also distinguished using isotope incorporation.
Associated droplet-image analysis code archive:
https://zenodo.org/records/14209842 (paper cited; direct record retrieval failed).
This is image analysis, not a mechanistic closure solver. Source data include
SRA PRJNA974729, jPOST JPST001809 and MetaboLights MTBLS7425.
Web refs: turn184search1, turn184search34.

3. Long-Term Protein Synthesis with PURE in a Mesoscale Dialysis System.
ACS Synthetic Biology (2025).
https://doi.org/10.1021/acssynbio.4c00618
https://pubs.acs.org/doi/full/10.1021/acssynbio.4c00618
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11744927/
Operation: commercial-component mesoscale dialysis and periodic feed exchange;
reported 1.16 mg/mL GFP and protein synthesis for at least 12.5 consecutive days.
This supplies a strong retention/feed comparator. It does not itself demonstrate
replacement of every original active catalyst. Web refs turn184search4/9/15.

4. Schwarz-Schilling et al. Bar-Ziv family localized translation-factor synthesis.
Nature Communications, published 30 December 2025, volume 17 (2026).
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-67772-8
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-67772-8
Operation: localized DNA-brush co-synthesis of 30 translation proteins; functional
thresholds and surface fluorescence measurements. DNA loading, protein ratios,
translation burden and ribosome-associated contamination are essential conditions.
Data archive https://zenodo.org/records/17546983 (record inspected, about 46.5 GB;
not downloaded). Useful physical localization operation; no full renewal claimed
here. Web refs turn151view2, turn154search2.

5. Jurado Z, Pandey A, Murray RM. Nucleotide-Level Chemical Reaction Network
Modeling Enables Quantitative Prediction of Reconstituted Cell-Free Expression
Systems. ACS Synthetic Biology (2026).
https://doi.org/10.1021/acssynbio.6c00163
https://github.com/zjuradoq/PURE_CRN_models
Pinned commit: 59f8c7b7c10b3a925d0be511e5fa8b633db7af46 (14 May 2026).
Operation: BioCRNpyler sequence-dependent mechanism, Bioscrape simulations and
calibration/inference assets. We retained original declarative models and audited
stoichiometry independently. Parameters combine prior models, experimental
inference and empirical scaling. Reporter synthesis does not certify active
regeneration. Original MIT license retained. Exact file Git blob hashes in
public_code/PURE_CRN_models/PROVENANCE.json. Web ref turn154search10.

6. Separate enzyme/cofactor family, screened before selecting the contender.
Bowie group ATP rheostat: https://doi.org/10.1038/nchembio.2418 (2017).
Phosphate-dependent switching between ATP-producing and non-ATP-producing
fluxes demonstrated energetic homeostasis during cell-free chemical production.
NADPH purge valve: https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms5113 (2014).
An enzymatic redox-balancing operation already addresses generic cofactor
overproduction. These are useful physical modules, but compatibility with PURE
and total fuel/oxygen/phosphate accounting cannot be assumed. The generic
homeostatic-cofactor proposal is therefore prior capability, not a new opening.
Web refs turn147search3/4, turn142search8.

7. Thermodynamic software/database family, surveyed but not adopted.
eQuilibrator 3.0: https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkab1106
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8728285/
Operation: thermodynamically consistent reaction free-energy estimates with
uncertainties. Existing MDF/enzyme-cost machinery already supports pathway
analysis. Relevant Zenodo datasets 3978439, 4128542, 4013788, 4010929 were identified;
no execution or data-driven claim from those assets is made here. Official code
is on GitLab, so this source family was not mistaken for a GitHub release.
