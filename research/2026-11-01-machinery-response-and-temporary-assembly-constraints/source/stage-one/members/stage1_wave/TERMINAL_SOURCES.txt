PRIMARY SOURCES FOR TERMINAL-ONLY ACTIVE ALTERNATIVE
Retrieved 1 November 2026. URLs identify actual inspected versions.

1. Real-time impedance-activated dielectrophoretic actuation for reconfigurable
   manipulation of single flowing particles. Lefevre et al., Lab Chip 24 (2024)
   5145-5154. DOI 10.1039/D4LC00622D.
   https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2024/lc/d4lc00622d
   Full accepted manuscript inspected:
   https://art.torvergata.it/retrieve/e6a89333-7e0d-4918-b15d-97bde64168bd/Lefevre_Caselli_LOC_2024.pdf
   Author record: https://art.torvergata.it/handle/2108/394079
   Electrical control; optical validation. 1.8 kHz processing; approximately
   1.5 particles/s; one particle handled at a time. Calibrated signatures and
   geometry-dependent DEP patterns. Not arbitrary complete matrix control.

2. Evolution of Electronic Circuits using Carbon Nanotube Composites.
   Massey et al., Scientific Reports 6, 32197 (2016).
   https://www.nature.com/articles/srep32197
   Full primary article inspected: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4997311/
   External electrodes and differential evolution train electrical functions in
   rearranging nanotube/liquid-crystal matter. Microscopy documents morphology;
   it is not the controller's input. 16 pads, including two inputs, two outputs
   and eight configuration electrodes. Demonstration is simple classification,
   not generic entire-response programming; long training/history effects.

3. Machine learning without a processor: Emergent learning in a nonlinear
   analog network. Dillavou et al., PNAS 121(28), e2319718121 (2024).
   DOI 10.1073/pnas.2319718121.
   Full primary article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11252732/
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.00537
   Local paired edges, gate capacitors and comparator circuits implement learning.
   Free/clamped networks are two physical copies; helper hardware is substantive.
   Zenodo record inspected: https://zenodo.org/records/12623376
   DOI 10.5281/zenodo.12623376.
   data.zip, 15.8 MB, MD5 82081580e42b8fd2f7553df11ce01c87;
   README.md, 3.1 kB, MD5 00effa94de3683837adb4eda6325eeab.
   Dataset contains figure data and analysis scripts; files not downloaded/run.
   Derived record https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.12009234
   Dryad https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.8w9ghx3vx
   No official associated GitHub repository established in this scoped search.

4. Demonstration of Decentralized, Physics-Driven Learning.
   Dillavou et al., Physical Review Applied 18, 014040 (2022).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.00275 (v4 dated 22 June 2022)
   https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevApplied.18.014040
   Earlier local physical learning construction. Source landing/abstract read.

5. Emerging Spatiotemporal Dynamics in Multiterminal Neuromorphic Nanowire
   Networks Through Conductance Matrices and Voltage Maps. Pilati et al. (2024).
   https://doi.org/10.1002/aelm.202400750
   Full author manuscript inspected:
   https://iris.polito.it/bitstream/11583/2994402/1/Adv%20Elect%20Materials%20-%202024%20-%20Pilati%20-%20Emerging%20Spatiotemporal%20Dynamics%20in%20Multiterminal%20Neuromorphic%20Nanowire%20Networks.pdf
   Their matrix is of pairwise effective two-terminal conductances with other
   ports floating. Sequential 100 mV reads; simultaneous 16-port voltage maps.
   Ag junction dynamics; no arbitrary full terminal-response target demonstration.

6. Multivariate Stochastic Approximation Using a Simultaneous Perturbation
   Gradient Approximation. Spall, IEEE TAC 37(3), 332-341 (1992).
   https://doi.org/10.1109/9.119632
   Primary author copy located:
   https://www.jhuapl.edu/spsa/pdf-spsa/spall_tac92.pdf
   Search/abstract source used for two-evaluation gradient construction; PDF
   endpoint retrieved but readable full text not recovered. No changing-plant
   global convergence claim is made from this paper.

7. Non-Hermitian fluctuations enable model-free particle manipulation.
   Zavatski, Nerson, Fleury and Martin, arXiv:2605.31151v1 (2026 preprint).
   https://arxiv.org/html/2605.31151v1
   Full PDF and supplementary notes inspected:
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.31151v1
   Optical guide-stars in actual experiment; ~2 s matrix acquisition; diagonal
   entries often suppressed. 16 driven electrodes, four grounded electrodes.
   Printed force sign independently audited in DGEM_SIGN_AUDIT.txt.

8. Optimal Multi-Objective Wave-Momentum Shaping in Scattering Media.
   Nerson et al., https://arxiv.org/html/2511.22279v1
   Exact displayed source inspected. Page displays date 24 August 2026 despite
   the arXiv identifier; DGEM cites an earlier title. Eqs.5-6 explicitly define
   inward current; Eq.14 gives positive fixed-voltage capacitance force. Version
   ambiguity is preserved rather than silently resolving the title/date.

Related initial wave, imaging, Github and Zenodo families: SOURCES.txt.

9. Circular planar graphs and resistor networks. Curtis, Ingerman and Morrow,
   Linear Algebra and its Applications 283, 115-150 (1998).
   https://doi.org/10.1016/S0024-3795(98)10087-3
   Full original author PDF inspected:
   https://sites.math.washington.edu/~curtis/cim.pdf
   Definition 1.1 specifies signed circular-minor inequalities in the inward
   current convention. Theorems 3 and 4 characterize realizable responses and
   the diffeomorphic edge-conductance parametrization of critical response cells.

10. On a Characterization of the Kernel of the Dirichlet-to-Neumann Map for a
   Planar Region. Ingerman and Morrow, SIAM J. Math. Anal. 29(1), 106-115 (1998).
   https://doi.org/10.1137/S0036141096300483
   Full primary author manuscript inspected:
   https://sites.math.washington.edu/~reu/jim_announcement/papers/imrev.pdf
   Theorem 1.1: alternating-sign circular kernel minors for a relatively compact
   simply connected C^2 planar domain and positive C^2 scalar conductivity.
   Kernel ordering differs from the discrete theorem; the column reversal is
   accounted for in ACTIVE_ALTERNATIVE.txt. Not a blanket claim about every
   finite-electrode or three-dimensional contact model.
