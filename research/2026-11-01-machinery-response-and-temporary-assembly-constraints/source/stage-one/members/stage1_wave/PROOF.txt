PARTIAL-CHANNEL WORK AMBIGUITY AND THE NEXT ACQUIRED OPERATION
Stage-one wave branch, 1 November 2026. Original construction; not a novelty claim.

Question and decision
---------------------
Can measured external waves supply mechanical intervention inside an unknown,
opaque medium without imaging the target or supplying a microscopic force model?
This would turn boundary access into a tool for contactless manipulation and
potentially repair or assembly. Its first tempting instance, collective cooling
without individual position tracking, is already supplied theoretically by Hupfl
et al. (PRL 130, 083203; PRA 107, 023112, 2023). We should not relabel that result.
Their time-dependent energy-shift operator is the strongest direct comparator.

The experimental GWS route in Orazbayev et al. (Nature Physics, 2024) physically
moves and rotates objects in disorder, but pays for a position/angle guide-star.
Marchenko single-sided virtual interior measurements provide a distinct working
operation, but need reflection acquisition plus a direct-arrival estimate. The
inspected reproduction script supplies this from an explicit velocity model.
Composition alone does not supply the unknown object's parameter derivative.

Exact obstruction
-----------------
Let H=diag(-1,1), eta in (0,1), and q be a dimensionless mechanical coordinate.
Let I be the 2x2 identity and define

  S0 = [[sqrt(eta) I,       sqrt(1-eta) I],
        [sqrt(1-eta) I,    -sqrt(eta) I]],
  D(q) = diag(exp(i a H q/2), exp(i(b-a/2)Hq)),
  S(q) = D(q) S0 D(q).

S0 is real symmetric orthogonal. D is diagonal unitary. Consequently S is
unitary and reciprocal (S transpose equals S). A network of phase leads and
lossless reciprocal beam splitters supplies this instantaneous scattering
construction. Lead-phase derivatives can arise from mechanical path changes;
the parameter speed can be scaled down to satisfy quasistatic conditions.

Drive and observe only the first two channels. The measured and unmeasured
output blocks for such input are

  A(q) = sqrt(eta) exp(i a Hq),
  B(q) = sqrt(1-eta) exp(i b Hq).

The entire A(q) history, all its derivatives, and the lost-power matrix
C=B*B=(1-eta)I are independent of b. Yet the true accessible-input work generator
and the generator formed using observed outputs only are

  Q = -i (A* A' + B* B') = [eta a+(1-eta)b] H,
  Q_A = Herm(-i A* A') = eta a H.

Thus eta=0.98, a=1 and b=0 or b=-100 give exactly identical accessible data,
with Q=0.98H and Q=-1.02H. The minimizing eigenvector of Q_A gives respectively
negative and positive work. The sign reversal is independent of the physical
convention used to call one sign cooling: an observed direction cannot have a
uniformly inferred true-work sign in this class. Only 2% output power is hidden.

This proves an information limitation for accessible S histories and lost power
alone. It does NOT exclude additional interventions that observe the mechanical
response or impose independently justified geometry/rate constraints. It does
not assert failure in the favorable restricted-channel simulations of the PRA
paper. It explains why their empirical robustness is not a general guarantee.

An explicit sufficient extra resource
------------------------------------
If the hidden block obeys B'=i K B, with K Hermitian and ||K||<=L, then

  -L C <= Q_B = B* K B <= L C.

The inequalities follow by applying -LI<=K<=LI to the vector Bv for every v.
Therefore an input v with v*(Q_A+LC)v<0 has guaranteed negative true work.
This converts a physically earned hidden phase-rate bound into a robust control
certificate. Small lost power alone cannot supply L. The example with L=100
correctly yields no certified negative direction. This bound is ordinary robust
matrix analysis, useful capital rather than the desired technological advance.

Strong existing constructions that change the next mechanism
-----------------------------------------------------------
1. Hupfl et al. already turn time-resolved full S into collective cooling without
individual coordinate observation. The 2023 numerical implementation estimates
400 S elements in 1 microsecond using an assumed 1 GHz modulator; this is an
assumption to earn, not an acquired experimental instrument. Partial channels
and readout noise were explicitly explored in favorable numerical examples.

2. Kleine et al. (Nature Communications 2025) already optimize optical stiffness
from stochastic motion: quadrant-like optical detection, resonance PSDs, 30
Zernike coefficients, and a gradient-free optimizer. It starts with a single
trapped particle and mechanically resolved directions. It is a strong comparator
for any claimed model-free stiffness optimization, not evidence of full boundary
matrix acquisition or unnamed multi-object force reconstruction.

3. Zavatski et al., arXiv:2605.31151v1 (2026 preprint), demonstrate model-free
dielectrophoretic motion from conductance derivatives, using position guide-stars.
For conduction-dominated isotropic materials (omega epsilon << sigma throughout)
in a homogeneous exterior fluid, their port identity is

  F dot delta r = -epsilon_m/(4 sigma_m) u* delta G u.

This is the equation AS PRINTED, not a sign convention endorsed here. The sign
audit in DGEM_SIGN_AUDIT.txt finds an apparent inconsistency with the inward
current convention of its cited derivation. For a complete positive input
conductance matrix, elementary fixed-voltage virtual work instead gives a plus
sign. Retain a signed coefficient until the measured terminal convention is set.

The identity follows by mapping the real conduction problem to an auxiliary
lossless dielectric problem. This is a materially different starting operation:
electrode currents are a finite boundary readout; dissipation is compatible with
force inference in the stated limit. Its experiment still buys optical tracking.
It does not establish thermal covariance calibration without a guide-star.

Root's separately developed response-Gram proposal must be compared with these
three constructions. The conductance route appears particularly worth checking:
it avoids collecting every far-field optical channel, but thermal signal size,
finite matrix acquisition, bath calibration, electrode/interface artifacts and
task attribution remain unearned. Neither this file nor its program implements
root's central invention.

Evidence status
---------------
partial_channels.py verifies exact identities and finite-difference agreement;
results.json records its run. Source and public-code inspection are documented
in SOURCES.txt. No experimental dataset was downloaded or rerun. No full-scale
wave propagation experiment was simulated. No claim of a civilization-changing
result is supported by this branch alone.
