A. Physical wave manipulation and its direct competitor
------------------------------------------------------
Orazbayev, Mallejac, Bachelard, Rotter, Fleury (2024), Nature Physics.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-024-02538-5
"Wave-momentum shaping for moving objects in heterogeneous and dynamic media."
Primary full text inspected. Actual acoustic motion/rotation using scattering
matrices; needs camera position/angle guide-star and boundary transducer arrays.

Data: https://zenodo.org/records/10207638
Record inspected: v1, 12 March 2024, 833.5 MB. Figure 1.zip MD5
3b61f7bf41c41071a100e721895dfc50; Figure 4.zip MD5
0b9d24145d55c35ba14f421d001343ea. Data not downloaded or analyzed.

Hupfl et al. (2023), PRL 130, 083203.
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.130.083203
Author PDF: https://rottergroup.itp.tuwien.ac.at/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/130.pdf
"Optimal Cooling of Multiple Levitated Particles through Far-Field Wavefront
Shaping." Full text inspected. Theory/numerics of collective cooling from
time-resolved S, without individual position tracking.

Hupfl et al. (2023), PRA 107, 023112.
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.107.023112
Author PDF: https://rottergroup.itp.tuwien.ac.at/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/131.pdf
"Optimal cooling of multiple levitated particles: Theory of far-field wavefront
shaping." Full text inspected, especially Sec.IV.B and App.F. Reduced channels
and noise tested numerically. The 1 GHz SLM / 400 elements per microsecond
acquisition estimate is hypothetical, not an experimental instrument report.

access" (2018): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5802802/

recaptcha. The required operation combines boundary reflection data with a
direct-arrival focusing estimate. This is not an interior coordinate oracle.

"Datasets for Deep Imaging Inside Scattering Media Through Virtual Spatiotemporal

The record was inspected: data_TiO2_Tb3.zip, 12.4GB, MD5
80f6ea66462024c03f6861a6557a4f55; data_usaf.zip, 14.3GB, MD5

D. Motion-based force inference and optimization competitors
-----------------------------------------------------------
Kleine et al. (2025), Nature Communications:
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-66713-9
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12749022/
"Wavefront Shaping of Scattering Forces Enhances Optical Trapping of Levitated
Nanoparticles." Primary full text inspected. Uses quadrant-like photodetection,
mechanical resonance PSDs and optimization over 30 Zernike coefficients.
Data are available by author request; no associated public code identified.

Bera et al. (2017), Scientific Reports 7, 41638:
https://www.nature.com/articles/srep41638
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5282562/
"Fast Bayesian inference of optical trap stiffness and particle diffusion."
Primary full text inspected. Exact Ornstein-Uhlenbeck transition likelihood and
sufficient statistics infer stiffness/diffusion from observed positions.
Code: https://github.com/ronojoy/pybisp
README.md inspected, blob b89333122da96d6f14deb0f26c28d4ec6e82615c.
Not run; it is a strong standard inference operation, not a full boundary-force
Gram reconstruction.

E. Consequential late lead: conductance-force mapping
----------------------------------------------------
Zavatski, Nerson, Fleury, Martin (2026 PREPRINT):
https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31151
https://arxiv.org/html/2605.31151v1
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.31151v1
"Non-Hermitian fluctuations enable model-free particle manipulation."
Full main text and full PDF supplement inspected. Reports finite-electrode DEP
control; still uses optical particle-position guide-stars. Data by author
request, no public research code located. See DGEM_SIGN_AUDIT.txt before adopting
its printed coefficient or experimental zero-diagonal matrix as an exact port
force operator.

Its cited immittance source, Nerson et al.:
https://arxiv.org/html/2511.22279v1
"Optimal Multi-Objective Wave-Momentum Shaping in Scattering Media."
Primary derivation inspected: Eqs.5-8 define input current convention, Eq.14
provides virtual work. This source allows a concrete sign audit and confirms
that constrained multi-object force optimization itself is already constructed
when the object-specific GWS derivatives are supplied.
