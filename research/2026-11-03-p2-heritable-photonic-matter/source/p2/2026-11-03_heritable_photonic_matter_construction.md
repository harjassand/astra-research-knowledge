# Source-grounded construction and failure audit: genotype-expressed abiotic photonic matter
Date: 2026-11-03, Brisbane. Status: **physical precursor mechanisms established in separate experiments; combined genotype-to-functional-PDI expression NOT demonstrated. Executed in-silico FRET selection and exact structural models only.**

## Executive scientific decision

The best current construction is a **genome-addressed, common-bath, noncovalent PDI excitonic array** on a rigid DNA six-helix-bundle (6HB). A single amplifiable DNA scaffold carries a program of local staple-recognition alleles. The same globally supplied, index-specific PDI-modified and structural oligonucleotides should form genotype-dependent 0Δ versus 4Δ PDI spacings. The material itself, under specified hydration and 465-nm illumination, supplies the output: emitted photon spectral flux. A separate stable reference signal, intended to count input origami, prevents selecting samples just for producing more particles. Recovered DNA alone is not a phenotype receipt; selected genomes must be amplified and *new* scaffolds rebuilt in new reagent preparations, with fresh spectral performance and yield counted.

The primary PDI experiment demonstrated the key physical output of closely versus distantly placed PDI molecules but used separately designed and supplied sets of staples for each configuration. It did not show allele-selective, mixed-genotype, single-reservoir expression or heritable optical selection. This distinction is decisive. The proposal is a **specific new interface to earn**, not a new achieved device or accepted novelty claim.

### Contract
- **Input:** M sequence-known or unknown variants of long ssDNA origami scaffold, each containing ten paired allele loci; common structural staples, 40 indexed PDI-modified allele-specific staple stocks (two alternatives for each of 20 PDI positions), Mg-containing origami buffers, compatible excitation optics, aqueous handling, amplification source, clonal isolation/selection machinery, dye reference and controls.
- **Output of expression:** per-genome origami rods with 20 PDI chromophores at one of two prescribed geometries in each of ten paired locations. Missing, misassembled and folded-but-wrong objects are counted in effective material yield. **Output of selection:** recovered amplifiable genotype sequence *and measured re-expression optical yield*, from distinct fresh preparation batches.
- **Functional endpoint:** a fixed-condition red-region emitted photon yield per input genome material and per incident illumination plus a spectral-band constraint. Red *fraction* alone is insufficient because coupled PDI states may be dimmer.
- **Costs:** 40 distinct PDI phosphoramidite oligonucleotide syntheses and quality control; all fixed structural staples; genotype pool construction and sequencing; 4–10 kb ssDNA scaffold production/recovery and PCR; separate folding/denaturation buffers; dyes, flow, copy numbers and gate photons; per-round empty clonal droplets and doublets; correct folded output and inherited material throughput; photo-bleaching, contamination and cross talk. No assumed price or yield assigned to unmeasured links.

## Source-anchored physical subsystems

1. **Actual spectroscopy (Hemmig et al., Nano Lett. 2016, DOI 10.1021/acs.nanolett.5b05139):** 170-bp-by-24-helix DNA breadboard, six Cy3 donor positions near Cy5 acceptor, R0=5.4 nm, distances approximately 5.2 and 5.6 nm, fitted low-fluence antenna effect AE=0.606 Σ E_FRET(r_i)+0.006. Full six-donor AE ~2, with independent sample replicates, solution-phase and ALEX single-particle methods. More than 3 donors gave AE>1. Six-site geometric map in the computational replay is an *illustrative alternating near/far pattern*, NOT the experimental location map. Original optical studies did not use amplifiable variant genomes with competing dye staples.
2. **Actual different PDI photoproducts (Gorman et al., Chem 2024, DOI 10.1016/j.chempr.2024.03.007):** 6HB origami ~150 nm, ten separated PDI dimers = 20 modified PDI staples, designed as 42-bp repeated structural units, optical measurements in aqueous dilute solution. Each tested sample used a selected set of 20 modified staples. Purified folded 6HB yield >77% in its own conditions; PDI insertion did not materially destabilise the reported 6HB, although similar PDI dimers on ordinary duplex DNA *were destabilised*. 0–3Δ dimer positions produced red-shifted excimer fluorescence; 4–5Δ reduced this feature. Source reported ~273 cm⁻¹ coupling for 0Δ / 2Δ and broadly *reduced* fluorescence quantum yields with stronger excimer signatures. This is a large physics improvement over a merely additive FRET antenna, but not an integrated genome-selected product.
3. **Copying custom origami scaffolds:** pScaf (Nafisi, Aksel, Douglas, Synthetic Biology 2018 DOI 10.1093/synbio/ysy015) produces 1,512–10,080-base custom ssDNA scaffold sequences with a 393-base fixed region; the method uses phagemid/cell production. MeRPy-PCR (Minev et al., Nucleic Acids Res 2019 DOI 10.1093/nar/gkz998) produces >7-kb ssDNA in vitro with a methanol-responsive precipitation step, demonstrated for origami folding. **Neither automatically produces thousands of ~4-kb single-genotype origami scaffolds inside sorting droplets.** RCA-produced origami scaffolds/nanoribbons are established (DOI 10.1002/marc.201800263; 10.1021/acsmaterialslett.9b00484), but strongly concatemerized RCA constructs do not certify individual 6HB-PDI monomers or clean cleavage/release.
4. **Genotype ownership/selection as a strong incumbent:** Rajwar et al., Adv Mater 2026 DOI 10.1002/adma.74852: single-stranded self-folding DNA structure genomes were recovered and amplified in iterative selection for cellular uptake, including independent follow-up measurements. Castells-Ballester et al., ACS Synth Biol 2026 DOI 10.1021/acssynbio.6c00061: clonal DNA nanoflower, in vitro protein expression, microdroplet optical sorting, DNA recovery and 400–600-fold enrichment in its *protein assay*. Neither supplies a validated PDI/6HB genotype-expression mechanism. A 2012 PNAS nanofluidic single-DNA sorter (DOI 10.1073/pnas.1117549109) reached >500 molecules/min and up to 98% classification in large-signal DNA tests, not high-confidence sorting of nearly equal-brightness seven-dye materials.
5. **Additional incumbents:** Gopinath et al., Nature 2016 DOI 10.1038/nature18287 programmed 65,536 photonic crystal cavities on one chip with DNA origami; DNA photonic-wires and pooled material screens existed well before this proposal. A 2026 nine-dye exciton collider (Schröder et al., DOI 10.1002/sstr.202500889) demonstrates many-dye nonlinear singlet annihilation, but it needs photon-correlation/time-resolved readout, not automatically high-throughput FADS. DNA-templated small molecule translation and selection are established in DNA-encoded library research (DOI 10.1002/ange.202215542).

## Molecular architecture: cis-addressed PDI position with constant dye count

**Rod:** source-class straight DNA 6HB with ten 42-bp repeat positions. Each repeat has two PDI-staple insertion loci anchored well within structural staples, never near fraying ends, following the source's stability lesson. One program bit g_j chooses a close dimer (0Δ, excimer-active source class) or a distant dimer (4Δ, weakly coupled source class). All clones receive the exact same common stock: stable structural staples and 40 site-indexed PDI-modified recognition staples (two alleles at each of 20 physical positions). A successfully decoded rod always contains 20 PDI molecules; the intended phenotype is geometry, *not varying dye count*. Dye-modified staples must be chemically synthesized and characterized upfront; they are **not** free components.

**Recognition design, executed as sequence-level candidate only:** code `pdi_common_bath_cassette_code.py` produced 40 distinct 24nt unmodified-DNA recognition domains, two per physical position, with candidate unmodified duplex Tm 69.11–73.80°C for a stipulated 12-mM Mg2+/50-mM Na+/100-nM per strand computation and max contiguous noncognate complement match 7nt. Candidate genome uses 20 such motifs (480nt variable scaffold among 40 choices); each PDI staple has a corresponding reverse-complement recognition motif. This is **not a valid PDI-staple thermodynamics prediction**: inserting the PDI into the phosphodiester backbone changes duplex thermodynamics, local deformation and binding. Worse, a 24nt complementary domain may not occupy a structural staple's complete crossover route, and the 40-staple common supply may alter folding kinetics. No caDNAno scaffold routing, PDI insertion indices, complete staple sequences, noncognate equilibrium ensemble, oxDNA check, or full fold exists. These are the exact unpaid inputs; do not substitute the motif Tm for any of them.

**Ownership:** the potentially amplifiable 6HB scaffold itself is the genotype, not a detached barcode. A live optical object contains its causal DNA sequence; selecting an optical event and recovering the attached DNA would transfer genotype with the physical material. Chemical attachment fidelity at each site is not claimed from topology; the actual optical phenotype bears the costs of all missed and wrong sites. A clone is *functionally heritable* only if an independently manufactured next generation gives the corresponding optical performance. For pooled single-genome handling, a Poisson-limited droplet must be seeded before growth; PCR/RCA must produce foldable ssDNA in the compartment, followed by compatible origami folding and PDI assembly, measurement, sorting, lysis/amplification and refolding in fresh preparations. The 2026 clonal DNA-nanoflower protein sorter demonstrates related compartmental operations but **not** droplet-local MeRPy ssDNA origami formation. The compatibility gap is genuine.

**Readout:** source PDI studies characterized fluorescence under 465-nm excitation. A product score based on the red emission fraction alone could prefer quenched dark excimers. Define physically useful per-input material flux B_red as background-corrected red spectral photons at fixed 465-nm incident flux and integration time divided by a separate, spatially isolated, invariant reference-dye photon count from the source genome. The reference is a manufacturing count, not the optical output. Its dye, placement, cross talk and survival are not experimentally acquired. A selection threshold also requires a red-band spectral constraint. Fresh isolated products must be assayed for actual absorption-normalized red photon yield η_red=Φ_F f_red, unlike the in-droplet reporter-normalized proxy. Low absorbance prevents asserting perfect single-droplet η_red measurements.

## Derivations: correlated defects and functional heredity

Let per-genome material output at source illumination be

 F(g,b,w) = Z(g,b,w) × A(g,b,w) × f(g,D(g,b,w),r(g,b,w)),

where Z is correct global fold, A is functional optical-acceptor/reference state, D is a vector of site occupancy, r molecular geometry, b is a shared batch, and w independent material disorder. The observed photon counts have conditional Poisson fluctuations and backgrounds. Do NOT set Z=1, acceptor loss=0 or replacement genotype ID equal to correct material by decree.

For linear FRET calibration, f(g,D,r)=q+Σ g_i D_i α/[1+(r_i/R0)^6]. For an exchangeable occupancy P~Beta(p κ,(1-p)κ) and D_i | P ~ Bernoulli(P), Cov(D_i,D_j)=p(1-p)/(κ+1) for i≠j. Thus
 Var(Σ_i w_i D_i) = p(1-p)Σ_i w_i² + [p(1-p)/(κ+1)] Σ_(i≠j) w_i w_j.

At equal weights this produces a nonzero variance floor p(1-p)/(κ+1) in the per-site average as n→∞. A shared global folding state adds variance f(1-f) μ² to F even if all individual sites are independently controlled. More sites do not necessarily make expression arbitrarily reproducible.

For ten physically separate PDI pairs with two modified staples per pair, if each individual staple is occupied with probability p and pair events are independent conditional on P, then M | P ~ Binomial(10,P²). Exact integration over Beta P gives a correlated occupancy distribution (see executable `pdi_pair_fidelity_and_information.py`). At the *hypothetical*, **unmeasured** p=.85 and κ=12, the all-20-perfect independent probability is .85^20=3.88%, while at least six complete dimers occur with probability 88.56% under independent-site assembly or 80.83% with correlated assembly. The pair-count variance rises 2.00 → 4.24. These **structural** occupancy probabilities are not usable red-photon yield probabilities; no threshold on M can be equated with an optical device function without spectroscopy and mixing measurements. The calculation demonstrates why 10,000 all-correct sites is the wrong capability specification.

Functional heredity is not a pointwise perfect map. A useful random-effects decomposition of genotype-mean output is
  Y_g,b,c = μ + G_g + B_b + (GB)_g,b + C_g,b,c + photon_noise.
If genotype dispersion is σ_G², genotype×batch variance σ_GB² and averaged clone/shot variance σ_e²/r, the expected fresh-preparation reproducibility of genotype ranking is roughly σ_G²/(σ_G²+σ_GB²+σ_e²/r) for balanced independent batches. This is an experimental population statistic, not a source-acquired parameter; specifically strong genotype×batch coupling can reverse ranks even when every assembly uses identical reagents. Evaluate held-out generations with batch-blocked replication, never same-bath reinjection alone.

Copy numbers K contaminate raw brightness: S_green≈K Y_g+B. A reference count R≈K r+B_r cancels K only *in expectation*, with denominator shot noise and reporter damage remaining. Conditional Cy5 ALEX detection in the 2016 experiment would ignore globally broken/acceptor-missing particles; a fair material-per-genome score requires a separate reference that counts inputs whether they fold correctly or not. The first executable v2 used an active-acceptor denominator and thus overestimated manufactured-material function. The corrected v3 and pooled-v2 models instead penalize all missing optical product while assuming an ideal one-per-genome reference dye; actual reference survival remains unearned. No "perfect assay" is asserted.

Droplet owner contamination: if initial genomes are Poisson λ, P(multiple | occupied) = 1 - λ/(e^λ−1). At λ=.05 this is ~2.48% while just 4.88% of droplets are occupied; at λ≈.002, occupied-droplet doublets drop to ~0.1% at a cost of ~500 droplets per initially occupied droplet. Selecting bright mixed droplets and taking a random recovered genotype can enrich the wrong genome. Count sort gates, empty droplets, chimeric PCR and loss, not just optical scores.

## Executed complete synthetic instance, with explicit limitations

Source-scaled first-stage FRET fixture: R0=5.4 nm, modeled alternating near/far distances 5.2/5.6 nm, fitted α=.606 and q=.006. Restrict to all 15 six-bit genotypes with exactly four Cy3 donors and one central Cy5, so brightness is **not** improved merely by choosing more dye molecules. Source-calibrated nominal optical effects fall into only three classes: 1.1534, 1.2205 and 1.2876. The source itself includes different geometries and actual sample structures; this toy alternate site map is not an empirical reproduction of all 15 arrays. Model full-six AE ≈1.8277 against ~2 in the paper.

Source-derived optics only; **all** site occupancy, folding, background, copy numbers, genotype-dependent offsets, photon counts and clonal PCR quantities are named unmeasured scenario parameters. `heritable_photonic_matter_v3.py` executes 150 independent selection campaigns in each scenario with independent fresh re-expression. In the optimistic unmeasured regime (site occupancy .94; fold .96; acceptor .96; per-reference photon scale 12; mean 140 per clone), the selection's fresh measured material-per-genome effect averages 1.1061 versus directly choosing known best geometry 1.1146, and raw-intensity selection 1.0575; two independently prepared 15-genome genotype-ranking correlation averages .676 (empirical fifth/95th .338/.808). In the stress regime (site occupancy .79; fold .85; acceptor .90; photon scale 4; mean 100 copies and larger batch/geometry variation), selection averages .7552 versus known geometry .7881, with genotype ranking correlation .127 (5th/95th -.292/.417). The favorable regime is not experimentally established; neither regime shows an advantage over equal-information design.

`pooled_photonic_selection_v2.py` adds genuine in-silico Poisson droplet loading, physical (simulated) copy-number variation, per-material two-channel Poisson photon sensing, recovery from multi-genotype droplets, mutation-free genome reweighting, two successive selection rounds, then a fresh independent material preparation. At λ=.05, 4,000 positive droplets per round require ~82,017 droplets per round; sorter reference gates also discard some positive droplets. That is *resource counting inside a simulated droplet model*, not demonstrated FADS throughput.

These values test whether the modeled physical phenotype survives independent preparation and selection. They **do not** establish successful wet genotype-to-origami expression, clonal droplet amplification, actual PDI photoproduct expression or a machine beating existing preparation methods. The model includes no favorable hidden PDI quantum-yield parameters.

## A second exact opening limit: decoupled photonic units do not form a rich materials language

The 2024 PDI rods were engineered with ten largely *decoupled* PDI dimer repeat units to amplify an ensemble spectroscopic measurement. Under the experimentally motivated but mathematically stronger idealization that all close dimers share the same spectrum C(λ), all far dimers share F(λ), and units do not interact, a 10-bit genome yields
  S_g(λ)=n_close(g) C(λ) + [10−n_close(g)] F(λ).
Therefore the 2^10=1,024 binary geometrical genotypes produce at most **11 distinct emission spectra**, not 1,024 independent optical functions. This is an exact conditional representation-capacity bound, not a physical proof that actual local sequence/batch variation is identically zero. Sequence-specific heterogeneity could increase the number of observed spectra but would need demonstrated reproducibility, not assumed useful diversity. It excludes treating combinatorial spatial addresses as combinatorial spectroscopic innovations.

To escape this obstruction, *source-realized* short-range coupling must be brought between independently configurable cells, or exciton transport/annihilation must make the output genuinely depend on spatial arrangement. For any two independently controlled genetic loci i,j define optical epistasis Δ_ij F = F(g^ij)−F(g^i)−F(g^j)+F(g). If Δ vanishes in suitably replicated physically functional emission, claims of nonlinear matter evolution via those loci collapse to direct hand design. 2026 nine-chromophore exciton collider validates nonlinear physics, but its rare-event photon coincidences require specialized detection and are not automatically useful in fluorescent microdroplet selection. A stronger coupled-PDI phase must still be physically engineered; four PDI inserts per nearby repeat could destabilize the origami and cannot inherit source's >77% folding yield.

## Competing mechanism / ingredient removal audit

**Remove the inheritable genotype and use separately supplied chosen staple recipes.** 2016 Cy3/Cy5 and 2024 PDI papers already supply the optical material and differential photophysics; source also shows folding and spectroscopy. At the 15-genotype or 11-PDI-configuration scale, common-bath genotype expression has not earned any stronger function, cost advantage, or priority. Strong manual/robotic preparation on the same samples equals or beats source-scaled FRET selection in the executed model.

**Remove genotype-caused physical architecture and merely attach DNA barcodes to samples.** Ordinary DNA-encoded library technology and DNA nanostructure selection already permit code-linked screening; a barcode that reports a separately fabricated sample does not let its descendants *recreate the material from the code under the same global reagent supply*. This is the precise candidate interface that is not cosmetic, provided allele-coded PDI folding is realized.

**Remove the actual optical readout and select genotype barcode, nanostructure presence or red emission fraction alone.** This does not establish useful material function; missing dye, red yet dim, folded yet optically dead and batch-bright false positives survive. The two-channel fluorescence and measured absolute photon output are essential physical criteria.

**Substitute the demonstrated Rajwar 2026 structure-genome screen, Castells-Ballester 2026 droplet system, or 2016 photonic-fabrication with a conventional controller.** These furnish strong separate components and competitors. No evidence in the inspected papers establishes the same integrated allele-addressed PDI photoproduct expression; equally, combining their names does not earn it.

### Highest-leverage experimental/theoretical residual, concentrated

1. **Actual cis-decoder on an origami that retains its published photophysics:** compile a complete, experimentally compatible caDNAno 6HB scaffold with competing allele-specific PDI backbone-inserted staples for just one close/far PDI pair, while all other positions use common fixed structural staples. The same mixture must produce physically different spectra for both scaffold alleles, not only different barcodes, despite competing PDI-dye stocks. Quantify gel/TEM folding yield, PDI chemical stoichiometry and fluorescence quantum yield for an allele mixture against equal-information separately batched 0Δ and 4Δ controls. If the inserted allele sequences change PDI geometry or folding so much that the spectral distinction disappears, the selected interface fails regardless of the success of the source's separate recipes.
2. **Scale in common bath and retain independent ownership:** extend to a two-locus interacting architecture *only when measured output is nonadditive*. Check correct PDI spectra per material, clone versus batch variance, and mixed-genome optical cross-contamination. The native ssDNA genome must remain recoverable from the actual optically selected structure; merely using MeRPy on already isolated single genotypes does not constitute pooled evolution.
3. **Clonal optical selection and fresh re-expression:** combine an acquired clonal amplification / ssDNA production process with one verified droplet-compatible or surface-imaging spectral selection method and an actually distinguishable reference channel. Measure input/empty droplets, PCR crossover and allele errors, material yield and fresh-batch correlated errors. Evidence of sequence enrichment without fresh measured absolute light output is not functional heredity.
4. **Higher ceiling:** after that interface works, replace isolated PDI cells with a coupled, spectrally measurable network. Demand independent spatial epistasis and a material endpoint exceeding separate-formulation controls at matched measured photons, mass, time and reagent stocks. Charge generation and durable inorganic solids are distinct targets requiring new source measurements, not accomplishments of a hydrated DNA optical reporter.

**Status:** source-bound optical chemistry and custom ssDNA preparation are available; in-silico 40-motif decoder and complete synthetic two-round inherited FRET selection are executed; the physical common-bath PDI decoder, clonal folded scaffold expression and material-dependent recovered optical heredity remain NOT EARNED. The strongest future opening is the combined *causal genotype-to-functional-material* interface, not the number of possible genomes. At present there is no demonstrated revolutionary or historical breakthrough.
