I carried the acquisition problem through to a finite-record construction for a constrained feedback intervention. The learned object is the response of an executable experimental program: its success probability and recorded completion-time distribution across a continuum of settings. It does not require identifying the hidden dynamics.

The construction now includes the command interface, finite acquisition design, estimator, simultaneous confidence band, controller selection, hard command-duration cap, and a competing joint-likelihood method trained on exactly the same records.

The mathematical construction and synthetic execution are complete. Laboratory validation is not. The remaining physical obligations—especially repeatable preparation and the interpretation of the sensor event—are explicit. The comparison also establishes an important limit: a correctly specified ordinary latent model is substantially more accurate on this test. There is no general efficiency advantage to claim.

The earlier results are preserved unchanged, including the two-equilibrium identification theorem, constructive path-response distinctions, forcing-information bounds, local-rate acquisition law, and positive finite-intervention construction.

1. The chosen capability

The question I selected is:

Can a researcher learn and choose the intervention rate of a fixed sensor-feedback program, with a finite guarantee over its entire response curve, while leaving the physical system’s hidden memory unresolved?

The concrete operation is a two-level force-command experiment. The program observes a noisy coordinate, decides when to apply the high command, and records a transition when two consecutive post-start measurements exceed a threshold. Every four-second trial has a hard limit of 1.5 seconds under the high command.

The setting being learned is the probability of a random intervention bit. The feedback logic, threshold, measurement processing, horizon, command levels, and duration cap are fixed before acquisition.

This target has a real interface. Optical-trap experiments have applied randomly switching force commands to DNA hairpins; the published apparatus reports 1 kHz acquisition, 0.1 pN force resolution, and 1 nm position resolution. The controlled variable is the force-clamp command, with the apparatus’s dynamics between that command and the measured molecular response. 
arxiv.org
 Open control software such as SmartTrap provides a further implementation route, although I did not install or operate hardware. 
GitHub

I kept the scientific meaning of the measurement precise. The acquired prediction concerns the specified recorded transition event. An extension threshold in a force-clamp experiment does not, by itself, establish a hidden folding state: beads and handles can move in response to the command. A claim about molecular conformation or continuous-time first passage needs additional measurement calibration.

Why another hidden-state reconstruction was insufficient

The strongest existing approaches already extract useful models from short, noisy single-molecule records. The recent simulation-based inference work by Dingeldein and collaborators reconstructs folding models from short constant-force measurements and explicitly discusses remaining discrepancies associated with memory or position-dependent diffusion. 
arxiv.org
+1

That made the relevant gap narrower than “learn kinetics from noisy data.” The gap pursued here is a reusable finite-data guarantee for an intervention family without assuming a particular hidden kinetic architecture.

The alternatives were developed far enough to make the selection substantive:

Route	What it supplies	What limited it here
Two-equilibrium reconstruction	Broad identification from complete observed laws	Finite law acquisition and inverse conditioning remain unsolved by the population theorem
Ordinary joint latent likelihood	Efficient use of complete noisy trajectories	Predictive guarantees depend on the model class or separate validation
Generic command importance sampling	Valid transfer under partial observation	Poor command overlap can produce exponential acquisition costs
Calibrated Gaussian force channel	Physical regularity of deterministic-force response functions	Stronger calibration assumptions and potentially poor conditioning at useful force action
Conditioned random seeds for a fixed feedback program	Directly acquired finite coefficients, continuum prediction, and certified selection	Restricted program family; preparation and experimental duration remain charged

The Gaussian-force alternative is preserved with its finite acquisition argument and bounded-actuator repair. The selected route has the cleanest connection to operations a digital controller can actually perform.

5. What was executed

The main test uses a synthetic four-state continuous-time process: a measured binary conformation coupled to a hidden slow gate. The gate changes the conformation kinetics. Measurements are noisy scalar values. Constant-command frames are advanced with exact matrix exponentials.

The direct learner receives only:

The planned random bits.
The realized commands.
The noisy sensor values.
The fixed program, threshold, sampling interval, and quota.

The generator’s true parameters and population response coefficients are confined to a separate evaluator.

The fixed program applies the high command when its quota permits and either the preceding post-start reading was high or the current intervention bit is one. It records success after two consecutive high readings and disables further high commands after success.

6. The competing route succeeded—and changes the interpretation

A separate collaborator began from conventional joint latent likelihood, rather than extending the direct estimator.

The strongest fitted model had fourteen unknown parameters: six kinetic rates, two force slopes, three initial-mixture coordinates, two sensor-mean parameters, and sensor noise. It received exactly the same 12,012 complete calibration records.

Its results were substantially better numerically:

Maximum final-deadline response error over the full parameter interval: 0.0018872.
Error bound over all fifteen deadline distributions and every setting: 0.0029556.
Actual synthetic decision regret: approximately 0.0000123.

A two-state alternative was also fitted, but the data decisively favored the four-state model. The correct model gained about 1,779 training log-likelihood units for six extra parameters, and another 1,433 held-out log-likelihood units on the same fresh records. The weaker fit is a rejected diagnostic, not the principal comparator.

The feedback likelihood needed one careful distinction. Commands depend on earlier observations, so the appropriate objective is the causal forward likelihood along the realized actions. The known seed distribution and program-consistency factors are parameter independent. Treating the whole future command word as an independent covariate would be the wrong justification.

Two conclusions follow.

First, the direct construction has no demonstrated accuracy advantage over correct joint likelihood. A correct kinetic model uses much more trajectory information and can support predictions beyond this fixed program family.

Second, a model-based method can propose a controller and validate that controller with fresh on-policy trials even if its model is wrong. It must receive the same validation budget.

The distinctive guarantee here is therefore narrower:

One count-designed acquisition campaign supplies a simultaneous response and deadline-distribution certificate for the entire fixed-program family, without assuming the hidden kinetic architecture.

An ordinary saturated likelihood using this same reduction obtains exactly that guarantee too.

8. What this contributes to the research programme

The fixed-count/binomial identity has a close established precedent: Newman–Ziff percolation computes fixed-count observables and combines them with binomial weights to obtain an entire parameter-response curve. Its efficient incremental lattice operations do not transfer to unknown physical trajectories; each prepared experimental episode here remains charged. 
arxiv.org
 The coverage design and polynomial norming machinery are established as well.

I therefore do not claim a new statistical identity, universal controller, or demonstrated civilisation-changing discovery.
