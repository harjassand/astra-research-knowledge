FINITE-RING COLLECTIVE RESPONSE FROM AN ACQUIRED DEFECT EXCURSION

Independent complementary derivation for the root's collective-response work.
This is an ordinary mathematical derivation, not a priority or novelty claim.

1. Physical system and measured response

Take a periodic chain of n>=3 Ising spins, Gibbs weight proportional to
exp(J sum_i sigma_i sigma_(i+1)), with J>0. Set t=tanh J in (0,1),
gamma=2t/(1+t^2). The generator K is positive, K=-L, and its physical rates are

  w_i(sigma)=nu/2 * (1+eta sigma_(i-1)sigma_(i+1))
                      * [1-gamma sigma_i(sigma_(i-1)+sigma_(i+1))/2],

with uniform clock nu>0 and -1<eta<1. The endpoint multiplier is positive and
unchanged when sigma_i flips, hence these rates obey detailed balance exactly.
For eta != 0, acting on one spin produces a cubic monomial, so the magnetization
equation does not close. In domain-wall variables this is diffusion with pair
creation and annihilation, with rates

  hop       = nu(1-eta)/2,
  creation  = nu(1+eta)(1-gamma)/2,
  annihilate= nu(1+eta)(1+gamma)/2.

The free-fermion equality creation+annihilate=2 hop fails unless eta=0.
The observable is the total magnetization M=sum_i sigma_i and its integrated
equilibrium response chi=<M,K^-1 M>. This is a collective observable and every
site has a non-Gaussian physical rate when eta != 0. No clock hierarchy is used.

2. Acquired equilibrium quantities

Let T_(a,b,c)=sum_i sigma_i sigma_(i+a) sigma_(i+a+b), where a,b,c are positive
integers with a+b+c=n. The three cyclic gap descriptions give the same T. Let
T_c=T_(1,1,n-2). Direct periodic Ising parity moments give

  v_n = Var(M)/n = (1+t)/(1-t) * (1-t^n)/(1+t^n),

  alpha_(a,b,c) = Cov(M,T_(a,b,c))/Var(M)
    = [t^a+t^b+t^c-t^(a+b)-t^(b+c)-t^(c+a)]/(1-t^n).

Write alpha=alpha_(1,1,n-2), R=T_c-alpha M. Then <M,R>=0 and

  KM=kappa M+nu eta R,
  kappa=nu[1-gamma(1+eta)+eta alpha].

Equivalently,

  kappa/[nu(1-gamma)] = 1-eta t^2
                  +eta t^(n-2)(1-t^4)/(1-t^n).

This is the exact Rayleigh coefficient of the physical K on M, not a guessed
effective mobility. Positivity follows independently from the physical
Dirichlet form. Its low-temperature mobility tends to nu(1-eta), matching the
domain-wall hop rate rather than the bulk flip multiplier nu(1+eta).

3. Exact positive covariance row

For any positive gaps a+b+c=n, let R_(a,b,c)=T_(a,b,c)-alpha_(a,b,c) M. Then

  Q(a,b,c) := Cov(R,R_(a,b,c))/n
    = (1-t^2)^2/(1-t^(2n))
       * [t^(a+b-2)(1-t^(2c))
          +t^(b+c-2)(1-t^(2a))
          +t^(c+a-2)(1-t^(2b))].                       (1)

In particular all entries are positive. There is no unearned sign assumption.

Derivation: for one specified three-spin monomial, the difference between its
correlation with sigma_(i-1)sigma_i sigma_(i+1) and with sigma_i vanishes unless
i is one of its three occupied sites. Summing the three exceptional sites gives

  Cov(T_c-M,T_(a,b,c))/n
     = (1-t^2)/(1+t^n)
         * [sum_cyclic t^(a+b-2)-sum_cyclic t^a].

Meanwhile

  (1-alpha)*(1+t)/(1-t)
    =(1-t^2)(1-t^(n-2))/(1-t^n).

Combining these with the explicit alpha_(a,b,c) gives (1).

Set ell=-log t. The bracket in (1) is

  2 t^(n-2) [sinh(a ell)+sinh(b ell)+sinh(c ell)].

Because sinh is convex on positive arguments and a,b,c>=1 sum to n, its maximum
occurs at a permutation of (1,1,n-2). Thus the entire covariance row is bounded
by its contact diagonal:

  0 < Q(a,b,c) <= q_n := Var(R)/n
     = (1-t^2)^2/(1-t^(2n))
        * [1-t^(2n-4)+2t^(n-3)(1-t^2)].               (2)

4. Reference inversion is an acquired killed walk, with no hidden backend

Let G be the unit-clock Glauber generator, eta=0 and nu=1, at the same Gibbs
equilibrium. G M=(1-gamma)M. Its action on the orthogonalized cubic sums is closed:

  G R_(a,b,c)=3 R_(a,b,c) -(gamma/2) sum_six R_neighbor.

The six neighbors move one unit from one gap to an adjacent gap. A zero gap is
an absorbing collision: the corresponding three-spin product reduces to M,
and its orthogonalized R is zero. Thus the coefficient operator H is

  H=3(1-gamma) I + gamma D,

where D is the symmetric continuous-time six-neighbor gap walk on the positive
integer triangle a+b+c=n, each hop rate 1/2, killed on a zero gap. H is an
explicit finite M-matrix. Its inverse has nonnegative entries.

One need not solve the O(n^2) system for an upper bound. The polynomial

  h(a,b,c)=abc/(gamma n)

vanishes at every collision face. Direct application of the six-neighbor walk
gives D(abc)=n, so

  H h = 1+3(1-gamma)abc/(gamma n) >=1.

The discrete maximum principle gives H^-1 1 <= h. At the contact triple,

  sum_y (H^-1)_(contact,y) <= (n-2)/(gamma n).          (3)

This is an explicit acquired excursion bound, uniform in the many-body state
count and in the critical relaxation time. It does not assume a fast mixing
time of the full spin system. Redundant cyclic descriptions of a triple do not
invalidate the construction: the coefficient identity is linear, and applying
G to the reconstructed function gives the desired source exactly.

Equations (1)-(3) give

  <R,G^-1R> <= Var(R)*(n-2)/(gamma n).                 (4)

The root independently constructed a different killed-walk supersolution from
the minimum of pairwise gap products. The smooth abc/n polynomial provides a
slightly smaller contact factor and a direct finite-ring boundary condition.

5. The physical nonlinear response enclosure

Pointwise physical multipliers imply K >= nu(1-|eta|) G as quadratic forms.
Use trial observable u=M/kappa. The exact residual is

  r=M-Ku=-(nu eta/kappa)R.

The variational completion-of-squares identity is

  chi=Var(M)/kappa + <r,K^-1 r>.

Combining the acquired reference inverse bound with (4) therefore proves

  Var(M)/kappa <= chi <= [Var(M)/kappa]*(1+delta_n),

  delta_n = eta^2/(1-|eta|)
             * [q_n/v_n] * [(n-2)/n] / [gamma*(kappa/nu)].  (5)

Every quantity is an explicit rational expression in t,eta,nu and integer n.
No response coefficient, spectral gap of the physical nonlinear process,
compressible state tensor, or favorable simulation variance is supplied.

6. Critical collective family and the earned resource improvement

Choose exp(2J)=n, equivalently t=(n-1)/(n+1), so the static correlation length
is of order n. Then

  gamma=(n^2-1)/(n^2+1),
  v_n ~ n tanh(1),
  q_n ~ 16/n^2,
  kappa/nu ~ 2(1-eta)/n^2,

and, for fixed -1<eta<1,

  delta_n ~ [8 eta^2 / ((1-|eta|)(1-eta)tanh(1))]/n.    (6)

Thus fixed-order trial response gains a provable relative accuracy as the
system becomes increasingly collective and its nonlinear interaction changes
remain extensive. This is not an interaction-word expansion. The central
operation is the positive, explicitly solved three-defect excursion control.

At fixed physical n,J, (5) has a finite width; it is not an arbitrary-accuracy
solver by itself. Full response series or additional acquired correctors would
be needed to refine beyond it. The theory is for this homogeneous zero-field
ferromagnetic ring and the specified positive kinetic family. It does not
currently extend to fields, higher dimension, disorder, arbitrary observables,
or kinetic modifiers approaching zero with n.

7. Infinite-chain limit at fixed t

As n tends to infinity at fixed t, alpha->2t-t^2, q_n->(1-t^2)^2, and the bound
becomes

  kappa=nu(1-gamma)(1-eta t^2),
  delta <= eta^2(1-t^4)
                  /[(1-|eta|)gamma(1-eta t^2)].

The exact infinite-chain covariance row is

  Cov(R,R_(a,b))/n=(1-t^2)^2 t^(a+b-2).

The infinite wedge supersolution h(a,b)=ab/gamma supplies the same finite
contact excursion time. This order of limits controls the response per site.

8. Prior and interpretation

The reference closure is Glauber's kinetic Ising model. Dirichlet-form
comparison, variational residual identities, and killed random walks are
standard tools. Low-temperature dynamic universality of kinetic Ising models
is also established scientific territory. The mathematical result above is
the explicitly acquired finite-n scalar response enclosure for the specified
nonfree extensive rate family, with its complete covariance sign and excursion
cost proved. A literature priority claim requires further primary-source work.

Governing primary source actually read: S.B. Dutta, M. Henkel, H. Park,
"Kinetics of a non-glauberian Ising model: global observables and exact results,"
J. Stat. Mech. (2009) P03023, https://arxiv.org/pdf/0901.3428 . Their equations
(4)-(6) specify the same general parity/spin-reversal symmetric rates, with
their delta=eta and their gamma=(1+eta)*gamma in this note. Their exact global
two-variable construction, equations (20)-(22), applies on the KDH line
eta=gamma/(2-gamma). This is a powerful conventional solution on that line,
not a missing capability. It has eta->1 at low temperature and exponent z=4;
the fixed-contrast hypothesis in the present theorem excludes that limit.
Their equations (13)-(14) show the extra spaced-triple term obstructing this
closure for generic parameters. The general rate family and its domain-wall
interpretation are therefore prior machinery, not inventions here.

Executable independent checks are in critical_ring_audit.py and its results.

9. A general finite-ring odd-defect excursion backend

The inverse primitive extends beyond three defects. Let k>=3 be odd, and let
g_0,...,g_(k-1)>0 be the cyclic gaps between k ordered defects, summing to n.
Define

  f_k(g)=sum g_i g_j g_l,

where the sum is over triples i<j<l for which all three cyclic index spacings
j-i, l-j, and k-l+i are odd. Let h_k=f_k/n and set h_1=0. Then

  h_k>=0,                  D h_k=(k-1)/2.

Here D moves one unit between adjacent gaps, each directed transfer at rate
1/2. To prove the identity, note that f is a multilinear cubic. The contribution
of a pair of opposite transfers between adjacent gaps i,i+1 is exactly
partial_i partial_(i+1) f. In the sum over adjacent pairs, each gap appears
exactly (k-1)/2 times. Therefore D f=n(k-1)/2.

Crucially this potential has the correct annihilation boundary, not just a
zero value at first collision. If g_0=0, the two coincident defects annihilate,
and the neighboring gaps g_(k-1),g_1 merge. Terms containing g_0 vanish. The
two merging gaps have identical coefficients in the remaining alternating
triples and never appear together; the other parity spacings are preserved.
Consequently

  f_k(0,g_1,...,g_(k-1))
     = f_(k-2)(g_(k-1)+g_1,g_2,...,g_(k-2)).

This proves compatibility with every subsequent degree-reducing collision.
For the positive annihilating dual that is killed only when one defect remains,
the coefficient operator is H=gamma D+k(1-gamma) on the k-defect stratum. Hence

  H(h/gamma)=(k-1)/2+k(1-gamma)h/gamma >=1

on every nonabsorbed stratum. Its positive Green kernel therefore has acquired
total mass at most h(initial)/gamma. For k=3 this is exactly abc/(gamma n).

For a fixed local initial pattern of diameter R<n, there is one external gap
n-R. Any monomial of f contains at least two of the other gaps. Bounding their
pair sum by R^2/2 and their triple sum by R^3/6 gives the simple size-independent
bound h <= R^2/2+R^3/(6n) <=2R^2/3. Sharper constants are available from the
explicit polynomial. No growing many-body relaxation time enters this bound.

Every global odd spin monomial orthogonalized against M obeys this acquired
reference dual: collision reduces the monomial degree by two, and the final
one-spin global monomial is M, which the orthogonalization removes. The full
inverse hierarchy through all odd degrees up to a fixed source degree is
therefore covered by this polynomial supersolution.

This does NOT by itself prove a general finite-range nonlinear-kinetic response
theorem. A corresponding acquired covariance-row bound and the physical
source decomposition are still required. The completed response result in
sections1-7 uses the exact three-defect row (1), whose sign and maximum have
been proved; no analogous general covariance assumption is imported.

10. Constructive conventional comparison changes the selection

The conventional collaborator subsequently supplied a stronger comparison than
the fixed multiplier bounds. For the acquired Glauber potential u0=M/(1-gamma),
both its trial potential and its physical reference current are explicit. If
the target multiplier is s on equal-neighbor updates and o on opposite-neighbor
updates, the energy-weighted equal-neighbor fraction is

  p=(1-t^2)(1+t^(n-2))/(2(1-t^n)).

The conventional potential/current pair yields exactly

  chi0/[s p+o(1-p)] <= chi <= chi0[p/s+(1-p)/o],

with relative width p(1-p)(s-o)^2/(s o). At exp(2J)=n this width is already
O(1/n), with all information acquired from equilibrium local marginals and no
three-defect inverse. Thus the leading asymptotic capability in sections1-7
is available conventionally. It should not be selected as a major new opening
on the strength of its scaling or closed formula alone.

The exact covariance and excursion constructions remain useful, including
better finite-accuracy reference-inverse information, and they led to the
positive all-order TARGET dual developed in positive_dual_potential.txt. The
all-order route must additionally earn its accuracy-versus-work control;
finite mean excursion cost alone is not that achievement.
