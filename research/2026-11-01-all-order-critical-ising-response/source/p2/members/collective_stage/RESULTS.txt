ALL-ORDER RESPONSE OF A COLLECTIVELY SLOW INTERACTING CHAIN
1 November 2026

Main finding

The interaction-word barrier is removed for a concrete nonlinear kinetic-Ising
family. The full magnetization response, including every interaction order,
has an explicit randomized algorithm with expected arithmetic cost

  O_b(n log n delta^-2 log(1/eta))

before integer precision factors. Delta is requested relative error and eta
the failure probability. The model has correlation length proportional to n
and collective relaxation time proportional to n^2. The operation uses no
2^n-state probability vector and does not simulate all physical spin events.

This is a scoped mathematical and executable advance. It is not yet a general
theory for interacting matter, and historical novelty has not been established.
The strongest conventional constructions remain part of the result: some are
substantially faster at moderate accuracy. The meaningful acquired step is an
all-order work/variance bound, not a claim to invent positive duality or
importance sampling.

Exact scope

The physical chain has zero field, periodic boundary, uniform microscopic
clocks, ferromagnetic nearest-neighbor equilibrium, and rates

 w_i=(1-b sigma_(i-1)sigma_(i+1))/2
       *[1-gamma sigma_i(sigma_(i-1)+sigma_(i+1))/2],
 gamma=tanh(2J), 0<b<1/2, gamma(1-b)>b.

The modification acts at every site and preserves the common Gibbs equilibrium.
Its rate contrast is fixed, (1+b)/(1-b). The executed collective sequence is
b=1/3 and exp(2J)=n. All rate and equilibrium inputs used computationally are
rational: t=(n-1)/(n+1), gamma=(n^2-1)/(n^2+1).

The observable is chi=<M,K^+M>=integral Cov(M(0),M(t))dt, M=sum sigma_i.
Var(M)=Theta(n^2) and chi/Var(M)=Theta(n^2). Thus the observable carries the
collective slow dynamics. Fields, arbitrary local kinetic modifications,
conservation and higher-dimensional lattices are outside this theorem.

What was constructed

The exact action of the nonlinear target generator on odd spin products is
a positive process of hopping, branching and pair annihilation, with killing
kappa N, kappa=(1-b)(1-gamma). All odd particle counts remain present. The
packed triple is the exact local departure state when a singleton branches;
it is not a retained three-defect sector.

Eliminating singleton waiting exactly gives

 chi/Var(M)=(1+b R)/[kappa(1+b U)],
 U=[H^-1 N](packed3), R=[H^-1 alpha](packed3),
 alpha(S)=Cov(M,product_S sigma)/Cov(M,sigma_0).

Alpha is acquired from a positive finite transfer formula in the cyclic gaps.
No dynamical coefficient, slow manifold, tensor rank or unknown corrector is
supplied. Directed dyadic arithmetic encloses the formula.

Ordinary excursions have finite mean length, but that does not solve the
variance problem. A positive transform based on the cubic h=F3/n gives bounded
terminal scores. The remaining cost was then proved, rather than declared
harmless: an explicit Ucost, a positive quintic Q=F5/n, and their branching
identities yield

 Wcost=(3Ucost+2Q)/d+[3b u0/(d epsilon)]h,
 Kint Wcost>=N h,

where d=gamma(1-b), epsilon=(d-b)/3+2b/n and
u0=(n-2)(n-1)/(6n). Therefore

 E_h events <=3u0/(d h0)*(1+b h0/epsilon), h0=(n-2)/n.

For b=1/3 on the collective sequence this approaches3n. This proves the cost
of the same transformed process whose score is bounded. It does not assume a
raw-excursion second moment, a few-particle cutoff or a favorable sign problem.

The root implementation maintains h in an AVL tree of alternating signed
moments. Rank selection and local updates take O(log n) integer operations.
A constant rejection envelope samples the transformed row without enumerating
all outgoing moves. The transition law uses only exact integer comparisons.
Streaming accumulation retains the current dual state, four sums/squares and
a bounded score cache. Working state can still contain up to n particles in
the worst case; no uniform small-population assertion is made.

Discriminating verification

Independent implementations constructed the complete physical spin generator
and the all-odd translation-quotiented dual through n16. They agree to below
4.8e-15 in the normalized response, with physical equation residual below
2.5e-12. These floating checks validate implementations; they are not the
source of the theorem's confidence guarantee.

Every odd subset through n11 passed exact Fraction tests of the cubic,
quartic-kernel and quintic identities, collision boundaries, positivity, the
pointwise work inequality and transition ratios. A separate generic-polynomial
audit checks the branching coefficients through17 particles. The ordinary
all-particle proof is retained, so these tests do not substitute for induction.

The streaming and histogram implementations agree exactly in all four score
sums/squares, interval endpoints, event counts and proposal counts on the
same10,000-path n16 test. Earlier exploratory confidence calculations had an
insufficient union allocation and an interval-variance issue. They are marked
SUPERSEDED_EXPLORATORY_OUTPUT. The final certificate uses four separately
bounded dyadic variables, four one-sided tail budgets, rational logarithm
bounds and outward integer square roots.

What the conventional constructions changed

1. Product-equilibrium support cones remove the word count, but ordinary
   matrix-free preconditioned CG already supplies the same operation. It is
   preserved as a successful comparator, not claimed as the central advance.

2. Conditional elimination on an interacting one-dimensional Gibbs chain was
   completed with exact averaged rates, fields, site/bond observables and
   local Schur bounds. Conditional coloring and conventional Schur algebra
   supply almost the same service. The algorithm and exact audits survive;
   a broad advantage claim does not.

3. On the critical chain, the explicit reference potential and current already
   obtain leading response universality. The three-defect reference inverse
   improves finite accuracy through established Gauss--Radau machinery. The
   new all-order construction addresses the remaining accuracy dependence.

4. Conservation alone was not a sufficient new opening. The conventional
   constructor implemented local Ritz upper bounds and exact feasible-flow
   lower bounds for the infinite nongradient exclusion process
   c_(i,i+1)=1+alpha eta_(i-1)eta_(i+2), density1/2. At contrast2 it obtained
   D in[1.2277988910688737,1.227810257121883], relative width9.26e-6.
   At contrast10 it obtained[2.3517170727218555,2.3576744492485107].
   Exact rounding repair and a convergent finite-box fallback were implemented.
   Their exponential support cost remains visible; the present Ising theorem
   does not remove that different obstruction.

5. The new Wcost can also support a conventional raw-regeneration estimator:
   it yields an O(n) second-moment bound that was previously missing. A
   compatible static-transfer aggregate gives the same asymptotic cost on
   paper. This reinforces that the acquired positive potential bridge is the
   reusable result. It is not an exclusive advantage of the Doob sampler.

No universal lower bound against all conventional acceleration is claimed.
First-passage kinetic Monte Carlo is a serious unresolved comparator; its
complete same-observable error/cost has not been acquired here.

Sources and scientific status

Positive double-branching duality, swapping-voter recurrence, the Herman cubic
and quintic, general Markov importance sampling, and Gauss--Radau bounds are
existing machinery. We read their governing conditions. The new construction
here is the branching-polynomial work bridge, its use in the complete killed
response, and the acquired finite-arithmetic algorithm. Targeted searches did
not establish historical priority; external proof review remains to be done.

The strongest remaining research question is whether this positive polynomial
control of both response and computational work survives a materially broader
class of interacting matter. The present result provides a fully constructed
case and an operation to generalize; it does not yet establish a
civilisation-changing general predictor.
