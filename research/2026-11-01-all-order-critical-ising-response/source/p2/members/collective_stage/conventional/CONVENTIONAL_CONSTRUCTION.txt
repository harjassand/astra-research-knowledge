CONVENTIONAL CONSTRUCTION AND COMPARISON
Date: 2026-11-01

1. Whole task and actual positive competitor

For a reversible finite Markov generator K=-L and centered observable f, acquire
chi=<f,K^+f> without a full many-body state or a trajectory through every event.
For uniform product equilibrium and local endpoint-independent flip rates
  w_i(sigma)=nu_i A_i(sigma), 0<m<=A_i<=M,
the standard preconditioned conjugate-gradient/Ritz construction solves this
task with a completely explicit sparse Walsh backend. It does not enumerate
interaction words, assume tensor ranks, or sample signed walks.

Let chi_S=product_{i in S} sigma_i. These are an orthonormal basis. Write
  A_i=sum_B a_iB chi_B, i not in B.
Then
  K chi_S=2 sum_{i in S} nu_i sum_B a_iB chi_{S xor B}.
Every coefficient is obtained from the microscopic rate truth table.
The reference D=K0 is diagonal, D_S=2 sum_{i in S} nu_i for S nonempty.
Dirichlet comparison gives mD<=K<=MD. Consequently
  B=D^(-1/2) K D^(-1/2), b=D^(-1/2)f
has spectrum in [m,M], independently of every site-clock ratio.

Conventional CG is applied to By=b. Its dictionary consists only of actually
generated Walsh supports; each multiply combines all paths reaching the same
support. Coefficients are never dropped by a size tolerance. The code stops
and reports a term cap if reached. No slow-manifold approximation is supplied.

2. Complete deterministic response bounds

For any trial y, put r=b-By and
  L=2<b,y>-<y,By>.
Completing the square gives
  chi-L=<r,B^(-1)r>.
Therefore
  L+||r||^2/M <= chi <= L+||r||^2/m.
The executable conservatively prints [L,L+||r||^2/m]. Its residual is recomputed
from b-By, rather than trusting only the CG recurrence.

CG minimizes the energy error over its Krylov space. Starting at zero,
  chi-L_k <= 4 q^(2k) chi,
  q=(sqrt(M/m)-1)/(sqrt(M/m)+1).
The reported residual certificate obeys
  ||r_k||^2/m <= (M/m)(chi-L_k).
Thus logarithmically many iterations suffice at fixed contrast. A fixed
Chebyshev polynomial is contained in the same Krylov space, so CG's Ritz lower
bound cannot be worse at equal maximum polynomial degree in exact arithmetic.
Its data-adaptive coefficients and inexpensive actual residual frequently
reduce the necessary degree.

3. Full representation cost, and its scope

A local source supported in an interval of length ell, under a radius-r local
generator, produces supports only in its radius-rk neighborhood after k
multiplications. For a sum of O(n) bounded-size local sources in one dimension,
the dictionary has at most O(n 2^(ell+2rk)) supports, not n^k interaction words.
Each support has O(ell+2rk) sites and O((ell+2rk)2^(2r)) outgoing terms. Sparse
site-list keys use O((ell+2rk)log n) bits. Together with CG convergence this gives
a deterministic polynomial-in-n-and-1/accuracy backend at fixed local range
and fixed contrast. The clock ratio affects input/output precision, not the
spectral iteration count.

The present Python backend encodes each sparse support by a global Python
integer bit mask, which is convenient but has O(n) bits for a support near site
n. Its measured times are honest; a sharp bit-complexity linear-in-n claim
requires sparse tuple or local-interval keys, not this particular encoding.
No assertion is made that the measured n=64/256 time ratio is an asymptotic
linear scaling proof.

In dimension d>1, a direct geographic-cone bound has 2^O(k^d) subsets. In a
general graph the ball volume can grow faster. In any dimension one can also
bound by connected update histories, but that does not automatically give a
polynomial in 1/accuracy. The one-dimensional fixed-contrast scope matters.

All printed brackets use float64 evaluation of an exact-arithmetic bound.
They are numerical validations, not outward-rounded machine certificates.
Residual drift is reported, and independent full-state solves check small
instances. Root's separate finite-precision construction is required for a
formal bit-level certificate. No sampling error is present.

5. What this successful competitor changes

An inverse-polynomial support-cone construction for this product-equilibrium
class is useful and removes extensive word enumeration, but PCG with the same
acquired local representation already provides the complete response service.
The claim should be an explicit tractable-class bridge, not superiority over
equally informed ordinary linear algebra or a new principle of inverse decay.

With all clocks equal, comparison implies gap(K)>=2m nu. Fixed m therefore
excludes system-size-growing collective relaxation in this nonconserved class.
An extensive observable can be genuinely nonlinear in its kinetic response,
but extensivity alone does not earn an emergent hydrodynamic or glassy slow
mode. Clock stiffness is a distinct difficulty. Conserved or genuinely Gibbs-
interacting dynamics require an additional bridge.

6. Conventional machinery beyond the product class

The reversible variational formula is general:
  chi=sup_u [2<f,u>-<u,Ku>].
For any comparison K>=mK0 one has the exact a posteriori bound
  chi-L(u)<=m^(-1)<f-Ku,K0^+(f-Ku)>.
This only becomes an algorithm after the reference inverse norm is acquired.
A Thomson flow upper bound is also exact, but a trial gradient alone does not
improve a fixed exact flow: if div J0=f, then
  L(u)+||J0-c grad u||_(c^-1)^2 = ||J0||_(c^-1)^2.
Independent divergence-free cycle corrections are essential to improve it.

For product-equilibrium conservative speed-change exclusion, current primary
research already constructs density-independent local correctors and proves
quantitative convergence of their conductivity; see source notes below.
Arbitrary local correctors still have 2^O(L^d) coefficients, and an asymptotic
diffusivity is not automatically a finite-size integrated-response certificate.
Actual materials software VKMC uses variational relaxation vectors/cluster or
neural representations from equilibrium single-transition data. Its variational
bound does not make the representation error or sampling confidence free.

7. Interacting Gibbs: constructive audit and useful obstruction

For two spins, pi proportional exp(J sigma1 sigma2), t=tanhJ, v=1-t^2,
  K=nu1(I-E1)+nu2(I-E2)
where Ei conditions on all spins except i. The orthonormal innovation basis
  e1=sigma1, e2=(sigma2-t sigma1)/sqrt(v)
gives on the linear sector
  H=[[nu1*v, -nu1*t*sqrt(v)],
     [-nu1*t*sqrt(v), nu1*t^2+nu2]].
Preconditioning by diag(nu1,nu2) has entry B22=1+t^2 nu1/nu2. Arbitrary clocks
therefore invalidate a fixed spatial-order diagonal innovation preconditioner
even at fixed weak J. Slow-first ordering removes this two-spin obstruction.

For any positive binary joint law and ordering v1,...,vn, define
  psi_i=(sigma_vi-E[sigma_vi|earlier]) / sqrt(Var[sigma_vi|earlier]).
Products psi_S are EXACTLY orthonormal: successively integrate the highest
remaining index; its conditional first moment is0 and second moment1.
Consider D_S=sum_{i in S}nu_vi. Full-state probes for n<=8 show physical order
can have condition number10^12--10^15 with8 decades of clock spread; slow-first
order in the tested open Ising chains gives condition1.4--1.9 atJ=.6 and<5.6
atJ=1.2. These are evidence, not a uniform theorem or efficient backend.

In a 1D Gibbs chain, each conditional innovation depends only on the current
spin and the nearest already-conditioned separators to its left and right.
Those separators can be physically distant. Applying the conjugated physical
generator may still introduce long ancestor expansions. Compact application,
uniform spectral equivalence and finite-precision control remain unearned.

A precise reduction for root's next attempt: let Ni be the commuting projection
onto innovation products containing i. If, for every fast suffix k,...,n,
  c sum_{i>=k}Ni <= sum_{i>=k}Hi <= C sum_{i>=k}Ni
holds uniformly, the arbitrary-clock comparison follows by layer-cake since
nu_i are nondecreasing. Ordinary conditional Poincare only compares the middle
sum to I-E_prefix; it does NOT automatically control the chaos-number sum Ni.

The latter is a second positive conventional construction: in a zero-field open
nearest-neighbor Ising chain with arbitrary couplings and site refresh clocks,
the linear spin span is exactly invariant. With f=a^T sigma, equilibrium
covariance C, and v_i=E Var(sigma_i|all other spins), its Dirichlet matrix is
H_ij=delta_ij nu_i v_i. Therefore
  chi=(C a)^T H^(-1)(C a)=sum_i (C a)_i^2/(nu_i v_i).
The covariance product C a is computed in two O(n) transfer recurrences, since
C_ij=product_{edges between i,j} tanh J_edge. The local variances v_i are exact
two-neighbor averages. This requires no long trajectory, matrix factorization,
or many-body state. It fails as an exact closure after generic kinetic
modulation, fields, or multispin equilibrium interactions are introduced.
For J_i=.7+.2cos(.63i) and nu_i=10^(-12*((17i)mod n)/(n-1)), f=total/sqrt(n),
n=65536 response1.3844018122051318e12 took0.0883sec. Independent n8,4-decade
full-state response11635.909036727862 agrees with formula11635.909036728406.
Numbers are float64 evaluations. This is a particularly strong reason not to
select a linear Ising source as evidence that an interacting-clock capability
was previously inaccessible.
