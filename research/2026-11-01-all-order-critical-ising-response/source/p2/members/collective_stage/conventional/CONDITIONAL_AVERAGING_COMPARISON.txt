CONDITIONAL AVERAGING: STRONG CONVENTIONAL FINITE-RESPONSE COMPARISON
Date: 2026-11-01

Outcome
-------
An equally informed conventional construction -- conditional averaging,
conditional product Poincare, two-coloring, variational bounds and ordinary PSD
Schur algebra -- acquires essentially the same complete finite-accuracy scalar
response as the proposed local-monomial construction on the collective test.
This does not establish that one cited paper or package already implements the
whole combination. It demonstrates that the available conventional ingredients
are constructive, including the accuracy bound, on the same microscopic input.

1. Exact reduction shared by both constructions

In L2(pi), split fast and slow flips, K=KF+KS, with P conditional expectation
given slow spins, Q=I-P. Let F=KF|Q, A=PKSP, B=QKSP, C=QKSQ.
Then K has block matrix [[A,B*],[B,F+C]], and KS>=0 implies
  C>=B A^+ B*.
For f=p+q, p=Pf, put a=<p,A^+p>, b=<q,F^-1q>.
If B*F^-1B<=theta A, eliminating A first gives
  chi=a+<q-B A^+p,(F+C-B A^+B*)^-1(q-B A^+p)>.
Since the inverse is bounded above by F^-1,
  a<=chi<=(1+theta)a+b+2sqrt(theta a b).
This is a direct block variational/Schur-complement bound, valid for alltheta>=0.
It is tight given only a,b,theta: take scalar A,F with C=B A^-1 B* and choose
the relative source sign to maximize the cross term.

The slow chain A is exactly the ordinary conditional averaged chain. Checkerboard
decimation of a nearest-neighbor Gibbs chain preserves nearest-neighbor Gibbs
potentials, including fields. Its averaged slow flips depend on only three
retained spins. A pair-additive source remains pair-additive, since the
conditional mean of a removed binary spin is an arbitrary function of its two
retained neighbors, hence a linear combination of1,sL,sR,sLsR. The residual
source is a sum of independent centered fast spins with acquired conditional
coefficients. Therefore b is a finite local sum. These closure facts apply to
the conventional construction as well.

2. Dimension-free coupling from elementary coloring

At a fixed slow configuration y, the fast spins are independent, and
  B u=sum_i [r_i-E_F r_i] Delta_i u.
Each residual rate depends on the two neighboring fast spins. The dependency
graph of slow rate residuals is a path; color its vertices with two colors.
Within a color, centered rate residuals are independent and orthogonal.
Consequently
  Var_F(sum_i [r_i-rbar_i] Delta_i u)
  <=2 sum_i Var_F(r_i)(Delta_i u)^2.
If gammaF is a conditional fast gap lower bound, F^-1<=gammaF^-1Q gives
  <Bu,F^-1Bu> <=2 E_y sum_i Var_F(r_i)/gammaF (Delta_i u)^2.
The slow Dirichlet form is (1/2)E_y sum_i rbar_i(Delta_i u)^2, so
  theta_col=4 max_(i,y_local) Var_F(r_i)/(gammaF rbar_i)
is a valid coupling constant. Only8 retained-spin configurations and4 fast-spin
configurations enter each local maximum. There is no n factor or slow gap.

Arbitrary within-layer clock heterogeneity is also possible: F_y^-1/2 preserves
each residual's original two-fast-site support, because the conditional product
generator acts diagonally on centered monomials. Apply coloring to
F_y^-1/2(r_i-rbar_i), and use its local spectral gap instead of gammaF:
  theta_col-local=4 max_(i,y_local)
      Var_F(r_i)/(rbar_i min(gamma_left(y),gamma_right(y))).
This is the same local variance/min-gap formula as the proposed monomial-overlap
bound on a chain. It follows without enumerating the monomials. Replacing the
min-gap estimate by the exact inverse quadratic form of the local4-state fast
generator sharpens constants while retaining O(n) local work.

3. Matched computation

The implemented independent comparator coloring_averaging.py imports root's
conditional averaging backend (the shared operation) and calculates theta_col
itself from the local microscopic rates. It retains the simpler lower bound
from the recursively lifted final slow trial; it does not use root's augmented
fast-source lower bound.

Microscopic input:
  n=2^L-1, fixed+ boundaries, J=.5log(n+1), nu_i=alpha^v2(i), alpha=1e-5,
  r_i=nu_i[.5+2*1{sigma_(i-1)=sigma_(i+1)=+1}]
      /[1+exp(2sigma_i J(sigma_(i-1)+sigma_(i+1)))],
  f=sum_i sigma_i/sqrt(n), centered exactly in the operator calculation.
The interaction length scales with n; kinetic modulation occurs at every site.

4. Exact primary literature and real public code

Hashemi, Nunez, Plechac, Vlachos, Stochastic averaging and sensitivity analysis
for two scale reaction networks, J.Chem.Phys.144,074104(2016),
DOI10.1063/1.4942008.
Full primary manuscript and proof appendix inspected:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1509.03802
TheoremII.4 assumes a finite state space and recurrent fast classes. It gives
  ||p0_T-p_epsilon_T|| <= O(epsilon+exp(-kappa*T/epsilon)).
The averaged generator is explicitly pi_fast Q_slow 1. AppendixB derives
finite-time singular perturbation expansions; the fast exponential constant
depends on the Jordan form of the fast generator. This is not an explicit
uniform-in-n relative bound on an infinite-time scalar Poisson response.
Stationary expectation bounds do not become that bound by integration.

Associated actual source repository:
https://github.com/VlachosGroup/Multiscale-KMC
Read src/TTS_sim.cpp in full, blob5d483c21e3821c01493ee24189c6fe0e2ea59ac6:
https://github.com/VlachosGroup/Multiscale-KMC/blob/master/src/TTS_sim.cpp
It performs microscale Metropolis sampling, averages slow propensities, advances
a macro KMC clock and accumulates sensitivity statistics. Its configured
n_micro_steps controls micro averaging. No deterministic integrated-response
certificate was found in this source. It was inspected, not compiled/executed
or reused in our code. RepositoryREADME labels the two-time-scale version
under development. Do not portray it as a verified ready-made solver for our
spin-chain objective.

5. Decision consequence

The local rate/source closure is valuable preserved capital. An improved
certificate for the manually favorable separated hierarchy has not earned
a qualitatively new capability over a strong conventional construction.
The harder opening is to acquire an efficient elimination architecture/error
budget for naturally specified systems without presupposing a recursively
favorable clock hierarchy, or to control the memory/coupling created when
conditional averaging is not accurate enough. A small constant improvement
in theta does not cross that bridge.
