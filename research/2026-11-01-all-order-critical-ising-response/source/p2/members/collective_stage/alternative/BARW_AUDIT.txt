ALL-ORDER RESPONSE: INDEPENDENT POSITIVE-DUAL AND COST AUDIT
1 November 2026

Scope and role
--------------
This file preserves the independent alternative construction and audit. The
parent owns the central all-order response synthesis and executable sampler.
The interacting agent owns the completed ordinary combinatorial proofs of the
gap identities. Earlier signed-dual and transport work is not overwritten.

The example is the periodic n-site ferromagnetic Ising chain, n>=4, with

 w_i(sigma) = (1-b L R)/2 * [1-gamma sigma_i(L+R)/2],
 L=sigma_(i-1), R=sigma_(i+1), 0<b<gamma(1-b).

The executed critical family has b=1/3, exp(2J)=n,
 gamma=(n^2-1)/(n^2+1), t=tanh(J)=(n-1)/(n+1).
The response is chi=<M,K^+M>, M=sum_i sigma_i, at the actual Gibbs equilibrium.
The entire nonlinear generator is retained. No number of dual particles,
branching order, path duration or spatial radius is truncated.

1. Exact positive dual and ordinary regeneration
------------------------------------------------
For odd S, phi_S=product_(i in S) sigma_i. Put

 a=gamma(1-b)/2, kappa=(1-b)(1-gamma), N=|S|.

 K phi_S = N phi_S
            -a sum_(i in S) [phi_(S xor {i,i-1})+phi_(S xor {i,i+1})]
            -b sum_(i in S) phi_(S xor {i-1,i+1}).

Thus the dual hops each particle left/right at rate a, toggles its two neighbors
at rate b, and is killed at rate kappa N. Odd parity is preserved by live moves.
Let Kint be the positive generator of these live moves. Let V(S)=<M,phi_S> and
V1=V({0}); Var(M)=n V1.

Starting at packed triple T={0,1,2}, stop the LIVE dual at its first singleton
time tau. Set A_s=integral_0^s N_r dr. Ordinary renewal, integrating singleton
waiting and translation exactly, gives

 U=integral_0^tau exp(-kappa A_s) N_s ds,
 R=integral_0^tau exp(-kappa A_s) V(S_s)/V1 ds,
 theta := kappa chi/Var(M) = [1+b E R]/[1+b E U].

In particular U=(1-exp(-kappa A_tau))/kappa. Rare killing itself need never be
waited for. This alone does NOT establish useful variance. Direct excursion
second moments can grow with n. Positive recurrence is insufficient to infer
dimension-independent second moments.

2. Doob transform with an acquired bounded terminal score
---------------------------------------------------------
Write ordered cyclic gaps g_0,...,g_(N-1), positive integers summing to n. Define

 F3(g)=sum_(i<j<k; j-i and k-j odd) g_i g_j g_k,
 h=F3/n, h(singleton)=0, h0=h(T)=(n-2)/n.

With D the positive annihilating-hop generator at rate 1/2 per direction, and
B the branching increment operator, the exact identities are

 D h=(N-1)/2,        B h=1-2N/n.

Let a0=a+2b/n, c0=a+b, epsilon=a0-c0/3>0. Then

 Kint h=g=a0 N-c0 >= epsilon N   (N>=3).

For H=Kint+kappa N, the h-transformed process has live rates q(S,T)h(T)/h(S)
and death rate (Hh)(S)/h(S). Its total rate is N. Singleton targets have h=0.
For any nonnegative F, the exact Green-function identity is

 [H^-1 F](T) = h0 E^h[ F(Z_death)/(g+kappa N h)(Z_death) ].

Accordingly the terminal scores

 U*=h0 N/(g+kappa N h),
 R*=h0 alpha/(g+kappa N h),   alpha=V/V1,

have means E U and E R. U* <=h0/epsilon, with no rare-event amplification.

The static reward is itself earned. Since the Glauber reference obeys
K0 M=(1-gamma)M, alpha solves

 gamma D alpha +(1-gamma)(N-1)alpha=0,   alpha(singleton)=1.

Pure-hop Feynman--Kac and Dh=(N-1)/2 imply

 0<alpha<=1,        1-alpha<=2(1-gamma)h/gamma.

Set theta0=a0/c0 and

 W*=R* -theta0 U* +h0/c0
   =h0[alpha-1+kappa N h/c0]/[g+kappa N h].

The condition 2a>b and N>=3 makes the bracket nonnegative. Also
0<=W*<=h0/c0. The exact response is

 theta=theta0+b E W*/(1+b E U*).

This paired construction removes the leading fluctuations as well as the rare
kill. At zero killing and constant alpha=1 the fluctuating combination is
identically zero, not merely mean zero. Finite-size corrections remain present.

3. The essential cost bound: bounded variance is not free
--------------------------------------------------------
The h transform changes the path law. Its expected number of events, including
death, is

 E^h steps = [H^-1(Nh)](T)/h0.

The loose estimate h<=n^2/6 only gives O(n^2). The following construction earns
LINEAR expected transition cost for the full branching process.

Define the nonnegative quintic gap polynomial

 Q=F5/n,
 F5=sum_(i1<...<i5; every consecutive index spacing odd) product_j g_ij.

It obeys DQ=(N-3)h/2. For 1<=d<N put

 c_d=d/2 if d even, and c_d=(N-d)/2 if d odd.

Exact branching expansion gives

 B F5=3F3 -2 sum_(i<j)c_(j-i)g_i g_j +(N-1)n/2.

Because c_d>=1 and g_i g_j>=(g_i+g_j)/2, this proves BQ<=3h.
The ordinary coefficient proof uses the local insertion
[x,y] -> [x-1,1,1,y-1], with zero-gap continuity accounting for annihilation.
Degree-five and degree-four terms cancel. An alternating cubic receives net
coefficient3, a pair at index distance d receives -2c_d, and a linear gap
receives (N-1)/2. The interacting proof file gives the detailed count.

For three particle positions x_i<x_j<x_k let a'=x_j-x_i, b'=x_k-x_j,
c'=n-a'-b', and

 u3=a'b'c'(a'b'+b'c'+c'a'+1)/(12n).

Extend this by the collision-compatible alternating particle-triple sum

 Ucost(S)=sum_(i<j<k)(-1)^(i+j+k+1)u3(x_i,x_j,x_k).

Adjacent-collision cancellation gives D Ucost=h. Positivity follows from its
finite killed Poisson representation. The exact branching identity is

 B Ucost = u0 N-2h,       u0=(n-2)(n-1)/(6n).

Put d=2a=gamma(1-b). The NONNEGATIVE supersolution

 Ccost=(3 Ucost+2 Q)/d + [3b u0/(d epsilon)] h

obeys Kint Ccost>=N h and therefore H Ccost>=N h. At the starting triple Q=0,
Ucost=u0. Consequently

 E^h steps <= Ccost(T)/h0
            = (n-1)/(4a) * (1+b h0/epsilon).

For the critical family b=1/3, this is asymptotically3n. This is an all-order
bound. It does not assume that all paths remain in the three-particle sector.

4. Exact constant-overhead transition sampling
----------------------------------------------
Enumerating all 3N possible successors is unnecessary. Every live transition
from a non-singleton obeys h(T)/h(S)<=2.

For a hop, one gap increases by1 and its neighbor decreases by1. Each positive
multiaffine monomial increases by at most a factor2; continuity handles a zero
gap. For a branch at a pivot, with gaps cyclically based there,

 F3(new)-F3(old)=sum_j (-1)^j g_j -2 <= n-2.

Also F3>=n-2 on all non-singletons: expand g_j=1+z_j with z_j>=0. Every variable
occurs in at least one alternating cubic and F3(1,...,1)>=N-2. Thus the branch
ratio is also at most2.

Let lambda=2a+b=1-kappa. Propose death with probability1/2; otherwise choose one
ordinary live event with probability q/(lambda N). Accept death with probability
p_death/2, and accept a live event with probability lambda h(T)/(2h(S)).
The accepted law is exactly the h-transformed row; acceptance probability is1/4
at every state. There is no unproved sign cancellation or rare acceptance rate.

Integer rates and h numerators permit exact random choices. One-candidate h
evaluation costs O(N) via the alternating-prefix recurrence, rather than
enumerating every successor. This immediately gives an O(n^2) arithmetic upper
bound per sample from the O(n) event theorem. The parent's acquired balanced
gap-tree backend can strengthen that to O(n polylog n), but that implementation
must be verified before the stronger arithmetic claim is used. A linear event
bound alone must not be reported as a linear total computational bound.

6. Confidence and precision audit
---------------------------------
Maurer--Pontil2009 Theorem4 is ONE-SIDED. Two scores and two directions require
four per-side allocations, e.g. failure/4, hence log(8/failure) in each radius.

If a score is enclosed numerically, a mean interval width alone does not justify
using the midpoint sample variance. Two valid constructions are:
 (a) separately apply the one-sided bound to the exact dyadic lower and upper
     score random variables; or
 (b) upper-bound the true sample standard deviation by the midpoint standard
     deviation plus sqrt(M/(M-1))*maximum observed score radius, and account for
     the sample mean's interval width as well.
The parent adopted (a), with rational upper bounds for the logarithm and integer
square-root bounds, so the final confidence computation need not rely on a
floating allowance. Dyadic upper score rounding enlarges its range by at most
one dyadic unit and that enlargement must be included.

7. Governing primary prior and limits of the claim
-------------------------------------------------
The positive live process is the classical symmetric double-branching
annihilating random walk. It is NOT the asymmetric offspring model with a
generic active-phase transition. Sudbury1990 is the original source; the primary
Blath--Kurt2011 paper gives its exact transitions and explains the distinction:
https://emis.de/ft/41910

Sturm--Swart2008, Tightness of voter model interfaces, arXiv0706.4405, proves
positive recurrence modulo translation for swapping voter interfaces. Its
nearest-neighbor inversion drift is the infinite-line analogue of Kint h.
This established recurrence does not supply the present killed-reward cost.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0706.4405

Balazs--Nagy2015, Dependent double branching annihilating random walk, EJP20(84),
DOI10.1214/EJP.v20-4045, extends singleton recurrence to dependent jump rates.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.00739

ben-Avraham--Leyvraz--Redner1994, PRE50,1843, closes interval-parity expectations
through a two-endpoint diffusion equation. This computes particle profiles; the
additional exp(-kappa integral N) path weighting does not automatically close.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/9405029

Bruna--Grigore--Kiefer--Ouaknine--Worrell2016, ICALP104, identifies exactly the
same alternating-gap cubic and quintic in Herman's self-stabilizing protocol.
Lemma4 gives h<=n^2(1-1/N^2)/24. Its synchronous dynamics differs from our
branching process, so the branch identities and killed-response construction
must be checked anew; the polynomial itself is established machinery.
https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol055-icalp2016/LIPIcs.ICALP.2016.104/LIPIcs.ICALP.2016.104.pdf

MacPhee--Menshikov--Volkov--Wade2010, Bernoulli16,1312, DOI10.3150/09-BEJ243,
shows heavy passage-time tails for exclusion-voter interfaces. Its higher-moment
mixture theorem needs exclusion asymmetry p>1/2 and cannot be imported for our
symmetric p=1/2 model. We did not assume such a theorem; the cost supersolution
above earns the needed transformed event moment directly.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0810.0392

Maurer--Pontil2009, Empirical Bernstein Bounds and Sample Variance Penalization,
Theorem4 gives the one-sided finite-sample empirical-variance bound used here.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.3740

This audit establishes a complete all-order algorithm for this nonlinear
critical kinetic-Ising family, subject to the verified root implementation.
It is not a claim that arbitrary interacting matter has a positive dual or a
usable Lyapunov function, or that historical novelty of every component has
been established. Conventional potential/current bounds already supply the
leading large-n response; the new service is prescribed finite accuracy through
all interacting sectors without full-state storage or a rare-kill waiting run.

8. Final independent root-backend audit
--------------------------------------
Reviewed root/all_order_sampler.cpp and root/certify_all_order.py after the
parent's fixes. No remaining mathematical blocker was found.

The AVL aggregate keeps p_r=sum_i(-1)^i x_i^r, r=0,...,3, and
e_r=sum_(i<j)(-1)^(i+j)(x_j-x_i)^r, r=2,3. Its concatenation formula follows
by the binomial theorem, with the right sequence's alternating sign multiplied
by (-1)^(left count). Therefore hnum=(3n e2-2e3)/6 is exact. AVL insertion,
deletion, rank selection and rollback after a rejected toggle preserve this
representation and take O(log N) node operations.

The implemented rejection envelope is4, a conservative version of the envelope2
proved above. Put D=3(n^2+1), lambda=(D-4)/D. Death is proposed with mass D and
live moves with total mass4(D-4); conditional live type weights are n^2-1,
n^2-1,n^2+1. Death accepts with its exact target probability; a live candidate
accepts with hn/(4h). Every accepted channel has its desired transformed mass
divided by (1+4lambda). Acceptance is state-independent 1/(5-4kappa), exactly
the proposal-cost multiplier used by the certificate code.

The declared 4<=n<=65536 protects all fixed-width intermediates. Loosely, the
degree-three aggregate cross products are at most a constant times n^5, under
2^84; the largest rejection denominator is at most n^6, under2^96. Thus signed
128-bit state arithmetic and the unsigned uniform rejection mask have ample
room. This bound is specific to the declared executable range; arbitrary n
requires the same formulas with multiprecision integers.

The updated confidence script uses four separate exact dyadic random variables,
four one-sided failure allocations, outward rational logarithm/square-root
bounds, and range enlargement by one dyadic unit. These operations correctly
enclose the true score expectations without an unproved float allowance.

Two presentation/cost qualifications remain:
  * The JSON's exact rational endpoints are authoritative. Its float endpoint
    conversions are nearest rounded; display them as approximations, or use
    an outward nextafter when calling the printed decimals rigorous endpoints.
  * The delivered executable stores an endpoint histogram. Its memory and
    lexicographic map-insertion cost must be included. The dynamic state alone
    is O(n) words, but the recorded histogram can require O(samples*n) words
    in the worst case. The histogram does not store a many-body configuration
    distribution or enumerate all states, but a streaming-memory claim would
    require actually accumulating endpoint scores during sampling.
