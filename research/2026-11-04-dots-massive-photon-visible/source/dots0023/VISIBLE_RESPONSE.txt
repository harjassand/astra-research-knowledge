We found why the massive-photon proof cannot simply be extended: long-range photon fluctuations interact across scales, and the current method loses the cancellations needed to control them. I’m working on a new way to group those interactions while keeping the physical gauge constraints intact. That central step is not solved. A completed massless construction could be a major advance in how we formulate and study chiral quantum theories; even then, its wider technological consequences would need to be established.
