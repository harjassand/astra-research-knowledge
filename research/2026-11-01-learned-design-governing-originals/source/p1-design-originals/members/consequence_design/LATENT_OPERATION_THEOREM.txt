ACQUIRING LOGICAL VARIABLES THAT SUPPORT LATER GLOBAL DESIGN
Main synthesis, 31 October 2026 UTC

SCIENTIFIC CONCLUSION

The selected capability is a learned, reusable global optimizer on logical
variables that are NOT supplied. The experiments are noisy scalar energy
evaluations of controllable binary configurations. The retained object is a
partition of the physical controls, a small acquired interaction forest, and
an operational error certificate for an entire future field region.

The physical energy can be dense, high rank, frustrated and far from the
retained forest as a uniform energy predictor. It need not have an exactly invariant subspace. A
large energy penalty outside the retained configurations can HELP the
computation rather than count as approximation error. The theorem learns the
reduction from the same energy observations that a conventional method gets,
certifies its effect on the requested optimization, and constructs the actual
consumer. It does not assert a speedup over all task-aware ordinary methods.

This is a stronger opening than the known-resource quotient in
QUOTIENT_THEOREM.txt: here the useful collective variables are themselves
acquired. The observation contract changes. Global quadratic energy is assumed;
the arbitrary unknown resource-dependent g of that other theorem cannot be
silently carried into the present global experiments. Both constructions are
preserved, along with the previous exact stochastic compiler and solver capital.

1. TASK, SOURCE, AND WHAT IS NOT GIVEN

There are n directly addressable binary controls x in {-1,+1}^n. An experiment
at a chosen full configuration returns an observation with fixed mean

 E(x)=c+b^T x+(1/2)x^T A_true x,

where A_true is real symmetric. Its diagonal contributes one constant on the
binary domain and is unidentifiable; no true diagonal is assumed to be known.
No partition, logical variable count, low-dimensional representation,
interaction graph, optimizer, eigenvalue gap, or useful matrix-vector oracle
is supplied. A warranted absolute noise scale sigma is part of the instrument
contract: fresh errors are conditionally mean zero and sigma-subgaussian.
Known bounded digitization errors are accounted for separately.

Every full configuration preparation and every repeated scalar observation is
charged. For a stochastic computational experiment, inputs can literally be
written and its entire evaluation cost paid. For a laboratory, preparation,
reset, equilibration and verification costs are C_prepare(x) and must be added.
Counting binary input writes is not a universal physical preparation-time
bound. The executable is a controlled-input numerical source, not a deployment.

After acquisition, an unlimited sequence of possibly adaptive queries gives
known fields h with |h_i|<=H. The service must produce actual xhat satisfying

 E(xhat)+h.xhat <= min_x [E(x)+h.x] + epsilon*n.                 (1)

Only this extensive accuracy is required. Exact physical sampling, exact
identification of real coefficients, and exact preservation of every ground
state are unnecessary. The compiled finite model will be optimized exactly
because an integer tree program can do so at small cost.

The field region is substantive. If a fixed retained configuration set Z
misses x*, then h=-M x* makes x* the unique optimum for M larger than half the
oscillation of E; the best design in Z then has unbounded regret as M grows.
A proper fixed reduction cannot serve every unbounded physical field with a
fixed error. This differs from the resource quotient, which retains all
placements within every requested resource class and permits arbitrary fields.

2. THE STRONG CONVENTIONAL ACQUISITION ROUTE, CONSTRUCTED

Let D=1+n+n(n-1)/2 be the number of degree-at-most-two Walsh features
1,x_i,x_i*x_j. Full scalar polarization is one simple acquired route:

 A_ij=[E(1)-E(1^i)-E(1^j)+E(1^{ij})]/4,
 b_i=[E(+1,s_-i)-E(-1,s_-i)+E(+1,-s_-i)-E(-1,-s_-i)]/4.

It needs four independent batches per coefficient, not one Hessian oracle.
Simultaneous coordinate accuracy a from this construction can cost
O(n^2+sigma^2 n^4 epsilon^{-2} log(n/delta)) shots when a=O(epsilon/n).
That is a sufficient construction, NOT an acquisition floor.

We adopt the much stronger conventional orthogonal-array design. Choose
m=ceil(log_2 n), a binary field F of size 2^m, and n distinct elements a_i.
Label each physical variable by the (1+2m)-bit vector

 v_i=(1,a_i,a_i^3).

For each t in F_2^{1+2m}, prepare x_i(t)=(-1)^{t.v_i}. There are
N=2^{1+2m}<8n^2 rows (with the harmless n=1 case handled directly).
The labels 0, v_i and v_i+v_j for i<j are all distinct. Unary labels have
first bit one; pair labels have first bit zero and are nonzero. If two pair
labels agree, s=a+b is nonzero and a^3+b^3=s^3+ab*s determines ab; the sum and
product determine the same unordered pair. Hence their Walsh characters are
distinct. Character orthogonality gives EXACTLY

 Phi^T Phi=N I_D.                                              (2)

This is established BCH/orthogonal-array machinery, not a new discovery.
The finite field and every actual control configuration are generated by code.
No useful physical representation is encoded in these universal labels.

Take L fresh readings at each row. With M=NL charged scalar shots, each
coefficient is its signed sample average. A fast Walsh transform computes all
such coefficients in O(N log N) arithmetic, after O(Nn) control-generation work.
Finite dyadic reports admit an exact integer transform and exact rational
averages. Repeated preparation can require O(Mn) control writes rather than
O(Nn); the source's actual preparations are counted separately. Conditioning
of a random regression matrix is not a premise.

For any fixed x, its fitted energy error due solely to observation noise has
subgaussian parameter sigma*sqrt(D/M), by (2). A union bound over all 2^n
configurations gives, on one event of probability at least 1-delta,

 sup_x |Efit(x)-E(x)|
 <= Delta_stat = sigma sqrt[(2D/M)(n log 2+log(2/delta))].      (3)

If report digitization has absolute bound tau, orthogonal projection contracts
its normalized l2 norm, so its prediction contribution is at most sqrt(D)*tau.
Rounding each fitted coefficient by at most q contributes at most D*q.
Add any other warranted deterministic arithmetic error explicitly. Set

 Delta=Delta_stat+sqrt(D)*tau+D*q+other_error.

The constant coefficient and any diagonal completion may be dropped after
fitting: they change energies only by constants and do not change oscillation.
The uniform objective-difference error is at most 2Delta. This is the physical
accuracy interface needed later; coordinate precision epsilon/n is not imposed
when it is stronger than the task requires.

Thus a sufficient shot scale for physical error <=epsilon*n is

 O(n^2 + sigma^2 [n+log(1/delta)]/epsilon^2),                  (4)

rounded to a multiple of N, with fixed fractions of the error budget reserved
for reduction and finite precision. The O(n^2) design is unavoidable for this
particular full identification route, not asserted to be an information lower
bound for operation learning. Group recovery can impose an additional
completion-accuracy requirement; soundness of an accepted service uses (3).

The earlier polarization route is retained and executed as a conventional
comparison. If it supplies entrywise errors <=a for every off-diagonal and
linear coefficient, the row-sum-zero completion has operator error <=2(n-1)a,
and objective oscillation error <=2n^2 a. Both source interfaces can feed the
same contraction and consumer. The better actual error/cost bound should be used.

3. A GAUGE THAT IS OBSERVABLE FROM BINARY EXPERIMENTS

From acquired off-diagonal coefficients form the symmetric rational matrix A
with those off-diagonal entries and

 A_ii=-sum_{j!=i} A_ij.                                       (5)

Then A*1=0. The fitted objective F(x)=bhat.x+(1/2)x^T A x agrees with the fitted
Walsh polynomial up to a constant. The choice (5) costs no observations and
does not claim to identify the physical diagonal. It gives a useful spectral
proposal coordinate system while the later certificate is explicitly checked.

All entries of A and bhat can be represented on a declared rational grid.
Confidence in their source is (3), not an exact-parameter assertion.

4. PROPOSING, AND THEN EARNING, LATENT VARIABLES

A proposed partition has r nonempty clusters. Let P be its n-by-r indicator
matrix, Dp=P^T P=diag(m_a), and W=P Dp^{-1}P^T its orthogonal projector.
These objects are computed, not supplied. The columns of P are the proposed
logical variables; aligned physical configurations have the form Pz,
z in {-1,+1}^r.

An explicit polynomial-time proposer diagonalizes A, tries every number
r=1,...,n. For each, form the row-distance graph, connecting pairs closer than
one of 1/(6sqrt(n)), 1/(3sqrt(n)), 2/(3sqrt(n)), and take its connected
components. Rows of the orthonormal eigenvector matrix give exactly the same
pair distances as rows of its projector. Keep partitions whose cluster count
matches r. A more inclusive polynomial proposer may instead try every midpoint
between sorted pair distances. The three stated thresholds suffice for the
completion conditions below and are the ones used in the executable.
Standard spectral clustering is used as a proposal,
not as a correctness certificate. Numerical mistakes in a proposal can only
cause a later rejection or another valid reduction.

For any proposed partition compute

 C=P^T A P,     T=(I-W)A(I-W),     F_cross=W A(I-W).

Three actual numerical certificates are acquired from the fitted law:

 (a) A transverse lower bound gamma:
       v^T A v >= gamma ||v||^2 for every v in ker(P^T).
     Build an integer basis B of differences e_i-e_anchor within each cluster.
     An exact rational LDL test of B^T(A-gamma I)B proves this inequality.
     An ordinary numerical eigensolver may propose gamma, but positive exact
     pivots verify it. A failure is inconclusive; gamma can be decreased.
     A finite fallback is the rational Gershgorin lower bound for A minus one,
     which makes the restricted test strictly positive definite. It may be
     useless for acceptance. The executable caps unsuccessful proposals and
     rejects that candidate, also a terminating sound procedure.

 (b) A cross-coupling upper bound d^2>=||F_cross||op^2.
     Let G=P^T A(I-W)A P. Since
       ||F_cross||op^2=lambda_max(Dp^{-1/2} G Dp^{-1/2}),
     it suffices to verify the r-by-r rational PSD condition
       d^2 Dp-G >=0.
     A positive-pivot LDL test of a proposed rational upper bound suffices;
     exact G=0 gives d=0. The squared Frobenius norm is a finite simpler fallback.

 (c) Future cost variation bounds
       alpha=max_a [(C_aa)_+ + sum_{b!=a}|C_ab|]/m_a,
       beta=max_i |bhat_i|+H,     g=gamma-2alpha-2beta.

The older alpha=max_a sum_b|C_ab|/m_a is also valid and sometimes more
conservative. No unknown physical parameters occur in these checks.
They may all depend on the same acquisition data, because (3) is already
uniform over configurations and the following reduction theorem is deterministic.

When r=n, the normal space is empty, F_cross=0, and the retained set is the
whole cube. No transverse check is necessary and the contraction loss is zero.
The coarse solver and its tail must still earn the requested total accuracy.

5. THE OPERATIONAL CONTRACTION THEOREM

For x in {-1,+1}^n, let z_a be its majority sign in cluster a (tie arbitrary),
let x_round=Pz, u=Wx, v=(I-W)x, and R=||v||^2. If D_a is the minority count
in cluster a and Dminor=sum_a D_a, then

 R=sum_a 4D_a(1-D_a/m_a),   2Dminor<=R<=4Dminor,
 ||u||^2=n-R.                                                 (6)

Write t=Dp^{-1}P^T x, so u=Pt and |t_a-z_a|=2D_a/m_a.
The transverse energy is at least gamma R/2. Comparing the coarse energies
(1/2)t^T C t and (1/2)z^T C z loses at most 2alpha Dminor. Negative diagonal
entries cannot hurt this comparison because t_a^2<=1; positive diagonal
entries and all cross terms give the alpha bound above. The linear cost
(bhat+h).x loses at most 2beta Dminor, uniformly for |h_i|<=H.
Finally |u^T A v|<=d sqrt[R(n-R)]. Therefore

 [F+h](x)-[F+h](x_round)
 >= (g/2)R-d sqrt[R(n-R)].                                   (7)

The right side is n times a two-dimensional Rayleigh quotient. Its minimum
over 0<=R<=n is -Lambda, where

 Lambda = n [sqrt(g^2+4d^2)-g]/4.                             (8)

Equation (8) is valid for every real g. For g>0 it is at most n*d^2/(2g).
It does NOT require the exact low-dimensional subspace of minimizers to
survive perturbations. It bounds the price of performing the operation in
the acquired logical coordinates at the requested accuracy.

For exact finite acceptance arithmetic no square root need be trusted. A
rational proposed loss Lred>=0 satisfies the Rayleigh certificate (8) when,
with psi=2Lred/n,

 psi>=0,   g+psi>=0,   psi(g+psi)>=d^2.                       (9)

These are the two-by-two PSD conditions for
[[psi,-d],[-d,g+psi]], expressed using d^2 only.

Sometimes the discrete domain gives an exact reduction. If g>0 and
g^2>4n d^2, then for every Dminor>=1 the cruder lower bound
g Dminor-2d sqrt(n Dminor) is positive. Every unaligned configuration is then
strictly worse than its majority rounding, and Lred=0 is warranted. This
stronger condition is optional, not imposed when approximate reduction suffices.

Consequently, for every future h in the field box,

 min_z [F+h](Pz) <= min_x [F+h](x)+Lred.                      (10)

The discarded transverse energy may have arbitrarily large amplitude, rank
n-r, and dense mixed-sign interactions. There is no global small-residual
assumption. What matters is its lower action on deviations, its coupling back
to the retained variables, and the permitted future costs.

6. THE DOWNSTREAM OPTIMIZER IS ALSO ACQUIRED AND EXECUTED

On x=Pz, the fitted objective is

 (1/2)z^T C z + sum_a [sum_{i in a}(bhat_i+h_i)] z_a.

The diagonal of C is a constant. Acquire a maximum-absolute-weight spanning
forest of its off-diagonal coefficients by Kruskal. Let Cforest retain its
edges and let

 Btail=2 sum_{a<b, edge discarded}|C_ab|.                     (11)

The discarded coarse interaction has oscillation at most Btail. This is an
explicit, computable, deterministic bound on the already acquired model, not
an estimate that needs a supplied graph or optimizer. More powerful actual
coarse solvers can be substituted with their proved costs; this construction
uses a forest because projection and exact execution are both elementary.

For each later query, sum its physical fields inside the acquired clusters,
run two-state tree dynamic programming on Cforest, and backtrace actual signs
z. Return Pz. With rational coefficients and finite rational inputs this is an
integer program of O(n+r) arithmetic operations, including reading the n fields
and producing n physical controls. It is not exhaustive search over 2^r.

Combine (3), (10), and (11). On the single acquisition event, EVERY answer from
the retained program obeys

 E(xhat)+h.xhat-min_x[E(x)+h.x]
 <= Bsource + Lred + Btail,                                  (12)

where Bsource=2Delta for orthogonal acquisition, or 2n^2a for the polarization
confidence construction. Accept only if the right side is <=epsilon*n.
The same event covers unlimited adaptive future queries in the field box.
Their known fields cancel in the source model error. No future physical
samples, validation resets, or per-query confidence spending are needed.

The operational state retains the n cluster labels, r coarse unary values,
at most r-1 coarse edges, H, and the certificate. The fitted dense matrix,
eigenvectors and calibration data may be discarded from the service. They
are retained separately in the experimental record for conventional comparison.
Per-query integer bit lengths include the actual field inputs and sums.

Acquisition/compilation cost is larger than online cost. One dense spectral
proposal decomposition costs O(n^3) arithmetic. The explicit three-threshold
loop over r has O(n^4) row-coordinate work in the worst case and at most 3n
distinct certificate attempts. Dense exact rational verification has polynomial
arithmetic and bit cost (a conservative O(n^4) rational-operation bound across
these attempts). Its integer lengths depend on matrix/report bit lengths and
verification margins; the executed peak bit lengths are recorded. Neither
the fitted n^2 matrix nor its acquisition is made free by later compression.

7. A NONEMPTY FAMILY WITH EARNED DISCOVERY AND COMPLETION

Soundness above holds for any fixed global quadratic source on accepted output.
To prove that a polynomial proposer can actually find useful reductions,
consider the following sufficient family. It is NOT information handed to
the learner:

 A_true=L+S^T Cstar S,  S=Dp^{-1}P^T,
 L>=0, ker L=range(P), L|ker(P^T)>=gamma0 I,

where P is an unknown partition with r<n, Cstar has an unknown approximate forest
and the field range is bounded. L can be dense with mixed-sign entries and
rank n-r. Since L*1=0, observable completion (5) is

 A0=L+K,
 K=S^T Cstar S-Diag(S^T Cstar 1).

K preserves range(P) and its orthogonal complement. If ||K||<=kappa and
gamma0>2kappa, the bottom r eigenspace of A0 is exactly range(P), with separation
at least gamma0-2kappa. The rows of its projector W are identical inside each
cluster and differ across clusters by sqrt(1/m_a+1/m_b)>=sqrt(2/n).
Therefore sufficiently accurate ordinary eigenspace recovery, followed by
the explicitly enumerated r and row-distance thresholds, recovers the partition.
A projector error below a fixed constant times n^{-1/2} is one sufficient
condition; it is not supplied as a representation oracle.

If the source design and precision are refined until this condition holds,
and the deterministic inequalities in sections 4-6 have strict slack, the
algorithm accepts. Independent acquisition rounds with summable failure
allowances give a sound adaptive refinement schedule. All rounds are charged.
Source precision for discovering the partition, preserving the reduction
margin, and certifying the coarse tail can exceed that needed merely for
uniform energy accuracy. The following bounds make these costs explicit.

Write zeta for a completion-confidence allowance, tau for total deterministic
report error, and q for coefficient rounding. For OA noise-only off-diagonal
error E, a 1/4-net on the unit sphere and row-sum concentration give

 ||E||op <=4sigma sqrt[(n log9+log(4/zeta))/M],
 ||E*1||infinity <=sigma sqrt[2(n-1)log(4n/zeta)/M].

The first follows because u^T E u has coefficient-weight norm at most sqrt(2),
and the net converts scalar quadratic forms to operator norm. The second is
a union bound on n linear combinations with n-1 unit weights. Consequently,
the row-completed matrix error rho is at most their sum plus
2(n-1)(tau+q). For gap Delta_eig=gamma0-2kappa, requiring

 rho <= Delta_eig/(64 sqrt(n))

and an equally small numerical eigenspace error suffices for the explicit
threshold proposer. Davis--Kahan gives a source projector error <=2rho/Delta_eig;
within-cluster row distances then lie below 1/(3sqrt(n)) and between-cluster
distances above it. This costs, up to logarithms,

 M=O(n^2+sigma^2 n^2/Delta_eig^2),

plus deterministic precision tau+q=O(Delta_eig/n^(3/2)).

For the true FIXED partition, coarse off-diagonal error has noise proxy
sigma sqrt(m_a*m_b/M). With another completion-confidence allocation,

 sum_{a<b}|Cerr_ab|
 <= [sigma*n*(r-1)/2] sqrt[2log(2n^2/zeta)/M]
                                      +(n^2/2)(tau+q).

Kruskal minimizes the sum of discarded absolute weights, so
 Btail_hat <= Btail_true+2sum_{a<b}|Cerr_ab|.
An available tail slack eta*n is therefore earned by
M=O(sigma^2 r^2 log(n/zeta)/eta^2) and tau+q=O(eta/n).

For a compact overall sufficient bound, let the planted instance have strict
spectral gap Delta_eig, tail slack eta*n, and a positive tolerance mu such that
perturbing the ideal certificate's gamma,d,alpha,beta by at most mu (and
refining rational check resolution) still leaves the required contraction
budget. Such a mu exists whenever that certificate has strict slack. Coarse
row and diagonal concentration bounds its error by
O_tilde(sigma*n/sqrt(M))+O(n(tau+q)); transverse, cross and unary errors have
no larger sufficient order. One conservative completion scale is therefore

 M=O_tilde(n^2 + sigma^2 n^2/Delta_eig^2 + sigma^2 n^2/mu^2
                     +sigma^2 r^2/eta^2 +sigma^2 n/epsilon^2). (13)

These are sufficient costs, not lower bounds or practical constants. The
learner need not receive P,r,Delta_eig,mu,eta: geometric acquisition/precision
refinement with summable confidence allowances and actual acceptance checks
attains the sufficient regime up to logarithms. Rational certificate and
eigensolver resolution must be refined too, rather than fixing a coarse grid
when the margin is smaller. The executable records its actual finite grid and
source count; it does not claim to have run the universal sufficient constants.

Crucially, the algorithm does not require exact invariance for soundness or
acceptance. Generic dense perturbations change F_cross and the transverse
lower bound continuously; (8)-(12) quantify their actual operational effect.
The certificate can accept approximate contraction even when no exact
low-dimensional optimum set remains. Large positive penalties are allowed,
while large harmful cross coupling is detected and charged.

8. CONVENTIONAL COMPARISON AND WHAT HAS ACTUALLY BEEN EARNED

The strongest matched ordinary route is NOT a predictor followed by an
unspecified hard solver. It is orthogonal-array quadratic identification,
standard spectral proposals, relational-persistency or SDP preprocessing,
an actual reduction certificate, and a concrete coarse optimizer. We adopt
these established ingredients wherever they solve a component.

Relevant primary predecessors include:

 - Wang and Kleinberg (2009), Analyzing Quadratic Unconstrained Binary
   Optimization Problems Via Multicommodity Flows. It discovers equal/opposite
   variable relations and reduces QUBO problems by relational persistencies.
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2808708/
 - Choi (2008), Minor-Embedding in Adiabatic Quantum Computation:
   I. The Parameter Setting Problem. Strong couplings synchronize supplied
   physical groups into logical variables; the embedding is an input there.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.4884
 - Damle, Minden and Ying, Robust and efficient multi-way spectral clustering.
   Recovery of indicator structure from an acquired approximate subspace is
   established numerical machinery.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.08251
 - Helmberg, Fixing Variables in Semidefinite Relaxations (2000), and stronger
   modern persistency methods are relevant conventional preprocessing.
 - Orthogonal arrays/BCH character designs and tree dynamic programming are
   established; the elementary design proof above makes this use auditable.
   Carlet, Kiss and Nagy, Simplicity conditions for binary orthogonal arrays,
   Designs, Codes and Cryptography 91 (2023), published online 2022.
   https://doi.org/10.1007/s10623-022-01105-4

The contribution being constructed is the full experimentally acquired
operation: no supplied logical variables, no exact kernel requirement, explicit
price of compression throughout a future field region, and a retained program
that performs the global design operation. Historical priority for the complete
theorem is unestablished. There is no proved complexity separation from every
strong conventional method allowed to adopt this certificate.

The executable comparison includes an ordinary full-model mixed-integer route
using the same acquired coefficients and the same required accuracy. A dense
or mixed-sign graph alone is not evidence that all ordinary solvers fail.
If a conventional method matches a fixture, that result is retained rather
than treating exact output semantics or a weak baseline as an advantage.

9. DECISIVE LIMITS AND INFORMATIVE FAILURES

 - A positive normal eigenvalue alone does not justify contraction: omitted
   cross coupling can make a nonaligned state optimal. An explicit n=4 example
   has gamma=g=1 and d^2=1 but contraction regret sqrt(2)-1. The bound (8)
   remains valid and charges that coupling.
 - A proper fixed reduction cannot handle arbitrary unbounded fields, as
   section 1 proves. Widening H requires recomputing the certificate.
 - A generic unmodelled high-order spike can change the optimum at an unseen
   configuration. The global quadratic contract is substantive and cannot be
   verified from a few favorable residual checks.
 - Exact PSD tests and exact integer DP certify arithmetic on finite acquired
   numbers. They do not calibrate an untrusted instrument, justify its noise
   law, or prove that a floating PRNG is a real laboratory.
 - A failed proposed partition or forest is inconclusive about the difficulty
   of the original problem. The learner may refine its evidence or try another
   actual algorithm; it may not output an uncertified design service.
 - Source count, control writes, retained dimension, acquisition arithmetic,
   exact verification bit sizes and execution arithmetic are different costs.
   No near-linear total acquisition claim is made from a small retained model.

The numerical records state which sufficient budgets and checks were actually
executed. The separate fixed-resource theorem and scalar spectral-certificate
branch remain useful capital, not substitutes for the acquisition proof here.

sigma=.05, field box H=.2, requested error .02*n, source failure allowance .05,
36-bit dyadic report/coefficient grids, and a separately stated 1e-9 absolute
instrument-readout guard. Neither the hidden partition nor its size is passed
to the learner. All raw source reports are actually generated and counted.

       .24 requested. All 4096 physical configurations checked on each of 12
       adaptive queries; largest actual regret .0130561172.
 n=24, weaker locking: 4096 reports; 6 logical variables; certificate
       .4073567533 versus .48 requested. This includes .1891438736 approximate
       contraction loss; the exact-alignment sufficient test did not pass.
       All 64 aligned configurations were independently scored per query;
       global 2^24 enumeration is NOT claimed.
 n=128: 32768 reports; 32 logical variables; certificate 1.5376671097 versus
       2.56 requested. Source transfer contributes .6875476205 and the coarse
       tail .8501194892; exact alignment is certified for the acquired full
       model. The physical-source uncertainty is still approximate. Twelve
       adaptive fields produced twelve different designs without new readings.

The n=128 run retained 128 labels and 63 scalar coefficients. Its acquired
source had a dense signed interaction matrix and a planted locking component
of rank 96 (truth used only after acquisition). It requested 4,194,304 input
bits and read 2,097,152 binary64 report bits. Integer Walsh acquisition used
491,520 additions; exact contraction checks used 305,792 rational products
and up to 3,868-bit numerators. The finite tree consumer consumed all 128
fields, made 62 integer comparisons, and used at most roughly 62-bit integers
in the recorded query. These separate costs are in the JSON records.

On this runtime, that source/acquisition took about .88 seconds, exact
compilation about 9.04 seconds, and twelve consumer queries about .0041 seconds.
These are synthetic-run measurements, not hardware or scaling claims. The
32768-shot budget is actually executed; the universal sufficient constants
behind completion bound (13) are not represented as executed.

The ordinary retaining HiGHS MILP baseline receives the SAME full acquired
matrix and source confidence, with presolve/cuts enabled and an ABSOLUTE
