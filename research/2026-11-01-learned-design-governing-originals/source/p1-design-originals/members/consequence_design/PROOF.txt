ACQUIRING AN EXECUTABLE GLOBAL DESIGN REDUCTION
Working scientific construction, 31 October 2026 UTC

STATUS AND QUESTION

The operation is inverse design, not exact stochastic output. From imperfect
chosen-configuration energy experiments, acquire a reusable program which
actually returns a globally near-optimal binary design after arbitrary later
unary fields and exact cardinality requirements. The true pairwise interaction
graph may be complete. An unknown near-forest approximation is neither supplied
nor required to be an exact property of the physical source.

This construction deliberately adopts model-based iterative hard thresholding,
bounded-orthonormal-system restricted isometry, Kruskal projection, and tree
dynamic programming. Those are conventional components. The residual validation
construction is in ../consequence_mechanism/OPERATIONAL_RESIDUAL_CERTIFICATE.txt.
The useful proposed organising object is an experimentally certified executable
reduction: an algorithm, its actual retained model, and an independently acquired
bound on the loss of using it for the physical task. This is not a claim that
sparse recovery, tractable subgraph inference, or an l2-to-uniform inequality is
new. A separation from the strongest possible composition of those methods has
not been established. This file makes that composition strong and explicit so
that further invention cannot rely on a weak comparator.

1. OBSERVATION AND LATER-TASK CONTRACT

There are n binary controls x in {-1,+1}^n. The fixed physical mean response is

 E(x) = c + sum_i b_i x_i + sum_{i<j} J_ij x_i x_j.                 (1)

It is a constitutive assumption that the response has degree at most two. Dense
quadratic discrepancies are allowed; arbitrary higher-order discrepancies are
not. The experimenter chooses and prepares all n controls, and an observation
has conditional mean E(x) and conditionally sigma-subgaussian error. Independent
fresh experiments are used. Preparation, source shots, stored bits, arithmetic,
and later optimizer operations are separately charged. No stationary sampler,
reset of an uncontrolled dynamical system, or equilibrium experiment is inferred
from this contract. It is suitable for an energy experiment or an already
available stochastic computational experiment with this access.

After acquisition, a query specifies rational unary fields h_i and a cardinality
B in {0,...,n}, or no cardinality constraint. The requested output xhat obeys
# {i:xhat_i=+1}=B when specified, and

 E(xhat)+h.xhat <= min_{x feasible} [E(x)+h.x] + epsilon*n.         (2)

The scale epsilon*n is an intensive-energy tolerance. If absolute total-energy
error epsilon is needed, replace epsilon by epsilon/n in every acquisition
bound; the resulting precision cost is not omitted. Fields and budgets may be
chosen adaptively after seeing all earlier designs and the acquisition record.
Arbitrary additional feasibility constraints are not free: their optimization
must be implemented separately.

The oracle's absolute noise scale matters. An observation of E/n with O(1)
noise has sigma=O(n) in (1), not sigma=O(1). A Bernoulli encoding with range
proportional to n has the same issue. Every averaging repetition is a source
shot. Counting one averaged estimate as one physical experiment is incorrect.

2. THE REPRESENTATION AND EXACT PROJECTION ARE CONSTRUCTED

Use the Walsh features phi_empty=1, phi_i=x_i, phi_ij=x_i*x_j.
Their number is D=1+n+n(n-1)/2. They are orthonormal under uniform independent
Rademacher controls and are bounded in absolute value by one. Let theta be the
coefficient vector. A forest model retains every constant/unary feature and
pair features whose edges form a forest. It has at most s=2n coefficients.

The Euclidean projection P_F(z) onto this union of subspaces is computable:
keep z's constant/unary coordinates; find a maximum-weight spanning forest with
edge weights z_ij^2 by Kruskal's algorithm; retain exactly those pair coordinates.
This minimizes the sum of squared omitted coordinates. A complete graph is
allowed in this projection. Zero-weight edges may be retained or omitted.
Sorting takes O(n^2 log n) comparisons and union-find work is smaller. No graph,
ordering, support, or approximation oracle is supplied.

For analysis only, let theta_F=P_F(theta), r=theta-theta_F, and rho=||r||_2.
The learner never sees these quantities. Small rho is a sufficient completion
condition for the forest route. Soundness of independent validation does not
require a promise about rho. A dense perturbation may change every exact graph
edge and still have small rho. There is no minimum nonzero-edge hypothesis.

3. RANDOM EXPERIMENTS AND MODEL-IHT

Choose M independent uniform configurations X_l, observe Y_l, and let Phi have
rows phi(X_l). Set A=Phi/sqrt(M) only as notation. Actual computation uses
Phi^T Phi/M and Phi^T Y/M, so square roots are unnecessary.

Starting from theta_0=0, perform

 theta_{t+1}=P_F(theta_t + Phi^T(Y-Phi theta_t)/M).                (3)

This is model-based IHT, used as an existing algorithmic component. A bounded
iteration count is chosen from a coefficient-norm upper bound B0 and desired
training tolerance. A source range bound can supply B0 through Parseval; B0 is
not a supplied representation. An aggressively chosen experimental budget is
also permissible because the separate validation step can reject the result.

One iteration costs O(MD+n^2 log n) elementary arithmetic with the straightforward
implementation. A row generator avoids retaining the full M-by-D matrix at the
price of repeating feature construction. Retaining it costs O(MD); retaining
only inputs, observations and coefficient workspace costs O(Mn+D). The returned
model and executable forest require O(n) coefficient/edge storage.

4. A DENSE-l2-TAIL RECOVERY BOUND

This derivation is included because the ordinary worst-case compressed-sensing
bound ||r||_1/sqrt(s) is needlessly large for a dense weak quadratic remainder.
The fixed low-degree remainder is random under the actual experimental design;
it is not adversarial noise chosen after the measurement matrix.

Suppose A has restricted-isometry constant delta_{3s}<=1/8. If 3s>D, use the
same statement with order D. Let Lambda=log(4D/eta). The degree-two
hypercontractive inequality is

 ||R||_p <= (p-1)||R||_2=(p-1)rho,  p>=2,                       (4)

where R(x)=sum_j r_j phi_j(x). Thus, for each feature j,
Z_j=phi_j(X)R(X)-r_j satisfies
E|Z_j|^k <= [2(k-1)rho]^k <= k!(6rho)^k, k>=2.
The usual exponential-moment proof of Bernstein's inequality then gives,
simultaneously for all D coordinates with failure probability at most eta/2,

 |(A^T A r-r)_j| <= lambda_r
 lambda_r = 12 rho [sqrt(Lambda/M)+Lambda/M].                    (5)

This remains true for a dense r. It is a concentration bound for one fixed
physical remainder, uniform over coordinates and hence over supports later
selected by (3). It is not a claim of uniform l2 instance optimality for every
remainder after observing A.

The conditionally subgaussian observation errors similarly give

 ||Phi^T xi/M||_infinity <= lambda_xi
 lambda_xi=sigma sqrt(2Lambda/M)                                (6)

with failure probability at most eta/2. Independence of errors from the chosen
X beyond the conditional subgaussian assumption is unnecessary for this bound.

Write e_t=theta_t-theta_F. Exact projection implies, for
S=supp(theta_{t+1}) union supp(theta_F),

 ||e_{t+1}||_2 <= 2 ||(z_t-theta_F)_S||_2,

because squared distances to z_t may be compared to the feasible theta_F and
coordinates outside S cancel. The union of S with supp(e_t) lies in at most
three forest supports. Therefore RIP controls the corresponding Gram submatrix.
Since |S|<=2s,

 ||e_{t+1}||_2
 <= 2 delta_{3s} ||e_t||_2 + 2rho
       +2 sqrt(2s)(lambda_r+lambda_xi).                         (7)

Here ||r_S||_2<=rho is retained explicitly. Replacing it by sqrt(s)||r||_infinity
would discard the dense-l2-tail advantage. Iterating (7) yields

 ||theta_t-theta||_2
 <= 4^{-t} ||theta_F||_2 + (11/3)rho
       +(8/3)sqrt(2s)(lambda_r+lambda_xi).                      (8)

For M>=C s Lambda and M>=C s sigma^2 Lambda/alpha^2, the right side is
O(rho+alpha)+4^{-t}B0. Constants can be made explicit by (5)-(8); no hidden
asymptotic limit is needed to run the algorithm with a particular M.

RIP acquisition is an established random-design theorem, not an assumed oracle.
Rauhut-Ward, Theorem 4.3, states that a K-bounded orthonormal system sampled at
M>=C delta^{-2} K^2 s log^3(s)log(D) has RIP with high probability. Here K=1.
For an arbitrary failure target, one may instead use a standard high-probability
bounded-system version, or repeat independent constant-success training runs
and validate them. The latter costs O(log(1/eta)) runs and requires no claim
about a universal constant in an unquoted tail refinement. Thus a sufficient
source budget, suppressing logarithms and universal constants, is

 M_total = ~O(n [1+sigma^2/alpha^2] log(1/eta)).                  (9)

The complete elementary fallback is also available: Hoeffding plus a union
bound makes every off-diagonal Gram entry at most delta/(3s) using
M=O(s^2 delta^{-2} log(D/eta)); Gershgorin then gives the required RIP. This
fallback is quadratic in n and is not advertised as the stronger sampling rate.

Finite arithmetic: if reported observations are rounded with deterministic
error at most q, (6) acquires an added q, and (8) an added (8/3)sqrt(2s)q.
Choose q commensurate with alpha/sqrt(s). For rational observations, rational
IHT and rational squared edge comparisons are exact; bit lengths grow by at
most O(t log M) plus the input observation length. A floating implementation
is a numerical demonstration, not an interval-verified implementation of this
statement. The certificate must include its actual evaluation error if deployed.

5. INDEPENDENT OPERATIONAL CERTIFICATION

The candidate G is a computed forest energy. Put R=E-G and center R by its
uniform mean. Constants do not affect designs and need not be recovered.
Let v=E R_center^2. For independent uniform X,Y, D_R=R(X)-R(Y) has

 E D_R^2=2v,  E D_R^4<=36v^2,  Var(D_R^2)<=32v^2.              (10)

The improved fourth-moment constant and median-of-block-means construction are
proved in the companion residual-certificate note. With 128 pairs per block,
each block average of exact squared differences lies in [v,3v] except with
probability at most 1/4. Medians over O(log(1/delta)) independent blocks amplify
this. If each observed paired residual difference has error at most nu, let
S be the median of the root-mean-square observed differences. The norm
triangle inequality gives, on the good event,

 sqrt(v) <= S+nu,
 S+nu <= sqrt(3v)+2nu.                                         (11)

There are K=D-1 nonconstant Walsh features. Cauchy-Schwarz gives

 span(R):=max_x R(x)-min_x R(x) <= 2sqrt(K) sqrt(v)
                                <= 2sqrt(K)(S+nu).             (12)

No maximizer of R, covariance matrix, true coefficients, or dense residual
representation is required for this test. Repeated noisy shots and a union
bound supply the simultaneous nu intervals. If sigma is the absolute energy
noise scale, a sufficient source count is

 O(log(1/delta) * max{1, sigma^2 nu^{-2}
                                log(log(1/delta)/delta)}).      (13)

The constants include the 128 pairs per block and the two energy measurements
per pair. Preparing them costs n control settings per shot in the worst case.

Accept only if 2sqrt(K)(S+nu) plus the downstream numerical optimization slack
is at most epsilon*n. Otherwise retain the informative failure and refine the
candidate or acquisition; rejection is not a proof that no useful compiler
exists. For an infinite adaptive sequence of candidate proposals, fresh tests
with delta_j=6delta/(pi^2 j^2) give one simultaneous soundness event. A previous
holdout cannot be reused after fitting to it without a separate valid argument.

Completeness: for rho sufficiently below a constant times epsilon, (8) with
alpha sufficiently below epsilon and enough iterations makes the candidate's
centered residual variance small enough to pass (11)-(12) with high probability.
That is a proved sufficient class, not a promise required to trust an accepted
certificate. The large universal RIP constants are not executed or presented
as necessary physical lower bounds. A posteriori certification permits smaller
actual training budgets to be tested honestly.

6. THE DOWNSTREAM OPTIMIZER IS IMPLEMENTED, INCLUDING THE BUDGET

For no cardinality restriction, ordinary two-state tree dynamic programming
minimizes G(x)+h.x in O(n) arithmetic. For an exact cardinality B, root every
tree. At a vertex i keep a table indexed by root spin s in {-1,+1} and the
number k of +1 spins in its subtree. Its entry is the minimum subtree energy,
including h, with those conditions. Initialize the singleton and merge a child
by minimizing over its root spin and splitting the count; add J_ij*s*t. Retain
backpointers. Merge separate components with a count convolution. These are
explicit finite operations, not calls to a QUBO oracle.

All cardinalities can be processed in O(n^2) arithmetic with a standard crude
global accounting: a merge between disjoint vertex sets of sizes a and b costs
O(ab) up to the fixed root-spin factor, and each unordered vertex pair is charged
only at the merge which first joins its two sets. Additional O(n^2) table/back-
pointer work is sufficient. The maximum number of states and actual candidate
convolutions are also instrumented in the executable implementation.

For any feasible family on which G+query can actually be optimized, if xG is
its exact minimizer and xE the true minimizer, then

 E(xG)+query(xG)-E(xE)-query(xE)
 <= R(xG)-R(xE) <= span(R).                                    (14)

This elementary transfer is not the invention by itself. Its operational use
requires all the preceding acquisition, empirical validation, and executable
optimization. The validity event in (12) is independent of later h and B. It
therefore covers unlimited adaptively selected queries without accumulating an
additional error probability. Approximate optimizers add their certified
objective gap to (14). Nonlinear query costs or constraints are covered only
when their execution cost is separately supplied by an actual algorithm.

7. TOTAL COST AND WHAT THE CONSTRUCTION DOES NOT CLAIM

For an approximately forest coefficient vector with rho=O(epsilon), a sufficient
training cost is (9) with alpha=Theta(epsilon). The actual retained forest has
O(n) size; each future constrained design costs O(n^2), unconstrained O(n).
Training arithmetic using the direct feature implementation is
~O(n^3[1+sigma^2/epsilon^2] log(B0/epsilon)), not linear time.
Validation costs (13) with nu=Theta(epsilon), plus all control preparation.
The source-cost bound and the larger arithmetic/preparation costs are both part
of the result. Generic precise reconstruction of all D coefficients is not
performed. This does not imply that every strong retaining algorithm needs it.

Dense generic quadratic perturbations are allowed. A tiny coefficient at every
edge can collectively change a design by O(n), so thresholding without the
operational certificate is unsound. Conversely, the Frobenius/L2 certificate can
be conservative by a dimension factor for some harmless high-rank remainders;
it is not a complete test for computational usefulness.

The bounded degree of the physical RESPONSE, here two, must not be confused with
the graph degree. Graph degree may be n-1. An unpromised term
-n*1{x=z} has almost invisible random prediction error and an extensive optimum
effect; finite random validation cannot exclude it. Noise scale, source
stationarity, and preparation access likewise remain actual premises.

The task is classical approximate global design. It neither simulates an exact
physical Gibbs law nor solves arbitrary planning. Per-site energy tolerance
does not give rare-conditioned probability accuracy or zero-temperature exact
ground-state identification. A future query may itself be computationally hard.

8. STRONG CONVENTIONAL COMPARISON

Baraniuk-Cevher-Duarte-Hegde already provide the model-based CS architecture.
Kruskal already supplies the forest projection. Fix-Chen-Boros-Zabih (ECCV2012)
already optimize tractable bounded-treewidth submodels of hard MRFs.
Tarlow et al. and later constrained-message-passing work already handle global
cardinality statistics. Van Doornmalen-Molina-Verdugo-Verschae (2026) explicitly
study noisy polynomial surrogates with uniform guarantees suitable for later
optimization. These are adopted, not dismissed as mere prediction methods.

What their bare interfaces do not by themselves provide is the complete
experiment-to-accepted-service claim above for a data-dependent tractable
candidate with an unlearned dense quadratic discrepancy. The proposed useful
ingredient is fresh, cheap operational validation coupled to a constructive
model search and an actual later optimizer. Strong ordinary methods are allowed
to use it. This construction presently establishes an acquisition-complete
capability and a credible research mechanism; it does not establish historical
priority or a general superiority over a carefully composed conventional route.
