OPERATION-QUOTIENT EXPERIMENTS FOR FIXED-COMPOSITION INVERSE DESIGN
Ordinary construction and proof, 31 October 2026

WHAT CHANGES

The scientific task fixes the number k of selected components but permits an
arbitrary later vector of component-specific fields h. Both k and h can be
chosen adaptively. The unknown energy is allowed to be

 E(x) = g(S(x)) + Q(x),       S(x)=sum_i x_i,

where g is completely arbitrary and unknown, while Q is an unknown quadratic
multilinear polynomial. g may be large, nonpolynomial, and impossible to infer
from the available experiment count. Its value is constant on every feasible
set {x: number of +1 spins=k}, so it cannot affect this scientific choice.

The acquired knowledge should therefore identify the operation modulo this
irrelevant energy, rather than approximate E on the whole cube. Paired
same-composition experiments cancel g before either learning or validation.
The representation is a learned executable surrogate plus a certificate in
the quotient relevant to the actual operation.

This note owns the residual-certificate and covariance details. A parent
construction supplies paired-contrast forest-IHT acquisition. The executable
here also supplies a strong ordinary O(n^2)-configuration alternative based on
four-point tomography, removal of common coefficient offsets, maximum-weight
forest projection, and actual cardinality tree DP. That alternative is a
baseline to adopt, not a weakened adversary.

1. ACCESS AND TASK SCOPE

A prepared configuration yields an independent mean-E(x) energy measurement
with centered sigma^2-sub-Gaussian error. Both measurements in each contrast
are charged separately, together with preparation, random bits, numerical
precision, and later optimization. This is not a reset-to-an-unobserved-state
oracle. It does require preparing full configurations and absolute energy
resolution. An O(n)-range normalized Bernoulli instrument has effective
sigma=O(n), which must be substituted in every cost formula.

An energy readout and numerical subtraction must retain enough bits to resolve
the stated absolute error despite large g. If measured values have magnitude
2^B, bit cost includes B. Large nuisance terms are cancelled mathematically,
not through free infinitely precise hardware or floating point. Rational
interval arithmetic and a declared readout error bound give a finite-bit
version. No efficiency claim independent of source dynamic range is made.

Future requests minimize E(x)+h^T x subject to # {i:x_i=+1}=k. k may be any
integer in [0,n], h arbitrary. A cardinality tree DP for the learned forest
is actually implemented. The guarantee does not cover unconstrained choice
of k, because g would then matter. It does not make arbitrary unrelated
constraints computationally cheap.

2. THE EXPERIMENTAL CONTRAST

Draw X uniformly from {-1,+1}^n. Draw an independent uniformly random coordinate
permutation pi and set Y=pi X. Then S(Y)=S(X). Conditional on S=s, X and Y
are independent uniform configurations of that composition: the conditional
distribution of pi X is uniform on the orbit for every fixed X in the orbit.
Permutation generation costs O(n) elementary random choices / swaps.

For a candidate quadratic F, the paired residual is

 D = [E(X)-F(X)] - [E(Y)-F(Y)]
   = [Q(X)-F(X)] - [Q(Y)-F(Y)].

g cancels exactly. No estimate of g, its derivative, or its coefficients is
used. The candidate may be acquired from any past observations. Its own
validator uses fresh pairs and fresh independent source measurements.

Let r=Q-F, and let Pi r(x)=E_pi r(pi x). Pi is the orthogonal projection in
uniform-cube L2 onto permutation-invariant functions. For a quadratic r,

 Pi r(x)=c + mean_i b_i * S(x)
                + mean_{i<j} J_ij * sum_{i<j} x_i x_j.

Thus r_perp=r-Pi r is still quadratic and is conditionally mean zero given S.
Its squared norm is explicitly

 v=||r_perp||_2^2
   =sum_i (b_i-mean b)^2
      +sum_{i<j}(J_ij-mean_{a<b}J_ab)^2.

This formula is for the proof / fixture verification. The validator does not
learn these coefficients or evaluate Pi r.

3. DIMENSION-INDEPENDENT RESIDUAL WITNESSING

For any centered degree-two polynomial on independent Rademachers,
E r_perp^4<=15v^2, as proved by Gaussian replacement in
OPERATIONAL_RESIDUAL_CERTIFICATE.txt.

Conditional independence of X,Y on their orbit gives

 E D^2=2v,
 E[D^4 | S]=2 E[r_perp^4 | S]
                 +6(E[r_perp^2 | S])^2.

Jensen and the fourth-moment bound imply

 E D^4<=8 E r_perp^4<=120v^2,
 Var(D^2)<=116v^2.

Use block size B_orbit=464. Each block mean of D^2 lies in [v,3v] with
probability at least 3/4 by Chebyshev. With an odd number

 L>=8 log(4/delta)

of independent blocks, the median of exact block RMS values lies in
[sqrt(v),sqrt(3v)] except with probability <=delta/4.

There are N=464L pairs. Average

 M=max(1, ceil[4 sigma^2/nu^2 * log(4N/delta)])

independent energy observations at each of their 2N configurations. After
subtracting F's values, every pair residual has measurement error <=nu except
with total probability <=delta/2. Block RMS and their median each change by
at most nu. Hence report

 S_cert=median(observed block RMS)+nu.

With probability at least 1-delta,

        sqrt(v)<=S_cert<=sqrt(3v)+2nu.                     (1)

The sufficient block constant is not a lower bound. It can be replaced by
sharper robust variance estimation. The executable uses the displayed finite
bill, including every actual energy-noise draw.

4. ONE CERTIFICATE FOR ALL FUTURE COMPOSITIONS AND FIELDS

Let K=n+n(n-1)/2. Fourier Cauchy-Schwarz gives

 sup_x |r_perp(x)|<=sqrt(K)*sqrt(v).

If x,y have the same S, both g and Pi r cancel from their energy difference.
Consequently

 |(E-F)(x)-(E-F)(y)| <=2 sqrt(K)*sqrt(v).

If the implemented surrogate optimizer returns xhat for (k,h) with known
surrogate suboptimality alpha, then

 E(xhat)+h^T xhat - min_{#plus=k}[E(x)+h^T x]
        <= alpha+Gamma,
 Gamma=2 sqrt(K)*S_cert.                                  (2)

This holds on one validation event simultaneously for every k and every h,
including adaptive choices after all previous outputs and validation data.
The guarantee is not averaged over composition, even though the experiment
draws composition from its binomial distribution. Polynomial structure of
r_perp turns its experimentally estimated norm into the uniform bound.

For required regret epsilon*n, accept Gamma<=epsilon*n. Because sqrt(K) is
Theta(n), nu can be Theta(epsilon). The source count is

 2*464*L*M = O(log(1/delta)
            *max{1,sigma^2/epsilon^2*log(log(1/delta)/delta)}),

with explicit constants above. Configuration preparation costs O(n*464*L)
bit operations, and evaluating a forest F costs O(n) per configuration.
Every raw energy has its own precision cost depending on its dynamic range.

For candidate t use fresh validation and delta_t=6delta/(pi^2 t^2), or the
rational telescoping allocation delta_t=delta/[t(t+1)]. Conditional on the
past, all arguments remain valid. A union bound gives probability <=delta
of any false certificate throughout adaptive candidate search. Failed holdout
data can train the next candidate; its new validator must be fresh.

5. EXACT COVARIANCE FOR ACQUISITION

Let psi(x) contain the n linear monomials and N2=n(n-1)/2 pair monomials. Their
uniform-cube covariance is identity. Define the normalized contrast row

 a=[psi(X)-psi(Y)]/sqrt(2).

The cross covariance E psi(X)psi(Y)^T equals

 P=diag(11^T/n, 11^T/N2),

with absent empty blocks interpreted naturally. Cross-degree blocks vanish.
Therefore

                       E aa^T=I-P.                       (3)

Equation (3) is exact: paired observations remove precisely the uniform unary
and uniform pair-coupling directions from this quadratic coefficient space.
These are operationally irrelevant at fixed composition.

Center unary coefficients by enforcing sum_i b_i=0. If the edge coefficient
vector has at most s nonzeros, Cauchy-Schwarz gives

 ||P theta||^2=(sum_e J_e)^2/N2 <=(s/N2)||J||^2,

and hence

 (1-s/N2)||theta||^2 <= E(a^T theta)^2 <=||theta||^2.       (4)

For a union of q forests, s<=q(n-1), so the deterministic isometry loss is
at most 2q/n. Very small n / tiny groups can contain exact null directions;
one must work in the quotient or handle such finite cases directly, not claim
uniform coefficient identifiability from an annihilated direction.

An elementary restricted sub-Gaussian fact makes the sampling rate strong.
For a forest support, the products X_i X_j along its edges are independent
Rademachers: choose one independent root spin per tree and independent edge
products, then reconstruct every vertex spin. Thus a pure forest quadratic
has sub-Gaussian proxy equal to its edge-coefficient squared norm. The unary
sum has proxy ||b||^2. Partition the edges of a union of q forests into q
disjoint forests. Holder's inequality across these q+1 parts gives

 E exp(t theta^T psi(X))
       <=exp[(q+1)t^2||theta||^2/2].

X,Y need not be unconditionally independent. Cauchy-Schwarz and their common
uniform marginal still give

 E exp(t a^T theta)<=exp[(q+1)t^2||theta||^2].              (5)

Thus contrast rows are sub-Gaussian with constant proxy on the union-q-forest
class. Standard epsilon-net covariance concentration, union-bounded over at
most N2^{q(n-1)} supports and their O(qn)-dimensional unit-sphere nets, yields
restricted isometry around (3) with

 m=O((q+1)^2 * gamma^-2 * [qn log n + log(1/delta)]),

up to universal constants, and total distortion gamma+2q/n. q is a constant
for the support unions in model-IHT. This proof does not require pretending
all K global features have a full-rank isotropic covariance.

The approximate-model residual also has a simple constant-probability bound:
if its quotient coefficient norm is eta, the expected normalized empirical
contrast-residual squared norm is eta^2. Markov makes it <=4eta^2 with
probability >=3/4. Averaged measurement noise has analogous expected squared
norm <=sigma^2/M. Thus stable model-IHT can use a constant-probability good
acquisition followed by an independently sound certificate, retrying when
needed. This avoids requiring a sharp unproved high-probability agnostic-tail
correlation theorem. The parent construction supplies the contraction, actual
projection, and total acquisition budget.

6. STRONG ORDINARY ACQUISITION ALTERNATIVE

Four-point tomography remains a strong baseline even when g is nonpolynomial.
At all-plus and its one/two flips, the estimated pair coefficient equals

 Jtilde_ij=J_ij+c_g,
 c_g=[g(n)-2g(n-2)+g(n-4)]/4,

apart from measurement noise. The paired all-plus/all-minus unary identity
similarly returns b_i plus a common unknown scalar. Mean-centering unary
coefficients removes the irrelevant unary scalar exactly. A robust common
offset estimate (e.g. median of pair coefficients for a sparse forest plus
small dense perturbation) removes c_g without learning g.

Then compute a maximum-spanning forest with squared corrected coefficients
and solve future cardinality-constrained field problems by the actual tree DP.
This costs O(n^2) energy configurations in its pilot; individual repetitions,
numeric cancellation, and control changes are charged. It can be much cheaper
than the generic sufficient sparse-IHT constants on a finite fixture. It must
be included in a fair comparison.

The orbit validator is independent of how this candidate was proposed. If its
offset estimate or structure is wrong, the certificate refuses it except on
its declared failure event. A maximum-spanning forest is not claimed to be the
best projection in the quotient metric; the baseline's exact Euclidean
projection property applies only after the selected common-offset correction.

7. SPECIES / MULTIPLE COMPOSITION COUNTS

For a known partition of the coordinates into species, use independent uniform
permutations within each species. The nuisance can be any unknown function of
the vector of species counts. Group averaging preserves polynomial degree;
the same conditional-iid argument and fourth-moment proof apply. The quadratic
projection averages unary coefficients within a species and pair coefficients
within each within-species or between-species edge block. Formula (3) becomes
a block-diagonal collection of these averaging projections. On sparse edge
supports the deterministic loss is controlled by the maximal occupied fraction
of an edge block; small blocks may be entirely invariant and must be quotiented.

The certificate remains valid for all future fixed-species-count tasks. Their
execution is not free: tree DP now carries a count vector and costs polynomial
in the product of the count ranges, which can be exponential in the number of
species. The executable and main rate here use one actual total-composition
constraint, for which O(n^2) tree DP is earned.

8. NATURAL FAILURE EXAMPLES AND BOUNDARIES

 - Large arbitrary composition energy g(S) makes an ordinary global residual
   fit or certificate fail its assumptions / budget even when every desired
   fixed-composition choice is unchanged. Same-orbit contrasts remove it.
 - Learning from only the central slice does not protect all compositions:
   a quadratic (S-s0)*(a^T x) vanishes on S=s0 but changes choices elsewhere.
   Random X followed by a same-orbit Y samples all compositions with the
   prescribed law, and the degree-two moment/norm argument protects all of
   them. No inaccessible single-slice extrapolation is presumed.
 - Fixed-composition guarantees cannot be used to choose composition itself.
   E=g(S) and F=0 are indistinguishable by every orbit contrast; their globally
   optimal compositions can differ arbitrarily. This is a precise operational
   nonidentifiability statement, not a numerical weakness.
 - An arbitrary noninvariant higher-order spike is not protected. Only g may
   be arbitrary; its cancellation is algebraic and task-specific.
 - Reused adaptive holdouts remain invalid within the quotient: a nonzero
   noninvariant quadratic can be chosen in the nullspace of finitely many old
   contrast rows, pass them perfectly, and fail a fresh validator.

PRIMARY PRECEDENTS TO COMPARE, NOT BYPASS

Fixed-composition cluster expansions, harmonic analysis on Boolean slices,
contrast / common-mode experimental designs, model-based compressed sensing,
robust residual norm estimation, and exact tree/cardinality dynamic programming
are strong antecedents. The proposed organizing principle is their paid
integration around the quotient of a consequential operation. No claim that
matched contrasts, invariance, or these numerical components are new is made.
