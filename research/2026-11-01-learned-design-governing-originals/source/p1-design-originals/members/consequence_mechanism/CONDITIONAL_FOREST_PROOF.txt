Ordinary construction and proof, 31 October 2026

1. THE PREVIOUS REPRESENTATION WAS UNNECESSARILY RESTRICTIVE

Keep the physical two-site modules, occupancy-fixed design requests, arbitrary
site fields, and same-occupancy experiments from MODULE_ORBIT_CERTIFICATE.txt.
The unknown energy is g(c)+quadratic, with arbitrary unknown occupancy-only g.
Use q_a=(x_a1-x_a2)/sqrt(2), s_a=(x_a1+x_a2)/sqrt(2).

The previous group-forest model required the joint support of qq, qs and sq
terms to be a forest. That is not the operation's actual computational
requirement. Once occupancies are specified, s_b is fixed and q_a is either
zero or a binary orientation. Every q_a*s_b term is therefore unary in a
remaining decision. Such terms can have an arbitrary directed support,
including many cycles or a dense graph, without creating a hard optimization
interaction. Only qq terms couple simultaneous free decisions.

The acquired family is consequently

 F(x)=sum_a b_a q_a
       +sum_{(a,b) in T} J_ab q_a q_b
       +sum_{(a,b) in C} L_ab q_a s_b,

where T is a forest and C is any directed support of size <=t (a!=b). Dense
cross-context dependence is allowed by taking t=k(k-1), with its honest
quadratic retention and execution cost. No claim of O(k) execution is made
when t is dense. The useful qq forest and cross support are both acquired.

2. EXACT PROJECTION, WITH NO STRUCTURE ORACLE

The q/s noninvariant basis is orthonormal under independent uniform original
site spins. For any fitted coefficient vector theta, squared Euclidean error
splits into independent sums over unary, qq, and cross coefficients.

The exact best projection onto the stated model is therefore:

 (i) keep all k unary coefficients;
 (ii) among qq coefficients choose a maximum-weight forest with edge weight
      J_ab^2, implemented by Kruskal, and retain selected coefficients;
 (iii) among all k(k-1) directed cross coefficients retain the t largest
       squared magnitudes.

For any fixed support, keeping the supplied coefficient is its exact least-
squares projection. Choosing the support minimizes the omitted squared norm,
equivalently maximizes retained squared weight. The qq support constraint is
the graphic matroid; the cross support constraint is just a cardinality bound.
There is no coupling between these two optimizations, proving exactness.
Tie-breaking can be arbitrary. Sorting/Kruskal and top-t selection have fully
explicit polynomial cost, e.g. O(k^2 log k) arithmetic with direct sorting.
The implementation orders absolute coefficient magnitudes instead of forming
their squares: this is exactly the same ordering and avoids overflow or a
rounded square comparison for finite binary inputs. Selected coefficients are
copied unchanged in the unrounded learning projection.

project_conditional_coefficients implements this projection without rounding
and is the intended IHT integration interface. conditional_iht supplies an
ordinary projected-gradient helper, explicitly recording the training residual
history. It does not assert a RIP or turn its training residual into a global
certificate. The parent construction supplies the acquisition guarantee.

The broad sparse class no longer has the earlier constant-arboricity moment
property when C is arbitrary. One must not reuse the old group-forest
restricted sub-Gaussian proof unchanged. The paired noninvariant basis still
has exact identity covariance and bounded entries, so bounded-orthonormal-
system sparse recovery is an appropriate conventional acquisition route.
Its sparsity is s=k+(k-1)+t, with support unions charged in the usual way.

3. ACTUAL EXECUTOR

Write d_a=(x_a1-x_a2)/2 and z_a=(x_a1+x_a2)/2. They belong to {-1,0,+1}.
For a requested occupancy c_a, z_a=c_a-1 is fixed. If c_a=1 then d_a is a
free sign; otherwise d_a=0.

The coefficients in these coordinates are

 omega_a=sqrt(2)b_a, K_ab=2J_ab, M_ab=2L_ab.

For active modules A={a:c_a=1}, the design energy, apart from context constants,
is

 sum_{a in A} [omega_a + h_a1-h_a2
                   +sum_{(a,b) in C} M_ab(c_b-1)] d_a
       +sum_{(a,b) in T, a,b in A} K_ab d_a d_b.

Scan each cross coefficient once to form these unary fields. The active qq
graph is an induced subforest. Two-state tree dynamic programming and
backtracking return its exact minimizer. Construction, execution, and retained
storage cost O(k+t), up to coefficient-bit arithmetic. The implementation
retains only unaries, qq edges, and cross terms; dense coefficient reconstruction
occurs only through an explicit coefficient_vector() call for learning/debugging.

The arbitrary g(c) never has to be predicted, represented, or evaluated by this
executor. Choosing c is still outside the learned operation, since changing g
can alter the best occupancy while leaving every contrast unchanged.

4. FINITE COMPILED OUTPUT AND ROUNDING

The compiled class quantizes omega, K and M onto a declared binary grid of
denominator 2^B (B=40 in the fixture). This is the actual candidate F that is
validated afterward. Therefore rounding is not silently added to an earlier
certificate; any resulting model error is part of the subsequently tested
residual. The learning projection helper remains unrounded for theoretical IHT.

Evaluation uses integer monomials d_a, z_a. With a proved sum-of-absolute-
coefficient bound below 2^62, the implementation safely uses int64 arithmetic;
otherwise it uses Python arbitrary-precision integers. Conversion to a floating
source-comparison value occurs only at the final division, with its error
included in the supplied contrast-rounding interval. An unsupported dynamic
range raises an explicit error rather than silently returning an infinite value.

For each actual design request, finite floating input fields are interpreted
as their exactly represented binary rationals; Fraction and integer fields
are also supported. A common denominator for the compiled coefficients and
requested fields is constructed. The dynamic program uses only exact integer
addition and comparison, then emits the literal site signs {-1,+1}. This is
an exact minimizer of the compiled finite candidate for that input, including
ties. It does not claim to be an exact minimizer of the unknown physical energy;
the independent operational certificate supplies its stated regret bound.
The output work record includes actual denominator bit length, largest dynamic-
program integer bit length, and input-field numerator size, rather than hiding
large coefficients behind a count of arithmetic operations.

The numerical size of cross terms is not free. Their coefficient bit length,
query-field denominator, large nuisance energy readout precision, and source
variance scale are charged. Huge coefficients cannot be hidden behind an
O(k+t) arithmetic-operation statement.

5. CERTIFICATION, INCLUDING EXACT ACCEPTANCE ARITHMETIC

All quotient residual proofs from MODULE_ORBIT_CERTIFICATE.txt remain valid:
the candidate is still quadratic, even though its cross support is cyclic or
dense. The statistical replicate-product validator requires no assumption that
the residual shares the candidate's forest structure. It tests an unknown dense
quadratic quotient residual and protects all adaptive occupancy contexts/fields.

The main fixture calls statistical_validate_group_action with

 exact_statistics=True,
 feature_radius_squared=2*k*k,
 contrast_rounding_radius=1e-8.

Once the finite observed U,V are formed, products, the deterministic rounding
correction, block averages, medians and variance-scale addition are evaluated
as exact rational numbers. Acceptance compares

 4*(2k^2)*(certified squared quotient norm) <= (epsilon*2k)^2

using exact rational arithmetic; an uncertified square root does not control
acceptance. Displayed square roots are rounded upward and checked by exact
rational squaring. The result records numerator/denominator bit sizes.

The physical source is still a simulation with finite floating Gaussian draws.
The prototype does not claim that an arbitrary physical instrument supplies
the stipulated mean/variance/readout contracts automatically. Its deterministic
rounding guard is explicitly supplied; the ordinary theorem supports an actual
instrument or interval implementation satisfying that bound.

6. STRONG CONVENTIONAL ACQUISITION AND EXECUTED DISCRIMINATING FIXTURE

full_contrast_conditional_regression uses the same fully paid energy oracle to
learn all D=k+3 choose(k,2) noninvariant coefficients by ordinary full contrast
least squares. It constructs BOTH candidate families from the same fitted
vector: the old group forest and the new operation-specific forest+cross model.
Thus the comparison does not give the new learner extra observations or a
preselected useful structure.

The main fixture has k=24, hence 48 physical sites and 3^24=282429536481
occupancy contexts. Its qq support has 23 strong tree edges; it has 96 strong
directed cross coefficients on several cyclic offsets, plus a dense generic
noninvariant perturbation. An arbitrary occupancy-only nuisance has amplitude
10^6. The useful graph and cross supports are not given to either learner.

The full conventional pilot uses 3408 paired design rows / 6816 energy shots
and learns all 852 noninvariant coefficients. Its singular values and source
costs are recorded. Exact qq-forest / top-96-cross projection retains only
24+23+96=143 scalar coefficients. Both candidates receive separate fresh
statistical validators with a summable confidence allocation.

The group-forest candidate fails its uniform regret certificate and incurs
observed true regret about 9.18836 on an enumerated context, above the requested
0.96. The operation-specific candidate passes, with a certificate about
0.063839; its checked contexts have regret within numerical zero on this fixture.
The all-active context is executed with exact integer DP, but its full 2^24
orientation space is not enumerated. Fifteen other requested contexts have at
most eight active modules and are checked by complete orientation enumeration.
The results distinguish that finite check from the proved all-context bound.

The full retained OLS route additionally supplies a same-data confidence-ball
certificate of about 0.094071 with zero extra shots. This remains a serious
strong ordinary alternative. The sparse IHT route's potential information
advantage must be compared against this full operation-aware regression,
not against raw prediction of the arbitrary nuisance g or a naive grouped
graph learner that fails to use the operation.
