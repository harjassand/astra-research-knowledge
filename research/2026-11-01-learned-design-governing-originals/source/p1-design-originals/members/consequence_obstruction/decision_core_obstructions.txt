DECISION CORES: STRONG BASELINES AND ALIGNED OBSTRUCTIONS
=======================================================

Task under discussion
---------------------
An unknown binary energy has fixed coefficients J and supports later
inverse-design queries with chosen fields h:

 E_J(x;h) = sum_{i<j} J_ij x_i x_j + sum_i h_i x_i, x_i in {-1,+1}.

The useful output is a configuration with small additive energy regret,
or an exact optimum if the actual scientific task needs one. A learner
must pay for its observations and for optimization. A number called
"ambiguity-core size" is a useful sufficient cost parameter only if
the core is actually acquired and its residual optimization is executed.

1. The ordinary route is substantially stronger than dominance alone
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Local worst-neighbor elimination is classical dead-end elimination
(DEE). Roof duality / QPBO adds polynomial-time persistent partial
labelings. Improving mappings unify DEE, QPBO, Kovtun methods and
iterative pruning: p is improving when E(p(x)) <= E(x) for every x.
Improving mappings compose, and restricting to their image preserves
an optimum. Verification for arbitrary mappings is itself NP-hard;
LP-relaxed sufficient certificates are a conventional tractable route.

Relevant primary sources:

Alexander Shekhovtsov, Maximum Persistency in Energy Minimization,
CVPR 2014 author slides, especially pages on DEE, QPBO, verification,
and relaxed improving mappings:
https://cmp.felk.cvut.cz/~shekhovt/publications/shekhovtsov-14-improving-mapping-slides.pdf

Rother, Kolmogorov, Lempitsky, Szummer, Optimizing Binary MRFs via
Extended Roof Duality, CVPR 2007, author PDF:
https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~pcarr/qpbo/RotherCVPR2007.pdf

Implementations must compare against these, or state explicitly that a
weaker elementary implementation is only a proof vehicle. In particular,
a ferromagnetic graph with zero or tiny fields defeats scalar dominance
at every vertex, but is globally solvable by a minimum cut. Thus a large
scalar-dominance core is not evidence of computational hardness.

The statistical half also has a strong existing route. Regard each
configuration as the feature vector (x_i, x_i x_j). Combinatorial pure
exploration and transductive linear bandits retain samples and actively
sample coordinates / experiments whose uncertainty changes the answer.
Their efficient versions invoke optimization over the feature-action
set. Here that oracle is the very QUBO we must actually solve.

Tongyi Cao and Akshay Krishnamurthy, Disagreement-Based Combinatorial
Pure Exploration: Sample Complexity Bounds and an Efficient Algorithm,
COLT 2019:
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v99/cao19a.html

Yihan Du, Yuko Kuroki, Wei Chen, Combinatorial Pure Exploration with
Full-Bandit or Partial Linear Feedback, AAAI 2021:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.07905

Andrew Wagenmaker, Julian Katz-Samuels, Kevin Jamieson, Experimental
Design for Regret Minimization in Linear Bandits, AISTATS 2021:
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v130/wagenmaker21a.html

The potentially useful missing operation is therefore earning and
executing the optimization oracle throughout acquisition. It is not
merely another confidence interval or sampling only disputed variables.
Confidence-set alternatives can be harder to optimize than the true
instance. A cost claim must account for those alternate-model queries.

2. Arbitrary future fields destroy a fixed dominance guarantee
------------------------------------------------------------
At a currently unpinned vertex, scalar dominance requires the effective
field to dominate the sum of absolute incident couplings. If future
fields are unrestricted, h can cancel every current effective field.
There is no uniform nonempty elimination set furnished by that rule.
For h=0 on a connected nontrivial graph, every vertex fails the strict
test. The same failure happens with arbitrarily small strictly positive
fields on a connected ferromagnet, despite a unique easy global optimum.

This does not invalidate query-specific cores. It rules out treating a
small core learned for one field as an all-future-fields capability
without a restricted field family or a fresh query-dependent certificate.

3. Exact all-fields service would identify real parameters exactly
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Already take a single unknown unary coefficient theta and energy

             E_theta(x;h)=(theta+h)x, x in {-1,+1}.

Any service correct for every rational h locates the switching threshold
-theta exactly by binary search. Thus such a service computes theta
exactly from its retained state. If theta has a continuous prior and all
finite observation sequences have likelihood densities with respect to
a common dominating measure, any almost surely finite noisy data set
leaves a nonatomic posterior. Additional randomness independent of theta
cannot identify theta exactly. The probability of the all-fields exact
service is therefore zero on average over the prior, hence it cannot
be uniformly at least 1-delta for all theta for any delta<1.

This covers an almost surely finite adaptive acquisition phase as well:
condition on its stopping time and finite transcript, then sum over the
countable possible stopping times. The argument does not prohibit fresh
experiments during later queries, nor a positive decision-margin promise.

For a pairwise interaction J_ij, assume known bounds on total coupling
strength so sufficiently large chosen fields can force other spins.
Threshold spin i once with spin j forced to +1 and once with j forced
to -1, keeping all other forced spins unchanged. Half the difference of
the two switching thresholds recovers J_ij (up to its known sign
convention). The nuisance interactions cancel. A numerical model with
finite fixed calibration therefore cannot promise exact optimization
over all later arbitrarily tuned fields.

4. Decision precision has a real one-bit cost
-------------------------------------------
Suppose the unknown unary theta is either +a or -a. Each direct assay
observes N(theta,sigma^2); this is an explicitly stated strong source,
not a presumed property of an arbitrary physical experiment. At h=0,
selecting the wrong sign costs 2a energy. If epsilon<2a, a uniformly
delta-correct epsilon-regret algorithm distinguishes the two sources.
For any adaptive sequential algorithm with finite expected stopping,
the likelihood-ratio chain rule and data processing imply

 E_{+a}[tau] * (2a^2/sigma^2) >= kl(1-delta,delta),

where kl denotes binary relative entropy. This is a usual testing lower
bound, not an exactness-specific obstacle. It remains after the entire
optimization problem has become computationally trivial.

5. Aggregate regret can require cubic-in-dimension scalar observation
   cost even when there are no interactions to optimize
--------------------------------------------------------------------
There are m independent unknown unary coefficients theta_i in {-a,+a}.
An experiment chooses a coordinate i and returns a Bernoulli bit with
mean 1/2 + theta_i/(2B), where a<=B/2. Thus 2B X-B is an unbiased noisy
measurement of theta_i, and the number of source bits is explicitly
charged. The decision energy is E_theta(x)=sum_i theta_i x_i.

Set a=4 epsilon/m, assuming epsilon<=mB/8. Suppose an algorithm returns
regret at most epsilon with probability >=1-delta for every theta,
where delta<=1/16. Under the uniform prior on signs, its expected regret
is at most epsilon + delta (2am) <= 3epsilon/2.

Let D include the complete adaptively acquired data, stopping time,
experiment choices, and independent algorithmic random bits. Conditional
on the past, one observed bit has mutual information about all hidden
signs at most

 KL(Ber(1/2 +/- a/(2B)) || Ber(1/2)) <= a^2/B^2,

using KL <= chi-square. (The first bound follows by taking the fixed
fair coin as a reference in the conditional information-radius identity.)
Consequently I(theta;D) <= (a^2/B^2) E[tau].

For independent prior signs, sum_i I(theta_i;D)<=I(theta;D).
Pinsker applied to each posterior sign distribution, followed by
Cauchy-Schwarz, bounds the smallest expected Hamming error H by

 E H >= m/2 - sqrt(m I(theta;D)/2).

If E[tau] <= m B^2/(8a^2), then I<=m/8 and E H>=m/4. The expected
energy regret is then at least 2a(m/4)=2epsilon, contradicting the
upper bound. Hence the worst-case expected number of scalar bit assays
must exceed

                      B^2 m^3/(128 epsilon^2).

This is not a claim that all observation models pay this cost. A single
full-vector assay can supply m bits, and a different controlled source
can have much more information. The observation contract must specify
and charge this distinction. The result shows that small computational
cores alone do not bound acquisition cost: this instance has no pairwise
optimization difficulty at all. At per-coordinate regret epsilon rather
than total regret epsilon, the scaling changes accordingly.

6. Model uncertainty can contain combinatorial hardness at every scale
---------------------------------------------------------------------
An interval box J_ij in [-rho,rho] around the identically zero energy
contains scaled versions of all bounded-weight Max-Cut instances. Exact
optimization remains NP-hard under arbitrarily small positive scaling.
This is computational fragility, not evidence that small perturbations
prevent useful approximate optimization: if total energy oscillation is
below the allowed regret, every output is acceptable.

More explicitly, for even n, any fixed candidate x, and the complete
graph, choose J_ij=rho x_i x_j. In coordinates y_i=x_i z_i the candidate
has y_i=1 for all i and energy rho*n(n-1)/2. A balanced y has energy
-rho*n/2, so the candidate's regret is rho*n^2/2. A uniform guarantee
from this interval box by returning an arbitrary candidate therefore
needs rho<=2epsilon/n^2. This counterexample exposes what a globally
valid confidence box has to exclude; it is not a general sample lower
bound because more informative experiments and coupled confidence sets
can improve the acquisition route.

7. Useful direction after these failures
----------------------------------------
Earn a query-specific computational certificate jointly with the
statistical evidence that makes it valid. The certificate should say
which concrete downstream computations remain and bound their cost,
without optimizing all statistically plausible models along the way.
Strong DEE/QPBO/improving-map and pure-exploration methods should be
adopted as components. A substantive new result would have to prove an
information/computation advantage at that interface, or deliver an
operation those components cannot already supply under the same access.

This note supplies comparators and obstructions. It does not assert
that such an advantage has yet been constructed.

8. Finite contrasts cannot certify an unrestricted orientation law
------------------------------------------------------------------
For the module quotient, fix B*=(1,...,1), whose feasible orbit has
L=2^k orientations. Consider the zero energy E0 and, for a hidden
orientation u in that orbit, the alternative

                 Eu(x)=-H 1{x=u},  H>epsilon*(2k).

Take noiseless observations; this is already an allowed subcase of a
subgaussian observation model. Until u is queried, all absolute readings
and all paired contrasts are identical in the two worlds. Run any adaptive
algorithm on E0, including its internal randomness. If it makes at most N
source queries, its queried set and its later output for (B*,h=0) together
contain at most N+1 orientations. Choose u uniformly in the orbit before
running the experiment. Coupling to the zero-world transcript gives

   P(query u or output u) <= (N+1)/2^k.

Otherwise the output has regret H. Hence some fixed u defeats the proposed
uniform guarantee with failure probability at least 1-(N+1)/2^k. An
expected-budget version replaces N by the expected number of queries on
the zero world. If the procedure is a certificate that accepts the zero
surrogate with probability p on E0, its average false-acceptance probability
over hidden spikes is at least p-(N+1)/2^k. Rejecting every candidate avoids
false acceptance but does not acquire a useful service.

The conclusion is a sample-complexity obstruction, not the assertion that
no finite data set can ever suffice: exhaustive acquisition is finite.
Polynomially many contrasts cannot certify the unrestricted law class.
The spike is a higher-degree polynomial on binary controls and violates the
quadratic-quotient premise. Its invariant orbit mean can be absorbed into g;
the remaining non-invariant part is still a hidden high-degree spike. Thus
the low-degree premise is essential and cannot itself be inferred from a
successful finite residual check.

This failure does not require a hard optimizer. One can instead hide an
unknown unary preference at one resource context:

   E(x)=H sigma 1{B(x)=B*} q_a(x)/sqrt(2),  B*_a=1.

Each individual resource-constrained problem is linear in its orientations.
An unobserved B* still leaves its preferred sign undetermined. An arbitrary
invariant g(B) is harmless for within-context decisions; an arbitrary
context-dependent coefficient multiplying q_a is not. The generalisation
boundary is therefore informational as well as computational.

9. Exact all-fields service identifies the full module quotient
---------------------------------------------------------------
Section 3 applies directly within a single active module, even with arbitrary
g. Here is the full module version, clarifying what an exact service would
implicitly reveal.

Write the non-invariant law as

 Q_perp = sum_a b_a q_a + sum_{a<b} J_ab q_a q_b
                           + sum_{a!=b} C_ab q_a s_b.

Activate only module a and fix every other module b to occupancy 0 or 2.
Write t_b=B_b-1 in {-1,+1}. Since q_a=sqrt(2) z_a, its effective orientation
coefficient, before the chosen physical field difference v, is

            lambda_a(t)=sqrt(2)b_a+2 sum_{b!=a} C_ab t_b.

An exact service for all rational v chooses the sign of -(lambda_a(t)+v).
Its switching threshold determines lambda_a(t) exactly. Flipping one t_b
changes lambda_a by 4 C_ab, which identifies every cross coefficient.
The unary coefficient b_a follows after subtracting these known terms.

Next activate modules a and b. Their qq term is 2 J_ab z_a z_b. With the
other terms known, force z_b successively to +1 and -1 using sufficiently
large fields (a known coefficient-range bound suffices). The two switching
thresholds of z_a differ by 4 J_ab, identifying the qq coefficient. Thus an
exact all-resource/all-fields service identifies the full non-invariant
coefficient vector, though it still cannot identify g.

Under a continuous coefficient prior and a finite adaptive acquisition with
ordinary everywhere-positive noisy likelihoods, the posterior is nonatomic.
The finite retained state and independent later random bits therefore cannot
identify those real coefficients exactly with positive uniform confidence.
Countably many rational field queries already suffice for the threshold
argument. This is why the selected theorem must promise bounded physical
regret while computing an exact minimizer only of its fitted surrogate.

For the constructive theorem, completion is for each fixed physical law.
The dense-tail Markov step E||Ar||_2^2=||r||_2^2 is not one event uniform over
dense residuals selected after A is known. Fresh validation does provide the
separate, intended uniformity over all later resource vectors and fields.
