LATENT BINARY CONTRACTION: ORDINARY ROUTES AND SMALL OBSTRUCTIONS
31 October 2026. Companion mathematics, not a priority claim.

1. The strongest ordinary route should receive the same scalar experiments

For a chosen binary x, observe E(x) plus fresh sigma-subgaussian noise. Write
the identifiable quadratic law as c + sum b_i x_i + sum_{i<j} J_ij x_i x_j.
There are D=1+n+n(n-1)/2 orthonormal Walsh coefficients. A conventional full
quadratic fit, spectral indicator recovery, a verified relational contraction,
and tree dynamic programming can implement the proposed operation. No speedup
over that task-aware composition is established. High rank or a dense signed
matrix alone does not rule out standard spectral or semidefinite methods.

Coordinate polarization uses O(n^2) distinct configurations, but if each
coefficient needs error a it generally pays O(sigma^2/a^2) repetitions per
coordinate. A much stronger classical design estimates every coefficient at
once with an exactly orthogonal feature matrix.

Let m=ceil(log2(n)), q=2^m, and choose n distinct a_i in the finite field F_q.
Use labels v_i=(1,a_i,a_i^3) in F_2^(1+2m). For every binary t of that length,
prepare x_i(t)=(-1)^(t dot v_i). There are N=2q^2<8n^2 configurations for n>1.
The degree-at-most-two feature labels are 0, v_i, and v_i+v_j (i<j).
They are all distinct. The leading coordinate separates unary and pair labels;
equality of two pair labels determines s=a_i+a_j !=0 and a_i^3+a_j^3.
Since a^3+b^3=(a+b)^3+ab(a+b) in characteristic two, it determines ab, hence
the unordered pair {a,b}. Distinct characters are orthogonal, so Phi^T Phi=N I.
Finite-field construction is not an oracle: enumerate monic degree-m binary
polynomials until an irreducible one is found, using modular exponentiation
and polynomial gcd tests. There are O(2^m)=O(n) candidates.

Repeat the whole array R times, M=NR. Then

  theta_hat_j = M^(-1) sum_l phi_j(x_l) Ehat_l

has conditional error subgaussian parameter sigma/sqrt(M). Hence

  P(max_j |theta_hat_j-theta_j|>a)
       <= 2D exp[-M a^2/(2 sigma^2)].

Thus M>=N and M>=2 sigma^2 a^(-2) log(2D/delta), rounded to a multiple of N,
provide the same entrywise event as coordinate polarization. For a=O(epsilon/n),
this gives O(n^2[1+sigma^2 epsilon^(-2)log(n/delta)]) scalar shots. Each complete
source configuration, actual preparation cost, and report bit length is still
charged. The Fourier coefficient sums can be computed by a fast Walsh transform
in O(N log N) arithmetic after averaging repetitions, without a dense OLS solve.
Quantized readings add their deterministic quantization bound; exact rational
verification remains available.

A sharper but optional bound uses all configurations only in the proof:
for any u, u dot (theta_hat-theta) is sigma||u||/sqrt(M)-subgaussian. Since
||phi(x)||^2=D, a union bound over 2^n possible x gives

 sup_x |Ehat(x)-E(x)| <= sigma sqrt[2D(n log2+log(2/delta))/M].

This event controls regret of every future-field surrogate optimizer at once.
It is not necessary for the simpler entrywise certificate above.

This is classical orthogonal-array/BCH experimental design machinery. A primary
modern source explicitly records O(n^2) strength-four BCH arrays, and proves
the orthogonality characterization: Carlet, Kiss, Nagy, Simplicity conditions
for binary orthogonal arrays, Designs Codes Cryptography 91 (2023), 151-163,
published 2022, Remark 5 and Lemma 6:
https://doi.org/10.1007/s10623-022-01105-4

2. Gauge and robust rounding certificate

Binary energies do not identify the diagonal of the quadratic matrix. Use its
observable zero-row-sum completion A=J_off-diag(J_off*1), with the convention
that energy is (1/2)x^T A x+b.x up to an additive constant.
For a proposed partition P, Dp=P^TP, S=Dp^(-1)P^T, W=P Dp^(-1)P^T, write

 C0=P^T A P, T=(I-W)A(I-W), F=W A(I-W).

For x binary let z=Sx, p=P sign(z), and let d_a be the minority count in block
a, with ties rounded arbitrarily. Dminor=sum d_a is an integer, and

 ||x-Wx||^2 = 4 sum_a d_a(1-d_a/m_a) in [2Dminor,4Dminor],
 ||x-p||_1=2Dminor,   ||Wx||<=sqrt(n).

If T>=gamma(I-W), alpha=max_a ||C0[a,:]||_1/m_a, and
B=||b||_infinity+H for future ||h||_infinity<=H, then rounding has gain at most

 E_h(p)-E_h(x)
 <=-[gamma-2alpha-2B-2sqrt(n)||F||_F] Dminor.

The cross bound uses |(Wx)^T F(x-Wx)|<=2sqrt(n)||F||_F sqrt(Dminor), followed by
sqrt(Dminor)<=Dminor for every unaligned binary input. Thus the strict margin
certifies all future fields in the box; it is not an assertion about all real
vectors or a continuous relaxation. PSD on W-perp can be verified by rational
LDL on an explicit within-block difference basis. A proposed partition need
not be the latent true partition for this sufficient certificate to be sound.

3. A small obstruction to treating plain Shor SDP as already exact

Take n=2, a single cluster, C=0,

 L=gamma(I-11^T/2), b=(h,-h), gamma>2h>0.

The two aligned binary points have energy zero. The two unaligned points have
energies gamma+2h and gamma-2h. Hence all binary minimizers are aligned.
In the standard Shor vector relaxation, choose anchor v0=(1,0),
v1=(a,sqrt(1-a^2)), v2=(-a,sqrt(1-a^2)). Its feasible objective is

 gamma a^2+2ha,

which at a=-h/gamma equals -h^2/gamma<0. Thus the ordinary Shor relaxation
is not automatically exact merely from finite locking dominance.
This is not a separation against SDP-based persistency, added inequalities,
probing, or roof duality: the two-node example is solved by a standard tree
or submodular method. A task-aware spectral/contraction certificate matches
the intended operation. Do not turn this narrow counterexample into a broad
claim against semidefinite methods.

4. Closest primary comparisons and their scope

Damle, Minden, Ying, Simple, direct and efficient multi-way spectral clustering,
Information and Inference 8(1),181-203 (2019), arXiv preprint 2016/2017:
https://doi.org/10.1093/imaiai/iay008
https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.08251
The work provides a direct QR-based recovery of cluster indicators from a
suitable approximate eigenspace. Hidden indicator recovery itself is standard.

Wang and Kleinberg, Analyzing Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization
Problems Via Multicommodity Flows, Discrete Applied Mathematics 157(18),
3746-3753 (2009):
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dam.2009.07.009
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2808708/
The paper explicitly studies relational persistencies/autarkies and merging
variables that agree or disagree in optimal assignments. Use this as prior
art for the operation, not as a verified claim that every spectral contraction
above is detected by that particular flow procedure.

Helmberg, Fixing Variables in Semidefinite Relaxations, SIAM J. Matrix Anal.
Appl.21(3),952-969 (2000), preliminary ESA1997 version:
https://doi.org/10.1137/S089547989631442X
Its primary abstract establishes SDP-based reduced-cost variable fixing for
quadratic binary optimization. The full article was not available through the
retrieved publisher/author endpoints, so no claim of an exact equivalence or
strict separation from its complete algorithm is made here.

Choi, Minor-Embedding in Adiabatic Quantum Computation: I. The Parameter Setting
Problem (2008), Theorem4.1:
https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.4884
This gives coupling-strength conditions ensuring that a supplied physical
spin tree acts as one logical variable and that contraction preserves minima.

Lobe and Kaibel, Optimal sufficient requirements on the embedded Ising problem
in polynomial time, Quantum Information Processing22,305 (2023):
https://doi.org/10.1007/s11128-023-04058-2
The given-embedding convention is explicit in section2.3. The paper derives
synchronization conditions and a polynomial parameter-setting procedure for
tree embeddings. This is closer than ordinary surrogate fitting: ground-state
preserving physical-to-logical contraction is already the operation of interest.

No primary paper with the exact full scalar-noisy-acquisition -> unknown dense
signed-locking partition -> uniform bounded-field certificate -> acquired
forest executor theorem was located in the targeted search. This is limited
search evidence, not a novelty proof. The defensible contribution is the
specified complete operation and a sufficient acquisition/certification theory.
