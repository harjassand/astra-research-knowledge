# A constructive comparator failure for rare-output transfer

This is a task-derived instantiation of an existing strong uncertainty
framework, not a refutation of that framework and not a novelty claim.

Subsequent stronger-baseline result: CYLINDER_COUPLING_BASELINE.md proves
that ordinary biased-product coupling already gives accurate rare mean
and deadline brackets for this cylinder, even for nonmonotone actual
rates and unknown actual stationary law. The entropy failure below
remains informative but is insufficient to establish a missing capability
for this target. The parent consequently strengthened the target geometry.

## Exact reference family

Take the uniform n-bit cube with independent flips at rate c. Set
A={the first m bits are zero}, with2<=m<n, B=A^c, and start the
hitting-time experiment at pi0(.|B). The target is a coarse cylinder;
its other n-m bits are unconstrained.
The reference exit rate is Lambda=nc, its gap is2c, and its relative
mixing is given by the ordinary hypercontractive certificate in
MISMATCH_AUDIT.md. No rare input is hidden in this reference description.

Let lambda_A be the smallest eigenvalue of the positive killed generator
L0_B. The Rayleigh test f=1_B gives

    lambda_A <= E0(1_B)/||1_B||_pi0^2
             = mc/(2^m-1).                          (1)

The killed chain is irreducible on B for m>=2. Perron positivity and the
positive stationary-conditional initial law imply a positive coefficient
for exp(-lambda_A t) in the survival probability. Therefore

    E_ref exp(lambda tau_A)=infinity for lambda>=lambda_A. (2)

These facts are exact; an approximately exponential lifetime assertion
is unnecessary for this failure.

## A local actual-source class with unknown stationary law

Perturb every allowed flip rate by a factor1+eta sigma_i(x), where
sigma_i(x) is either+1 or-1 and depends on a supplied constant-size local
context. For a concrete nonreversible family, away from the right endpoint
take sigma_i(x)=(-1)^(x_i+x_(i+1)+x_(i+2)); truncate the context at the
endpoints of the target-coordinate block and of the free-coordinate block.
These positive rates have the same support and satisfy the uniform local
rate box. They need not preserve the reference law or have a nearest-
neighbor Gibbs form. For n>=4 the square generated by flips0 and1 at the
all-zero state has clockwise/reverse product ratio

    [(1+eta)/(1-eta)]^2 !=1,

so this is not merely a common time rescaling or a reversible perturbation.

The CTMC stopped relative-entropy integrand, under the actual source,
is

    k(x)=sum_i c[(1+eta sigma_i(x))log(1+eta sigma_i(x))
                    -eta sigma_i(x)].

Define psi(a)=(1+a)log(1+a)-a. Since psi(-eta)>=psi(eta) and
psi''(a)=1/(1+a),

    k(x)>=nc psi(eta)>=nc eta^2/[2(1+eta)] = K_floor. (3)

Initial-law relative entropy is an additional nonnegative constant and
cannot fix the divergence below. Even retaining the exact state-dependent
compensator G=integral_0^tau k(X_s)ds gives G>=K_floor tau.

Thus if

    n eta^2/[2(1+eta)] >= m/(2^m-1),                  (4)

then K_floor>=lambda_A. Every reference expectation
E_ref exp(lambda tau+G) for lambda>0 is infinite. Consequently the
Corollary4.3 Eq(4.3) instantiation with this stopped entropy compensator
is infinite. Corollary4.5 is likewise infinite: its optimization requires
lambda>K, whereas every valid uniform rate bound has K>=K_floor and
the reference moment-generating function is infinite for lambda>=lambda_A.
These conclusions remain true even granting exact evaluation of reference
exponential moments at no computational cost.

## Concrete regime and surviving operation

For n=80,m=64 and eta=10^-6, the ratio of the lower entropy rate to the Rayleigh
upper bound in(1) is at least

    80(2^64-1) / [64*2*10^6*(10^6+1)] > 1.15*10^7.

This is an exact rational inequality. In contrast, the parent's local-rate
admission theorem with c=1/2,Lambda=40,g=1 and optimized beta has a two-sided
log mean factor about0.0155, and the sharper rare-target upper factor is
about1.0143, independent of target rarity. The executable
entropy_comparator.py records the actual numerical constants and the exact
rational checks. A wider actual-source class than the explicit sigma_i
family is covered by the rate-box theorem.

We also strengthen the ordinary comparator by discarding the n-m free
coordinates. Because the perturbations were truncated at the target block
boundary, the m-coordinate process is itself Markov. Its stopped entropy
still has floor mc eta^2/[2(1+eta)], giving the divergence ratio

    (2^64-1)/[2*10^6*(10^6+1)] > 9.22*10^6.

Thus merely eliminating irrelevant free coordinates does not repair the
stopped-path bound. The reference mean itself is available exactly from
the ordinary m+1-state Hamming-weight birth-death chain. Writing
d_k=t_k-t_(k-1), its recurrence is

    d_m=1/(mc),
    d_k=[1+(m-k)c d_(k+1)]/(kc), k=m-1,...,1,
    M0=sum_(k=1)^m binom(m,k)t_k/(2^m-1).

The script evaluates this exact rational comparator too. No difficulty in
computing the simple reference mean is offered as a new opening; the
remaining task concerns arbitrary local actual-rate boxes that destroy
this lumped representation.

The missing operation in this comparison is a tractable compression of
local model uncertainty to the slow rare-output quantity. Stopped path
relative entropy retains information about all the ordinary background
jumps before failure; its scale grows with the long waiting time. The
balanced-flow and relative-mixing certificate controls the hitting
functional without retaining that accumulated path information.
For this particular cylinder, the subsequently constructed ordinary
coupling supplies that compression too; the statement is a comparison
with the specified entropy instantiation, not with the strongest surviving
ordinary construction.

This is a failure of Corollary4.5 and of Corollary4.3 Eq(4.3) with the
stopped entropy compensator just specified. It is not a proof that every
full-path comparison, or every use of the Birrell et al. framework, fails.
Variants using an independently earned finite stopped-KL budget or mean
upper bound may be finite but weak. A smaller sigma-algebra, better
analytical information, or a different potential-theoretic construction
can improve the comparison. Likewise, ordinary exact rescaling solves the
special source Q=(1+eta)Q0 immediately; that solved subcase must not be
offered as the new capability.
