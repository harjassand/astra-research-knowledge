DRIVEN REFERENCE FOR THE SAME RARE CODE TARGET

Scope and relation to the solved baseline

CODE_TARGET_PROOF.txt preserves the exact ordinary product-reference solution:
MacWilliams enumerators and the syndrome quotient compute its mean exactly.
This separate execution changes the REFERENCE DYNAMICS. It retains the same
80-bit linear target of uniform mass2^-64 but adds overlapping directed local
three-cycles. The old product mean and its quotient spectrum are not reused
as a mean or spectral formula for this driven chain.

The reference rates, the target matrix and its uniform law are supplied
mathematical data. No 80-site native acquisition was executed. A consequence
for an unknown native generator remains conditional on the separate root
admission and perturbation theorem. In particular, these computational
reference trials do not constitute measurements of that unknown generator.

The common Astra source cutoff remains
51ce47ce39f976517d60e2f87bbe280b651f6056. CODE_TARGET_PROOF.txt records the
exact N1213 source paths/blob IDs and its preserved exception conditions:
fixed-floor point-target results remain accepted, generic normalized coarse-
indicator substitution fails, and supplied symmetry/clocks are not acquired
information. Here we explicitly avoid extending the supplied product symmetry
to a driven reference that does not possess it.

1. Fully specified driven generator and earned gap

The state is x in{0,1}^80. There are159 known unit-rate primitive maps:

  - 80 coordinate flips;
  - 79 pair maps on adjacent sites i,i+1, indexed from zero.

On EVEN pair index i the directed cycle, in physical bit order, is

    00 -> 10 -> 11 -> 00,

and on ODD i it is

    00 -> 11 -> 10 -> 00.

Both fix01. A fixed pair is a null primitive proposal. The generator is

    Qref f=sum_(a=1)^159 [f(T_a x)-f(x)].

Each T_a is a permutation. Therefore every primitive preserves the uniform
measure pi0(x)=2^-80. The coordinate flips make the full chain irreducible.
For a permutation operator P in this uniform inner product,

    Re <f,(I-P)f> = (1/2)||f-Pf||^2 >=0.

The 80 unit coordinate flips alone have spectral gap2, as is seen from the
binary characters. Adding the directed permutation generators therefore
gives an additive-symmetric Poincare gap at least2. This is a proved gap for
the nonreversible chain, not an inference from nonnormal eigenvalues.

The chain is explicitly nonreversible: for e0 with only site0 set,

    qref(0,e0)=2,    qref(e0,0)=1.

The extra forward transition is the even pair0 cycle. The two configurations
have equal stationary weights, so detailed balance fails.

2. Why the product quotient does not survive

The target is still C=ker[I64|P], with P stored in code_target_exact.json.
Its exact size is 2^16, minimum nonzero weight 22, and conditional samples are
still(Pz,z). However, the current syndrome Hx no longer determines the rates
of its future increments, because the pair maps also depend on their current
two bits.

There is a decisive exact witness. Both

    x=0,
    x'=0xc84425e22c2984a0000

are codewords with syndrome0. At0 every pair is 00 and every primitive changes
the state. The rate of exiting C is 159. The second codeword has16 pair01
patterns, at edge indices

    16,18,21,26,30,32,37,40,44,48,53,56,61,65,70,73.

Those pair maps are fixed points, so its rate of exiting C is 143. Every other
primitive changes at most two bits and therefore leaves C, since the code's
minimum nonzero weight is 22.

Thus states in the SAME syndrome block have different aggregate rates out of
that block. This violates the necessary strong-lumpability condition. It
precludes simply transplanting the earlier syndrome Markov generator or its
MacWilliams eigenvalue formula. It does not prove that every conventional
method must fail; ordinary nonreversible potential theory remains applicable.

The implemented receiver keeps the full 80-bit state in an unsigned128-bit
integer. The 64-bit syndrome is maintained only as an exact membership
statistic. It is updated by XORing the H columns for the one or two bits
actually changed by a primitive.

3. Boundary and killed-path statistic, including null proposals

Let A=C, p=2^-64, b=1-p, s=1/10. For the driven reference define

    u_s(y)=E_y exp(-s tau_A),
    r_s=s+E_(pi0|A) sum_(y outside A) qref(x,y)[1-u_s(y)],
    R_s=1/(p r_s)-1/s.

A trial does the following:

  1. Draw16 independent uniform free bits z and form x=(Pz,z).
     Inspect all 80 coordinates to recompute and check Hx=0.
  2. Choose one of 159 primitive maps uniformly and apply it.
     If it stays in A, record Bernoulli0 and END THIS TRIAL.
  3. Otherwise draw repeatedly from 1591 integer categories. Each of the 159
     primitive maps has10 categories; one category is independent killing.
     On kill record1. On a return to Hx=0 record0. Otherwise continue.

The initial null proposal is not discarded or replaced by another target
sample. Its zero contribution is essential. In the executed code target all
initial stay proposals are exactly pair01 nulls, by minimum distance22.

Uniformization represents primitive rate 1 by integer weight 10 and killing
rate 1/10 by weight 1. Thus the Bernoulli mean is

    mu=(r_s-s)/159.

Every following null proposal is simulated and charged. Its time-order effect
is correct in the uniformized chain. No floating waiting times or acceptance
thresholds are needed. The initial source-law mixture is exact because
pi0 conditioned on C is exactly the uniform(Pz,z) sampler.

For K successes in T=40000 trials, set

    muhat=K/T,    rhat=1/10+159 muhat,
    Rhat=1/(p rhat)-10.

Use fixed rational halfwidth h=1/120. Under independent ideal random bits,

    Pr[|muhat-mu|>h] <=2 exp(-2 T h^2)
                        =.007731840278945615...

Clip the mu interval to[0,1], map to rlo,rhi, and reverse its endpoints under
R(r)=1/(p r)-10. The nonreversible screened-time theorem with g>=2 gives

    Mref in [Rlo/(b*(21/20)), Rhi*(21/20)/b].

Both sides include the 21/20 factor. A reversible one-sided inequality is not
used for this driven reference. All endpoint arithmetic is rational and the
exact fractions are preserved in driven_code_receiver_results.json.

5. Numerical and scientific qualifications

The receiver's maps, bit arithmetic, membership tests and threshold choices
are exact integers. Uniform categories use rejection sampling from 64-bit
words, with accepted-word counts equally divisible among categories. The
executed mt19937_64 seed supplies a reproducible diagnostic stream; the stated
statistical coverage theorem assumes independent ideal random bits.

No generic nonreversible exact mean solver or global spectral formula is
assumed. The full-state simulation, known uniform reference mass, earned
additive-symmetric gap and measured boundary-return statistic are sufficient
for this reference interval. The classical product/code calculation remains
credited as a stronger exact baseline where its hypotheses hold.

To apply the root's reference-to-native theorem, the actual native transition
cells, local support, complete observation contract and simultaneous admission
event must separately be earned. This file does not identify arbitrary native
state-dependent rates, stationary weights, missing clocks or hidden channels.
In particular, neither the old exact Gibbs-family passive fixture nor these
reference trials is presented as completed 80-site native calibration.
