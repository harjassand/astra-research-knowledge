PASSIVE RARE-LIFETIME IDENTIFICATION: OBSTRUCTION AND NATIVE REPAIR
Construction and audit, 1 November 2026

This note complements the root's rare coarse-target mean/deadline theorem.
It gives (1) a finite passive-information obstruction without hidden states,
(2) an explicit way to remove clamps and null-event marks from the declared
heatbath-plus-directed-cycle architecture, and (3) finite confidence and
exposure bounds. It does not claim that the source architecture can itself
be certified from a short ordinary trajectory.

1. A RARE-BOUNDARY OBSTRUCTION WITH NO HIDDEN CLOCK OR STATE

Fix n>=m>=6 and A={x in {0,1}^n: x_1=...=x_m=0}. There are 2^(n-m) target
states, p=2^-m, and b=1-p. Target membership, mass and conditional sampling
are all directly available. The final n-m bits are independent spectators.

Q_1 flips each bit at rate 1. Q_K, for a fixed K>1, multiplies by K exactly
those constrained-bit edge rates crossing the boundary of A. Both directions
of each such edge receive the same multiplier. All other rates remain 1.
Both chains are irreducible, reversible for the SAME uniform invariant law,
have only single-bit jumps, and have rates in [1,K]. Since Q_K only adds
nonnegative symmetric edge conductance, its spectral gap is at least the
independent-flip gap 2. Spectators make the full gap exactly 2 if n>m.

Thus the example has no hidden clock, no omitted state, no slow full mixing,
no unknown target sampler, and no ambiguity about null attempts. The changed
rates depend on whether an m-bit pattern is present. Jump locality has not
imposed bounded dependence range.
The stationary Gibbs law is a product law, so its equilibrium dependency graph
has degree zero. A sparse Gibbs graph therefore does not certify sparse kinetic
dependence: the rate architecture needs its own justification.

PASSIVE PATH DISTANCE. Start either chain from nu=pi(.|A^c). For a complete
observed path up to time T, let P_1^T and P_K^T denote the two path laws. Then
  TV(P_1^T,P_K^T) <= min(1, 2 m (K-1) p T / b).                 (P1)

Proof: couple using all Q_1 flips and additional rate K-1 clocks on each
modified edge. Before the first additional transition the paths agree. The
total additional exit rate is
  d(x)=(K-1)[m 1_A(x)+1_{distance(x,A)=1}].
Its stationary expectation is 2m(K-1)p. Since nu<=pi/b and the base semigroup
preserves pi, this bounds its expected integral by 2m(K-1)pT/b. A first-event
union bound proves (P1). This bound concerns complete histories, not merely
one-time marginals or a small collection of fitted statistics. Data processing
therefore gives the same bound for any coarse observation of these histories.

EXACT MEANS. Let M_K=E_nu tau_A under Q_K. There is a nonnegative quantity R_m,
independent of K, such that
  M_K = 2^m b/(m K) + R_m.                                  (P2)
Writing r_m=R_m/[2^m b/m],
  0<=r_m<=(1+2/m)/b^2-1,                                    (P3)
  M_1/M_K=(1+r_m)/(1/K+r_m) -> K as m grows.                 (P4)

Proof of (P2): lump the constrained bits by Hamming weight j. Their stationary
mass is pi_j=binom(m,j)/2^m. The conductance between j-1 and j is
  c_j=j binom(m,j)/2^m, except c_1=mK/2^m.
Put s_j=sum_{k=j}^m pi_k. The birth-death hitting recurrence gives
  t_j-t_(j-1)=s_j/c_j,
and averaging t over pi(.|j>=1) gives
  M_K=(1/b) sum_{j=1}^m s_j^2/c_j.
Only its first term changes with K, proving (P2).

For (P3), the independent-cube return formula, or its binomial spectral
decomposition, gives
  M_1=(1/(2b)) S_m,  S_m=sum_{j=1}^m binom(m,j)/j.
The identity S_m=S_(m-1)+(2^m-1)/m follows either from Pascal's identity or
integrating ((1+x)^m-1)/x. For m>=6,
  S_m<= [2^(m+1)/m](1+2/m).
The m=6 case follows from S_6=1517/60. In the induction step m>=7 it is enough
that m^2-7m+4>=0, which holds. Substituting this bound and the first term in
(P2) proves (P3).

STATISTICAL CONSEQUENCE. If
  M_1/M_K > (1+epsilon)/(1-epsilon),
then every estimator based on the passive path has
  max_j P_j(|Mhat/M_j-1|>epsilon)
      >= [1-TV(P_1^T,P_K^T)]/2.                             (P5)
This is the elementary two-point testing bound: a relative-accurate estimate
would distinguish the disjoint intervals around the two means. In particular
error probability alpha<1/2 requires
  T >= b(1-2alpha)/[2m(K-1)p].                              (P6)
For fixed K this is of the order of the rare lifetime itself. A confidence
interval valid with probability 1-alpha for BOTH models must, under the base
model, contain both means with probability at least 1-2alpha-TV unless it
already fails its coverage claim. A short-data interval must therefore remain
wide enough to span the separated means.

DEADLINES. Use the root's already proved finite exponential-law bound with
g=2, p=2^-m, and scale bM. If
  delta_K=1/(2bM_K),
  D(delta)=(4delta/pi) log(1+1/(4delta^2))+48delta/pi,
  E_K=[D(delta_K)+p]/b,
then at deadline d=bM_1,
  F_K(d)-F_1(d) >= e^-1-exp(-M_1/M_K)-E_K-E_1.              (P7)
This turns the mean obstruction into a coarse deadline-probability obstruction.
For m=48,K=2, the analytical ratio bound exceeds 1.92 and the two law errors
are below 3e-11 each, so the CDF separation exceeds 0.22. For n=64 this target
still contains 2^16 states. Meanwhile the observation-path TV at T=10^6 is
at most 3.411e-7. A decoder of the short record cannot reliably choose between
these deadline probabilities.

Exact Fraction calculations in rare_gate_check.py check (P2)-(P3) for m=6..128.
Independent full-generator solves for m=2..8 agree to relative error <4e-15
and verify the gap comparison numerically. At m=48,K=2, the exact rational
means are approximately 5.994652963815e12 and 3.062621956413e12, ratio 1.95736;
the evaluated bound (P7) is approximately 0.2266486. These floating decimals
are diagnostics; the finite inequalities above carry the proof.

2. NATIVE OBSERVED TRANSITIONS IDENTIFY THE DECLARED DRIVEN MODEL

Use the root's exact path architecture:
  w(x)=product_i a_i^(x_i) product_i b_i^(x_i x_(i+1)),
  0.9<=a_i,b_i<=1.1, 0.5<=alpha_i<=1.
Site i has heatbath redraw rate 1. Its odds are
  r_i=a_i b_(i-1)^(x_(i-1)) b_i^(x_(i+1)).
Pair i carries either the forward three-cycle [1,3,2,0] or its reverse
[3,0,2,1], in code x_i+2x_(i+1), at rate alpha_i/v_i(x), where v_i is the
product of all factors touching the pair. The orientations are known, but
need not alternate. Full jump times and changed-bit masks are observed.
No channel mark, failed proposal, preparation, clamp or reset is observed.

The ordinary move table is the mechanism for component isolation:
  Forward: 00->10 flips left; 10->11 flips right; 11->00 flips both.
  Reverse: 00->11 flips both; 11->10 flips right; 10->00 flips left.
The state 01 is fixed for both directions.

Whenever the observed left neighbor of site i is 0, the pair on its left
cannot produce a transition changing ONLY i. Whenever its right neighbor
is 1, the pair on its right cannot produce such a transition either. Thus
both central-only directions in the context (left,right)=(0,1) are pure
heatbath, despite all other channels continuing to operate.

For unit heatbath clocks use the following H=3n-2 measured rate cells:

  (a) For each i<n, count central-only 0->1 transitions when the neighbors
      are 01. The native rate is z_i/(1+z_i), z_i=a_i b_i.
  (b) For each i<n, with both neighbors 0, count central-only 1->0 if the
      right pair cycle is forward, or 0->1 if it is reverse. The first
      native rate is 1/(1+a_i), the second a_i/(1+a_i).
  (c) At the final site, count 0->1 when its left neighbor is 0. Its rate
      is a_n/(1+a_n).
  (d) For a reverse pair, count simultaneous 00->11 transitions. Their
      rate is alpha_i, since v_i(00)=1 for every exterior configuration.
      For a forward pair, count simultaneous 11->00 while both exterior
      neighbors are 0. Their rate is alpha_i/(a_i a_(i+1)b_i).

Missing boundary neighbors are omitted. Every cell fixes at most four bits.
Every measured rate is >=1/3 under the displayed factor promises. These
rates give all a,b,alpha explicitly by odds, ratios and products. They are
AGGREGATE native jump rates: other primitive clocks need not be identified.
The actual counting process distinguishes a simultaneous pair jump from two
successive single jumps; sufficient temporal resolution is part of the source
observation interface.

Unknown heatbath clock rates lambda_i are also identifiable: measure both
central-only directions in context 01; their sum is lambda_i and their ratio
is a_i b_i. Then the one clean direction in context 00 determines a_i using
the acquired lambda_i. At the final site both directions at left-neighbor 0
give lambda_n and a_n directly. Identifiability therefore does not rely on
knowing null-event rates. Finite constants below use the root's unit-clock
model; the unknown-clock extension needs its own error propagation.

native_clean_check.py verifies 8417 exact-rational rate identities over every
global state of 32 models of size 1..8, with all-forward, all-reverse,
alternating and random orientations. It also recovers arbitrary tested positive
lambda_i exactly, together with every a_i,b_i,alpha_i. No large-state empirical
fit or hidden primitive attribution is used in those checks.

3. FINITE CONFIDENCE FROM COUNTS AND EXPOSURE

For any one of these cells c let E_c(t) be the amount of time spent in its
pre-transition context, and N_c(t) the number of its specified observed jumps.
The compensator of N_c is q_c E_c. For every fixed real theta,
  exp(theta N_c(t)-(exp(theta)-1)q_c E_c(t))
is a nonnegative mean-one martingale (or the corresponding nonnegative local
martingale, which is enough for Ville's inequality). Take theta=log(1+eta)
and theta=log(1-eta), respectively. For 0<eta<1,
  P(exists t: |N_c(t)/E_c(t)-q_c|>eta q_c,
                  q_c E_c(t)>=L) <=2 exp(-eta^2 L/3).       (P8)

Consequently, at ANY common data-dependent stopping time when every exposure
exceeds
  E_min=9 eta^-2 log(2H/alpha_rate),                        (P9)
the ratios qhat_c=N_c/E_c are simultaneously relative-eta accurate with
probability >=1-alpha_rate, because q_c>=1/3. Independence among counts,
Poisson counts conditional on random exposure, and stationarity are NOT
assumed for this statement. In particular it would be incorrect simply to
declare the observed N_c conditionally independent Poisson(E_c q_c).

PARAMETER TRANSFER, unit clocks. Clip each pure heatbath rate estimate to
[1/3,2/3]. The true rate lies in that interval, so clipping reduces its error.
On this interval the absolute derivative of logit is <=9/2; relative-eta
rate error implies absolute error <=2eta/3. Thus
  |log(ahat_i/a_i)|<=3eta,
  |log(bhat_i/b_i)|<=6eta.
Let t_eta=-log(1-eta). Reverse alpha estimates have log error <=t_eta;
forward alpha estimates have log error <=t_eta+12eta. Clipping factors to
their known ranges again reduces these errors. Before rational rounding,
  u=(9n-6)eta,
  v=u+t_eta+12eta                                         (P10)
bound the full unnormalized weight error and every stationary-flow cycle
coefficient error. The root's flow/weight theorem therefore gives, for every
target simultaneously,
  |log(Mhat_A/M_A)|<=3u+(7/6)v.                            (P11)
If each rounded local parameter adds at most q to its log error, replace
u by u+(2n-1)q and v by that revised u+t_eta+12eta+q.
The ordinary rational transfer-matrix cylinder sampler and screened receiver
then apply exactly as in UNKNOWN_GIBBS_PROOF.txt. Passive acquisition changes
the source-data interface and its cost; it does not erase either receiver cost
or target-access requirements.

4. PAYING FOR ALL LOCAL EXPOSURES

The root has already earned a full additive-symmetric gap g>=1/2 for the entire
driven model, using the reversible heatbath component and nonnegative cycle
energy. Full Gibbs single-site conditionals lie in [1/3,2/3]. By conditioning
successively, any pattern on at most four bits has stationary mass >=p0=1/81.

Under stationary initialization and a context indicator f with mass p,
L2 semigroup contraction yields
  |Cov(f(X_0),f(X_t))|<=p(1-p)exp(-gt).
Therefore Var(E_c(T))<=2pT/g and Chebyshev gives
  P(E_c(T)<p0 T/2)<=8/(g p0 T).
For H cells,
  P(min_c E_c(T)<p0 T/2)<=8H/(g p0 T).                    (P12)
Choose T>=2E_min/p0. Then the event-count confidence (P8) together with (P12)
gives total acquisition failure at most
  alpha_rate + 8H/(g p0 T).                              (P13)
This is deliberately elementary; sharper Markov concentration can improve it.

For a deterministic initial configuration, the factor promises give
  pi_min>=2^-n (9/11)^(2n-1).
Discard an initial burn-in t0 satisfying
  t0 >= g^-1[ (1/2)log(1/pi_min)+log(1/(2alpha_start)) ].
Then TV of the burn-in endpoint from pi is <=alpha_start. The entire subsequent
path law differs by at most that amount, so add alpha_start to (P13). This
avoids assuming physical equilibrium preparation. Burn-in is O(n+log1/alpha)
in the present source model.

For target-uniform fixed accuracy, eta=O(1/n). The observation duration from
(P9)-(P13) is O(n^2 log(n/alpha)) up to accuracy/failure constants, and the
expected number of native/proposal operations is O(n^3 log(n/alpha)). A given
trajectory exposes all local contexts concurrently. These operation counts
must be charged; this note has not physically generated a trillion-event
passive record. The algebraic and small finite-generator executions above are
the actual executions performed here.

One can also get finite local-context exposure without a global gap when the
rate model has a known bounded dependence range, uniformly positive independent
single-site refresh, and bounded total rate touching each local block. Use the
graphical construction: during the first half of a short window suppress all
residual updates touching the block, require at least one baseline reset at
each site, and require the last reset value to equal the chosen pattern; during
the second half suppress all touching updates. This supplies an independent
positive-probability block event and a fixed positive amount of occupation.
For a k-site block its probability can be bounded by
  beta=exp[-R Delta-H Delta] [(1-exp(-rho Delta))/2]^k,
where rho is the per-site baseline uniform-reset rate, R bounds residual
touching clocks, and H bounds all touching clocks. Binomial concentration over
disjoint windows then earns exposures. Constants can be exponential in k and
in rate ratios. They are independent of n when the known range and rate bounds
are fixed. This general construction is less sharp than (P12) in our source.

5. WHAT THE RESULT DOES AND DOES NOT IDENTIFY

Null candidate clocks are not an obstacle if two mechanisms generate the same
observable Q: all observable hitting laws are then identical. We estimate the
native aggregate Q-relevant rates, not an arbitrary nonidentifiable clock
decomposition. Clamps previously risked changing channel rules; native local
exposures avoid that particular intervention bias.

The no-hidden-dynamics/known-range promise remains substantive. A good fit to
ordinary trajectories cannot certify it uniformly: the obstruction (P1)-(P7)
has a constant full gap and perfectly ordinary short histories, yet its rare
boundary gate changes the intended lifetime substantially. Coarse observation
or unobserved controllers cannot improve this information situation. A known
finite number of hidden states also does not imply a finite observable memory
order or a lower bound on the frequency of informative histories.

For a genuinely known finite-memory observable model, replace spatial context
c by the specified predictable history context. The same compensator proof
applies if the intensity is constant within each context. A separate coverage
bound must earn time in every needed context. Neither a known maximum memory
length without positive excitation, nor a Markov model fitted to finite data,
is itself that bound. If the history/context grammar is allowed to grow to
include rare global patterns, (P1)-(P7) show why exposure costs can return to
the rare-lifetime scale.

The concrete advance in this continuation is the clean-pattern identification
of the root's driven local model from native trajectories, its finite-confidence
composition with the already developed lifetime mechanism, and the explicit
fast-gap minimax obstruction showing which structural promise makes that
composition possible. Ordinary likelihood estimation and potential theory
retain credit. No historical priority or superiority over all equally informed
existing constructions is established.
