STATUS AND SCOPE

This note replaces the earlier clamp/marked-null observation contract for ONE
explicit known architecture. It derives the missing inputs from one ordinary
native trajectory. The observation is an initial state followed by every actual
state change and its timestamp. There are no clamp interventions, channel
labels, attempted-update marks, null-event marks, or stationary initial samples.

The statistical engine is conventional continuous-time Markov-chain likelihood
and counting-process concentration. The useful architecture-specific algebra
is an explicit selection of unmixed local intensities. This is not a claim to
have invented a new likelihood estimator. Correctly informed ordinary methods
already supply this acquisition step. The surviving research contribution must
therefore lie in the downstream rare-event transfer mechanism and its scope,
not in presenting count/exposure estimation as new.

The source is assumed to be exactly in the architecture below. The selected
data do NOT certify arbitrary additional channels, state-dependent rare gates,
or a generally misspecified native generator. Applying a general perturbation
certificate would require acquiring all local aggregate-rate boxes that its
hypotheses demand. That has not been done in the accompanying fixture.

1. NATIVE GENERATOR AND OBSERVATION CONTRACT

Use zero-based sites i=0,...,n-1. The unknown factors obey

    9/10 <= a_i,b_i <= 11/10,   1/2 <= alpha_i <= 1,

and the unnormalized stationary weight is

    w(x) = product_i a_i^(x_i) product_i b_i^(x_i x_(i+1)),
    x in {0,1}^n,   pi(x)=w(x)/Z.

There is a known unit-rate heatbath contribution at every site. Its conditional
odds and actual single-bit transition rates are

    r_i(l,r)=a_i b_(i-1)^l b_i^r,
    q_i(0->1)=r_i/(1+r_i),   q_i(1->0)=1/(1+r_i),

with absent boundary factors omitted. The rate one is an independently known
time scale. It is not estimated from invisible nulls.

On adjacent sites i,i+1 let

    v_i(u,v;l,r)=a_i^u a_(i+1)^v
                b_(i-1)^(l u) b_i^(u v) b_(i+1)^(v r).

Even i has the directed three-cycle, written in physical bit order,

    00 -> 10 -> 11 -> 00,

and odd i has the reverse cycle

    00 -> 11 -> 10 -> 00.

Both leave01 fixed. The actual rate of an active cycle step is alpha_i/v_i.
For each fixed outside configuration the stationary unnormalized circulation
is alpha_i C(outside), constant around that cycle, because w=C v_i. Thus each
cycle separately preserves pi. The total generator is irreducible because the
heatbath flips are all positive.

The observer sees the initial bits and a complete, ordered stream of actual
changes with elapsed times. Two-bit jumps and single-bit jumps can be told
apart. Simultaneous distinct native jumps have probability zero. No property
of a hidden proposal label enters the observer. Mathematical uniformization
is allowed for analysis or simulation; it creates no additional observed data.

2. PURE LOCAL RATES WITHOUT CHANNEL LABELS

The crucial elementary check is the following table.

    Edge parity    Cycle single-bit steps       Unique two-bit step
    even           left-up, right-up           11 -> 00
    odd            left-down, right-down       00 -> 11

For site i EVEN, observe single-bit1->0 jumps while the left neighbor is0.
The right even edge never produces a single down-jump. The left odd edge
can produce a down-jump at its right site only when its left bit is1, so that
edge is disabled in the selected context.

For site i ODD, observe single-bit0->1 jumps while the left neighbor is0.
The right odd edge never produces a single up-jump. The left even edge can
produce an up-jump at its right site only when its left bit is1.

Consequently these selected AGGREGATE intensities are exactly heatbath rates,
even though all events are unlabelled. This argument includes boundaries by
omitting missing edges. Use right-neighbor0 and, for i<n-1, right-neighbor1.
There are2n-1 heatbath contexts. A context includes the selected current bit,
its existing left neighbor0, and its existing right neighbor k.

Let theta_(i,k) be its constant selected intensity, and r_(i,k) its odds. Then

    even i: r_(i,k)=(1-theta_(i,k))/theta_(i,k),
    odd i:  r_(i,k)=theta_(i,k)/(1-theta_(i,k)),

    a_i=r_(i,0),   b_i=r_(i,1)/r_(i,0).

For cycle i ODD, count the unique two-bit00->11 jumps while the pair is00.
Its intensity is nu_i=alpha_i because v_i(0,0;l,r)=1 for every outside state.

For cycle i EVEN, count the unique two-bit11->00 jumps while both existing
outer neighbors are0. Its intensity is

    nu_i=alpha_i/(a_i a_(i+1) b_i).

It follows that

    alpha_i=nu_i,                                 i odd,
    alpha_i=nu_i r_(i,1) r_(i+1,0),               i even.

The latter product is preferable to separately multiplying fitted a_i,b_i:
it uses only two acquired odds and avoids needlessly accumulating errors.

There are m=(2n-1)+(n-1)=3n-2 counters. Each context fixes at most four bits.
Each selected intensity is at least1/3: the heatbath odds in these contexts
belong to[81/100,121/100], while even-cycle intensity is at least500/1331.

Useful failed naive construction. At n=3, a=b=alpha=1, site1 with both
neighbors0 has aggregate up intensity1/2 and down intensity3/2. Taking their
ratio as heatbath odds gives1/3, although the true odds is1. This bias persists
with unlimited data. Correct full-model CTMC likelihood or the isolation above
resolves it; it is not a limitation of conventional likelihood itself.

3. EXPOSURE STOPPING AND SIMULTANEOUS CONFIDENCE

For a selected context c let I_c(t) be its left-continuous activity indicator
and let N_c(t) count the selected actual transitions. Define occupation

    H_c(t)=integral_0^t I_c(u) du,
    tau_c=inf{t:H_c(t)>=R},

where R is a positive integer quota. The count is frozen at tau_c, not at the
final completion time of another context. A quota can be completed inside a
sojourn. The observer can schedule this from its current state and its own
clock; it does not need to wait for an unseen native proposal.

For the full observed filtration the compensator of N_c is theta_c H_c.
Hence for every real lambda the usual nonnegative exponential local
martingale/supermartingale is

    E_c(t)=exp{lambda N_c(t)-(exp(lambda)-1)theta_c H_c(t)}.

This can also be proved directly by conditional expansion over a small time
increment: a selected jump has probability theta_c I_c dt+o(dt), and the drift
of E_c cancels. Nonnegativity permits optional stopping and Fatou without an
unearned uniform-integrability assumption. At tau_c, H_c=R. Chernoff choices
lambda=log(1+eta) and log(1-eta) imply, for0<eta<1,

    Pr[tau_c<infinity and |N_c(tau_c)/(theta_c R)-1|>eta]
       <=2 exp(-theta_c R eta^2/3)
       <=2 exp(-R eta^2/9).

Counts for different contexts can be dependent. No independence, equilibrium
initialization, IID sample claim, or independent-Poisson replacement is used.
With

    R >= 9 eta^(-2) log(2m/delta_cal),

all acquired intensities theta_hat_c=N_c(tau_c)/R satisfy their relative bands
simultaneously with probability at least1-delta_cal, provided completion occurs.
Completion is paid separately in section5. A fixed finite horizon protocol
reports acquisition failure if some quota has not completed.

4. RATIONAL FIT AND CONFIDENCE TRANSFER

Every selected heatbath intensity lies in the common rational interval

    [qmin,qmax]=[81/181,100/181].

Clip its measured count/R to that interval, then apply the parity-dependent
odds formula from section2. Denote the resulting raw odds by rhat_(i,k).
Clip the recovered a_i and b_i to[9/10,11/10]. For alpha, use nu_hat alone on
odd edges and nu_hat_i rhat_(i,1) rhat_(i+1,0) on even edges, then clip to[1/2,1].
Use RAW odds in that last formula; they need not equal the odds reconstructed
from already clipped factors. This is a consistent fitted generator because
the final factor vector defines a single global product weight.

Everything in this fit is rational: integer counts, an integer denominator R,
ratios, products, comparisons and clipping. No logarithm or irrational random
threshold is evaluated to construct the fitted model. Logs below only describe
error bounds. Optional rounding of fitted factors requires its own error term;
the accompanying implementation does not round them.

Let A0=181/81 and t_eta=-log(1-eta). Clipping does not increase absolute error
relative to a true number inside the interval. The derivative of logit on
[qmin,qmax] is at most1/(qmin qmax). Therefore relative rate error eta gives

    |log(rhat/r)| <= (qmax eta)/(qmin qmax)=A0 eta.

It follows, on the single simultaneous calibration event, that

    |log(ahat_i/a_i)| <= A0 eta,
    |log(bhat_i/b_i)| <= 2A0 eta,
    |log(alphahat_i/alpha_i)| <= t_eta+2A0 eta.

The odd-edge alpha bound improves to t_eta. Factor clipping cannot increase
any of these log errors. Hence all configurations obey

    |log(what(x)/w(x))| <= u=A0(3n-2)eta.

This earns a stationary-weight confidence band, and implies

    exp(-2u) <= pihat(x)/pi(x) <= exp(2u).

The same band bounds every target mass, with interval endpoints clipped to[0,1].
It does not make a sample from pihat an exact sample from the unknown pi.

The heatbath stationary flow across a flip is w0 w1/(w0+w1), a positive,
coordinatewise monotone, homogeneous function of its two weights. Its fitted
flow therefore has log error at most u. A cycle flow is alpha_i times the
product of factors entirely outside that pair. Its log error is at most

    v=u+t_eta+2A0 eta.

The exact-family mean transfer result audited earlier then gives, uniformly
over targets A, a factor

    F=exp{3u+(2/sqrt(3))v}

between true and fitted stationary-outside hitting means. This citation is to
the separate proved flow-perturbation result in the current research, not to
an assertion that count/exposure likelihood itself proves rare-event stability.

For n=80, eta=1/10000, the exponent is approximately0.22159, below log(5/4),
without parameter rounding. Paying for such eta still requires a very large
native observation budget. At n=8, eta=1/50 the factor is67.45636543; the finite
demonstration below reports this broad guarantee honestly.

5. EARNED GAP AND COMPLETION FROM AN ARBITRARY INITIAL STATE

First establish a source and fitted gap from architecture. A neighboring bit
changes heatbath log odds by log b. The maximal change in a Bernoulli heatbath
redraw probability is tanh(|log b|/4). Since b is in[.9,1.1], the influence of
one neighbor is at most d=19-6sqrt(10). A shared-clock/common-uniform coupling
contracts expected Hamming distance at rate

    kappa=1-2d=12sqrt(10)-37 > .9473.

Each site has at most two neighbors, so summing the influences proves this
differential inequality directly. For the reversible heatbath generator,
applying the semigroup contraction to a nonconstant eigenfunction forces its
eigenvalue to be at least kappa. Each added stationary directed cycle has a
positive semidefinite additive symmetric Dirichlet form. Therefore the full
nonreversible generator has additive symmetric gap at least kappa. Fitted
factors are clipped to the same domain, so the same argument applies to them.
The rational lower bound g=1/2 is safe for both.

Second establish nonrare local occupancy. Every desired one-bit value has full
conditional probability at least

    gamma=729/1729,

because all complete local odds belong to[.9^3,1.1^3]. Conditional probabilities
given only some other bits are averages of full conditional probabilities, so
the same bound survives partial conditioning. Successive conditioning proves
that every prescribed pattern on k<=4 sites has stationary probability at least

    gamma^k >= p0=gamma^4 =0.0316031330959382...

This is a local conditional lower bound, independent of n and of the rare
target. It supplies local exposure; no visits to the later rare target enter.

For completeness, the entire stationary law obeys

    pi_min >= 2^(-n)(9/11)^(2n-1).

Thus an arbitrary initial distribution rho has L2(pi) density norm at most

    Cn=2^(n/2)(11/9)^((2n-1)/2).

Its logarithm is only O(n). There is no assumed stationary initial draw.

Here is a self-contained conservative occupation bound. Let f=1_c and
mu=pi(c)>=p0. Put L=-Q and let H=(L+L*)/2. Write v=a1+w with pi(w)=0.
Pointwise |a+w|^2 >= |a|^2/2-|w|^2, so

    <v,fv> >= (mu/2)|a|^2-||w||^2.

The gap gives <w,Hw>>=g||w||^2. Taking theta=g/2 yields

    <v,(H+theta f)v>
       >= (g mu/4)|a|^2+(g/2)||w||^2
       >= (g mu/4)||v||^2.

The Feynman-Kac semigroup exp[t(Q-theta f)] therefore contracts L2 norm by
at least exp(-g mu t/4), even though Q is not reversible. Indeed differentiate
the squared norm; only the additive symmetric part enters. By Cauchy-Schwarz,

    E_rho exp[-theta H_c(T)] <= Cn exp(-g mu T/4).

Markov's inequality now implies

    Pr[H_c(T)<=p0 T/4] <= Cn exp(-g p0 T/8).

Union over the m contexts. A sufficient deterministic observation horizon is

    T >= max{4R/p0,
             [8/(g p0)] [log(m/delta_exp)+log Cn]}.

Then all exposure quotas complete by T except with probability delta_exp.
The acquisition event, with completion, has probability at least
1-delta_cal-delta_exp. This is ordinary spectral/Feynman-Kac concentration,
with its source-local constants earned above. It avoids the extremely poor
constants of forcing a prescribed local sequence of updates block by block.

For fixed desired whole-weight error u0, eta is O(u0/n). Thus R and T are
O(n^2 u0^(-2) log(n/delta)+n), with explicit constants as above. This is an
asymptotic statement about this known weakly interacting path architecture;
it is not a generic passive-learning theorem for arbitrary driven systems.

6. FULL TIME, EVENT, COMPUTATION AND TIMESTAMP COSTS

Every actual source exit rate is at most

    Lambda=n+2(n-1)=3n-2,

since alpha_i/v_i<=1/.9^5<2. Thus the number J of actual state-change records
up to deterministic walltime T has expected value at most Lambda T and is
stochastically dominated by Poisson(Lambda T). A conservative additional
high-probability bound is

    J <= ceil[2 Lambda T+2 log(1/delta_evt)].

The same formula follows directly from the bounded jump intensity and its
exponential supermartingale. It requires no observed or physically meaningful
null-event clocks. All background actual changes are counted in this bill.

Every observed one- or two-bit change affects O(1) of the width-four contexts.
Maintaining active exposures and a set of at most m scheduled quota times
costs O((J+m)log m) local operations and O(n) state/counter storage. Counter
and rational-factor bit lengths are O(log(J+R)). An initial n-bit state followed
by changed supports is a lossless record encoding. If the available instrument
instead transmits a full n-bit snapshot per event, reading those records costs
O(nJ) bits/operations; that extra bill must be charged, not silently omitted.

The theorem above uses exact elapsed-time observations. A finite-clock version
is available if event order and supports are exact and no event is missed.
Suppose each reported absolute timestamp has certified error at most htime,
and J<=Jmax. Every reconstructed context exposure then differs from its true
value by at most2(Jmax+1)htime. This follows by summing the two endpoint errors
for every included sojourn. Choose zeta<=eta/4 and

    htime <= zeta R/[2(Jmax+1)].

A measured quota R then corresponds to true occupation in
[(1-zeta)R,(1+zeta)R]. Use time-uniform exponential crossing bounds rather than
asserting that an arbitrarily rounded retrospective cutoff is a stopping time.
For0<eta<=1/10, upper and lower Chernoff exponents are at least
theta_c R eta^2/6. Hence

    Pr[relative count/R error exceeds eta] <=2 exp(-R eta^2/18).

For the upper tail use lambda=log[(1+eta)/(1+zeta)], and for the lower use
lambda=log[(1-eta)/(1-zeta)]; the standard inequalities for the Chernoff rate
function give the displayed constant. Ville's inequality applies uniformly
over all times in the occupation band, so no dependence of the rounding error
on future data is hidden. Replace9 by18 in the quota choice and4R/p0 by
4(1+zeta)R/p0 in the completion bound. Add delta_evt to the failure budget.
Required timestamp bits scale logarithmically in T/htime. Complete ordered
jump detection remains an explicit information assumption.

This finite-clock theorem is not automatically a certification of floating
simulation timestamps. The diagnostic code uses finite floating arithmetic
and a seeded PRNG without a machine-checked error enclosure for its native
clock. Its numerical results are an interface implementation check; the exact
statistical statement concerns the ideal observation model just specified.
