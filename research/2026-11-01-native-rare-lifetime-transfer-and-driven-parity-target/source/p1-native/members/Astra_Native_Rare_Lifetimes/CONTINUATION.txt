MAIN GENERAL THEOREM
Let Q0 have known pi0, symmetric gap g and exit bound Lambda. Native local rate
intervals must earn(1-eta)q0_xy<=q_xy<=(1+eta)q0_xy on every supported edge.
The actual Q may have a different stationary law and need not share a Gibbs
or stationary short-cycle representation. Known kinetic range/support permit
these global bounds to be earned from finitely many ordinary local contexts.

Let T_infinity bound the stationary-adjoint Poisson inverse on mean-zero
bounded functions. For the uniform n-bit reference containing rate-c flips
plus any additional uniform-preserving Markov generator,
  T_infinity <= [log(4(n-1))+2]/(4c).
Put a=2eta Lambda T_infinity. If a<1/2, then
  u=log[(1-a)/(1-2a)]
certifies exp(-u)pi0<=pi<=exp(u)pi0. A more general Metzler bound is available
when this contraction condition fails, and a certified stationarity residual
can improve u. The proof uses ordinary resolvent perturbation, with its compact
norm supplied explicitly by hypercontractivity and an invariant Markov Trotter
argument. Actual mixing is not assumed to earn its own certificate.

Put v=u-log(1-eta), K=sqrt(2Lambda/g),
  F=exp[2asinh(K sinh(v/2))].
Then every target A has its own stationary-outside means satisfying
  exp(-3u)/F <= M_actual/M_reference <= exp(3u)F.
For a rare target the exact outside-mass correction saves approximately u:
  exp(-2u)b0/[b_plus F] <= M_actual/M_reference
                          <= exp(2u)b0 F/b_minus.
Here b_minus=1-exp(u)pi0(A)>0, b_plus=1-exp(-u)pi0(A),b0=1-pi0(A).
The actual symmetric gap is at least(1-eta)exp(-2u)g. Combining the mean band,
this gap and the prior finite lifetime-law theorem gives all-deadline additive
CDF bounds. No factor proportional to the rare mean or inverse target mass
appears in the source-error multiplier.

The mixed balanced-flow estimate and the positive killed-inverse weight
formula are proved in full. Reference reversibility gives a stronger
quadratic-current comparison, but this refinement is NOT used for the final
driven reference. Skew inverse algebra, likelihood, coding theory and
hypercontractivity receive explicit ordinary-method credit.

INFORMATIVE FAILURE: KINETIC LOCALITY IS SUBSTANTIVE
Two fully observed chains can share a known uniform invariant law, gap>=2,
single-bit jumps and bounded rates while short passive histories do not
identify their rare lifetimes. Multiply both directions of the boundary edges
of a48-bit cylinder by 2. The base and modified means are about 5.99465e12 and
3.06262e12; their complete path laws over 1e6 units differ in total variation
by at most3.411e-7. Their deadline CDFs can differ by more than 0.22.
The modified rate depends on a rare large pattern. A sparse equilibrium graph
does not certify sparse kinetic dependence. Finite native fitting therefore
does not establish the support/range promise needed above.

Another retained failure: a computable stopped-path relative-entropy bound
becomes infinite on a particular rare cylinder, even with tiny local rate
errors. Stronger ordinary coupling solves that example, so its entropy failure
is recorded as an informative limitation of that instantiation, not as proof
of a new capability or a refutation of the broader information framework.
