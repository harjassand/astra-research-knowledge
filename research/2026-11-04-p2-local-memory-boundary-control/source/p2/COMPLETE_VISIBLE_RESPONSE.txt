## 1. The principle constructed

I concentrated on this question:

> Can accessible interventions experimentally certify that a local prediction has become insensitive to an unknown hidden initial state?

The answer is **yes within a specified class of interacting transport systems**.

The source is an exclusion network: each site holds at most one particle, particles hop into vacancies, and a few accessible ports exchange particles with reservoirs. The hidden bulk can have no reservoir refresh at all.

For the symmetric reference dynamics, let

\[
s_i(t)=\left[e^{-(L+R)t}\mathbf 1\right]_i.
\]

This is the survival probability of a backward reference walker before absorption at a reservoir. It also bounds how much the mean occupancy at port \(i\) can retain its initial condition. The underlying absorbing-walk and exclusion machinery is established; it is not itself a new discovery. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

The constructive operation is to choose a fresh fair sign \(Z\), change the accessible reservoir densities to \(1/2+aZ\) while preserving their total exchange rates, wait a fixed time \(\tau\), and read the local occupancy \(Y_i\). In the symmetric case,

\[
\mathbb E[Z(2Y_i-1)\mid\text{entire preceding history}]
=2a[1-s_i(\tau)].
\]

The two possible signs start from the same carried hidden state. Randomization cancels that unknown initial contribution in expectation. **No reset, stationary-start preparation or copied physical branch is needed.**

I then strengthened the construction to tolerate bounded failure of mean closure. For directed exclusion, the exact nonlinear residual has range width at most

\[
h_i=\sum_j\left|\frac{q_{ij}-q_{ji}}2\right|.
\]

Under the supplied structural guarantee \(h_i\leq\eta r_i\), this earns

\[
\mathbb E[Z(2Y_i-1)\mid\mathcal H]
\leq(2a+\eta)[1-s_i(\tau)].
\]

Transforming the scores into binary outcomes gives a binomial stochastic-domination bound even though the successive physical trials are dependent. Exact integer tail inversion then produces an upper confidence bound \(S_i\) on the unobserved memory \(s_i(\tau)\).

That same bound transfers to every allowed fixed combination of additional source injections:

\[
\left|\mathbb E_xX_i(t)-\rho_i^u\right|
\leq \eta+(1-\eta)S_i,
\qquad t\geq\tau.
\]

This earns the finite-acquisition premise needed by the retained intervention-composition machinery. Baseline and isolated-control observations can then enclose later joint stationary means.

The restrictions matter: the transport class and defect bound remain warranted premises; reservoir probes must preserve total exchange kinetics; source strengths must remain reproducible; and controls stay fixed within each sampling interval. The certificate concerns local means, not arbitrary feedback or the full hidden-state distribution.

## 4. The foundational consequence is a separation of local and global memory

The hidden bulk can be enlarged behind the same weak connection while retaining the same small set of observed ports.

If \(L\) unrefreshed bulk sites communicate through a neck of rate \(\gamma\), comparing initially full and initially empty bulk configurations gives

\[
t_{\mathrm{mix}}(\epsilon)
\geq \frac{(1-2\epsilon)L}{2\gamma},
\qquad 0<\epsilon<\frac12.
\]

Thus global state mixing becomes arbitrarily slow as the bulk grows.

Meanwhile, the local reference-memory bound at the accessible ports remains uniformly small. For the constructed family, at time \(4\) its analytical upper bound is below **0.001584**, independently of bulk size. The finite measured bound in the executed source is looser—at most **0.020017435**—and is the bound actually used by the learner.

This establishes a concrete possibility: **a local intervention prediction can be certified while the hidden system retains substantial global memory.** It does not make the bulk material free, and it does not make local throughput grow with that material.

## 6. Two constructive boundaries were also completed

The collaborators produced useful limits on extending this approach.

**Actuation capacity can be an information resource.** An explicit \(n+2\)-state family has additive rank-one controls, known fast fixed-mode relaxation and no exponentially small positive rates. Yet any learner restricted to total actuation amplitude \(B\) faces an acquisition-time lower bound exponential in order \(n/(B+2)\). A fractional concurrent-control construction matches the order, giving a threshold

\[
B=\Theta(n/\log n)
\]

for polynomial-time identification in that family.

At \(n=64\), any budget-one strategy needs at least **20,588,177** native time units for uniform 95% identification; a protocol with all 64 controls concurrently available needs **305.0932**, using 96 snapshots. The concurrent protocol was executed. Its channel availability and background reset are supplied resources, and “source-state local” does not imply spatial many-body locality. General combination-lock hardness under partial observation is already established; the explicit matching actuation/time law is the useful result here. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="3"}

**The observation record can change the acquisition exponent.** A separate four-state construction requires order \(q^{-2}\) endpoint episodes to detect a squared closure residual \(q\), even with adaptive endpoint lengths. Retaining intermediate observations of the same visible variable permits order \(q^{-1}\) steps under a richer observation contract. Its proof, optimal same-access likelihood comparator and executed richer-record test are included. This prevents treating an algebraic squared residual as an automatically available, inexpensive physical measurement.
