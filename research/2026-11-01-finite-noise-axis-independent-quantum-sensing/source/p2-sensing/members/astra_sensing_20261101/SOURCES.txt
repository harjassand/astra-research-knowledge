PRIMARY SOURCES AND ACTUAL DEPENDENCIES
Research cutoff: 1 November 2026, Australia/Brisbane.

No claim of historical novelty is made. The core identities, controller,
finite-time reduction and estimator are derived in the accompanying proofs.
The sources below define strong competitors and established machinery.

1. S. Zhou, M. Zhang, J. Preskill and L. Jiang,
   Achieving the Heisenberg limit in quantum metrology using quantum error correction,
   Nature Communications9,78 (2018).
   https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-017-02510-3
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5758555/
   Governing scope: finite-dimensional Markov model, HNLS condition, noiseless
   ancillas and arbitrarily fast accurate control. Strong known-noise construction.
   It does not supply unknown static-axis calibration or the new finite detector.

2. R. Demkowicz-Dobrzanski, J. Czajkowski and P. Sekatski,
   Adaptive Quantum Metrology under General Markovian Noise,
   Physical Review X7,041009 (2017).
   https://journals.aps.org/prx/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevX.7.041009
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.06280
   Established adaptive-channel precision-bound framework. Our exact finite
   nuisance-path proof is an application/refinement, not a claim to invent it.

3. F. Albarelli and R. Demkowicz-Dobrzanski,
   Probe Incompatibility in Multiparameter Noisy Quantum Metrology,
   Physical Review X12,011039 (2022).
   https://journals.aps.org/prx/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevX.12.011039
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.11264
   Strong adaptive, entanglement-assisted multiparameter bounds, including
   simultaneous unitary/noise parameters. Constrains novelty of nuisance geometry.

4. E. Abbasgholinejad et al.,
   Error-corrected function estimation advantage in multiparameter Hamiltonians,
   arXiv:2609.16226 (September2026).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16226
   Full HTML read at https://arxiv.org/html/2609.16226v1
   Functional HNLS, Hamiltonian nuisance removal and tight SQL/HL results. The
   present nuisance instead parameterizes a jump orientation. Unknown-parameter
   function estimation itself is already established work.

5. Q. Liu and S. Zhou,
   Subsystem Quantum Error Correction for Noisy Quantum Metrology,
   arXiv:2606.19628 (June2026).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19628
   Full HTML read at https://arxiv.org/html/2606.19628v1
   Specified Pauli error operators; local estimation with control centering;
   subsystem constructions, gauge resets and reduced ancilla/syndrome requirements.
   We do not claim to introduce subsystem sensing or syndrome-free sensing.

6. I. Rojkov et al., Bias in Error-Corrected Quantum Sensing,
   Physical Review Letters128,140503 (2022).
   https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.128.140503
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.05817
   Shows why recovery-induced frequency bias must be charged along with dephasing.

7. A. Halaski and C. P. Koch,
   Obstacles to continuous quantum error correction via parity measurements,
   Physical Review Applied26,034074 (30September2026).
   https://journals.aps.org/prapplied/abstract/10.1103/8bkm-b48b
   Current physical obstruction: continuous two-body approximations to parity
   readout can reveal logical information, especially through transient meter
   dynamics. Our retained meter and subsequent erasure/record correction change
   the operations and do not refute that scoped result.

8. A. Frisk Kockum, L. Tornberg and G. Johansson,
   Undoing measurement-induced dephasing in circuit QED,
   Physical Review A85,052318 (2012).
   https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.85.052318
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.2386
   Close predecessor: stochastic measurement backaction can be corrected when
   the relevant record is fully accounted for. Quantum erasure and deferred Pauli
   corrections are not historical firsts here. The present work explicitly acquires
   the record under its QND/stable-population assumptions and charges its pipeline.

9. H. Kwon, U. R. Fischer, S.-W. Lee and L. Jiang,
   Restoring Heisenberg scaling in time via autonomous quantum error correction,
   npj Quantum Information12,103 (2026).
   https://www.nature.com/articles/s41534-026-01268-1
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13168
   Their stated sufficient condition includes noise operators commuting with the
   signal plus a code equation; finite engineered/native dissipation ratio R gives
   a finite-time error O(kappa T/R^c). The transverse jump used here anticommutes
   with Z. Autonomous metrological protection is established, not newly claimed.

10. L. O. Conlon et al., Fault-Tolerant Heisenberg-Limited Quantum Sensing,
    arXiv:2608.00171 (July2026).
    https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00171
    https://quics.umd.edu/publications/fault-tolerant-heisenberg-limited-quantum-sensing-0
    Stronger controller-noise comparison: specified highly biased qubit noise and
    repetition-code lifetime improvements. Our architecture does not establish a
    comparable fault-tolerance threshold or replace that protection.

11. Noise-Resilient Heisenberg-Limited Quantum Sensing via
    Indefinite-Causal-Order Error Correction, Physical Review Letters137,020801 (2026).
    https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/9mjc-nhxx
    https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01404
    Different-resource comparator: coherent ordering and time-reversal regime.
    Those operations are not silently granted to the scalar-reservoir controller.

ACTUAL REUSE AND CONDITIONS
For a known QND word K_w=sum_m k_w(m)P_m on a meter that no longer interacts with
the data, actual tracing of that meter kills cross-m terms:
    Tr_M(K_w rho K_w^dagger)
      =sum_m |k_w(m)|^2 Tr_M(P_m rho P_m).
It includes arbitrary data/reference correlations. In this investigation each
retired meter is decoupled, repeatedly QND-read, then discarded/reset. The identity
justifies a latent classical meter bit even when the first physical read is weak.
It does not reveal that bit, guarantee its population stability, or establish the
independent repeated-report error model; those are separately supplied and charged.

The source's calibration-history mixture is not a certificate for arbitrary future
environments. Its model-average regret and accepted error-weight theorems are not
converted into per-record or conditional accepted-error guarantees. Its reported
costs and noisy gates are not assumed available here. Source internal acceptance
at its actual scope is retained; no source result was downgraded merely because
it was not re-proved in this investigation.

The source corpus supplies this useful instrument identity. It does not supply
the new scalar rate-control primitive, finite Hamiltonian meter, stationary
controller solution, global estimator, or physical noise-protection premises.
