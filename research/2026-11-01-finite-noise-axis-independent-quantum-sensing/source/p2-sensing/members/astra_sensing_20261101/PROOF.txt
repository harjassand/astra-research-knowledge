QUANTUM SENSING WITHOUT CALIBRATING THE NOISE AXIS
A finite controller based on returning error trajectories
1 November 2026 (Australia/Brisbane)

Research status
===============
This investigation derives a physical-model construction, ordinary proofs, and
independent executable checks. Historical novelty, laboratory realization, and
practical superiority over the strongest conventional methods are unestablished.
The main result includes continuing sensor noise during finite meter interaction,
feedback latency, readout and reset. It allows constant classical report errors.
Protected reference memory, specified coherent interactions, and a shared static
native noise axis remain material premises. Generic quantum controller faults
are separately charged; this is not a fault-tolerance threshold theorem.

The initial scientific objective was a quantum sensor whose improving precision
does not demand equally improving knowledge of an environmental noise direction.
The strongest ordinary known-noise QEC constructions were considered first.
Their failure with a nuisance noise direction identified the opening. The useful
result changes the physical operation set by allowing scalar reservoir-rate
control. That operation is neither free information nor an ordinary Pauli pulse.

1. Task and exact physical contract
==================================
There is one sensing qubit S with unknown frequency omega and one protected
reference qubit A. Z|0>=|0>, Z|1>=-|1>. Encode a logical qubit by

    |0_L>=|0_S 0_A>,    |1_L>=|1_S 1_A>.

Its input can be unknown and entangled with an arbitrary untouched reference R.
For the final sensing task the input is |+_L>. The sensor has Hamiltonian
H_omega=omega Z_S/2 and Markov noise

    L_theta=sqrt(gamma) J_theta,
    J_theta=cos(theta) X_S+sin(theta) Y_S.

The azimuth theta is unknown, constant, and common to every native-rate setting.
Two strictly positive rates gamma_l<gamma_h can be selected. The physical control
changes only this scalar coefficient. It does not turn off the noise, resolve
theta, add an independently oriented noise source, or alter H_omega. A microscopic
resource consistent with this contract is a tunable scalar coupling to one fixed
system operator J_theta and a sufficiently broad stationary Markov reservoir.
Its laboratory synthesis and Markov approximation are not supplied by the theorem.

One fresh meter qubit M per cycle starts in |0>. During a finite time tau the
actual Hamiltonian, with sensor noise still active at the old selected rate, is

    H_active=omega Z_S/2 + g_s Z_S X_M + g_a Z_A X_M.

Both interactions involve two qubits. Set g_a tau=pi/4 and
0<lambda tau<=pi/2, where lambda=2g_s. After this interval, turn off both meter
interactions and coherently apply

    C=I_A tensor |0><0|_M + Z_A tensor |1><1|_M.

C is a controlled-Z between A and M. It can have any finite duration compatible
with the dead period d; its generator commutes with continuing sensor Hamiltonian
and noise. Its duration, meter measurement, reset/preparation, routing and feedback
latency are charged in d. The old rate remains active throughout d. Thus every
cycle lasts Delta=tau+d. No instantaneous or perfect syndrome readout is assumed.

Measure meter Z. Relabel actual outcome1 as u=+ and outcome0 as u=-. The classical
report is independently flipped with probability epsilon<1/2; eta=1-2epsilon.
Choose gamma_l after reported + and gamma_h after reported -. These classical
errors are explicitly admitted. There is no sensor recovery flip.

This ideal-model contract assumes accurate initial encoding, final local Pauli
measurements, protected A, and the erasure gate C. Their deviations appear in
Section10. A separate deferred-record construction replaces C by parallel repeated
classical readout under its own stable-record premise (see supplement).

2. Why the ordinary fixed-rate route fails
==========================================
Known theta satisfies the usual Hamiltonian-not-in-Lindblad-span condition. The
standard parity code can then correct J_theta without suppressing the Z signal.
For unknown theta, correcting by nominal X makes every detected jump contribute
exp(i theta Z_L). A retained record N has phase

    phi=omega T-2theta N,    N~Poisson(gamma T).

Unknown orientation and frequency are nearly collinear regressors because
N~gamma T. Keeping all records alone does not solve that information problem.

A precise finite collision model makes the obstruction elementary. For a step
Delta, p=gamma Delta<1, put
    K0=sqrt(1-p) U_omega,
    K1=sqrt(p) J_theta U_omega,
    U_omega=exp(-i omega Delta Z/2).
Along the nuisance path omega(s)=omega0+2gamma s, theta(s)=theta0+s, direct
differentiation gives
    sum dot(Kj)^dagger Kj=0,
    sum dot(Kj)^dagger dot(Kj)=p(1-p)I.
In a purification of any adaptive n-step protocol, cross-time derivative inner
products vanish by the first identity. Thus F_s<=4np(1-p). A locally unbiased
frequency estimator in the nuisance model obeys
    Var(omega_hat)>=gamma/[T(1-gamma Delta)],  T=n Delta.
The continuous Markov limit gives Var>=gamma/T. Accessible classical jump flags,
and the retained output of this canonical collision dilation, do not change the
identities. This is not a statement about every possible additional bath-control
operation or microscopic dilation.

The ordinary parity strategy with complete records has efficient information
    F_omega|theta=T^2/(1+gamma T)
in the continuous limit, attaining the limiting SQL coefficient. This is a strong
conventional comparator, not an intentionally weak straw man. The full finite
proof, exact binomial formula and local-unbiased qualifications are in
sensing_nuisance_no_go.txt.

Useful failed argument: replacing theta by an estimate with error delta gives
averaged coherence exp[-i omega T+gamma T(exp(2i delta)-1)]. The dephasing is
quadratic, 2gamma delta^2+O(delta^4), but the false frequency is linear,
2gamma delta+O(delta^3). A small decoherence estimate alone misses the metrological
calibration burden. This is consistent with established work on QEC sensing bias.

3. Returning trajectories cancel the unknown rotation
======================================================
Let r(t)=+1 in even S-A parity and -1 in odd parity. Native jumps toggle r. For
each quantum-jump trajectory, define its signed exposure
    A_T=integral_0^T r(t) dt.
The jump law is independent of the logical state because L^dagger L=gamma I.

Two identities are decisive:
    J_theta^2=I,
    J_theta exp(-ixZ/2) J_theta=exp(+ixZ/2).
Every path starting and ending even therefore acts on the logical input as
    exp(-i omega A_T Z_L/2),
with no theta dependence. This is an operator identity and hence includes R.
Every odd endpoint carries one remaining J_theta factor; we do not pretend that
this branch is repaired without knowing theta.

At a single fixed rate, even-return stopping cannot make A_T grow linearly in
time. For every integrable stopping time tau with even event count, the compensated
Poisson martingale gives exactly
    E[A_tau^2]=E[tau]/gamma.
With fully observed events, using gamma_l in even parity and gamma_h in odd parity
changes the mean residence times. Stopping at 2n jumps gives independent
S_l~Gamma(n,gamma_l), S_h~Gamma(n,gamma_h), and A=S_l-S_h. Its quadratic information
coefficient relative to mean total time is
    [(gamma_h-gamma_l)/(gamma_h+gamma_l)]^2.
The finite detector below does not assume these event times are observed. They
are a reference construction and a proof instrument, not supplied data.

4. The actual finite meter preserves the logical phase
======================================================
Fix one sensor jump history during the active interval. Let z=+/-1 denote the
unchanged eigenvalue of Z_A, which labels the logical computational state. Along
the path Z_S=z r(t). Set Y=integral_active r(t)dt and Phi=g_a tau+g_s Y.
Because all active meter rotations are about X_M, its state is exactly
    cos(Phi)|0>_M - i z sin(Phi)|1>_M.
The signal contributes exp(-i omega zY/2). This calculation includes jumps
DURING the sensor-meter interaction and does not assign them to ideal gaps.

The controlled-Z C removes the z in the second amplitude, leaving
    cos(Phi)|0> - i sin(Phi)|1>.
Conditional on the actual sensor path, the meter is now independent of the
unknown logical state. Measurement and classical report errors subsequently
alter the feedback only. All additional sensor jumps during C and the rest of
d are already included; they commute with C.

The path-conditioned report probability is
    Pr(u|path)=[1+u eta sin(lambda Y)]/2.
This observes the time spent in the parity sectors, not the endpoint parity.
Replacing it by an instantaneous weak-parity oracle would omit real backaction.

More general symmetry lemma. Suppose the conditional meter Hamiltonians obey
Z_M H_m(z,t) Z_M=H_m(-z,t), with initial meter |0>. Then
U_m(-z)=Z_M U_m(z) Z_M; outcome0 amplitudes are equal and outcome1 amplitudes
opposite in the two logical components. The same C erases the logical label.
Uncertain transverse meter coupling amplitudes and axes, and meter Z detuning,
can preserve this symmetry. They change observation quality without necessarily
creating logical decoherence. Initial meter population errors, transverse meter
faults and a wrong controlled-Z angle are different and are not covered by that
robustness statement.

4A. Removing the coherent erasure gate by delaying accurate information
---------------------------------------------------------------------
A stronger implementation trade was constructed after the constant-hardware
reference above. Omit C. The true meter Z outcome m contributes only the known
Pauli frame Z_A^m. This operator commutes with the sensor Hamiltonian, its native
jumps, and every future meter coupling. Therefore the first noisy report can drive
the real-time rate choice, while accurate decoding of the logical phase frame is
delayed. The final product-Pauli outcome is multiplied by (-1)^(sum_i m_i).
No quantum correction gate on S or A is needed during sensing.

Assume each meter is decoupled from S and A, its Z populations remain stable until
discard, and repeated QND Z readings have conditionally independent error at most
(1-eta_r)/2<1/2. A perfect first projection is unnecessary: for every fixed complete
QND word, tracing the retired meter kills its cross-Z terms. It gives exactly the
same data-plus-record statistics as a latent classical Z value. This is the actual
terminal-readout identity retrieved from Astra N1000 at the pinned source revision,
applied only to decoupled, eventually discarded meters. It does not supply the
latent value to the controller. With M total meters, an odd number of repetitions
    L >= 2 log(M/alpha_frame)/eta_r^2
makes the probability of any wrong majority-decoded frame bit <=alpha_frame.
The first report may be included in its later majority; feedback correlations
do not invalidate the per-bit bound. Couple the decoded protocol to the ideal
true-frame protocol. They differ only if a frame is decoded incorrectly. Add
alpha_frame to the final estimation failure probability; do not apply Hoeffding
after conditioning on successful decoding.

All true-frame corrected outcome laws and the exact finite controller K below
are unchanged. There is O(M log(M/alpha_frame)) read work. Fixed sensor cycle time
is maintained with O(1+ceil(L t_read/Delta)) parallel retained meters/readout lanes.
Adaptive frequency stages must wait for frame decoding before selecting their
next counterdrive, adding O(K L t_read)=O(log^2 M) latency across K stages.
The data can be measured immediately; only its classical sign waits for decoding.

For M=10000, 10% independent read assignment errors and alpha_frame=.01,
L=45 is sufficient. With t_read=.1 and Delta=.2, the simple pipeline bound is 24
meters/lanes and 450000 total meter reads. This is a substantial, explicitly
charged alternative to a high-quality controlled-Z every cycle.

The stable-bit premise is not earned merely by repeating measurements. Population
mutations during meter retention can create an error floor and require their own
protected classical storage model. Likewise, active-meter transverse faults and
reference-qubit faults remain. Heisenberg scaling here is in sensing time;
including all repeated-read work gives an additional logarithmic resource factor.

5. Exact transfer matrix: all histories and all exposure
=======================================================
Use column vectors indexed by actual parity (+,-). For rate gamma_j define
    F_j(w,t)=exp(t[[-gamma_j-iw,gamma_j],
                  [gamma_j,-gamma_j+iw]]).
It is the characteristic-function transition matrix for the entire telegraph
history with phase exp(-iw integral r dt). Define
    S_j(w)=[F_j(w-lambda,tau)-F_j(w+lambda,tau)]/(2i).
The corrected logical coherence and report kernel is exactly
    K_u^j(w)=F_j(w,d)[F_j(w,tau)+u eta S_j(w)]/2.
The 4x4 matrix K(w), with state (selected rate, actual parity), has this as block
(next rate j', old rate j), where j'=l for u=+ and h for u=-.

K(0) is a stochastic matrix. Starting in (l,+), v0=(1,0,0,0), let
e=(1,0,1,0). At T=m Delta,
    p_e(T)=e K(0)^m v0,
    C_e(w,T)=e K(w)^m v0,
    M(T)=i e [d/dw K(w)^m]_(w=0) v0.
Here C_e=E[1_even exp(-iw A_T)] and M=E[A_T 1_even]. The unnormalized even quantum
channel maps populations to p_e times the original populations and coherence to
C_e times the original coherence. It remains independent of theta on all inputs
and untouched references. This does not make every hidden A_T available or turn
the mixture into a known unitary at arbitrary w.

No event-count truncation, mean-energy replacement, rare-history assumption, or
first-order-in-noise approximation enters K. The independent check exponentiates
the full 64x64 three-qubit Lindbladian and agrees with this 4x4 reduction.

6. Positive signal survives with finite observation and latency
==============================================================
Put Delta=tau+d, Q=q_l+q_h, and
    q_j=[1-exp(-2gamma_j Delta)]/2,
    b_j=exp(-2gamma_j d),
    s_j=lambda exp(-gamma_j tau)
        sinh(tau sqrt(gamma_j^2-lambda^2))/sqrt(gamma_j^2-lambda^2).
Use the real analytic continuation of the last expression. For
0<lambda tau<=pi/2 it is positive. The zero-signal block is
    K_u^j(0)=[P_j(Delta)+u eta s_j P_j(d) Z]/2,
where P_j=F_j(0,.) and Z=diag(1,-1).

The exact stationary solution gives
    D=eta(s_l b_l+s_h b_h)/
      [2-eta^2(s_l b_l-s_h b_h)(s_l q_h-s_h q_l)/Q],
    v=D q_l q_h(1/gamma_l-1/gamma_h)/(Delta Q),
    pi_e=[1+D(q_h-q_l)/Q]/2.
For gamma_h>gamma_l>0, finite d and eta>0, v>0. The denominator of D lies between
1 and3. Thus a coarse positive gain persists on compact parameter boxes bounded
away from zero rate contrast, zero usable measurement and infinite delay.

One direct derivation is to define stationary signed parity masses m_l,m_h and
rate-setting probabilities x_l,x_h. Endpoint stationarity implies
q_l m_l+q_h m_h=0, so m_l=q_hD/Q and m_h=-q_lD/Q. The report and parity updates give
    x_l=[1+eta(s_lm_l+s_hm_h)]/2,
    D=eta(s_lb_l x_l+s_hb_h x_h).
Solving these yields the expression above. The signed interval reward from
state (r,j) is r q_j/gamma_j; its stationary mean is v Delta.
Finite-state mixing then implies
    M(T)=pi_e vT+O(1).
This explicitly charges detector latency: large d can make v exponentially small.

7. An actual sensing measurement, without perfect final parity
==============================================================
Prepare |+_L>. At the end choose uniformly to measure local Pauli product X_S Y_A
or Y_S X_A, and retain only their product outcome z=+/-1. This implements the
binary POVM (I+/-O)/2 with
    O=(X_S Y_A+Y_S X_A)/2.
O is logical Y on the even sector and zero on the odd sector. Therefore the
entire binary distribution is
    Pr(z)=[1+z f_T(omega)]/2,
    f_T(omega)=E[1_even sin(omega A_T)].
It is exactly independent of theta at every frequency. All attempts, including
odd endpoints, are counted. This measurement is statistical suppression of the
odd sector; it does not purify a retained unknown quantum input.

At omega=0 the achieved classical Fisher information, without a QFI attainability
argument, is
    I(0,T)=M(T)^2=pi_e^2 v^2 T^2+O(T).
It is not E[A_T^2], since the residence histories were not observed. The exact
finite-time value is computed by the derivative of K(w)^m.

There is also an operational finite weak-signal guarantee. From |A_T|<=T,
    |f_T(omega)-omega M(T)|<=|omega|^3 T^3/6.
If M(T)/T>=c0>0 and h^2<=3c0, the signal omega=h/T has mean at least h c0/2.
O(log(1/alpha)/(h^2c0^2)) repeated experiments distinguish it from zero with
error at most alpha. The number of repetitions is independent of T.

8. Global frequency estimation can be earned conditionally
=========================================================
Unobserved signed-time fluctuations generally dephase a fixed nonzero omega at
long times. A local quadratic FI alone is not a global precision theorem.

Assume an actual counter-Hamiltonian -c Z_S/2 can be applied, leaving the admitted
noise/meter model fixed or within a known compact box. This is a physical
counterdrive, not a mathematical rotating frame or a change of readout phase.
Suppose a coarse certificate gives M(T)/T>=c0 for T>=T0. No accurate theta or
full response calibration is required. Let b^2<=c0. On |delta|T<=b,
    f_T'(delta)>=M(T)-delta^2 T^3/2>=c0T/2.

Maintain a certified frequency interval centered at c with radius R=b/T.
Counterdrive by c and estimate f_T(delta) using binary shots to additive error
e0=c0b/8. If the observed mean is >=e0, retain the right half of the interval;
if <=-e0, retain the left half; otherwise retain its middle half. Each update
keeps the true frequency on the confidence event and halves the radius. Double
T and repeat. Hoeffding gives
    N_k>=128 log(2/alpha_k)/(c0^2 b^2).
For T_k=2^kT0 and alpha_k=(alpha/2)2^{-(K-k+1)}, the total sensing/control time is
bounded by
    [128/(c0^2b^2)] T_K[2log(4/alpha)+4log2]+2T_K,
plus target-independent acquisition and lower-order preparation/readout overhead.
The final error is <=b/(2T_K) with probability >=1-alpha.
For deferred records, allocate their decoding failure within the same overall
alpha, alongside acquisition and statistical failures (for example three equal
budgets), and use the corresponding tighter stage confidence levels. A union/
coupling bound gives the same overall 1-alpha guarantee, with altered constants.

A positive c0,T0 can be obtained from coarse parameter bounds using the explicit
stationary formula and a four-state Doeblin bound; details and conservative
constants are in sensing_feedback_statistical_audit.txt, Sections3A-3C. These
generic sufficient constants can be large. They are not device lower bounds.
Initial coarse frequency localization is paid by short-time process tomography:
the x-y Bloch generator B has omega=(B21-B12)/2, independent of theta. A short-time
matrix logarithm in a controlled neighborhood of identity gives a fixed-cost
initial bracket. Accurate initial state preparations and Pauli readout are part
of that conventional acquisition interface.

9. Numerical instance and independent evidence
==============================================
Use dimensionless gamma_l=1, gamma_h=4, tau=.1, d=.1, eta=.8 (10% erroneous
classical reports), lambda tau=pi/2. The active couplings are finite,
g_s=g_a=pi/(4tau). The exact solution gives
    v=.1962121845068183,
    pi_e=.5931497426850304,
    lim I/T^2=.013545050813852473.
Selected achieved information values, including every active/dead sensor interval:
    T=20:   I=6.8472811036,
    T=200:  I=555.34260349,
    T=2000: I=54314.85692783.
These numbers are an analytic-model calculation, not experimental forecasts.

research_feedback_sensor.py checks 100 parameter sets against the stationary
formula and exact matrix derivatives. verify_sensing_two_body_meter.py separately
exponentiates the full sensor/ancilla/meter Lindbladian, including a nonzero signal,
and verifies populations and logical coherences across distinct theta values.
The maximum discrepancies are of order 10^-16 for finite-matrix identities.
Additional programs check the nuisance bound, finite-interval return instrument,
the cyclic generalization and the explicit robust conventional code. Numeric
agreement supports these derivations; it is not a proof of priority or a device.

10. Costs, informative failures, and finite useful lifetime
=========================================================
The constant report error tolerance is not a tolerance for arbitrary quantum
faults. A meter reset into the wrong computational state, an undetected transverse
meter error, dephasing of A, or an incorrect erasure entangling phase can introduce
logical Z faults. A small static erasure-angle error can create a coherent bias
proportional to the number of relevant meter outcomes, recreating a calibration
problem. The symmetry lemma does not remove it.

If every full cycle differs from its admitted instrument by half-diamond error
at most e (including any memory), m cycles differ by at most me. This valid but
conservative bound requires me to be below the allowed statistical or channel
budget. Preparation and final readout deviations are additional one-time terms.

For the concrete model of an independent logical Z fault of probability p per
cycle, the signal contrast is multiplied by (1-2p)^m and the achieved local FI by
(1-2p)^(2m). Thus fixed p removes indefinite Heisenberg scaling. Defining
Gamma=-log(1-2p)/Delta, the large-time FI is approximately
    C T^2 exp(-2Gamma T),  C=pi_e^2v^2.
Relative to the fixed-native-rate nuisance information bound T/gamma_l, the best
asymptotic ratio is gamma_l C/(2euler_e Gamma). At the numerical parameters above,
it exceeds one only if p is below approximately 2.49e-4 in this specific model.
For p=1e-5 and T=2000 the exact ideal FI times the fault factor is about 36408.
These comparisons use a fixed-rate information bound, not the stronger two-rate
QEC competitor discussed below. They illustrate a real operating window, not a
hardware error-rate measurement or a general threshold.

Static-axis equality is structural. If changing gamma also changes theta, even
paths need not cancel. If theta(t) drifts, adjacent return pairs leave phase
increments from the differences of their jump angles. For bounded |theta_dot|<=L,
the worst remaining phase is at most2L times the total odd residence time, hence
<=2LT. Arbitrary drift and generic additional noise are excluded. A laboratory
realization should use one fixed system coupling operator times a scalar control,
not independent nominally aligned noise sources requiring precision matching.

11. Strong conventional comparisons and the actual contribution
==============================================================
Known-axis HNLS QEC already supplies quadratic information with ideal frequent
recovery and protected ancillas. The general machinery is established.

More importantly, once two native scalar rates are allowed, ordinary fixed-time
parity QEC with retained counts and joint inference of omega and theta ALSO has
local quadratic information. For two equal-duration t runs its efficient FI is
    t^2[(gamma_l-gamma_h)^2+2(gamma_l+gamma_h)/t]
       /[gamma_l^2+gamma_h^2+(gamma_l+gamma_h)/t].
Therefore rate control plus quadratic scaling is not, by itself, a unique new
capability of the present construction. Our specific result is an explicit finite
interaction/readout controller with exact elimination of the static noise-axis
nuisance, no sensor recovery flips, no jump-time acquisition, and an actual simple
statistical consumer. A practical advantage under fully matched finite hardware
costs remains unestablished.

Subsystem QEC, autonomous dissipative sensing, quantum-erasure/measurement-dephasing
correction, and adaptive frequency unwrapping are strong established ingredients.
Recent subsystem work already reduces ancilla and syndrome burdens for specified
Pauli error sets. Recent fault-tolerant sensing work addresses noisy controller
lifetimes under specified bias. Those achievements are not replaced by the model
here. The same additional static syndrome-dependent dissipator even permits an
explicit three-qubit robust HNLS comparator; it is constructed in the supplement.

The current continuous-parity-measurement obstruction is also respected. Its
two-body continuously read meter may leak logical information. Here the meter is
retained coherently, its logical information is erased before readout (or its
known Pauli backaction is corrected using a later reliable record), and the
observation concerns integrated parity. These changed operations are essential.

12. Broader principle and remaining research target
===================================================
For a q-level phase-dressed cyclic jump U_phi|k>=exp(i phi_k)|k+1 mod q>, all phases
may be unknown. U_phi^q is a global phase. For a diagonal known signal Hamiltonian,
its conjugates by U_phi are known permutations independent of the phases. Closing
the cycle cancels the nuisance on arbitrary input/reference states. Biasing
residence times can preserve a signal that uniform cycle averaging erases. Full
operator, cost, concentration and martingale proofs are retained in the supplement.

The consequential next mechanism is a realization in which the shared-axis rate
control, helper coherence and meter population protection jointly survive actual
hardware noise with competitive cost. A new fault-tolerant version must protect
the observer without suppressing the signal or merely relocating its calibration
burden. This investigation supplies a solvable finite physical starting point and
explicit failure thresholds for that task. It does not establish general protected
quantum computation, arbitrary-noise sensing, or a civilisation-changing device.
