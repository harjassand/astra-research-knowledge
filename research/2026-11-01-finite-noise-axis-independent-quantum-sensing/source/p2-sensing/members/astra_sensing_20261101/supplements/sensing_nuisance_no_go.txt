Unknown noise orientation is a metrological resource
Independent construction and obstruction note, 1 November 2026

Status: proved finite collision statements; continuous-time consequence uses the
standard Markov collision limit. These are applications/extensions of established
channel-metrology methods, not a claim of historical novelty. Local precision
statements assume local unbiasedness in BOTH the target and nuisance directions.

1. Task and strongest known starting point

A sensor qubit has H = omega Z/2 and noise jump
L_theta = sqrt(gamma) A_theta, A_theta = cos(theta) X + sin(theta) Y.
gamma > 0 is known; omega and the stationary theta are unknown. The Lindblad
generator is -i[omega Z/2,rho] + gamma(A_theta rho A_theta-rho).
For each KNOWN theta, its Lindblad span is span_R{I,A_theta}; Z lies outside.
The 2018 HNLS theorem therefore permits Heisenberg time scaling with a noiseless
ancilla and arbitrarily fast accurate processing. Our question retains theta as
an unknown nuisance and charges all sensor exposure used to learn it.

The last paper gives tight functional-HNLS results for Hamiltonian nuisance
parameters and fixed Markov noise; the present obstruction involves the
parameter dependence of the jump orientation itself. Full-text scope must be
checked before claiming this distinction is a novelty.

2. Exact finite collision theorem

Choose a sensing period Delta > 0 with p = gamma Delta < 1. One physical use is
the parameter-dependent channel with Kraus operators
  K_0(omega,theta) = sqrt(1-p) U_omega,
  K_1(omega,theta) = sqrt(p) A_theta U_omega,
  U_omega = exp(-i omega Z Delta/2).
This is an exact collision model. It is not identical at finite Delta to the
continuous GKSL semigroup; its small-Delta generator is the one above.
The experimentalist may use any parameter-independent adaptive operations,
noiseless memories and ancillas, initial states, measurements, and feedback
between n uses. Every use is charged, including calibration uses. T = n Delta.
Optionally grant each use's classical event flag 0/1 without error.

THEOREM. At any interior (omega_0,theta_0), the variance of an estimator that
is locally unbiased for omega in both coordinates satisfies
  Var(omega_hat) >= gamma / [T(1-gamma Delta)].
This bound survives perfect event-flag access.
For m repetitions each containing n uses, replace T by total exposure mnDelta.

Proof. Restrict parameters to the path
  omega(s)=omega_0+2 gamma s, theta(s)=theta_0+s.
Differentiation is with respect to s. Since A_theta^2=I, A_theta Z=-Z A_theta,
and dot A_theta=-i Z A_theta,
  dot K_0 = -i p Z K_0,
  dot K_1 = -i(1-p) Z K_1.
(The second identity uses anticommutation to move the derivative of U through A.)
Therefore
  sum_j dot K_j^dagger K_j =
      i p(1-p) Z - i p(1-p) Z = 0,
  sum_j dot K_j^dagger dot K_j =
      (1-p)p^2 I + p(1-p)^2 I = p(1-p)I.
The sign of the j=1 term in the first line is negative because
K_1^dagger Z K_1 = -p Z. With V_s=sum_j K_j(s) tensor |j>_E, the two identities
are V_s^dagger dot V_s=0 and dot V_s^dagger dot V_s=p(1-p)I.

Purify the initial state and every adaptive operation into parameter-independent
isometries; defer all measurements by storing outcomes coherently. Differentiating
the entire n-use purified output gives n terms. Terms with derivatives inserted
at distinct uses have zero inner product, by V^dagger dot V=0 at the later use;
each derivative term has squared norm p(1-p). The purified output QFI is exactly
4np(1-p), and the accessible output QFI cannot exceed it. Local unbiasedness for
omega in both coordinates implies d E[omega_hat]/ds = 2 gamma. The scalar
Cramer-Rao inequality along this path yields
  Var >= (2 gamma)^2/[4np(1-p)] = gamma/[T(1-p)]. QED.

To include a classical flag, replace K_j by K_j tensor |j>_R and retain a
purifying |j>_E. Both algebraic identities remain identical. One may even grant
the specified collision isometry's coherent output records; the same derivative
orthogonality proves the bound. This does NOT license arbitrary bath access for
an unspecified physical dilation of the same Lindblad generator: the accessible
record or dilation must be specified physically.

3. Continuous-time consequence

For a fixed finite adaptive schedule, Trotter convergence of both the channel
and its parameter derivative takes the collision model to the GKSL model as
Delta -> 0. The Fisher-information bound can be passed through the purification
or local fidelity formulation, giving QFI_s <=4 gamma T and
  Var(omega_hat) >= gamma/T.
Allowing arbitrary fast adaptive controls is the closure of finite schedules.
The finite-collision bound is uniform in their number, form and ancillary size.
The same result therefore holds in the usual continuous fast-control model.

Direct infinitesimal check: K_0=I-(iH+L^dagger L/2)dt, K_1=L sqrt(dt).
Writing beta=i sum dotK^dagger K gives
  beta=[-dot H + (i/2)(dot L^dagger L-L^dagger dot L)]dt+O(dt^2).
Here dot H=gamma Z and dot L^dagger L=-i gamma Z, so beta=O(dt^2).
alpha=sum dotK^dagger dotK=gamma I dt+O(dt^2).
The exact collision proof avoids relying solely on this formal expansion.

4. Strong conventional construction: the bound is asymptotically tight

Use one noiseless ancilla and code |0_L>=|00>, |1_L>=|11>. Prepare |+_L>.
After each collision measure Z_P Z_A and correct a detected flip with X_P.
In the odd branch X A_theta = exp(i theta Z), while the even branch is identity.
Every branch is an exactly known one-parameter unitary family: conditional on
N observed flips, the final logical state has relative phase
  phi = omega T - 2 theta N.
The syndrome probabilities are parameter independent, with N~Bin(n,p).
Hence the accessible classical-record/quantum-output QFI matrix is
  F = [[T^2, -2T np],
       [-2T np, 4((np)^2+np(1-p))]].
Treating theta as a nuisance gives efficient target information
  F_eff = F_ww - F_wt^2/F_tt
        = T^2 (1-p)/(np+1-p).
Thus (F^{-1})_ww = 1/T^2 + gamma/[T(1-p)].
In many independent repetitions a fixed logical equatorial quadrature
measurement achieves the conditional phase Fisher information locally away
from singular working points; ordinary likelihood inference therefore attains
this local coefficient. The asymptotic SQL coefficient matches the universal
lower bound as the individual interrogation time increases. Phase unwrapping,
global identifiability, and finite-data confidence require a separate protocol.

This is more informative than simply saying that an unknown axis ruins QEC.
The error-corrected state remains perfectly pure conditional on N. What fails
is distinguishing the signal phase from the phase imparted by the unknown
recovery angle: N is approximately gamma T, so the two regressors become
collinear. Record fluctuations supply only O(T) independent information.

5. Useful failed reasoning: quadratic residual decay is insufficient

If the guessed recovery axis is theta_hat, each jump leaves the logical unitary
exp(i delta Z), delta=theta-theta_hat. Ignoring the count record gives coherence
  exp[-i omega T + gamma T(exp(2i delta)-1)]
in the continuous limit. Its magnitude decays at gamma(1-cos 2delta), which is
2 gamma delta^2+O(delta^4), but its phase shifts at gamma sin 2delta, which is
2 gamma delta+O(delta^3). SQL calibration delta=O(T_cal^{-1/2}) therefore leaves
a first-order unknown frequency bias. The quadratic decay bound alone cannot
justify Heisenberg frequency accuracy. Retaining all flags does not remove the
single-rate information obstruction proved above.

6. General tangent obstruction (sufficient no-go, not complete classification)

For smooth finite-dimensional GKSL models H(x), L_k(x), take any parameter path
x(s) along which the target function changes. Define
  B_s = dot H + Im(sum_k dot L_k^dagger L_k),
where Im M=(M-M^dagger)/(2i).
If B_s=0 as an operator at the point, then the canonical infinitesimal channel
has beta=0 to first order and alpha=sum dot L_k^dagger dot L_k dt+O(dt^2).
Consequently the usual adaptive channel bound is linear in total time, with
QFI_s <= 4 integral ||sum dotL_k^dagger dotL_k|| dt under uniform boundedness.
If the target derivative is nonzero this gives an SQL lower bound for target
estimation uniformly over nuisance directions. Kraus gauge optimization can
generalize B_s=0 to cancellation by the corresponding Lindblad span, but that
extension needs a separately written theorem. The statement here is sufficient
and already proves the exact example.

7. Opening exposed by this failure

An independent intervention can break target/noise-tangent collinearity. For
example, two controllable nonzero noise rates gamma_1 != gamma_2, with the SAME
unknown theta and unchanged signal, yield two logical phases
omega t_1-2theta N_1 and omega t_2-2theta N_2. The observed N_i remove any need
to know the rates precisely for inference. With equal t and long-time Poisson
counts, the efficient expected QFI is
 t^2 [ (gamma_1-gamma_2)^2 +2(gamma_1+gamma_2)/t ]
     / [gamma_1^2+gamma_2^2+(gamma_1+gamma_2)/t].
It scales quadratically when rates stay separated. This is a constructive
direction, NOT an unqualified device claim: one must earn modulation that
preserves theta and the signal, count visibility, control fidelity, finite-rate
timing, ancilla protection, and a global estimator. The parent investigation
is developing this branch.
