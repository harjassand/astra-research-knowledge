# Fresh transduction frontier: competitor audit and a noise-weighted cut bound

Date: 1 November 2026 (Australia/Brisbane). This note is independent construction and primary-source research; no earlier Astra conversation or repository has been used.

## Consequential capability

Transfer an unknown microwave quantum state, including its entanglement with an arbitrary reference, to optical degrees of freedom despite a warm intermediate apparatus and finite inaccessible losses.

Consequently, neither an unknown hot initial oscillator state nor incomplete coherent mode conversion alone defines a fresh opening. The surviving physical obstruction is continuously injected inaccessible noise, source/storage loss, finite control resources, and robust acquisition of the needed transfer/noise information.

## Constructed theorem: passive noise-weighted cut bound

This is an ordinary derivation, not a claim of historical novelty. Its relation to known cooperativity limits needs further literature examination.

### Assumptions

There is one source oscillator a, a vector of mediator oscillators b, and arbitrary receiver/internal oscillators. The joint controlled dynamics is passive and linear, with arbitrary time-dependent number-preserving quadratic Hamiltonian. The source has Markov vacuum loss rate kappa > 0, and couples to the rest only through

    H_cut(t) = a^dagger g(t)^dagger b + b^dagger g(t) a.

The mediators have independent-of-input, gauge-invariant Gaussian Markov input noise with normally ordered diffusion matrix D(t) > 0. Independent thermal modes have D_jj = gamma_j n_j. Correlated baths are allowed if D is the actual positive diffusion matrix and the associated dissipative drift is included. The source loss bath is independent of these baths. Source detuning is arbitrary and known. No squeezing, measurement-conditioned feedback, access to the source loss bath, or external coherent bypass is permitted.

The receiver is a different canonical mode c at time T. Write its Heisenberg expansion as

    c(T) = s a(0) + other initial-mode terms + bath terms,

and eta = |s|^2. Let N be the contribution of the mediator baths to the output's normally ordered noise photon number. Any additional independent thermal noise only increases N.

### Claim

Define

    W_T = integral_0^T exp(-kappa t) g(t)^dagger D(t)^(-1) g(t) dt.

Then, for eta > 0,

    N / eta >= 1 / W_T.

If g(t)^dagger D(t)^(-1)g(t) <= C for all t, then

    N / eta >= kappa / [C(1-exp(-kappa T))] >= kappa/C.

### Proof

Let v_j(t) be the coefficient of mode j at time t in the final receiver c(T), i.e. the c row of the backward homogeneous propagator. In particular v_a(0)=s and v_a(T)=0. The source column of the backwards equation gives

    derivative v_a = (kappa/2 + i Delta_a(t)) v_a + i v_b g(t)

up to the harmless convention for the sign of Delta. Multiply by the integrating factor exp[-kappa t/2 - i integral_0^t Delta_a] and integrate. The boundary values imply

    |s| = | integral_0^T exp(-kappa t/2) phase(t) v_b(t) g(t) dt |.

The mediator-noise contribution is exactly

    N = integral_0^T v_b(t) D(t) v_b(t)^dagger dt.

Weighted Cauchy–Schwarz in the time × mediator Hilbert space now gives eta <= N W_T. No adiabatic approximation, short-time expansion, pulse smoothness or bound on the receiver network is required.

### Operational consequence

For a one-mode phase-insensitive Gaussian attenuator with vacuum-normal-ordered output noise N_total, entanglement breaking occurs when N_total >= eta. Therefore W_T <= 1 is a sufficient architecture-level obstruction to any passive protocol preserving source-reference entanglement. Under the uniform bound C, kappa >= C is sufficient for this obstruction at every T.

For one mediator, the condition reads g_max^2 <= kappa gamma n. This is a cooperativity/thermal-occupancy obstruction. Slower adiabatic transfer can suppress instantaneous mediator occupation but cannot make the input-referred noise vanish while the source continually loses amplitude.

The entanglement-breaking conclusion requires Gaussian gauge-invariant baths; the second-moment inequality itself needs only the stated independent diffusion covariance. A large second moment alone does not prove entanglement breaking for an arbitrary non-Gaussian bath.

### Multimode extension

For a source block S with uniform vacuum loss kappa and arbitrary passive internal Hamiltonian, let U_S(t) be its unitary isolated propagator and G(t) its coupling to the noisy cut modes. In a suitable interaction-picture convention,

    W = integral_0^T exp(-kappa t) U_S(t)^dagger G(t)^dagger D(t)^(-1) G(t) U_S(t) dt.

For any selected receiver block, let M be the source-to-receiver transfer matrix and N its mediator-bath normally ordered covariance. The Gram matrix of the corresponding time-domain kernels is positive:

    [[N, M], [M^dagger, W]] >= 0.

Hence N >= M W^(-1) M^dagger when W is positive definite. If W <= I, then N >= M M^dagger, the gauge-covariant Gaussian entanglement-breaking condition. This rules out an evasion by parallel passive encoding across the source block. Singular W is handled on its support with the associated range condition.

### Exact exits from the obstruction

* A genuine dark direction in ker D with nonzero controllable coupling makes W unbounded in this formulation; correlations can help, but one must earn the small diffusion eigenvalue and maintain alignment.
* Monitoring/collecting the otherwise lost bath makes its noise conditionally accessible and changes the task model.
* Squeezing and active/feedforward protocols change the passive equations and have their own finite-energy, detection and extraction-loss costs.
* Error-corrected non-Gaussian encoding changes the allowed operations and can exploit non-entanglement-breaking channels, but cannot recover quantum capacity from a truly entanglement-breaking cut using only local pre/postprocessing.
* Cooling or stronger coupling improves C and is an actual paid physical resource.

## Judgment

This bound is a useful obstruction and comparison instrument. It is not yet the most compelling fresh central investigation. A strong new transduction program would need a physically earned way to access/neutralize continuously injected noise, or a robust mechanism creating a useful noise-null direction without requiring a more precise and colder service than the interface itself. Merely demonstrating hot-bus independence at the end of a noiseless pulse sequence would leave the decisive failure untouched.
