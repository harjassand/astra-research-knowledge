# Counted cyclic jumps, residence control, and a robust-QEC comparator

Complementary derivations for the fresh Astra investigation, 1 November 2026.
These are mathematical constructions under the explicit instruments below, not hardware observations or historical novelty claims.

## 1. Physical model and exact qubit identity

Take a sensor qubit S, optional noiseless reference ancilla A, signal

\[
H_\omega=\omega Z_S/2,
\qquad L_\theta(t)=\sqrt{\lambda(t)}\,\sigma_\theta^S,
\qquad \sigma_\theta=\cos\theta X+\sin\theta Y.
\]

The unknown angle is fixed. The intensity \(\lambda\) is a scalar chosen predictably from an acquired record and does not depend on the unknown quantum input. Since \(L_\theta^\dagger L_\theta=\lambda I\), waiting probabilities contain no logical-state information. The identities

\[
\sigma_\theta^2=I,
\qquad
\sigma_\theta e^{-i\omega tZ/2}\sigma_\theta
=e^{+i\omega tZ/2}
\]

hold for every unknown \(\theta\).

Suppose every jump is recorded. For any trajectory ending at the \(2n\)-th jump, let \(w_1,\ldots,w_{2n}\) be consecutive waiting times and set

\[
\tau=\sum_j w_j,
\qquad A=\sum_{j=1}^{2n}(-1)^{j-1}w_j.
\]

Its normalized final operator is exactly

\[
\sigma_\theta U(w_{2n})\cdots\sigma_\theta U(w_1)
=U(A),\qquad U(t)=e^{-i\omega tZ/2}.
\]

Thus every unknown jump-axis phase cancels. This is an operator identity and consequently preserves arbitrary unknown input states and entanglement with a reference. It does not assert that the sensor was protected at every intermediate time.

### Instrument access must be earned

One may not infer an accessible jump record from the Lindblad generator alone. Two explicit options are:

1. A physically measured random-unitary collision instrument. With an environment qubit initially in \(|0\rangle\), use \(e^{-i\alpha\sigma_\theta\otimes Y_E}\) and measure E in the computational basis. Its two Kraus operators are proportional to \(I\) and \(\sigma_\theta\). Changing collision strength or rate controls a scalar intensity while retaining the same unknown axis. A positive minimum intensity must be imposed if turning the noise off would make the task trivial.
2. Encode \(|k\rangle_L\mapsto|k,k\rangle_{SA}\), and acquire an ideal continuous QND record of \(Z_SZ_A\). Every sensor jump toggles parity independent of the logical input. Do not correct the sensor or the ancilla between events. After an even number of jumps, the state returns to the code space automatically. The parity record determines the signed exposure A. Finite monitoring is a separate model, not supplied by this ideal-count construction.

Under direct event detection, finite delay in changing the intensity changes waiting-time statistics but not the exact trajectory identity. Unknown or missed event times, unobserved channels, and final stopping errors are separate costs.

## 2. Controlled residence times

Choose \(\gamma_\ell>0\) while event parity is even and \(\gamma_h>\gamma_\ell\) while parity is odd. Stop after \(2n\) jumps. The sums of even-parity and odd-parity waiting times are independent:

\[
S_\ell\sim\operatorname{Gamma}(n,\text{rate }\gamma_\ell),\quad
S_h\sim\operatorname{Gamma}(n,\text{rate }\gamma_h),
\quad A=S_\ell-S_h,
\quad\tau=S_\ell+S_h.
\]

For a logical plus state the conditional quantum Fisher information is \(A^2\). The record is independent of \(\omega\), so the joint classical-quantum information is

\[
F_\omega=\mathbb E A^2
=n^2\left(\frac1{\gamma_\ell}-\frac1{\gamma_h}\right)^2
+n\left(\frac1{\gamma_\ell^2}+\frac1{\gamma_h^2}\right).
\]

Meanwhile

\[
\mathbb E\tau=n\left(\frac1{\gamma_\ell}+\frac1{\gamma_h}\right),\quad
\operatorname{Var}\tau=n\left(\frac1{\gamma_\ell^2}+\frac1{\gamma_h^2}\right).
\]

Therefore the leading coefficient relative to squared mean duration is

\[
\frac{F_\omega}{(\mathbb E\tau)^2}
\longrightarrow
c^2,
\qquad c=\frac{\gamma_h-\gamma_\ell}{\gamma_h+\gamma_\ell}.
\]

Rates need not be known precisely for the exact output channel or the estimator: A comes from the record. Rate knowledge is used here to predict resource statistics. Unknown rates add classical count/waiting-time nuisance parameters; the normalized conditional quantum state depends only on observed A, so their Fisher cross-block with \(\omega\) vanishes.

The information statement is local. One long Ramsey experiment is not a globally unambiguous estimator over an arbitrary frequency range. Repetition or a preliminary/multiscale phase-estimation procedure is needed to resolve phase wrapping and approach the local bound.

## 3. Why stopping alone is insufficient: exact martingale SQL

This result extends beyond a fixed number of cycles. Let \(N_t\) be a Poisson process of fixed rate \(\gamma\), \(y_t=(-1)^{N_{t-}}\), and let \(\tau\) be any stopping time ending with an even count, with \(\mathbb E\tau<\infty\). Define

\[
A_\tau=\int_0^\tau y_t\,dt,
\qquad M_t=\int_0^t y_s(dN_s-\gamma ds).
\]

Since the signed event sum alternates \(+1,-1\) and finishes after an even number,

\[
0=\int_0^\tau y_t\,dN_t=\gamma A_\tau+M_\tau.
\]

The compensated stochastic integral has predictable quadratic variation \(\langle M\rangle_\tau=\gamma\tau\). The stopped isometry (obtained first for bounded stopping times and then by the L2 limit using finite expected exposure) yields

\[
\boxed{\mathbb E A_\tau^2=\frac{\mathbb E\tau}{\gamma}.}
\]

Thus no clever even-event stopping rule within this monitored-telegraph model recovers a quadratic time coefficient at a fixed rate. Scalar rate control changes the attainable physical information; it is not merely an improved estimator.

### Scoped optimality for bounded rate control

Suppose instead \(\gamma_\ell\le\lambda_t\le\gamma_h\). Let

\[
\lambda_0=(\gamma_\ell+\gamma_h)/2,
\quad c=(\gamma_h-\gamma_\ell)/(\gamma_h+\gamma_\ell).
\]

At any even-event stop,

\[
0=\lambda_0 A_\tau+
\int_0^\tau y_t(\lambda_t-\lambda_0)dt+M_\tau.
\]

Hence

\[
|A_\tau|\le c\tau+|M_\tau|/\lambda_0,
\quad
\sqrt{\mathbb EA_\tau^2}
\le c\sqrt{\mathbb E\tau^2}
+\frac{\sqrt{\gamma_h\mathbb E\tau}}{\lambda_0}.
\]

If durations concentrate so \(\mathbb E\tau^2/(\mathbb E\tau)^2\to1\), the leading quadratic coefficient cannot exceed \(c^2\). The endpoint-per-parity construction attains it. This optimality is scoped to the indicated count-preserving telegraph policies, not arbitrary quantum strategies.

There is also an exact mean-exposure inequality. Expected plus-sector and minus-sector event counts agree at an even stop. Bounding those counts by \(\gamma_\ell\mathbb ET_\pm\) and \(\gamma_h\mathbb ET_\pm\) gives

\[
|\mathbb EA_\tau|\le c\mathbb E\tau.
\]

Mean time alone can be a misleading resource for general variable-duration quantum protocols; both the controlled second moment and the hard-cap result below are therefore included.

## 4. Hard deadline with an explicitly charged erasure

Let \(a=1/\gamma_\ell\), \(b=1/\gamma_h\), \(c=(a-b)/(a+b)>0\). For \(0<u<c\), define

\[
B=n(1+u)(a+b),\qquad h(u)=u-\log(1+u).
\]

Gamma Chernoff bounds give

\[
\Pr\left(\left|\frac{\gamma_jS_j}{n}-1\right|>u\right)
\le2e^{-nh(u)},\quad j\in\{\ell,h\}.
\]

On the joint good event, which has probability at least \(1-4e^{-nh(u)}\),

\[
\tau\le B,
\qquad A\ge n[(a-b)-u(a+b)]
=B\frac{c-u}{1+u}.
\]

Terminate every attempted experiment at B. If \(2n\) jumps have not completed, output an explicit erasure and discard the quantum state. This failure event is independent of the unknown input and frequency under the model. The complete instrument therefore includes its cost, rather than postselecting it away. Its total information per attempt obeys

\[
F_\omega^{\rm cap}
\ge\bigl[1-4e^{-nh(u)}\bigr]_+
B^2\left(\frac{c-u}{1+u}\right)^2.
\]

On every successful record, the channel identity for arbitrary input/reference is exact. The erasure probability is at most the stated failure bound. If rates are not known, conservative upper/lower rate bounds suffice to choose a safe cap and contrast; precise axis or rate calibration is not required by the cancellation identity.

## 5. Extension to unknown phase-dressed cyclic jumps

Let S and A have dimension q, encode \(|k\rangle\mapsto|k,k\rangle\), and take

\[
H_\omega=\omega D_S,\qquad D=\operatorname{diag}(h_0,\ldots,h_{q-1}),
\]

\[
U_\phi|k\rangle=e^{i\phi_k}|k+1\bmod q\rangle,
\qquad L=\sqrt\gamma\,U_\phi.
\]

All \(\phi_k\) are fixed and unknown. Their sum gives only a global cycle phase:

\[
U_\phi^q=e^{i\sum_k\phi_k}I.
\]

Moreover

\[
U_\phi^{-j}DU_\phi^j=D_j,
\qquad (D_j)_{kk}=h_{k+j\bmod q},
\]

which is known and diagonal. A QND measurement of modular difference between S and A identifies the syndrome \(j=N\bmod q\), independently of the logical input. No intermediate correction is required.

For any trajectory stopped after qn jumps, let \(S_j\) be total waiting time in syndrome j. Pulling each jump unitary to the left and using the commuting diagonals gives, up to global phase,

\[
\boxed{U_{\rm out}=\exp\left[-i\omega\sum_{j=0}^{q-1}S_jD_j\right].}
\]

All unknown phases disappear from the actual output operator. This equality again holds with an arbitrary reference.

With constant chosen intensity \(\gamma_j\) in syndrome j, the \(S_j\) are independent \(\operatorname{Gamma}(n,\gamma_j)\). For initial \((|a\rangle+|b\rangle)/\sqrt2\), write \(d_j=h_{a+j}-h_{b+j}\). Then

\[
F_\omega
=n^2\left(\sum_j\frac{d_j}{\gamma_j}\right)^2
+n\sum_j\frac{d_j^2}{\gamma_j^2},
\quad \mathbb E\tau=n\sum_j\gamma_j^{-1}.
\]

At equal rates \(\sum_jd_j=0\), leaving only the linear term. Unequal dwell weights can make the first term nonzero. The general principle is cancellation of unknown cyclic jump phases combined with controlled occupation of a known signal orbit. It requires an actual cyclic unitary error model and acquired trajectory information; it does not cover arbitrary unknown quantum noise.

## 6. Slow drift and event pairing

For qubits with time-dependent axis \(\theta(t)\), consecutive jumps no longer cancel exactly. The kth adjacent pair contributes a Z rotation proportional to \(\theta(t_{2k-1})-\theta(t_{2k})\). If \(\theta\) is L-Lipschitz, the absolute additional relative phase is at most

\[
2L\sum_{k=1}^n(t_{2k}-t_{2k-1})=2L S_h.
\]

This adjacent-pair construction is materially better against smooth drift than canceling all first-half jumps against all second-half jumps, whose worst-case drift difference can grow like \(L\gamma T^2\). Drift still produces a real frequency-error floor; exact stationary cancellation does not solve it.

## 7. Comparison with separate fixed-time nuisance estimation

For two separately measured corrected blocks of durations \(T_1,T_2\) and rates \(\gamma r_1,\gamma r_2\), the conditional phase is \(\omega T_j-2\theta N_j\). The classical-quantum Fisher matrix is

\[
F_{\omega\omega}=\sum_jT_j^2,\quad
F_{\omega\theta}=-2\gamma\sum_jr_jT_j^2,\quad
F_{\theta\theta}=4\sum_j(\gamma^2r_j^2T_j^2+\gamma r_jT_j).
\]

Let \(R=r_2/r_1>1\). Optimizing the leading nuisance-projected information for fixed \(T_1+T_2=T\) gives

\[
\frac{F_{\omega|\theta}}{T^2}\to
\frac{(R-1)^2}{(1+R^{2/3})^3},\qquad
\frac{T_1}{T_2}=R^{2/3}.
\]

The coherent counted-echo coefficient is \((R-1)^2/(R+1)^2\), which is larger. This is a comparison with this specific independently measured two-block strategy, not a universal comparator against all optimized conventional protocols.

## 8. Static syndrome-dependent dissipation and the strong conventional code

A different physical resource is a joint dissipative coupling

\[
L_\theta=\sigma_\theta^S R,\qquad
R=\sqrt{\gamma_e}P_e+\sqrt{\gamma_o}P_o,
\quad P_{e/o}=(I\pm Z_SZ_A)/2.
\]

This directly supplies parity-dependent rates. It is a specified two-body dissipative interaction, not supplied by an arbitrary tunable local bath.

### Dressed subsystem interpretation

Choose basis \(|k,0\rangle_{\rm phys}=|k,k\rangle\), \(|k,1\rangle_{\rm phys}=\sigma_\theta^S|k,k\rangle\). Then

\[
L_\theta=I_L\otimes
(\sqrt{\gamma_e}|1\rangle\langle0|+\sqrt{\gamma_o}|0\rangle\langle1|),
\qquad H_\omega=\omega Z_L\otimes Z_{\rm syn}/2.
\]

At \(\omega=0\), L acts only on the syndrome subsystem. For unequal positive rates, its unique stationary syndrome population is \(\pi_e=\gamma_o/(\gamma_e+\gamma_o)\), \(\pi_o=\gamma_e/(\gamma_e+\gamma_o)\). The first-order effective logical signal is \(\omega vZ_L/2\), where

\[
v=(\gamma_o-\gamma_e)/(\gamma_o+\gamma_e).
\]

The unknown angle lives in the dressing. Preparation and readout in the even sector avoid knowing that dressing.

### Exact finite-detuning output for the static model

Starting in the even sector, let \(f_e(\omega,t)=\mathbb E[e^{-i\omega A_t}\mathbf1_{\mathrm{even}}]\), where \(A_t\) is integrated signed residence time. The Feynman–Kac equations are

\[
\frac d{dt}\binom{f_e}{f_o}=
\begin{pmatrix}-\gamma_e-i\omega&\gamma_o\\\gamma_e&-\gamma_o+i\omega\end{pmatrix}
\binom{f_e}{f_o},\qquad(f_e(0),f_o(0))=(1,0).
\]

Write \(s=\gamma_e+\gamma_o\), \(d=\gamma_o-\gamma_e\), and
\(\kappa=\sqrt{s^2/4-id\omega-\omega^2}\), with the branch continuous from \(\kappa(0)=s/2\). Then

\[
f_e(\omega,t)=e^{-st/2}
\left[\cosh(\kappa t)+\frac{d/2-i\omega}{\kappa}\sinh(\kappa t)\right].
\]

The even-sector success probability is
\(p_e(t)=f_e(0,t)=\gamma_o/s+(\gamma_e/s)e^{-st}\).
The slow eigenvalue has expansion

\[
-s/2+\kappa=-iv\omega-D\omega^2+O(\omega^3),
\quad v=d/s,
\quad D=(1-v^2)/s=4\gamma_e\gamma_o/s^3.
\]

Consequently the long-time normalized even-sector coherence has leading visibility \(e^{-D\omega^2t}\) and phase \(-v\omega t\). The useful residual-frequency window is of order \(1/\sqrt{Dt}\), while the desired local resolution is of order \(1/t\). This supports an adaptive operating-point route but is not itself a global adaptive estimation proof. Adding a physically implemented compensating Z Hamiltonian must preserve the assumed jump instrument; merely changing a rotating-frame convention does not earn that fact. The model does not provide noiseless sensing at arbitrary fixed nonzero residual frequency for arbitrarily long time.

### Existing robust-QEC theory already permits the capability with these interactions

Do not compare only with a single-qubit unknown-axis obstruction once the bath is allowed to depend on A. Define

\[
L_x=X_SR,\quad L_y=Y_SR,\quad
a=(\gamma_e+\gamma_o)/2,\quad b=(\gamma_e-\gamma_o)/2.
\]

Their error products include

\[
L_x^\dagger L_x=L_y^\dagger L_y=R^2=aI+bZ_SZ_A,
\quad L_x^\dagger L_y=i(aZ_S+bZ_A).
\]

For \(b\ne0\), \(Z_S/2\) lies outside the robust Lindblad span generated by \(\{L_x,L_y\}\). The relevant operator-norm distance is

\[
\min_c\left(\left|\frac12-ca\right|+|cb|\right)
=\frac{|b|}{2a}=\frac{|v|}{2}.
\]

Pinching eliminates off-diagonal candidates without increasing norm. Adding identity and parity terms cannot reduce this minimum: pairwise diameters of diagonal entries with simultaneously inverted Z signs already force the displayed maximum. The standard HNLS metrology construction therefore allows a logical signal gap \(|v|\), optimal for the enlarged simultaneous two-channel model or a single exact common code correcting its full error span, given its noiseless-ancilla and fast-recovery resources. This is not an upper bound on all adaptive strategies for one unknown but stationary angle.

There is an explicit theta-independent code using one further noiseless qubit Q. Let \(p=(1+v)/2\). In S,A,Q order, set

\[
|0_L\rangle=\sqrt p|000\rangle+\sqrt{1-p}|101\rangle,
\]

\[
|1_L\rangle=\sqrt{1-p}|010\rangle+\sqrt p|111\rangle.
\]

For its projector P,

\[
PL_xP=PL_yP=0,\quad
PR^2P=(a+bv)P,\quad
PL_x^\dagger L_yP=0,\quad
PZ_SP=vZ_L.
\]

Thus the Knill–Laflamme conditions hold for \(\{I,L_x,L_y\}\), uniformly in unknown theta. Ideal frequent recovery supplies \(v^2T^2\) QFI. The code depends on the rate ratio through v, and its gates and extra ancilla must be charged. A finite-monitoring/no-recovery construction should therefore be assessed for its operational simplification and calibration demand against this comparator, not presented as a new possibility of Heisenberg scaling under the enlarged interactions.

The generic theoretical precursor is Zhou et al., *Achieving the Heisenberg limit in quantum metrology using quantum error correction*, Nature Communications 9, 78 (2018), https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-017-02510-3 and https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5758555/ . The explicit three-qubit code and comparison above were derived here.

## 9. Executable checks

`count_echo_complement.py` checks the cyclic output identity at q=2,3,5 with arbitrary random unknown phases, checks every Knill–Laflamme product of the robust comparator code, and compares the Gamma-law QFI with Monte Carlo waiting times. Seed: 20261101. Observed maximum operator-norm residuals were 4.08e-15, 4.13e-15, and 9.15e-15. The robust code residuals at rates 1 and 4 were exactly zero to the used floating-point arithmetic. For n=200 and rates 1 and 4, analytic QFI 22712.5 was compared with 22717.90 ± 9.88 Monte Carlo standard error. These checks validate algebra and statistics only.
