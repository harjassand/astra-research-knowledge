# Finite-interval jump-count echo and accessible-bath obstruction

Independent derivation supporting the root investigation, 1 November 2026.

## Model and encoding

Sensor Hamiltonian H = omega Z/2, state-independent Hermitian Markov jump L_theta = sqrt(gamma)(cos(theta) X + sin(theta) Y). One noiseless ancilla. Code |0_L>=|00>, |1_L>=|11>. Measure ZZ parity once per fixed interval Delta and apply nominal X on the sensor iff parity is odd. Boundary measurements and corrections are ideal in the exact theorem below; their duration and error are separate physical costs.

Let J_theta = cos(theta)X + sin(theta)Y. Then J_theta^2=I and X J_theta = exp(i theta Z). During one interval a Poisson jump history with count m and jump times generates the corrected logical unitary

    U_history = exp(i theta (m mod 2) Z_L) exp(-i omega Y Z_L/2),
    Y = integral_0^Delta (-1)^(N_s) ds.

This follows by commuting all sensor Z rotations to the right through J_theta. Hidden even jump pairs cancel their theta dependence exactly. Every odd *syndrome episode*, regardless of its actual jump count, contributes exactly one theta rotation.

## Exact finite-interval instrument

Define q_o=(1-exp(-2 gamma Delta))/2 and q_e=1-q_o. Let

    d = sqrt(gamma^2-omega^2),
    F_e = exp(-gamma Delta)[cosh(d Delta) - i omega sinh(d Delta)/d],
    F_o = exp(-gamma Delta) gamma sinh(d Delta)/d,
    f_p = F_p/q_p.

At d=0 the expression is continued using sinh(d Delta)/d -> Delta. For |omega|>gamma it is interpreted by analytic continuation (sine and cosine). F_p is the characteristic function E[exp(-i omega Y) 1_{N_Delta mod 2=p}], derived from the two-state tilted generator

    [[-gamma-i omega, gamma], [gamma, -gamma+i omega]].

Conditional on syndrome p, the logical populations are unchanged and coherence rho_01 is multiplied by

    exp(i 2 p theta) f_p.

The syndrome probabilities q_p are independent of omega, theta, logical input, and any reference entanglement. Therefore this is an instrument equality on arbitrary joint logical-reference states, not only a chosen sensing input.

## Count-matched echo

Stage 1 uses rate gamma_1, interval Delta_1, and runs until n odd syndrome episodes have been corrected. Apply logical X_L. Stage 2 uses gamma_2, Delta_2, and likewise runs until n odd episodes. Undo X_L at the end. For observed record containing E_j even intervals and n odd intervals in stage j, the net normalized coherence factor, up to choosing which stage is conjugated, is

    c_record(omega) = (f_e,1^(E_1) f_o,1^n)^* (f_e,2^(E_2) f_o,2^n).

The factors exp(i 2 n theta) cancel exactly. The cancellation holds for every record, arbitrary number of hidden jumps, arbitrary omega, and arbitrary unknown but constant theta. It does not require gamma_j to be known. Gamma_j determines signal calibration and record likelihood, not cancellation of theta.

Changing theta between stages would leave a residual logical rotation proportional to n(theta_2-theta_1); slowly drifting theta is a different model and must be paid for.

## Exact local information and elapsed time

At the controlled working point omega=0,

    E[Y | odd] = 0,
    a_j := E[Y | even] = tanh(gamma_j Delta_j)/gamma_j.

Consequently, for a logical |+> input a final logical Y measurement has conditional classical Fisher information for omega

    F_record = B_record^2,
    B_record = E_2 a_2 - E_1 a_1.

There is no hidden-history dephasing at omega=0. The derivative is a coherent phase derivative, so the stated information is attained by that explicit measurement. The classical record probabilities contain no omega information. Calling this achieved classical information avoids a subtlety: the state changes rank at this working point, and the continuous limit of quantum Fisher information can differ from its SLD value exactly at the pure point.

E_j has the negative-binomial distribution counting failures before n successes, with success q_o,j and failure p_j=q_e,j. Therefore

    E[E_j] = n p_j/q_o,j,
    Var(E_j) = n p_j/q_o,j^2,
    E[a_j E_j] = n/gamma_j,
    Var(a_j E_j) = n/(gamma_j^2 p_j).

The total Fisher information attained by the record plus the logical Y measurement is exactly

    F = n^2 (1/gamma_2 - 1/gamma_1)^2
        + n[1/(gamma_1^2 p_1) + 1/(gamma_2^2 p_2)].

The expected actual elapsed sensing time is exactly

    E[T] = n[Delta_1/q_o,1 + Delta_2/q_o,2].

Thus distinct fixed rates retain F proportional to E[T]^2 at this local operating point even at fixed, finite parity-readout spacing. In the fine-readout limit the leading information per time squared is

    [(gamma_1-gamma_2)/(gamma_1+gamma_2)]^2.

For identical rates and readout spacings the n^2 term vanishes and

    F/E[T] = tanh(gamma Delta)/(gamma^2 Delta) <= 1/gamma.

It tends to 1/gamma as Delta tends to zero. This exactly matches the accessible-bath nuisance bound below, so the equal-rate case is a useful internal consistency check.

The total stopping time is unbounded; an experiment needing a hard deadline must cap intervals and keep the resulting abort flag. An exponentially small tail is available from the negative-binomial/binomial correspondence, and the capped channel must charge aborts. Expected-time information alone is not a deadline guarantee.

## Hidden-time phase uncertainty away from the working point

Each Y lies in [-Delta,Delta], hence Var(Y | syndrome) <= Delta^2. Given a fixed record and independent reset intervals, the residual exposure fluctuation V about its conditional mean has Var(V) <= M_1 Delta_1^2+M_2 Delta_2^2, with M_j=E_j+n. The elementary inequality |exp(-ix)-1+ix|<=x^2/2 gives

    |c_record(omega)-exp(-i omega B_record)|
      <= (omega^2/2)[M_1 Delta_1^2+M_2 Delta_2^2].

This is also an upper bound on the diamond distance between the corresponding qubit dephasing channel and the ideal known-exposure rotation. The half-diamond convention introduces one additional factor 1/2.

For fixed nonzero residual omega, accumulated hidden timing dephases long experiments. Local quadratic information scaling does not itself establish a globally unambiguous estimator. A physically calibrated compensating Z drive, a coarse-estimation stage and adaptive phase unwrapping must be earned to keep the residual near zero. The required coarse localization from this simple bound is |omega_residual| Delta sqrt(M) << 1.

## Imperfect boundary operations

If one implemented readout/correction cycle differs from the ideal interval instrument by diamond norm at most epsilon_cycle (including all gate, waiting, readout, switching and controller exposure), then a fixed record-independent cap M_max gives total instrument diamond error <= M_max epsilon_cycle by telescoping. A random-duration protocol can similarly charge an expectation under additional care, but a deterministic cap gives the clean statement.

This requires an actual acquired or proved complete-instrument error bound; merely assigning a gate infidelity is insufficient. At fixed nonzero epsilon_cycle, asymptotic Heisenberg scaling is not established. The exact construction supplies a finite useful window M_max epsilon_cycle << 1. More fault-tolerant control would be an additional resource.

## Why output-bath monitoring alone does not remove the fixed-rate nuisance obstruction

Consider a discrete collision regularization with dt, p=gamma dt, U_omega=exp(-i omega Z dt/2), and the full-output isometry

    V_(omega,theta) = |0>_bath sqrt(1-p) U_omega
                     + |1>_bath sqrt(p) J_theta U_omega.

All output bath modes are retained; this is more information than photon counting or homodyne monitoring. Along the nuisance path theta(xi)=theta_0+xi, omega(xi)=omega_0+2 gamma xi, direct calculation gives

    V^dagger derivative_xi V = 0,
    derivative_xi V^dagger derivative_xi V = p(1-p) I.

For n=T/dt repeated collisions, allow arbitrary parameter-independent controls, ancillas, measurements implemented coherently, and even coherent reuse of every earlier output mode. Cross-time derivative inner products vanish because V^dagger derivative V is zero on the entire input space. The global pure-output QFI for xi is therefore at most (indeed exactly for this pure regularization)

    F_xi <= 4 n p(1-p) -> 4 gamma T.

Any locally unbiased estimator of omega in the presence of unknown theta must remain unbiased along this path. Since derivative_xi omega=2 gamma,

    Var(omega_hat) >= (2 gamma)^2/F_xi >= gamma/T.

This derivation includes a bath interferometer or downstream phase reference because these are parameter-independent transformations of already retained outputs. It does not include driving the bath *before* interaction, changing its coupling rate, adding an independent coherent theta-dependent Hamiltonian, or accessing a different physical dilation. Those change the experiment itself.

The continuum claim follows through the collision limit with p=gamma dt+O(dt^2); for a fully formal publication, specify the quantum stochastic dilation and regularity used to pass QFI bounds through this limit.
