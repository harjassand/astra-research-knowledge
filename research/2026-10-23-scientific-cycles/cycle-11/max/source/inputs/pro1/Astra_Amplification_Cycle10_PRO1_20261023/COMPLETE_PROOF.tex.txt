\begin{abstract}
For the same infinite evolving bath and root-retaining qutrit device,
we prove a reliable unassisted rate at least
\[
\frac32+\frac{\ln2}{2^{20}}\beta^2,\qquad 0<\beta\le1/32.
\]
The input block has length of order \(\beta^{-2}\), fixed before the
coding horizon. The same code sequence works pointwise for every fixed
normal initializer in full diamond norm.

The new exact response estimate separates scalar diagonal charge drift
from all coherent input contributions. The latter have full bath-operator
norm at most \(r e^{2r^2}\) times a weighted input norm, where
\(r=\beta^2 k\). It controls higher-endpoint coherences without treating
the device as classical noise. A square normalized within complete charge
patterns is positive and preserves its diagonal exactly. Its nonlinear
offdiagonal remainder is small in the norm controlled by the response
theorem. A charge-only tilt then gives entropy gain and strict inward drift.

The normalization is necessary for this construction: the ordinary
trace-normalized square has an exactly computable positive charge bias of
order \(\sqrt{k}\) at fixed \(r>0\), and actual uniform outward bulk drift.
This obstruction is scoped to that input family. No sharp capacity law,
feedback equality, terminal bath return, efficient compiler or physical
device performance is asserted.
\end{abstract}

\section{All-distance coherent response in a weighted input norm}
\label{sec:hammingresponse}

Retain the actual root-retaining device and its ordered $k$-call word.
Write $b=\beta^2$, $a=1-b$, $Q=\sum_iq_i$, $A=Q-k/2$, and
$G_\beta=Q-W_k^*QW_k$. Restrict the initial bath to depths at least $k$.
After total input-charge pinching, this displacement is represented on
the input and its first $k$ bath colors, tensor identity on the seed and
untouched tail. Denote this finite representation by $\mathcal G_k(b)$.
The actual root is retained outside this bulk; it has not been replaced
by a reflecting boundary or a fresh bath.

For $x\in\{0,1\}^k$, let $P_x$ be the complete input charge-pattern
projection and let $\mathcal P(X)=\sum_xP_xXP_x$. All these maps act as
identity on the bath-prefix factor when applied to joint operators.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform all-coherence response]\label{thm:hammingresponse}
Let $\omega$ be any normalized input density with
$\mathcal P(\omega)=\omega$. Define the unital completely positive map
\[
 \Phi_\omega(X)=\operatorname{Tr}_{\rm input}[(\omega\otimes I)X].
\]
For every Hermitian input $Y$ commuting with $Q$, put
$Y_\perp=Y-\mathcal P(Y)$ and
$\|Y_\perp\|_{2,\omega}^2=\operatorname{Tr}(\omega Y_\perp^2)$.
Then, on the full bath-prefix operator space,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hammingresponse}
 \left\|
 \Phi_\omega[(Y\otimes I)\mathcal G_k(b)]
 -2a\operatorname{Tr}(\omega AY)I
 \right\|
 \le r e^{2r^2}\|Y_\perp\|_{2,\omega},\qquad r=bk.
\end{equation}
In particular the coefficient is at most $2r$ for $0\le r\le1/2$.
For the actual reference
$\sigma=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1/4,1/4)^{\otimes k}$, every
charge-preserving $Y$ commutes with $\sigma$, so this is the Hermitian
physical signed-input response $D_b(\sigma Y)$.

The exact diagonal identity used here is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hammingdiagonal}
 \mathcal P(\mathcal G_k(b))=a(2Q-kI)\otimes I.
\end{equation}
The reference may have correlated, nonuniform color fibers. No choice of
bath-prefix state is made in \eqref{eq:hammingresponse}.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Exact selection at \texorpdfstring{$2m$}{2m} flipped endpoints}

Fix input charge patterns $x,y$ of equal weight. Let
$T=\{i:x_i\ne y_i\}$, $|T|=2m$, with $m\ge1$.
There are $m$ zeros and $m$ ones of $x$ on $T$.
At a bulk call, a move flips the binary input charge and a stay retains it.
Thus equality of the two output charge words forces identical choices
at every spectator and complementary choices at each endpoint in $T$.

Let $S\subseteq T^c$ be the common spectator stay set, and
$s\subseteq T$ the ket endpoint stay set. The bra endpoint stay set is
$T\setminus s$. The product of the two amplitudes is
\[
 (ab)^m b^{|S|}a^{k-2m-|S|}\epsilon_s,
 \qquad \epsilon_s=(-1)^{m+|s|}.
\]
Let $R_{s,S}^{yx}$ denote the associated compressed raw cross-history
block, retaining input color wires and all final bath overlaps.
It is a contraction. On this selected charge block the common output
charge has the form
\[
 q_{\rm out}(s,S)=q_{\rm spectator}(x,S)+q_T(x,s),
 \qquad
 q_T(x,s)=m+\sum_{i\in T}(2x_i-1)1_{i\in s}.
\]

Keep spectator calls equal to their fixed $U$ or $I$ choices and endpoint
calls equal to $V_\beta$. This auxiliary ordered word is unitary. Its
$y,x$ block in $W^*W$ vanishes. All its contributing terms have the
same nonzero factor $(ab)^m$ for $0<b<1$, so
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hammingunitarity}
 \sum_{s\subseteq T}\epsilon_sR_{s,S}^{yx}=0.
\end{equation}
The raw blocks do not depend on $b$, so the identity also controls the
endpoint values by continuity. It cancels the entire spectator output
charge, and also permits subtracting the constant $m$ from endpoint
output charge. Since the input $Q$ term is offdiagonal-zero,
\[
 P_y\mathcal G_k(b)P_x
 =(ab)^m\sum_{S\subseteq T^c}
       b^{|S|}a^{k-2m-|S|}B_{yx}^S,
\]
where
\[
 B_{yx}^S=-\sum_{s\subseteq T}
        \epsilon_s(q_T(x,s)-m)R_{s,S}^{yx}.
\]
The spectator weights sum to one. The map from endpoint stay bits to
endpoint output bits is a bijection, hence
\begin{align}
 \|B_{yx}^S\|
 &\le \sum_{j=0}^{2m}\binom{2m}{j}|j-m|
 =m\binom{2m}{m}=:c_m,\notag\\
 \|P_y\mathcal G_k(b)P_x\|&\le c_m(ab)^m.
 \label{eq:hammingblock}
\end{align}
For the displayed binomial identity, pair the terms on the two sides of
$m$ and use
$ (j-m)\binom{2m}{j}
=m[\binom{2m-1}{j-1}-\binom{2m-1}{j}]$
in the upper half; the sum telescopes.
For $m=1$, $c_1=2$, reproducing the completed pair-supported block bound.
For $m\ge2$, all higher-endpoint coherences remain present.

If $x=y$, equality of output charges instead forces the two stay masks
to be identical at every call. For any one mask, the raw word is an
isometry on that input charge pattern, and its output charge is scalar.
The squared amplitudes give mean output charge
$b|x|+a(k-|x|)$. The corresponding displacement is
$|x|-b|x|-a(k-|x|)=a(2|x|-k)$, independently of input colors and the
prefix. This proves \eqref{eq:hammingdiagonal}.

\subsection{Row-square control and operator Cauchy--Schwarz}

Set
\[
 Z=\mathcal G_k(b)-a(2Q-kI)\otimes I.
\]
Thus $Z=Z^*$, $\mathcal P(Z)=0$, and its nonzero input charge blocks
have equal total charge. For fixed $x$ of weight $q$, exactly
$\binom qm\binom{k-q}m$ patterns $y$ have distance $2m$ and the same
weight. Complete-pattern pinching of the square has no cross-column terms:
\[
 P_xZ^2P_x=\sum_y P_xZP_yZP_x.
\]
Each summand is positive and bounded by the square of
\eqref{eq:hammingblock}. Consequently
\begin{align}
 P_xZ^2P_x
 &\le \sum_{m\ge1}\binom qm\binom{k-q}m
                      c_m^2(ab)^{2m}P_x\notag\\
 &\le \sum_{m\ge1}
       \frac{k^{2m}}{4^m(m!)^2}c_m^2 b^{2m}P_x.
 \label{eq:hammingrows}
\end{align}
We used $q(k-q)\le k^2/4$ and $a\le1$; nonexistent binomial terms
are zero. The central binomial bound
$\binom{2m}{m}\le 2^{2m-1}$, valid for every $m\ge1$, now gives
\begin{align}
 \mathcal P(Z^2)
 &\le\sum_{m\ge1}
       \frac{m^24^{m-1}r^{2m}}{(m!)^2}I\notag\\
 &=r^2\sum_{j\ge0}\frac{(4r^2)^j}{(j!)^2}I
 \le r^2e^{4r^2}I.
 \label{eq:hammingrowmoment}
\end{align}
The finite physical sum was only enlarged by the infinite positive series.

Because $\omega=\mathcal P(\omega)$,
$\Phi_\omega(Z^2)=\Phi_\omega(\mathcal P(Z^2))
\le r^2e^{4r^2}I$.
The map $\Phi_\omega$ is unital and completely positive: it can equivalently
be written with $\omega^{1/2}$ on each side of its argument before the
input partial trace. Applying it to the positive $2$-by-$2$ operator matrix
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}
 (Y_\perp^2\otimes I)&(Y_\perp\otimes I)Z\\
 Z(Y_\perp\otimes I)&Z^2
 \end{pmatrix}
 =
 \begin{pmatrix}Y_\perp\otimes I\\Z\end{pmatrix}
 \begin{pmatrix}Y_\perp\otimes I&Z\end{pmatrix}
\]
gives, by its scalar upper-left block,
\[
 \|\Phi_\omega[(Y_\perp\otimes I)Z]\|^2
 \le \operatorname{Tr}(\omega Y_\perp^2)\,
        \|\Phi_\omega(Z^2)\|.
\]
No commutation of $Y_\perp$ and $Z$ is needed. The diagonal part of $Y$
paired with $Z$ has zero input partial trace, and the remaining diagonal
response is exactly $2a\operatorname{Tr}(\omega AY)I$.
This proves \eqref{eq:hammingresponse}. Since $e^{1/2}<2$, its final
$2r$ form holds for $r\le1/2$.

\subsection{A separate flip-set norm interface}

For the actual reference $\sigma$, define
$Y_T=\sum_xP_{x\mathbin{\triangle}T}YP_x$, restricting to equal-charge
pairs. Distinct $T$ are orthogonal in the weighted Hilbert--Schmidt inner
product. If $|T|=2m$, $Y_T$ is supported where $Q_T=m$, whose $\sigma$
mass is $p_m=\binom{2m}{m}/4^m$. Hence
\[
 \|\sigma Y_T\|_1
 \le\sqrt{p_m}\|Y_T\|_{2,\sigma}.
\]
Combining the exact $T$ response with Cauchy--Schwarz over $T$ gives
the occasionally sharper finite-$k$ coefficient
\[
 C_k(b)^2=\sum_{m=1}^{\lfloor k/2\rfloor}
 \binom{k}{2m}p_m c_m^2(ab)^{2m}.
\]
Thus the right side of \eqref{eq:hammingresponse} may also be replaced
by $C_k(b)\|Y_\perp\|_{2,\sigma}$. Its first squared term is
$k(k-1)(ab)^2$. The same central-binomial estimate bounds
$C_k(b)\le re^{2r^2}$. This alternate derivation is not needed for the
general-reference row proof.

\subsection{Necessary scope and receiving use}

The diagonal charge term cannot be omitted. For the actual $\sigma$,
take $Y=A$: $\|A\|_{2,\sigma}=\sqrt{k}/2$, while its exact bulk response
is $D_b(\sigma A)=akI/2$. At fixed small $r=bk$, even the normalized
choice $Y=A/\sqrt{k}$ has bounded weighted norm and response diverging
as $\sqrt{k}$. The new theorem isolates this term instead of hiding it
in an offdiagonal estimate.

The bulk premise is also necessary. At the actual vacuum and $k=1$,
the reference $\sigma=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1/4,1/4)$ gives depth
drift $a/2$, because an input color pushes and input zero performs the
seed exchange. The claimed bulk diagonal response of this reference
would be zero. Root control must therefore remain in the inherited
operator Lyapunov theorem.

The theorem is an exact signed-input response interface. It supplies no
new bath operation and does not itself prove positive input synthesis,
entropy gain or a communication rate. A receiving positive construction
must independently control its charge excess and its $\|\cdot\|_{2,\sigma}$
offdiagonal remainder, retain the physical root, and invoke the full
arbitrary-reference coding bridge. Spectator weights in the proof arise
only after coherent histories have been paired; they are not a classical
noise replacement for the device.


\section{Positive normalization with an exact charge budget}

\begin{lemma}[Normalization within complete charge patterns]\label{lem:gram}
Let \(X=X^*\), \([X,Q]=0\), and \(\pin(X)=0\). For \(C=I+X/2\),
\(D=\pin(C^2)\), and \(F=D^{-1/2}C^2D^{-1/2}\),
\[
 D=I+\pin(X^2)/4\ge I,\qquad F\ge0,\qquad [F,Q]=0,
 \qquad\pin(F)=I.
\]
For any faithful \(\sigma=f(Q)\) and positive charge-only \(T=T(Q)\)
with \(\Tr\sigma T=1\), the matrix \(\rho=\sigma TF\) is a density
and \(\pin(\rho)=\sigma T\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The pinching is a conditional expectation onto its block algebra.
Since \(D^{-1/2}\) belongs to that algebra, its bimodule identity gives
\[
 \pin(D^{-1/2}C^2D^{-1/2})
 =D^{-1/2}\pin(C^2)D^{-1/2}=I.
\]
Total charge commutes with every factor. Consequently \(F\) commutes
with \(\sigma\) and \(T\); positivity and normalization follow.
No commutation of \(D\) with \(H\) or \(X\) is assumed.
\end{proof}

The specific charge-only tilt is essential to the simple product form:
an arbitrary positive matrix in the pattern algebra need not commute with
\(F\). Complete-pattern normalization, not scalar trace normalization,
sets the diagonal budget exactly. Section~\ref{sec:rawsquare} proves an
actual counterexample to the scalar replacement.

\subsection{Uniform weighted remainder}

For the rest of the transfer assume
\begin{equation}\label{eq:window}
 k\ge2,\qquad 0<b\le1/1024,\qquad r=bk\le1/256.
\end{equation}
Write \(\wn{Z}^2=\Tr(\sigma Z^*Z)\),
\(u=\wn X=\gamma b\sqrt v\), and \(J=D^{-1/2}\).
All multipliers below commute with \(\sigma\). Thus weighted
Hilbert--Schmidt multiplication on either side is bounded by operator norm.
The moments give
\[
 0\le D-I\le3\gamma^2r^2I/4,\quad
 \norm J\le1,\quad\norm{J-I}\le3\gamma^2r^2/8,
 \quad u\le\gamma r/\sqrt8<r/8.
\]
Since \(JDJ=I\),
\[
 F-I=JXJ+\tfrac14J(X^2-\pin(X^2))J.
\]
Pinching is an orthogonal projection in this weighted norm. Therefore
\begin{align}
 e:=\wn{F-I-X}
 &\le2\norm{J-I}u+\tfrac14\wn{X^2-\pin(X^2)}\notag\\
 &\le\frac34\gamma^2r^2u+\frac{\sqrt{105}}4\gamma^2r^2
 \le3\gamma^2r^2.\label{eq:gramremainder}
\end{align}
The last inequality already holds for \(r\le1/16\), since
\(u<1/128\) and \(\sqrt{105}/4+3/512<3\).
Also, using \(v\ge k^2/32\) and \(\gamma<1/4\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:relativeerror}
 e/u\le3\gamma\sqrt{32}\,r\le5r.
\end{equation}

The tilt satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tiltsize}
 \eta:=\norm{T-I}=2mb^2=3\ell vb^2/16
 \le3\ell r^2/128<r^2/32.
\end{equation}
It is strictly positive. Lemma~\ref{lem:gram} yields normalization and
\[
 \Tr(A\rho)=\Tr(\sigma AT)=mb^2,
\]
because \(\Tr\sigma A=0\) and \(\Tr\sigma A^2=k/4\).

Define the complete offdiagonal correction to the signed comparison by
\[
 Y=T(F-I)-X.
\]
It is Hermitian and charge preserving, with \(\pin(Y)=0\).
Equations~\eqref{eq:gramremainder}--\eqref{eq:tiltsize} give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Ybudget}
 \wn Y\le(1+\eta)e+\eta u<4\gamma^2r^2.
\end{equation}
For an explicit check, after division by \(\gamma^2r^2\), the right side
is at most
\(3+3r^2/32+r/(32\gamma\sqrt8)<4\), using \(\gamma>1/8\)
and \(r\le1/16\). This controls every coherence created by the normalizer.

\section{Entropy and exact inward drift}

\subsection{Global entropy control}

Put \(\mathcal B=\ell vb^2\). Since \(\sigma\) and \(TF\) commute,
the scalar inequality \(z\ln z-z+1\le(z-1)^2\), with its continuous
value at zero, gives
\[
 D_2(\rho\Vert\sigma)\le\frac{\Tr\sigma(TF-I)^2}{\ell}.
\]
Here \(D_2\) is relative entropy with base-two logarithms. It is not a
small-operator-norm expansion. The diagonal \(T-I\) is orthogonal to
the offdiagonal \(T(F-I)\), so
\begin{align}
 \Tr\sigma(TF-I)^2
 &=\Tr\sigma(T-I)^2+\wn{T(F-I)}^2\notag\\
 &\le\frac{4m^2b^4}{k}+(1+\eta)^2(u+e)^2.\label{eq:entropyvariance}
\end{align}
At the full window~\eqref{eq:window},
\[
 (1+\eta)^2(1+5r)^2
 \le(1+2^{-21})^2(261/256)^2<17/16.
\]
Let \(u_0=vb^2/k\). Since \(u_0\le r^2/(8k)\), we have \(9u_0<1\).
Using \(u^2=\ell^2vb^2/16\) in \eqref{eq:entropyvariance},
\[
 D_2(\rho\Vert\sigma)
 \le\frac{17}{256}\mathcal B+\frac{9u_0}{256}\mathcal B

\section{Why ordinary trace-normalized squaring fails}\label{sec:rawsquare}

The complete-pattern normalizer is not an optional cosmetic change.
The following exact identity resolves a genuine obstruction in the
apparently simpler positive construction.

\begin{theorem}[Exact charge bias and raw-square outward drift]
\label{thm:rawsquare}
For the actual fixed \(H_k\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:chargeidentity}
 c_k:=\Tr(\sigma A H_k^2)
 =\frac18\sum_{j=1}^{k-2}(k-1-j)\E|S_j|
 =\frac{\sqrt{2/\pi}}{30}k^{5/2}+O(k^{3/2}).
\end{equation}
Fix any \(\gamma_0>0\), \(0<r_0\le1/2\), and consider the outer
parameter family \(b=r_0/k\), \(s=\gamma_0b\). The actual input
\[
 \widehat\rho_s=\frac{\sigma(I-sH)^2}{1+s^2v_k}
\]
has strictly uniform outward bulk drift for all sufficiently large \(k\).
Its drift lower bound diverges as a positive multiple of \(\sqrt k\).
This does not establish a capacity converse or a statement about Cycle9's
different positive-part density.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
We give the exact charge calculation first. Define
\(\sigma_p=\operatorname{diag}(1-p,p/2,p/2)^{\otimes k}\), keeping
the original \(H_k\) fixed. Distinct endpoint pairs occupy distinct
charge matrix blocks, so the cross terms in
\(v_k(p)=\Tr(\sigma_pH_k^2)\) vanish.
The inherited oriented color block is \(P(I-\SWAP),P/2,0\) when the
unmatched earlier push count \(h\) is greater than, equal to, or below
the middle deficit \(d\). Its normalized squared color trace is
respectively \(1,1/4,0\).

Concatenate the charge bits before and between the endpoints into a walk
of length \(n\le k-2\), with step \(+1\) for color charge. Let
\(f_n,l_n\) be its first and last global minimum times, including zero.
At a split \(i\), the exact relations are
\(h>d\iff i>l_n\) and \(h=d\iff f_n\le i\le l_n\).
Summing all splits and the two endpoint orientations gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tiltedmoment}
 v_k(p)=2p(1-p)\sum_{n=0}^{k-2}
 \left[n+\tfrac14-\tfrac34\E_p l_n-\tfrac14\E_p f_n\right].
\end{equation}

Here are elementary generating functions for the needed derivatives.
Let \(A_p(z)\) generate probabilities of walks whose every partial sum
is nonpositive, and let \(B_p(z)\) generate strictly positive walks,
including the empty path. For \(q=1-p\), the Catalan excursion series is
\[
 C_p(z)=\frac{2}{1+\sqrt{1-4pqz^2}},\qquad
 (1-z)A_p(z)=1-pzC_p(z).
\]
The second identity counts the first upward crossing from zero.
Decomposing any path at its last global minimum gives
\(A_pB_p=(1-z)^{-1}\) and
\[
 \sum_{n\ge0}\E_p l_nz^n
 =\frac{z}{1-z}\partial_z\log A_p(z).
\]
At the first global minimum the reversed prefix is strictly negative
and the suffix nonnegative, so
\[
 \sum_{n\ge0}\E_p f_nz^n
 =\frac{z}{1-z}\partial_z\log B_q(z).
\]
The derivative of \(C_p\) at \(p=1/2\) vanishes, and direct algebra gives
\[
 \left.\partial_p\log A_p(z)\right|_{1/2}
 =1-\frac{1+z}{\sqrt{1-z^2}}.
\]
The two minimum-time series therefore have the same derivative,
\(-z/[(1-z)^2\sqrt{1-z^2}]\). For the symmetric walk,
\(\E|S_{j+1}|-\E|S_j|=\Prob(S_j=0)\), giving
\[
 \sum_{j\ge0}\E|S_j|z^j=\frac{z}{(1-z)\sqrt{1-z^2}}.
\]
Comparison of formal coefficients proves
\[
 \left.\partial_p\E_p l_n\right|_{1/2}
 =\left.\partial_p\E_p f_n\right|_{1/2}
 =-\sum_{j=1}^n\E|S_j|.
\]
Since \(c_k=v'_k(1/2)/4\), differentiating
\eqref{eq:tiltedmoment} and reversing the finite double sum proves
\eqref{eq:chargeidentity}. The exact elementary values
\[
 \E|S_{2j}|=2j\binom{2j}{j}4^{-j},\qquad
 \E|S_{2j+1}|=(2j+1)\binom{2j}{j}4^{-j}
\]
give \(\E|S_j|=\sqrt{2j/\pi}+O(j^{-1/2})\). Summation yields the
stated asymptotic, since \(\int_0^1(1-x)\sqrt x\,dx=4/15\).

For the raw square the charge excess is exactly
\[
 \Tr(A\widehat\rho_s)=\frac{s^2c_k}{1+s^2v_k}.
\]
The fourth moment bounds its offdiagonal normalized relative density by
\[
 \left\|\frac{-2sH+s^2(H^2-\pin(H^2))}{1+s^2v_k}\right\|_{2,\sigma}
 \le\frac{|s|k}{\sqrt2}+\sqrt{105}(sk)^2.
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:hammingresponse} then gives the actual bulk operator bound
\[
 D_b(\widehat\rho_s)\ge
 \left[\frac{2a s^2c_k}{1+s^2v_k}
 -2r_0\left(\frac{\gamma_0r_0}{\sqrt2}
                  +\sqrt{105}\gamma_0^2r_0^2\right)\right]I.
\]
Its first term grows like a strictly positive multiple of \(\sqrt k\),
while the subtracted term is constant. This proves uniform outward drift.
\end{proof}

The conclusion rejects raw squaring as a replacement for the inward-drift
construction at fixed \(bk\). It proves neither escape from every normal
initializer nor impossibility of every code using that input. Each device
in this outer parameter family is still fixed before its own coding horizon.

\subsubsection{The weighted trace norm that makes the estimate uniform}

\begin{lemma}[Endpoint trace budget]\label{lem:trace}
For the actual inherited \(H\),
\[

 |P(I-\SWAP)|=I-\SWAP,\qquad

\[
 \norm{\sigma H_{ij}}_1
 =\Tr(\sigma|H_{ij}|)\le\frac12\frac12=\frac14.
\]

\[
 \norm{\Tr_A[(X\otimes I)B]}\le\norm X_1\norm B.
\]

The empty-mask certificate is
\(\sum_{i<j}A_{ij,\varnothing}=v_kI\), by \eqref{eq:scalar}.

\]
and, as a positive quadratic-form identity,
\[
 \Phi_\rho^*e^{tL}
 =\left(\sum_\Delta e^{t\Delta}A_\Delta\right)e^{tL}.
 \tag{C2}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Diagonalize $\rho$ in joint eigenvectors of $Q_k$, and take computational
output words $o$. Charge conservation gives each corresponding bath Kraus
operator degree $\Delta=q-N(o)$:
$[L,K]=\Delta K$. The exchange $R$ has degree zero and satisfies this
identity at the vacuum as well. Set $A_\Delta=\sum_{\deg K=\Delta}K^*K$.
Trace preservation proves their sum is $I$; homogeneity proves they commute
with $L$ and yields (C1)--(C2) on finite-coordinate vectors. Positive
quadratic forms and monotone limits extend the exponential identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform operator drift gives actual reliable codes]
\label{thm:operatorcode}
Fix $k$, $0<\beta<1$, and a charge-preserving density $\rho$.
Suppose its exact drift obeys
\[
 P_{\ge k}D_s(\rho)P_{\ge k}\le-\kappa P_{\ge k},
 \qquad0<\kappa\le k.
 \tag{C3}
\]
Then one deterministic unassisted code sequence, independent of the bath
initializer, has rate at least $S(\rho)/k$ and pointwise vanishing
full-diamond error for every fixed normal initializer.
For the actual $\tau_\pi$, the product-block coherent-information limit
is also $S(\rho)/k$. No scalar depth chain or radial state preservation
is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Set
\[
 t=\kappa/(2k^2),\quad
 \lambda=1-\kappa^2/(4k^2),\quad
 C=\frac{e^{2kt}}{1-\lambda}.
 \tag{C4}
\]
Since $kt\le1/2$, for $|\Delta|\le k$ Taylor's inequality gives
$e^{t\Delta}\le1+t\Delta+k^2t^2$.
Multiply by each positive effect in (C1) and sum. On the high-depth sector
this is at most $\lambda I$. The low-depth sector reaches depth at most
$2k-1$, so (C2) gives the global form inequality
\[
 \Phi_\rho^*e^{tL}
 \le\lambda e^{tL}+e^{2kt}P_{<k}.
 \tag{C5}
\]
There are no low/high cross terms. Iteration yields
\[
 M_m\le\lambda^m M_0+C(1-\lambda^m).
 \tag{C6}
\]
For any coherent initializer supported in $P_{\le A}$,
\[
 \Pr(L_m>H)\le(1+C)e^{-t(H-A)}.
 \tag{C7}
\]

Let $d=\rank\rho\le3^k$, using only positive eigenvalues. A
maximum-probability exact spectral type $T_m$ of $\rho^{\otimes m}$
has probability $w_m\ge(m+1)^{-d}$, rank $D_m$, and
\[
 T_m/D_m\le\rho^{\otimes m}/w_m,\qquad
 \log_2D_m/m\longrightarrow S(\rho).
 \tag{C8}
\]
For completeness, with type counts $n_i$, maximality against a transfer
from count $i$ to $j$ gives
$n_i/p_i\le(n_j+1)/p_j$. Summing against $p_i$ shows
$n_j\ge mp_j-1$, hence every frequency tends to $p_j$.
The type scalar eigenvalue and the polynomial mass give the rank limit.
This is an operator statement in the actual eigenbasis, even if eigenvectors
are entangled across calls.

Choose
\[
 A_m=\lfloor\log_2(m+1)\rfloor,\quad
 H_m=A_m+\left\lceil
 \frac{(d+16)\ln(m+1)+\ln(1+C)}t\right\rceil,\quad
 \epsilon_m=(m+1)^{-16}.
 \tag{C9}
\]
Apply (C7) to the uniform state on $P_{\le A_m}$, and then (C8).
Mean overflow for the type subspace and that initial bath space is at
most $\epsilon_m$. The inherited joint compression lemma, reproduced
in Appendix~\ref{app:compression}, gives
\[
 K_m\ge\left\lceil
 \frac{\lfloor D_m/2\rfloor}{(r_{A_m}r_{H_m})^4}
 \right\rceil,\quad
 e_m=r_{A_m}\sqrt{2\epsilon_m}+(1-2\epsilon_m)^{-1/2}-1\to0.
 \tag{C10}
\]
It retains spectral expurgation, the $r^2$ positive-block factor,
the auxiliary CPTP normalization and its polar repair.
The actual encoder is isometric; no auxiliary normalization is implemented.
For every fixed normal $\tau$, the full-diamond error is at most
\[
 \min\{2,\ 4e_m+2\sqrt{1-\Tr(P_{\le A_m}\tau)}\}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{C11}
\]
In half-diamond convention this is
$\min\{1,2e_m+\sqrt{1-\Tr(P_{\le A_m}\tau)}\}$.
This follows by comparing $\tau$ outside the type domination with its
normalized coordinate truncation. Thus no type-probability factor multiplies
an uncontrolled initial tail. Since $\log r_{A_m}r_{H_m}=O(\log m)$
for this fixed device and block, (C10) proves the rate.

For $\tau_\pi$, $t\le1/2<\ln3$ ensures a finite initial exponential
moment. Equations (C6)--(C7) bound mean depth uniformly. The bath Gibbs
density $g=4^{-L}/4$ has trace one and
$-\log_2g=2L+2$, so $S(E_m)\le2\E L_m+2$.
This entropy-energy inequality also follows by finite cutoffs and monotone
limits; finite mean guarantees finite entropy. The initial bath entropy
is finite. Unitary entropy conservation, subadditivity and Araki--Lieb give
\[
 mS(\rho)-2S(E_m)\le\Ic(\rho^{\otimes m},\mathcal N_{km}^{\tau_\pi})
 \le mS(\rho).
 \tag{C12}
\]
Divide by $km$. Extra data-independent calls may fill incomplete final
blocks; all count and do not alter already emitted output marginals.
\end{proof}

The initializer-independent code quantifier is pointwise in the normal initializer.
It is distinct from the inherited uniform all-normal restricted upper bound.
No invariant initializer is prepared. The boundary term in (C5) is essential:
a global strict exponential contraction would contradict trace preservation
and $e^{tL}\ge I$.


\section{The inherited joint compression operation}\label{app:compression}

This appendix restates the actual inherited proof \cite{Cycle2}, rather than
using a summary as a coding theorem. It records the normalization and coherent
initializer quantifiers needed by the new receiving use. The underlying
finite-error mechanism is established \cite{KLV,GBM,Tverberg}.

\begin{lemma}[Finite-error-set code]\label{lem:finitecode}
A CPTP map from a $D$-dimensional input space to a finite output space, with
at most $k$ Kraus operators, has an exact subspace code of dimension at least
$\lceil D/k^4\rceil$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The self-adjoint operator space spanned by $A_i^*A_j$ contains $I$ and has
complex dimension $d\le k^2$. Choose a Hermitian basis $I,H_2,\ldots,H_d$.
Greedily choose orthonormal vectors $v_1,\ldots,v_N$ with all cross matrix
elements of each $H_j$ zero: after $m$ choices avoid the span of at most
$md$ vectors $H_jv_a$. This yields $N\ge\lceil D/d\rceil$.
Their diagonal expectation vectors lie in $\mathbb R^{d-1}$. The affine
Tverberg theorem partitions any $(r-1)d+1$ such points into $r$ groups with
intersecting convex hulls. Choose $r=\lceil N/d\rceil$. Square roots of the
corresponding convex weights form orthonormal code vectors with identical
diagonal expectations and zero cross expectations. Thus
$C^*A_i^*A_jC=\gamma_{ij}I_r$ and $r\ge\lceil D/k^4\rceil$.

Choose syndrome vectors of Gram matrix $\gamma$ and send
$A_iC\ket\psi\mapsto\ket\psi\otimes\ket{\xi_i}$ on the error-image span.
The Gram identities make this map isometric; extend it with an external
syndrome space to a recovery isometry. All outcomes are recovered. Nothing
requires equal input and output dimensions.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Joint compression from averaged overflow]\label{lem:compression}
Let $W:A\otimes E_0\to B\otimes E$ be an isometry, with
$\dim A=D$, $\dim E_0=r<\infty$, and let $P$ be a rank-$d$ bath projection.
Define
\[
 F=W^*(I_B\otimes(I-P))W,\qquad L_A=\frac1r\Tr_{E_0}F.
\]
If $D^{-1}\Tr L_A\le\epsilon$, $0<\epsilon<1/2$, there is an isometric
encoder and deterministic decoder of dimension
\[
 K\ge\left\lceil\frac{\lfloor D/2\rfloor}{(rd)^4}\right\rceil
\]
whose recovered logical channel has full-diamond error at most $4e$ for
every initializer supported in $E_0$, provided $e<1$, where
\[
 e=r\sqrt{2\epsilon}+(1-2\epsilon)^{-1/2}-1.
\]
The same code is valid for coherent initializers and arbitrary external
references. More strongly its recovered joint isometry is within $2e$ of
$I_{\rm logical}\otimes Z_0$ for a bath/syndrome isometry $Z_0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $Q=1_{[0,2\epsilon]}(L_A)$. Eigenvalue counting gives
$\dim QA\ge D/2$ and $QL_AQ\le2\epsilon Q$.
For any positive operator $X$ on $A\otimes\mathbb C^r$,
\[
 X\le r(\Tr_{\mathbb C^r}X)\otimes I_r.
 \tag{A1}
\]
Indeed pinching into $r$ diagonal bath blocks gives
$X\le r\sum_v(I\otimes|v\rangle\langle v|)X(I\otimes|v\rangle\langle v|)$
by Cauchy--Schwarz, and each positive diagonal block is at most their sum.
For $F_Q=(Q\otimes I)F(Q\otimes I)$, (A1) implies
$F_Q\le r^2 QL_AQ\otimes I\le2r^2\epsilon I$. The discarded isometry
amplitude is therefore at most $r\sqrt{2\epsilon}$, uniformly on joint
inputs, including entangled references.

For bases $v$ of $E_0$ and $w$ of $PE$, put
\[
 B_{wv}=\frac1{\sqrt r}\bra w(I\otimes P)W\ket v\big|_{QA}.
\]
Then $\sum B_{wv}^*B_{wv}=I_{QA}-QL_AQ$. The auxiliary operator
$J=(I_{QA}-QL_AQ)^{-1/2}$ satisfies
$\norm{J-I}\le(1-2\epsilon)^{-1/2}-1$, and $B_{wv}J$ define a CPTP map
with at most $rd$ Kraus operators. Lemma~\ref{lem:finitecode} gives an
isometric code inclusion $C$ and recovery isometry $D_{\rm rec}$ with
$D_{\rm rec}B_{wv}JC\ket\psi=\ket\psi\otimes\ket{\xi_{wv}}$.
Consequently
\[
 (D_{\rm rec}\otimes I)(I\otimes P)W(JC\otimes I_{E_0})
 =I_{\rm logical}\otimes Z,\quad
 Z\ket v=\sqrt r\sum_w\ket{\xi_{wv}}\otimes\ket w.
 \tag{A2}
\]
This uses all initial and final bath indices, not one pure initial bath.

The actual encoder is $C$, never $JC$. Its recovered joint isometry
$H=(D_{\rm rec}\otimes I)W(C\otimes I)$ differs from $I\otimes Z$ by at
most $e$. Since $H$ is isometric, every singular value of $Z$ lies in
$[1-e,1+e]$. Its polar part $Z_0$ is therefore isometric and
$\norm{Z-Z_0}\le e$, giving $\norm{H-I\otimes Z_0}\le2e$.
Two isometries differing in norm by $u$ induce channels at full-diamond
distance at most $2u$: apply the pure-state trace-distance inequality after
tensoring a reference, then trace unused outputs. This proves $4e$, or $2e$
in half-diamond convention. The recovery syndrome is retained in the dilation;
discarding it to define a logical marginal is not an internal bath reset.
\end{proof}

The $r$ in (A1) cannot be removed: for the maximally entangled projector
$X=|\Omega_r\rangle\langle\Omega_r|$ one has $\norm X=1$ but
$(\Tr_E X)\otimes I=I/r$. Thus a bath-averaged effect of norm $1/r^2$ can
hide a joint failure of probability one. Likewise the projected channel is
not automatically trace preserving. The auxiliary $J$ and the polar repair
are mathematical comparisons; implementing $J$ as a deterministic encoder
or omitting its error would be invalid.
