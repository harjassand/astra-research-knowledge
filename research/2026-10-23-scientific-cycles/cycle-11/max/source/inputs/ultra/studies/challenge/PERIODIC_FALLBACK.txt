This is not the selected logarithmic-rate receiver. It preserves a simple
fully specified alternative and diagnoses why sparse single-site testing
alone loses too much. The operation uses the actual uniform K=1,...,L queue
Phi_D^K tensor nu^(L-K), nu=(I/D) tensor (I/D), and no knowledge of K.

Choose integer b with 1<=b<=L. Write L=m b+t, 0<=t<b. Sequentially test
positions b,2b,...,m b by local computational measurements and compare the
outcomes. Stop at the first mismatch j, retain every UNMEASURED site before
j b, and reset all other external sites. If none mismatches, retain every
unmeasured site in 1,...,L. The observable output length is q_j=j(b-1) on
a mismatch, or q_none=L-m. Retain all outcomes and stop records.

Conditional on K, no Bell site can mismatch. Hence no true Bell site is
discarded except a measured sentinel. EXACTLY
 B(K)=K-floor(K/b)
true Bell pairs survive. Every retained non-Bell pair is still exactly nu:
the source is a product across sites conditional on K, and tests act only
on removed sentinels. After local reordering, the remaining state conditional
on K and transcript is Phi_D^B(K) tensor nu^Q, Q=q-B(K)>=0.
This conditional statement does not assert that B(K) is observable.

Let G count matched tail sentinels before the first mismatch/end. Its tails
obey Pr(G>=g)<=D^(-g), and E G<=1/(D-1). With S=K mod b,
 Q <= (G+1)(b-1)-S <= (G+1)(b-1).
Thus E Q <=D(b-1)/(D-1). On a mismatch j, floor(K/b)=j-1-G; on no mismatch
floor(K/b)=m-G. Consequently K is determined by the stop record, G and S.
The maximum entropy of a nonnegative integer of mean <=1/(D-1) is the
geometric entropy
 h_geo(D)=D/(D-1) ln D-ln(D-1),
so H(K|transcript)<=ln b+h_geo(D). All measured symbol outcomes may be
included; conditioning on more classical information can only improve this
bound, and it does not change untouched sites conditional on K.

Bob's retained marginal conditional on every transcript is exactly maximally
mixed on the known q sites. Entropy of a mixture is at most the classical
mixing entropy plus mean conditional entropy. Therefore the actual flagged
coherent information, in nats per input queue, obeys

 I_c >= [E K-E floor(K/b)-D(b-1)/(D-1)] ln D-ln b-h_geo(D).

Here E K=(L+1)/2 and the floor expectation is exactly
 [b m(m-1)/2+m(t+1)]/L.
In particular E floor(K/b)<=(L+1)/(2b), so b approximately sqrt(L) gives
rate E K-O_D(sqrt L)-O_D(log L) D-level Bell pairs per queue. Actual iid

Cost: at most floor(L/b) local D-outcome measurements per party, a D-symbol

EXACT FINITE OUTPUT SPECTRUM (NO EXPONENTIAL DENSITY MATRIX)
For a fixed stop T with q retained sites and posterior p(K|T), write Y for
the full Bell-basis string of the q pairs and j its number of leading zero
Bell symbols (0<=j<=q). Every such string has identical eigenvalue

 lambda_j=sum_{K:B(K)<=j} p(K|T) D^(-2(q-B(K))).

There are (D^2-1)D^(2(q-j-1)) strings with j<q, and one with j=q. Hence
the exact entropy follows from q+1 spectral classes using rational lambda_j
and their integer multiplicities. No Bell-basis measurement is physically
performed or granted free to the receiver; this is a calculation of its
unconditional output spectrum. Conditioned on any equality event, both true
and tail measured symbols are uniform over the D equal pairs. Conditioned
on the final mismatch, symbols are uniform over the D(D-1) unequal pairs.
Thus the detailed measured values add no K information beyond T, and the
spectrum also describes conditioning on the complete transcript.
