SOURCE AND SCOPE
The governing input is the actual queue in the Cycle9 principal proof,
SHA256 a91d763e1526ffb934e5cfeb996c9bcb04bbdc624e2e4a93d175a51dfcad8bf2.
Let D,L>=2 and
 rho_L=(1/L) sum_(k=1)^L Phi_D^tensor k tensor nu^tensor(L-k),
 nu=(I_D/D) tensor(I_D/D), B=((L+1)/2)ln D.
The full original source retains the orthogonal current clocks |kk><kk| and
complete old memory nu^L, which receivers cannot access. The accessible source
is rho_L; r original queues have accessible state rho_L^tensor r. Initial
apparatus must tensor-factor from the COMPLETE source and is separable across
Alice/Bob in the zero-added-entanglement statement. Independent local work and
shared random coins are allowed. A K-independent auxiliary marginal alone is
insufficient. No mathematical Bell-basis witness is an allowed free operation.

The output considered here is a fixed maximally entangled state Phi_M in
designated accessible output registers of local dimension M, produced by an
arbitrary finite-round LOCC channel acting collectively on the r queues.
Every retained record, inaccessible clock, complete old memory and all failure
branches can be included in a stronger joint reference. We require only the
necessary marginal condition T(omega_AB,Phi_M)<=epsilon, where T=||.||_1/2.
Therefore every result below also applies whenever that full joint reference
has half-trace error <=epsilon. This necessity does not prove a sufficient
complete-joint output construction, which belongs to the principal owner.
Variable unbounded target dimension on rare branches is not this contract.

RESULT
Put q=D^-2 and, for j>=1,
 Z_j(q)=(1-q^j)/(1-q),
 mu_j(q)=q/(1-q)-j q^j/(1-q^j),
 h_j(q)=ln Z_j(q)-mu_j(q)ln q,
 p_j(q)=(1-q^j)/L for 1<=j<L,
 p_L(q)=(1-q^L)/(L(1-q)),
 C_L(q)=sum_(j=1)^L p_j(q) h_j(q),
 H_geo(q)=-ln(1-q)-[q/(1-q)]ln q,
 U_(D,L)=B-(1/2)ln L+(1/2)C_L(D^-2).
Then 0<=C_L(q)<=H_geo(q) and every such receiver obeys, for epsilon<1/2,

 (1-2epsilon)ln M <= r U_(D,L)+g(epsilon),                 (1)
 g(epsilon)=(1+epsilon)h(epsilon/(1+epsilon)),

where h is binary entropy, h(0)=h(1)=0. In particular the zero-added-aid
asymptotic pooled Bell rate R in nats per original queue, at fixed D,L and
vanishing global error, satisfies

 R <= U_(D,L) <= B-(1/2)ln L+(1/2)H_geo(D^-2).             (2)

The loss B-R is preparation-supply minus pooled Bell output rate. It is NOT
the Cycle9 pointwise per-queue loss K_a-N_a, and no multiplicative/additive
factor from that theorem is transferred here. Since C_L(D^-2)=O_D(1), the
pooled supply deficit is at least (1/2)ln L-O_D(1). This is positive and
consequential for large L but is an asymptotic-rate converse, not a constant-
epsilon strong converse. A factor (1-2epsilon) is explicit at finite error.

1. EXACT ACCESSIBLE ENTROPY
Use a generalized D-level Bell basis on each pair, labelled (phase,shift) in
Z_D^2, with (0,0) denoting Phi_D. Conditional on K=k, the first k labels are
zero and the remaining labels are independent uniform among D^2 possibilities.
Let Y be the complete label string and J its leading all-zero length. Then
J>=K>=1. Conditional on J=j (and, in fact, on the complete Y),

 P(K=k|Y)=D^(2k)/sum_(ell=1)^j D^(2ell), 1<=k<=j,
 P(J-K=g|J=j)=q^g/Z_j(q), 0<=g<=j-1.                    (3)

The distribution of J is p_j(q) above: for j<L, sum the geometric conditional
probabilities (1-q)q^(j-k)/L over k<=j; for j=L omit the final factor 1-q.
Consequently H(K|Y)=H(K|J)=C_L(q). The finite geometric sum and its derivative
give the displayed mean mu_j(q), hence h_j(q). The formula also gives

 h_j(q)=H_geo(q)+ln(1-q^j)
                    +[j q^j/(1-q^j)]ln q <=H_geo(q).     (4)

This proves the entropy bound with no state tomography or clock observation.
The joint classical entropy identity yields

 S(rho_L)=H(Y)=H(K)+H(Y|K)-H(K|Y)
          =ln L+2(L-E K)ln D-C_L(q).                      (5)

Here E K=(L+1)/2. Each Bell state and nu have uniform local marginals, so
rho_A=rho_B=I_(D^L)/D^L. Thus

 (1/2)I(A:B)_(rho_L)=L ln D-(1/2)S(rho_L)=U_(D,L).        (6)

For completeness C_L(q)->H_geo(q) as L->infinity. An explicit remainder is
0<=H_geo(q)-C_L(q)<=A(q)/L, with
 A(q)=q/(1-q)^2-q ln q/(1-q)^3.
Indeed (1-q^j)[H_geo-h_j]
 =-(1-q^j)ln(1-q^j)-j q^j ln q <=q^j-j q^j ln q.
The j=L term has the extra factor 1/(1-q); applying that factor also to all
other nonnegative terms and summing the infinite geometric series proves it.

2. KNOWN MONOTONE AND A SELF-CONTAINED FINITE-ERROR REDUCTION
Define squashed entanglement E_sq(rho_AB)=(1/2)inf_E I(A:B|E), the infimum
over extensions rho_ABE. Its LOCC monotonicity, normalization on Phi_M and
additivity were proved by Christandl and Winter; the primary source is below.
The trivial extension gives E_sq(rho_L)<=I(A:B)/2=U_(D,L).
Product extensions give E_sq(rho_L^r)<=r U_(D,L). Independent separable
apparatus adds zero, by conditioning on a separable-ensemble label.
The minimizing extension is a mathematical variational device, not a demand
to erase the actual clock. Since the real receiver acts only on the accessible
input, its output marginal is the same channel applied to rho_L^r irrespective
of which inaccessible extension is used in the monotone proof. The true
clock/memory remain retained in the operational joint-error contract.

The operational ingredients needed here have a short independent proof.
Local channels cannot increase conditional mutual information. For a local
instrument producing a classical record C, use the chain rule
 I(AC:B|E)=I(C:B|E)+I(A:B|EC)>=I(A:B|EC).
Copy C to Bob and the extension E; conditioned on C this is the same last
quantity. Repeating over the finite-round protocol proves LOCC monotonicity
for the present use, including every transcript branch. Discarding residual
local registers also cannot increase the optimized quantity. Hence
 E_sq(omega_AB)<=r U_(D,L).

For every extension omega_ABE, weak monotonicity gives
 S(A|E)>=-S(A|B), and strong subadditivity gives
 S(A|BE)<=S(A|B). Therefore

 I(A:B|E)=S(A|E)-S(A|BE)>=-2S(A|B),
 E_sq(omega_AB)>=-S(A|B)_omega.                            (7)

We need only elementary conditional-entropy continuity at Phi_M, not a
continuity claim for the optimized measure. For two states x,y with
delta=T(x,y), write x-y=delta(tau_+-tau_-) using normalized positive/negative
parts, and
 z=(x+delta tau_-)/(1+delta)=(y+delta tau_+)/(1+delta).
Conditional entropy of a binary mixture is between its average component
conditional entropy and that average plus the binary mixing entropy. This
follows by adjoining its classical mixture flag and applying SSA, together
with 0<=I(A:C|B)<=H(C) when C is classical. Comparing the two mixtures, and
using -ln M<=S(A|B)_tau<=ln M, proves

 |S(A|B)_x-S(A|B)_y|<=2delta ln M+g(delta).                (8)

The right side is increasing in delta>=0. Since S(A|B)_(Phi_M)=-ln M,
(7)--(8) give E_sq(omega)>=(1-2epsilon)ln M-g(epsilon), proving (1).
Divide by r for a sequence epsilon_r->0 and take limsup to get (2). This
also proves directly why the standard distillable-entanglement upper bound
is valid under the fixed-output marginal contract. Monotone bookkeeping
never conditions the receiver on inaccessible source K.

3. FAILED BRIDGE: THE PROPOSED SEPARABLE COMPARATOR LOSES ONLY A CONSTANT
Let chi_D=(1/D)sum_i |ii><ii| and
 zeta_L=(1/L)sum_k chi_D^k tensor nu^(L-k).
This is separable. It is exactly complete computational dephasing on Alice
applied to rho_L, and is diagonal in the same generalized Bell basis. Its
Bell labels have zero shift in each prefix, with phase independent uniform.
Let H be the leading zero-shift length in a Bell string sampled from rho_L.
The same calculation as (3), with q=D^-1, gives
 H(K|complete shift string)=C_L(D^-1).
The dephased entropy identity gives the exact relative entropy

 D(rho_L||zeta_L)=S(zeta_L)-S(rho_L)
                =B-C_L(D^-1)+C_L(D^-2).                  (9)

Equivalently the log likelihood ratio for a string with leading zero Bell
length J and leading zero shift length H is
 ln[sum_(k=1)^J D^(2k)]-ln[sum_(k=1)^H D^k].
The factors 1/L cancel exactly. Because full Bell labels include all shifts,
C_L(D^-1)>=C_L(D^-2), as also follows from data processing for K. As L grows,

 D(rho_L||zeta_L)=B-c_D+O_D(1/L),
 c_D=H_geo(D^-1)-H_geo(D^-2)
    =ln(D+1)-[D/(D+1)]ln D >0.                            (10)

Thus this explicit separable relative-entropy comparison does not establish
a logarithmic deficit. It is weaker than (2) for sufficiently large L.
This failure is preserved because it changes which known monotone supplies
the useful structural obstruction. No optimality of zeta_L for E_R is claimed.

4. COST, STATUS, GAP
The proof requires no receiver measurement of Y,J,H,K. Their distributions
are mathematical spectral calculations on the exact supplied finite model.
Numerical evaluation of C_L uses O(L) scalar arithmetic, O(1) working storage;
the exact rational replay deliberately retains O(L) lists for comparison.
For fixed D the rational bit lengths are O(L), and naive exact arithmetic is
polynomial, not an advertised fast quantum simulation. Small replay cases
enumerate all (D^2)^L Bell strings as independent admission checks only.
No state preparation, hardware or paid jobs are performed. Computational
evaluation of the entropy upper bound is not a distillation implementation.

Internal proof: complete within the finite-dimensional default contract.
Historical novelty: UNKNOWN; the useful machinery is standard squashed
entanglement, conditional entropy and classical geometric posteriors.
Physical usefulness and efficient pooled receiver: not established here.
Scientific consequence: even collective pooling cannot asymptotically recover
the full preparation supply B; missing accessible inventory costs at least
(1/2)ln L-O_D(1) nats per queue in this fixed-Bell interface. The best possible
coefficient of ln L, and finite-error strong converse, are not proved.
The principal owner's receiving construction may match the order of the
deficit; it must separately certify the complete joint reference and costs.
