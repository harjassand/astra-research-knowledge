## 1. Pro1: preserve credible multi-lemma investigations; do not infer a universal method

**The evaluator's proposed conclusion is defensible with a route-specific qualification.** The accepted Pro1 construction requires both a feasible normalized input and control of its complete operator response. The complete-pattern congruence normalization fixes charge expenditure exactly; the all-coherence response theorem controls the nonlinear remainder in the relevant weighted norm. Their composition gives the new quadratic attainable floor under unchanged channel and coding definitions. The source explicitly states that evaluator representation studies were not premises (`inputs/pro1/Astra_Amplification_Cycle10_PRO1_20261023/RECEIVING_USE.md`).

The negative counterfactuals have different strength:

- For the specified raw trace-normalized square at fixed positive `bk`, an exact charge moment and the new response bound prove eventual outward bulk drift. That actually excludes this state family as the proposed strict inward-drift input. It does not exclude every positive input, every quantum code, or the distinct clipped family.
- Keeping only the previously offered generic trace-norm estimate leaves an unacceptable factor of `k` in this sufficient certificate. That is failure of the supplied bound, not a theorem that every alternative response argument fails. A strong conventional investigator may derive a sharper state-specific bound, choose another input or use another coding argument.

Accordingly, it is reasonable **not to terminate a multi-lemma investigation merely because neither partial ingredient independently closes its end-to-end objective**, when the owner has a concrete compatible composition and a consequential target. This protects a demonstrated form of scientific complementarity.

It does not show that all important discoveries require two ingredients, that the evaluator should prescribe two-factor decompositions, or that parallel allocation caused the result. A single researcher could discover both, another proof could avoid either, and an unproductive programme can also contain indefinitely promising complementary gaps. Neither a route-level necessity analysis nor a retrospective sequence of successful lemmas measures the counterfactual research cost. Preserve the distinction between a credible unfinished mathematical bridge and an unlimited justification for continued expenditure.

No causal allocator claim survives: the archive contains no matched allocation control, and it explicitly attributes no benefit to evaluator prompting, subagent count or reset strategy. The strongest warranted lesson is to preserve compatible scientific capital and avoid one-component success gates that would reject the actual multi-lemma proof before its scientific composition can be assessed.

## 2. Dots: the narrow product-per-dose claim does not require all gas species

### Logical identification

Define a finite treatment operation with a specified system boundary and interval. Let `P` be the independently measured **net amount of nitrate nitrogen derived from the specified original feed**, retained in a declared product compartment or collected in a declared outlet by the endpoint. Let `Q>0` be the calibrated photon dose delivered at a stated optical boundary during the relevant operation. For the claim

`Y_product,delivered = P / Q`,

measurements that identify `P` and `Q` identify the ratio even if some gas nitrogen species and intermediate pathways remain unknown. Two admissible physical histories with equal `P,Q` but different unmeasured gas partitions give the same value of this estimand. Thus a demand for **all gas-species measurements** is not a logically necessary prerequisite for this narrow claim.

This is an accounting/identifiability statement, not a new experimental result or new chemical law. It supports releasing an unnecessary gate only after the narrow claim and its premises are explicit. It does not make the currently available Dots observations satisfy those premises.

### Hidden requirements that cannot be dropped

1. **Absolute amount, not a normalized curve or concentration alone.** Nitrate amount requires calibrated response and volume/flow/recovery accounting, with the relevant matrix effects, limits and uncertainty. Evaporation or sampling can alter concentration without altering product moles. A separately validated inventory method can replace volume measurement; no particular instrument is compulsory.
2. **Origin and baseline.** Initial nitrate, imported nitrate, other nitrogen pools and background contamination cannot silently enter the numerator. The originating feed and allowed nitrogen inputs must be defined. Isotopic provenance or validated exclusion/control of alternative sources can identify original-N product, but merely saying “isotope” is not a certificate: enrichment, isotope recovery, fractionation or exchange assumptions must be adequate for the exact inference. Distinct original nitrite-to-nitrate conversion must not be relabelled new atmospheric nitrogen fixation.
3. **Net retained product at a specified endpoint.** Counting repeated creation events or cumulative positive nitrate increases can double-count molecules later destroyed or recycled. State whether `P` is endpoint inventory, collected export, isolated recovered nitrate, or gross formation. Include samples or exports consistently. A transient in-droplet amount is not automatically isolated product or stable final quality.
4. **One denominator boundary.** Delivered sample photons differ from absorbed photons, photons generated by the source, electrical input, and accumulated optical path encounters. Count all relevant illuminating beams, pulses, trap and probe exposure within the declared boundary. For a spectrum, the photon dose requires the appropriate wavelength conversion, not total joules at an arbitrary single photon energy. Recycling needs a stated crossing convention so the same supplied photon is not counted inconsistently.
5. **Matched time and process history.** Pair product and dose on the same operation, with specified illumination duration, sampling, subsequent dark reaction, residence and quench. Post-light conversion can count if the declared operation includes it; it is not instantaneous photochemical yield. Batch endpoint yield needs no gratuitous complete periodic reset, but sustained throughput and cyclic performance require their own inventory/reset accounting.
6. **Causal wording and other resources.** A product-per-delivered-photon ratio for an illuminated process can be measured without identifying mechanism. Calling it a light-caused increment requires an appropriate counterfactual/background control. Calling it intrinsic or absorbed quantum yield requires a different denominator and often additional evidence. A ratio alone does not price peroxide, oxygen, sacrificial reagents, heating, capture or product isolation; those matter for claims about efficiency or superiority of the complete treatment.
7. **Comparison and uncertainty.** To beat another process, both must satisfy the same product quality, original-N definition, starting conditions and relevant resource contract. Product and dose uncertainties must support the claimed ordering. A lower bound on one process's component cost and another lower bound cannot establish which complete process is superior.

The existing Dots source itself distinguishes fitted primary events from net disappearance and net nitrate production (`photon_independent/matched_photon_proof.md`, §§5–6). The current new preprint has normalized nitrate curves and unresolved other-phase transfer; the inspected older datasets do not independently establish absolute original-N closure. Releasing a universal gas-speciation gate therefore does **not** admit an empirical nitrate-per-photon advantage from the current record.

### Broader claims need matching observations, not one universal maximal closure

An actual full nitrogen-retention fraction needs the appropriate initial and retained total original-N inventories. It need not always require resolving **every** gas species individually: validated aggregate total-N measurements or a justified residual bound may suffice for a total-inventory claim. Conversely, an emission limit for a specified species requires information that bounds that species; low nitrate loss is not a safe-exhaust certificate. Product selectivity relative to nitrite loss requires an independently established conversion denominator, while the direct product/dose ratio does not. A complete plant advantage adds feed acquisition, all energy/reagent inputs, separations, recovery, waste and repeatability or reset under the actual system conditions.

The warranted release is therefore **claim-specific evidence sufficiency**, not “closure no longer matters.” The current archive's full-retention/plant caution remains valid. Its continuation language should avoid making the strongest desired plant-level closure a prerequisite for every independently useful narrower measurement.

## 3. Can an auxiliary proof obligation become the mistaken objective?

**Yes, but this diagnosis must itself be constrained.** The artifacts contain concrete examples:

- Pro2's original target is stable recovery, not star coloring. Universal star-four gluing is false, but paid short-arm nonstars satisfy the recovery target. Treating stars as the objective would discard a valid capability. Current `inputs/pro2/.../PROOF.md`, §§1–8, and the actual Cycle9 star-four counterexample make this distinction precise.
- Pro1 need not settle the older clipped family's fixed-`bk` question to prove a stronger capacity lower with a different admissible input. The original unresolved question stays unresolved; the capacity goal is advanced by a distinct valid route (`RESULTS.txt` §§3–4; `RECEIVING_USE.md`).
- Dots need not identify all gas species to measure a directly identified product/dose ratio. It still needs matching closure for its broader retention or plant claims. The measurement objective must be named before its evidence requirements can be simplified.

There are three important challenges to a general narrative of “removing unnecessary proof obligations.”

**First, a difficult obligation may be essential to the actual promised use.** Complete quantum output/reference error, the original-action recovery contract, original-N provenance, and complete system reset when claiming cyclic power are scientific requirements. They cannot be discarded because a smaller claim is easier to prove. Pro3's full-law returns and native row budgets similarly cannot be replaced by a convenient killed-control model without a valid bridge.

**Second, changing the task is sometimes a legitimate new investigation rather than revealing a mistaken old gate.** A useful pooled Bell output and a per-queue attributed output are different tasks. Likewise a product/dose endpoint and a full-retention process are different claims. The former may be worth studying, but they do not retrospectively satisfy or refute the latter. The analysis must state whether it preserved the original end use or selected a different one.

**Third, releasing a gate can decrease significance.** Showing that a narrower quantity is identifiable may save evidence acquisition, yet contribute little to the long-term scientific objective. A narrower measurement is worth pursuing when it changes an independently meaningful decision or enables a next scientific step. It should not become a method of manufacturing progress by successively weakening the goal.

The safe meta conclusion is therefore: distinguish the intended scientific claim from a sufficient proof route; allow the owner to replace the route while preserving the claim, or explicitly choose and justify a different consequential claim. Preserve the old result and limitations. This is supported by the current source history, but it is not yet evidence of a novel universal discovery method or measured intelligence amplification.

## 4. Suggested wording for the evaluator

“The new records show why a failed intermediate method should not close a scientific mission. Pro1 required complementary advances in an admissible input and its response analysis; Pro2 replaced an unnecessarily strong star-color requirement while preserving stable recovery. These are specific constructive successes, not proof of a general two-factor research method or a causal advantage of our allocations. For Dots, a narrowly defined original-N nitrate amount per delivered photon can in principle be measured without resolving every gas species, provided product origin, absolute amount, dose boundary and time history are independently controlled. The present datasets do not yet establish that ratio or a practical advantage. Broader retention, emissions and plant claims still require their own matching evidence. We should remove only gates that are unnecessary for a meaningful stated claim, rather than weaken the claim until a result can be reported.”
