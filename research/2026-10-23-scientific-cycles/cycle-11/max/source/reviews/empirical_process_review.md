### 2.2 The actual finite result

Within the stated straight-pass Gaussian model, the intensity-weighted aqueous path obeys `ell_G <= 2R`. With `b = epsilon * phi`, initial concentration `C`, delivered power `P` and molar photon energy `e_gamma`, the effective-event rate satisfies

`r <= (P/e_gamma) ln(10) b C (2R)`.

Since initial inventory is proportional to `R^3`, the ideal fractional rate is proportional to `P/R^2`, while absolute molar rate is proportional to `P R`. Halving the radius at fixed power and concentration therefore gives a fourfold faster fractional trace and half the initial molar rate. The ordinary comparator in the proof has the **same inventory, concentration, delivered power and path `2R`**; its fully captured ideal beam yields the same model rate. The construction is an admissible model comparator, not a validated installed micrometre reactor. A centimetre path cannot be granted for free while retaining the same tiny beam/inventory contract. [Sources: `photon_independent/matched_photon_proof.md`, sections 1–4; `calculations/photon_ledger.py`.]

For the declared, not measured, `R=30 micrometres`, `C=2.5 M`, `P=0.1 W`, 532 nm fixture, the stored output gives an effective-event floor of `1.881624486 x 10^12 J/mol`. The bound is useful for rejecting literal single-pass replication under the model, but **it is not automatically a nitrate energy floor**. If `b` identifies primary ionization, net recovered nitrate per initiation requires an independent bound. Only under the separate net-disappearance interpretation plus corrected original-N conservation and recovery can nitrate be bounded by net nitrite loss. The independent proof caught precisely this attempted promotion. [Sources: Dots `CORRECTIONS_AND_FAILURES.txt` item 5; proof sections 5–8; actual calculation inputs/output.]

The second finite calculation is a necessary instantaneous sink constraint. At the supplied dissolved oxygen and coefficients, oxygen electron capture is at most

`a/[a + k2*c_nitrite + k3*c_nitrate]`.

At total concentration 1 mM it cannot be a strict majority once nitrate fraction reaches `0.207142857`; maintaining the *possibility* of strict majority through 90% nitrate requires concentration below `0.575492 mM`. Equality is only a tie. Passing this condition is not yield, retention, transport, reactor-time or solar-performance evidence. The independent reader corrected a strict-inequality wording error without changing the numerical coefficients. [Sources: `calculations/electron_competition.py/.json`; `photon_independent/electron_competition_review.md`.]

## 6. One consequential meta-research opportunity

### Test the invention of a discriminating observation, not another prescribed reasoning style

The promising capability is to formulate a new observable or feasible perturbation whose outcome separates consequential scientific alternatives, then make a correct prospective prediction on independent evidence. This could improve discovery by changing what becomes knowable, rather than improving accuracy on the same underidentified model.

Dots supplies an instructive retrospective case: power dependence and optical inventory matter; fractional kinetics alone do not decide useful photon economy. This is **not** a fresh testbed on which to claim superiority, because the answer has already been exposed. It can seed a method, but testing that method here would be saturated and contaminated.

A credible next test should use one genuinely unresolved obligation independently selected by a scientific owner. Freeze the starting evidence, competing strong scientific explanations and actual resource/product target. Have the proposed meta method select a discriminating existing dataset, observable or admissible perturbation, and derive a quantitative consequence or a justified nonidentifiability result. The **strong conventional control** is an unconstrained domain researcher who receives the same evidence, resources and access, may reformulate the question, perform standard optimal-design/identifiability analysis, choose an independent source and abstain. It must not be a passive fit of the incumbent model.

Validation must come from an independent held-out intervention/measurement or already-authorized owner calculation whose result was not used to invent the prediction. Both arms receive equal access to final validation and all costs, failed searches, ambiguous conclusions and integration are retained. A proposed new representation only wins if it enables a correct decision the strong ordinary method cannot reach at comparable total effort, or makes a more consequential scientific question answerable. A researcher who merely notices the already published power dependence does not establish a new invention capability.

For repeated use, the acquired object would be a concrete scientific mapping or measurement construction with explicit invariants and failure cases, not an instruction to “think counterfactually.” A later owner must actually use it to obtain an independently checked improvement. Only subsequent generations could test compounding.

**Failure condition:** the conventional investigator selects the same observation with comparable cost; the supposed discriminator depends on unknown transfer conditions; the information does not exist at an allowed source; validation only rewards qualitative wording; or the observation changes a proxy but not the scientific decision. If no independent validation is accessible, do not spend a full research cycle pretending another agent response is an oracle. The correct action is either a narrower legitimate theorem or a different owner-chosen evidence opportunity.

This is a substantive scientific experiment about acquiring information, not a dashboard or standing evaluation platform. It does not centrally choose a Pro's scientific programme and does not require paid work, equipment or outreach. No superiority is established by this review, and the conventional methodology is strong enough that the proposal may fail. That is a preferable test to another tiny solved scalar exercise.
