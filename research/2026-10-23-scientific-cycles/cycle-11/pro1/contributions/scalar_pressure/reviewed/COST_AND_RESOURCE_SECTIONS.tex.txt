\section{Finite-error quantum communication and charged bath depth}

Consider an arbitrary deterministic fixed-\(N\) unassisted code, with a
CPTP encoder from a \(K\)-dimensional logical system to \(N\) qutrit
inputs, and a CPTP decoder on the emitted outputs. The encoder may use
local retained systems, but supplies no system correlated initially
with the bath and has no receiver-to-sender feedback. Such a protocol
can be represented by one correlated input state on all \(N\) slots.
Receiver processing may be deferred to the decoder.

Let \(\rho_N\) denote the physical input when the logical system is
maximally mixed. Let the full-diamond error from the logical identity
be at most \(\epsilon_N\le1\). Its half-diamond value is
\(\epsilon_N/2\).

\begin{lemma}[Reliable transmission requires input entropy]
\label{lem:codeentropy}
For the above code,
\[
 S_2(\rho_N)\ge
 (1-\epsilon_N)\log_2K-h_2(\epsilon_N/2).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use half of a maximally entangled logical state, with untouched reference
\(R\), as input. The decoded state \(\omega_{R\widehat R}\) has half
trace distance at most \(\delta_e=\epsilon_N/2\) from that pure state.
Its overlap is at least \(1-\delta_e\). Pinching into the target ray
and its orthogonal complement increases entropy, whence, for
\(\delta_e\le1/2\),
\[
 S_2(R\widehat R)\le
 h_2(\delta_e)+\delta_e\log_2(K^2-1)
 \le h_2(\delta_e)+2\delta_e\log_2K.
\]
The case \(K=1\) is trivial. The reference marginal is exactly uniform.
Araki--Lieb gives
\[
 I(R:\widehat R)\ge2\log_2K-2S_2(R\widehat R).
\]
Because the bath is initially independent and there is no feedback,
data processing and \(I(R:A^N)\le2S_2(A^N)\) give
\(I(R:\widehat R)\le2S_2(\rho_N)\).
Combining these statements proves the lemma. It allows a general CPTP
encoder; no isometric-encoder assumption is hidden in the upper bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Rate--depth expenditure]\label{thm:codecost}
Every such code obeys the whole-bath inequality
\begin{equation}\label{eq:codeoperator}
 D_b(\rho_N)\ge
 2[(1-\epsilon_N)\log_2K-h_2(\epsilon_N/2)-Nc(b)]I_E.
\end{equation}
For a bath initializer with finite mean depth, and in particular
\(\tau_\pi\), this implies
\begin{align}
 (1-\epsilon_N)\frac{\log_2K}{N}
 &\le c(b)+\frac{\E L_{\rm final}-\E L_{\rm initial}}{2N}
      +\frac{h_2(\epsilon_N/2)}N. \label{eq:finiteratecost}
\end{align}
For reliable rate \(R\), a rate exceeding \(c(b)\) requires asymptotic
mean-depth expenditure at least \(2(R-c(b))\) per forward call.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Combine Lemma~\ref{lem:codeentropy} and
Theorem~\ref{thm:entropyoperator} with \(k=N\).
Conservation gives the quadratic-form inequalities
\[
 L-NI\le W_N^*LW_N\le L+NI.
\]
They follow first on the finite-coordinate core and then by positive
form approximation. Thus finite initial mean gives finite final mean
and the bounded displacement expectation equals their difference.
For an infinite-mean normal initializer, \eqref{eq:codeoperator}
remains valid; \eqref{eq:finiteratecost} is not written as an undefined
infinity minus infinity.
\end{proof}

The full-error and half-error conventions are not interchangeable:
if the error is reported as half diamond \(\delta_e\), the first factor
in \eqref{eq:finiteratecost} is \(1-2\delta_e\), and the entropy term is
\(h_2(\delta_e)\).

The general tilt gives the stronger envelope, whenever a reliable code
has limiting depth expenditure at most \(v\ge0\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:costcurve}
 R\le C_b(v):=\inf_{\lambda\ge0}
 \left\{\log_2[3b+a(e^\lambda+2e^{-\lambda})]
             +\frac{\lambda v}{\ell}\right\}.
\end{equation}
This is an upper envelope, not an asserted achievable tradeoff.
At \(v=0\) it is exactly \(c(b)\).
For \(0\le v<a/3\) and \(a>0\), its minimizing \(z=e^\lambda\) is
\[
 z=\frac{\sqrt{9b^2v^2+8a^2(1-v^2)}-3bv}{2a(1+v)}.
\]
For \(v\ge a/3\), the minimum is the port-dimension bound \(\log_2 3\)
at \(\lambda=0\). Differentiation of the strictly convex
log-sum-exponential verifies these statements. At \(b=0\),
\(C_0(v)=h_2((1+v)/2)+(1+v)/2\) for \(0\le v\le1/3\),
recovering the ordinary diagonal charge-entropy comparator.

\section{A matching operational order with sublinear depth cost}

\begin{definition}[Sublinear-depth communication]
Let \(\Qcap_{\rm sub}(\beta,\tau_\pi)\) be the supremum of reliable
unassisted rates for the unchanged fixed device and actual initializer,
in full diamond norm, with final mean bath depth \(o(N)\) on the encoded
maximally mixed logical input. All \(N\) forward slots count.
This is an explicitly additional resource constraint on the original
operation, not a redefinition of its unrestricted capacity.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Sharp small-angle order for this resource-constrained capacity]
\label{thm:sublinear}
For every fixed \(0<\beta\le1/32\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sublinearcapacity}
 \boxed{\frac32+\frac{\ell}{2^{20}}\beta^2
 \ \le\ \Qcap_{\rm sub}(\beta,\tau_\pi)
 \ \le\ \frac32+\log_2(1+d_*\beta^2).}
\end{equation}
The same lower code is initializer independent in choice and has
pointwise vanishing full-diamond error for every fixed normal initializer.
At \(\tau_\pi\) its mean-depth bound is in fact \(O_\beta(\log N)\),
uniformly over all logical inputs and retained references.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The upper is Theorem~\ref{thm:codecost}. The lower rate and full-diamond
quantifiers are the completed Cycle10 theorem, whose complete actual
proof is preserved. Its physical encoder is the isometry \(C_n\)
from joint compression, not its auxiliary normalization.
The new resource step is Lemma~\ref{lem:code-depth} below: it proves
that this same code belongs to the new resource class. No extra bath
operation is added. Inner \(\beta,k,\rho\) remain fixed before the outer
coding horizon. Since \(d_*>0\), \eqref{eq:csmall} gives the stated
matching \(\Theta(\beta^2)\) order.
\end{proof}

For clarity, the precise retained lower input is
\[
 k=\left\lfloor\frac1{256b}\right\rfloor,\quad
 \sigma=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1/4,1/4)^{\otimes k},\quad
 A=Q_k-k/2,\quad v_k=\Tr\sigma H_k^2.
\]
With \(\gamma=\ell/4\), \(m=3\ell v_k/32\), it is
\[
 X=-\gamma bH_k,\quad C=I+X/2,\quad
 D=\pin(C^2),\quad F=D^{-1/2}C^2D^{-1/2},\quad
 T=I+(4m/k)b^2A,\quad \rho=\sigma TF.
\]
The inherited proof establishes positivity, \(\pin(F)=I\), exact charge,
and the two full bounds
\[
 S_2(\rho)-3k/2\ge\ell v_kb^2/64,\qquad
 P_{\ge k}D_b(\rho)P_{\ge k}\le
 -\frac{a\ell v_kb^2}{128}P_{\ge k}.
\]
No new claim of efficient preparation follows. The following resource
proof uses the actual inherited encoder and cutoff, including the
\(r^2\) positivity factor, auxiliary CPTP normalization, and polar repair.
