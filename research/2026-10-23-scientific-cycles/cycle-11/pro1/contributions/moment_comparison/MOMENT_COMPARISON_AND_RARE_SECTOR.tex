\section{A complete trace comparison and its information limit}
\label{sec:moment-comparison}

We use the actual Cycle10 block estimate, not a finite spectral fit.
Let the input decompose as $\mathcal H=\bigoplus_x\mathcal H_x$ over
binary charge patterns $x\in\{0,1\}^k$. For the actual qutrit input,
$\dim\mathcal H_x=2^{|x|}$ and
\[
 \sigma|_{\mathcal H_x}=2^{-k-|x|}I_{\mathcal H_x},\qquad
 \operatorname{Tr}(\sigma P_x)=2^{-k}.
\]
The different dimensions of the color fibers are not discarded.

\begin{lemma}[Closed-walk trace comparison]\label{lem:closedwalk}
Suppose $K=K^*$, $P_xKP_x=0$, and
$\|P_yKP_x\|\le M_{yx}$ for a symmetric, entrywise nonnegative scalar
matrix $M$ with zero diagonal. If $\omega=\sum_xP_x\omega P_x$ is a
density and $\pi_x=\operatorname{Tr}(\omega P_x)$, then for every
integer $p\ge1$,
\[
 \left|\operatorname{Tr}(\omega K^p)\right|
 \le\sum_x\pi_x(M^p)_{xx}.
\]
Consequently, for real $t$,
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(\omega e^{tK})
 \le\sum_x\pi_x(e^{|t|M})_{xx}.
\]
For the physical reference $\sigma$, the right sides are respectively
$2^{-k}\operatorname{Tr}M^p$ and $2^{-k}\operatorname{Tr}e^{|t|M}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Expand the trace into closed pattern walks $x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_p=x_0$.
Every summand satisfies
\[
 \left|\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathcal H_{x_0}}
   (\omega_{x_0}K_{x_0x_{p-1}}\cdots K_{x_1x_0})\right|
 \le\pi_{x_0}\prod_{j=1}^p M_{x_jx_{j-1}}.
\]
This uses $|\operatorname{Tr}(\omega_{x_0}B)|
\le\pi_{x_0}\|B\|$, so no common color-fiber dimension is assumed.
Sum the bounds and then the absolutely convergent exponential series.
Its first-order term vanishes on both sides. This is a trace inequality,
not an operator order comparison between $K$ and $M$.
\end{proof}

For every positive unit-trace analytic prefix density $\zeta$, contraction
of the actual bulk operator is a unital completely positive map. It
preserves the pattern blocks and contracts their operator norms. Thus the
actual $K_\zeta$ obeys this lemma with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scalar-majorant}
 M_{yx}=\begin{cases}
 m\binom{2m}{m}(ab)^m,
 &|x|=|y|,\quad |x\mathbin\triangle y|=2m>0,\\
 0,&\text{otherwise},
 \end{cases}\qquad a=1-b.
\end{equation}
This comparison is uniform over all coherent or entangled prefix
densities. The prefix remains an analytic contraction, not a physical
initialization resource.

\subsection{Why the absolute scalar majorant is not the desired pressure bound}

Fix $r>0$, take even $k$, and put $b=r/k$.
On the scalar charge-$k/2$ sector, every row of $M$ has the same sum.
Its distance-four term alone is
\[
 d_k=12(ab)^2\binom{k/2}{2}^2
     =\frac{3a^2r^2}{16}(k-2)^2.
\]
The constant vector is a Perron eigenvector, with eigenvalue at least
$d_k$. For every fixed $t>0$,
\[
 \log\bigl[2^{-k}\operatorname{Tr}e^{tM}\bigr]
 \ge td_k-k\ln2\longrightarrow+\infty.
\]
Therefore the valid trace comparison alone cannot prove an
$O(r^2)$ logarithmic-moment bound. It loses signs and history
cancellations. The matrix $M$ is not a physical response: its quadratic
Perron scale already violates the physical linear spectral range.

\subsection{Even the physical spectral range does not repair the block-only premise}

The following construction shows precisely which inference is unavailable.
It is an abstract input operator, deliberately not claimed to arise from
the specified unitary histories or from any $K_\zeta$ of that device.

\begin{theorem}[Rare-sector counterexample to block-data-only concentration]
\label{thm:abstract-rare-sector}
Fix $0<r\le1/256$. For all sufficiently large even $k$, set
\[
 b=r/k,\quad a=1-b,\quad
 h=\left\lfloor\frac{k^{3/4}}{4\sqrt r}\right\rfloor,
 \quad L=2h,\quad n=k-L.
\]
There is a Hermitian charge-preserving input operator $K^{\rm fake}_k$
such that:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item $\mathcal P(K^{\rm fake}_k)=0$ and every pattern block satisfies
 the actual bound \eqref{eq:scalar-majorant}.
 \item $\|K^{\rm fake}_k\|\le3k/256$ and
 \[
  \mathcal P((K^{\rm fake}_k)^2)
       \le\frac{9r^2}{64k}I\le r^2e^{4r^2}I.
 \]
 \item With $\mathcal G^{\rm fake}=a(2Q-kI)+K^{\rm fake}_k$,
 \[
  \mathcal P(\mathcal G^{\rm fake})=a(2Q-kI),\qquad
  0\le Q-\mathcal G^{\rm fake}\le kI,\qquad
  \|\mathcal G^{\rm fake}\|\le k.
 \]
 Thus it even obeys the spectral interval inherited from a compressed
 output charge, not merely a generic $O(k)$ norm estimate.
 \item Nevertheless
 \[
  \log\operatorname{Tr}\sigma
    e^{-(\ln2)K^{\rm fake}_k/(2a)}\longrightarrow+\infty.
 \]
 In particular no bound $C r^2$ uniform in $k$ follows from these data.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take even $k\ge r^{-2}$ and sufficiently large that
$x=k^{3/4}/(4\sqrt r)\ge2$. Then $L\le k/2$ and $n$ is even.
Use only patterns $x=(u,v)$ with $|u|=h$ on the first $L=2h$ positions
and $|v|=n/2$ on the remaining positions. They all have total charge
$k/2$. For every fixed spectator word $v$, join two active words when
they differ by exchanging exactly two ones with two zeros. The resulting
distance-two Johnson graph has degree
\[
 d=\binom h2^2.
\]
Its adjacency matrix is denoted $A_2$. Identify each color fiber in
this total-charge sector with $(\mathbb C^2)^{\otimes k/2}$ by reading
colors in their input order. These are genuine unitary identifications.
Define
\[
 K^{\rm fake}_k=-wA_2\otimes I_{2^{k/2}},\qquad
 w=12(ab)^2,
\]
on each fixed spectator block, and set it to zero on the orthogonal
complement. Adjoint blocks use inverse identifications. This defines a
Hermitian operator on the original qutrit input space.

Every nonzero block has Hamming distance four and norm $w$, exactly the
$m=2$ bound. All other offdiagonal blocks and all diagonal blocks vanish.
Since $A_2$ is a symmetric regular adjacency matrix, its norm is $d$ and
its constant vector has eigenvalue $d$. Hence, writing $\lambda=wd$,
\[
 \|K^{\rm fake}_k\|=\lambda,\qquad
 \mathcal P((K^{\rm fake}_k)^2)=w^2dP_{\rm supp}=w\lambda P_{\rm supp}.
\]
For $x\ge2$, $h\ge x/2$, $h\le x$, and
$h^2/4\le\binom h2\le h^2/2$. Also $a\ge1/2$. It follows that
\[
 \frac{3k}{65536}\le\lambda\le\frac{3k}{256},\qquad
 w\lambda\le\frac{9r^2}{64k}.
\]
On the support $Q=k/2$, so
$Q-\mathcal G^{\rm fake}=kI/2-K^{\rm fake}_k$ lies in $[0,kI]$.
Off the support this operator equals $a(kI-Q)+bQ$, which also lies in
$[0,kI]$. The advertised spectral constraints follow.

Each spectator word contributes at least one active constant eigenvector
with eigenvalue $-\lambda$, for every color vector. Its full reference
weight is $2^{-k}$. There are $\binom n{n/2}$ spectator words.
Using $\binom n{n/2}\ge2^n/(n+1)$ gives
\begin{align*}
 \log\operatorname{Tr}\sigma e^{-(\ln2)K^{\rm fake}_k/(2a)}
 &\ge\frac{\ln2}{2a}\lambda-L\ln2-\ln(n+1)\\
 &\ge\frac{3\ln2}{131072}k
   -\frac{\ln2}{2\sqrt r}k^{3/4}-\ln(k+1)
   \longrightarrow+\infty.
\end{align*}
The reference-weighted second moment even tends to zero: it is bounded
by $9r^2/(64k)$. This confirms that the failure is a rare spectral sector,
not a missing low-order variance calculation.
\end{proof}

The construction also obeys the Cycle10 weighted response estimate by
the same row-square and completely positive Cauchy--Schwarz argument.
Tensoring $\mathcal G^{\rm fake}$ with a prefix identity makes that
abstract observation valid for every analytic prefix contraction. It
does not identify it with the physical $\mathcal G_k(b)$.

\subsection{Receiving consequence and necessary restriction}

These results retire a proposed proof route, not the physical question.
The inherited Hamming block norms, zero complete-pattern diagonal,
conditional second moment, charge preservation and physical spectral
range are insufficient to establish the desired logarithmic moment.
A successful theorem for the actual device must additionally use its
signed unitary-history structure, a suitable analytic prefix, or another
property excluding the abstract concentrated Johnson sectors above.
The counterexample is not a capacity converse or an admissible input
construction for the channel. In particular it does not refute the
existence of a useful prefix $\zeta(k,b)$ for the actual bulk operator.
