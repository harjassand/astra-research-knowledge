# Primary comparisons and exact reading scope

No historical-originality claim is made for the new device theorem. The comparison is focused on the actual proof mechanism and coding interface.

## Operator compression and trace inequalities

- F. Hansen and G. K. Pedersen, *Jensen's Operator Inequality*, Bulletin of the London Mathematical Society 35 (2003), 553–564. Theorem 2.1 gives the compression form of operator Jensen. The original preprint was read, including the relevant proof and arbitrary-Hilbert-space formulation: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0204049. Cycle11 also proves the needed logarithmic case by the resolvent integral.
- S. Golden, *Lower Bounds for the Helmholtz Function*, Physical Review 137 (1965), B1127–B1128. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.137.B1127. The original publisher abstract was read; the full subscription article was not obtained. No claim of a full-article reading is made.
- C. J. Thompson, *Inequality with Applications in Statistical Mechanics*, Journal of Mathematical Physics 6 (1965), 1812–1813. https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1704727. The original bibliographic attribution and inequality are also given by Kafri–Deffner below. The DOI opening returned an internal retrieval error, not the full article.

These are established ingredients. In particular, Golden–Thompson is applied only to finite input matrices. The actual all-normal extension uses logarithmic compression; an operator-convex exponential step would be invalid.

## Closest information and thermodynamic mechanisms

- J. Goold, M. Paternostro and K. Modi, *Nonequilibrium Quantum Landauer Principle*, Physical Review Letters 114 (2015), 060602. Original full preprint: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.4499. The initial product/thermal-bath assumptions and equations (2)–(8) were read. They derive a heat bound through a CP-operation exponential moment and non-unitality. Cycle11 does not identify its bath depth with calibrated heat and does not impose an initial thermal assumption in the upper theorem.
- D. Kafri and S. Deffner, *Holevo's bound from a general quantum fluctuation theorem*, Physical Review A 86 (2012), 044302. Original full preprint: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1207.2978. The measurement-dependent Holevo derivation and Golden–Thompson step were read. It is a meaningful conventional comparison for exponential-moment information inequalities, not the present same-bath quantum capacity law.
- D. Reeb and M. M. Wolf, *An improved Landauer Principle with finite-size corrections*, New Journal of Physics 16 (2014), 103011. Original full preprint: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1306.4352. Theorem 3 and its product thermal-reservoir assumptions were read; the paper also treats infinite-dimensional extensions in Appendix C.3. Its equality relates entropy decrease, heat, mutual information and relative entropy. Cycle11 instead bounds input entropy against a bounded conserved charge displacement for an arbitrary normal initially independent memory.

The new content claimed internally is the exact root-retaining tilted envelope for this interaction, its iteration on one unchanged infinite bath, the resulting all-length entropy threshold, and the operational rate–depth and resource-constrained order consequences. A field-wide priority search has not been completed.

## Actual coding comparator

V. Giovannetti, D. Burgarth and S. Mancini, *Communication Through a Quantum Link*, Physical Review A 79 (2009), 012311. Original full preprint: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0810.0170. Definition 1 and Theorem 2 were read. Their perfect-memory criterion uses subexponential environment growth to construct quantum codes.

The present lower is the actual inherited infinite-bath compression theorem, with its initializer and full-diamond quantifiers, positive-block factor and normalization/polar repairs. The source is retained, not replaced by a citation to a memoryless channel theorem.
