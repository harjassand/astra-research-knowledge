1. WHOLE-BATH ENTROPY–DISPLACEMENT THEOREM

Let E=C2_seed tensor Fock(C2), S_j prepend color j, R exchange vacuum seeds
and vanish off vacuum. The actual unitary is
  U=[[R,S1,S2],[S1*,0,0],[S2*,0,0]],
  V_beta=sqrt(1-beta^2) U+i beta I.
Let q=diag(0,1,1), Q_k=sum_i q_i, W_k the ordered k-call unitary, and
  G_k=Q_k-W_k*Q_k W_k, D_b(rho)=Tr_input[(rho tensor I)G_k].
G_k is bounded by k. It represents bath-depth displacement without ever
subtracting infinite means.

For every k, beta in [0,1], arbitrary correlated input rho, and arbitrary
normal initializer initially independent of the input,
  D_b(rho) >= 2 [S(rho)-k c(b)] I,
  c(b)=log2[2 sqrt(2)(1-b)+3b], b=beta^2.
Here S is base-two von Neumann entropy, not Renyi entropy. The operator
inequality is global, including the root. No [rho,Q_k]=0 condition appears.

The exact one-call tilted CP map is
  Psi_lambda(I)
  = [3b+(1-b)(exp(lambda)+2exp(-lambda))] I
    -(1-b)(exp(lambda)-1) P0.
For lambda>=0 its root term has the favorable sign. Iterating this exact
same-memory map, logarithmic isometric compression, Golden–Thompson,
and finite-dimensional Gibbs variational inequality give the theorem.
lambda=(ln2)/2 gives c(b). No memory entropy bound, normal-state truncation,
fresh-channel approximation or classical-noise substitution is used.

The general reusable interface is proved: any finite port observable q
and same-memory unitary V with Psi_lambda(I)<=f_lambda I give
  S(rho)<=k log2 f_lambda+(lambda/ln2) Tr[(rho tensor tau)G_k].
Initial input–bath independence is necessary; an explicit same-device
correlated-edge state violates the proposed extension without it.

2. ALL-LENGTH INWARD-DRIFT ORDER

Any density with nonpositive full bulk operator drift has S(rho)/k<=c(b).
Even one nonpositive normal expectation suffices. This holds over all
block lengths and all input correlations. Since
  c(b)-3/2=log2(1+d_*b), d_*=3/(2sqrt2)-1,
the excess is O(beta^2).
The actual completed Cycle10 positive inward-drift input gives
  S(rho)/k>=3/2+(ln2/2^20) beta^2, 0<beta<=1/32.
Thus the optimized entropy excess in the admitted inward construction
class is Theta(beta^2), uniformly over temporal length. This is not a
full capacity or feedback converse.

For a repeated independent charge-preserving input block with
delta=2[S(rho)-kc(b)]>0, the global operator lower implies all-normal escape:
  Pr(L_m<=H)<=exp(tH) lambda_-^m,
  t=delta/(2k^2), lambda_-=1-delta^2/(4k^2),
and Pr(L_m<=delta m/4)<=exp[-delta^2 m/(8k^2)].
Charge preservation is required for this homogeneous-depth consequence,
not for the main entropy theorem. Escape alone says nothing sufficient
about reference leakage.

3. ARBITRARY-CODE RATE–DEPTH RESOURCE BOUND

For any deterministic unassisted fixed-N code, a general CPTP encoder,
logical dimension K, and full-diamond error epsilon_N<=1, let rho_N be
the encoded maximally mixed input. Then
  S(rho_N)>=(1-epsilon_N)log2 K-h2(epsilon_N/2),
and the whole-bath displacement is at least
  2[(1-epsilon_N)log2 K-h2(epsilon_N/2)-Nc(b)] I.

For finite-mean initializer, including actual tau_pi,
  (1-epsilon_N) log2 K/N
  <=c(b)+(E L_final-E L_initial)/(2N)+h2(epsilon_N/2)/N.
Full error epsilon corresponds to half error epsilon/2. If half-diamond
error is called delta_e, the coefficient is 1-2delta_e, not 1-delta_e.

Consequently reliable rate R>c(b) requires at least 2[R-c(b)] asymptotic
mean-depth expenditure per forward call. For arbitrary normal infinite-
mean initializers the bounded operator statement remains valid, while
an undefined difference of infinite means is not written.

More generally, at limsup depth expenditure per call at most v>=0,
  R<=C_b(v)=inf_{lambda>=0}
     {log2[3b+(1-b)(exp(lambda)+2exp(-lambda))]
      +lambda v/ln2}.
The closed optimizer and its transition v=(1-b)/3 are proved. The curve
is an upper envelope, not a claimed achievable tradeoff.

4. MATCHING OPERATIONAL ORDER UNDER SUBLINEAR DEPTH COST

The actual inherited Cycle10 encoder is newly shown to have final mean
bath depth O_beta(log N) at tau_pi, uniformly over logical states and
arbitrary retained logical references. It is the same physical encoder;
the auxiliary CPTP normalization is not implemented.

The proof uses the exact joint overflow bound with its r_A^2 factor,
finite propagation, and the actual geometric initial tail:
  A_n=floor(log2(n+1)), epsilon_n=(n+1)^(-16),
  r_A=2^(A+2)-2, H_n=O_beta(log(n+1)),
  E L_final <= H_n
      +(A_n+nk)2 r_A^2 (n+1)^(-16)
      +(A_n+nk+3/2)3^(-(A_n+1)).
The two corrections vanish. No physical cutoff, reset or measurement
is introduced.

Define Q_sub using the original universal-initializer code quantifier
and, additionally, final mean depth o(N) at tau_pi on the encoded
maximally mixed logical input. Then for fixed 0<beta<=1/32,
  3/2+(ln2/2^20) beta^2 <= Q_sub(beta,tau_pi)
  <=3/2+log2(1+d_* beta^2).
The lower actually has a uniform-logical O(log N) resource bound.
Thus the excess has sharp order Theta(beta^2). The upper also holds for
codes chosen solely for tau_pi. This added resource contract must not
be silently substituted for unrestricted capacity.
It bounds final mean depth, not peak intermediate depth or trace-norm
return to the initializer.

5. ACTUAL COUNTERMECHANISM AND FAILED MOMENT ROUTES

The pure input |1>^tensor N has exact final bath state
  sum_j Bin(N,1-b)(j) S1^j tau_pi S1*^j.
Its mean depth is 1/2+(1-b)N, but bath entropy is at most
S(tau_pi)+log2(N+1). Including the initial bath purifier, entropy is at
most H(Bin(N,1-b)). At b=0 it is constant. Hence linear depth does not
force extensive complementary entropy. This is not a high-rate code.

The full positive block-entry Johnson comparator has a divergent
distance-four rare-sector pressure and is not physically admissible.
A refined abstract counterexample also satisfies the correct linear
spectral range, conditional second moment and output-charge interval,
yet has divergent pressure. It is still not a physical K_zeta. These
results close those sufficient comparison methods, not the actual
O((bk)^2) physical-pressure claim.
The actual all-prefix bound obtained is
  Tr sigma exp(-t K_zeta)
  <=[(1-b) cosh(tb)+b cosh(t(1-b))]^k.
Its generic logarithm is O(bk), not the stronger uniform O((bk)^2).

6. COSTS, COMPARISON AND CONTINUATION

The completed lower uses input dimension 3^k, k=floor[1/(256 beta^2)],
and no efficient compiler. At beta=1/512, k=1024 and log10(3^k)=488.572...
The Lyapunov cutoff coefficient is enormous. An asymptotic logarithmic
resource bound is not a useful finite-horizon or laboratory claim.

Older pair, nonscalar four-call, cubic and clipping certificates are
preserved. The Cycle9 lower still beats the Cycle10 certificate at
beta=1/512. No lower coefficient is improved in this checkpoint.
Numerical observations are new bounded checks and reading of unchanged
certificate data, not reruns of old proof suites. Discovery, inference
and total review costs remain unknown.

The next selected scientific question is reference leakage in escape-
compatible high-entropy correlated inputs/codes. For a purification R
of the physical input and initial bath purifier F0,
  Ic(rho_N,N_N)=S(rho_N)-I(R:E_N F0).
A possible converse would lower-bound leakage by
  [S(rho_N)-Nc(b)]_+-o(N),
with appropriate uniformity and an encoder reduction for general CPTP
logical encoders. This is unproved, and an actual high-rate counterfamily
would be equally consequential. The pure-push example rules out depth
alone as the missing argument.
