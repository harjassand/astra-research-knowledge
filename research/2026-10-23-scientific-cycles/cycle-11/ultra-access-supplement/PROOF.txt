1. ACTUAL SOURCE AND INTERFACE

Fix D,L>=2 and a known finite p on K=1,...,L. The complete iid queue source
is exactly
 Omega_p=sum_k p_k |kk><kk|_F tensor
         [Phi_D^tensor k tensor nu^tensor(L-k)]_O tensor nu_M^tensor L,
 nu=(I_D/D) tensor(I_D/D).
Current clocks F and old memory M are inaccessible. Default apparatus is
normal local PRODUCT across A:B and tensor-factor from the whole source.
There is no entangled aid. Output rank N_r is deterministic on EVERY branch,
with error T(omega_QW,Phi_(N_r) tensor omega_W)->0 against ACTUAL residual W.
R(p) denotes its zero-aid rate. The source and D,L,p are fixed before r grows.
Set B=E[K]lnD, H=H(K), C_p=H(K|Y), where Y is only a mathematical product
Bell-basis label. The included unchanged principal proof establishes
 R(p)<=U_p:=B-H/2+C_p/2,
 C_p<=c(D^-2), c(q)=-ln(1-q)-q lnq/(1-q),
 (1-2e)lnN<=rU_p+g(e), g(e)=(1+e)h2(e/(1+e)),             (1)
whenever the Bell marginal has half-trace error e<1/2. Its proof uses finite
output conditional entropy, normal local-workspace compression and E_sq, not
an inaccessible-label measurement. Bell marginal convergence lifts to the
actual full residual by T<=2sqrt(1-Tr(Phi_N omega_Q)).

2. SUPPLIED RECORD WITH A GLOBAL NO-INFORMATION BRANCH

Fix alpha in (0,1) and beta=1-alpha. At each r, ACTUALLY prepare a common
classical record Z at both parties by the stochastic law
 Z=? with probability beta,
 Z=(reveal,K^r) with probability alpha.                    (2)
The Bernoulli selector is independent of the ENTIRE original complete source.
Conditioned on K^r, its quantum state remains EXACTLY sigma_(K^r), including
the original clock/old-memory extension. Tracing Z restores Omega_p^tensor r
exactly. Thus this is admissible actual classical access under the principal
optional-access contract, not a replacement by a favorable input component.
Record preparation, storage and communication are actual resources; their
information contents below are not a claim of free experimental readout.
Finite r uses a finite record alphabet of size at most1+L^r.

Let R_outage(p,alpha) be the reliable fixed-rank rate when (2) is supplied.

THEOREM (exact no-help-rate preservation).
 R_outage(p,alpha)=R(p) for every fixed 0<alpha<1.           (3)
Yet the supplied record has the extensive average information
 H(K^r|Z)=beta rH,
 I(K^r:Z)=alpha rH,
 I(Y^r:Z)=alpha r(H-C_p).                                 (4)
In particular an average Shannon budget is a necessary converse resource
description but is not a sufficient reliable-yield description for arbitrary
collective records. This is a standard outage/reliability obstruction in this
precise source/target; no historical novelty is claimed for that general idea.

PROOF. Ignoring Z gives R_outage>=R(p): the acted-on quantum source marginal
is still exactly rho_p^tensor r, so its Bell marginal code is unchanged. The
pure-output lift then includes the NEW actual Z extension in the residual;
we do not tensor the old full-state comparison with a correlated record.
For the opposite direction, take ANY
admitted access-assisted code with fixed rank N_r and global joint error
epsilon_r. Contract to its Bell marginal and test Phi_(N_r). Its Bell
infidelity delta_r=1-Tr(Phi_N omega_Q) is at most epsilon_r. Conditional on
the input branch Z=?, write its Bell infidelity delta_(?,r). Linearity gives
 beta delta_(?,r)<=delta_r<=epsilon_r.                      (5)
For a pure target, the Fuchs–van de Graaf bound then gives
 T(omega_(Q|?),Phi_N)<=sqrt(epsilon_r/beta).                 (6)
Hardwire the constant input record '?' in the same finite normal LOCC device.
This is a valid ZERO-AID operation on the ORIGINAL Omega_p^tensor r, with
the SAME deterministic rank N_r. Conditional on '?' the original source is
unchanged, since its selector was independent. There is no correlated record,
branch selection of K, donated seed or aid left in this hardwired protocol.
Equation (6) tends to zero. The pure-output lift supplies its own ACTUAL
complete residual, so this hardwired sequence is admitted in R(p). Every
achievable access rate is therefore <=R(p), proving (3).

This proof does NOT assume that a receiver preserves Z or a hidden copy of
its selector in the final memory. Even if all accessible records are erased,
Bell infidelity is linear in the ACTUAL input mixture, and (5) still holds.
It does not assert hidden-memory return, uniform per-branch correctness, or
an experimentally observable flag; a nonvanishing-probability branch simply
cannot be hidden inside vanishing global pure-target error.

Given '?' the K vector is iid p, whereas a reveal record specifies it exactly.
This proves the first two identities in (4). The selector is independent of
Y^r and K^r, and I(Y^r:K^r)=r(H-C_p), proving the third. Markov
Z<-K^r->Y^r holds under the actual preparation. The principal exact AVERAGE
converse remains correct, but here becomes only
 R_outage<=B-beta(H-C_p)/2,
which is weaker than the branch obstruction (1),(3). This is a limit on the
achievability interpretation of that average witness, not a contradiction.

Finite error: put e=sqrt(epsilon/beta). If epsilon<beta/4, apply (1) to the
hardwired no-information branch, obtaining the stronger necessary condition
 (1-2sqrt(epsilon/beta))lnN
    <=rU_p+g(sqrt(epsilon/beta)).                          (7)
There is no strong-converse or error-exponent claim in (7).

A STRONGER LINEAR FINITE-ERROR BOUND is available. Put a=epsilon/beta<1/2.
Append a finite bilateral generalized-Weyl twirl to the hardwired branch's
N by N Bell output (N>=2). The N^2 unitaries W_(u,v) tensor conjugate(W_(u,v))
have distinct characters on all N^2 generalized Bell states, for EVERY
integer N: their commutation character is exp[2pi i(uv'-vu')/N], a nondegenerate
pairing on Z_N^2, with no prime-field assumption. Averaging dephases the Bell
basis, preserving Phi_N fidelity and giving uniform local marginals I_N/N.
The finite classical unitary label is generated locally and communicated;
it is a genuine finite LOCC operation with no entangled aid or free shared seed.
If the branch infidelity is delta<=a, its twirled entropy is at most
h2(delta)+delta ln(N^2-1)<=h2(a)+a ln(N^2-1). Coherent information and
squashed-entanglement monotonicity therefore give
 lnN-h2(a)-a ln(N^2-1)<=rU_p,
 (1-2epsilon/beta)lnN<=rU_p+h2(epsilon/beta).             (7a)
The entropy bound is monotone for a<1/2 and N>=2. Normal-workspace compression
uses fixed finite N just as in the principal proof. A witness-dependent
universal consequence, valid without a<1/2, is
 epsilon >= (beta/2) [1-(rU_p+ln2)/lnN]_+, N>=2.          (7b)
For a>=1/2 it is immediate since U_p>=0; otherwise use h2(a)<=ln2 in (7a).
These are error lower bounds, not a strong converse at the unknown exact R(p).

MATCHED RECORD CONTROL. Compare (2) with independently supplied per-queue
records: reveal K_a with probability alpha, else give '?', independently for
each queue. Both actual preparations preserve the identical COMPLETE original
source and all conditional sigma_(K^r), with zero entangled aid. The iid record
has EXACTLY the same three information quantities in (4). Its entropy is
r h2(alpha)+alpha rH; the original global record entropy is only
h2(alpha)+alpha rH. To match that resource too, pad the GLOBAL record with
r-1 independent Bernoulli(alpha) bits and supply their duplicated copies.
They carry no information about source or useful record. They can also be

I(K^r:Z) and I(Y^r:Z).

Even the fixed register/description budget can be matched: use r flag bits
and r payload slots of ceil(log2L) bits at each party for both preparations.
For global access, the first flag chooses full reveal versus full erasure,

each flag controls just its own payload, with the same dummy convention.
Unused binary labels are simply never prepared. Both use r independent

The main IID access theorem gives R_iid>=B-beta H-kappa_D, whereas the

Whenever (1/2-beta)H>kappa_D+C_p/2, these matching resource summaries accompany

3. EXPLICIT COUNTEREXAMPLE TO A COLLECTIVE CONDITIONAL-ENTROPY LOWER LAW

The principal iid-record receiver gives lower B-H(K|Z)-kappa_D, where
kappa_D=H(XOR(A,B))/(1-1/D) for independent uniform D-level digits. It would
be FALSE to replace this by B-H(K^r|Z)/r-kappa_D for arbitrary actual
collective Z. Choose the ORIGINAL uniform prior at
 D=2, L=4096=2^12, beta=1/4, alpha=3/4.
Then H=12ln2, kappa_2=2ln2, and (4) makes the proposed lower
 B-[(1/4)12ln2]-2ln2=B-5ln2.                              (8)
But (1),(3) give
 R_outage<=B-6ln2+c(1/4)/2.                               (9)
The claimed lower (8) exceeds the rigorous upper (9) by at least
 ln2-c(1/4)/2=(ln3)/2-(ln2)/3>0.                          (10)
Positivity needs no floating-point certificate: 3^3>2^2. It is approximately
0.318257 nats. Thus even the additive constant kappa_D of the iid receiver
cannot rescue that collective entropy substitution. All source branches and
the deterministic output/error contract are the SAME; only the explicitly
supplied record (2) is changed. No large quantum matrix is diagonalized.

4. VANISHING OUTAGE HAS A DIFFERENT, EXACT RESOURCE CONSEQUENCE

If beta=beta_r->0 in (2), with arbitrary rate of convergence, the same actual
source instead achieves the FULL mean supply B. This distinguishes a fixed
rare failure from a failure probability that truly vanishes under the target.

PROOF. Set gamma_r=r^-1/4 and t_r=max{0,floor[r(EK-gamma_r)]}. Demand fixed
rank N_r=D^t_r on every branch. On reveal outcomes with sum_a K_a>=t_r,
use the supplied record to select and concatenate t_r TRUE prefix Bell pairs,
and perform local relabeling. Each selected pair is a pure factor of the
COMPLETE conditional source, giving exactly Phi_(N_r). On '?' or insufficient
total inventory, output the declared product filler in the same fixed space.
This is finite TP LOCC with all failures counted. If L>1, Hoeffding gives
 Pr(sum K_a<t_r)<=exp[-2r gamma_r^2/(L-1)^2]
                 =exp[-2sqrt(r)/(L-1)^2].                 (11)
Its actual-complete-residual error is at most beta_r plus this tail DIRECTLY.
To see this without assuming record retention, decompose the actual output
into successful and failed positive subnormalized blocks. Every successful
block is exactly Phi_N tensor its own residual. If the total failed weight
is f, their difference from Phi_N tensor the ACTUAL full residual is just
the failed block minus Phi_N tensor its residual; its half-trace norm is
at most f. Thus error<=beta_r+the tail in (11). It tends
to zero and lnN_r/r->B. The source-supply upper B still applies, by squashing
on K^r in the actual source-preserving classical preparation. Thus this
vanishing-outage family has exact reliable rate B. The finite tail bound can
be very weak at large L; no useful blocklength or readout cost is asserted.

MATCHED RELIABILITY SCALE. For a nondegenerate p, H-C_p=I(K:Y)>0 and B>U_p.
For ANY target sequence lnN_r/r->B and beta_r>0, (7b) gives
 liminf epsilon_r/beta_r >= (B-U_p)/(2B)>0.                (12)
The explicit receiver gives epsilon_r<=beta_r+exp[-2sqrt(r)/(L-1)^2].
Whenever that displayed inventory tail is o(beta_r), the optimum achievable
global error for this target has order beta_r, up to fixed source constants.
This matches the outage dependence while charging the inventory fluctuation
separately; no uniform error exponent or exact leading error constant follows.

5. CONSEQUENCE, COSTS AND THE PRECISE REMAINING QUESTION

The record information density has a zero-information branch with probability
beta and a full-information branch otherwise. Expected information and its
relevance witness are extensive, but reliable FIXED output is controlled by
that nonvanishing lower tail. The same conceptual issue underlies the main
heavy-tailed Bell-stock warning. These are distinct actual counterexamples,
not a declaration that random resource budgets are useless in other tasks.

This removes a tempting generalization from the continuation queue. The
remaining access question is a LOW-OUTAGE/INFORMATION-SPECTRUM receiving law,
or a precise additional concentration hypothesis under which collective
records can support an entropy-based achievable rate. No such general law
is proved here. The owner's single principal continuation remains the broad-
prior half-versus-one entropy coefficient under the original zero-aid task.
This supplement establishes a structural boundary useful to that programme;
it does not start a new domain or assign work to another owner.
