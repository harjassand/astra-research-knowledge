# Applicable ordinary methods and what changed

## Scientific contribution at this checkpoint

Cycle10 covered bridgeless chordless partial two-trees of maximum degree four.
Cycle11 removes the bridge restriction while preserving the original graph,
all four full actions, free diagonal and the absolute Frobenius-noise contract.
The new objects are a source-bound degree-three missing-neighbor-color
qualification, a two-condition bridge reset, and a quantitative module
composition theorem. Bounded component geometry is used to pay the inverse
cost; a local rank or coloring existence claim alone is not the result.

The reusable theorem accepts module-specific root qualifications and Poincare
constants. It therefore identifies a concrete sufficient interface through
which a future nonchordless module construction could be received. That is
structural capital; it does not imply that such a future module exists.

| Published treewidth-two star-color upper | 6 | Star/Hessian compression applies; stronger star constraint may use more actions than stable nonstar recovery |

Four is a sharp worst-case count only within the inherited coordinate lower's
scope on the new class. Arbitrary-real four-query recovery on all degree-four
partial two-trees remains unresolved. The new method may save one action
against the full-class five-action control on its covered subclass; that is
not a claim of better conditioning, preparation time, or total runtime.

## Primary-source boundary

1. Gebremedhin, Pothen, Tarafdar and Walther, *Efficient Computation of Sparse
   Hessians Using Coloring and Automatic Differentiation*, INFORMS Journal
   on Computing (2009). The original paper models direct recovery by star
   coloring and substitution by acyclic coloring, and analyzes tree-dependent
   roundoff in Section 3.3, Theorem 3.1.
   <https://www.cs.purdue.edu/homes/apothen/Papers/hessian-coloring-joc.pdf>

   Coloring compression, incidence/tree substitution and numerical-error
   analysis are established machinery. The current contribution is the
   precise graph class, rooted/global interface and uniform absolute-noise
   operator consequence, not the invention of those basic operations.

2. Basavaraju, Hegde and Kulamarva, *Acyclic Chromatic Index of Chordless
   Graphs*, arXiv:2302.01638. Theorem 1 determines the acyclic EDGE chromatic
   index; Corollary 2 gives two linear forests at maximum degree four.
   <https://arxiv.org/html/2302.01638v1>

   This is a relevant structural comparison, but its edge partition is not
   a vertex query design. Linear forests may contain long paths, and actions
   on their separate edge supports are not available from the original
   action without a further proved operation. Importing that result alone
   would not discharge the new bridge/root or inverse-noise obligations.

3. Aravind and Subramanian, *Bounds on vertex colorings with restrictions
   on the union of color classes*, Journal of Graph Theory 66 (2011),
   213-234. Definition 1.2 and Theorem 1.3 give bounded-component proper
   colorings with degree-dependent color counts.
   <https://people.iith.ac.in/aravind/Files/jgt-gvc.pdf>

   Bounded bichromatic component size is an established coloring objective.
   The inspected theorem does not supply four colors at degree four,
   acyclicity automatically, or the original-action inverse constant here.

The bounded primary-source check did not establish historical originality.
Full contributor inspection notes and source locations are preserved in
research/primary_boundary/. Absence of a matching theorem in this inspection
is not evidence of priority or a complete literature search.

## No practical performance claim

The new rational receivers deliberately supply explicit matrix coefficients
to assemble and check full actions. They certify the construction and its
error boundary; they are not a native application with an expensive measured
product oracle. Direct assembly is already available on those records. The
timed bodies do not establish superiority over assembly, AD, a five-action
implementation, or the original NIST dense incumbent. Historical molecular,
NIST and physical campaigns remain closed with their existing cost defeats.

The old 99-vertex source certificate remains at squared constant20. This
checkpoint does not replace it by the weaker49 transfer bound or infer a new
application advantage from a different structural theorem.
