## Algorithmic boundary

Canonical edge/module/atom sorting and the inherited native normalizer give
O(n+m log m) arithmetic/comparison preparation, plus one fixed certificate
load and the new finite18-row projection. Coloring/certification and each
canonical LS decode cost O(nq+m) arithmetic after preparation. All original
actions and O(n+m) sparse output are charged. Fractions may grow; bit complexity
is additional to these operation counts. The executable uses no floating
solver acceptance as a certificate.

A floating receiving implementation must bound acquisition/conversion and
assembly noise and its projected numerical residual. Probe perturbation is
not covered by row normalization alone. The current exact receivers have
zero projected numerical residual and explicitly nonzero raw residual.

The full-class five-query palette control, ordinary forest and A_L three-query
controls and direct available assembly/AD remain applicable comparisons.
No matched application runtime competition was undertaken or claimed here.
This checkpoint is a proved enlargement of a structural recovery class and
a reusable bridge-composition principle, with measured implementations/checks.
