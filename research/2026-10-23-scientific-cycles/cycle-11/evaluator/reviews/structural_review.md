## 3. Pro2: substantive change and its limits

### Before and after

Cycle9 proved stable coordinate recovery with `p+2` actions on a specific intrinsic class whose maximal blocks are `K_(p,k)`, with each cut on the large side and in exactly two blocks. It distinguished exact identification, uniform stability, and star-color recovery. It also proved that a 16-vertex chain of three `K_(2,4)` blocks has no star-four coloring even though it has a stable four-action construction. The actual one-mark theorem supplied one realizable star coloring at a specified degree-two root. It did not promise independently selectable profiles at multiple roots.

Cycle10's new Theorem A covers every connected bridgeless chordless graph with an edge, treewidth at most two and maximum degree four. Graph size, depth, branch length, number of attachments, and adjacent marks are unrestricted. Four coordinate probes and the canonical inverse give

`||Dhat-D||op <= ||Dhat-D||F <= sqrt(3+sqrt(5)) ||E||F`.

The operation uses the original known support, an arbitrary real symmetric `D` with free diagonal, whole probe-row norm at most one, all original `DX+E`, and arbitrary absolute Frobenius action noise. This is an all-size theorem, not an extrapolation from the 57- and 30-vertex implementations.

The load-bearing invention is a constrained gluing rule. At a child root, an actual local coloring is permuted so that parent and child share at most one channel, unflagged on the child side. That inherited two-color component is then an isolated edge. If the far endpoint is another cut, one message requires the next child to avoid its incoming color. Each block is colored once at its actual incoming root. The rule constrains the next child rather than asking the current block to satisfy independent multiple-root promises. Hence global components become stars with at most one extra pendant edge at each vertex: short-arm spiders with a uniform inverse bound.

This meaningfully exceeds Cycle9's selected arbitrary-length theta frontier: non-theta blocks, cycles, and triangles are covered too. A separate table-free theta construction supplies a useful simpler implementation on its narrower domain. Its existence is not necessary for the main theorem.

### What the failures taught

The source preserves an actual C4-chain counterexample: unflagged isolated entry at every child does not by itself stop unbounded concatenation. The inverse squared constant has a lower bound `ad/(a+d)` that diverges with depth. The next-child message stops the very transmission that defeats the weaker rule. This is a constructive response to an obstruction, not an abandoned programme.

The alternate theta route also found that stable coloring after deleting a hub does not automatically survive restoring the hubs. Its endpoint correction is substantive. The shape-checker error and singleton-scope clarification are implementation and statement repairs, not independent physical or mathematical discoveries.

### Correctness and practical interpretation

The all-depth induction is explicit. The source's independent review checks likely failure points: shared channels, collision of requests, marked centers, multiple-block incidence, and the distinction between one actual root and freely prescribed root profiles. The native code implements the same conditions rather than silently relying on a supplied expression. Independent saved-operation checks use different block reconstruction and exact normal equations without producer imports. This supports internal correctness and realizability; I have not reproduced those executions or supplied external peer review.

Four is the worst-case uniformly stable **coordinate** minimum on the new class, including signed unequal single-coordinate amplitudes. It is not an arbitrary-real lower for this subclass. The full all-real four-versus-five problem on every degree-four partial two-tree remains open. Bridges and nonchordless blocks remain outside the positive theorem.

The serious comparator is established sparse-Hessian star/acyclic compression and substitution, including its own error analysis, plus the inherited five-action full-class design. Forests and special atom families retain better three-action operations. Star designs, where available, can have stronger conditioning. The current exact matrices expose entries cheaply, so these receivers establish no practical runtime advantage over direct assembly or AD. The known four-versus-five action saving cannot be converted into total speedup without an actual action source and complete preparation, precision and acquisition costs.

The seven measured computation/check bodies total approximately 0.15448 seconds, but discovery, inference, proof writing, review, integration and one failed diagnostic were not completely metered. That sum is not the cost of finding the theorem.

Its broader potential lies in a composition principle that avoids over-demanding local interfaces, and in separating exact identifiability from uniform stability. Its relevance to consequential numerical science remains a hypothesis until an actual recipient has compatible supports and expensive enough actions.
