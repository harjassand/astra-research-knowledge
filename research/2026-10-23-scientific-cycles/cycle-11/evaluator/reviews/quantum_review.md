## 3. Pro1: what changed, and what did not

### Scientific delta

Cycle9 proved an attainable excess of order `beta² / log(1/beta)` using a positive-part repair at inner block length `k ~ 1/[beta² log(1/beta)]`. The expensive unresolved step was the signed full bath response and charge cost of a positive repair at fixed `r=beta² k`.

Cycle10 proves, for fixed `0<beta<=1/32`,

`Q(V_beta,tau) >= 3/2 + (ln 2 / 2^20) beta²`,

using `k=floor[1/(256 beta²)]`. The stronger unsimplified floor is `(ln2) v_k beta^4/(64k)` above `3/2`.

The device, evolving infinite bath, actual root, resource permissions and all-normal-initializer coding quantifiers are unchanged. The same code sequence is initializer independent, but its convergence is **pointwise for each fixed normal initializer**, not uniform at finite horizon. The error is full diamond norm, including arbitrary logical reference entanglement. There is no fresh bath, bath access/reset, postselection, shared entanglement, backward wire, donated timing or terminal bath-return condition. Sources: main proof lines 80–147, 366–411; `RESULTS.txt` §§1,3.

### Two complementary new mechanisms

**First, an all-coherence response theorem.** After total-charge pinching, complete binary charge-pattern blocks differing at `2m` sites have norm bounded by `m binom(2m,m)(ab)^m`, with `a=1-b`, `b=beta²`. Complementary endpoint masks and auxiliary unitarity cancel spectator output charge before norms are taken. Summing positive row squares gives `P(Z²)<=r² exp(4r²) I`. Completely positive Cauchy–Schwarz then yields

`||D_b(sigma Y)-2a Tr(sigma A Y)I|| <= r exp(2r²)||Y-PY||_(2,sigma)`.

This is full bath-prefix operator control, including higher endpoint coherences. It is not a chosen bath expectation and not a classical-noise approximation. The exact diagonal term and the deep-bulk restriction are indispensable. Source: `FULL_HAMMING_RESPONSE.tex` lines 22–186, necessary-scope counterexamples at 211–234.

**Second, a positive family with exact diagonal normalization.** For `X=-(ln2)bH/4`, set `C=I+X/2`, `D=P(C²)`, `F=D^-1/2 C² D^-1/2`, and use a total-charge-only positive tilt `T`. Conditional-expectation bimodularity gives `P(F)=I` exactly, so `rho=sigma T F` has the intended charge cost, positivity and normalization. The inherited fourth moment bounds the nonlinear remainder in precisely the weighted norm controlled by the new theorem. The normalizer does not generally commute with `H`; both congruence factors matter. Source: main proof lines 204–360.

Neither generic normalization nor weighted norm language alone is the advance. The source-specific response estimate and a constraint-preserving feasible family jointly make the transfer possible.

### A real failed construction supplied design information

For the simpler trace-normalized square, the exact charge moment is

`Tr(sigma A H²)= (1/8) sum_(j=1)^(k-2) (k-1-j) E|S_j| ~ sqrt(2/pi) k^(5/2)/30`.

At fixed `bk`, this produces a positive charge bias of order `sqrt(k)`. The all-coherence theorem bounds the remaining term by a constant, proving eventual uniform outward bulk drift for that square family. This failure motivates the changed diagonal normalization; it is not a generic no-go for positive inputs or a capacity converse. Source: main proof lines 413–530; `FAILED_ROUTES.md`.

The original clipped family's fixed-`bk` behaviour remains unresolved. Cycle10 correctly does not claim to have solved it by changing the state. This is healthy abandonment of an expendable construction while retaining the scientific objective.

### Significance and costs

Removing a logarithmic loss is a structural asymptotic advance under unchanged operation and error definitions. The new full-response theorem is a broader reusable input interface than a single better trial state. However, a lower bound is not a capacity law. The next upper-bound question is restricted to charge-preserving inputs satisfying full inward drift at the declared temporal scale.

The conservative new certificate is numerically weaker than retained older certificates at `beta=1/32,1/64,1/256,1/512`. It only wins in the reported examples at `1/2048` and `1/4096`. At `1/512`, the new block length is 1024 and its input space dimension is `3^1024 ~ 10^488.572`; the old Cycle9 floor is still stronger. No efficient preparation, representation, precision, encoder or decoder follows. A large Hilbert dimension is not itself a proven lower bound on circuit complexity, so neither “efficient because explicit” nor “necessarily exponential-time” is licensed. Source: main proof lines 532–603 and `RESOURCE_RECORD.json`.

The contribution is therefore credible foundational mathematical progress, not a demonstrated communication technology. Its broader consequence would grow if the response/normalization mechanism covered a nontrivial class of recurrent channels, resolved a genuine capacity distinction, or generated an independently useful theorem. Those are prospects, not delivered outcomes.

## 4. Ultra: productive question change, ordinary machinery, source-specific theorem

### The old and new objectives are genuinely different

Cycle9 solved a per-queue pure-profile task with output counts `N_a<=K_a` and a pointwise loss bound on every reference branch. Its collective global-error converse prevents useful fixed-loss profiles at fixed queue length as the number of queues grows.

Cycle10 keeps the same complete source,

`Omega = L^-1 sum_k |kk><kk|_F tensor (Phi_D^k tensor nu^(L-k))_O tensor nu^L_M`,

with inaccessible current clocks and all old memory retained. It instead asks for one fixed pooled Bell rank `N_r` on **every** branch and

`T(omega_QZ, Phi_(N_r) tensor omega_Z) -> 0`,

where `omega_Z` is the exact actual residual. No output is assigned back to a queue. This serves ordinary teleportation, but it does not satisfy the old attribution requirement. The old result is not contradicted or reopened. Source: current `PROOF.txt` lines 12–46 versus actual Cycle9 `PROOF.txt` §§1–4.

### Delivered result

At fixed finite `D,L`, first take `r -> infinity`, then study `L -> infinity`. With `B=(L+1)lnD/2`,

`B - R_pool(D,L) = Theta_D(ln L)`.

The converse upper is `U=B-(lnL)/2+C_L/2`, where the exact commuting Bell-label posterior gives `0<=C_L=H(K|Y)<=c(D^-2)`. The label `Y` is an analytic variable, not a free measurement. Squashed entanglement and ordinary conditional-entropy continuity yield the finite-r bound `(1-2epsilon)ln N_r <= rU+g(epsilon)`.

For achievability, use the actual inherited finite receiver at fixed `eta`, append the already developed coarse last-nonzero Lüders instrument, and code the **actual** observable-flagged iid output via Devetak–Winter hashing. The coarse operation preserves a Bell prefix in the reference while losing at most `D/(D-1)` pairs on average; individual kept digits are not measured. Continuity bounds the coherent information of the actual flagged state without charging its classical transcript dimension. Coding that actual state avoids the invalid `r eta` accumulation from tensoring a nearby ideal reference. Pure-output decoupling then supplies the complete joint target with its actual residual. Source: `PROOF.txt` lines 48–189.

The additional record/stock bound is

`(1-2epsilon)lnN <= rU + I(K^r:Z)/2 + E lnJ + g(epsilon)`.

Its initial record must have the explicitly stated classical conditional preparation with unchanged quantum state conditional on `K^r`; its Bell stock is independent of the complete preparation. These are substantial premises. The result is not a licence to substitute Shannon information into Cycle9's different max-likelihood resource theorem. Source: lines 191–257.

### An important classification consequence

At each fixed finite `D,L`, **the defined pooled rate is the ordinary two-way distillable-entanglement rate of `rho_L` under the same finite-round LOCC convention**. This follows directly from the delivered error bridge:

- A complete-joint protocol gives an ordinary marginal distillation protocol by tracing the residual.
- Conversely, any fixed-rank marginal Bell distillation protocol acts on the accessible marginal of the complete source. Its vanishing Bell infidelity implies vanishing distance to `Phi_N tensor ACTUAL residual` by the universal pure-output lift, irrespective of correlations in that residual.

This is a structural interpretation of the delivered proof, not a new solution of the coefficient frontier. It calibrates significance: complete clocks do not generate a separate asymptotic rate resource for this pure pooled target. The substantive theorem is a source-specific distillation deficit and access budget. It is not a new general memory-coding paradigm.

#### Complete equivalence proof under the matching convention

Fix finite integers `D,L>=2`. Let `rho_L=Tr_FM Omega` and define `D_two(rho_L)` by exactly the owner's asymptotic convention: supremum of `liminf_r (ln N_r)/r` over sequences of finite-round, finite-outcome LOCC operations, fixed Bell output rank `N_r` on all branches, no donated entanglement or correlated source access, and marginal Bell half-trace error tending to zero. Local apparatus must initially be separable and a tensor factor of the **complete** source. Classical randomness can be generated and communicated within such a finite LOCC operation; no correlated external seed is needed. This definition avoids importing a different one-shot error or LOCC-closure convention.

**Claim:** `R_pool(D,L)=D_two(rho_L)`.

**First inequality.** For any admitted pooled protocol, tracing its actual residual contracts trace distance:

`T(omega_Q, Phi_(N_r)) <= T(omega_QZ, Phi_(N_r) tensor omega_Z)`.

The accessible input marginal is exactly `rho_L^tensor r`. Thus the same physical protocol is an admitted ordinary marginal distillation protocol, with the same rank and at least the same rate. Hence `R_pool<=D_two`.

**Reverse inequality.** Take any admitted marginal distillation sequence with error `epsilon_r->0`. Implement its same finite local instruments on the `O` registers of `Omega^tensor r`, retaining all declared transcripts, work, inaccessible clocks and old memory as `Z`. The apparatus premise makes this the same channel on the same accessible marginal; no clock is measured or selected. Commuting the output partial trace with the channel proves that its `Q` marginal is exactly the marginal distillation output. Put `delta_r=1-Tr(Phi_(N_r) omega_Q)`. A two-outcome Bell test gives `delta_r<=epsilon_r`.

For completeness the needed lift is dimension independent. Let `P=Phi_(N_r) tensor I_Z`, `Pperp=I-P`, and `delta=Tr(Pperp omega)`. Since `P` has rank one on `Q`, `P omega P=Phi_(N_r) tensor A_Z`. Set `B_Z=Tr_Q(Pperp omega Pperp)`; the cross blocks have zero `Q` partial trace, so `omega_Z=A_Z+B_Z`. Positivity gives `||P omega Pperp||_1<=sqrt(delta(1-delta))`. The orthogonal positive blocks `Pperp omega Pperp` and `Phi_(N_r) tensor B_Z` each have trace `delta`, and their difference has half-trace norm `delta`. Decomposing the full difference therefore yields

`T(omega_QZ, Phi_(N_r) tensor omega_Z) <= sqrt(delta(1-delta))+delta <= 2 sqrt(delta)`.

Thus the same marginal protocol, with no additional processing, has pooled error at most `2 sqrt(epsilon_r)->0`. Its output rank and rate are unchanged, giving `D_two<=R_pool` and equality. The equality is at fixed `D,L`; it supplies neither a uniform finite-size bound in `L` nor efficiency. It also does not identify finite-error regions, since the error functions differ. It cannot be used for the old variable per-queue pure-profile task, whose demanded output depends on inaccessible `K` and is not one fixed pure pooled state.

## 5. What the new evidence changes about Astra

### A more precise causal story than “better representations”

The two successes support **freedom to replace an auxiliary construction or output specification when the intended scientific use is preserved and the change is declared**. They do not support free redefinition of success.

- Pro1 fixes the task and changes a feasible input family. Its old logarithmic loss was a method cost, not a law of nature.
- Ultra changes a previously over-demanding output attribution requirement but keeps a clear operational utility: fixed entanglement usable for teleportation. It pays the loss of the old guarantee explicitly.
- Both reuse established mathematics heavily. Their new science lies at the interaction between exact native constraints and that machinery.

A blanket prescription to “invent representations” could distract a branch whose real obstruction is a missing experiment, unavailable constitutive law, or an impossible task. A blanket instruction to “never change the task” would have prevented Ultra's useful reformulation. The transferable judgment is distinguishing physical/resource invariants from the provisional proof target. That judgment has not been automated or causally improved by this cycle.

### Analytical ablation on the real obligations

The actual sources support a useful, demanding leave-one-operation-out analysis without rerunning their suites:

| Remove or replace | What the actual proof says fails | What this establishes |
|---|---|---|
| Pro1 all-coherence response; keep generic `k||Z||_1` | The fixed-`bk` repair budget cannot be transferred by that estimate | The broader response interface is load-bearing, not decorative algebra |
| Pro1 pattern normalization; use raw trace-normalized square | Exact charge bias grows as `sqrt(k)` and makes bulk drift outward | Positivity alone is insufficient; the new feasible family fixes a physically relevant constraint |
| Ultra observable preprocessing; directly hash `rho_L` | Raw coherent information `lnD-lnL+C_L` is negative for large `L` | Known hashing alone does not yield the claimed rate |
| Ultra actual-state coding; tensor the approximate reference | The error bound grows as `r eta` at fixed `eta` | A good one-queue surrogate is not a sufficient asymptotic coding input |

This is source-based analytical necessity within these constructions, not an experimental causal estimate for agent strategies. It reveals **complementarity**: improving only one component can leave the result impossible or unproved. That is a stronger explanation than counting reused lemmas or assuming any one “creative representation” produced the success.

#### Strong conventional control and the scope of each ablation

The table must not be read as a universal lower bound on scientific discovery or on all alternative methods. Its four entries have different logical strength:

1. **Generic response control:** using only the offered `O(r²)` remainder estimate and `k||Z||_1` leaves a bound with a growing `k` factor at fixed `r`. This is a failure of that supplied sufficient certificate, **not** a proof that every trace-norm argument or every alternative input fails. A conventional investigator is allowed to derive sharper state-specific trace estimates, use a different positive input, or change the drift proof. The current full-response theorem supplies one successful solution, not a unique possible solution.
2. **Raw square:** the complete operator lower bound proves actual outward bulk drift for this specified raw-square family for all sufficiently large `k` at fixed positive `r` and fixed positive square coefficient. This is stronger than failure of a bound. It excludes that family as a strict inward-drift input at that scale. It still does **not** exclude all quantum codes using such states, every square/congruence family, the older clipped state, or the channel itself.
3. **Raw hashing:** negative one-copy raw coherent information means the unpreprocessed single-letter hashing lower certificate is uninformative. It does **not** prove zero distillable entanglement, impossibility of all one-way coding, or failure of every ordinary distillation protocol. In fact the ordinary periodic preprocessor already gives useful actual coherent information and is a mandatory strong finite-size control. The delivered `O(log L)` receiver has an asymptotic order advantage over that particular `O(sqrt L)` analytic fallback, while its conservative guarantee loses badly for several finite parameters. Other conventional preprocessing is not excluded.
4. **Tensorized reference:** `r eta` is an unusable generic telescoping upper bound as `r` grows at fixed positive `eta`. It is not a lower bound showing the actual protocol fails, nor proof that all reference-based analyses fail. A conventional researcher may code the actual state, choose a justified decreasing `eta_r` and pay its resource costs, or exploit special structure to control the global error. The delivered bridge is a valid known-method repair, not a new impossibility theorem.

Consequently the defensible inference is **route-level complementarity of the actual sufficient proof**, with one family-level physical obstruction. There is no universal theorem that two prescribed ingredients must be jointly invented, and no evidence that an instruction to seek them would outperform strong autonomous science. A prospective ablation must give its conventional control access to the same old lemmas and full freedom to choose alternative inputs, analyses, interfaces preserving the use, and known distillation methods. Otherwise it would manufacture the claimed advantage by withholding the actual conventional solution space.

The most useful bounded test this cycle is therefore a source-bound necessity classification, not a contrived discovery contest: identify exactly which result survives each removal, distinguish failure of a sufficient bound from an actual counterexample, and compare with the strongest alternative already in the packet. These distinctions themselves change the programme interpretation. They do not establish a new general research strategy.


### A consequential, falsifiable meta-research hypothesis

**Hypothesis:** Astra's strongest existing scientific capital has option value that is missed when agents are asked only to sharpen the previous theorem. A capability can emerge from a jointly compatible change of target interface and construction even when every isolated method improvement fails. The bottleneck is reliable identification of which constraints are essential to the real use and which belong only to the current proof—not the amount of reasoning alone.

The two current successes motivate this hypothesis but are selected positive examples. It would be falsified as an adopted programme intervention if equally informed autonomous investigators, given equal ability to reformulate, consistently do as well or better, or if the apparently successful reformulations fail to preserve a consequential use. It is not yet a demonstrated general amplification method.

A serious future test should attach to a **native independently needed scientific obligation whose owner has not already supplied the decisive target language**. Freeze the physical assumptions and a real receiving use, then permit both strong independent baseline and candidate process to change representation, intermediate quantities and algorithms. The outcome must be a valid new result or an actual increase in reachable consequence under that use, with full design/integration cost. A donor-shaped matrix toy or another test whose answer is encoded by the provided invariant would repeat the earlier saturation problem. If no such current obligation is available, do not fabricate a convenient benchmark or occupy autonomous branches to obtain one.

One immediately justified practice is much smaller than a new mandatory workflow: when a branch reports success, retain the exact failed alternative and the explicit operational statement that distinguishes a useful reformulation from a weaker surrogate. The current owners already do this well. No new dashboard is needed.

It does not justify prescribing a single invention method across all branches, replacing autonomous research with benchmarks, or claiming self-improving scientific intelligence.

The most important programme distinction is now **better results from a growing mathematical stock versus a better process for generating consequential new questions and mechanisms**. The former is increasingly supported. The latter remains the evaluator's unresolved scientific subject. Treat the actual complementary mechanisms and honest changes of operational target as evidence to investigate, not as a generic prompt recipe to impose.
