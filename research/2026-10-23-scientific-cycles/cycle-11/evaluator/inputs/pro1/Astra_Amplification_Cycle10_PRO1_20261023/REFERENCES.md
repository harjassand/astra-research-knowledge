## Matrix-valued Fourier and concentration methods

Avraham Ben-Aroya, Oded Regev and Ronald de Wolf, *A Hypercontractive Inequality for Matrix-Valued Functions with Applications to Quantum Computing and LDCs*, FOCS 2008.

Primary text: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0705.3806 — Theorem 1.

The theorem controls a degree-weighted sum of Schatten norms of Fourier coefficients for matrix-valued Boolean functions. It is a meaningful ordinary comparator for norm suppression by charge distance. The current proof does not claim a new general hypercontractive inequality. It establishes the physical word cancellation, complete-pattern operator row bound and weighted partial-trace norm interface directly for the recurrent device, including its bulk scope.
