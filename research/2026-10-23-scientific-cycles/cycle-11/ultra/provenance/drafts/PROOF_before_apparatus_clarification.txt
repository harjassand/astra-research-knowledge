2. THEOREM AND CONSEQUENCES

Let q=D^-2 and c(q)=-ln(1-q)-q lnq/(1-q). In the product generalized Bell
basis let Y be the MATHEMATICAL label of rho_p, with distinguished symbol0
for Phi_D. Set C_p=H(K|Y). It satisfies 0<=C_p<=min{H,c(q)}.

Independently, for uniform a,b in {0,...,D-1}, embed their binary labels in
h=ceil(log2D) bits and define e=a XOR b. Put
 R_D(e)=#{a<D: a XOR e<D}/D^2, r0=R_D(0)=1/D,
 kappa_D=H(R_D)/(1-r0)
         =-ln(1-r0)-r0 ln r0/(1-r0)+H(R_D(.|e!=0)).                      (3)
Thus kappa_D is an explicit finite constant; for powers of two it is
D lnD/(D-1). For every integer D>=2,
 kappa_D<=D lnD/(D-1)+ln2.                                              (4)
The full-XOR record used here is intentionally simple; its constant is not
claimed optimal. No field of order D is assumed.

THEOREM. Define a stopped record V and retained MC state theta_v by Section3,
 c=H(V), d=sum_v p(v) I_c(theta_v), G_p=d-c.
Then
 max{k0 lnD, G_p} <= R(p) <= B-H/2+C_p/2 <= B.                           (5)
The last expression B-H/2+C_p/2 is <=B since C_p<=H. In particular
 max{0,(H-c(q))/2} <= B-R(p) <= H+kappa_D.                               (6)
For every finite-r admitted receiver with actual error epsilon<1/2,
 (1-2epsilon)lnN_r <= r[B-H/2+C_p/2]+g(epsilon),
 g(x)=(1+x)h_2(x/(1+x)).                                                (7)
The stronger exact scalar lower expression is
 G_p=B-H(K)-H(V|K)+H(K|X,V),                                            (8)
where X is the mathematical phase-Bell label of the retained MC state.
Neither X nor Y is supplied as physical side information.

Consequences. For EVERY family of known finite priors at fixed D with

 H(V|K=k)<=H(G)+H(E|E!=0)=kappa_D.                                     (11)
Combining (10) with c=H(V) gives
 d-c>=B-H(K|V)-H(V)=B-H(K)-H(V|K)>=B-H-kappa_D.                         (12)
For (4), H(E|nonzero)<=ln(2^h-1). For powers of two the exact law is uniform

4. PHYSICAL COMPRESSED CLASSIFICATION AND ITS PAID COST

A binary parity of any selected bit functions of Alice's and Bob's site
labels, across any selected source copies, is measured by appending ONE Phi_2,
locally XOR-controlling X on its two halves, then measuring those halves.
If their measured bits are u,v, the source Kraus operator is exactly
 K_uv=2^-1/2 P_(parity=u XOR v).                                        (13)
The redundant bit is uniform. Thus the operation preserves every coherence
within the queried XOR sector; it does not measure an individual Schmidt
digit. Every parity consumes one ebit. This is the established partial-
breeding primitive, explicitly credited, not an uncharged LOCC measurement.

For a block of m iid queues, stage i queries only active copies with preceding
site-XOR labels zero. Let s_i be their probability and H_i the entropy of
the h-bit E_i conditional on that event. Conditional on ideal prior histories,
the active copies' next labels are iid with that fixed law; the activity
counts have their ordinary typical concentration. Random binary linear hashes
of these h-bit strings permit decoding at rate H_i/ln2 bits per active copy.
For a typical set of size<=exp[n(H_i+eta)], use at least
n(H_i+2eta)/ln2 rows. Distinct binary strings collide with probability2^-rows,
so atypical probability plus exp(-n eta) bounds decoding failure. If the hash
would cost more than hn rows, reveal the h bits of all n labels instead.
For n<sqrt(m), literal revelation also costs o(m) per stage. Matrices can be
generated independently locally and sent, with their full descriptions charged.
All outcomes remain finite. Codes/rates may be selected to rational slack
above the known entropy; computability/oracle precision is not free.

There are only L stages at fixed source. The stopped chain rule is
 sum_i s_i H_i=H(V)=c.                                                  (14)
Provide a deterministic stock ceil[m(c+delta)/ln2] of Phi_2 pairs and abort
on cumulative overflow, unexpected count, atypical string or ambiguous decoder.
Choose finitely many small stage slacks whose total is <delta. Concentration,
typicality and a union bound show overflow/first decoding failure beta_m->0.
On abort retain the flag and output a fixed product filler. Nothing is
postselected and no branch borrows more stock than the fixed budget.

The source is block diagonal in ALL E labels, INCLUDING its inaccessible clock.
Couple it to the ideal stopped instrument using those mathematical sector
labels. On the event every decoder is correct, (13) gives exactly the ideal
retained state, with extra random masks and hash descriptions ignored by the

For the ideal iid flagged MC block, ordinary actual-state hashing gives any
