ASTRA CYCLE11: STOPPED XOR SOURCE CODING AND SEED ELIMINATION
Bounded receiving lemma for the root's actual-state construction
23 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane

Scope. This file proves source-specific implementation and resource lemmas.
It does not prove the root's entropy cancellation, optimal rate, historical
novelty or physical advantage. All logs in this file are base two. A Bell
qubit means Phi_2. The number of source queues, n, is independent of L, and
limits take n->infinity at fixed FINITE D,L and fixed known inventory law p.

1. EXACT SOURCE AND THE IDEAL STOPPED INSTRUMENT

For every queue K has its actual known prior p on {1,...,L}. Conditional on
K=k its first k accessible pairs are Phi_D and the remaining pairs are
nu=(I_D/D) tensor(I_D/D). Complete hidden clocks and old memory are retained,
inaccessible, as in the governing source. The n COMPLETE queues are iid.
All apparatus and any fresh local random generator initially factor from the
COMPLETE source. No initial shared seed, supplied inventory record or hidden
clock access is used in the following instrument.

Embed each local basis a in {0,...,D-1} into h=ceil(log_2 D) binary digits,
with zeros on unused basis states. At site i define the mathematical joint
label E_i=a_i XOR b_i in {0,1}^h. This label is not granted as accessible
information. Let P_e^(i) be its joint spectral projector. Each Phi_D prefix
pair is entirely in the E_i=0 sector; each nu tail is diagonal. Consequently
the ACTUAL COMPLETE source commutes with every P_e^(i), and with their joint
products for all sites and all copies. This holds before tracing hidden clocks
or old memory. Coherence inside the E_i=0 sector, essential to the Bell prefix,
is retained. It is not a measurement of Alice's or Bob's individual digits.

The ideal stopped instrument reveals E_i for each still-active queue, ending
that queue at its first nonzero E_i. Thus V=(U,E_U) for a first nonzero site
U, or V=star if every E_i is zero. It leaves all inactive later sites
unmeasured. The following coded instrument approximates this COMPLETE ideal
instrument on this actual source, including all its public records, retained
work, hidden registers and correlations. It is NOT a diamond-norm simulation
of the ideal instrument on arbitrary inputs with inter-sector coherence.

2. PHYSICAL ONE-EBIT NONLOCAL PARITY

For one binary linear row r on selected embedded local digits, start an exact
independent Phi_2 in local target ancillas. Each party CNOTs its selected data
digits into its own ancilla half, then measures that ancilla in the Z basis.
Let the outcomes be a,b in {0,1}; exchange BOTH outcomes. If rE denotes the
row parity of the XOR-label vector, the complete branch Kraus map is

 K_(a,b)=2^(-1/2) P_(rE=a XOR b).                         (1)

This follows directly on each computational-basis vector: initially equal
ancilla bits become a=t XOR r x and b=t XOR r y for t=0,1. The original
data vector is otherwise unchanged. Thus (1) is valid for every embedded D,
including D not a power of two. The local outcome a is a uniform bit independent
of the data conditional on the parity; b=a XOR rE. Each call CONSUMES one Bell
qubit, uses two local measured bits and exchanges two classical bits. All
control outcomes and seeds are retained or explicitly exported. Multiple
rows commute and implement their joint parity projector with the appropriate
uniform local outcome strings. No postselection is used.

3. FINITE FIXED-BUDGET HASHING LEMMA

Let s_i=Pr(E_1=...=E_(i-1)=0), P_i the conditional law of E_i given survival,
and H_i=H(P_i). Terms with s_i=0 are omitted. Because V exactly determines the
stopped sequence and conversely, the ordinary chain rule gives

 H(V)=sum_(i=1)^L s_i H_i.                               (2)

For the actual source all s_i>0, since K>=1 and finite D. Formula (2) does
not assume that E_i itself is accessible. It determines the asymptotic cost
of physically measuring only the stopped information through (1).

THEOREM. For every fixed delta>0 there is, for all sufficiently large n, a
finite TP finite-round LOCC protocol supplied with exactly

 c_n=ceil(n[H(V)+delta])                                 (3)

independent Bell qubits, whose full output on the n actual COMPLETE queues
is within half trace distance e_n->0 of the full ideal stopped instrument.
The ideal comparison includes the SAME freshly generated hash seeds and
uniform local parity outcomes, with b=a XOR(actual row parity), and the actual
complete source, not a tensor power of an approximate reference. Extra blank
record slots/unused stock are padded by the declared deterministic convention.
Every decode/overflow/timeout flag remains an output; unsuccessful branches
are neither discarded nor declared safe. Applying any identical subsequent TP
processing to both outputs preserves this bound.

All layouts are finite and fixed: reserve hLn stopped-label/flag slots and
c_n parity-outcome slots, with blank values for unused slots; matrix-seed
slots use the finite maximum c_n h n. The ideal stopped instrument always
reveals its full TRUE stopped labels. Its appended auxiliary-record generator
runs the declared hashing/cost-tracking procedure using those true labels,
up to the same stock cap. If that auxiliary generator reaches the cap it pads
all remaining parity/seed slots, sets overflow, and still retains the true
stopped labels and ideal stopped quantum state. Thus the target is the FULL
stopped instrument, including on overflow, with a bounded redundant record
layout. This mathematical comparison does not claim that the ideal instrument
can physically reveal remaining labels without further stock. Actual and
ideal records and quantum blocks agree before the first bad event; the rare
overflow branch is included in the error estimate.

Construction and explicit error bound follow. Put lambda=delta/(8L). Choose
known rational coding rates R_i with H_i<R_i<=H_i+lambda when H_i<h. If a
chosen R_i>=h, use the literal alternative below. No accuracy assumption is
made for unknown p. Exact rational p suffices for a runnable finite likelihood
decoder and certified entropy interval. More general computable p requires
its stated precision modulus and interval computation. A free uncomputable
prior description or efficient coding oracle is not being supplied.
If H_i=h set R_i=h and use literal revelation; no positive coding gap or
Hoeffding term is required for that literal stage.

Put a_n=ceil(sqrt(n)), t_n=ceil(2 log_2(n)+log_2(L)+2).
At stage i let A_i be the active index set and N_i=|A_i|. On N_i<a_n reveal
all hN_i bits literally using one row per bit. Otherwise, if
m_i=ceil(N_i R_i)+t_n>=hN_i, again reveal all bits literally. In all other
cases generate a uniform binary m_i by hN_i matrix M_i using fresh private
Alice coins; send its FULL seed to Bob, then use (1) for every row. Decode
uniquely, if possible, among

 T_(i,N_i)={x: P_i^tensor N_i(x)>=2^(-floor(N_i R_i))}.    (4)

Choose a fixed supported guess and set an explicit ambiguity/failure flag if
there is not a unique candidate. The stage's inferred nonzero labels leave
the active set; inferred zero labels continue. All parties apply the same
public decoder, including on failed branches. Before every parity operation
test the deterministic remaining stock. Before generating a matrix seed or
starting a literal stage also compare that stage's ENTIRE prescribed row count
to the remaining stock, and overflow immediately if it cannot fit. If exhausted,
take a declared TP
overflow branch, do no more parity operations and output fixed guesses and
all remaining source registers/records. There are at most L stages, each
with at most c_n finite operations. This defines a TP operation on EVERY input.

4. CODING ERROR AND SOURCE-SPECIFIC FULL-INSTRUMENT COUPLING

Fix one stage on the IDEAL stopped history. Given the complete earlier stopped
labels of all queues, its active index set is known and its active labels
are iid with law P_i. This follows from independence of complete queues; each
surviving queue is conditioned only on its own earlier zero labels. Earlier
uniform local ancilla outcomes and freshly chosen matrix seeds add no source
information beyond their already revealed parities/labels.

The typical set (4) has size <=2^floor(N_i R_i). For any distinct binary
strings x,y and uniform M_i, Pr(M_i x=M_i y)=2^-m_i. A union bound over other
typical strings shows

 Pr(stage decode error |N_i) <=
 Pr[-log P_i^tensor N_i(X)>floor(N_i R_i)]+2^-t_n.        (5)

There is no efficiently executable decoder claim: enumeration can cost
2^(hN_i) likelihood tests, each followed by a matrix-vector calculation.

Let gamma_i=R_i-H_i>0, and B_i=max_(e:P_i(e)>0)(-log P_i(e)). When B_i=0,
the law is deterministic and the atypical event is zero. For all sufficiently
large n, N_i>=a_n implies floor(N_i R_i)>=N_i(H_i+gamma_i/2). Hoeffding then
bounds (5) by

 exp[-N_i gamma_i^2/(2 B_i^2)]+2^-t_n
 <=exp[-a_n gamma_i^2/(2 B_i^2)]+2^-t_n.                (6)

Literal stages have zero decoding error. Only finitely many i occur. This
proves a vanishing bound uniformly over possible ideal active counts, even
when s_i is small. No assumption that every N_i is close to n s_i is used for
the decoding bound.

The full-instrument comparison has an especially simple proof on THIS source.
Introduce the complete mathematical XOR-label string as an unobserved cq
extension of its orthogonal block decomposition. This does not physically
measure or supply the label; it preserves the exact original source marginal.
Generate the same matrices and local uniform bits for the actual and ideal
operations. Given any full label string, each physical hash row acts by the
scalar branch (1) and otherwise leaves the conditional source block unchanged.
Until the first decoding error or overflow, actual and ideal active sets,
guesses, all complete quantum blocks and all public/private branch records
are IDENTICAL. The nonselective exact E measurement in the ideal operation
does not additionally disturb an already block-diagonal source. The ideal
comparison can append the same uniform local outcomes and corresponding row
parity strings because these are redundant functions of its revealed labels.

Thus their complete state-valued coupling disagrees only on the first bad
event. Its half trace distance is at most that event's probability. A union
bound over the L IDEAL stages uses (6). Incorrect later actual guesses need
no separate estimate. This is a source-specific classical-block argument,
not a free measurement of a hidden clock or a generic gentle-measurement claim.

5. DETERMINISTIC BUDGET AND OVERFLOW

In the ideal process N_i is the sum of n iid Bernoulli(s_i) indicators. The
indicators need not be independent across stages. Set zeta=delta/(8Lh).
With probability at least 1-2L exp(-2n zeta^2), simultaneously
|N_i-ns_i|<=n zeta for every i. On that event,

 sum_i N_i H_i <=nH(V)+n zeta sum_i H_i
                <=nH(V)+delta n/8.                     (7)

The number of rows for a coded/literal-by-rate stage is at most
N_i R_i+t_n+1. Stages forced literal by N_i<a_n add at most Lh a_n rows.
Using N_i<=n, R_i<=H_i+lambda, and (7), total ideal row count is at most

 nH(V)+delta n/4+L(t_n+1)+Lh a_n.                       (8)

For sufficiently large n the last two terms are at most delta n/2, so (8)
is below (3). This threshold is finite and computable from fixed D,L,delta
and the specified coding-rate gaps. Hence overflow on an otherwise-correct
coupling is bounded by the complement probability in (7).

One complete explicit bound, after this threshold, is

 e_n <=2L exp(-2n zeta^2)+L 2^-t_n
       +sum_(i coded,B_i>0) exp[-a_n gamma_i^2/(2 B_i^2)] ->0. (9)

If one includes zero-probability/unsupported ideal records, their branch is
defined arbitrarily but TP. Unused stock and all record slots are carried
forward or deliberately exported. No good-branch inventory claim is made for
overflow or decoding failures. All deterministic c_n Bell qubits are charged,
even if the actual branch consumes fewer. They can be reset/discarded on both
actual and ideal outputs by an identical local channel if a fixed residual
layout is wanted. That channel's entropy export is part of the resource account.

6. SEED, COMMUNICATION AND EXECUTABLE COST ACCOUNT

At a coded stage the full matrix needs m_i hN_i fresh local coin bits. Alice
generates them after initialization, independently of the complete source,
and sends them explicitly to Bob. This is not an initial shared seed. The
whole protocol uses at most c_n h n seed bits, sends at most c_n h n seed bits
plus 2c_n local parity-outcome bits, and can store all these plus O(Lhn) labels
and flags. Literal stages use no random matrix. Padding unused coins is optional
but must be explicit; all actually generated seeds/outcomes are in the full
instrument comparison. A brute-force implementation takes at most
sum_i 2^(hN_i) likelihood tests, with a separately charged finite matrix cost.
Each row uses at most h n local CNOTs per party, so the total is at most
2 c_n h n such CNOT gates, plus local basis embeddings, measured ancillas,
classical reversible work and TP overflow handling. These are loose worst-case
bounds, not an efficient compiler or a physical runtime estimate.

7. EXACT INITIAL BELL SEED FROM A VANISHING FRACTION OF QUEUES

Every queue's FIRST physical pair is an exact Phi_D factor of its COMPLETE
remaining state, independently of K. One sacrificed queue can yield one exact
Phi_2 even for odd D. Alice uses the POVM with unordered-pair outcomes
{u,v} subset {0,...,D-1}, u<v, and Kraus

 A_{uv}=(D-1)^(-1/2)(|u><u|+|v><v|).

The effects sum to I. On Phi_D, each outcome has probability
2/[D(D-1)] and produces (|uu>+|vv>)/sqrt2. Send the pair label; both parties
isometrically relabel u,v as 0,1. The resulting Phi_2 is exact and factors
from all source/measurement records because every corrected branch is the
same pure vector. Keep/discard the other registers only by an explicitly
counted channel. Thus c_n extra source queues prepare c_n exact Bell qubits
with finite LOCC, no entangled aid and at most c_n ceil(log_2 binom(D,2))
fixed-label bits (zero bits at D=2). The input source uses of these queues are
charged, including their sacrificed remaining entanglement. No claim that
this seed extraction is optimal is made.

8. GENERAL ZERO-ADDED-AID RECYCLING LEMMA

Suppose, on n iid MAIN complete queues, an assisted finite TP LOCC A_n takes
exactly c_n deterministic Bell qubits and yields d_n deterministic Bell qubits,
with full joint error epsilon_n->0. Specifically its actual full output is
within epsilon_n of Phi_(2^d_n) tensor its ACTUAL complete residual. Assume

 c_n/n ->c<infinity,   d_n/n ->d,   d>c.                 (10)

Unused reserve is counted in the assisted channel or explicitly discarded;
all stock preparation and source inputs use the same convention. Then without
any added entanglement the source achieves net rate at least d-c Bell qubits
per original complete queue, with deterministic Bell rank and vanishing
global error relative to the ACTUAL final complete residual. The statement
does not assert an efficient compiler or finite-block optimality.

Proof on a subsequence first makes the bookkeeping transparent. Prepare c_n
exact seed Bell qubits from c_n separate queues by Section 7. Run j equal
batches of n fresh main queues. Reserve c_n of the d_n output qubits for the
next batch; keep d_n-c_n untouched. After the last batch release the reserve
as well. For large n, d_n>=c_n, so the stock never runs negative on the ideal
execution. The prescribed deterministic final number is

 q_(n,j)=j(d_n-c_n)+c_n,
 r_(n,j)=jn+c_n.                                        (11)

All unsuccessful branches use the same deterministic stock/output registers
and the same TP circuit; they do not need to recognize whether the stock is
good. A trace-distance hybrid replacing each ideal-input batch by its Bell
output comparison shows the total state is within j epsilon_n of
Phi_(2^q) tensor SOME residual state. This inductive comparison residual need
not equal the ACTUAL residual at intermediate times. Partial trace bounds
its distance from the final actual residual by j epsilon_n; one final triangle
inequality gives

 T(actual_final, Phi_(2^q) tensor actual_final_residual)
 <=2j epsilon_n.                                       (12)

This avoids incorrectly replacing a non-actual residual after every batch,
which would introduce an artificial exponential recurrence. Fresh main queues
initially factor from all earlier seeds, histories and complete residuals.
Global errors are never declared independent; (12) is a controlled hybrid sum.
Choose j=j(n)->infinity so slowly that j epsilon_n->0. Equation (11) gives
q/r->d-c and c_n/r->0. The initial resource is genuinely zero added aid; its
source-derived reserve costs only a vanishing fraction of ALL original queues.

For a full all-r rate, rather than a subsequence, take a known uniform error
bound/diagonal choice. Choose integer C>c and monotone thresholds N_j so that
for every n>=N_j, c_n<=Cn, d_n>=c_n, epsilon_n<=j^-3 and both ratios in
(10) are within 1/j. Choose j(r)->infinity slowly with j(r)<=sqrt(r) and
floor(r/[j(r)+C])>=N_(j(r)). Put n=floor(r/[j+C]). Then jn+c_n<=r; leave
any remaining input queues unused but counted in the total denominator and
complete residual. The unused fraction is O(1/j)+O(j/r). Equations (11),(12)
give deterministic q_r/r->d-c and global error <=2/j^2->0. If only existential
error convergence is known the diagonal schedule is existential; a runnable
schedule requires a computable certified epsilon_n bound. Formula (9) provides
such a bound for the stopped instrument; any later root coding stage must
likewise supply or explicitly distinguish its error schedule.

MARGINAL-ERROR VARIANT USED BY THE ROOT. If A_n is initially known only to
have Bell MARGINAL error f_n->0, the same seed construction works without
lifting each batch separately. Track the joint marginal of ALL previously
kept Bell registers and the reserve. Fresh complete main queues factor from
that marginal and from every old residual. Applying the next batch channel
and tracing its junk therefore gives an error recurrence E_(j+1)<=E_j+f_n:
the ideal input Bell marginal is a pure product of kept pairs and reserve,
and the batch's ideal-input Bell output has error at most f_n. Thus the final
whole Bell marginal has error <=j f_n. One final pure-output lift gives

 T(actual_final, Phi_(2^q) tensor actual_final_residual)
 <=2 sqrt(j f_n).                                       (13)

For completeness, if delta=1-Tr(Phi_Q omega_Q), write
P omega P=Phi_Q tensor A and Tr_Q(Pperp omega Pperp)=B. The actual residual
is A+B. Positivity bounds ||P omega Pperp||_1<=sqrt(delta[1-delta]); the
remaining two positive diagonal differences have trace delta each. Hence
T(omega,Phi_Q tensor omega_W)<=sqrt(delta[1-delta])+delta<=2sqrt(delta).
Since delta<=f for a Bell marginal f-close to its pure target, (13) follows.
There is no assumption that intermediate approximate reserves are exact or
uncorrelated with old residuals. Choose j so that j f_n->0. The all-r diagonal
schedule proof above applies with f_n replacing epsilon_n in that condition.

9. LIMITS AND KNOWN-METHOD BOUNDARY

Linear universal hashing, information-typical source coding, nonlocal parity
breeding and asymptotic seed recycling are known machinery. The contribution
of this bounded lemma is an explicit proof under the actual finite queue's
stopped XOR-label and full-residual contract. It cannot by itself establish
the root's claimed net d-c value: the root must prove its output entanglement
and exact entropy cancellation on the actual stopped state, and then combine
all finite errors. It also does not prove a useful blocklength, efficient
decoder, practical source formation advantage or historical novelty.

The theorem is source specific. If the source has coherence between different
XOR labels correlated with inaccessible registers, the Section 4 coupling is
not available as stated; coarse hashing is not the same quantum instrument
as revealing all stopped labels on such inputs. Independent apparatus must
factor from the entire source, not merely have an inventory-independent marginal.
