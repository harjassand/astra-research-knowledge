CYCLE11 BOUNDED PRIOR CHALLENGE: EXACT TWO-POINT ADMISSION
23 October 2026. Natural logarithms; half trace distance.

SCOPE AND DEPENDENCIES
This note starts from the actual Cycle10 complete-source/output convention.
It replaces the uniform inventory by a known two-point prior, and proves a
bounded constructive admission. It does not establish an arbitrary-prior
theorem or a sharp coefficient for the uniform-prior family. The decisive
new receiving observation is the exact reduction to isotropic states. The
constant-gap protocol uses established coherent parity queries, partial
breeding, hashing and seed recycling. A recent primary working preprint,
Eriksson, 18 October 2026, Theorem 2, provides the stopped-classification
architecture. Its priority, peer-review status and broad correctness are
separate from the special-case derivation below. No historical novelty is
claimed here. The first failed method is preserved in section 6.

1. ACTUAL TWO-POINT SOURCE

Take D=2, 1<=a<b, m=b-a, d=2^m, and 0<lambda<1. The actual inventory is
K=b with probability lambda and K=a otherwise. All clocks and the complete
old memory remain inaccessible, with the same actual classical law and
product old-memory state. Local apparatus factors from the entire source.
There is no added entanglement or supplied correlated record.

The accessible state factors EXACTLY as
  rho = Phi_2^a tensor gamma_(d,lambda) tensor nu^(L-b),
  gamma_(d,lambda)=lambda Phi_d+(1-lambda) I_(d^2)/d^2.       (1)
Thus arbitrary separation at constant inventory entropy is a genuine
high-dimensional isotropic-state admission, rather than a binary-search
exercise. Denote q=1-lambda, z=1/d and
  w=lambda+q z,
  e=lambda+q z^2,
  f=q z^2,
  v=w ln d+e ln(e/w)+(d-1)f ln(f/w).                        (2)

The deterministic-rank pooled task compares to the ACTUAL complete residual,
exactly as Cycle10. At fixed a,b and then r->infinity, the following rate is
achievable:
  R >= a ln2 + max{0,v-H(pi)},                              (3)
where
  pi_0=lambda+q 2^-m,
  pi_j=q 2^-j, 1<=j<=m.                                    (4)
Consequently, with mean supplied entanglement B=(a+lambda m)ln2,
  0<=B-R <= h2(lambda)+q ln5.                               (5)
For the nontrivial branch v>H(pi), the exact displayed achievable rate has
deficit tending to
  h2(lambda)+2q ln2,   m->infinity.                         (6)
This is a dimension-independent gap, despite arbitrarily distant inventory
values. It is not a proof that this achievable endpoint is optimal.

2. A LOW-ENTROPY CLASSIFIER OF AN INACCESSIBLE DIFFERENCE

Use the d-dimensional computational paired basis, with bitstrings j,k of
length m. Let Z=j XOR k and J be the first nonzero bit of Z, with J=0 for
Z=0. This label is initially mathematical only. The state (1) is block
diagonal in Z: all off-diagonal coherence lies inside the Z=0 block. Direct
calculation gives (4). The equality block is
  B_eq = lambda/d * 11^T + q/d^2 * I_d,
with trace w and normalized eigenvalues e/w, f/w (multiplicity d-1).
It is maximally correlated, has maximally mixed local marginal and coherent
information ln d-S(B_eq/w). Its weighted coherent information is exactly v.

The primitive measuring a parity of local binary control functions consumes
one Bell qubit pair. Apply controlled X from each local source control into
the corresponding Bell half, then measure the two ancillary halves. If their
outcomes are u,v, the source Kraus operator is
  2^-1/2 P_(u XOR v),
where P_y projects onto the indicated XOR-parity eigenspace. The redundant
bit is uniform and reveals no Schmidt index. The same construction measures
a parity of such controls across any subset of source copies. Each query
costs exactly one ebit; all source coherence inside its eigenspace survives.

For n iid copies, at stage ell classify the ell-th XOR bit only on copies
whose previous bits were zero. Discard a copy at its first nonzero bit.
Put r_ell=pi_0+sum_(j=ell)^m pi_j. Its bit-one probability is pi_ell/r_ell.
Binary typical-set hashing learns the entire stage string using asymptotic
  n r_ell h2(pi_ell/r_ell)/ln2
coherent parity queries, with vanishing decoding error. Fixed good hash
matrices exist by the random-linear-hash averaging argument. They may be
chosen and built offline; no free shared runtime seed is assumed. No
efficient method of selecting these codes is asserted.

There are only m finite stages at fixed source. Typical caps and an explicit
abort on atypical counts give fixed finite budgets. The stopped-prefix chain
rule gives total consumed entanglement per input copy
  sum_ell r_ell h2(pi_ell/r_ell)=H(pi).                      (7)
Although the number of possible stages grows with separation, the entanglement
cost is entropy of the stopped classifier, not that number. Correct decoding
implements the equality/mismatch projectors on the block-diagonal actual
source and does not measure individual Schmidt indices. The decoding failure
probability bounds the complete trace error, including the actual clocks,
not just an ideal source marginal. Ancilla outcomes and abort flags remain
in the actual residual.

Equality survivors are iid states theta=B_eq/w conditional on their retained
positions. Ordinary hashing of the actual survivor state yields v nats per
original copy. Typical counts and a fixed output below their typical yield
give a deterministic final Bell rank on every outcome; failed/atypical
branches are counted in the error. The initial a perfect Bell qubit pairs
of each ordinary source copy can otherwise remain untouched.

3. ALL INITIAL ENTANGLEMENT IS PAID

If v-H(pi)<=0, keep the guaranteed prefix and discard the tail. Otherwise
choose a sufficiently long block length ell. Tail classification and hashing
consume at most [H(pi)+eta]ell nats of Bell reserve and return at least
[v-eta]ell nats. Generate the initial reserve by sacrificing O(ell) original
complete source copies and taking only their a guaranteed prefix pairs. This
has a positive exact seed rate and needs neither a reduction criterion nor
an auxiliary resource theorem. Run t_ell->infinity blocks, recycling the
reserve and taking t_ell slowly enough that t_ell times the block error tends
to zero. The initial seed fraction is O(1/t_ell). Reserve conversion to the
required integer Bell ranks and output floors has vanishing asymptotic cost.
This gives net tail rate v-H(pi), while each processed ordinary source keeps
its original a-prefix contribution. Every retained or consumed reserve was
prepared from charged original source copies.

Finally, the Cycle10 pure-output argument lifts marginal Bell overlap
1-delta to half trace distance at most 2sqrt(delta) from Phi_N tensor the
ACTUAL complete residual. This includes all inventory clocks, old memory,
query outcomes, work, code records, abort flags and any retained environments.
Thus neither a reference residual nor physical access to Z or J was granted.
This proves (3) under precisely the requested pooled interface.

4. THE CONSTANT GAP

Let delta=q/d. The normalized equality survivor is a mixture of Phi_d of
unnormalized weight lambda and the maximally mixed correlated-diagonal state
of unnormalized weight delta. The entropy-of-mixture bound gives
  v >= lambda ln d - b(lambda,delta),
  b(x,y)=(x+y)h2(y/(x+y)).
Conditional on J>0, the law of J is a truncated geometric with entropy at
most 2ln2. Hence
  H(pi)<=h2(w)+2(q-delta)ln2.
The three-branch entropy identity gives
  b(lambda,delta)+h2(w)=h2(lambda)+q h2(delta/q).
Consequently the achievable deficit is bounded by
  h2(lambda)+q[h2(1/d)+2(1-1/d)ln2]
  <=h2(lambda)+q ln5,
because the bracket's maximum on [0,1] is ln5 (at 1/5).
Keeping the guaranteed prefix if necessary only improves this deficit.
As m->infinity, w->lambda, v-lambda ln d->0, while the explicit entropy
of (4) tends to h2(lambda)+2q ln2, proving (6).

5. COSTS AND CLAIM BOUNDARIES

This is asymptotic at fixed finite source a,b, followed by separation growth.
The runtime query parity controls can span many iid copies. Entanglement
consumption H(pi)/ln2 ebits per source copy is distinct from gate counts,
query support sizes, code generation, classical computation and required
blocklength. Those efficient implementation costs are UNKNOWN here.
There are m finite stopping stages and ordinary hashing. Fixed code choices
remove runtime randomness; if a randomized implementation is used, every
seed and its communication must be charged and retained as an actual record.
No external Bell stock, clock information, postselection or source creation
advantage is claimed. Equally informed conventional Bell stock can run the
same operation. This bounded receiving result says that an H(K)-only
achievable deficit is NOT contradicted by widely separated two-point priors.
It does not establish that general theorem or identify an optimal coefficient.

6. DECISIVE FAILED BRIDGE: SACRIFICIAL COORDINATE EQUALITY TESTS

Preserve a valid weaker receiver, and its limitation. Measure t tail pairs
locally in the computational basis, pass only if all outcomes match, and
discard the entire tail on failure. Let n=m-t, u=D^-t (here D=2). The pass
probability is s=lambda+q u. The untouched passing tail is exactly
  [lambda Phi_(D^n)+q u I/(D^(2n))]/s.
Leaving the guaranteed prefix intact and then coding the actual flags gives
the exact single-letter tail coherent information s Ic of that isotropic
state, truncated below at zero. The entropy upper/lower mixture bounds imply
that, whenever that tail coherent information is nonnegative, its deficit
from lambda m lnD lies between
  f_t=[lambda t+q D^-t(m-t)]lnD
and
  f_t+s h2(lambda/s).                                      (8)
If it is negative, discarding gives deficit lambda m lnD.

For fixed D and lambda in (0,1), minimizing (8) over integer t yields
  optimum deficit = lambda ln m+O_(D,lambda)(1).             (9)
For the upper bound take t=min{m,ceil(log_D m)}. For the lower bound, if
t<=m/2, then f_t>=lambda t lnD+(q m lnD/2)exp(-t lnD), whose real minimum
is lambda ln m+O_(D,lambda)(1). If t>m/2 its first term is linear in m.
The all-discard branch is likewise linear. Thus binary inventory entropy
alone does NOT justify a constant-deficit claim for the natural sacrificial
coordinate-check followed by raw flagged hashing. Its rare false-high
branch multiplies the retained dimension in the coherent information.
The stopped classification succeeds by amortizing coherent syndrome cost
across iid copies, rather than by making that per-copy false-high error tiny.

APPENDIX: EVERY INTEGER D, WITH AN EXPLICIT OFFLINE-SEARCH COST BOUNDARY

The same two-point rate and gap hold for every D>=2, with d=D^(b-a) and the
initial untouched prefix contributing a lnD. Only the simple explicit pi-law
in (4) is specific to binary d. Here is the finite replacement.

Embed local computational indices 0,...,d-1 into binary strings of length
n=ceil(log_2 d), padding locally with unused basis states. For an invertible
binary n-by-n matrix A, let J_A be the first nonzero bit of A(j XOR k), or
zero if j=k. Equality mass is still w=lambda+q/d, and all nonzero mismatch
mass is q(1-1/d). Uniform averaging over the finite group GL(n,2) sends
every nonzero j XOR k uniformly over the 2^n-1 nonzero vectors. Thus the
averaged conditional nonzero first-bit law is
  Pr(J=ell|J>0)=2^(n-ell)/(2^n-1), 1<=ell<=n.
Its entropy is <=2ln2. Concavity of Shannon entropy implies that at least
one FIXED matrix A obeys
  H(J_A)<=h2(w)+2(1-w)ln2.
Choose such an A offline from the exactly known finite source distribution.
This is a finite exhaustive existence procedure; its gate/code-selection
complexity can be enormous and no efficient search is claimed.

Each control bit is a local binary function of the local d-dimensional
index, so the same coherent parity primitive uses Bell QUBIT pairs, with
cost H(J_A) nats per source copy. Alice and Bob can implement the required
embedding and bit controls locally without measuring the local index. The
equality projector remains exactly j=k; the survivor's d-dimensional block
and v are unchanged. For a paid initial qubit seed, project initial-source
guaranteed prefix Phi_D pairs onto local span{|0>,|1>}; successes are exact
Phi_2 with probability 2/D. Announce outcomes, collect sufficiently many
successes and count all failed/unused original seed copies in the vanishing
initial fraction. This gives a strictly positive seed rate for each fixed D.
Subsequent survivor hashing is ordinary qubit-entanglement distillation and
recycles those qubit reserves. The untouched Phi_D prefix and final qubit
Bell stock combine by local relabelling into one deterministic Bell rank.
All previous actual-residual and failure accounting remains applicable.

Therefore (3) holds with H(pi) replaced by this selected H(J_A), and the
uniform finite gap (5) holds with B=(a+lambda(b-a))lnD. The sharper displayed
asymptotic equality (6) is asserted only for the explicit D=2 classifier.
