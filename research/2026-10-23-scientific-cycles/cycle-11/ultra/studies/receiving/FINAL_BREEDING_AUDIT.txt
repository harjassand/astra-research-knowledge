The full actual source, including inaccessible K clocks and old memory,
commutes with each joint XOR projector. The physical parity branch is the
stated scalar projector P/sqrt2, so it leaves each complete XOR sector
unchanged and has an independent uniform redundant measurement bit. This
supports a state-valued coupling on the orthogonal sector decomposition.
It is source specific and does not assert diamond-norm simulation on states
with inter-sector coherence. Composite/nonpower D is covered by local binary
embedding; no Z_D field or free inaccessible-sector measurement is used.

The final theorem explicitly fixes finite layouts for true/inferred stopped
labels, ancilla outcomes, hash seeds and unused stock. Its ideal comparison

2. FIXED RESOURCE BUDGET AND DECODING ERRORS: PASS

The c_n=ceil[n(H(V)+delta)] Bell-qubit budget is deterministic. A complete
stage's row count is checked BEFORE generating its seed or starting parity
operations, and every subsequent row is guarded by the remaining stock.
There is no branch borrowing beyond c_n. Ambiguous/absent candidate and
overflow branches are finite TP branches, with every flag/record retained.
An atypical actual label string can decode wrongly to a unique typical
candidate; equations (5)-(6) count that event without falsely heralding it.

For a stage with N_i>=ceil sqrt(n), the finite typical set has cardinality
at most 2^floor(N_i R_i), and random binary linear collisions occur with
probability 2^-m_i. The given likelihood bound and Hoeffding exponent in
(6) are conservative and valid. Literal stages have no decoding error and
the small-count literal alternative has O(sqrt n) overhead per fixed stage.
Ideal active counts have Bernoulli concentration; independence between
different stages is unnecessary for the finite union bound. Equations (7)
and (8) leave positive asymptotic slack under the deterministic cap. Thus
(9) tends to zero at fixed finite D,L,p and chosen rate gaps.

The apparent first-error recursion does not require conditioning the future
on a wrong history. The union bound is taken over ideal histories; before
the first failure the actual history is identical to that ideal history.
Past correctly learned stopped labels condition each active copy only on
its own prior zeros. Previous hash seeds and parity outcomes give no further
future-label information once that stopped history is specified. This is
why the iid conditional next-label law used for source coding is legitimate.
