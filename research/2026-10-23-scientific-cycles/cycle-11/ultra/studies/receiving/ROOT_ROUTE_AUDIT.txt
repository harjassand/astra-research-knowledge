Therefore these are genuinely commuting quantum-label sectors, although
their labels initially are inaccessible and must be learned operationally.

no algebraic failure; the coherent embedding uses only local isometries.

4. H(V|K) AND THE CLAIMED CONSTANT

Let P_D(e)=D^-2 # {(a,b):0<=a,b<D, a XOR b=e}. Its zero mass is alpha=1/D.
Given K, the first K symbols are zero. Subsequent symbols are iid with law
P_D. Thus the uncensored stopped waiting time and first nonzero symbol have
entropy
  kappa_D=H(P_D)/(1-alpha).
The finite end-of-queue sentinel is a deterministic censoring of that
uncensored pair, so H(V|K=k)<=kappa_D, including k=L where it is zero.
No inventory-spacing or maximum-length factor enters this entropy bound.

For every D>=2,
  kappa_D <= [D/(D-1)]lnD+ln2.
Here is a proof of the slightly nonobvious final constant. If D is a power
of two, H(P_D)<=lnD. Otherwise 2^h<=2(D-1), and
  H(P_D)<=h ln2<=lnD+ln2+ln(1-1/D)
             <=lnD+(1-1/D)ln2.
The last step uses ln(1-x)<=-x<=-x ln2. Divide by 1-1/D. For powers of two
the same final bound is weaker but valid. The exact scalar H(P_D) is often
sharper; for D=2, kappa_2=2ln2.
