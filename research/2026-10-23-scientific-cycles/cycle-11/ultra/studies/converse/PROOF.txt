1. EXACT SOURCE ENTROPY FOR AN ARBITRARY FINITE INVENTORY PRIOR

Fix integers D,L>=2. Let p=(p_1,...,p_L) be any known probability vector,
allowing zero entries. Let K have law p and
 sigma_k=Phi_D^tensor k tensor nu^tensor(L-k),
 nu=(I_D/D) tensor(I_D/D),
 rho_p=sum_k p_k sigma_k,
 B_p=E[K] lnD,   q=D^-2.
The COMPLETE source includes the inaccessible duplicated current clock K and
the original independent old memory nu^tensor L exactly as in Cycle10.
The physical receiver sees rho_p, not the clock or the labels below.

Choose the product orthonormal generalized Bell basis, with distinguished
symbol 0 denoting Phi_D at each coordinate. Its full classical label is Y.
Conditional on K=k, the first k symbols are 0 and the remaining L-k symbols
are independent uniform on D^2 labels. Let J be the number of leading zero
symbols of Y and G=J-K. The labels Y,J,G are mathematical variables only.

Define s_j=sum_(k<=j) p_k q^(j-k). Then
 P(J=j)=(1-q)s_j for j<L;   P(J=L)=s_L.                 (1)
For every j with s_j>0 the posterior is
 P(K=k|Y)=P(K=k|J=j)=p_k q^(j-k)/s_j, 1<=k<=j.          (2)
Thus C_p:=H(K|Y)=sum_j P(J=j)H((p_k q^(j-k)/s_j)_(k<=j))
is an exact O(L^2) scalar formula, with zero-probability terms omitted.

In particular,
 0<=C_p<=min{H(p),c(q)},
 c(q)=-ln(1-q)-q lnq/(1-q).                              (3)
PROOF. Given K=k, G has the distribution of min(T,L-k), where
P(T=g)=(1-q)q^g for g>=0. Hence EG<=q/(1-q). Since K=J-G and J is a
function of Y, H(K|Y)=H(G|Y)<=H(G). For any nonnegative-integer law with
mean m<=q/(1-q), nonnegativity of relative entropy to that geometric law
gives H(G)<=-ln(1-q)-EG lnq<=c(q). All variables here are finite, so no
entropy-domain issue occurs. H(K|Y)<=H(K) is immediate. This proof does not
assume a uniform prior or a geometric posterior under a nonuniform prior.

Since H(Y|K)=2(L-EK)lnD, the chain rule gives
 S(rho_p)=H(Y)=2(L-EK)lnD+H(p)-C_p.                       (4)
Both local marginals of rho_p are I_(D^L)/D^L. Therefore
 I(A:B)_(rho_p)/2=B_p-H(p)/2+C_p/2=:U_p.                 (5)
The exact scalar identity replaces a D^(2L)-dimensional diagonalization.

2. ZERO-AID FINITE-ERROR CONVERSE WITH THE ACTUAL COMPLETE RESIDUAL

Let r COMPLETE queues be iid with the declared prior p. Local apparatus
factors from their entire source; no entangled aid, inventory record or clock
access is supplied. Let a finite-round LOCC receiver declare deterministic
Bell rank N on every outcome, with actual complete residual W, and satisfy
 T(omega_QW,Phi_N tensor omega_W)<=epsilon<1/2.            (6)
Then
 (1-2epsilon)lnN<=rU_p+g(epsilon),
 g(x)=(1+x)h_2(x/(1+x)).                                 (7)
Consequently the vanishing-error asymptotic pooled rate obeys
 R_pool(p)<=B_p-H(p)/2+C_p/2,
 B_p-R_pool(p)>=[H(p)-c(q)]/2.                           (8)
The second lower bound is useful when positive; B_p is also an upper bound
by conditioning the squashing extension on K. Thus one may always replace
its right side by max{0,[H(p)-c(q)]/2}.

PROOF. Use the known finite-dimensional squashed-entanglement monotone
E_sq=inf_E I(A:B|E)/2, exactly as proved/identified in Cycle10. A trivial
extension of rho_p and product extension of r iid copies give
 E_sq(rho_p^tensor r)<=r I(A:B)_(rho_p)/2=rU_p.
For any extension of output x_AB, weak monotonicity and strong subadditivity
give E_sq(x_AB)>=-S(A|B)_x. The Bell marginal is epsilon-close by contraction.
Finite-A conditional-entropy continuity gives
 -S(A_Q|B_Q)_omega>=(1-2epsilon)lnN-g(epsilon).
LOCC monotonicity proves (7). This uses no inaccessible-clock measurement,
no optimizer, no E_sq continuity assertion, and no bound on dim W. The actual
complete-residual requirement is stronger than the Bell-marginal consequence
needed in this converse. Achievability still has to prove that requirement.

3. ACTUALLY SUPPLIED CLASSICAL ACCESS AND EXPECTED LOG-RANK STOCK

Supply an ACTUAL initial classical record Z, duplicated at the two parties,
with arbitrary finite or countable law p(z|Kvec). Its preparation must retain
the conditional quantum source EXACTLY sigma_Kvec; source-factorization
cannot be replaced by independence of an auxiliary marginal. Let J_stock be
an independent initially known Bell-stock rank, jointly independent of the
COMPLETE original source and Z. Charge b=E ln J_stock<infinity, with no
assumption on E J_stock. The stock need not be returned.

For this contract the stronger exact finite-error bound is
 (1-2epsilon)lnN <= rB_p - I(Kvec:Yvec|Z)/2
                         + b + g(epsilon).                (9)
Its useful entropy-only consequences are
 RHS <= rB_p-H(Kvec|Z)/2+rC_p/2+b+g(epsilon)
     = rU_p+I(Kvec:Z)/2+b+g(epsilon),                       (10)
and the source term is capped by rB_p. A slightly weaker prior-independent
residual bound replaces rC_p by r c(q).
Because the actual preparation has the Markov relation Z<-Kvec->Yvec,
 I(Kvec:Yvec|Z)=rI(K:Y)-I(Yvec:Z).                         (11a)
Thus the exact right side of (9) is also
 rU_p+I(Yvec:Z)/2+b+g(epsilon),                            (11b)
which is at least as strong as (10), by data processing. This is a bound in
terms of the supplied record's relevance to the mathematical label witness,
not a claim that the label is physically observable.

PROOF. Given z, each local quantum marginal is still maximally mixed on
D^(rL). Repeating (4), now for the entire inventory vector and its Bell labels,
gives
 I(A:B)_(rho_z)/2=E[sum_a K_a|z]lnD
                  -H(Kvec|z)/2+H(Kvec|Yvec,z)/2.
Classical Z is an allowed mathematical squashing extension of the actual
accessible flagged state; average this display over z. It is exactly
rB_p-I(Kvec:Yvec|Z)/2. Moreover
H(Kvec|Yvec,Z)<=H(Kvec|Yvec)=rC_p,
since the unconditional COMPLETE queues and label channels are iid. Equation
(10) follows. Squashing further on Kvec gives the source cap rB_p.

Conditional on an independent stock label j, tensor its pure Bell state with
the same extension. Its contribution to half conditional mutual information
is ln j, giving the average b. For countable labels, apply the finite-output
conditional-entropy argument branchwise, mix a finite set and a tail, and
bound the tail coherent information by its probability times lnN. Let the
tail probability tend to zero. The source contribution is uniformly bounded
by rLlnD and E ln J_stock is finite. This is the same finite-output truncation
argument as Cycle10; it does not require infinite-dimensional continuity or
the false replacement E ln J_stock=ln E J_stock.

All initial-record formation, communication, encoding and correlated source

4. EXACT COUNTEREXAMPLE TO A GLOBAL MULTIPLICATIVE ENTROPY-DEFICIT LAW

For any D>=2, 1<=m<L, and 0<p<=1/(D+1), let
 P(K=m)=1-p, P(K=m+1)=p, all other probabilities zero.
Then the ACTUAL accessible state factors as
 rho_p=Phi_D^tensor m tensor zeta_p tensor nu^tensor(L-m-1),
 zeta_p=(1-p)nu+pPhi_D.                                   (11)
The middle state is separable in this parameter range. Hence
 R_pool(p)=m lnD,
 B_p-R_pool(p)=p lnD,
 H(K)=h_2(p).                                              (12)
As p->0, [B_p-R_pool(p)]/H(K)->0. There can therefore be no universal
strictly positive D-dependent multiplicative lower constant times H(K),
even for just two adjacent inventory values. The additive O_D(1) correction
in (8) is essential at low entropy. This does not contradict an entropy-order
law in a regime H(K)->infinity or an upper deficit bound O_D(H(K)+1).

SELF-CONTAINED FINITE SEPARABLE DECOMPOSITION. Let Delta=sum_i |ii><ii|.
For independent phases u_i uniform on {1,i,-1,-i}, put
 |psi_u>=D^-1/2 sum_i u_i|i>.
A direct fourth-root moment calculation gives the finite product mixture
 E_u[|psi_u><psi_u| tensor |bar psi_u><bar psi_u|]
       =(I+D Phi_D-Delta)/D^2=:S.
Indeed its (ij,kl) coefficient is 1/D^2 exactly when the multiset {i,l}
equals {j,k}; this gives the two contractions I and D Phi, subtracting their

Thus
 (D/(D+1))S+(1/(D+1))(Delta/D)
       =(I+D Phi_D)/(D(D+1))
       =(1-1/(D+1))nu+(1/(D+1))Phi_D
is separable. Convex mixing with nu proves separability for every stated p.

For the operational lower bound keep the first m Bell pairs of each queue,

5. SCOPE, COST AND RECEIVING USE

The new source-specific converse identifies inventory ENTROPY, rather than
support size alone, as the quantity constrained by unknown-inventory pooled
operation. It does not prove general-prior achievability, the sharp one-half
coefficient, a finite-error strong converse, efficient quantum codes, physical
formation advantage, or historical novelty. The known squashed-entanglement
and entropy-continuity machinery is inherited explicitly from Cycle10.

Formula evaluation costs O(L^2) arithmetic operations and O(L) storage as
written; exact rational bit cost depends on prior denominators and D,L.
The executable admissions are small, finite exact/float consistency checks,
not a simulation of a distillation code and not a proof by enumeration.
The finite separable decomposition in Section4 has 4^D+D terms and is an
analytic witness, not an efficient state-preparation implementation claim.
Total discovery/inference costs and physical usefulness remain UNKNOWN.

Receiving use for principal integration: pair (8) with a source-preserving
entropy-adaptive receiver if one is proved; test its prior dependence against
(12). For optional access, the exact obstruction (9) refines the simpler
Shannon budget and isolates the residual inaccessible information. Do not
claim a complete entropy-deficit law on the basis of this converse alone.
