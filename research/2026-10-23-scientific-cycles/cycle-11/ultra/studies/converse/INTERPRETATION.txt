1. WHAT THE PROPOSED ACHIEVABILITY WOULD ESTABLISH

Let p be any known finite prior on K in {1,...,L}. Set
 B=E K lnD, H=H(K), C_p=H(K|Y), c_D=c(D^-2),
 Delta=B-R_pool(p).
Suppose the principal construction proves, with the actual complete residual,
 R_pool(p)>=B-H-kappa_D,                                  (A)
where kappa_D>=0 is finite and depends only on D. Keep-first and the known
source-supply bound make R finite and 0<=Delta. The converse then gives
 max{0,(H-c_D)/2}<=Delta<=H+kappa_D.                       (B)
The exact lower side is (H-C_p)/2; using c_D only makes it universal.

Consequences, with no support-size assumption beyond finiteness:
* For EVERY family of known finite priors with fixed D and H->infinity,
  Delta=Theta_D(H). The support or largest possible inventory may grow
  arbitrarily fast relative to H.
* Uniformly over ALL finite priors, including H=0,
  Delta+1=Theta_D(H+1). In fact c_D<=c(1/4)<1, so
  (H+1)/2 <= Delta+1 <= (kappa_D+1)(H+1).                  (C)
* If H=o(B) along a family with B->infinity, R_pool/B->1.
  This follows from an entropy-adaptive receiving theorem, not merely the
  uniform-prior logarithmic result or a substitution of support size.

Do NOT claim Delta=Theta_D(H) uniformly through low entropy. The exact
separable-tail example in converse/PROOF.txt has Delta=p lnD and
H=h_2(p), so Delta/H->0. At deterministic inventory H=0, Delta=0 exactly.
An additive regularization is essential. Also, (B) does not resolve a leading
coefficient for high entropy: it leaves a factor-two gap between 1/2 and 1.
The exact C_p term can be useful at small entropy; replacing it by c_D loses
information. The equivalence with ordinary two-way distillable entanglement
under the established interface remains unchanged. No supply, formation,
computational or physical advantage over the same-informed ordinary source
follows from (B).

2. IID ACTUAL INITIAL CLASSICAL ACCESS: CONDITIONAL ENTROPY LAW

Prepare iid COMPLETE queue/record pairs by the ACTUAL classical law p(k,z),
with duplicated accessible record Z at Alice/Bob, conditional quantum input
EXACTLY sigma_k, inaccessible clock k and original old memory retained.
Source-factor apparatus is otherwise zero-aid. Let B remain the unconditional
E K lnD. For every z of positive probability, the conditional quantum source
has prior p(k|z), the same finite L, and the same source structure.

If (A) is proved for every such prior, then
 R_pool(p,Z)>=B-H(K|Z)-kappa_D.                           (D)
The exact converse is
 R_pool(p,Z)<=B-I(K:Y|Z)/2
           =B-H(K|Z)/2+H(K|Y,Z)/2,                       (E)
with 0<=H(K|Y,Z)<=min{H(K|Z),C_p,c_D}.
Thus the entropy-controlled bounds (B),(C) apply with H replaced by H(K|Z).

PROOF OF (D), FINITE Z. For each z run the conditional receiver/coding scheme
for prior p(k|z). If the principal receiver directly supplies actual flagged
coherent information >=B_z-H(K|z)-kappa_D, ordinary hashing on that ACTUAL
finite flagged state immediately averages these inequalities. Alternatively
the asymptotic RATE assertion (A) alone suffices: among r iid record labels,
choose a predetermined slightly subtypical count of copies for each z;
concatenate finite block codes on those groups. The probability of missing
any of the finitely many required group counts tends to zero. Each selected
group has the actual iid conditional COMPLETE source, and finite union of
the per-group code errors tends to zero. Declare a deterministic concatenated
Bell rank; use a product fallback on atypical counts. Its marginal Bell
fidelity tends to one, hence the established pure-output lift compares it
with its ACTUAL complete residual, including all original Z and clocks.
Taking rates arbitrarily close and the count slack to zero gives the average
sum_z p_z(B_z-H(K|z)-kappa_D)=B-H(K|Z)-kappa_D. Negative
individual lower bounds cause no problem because a zero rate is available.

COUNTABLE Z. First choose a finite set S of actual labels with tail probability
delta. Retain that finite discrimination/control and put all labels outside S
into one fallback branch. Missing average source supply is <=delta LlnD;
conditional K entropy is <=lnL. At fixed L these quantities vanish as S grows.
The finite-alphabet construction therefore gives rates arbitrarily close to
(D). Original uncoarsened labels remain in the ACTUAL residual; the final
pure-output lift has no finite-residual-dimension assumption. This is a
supremum-over-finite-protocol argument, not a free infinitely large classical
controller, unbounded-round protocol or efficient record-readout claim.

The initial record's actual formation, encoding, communication and the
conditional-code controller are resources. An INTERNAL transcript is not
such a supplied record: conditioning on it usually changes sigma_k. The
lower (D) is for IID supplied records. It does NOT automatically become
B-H(Kvec|Z)/r-kappa_D for arbitrary collective Z. Independent perqueue
receivers in that setting only charge sum_a H(K_a|Z), which can be much
larger than H(Kvec|Z). A receiving operation exploiting those conditional
correlations would be new work. The finite-error converse, however, does
allow arbitrary collective Z under its stated actual preparation.

3. EXACT FINITE-ERROR ACCESS/STOCK CONSEQUENCE

For arbitrary actual collective Z and independent preselected Bell stock
J with b=ElnJ, the source-specific exact bound is
 (1-2epsilon)lnN <= rU_p+I(Yvec:Z)/2+b+g(epsilon),
 U_p=B-H/2+C_p/2, g(x)=(1+x)h_2(x/(1+x)).                 (F)
This follows from the Markov chain Z<-Kvec->Yvec and source-preserving
conditional quantum preparation. Y remains a mathematical variable; this
does not grant a nonlocal measurement or hidden-clock readout.
Write delta=B-lnN/r, including any supplied-stock benefit in the output.
Rearrangement gives the necessary resource budget
 I(Yvec:Z)+2b >= r[H-C_p-2delta-4epsilon(B-delta)]
                        -2g(epsilon).                     (G)
The weaker inventory-information budget replaces I(Yvec:Z) by I(Kvec:Z).

The finite-error term matters. To reveal an entropy-sized deficit in a
SIMULTANEOUS large-L/finite-error comparison, this particular bound requires
epsilon B=o(H), or an explicitly retained correction. Taking r->infinity
at fixed finite L with epsilon_r->0 is sufficient for the established rate
definition. An unspecified error limit is not a uniform finite-L theorem.

Expected log-rank stock is a valid converse COST, not automatically reliable
deterministic-rate supply. Example: at blocklength r, let J=1 with probability
1-1/r and J=ceil(exp(r^2)) with probability 1/r. Then ElnJ/r->1. On the
dominant J=1 branches no extra stock exists. For a deterministic inventory m,
the source is exactly Phi_D^(mr) times a separable tail; every no-stock LOCC
output has Schmidt number <=D^(mr). Its overlap with Phi_N is <=D^(mr)/N.
For lnN>=r(m lnD+a), a>0, total Bell overlap is at most
 (1-1/r)exp(-ra)+1/r ->0.
Thus global error tends to one, despite the positive expected stock budget.
Deterministic independently known stock, in contrast, can simply be
concatenated. A converse in b is not a full achievability/formation theorem.

4. NORMAL INDEPENDENT LOCAL WORKSPACE: EXPLICIT CLOSURE LEMMA

The finite-dimensional squashed-entanglement argument must not silently
assume a theorem about arbitrary infinite-dimensional intermediate systems.
The following approximation is sufficient and keeps that argument finite.

LEMMA. Fix r,L,D,N finite and an actual finite-round, finite-outcome NORMAL
LOCC protocol whose Bell output has dimension N by N. Allow arbitrarily
large, even infinite-dimensional, local workspaces initially in normal
trace-class local product states, tensor-factor from the COMPLETE source.
Every Bell marginal produced by that protocol on the fixed finite source
is a trace-norm limit of Bell marginals from finite-dimensional finite-round
LOCC protocols with no extra entanglement. Therefore (7),(9),(F) extend to
that normal-workspace class by a finite-output limit.

PROOF. First approximate each initial local normal state by a normalized
finite-rank truncation in trace norm. Tensoring these local approximations
does not create entanglement or source correlations. At each of the finitely
many outcome-history nodes and local operations, consider the ideal reached
normal trace-class state. Finite-rank projections increase strongly to the
identity, so choose finite local output projections with arbitrarily small
leakage on every such reached state. A compression channel can be written
 C_P(X)=PXP+Tr[(I-P)X]|0><0|,
where |0> is a fixed unit vector in ran P. It is local CPTP and has finite
output support. On a joint state with leakage t outside P, the gentle
projection inequality gives
 T(state,(C_P tensor id)(state))<=sqrt(t)+t/2.
Applying both local compressions has the sum of their bounds. All history
sets are finite, so leakages can be chosen to make their total error as
small as desired. A telescoping hybrid argument and trace contraction bound
the final Bell-marginal error by the initial truncation error plus that
finite sum. Reachability weights can equivalently be kept as subnormalized
trace-class branch states; zero-probability branches are immaterial.

Between successive finite-support compressions, restrict each original
normal CP instrument to the finite input support and compose with its
finite output compression. This is a genuine finite-dimensional CP
instrument, with the original finite classical outcome set and trace
preservation when outcomes are summed. It therefore has a finite local
implementation; no infinite-dimensional E_sq statement is invoked. The
resulting finite protocol acts trivially on inaccessible clocks/old memory.
It need only approximate the Bell marginal of the actual fixed source for
this converse. Uniform strategy-norm approximation is not required.

Let its Bell marginal be omega_Q^(t) with
T(omega_Q^(t),omega_Q)->0. From the actual joint-error assumption, its
distance to Phi_N is <=epsilon+o(1). Apply finite-dimensional E_sq
monotonicity and finite-A conditional-entropy continuity to each approximate
protocol, then take the error to zero with N fixed. This yields exactly
the same bound at epsilon. No continuity assumption on the full residual
or infinite workspace is used, and no claim is made that the approximate
protocol preserves the ACTUAL original residual. Only its Bell marginal is
needed by the converse. Branchwise finite source/stock ranks plus the
finite-output truncation argument in converse/PROOF.txt handle countable
paid initial labels. Countably many initial record values are not confused
with an unbounded outcome tree at fixed protocol.

If independent normal SEPARABLE initial apparatus beyond local product
states is explicitly allowed and priced, approximate it by finite convex
mixtures of finite-rank local product states in trace norm before the above
argument. Such mixtures are free only if the declared contract actually
allows that independent classical preparation. Arbitrary shared entangled
apparatus, nonnormal states, infinite uncharged stock, nonfactor source
correlations or an infinite-round/outcome protocol do not follow from this
lemma. All retained workspace is still counted in the actual target.

5. REMAINING SCIENTIFIC GAP

If the principal receiver establishes (A), the new question becomes a
prior-universal sharp entropy coefficient or a tighter ACCESS conversion,
rather than whether support-size logarithms can be replaced by entropy.
The universal factor-two interval does not determine distillable
entanglement for a general prior, a finite-error strong converse, optimal
collective-record receiving, reliable yield from heavy-tailed random stock,
efficient coding, physical preparation advantage or historical novelty.
The low-entropy counterexample identifies where an exact multiplicative
entropy law is false instead of proposing to improve an invalid constant.
