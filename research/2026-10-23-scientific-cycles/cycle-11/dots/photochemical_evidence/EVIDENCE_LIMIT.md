# Dilute photochemical nitrite finishing: primary evidence and bounded conclusion

23 October 2026. Literature reconstruction only. No new experiment, fitted network, purchases, outreach, hardware, or old Wallace/Meroni/Monroe SI searches.

## Decision

There is positive primary evidence for ordinary bulk nitrite-to-nitrate chemistry. This pass did not recover a qualifying independent absolute original-N nitrate / delivered-photon endpoint. Do not interpret this as proof that no such measurement exists. The most concrete recovered dilute solar operation explicitly lacks actinometry, while the directly relevant nitrate-quantum-yield paper remains abstract-only. Thus no numerical SEC or delivered-photon advantage is certified.

## A. Actual dilute operation, explicit missing denominator

Spanggord, Chou, Mill, Haag and Lau, *Environmental Fate of Nitroguanidine, Diethyleneglycol Dinitrate, and Hexachloroethane Smoke*, Final Report Phase II, September 1987, SRI LSU-7706, contract DAMD17-84-C-4252. Primary author-uploaded report: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/235164180_Environmental_Fate_of_Nitroguanidine_Diethyleneglycol_Dinitrate_and_Hexachloroethane_Smoke

Report pp. 10–11 and 18–21: nitrite-only subexperiments began at 50 μM in 2 mM phosphate, pH 7, with distilled-water comparison and 1 mM pentanol or 10 mg/L humic-substance additions. Sunlight exposures used quartz tubes in August and October–November 1986. Nitrite loss and nitrate formation were separately measured by ion chromatography (Dionex 2000, AS4A, conductivity). Figure 5 follows both species and their sum. Authors describe complete conversion to nitrate in the humic-acid condition; with pentanol, 60% of disappearing nitrite was not recovered as nitrate. They explicitly state that these nitrite sunlight exposures had no actinometry and did not determine quantum yields. Nearby PNAP/PYR calibration belongs to xenon-lamp work and cannot be transferred to these runs.

Qualification: plot amplitudes, initial nitrate and reagent-N blanks were not independently verified from figure pixels; humic material may introduce other N. Therefore do not certify 50 μM net original-N nitrate, even though the authors' conversion statement is positive. No absolute photon denominator or useful nontrivial photon bound follows from days of exposure alone.

## B. Direct nitrate quantum-yield lead, not a certified delivered-dose endpoint

Fischer and Warneck, J. Phys. Chem. 100 (1996), 18749–18756, DOI https://doi.org/10.1021/jp961692+ ; publisher page https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/jp961692%2B .

Publisher abstract explicitly reports nitrate and phenol quantum yields from nitrite/HNO2 photolysis with benzene as OH scavenger over 280–390 nm. Nitrate yields are described as comparable to phenol yields. Nitrate production rate decreases with exposure for nitrite but increases for HNO2, unexplained by the authors. The numerical 0.069 to 0.022 and 0.35 yields in the abstract are OH yields, NOT nitrate yields. Do not relabel them. Benzene participates in the downstream chemistry, so this is not oxygen-only finishing.

Full article/PDF access was not obtained. The publisher search result explicitly says unavailable to current access; direct page retrieval returned 403. Starting concentration, absolute nitrate amplitudes, blanks, independent light calibration and incident/absorbed photon definition remain unverified. This is the most targeted next primary-source recovery, not a completed efficiency measurement.

## C. Treatment endpoint lead and product-retention warning

Wang et al., Chemosphere 184 (2017), 1003–1011, DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chemosphere.2017.06.078 ; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0045653517309815 .

Publisher abstract/preview reports simultaneous inorganic-N observations and maintained nitrogen balances in UV254/H2O2 experiments; nitrite oxidizes to nitrate while peroxide is present, but nitrate can photoreduce back to nitrite after peroxide depletion at high pH. The preview identifies analytical-grade salts, ultrapure water, and low-pressure 254 nm lamps. This is an actual product-retention boundary: a peak nitrate concentration is not necessarily the final retained endpoint under continued illumination. The accessible preview does not expose a nitrite-specific endpoint amplitude, calibrated delivered fluence, baseline/provenance controls, or dose uncertainty. The assertion that no N2 formed is their inference from the balance, not a separately recovered gas measurement.

## Accounting consequence

A narrow endpoint comparison can be accepted without reconstructing every intermediate or full gas closure if independently bounded original-N nitrate and delivered dose are available. For this search they are not jointly available. The numerator must subtract bounded initial/imported nitrate and non-original-N contributions; the denominator must integrate optical input at a stated sample boundary. Absorbed-photon quantum yield cannot become incident-photon yield without absorption/geometry. Lamp nameplate watts cannot become delivered photons. Organic scavengers and peroxide must be accounted as resources. These are qualification rules, not new kinetic assumptions.
