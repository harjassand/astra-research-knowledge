## What is and is not identified

The positive scientific conclusion is that a conventional dark peroxide operation produces predominantly nitrate from dilute nitrite under the source conditions. This is a meaningful comparator, not merely disappearance or a normalized growth curve. The stated non-nitrite reagents do not intentionally introduce nitrogen, and initial nitrate is measured. Exact original-nitrite provenance still depends on excluding changing contamination/interference and on correct dilution/calibration. Isotopic labeling is one possible check, not mandatory by definition.

The nitrate amount divided by **nominal** dosed peroxide can be reconstructed under an explicit collection history. A stronger independently calibrated delivered-dose interval is not established: independent stock assay, full IC calibration/recovery uncertainty, exact sample volumes and cross-time covariance were not identified. That limitation does not erase the published conversion observation.

The peroxide is charged in excess. One mole per mole is balanced reaction stoichiometry, not measured reagent consumption or charge/product. Residual peroxide and its recovery were not measured in the inspected record. A dark process has no specified treatment-photon denominator; division by zero is undefined, not infinite photon efficiency.

The blank supplies nitrite but not nitrate time courses. Its nitrite slowly declines, so a peroxide-caused **nitrate increment** cannot be estimated by inventing a matched blank nitrate curve. The process output itself remains measurable. Source SDs are replicate scatter, not complete calibration bounds or confidence intervals. Our coordinate-wise one-SD box is labeled sensitivity only. The separate inorganic-N sum is derived from the two ions, not independent total-N evidence.

No gas-emission identity is inferred from the small inorganic-N shortfall. No fertilizer-grade product, full optical/electrical SEC, thermal reset, acid/base recovery, peroxide manufacture or steady throughput advantage is asserted.

## Strong alternatives and failures that changed the decision

The direct photochemical search found real bulk nitrate formation but not a qualified common delivered-photon endpoint. Fischer/Warneck1996 measures nitrate quantum yields, but accessible text did not establish the needed absolute data/dose interface; its numerical OH yields must not be relabeled nitrate. The SRI1987 dilute sunlight experiment explicitly lacked actinometry. Wang2017 reports a product-retention risk under continuing UV after peroxide depletion, but the full paired endpoint/dose was not recovered. These finite limits are not claims that no such data exist.

We did not compare a peroxide point value with the old532nm **effective-initiation** energy floor as if both were measured nitrate efficiencies. Organic-peroxide source arms also have different endpoint times and molecular oxidizing equivalents; their calculations are descriptive, not a matched-quality/time superiority ranking. No new assay is invented to fill these gaps.

The ordinary H2O2 record is therefore the strongest current receiving result. Its advantage over our earlier evidence is identification of absolute product concentration in a relevant dilute operation. That is useful scientific evidence, not a novel chemical discovery, new apparatus, plant breakthrough or measured research-amplification effect.
