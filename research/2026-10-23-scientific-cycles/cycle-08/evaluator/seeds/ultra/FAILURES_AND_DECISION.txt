ONE CONTINUATION DECISION, WITH THE OBLIGATIONS IT REPLACES
22 October 2026. Mission ACTIVE.

The proposed cheap exact-MC normalization step is now CLOSED for an actual
full-support queue, beyond the old pairwise restriction. Arbitrary state-
dependent joint output-only SEP has Schmidt number at most the guaranteed first
Bell seed. More copies do not fix the corresponding zero-Q resource argument.

and finite interface price are proved, with exact optimal controls and score.
Thus merely dropping private entropy or invoking a joint LOCC normalizer does
not replace the expensive obligation. The missing access or replacement supply
must actually be provided, not inferred from a mathematical decomposition.

The result is an optimal receiving operation/structural obstruction on a fully
admitted source, not a favorable defect or a new reservoir advantage. Ordinary
stock and the strongest methods get the same source, access and DP. Its proofs
retain branches, output-memory correlations and complete hidden registers.

TYPE-doped causal family. The strong uniform calibration theorem has a constant
approximation obstruction but a stronger premise; it does not settle arbitrary

Other bounded routes: direct PODS tensorization cannot be cited; hierarchy is
outside that class in any basis. A critical unpriced ancillary inequality is
false. Exact support intersection is unstable even for exact Bell extraction

These are preserved failures at their precise scope, not reasons to abandon
the larger scientific mission or change domains speculatively.

Continue at approximate state-dependent normalization with explicitly priced
leakage, global epsilon and access, or ordinary hierarchical matrix entropy

a request for user strategy or benchmarks. No new recursion/rate/efficiency
