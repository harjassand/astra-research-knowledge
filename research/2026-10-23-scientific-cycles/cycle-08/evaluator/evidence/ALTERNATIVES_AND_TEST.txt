1. REVISED DIAGNOSIS

Cycle7 weakens an undifferentiated diagnosis of poor idea generation. The three
Pro returns already contain substantial invention: an exact prefix optimizer
and all-length collision formula (Pro1), a realizable four-family boundary
construction for both series parities (Pro2), and a reciprocal-proposal energy
argument plus a necessity theorem for matched powers (Pro3). Ultra has a general
product-Kraus support lemma and exact access/resource-price constructions. The
fact that these are not yet civilization-changing does not identify a missing
reasoning trick. Scientific significance, the generation rate of good ideas,
correctness and downstream utility are different unknowns.

In particular, the previous positive chain—capital retained, then reused, then
strengthened—continues. It does not demonstrate a causal improvement in the
mechanism that invents the next step. Nor does the absence of that demonstration
prove the mechanism has not improved. We lack enough controlled counterfactuals
and reliable total-cost observations to decide that broad causal question.

The common phrase “invent new representations” describes several successful
proofs retrospectively. It does not by itself define an executable learner.
A more specific, testable mechanism is replacing an unnecessarily strong
intermediate obligation while preserving the receiving theorem. Pro1's failed
bath-independent word identity was not needed to obtain a useful contracted
coefficient; retaining input/bath overlap changed a discarded sector into a
uniform positive contribution. Pro3's failed counting-column bound was not the
required energy estimate; reverse proposal probabilities and the true rejection
diagonal supplied it. Pro2's excluded atom exposes a grammar defect rather than
a recovery obstruction. These are important examples, but they do not show that
telling an agent to “weaken the right lemma” causes such inventions.

A second rival diagnosis is missing evidence. Dots' frontier is now constrained
by actual outlet speciation, energy disposition and a complete captured-product
boundary. Improved syntax, more subagents or exact reasoning cannot supply
unmeasured hardware facts. Whole-device observations can sometimes answer a
question despite incomplete microscopic closure; they cannot justify arbitrary
mechanistic transplantation. This remains distinct from mathematical invention.

2. THREE SERIOUS MECHANISMS

A. Learn executable abstractions, not merely named lemmas.

A completed proof can be mined into an operation that maps new scientific inputs
to a construction and a certificate. The operation must expose the premises
under which it is valid. Learning would comprise identifying that operation from
experience, enlarging a usable language of constructions, and learning when to
invoke it. The plausible benefit is a change in feasible search or expression,
not just less reading. This is the strongest locally executable mechanism.

Primary precedent: DreamCoder alternates symbolic-library acquisition with
neural search learning, including imagined and replayed tasks. Its results
support learning compositional programs in specified domains; they do not prove
an open-ended scientific invention mechanism. The important comparison is an
equally informed conventional algorithm using the same primitives and mature
search, not an agent denied existing background knowledge.
Source: Ellis et al., https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381

For Astra, the useful substrate is already present: Pro1's stack-word calculus,
Pro2's finite state interface, and Pro3's exact weighted-energy inequality. A
new prose summary alone is not a new executable abstraction. The test below
asks whether one completed native operation can actually produce and certify a
new receiving result. It does not claim recursive improvement from one transfer.

B. Jointly invent scientific constructions and their proofs.

A researcher need not finish a candidate before validation. Candidate operations
and the invariant needed to establish them can evolve together, with exact
failed obligations providing semantic feedback. The scientific question is
whether these failures identify a better admissible construction or a weaker
sufficient intermediate claim, rather than causing endless local patching.
This is stronger than a generic “critic agent” architecture.

Primary precedent: Inductive Deductive Synthesis builds code and formal proofs
jointly. The paper's evaluations concern formal distributed-system specifications,
and the authors identify specifying intended behavior as an outstanding problem.
Its performance reports do not transfer automatically to unformalized physics or
novel mathematical definitions. Native Astra proofs are presently not a complete
machine-checked training corpus.
Source: Agarwal et al., https://arxiv.org/html/2605.23109v1
Relevant source location: section 6, “The specification bottleneck.”

Astra can test a small proof-producing operation without converting the entire
repository to a proof assistant. A proof checker would certify a formalized
claim, not historical originality, the physical premises, or scientific value.
The new assumption interface must be included in the experiment cost.

C. Improve the acquisition of discriminating evidence.

Instead of scoring ideas, construct observations or counterexamples that separate
live explanations of a scientific obstruction. In mathematics these can be
admitted finite configurations; in physics they must be attainable measurements
or already available primary evidence. The aim is to change which model or
operation is worth pursuing, not maximize activity or prediction accuracy on an
irrelevant surrogate.

There is controlled primary evidence for counterexample feedback in symbolic
induction, but its clean oracle is a serious advantage over real science. A 2026
regular-expression study reports improved induction under counterexample-guided
reflection/repair. It also reports extra token cost and much smaller gains from
some prompt refinements once output syntax is clear. Astra must compare to mature
automata/constraint methods when those solve the stated task.
Source: Liu et al., https://arxiv.org/html/2606.11521v1
Relevant source locations: sections 5.2–5.5 and E.3.

For Dots, the strongest observation may be complete cooled-outlet and energy
information. No experiment in this review should invent those measurements or
spend money/outreach to acquire them. A model-based “optimal” observation is only
conditional on the admitted hypothesis class and measurement physics.

3. WHY NOT INSTALL AN EVOLUTIONARY CONTROLLER NOW?

AlphaEvolve demonstrates that evolving search programs under automated evaluation
can generate useful algorithms. Its construction includes task specification,
automated evaluators, repeated model samples and significant computation. The
paper explicitly limits its approach where manual experimentation is required;
its mathematical search settings can provide a 1000-second evaluation budget to
a heuristic, and some evaluations use large parallel resources. Those examples
are evidence for an available methodological family, not evidence that our next
Astra prompt should impose an evolutionary algorithm.
Source: Novikov et al., https://arxiv.org/html/2506.13131v1
Relevant source locations: sections 2.4 and 3.2.

We have not identified a new scientific evaluator with the reliability and
scope needed to make a large search campaign decisive. A numerical score can
also reward a weakened task, uncounted resource or representational artifact.
The earlier Astra comparisons already supplied real examples of that danger.
The sensible near-term experiment is to acquire a useful proof-producing
operation and test one new consequence, with strong controls, before building
or installing a large search controller.

Exact starting model

Let d>=2. The port is C^(d+1), with charge zero basis |0> and d color basis
vectors. The bath is C^2_seed tensor F(C^d), with prepend isometries S_j and
vacuum seed involution R. Set

 U = [[R,S_1,...,S_d],[S_1*,0,...,0],...,[S_d*,0,...,0]],
 V_s = cos(s) U + i sin(s) I, Q = sum_i (I-|0><0|)_i.

Use the same ordered physical interaction W_k and the depth>=k bulk coefficient
representation of the input-charge-pinched Q-W_k* Q W_k:

 G_k(b) = (2Q-kI) tensor I + b G_1 + O(b^2), b=sin^2(s).

All bath overlaps are retained; there is no reflecting cutoff. F=(C^d)^tensor k
is an analytic prefix space. For any full-rank color density P with Tr P=1,

 sigma_P = (|0><0|/2 direct_sum P/2)^tensor k,
 H_zeta = Tr_F[(I tensor zeta)G_1]+2Q-kI,
 v_k(P)=min_{zeta>=0,Tr zeta=1} Tr(sigma_P H_zeta^2).

Receiving obligation

Derive the exact all-k variational optimum, an explicit admissible prefix density
attaining it, and the correct operator dual statement for arbitrary P. Include
full coherent/entangled competing prefixes, rank changes of an optimizer as P
varies, and rational checks on declared small cases. Do not assume the old scalar
dual equality survives on all of F. No nonlinear coding theorem is requested
or licensed. The benchmark cannot be replaced by finite numerical optimization.

Use a strongly anisotropic rational case such as d=3 and
P=diag(4/5,3/20,1/20) to prevent the test being merely a dimension substitution.
A unitary conjugate checks that the answer is not tied to a diagonal input
encoding. A uniform d=3 case is a check, not the primary achievement. Root may
freeze a stronger general-d version before the receiving solve; the chosen
contract and its selection timing must be retained.

Candidate acquired operation

Extract the endpoint stack normal form and a valid weighted contraction rule
from the completed d=2 source. The operation must:
(a) preserve actual bath overlaps rather than discard non-restoring sectors;
(b) reduce the all-prefix problem to valid local matrix obligations;
(c) construct a single joint prefix attaining those local minima simultaneously;
(d) produce the operator inequality/equality and its support conditions;
(e) transfer the resulting sector weights through the exact all-length counting.

It is not enough to insert a favorable one-color state independently at each
location. The same admissible joint prefix must attain all sectors, including
those with exported input color. The source's repeated-color cancellation is a
potential reusable operation; the altered weighted trace and rank boundary are
receiving obligations that must be proved.

Independent contract check already completed

- Input-color permutations preserve product P weights because every colored wire
  has the identical P, and each endpoint block preserves total color count.
- Arbitrary H_zeta need not commute with sigma_P if zeta carries color coherences.
  Do not inherit the source's universal commutation assertion for the altered
  input. At a constructed optimum whose marginal is a spectral function of P,
  commutation may be proved and used there.
- The new optimum can be rank deficient. The appropriate certificate can be an
  operator inequality with equality on the optimizing support, rather than the
  old scalar identity on the whole prefix space.
- Repeated-eigenvector joint mixtures are admissible full prefixes. Their
  usefulness must be proved by linearity of the actual contraction, not an
  assumption that coherent competitors are diagonal.
- The old entropy/coding transfer does not follow. In the anisotropic model,
  -log(sigma_P) contains color observables beyond Q.

Strong conventional control

Give the control the complete owner source, the same changed model, the same
available symbolic/numerical tools and the same requested proof standard.
Allow ordinary convex optimization, spectral methods, exact arithmetic, generic
constraint solvers, and standard dynamic programming. Do not force it to reason
without inherited sector classifications or to enumerate the full exponential
matrix if its analytic reasoning can avoid that. A compiled-operation condition
receives the newly acquired operation; its extraction and proof cost must be
charged. This is new-capital transfer, not an equal-background prompt experiment.
If both conditions receive the compiled operation, it instead tests execution.

Do not handicap the control by asking only for an eigenvalue estimate or hiding
a load-bearing counterexample. The local matrix minimization belongs to mature
convex methods and may make both approaches succeed quickly. That outcome is
informative: the new generalization is a positive scientific result, but no
advantage of a novel discovery procedure has been established.

Measurement and interpretation

Preserve complete proof, source identity, counterexamples, attempts and actual
compute receipts. Score correctness and the full all-k consequence first.
Measure actual acquisition, solve, checking and integration costs where visible;
unavailable model costs remain unknown. A comparison of two single agents is
not a statistical estimate of a strategy's success rate. The root already read
and selected the originating proof, so this is not blind rediscovery or an
unseen historical test. Held-out variation after freezing an operation tests
robustness, not historical novelty.

A positive receiving derivation would demonstrate that the acquired operation
has more scope than its source result. It would not prove a generic invention
advantage, historical novelty, practical quantum usefulness, or recursive
intelligence growth. A second independently selected scientific use is needed
for a broader transfer claim; a later operation actually enabled by this one
would be needed even to start testing a second generation.

5. ADOPTION RECOMMENDATION

Preserve the autonomous architecture. The new sources justify continuity and
careful mining of completed operations. They do not justify a central selector,
a mandatory reasoning recipe or replacement of scientific owners by benchmark
roles. Adopt the constructive transfer only if its complete new consequence is
proved; do not market its name as the result.

The larger ambition should remain acquisition of reusable, premise-sensitive
scientific operations that can change future feasible reasoning. The concrete
next experiment above is intentionally a test of that mechanism, not a claim
that this modest mathematical variation has civilization-level significance.
If the strong conventional control matches it, retain the science and reject
the claimed procedural advantage. That preserves useful progress while leaving
the more ambitious intelligence question genuinely open.
