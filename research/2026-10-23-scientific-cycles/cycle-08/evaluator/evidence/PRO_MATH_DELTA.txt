4. WHAT THIS CHANGES ABOUT THE META DIAGNOSIS

(i) These results contradict any claim that Astra merely accumulates unrelated
negative results. Each Pro finishes a prior substantive frontier and creates
an explicit new one through the same original model. Pro1 supplies a missing
premise to coding; Pro2 executes a stable inverse on an actual failed support;
Pro3 combines a widened upper with native adverse constructions into a structural
classification. Mathematical significance must still be assessed separately
from transformative consequence and historical priority.

(ii) “More precise formalism always causes progress” is also false. Pro1 needed
to DROP a stronger intermediate claim. Pro2 needed to ADD a stronger but locally
checkable representation. Pro3 needed to change the measure in a known inequality.
The productive choice was target-sensitive; no fixed direction of abstraction
is justified by these three successes.

(iii) There is observed transfer, but not yet learned invention. Pro2's old
quadratic proof becomes a stronger theorem about a new decoder; Pro1's prior
coding interface converts a new optimizer to an unconditional rate; Pro3's
capture/entropy proof transports through a new local energy operation. This
establishes cumulative scientific capital. No new trained rule, improved
proposal distribution, calibrated selection policy, or repeated-generation
performance gain has been measured. Treating all mathematical receiving use
as “not progress until recursive amplification” would be as misleading as
equating that use with demonstrated intelligence growth.

(iv) Conventional methods participate in the inventions. Star coloring, Jensen,
swap partial traces, symmetric-power integration and random-walk generating
functions are established tools. The new work lies in their exact interfaces
and consequences for the native question. “Reject anything subsumed by a
conventional ingredient” would incorrectly reject good scientific synthesis.
A superiority control is required for a claimed process/cost advantage, not
for the mere legitimacy of a theorem built from known tools.

(v) Current negative results are often a form of scientific localization, not
endpoints: an old interface fails -> an exact revised interface works -> the
proof exposes an additional scale or resource that was previously hidden.
The new unresolved nonlinear window, terminal load and proposal asymmetry
budget are scientifically motivated questions. Their significance cannot be
reduced to how many cycles were completed.
