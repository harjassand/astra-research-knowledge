## Decisive scoped negatives

**Three star colors do not suffice for this enlarged grammar.** The explicit
17-vertex expression is P2(S2(L,L),P3(L,S2(L,L),P2(L,L))) with L=path2.
Exact port propagation yields no three-color star state. A different direct
enumeration checked all 49,320 labeled proper three-colorings and found zero
star colorings. Combined with the new upper, its star chromatic number is four.
This excludes this particular stronger coloring representation; it is not a
lower against arbitrary real three-column designs or all acyclic colorings.

**The grammar is not all chordless atoms.** The unbounded family A_L has two
length-two paths a-b, two length-two paths a-c, and a length-L path b-c.
For L>=2 it is a chordless, triangle-free, two-connected, clique-cutset-free
series-parallel graph of maximum degree four, with exactly one degree-four
vertex. Every cyclic two-connected core of the grammar has an even number of
degree-four vertices. Thus no choice of exterior ports represents A_L. The
small A_2 is nevertheless star-three colorable. The obstruction concerns the
degree-one port grammar, not recovery difficulty. The proof, width-two
decomposition and exact structural diagnostic are included.

The original forced equal-terminal examples remain valid. The interface
accepts only realized states and charges actual terminal degrees. It neither
asserts arbitrary boundary extension nor obtains observations on reduced cores.
