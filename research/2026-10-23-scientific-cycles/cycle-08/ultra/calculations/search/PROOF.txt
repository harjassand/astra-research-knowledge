CONSERVATIVE FRACTIONAL-MEDIAN SEARCH WITH AN ARBITRARY INDIFFERENCE BAND
Cycle8 bounded independent construction by cycle8_search_protocol.
22 October 2026. Internal theorem/proof; novelty is not asserted.

1. Abstract finite search lemma

Let the unknown integer K belong to {0,...,L}. Query integers j in {0,...,L+1}.
Queries j=0 and j=L+1 are synthetic, deterministically YES and NO respectively.
Fix a nonnegative integer b. On every query, conditional on the full previous
transcript and K:
  j<=K: YES with certainty;
  j>K+b: a raw query is YES with probability at most 1/D;
  K<j<=K+b: any history-dependent behavior is permitted.
Every actual NO certifies j>K. Each raw query uses a fresh source of randomness
or a fresh, conditionally independent quantum difference coordinate. No iid
assumption is imposed inside the band. D>=2.

A logical query at j performs four raw queries and returns YES iff all four are
YES. Outside the band on the right, its conditional error is at most D^-4<=1/16.
Maintain U=min({L} union {j-1: an actual or synthetic NO was observed at j}).
Thus U>=K on EVERY branch, including search failure branches.

Put n=L+1. Choose positive integers T,s satisfying
  T>=32 log_2 n, T>=128 ln(2/epsilon), T>=32,
  D^-s<=epsilon/2.
An exactly verifiable choice for rational 0<epsilon<1 is
  T=32 ceil(log_2 n)+128 ceil(log_2(2/epsilon)),
  s=ceil(log_D(2/epsilon)).
All logarithms specifying these ceilings may be replaced by exact rational
integer-power comparisons. The count is deterministic, including every branch.

There are atoms t=0,...,L, initially of weight 1, represented by the intervals
[t,t+1]. At each of T steps find a weighted median point x=h+theta, with
0<=theta<=1 and atom h. Query the closest integer j: h if theta<=1/2, h+1
otherwise. Update the weights AS THOUGH querying x, using multiplier 1/2 on
the lower half if YES and on the upper half if NO. In the median atom use the
weighted average of these two multipliers. Re-uniformize its density inside
the atom. This is an algorithmic weight update, not a claimed exact posterior.
The query itself is at the physical integer j. Record all T queried integers.

The updated total weight is exactly 3/4 of the previous total, irrespective of
the answer. These are the unscaled proof weights. The stored integer weights
below are 4^a times them after a steps. If integer weights have total W and cumulative weight C before
atom h, the update after multiplying all new weights by 4 is:
  YES: t<h gets 2w_t; t>h gets 4w_t;
       h gets 4w_h-(W-2C).
  NO:  t<h gets 4w_t; t>h gets 2w_t;
       h gets 4w_h-[2(C+w_h)-W].
Thus integer arithmetic suffices, the new total is 3W, and all weights remain
positive. Query h iff W-2C<=w_h, otherwise h+1. All ties may follow these rules.

Call a query GOOD if j lies in [K,K+b+1]. The extra endpoint K+b+1 is harmless;
the proof can use the tighter interval [K,K+b] when b>=1. In the no-band case
b=0 use [K,K+1]. This convention ensures that whenever the median atom h=K,
the query is GOOD, since its integer j is K or K+1. On a non-good query the
target atom K is strictly on the correct side of the median and is not the
median atom. Its weight is unchanged on a correct answer and halved only on
an erroneous answer. On a good query its multiplier is at least 1/2 regardless
of the answer. If G is the count of good queries and E is the count of erroneous
non-good queries, then
  w_K(T)>=2^(-G-E),
  W(T)=n(3/4)^T,
so
  G+E>=T log_2(4/3)-log_2 n.                         (1)

Conditional on every previous history, a non-good wrong logical answer has
probability at most 1/16; left-side errors have probability zero. The errors
are therefore dominated by T independent Bernoulli(1/16) variables. This can
be proved by exposing independent uniforms realizing their successive
conditional probabilities, or by iterated moment-generating-function bounds.
Hoeffding gives
  Pr(E>T/8)<=exp(-T/128)<=epsilon/2.
On the complementary event, using log_2(4/3)>13/32 (equivalently 2^51>3^32)
and T>=32 log_2 n, (1) implies G>=T/4.

Sort the queried integers. Select ranks
  r_l=min(T,1+floor(l*T/8)), l=0,...,8,
and discard duplicate candidates. The gaps between selected ranks are at most
ceil(T/8). A contiguous cluster occupying at least T/4 ranks contains a selected
rank (T>=32). All good query values form one contiguous integer interval, so
at least one of the at-most-nine selected candidates is good.

For each selected candidate j, perform s additional raw queries at
  i=min(L+1,j+b+2).
Do ALL candidate validations; do not stop at the first apparent success.
Record every NO in U. For a good candidate we have i>K+b unless i=L+1 is the
synthetic NO. Therefore it produces a NO except with probability D^-s, and
  U<=i-1<=K+2b+2.
This statement fixes K and conditions on the entire first-phase transcript:
choose any deterministic good candidate as a mathematical function of K and
that transcript. The receiver does not make that choice and instead validates
ALL candidates. All the selected good candidate's fresh validation
queries retain the conditional collision bound. No union bound over candidates
is needed. Thus with probability >=1-epsilon,
  K<=U<=K+2b+2.                                     (2)
Always U>=K. The total raw query budget is at most 4T+9s.

For b>=1 the tighter good interval [K,K+b] is valid: the only additional case
is median atom K queried at K+1, which is already in that interval. Then validate
at i=min(L+1,j+b+1), obtaining U<=K+2b. This tighter version is used below.

2. Deterministic-color parity implementation on the actual queue

Take ANY integer D>=2, with the specified computational basis indexed by Z_D.
The deterministic all-one, disjoint-support rows below need no field assumption.
This distinguishes them from arbitrary random-coefficient F_D parity hashes,
whose 1/D collision law does require a prime-power field or another proof.

Actual L-slot output conditional on the mathematical inventory K in {1,...,L}
is Phi_D^tensor K tensor nu^tensor(L-K), where nu is maximally mixed product.
The mathematical conditioning is used only in the proof. No clock, inventory
record or hidden memory is queried by the operation.

Choose T,s above and define Q=4T+9s and b=Q. Color physical positions 2,...,L
cyclically with colors 1,...,Q. Raw query number a uses color a, which has NEVER
been queried before. Its threshold-j operator is the local mod-D sum of the
computational-basis values on positions <=j carrying color a. Each party
measures its own sum using a fresh local D-level ancilla and controlled-addition
gates, and sends the sum to the other party (or sends Alice's sum followed by
Bob's equality bit). YES means equality. Empty sums match deterministically.
Position 1 is excluded from every row, preserving the exact first Bell pair.

Conditional on K, write the classical computational-basis difference as e_i.
For i<=K it is exactly zero. For i>K it is independent uniform in Z_D. All
these diagonal parity measurements commute with every difference e_i. Their
full local outcomes on previous colors reveal nothing about unqueried color
coordinates, conditional on K, because those coordinates occupy independent
tensor factors. Adaptive threshold choice depends only on previous colors.

If j<=K, the parity difference is zero with certainty. If j>K+Q, the interval
(K,j] contains every color and hence contains at least one as-yet-unqueried
uniform tail difference of the current color, with coefficient one. Its sum is
exactly uniform mod D even for composite D, independently of the full previous
transcript. Thus the YES probability
is exactly 1/D. In the band K<j<=K+Q no property is required. This proves the
abstract oracle premise with b=Q even though no public random-row seed exists.
Each physical position belongs to at most one raw parity measurement. There
are at most Q raw calls, so no color is exhausted or reused.

Use the tighter b>=1 search form. It yields the certified U>=K on all branches
and U<=K+2Q except probability epsilon. Put
  c=max(1,U-2Q).
Then c>=K-2Q on all branches and c<=K on the good event.

3. Receiving quantum operation, including all branches

After all parity calls, locally measure every physical output position i>c in
the computational basis. Let H be the matrix of all measured parity rows
restricted to positions <=c. Each party subtracts its OWN measured-tail
contribution from its recorded parity outcomes, obtaining a syndrome u_A or
u_B. On every branch with c<=K, the first c input pairs were all Bell pairs.
The difference syndrome is zero, so u_A=u_B. The retained quantum state is
exactly the uniform maximally entangled vector on the affine code
  {x in Z_D^c: Hx=u_A}.
All nonempty rows have disjoint supports and coefficients one. Choose one
pivot position in each nonempty row. Replace that pivot coordinate by the sum
of its row while preserving all other coordinates. This transformation is
invertible over Z_D because the pivot coefficient is one and different rows
have disjoint supports. Each party then subtracts its own syndrome at the
pivot coordinate. Let r be the number of nonempty restricted rows. The affine
code is a translate of Z_D^(c-r), even for composite D. This maps the state to exactly
  Phi_D^tensor n tensor |00><00|^tensor(c-n),
where n=c-r. Reset all remaining output positions to |00>. Since every
row has first coordinate zero, the coordinate transformation can fix position
1, and n>=1. This exact first Bell pair is preserved on bad branches too.

r<=Q, so on EVERY branch the declared n satisfies n>=K-3Q, although a
bad branch is not asserted to have n actual Bell pairs. For the full joint
state, including the original counted current clock and all quantum memory,
and any explicitly retained protocol records, define the comparison target
using exactly the same classical K/record probabilities and conditional memory
states, but replace each output by Phi_D^tensor min(K,n) followed by |00>.
On good branches this is the actual output. On bad branches replace only the
output state. The full joint half-trace distance to this single MC-support
target is at most epsilon. This is an averaged trace-distance statement, not
postselection or an assumption that the receiver accesses K. Positivity of
the classical decomposition permits the coupling proof. The memory marginal
is unchanged by the trace-preserving output-only instrument when records are
discarded; any records retained as MEMORY instead require their own return
and resource accounting, not a free convention change.

The joint comparison target has at least max(1,K-3Q) Bell pairs in every branch.
The actual prefix-score functional has norm at most L ln D, so the trace-distance
penalty for its expectation is at most epsilon L ln D. No claim is made that
an arbitrary epsilon-close initializer has the same affine-code property.

4. Explicit costs and scope

Auxiliary entanglement: zero. Hidden-memory access: zero. Shared random seed:
zero. Fresh local parity ancillas: at most Q D-level ancillas if all retained,
or one reusable ancilla with reset/entropy disposal charged. Local controlled-
addition touches: at most L-1 per party in the parity stage because supports
of distinct colors are disjoint. Tail computational-basis measurements: at
most L-1 per party. Decoding and resets require additional O(L+Q) local mod-D
additions/gates with the explicit disjoint-row structure; no Gaussian field
elimination is needed. Nothing implies a physically
free reset or low thermodynamic cost.

Classical communication can send one D-symbol each direction per raw query:
at most Q ln D nats in each direction, plus agreed framing. Alternatively send
Alice's symbol and Bob's equality bit; both know every threshold from their
common answer history. No tail outcomes need to be sent: each party computes
its own syndrome. Protocol transcripts cost O(Q(log L+log D)) classical bits
if every query index is retained literally; indices can instead be recomputed
from answers and the public algorithm. Retaining both parity outcomes needs
O(Q log D) bits besides the algorithm state. All retained records and their
correlations must be counted in the chosen complete memory/output contract.

The included exact integer MW implementation uses an implicit lazy interval
tree for weights and costs O(T log(L+1)) integer operations on
O(T+log L)-bit weights, O(T log(L+1)) tree nodes, plus O(T log T) sorting and
at most nine validations. A direct array implementation gives the looser
O((L+1)T) bound. This efficient control calculation is not a comparison to an optimized
ordinary entanglement factory. Local parity support and syndrome decoding are
explicit, not an E_F convex-roof oracle. Search Q=O(log L+log(1/epsilon)) replaces
the linear communication of a full descending inventory scan. It does not
reduce the leading ordinary stock formation cost or prove a stock advantage.

5. Relation to prior primary search literature

Fractional-median weight updates and obtaining a list with many good intervals
are known noisy-search ideas. The proof above is deliberately complete with
loose constants and an arbitrary history-dependent indifference band. It makes
no historical novelty claim. Relevant primary source consulted:
  Gretta and Price, Sharp Noisy Binary Search with Monotonic Probabilities,
  ICALP 2024, DOI 10.4230/LIPIcs.ICALP.2024.75, Algorithm 1/Lemma 6.
  https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol297-icalp2024/LIPIcs.ICALP.2024.75/LIPIcs.ICALP.2024.75.pdf
Their fresh-coin model alone is not a proof for depleted quantum sites. The
deterministic-color implementation and the explicit joint receiving argument
above supply that premise for this actual queue. KW/HJW optimization is unused.

Remaining boundary: this source-specific queue construction depends on its
product maximally mixed nuisance tail and explicitly known coordinate order.
It does not give generic causal normalization, black-box hidden-memory access,
or a physical entropy/energy advantage. The source's complete initialization
cost and finite global error frontier remain those of ordinary counted stock.
