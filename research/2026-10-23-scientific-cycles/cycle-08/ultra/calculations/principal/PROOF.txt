1. THE PRECISE CHANGE FROM CYCLE7

Cycle7 proved that exact output-only maximally correlated (MC) normalization
of the full-product-tail queue cannot retain more than its guaranteed Bell
seed without paid entanglement or actual inventory access. Exact support is a
strong premise. We now give a source-specific, finite-round LOCC operation
which, at any prescribed GLOBAL joint error epsilon, reaches an explicit
pure-prefix MC reference with only O(log L+log(1/epsilon)) lost Bell pairs.
The reference includes the complete old memory and every retained transcript.
No old clock is read, no additional entanglement or shared random seed is used.
The operation uses O(log L+log(1/epsilon)) classical D-symbols in EACH direction
and O(L) local controlled-add gates. We also prove that logarithmic two-way
communication is necessary for this target, even with arbitrary independent
entangled auxiliaries. This does not prove optimal Bell loss or a formation
advantage over ordinary consumable stock.

The bounded help developed the fractional-median/disjoint-color search and
the information-flow converse. Principal work developed the commuting parity
normalizer, independent public-coin tree alternative, exact affine/coherence
argument, executable admissions, matched resource contract and integration.
All actual subproofs, independent challenges and alternatives are included.

2. ACTUAL SOURCE AND THEOREM

Let D>=2 be any INTEGER, L>=2, m=ln D, Phi=Phi_D and
nu=I_D/D tensor I_D/D. The exact counted-current-clock finite queue, after L
uses of its same local-unitary device with faithful product eta=nu, has state

 Omega=(1/L) sum_(k=1)^L |kk><kk|_F tensor
             [Phi^k tensor nu^(L-k)]_O tensor nu^L_M.              (1)

F is the pair of CURRENT clock registers; M contains all old quantum memory.
Both are inaccessible to the receiving operation Gamma. This is the actual
joint queue state, not an arbitrary decomposition granted to its receiver.
The original complete local memory dimension is L D^L; the exact first output
is Bell and complete one-use return distance is delta=(1-D^(-2L))/L. Its mean
preselected entangled-stock supply and E_F of the initial complete memory are
B=(L+1)m/2. Exact fixed maximally entangled supply is Lm. These inherited facts
and their original proofs/replay are preserved unchanged in the predecessor.

Choose rational 0<epsilon<=1/2 and set, using EXACT integer-power ceilings,

 t=32 ceil(log_2(L+1))+128 ceil(log_2(2/epsilon)),
 s=ceil(log_D(2/epsilon)),
 Q=4t+9s,                w=3Q.                                  (2)

THEOREM. There is an explicit finite-round output-only LOCC Gamma with zero
additional entanglement, zero shared random seed and zero hidden-clock access
whose output rho admits the following single, fully specified comparison state:

 Theta=(1/L) sum_(k,T) p(T|k) |kk><kk|_F tensor nu^L_M tensor
             |TT><TT| tensor [Phi^nstar(k,T) tensor |00><00|^(L-nstar)], (3)

where p is the ACTUAL public transcript law and nstar is defined by the
deterministic circuit/decoder below, such that

 T(rho,Theta)<=epsilon,
 max(1,k-w)<=nstar(k,T)<=k on EVERY reference branch.              (4)

|TT> denotes identical classical transcripts at Alice/Bob. The entire old
memory, not just a clock or chosen marginal, is included in (3),(4). It has
its original marginal exactly. Conditional transcript-clock correlations are
retained. If T is actually discarded, trace contraction gives the same bound
for that declared interface. Private tail outcomes and work are ACTUALLY
discarded after correction; their entropy export is charged, not hidden in a
returned memory. The first PHYSICAL Bell pair remains exact on every branch.

Let O=m sum_(i=1)^L Phi_(1..i). Then

 Tr O Theta >= B-wm,
 Tr O rho >= B-wm-epsilon Lm.                                    (5)

For epsilon=1/L the right loss is at most (3Q+1)m=O(log L), whereas exact MC
normalization loses order L. The actual state need not itself have MC support
or zero conditional defect. Unsafe branches have not been postselected away.

3. DETERMINISTIC FRESH-COLOR PARITY ORACLE

Color sites2,...,L cyclically with colors1,...,Q: color(i)=1+((i-2) mod Q).
Raw query number a uses color a ONCE, even if previous calls were synthetic.
At a threshold j each party coherently computes the mod-D sum of its digits
on sites 2<=i<=j of that color into a pure local D-level ancilla, measures the
SUM ONLY, and sends its sum to the other party. YES means the two sums agree.
Measuring each data digit here would destroy the resource and is not the
operation. Threshold0 is synthetic YES and L+1 synthetic NO. No color repeats.

For proof only, condition on the classical clock k in (1). The difference
digits e_i=b_i-a_i (mod D) of any diagonal statistics vanish for i<=k and are
independent uniform Z_D digits for i>k. Bell digits have not individually been
measured: this is a coupling of commuting local SUM projectors. Conditional
on k, the source is a tensor product across color groups. Previous local
measurements and their complete outcomes act only on other colors. Even
conditional on the ENTIRE previous transcript, the next color factor is
untouched. Adaptive thresholds depend only on that previous transcript.

If j<=k, the sums match with certainty. If j>k+Q, (k,j] contains every color
at least once and hence an untouched uniform nuisance digit of the new color.
Its coefficient is ONE, so the sum is uniform in Z_D for EVERY integer D.
Thus, conditional on all prior outcomes and k,

 j<=k: YES certainly;  j>k+Q: Pr(YES)=1/D.                        (6)

No premise is imposed in the indifference band k<j<=k+Q; its behavior may
depend arbitrarily on previous history. This is why ordinary fresh-coin noisy
search cannot simply be invoked on repeatedly measured individual sites.
The disjoint-color implementation proves the needed freshness instead.

Maintain U=min({L} union {j-1: a raw query at j says NO}). Every NO, including
a synthetic endpoint NO, certifies j>k. Consequently U>=k on every branch.

4. COMPLETE FINITE SEARCH LEMMA AND ALGORITHM

The following elementary weighted-median construction applies to any oracle
with (6), replacing Q by any integer band width b>=1. Four fresh raw queries
at the same j constitute a logical query: YES iff all four are YES. Outside
the band on the right its conditional wrong-YES probability is <=D^-4<=1/16;
left-side errors have probability zero. All four calls occur, and all NOs
update U. Use atoms0,...,L initially of weight1, each viewed as a unit interval.

At each of t steps find a weighted median point x=h+theta, where h is its atom
and 0<=theta<=1. Query integer j=h if theta<=1/2, else j=h+1. Update the weights
AS IF querying x: on YES halve the mass below x; on NO halve mass above x.
In atom h use the aggregate multiplier and then re-uniformize its density.
These are algorithmic weights, not an assumed physical posterior. Total
unscaled weight becomes exactly3/4 of its previous value under either answer.

An exact integer implementation avoids all floating arithmetic. If old total
is W, atom h has weight v and cumulative weight C before it, query h iff
W-2C<=v. Multiply resulting weights by4 each round. On YES the side t<h gets
2v_t, side t>h gets4v_t, and h gets4v-(W-2C). On NO the sides get4v_t and2v_t,
and h gets4v-[2(C+v)-W]. Each new weight is positive and new total is3W.
The included implicit interval tree implements these exact updates.

Call j GOOD when j belongs to [k,k+b]. If a query is not good, the target atom
k is strictly on the correct side of x and is not the median atom (b>=1 is
used here). Its weight stays unchanged on a correct answer and is halved only
on a wrong answer. On every good query its multiplier is at least1/2. Thus
if G is the number of good queries and E the erroneous non-good logical ones,

 2^(-G-E)<=weight_k(t)<=W(t)=(L+1)(3/4)^t,
 G+E>=t log_2(4/3)-log_2(L+1).                                   (7)

Conditional wrong-event probabilities are <=1/16 even under adaptation. Their
sum is stochastically dominated by Binomial(t,1/16), as follows by iterating
conditional exponential moments. Hoeffding's bound gives
Pr(E>t/8)<=exp(-t/128)<=epsilon/2. Since t>=32log_2(L+1) and
log_2(4/3)>13/32 (equivalent to the integer inequality 2^51>3^32), (7) gives
G>=t/4 outside that failure event.

Sort all t queried integers and select ranks min(t,1+floor(ell*t/8)), ell0..8.
Gaps are at most ceil(t/8); because t>=32, a contiguous cluster of >=t/4 good
ranks contains a selected one. Hence the at-most-nine distinct candidate
values contain a good candidate. Validate EVERY candidate j by s further
fresh raw queries at i=min(L+1,j+b+1), updating U from all NOs. For any good
candidate, i>k+b unless it is the exact sentinel. Therefore these validations
contain a NO except with probability <=D^-s<=epsilon/2, and imply
U<=i-1<=k+2b. For analysis choose any deterministic good candidate conditional
on k and the first-phase transcript; the physical algorithm need not know
which it is because it validates all candidates. No union bound over nine
failures is needed. Intervening validations still use fresh colors.

The number of raw calls is <=4t+9s=Q. With b=Q, all colors are distinct and
the lemma gives

 U>=k on EVERY branch;  Pr(U>k+2Q)<=epsilon.                      (8)

Set c=max(1,U-2Q). Always c>=max(1,k-2Q), and c<=k except probability epsilon.
All stop/null branches are counted; no receiver learns whether it succeeded.

5. BACK-ACTION, LOCAL DECODING AND THE FULL JOINT ERROR

Each party now measures its sites i>c in the computational basis. Retain the
private tail values temporarily. For every earlier SUM outcome subtract that
party's own measured-tail contribution, giving the syndrome on sites<=c.
Fix k, the complete public transcript and both private tail strings in a proof
extension. The chosen adaptive path specifies only a product of commuting
real diagonal SUM projectors. If c<=k, the first c pairs began as Phi^c; after
these projections their normalized state is the equal-phase affine-code state

 |A>=(#A)^(-1/2) sum_(x in A)|x>_A|x>_B,
 A={x in Z_D^c: each used restricted color sum equals its syndrome}. (9)

Alice/Bob restricted syndromes agree on each nonempty row because all those
prefix digits agree. Empty restricted rows have zero syndrome on nonzero safe
branches. No phase is generated: every projector has only0/1 diagonal entries.
Private tail measurement leaves precisely these prefix constraints; forgetting
the tail before subtracting it would be an invalid decoding argument.

Restricted rows have DISJOINT supports. For each nonempty row S choose a
common pivot p in S and transform locally y_p=sum_(i in S)x_i mod D, y_i=x_i
otherwise. This map is invertible for every D, its inverse subtracting the
other digits from y_p. Translate the pivot by that party's syndrome. The row
constraint now fixes its pivot to zero and leaves all other digits free.
Apply this for all r nonempty rows and permute free coordinates to the first
n=c-r slots. Coordinate1 is in no row and remains the exact first Bell pair.
Reset measured tail and known pivots to zero. On every safe branch (9) becomes

                 Phi_D^n tensor |00><00|^(L-n).                  (10)

No unsupported field rank/Gaussian elimination over a composite modulus is
used. Since r<=min(Q,c-1), on EVERY branch the declared allocation n=c-r obeys
n>=max(1,k-3Q). On unsafe branches use the same well-defined local maps with
each party's own syndrome; n is just an allocation there, not a Bell claim.
The protected first physical Bell still factors on every branch.

Define nstar(k,T)=max(1,min(k,n(T))). In the proof extension carrying the
actual private tail strings, keep their SAME law together with k,T and the
complete old memory and replace the quantum output by (10) with nstar.
On every safe branch this reference equals the actual quantum output. On a
bad branch its half trace distance is at most1. Average using (8), then trace
the actually discarded private tail/work. This proves (3),(4) without erasing
clock correlations, postselection or a hidden inventory readout. Syndrome
correction makes the decoded safe output the SAME pure vector for every
compatible private tail string, so tracing these strings does not dephase it.

Every pure nstar-prefix has score>=m nstar; averaging uniform k proves the
first part of (5). Because 0<=O<=Lm I, equal-trace states at half trace distance
epsilon differ in its expectation by <=epsilon Lm, proving the second part.
If the exact complete source (1) is instead initialized within half trace
alpha, contraction gives distance <=alpha+epsilon to the SAME exact-source
reference. This is one global initializer error; no per-branch replacement.

6. MATCHED COMMUNICATION/ACCESS OBSTRUCTION

The converse holds for ANY interactive finite-round output-only LOCC, not
only this search. Suppose its complete final rho is within global half trace
epsilon<=1/2 of a target of form (3), more generally allowing any transcript
and 0<=k-nstar<=w on each target branch. Put

 g(u)=(u+1)ln(u+1)-u ln u (g(0)=0), mu_D=1/(D-1),
 R=(1-epsilon)ln L-g(w+mu_D)-h2(epsilon).                         (11)

Let I_B=I(K; ALL Bob initially accessible systems), including supplied records
and auxiliaries. Charge messages in each direction by the sum of their actual
conditional entropies given the previous PUBLIC transcript, including any
informative stop/timing/null symbols. Then

 C_(A->B)+I_B >=R,           C_(B->A)+I_A >=R.                    (12)

These are discrete finite/countable-message Shannon entropies, evaluated on
the EXACT source (1). An alpha-initializer perturbation transfers a fixed
alphabet budget with target error alpha+epsilon; source-dependent expected
lengths/entropy charges require their own analysis and are not transferred
by that triangle inequality alone.

Without a supplied correlated inventory record, I_A=I_B=0 even with arbitrary
independent entangled auxiliary states. For w=O(logL) and epsilon=1/L each
direction requires lnL-O(lnlnL) nats. Our deterministic operation's Q lnD
upper bound matches logarithmic order at fixed D; constants are not matched.
Unlike the preserved public-coin alternative, it has no missing seed-message
cost in this statement.

Proof. Bob measures his target digits in Z and lets J be the last nonzero
position (0 if none). Conditional on nstar, E(nstar-J)<=sum_(a>=1)D^-a=mu_D.
Hence G=K-J>=0 has mean <=w+mu_D. The maximum entropy of an integer variable
with nonnegative support and mean u is g(u), by relative entropy against the
geometric law. Therefore H(K|J)<=H(G)<=g(w+mu_D). Measuring/tracing rho,Theta
gives classical joint laws of (K,J) at variation <=epsilon, with the same
uniform K marginal. The common-part mixture decomposition proves
|H_rho(K|J)-H_Theta(K|J)|<=epsilon ln L+h2(epsilon): in each residual component
conditional entropy lies in [0,lnL], and the mixture flag costs at most h2.
Consequently I_rho(K;B_final)>=R.

Track all Bob systems, keeping copies of any classical outcomes only in a
bookkeeping extension if the actual protocol discards them. Local Bob maps
cannot increase K-information, and Alice local maps without a sent message
do not change Bob's marginal (no signaling). A classical incoming C increases
his information by I(K;C|B)<=H(C|past), since its quantum conditional entropy
given K,B is nonnegative and conditioning on all of B only lowers H(C|past).
Iterating yields I(K;B_final)<=I_B+C_(A->B). Swapping parties proves (12).
The bookkeeping extension is traced to the declared actual interface; it is
not a free retained-memory return. This completes the converse.

A stronger ONE-WAY obstruction uses only no signaling and a classical test.
For one-way LOCC in either direction with no correlated receiving access,
the sender's final local state remains K-independent even with arbitrary
independent entanglement. Its last-nonzero J is independent of uniform K, so
Pr(0<=K-J<=w+a)<=(w+a+1)/L for every integer a>=0. In the target this event
has probability >=1-D^(-(a+1)) from its trailing-zero law. Thus

 epsilon >=[1-D^(-(a+1))-(w+a+1)/L]_+
          >=[1-(w+floor(log_D L)+2)/L]_+ when a=floor(log_D L).   (13)

For logarithmic deficit this is nearly maximal joint error. This sharp form
requires the POINTWISE deficit premise; the entropy bound (12) also permits
a mean deficit. Their distinction and the valid mean-only Markov variant are
in the full lower proof. With actual supplied inventory records Z_A,Z_B,
charge I_A,I_B (under the stated conditional independence I(K;Z_A/B)), not
the entropy of a mathematical source selector no receiver was given.

For r independent inventories and ONE global target error epsilon, the same
argument on Kvec gives in each direction

 C+I(Kvec;initial_receiver)>=
 (1-epsilon)r ln L-sum_a g(w_a+mu_D)-h2(epsilon).                 (14)

It permits collective processing, correlated transcript and n_a depending on
all inventories/transcripts. H(Kvec|Jvec)<=sum H(K_a|J_a) suffices. This is
an information/communication price, not a Bell-consumption lower bound.

7. COMPLETE COSTS, COMPARATOR AND LIMIT ORDER

Source: unchanged ordinary counted stock; expected preselected B, deterministic
exact Lm, local old-memory dimension L D^L. Source preparation and L device
calls remain additional costs. Receiver: no entangled auxiliaries, no clock
access, no shared random seed. At most Q local SUM measurements, Q D-symbol
messages in EACH direction, and <=L-1 local controlled-add touches in the
parity stage per party (supports are disjoint). Decoding needs <=L-1 further
controlled-adds plus O(L) local permutations/translations/reset operations.
Each party also has <=L-1 private tail Z measurements and O(L+Q) temporary
digits/records; outcomes, ancilla reset and tail disposal export entropy.
They have not been included in an unproved enlarged-memory return claim.

The exact median controller takes O(t log(L+1)) integer operations on
O(t+logL)-bit weights with the included lazy interval tree, O(t log(L+1))
nodes and O(t log t) candidate sorting, with O(t log(L+1)(t+logL)) controller
bits as a conservative workspace bound. Controller weights are actually
discarded at the end; if retained,
their deterministic copies must be included as functions of T in (3).
Literal query-index/transcript storage
is O(Q(logL+logD)) bits; indices can instead be recomputed from the common
answer history. Two matching parity-outcome records use O(QlogD) bits. At most
nine validations are executed. All finite choices are actual executable
integer arithmetic; no hidden E_F, SOS or convex-roof oracle is invoked.

The strongest ordinary comparator is supplied EXACTLY the same queue state,
access restrictions, global error, local workspace and optimization rights.
It can run this very operation with the same costs. The queue is itself
ordinary preselected Bell stock with product nuisance filling; the operation
does not lower its leading supply B. If both controllers are GIVEN actual K,
they can retain K Bells and reset the tail directly, but that is a different
priced-access contract. Independent catalysts/embezzlers cannot supply K
information by themselves, so (12),(13) apply even if they supply unlimited
independent entanglement. Their total initial supply and any required joint
return error must still be charged; no catalytic advantage is established.

In particular, a joint MC REFERENCE at distance epsilon does not turn the
ACTUAL finite-epsilon state into exact MC or set its conditional defect Q=0
in the earlier formation witness. Nor may one divide a finite-r error by r
after the calculation. Applying this receiver independently to r queues at
per-task error epsilon/r gives one global bound epsilon and
w=O(logL+log(r/epsilon)). At fixed L, this grows to a vacuous guarantee as
r->infinity. Neither that union bound nor the collective communication
converse (14) closes the generic iid formation gap. This unresolved limit
order is scientifically material, not an implementation detail.

No efficient preparation, noise tolerance, low thermodynamic reset cost,
experimental hidden-memory/resource certification or useful hardware advantage
is proved. Recorded script CPU/wall timings establish bounded calculation
cost only. Total discovery/agent inference cost remains UNKNOWN.
