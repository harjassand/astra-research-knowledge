WHY THE OLD MC-STOCK ER GAP DOES NOT TRANSFER AUTOMATICALLY
22 October 2026. Preserved unsuccessful route; not a claim that ER is optimal.

The old chi-tail MC queue has ER=ED=B-ln L+O_D(1). Its proof uses common MC
support and a K-independent diagonal reference. The current product-noise
queue omega=(1/L)sum_k Phi^k nu^(L-k) is a different output. Its full Bell
basis spectrum is not the old MC spectrum, and that old formula is not an
upper bound which can simply be reused.

A natural separable comparison is zeta=(1/L)sum_l chi^l nu^(L-l), where
chi=D^-1 sum_x |xx><xx|. Both omega and zeta are diagonal in the product Bell
basis. Label a Bell basis vector by its D-ary shift and phase at each site.
Let u be the first site whose Bell label is nonzero, or L+1 if none; let v be
the first site whose SHIFT is nonzero, or L+1 if none. Then u<=v, and for an
individual full label string x,

 p_omega(x)=D^(-2L)/L sum_(k=1)^(u-1) D^(2k),
 p_zeta(x)=D^(-2L)/L sum_(l=1)^(v-1) D^l.                           (1)

Every omega string has u>=2, so both probabilities are positive. For a string
drawn from inventory K, u-1=K+T2 and v-1=K+T1, where 0<=T2<=T1 are capped
trailing runs with means bounded by 1/(D^2-1) and 1/(D-1), respectively.
Writing geometric sums in (1),

 ln[p_omega/p_zeta]=(2(u-1)-(v-1))ln D + c_(u,v),

where c_(u,v) is uniformly bounded in D alone. Thus
D(omega||zeta)=B+O_D(1), not B-ln L+O_D(1). In particular the 1/L mixture
factors cancel exactly in (1). This candidate comparison does not establish
the desired logarithmic monotone obstruction.

No lower bound on ER(omega) follows from a failed upper-bound candidate. The
unknown generic formation gap is not closed. The robust communication theorem
in PROOF.txt bypasses this incorrect transfer by pricing a specified joint
target's actual local inventory information, rather than asserting a new ER
formula. That theorem is an access/communication bound, not an entanglement
loss converse.
