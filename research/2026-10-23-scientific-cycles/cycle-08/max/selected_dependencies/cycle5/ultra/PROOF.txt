For EVERY finite output state sigma and these readouts,

 E_F(sigma) >= Tr(O sigma)-r C_D-sum_a Q_a(sigma).        (3)

with its canonical square-root Kraus operators. The unnormalized pure

Zero probability branches contribute zero.

Because the initial vector and each branch are pure, the average branch
entanglement is EXACTLY E(X)-I(Y_a:B_all) in the corresponding cq state:
Bob's unconditioned state is unchanged, so this is the definition of its
Holevo mutual information. Data processing under tracing Bob's task gives
 I(Y_a:B_all)>=I(Y_a:B_-a)=S(rho_-a)-Q_a.
Consequently
 score_a <= S(rho)-S(rho_-a)+Q_a+C_D.                  (4)

Sum a and use the quantum complementary Shearer inequality

For completeness, expand each complementary entropy in task order by the

only INCREASE conditional entropy by SSA. Each full-chain conditional term
appears r-1 times. This proof allows negative quantum conditional entropy.
Equation (3) follows for every pure vector, with no normality premise.

COMPLETE MIXED-STATE STEP. For ANY pure ensemble sigma=sum_j w_j psi_j,
the readout channel is linear and cq conditional entropy is concave:

One elementary proof introduces the ensemble label J and uses SSA:
S(B|Y)>=S(B|YJ)=sum_j w_j S(B|Y)_(psi_j).

This proves (3) on the entire state space, not just a chosen decomposition.
No E_F additivity or infinite-dimensional entropy continuity is used. QED.

This is a failed mixed-side allocation shortcut, not a counterexample to (3).
