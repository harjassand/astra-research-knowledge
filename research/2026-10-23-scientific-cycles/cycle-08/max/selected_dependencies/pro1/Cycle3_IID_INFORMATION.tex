\section{The optimized iid coherent information is exactly \texorpdfstring{$3/2$}{3/2}}
\label{sec:iidinformation}

Let $\mathcal N_n^{\tau_\pi}$ be the complete $n$-call port channel with
the one fixed actual initializer $\tau_\pi$. No bath is replaced between
calls. For an input density $\omega$, write
\[
 I_c(\omega,\mathcal N_n^{\tau_\pi})
 =S(B^n)-S(E_nF),
\]
where $F$ purifies the initial bath. This is an analytic purification,
not an accessible resource or a return obligation. The finite entropy
$S(F)=S(\tau_\pi)$ ensures that all displayed entropies are finite for
the inputs used below.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform iid information ceiling and matching limit]
\label{thm:iidinformation}
For every fixed $0\le\beta\le1/8$ and every $n\ge1$,
\[
 \frac1n\max_{\rho\in\mathcal D(\mathbb C^3)}
 I_c(\rho^{\otimes n},\mathcal N_n^{\tau_\pi})
 \le \frac32+\left(\frac4n\right)^{2/3}.
 \tag{I1}
\]
Moreover,
\[
 \boxed{\displaystyle
 \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\max_{\rho\in\mathcal D(\mathbb C^3)}
 I_c(\rho^{\otimes n},\mathcal N_n^{\tau_\pi})=\frac32.}
 \tag{I2}
\]
The maximization in (I1)--(I2) may choose a different density at each
horizon. Within each block the input remains a tensor power. For one
fixed density with $S(\rho)=3/2+\epsilon>3/2$ and
$\delta=1/2-\rho_{00}$, a sharper statement is
\[
 \limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac1n
 I_c(\rho^{\otimes n},\mathcal N_n^{\tau_\pi})
 \le\frac32-\epsilon-\delta<\frac32.
 \tag{I3}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First take $S(\rho)>3/2$. Unitary invariance gives
$S(B^nE_n)=nS(\rho)+S(\tau_\pi)$; subadditivity and the entropy
triangle inequality then give
\[
 S(E_nF)\ge S(E_n)-S(F)
 \ge nS(\rho)-S(B^n).
\]
Thus $I_c\le2S(B^n)-nS(\rho)$. Combining (E4) with the corrected
drift and $\nu\ge3\delta/2$ yields
\[
 I_c(\rho^{\otimes n},\mathcal N_n^{\tau_\pi})
 \le n\left(\frac32-\epsilon-\delta\right)+4M.
 \tag{I4}
\]
For a fixed density $M<\infty$, proving (I3).

The dependence of $M$ on the density must be controlled before placing
the maximization inside the limit. From (8), (10) and (15),
\[
 1-r=\frac{2\delta}{1/2+\delta},\qquad
 M\le\frac{2\sqrt3\,\beta(1/2+\delta)}{\alpha\sqrt\delta}
 \le\frac{2\sqrt3\,\beta}{\alpha\sqrt\delta}
 \le\frac1{\sqrt\delta}.
 \tag{I5}
\]
The final inequality uses $\beta\le1/8$ and
$2\sqrt3\,\beta/\alpha\le2/\sqrt{21}<1$.
Also $I_c\le S(\rho^{\otimes n})=nS(\rho)$, by the entropy triangle
inequality with the purifying input reference. Pinching the input into
zero/color sectors gives $S(\rho)\le h_2(q)+1-q\le3/2+\delta$.
Together with (I4)--(I5),
\[
 \frac{I_c}{n}-\frac32
 \le\min\left\{\delta,\frac4{n\sqrt\delta}\right\}
 \le\left(\frac4n\right)^{2/3}.
 \tag{I6}
\]
The last scalar bound follows by splitting at
$\delta=(4/n)^{2/3}$. Inputs with $S(\rho)\le3/2$ satisfy
$I_c\le nS(\rho)\le3n/2$ directly. This proves the uniform upper bound.
The maximum exists since the input and output port spaces are finite
dimensional and coherent information is continuous in the input.

For the lower limit use the unchanged critical input
$\rho_c=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1/4,1/4)$. Its exact depth law is inherited
from Cycle2. Here is the short entropy consequence, including the boundary.
Starting from $\tau_\pi$, the bath stays diagonal and scalar on each depth
level: the equal color weights preserve this property, and the two vacuum
weights remain equal. Hence $[R,\tau_j]=0$ and the coherent root term
vanishes for this particular trajectory. At depth at least one the lazy
reflected walk moves up and down with probabilities $\alpha^2/2$ each;
at zero it moves up with probability $\alpha^2/2$ and otherwise stays.
Its squared-depth increment is at most $\alpha^2$, and
$\Tr(L^2\tau_\pi)=1$. Consequently
\[
 m_n=\Tr(L\tau_n)\le\sqrt{1+\alpha^2n}.
\]
For a nonnegative integer-valued variable of mean $m$, the entropy is
at most that of the geometric law of mean $m$; relative entropy to that
law proves this directly. It is at most $\log_2(e(m+1))$.
Conditioned on depth $\ell$, the bath has $2^{\ell+1}$ equiprobable
vertices. Therefore
\[
 S(\tau_n)=H(L_n)+1+m_n
 \le1+m_n+\log_2(e(m_n+1))=o(n).
 \tag{I7}
\]
Finally, unitary invariance and subadditivity give
$S(B^n)\ge3n/2+S(\tau_\pi)-S(\tau_n)$, while
$S(E_nF)\le S(\tau_n)+S(\tau_\pi)$. Thus
\[
 I_c(\rho_c^{\otimes n},\mathcal N_n^{\tau_\pi})
 \ge\frac{3n}{2}-2S(\tau_n).
 \tag{I8}
\]
This proves the lower limit in (I2).
\end{proof}

This theorem is an information result for a restricted family of block
inputs. It is not a single-letter quantum-capacity formula for this memory
channel. Maximizing over arbitrary temporally correlated inputs is a
strictly stronger operation than the maximization in (I2), and feedback
protocols are not represented by these product averages. The inherited
capacity floor already supplies actual arbitrary-reference codes; (I2)
does not replace that coding proof or create a fresh-bath block model.
Even independent but nonidentically distributed products
$\rho_1\otimes\cdots\otimes\rho_n$ lie outside the optimized class in
(I2). The horizon-dependent choice of one common marginal is a genuine
uniform strengthening over a fixed-marginal limit; it is not the same as
allowing a separate marginal at every call.
