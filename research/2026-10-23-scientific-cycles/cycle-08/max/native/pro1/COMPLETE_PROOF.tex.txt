\begin{abstract}
We resolve the temporal-growth question left by the complete Cycle6 source.
For the same infinite-bath qutrit device, the full-prefix variational
susceptibility has an exact formula and satisfies $v_k/k^2\to1/8$.
The proof classifies every temporal endpoint coefficient by two stack
heights. Paths that consume an original bath color need not be discarded:
their full operator contractions supply an additional uniform contribution.
A normalized symmetric-prefix density simultaneously attains every local
minimum and gives the exact scalar certificate $T_k(\sigma H)=v_k I$.
This density is only an analytic dual witness and is never prepared.

The exact all-length lower $v_k\ge k^2/32$ supplies the previously missing
premise of the uniform nonlinear transfer. For each fixed
$0<\beta\le2^{-8}$, the block length
$k=\lfloor\beta^{-1/2}/8\rfloor$, fixed before the coding horizon,
gives an actual positive input with exact inward operator drift. The
inherited same-bath coding operation therefore achieves
$3/2+(\ln2/16384)\beta^{7/2}$ qubits per forward call, with pointwise
vanishing full-diamond error for every fixed normal initializer, using
one initializer-independent code sequence. No terminal bath return,
fresh bath, bath control or postselection is introduced.

The exact susceptibility is a restricted local variational result;
the rate is an achievable lower bound, not a sharp capacity exponent.
Historical priority, efficient implementation, useful finite blocklength
and physical device performance are not established.
\end{abstract}

\section{The unchanged operation and the two new theorems}

The bath, actual root and interaction are
\[
 E=\mathbb C^2_{\rm seed}\otimes\mathcal F(\mathbb C^2),\qquad
 U=\begin{pmatrix}R&S_1&S_2\\S_1^*&0&0\\S_2^*&0&0\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad V_s=\alpha U+i\beta I,
\]
where $S_j$ prepends color $j$, $R$ exchanges the two vacuum seeds
and is zero elsewhere, $\alpha=\cos s$, $\beta=\sin s$ and
$0\le s\le\pi/2$. Thus $U=U^*=U^{-1}$ and
\[
 S_i^*S_j=\delta_{ij}I,\qquad \sum_jS_jS_j^*=I-P_0,
 \qquad R^2=P_0,\quad RS_j=S_j^*R=0.
\]
Let $L$ be word depth, $q_i=I-\ket0\bra0$ the color charge of
input port $i$, and $Q=Q_k=\sum_{i=0}^{k-1}q_i$.
The ordered product is $W_k(s)=V_{k-1,E}\cdots V_{0,E}$.
Every call conserves $L+Q$, including the actual vacuum exchange.
Write $P_{\le h}=1_{[0,h]}(L)$ and
$r_h=\rank P_{\le h}=2(2^{h+1}-1)$.

The actual reference initializer and port reference remain
\[
 \tau_\pi=\frac{I_2}{2}\otimes\frac23\sum_w6^{-|w|}\ket w\bra w,
 \qquad\eta=\operatorname{diag}(3/4,1/8,1/8).
\]
Thus the depth mass is $(2/3)3^{-h}$, uniform within its
$2^{h+1}$ seed/color vertices. Every forward use shares the same evolving
bath. There is no bath access, reset, fresh bath, preshared entanglement,
postselection, backward quantum wire or timing channel. All slots,
outcomes and retained registers count. There is no terminal-return
requirement. Codes are deterministic, unassisted, and preserve arbitrary
logical-reference entanglement in full diamond norm. The universal
initializer statement is pointwise for every fixed normal initializer;
it is not uniform over all normal initializers at a fixed horizon.

Put
\[
 b=\beta^2,\qquad \sigma=\rho_c^{\otimes k},\quad
 \rho_c=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1/4,1/4),\qquad
 -\log_2\sigma=kI+Q.
\]
Section~\ref{sec:bulk} defines the exact charge-pinched bulk coefficient
$G_1$, its contraction $T_k$, and the full-prefix observables $H_\zeta$.
The analytic prefix $F=(\mathbb C^2)^{\otimes k}$ is finite dimensional;
the physical bath is not truncated or reflected.

\begin{theorem}[Exact full-prefix susceptibility]\label{thm:sharp}
For every $k\ge2$, let
\[
 v_k=\min_{\zeta\ge0,\Tr\zeta=1}\Tr(\sigma H_\zeta^2).
\]
If $S_n$ is a simple symmetric walk starting at zero, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:exactv}
 v_k=\frac{k(k-1)}8-\frac18\sum_{n=0}^{k-2}(k-1-n)\Prob(S_n=0).
\end{equation}
In particular
\begin{equation}\label{eq:vasympt}
 v_k=\frac{k^2}8-\frac{k^{3/2}}{6\sqrt{2\pi}}-\frac{k}8
       +O(\sqrt k),\qquad
 \frac{k^2}{32}\le v_k\le\frac{k(k-1)}8.
\end{equation}
The density
\[
 \zeta_{\rm sym}=\int(\ket u\bra u)^{\otimes k}\,d\mu(u)
               =\frac{P_{{\rm sym},k}}{k+1}
\]
is an exact minimizer. For $H=H_{\zeta_{\rm sym}}$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scalar}
 T_k(\sigma H)=v_k I_F,\qquad
 \Tr(\sigma H_\zeta^2)=v_k+\Tr\bigl(\sigma(H_\zeta-H)^2\bigr)
 \quad\text{for every density }\zeta.
\end{equation}
The preserved local variational quantity obeys
$\mathcal A_k=(\ln2)v_k/8$, so
$\mathcal A_k/k^2\to\ln2/64$. These are not capacity formulae.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Unconditional reliable small-angle rate]\label{thm:rate}
For every fixed $0<\beta\le2^{-8}$, choose
\begin{equation}\label{eq:kchoice}
 k=\left\lfloor\frac{\beta^{-1/2}}8\right\rfloor.
\end{equation}
There is an explicit charge-preserving $k$-call density $\rho_\beta$
and one deterministic unassisted encoder/decoder sequence, independent
of the bath initializer, with rate
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rate}
 \Qcap(V_s,\tau)\ge\frac{S(\rho_\beta)}k
      \ge\frac32+\frac{\ln2}{16384}\beta^{7/2}
\end{equation}
and pointwise vanishing full-diamond error for every fixed normal $\tau$.
At the actual initializer,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:iclimit}
 \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1{nk}
 \Ic(\rho_\beta^{\otimes n},\mathcal N_{nk}^{\tau_\pi})
       =\frac{S(\rho_\beta)}k.
\end{equation}
The channel in \eqref{eq:iclimit} is the full same-bath process. Both
$\beta$ and the resulting $k,\rho_\beta$ are fixed before $n\to\infty$.
\end{theorem}

The exact Cycle6 archive is preserved unchanged \cite{Cycle6}, including
its $k^{3/2}$ guarded-word proof, $64/17$ rate, and complete earlier
source chain. Its conditional quadratic transfer now has its missing
premise proved. Cycle5's sharp restricted quartic fixed-block law and
actual non-scalar four-call code remain valid \cite{Cycle5}. No full
capacity converse or feedback equality follows from either new theorem.

\section{The inherited bulk observable, norms and variational interface}
\label{sec:bulk}

The bounded displacement
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bounded}
 G_s=Q-W_k(s)^*QW_k(s),\qquad \norm{G_s}\le k
\end{equation}
equals $W_k^*LW_k-L$ on the finite-coordinate core and defines the
observable without subtracting possibly infinite mean depths.
For $[X,Q]=0$, set $D_s(X)=\Tr_A[(X\otimes I)G_s]$.
It commutes with depth by total-charge conservation.

On initial depth at least $k$, a physical $k$-call history has no vacuum
exchange before its last call. Its input-charge pinching has an exact
finite-prefix representation
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bulkpoly}
 \mathcal G_k(b)=G_0+bG_1+\cdots+b^kG_k,
 \qquad G_0=(2Q-kI)\otimes I_F.
\end{equation}
Indeed each history consumes at most $k$ initial colors. Equal input
charges and a common output word imply equal final bath depths, so all
final-bath overlaps depend only on the first $k$ colors and have untouched
equal-length tails. The contributing stay counts have the same parity;
after pinching, the sine and cosine degrees are even and give polynomials
in $b$. Thus $\mathcal G_k$ is tensor identity on the seed and remaining
tail. This is a bulk coefficient representation, not a reflecting cutoff.

Define the Hermitian contractions
\[
 T_k(X)=\Tr_A[(X\otimes I)G_1],\quad
 F_\zeta=\Tr_F[(I\otimes\zeta)G_1],\quad
 H_\zeta=F_\zeta+2Q-kI.
\]
Equivalently $\Tr(XF_\zeta)=\Tr(\zeta T_k(X))$; this convention
includes complex coherences. For a fixed computational input word, move
and stay histories have distinct output charge words, giving exact
diagonal drift $(1-b)(2Q-kI)$. Consequently $H_\zeta$ is input-offdiagonal,
commutes with $Q,\sigma$, and has
$\Tr\sigma H_\zeta=\Tr\sigma QH_\zeta=0$.

\begin{lemma}[Endpoint decomposition and uniform norms]\label{lem:endpoints}
On a sufficiently deep coordinate core put
\[
 C_i=U_0\cdots U_{i-1}U_iU_{i-1}\cdots U_0,
 \qquad C_\pm=\sum_i C_i^\pm,
\]
where $C_i^\pm$ change input charge by $\pm1$.
The physical finite coefficient satisfies $G_1=[C_-,C_+]$.
Its offdiagonal endpoint terms are
\[
 B_{ij}=C_i^-C_j^++C_j^-C_i^+-C_i^+C_j^--C_j^+C_i^-.
\]
Distinct pairs occupy orthogonal weighted matrix blocks, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:norms}
 \Tr(\sigma H_\zeta^2)\le k(k-1),\quad
 \norm{H_\zeta}\le k(k-1),\quad\norm{G_1}\le k^2.
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use, for example, depth at least $4k$ so every displayed finite toggling
word avoids the root. Then $C_i$ flips only charge bit $i$; earlier
color values may move. With $Z=W_k(0)^*W_k(s)$,
\[
 Z=e^{isC_{k-1}}\cdots e^{isC_0},\qquad
 G_s=2Q-kI+(Z^*QZ-Q).
\]
The order-$s^2$ charge pinching is
$-[\sum_iC_i,[\sum_iC_i,Q]]/2=[C_-,C_+]$.
The additional ordered-product commutator is a commutator with $Q$,
whose charge pinching vanishes. Since $b=s^2+O(s^4)$ this identifies
$G_1$. Computing on arbitrary first-$k$ colors with a deeper buffer
identifies the same finite coefficient in \eqref{eq:bulkpoly}; it does
not move the physical boundary from depth $k$ or extend the formula to root.

Same-index terms sum to $kI-2Q$. Each remaining $B_{ij}$ changes exactly
bits $i,j$. Different unordered pairs therefore have disjoint blocks
between complete binary charge patterns, even after arbitrary bath-state
contraction. Each oriented block is a difference of two contractions,
of norm at most two; adding its adjoint across orthogonal sectors still
has norm at most two. Its square is supported on endpoint sectors $01,10$,
of total $\sigma$ probability $1/2$, giving weighted square at most two.
Sum over pairs to obtain \eqref{eq:norms}, adding diagonal norm $k$ for
$G_1$. All earlier root and coding corrections are retained.
\end{proof}

For completeness, the preserved variational problem is over Hermitian
$[X,Q]=0$, $\Tr X=\Tr QX=0$:
\[
 \mathcal A_k=\sup_X\left[-\frac12\lambda_{\max}T_k(X)
               -\frac{\Tr(\sigma^{-1}X^2)}{2\ln2}\right].
\]
The compact convex family $\{H_\zeta\}$ has a minimum-norm point $H$
in the real weighted Hilbert space with inner product
$\langle A,B\rangle_\sigma=\operatorname{Re}\Tr(\sigma AB)$.
Its projection inequality gives $T_k(\sigma H)\ge v_kI$, with equality
in expectation at a minimizing density. For any feasible $X$, evaluating
against that density and completing the square yields
\[
 -\tfrac12\lambda_{\max}T_k(X)
 -\frac{\Tr(\sigma^{-1}X^2)}{2\ln2}
 \le-\tfrac12\Tr(XH)-\frac{\Tr(\sigma^{-1}X^2)}{2\ln2}
 \le\frac{\ln2}{8}v_k.
\]
The feasible $X_*=-(\ln2/2)\sigma H$ attains equality. This ordinary
finite-dimensional closest-point argument allows boundary minimizers.
The next sections identify an explicit one and strengthen its operator
inequality to equality.

\section{An all-length insertion--pop classification}
\label{sec:tokens}

Fix endpoint charges $z_i=0,z_j=1$, $0\le i<j<k$, and a complete
binary charge pattern. Under $\sigma$ every pattern has probability
$2^{-k}$, and conditional color wires are independent maximally mixed
qubits. Match colored pushes to subsequent zero pops in last-in-first-out
order. Let $m,h$ be the unmatched pop and push counts before $i$, and
$d,e$ those counts in the middle $(i,j)$. For fair walks of these bits,
\[
 h=S_i-\min_{0\le a\le i}S_a,\qquad
 d=-\min_{0\le a\le j-i-1}T_a.
\]
The empty prefix or middle is allowed. For the oriented endpoint block,
$K_{ij}=C_j^-C_i^+-C_i^+C_j^-$.

\begin{lemma}[Exact three-case normal form]\label{lem:trichotomy}
On the selected finite bulk block, the normalized input-color square of
the contracted coefficient has the following universal minima:
\[
 \begin{array}{c|c|c}
 \text{condition}&\text{raw word mechanism}&
       \text{lower for every prefix density}\\\hline
 h>d&\text{two input colors transposed; both baths restored}&1\\
 h=d&\text{one input/bath swap; one bath restored}&1/4\\
 h<d&\text{both words export one input color}&0.
 \end{array}
\]
For $h=d$, the exact square is $\Tr(\zeta_m^2)/2$, where $\zeta_m$
is one reduced bath-color density. For $h<d$, every repeated-color
pure prefix $\ket u^{\otimes k}$ makes the contracted difference zero.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $E=U_{i-1}\cdots U_0$, $M=U_{j-1}\cdots U_{i+1}$, and
$D=M^*U_jM$. Direct cancellation of involutions gives raw identities
\begin{equation}\label{eq:raw}
 C_jC_i=E^*U_iDE,\qquad C_iC_j=E^*DU_iE.
\end{equation}
Evaluate first on a sufficiently deep coordinate core. A matched Dyck
factor transfers its locally pushed colors to its matching pop ports
and is an input-color permutation tensor bath identity. Its inverse
cancels in \eqref{eq:raw}, even after an intermediate bath change:
nothing else acts on its ports. Deleting all such matched factors leaves
the unmatched pop/push normal forms below, without a probability
replacement for coherent amplitudes.

Write the initial bath colors $B_0,B_1,\ldots$ and the unmatched prefix
push colors in temporal order $a_1,\ldots,a_h$. After $E$, its unmatched
pop ports hold $B_0,\ldots,B_{m-1}$, and the stack is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:prefixstack}
 P=(a_h,\ldots,a_1,B_m,B_{m+1},\ldots).
\end{equation}
Let unmatched middle push colors be $u_1,\ldots,u_e$ and the endpoint
color be $z$. The conjugated toggle $D$ inserts one input color $w$ at
stack index $d$. If $e>0$, $w=u_1$, the later $u$ colors shift toward
the first middle-push position, and $z$ fills the last. If $e=0$, $w=z$.
In either case port $j$ becomes zero and all other middle charge bits
return to their initial values.

To check this insertion, $M$ first removes $d$ stack colors and pushes
$u_1,\ldots,u_e$. The call at $j$ pushes $z$. In $M^*$, the reverse
middle pushes pop the shifted color queue, leaving $u_1$ (or $z$);
the reverse unmatched pops then restore the removed $d$ colors above
it. Matched factors restore their own colors. Thus the two local words
in \eqref{eq:raw} are precisely pop-after-insertion and
insertion-after-pop at index $d$, with identical other input shifts.

For $d=0$, the first puts $w$ into $i$ and leaves $P$ unchanged, while
the second puts $P_0$ into $i$ and replaces the stack top by $w$.
For $d\ge1$, both put $P_0$ into $i$ and their stacks become
\begin{align}
 A&:(P_1,\ldots,P_{d-1},w,P_d,P_{d+1},\ldots),\label{eq:stackA}\\
 B&:(P_1,\ldots,P_{d-1},P_d,w,P_{d+1},\ldots).\label{eq:stackB}
\end{align}
They differ by the single transposition of distinct source wires $w,P_d$.
Undoing $E$ removes the first $h$ stack colors into the unmatched prefix
push positions and restores $B_0,\ldots,B_{m-1}$ from the stored pop
ports. This gives the three cases.

If $h>d$, both changed positions are consumed by $E^*$ and $P_d$ is
an input color. Both baths are restored and the selected coefficient is
\[
 K=(P_A\otimes I_F)(I-\SWAP_{w,a_{h-d}})\otimes I_{\rm tail},
\]
where $P_A$ is a color permutation to the endpoint-swapped input sector.
Normalized trace of a swap on two distinct color qubits is $1/2$;
therefore the normalized square of $I-\SWAP$ is one, for every prefix
contraction.

If $h=d$, the first word restores the bath and the second replaces
$B_m$ by $w$, putting $B_m$ into the input position that otherwise
receives $w$. Thus
\[
 K=(P_A\otimes I_F)(I-\SWAP_{w,B_m}),\qquad
 K_\zeta=P_A(I-\zeta_m)_w.
\]
Partial trace of the swap is the reduced density $\zeta_m$ in our
convention. No transpose or assumption on the other coordinates is used.
For a charge sector with $q$ input colors,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:criticalsquare}
 2^{-q}\Tr(K_\zeta^*K_\zeta)
  =\tfrac12\Tr[(I-\zeta_m)^2]
  =\tfrac14+\tfrac12\Tr[(\zeta_m-I/2)^2]\ge\tfrac14.
\end{equation}
This permits complex coherences and entanglement with other prefix colors.

If $h<d$, put $r=d-h\ge1$. Both words export $w$ and extract $B_m$
to the same input position. Apart from identical restored leading colors,
the final baths are
\begin{align*}
 &(B_0,\ldots,B_{m-1},B_{m+1},\ldots,B_{m+r-1},
                         w,B_{m+r},B_{m+r+1},\ldots),\\
 &(B_0,\ldots,B_{m-1},B_{m+1},\ldots,B_{m+r-1},
                         B_{m+r},w,B_{m+r+1},\ldots).
\end{align*}
The changed coordinate is at most $m+d-h\le i+d\le j-1<k$.
Against $\ket u^{\otimes k}$ both input-operator contractions extract
the same $\ket u$ and multiply by the same $\bra u w\rangle$.
Their insertion positions among copies of $u$ make no difference, so
they cancel before any average. For an arbitrary prefix, only the
nonnegativity of the squared norm is asserted.

These deep-core word identities determine the finite coefficient on
every initial depth at least $k$. This enlarged classification is a
\emph{bulk} result; it is not a new claim about raw words through arbitrary
vacuum encounters. The old narrower guard/meander whole-bath identity
remains valid separately. The physical root is retained later.
\end{proof}

\section{The simultaneous symmetric-prefix optimizer}

Let $\mu$ be invariant probability on unit qubit colors modulo phase.
The density
\[
 \zeta_{\rm sym}=\int(\ket u\bra u)^{\otimes k}d\mu(u)
\]
is admissible, separable, and has every one-color marginal $I/2$.
Explicitly write $u=\sqrt t\ket1+e^{i\phi}\sqrt{1-t}\ket2$,
with uniform $t\in[0,1]$ and $\phi\in[0,2\pi]$. Matrix entries
of different color weights vanish; entries with common weight $r$ equal
\[
 \int_0^1t^{k-r}(1-t)^r\,dt
   =\frac1{(k+1)\binom{k}{r}}.
\]
These are exactly the entries of $P_{{\rm sym},k}/(k+1)$.
No asymptotic representation theorem is needed.

In Lemma~\ref{lem:trichotomy}, this one density attains the universal
values $1,1/4,0$ simultaneously for every endpoint and complete pattern.
Distinct endpoint pairs and distinct charge patterns give orthogonal
weighted matrix blocks. The reverse orientation is the adjoint and has
the same charge and weight. Thus summing the local lower bounds proves
global optimality over \emph{all} prefix densities, not merely symmetric
ones. The explicit input operator $H$ has blocks
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Hblocks}
 H:\quad
 \begin{cases}
 P_A(I-\SWAP),&h>d,\\
 P_A/2,&h=d,\\
 0,&h<d,
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}
and the adjoint blocks, with zero input diagonal.

There is a stronger certificate. Pair $H$ against the full coefficient
$G_1$ and trace normalized input colors. In the first case the trace
is $1\cdot I_F$. In the second it is
\[
 \tfrac12\bigl(I_F-\tfrac12 I_F\bigr)=\tfrac14 I_F,
\]
using normalized input partial trace of a qubit swap equal to $I_F/2$.
In the third case $H=0$. The diagonal part of $G_1$ is orthogonal to
$H$. Adding all weights and adjoints gives $T_k(\sigma H)=v_kI_F$.
Equivalently $\langle H,H_\zeta\rangle_\sigma=v_k$ for every density;
expanding the square proves \eqref{eq:scalar}. This also proves the
explicit variational optimizer $X_*=-(\ln2/2)\sigma H$.
The minimizing observable is unique; uniqueness of a representing
prefix density is neither needed nor asserted.

The witness has rank $k+1$, rather than full rank $2^k$. It is an
analytic comparison variable, never an alternative physical initializer.
Only the finite input matrix built from \eqref{eq:Hblocks} is used in
the communication construction.

\section{Exact random-walk summation and the sharp order}

The number of unmatched prefix pushes, $S_i-\min_{a\le i}S_a$, has
the distribution of the maximum $M_i=\max_{a\le i}S_a$ by reversal
of increments. The middle deficit has distribution $M_r$ by sign
symmetry, where $r=j-i-1$. These two walks are independent since their
charge bits are disjoint. The oriented endpoint probability is $1/4$;
its disjoint adjoint doubles the contribution. Therefore
\begin{equation}\label{eq:walksum}
 v_k=\frac12\sum_{\substack{i,r\ge0\\i+r\le k-2}}
 \left[\Prob(M_i>M_r)+\tfrac14\Prob(M_i=M_r)\right].
\end{equation}
The two maxima in a term are independent, including when their lengths
coincide. There are $N=k(k-1)/2$ terms. The triangular index set is
invariant under interchanging $i,r$. If
$C=\sum_{i+r\le k-2}\Prob(M_i=M_r)$, the strict-comparison sum is
$(N-C)/2$, hence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:collision}
 v_k=\frac{k(k-1)}8-\frac C8.
\end{equation}
Already $0\le C\le N$ gives
$v_k\ge k(k-1)/16\ge k^2/32$ for all $k\ge2$.

To evaluate $C$, put $p_n(a)=\Prob(S_n=a)$. Reflection at the first
visit to a level, and subtraction at consecutive levels, give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:maxlaw}
 \Prob(M_n=m)=p_n(m)+p_n(m+1),\qquad m\ge0.
\end{equation}
At $m=0$ this follows by symmetry as well. This is the lattice maximum
law, not an identification with $|S_n|$ that would lose parity.
For $0\le z<1$ put $D=\sqrt{1-z^2}$ and $a=(1-D)/z$, with
$a(0)=0$. The nearest-neighbor resolvent recurrence yields
\[
 \sum_{n\ge0}p_n(m)z^n=a^m/D\quad(m\ge0),\qquad
 F_m(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}\Prob(M_n=m)z^n=(1+a)a^m/D.
\]
For example the first identity is the bounded solution of
$A_m=\delta_{m0}+(z/2)(A_{m-1}+A_{m+1})$, with $A_{-m}=A_m$.
Absolute convergence or formal power series justify the following
convolution and geometric sum:
\begin{align}
 \sum_{t\ge0}z^t\sum_{i=0}^t\Prob(M_i=M_{t-i})
 &=\sum_{m\ge0}F_m(z)^2\notag\\
 &=\frac{1+a}{(1-a)D^2}
  =\frac1{(1-z)\sqrt{1-z^2}}.\label{eq:GF}
\end{align}
Since $(1-z^2)^{-1/2}=\sum_n p_n(0)z^n$, coefficient comparison gives
$\sum_{i=0}^t\Prob(M_i=M_{t-i})=\sum_{n=0}^t p_n(0)$.
Summing over $t\le k-2$ proves \eqref{eq:exactv}.

For a finite closed expression, set $N_0=\lfloor(k-2)/2\rfloor$ and
$a_N=4^{-N}\binom{2N}N$. Induction from
$a_{N+1}/a_N=(2N+1)/(2N+2)$ gives
\[
 \sum_{m=0}^N a_m=(2N+1)a_N,\qquad
 \sum_{m=0}^N m a_m=\frac{N(2N+1)}3a_N.
\]
Thus the collision correction in \eqref{eq:exactv} is one eighth of
\begin{equation}\label{eq:closedC}
 C=(2N_0+1)a_{N_0}\left(k-1-\frac{2N_0}3\right).
\end{equation}
Stirling's formula gives
$C=4k^{3/2}/(3\sqrt{2\pi})+O(\sqrt k)$, which proves
\eqref{eq:vasympt}. This completes Theorem~\ref{thm:sharp}.
The convolution is an aggregate identity. Individual endpoint terms
are not functions of endpoint distance alone.

\section{Uniform positivity, entropy and exact nonlinear drift}
\label{sec:transfer}

We now discharge the operational obligations rather than identify
coefficient growth with capacity. The following estimates reproduce
the complete conditional transfer in Cycle6 \cite{Cycle6}, now with its
all-length premise proved and an explicit optimizer.

Every derivative of a one-call $V_s$ has norm one. Product differentiation
gives $\norm{W_k^{(r)}(s)}\le k^r$ and
$\norm{G_s^{(r)}}\le2^rk^{r+1}$. Charge pinching and bulk compression
contract norms. Evenness and Taylor's theorem give
\[
 \norm{\mathcal G_k(\sin^2s)-G_0-s^2G_1}
    \le\tfrac23 k^5s^4.
\]
For $\beta\le1/2$, use $s^2\le b/(1-b)$,
$s^2-b\le4b^2/3$ and $s^4\le16b^2/9$. Even the coarse
$\norm{G_1}\le2k^3$ suffices for
\begin{equation}\label{eq:remainder}
 \mathcal G_k(b)=G_0+bG_1+R_b,\qquad\norm{R_b}\le4k^5b^2.
\end{equation}
The critical product $\sigma$ has exactly zero bulk drift at every $b$:
each call has symmetric inward/outward depth probabilities $(1-b)/2$,
and depth initially at least $k$ cannot feel the root before the last
call. Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sigmazero}
 \Tr_A[(\sigma\otimes I)\mathcal G_k(b)]=0.
\end{equation}
This exact cancellation is essential; the remainder is charged against
$\rho-\sigma$, never against all of $\sigma$.

Write $\ell=\ln2$, $v=v_k$, $H=H_{\zeta_{\rm sym}}$ and define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:actualrho}
 X=-\tfrac\ell2\sigma H,\quad m=\tfrac{3\ell v}{16},\quad
 Y=\frac{4m}{k}\sigma(Q-k/2),\qquad
 \rho=\sigma+bX+b^2Y.
\end{equation}
All commute with $\sigma,Q$; $X,Y$ commute with each other.
Input offdiagonality gives $\Tr X=\Tr QX=0$, while
$\operatorname{Var}_\sigma Q=k/4$ gives $\Tr Y=0$, $\Tr QY=m$.
Thus $\Tr\rho=1$.
For every $k\ge2$ impose the actual uniform window
\begin{equation}\label{eq:window}
 bk^4\le\delta_0=2^{-12}.
\end{equation}
With $Z=\sigma^{-1}(\rho-\sigma)$, \eqref{eq:norms} and $v\le k^2$
give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Z}
 \norm Z\le\tfrac\ell2 bk^2+\tfrac{3\ell}{8}b^2k^2
          \le\ell bk^2<\tfrac12.
\end{equation}
Hence $\rho$ is a strictly positive charge-preserving density.
Weighted Cauchy--Schwarz also gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tracebounds}
 \norm X_1\le\tfrac\ell2\sqrt v,\qquad
 \norm Y_1\le\frac{3\ell v}{8\sqrt k}.
\end{equation}

The exact scalar identity \eqref{eq:scalar} yields leading drift
\[
 b^2[T_k(X)+2mI]=-\ell v b^2 I/8.
\]
Using \eqref{eq:remainder}--\eqref{eq:sigmazero}, its complete remainder is
\[
 b^3T_k(Y)+\Tr_A[((bX+b^2Y)\otimes I)R_b].
\]
Dividing its norm by $\ell vb^2/8$, and using
$v\ge k^2/32$, gives
\begin{align}
 &3bk^{3/2}+\frac{16bk^5}{\sqrt v}+12b^2k^{9/2}\notag\\
 &\hspace{15mm}\le(3+16\sqrt{32}+12)\delta_0<\tfrac12.\label{eq:relative}
\end{align}
For the last term use $b\sqrt k\le1$; no uncontrolled $O_k$ is used.
Thus the exact physical bulk drift satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:exactdrift}
 P_{\ge k}D_s(\rho)P_{\ge k}\le-\kappa P_{\ge k},
 \qquad\kappa=\frac{\ell vb^2}{16}>0.
\end{equation}
Also $\kappa\le k$ under \eqref{eq:window}. No negative sign is imposed
on the root sector, whose finite remainder is retained below.

The exact entropy identity is
\[
 S(\rho)-3k/2=b^2m-D_2(\rho\Vert\sigma).
\]
Charge orthogonality gives
\[
 \Tr(\sigma Z^2)=\frac{\ell^2vb^2}4
                   +\frac{9\ell^2v^2b^4}{64k}.
\]
For $|z|\le1/2$,
$|(1+z)\ln(1+z)-z-z^2/2|\le2|z|^3/3$.
Joint spectral calculus applies since $[Z,\sigma]=0$.
The correction to $D_2$ beyond $\ell vb^2/8$, divided by the intended
gain $\ell vb^2/16$, is at most
\begin{equation}\label{eq:entropyrem}
 \frac98 b^2v/k+\norm Z\left[\frac83+\frac32b^2v/k\right]<\frac12
\end{equation}
under \eqref{eq:window}, using $v\le k^2$ and \eqref{eq:Z}.
Therefore the same actual state has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:entropy}
 S(\rho)-3k/2\ge\frac{\ell vb^2}{32}
                   \ge\frac{\ell b^2k^2}{1024}.
\end{equation}

For \eqref{eq:kchoice} and $0<\beta\le2^{-8}$, one has $k\ge2$,
$k\ge\beta^{-1/2}/16$ and $bk^4\le2^{-12}$.
The inherited operator-drift theorem, reproduced in
Section~\ref{sec:inheritedcoding}, applies to \eqref{eq:exactdrift}.
It gives actual reliable rate $S(\rho)/k$ and the $\tau_\pi$
coherent-information limit. Divide \eqref{eq:entropy} by $k$ to obtain
\eqref{eq:rate}. This proves Theorem~\ref{thm:rate}.

\section{Consequences, failures and the remaining scientific boundary}

\subsection{What the new result changes}

Cycle6 proved that benefit per call in this local operator variational
problem diverges, using only guarded nonnegative middle walks. The new
classification uses every complete charge pattern, including legitimate
contributions from words that exchange an original bath color. It closes
the full-prefix growth question with $v_k/k^2\to1/8$, identifies the
optimizer, and makes the preceding conditional $\beta^{7/2}$ transfer
an unconditional communication theorem. The scalar certificate is stronger
than a favorable expectation or a numerical minimum.

This is actual receiving use of inherited science: the new word theorem
supplies a previously unproved premise; the unchanged nonlinear/coding
interface then supplies a new achievable asymptotic power. Delivery,
indexing and bounded replay are not themselves this receiving use, and
no causal claim about a prompting instrument or research process follows.

The strongest existing internal comparators remain the correlated pair
rate, the strictly better non-scalar four-call state, and the Cycle6
$\beta^{64/17}$ construction. The new exponent improves the attainable
small-angle order. The coarse bound in \eqref{eq:rate} at $\beta=1/512$
is slightly below the preserved pair floor $\beta^4/1024$; we retain the
stronger existing pointwise bound there. We do not claim a new finite-angle
optimum or tune a coefficient to manufacture one.

For fixed $k$, the preserved restricted coherent-information excess is
$\Theta_k(\beta^4)$; its controlled logarithmic window does not include
arbitrary $k(\beta)$. Here $k\asymp\beta^{-1/2}$ is an outer parameter
choice made before the horizon. It does not contradict that fixed-block
law and does not settle general capacity, feedback equality or a converse.

\subsection{A reusable operation and necessary premises}

The exported interface is Lemma~\ref{lem:trichotomy} together with
\eqref{eq:Hblocks} and \eqref{eq:scalar}. Given the exact bulk
insertion/pop algebra and fair charge-product reference, it replaces
the full prefix minimization by explicit input-color blocks. Applying it
to another device requires those same identities; it is not a generic
theorem that feedback or bath correlations are irrelevant.

Correct one-site marginals are insufficient for the minimizing prefix.
For charge word $001$ and endpoints $i=0,j=2$, the two raw words map
input color $z$ and bath $(B_0,B_1)$ to the same output input color $B_0$
but baths $(z,B_1)$ and $(B_1,z)$. Against $I/4$, their contractions
are $I/2$ and $I/4$, leaving $I/4$ with normalized square $1/16$.
Against $\int(\ket u\bra u)^{\otimes2}d\mu(u)$ they cancel, despite
the same maximally mixed one-site marginals. The correlations in the
analytic witness are essential to this cancellation. They are not a
new physical bath resource.

The earlier failed word $100111$ with endpoints $1,5$ has $h=d=1$.
It still fails the stronger bath-independent shortcut, but now receives
its legitimate lower $1/4$ through \eqref{eq:criticalsquare}. The failure
was of that shortcut, not evidence that these patterns contributed zero.
Likewise a single relative transposition does not force a positive
contracted contribution in the third case.

The new enlarged classification is not a whole-bath grading theorem.
At vacuum a raw toggle can preserve input charge through $R$; the old
vacuum counterexample remains in the preserved source. The finite root
remainder, the $r^2$ compression factor, auxiliary CPTP normalization and
polar repair are all retained in the actual code. Removing any of these
scope conditions is not a change of notation.

\subsection{Closest primary theorem and priority boundary}

Giovannetti--Burgarth--Mancini \cite{GBM}, Definition 1 and Theorem 2,
give a zero-error coding result when a multi-use channel has a
subexponential effective environment dimension. This is the closest
ordinary coding mechanism behind the inherited compression argument.
Our infinite bath is not globally a finite Kraus environment: selected
input drift, spectral-type restriction, uniform initial-coordinate
compression and normalization repairs supply the required approximate
finite operation. The new contribution is the model-specific exact
susceptibility and its admissible small-angle construction, not the
general finite-error-set coding principle.

Kretschmann--Werner \cite{KW}, Theorem 13, assume finite-dimensional
forgetful memory. We do not prove those hypotheses for this bath, and
selected-input inward drift does not replace them. Their coding theorem
therefore cannot be invoked to turn block-product inputs into independent
physical channels. These focused primary comparisons do not establish
historical priority for the present exact result. Internal checks are
not external expert certification.

\subsection{Cost scope and explicit continuation}

The optimizer is now specified without a finite-dimensional numerical
minimization. Its matrix still has input dimension $3^k$, and the
inherited spectral-type exponent and Lyapunov constants can be enormous.
No efficient encoder, decoder, useful blocklength or physical device
implementation follows. The exact replay receipts record process runtime
and memory; inference, discovery and review totals remain unknown.

The sharp $v_k$ question is closed at its stated full-prefix variational
scope. The chosen next question is the nonlinear range for the now
explicit input direction. Determine the true full-prefix operator bound
on
\[
 \Tr_A[((bX+b^2Y)\otimes I)R_b]
\]
and whether exact inward drift survives beyond $bk^4=O(1)$. The current
$4k^5b^2$ derivative estimate is a proved sufficient bound, not a
structural obstruction. Any larger admissible window must retain input
positivity, entropy, root correction and the same arbitrary-reference
coding operation. No improved window or sharper capacity exponent is
claimed at this checkpoint. The broader mission remains active.

\section{Inherited operator drift and the full coding consequence}\label{sec:inheritedcoding}

This section reproduces the actual Cycle5 proof \cite{Cycle5}, carried unchanged through Cycle6 apart from labels. Its hypotheses are verified by \eqref{eq:exactdrift}; no fresh-channel replacement is made.

\begin{lemma}[Homogeneous increments]\label{lem:grade}
For any charge-preserving $k$-call density $\rho$, its normal bath channel
has Kraus operators of homogeneous depth degree
$\Delta\in\{-k,\ldots,k\}$. Consequently there are positive effects
$A_\Delta$ such that
\[
 \sum_\Delta A_\Delta=I,\quad [A_\Delta,L]=0,\quad
 D_s(\rho)=\sum_\Delta\Delta A_\Delta,
 \tag{C1}
\]
and, as a positive quadratic-form identity,
\[
 \Phi_\rho^*e^{tL}
 =\left(\sum_\Delta e^{t\Delta}A_\Delta\right)e^{tL}.
 \tag{C2}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Diagonalize $\rho$ in joint eigenvectors of $Q_k$, and take computational
output words $o$. Charge conservation gives each corresponding bath Kraus
operator degree $\Delta=q-N(o)$:
$[L,K]=\Delta K$. The exchange $R$ has degree zero and satisfies this
identity at the vacuum as well. Set $A_\Delta=\sum_{\deg K=\Delta}K^*K$.
Trace preservation proves their sum is $I$; homogeneity proves they commute
with $L$ and yields (C1)--(C2) on finite-coordinate vectors. Positive
quadratic forms and monotone limits extend the exponential identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform operator drift gives actual reliable codes]
\label{thm:operatorcode}
Fix $k$, $0<\beta<1$, and a charge-preserving density $\rho$.
Suppose its exact drift obeys
\[
 P_{\ge k}D_s(\rho)P_{\ge k}\le-\kappa P_{\ge k},
 \qquad0<\kappa\le k.
 \tag{C3}
\]
Then one deterministic unassisted code sequence, independent of the bath
initializer, has rate at least $S(\rho)/k$ and pointwise vanishing
full-diamond error for every fixed normal initializer.
For the actual $\tau_\pi$, the product-block coherent-information limit
is also $S(\rho)/k$. No scalar depth chain or radial state preservation
is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Set
\[
 t=\kappa/(2k^2),\quad
 \lambda=1-\kappa^2/(4k^2),\quad
 C=\frac{e^{2kt}}{1-\lambda}.
 \tag{C4}
\]
Since $kt\le1/2$, for $|\Delta|\le k$ Taylor's inequality gives
$e^{t\Delta}\le1+t\Delta+k^2t^2$.
Multiply by each positive effect in (C1) and sum. On the high-depth sector
this is at most $\lambda I$. The low-depth sector reaches depth at most
$2k-1$, so (C2) gives the global form inequality
\[
 \Phi_\rho^*e^{tL}
 \le\lambda e^{tL}+e^{2kt}P_{<k}.
 \tag{C5}
\]
There are no low/high cross terms. Iteration yields
\[
 M_m\le\lambda^m M_0+C(1-\lambda^m).
 \tag{C6}
\]
For any coherent initializer supported in $P_{\le A}$,
\[
 \Pr(L_m>H)\le(1+C)e^{-t(H-A)}.
 \tag{C7}
\]

Let $d=\rank\rho\le3^k$, using only positive eigenvalues. A
maximum-probability exact spectral type $T_m$ of $\rho^{\otimes m}$
has probability $w_m\ge(m+1)^{-d}$, rank $D_m$, and
\[
 T_m/D_m\le\rho^{\otimes m}/w_m,\qquad
 \log_2D_m/m\longrightarrow S(\rho).
 \tag{C8}
\]
For completeness, with type counts $n_i$, maximality against a transfer
from count $i$ to $j$ gives
$n_i/p_i\le(n_j+1)/p_j$. Summing against $p_i$ shows
$n_j\ge mp_j-1$, hence every frequency tends to $p_j$.
The type scalar eigenvalue and the polynomial mass give the rank limit.
This is an operator statement in the actual eigenbasis, even if eigenvectors
are entangled across calls.

Choose
\[
 A_m=\lfloor\log_2(m+1)\rfloor,\quad
 H_m=A_m+\left\lceil
 \frac{(d+16)\ln(m+1)+\ln(1+C)}t\right\rceil,\quad
 \epsilon_m=(m+1)^{-16}.
 \tag{C9}
\]
Apply (C7) to the uniform state on $P_{\le A_m}$, and then (C8).
Mean overflow for the type subspace and that initial bath space is at
most $\epsilon_m$. The inherited joint compression lemma, reproduced
in Appendix~\ref{app:compression}, gives
\[
 K_m\ge\left\lceil
 \frac{\lfloor D_m/2\rfloor}{(r_{A_m}r_{H_m})^4}
 \right\rceil,\quad
 e_m=r_{A_m}\sqrt{2\epsilon_m}+(1-2\epsilon_m)^{-1/2}-1\to0.
 \tag{C10}
\]
It retains spectral expurgation, the $r^2$ positive-block factor,
the auxiliary CPTP normalization and its polar repair.
The actual encoder is isometric; no auxiliary normalization is implemented.
For every fixed normal $\tau$, the full-diamond error is at most
\[
 \min\{2,\ 4e_m+2\sqrt{1-\Tr(P_{\le A_m}\tau)}\}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{C11}
\]
In half-diamond convention this is
$\min\{1,2e_m+\sqrt{1-\Tr(P_{\le A_m}\tau)}\}$.
This follows by comparing $\tau$ outside the type domination with its
normalized coordinate truncation. Thus no type-probability factor multiplies
an uncontrolled initial tail. Since $\log r_{A_m}r_{H_m}=O(\log m)$
for this fixed device and block, (C10) proves the rate.

For $\tau_\pi$, $t\le1/2<\ln3$ ensures a finite initial exponential
moment. Equations (C6)--(C7) bound mean depth uniformly. The bath Gibbs
density $g=4^{-L}/4$ has trace one and
$-\log_2g=2L+2$, so $S(E_m)\le2\E L_m+2$.
This entropy-energy inequality also follows by finite cutoffs and monotone
limits; finite mean guarantees finite entropy. The initial bath entropy
is finite. Unitary entropy conservation, subadditivity and Araki--Lieb give
\[
 mS(\rho)-2S(E_m)\le\Ic(\rho^{\otimes m},\mathcal N_{km}^{\tau_\pi})
 \le mS(\rho).
 \tag{C12}
\]
Divide by $km$. Extra data-independent calls may fill incomplete final
blocks; all count and do not alter already emitted output marginals.
\end{proof}

The initializer-independent code quantifier is pointwise in the normal initializer.
It is distinct from the inherited uniform all-normal restricted upper bound.
No invariant initializer is prepared. The boundary term in (C5) is essential:
a global strict exponential contraction would contradict trace preservation
and $e^{tL}\ge I$.

\section{The inherited joint compression operation}\label{app:compression}

This appendix restates the actual inherited proof \cite{Cycle2}, rather than
using a summary as a coding theorem. It records the normalization and coherent
initializer quantifiers needed by the new receiving use. The underlying
finite-error mechanism is established \cite{KLV,GBM,Tverberg}.

\begin{lemma}[Finite-error-set code]\label{lem:finitecode}
A CPTP map from a $D$-dimensional input space to a finite output space, with
at most $k$ Kraus operators, has an exact subspace code of dimension at least
$\lceil D/k^4\rceil$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The self-adjoint operator space spanned by $A_i^*A_j$ contains $I$ and has
complex dimension $d\le k^2$. Choose a Hermitian basis $I,H_2,\ldots,H_d$.
Greedily choose orthonormal vectors $v_1,\ldots,v_N$ with all cross matrix
elements of each $H_j$ zero: after $m$ choices avoid the span of at most
$md$ vectors $H_jv_a$. This yields $N\ge\lceil D/d\rceil$.
Their diagonal expectation vectors lie in $\mathbb R^{d-1}$. The affine
Tverberg theorem partitions any $(r-1)d+1$ such points into $r$ groups with
intersecting convex hulls. Choose $r=\lceil N/d\rceil$. Square roots of the
corresponding convex weights form orthonormal code vectors with identical
diagonal expectations and zero cross expectations. Thus
$C^*A_i^*A_jC=\gamma_{ij}I_r$ and $r\ge\lceil D/k^4\rceil$.

Choose syndrome vectors of Gram matrix $\gamma$ and send
$A_iC\ket\psi\mapsto\ket\psi\otimes\ket{\xi_i}$ on the error-image span.
The Gram identities make this map isometric; extend it with an external
syndrome space to a recovery isometry. All outcomes are recovered. Nothing
requires equal input and output dimensions.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Joint compression from averaged overflow]\label{lem:compression}
Let $W:A\otimes E_0\to B\otimes E$ be an isometry, with
$\dim A=D$, $\dim E_0=r<\infty$, and let $P$ be a rank-$d$ bath projection.
Define
\[
 F=W^*(I_B\otimes(I-P))W,\qquad L_A=\frac1r\Tr_{E_0}F.
\]
If $D^{-1}\Tr L_A\le\epsilon$, $0<\epsilon<1/2$, there is an isometric
encoder and deterministic decoder of dimension
\[
 K\ge\left\lceil\frac{\lfloor D/2\rfloor}{(rd)^4}\right\rceil
\]
whose recovered logical channel has full-diamond error at most $4e$ for
every initializer supported in $E_0$, provided $e<1$, where
\[
 e=r\sqrt{2\epsilon}+(1-2\epsilon)^{-1/2}-1.
\]
The same code is valid for coherent initializers and arbitrary external
references. More strongly its recovered joint isometry is within $2e$ of
$I_{\rm logical}\otimes Z_0$ for a bath/syndrome isometry $Z_0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $Q=1_{[0,2\epsilon]}(L_A)$. Eigenvalue counting gives
$\dim QA\ge D/2$ and $QL_AQ\le2\epsilon Q$.
For any positive operator $X$ on $A\otimes\mathbb C^r$,
\[
 X\le r(\Tr_{\mathbb C^r}X)\otimes I_r.
 \tag{A1}
\]
Indeed pinching into $r$ diagonal bath blocks gives
$X\le r\sum_v(I\otimes|v\rangle\langle v|)X(I\otimes|v\rangle\langle v|)$
by Cauchy--Schwarz, and each positive diagonal block is at most their sum.
For $F_Q=(Q\otimes I)F(Q\otimes I)$, (A1) implies
$F_Q\le r^2 QL_AQ\otimes I\le2r^2\epsilon I$. The discarded isometry
amplitude is therefore at most $r\sqrt{2\epsilon}$, uniformly on joint
inputs, including entangled references.

For bases $v$ of $E_0$ and $w$ of $PE$, put
\[
 B_{wv}=\frac1{\sqrt r}\bra w(I\otimes P)W\ket v\big|_{QA}.
\]
Then $\sum B_{wv}^*B_{wv}=I_{QA}-QL_AQ$. The auxiliary operator
$J=(I_{QA}-QL_AQ)^{-1/2}$ satisfies
$\norm{J-I}\le(1-2\epsilon)^{-1/2}-1$, and $B_{wv}J$ define a CPTP map
with at most $rd$ Kraus operators. Lemma~\ref{lem:finitecode} gives an
isometric code inclusion $C$ and recovery isometry $D_{\rm rec}$ with
$D_{\rm rec}B_{wv}JC\ket\psi=\ket\psi\otimes\ket{\xi_{wv}}$.
Consequently
\[
 (D_{\rm rec}\otimes I)(I\otimes P)W(JC\otimes I_{E_0})
 =I_{\rm logical}\otimes Z,\quad
 Z\ket v=\sqrt r\sum_w\ket{\xi_{wv}}\otimes\ket w.
 \tag{A2}
\]
This uses all initial and final bath indices, not one pure initial bath.

The actual encoder is $C$, never $JC$. Its recovered joint isometry
$H=(D_{\rm rec}\otimes I)W(C\otimes I)$ differs from $I\otimes Z$ by at
most $e$. Since $H$ is isometric, every singular value of $Z$ lies in
$[1-e,1+e]$. Its polar part $Z_0$ is therefore isometric and
$\norm{Z-Z_0}\le e$, giving $\norm{H-I\otimes Z_0}\le2e$.
Two isometries differing in norm by $u$ induce channels at full-diamond
distance at most $2u$: apply the pure-state trace-distance inequality after
tensoring a reference, then trace unused outputs. This proves $4e$, or $2e$
in half-diamond convention. The recovery syndrome is retained in the dilation;
discarding it to define a logical marginal is not an internal bath reset.
\end{proof}

The $r$ in (A1) cannot be removed: for the maximally entangled projector
$X=|\Omega_r\rangle\langle\Omega_r|$ one has $\norm X=1$ but
$(\Tr_E X)\otimes I=I/r$. Thus a bath-averaged effect of norm $1/r^2$ can
hide a joint failure of probability one. Likewise the projected channel is
not automatically trace preserving. The auxiliary $J$ and the polar repair
are mathematical comparisons; implementing $J$ as a deterministic encoder
or omitting its error would be invalid.
