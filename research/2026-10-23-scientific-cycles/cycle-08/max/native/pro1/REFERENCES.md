## Closest coding theorem

V. Giovannetti, D. Burgarth and S. Mancini, *Communication Through a Quantum Link*,
Physical Review A79,012311(2009), arXiv0810.0170.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0810.0170

Definition1 calls a multi-use family perfect-memory when an environment dilation
can have subexponential dimension. Theorem2 supplies large zero-error quantum
subspace codes in that setting. This is the closest ordinary mechanism behind
the inherited finite-error-set code. Our selected-input Lyapunov bound, spectral
type, initial-coordinate compression and normalization repairs establish the
needed approximate finite operation for the infinite bath. The Cycle7 advance
is the model-specific exact susceptibility and admissible small-angle input,
not invention of that general coding principle.

## A theorem whose hypotheses must not be silently assumed

D. Kretschmann and R. F. Werner, *Quantum Channels with Memory*,
Physical Review A72,062323(2005), arXivquant-ph/0502106v2.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0502106

Theorem13 states coding theorems for finite-dimensional forgetful memory.
Selected-input inward drift on the present infinite tree bath neither proves
those assumptions nor makes repeated blocks independent physical channels.
We retain the complete inherited same-bath proof rather than invoking this
theorem outside its scope.

integral, walk reflection and generating-function convolution are ordinary tools.
