PRIMARY KNOWN-METHOD BOUNDARIES THAT CHANGE THIS CONTINUATION

1. Terhal--Horodecki, Schmidt number for density matrices,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/9911117
Schmidt-number monotonicity and rank-R Bell overlap<=R/d are standard. The new
full-support seed/diagonal-factor argument supplies the stronger input-specific
Schmidt-number collapse used here. We give its complete proof rather than infer
it from a numerical E_F or support test. No exhaustive priority claim follows.

2. Gheorghiu--Griffiths, Entanglement transformations using separable operations,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0705.0369
Their known pure-state rigidity covers the exact-full-Bell calibration used in
the separate robust distance theorem. It is not the new state-dependent seed
lemma, and its exact preservation premise is not silently relaxed.

3. Regula--Fang--Wang--Gu, One-shot entanglement distillation beyond LOCC,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1906.01648 , Section3.4 and Theorem19.
Operational simplifications for states ALREADY maximally correlated are established.
Those results do not grant an LOCC map putting an arbitrary source inside that
support. Their discussion also warns that an MC output restriction is material
for broader operation classes. We distinguish product-Kraus SEP from merely
separability-preserving SEPP; the new theorem uses the former. It is not a new
generic distillation LP, environment-assistance theorem or coherence equivalence.

4. Dong--Gao--Ou--Yao--Zhou, arXiv:2606.17729v3,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.17729v3
The current version proves PODS hypercontractive/log-Sobolev tensorization,
including exact qubit cases and approximate qudit results. A hierarchy over
D^N-dimensional chains is not a qubit semigroup just because D=2. Its smallest
nonzero eigenspace rules out PODS in ANY basis. The paper's theorem cannot
therefore be cited as the missing ordinary hierarchy inequality.

5. Fang, Entanglement cost of quantum depolarization,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.00187
Its coefficient-matrix entropy/semigroup formation method and tuned-qubit
application are current known machinery, not a new generic approach invented
here. Our actual non-PODS hierarchy needs its own proof; merely rewriting the
problem as a log-Sobolev constant is not a solution or efficient certificate.

6. Ahmed--Smith, No cardinality bound for squashed entanglement,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.14031 , Theorem2.
This preprint proves strict finite-auxiliary improvement for a dephased Bell pair.
An actual two-slot chi queue outputs Phi_2 tensor that pair, so the theorem
transfers directly. A fixed auxiliary optimization supplies an upper candidate,
not the universal lower resource certificate. The known result does not exclude
an analytic dimension-independent CMI lower bound. External acceptance of this
recent primary preprint remains separate from reading its theorem.
