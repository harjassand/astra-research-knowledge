RECEIVING QUESTION AND SCOPE

The parent is considering a private-noise-insensitive, tensor-stable formation
witness for actual Bell-prefix outputs. For a pure bipartite vector write its
coefficient matrix X, normalized by Tr X*X=1. A Bell projection on the first i
D-sites of one task has probability ||E_i(X)||_2^2, where
 E_i(X)=(I_(D^i)/D^i) tensor Tr_(first i)(X).
Thus the candidate universal r-task entropy inequality is
 S(X*X) >= m sum_(a=1)^r sum_(i=1)^N ||E_(a,i)(X)||_2^2-r C_D,   (T)
with m=ln D, C_D independent of N,r, and completely arbitrary nonnormal X.
If established, convex-roof averaging would make (T) a resource converse without
the raw complementary-uncertainty penalty. The received single-chain proof does
not establish (T). The following three results block specific automatic routes.

I. HIERARCHICAL GENERATOR IS NOT PODS IN ANY BASIS

Let H=(C^D)^tensor N, N>=2,D>=2, E_0=id, E_N(X)=Tr(X)I/D^N, and
 L_N=sum_(i=1)^N(id-E_i).
All E_i are self-adjoint Hilbert--Schmidt orthogonal projections and
 E_i E_j=E_max(i,j).
Consequently the full matrix space is the orthogonal sum
 ran E_N plus sum_(j=0)^(N-1) V_j,
 V_j=ran(E_j-E_(j+1)).
On V_j the generator has eigenvalue N-j. In particular its eigenvalue-one space is
 V_(N-1)=I_(D^(N-1)) tensor {Z in M_D:Tr Z=0}.                 (1)

A linear map has positive off-diagonal scaling (PODS) in an orthonormal basis
if each off-diagonal matrix unit |u><v|,u!=v, is mapped to a nonnegative scalar
multiple of itself; the diagonal algebra is invariant. For a semigroup having
PODS for all times, every such off-diagonal matrix unit is an eigenvector of its
generator. We prove no basis can give PODS to exp(-t L_N).

Every nonzero operator in (1) has rank at least D^(N-1)>=2. Hence no rank-one
off-diagonal matrix unit can belong to the eigenvalue-one space. If all
off-diagonal matrix units were generator eigenvectors, all of them would be
orthogonal to (1), because L_N is self-adjoint and their eigenvalues are not one.
It would follow that (1) is contained in the diagonal algebra of that basis.
This is impossible: I tensor traceless M_D contains noncommuting operators,
for example embedded Pauli X and Z on a two-dimensional subspace of the last
site, whereas all diagonal operators commute. This proves basis-independent
failure of PODS. The proof allows any entangled change of basis on H.

L_N is a legitimate primitive tracial symmetric quantum Markov generator:
each id-E_i generates a convex combination of id and E_i, and these commuting
semigroups compose. The obstruction is precisely PODS, not positivity,
reversibility, primitivity or the treatment of nonnormal coefficient matrices.

Known-machinery boundary. Dong--Gao--Ou--Yao--Zhou, arXiv:2606.17729v3,
Theorems 1.2,1.3,3.14, give approximate tensorization for reversible PODS
semigroups and exact tensorization in their qubit case. The hierarchy here has
local dimension D^N and is outside PODS even when D=2. Those results therefore
do not prove (T). Fang, arXiv:2610.00187, Lemma2 and Lemma3, demonstrates that
matrix entropy plus carefully tuned tensorizing qubit semigroups already yields
formation converses. The general method is established capital; a correct new
receiving theorem must prove its special hierarchical tensorization premise.

II. CRITICAL CONDITIONAL/ANCILLARY COMPLETION HAS A LINEAR DEFECT

A tempting completion of the single-chain inequality is to demand, for every
unpriced ancillary quantum register R and every Hilbert--Schmidt-normalized X
on H tensor R,
 S(H|R)_(X*X) >= m sum_(i=1)^N ||(E_i tensor id_R)(X)||_2^2-C_D. (C)
This is false for every N-independent C_D.

PROOF. Let n=D^N, dim R=n, and choose X=Phi_n, the rank-one projector onto a
maximally entangled vector between H and R. Then X*X=Phi_n, Tr X*X=1,
 S(H|R)=-ln n=-Nm.
Across a split of the first i sites from the rest, Phi_n=Phi_(D^i) tensor
Phi_(D^(N-i)), up to reordering. Applying E_i on its H half gives
 (I_(D^i)/D^i)_H tensor (I_(D^i)/D^i)_R tensor Phi_(D^(N-i)).
Its squared Hilbert--Schmidt norm is D^(-2i). Therefore (C) would require
 C_D >= Nm + m (1-D^(-2N))/(D^2-1).                         (2)
This grows linearly in N. QED.

Equivalently, a complete relative entropy version using
 D(Y || I_H/n tensor Y_R)=ln n-S(H|R)_Y
and the critical m times hierarchical Dirichlet form also needs a linear defect.
Changing to a different CB norm/entropy definition requires a fresh derivation;
the counterexample does not dismiss every complete LSI formulation.

IMPORTANT NONIMPLICATION. Result (2) does NOT disprove (T). If R is another
charged output chain, X=Phi_n is the coefficient matrix of a state that is a
product across Alice versus Bob, with entanglement entropy zero. Both task
scores are bounded geometric sums. The failure is the attempted unpriced
ancillary conditional inequality. An ordinary multichain proof could still
exploit cancellations that the termwise conditional completion loses.

III. EXACT EXTRACTION WITH SMALL RETURN DOES NOT STABILIZE EXACT SUPPORT

Fix D>=2 and integer n>=1. Let H_n=sum_(i=1)^n 1/i, and let each local memory
be C^(Dn), with basis indexed j=1,...,Dn. Define the two pure states
 |mu_n>=sum_(j=1)^n (j H_n)^(-1/2)|j j>,
 |psi_n>=sum_(j=1)^(Dn) [D ceil(j/D) H_n]^(-1/2)|j j>.       (3)
The returned vector mu_n is embedded in the SAME complete memory space.
Both states are normalized. Their one-dimensional supports have zero
intersection, because psi_n has positive amplitudes at j>n and mu_n does not.

Specify the faithful external product input eta=(I_D/D)_A tensor(I_D/D)_B.
The device genuinely discards this local input and implements the local isometry
 V|D(i-1)+d> = |d>_out tensor |i>_memory,
  i=1,...,n, d=1,...,D.                                    (4)
V maps all Dn input basis vectors to orthonormal vectors in C^D tensor C^(Dn).
It is a CPTP isometric channel on the complete memory and can be implemented
with a fresh pure local ancilla. The discarded faithful input and preparation
work are not retained; retaining their purifiers/garbage would be a different
complete-memory contract. No entangled catalyst, other shared flag or hidden
work register is present. This is a fixed finite-round one-outcome local device
for each chosen n,D, with no communication, on all inputs.

By grouping the D repeated coefficients in (3), the exact complete first call is
 (V_A tensor V_B)|psi_n> = |Phi_D>_out tensor |mu_n>_memory. (5)
Thus first Bell output is exact and purity gives exact decoupling. The device
does not produce merely a high-fidelity Bell state.

The complete memory half-trace return error is exactly
 delta_(n,D)=sqrt(1-a_(n,D)^2),
 a_(n,D)=1/(sqrt(D) H_n) sum_(j=1)^n [j ceil(j/D)]^(-1/2).   (6)
Since D ceil(j/D)<=j+D-1,
 a_(n,D) >= (1/H_n) sum_(j=1)^n 1/sqrt[j(j+D-1)]
           >= [H_(n+D-1)-H_(D-1)]/H_n
           >= 1-H_(D-1)/H_n.
For H_n>=H_(D-1), this gives
 delta_(n,D)^2 <= 2 H_(D-1)/H_n.                           (7)
Hence delta tends to zero at each fixed D, while the exact support intersection
stays zero. For every n, all later output-memory correlations are explicitly
tracked by iteration of (4): if p_j=[D ceil(j/D)H_n]^-1, then after t calls
 |Psi_t>=sum_j sqrt(p_j) |digits_t(j-1)>_Aout
                         |digits_t(j-1)>_Bout
                         |1+floor((j-1)/D^t)>_Amemory
                         |1+floor((j-1)/D^t)>_Bmemory.     (8)
The output digit string is read in the fixed little-endian wire order.
Formula (8) contains every correlation; later marginals are not assumed iid.
At large t the memory is |11> and the finite stock is exhausted. This family
therefore does not give recurrence of one fixed normal memory state.

COMPLETE SUPPLY ACCOUNTING. The initial pure target psi_n has Schmidt rank Dn.
Every branch of an exact factory for this pure target must output this same
pure state. LOCC from Phi_M cannot increase Schmidt rank, so every preselected
branch with nonzero probability needs M>=Dn. Supplying Phi_(Dn) is sufficient
by ordinary pure-state LOCC dilution (the uniform Schmidt vector is majorized
by the target's Schmidt vector). Thus exact deterministic AND exact expected
preselected supply both cost ln(Dn), with zero initialization error.
Its entropy of entanglement is
 E(psi_n)=ln D+E(mu_n)
        =ln D+ln H_n+(1/H_n) sum_(i=1)^n (ln i)/i.          (9)
The usual vanishing-global-error iid pure-state formation rate is this entropy.
The entropy difference in (5) is exactly ln D; no entanglement is free.
For r exact copies the Schmidt-rank charge is r ln(Dn). All physical local
reset, pure-ancilla and disposal costs remain separate from entanglement supply;
no physical efficiency or energy advantage is asserted.

This is an explicit exact-output orientation of the harmonic embezzling
construction, not a historically new embezzler: psi_n is exactly the ordered
Schmidt-coefficient vector of mu_n tensor Phi_D in van Dam--Hayden's argument.
Choosing that nearby vector as the INITIAL state makes the emitted Bell state
exact. Its receiving use here is narrow: no continuity of support intersection
or exact-support layer can be inferred from complete trace return alone.
It does not defeat trace-weighted support tests, smooth Schmidt-number methods,
or the received finite-output prefix argument. The large rank/entropy costs
do not beat ordinary matched stock.

DECISION AND REMAINING GAP

The hierarchy route remains mathematically substantive, but it needs a new
ordinary product-hierarchy entropy estimate or a direct counterexample. It
cannot be completed by citing PODS tensorization, inserting an unpriced quantum
ancilla into the scalar proof, or assuming trace-return stabilizes exact support.
No universal stock-saving reservoir, generic formation optimum or physical
advantage has been established. These are structural obstructions to particular
proof routes, not evidence against the still-open ordinary multichain target.
