CYCLE7 INDEPENDENT SUPPORT-RANK AUDIT
22 October 2026. Agent: cycle7_mc_support_audit.
Natural logarithms. Analytic result; no numerical or historical-novelty claim.

VERDICT

PASS, with the exact-support and full-product-nuisance hypotheses below.
The proposed product-Kraus lemma is valid. It excludes even state-dependent,
joint, output-only SEP normalization of the actual unflagged queue into a fixed
maximally correlated support while retaining a linear Bell-prefix score.
This is stronger in processing scope than Cycle6's pairwise rigidity, but it
has a different, explicitly exact target-support premise. It does not rule out
approximate normalization, another normal form, or clock-assisted processing.

1. PRECISE FINITE-DIMENSIONAL STATEMENT

Let K>=1. The input local spaces are
 A=A0 tensor A1, B=B0 tensor B1, with dim A0=dim B0=K.
Let |Phi_K>=K^(-1/2) sum_x |x>_A0|x>_B0 and
 S=span{|Phi_K>} tensor (A1 tensor B1), with projector P_S.
The nuisance space is the ENTIRE product A1 tensor B1. It need not be in a
separable state when testing inputs supported on S.

Let rho have support exactly S and be strictly positive on S. Let Gamma be a
separable CP map, Gamma(X)=sum_l (A_l tensor B_l)X(A_l tensor B_l)^dagger.
Trace preservation is not needed for the first conclusion. Output dimensions
are finite. Choose matched orthonormal output vectors |i>_A',|i>_B', and put
 W=span{|i>_A'|i>_B':1<=i<=d}.
Suppose Gamma(rho) has support contained in W, EXACTLY.

Then for each product Kraus operator L_l=A_l tensor B_l there is a set I_l
of at most K matched indices such that
 L_l(S) is contained in span{|ii>:i in I_l}.             (1)
The sets I_l can differ. Consequently, for every positive sigma supported on S,
Gamma(sigma) belongs to the cone of operators with Schmidt number at most K.
If its trace is positive, its normalized state has Schmidt number at most K
and entanglement of formation at most ln K. In particular this holds for every
state sigma if Gamma is trace preserving.

2. POSITIVITY PROPAGATES THE EXACT SUPPORT CONDITION

Write Q=I-P_W. Since Gamma(rho) is positive and supported on W,
 0=Tr[Q Gamma(rho)]
  =sum_l ||Q L_l rho^(1/2)||_HS^2.
Every summand is nonnegative, so Q L_l rho^(1/2)=0 for every l. The range of
rho^(1/2) is S in finite dimension. Hence Q L_l P_S=0: each product Kraus
operator sends the WHOLE subspace S into W. There is no cancellation between
outcomes, reliance on a chosen ensemble, or selection of successful outcomes.
Strict positivity is only used for this support propagation.

3. PRODUCT-KRAUS RANK ARGUMENT

Fix a product operator L=A tensor B with L(S) subset W. For nuisance vectors
u in A1 and v in B1 define the length-K complex vectors
 a_i(u)_x=<i|A(|x> tensor u),
 b_j(v)_x=<j|B(|x> tensor v).
The output coefficient at |i> tensor |j> of
 L(|Phi_K> tensor u tensor v) is K^(-1/2) a_i(u)^T b_j(v).
This is a complex bilinear pairing, so transpose, not adjoint, is correct.
Since every such input is in S and L(S) subset W,
 a_i(u)^T b_j(v)=0 for ALL u,v whenever i!=j.           (2)

Call i active if there are u_i,v_i with a_i(u_i)^T b_i(v_i)!=0.
For any finite collection of active indices form the square matrix
 G_ij=a_i(u_i)^T b_j(v_j).
By (2), G is diagonal with every diagonal entry nonzero. Thus its rank is the
number of selected active indices. But G=UV^T for matrices U,V with K columns,
so rank G<=K. There can therefore be at most K active indices.
Every inactive diagonal coefficient vanishes identically for all u,v; every
off-diagonal coefficient also vanishes by (2). Since nuisance product vectors
span A1 tensor B1, all vectors in L(S) use only the active matched coordinates.
This proves (1). It also handles zero Kraus restrictions and K=1.

4. SCHMIDT NUMBER AND ENTANGLEMENT CONSEQUENCE

For a pure vector |psi> in S, each nonzero L_l|psi> is a superposition of at
most K matched product vectors, so its Schmidt rank is at most K. Decompose
an arbitrary positive sigma supported on S into pure vectors and apply the
Kraus representation. This gives an explicit positive pure-state decomposition
of Gamma(sigma) using Schmidt rank at most K. After normalization this is the
Schmidt-number conclusion. Every vector in that decomposition has entropy of
entanglement at most ln K. The convex-roof definition gives E_F<=ln K.
It is not claimed that Gamma(sigma) as a whole uses only K matched coordinates:
different classical Kraus branches can occupy different sets I_l.

5. MAP CLASS, CLOSURE AND INFINITE-SPACE CAVEATS

All finite-input/finite-output separable CP maps admit a finite product-Kraus
representation: their Choi operator is in the finite-dimensional separable cone,
decompose it into finitely many positive product terms, and diagonalize each
factor. The cone is closed. One direct reason is that its normalized trace-one
section is the convex hull of the compact set of product pure projectors, hence
compact in finite dimension. Thus finite-round LOCC and the finite-dimensional
norm closure of LOCC are included. LOCC can use joint operations across every
output pair and task; there is no pairwise, unital, calibration or fixed-vertex
condition here. General separability-preserving maps without product Kraus are
NOT asserted to be covered.

If nuisance spaces are infinite but output is finite, Step 3 still works for
bounded A,B by taking arbitrary nuisance vectors. Step 2 still works for a
faithful trace-class rho on S because the range of rho^(1/2) is dense in S,
and the bounded operator QL vanishes on that dense range. A supplied countable
product-Kraus representation then gives the same conclusion by the trace-class
sum. No unrestricted infinite-input SEP-closure theorem is needed or claimed.
The physical queue application below is entirely finite dimensional.

The exact zeros are load bearing. No dimension-independent approximate variant
is established. A naive conversion of an approximate output-support condition
to an operator bound would pay the smallest eigenvalue of rho on S, which is
exponentially small for this queue. This audit provides no useful robustness
bound and does not assert that every approximate map can evade one.

6. ACTUAL UNFLAGGED QUEUE: ONE TASK, ARBITRARY JOINT OUTPUT MAP

For D>=2, L>=1 and 1<=N<=L, put m=ln D, nu=I_(D^2)/D^2, and use the exact
Cycle6 output marginal
 omega_(L,N)=(1/L)sum_(k=1)^L Phi_D^tensor min(k,N)
                                      tensor nu^tensor(N-k)_+.
All terms have their first pair exactly Phi_D. Thus
 omega_(L,N)=Phi_D tensor rho_tail,
where rho_tail includes the positive term (1/L)nu^tensor(N-1). Therefore
 rho_tail >= [1/(L D^(2(N-1)))] I_tail,
and omega_(L,N) is strictly positive on
 S=span{|Phi_D>} tensor H_(A,slots2..N) tensor H_(B,slots2..N).
At N=1 the nuisance spaces are one-dimensional and the statement still holds.

Take ANY output-only, trace-preserving separable channel Gamma, with arbitrary
joint cross-pair action, chosen with complete knowledge of omega_(L,N). Suppose
Gamma(omega_(L,N)) is EXACTLY supported on a fixed maximally correlated subspace
of its output. By Sections 1--4,
 E_F(Gamma(omega_(L,N)))<=m.                              (3)
No assumption that Gamma fixes a fully Bell-entangled input is used. Changing
the channel with L,N,D or with the known density matrix is permitted.

To compare the same N-pair output task, output ports must again be N D-pairs
and scores are evaluated in their declared Bell basis. The inherited one-task
prefix witness states for EVERY such finite output state zeta,
 E_F(zeta)>=m sum_(i=1)^N Tr[Phi_(1..i) zeta]-C_D.
Combining it with (3) gives the exact normalized score ceiling
 m sum_i Tr[Phi_(1..i) Gamma(omega_(L,N))]<=m+C_D.         (4)
This is valid for any fixed MC basis: for one task there is no complementary
task conditional-entropy penalty to control.

At active horizon N=L the actual queue prefixes obey
 p_i=(L-i+1)/L + [1-D^(-2(i-1))]/[L(D^2-1)].
Therefore sum_i p_i >= (L+1)/2 and the original score is at least
 m(L+1)/2. Every exact MC normalizer of the stated output-only SEP class loses
at least
 m(L-1)/2-C_D                                             (5)
of actual Bell-prefix score. The lower bound is meaningful when positive and
grows linearly with L at fixed D. More exactly, the lost score is at least
 m[(L+1)/2 + (1/[L(D^2-1)])sum_(i=1)^L(1-D^(-2(i-1)))]
 -m-C_D. No score-zero tail approximation is used.

7. JOINT NORMALIZATION ACROSS r QUEUE TASKS

For the independent source omega_(L,N)^tensor r, regroup the first Bell pair
of every task into Phi_(D^r), and treat every other slot as nuisance. Its support
is again full product nuisance. Hence an arbitrary JOINT output-only SEP map
whose actual output lies in ANY fixed MC support has
 Schmidt number<=D^r, E_F<=r m.                           (6)

For a receiving consequence involving the original r task prefixes, specify the
STANDARD computational-string MC support
 W_std=span{|x>_A|x>_B: x=(x_(a,i))_(a,i)}.
For every state in this support, Alice's computational-basis readout on all
complementary tasks leaves Bob's complementary tasks in their known pure basis
string. Hence each actual Q_a=0. The inherited r-task theorem and (6) give
 Tr(O Gamma(omega_(L,N)^r))<=r(m+C_D).                   (7)
At N=L, the per-task score loss is again bounded below by (5).
For an arbitrary MC basis entangling the task tensor factors, Q_a=0 does NOT
follow automatically. Equation (6) still holds; (7) is only claimed for the
declared standard MC target or another target separately proved to give Q=0.

8. AN ADDITIONAL INDEPENDENT CHARGED RESOURCE

Let a receiver additionally receive Phi_M, INDEPENDENT of the exact source
omega_(L,N)^tensor r. The receiver may consume or discard this entire resource;
granting that freedom makes a converse stronger than requiring its return. Any
fixed local separable ancillas and randomness independent of the source can be
absorbed into the SEP receiver. The combined input support has the form in
Section 1 with K=D^r M and the same faithful full-product nuisance. Hence an
exactly MC main output has E_F<=r m+ln M, even if auxiliary outputs are traced.
For the standard MC target in Section 7,
 Tr(O zeta)<=r(m+C_D)+ln M.                               (8)

If M is genuinely preselected with law p_M, with each branch receiving the SAME
source omega^r independently of that choice, write zeta=sum_M p_M zeta_M. The
receiver is TP, so these are the actual averaged weights, not selected success
weights. Exact support of zeta in W forces every positive-weight zeta_M into W
by positivity. Apply the rank lemma in each branch, then convexity of E_F:
 E_F(zeta)<=sum_M p_M(r m+ln M)=r m+E[ln M].
Thus (8) holds with b=E[ln M] in place of ln M. This includes countable rank
laws with finite mean cost; infinite mean cost makes the bound vacuous. Every
outcome is retained in this statement; postselection is not permitted.

If the output mean score is at least B-s, with B=(L+1)m/2, then necessarily
 b/r>=B-m-C_D-s=(L-1)m/2-C_D-s.                           (9)
More sharply use the actual source mean score instead of its lower bound B.
Thus keeping bounded per-task score loss by this exact normalization costs
additional supplied entanglement of order L. This is a necessary total initial
auxiliary supply; if a catalyst is returned it is not called consumed. No new
complete-memory return claim is inferred from a receiver allowed to consume it.

INDEPENDENCE IS LOAD BEARING. Marginal source omega^r alone does not suffice if
the additional resource selector or free receiver transcript carries inventory
side information. A factory-correlated K label can reveal the active prefix
and permits the old clock repair. If M is chosen in correlation with K and the
receiver learns its choice, conditional source states need not be faithful on
the same full-product nuisance support. The rank lemma must then be rechecked
for each conditional source; one cannot replace those states by omega^r. All
such source-correlated records belong in the complete joint input and return
model. The resource-converse statement above grants independent extra Phi_M,
not free access to a correlated clock or arbitrary source extension.

9. WHY COUNTED CLOCK ACCESS AND CATALYSTS ARE DIFFERENT

The Cycle6 exact joint output-memory state carries a current cyclic clock. Its
flag-conditioned nuisance support is not the full product space in Section 1.
Access to that explicit clock can reveal the actual active prefix and permits
the previously proved reset operation. It is outside the output-only input
support premise, even though its reduced output equals omega_(L,N).
This result therefore does not contradict the complete clock-assisted repair.

Nor may an entangled catalyst be silently added as free full-product nuisance.
A catalyst with restricted entangled support changes S; all its support and
supply must be included before applying any version of this lemma. If an extra
maximally entangled pure resource Phi_R is added, the direct lemma has K=DR
per task and gives only E_F<=ln(DR), with that resource explicitly charged.

The full-product hypothesis cannot be replaced by positivity on an arbitrary
correlated nuisance subspace. For K=1 let the nuisance support be span{|ii>},
let rho be strictly positive within that subspace, and let Gamma be identity.
Its output is MC, but its image uses arbitrarily many coordinates; that support
also contains maximally entangled vectors of arbitrarily large Schmidt rank.
Likewise a pure entangled nuisance input directly violates an unqualified K=1
Schmidt-number conclusion. Neither example is faithful on the full product
nuisance space required above.

10. RECEIVING DECISION AND EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

Retire the proposed EXACT output-only MC normalization route for the noisy queue
if it is intended to keep its linear active-horizon Bell-prefix score. Joint
processing, state dependence and cross-task adaptation cannot repair that route
under the stated exact support requirement. The remaining receiving routes must
change an actual premise: use explicitly priced memory/flag access, accept an
approximate or different normal form with a proved score/resource bound, or use
a private-noise-insensitive witness without exact MC projection.

This is a mathematical obstruction for an ordinary-stock queue, not a stock
advantage or a universal impossibility theorem for approximate reuse. It does
not settle the generic logarithmic formation gap. The elementary support-rank
argument is proved here; historical priority has not been established.
