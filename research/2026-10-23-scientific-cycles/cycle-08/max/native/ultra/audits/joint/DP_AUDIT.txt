1. EXACT J-LABEL SCORE DYNAMIC PROGRAMME

Definitions. K is uniform on {1,...,L}. The receiver gets a classical label C
with at most J positive-probability values and the unchanged conditional queue
state. Set t_c=min{k:p(c|k)>0}. With D>=2 define
 F_L(a)=a+sum_(h=1)^(L-a)D^(-h), 1<=a<=L.
The seed lemma and its attaining reset operation prove that the exact maximum
MC-normalized score for a specified interface is m E F_L(t_C), m=ln D.

For a fixed set of distinct thresholds a_z, any supported label for K=k has
a_z<=k. Since F_L is strictly increasing, the best conditional assignment
chooses the largest such threshold with probability one. Its threshold bins
are consecutive intervals. Conversely any partition into nonempty consecutive
intervals is a valid deterministic label interface whose actual support minimum
is its interval start. Thus stochastic interfaces cannot improve the optimum
over interval partitions. No stochastic convex optimization remains hidden.

Strict increase follows from
 F_L(a+1)-F_L(a)=1-D^(-(L-a))>0 for 1<=a<L.
Hence adding a threshold strictly improves the score whenever some bin has
length >=2. The optimum with at most J labels uses exactly q=min(J,L) labels.
This statement requires J>=1. The single-label and full-label cases are covered.

Define V(j,b) as the maximum UNNORMALIZED sum of F-values over a partition of
{1,...,b} into j nonempty consecutive bins, while retaining the same horizon-L
function F_L throughout. Boundary conditions are V(0,0)=0, V(0,b>0)=-infinity,
and V(j,b)=-infinity for b<j. Then
 V(j,b)=max_(a=j,...,b) [V(j-1,a-1)+(b-a+1)F_L(a)].
Proof: the last interval begins at a, has length b-a+1, and its best earlier
partition contributes V(j-1,a-1). Every permissible a is represented once.
The exact maximum normalized score is m V(q,L)/L. Maximizing partition choices
can be recovered by standard backtracking through the attaining a values.

Cost. Precompute F_L(a) in O(L) rational operations. The DP uses O(q L^2)
rational operations and O(q L) stored entries (or O(L) value memory if only the
value is needed). Every F_L(a) has denominator dividing D^L, so multiply every
objective by D^L and use integer arithmetic. DP entries then have
O(L ln D+ln L) bits: F_L<=L and unnormalized sum <=L^2. Bit complexity is
polynomial in L,J,ln D under ordinary integer arithmetic. This is a classical
control-interface optimization; it is neither an E_F optimizer nor a search
through quantum channels. It does not claim efficient physical access to C.

Independent admission by brute force over all J^L deterministic labelings at
L<=6 is sufficient to check the implemented recurrence on those cases.
For each nonempty label compute its minimum assigned K and use the actual
weight of that label. Label permutations and unused labels do not change the
value. The analytic largest-threshold argument proves the continuous stochastic
boundary beyond those admissions; finite enumeration alone would not do so.

2. MATCHED CONSUMABLE EXTRA-SUPPLY RECEIVER WITHOUT INVENTORY ACCESS

The queue output factors as first Phi_D tensor a remaining state. It therefore
contains one exact, known Bell pair usable without learning K. Independently
preselect J0 uniformly in {1,...,L}, supply Phi_(D^(J0-1)), and declare its
preselected rank and local/shared preparation records as part of the auxiliary
resource. The rank-one J0=1 branch is free. Split the supplied resource into
J0-1 Bell pairs, keep the queue's first Bell pair, route the supplied pairs into
the next ports and reset/discard every old output register not used. The final
L-port state conditional on J0 is exactly
 Phi_D^tensor J0 tensor |00><00|^tensor(L-J0).
This is standard product-string MC and has Q=0. Every supply and outcome branch
is counted. J0 is independent of K: it supplies no covert queue-clock interface.

Its complete expected preselected EXTRA entanglement cost is
 b=m E[J0-1]=(L-1)m/2.
A deterministic exact extra stock uses L-1 Bell pairs, costing (L-1)m; it can
prepare the same mixture by sampling J0 and disposing of unused Bell pairs.
These two conventions are distinct. Zero global preparation error is used.
The factory creates the specified actual target, not merely a favorable
component. Its retained shared records must be included in any complete target
for that receiving stage; no source-correlated original-K record is assumed.

For i=1,...,L, the resulting prefix probability is
 p_i'=(L-i+1)/L+(1/L)sum_(j=1)^(i-1)D^(-(i-j)),
whereas the old queue has
 p_i =(L-i+1)/L+(1/L)sum_(j=1)^(i-1)D^(-2(i-j)).
Thus p_i'>=p_i termwise, including all nonzero product-tail overlaps. Exact MC
normalization with no score loss is possible at this explicitly charged
consumptive extra cost. The seed-plus-resource lower bound
 b>=m(L-1)/2-C_D-s
for allowed total score loss s shows that its leading order is matched; no
new additive-constant optimization is being claimed.

This is a SEPARATE PRICED RECEIVING OPERATION AFTER THE QUEUE RUN. It can consume
the entire extra supply. It does not assert the augmented memory returns to its
initial state, nor convert this receiving stage into the same fixed reusable
protocol. The untouched original memory marginal can remain unchanged, but the
complete supplied resource ledger has been consumed and cannot be omitted.
Returning catalysts are a subclass of the receiver allowed to consume its
entire initially charged auxiliary resource, so the lower bound is a bound on
total initial supply, not on net catalytic consumption. Approximate embezzling,
approximate global preparation and approximate-MC output need their own
error/access analysis; they are not covered by an exact-support conclusion.

3. DECISION BOUNDARY

The DP and resets supply an exact classical-access receiving operation and
complete finite computation. The independent stock replacement supplies the
strong applicable consumable comparator under zero global initialization error.
These results close the exact-MC normalization route at its justified scope:
output-only low-cost normalization cannot erase the full-support nuisance while
retaining its order-L actual score; clock information or order-L extra supply
is needed. They do not settle the unrestricted formation/reuse logarithmic gap,
provide an advantage over ordinary stock, or establish a generic efficient
normalizer. Historical novelty and physical usefulness remain separate.
