EXACT KY FAN AUDIT — NESTED BELL/PRIVATE-NOISE QUEUE
22 October 2026

Verdict: PASS. The proposed formula is exact for arbitrary projectors of
rank D^L. The qualifier arbitrary is material: this is an upper bound for
any restricted class of receiving maps, not by itself a construction of an
LOCC map attaining it.

Definitions. Let D>=2 and L>=1 be integers. On (C^D tensor C^D)^tensor L put
Phi=|Phi_D><Phi_D|, nu=I_(D^2)/D^2, and

  omega = (1/L) sum_(k=1)^L Phi^tensor k tensor nu^tensor(L-k).

Write Pi_k=Phi^tensor k tensor I^tensor(L-k), S_k=range(Pi_k), and
n_k=dim(S_k)=D^(2(L-k)). Then

  S_L subset S_(L-1) subset ... subset S_1,
  omega=(1/L) sum_k Pi_k/n_k.

The exact Ky Fan rank-R sum, for every integer 0<=R<=D^(2L), is

  kappa_R(omega)
    := max_(P projector, rank P=R) Tr(P omega)
     = (1/L) sum_(k=1)^L min{1, R D^(-2(L-k))}.                 (1)

Proof. For each rank-R projector P,
Tr(P Pi_k)<=min{R,n_k}, so (1) is an upper bound. To attain all L bounds
simultaneously, extend the nested filtration by S_0=the whole space and
S_(L+1)={0}. Select j with n_(j+1)<=R<=n_j, where n_0=D^(2L) and
n_(L+1)=0, and select any R-dimensional subspace T satisfying

  S_(j+1) subset T subset S_j.

Such T exists by elementary finite-dimensional linear algebra. For k<=j,
T subset S_k, so Tr(P_T Pi_k)=R. For k>=j+1, S_k subset T, so the trace
is n_k. These are exactly min{R,n_k}. This includes R=0 and all ranks
larger than the support rank n_1. Thus equality holds. No commutation or
optimization assumption beyond the displayed nested supports is needed.

Equivalently, for n_(j+1)<=R<=n_j,

  kappa_R = [L-j + R sum_(k=1)^j D^(-2(L-k))]/L.               (2)

The choice of j at a shared boundary does not change this value. For
j=0, the empty sum is zero and kappa_R=1. For j>=1 the geometric sum is

  sum_(k=1)^j D^(-2(L-k))
    = D^(-2(L-j)) (1-D^(-2j))/(1-D^(-2)).

Specialization R=D^L. Equation (1) becomes exactly

  kappa_(D^L) = (1/L) sum_(k=1)^L min{1,D^(2k-L)}.            (3)

For even L=2h, h>=1,

  kappa_(D^L)
    = [h+1 + sum_(u=1)^(h-1) D^(-2u)]/(2h)
    = 1/2 + [D^2-D^(2-L)]/[L(D^2-1)].                       (4)

For odd L=2h+1, h>=0,

  kappa_(D^L)
    = [h+1 + D^(-1) sum_(u=0)^(h-1) D^(-2u)]/(2h+1)
    = 1/2 + [1/2 + D(1-D^(-(L-1)))/(D^2-1)]/L.              (5)

The empty sums in L=1 and L=2 give kappa=1 in both cases. Examples:
L=3 gives (2+D^(-1))/3; L=4 gives (3+D^(-2))/4. For either parity,
kappa_(D^L) tends to 1/2 as L tends to infinity at fixed D.

Spectrum cross-check. On S_j intersect S_(j+1)^perp, 1<=j<L,
omega has eigenvalue (1/L) sum_(k=1)^j 1/n_k with multiplicity
n_j-n_(j+1). Its top eigenvalue is (1/L) sum_(k=1)^L 1/n_k on S_L;
outside S_1 it is zero. These eigenvalues strictly increase with j,
so the maximizing filtration used above is also the ordinary spectral
Ky Fan maximizer.

Scope. This proof uses no approximation, numerical eigensolver, Bell-tail
postselection, or entanglement-formation optimizer. The rank projector can
be fully nonlocal. Consequently a conclusion about a physically admitted
normalizer still needs a separate receiving-map theorem or an upper-bound
reduction from that map to a rank-R projector. Exact attainment in the
unrestricted projector problem does not establish LOCC attainability.
