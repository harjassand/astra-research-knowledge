CYCLE7: JOINT OUTPUT NORMALIZATION OBSTRUCTIONS
Independent audit by /root/cycle7_joint_normalizer, 22 October 2026
Status: internally proved; historical novelty not asserted.

0. RECEIVING QUESTION AND SCOPE

The receiving operation is an output-only, finite-dimensional separable CPTP
channel Gamma. It may jointly process every emitted pair, use local ancillas,
communicate classically, discard local systems and depend on the known queue
state. There is no free additional entangled supply or catalyst. The final
port may be expressed in arbitrary fixed local bases. All outcome branches
are averaged. LOCC and its finite-dimensional norm closure are included because
they are separable. A channel that also accesses the counted memory clock is a
different operation and is not covered.

The principal exact result below is STATE DEPENDENT: any such Gamma whose actual
queue output has exactly maximally correlated (MC) support has Schmidt number
at most D, independently of queue length. This forces a linear loss of the
actual complete-prefix Bell score. It replaces the Cycle6 pair-by-pair
restriction with unrestricted joint processing, at the explicit cost of an
exact MC-support premise. The separate uniform-calibration result in section 5
is robust in output distance, but has a stronger calibration premise. Neither
result closes the generic formation gap or rules out approximate state-dependent
normalization with a charged score budget.

1. FULL-SUPPORT ENTANGLED-SEED LEMMA

Let A=A0 tensor A1 and B=B0 tensor B1, with dim A0=dim B0=s. Write
 |Phi_s> = s^(-1/2) sum_{x=1}^s |x x>,
 S = span{|Phi_s>} tensor H_A1 tensor H_B1.
Suppose a density operator sigma has support exactly S; equivalently, in these
finite dimensions it is strictly positive on S. Let Gamma be separable CPTP
with product Kraus operators K_l=A_l tensor B_l, to arbitrary finite local
output spaces. Suppose Gamma(sigma) is supported on a fixed MC subspace
 W = span{|e_i> tensor |f_i>: i in I},
where the e_i and f_i are orthonormal families. Then Gamma(rho) has Schmidt
number at most s for EVERY state rho supported in S. In particular
 E_F(Gamma(sigma)) <= ln s.

Proof. Let P_Wperp be the complementary projector. Positivity and
 Tr[P_Wperp sum_l K_l sigma K_l^dagger]=0
imply P_Wperp K_l sigma^(1/2)=0 separately for each l. Since sigma^(1/2)
has range S, K_l S is contained in W for every product Kraus operator.

Fix l and suppress its label. Use input bases |x,u> and |x,v>, with x=1,...,s
and u,v indexing A1,B1. In output bases extending the displayed MC families,
the coefficient of |e_i f_j> in
 (A tensor B)(|Phi_s> tensor |u v>)
is
 C_ij(u,v) = s^(-1/2) sum_x A_{i,xu} B_{j,xv}.
The support condition says C_ij(u,v)=0 for i!=j and all u,v; output indices
outside the MC family also vanish. Call i active if C_ii(u,v) is nonzero for
some u,v. For each active i choose u_i,v_i with C_ii(u_i,v_i)!=0. The matrix
 G_ij = sum_x A_{i,xu_i} B_{j,xv_j}
over active indices is diagonal and every diagonal entry is nonzero. It factors
as a (#active)-by-s matrix times an s-by-(#active) matrix. Its rank is at most s,
so #active<=s. This is valid with a bilinear product: no missing complex
conjugation is needed in a tensor-product state amplitude.

Thus K_l S is contained in the span of at most s matched output basis vectors.
Every vector in K_l S has Schmidt rank at most s. Decompose any rho supported
in S into pure vectors and apply all Kraus operators, retaining the normalized
nonzero images with their actual weights. This is a pure-state decomposition
of Gamma(rho), each member having Schmidt rank <=s and entropy <=ln s. Hence
Schmidt number <=s and E_F<=ln s. QED.

Remarks. Only positivity on the seed support and product Kraus structure enter;
no efficiency or method to discover Gamma is assumed. The MC bases and channel
may both be chosen after learning sigma. The lemma also holds for a trace-
nonincreasing separable branch, after normalization when its probability is
nonzero. Exact support is essential: the smallest seed eigenvalue may be very
small, so this proof supplies no dimension-uniform approximate version.

2. ACTUAL QUEUE APPLICATION AND SCORE LOSS

Fix D>=2, L>=1, m=ln D and nu=I_(D^2)/D^2, a faithful product state. The actual
L-slot output marginal from the admitted queue is
 omega_L = (1/L) sum_{k=1}^L Phi_D^tensor k tensor nu^tensor(L-k).
It contains the positive summand
 (1/L) Phi_D tensor nu^tensor(L-1).
All other summands have the same first Bell pair. Therefore its support is
exactly S=span{|Phi_D>} tensor H_(A,2..L) tensor H_(B,2..L).
The lemma applies with s=D. Any output-only separable Gamma with Gamma(omega_L)
exactly MC has E_F(Gamma(omega_L))<=m. This is not a uniform-channel assertion:
it applies separately to every Gamma tailored to this actual known omega_L.

Use the inherited single-chain witness, without improving or re-proving its
constant:
 O_L=m sum_{i=1}^L Phi_(1..i),
 E_F(rho)>=Tr(O_L rho)-C_D.
Here C_D is the received dimension-only constant (C_2=2-ln 2 is allowed).
Consequently
 Tr[O_L Gamma(omega_L)] <= m+C_D.
The actual unprocessed prefix probabilities are
 p_i = (L-i+1)/L + (1/L) sum_{k=1}^{i-1} D^(-2(i-k)).
They include the nonzero overlap of the product-noise suffix. In particular
 Tr(O_L omega_L) >= (L+1)m/2.
Thus every exact-MC joint output-only separable normalizer loses actual score
 Delta >= (L-1)m/2-C_D.
This is Theta(L) as L grows at fixed D. A negative small-L lower bound is merely
vacuous and is not reported as a positive loss.

A direct stronger score estimate avoids the inherited constant entirely.
Partial trace preserves the Schmidt-number upper bound D. A state of Schmidt
number <=D has overlap with an i-pair maximally entangled state at most
min{1,D/D^i}; this follows for each pure Schmidt-rank-D component by
Cauchy-Schwarz and then by convexity. Thus
 Tr[O_L Gamma(omega_L)] <= m sum_(i=1)^L D^(1-i)
                          =m(1-D^(-L))/(1-D^(-1)).
The actual score loss is therefore at least
 m[(L+1)/2-(1-D^(-L))/(1-D^(-1))].
This is a consequence of the queue-specific Schmidt-number collapse, not a
new dimension-only constant or a repair of the inherited witness.

The unchanged Cycle6 clock queue already realizes this omega_L, exact first
Bell output, complete return delta=(1-D^(-2L))/L, and ordinary flagged supply.
Thus the obstruction is at the active horizon L~1/delta, not in an irrelevant
late tail. The controller-assisted Cycle6 repair escapes because its access to
the actual current memory clock resolves k before resetting only noise slots.
An output-only operation lacks that additional input. The present proof does
not infer physical accessibility of an arbitrary hidden clock.

3. SAME-DEVICE CAUSAL COUNTERFAMILY FOR UNIFORM NORMALIZERS

For the supplementary uniform result use M=2L memory pairs and an M-level
CURRENT shared classical clock. Let
 w_k=Phi_D^tensor k tensor nu^tensor(M-k),
 tau_I=(1/L) sum_{k in I}|kk><kk|_F tensor w_k,
 I_noise={1,...,L}, I_full={L,...,2L-1}.
The SAME fixed local-unitary device receives nu each call, emits the first
memory pair, shifts the remaining pairs and appends the input. It updates each
local clock by f(k)=k-1 for k>1, f(1)=M. One outcome, no communication or reuse
discard. Both memories emit an exactly ideal first Bell pair.

After one call the w_k content becomes w_(k-1), interpreting w_0=nu^tensor M,
and the clock is f(k). Each interior clock block agrees exactly with the initial
block. The initial interval has one endpoint clock absent after the call, and
the final interval has one endpoint clock absent initially. These two blocks
are orthogonal and each has weight 1/L. Therefore the COMPLETE half-trace
return error is exactly 1/L for both families, including the noise wrap to
clock M. At L=1 the two initial memories happen to coincide; the later
asymptotic obstruction is vacuous there.

For public time t, retain the full joint output-memory state:
 Omega_t^I=(1/L) sum_{k in I}
   [Phi_D^tensor min(k,t) tensor nu^tensor(t-k)_+]_outputs
   tensor |f_t(k) f_t(k)><f_t(k) f_t(k)|_F
   tensor [Phi_D^tensor(k-t)_+ tensor nu^tensor(M-(k-t)_+)]_memory,
where f_t(k)=1+((k-t-1) mod M). This keeps all output-output and output-memory
correlations. Even after the pair contents exhaust, the current clock remains
correlated with previous outputs. At t=L the noise marginal is omega_L above,
whereas the full marginal is exactly Phi_d, d=D^L.

4. COMPLETE ORDINARY RESOURCE ACCOUNTING

All flags, M queue pairs, and their correlations belong to the target memory.
For either interval I the exact target entanglement of formation is
 E_F(tau_I)=m E_I[k].
An upper bound is the actual ordinary factory: preselect k uniformly, supply
Phi_(D^k), install its k Bell pairs, locally prepare the remaining faithful
product pairs, and create the matching classical clock. A lower bound follows
by locally reading that clock and applying average LOCC monotonicity of E_F;
the conditional resource contains exactly k Bell pairs and separable tails.
The same argument gives exact additivity for r copies.

Thus the exact genuinely preselected expected supplies per copy are
 B_noise=(L+1)m/2,
 B_full=(3L-1)m/2.
Exact deterministic supply per copy is Lm and (2L-1)m, respectively: the
positive-probability largest-inventory branch requires that Schmidt number,
while a supply of that maximal rank prepares every branch. For r copies the
exact deterministic costs are r times these values, because the all-maximal
clock branch has positive probability. No entangled catalyst is free; a
catalyst would have to be included in the initialized target and supply.

These constructions do not save supply. They are fully ordinary consumable
stock, with different genuine inventories and different expected costs. Their
role is to falsify an output-only normalization obligation, not to conflate
resource inequivalence with advantage. The uniform result may be tested on
these zero-global-initialization-error targets, already a subclass of any
claimed uniformly error-robust scheme. We make no unproved assertion about
arbitrary epsilon-close approximate factories. Local preparation, clock
control and entropy disposal do not count as free physical energy; only their
entanglement charge is zero under the stated abstract resource convention.

5. UNIFORM EXACT-CALIBRATION / APPROXIMATE-SUPPORT OBSTRUCTION

Let one same-dimensional separable CPTP output-only channel Gamma be required
to preserve the full family's complete Bell-prefix score exactly. The input
Phi_d already attains the maximum Lm, so this requires Gamma(Phi_d)=Phi_d.
The known exact Bell-preserving separable rigidity lemma implies Gamma is a
mixture of matched bilateral unitaries U tensor conjugate(U); hence it is unital.
For completeness, the elementary proof is recalled in section 6, as an existing
lemma rather than a claimed new result.

For any unital CPTP map and rank-R projector Pi,
 Tr[Pi Gamma(omega_L)] <= kappa_R(omega_L),
where kappa_R is the sum of the top R eigenvalues. One proof is that
E=Gamma^dagger(Pi) satisfies 0<=E<=I and Tr E=R, so its expectation is at most
the Ky Fan maximum.

The queue supports are nested:
 R_k=span{|Phi_D>^tensor k} tensor H_(remaining pairs),
 dim R_k=D^(2(L-k)), R_(k+1) subset R_k,
and its k-th summand is the uniform density on R_k. Thus for every integer
1<=R<=d^2,
 kappa_R(omega_L)=(1/L) sum_{k=1}^L min{1,R D^(-2(L-k))}.
Each summand has this separate upper bound. A single R-dimensional subspace
can attain all of them simultaneously: place it between the two consecutive
nested supports whose dimensions straddle R. It contains all smaller supports
and is contained in all larger ones. If R exceeds the whole support, extend
by arbitrary null directions. This attains the Ky Fan value, without claiming
that such a projector is LOCC implementable.

For R=d=D^L write kappa_L for this value. If L=2h,
 kappa_L=[h+1+sum_{j=1}^{h-1}D^(-2j)]/(2h).
If L=2h+1,
 kappa_L=[h+1+sum_{j=0}^{h-1}D^(-(2j+1))]/(2h+1).
Both equal 1/2+O_D(1/L). The finite exact formulas cover L=1,2, where kappa=1.
For ANY state rho supported on ANY rank-d subspace Pi (thus, in particular,
any MC state in any local bases), a two-outcome projector test gives
 T(Gamma(omega_L),rho) >= 1-Tr[Pi Gamma(omega_L)] >= 1-kappa_L.
Therefore one universal exact-full-Bell-calibrated joint separable normalizer
cannot even approximately place all admitted queue outputs in MC support with
error tending to zero as their complete return error tends to zero. The
obstruction approaches one half, not merely an exponentially small tolerance.
It is stronger in approximation tolerance than the state-dependent theorem but
weaker in its explicitly uniform calibration premise. There is no inference
that every state-dependent approximate normalizer is impossible.

More generally, a rank-R target support within distance epsilon requires
(1/L)sum_k min{1,R D^(-2(L-k))}>=1-epsilon. This is an exact support-size
obligation. At exponential scale R=D^(alpha L), 0<=alpha<=2, its left side is
alpha/2+O_D(1/L), so support dimension approximately D^(2(1-epsilon)L) is
necessary under this calibration. It does not price physical synthesis or
grant a larger MC output port without charging that change of model.

6. KNOWN BELL-PRESERVING RIGIDITY, RECALLED WITH ATTRIBUTION

For product Kraus A_l tensor B_l of a same-dimension separable CPTP map fixing
Phi_d, purity gives A_l B_l^T=c_l I and sum_l |c_l|^2=1. Completeness gives
 sum_l Tr(A_l^dagger A_l) Tr(B_l^dagger B_l)=d^2.
For c_l!=0, singular-value Cauchy-Schwarz gives
 Tr(A_l^dagger A_l) Tr(B_l^dagger B_l)>=d^2 |c_l|^2,
with equality only for scaled unitaries matched by B_l^T=c_l A_l^(-1).
The total trace equality forces equality termwise and makes every c_l=0
Kraus zero. Thus Gamma is the asserted convex mixture. This is a known
special case of Gheorghiu--Griffiths, Physical Review A 76, 032310 (2007),
https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.0369 . It is not claimed as Cycle7 novelty.

7. DECISION, LIMITATIONS AND OBSERVED COST

Retire exact-MC output-only normalization as a low-score-loss receiving step
for this admitted full-support queue, even when all outputs are processed
jointly and the map knows the state. Its exact support obligation collapses
the retainable entanglement to the always-present first Bell seed. Known
explicit clock access can evade it, so unrestricted joint memory access and
its operational cost remain separate questions. A score-priced APPROXIMATE,
state-dependent normalization or a private-noise-insensitive resource defect
remains open. Uniform exact-score normalization has the independent constant
support-distance obstruction in section 5.

This is an analytic proof, not a numerical extrapolation or proof of useful
implementation. The only new check run in this subtask is independent symbolic
reasoning on the nested support and diagonal-rank lemmas. No numerical search,
paid resource, hardware experiment, publication, external acceptance or generic
formation-gap closure is claimed. Full discovery and historical-priority costs
are unknown. Root's package should preserve the input derivation, inherited
witness and independent audits alongside this source.

8. PAID AUXILIARY / CATALYTIC RECEIVING OBLIGATION

The obstruction extends to a receiver explicitly supplied additional
entanglement. Take r exact iid queue outputs and an independent maximally
entangled resource Phi_M. The joint input is strictly positive on
 span{Phi_D^tensor r tensor Phi_M} tensor H_Aprivate tensor H_Bprivate.
After grouping the fixed Bell factors its seed Schmidt rank is D^r M. Hence
any output-only separable receiver producing exact MC support has
 E_F(rho_ports)<=r m+ln M.
This remains true if the receiver may CONSUME the whole auxiliary supply.
A receiver that returns an entangled catalyst is a restricted special case.
Local ancillas, controls and branch records are included in that receiver;
tracing any final auxiliary is an LOCC step, so the port bound still applies.

If M is genuinely selected before receipt, independently of the hidden queue
inventories, and costs b=E[ln M], exact support
of the averaged port output in ONE common MC subspace forces every positive
probability M branch into that same subspace by positivity. The conditional
bound and convexity give
 E_F(rho_ports)<=r m+b.
The same conclusion applies to an auxiliary exactly prepared by LOCC from
that charged preselected supply: compose its preparation with the receiver.
It is a bound on TOTAL INITIAL auxiliary supply, not net consumption, and
does not silently credit a returned catalyst or embezzler as free.

Independence here is an access premise, not a gratuitous bookkeeping detail:
conditional on each supplied M/auxiliary label the source must be the same
omega_L^tensor r (or at least retain the same full-support seed). A factory-
correlated classical label revealing K supplies the missing controller
interface, even at zero entanglement cost, and allows the known clock-assisted
repair. The theorem does not bound such a receiver merely by E[ln M]. All such
records and their correlation with the source must be specified and counted.

For the intended zero-Q receiving witness, specify the common MC subspace
to be STANDARD PRODUCT-STRING MC across tasks and sites:
 span{|x_1,...,x_r>_A tensor |x_1,...,x_r>_B}.
Measuring Alice's complementary computational strings makes Bob's
complementary string pure, so every Q_a is exactly zero. (Arbitrary MC bases
entangling different tasks do not imply this taskwise statement.) The inherited
witness now implies
 Tr(O rho_ports)<=r(m+C_D)+b.
The original exact iid queue has Tr(O omega_L^tensor r)>=r(L+1)m/2.
If the receiver is required to lose at most r*s in this actual score, then
 b/r >= (L-1)m/2-C_D-s.
Thus even collective output processing must pay an order-L initial auxiliary
formation budget to make this exact zero-Q normalization preserve the order-L
queue score. It does not manufacture a better formation/reuse resource bound:
the underlying queue is already ordinary stock with matched supply B.

All statements here use exact source preparation and exact MC support. Global
epsilon-approximate preparation or approximate MC output is not automatically
covered; its leakage and its mN epsilon witness cost require a separate proof.
Catalysis or embezzling that changes those definitions cannot be treated as a
counterexample under an uncharged resource convention.

9. EXACT RECEIVING OPERATION WITH A PRICED CLASSICAL INTERFACE

Specify a classical side-information interface C generated from the initial
uniform inventory K in {1,...,L}, with stochastic kernel p(c|k). The receiver
is given C and the undisturbed queue output conditional on C:
 omega_c=sum_k p(k|c) Phi_D^tensor k tensor nu^tensor(L-k).
This is a NEW access interface; it is not inferred from the output marginal.
For every positive-probability c define t_c=min{k:p(k|c)>0}. The conditional
state is positive definite on the seed support with t_c fixed Bell pairs and
an otherwise full-rank private tail. The seed lemma gives Schmidt number
at most D^(t_c) for any conditional output-only SEP exact-MC normalization.
Therefore every i-prefix Bell overlap is at most
 min{1,D^(t_c-i)}.
These inequalities hold even if arbitrary joint processing knows the entire
conditional state. They give the exact maximum conditional normalized score
 F_L(t_c)=t_c+sum_(j=1)^(L-t_c) D^(-j)
        =t_c+(1-D^(-(L-t_c)))/(D-1).

An explicit operation attains EVERY one of these prefix bounds simultaneously:
keep the first t_c output Bell pairs and locally reset all later pairs to |00>.
Every source branch conditional on c has at least t_c Bell pairs, so the final
port state is exactly Phi_D^tensor t_c tensor |00><00|^tensor(L-t_c), independent
of the unresolved K. It has common standard MC support; no E_F optimization,
state tomography or gap premise is needed. Thus the exact maximum actual score
for the specified interface is
 max_Gamma Tr(O_L Gamma(omega with C))=m sum_c p(c) F_L(t_c).
This is an optimal receiver given C, not a claim that C is freely obtainable.
The retained C outcome is classical and separable; all c branches are included.

For a bound on the size of that interface put d_err=E[K-t_C]. If C has at most
J distinct values, let q=min{J,L}, write L=q a+b with 0<=b<q. Then
 d_err >= d_min(L,J)
       =[(q-b)a(a-1)+b a(a+1)]/(2L)
       >=(L/J-1)/2.
To prove it, fix the distinct thresholds t_c. For each actual k, a supported
label has t_c<=k, so E[t_C|K=k] is at most the largest available threshold
not exceeding k. Assigning k to that threshold deterministically can only
increase E[t_C]. This assignment partitions {1,...,L} into consecutive bins
of lengths n_j. The mean underestimation is sum_j n_j(n_j-1)/(2L). Convexity
of n(n-1) minimizes this expression with q balanced bins, proving the formula.
No stochastic labeling improves it. Balanced bins therefore give an explicit
interface and receiver attaining this minimum mean underestimation.

The exact original normalized queue score is E K+u, with
 0<=u=(1/L)sum_k sum_(j=1)^(L-k)D^(-2j)<=1/(D^2-1).
The exact receiver score is E t_C+v, with 0<=v<=1/(D-1). Its loss obeys
 m[d_err-1/(D-1)] <= Delta <= m[d_err+1/(D^2-1)].
The upper bound is attained as an inequality by the explicit balanced-bin
receiver above, and every J-label exact-MC receiver obeys the lower bound.
Consequently a fixed dimension-only allowed loss needs J=Omega(L), and a
clock-sized interface J=Theta(L) suffices. For J=1 this reproduces a linear
loss; J=L supplies the full clock, recovers every branch's actual Bell stock,
and can even increase the original score by replacing private noise with |00>.
The exact optimal J-label thresholds for the F_L objective need not be balanced;
we claim balanced bins are exact for mean underestimation and optimal for actual
score up to the stated dimension-only constants.

There is also a Shannon-information access charge. E=K-t_C is a nonnegative
integer and K is determined by (C,E), so
 I(K:C)=ln L-H(K|C)=ln L-H(E|C)>=ln L-g(d_err),
 g(d)=(d+1)ln(d+1)-d ln d, g(0)=0.
The last bound is the maximum entropy of a nonnegative integer of mean d,
proved by nonnegative relative entropy against the geometric distribution of
that mean. Also H(C)>=I(K:C). Bounded score loss implies bounded d_err by the
preceding inequality, hence requires ln L-O_D(1) nats of actual inventory
information at this interface. This is information available to the receiver,
not a new entanglement cost or an assumption about laboratory observability.

The specified admitted queue can implement the interface at public horizon L
because its known current finite clock is a reversible function of original K.
Each party locally computes the bin label from that clock into a fresh local
register, performs the indicated controlled resets, and uncomputes the label
using the unchanged clock. This uses no communication or entangled auxiliary.
A finite table of L thresholds specifies the control; at most L-1 D-level pairs
are reset, with their previous contents discarded into local environments.
The complete retained memory marginal is unchanged: the clock is already
classical and the controlled output maps are trace preserving on each block.
Fresh local label workspaces can return to their pure initial states. If instead
labels or discarded reset garbage are retained, they enlarge the complete
memory and need a separate return argument. Physical reset/control/entropy
costs are not asserted zero; only additional entanglement supply is zero.
An arbitrary black-box memory is not granted this readout connection.

This operation uses exact source states. It does not extend exact-MC support
to arbitrary globally epsilon-close initializers by continuity. The prior
mN epsilon score charge and actual support/uncertainty conditions remain in
force for such preparations. No stock advantage is produced: the initialized
queue still costs its ordinary complete flagged stock. The new receiving
consequence is an exact optimal state-dependent normalizer conditional on a
specified, information-priced interface, alongside a proof that output-only
normalization cannot replace that access at small score loss.
