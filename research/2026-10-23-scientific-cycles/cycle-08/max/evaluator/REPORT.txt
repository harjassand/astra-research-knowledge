EXECUTIVE DECISION

Preserve the autonomous scientific architecture. The new returns strengthen the
case for continuity: the researchers have generated substantive new ideas and
used prior discoveries to prove stronger results. They weaken a blanket diagnosis
that Astra is failing because it cannot invent useful representations or methods.

The central unresolved issue is more precise. We have evidence that Astra's
scientific knowledge and the reach of particular programmes are increasing. We
do not yet have evidence that its general mechanism for generating the next
important discovery improves with experience, or that this improvement compounds.
Absence of that evidence does not prove an absence of learning.

This round executed a constructive transfer experiment rather than another
catalogue of impossibility results. A completed Pro1 proof was extended to a
different, anisotropic quantum-prefix problem. The new all-length theorem has
an explicit optimizer, a changing support boundary and a corrected operator
certificate. A fresh receiver using a compact extracted operation solved it;
an equally informed ordinary receiver also solved it. A separate reviewer
accepted both, and independent native word calculations corroborated the result.

Retain the new scientific result. Do not install the compact operation as a
superior research strategy. No comparative amplification was demonstrated.

The most consequential change is to the evaluator's own research emphasis:
stop treating every round as a reason to invent another universal reasoning
prescription. Investigate how Astra can create scientifically consequential
questions and discriminating observations, including the premises that make
an answer usable. The next serious amplification experiment must test that
generative step or a genuine change in feasible science, not another neighboring
exercise that strong source-informed reasoning already solves.

2. WHAT THE NEW SCIENCE CHANGES

Pro1: a sharp problem is resolved and its operational bridge is used

The preceding full-prefix question was whether a coefficient grew like k^(3/2)
or k^2. It is now resolved exactly:

  v_k = k(k-1)/8 - C_k/8,
  C_k = sum_{n=0}^{k-2}(k-1-n) Pr(S_n=0),
  v_k = k^2/8 - k^(3/2)/(6 sqrt(2 pi)) - k/8 + O(sqrt(k)).

The minimization includes all coherent and entangled prefix densities. A single
correlated symmetric-prefix density attains every local bound, and the explicit
input observable satisfies the full scalar operator certificate. This closes an
actual mathematical frontier, not just a finite-size extrapolation.

The same-bath coding bridge is also used: for each fixed 0<beta<=2^-8, a positive
input at k=floor(beta^(-1/2)/8) gives an initializer-independent code sequence
with achievable rate at least 3/2+(ln 2/16384) beta^(7/2), and pointwise vanishing
full-diamond error for every fixed normal initializer. This is an achievable
asymptotic lower bound, not the exact capacity or a useful finite-code claim.
At beta=1/512 the displayed coarse floor does not beat the retained pair floor;
the source correctly preserves that stronger pointwise comparator.

The conceptual move matters. A failed stronger demand that certain words be
bath independent had discarded useful sectors. The new proof retains the actual
input/bath contraction and obtains a uniform contribution. Pro1's next owned
frontier is the nonlinear temporal window for the now explicit operator. Asking
it to resolve sharp v_k growth again would erase the new progress.

Pro2: an explicit infinite-family construction repairs a real prior failure

The new theorem covers both series parities, arbitrary path leaves of length
at least two, and the stated stubbed two-/three-child parallel grammar at all
finite depths. Four realizable families, 46 transition cases and 200 explicit
child witnesses provide a proper star-four coloring. The original full action
Y=DX+E has a least-squares inverse satisfying

  ||Dhat-D||op <= sqrt(3/2)||E||F,
  ||Dhat-D||F  <= sqrt(2)||E||F,

for arbitrary real symmetric supported matrices with free diagonals and the
unchanged total query-row norm. The sharper operator bound actually reuses the
recovered Cycle2 proof. This is an identifiable receiving use of older capital.

The construction was applied to the exact 1,788-vertex support on which the
previous three-coordinate rule failed, with the additional query charged.
This does not prove that every three-query design fails on that graph, or that
the method wins an application runtime comparison. Four-query recovery on the
full degree-four, treewidth-two arbitrary-real class remains open.

The successful change was a stronger, locally checkable star invariant, not
simply weakening a failed requirement. The new grammar still excludes an
unbounded family of genuine chordless atoms; the owner distinguishes that
representation limitation from recovery hardness. Its next question concerns
unequal terminal degree loads, including the (2,1) interface, or another
owner-selected route to the original recovery problem.

The decisive argument uses reverse proposal probabilities in the weighted

Within the specified two-power screen

Ultra: a desirable normal form is priced as a real operation

Ultra proves a general finite-dimensional exact-support lemma. A state faithful
on an entangled seed Phi_K tensored with a full product nuisance space cannot be
mapped by a separable operation into finite-dimensional exact maximally correlated
support with normalized-output Schmidt number greater than K. Positivity first forces every product Kraus map to respect the
support; a diagonal-rank factorization then supplies the restriction.

For the actual counted-clock queue this gives the exact optimal joint output-
only normalizer. Keeping the guaranteed Bell prefix and resetting the remainder
loses order 1/delta score. Preserving score requires replacement-scale independent
entanglement or a specified inventory record. The access-dependent optimum,
finite label optimization and matched ordinary replacement are explicit.

This generalizes a prior pairwise obstruction to joint, state-dependent
processing and gives a meaningful resource/access law. It does not establish
a stock-beating reservoir. Exact support is discontinuous: the included counted
TYPE-doping construction prevents silently extending the result to approximate
state-dependent normalization. That boundary and the broader formation question
remain open. The evaluator's earlier private-noise fixture was not a premise
of Ultra's new theorem, so its presence in the packet is not counted as transfer.

3. WHAT THESE HISTORIES SAY ABOUT SCIENTIFIC INTELLIGENCE

The strongest change to the previous diagnosis is that a general creativity
deficit is not established. Four mathematical owners have produced genuinely
different constructions. Their methods are not instances of one reliable
instruction: Pro1 weakens an excessive intermediate demand, Pro2 strengthens an
invariant, Pro3 changes the reference weights, and Ultra prices access needed
to realize a desired representation. Dots changes the empirical comparison.

The common issue is the relationship between an intermediate object and the
scientific conclusion. A failed intermediate claim can be stronger than needed;
a successful component response can be insufficient for the desired operation.
Both mistakes appeared in earlier physical work. Their remedies run in opposite
directions, so a fixed "always relax" or "always strengthen" prompt is unsupported.

There are at least three live explanations for current cumulative progress:
(1) persistent domain expertise and increasingly informative constraints;
(2) reusable results and constructions reducing later obligations;
(3) improved general invention strategies acquired from experience.
The sources strongly support the first two. They do not distinguish the third
from a capable fixed model doing better because it now knows more mathematics.
These are not mutually exclusive explanations.

It would also be wrong to say Astra has not improved at all. It can now state,
prove and use results unavailable at earlier checkpoints. Knowledge is part of
system capability. The stronger claim at issue is an improvement in the process
that generates future discoveries, beyond the ordinary benefit of possessing
the facts needed for a particular proof. Repeated generations of that improvement
would be required to support recursive or exponential amplification.

The records distinguish useful subagent roles. Pro1 used independent word and
dual obligations; Pro2 used exact finite closure and an independent interface
checker; Pro3 received a distinct lower construction and extended it. These are
examples of complementary work. They do not establish an optimal agent count.
In Pro3, much numerical checking followed the theorem and should not be credited
as generating it. In Pro2, constructive finite search helped produce the actual
closure certificate. The appropriate computation depends on the scientific need.

4. EXECUTED CONSTRUCTIVE META-EXPERIMENT

Question and selection

Could a completed native proof be turned into an operation that produces a
correct new result under a change that invalidates some of its original premises?
The uniform d-color substitution was rejected during design as too easy. The
selected task uses a positive anisotropic color density P, including examples
where the optimum loses support. It is distinct from Pro1's owned nonlinear
temporal-window question. No owner was diverted to this experiment.

Both fresh receivers received the complete original Pro1 proof, the same new
contract, the same mathematical tools, and the same three diagnostic inputs.
The ordinary control was free to use convex optimization, spectral methods,
exact arithmetic or any stronger analytic method. It was not forced into an
exponential matrix search. The other receiver additionally received a compact
sector operation extracted and revised by the evaluator and design reviewer.
The protocol and operation were frozen before receiving execution.

This was not a blind historical test: the evaluator and designer had already
considered candidate answers when selecting the task. It was one receiver per
condition, not a randomized population estimate. Token budgets and internal
inference costs were unavailable. The treatment's extra idea-development and
review cost is explicitly part of acquisition, not a free advantage.

The new scientific result

Let sigma_P=(|0><0|/2 direct-sum P/2)^tensor k and retain the actual bulk
stack-word coefficient H_zeta, with minimization over every prefix density.
Write s_P=1-Tr(P^2), and choose t>0 uniquely from

  sum_i (1-t/p_i)_+ = 1.

Set z_i=(1-t/p_i)_+, f_P=sum_i p_i(1-z_i)^2. Both independent receivers derive

  v_k(P) = s_P k(k-1)/4 + (f_P-s_P) C_k/2,
  zeta_* = sum_i z_i (|i><i|)^tensor k.

The proof includes complex coherent and entangled competitors. Input/input
swaps, input/bath swaps and exported-color sectors have different weighted
costs. A single correlated repeated-color mixture attains all local minima
simultaneously, and fair-walk counting supplies the exact all-k expression.

For P=diag(4/5,3/20,1/20), the optimal one-color marginal is
diag(16/19,3/19,0), although P itself is strictly positive. The old universal
commutation and scalar-dual statements do not survive automatically. The new
dual is v_k I plus an explicit nonnegative sum of one-site slack operators
diag((t-p_i)_+), with equality on the optimizer's support. At a threshold tie
p_i=t, the optimizer can be rank deficient while the dual remains scalar.

These are complete receiving statements, not numerical extrapolations. The
physical initializer is unchanged; the optimizing prefix is analytic. No new
communication-rate theorem follows, because the logarithm of the changed
reference state contains additional color observables. The full proofs are in
meta/control/REPORT.md and meta/operation/REPORT.md.

Checks and outcome

The independent adjudicator accepted both proofs at the stated scope. Root's
separate exact checker constructs the native toggle commutator directly and
contracts it; it does not import either receiver implementation or merely test
the reduced formula. Ten exact small cases passed, including coherent and
entangled competitors and non-scalar dual slack. The control independently
checked native matrices through k=4; the operation receiver also checked complex
coherences numerically. These finite checks corroborate, not prove, the all-k law.

Both receivers solved the complete task. Therefore the new operation has a
successful receiving derivation, but no comparative success advantage. Recorded
receiver elapsed times were approximately 430 and 450 seconds; their numerical
checks differed in scope, and inference costs were not measured. These numbers
do not establish a speed advantage in either direction. Root's exact script
took about 0.216 seconds in its measured body, which is not discovery cost.

What this experiment changes

It positively establishes that the native operation can support a new all-length
theorem while exposing and repairing changed premises. It also shows that a
strong fresh source-informed solver could perform the same generalization
without the added operation. The result does not justify preferential deployment
of the compact prompt or a general learning mechanism. It also does not prove
such methods can never help harder or more distant problems.

The difficult mathematical obligation was met, but the trial was not
discriminating about comparative strategy. Repeating adjacent variations would
probably measure existing mathematical competence again. This is a stopping
condition for this experiment family, not closure of the meta-research mission.

5. SERIOUS ALTERNATIVES AND THE NEXT AMPLIFICATION HYPOTHESIS

Executable abstraction learning

DreamCoder demonstrates joint acquisition of symbolic libraries and search
guidance within specified domains [1]. DreamProver reports reuse of evolved
lemmas on held-out theorem sets [2]. These are relevant constructions, but do
not prove that open-ended scientific research automatically benefits from a
growing archive. A controlled study of three in-context library systems found
that claimed gains did not consistently survive compute-matched baselines and
found no effective lemma reuse in its detailed LEGO-Prover case [3].

Astra's exact source-based comparison reinforces the need for that distinction.
A theorem library can be useful without every extra abstraction improving
invention. We should preserve usable operations when there is an actual receiver,
not mandate a library-learning phase or build a new platform before the need.

Joint synthesis of constructions and proofs

Inductive Deductive Synthesis jointly develops executable systems and formal
proofs, while explicitly acknowledging the specification bottleneck [4]. For
Astra, this suggests letting a candidate construction and its sufficient
invariant change together. Pro2 already exhibits a native form of constructive
computational feedback. The limitation is substantial: a correct proof of a
misstated scientific question is still the wrong result. Formalization cannot
supply missing physical premises, novelty or importance.

Evolution of search programmes

AlphaEvolve's primary report gives real examples of evolving algorithms under
automated evaluation, including mathematical constructions and infrastructure
improvements [5]. Its operation relies on a valid task specification and repeated
evaluations. It explicitly excludes tasks needing manual experimentation from
that evaluation loop. Astra has not identified a new consequential evaluator
that would make installing a central evolutionary controller the best investment.
No model training, paid API, purchased compute or new platform was initiated.

Creation of discriminating scientific observations

This is the higher-ceiling unresolved opportunity. Instead of only learning how
to solve a supplied problem, can Astra invent a new observable, intervention or
formulation that makes an important inference possible? In mathematics this
could be a new invariant with a proved distinguishing consequence. In empirical
science it must connect to accessible measurements or a real control interface.
It is a scientific operation, not a checklist or a score for interesting ideas.

The native motivation is specific. Dots' earlier same-enthalpy mode examples
showed that an equilibrium temperature did not identify the usable thermal state.
Ultra shows that knowing a distribution is different from possessing the
inventory record required to implement a normalizer. Pro1 distinguishes identical
one-color marginals from the correlations needed for cancellation. In each case,
choosing what can be observed or controlled changes what can be inferred or done.

The hypothesis is that experience can teach the system to invent those
distinguishing operations, rather than merely improve inference after the
question and data are supplied. Counterexample-guided learning has primary
evidence in controlled symbolic tasks, with an explicit equivalence oracle [6].
Real scientific feedback rarely supplies such an oracle; that is a limitation
to investigate, not conceal.

A credible next test needs two scientifically meaningful explanations that
survive the currently available evidence, an independently selected receiving
question, an attainable distinguishing observable, and held-out evidence or a
complete proof that determines which explanation survives. The ordinary control
gets the same source information, conventional experimental design, domain
invariants and full acquisition costs. A successful first use is transfer. A
later operation actually improved by that use is the next necessary generation.

No such empirical admission was completed in this round. None of the supplied
physical files contains the missing complete-outlet measurements. The evaluator
will not manufacture them or reassign Dots to a benchmark. Nor is a synthetic
two-hypothesis puzzle a substitute for a consequential scientific obligation.
The positive transfer result above is the actual executed work; this broader
mechanism remains a research hypothesis.

Continue the three autonomous Pro programmes. The new all-length and structural
results justify continued attention to their live questions. They do not
establish civilization-level significance or historical priority, but there is
no evidence that replacing them with centrally chosen grand-challenge names
would increase the probability of such significance.

that every useful result become a generic tool, or make another variation of
the evaluator's new theorem its next compulsory task.

Let Max integrate the actual current source and corrections through the existing
authorized private workflow. Distinguish source availability, integration,
remote deployment and actual scientific use. Do not equate these or create a
dashboard to track them. Public/private authorization is not changed here.

Let Dots continue substantive independent science. A complete captured-product
comparison or an owner-justified alternative is more useful than repeating the
conditional conditioning arithmetic. Its persistent mission remains active.

Use fresh independent work for genuinely different obligations or a critical
transfer, not as a universal replacement for a warm owner. Use computation when
it can construct an object, distinguish models or test a load-bearing claim.
Do not rerun settled suites to make an allocation look busy. These are narrow
evidence-informed operating choices, not a proven optimal architecture.

For the evaluator, retire another adjacent source-summary-versus-operation
prompt comparison as the default experiment. Continue the scientific question
of intelligence amplification with a more consequential generative obligation.
The six branch continuations do not force a shared method or terminate missions
at the next checkpoint. A new local result supersedes a seed when supported;
owners must not roll back to a supplied archive merely because it is packaged.

7. WHAT WOULD JUSTIFY A STRONGER CLAIM OR A CHANGE OF STRATEGY

Evidence for an amplification mechanism would include a previously infeasible
independent investigation becoming feasible because of a learned operation;
improvement surviving a source-informed mature-method control and acquisition
cost; and the acquired capability enabling a further improved operation on
another consequential task. Replication across such generations is needed
before discussing a growth law. There is no exponential-rate estimate here.

Conversely, if strong ordinary reasoning continues to match an added mechanism,
retain any useful science but stop paying for the mechanism as a general
upgrade. If novelty or scientific consequence fails under a strong primary
comparison, revise that programme's claims without erasing its verified results.
If a physical inference remains unidentified by all accessible observations,
additional mathematical polish is not evidence that the missing observation
has been acquired.

The current decision is continuity with a corrected diagnosis and a narrower
claim about the new experiment. Astra is accumulating more powerful mathematics
and learning specific scientific facts. The mechanism for reliably inventing
increasingly consequential questions, methods and observations remains open.
