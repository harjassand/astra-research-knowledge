4. DEEPER DIAGNOSIS AND A POSITIVE META-RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS

The evidence challenges the evaluator's representation-centered interpretation
in a useful way. Ultra's desired MC representation is valuable because it would
remove a troublesome witness penalty. But realizing it is an OPERATION with an
information/supply price, not merely a better description. Dots' missing cooled
state is likewise an interface that cannot be inferred by making an elegant
conditional downstream model. A more expressive mathematical vocabulary alone
would not remove either obstruction. Max proves that excellent source access
is necessary and real, while providing no evidence of changed inventive power.

A stronger candidate mechanism is joint invention of (i) a representation,
(ii) the admissible observation/control interface realizing it, and (iii) a
receiving consequence that justifies its price. Call this a hypothesis, not a
newly established universal method. Its advantage could be that the system
learns to search for transformations of what can be observed or controlled,
rather than spending most reasoning effort optimizing an unavailable object.
The current evidence includes constructive optima and measurable thresholds,
not just exclusions. However, the best ordinary experimental-design and
resource-theoretic methods already do parts of this. A generic checklist would
not constitute an advance.

A decision-changing experiment must test invention of a useful interface on an
unresolved but bounded task whose target is external to the proposed method.
Both conditions should receive identical complete raw evidence, existing
scientific capital, conventional algorithms and equal total reasoning/compute
budgets. The treatment gets an acquired procedure for converting a failed
mathematical simplification into priced candidate probes/controls and a receiving
construction; the control gets unrestricted ordinary scientific reasoning and
standard experiment design, not a weaker checklist. Do not withhold the actual
source model from the control. Score a correct new capability or decisive
observable discrimination, including acquisition/integration cost, not number
of proposed interfaces, errors found or formal statements.

For this collection, Ultra's approximate-normalization and Dots' complete
conditioning interfaces are live OWNER frontiers, so root must not seize them
as benchmark assignments. The new exact-Ultra result can be a training case,
with its answer fully disclosed; it cannot be claimed an unseen invention test.
A receiving target should be independently selected outside active ownership
and source-complete before running the trial. Success requires an explicit
operation beyond what equally informed conventional design obtains, not a
renamed dynamic programme or another impossibility proof. If no scientifically
consequential receiving contract can be admitted, do not manufacture a toy
