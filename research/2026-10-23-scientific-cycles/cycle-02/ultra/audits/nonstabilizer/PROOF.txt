GLOBAL NONSTABILIZER EIGENBASES WITH COHERENCE REMOVED BY EVERY FIRST ERASURE
Date: 2026-10-22 Australia/Brisbane
Scope: exact restricted critical tensor witness, arbitrary local D>=2.

Setup. There are r tasks, each N sites of dimension D. In the computational
basis write physical strings x=(x_(a,i)). P_(a,i) is the actual joint first-i
Bell event for task a. For a positive coefficient matrix X with Tr X^2=1,
its probability is ||E_(a,i) X||_HS^2, where E replaces the erased sites by
normalized identity times partial trace. The target defect is

 F(X)=(ln D) sum_(a,i) ||E_(a,i)X||_HS^2-S(X^2).

We use the prior scalar/classical tensor lemma, with its proved constant
C_D independent of N,r: for any nonnegative vector c with sum c_x^2=1,

 (ln D) sum_(a,i) ||E_(a,i) diag(c)||_HS^2 <= H(c^2)+r C_D.       (1)

Here C_D may be min{2D-ln D,ln D/(1-D^(-1/2))^2}, and D=2 permits
C_2=2-ln2. Equation (1) is the exact inherited classical entropy lemma;
this note supplies the new eigenbasis passage, not a new constant.

Theorem. Partition all computational strings into disjoint sets G_b with
the following property: for every task a, the map x -> x_(a,1) is injective
on each G_b. Let U be any unitary that preserves each span{|x>:x in G_b}.
For every diagonal nonnegative c with sum c_x^2=1, set

 X=U diag(c) U^*.

Then F(X)<=r C_D. All within-block phases, all rotation angles, the number
of blocks, and their global physical support are unrestricted. No product
eigenvectors, stabilizer structure, prime D, or finite circuit depth is used.

Proof. If x!=y belong to the same G_b, their first coordinate differs for
EVERY task. Every allowed task-prefix erasure therefore contains a site
where they differ, and Tr_(a,prefix i)|x><y|=0. Since U preserves these
blocks, all off-diagonal entries of X are of this form. Thus, simultaneously
for every task and prefix,

 E_(a,i)X=E_(a,i)diag(Bc),       B_(xy)=|U_(xy)|^2.             (2)

The matrix B is doubly stochastic. Birkhoff's finite-dimensional theorem
writes B=sum_pi w_pi Pi with w_pi>=0, sum w_pi=1, and permutations Pi.
For completeness this decomposition needs no asymptotic limit: a doubly
stochastic matrix's positive support satisfies the bipartite Hall condition,
because a set of rows has total mass its cardinality and its neighboring
columns each have mass at most one. Choose a supported permutation, subtract
the least positive entry along it, renormalize if needed, and iterate. Each
nonfinal subtraction removes a positive entry; the procedure terminates.

By (2), convexity of the squared Hilbert-Schmidt norm, and (1),

 (ln D) sum_(a,i)||E_(a,i)X||_HS^2
 <=sum_pi w_pi (ln D)sum_(a,i)||E_(a,i)diag(Pi c)||_HS^2
 <=sum_pi w_pi [H((Pi c)^2)+r C_D]
 =H(c^2)+r C_D=S(X^2)+r C_D.

The same decomposition works for the TOTAL score, so there is no illicit
independent spectral rearrangement for different tasks. QED.

Normalization audit. Bc usually has norm strictly below one. The proof does
NOT apply (1) to Bc or identify H((Bc)^2) with S(X^2). It applies (1) separately
to each unit-norm permutation Pi c after the squared-norm Jensen step. Every
such vector has exactly the original entropy H(c^2). No norm or entropy of
Bc is inserted anywhere in the argument.

Why this is not the stabilizer theorem. For qubits take pairs {x,bar x},
where bar x is the bitwise complement on ALL rN sites, and choose independent
arbitrary 2-by-2 unitaries on each pair. Typical basis vectors are

 cos(theta_x)|x>+exp(i phi_x)sin(theta_x)|bar x>.

If both tasks contain sites and 0<theta_x<pi/2, these vectors are genuinely
entangled across tasks. For generic angles (for example pi/7) they are not
stabilizer states. The rotations can have support on every site and can vary
independently between exponentially many pairs. Thus a bound based on small
gate support or fixed circuit depth is not the premise of this theorem.

The compressed kernels need not be projections or commute. In computational
label coordinates Q_(a,i) is the ordinary averaging projection on the erased
classical coordinates. Equation (2) gives exactly

 K_(a,i)=B^T Q_(a,i) B.                                      (3)

For nontrivial angles B is generally not orthogonal; K^2=K need not hold.
With pair-dependent angles, compressed kernels can also fail to commute.
The finite replay constructs actual U,X, traces the density-matrix entries,
and independently checks (2),(3), including an explicit noncommuting example.
The Birkhoff domination argument bypasses the projection obstruction.

Exact noncommuting example. Take r=2,N=2,D=2, with computational integer
labels 0,...,15, first-task sites 0,1 and second-task sites 2,3 (most
significant bit first). In each pair {x,15-x}, 0<=x<=7, put

 t_x=(x+1)/(x+3), a_x=(1-t_x^2)/(1+t_x^2), b_x=2t_x/(1+t_x^2),
 U_pair=[[a_x,-b_x],[b_x,a_x]].

All entries are rational, a_x^2+b_x^2=1, and a_x,b_x are strictly positive
and unequal. Let B=|U|^2. Let Q_0 average the first physical bit, and Q_2
average the third physical bit. K_0=B^T Q_0 B, K_2=B^T Q_2 B. Exact rational
arithmetic gives

 [K_0,K_2]_(0,2)=1705728024/377149515625 != 0.                  (4)

This is independently computed from the explicit finite formula

 (K_s)_(j,k)=1/2 sum_x B_(x,j)[B_(x,k)+B_(x XOR 2^(3-s),k)],

and the commutator's usual 16-term matrix product. The supplied fractions
code checks (4) without floating tolerances; direct projector partial traces
give the same kernels. Thus this class includes genuine noncommuting
compressed erasure kernels, not merely nonprojective commuting examples.

For an actual positive matrix choose lambda_j=(j+1)/136 and
X=U diag(sqrt(lambda)) U^T. This spectrum is strictly positive and distinct.
Every eigenvector has support on a complementary pair and unequal moduli.
A qubit stabilizer vector with support of size two must have equal moduli:
if its full stabilizer were diagonal, its common eigenspace would be a single
computational vector; otherwise a nontrivial X-part Pauli translates and
preserves its two-point support, hence swaps the two points with unit-modulus
coefficients, forcing equal moduli. Our vectors therefore are not stabilizer
states. Nondegeneracy rules out another stabilizer eigenbasis for this X.
The actual entanglement between tasks is also immediate from its two-term
Schmidt expansion with nonzero coefficients a_x,b_x.

Limits. The injectivity premise is sufficient, not claimed necessary. It
forces |G_b|<=D. Arbitrary positive matrices do not admit this partition or
this common erasure identity. No unrestricted critical tensor witness or
general O(1) iid preparation refinement follows. This is a mathematically
defined broad global basis class, not a proposed physical implementation.

Mixed-state support passage. Fix any U in this class. The span of vectors
with coefficient matrix U diag(c) U^* is a maximally correlated support in
a possibly entangled global eigenbasis (use the conjugate basis at the other
party). For complex c, (2) still holds. Each Q_(a,i) and B is entrywise
nonnegative, so |Q_(a,i) Bc|<=Q_(a,i) B|c| componentwise. Its actual score
is therefore no larger than that of U diag(|c|) U^*, with the same entropy
H(|c|^2). Every ensemble of a density matrix supported in this span stays
in the span. Convex-roof averaging gives the same E_F witness on this support.
No generic E_F additivity is inferred.

Operational resource passage is only the previously accepted one: applying
this supported-state tensor witness to iid preparation requires the relevant
multi-copy coefficient eigenbasis to satisfy the class premise. It is not
enough for only a single-copy spectral decomposition to be in the class.

Failure and next operation. Numerical dense non-Clifford circuits below
are not covered by the erasure identity, and their bounded tests cannot
replace a theorem. A useful next extension is a quantitative approximation
to (2), charged via its TOTAL Hilbert-Schmidt score error rather than a
count of nonlocal gates. An extensive error bound would not answer the
unrestricted critical problem.
