The exact measurement statement below holds for every stock distribution.
It also yields a complete explicit composite-separable hypothesis test with a

1. PHYSICAL FAMILY, COMPLETE STATE, AND FIXED OPERATION

Fix D>=2, N>=1 and a probability distribution w_0,...,w_N. Define
 Phi=|Phi_D><Phi_D|, |Phi_D>=D^(-1/2)sum_x |xx>,
 chi=D^(-1)sum_x |xx><xx|,
 tau_w=sum_{h=0}^N w_h Phi^(tensor h) tensor chi^(tensor(N-h)).        (1)
Every branch is averaged. The stock label h is genuinely erased before use;
there is no retained copy of its initial value. The fixed normal finite LOCC
device emits the first pair, shifts, and appends chi using ordinary shared
classical randomness. No new entanglement enters during use.

After any T calls, the combined T output pairs plus the N memory pairs have,
up to a known local reordering, state tau_w tensor chi^(tensor T). This is an
exact equality of density operators, including every exhaustion branch and
every output-memory correlation. Retained copies of the randomness used to
prepare the appended chi may be included in its separable tail. They do not
encode the erased stock label. No inaccessible-memory return measurement is
asserted experimentally available.

For w_h=1/N on h=1,...,N and w_0=0, denote tau_w by tau_N. The first output
is exactly Phi and decouples. Its full-memory half-trace return error is
 delta_N=(1-D^(-N))/N.                                        (2)
This is the original explicit physical reservoir, not a scalar relaxation.
For a J-call block, D=d^J and the same grouping is legitimate.

2. THE STOPPING EXPERIMENT AND A SUFFICIENT STATISTIC

On N output pairs put P_i=Phi^(tensor i) tensor I, P_0=I,
 F_l=P_l-P_(l+1) for 0<=l<N, F_N=P_N.
The mathematical Bell stopping measurement {F_l} is used only as a converse
observation, not claimed to be LOCC. Its record L is the number of initial
accepted Bell factors. It includes L=0 and L=N, so no branch is discarded.

Let a=1/D. The separable comparator chi^(tensor N) has law
 g_l=(1-a)a^l for 0<=l<N, and g_N=a^N.                         (3)
For (1), the exact law p_l is
 p_l=(1-a) sum_(h=0)^l w_h a^(l-h), 0<=l<N,
 p_N=sum_(h=0)^N w_h a^(N-h).
In particular
 R_l:=p_l/g_l=sum_(h=0)^l w_h D^h, 0<=l<=N.                  (4)
The likelihood ratio R_l is nonnegative and nondecreasing. Whenever p_l>0,
R_l>0. All finite expectations of ln R below are restricted to this support.

Choose the Bell-phase basis |b_z>=D^(-1/2)sum_x exp(2pi izx/D)|xx>.
In its N-fold product basis tau_w and chi^N commute. Conditional on stock h,
the first h phase symbols vanish and the rest are uniform. The ratio of a
phase-string probability to the uniform comparator probability depends ONLY
on its initial zero-run length l, and is precisely R_l. Thus the stopping
record is a sufficient statistic for this pair of quantum states:
 D(tau_w || chi^N)=sum_l p_l ln R_l=:R(w).                    (5)
No assertion of sufficiency for other states or other resource measures is
made.

3. EXACT OPTIMALITY AGAINST ALL SEPARABLE COMPARATORS

For any separable state sigma across all Alice systems versus all Bob systems,
the stopping law q obeys
 Pr_q(L>=i)<=D^(-i).                                       (6)
Indeed P_i is a rank-D^i Bell projector on the corresponding Alice:Bob factors,
times identity. Its overlap with a product state is at most D^(-i); convex
averaging gives (6). Since R_l=w_0+sum_(i=1)^l w_i D^i,
 E_q R_L<=w_0+sum_(i=1)^N w_i D^i D^(-i)=1.                 (7)
The tangent inequality -ln x>=1-x gives, when q is positive on supp p,
 D(p||q)-D(p||g)
 =sum_(p_l>0) p_l ln(g_l/q_l)
 >=sum_(p_l>0) p_l(1-q_l/g_l)
 =1-E_q R_L>=0.                                           (8)
If q has a zero with p_l>0, the left side is infinite and the result remains
valid. Terms with p_l=0 have R_l=0, so the displayed equality is unaffected.
Thus the actual separable comparator (3) is optimal for this measurement.

The full-state relative entropy of entanglement also equals R(w). Relative-
entropy data processing and (8) give D(tau_w||sigma)>=R(w) for every separable
sigma. The actual comparator chi^N attains equality by (5). Hence
 E_R(tau_w)=R(w)=inf_(sigma separable) D(M_stop(tau_w)||M_stop(sigma)). (9)
This is consistent with the established general MC identity
E_R(tau_w)=N ln D-S(tau_w). The elementary monotone-likelihood proof above
already suffices here, so that known identity is not an additional premise.
The result is not a historical novelty claim.

4. NO COLLECTIVE STOPPING-RECORD RESCUE

For t task copies, an arbitrary GLOBALLY separable state, possibly correlated
across the t tasks, has a joint stopping law q_t satisfying
 Pr_q(L_b>=i_b for all b with i_b>0)<=D^(-sum_b i_b).          (10)
The intersection is a Bell projector with total Schmidt rank D^(sum i_b),
times identity. Expanding the product of the nonnegative tail-indicator sums
in (4) and using (10) proves
 E_(q_t) product_(b=1)^t R_(L_b)<=1.                         (11)
Repeating the tangent proof (8), now against g^(tensor t), yields
 inf_(sigma_t globally separable)
  D(p^(tensor t)||M_stop^(tensor t)(sigma_t))=t R(w).         (12)
The product comparator chi^(tensor Nt) attains equality. Therefore optimizing
the whole correlated stopping distribution, retaining all its cells, changing
the test statistic, or applying arbitrary classical processing to all t
records cannot improve the per-copy relative-entropy rate above R(w).

The same comparator is also optimal for EVERY classical Renyi divergence
D_alpha of the complete stopping records, for 0<alpha<infinity (with the
continuous alpha=1 limit). Indeed R_l^alpha is nondecreasing. The same tail
expansion gives E_(q_t) product_b R_(L_b)^alpha no larger than its expectation
under g^t. For Z_alpha(q)=sum_x (p^t_x)^alpha q_x^(1-alpha), its tangent at
g^t has change (1-alpha)[E_q(R_product^alpha)-E_(g^t)(R_product^alpha)].
For alpha>1, Z_alpha is convex and this change is nonnegative. For 0<alpha<1,
Z_alpha is concave and the change is nonpositive. In either case the sign of
1/(alpha-1) in D_alpha yields
 inf_(sigma_t separable) D_alpha(p^t||M_stop^t(sigma_t))
       =t D_alpha(p||g).
This is a classical stopping-record statement, not an unstated quantum Renyi
data-processing theorem at every order. It excludes an improvement obtained
merely by changing the stopping likelihood moment.

In fact full-state data processing, (12), and the product comparator give
 E_R(tau_w^(tensor t))=t R(w).                              (13)
Data processing against the product comparator bounds ANY global quantum
measurement or channel, not only stopping measurements, by t R(w). This is
a cap on relative-entropy-to-separable-output methods for this physical state;
it does not cap entanglement-cost converses using other structure.

5. AN EXPLICIT OPTIMAL COMPOSITE HYPOTHESIS-TESTING EXPONENT

Fix N and w throughout the t->infinity limit. Let
 V(w)=Var_p(ln R_L), finite on the finite positive support of p.
Given eta>0, measure the t stopping records and accept tau_w^(tensor t) if
 sum_b ln R_(L_b)>=t(R(w)-eta).                              (14)
Under the target, Chebyshev gives type-I error at most V(w)/(t eta^2).
Under EVERY globally separable alternative, (11) and Markov give type-II
error at most exp[-t(R(w)-eta)]. If R(w)=0, the zero exponent is already
optimal; no positive threshold is needed.

Conversely, consider any quantum test with type-I error alpha_t->0 and worst
separable type-II error beta_t. Its error against the PARTICULAR separable
comparator chi^(tensor Nt) is at most beta_t. Binary relative-entropy data
processing and (13) give
 tR(w)>=(1-alpha_t) ln(1/beta_t)-h_2(alpha_t).
Thus limsup_t[-ln beta_t]/t<=R(w). Together with (14), the optimal composite
separable exponent is EXACTLY R(w). No general composite quantum Stein lemma
or an interchange of the fixed-N and iid limits is used here.

6. SMOOTH MAX-RELATIVE-ENTROPY CANNOT RESTORE THE LOST RATE

This is a separate finite-dimensional smoothing construction, not a noisy
reuse protocol. In the common phase eigenbasis of tau_w^t and chi^(Nt), retain
only strings with log likelihood at most t(R(w)+eta), then renormalize the
target restriction to a state omega_t. If A_t is the retained probability,
Chebyshev gives 1-A_t<=V(w)/(t eta^2), and
 T(omega_t,tau_w^t)=1-A_t,
 D_max(omega_t||chi^(Nt))<=t(R(w)+eta)-ln A_t.                (15)
Here D_max is the operator-order max-relative entropy. Thus even allowing a
suitable vanishing global error, this comparator has max-relative-entropy
rate no larger than R(w). The claim is not about Schmidt number, zero-error
rank cost, or entanglement of formation, for which this order bound does not
provide an implementation.

The last line uses the known maximally-correlated E_C=E_F theorem and the
