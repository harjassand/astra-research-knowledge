1. A1: why the partition restriction really is a conditional expectation.

E_S is the trace-orthogonal conditional expectation onto
R_S=I_S tensor B(H_complement). If E_S(A) is contained in the diagonal MASA
A, its range on A equals A intersect R_S. That intersection is a unital
commutative subalgebra. Consequently its minimal projections P_C form a
partition of the rank-one diagonal projectors, and trace orthogonality gives
E_S(Pi_j)=P_C/|C|. Since P_C is a projection in R_S, it has the exact form
I_S tensor Q_C for a projection Q_C. Comparing traces and the explicit
partial trace formula yields

  |C|=D^|S| rank Q_C,
  Tr_S Pi_j=Q_C/rank Q_C.

The nonzero Schmidt spectrum of e_j is flat with rank rank Q_C. Hence that
rank is at most both subsystem dimensions; the stated bound by D^|S| is
valid. Empty and full erasures satisfy the same formula. No positivity-only
or approximate invariance hypothesis is being substituted for exact MASA
invariance.

The intersection identity A2 follows entrywise from K_SK_T=K_(S union T).
If j is in C and k in B, the jk entry is
|C intersect B|/(|C||B|); its union entry is 1/|U| when both are in U and
zero otherwise. Thus the exact commuting-square formula is correct.

2. A2: the common block rank cannot vary with the two local labels.

Let E_A cells be I_A tensor Q_b and E_B cells R_a tensor I_B. Their products
R_a tensor Q_b lie in A and have strictly positive rank dim R_a dim Q_b
for EVERY pair (a,b). Each such product therefore contains basis vectors.
For any of those vectors, A1 forces the reductions to be exactly the
normalized projections on R_a and Q_b. Equality of the nonzero spectra of
the two pure-state reductions forces dim R_a=dim Q_b. Since every pair
occurs, there is one common k, rather than row-dependent or column-dependent
ranks. Equal dimensions q imply k divides q. Each block contains precisely
k^2 orthonormal, maximally entangled vectors. Conversely, summing a complete
block basis gives R_a tensor Q_b; summing over a gives I_A tensor Q_b,
so each erasure of a rank-one basis projector is diagonal as claimed.

3. B1: exact support size and simultaneous coordinate containment.

For nonempty proper S, any one erased physical coordinate kills the g!=g'
terms because g maps to g a_j injectively. The residual support V has
exactly D elements, even when only one physical site remains. Two residual
supports have an intersection precisely when all their surviving a-labels
are related by a common left multiplier. If site 1 survives, its a_1=e
fixes that multiplier, leaving D^|S| erased a-label choices. If site 1 is
erased, a common multiplier has D choices and the other |S|-1 erased labels
are free, again D^|S| choices. The phase label is independently free.
Therefore every nonempty proper erasure cell has D^(|S|+1) labels.
For |S|=ell-1 this is the complete label space, as it must be because
every individual one-site reduction is maximally mixed.

The coordinate assignment y_1=phi(c), y_j=y_1 a_j is invertible. Holding
the y-labels outside S fixed implies, when 1 is erased,

  a'_j = (y'_1)^(-1) y_1 a_j  for every surviving j.

Thus a single common LEFT multiplier relates the surviving a-labels.
No commutativity was used. Every ordinary coordinate fiber lies inside
one true cell, not conversely. With uniform counting measure this gives
K_S A_S=K_S, and since both maps are orthogonal projections,
||K_S f||_2 <= ||A_S f||_2 for complex as well as positive f. This proves
the required simultaneous domination, with one fixed coordinate system
for all S. Hadamard completeness, not a group-character identity, is the
only phase premise.

4. B2: the common-classical entropy operation is valid and correctly scoped.

Blockwise bijections tensor together to yield one D-valued coordinate at
each physical site even when the blocks cross task boundaries. Erasure
conditional expectations factor over these disjoint physical blocks.
Consequently the B1 containment holds for every global subset with the
same coordinates. There is no assumption that the spectrum factors.

For a fixed task a, let z collect all other coordinates and set
q_z=sum_y f(y,z)^2. When q_z>0 apply the normalized one-chain lemma to
g_z=f(.,z)/sqrt(q_z). Its norm scores average to the actual global
coordinate scores because A_(a,i) acts only inside task a. Summing gives

  (ln D) sum_i ||A_(a,i) f||_2^2
       <= H(Y_a|Y_other tasks)+C_D sum_z q_z
       = H(Y_a|Y_other tasks)+C_D.

Zero-mass slices contribute zero and do not require normalization. Finally
H(Y_a|Y_other tasks)<=H(Y_a|Y_1,...,Y_(a-1)); summing the latter conditional
entropies gives H(Y_1,...,Y_r) by the ordinary chain rule. This proves the
stated r C_D defect. It uses classical conditional entropy only after the
explicit label construction, so it does not repeat the disproved stronger
quantum conditional-entropy premise.

The support corollary is also sound. Every pure vector in any decomposition
of a state supported on V lies in V, and therefore has a normal coefficient
matrix diagonal in this MASA. Replacing its diagonal coefficients by their
absolute values preserves the Schmidt spectrum. In this invariant class
each erasure score is c* K_S c with K_S entrywise nonnegative, so phase
removal can only increase each score. Applying B2 and then averaging the
ensemble proves the corresponding E_F witness. This does not constrain
general iid dilution outputs that lack that exact support.

5. C: what the nonabelian obstruction does and does not prove.

For ell=3 the three one-site-erasure partitions have quotient labels
a, b and a^(-1)b, with c free. Their joint incidence is the group Latin
square, with every pair of labels independent and the third determined
by the first two. Any arbitrary permutation of the full spectral-label
set preserving the three partitions induces only independent relabelings
of these quotient values. It cannot change that Latin-square incidence.

For a field-linear partition triple with the same cardinalities, the
first two quotient forms are independent. The third is their linear
combination; pairwise independence forces both coefficients nonzero.
Absorbing those coefficients into quotient relabelings presents the
Latin square of abelian addition. If the group triple admitted such
a presentation, there would be bijections f,g,h into an abelian group
with h(a^(-1)b)=f(a)+g(b). Taking b=a and then b=at gives

  f(at)-f(a)=h(e)-h(t)=f(t)-f(e).

Hence t -> f(t)-f(e) is an injective homomorphism into an abelian group;
the finite groups have equal size, so it is an isomorphism. A nonabelian
G is impossible. The Section C field-linear obstruction therefore passes.
The claim does not assert that individual GHZ-type vectors fail local
equivalence to stabilizer vectors, nor does it classify every invariant
MASA. If comparison with the earlier theorem is only prime D, the order-8
example should be described as a precise field-linear obstruction rather
than as a directly inside-prime-D counterexample. The current text does
make that distinction.

The same obstruction also rules out a common F_p-linear coset model when
D=p^m, provided the three erasure partitions are genuinely cosets in the
same additive label space. Let their surjective quotient maps be
L_i:V -> W_i, dim W_i=m. Functional dependence gives
ker(L_1,L_2) contained in ker L_3, so L_3=A L_1+B L_2. The Latin property
makes both A and B invertible. Relabeling the quotient values therefore
produces addition in the abelian group W_3 and the same impossible isotope.
Thus the dihedral order-8 example also excludes grouped F_2-linear cosets;
one need not restrict the distinction to F_8-linear scalar forms. This
optional strengthening makes no assertion about general composite modules.

5a. Updated tuple-dependent-Hadamard and fixed-D claims.

The proof was strengthened during this audit to permit a separately chosen
Hadamard h^(a) on EVERY relative support a. This modification passes. For
nonempty S, injectivity of g -> g a_j still removes all cross terms before
any phase is considered; unit modulus gives the same uniform reduction.
For the diagonal cell projector, sum over c first with a fixed a. Each
individual Hadamard has complete orthogonal rows, hence also complete
columns, giving sum_c h^(a)_c(g) conjugate(h^(a)_c(g'))=D delta_(g,g').
No comparison between Hadamards at different a occurs. All partition,
coordinate and entropy arguments therefore remain unchanged.

At D=2 the displayed rows (1,(-1)^c exp(i theta(a))) form an exact complex
Hadamard for every a. The theta(a_0)=pi/4 vector is not a Pauli-stabilizer
vector: a pure stabilizer state on two computational basis points must
have some stabilizer with bit-flip support equal to their difference.
(All-diagonal stabilizers cannot uniquely specify that two-point state.)
The Pauli action between the two points has a fourth-root-of-unity phase,
so their amplitude ratio belongs to {1,-1,i,-i}. The proposed ratio does
not. This establishes strict extension of the actual stabilizer-projector
hypothesis, without asserting inequivalence under arbitrary local rotations.
No circuit-depth lower bound was audited. The safe complexity statement is
that the theorem imposes no bounded-depth phase-circuit premise.
