TWO-TEMPERATURE GAS DISPERSION: A CONSTRUCTIVE ADMISSION TEST
Prepared 2026-10-21 UTC. Mathematical obligation, not a new process trajectory.

RESULT FIRST
A conservation- and entropy-consistent extension can be constructed without
borrowing total bed conductivity for the gas equation. Its coefficients cannot
be identified from the evidence presently available. The mathematical design
obligation is resolved below; the hot-bed physical-admission obligation is not.

Two decision-changing structural results are established:
1. Even an exact same-condition local-thermal-equilibrium total conductivity does
   not identify the gas/solid partition or bound species dispersion without an
   additional constitutive assumption. Nonnegative species mobilities of arbitrary
   scale produce the same composition-uniform, constant-pressure thermal response.
   Thus selecting a species Peclet number from that thermal coefficient is not an
   inference the data support. Positivity of entropy is not a calibration.
2. If K_total = k_g + k_s in a local diagonal two-temperature closure, adding all
   K_total to gas while retaining the explicit solid flux adds exactly
   -k_s * dT_g/dx. In the co-temperature limit this replaces K_total by
   K_total + k_s, a factor between one and two. In a non-LTE state it can give
   nonzero net heat flux where the correct two-phase contributions cancel, and
   its relative flux error is unbounded near that cancellation. Both operators
   may conserve energy and produce entropy; passing those checks alone cannot
   detect duplicate transport.

Consequently a long run with an arbitrarily selected favorable gas dispersion is
not physically admitted by this result. Neither is zero physical dispersion
validated. The current physical blocker remains explicit rather than fitted away.
The species/energy interface specification below is a usable conditional design
asset for a coupled construction, not a completed numerical implementation.

1. VARIABLES, BASIS AND CONSERVATIVE BALANCES
Use x in metres along bed and all face fluxes per total bed cross-section.
q_i = rho Y_i [kg/m3 gas]; U = rho e [J/m3 gas]; epsilon is porosity.
G is barycentric superficial mass flux [kg/m2/s]. j_i is a relative species
mass flux [kg/m2/s], required to obey sum_i j_i = 0.
Let Q = H(T_s - T_g) [W/m3 bed], with H >= 0. Let C_s be the actual solid
energy derivative per bed volume, not automatically an independently validated
constant. Surface sources, if admitted separately, require their own balances.

  epsilon d_t q_i + d_x(G Y_i + j_i) = epsilon omega_i
  epsilon d_t U + d_x(G h + sum_i h_i j_i + q_g) = Q + external gas heating
  d_t E_s + d_x q_s = -Q + external solid heating

Here q_g is reduced gas heat flux, meaning heat other than transported species
enthalpy; q_s is separately modeled solid/radiative heat flux. The advective
energy flux and relative species energy flux use enthalpy, even though the
stored gas variable is internal energy. Formation energies are already in U
and h_i. Do not add a second chemical heat source to this conservative form.

Internal faces transfer exactly the same j_i and energy flux out of one cell
and into the other. This preserves each global species inventory in a sealed
nonreacting assembly and each elemental inventory with element-conserving
chemistry. Relative species diffusion need NOT have zero local elemental flux:
elements are allowed to move between cells. Its total mass sum must vanish.
A kg/m2/s face flux is divided by gas volume per unit area, epsilon dx, in
q_i or U evolution. A bed-basis conductivity k_g has units W/(m K).
If a gas-volume thermal diffusivity alpha is used, k_g = epsilon rho cp alpha.
The same epsilon must not be inserted or removed twice.

2. CONTINUUM ENTROPY-CONSISTENT GAS OPERATOR
For the ideal-gas mixture let mu_i be partial chemical potential per unit mass
[J/kg], beta = 1/T_g, and define the reduced diffusion force

  z_i = -d_x(mu_i/T_g) + h_i d_x beta.

Choose a symmetric positive-semidefinite mobility Lambda with Lambda 1 = 0:

  j = Lambda z,
  q_g = k_g T_g^2 d_x beta = -k_g d_x T_g,       k_g >= 0.

Then the diffusion-plus-reduced-heat entropy production is exactly

  sigma_g = j dot z + q_g d_x beta
          = z^T Lambda z + k_g T_g^2 (d_x beta)^2 >= 0.

This is a deliberately minimal closure with no independent Soret/Dufour
cross-coefficients. A general Onsager block is possible, but its cross terms
would also require identification. One cannot set those unknowns favorably
and then call the response measured.

For ideal gases, write R_i = R_u/W_i, mole fraction x_i, and standard chemical
potential mu_i^0(T). Gibbs-Helmholtz gives

  d(mu_i^0/T)/d(1/T) = h_i.

Therefore z_i = -R_i d_x ln(x_i p). The thermal part of chemical potential
cancels species enthalpy transport. There is no artificial diffusion driven
solely by temperature when p and composition are uniform. The remaining
pressure term matters in the present pressure-relaxation model.

One explicit admissible common-D family, for strictly positive Y, is

  P = I - Y 1^T,
  Lambda = (epsilon rho D / R_u) P diag(W_i Y_i) P^T,    D >= 0.

It is symmetric positive semidefinite and Lambda 1 = 0. At constant pressure
this yields j = -epsilon rho D d_x Y exactly in the continuum. Proof: with
W_mix = 1/sum(Y_i/W_i), b_i = 1/W_i, the composition Hessian is

  A = R_u [diag(1/(W_i Y_i)) - W_mix b b^T],
  A Y = 0,
  P diag(W_i Y_i) P^T A = R_u P,
  P d_x Y = d_x Y.

At variable pressure the same mobility retains a consistent pressure-driven
term; do not silently delete it. This family is an admissible phenomenological
extension, not a measured Maxwell-Stefan matrix for hot air in this bed.
A bare common-D Fick law at variable pressure does not, on its own, guarantee
nonnegative diffusion entropy. For a dimensionless isothermal binary example
R=(1,2), Y=(1/2,1/2), Y_x=(a,-a), b=d_x ln p, its normalized production is
(16/3) a^2 - a b. Taking a=1, b=6 gives -2/3. For arbitrarily small nonzero b,
a=3b/32 gives -3b^2/64. This is a counterexample for the diffusion component,
not a claim that the full pressure-flow model's total entropy must decrease:
other mechanisms, including drag, can contribute positive entropy.

If a common eddy displacement is specifically assumed to mix both enthalpy
and composition, the local energy dispersion is -epsilon rho D d_x h:

  -epsilon rho D d_x h
    = sum_i h_i (-epsilon rho D d_x Y_i)
      -epsilon rho cp D d_x T_g.

Thus the same-eddy submodel implies a reduced thermal term epsilon rho cp D.
It is not an empirical identity between arbitrary thermal and molecular
species dispersion. Distinct species/thermal coefficients or a full mobility
matrix remain possible. This conditional relation cannot supply its own D.

3. AN EXACT ENTROPY-PRODUCING FINITE-VOLUME FACE
For positive left/right temperatures and species, define Delta f = f_R-f_L,
face distance d > 0 and beta = 1/T_g. Define

  a_i(T) = mu_i^0(T)/T,
  hbar_i = Delta a_i / Delta beta,  if T_L != T_R,
  hbar_i = h_i(T_L),               if T_L = T_R,
  zface_i = -Delta(mu_i/T_g) + hbar_i Delta beta.

This inverse-temperature secant is a mean species enthalpy, not a new fitted
transport coefficient. With positive species cp it lies between the endpoint
enthalpies. It exactly gives zface_i = -R_i Delta ln(x_i p), so temperature
cancellation holds for a finite face as well as an infinitesimal one.
Numerically near equal temperatures use a cancellation-safe enthalpy mean;
the simple diagnostic uses separated temperatures and does not claim to be
a production implementation of that near-equality limit.

Choose symmetric PSD L_face with L_face 1 = 0, e.g. the preceding Lambda
using positive face Y and rho, divided by d. Then

  j_face = L_face zface,
  qg_face = k_g T_L T_R Delta beta / d = -k_g Delta T_g / d,
  fE_relative = hbar dot j_face + qg_face.

At fixed cell volumes, gas entropy density has differential

  dS_g = -sum_i (mu_i/T_g) dq_i + beta dU.

The summed entropy change caused by this one internal face is exactly

  j_face dot [-Delta(mu/T)] + fE_relative Delta beta
  = zface^T L_face zface + k_g T_L T_R (Delta beta)^2/d >= 0.

This is a semidiscrete operator proof. It is not a positivity theorem for BDF,
a truncation-error bound, or validation of the advective/momentum/chemical
operators. The log chemical potentials are undefined for negative species;
the existing signed Newton-trial chemistry extension does not automatically
extend this new operator. Zero-species limits and positivity-preserving or
properly continued nonlinear numerics must be handled explicitly before use.
Do not insert an unreported concentration floor and call the proof unchanged.

4. KEEP SOLID CONDUCTION AND PHASE EXCHANGE SEPARATE
A passive scalar solid closure q_s = -k_s d_x T_s with k_s >= 0 contributes
k_s (d_x T_s)^2/T_s^2 >= 0. The existing code's radiative proxy

  q_s,face = -(K_R/4) (T_s,R^4 - T_s,L^4)/d

has the positive face conductivity

  k_s,face = (K_R/4)(T_R^3 + T_R^2 T_L + T_R T_L^2 + T_L^3).

Its differential value is K_R T_s^3. The existing prescribed K_R yields
1.391557832 W/(m K) at 1700 K, 4.425624086 at 2500 K and 5.575011768 at
2700 K. These are properties of the current conditional equation, not new
zirconia measurements or a transferable qualification of that equation.

Interphase exchange contributes

  Q/T_g - Q/T_s = H (T_s-T_g)^2/(T_g T_s) >= 0.

The gas/solid exchange cancels exactly in total energy and must not also be
counted as axial transport. A finite-particle temperature model changes the
interfacial solid temperature/energy representation; its own radial conduction
and boundary exchange must retain this separation.

5. RIGOROUS NON-DOUBLE-COUNTING AND IDENTIFIABILITY BOUNDS
Restrict this section to a local diagonal two-phase conductive closure with
no additional cross-gradient terms. Suppose a same-condition LTE experiment
really establishes K_total = k_g + k_s. It follows only that

  0 <= k_g <= K_total; 0 <= k_s <= K_total.

For gradients g_g and g_s the correct total flux belongs to the sharp interval
between -K_total g_g and -K_total g_s. Every point of the interval corresponds
to a nonnegative partition. In particular, when the two gradients oppose,
the sign of the total flux may be undetermined. Assigning all K_total to gas
and omitting the solid channel gives the error bound

  |q_true - (-K_total g_g)| <= K_total |g_s-g_g|.

This is an instantaneous structural flux bound, NOT a bound on the final
product, heat quotient or trajectory. It vanishes in the LTE gradient limit.
If k_s has been independently identified for that same local decomposition,
then k_g = K_total-k_s >= 0 is necessary. K_total < k_s is an impossibility
certificate for that proposed passive split. A scalar conductivity measured
under different geometry, material, flow, temperature, or an inverse fit with
finite interphase resistance does not automatically satisfy this additive
interpretation; no arbitrary subtraction is justified.

If all K_total is instead put into gas AND the known solid term retained,

  q_bad = -K_total g_g - k_s g_s,
  q_bad - q_true = -k_s g_g.

At g_g=g_s this duplicates one whole solid contribution. The flux ratio is
1+k_s/K_total, between one and two when K_total>0. An explicit counterexample
is k_g=k_s=1, g_g=1, g_s=-1: q_true=0 and q_bad=-1. In the same example a
measured K_total=2 permits partitions (k_g,k_s)=(0,2) through (2,0), all with
the same LTE response but off-LTE fluxes spanning +2 to -2.

No finite upper bound on species mobility follows from that LTE thermal
experiment. Hold k_g,k_s,H fixed, keep p and composition uniform in the
experiment, and replace Lambda by c Lambda for any c>=0. Then z=0, j=0 and
the entire thermal measurement is unchanged. In a reacting composition field
the species transport changes with c. Every member still passes conservation
and entropy tests. The common-eddy assumption in section 2 could impose an
additional thermal/species relation, but it is an added constitutive premise,
not a consequence of K_total. No measured species Peclet number is available.

6. EXACT INTEGRATION OBLIGATIONS FOR reacting_heater_signed.py
These are design requirements; the preserved solver was NOT edited or run.
- Form the total face arrays S_i = G Y_i,donor + j_i and
  E = G h_donor + hbar dot j + q_g. Use those same arrays in every inventory
  and boundary ledger. Keep the separately indexed solid q_s array separate.
- Heater-boundary molar flow is sum_i S_i/W_i, not merely G/W_mix,donor once
  species dispersion crosses that interface. Net relative mass can be zero
  while relative total molar flow is nonzero. The current molL/molR formulas
  must therefore be generalized, or a precisely justified zero-dispersion
  interface condition is required. Do not assume impermeable dispersion at
  every bed/header join merely because that is convenient.
- The uniform-pressure header molar-source constraint must include every
  relative molar face flux. Internal advective molar fluxes must be solved
  after subtracting any prescribed relative molar contribution. Its stored
  energy invariant dU_H=sum_i e_i(T_H)dq_i remains valid, but heater input is
  computed with TOTAL energy fluxes on both faces. A heat-dispersion path into
  an isothermal header changes required heater power and must not disappear.
- NOx export uses total boundary S. Boundary enthalpy uses total E. Reservoir
  and reversal boundary conditions must specify whether feed composition is
  the advective donor composition or the prescribed total inflowing species
  flux (a Danckwerts-type condition); these are not generally the same once
  diffusion is present. Count any imposed reservoir exchange in the ledger.
- Artificial upwind dispersion remains in addition to explicit physical
  dispersion. Do not subtract a modified-equation D_num as though it were a
  guaranteed physical coefficient; that asymptotic estimate is local and
  constant-coefficient, and can change with grid, state, boundary and limiter.
- Supply actual coefficient definitions, pressure treatment, reversal/end
  behavior and interfacial matching before claiming this as a physical model.
  Preserve strict accepted-state checks and establish the new operator's
  Jacobian/zero-species/positivity behavior before a stiff coupled evolution.

7. WHAT EVIDENCE WOULD CHANGE ADMISSION
The unresolved observable is not 'some total thermal conductivity'. It is
an applicable gas mixing response, including the species/thermal distinction,
in the present packing and relevant flow/reversal regime. A matched inert
tracer residence-time/spatial response can constrain species dispersion;
phase-resolved transient gas/solid temperatures can constrain thermal
partition and separate it from particle transfer. Those tests need compatible
end conditions and dynamic response, not just one LTE scalar. They may be
measured or supplied by independently validated pore-scale homogenization.
The present documents contain no such applicable calibration. No new search
was undertaken here and no synthetic fixture is promoted to a measurement.

A safe next numerical use is the coupled conservation/entropy/interface unit
construction with coefficients left explicit and physical qualification left
open. A robust response claim would require independently justified bounded
coefficient sets and success for the whole admitted set, not one favorable
member. With the current unbounded/unknown species mobility and unqualified
phase partition, this work supplies no rigorous process-performance envelope.
It blocks a thermal-Peclet shortcut, not the physical possibility of nitrogen
fixation or a future qualified regenerative construction.

8. EXECUTION AND LIMITS
face_diagnostics.py evaluates algebraic face identities with the pinned
continuous NASA thermodynamics. It does not import or call a trajectory driver.
Its normalized mobility and conductivity values are unit-test fixtures only.
It checks exact conservative face transfer, entropy-factorization identity,
ideal-gas force cancellation, the conductive counterexample and the pressure-
gradient Fick counterexample. The result JSON records machine residuals and
runtime/RSS. Only this small diagnostic was executed, with no fitted coefficients,
no source search, and no edits to the preserved model. An initial assertion
used an incorrect expected coefficient for the hand-written binary example;
the direct formula exposed it. The expected coefficient was corrected from
8/3 to 16/3, rerun, and all checks passed. This was an algebraic diagnostic
repair, not adjustment of a process or transport coefficient.
