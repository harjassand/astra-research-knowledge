Python3 standard library is sufficient for all NEW checks. From any directory:
  python3 /path/to/Astra_Amplification_Cycle2_PRO2_20261022/replay.py --output /new/writable/directory
The wrapper validates this archive's internal payload hashes, copies new code
and frozen witnesses into that directory, and runs the independent witness
checker plus the small exact decoder/failure checks. It does not construct new
witnesses, change frozen receipts, or replay any prior benchmark or moment trial.
An internal manifest detects corruption; authenticate the archive against the
external SHA256 printed on the private handoff Page.

To produce the same rational witness data from source in a separate directory:
  python3 checks/produce_separator_witnesses.py --output /new/witnesses --large

deterministic. This optional construction is not needed to verify saved data.
