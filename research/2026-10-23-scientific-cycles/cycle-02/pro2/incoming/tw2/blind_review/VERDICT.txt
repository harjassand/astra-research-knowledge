1. TARGET AND BLINDING

The target is a dimension- and degree-independent C for three columns,
row Euclidean norms at most one, and all real symmetric supported D,
with operator norm on D and Frobenius norm on DX. A negative answer must
quantify over every allowed X on an explicit treewidth-two graph family.

I read only COMMON_CONTRACT.txt, proof_x/RESULT.txt, and
proof_y/RESULT.txt from the supplied blind_review directory, then wrote
and ran my own small checks under blind_review/checks. I did not inspect
parent arm directories, experiment roles or mappings, participant costs,
attempt records, evaluator reports, or the original Pro2 source archive.
No source beyond the self-contained arguments was needed.

2. PROOF X: ACCEPTED CLAIMS AND REASONS

2.1 Principal lower bound -- FULLY VALID.

For n=8m, m>=1, on the square of the n-vertex path, and for every real
n-by-3 X with all row norms <=1, the accepted conclusion is

  K_2(X) >= max{1, n^(1/4)/sqrt(24 sqrt(5))}.

In the nonzero-row case, r=min_i ||x_i||, the stronger intermediate
conclusion is also accepted:

  K_2(X) >= max{1/r, r sqrt(n)/(24 sqrt(5))}.

If the action has a nonzero kernel, K_2(X)=infinity and these bounds
hold automatically. A zero row supplies an invisible diagonal. Thus
the proof genuinely covers all capped X, including non-generic and
singular designs, and taking the infimum over X is legitimate.

The decisive argument checks out as follows.

* The supported-symmetric space has dimension n+(n-1)+(n-2)=3n-3.
  Under the injectivity reduction, the adjoint kernel has exactly
  dimension 3 in its 3n-dimensional domain. This is a rank-nullity
  statement; it does not assume that the kernel consists of a
  particular set of rotation fields.

* The adjoint is P_G sym(YX^T), with the claimed 1/2 factor on each
  off-diagonal entry. Substituting y_i=f_i A x_i gives exactly
  (f_j-f_i) x_i^T A x_j / 2. Counting each unordered edge once and
  using the symmetric Frobenius norm gives the residual energy
  factor 1/2 in the proof.

* The four integer tents have disjoint nonzero row supports, including
  at m=1, and their full squared masses are (2m^3+m)/3. The three
  displayed rotations satisfy sum_A ||Ax||^2=2||x||^2. Consequently
  each tent supports a nonzero selected field with norm at least
  (2/3)r m^(3/2). These four fields are orthogonal and independent.

* The distance-one and distance-two difference energies are at most
  2m and 8m. The residual norm bound is therefore sqrt(5m). The
  triangle inequality followed by Cauchy-Schwarz correctly pays a
  factor 2 for arbitrary linear combinations of the four fields;
  residual orthogonality is not being assumed.

* Four dimensions suffice to find a nonzero field orthogonal to the
  entire three-dimensional adjoint kernel. On that orthogonal
  complement the adjoint has the same positive singular values as
  the action map. Therefore the resulting small Rayleigh quotient
  proves a small NONZERO singular value of the action map. There is
  no mistaken use of one of the automatic zero adjoint singular
  values.

* The Frobenius inverse lower bound r n/(24 sqrt(5)) is converted
  to an operator inverse lower bound using the full inequality
  ||D||_F <= sqrt(n)||D||_2, giving r sqrt(n)/(24 sqrt(5)). The
  argument does not silently substitute a Frobenius recovery goal.

* The diagonal witness supplies 1/r. Multiplying the two lower
  bounds and taking a square root removes r without any row
  normalization or monotonicity assumption. Thus arbitrarily short
  nonzero rows do not evade the obstruction.

2.2 Admissible graph family -- FULLY VALID.

The consecutive triple bags form a width-two tree decomposition of
the squared path. All D are drawn from the supported-symmetric space
for this graph. Its maximum degree is at most 4. The negative result
therefore holds even on a family with uniformly bounded degree.

2.3 Unit-row specialization -- FULLY VALID.

For unit rows, r=1 and the stated sqrt(n)/(24 sqrt(5)) lower bound
follows. This is a specialization of an already valid row-cap proof,
not an unjustified replacement of the capped problem by unit rows.

2.4 Decoder consequence and witness procedure -- FULLY VALID within
the stated scope.

An exact decoder obeying the asserted adversarial noise estimate
would force the inverse inequality by comparing zero data with D
and noise -DX. This also applies to nonlinear decoders. An SVD of
the finite action matrix supplies the stated witness; the quoted
O(n^3) dense arithmetic work and O(n^2) storage are consistent with
its dimensions. No efficient stable decoder is being claimed.

3. PROOF Y: ACCEPTED CLAIMS AND REASONS

3.1 Principal lower bound -- FULLY VALID.

For the fan F_m with m leaves, m>=4, and every real (m+1)-by-3 X
with row norms <=1, the accepted conclusion is

  C_2(F_m,X) >= max{1,
       [2 sqrt(m-1) sin(3 pi/(2m))]^(-1/2)}
     >= max{1, m^(1/4)/sqrt(3 pi)}.

The decisive argument checks out as follows.

* A zero apex row supplies an invisible diagonal. If any adjacent
  projected determinant vanishes, the three query rows on the
  corresponding triangle are linearly dependent. A nonzero vector
  in that local dependence produces ww^T supported entirely on
  that triangle with zero action. Every leaf belongs to such a
  triangle. Hence the nondegenerate reduction, including positive
  projected row lengths, is exhaustive.

* For a projected row z_i, the vector with entries w_i=1 and
  w_0=-t_i/r_0 has w^T X=z_i^T. Its rank-one matrix has admissible
  edge-and-diagonal support and exact ratio ||w||/||z_i|| >= 1/r_i.
  This proves the local bound 1/delta even when the original row
  has substantial component parallel to the apex query row.

* The tridiagonal B has precisely the claimed action
  (c_i-c_(i-1)) Jz_i/r_i^2. Both the orientation signs and the
  denominator are correct. The energy is exactly the weighted
  difference energy. The upper bound using delta and the lower
  operator bound ||B||_2 >= max_i |b_i| >= ||c||_2/sqrt(m-1)
  are valid under row caps.

* For m>=4 the three sine modes are independent, with the stated
  Dirichlet eigenvalues. Their largest energy eigenvalue is
  4 sin^2(3 pi/(2m)). The map c -> t^T B(c)Z has only two scalar
  outputs and is linear, so its kernel inside this three-dimensional
  space contains a nonzero flow. This argument works for every X;
  it makes no rank or genericity assumption about the constraints.

* The lift D=L B L^T is supported on the fan: the leaf principal
  block stays tridiagonal, and every newly introduced off-diagonal
  entry is an allowed apex-to-leaf entry. Its operator norm is at
  least that of its principal block B. The identity L^T X=[Z,0]
  is exact. The chosen two constraints cancel the entire apex
  action, so ||DX||_F=||BZ||_F, without an unaccounted factor
  involving ||L|| or 1/r_0.

* A nonzero flow cannot have zero difference energy under the two
  Dirichlet boundary values. Thus the denominator is nonzero in
  the constructed nondegenerate case. Combining the flow witness
  delta Q_m with the local witness 1/delta gives sqrt(Q_m).
  The sine estimate and ensuing m^(1/4)/sqrt(3 pi) bound have the
  correct direction and constants.

3.2 Admissible graph family -- FULLY VALID.

The bags {0,i,i+1} form a width-two tree decomposition. The family
has maximum degree m, so the proof establishes the requested
unrestricted-degree negative endpoint. This argument itself makes
no bounded-degree claim.

3.3 Decoder consequence and witness cost -- FULLY VALID within the
stated scope.

The noise comparison forces the inverse inequality even for a
nonlinear exact decoder. The witness construction uses only
tridiagonal operations, three sine recurrences, and a constant-size
nullspace calculation, so O(m) exact-real arithmetic and O(m)
storage are justified with the stated convention for evaluating
trigonometric constants. No bit-complexity or floating-point
conditioning guarantee is asserted or inferred.

3.4 Four-query stability and minimum query count on the fan family
-- FULLY VALID.

Giving the apex color 4 and cycling colors 1,2,3 on the leaves
isolates all diagonals, every apex edge in a leaf's fourth action
coordinate, and each path edge in an endpoint's neighbor-color
coordinate. The selected cells are distinct. Reconstructing a
symmetric matrix doubles only off-diagonal squared errors, giving
the claimed Frobenius and operator error factor sqrt(2). Work and
storage are O(m). Three queries fail uniformly by the main theorem;
one or two queries would also give a three-column design by adding
zero columns. Thus four is indeed the minimum fixed number for
uniform stability on this fan family. This is not an upper bound
for all treewidth-two graphs.

4. EXACT ACCEPTED ENDPOINT AND LIMITS

Both proofs establish an explicit graph family of treewidth at most
two for which the infimum over ALL real dense-or-sparse capped
three-column designs of the operator/Frobenius-action inverse
constant diverges. Both therefore answer the common endpoint in
the negative. Their conclusions are compatible with generic exact
identification and the supplied six-query stable upper bound.

Neither proof establishes an optimal rate or constant, or decides
whether four or five queries suffice for all treewidth-two graphs.
Proof X additionally supplies a bounded-degree obstruction; proof Y
additionally determines the exact uniform query count on its own
fan family. These are different accepted scopes, not a preference
between anonymized participants.
