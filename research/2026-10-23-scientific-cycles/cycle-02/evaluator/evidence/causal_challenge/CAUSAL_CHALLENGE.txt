audit explicitly distinguishes these. [T §§1–5; C §§1–3; P §1]

The prospective result is also substantive: two isolated, source-informed
investigators produce different internally accepted proofs of the all-design,

primary endpoint; neither merely rediscovers exact rank or a six-query upper

The strongest defensible wording is "documented reuse and subsequent scientific

It is not "measured amplification," "the treatment improved intelligence," or

2. WHAT THE ACTUAL COMPARISON IDENTIFIES

TW2 assigned A to dependency bypass and B to ordinary unrestricted continuation
using a random bit before dispatch, with the same stated sources/settings, a
720-second allowance, 30 top-level calls, and no subagents. Its frozen endpoint
was met by both. The observed primary contrast is therefore zero successful
endpoints, treatment minus control. One pair supplies neither an informative
average-effect estimate nor evidence of equivalence. Control success does show
that the extra instruction was not necessary for this realized successful attempt;
it does not show that the instruction can never help. [R; A; B; V]

Different proof mechanisms do not rescue a positive treatment effect. The blind
review accepts a bounded-degree squared-path obstruction and a fan obstruction
with an exact four-query minimum on that family. These are different additional
scopes, with no preregistered value ordering. Choosing the preferred extra theorem
after unblinding would change the outcome rule. A proof that happens to look like
"bypass" is also not evidence that the extra sentence caused that reasoning:
ordinary researchers were allowed to bypass prerequisites too. [R; V §§2–4]

Blind adjudication substantially protects validity assessment from preference for
an arm. It does not create a counterfactual for receiving complete scientific
information, choosing this frontier, using a fresh context, or receiving additional
research labour. Both arms share those conditions. Common model and source
background also mean role separation is not statistical independence of all error
risks. The review is substantive internal evidence, not historical-priority review,
external acceptance, or proof of owner usefulness. [R; V §§1,5–6]

4. PLAUSIBLE COMPETING EXPLANATIONS

* Scientific information plus more capable labour. Complete incoming lemmas,
  corrected assumptions, counterexamples and ordinary additional investigation
  can explain progress with unchanged underlying research capability. Both TW2
  arms inherited a mature mathematical source containing path and coloring
  machinery. The trial tests an extra instruction conditional on that capital,
  not the effect of having the capital. [T §5; C §§5–7; R]

* Nearby opportunities and a mature question. Owners selected adjacent extensions
  that fit received mechanisms. TW2 selected one precise next frontier. Two quick
  successes may mean this checkpoint was tractable for ordinary source-informed
  reasoning, not that previous ownership was inefficient. No matched earlier
  opportunity, exposure, work budget or stopping decision was observed. [T §5;
  C §§1,6; P §3; V]

* Changed scientific contracts. Mean stopping cost, initialization and sparse
  information can reveal substantial new theorems without improving the earlier
  endpoint under its original contract. Pro3's restored exponent already matches
  pure IMH, and its evaluated finite hybrid certificates lose. Pro2's dense
  completion theorem is a different information-class result, not a practical
  improvement over its dense verified incumbent. [T §§3–5; C §§3–4]

* Ordinary stochastic diversity. Independent attempts can find different valid
  routes. A second route can be valuable redundancy or extra scope without any
  treatment superiority, optimal agent count or recursive gain. [R; V]

* Evaluation and selection effects. Proof-producing adjacent tasks are easier to
  adjudicate here than physical applicability and absent constitutive evidence.
  Successful owner continuations are not a denominator of all comparable failed
  opportunities. Three observed acceptances are not three randomized tests of an
  architecture. The process should not steer owner science toward what is easiest
  for the evaluator to certify. [E; T §5; P §§3,6]

Compact handoffs, fresh contexts and extra reviewers consequently remain candidate
methods. Neither their existence nor successful work after them identifies their
marginal benefit. A compact packet carrying a new lemma changes information as
well as presentation. Both TW2 contexts are fresh. The observational owners use
multiple contributors, while each TW2 investigator uses none. There is no matched
contrast for either choice. A lineage of useful proofs demonstrates accumulation
of scientific knowledge; it does not demonstrate recursively improving the
research process or capability at fixed resources. [T §§5–7; P §§3–5; R]

6. ONE MORE DISCRIMINATING NEXT EXPERIMENT

Run an equal-total-time HANDOFF VERSUS CONTINUATION experiment in shadow copies,
using the next three consequential milestones already recorded by owners before
allocation. Owners keep their actual programmes and need not participate. Freeze
the complete available source and the owner-defined acceptance obligation for
each; retain all three outcomes, including failures. Do not substitute easier
questions after seeing results.

For each milestone, randomize independent same-model sessions between:
  (a) one ordinary unrestricted investigator continuing for 24 minutes; and
  (b) a 12-minute investigator followed by a fresh 12-minute receiver, with the
      first investigator's source-bound proof/attempt handoff produced inside its
      allowance. The receiver gets the identical original source plus that work.
Both may use any valid mathematical method. Match tool/calculation allowances and
sum allotted investigator time; include packet preparation and reading within it.
The comparison deliberately allocates the same wall-time allowance differently.
Without exposed inference metering, do not rename it equal compute or equal money.

Freeze existing proof files at common prespecified cumulative-work checkpoints,
without prompting extra scientific analysis, and adjudicate them blind. The useful
endpoint is the first frozen complete result accepted against the owner's actual
obligation, with final success/failure and all correction/review work retained.
This provides a prespecified distinction even when both final outputs succeed.
Cap and charge review work consistently; two handoff-stage records do not earn
two independent discoveries. Any later owner use is a separate receiving outcome,
not an extra intervention imposed on the owner during the trial.

This directly challenges the strongest alternative explanation, extra labour:
does allocating a fixed investigator-time allowance across a handoff and fresh
receiver add useful progress beyond uninterrupted ordinary reasoning? It tests
that allocation bundle, not pure compactness, freshness alone or general recursive
amplification. Three milestones still provide only local evidence, but this is
more discriminating than adding another equally informed researcher and crediting
all ensuing mathematics to a new architecture. A tie or loss leaves the ordinary
continuation default intact. Any advantage must survive its packet and review
costs before being called an efficiency gain.
