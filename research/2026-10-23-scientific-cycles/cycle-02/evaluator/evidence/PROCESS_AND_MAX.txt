5. UNCONVENTIONAL TESTABLE METHOD: CHALLENGE THE NECESSITY OF THE BOTTLENECK

Hypothesis: on some persistent programmes, the highest-return independent
contribution is an explicit attempt to make the selected difficult lemma
unnecessary for the desired scientific endpoint. The treatment asks a collaborator
to prove that an accepted operation, stronger incumbent, or permitted construction
already reaches the endpoint despite the open lemma, or to establish that the
lemma really is indispensable under the same contract. This differs from another
generic audit: its product is a new scientifically valid route or a precise
necessity result. Ultra's stopping-vs-LSI/tensor decision is direct motivation;
Pro2's sparsity distinction and Pro3's one-transition result are supporting cases,
not retrospective experimental successes [U/CHECKPOINT.txt:7–13,27–43;
C/pro2/VISIBLE_WORK_SUMMARY_RENDERED.txt:180–205; P3/CONTINUATION_SEED.md:15–20].

Proposed prospective test, not executed or assigned here:

1. Let a continuing owner select one consequential unsolved endpoint and declare
   its operational assumptions, strongest accepted comparator, presently selected
   technical bottleneck, and exact success/failure criteria. Fix these before
   allocation. Use an actually open question; do not rerun the already closed
   Ultra leading law or manufacture another ell7 transfer.
2. Give two same-type independent contributors the identical complete source,
   failures, incumbent and current owner state, with matched elapsed and tool
   budgets. The owner remains warm and receives neither arm's hints before freeze.
   Treatment explicitly tries to eliminate the bottleneck's necessity for the
   SAME endpoint. Active control is ordinary constructive continuation with full
   freedom to choose methods, including a bypass, and a request to beat/check the
   same incumbent. Do not cripple the control by withholding sources, banning
   alternatives, forcing an inferior lemma, or substituting proofreading.
3. Freeze all visible work at the same prespecified point. A blinded scientific
   adjudicator checks endpoint validity, exact assumptions, whether a weakened
   contract or relaxed information class produced the apparent win, and whether
   it beats the strongest ordinary method on the same metric. Count treatment
   framing, adaptation, failed attempts, verification, owner integration and
   delivery separately; record unknown model/monetary costs as unknown.
4. Primary outcome: accepted progress on the original endpoint that changes the
   owner's actual next calculation or proof. A negative result can qualify only
   if it closes a precise admitted route or necessity question, not because an
   arm wrote a detailed report. A within-budget theorem is not an end-to-end
   speedup until downstream use and integration are counted. Secondary outcomes:
   wrong-assumption escapes, incumbent rediscoveries, useful necessity proofs,
   adaptation burden and whether a receiver can use the operation without repair.
5. Retain null/negative pairs. Repeat only across additional owner-selected live
   obligations if the first yields a useful discriminating result; no indefinite
   tournament or mandatory review headcount. One successful pair would establish
   feasibility on that obligation, not a general causal law of intelligence.

This is a stronger control than comparing fresh advice to an exhausted old seed,
or scoring the treatment for noticing information the control was denied. Its
mechanism is testable: does explicit pressure to question a proof dependency add
scientific value beyond ordinary capable construction with the same information?
The present observational record cannot answer that causal question.
