# Primary comparisons and scope

## Closest theorem for the final coding step

V. Giovannetti, D. Burgarth and S. Mancini, *Communication Through a Quantum Link*, Physical Review A 79, 012311 (2009), [arXiv:0810.0170](https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.0170), [primary full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0810.0170).

Definition 1 and Theorem 2 give large exact codes for causal channel sequences with subexponential exact environment dimension; the displayed size is greater than D/(k^4+k^2). The proof uses input dimension and Kraus count, without an IID or bath-reset premise. This is the direct predecessor of the final low-environment coding step. It does not establish the present type restriction, approximate infinite-bath compression or common recovery over coherent initial bath states.

## Exact reduced interface and reuse

T. Eggeling, D. Schlingemann and R. F. Werner, *Semicausal operations are semilocalizable*, Europhysics Letters 57, 782–788 (2002), [arXiv:quant-ph/0104027](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0104027), equation (3). The Heisenberg identity W*(X tensor I)=N*(X) tensor I is the relevant established semicausal interface. The proof here iterates that identity directly on normal processes; it does not import the finite-dimensional factorization theorem to an infinite bath. The explicit two-call tree realization is separately proved.
