EXECUTIVE ASSESSMENT

Astra now has stronger evidence of cumulative science than at the preceding review. All three autonomous Pro owners explicitly accepted supplied scientific contributions. Pro1 and Pro3 used received lemmas in later proofs; Pro2 used the accepted moment result to move to a stronger information question. Ultra also obtained its leading operational iid result through a direct stopping argument without resolving the harder full tensor conjecture. These are substantive changes in what the system can establish. They are not evidence of a revolutionary discovery, historically new mathematics, measured research acceleration, or recursive growth.

Preserve the restored architecture. The current evidence does not justify taking scientific question selection away from the Pros, turning Dots into a checker, or occupying Max with repeated intake. Change the evaluator's investment: stop treating a novel instruction, a fresh context, or a successful archive transfer as a capability improvement. Develop reusable scientific operations whose assumptions can be checked, and measure whether an independent owner obtains more consequential results at lower total effort because of them.

This round executed two prospective, source-matched comparisons on live questions. A short explicit instruction to challenge unnecessary proof prerequisites was compared with competent unrestricted research. The sparse-operator comparison produced two independently reviewed impossibility proofs. The optional-prefix comparison also produced two independently reviewed impossibility proofs. Both ordinary controls and both explicitly instructed arms solved their respective primary endpoints. There is no basis for declaring the extra instruction superior. Useful new science and an unsuccessful attempt to distinguish research strategies can coexist.

3. THE EXECUTED META-RESEARCH EXPERIMENT

Hypothesis: a brief instruction to question whether the selected technical bottleneck is actually necessary can improve accepted scientific progress, with no relaxation of the endpoint and no additional scientific information.

Design: two live owner obligations, each with a fresh ordinary-research arm and a fresh explicit-dependency-bypass arm. Role assignment was randomized before dispatch. Each arm received the identical complete owner source, contract, relevant elementary identities and freedom to choose any valid method. The ordinary arm was not forced to prove a harder lemma, denied failure records, or limited to a weak baseline. Both had a 720-second elapsed allowance, a 30-tool-call ceiling, no subagents and the same stated numerical resource limits. Model settings were inherited without overrides. Frozen protocols precede outputs. Separate reviewers saw anonymized proofs and the scientific contract, not strategy labels or participant costs. Root independently checked decision-changing steps.

These are two small prospective comparisons, not a population estimate. Launches were staggered. Reasoning compute, token costs, prices and energy are not exposed. Scientific problem preparation, source audit, independent review and integration are additional costs. Root has not converted checker time into total research time. Reported participant finalization windows were 654 and 744 seconds for sparse recovery, and 590 and 570 seconds for optional prefixes. Their sum is 2558 seconds (42 minutes 38 seconds) of participant elapsed windows, which overlap and do not measure project latency or inference compute. The two blinded reviews add 480.820653 reported seconds; root work, earlier audits and receiving integration are additional. One sparse-operator participant reported 744 seconds including a 24-second administrative overrun; its claim of earlier mathematical completion was not independently frozen at that earlier instant. That deviation remains in the evidence.

3.1 Sparse recovery: both strategies close the same live endpoint.

The question was whether three arbitrary dense real query directions, with total row norm at most one, permit graph-uniform operator-error stability for every symmetric matrix supported on a treewidth-two graph, including diagonals. Generic exact identification was already known. Six-query star coloring was the strong stable comparator; a coloring lower bound was not an all-design obstruction.

Both proofs are accepted internally. One constructs a squared-path family with maximum degree four and forces the inverse sensitivity to grow at least as n^(1/4)/sqrt(24 sqrt(5)). Four localized adjoint rotation fields and the exact adjoint-kernel dimension force a small positive singular value; the operator/Frobenius norm loss is paid. The other reuses Pro2's transverse path-flow representation, projects away a fan's apex direction, cancels two apex constraints and lifts the path witness without breaking support. It forces growth at least m^(1/4)/sqrt(3 pi) and also supplies a four-query stable decoder on that fan family.

The ordinary arm produced the fan proof; the explicit-instruction arm produced the bounded-degree proof. These scopes are complementary. Both settle the primary three-query question. Neither determines the general four/five/six-query boundary or the optimal divergence exponent. Root's relocated producer checks and the reviewer's exact rational checks passed; those checks supplement analytic review and do not infer a universal theorem from finite data. Historical novelty and Pro2's receiving acceptance remain open.

The control's explicit reuse of the inherited path representation is an additional receiving operation on a new graph family. It is not a counterfactual demonstration that the inherited representation saved effort. No live-owner acceptance of these newest two proofs is invented.

3.2 Optional prefix: the access model is part of the theorem.

The second question specialized Pro3's broader goal to a public labelled proposal q and symmetric proposal Q, a known positive KL bound, unknown target normalizer, target evaluations only at counted iid candidates during the global prefix, a bounded fallback and a fixed subsequent MH local/global kernel. Candidate identities, repeats, known q-values and private randomization were all admitted. The target was a fixed fractional reduction of expected candidate count uniformly over the easy pi=q family, while preserving uniform subsequent safety at the sharp exponent.

The frozen outputs use positive finite paired spaces with a hidden target assignment. On a carefully identified event, the entire observed stopped transcript matches the easy null, including labels and raw weights. A finite-population average and only then Jensen control the stopped probability; no fixed-prefix formula substitutes E[T] for N. A subsequent ordinary local transition captures that mass into a slowly escaping trap. Both proofs are internally accepted without repair. They force liminf sup E_null[T]/n_safe>=1, ruling out every fixed uniform saving fraction rho<1. The ordinary arm covers any finite deterministic common subsequent-time schedule; the treatment also proves E_null[T]>=n_safe-O(1) on an explicit public null sequence at the prescribed exponent. These additional scopes were not ranked in advance and do not create a post-hoc winner. Full proofs, review and exact limits are preserved in experiments/prefix/.

This restricted access model must not be promoted to a universal impossibility for adaptive sampling. Full target-code inspection, off-proposal target evaluations, local probes, a fixed small known support or additional structural assumptions change the scientific question. Neither trial is an empirical comparison of practical samplers.

3.3 What the comparisons do and do not establish.

They produced new substantive mathematical contributions relative to the supplied checkpoints. They do not establish a scientific-success advantage for the extra prompt instruction. Both endpoints saturated despite being genuinely open in the owners' latest source. They were newly selected scientific frontiers, not evidence that the owners had spent long periods unsuccessfully trying these exact obligations. An unresolved checkpoint is not a calibrated measure of task difficulty. Do not manufacture discrimination afterward by hiding information from controls, weakening a comparator, choosing toy problems, or scoring the preferred arm for verbosity.

A useful process option can fail to beat ordinary research. The intervention is inexpensive enough for an owner to use when a particular dependency looks suspect, but these trials do not justify a mandatory new prompt stage, wholesale fresh resets, more helpers by default, or reduced owner autonomy. Ordinary capable research already questions premises.

4. STRATEGIC DIAGNOSIS AND COMPETING EXPLANATIONS

The strongest positive finding is the emergence of usable scientific representations: unnormalized transcript operators, exact moment/completion descriptions, transverse flow identities, near-harmonicity estimates, and bounded tests before asymptotic limits. These let a recipient reason about a new object without rebuilding every underlying argument. This is a plausible mechanism of scientific accumulation, and receiving uses are now documented.

The central unresolved weakness is whether that accumulation increases the reach and significance of research at comparable total resources. The available records show additional results and several corrected conclusions, but not that successive discoveries become larger, cheaper, more general or historically important. They also show a recurring gap between a tractable formal endpoint and a useful operational capability: exact rank versus numerical stability, an unconstrained completion versus the original sparse model, conservation versus a closed physical cycle, and a strong asymptotic theorem versus the fastest established practical method.

It would be wrong to diagnose all exclusions as failure or all foundational work as avoidance. Pro1's coherent construction, Pro2's forest decoder and Ultra's matching reservoirs are constructive counterexamples. Negative theorems can expose a real information or stability limit. It would also be wrong to diagnose agents as unable to correct themselves: the record contains row-cap, stopped-event, coherent-amplitude, rejected-root and signed-Newton corrections. The remaining question is whether the chosen endpoints bear enough scientific weight, not whether the work looks sophisticated.

There is insufficient evidence that longer contexts, extra branches, fresh instances or increased coordination alone explain these outcomes. Warm Pro1 work closed a question after an earlier within-turn continuation note still listed it as open. Fresh recipients solved two later obligations with complete source. Neither observation isolates the effect of context. Subagent activity counts also overcount people: Ultra's twenty activity events corresponded to six distinct visible agent threads, and seven Pro1 review documents did not mean seven reviewers.

Some limitations are removable now: stale frontiers, missing exact source, conflated oracle assumptions, omitted error/reset costs and premature declarations that a mission ended. Other limits require new scientific evidence: applicable material measurements, external validation of historical novelty, or a successful algorithm on a consequential realistic workload. A source archive cannot substitute for those observations.

5. STRONGEST AMPLIFICATION MECHANISM TO DEVELOP

The most credible next mechanism is to turn useful discoveries into operations that another scientist can actually apply, with their admissibility conditions and failure witnesses attached. This is more than summarizing a result. For a mathematical operation, preserve the transformation, the hypotheses it checks, and the conclusion/error guarantee. For a computational operation, preserve an executable transformation, resource behavior and a falsifiable validation boundary. For a physical model, preserve which observations support its constitutive laws; do not compile assumptions into apparent facts.

Capability: reduce the repeated derivation and error-correction work needed to attack a new scientific object. Bottleneck: useful methods are still entangled with project-specific notation, implicit access assumptions and an owner's context. Construction: start with one already successful receiving use, such as the path-flow projection/lift, and expose exactly the transformation and conditions needed by a new application. Do not build a general platform first.

Transfer test: use an actually consequential unresolved obligation already chosen by an independent scientific owner, requiring a substantive change of object or assumptions. Do not ask the owner to stop its real programme for the experiment. Give competent fresh recipients the same full scientific corpus. Compare ordinary retrieval and derivation with the proposed reusable operation and its explicit conditions. The ordinary control must retain access to every scientific fact; the treatment changes representation or executable support, not information. Charge construction, integration, rejected applications and review. Include an inadmissible near-neighbour where the operation must refuse a premise rather than manufacture a result. The completed obligations in this packet must not be rerun as if new. A particularly discriminating first comparison is one uninterrupted 24-minute shadow investigator versus a 12-minute investigator followed by a fresh 12-minute receiver. Both get the same full original source; the staged arm must create and read its new operation/handoff inside its allowance. Freeze existing proof files at prespecified cumulative-time points and judge them blind. This tests the allocation bundle at equal investigator-time allowance, not equal inference compute or a pure effect of freshness. It directly addresses the additional-labour explanation. Three owner-recorded milestones would provide local replication; no new owner problems or permanent workflow are prescribed. The larger operation-compilation claim requires additional comparisons against equally informed ordinary retrieval, with development costs counted.

Verification: require a complete accepted theorem or a matched computational improvement on the real target, plus evidence of receiving use. No archive-count or helper-utilization target. A blind assessor judges correctness and significance before seeing process labels. Difficulty must be judged by the actual unresolved scientific obligation and strongest comparison, while accepting that pilot tasks can still saturate. If both arms succeed, compare only prespecified costs or stronger endpoints with defensible measurements; do not invent a post-hoc win.

Compounding: after one successful use, ask whether the recipient's new scientific result expands the reusable operation so that a third investigation can address a previously inaccessible object. A second historical generation in a narrative is not enough; its additional capability and cost must be shown. Astra's present chain of proof reuse is a candidate for this test, not a demonstration of exponential research growth.

Failure conditions: no lower all-in effort, only nearby cosmetic variants, repeated manual repairs by the originator, invalid hypotheses on changed information models, or no further beneficiary. Any of these would weaken or invalidate the amplification mechanism. A negative result should retire that attempted operation, not the scientist's broader mission.

6. ALTERNATIVES AND ALLOCATION

A mass reset into fresh branches is unsupported. Preserve warm owners; use an isolated fresh contributor for a concrete unresolved construction or high-value check. More parallelism is justified only by genuinely different scientific operations. Several contributors proving the same easy lemma do not establish increased intelligence.

A compulsory adversarial audit after every result is also unsupported. Existing reviews caught consequential problems, but repeating settled tests spends resources without new evidence. Review a new load-bearing interface, unexplained numerical discrepancy or changed assumption. Root's latest Dots ledger replay was already done, so it was not repeated here.

Automated scientific search is a serious alternative when a valid evaluator exists. AlphaEvolve's primary report explicitly ties its operation to evaluable code-based objectives and describes limits of that coverage. Finite counterexamples can guide theorem search, but finite checks cannot certify a universal result. Dots' conservation checker cannot score physical applicability. An evaluator must test the scientific property that matters before an automated loop is authorized to optimize it.

Context engineering is another tool, not an established cure. ACE offers evidence on specific agent benchmarks for preserving useful evolving detail, with dependence on the quality of extracted lessons. Our own corrected-assumption records support retaining detail where it changes a proof. They do not show that maximal context length or a universal checklist is best. FIRE-Bench and primary work on agent research failures provide useful external cautions about verifiable tasks, run variability and artifact-level inspection; they do not replace Astra's own evidence.

The six continuation prompts preserve standing responsibilities and contain exact native source identities. Ultra retains its capital-based programme and may choose a stronger consequence. Max integrates actual new proof deltas within existing permissions. Pro1 keeps its fixed-parameter capacity frontier. Pro2 receives the newly reviewed q3 obstructions before choosing its next question. Pro3 receives the exact scope of the optional-prefix result before choosing its next information model or constructive question. Dots retains physical-admissibility ownership; no automatic long run or material-rescue assumption is imposed. All may preserve newer local progress and revise scientific questions on their own grounds.

The evaluator should next test whether a reusable scientific operation changes an independent investigation's achievable result or total effort. That is a more demanding claim than showing that four fresh contributors can write good proofs. The architecture should continue; the standard for claiming intelligence amplification should become stricter.
