PRIMARY RESEARCH CHECKS FOR THIS META-RESEARCH ROUND
Recorded 22 October 2026 Brisbane. External results are comparators, not Astra evidence.

1. AlphaEvolve, Novikov et al., arXiv:2506.13131v1.
https://arxiv.org/html/2506.13131v1
Personally read task specification, abstraction choice, evaluation, deployment/held-out tests and discussion. Relevant design principle: code-generating scientific search can be productive when an evaluator directly tests the object of interest. Authors explicitly limit coverage to evaluable problems and describe current infrastructure feedback gains as moderate, with next-version loops taking months. This supports considering scientific search operations with exact evaluators; it does not support copying a large platform into Astra or asserting exponential progress. Critical mismatch with Astra's current open theorems: finite matrix tests are proposal search, not universal proof; Dots' conservation checks do not evaluate physical applicability. No numerical performance claim from this source is transferred to Astra.

2. Agentic Context Engineering, Zhang et al., arXiv:2510.04618v1.
https://arxiv.org/html/2510.04618v1
Personally read stated adaptation procedure and limitations. It supplies an external example of preserving detailed, incrementally revised procedural context. Its evaluation concerns particular agent/domain benchmarks, not foundational discovery. Its limitations explicitly depend on the reflector's ability to extract useful information and on tasks actually benefiting from detailed context. Astra should preserve corrected assumptions and working mechanisms, but this source does not establish that longer contexts or generic playbooks will improve our scientific outcomes. The present experiment keeps scientific content equal rather than crediting a strategy for receiving more facts.

3. FIRE-Bench, Wang et al., ICML 2026, PMLR306.
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/wang26d.html
Read primary abstract and task definition. Rediscovery of published, verifiable findings is a useful evaluation mode; reported run variance cautions against an organizational law from one pair. A reconstruction exercise would be a calibration task here, not a new scientific discovery. We selected live owner obligations to avoid repeating the already saturated feedback/moment tests. This decision trades easier ground truth for harder mathematical adjudication; both limitations are explicit.

4. How Do Agents Fail on AutoResearch, Fei et al., arXiv:2608.14905v1.
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.14905v1
Read primary task/evidence and limitations. Its artifact-level failure cases motivate inspecting actual code and claims rather than verbal self-criticism alone. The study's model/harness sample and budgets cannot identify a universal metacognitive cause; it explicitly does not experimentally establish that orchestration can repair the failures. Astra has counterexamples to a simple 'agents cannot self-correct' diagnosis: accepted row-cap, bounded-event, coherent-amplitude and signed-Newton corrections. We therefore do not adopt a general diagnosis from that preprint.

5. Kurennoy, Yarullin, Reid, arXiv:2610.03480v1, Theorem6 and Appendix B.5.
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03480v1
Read primary theorem, proof, and known-q/support realization. Its finite-pool remaining-error obstruction does not directly settle certified-KL, optional-prefix stopping before an arbitrary fixed reversible local/global mixture. It also explicitly distinguishes weight-only information from known support/identities. Any second live trial must retain K and define that access; a fixed-N proof inserted at random N is not sufficient. Full source is accessible to both trial arms.

No prior-art search here establishes historical novelty of the Astra theorems or of the proposed research tactic. The strategic diagnosis rests chiefly on supplied original scientific source and the new controlled attempts, not on these papers' aggregate scores.

6. Established sparse-recovery comparators, bounded historical-scope check.
Albertson et al. (2004), primary paper, Theorem 6.1:
https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/viewFile/v11i1r26/pdf/
Read the treewidth-dependent star-coloring bound, which gives six colors at treewidth two. A sharp coloring count does not itself rule out arbitrary dense query directions. Gebremedhin et al.'s primary sparse-Hessian paper distinguishes star-coloring direct recovery from acyclic-coloring indirect recovery:
https://www.cs.purdue.edu/homes/apothen/Papers/hessian-coloring-joc.pdf
Halikias and Townsend's primary matrix-recovery paper treats exact query complexity as its central model, with separate approximation questions:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.09841
These are relevant established comparisons. The bounded search did not establish historical priority of either new TW2 argument; no absence-of-prior-art claim follows. The final scientific verdict is internal correctness under the stated row cap and operator-error model.
