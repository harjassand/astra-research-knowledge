# Independent finite-inventory transient receipt

## Decision
The inspected implementation is a coherent **conditional conservative finite-volume thermal/chemical network**, not a demonstrated periodic regenerative process. The actual saved t=2 and t=4 states fail full reset. No new trajectory was run. This assessment neither certifies discretization accuracy nor rejects the architecture's possible long-time performance.

## Independent observations
`check_saved.py` independently reconstructs masses and gas-plus-solid storage from checkpoint arrays without importing the producer RHS. It uses the existing thermochemical helper only for header-energy and pressure/clamp diagnostics. Sources and observed immutable state files are SHA-256 pinned in `results.json`.

At t=4, independently reconstructed total-energy residual is 6.248e-12 MJ/m² and total-mass residual is -2.053e-11 kg/m². Header stored energy agrees with its imposed isothermal species-energy expression within 1.75e-10 J/m³. These tiny residuals test consistency, not physical accuracy: many shared errors could preserve a global ledger.

Matching phase t=2→4 changes maximum grain temperature by 2.363873 K, pointwise species density by 2.943852e-4 kg/m³, and pointwise gas internal-energy density by 118.6793 J/m³. Aggregate species-inventory change is only 1.321919e-6 kg/m², illustrating why aggregate inventories alone are not reset evidence. Solid storage decreases 0.09340525 MJ/m² while positive heater input is 1.70600167 MJ/m² and export is 1.68072906 mol NOx/m². Storage drawdown is about 5.475% of heater input. The ratio 1.01503670 MJ/mol is therefore a transient thermal ratio, not periodic electric or process work. Simply adding storage depletion to heater work is also not a validated reset protocol.

Hostile omissions discriminate accounting defects: ignoring solid storage leaves a 0.18787785 MJ/m² residual at t=4; ignoring radiation leaves -0.15847691 MJ/m². Both are far larger than roundoff.

Completed raw-transport blows 4 and 5 were read once, totaling 1814 saved states. There were no observed energy-clamped accepted states or subambient connected-outlet states; pressure was at least 101325.602 Pa. Minimum species densities were -3.46117e-9 and -2.29968e-10 kg/m³, respectively. Negative traces are therefore not literally positivity-certified. Header constraint residual stayed below 5.83e-10 J/m³. This read-only calculation cost 1.978 s and peak Linux RSS 27,784 KiB; CPU and host overhead unmeasured.

## Actual equation review
- Conservative state is species mass per gas volume and total formation-plus-sensible gas internal energy per gas volume. Species chemistry is per gas volume. Gas-solid film heat is per bulk volume: dividing by porosity in gas equations and multiplying gas storage by void volume cancels the solid exchange exactly. No extraneous reaction heat is added.
- Shared signed face mass/species/enthalpy fluxes cancel between adjacent cells. Negative flow selects the actual opposite donor. During the valve overlap, independent feed and atmospheric-port components each select their own donor before summation, which is preferable to assigning a donor from only their net flow. Stored fields are not swapped or reset at reversal.
- At the isothermal finite-volume header, dU/dt equals sum of species internal energies at 2700 K times their density derivatives; heater power equals header storage rate minus net enthalpy inflow. This includes chemical formation-energy changes and unequal port flows. Positive heater power and required heat removal are accumulated separately; removal is not credited as recovered electricity.
- Internal solid fluxes and both external radiating faces have consistent signs. The central solid ends are insulated. Constant bulk solid capacity and the specified radiative coefficient remain model assumptions, not material qualification.

## Raw versus clipped transport
The only physical RHS edit in the inspected diff is using raw q/sum(q) for donor species transport instead of normalized clipped helper Y. Nonnegative states are equivalent apart from floating arithmetic. On negative numerical trace states, clipping makes outgoing trace transport zero and can trap a negative concentration, whereas raw donor transport retains its linear restoring derivative. The producer's saved diagnostic reports a -6.8719e-10 kg/m³ trace with clipped derivative ~3e-100 versus raw derivative +4.50505e-6 kg/m³/s and positive-state RHS difference 1.33e-13. I inspected this diagnostic and the source diff; I did not rerun its RHS comparison. Raw transport is a defensible off-physical numerical extension, not evidence that negative traces are physical. Clipped thermo/kinetics plus raw transport is not a rigorous positivity-preserving or exact thermodynamic extension outside the positive cone. Its impact still needs numerical error control before final predictive claims.

## Concrete remaining defects/limits
1. `previous` resets on process restart; the producer's two-blow segments cannot compare matching phase across segment boundaries. Saved states enable the independent t2/t4 check above. Within one longer invocation, only total species inventories and solid pointwise temperature are compared. Full gas species and internal-energy fields, header inventory, and valve phase must also reset. No numerical reset tolerance or automatic stationarity criterion is implemented.
2. `feed_mass` derivative is fixed at 1 kg/m²/s. If the nominal atmospheric outlet admits air during reversal, additional ingress is included in conservative fluxes but omitted from this feed counter. Correct total inlet rate would add alpha*max(gl,0)+(1-alpha)*max(-gr,0). This is a real generic counter defect, **not an observed error in the first two raw blows**, which remain above ambient pressure.
3. Pump ledger is a favorable reversible isothermal compression estimate to the inlet cell pressure. It omits any feed-port/half-cell pressure drop, efficiency, valve dynamics and full heater/capture/reset electrical costs. It is not total pumping work. Quasistatic Ergun resistance also has no momentum/kinetic-energy storage or acoustic dynamics. Producer accepted-state Mach maxima ~0.01856 for the reviewed two blows support a low-Mach approximation, not a time-dependent momentum validation. Those Mach values are producer-reported, not independently reconstructed here.
4. Endpoint negative-species and energy-clamp stopping tests do not enforce the same gates on all intermediate accepted states; the sampled extrema are diagnostics only. This did not conceal an energy clamp in the independently read blows. Likewise grain/pressure/Mach limits are reported rather than admission thresholds. WALL_SECONDS is checked only between blows and is not a hard process wall cap.
5. There are no separate integrated N/O boundary-element ledgers in the nine counters. The helper's source elemental conservation and common face cancellation support the algebra, but the stored total-mass ledger cannot independently establish both element balances. No claim of an independently closed integrated N/O ledger is made.
6. Initial bed heat inventory is roughly 805.7 MJ/m² in the adopted arbitrary constant-capacity reference. Absolute thermal startup accounting needs an actual ambient reference/material heat capacity and electricity model. Source labeling already excludes startup/capture; this receipt retains that boundary.
