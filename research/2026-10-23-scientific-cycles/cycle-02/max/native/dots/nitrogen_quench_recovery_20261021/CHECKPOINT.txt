Why this question matters
Electrical air-to-NOx could supply distributed nitrogen fertilizer without making
hydrogen first. Useful output is retained/captured fixed nitrogen, not excitation,
peak concentration, or plasma current. Our question is whether recovering heat
can coexist with the rapid chemical quench needed to retain that nitrogen.
The ordinary comparator is optimized thermal reaction/quench/heat integration,
not an inefficient torch or a weak constant-input reference. Heat recovery and
staged quenching are established approaches, not claimed inventions here.

What was actually established
1. A source-bound ground-state chemistry/enthalpy model reproduces a meaningful
quench-rate tradeoff. At3100K hot equilibrium, quench to2100K followed by slow
1000K/s cooling retains only~82.5% NOx. At10000K/s it retains~95.3%.
A scalar recovery assumption conceals this product loss.
2. A genuinely coupled finite-film counterflow model exposes radical-recombination
reheating. Cooling the inlet to1800K does NOT guarantee it stays below1800K.
Increasing the accepted exchanger length from20mm to100mm increases heat recovery
9.25%, loses6.50% product, and improves modeled MJ/mol by only2.64%; peak gas
rises2139→2395K. This is a model result, not an experimental discovery.
3. The favorable2400K/20mm case independently reproduces1.130726328MJ/mol
retained NO+NO2 at300K. Independent conserved-enthalpy Radau integration matches
our temperature-state BDF model within1.6e-8MJ/mol. Final654→300K cooling was
explicitly calculated and rejects367.001kJ/kg without heat-recovery credit.
4. That favorable case requires a passive wall somewhere at least2653K. No
continuous gas-tight exchanger wall qualified for this service was established.
A finite-front-quench lower-temperature case gives1.435MJ/mol gas-heating duty,
but still needs wall≥1937K and electric-efficiency×capture≥0.9566 merely to reach
1.5MJ/mol before auxiliaries. It has no demonstrated useful margin.
5. We did NOT infer a universal material prohibition. Actual SiC wall service
and product ratings are cooler; zirconia pebble beds have reached~2500–2533K,
but are not proof of a thin-wall exchanger or the proposed nitrogen process.
6. Alumina is not automatically inert to NO, but the measured old AVCO geometric
surface flux, mapped only as a sensitivity, is NOT fatal: it predicts~0.36% loss
for one lower case. Different gas, NO level, temperature, surface and geometry
prevent treating this as a validated dry-air rate. Surface-loss uncertainty is
quantified, not assigned a favorable or fatal value.
