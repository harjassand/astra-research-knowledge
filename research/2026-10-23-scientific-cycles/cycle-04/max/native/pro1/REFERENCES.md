# Targeted primary comparisons

The owner and the comparison contributor read the relevant original public sources. This is a bounded mechanism and scope comparison, not a generic novelty audit.

1. **David Bücher, Invariant states of quantum birth and death chains**, arXiv:1408.6162, 2014. Original: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.6162. Proposition 6 gives an operator Foster–Lyapunov invariant-state criterion with compact spectral sublevels. Proposition 9 uses coherence-corrected number observables and controlled bounded truncations to exclude normal invariant states. This supports the method boundary; its scalar half-line coefficients do not classify the two-color branching tree.

2. **David Bücher, Andreas Gärtner, Burkhard Kümmerer, Walter Reußwig, Kay Schwieger and Nadiem Sissouno, Ergodic Properties of Quantum Birth and Death Chains**, arXiv:1306.3776, 2013. Original: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1306.3776. Proposition 5.1 and Theorem 5.5 classify invariant states in homogeneous one-shift models, including coherent two-level inputs. These are meaningful precedents for coherent reservoir questions, but their bath multiplicity, incoming dimension and boundary differ from the present model.

3. **Christopher Cedzich, Alain Joye, Albert H. Werner and Reinhard F. Werner, Exponential Tail Estimates for Quantum Lattice Dynamics**, arXiv:2408.02108, 2024. Original: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.02108. Lemmas 3.1–3.2 and Theorem 2.1 make exponential position conjugation rigorous and derive tails for translation-invariant unitary lattice dynamics with finite site multiplicity. The present rooted CP channel requires its own strict drift, bounded tilted dilation and uniform Taylor remainder. No new generic principle of exponential tilting is claimed.

4. **Raffaella Carbone and Yan Pautrat, Homogeneous Open Quantum Random Walks on a Lattice**, arXiv:1408.1113, original submission 2014. Original: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.1113. The paper uses deformed positive maps for position large deviations. Equations (2.1) and (2.4) erase position off-diagonal terms after one step. That structure does not apply to the spatial coherences retained by the present bath. Treating coherent move/stay alternatives as classical probabilities would change the mechanism.
