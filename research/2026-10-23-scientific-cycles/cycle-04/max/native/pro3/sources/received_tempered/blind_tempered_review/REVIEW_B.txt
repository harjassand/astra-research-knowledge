1. NORM DEFINITIONS, MATRIX ORIENTATION, AND MOMENT BOUND

These are correct. Let theta=min(eta,1/2), p=1/(1-theta), r=1/theta.
Then 1/p+1/r=1, theta p=p-1, and r>=2. All norms in the conjugation argument
are explicitly counting-measure norms; the final density norm is correctly
converted to the probability-weighted Lp(q) norm.

For every a>0 and eta>=theta>0, min(1,a^eta)<=a^theta. With
D=diag(q^theta), P=D C_eta D^{-1}, u=q^theta, and v=q^(1-theta), the
off-diagonal comparisons yield

  D L_eta D^{-1} <= I+Q,
  D G D^{-1} <= I+u v^T,
  0 <= P <= I + (alpha u v^T + beta Q)/2.

Diagonal entries are covered because every Markov diagonal is at most one.
The total coefficient of I in the mixture is exactly one. Thus the majorant
has the correct orientation and no omitted self-loop term.

For counting-measure column vectors f, Jensen and the column sums of Q give

  sum_x |sum_y Q(x,y)f(y)|^r
  <= sum_{x,y} Q(x,y)|f(y)|^r = sum_y |f(y)|^r.

Hence ||Q||_{r->r}<=1. Also ||u||_r=||v||_p=1, so the rank-one matrix has
norm ||u v^T||_{r->r}=1. Positivity permits entrywise majorization of the
operator norm: |P f|<=P|f|<=majorant |f|. The triangle inequality therefore
gives ||P||_{r->r}<=1+(alpha+beta)/2=3/2.

The ROW vector identity is

  mu_j D^{-1} = q D^{-1} P^j = v^T P^j.

Its ell^p norm is bounded by (3/2)^j by duality, not by incorrectly treating
P as an ell^p column contraction. Explicitly, test v^T P^j against every
ell^r column f of norm one and use Holder. Finally,

  ||mu_j D^{-1}||_p^p
  = sum_x mu_j(x)^p q(x)^(-theta p)
  = sum_x q(x)(mu_j(x)/q(x))^p.

This establishes (1) and its setwise consequence (2) independently of state
count or minimum q. The optional full-strength moment exponent
p=1/(1-eta) for 0<eta<1 is also valid: the operator proof needs only r>1.
The cap theta<=1/2 is needed later for the simple bounded-test interpolation,
not for that stronger moment statement itself.

  D_bin(a||b_h) = -H_2(a)-a log b_h-(1-a)log(1-b_h)

Here H_2 is increasing on [0,1/4], and the dropped -a log b_h is nonnegative.

  ||d nu_j/d pi||_Lp(pi)^p

Taking the p-th root gives h^(-theta)c^j exactly. There is no normalization
of nu_j and no invalid substitution of a conditional distribution.

5. LOWER BOUND AND DETERMINISTIC-TIME INVERSION

The identity proposal is admissible and makes L_eta=I for every eta. In the
two-state family, the off-diagonal IMH probabilities sum to u_n. Thus the
hybrid nontrivial eigenvalue is 1-alpha u_n/2, and the initial deficit is
p_n(1-u_n). The stated exact TV expression follows. KL is strictly below K
for all sufficiently large n, with strictly positive probabilities. Since
n u_n->0 and p_n log n->K, the lower coefficient is K.

The use of a different admitted instance at each n is allowed. Each instance
keeps its kernel fixed. TV contraction under that fixed invariant kernel
makes its distance nonincreasing. The strict lower bound on R at the chosen
integer time guarantees an instance above epsilon even if the supremum is
not attained; the uniform upper bound controls every instance. The stated
exponential-time sandwich and limit for T therefore follow correctly.

6. EXTENSION AND ETA=0 ENDPOINT

The all-positive-eta extension follows from the SAME argument, since

The symmetric local-flow identity is algebraically valid for all positive

For the endpoint family, the A contribution to KL is 2/log n->0; the B

log(1/(1-b))<K. All finite members are strictly positive eventually. W(A)=n^2
eventually exceeds the other two weights for each fixed allowed b, so the

bounds legitimately yields beta(1-exp(-K))/2; it does not require swapping
an unproved uniform-in-b convergence with the n limit. Thus the stronger
displayed endpoint floor passes as well.
