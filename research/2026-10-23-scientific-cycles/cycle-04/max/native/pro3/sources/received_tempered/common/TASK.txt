EXACT TASK
For fixed K>0, alpha in(0,1) and eta in(0,1), consider finite labelled spaces with strictly positive pi,q, D(pi||q)<=K and fixed symmetric stochastic local proposal Q. Space size and probabilities vary across the supremum; all parameters stay fixed within each admitted instance. G is independence Metropolis-Hastings from q. The local kernel L_eta uses Q with acceptance
min(1,(q(y)/q(x))^eta)*min(1,(pi(y)/pi(x))*(q(x)/q(y))^eta),
and C_eta=(I+alpha G+(1-alpha)L_eta)/2 starts from q. The target normalizer is unknown, and multiplying all raw target values by the same constant changes no transition.

Let R_eta(n)=sup TV(q C_eta^n,pi). Let T_eta(epsilon) be the supremum of the first deterministic time at which that distance is at most epsilon.

Establish or refute that R_eta(n)->0 for EVERY fixed eta>0. Then determine the strongest interval of eta for which you can prove the sharp coefficient lim(log n)R_eta(n)=K and the corresponding lim epsilon log T_eta(epsilon)=K. A stronger result or a valid counterconstruction is welcome. If the full task remains open, state exactly what is proved and where the argument stops.

The theorem must be uniform over the stated class. Finite examples alone do not establish it. The existing pure-IMH comparator already has coefficient K, so this is a robustness question, not a speedup claim. Do not substitute failure of a sufficient condition for a lower theorem. The completed proof or counterconstruction, its exact hypotheses and any unresolved gap are the deliverable. All methods and tools permitted by the experimental arm remain available; no particular proof method is required by the common task.
