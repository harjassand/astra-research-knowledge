4. FROM PERMUTATIONS TO THE COMMON-DS CONGRUENCE CONE

If B is doubly stochastic, write its finite Birkhoff decomposition

5. WHAT THE COUNTEREXAMPLE DOES AND DOES NOT SHOW

This is a real physical failure of a particular sufficient common-Gram cone
with kappa=0, and a quantitative positive lower bound on its required defect.
It is not an entropy-witness violation: a PSD dual need not be rank one on a
nonnegative eigenvalue vector, and common-cone membership is only sufficient.
It does not prove that no uniform useful kappa=O(r) exists, and gives no family
with kappa/r unbounded. No iid entanglement-cost converse is changed by this
finite obstruction. The unrestricted critical witness and logarithmic generic
formation-cost boundary therefore remain open. Even a tensor-product repetition
of this basis cannot be claimed to add the separation: a global permutation of
the larger computational reference may correlate the copies. No such shortcut
is used.
