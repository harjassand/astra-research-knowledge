INVARIANT MASAS: CUT DUALITY AND TWO-COORDINATE ENTROPY TRANSPORT
22 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane | bounded complete arguments
Internal correctness candidate; historical novelty not established.

1. Scope and notation

There are n=rN physical D-sites, D>=2 integer. A=span{Pi_j} is a MASA
with rank-one Pi_j=|e_j><e_j|, j=1,...,D^n. For S a physical subset,
E_S(X)=I_S/D^|S| tensor Tr_S X. In this note an invariant MASA means
E_S(A) subset A for EVERY S. It is enough instead to assume invariance
under the individual site erasures, since their products give all E_S.
On diagonal coefficients K_S is uniform averaging on its erasure cells.
All norms below are ordinary counting Hilbert-space / Hilbert-Schmidt norms.
For a normalized positive diagonal X, its eigenvalue vector f satisfies
f>=0, sum f_j^2=1 and S(X^2)=H(f^2).

The generic critical tensor witness for ALL invariant MASAs remains unproved
here. The result below proves it for every such MASA whose basis is maximally
entangled across one balanced bipartition which is a union of entire tasks.
No common D-coordinate system is assumed. Two separate D^2-coordinate
systems suffice. The proof does not put generic positive matrices or generic
approximate iid dilution outputs into this class.

2. Uniform cut classification

Theorem 1. Fix any nontrivial cut S:S^c, dimensions a=D^|S|, b=D^(n-|S|).
Assume just that E_S and E_(S^c) preserve A. There is one integer k=k_S,
dividing BOTH a and b, and orthogonal decompositions
 H_S=directsum_(u=1)^(a/k) R_u,
 H_(S^c)=directsum_(v=1)^(b/k) Q_v,
with dim R_u=dim Q_v=k, such that in every R_u tensor Q_v the e-basis
is an arbitrary complete maximally entangled basis of k^2 vectors.
Consequently EVERY basis vector has the same nonzero cut Schmidt spectrum,
exactly k entries 1/k; E_S cells all have size a k; E_(S^c) cells all
have size b k. In particular k_S=k_(S^c).

Proof. The inherited conditional-expectation calculation gives, on each
E_S cell C, P_C=sum_(j in C)Pi_j=I_S tensor Q_C for a projection Q_C,
and Tr_S Pi_j=Q_C/rank(Q_C). The E_S cells therefore correspond to an
orthogonal decomposition {Q_v} of H_(S^c). Similarly the E_(S^c) cells
give an orthogonal decomposition {R_u} of H_S. The product
R_u tensor Q_v is diagonal in the MASA and has positive rank for EVERY
pair u,v. Its basis vectors have reduced states R_u/dim R_u and
Q_v/dim Q_v. Equality of the two nonzero Schmidt spectra forces
dim R_u=dim Q_v. Because every pair intersects, one common dimension k
works for all u,v and divides a,b. Each block has k^2 orthonormal vectors
maximally entangled on k by k dimensions. The converse is immediate by
summing each complete block basis. QED.

Corollaries. If k_S=1, the entire MASA factors as a product of two fixed
MASAs across this cut; there is no basis-dependent controlled choice of
the complementary local basis. If D is prime, every k_S is a power of D.
For general D it is a divisor of both cut dimensions, not necessarily an
integer power of D. For every invariant MASA all basis vectors have the
same flat entropy function h(S)=ln k_S. This function is symmetric under
S<->S^c and obeys ordinary pure-state entropy inequalities, including
strong subadditivity, by applying them to any one e_j.

Thus the previous isolated rank/cardinality bounds omit a load-bearing
duality and a shared pure-state entropy function. These necessary conditions
are not asserted sufficient for quantum realization.

3. Multiplicative erasure cells force an exact product coordinate system

Lemma 2. Let U be a set of m indices. Suppose commuting conditional
expectations K_S, S subset U, act on a uniform set Omega of size Q^m,
K_S K_T=K_(S union T), K_empty=I, K_U=global averaging. Suppose EVERY
K_S cell has size Q^|S|. Then a bijection Omega<->[Q]^m simultaneously
identifies K_S with averaging exactly the coordinates in S.

Proof. The commuting-square identity gives, whenever cells C_S,C_T lie
in a common union-cell,
 |C_S intersect C_T|=|C_S||C_T|/|C_(S union T)|
                       =Q^|S intersect T|.
For cells containing a fixed omega, C_(S intersect T)(omega) is a subset
of this intersection and has exactly that size. Hence the intersection
equals C_(S intersect T)(omega). For each u let Y_u be the label of the
cell of K_(U minus {u}). It has Q possible values. The joint labels
(Y_u: u in U) separate points, because intersection of all their cells
is C_empty(omega)={omega}. There are Q^m label tuples and Q^m points, so
this injective map is a bijection. C_S is exactly a fiber with coordinates
outside S fixed, by the same repeated-intersection identity and cardinality.
QED. Pairwise independence of arbitrary marginal labels alone would not
prove this lemma: the exact multiplicative cells and all union identities
are used.

4. Balanced maximally entangled cuts: a critical witness without one
   shared D-coordinate representation

Theorem 3. Let r be even. Split the r entire ordered tasks into equally
sized groups L and R. Suppose A is invariant and its basis vectors are
maximally entangled across the balanced L:R cut (equivalently, their
cut Schmidt rank equals D^(rN/2)). Then every normalized positive X in A
obeys

 S(X^2) >= (ln D) sum_(a=1)^r sum_(i=1)^N
              ||E_(task a,prefix i) X||_HS^2 -(r/2) C_(D^2),       (T3)

where C_Q is ANY valid inherited scalar one-chain constant in alphabet Q,
for example
 C_Q=min{2Q-ln Q, ln Q/(1-Q^(-1/2))^2}.
The constant is independent of N and of all intermediate erasure partitions.
It is not an optimization of the old constant C_D.

Proof. Put m=rN/2 and Q=D^2. For every S contained in L, each basis
vector has maximally mixed reduced state on S: tracing the maximally mixed
full L marginal proves this. Its cut Schmidt rank is therefore D^|S|.
Theorem 1 gives uniform E_S cells of size D^(2|S|)=Q^|S|. Full L erasure
has a single cell of size D^(2m), since its Schmidt rank is D^m.
Lemma 2 supplies ONE exact Q-coordinate system indexed by the physical
sites of L, in which every E_S, S subset L, is ordinary S-coordinate
averaging. This statement is about the restriction to the MASA.

Write the normalized eigenvalue function f in those coordinates. Holding
all other L-task coordinates fixed, apply the inherited scalar one-chain
lemma in alphabet Q to the normalized slice and average. For task a in L,
 (ln Q) sum_i ||K_(a,i) f||_2^2
       <= H(Y_a | Y_(other L tasks)) + C_Q.
The sum of these classical conditional entropies is at most H(Y_L)=H(f^2).
Thus
 2 ln D sum_(a in L,i) ||K_(a,i)f||_2^2
       <= H(f^2)+(r/2) C_Q.                                  (L)

Independently repeat the argument on R. It gives another exact Q-coordinate
system, possibly a completely different bijection of the SAME spectral
labels, and
 2 ln D sum_(a in R,i) ||K_(a,i)f||_2^2
       <= H(f^2)+(r/2) C_Q.                                  (R)
Entropy is unchanged by either bijection. Add (L),(R) and divide by two
to obtain (T3). No relation between the two bijections is used. QED.

Theorem 3 is a full theorem for ALL invariant MASAs satisfying its balanced
maximal-entanglement cut premise. It needs no stabilizer, field-linear,
Hadamard/group-GHZ, circuit-depth, or common-D-coordinate classification.
It does not assert that this class strictly contains every earlier class,
nor that every invariant MASA has such a cut. GHZ states on many sites,
for example, generally do not have maximal rank across a large balanced cut.

Normal coefficient / mixed-state support extension. For arbitrary complex
diagonal coefficients c, the actual K_S are entrywise nonnegative, so
c* K_S c <= |c|^T K_S |c|, while entanglement entropy is H(|c|^2).
Hence (T3) holds for every pure coefficient matrix in this diagonal support.
For a density matrix supported on span{|e_j>_A|conjugate(e_j)>_B}, every
ensemble vector remains in that support. Convex-roof averaging therefore
gives its E_F witness with defect (r/2)C_(D^2). This is a finite-state
support theorem, not a generic iid formation characterization.

5. What remains open

All invariant MASAs need not satisfy the balanced maximal-entanglement
premise, and Theorem 1 alone gives no useful tensor entropy allocation for
their intermediate prefixes. The exact cut duality, common flat entropy
function and commuting intersection identities are necessary certificates.
The accompanying classical construction shows that even these CARDINALITY
constraints do not force common coordinates; it is not a quantum basis.
An abstract partition failure is not a physical witness counterexample.

No unrestricted critical witness, cone certificate, or improved generic iid
preparation law follows. A useful remaining operation is to characterize
the joint commuting quantum support projectors beyond their cardinalities,
or to obtain entropy transport using several incompatible coordinate systems
as in Theorem 3. It would be invalid to apply Theorem 3 to an approximate
formation output merely because the target has a convenient eigenbasis.
