PRIMARY COMPARATORS, NOVELTY BOUNDARY AND COST SCOPE
22 October 2026. Focused primary reads; no exhaustive novelty claim.

1. One-shot formation and resource strong converses already exist.
Buscemi--Datta, Entanglement cost in practical scenarios,
https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.3698 ; PRL106,130503 (2011), study finite-error
one-shot dilution and its Schmidt-resource characterization. Takagi--Regula--
Wilde, https://arxiv.org/html/2110.02212v3 , establish resource yield/cost
relations and strong-converse bounds. Theurer--Fang--Gour,
https://arxiv.org/html/2312.17088v1 , give a mixed-state one-shot formulation
with a specified squared-purified-distance error. These sources rule out
presenting operator-order testing or finite-error dilution as new inventions.
We do not identify their error/resource definitions with preselected expected
Bell supply or assume a finite-dimensional characterization extends verbatim
to a normal infinite memory. Our direct proof goes through the entire normal
channel to a bounded finite record and prices every supplied branch.

2. Existing small-matrix tools are not new scientific mechanisms.
Audenaert, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1410.4941 , states a singular-value difference
inequality whose linear specialization is the trace-norm Mirsky bound used
in the q4 proof. Original Mirsky reference DOI10.1093/qmath/11.1.50.
Thompson, Singular values and diagonal elements of complex symmetric matrices,
https://doi.org/10.1016/0024-3795(79)90173-3 , characterizes their possible
diagonals. Its n=4 unitary specialization already gives the polygon condition
on diagonal moduli: the final inequality reduces to d1<=d2+d3+d4. A research
paper displaying the explicit Thompson conditions is
https://faculty.sites.iastate.edu/ytpoon/files/inline-files/23.pdf .
Thus the symmetric-unitary polygon fact is KNOWN machinery; our independent
short proof is not an originality claim. The matching-domination and erasure-
Gram application have UNKNOWN historical novelty. The operative unresolved
boundary is beyond q4, where the q8 physical construction actually separates.

3. Current tensorization work changes the applicable premise boundary.
Dong--Gao--Ou--Yao--Zhou,
https://arxiv.org/html/2606.17729v3 , Definition2.11 requires one common
basis whose off-diagonal matrix units are scaled and whose diagonal algebra
is preserved. Theorems1.2--1.3 give stated PODS tensorization results, exact
in their qubit scope. PODS_OBSTRUCTION.txt proves our strictly weighted
prefix generator has NO such basis for N>=2. A direct invocation is therefore
invalid. This is not a counterexample to that paper or to a different
semigroup comparison with proved constants.
Fang, https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00187 , derives cost results for two-qubit
Bell-diagonal/isotropic states using appropriate tuned semigroups. It does
not assert additivity of a correlated many-output inventory state. No such
transfer is assumed here, and no closed noisy-output programme is reopened.
