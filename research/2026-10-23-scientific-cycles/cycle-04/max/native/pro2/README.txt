  python3 replay.py --output /absolute/path/to/new_receiving_directory

creates a relocated temporary copy, checks all five saved rational witnesses

negative controls are included. Universal real-design quantifiers remain an
analytic proof obligation in PROOF.md; finite checks do not prove them.
