EXECUTIVE ASSESSMENT

A. Where is Astra today?
Astra is producing substantive, increasingly connected mathematics and more
careful physical models. Several independent owners have crossed real internal
frontiers: they have proved stronger universal results, rejected plausible
mechanisms and preserved operations another investigation can use. We have not
established civilization-changing significance, a practical technology advantage,
or a system that improves its own general scientific intelligence recursively.

B. Are we progressing or accumulating disconnected results?
The latest mathematical work is cumulative. Pro3's explicit acceptance and
extension of two prior incoming proofs is particularly strong evidence: the
receiving owner created a new uniform theorem using their different strengths.
That is more than receiving files. It is also compatible with ordinary cumulative
research at an unchanged rate. Stronger theorems after more work do not by
themselves show accelerating discovery. The physical programme remains constrained
by missing constitutive information, not merely unfinished simulations.

C. What capabilities improved?
The system can now reason about stronger quantifiers and larger admissible
classes in three active mathematical programmes. It can construct physical
counterexamples to apparently harmless modeling assumptions. It has demonstrated
actual proof reuse and a limited numerical-helper receiving use. The new pilot
tests a more ambitious executable-adversary mechanism on a current unresolved
obligation. Both independent submissions improved the existing upper theorem;
one also proved a limit on the chosen proof method. Both passed internal
source-bound review. No general strategy advantage was established.

D. Most consequential weakness?
We have no demonstrated mechanism converting these local successes into a
reliably expanding ability to solve consequential new classes of problems.
The immediate scientific bottleneck varies: a missing universal argument in
mathematics, or physically unidentified dynamics in Dots' model. More agents,
more simulation and more explicit thinking instructions cannot be assumed to
remove either. Historical novelty and significance also remain unevenly assessed.

E. Continue, redirect or rebuild?
Continue the restored autonomous architecture. Redirect the evaluator's own
research from universal reasoning prescriptions toward experimentally testing
new scientific operations that create information or prove statements existing
operations cannot. No evidence supports replacing the Pro researchers' questions
with another centrally assigned common project or rebuilding a management layer.

F. Most important next action?
Put the actual new scientific capital into its owner's hands, then investigate
whether such capital can become a reusable discovery operation on a different
unresolved obligation. The criterion is a changed scientific frontier with
accounted preparation and receiving costs. Do not adopt the tested instrument
as a mandatory system-wide workflow on the basis of one pilot.

1. WHAT THE NEW OWNER EVIDENCE CHANGES

1.1 Pro1: a stronger communication obstruction, not a capacity solution

The earlier exact infinite-bath representation enabled a new root-retaining
insertion corrector. A commutator cancellation prevents a near-critical resolvent
norm from destroying the drift estimate. The proof then supplies two bridges
that cannot be skipped: a finite-band exponential tilt converts corrected drift
to escape for every normal initial state; controlled initializer tails and finite
propagation convert entropy growth to a high-probability arbitrary-rank compression
obstruction. Positive drift alone and entropy growth alone were explicitly shown
insufficient for those conclusions.

The resulting optimized iid coherent-information limit is exactly 3/2 for each
fixed beta<=1/8, with maximization inside the limit. That last quantifier matters:
the chosen one-site input may change with the horizon. It closes more than one
numerically selected seed. It does not establish the general communication
capacity, a correlated-block converse, feedback equality or an efficient code.
At the live fixed beta=1/512 the broader capacity bracket remains approximately
[1.5,1.5816427609]. The owner has selected a concrete correlated two-call family.

The corrector, finite-band interface and controlled-tail argument are distinct
reusable pieces. Their several consequences should not be counted as independent
breakthroughs. Very long asymptotic scales and the absence of a finite-device
advantage limit the present operational interpretation. This is worthwhile
foundational work even if no immediate technological benefit follows. [S1]

1.2 Pro2: the hard quantifier is now controlled

For known symmetric supports of treewidth at most t, under the actual total
probe-row bound, free diagonals, absolute Frobenius action noise and operator
recovery error, the source establishes exact query minimum t+1 and stable
dimension-independent minimum (t+1)(t+2)/2. The upper count is connected to
classical star coloring. The substantive lower handles arbitrary dense designs
through support-preserving coning and a covariance alternative. Local rank or a
bad finite palette would not establish this result.

This settles the selected ordinary fan-tree problem at six stable queries. The
broader induction is self-contained from a singleton; it would be wrong to claim
that every part logically depends on the incoming fan proof. The inherited fan
argument supplies the separate quantitative fan-tree rate and useful machinery.

The new owner-selected boundary is four versus five queries for treewidth<=2
and maximum degree<=4. Three are excluded and five suffice under the unchanged
contract. The all-treewidth hard graphs have unbounded degree, so the old proof
cannot be imported by ignoring this restriction. The NIST practical incumbent
remains unbeaten. A stable-query theorem and a useful numerical implementation
are separate achievements. [S2]

1.3 Pro3: actual scientific transfer and a substantive synthesis

Both earlier evaluator proofs are preserved byte for byte and explicitly
accepted without repair. One supplies a fixed-exponent density representation;
the other an affine reset based on the actual law's total mass. Combining the
recurrences is routine algebra. The receiving owner's new step is stronger:
interpolate mass decay and norm growth for each unconditioned first-hit
submeasure, sum uniformly in the screen parameter and smooth each at its own
remaining horizon. This controls a joint limit the fixed-parameter theorems
did not settle.

The owner proves uniform convergence exactly when eta_n log n tends to infinity,
and constructs a growing symmetric path that falsifies the proposed universal
critical multiplier one. An exact integer inequality, not a favorable finite
plot, certifies the counterconstruction. Before the current pilot, the finite-d
accuracy coefficient at K=1, alpha=1/2, d=1 lay between approximately 1.08848
and 3.31290. Its exact value and existence at that point remained unresolved.

This is the clearest current chain of productive receiving use. It does not
identify which earlier prompt caused the useful proof, prove that duplication
was worth its cost in advance, or show that the repository index caused the
discovery. The accepted proof transfers predate Max's index. Pure independence
Metropolis-Hastings already has the favorable K coefficient in the comparison;
these theorems establish robustness boundaries, not a practical sampler speedup.
[S3, S6]

1.4 Ultra: a physical counterexample and carefully scoped extensions

Ultra's actual rational three-qubit Cayley basis defeats the full computational
common-doubly-stochastic domination cone with defect exceeding ln(2)/500. Exact
checks cover all 40,320 reference placements. This upgrades an abstract candidate
into a physically compatible obstruction to the proposed sufficient mechanism.
The q4 case, by contrast, admits zero-defect compatibility for every actual basis.

Cone failure is not entropy-witness failure. Tensoring the example does not
establish additive separation when global permutations can mix the copies. The
finite-copy initialization-error laws distinguish deterministic Bell supply
from preselected expected supply and retain global correlations. Ordinary erased
stock matches their leading coefficients. The balanced-cut invariant-MASA
extension and basis-independent non-PODS obstruction are useful structural
capital, but the generic critical witness and logarithmic formation gap remain
open. The owner's selected next step is invariant cut compatibility beyond the
balanced maximal-cut class. [S4]

1.5 Dots: the supplement changes what further computation can establish

The main Cycle3 source preserves a conditional fully relaxed endpoint export
bound of at least 53.995135 kJ/kg fresh. Molecular-sheet cooling is temporary;
the compressible comparison stopped at 127.515 microseconds, short of its
requested 150. Neither the one-temperature margin nor NO-only radiation admits
a physical complete operation. A budget cutoff is not a physical reset deadline.

The newly supplied follow-on is more consequential for our process diagnosis.
It constructs a thermodynamically consistent four-temperature closure and two
admissible local constitutive models with the same equal-temperature chemical
rates and VT/VV operator but different off-manifold chemistry and energy
production. It does not claim identical full coupled linear response. These are
synthetic countermodels, not measured nitrogen coefficients.

The same composition and total enthalpy admit equilibrium temperature1724.578691 K
and a conditional nonequilibrium bath temperature1687.597770 K with nitrogen
vibrational temperature1959.507539 K. Thus the missing modes are not known merely
to worsen the previous cooling margin. Passing a global entropy screen does not
establish dynamical reachability, a local maximum-temperature bound or a feasible
heat exchanger. Stored vibration still has to be reset.

This is an information obstruction. More accurate trajectories inside an
arbitrarily selected closure cannot determine which closure describes the gas.
The next useful operation must acquire identifying physical evidence, derive a
conclusion robust across admitted models, or find a different admissible design.
The ordinary species-specific five-species model is relevant prior work but does
not automatically qualify the six-species NO2 and transport contract. No physical
nitrogen-fixation benefit is established. The scientific mission remains active.

2. REVISED DIAGNOSIS: WHAT IS, AND IS NOT, LIMITING DISCOVERY

First, our earlier prompt interpretation was too broad if read as a verdict on
representations. Four successes on two earlier obligations showed no advantage
for the extra representation instruction. The control was allowed to reformulate
and had the same curated mathematical capital. The evidence rejects adoption of
that extra ritual on those results; it does not show that representations,
independent attempts, or fresh instances are unhelpful. Nor did it compare warm
continuation against a fresh instance on equal relevant information.

Second, the current mathematics is advancing most where an argument removes a
real restriction: from a fixed input to a horizon-dependent optimum; from selected
probe palettes to all dense designs; from a fixed screen to a joint limit; from
an abstract cone candidate to an actual physical basis. These are expansions in
what the system can prove. They are not merely additional cases. The unproven
causal hypothesis is that making these proof operations reusable can accelerate
the next such expansion.

Third, the scientific objects now expose two distinct bottlenecks. In the
mathematical models the admissible class is explicit but the universal argument
is missing. In Dots' physical model, supplied observations admit incompatible
off-equilibrium dynamics. A formally perfect solver of one arbitrary closure
does not solve that identification problem. The correct response is not simply
more reasoning in both cases, and not automatically abandoning physics in favor
of easy formal problems.

Fourth, the available costs do not support a productivity ranking by agent.
Pro1's visible turn record has user/final timestamps but no complete internal tool
tree. Ultra's has detailed parent commands and selected subagent events. Summing
these as though they were comparable reasoning costs would be false precision.
Many exact or finite diagnostic computations took fractions of a second, while
the missing step was an analytic argument. Dots' simulation budgets matter, but
extra compute cannot identify a constitutive law. Inference, total discovery
effort and opportunity cost remain incompletely observed.

Finally, the most important contrary explanation remains ordinary accumulation:
strong researchers working longer in well-defined neighborhoods obtain stronger
results. The selected problems may also be migrating toward what the models can
prove while original operational goals stay unresolved. The communication and
stable-recovery results have independent mathematical value; that does not make
them evidence of revolutionary technological progress. We should preserve both
statements rather than devalue mathematics or exaggerate its implications.

A deeper working hypothesis emerges from these particular proofs: several of
our worst obstructions came from a representation that discarded a dependence
needed by the real operation. Pro1's naive corrector-norm estimate lost a
commutator cancellation. Pro2's arbitrary-root reduction created coefficients
on forbidden nonedges. Pro3's separate norm-growth and escape bounds lost the
coupling between transport, rejection and killing. Ultra's abstract q4 candidate
did not satisfy the physical basis constraints. Dots' common total enthalpy did
not specify how that energy was distributed between modes.

These are not the same scientific problem, and the comparison does not prove a
universal cause. But it suggests a more serious capability target than asking
agents to think harder: acquire transformations that preserve the load-bearing
joint constraints while making a new class of constructions searchable or
provable. The current instrument partially tests that idea by constructing the
actual kernel rather than an independently favorable mass-decay and norm model.
Its floating admission defect also shows why a faithful formula is insufficient
without an exact route back to admissible objects. Physical admissibility still
requires evidence about nature, not just algebraic consistency.

3. THE NEW EXPERIMENT

The complete locked outcomes, independent reviews, costs and principal decision
are in meta_experiment/EXPERIMENT_ASSESSMENT.txt. The experiment was prospective:
its common scientific model, current unresolved obligation, endpoint, budgets and
failure conditions were frozen before the investigators or search ran.

It tests a scientific operation rather than another wording instruction. A small
executable adversary constructs the actual delayed-acceptance chain, preserves
killed probability mass and searches for explicit trial-vector violations of a
candidate weighted-operator inequality. Its job is to supply falsifiable feedback;
it cannot certify an upper norm bound or an asymptotic theorem from finite survival.

Both fresh investigators received the same native proofs, failed attempts,
unresolved owner question, candidate inequality and mathematical search family.
The unrestricted investigator could use any ordinary reasoning, code or counter-
example strategy. The instrument investigator additionally received the working
search code. Both had an 18-minute execution ceiling, 60 numerical CPU seconds,
512 MiB target numerical limit, and no subagents. Root's instrument construction,
smoke queries, preparation and independent review are additional charged work.
This is therefore a feasibility pilot with a strong comparison, not a matched
total-cost or statistically powered causal trial.

The target was a better actual killed-operator argument with a proved consequence
for Pro3's unresolved coefficient, or a structural obstruction to a load-bearing
class of such arguments. Merely refuting one arbitrary parameter, surviving
search, improving a toy case or renormalizing away killing could not count.
The final assessment records which outcomes actually survived review.

Both investigations obtained the same new upper coefficient: approximately
1.5422114197 in place of3.3129027138, against the unchanged lower1.0884827657
at K=1,alpha=1/2,d=1. They retained the actual local rejection diagonal and
global killing, then reused the owner's first-hit smoothing theorem. They did
not replace the sampler, infer an upper norm from finite tests, or solve the
remaining coefficient gap. The unrestricted investigator needed no root-built
search for this result. The instrument investigator additionally produced an
exact counterexample and a structural ceiling for that one-step weighted-norm
class after a floating search suggested a violation. Its numerical-to-exact
transition and the final acceptance scope are documented in the pilot record.

The elapsed investigator intervals were both about10.7minutes; complete reasoning
costs were unavailable and the clocks do not justify a speed comparison. Recorded
numerical CPU was about0.08s for the control and3.97s for the instrument investigator,
plus root's separate0.46s smoke calculation and unmetered construction overhead.
The main theorem endpoint saturated even on this previously unresolved obligation.
The additional structural obstruction is useful local evidence, but a single
trial cannot show whether the tool caused a reproducible advantage or whether an
unrestricted investigator would obtain it with a different allocation of effort.

The owner who supplied the earlier theorem is not a failed control. Both new
investigators inherited its proof architecture, adverse families and explicitly
selected next frontier. Their short follow-on success cannot be compared with
the owner's full preceding investigation as a discovery-speed measurement.

4. WHAT A CREDIBLE AMPLIFICATION MECHANISM WOULD REQUIRE

The candidate mechanism is acquisition of reusable scientific operations, not an
ever larger collection of summaries. An operation takes a new object,
checks its scientific premises, and produces a theorem, counterconstruction,
experiment or useful calculation that was previously unavailable. A corrected
drift-to-escape argument, a support-preserving adversarial construction and a
faithful countermodel search can have this form. A paper title or a successful
certificate replay cannot by itself do the same work.

The strongest version is an admissibility-preserving construction: a program or
proof transformation that generates a legitimate scientific object together with
the obligations needed to use it. It could generate an actual unitary basis,
support-preserving hard graph, killed kernel or finite-band channel, rather than
an unconstrained surrogate whose favorable response is later mistaken for the
original problem. The current source contains pieces of such constructions.
No single general compiler across these domains exists here, and building one
without a live scientific consumer would repeat our earlier infrastructure drift.
The next worthwhile test is whether extracting one such operation lets a different
owner establish a new result with fewer unresolved obligations or a stronger
proved domain. A receiver must supply and check the new adapter, not merely run
the producer's example again.

For the current instrument the exact intended capability was: turn a plausible
but false intermediate inequality into a small admitted obstruction, explain
why it fails, and support a better universal argument. Its bottleneck is the
distance between finite numerical feedback and the actual quantified claim.
Its construction is an explicit admissible kernel plus norm residual, followed
by analytic/exact certification. The receiving target is Pro3's current joint-
limit theorem, not a separate easy benchmark. The pilot tests this mechanism
without forbidding the control from doing the same mathematics.

Recursive improvement would require a further step. A discovered operation
would have to help build a better discovery operation, and that successor would
then have to improve a subsequent unresolved task with development and receiving
costs included. A chain of theorems can be valuable scientific accumulation without
that recursion. No model weights were changed in this work. The durable changes
we can observe are proofs, source tools, explicit scientific representations and
their receiving uses.

The most promising form of diversity in the latest record is complementary
mathematics: two correct proofs exposed different controllable quantities, and
the owner combined them. This is an observation about the received artifacts,
not evidence that a fixed number of redundant agents is optimal. Two agents
following the same hidden simplification can be correlated even when their prose
looks independent. Independent review should challenge a load-bearing obligation
with the full source; it should not be counted as a vote for truth.

Failure conditions are concrete. The operation fails to amplify research if it
only solves historical obligations, searches an inadmissible surrogate, creates
no information the unrestricted investigator needs, cannot cross the original
proof interface, or costs more than the useful work it replaces. Acceptance by
a receiver establishes a scientific use, not the missing counterfactual. A
future prospective comparison must include the cost of constructing the tool
and recovering/understanding its prerequisites. It must also allow a competent
control to invent the same approach.

5. SERIOUS ALTERNATIVES AND WHY THEY ARE NOT A SYSTEM-WIDE PRESCRIPTION

Autonomous long continuations are already producing the strongest source-backed
science. Preserve them. Use fresh forks when they can create an independent
construction or challenge, and compact seeds when the exact model and current
obligation can be transferred faithfully. We have no causal evidence that warm
or fresh is universally better. A complete short seed is a scientific interface,
not an instruction to discard useful context at every checkpoint.

Large-scale program evolution and verified theorem search deserve serious
consideration. FunSearch demonstrated discoveries by coupling generated programs
to efficient evaluators and a human-supplied problem skeleton. AlphaEvolve's
authors report deployed algorithmic improvements, including changes to parts of
their own computing stack. AlphaProof couples proof search to Lean feedback and
training on very large sets of formal problems. These are primary examples of
scientific feedback changing the search, rather than merely adding agents or
longer prompts. They do not establish that our small resources or our current
asymptotic/physical obligations admit an equally useful evaluator. [P1-P3]

A wholesale formalization campaign would be premature. Kernel verification could
remove proof errors, but importing the exact infinite-bath or asymptotic model
may be substantial scientific work. A checked formal statement can still be the
wrong physical or operational statement. A focused formal component is warranted
when recurring proof obligations make its receiving value demonstrable; formal
coverage as an end in itself is not the current target.

Central concentration of all branches on one grand problem is also not supported
by these returns. It sacrifices autonomous question formation and parallel
scientific opportunities before we have identified a shared, tractable bottleneck
whose removal would justify that cost. The same caution applies to promoting a
large language-model training project without identified data, hardware or an
executable evaluation contract. None is authorized by enthusiasm alone.

Stronger empirical feedback remains essential for useful physical technology.
Under the present free-resource boundary, existing primary measurements,
constitutive-model comparisons and robust bounds can change decisions. They
cannot manufacture unmeasured material response. Dots should own the choice
between obtaining sufficient evidence, deriving a robust result and moving to a
better physical operation. This is not a blanket retreat from ambitious physics.

6. STRATEGIC DECISION AND NEXT DEPLOYMENT

Continue the restored architecture. The most justified change is in what the
evaluator tests and what it counts as improvement. Test operations that can
create new scientific information or expand a proved class. Retain null results
against competent autonomous controls. Do not require every scientist to follow
a new reasoning routine, export a tool or manufacture a transfer every round.

Pro1 keeps the correlated recurrent-communication frontier selected in its own
source. Pro2 keeps its independently selected bounded-degree stability question.
Pro3 receives the incoming proof packet, accepts or rejects it, and retains
ownership of the remaining coefficient or a better independently chosen question.
Ultra continues preparation-sensitive capital mining and may choose actual
cross-programme synthesis where the premises match. Max reconciles the new
scientific delta using existing private/GitHub routes. Dots continues identified
or robust mode-resolved physical research under its owned admissibility mission.

The six complete prompts preserve exact sources, comparators, scientific stopping
conditions, autonomy and agent-owned handoffs. No scientific questions are chosen
merely because a branch is available. No extra permanent agent allocation or new
research-management system is proposed. No active owner's programme is closed by
completion of this review or of one failed construction.

What would change this decision? A reusable operation that repeatedly enables
independent owners to cross otherwise inaccessible frontiers at accounted cost
would justify concentrating more meta-research on it. A tool that produces only
surrogate wins, or repeated owner-independent reproduction of its result with
less work, would argue against investment. A verified common bottleneck across
important autonomous programmes could justify a shared capability effort. A
physical identification requirement inaccessible under our resources can justify
a scoped pause or a different owned question; it is not a verdict on a field.

We should be ambitious about the depth of the next discovery and restrained about
what the present evidence proves. Astra has real scientific capital. It has not
yet shown that its mechanism for producing that capital improves recursively.
