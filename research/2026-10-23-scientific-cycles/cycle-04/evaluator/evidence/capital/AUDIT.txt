1. PRO1: EXACT DELTA AND WHAT ENABLED IT

This was not already answered by positive one-step drift or a finite cutoff.

For q<1/2, r=q/(1-q), f=(g1/a2,g2/a1), B_f=sum_k r^k C^k(S_f), the commutator identity contains the factor (1-r) that cancels the large resolvent norm. This yields ||[D_g,B_f]|| <= 4||g||||f||. H=L-(2 beta/alpha) Re B_f has a root-retaining positive-drift bound. Crossed color denominators and the exceptional root square completion are substantive details, not cosmetic generalization. The inherited exact representation makes the operation possible to state and check; it does not itself prove the new cancellation. [P/COHERENT_INPUT_ESCAPE.tex, sections 2-4; P/contributions/general_coherent_stability_review.md; Cycle2 RESULTS.txt C2,C8 and reviews/COHERENT_DRIFT_CHECK.md.]

The entropy spectral window I/6<rho<I/2 supplies a model-specific bridge from every qutrit input with S(rho)>3/2 to the corrected drift. The resulting bounds are nu>=4 delta/5 for beta<=1/4 and nu>=3 delta/2 for beta<=1/8, where delta=1/2-rho_00. This turns one specially selected seed into an entire class.

Three downstream steps are real, but their dependence matters:

It constructs exp(-tL/2)(I-tC)exp(-tL/2), with an explicit bounded Taylor remainder, rather than assuming operator monotonicity of exp(-tH). General exponential tilting/Lyapunov ideas are established; historical novelty of this exact formulation was not independently determined. [P/FINITE_BAND_ESCAPE.tex.]

(b) Exact conservation of L+N_port and output subadditivity give extensive bath entropy for high-entropy iid inputs from actual tau_pi. 2. ULTRA: EXACT DELTA AND WHAT ENABLED IT

Cycle2 already supplied common total-Gram transport, physical noncommuting compatible examples, nonlinear scalar-coordinate witnesses, true joint-prefix survival bounds, the erased-stock preparation family, and an exact testing blind spot: on a real inventory family E_R-based testing misses formation by ln N+O(1). The earlier source explicitly said a cone failure would refute only that sufficient method. Cycle3 fulfills that narrower obligation.

The downward cone of common doubly stochastic congruences equals the downward cone of convex permutation congruences for PSD reference G_e. This is a short Birkhoff/Jensen PSD identity; useful reduction, ordinary machinery. It makes a finite separation certificate definitive against arbitrary common DS mixtures.

For D=2,r=3,N=1, an actual rational Cayley unitary basis and PSD dual have normalized gap >1/500 against every reference placement, forcing kappa>ln(2)/500. Native exact residual certification is backed by a distinct root implementation using exact complex Fraction inversion and actual partial traces. All 40,320 placements are checked. This resolves a physical compatibility question that abstract stochastic examples did not resolve. For D=2,r=2,N=1, the full matching-domination characterization and symmetric-unitary polygon property establish zero-defect compatibility for every actual basis. The source correctly attributes the polygon ingredient to known Thompson machinery. [U/PROOF.txt sections 2-3; U/audits/cone_challenge/PROOF.txt and Q4_FULL_CHARACTERIZATION.txt; U/audits/ROOT_EXACT_AUDIT.json; U/COMPARATOR.txt.]

Significance limit: the q8 example does not violate the entropy witness. At N=1 the retained scalar additive defect already dominates its maximum score. It gives no unbounded kappa/r sequence and no formation saving. Repeating the example does not prove additive gaps because global reference permutations may mix copies. Useful uniform O(r) defect remains open. Treating the q8 separation as a generic entropy counterexample would materially exaggerate progress.

The finite-copy supply theorem handles GLOBAL complete-memory initialization error and arbitrary cross-copy separable correlations by a bounded stopped-record test. Phi_M <= M zeta_M propagates through the same complete normal LOCC channel. Deterministic subcritical supply obeys an exponential strong converse at the proved finite-record threshold C_N. Preselected random supply instead obeys mean and quantile bounds, matched by a genuinely pre-resource erased coin. Its leading cost is (1-epsilon)m/(2delta), whereas deterministic supply has m/(2delta). Finite inequalities justify arbitrary simultaneous t->infinity, delta_t->0 limits. [U/PROOF.txt section 4; U/audits/endpoint/PROOF.txt; U/audits/independent_audit/AUDIT.txt.]

This is a valid operational extension with carefully changed resource definitions. The leading vanishing-error law and ordinary stock mechanism were inherited. Operator-order testing, concentration and gambling are standard. It neither determines exact finite-delta formation cost nor repairs the ln(1/delta) generic gap. It should be classified as sharpened reliability/resource accounting, not a new general strong-converse method.

The invariant-MASA result proves a critical entropy witness when a basis has one balanced maximally entangled cut comprising whole tasks. Complementary erasure cells force equal flat cut ranks. Two separately constructed D^2 coordinate systems, one per side, suffice; they need not agree. This is a meaningful structural extension beyond demanding one common coordinate representation, but not a classification of all invariant MASAs and not a generic approximate-formation theorem. It reuses the scalar chain certificate. [U/audits/invariant/PROOF.txt, Theorems 1 and 3.]

The non-PODS result is a concise, useful premise obstruction: strictly positive prefix weights give distinct eigenspaces, so E1 is polynomial in the generator; any hypothetical PODS basis would force its noncommutative range into a diagonal algebra. It kills direct invocation of the named tensorization theorem for N>=2. It does not kill a different semigroup comparison or the critical witness itself. Its simplicity lowers the evidence for a new broad method while increasing its practical value as a prevented false shortcut. [U/PODS_OBSTRUCTION.txt.]

Real frontier: uniform-defect common-Gram transport, generic critical tensor witness, nonmaximal intermediate flat-cut compatibility, generic logarithmic formation gap, and any physical collective saving. No efficient generic algorithm or hardware advantage has been established.

ULTRA's total-Gram formulation was used to formulate and decisively falsify a proposed sufficient cone; old joint-record and stock constructions enabled a new reliability statement. Not supported: improved discovery per unit total resource, a causal advantage of many agents or warm context, repository-mediated discovery, or a self-amplifying loop. The returns have no matched competent baseline, different targets and unknown all-in costs. Direct predecessor dependence would occur in an ordinary sequential research programme. Multiple corollaries from one mechanism should not be added as independent evidence of amplification. Internal reviewer agreement and exact small-instance certification do not establish historical priority.

A stronger claim needs a new consumer to meet the exported interface's hypotheses on an unresolved target and obtain an independently checked result. That result must exceed rederiving the producer theorem or rechecking its certificate. A controlled comparison can then ask whether the reusable interface changes success or cost under comparable access and budgets. One success would still be local evidence, not a general recursive scaling law.
