# Scientific amplification: adversarial audit and a falsifiable next test

Prepared for the Cycle 4 evaluator. Scope: source inspection and experimental design only. No owner frontier was solved, no scientific computation was run, and no branch was contacted.

## Verdict

Cycle 3 supports **genuine cumulative mathematical progress**, including correct extensions beyond the supplied predecessor. It does not establish that the research system has acquired a superior discovery process. The report mostly respects that distinction; its recommendation to prioritize reusable operations is nevertheless a hypothesis, not the winning intervention of a comparison.

The strongest materially different mechanism worth testing now is **counterexample-guided synthesis of scientific certificates**: turn a proposed intermediate inequality into an executable, faithful adversarial search; minimize failures into structural witnesses; use those failures to revise the certificate; then prove the surviving statement at the original quantifiers. The intervention is a reusable computational operation, not an instruction to “think of another representation.” Its first live target should be Pro3's unresolved finite-d crossover. This recommendation is conditional on building a faithful checker; a merely approximate numerical search is not sufficient.

## 1. What the prior experiment does and does not refute

The frozen trial used two historical obligations, one fresh solver per condition and task, and twelve minutes per participant. Both controls could reformulate freely. Treatment added a compulsory search over two representations, premise challenge and coverage accounting. Every arm completed its task. Controls took 222.53 and 529 seconds; treatments took 348.73 and 561.49 seconds. [S1–S4]

**Supported conclusion:** this particular instruction has not demonstrated added value in these four runs. Retaining autonomous reasoning rather than mandating the instruction is warranted.

**Unsupported stronger conclusions:** representation change does not matter; autonomous research is optimal; prescribed strategies generally fail; a treatment advantage is absent on live frontier questions; fresh contexts are better than warm owners; or capital composition is therefore the best alternative. One realization per cell, retrospective task selection and a saturated endpoint do not identify those effects. The two timing differences also mix reasoning, numerical checks and authoring.

The common seed is itself a strong intervention. The sampling participants received the earlier safe-local theorem and starting lemmas. Source selection and scientific formulation were informed by historical success and charged outside the participant clock. A scientifically complete predecessor can make a previously difficult question straightforward for both arms. This is not leakage of the withheld proof, but it means the experiment tested an instruction **conditional on an unusually useful prepared representation**. The most expensive representational decision may already have been made by the preparer. [S1–S3]

The trials were not trivial in their mathematical quantifiers. They were easy *for the tested models with these seeds relative to the budget*: all four completed, with considerable spare time in the anchor task. A universal theorem can be mathematically substantial and still be a poor discriminating benchmark. The new all-positive-eta result remains valuable contributed science independently of this measurement failure. [S1]

### Falsifiable alternatives to a strategy effect

| Explanation | Evidence that would discriminate it |
|---|---|
| Strong seed does most of the work | Equal-budget comparison of the full native source and the compact seed, with source preparation charged; use the same live target and do not remove necessary facts. |

These are competing explanations, not claims about inaccessible private reasoning.

## 2. Why connected results are not yet amplification

The latest source strengthens the evidence for actual dependence: Pro3 explicitly accepts both fixed-eta proofs and uses their moment exponent and affine reset in its new first-hit argument. Its new uniform-convergence criterion and adverse growing path concern a different, harder joint limit. This is more than receipt or indexing. [S5–S6]

The other current returns also contain substantive science: Pro1 gives an iid coherent-information ceiling while keeping the correlated capacity question open; Pro2 gives the worst-case stable treewidth query count and identifies a new bounded-degree boundary. These should not be dismissed as administrative activity. [S7–S8]

But ordinary mathematics also accumulates lemmas and composes them. The following explanations remain viable:

1. **More cumulative effort, unchanged capability.** A stronger theorem after another research interval is expected without any improvement in the rate or quality of discovery.
2. **Task migration.** Successive questions can move toward a mathematically accessible neighborhood while the original operational objective remains unresolved. This can still be worthwhile science; it is not evidence of acceleration on the original objective.
3. **Curation and evaluator selection.** A root chooses useful sources and presents the strongest resulting chains. The unseen denominator is all attempted transfers, dead ends and preparer effort.
4. **Ordinary specialized capital.** A theorem gives a larger solved mathematical domain, but does not improve a general operation for choosing, testing or proving future hypotheses.
5. **Missing operational consequence.** Pro2's theorem leaves the practical NIST incumbent intact; Pro3's sampler remains worse than pure IMH in the newly demonstrated policy regime; Dots' additional calculations do not identify an attainable physical cooling path. These are source-supported limitations, not reasons to reject their mathematical contributions. [S5, S8–S9]

To claim an improved discovery operation, show that a reusable operation changes the attainable frontier on a later unsolved question at accounted cost. To claim recursive improvement, show that this operation helps build a better successor discovery operation, and that the successor then improves a further comparable task. A chain of domain theorems alone does not establish this second relationship. No exponential law is justified by a rising number of stored or accepted results.

A useful falsifier of the current capital hypothesis is not merely “the owner declined the artifact.” It is **no improvement over an unrestricted solver given the same scientific information and resource budget**, across consequential prospective obligations, after including source recovery, tool construction and verification. Acceptance establishes correctness or usefulness; it does not supply the counterfactual.

## 3. The mechanism to test

Build a small **counterexample-guided certificate synthesizer**, with four scientific functions:

1. **Compile the original model and a candidate lemma.** Represent its exact quantifiers, admissibility constraints and mathematical residual. The compiler must retain a map to the native scientific claim; it cannot quietly change the kernel, target, error norm or allowed inputs.
2. **Search for an admitted violation.** Use optimization/enumeration to propose countermodels and an independent exact or outward-bounded calculation to certify the violation. Failed numerical searches carry no universal force.
3. **Minimize and explain the failure mathematically.** Reduce state count, support or parameter complexity while preserving the violation. Return the precise inequality that fails and an executable witness. Structural feedback should change the next candidate certificate; generating a large scatter plot is not the mechanism.
4. **Require the original proof consequence.** A surviving finite search is only conjecture support. Universal bounds need proof; asymptotic counterclaims need an admitted scaling family and control of the relevant errors.

The proposed gain is shorter, more reliable conversion from a false intermediate abstraction to a better one. It is falsified if the loop only finds already-known failures, spends its budget optimizing a surrogate, or returns certificates that cannot cross the native proof interface. This is not model-weight self-improvement and should not be described as such.

There is a serious contrary hypothesis: the dominant difficulty is inventing the right asymptotic argument, not obtaining finite violations. The latest Pro3 record reports small numerical runtimes alongside substantial proof work, and the adverse path's divergence is too slow to be visibly established at the evaluated horizons. An automated loop may therefore make failed conjectures cheaper without improving discovery. The fixed frontier endpoint and the equal-evidence replay are needed precisely because this mechanism is plausible, not already supported as the bottleneck remedy. [S5, S10]

### Why Pro3 is the strongest immediate testbed

Its owner-selected open question is the sharp finite-d accuracy coefficient. At K=1, alpha=1/2 and d=1, the accepted interval is approximately **[1.0884827657, 3.3129027138]**. The current upper proof separates norm growth and killing; the adverse path couples them. The owner explicitly proposes a killed positive operator as the next representation. This is an unresolved live obligation, not a reconstructed result chosen after it was solved. [S5–S6]

The native code already constructs the complete delayed-acceptance kernel and evaluates finite laws, and the proof states its admission requirements. These are components for a checker, not an already complete adversarial oracle: the saved Decimal checks explicitly do not give directed-rounding interval certification. [S10–S11]

The candidate interface should expose the unsafe-state killed subkernel of the **actual** C_eta, with removed mass retained as exit/killing. A conditional renormalization would change the problem. Candidate weighted norms, positive potentials or comparison inequalities must be specified as formulas with domains before searching. The search can vary positive pi,q, symmetric stochastic Q, support size and extreme scales within the exact contract. New witnesses must pass positivity, normalization, KL admission, kernel identity and the claimed residual check. Finite refutation applies to the declared finite lemma; it cannot alone refute or prove a final asymptotic theorem.

Do not hardcode a desired exponent or a hidden new lemma into treatment. The killed-operator suggestion is already in the owner source and must be visible to every arm. The searchable expression grammar and its scientific assumptions must also be common information. The intervention is the automated synthesis/minimization/feedback implementation.

These are reasons for selecting a first test, not owner directives. [S6–S9, S12]

## 4. Concrete prospective experiment

### Freeze before running

Use the latest Pro3 native proof, continuation, failures and numerical source, with hashes. Supply identical complete scientific information to both arms; no hidden answer, preferred certificate or privately prepared “key insight.” The target is the existing finite-d crossover under the original assumptions. Do not narrow it to the old fixed-eta result.

**Primary endpoint:** a complete theorem determining the coefficient/existence at the selected live parameter point, or a valid obstruction to a precisely frozen candidate universal characterization.

**Secondary, ordered endpoint:** a proved reduction of the currently certified interval by at least 10% of its width under unchanged assumptions, then a new necessary intermediate lemma whose implication for the coefficient is explicit. Choose the numerical threshold before results; it is a measurement convention, not a scientific significance claim. A numerical improvement, failed palette, renamed bound or conjectural counterfamily earns no theorem score.

The “candidate characterization” endpoint is admissible only if that characterization is a genuine live conjecture stated before either arm works. Otherwise omit it; do not invent a claim convenient to refute after seeing a counterexample.

### Primary comparison: strong autonomous control

| Resource | Treatment | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Model, effort setting, scientific corpus | Identical | Identical |
| Native evaluator, constraints, expression grammar | Identical | Identical |
| Method | Delivered executable counterexample-guided synthesis loop | Unrestricted autonomous research with raw evaluator and all ordinary coding/calculation tools |
| Permission to reformulate, search countermodels or implement automation | Yes | Yes |
| Total research allocation | Same measured budget, including tool construction and repairs | Same measured budget, available for any strategy |
| Final review | Condition-blind, native-source-aware | Condition-blind, native-source-aware |

A feasible first pilot is 60 measured agent-minutes per arm, with a predeclared cap on external numerical evaluations and no external spending. This is an allocation choice, not a claim that a one-hour frontier proof should exist. Treatment setup consumes its allocation; report reusable setup cost separately as well. Match participant configuration and simultaneous start where possible. Record available elapsed/CPU/call costs and state unavailable inference-token totals. Do not equate tiny numerical runtimes to total discovery cost.

This comparison estimates the benefit of an engineered research tool under equal starting information and resources. It intentionally permits the control to rediscover the tool. Prohibiting the control from counterexamples, representations or code would create an artificially weak baseline.

### Comparable-information diagnostic: replay, not hidden extra advice

Active acquisition changes which evidence exists. No single two-arm test can both preserve that mechanism and force identical generated evidence. Use a second, explicitly separate diagnostic if the first comparison produces a useful new trace:

* Freeze the complete treatment-generated set of candidate statements, admitted witnesses, residuals, failed checks and exact costs.
* Give a fresh unrestricted solver the **same entire scientific evidence**, the grammar, raw evaluator and a full analysis budget. Do not give it only a summary or hide adverse cases. Compare this evidence-only replay with a fresh solver using the tool on the identical accumulated data.
* If replay matches treatment, the gain is explained by access to useful evidence; do not claim special superiority of a prescribed reasoning loop. The active search may still have value as an evidence generator, but its cost must be compared with autonomous evidence generation in the primary trial.
* If a structural-feedback ablation is desired, both fresh solvers must receive identical witnesses and residuals; only machine-guided candidate synthesis differs. Withholding the causal labels would again make information unequal.

This is stronger than a historical predecessor-without-capital control, which confounds missing mathematics with the proposed mechanism. It also avoids feeding a control the treatment's answers during the primary comparison and then pretending to have independent outcomes.

### Verification and stopping

An independent checker should rebuild the native kernel, not import the producer's candidate evaluator. Certify numerical violations with rational identities or rigorous bounds where needed; repeated Decimal precision is not a proof substitute. Final analytic review must check growing support, fixed kernel per run, every return path, normalizer access and the claimed asymptotic quantifier.

If both arms solve the target, credit the science and report saturation; do not claim tool superiority from the number of candidates visited. If neither crosses the frozen endpoint, report an unvalidated mechanism at that budget; do not replace the target with a solved easy one. A treatment-only success is promising local evidence, not a general effect; require replication on another consequential prospective obligation before architectural adoption. If preparation cost dominates or independent verification rejects the apparent gain, the mechanism has not earned priority.

## 5. Decision for the evaluator

Preserve the autonomous owners. Do not issue another universal reasoning protocol, centralize their questions or restart closed work. The evaluator can investigate whether **faithful executable scientific feedback** increases what the existing models can discover. Ultra can later extend a proven useful tool; Max can preserve it once there is actual scientific content to preserve. Neither more transfers nor more prompt trials substitutes for that test.

The immediate evidence supports cumulative science with some real receiving use. The next claim to earn is a useful discovery operation under a strong control. A recursive scientific-intelligence claim is a further experimental obligation.
