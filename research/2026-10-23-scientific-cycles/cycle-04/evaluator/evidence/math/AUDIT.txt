1. PRO2: A NEW ALL-DESIGN LOWER THEOREM, WITH A CLASSICAL UPPER

Under known symmetric support, free diagonals, a total query-row norm cap of
one, arbitrary absolute Frobenius action noise, and operator-norm recovery
error, the new worst-case query thresholds for treewidth at most t are:
exact identification t+1; dimension-independent stable recovery
K_t=(t+1)(t+2)/2. The lower says: for every target inverse factor R, one finite
graph is fixed BEFORE X, and EVERY capped real (K_t-1)-column X has a supported
unit-operator witness whose action is at most 1/R. The decoder may be nonlinear.
A noiseless-zero transcript also extends the obstruction to deterministic
adaptive queries under this same adversarial-noise contract. It is not a
fixed-input randomized minimax lower bound. [P2/PROOF.md §§1–5,8]

The original selected five-query question is actually closed: a root F_6 with
N ordinary child fans F_M at each of six path vertices already fails uniformly
at one attachment generation. For N=M=k, the inverse is at least
k^(1/4)/(sqrt(3*pi)+sqrt(3)); six queries suffice. The graph is chosen before
the dense design, so this goes beyond an ill-conditioned saved palette.
[P2/PROOF.md §7; P2/INTAKE.md; predecessor CONTINUATION.txt inside the Cycle2 ZIP]

The substantive new operations are support-preserving coning and a covariance
alternative. Coning k+1 bad k-query copies adds one universal apex, projects
off its row, chooses k+1 coefficients against k apex-action constraints, and
forms L B L^T with EXACT zero apex action. The potentially large inverse apex
row length is not paid in the action norm. The principal block preserves the
operator lower bound. In the branching lemma, a small h-th aggregate covariance
eigenvalue makes one child nearly rank h, with its true-input perturbation
charged explicitly. If every port has h large eigenvalues, a root coefficient
dimension surplus permits a nonzero root action which many child spokes cancel
cheaply. The universal-apex condition makes every added spoke a legal edge.
[P2/PROOF.md §§3–5; notes/coning_lemma_review.md; notes/fan_spectral_rank_review.md]

The all-treewidth induction starts from a singleton with zero queries. Its
general theorem therefore has NO logical dependence on the inherited fan
lower bound. The separate quantitative fan-tree rate DOES use the accepted
incoming three-query fan theorem. This distinction is essential when crediting
accumulation: inherited exact-cancellation ideas and rational witness source
were reused, and the original selected family was solved using accepted
capital, but the final broader proof was made self-contained. Do not describe
the latter as merely concatenating two received independent theorems.
[P2/INTAKE.md; PROOF.md §§1,5,7; inherited/separator_witness.py]

The matching star-color count, star-coloring sharpness, direct symmetric
recovery, and broad clique/path/recursive-attachment architecture are classical.
The robust extension to all dense row-capped designs is new RELATIVE TO THIS
CHECKPOINT; historical priority is not established. The file explicitly credits
Albertson et al., Theorems 6.1 and 6.3, and sparse-Hessian recovery literature.
Its literature contributor also checked stronger direct multicolor covers and
noiseless probing comparators. Coloring sharpness alone does not prove noisy
all-design sharpness. [P2/PROOF.md references; notes/literature_cycle3.md]

The exact identification proof and the coordinate-decoder noise inequality
