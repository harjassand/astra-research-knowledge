ADDITIONAL DISCLOSED SEARCH FAMILY — available equally to both investigators

Root's experimental finite probe uses m unsafe states and one safe state.
For b in (.1,.62), set q_A=b*softmax(a_i), q_safe=1-b, and
pi_i=q_i*h*exp(-z_i), z_i>0; pi_safe=1-sum pi_i.
Every case checks KL<=1 explicitly. Q is selected from fixed symmetric path,
star, complete, or disjoint-pair matrices on unsafe states and fixes the safe
state. Q is fixed independently of target evaluations; no adaptive chain.

For a chosen c,eta the search constructs the exact specified C numerically,
restricts C[A,A] without normalization, forms D C[A,A] D^-1, and searches
nonnegative row vectors for a large l^p norm ratio. Each trial vector certifies
only a floating LOWER estimate. It is not a norm upper-bound algorithm. All
q,pi,Q,v and ratios are logged; any strict violation still needs an independent
admissibility/arithmetic argument. No asymptotic claim follows from a finite
point. This is a restricted search family, not the full theorem domain.

Both investigators may use this family or any better construction. The control
is free to write search/evaluation code; no reasoning technique is prohibited.
Root has supplied no analytic lemma or undisclosed scientific observation to
the instrument investigator. Instrument development is extra charged overhead,
so equal solver execution time alone cannot establish superior total efficiency.
