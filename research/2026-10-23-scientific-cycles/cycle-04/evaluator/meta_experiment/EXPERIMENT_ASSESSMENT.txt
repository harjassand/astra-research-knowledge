META-RESEARCH INFERENCE
Observed: two separated fresh investigations used the same new native obligation
and accumulated owner mathematics to prove the same stronger upper. The supplied
instrument was not necessary for that advance in this comparison. Its investigator
also produced a broader method obstruction, with a documented numerical-to-exact
route. This is evidence of a useful possible function for the instrument.

Not established: a causal success-rate advantage, total-cost advantage, optimal
agent count, optimal context policy, historical mathematical novelty, practical
sampling gain, original owner acceptance or recursive amplification. A single
paired trial with extra instrument-development resources cannot distinguish
tool benefit from individual search choices, unused alternative control work,
or chance variation. The core theorem endpoint saturated again, despite being
selected from a genuinely unresolved owner frontier. That limits discrimination;
it does not invalidate the new mathematics.

The stronger interpretation is narrow: a faithful executable adversary can help
turn an informal limitation of a proposed proof into an admitted structural
obstruction. We should investigate this where countermodels can change a live
scientific decision. We should not impose it on every investigator, scale it
because it generated more queries, or repeat this now-solved pilot with cosmetic
prompt changes. Before adopting a general discovery operation, test transfer to
a different unresolved obligation, with construction and receiving cost included
and an unrestricted control allowed to invent the same approach.
