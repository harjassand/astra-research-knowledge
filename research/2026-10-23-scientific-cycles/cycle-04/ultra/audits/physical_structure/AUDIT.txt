INDEPENDENT ANALYTICAL AUDIT: THREE-QUBIT INVARIANT ERASURE MASAS
22 October 2026 | finite proof audit; no numerical campaign

Verdict: no gap or counterexample found. The product / Bell-times-single /
independently phased complementary-pair GHZ classification follows from
the stated hypotheses. The common product-local-unitary conclusion is
proved, rather than inferred from individual-vector normal forms.

1. Source cut theorem checked. The restriction of a normalized erasure to
the diagonal MASA is a nonnegative symmetric stochastic idempotent. Its
fixed functions are constant on each connected component, so it averages
uniformly on cells. For a cell C, its fixed projector is I_S tensor Q_C;
|C|=a rank Q_C and every atom in C has reduction Q_C/rank Q_C. Complementary
cut projectors R_u tensor I and I tensor Q_v lie in the MASA, and every
product R_u tensor Q_v is nonzero. Matching the two flat Schmidt spectra
therefore forces one common rank k. In particular k=1 forces every fixed
rank-one tensor projector R_u tensor Q_v into the MASA: the full MASA is
a product of two fixed MASAs. A basis-dependent controlled choice is
excluded by this argument.

2. All-rank-two case checked. Each one-site erasure has exactly two cells
of size four, hence supplies the claimed Hermitian unitary O_s=I_s tensor
T_s. For t different from s, E_t O_s=E_{s,t} O_s=0, because complementary
cut duality makes E_{s,t} global averaging and O_s is traceless. Thus both
one-qubit partial traces of T_s vanish. The SO(3)-SVD proof is valid with
signed singular values: squaring a XX+b YY+c ZZ forces ab=bc=ca=0 and
a^2+b^2+c^2=1. Every T_s is consequently one product of Pauli axes.

3. Simultaneous alignment checked. Any two O_s overlap on exactly one
site; their other tensor factors are nonzero and invertible. Their
commutator vanishes only if the two Pauli axes on the overlapping site
are parallel or antiparallel. All three pairs therefore admit ONE common
product local unitary, giving Z_2 Z_3, Z_1 Z_3, and +/- Z_1 Z_2. The joint
eigenspaces of the first two are precisely span{|x>,|bar(x)>}. Every MASA
atom lies in one such space. Its flat rank-two one-site spectrum forces
equal coefficient moduli, and orthogonality forces the other vector in
that space to be its minus-phase partner. Four independent phases remain;
the proof never incorrectly attempts to remove them by local phases.

4. Factor case and converse checked. If one site has k=1, source cut
duality gives the fixed tensor factorization used in the proof. Invariance
passes to the remaining two-qubit MASA, whose cut rank is 1 or 2. In the
rank-two case, after U_0=I, every other unitary coefficient matrix is a
scalar phase times a real Pauli unit vector. Their Hilbert--Schmidt
orthogonality gives an orthonormal real frame; individual irrelevant signs
make it oriented, so one common SU(2) conjugation gives the Bell basis.
For the GHZ converse, E_s(Pi_{x,+})=E_s(Pi_{x,-})=P_C/4, where P_C is the
sum of the four atoms with the same parity on the other two sites. This
holds for all four arbitrary phases. Two-site erasures give I/8. Thus all
the stated families really are invariant. Their one-site rank patterns
(1,1,1), (1,2,2), and (2,2,2) also distinguish the three cases.

The four-site path-cluster boundary has the stated balanced cut-rank
multiset {2,4,4}, different from {2,2,2} for one four-site GHZ block and
{1,4,4} for two Bell blocks. Its stated use as a classification boundary
is consistent. This audit does not establish historical novelty, an
unrestricted entropy witness, or any preparation/experimental advantage.

Method: direct reconstruction of the cut-cell argument, Pauli/SVD algebra,
commutators, and coefficient-matrix argument. A second independent reader
also checked the k=1 factorization and common-LU Bell step and found no gap.
