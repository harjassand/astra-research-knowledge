1. COMMUTING COMPLEMENT-SECTOR SUPPORT

nonzero joint spectral projections

 R_alpha=product_a P_(a,y_a), alpha=(y_1,...,y_r).

They are mutually orthogonal and sum to I. The common commutant is exactly

 B=direct-sum_alpha B(range R_alpha).

Indeed a matrix commuting with every P_(a,y) has no component between two
different joint label sectors. Conversely every operator block diagonal
in those sectors commutes with all P_(a,y). Vectorization therefore gives

 Pi_W=sum_alpha R_alpha tensor conjugate(R_alpha).

Alice and Bob locally measure the respective full joint resolutions
{R_alpha} and {conjugate(R_alpha)}, communicate the labels, and accept if
they agree. The resulting accept EFFECT is exactly Pi_W, so the failure
probability is exactly the global leakage 1-Tr(Pi_W sigma).

The accepted channel is the sum of fine-grained matching-sector branches.
It dephases between different matched alpha sectors and is generally not

resource-preserving support enforcement or recovery operation is claimed.

2. WEYL IDENTITY FOR ANY INTEGER LOCAL DIMENSION
For one D-level site choose the cyclic shift X_D and phase Z_D with
omega=exp(2 pi i/D), and W_(p,q)=X_D^p Z_D^q for p,q in {0,...,D-1}.
Their trace orthogonality holds for composite D as well as prime D:

 Tr(W_(p,q)* W_(p',q'))=D delta_(p,p') delta_(q,q').

For a prefix of i sites, use the full tensor-Weyl basis {W_u}, with local
prefix dimension L=D^i and exactly L^2 elements. It obeys

 Phi_L=(1/L^2)sum_u W_u tensor conjugate(W_u).           (W)

Direct proof: operator-basis completeness gives
sum_u (W_u)_(ij) conjugate((W_u)_(kl))=L delta_(i,k)delta_(j,l).
These are the matrix entries of L^2 Phi_L on row (i,k), column (j,l).
The identity is therefore exact. No finite-field construction or prime
assumption is used; arbitrary phase conventions for W_u cancel against
their conjugates.

3. ONE BOUNDED REAL LOCC OUTCOME
Choose u uniformly, sharing the same u between Alice and Bob. Alice
measures the spectral resolution of W_u; Bob measures that of
conjugate(W_u). Record the unit-modulus eigenvalues z_A,z_B and set

 Z=Re(z_A z_B) in [-1,1].

For a fixed u its expectation is Re Tr[(W_u tensor conjugate(W_u))sigma].
By (W), averaging u makes E Z=Tr(Phi_L sigma), with identity on every
other physical output and every retained part included in sigma. In
particular the outcome mean is a JOINT prefix probability, not a product
of marginal pair correlations. Arbitrary correlations within sigma remain.

The unitary need not be Hermitian. Its spectral measurement is a legitimate
local projective measurement because it is normal. With tensor Weyls,
measuring each local site factor and multiplying its recorded eigenvalues
is a refinement of that spectral measurement and gives the same estimator.
Using Bob's W_u rather than conjugate(W_u), or independently selecting
Alice's and Bob's settings, would in general estimate a different object.
