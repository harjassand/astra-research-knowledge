ADVERSARIAL AUDIT: COMPLEMENT-SECTOR COMMUTANT WITNESS
22 October 2026 | /root/cycle4_witness_audit

DISPOSITION
PASS. A common MASA, normal coefficient matrices and prefix invariance can
all be removed. The sufficient support is the vectorization of an exact
intersection commutant. Noncommuting families across tasks cause no gap in
the entropy proof. They DO prevent a uniform approximate-support conclusion
from small individual matching-test failures; an exact rational construction
below proves this additional limitation.

1. PRECISE THEOREM
Let H=the tensor product of r tasks H_a, each H_a=(C^D)^(tensor N), with
r,N>=1 and integer D>=2. For each a, fix an arbitrary orthogonal projection
resolution {Q_(a,y)} of the complementary physical Hilbert space H_(not a).
Discard zero projections and write k_(a,y)=rank Q_(a,y). Put

 P_(a,y)=I_task a tensor Q_(a,y),
 A={X in B(H): [X,P_(a,y)]=0 for every a,y}.

A is a complex linear unital *-algebra, even when projections belonging to
different tasks do not commute. Let ||X||_HS=1, X in A, with no normality
assumption, and set rho=X*X, q_(a,y)=Tr(P_(a,y)rho). Then

 S(rho) >= ln(D) sum_(a,i) ||E_(a,i) X||_HS^2
              -r C_D-sum_(a,y) q_(a,y) ln(k_(a,y)).             (T*)

In particular, if k_(a,y)<=K_a for every y, the last term is at most
sum_a ln(K_a). All actual prefixes E_(a,i) refer to the specified physical
task ordering. There is no invariance hypothesis for those prefixes.

2. NONNORMAL BLOCK DECOMPOSITION AND ORTHOGONALITY
Fix task a and suppress its label. Since X commutes with every orthogonal
P_y and sum_y P_y=I,

 X=sum_y X_y,  X_y=P_y X P_y,
 rho=sum_y X_y* X_y,
 q_y=||X_y||_HS^2.

Each prefix erasure acts only inside task a. Since P_y=I_task tensor Q_y,
its bimodule identity gives E_i(X_y)=P_y E_i(X_y)P_y. Mutually orthogonal
P_y supports therefore remain HS-orthogonal, and

 ||E_i X||_HS^2=sum_y ||E_i X_y||_HS^2.                 (B)

This uses two-sided block support, not normality. The commutator premise
ensures precisely that support. Merely assuming rho commutes with the P_y
would NOT ensure (B) for X.

3. OLD SCHMIDT HIERARCHY IN EACH BLOCK
For q_y>0, vectorize X_y/sqrt(q_y). Its two local spaces have physical form
(C^D)^(tensor N) tensor range(Q_y), with dimension D^N k_y. Its Schmidt
probabilities are the eigenvalues of rho_y=X_y*X_y/q_y. The old universal
one-chain theorem, with a residual k_y-dimensional pair, gives

 ln(D) sum_i ||E_i(X_y/sqrt(q_y))||_HS^2 <= S(rho_y)+C_D.     (S)

This theorem applies to an arbitrary coefficient matrix; its proof uses
singular values rather than eigenvalues of X_y. The unchanged constant for
arbitrary integer k_y was separately checked in FULL_TASK_UPGRADE.txt by
zero padding the sorted Schmidt-root list to D^(N+ceil(log_D k_y)) entries
and dropping the additional nonnegative prefix scores.

Average (S) with weights q_y and use (B). Since rho is block diagonal,

 S(rho)=H(q)+sum_y q_y S(rho_y),
 ln(D) sum_i ||E_i X||_HS^2 <= S(rho)-H(q)+C_D.          (C)

No equal block ranks and no shared eigenbasis are required.

4. COMPLEMENT ENTROPY AND ALL TASK CORRELATIONS
The physical complementary state is

 rho_(not a)=sum_y q_y Tr_task(rho_y).

These terms have mutually orthogonal Q_y supports. Therefore

 S(rho_(not a))=H(q)+sum_y q_y S(Tr_task(rho_y))
                <=H(q)+sum_y q_y ln(k_y).

Substitute the resulting lower bound on H(q) into (C). Sum over a and use
the quantum entropy inequality

 sum_a S(rho_(not a)) >= (r-1)S(rho).

This yields (T*). The physical complementary states belong to ONE rho;
their correlations have not been discarded. Quantum Shearer follows by a
common task-order chain expansion and strong subadditivity; no independent
task states or positivity of conditional entropies is assumed.

5. MIXED-SUPPORT E_F EXTENSION
Let W=vec(A), a linear subspace of H_A tensor H_B. If a positive state sigma
is supported in W, every vector in every pure ensemble of sigma is in W.
Indeed its expected weight on W-perp is zero, with nonnegative summands.
Thus averaging (T*) proves

 E_F(sigma) >= ln(D) sum_(a,i) Tr(BellPrefix_(a,i) sigma)
                    -r C_D-Tr[(I_A tensor conjugate(L))sigma],

 L=sum_(a,y) ln(k_(a,y))P_(a,y).

The weighted-rank defect is linear: for vec(X),
<vec(X), I tensor conjugate(P_y) vec(X)>=Tr(X*X P_y)=q_y.
The simpler defect r C_D+sum_a ln K_a follows immediately. No general
strong superadditivity of E_F is invoked.

6. MATCHING EFFECTS ARE LOCC, THEIR LUDERS MAP NEED NOT BE
For each a define the orthogonal projection

 W_a=sum_y P_(a,y) tensor conjugate(P_(a,y)).

Its range is exactly the vectorized operators block diagonal in {P_(a,y)},
and W=intersection_a range(W_a). Alice and Bob can locally measure their
respective resolutions {P_(a,y)} and {conjugate(P_(a,y))}, communicate the
labels and accept when they match. The acceptance EFFECT is exactly W_a.

The accepted channel of this local protocol is the incoherent sum of the
matching-sector branches. It is generally not the coherent Luders channel
z -> W_a z W_a, which retains off-diagonal coherences between different
matching sectors. No free LOCC projection into W is obtained.

If Tr(W_a sigma)=1 for every a, positivity implies supp(sigma) subset W,
even if the W_a do not commute. Finite samples estimate these probabilities
with confidence bounds; they cannot establish exact probability 1. The
tests consume copies and say nothing about complete hidden-memory return.
Their efficient implementation is not supplied by their LOCC definition.

If all P_(a,y) commute across tasks, the W_a commute. Then their product is
the intersection projector W_0 and the operator union bound gives

 1-Tr(W_0 sigma) <= sum_a [1-Tr(W_a sigma)].

This is a mathematical leakage bound, not a free preparation or enforcement
channel. A dimension-dependent continuity extension requires a separate
error calculation and is not asserted here.

7. EXACT RATIONAL OBSTRUCTION TO A UNIFORM NONCOMMUTING LEAKAGE BOUND
Take r=3,N=1,D=2. Name the three qubits 1,2,3, and let Z,X be the ordinary
Pauli matrices on qubit 3. For an arbitrary integer M>=2 define

 c=(M^2-1)/(M^2+1), s=2M/(M^2+1), Z_M=c Z+s X,
 Z-basis projections R_+=(I+Z)/2, R_-=(I-Z)/2,
 tilted projections T_+=(I+Z_M)/2, T_-=(I-Z_M)/2.

All matrix entries are rational, c^2+s^2=1, and c,s are nonzero. Choose
the complementary resolutions

 task 1: Q_(1,+/-)=I_2 tensor R_+/- on qubits 2,3; ranks 2;
 task 2: Q_(2,+/-)=I_1 tensor T_+/- on qubits 1,3; ranks 2;
 task 3: Q_(3,uv)=|uv><uv| on qubits 1,2; ranks 1.

Thus the uniform rank bounds are K=(2,2,1), independent of M. Commuting
with both the Z and Z_M resolutions on qubit 3 forces the qubit-3 factor
to be scalar, because Z and Z_M generate the full 2x2 matrix algebra.
Commuting with the four computational projections on qubits 1,2 then gives

 A=diagonal(H_1 tensor H_2) tensor I_3.

Consider the normalized coefficient matrix

 X_bad=(I_12/2) tensor (Z_3/sqrt(2)).

It is HS-orthogonal to A, because Tr Z=0. Consequently the pure state
|vec(X_bad)><vec(X_bad)| has half trace distance EXACTLY 1 from every
state supported in W=vec(A).

Nevertheless its three matching-test accept probabilities are

 Tr(W_1 sigma_bad)=1,
 Tr(W_2 sigma_bad)=c^2,
 Tr(W_3 sigma_bad)=1.

The middle identity follows directly from tilted pinching:
T_+ Z T_+ +T_- Z T_-=c Z_M, whose normalized HS norm squared is c^2.
Thus the sum of individual failures is

 1-c^2=s^2=4M^2/(M^2+1)^2 -> 0,

while the distance to exact admissible support stays 1. There is NO uniform
leakage-to-support modulus tending to zero based only on these individual
failures and bounded K_a. An angle condition, commuting premise or other
quantitative compatibility input is necessary for that robustification.

This is a failure of one approximate-support certification implication.
It does not violate (T*), since sigma_bad is outside the exact support.
It also does not lower-bound the cost of every possible LOCC measurement;
the assertion concerns the declared matching effects and their attempted
uniform support inference.

8. SCOPE, COST AND OPEN BOUNDARY
r=1 is the original arbitrary-coefficient single-chain theorem. Varying
sector ranks, zero-weight sectors and composite D are covered. All spaces
are finite. Across-task projection families may be incompatible; only the
exact commutant support premise is used in the theorem.

No numerical search or optimizer was run for this audit. The rational
counterconstruction and all entropy arguments are analytic. Historical
novelty, scalable implementation and experimental usefulness remain UNKNOWN.
General unrestricted coefficient matrices need not belong to a bounded-rank
complement commutant. Approximate support for noncommuting sector families
needs additional compatibility control as section 7 proves. The generic
