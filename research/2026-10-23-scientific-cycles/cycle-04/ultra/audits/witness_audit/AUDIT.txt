ADVERSARIAL AUDIT: LOW-FULL-TASK-CUT-RANK CRITICAL HIERARCHY WITNESS
22 October 2026 | /root/cycle4_witness_audit

DISPOSITION
The proposed theorem is internally valid under the stated exact invariance and
support premises. No fatal gap was found. The simultaneous alignment, entropy
identity and quantum Shearer step all have the required directions. This audit
does not establish historical novelty, efficient certification of invariance,
or applicability to unrestricted approximate formation outputs.

PRECISE CONTRACT
Let r,N>=1 and D>=2 be integers, with r tasks each containing N D-level sites.
Let A=span{Pi_j} be a rank-one MASA on all rN sites. Assume ONLY that every
actual task-prefix normalized erasure E_(a,i), 1<=a<=r,1<=i<=N, preserves A.
Let k_a be the common Schmidt rank of the basis vectors across full task a
versus its physical complement. For f>=0, sum_j f_j^2=1, put X=sum_j f_j Pi_j.
Using the inherited valid scalar constant C_D, the audited conclusion is

 S(X^2) >= ln(D) sum_(a,i) ||E_(a,i)X||_HS^2
                  - r C_D - sum_a ln(k_a).

The same inequality holds for complex diagonal coefficient matrices, with
S(X*X) in place of S(X^2), and as an E_F witness on states supported in
span{|e_j>_A tensor |conjugate(e_j)>_B}. It is not a theorem for arbitrary
coefficient matrices or arbitrary states outside that support.

1. WHY THE WEAKER PREFIX HYPOTHESIS SUFFICES
Every full-task erasure E_(a,N) is among the assumptions. The erasure of the
complement of full task a is the product of E_(b,N) over b!=a; composition
therefore also preserves A. These physical normalized erasures commute.
For r=1 the complementary erasure is the identity, which preserves A.
Thus the two-sided full-cut classification proved in Cycle3 applies to each
full-task cut; no invariance of every physical subset is being smuggled in.

2. DIVISIBILITY OF EVERY PREFIX CELL
On the diagonal coefficient space, K_i=E_(a,i)|A is a real, symmetric,
entrywise nonnegative, stochastic idempotent. It is consequently uniform
averaging on the cells of a partition. For a cell C and j in C,

 E_(a,i)(Pi_j)=P_C/|C|,
 P_C=sum_(l in C)Pi_l=I_(a,i) tensor Q_C,

where Q_C is a projection. To see the integer constraint directly, compare
E(Pi_j)=I/D^i tensor Tr_prefix(Pi_j) with P_C/|C|. Since P_C is a projection,
Tr_prefix(Pi_j)=Q_C/k_C for k_C=|C|/D^i=rank Q_C, a positive integer.
Hence EVERY cell size is a multiple of D^i, including nonuniform cells.

The nested erasures satisfy K_i K_j=K_max(i,j). Therefore their partitions
are nested: every level-i cell lies in exactly one level-j cell when i<=j.

3. DFS ALIGNMENT: THE NONUNIFORM-CELL ISSUE IS RESOLVED
Fix one full-task cell C_N. Recursively order the leaves of its nested
partition tree by depth-first traversal, with any fixed ordering of siblings.
Every cell at every level then becomes a contiguous interval. At a fixed
level i, these intervals partition C_N and all have lengths divisible by D^i.
Starting the indexing at zero, each interval start is the sum of previous
level-i interval lengths and is therefore divisible by D^i too.

The standard chunks [h D^i,(h+1)D^i) thus refine every actual level-i cell,
SIMULTANEOUSLY for all i. Since |C_N|=D^N k_a, the index set can be written
as [D]^N times [k_a], with the first i little-endian D-coordinates producing
exactly these chunks. Let Q_i average those first i coordinates and retain
the residual [k_a] coordinate. The range of K_i is contained in the range
of Q_i, so Q_i K_i=K_i Q_i=K_i, and

 ||K_i f||_2^2 <= ||Q_i f||_2^2.

No equal-size child count, common coordinate system across different tasks,
or bounded prefix Schmidt rank was used. The ordering may depend on task a
and on its full-task cell; the subsequent entropy is invariant under either
permutation, so this creates no compatibility obligation across tasks.

4. CONDITIONAL SCALAR BOUND AND WEIGHTS
Write p_j=f_j^2 and w_C=sum_(j in C)p_j. Ignore cells of zero weight. Normalize
f within each full-task cell. For each residual coordinate z in [k_a], apply
the inherited scalar one-chain bound to the normalized D^N amplitude slice
and average its probability weight. This proves on C

 ln(D) sum_i ||Q_i f_C||^2 <= H(X_coords | Z,C)+C_D
                             <= H(J | C)+C_D.

Since every K_i preserves each full-task cell, its squared norm decomposes
with weights w_C. Averaging the last inequality gives

 ln(D) sum_i ||K_(a,i) f||^2 <= H(J | Y_a)+C_D,

where Y_a is the full-task cell label. The constant is paid once per task,
not once per full-task cell or residual slice, because all weights sum to 1.

5. EXACT ENTROPY IDENTITY
The two-sided full-cut classification gives one common k_a for all full-task
cells and |C|=D^N k_a. Put rho=X^2=sum_j p_j Pi_j. Then

 E_(a,N)(rho)=I_task/D^N tensor rho_complement

has eigenvalues w_C/(D^N k_a), each with multiplicity D^N k_a. Consequently

 N ln(D)+S(rho_complement)=H(Y_a)+N ln(D)+ln(k_a),
 H(J|Y_a)=S(rho)+ln(k_a)-S(rho_complement).

The identity uses p=f^2, not the averaged amplitude distribution Kf. Mixing
those two distributions would invalidate the proof; the proposed argument
does not do so.

6. QUANTUM SHEARER AND END OF THE PROOF
For the r full tasks, the quantum entropy inequality

 sum_a S(rho_complement a) >= (r-1)S(rho)

has the required direction. One direct proof expands each complementary
entropy by the chain rule in the same task order. For each retained task j,
conditioning on its earlier retained tasks gives at least its conditional
entropy given ALL earlier tasks, by strong subadditivity. Every task is
retained exactly r-1 times; summing gives (r-1)S(rho). Negative conditional
entropies cause no problem because the coverage count is exactly r-1.

Sum the task bounds from section 4, substitute section 5 and apply this
inequality. The right-hand entropy sum is at most
S(rho)+sum_a ln(k_a), proving the conclusion.

7. PHASES AND CONVEX ROOFS
For each task-prefix, ||E X||_HS^2=c* K c with K an entrywise nonnegative
orthogonal projection. It is at most |c|^T K |c|, while the Schmidt entropy
of the vectorized normal coefficient matrix remains H(|c|^2). Thus replacing
c by |c| proves the claimed complex extension.

A pure-state ensemble of a positive state supported in the maximally
correlated support above has every vector in that support: expectation of
the orthogonal-complement projection is zero and every term is nonnegative.
The pure witness therefore averages to the E_F witness, since its prefix
overlaps are linear expectations of the actual Bell-prefix projectors.
This support argument cannot be applied to a generic approximate preparation
output merely because its target lies in the support.

8. EDGE CASES AND CONSEQUENCE
r=1: k_1=1 because the complementary dimension is 1; quantum Shearer reads
0>=0; the argument reduces to the single nested partition chain.
N=1: the DFS argument only needs one partition and remains valid.
D composite: k_a need only divide D^N and D^((r-1)N); it need not be a power
of D. The proof only uses integer |C|/D^i, never log_D(k_a) integrality.
Zero weights and zero residual-slice probabilities are omitted before
normalization. All spaces and entropies in this finite theorem are finite.

If k_a<=K uniformly in N, this gives the horizon-independent defect
r(C_D+ln K). The low-cut-rank premise is structural and must itself be
established for any proposed application. It does not show a new physical
method for measuring hidden return error or certifying preparation resources.

REMAINING GAP
Unbounded full-task cut ranks can make this defect extensive in N, and the
argument does not prove the unrestricted critical witness. The earlier
balanced maximal-cut theorem remains useful in such cases. Neither theorem
by itself determines unrestricted iid formation cost or removes its generic
logarithmic gap. Historical priority and efficient implementation are UNKNOWN.
