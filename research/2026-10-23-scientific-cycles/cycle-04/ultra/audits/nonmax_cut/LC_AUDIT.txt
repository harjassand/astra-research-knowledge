INDEPENDENT CHALLENGE OF THE PRINCIPAL'S DFS LOCAL-CHAIN PROOF
22 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane. Complete proof audit, not external review.

VERDICT: PASS within the explicit finite rank-one MASA model. No numerical
search or extra physical access premise is needed. The proof works with
invariance under the ACTUAL task prefixes only, not every physical subset.
The central domination is a one-chain relabeling, separately chosen for each
task and full-task cell. No common multi-task classical coordinates follow.

1. RESTRICTION OF A PHYSICAL ERASURE

Let A=span{Pi_j} be a rank-one MASA, Pi_j=|e_j><e_j|, on the finite
physical Hilbert space. Let E_S erase the sites of S, with dimension a=D^i,
and assume E_S(A) subset A. The restriction K_S is a real symmetric,
entrywise nonnegative, stochastic, idempotent matrix: these properties follow
respectively from self-adjointness, complete positivity, unitality/trace
preservation and idempotence of E_S in the orthonormal Pi basis.

Every such matrix averages uniformly on disjoint cells. One direct proof
decomposes its nonnegative graph into connected components; the Perron
eigenspace of each irreducible stochastic component at eigenvalue 1 is
one-dimensional, while idempotence permits only eigenvalues 0 and 1.
Symmetry then gives the uniform rank-one projection on each component.
Thus if C is the cell containing j,

 E_S(Pi_j)=P_C/|C|,   P_C=sum_(l in C) Pi_l.

The range condition on E_S gives P_C=I_S tensor Q_C for a projection Q_C.
Consequently |C|=D^i rank(Q_C). This integer divisibility is the only local
cell-size input needed by the DFS argument. It does not require complementary
prefix invariance, uniform intermediate ranks, a prime D or flat spectra
shared by all intermediate cells.

2. ALL LEVELS ADMIT ONE CHUNK REFINEMENT FOR A SINGLE CHAIN

Fix a task and let C_i be its prefix-i partition, i=0,...,N. Physical
prefix expectations satisfy E_i E_j=E_max(i,j), so the restriction cells
are nested, C_i refining C_(i+1). Fix a full-task cell C in C_N, of size
D^N k_C. List its leaves in depth-first order in the nested partition tree;
children may be listed arbitrarily at every node. Then every intermediate
cell is a contiguous interval in this one ordering.

At level i all such intervals have length divisible by D^i. Since they
partition C and the first starts at index zero, every boundary is a multiple
of D^i. It follows that EACH standard consecutive chunk of length D^i is
contained in one level-i cell. This implication is simultaneous for all i.

Let Q_i average the standard chunks of length D^i. The Q_i are themselves
nested orthogonal conditional expectations. Their partitions refine the
actual C_i partitions, so

 K_i Q_i=K_i,  Q_i K_i=K_i,  ||K_i f||_2<=||Q_i f||_2.     (L1)

This remains true if some adjacent actual levels are identical, if a level
cell has rank greater than one, or if different level-i cells have different
ranks. These are the most plausible hidden failures and none breaks the
aligned-boundary argument.

3. APPLY THE SCALAR CHAIN BOUND WITH THE CORRECT RESIDUAL

The ordered C has D^N k_C labels, so write its index as N base-D low digits
and one residual label R in [k_C]. Q_i averages the first i low digits.
For every nonnegative normalized coefficient vector z on C, the inherited
scalar chain lemma, applied to normalized slices with R held fixed, gives

 (ln D) sum_(i=1)^N ||Q_i z||_2^2
    <= H(J | R)_(z^2)+C_D <= H(J)_(z^2)+C_D.              (L2)

Here Q_N need NOT equal K_N on C: it averages each D^N chunk, leaving
the residual label. Only (L1), not equality at the terminal level, is used.
The proof does not pretend that R is conserved by the actual device or
experimentally accessible. It is a mathematical auxiliary partition.

Apply (L1)-(L2) to each normalized full-task cell slice of the original
global f, weighted by that slice's squared norm. Zero-norm cells contribute
zero. Since the actual K_i preserve every full-task cell, the result is

 (ln D) sum_i ||K_i f||_2^2
      <= H(J | Y_full)_(f^2)+C_D.                         (LC)

The C_D is paid once, because the slice masses sum to one. This is exactly
the local-chain premise left conditional in ENTROPY_ALLOCATION.txt.
Different tasks may use completely different DFS orders and residual labels;
they disappear from (LC), so no joint relabeling assumption is needed.

4. THE NARROWED GLOBAL HYPOTHESIS IS SUFFICIENT

Assume the MASA is invariant under each ACTUAL prefix of every task.
Every full-task erasure preserves it. The product of the full erasures of
all OTHER tasks equals the complementary full-task erasure and therefore
also preserves it. Thus both complementary expectations for T_a:T_a^c
preserve the MASA, even though intermediate complementary prefixes need
not preserve it. The prior cut theorem therefore supplies one common full-
task Schmidt rank k_(T_a), independent of spectral label j.

For rho=sum_j f_j^2 Pi_j, the exact identity proved in the accompanying
ENTROPY_ALLOCATION.txt is now applicable to every full task:

 H(J | Y_(T_a)) = S(rho)+ln k_(T_a)-S(rho_(T_a^c)).

Sum (LC) over tasks and apply strong subadditivity in its disjoint-task
Shearer form sum_a S(rho_(T_a^c)) >= (r-1)S(rho). This proves

 S(rho) >= (ln D) sum_(a,i)||K_(a,i)f||_2^2
                   -r C_D-sum_a ln k_(T_a).               (L3)

For positive X=sum_j f_j Pi_j with Tr X^2=1, rho=X^2 and K norms are the
actual HS erasure norms. Normal complex coefficients extend by taking
absolute values, since every actual K is entrywise nonnegative. All mixed
states in the specified maximally-correlated diagonal support obtain the
corresponding E_F witness by convex-roof averaging. This is not an assertion
that unrestricted approximate dilution outputs belong to the support.

If sum_a ln k_(T_a)<=c r with c independent of N, (L3) has critical
coefficient ln D and defect r(C_D+c), uniform in N. With exponentially
large full-task ranks the defect may be extensive and this argument alone
does not resolve the high-rank boundary. It does not change generic iid
formation costs or the proved limitation of E_R tests.

5. ACTUAL CLASS BOUNDARY AND THE STRONGEST OLDER COMPARATOR

Even a tensor product of Bell bases between selected task pairs, or a GHZ
basis on one site of each task followed by arbitrary product-basis sites,
can have bounded full-task ranks and no balanced maximal-entanglement task
cut at large N. Therefore (L3) applies strictly beyond the literal premise
of Cycle3's balanced maximal-cut theorem. These simple examples themselves
are already covered by the stronger older stabilizer/group-GHZ constructions;
they are admission examples, not a new advantage claim.

The substantive generality is that (L3) requires only actual task-prefix
invariance and bounded full-task ranks. It assumes no stabilizer, field,
group, Hadamard form, all-subset invariance, tensor-component decomposition
or common coordinate system across the tasks. The DFS comparison is fully
constructive as a finite proof, but explicitly listing the basis/partitions
can be exponential. No efficient assay or implementation is proved.

The weakened invariance premise is physically strict. On two ordered qubits,
take the basis

 e_(j,0)=|j> tensor |0>,
 e_(j,1)=V|j> tensor |1>,
 V=[[3/5,-4/5],[4/5,3/5]],  j=0,1.

Erasing the first site uniformly averages j at fixed last-site label, and
erasing both sites gives the global average, so both actual prefix erasures
preserve this rank-one MASA. Erasing ONLY the second site does not: explicitly

 <e_(0,1)| E_{second}(|00><00|) |e_(1,1)> = -6/25 != 0.

Tensoring such task bases across the complete tasks and padding each task
with product-basis suffix sites preserves actual-prefix invariance for any
larger N and gives k_(T_a)=1. It need not preserve every physical subset.
This is an exact model-class separation from the earlier all-subset premise.
Its actual chain kernels are themselves conventional coordinate/tree
averages, so it is not a new performance advantage over that comparator.
