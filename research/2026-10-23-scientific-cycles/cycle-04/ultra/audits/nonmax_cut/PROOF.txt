NONMAXIMAL CUTS: PHYSICAL BLOCK-MIXING OBSTRUCTION AND TENSOR-COMPONENT CLOSURE
22 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane
Bounded Cycle4 lemma. Internal complete arguments; historical novelty UNKNOWN.

0. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT RESOLVE

Two mathematical statements are proved below. First, the physical block
decomposition supplied by a nonmaximal invariant cut does NOT give invariant
sub-erasure dynamics inside the cut blocks. This failure occurs already for
an exact tensor product of three Bell bases, with nontrivial cut rank k=2.
Thus conditioning a maximal-cut proof on those block labels is invalid without
a further invariance hypothesis. This resolves a concrete proposed extension
method, not the critical witness.

Second, critical hierarchy witnesses close under actual tensor components
that are interleaved along the same physical task chains, and under internal
tensor factors of the D-level sites. Spectral coefficients may be completely
correlated across components. This strictly extends the balanced maximal-task-
cut premise as a theorem class. Its proof is ordinary conditional entropy
tensorization, and its explicit Bell example is already covered by the older
stabilizer result. It does NOT resolve the generic invariant-MASA frontier.
It is a reusable composition lemma, not the proposed main scientific result.

1. ACTUAL NONMAXIMAL-CUT BLOCK MIXING

There are six qubits, numbered 1,...,6. Let the orthonormal rank-one MASA be
the tensor product of Bell bases on pairs (1,4), (2,3), and (5,6). It is
invariant under every subset erasure: erasing either endpoint of any Bell
pair maps each of that pair's four projectors to I_4/4, and erasing neither
endpoint fixes it. Products prove invariance under every subset exactly.

Consider L={1,2,3}, R={4,5,6}, each of dimension 8. Every basis vector has
Schmidt rank k=2 across this cut, flat spectrum (1/2,1/2): only pair (1,4)
crosses the cut. The common-rank cut theorem decomposes the local spaces as

 R_u = I_1 tensor Pi_u^(23),  u=0,...,3,
 Q_v = I_4 tensor Pi_v^(56),  v=0,...,3,

where each R_u and Q_v is a rank-two projection. In every R_u tensor Q_v,
the four choices of the (1,4) Bell vector form a complete maximally entangled
basis of the corresponding 2 by 2 block. This exactly realizes the theorem's
nonmaximal block decomposition, with no missing labels.

Nevertheless, erasing physical site 2 within L gives

 E_2(R_u) = I_123/4 = (1/4) sum_(u'=0)^3 R_(u').            (1)

In particular E_2(R_u) is not supported on R_u, for ANY u. On an input
whose support lies in R_u, conditioning on the same outgoing block label
has probability 1/4. The compression R_u E_2(.) R_u is therefore trace
decreasing on that block. It is not a normal unital conditional expectation
for a smaller normalized physical system. Similarly E_5 mixes all Q_v.

The block labels u,v are real spectral labels and cannot be held fixed for
free while applying the original physical hierarchy. A proof which applies
the balanced-maximal-cut witness separately inside each R_u tensor Q_v and
then averages these normalized witnesses must first establish how it pays
for exactly this block mixing. Equation (1) disproves the premise that the
restriction is automatically invariant. It does not disprove another proof
of the witness for this MASA; this example itself is conventional Bell stock.

2. INTERLEAVING CLOSURE WITH ALL SPECTRAL CORRELATIONS

Fix r tasks of N D-level sites. Partition the physical sites into s tensor
components so that every task has N_l sites in component l, sum_l N_l=N.
The positions assigned to a component may be arbitrarily interleaved in a
task. Order a component's sites by their actual physical order in each task.
Assume A_l is a rank-one diagonal MASA on component l, and its actual local
prefix-erasure kernels K_(l,a,j) obey the complete coefficient witness

 (ln D) sum_(a=1)^r sum_(j=1)^(N_l) ||K_(l,a,j) z||_2^2
       <= H(z^2) + c_l                                         (2)

for every nonnegative normalized coefficient vector z on A_l's labels.
For instance c_l=(r/2)C_(D^2) when component l satisfies the previous
balanced maximal-task-cut theorem. No relation among those cuts is required.

Let A=tensor_l A_l. Then for every nonnegative normalized coefficient array
f(j_1,...,j_s), with ARBITRARY correlations among all component labels,

 (ln D) sum_(a=1)^r sum_(i=1)^N ||K_(a,i) f||_2^2
       <= H(f^2) + sum_l c_l.                                  (3)

Proof. If j_l(a,i) counts component-l sites in physical prefix (a,i),
the actual physical erasure restriction is exactly

 K_(a,i) = tensor_l K_(l,a,j_l(a,i)),

with K_(l,a,0)=identity. Each factor is an orthogonal projection. Charge
physical step (a,i) to its unique component l, whose counter has just
advanced to j=j_l(a,i). Commutativity of different tensor factors gives

 ||K_(a,i) f||_2^2 <= ||(K_(l,a,j) tensor I_(-l)) f||_2^2.

Each local positive-index prefix (l,a,j) is charged exactly once. Holding
the other spectral labels fixed, apply (2) to the normalized l-slice of f.
Multiply by its squared norm and sum all slices, with zero-norm slices
contributing zero. For p=f^2 this gives

 (ln D) sum_(a,j) ||(K_(l,a,j) tensor I_(-l)) f||_2^2
       <= H(J_l | J_(-l))_p + c_l.

Finally sum l and use the general classical inequality

 sum_l H(J_l | J_(-l)) <= H(J_1,...,J_s).

One proof expands H(J_1,...,J_s) by the chain rule, and observes that the
conditioning set J_(-l) contains the earlier labels in that ordering.
This proves (3). No product distribution, independent spectrum, hidden
memory return, or experimental accessibility is assumed. QED.

For complex normal diagonal coefficient arrays, each kernel is entrywise
nonnegative and f* K f <= |f|^T K |f|; entropy is H(|f|^2). Thus (3)
extends unchanged. The full mixed support convex-roof E_F extension is
valid by averaging supported pure ensemble vectors, exactly as previously.
It does not put a generic approximate iid formation output in this support.

3. INTERNAL FACTOR CLOSURE

Suppose instead each physical site has a specified tensor factorization
H_D=tensor_l H_(D_l), D=product_l D_l, constant over all physical sites.
Let A_l be a component MASA on the l-factors of all rN sites, satisfying
(2) with ln(D_l), N_l=N and defect c_l. The global actual prefix kernel is
again the tensor product of all component prefix kernels. For each (a,i),

 (ln D)||K_(a,i)f||^2
   = sum_l (ln D_l)||K_(a,i)f||^2
   <= sum_l (ln D_l)||(K_(l,a,i) tensor I_(-l))f||^2.

The same normalized-slice argument and conditional entropy inequality give
(3), now with coefficient ln D and defect sum_l c_l. Both closure operations
can be composed finitely many times. The defect is uniform in N only when
the number and defects of components are uniformly bounded; dividing every
site into its own component would not establish the desired uniform bound.

4. EXPLICIT CLASS BEYOND ONE BALANCED MAXIMAL TASK CUT

Take r=4, D=2 and N=3L, L>=1. Use three consecutive slabs of L positions
in each task. In slab 1 put Bell bases between task pairs (1,2),(3,4);
in slab 2 use (1,3),(2,4); in slab 3 use (1,4),(2,3), matching equal
positions. This is an actual all-subset invariant tensor-product MASA.
Each slab has a balanced maximal-entanglement task cut, so (3) gives
the critical witness with defect 3*(r/2)C_4=6C_4, independent of L.

The complete MASA has NO balanced maximal-entanglement task cut. Indeed
each two-versus-two cut of the four tasks has exactly one of these perfect
matchings wholly internal on both sides; the other two matchings cross the
cut. Every basis vector consequently has 4L crossing Bell pairs, cut rank
2^(4L), strictly smaller than the balanced side dimension 2^(6L).
This is true for each of the three balanced task cuts.

Thus tensor-component closure goes beyond the literal premise of the
Cycle3 theorem. However this explicit basis is a stabilizer basis, and the
older vector-Hall stabilizer theorem already gives a stronger defect 4C_2.
It must not be counted as a new witness capability defeating that comparator.
The general closure applies to arbitrary already-certified components,
including future nonstabilizer invariant components, without requiring one
common cut or independent spectral coefficients. Its historical novelty is
UNKNOWN; the mathematical tensorization machinery is standard.

5. DECISION AND REMAINING BOUNDARY

Retire the automatic 'condition on the nonmaximal-cut blocks' extension.
It has an explicit quantum premise failure, (1). Retain the composition
lemma as a low-cost certified constructor. It supplies no universal theorem
for irreducible nonmaximal invariant MASAs, no unrestricted formation law,
no generic logarithmic-gap closure and no efficient realization theorem.

The unresolved structural question is whether physical joint sub-erasure
maps on the cut-block labels admit an entropy allocation that pays their
mixing while retaining coefficient ln D and an O(r) defect. Merely knowing
the common Schmidt rank and block sizes cannot justify a normalized
within-block reduction. The actual commuting quantum maps are essential.
