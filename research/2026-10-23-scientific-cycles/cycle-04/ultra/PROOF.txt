3. PROOF OF THE BLOCK-ALGEBRA THEOREM

Fix a. Because X commutes with its P_y, it decomposes X=sum_y X_y,
X_y=P_y X P_y. Its blocks have mutually orthogonal complementary supports
Q_y. Prefix erasure acts ONLY within task a, so it is block bimodular:
 E_(a,i)(P_y X P_y)=P_y E_(a,i)(X)P_y.
Thus the erased blocks remain Hilbert-Schmidt orthogonal and
 ||E_(a,i)X||^2=sum_y ||E_(a,i)X_y||^2.                   (C)
This step is exactly why no prefix-MASA invariance is needed.

Set q_y=||X_y||^2=Tr(P_y rho). When q_y>0, Y_y=X_y/sqrt(q_y) is a
normalized matrix on H_(T_a) tensor ran Q_y, with local dimension D^N k_y.
Apply (B) to Y_y, then average with weights q_y. Zero-weight blocks vanish.
Since rho is block diagonal, ordinary block entropy gives
 S(rho)=H(q)+sum_y q_y S(Y_y*Y_y).
Consequently
 m sum_i ||E_(a,i)X||^2 <= S(rho)-H(q_a)+C_D.              (D)

The reduced state rho_(T_a^c) is also block diagonal in the Q_y. Its
conditional block states have rank at most k_y. Therefore
 S(rho_(T_a^c))=H(q_a)+sum_y q_y S(rho_(T_a^c)|y)
                 <=H(q_a)+sum_y q_y ln k_y.               (E)
There is no assertion that these block states are pure or flat. Combining
(D),(E), summing a, and using quantum Shearer's inequality gives (A):
 sum_a S(rho_(T_a^c)) >=(r-1)S(rho).
For clarity this version follows directly from SSA and the entropy chain
rule: expand every complementary entropy in task order; removing a task
from a conditioning set increases the conditional entropy. Each full-chain
conditional term appears r-1 times in the sum. Quantum conditional entropies
may be negative; this argument does not assume otherwise. QED.

At r=1 the complement has dimension1, k=1 and (A) is the old one-chain
theorem. At N=1, composite D, zero spectral probabilities or nonnormal X,
the proof is unchanged. Cross-task projectors need not commute with each
other; only X's declared block commutators vanish.
