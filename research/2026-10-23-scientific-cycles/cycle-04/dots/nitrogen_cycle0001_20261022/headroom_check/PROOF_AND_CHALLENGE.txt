CONDITIONAL RETENTION-ONLY HEADROOM BOUND
2026-10-21 UTC. Saved-data analysis only; no new trajectories.

RESULT
At the saved 2700 K heater boundary, the maximum reduction in heater-input
energy per mole relative to the saved ordinary ideal-quench reference is
0.07843772414%, provided the architecture can change retention only and cannot
reduce the heater-input floor. Perfect retention allows strictly less than
1180.400074835 J/kg fresh additional counted energy to beat that reference.
This is an algebraic architecture-class bound CONDITIONAL ON THE CONDITIONS
BELOW, not a physical exclusion of regeneration or a validated plant SEC.

1. DEFINITIONS AND CONDITIONS
Normalize to 1 kg original fresh dry 79/21 air, 300 K, 1 atm. Net final product
is 1 kg gas at 300 K, 1 atm, with all modes relaxed; do not claim capture or
fertilizer. The saved observable N is moles NO plus NO2 per kg ORIGINAL fresh
feed, not gross circulating mass, nitrate equivalents, or total fixed nitrogen.

The heater delivers the exact same hot composition/state and fresh-flow
throughput as the saved heater, N_h = 1.0314123205327212 mol/kg. Same nominal
heater temperature alone does NOT establish this condition. Recycle entering
the heater, different chemistry, residence, pressure, or mode state can change
both its output and heat demand and leave this class.

Downstream there is no NET addition to this counted species inventory from
other species/external sources. Write net destruction D >= 0. All recycle,
buffers, sorbents, and chemical/internal-mode stores must return to their
initial inventories in a periodic cycle, or have zero accumulation in steady
operation. Incoming loaded product is not free. Exported count N = N_h - D > 0.

Define W consistently as the same heater-input-energy accounting used by the
saved script plus all added energy charged in that metric. It is called 'work'
in the script, but is supplied thermal duty unless an electrical source and
conversion convention are explicitly stipulated. Mixing thermal joules and
shaft/electrical work as identical costs is an additional model convention.
For a physical exergy or electricity objective, rederive W consistently.

Require W >= W_0 = 1503707.8253409273 J/kg. Sufficient source-bookkeeping
conditions for this inequality are: the same fixed heater history/chemical
duty; same equilibrium, unchanged fresh composition entering the heater; only
passive fresh-feed sensible recuperation; maximum fresh-feed inlet temperature
1700 K; no uncounted energy sources or credited output work; all additional
costs nonnegative. Equilibrium fresh-feed heat capacity positive gives
h_in <= h_fresh(1700 K), hence the minimum initial heating duty is the saved
h_fresh(2700 K)-h_fresh(1700 K). The fixed positive chemical heating duty adds
the same amount. At this saved point heater heat removal is zero. Lower inlet
temperature, pinch, leakage, exchanger losses and auxiliary loads can only
increase the counted cost under these conditions.

A cap on TRANSLATIONAL/BATH temperature alone is NOT enough. A nonequilibrium
feed with bath <=1700 K can carry excess vibration, excited species or chemical
energy and have h_in > h_equilibrium_fresh(1700 K). Its eventual complete reset
does not prohibit within-cycle recovery of this energy into the heater.
Either explicitly fix the inlet's complete state or independently prove the
same heater-input floor for the candidate. The mode-state construction in
PARENT_CONTINUATION.txt makes this qualification essential, not cosmetic.

2. PROOF
Let reference retained yield be N_0 = 1.0306033041820177 mol/kg, reference SEC
S_0 = W_0/N_0, and candidate SEC S = W/N. Then
  S >= W_0/(N_h-D) >= W_0/N_h.
Therefore
  (S_0-S)/S_0 <= 1 - N_0/N_h = 0.0007843772413787394.
This holds independently of unknown downstream mode relaxation, transfer,
chemistry partition, or mixing rates, provided they preserve the conditions.
Equality is a favorable envelope requiring D=0 and W=W_0; it is not an
attainability claim.

For W=W_0+C, C>=0, exact strict-win condition is
  C < W_0[(N_h-D)/N_0 - 1]
or equivalently
  D + (N_0/W_0) C < N_h-N_0.
With D=0, C < 1180.400074834962 J/kg. With C=0, D <
0.0008090163507035 mol/kg. The fractional cost allowance C/W_0 is
0.07849929720%, which differs slightly from the SEC-reduction percentage;
the denominators differ. The floor S_min is 1.457911443761 MJ/mol, versus
S_0=1.459055893998 MJ/mol. The 1.18 kJ number is per kg fresh, not per mol.

3. RECYCLE INVENTORY PROOF
For the saved splitter architecture with recycle ratio r and product count
concentration N_p, a steady pass has incoming count N_h+r N_p and outgoing
count (1+r)N_p. If its net destruction is D_pass >=0 (per kg fresh),
  N_h+r N_p-(1+r)N_p=D_pass,
so N_p=N_h-D_pass, regardless of r. Gross hot/cold circulating count cannot
create an additional denominator credit. More generally integrate the species
balance across the external boundary over a complete periodic reset. Internal
recycle cancels. A transient reservoir can produce N>N_h temporarily only by
depletion or previous formation; charge that loading and restore its inventory.
The ideal_recycle.py heater takes ONLY fresh feed; recycle is mixed downstream,
so this cancellation matches that script's source bookkeeping.

4. DECISION-CHANGING EXITS AND NON-EXITS
- Additional net NOx formation below the hot boundary, conversion of a formerly
  uncounted nitrogen species into NO/NO2, or a new heater outlet composition:
  invalidates the retention-only yield ceiling; include formation and its full
  energy/source balance, then rederive. NO <-> NO2 alone preserves this count.
- Different product specification, separation/capture credits or product purity:
  requires a new common contract. Six-species data omit N2O and surfaces.
- Internal-mode/chemical energy: can delay bath heating without changing total
  enthalpy. Storage is not net energy removal. Complete reset prevents free
  permanent storage, but does NOT rule out useful within-cycle work extraction
  or feeding a nonthermal preheater state. Those may invalidate W>=W_0.
- Radiation: exporting it as waste heat does not lower a fixed heater's supplied
  duty. Crediting solar/external radiation, converting emitted radiation into
  useful electricity, or returning energy through modes above the fixed inlet
  state is a different source/recovery model; quantify it, do not silently set
  its gain to zero or assume it is attainable. Radiation material loads remain
  separate from the bath-temperature test.
- Pumps, pressure losses, fans, switching, separation, finite exchanger approach,
  or nonideal heating: count them once on the same energy basis. For illustration
  only, saved reversible isothermal recycle pump costs are 687.07 J/kg at 1%
  pressure rise and 6581.12 J/kg at 10%. The former consumes much, not all, of the
  perfect-retention allowance; the latter exceeds it. Neither pressure loss is
  a measured/admitted minimum, and a reversible estimate is not an actual pump.
- Work-producing expansion, heat engines, heat pumps or hotter preheating can
  change net supplied work and invalidate the assumed floor. Excluding these
  defines the architecture class; it is not a thermodynamic impossibility proof.
- Changed nitrogen chemistry or surface losses may worsen physical yields, but
  cannot be inferred numerically from an omitted reaction mechanism.

5. BENCHMARK IS NOT AN ATTAINABLE COMPETITOR
The ordinary comparator in ideal_recycle.py takes heater composition unchanged
to 1700 K instantaneously, then imposes 10000 K/s cooling to 300 K and credits
ideal fresh-feed preheat to 1700 K. The abrupt quench lacks a qualified device,
finite mixing/transport, complete quench cost and materials solution. The saved
pinch diagnostic is sampled, not a continuum proof of finite-area realizability.
The recycle candidate is similarly deliberately favorable and unqualified.

Thus the theorem bounds retention-only improvement OVER THIS MATHEMATICAL
REFERENCE. It does not prove a real ordinary design can achieve its SEC or
retention; it does not show a candidate unable to beat the ideal reference is
inferior to every physical ordinary option. In particular, a recycle design
might realize quenching when the ideal once-through quench is not realizable,
and be worthwhile despite worse SEC than the ideal number. A strong actual
same-cap ordinary/regenerative competitor must be independently admitted.
The ideal point is not a universal physical lower bound either: it still loses
some NOx, and differently recovered work/new formation can change the envelope.

For an actually admitted comparator (W_B,N_B), replace S_0 by W_B/N_B. Under the
candidate's fixed retention class, the corresponding overhead condition is
  C < W_B*(N_h-D)/N_B - W_0.
Physical comparator losses/costs can materially enlarge this allowance. Until
(W_B,N_B) is qualified, the 1.18 kJ/kg result cannot establish practical exclusion.

6. WHAT CONSTITUTIVE INFORMATION CAN CHANGE THE DECISION?
If the complete inlet state, heater, counting contract and no-work-recovery
class remain fixed, constitutive physics can establish reachability, spatial
material safety, D, and C; it cannot exceed the retention-only envelope. A
necessary test for beating the mathematical point is the linear budget in
section 2. A lower bound on C above the allowance alone excludes that candidate
from beating that point, not from practical utility. Exact mode partitions are
unnecessary for this narrow conditional screen.

Constitutive information IS decision-changing if it can justify: materially
better practical quench admission than ordinary alternatives; extra downstream
NOx formation; a different complete fresh-feed state/energy delivery; a revised
heater output; or useful net work recovery. The parent mode construction only
shows caloric underidentification of bath temperature, not any of those pathways
as an attained process. No arbitrary partition trajectory is required here.
