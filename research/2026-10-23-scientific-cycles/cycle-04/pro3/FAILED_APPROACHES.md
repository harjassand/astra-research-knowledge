# Failures and corrections that affect continuation

## The old path was not the strongest adverse geometry

A symmetric line graph spends one half of its proposal mass on the forward
edge. Its exponent therefore used competition `2 alpha exp(-K)/beta`. This
was a valid lower, not a sharpness theorem. The new symmetric branching tree
has many forward edges and only one parent edge, so aggregate forward mass
approaches one. The path remains in unchanged source, but its coefficient is
superseded as the strongest lower.

## Reverse acceptance needs the per-vertex multiplicity

The initial extension sketch in the contributor/challenger task omitted D in
reverse acceptance. Correct ratios are q_child/q_parent=z/D and
pi_child/pi_parent=n²/D. Reverse acceptance is D n^-2 s, not n^-2 s;
its mixture probability is f/n². Both contributors immediately found this
before the complete proof was written. Every delivered proof and calculation
uses the correction. It is essential for detailed balance.

## Full-state and level exits cannot be interchanged silently

On the tree, global moves to another vertex in the same layer exist. The
contributor's restrictive full-state marked event charges them as competing
exits. The principal instead proves exact lumpability and keeps them as
quotient self-loops. That enables the sharper exit upper in main (11).
Using that upper for an unproved full-state nonself exit would be invalid.

Following only one designated ray would also discard the essential branching
factor. The successful event allows all children. Retention concerns the leaf
union; global moves among leaves remain inside it.

## The norm obstruction did not supply the mixing lower

The incoming exact three-state norm witness has neither the required reachable
trajectory mass nor a retained high-weight leaf. Its induced-norm violation
cannot be converted directly into TV. The new tree supplies those missing
properties. Conversely the sharpness result does not refute the incoming
uniform one-step ceiling: it shows that its exponent is sharp for actual
worst-case mixing, by a different admitted family.

## First-order arguments do not settle the critical prefactor

Fixed strict c<c_* and a growing geometric-sum constant cannot simply be
evaluated at c=c_*. Similarly the main fixed-b,D argument has an O(1) log
capture error. The separate critical-window lower resolves its own errors
using growing D, controlled KL slack and stationarity of the optimal path
length, but supplies no endpoint upper. Adding independent ordinary-tail and
branching lower coefficients would spend the one KL budget twice; the
proved lower is their maximum, not their sum.

## A serialization failure, preserved without changing the cases

The first principal numerical invocation completed all five exact checks and
Decimal laws and wrote its result file. It then failed while serializing a
generated Fraction with more than Python's default 4,300 decimal digits.
The source and partial output are preserved in `evidence/`. The repair sets
a finite 100,000-digit conversion allowance for these generated trusted
integers. No scientific assertion, case, precision target or acceptance rule
was relaxed. Successful 80-digit output is byte-identical to the earlier
partial result. The failed invocation's unrecorded runtime/RSS are unknown.
