1. ACTUAL KILLED MASS AND NORM ORIENTATION

The row-vector convention is consistent throughout. For p=1/(1-theta),
the transformation mu D_theta^(-1), D_theta=diag(q^theta), obeys

 sum_x |mu_x q_x^(-theta)|^p
   = sum_x q_x |mu_x/q_x|^p,

because p theta=p-1. Thus a counting l^p induced row norm really is the
claimed q-relative density norm. The row l^1 norm is the maximum row sum,
and row l^infinity norm is the maximum column sum; neither orientation is
reversed in the proof.

Let A={W<h}, B=A^c, and b=q(A). Every independence proposal from A to B is
accepted, since W_y>=h>W_x. The exact killed G row mass is consequently b,
including its actual self-loop. This is stronger and more useful than merely
bounding a separately normalized or substituted matrix. In particular G_yy<=b
for y in A is valid. The transformed weighted-column estimate

 q_y^(-1) sum_{x in A} q_x G_xy
 =G_yy+sum_{x!=y,x in A} q_x min(1,W_y/W_x) <=2b

has the correct weights. It is not asserting an incorrect counting-column
bound for the raw G. Weighted Jensen then gives b*2^theta as the induced
norm. Explicitly the p-th power factor is (2b)^(p-1)b; taking the p-th root
gives b 2^((p-1)/p)=b2^theta, as claimed. All internal independence moves
and the escape mass are retained. If A is empty the assertion is vacuous;
strict positivity of q makes nonempty A equivalent to b>0.
