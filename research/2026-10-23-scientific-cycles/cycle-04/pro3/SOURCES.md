## Public primary literature

Two relevant primary sources were reopened on 2026-10-21 UTC. This was a
focused mechanism/claim-boundary check, not an exhaustive priority search.

1. Marco Banterle, Clara Grazian, Anthony Lee and Christian P. Robert,
   *Accelerating Metropolis-Hastings algorithms by Delayed Acceptance*,
   arXiv:1503.00996v2, Sections 2.1-2.3 and Lemma 2.
   https://arxiv.org/html/1503.00996v2
   The paper gives the reciprocal-factor delayed-acceptance construction and
   its reversibility, and discusses acceptance/cost and stationary variance
   comparisons. Our local operation is an instance of that established
   factorization. It is not claimed to be a new acceptance algorithm.

2. Chris Sherlock and Anthony Lee, *Variance bounding of delayed-acceptance
   kernels*, arXiv:1706.02142v4.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.02142v4
   This studies conditions under which delayed acceptance inherits variance
   bounding and explains why reduced per-step computation need not preserve
   convergence properties. Those conditions and stationary variance questions
   are distinct from the present proposal-start, support-uniform, forward-KL
   minimax coefficient with a vanishing fixed screen.

The new proof is elementary and self-contained apart from the explicitly
received upper/anchor inputs. A standard layer-quotient fact is proved directly
from constant transition masses; no undocumented lumpability theorem is an
extra dependency. No claim of historical originality follows from these two
literature reads or from the internal acceptance.
