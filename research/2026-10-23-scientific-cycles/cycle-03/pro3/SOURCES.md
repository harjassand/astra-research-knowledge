## Primary literature used to test the novelty and mechanism boundary

The full focused assessment, failed retrievals and observed resources are in
reviews/vanishing_literature.md and its resource JSON. Root reopened the
first, third and fourth sources below and inspected their relevant theorem
passages. No source here proves the new diagonal theorem by citation.

1. Banterle, Grazian, Lee and Robert, *Accelerating Metropolis-Hastings
   algorithms by Delayed Acceptance*, arXiv:1503.00996v2.
   https://arxiv.org/html/1503.00996v2
   Sections 2.1-2.3 supply the established factorized algorithm and the
   conditional spectral/variance comparison. Our unrestricted q ratios do
   not satisfy its common positive factor lower bound.
2. Sherlock and Lee, *Variance Bounding of Delayed-Acceptance Kernels*,
   arXiv:1706.02142v4 / MCAP 24 (2022).
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.02142
   https://doi.org/10.1007/s11009-021-09914-1
   Qualitative conductance inheritance and surrogate-failure examples are
   relevant precedents; their assumptions do not settle this class supremum.
3. Kutri and Scheichl, *Fast-Mixing Markov Chains without Gradients*,
   arXiv:2606.27564v1.
   https://arxiv.org/html/2606.27564v1
   Theorem 3.4 fixes theta=1/2 and uses warm start, strong convexity,
   smoothness, gradient fidelity and inner-chain control. It establishes
   a different mixing guarantee, not the KL-only vanishing-eta result.
4. Andrieu, Lee, Power and Wang, *Comparison of Markov chains via weak
   Poincare inequalities with application to pseudo-marginal MCMC*,
   arXiv:2112.05605v2.
   https://arxiv.org/html/2112.05605v2
   Theorem 8 and Remark 12 clarify the initial-density functional required
   for one L2-to-TV route. Forward KL alone does not bound that functional
   uniformly for dq/dpi across the present finite class.
5. Peres and Sousi, *Mixing times are hitting times of large sets*,
   arXiv:1108.0133v3 / JTP 28 (2015).
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1108.0133
   Theorem 1.1 concerns worst-start hitting of large stationary sets; the
   specified-q-start transport requirement here is different.

The search was focused, not a priority review. No claim of historical
originality, external mathematical acceptance, prompt-method superiority or
practical acceleration is licensed by these comparisons.
