## Load-bearing checks by the principal

The control proof conjugates C by diag(q^e), e=min(eta,1/2), and works
with conjugate counting-measure norms. Q is doubly stochastic because it
is symmetric stochastic. The rank-one comparison has norm one. Thus the
Lp(q) moment grows at most (3/2)^j without support-size or minimum-q
constants. This keeps e fixed; it does not replace e by e^j. The first-hit
measures are unconditioned, and invariance propagates their Lp(pi) bounds
with all returns included. Bounded-test interpolation legitimately avoids
an unavailable uniform L2 density bound. Entropy and the spectral anchor
then give the uniform fixed-eta asymptotic; the lower family and explicit
deterministic inversion supply equality and existence of the time limit.

The representation proof telescopes M=diag(q^eta)^(-1) Q diag(q^eta),
so M^j has the same exponent eta. Its reset uses the actual current law's
mass one, not the mass of a dominating expansion. Its setwise coefficient
(rho_g^j-alpha)/beta, rho_g=1+beta/2, follows from that affine recurrence.
Layer-cake transport, first-hit decomposition and core truncation preserve
all paths. Its proof covers fixed positive eta and correctly declines
uniformity as eta tends to zero.

The positive finite eta=0 examples establish a nonzero obstruction; the
old stronger endpoint evidence is not replaced by a weaker example. Neither
proof asserts off-diagonal TV ordering, a novel acceptance algorithm,
normalizer access, a sampler speedup or an empirical-KL guarantee.
