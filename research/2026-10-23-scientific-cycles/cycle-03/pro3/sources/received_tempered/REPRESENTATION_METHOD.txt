METHOD: Test a representation-changing strategy. Before committing to a long proof or parameter search, spend at most one quarter of your budget seeking two mathematically distinct representations of the obstruction. For each, state the exact map to the original target and what is preserved or lost; test a load-bearing premise with a boundary example or a proof. Choose the representation that removes the obstacle while preserving the target, then personally finish the strongest complete argument it permits. A new name or a sufficient condition with no coverage proof is not a successful reformulation. If this search is unproductive, return to your strongest ordinary method.
