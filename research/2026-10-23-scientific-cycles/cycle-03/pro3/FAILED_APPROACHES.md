# Failures that affect the present continuation

## Retired scientific conjecture: one trap determines the sharp scale

The three-state construction supplies the exact necessary convergence scale
eta log n->infinity. Its critical capture is proportional to exp(-eta log n),
which suggests a coefficient-preserving multiplier one on loglog n/log n.
That suggestion is false as a universal assertion. The admitted growing path
at K=1,alpha=1/2, lambda=1.08 has exponent 0.99429479028...<1, so
log n R_eta(n) diverges. The exact integer separation is preserved. The
single trap remains useful for convergence necessity and the d=0 policy;
it is not used to close the finite-constant frontier.

## Insufficient transport representations

The original power iteration losing eta^j is preserved in Cycle2, but is
superseded for fixed eta by both received proofs. Repeating that estimate
cannot resolve vanishing eta sharply. Even retaining eta while propagating
all first hits to a growing common time leaves a growing moment norm.
The present construction changes the representation: interpolate each
original submeasure's mass decay with its norm, then sum and smooth at
individual remaining horizons. The exact finite bound, not a nicer-looking
asymptotic surrogate, is the accepted result.

The improved upper bound still has loss: its sufficient Lambda_* and the
path's necessary 1/chi do not match. Neither is called an exact threshold.
For K=1,alpha=1/2 they are 3.31290... and 1.08848.... Resolving that gap
requires better transport or a stronger admitted capture mechanism.

## Invalid shortcuts explicitly excluded

- A theorem for each fixed eta cannot be made uniform merely by substituting
  eta_n. The new finite inequality supplies the missing uniformity.
- R_{eta_n}(n) need not be monotone. Accuracy-policy statements are proved
  directly at exp(A/epsilon), holding eta(epsilon) fixed within the run.
- A divergent qL/q ratio is not adverse mixing evidence. The lower result
  instead uses actual path mass, competing exits and retention.
- An absolute waiting-time error could dominate a small capture event.
  The marked-exit construction gives relative timing control.
- Larger local acceptance or a stationary Dirichlet comparison does not
  imply smaller finite-time TV from q.
- Target tables in verifier code do not constitute honest oracle restrictions
  for a recipient who actually possesses those tables. Such a recipient may
  use direct discrete sampling.
