## Primary literature actually inspected

### S1. Kurennoy, Yarullin and Reid

Metropolis-Hastings Dominates Importance Resampling for Policy Composition.
arXiv:2610.03480v1, 2 October 2026.

https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03480v1

Appendix B.5, Theorem 6 and its known-proposal/support discussion were read.
The theorem is a fixed-pool rollout remaining-TV certificate barrier. It is
not automatically the known-positive-K optional-prefix theorem and does not
address the new qL-domination restriction. The source is compared at its
actual information contract, not treated as a generic adaptive impossibility.

### S2. Banterle, Grazian, Lee and Robert

Accelerating Metropolis-Hastings Algorithms by Delayed Acceptance.
arXiv:1503.00996v2; Foundations of Data Science (2019).

https://arxiv.org/html/1503.00996v2

https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/fods.2019005

The reciprocal-factor invariance lemmas, acceptance comparison, stationary
efficiency remarks and geometric-ergodicity counterexample were inspected.
They establish the operation used here as prior art and constrain efficiency
claims. The exact forward-KL uniform proposal-start theorem above is a
separate claim; it is not attributed to this paper.

### S3. Andrieu, Lee, Power and Wang

Comparison of Markov Chains via Weak Poincare Inequalities with Application
to Pseudo-Marginal MCMC. Annals of Statistics 50(6), 3592-3618 (2022).
arXiv:2112.05605v2.

https://arxiv.org/html/2112.05605v2

Theorem 8, the initial-law discussion and the IMH section were inspected.
The L2/initial-functional conditions matter: a forward-KL bound alone need not
control dq/dpi=1/W in the required norm. The new first-hit/density argument is
not supplied by a bare Dirichlet ordering.

### S4. Tierney

A Note on Metropolis-Hastings Kernels for General State Spaces.
Annals of Applied Probability 8(1), 1-9 (1998).

https://doi.org/10.1214/aoap/1027961031

https://www2.stat.duke.edu/~scs/Courses/Stat376/Papers/TransdimMCMC/TierneyAAP1998.pdf

The stationary asymptotic-variance comparison and Proposition 5 mixture
comparison, including the component-density computational caveat, were read.
These do not assert finite-time TV ordering from the proposal start.
