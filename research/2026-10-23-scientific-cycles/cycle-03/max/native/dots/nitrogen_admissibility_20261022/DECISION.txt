DOTS: PHYSICAL ADMISSIBILITY CHECKPOINT, 22 October 2026 AEST

Decision
Park admission of the current inert-surface packed-bed construction. Do not extend
its long trajectories with an arbitrary dispersion or surface coefficient. This is
NOT a physical exclusion of regenerative nitrogen fixation. The broader owned
investigation continues through a lower-contact cooled-product recycle quench,
whose favorable and finite-mixing calculations now execute but show no energy
advantage over a same-temperature-cap ideal once-through reference.
No practical benefit, complete physical process, historical originality, or general
scientific-amplification effect is established.

New load-bearing results
1. Thermal measurements do not identify the missing species mixing law. An explicit
conservative, entropy-producing two-temperature face operator now separates gas
species enthalpy, reduced gas heat flux, and solid heat transport. An LTE total
conductivity leaves species mobility undetermined. Adding that total to gas while
retaining the solid path double-counts transport, even if conservation and entropy
tests pass. The header molar-flow constraint would also need total diffusive species
flux, not just advective mass flow divided by donor molecular weight. The conditional
operator is an integration specification with algebraic tests, not a qualified hot-bed
coefficient or completed stiff trajectory implementation.

2. Passivity and surface reset cannot replace a net-NOx-loss measurement. Two
explicit thermodynamic completions use the same initial gas/solid inventory,
reversible surface stoichiometry, equilibrium constants and finite catalyst inventory,
but different common kinetic scales. In a one-second isolated, energy-coupled cold-end
cell they retain about 100% versus 3.77% NO+NO2. The catalyst resets every reaction;
the adverse completion warms the finite solid/gas only 0.004515 K. Its additional
solid sensible reset rejects 94.066 J/m2. These are NOT bounds on zirconia's actual
kinetics, not a fit to AVCO, not whole-bed predictions, and not 300 K product results.
They disprove the proposed inference from thermodynamics/reset to high retention.

3. Finite grain conduction matters to the correct observable, not automatically to
whole-device viability. Using the existing source's tabulated solid-conductivity
inputs only within their temperature range, 18 selected cold/intermediate grains
were driven by actual saved gas/neighbor histories. A uniformly initialized radial
model gives about 27.75 K surface-versus-mean separation and 2.95-5.41% changes in
one-blow net heat versus a matched lumped control. Mean temperature differs by less
than 0.734 K. This is NOT a periodic grain state, actual material validation, or a
hot-region bound. The independent control reconstructs the original lumped history
within 0.000077 K. A sample-weighted harmonic averaging error was caught, corrected,
and preserved; corrected harmonic heat-amplitude attenuation is 1.93-3.56%, not the
initially reported 1.4-2.0%. No radial trajectory depended on that averaging.

Construction redirected without discarding thermochemical capital
A cold-product recycle loop can mix hot product with its own cooled return before
an indirect recuperator. It avoids assuming direct contact with hot packed grains;
it does NOT remove all heater/mixer/exchanger surface or mixing requirements.
This is an established architecture, not an invention. Primary US3439196A explicitly
describes cool downstream gas recirculation, in a different combustion/MHD process.
No nozzle-only energy recovery is credited: static cooling is reversed by
adiabatic deceleration unless total enthalpy is removed.

Rebuilt favorable contract
One kg of fresh dry 79/21 N2/O2 at 300 K and 1 atm produces one kg net gas at 300 K
and 1 atm. Internal recycled gas is not credited as additional feed or product.
A controlled finite-volume heater has V/mdot = 0.1 m3 s/kg fresh. Six-species,
seven-reaction continuous-NASA chemistry is unchanged. Hot-temperature cases are
2700, 2900 and 3100 K. Instantaneous adiabatic mixing to 1700 K is followed by an
explicit prescribed 10^4 K/s cooldown. All recycle species are solved to a fixed
point; actual formation enthalpy and heater heating/removal are counted. Ideal
fresh-feed preheating is limited by the 1700 K exchanger cap and a sampled pinch
check. Excess heat is rejected without credit. Zero pinch implies an infinite-area
limit, not a feasible finite exchanger. N2O, capture, real electrical conversion,
pressure drops, fan/valve work, apparatus startup and surface kinetics are NOT
qualified. These are favorable gas-process calculations, not finished fertilizer SEC.

Instantaneous reference results
At 2700/2900/3100 K the required recycle is 0.7987/0.9625/1.1277 kg per kg fresh,
and net retained gas NOx is 1.03056/1.31825/1.58260 mol per kg fresh. Positive heater
heat per retained NOx is 1.45911/1.47716/1.57819 MJ/mol, before actual pumping or
conversion losses. The same-cap ideal once-through quench has identical heater
heat and slightly higher retained NOx. There is NO demonstrated energy advantage.
These three cases are not a global optimization across heater designs or temperatures.
The 1-1.5 MJ/mol literature screen is not used as a matched plant comparator.

Finite mixing, not a free instantaneous quench
A constant-pressure entraining gas parcel now couples species, increasing mass,
chemical heat and temperature, with a 300 K return ratio of 0.85. The signed Newton
helper is actually reused in this new receiving operation. At mixing durations of
10/100/1000/10000 microseconds, the full steady composition-return calculation gives
mixer exit temperatures of 1666.46/1682.50/1722.42/1728.62 K and net NOx of
1.03050/1.02787/1.00871/0.92960 mol/kg fresh. Gas recombination heats the mixer beyond
an enthalpy estimate that freezes composition. A 300 microsecond case reaches
1703.10 K; 500 microseconds reaches 1713.23 K. Thus the tested 100 microsecond case
passes the selected exit-temperature cap while 300 microseconds fails. This is a
sampled bracket, not a proof of monotonicity or an experimental mixing-time bound.
All cases beyond the cap are REJECTED as that cold-wall construction, not advertised
with their favorable heat quotient. Raising recycle could change this result and
would require a rebuilt flow/work/volume comparison, not a free parameter credit.
The passing 100 microsecond case has favorable heater heat about 1.48402 MJ/mol,
still not better than the ideal once-through 1.45906 MJ/mol reference. A tighter
numerical tolerance changes its exit temperature by 3.14e-7 K and yield by
2.83e-10 mol/kg; this is a local numerical check, not a formal error bound or an
experimental uncertainty estimate. It cannot qualify unresolved jet micromixing.

It is not directly comparable to these new explicitly 300 K steady gas outputs.
The old 1.130726328 MJ/mol closure and unqualified >=2653 K wall remain unchanged.
