# Failed routes and limits that change continuation

## Balanced inputs cannot supply the desired rate gain

The full colored-Dyck space has dimension `C_m 2^m`; the larger critical type has dimension `binom(n,n/2) 2^(n/2)`. Both have dimension exponent 3/2. Even an exact decoder on their entire space cannot transmit at a larger asymptotic rate. The new balanced filter provides useful interference and terminal-return structure, but the gain target requires leaving this restriction.

The explicit dark-pair family has dimension m+1 and constant accepted probability alpha^4. Its rate tends to zero. The finite maximal-singular multiplicities 2,3,4 do not prove exhaustiveness at all lengths. Accepted outcomes may not be credited while discarding failures.

## Raw finite ranks and exact codes do not decide reliable capacity

The exact two-call subspace obstruction is proved, but it coexists with the new asymptotic approximate capacity floor. The three-call modular rank 626/729 is only a lower bound at the tested points. The rank-one-kernel method stopping at residual dimension eight is not an eight-dimensional code. Those probes were stopped when they failed to close the larger claim.

Operator Schmidt ranks five and six exclude a one-use local gauge equivalence only. A perturbed finite block's enlarged Kraus rank is not a reliable communication code or a capacity theorem.

## Diagonal high-entropy bias does not preserve the compression premise

For q<1/2, the exact reduced depth walk has positive drift. Any sublinear-depth coordinate cutoff receives exponentially vanishing product-averaged mass, and type domination gives the same conclusion for the matching typical composition. The critical proof cannot be extended by changing proportions alone. This says nothing conclusive about non-coordinate compression, selected coherent codes, or correction with an extensive environment.

## Stronger product stationarity is impossible at the desired input entropy

For alpha!=0, exact product stationarity under V_s is equivalent to that under U. Color blocks force geometric depth masses with ratio (1-q)/q. Normality requires q>1/2, hence H(rho)<3/2 even with coherent port entries. The next operation must address the reduced bath and its correlations; it cannot assume a high-entropy invariant product.

## Why the compression repairs are load bearing

An input failure projector can have average 1/D and worst failure one. Expurgation is needed before claiming complete error. A maximally entangled rank-one joint effect has norm one but averaged bath effect I/r^2; the bath-dimension factors cannot be dropped. The projected channel is not TP without auxiliary normalization. Applying that normalizer physically would change the encoder; the actual encoder is an isometry and its difference from the auxiliary map is charged to the error.

The actual normal initializer may have arbitrarily slow tails. Its trace-distance comparison is made on the normalized true channel, outside the type-probability division. Multiplying its uncontrolled tail by sqrt(n) would not be justified. The new common-code theorem is pointwise in each fixed initializer; it is not uniform over all normal densities at each n.

## Marginal return is not a reusable complete process

One stationary shared bath bit controlling Z on every carrier produces dephasing on each isolated carrier but correlated repeated dephasing. It preserves the bath marginal and still fails to produce the product channel. The exported erasure interface works because its identity holds on every joint logical--bath input, including references. Its full retained syndrome process is not asserted to be IID.

## Continuation limits

The explicit coherent input's entropy exceeds 3/2; this is not a capacity lower bound. A zero one-step drift is not normal stationarity, and every finite reflecting truncation has stationary states irrespective of infinite-bath escape. A genuine infinite-space tail/information estimate and a deterministic reference-uniform code are still needed. If an invariant initializer differs from tau_pi, counted reachability is an additional obligation.

No method here chooses s with the horizon, hides fresh bath cells, replaces the coherent channel by a classical mixture, claims terminal return for the perturbed capacity code, or uses success-only accounting.
