2. COMMON DOUBLY-STOCHASTIC GRAM TRANSPORT

Theorem A. Suppose e satisfies (1). For another orthonormal basis f, suppose
one DOUBLY STOCHASTIC q-by-q matrix B and a number kappa>=0 satisfy
 G_f <= B^T G_e B+kappa I as real quadratic forms.            (2)
Then every X>=0 diagonal in f, Tr X^2=1, satisfies
 m sum_(a,i)||E_(a,i)X||_HS^2 <= S(X^2)+r C_D+kappa.          (3)
The kernels K_f,S may be nonprojective and mutually noncommuting.

Proof. A finite doubly stochastic matrix is a convex combination of permutation
matrices. Here is the precise finite argument used. Its positive support has
a matching covering the rows: any row set has mass equal to its cardinality,
supported on neighboring columns of total capacity at most their cardinality,
so Hall's condition holds. Select a supported permutation pi, put
alpha=min_j B[j,pi(j)]>0, subtract alpha P_pi from B, and, if alpha<1,
renormalize by 1-alpha. The remainder
is doubly stochastic with fewer positive entries. Recursion terminates. Thus
B=sum_l w_l P_l, w_l>=0, sum_l w_l=1. Hall is also the elementary special
case of the self-contained vector-Hall proof in the prior native source.

For positive X=V_f c the norm of c is one and S(X^2)=H(c^2). Positivity of G_e
and quadratic convexity give
 c^T G_f c <=(Bc)^T G_e(Bc)+kappa
             <=sum_l w_l (P_l c)^T G_e(P_l c)+kappa
             <=sum_l w_l[H((P_l c)^2)+r C_D]+kappa
             =H(c^2)+r C_D+kappa.
This proves (3). Crucially Bc need not be normalized and its squared entries
are not assumed to have entropy H(c^2). Equation (1) is applied only to the
unit-norm permutations P_l c. One common B is used for ALL task prefixes. QED.

This is a reusable sufficient condition on the TOTAL common Gram matrix,
not an assertion that arbitrary quantum eigenbases have such a certificate.
The same proof allows a convex mixture sum_s v_s B_s^T G_e B_s on the right
of (2), with v_s>=0, sum v_s=1 and every B_s doubly stochastic: apply the
proved bound to each term before averaging. No spectral entropy is charged
more than once. This is the common-congruence cone chosen for the next step.
For finite explicit matrices a rigorously bounded largest eigenvalue of
G_f-B^T G_e B supplies kappa. Materializing these dense matrices uses Theta(q^2)
storage, exponential in n; no lower bound on every possible verification
algorithm is asserted. A uniformly useful
theorem needs a structural certificate, as the next two sections provide.

For any nonempty proper erasure subset S, tracing one erased site kills

Two such supports either coincide or are disjoint: one coordinate fixes the
common left multiplier. Equal-support labels form a cell of size D^(|S|+1)

cell. This follows also by summing the Hadamard phase labels to remove all
cross terms and summing free a-lists to produce identity on S. Full erasure
averages all labels and empty erasure is identity.

meets the premise with h_a<=h, even with arbitrary non-Clifford gates.

Proof. Ad(U*)E_ai Ad(U) is the HS orthogonal projection onto Ad(U*)B_ai.
By (9) its range lies in that of E_a,max(i-h_a,0). Orthogonal projection
contraction gives
 p_ai(U XU*)<=p_a,max(i-h_a,0)(X), p_a0=1.                  (11)
Also p_aN is INVARIANT, since full task trace commutes with a task-product
unitary. Put h'=min(h_a,N-1). Summing (11) only for i<N and retaining the

 <=h'-sum_(j=N-h')^(N-1) p_aj(X)<=h'.                      (12)
Entropy is invariant under conjugation. Sum over tasks to prove (10). A
