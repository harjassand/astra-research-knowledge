SUPPORT INTEGRATION CHECK — PASS
22 October 2026

Scope: Section 8 first paragraph, with the coefficient-matrix and Gram
definitions in Section 1 and Theorem A in Section 2. No numerical checks.

The claimed passage is correct for every fixed basis f with a uniform
positive-diagonal certificate (3), using that certificate's same kappa.

1. Conjugation is in the correct place. With the standard coefficient-matrix
   convention, |f_j>_A |conjugate(f_j)>_B has matrix Pi_j = f_j f_j^*.
   Therefore every normalized vector in this support has
       X = sum_j c_j Pi_j = U diag(c) U^*,  sum_j |c_j|^2 = 1.
   It is normal, X^*X = sum_j |c_j|^2 Pi_j, and its entanglement entropy is
   H(|c|^2). Replacing c by |c| yields a positive matrix without changing
   normalization or entropy. Replacing conjugate(f_j) by f_j would not in
   general give this conclusion; the stated conjugated support avoids that
   problem.

2. The phase comparison has the correct orientation. Each actual K_f,S is
   real symmetric and entrywise nonnegative, since its entries are positive
   multiples of Tr(A_j A_k) for positive partial traces A_j,A_k. Consequently
       c^* K c = sum_jk K_jk Re(conjugate(c_j)c_k)
               <= sum_jk K_jk |c_j||c_k|.
   Thus each actual Bell-prefix score of X is bounded above by that of
   V_f |c|. Theorem A applies to the latter normalized positive matrix and
   gives S(X^*X) >= m sum_(a,i) p_ai(X) - r C_D - kappa.
   Its hypothesis as a real quadratic-form inequality suffices, since the
   theorem is invoked only after taking entrywise absolute amplitudes.

3. The ensemble quantifier is correct. If rho is supported on this span,
   every positive-weight vector in any pure decomposition of rho lies in
   the span: the expectation of its orthogonal-complement projection is a
   sum of nonnegative terms and equals zero. For a general measure-valued
   ensemble the statement holds almost everywhere, which is sufficient.
   The preceding inequality therefore applies to every relevant ensemble
   vector. Bell-prefix probabilities are linear functionals of the physical
   density operator, so averaging and then taking the convex-roof infimum
   yields the genuine witness
       E_F(rho) >= m sum_(a,i) p_ai(rho) - r C_D - kappa.
   One favorable ensemble alone would indeed be insufficient, as Section 8
   correctly notes.

No transpose, phase, normalization, or support/ensemble gap was found in
this bounded integration passage. This check establishes the passage for
the certified supports; it makes no assertion about arbitrary supports or
additivity of E_F.
