Surviving mathematical result
----------------------------
PROOF.txt establishes the unchanged critical tensor bound for arbitrary
spectra in eigenbases obtained by arbitrary unitary rotations within
computational-string blocks whose strings differ at the first site of EVERY
task. It covers globally entangled nonstabilizer vectors and arbitrarily
nonlocal rotations, with no gate-count or depth premise. The common
doubly-stochastic matrix B and one Birkhoff decomposition give simultaneous
score domination. The argument never normalizes Bc or substitutes its entropy.

The exact rational r=2,N=2 example has nondegenerate full-rank spectrum,
unequal-amplitude entangled eigenvectors and
[K_(task1,1),K_(task2,1)]_(0,2)=1705728024/377149515625 !=0.
Its actual Gram commutator Frobenius norm is 0.21299144007943363. Thus the
class extends beyond the earlier stabilizer and projection-kernel arguments.

Analytical exclusion
--------------------

Actual finite tests
-------------------
check_actual.py directly forms physical matrices X>=0 and traces their
entries. Twelve rotated-complement tests (N=1,2,3,4; spectra are stock mixture,
coherent stock amplitude square, and full-rank Dirichlet) verified the erasure
identity with maximum score discrepancy 2.22e-16. The matrices have dimension
at most256. One exact rational noncommuting example is separately replayed.

Outside the theorem class, deterministic layered non-Clifford intertask
circuits were tested at N=2,3 with depths N and2N. A small, explicitly bounded
L-BFGS spectrum optimization used two stock starts per circuit, max80
iterations each. Four reached the iteration cap; that failure is retained in
RESULT.json. The largest observed defect was0.8343895492449037, below the
unchanged two-task theorem constant2(2-ln2)=2.613705638880109. No global
optimization claim follows. The optimizers generally collapsed towards low
effective rank, so their absence of a growing gap is a weak negative search
result, not evidence against a high-rank divergent sequence.

Two direct N=4,depth4 circuit checks used the actual high-rank stock spectra;
their defects were -1.1477317474924158 and -0.7724712484929426. These specific
families did not supply a counterexample. This is a preserved failed route.

-------------------
