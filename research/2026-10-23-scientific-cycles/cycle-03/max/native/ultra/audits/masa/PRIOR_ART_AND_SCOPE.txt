https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0003070 (v1, 17 March 2000).
Theorem 1 / Section 3 identify complete maximally entangled bases with
orthogonal unitary bases. Definition 8 and Proposition 9, pp.17-18, give the
shift-and-multiply construction from a Latin square and Hadamard data. The
ell=2 case of (B1) is therefore standard machinery, and no new-basis claim is
made. Werner explicitly permits arbitrary group multiplication tables as
Latin squares and discusses the much wider construction space. The relevant
new obligation here is simultaneous erasure-cell refinement (B4), arbitrary
spectra, and the critical forest entropy bound, not inventing Hadamard bases.
