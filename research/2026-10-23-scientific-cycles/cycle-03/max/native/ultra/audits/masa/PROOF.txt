INVARIANT DIAGONAL ALGEBRAS: EXACT PARTITIONS AND NONLINEAR COORDINATES
22 October 2026 | bounded Cycle 2 contribution | internal proof candidate

This note extends the existing restricted critical tensor witness. It does
not claim the unrestricted witness or a general iid constant-defect law.
All logs are natural. H has n physical sites of dimension D, partitioned into
r ordered tasks of N sites, n=rN. Ordinary Hilbert-Schmidt norm is used.
For S a set of sites, E_S(X)=I_S/D^|S| tensor Tr_S X.
An invariant MASA is A=span{Pi_j}, Pi_j=|e_j><e_j| rank one, such that E_S(A)
is contained in A for each specified S. The old proof already covers global
field-stabilizer eigenbases. No generic positive matrix is put in this class.

A. EXACT CLASSIFICATION OF EACH INVARIANT COMPRESSION

Proposition A1. If E_S preserves A, its restriction is uniform averaging on
a partition C_S of the basis labels. For any cell C and any j in C,

 P_C:=sum_(k in C) Pi_k=I_S tensor Q_C,
 Tr_S Pi_j=Q_C/k_C,  k_C=rank Q_C,
 |C|=D^|S| k_C,  1<=k_C<=D^|S|.                         (A1)

Proof. E_S|A is a trace-preserving conditional expectation onto the unital
commutative subalgebra A intersect (I_S tensor B(H_complement)). Every unital
subalgebra of the diagonal algebra consists of functions constant on a
partition. Orthogonal projection in counting l2 is uniform cell averaging,
so E_S(Pi_j)=P_C/|C|. Each P_C belongs to the range algebra and is a projection,
hence has form I_S tensor Q_C with Q_C a projection. Comparing with
I_S/D^|S| tensor Tr_S Pi_j proves (A1). The last upper bound is the Schmidt
rank bound for the pure vector e_j across S versus its complement. QED.

If the specified family is closed under unions, its restrictions satisfy
K_S K_T=K_(S union T). Thus the partitions have exact commuting-square
intersection identities. If C in C_S and B in C_T lie within the same cell U
of C_(S union T), then

 |C intersect B|=|C||B|/|U|;                               (A2)

otherwise their intersection is empty. To check (A2), the jk entry of K_SK_T
is |C_S(j) intersect C_T(k)|/(|C_S(j)||C_T(k)|), while the entry of K_union is
1/|U| for j,k in U. This is genuine shared-dilation information. Individual
rank envelopes alone do not imply it. Neither (A1) nor (A2) alone is asserted
to imply the full common-coordinate domination needed below.

Proposition A2 (two full task erasures). On H_A tensor H_B with both local
dimensions q, a MASA invariant under E_A and E_B has the following exact form.
For one common divisor k of q there are decompositions
 H_A=directsum_(a=1)^(q/k) R_a, H_B=directsum_(b=1)^(q/k) Q_b,
with every R_a and Q_b of dimension k. In each R_a tensor Q_b its basis is
an arbitrary complete maximally entangled basis of k^2 vectors. Conversely
every basis with this form has invariant E_A,E_B.

Proof. Proposition A1 makes the E_A cells I_A tensor Q_b and the E_B cells
R_a tensor I_B for orthogonal decompositions of B and A respectively. Their
product is R_a tensor Q_b and is diagonal in the common MASA, so it is the
span of exactly dim(R_a)dim(Q_b) basis vectors. In particular EVERY pair of
blocks intersects. Each such vector has flat reductions on the full R_a and
Q_b by (A1). A pure vector's two nonzero Schmidt spectra coincide, forcing
dim R_a=dim Q_b. Since every pair intersects, one common value k works for
all a,b and divides q. The vectors are consequently maximally entangled in
the indicated k by k block. The converse follows by summing each complete
maximally entangled block basis: E_A Pi_j=I_A/q tensor Q_b/k is diagonal,
and similarly for E_B. QED.

For q=D^N and prime D, k is D^h. This classification is stronger than merely
bounding cell sizes. It supplies only the two FULL-task partitions: it does
not classify or simultaneously control all intermediate prefixes. That is
an explicit remaining obstruction, not an asserted solution of all invariant
MASAs.

B. AN EXACT NONLINEAR COORDINATE EXTENSION

Let G be ANY finite group of order D; no commutativity or field structure is
assumed. For every relative tuple a=(a_2,...,a_ell), choose INDEPENDENTLY
any complex Hadamard h^(a)_c(g), indexed by c in a D-element set C and g in G:
every entry has modulus one and
sum_g conjugate(h^(a)_c(g))h^(a)_(c')(g)=D delta_(c,c').
There is NO requirement that Hadamards agree for different a tuples.
For any ell>=2 define a complete orthonormal basis of ell physical D-sites:

 e_(a_2,...,a_ell,c)
 =D^(-1/2) sum_(g in G) h^(a)_c(g)|g,g a_2,...,g a_ell>.          (B1)

Distinct a tuples have disjoint computational supports; Hadamard row
orthogonality distinguishes c on a fixed support. Thus (B1) is genuinely a
complete orthonormal basis of D^ell vectors. It may have arbitrary ell, with
no circuit-depth or bounded interaction-diameter assumption.

Theorem B1. Every erasure subset S preserves the MASA of (B1). There is ONE
bijection of the D^ell spectral labels with G^ell such that for every S the
true erasure conditional expectation is a further projection of ordinary
S-coordinate averaging. Therefore, simultaneously for every diagonal X,

 ||E_S X||_HS^2 <= ||A_S f||_2^2,                           (B2)

where f is the eigenvalue function in the new coordinates and A_S averages
precisely the coordinates in S. The identity holds also for arbitrary
complex diagonal coefficients, not just positive X.

Proof. Put a_1=e_G. For nonempty proper S, tracing over ANY erased site kills
all cross terms g!=g', because the map g->g a_j is injective. The resulting
reduction is uniform on the D computational tuples
 V_(a,S)={(g a_j)_(j notin S):g in G}.
Two such supports are either equal or disjoint. Indeed, equality of one
remaining coordinate fixes a common left multiplier h between the two a
lists, and the other remaining coordinates either respect that multiplier
or forbid every intersection. Thus equal-support labels form an erasure cell.
There are D^|S| possible a lists with the same support (if site 1 remains,
all remaining a's are fixed and the erased a's are free; if site 1 is erased,
there is additionally one free common left multiplier and |S|-1 erased a's).
The phase label c is also free. The cell size is D^(|S|+1), and

 E_S Pi_(a,c)=D^(-|S|-1) sum_(a',c' in same cell) Pi_(a',c'). (B3)

One way to check the cell projector in (B3) is to sum the D phase labels:
Hadamard completeness separately for each a
removes all g cross terms; summing the allowed a'
then yields exactly I_S tensor projector(V_(a,S)). Full erasure is global
averaging, and empty erasure is identity.

Choose ANY bijection phi:C->G. Relabel by
 y_1=phi(c), y_j=y_1 a_j for j>=2.                          (B4)
This is invertible: c=phi^(-1)(y_1), a_j=y_1^(-1)y_j. If all y coordinates
outside S are held fixed and the coordinates in S vary, every resulting
label stays in the same erasure cell. If 1 is outside S this is immediate;
if 1 is in S, the remaining a'_j are obtained from a_j by the common left
multiplier y'_1^(-1)y_1. Thus each ordinary S-coordinate fiber lies inside
a true erasure cell. Both are counting-measure conditional expectations,
so K_S A_S=K_S and the projection contraction gives (B2). QED.

Theorem B2 (critical tensor witness beyond linear stabilizer labels).
Partition ALL physical sites into arbitrary disjoint blocks. On each block
choose any basis (B1), permitting different groups of the same order D,
arbitrary tuple-dependent Hadamards, and arbitrary block sizes; singleton blocks may use any
one-site basis. The blocks may cross task boundaries and span unbounded
numbers of sites. If X>=0 is diagonal in the resulting tensor-product basis
and Tr X^2=1, then

 S(X^2)>=(ln D) sum_(a=1)^r sum_(i=1)^N
                    ||E_(task a,prefix i)X||_HS^2-r C_D.    (B5)

Here C_D is EXACTLY the inherited one-chain constant, e.g.
min{2D-lnD, lnD/(1-D^(-1/2))^2}; no new additive optimization is claimed.
The spectrum of X is arbitrary and globally correlated across all blocks.

Proof. Take the tensor product of the blockwise bijections (B4), assigning
one independent D-valued label coordinate to each physical site. Physical
erasure factorizes across the blocks, and (B2) gives simultaneous domination
by averaging those same site coordinates for every subset, hence every task
prefix. Let p(y)=f(y)^2, where f>=0 are the diagonal entries of X in these
coordinates. Then H(p)=S(X^2). For each task a hold all other task coordinates
fixed, apply the inherited scalar single-chain lemma to each normalized
slice, and average. This gives

 (lnD) sum_i ||A_(a,i) f||_2^2<=H(Y_a|Y_other tasks)+C_D.

Finally sum_a H(Y_a|Y_other tasks)<=H(Y_1,...,Y_r) by the classical chain
rule and conditioning. Combining the simultaneous projection domination
proves (B5). This is a classical conditioning step after an explicit nonlinear
coordinate construction; it is NOT a quantum conditional-entropy assumption.
QED.

The same mixed-state support corollary as in the prior cycle follows for
V=span{|e_j>_A tensor |conjugate(e_j)>_B}. Every ensemble vector of a density
matrix supported on V has a diagonal coefficient matrix; its polar moduli
remain in this MASA, and the accepted total-score polar reduction applies.
Taking the convex roof gives the corresponding r C_D E_F witness. Generic
positive matrices and generic approximate iid dilution outputs need not have
this support. No unrestricted operational E_C improvement follows.

C. WHY THIS IS NOT JUST A FIELD-LINEAR LABEL ARGUMENT

Take ell=3. The nontrivial erasure cell labels in (B1) are, up to naming,
 a, b, a^(-1)b; the phase label c is free. Their first two jointly determine
the third, and every pair is uniform independent under uniform basis labels.
For a global field-linear stabilizer basis with the same cell cardinalities,
these three cell labels would be PAIRWISE independent nonzero linear forms, with the
third a linear combination of the first two (because it is their function).
They would therefore present a Latin square isotopic to abelian addition.
A nonabelian group table cannot have such an isotope. Explicitly, suppose
bijections f,g,h:G->A into an abelian group obey
 h(a^(-1)b)=f(a)+g(b).
Setting b=a gives g(a)=h(e)-f(a), and then setting b=at gives
 f(at)-f(a)=h(e)-h(t), independent of a. Consequently f(t)-f(e) is an
injective homomorphism G->A, forcing G abelian, a contradiction.

For G the dihedral group of order 8, this excludes a realization of these
three invariant partitions as field-linear erasure partitions in the prior
F_8 stabilizer model, even after arbitrary spectral-label permutation. It
is a precise partition obstruction, not an unproved assertion that an
individual basis vector is not locally equivalent to some GHZ vector. Each
individual vector in (B1) is of GHZ type; the simultaneous basis and its
three partition relations carry the nonabelian information.

For G=S_3 of order 6, the replay below checks actual finite quantum matrices.
Dimension 6 is already outside the prior prime/prime-power FIELD statement.
One may use the same proof with the dihedral order-8 group without dense
matrices. The proof permits independently chosen Hadamards for every relative
tuple a and is not based on a claim that their rows form group characters.

There is also a fixed-prime-D extension: at D=2 take G=Z_2 and set
 e_(a,c)=(|0,a_2,...,a_ell>
        +(-1)^c exp(i theta(a))|1,1+a_2,...,1+a_ell>)/sqrt(2).
The function theta(a) is ARBITRARY, including functions of all ell-1 relative
bits. These are valid tuple-dependent Hadamards. Every nonempty erasure
removes the cross term and has the identical partitions as untwisted GHZ,
so the unchanged critical defect survives arbitrary global support-dependent
phase twists and unbounded ell. Taking theta(a_0)=pi/4 makes that rank-one
eigenvector non-Pauli-stabilizer, because its two nonzero computational
amplitudes have relative phase exp(i pi/4), whereas a qubit stabilizer vector
on two-point support has relative phase in {1,-1,i,-i}. No claim is made here
that every such basis is inequivalent under all product local rotations;
the nonabelian partition argument above supplies a stronger simultaneous
partition distinction. This fixed-D class nevertheless exceeds the actual
Pauli-stabilizer eigenbasis hypothesis and imposes no bounded-depth phase-circuit
premise.

D. REMAINING GAP / RECEIVING-USE INSTRUCTION

Theorem B2 solves a broad exactly certifiable invariant class and supplies
unbounded-range, nonlinear group examples with the original critical defect.
It does NOT prove (B5) for ALL invariant MASAs. A general invariant MASA has
(A1),(A2), but no known common coordinate-fiber refinement has been obtained
here. Nor is such a refinement asserted necessary for the witness itself.
A useful next operation is to classify intermediate-prefix refinements of
Proposition A2, or identify an invariant MASA whose erasure partitions have
no common D-coordinate domination and then prove its witness by a different
entropy operation. Merely trying scalar rank envelopes again discards (A2).

For integration, use (A1),(A2) as exact necessary certificate checks; use
(B4) as a complete common-coordinate certificate on group-GHZ blocks; then
reuse the prior scalar one-chain lemma and classical conditioning verbatim.
Charge construction of the dense basis exponentially if one actually forms
it. The theorem is analytic and gives no efficient state-preparation or
experimental certification claim. Internal correctness remains distinct
from historical novelty, physical usefulness and implementation advantage.
