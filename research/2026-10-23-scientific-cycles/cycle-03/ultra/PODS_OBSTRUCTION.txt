THE PREFIX-ERASURE GENERATOR HAS NO PODS BASIS
22 October 2026. Principal-owner structural lemma.

The focused primary-source update is consequential: Dong et al.,
https://arxiv.org/html/2606.17729v3, prove tensorization results under a
positive off-diagonal scaling (PODS) premise. Fang,
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00187, uses suitable qubit semigroups for
two-qubit entanglement-cost results. These are existing research results,
not results attributed to Astra. They cannot simply be applied to the
prefix-erasure hierarchy. The missing premise is false in EVERY basis,
not merely unverified in its computational coordinates.

THEOREM. Let H=(C^D)^tensor N, D>=2, N>=2. For i=1,...,N define
 E_i(X)=I_(first i)/D^i tensor Tr_(first i) X.
Let a_i>0 and let the positive QMS generator be
 L=sum_(i=1)^N a_i (id-E_i).
There is no orthonormal basis f of H such that exp(-tL) preserves its
diagonal algebra and scales EACH off-diagonal matrix unit
 |f_j><f_k|, j!=k, by a scalar for every t>=0.
In particular the semigroup is not PODS in any basis. The conclusion
already excludes arbitrary scalar scaling; positivity of the scalars
cannot repair it.

PROOF. Use ordinary Hilbert-Schmidt operator space. E_0=id and the E_i
are nested orthogonal projections:
 E_i E_j=E_max(i,j), ran E_i=I_(D^i) tensor M_(D^(N-i)).
The orthogonal difference spaces
 V_k=ran E_k intersect ker E_(k+1), 0<=k<N,
and V_N=ran E_N partition the entire operator space. They are all
nonzero, since dim ran E_k=D^(2(N-k)) strictly decreases. On V_k,
 L=r_k id, r_k=sum_(i=k+1)^N a_i,
with r_N=0. Strict positivity of every a_i makes all r_k distinct.
Thus E_1 is a real polynomial in L: it is the spectral projection onto
the eigenvalues r_1,...,r_N, omitting r_0. This also follows by ordinary
Lagrange interpolation on the N+1 distinct eigenvalues.

Suppose the claimed basis exists. Differentiating the finite-dimensional
semigroup at t=0 shows that every off-diagonal unit F_jk=|f_j><f_k|
is an eigenoperator of L and the diagonal algebra A_f is invariant under
L. The polynomial identity shows A_f is invariant under E_1, and each
F_jk is an eigenoperator of E_1. Because E_1 is a projection, its scalar
eigenvalue must be zero or one.

It cannot be one: every nonzero element of ran E_1 has form I_D tensor Y
and therefore rank D rank(Y)>=D, whereas F_jk has rank one. Consequently
E_1 annihilates EVERY off-diagonal unit. Expanding any operator in these
units and the diagonal units now gives ran E_1 subset A_f. This is
impossible: A_f is commutative, but ran E_1=I_D tensor M_(D^(N-1)) is
noncommutative since D^(N-1)>=2. Contradiction. QED.

The semigroup really is a QMS: exp[-t a_i(id-E_i)] equals
e^(-ta_i)id+(1-e^(-ta_i))E_i, a completely positive unital map, and
these maps commute. The fixed algebra of the full hierarchy is scalar,
so its full-rank stationary state is the normalized identity. None of
these properties supplies PODS. For N=1 the noncommutative-range step
fails, and ordinary depolarization is PODS; that boundary is essential.

CONSEQUENCE AND REMAINING GAP. This proves a basis-independent obstruction
to a DIRECT invocation of the cited PODS tensorization for this weighted
hierarchy. It does not refute a log-Sobolev inequality, a different
comparison semigroup, the generic critical entropy witness, or any theorem
in the cited papers. A comparison with another generator must prove and
charge its Dirichlet/entropy comparison constants; the cited theorems alone
do not supply the required critical coefficient. The mixed two-qubit
formation result does not assert additivity for a correlated many-output
stock reservoir. No noisy-output programme is resumed by this premise audit.

This proof is finite algebra and uses no numerical experiment. Root
developed it after reading the primary sources, and a separate agent
audited the spectral-projector/rank contradiction. The primary paper's
Definition 2.11 and Theorems 1.2--1.3 were directly checked: their PODS
quantifiers require exactly the common basis excluded above.
