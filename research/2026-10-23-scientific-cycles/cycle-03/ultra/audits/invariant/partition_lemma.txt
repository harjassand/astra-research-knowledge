CLASSICAL PARTITION OBSTRUCTIONS TO COMMON COORDINATE REFINEMENT
Owner: narrow lemma subagent /root/cycle3_masa_entropy/classical_partition_lemma
Date: 2026-10-22 (Australia/Brisbane)
Scope: actual finite classical partitions, not asserted quantum realizations.

QUESTION AND PRECISE CERTIFICATE
Let Omega have D^n equiprobable points. K_S is averaging within a homogeneous
partition, with K_empty=I, K_full=global averaging, and K_S K_T=K_(S union T).
A common D-coordinate fiber refinement means a bijection
 Omega ~= {0,...,D-1}^n
such that every coordinate-S fiber lies within a K_S partition cell. If E_S is
ordinary coordinate erasure, the certificate is K_S E_S=K_S (equivalently,
range K_S is contained in range E_S).

RESULT
The listed commutation, size/divisibility assumptions do not imply that
certificate. Even adding complement cut-duality k_S=k_(S complement) does not
imply it. Two independent arguments follow: a small explicit exhaustive
obstruction without duality; and an exact counting existence theorem WITH
duality, already for n=3 and prime D=107. Neither is an entropy-witness
counterexample. Neither establishes existence of a compatible quantum basis.

1. SMALL EXPLICIT OBSTRUCTION (DUALITY NOT SATISFIED)
Put D=4,n=3. Take sixteen row permutations g_r of {0,1,2,3}; repetitions are
allowed, and take Omega={(r,b):r=1,...,16,b=0,...,3}. Let K1 condition on r,
K2 condition on b, and K3 condition on c=g_r(b). A K1 cell has size4; a K2 or
K3 cell has size16.

In lexicographic permutation order 0123,0132,0213,0231,0312,0321,1023,1032,
1203,1230,1302,1320,2013,2031,2103,2130,2301,2310,3012,3021,3102,3120,
3201,3210, use the multiplicity vector
 (1,0,1,0,2,0,2,0,0,0,1,1,0,2,0,2,0,0,0,0,1,0,2,1).
Its sum is16. For every b,c exactly four row permutations send b to c.
Thus the three partition statistics r,b,c are pairwise independent. For
conditional expectations this implies K_i K_j=global averaging for i!=j.
Define all K_S with |S|>=2 to be global averaging. The semilattice law follows.
The stipulated cell-size formula holds: k_1=1,k_2=k_3=4; all k_ij=4.
However k_1!=k_23, so this example is deliberately NOT a cut-dual example.

Suppose common coordinate fibers existed, with coordinates x,y,z corresponding
to 1,2,3. Since K1 cells have exactly D points, they equal coordinate-1 fibers;
therefore rows r correspond bijectively to pairs (y,z). Since b is constant
on coordinate-2 fibers, b=h_z(x). On each row b takes all four values exactly
once, so every h_z is a permutation. Similarly c=k_y(x), with every k_y a
permutation. Consequently the row-permutation multiset MUST have the form
 {k_y h_z^{-1}: y,z=0,...,3}.

The accompanying exact-integer replay exhausts this finite factorization.
Normalize h_0=id by replacing every h_z,k_y with h_z h_0^{-1},k_y h_0^{-1}.
Then B={k_y:y=0,...,3} is a four-element submultiset of the specified row
multiset C. There are exactly565 possible B. For each B enumerate its24 right
translates B h^{-1}; ask whether three such translates sum to C-B. An exact
meet-in-the-middle search over all pair sums finds none. This exhausts all
factorizations, including repetitions in h and k. The proof of impossibility
here is finite exhaustive enumeration, not a claimed shorter analytic identity.

2. COMPLEMENT-DUAL OBSTRUCTION: EXACT COUNTING EXISTENCE THEOREM
For D=107 let q=floor(D/2)=53. We construct many integer arrays c_abc,
0<=a,b,c<D, with every two-coordinate marginal equal to D:
 sum_c c_abc=D, sum_b c_abc=D, sum_a c_abc=D.
Begin with every entry1. Pair coordinates (0,1),(2,3),...,(104,105), leaving106
unpaired. On each of the q^3 disjoint 2x2x2 cubes, independently choose a
parameter t in {-1,0,1}; replace its entries by
 1 + t (-1)^(alpha+beta+gamma), alpha,beta,gamma in {0,1}.
Every entry remains in {0,1,2}. Summing the perturbation along any cube
coordinate gives zero. Thus all two-coordinate marginals remain D. Distinct
parameter choices give distinct arrays, for exactly 3^(q^3) arrays.

For each array construct the ACTUAL finite uniform space
 Omega_c={(a,b,c,l): 1<=l<=c_abc};
there are D^3 points. Let K1,K2,K3 condition respectively on a,b,c. Every
singleton partition has D cells of size D^2, and pairwise statistics are
independent, since each pair of values has D points. Hence K_i K_j is global
averaging when i!=j. Again let K_S be global for |S|>=2. These are genuine
homogeneous commuting partition expectations obeying the semilattice law.
Now k_singleton=D, k_pair=D, and k_empty=k_full=1. Therefore EVERY complement
cut-duality k_S=k_(S complement) holds, and every stipulated divisor condition
k_S | D^min(|S|,3-|S|) holds. D=107 is prime, so prime D does not rescue this
abstract partition argument.

If common D-coordinate fibers existed, the three statistics would be functions
  a=f(y,z), b=g(x,z), c=h(x,y)
on the uniform product space [D]^3. There are at most
  D^(3 D^2)
ordered triples of such functions, and therefore at most that many distinct
contingency arrays c_abc obtainable from common coordinates. This is an upper
bound; many function triples can give the same table.

But the following strict inequality holds by exact integer arithmetic:
  3^(53^3) > 107^(3*107^2).
The left integer has235965 binary digits and the right has231550. Equivalently
the natural-log count margin is approximately3060.4497, but the proof/replay
uses the exact integer comparison, not floating-point rounding.
Consequently at least one of the explicit finite family of classical partition
systems above has NO common D-coordinate fiber refinement, even with full
cut-duality. The result is an existence proof: this checkpoint does not select
an individual nonrealizable table among the 3^148877 candidates. Selecting one
by exhaustive enumeration is a terminating finite procedure, but no feasible
cost claim is made for it.

3. USE AND LIMITATIONS
The argument establishes that a proof of common coordinate refinement from only
commutation, homogeneous cell sizes, divisor constraints and complementary ranks
must fail. Actual physical erasure Grams have further joint compatibility; this
lemma does not identify or defeat that compatibility. In particular:
* Neither example has been realized as compressions from a shared quantum
  eigenbasis or as a physical erasure-invariant MASA.
* Failure of the coordinate certificate does not imply failure of the entropy
  inequality, total common-Gram transport cone, or operational formation theorem.
* Here n=3 is fixed; there is no growing-horizon entropy violation.
* The strengthened counting proof can guide a search for an extra physical
  condition. It does not license calling arbitrary pairwise-independent classical
  tables admissible quantum spectra.

4. POSITIVE PEELING LEMMA AT A UNIT CUT
There is also a useful exact positive statement, independent of quantum physics.
Suppose for one coordinate label i,
 k_{i}=k_{[n]\{i}}=1.
Then the entire abstract partition system factors as a genuine ordinary
D-coordinate in direction i times a system on D^(n-1) points. If cut-duality
and the divisor assumptions hold initially, they descend to the smaller system.
In particular, if every k_S=1, the whole system is an ordinary product-coordinate
partition system (up to a bijection of Omega).

Proof. Relabel i=n. K_n cells have D points; K_[n-1] cells have D^(n-1) points.
Their product is global averaging, so they are independent partitions. Each pair
of cells has intersection size D*D^(n-1)/D^n=1. Thus Omega identifies with
A x B, |A|=D,|B|=D^(n-1), where K_n averages the A coordinate and K_[n-1]
averages B. For S subset [n-1], the partition K_S refines K_[n-1], hence acts
within each A slice by some partition expectation Q_S^a on B. Commutation with
K_n forces Q_S^a to be independent of a: apply K_n K_S=K_S K_n to a function
supported on a single A slice with arbitrary B dependence. The two sides are
D^-1 Q_S^(a0) h and D^-1 Q_S^a h, respectively. Therefore
 K_S=I_A tensor Q_S,
 K_(S union {n})=U_A tensor Q_S,
where U_A is uniform averaging. The Q_S inherit the semilattice law. Their cell
sizes are D^|S| k_S, and comparing the two displayed formulas gives
 k_(S union {n})=k_S.
Cut-duality in the full system now gives k_S=k_([n-1]\S). The two original
power-divisor conditions for S and S union {n} imply the smaller divisor
condition k_S | D^min(|S|,n-1-|S|). This proves the peeling statement.
If all k_S=1, peel one coordinate and induct. No nonlinear compatibility or
numerical search is needed for this positive lemma.
