FOCUSED PRIMARY-REFERENCE UPDATE — DOES NOT CLAIM A NEW THEOREM
Actual primary sources read during endpoint work:
1. Kun Fang, Entanglement cost of quantum depolarization,
   https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00187
   Sections3.1-3.2 report EC=EF for all TWO-QUBIT Bell-diagonal states, including
   qubit isotropic states, using tuned qubit semigroups. For qudit isotropic states
   d>=3, Section4 retains a gap. A current statement that EVERY Bell-diagonal/
   isotropic cost is wholly unknown would therefore miss this source's claim.
   No external acceptance or independent proof validation is inferred here.
2. Dong, Gao, Ou, Yao, Zhou, Dimension-Free Approximate Tensorization of Quantum
   Hypercontractivity for Qudit Depolarizing Semigroups,
   https://arxiv.org/html/2606.17729
   The stated hypotheses are primitive reversible PODS (positive off-diagonal
   scaling) semigroups. It reports exact qubit tensorization and qudit approximate
   tensorization factor2/(3 ln2), with exact integer-norm estimates.

Why this could change a real question: the actual stock memory with white-noise
separable tail, sum_h w_h Phi_D^h tensor (I/D^2)^(N-h), is a genuine non-MC
inventory family. Its one-pair marginals are isotropic. This does NOT identify
its whole multi-pair regularized formation cost, because the entire correlated
inventory is not a two-qubit state. No additivity inference is justified from
one-pair marginals. The whole class may remain a useful physical construction
for testing a future resource theorem, rather than an abstract scalar family.

Direct substitution into the common prefix generator also lacks a proved PODS
premise: E_i(|x><y|) can mix off-diagonal matrix units when x and y agree on the
erased prefix but differ on the suffix. Thus primitive reversible PODS exact
qubit tensorization is not automatically a theorem for an N-site hierarchical
generator. This precise obstruction must be resolved before transporting it.
The endpoint reliability theorem does not depend on either new result.
