ASTRA — SCIENTIFIC INTELLIGENCE AMPLIFICATION, CYCLE3
Independent meta-research assessment, 22 October 2026 Australia/Brisbane

EXECUTIVE ASSESSMENT
Astra is beginning to accumulate connected mathematical capability. The latest original returns establish more than increasingly careful exclusions: they contain positive operational theorems, accepted generalizations of incoming proofs and successful combinations of independently developed mathematics. They do not establish revolutionary physical capability, historical novelty for every theorem, causal research acceleration or recursive growth in scientific intelligence.

Preserve the restored autonomous architecture. Do not replace it with another centrally prescribed common project or compulsory reasoning protocol. The strongest immediate investment is to turn actual results into mathematical or computational operations that another investigator can use to remove a live limitation. That often requires new science at the interface, not another summary or more copies of the same solver.

This review tested, rather than merely recommending, explicit representation-changing guidance. Four fresh equal-budget investigations completed two real proof obligations. All succeeded; the controls were at least as strong. The trial therefore does not validate the guidance. It nevertheless produced two internally checked proofs closing the low-tempering gap in Pro3's last source, plus an explicit quadratic-anchor characterization. These are delivered as new contributed mathematics, with owner acceptance pending. The scientific result and the failed attempt to establish prompt superiority are distinct outcomes.

3. WHAT SEEMS TO MAKE THE PRODUCTIVE EPISODES WORK
A. Separate a constraint of the scientific object from a restriction of the current method.
The Morrey record supplies unusually strong evidence. An exact member excludes the graph-vanishing calibration architecture; allowing a transverse term and proving the resulting conditions covers that member. More search inside the excluded architecture could not achieve the extension. Pro2's fixed-palette failure similarly does not exclude all graph-adapted queries. Pro3's failed linear domination does not exclude uniform mixing. This is a precise scope distinction, not a promise that every failure has a clever rescue.

B. Reduce the problem while retaining the information the conclusion needs.
Pro1's depth law is exact for the relevant observables without pretending the full coherent system is classical. The new sampling proof changes the representation of repeated transitions while retaining the same kernel and all return paths. Useful abstraction comes with an exact map and retained quantifiers. Merely naming an analogy or dropping a difficult physical component is not such a reduction.

C. The receiver may have to become stronger before capital can be used.
Morrey's global anchor is generally a full PSD object. A bridge accepting only rank-one updates could not use it; its admissible input class had to be generalized. Pro2's receiving theorem similarly turns one apex into a separator, with additional constraints exactly cancelled. This suggests a concrete role for Ultra: identify a potentially valuable proof/tool and a receiving theorem whose input restriction prevents their composition, then do the mathematics needed to connect them. This remains Ultra's choice of scientific opportunity, not a centrally assigned checklist.

D. Protect the last operational step.
A mathematical estimate can be correct while its capacity consequence, decoder stability, sampler cost claim or physical cycle is false. Pro1 required a valid coding interface after compression. Pro3's counterexamples prevented an invalid density-growth shortcut and a false cost advantage. Dots corrected a mean-versus-integral issue without claiming the entire physical model was validated. Focused independent challenge at a load-bearing boundary has repeatedly changed the actual result. Endless repetition of a settled audit has not demonstrated the same value.

These mechanisms do not prove that Astra lacks representation ability or needs more compulsory process. The autonomous experimental controls already used that ability. An alternative diagnosis is that the scarce work lies in selecting the useful intermediate question, preserving operational quantifiers and making separate constructions compose. The present evidence supports that alternative at least as strongly as it supports adding an explicit brainstorming phase.

4. THE EXECUTED COMPARISON AND ITS SCIENTIFIC OUTPUT
The experiment compared unrestricted autonomous reasoning against a short explicit representation search within the SAME 12-minute allowance, scientific inputs and tool permissions. The answers were held out. The participants were fresh evaluation subagents, not reassignments of Pro1–3, Ultra, Max or Dots.

The anchor task was all-dimensional and included zero weights/singular certificates. Both arms proved it and characterized every feasible weight. Control took about3m43s; guidance took5m49s.

The sampling task asked for zero uniform floor for all positive exponents and the strongest sharp-coefficient interval. Both arms obtained the entire positive interval, beyond the later source checkpoint's sufficient threshold. Control took8m49s; guidance took9m21s. Independent condition-blind internal mathematical review passed both. I also checked their load-bearing steps.

Thus the primary completion comparison saturated again. The instruction did not win, and the controls finished sooner in this tiny sample. We cannot infer a general timing effect, a warm/fresh effect or an optimal number of agents. We should not add another selected easy test or retrospectively change the endpoint to make the intervention appear effective. Full protocol, proofs, public process notes, timing receipts and blind reviews are in meta_experiment/.

The consequential new result is mathematical. The previous proof repeatedly applied a power bound whose exponent weakened with time. One new proof exploits the telescoping identity M^j=D^(-1)Q^j D; the other proves a fixed L^p density bound for the conjugated transition operator. Both retain enough control for the inherited spectral argument to give
  (log n) R_eta(n) -> K
for every FIXED eta>0, uniformly over the admitted growing finite spaces. The complete kernel, start law and error definition are unchanged. No convergence constant uniform as eta tends to zero, practical speedup or historical novelty is claimed. The control even gives a stronger kind of intermediate moment guarantee, reinforcing the absence of an instruction advantage.

This output is greater than a replay but less than a new research system. It demonstrates that a compact, scientifically complete predecessor seed can support a genuine extension by a fresh investigator. It does not show that the seed is better than a full context, that a warm owner would have failed at equal resources, or that the entire discovery cost was nine minutes. Pro3's earlier formulation, lemmas and main proof are part of the intellectual and computational cost.

5. SCIENTIFIC INTELLIGENCE AMPLIFICATION: WHAT IS PRESENT AND WHAT IS MISSING
A credible local mechanism is now concrete: one result supplies a reusable mathematical operation; another investigator composes it with a different argument; the resulting stronger operation becomes available for subsequent questions. The observable benefit is a larger class of questions with a justified solution, not merely more stored prose.

Existing examples are Pro2's accepted fan-to-separator generalization, the Morrey PSD bridge and Dots' actual second local use of a guarded numerical helper. They have different strengths: a broader theorem, a necessary composition, and a narrower code reuse. They should not all be called the same degree of amplification.

The new density-moment lemma is a precise candidate for the next transfer. Capability: control repeated screened transitions without losing the useful exponent. Bottleneck: iterating a weaker one-step inequality creates an artificial restriction on eta. Construction: conjugation and a norm/set estimate proved for the unchanged operation. Verification: a complete sharp upper/lower theorem and independent analytic review under the same contract. Transfer: source-complete delivery to the existing owner; owner acceptance and further use remain pending. Failure conditions: loss of the required stochastic-column control, invalid fixed-instance conditions, missing return paths or a claimed uniform eta->0 constant. None may be silently relaxed.

For this to become recursive intelligence improvement rather than a mathematical sequence, acquired results must improve a reusable discovery operation, and that improved operation must then help develop a still better one at accounted total cost. No such resource-normalized multi-generation loop has been demonstrated. A growing library, source acceptance or one faster kernel would not establish an exponential growth law. The current evidence supports cumulative capital and some productive composition, not a measured compounding rate.

6. SERIOUS ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED
Concentrating all branches on one famous problem would trade away independent question formation without evidence that the extra copies address its decisive bottleneck. The Morrey record demonstrates substantial partial progress and some duplication, but no causal return to a large team. Preserve the Pro branches' independence; concentration can be justified when their owners identify genuinely complementary scientific obligations.

A universal fresh reset is also unsupported. Both experimental methods were fresh; there was no warm-owner comparison. Long-lived owners retain definitions, failures and physical contracts that an isolated seed can lose. A bounded fresh contribution can challenge an inherited proof restriction while the owner preserves continuity. That is a selective option, not a reset rule.

Automatic search over executable scientific operations is a serious alternative to prose-only research. It is appropriate where candidates have a faithful evaluator and repeated numerical experimentation is itself the bottleneck. It should search constructions or algorithms, rather than generate a dashboard or rank speculative programmes. Without a trustworthy evaluator, increasing search only increases the opportunity to optimize the wrong surrogate. Primary research and its limitations are summarized in evidence/PRIMARY_RESEARCH.txt.

Formal proof tools are another credible route to greater compositional depth. They could reduce the risk that long chains of reused lemmas rest on an unnoticed error, provided the formal statement matches the scientific claim. Their setup and formalization cost can be substantial. No formal-tool advantage was tested here, and installing a new proof platform is not assigned to the branches by this report. A small load-bearing lemma with an actual second user is a better initial target than wholesale formalization or another management system.

Problem-specific model training is a more radical alternative than changing prompts. It requires a meaningful evaluator and training infrastructure; published demonstrations are not evidence for Astra's present ability or for transfer to other problems. This review did not spend credits or launch training. It remains a conditional research direction, not the selected next allocation.

Better access to empirical evidence is indispensable for physical claims. Dots' missing transport/surface response is a different kind of limitation from a weak proof bound. Accessible primary measurements, an admissible universal bound or a different physically justified construction may change the decision; more agent coordination alone will not. Dots retains the choice of approach, and no spending, experiment purchase or outreach is requested.

7. DECISION AND NEXT DEPLOYMENT
Continue the restored architecture and worthwhile long-term scientific questions. Substantially change what the evaluator rewards: an actual removed frontier restriction, valid composition or verified useful operation is more important than another closed task or larger source bundle. Do not mandate the tested representation instruction. Do not use these results to claim that foundational mathematics is merely AI-friendly busywork; equally, do not assume its current internal theorems have established historical significance.

Pro1 continues its independently selected coherent gain-versus-plateau question, preserving the new capacity floor and actual infinite-bath contract. Pro3 receives the two complete new proofs for assessment; if accepted, its former low-eta gap is closed in this finite model and it chooses the next consequential question itself. A new numerical search is not assigned as a substitute.

Ultra retains a broad capital-mining mission, with its common-Gram frontier and optional new mathematical operations available. Max integrates genuine later source changes and actual acceptance without attributing earlier uses to a new index. Dots continues independent physical-admissibility research; a parked construction does not close its mission. Morrey remains halted. Hidden-entropy, N33, N557, IQM and other separately owned programmes are not reassigned.

All six full prompts are in prompts/. They contain actual source names, hashes, read points, current premises, settled exclusions, success distinctions and agent-owned retrieval/delivery. Every original return ZIP is included unchanged. The prompts explicitly preserve any newer owner-local work over this dated packet. They are ready to begin without source handling or benchmark selection by Harjas.

The next most valuable meta-research evidence is an actual receiving use of a stronger operation on a consequential remaining obligation, with the old approach and complete costs preserved. A contrived receiving benchmark would not establish this. If our strongest contributions repeatedly prove correct but fail to alter any subsequent scientific decision, or all apparent extensions collapse under native operational checks, the capital-composition strategy should lose priority. If independent owners repeatedly use these operations to settle previously inaccessible questions at defensible cost, that would justify deeper investment in verified reusable reasoning and experimentation.

Astra has demonstrated enough mathematical capability to justify continuing ambitious research. It has not demonstrated enough recursive improvement to claim a trajectory toward exponential scientific progress. The present correction is to preserve scientific autonomy while making genuine discoveries more usable and more composable, and to require evidence that those changes lead to deeper subsequent discoveries.
