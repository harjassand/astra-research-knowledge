PRIMARY EXTERNAL RESEARCH CONSULTED — BOUNDED RELEVANCE
These sources informed alternative mechanisms; they do not substitute for Astra evidence. No external reported result was reproduced here. All numerical claims below are authors' reports.

1. AlphaEvolve: A coding agent for scientific and algorithmic discovery (2025), https://arxiv.org/html/2506.13131v1 . Uses generated programs and automatic evaluators to evolve algorithms, with mathematical constructions and computational-stack improvements. Its authors report improving parts of their own training infrastructure. This supports search over executable discovery operations when a valid evaluator exists; it does not establish open-ended recursive scientific intelligence or supply Astra with equivalent infrastructure.

2. Advancing Mathematics Research with AI-Driven Formal Proof Search (2026), https://arxiv.org/html/2605.22763v1 . Reports research-level formal proof results and compares basic versus elaborate agents on the nine Erdős successes selected after the broader search. Basic agents reproduced those successes; elaborate agents were cheaper on the hardest subset and worse on others. The comparison is selected and cost accounting has qualifications. Relevant lesson: formal checking can make longer compositional reasoning reliable, while more agent machinery is not automatically better. No Lean experiment was performed in this review.

3. Learning to Discover at Test Time (2026), https://arxiv.org/html/2601.16175v2 . Updates model weights with problem-specific reinforcement learning and continuous evaluators. The stated objective is one good solution to the test problem, not cross-problem generalization. Reported implementations use paid training services. This is a serious alternative to frozen-model prompting, but was neither run nor funded here, and is not evidence for Astra's present recursive improvement.

4. The more you automate, the less you see: Hidden pitfalls of autonomous AI scientists (PNAS2026), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13663820/ . Controlled workflow studies find that final manuscripts conceal methodological problems detected more readily with code/traces. This supports source-based assessment rather than accepting polished final summaries. It does not justify turning all researchers into permanent auditors or transferring its measured rates to Astra.

5. FunSearch, Mathematical discoveries from program search with large language models (Nature2023/2024), https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06924-6 . Consulted as a predecessor for searching compact programs rather than isolated candidate solutions. It is background for the executable-operation alternative, not a demonstrated Astra intervention.
