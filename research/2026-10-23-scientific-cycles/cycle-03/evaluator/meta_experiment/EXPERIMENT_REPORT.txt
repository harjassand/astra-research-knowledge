CONTROLLED META-RESEARCH: REPRESENTATION GUIDANCE VERSUS AUTONOMOUS REASONING
Completed 22 October 2026 Australia/Brisbane

QUESTION
Does an explicit instruction to compare mathematical representations improve completion of difficult, genuine Astra proof obligations over a strong autonomous control with the same information, tools, model configuration and elapsed-time budget?

WHY THESE TASKS
The supplied Morrey and Pro3 histories show successful changes of certificate/proof representation. The anchor task is an all-dimensional global inequality, including singular and boundary cases. The sampling task must be uniform over growing finite spaces and asks for the strongest provable sharp-coefficient interval. Neither is a tiny diagnostic or a fabricated test disconnected from Astra. The completed owner proofs were held out. Nevertheless, the selection was retrospective and informed by known productive mechanisms. Scientific formulation and inherited-theorem preparation were substantial uncharged inputs to the participant clock.

Two tasks, two fresh arms each, same inherited root model/reasoning configuration, 12 minutes per arm from its first clock call. The precise serving version, token usage and billing were not exposed. No owner branch was restarted or reassigned. No web, other agents or sibling-source access was permitted to participants. Filesystem separation was instructed, not enforced as a security boundary. Start order alternated across tasks, but the experiment was not a randomized population trial.

Every arm received BASE_INSTRUCTIONS.txt plus its task's identical scientific files. The control was allowed any method, including reformulation. Treatment reserved at most one quarter of the same budget for two representations, exact preservation/coverage, a premise challenge and then a complete proof. It received no special known theorem, answer, D-optimality hint or extra source. The intervention is a bundle, not a clean isolation of one cognitive step.

The common anchor seed is common/ANCHOR_SEED.txt. The common sampling seed is common/tempered/TASK.txt plus unchanged SAFE_LOCAL_THEOREM.md and TEMPERED_STARTING_LEMMAS.md. Source hashes and original paths are retained. The primary endpoint was a complete correct quantified result or valid counterconstruction. Stronger partial lemmas, unsupported claims, representations and elapsed cost were secondary. The frozen stopping rule prohibits inventing an easier follow-up or changing endpoints after observing saturation.

The completed tasks have therefore saturated the primary endpoint again. The autonomous controls also finished sooner in these two realizations; output/diagnostic effort differed, and two timings do not establish a general causal slowdown or speedup.

No method adoption follows from this comparison. It does not show that representation ability is unimportant; both controls used it spontaneously. It does show that successful reformulation by a guided investigator is insufficient evidence for the guidance. Repeating well-packaged historical proof completion is a poor way to discriminate these models on this sample. A future experiment needs an actual harder remaining scientific obligation or a different decision unit, such as selecting a useful intermediate question, without weakening the control or concealing necessary evidence.

NEW SCIENTIFIC CONTENT ARISING DURING THE TEST
1. Anchor. For a positive kernel vector k of the spanning r-by-(r+1) matrix, put a_i=k_i^2. Every feasible probability weight vector is either a vertex e_m with a_m>=sum_(i!=m)a_i, or an interior vector satisfying a_i=T theta_i(1-theta_i) for a common T>0. A scalar square-root construction covers every nondominant vector a. This is a more explicit characterization than the supplied optimal-design existence proof. It includes zero weights, singular certificates and r=1. Both independent arms found it; it is not credited to the instruction. Historical novelty is unknown. Morrey remains halted.

2. Sampling. With the original positive finite pi,q, fixed symmetric stochastic Q, fixed alpha in(0,1), D(pi||q)<=K, and unchanged tempered transition C_eta, both arms obtain
  lim_(n->infinity) (log n) sup TV(q C_eta^n,pi)=K,
  lim_(epsilon->0) epsilon log T_eta(epsilon)=K
for EVERY fixed eta>0 under the same formula. Constants are not uniform as eta tends to zero. Pure IMH already attains K. No speedup or new target-normalizer access is introduced.

The treatment's proof sets M=diag(q^eta)^(-1) Q diag(q^eta). Since M^j=diag(q^eta)^(-1) Q^j diag(q^eta), Holder bounds q M^j(A) by q(A)^eta, keeping the exponent fixed rather than degrading it to eta^j. Rank-one proposal resets give
  q C_eta^s(A) <= [((1+(1-alpha)/2)^s-alpha)/(1-alpha)] q(A)^eta,
for 0<eta<1. First-entrance submeasures and the inherited spectral anchor yield the full sharp coefficient.

The control's proof gives a stronger kind of intermediate guarantee: for p=1/(1-eta), 0<eta<1,
  ||d(q C_eta^s)/dq||_(L^p(q)) <= (3/2)^s.
It uses a conjugated matrix norm, then caps the exponent at min(eta,1/2) for bounded-test interpolation. This also handles eta>=1 directly. Its analytical split is O(log log n); the other proof uses O(sqrt(log n)). Neither comparison establishes that one entire finite-time certificate dominates the other.

The control additionally proves an explicit eta=0 lower floor of (1-alpha)(1-exp(-K))/2. The treatment's distinct positive endpoint example has the smaller K/4 expression. Both separate fixed positive eta from the zero endpoint.

WHY THIS IS A LEGITIMATE EXTENSION, NOT A CHANGED TARGET
The kernel, start law, unknown-normalizer convention, finite growing-support supremum, all return paths and deterministic time are unchanged. The improvement is in a proved relation across repeated transitions. Both proofs explicitly reuse the supplied entropy and spectral arguments. This establishes inspectable mathematical dependence on prior capital. It does not establish how long a solver without that capital would take, practical superiority, historical priority, or the scientific owner's acceptance.

VERIFICATION

A separate fresh reviewer, given anonymized sampling submissions and the same allowed predecessor proofs, passed both, including the full positive-eta quantifier, uniformity over growing support, lower bound, inversion and endpoint. Sampling B was reviewed after A without disclosure of method conditions. See blind_anchor_review/REVIEW.txt and blind_tempered_review/{REVIEW.txt,REVIEW_B.txt}. These are analytic internal reviews, not formal proof-kernel certification or external peer review.

Each participant records supplementary numerical checks. These are participant-reported executions, not independently rerun by the evaluator; their counts are not proof strength. The complete mathematical arguments are self-contained. Ephemeral diagnostic scripts were not required by the frozen protocol, so exact numerical-run replays are not promised. The source inputs, finished proofs, public process notes, method conditions and receipts are retained. There is no hidden substitution of finite tests for the universal results.

COSTS AND LIMITS
Participant elapsed time sums to about27.70 agent-minutes. This is neither wall time nor total programme cost. It excludes shared source recovery/preparation, historical discovery, root analysis, three source audits, independent proof reviews, the adversarial meta-review, packaging and integration. Per-agent receipts retain their measured spans; model tokens and monetary totals are unknown. We do not claim a resource-normalized discovery improvement.

One run per condition/task cannot identify general tactic efficacy, warm-versus-fresh benefit, optimal branch count or an exponential trend. Mathematical tasks were prepared after seeing a successful historical mechanism. Public process summaries are retrospective evidence, not complete causal measurements of reasoning. No claims about private unexposed thought processes are made.

DECISION
Retain autonomous research as the default. Offer selective alternative-representation challenges where an identified restriction blocks a live target, without imposing a two-representation ritual. Preserve and deliver both new sampling proofs to Pro3 for scientific acceptance and choice of its next consequential question. Keep the explicit anchor characterization as optional capital; do not restart Morrey. Credit the new internally checked mathematics separately from the unsuccessful attempt to demonstrate a superior prompting strategy. Do not launch another easy comparison to manufacture a positive result.
