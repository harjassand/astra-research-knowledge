The original coherent iid seed had entropy just above 3/2, but its bath coherence did not provide stabilizing drift. ## A naive corrector norm loses the useful scale

The norm of the insertion resolvent grows like `(1-r)^(-1)` near critical bias. Bounding the commutator by twice the product of norms loses the required small-angle estimate. The exact identity contains an extra factor `1-r`, giving the uniform bound 4. That algebraic cancellation is load-bearing.

A stronger positivity conjecture for the insertion commutator survived small floating-point principal-section probes. It is not used or accepted as a theorem. The accepted proof uses only the exact norm bound. Source and observed probes are included for continuity, with their finite scope explicit. No more probes are justified merely by their positive appearance.

## Operator drift cannot simply be exponentiated

The scalar implication for exp(-tH) is not a valid general operator-monotonicity argument. The actual proof constructs `exp(-tL/2)(I-tC)exp(-tL/2)` and uses a bounded finite-band dilation with an explicit second-order norm remainder. A reflected heavy-tail walk has uniformly positive bounded drift but only polynomial low-depth probability. Finite bandwidth is sufficient, not proved necessary.

## Entropy alone does not control smooth support

States with mass 1-1/n on one vector and mass 1/n uniform over 2^(n^2) orthogonal vectors have entropy at least n and asymptotically complete rank-one capture. The actual bath theorem needs its exact initial tail entropy plus finite propagation. Trace closeness alone would not justify discarding that obligation.

On the SAME device and actual tau_pi, repeatedly feeding a pure color gives a binomial mixture of shifts along one ray. Depth is ballistic, but bath entropy is O(log n) and a thin non-radial coordinate subspace of polynomial rank captures asymptotically all mass. This does not carry a logical quantum code.
