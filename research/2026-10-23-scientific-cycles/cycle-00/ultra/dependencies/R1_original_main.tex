\title{Sharp finite reuse of normal bipartite quantum memory\\
\large Exact Bell streams, mixed reservoirs, and the noisy-output boundary}
\author{Astra R1 continuation: internal analytic report}
\date{21 October 2026}

\begin{abstract}
For a fixed finite-round, finite-outcome normal LOCC device, suppose \(J\) uses
emit \(J\) exact rank-\(d\) Bell pairs from a normal retained memory and return
that complete memory within half trace distance \(\delta\).
Writing \(m=J\ln d\), a dimension-independent bound is
\[
 \ER(\tau)\ge \frac{m}{2\delta}-\ln(1/\delta)
       +\frac m2-\ln(1+\delta).
\]
A mixed reservoir with no retained rung label attains both divergent terms.
In fact the optimal resource, even if return is required after every
elementary use, is \(m/(2\delta)-\ln(1/\delta)+O_{J,d}(1)\).
The negative logarithmic term is necessary: a proposed clean inequality
\(m\le 2\delta\ER(\tau)\) is false.
The proof uses a joint stopping measurement on all outputs; no independence
assumption is imposed on later outputs or their correlations with memory.
We also give a smoothed-resource bound, rigorous noisy-output bounds, a noisy
consumable counterexample to an attempted extension, and an external-data
non-identifiability result.
These are internally checked mathematical claims. Historical novelty,
external correctness certification, optimal preparation cost, and physical
advantage remain separate and unresolved.
\end{abstract}

\section{Contract and result}
All logarithms are natural. Set
\[
 \dist(\rho,\sigma)=\tfrac12\|\rho-\sigma\|_1,\qquad
 \ER(\rho)=\inf_{\gamma\in\SEP}D(\rho\Vert\gamma).
\]
Thus the seed's full trace-norm return error is \(2\delta\).
The retained memory is a normal density operator on separable
\(C_A\otimes C_B\); every retained shared register belongs to it.
Each elementary call is the same normal finite-round, finite-outcome LOCC
channel \(\Lambda\), with finite external ports. Outcomes are averaged, with
no successful-branch postselection and no uncounted incoming entanglement.
Fix the seed's faithful product reference input \(\eta\) and let \(\Phi\)
be its induced memory channel.

The concrete task is to emit a rank-\(d\) Bell pair on each of the first
\(J\) calls under these reference inputs. Define
\[
 |\ph_d\rangle=d^{-1/2}\sum_{a=0}^{d-1}|aa\rangle,\qquad
 \ph_d=|\ph_d\rangle\langle\ph_d|,\qquad D=d^J,\quad m=\ln D.
\]
The \emph{complete} first-\(J\) output state must be exactly
\(\ph_d^{\otimes J}\), identified locally with \(\ph_D\).
This is a joint-state condition, not a stipulation about separate approximate
marginals. Exact pure Bell marginals would imply the joint condition.
It automatically implies decoupling of these outputs from all other registers.

We impose \(\dist(\Phi^J(\tau),\tau)\le\delta\).
The stronger version imposes this at every \(1\le j\le J\).
Let \(R_{\rm end}^*(J,d,\delta)\) and \(R_{\rm all}^*(J,d,\delta)\)
be the infima of the initial \(\ER\) over all permitted memories and devices
meeting the respective tasks. The same device is used on every call in each
instance; its memory size may change between members of a reservoir family.

The lower bound is a necessary operational witness for any device that can do
this task. It does not assert that every process outside the seed's
\(\mathcal F_J\) must emit Bell pairs at the same cost.
The explicit devices below ignore external inputs entirely, so their stated
external process is valid against arbitrary adaptive and reference-entangled
testers, not just the reference-input experiment.

\begin{theorem}[Finite bound and sharp two-term law]\label{thm:main}
Let \(0<\delta\le1\) and \(R=\ER(\tau)<\infty\).
For every positive integer \(N\),
\[
 R\ge m\sum_{k=1}^N[1-(k-1)\delta]_+
       -\ln\!\left[1+(N-1)(1-D^{-1})\right].                \tag{1}
\]
Also \(R\ge m\). In particular, with \(N=\lceil1/\delta\rceil\),
\[
 R\ge m\left(\frac1{2\delta}+\frac12\right)
             -\ln\lceil1/\delta\rceil
 \ge \frac m{2\delta}-\ln(1/\delta)+\frac m2-\ln(1+\delta).
                                                               \tag{2}
\]
Define \(c_d=-\ln(1-d^{-1})+\ln d/(d-1)\le2\ln2\).
Then
\[
\begin{split}
 \frac m{2\delta}-\ln(1/\delta)+\frac m2-\ln(1+\delta)
 &\le R_{\rm end}^*\le R_{\rm all}^*\\
 &\le \frac m{2\delta}-\ln(1/\delta)+m-\ln J+c_d.
\end{split}\tag{3}
\]
Consequently, for fixed \(J,d\), both optima equal
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 R^*(J,d,\delta)=\frac{J\ln d}{2\delta}-\ln(1/\delta)
                       +O_{J,d}(1),\qquad \delta\downarrow0 .
 \quad}                                                    \tag{4}
\]
For \(\delta=0\), no finite-\(\ER\) memory can meet the exact Bell task.
\end{theorem}

This is a sharp resource--error law, rather than a dimension or energy
substitution in R1's tomography inequality. It has no tomography constant,
and its construction shows why even the second term cannot be removed.
For qubit outputs, (2) implies the useful finite-use restriction
\[
 J\le \frac{2\delta}{(1+\delta)\ln2}
                  \left[R+\ln\lceil1/\delta\rceil\right].    \tag{5}
\]
The resource is the initial relative entropy of entanglement, not an asserted
Bell-pair preparation cost or an experimentally certified quantity.

\section{Universal proof: one complete joint output test}
Write \(\mathcal T\) for the \(J\)-call channel from memory to block output
\(Q=Q_AQ_B\) and memory, under the product reference inputs, and
\(\Psi=\Phi^J\). Purity gives
\[
 \mathcal T(\tau)=\ph_D\otimes\tau_1,\qquad
 \tau_1=\Psi(\tau).
\]
Define the normal completely positive trace-nonincreasing map
\[
 S(x)=\tr_Q[(\ph_D\otimes\I)\mathcal T(x)(\ph_D\otimes\I)].
\]
It satisfies \(S(\tau)=\tau_1\).
The map \(S\) need not be LOCC or preserve separability. No such claim is used.

Repeat the same block channel \(N\) times without any resource reset.
For \(k\le N\), let \(p_{\ge k}\) be the probability that Bell projections
pass on the first \(k\) block outputs. These projections can all be deferred
until the outputs have been released: future channels act only on memory.
Thus
\[
 p_{\ge k}=\tr S^k(\tau),\qquad p_{\ge1}=1.
\]
For \(i\ge0\), \(0\le(S^*)^i(\I)\le\I\). Because \(\tau-\tau_1\)
has trace zero, its positive part has trace at most \(\delta\). Therefore
\[
 \tr S^i(\tau-\tau_1)\le\delta.
\]
Telescoping yields
\[
 p_{\ge k}\ge[1-(k-1)\delta]_+.                              \tag{6}
\]
This argument keeps the actual correlated state of all output blocks and
the complete memory; it does not replace them by identical marginals.

For a normal separable initializer \(\gamma\), the \emph{unconditioned}
first-\(k\) output state is separable across all Alice outputs versus all Bob
outputs. Every such state has overlap at most \(D^{-k}\) with
\(\ph_D^{\otimes k}\). This follows for product pure states by
Cauchy--Schwarz, then for their closed convex hull by linearity and continuity.
Hence its corresponding pass probability obeys
\[
 q_{\ge k}\le D^{-k}.                                      \tag{7}
\]
It would be incorrect to justify (7) by saying the postselected memory
stays separable. Deferral and the unconditioned joint output give the result.

Measure the number \(K\in\{0,\ldots,N\}\) of consecutive initial Bell passes,
capped at \(N\). Under \(\tau\), \(p_0=0\), so \(H(p)\le\ln N\);
\(\mathbb E_pK=\sum_{k=1}^Np_{\ge k}\).
For the comparison distribution, \(q_k\le q_{\ge k}\le D^{-k}\).
Relative-entropy data processing for this finite joint measurement gives
\[
\begin{split}
 D(\tau\Vert\gamma)
 &\ge D(p\Vert q)
   =-H(p)-\sum_kp_k\ln q_k\\
 &\ge m\mathbb E_pK-H(p)
 \ge m\sum_{k=1}^N[1-(k-1)\delta]_+-\ln N.
\end{split}
\]
A slightly stronger finite bound removes avoidable stopping entropy.
Put \(Z_q=\sum_{k=1}^N D^kq_k\). Summation by parts and (7) give
\[
 Z_q=Dq_{\ge1}+\sum_{k=2}^N(D^k-D^{k-1})q_{\ge k}
       \le 1+(N-1)(1-D^{-1}).
\]
The log-sum inequality, or normalization of \(D^kq_k\), gives
\(D(p\Vert q)\ge m\mathbb E_pK-\ln Z_q\).
This proves the stronger finite statement (1); the preceding entropy bound
suffices for (2). One can optimize (1) over integers \(N\) rather than always
using \(\lceil1/\delta\rceil\).
Take the infimum over normal separable \(\gamma\).
No minimizer, compactness, continuity of \(\ER\), or subtraction of infinities
is needed. \(N=1\) also gives \(R\ge m\).

For \(N=\lceil1/\delta\rceil\), all terms in the sum are positive and
\[
 A_N=N-\frac{\delta N(N-1)}2
 =\frac1{2\delta}+\frac12+\frac{\delta r(1-r)}2,\quad
 r=N-1/\delta\in[0,1).
\]
Also \(N\le(1+\delta)/\delta\). This proves (1)--(2).
If \(\delta=0\), let \(N\to\infty\) in \(R\ge Nm-\ln N\).
The constructive part of (3) is proved next. The refinement in (1) only
changes bounded constants; the two-term sharp law and its matching mixed
construction are the substantive claim.

\section{A reservoir without a retained rung label}
Put
\[
 \chi_d=\frac1d\sum_{a=0}^{d-1}|aa\rangle\langle aa|,\qquad
 \rho_{M,k}=\ph_d^{\otimes k}\otimes\chi_d^{\otimes(M-k)}
 \quad(0\le k\le M).
\]
The memory consists of \(M\) pairs of local \(d\)-level systems.
A fixed device emits its first pair, shifts the other pairs left, and appends
\(\chi_d\). The append uses only LOCC: Alice samples a uniform \(a\),
communicates it to Bob, and both prepare \(|a\rangle\).
Every branch is included. There is no fresh shared entanglement.
A sampled rung label used during initialization is genuinely discarded and
unavailable to all future calls. If retained anywhere accessible, it must be
counted, and the resource saving below disappears.

For any distribution \(w_k\), initialize
\(\tau=\sum_kw_k\rho_{M,k}\). The full output--memory state after any
\(t\ge0\) calls is
\[
 \Omega_t=\sum_kw_k\,
 \ph_d^{\otimes\min(k,t)}
 \otimes\chi_d^{\otimes(t-\min(k,t))}
 \otimes\rho_{M,\max(k-t,0)}.                               \tag{8}
\]
In particular, when \(t\le\min\{k:w_k>0\}\), the output is exactly
\(\ph_d^{\otimes t}\), in tensor product with the returned memory.
Equation (8) also states the later exhaustion branches and all correlations.
There is no recurrence: at late times the finite initial Bell stock is gone.

\subsection{Exact resource, rather than an upper estimate}
All these states are maximally correlated in the common computational basis,
with local marginal \(\I/d^M\) and diagonal \(\chi_d^{\otimes M}\).
For finite-dimensional separable \(\gamma_{AB}\),
\(\gamma_{AB}\le\I_A\otimes\gamma_B\).
Operator monotonicity of the logarithm, with support regularization, gives
\[
 D(\tau\Vert\gamma)\ge S(\tau_B)-S(\tau)
                  +D(\tau_B\Vert\gamma_B)
 \ge M\ln d-S(\tau).
\]
The separable comparator \(\chi_d^{\otimes M}\) attains this bound, so
\[
 \boxed{\ \ER(\tau)=M\ln d-S(\tau).\ }                       \tag{9}
\]
This known maximally correlated-state identity is not itself a novelty claim.

For spectral calculations use the Bell-phase basis
\[
 |b_z\rangle=d^{-1/2}\sum_{a=0}^{d-1}e^{2\pi iza/d}|aa\rangle.
\]
Here \(\ph_d=|b_0\rangle\langle b_0|\) and \(\chi_d\) is the uniform identity
on their span. All \(\rho_{M,k}\) commute. Sample \(K\) with law \(w\);
conditional on \(K=k\), a phase string \(Z\) has its first \(k\) entries zero
and its other entries independent uniform \(d\)-ary symbols. Thus
\[
 \ER(\tau)=\mathbb E[K]\ln d-H(K)+H(K\mid Z).                \tag{10}
\]

\subsection{Terminal-return construction and complete finite spectrum}
Take \(M=JN\), with \(K\) uniform on \(\{J,2J,\ldots,NJ\}\).
Group \(J\) adjacent local systems into dimension \(D=d^J\) and denote the
grouped rung by \(k=1,\ldots,N\).
After \(J\) elementary calls the state is
\[
 \ph_D\otimes\tau_N',\quad
 \tau_N=\frac1N\sum_{k=1}^N\ph_D^{\otimes k}\chi_D^{\otimes(N-k)},
 \quad
 \tau_N'=\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\ph_D^{\otimes k}\chi_D^{\otimes(N-k)}.
\]
Tensor symbols between adjacent factors are implicit here.
Telescoping gives
\[
 \tau_N-\tau_N'=\frac{\ph_D^{\otimes N}-\chi_D^{\otimes N}}N,\qquad
 \boxed{\ \delta_N=\dist(\tau_N,\tau_N')=\frac{1-D^{-N}}N.\ } \tag{11}
\]
The rank-one Bell projector lies inside a correlated subspace on which
\(\chi_D^{\otimes N}\) is the normalized identity, proving (11) directly.

For completeness, let \(\ell\) be the initial zero run of the grouped phase
string. The nonzero spectrum is
\[
 \lambda_\ell=
 \frac{D^{-(N-\ell)}(1-D^{-\ell})}{N(1-D^{-1})}
 \quad(1\le\ell<N),\qquad
 a_\ell=(D-1)D^{N-\ell-1},
\]
and
\[
 \lambda_N=\frac{1-D^{-N}}{N(1-D^{-1})},\qquad a_N=1.
                                                               \tag{12}
\]
The \(\ell=0\) eigenvalues and the complement of the correlated subspace are
zero. Equations (9) and (12) are an exact \(N\)-term resource formula.

Write \(a=1/D\). Conditional on a zero run \(\ell\), the gap
\(G=\ell-K\) has truncated geometric distribution
\[
 \Pr(G=g\mid\ell)=\frac{(1-a)a^g}{1-a^\ell},
       \qquad 0\le g<\ell.
\]
Its entropy is
\[
 h_\ell=c_D+\ln(1-a^\ell)
                   -\frac{\ell m a^\ell}{1-a^\ell},\qquad
 c_D=-\ln(1-a)+\frac{ma}{1-a}.
\]
Consequently \(0\le h_\ell\le c_D\).
The zero-run weights are \((1-a^\ell)/N\) for \(\ell<N\), and
\((1-a^N)/[N(1-a)]\) for \(\ell=N\).
Thus
\[
 \boxed{\ R_N=\frac{N+1}{2}m-\ln N+h_N^{\rm cond},\qquad
                  0\le h_N^{\rm cond}=H(K\mid Z)\le c_D.\ } \tag{13}
\]
In fact \(h_N^{\rm cond}=c_D-O_D(N^{-1})\).
For fixed \(D\), (11)--(13) give
\[
 R_N=\frac{m}{2\delta_N}-\ln(1/\delta_N)+\frac m2+c_D+o_D(1). \tag{14}
\]
Taking \(N=\lceil1/\delta\rceil\) proves the terminal upper bound
\(m/(2\delta)-\ln(1/\delta)+m+c_D\).

The ordinary \emph{flagged} version has resource exactly \((N+1)m/2\).
Erasing the rung label saves \(\ln N-H(K\mid Z)\), not a new source of
entanglement. This saving explains the logarithmic term.

\subsection{Return after every elementary call}
To meet the stronger task choose \(L\ge J\), \(M=J+L-1\), and
\[
 \widetilde\tau_{J,L}=\frac1L\sum_{k=J}^{J+L-1}\rho_{M,k}.
\]
All first \(J\) outputs are exact independent Bell pairs, decoupled from memory.
For \(1\le j\le J\), the returned memory is the uniform mixture of rungs
\(J-j,\ldots,M-j\). Direct comparison gives
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \delta_j=\frac1L\left[j-
                   \frac{(d^j-1)d^{-L}}{d-1}\right]
                  \le\frac jL .
 \quad}                                                    \tag{15}
\]
Here is a sign check establishing equality, not only the upper bound.
The difference of the two mixtures is \(L^{-1}(A_+-A_-)\), where
\[
 A_+=\sum_{k=M-j+1}^{M}\rho_{M,k},\qquad
 A_-=\sum_{k=J-j}^{J-1}\rho_{M,k}.
\]
Let \(Q\) be the phase subspace with the first \(M-j+1\) symbols zero.
All positive summands are supported in \(Q\).
On \(Q\), \(A_+\ge d^{-(j-1)}Q\), whereas \(A_-\) is scalar and its
eigenvalue is no larger than this when \(L\ge j\).
Outside \(Q\) the difference is negative.
Since \(\tr QA_+=j\) and
\(\tr QA_-=(d^j-1)d^{-L}/(d-1)\), (15) follows.
The hypothesis \(L\ge J\) is used and must not be dropped.

The same truncated-geometric posterior as above gives
\[
 \boxed{\ 
 \ER(\widetilde\tau_{J,L})
 =\left[J+\frac{L-1}{2}\right]\ln d-\ln L+\widetilde h,\qquad
                         0\le\widetilde h\le c_d .
 \ }                                                       \tag{16}
\]
Choose \(L=\lceil J/\delta\rceil\).
Every prefix returns within \(\delta\), and (16) is at most
\[
 \frac{J\ln d}{2\delta}-\ln(1/\delta)+J\ln d-\ln J+c_d.
\]
This proves the remaining part of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.

\subsection{A proposed stronger law is false}
For the terminal family, whenever \(\ln N>m/2+c_D\), (13) gives
\(R_N<Nm/2\). By (11),
\[
 2\delta_NR_N<m.
\]
Thus \(m\le2\delta R\) is false, despite being asymptotically correct to leading
order. No bound \(R\ge m/(2\delta)-C_{J,d}\) can hold with a constant independent
of \(\delta\). The unflagged mixed construction is an explicit permitted
counterexample, including all branches and the whole retained memory.
For an exact finite witness choose \(d=2,J=1,N=16\). Equations (11) and
(13) imply
\[
 \delta=\frac{65535}{1048576},\qquad
 R\le\frac{13}{2}\ln2,\qquad
 \frac{2\delta R}{\ln2}\le\frac{851955}{1048576}<1.
\]
This counterexample needs no numerical entropy estimate.

\section{Smoothed resource and imperfect outputs}
These extensions state what is proved; they do not claim the noisy optimum.

\subsection{Normal states with possibly infinite relative entropy}
For \(\kappa\in[0,1)\), let
\[
 R_\kappa(\tau)=
   \inf_{\sigma:\,\dist(\sigma,\tau)\le\kappa}\ER(\sigma).
\]
For each specified approximant \(\sigma\), any finite output measurement
changes its probability of a prefix event by at most \(\kappa\), uniformly
in the tested horizon. Repeating the exact-task stopping proof gives
\[
 \ER(\sigma)\ge
 m\sum_{k=1}^N[1-\kappa-(k-1)\delta]_+-\ln(N+1).             \tag{17}
\]
The entropy bound is now \(\ln(N+1)\), since the first test can fail under
\(\sigma\). Taking the infimum proves (17) for \(R_\kappa\).
No recurrence of \(\sigma\), or return estimate for it, is assumed.
For normal \(\tau\), locally finite-support approximants make \(R_\kappa\)
finite for every \(\kappa>0\), but its magnitude is an additional resource
constraint. This result does not solve exact same-protocol removal at
infinite \(\ER\).

\subsection{A correlation-safe noisy stopping bound}
Let the first block have Bell infidelity
\[
 \alpha=1-\tr[\ph_D\,\mathcal T(\tau)_Q],
 \qquad \dist(\Psi(\tau),\tau)\le\delta .
\]
Use the same Bell-success map \(S\). Write
\(\tau_1=\Psi(\tau)=S(\tau)+F(\tau)\), with \(F\) completely positive
trace-nonincreasing and \(\tr F(\tau)=\alpha\).
The one-sided defect
\[
 h=\tr(\tau-S(\tau))_+\le\delta+\alpha                       \tag{18}
\]
follows from \(\tau-S(\tau)=(\tau-\tau_1)+F(\tau)\).
For any effect \(0\le E\le\I\), \(\tr E(\tau-S(\tau))\le h\).
It follows that
\[
 p_{\ge k}\ge[1-\alpha-(k-1)h]_+ .
\]
For any \(\dist(\sigma,\tau)\le\kappa\) with finite \(\ER\),
\[
 \boxed{\ 
 \ER(\sigma)\ge
 m\sum_{k=1}^N[1-\alpha-\kappa-(k-1)(\delta+\alpha)]_+
                      -\ln(N+1).
 \ }                                                       \tag{19}
\]
This avoids a square-root decoupling penalty by using the positive-part defect.
It permits arbitrary output--memory correlations.
It is not sharp when \(\alpha>0\) is fixed and \(\delta\to0\).

\subsection{A stronger fixed-noise bound from inherited machinery}
For fixed nonzero noise, one can do better using Piani's entropy-disintegration
mechanism, stated explicitly as known machinery rather than the new result.
There is a finite LOCC verification experiment on a rank-\(D\) output whose
accept effect is
\[
 V_D=\frac{\I+D\ph_D}{D+1}.
\]
To see finite realizability, average local equal-outcome tests in a basis and
its complex conjugate over Haar bases. The average has this form by
\(U\otimes\overline U\) symmetry, trace \(D\), and Bell acceptance one.
A finite convex decomposition exists by finite-dimensional Caratheodory.
A separable state accepts with probability at most \(c=2/(D+1)\).
An output with Bell infidelity \(x\) accepts with
\(v=1-Dx/(D+1)\).

Define, for \(x_*=1-1/D\),
\[
 e_D(x)=
 \begin{cases}
 v\ln(v/c)+(1-v)\ln[(1-v)/(1-c)],&0\le x<x_*,\\
 0,&x\ge x_* .
 \end{cases}
\]
A normal free instrument with average memory map \(\Psi\) obeys
\[
 \ER(\xi)-\ER(\Psi(\xi))\ge e_D(x)
\]
whenever its output infidelity is at most \(x<x_*\) and \(\ER(\xi)<\infty\).
Indeed choose a separable relative-entropy approximate minimizer; apply
data processing to the LOCC transcript and conditional memory; the latter
remains separable under the comparison input. Convexity gives the memory
term. Coarse-graining to acceptance gives the binary divergence above.
This proof is valid with normal infinite memory and never subtracts infinities.

Contractivity gives \(\dist(\Psi^i(\tau),\tau)\le i\delta\).
Its next block output has infidelity at most \(\alpha+i\delta\).
Summing resource drops gives
\[
 R\ge\sum_{i=0}^{M-1}e_D(\alpha+i\delta).
\]
For \(\alpha<x_*\) and \(\delta>0\), the decreasing left Riemann sum yields
\[
 \boxed{\qquad
 \delta R\ge C_D(\alpha):=
                    \int_\alpha^{x_*}e_D(x)\,dx>0 .
 \qquad}                                                   \tag{20}
\]
This genuinely diverges as \(1/\delta\) at fixed entangled-output fidelity.
For \(\delta=0\), summing indefinitely rules out finite \(R\).
For a \(\kappa\)-approximant replace \(\alpha\) by \(\alpha+\kappa\);
output trace-distance error stays at most \(\kappa\) at every horizon.
Neither (19) nor (20) identifies the optimal noisy coefficient.

\subsection{Ordinary noisy fuel defeats a tempting extension}
It is wrong to replace \(m\) in the exact leading coefficient simply by
\((1-\alpha)^2m\).
For \(J=1,d=2,0\le\alpha\le1/2\), let
\[
 \omega_\alpha=(1-\alpha)\ph_2^++\alpha\ph_2^-,
 \qquad |\ph_2^\pm\rangle=(|00\rangle\pm|11\rangle)/\sqrt2 .
\]
Its half trace distance from \(\ph_2^+\) and its Bell infidelity are both
\(\alpha\). Equation (9) gives the exact additive resource
\[
 \ER(\omega_\alpha^{\otimes k})
                         =k[\ln2-\hbin(\alpha)],
\]
where \(\hbin(x)=-x\ln x-(1-x)\ln(1-x)\).

Use an ordinary flagged consumable ladder, uniform on \(k=1,\ldots,L\),
containing \(k\) copies of \(\omega_\alpha\), with local padding.
It emits \(\omega_\alpha\) exactly and returns memory with
\(\delta=1/L\). Local orthogonal flags imply
\[
 R=\frac1L\sum_{k=1}^L\ER(\omega_\alpha^{\otimes k})
       = \frac{L+1}{2}\,[\ln2-\hbin(\alpha)] .
\]
For clarity, the flag identity follows by measuring the locally readable
flags for a lower bound and using a flagged separable comparator for the
upper bound. All failure and exhaustion branches are included.
The complete first output is in product with the returned memory.
Its joint half trace error from \(\ph_2^+\otimes\tau\) is
\(\beta=\delta+(1-\delta)\alpha\): the two edge flag blocks contribute
\(\delta\), and overlapping flag blocks contribute \((1-\delta)\alpha\).
Thus the output error, marginal return error, and joint error are all explicit.
In particular
\[
 \lim_{L\to\infty}\delta R
                  =\frac{\ln2-\hbin(\alpha)}2.              \tag{21}
\]
At \(\alpha=0.1\), this is about \(0.18403\) nats, below
\((1-\alpha)^2\ln2/2\approx0.28072\).
The source is a permitted ordinary consumable state, so a proposed universal
lower bound with the latter coefficient is explicitly false.
An isotropic noisy Bell state has the same single-copy \(E_R\) and gives
a related subadditive upper bound; the phase-noise choice makes the entire
resource ledger exact.
More powerful correlated, unflagged, or regularized noisy consumables may
improve (21). This is a real unresolved boundary, not a reason to claim
sharpness for (19)--(20).

\section{Matched comparators and what is operationally certified}
\paragraph{Ordinary consumption.}
Without return, exact \(J\)-pair output costs at least \(m\) in initial
\(\ER\), attained by \(J\) Bell pairs and local swaps. Retaining a consumed
inventory flag can make the return distance one. To require small return,
a large ordinary consumable stock distributed over possible inventories
attains the \(m/(2\delta)\) leading law. The unflagged construction is itself
a consumable reservoir. It does not outperform an optimally informed
ordinary source under these same definitions.

\paragraph{Preparation and storage.}
The all-prefix reservoir has \(M=J+\lceil J/\delta\rceil-1\) local
\(d\)-level systems per party and local dimension \(d^M\).
Sampling \(K\), preparing \(K\) Bell pairs, and irreversibly discarding the
sample label gives a simple preparation consuming
\([J+(L-1)/2]\ln d\) of supplied Bell entanglement on average,
at most \(M\ln d\) on a run.
The initial \(\ER\) is smaller by \(\ln L-H(K\mid Z)\).
No equality between this \(\ER\) and optimal preparation cost is asserted.
A shift uses \(O(M)\) elementary local swaps in a simple circuit and a
finite classical message to append \(\chi_d\); efficient state synthesis at
cost \(\ER\) is unresolved.
A physical qudit-number Hamiltonian measures stored registers, not the
unretained rung \(K\). An energy model proportional to \(K\) would need a
separate sector/flag structure and a new resource comparison.

\paragraph{Pure catalytic and embezzling families.}
The standard harmonic family should not be used as the strongest pure
incumbent. Smooth, sine-profile coherent entanglement ladders can also
extract exact Bell pairs by local isometries with return scaling
\(R=O(J\ln d/\delta)\). In such a pure ladder, coefficients over rung
length \(L\) proportional to \(\sin[\pi(r+1)/(L+1)]\) have shifted-state
overlap \(1-O(J^2/L^2)\), hence half trace return \(O(J/L)\).
Its entanglement includes the Shannon entropy of the coherent rung
distribution; it does not acquire the mixed reservoir's
\(-\ln(1/\delta)\) saving. The universal lower bound applies to any pure or
mixed permitted LOCC competitor, whether or not described as embezzling.
Zanoni--Theurer--Gour give the stronger general pure-conversion framework;
no superiority over every pure construction is claimed here.

Rubboli--Tomamichel allow mixed catalysts and give general logarithmic resource
divergence with a \emph{joint purified-distance} error. Our exact-output
construction has joint half trace error exactly \(\delta\), because
\(\Omega_J=\ph_D\otimes\tau'\).
For general states, half trace distance \(T\) and purified distance \(P\)
obey \(T\le P\le\sqrt{2T-T^2}\).
One cannot compare a numerical \(\delta\) in one metric directly with the same
number in the other. No exact-return finite-\(\ER\) Bell broadcaster exists.
Type-III and singular embezzlers lie outside the normal tensor-product model.

\paragraph{Complete process, correlations, and testing.}
The lower bound uses the entire output state through one stopping measurement.
Equation (8) gives the construction's exact joint output--memory process at
all times. Its initial exact Bell outputs are decoupled by purity; later
outputs are generally correlated with each other and with remaining memory.
For noisy outputs, joint Bell infidelity, marginal memory error, and joint
output--memory error are distinct. Equations (19)--(21) keep these distinctions.
Bell fidelity can be estimated from external local measurements, but finite
samples do not certify exact equality, and full-\(J\) verification can be costly.

\begin{proposition}[External data do not certify hidden return or a resource cap]
No arbitrary finite-horizon external experiment alone can universally
upper-certify the complete-memory return error or an initial \(\ER\) cap
in this uncharacterized-memory model.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For any horizon \(H\), use the seed's flagged Bell-inventory shift on a fixed
sufficiently large finite memory. Initializer one is uniform on inventories
\(H,\ldots,H+L-1\); initializer two has inventory exactly \(H\).
For both, the entire first-\(H\) causal process ignores all inputs and emits
\(\ph_d^{\otimes H}\), so no adaptive or entangled-reference tester can
distinguish them. At call \(H\), initializer one has return half distance
\(H/L\) (take \(L\ge H\)), while initializer two has distance one because
its retained inventory flag changes from \(H\) to \(0\).
This is even the same device and same memory space.
An arbitrarily large entangled spectator on which the device acts trivially
also leaves every external process unchanged while increasing the initial
resource arbitrarily: discarding the original memory lower-bounds the enlarged
resource by the spectator Bell entanglement, without requiring additivity of
relative entropy of entanglement. More generally, an invisible local clock
of dimension \(H+1\), initially in \(|0\rangle\) and advanced once per call,
can be appended to any compatible device. Its clock is orthogonal to its initial
state after \(H\) calls, so the complete-memory return distance is one while
every external observation is unchanged. Therefore observed outputs do not
supply the missing hidden-memory upper bounds.
\end{proof}
Calibrated memory control, trusted dimension/energy assumptions, or additional
access may permit such certification, but no such connection is proved here.

\section{Primary-reference and novelty boundary}
The exact Bell overlap, relative-entropy data processing, maximally correlated
identity, and geometric entropy estimates are standard ingredients.
The candidate contribution is their sharp mixed-reservoir stopping law,
including the necessary logarithmic correction and the all-prefix construction.
A targeted primary-source check found no identical optimal law; this is
\emph{not} a historical-priority certificate.

Piani \cite{piani} supplies the resource-disintegration mechanism inherited by
R1 and used explicitly in (20). A routine witness-budget extension of that
result is not counted as the main contribution.
Lami--Shirokov \cite{lami} establish attainment and lower semicontinuity for
normal states; neither implies trace-distance continuity without resource
regularity. The main proof here avoids minimizers.
Son et al.\ \cite{son} concern exact catalyst return robust to a ball of input
noise, not fixed nonzero memory-return error.
Yamasaki et al.\ \cite{yamasaki} identify asymptotic entanglement cost with
regularized convex-roof entanglement under finite-local-entropy assumptions;
they do not identify \(\ER\) with cost.
Van Luijk et al.\ \cite{vluijk} distinguish families from fixed normal
type-I embezzlers and explain the type-III boundary.
Rubboli--Tomamichel \cite{rubboli} and Zanoni et al.\ \cite{zanoni}
are the relevant mixed-catalyst and optimal pure-conversion comparisons.

 No hardware operation, energy advantage, purchase, account signup,
