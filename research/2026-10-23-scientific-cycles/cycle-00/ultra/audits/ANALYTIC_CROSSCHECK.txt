INDEPENDENT LAST-COORDINATE INDUCTION

Scope. This is a mathematical proof of the proposed entropy/projection bound,
not a numerical claim of a sharp constant. It concerns the new preparation
resource distinction and does not reopen the old finite-reuse additive law.
All logarithms are natural. D is an integer >=2; N>=1.

Let |+> = D^(-1/2) sum_{a=1}^D |a>, and
P_i = (|+><+|)^{tensor i} tensor I, i=1,...,N.
For every normalized complex vector c on (C^D)^{tensor N}, let
q_z=|c_z|^2 and F_N(c)=ln(D) sum_i ||P_i c||^2 - H(q).
Then
  F_N(c) <= C_D [1-||P_N c||^2] <= C_D,
where C_D=L_D-ln(D), and one may take L_D=2D for every D,
or L_2=2 and hence C_2=2-ln(2).

ONE-SITE INPUT, WITH SELF-CONTAINED CONSTANT
Write a_z=sqrt(p_z), v_z=D^(-1/2), and
s=|<v,a>|^2. Since ln x <= x-1,
  KL(p||u) <= chi^2(p||u) = D ||p-u||_2^2.
For a zero-sum real vector h, ||h||_2^2 <= ||h||_1^2/2:
the positive and negative parts each have total ||h||_1/2,
and the sum of squares of each part is bounded by its squared total.
Cauchy-Schwarz gives
  ||p-u||_1 <= ||a-v||_2 ||a+v||_2 = 2 sqrt(1-s).
Consequently KL(p||u) <= 2D(1-s). Equivalently,
  H(p) >= ln(D)-L_D(1-s),  L_D=2D.

For D=2, put x=|2p_1-1| in [0,1]. Define
  f(x)=KL(((1+x)/2,(1-x)/2)||(1/2,1/2)),
  g(x)=1-sqrt(1-x^2).
Then f(0)=g(0)=0, f'(x)=atanh x, g'(x)=x/sqrt(1-x^2),
and g''(x)=(1-x^2)^(-3/2) >= (1-x^2)^(-1)=f''(x).
Thus f<=g=2(1-s), proving the asserted L_2=2. Endpoints
follow by continuity. None of these constants are claimed optimal for F_N.

ENTROPY PERSPECTIVE MONOTONICITY
For x=(x_1,...,x_D)>=0 and S=sum x_i, define
  G(x)=-sum_i x_i ln(x_i/S), G(0)=0.
On the positive orthant, partial_i G=ln(S/x_i)>=0.
Continuity extends this monotonicity to the boundary. Thus if 0<=x<=t
coordinatewise and sum t_i=1, then G(x)<=G(t)=H(t).

INDUCTION
It suffices first to treat c nonnegative: replacing complex c entrywise
by |c| preserves H(q) and increases every ||P_i c||, because each P_i
has entrywise nonnegative matrix elements. Since C_D>=0, a bound
F_N(|c|)<=C_D(1-||P_N|c|||^2) also bounds the original c.

For N=0 set P_0=I, F_0=0; the claimed strengthened inequality is 0<=0.
Split a nonnegative c along its LAST coordinate into D slices:
  c=(sqrt(t_1) u_1,...,sqrt(t_D) u_D), ||u_b||=1,
where t_b>=0 and sum t_b=1. Zero-weight slices can be arbitrary.
Let
  s_b=||P_{N-1}u_b||^2,
  r_b=sqrt(t_b s_b), S=sum_b r_b^2,
  s=||P_N c||^2=(sum_b r_b)^2/D.
Here for N=1, P_0=I and s_b=1. The last equality uses nonnegativity.
The chain rule H(|c|^2)=H(t)+sum_b t_b H(|u_b|^2) and the block form
of P_i for i<N give
  F_N(c)=sum_b t_b F_{N-1}(u_b) + ln(D) s - H(t)
       <= C_D(1-S) + ln(D) s-H(t).
We must show this is <=C_D(1-s), namely
  (C_D+ln D)s-C_D S <= H(t).
If S=0 it is immediate. Otherwise put p_b=r_b^2/S. Its one-site overlap
is s/S, so the one-site entropy inequality gives
  (C_D+ln D)s-C_D S
    = S [ln D-L_D(1-s/S)]
   <= S H(p) = G((r_b^2)_b)
   <= H(t),
because r_b^2=t_b s_b<=t_b. This closes the induction.

EXACT APPLICATION TO THE MIXED RESERVOIR
The maximally correlated support is isometric to a single-system space
(C^D)^{tensor N}, with |zz> identified with |z>. On it,
  tau_N = (1/N) sum_{k=1}^N
          (|+><+|)^{tensor k} tensor (I/D)^{tensor(N-k)}.
Every pure vector in any decomposition of tau_N lies in this support
(otherwise its nonnegative orthogonal-support weight could not average to 0).
The entanglement entropy of such a vector is exactly H(|c_z|^2).
Averaging the proved inequality over any decomposition yields
  E_F(tau_N) >= ln D sum_i Tr(P_i tau_N)
                   - C_D [1-Tr(P_N tau_N)].
The required quantities are explicit:
  sum_i Tr(P_i tau_N)
    = (N+1)/2 + (1/N) sum_{h=1}^{N-1}(N-h)D^{-h},
  Tr(P_N tau_N)=(1/N) sum_{h=0}^{N-1}D^{-h}.
The usual rung preparation gives E_F(tau_N)<=(N+1)ln(D)/2:
prepare k perfect maximally entangled pairs and a separable diagonal tail,
then mix uniformly in k and discard its label.
Therefore E_F(tau_N)=(N+1)ln(D)/2+O_D(1). Combined with the actual old
reservoir identity E_R(tau_N)=(N+1)ln(D)/2-ln N+H(K|Z),
0<=H(K|Z)<=c_D, this proves E_F(tau_N)-E_R(tau_N)=ln N+O_D(1).

LIMITATION
E_F is the single-copy convex-roof resource. Calling this asymptotic
entanglement preparation cost requires an additional proof of the relevant
regularization/additivity statement, or a matched established theorem. This
crosscheck does not supply that additional identification.

NUMERICAL CHECK SCOPE
The separate check_entropy.py used 8 deterministic/random starts for each
D=2,N=1,...,8 under one BLAS thread. The largest numerical F found was
0.9465499518710612 at N=8. This is heuristic lower-bound evidence for the
best constant, not a certified global maximization and not evidence against
the proven C_2=2-ln 2. Exact vectors are preserved in witnesses.npz.
