1. MATCHED OBJECT AND KNOWN COST IDENTIFICATION

Let D>=2, N>=1, q=D^N. In the correlated computational basis, write
tau_N=V sigma_N V*, V|x>=|xx>, where
 sigma_N=(1/N) sum_{k=1}^N |+_D><+_D|^tensor k
                         tensor (I_D/D)^tensor(N-k).
Both the state and the resource constraints are finite dimensional at each
fixed N. All following entropies may use natural logs; sources mostly use
base 2, so multiply source values by ln 2.

[VDC02] G. Vidal, W. Duer, J. I. Cirac, "Entanglement cost of mixed states",
Phys. Rev. Lett. 89, 027901 (2002), quant-ph/0112131v4.
Primary statement and proof read: pp.1-3, theorem Eq.(12), especially
partial-trace condition Eq.(11), proof Eqs.(14)-(21).
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0112131
 https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.89.027901
The theorem makes E_F(rho_V) additive even against an arbitrary bipartite
sigma if partial trace on support V is entanglement breaking. On the support
span{|xx>}, tracing B is the completely dephasing channel. Therefore it
applies to tau_N and yields E_C(tau_N)=E_F(tau_N); no regularization is
needed. This is old machinery, not the new extension. The theorem does not
evaluate the required high-dimensional convex roof or identify E_C with E_R.

[WY16] A. Winter, D. Yang, "Operational Resource Theory of Coherence",
Phys. Rev. Lett. 116, 120404 (2016), arXiv:1506.07975v3.
Primary statements read: Definition7, Theorems8-10, Discussion, Appendix A
Lemma15 and Appendix B proofs of Theorems8-10.
 https://arxiv.org/html/1506.07975
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.07975
Theorem8 identifies asymptotic IO coherence cost with coherence of formation
C_f; Theorem9 proves its tensor-product additivity. Discussion explicitly
matches C_f(sigma) to E_C(V sigma V*)=E_F(V sigma V*) for maximally correlated
states. Lemma15 provides a dimension-dependent continuity estimate in their
Bures metric. The reversible-state characterization only decides when the
gap vanishes; it does not give its N-dependence. A generic finite-dimensional
continuity substitution carries log q=N log D and cannot itself establish
a dimension-independent error bound or a logarithmic separation.

Elementary matching argument (included to make assumptions checkable):
Every pure vector in any positive decomposition of tau_N lies in its support,
hence in span{|xx>}; it has form sum_x a_x|xx>. Its entanglement is
H(|a_x|^2). Applying V* to all ensemble vectors is a bijection between pure
decompositions of tau_N and sigma_N, with exactly that convex-roof objective.
Consequently E_F(tau_N)=C_f(sigma_N). Combined with VDC02, the target quantity
is a single convex roof, but its value remains a genuine obligation.

2. COST / ERROR DEFINITIONS THAT MUST NOT BE CONFLATED

[BD11] F. Buscemi, N. Datta, "Entanglement cost in practical scenarios",
Phys. Rev. Lett. 106, 130503 (2011), arXiv:0906.3698v3.
Read Definition1, Theorems1-2, Discussion Eqs.(7)-(9), and exact identity
Eq.(17) with dilution fidelity Eq.(18) in detailed proof.
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/0906.3698
 https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.106.130503
Zero-error deterministic one-shot Bell cost is log Schmidt number, not E_F.
Nonzero-error cost is characterized through smoothed ensemble conditional
zero-Renyi entropies; their dilution error is fidelity-based and their c-q
smoothing uses trace norm. Theorem2 takes many copies, then vanishing error,
to recover E_F regularized. Its asymptotic result does not supply a uniform
finite-block complexity as N grows. The exact identity expresses cost using
ensemble truncation ranks and average retained mass, so a chosen explicit
ensemble can give a valid finite-block protocol; the optimization itself
does not make it computationally cheap.

Operational ledgers for this project:
(a) E_C(tau_N): rate of supplied ebits per copy for iid copies at fixed N,
with vanishing preparation error and unconstrained finite LOCC cost. N->infty
is an additional outer limit requiring separately charged block size/error.
(b) E_F(tau_N): least average entanglement entropy of an exact pure ensemble;
it is equal to (a) here, but is not exact single-run Bell inventory.
(c) Variable Bell inventory sampling K: the existing exact initializer costs
E[K] ln D on average and N ln D in the worst branch, with all labels genuinely
discarded. These are two distinct supplied-Bell resource definitions.
(d) Deterministic exact one-shot supplied maximally entangled rank M:
cost ln M, controlled by Schmidt number as above.

Our model-specific elementary consequence of standard Schmidt machinery:
tau_N has overlap with |Phi_q> equal to
 f_N=(1-D^(-N))/(N(1-D^(-1))).
[TH00] B. Terhal, P. Horodecki, "A Schmidt number for density matrices",
Phys. Rev. A 61, 040301(R) (2000), quant-ph/9911117v4.
Read Definition1, Proposition1 and Lemma1 Eq.(4).
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/9911117
 https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.61.040301
Their Schmidt number is LOCC nonincreasing, and maximally entangled overlap
for Schmidt number s in q by q dimension is at most s/q.
Thus exact deterministic one-shot M must satisfy
 M >= ceil(q f_N),
 ln M >= N ln D - ln N + ln[(1-D^(-N))/(1-D^(-1))].
The trivial upper bound is N ln D. This near-full-storage requirement is a
useful failure boundary for a claim that average K or E_R describes the exact
deterministic Bell cost, but it is a routine old-witness application and does
NOT establish a lower bound of this size on asymptotic E_C.
With half-trace preparation error epsilon the same witness only gives
 M/q >= max(0,f_N-epsilon).
Hence fixed epsilon can make this lower bound void for large N. It must not
be presented as robust at fixed preparation error.

Error propagation (elementary project-specific check): if an initializer
rho has half-trace distance epsilon from tau_N, the fixed LOCC shift maps it
within epsilon of the ideal entire output-memory state. Its own marginal
return error is at most delta_N+2 epsilon by contractivity and the triangle
inequality. Pure exact Bell output need not survive a generic approximation.
Because tau_N=Phi_D tensor omega_{N-1}, an initializer formed as exact Phi_D
tensor an approximate omega preserves the first grouped exact output, but
that stronger structural preparation must be explicitly required and costed.

3. SYMMETRY IS A REDUCTION, NOT A SOLUTION

[VW01] K. G. H. Vollbrecht, R. F. Werner, "Entanglement Measures under
Symmetry", Phys. Rev. A 64, 062307 (2001), quant-ph/0010095v2.
Read Sec.IV.A and the general recipe Eqs.(38)-(40), pp.9-10.
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0010095
 https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.64.062307
For an invariant state, minimize pure-state cost over all preimages of its
twirl and then convexify on the invariant state set. One pure orbit with a
chosen set of Fourier amplitudes gives only an upper bound. The convex hull
step cannot be dropped unless convexity/tightness is proved. The present
nested-prefix family is not generally isotropic under the full U(q) symmetry;
applying a single-fidelity isotropic formula without an actual twirl equality
would throw away its hierarchy and need not compute its cost.

4. ENTROPY/HARDY PRIOR ART AND PRECISE UNMATCHED STEP

[O16] A. Osekowski, "Sharp Logarithmic Inequalities for Hardy Operators",
Zeitschrift fuer Analysis und ihre Anwendungen 35 (2016), 1-20,
DOI10.4171/ZAA/1552. Original author preprint heading/search metadata says
"Logarithmic Hardy Inequalities"; cite the published title above.
Primary official PDF read: abstract, Eq.(4), Theorem1.2, proof of ell=1
(Sec.2), and beginning of Sec.3.
 https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/35920
 https://doi.org/10.4171/ZAA/1552
For nonnegative f with integral1 on (0,1], it determines sharp L(K,ell) in
 integral_0^1 [integral_0^t f(r)dr]^ell dt/t <= K Ent(f)+L(K,ell).
For ell=2,K=1 the finite optimal additive constant is1. For ell=1 no finite
constant exists at K=1. This is a sharp entropy-versus-cumulative-mass Hardy
inequality. It does not directly give entropy of |psi|^2 in terms of the sum
of squared L2 nested conditional expectations of psi: those are different
normalizations, operators and objectives. A discrete/tree/rearrangement or
duality reduction must be established rather than inferred from the name.

The genuinely new target obligation is quantitative control of the actual
C_f(sigma_N), uniformly in N, strong enough to resolve how much of the R1
E_R discount ln N survives supplied-entanglement formation. Additivity,
generic uncertainty/relative-entropy lower bounds, a dimension substitution,
or strict irreversibility of each fixed state alone do not settle this.
An entropy-versus-hierarchical-projection estimate with N-independent defect
could settle it, but would need its exact constants, domain (complex vectors),
normalization, direction, and reduction to every convex-roof ensemble proved.
