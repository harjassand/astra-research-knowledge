UNIVERSAL PREPARATION WITNESS: FRESH ADVERSARIAL AUDIT
21 October 2026, restore_preparation_lemma.

QUESTION AUDITED
The principal's new extension removes the maximally-correlated (MC) support
hypothesis from the formation witness on finite outputs. This audit is a fresh
check of the Schmidt comparison, not a repetition of the prior MC proof.
The hierarchy is P_i=phi_D^{tensor i} tensor I on all later output pairs,
1<=i<=N. Entire output states and all their correlations are retained.

VERDICT
The proposed Schmidt comparison and universal E_F consequence are correct.
They yield a universal exact expected Bell-factory preparation-cost lower
bound for normal-memory exact Bell-output/approximate-return devices. They do
NOT alone give a general regularized E_C lower bound for arbitrary reservoirs.

1. SCHMIDT REARRANGEMENT DOMINANCE, WITH ALL INDICES
Let H_A=H_B=(C^D)^{tensor N}; write L=D^i and R=D^{N-i}. Order the local basis
with the i prefix digits varying fastest, so each fixed suffix gives one
contiguous block of L coordinates. Let |psi> be arbitrary, with sorted Schmidt
weights lambda_1>=...>=lambda_{LR}>=0. Set c_k=sqrt(lambda_k), and construct
  |psi_sorted_MC>=sum_{k=1}^{LR} c_k |k>_A|k>_B.
For every i simultaneously,
  <psi,P_i psi> <= <psi_sorted_MC,P_i psi_sorted_MC>
                = B_i(lambda)
                :=(1/L)sum_{j=1}^R (sum_{k=(j-1)L+1}^{jL}c_k)^2.      (S)

Proof. Write |v>=(<phi_L| tensor I_rest)|psi>. Then
  p_i:=<psi,P_i psi>=||v||^2=max_{||eta||=1}|<phi_L tensor eta|psi>|^2.
The equality remains valid at v=0. If eta has descending Schmidt weights
r_1,...,r_R, the Schmidt roots of phi_L tensor eta are sqrt(r_j)/sqrt(L),
each repeated L times. The singular-value trace inequality for coefficient
matrices bounds overlap between two normalized bipartite pure states by the
dot product of their descending Schmidt-root lists. Therefore
  |<phi_L tensor eta|psi>|
  <=(1/sqrt(L))sum_j sqrt(r_j) C_j,
  C_j=sum_{k=(j-1)L+1}^{jL}c_k.
Cauchy-Schwarz and sum r_j=1 give squared overlap <=sum_j C_j^2/L.
The relaxation to arbitrary relative Schmidt bases can only increase overlap.
The maximizing choice r_j=C_j^2/sum_l C_l^2 is descending because c is
nonincreasing, so no ordering obstruction invalidates the optimization.
Finally a direct prefix projection of psi_sorted_MC gives exactly B_i. The
same one-time ordering lambda_1>=...>=lambda_{D^N} is used for all i, and the
nested contiguous D^i blocks agree with the chosen fixed digit ordering.
There is no separately optimized, incompatible permutation for different i.
This proves S, including simultaneous attainability by a single MC vector.

Important distinction: the physical psi need not be local-unitarily transformable
into psi_sorted_MC while preserving its measured prefix overlaps. That is not
required. The comparison is an upper envelope at fixed Schmidt spectrum, and
entropy of entanglement depends only on that spectrum.

2. UNIVERSAL PURE AND MIXED WITNESSES
Apply the proved uniform entropy hierarchy to the single-system vector c:
  H(lambda)>=m sum_i ||P_i^{single} c||^2-C_D
            =m sum_i B_i(lambda)-C_D
            >=m sum_i <psi,P_i psi>-C_D,                  (U1)
where m=ln D and the principal's elementary induction gives C_D=2D-ln D.
The independent rearrangement/Hardy proof gives an alternative finite C_D,
so the conclusion does not rely on an unverified log-Sobolev constant.
Average U1 over every pure decomposition of any density operator rho on the
finite output space, then take the infimum:
  E_F(rho)>=m sum_i Tr(P_i rho)-C_D.                       (U2)
This argument allows arbitrary classical or quantum correlations among output
blocks. It neither assumes independent block states nor postselects a free
separable memory. Later retained memory is simply traced out when defining
rho; local tracing cannot increase E_F. U2 itself makes no independence claim.
A negative right-hand side is harmless; one can also take max{0,RHS}.

3. APPLYING THE EXISTING STOPPING BOUND
Use the old exact-task normal-memory construction, not an approximate-output
surrogate. One block emits an exact rank-D Bell state and returns the COMPLETE
retained memory within half trace distance delta. The existing trace-contraction
and telescoping argument, repeated for N calls of the same block channel,
gives for the actual joint finite output state rho_N
  Tr(P_i rho_N)=p_{>=i}>=[1-(i-1)delta]_+.
All prefix projections can be deferred until outputs are released. Therefore
  E_F(rho_N)>=m A_N(delta)-C_D,
  A_N(delta)=sum_{i=1}^N[1-(i-1)delta]_+.                  (U3)
For 0<delta<=1 choose N=ceil(1/delta). Then
  A_N(delta)>=1/(2delta)+1/2,
so
  E_F(rho_N)>=m/(2delta)+m/2-C_D.                         (U4)
Unlike the relative-entropy stopping measurement, no stopping-variable entropy
is subtracted here. This is a different resource/preparation consequence of
the existing return argument, not an improvement of its old E_R constant.

4. THE CLEAN OPERATIONAL CONVENTION: EXACT EXPECTED BELL FACTORY COST
Define a permissible factory as follows. It starts from separable ancillary
systems and a counted supply of pure Bell entanglement. A classical random
branch t consumes a finite rank-M_t maximally entangled state, costing ln M_t
nats; more generally t consumes pure entangled inputs of total entropy b_t.
After allowed LOCC preparation it supplies the EXACT declared memory state tau,
with all retained shared registers included. Discarded classical records are
actually inaccessible during reuse. The expected charge is B=sum_t w_t b_t,
possibly infinite. Unconditioned output states include every branch.

Compose this factory with the complete N-block device. Conditional on t the
operation is LOCC from the counted entangled input to the finite output rho_N^t.
The entanglement of formation cannot exceed that input pure entropy:
  E_F(rho_N^t)<=b_t.
Convexity and the exact ensemble rho_N=sum_t w_t rho_N^t give
  E_F(rho_N)<=sum_t w_t E_F(rho_N^t)<=B.
Thus every such exact initializer must satisfy
  B>=m A_N(delta)-C_D>=m/(2delta)+m/2-C_D.                 (U5)
This direct formulation avoids relying on which convex-roof extension of E_F
is chosen on normal infinite-dimensional memory. Local ancillary Hilbert spaces
may be infinite: the combined operation still consumes a specified pure finite
entanglement resource and ends on the finite output. Standard pure-state
LOCC entropy monotonicity applies to any normal local channel, and discarding
systems can only lower the convex-roof output entanglement. If a broader
factory model is intended (arbitrary non-countable ensembles, uncounted borrowed
catalysts, retained inaccessible entanglement, or approximate initialization),
state and prove the corresponding monotonicity/error step separately.

A factory allowed to retain a classical preparation label in the actual memory
must meet the return condition for that label too. One cannot use the unflagged
return error while secretly retaining the flag. The exact unflagged initializer
is permitted to sample a rung and then discard that sample record.

5. MATCHING FLAGGED CONSUMABLE RESERVOIR
For delta=1/N take the ordinary uniform flagged rung bank k=1,...,N. Each
component stores k rank-D Bell pairs followed by classical chi_D pairs and
retains a perfectly correlated local classical flag k. The fixed device emits
the first bank pair, shifts the bank left, appends chi_D, and decrements flag k
(with the exhausted branch defined deterministically). After one block, the
uniform flagged mixture k=1,...,N becomes uniform k=0,...,N-1. Identical
interior flagged states cancel, while the two endpoint flagged states have
orthogonal support, giving complete-memory return error exactly 1/N.
The first output is exact Bell and decoupled because every initializer rung
has k>=1. Every branch and retained flag is counted. Exact expected Bell
preparation cost is
  B_flag=(N+1)m/2=m/(2delta)+m/2.
Together U5 and this construction match up to a D-dependent constant along
this exact grid. For arbitrary small delta choose N=ceil(1/delta); return
error is <=delta and cost <=m/(2delta)+m, retaining a bounded gap.
This is an ordinary consumable-entanglement matched comparator, not evidence
of a practical catalyst advantage. The construction is finite for each N.

6. CLOSEST UNSOLVED RESOURCE/ERROR BOUNDARIES
A. General E_C is lim_q E_F(tau^{tensor q})/q, and is <=E_F(tau), not >= it.
   The universal single-state E_F witness alone does not control regularization.
   E_C=E_F for the actual old MC reservoir is separately known/proved, so its
   preparation conclusion is valid. Do not transfer that equality to arbitrary
   reservoirs or arbitrary correlated output states.
B. Applying the one-copy witness separately to q marginals cannot be summed
   without a suitable strong-superadditivity statement. General E_F does not
   supply that statement. This is a load-bearing missing step, not bookkeeping.
C. An approximate initializer tau_tilde may perturb an N-block finite output by
   at most its half trace distance from tau. A finite-dimensional E_F continuity
   inequality can control this at FIXED N, but its dimension cost grows with N.
   A uniform joint limit or an asymptotic iid initialization argument requires
   explicit rates and is not proved by U5.
D. Exact expected Bell factory cost is different from fixed worst-case stock.
   A uniform flagged sample has mean (N+1)m/2 and worst-case Nm. State which
   convention is used when comparing implementations. U5 controls the mean;
   it automatically lower-bounds any deterministic worst-case budget too.
E. First-block exact Bell output is load-bearing for the existing linear prefix
   success bound with p_{>=1}=1. No noisy-output claim is made here.
F. Zero return error forces infinite exact expected preparation cost by letting
   N tend to infinity in U3. This does not establish a statement about arbitrary
   infinite E_R normal catalysts or experimentally accessible hidden-state cost.

ADVERSARIAL TESTS / ATTEMPTS
- Potential ordering counterexample: eta's repeated Schmidt roots could in
  principle reorder blocks. Rejected: both its roots and the block sums are
  decreasing; the maximizing r is already in the permitted order.
- Potential incompatible-prefix counterexample: independently sorting each
  prefix might not correspond to one vector. Rejected: all inequalities use
  the single sorted full Schmidt-root list and the common little-endian nested
  blocks. The aligned MC vector attains every B_i simultaneously.
- Potential overlap/purity confusion: p_i is not an amplitude. The maximum over
  eta is squared after the variational projection identity, and Cauchy-Schwarz
  supplies exactly 1/L times the SUM of squared block sums, not the square of
  a sum of block sums or an extra R factor.
- Potential hidden independence: no step factorizes rho_N into its marginals.
  P_i acts jointly on the first i outputs and remains tensor identity on all
  others. The convex-roof witnesses all correlations.
- Potential E_C extrapolation: invalid without new argument; retained as open.

STATUS / COST
No computation or numerical optimizer was run. This is a complete internal
analytic check of S, U1-U5, and the matching expected-cost convention. It is
not external mathematical certification or a historical novelty determination.


ADAPTIVE-FACTORY PRECISION (FOLLOW-UP)
The per-branch inequality in Section4 refers to a branch t chosen as classical
INPUT randomness specifying the resource state before a deterministic LOCC
factory. It must not be applied to arbitrary postselected measurement outcomes:
those can raise conditional entanglement. For a more general adaptive factory
that injects Bell entanglement along an LOCC tree, use budget flow instead.
At every local measurement, conditional expected E_F does not increase; at an
injection of b nats of pure Bell resource it increases by at most b (pure Bell
additivity gives equality for that tensor factor). Starting from separable
systems, telescoping over any finite tree gives average final E_F no greater
than expected total injected b. Convexity then bounds the unconditioned finite
output. For countably unbounded stopping times, truncate the protocol and use
a defined convergence convention plus finite-output continuity; finite expected
cost and almost-sure termination suffice under trace-norm convergence. All
retained catalysts must be included in the input budget or separately returned
under a contract that justifies subtracting them. This refinement is compatible
with U5 but should replace the naive conditional-branch line if the principal
uses the broader adaptive-factory convention.
