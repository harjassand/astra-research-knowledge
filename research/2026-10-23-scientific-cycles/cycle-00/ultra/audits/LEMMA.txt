THEOREM
Let D>=2 be an integer, m=ln D, and B_D=m/(1-D^{-1/2})^2.
For the ordinary asymptotic LOCC entanglement cost E_C, measured in nats
(consumed Bell bits times ln 2),
  (N+1)m/2 - B_D <= E_C(tau_N)=E_F(tau_N) <= (N+1)m/2.       (T1)
In particular E_C(tau_N)-E_R(tau_N)=ln N+O_D(1).
The missing log N is therefore not an operational Bell preparation saving,
even under collective, asymptotically vanishing-error preparation of many
copies of a fixed reservoir. The order of limits is fixed D,N, copy count
q->infinity for E_C, followed by N->infinity for the displayed asymptotics.
No finite-block implementation efficiency or single-copy optimal exact cost
is claimed. The constant B_D is deliberately crude and not the new target.

UNIFORM HIERARCHICAL ENTROPY WITNESS
On H=(C^D)^{tensor N}, let u=D^{-1/2}sum_a|a>, and
  P_i=(|u><u|)^{tensor i} tensor I^{tensor(N-i)}, 1<=i<=N.
For EVERY unit vector psi=sum_x c_x|x>, with p_x=|c_x|^2,
  H(p) >= m sum_{i=1}^N <psi,P_i psi> - B_D.                (W)
This is the load-bearing new mathematical obligation in this note.

Proof of W, including the rearrangement step.
Order basis coordinates so that each first-i-coordinate averaging block is a
contiguous block of D^i indices (little-endian lexicographic order suffices).
For any complex c,
  <c,P_i c> = D^{-i} sum_blocks |sum_{x in block} c_x|^2
             <= D^{-i} sum_blocks (sum_{x in block}|c_x|)^2.
Let g be the same absolute amplitudes arranged in nonincreasing order in this
common coordinate ordering. For a fixed block size B=D^i, the vector of sums
of contiguous sorted blocks majorizes the block-sum vector from ANY grouping
of the same nonnegative amplitudes into B-element blocks: the sum of the
largest t arbitrary block sums is at most the sum of the largest tB individual
amplitudes, which is exactly the sum of the first t sorted block sums. Totals
are equal. Convexity of square therefore gives
  <psi,P_i psi> <= <g,P_i g>, for every i simultaneously.
Entropy is unchanged by phases and permutations, so it suffices to treat g.

Set A_0={1}, A_t={D^{t-1}+1,...,D^t}, 1<=t<=N,
n_t=|A_t|, g_t=1_{A_t}g, and q_t=||g_t||_2^2.
Since p_j=g_j^2 is decreasing and sum p_j=1, p_j<=1/j. Thus
  H(p)=sum_j p_j ln(1/p_j) >= sum_j p_j ln j
      >= m sum_{t=1}^N (t-1)q_t.                         (W1)
Zero probabilities are interpreted by continuity.

For fixed i, every shell A_t with t>i is a union of complete D^i-blocks.
P_i therefore preserves its support, and it is a contraction there. All shells
t<=i lie in the first block. Different outer shells and the first block do
not mix. Consequently
  <g,P_i g> <= sum_{t>i} q_t
      + D^{-i} (sum_{t=0}^i ||g_t||_1)^2
  <= sum_{t>i} q_t
      + (sum_{t=0}^i r^{i-t} sqrt(q_t))^2,               (W2)
where r=D^{-1/2}; the last inequality uses n_t<=D^t.
After summing the first term over i=1,...,N it is
  sum_{t=1}^N (t-1)q_t.
For the second term, extend v_t=sqrt(q_t) by zero outside 0,...,N, and apply
Young's l^1*l^2 convolution inequality to a_j=r^j 1_{j>=0}:
  sum_{i=1}^N (sum_{t=0}^i r^{i-t}v_t)^2
  <= ||a*v||_2^2 <= ||a||_1^2 ||v||_2^2=(1-r)^{-2}.
Together W1 and W2 give W. Every constant is independent of N. QED.

FROM THE WITNESS TO FORMATION
Use the isometry V: |x> -> |x>_A|x>_B. Its image is the maximally correlated
(MC) support. Every pure vector in every ensemble decomposition of a density
operator supported there lies there: for any vector z in the orthogonal
complement, 0=<z,rho z>=sum_l p_l |<z,psi_l>|^2.
A vector V|psi> has entanglement entropy H(|c_x|^2). Averaging W over an
arbitrary ensemble therefore gives, for any MC rho,
  E_F(rho) >= m sum_i Tr(rho V P_i V*) - B_D.               (F1)
Here V P_i V* is a projector within the MC support, not a claimed physically
free measurement. This is a mathematical witness only.

For the k-th prefix component of tau_N,
  Tr(rho_k V P_i V*) = 1              if i<=k,
                       D^{-(i-k)}    if i>k.
Thus
  sum_i Tr(tau_N V P_i V*)
  = (N+1)/2 + (1/N)sum_{r=1}^{N-1}(N-r)D^{-r}
  >= (N+1)/2.                                             (F2)
With a=1/D the extra term is
  a/(1-a) - a(1-a^N)/(N(1-a)^2).
This proves the lower bound for E_F. For the upper bound, each chi_D tail is
a mixture of computational basis product vectors. Sample k uniformly and
sample its classical correlated tail. Every resulting pure MC vector has
exactly k ln D entanglement, giving average (N+1)m/2.

WHY E_C=E_F HERE (KNOWN MACHINERY, NOT NOVELTY)
MC E_F equals the coherence of formation C_f of V* rho V. One can check the
needed additivity directly. For any pure bipartite SINGLE-SYSTEM vector
|psi>_{XY}, let p(x,y) be its squared basis amplitudes. The chain rule gives
  H(X,Y)=H(X)+sum_x p_x H(Y|X=x).
The first term is at least C_f(Tr_Y |psi><psi|), since C_f(sigma)<=H(diag sigma).
The second term is at least C_f(Tr_X |psi><psi|), since measurement in the X
basis supplies a pure-state ensemble of the Y marginal. Average over a pure
ensemble of any rho_XY and use convexity of C_f to obtain strong
superadditivity C_f(rho_XY)>=C_f(rho_X)+C_f(rho_Y). Product ensembles give the
opposite inequality on product states. Therefore C_f, and hence MC E_F,
is additive on tensor products. The standard finite-dimensional LOCC
entanglement-cost theorem E_C=lim_q E_F(rho^{tensor q})/q now yields E_C=E_F.

Primary comparator actually read: Winter and Yang, Operational Resource Theory
of Coherence, PRL 116,120404 (2016), arXiv:1506.07975v3,

Discussion explicitly identifies E_C=E_F for MC states; proof of Theorem 9

The finite-dimensional regularized-cost theorem is standard existing machinery;

EXPLICIT PREPARATION UPPER BOUND AND COST CONVENTION
For fixed D,N and q independent reservoirs, sample K_1,...,K_q uniformly.
Their total Bell-dimension stock is S=sum K_j, E S=q(N+1)/2. Choose a fixed
stock corresponding to S<=q[(N+1)/2+epsilon]. Hoeffding gives failure
probability <=exp[-2 q epsilon^2/(N-1)^2] for N>1. On success allocate the
necessary maximally entangled pairs to the selected prefix components and
prepare the remaining chi tails by free shared randomness and LOCC; discard
all K labels. On failure prepare any free replacement. Mixing changes the
ideal target by half trace distance at most the failure probability.
When D is not a power of two, asymptotic pure-state dilution implements the
stock at rate ln D per D-Bell pair, with its own vanishing error. One may
instead use rank-D maximally entangled states as the unit for the upper
construction, converting rates asymptotically. This charges the complete
initialization and discarded flags; it does not claim those labels remain
accessible to subsequent reuse.

CONSEQUENCE / MATCHED COMPARISON
The old unflagged E_R saving ln N+O_D(1) was real as a state monotone and
remains unchanged. Under ordinary vanishing-error asymptotic LOCC preparation,
its Bell cost stays within a D-dependent constant of the obvious flagged
mixture's mean stock. Hence the saving becomes an asymptotically logarithmic
formation-versus-relative-entropy gap, rather than a logarithmic reduction in
ordinary entanglement consumed to initialize this reservoir.
For MC states the known equality E_D=E_R would also turn this into
E_C-E_D=ln N+O_D(1), but the principal should charge and cite the hashing
achievability if using that extra operational interpretation.

CLOSE FAILURE BOUNDARIES
1. F1 requires known MC support and the actual uniform prefix hierarchy.
   It does not give a universal preparation lower bound for arbitrary devices,
   non-MC reservoirs, arbitrary approximate output, or arbitrary embezzlers.
2. Merely knowing E_R cannot imply this formation cost: the old exact E_R law
   is lower by ln N, and general resource measures differ operationally.
3. No statement here gives finite-q optimal preparation, a dimension-free
   continuity bound for approximate reservoirs with N->infinity jointly, or
   inexpensive experimental certification of hidden retained memory.
4. A retained flag changes the state/return contract. The upper preparation
   samples then actually discards it; the lower bound already optimizes over
   every pure decomposition of the resulting unflagged state.
5. E_C=E_F is specific to MC support. General E_F is not additive.

FAILED / NONDECISIVE ROUTES PRESERVED
A. Ordinary entropic uncertainty H(X)+H(Z)>=N ln D gives only
   H(X)>=ln D E[K]-H(K); the ln N defect survives and cannot settle preparation.
B. A Golden-Thompson trace-exponential estimate gives
   (1/D^N)Tr exp[m sum_i P_i]=1+N(1-D^{-1}); its logarithm also loses ln N.
   It is too weak for the critical witness, not a counterexample to W.
C. The coherent square-root spectral ensemble was considered analytically.
   Its amplitudes are near a coherent sum of nested prefix vectors; no
   improved formation cost was established. No optimizer was run, and no
   numerical failure is promoted to a bound.
D. Standard dimension/energy substitution and small old-constant improvements
   are unnecessary and outside this obligation. The finite B_D is not sharp.

INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF PRINCIPAL'S SHORTER ENTROPY INDUCTION
The principal supplied the following alternative while this rearrangement
proof was being written. It is valid and gives C_D=2D-ln D (often smaller).
This audit was analytical and did not run computation.

Replace c by |c|, since every P_i has nonnegative entries, retaining entropy
and increasing every projector expectation. Write x for the first D-ary
coordinate, y for the suffix, q_y=sum_x c_xy^2, p(x|y)=c_xy^2/q_y,
r_y=(sum_x sqrt(p(x|y)))^2/D, a_y=D^{-1/2}sum_x c_xy,
and s=sum_y a_y^2=sum_y q_y r_y. The recursion of prefix projections is exact:
  sum_{i=2}^N <c,P_i c> = s sum_{j=1}^{N-1}<v,P_j v>, v=a/sqrt(s).
If s=0 the positive-amplitude vector is zero, impossible for a unit vector;
more generally the limiting branch is harmless. Terms q_y=0 are omitted.
The induction hypothesis bounds the defect by
  s C_D + m s + s H(q_y r_y/s) - H(q_y) - sum_y q_y H(p(.|y)).
Introduce an ordinary accept bit A with Pr(A=1|Y=y)=r_y. The entropy chain
rule and nonnegative mutual information give
  H(Y) >= H(Y|A)
       = s H(Y|A=1)+(1-s)H(Y|A=0)
       >= s H(q_y r_y/s).
It remains to use H(p)>=m r-C_D(1-r), equivalently
D(p||uniform)<= (m+C_D)(1-r). This follows elementarily with m+C_D=2D:
D(p||u)<=chi^2(p,u); set f_x=sqrt(Dp_x). Then
chi^2=Var_u(f^2). For distinct x,z,
  (f_x+f_z)^2 <=2(f_x^2+f_z^2)<=2D,
so the pairwise-variance identity yields
  Var_u(f^2)<=2D Var_u(f)=2D(1-r).
Consequently the remaining defect is at most
sC_D+ms-[ms-C_D(1-s)]=C_D, closing the induction from N=0.
The accept-bit reasoning is essential: entropy of a selected branch can exceed
unconditional entropy, but its probability-weighted entropy is bounded as above.
