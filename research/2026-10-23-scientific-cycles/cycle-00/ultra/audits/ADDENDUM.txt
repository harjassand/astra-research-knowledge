1. STRONG SINGLE-FIDELITY E_F COMPARATOR

[TV00] B. M. Terhal and K. G. H. Vollbrecht, "The Entanglement of Formation
for Isotropic States", Phys. Rev. Lett. 85, 2625 (2000).
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0005062
 https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.85.2625
Read the original complete 4-page argument, particularly Lemma1 Eqs.(9)-(10),
Lemma2, Theorem1 Eqs.(21)-(23), and concluding convex-hull discussion.
The exact isotropic E_F is the convex hull of
 R_q(F)=h(gamma)+(1-gamma) ln(q-1),
 gamma=q^(-1)[sqrt(F)+sqrt((q-1)(1-F))]^2,
with zero below F=1/q. The convex-hull expression is exact in all dimensions;
the paper initially leaves the simple piecewise hull formula conjectural for
general q. LOCC twirling implies E_F(omega)>=coR_q(<Phi_q|omega|Phi_q>)
for every q by q state. Thus this is the optimal bound that uses only that
single maximally entangled fidelity, not merely a convenient continuity bound.

[FLJ06] S.-M. Fei and X. Li-Jost, "R-function Related to Entanglement of
Formation", Phys. Rev. A 73, 024302 (2006).
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0602137
 https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.73.024302
Read the original 3-page proof. It establishes the required one-inflection
property and validates the all-dimensional piecewise convex hull. For q>=3,
on the upper linear branch F>=4(q-1)/q^2 the natural-log value is
 coR_q(F)=ln q + [q ln(q-1)/(q-2)](F-1).
For q=2 use the binary formula instead of the singular q-2 expression.

Model-specific matched calculation (our derivation):
If f_i=Tr(P_i rho), tracing the tail and twirling gives the valid old bound
 E_F(rho) >= max_i coR_{D^i}(f_i).
Suppose the only certified numerical lower bounds supplied by a factory's
return argument are f_i >= [1-(i-1)delta]_+. Then the single-prefix reduction
gives max_i coR_{D^i}([1-(i-1)delta]_+).
As delta->0 at fixed D, for i near 1/(2delta) the q=D^i linear branch applies
and coR_q(f)=f ln q+o(1). Uniformly in q the difference from f ln q is bounded
by an absolute constant (the nonlinear branch occurs at F=O(1/q)). Hence
 max_i coR_{D^i}([1-(i-1)delta]_+)
   = (ln D)/(4delta)+O_D(1).
Indeed max_i i[1-(i-1)delta] = 1/(4delta)+O(1).

By comparison, the principal's N-independent-defect sum yields
 (ln D) sum_i [1-(i-1)delta]_+ - C_D
   = (ln D)/(2delta)+O_D(1)
when enough prefixes are retained. This factor-two comparison is confined to
using the individually optimal single-prefix bounds separately and taking
their maximum. It is NOT a proof of superiority to every multi-observable
convex-roof optimization or every other consequence of the complete process.
The point is that adding separately valid E_F bounds would require a new
argument; general strong superadditivity of E_F is unavailable.

2. VERY CLOSE 2026 MULTISCALE PAPER: INPUTS AND OBJECTIVES DIFFER

[X26] L. Xiong, Z. Jin, Y. Wang, W. Chen, H. Tao and N.-s. Sze,
"Multiscale Schmidt-Spectrum Bounds for High-Dimensional Entanglement:
Geometric Measures and Schmidt-Number Witnesses", arXiv:2609.21209v1,
18 September 2026 (36 pages).
 https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21209
 https://arxiv.org/html/2609.21209
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.21209
Read original Secs.I, III.2 (Theorem1/Eq20 and Proposition5), III.2.1,
III.3 (Theorem2's setup and statement), and explicit limitations in Sec.VI.
Theorem1 bounds normalized nuclear norm (sum sqrt(mu))^2/q using supplied
ordered Vidal-tail block masses, with blockwise uniformity as equality.
Theorem2 optimizes weighted Vidal-tail costs under a Hellinger/nuclear-norm
constraint. Its geometric tails and CREN are different from Shannon E_F.
Its nested local projectors require certified Schmidt modes/pure-source
information. Sec.III.2.1 explicitly warns that mixed-state fixed-subspace
populations are not convex-roof Vidal tails. No conversion of the proposed
physical Bell-prefix expectations into those required tail inputs is given.
Consequently these theorems do not by themselves prove the principal's sum
bound. They are close prior art for using several spectral scales, so avoid
claims that multiscale entanglement witnesses or blockwise Cauchy-Schwarz
arguments themselves are new.

3. GENERAL MULTI-OBSERVABLE WITNESS MACHINERY IS OLD

[EBA07] J. Eisert, F. G. S. L. Brandao and K. M. R. Audenaert,
"Quantitative entanglement witnesses", New J. Phys. 9, 46 (2007),
arXiv:quant-ph/0607167v4.
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0607167
 https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/9/3/046
Read original pp.3-5, "Convex hull measures and the conjugate function",
Eqs.(21)-(36). For pure-state reduced entropy f, convex-roof duality gives
 E_F(rho)>=Tr(A rho)-f*(A),
 f*(A)=sup_pure psi [<psi,A psi>-S(psi_A)].
Using all observed operators and optimizing coefficients supplies the best
data-consistent bound in principle. Its global optimization need not be cheap.
For A=(ln D)sum_i P_i, the proposed theorem is precisely a uniform upper
bound f*(A)<=C_D. The new obligation is therefore the concrete conjugate/
hierarchical entropy bound, not general witness duality or convexification.
This old framework must remain in the strongest conceptual comparator.

4. HARDY / LOG-SOBOLEV BOUNDARY

PRIOR_ART.txt records the original sharp Osekowski 2016 result, DOI
10.4171/ZAA/1552, which was read again while assessing this extension.
Its L1 cumulative-mass entropy bound does not directly bound the sum of
L2 prefix conditional expectations of amplitudes. The normalization, operator
and entropy objectives differ. No exact theorem matching the principal's
complex-vector finite D-ary tensor hierarchy was found in this bounded check.
This is an unresolved historical search boundary, not proof of novelty.

The principal's Schmidt reduction is structurally consistent with known
singular-value overlap methods: a vector in range(phi_k tensor I) has Schmidt
amplitudes sqrt(nu_j/k), each repeated k times. Maximizing its overlap with
a pure vector having ordered Schmidt probabilities lambda gives
 [1/k] sum_j [sum_{r=1}^k sqrt(lambda_{(j-1)k+r})]^2.
The same ordered lambda can be used for all k=D^i. The entropy-projection
lemma must then control their sum with one dimension-independent defect.
Individual fidelity optimizations do not justify summing their entropy costs.

5. OPERATIONAL CLAIMS THAT THESE COMPARATORS DO / DO NOT SUPPORT

For an arbitrary exact Bell factory, an E_F lower bound on its complete
finite output state pulls back to the initial reservoir by LOCC monotonicity.
This is a universal E_F/resource-witness consequence, allowing all output
correlations and non-MC memories. It is not automatically a universal lower
bound on regularized asymptotic E_C of arbitrary non-MC memories. Additivity
cannot be silently assumed. For the actual tau_N family, VDC02 does supply
the separate known E_C=E_F fact, as recorded in PRIOR_ART.txt.

The fixed nonzero return condition and stopping argument must independently
establish the entire set of certified f_i lower bounds. Neither fidelity
tomography nor the new witness certifies hidden-memory return without the
extra access/trust assumptions already identified by R1.
