specified records under these models. An inconclusive result will not be
reported as absence of cores. A failure of both predictive models blocks a
physical core/cusp conclusion, regardless of their relative score.

ORDINARY ANALYSIS AND IMPROVED OPERATION
Reproduce the published NFW and source-iso forward models and full normalized
Gaussian likelihood, including fitted squared-exponential radial covariance.
The corrected model replaces only the iso enclosed-mass function by x-atan(x),
or equivalently removes r200/R from the source iso squared-velocity expression.
All models receive the same input, priors, covariance flexibility and fitting
budget. Independent-error fits are diagnostic only; the strong GP comparator
is mandatory. The improvement is a physical forward-model repair and a blocked
predictive discrimination, not a novel generic estimator or faster sampler.

PARAMETERS / ERROR CONVENTIONS
Use H0=0.073 km/s/kpc and G=4.30091727003628e-6 kpc(km/s)^2/Msun.
Published priors: log10 M200 uniform[8,14], log10 c uniform[0,3],
log10 stellar M/L Normal(-0.3,0.2). GP log10 variance uniform[-4,5] in
(km/s)^2 and log10 scale uniform[-2,3] in fiducial kpc. Retain the log-determinant
and likelihood normalization; a reduced chi-square alone is not model evidence.
Primary validation adds independent catalog-centered Gaussian priors on
D/D0 with sd eD/D0, truncated positive, and inclination with sd eInc truncated
to (1,89) degrees. These Gaussian priors approximate quoted catalog errors.
The forward model uses physical R=(D/D0)R0, baryonic squared velocities scaled
by D/D0, and maps velocities to reported fiducial-inclination units via
sin(i)/sin(i0). Quoted velocity errors and GP covariance stay in those reported
units; GP radial separation is in the catalog's fiducial kpc coordinates.
The template shape is held fixed, as in this class of reduced-curve analysis;
noncircular motion, beam smearing, warps and other discrepancies are not
separately observed and may be absorbed by the empirical GP.

Run a required sensitivity replacing the squared-exponential kernel by
Matern-3/2 at the same variance/scale priors. The kernel is a discrepancy
model, not a measured instrumental covariance. Equal broad parameter priors
do not make model evidence prior-independent; conditional outer-to-inner
prediction is therefore the main scientific score.

SCORES AND ACCEPTANCE (FROZEN)
For model m and each galaxy calculate S_m=log Z_full - log Z_outer, the
log posterior predictive density of the inner vector given the outer data.
Z includes every declared nuisance parameter. Primary discrimination is
Delta=sum_g(S_core-S_NFW), positive for the true core. Report the source-iso
score separately to expose the scientific effect of the code repair.
Support a core (or conversely NFW) only if the sign is shared by at least two
of the three galaxies, |Delta|-2*MC_standard_error > log(10), and the sign and
threshold survive the Matern sensitivity. In addition the favored model must
not fail the prespecified conditional predictive check: simulated inner
vectors from its outer posterior, tested by a Mahalanobis distance using the
mixture predictive mean/covariance, must have tail probability >=0.025 in
each galaxy. This is a model-conditional diagnostic, not a guarantee against
unmodeled physics. If numerical error can change acceptance, report unresolved
or increase integration accuracy under the same unchanged scientific rule.
No result is selected by the training fit alone. No historical novelty claim.
