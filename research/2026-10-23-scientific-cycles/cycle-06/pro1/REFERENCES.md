This is a bounded comparison of theorem premises, not a claim of exhaustive
historical novelty. The actual primary theorem passages were read on
22 October 2026. Search was limited to public literature; no private proof
content or request to authors was sent.

## Closest coding machinery

Vittorio Giovannetti, Daniel Burgarth and Stefano Mancini,
*Communication Through a Quantum Link*, Physical Review A79,012311(2009).
https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.0170
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0810.0170

Definition1 and Theorem2 give established perfect-memory coding through a
subexponential dilation environment, including a finite-error-set code
bound. This is the closest ordinary coding mechanism behind the inherited
compression operation. Our full bath is infinite. The additional work is
input-relative overflow, coherent-initializer joint control, normalization
and polar repair; Cycle6 then supplies the all-length guarded susceptibility
and its uniform nonlinear realization. The broad coding principle is inherited.

Dennis Kretschmann and Reinhard F.Werner, *Quantum Channels with Memory*,
Physical Review A72,062323(2005).
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0502106
https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0502106

Theorem13 requires finite-dimensional forgetful memory. The present bath is
infinite, and selected-input inward drift is not a proof of forgetfulness.
That theorem cannot be applied by treating repeated inputs as independent
physical block channels. The full same-bath isometry is used instead.

## Temporal extent is not a generally new phenomenon

Toby Cubitt, David Elkouss, William Matthews, Maris Ozols,
David Pérez-García and Sergii Strelchuk,
*Unbounded number of channel uses may be required to detect quantum capacity*,
Nature Communications6,6739(2015).
https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms7739
https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.5115
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.5115

Their memoryless channel family can require arbitrarily many uses before
positive coherent information is detected. It is therefore inappropriate
to claim generic priority for the importance of long temporal inputs.
The new result is a quantitative growing-block construction for the
specified fixed infinite-memory unitary family, with a verified full
operator constraint and actual small-angle coding consequence. No theorem
in that paper is asserted to prove this model-specific rate.
