\begin{abstract}
For the fixed recurrent qutrit tree device, the previous source proves a
quartic small-angle information law for every fixed temporal block and
an operator-drift coding interface that already admits non-scalar bath
dynamics. We preserve both results and resolve their selected next
boundary: the benefit per call of the complete operator variational
problem is unbounded with temporal length.

An exact closest-point dual identifies this benefit with a minimum
weighted variance over all bath-prefix densities. Guarded nonnegative
temporal paths contain input-only color transpositions that leave the
entire bath unchanged. Their contributions survive every coherent prefix
state and prove a variance lower bound of order $k^{3/2}$. No prefix
average substitutes for the operator constraint.

Uniform positivity, entropy and nonlinear drift estimates then turn this
growth into an actual communication theorem. For $0<\beta\le2^{-11}$,
one initializer-independent deterministic unassisted code sequence has
rate at least $3/2+(\ln2/4096)\beta^{64/17}$, with pointwise vanishing
full-diamond error for every fixed normal initializer. The block length
is chosen from the fixed device parameter before the coding horizon.
The exponent is a proved lower-bound exponent, not a sharp capacity law.
No useful finite blocklength, efficient compiler, feedback equality,
physical device performance or historical priority is established.
\end{abstract}

\section{The exact bulk observable and its temporal decomposition}

Set $b=\beta^2$ and
\[
 \rho_c=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1/4,1/4),\quad
 \sigma=\rho_c^{\otimes k},\quad -\log_2\sigma=kI+Q_k.
\]
We use $Q=Q_k$ when $k$ is fixed. The bounded displacement is
\[
 G_s=Q-W_k(s)^*QW_k(s),\qquad \norm{G_s}\le k.
 \tag{5}
\]
It equals $W_k^*LW_k-L$ on the finite-coordinate core; (5) defines it
without subtracting infinite mean depths. For a charge-preserving input
matrix $X$, its contraction gives the bath drift
$D_s(X)=\Tr_A[(X\otimes I)G_s]$, commuting with $L$.

On initial depth at least $k$, a physical $k$-call path has no vacuum
exchange. Input-charge pinching of $G_s$ is even in $s$: two move/stay
histories with the same input charge and output word have stay counts
of the same parity. It has the exact prefix representation inherited
from the full path algebra:
\[
 \mathcal G_k(b)=G_0+bG_1+\cdots+b^kG_k,\qquad
 G_0=(2Q-kI)\otimes I.
 \tag{6}
\]
Here the second factor is the first $k$ bath colors, and the operator
is tensor identity on the seed and untouched tail. This is an exact
bulk representation, not finite reflecting dynamics. The actual root
region remains in the channel and later Lyapunov estimate.

For completeness, finite-prefix dependence follows by expanding the
physical $k$-step histories. They can consume at most $k$ initial
colors. For an input-charge-preserving matrix element and a common
output word, the two final bath depths agree. Their equal-length tails
are untouched, so every final-bath overlap is determined by the first
$k$ initial colors. Products of the amplitudes have even sine and cosine
degrees after charge pinching and hence are polynomials in $b$.

\begin{lemma}[Endpoint decomposition and uniform bounds]\label{lem:endpoints}
For a prefix density $\zeta$ define the Hermitian contraction
\[
 F_\zeta=\Tr_F[(I\otimes\zeta)G_1],\qquad
 H_\zeta=F_\zeta+2Q-kI.
 \tag{7}
\]
Then $H_\zeta$ is charge preserving and has zero computational input
diagonal. For every $\zeta$,
\[
 \Tr(\sigma H_\zeta)=\Tr(\sigma QH_\zeta)=0,
 \quad \Tr(\sigma H_\zeta^2)\le k(k-1),
 \quad \norm{H_\zeta}\le k(k-1),\quad \norm{G_1}\le k^2.
 \tag{8}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For a fixed computational input word in the deep bulk, move/stay
patterns have distinct output charge-bit words. Their bath vectors are
therefore summed incoherently after the output trace. The exact diagonal
displacement is $(1-b)(2Q-kI)$, which proves the diagonal assertion.

Here is the useful all-length operator representation. Define
\[
 C_i=U_0\cdots U_{i-1}U_iU_{i-1}\cdots U_0,
 \qquad C=\sum_iC_i.
 \tag{9}
\]
One may first work on coordinates sufficiently deep, for example depth
at least $4k$, that every displayed toggling word avoids the vacuum.
$C_i$ changes only the zero/nonzero charge bit at $i$: conjugation by
$U_j$ flips $q_j$ and commutes with $q_i$ for $i\ne j$ on this core.
Write $C_i=C_i^++C_i^-$ for its charge-raising/lowering parts; each
is a contraction and $(C_i^+)^*=C_i^-$. The interaction frame obeys
\[
 Z=W_k(0)^*W_k(s)=e^{isC_{k-1}}\cdots e^{isC_0},\qquad
 G_s=2Q-kI+(Z^*QZ-Q).
 \tag{10}
\]
The coefficient of $s^2$ after charge pinching is
$-[C,[C,Q]]/2=[C_-,C_+]$, where $C_\pm=\sum_iC_i^\pm$.
The additional ordered-product commutator is a commutator with $Q$
and its charge pinching vanishes. Since $b=s^2+O(s^4)$,
\[
 G_1=[C_-,C_+].
 \tag{11}
\]
Both sides compute the coefficient of the physical finite-prefix
operator (6). Proving the identity on arbitrary first-$k$ colors at
depth at least $4k$ identifies that finite operator, so its resulting
coefficient applies on every initial depth at least $k$. The extra
algebraic buffer does not change the physical boundary threshold.

The same-index terms in (11) sum to $kI-2Q$. For $i<j$ the remaining
term is
\[
 B_{ij}=C_i^-C_j^++C_j^-C_i^+-C_i^+C_j^--C_j^+C_i^-.
 \tag{12}
\]
It changes exactly the two endpoint charge bits. Distinct unordered
pairs therefore occupy disjoint matrix blocks between complete binary
charge patterns; after any bath-state contraction they are orthogonal
in the $\sigma$-weighted Hilbert--Schmidt inner product.

Write $B_{ij}=A_{ij}+A_{ij}^*$ with
$A_{ij}=C_i^-C_j^+-C_j^+C_i^-$, mapping endpoint pattern $10$ to
$01$. Its norm is at most two and its domain and range are orthogonal,
so $\norm{B_{ij}}\le2$. Bath-state contraction is unital and positive,
and preserves this norm bound. The square is supported on the two
endpoint sectors, of total $\sigma$ probability $1/2$. Each pair
contributes at most two to the weighted square norm. Summing proves
the variance and $H$ norm bounds. Adding the diagonal norm at most
$k$ proves $\norm{G_1}\le k+2\binom{k}{2}=k^2$.
\end{proof}

\section{An exact full-operator variational dual}

For Hermitian charge-preserving $X$ with $\Tr X=\Tr QX=0$ define
\[
 T_k(X)=\Tr_A[(X\otimes I)G_1],\quad
 \mathcal H_k(X)=\frac{\Tr(\sigma^{-1}X^2)}{2\ln2},\quad
 \mathcal A_k=\sup_X\left[-\frac12\lambda_{\max}(T_k(X))-
                               \mathcal H_k(X)\right].
 \tag{13}
\]
The quantity is per block. It is not an unrestricted capacity formula.

\begin{theorem}[Closest-point identity]\label{thm:dual}
Let
\[
 v_k=\min_{\zeta\ge0,\Tr\zeta=1}\Tr(\sigma H_\zeta^2).
 \tag{14}
\]
Then $\mathcal A_k=(\ln2)v_k/8$. A minimizing $\zeta_*$ exists.
For $H=H_{\zeta_*}$ and $M=T_k(\sigma H)$,
\[
 M\ge v_k I,\quad \lambda_{\min}(M)=v_k,\qquad
 X_*=-\frac{\ln2}{2}\sigma H
 \tag{15}
\]
attains (13). In particular (15) is a worst-prefix operator inequality.
The auxiliary density $\zeta_*$ is an analytic dual variable and is
never physically prepared in the bath.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The set $\{H_\zeta\}$ is compact and convex in the finite-dimensional
real Hilbert space with inner product
$\langle F,H\rangle_\sigma=\operatorname{Re}\Tr(\sigma FH)$.
All observables commute with $\sigma$, which is scalar on each charge
sector. Its minimum-norm point $H$ obeys the projection inequality
$\langle H,H_\zeta\rangle_\sigma\ge v_k$ for every density $\zeta$.
Since $H$ has zero input diagonal, this is precisely
$\Tr(\zeta M)\ge v_k$. Equality holds at $\zeta_*$, proving (15),
including possible boundary or non-full-rank minimizers.

For any feasible $X$, evaluate the maximum eigenvalue against
$\zeta_*$ and complete the square:
\[
 -\frac12\lambda_{\max}(T_k(X))-\mathcal H_k(X)
 \le-\frac12\Tr(XH)-\frac{\Tr(\sigma^{-1}X^2)}{2\ln2}
 \le\frac{\ln2}{8}v_k.
\]
The affine term $2Q-kI$ disappears by the two constraints on $X$.
The choice (15) is feasible by (8), and the operator inequality in
(15) gives equality. This is an application of the ordinary
closest-point principle, with no infinite-dimensional minimax exchange.
\end{proof}

\section{Guarded paths force superlinear susceptibility}

\begin{theorem}[All-prefix lower bound]\label{thm:growth}
For every $k\ge3$ and every prefix density $\zeta$,
\[
 \Tr(\sigma H_\zeta^2)
 \ge\frac14\sum_{r=0}^{k-3}(k-r-2)
             2^{-r}\binom r{\lfloor r/2\rfloor}.
 \tag{16}
\]
Consequently, for $k\ge4$,
\[
 \frac{k^{3/2}}{32}\le v_k\le k(k-1),\qquad
 \frac{\mathcal A_k}{k}\ge\frac{\ln2}{256}\sqrt{k}.
 \tag{17}
\]
The lower is uniform over coherent, entangled and non-product prefix
states. No assumption on a physical initializer enters (16).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $1\le i<j\le k-1$, and let $g=i-1$ be a guard position.
Select binary input charge patterns with $z_g=1,z_i=0,z_j=1$.
Require the intervening bits $z_{i+1},\ldots,z_{j-1}$, interpreted as
$+1$ for a color and $-1$ for zero, to have nonnegative partial sums.
All earlier and later bits are arbitrary. Such a middle word is a
nonnegative meander, not merely a word of nonnegative final height.

Put $E=U_{i-2}\cdots U_0$ and $M=U_{j-1}\cdots U_{i+1}$, with
empty products equal to $I$. Direct word cancellation gives
\begin{align}
 C_jC_i&=E^*[U_gU_iM^*U_jMU_g]E,\tag{W1}\label{eq:worda}\\
 C_iC_j&=E^*[U_gM^*U_jMU_iU_g]E.\tag{W2}\label{eq:wordb}
\end{align}
These identities use only $U_l^2=I$ and hold on the whole bath.

Decompose the middle meander uniquely as
$D_0\,1\,D_1\,1\cdots1\,D_h$, where the $D_r$ are balanced
nonnegative Dyck words and the displayed ones are unmatched pushes.
An all-move Dyck block sends each input color to its matching pop port,
and is an input-color permutation tensor the identity on the entire
bath: each pop consumes a color pushed inside the block. Its inverse
on the resulting output charge sector has the same bath-independent
property. Therefore the matched Dyck factors cancel in $M^*U_jM$,
even when the intermediate bath has changed. Nothing outside a Dyck
factor acts on its ports. This explains the cancellation as an operator
identity, rather than a classical probability replacement.

Label the guard color $a_0$, the unmatched middle colors in temporal
order $a_1,\ldots,a_h$, the endpoint color $a_{h+1}$, and the
arbitrary initial bath word $w$. On the reduced local registers, the
two bracketed words in \eqref{eq:worda}--\eqref{eq:wordb} give
\begin{align*}
 (a_0,0,a_1,\ldots,a_h,a_{h+1};w)
   &\longmapsto(a_0,a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_h,a_{h+1},0;w),\\
 (a_0,0,a_1,\ldots,a_h,a_{h+1};w)
   &\longmapsto(a_1,a_0,a_2,\ldots,a_h,a_{h+1},0;w).
\end{align*}
For $h=0$, $a_1$ means the endpoint color. In the first word the guard
is pushed, then the unmatched colors and endpoint are pushed; reversing
the middle pops shifts their colors and leaves stack $a_1a_0w$.
The last two pops return $a_1$ to $i$ and $a_0$ to the guard. In the
second word the guard is popped into $i$ immediately, and the final
guard pop obtains $a_1$. Both leave exactly $w$. Every pop is supplied
by a displayed local push, so even a vacuum bath invokes no exchange.
Matched middle colors are restored in both words.

Both local maps are input-only permutations tensor $I_E$. They commute
with the earlier-prefix operator $E$, so the outer conjugations cancel.
In the deep bulk the raising/lowering restrictions select exactly the
two products above. Thus the $01\to10$ block of (12) equals
\[
 K_{ij}=C_j^-C_i^+-C_i^+C_j^-
       =P(I-S)\otimes I_E
 \tag{18}
\]
on each selected charge pattern. $P$ is a color-register unitary to
the endpoint-swapped sector, and $S$ swaps the guard color with the
first unmatched color, or with the endpoint color if $h=0$. The two
wires are distinct. The raw bracketed-word permutations hold on the
entire bath; their identification with the raising/lowering coefficient
$K_{ij}$ and $G_1$ in (18) is a bulk statement. That finite-prefix
identity survives every prefix-state contraction, including coherent
prefixes and entanglement with an untouched tail. No global root formula
for the charge-raising/lowering pieces is asserted.
For example, with three ports, colors $(g,0,z)$ and a vacuum bath,
the raw toggle $C_1$ can preserve total input charge because a final
port-zero interaction uses $R$. Its global charge-raising component
then annihilates this vector. This does not affect the raw whole-bath
word identity, but rules out promoting the bulk identification to the root.

If the full charge pattern has $q$ colors, its normalized color density
is $I/2^q$. A swap on two color qubits has normalized trace $1/2$, so
\[
 2^{-q}\Tr[(P(I-S))^*P(I-S)]=2-2(1/2)=1.
 \tag{19}
\]
Under $\sigma$, charge bits are independent fair bits. The guard and
oriented endpoints have probability $1/8$. A length-$r$ fair walk is
a nonnegative meander with probability
$p_r=2^{-r}\binom r{\lfloor r/2\rfloor}$: reflection counts walks
ending at height $h\ge0$ as the difference of adjacent binomial
coefficients, and summing over allowed heights telescopes.

Each selected forward matrix block has weighted square norm
$p_r/8$. Its adjoint has equal weight and a disjoint input charge
sector, giving $p_r/4$ for the pair. Contributions from other complete
input charge patterns are nonnegative. Distinct endpoint pairs are
orthogonal by Lemma~\ref{lem:endpoints}. There are $k-r-2$ eligible
pairs with $j-i-1=r$, proving (16).

For all $r\ge0$, $p_r\ge[2(r+1)]^{-1/2}$. Indeed
$a_m=4^{-m}\binom{2m}m\ge1/(2\sqrt m)$ follows by induction from
$a_1=1/2$ and
$(2m+1)/(2m+2)\ge\sqrt{m/(m+1)}$; the odd walk probabilities obey
$p_{2m+1}=p_{2m+2}=a_{m+1}$. Therefore
\[
 \frac14\sum_{r=0}^{k-3}(k-r-2)p_r
 \ge\frac{(k-2)(k-1)}{8\sqrt{2k}}
 \ge\frac{3k^{3/2}}{64\sqrt2}>\frac{k^{3/2}}{32}
 \quad(k\ge4).
\]
Combine with (8) and Theorem~\ref{thm:dual}.
\end{proof}

\section{Uniform nonlinear transfer to an actual input state}

An unbounded local coefficient alone would not imply a rate gain at
any nonzero angle. We now pay the complete $k$ dependence.

\subsection{A uniform remainder for the physical unitary}

All derivatives of one $V_s$ have norm one. The product rule gives
$\norm{W_k^{(r)}(s)}\le k^r$ and, for $r\ge1$,
$\norm{G_s^{(r)}}\le2^rk^{r+1}$. Input-charge pinching and bulk
compression are norm contractions. Evenness from (6) and Taylor's
theorem give
\[
 \norm{\mathcal G_k(\sin^2s)-G_0-s^2G_1}
 \le\frac23k^5s^4.
\]
For $0\le\beta\le1/2$,
$s^2\le b/(1-b)$, $0\le s^2-b\le4b^2/3$ and
$s^4\le16b^2/9$. Even the coarse derivative bound
$\norm{G_1}\le2k^3$ yields
\[
 \mathcal G_k(b)=G_0+bG_1+R_b,\qquad
 \norm{R_b}\le4k^5b^2.
 \tag{20}
\]
The bound is uniform over all bath-prefix vectors, with explicit
dependence on $k$ and no discarded infinite tail.

The critical product density has \emph{exactly} zero bulk drift for
every $b$. Its one-site depth law is the lazy symmetric birth/death law
with inward and outward probabilities $(1-b)/2$ and a finite root
correction. An initial depth at least $k$ cannot encounter that
correction before the last of $k$ calls. Thus
\[
 \Tr_A[(\sigma\otimes I)\mathcal G_k(b)]=0.
 \tag{21}
\]
We use this exact cancellation, rather than bound the remainder against
the full density, which would cost too much.

\subsection{Positive state and exact negative operator drift}

Write $\ellog=\ln2$, $v=v_k$, and let $H=H_{\zeta_*}$ be the
minimum-norm observable. Define
\[
 X=-\frac{\ellog}{2}\sigma H,\quad
 m=\frac{3\ellog v}{16},\quad
 Y=\frac{4m}{k}\sigma(Q-k/2),\quad
 \rho=\sigma+bX+b^2Y.
 \tag{22}
\]
These are finite input matrices, not bath preparations. All commute
with $\sigma$ and $Q$, and $X,Y$ commute with one another.
$\Tr X=\Tr Y=\Tr QX=0$ and $\Tr QY=m$, using
$\operatorname{Var}_\sigma Q=k/4$. Hence $\Tr\rho=1$.
Set $Z=\sigma^{-1}(\rho-\sigma)$. For $b\le1$,
\[
 \norm Z\le\frac{\ellog}{2}bk^2+
                   \frac{3\ellog}{8}b^2k^2\le\ellog bk^2.
 \tag{23}
\]
Impose the single useful uniform window
\[
 k\ge4,\qquad b k^{17/4}\le\delta_0=2^{-12}.
 \tag{24}
\]
It implies $\norm Z<1/2$, so $\rho$ is a strictly positive actual
charge-preserving input density.

By (15), its leading bulk drift is
\[
 b^2[T_k(X)+2mI]\le-\frac{\ellog v b^2}{8}I.
 \tag{25}
\]
The exact drift remainder, using (20)--(21), is
\[
 b^3T_k(Y)+\Tr_A[((bX+b^2Y)\otimes I)R_b].
\]
Weighted Cauchy--Schwarz and the binomial charge variance give
\[
 \norm X_1\le\frac{\ellog\sqrt v}{2},\qquad
 \norm Y_1\le\frac{3\ellog v}{8\sqrt k}.
 \tag{26}
\]
Dividing the norm of the complete remainder by $\ellog v b^2/8$
and using $\norm{G_1}\le k^2$ yields
\[
 3bk^{3/2}+\frac{16bk^5}{\sqrt v}+12b^2k^{9/2}
 \le(3+16\sqrt{32}+12)\delta_0<\frac12.
 \tag{27}
\]
Here $v\ge k^{3/2}/32$ and $bk^{1/4}\le1$. Therefore the exact
physical bulk drift, not just its first favorable derivative, satisfies
\[
 P_{\ge k}D_s(\rho)P_{\ge k}\le-\kappa P_{\ge k},
 \qquad \kappa=\frac{\ellog v b^2}{16}>0.
 \tag{28}
\]
Also $\kappa\le k$ under (24). No root term is asserted negative;
the inherited coding theorem retains the finite boundary remainder.

\subsection{Uniform entropy excess}

The exact relative-entropy identity is
\[
 S(\rho)-\frac{3k}{2}=b^2m-D_2(\rho\Vert\sigma).
 \tag{29}
\]
By weighted charge orthogonality,
\[
 \Tr(\sigma Z^2)=\frac{\ellog^2v b^2}{4}
                    +\frac{9\ellog^2v^2b^4}{64k}.
 \tag{30}
\]
For $|z|\le1/2$ the remainder of
$(1+z)\ln(1+z)-z-z^2/2$ has magnitude at most $2|z|^3/3$.
Joint spectral calculus applies since $[Z,\sigma]=0$. Consequently
the correction to $D_2$ beyond $\ellog v b^2/8$, divided by the
intended entropy excess $\ellog v b^2/16$, is at most
\[
 \frac98 b^2v/k+\norm Z\left[\frac83+\frac32b^2v/k\right]
 <\frac12
 \tag{31}
\]
under (24), using $v\le k^2$ and (23). Combining (29)--(31) proves
\[
 S(\rho)-\frac{3k}{2}
 \ge\frac{\ellog v b^2}{32}
 \ge\frac{\ellog}{1024}b^2k^{3/2}.
 \tag{32}
\]
The same actual density has both (28) and (32).

\subsection{The fixed-before-horizon choice and its power}

For (1) and $\beta\le2^{-11}$,
\[

\subsection{Closest primary results and what is additional here}

Theorem 2 of Giovannetti--Burgarth--Mancini \cite{GBM}, with their
Definition 1, supplies established exact quantum coding when the dilation
environment dimension grows subexponentially. Our inherited compression
argument uses that finite-error-set mechanism. The complete bath here is
infinite; a global small environment is not assumed. The additional
model-specific work is to select a high-entropy input space whose actual
overflow is controlled, jointly over coherent initializers, and then pay
normalization and polar errors. Cycle6 supplies the guarded-path lower,
its complete operator dual, and a uniform nonlinear realization beyond
the quartic fixed-block window. It does not claim to invent the
finite-error-set coding principle.

Theorem 13 of Kretschmann--Werner \cite{KW} assumes finite-dimensional
forgetful memory. Selected-input inward drift here neither proves those
hypotheses nor creates independent block channels. Their coding theorem
therefore is not used as a substitute for the complete same-bath bridge.

Cubitt et al.\ \cite{Cubitt} show that arbitrarily many uses can be
needed to detect quantum capacity in a family of memoryless channels.
That theorem prevents any broad priority claim that long temporal codes
can matter. It does not give our coefficient growth or small-angle law
for this fixed infinite-memory device. The comparison is structural,
not an identification of models or error guarantees. This is a focused
primary-source comparison, not an exhaustive historical-novelty result.

\subsection{Failures, review and cost scope}

Uniform prefix averaging alone is insufficient: the exact $k=4$ uniform
prefix value is $561/512$, whereas the closest-point optimum is $17/16$,
with a full Laplacian certificate in the supplied rational replay.
An average does not supply the required worst-prefix sign. The new proof
uses a minimizer over all prefix densities and an input-only contribution
that survives every density.

Positive final middle displacement alone is also insufficient for the
path identity. Paths may consume original bath colors. The exact token
replay records omitted-guard and omitted-meander witnesses. The two-wire
transposition (18), not a net-height approximation, is the load-bearing
operation. This omission can destroy bath independence even though a
finite entropy or prefix-variance calculation remains favorable.

Small-block Laplacian patterns suggested a quadratic susceptibility law;
that stronger all-length premise is unproved. The early conditional
$\beta^{7/2}$ note is retained as a labeled development route. We stopped
that numerical escalation after a separate all-length $k^{3/2}$ proof
became available, which is already enough for a genuine improved power.
No reflecting cutoff or simulated stationary bath was used.

The principal investigator constructed and integrated the uniform
nonlinear transfer. Distinct contributors supplied the full-operator
duality and endpoint norms, the guarded-word mechanism, and a focused
independent challenge of the new load-bearing chain. Their actual notes,
code and review decisions are included. Internal agreement is not external
certification. Exact bounded replays are diagnostic verification, not the
proof of an all-length claim and not a measure of discovery cost.

Observed script runtimes and peak process memory are in their JSON
receipts. Total inference, discovery and review costs are unknown. There
is no complexity bound for constructing the minimizing input matrix,
spectral-type code or recovery. In the existence proof, $\rank\rho=3^k$
and the overflow constants can be enormous. No hardware performance or
practical finite-horizon benefit is asserted.
