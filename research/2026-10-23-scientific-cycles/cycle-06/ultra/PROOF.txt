0. CONTRACT AND RECEIVING QUESTION

We use the received R1 model: finite external ports, normal complete memory on
C_A tensor C_B, fixed normal finite-round finite-outcome LOCC device, faithful
product external input. All retained flags, catalysts and shared registers count.
Every branch is averaged. T(rho,sigma)=||rho-sigma||_1/2. The complete one-step
memory return delta is distinct from GLOBAL factory initialization error epsilon.
The first ideal output is exactly Phi_D. We do not replace that task by an
approximate Bell output, recurrence, or a conditioned successful branch.

For r tasks, each with N emitted D-pairs, the received Cycle5 theorem says

 EF(sigma)>=Tr(O sigma)-r C_D-sum_a Q_a(sigma),               (0.1)
 Q_a=S(B_-a|Y_a),   O=m sum_(a,i) Phi_(a,1..i),   m=ln D.

Alice's POVM Y_a may act JOINTLY on all complementary tasks. A single global
Alice/Bob orientation is fixed; a global swap is allowed. Define
 Qbar_*=(1/r)sum_a inf_(POVM Y_a) Q_a.
It is not generally a cheap observable or an independently priced resource.
Known KW/HJW identifies it with a complementary E_F convex roof. The conjectured
receiving shortcut was that actual exact-output/complete-return structure forces
this optimized penalty bounded independently of r and the return horizon.

We refute that shortcut INSIDE an explicit finite local-unitary device, at its
active horizon, and prove its exact ordinary supply frontier. Large Q alone is
therefore not evidence for the necessary correlations of a resource saving.
We also construct a finite controller-assisted repair with its additional access
and reset costs, and state what output-only normalizers cannot accomplish.

1. COMPLETE COUNTED QUEUE AND FIXED UNITARY DEVICE

Fix integers D>=2,L>=1. Write Phi=|Phi_D><Phi_D| and
 nu=(I_D/D)_A tensor(I_D/D)_B.
Each local memory has one L-level current clock F and L D-level registers.
Put w_k=Phi^tensor k tensor nu^tensor(L-k), 0<=k<=L, and

 tau=(1/L)sum_(k=1)^L |kk><kk|_F tensor w_k.                (1.1)

There are no other retained records. This is a finite normal state; local memory
dimension is L D^L. A factory can sample K, prepare matching clocks and K Bell
pairs plus product nu junk. Duplicate factory work registers are genuinely
discarded or reversibly cleared using the retained clock BEFORE reuse. No
immutable extra copy of the original inventory is silently omitted.

Each call supplies the explicit independent faithful product input eta=nu.
Each party applies the SAME fixed local register permutation
 (input,M1,...,ML) -> (output=M1, memory=M2,...,ML,input),
and cyclic clock permutation f(k)=k-1 for k>=2, f(1)=L.
These are local unitaries. The device has one outcome, zero communication and
no reset/discard/environment during reuse. Its action is defined on ALL inputs.
External free local mixedness is specified; it supplies no entanglement.

The first output is exactly Phi and factors by purity. In the new memory, every
clock block j<L equals its old block w_j, while block L changes from w_L to w_0.
Consequently

 tau_1-tau=(1/L)|LL><LL| tensor(w_0-w_L),
 delta_L=T(tau_1,tau)=(1-D^(-2L))/L.                        (1.2)

The trace norm follows from the commuting pure state Phi_(D^L) and maximally
mixed I/D^(2L). This is return of the COMPLETE memory, including both clocks.
If a single original-K flag is left immutable INSTEAD of the cyclic clock, its
complete return error is 1-D^-2. If an extra immutable original-K record is
retained ALONGSIDE our cyclic clock, their old and returned flag supports are
disjoint for L>1 and the complete error is 1 (for L=1 it is 1-D^-2). None of
these different retained-memory models can use (1.2).

For any public t>=0 the exact joint output-memory state is

 Omega_t=(1/L)sum_k [Phi^tensor min(k,t) tensor nu^tensor(t-k)_+]_out
  tensor |f_t(k) f_t(k)><f_t(k) f_t(k)|_F
  tensor [Phi^tensor(k-t)_+ tensor nu^tensor(L-(k-t)_+)]_mem, (1.3)

where f_t(k)=1+((k-t-1) mod L). Formula (1.3) follows by tracking the actual
wire permutation, not by multiplying output marginals. At t>=L memory PAIR
CONTENT is nu^L, but the counted clock remains correlated with early outputs.
The entire memory must NOT be said to factor after stock exhaustion.

Its external output marginal is
 omega_(L,N)=(1/L)sum_k Phi^tensor min(k,N) tensor nu^tensor(N-k)_+.
The actual complete-prefix Bell probability is

 p_i=(1/L)[(L-i+1)_+ + sum_(k=1)^min(L,i-1) D^(-2(i-k))].  (1.4)

In particular tails have nonzero Bell overlap; they are not zero-score outputs.
ceil(1/delta_L)=L+1: (L+1)D^-2L<1. At N=L and L+1 both the actual score and
remaining branches are kept. Small fixed delta is approximate reuse, not normal
recurrence. This finite consumable queue eventually emits only product input.

2. RAW OPTIMIZED UNCERTAINTY IS EXTENSIVE, EVEN WITH EVERY INVENTORY GIVEN

Let sigma=omega_(L,N)^tensor r and
 b_(N,L)=(1/L)sum_(k=1)^L(N-k)_+.
For EVERY collective complementary Alice POVM and either global orientation,

 Q_a(sigma)>=(r-1)m b_(N,L),
 (r-1)m b_(N,L)<=Qbar_*<=(r-1)[m b_(N,L)+ln L].           (2.1)

PROOF OF LOWER. Extend sigma by its actual entire classical inventory vector
Kvec. This is a mathematical cq extension, not an extra uncounted device flag.
Conditioned on Kvec and any outcome of ANY joint Alice readout, Bob's suffixes
in complementary copies remain independent I/D factors. There are exactly
sum_(b!=a)(N-K_b)_+ such factors; their entropy is that count times m. Remaining
conditional Bob entropy is nonnegative. Strong subadditivity gives
 S(B_-a|Y_a)>=S(B_-a|Y_a,Kvec).
Average over the actual joint law of Kvec,Y; its K marginals stay uniform.
This proves the lower without E_F additivity, product-readout optimality, or
hidden inventory uncertainty. Giving all inventories to Alice cannot remove it.

PROOF OF UPPER. Measure every complementary Alice output in the computational
basis. Her outcome is uniform independently of K. Given K and her string,
Bob's active partners are known pure basis states, with private suffix entropy
m b. Entropy of mixing gives at most m b+ln L per copy. For this chosen product
readout the complementary copies stay conditionally product, so their entropies
add. Optimizing all readouts can only improve this upper. QED.

Elementary summation gives
 b=N(N-1)/(2L) for N<=L;  b=N-(L+1)/2 for N>=L.
Thus at ACTIVE horizon N=L, Qbar_*>=(r-1)(L-1)m/2. At canonical N=L+1,
 Qbar_*>=(r-1)(L+1)m/2. For fixed L>=2 it diverges with iid multiplicity r;
for r=2 it grows linearly in 1/delta. No irrelevant terminal-noise extension is
needed. This refutes the proposed uniform implication, not the valid (0.1).

For completeness, KW/HJW gives the exact general iid identity
 Qbar_*(omega^r)=EF((Tr_A |psi_ABE><psi|)^tensor(r-1)),
with cut B^(r-1):E^(r-1), for any purification psi of omega. The own-task pure
factor is local on the right and can be added/traced for free. All ensemble
refinements come from Alice POVMs; entropy concavity permits rank-one refinement.
Regularization is generally required. This is KNOWN machinery, not an algorithm
or new identity. The direct proof (2.1) avoids solving that convex roof entirely.

3. EXACT MATCHED ORDINARY SUPPLY: NO SAVING HIDES IN THE COUNTEREXAMPLE

Supply consists of Phi_M, or a rank M chosen genuinely BEFORE receipt with
charge E ln M, followed by normal LOCC. Initial supply includes every shared
catalyst and retained ancilla; no free entangled catalyst is admitted. Error is
GLOBAL half-trace distance to tau^r on every counted memory register.

Set B=(L+1)m/2. Exactly
 EF(tau^r)=rB.                                               (3.1)
Upper: sample inventories and use Bell pairs, decomposing nu into pure product
states. Lower: local clock readout yields flags Kvec, whose active Bell state
has entanglement S m, S=sum K. EF is monotone on average under this local readout;
separable junk contributes zero. Both directions retain all branches.

Hence exact preselected EXPECTED supply costs rB optimally. Exact deterministic
supply costs rLm: branch all K=L has positive target probability and Schmidt
number D^(rL), which LOCC from Phi_M cannot exceed; supplying that rank suffices.
With vanishing positive global error, deterministic iid rate is B. More strongly,
every fixed epsilon<1 has deterministic asymptotic rate B, while expected
preselected fixed-error rate is (1-epsilon)B. These are distinct error contracts.

Here is a complete finite comparator, stronger than simply using mean inventory.
Let p_s=Pr[S=s], d_s=D^s, s=r,...,rL. The exact deterministic-rank error is

 e_r(M)=1-max_(q_s>=0,sum q=1)sum_s min(p_s,q_s f_s),
 f_s=min(1,M/d_s).                                         (3.2)

To compute it, process s increasing (f decreasing), fill pass credit y_s up to
p_s using mass y_s/f_s, until mass one is spent. This fractional-knapsack rule
is exact, including possible bias in approximate clock probabilities.

PROOF. Locally dephase flags; target is invariant. In each matched flag sector,
trace local junk. Any state of Schmidt number <=M overlaps the active Bell
projector by at most min(1,M/d_s), by Schmidt-vector Cauchy--Schwarz. Measuring
flag plus Bell success as a mathematical trace-distance converse effect yields
T>=1-sum min(p_s,q_s f_s). It need not be an experimentally local test. Mismatched
flags can only waste mass. For individual inventory vectors with the same s,
sum minima<=minimum of sums; equality is attained by choosing target-relative
probabilities p_K/p_s inside that inventory, so grouping loses nothing.

For attainment sample those counted flags. If M>=d_s convert Phi_M to Phi_d_s;
explicitly Alice measures all d_s-subsets of M basis positions with normalized
diagonal subset projectors, sends the subset, and both relabel. Completeness
follows because each position occurs binom(M-1,d_s-1) times. If M<d_s embed
Phi_M and perform the bilateral U tensor conjugate(U) isotropic twirl, obtaining
 rho_f=f Phi_d+(1-f)(I-Phi_d)/(d^2-1), f=M/d.
An exact finite twirl exists: its finite-dimensional superoperator Haar average
is in the compact convex hull of conjugations, hence has a finite Caratheodory
decomposition. Its efficient synthesis is NOT claimed. Append the target nu
junk and genuinely erase twirl/work records. Every branch is averaged.
Each block's trace norm is |p-qf|+q(1-f); summing gives (3.2) exactly.
Greedy optimality follows by exchanging mass from a smaller f to a larger f.

For arbitrary expected preselected supply the exact finite optimum is

 C_av(epsilon)/m = min sum_s s v_s,
 0<=v_s<=y_s<=p_s,  sum_s y_s>=1-epsilon,
 sum_s[d_s y_s-(d_s-1)v_s]<=1.                              (3.3)

PROOF. Initially allow joint mass x_(M,s) of resource choice M and target
inventory s, sampled before supply. The previous overlap proof gives pass mass
c_s=sum_M min(1,M/d_s)x_(M,s). Minimize sum x ln M subject to credited overlap
y_s<=min(p_s,c_s), sum y>=1-epsilon, total mass one. Attainment uses the same
isotropic construction. Resource choices can correlate with s; this is allowed
by the preselected contract, not postselection after resource receipt.
For 1<=M<=d replace a resource by free rank1 and full rank d with full proportion
theta=(M-1)/(d-1). It preserves mean fidelity M/d and lowers cost, since
theta ln d<=ln M by concavity. M>d is dominated by d. Thus use free mass u_s
and full mass t_s with total sum(u+t)=1 and y_s<=u_s/d_s+t_s.
Reduce t_s to v_s=min(t_s,y_s) and u_s to d_s(y_s-v_s), deleting only unneeded
mass in this intermediate optimization. This yields (3.3) and no greater cost.
Conversely any (3.3) gives full mass v_s, free mass d_s(y_s-v_s), total <=1.
Give leftover probability to any free matched-flag branch. It can only improve
overlap. This constructs a genuine complete averaged factory of the stated
cost and error, proving equality. At epsilon=0, y=p and the mass constraint
forces v=y, giving rB. No E_F oracle occurs in (3.2) or (3.3).

The coefficient p_s is [z^s](z+...+z^L)^r/L^r. Exact dynamic programming has
at most r(L-1)+1 inventory values, O(r^2 L^2) straightforward integer additions,
and numerator bit lengths O(r ln L). The LP has two variables per inventory,
O(rL) inequalities, and integer d_s of O(rL ln D) bits. Rational LP gives a
finite classical optimum/certificate; its bit costs and preparing flags/twirls
are paid. This says nothing about efficient physical twirl implementation.

Example: D=2,L=2,r=1 has C_av/m=max(0,3/2-3epsilon); deterministic ranks
M=1,2,3,4 have errors 1/2,1/4,1/8,0. An independent rational dual and primal
are in audits/resource/FINAL_FRONTIER_CHALLENGE.txt. Our executable uses SciPy
only to propose rational witnesses, then checks exact primal/dual feasibility
and objective equality with Fraction arithmetic. Solver success alone is not
accepted as proof. These bounded checks validate the interface, not the theorem.

To verify the asserted iid rates without invoking an unpriced formation theorem,
for an integer threshold t measure flags, extract t active Bell pairs when S>=t,
and use the Bell success projector. Any supplied M has acceptance <=min(1,M/D^t).
Thus epsilon>=Pr[S>=t]-M D^-t. S/r concentrates around (L+1)/2; a deterministic
rate below B forces epsilon->1. Supplying D^t for t/r above that mean and using
free fallback on S>t achieves error <=Pr[S>t]->0, proving matching upper rate.
For random X=ln M, the same test implies for every u<t m
 Pr[X>u]>=Pr[S>=t]-epsilon-exp(u-t m).
Then EX>=u Pr[X>u]. Choose t m just below rB and u slightly lower; this gives
liminf EX/r>=(1-epsilon)B. The converse is attained by a preselected outer gamble
between the exact expected factory and a free factory, with probabilities
1-epsilon and epsilon. All gambling branches are included. No success branch
is conditioned away. Exact epsilon=0 expected cost is already (3.1).

4. WHAT FREE OUTPUT-ONLY REPAIRS FAIL, AND WHY

Truncating to n<=L slots preserves every retained prefix score, but (2.1) leaves
q>=m n(n-1)/(2L). Bounded penalty at fixed r>=2 requires n=O(sqrt L), whereas
full score is at least (L+1)m/2 and retained score at most nm. This loses Theta(L)
actual score. Dropping o(L) slots, including logarithmically many, leaves the
linear uncertainty. The exact score loss is computed in audits/repair/PROOF.txt.

Known full-Bell vertex rigidity: a same-dimension separable CPTP map fixing
Phi_d is a random matched bilateral-unitary channel, hence fixes I/d^2.
Complete elementary proof: in product Kraus form A_j B_j^T=c_j I and
sum|c_j|^2=1. Completeness has trace sum||A_j||_HS^2||B_j||_HS^2=d^2.
Cauchy--Schwarz on singular values gives each nonzero-c term >=d^2|c_j|^2,
with equality only for scaled unitaries. Every nonzero zero-c Kraus would add
strictly positive trace, so none exists. All surviving pairs are matched unitaries.
This is a special case of Gheorghiu--Griffiths (2007), not a new generic lemma.

There is also a scoped ACTUAL averaged-queue rigidity result. For any single-pair
SEP CPTP map put a=F_Phi(Lambda Phi), b=F_Phi(Lambda nu). The known separable
isotropic state (Phi+D nu)/(D+1) and separable Bell overlap<=1/D imply
 b-D^-2<=(1-a)/D. After earlier pairs are fixed, actual prefix j has weights
 alpha=(L-j+1)/L, beta=(1/L)sum_(h=0)^(j-2)D^-2h.
Its score change is at most -(alpha-beta/D)(1-a), with alpha-beta/D>0.
Nondecrease therefore forces a=1 and the rigidity lemma fixes both Phi and nu.
Inductively ANY product of individually CPTP pair maps, or a shared-random
mixture of such products, nondecreasing EVERY actual prefix leaves omega_L
unchanged. Every positive-weight random component is forced to equality.
Full proof/independent challenge is in audits/repair/sublemma/.

This does not cover adaptive joint cross-pair LOCC, approximate score budgets,
entangled supplied ancillas, changed port dimensions, or a controller-assisted
operation on the joint memory-output state. Those are genuinely different maps.
For a specified output-only LOCC Gamma the valid old witness charges BOTH actual
score loss Delta=Tr(O sigma)-Tr(O Gamma sigma) and Qbar(Gamma sigma).
Optimizing arbitrary Gamma without a construction could hide the original hard
problem. None of these scoped failures proves all repair operations impossible.

5. A CONCRETE CONTROLLER-ASSISTED REPAIR, WITH AN EXPLICIT ACCESS BOUNDARY

For THIS constructed device, expose the known current clock register to local
control after a specified public horizon N. This is additional architectural
access; the original external D-pair ports alone do not supply it. Its existence
is proved here by specifying the register and gate, not inferred from outputs.
No claim is made that an unknown hidden memory can be accessed or certified.

For a clock value j, locally compute k=f_N^-1(j). On each output position i>k,
replace the local D-register by |0><0|; on i<=k act as identity. This is a finite
local CPTP channel controlled by the classical counted clock. Both parties do it
without communication or entangled ancillas. Because the actual clock is diagonal,
the operation preserves the COMPLETE retained memory marginal exactly; it retains
all correlations indexed by K. Its output is

 omega'=(1/L)sum_k Phi^tensor min(k,N) tensor |00><00|^tensor(N-k)_+.

Every vector in its support is maximally correlated in the common product basis.
Computational readout of every complementary Alice register makes Bob pure,
so Qbar_*(omega'^r)=0 exactly. Prefix probability becomes
 p'_i=(1/L)[(L-i+1)_+ +sum_(k=1)^min(L,i-1)D^(-(i-k))]>=p_i.
This removes the private-noise penalty with zero score loss on the ideal queue.

An explicit circuit uses at most N fresh pure D-ancillas per party, conditional
swaps on i>k, and local discard of the old registers now in those ancillas.
Clock copies/workspace can be reversibly cleared using the still-retained clock;
no immutable extra historical copy remains. A naive table implementation uses
O(NL) controlled tests, or classical modular arithmetic/comparisons with explicit
O(log L+log N) working bits and their circuit cost. At most 2N local D-level reset
operations and ancillas are used. Conditional on K, exported junk entropy is
2m(N-K)_+; its unconditional disposal/control/thermodynamic cost is not zero
and no physical energy estimate is asserted. If this garbage is retained for
future use, it joins COMPLETE memory and the previous return proof no longer
automatically applies. This repair is CPTP, not the all-unitary original reuse.

Crucially it acts on Omega_N, not generally on omega_(L,N) alone. We do NOT assert
EF(original output)>=EF(repaired output) by an unavailable output-only channel.
Instead the COMPLETE supplied memory undergoes rN reuse steps and this explicitly
local joint-output-memory repair, then memory is locally traced. Therefore its
initial supplied entanglement still lowerbounds EF of the repaired output.
This makes the receiving resource argument valid without a hidden access claim.

For approximate initialization one must evaluate Q on the ACTUAL repaired state.
For any initializer with global error epsilon, the same specified composite
channel contracts trace distance, so its Bell score differs from the ideal
repaired score by at most rNm epsilon. The general implication is only
 C_sup/r>=m A_N(delta)-C_D-Qbar(actual repaired output)-mN epsilon.
Zero Q does not follow from proximity alone. It does hold for an explicitly
matched typical factory: sample Kvec; if S<=t prepare its exact active Bells;
otherwise use |00> on ALL nominal active pairs, with the same flags and nu junk.
Its global error is <=Pr[S>t], supplied deterministic rank D^t, and after the
clock-assisted suffix reset every branch has maximally correlated output support.
This remains true despite correlations across all r tasks and all fallback
branches. Thus actual Q=0, epsilon_r->0 at fixed L, and the inherited witness
again gives the ordinary leading 1/(2delta) law. It is not a saving or a new
additive constant. The device's ideal initial state/first output remain (1.1).

6. COMPARATOR, KNOWN BOUNDARY AND THE ONE CONTINUATION DECISION

The counterexample is ordinary flagged consumable stock. Its complete-target
optimal costs (3.1)-(3.3) exclude a concealed saving under every specified supply
convention. Unflagged erased-stock constructions from unchanged predecessors
remain a strongest admissible ordinary comparator with leading m/(2delta).
We do not identify the optimal unflagged noisy mixture's E_C with its mean
inventory cost: the corresponding formation uncertainty was a superseded attempt.

Exact catalysts (Jonathan--Plenio) enable conversions of already entangled input
states; exact extra Bell extraction from a product input with a finite catalyst
returned exactly is ruled out here by additive entanglement monotonicity.
Van Dam--Hayden's universal family has Schmidt weights 1/(j H_n). Its local
rearrangement approximates the JOINT catalyst-plus-target with amplitude overlap
>=1-ln D/ln n, hence joint half-trace error <=sqrt(2 ln D/ln n). That particular
construction allows Bell-output error and does not automatically meet exact
first-output R1. Its entire initial family is charged (rank n for exact one-shot
preparation; Schmidt entropy for iid pure-state formation). These known facts
do not rule out tailored exact-output reservoirs; any admitted one must satisfy
the inherited resource converse with all its resources counted. No claim of
optimality over every embezzling family or of a new noisy-output law is made.

Primary boundaries used: KW/HJW convex-roof identity; Schmidt-number Bell overlap
(Terhal--Horodecki); isotropic twirling/separable boundary (Horodecki et al.);
Gheorghiu--Griffiths exact SEP rigidity; Jonathan--Plenio exact catalysis and
van Dam--Hayden universal embezzlement. See COMPARATOR.txt for URLs and scope.
Classical knapsack and rational LP are known optimization machinery. Historical
novelty of the queue receiving obstruction/repair is UNKNOWN; no known lemma is
represented as a new general theorem merely because we supplied its proof.

DECISION: retire the raw-Q-uniformity conjecture and the settled exact/pairwise
or truncation shortcuts. Continue the unrestricted resource question using a
joint, state-dependent, score-controlled normalization or an entropy-noise-
insensitive defect. The accessible-clock construction is a concrete admissible
positive repair on a specified class; extending it to arbitrary causal memory
without assuming access, solving an E_F oracle, or paying away a potential saving
is the precise gap. The generic logarithmic supply interval remains OPEN.
