WAVE QUENCH ADMISSION: MECHANISM IDENTIFIED; COMPLETE NITROGEN OPERATION NOT ADMITTED
2026-10-22 UTC. Bounded free-primary-evidence review. No new engine programme or trajectory.

Decision
The recovered evidence does NOT establish a pressure-wave nitrogen-quench apparatus with measured retained NO, complete compression/expansion reset, driver input, and material survival together. Stop this branch at the finite source limit. This is an evidence decision, not a thermodynamic impossibility claim and not a claim that no historical experiment occurred.

The important distinction
An unsteady gasdynamic pressure wave can transfer energy to another gas and change a material parcel's stagnation enthalpy. It is not equivalent to a passive steady adiabatic nozzle. The physical heat/work path is therefore identifiable in principle. But identifying that path is not showing a nitrogen operation that resets every inventory and pays all its inputs. Nothing recovered permits replacing the Cycle5 complete-reset obligation by static-temperature cooling alone.

Balance test against Cycle5 (our reasoning, not a source claim)
For a reacting material parcel with no diffusive/species energy flux, the inviscid adiabatic relation is D(h+|u|^2/2)/Dt = (1/rho)*partial(p)/partial(t). Thus h0 conservation along a streamline is a steady-flow result; a pressure wave can change h0. Equivalently, a moving gas interface does pressure work on the driver. This is a genuine possible export route, not mere conversion of h into the same stream's velocity.

For a periodic apparatus with all stores returned, however, the cycle-integrated energy balance still reads:
  sum(m_in*h0_in) + Q_in - W_out = sum(m_out*h0_out).
Every driver, coolant, purge, fuel/oxidant and exhaust stream belongs in this sum, and creation/restoration of pressure inventories must be included. Internal wave transfer cancels when both donor and receiver are inside the boundary. A recirculating driver is not an indefinitely accumulating energy sink. Recovery into the next feed can be useful but must be closed along with chemical product energy and losses.

The Cycle5 1.407672706 MJ/kg-fresh hot-to-benign-reset export obligation applies to its saved hot composition and stated six-species, retained-NOx, relaxed-at-rest contract. A wave receiver would provide an additional energy pathway, not invalidate conservation. The figure cannot automatically be assigned as heat or shaft work for a differently defined multistream wave apparatus. The 3300 K/200 atm patent design also does not share the Cycle5 2700 K/1 atm hot state, so no numerical comparison or performance ratio is authorized from these sources alone.

What is missing at the admission boundary
- Original measured downstream NO inventory and retention, separately from calculated equilibrium or proposed yield.
- Complete pressure/temperature/time and rest/reset states, including post-quench deceleration and recompression.
- Driver/fuel/electrical/compressor inputs, coolant and exhaust outputs, and any feed-regeneration credit on one common boundary.
- Nitrogen-specific tube/endwall/port/seal material and measured thermal/cyclic survival at the relevant conditions.

Finite stopping condition reached
Exact-title and conference-title searches, accessible 2022 citation chain, a free inventor patent, and a NASA index lead did not recover the 1975 chapter or a complete measured nitrogen-operation package. Source retrieval failures were technical availability failures, not a permission denial. No outreach, paid access or spending was attempted. Do not expand this into unrelated energy-exchanger, flywheel-engine, hydrogen-pyrolysis or general power-cycle optimization. A future reopening would require the missing primary experimental package, not another cycle schematic.
