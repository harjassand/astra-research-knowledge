1. EXACT IDENTIFICATION AND SELF-CONTAINED ARGUMENT

For a finite output state sigma_(A_all B_all), fix any purification
 |Omega>_(A_all B_all E).
For a task a, relabel the three systems
 C=A_(T_a^c), B=B_(T_a^c), R=A_(T_a) B_(T_a) E.
Thus |Omega> is pure on CBR. Its marginal rho_BR=Tr_C Omega is fixed.
For an arbitrary finite POVM M on C, let Y be its outcome and set
 Q_a(M)=sum_y p_y S(rho_(B|y))=S(B|Y).
Then the EXACT identity is

 inf_(finite POVM M on C) Q_a(M) = E_F(B:R)_(rho_BR).     (KW)

This is known Koashi--Winter/Hughston--Jozsa--Wootters machinery, not a
new interpretation of entropy or a new efficient formula for E_F.

First refine every non-rank-one POVM effect into rank-one effects, retaining
the finer outcome. Each original conditional B state is the mixture of its
refined states, so entropy concavity shows that the average entropy cannot
increase. A rank-one effect on C leaves BR conditionally pure. Thus every
refined measurement realizes a pure ensemble of rho_BR, with average
entanglement exactly Q_a. This proves inf Q_a>=E_F(rho_BR).

Conversely take any finite pure ensemble rho_BR=sum_y |w_y><w_y|. In a
Schmidt decomposition |Omega>=sum_j sqrt(lambda_j)|j>_C|e_j>_BR, the
ensemble coefficients satisfy
 |w_y>=sum_j u_(y,j) sqrt(lambda_j)|e_j>,
 sum_y conjugate(u_(y,j)) u_(y,k)=delta_(j,k)
on the positive Schmidt support. The row vectors u define rank-one POVM
effects on that support, with the complex conjugations chosen so applying
the corresponding measurement bra to |Omega> gives |w_y>. Complete the
POVM arbitrarily on the zero-weight orthogonal complement. This realizes
the ensemble by measuring C. Finite-dimensional convex-roof minima are
attained by finite ensembles, or one may simply approach the infimum.
It follows that inf Q_a<=E_F(rho_BR). QED.

The identification is invariant under the choice of purification, since
changing a purification acts by an isometry on E, hence locally on R.
The optimizing measurement can require many outcomes, dense effects,
and state knowledge. Existence does not make its circuit, classical
optimization or calibration free.

2. CONSEQUENCE FOR THE NEW MULTISCALE WITNESS

The principal bound, independently checked in KNOWN_BOUNDARY.txt, is
 E_F(sigma)>=Tr(O sigma)-r C_D-sum_a Q_a(M_a)
for EVERY choice of complementary Alice POVMs M_a, with no commutation
or support premise. The choices for the separate tasks can be optimized
independently. Therefore it also has the exact formal version

 E_F(A_all:B_all)_sigma
  +sum_a E_F(B_(T_a^c): A_(T_a)B_(T_a)E)_(Tr A_(T_a^c) Omega)
 >= Tr(O sigma)-r C_D.                                (P)

This is the explicit hierarchy receiving inequality with a known optimized
penalty. Neither (KW) by itself nor the existence of quantitative-witness
duality supplies the coefficient and N-independent constant on the right.
No claim of historical priority for (P) follows from this bounded check.

Let the principal finite preparation law be
 C_sup/r >= m A_N-C_D-(1/r)sum_a Q_a(M_a)-m N epsilon,
 A_N=sum_(i=1)^N [1-(i-1)delta]_+.
It follows for EVERY family M_a that
 (1/r)sum_a Q_a(M_a)
 >= m A_N-C_D-m N epsilon-C_sup/r.
Taking the independent infima and using (KW) gives

 (1/r)sum_a E_F(B_comp_a:R_a)
 >= m A_N-C_D-m N epsilon-C_sup/r.                    (R)

This is a necessary output/purification structure for ANY proposed supply
saving, including arbitrary non-block coefficient operators. A saving of
c ln N relative to m A_N, with constant losses controlled and N epsilon
negligible on that scale, forces the left side to be at least
c ln N-O(1). It is not enough that one selected output test has large
entropy: the irreducible quantity remains large after EVERY complementary
Alice POVM, equivalently it is the average of the indicated convex roofs.

The term on the left is NOT an additional ancillary resource that should
be charged independently on top of C_sup. R_a includes parts of the same
external output and a mathematical purifying reference, and the R_a vary
with a. It is a structural obstruction. Estimating it by output tomography
or synthesizing an optimizing POVM can be prohibitively expensive. No
observable certificate for complete hidden-memory return is implied.

3. RELATION TO STRONG SUPERADDITIVITY: ONE SUFFICIENT ROUTE, NO EQUIVALENCE

For the reduced single-task state sigma_(A_a B_a), the received universal
one-chain lemma and convex-roof averaging alone imply
 score_a<=E_F(A_a:B_a)+C_D.
Hence the generic strong-superadditivity statement
 E_F(A_all:B_all)>=sum_a E_F(A_a:B_a)
would IMPLY the unrestricted critical hierarchy
 E_F(A_all:B_all)>=Tr(O sigma)-r C_D.
That implication is elementary, but the premise is false in full generality.
It may not be used as an unproved additivity hypothesis in this programme.

Shor's equivalence theorem relates the fully generic strong-superadditivity
and additivity conjectures to minimum-output-entropy and Holevo additivity.
Hastings' counterexamples disprove those universal conjectures. This does
NOT provide a counterexample to the special Bell-prefix hierarchy, because
the hierarchy retains only particular overlapping Bell-prefix expectations;
the single-task inequalities need not be tight on an additivity counterexample.
Conversely, the special hierarchy has not been shown to imply full generic
strong superadditivity. The present work does NOT establish equivalence.

Optimizing the penalty in (KW) is a known E_F problem, and regularizing it
can involve the usual entanglement-cost/additivity difficulties. That is
a tractability/remaining-boundary warning, not a proof that the entire
resource question has collapsed to a previously settled problem. A new
construction would have to exploit or force the particular B_comp:R_a
convex roofs while meeting the exact Bell-output, return, initialization,
and supplied-entanglement definitions. A generic additivity violation
does not automatically provide such a construction.

4. WHY THIS IS A STRICTLY STRONGER RECEIVING CONCLUSION THAN SUPPORT TESTING

The previous Cycle4 bound required exact membership in the vectorized
commutant or a separately controlled leakage error. Its noncommuting tests
could have vanishing failure while the state stayed orthogonal to the
common support. The new conditional bound applies to EVERY output state;
it asks for finite conditional quantum uncertainty, or a locally measured
rank/conditional-Shannon upper bound, with no common support implication.

In the inherited noncommuting binary-label counterexample, the measured
conditional-entropy penalty is 2 h2(1/(M^2+1))->0, even though support
distance is one. Thus bounded conditional uncertainty can hold when no
small-leakage common-support conclusion is true. Separate sparse-orbit
work supplies a different strict class outside any common bounded-rank
commutant. Those are mathematically consequential enlargements of the
receiver class, not a repair of the false support inference.

The resource statement is still CONDITIONAL on a bounded quantum or
certified classical uncertainty budget; it does not assert such a bound
for unrestricted reservoirs, solve the generic logarithmic gap, establish
historical novelty, or demonstrate an efficient physical implementation.
