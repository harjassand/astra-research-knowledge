1. MODEL AND THE GRAPH-RANK WITNESS

Use the exact Cycle4 finite model: r>=1 ordered tasks, N D-sites each,
q=D^(rN), m=ln D. |X>> has normalized HS coefficient X and entanglement
S(X*X). E_(a,i) erases the first i sites of task a; the actual Bell-prefix
probability is ||E_(a,i)X||^2. All correlations are included.

For each task a choose an orthogonal complementary resolution
P_(a,y)=I_(T_a) tensor Q_(a,y), rank Q_(a,y)=k_(a,y)>=1.
Choose a directed bipartite label graph Gamma_a. It permits exactly the
coefficient blocks P_(a,y) X P_(a,z) with (y,z) in Gamma_a. Put
W_Gamma={|X>>: these forbidden blocks vanish for every a}.
This is a LINEAR SUBSPACE. It need not be an algebra, *-closed, or a
common-eigenbasis space. Projector families across different a need not
commute for the entropy theorem itself.

For a nonempty row define its permitted complementary dimension
 b_(a,y)=sum_{z:(y,z) in Gamma_a} k_(a,z), c_(a,y)=ln b_(a,y).
For an empty row set c_(a,y)=0; supported X has no weight on that row.
Define the known ALICE observable
 L_Gamma=sum_(a,y)c_(a,y)P_(a,y),
 Cmax=sum_a max_y c_(a,y).
Then 0<=L_Gamma<=Cmax I. The rank-one case charges log row degree; the
formula with sum of neighbor ranks is sharper than separately bounding a
maximum rank and a row degree.

THEOREM. For every normalized X with |X>> in W_Gamma,

 S(X*X)>=m sum_(a,i)||E_(a,i)X||^2-r C_D

Consequently every density sigma supported on W_Gamma obeys

 E_F(sigma)>=Tr[(O-L_Gamma tensor I)sigma]-r C_D,              (2)

where O is the actual sum of Bell-prefix effects weighted by m. This holds
on the entire support, including nonnormal operators and arbitrary coherent

its proof, optimization and the old recurrence programme are not reopened.

2. COMPLETE PURE-STATE PROOF

Fix one task and suppress a. Let q_yz=||P_y X P_z||^2, q_y=sum_z q_yz,
and q_z=sum_y q_yz. Erasure acts inside this task, so the complementary
blocks remain HS orthogonal under E_i. Thus the one-chain lemma on each
normalized rectangle Y_yz=P_y X P_z/sqrt(q_yz) gives

 m sum_i||E_iX||^2<=sum_yz q_yz E(Y_yz)+C_D.                 (3)

The residual local dimensions k_y and k_z may differ. Embed both residual
spaces isometrically in dimension max(k_y,k_z) and pad zeros; this leaves
all E_i norms and nonzero Schmidt roots unchanged. Therefore the received
one-chain lemma applies without an equal-rank premise.

Measure Alice's complementary label Y first. Her outcome-y conditional
pure state has Bob state rho_B^y and probability q_y. The entropy identity

 S(rho_B)=I(Y:B)+sum_y q_y S(rho_B^y)

holds for the corresponding cq state, since the unconditioned Bob state is
unchanged. Bob's subsequent label measurement Z is a channel, so ordinary
Holevo/data processing gives I(Y:B)>=I(Y:Z). Strong monotonicity of pure
entanglement under that Bob projective measurement gives

 sum_y q_y S(rho_B^y)>=sum_yz q_yz E(Y_yz).

Combined with (3),

 m sum_i||E_iX||^2<=S(rho_B)-I(Y:Z)+C_D
                 =S(rho_B)-H(Z)+H(Z|Y)+C_D.                 (4)

Under the declared coefficient convention rho_B=(X*X)^T, so Bob's label
measurement uses conjugate(P_z), and its complementary projection is
conjugate(Q_z). All displayed entropy and rank expressions respect this
transpose convention; for real computational labels conjugation is silent.

Bob's actual complementary marginal rho_(T_a^c) need not be block diagonal.
Pinching it by Q_z cannot decrease entropy; block entropy then gives

 S(rho_(T_a^c))<=H(Z)+sum_z q_z ln k_z.                     (5)

For each row y, q_(z|y) is supported on its graph neighbors. Relative entropy
to the distribution k_z/b_y gives the EXACT entropy-dimension bound

 H(Z|Y=y)+sum_z q_(z|y)ln k_z <= ln b_y.                    (6)

Equations (4)--(6) imply

 m sum_i||E_(a,i)X||^2
 <=S(rho_B)-S(rho_(T_a^c))+C_D+sum_y q_y c_(a,y).            (7)

Sum (7) over a. Quantum Shearer, a direct consequence of SSA, gives
sum_a S(rho_(T_a^c))>=(r-1)S(rho_B). The q_y are Alice's actual local
label weights, Tr(XX*P_y). This proves (1). Empty rows have q_y=0.

For (2), every pure component of every ensemble of supported sigma belongs
to W_Gamma, by positivity. Average (1); O and Alice's rank/degree observable
are linear. Infimize over all pure ensembles. No favored decomposition is
substituted for E_F. QED.

The graph theorem is a corollary of the principal universal measured-
conditional-entropy operation. Conversely, this proof establishes it without
assuming that operation as a black box. Holevo, SSA, pinching entropy and
the one-chain lemma are received ordinary machinery, not novelty claims.

3. EXACT OUTPUT EFFECT AND ROBUSTNESS

The individual graph-support projector is

Alice and Bob measure their complementary labels and accept exactly graph

instrument is not a free coherent support projection or state preparation.

If all physical P families commute, measure their full joint tuples alpha
and beta locally and accept iff every task pair is an edge. The acceptance
effect is EXACTLY Pi_Gamma=product_a W_a, the orthogonal projector onto the
intersection W_Gamma. Its failure ell=1-Tr(Pi_Gamma sigma) directly certifies
support leakage. The same Alice tuple supplies the charge sum_a c_(a,y_a).
All outcomes, including rejection, are counted. No hidden-memory return
claim follows from these finite output statistics.

With A_Gamma=O-L_Gamma tensor I, the exact Cycle4 pure-projection/entropy-
continuity argument gives, for every finite output sigma,

 E_F(sigma)>=Tr(A_Gamma sigma)-r C_D
 -(2ln q+Cmax)sqrt(ell)-h2(min(sqrt(ell),1/2)).              (8)

This is established continuity machinery with changed known observable;
it is not a new continuity constant. The global projection is used only
inside the proof. If W_Gamma is zero the ell=1 bound is trivially negative;
otherwise the ordinary normalized-support-projection proof applies. For
noncommuting families a certified gap gamma of
sum_a(I-W_a) gives ell<=sum_a failure_a/gamma. Without such a gap, small
individual failures alone do not suffice; Cycle4's actual counterexample
already proves the need for a conditioning premise.

For iid preparations of the SAME full sigma, graph leakage is a Bernoulli
variable and c(alpha)/r lies in [0,Cmax/r]. The same Hoeffding counts and

Cmax. Within sigma no independence is assumed. Generic projectors may
cost exponentially much; the explicit family below has elementary local
computational-basis tests. Do not identify a short replay with discovery cost.

4. LONG-CYCLE FAMILY WITH CONSTANT GRAPH CHARGE AND NO OLD BOUNDED-RANK COVER

Assume r>=2 and Q=D^N>=3. Index each task computational string by
j in Z_Q, with the LAST digit least significant. Let F_a|j>=|j+1 mod Q>.
Let F=product_a F_a, and for task a let F_(-a) increment every other task.
Use rank-one computational complementary labels and graphs

 Gamma_a={(y,z):y=z or y=F_(-a)z}.                           (9)

The two neighbors are distinct, so b_(a,y)=2 for every y and
Cmax=r ln2, independently of N and r. All test projectors commute. The
support includes ALL diagonal coefficients D_0 and ALL F D_1 for arbitrary
global diagonal D_0,D_1. Hence it includes arbitrary coherent sums
D_0+F D_1, with arbitrary globally correlated coefficients. It contains a
subspace F Diag that is HS-orthogonal to the OLD rank-one diagonal commutant,
since F has no fixed point. Generic weighted shifts are nonnormal.

For Q>=3, F* is not allowed by these graphs: its complementary shift is
-1, neither 0 nor +1. Thus W_Gamma is not *-closed. This already rules out
identifying it literally with a commutant algebra.

More strongly, no old uniformly bounded-complementary-rank commutant can
contain this WHOLE linear support. Suppose P=I_(T_a) tensor Q_0 commutes
with every coefficient in W_Gamma. Since all diagonal coefficients belong,
P itself must be computationally diagonal. Since F belongs, its diagonal
indicator is invariant under F_(-a). Every F_(-a) orbit has length Q. Every
nonzero such complementary projector therefore has rank at least Q=D^N.
Every resolution defining a commutant containing W_Gamma has weighted
log-rank charge at least N ln D PER TASK, whereas (1) charges ln2.

This is an exact structural separation of RESOURCE CLASSES. It does not
state that no individual favorable state could have another small-rank
description. It does not prove a Bell-supply advantage or generic critical
hierarchy. It shows why replacing graph support by an algebra closure loses
the useful finite-horizon bound: the closure must follow length-D^N orbits.

One can also produce a single weighted-shift coefficient requiring large
old ranks: choose all global diagonal weights c_j>0 with distinct magnitudes,
X=F diag(c), normalized. Any selfadjoint projector commuting with X also
commutes with X*X=diag(c_j^2), so is diagonal. The same orbit argument then
forces every nonzero complementary block rank to be at least Q. Such X is
in F Diag and is nonnormal unless weights are constant along F orbits.

This is an EXACT separation. It supplies no lower bound on the distance
from a particular graph state to every alternative bounded-rank commutant.
In particular near-uniform weighted shifts may approach a Fourier-diagonal
special case. An approximate algebra reduction would require a new charged
leakage theorem; it is not excluded by the exact orbit argument alone.

5. PROVED RECEIVING OPERATION ON THE ACTUAL CONSUMABLE STOCK

The exact inherited stock building block is
 sigma_k=Phi_D^(tensor k) tensor chi_D^(tensor(N-k)),
 chi_D=D^-1 sum_t |tt><tt|, 0<=k<=N.
Its actual Bell-prefix probability is p_i=1 for i<=k and D^(k-i) for i>k.
Apply the fixed local unitary F_a to ALICE'S N-site task output. This costs
no shared entanglement and preserves every entanglement cost exactly. It
does not erase any memory, label, branch, or output correlation. Its entire
coefficient support is F_a Diag, orthogonal to the unshifted diagonal support.

The resulting EXACT Bell-prefix probabilities are

 p'_i=p_i[1-D^(-(N-i))], 1<=i<=N.                          (10)

Proof: decompose chi tail into uniform computational strings t. Partial
trace over the first i digits of F_a X_(k,t) keeps precisely those terms
where incrementing the final N-i digits does not carry into that prefix.
More explicitly, T=D^(N-k), L=D^(N-i), and the normalized coefficient is
X_(k,t)=D^(-k/2)diag(1_{j mod T=t}). For i<=k every compatible suffix has
D^i equal prefix amplitudes; the exact fixed-tail erased norm squared is
1-D^(i-k)1_{t=T-1}. Averaging t gives 1-D^(i-N). For i>k there is one
fixed remaining suffix and D^k equal prefix amplitudes; the fixed-tail
norm squared is D^(k-i)[1-1_{t mod L=L-1}]. Averaging t gives
D^(k-i)(1-1/L). In a bad-carry term the prefix shifts by one mod D^i and
no diagonal partial-trace entry survives. These two counts prove (10),
including i=N where all terms carry and p'_N=0.

Equivalently, for full Bell stock k=N the actual partial trace is
Tr_prefix F_a=D^i S_(D^(N-i)), where S_l is the truncated forward shift
with its wrap edge omitted; its HS norm squared is l-1. For partial stock
the same count is restricted to the fixed tail and averaged as above.

By linearity (10) holds for ANY inventory mixture sum_k pi_k sigma_k.
For arbitrarily correlated inventory across r tasks it holds taskwise for
the relevant marginal; no independence of inventories is required. Thus

 sum_i(p_i-p'_i)<=sum_(j=0)^(N-1)D^-j < D/(D-1).           (11)

The graph family therefore receives the ACTUAL ordinary stock with only

preparation entanglement exactly. Its row degree is at most2 (indeed the
transformed component alone needs permutation degree1). This is not an
unrelated favorable example: it is an explicit local operation on the
strongest matched stock comparator and a proved calculation of the changed
observables. It gives no improved reservoir or catalytic advantage.

For the actual uniform inventory k=1,...,N, sum_i p_i>=(N+1)/2. Thus its
transformed state has sum_i p'_i>=(N+1)/2-D/(D-1): its physical Bell-prefix
signal remains extensive in N, rather than being an isolated favorable

m(N+1)/2. The shifted stock's exact entanglement cost already equals the

Crucially, F changes the target Bell-prefix probabilities, including the

The explicit increment is computationally modest, unlike arbitrary dense
projectors: perform the most-significant digit controlled increment when
all lower digits equal D-1, proceeding down toward the least-significant
digit. Each multi-control can be computed into clean local ancillas,
applied, and uncomputed before lower digits change. For fixed D this gives
O(N^2) finite local gates with O(N) reusable local workspace; product
ancillas carry no shared resource and are returned/retained explicitly.
The graph test is local computational-basis readout plus classical modular
increment comparison, O(r^2 N) naive classical work and O(rN) readout.
These are constructive asymptotic circuit counts, not hardware timings.

6. CONSEQUENCE AND REMAINING GAP

Replacing the Cycle4 block support by W_Gamma in its complete finite reuse
proof changes only the known output charge and support test. With Cmax/r
bounded and global initialization/support errors vanishing in the stated
fixed-N iid order, the supplied-entanglement rate is still
m/(2 delta)+O_(D,Cmax/r)(1), matched by admitted ordinary consumable stock.
The resource class now includes bounded-neighbor-dimension graph supports
whose smallest old commutant closure has exponentially growing block rank.

The exact finite formula must retain N epsilon and N sqrt(eta) leakage
terms; bounded graph degree does not remove them. Fixed nonzero global
initialization error, expected versus deterministic preselected supplies,
and unobserved complete-memory return remain distinct contracts.

The unrestricted case, with arbitrary measured label confusion and no
bounded neighbor dimension, remains OPEN. This graph corollary does not
prove unrestricted critical hierarchy or close generic formation's loggap.
Next science should test whether the universal conditional-entropy charge
can be reduced using actual erasure structure, or exhibit a necessary large
charge. Replacing a sparse graph by its full algebra closure is now proved
an inappropriate reduction for that purpose.
