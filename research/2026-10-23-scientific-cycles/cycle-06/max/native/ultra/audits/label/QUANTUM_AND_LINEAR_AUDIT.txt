1. PURE QUANTUM CONDITIONAL-ENTROPY WITNESS

For task a choose only Alice's finite complementary POVM {M_(a,y)}. Apply its
canonical square-root instrument to pure |X>>, and retain outcome Y. The
normalized branches remain pure. As before, prefix-score norms decompose by
POVM completeness because every M_(a,y) acts only on the complement of task a.
The received universal one-chain theorem, with a residual embedding when needed,
therefore gives

 m sum_i p_(a,i) <= sum_y p_y E(psi_y)+C_D.

Bob's conditional full states rho_(B_all|y) average to rho_(B_all), and each
branch is pure globally. Hence the following identity is exact:

 sum_y p_y E(psi_y) = S(B_all)-I(Y:B_all).

Data processing under partial trace on Bob implies
 I(Y:B_all)>=I(Y:B_comp_a).
With Q_a=S(B_comp_a|Y)=sum_y p_y S(rho_(B_comp_a|y)), this yields

 m sum_i p_(a,i)
 <= E(psi)-S(B_comp_a)+Q_a+C_D.

Sum over a and use quantum Shearer on the SINGLE Bob global marginal:
 sum_a S(B_comp_a)>=(r-1)S(B_all)=(r-1)E(psi).
Therefore

 E(psi)>=m sum_(a,i) p_(a,i)-r C_D-sum_a Q_a.             (Q1)

There is no support, normality, block, rank, gap, output independence, Alice/Bob
basis alignment or cross-task POVM compatibility premise. Each a is a separate
mathematical/output-measurement setting. Since Y is classical, Q_a>=0; this is
not an unrestricted negative quantum-conditional-entropy correction.

2. ARBITRARY MIXED OUTPUT STATES

For any finite sigma, define tau^a_(Y B_comp_a) by Alice's local POVM outcome,
tracing Alice's quantum registers and Bob's task a registers. This cq state is a
LINEAR CPTP image of sigma: its unnormalized y-block is
 Tr_(A_all B_task_a)[(M_(a,y) tensor I) sigma].
The square-root sandwich produces the same reduced block after tracing Alice.

For any pure ensemble sigma=sum_j t_j |psi_j><psi_j|, (Q1) gives
 sum_j t_j E(psi_j)>=Tr(O sigma)-r C_D-sum_a sum_j t_j Q_a(psi_j).
CQ conditional entropy is concave in the cq state: it is the sum of perspectives
p_y S(rho_y) of von Neumann entropy. Thus
 sum_j t_j Q_a(psi_j)<=Q_a(sigma).
Taking the infimum over all ensembles proves

 E_F(sigma)>=Tr(O sigma)-r C_D-sum_a S(B_comp_a|Y_a).       (Q2)

Every sigma on the finite external output space is covered. Q_a is a property
of its cq reduced state. In general evaluating it requires quantum-state
inference or tomography; it has NOT become a cheap scalar output observable.
Nor does the theorem determine unobserved complete-memory return.

3. CLASSICAL RANK/PREDICTABILITY IS AN ACCESSIBLE UPPER RELAXATION

For each a additionally choose any finite POVM {B_(a,z)} on Bob complement,
with nonzero ranks k_(a,z). For each Alice conditional quantum state, the
isometric-record/dephasing argument in POVM_UPGRADE.txt gives

 S(rho_(B_comp|y))
 <= H(Z|Y=y)+sum_z q_(z|y) ln k_z.

Average over y. Therefore, for EVERY mixed sigma,
 Q_a(sigma)<=H_sigma(Z_a|Y_a)+sum_z q_(a,z) ln k_(a,z).
This proves that the previously audited universal classical/rank law is an
upper relaxation of the stronger quantum conditional-entropy penalty, rather
than an independent support assumption.

4. FIXED CLASSICAL KERNEL: UNIVERSAL LINEAR WITNESS

Fix strictly positive probability kernels pi_a(z|y), sum_z pi_a(z|y)=1.
Choose them before the test used for simple finite-sample concentration.
Let
 c_(a,yz)=ln[k_(a,z)/pi_a(z|y)],
 R_a=sum_yz c_(a,yz) [I_Ta tensor M_(a,y)]_A
                       tensor [I_Ta tensor B_(a,z)^T]_B.
Identity factors on unaffected registers are implicit. Since effects are
Hermitian, Bob's conjugate effect equals its transpose in this expression.
The joint law is q_(a,yz); hence Tr(R_a sigma)=sum_yz q_(a,yz)c_(a,yz).
Conditional crossentropy minus conditional entropy is exactly
 sum_y q_y D(q(.|y)||pi_a(.|y))>=0.
Together with (Q2) and section3 this gives, for ALL sigma,

 E_F(sigma)>=Tr[(O-sum_a R_a)sigma]-r C_D.                (L1)

This is an ordinary linear entanglement witness family, universally valid on
the finite external output space. Optimizing or learning the kernel can improve
its observed mean, but the optimization cost and statistical reuse must be
charged. It does not establish that the best possible mean has a uniform
bound for arbitrary useful reservoirs.

All c_(a,yz)>=0 because k_z>=1 and pi<=1. With
 B_a=max_yz c_(a,yz),
POVM completeness gives 0<=R_a<=B_a I. Measure Alice and Bob local effects,
compare classical outcomes, and assign c_(a,yz). This single-copy scalar is in
[0,B_a] and has exactly the required mean; coherent control or global projection
is unnecessary. For n independent preparations of the FULL sigma and a fixed
setting a, Hoeffding gives
 Pr[|samplemean-Tr(R_a sigma)|>=zeta]<=2 exp[-2n zeta^2/B_a^2].
Thus n>=B_a^2 ln(2/beta)/(2 zeta^2) suffices for error zeta with confidence
1-beta. Internal outputs of each sigma may be arbitrarily correlated.

To estimate the PER-TASK average (sum_a Tr R_a sigma)/r, choose a uniformly
random task on each independent full-state preparation and use its cost.
Its range is [0,max_a B_a], so the same sample formula applies with that maximum.
For the UNNORMALIZED sum at absolute error zeta, the random-task estimator is
r times the recorded cost and the range is [0,r max_a B_a]; the r^2 factor
must then be retained. Cross-task POVMs need not commute because only one task
setting is measured per prepared full state. No iid experimental preparation
or inexpensive POVM synthesis is inferred by the mathematical theorem.

5. OPTIONAL QUANTUM-REFERENCE LINEAR FAMILY

For each a,y fix any strictly positive normalized density operator omega_(a,y)
on the COEFFICIENT-SIDE copy of Bob's complement. Its physical Bob
reference is omega_(a,y)^T. If rho^phys_(B_comp|y) denotes the actual Bob
conditional state, put tilde(rho_y)=(rho^phys_(B_comp|y))^T. Relative entropy
nonnegativity and transpose invariance of entropy give
 S(rho^phys_(B_comp|y))<=-Tr[tilde(rho_y) ln omega_(a,y)].
Consequently
 R_a^q=sum_y [I_Ta tensor M_(a,y)]_A
                   tensor [I_Ta tensor (-ln omega_(a,y))^T]_B
satisfies the universal linear witness (L1) with R_a replaced by R_a^q.
It is measurable by Alice's local outcome followed by Bob's spectral
measurement of (-ln omega_y)^T. Its outcome range is [0,B_a^q], where
 B_a^q=max_y[-ln lambda_min(omega_(a,y))].
Generic dense reference synthesis/measurement may be exponential, and
B_a^q>=ln dim(H_comp_a). This corollary supplies a mathematical measurement
interface, not an efficiency claim.

For projective Bob blocks Q_z, choosing
 omega_y=sum_z pi(z|y) Q_z/k_z
makes -ln omega_y=sum_z ln[k_z/pi(z|y)]Q_z exactly, recovering the fixed
classical kernel witness. For general nonprojective POVMs no such identification
is presumed; their section4 witness already follows from the separate
Stinespring/dephasing and classical crossentropy inequalities.
