STRONGEST INDEPENDENT AUDIT: DIFFERENT COMPLEMENTARY POVMS
22 October 2026. Addendum to AUDIT.txt. No numerical search.

DECISION: PASS. The principal projective-label theorem extends to arbitrary
finite known POVMs, independently selected on Alice and Bob for each task.
The previous audit remains a correct special case. The following replaces
its block decomposition and complementary pinching arguments.

Definitions and vectorization convention are as in AUDIT.txt. For each task a,
let {A_(a,y)} and {B_(a,z)} be arbitrary finite positive effects on its complement,
summing separately to I. Remove zero effects. Set k^A_y=rank A_y,
k^B_z=rank B_z. Alice's physical POVM is I_Ta tensor A_y and Bob's is
I_Ta tensor conjugate(B_z). Effects, ranges and ranks must be known/charged;
no unknown memory resource is inferred from these output measurements.

Use the canonical square-root local instrument in the mathematical proof.
Suppress a, and set
 X_yz=(I_T tensor sqrt(A_y)) X (I_T tensor sqrt(B_z)),
 q_yz=||X_yz||_HS^2.
This q is exactly the joint law of the stated local effects. Completeness gives
sum_yz q_yz=1. The Bob outcome law satisfies q_z=Tr[rho(I_T tensor B_z)].
Each nonzero normalized branch is pure, with row residual support contained in
ran A_y and column residual support contained in ran B_z. Thus the rectangular
padding argument of AUDIT.txt section2 applies, with residual dimensions
k^A_y,k^B_z. Canonical square-root instruments are only a proof operation;
observing the joint law does not require certification of their disturbance.

Prefix erasure commutes as a superoperator with the complementary left/right
factors. Although the resulting branches are NOT orthogonal, their squared
norms still sum correctly by POVM completeness. For Z=E_(a,i)X,

 sum_yz ||sqrt(A_y) Z sqrt(B_z)||_HS^2
 = sum_yz Tr[sqrt(B_z) Z† A_y Z sqrt(B_z)]
 = Tr[Z†(sum_y A_y)Z(sum_z B_z)]
 = ||Z||_HS^2.

All identities understand identity factors on task T. Consequently equations
(1),(2) of AUDIT.txt remain valid with the canonical POVM branches. No
projective-support premise survives this replacement.

Bob's square-root outcome Z first, followed by Alice's square-root outcome Y,
still produces pure conditional branches. Equation(3) of AUDIT.txt is the exact
cq entropy loss after Bob; equation(4) is average entanglement monotonicity
under Alice's measurement; data processing to Y remains valid. Thus

 sum_yz q_yz E(X_yz/sqrt(q_yz)) <= S(rho)-I(Y:Z).

For the complementary marginal rho_C, define the isometry
 V: |v> -> sum_z |z> tensor sqrt(B_z)|v>.
V†V=I by POVM completeness. Thus S(V rho_C V†)=S(rho_C). Dephase only the
record z; finite dephasing is a mixture of unitaries, hence increases entropy.
The resulting block state has conditional components
 sqrt(B_z)rho_C sqrt(B_z)/q_z,
with support rank at most k^B_z. Therefore

 S(rho_C) <= H(Z)+sum_z q_z ln k^B_z.

This is the exact replacement for projective pinching. Combining with the
measurement-loss argument and taskwise quantum Shearer proves, for any pure X,

 S(X†X) >= m sum_(a,i) p_(a,i) - r C_D
    -sum_a [sum_z q_(a,z) ln k^B_(a,z)+H(Z_a|Y_a)].        (P1)

The mixed-state proof is identical to AUDIT.txt section5. Rank costs and scores
are linear in the state; conditional entropy is concave in its measured joint
law. Every finite output sigma therefore satisfies

 E_F(sigma) >= Tr(O sigma)-r C_D-R_B(sigma),
 R_B(sigma)=sum_a [sum_z q_(a,z) ln k^B_(a,z)
                                  +H_sigma(Z_a|Y_a)].    (P2)

There is no commutant support, leakage, gap, normality, same-basis, same-POVM,
equal-rank, or cross-task commutativity premise. Only the stated finite external
model, inherited universal one-chain bound and known POVMs enter the proof.

FANO CERTIFICATE, WITH THE NECESSARY MONOTONE ENVELOPE

For each task fix a deterministic decoder g_a:Y_a -> Z_a BEFORE evaluation,
and let f_a=Pr[Z_a != g_a(Y_a)]. If the Bob alphabet has L_a>=2 elements,
ordinary Fano gives
 H(Z_a|Y_a) <= h2(f_a)+f_a ln(L_a-1).
When only an upper confidence bound f_a<=u_a is available, the RHS is not
monotone on all [0,1]. The valid upper certificate is
 F_(L_a)(u_a)=h2(v_a)+v_a ln(L_a-1),
 v_a=min(u_a,1-1/L_a),
which equals ln L_a when u_a>=1-1/L_a. For L_a=1 the conditional entropy is
identically zero. A high-confidence upper f estimate is a statistical premise
requiring an actual sampling model, separately charged full output preparations,
and decoder selection/holdout handling if the decoder was learned from data.
Fano avoids claiming an efficiently estimated entire large-alphabet law; it
retains the actual alphabet charge ln(L_a-1). It does not estimate hidden memory.

GLOBAL ORIENTATION STRENGTHENING AND A CAUTION

Swap Alice and Bob throughout the complete proof, including quantum Shearer,
to obtain the valid alternate penalty
 R_A(sigma)=sum_a [sum_y q_(a,y) ln k^A_(a,y)
                                  +H_sigma(Y_a|Z_a)].
Hence the best of the two derived inequalities is

 E_F(sigma) >= Tr(O sigma)-r C_D-min(R_A(sigma),R_B(sigma)). (P3)

Both orientations use the SAME measured joint laws. It is valid to choose the
smaller GLOBAL total after computing it, because both complete inequalities
hold pointwise. It is NOT justified to minimize orientation separately per task:
mixed-side complementary marginals need not obey the Shearer allocation. For
r=2,N=1,D=2, the pure global state |0>_(A1) tensor Phi2_(A2 B1) tensor |0>_(B2)
has total entanglement ln2 but S(A1)+S(B2)=0. Thus the hypothetical mixed-side
complement entropy lower bound by total entanglement is false. This observation
invalidates that proof shortcut; it is not claimed to falsify a separately
proved stronger full witness.

SCOPE

(P2) changes the surviving resource class: finite-state witnesses on the entire
external output space now use observed rank plus classical predictability,
rather than distance to a common operator algebra. It does not prove that an
unrestricted near-optimal reservoir has bounded R_B/r or R_A/r. The previous
gap-free inference of support distance remains false; this result avoids that
inference rather than repairing it. The physical return condition, full global
initialization error, deterministic/preselected expected supply, and supplied
entanglement monotonicity remain distinct and unchanged. No arbitrary quantum
instrument with unresolved sub-Kraus labels has been substituted in the proof.
