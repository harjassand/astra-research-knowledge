The fixed-producer's 9 definite gains include 2 incomplete pairs whose lower
and upper bounds nevertheless establish the direction. Thus do not describe
all 9 as complete-pair gains. The broad 60-task maxima are maxima over native
admitted candidates with missing labels bounded, not expected accuracy
estimates or independently rerun task successes.

The main native-best final edge is confirmed: parent has 5 successes and child 4
on the common10, while parent has 23 known successes plus 2 unknown and child 30
known successes with zero unknown on common60. This is direct evidence that
these two evaluation views can rank the same edge differently.

Primary paper Appendix E.2 labels the first 10 cases as basic-functionality
checks. Their results are not an appropriate stand-alone measure of broad
coding capability or improvement-generation ability. Root's explicit correction
and common60 sensitivity are essential, and should appear near any primary
numbers rather than in a distant disclaimer.

Three further reporting qualifications:

1. Verified gain means verified arithmetic on released original outcomes, not
   an independent rerun of either agent or an estimated persistent increase in
   success probability. New task IDs are newly observed passes relative to a
   parent snapshot; stochastic task solving can create both passes and losses.

2. Missing-run data and eligibility are outcome-dependent. First 80 versus all 82
   is an explicit sensitivity, not a substitute for equal resources. Main's
   deeper evaluation and per-agent code can consume more compute per attempt.

3. The source/trace baseline discrepancy prevents treating the main versus
   fixed-producer difference as an isolated manipulation of producer ability.
   It does not justify attributing that difference to a bug or dismissing the
   main pipeline's observed progress. One trace establishes the workspace
   difference locally; its aggregate effect is unknown.

Minor code-label point: new_task_ids_10 is emitted even for incomplete pairs.
If those per-row lists are presented outside the aggregate complete-pair
calculations, label them observed child passes absent from reported parent
passes, not verified parent-failure-to-child-success events. The aggregate
gain_tasks_10 field correctly requires complete pairs.

The strongest defensible interpretation is:
  The recovered records support useful accumulation in this tested coding
  pipeline and allow nontrivial output-level analysis. They do not identify
  increased producer ability as the causal mechanism, establish a resource-
  normalized self-improvement rate, or demonstrate compounding acceleration.
  Apparent failure on a narrow common probe can coexist with broader progress.

This is an argument against both overclaiming recursive intelligence and
prematurely rejecting a useful system from an inadequate proxy. It is not
evidence that Astra should substitute a coding-agent search for its autonomous
scientific architecture.
