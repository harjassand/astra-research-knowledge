The new evidence strengthens the case for genuine cumulative mathematical
capability, but not a measured improvement in the method of discovery. Pro1 now
proves and actually uses a coding criterion beyond scalar bath-depth dynamics.
Pro2 now proves and actually uses degree-free operator recovery beyond the old

constants: their proved interfaces admit operations excluded by earlier sufficient
conditions. Their chosen major unresolved questions nevertheless remain open.

The common reasoning move is more precise than 'retain more structure'. Each owner

scalar closure was sufficient for bath control but not necessary; Frobenius output
control was stronger than the requested operator-norm output. The successful new
objects are sufficient replacements with exact end-to-end arguments. Merely dropping
the old requirement would have been invalid. This is an observational explanation,
not evidence that a generic instruction to weaken assumptions causes discovery.

1. PRO1: WHAT ACTUALLY CHANGED

1.1 A sharp restricted order law, with substantially stronger quantifiers
For each fixed block length k, independent k-call input densities may vary by block
and by horizon but must commute with total color charge. The new upper is
  Ic/(nk) <= 3/2 + 8 k^5 ln(2) beta^4,
for beta <= (3/8)^(2k)/(64 k^4 ln 2), uniformly in every horizon and every normal
bath initializer. It permits infinite mean depth and infinite bath entropy.
The inherited pairs, padded for odd k, give matching Theta_k(beta^4) excess for
the actual initializer and every fixed k>=2. Thus fixed four-call replacement
cannot improve the power in the admitted class. This is not a general capacity
converse: correlations between blocks, charge-changing coherences and feedback
remain outside the upper. The k=4 explicit angle threshold is far below 1/512.

The constants justify one simultaneous limit, not all growing blocks:
k(beta)<=c ln(1/beta), c<1/[2 ln(8/3)], gives O(beta^4 ln^5(1/beta)). The owner
properly preserves the distinction between fixed-k theorems and a growing-k law.

1.2 The proof removes two unnecessary infinite-dimensional obstructions
The bounded observable G=Q-W*QW replaces a difference of potentially infinite
depth expectations. Its block transfers telescope despite correlations between
memory and past outputs; only the incoming block must be independent. The entropy
upper uses finite input reference R and external output B, via Ic<=S(R) and
Ic<=2S(B)-S(R), rather than an undefined bath-entropy subtraction.

Bulk parity kills linear beta terms only after input-charge pinching. The proof
does not apply that parity at the root. Instead the true root-retaining reference
has a strictly positive boundary margin, at least (3/8)^k on depth<k. Charge
covariance removes low/high-depth cross terms, so that margin absorbs near-reference
corrections. Far inputs pay relative entropy. The final optimization has the form
-a D+b beta^2 sqrt(D), hence quartic order. These steps were checked algebraically
against the main source; the constant is conservative but the quantifiers are real.

1.3 The constructive advance is a new sufficient interface, not the upper alone
An exact admissible four-call state has drift depending on the bath's top color.
Thus charge covariance does not imply a scalar depth chain. The replacement uses
homogeneous Kraus increments Delta in [-k,k], with positive effects A_Delta,
sum A_Delta=I and [A_Delta,L]=0. The exact form identity is
  Phi* exp(tL) = [sum exp(t Delta) A_Delta] exp(tL).
A uniformly negative bulk OPERATOR drift implies a genuine exponential moment,
with a finite root remainder. The code then uses spectral-type domination and
the inherited joint compression, including its r^2 factor, CPTP auxiliary
normalization and polar repair. This proves initializer-independent codes with
pointwise all-normal full-diamond convergence, without scalar closure.

The actual receiving state rho4 deletes a reducing two-by-two coherence from
omega tensor omega. Its entropy increases strictly while exact drift obeys
  D_rho4=-2 kappa I+2 x kappa (I-S1 S1*) <= -2 kappa(1-x) I.
It is genuinely non-scalar and yields coded rate S(rho4)/4>S(omega)/2. The small
coefficient increment is not the main consequence; the admissible construction
class has expanded through an end-to-end proved replacement criterion.

1.4 What remains open and consequential

coefficient variational problem A_k, not a full capacity formula. The owner next

Existing k=2..6 susceptibility numbers suggest a question, not its answer.

2. PRO2: WHAT ACTUALLY CHANGED

2.1 A degree-free information/stability criterion
For proper coordinate designs, exact recovery still requires every bichromatic
component to be a tree. The new controlling quantity is cut balance:
  Gamma=max_edges ab/(a+b), M=max_edges min(a,b), Gamma<=M<=2Gamma.
With r the maximum number of neighbor colors,
  max(1,sqrt Gamma) <= rho = ||canonical LS decoder||_(F->op)
     <= sqrt(1+4rM) <= sqrt(1+8rGamma).
For fixed q this is an if-and-only-if uniform-stability criterion, with no maximum
degree requirement. The canonical projection/inverse decoder is minimax optimal
for this fixed design; the explicit upper expression need not equal its true norm.
The lower remains representation-specific, not an all-real-design obstruction.

I checked the proof's key mechanism. Centered child-subtree indicators have Gram
diag(a)-aa^T/N, giving a squared-row estimate independent of the number of branches.
Centroid orientation bounds incoming entries per column by r. This yields direct
operator control. The information lower is a cut flow with one unit cut edge and
action norm squared 1/a+1/b. Orthogonal projection onto the exact action range
gives the decoder norm equality and the zero-observation argument extends the
lower to nonlinear exact decoders. The proof does not assume stochastic noise.

2.2 The required output norm changes which operations are admissible
Hub trees with arbitrarily many arms of length <=L admit two queries with operator
constant sqrt(1+4L), independent of degree. A two-arm path gives a matching
Theta(sqrt L) worst scaling. On the SAME design, an alternating ramp forces any
exact Frobenius-output decoder to have constant Theta(L). Thus demanding the
stronger output norm would wrongly reject a valid target operation.
The 397-vertex exact fixture establishes this distinction concretely, but its
ordinary three-query forest comparator has constant sqrt2, so one saved query
does not establish total computational superiority.

A double-star family proves that individually stable pieces can become unstable
when joined: diameter three and two pieces do not prevent a large balanced cut.
This is a global composition failure, not evidence that a particular local solve
was insufficiently precise. Its unbounded degree and specified-coordinate scope
are preserved; it does not resolve the degree-four all-design question.

2.3 A genuine structural reduction, not a hidden solver
Every maximal edge-connected triangle union in a partial two-tree is a 2-tree.
The source proof uses two-degeneracy and a minor/edge-count argument for clique
attachments. The inherited degree-four classification then identifies long
triangle components as squared paths with only endpoint ports.

The new finite twelve-state boundary transition certificate extends arbitrary
endpoint colors through a period-nine core, with bounded bichromatic pieces and
no bichromatic connection between opposite ends. Removing long strips reduces
the full coordinate-existence question to graphs whose triangle components have
order at most29. A valid reduced coloring extends with component bound65K.
The corresponding attachment lemma controls cut mass directly:
  Gamma_final <= max((1+A) Gamma_base, A, B/4).
The stated disjoint-root attachment convention is essential.

The owner actually received the local strip operation into a full original-support
matrix recovery calculation. It did not mistake original action cells for reduced
graph observations. On this fixture the new squared certificate49 loses to the
inherited squared certificate20; the implementation correctly keeps20. The new
result is a class/reduction/composition extension, not a universal conditioning win.

2.4 What remains open and consequential
Four arbitrary real directions for every treewidth-two, degree-four support remain
open; three fail and five suffice. The coordinate subproblem now concentrates on
triangle-free/chordless two-terminal atoms and their interaction with bounded
triangle pieces. Local elimination/reinsertion needs a size or cut-mass invariant
through arbitrary separator depth. A low-degree vertex theorem alone does not
supply that invariant. A counterfamily to every coordinate coloring would retire
only that representation, not all real four-column designs.

The primary-literature notes properly credit classical Hessian coloring and
substitution mechanisms; their reported readings do not amount to exhaustive

3. WHAT THIS CHANGES ABOUT THE META-RESEARCH DIAGNOSIS

A. The 'only rigorous exclusions' interpretation no longer fits these returns.
Pro1 closes an improved-power route but simultaneously extends constructive coding;
Pro2 leaves its major four-query problem open but extends a general stability law
and a structural reduction. A negative and positive result coexist in each programme.

B. Autonomy is doing substantive work. Pro1's three bounded contributors covered
an upper obstruction, an exact representation and independent challenge, while
the owner constructed and integrated the new operator-to-code bridge. Pro2's
structural/literature/review work fed a new owner theorem and actual receiving
operation. This is a useful division by mathematical obligation, not measured
evidence of an optimal agent count, model choice or context policy.

C. The prior evaluator's 'preserve the relevant dependence' hypothesis remains
compatible but incomplete. Both successes also show the value of requiring LESS
intermediate structure when the downstream task needs less. Operator drift is
weaker than scalar Markov closure; operator error is weaker than Frobenius error.
The proper replacement is a proved sufficient condition, not relaxed rigor.

D. The opposite risk persists. Pro1's new fixed-k upper and Pro2's coordinate
lower can tempt overgeneralization. Exact scope is scientific content, not merely
reporting hygiene. General capacity and arbitrary real designs remain harder
questions, and improving a subclass does not prove an acceleration toward them.

E. No recursive amplification claim follows. Pro1 has actual receiving use of a
new criterion, Pro2 of a new extension, but both occur inside the same owner cycle.
We have not measured whether the system became better at inventing the next
criterion, nor demonstrated an independent cross-investigation methodological gain.

4. ONE AMBITIOUS FEASIBLE META-RESEARCH TEST

Test automatic discovery of a STRICTLY WEAKER SUFFICIENT SCIENTIFIC INTERFACE.
The target is not a shorter prompt, smaller summary or a predicted answer. It is
an operation that removes an incidental obstruction while preserving the complete
scientific endpoint, thereby admitting a genuinely new construction.

Use a fresh, source-complete obstruction selected by an owner because it matters
to their investigation. Both a proposed interface-search operation and a strong
unrestricted control receive the same exact target, source, incumbent methods,
known failed premises, tool access and accounted budget. The proposed operation
must make its replacement condition explicit and prove target sufficiency. A
separate receiver supplies an actual construction outside the old interface and
checks the full goal. Success requires this new admissible operation, not a
counterexample to an overstrong premise or a looser-looking bound alone.

Potential scientific value: the system learns to change the class of objects it
can reason about while keeping the required conclusion exact. This could remove
structural barriers rather than merely reduce computation. The Pro1/Pro2 histories
provide compelling motivation, but the now-solved scalar/norm examples are not
valid test problems; using them would be retrospective and likely saturated.

Failure conditions: the unrestricted control finds the same interface at comparable
cost; the weaker condition is known machinery with no receiving gain; its proof
cannot cover the actual operation; or scope/error/resource relaxation silently
changes the scientific task. In those cases preserve the theorem or failure but
reject the amplification claim. If no fresh consequential obligation with complete
inputs is available this round, do not manufacture a benchmark. The observational
inference above is already a stricter, useful update to the research hypothesis.
