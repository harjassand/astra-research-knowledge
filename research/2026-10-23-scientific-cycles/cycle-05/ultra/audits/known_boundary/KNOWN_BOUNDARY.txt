1. A COMPLETE INDEPENDENT DERIVATION OF THE PROPOSED OBSERVED-LABEL BOUND

Use the principal finite model: r tasks of N D-sites on each party, m=ln D,
O=m sum_(a,i) BellPrefix_(a,i), and the received universal pure one-chain
bound m sum_i p_i <= entanglement+C_D. The received one-chain statement also
holds for rectangular residual spaces by padding either side with zero rows
or columns. No hierarchy improvement is claimed in this audit.

For each task a choose arbitrary finite complementary POVMs A_(a,y) on
Alice and F_(a,z) on Bob. They may differ, and different task POVMs need
not commute. A single square-root Kraus operator per outcome leaves each
outcome branch of a pure input pure. Put k_(a,z)=rank F_(a,z), discard any
zero effects, and let Y_a,Z_a be their joint actual outcomes.

Complementary Kraus operators commute with the task Bell-prefix effects.
Trace preservation therefore makes outcome-averaged Bell-prefix scores
equal their scores before this measurement. Each branch has Bob residual
support dimension at most k_(a,z), and Alice residual support at most
rank A_(a,y), so the rectangular one-chain lemma applies.

For a pure input psi, write E=S(B_all). After Alice's outcome Y=y the pure
branch entanglement is E_y=S(B_all|Y=y). The ensemble of Bob states has
Holevo information chi(Y:B_all)=E-sum_y p_y E_y. Bob's outcome Z is a
measurement of this ensemble, hence I(Y:Z)<=chi(Y:B_all). Bob's subsequent
local measurement cannot increase average pure-state entanglement. Thus

 E >= I(Y:Z)+sum_yz p_yz E_yz,
 score_a <= E-I(Y_a:Z_a)+C_D.                         (1)

For Bob's complementary marginal, an isometry
 V=sum_z sqrt(F_z) tensor |z>
preserves entropy. Dephasing its outcome register increases entropy, and
the z output occupies a k_z-dimensional space. Consequently

 S(B_comp_a)<=H(Z_a)+sum_z p_z ln k_(a,z).             (2)

Quantum Shearer gives (r-1)E<=sum_a S(B_comp_a). Sum (1), substitute (2),
and use H(Z)-I(Y:Z)=H(Z|Y):

 E >= Tr(O psi)-r C_D
      -sum_(a,z) p_(a,z) ln k_(a,z)
      -sum_a H(Z_a|Y_a).                             (3)

For a mixed sigma, apply (3) to every pure ensemble. The score and rank
charge are linear. Conditional classical entropy is concave in the joint
law, so the ensemble-averaged penalties are at most those of sigma itself.
Taking the convex roof establishes the same (3) with E_F(sigma) on the left.
All physical outcomes, all cross-task correlations, and all pure ensembles
are included. No joint support, commutator, angle or spectral gap is needed.

2. A STRONGER QUANTUM CONDITIONAL-ENTROPY COMPARATOR

The following is stronger formally and uses only an Alice complementary
POVM Y_a. Let the remaining Bob complement be quantum, and define
 Q_a=S(B_comp_a|Y_a) on the resulting classical-quantum marginal. Then

 E_F(sigma)>=Tr(O sigma)-r C_D-sum_a Q_a.              (4)

Proof for pure inputs: apply the one-chain lemma after Alice's Kraus
outcome only. Its average score remains unchanged, and
 score_a<=E-chi(Y_a:B_all)+C_D.
Use Shearer and data processing chi(Y:B_all)>=chi(Y:B_comp) to obtain
 Tr(O psi)<=E+rC_D+sum_a[S(B_comp_a)-chi(Y_a:B_comp_a)]
          =E+rC_D+sum_a S(B_comp_a|Y_a).
For mixed sigma, conditional cq entropy is concave, giving (4) by the
same convex-roof argument. To recover (3), for each fixed Y=y apply the
Bob isometry/dephasing/rank bound from (2), and average y:
 Q_a<=H(Z_a|Y_a)+sum_z p_z ln k_(a,z). QED.

This formal comparator does not establish a cheap experimental operation:
evaluating arbitrary Q_a may require tomographic information in an
exponentially large complement. It observes finite outputs, not hidden
memory. It is useful to avoid claiming optimality for the accessible
classical label/rank penalty. This is standard Holevo/SSA machinery received
through the one-chain hierarchy; historical novelty remains UNKNOWN.

3. FANO GIVES A ONE-SCALAR INTERFACE, WITH AN UNAVOIDABLE ALPHABET CHARGE

Fix a decoder g from Alice labels to Bob labels. Bob has L>=2 possible
outcomes. Let f=Pr[Z!=g(Y)] and E be that binary error indicator. Since E
is determined by Y,Z,
 H(Z|Y)=H(E|Y)+H(Z|E,Y)
       <=h2(f)+f ln(L-1).                             (5)
On E=0, Z is known; on E=1 it belongs to at most L-1 labels. This proves
the needed Fano inequality directly. It is known information theory.

When only f<=u is certified, use the monotone envelope
 F_L(u)=h2(u)+u ln(L-1) for u<=1-1/L,
 F_L(u)=ln L otherwise.
The threshold is the maximum of the expression. Replacing f by u without
this cap is invalid near one. At L=1 conditional entropy is zero directly.

No full joint-law tomography is required to bound f. For n independent
preparations of the FULL finite output sigma, including arbitrary
correlations within each preparation, record one Bernoulli failure per
trial. Hoeffding gives f<=f_hat+sqrt[ln(1/beta)/(2n)] with confidence
1-beta. Cap at one. With r separately estimated tasks and failure budget
beta, replace beta by beta/r and charge r distinct settings/preparations,
or supply a justified shared sampling scheme. Zero observed failures permit
the exact binomial upper bound f<=1-beta^(1/n). None of this proves that
repeated executions of an unknown recurrent device actually supply iid
whole-state preparations.

Small mismatch alone does NOT imply a bounded conditional entropy when L
grows, even if every Bob measurement effect has rank one. The bound (5)
is sharp at every 0<=f<=1. Set Y=0 surely, and
 Pr(Z=0)=1-f, Pr(Z=z)=f/(L-1), z=1,...,L-1.
Then H(Z|Y)=h2(f)+f ln(L-1) exactly.

This is an actual finite quantum input, not an abstract unphysical law.
Take a product pure state across the Alice/Bob split. Alice's complement is
fixed in |0>; Bob's complement is
 sqrt(1-f)|0>+sum_(z=1)^(L-1) sqrt[f/(L-1)]|z>.
Use computational projective measurements on both complements, and the
decoder g(0)=0. All Bob projectors have rank one and the state has E_F=0.
Embed the complement in enough D-sites; L can be a power of D or unused
basis vectors can be appended. Add fixed product states on all task sites.

Choosing f_L=c/ln L gives f_L->0 while H(Z|Y)->c. Choosing
f_L=1/sqrt(ln L) gives f_L->0 while H(Z|Y) diverges. Thus a rank constraint
does not replace an alphabet/tail/predictability constraint. In the reuse
model a rank-one resolution can have L=D^((r-1)N), so the raw Fano charge
can be order f r N ln D per task. An iid resource limit must retain this
actual charge; calling f vanishing is insufficient.

There is a finite-sample obstruction too. Compare the all-correct law P0
with the preceding tail law Pf. With n independent samples the probability
of the identical all-correct data under Pf is (1-f)^n. If an entropy upper
bound based on that data is below h2(f)+f ln(L-1), it fails under Pf with
at least this probability. Thus an upper confidence claim of failure beta
on the all-correct record must accommodate every f satisfying
(1-f)^n>beta, including f just below 1-beta^(1/n). If L is unbounded there
is no finite uniform entropy upper bound from that record, at any finite n.
If L is specified, controlling the Fano tail contribution to a constant c
requires, in this worst case, n of order at least ln(L) ln(1/beta)/c as
L grows. The exact binomial zero-error upper bound attains that scale.
This is a confidence-validity obstruction, not a claim that every structured
reservoir requires that many samples or that full entropy estimation is
always necessary. The physical product-state realization above applies.

4. KNOWN CROSS-ENTROPY OPERATION AVOIDS A FULL HISTOGRAM WHEN A MODEL IS GIVEN

For any FIXED normalized trial conditional kernel q(z|y)>0,
 H(Z|Y)<=C_q:=E[-ln q(Z|Y)]
by nonnegativity of conditional relative entropy. Therefore (3) implies
a parameterized family of linear quantitative witnesses, with observed
label score ln q(z|y). Optimizing all such kernels recovers H(Z|Y).

If q(z|y)>=exp(-B) for every possible y,z, then the score -ln q lies in
[0,B]. Its mean can be upper bounded from n iid FULL-output preparations
by C_q<=C_hat+B sqrt[ln(1/beta)/(2n)]. This uses a scalar running average,
not storage of the full joint table. Charge the computation, description,
evaluation and calibration of q and all complementary POVM circuits.
If q is learned, freeze it before the certification trials or use independent
training and holdout samples. Adapting q to the same sparse test data can
produce arbitrarily misleading entropy estimates.

Without support restrictions B>=ln L, because each row sums to one.
Tiny tail probabilities cause unbounded surprise, so bounded-range
Hoeffding cannot be used without an explicit floor, truncation remainder
or a proved tail bound. A floor can be implemented as
 q_lambda=(1-lambda) q_model+lambda/L,
which gives B<=ln(L/lambda), with its full resulting cross-entropy charge.
This is conventional predictive log-loss/cross-entropy machinery, not a
new witness principle. Separate graph-support work belongs to its owner.

5. NATIVE NONCOMMUTING COUNTEREXAMPLE: WITNESS GAIN, NOT SUPPORT INFERENCE

The inherited fixed-rank three-qubit example uses M>=2, with Alice/Bob
coefficient X=I_12/2 tensor (M Z+X_pauli)/sqrt[2(M^2+1)]. Its two binary
complement-label tests each fail with f=1/(M^2+1); the third computational
complement test agrees perfectly. Each binary marginal is uniform and
the off-diagonal conditional probability is f, so its actual conditional
entropy is exactly h2(f). The entire penalty is 2h2(f)->0. Meanwhile the
vectorized coefficient remains distance one from the common-commutant
support, and the support-test gap tends to zero. Thus the new theorem
does remove the old support/gap premise for the ENTANGLEMENT WITNESS.
It does not repair support inference or infer complete memory return.
This example is an interface check, not a resource saving or a new search.

6. STRONGEST APPLICABLE CONVENTIONAL COMPARATOR AND NOVELTY STATUS

Known entropic uncertainty witnesses already turn correlations of local
measurement outcomes into E_F lower bounds. The standard route combines
E_F>=max{0,-S(A|B),-S(B|A)} with a quantum-memory uncertainty relation.
Such results may be much tighter on states characterized by their chosen
complementary measurements. They are not identified here as dominating
the explicit MULTISCALE Bell-prefix witness under the SAME restricted
score, POVM label and weighted-rank data. Nor is the present witness
claimed to dominate them. Compare with the maximum of all applicable
certified bounds when those extra measurement data are actually charged.

General quantitative-witness convex duality also contains every fixed-q
linear witness above: optimal lower estimation of E_F from a collection
of observable means dominates any explicit valid lower bound using those
same means. The hard step is evaluating the needed global pure-state
dual optimization. This audit does not claim to solve it. The explicit
hierarchy coefficient/constant and the resulting support-free receiving
operation remain the principal result, not the abstract fact that local
information can quantify entanglement.

For projective measurements, H(Z)+sum_z p_z ln rank F_z is the familiar
coarse-grained/observational entropy upper bound. Its use alone is not a
new entropy principle. The POVM square-root/isometry variant is likewise
proved with elementary standard machinery.
