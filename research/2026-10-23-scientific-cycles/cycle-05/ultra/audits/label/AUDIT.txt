INDEPENDENT AUDIT: CLASSICAL CONDITIONAL-LABEL ENTROPY WITNESS
22 October 2026. Bounded mathematical audit; no numerical search.

DECISION: PASS, CONDITIONAL ON THE INHERITED UNIVERSAL ONE-CHAIN LEMMA.
The candidate holds for every normalized coefficient matrix, with no normality,
commutant, common-basis, support, cross-task commutation, or gap premise.
The inherited one-chain constant is unchanged. Its extension is substantial as
an inequality, but the condition that its measured penalty is small is an
explicit operational resource restriction, not a proof of the unrestricted law.
No historical novelty, efficient implementation, or physical usefulness is
asserted by this audit.

1. DEFINITIONS AND TRANSPOSE CONVENTION

All logs are natural. H = tensor_(a=1)^r H_Ta, each task N D-level sites,
q=D^(rN), m=ln D. Let |X>>=sum_uv X_uv |u>_A|v>_B with Tr X†X=1.
The Bob reduced state is (X†X)^T. Write rho=X†X; transpose leaves every
entropy below unchanged.

For each task a independently choose a finite projective resolution Q_(a,z)
of the task complement C_a, with positive ranks k_(a,z). Set
P_(a,z)=I_Ta tensor Q_(a,z). Alice measures P_(a,y); Bob measures bar(P_(a,z)).
By vectorization, the unnormalized coefficient of outcome (y,z) is
X_(a,yz)=P_(a,y) X P_(a,z), and
q_(a,yz)=||X_(a,yz)||_HS^2.
The Bob outcome probabilities are q_(a,z)=Tr(rho P_(a,z)). Alice's analogous
marginal need not agree. Denote the resulting classical variables Y_a,Z_a.
Define the OBSERVED Bob weighted log-rank cost
ell_a=sum_z q_(a,z) ln k_(a,z).
The prefix erasure E_(a,i)=I_prefix/D^i tensor Tr_prefix is the Hilbert-Schmidt
projection whose squared norm is the physical Bell-prefix probability p_(a,i).
All these definitions permit correlations across every output and task.

2. RECTANGULAR BLOCKS: NO EQUAL-RANK PREMISE

Each X_yz maps H_Ta tensor ran Q_z to H_Ta tensor ran Q_y. Its row and
column residual dimensions can differ. Choose orthonormal bases separately in
ran Q_y and ran Q_z. Embed the residual spaces isometrically into C^k with
k=max(k_y,k_z), leaving H_Ta untouched on each party. The resulting square
coefficient matrix has precisely the same nonzero Schmidt roots, hence the
same pure entanglement. These local residual isometries commute with every
physical task-prefix Bell effect, so all prefix probabilities are unchanged.
The square residual-k version of the received one-chain lemma therefore gives
for each normalized nonzero block Y_yz=X_yz/sqrt(q_yz):

 m sum_i ||E_(a,i)Y_yz||_HS^2 <= E(Y_yz)+C_D.             (1)

No factor ln k or ln(k_y/k_z) occurs in this step. The rank cost enters only
through complementary marginal entropy, below.

Since prefix erasure acts only on T_a, it is bimodular over all P_y and P_z:
E_i(P_y X P_z)=P_y E_i(X)P_z. All distinct rectangular supports (y,z) are
Hilbert-Schmidt orthogonal, even when they share a row or column. Therefore

 p_(a,i)=sum_yz ||E_(a,i)X_yz||_HS^2,
 m sum_i p_(a,i) <= sum_yz q_yz E(Y_yz)+C_D.              (2)

Zero-probability blocks contribute zero and need no normalization.

3. LOCAL MEASUREMENT ENTROPY LOSS: CRITICAL SIGN CHECK

First measure Bob's projective resolution with classical outcome Z. The
conditional global states are pure, with Alice marginals rho_(A|z). The
original Alice marginal rho_A is their average. Consequently

 S(rho_A)-sum_z q_z S(rho_(A|z)) = I(A:Z),               (3)

where the right side is mutual information of the resulting cq state.
Now perform Alice's projective Y measurement on each conditional pure state.
Concavity of the conditional Bob entropy, or pure-state average LOCC
monotonicity, gives

 sum_yz q_yz E(Y_yz) <= sum_z q_z S(rho_(A|z)).           (4)

Applying Alice's measurement channel to the cq state in (3), data processing
says I(A:Z)>=I(Y:Z). Combining (3),(4), with S(rho_A)=S(rho), yields

 sum_yz q_yz E(Y_yz) <= S(rho)-I(Y:Z).                  (5)

The direction is correct: observed classical correlation is a LOWER bound on
the entanglement lost by Bob's local label measurement, so subtracting it
gives an UPPER bound on the surviving average entanglement. Replacing it by
a larger unknown quantum information would require separate justification.
Alice and Bob commute as operations on different parties, so this reasoning
uses exactly the joint law q_yz defined above. It makes no assumption that
a coherent support-enforcing Lueders operation is free.

4. COMPLEMENTARY MARGINAL ALLOCATION

Let rho_C=Tr_Ta rho. Pinching it in Q_z is a mixture of unitaries and cannot
decrease entropy. Its exact direct-sum entropy and block-rank bound give

 S(rho_C) <= S(sum_z Q_z rho_C Q_z)
           = H(Z)+sum_z q_z S(rho_(C|z))
           <= H(Z)+ell_a.                              (6)

Neither rho nor rho_C has to be block diagonal. This is the direction needed.
From (2),(5), I(Y:Z)=H(Z)-H(Z|Y), and (6),

 m sum_i p_(a,i)
 <= S(rho)-S(rho_C)+C_D+ell_a+H(Z_a|Y_a).               (7)

Sum over tasks. Quantum Shearer, proved from SSA and the entropy chain rule,
reads sum_a S(rho_(C_a)) >= (r-1)S(rho). Thus

 S(X†X) >= m sum_(a,i) p_(a,i)-r C_D
            -sum_a ell_a-sum_a H(Z_a|Y_a).              (8)

At r=1 the complement is trivial, ell=H(Z|Y)=0, recovering the inherited
one-chain bound. At perfect matched labels, H(Z|Y)=0 and (8) includes the
old exact commutant theorem. Perfect labels need not be matched: any
deterministic relation Z=f(Y) has zero conditional penalty.

5. ARBITRARY MIXED STATES AND THE CONVEX ROOF

Let sigma be ANY state on the finite external A:B output; no support promise.
Let O=m sum_(a,i) BellPrefix_(a,i) and L=sum_(a,z) ln k_(a,z) P_(a,z).
For any pure-state ensemble sigma=sum_j t_j |X_j>><<X_j|, (8) gives

 sum_j t_j E(X_j)
 >= Tr(O sigma)-r C_D-Tr[(I tensor L^T)sigma]
    -sum_a sum_j t_j H_(q^j_a)(Z_a|Y_a).                (9)

Measured joint laws depend linearly on sigma. Classical conditional entropy
is concave in the full joint law: H(Z|Y)=sum_y q_y H(q_(z|y)); the perspective
of Shannon entropy is concave, including zero rows by continuity. Hence

 sum_j t_j H_(q^j_a)(Z|Y) <= H_(q_a(sigma))(Z|Y).         (10)

Substituting the larger conditional entropy in (9) weakens the lower bound
in the valid direction. Taking the infimum over ALL ensembles gives

 E_F(sigma) >= Tr(O sigma)-r C_D-Tr[(I tensor L^T)sigma]
                 -sum_a H_sigma(Z_a|Y_a).               (11)

This is an observable nonlinear witness valid on the entire output space.
It is not a postselected conditional statement. Outcomes must not be discarded.

6. CONSEQUENCE AND SCOPE CHECK

In the established exact Bell-output/complete-memory-return model, with global
initialization half-trace error epsilon and charged supplied entanglement C_sup,
the pre-existing output lower estimate and LOCC monotonicity yield

 C_sup/r >= m sum_i [1-(i-1)delta]_+ - C_D - m N epsilon
            -(sum_a ell_a + sum_a H_sigma(Z_a|Y_a))/r.   (12)

No support leakage or spectral-gap term is needed. The rank cost, conditional
label entropy, and global initialization error remain distinct. At each fixed
N, with vanishing epsilon and a bounded per-task observed cost ell+H, the
conditional iid rate is m/(2delta)+O(1), as in the previous bounded-rank law.
Unlike that law, this result allows arbitrary noncommuting resolutions and
arbitrary operators outside the common commutant. The ordinary erased-stock
comparator has rank-one blocks and perfectly matched labels, so still fits.

The penalty need not be O(1). Without a bound on the observed conditional
label entropy, its alphabet may be of size D^((r-1)N), and this argument does
not close the unrestricted logarithmic formation gap. Estimating Shannon
entropy over a large alphabet can have substantial sampling cost. A classical
error-probability/Fano upper certificate is possible only after fixing and
charging a decoder and its alphabet; it must not be declared dimension-free.
These output statistics do not certify hidden-memory return or initialization.

7. EXACT STRICT CLASS-INCLUSION EXAMPLE

This example establishes logical strictness only, not operational advantage.
Take r=2,N=1,D=2. For task a use the computational rank-one resolution on its
single-qubit complement. Let F be the Pauli bit-flip matrix and let
X=(F tensor I)/2, normalized in Hilbert-Schmidt norm. Then for task 1 the
complement labels coincide exactly, while for task 2 they are opposite exactly.
Thus both H(Z_a|Y_a)=0 and all ranks are one. However [X,Z_1] != 0, and its
vector lies orthogonally to the old common vectorized commutant (the diagonal
coefficient matrices). In the old language, support leakage is exactly one.
Here the new penalty is zero and (11) remains valid. This verifies that the
new hypothesis cannot be replaced by old matched-label support, even with
zero rank and conditional-entropy charge. The simple example is a known
local relabeling and is not itself a new reservoir or a generic-gap resolution.
