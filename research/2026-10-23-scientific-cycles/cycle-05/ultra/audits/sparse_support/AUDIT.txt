1. GRAPH ENTROPY THEOREM

For a fixed task the row-column blocks P_y X P_z remain Hilbert-Schmidt
orthogonal after every task-prefix erasure: E_i is a bimodule map over
operators on the complementary subsystem. Thus their squared norms add.
The existing one-chain inequality applies to each normalized rectangle.
If k_y != k_z, use separate row and column residual isometries into a common
space of dimension max(k_y,k_z). They act only on the residual subsystem,
so every prefix partial trace and its Hilbert-Schmidt norm are preserved;
the nonzero singular values are preserved as well. This verifies equation
(3) without adding a rank penalty at this step.

Measuring Alice's row label leaves Bob's average state unchanged. Therefore
S(rho_B) = I(Y:B) + sum_y q_y S(rho_B^y) is exactly the cq mutual-information
identity. Bob's complementary label measurement gives
I(Y:B) >= I(Y:Z). Conditional on y, Bob's local measurement has average pure
entanglement no larger than the preceding pure state's entanglement, by
concavity of the opposite marginal's entropy. These two facts give (4).

Pinching Bob's complementary marginal by its label resolution increases or
preserves entropy. Its pinched block entropies are bounded by ln k_z, giving
(5). For q_y>0, the neighbor distribution k_z / b_y is normalized on the
allowed row. Nonnegativity of D(q_(.|y)||k_./b_y) gives (6) with the stated
weighted log neighbor dimension. Combining (4)--(6) has the correct signs:

 -H(Z)+H(Z|Y) <= -S(rho_comp)+sum_z q_z ln k_z+H(Z|Y)
                <= -S(rho_comp)+sum_y q_y ln b_y.

Quantum Shearer on Bob's r task factors then gives the claimed coefficient
one in front of S(rho_B), after summing the r one-task inequalities. It does
not require commuting label resolutions across tasks or nonnegative quantum
conditional entropies. The Alice row charge is linear in XX*, as claimed.

Transpose convention: with |X>> = sum_ij X_ij |i>_A |j>_B,
rho_B = (X*X)^T. Bob measures conjugate(Q_z), not Q_z, in the chosen
computational convention. The proof's entropy and probability formulas are
unchanged under this transpose/conjugation. Section 3's explicit support
effect already states this correctly. It is useful to keep that convention
explicit whenever applying equations (4)--(5) to complex resolutions.

Every vector in an ensemble of a state supported in W_Gamma lies in that
linear support: its squared projection onto the orthogonal complement has
nonnegative ensemble average zero. Hence averaging the pure inequality and
then taking the convex roof gives (2) for E_F, without selecting a favorable
decomposition.

The inherited robust proof has the correct constants here: 0<=O<=ln q I,
0<=L_Gamma<=Cmax I, so A_Gamma has oscillation at most ln q+Cmax. Adding
pure marginal entropy continuity contributes another ln q and the stated
binary-entropy envelope. The global projection is a proof device only.
Commuting local label resolutions give the exact intersection-projector
EFFECT by destructive local joint-label comparison. No coherent free
projection or free preparation follows. For noncommuting tests the gap
qualification remains essential; the sparse graph does not remove it.

2. LONG-CYCLE OLD-RANK OBSTRUCTION

For r>=2 and Q=D^N>=3, the simultaneous increment F has no fixed basis
vector, and every complementary simultaneous increment F_(-a) orbit has
length exactly Q. The graph includes all diagonal coefficients and F times
all diagonal coefficients. It excludes F* since -1 mod Q is distinct from
0 and +1 mod Q on every complementary task. Thus the proposed support is
not a commutant algebra, which is necessarily *-closed.

Suppose an old complementary projector P=I_task tensor Q_0 commutes with
the entire new support. Commutation with every computational diagonal
matrix forces P diagonal. Its diagonal indicator depends only on the
complement, and commutation with F makes it constant on F_(-a) orbits.
Every nonzero complementary projector therefore has rank >=Q. For every
orthogonal resolution whose commutant contains the entire new support, the
weighted log rank is consequently >=ln Q per task. This is not merely a
maximum-rank statement: every occupied nonzero block has that minimum rank.
The new graph charge is ln 2 per task, independent of N.

The stronger single-operator version is correct. Let X=F diag(c), with
all c_j>0 and all c_j^2 distinct. Any selfadjoint projector commuting with
X commutes with X* as well, hence with X*X=diag(c_j^2), and is diagonal.
Since diag(c) is invertible, commutation with X now implies commutation
with F. The same complementary orbit-rank argument applies. Its nonnormality
is genuine because XX*=F diag(c_j^2) F* differs from X*X when the weights
vary along a nontrivial F orbit. Distinct weights ensure this condition.

This is a separation from the stated old block-commutant resource class,
not from all possible entropy witnesses or unrestricted entanglement
preparation methods. The proof appropriately makes that distinction.

3. EXACT STOCK RECEIVING IDENTITY

Here is a direct normalization check, independent of an intuitive carry
description. Write L=D^(N-i), T=D^(N-k). For fixed tail string t, use the
normalized coefficient

 X_(k,t) = D^(-k/2) diag(1_{j mod T=t}).

Its average over t with weight 1/T is exactly sigma_k. The shifted
coefficient is F X_(k,t). A matrix entry with column suffix v in [0,L-1]
survives Tr_prefix iff v<L-1. The case v=L-1 carries into the nonempty
prefix, shifts its value by one modulo D^i, and has zero diagonal prefix
trace. This also covers i=N, when L=1 and every entry fails.

If i<=k, for each compatible suffix there are D^i equal prefix amplitudes.
There are D^(k-i) compatible suffixes before removing the wrap edge. The
fixed-tail squared erasure norm is therefore

 ||E_i(F X_(k,t))||^2
   = 1 - D^(i-k) 1_{t=T-1}.

This formula includes k=N, when T=1. Averaging over t gives
1-D^(i-N)=p_i [1-D^(-(N-i))], since p_i=1 in this case.

If i>k, the fixed tail fixes a unique remaining suffix. There are D^k equal
prefix amplitudes, so

 ||E_i(F X_(k,t))||^2
   = D^(k-i) [1-1_{t mod L=L-1}].

The suffix t mod L is uniform under the tail average. Hence its averaged
value is D^(k-i)(1-1/L), exactly (10). These two cases prove the full claimed
identity, including k=0, k=N and i=N. They also prove the mixture extension
by linearity. Taskwise correlated inventory causes no problem because each
Bell-prefix observable depends only on the relevant task marginal.

Summing the losses uses only p_i<=1 and the geometric series, giving the
strict bound in (11). Applying F is an invertible local unitary, so it
preserves entanglement preparation costs and the full retained state up to
that unitary. It changes the tested Bell-prefix scores and is not a new
exact-output R1 operation; the proof says this expressly.

Minor exposition suggestion, not a mathematical correction: replace the
phrase 'all D^k available Bell amplitudes survive in a good term' by the
two-case partial-trace count above if an entirely explicit normalization
proof is desired. For i<=k each fixed suffix has D^i prefix amplitudes;
the other Bell amplitudes live on different suffixes.

4. SCOPE / DECISION

The graph theorem strictly enlarges the previously proved output-support
resource class while keeping a linear observable charge, and the long-cycle
family proves that bounded graph charge cannot generally be converted to
bounded old complementary rank. This is a consequential receiving obstruction
to taking algebra closure. The actual stock map pays a uniformly bounded
prefix-score loss and preserves preparation entanglement; it does not
improve on ordinary stock or solve unrestricted formation's logarithmic gap.
Finite N epsilon and N sqrt(eta) charges, support-test calibration, copy
counts, actual complete-memory return, and deterministic versus expected
supply must retain their previous separate definitions. No stronger claim
is established by this audit. Historical novelty and total discovery cost
remain UNKNOWN; replay speed is not discovery cost.
