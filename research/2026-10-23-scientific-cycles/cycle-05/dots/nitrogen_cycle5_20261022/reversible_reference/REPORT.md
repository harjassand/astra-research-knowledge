# Reversible state-reference availability decision

## Decision

**Do not promote the saved forward N2/O reaction fit into an identified reversible five-species mechanism or a net-NEW-NO operation. Park that construction, not the nitrogen-fixation mission.** The bounded free-primary-source check did not retrieve the source-matched equilibrium weights, common-level mapping and complete numerical channel set needed for a reproducible 1700–2700 K reference. This is an availability/identifiability result for the inspected sources, not a claim that reversible models do not exist or that all conceivable free sources have been exhausted.

A particularly relevant 2025 primary paper has now been recovered in full. It confirms that the authors have implemented reversible exchange together with VT/VV and dissociation/recombination, while explicitly saying its reusable data are not publicly available at publication. The paper's numerical operation is at 300–500 K, not the proposed 1700–2700 K window. Its existence therefore cannot certify the proposed high-temperature construction.

No coefficient arithmetic, first-event branching contraction, flow/ODE trajectory, expenditure, correspondence, or source-code execution was performed in this assessment.

## New free primary evidence

### 2025 implemented model, but not a freely reproducible complete reference

I. Armenise, V. Laporta and F. Esposito, “State-to-state modeling of NO production by nanosecond electrical discharge in a nitrogen–oxygen mixture at atmospheric pressure,” Plasma Sources Science and Technology 34 (2025) 085012, DOI https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6595/adf996.

The free publisher PDF is deposited at the German National Library of Science and Technology: https://oa.tib.eu/renate/server/api/core/bitstreams/c5120117-3c7d-4673-b012-f9bb7d212711/content . It is saved here as `2025_nanosecond.pdf` with extracted text. Its CC BY 4.0 attribution is on printed page 1. The PDF has a repository/publisher cover before the numbered article.

Printed pages 2–3 and Table 1 enumerate reversible N+N2 and O+O2 VT/dissociation, molecular self VT/VV, both state-to-state Zeldovich exchanges, O+N2 dissociation, and N+O2 VT/dissociation. The sources are heterogeneous: QCT, semiclassical molecular VT, and experimental nitrogen VV. Molecular dissociation is also represented through a ladder-climbing pseudolevel.

Table 1 contains no heavy-particle NO VT/dissociation, O+N2 VT, or cross-molecule N2/O2/NO VV set. Their omission in this particular model does not prove absence elsewhere or quantitatively establish their importance here. It does rule out treating this table as an explicitly complete heavy-particle inventory without further justification.

Printed page 16 says reusable data were not published and are obtainable by author request. No request was sent. The paper supplies no explicit state-equilibrium partition/degeneracy convention resolving our inverse-rate problem. Model existence is not a license to reconstruct unspecified inputs by guessing.

### 2023 primary continuation explains what its inverse means

I. Armenise, “Low temperature state-to-state vibrational kinetics of O+N2(v) and N+O2(v) collisions,” Chemical Physics 571 (2023) 111937, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chemphys.2023.111937 . Official indexed publisher text and CNR full-text passages were retrieved; direct PDF retrieval returned HTTP 403, so no complete local PDF is claimed.

Its fitted range is 300–1000 K. Section 2.1 lists 61 N2, 44 O2 and 48 NO vibrational levels and points to earlier energy tables. Its molecular-collider dissociation uses pseudolevel extrapolation, identified by the author as a strong approximation. Its state-selected reverse uses a Boltzmann-weighted NO distribution at gas temperature; state-to-state inverses themselves are obtained by detailed balance. That is a declared physical closure, not an independent reverse-QCT table. The accessible passages do not specify the full state-weight convention. The 61-level N2 claim also cannot silently replace the verified 60-state rate loop in the saved 2017 source.

### 2021 high-temperature interpolation: the correct unresolved target

I. Armenise and F. Esposito, “N+O2(v) collisions: reactive, inelastic and dissociation rates for state-to-state vibrational kinetic models,” Chemical Physics 551 (2021) 111325, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chemphys.2021.111325 . Official CNR abstract: https://www.istp.cnr.it/research-product/no2v-collisions-reactive-inelastic-and-dissociation-rates-for-state-to-state-vibrational-kinetic-models-2/ .

This is the directly relevant 1000–20000 K interpolation and five-species implementation. The abstract confirms the scope, but the complete coefficient/energy/reverse-convention package was not recovered in this finite pass. Direct publisher retrieval was blocked. Thus we have an exact source to preserve, not a successful numerical input acquisition. The already verified duplicated 2021 ACS dissociation supplement does not substitute for it.

### 2015 common-ladder reconciliation is a real prerequisite, not cosmetic normalization

Armenise and Esposito, Chemical Physics 446 (2015) 30–46, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chemphys.2014.11.004 ; official repository record https://iris.cnr.it/handle/20.500.14243/275207 . Its primary abstract identifies mismatched vibrational ladders from different collision potentials, especially high states, and rescales them onto common molecular ladders. The repository marks the PDF for authorized users only. That restricted attachment was not downloaded.

## Exact missing information

The decisive missing object is a **source-matched state-equilibrium and rate package**, not an extra scalar equilibrium constant:

1. The complete 2021 Chemical Physics interpolation coefficients and common N2/O2/NO level tables, including its mapping from the 2017 forward source and treatment of the highest bound/quasibound/pseudolevel states.
2. Explicit state equilibrium density ratios used by that implementation: electronic/fine-structure weights, nuclear-spin/symmetry conventions, level-dependent rotational partition sums, translation/mass factors, dissociation energy zeros and truncation. These determine the reverse branching. A bulk NASA Kc plus arbitrary finite Boltzmann populations is a different, synthetic mechanism.
3. Collider-specific state-resolved dissociation/recombination data with units, and the declared VT/VV set at the target temperatures, including either the omitted NO/cross channels or defensible negligibility bounds. The 2025 table shows that a publication's use of the phrase state-to-state does not itself establish all-channel coverage.
4. For the original six-species problem, NO2-associated kinetics/partition remains an additional requirement. This task's five-species question must not obscure that original scope gap.

A detailed-balance inverse is not inherently arbitrary. It is physically legitimate when its state equilibrium measures match the forward rate averaging and species model. The failure here is that those measures and mappings have not been identified from the recovered files. Reusing an unrelated bulk thermochemistry or fitting reverse weights until equilibrium looks right would resolve an algebraic inconsistency while leaving physical identification unresolved.

## What remains scientifically usable

The existing forward-event mode-selectivity finding survives as a source-conditional observation. It is not a process operation or net production certificate. Do not elevate another favorable forward contraction into the next mission milestone.

A possible *diagnostic*, preserved but not executed, is the same-source forward branch ratio B_v = sum_w k_exchange(v,w) / [sum_w k_exchange(v,w) + k_O-collider-dissociation(v)]. It would test whether a selected high-v population tends to react to NO or dissociate in its first O-driven chemical encounter. It needs no reverse convention. However, it excludes VT/VV, other colliders, repeated encounters, reverse NO loss, tail preparation and reset; it cannot establish a physical NEW-NO operation. The parent explicitly elected not to pursue this diagnostic now.

The stronger admissible path is a physical quench-admission observation: establish a measured or validated gas cooling history and transport loss budget for wall cooling or expansion, then compare recovered NEW-NO inventory and complete resource/reset accounting against an ordinary reference. This avoids pretending that a one-channel enhancement supplies a plant gain. The independent equipment work owns that decision; this source check does not assert that a suitable device has been admitted.

A relevant primary mixed-model comparator is Shen et al., “Pinpointing energy transfer mechanisms in the quenching process of microwave air plasma,” J. Phys. D 58 (2025) 275203, https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6463/ade3e6 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.20952 ; institutional record https://research.tue.nl/en/publications/pinpointing-energy-transfer-mechanisms-in-the-quenching-process-o/ . It explicitly uses wall-forced cooling and generalized Fridman–Macheret rates where MD rates are absent. Those declared approximations are useful comparison material, not a recovered fully state-resolved reversible reference or experimental equipment qualification. Only the abstract/record was inspected in this pass.

## Finite stopping rule and access record

The high-temperature interpolation target, its common-ladder prerequisite, its low-temperature continuation and its 2025 implemented descendant were checked. The descendant's explicit data-unavailability statement and the unrecovered weights/mapping are sufficient to withhold source identification now. Searches were stopped rather than converted into an unbounded review. Public indexed source text was used; no paywall, CAPTCHA or access restriction was bypassed. The CNR/Elsevier HTTP failures are recorded as retrieval failures, not proof that the underlying publications lack the needed formulas. No author contact is authorized or initiated.
