ASTRA CYCLE5 — INFORMATION EXPERIMENT AND SCIENTIFIC DECISION
22 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane

DECISION
Reject adoption of the proposed threshold-observation rule. The stronger ordinary
moment control wins the stipulated worst-case information comparison. Preserve the
finite-table inference operation and its limitations; no receiving-owner use,
physical improvement or recursive intelligence improvement has occurred.

WHY THIS TEST
Several owner advances recovered joint structure that earlier summaries omitted.
That observation is too broad to prescribe a research strategy. We tested one
concrete implication: whether selecting rare-state population thresholds from
actual reaction coefficients beats conventional energy-moment refinement under
the same stated abstract observation budget. This tests information, not agents.

CONTRACT AND DESIGN
Use the unchanged Dots transcription of the 2017 N2(v)+O -> NO(w)+N QCT fit,
60 N2 states and 48 NO states. Both the literal source-index convention and the
explicit corrected-index interpretation remain separate. Population p is any
nonnegative normalized vector with exact mean excitation
0.06417802570979354 eV. Three additional observations lie in [0,1], each with
stipulated deterministic absolute error epsilon. Targets are the forward rate R
and unnormalized chemical mode-excitation source M. Neither is a permitted oracle
observation. M is not bath heat, total reaction heat, retained yield or net NO.

The objective is the largest target difference between two populations satisfying
the exact constraints and observation differences <=2 epsilon. Half this diameter
is the exact worst-case deterministic inference error over the observation model.
The proof uses an indistinguishable pair lower bound and midpoint of each feasible
output interval for the upper. This is established optimal-recovery reasoning.

Preregistered training: 1700, 2300, 2700 K, corrected-index source, both outputs,
epsilon=1e-6. Held-out evaluations: 1500, 1900, 2100, 2500, 3000 K, both source
variants, epsilon=0,1e-8,1e-6,1e-4. These are same-source robustness checks, not
independent empirical validation or a transfer between investigations.

OUTCOME
At epsilon=1e-6, the worst remaining diameter, normalized to the matching
mean-only diameter, is:
  selected thresholds 22,38,39             0.5650260179
  bounded Chebyshev degrees 2,3,4          0.5654498739
  ordinary power degrees 2,3,4             0.5727098008
  stronger ordinary power degrees 7,10,12  0.2509488398
  complete population, 59 coordinates     0.0035781923
These are uncertainty widths, not relative prediction errors.

The stronger ordinary control was added AFTER the primary run and criticism that
the initial conventional moments did not exploit the same training opportunity.
It exhaustively considered 165 triples from degrees 2..12, using 990 training LPs
versus 1044 threshold-selection LPs. It won on all reported worst-case held-out
error levels. The treatment was not retuned. This is an openly post-primary
falsification, not preregistered confirmation of an advantage.

Equal scalar count is not equal experimental cost. One resolved state observation
may compute all these features. Assigning independent coordinate error to a new
basis changes the observation model; noiseless moment/Chebyshev equivalence is
preserved. The experiment does not recommend a new sensor or measurement scheme.

NARROWER NEW RESULT
The primary script omitted a planned sign check. The independent review caught
this omission and completed the check separately. Since the owner mean is below
the first excitation, the admissible simplex slice has exactly the 59 extreme
populations supported on {0,j}. For the frozen evaluated coefficient vectors at
the native bath temperature 1686.4595074210936 K, M is negative for EVERY such
population under both source conventions:
the maximum is -1.2402959761400515e-21 eV cm^3/s, while the minimum is about
-1.72e-11. Thus the sign can be fixed when the magnitude is poorly identified.
At 3000 K, exact rational equal-mean populations on {0,1} and {0,21} give the
same three selected threshold observations but opposite fitted M signs.

The independent review supplies exact rational affine majorants for the FROZEN
computed binary64 M vectors. This certifies the finite linear claim relative to
those numbers; it does not interval-certify the exponential QCT fits, measured
rate uncertainty, temperature interpolation or real chemistry. All populations
pass the owner's necessary scalar entropy screen at the one native caloric state
because its population-independent entropy margin is 771.446269464257 J/kg/K.
Neither entropy feasibility nor a simplex extremum establishes kinetic reachability.

WHY THIS DOES NOT CLOSE DOTS' SCIENCE
Even exact N2/NO populations and forward coefficients do not supply the compatible
state statistical weights, reverse exchange, dissociation/recombination and
population-changing channels needed to identify a reversible kinetic operation.
Detailed balance defines reverse rates only after those premises are specified.
The algebraic note proves under-specification of the supplied interface, not
physical freedom to choose arbitrary nitrogen rates. No trajectory was run.

VERIFICATION AND COST
Primary: 1630 LPs; 3.870724306 measured CPU s; 3.842317 wall s; 67872 KiB RSS.
Strong control: 1096 LPs; 2.442464055 measured CPU s; 2.408240 wall s; 64424 KiB RSS.
The latter body timer excludes setup. Both used 120 s soft CPU and 1 GiB
address-space caps. Full research, inference, preparation and integration costs
are unknown; these runtimes are not all-in productivity estimates.
Independent review checked all 560 primary and 80 stronger-control stored primal
pairs, and rebuilt four representative optima in original eV coordinates with
HiGHS IPM. It checked exact population constraints, fixed-table sign certificates
and dual feasibility. A redundant solver-marginal diagnostic failed at 1.74294e-8;
the saved explanation separates that failure from reconstructed dual feasibility
(about 1.53e-16) and objective gap (about 2.17e-19). Failed code is retained.

ADOPTION BOUNDARY
No new generic reasoning rule, observation-design layer, agent allocation or
physical closure is justified by this experiment. The useful surviving operation
asks what conclusion is identified over an admitted class before paying to infer
more of the state. Its broader research benefit still needs independent receiving
use. This capsule offers that evidence without assigning Dots another audit.
