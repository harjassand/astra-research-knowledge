## 1. Pro1: a real separation, with extreme asymptotic constants

### Scientific change

The earlier source established the horizon-optimized single-site iid coherent-information limit of exactly 3/2 for fixed beta<=1/8. The new theorem keeps the same device `V=sqrt(1-beta^2) U+i beta I`, full infinite bath and actual initializer, but uses a temporally correlated two-call average. For each fixed 0<beta<1 there is one deterministic unassisted code sequence, independent of the fixed normal initializer, with pointwise vanishing full-diamond error and rate strictly above `3/2+beta^4/1024`. At the original beta=1/512 the guaranteed gain exceeds 2^-46; the explicit entropy rate is about 1.50000000000001817 qubits/call. [P1: `CORRELATED_CAPACITY_GAIN.tex`, §§1–7; `RESULTS.txt`.]

This is not a small numerical improvement being promoted into a capacity theorem. Its mathematical consequence is qualitative: no nonzero small-angle interval has a 3/2 capacity plateau in this model. Conversely, the tiny gain and enormous constants make it inappropriate to claim a useful communication advantage. The tail parameter is approximately 3.55e-15 and the exponential-moment constant approximately 4.95e27 at the original angle. No code compiler or useful finite blocklength is supplied. [P1: main §9; `RESOURCE_RECORD.json`.]

`d=alpha^2(4 delta+2 beta^2 c)`.

An analytic entropy bound proves excess input entropy of order beta^4. The actual root term is finite and additive in the Lyapunov inequality; it is never deleted. ### Still open: are these discoveries improving the discovery system itself?

The observed dependency chain is longer, but all evidence is compatible with capable researchers accumulating results at an unchanged rate. No matched counterfactual establishes that the same owners could not have found these results unaided. There is no cost-complete cross-domain transfer trial, no measured change in the rate of hard open-problem resolution, and no general method whose improved version has enabled a further improvement of that method. The correct claim is stronger cumulative mathematics, not recursive intelligence growth.

### Important selection risk

The branch frontier moves inward toward progressively sharper statements in a specialised model. Pro1's plateau resolution is a qualitative advance; Pro3's coefficient closes a meaningful minimax question; Pro2's stable-query characterization is structural. But a sequence of refinements may eventually become less consequential even as technical difficulty rises. The evidence does not warrant centrally terminating these programmes. It does warrant periodic owner-led articulation of what structural question a new coefficient, longer block or coloring would settle and why that question matters beyond the preceding lemma.

## 7. Consequentiality: structural progress versus local saturation

### Pro1

The strict numerical gain is extraordinarily small, but the theorem's qualitative statement is not vacuous: the optimized iid information rate is strictly below an achievable unassisted rate for the same fixed memory channel. The current scientific claim should be framed as a separation between temporal independence and controlled correlation, not as useful improved communication. Its root-retaining infinite-memory model is specialised, so broader foundational significance depends on what it reveals about a wider class of quantum memories.

The owner's small-angle question can test a substantial principle: is the fourth-order gain intrinsic to all fixed finite temporal blocks, or an artefact of the pair representation? A further issue is the order of limits: even a theorem for every fixed block need not cover block lengths growing as beta tends to zero. An eventual general criterion connecting temporal coherence, memory tightness and achievable information would be stronger scientific capital than optimizing this one pair's constant. These are implications and questions arising from the owner's chosen direction, not replacement assignments.

The strongest existing applicable comparator inside the declared model is the optimized all-one-site-density iid theorem, which the construction genuinely exceeds. The inherited full capacity upper remains far above the achieved rate. Outside this model, known perfect-memory coding and Lyapunov arguments already supply much of the mechanism; the new material is the exact admitted coherent operation and its premise verification. Historical originality and external interest cannot be inferred from internal depth.

### Pro2

The stability equivalence is the strongest broadly reusable content: global noise amplification is controlled by bichromatic component size, not merely local rank, acyclicity or exact identifiability. This can be relevant to sparse derivative recovery when a directional action is expensive. The present application record does not show that relevance has delivered a useful speedup; ordinary sparse-Hessian methods are strong comparators and source formation/action costs remain unmeasured.

The next four-versus-five question is sharper than its likely immediate operational consequence. On the banded class, five actions give constant1 while four give about2.2883; neither uniformly dominates when measurement precision and action acquisition both count. A query saving can be valuable, but the scientific argument must explain the acquisition/accuracy regime. A full result across all degree-four partial two-trees could reveal a structural separator-composition law or a separation between coordinate and arbitrary real designs. Such a principle would have broader significance than another periodic seed alone.

The prior general treewidth exact/stable separation is broader than this narrow frontier. Continuing here is defensible as a stress test of the general relationship between identification, stability, degree and geometry. It should not be described as approaching a transformative computational capability without a receiving scientific problem or new general characterization.

### Pro3

Calling the whole result a bespoke numerical constant would be inaccurate. The theorem ranges over every admitted finite positive target/proposal pair with the KL bound and every fixed symmetric target-independent local proposal, and determines an explicit function of K,alpha,d. It is a broad exact-real robustness result for a specified established hybrid algorithm.

The potentially important principle is that adding legitimate target-reversible local moves can worsen proposal-start worst-case robustness under only forward-KL information, even though a fixed fraction of independence-MH moves remains. The actual local mechanism can push proposal mass into exceptionally persistent high-weight regions. Ordinary spectral intuition or an isolated norm witness does not settle that operational effect. However, the earlier path lower had already established a penalty at the example parameters. This cycle closes its exact phase boundary; it does not newly invent that whole qualitative phenomenon.

The critical prefactor is a scientifically natural continuation, but the danger of local saturation is real. A solution is more consequential if it reveals a general resource-allocation law—how one KL budget jointly limits entrance mass, transport and retention—or exposes a qualitatively new heterogeneous mechanism. Merely adding a decimal coefficient or another plot would be weaker. The ordinary IMH comparator remains better in this worst-case criterion, and a practical improvement would require additional structure, a different risk/cost criterion or a different operation; none is supplied in this checkpoint.
