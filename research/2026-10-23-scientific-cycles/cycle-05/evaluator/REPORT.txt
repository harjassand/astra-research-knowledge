EXECUTIVE DECISION

Preserve the restored autonomous architecture. The latest returns establish more
than a growing collection of disconnected results: inherited proofs now support
new operational separations and a completed sharp theorem. They do not establish
a growing rate of discovery, a general improvement in reasoning, or a path already
validated for civilization-changing technology. My decision is to continue the
scientific owners' substantial work while changing what the evaluator attempts
to improve and what it counts as success.

The strongest new system-level evidence is Pro3's actual acceptance and use of the
incoming operator upper bound in a matching branching theorem. That closes the
question left open in Cycle4. Pro1 independently turns inherited infinite-memory
compression mathematics into a strict communication-capacity separation. These
are genuine cumulative scientific consequences. They do not prove the numerical
instrument, more agents, or a prescribed reasoning strategy caused the progress.

I investigated a deeper hypothesis: Astra might gain much more by discovering
which scientific quantities preserve the mechanism than by extending computation
inside a lossy representation. The historical pattern supports investigating it.
A new prospective finite-table experiment DID NOT support my proposed concrete
shortcut. Three selected high-energy population thresholds barely improved on a
standard polynomial description; an equally informed optimized ordinary moment
control was substantially better. We should preserve that failure rather than
install a new reasoning ritual on the basis of attractive examples.

An independent follow-up uncovered a narrower useful distinction: for the frozen
evaluated coefficient vectors at the native source state, the SIGN of one forward
chemical mode-energy contribution is fixed over all allowed populations, despite
enormous uncertainty in its magnitude.
At another temperature, identical selected observations allow opposite signs.
What must be learned depends on the actual question. None of these table results
establishes microscopic reverse kinetics, reachable dynamics or a useful process.

The next cycle should protect autonomous scientific invention, favor deeper
principles over repeated local refinements, and require a new reusable method to
earn its status through an independent scientific consequence. No branch is
reassigned, no retired physical campaign is reopened, and no new infrastructure,
purchase, outreach or hardware allocation is proposed.

3. COMPETING EXPLANATIONS

The evidence now contradicts a blanket claim that Astra only accumulates rigorous
exclusions. Two previously open mathematical questions closed, and Pro2/Ultra
expanded genuine constructive/theorem classes. It also contradicts a blanket
claim that strong results are merely prompt-compliance: the productive moves
were substantive scientific inventions, often beyond the initial intermediate.

There is a recurring pattern of lost joint structure: equal input entropy with
different memory drift; equal query count with different stability; a norm-test
vector versus reachable retained mass; mean mode energy versus selective reaction
populations; a fixed support label versus the actual erasure operation. This is
an observation across the histories, not yet a causal strategy. It could be an
overbroad retrospective description of virtually any good science. The new test
below was chosen to expose rather than protect that weakness.

More subagents or elaborate methods are not uniformly better. Distinct reviewers
found specific algebra/interface errors; generic redundant review would not follow
from that. Both prior instrument/control arms obtained the useful upper; the tool
was not uniquely responsible. Ordinary constructions beat elaborate ones in Pro2
and in the new meta experiment. Full inference, opportunity and integration costs
remain unknown, preventing productivity rankings by measured script runtimes.

The biggest unresolved strategic weakness is consequence: Astra has a functioning
way to deepen selected models, but no demonstrated mechanism ensuring the resulting
advances progressively unlock more important independent problems. A numerical
benchmark, an internally correct theorem and a physical capability have different
obligations. Deeper proof is valuable in its own right; it need not be sold as
technology. Equally, local theorem refinement should not automatically inherit the
ultimate ambition's significance. Owners should retain discretion to pursue a
broader structural question when it offers more than another constant.

The primary script omitted its planned sign check; independent review identified
and completed it separately. Since the native mean lies below the first excitation,
every admissible population is a convex combination of59 distributions supported
on{0,j}. This gives an exhaustive finite-table sign test without estimating the
full population. For the frozen evaluated coefficient vectors at
bath1686.4595074210936K, both source variants give
 max_p M(p)=-1.24029597614e-21 eV cm^3/s,
while the minimum is about-1.72e-11. Within this fitted table the sign is fixed,
even though magnitude is uncertain by many orders. The stated scalar entropy
screen also covers every population at this one caloric state; an independent
algebraic calculation gives a771.446J/kg/K baseline entropy margin before adding
nonnegative population entropy. Neither fact proves reachable dynamics.

At3000K, exact equal-mean populations on{0,1} and{0,21} have identical observations
for all three selected thresholds, yet opposite modeled M signs. Thus that
observation set cannot answer even the sign question throughout the test family.
The rational population equalities and affine majorant certificates are exact
for the frozen binary64 coefficient vectors. They do not outward-round or certify
the underlying exponential fit evaluation or physical rate uncertainty. No
temperature-continuum or complete thermal-cycle claim follows.

The general lesson is useful but scoped: choose the scientific conclusion before
deciding what must be reconstructed. A sign can be identifiable when a value is
not. Conversely, a beautiful static representation can leave the important
dynamic question untouched. Even exact N2/NO populations would not identify the
missing reverse rates and reversible state-changing timescales. Detailed balance
only fixes reverse rates after the appropriate state statistical weights and
conventions are supplied. More inference on the same underspecified interface
cannot uniquely select the real microscopic kinetics. The independent note
proves this algebraic under-specification, not arbitrary freedom of real nitrogen.

6. INTELLIGENCE AMPLIFICATION: WHAT EXISTS, WHAT IS MISSING

There is now observed scientific accumulation with a stronger downstream result:
native fixed-screen theory -> incoming improved upper -> owner branching lower
plus upper -> exact first-order law -> new critical-window question. Pro1 has an
independent chain from joint compression to an actual correlated-input code.
These are legitimate instances of scientific capital making a later theorem
possible in the delivered argument. They are not controlled estimates of how
long the owner would have taken without that capital, and they do not show that
the discovery method itself improved across generations.

Recursive scientific intelligence requires a second loop: a discovered method
must improve the system's ability to discover further methods or consequential
results on new problems, with development/verification/integration costs counted.
Neither a growing archive nor repeated same-domain theorem dependencies alone
establishes that. There is no established exponential growth rate here.

The deepest feasible mechanism remains open: learn scientific representations,
model classes or constructive operators whose semantics are selected by externally
checkable consequences, rather than by narrative plausibility. DiscoRL provides
an external example of learning what to predict and how to update, then evaluating
on unseen environments. Co-Scientist's strongest evidence includes independent
laboratory validation of hypotheses. Those are relevant existence examples, not
evidence that their architectures or reported scale can be reproduced by Astra's
current branch allocation. The model-learning/data/validation loop is the important
difference from simply adding debate. [L1,L2]

We have not yet built that loop for general science. This round's actual experiment
rejects one tempting representation rule and preserves a conditional inference
tool; no independent owner receiving use has yet occurred. The positive meta
finding is improved discrimination between an information problem, a derivation
problem, and an implementation/physical-admission problem. Its effectiveness as
a general research policy remains to be tested.

7. ALTERNATIVES AND THE NEXT STRATEGIC DIRECTION

Concentrating everyone on a named grand challenge would increase continuity but
currently discard productive autonomous programmes without a demonstrated entry
mechanism or decisive feedback. The aspiration alone does not justify that shift.
Longer reasoning or more agents may help a particular proof but cannot identify
missing physical rates or establish a receiving map that is mathematically false.
Mandatory argument recipes have not earned adoption; neither has our new threshold
rule. Rebuilding retrieval/integration infrastructure is not justified by these
scientific bottlenecks. Existing source routes and Max's role are sufficient for
the next cycle, with one correction of dated scientific status.

The serious alternative with the highest potential is to make the system capable
of changing its scientific abstractions and operations through checked feedback,
while sustaining work long enough for difficult constructions to mature. That
includes foundational mathematical methods, actual physical evidence and usable
computational operations. It cannot be reduced to one mandatory procedure. Its
next decisive evidence is an operation learned in one investigation that unlocks
a consequential independent question, with a comparator that already has access
to the same relevant conventional mathematics/data. The opportunity should come
from an owned scientific obstruction, not an empty branch slot or a new leaderboard.

Adopt now: preserve long-term autonomous ownership; use subagents for independent
constructions or distinct load-bearing challenges; retain original counterexamples
alongside corrected methods; update actual receiving consequences; permit a cheaper
conclusion when it settles the real question. Treat all as evidence-sensitive
working choices, not a proven optimal architecture.
Do not adopt: universal threshold/rare-state measurement rules, mandated fresh
resets, increased agent quotas, global prompt tournaments, repeated settled checks,
or claims of practical benefit from a favorable component or asymptotic coefficient.

Revision triggers: reconsider continuation if owner work repeatedly yields only
local refinements with no broader theorem class or independent use; reconsider
the representation direction if well-controlled transfers fail beyond their
training families; favor evidence acquisition when distinct admitted physical
models remain indistinguishable; favor formal mathematics when all premises are
specified and the remaining barrier is derivation. One failed tool does not close
an autonomous scientific mission. One successful theorem does not prove the whole
programme's ultimate trajectory.
