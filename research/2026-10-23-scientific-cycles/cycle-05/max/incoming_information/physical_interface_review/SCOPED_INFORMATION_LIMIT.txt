1. WHAT THIS OPERATION CAN DECIDE

The LP compares how much of TWO FORWARD source-table functionals is identifiable
from a declared finite list of bounded scalar observations at stated absolute
errors. It is a valid static mathematical question. It neither supplies those
observations experimentally nor identifies net NO production, population dynamics,
quench attainability, complete reset or useful process energy.

Even perfect recovery of the full N2 population p would give only
  R_f = Σ_v p_v Σ_w k_f(v,w),
  M_f = Σ_v p_v Σ_w k_f(v,w)[epsilon_NO(w)-epsilon_N2(v)]
under this published forward fit and population convention. The direct full-
population zero-noise control consequently closes this static uncertainty, not
the owner's physical-admissibility question. The current threshold improvement
over bounded Chebyshev moments is tiny in worst normalized diameter and not a
meaningful new capability. The stronger optimized-power comparator should be
reported transparently as post-primary adversarial comparison, with thresholds
unchanged and its design costs charged; it must not be reclassified as preregistered.

2. WHY EVEN AN EXACT SNAPSHOT IS NOT A REVERSIBLE GENERATOR

Let n_A,n_B,n_C,n_D be the bulk species densities for A(v)+B <-> C(w)+D,
p_v the actual A-state probabilities, u_w the actual C-state probabilities.
The net number source is
  J = n_A n_B Σ_vw k_f(v,w)p_v
      -n_C n_D Σ_vw k_b(w,v)u_w.
The corresponding mode-energy source weights EACH signed state-pair flux by
epsilon_C(w)-epsilon_A(v). R_f,M_f do not determine either reverse contraction.
Even supplying exact p AND u does not specify k_b.

Detailed balance resolves this only AFTER identifying compatible equilibrium
state weights a_v,b_w and equilibrium bulk ratio Kc. It would require
  k_b(w,v)=k_f(v,w) a_v/(Kc b_w).
The present forward data do not specify these weights. At a fixed temperature,
different positive normalized a,b define different algebraically reversible
completions with the same forward matrix and bulk Kc. The reverse contraction is
  (1/Kc) Σ_vw k_f(v,w) a_v u_w/b_w,
which generally varies with a,b. Positive pairwise forward/reverse fluxes obey
(x-y) log(x/y)>=0 in each such declared completion, so scalar nonnegative reaction
entropy production does not select the microscopic completion. This is a proof
of UNDER-SPECIFICATION of the available interface, not a claim that arbitrary
weights are physically admissible for real nitrogen. Actual electronic/rotational
statistics and source conventions constrain the weights and must be recovered.
The owner's synthetic NASA/normalized-ladder completion chooses one member; it
does not establish that it is the physical member.

There is a second independent issue even if that reverse channel is supplied.
For any missing reversible state-changing reaction pair, multiplying BOTH forward
and reverse coefficients by a positive factor preserves its equilibrium ratio,
conservation law and detailed-balance entropy sign but changes its transient
generator. A single exact state snapshot and equilibrium thermochemistry do not
identify that factor. This observation does not modify known forward coefficients;
it concerns omitted dissociation/recombination, relaxation or other channels.
No arbitrary numerical factors are fitted or advocated here. Rates of omitted
channels require evidence or bounds; energy/population reset requires their path.

Therefore no size of population-observation improvement in this experiment, even
zero uncertainty in R_f,M_f, alone resolves the native physical question. It can
become a useful intermediate result if the owner later needs this precise static
information guarantee in a separately qualified decision. No such receiving use
or process improvement is currently demonstrated.

This critique does not request measurements, outreach, spending or another run.
