SCIENTIFIC INFORMATION: WHAT THE COMPUTATION DOES AND DOES NOT PROVE
22 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane

1. Information ambiguity, independent of the inference method
Let P={p>=0: 1.p=1,E.p=mu}, and let observations be y=Ap+z with
|z_i|<=epsilon. For a scalar target k.p define
 W(A,k,epsilon)=max{k.(p-q):p,q in P,|A(p-q)|<=2epsilon}.
Every algorithm using only y has worst-case absolute error at least W/2.
Indeed p,q attaining W have a common permissible observation y=(Ap+Aq)/2;
one returned estimate has error>=|k.p-k.q|/2. Conversely the midpoint of the
minimum and maximum k.p on the feasible fibre of each y attains error<=W/2.
Thus W/2 is the exact minimax error in this deterministic observation model.
Compactness ensures extrema exist. No linear-estimator assumption is needed.
This is ordinary optimal-recovery/convex-optimization reasoning, not a claimed
new universal theorem. The new work tests its scientific consequences on the
actual Dots constitutive table, with strong controls and explicit failures.

Our two outputs are R_v=sum_w k_vw and
M_v=sum_w k_vw(epsilon_NO,w-epsilon_N2,v). The latter is a FORWARD chemical
mode-excitation source. It is not bath heat, net NO, a time derivative of the
whole gas, or an energy-normalized process benefit. No division by R is used.

Numerical LPs are scaled by max|k| and mean energy by maxE. Affine equalities
retain actual probability and mean, independently checked in original eV units.
Source rates are finite QCT fit evaluations with no supplied physical confidence
interval. Small residuals and dual gaps support numerical correctness; they do
not give an exact physical or rational certificate for the entire fit.

2. A basis change is not extra information
With exact mean and normalization, the degree2..4 ordinary power moments and
shifted Chebyshev observations span the same information. Their noiseless
diameters therefore agree (observed to rounding error). Assigning independent
equal absolute errors to the new coordinates defines a DIFFERENT observation
uncertainty set. The finite-noise comparison treats them as separate bounded
observation operators, not a free numerical preconditioner. We do not claim
one is cheaper or experimentally measurable. Transporting the original error
polytope would preserve the original information.
Likewise, if an experiment reveals the molecular level v, all bins and moments
can be computed from the same data. Feature count alone is not a sample, time,
money, photon or apparatus cost. The full-state control explicitly prevents
claiming a scalar-feature saving against an equally costly full measurement.

3. The preregistered threshold hypothesis failed
The selected thresholds22,38,39 were frozen using both source outputs at
1700,2300,2700K and the corrected-index table, epsilon1e-6. Evaluation used
both source-index variants at1500,1900,2100,2500,3000K. These are held-out

Worst normalized diameter at epsilon1e-6 was0.565026 for selected thresholds,
0.565450 for bounded Chebyshev2..4, and0.572710 for power2..4. This is not a
material new advantage. A post-primary equally informed strong control exhaustively

better than the proposed threshold strategy. Its status is POST-PRIMARY; treatment
was not retuned. The exact-observation and epsilon1e-4 cases also favor that control.
No universal optimality is claimed for either finite search family.

5. Why this does not yet amplify the owner investigation
Even exact N2 and NO populations do not identify microscopic reverse exchange
without source-compatible equilibrium state weights. Detailed balance gives
k_back(w,v)=k_forward(v,w) a_v/(Kc b_w) only AFTER those weights are known.
Omitted reversible channels also have unidentified timescales even when their
equilibrium ratios and entropy signs are fixed. The separate independent note
SCOPED_INFORMATION_LIMIT.txt proves these interface limitations and identifies
which unknowns remain, without inventing their physical values.
At the owner's one caloric state, its necessary global entropy budget passes for
every p because S(p)=B+nR H(p), H>=0, and B-S_initial=771.446269464257J/kg/K.
That does not establish reachable p, local entropy production or a complete reset.
It does not automatically cover the held-out bath temperatures.

The new information operation is reusable for static scientific questions.
No independent owner has yet accepted or used it; no new physical capability,
discovery-rate gain, general strategy advantage or recursive amplification follows.
The strongest lesson is conditional and falsifiable: changing WHAT is inferred
can matter as much as predicting more accurately, but the permitted observations
and dynamics determine which questions are actually answerable.
