POST-PRIMARY STRONGER-CONTROL CHECK
The primary results were inspected before this addendum. They show no substantial
advantage for selected thresholds over the strongest prespecified polynomial
control. Do not relabel this follow-up as preregistered or tune the treatment.

Independent critiques correctly noted that a fixed low-order moment comparator
does not exploit the same training data as threshold selection. Add a stronger
ordinary comparator by exhaustive selection of three degrees from2..12 (165
triples,990training LPs), using the SAME six training responses and epsilon1e-6.
This numerical selection budget is comparable to threshold selection's1044 LPs.
Evaluate the winner on the unchanged held-out grids/noise variants. Keep all
primary outputs unchanged. This can falsify a threshold-design advantage; it
does not convert new observables into experimentally available measurements.
