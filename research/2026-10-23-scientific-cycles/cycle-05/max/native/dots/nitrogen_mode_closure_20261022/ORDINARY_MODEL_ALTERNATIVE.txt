EXISTING ORDINARY ALTERNATIVE, NOT AN ASTRA INVENTION
The already retrieved primary numerical paper Species-Specific Vibrational Energy Model for Hypersonic Flow Simulations, DOI10.13009/EUCASS2025-079, provides a conventional multi-vibrational construction. Its Section2.2/Eq7 assigns non-preferential chemical mode energy from species production times average internal-mode energy. Sections2.2.1–2.2.2 prescribe VT and fitted VV models; Section2.1 uses five neutral species N2/O2/N/O/NO, not the current six-species NO2-containing system. Transport uses modified Fick mass diffusion and mode-specific Fourier heat flux.

This is a real available modeling alternative, so the present mathematical closure should not be presented as creating multi-temperature gas dynamics. It does not identify the actual chemical energy partition in our returned-product lamella, supply NO2 mode/transfer data, or validate the roughly6K gate. Blindly transplanting it changes both chemical and transport contracts. A future controlled comparison could adopt its non-preferential model explicitly as a conventional hypothesis, alongside a scientifically justified competing partition, but no new trajectory is selected here merely because equations are available.
