# Failed or superseded routes that affect continuation

## Exact identification as a surrogate for stability

The fixed254-vertex forest prism has a naive proper acyclic four-color design.
It is exactly identifiable, but a63/64 cut gives a unit edge with action norm
squared127/4032. The all-size cut theorem shows divergence whenever a bounded-
degree bichromatic tree grows. This is a failure of that information encoding,
not an inadequacy of floating elimination and not an all-design graph lower.
The same support has the good four-query seed with error constant sqrt2.

## The first successful signed seed lost its matched comparison

The six-vector period-six design and scalar SOS are valid. Its Frobenius
constant is approximately5.0413. The later period-nine coordinate construction
has constant approximately2.2883 with the same four-action contract. Therefore
the signed seed is not the accepted banded operation on conditioning grounds.
Both are linear work; no isolated timing was used to manufacture a runtime win.
The signed local-nullspace representation is retained for future non-coordinate
designs, where the coordinate characterization may not apply.

## Completion and bounded-bandwidth shortcuts

A genuine Delta4 2-tree has a very constrained structure: squared path or
one of two finite exceptions. Completion of a general partial2tree can raise
degrees. Saturated tree neighbors already rule out a universal degree-
preserving completion. The finite local decoder for a supplied completion
therefore does not extend by changing the word '2-tree' to 'partial2tree'.

Even2-connected graphs in the selected class can have unbounded bandwidth:
the prism of a binary tree has n=2(2^(h+1)-1), diameter2h+1 and bandwidth at
least(n-1)/(2h+1). This counterexample motivated the new prism operation;
it was not used as a recovery impossibility.

## Local rank, palettes, anchors and gluing

Low-degree anchors need not lie within bounded distance: long squared paths
and deep saturated trees defeat that premise. Proper three-coloring also
does not force the balanced2+2 neighbor-color pattern: book3 has3+1.
Finite locally full-rank palettes alone give no uniform bound on the global
scalar compatibility inverse. A composition proof across shared vertices
must control noise reuse and long balanced ambiguity chains. No block-gluing
or octahedral-palette theorem for all current supports was completed here.
Those ideas remain unproved proposals, not negative results for all designs.

## Unequal-gain expansion is not ordinary Cheeger coercivity

On a depth-H branching tree, energy(z_child-z_parent/2)^2 with amplitude
z_v=2^-depth(v), zero boundary, has vanishing interior energy and boundary
energy(4/3)(3/4)^H although the root is one. Endpoint gains stay between1/2
and1. This defeats the proposed generic weighted-gain lemma even on an
expanding tree. It is a counterexample to a scalar proof premise; no claim
is made that these arbitrary gains are realized by an admitted query design.
The signed positive construction proves exact unit-magnitude gains instead.

## Coloring results that do not yet close the norm contract

Proper coloring plus bounded component size does not ensure injectivity:
the alternating C4 witness has action exactly zero. Acyclicity is needed.
Linear coloring ensures bichromatic paths but may leave their length
unbounded. The current operator theorem identifies why that is insufficient.
The inspected outerplanar four-linear-color theorem supplies neither the
missing uniform bound nor the full partial2tree hypothesis.
