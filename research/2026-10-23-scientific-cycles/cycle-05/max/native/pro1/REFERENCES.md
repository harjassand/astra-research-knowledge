## Closest coding theorem

V. Giovannetti, D. Burgarth and S. Mancini, *Communication Through a Quantum
Link*, Physical Review A79,012311(2009), arXiv:0810.0170.

- https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.0170
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/0810.0170
- Actual locations: Definition1 and Theorem2, SectionII, pp2–3.

Definition1 uses subexponential exact environment dimension for a sequence of
causal channels. Theorem2 supplies large quantum codes by an error-space and
convex-partition argument, without a memoryless-noise premise. Our tree's
complete channel does not automatically satisfy that exact environment premise.
The inherited finite-coordinate compression/normalization theorem supplies the
approximate input-restricted operation. The new contribution is the exact
correlated-pair kernel, stable high-entropy average and above3/2 receiving use.
The generic low-environment coding idea is not newly claimed.

- https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9908066

## A theorem whose premise is not silently invoked

D. Kretschmann and R.F. Werner, *Quantum Channels with Memory*, Physical Review
A72,062323(2005), arXiv:quant-ph/0502106, Theorem13.

- https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0502106
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0502106

The coding theorem for forgetful memory channels requires its forgetfulness
hypothesis. Negative drift of one selected input-averaged bath map is not a
proof of forgetfulness of the whole channel. Our deterministic whole-block
code argument does not invoke that premise or independent physical blocks.
