## Question and acceptance rule

Can a native symmetric branching proposal remove the predecessor lower bound's
factor two and match the accepted killed-operator upper? Accept only a complete
positive finite construction, strict forward-KL admission, actual local/global
competition, relative timing and retention, and a direct fixed-kernel policy
argument. Finite matrix powers cannot establish an asymptotic rate. A source-bound
challenge must inspect the final proof and the count of vertices in each layer.

The main construction and incoming acceptance are frozen in
`BRANCHING_SHARPNESS.md` and `INCOMING_ACCEPTANCE.md` before the following runs.
The core correction already incorporated is that reverse local acceptance is
`D s/n^2`, with aggregate probability `f/n^2`. Same-level global moves are kept
as quotient self-loops. No floating normalization is an admission certificate.

## Native information and cost boundary

The sampler can sample/evaluate public q and the fixed symmetric Q and evaluate
an unnormalized positive target at proposed points. The supplied population K
is an assumption. Every run keeps eta,alpha,Q fixed. The proof uses normalized
tables for witnesses and verification; it does not make those tables or a
normalizer available to the sampler. A recipient with a complete target table
can use direct discrete sampling and is outside any claimed oracle advantage.

One hybrid step stays with probability 1/2, proposes globally with alpha/2 and
locally with beta/2. A local q-based screen precedes the target evaluation. Full
caching is allowed to both methods. Count initialization, proposals, q ratios,
acceptance uniforms, all uncached target calls and cache memory. The finite
matrix comparison supplies iteration/global-attempt counts; it does not supply
measured oracle latency or a distribution of cache misses. It cannot establish
an end-to-end speedup. The strongest ordinary reference remains pure IMH with
coefficient K. Decreasing eta raises local screen passage and does not itself
reduce target work. No uniform finite-precision guarantee on arbitrary target
or proposal ratios is claimed.

Complete code, exact inputs, results and timing/RSS receipts are included.
Body runtime, source-review wall span and unknown active inference/token/cost
totals are reported separately. File counts and replay times are not discovery
costs or productivity measurements.
