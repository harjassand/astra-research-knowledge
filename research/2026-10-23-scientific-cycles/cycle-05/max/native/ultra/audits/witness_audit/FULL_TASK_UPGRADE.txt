1. CONTRACT AND CONCLUSION
Let H=the tensor product of r full tasks H_a, each of dimension D^N, with
r,N>=1 and integer D>=2. Each task has its specified ordering of N physical
D-level sites, so the actual prefix erasures E_(a,i) are fixed. Let A be a
rank-one MASA, with Pi_j=|e_j><e_j|. Assume ONLY

 E_(a,N)(A) subset A for every full task a.

The complementary full-cut erasure preserves A as a composition of the
other full-task erasures. The Cycle3 two-sided cut classification therefore
gives one common cut rank k_a across task a versus all other tasks.

For any normal diagonal coefficient matrix X=sum_j c_j Pi_j of HS norm 1,

 S(X*X) >= ln(D) sum_(a=1)^r sum_(i=1)^N ||E_(a,i)X||_HS^2
                       -r C_D-sum_a ln(k_a),                    (T)

where C_D is any inherited valid scalar one-chain constant. In particular
the previously stated minimum constant remains usable; no new constant is
introduced for the residual dimension k_a.

2. PHYSICAL BLOCK SUPPORT IS THE LOAD-BEARING STRUCTURE
Fix task a. Its full-task erasure acts on A by uniform cell averaging. For
each full-task cell y, write

 P_y=sum_(j in y)Pi_j=I_task tensor Q_y,
 rank Q_y=k_a,
 |y|=D^N k_a.

The Q_y are mutually orthogonal projections resolving the complementary
physical space. Since X is diagonal in A,

 X=sum_y X_y,   X_y=P_y X P_y=sum_(j in y)c_j Pi_j.

For every actual prefix erasure E_i=E_(a,i), whether or not E_i(A) subset A,

 E_i(X_y)=P_y E_i(X_y) P_y.

This follows from normalized partial trace's bimodule identity for operators
acting outside the erased subsystem. Here P_y=I_task tensor Q_y is outside
the ENTIRE task, so it is outside every erased prefix. Therefore the images
E_i(X_y) have mutually orthogonal Hilbert-Schmidt supports and

 ||E_i X||_HS^2=sum_y ||E_i X_y||_HS^2.              (B)

It would be invalid to assert this identity for arbitrary abstract spectral
blocks; the physical tensor form of P_y is exactly what proves it here.

3. THE OLD SCHMIDT ENVELOPE WITH AN ARBITRARY RESIDUAL DIMENSION
Put w_y=||X_y||_HS^2=sum_(j in y)|c_j|^2. For w_y>0, vectorize X_y/sqrt(w_y).
This is a normalized bipartite pure state with local physical spaces

 (C^D)^(tensor N) tensor range(Q_y)

on Alice, and the conjugate residual support on Bob. Both have dimension
D^N k_a. Its Schmidt probabilities are precisely the list

 lambda_j=|c_j|^2/w_y, j in y,

because X_y is normal and diagonal on this block. No claim is made about
the Schmidt vectors preserving the physical prefix observables.

Sort the full list of Schmidt roots u_j=sqrt(lambda_j) once in descending
order. For prefix i, set L=D^i and R=D^(N-i)k_a. The range of the actual
prefix Bell projector consists of |Phi_L> tensor |eta>, where eta has
residual local dimension R. The old variational overlap/singular-value
inequality therefore gives

 ||E_i(X_y/sqrt(w_y))||_HS^2
  <= B_i(lambda)
   = (1/L) sum_(h=1)^R (sum_(j=(h-1)L+1)^(hL) u_j)^2.       (S)

Proof of (S), including all dimensions: the Schmidt roots of Phi_L tensor
eta are sqrt(r_h/L), each repeated L times, for a probability vector r on
R roots. The singular-value trace inequality bounds overlap by
L^(-1/2) sum_h sqrt(r_h) C_h, with C_h the contiguous root block sums above.
Cauchy-Schwarz bounds its square by sum_h C_h^2/L. The single descending
list u works for all i simultaneously; no incompatible per-prefix
permutation is selected. This is the original universal one-chain argument
with the suffix dimension enlarged from D^(N-i) to D^(N-i)k_a.

4. WHY THE SAME C_D SURVIVES THE ANCILLA
The inherited scalar lemma was proved with an arbitrary finite/countable
residual coordinate, but a separate padding argument also verifies the
constant without relying on that wording. Let ell=ceil(log_D k_a). Append
zeros to u, obtaining a vector of length D^(N+ell). Entropy is unchanged.
For i<=N its ordinary little-endian size-D^i chunk score is exactly B_i:
the old length D^N k_a is divisible by D^i, so padding only adds complete
zero blocks. Apply the original scalar lemma at horizon N+ell, then drop
the extra nonnegative prefix scores. It gives

 ln(D) sum_(i=1)^N B_i(lambda) <= H(lambda)+C_D.       (Gk)

This proves the extension for EVERY inherited valid C_D, including a
minimum chosen from separate proofs. There is no additive ln(k_a) charge
in this one-chain step; that charge enters later through the full-cut
entropy identity. k_a need not be a power of D.

5. CONDITIONAL BLOCK ENTROPY
Combine (B), (S), and (Gk), then average the nonzero w_y. Let J have law
p_j=|c_j|^2 and let Y_a denote its full-task cell. Since sum_y w_y=1,

 ln(D) sum_i ||E_(a,i)X||_HS^2 <= H(J|Y_a)+C_D.       (C)

This step is valid for complex c directly: the singular values are |c_j|.
No entrywise positivity of the actual prefix Gram and no prefix invariance
is being assumed or used.

6. ENTROPY IDENTITY AND GLOBAL TASK CORRELATIONS
Let rho=X*X=sum_j p_j Pi_j. By full-task cut classification,

 S(rho_complement a)=H(Y_a)+ln(k_a),
 H(J|Y_a)=S(rho)+ln(k_a)-S(rho_complement a).

Quantum Shearer gives sum_a S(rho_complement a)>=(r-1)S(rho), with no
independence assumption. Summing (C) therefore gives (T).

The chain-rule/SSA proof of this entropy inequality is in AUDIT.txt. The
argument preserves arbitrary correlations between full tasks throughout;
it does not sum unrelated marginal E_F bounds.

7. SUPPORT EXTENSION AND BOUNDARIES
For the bipartite vector |psi>=vec(X), S(X*X) is its entanglement entropy
and ||E_(a,i)X||_HS^2 is its ACTUAL joint Bell-prefix probability on task a.
A density operator supported on span{|e_j> tensor |conjugate(e_j)>} has all
pure ensemble vectors in this same support. Averaging (T) yields its E_F
witness with the same r C_D+sum_a ln(k_a) defect.

The shared MASA and its exact full-task invariance are structural premises.
They are not implied by a normal coefficient matrix alone. Generic
non-normal coefficient matrices and unrestricted approximate formation
outputs have not been brought into this class. In particular, closeness
of a target to this support does not put its preparer's output in support.

No per-site or prefix invariance is needed. Thus arbitrary unitaries acting
inside each full task preserve the full-task invariant-MASA premise and the
cut ranks, even if their depths grow with N or they destroy every proper
prefix invariance. This is a mathematical extension, not an efficiency or
experimental accessibility result.

8. EDGES, COST AND REMAINING GAP
r=1 gives k_1=1 and a single global full-task cell; (T) is the old universal
one-chain inequality. N=1 is included. Zero-weight cells are omitted before
normalization. All dimensions are finite, and no entropy-limit exchange is
used. Composite D and non-power-D cut ranks are covered by the padding proof.

If every k_a<=K uniformly in N, the defect is at most r(C_D+ln K), independent
of the task horizon. Without rank control it can be extensive in N. The
balanced maximal-cut theorem supplies a different useful regime but does
not automatically remove this remaining limitation. Unrestricted critical
witness and generic formation-gap questions remain open.

This audit was analytic; no numerical optimizer or search was run. It read
the original universal Schmidt audit and scalar proof, and checked their
precise residual-dimension extension. Historical novelty, efficient premise
testing, finite gate realization and physical usefulness remain UNKNOWN.
