DISPOSITION
PASS with the normalizations below. The continuity extension is a routine
application of the established entropy continuity theorem; its contribution
is the precise conversion of this support-restricted witness into an error
bound and the explicit compatibility-gap obligation. It does not establish
a generic unrestricted formation law or an accessible hidden-memory test.

1. EXACT WITNESS AND SPECTRAL RANGES
Let q=D^(rN) be each party's total physical output dimension, and let Pi be
the orthogonal projection onto W=vec(A), where A is the intersection
commutant in COMMUTANT_UPGRADE.txt. Define

 O=ln(D) sum_(a=1)^r sum_(i=1)^N BellPrefix_(a,i),
 L=sum_(a,y) ln(rank Q_(a,y)) P_(a,y),
 A_wit=O-I tensor conjugate(L),
 Lmax=sum_a ln K_a.

The exact supported-state witness is E_F(omega)>=Tr(A_wit omega)-r C_D.
All summands of O are positive effects, so 0<=O<=ln(q)I. Likewise
0<=L<=Lmax I. It follows that

 -Lmax I <= A_wit <= ln(q) I,
 spectral oscillation(A_wit)<=ln(q)+Lmax.

No commutation of O and I tensor conjugate(L) is needed for these bounds.

2. PURE-STATE CONTINUITY AT A PROJECTION
For a normalized pure vector psi, let ell_psi=1-||Pi psi||^2. If ell_psi<1,
put phi=Pi psi/||Pi psi||. At ell_psi=1 choose any normalized phi in W;
W is nonempty because A contains I. In either case

 T(psi,phi)=sqrt(ell_psi),

where T is half trace norm on the corresponding pure density operators.
Partial trace cannot increase this distance. Audenaert's sharp finite-
dimensional entropy continuity theorem, converted to natural logarithms,
implies the safe monotone modulus

 |S(psi_A)-S(phi_A)| <= t ln(q)+hbar(t),
 hbar(t)=h_2(min(t,1/2)), t=sqrt(ell_psi).

Indeed for the actual reduced distance u<=t, the sharp theorem gives
u ln(q-1)+h_2(u), which is at most u ln(q)+hbar(u). The latter expression
is monotone on [0,1], so it is bounded by t ln(q)+hbar(t).

The source is Audenaert, "A Sharp Fannes-type Inequality for the von Neumann
Entropy", Theorem 1 (full trace-distance range), arXiv:quant-ph/0610146:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0610146
https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8113/40/28/S18

Expectation of a Hermitian observable with spectral oscillation b changes
by at most b T. Apply the exact witness to phi and the preceding bounds:

 S(psi_A) >= Tr(A_wit psi)-r C_D
              -(2 ln(q)+Lmax)sqrt(ell_psi)-hbar(sqrt(ell_psi)).

Here psi in an expectation denotes its pure density operator. The first
ln(q) comes from entropy continuity; the second ln(q)+Lmax comes from the
linear witness expectation. The projection is solely a mathematical proof
device, not a claimed LOCC channel.

3. MIXED STATE AND JENSEN STEP
Let sigma be arbitrary and ell=1-Tr(Pi sigma). In every pure ensemble,
the weighted mean of ell_psi equals ell. The map t->hbar(t) is concave and
nondecreasing: it equals the increasing concave binary entropy on [0,1/2]
and is constant afterwards, with matching zero derivative at the join.
Therefore

 average sqrt(ell_psi) <= sqrt(ell),
 average hbar(sqrt(ell_psi)) <= hbar(sqrt(ell)).

Average the pure inequality and infimize over ensembles. This proves

 E_F(sigma) >= Tr(A_wit sigma)-r C_D
              -(2 ln(q)+Lmax)sqrt(ell)-hbar(sqrt(ell)).          (R)

The right side is always valid, though it may be negative. Replacing ell
by an established upper bound eta in [0,1] weakens it monotonically.

4. WHAT MATCHING TESTS DO AND DO NOT CERTIFY
The LOCC effect W_a=sum_y P_(a,y) tensor conjugate(P_(a,y)) has acceptance
probability Tr(W_a sigma), as proved in COMMUTANT_UPGRADE.txt. Let

 H=sum_a (I-W_a).

Its kernel is exactly W. If H is nonzero and its smallest strictly positive
eigenvalue has a proved lower bound gamma>0, then

 H>=gamma(I-Pi),
 ell <= [sum_a (1-Tr(W_a sigma))]/gamma.               (G)

Cap the right side at 1. If H=0, Pi=I and ell=0 directly. Computing or
certifying gamma can be expensive; finite dimensionality alone supplies no
useful lower bound. If all P_(a,y) commute across tasks, the W_a commute,
their positive spectrum is integral and gamma>=1; the usual union bound
follows. No coherent projection or preparation enforcement is obtained.

The sum in (G) must not be replaced by its average. If r tests have average
failure nu, the bound is ell<=r nu/gamma. Statistical sample cost, gamma
certification and the gate cost of the local projectors must be charged in
an implementation claim. Exact acceptance cannot be established by finite
samples; data only give confidence bounds. None of these output tests
certifies complete hidden-memory return or a memory-resource cap.

5. THE SMALL-RANK GAP OBSTRUCTION IS SHARP
Use the exact rational three-qubit construction in section 7 of
COMMUTANT_UPGRADE.txt, with integer M>=2. The rank bounds stay K=(2,2,1),
and c=(M^2-1)/(M^2+1), s=2M/(M^2+1). Its exact compatibility gap is

 gamma=1-c=2/(M^2+1).

Proof. On the qubit-3 operator Hilbert space, the two matching projections
are identity on the identity operator, and rank-one projections onto Z and
Z_M in the traceless real span of X,Z. The sum of their complementary
projections has eigenvalues 1-c,1+c on that span and eigenvalue 2 on Y.
The task-3 matching projection retains diagonal operators on qubits 1,2;
off-diagonal qubit-1,2 operator components add 1 to H and cannot decrease
the minimum. Thus the smallest positive eigenvalue is 1-c exactly.

The sharper normalized bad coefficient matrix

 X_bad=(I_12/2) tensor (M Z+X)/sqrt(2(M^2+1))

is orthogonal to the exact commutant and has distance 1 from every supported
state. Each of the first two tests accepts with probability M^2/(M^2+1),
and the third accepts with probability 1. Hence its summed failure is

 2/(M^2+1)=gamma,

so (G) is saturated at ell=1. This proves quantitatively that bounded
complement ranks do not replace a compatibility-gap premise. It concerns
support inference, not a counterexample to (R) or to the exact witness.

6. CONDITIONAL FORMATION / SUPPLY ACCOUNTING
For r target copies, run the fixed device N blocks per copy. The ideal
complete joint output has task-prefix successes at least
s_i=[1-(i-1)delta]_+. If a globally approximate initialized memory has half
trace error epsilon from the ideal tensor-power memory, the actual full
output sigma has half trace error at most epsilon by channel contraction.
Since O has spectral oscillation at most r N ln(D),

 Tr(O sigma)>=r ln(D) sum_i s_i-r N ln(D) epsilon.

Suppose also the actual output has proved leakage at most eta into W-perp.
A counted deterministic Bell supply ln M, or a preselected expected Bell
supply B, must be at least E_F(sigma) by the existing complete-outcome LOCC
factory argument. Combining with (R), and writing C_supply for either
declared supply convention, gives

 C_supply/r >= ln(D) sum_i s_i-C_D
  -Tr[(I tensor conjugate(L))sigma]/r-N ln(D) epsilon
  -(2N ln(D)+Lmax/r)sqrt(eta)-hbar(sqrt(eta))/r.          (F)

All normalizations agree with the root's formula. The rank-cost term is a
linear observable of the actual external output, not an entropy proxy.

For fixed N before the copy/error limits, if epsilon_r,eta_r->0 and
Lmax/r<=L uniformly, the asymptotic supply rate is at least
ln(D)sum_i s_i-C_D-L. Choosing N=ceil(1/delta) only afterwards yields

 C_rate >= ln(D)/(2delta)+ln(D)/2-C_D-L.

Ordinary erased-stock preparation in the computational maximally correlated
support has K=1 and eta=0, and matches the leading ln(D)/(2delta) law under
the same resource/error convention. This is a constrained structural result,
not an unrestricted generic E_C theorem. For nonvanishing preparation error,
the already-proved strong-converse comparator must remain in the comparison.

If N is allowed to grow together with the copy/error index, the displayed
formula requires N epsilon_r->0 and N sqrt(eta_r)->0 (plus the other stated
controls); the fixed-N argument alone does not authorize that interchange.
