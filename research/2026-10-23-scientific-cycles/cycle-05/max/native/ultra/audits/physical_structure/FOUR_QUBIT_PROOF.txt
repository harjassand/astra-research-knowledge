FOUR-QUBIT INVARIANT ERASURE MASAS: PARITY-BLOCK COMPATIBILITY
22 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane | bounded Cycle 4 contribution
Internal proof candidate; independent analytical challenge found no gap.
Historical novelty remains unestablished.

1. Statement and inherited facts

A is a rank-one MASA on four physical qubits, invariant under all individual
normalized erasures. Thus every subset erasure preserves A. Inherited cut
duality gives one common flat Schmidt rank k_S for every basis vector at
every cut, with k_S dividing both cut dimensions; erasure cells all have
size 2^|S| k_S. The two-qubit involution lemma in PROOF.txt is also used.

THEOREM. Up to a COMMON product local unitary, a permutation of physical
sites, and relabeling/rephasing basis vectors, every such basis is either

 (i) a tensor product of singleton, Bell, or complementary-pair phase-GHZ
     bases, with disjoint blocks whose total size is four; or
 (ii) the four-site path-cluster stabilizer basis.

A phase-GHZ block of length ell consists, independently for every pair
{x,bar(x)}, of (|x> +/- exp(i theta_[x]) |bar(x)>)/sqrt(2). It includes
the phased two-site case, locally equivalent to Bell under invariance.

In particular, when all one-site ranks equal two, the only possible three
balanced-cut rank multisets are {1,4,4}, {2,2,2}, {2,4,4}. The profile
{2,2,4} is not physically admitted. All-rank-four balanced cuts are also
impossible, as proved directly below.

2. Immediate factorization cases

If any one-site k_s=1, cut duality factors A as a fixed singleton MASA
times a three-qubit invariant MASA. PROOF.txt classifies the latter, giving
(i). Hence assume all k_s=2.

If any pair-cut k_S=1, cut duality factors A across that balanced cut.
Invariance and k_s=2 then force a Bell basis on each pair, by the two-site
classification in PROOF.txt. This is the {1,4,4} case. Hence it remains to
analyze a rank-two pair cut or rule out every pair cut having rank four.

3. A rank-two pair cut forces parity coordinates

Assume k_{12}=k_{34}=2. Each of E_{12}|A and E_{34}|A has two equal cells.
Their cell-projector differences have the forms I_{12} tensor T_{34} and
T_{12} tensor I_{34}, with T Hermitian unitary. Both one-qubit partial
traces of each T vanish: for example E_3 E_{12}=E_{123} is global averaging
on A, because its complementary rank is k_4=2. Apply the two-qubit
involution lemma and one common product local unitary to arrange that A
contains

        P=Z_1 Z_2,       Q=Z_3 Z_4.

Signs only rename the cell labels. Let p,q in {0,1} denote these parities,
and identify their four-dimensional joint eigenspace with two logical
qubits A,B by

 |a>_A |b>_B  <-->  |a,a xor p,b,b xor q>.

Cut duality says the four physical basis vectors in EVERY (p,q) block form
a complete maximally entangled logical-qubit basis. In particular, each
one can be written

 |psi>=( |0>_A |v_0>_B + |1>_A |v_1>_B )/sqrt(2),

where v_0,v_1 are an orthonormal B basis (phases may be absorbed there).
Let n be the real Bloch axis with (n dot sigma)|v_a>=(-1)^a |v_a>.

4. Single erasures align all conditional axes

Take a physical basis vector psi in block (p,q). Its E_1 image is 1/4
times a rank-four projector C_psi, since k_1=2. It is supported in the
same q sector but spans both p' sectors. On the logical block (p',q), its
rank-two projector is exactly

 C_psi |_(p',q) = [I + (-1)^(p xor p') Z_A tensor(n dot sigma_B)]/2.   (1)

To check (1), tracing site 1 kills the a cross term and retains physical
site 2 in |a xor p>; insertion of I_1 permits either p', with logical
a'=a xor p xor p'. The B vector is still v_a. This gives the stated
correlation eigenspace. The coefficient 1/4 is irrelevant to commutation.

All C_psi lie in A and commute. Fix q, and restrict them to any common
(p',q) block. They are all nontrivial rank-two projectors of the form (1),
even when their originating p differs. Product operators Z_A tensor n·sigma
and Z_A tensor n'·sigma commute exactly when n,n' are parallel. Thus one
axis n_q works, up to signs absorbed into eigenvalue labels, for EVERY
basis vector originating in either p sector. Consequently every basis
vector in block (p,q) is an eigenvector of

        L_q=Z_A tensor(n_q dot sigma_B).

The corresponding E_3 argument, now fixing p and varying q, provides one
axis m_p for which every basis vector in block (p,q) is an eigenvector of

        M_p=(m_p dot sigma_A) tensor Z_B.

These two Hermitian operators are diagonal in the same complete block basis
and hence commute. Their commutator is a sum of independent local Pauli
terms proportional to

 (Z dot m_p) I tensor[(n_q cross Z) dot sigma],
 (n_q dot Z) [(Z cross m_p) dot sigma] tensor I.

Both must vanish. Unit vectors then give EXACTLY two possibilities in each
block: both m_p,n_q parallel to Z, or both perpendicular to Z. The full
Cartesian family of all four (p,q) blocks forces ONE regime globally: if
one m_p is parallel, every n_q and then every m_p is parallel; otherwise
all m_p and n_q are perpendicular. An oblique axis cannot occur.

5. The parallel regime is exactly a phase-GHZ basis

Here L_q and M_p are, up to sign, the same Z_A Z_B. Every block basis
therefore refines its two two-dimensional parity eigenspaces. A maximally
entangled logical vector in such an eigenspace must have equal amplitudes
on its two complementary logical strings. The orthonormal pair is

       (|a,b> +/- exp(i theta) |bar(a),bar(b)>)/sqrt(2).

These two physical strings are complements on ALL four sites. The eight
phases, one for each physical complementary pair, can vary independently.
Thus the entire physical basis has the phase-GHZ4 form in (i), after the
one common local unitary already selected. Its balanced ranks are all 2.

6. The perpendicular regime is exactly the cluster MASA

Write m_p,n_q as equatorial axes. Diagonal physical single-qubit unitaries
on sites 1,2 act, within logical parity p, as arbitrary logical Z rotations
with angles alpha_1+(-1)^p alpha_2. The two linear equations for p=0,1
can set m_0 and m_1 independently to X, using ONE pair of physical local
unitaries. Likewise sites 3,4 independently set n_0,n_1 to X. None of these
rotations changes physical P,Q or the logical Z axes.

After these rotations every (p,q) block basis diagonalizes the two
independent commuting operators Z_A X_B and X_A Z_B. Their four joint
eigenspaces are one-dimensional, so the basis is uniquely their joint
eigenbasis. Globally A is therefore precisely the rank-one stabilizer MASA
generated by

        P=Z_1 Z_2, Q=Z_3 Z_4,
        L=Z_1 X_3 X_4, M=X_1 X_2 Z_3.                       (2)

The logical Pauli X_A is physical X_1 X_2 and X_B is X_3 X_4; logical Z_A
and Z_B can be represented by physical Z_1,Z_3. This proves (2) without
choosing any parity-dependent GLOBAL unitary. Individual basis-vector
phases and eigenvalue labels do not change the MASA.

To identify (2) with the path-cluster basis, conjugate the standard path
graph generators by physical H_1 H_4. The endpoint generators become P,Q;
the two middle generators become M and P L. These generate exactly (2).
Its balanced ranks are {2,4,4}, as independently checked from the actual
graph-state coefficient matrices by replay.py.

7. All pair ranks four are impossible: a self-contained positivity proof

If all balanced-cut ranks were four, every two-site marginal of any basis
vector would be I/4. Consider just one pure state rho with this property.
Expand it in Hermitian four-qubit Pauli strings:

 rho=1/16 sum_T t_T T,    t_T real, t_I=1.

All weight-one and weight-two coefficients vanish. Write A_j as the sum of
t_T^2 over strings of weight j. Purity gives 1+A_3+A_4=16. Every three-site
reduction has purity 1/2, because its complementary one-site marginal is
I/2 and rho is pure. Its only nonidentity terms have weight three, so the
sum of their squares is 8*(1/2)-1=3. Sum this over the four triples to get
A_3=12 and hence A_4=3.

The positive spin-flipped matrix tilde(rho)=Y^tensor4 rho^T Y^tensor4 has
Pauli coefficients (-1)^weight(T) t_T, since Y sigma_a^T Y=-sigma_a.
Both rho and tilde(rho) are positive, so Tr(rho tilde(rho))>=0. But Pauli
orthogonality instead gives

 Tr(rho tilde(rho))=(1-A_3+A_4)/16=(1-12+3)/16=-1/2,

a contradiction. This proves the needed no-all-rank-four statement without
assuming an external classification of absolutely maximally entangled states.
Together with sections 2-6 it completes the theorem. QED.

8. An explicit coordinate certificate for the cluster case

The new classified case does not require a fresh general stabilizer theorem.
For the path graph basis e_z=Z^z|G>, physical Z_s permutes labels by adding
the standard binary vector e_s. Physical X_s permutes labels by adding the
neighbor vector Gamma_s, up to irrelevant vector phases, because
X_s=K_s product_(t neighbor s) Z_t and K_s acts diagonally on graph labels.
Normalized erasure of a subset S is the average of conjugations by all
physical Pauli operators on S. On the diagonal coefficients it is therefore
uniform averaging over the binary subspace

       W_S=span_F2{e_s,Gamma_s : s in S}.

This subspace contains span{e_s:s in S}. If A_S averages exactly the binary
label coordinates in S, then K_S A_S=K_S. Consequently
||K_S f||_2<=||A_S f||_2 simultaneously for every S and every coefficient
vector f. This explicit common-coordinate certificate has precisely the
form needed by the inherited scalar-chain and classical-conditioning proof.

9. Consequence and limits

The classification supplies exact common-support certificates for ALL
invariant erasure MASAs on at most four qubits. All cases were already
covered by the accepted critical entropy witness: product/phase-GHZ blocks
by the nonlinear coordinate construction; cluster blocks by global
stabilizer transport. Thus every tensor product of arbitrary invariant
blocks of at most four physical qubits obeys the unchanged critical witness
with globally correlated spectral coefficients and its usual convex-roof
support extension. This removes a basis-normal-form premise; it does not
improve the defect, admit generic noninvariant matrices, or close a formation
gap. Common local unitaries commute with the normalized-erasure operation
in the required covariance sense and preserve entropy and Hilbert-Schmidt
norms, so they do not change the witness.

The useful decision is to retire searches for a new invariant compatibility
class at <=4 physical qubits: the first unclassified qubit block size is
five. Larger local dimensions are also not classified here. The proof uses
exact cut-duality and actual quantum cell projectors; it does not elevate
abstract partitions or integer rank profiles to physical realizations.
