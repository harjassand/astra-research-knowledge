1. Contract and consequence

Let A be a rank-one MASA on three physical qubits, invariant under each
single-site normalized erasure E_s(X)=I_s/2 tensor Tr_s X. The erasures
commute, so A is invariant under every subset erasure. The cut-duality
theorem in Cycle3/invariant/PROOF.txt implies that every basis vector has
one common flat Schmidt rank k_s in {1,2} across s versus its complement.

THEOREM. After one product local unitary and a permutation of physical
sites, the entire basis of A is of exactly one of the following forms:

 (a) a product of three fixed one-qubit bases;
 (b) a Bell basis on two sites times a fixed one-qubit basis on the third;
 (c) for each complementary bit pair {x,bar(x)}, the two vectors

       ( |x> +/- exp(i theta_[x]) |bar(x)> ) / sqrt(2),

     where the four phases theta_[x] are arbitrary and independent.

The conclusion concerns a COMMON product local unitary for the complete
basis. It is stronger than separately placing each vector in GHZ form.
Conversely all three displayed families are invariant under all erasures.

Receiving use. Arbitrary invariant three-qubit blocks are therefore already
inside the admitted phase-GHZ/Bell/product witness class of Cycle2/masa.
One may allow a tensor product of arbitrary such blocks crossing the tasks,
and a globally correlated coefficient spectrum, without separately supplying
a GHZ-coordinate certificate for each block. The old critical entropy witness
and its same defect r C_2 follow. This is a structural closure, not a new
constant or a proof of the unrestricted critical witness. The first new
qubit support-compatibility boundary begins at four physical sites; section
5 gives an admitted boundary example.

2. A two-qubit involution lemma

LEMMA. If T=T* on two qubits, T^2=I, Tr_1 T=Tr_2 T=0, then
T=(u dot sigma) tensor (v dot sigma) for real unit vectors u,v, up to a sign.

Proof. The zero partial traces remove the identity and one-body terms in
the Pauli expansion, so T=sum_(a,b=1)^3 t_ab sigma_a tensor sigma_b with
t a real 3 by 3 matrix. A proper-rotation singular-value decomposition
diagonalizes t using SO(3) on both sides, permitting signed singular values.
These rotations are implemented by product qubit unitaries. Thus it is
enough to consider T=a XX+b YY+c ZZ, a,b,c real. Pauli multiplication gives

 T^2=(a^2+b^2+c^2) I -2ab ZZ -2bc XX -2ca YY.

Linear independence and T^2=I force a^2+b^2+c^2=1 and ab=bc=ca=0.
Exactly one coefficient is +/-1. Undoing the local rotations proves the
claim. This argument also proves that an arbitrary correlation tensor cannot
be used as an invariant erasure cell involution. QED.

3. Proof when all three one-site ranks equal two

The invariant E_s restriction averages on two cells of size four. Let their
projectors be P_(s,0),P_(s,1). They have the form I_s tensor Q_(s,b), where
Q_(s,b) are complementary rank-two projections on the other two sites.
The cell involution O_s=P_(s,0)-P_(s,1) therefore has the form
I_s tensor T_s, with T_s Hermitian unitary. All O_s belong to A and commute.

For t!=s, E_t O_s=E_t E_s O_s=E_{s,t} O_s=0. The last equality uses the
complementary cut rank k_{s,t}=k_u=2: the E_{s,t} cell size is 4*2=8,
so its restriction to A is global averaging, and Tr O_s=0. Thus T_s has
both one-qubit partial traces zero. The lemma gives

 O_1=I tensor (a_2 dot sigma) tensor (a_3 dot sigma),
 O_2=(b_1 dot sigma) tensor I tensor (b_3 dot sigma),
 O_3=(c_1 dot sigma) tensor (c_2 dot sigma) tensor I.

Commutation of O_1,O_2 forces a_3 parallel to b_3: their commutator is the
tensor product of two nonzero Pauli operators with
[a_3 dot sigma,b_3 dot sigma], and the latter vanishes exactly for parallel
axes. The other pairs similarly align the axes on sites 1 and 2. A single
product local unitary now puts, after absorbing independent signs,

 O_1=Z_2 Z_3, O_2=Z_1 Z_3, O_3= +/- Z_1 Z_2.

The joint eigenspaces of O_1,O_2 are exactly the four two-dimensional
spaces span{|x>,|bar(x)>}. A refines these eigenspaces. Every basis vector
in one such space has maximally mixed one-qubit marginals (the assumed
rank-two flat cut spectrum), so its two coefficient moduli are 1/sqrt(2).
The two orthonormal vectors in that space are consequently the +/- pair
in (c), with a phase that may vary independently between the four spaces.
No simultaneous choice of local phases was assumed. QED.

Corollary. The three nontrivial one-site erasure partitions have only TWO
independent binary cell labels: their third cell involution is the product
of the first two, up to sign. Three abstract independent binary labels on
eight points cannot be realized by this physical all-rank-two invariant
MASA. Pairwise commuting classical expectations and correct cell sizes do
not replace this quantum support constraint.

4. Proof when a one-site rank equals one

If k_1=1, cut duality with k=1 gives A=A_1 tensor B_23 for fixed rank-one
MASAs A_1 and B_23. Invariance under E_2,E_3 passes to B_23. Two-site cut
duality gives either a product basis on 2,3, or a complete maximally
entangled basis. This proves (a) or (b), except for a common local-unitary
normal form for the latter, which follows as follows.

Represent the four maximally entangled vectors by the unitary coefficient
matrices U_j/sqrt(2); orthogonality means Tr(U_j* U_l)=2 delta_(j,l).
One common local unitary sets U_0=I. Every remaining U_j is a traceless
unitary and hence, up to an irrelevant scalar phase, u_j dot sigma for a
real unit vector u_j. Orthogonality makes these three real vectors an
orthonormal frame. A common unitary conjugation rotates that frame to the
three Pauli axes (signs affect only irrelevant vector phases). The basis
is therefore the ordinary Bell basis after common local unitaries.

The converse for (a),(b) is immediate. For (c), any nonempty proper partial
trace kills the off-diagonal |x><bar(x)| term. The diagonal supports have
the same erasure partitions as the usual GHZ basis, independently of the
four phases. This proves invariance and completes the theorem. QED.

5. Exact first boundary: a four-qubit path-cluster basis

The above theorem cannot extend to all qubit counts by merely allowing
larger GHZ blocks. The four-site path graph state has coefficient amplitudes

 |G>=1/4 sum_(x in {0,1}^4) (-1)^(x1 x2+x2 x3+x3 x4) |x>,

and its 16 graph-basis vectors are Z^z |G>. Its MASA is generated by
K_1=X_1 Z_2, K_2=Z_1 X_2 Z_3, K_3=Z_2 X_3 Z_4, K_4=Z_3 X_4.
These independent commuting Pauli generators yield a rank-one MASA.
Every subset erasure either kills a Pauli monomial or leaves it unchanged,
so the MASA is invariant. This is an actual finite quantum basis, not an
abstract partition candidate.

For any graph state and cut S, phases internal to each side are removable
by local diagonal unitaries relative to that cut. The coefficient matrix
then consists of the characters (-1)^(x_S^T Gamma_(S,S^c) x_(S^c)).
Orthogonality of binary characters shows exactly 2^rank_F2(Gamma) equal
nonzero Schmidt values. Thus this graph basis has all single-site ranks 2,
and the three balanced ranks are

       k_{12|34}=2, k_{13|24}=4, k_{14|23}=4.

A tensor product of GHZ blocks with all one-site ranks two can only be one
four-site GHZ block or two Bell blocks. The former has all balanced ranks
2; the latter has one balanced rank 1 and the other two 4. Therefore the
path-cluster basis cannot be put into tensor-GHZ-block form by ANY product
local unitary, basis relabeling, and physical-site permutation. Cut-rank
multisets are invariant under all these operations.

The cluster basis is already covered by the earlier global stabilizer
witness. Its purpose here is to identify precisely where the new small-site
classification stops, rather than claim an entropy counterexample. More
general nonstabilizer four-site invariant support compatibility remains a
surviving frontier. There is no inference of entropy-witness violation.

6. Costs, provenance, and remaining gap

The complete three-qubit classification is analytical and uses only the
already supplied cut-duality theorem, elementary Pauli multiplication, and
singular-value decomposition. It uses neither an unproved semigroup
comparison nor a borrowed conditional entropy inequality. The exact replay
constructs the 16 actual path-graph basis vectors with rational coefficients,
verifies orthonormality, all flat cut spectra, and all erasure invariances,
then distinguishes their cut-rank pattern from every GHZ block partition.
It is finite evidence for the explicit boundary, not a test proving the
universal classification. Measured cost is recorded in RESULT.json.

An unrestricted invariant-MASA witness is not obtained. This result retires
the search for a three-qubit invariant basis outside the admitted product /
Bell / phase-GHZ family, and redirects genuinely new support compatibility
to at least four qubits (or larger local dimensions). Formation gaps and
deterministic versus expected preparation costs are unchanged. Dense
classification testing is exponential; no efficient preparation or hidden
memory certification interface is claimed. Historical novelty, experimental
