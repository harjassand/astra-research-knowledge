FULL-TASK-ONLY UPGRADE AND A NONNORMAL BLOCK-COMMUTANT EXTENSION
22 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane. Independent internal proof challenge.

VERDICT: The principal's full-task-only upgrade PASSES. No prefix-invariance
premise is needed. Moreover, its proof extends to arbitrary, potentially
NONNORMAL coefficient matrices that reduce the full-task-cell projections.
The precise general theorem and proof are given below. This is a scoped
support extension, not an unrestricted formation or nonnormal theorem.

1. OLD UNIVERSAL ONE-CHAIN LEMMA, INCLUDING AN ARBITRARY RESIDUAL

Let one task consist of N D-level physical sites, with an arbitrary residual
space of dimension k. For ANY matrix Z on H_task tensor C^k with ||Z||_HS=1,
let E_i erase the first i physical task sites. The inherited scalar hierarchy
lemma and singular-value rearrangement imply

 (ln D) sum_(i=1)^N ||E_i Z||_HS^2 <= S(Z*Z)+C_D.           (U1)

There is no normality or basis-invariance premise in (U1). Here is the full
reduction to the old scalar lemma. Vectorize Z across the two copies of its
whole local Hilbert space. Its Schmidt roots are the singular values
c_1>=...>=c_(D^N k)>=0. At prefix i put L=D^i, R=D^(N-i)k. Then

 ||E_i Z||_HS^2
   = ||(<Phi_L| tensor I)|vec Z>||^2
   = max_(||eta||=1) |<Phi_L tensor eta|vec Z>|^2.

If eta has Schmidt roots t_j, the normalized Phi_L tensor eta has Schmidt
roots t_j/sqrt(L), each repeated L times. Singular-value overlap comparison
and Cauchy-Schwarz give

 ||E_i Z||_HS^2 <= (1/L) sum_(j=1)^R
                  (sum_(l=(j-1)L+1)^(jL) c_l)^2
                 = ||Q_i c||_2^2.                         (U2)

The SAME sorted singular-value list is used for every i, and Q_i averages
the ordinary contiguous chunks of length D^i. Represent its index by N
base-D low digits and one residual label in [k]. Apply the scalar chain
lemma to normalized residual slices and average. This gives

 (ln D) sum_i ||Q_i c||^2 <= H(c^2 | residual)+C_D
                              <= H(c^2)+C_D=S(Z*Z)+C_D.

This proves (U1) with the same C_D, independent of k and N. The scalar lemma
is the sole inherited nontrivial estimate. The overlap step is the original
one-chain Schmidt envelope, not a new general strong-superadditivity claim.

2. GENERAL BLOCK-COMMUTANT THEOREM

Partition the physical system into r tasks T_a, each N D-sites. For EVERY
task a, choose an orthogonal decomposition {Q_(a,y)} of its COMPLEMENTARY
Hilbert space. Put P_(a,y)=I_(T_a) tensor Q_(a,y) and k_(a,y)=rank Q_(a,y).
The decompositions for different a need NOT commute with each other.

Let X be ANY matrix with Tr X*X=1 which COMMUTES with EVERY P_(a,y).
It may be nonnormal. Write rho=X*X and
 q_(a,y)=Tr[P_(a,y)rho]=||P_(a,y) X P_(a,y)||_HS^2.
Then the actual physical prefix erasures satisfy

 S(rho) >= (ln D) sum_(a=1)^r sum_(i=1)^N
                         ||E_(a,i) X||_HS^2
            -r C_D-sum_(a,y) q_(a,y) ln k_(a,y).           (BC)

Proof. Fix a task a. Commutation gives the orthogonal block decomposition
X=sum_y X_y, X_y=P_(a,y) X P_(a,y). Prefix erasure E_(a,i) acts only inside
T_a, so it is bimodular over complementary operators and

 E_(a,i)(X_y)=P_(a,y) E_(a,i)(X_y) P_(a,y).

The images for different y therefore remain orthogonal in HS inner product:
||E_(a,i)X||^2=sum_y ||E_(a,i)X_y||^2. This is the crucial point that does
not require the prefix erasure to preserve a diagonal MASA.

Apply (U1) to each nonzero normalized block X_y/sqrt(q_y), whose physical
space is H_(T_a) tensor ran Q_(a,y), with residual dimension k_(a,y).
Average with weights q_y. Because rho is block diagonal,

 (ln D) sum_i ||E_(a,i)X||^2
       <= sum_y q_y S(X_y*X_y/q_y)+C_D
        = S(rho)-H(q_a)+C_D.                              (B1)

The complementary reduced state is block diagonal on the orthogonal Q_y:
rho_(T_a^c)=directsum_y q_y Tr_(T_a)(X_y*X_y/q_y). Its block entropies
are bounded by ln k_(a,y), so

 S(rho_(T_a^c)) <= H(q_a)+sum_y q_y ln k_(a,y).             (B2)

Combining (B1) and (B2) gives

 (ln D) sum_i ||E_(a,i)X||^2
 <= S(rho)-S(rho_(T_a^c))+sum_y q_y ln k_(a,y)+C_D.

Sum over a. Quantum SSA yields
sum_a S(rho_(T_a^c)) >= (r-1)S(rho), proving (BC). No physical output,
task, block, row/column or spectral correlations were discarded. QED.

If k_(a,y)<=k_a uniformly in y, the defect is at most
r C_D+sum_a ln k_a. The expected-log-rank version (BC) is slightly stronger
and does not require uniform block ranks. The entropy dimension bound (B2)
is explicit mathematical bookkeeping, not a resource assay assertion.

3. RECOVER THE PRINCIPAL'S FULL-TASK-INVARIANT MASA THEOREM

Let A be a rank-one diagonal MASA invariant under every FULL-task erasure.
There is no hypothesis on any proper prefix. For each a the full-task
restriction averages on cells y with projections

 P_(a,y)=sum_(j in y) Pi_j=I_(T_a) tensor Q_(a,y).

Every X in A commutes with all these projections. Moreover the complementary
full-task erasure is the product of the other full-task erasures, hence also
preserves A. The complementary-cut theorem then gives one common rank k_a
for all Q_(a,y), equal to the flat full-task Schmidt rank of every basis
vector. The normal diagonal coefficient matrices in A therefore obey

 S(X*X) >= (ln D) sum_(a,i)||E_(a,i)X||_HS^2
                     -r C_D-sum_a ln k_a.                 (FT)

In fact (FT) holds for ALL X in the full commutant of the P_(a,y), by (BC).
It is not restricted to A or to normal X. The P_(a,y) commute in this MASA
application, so their common refinement gives orthogonal projections M_z.
Their commutant is the direct sum of full matrix algebras B(ran M_z).
Thus arbitrary nonnormal matrices within these common blocks are included.

If one wants an E_F supported-state extension, vectorize this LINEAR
commutant subspace. Every pure ensemble vector of a density operator whose
support lies in that subspace remains in it. Applying (BC)/(FT) and taking
the convex roof gives the same erasure-score witness for such supported
mixed states. The claim concerns exactly this explicit support. Arbitrary
approximate iid formation outputs need not satisfy it.

4. STRICT HYPOTHESIS SEPARATION UNDER ARBITRARY INTERNAL UNITARIES

Full-task erasure is equivariant under ANY unitary U_a acting within task a.
Therefore conjugating A by tensor_a U_a preserves all full-task invariance
and all k_a, even when the U_a have arbitrarily deep/nonlocal internal
circuits. Prefix invariance generally fails. No light-cone depth penalty is
required by (FT), because (U1) works for arbitrary matrices in each block.

An exact two-site admission example is any task basis related to the product
basis by an arbitrary unitary. For example the controlled basis described
in LC_AUDIT.txt already fails one nonprefix subset erasure; a generic
entangling internal unitary also fails the first-prefix erasure. Tensoring
the task bases makes k_a=1 and satisfies only the needed full-task premise.
The resulting witness is valid for every correlated coefficient spectrum.

To see a direct prefix failure explicitly on two sites, use the orthonormal
product basis |0> tensor |j> and |1> tensor V|j>, with
V=[[3/5,-4/5],[4/5,3/5]]. Full erasure preserves every MASA. Erasing the
FIRST site does not preserve this one, since

 <1,V0| E_first(|00><00|) |1,V1> = -6/25 != 0.

The whole-task tensor product across r tasks still obeys (FT), with k_a=1.
This is an exact separation from the prefix-invariant class. Its conventional
Schmidt/tree comparator may already certify it; it is an admission check,
not an advantage claim over all prior constructions.

5. LIMITS AND DECISION

The full-task-only theorem is stronger than the DFS proof and makes its
prefix-invariance assumption unnecessary. Retain DFS as a complete local
construction/proof alternative, not as the final maximal scope.

The block-commutant extension is stronger than normal-diagonal support, but
it remains a structural resource constraint. No assertion of generic
low-rank decompositions, approximate support stability, experimentally
accessible hidden memory, arbitrary iid formation or logarithmic-gap
closure follows. If k_a grows exponentially in N, its defect can again be
extensive. No general high-rank critical witness has been proved here.
