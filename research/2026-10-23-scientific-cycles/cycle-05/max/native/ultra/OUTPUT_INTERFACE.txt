OUTPUT-ONLY INTERFACE: EXACT EFFECTS, STATISTICS AND COST
22 October 2026. Companion to PROOF.txt, not a hidden-memory certificate.

Use its finite output state sigma on C^q tensor C^q, q=D^(rN), m=ln D.
Keep every output, retained shared register and branch in the underlying
preparation/return contract. The tests below act on its finite external
outputs only. They do NOT prove complete-memory return accuracy delta,

Those remain separate premises with separately charged interfaces.

1. EXACT COMMUTING SUPPORT EFFECT

Suppose all prescribed physical P_(a,y) commute, including across tasks.
For a tuple alpha=(y_1,...,y_r), set R_alpha=product_a P_(a,y_a).
Discard zero R_alpha. These are orthogonal projections summing to I.
Commutation with all P_(a,y) is equivalent to block diagonality in these
joint sectors. Thus
 B=direct_sum_alpha B(ran R_alpha),
 Pi_W=sum_alpha R_alpha tensor conjugate(R_alpha).
In particular this is exactly the intersection of all individual support
effects W_a=sum_y P_(a,y) tensor conjugate(P_(a,y)).

Alice measures the joint sector alpha; Bob measures its conjugate beta.
They compare the full tuples by classical communication, accepting exactly
when alpha=beta. The acceptance EFFECT is Pi_W. All reject outcomes count.
The actual failure probability is ell=1-Tr(Pi_W sigma), with no factor r.
Measuring the r compatible resolutions sequentially implements the same
joint projective measurement. This consumes the state and resolves its
individual labels. It does NOT implement the coherent two-outcome Lueders
instrument, and it is NOT an operation preparing sigma in W for free.

Bob's tuple also supplies the real charge
 c(beta)=sum_a ln k_(a,y_a), 0<=c<=Lmax.
Its expectation is Tr[(I tensor L^T)sigma]. Reading the charge from this
measurement therefore needs no hidden entanglement estimate. The specified
projectors and their ranks must be available as a calibrated mathematical
and implementation model. An arbitrary unknown projector's rank is not
inferred from observed frequencies.

If P families do not commute, the individual tests remain LOCC effects,
but a joint-tuple test is unavailable in general. A proved gap gamma>0 of
H=sum_a(I-W_a) gives ell<=sum_a failure_a/gamma. The fixed-rank physical
counterexample in audits/witness_audit shows that ranks alone cannot lower
bound gamma. A gap computation is additional work, not a free premise.

2. BELL-PREFIX SCORE BY LOCAL MEASUREMENTS

For a prefix of i D-sites, let d_i=D^i. On one D-site let
 U|j>=|j+1 mod D>, V|j>=exp(2 pi i j/D)|j>.
Take the d_i^2 tensor-Weyl operators W_u=product_sites U^s V^t.
They form an orthogonal unitary operator basis, also for composite D.
Completeness of this basis gives the EXACT operator identity

 Phi_(d_i)=d_i^(-2) sum_u W_u tensor conjugate(W_u).

For completeness, vectorization gives
 (W_u tensor conjugate(W_u))|X>>=|W_u X W_u^dagger>>.
The uniform Weyl twirl sends X to Tr(X)I/d_i, so the sum is precisely the
rank-one projection onto |I>>/sqrt(d_i). Equivalently verify each matrix
entry using operator-basis orthogonality.

Choose u uniformly, locally measure the unitary W_u on Alice and
conjugate(W_u) on Bob, and multiply their complex eigenvalue outcomes.
Its real part Z lies in [-1,1]. Averaging over settings and outcomes gives
E Z=Tr[(Phi_(d_i) tensor I_rest)sigma]=p_(a,i). A unitary's spectral
measurement is a finite local measurement; tensor Weyls require only
single-site spectral measurements and classical multiplication. The
physical eigenbasis circuits/calibration still have to be supplied.

Choose prefix (a,i) uniformly among all rN prefixes as well. The random
variable Y=Nm Z then satisfies E Y=Tr(O sigma)/r, |Y|<=Nm. A trial measures
at most N D-sites on each party, but consumes the complete sigma preparation.
One need not enumerate all exponentially many Weyl strings: sample their
2i exponents independently. This is the known local direct-fidelity method,
not a new measurement-priority claim (Flammia--Liu, arXiv:1104.4695).

3. EXPLICIT STATISTICAL CONTRACT

Assume independent preparations of the SAME full state sigma. No iid or
factorization assumption is made between its r tasks, its N output slots,
or their retained memory history. Without an actual preparation/sampling
model, the following confidence statements must not be asserted.

For additive per-task score precision zeta>0 and failure probability beta,
Hoeffding gives the sufficient full-preparation count

 n_score >= ceil[2(Nm)^2 ln(2/beta)/zeta^2].

For joint-test failure precision a>0, take
 n_support >= ceil[ln(2/beta)/(2a^2)].
With confidence 1-beta, ell<=min(1,observed_failure+a). Use that upper eta
in Theorem B or finite tradeoff (I); do not insert the point estimate alone.
For per-task rank-cost precision zeta_rank>0 (Lmax>0), take
 n_rank >= ceil[(Lmax/r)^2 ln(2/beta)/(2 zeta_rank^2)].
Support and rank statistics may be read from the same joint-test trials;
using n_joint=max(n_support,n_rank) suffices for both individual bounds.
Use a union bound across the three reported statements, for example beta/3
for each. Score and rank errors enter the per-task lower bound subtractively.
At Lmax=0 the rank cost is identically zero and requires no estimation.

These are conservative sufficient counts, not minimax claims. Each of the
n_score+n_joint trials costs an independently prepared FULL rN-output state,
including its charged memory initialization and uses of the device. The
construction does not recycle tested copies. For small certified eta the
support precision must also be small; this cost is explicitly retained.
Individual-test alternatives would charge the SUM of all r errors (and
gamma for noncommuting families), rather than their average.

4. IMPLEMENTATION BOUNDARY

All effects are finite-round finite-outcome LOCC once their specified local
projective measurements are given. Generic dense q-dimensional projectors
can require exponential classical descriptions, synthesis and execution.
Known ranks, mutual commutation and exact local circuits are mathematical
premises here; a physical uncertainty in these effects would require an
additional calibrated operator-error term. No hardware data or such
calibration was acquired. The Weyl score has an explicit local sampler;
this does not establish an efficient implementation of arbitrary block
tests or of the reservoir's memory-return measurement.

The useful proved connection is narrow and exact: prescribed compatible
output label tests certify leakage from the full coefficient-block support,
and their local charges certify its weighted log-rank penalty. This
connection, together with the robust entropy bound, is sufficient for the
conditional supplied-entanglement law. It does not certify hidden resources.
