ARM A: SQUARED PATH
On G=P_(8m)^2, maximum degree four, every X obeys
sup_D ||D||2/||DX||F >= max(1, n^(1/4)/sqrt(24 sqrt(5))), n=8m.
An exact kernel means infinity. Otherwise the action map has codomain dimension
three larger than its domain, so its adjoint kernel has exactly dimension three.
Four disjoint slowly varying rotation fields contain a nonzero vector orthogonal
to that entire kernel. Thus the estimate concerns a positive singular value,
not merely the already-present adjoint nullspace. Root checked this step, the
tent estimates, the factor sqrt(n) paid for conversion to operator norm, and the
diagonal witness 1/min_i ||x_i|| which handles short rows. None changes the cap
to equality or assumes a favorable design. This closes the uniform q3 question
even when maximum degree is bounded.
