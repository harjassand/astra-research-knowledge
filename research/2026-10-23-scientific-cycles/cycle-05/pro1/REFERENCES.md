# Primary comparison and attribution boundary

- Giovannetti, Burgarth, Mancini, Communication Through a Quantum Link,
  Physical Review A79,012311(2009), arXiv0810.0170.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/0810.0170
  Definition1/Theorem2 give established perfect-memory/subexponential-
  environment coding. The present full bath is infinite; input-relative
  overflow and coherent-initializer normalization are additional obligations.

- Kretschmann, Werner, Quantum Channels with Memory, Physical Review A72,
  062323(2005), arXivquant-ph/0502106.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0502106
  Theorem13 assumes finite-dimensional forgetful memory. Neither that
  condition nor independent physical channels follows from selected-input
  drift. Our proof uses the full same-bath isometry.

- Pan, Amini, Miao, Gough, Ugrinovskii, James, Heisenberg Picture Approach to
  the Stability of Quantum Markov Systems, arXiv1405.6770.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1405.6770
  Sections3–4 give established operator-Lyapunov/tightness reasoning.
  The new bounded-grade identity and coding application do not make
  Lyapunov reasoning itself novel.

The inherited appendix retains Knill–Laflamme–Viola,
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9908066, and affine Tverberg,
https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/s1-41.1.123. Those tools are not new claims.

New model-specific work: charge-sector bulk parity plus the root margin
in the uniform block-information upper; the controlled growing-length
window; homogeneous-increment operator-to-code interface; non-scalar
four-call use; entropy-Hessian/full-prefix variational frontier.

This is bounded primary comparison, not exhaustive priority assessment.
No unpublished source was disclosed in external search. Internal proof,
external acceptance, efficient implementation and physical usefulness are
separate. Primary papers and relevant theorem sections were read.
