\begin{abstract}
The preserved Cycle4 construction proves a strict quantum-capacity gain
above $3/2$ on the fixed coherent recurrent qutrit tree. We now prove that
every fixed length of independent charge-preserving input blocks has only
quartic small-angle coherent-information excess. Blocks may be nonidentical
and optimized anew at every horizon; the channel and bath remain the actual
same-memory process. The upper bound is uniform over all normal initializers.
Together with the inherited pair construction it gives a sharp order law
for the optimized block-input coherent information at the actual initializer,
not a converse for unrestricted capacity.

The proof retains the vacuum exchange. A bulk move/stay parity rule removes
linear perturbations; a strictly positive root margin absorbs boundary
coherences; relative entropy then controls arbitrary block inputs.
Explicit constants also control a logarithmically growing block-length window.
Generic four-call inputs do not close on a scalar depth chain. We replace
that assumption by a homogeneous-Kraus operator Lyapunov criterion and carry
it through the inherited arbitrary-reference compression operation.
An explicit four-call density has non-scalar depth dynamics, more entropy
than the two-pair average, and uniform negative operator drift, so the
criterion yields genuine reliable codes.

We derive the exact fixed-block entropy Hessian and its operator-valued
variational interface. Neither bath-prefix averaging nor finite matrix size
certifies a coding gain. The remaining question is whether longer temporal
structure outside the proved window changes the small-angle order.
\end{abstract}

\section{Unchanged device and distinct quantifiers}

The bath is $E=\mathbb C^2_{\rm seed}\otimes\mathcal F(\mathbb C^2)$.
Let $S_j$ prepend color $j$, and let $R$ exchange the two vacuum seeds and
vanish off the vacuum. With $P_0=R^2$,
\[
 S_i^*S_j=\delta_{ij}I,\quad \sum_jS_jS_j^*=I-P_0,\quad
 RS_j=S_j^*R=0,\qquad
 U=\begin{pmatrix}R&S_1&S_2\\S_1^*&0&0\\S_2^*&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Thus $U=U^*=U^{-1}$. The actual device is
$V_s=\cos s\,U+i\sin s\,I$, with
$\beta=\sin s$, $\alpha=\cos s$, $a=\alpha^2=1-b$, $b=\beta^2$.
The angle is fixed before every communication horizon.
Bath depth is $L\ket{j,w}=|w|\ket{j,w}$, and
\[
 r_h=\rank P_{\le h}=2(2^{h+1}-1),\qquad
 \tau_\pi=\frac{I_2}{2}\otimes\frac23\sum_w6^{-|w|}|w\rangle\langle w|.
 \tag{1}
\]
The reference remains $\eta=\operatorname{diag}(3/4,1/8,1/8)$.
All calls use $W_N=V_{NE}\cdots V_{1E}$ on the same evolving bath.
All forward slots, outcomes and retained output registers count.
There is no bath access, fresh bath, reset, preshared entanglement,
backward quantum wire, timing channel or postselection.
Terminal bath return is neither imposed nor concluded.

Let $Q_k=\sum_{j=1}^k(I-|0\rangle\langle0|)_j$ be input color charge.
A charge-preserving block density satisfies $[\rho,Q_k]=0$; it may be
entangled within its block and may have complex coherences inside each
charge sector. Independence below concerns input blocks, never independent
physical channels. Entropies and rates use bits. The code error is full
diamond norm; its half-diamond version is half that bound.

The unchanged Cycle4 archive \cite{Cycle4} proves an initializer-independent
code sequence of rate $S(\omega)/2>3/2+\beta^4/1024$ for every fixed
$0<\beta<1$, with pointwise vanishing full-diamond error for every fixed
normal initializer. At the actual $\beta=1/512$ this exceeds $3/2+2^{-46}$.
The optimized identical single-site input theorem remains exactly $3/2$
for $\beta\le1/8$. Neither result is weakened here.

\section{The quartic upper bound, including nonidentical blocks}

Set
\[
 \gamma_k=(3/8)^k,\quad
 d_k=\frac{\gamma_k^2}{8k^2\ln2},\quad
 B_k=4k^2,\quad C_k=4k^3,\quad
 \beta_k=\min\!\left\{\frac12,\frac{d_k}{2B_k}\right\}.
 \tag{2}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Uniform product-block information bound]\label{thm:upper}
For each fixed positive integer $k$, every $0\le\beta\le\beta_k$, every
normal initializer $\tau$, every number $n$ of blocks, and arbitrary
charge-preserving block densities $\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_n$,
\[
 \frac1{nk}\Ic\!\left(\bigotimes_{j=1}^n\rho_j,
                  \mathcal N_{nk}^{\tau}\right)
 \le\frac32+8k^5\ln2\,\beta^4.
 \tag{3}
\]
The densities may be chosen separately for every final horizon.
The bound is uniform in $\tau$, with no finite-entropy or finite-mean
assumption on it. This is an input-class coherent-information bound,
not an unrestricted quantum-capacity or feedback converse.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Bounded charge transfer and the exact bulk selection rule}

Every interaction conserves $L+Q_k$, including the vacuum exchange.
Define the bounded joint displacement observable
\[
 G_s=Q_k-W_k(s)^*Q_kW_k(s),\qquad \norm{G_s}\le k.
 \tag{4}
\]
It equals $W_k^*LW_k-L$ on the finite-coordinate core. For an input matrix
$X$ let $D_s(X)=\Tr_A[(X\otimes I)G_s]$. For Hermitian $X$,
\[
 \norm{D_s(X)}\le k\norm X_1,\qquad
 [D_s(\rho),L]=0\quad\hbox{if }[\rho,Q_k]=0.
 \tag{5}
\]
The second identity follows from charge covariance. It eliminates cross
terms between low and high initial bath depths.

At $s=0$ the complete interaction is a basis permutation. Each ordinary
move flips zero versus colored port; a zero input at the vacuum instead
exchanges seeds. On every basis vector,
\[
 \Delta L=2Q_{\rm input}-k+N_0,\qquad 0\le N_0\le k,
 \quad\text{hence }G_0\ge2Q_k-kI.
 \tag{6}
\]
Equality holds on initial depths at least $k$.

Let $P_{\rm hi}=1_{[k,\infty)}(L)$. Expand $W_k$ into move/stay histories.
There is no vacuum encounter during an active move from this initial
subspace. For a history with $r$ stays, input and output charges $q,q'$
satisfy $q'-q\equiv k-r\pmod2$. Two histories contributing to the same
output from the same input charge therefore have stay counts of equal
parity. Their total sine degree is even. Thus the input-charge pinching
of $P_{\rm hi}G_sP_{\rm hi}$ is even in $s$, including complex
within-sector coherences.

All $V_s,V_s',V_s''$ have norm one, so
\[
 \norm{W_k'}\le k,\quad \norm{W_k''}\le k^2,\quad
 \norm{G_s'}\le2k^2,\quad \norm{G_s''}\le4k^3.
 \tag{7}
\]
For $\beta\le1/2$, $s\le2\beta/\sqrt3$.
Writing $m_\rho=\Tr(Q_k\rho)-k/2$, this proves the coarse global bound
\[
 D_s(\rho)\ge(2m_\rho-B_k\beta)I,
 \tag{8}
\]
and, for charge-preserving Hermitian $X$,
\[
 \norm{P_{\rm hi}[D_s(X)-D_0(X)]P_{\rm hi}}
 \le C_k\beta^2\norm X_1.
 \tag{9}
\]
No assertion of bulk parity has been extended to the root.

\subsection{A positive root margin and relative entropy}

Let $\rho_c=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1/4,1/4)$ and
$\sigma=\rho_c^{\otimes k}$. Its one-call depth law is the actual lazy
symmetric birth/death law, with
$T_s^*L-L=(a/2)P_0$. Therefore
\[
 D_s(\sigma)=\frac a2\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}(T_s^*)^j(P_0).
 \tag{10}
\]
It is zero on $P_{\rm hi}$ and, on $P_{\rm lo}=I-P_{\rm hi}$,
\[
 D_s(\sigma)\ge(a/2)^kP_{\rm lo}\ge\gamma_kP_{\rm lo}.
 \tag{11}
\]
Indeed from each depth $\ell<k$, the $\ell$ consecutive down steps have
probability $(a/2)^\ell$, and their contribution occurs in the
$j=\ell$ term of (10). This is an operator bound, including all
seed and color coherences.

Put $X=\rho-\sigma$, $x=\norm X_1$. Since $\Tr X=0$,
$|2m_\rho|\le kx$. On the low sector, (5) and (11) give
\[
 P_{\rm lo}[D_s(\rho)-2m_\rho I]P_{\rm lo}
 \ge(\gamma_k-2kx)P_{\rm lo}.
 \tag{12}
\]
If $x\le\gamma_k/(2k)$, combine (9), (12), and the exact block
diagonality in depth to conclude
\[
 D_s(\rho)\ge(2m_\rho-C_k\beta^2x)I.
 \tag{13}
\]

The finite input reference obeys $-\log_2\sigma=kI+Q_k$. Hence, with
$D_\rho=D_2(\rho\Vert\sigma)$,
\[
 S(\rho)-3k/2=m_\rho-D_\rho,\qquad
 \norm{\rho-\sigma}_1\le\sqrt{2\ln2\,D_\rho}.
 \tag{14}
\]
The latter is quantum Pinsker. Equation (13) applies whenever
$D_\rho\le d_k$. Combining it with (8) for larger $D_\rho$ gives,
for every charge-preserving density,
\[
 D_s(\rho)\ge
 \left[2m_\rho-C_k\beta^2\sqrt{2\ln2\,D_\rho}
       -uD_\rho\right]I,\qquad u=\frac{B_k\beta}{d_k}.
 \tag{15}
\]
For $D_\rho>d_k$ the last term pays at least the entire coarse error;
for $D_\rho\le d_k$ it is harmless. This step includes inputs far from
the critical reference.

\subsection{Output correlations, arbitrary normal bath and all horizons}

For independent input blocks, the current block is independent of the
current bath marginal, although the bath may be correlated with all past
outputs. Its marginal evolves by the exact $\Phi_{\rho_j}$.
Let $m_j,D_j$ denote (14), $M=\sum_jm_j$, and $D_\Sigma=\sum_jD_j$.
The bounded total charge transferred to the bath is
\[
 \Delta=\sum_j\Tr[\tau_{j-1}D_s(\rho_j)]
 \ge2M-C_k\beta^2\sqrt{2\ln2}\sum_j\sqrt{D_j}-uD_\Sigma.
 \tag{16}
\]
This is defined by bounded charge transfer, not by subtracting infinite
mean depths. Each output port charge commutes with all later interactions.
Telescoping the bounded operators in (4) therefore gives exactly
\[
 \Tr(Q_{nk}\rho_{\rm out})=nk/2+M-\Delta
 \tag{17}
\]
for every normal initializer.

For any qutrit marginal $\zeta$,
$S(\zeta)\le1+\Tr(Q_1\zeta)$ by relative entropy against $\rho_c$.
Subadditivity, retaining all output correlations, then yields
\[
 S(B^{nk})\le3nk/2+M-\Delta.
 \tag{18}
\]
The finite input-reference entropy is
$H_{\rm in}=3nk/2+M-D_\Sigma$. Purify the input with finite $R$.
On the finite system $RB$, Araki--Lieb gives
\[
 \Ic=S(B)-S(RB)\le H_{\rm in},\qquad
 \Ic\le2S(B)-H_{\rm in}.
 \tag{19}
\]
This avoids all bath-entropy assumptions.

For $E=\Ic-3nk/2$, (16)--(19) imply
\[
 E\le\min\left\{M-D_\Sigma,\,
 -3M+(1+2u)D_\Sigma+
 2C_k\beta^2\sqrt{2\ln2}\sum_j\sqrt{D_j}\right\}.
 \tag{20}
\]
Take three quarters of the first bound and one quarter of the second,
and apply Cauchy--Schwarz. With $\overline D=D_\Sigma/n$,
\[
 E/n\le-\frac{1-u}{2}\overline D
       +\frac{C_k\beta^2\sqrt{2\ln2}}2\sqrt{\overline D}.
 \tag{21}
\]
For $\beta\le\beta_k$, $u\le1/2$. Maximizing this quadratic in
$\sqrt{\overline D}$ gives
$E/n\le C_k^2\ln2\,\beta^4/2$. Dividing by $k$ proves (3).
Every inequality is uniform in the horizon and the block choices.

\subsection{A controlled growing-block window}

Since
\[
 \beta_k=\min\left\{\frac12,
 \frac{(3/8)^{2k}}{64k^4\ln2}\right\},
 \tag{22}
\]
the constants also give a limited simultaneous limit.
\begin{corollary}\label{cor:log}
Fix $0<c<1/[2\ln(8/3)]$. If
$1\le k(\beta)\le c\ln(1/\beta)$, then for all sufficiently small
$\beta$, (3) applies and its excess is
$O_c(\beta^4\ln^5(1/\beta))$ per call.
Thus this window cannot support a lower power $\beta^p$, $p<4$,
within the stated independent charge-preserving block-input class.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Put $T=\ln(1/\beta)$. The threshold condition follows from
$64(cT)^4\ln2\,e^{-[1-2c\ln(8/3)]T}\to0$.
Insert $k\le cT$ into (3).
\end{proof}
This does not exclude logarithmic enhancements or faster-growing blocks.
At $k=4$ the conservative threshold (22) is far below $1/512$;
the asymptotic upper is not a useful numerical bound at that selected angle.

\section{Operator drift replaces scalar depth closure}

The four-call representation reveals why a scalar chain is too restrictive.
For example
\[
 \rho_c^{\otimes4}
 +\epsilon(|1000\rangle\langle0100|+\mathrm{h.c.}),
 \qquad0<\epsilon\le1/32,
 \tag{23}
\]
is a positive charge-preserving density. On initial depths at least four,
its exact drift is
\[
 2\epsilon ab(I-S_1S_1^*).
 \tag{24}
\]
Two bath vectors at the same depth but with different top colors have
different drift. Thus charge covariance does not imply scalar depth closure.
The included finite-path algebra retains every final-bath overlap and root
exchange. It exports the full degree-four polynomial in $b$ on an
$81$-dimensional input and $16$-dimensional bath-prefix space.
That is an exact representation of the bulk operator, tensor identity on
the seed and untouched tail, not a reflecting-bath approximation.

\begin{lemma}[Homogeneous increments]\label{lem:grade}
For any charge-preserving $k$-call density $\rho$, its normal bath channel
has Kraus operators of homogeneous depth degree
$\Delta\in\{-k,\ldots,k\}$. Consequently there are positive effects
$A_\Delta$ such that
\[
 \sum_\Delta A_\Delta=I,\quad [A_\Delta,L]=0,\quad
 D_s(\rho)=\sum_\Delta\Delta A_\Delta,
 \tag{25}
\]
and, as a positive quadratic-form identity,
\[
 \Phi_\rho^*e^{tL}
 =\left(\sum_\Delta e^{t\Delta}A_\Delta\right)e^{tL}.
 \tag{26}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Diagonalize $\rho$ in joint eigenvectors of $Q_k$, and take computational
output words $o$. Charge conservation gives each corresponding bath Kraus
operator degree $\Delta=q-N(o)$:
$[L,K]=\Delta K$. The exchange $R$ has degree zero and satisfies this
identity at the vacuum as well. Set $A_\Delta=\sum_{\deg K=\Delta}K^*K$.
Trace preservation proves their sum is $I$; homogeneity proves they commute
with $L$ and yields (25)--(26) on finite-coordinate vectors. Positive
quadratic forms and monotone limits extend the exponential identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform operator drift gives actual reliable codes]
\label{thm:operatorcode}
Fix $k$, $0<\beta<1$, and a charge-preserving density $\rho$.
Suppose its exact drift obeys
\[
 P_{\ge k}D_s(\rho)P_{\ge k}\le-\kappa P_{\ge k},
 \qquad0<\kappa\le k.
 \tag{27}
\]
Then one deterministic unassisted code sequence, independent of the bath
initializer, has rate at least $S(\rho)/k$ and pointwise vanishing
full-diamond error for every fixed normal initializer.
For the actual $\tau_\pi$, the product-block coherent-information limit
is also $S(\rho)/k$. No scalar depth chain or radial state preservation
is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Set
\[
 t=\kappa/(2k^2),\quad
 \lambda=1-\kappa^2/(4k^2),\quad
 C=\frac{e^{2kt}}{1-\lambda}.
 \tag{28}
\]
Since $kt\le1/2$, for $|\Delta|\le k$ Taylor's inequality gives
$e^{t\Delta}\le1+t\Delta+k^2t^2$.
Multiply by each positive effect in (25) and sum. On the high-depth sector
this is at most $\lambda I$. The low-depth sector reaches depth at most
$2k-1$, so (26) gives the global form inequality
\[
 \Phi_\rho^*e^{tL}
 \le\lambda e^{tL}+e^{2kt}P_{<k}.
 \tag{29}
\]
There are no low/high cross terms. Iteration yields
\[
 M_m\le\lambda^m M_0+C(1-\lambda^m).
 \tag{30}
\]
For any coherent initializer supported in $P_{\le A}$,
\[
 \Pr(L_m>H)\le(1+C)e^{-t(H-A)}.
 \tag{31}
\]

Let $d=\rank\rho\le3^k$, using only positive eigenvalues. A
maximum-probability exact spectral type $T_m$ of $\rho^{\otimes m}$
has probability $w_m\ge(m+1)^{-d}$, rank $D_m$, and
\[
 T_m/D_m\le\rho^{\otimes m}/w_m,\qquad
 \log_2D_m/m\longrightarrow S(\rho).
 \tag{32}
\]
For completeness, with type counts $n_i$, maximality against a transfer
from count $i$ to $j$ gives
$n_i/p_i\le(n_j+1)/p_j$. Summing against $p_i$ shows
$n_j\ge mp_j-1$, hence every frequency tends to $p_j$.
The type scalar eigenvalue and the polynomial mass give the rank limit.
This is an operator statement in the actual eigenbasis, even if eigenvectors
are entangled across calls.

Choose
\[
 A_m=\lfloor\log_2(m+1)\rfloor,\quad
 H_m=A_m+\left\lceil
 \frac{(d+16)\ln(m+1)+\ln(1+C)}t\right\rceil,\quad
 \epsilon_m=(m+1)^{-16}.
 \tag{33}
\]
Apply (31) to the uniform state on $P_{\le A_m}$, and then (32).
Mean overflow for the type subspace and that initial bath space is at
most $\epsilon_m$. The inherited joint compression lemma, reproduced
in Appendix~\ref{app:compression}, gives
\[
 K_m\ge\left\lceil
 \frac{\lfloor D_m/2\rfloor}{(r_{A_m}r_{H_m})^4}
 \right\rceil,\quad
 e_m=r_{A_m}\sqrt{2\epsilon_m}+(1-2\epsilon_m)^{-1/2}-1\to0.
 \tag{34}
\]
It retains spectral expurgation, the $r^2$ positive-block factor,
the auxiliary CPTP normalization and its polar repair.
The actual encoder is isometric; no auxiliary normalization is implemented.
For every fixed normal $\tau$, the full-diamond error is at most
\[
 \min\{2,\ 4e_m+2\sqrt{1-\Tr(P_{\le A_m}\tau)}\}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{35}
\]
This follows by comparing $\tau$ outside the type domination with its
normalized coordinate truncation. Thus no type-probability factor multiplies
an uncontrolled initial tail. Since $\log r_{A_m}r_{H_m}=O(\log m)$
for this fixed device and block, (34) proves the rate.

For $\tau_\pi$, $t\le1/2<\ln3$ ensures a finite initial exponential
moment. Equations (30)--(31) bound mean depth uniformly. The bath Gibbs
density $g=4^{-L}/4$ has trace one and
$-\log_2g=2L+2$, so $S(E_m)\le2\E L_m+2$.
This entropy-energy inequality also follows by finite cutoffs and monotone
limits; finite mean guarantees finite entropy. The initial bath entropy
is finite. Unitary entropy conservation, subadditivity and Araki--Lieb give
\[
 mS(\rho)-2S(E_m)\le\Ic(\rho^{\otimes m},\mathcal N_{km}^{\tau_\pi})
 \le mS(\rho).
 \tag{36}
\]
Divide by $km$. Extra data-independent calls may fill incomplete final
blocks; all count and do not alter already emitted output marginals.
\end{proof}

The initializer-independent code quantifier is pointwise in the normal initializer.
It is distinct from the uniform all-normal upper bound in Theorem~\ref{thm:upper}.
No invariant initializer is prepared. The boundary term in (29) is essential:
a global strict exponential contraction would contradict trace preservation
and $e^{tL}\ge I$.

\section{A non-scalar four-call construction that passes the coding bridge}

Let $\omega$ be the full nine-dimensional Cycle4 pair density, with
\[
 \delta=b^2/512,\quad q=1/2-\delta,\quad c=-b/128,\quad
 x=q^2,\quad y=q(1-q)/2,\quad z=(1-q)^2/4.
 \tag{37}
\]
Its exact bulk drift is $-\kappa_2 I$, where
$\kappa_2=ab^2/128$, and $S(\omega)>3+b^2/512$.
Define the actual four-call density
\[
 \rho_4=\omega\otimes\omega
       -xc(|1000\rangle\langle0100|+\mathrm{h.c.}).
 \tag{38}
\]
The span of $|1000\rangle,|0100\rangle$ is a reducing subspace of
$\omega\otimes\omega$. Its matrix has diagonal $xy,xy$ and off-diagonal
$xc$. Equation (38) deletes just that off-diagonal. Positivity, unit trace,
full rank and charge preservation follow, with
\[
 S(\rho_4)-2S(\omega)
 =2xy\left[1-h_2\left(\frac{1+c/y}{2}\right)\right]>0.
 \tag{39}
\]
This is an analytic entropy identity, not a finite precision assertion.

For the two-pair baseline the bulk drift is $-2\kappa_2 I$ on initial
depths at least four: after the first pair there is sufficient depth to
use the exact bulk identity for the second. The cross-amplitude identity
in (24), also verified by the complete four-call polynomial, therefore gives
\[
 D_s(\rho_4)\big|_{\ge4}
 =-2\kappa_2 I+2x\kappa_2(I-S_1S_1^*)
 \le-2\kappa_2(1-x)I<0.
 \tag{40}
\]
The two top-color sectors have distinct drift, so a scalar depth chain
cannot describe this construction. Nevertheless Theorem~\ref{thm:operatorcode}
applies with $k=4$, $\kappa=2\kappa_2(1-x)$, and proves
\[
 Q(V_s,\tau)\ge S(\rho_4)/4>S(\omega)/2
 \tag{41}
\]
with the same pointwise all-normal code quantifier. This is the actual
receiving use of the new operator criterion. The scientific point is the
extension beyond scalar depth dynamics; its small coefficient improvement
is not proposed as a practical throughput gain or as the next optimization.

\begin{corollary}[Sharp fixed-block order at the actual bath]\label{cor:theta}
For each fixed $k\ge2$, optimize the asymptotic coherent information
over nonidentical independent charge-preserving $k$-call block inputs,
using $\tau_\pi$. Its excess above $3/2$ is $\Theta_k(\beta^4)$ as
$\beta\downarrow0$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:upper} gives the upper order uniformly in the horizon.
For the lower, put $r=\lfloor k/2\rfloor$ copies of $\omega$ into a
block, and for odd $k$ append one $\rho_c$. This is a charge-preserving
density of entropy $3k/2+r[S(\omega)-3]$.
Its bulk drift is exactly $-r\kappa_2 I$ on initial depths at least $k$:
before each constituent pair there is at least the remaining maximum
downward propagation, so its bulk identity applies; the optional last
critical site has zero bulk drift. Complete positivity preserves constants
when these constituent duals are composed. All remaining effects are in
the actual finite boundary region. Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:operatorcode};
for $\tau_\pi$ it also proves the coherent-information limit
$S(\rho)/k>3/2+r\beta^4/(512k)$.
\end{proof}
For $k=1$ the stronger inherited identical-input theorem remains available;
the present nonidentical bound is an additional scoped upper, not a new
claim that its coefficient is sharp at $k=1$.

\section{The entropy Hessian and a genuine local-to-coding interface}

The four-call calculation can be stated as a general fixed-block
variational problem without averaging away bath-prefix dependence.
For initial depth at least $k$, the input-charge-compressed displacement
has an exact polynomial representation
\[
 \mathcal G_k(b)=\sum_{j=0}^k b^j\mathcal G_{k,j},\qquad
 \mathcal G_{k,0}=(2Q_k-kI)\otimes I.
 \tag{42}
\]
It acts on the input and the first $k$ bath colors, tensor identity on the
seed and untouched tail. To see finite-prefix sufficiency, every history
modifies at most $k$ prefix symbols. Equal-charge, common-output histories
have equal final depth; their overlap cancels the common untouched tail.
The parity argument makes total sine degree even, and the remaining cosine
degree is even as well. Substituting $\alpha^2=1-b$ gives (42).
The supplied code exports every coefficient for $k=4$.

Let $X,Y$ be fixed Hermitian, charge-preserving, trace-zero input matrices,
with $\Tr(Q_kX)=0$. Since $\sigma$ is scalar on each charge sector,
it commutes with both. For
$\rho_b=\sigma+bX+b^2Y$ and sufficiently small positive $b$, positivity
follows from the strict positivity of $\sigma$. Taylor expansion gives
\[
 S(\rho_b)=\frac{3k}{2}
 +b^2\left[m-\mathcal H_k(X)\right]+O_k(b^3),\quad
 m=\Tr(Q_kY),\quad
 \mathcal H_k(X)=\frac{\Tr(\sigma^{-1}X^2)}{2\ln2}.
 \tag{43}
\]
For clarity, along $\sigma+tX$, the first entropy derivative is
$\Tr(Q_kX)$ and the second is
$-\Tr(\sigma^{-1}X^2)/\ln2$. Formula (43) follows by applying this
second derivative to $bX+b^2Y$; no simultaneous diagonalization of $X$
and $Y$ is required.

Define the bath-prefix operator
\[
 \mathcal T_k(X)=\Tr_A[(X\otimes I)\mathcal G_{k,1}].
 \tag{44}
\]
The reference $\sigma$ has zero bulk drift at every $b$. Using (42),
\[
 D_s(\rho_b)\big|_{\ge k}
 =b^2[\mathcal T_k(X)+2mI]+O_k(b^3)
 \tag{45}
\]
in operator norm. The finite matrix makes the remainder uniform over
all deep bath vectors; it is not only an averaged asymptotic formula.

\begin{proposition}[Strict local direction yields actual codes]\label{prop:local}
If
\[
 \mathcal H_k(X)<m<-\tfrac12\lambda_{\max}(\mathcal T_k(X)),
 \tag{46}
\]
then choose
$Y=(4m/k)\sigma(Q_k-k/2)$.
For every sufficiently small fixed $\beta>0$,
$\rho_b$ is an admissible averaging input and yields actual reliable rate
\[
 \frac32+\frac{\beta^4}{k}
 [m-\mathcal H_k(X)]+O_k(\beta^6)
 \tag{47}
\]
with the pointwise all-normal arbitrary-reference code guarantee.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Under $\sigma$, $Q_k$ has mean $k/2$ and variance $k/4$, so the given
$Y$ has trace zero and $\Tr(Q_kY)=m$. Equation (46) gives a strict
negative leading operator in (45), hence a negative exact bulk drift for
all sufficiently small fixed $b$. Positivity and (43) hold in the same
neighborhood. Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:operatorcode}.
\end{proof}

The functional
\[
 \mathcal A_k=
 \sup_{\substack{X=X^*,\ [X,Q_k]=0\\
                  \Tr X=\Tr(Q_kX)=0}}
 \left\{-\tfrac12\lambda_{\max}(\mathcal T_k(X))
              -\mathcal H_k(X)\right\}
 \tag{48}
\]
is a per-block quantity; $\mathcal A_k/k$ supplies attainable per-call
leading coefficients by strict-margin approximation whenever the expression
is positive.
It is not asserted equal to full capacity, or to the complete block-input
optimum. Every $X$ and $k$ in this argument is fixed before the small-angle
limit. Uniform control when $k$ varies remains an additional obligation.

For diagnostics only, the uniform-prefix averaged four-call correlation
matrix
\[
 F_4=2^{-4}\Tr_{\rm prefix}\mathcal G_{4,1}+(2Q_4-4I)
 \tag{49}
\]
has 216 nonzero rational input entries,
$\Tr(\sigma F_4)=0$ and $\Tr(\sigma F_4^2)=561/512$.
The exact finite diagnostics for lengths two through six are retained.
They do not determine $\lambda_{\max}\mathcal T_k(X)$, a large-$k$
law or a coding rate. Replacing the operator in (46) by its trace
average would remove a load-bearing premise.

\section{Failures, primary comparison and the selected next operation}

\paragraph{Charge preservation is essential to this upper proof.}
For $k=1$, the valid density
$\rho=\rho_c+ig|0\rangle\langle1|-ig|1\rangle\langle0|$,
$0<g<1/(2\sqrt2)$, has exact drift
\[
 D_s(\rho)=\frac a2P_0-\alpha\beta g(S_1+S_1^*).
 \tag{50}
\]
On $(|j,w\rangle+|j,1w\rangle)/\sqrt2$, $|w|\ge1$, its expectation
is $-\alpha\beta g$, linear in $\beta$. Thus the bulk parity estimate
fails without the input-charge condition. This is not a capacity gain:
the input entropy is below the critical value, and the inherited iid
obstruction remains valid.

\paragraph{The root cannot be discarded.}
The positive margin (11), not bulk parity at vacuum, makes the global
upper estimate possible. For lower bounds, the finite boundary term (29)
prevents a false strict contraction of a normalized state to zero mass.
All root exchange amplitudes remain in the exact representation.

\paragraph{Four-call scalar closure is a falsified premise.}
Equation (24) is a concrete counterexample on this exact device.
The replacement is the operator inequality (27), and its actual use is
(38)--(41). An average over bath colors alone cannot verify it.

\paragraph{Fixed block length cannot improve the power in this class.}
The hoped-for quadratic gain from merely replacing pairs by a fixed
four-call charge-preserving product-block input is excluded by (3).
The same holds for every other fixed $k$, and for the controlled window
in Corollary~\ref{cor:log}. This closes that operation while leaving
unrestricted codes, feedback, non-charge-preserving block coherences and
longer temporal structure live. It does not convert an input-class
obstruction into a general capacity upper bound.

\paragraph{Primary comparison.}
Finite-error quantum code constructions are established
\cite{KLV,GBM,Tverberg}; the inherited compression lemma keeps their
approximation, coherent-initializer and normalization obligations.
Theorem 2 of \cite{GBM} assumes subexponential environmental dimension.
Here the full bath is infinite, and the new operator drift proves
the required input-relative overflow control instead.
Heisenberg-picture Lyapunov operators, tightness and invariant-state
criteria are also established \cite{Pan}. Our new model-specific work is
the homogeneous bounded-increment identity, its full-root code interface,
and the non-scalar example; general quantum Lyapunov reasoning is not
claimed as novel. Theorem 13 of \cite{KW} requires finite-dimensional
forgetful memory. No such premise is assumed or inferred from negative
drift of one averaging input. The present comparison is bounded primary
source work, not an exhaustive historical-priority assessment.

\paragraph{Scientific consequence and limits.}
Temporal correlations do beat the optimized iid comparator, as preserved
in Cycle4. Increasing to any fixed charge-preserving product-block length
cannot change the small-angle power beyond quartic. The lower proof no
longer needs scalar bath-depth dynamics and is used by an explicit
non-scalar four-call state. None of these facts gives efficient encoders,
useful finite blocklength, physical device performance or equality of
assisted and unassisted capacity. Small drift still creates very large
existence constants. All finite replay times are reported separately from
unknown discovery, inference and encoder-synthesis costs.

\paragraph{Chosen next question.}
Determine whether the operator variational benefit per call
$\mathcal A_k/k$ remains bounded as temporal length grows, or whether
there is a growing-block family with a genuinely stronger small-angle
gain. The next concrete operation is to control the full bath-prefix
operator $\mathcal T_k$ and the nonlinear remainder uniformly in $k$,
not merely its uniform-prefix trace.
Any apparent growth must survive positivity of the input, a strict exact
operator drift, and the actual coding limit with $\beta$ held fixed.
The controlled logarithmic window above is an existing restriction.
No current computation establishes divergence or its absence.
Protected programmes and previously closed physical methods retain
their owners and scopes.
