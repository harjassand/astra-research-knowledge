INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF ROOT'S MAXIMAL-ISOTROPIC / CLIFFORD-DIAGONAL EXTENSION
21 October 2026; independently checked critical chain after root's message.

VERDICT
The proposed theorem is valid as stated: the ordinary tensor witness holds
for every positive coefficient matrix diagonal in a common maximal commuting
Pauli algebra, with the existing one-chain constant C_D per task. It permits
arbitrary nonuniform spectra and a globally entangled stabilizer eigenbasis.
It does not prove the arbitrary-positive-matrix theorem or general iid E_C.
The route uses classical conditional entropies only after an exact algebraic
reduction. It never inserts the excluded quantum conditional coefficient-one
premise. It adds genuine Pauli-commutation compatibility, not scalar envelopes.

The elementary uniform-projector theorem in STABILIZER_PROJECTOR_THEOREM.txt
was found independently by this counterchallenge, before root supplied this
broader construction. That theorem has a sharp simpler restricted constant;
it is a subcase, not a competing resolution. No historical novelty claim is
made for either use of standard stabilizer linear algebra and Rado's lemma.

1. EXACT ALGEBRAIC IDENTIFICATION

Let D be prime, n=rN, and let L be an n-dimensional maximal isotropic subspace
of F_D^(2n). Its commuting Pauli algebra has a simultaneous orthonormal basis
indexed by characters t in L*, a vector space of dimension n. For a positive
matrix X in this algebra write its nonnegative diagonal entries x(t), and
normalize d^(-1)sum_t x(t)^2=1, d=D^n. Put c(t)=x(t)/sqrt(d), so that the
Schmidt/eigenvalue probability distribution is lambda(t)=c(t)^2 and its
entropy is exactly H(lambda).

For task a's first i physical qudits S_(a,i), let H_(a,i) be the subspace of L
whose Pauli labels vanish on S_(a,i). Normalized partial-trace expectation
E_(a,i) removes exactly the Pauli Fourier terms outside H_(a,i). Under the
character basis this is exactly uniform translation averaging of x(t) along

  V_(a,i)=H_(a,i)^perp subset L*.

No phase choice is lost: every maximal commuting Pauli group can be fixed
as a character group, and Fourier annihilator identities are then exact.
The normalized matrix Hilbert-Schmidt norm is the uniform classical L2 norm.
Thus the actual physical score equals ||E_(V_(a,i)) c||_2^2 with ordinary
counting measure for c. The subspaces V_(a,i) are nested increasing in i.

2. THE FULL VECTOR HALL CONDITION, NOT JUST PREFIX CHOICES

For arbitrary prefix lengths i_a, the intersection of H_(a,i_a) is the
subspace of L supported on the h=n-sum_a i_a remaining qudits. Being isotropic
there, it has dimension at most h. Therefore

  dim(sum_a V_(a,i_a))
    = n-dim(intersection_a H_(a,i_a)) >= sum_a i_a.          (A)

There are n vector-selection requests indexed by positions (a,j), j=1,...,N,
where request (a,j) permits any vector in V_(a,j). For an ARBITRARY subset T
of requests let i_a be the greatest selected j for task a, or zero if none.
Nestedness implies its permitted-subspace sum is exactly sum_a V_(a,i_a).
The number of selected requests from task a is at most i_a. By (A),

  dim(sum_((a,j) in T) V_(a,j)) >= sum_a i_a >= |T|.        (B)

This verifies every Hall/Rado subset, including holes in selected prefixes.
It is stronger than checking only cardinalities of complete prefixes.

3. SELF-CONTAINED FINITE VECTOR-HALL LEMMA

If finite subspaces S_1,...,S_k satisfy dim(sum_(j in J)S_j)>=|J| for every
J, one can select independent v_j in S_j. Proof by induction on k:

If a nonempty proper J is tight, let W=sum_(j in J)S_j, dim W=|J|. Induct
inside W to choose representatives for J. For any I in the complementary
indices, the sum of their images in the quotient by W has dimension

  dim(W+sum_(i in I)S_i)-dim W >= |I|,

by the original condition on J union I. Induct in the quotient, then lift;
these representatives together with the basis of W are independent.

If no nonempty proper subset is tight, every such subset has dimension at
least its cardinality plus one. Pick any nonzero v_k in S_k. In the quotient
by span(v_k), every subset of the first k-1 spaces still has dimension at
least its cardinality, since quotienting lowers dimension by at most one.
Apply induction and lift. The base k=1 is immediate. This proof works over
the finite field in use and requires no generic random-vector argument.

Apply this lemma to (B). The selected n independent vectors form a basis
v_(a,j) of L*. Define W_(a,i)=span{v_(a,1),...,v_(a,i)}. By nestedness,

  W_(a,i) subset V_(a,i).

Uniform translation expectations satisfy E_V E_W=E_V when W subset V, and
are orthogonal projections in classical L2. Consequently

  ||E_(V_(a,i))c||_2^2 <= ||E_(W_(a,i))c||_2^2.            (C)

Expressing t in the selected basis is a bijection of the D^n character labels.
It preserves the full eigenvalue multiset and entropy. The right-hand scores
of (C) are exactly ordinary coordinate-prefix averaging scores for r disjoint
classical D-ary tasks in this new basis. No product basis for the quantum
eigenvectors has been asserted.

4. THE REMAINING TENSORIZATION IS CLASSICAL AND VALID

For each classical task a, condition on all other task coordinates y. Write
q(y)=sum_x |c(x,y)|^2 and normalize c_y(x)=c(x,y)/sqrt(q(y)) whenever q(y)>0.
The existing source one-chain coherent-vector lemma gives

  H(X_a | X_rest=y) >= m sum_i ||Q_i c_y||_2^2 - C_D.

Multiplying by q(y) and summing yields

  H(X_a | X_rest) >= m sum_i ||Q_(a,i)c||_2^2 - C_D.

The exact equality of the averaged norms follows because Q_(a,i) acts only
on the x coordinates and Hilbert-Schmidt norm squares sum over y. There is
no state-dependent quantum conditioning map here. Finally the ordinary
classical entropy chain rule gives

  sum_a H(X_a|X_rest) <= H(X_1,...,X_r),

since conditioning on all other variables cannot increase entropy relative
to conditioning only on preceding variables. Combine this with (C) to obtain

  H(lambda) >= m sum_(a,i) p_(a,i)(X) - r C_D.

This completes the audited critical argument. Singular X/eigenvalues zero
are harmless: ignore zero-probability conditional slices. No full-rank
assumption or asymptotic continuity is needed at fixed finite dimension.

5. INDEPENDENT NONUNIFORM BELL-DIAGONAL CHECK

bell_diagonal_audit.py constructs actual four-dimensional matrices with the
entangled Bell eigenbasis, including lambda=(0.61,0.23,0.11,0.05), a zero
eigenvalue case, the uniform distribution and a rank-one case. Here n=2,
r=2,N=1. L is generated by XX and ZZ; restricting to either individual
qubit has full rank2, so V_(1,1)=V_(2,1)=L*. Actual physical scores are

  p_(1,1)=p_(2,1)=(sum_j sqrt(lambda_j))^2/4.

The independent coordinate averages selected by any two coordinate basis
vectors have score at least the actual full-space average, exactly as (C).
The script checks both actual partial traces and this domination. This is
an implementation check of the proof, not empirical support for arbitrary
positive matrices. Actual measured costs are in BELL_DIAGONAL_CHECK.json.

6. REMAINING GAP

General positive matrices need not lie in any commuting Pauli algebra; a
general unitary eigenbasis cannot be converted to this setting by a physical
tensor-factor relabeling. Pauli Fourier support, the isotropic support bound,
and the finite-field translation expectations are the real hypotheses.
The existence of an arbitrary spectral decomposition alone supplies none of
them. In particular this theorem does not establish general E_F additivity,
nor the unrestricted iid formation law. A rigorous extension should identify
which quantum compatibility invariant replaces (A) beyond stabilizer MASAs.
