PODS NO-GO FOR THE NESTED PREFIX DEPOLARIZATION SEMIGROUP

Scope and conventions
---------------------
Let D >= 2 and N >= 1 be integers, H = (C^D)^{tensor N}, q = D^N,
m = ln D, and let the sum defining the generator run over i = 1,...,N:

  E_i(X) = (I_{D^i}/D^i) tensor Tr_{1,...,i}(X),
  L = m sum_{i=1}^N (Id - E_i),
  Phi_t = exp(-t L).

The operator basis used below is an orthonormal Hilbert-space basis of H.
PODS means that every off-diagonal matrix unit in that basis is an
eigenoperator of Phi_t (with nonnegative eigenvalue), and that diagonal
operators map to diagonal operators. The no-go below needs only the first
of these requirements. No literature or theorem beyond the definition
supplied in the task is assumed.

Proposition
-----------
For every N >= 2 and every finite t > 0, Phi_t has no PODS basis.
Consequently there is no single basis making this semigroup PODS.

Proof
-----
Each E_i is the orthogonal projection, for the Hilbert-Schmidt inner
product, onto

  A_i = I_{D^i} tensor M_{D^{N-i}},

and E_i E_j = E_j E_i = E_max(i,j). Put E_0 = Id and

  P_k = E_{k-1} - E_k,  k = 1,...,N.

The P_k and E_N are mutually orthogonal projections summing to Id.
For X in ran P_k, E_i(X)=X when i<k and E_i(X)=0 when i>=k.
Therefore the exact spectral decompositions are

  L = sum_{k=1}^N m(N-k+1) P_k,

  Phi_t = E_N + sum_{k=1}^N exp[-tm(N-k+1)] P_k.

Because m>0 and 0<t<infinity, all the displayed eigenvalues of Phi_t
are distinct. Its fastest-decay eigenspace is ran P_1 = ker E_1.
Every other eigenspace is contained in ran E_1 = A_1: for k>=2,
E_1 P_k = P_k, and E_1 E_N = E_N.

Every nonzero operator in A_1 has the form I_D tensor B and has rank
D rank(B), hence cannot have rank one. It follows that EVERY rank-one
eigenoperator of Phi_t belongs to ker E_1 and has eigenvalue
exp(-tmN).

Suppose now that an orthonormal basis {|a>} made all off-diagonal
matrix units e_ab=|a><b|, a!=b, eigenoperators. They have rank one, so

  E_1(e_ab) = 0   for every a != b.

Let Delta denote the diagonal matrix algebra in this basis. The e_ab
span Delta's Hilbert-Schmidt orthogonal complement. Since E_1 is an
orthogonal projection and annihilates that complement,

  ran E_1 is contained in Delta.

Equivalently, for Y in ran E_1 and a!=b,

  <e_ab,Y>_HS = <E_1(e_ab),Y>_HS = 0.

Every pair of elements of Delta commutes, so ran E_1 would have to be
commutative. But

  ran E_1 = I_D tensor M_{D^{N-1}}

is noncommutative for N>=2. Explicitly, on any two-dimensional subspace
of the remaining N-1 factors, I_D tensor |0><1| and
I_D tensor |1><0| have a nonzero commutator. This contradiction proves
the proposition. QED.

Dimension cross-check
---------------------
All q(q-1) off-diagonal matrix units would have to fit in ker E_1,
whose dimension is q^2-(q/D)^2. For N>=3 this alone is impossible:

  q(q-1) > q^2-(q/D)^2.

For N=2 the dimensions coincide; the noncommutative-range argument is
what rules out that equality case. The argument above covers both cases.

Boundary cases and interpretation
---------------------------------
* N=1: Phi_t is the usual depolarizing semigroup, hence is PODS in every
  orthonormal basis, with off-diagonal eigenvalue exp(-tm).
* t=0: Phi_0=Id is PODS in every basis.
* t->infinity: the limiting channel E_N is completely depolarizing and
  is PODS in every basis, with off-diagonal eigenvalues zero. This limit
  does not contradict the no-go at every finite positive time.
* D=1: the Hilbert space is one-dimensional and the off-diagonal
  condition is vacuous; this degenerate case was excluded above.
* Adding i=0 to the sum changes nothing, since E_0=Id.

This resolves only PODS applicability for this particular nested-prefix
generator. It does not establish or refute a uniform tensor witness,
a conditional-entropy inequality, or applicability of any unrelated
entropy-production result.
