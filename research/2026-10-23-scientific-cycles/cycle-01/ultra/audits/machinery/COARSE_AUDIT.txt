INDEPENDENT AUDIT: CHUNK COARSE-GRAINING CONVERSE

Verdict
-------
The proposed quadratic-form comparison, entropy algebra, stopping sum,
and beta/(2 delta) leading lower bound are correct, conditional on the
quoted tensor LSI and the stated fixed-N iid formation/stopping bridge.
There are two precision requirements:

1. At Q=2, A_b=2/beta, NOT 2. The ratio before division by beta has
   continuous value 2.
2. Use an explicit choice such as b=ceil(log_D(1/delta)). Merely requiring
   b/log_D(1/delta)->1 does not guarantee the claimed logarithmic error.

The arbitrary-N identity-prefix embedding supplied in the follow-up is
also correct and removes the divisibility restriction entirely.

This is a correct but asymptotically losing attempt relative to the
stronger stopping-POVM converse reported by root: beta=2/(3 ln 2)<1,
whereas that converse has leading coefficient 1. No optimization of
source costs is established here; the statements are proof-level lower
bounds only.

1. Spectral ordering and comparison
----------------------------------
Use natural logarithms and let m=ln D. Initially let N=b n. On each
task chain, use a tensor operator basis with each local factor either
identity or traceless. If the first active factor is j, then E_i fixes
the basis operator for i<j and kills it for i>=j. Therefore

  H=m sum_{i=1}^N (Id-E_i)

has eigenvalue m ell, ell=N-j+1. The identity has ell=0.

Let c be the number of active aligned b-factor chunks. Every active
chunk intersects the suffix beginning at j, so

  c <= ceil(ell/b),    b c <= ell+b-1.

This remains valid for the identity (c=ell=0). H and the chunk generator
K are simultaneously diagonal in this basis. Summing over r tasks gives
the exact quadratic-form inequality

  b K <= H/m + r(b-1) Id.

Here H denotes the sum of the r chain generators. The Id on the right is
the identity superoperator, not a depolarizing expectation. Reversing
prefix/suffix or replacing ell by N-j would introduce an indexing error;
the displayed ordering is correct for E_1,...,E_N.

2. LSI normalization and entropy algebra
--------------------------------------
Let tau be normalized trace on dimension d=D^(rN), and normalize
tau(X*X)=1. Take exactly the supplied uniform tensor LSI, with

  Q=D^b,  beta=2/(3 ln 2),
  Ent_tau(X*X) <= A_b <X,KX>_tau,
  A_b=ln(Q-1)/[beta(1-2/Q)],  a_b=A_b/(b m).

This use requires the quoted result to hold uniformly for the product
of r n Q-level factors. A bound only for one Q-level depolarizing
generator would not suffice. No source-paper convention or theorem has
been independently verified in this audit: the displayed entropy/
Dirichlet normalization is the premise being audited.

The quadratic-form comparison yields

  Ent_tau(X*X) <= a_b <X,HX>_tau + a_b r(b-1)m.

Without tau(X*X)=1, multiply the additive term by tau(X*X).
At Q=2, l'Hopital gives

  lim ln(Q-1)/(1-2/Q)=2,
  A_b=2/beta.

In particular D=2,b=1 gives a_b=3, not 2/ln 2.

For rho=X*X/d, and for

  p_{s,i}=||E_{s,i}(X)||_(2,tau)^2,

the exact identities are

  Ent_tau(X*X)=rNm-S(rho),
  <X,HX>_tau=m[rN-sum_{s,i}p_{s,i}].

Thus the proposed sign pattern is correct:

  S(rho) >= a_b m sum_{s,i}p_{s,i}
            -(a_b-1)rNm-a_b r(b-1)m.

An application to differently defined stopping probabilities must
separately establish their identification with these p_{s,i}; the LSI
alone does not establish that bridge.

3. Stopping substitution and iid limit
-------------------------------------
Assume the supplied stopping/formation argument gives, after its
fixed-N iid limit, the bounds p_i >= [1-(i-1)delta]_+ in the required
per-task average. For N<=1+1/delta,

  sum_{i=1}^N p_i >= N-delta N(N-1)/2.

Substitution and division by r give exactly

  C_iid(tau) >= m[N-a_b delta N(N-1)/2-a_b(b-1)].

The positive-part truncation must be retained beyond N<=1+1/delta.
For every fixed delta choose finite b and N first, then take the iid
copy limit. Only afterward let delta tend to zero. This order uses no
uniform-in-N continuity assertion. This audit does not independently
prove the formation bridge or the operational meaning of C_iid.

4. Optimization and rounding
----------------------------
Write a=a_b and define F(N)/m=N-a delta N(N-1)/2-a(b-1). Its continuous
vertex and completed-square formula are

  v=1/(a delta)+1/2,

  F(N)/m=1/(2a delta)+1/2+a delta/8-a(b-1)
         -(a delta/2)(N-v)^2.

For a nearest multiple of b, |N-v|<=b/2; the rounding loss is at most
m a delta b^2/8. Choose

  b=ceil(log_D(1/delta)),   Q=D^b.

Then Q^-1<=delta and

  a_b=1/beta+O_D(delta),
  1/a_b=beta+O_D(delta).

Because beta<1, the vertex and its nearest multiple are positive and
below 1+1/delta for all sufficiently small delta: the upper-bound slack
is (1-beta)/delta+O_D(1), while rounding costs only O_D(log(1/delta)).
Consequently

  1/(2a_b delta)=beta/(2delta)+O_D(1),
  a_b(b-1)=O_D(log(1/delta)),
  a_b delta b^2=O_D(delta log^2(1/delta)),

and hence

  C_iid(tau) >= beta m/(2delta)-O_D(m ln(1/delta)).

In fact this explicit b choice gives the sharper bookkeeping form

  C_iid(tau) >= beta m/(2delta)
                -(1/beta) ln(1/delta)-O_D(1).

The symbol b approximately log_D(1/delta) should mean additive O_D(1)
here. For example subtracting sqrt(log_D(1/delta)) still gives ratio
one, but its coefficient error divided by delta can exceed every
logarithmic bound.

5. Arbitrary-N embedding: checked
--------------------------------
For any N, let k=(-N mod b), with 0<=k<=b-1. Prepend k mathematical
identity factors to EACH task chain:

  X'=(I_D^(tensor k))^(tensor r) tensor X,

with the obvious factor reordering that keeps each prefix with its
own chain. Use normalized trace on the enlarged space. Then

  tau'((X')*X')=tau(X*X),
  Ent_tau'((X')*X')=Ent_tau(X*X).

For each task, its first k prefix expectations fix X'; later prefix
expectations correspond exactly to the original E_1,...,E_N. Thus

  <X',H_(N+k)X'>_tau'=<X,H_N X>_tau.

Apply the divisible-length chunk inequality to X'. It gives the SAME
bound

  Ent_tau(X*X) <= a_b <X,H_N X>_tau+a_b r(b-1)m

for the original X and arbitrary N. There is no additional k m entropy
penalty: normalized relative entropy is unchanged; equivalently, both
log dimension and ordinary state entropy increase by r k m.

Therefore the finite-N stopping bound above holds for every admissible
integer N, and N may instead be rounded to the nearest integer to v.
The rounding loss then improves to at most m a_b delta/8. This
embedding changes neither the leading coefficient nor the logarithmic
defect. It is a mathematical embedding, not a physical source-preparation
protocol or an optimized source-cost claim.

6. Comparison with the stronger stopping converse
-------------------------------------------------
Root reports a separately derived simultaneous-stopping POVM bound

  C_iid >= m E[K_stop]-H(K_stop),

using a global separable comparator obeying
q(k_vector)<=D^(-sum k_s), and exact iid outcome entropy t H(K_stop).
Taking that stronger result as supplied, its reported leading behavior
is m/(2delta)-ln(1/delta), so it strictly beats this coarse LSI law at
order 1/delta because beta<1. The present audit verifies the coarse
argument; it does not independently audit that separate POVM proof.
The coarse argument should be retained as a correct losing attempt,
not presented as the strongest converse or as an optimal source cost.
