1. NOTATION AND PRECISE PRIMARY INPUT
Let m=ln D, E_i(X)=I_(D^i)/D^i tensor Tr_(first i) X, and
 H_(N,r)=m sum_(a=1)^r sum_(i=1)^N (Id-E_(a,i)).
For tau(X*X)=1 set Ent(X*X)=tau(X*X ln(X*X)). Its quadratic energy is
 E_H(X)=m[rN-sum_(a,i)||E_(a,i)X||_2^2].

Primary input, read directly: Dong, Gao, Ou, Yao, Zhou,
"Dimension-Free Approximate Tensorization of Quantum Hypercontractivity for
Qudit Depolarizing Semigroups", arXiv:2606.17729v1 (16 June 2026),
https://arxiv.org/html/2606.17729v1 . Theorem 3.12, equation (7), gives
 alpha_2(L^(s)) >= beta alpha_2(L), beta=2/(3 ln 2),
for reversible PODS semigroups. The simple Q-dimensional tracial depolarizer
L=Id-tau(.)I is an eligible example. Its single-site constant is
 alpha_2(L)=(1-2/Q)/ln(Q-1), with Q=2 limit 1/2;
see the paragraph after Lemma 2.18, citing Beigi-Datta-Rouze, Theorem 25.
Their Definition 2.5 and Section 2.4 give Ent_2,I/Q(X)=Ent_tau(X^2) and
E_2,L(X)=tau(X L(X)); therefore no extra factor 2 enters our conventions.
The theorem concerns positive matrices; the accepted polar/positive reduction
in warm_frozen/RESULT.txt extends our final nonnegative-prefix witness to all
coefficient matrices. This recent preprint is an external theorem dependency,
not independently re-proved or claimed historically new here.

The original single-site primary source is Beigi, Datta, Rouze,

753-794, 2020). Its Theorem 25, equation (43), was read directly and

2. WHY THE RECENT PODS THEOREM DOES NOT APPLY DIRECTLY
Full independently audited proof is in PODS_LEMMA.txt.
Put E_0=Id. The orthogonal spectral projections of H_(N,1) are
 F_j=E_(j-1)-E_j (1<=j<=N), and E_N for the scalar eigenspace.
On F_j the eigenvalue is m(N-j+1). All these values are distinct.
For j>=2, ran F_j is contained in ran E_1=I_D tensor M_(D^(N-1));
the same is true of the scalar eigenspace. Every nonzero operator in that
algebra has rank divisible by D. Hence any rank-one eigenoperator of H,
or of T_t=exp(-tH) at a finite t>0, belongs to ker E_1=ran F_1.
If an orthonormal basis {v_a} made all off-diagonal units |v_a><v_b|
eigenoperators, E_1 would annihilate all of them. Self-adjointness would
force every operator in ran E_1 to have zero off-diagonal entries in this
basis. This would make ran E_1 commutative. For N>=2 it is noncommutative.
Contradiction. Thus no such basis exists, even without the remaining PODS
hypotheses about diagonal preservation or positivity of eigenvalues.
The N=1 depolarizer and t=0/infinite-time boundary cases are excluded.

3. EXACT ULTRACONTRACTIVE ENDPOINT
Let T_t=exp(-tH_(N,1)), u=e^(-tm). Nested projections give the exact expansion
 T_t=u^N Id+(1-u) sum_(i=1)^N u^(N-i) E_i.                 (A)
Let q=D^N, ||.||_2^2=Tr(.)*.()/q, and ||.||_infinity the operator norm.
Then
 ||T_t||_(2->infinity)^2
   =(D v)^N+(1-v)sum_(l=0)^(N-1)(D v)^l,
 v=e^(-2tm).                                               (B)
For r task blocks the squared norm is the r-th power of (B).

Proof. Because T_t is CP, the induced norm may be maximized on positive
inputs (this also follows from the same positive-block Holder argument used
in the accepted reduction). For positive A,
 ||T_t A||_infinity=sup_(rho pure) Tr[rho T_t A].
Hilbert-space duality with normalized trace implies
 ||T_t||_(2->infinity)^2=q sup_(rho pure) Tr[rho T_(2t)(rho)].
The same equality can be obtained by taking the Hilbert-Schmidt adjoint of
the functional A -> Tr[rho T_t(A)], using T_t*=T_t. Each reset term satisfies
 Tr[rho E_i(rho)]=D^(-i)Tr[(Tr_prefix i rho)^2]<=D^(-i).
Every term is simultaneously saturated by a product rank-one rho. Substituting
(A) at time 2t proves (B). For r blocks, expand T_(2t)^(tensor r); each term
resets the union of chosen prefixes. Its overlap is at most the reciprocal
of that reset dimension, again simultaneously saturated by a rank-one state
product across all D-level factors. The coefficients factor, proving the
r-th-power statement without a general channel-norm multiplicativity premise.

In particular, at t=1/2, v=1/D and
 ||T_(1/2)^(tensor r)||_(2->infinity)^2=[1+(1-1/D)N]^r.     (C)
For t>1/2 the norm is bounded uniformly in N; for t<1/2 it grows exponentially
in N. Thus a method that requires a uniform finite endpoint 2->infinity norm
AT t=1/2 necessarily fails and its logarithmic norm cost is Theta(r ln N).
This does not disprove critical defective log-Sobolev: that statement does
not itself require finite uniform 2->infinity norm at a finite time.

4. COARSE-GRAINED DEFECTIVE INEQUALITY (TRUE BUT LOSING ATTEMPT)
Choose b>=1, Q=D^b, beta=2/(3 ln 2), and define
 A_b=ln(Q-1)/[beta(1-2/Q)],
 a_b=A_b/(b m).
The continuous value at Q=2 is A_b=2/beta (NOT 2).
For every N,r and every normalized coefficient matrix X,
 Ent(X*X) <= a_b E_H(X)+a_b r(b-1)m.                       (D)
Consequently, for every r-task bipartite state omega,
 E_F(omega) >= a_b m sum_(a,i)Tr[P_i^(a)omega]
                 -(a_b-1)rNm-a_b r(b-1)m.                (E)
All joint prefixes, output correlations, and entanglement within a party
are allowed. The right side can be negative; it is still a valid witness.
Unlike the actual target, the extensive negative term is present because
a_b approaches 1/beta>1 rather than 1.

Proof first when b divides N and X>=0. Partition each task chain into
contiguous b-factor chunks. Let F_chunk depolarize that entire chunk,
leaving all other factors unchanged, and K=sum_chunks(Id-F_chunk).
Use a normalized tensor operator basis, identity on each factor together
with a traceless orthonormal basis. A nonidentity word in one chain whose
first nonidentity is at position j has H/m eigenvalue ell=N-j+1.
If k chunks are active, they all lie among the final ceil(ell/b) chunks,
so bk<=ell+(b-1). Summing over r chains gives the quadratic form inequality
 b K <= H/m+r(b-1)Id.                                     (F)
The primary depolarizing tensor theorem, applied to all rN/b chunks of
equal dimension Q, yields Ent(X^2)<=A_b tau[X K(X)].
Combining with (F) proves (D).

For arbitrary N, prepend k=(-N mod b) identity matrix factors to every
chain. This mathematical embedding X'=I_D^(tensor kr) tensor X preserves
normalized squared norm and Ent. The first k new prefix expectations act
as the identity; each later prefix is exactly the corresponding old one.
Therefore E_H(X')=E_H(X), with no padding energy cost. Apply the divisible
case to X', obtaining exactly (D) with the SAME r(b-1)m defect.

For general X, the accepted lossless positive reduction selects Y=|X| or
|X*| with identical singular values and a no smaller total prefix score.
Thus Ent(Y^2)=Ent(X*X) and E_H(Y)<=E_H(X); (D) for Y proves (D) for X.
For pure coefficient states S=Nr m-Ent and E_H=m(rN-sum p), giving (E).
Convex-roof averaging proves it for mixed omega. QED.

5. OPERATIONAL FORMATION CONSEQUENCE AND ORDER OF LIMITS
Let an exact initialized normal memory tau produce exact rank-D Bell output
on its first block use and let its complete-memory half-trace return error
be delta. Inherited stopping-map reasoning gives the actual joint-prefix
probabilities p_i >= [1-(i-1)delta]_+. There is no extra supply during reuse.

Define C_iid(tau) operationally: infimum limsup_t ln M_t/t over normal LOCC
protocols supplying Phi_(M_t) and preparing sigma_t with
 T(sigma_t,tau^(tensor t))<=epsilon_t ->0.
Every branch and retained shared register counts. This definition does not
assume that E_F regularization characterizes infinite normal memories.
Apply the fixed-N output device independently to the approximate t-copy
initialization. The total positive witness has spectral width <=a_b m tN,
so its expectation changes by at most a_b m tN epsilon_t. Preparation and
normal LOCC cannot create output Schmidt number above M_t; hence output
E_F<=ln M_t. Applying (E), dividing by t, and fixing N,b BEFORE t->infinity,
 C_iid(tau) >=a_b m sum_i p_i-(a_b-1)Nm-a_b(b-1)m.          (G)
No approximate preparation is asserted to have exact Bell output.
For every N<=1+1/delta this implies
 C_iid(tau)>=m[N-a_b delta N(N-1)/2-a_b(b-1)].             (H)
Take N nearest to N*=1/(a_b delta)+1/2. N is arbitrary by padding above,
so rounding costs at most m a_b delta/8. Whenever this rounded N is in the
stated range, completing the square yields
 C_iid(tau)>=m[1/(2a_b delta)+1/2+a_b delta/8
                         -a_b(b-1)-a_b delta/8]
             =m[1/(2a_b delta)+1/2-a_b(b-1)].             (I)
A rounded N could have a smaller rounding loss than the stated bound; (I)
is a conservative bound. Integer rounding admissibility holds for small
delta, since a_b>1 and N* is separated from 1/delta by order 1/delta.

For 0<delta<=1/4 choose b=ceil(log_D(1/delta)), Q^-1<=delta.
Then a_b=1/beta+O_D(delta) and b=O_D(ln(1/delta)). Thus
 C_iid(tau)>= beta m/(2delta)-O_D(m ln(1/delta)).           (J)
For J original rank-d outputs grouped into one block, D=d^J and m=J ln d.
Fixed delta=0 permits arbitrary N in (H) and forces infinite finite-rate
formation cost. No finite-time or hardware efficiency claim follows.

6. DECISIVE MATCHED COMPARATOR: WHY (J) IS NOT RETAINED AS AN ADVANCE
Root supplied a stronger universal stopping-entropy converse. I independently
checked the essential argument. Let K in {0,...,N} be the disjoint stopping
outcome of the JOINT nested Bell projectors, so E K=sum_i p_i. For t copies,
the POVM effect for k_vector is bounded by the rectangular Bell projector
of total rank D^(sum_a k_a). Every state separable across all A:all B has
 q(k_vector)<=D^(-sum_a k_a).
Classical relative entropy therefore gives
 D(p' || q)>=m E_(p')sum_a k_a-H(p').
At exact tensor output p'=p^(tensor t), this is
 t[m E K-H(K)].
For approximate t-copy initialization, global trace error epsilon_t controls
the classical score by m tN epsilon_t and entropy by
 epsilon_t t ln(N+1)+h_2(epsilon_t).
At fixed N these errors vanish per copy. Any separable input comparator is
LOCC-mapped to a globally separable output, or equivalently finite-output
relative entropy of entanglement is <=ln M_t. Hence
 C_iid(tau)>=m sum_i p_i-H(K)
            >=m sum_i p_i-ln(N+1).                       (K)
For delta>0 choosing N near 1/delta yields
 C_iid(tau)>=m/(2delta)-O_D(ln(1/delta)).
This has the optimal leading coefficient 1/2 and strictly dominates (J)
as delta tends to zero. General E_R additivity is NOT required: the relative
entropy bound was applied directly to the entire t-copy output and a globally
separable comparator. The exact critical tensor witness is still stronger
because it would remove the diverging H(K) allowance, replacing it by a
D-dependent constant per task. No claim about that critical improvement is
made here. Complete details and ownership of the stopping converse remain
with root's principal proof; this paragraph is an independent scope check.
