CLAIM
For the specified fixed d=3 tree unitary, the specified diagonal stationary normal initializer, and deterministic n-forward-slot quantum transmission with arbitrary local ancillas and unlimited noiseless BACKWARD CLASSICAL feedback, the feedback capacity equals 3/2 qubits per forward slot. For every logical dimension K, its entanglement fidelity obeys

    F_e <= min{1, floor((2 sqrt(2))^n)/K}.

The new step is an explicit branchwise causal-circuit rearrangement. It does NOT assert that there is a single ordinary encoder and decoder for the adaptive protocol. There is a different encoder for each complete classical transcript. Trace preservation is used only after summing all transcript branches.

1. GENERAL SETUP AND THE ACTUAL BOTTLENECK
Let a causal n-slot device have quantum input ports X_1,...,X_n and output ports Y_1,...,Y_n. Its ordinary simultaneous-input block channel is N_n. The actual device may have arbitrarily large coherent memory between slots. Let {C_z} be any Kraus representation of N_n in which rank(C_z)<=M for every z.

Claim: with no preshared entanglement, no feedback of quantum systems, and no other forward communication, every n-slot adaptive classical-feedback transmission protocol has a Kraus decomposition whose members have rank at most M. Consequently F_e<=M/K.

This is a property of a representation of the entire block channel, not a claim about Kraus rank of each one-use marginal. It can therefore be informative for a correlated memory process. It does not assert that feedback never improves quantum capacity: many familiar noisy channels have a full-rank Kraus member, making this particular bound no stronger than the port-dimension ceiling.

2. EXPLICIT CAUSAL MODEL
Alice initially holds logical system L and arbitrary private ancillas; Bob has independent local ancillas. The state of the device memory is independent of these and of an arbitrary reference R entangled with L. A shared random seed, if supplied through the backward classical channel, can be included in the transcript.

Immediately before slot i, Alice applies a CPTP map

    A_i^(t_<i): A_(i-1) -> A_i X_i.

Its classical control is the full feedback transcript already received. Alice may measure locally and retain private outcomes; these are included in A_i's memory or in its local discarded environment. Immediately after receiving Y_i, Bob applies an instrument

    {B_i^(t_i | t_<i): B_(i-1) Y_i -> B_i}_{t_i},

retains its outcome, and sends the classical value t_i to Alice. Bob's memory includes previous outputs and all retained records. A final CPTP decoder, also depending on the transcript, produces Lhat of dimension K. Arbitrarily many feedback messages between adjacent forward slots can be grouped into t_i. Initial and final feedback can be included as additional local stages. The n quantum forward slots remain the sole forward communication resource.

3. FROZEN-TRANSCRIPT REARRANGEMENT LEMMA
First take finite or countable feedback alphabets and finitely many local stages. Fix a complete transcript t. DO NOT normalize the corresponding branch.

Run Alice's prescribed maps with those classical controls, keeping each emitted X_i in an external register rather than passing it to the device yet. Trace her final private memory. This defines a CPTP block encoder

    E_t: L -> X_1 ... X_n.

Run Bob's branch operations on Y_1,...,Y_n in their usual order, keep exactly the outcome sequence t, and perform his final decoder. This defines a CP trace-nonincreasing block map

    D_t: Y_1 ... Y_n -> Lhat.

The unnormalized branch map in the actual adaptive experiment is exactly

    Lambda_t = D_t o N_n o E_t.                 (1)

Proof: purify local maps, or refine them into local Kraus operators. After t is fixed, all classical controls are fixed labels, not wires carrying quantum systems. Alice's later local operations act only on her retained memory and future input ports. They commute with every earlier device interaction and every Bob operation, whose quantum supports are disjoint. They can thus be moved before the device interactions while preserving their order on Alice's memory. Bob's earlier branch operations act on his memory and completed output ports. They commute with all later device interactions and Alice operations, and can be moved after the device interactions while preserving their order on Bob's memory. The device interactions retain their original order, giving its same global block operator W_n. Finally take all local partial traces. This proves (1) as an equality of CP maps, also when tensoring with an arbitrary reference.

A possible concern is measurement steering of Alice's retained memory. It does not invalidate this rearrangement: a fixed unnormalized Bob Kraus operator commutes with Alice operators on disjoint quantum subsystems, even when their states are entangled. Normalizing a conditional state during the rearrangement would be invalid and is expressly not done.

There need not be one E independent of t. There is no assertion that the physically conditioned Alice state before every slot equals that of the artificially frozen encoder by itself. Equality concerns the full unnormalized circuit with the matching Bob branch applied.

4. ALL BRANCHES, RANK, AND NORMALIZATION
Choose Kraus operators E_(t,alpha) and D_(t,beta) for the two maps in (1). Then

    F_(t,beta,z,alpha) = D_(t,beta) C_z E_(t,alpha)       (2)

are Kraus operators for the complete adaptive channel after its classical transcript is ignored. Every one has rank at most M. The physical causal protocol is deterministic, so its complete channel is trace preserving:

    sum_(t,beta,z,alpha) F* F = I_K.                     (3)

Equation (3) follows from the actual sequential CPTP maps and complete receiver instruments, or equivalently by summing their unnormalized branches. It is NOT derived by assuming that the same encoder occurs in every branch. One should not replace E_t by a single E or separately normalize any D_t branch.

For the normalized maximally entangled input on R L,

    F_e = K^(-2) sum_(t,beta,z,alpha) |Tr F|^2
        <= M K^(-2) sum_(t,beta,z,alpha) Tr(F*F)
        = M/K.

The inequality follows from |Tr F|<=sum singular values and Cauchy-Schwarz on at most rank(F)<=M nonzero singular values. All terms are nonnegative; countable sums are harmless on the finite-dimensional logical space. No factor depending on the number of transcripts is incurred. Retaining a copy of the transcript cannot help evade this bound: the decoder may use it before producing Lhat, and the fidelity of Lhat with R is the sum over all branches.

Equivalent invariant: the final normalized R:Lhat state has Schmidt number at most M, since (2) supplies an ensemble of vectors with Schmidt rank <=M. This invariant is transcript-aware and survives arbitrary Bob-dependent choices of E_t because the branches are mixed classically rather than superposed coherently.

5. UNLIMITED ALPHABETS AND ANCILLAS
No finite alphabet rate penalty is used. For general measurable classical outcomes, Bob's entire local receiver strategy defines a quantum instrument on the finite-dimensional space Y_1...Y_n, with output Lhat and transcript t. Its Choi-valued measure has a density relative to the finite scalar measure obtained by taking its trace. Thus D_t can be interpreted as a measurable CP density, and the complete adaptive map is

    Lambda = integral D_t o N_n o E_t dmu(t).

The same frozen-control commutation holds pointwise almost everywhere. Each integrand has output Choi state in the cone of states with Schmidt number at most M. In finite dimension that cone is closed and convex: normalized vectors with Schmidt rank at most M form a compact set, and its convex hull is compact. Integrals, and limits of finite or countable physical protocols, remain in that cone. The normalized final state's overlap with Phi_K is therefore <=M/K. This avoids any cardinality count or assumption of a finite transcript alphabet.

Arbitrary local ancillas do not change the finite input/output domains of the collapsed maps E_t and D_t; their discarded private systems are already traced out. Operational limits of protocols inherit the inequality by continuity on finite R Lhat. Ordinary unlimited classical feedback is therefore included. A genuinely coherent backward quantum wire would prevent the local commutations and is excluded by the problem statement.

6. APPLICATION TO THE ACTUAL TREE, NOT A FINITE SUBSTITUTE
The source proves, for the same fixed tree device W_n and vertices v,w,

    A_(w,v) = <w| W_n |v>,
    rank A_(w,v) = #{length-n walks from v to w}
                  <= (2 sqrt(d-1))^n.

The rank equality follows because A_(w,v) is a partial permutation: distinct input words ending at w have distinct output words by invertibility of the full permutation W_n. The uniform bound follows from the weighted Schur estimate for the adjacency operator of the infinite d-regular tree, with h(v)=(d-1)^(-depth(v)/2), which gives ||H||<=2 sqrt(d-1). This is the exact owner bound, not a small-tree numerical estimate.

For the actual fixed diagonal normal initializer,

    C_(v,w) = sqrt(pi(v)) A_(w,v)

is a countable Kraus family of N_n, with rank bounded by M_n=floor((2 sqrt(2))^n). Use this same N_n and its same initializer in (1) for every t. Applying Sections 3-5 gives

    F_(e,n) <= min{1, M_n/K_n}
            <= min{1, 2^[n(3/2 - R_n)]},
    R_n = log2(K_n)/n.

Every fixed rate strictly above 3/2 has fidelity tending to zero exponentially. All n forward qutrit slots and all feedback outcomes are counted. No bath access, bath reset, bath measurement, bath replacement, fresh internal bath cell, preshared entanglement, or quantum feedback is introduced. Conditioning on w is only a mathematical Kraus decomposition after tracing the final bath; no party observes w.

7. MATCHING LOWER BOUND AND ANSWER
The existing colored Dyck code is still a legal protocol when backward classical feedback is available. For n=2m it has exact dimension

    K_m = Catalan_m * 2^m,

and the source establishes a bath-independent output basis injection with exact bath return at block end, preserving arbitrary reference entanglement. Since log2(K_m)/(2m)->3/2, it gives the matching achievable rate. Thus the answer under the stated deterministic fixed-n transmission definition is

    Q_backward-classical = 3/2 qubits per forward use.

There is no feedback advantage for this exact device under this contract.

8. OPTIONAL EXTENSION AND LIMITS
The same argument extends the owner's finite-vertex-support approximation to any fixed normal coherent initializer. If P has rank r and Tr(P tau)>=1-epsilon, the truncated process has a Kraus representation of ranks <=r M_n. The whole adaptive protocol is one CPTP map from its initial bath to the final decoded state, so initial trace distance bounds its fidelity change uniformly in n. This yields

    F_e(tau) <= min{1, r M_n/K + sqrt(epsilon)}.

Fix P before taking n to infinity, then enlarge P. This gives the same strong converse for any fixed normal initializer. It is not needed for the specified stationary diagonal bath and does not authorize horizon-dependent initialization.

Scope is fixed n, or protocols with a maximum of n forward slots padded to n. An alternative definition allowing only an expected communication-cost constraint with unbounded stopping times is not established here. The actual task specified n forward slots and a deterministic recovered transmission channel.

This trial gives a complete mathematical argument, subject to independent checking of the frozen-transcript lemma and its measurable-outcome formulation. It is not external certification or a historical-priority claim. The owner source already established the walk rank and Dyck lower bound; the novel trial obligation is the transcript-dependent factorization and its global normalization, supplied explicitly above.

9. FAILURE AND CHECK RECORD

Rejected shortcut: assume one global encoder and one decoder for the feedback protocol. That is false in general. The proof instead retains E_t for every full transcript.
Rejected shortcut: apply a rank-fidelity estimate independently to normalized successful branches and add unweighted bounds. This would mishandle branch probabilities. Equations (2)-(3) keep every branch unnormalized and use one global trace-preserving sum.
Rejected shortcut: replace classical feedback by a coherent delayed measurement carrying the feedback register quantumly. That enlarges the resource model. The rearrangement is performed separately on genuinely classical transcript branches.

A direct finite two-slot algebraic check is provided in check_factorization.py and CHECK_OUTPUT.txt. It uses a nonroot pure vertex, a random first sender isometry, a random receiver measurement, and transcript-dependent second sender isometries. The outcome probabilities depend on the logical input, so feedback is informative in the diagnostic. It tests only the new circuit-rearrangement identity for a nontrivial adaptive sender, not the asymptotic capacity. The capacity proof above uses the exact infinite-tree bound.
