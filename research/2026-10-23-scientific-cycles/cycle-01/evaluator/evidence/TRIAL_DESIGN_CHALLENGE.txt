PRE-OUTCOME CHALLENGE OF WARM VS COMPLETE-HANDOFF TRIAL
2026-10-21

Read: trial/PREREGISTRATION.json only. No trial outputs, participant arguments,
continuation results or sibling artifacts were read for this challenge.

VERDICT
The design is defensible as a bounded operational comparison on two difficult,
real obligations. It is not a population estimate, a native Pro/Sol comparison,
or a clean estimate of hidden-memory benefit. The registration already states
those limits and correctly preserves null and saturated outcomes. No fatal
confound requires abandoning the trial. The following safeguards improve the
interpretability of the result without changing its scientific tasks.

1. FREEZE THE ACTUAL VISIBLE SCIENTIFIC STATE
Freeze not only created files but the first-phase final response, observable
commands/results, failed attempts and explicit unresolved obligations that the
warm continuation can use. Do not request hidden chain-of-thought. If a useful
visible derivation exists only in a message, save it for the fresh arm. Record
exact source/artifact hashes and freeze only after the initial agent and its
processes finish or are explicitly checkpointed. Otherwise late writes create
different scientific inputs.

Warm context still contains internal state unavailable in a visible handoff.
That is the operational treatment, not a repairable information-equivalence
claim. Describe the result as continuing versus reconstructing from the complete
visible checkpoint. Do not claim equal actual working information or isolate
the effect of cache, latent reasoning, attention, prompt length or memory.

2. IDENTICAL INSTRUCTIONS AND ACTUAL RESOURCE ACCOUNTING
Give both arms the same continuation instruction and same permitted source set.
Use separate output directories and forbid access to sibling outputs. Keep source
files immutable. The warm arm should not receive a tailored correction that is
absent from the fresh prompt. Any late clarification must reach both.

The current budgets are requested caps. Record actual start/end, source-reading
overhead, tool count, spawned process CPU and violations. If one arm exceeds 360
seconds or 12 calls, do not report that pair as strictly cost-matched. Preserve the
overrun as an observation; do not selectively discard an inconvenient arm. Equal
wall/tool caps cannot equalize unavailable inference tokens. Where practical run
the arms contemporaneously with single-threaded numerical jobs, or record order
and contention; a sequential cache/network difference is another operational
cost, not an intelligence effect.

3. MAKE 'ADVANCE' TASK-SPECIFIC BEFORE OUTPUTS
The broad primary endpoint permits post hoc preference. Freeze a short acceptance
checklist now, without guessing the answer.

Moment task: an accepted result must use the actual first k64 Jacobi input,
declared exact threshold and proved support. It must construct a positive feasible
measure that matches the retained information, with a checkable representation,
and either matching dual bounds or two admissible measures proving a nontrivial
actual-data ambiguity. Inclusive-CDF endpoint atoms and limiting nonattainment
must be treated correctly. A numerical optimizer status, a restated CMS floor or
ordinary diagonalization of the original matrix does not solve extremality over
the retained-moment class. Classical theory can solve the obligation without
being a new theorem; correctness and historical novelty are separate endpoints.

Mixing task: an accepted advance must concern the original fixed-alpha lazy
mixture, proposal start, both population KL budgets and J>K. It must narrow the
existing bracket or prove a structural/order statement or counterconstruction
that changes a specific unresolved premise for that contract. Re-proving the
forward-only counterexample, improving a spectral gap without the initial-law
connection, or showing extra finite-state plots is insufficient. If a valid but
restricted lemma is obtained, grade its exact restriction rather than treating
it as a resolution of the worst-case coefficient.

For both tasks, a complete proof is primary evidence; computational checks can
falsify and support it but cannot fill a missing universal quantifier. Failed and
incomplete attempts remain part of the outcome. Do not require all arms to produce
a result within one run merely to make the table look complete.

4. BLINDED SCIENTIFIC ADJUDICATION
If feasible, use one fresh fork-none reviewer given anonymized A/B artifacts,
the same original sources and the frozen checklist. Do not give the role labels,
elapsed times or preferred hypothesis until mathematical judgments are written.
Remove identifying path names from the review copy without changing arguments
or code. Then attach resource receipts and integrate. If blinding is infeasible,
state root's treatment knowledge and allow it as a limitation; do not label the
review independent/blind merely because another agent performed it.

5. WHAT THIS TRIAL CAN ESTABLISH
It can show that, at these particular frozen hard checkpoints, a certain
continuation procedure produced a checked new contribution, defect correction,
or no advance within the recorded resources. It can document reconstruction loss
or a useful change in line of attack. It cannot establish a general reset policy,
optimal subagent count, recursive intelligence amplification or exponential growth.
One paired observation per heterogeneous task is too little for those claims.

The proposed tasks address a real information/theorem bottleneck, not the earlier
saturated toy. If both fail, examine the actual failed obligations and treat the
null as evidence against a large immediate benefit on these tasks. Do not infer
that the scientific problem is impossible or that fresh/warm methods are globally
equivalent. If an initial phase solves its obligation, follow the registered
saturation rule rather than manufacturing a second easy success.

RECOMMENDED MINIMAL AMENDMENT
Before phase two, add the exact frozen-visible-state list, the task-specific
acceptance checklist and actual cap-recording rule. Keep all other design choices
unchanged. Complete source handoff and honest negative outcomes are more important
than increasing the number of arms during this pilot.
