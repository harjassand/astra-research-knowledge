1. PRECISE GENERALIZABLE LEMMA

Let a fixed causal n-slot device have finite-dimensional ports X_i,Y_i and an
initial memory independent of the logical system, its reference, and the two
laboratories. Let N_n: X_1...X_n -> Y_1...Y_n be its simultaneous-input block
channel, retaining all completed outputs. Suppose N_n admits a Kraus
representation {C_z} with rank(C_z) <= M for every z.

Use the device in its original chronological order, with arbitrary local
ancillas and local normal quantum operations, genuinely classical noiseless
communication from Bob to Alice, no backward quantum wire, no prior shared
entanglement, and no other forward quantum communication. For any resulting
deterministic logical channel Lambda: L -> Lhat with dim L=dim Lhat=K,

  Schmidt-number(J_Lambda) <= Schmidt-number(J_N_n) <= M,
  F_e(Lambda) <= min{1,M/K}.

The first inequality uses the entire causal block channel, with the same
initial memory in every branch. It is not a bound obtained by multiplying
ranks of one-slot reduced channels. Neither independence of different slots
nor stationarity is required. The minimum over Kraus representations of the
largest individual operator rank is precisely the channel Choi state's
Schmidt number. This is not the number of Kraus operators, which is often
called the channel's Kraus rank.

The lemma is useful when a uniformly bounded-rank representation of N_n is
available. By itself it is not an exact feedback-capacity formula for a
general channel, nor a statement that feedback cannot help in general.

2. CAUSAL COMMUTATION: THE ACTUAL LOGICAL CHECK

Fix a full classical transcript t, and do not divide by its probability.
At the circuit/Kraus level, Bob's recorded outcomes are now fixed labels.
Alice's choice of every controlled operation is fixed as well.

Alice's later operation A_j acts on her retained private memory and on the
future emitted port X_j. An earlier device interaction acts on its own memory
and its earlier port X_i, i<j. Bob's operations act on his memory and already
emitted outputs. These quantum supports are disjoint. Their operators commute,
even if the state on those supports is entangled. Move Alice's operations to
the beginning, preserving Alice's internal order.

Likewise Bob's operation after slot i acts on his retained memory and
completed Y_i. It commutes with later device interactions, which act on the
device memory and later ports. Move Bob's operations to the end, preserving
his internal order. None of these swaps changes the relative order of the
device interactions.

After tracing Alice's final local memory, the first collection is the
transcript-specific CPTP encoder E_t. Bob's fixed outcome branch and final
decoder form a CP trace-nonincreasing D_t in the discrete-outcome case.
The device remains the original N_n. Thus

  Lambda_t = D_t o N_n o E_t.

This equality is of unnormalized CP maps and remains true with an arbitrary
external reference. It does not say that E_t is the actual conditional
state-preparation map seen in the experiment before conditioning on t.

The potentially dangerous objection is remote steering: Bob's measurement
can change Alice's conditioned memory state. That objection fails here
because the proof compares fixed unnormalized linear operators, which commute
on disjoint supports. It would succeed against a proof that normalized each
intermediate branch, or replaced all E_t by one encoder. The trial explicitly
does neither.

A further possible objection is that Alice and Bob can generate and retain
entanglement using earlier forward slots. That is already included: Alice's
retained half is in her memory, Bob's half is in his memory, and all such
entanglement arose inside the same circuit being rearranged. Disjoint-support
commutation does not require separability at intermediate times.

For a general causal noisy device, purify its local interactions and retain
their discarded environments mathematically. They can be included with its
memory in the same argument. The argument requires the actual causal device
with the specified port structure; an arbitrary noncausal global channel
would not provide that operational premise.

3. INPUT-DEPENDENT TRANSCRIPT PROBABILITIES AND ALL BRANCHES

The probability of t is correctly

  p_t(rho) = Tr[D_t N_n E_t(rho)],

which may depend on rho. No assertion of an input-independent transcript
distribution is used.

For Kraus operators E_(t,alpha), D_(t,beta), and the same C_z for N_n, each
complete logical branch operator is

  F_(t,beta,z,alpha) = D_(t,beta) C_z E_(t,alpha).

It has rank at most M. The sum over every receiver outcome and every local
Kraus index is the actual deterministic physical channel, so

  sum F*F = I_K.

This follows from chronological instrument completeness. It does not follow
by incorrectly assuming that the E_t are identical, and it does not require
that the sum of E_t be trace preserving.

For the maximally entangled logical input,

  F_e = K^(-2) sum |Tr F|^2
      <= (M/K^2) sum Tr(F*F)
      = M/K.

Thus there is no transcript-count penalty. The probabilities, including rare
branches, already occur in the unnormalized operators. A collection of many
rank-M branches cannot implement the K-dimensional identity for K>M: its
decoded Choi state stays in the Schmidt-number-M cone.

The receiver may keep all transcript records and use them in the final
decoder. The target comparison is with the final K-dimensional quantum
output, after that use. Tracing unused records then sums branches exactly.
This does not discard a helpful record before decoding and does not give
success-only credit.

4. LOCAL MEMORY, COUNTABLE AND CONTINUOUS OUTCOMES

Arbitrary sender memory, private local measurements with retained records,
and Bob's joint processing of all previous outputs are represented by the
causal local maps. Once their internal systems are traced out, E_t and D_t
have finite-dimensional input/output spaces. Initial local mixed states can
be purified locally; shared randomness generated through feedback is another
classical transcript label. Prior shared entanglement would violate the
product-laboratory premise and is excluded.

For countably many outcomes, positive sums converge on the finite-dimensional
logical space and the displayed fidelity proof is valid directly.

For continuous outcomes, Bob's entire receiver strategy, regarded as acting
on all Y ports supplied at once, is a CP instrument D(S) with final output
Lhat and transcript outcome in a measurable set S. It exists independently
of Alice's choice of inputs. Its finite-dimensional Choi-valued measure is
dominated by the finite scalar measure mu(S)=Tr J_D(S). Entrywise
Radon-Nikodym derivatives give positive Choi densities almost everywhere,
hence CP maps D_t. Measurable feedforward controls supply measurable E_t.

The CP densities D_t need not be individually trace-nonincreasing relative
to this arbitrary reference measure. That fact is not needed: all
integrands are positive and their integrated output is normalized by the
actual deterministic experiment. The branch identity yields

  Lambda = integral D_t o N_n o E_t dmu(t).

Each integrand's Choi operator belongs to the cone of positive operators
with Schmidt number at most M. In finite dimension this cone is closed and
convex; on the trace-one slice it is the convex hull of a compact set of
bounded-Schmidt-rank pure states. Consequently positive integrals and
operational limits stay in the cone. The same fidelity bound follows.

This supplies a sound reading of the trial's measurable-outcome paragraph.
For incorporation, say "measurable controls and normal instruments, e.g.
standard Borel classical outcomes" and do not call their densities
trace-nonincreasing. Pathological nonmeasurable control rules or infinite
local procedures without a well-defined limiting channel are not physical
protocols included by that statement. Unlimited message size is otherwise
covered; there is no alphabet-size rate restriction.

5. TREE DEVICE, STATIONARY BATH, AND THE STRONG CONVERSE

The immutable source's tree unitary reverses directed edges. The root's
ports are 0,1,2; elsewhere port 0 points upward and ports 1,2 downward.
For its actual ordered product W_n and vertex endpoints v,w,

  A_(w,v) = <w|W_n|v>

is a partial permutation. Distinct input words ending at the same w must
have distinct output words because W_n is injective on the full basis.
Therefore its rank is exactly the number of n-step walks from v to w.

The weighted adjacency estimate with h(v)=2^(-depth(v)/2) gives
||H||<=2 sqrt(2). The root contributes 3/sqrt(2)<=2 sqrt(2), so its special
port convention does not spoil the estimate. Thus

  rank A_(w,v) <= floor((2 sqrt(2))^n) = M_n.

For the specified normal diagonal stationary initializer tau_pi,
C_(v,w)=sqrt(pi(v)) A_(w,v) is a countable Kraus representation. Its
completeness follows by tracing the same initialized bath; no branch
measurement or access to that bath is physically introduced. The stationary
distribution has pi(root)=1/3 at p=3/4, as the owner continuation requires.

The transcript lemma applies to this same N_n and this same initial bath
in every t branch. It is not legitimate to reset or choose a bath according
to t; the proof makes no such move. Initial/final vertex labels are Kraus
indices only, not information accessible to Alice or Bob.

At R_n=(log_2 K_n)/n >=3/2+delta eventually,

  F_(e,n) <= M_n/K_n <= 2^(-n delta).

This proves an exponential strong converse for the stated diagonal
initializer, and in fact for any independent diagonal normal initializer
of this same tree device. Stationarity is part of the owner's fixed example
but is not needed for this particular rank upper bound.

6. EXACT LOWER BOUND AND CAPACITY CONCLUSION

The source's length-2m colored Dyck words use pushes 1,2 and pops 0. Every
pop closes an edge opened earlier within that block, so no pop traverses
above the initial vertex. This also removes the root convention concern.

At each push the output is zero; at each matching pop the output reveals
that push's color. The output's zero/nonzero pattern recovers the parentheses,
and matching those parentheses recovers the input colors. Hence the map is
an input-basis injection independent of the initial vertex. The bath ends
exactly at that vertex.

By linearity,

  W_(2m)|_(code tensor E) = V_m tensor I_E

on the entire code subspace and bath Hilbert space. This is an exact quantum
code preserving arbitrary external entanglement, not just classical
distinguishability. It works without feedback and therefore remains legal
with feedback. Its dimension is

  K_m = Catalan_m * 2^m,
  lim log_2(K_m)/(2m) = 3/2.

Restricting to a power-of-two logical subspace loses less than one qubit
per block. Odd horizons can use the preceding even code and one unused
forward slot. The lower and upper bounds therefore match asymptotically.

No claim is established that M_n is the exact optimal finite-block dimension
or that its fidelity bound is tight at every n. The exact-capacity assertion
is the asymptotic threshold, with the stated zero-error sequence.

7. OPTIONAL COHERENT-INITIALIZER EXTENSION: EXACT REPAIR

The Section 8 sentence "If P has rank r and Tr(P tau)>=1-epsilon" is
underspecified. Replace that sentence and the subsequent rank justification
by the following:

"Choose a finite vertex set F, let
 P_F=sum_(v in F)|v><v|, r=|F|, q=Tr(P_F tau)>0, and put
 tau_F=P_F tau P_F/q.
 Every vector in the support of tau_F is a sum of at most r vertex basis
 vectors. Hence its vertex-conditioned channel Kraus operators are sums of
 at most r A_(w,v), and have rank at most r M_n. The transcript lemma gives
 F_e(tau_F)<=min{1,r M_n/K}.
 Projecting a purification gives
 (1/2)||tau-tau_F||_1<=sqrt(1-q).
 For a fixed feedback protocol its final decoded Choi state is a CPTP
 function of the initial bath, including all outcome branches. Therefore
 F_e(tau)<=min{1,r M_n/K+sqrt(1-q)}."

An arbitrary rank-r projection can have vectors supported on infinitely
many vertices; rank r alone does not justify the finite sum of r
vertex-conditioned Kraus matrices. The correction is to use coordinate
projections as in the original source, not to assert an unproved rank bound
for arbitrary projections. I do not claim an actual counterexample to the
broader inequality; its offered proof only establishes the coordinate case.

Normality supplies coordinate cutoffs with q approaching one. Fix each
cutoff before taking n to infinity, then increase it. The strong converse
and capacity threshold extend to every fixed normal coherent initializer.
A common exponential fidelity decay rate for unrestricted coherent tails
does not follow. The diagonal finite-block formula without the factor r
and approximation term must not be silently asserted for arbitrary
coherent initializers. The actual stationary-diagonal task needs none of
this optional extension.

8. KNOWN FEEDBACK PHENOMENA: NO CONTRADICTION

Leung, Lim and Shor explicitly distinguish unassisted Q, backward-classical
Q_B, and two-way-classical Q_2 for the qubit erasure channel. Their first
page gives Q=max(0,1-2p) and the elementary backward-classical lower bound
Q_B>=(1-p)/3 for p>=2/5. In particular p=1/2 has Q=0 and Q_B>=1/6. Thus
a blanket claim that classical feedback never improves quantum capacity
would be false.

Primary source:
  Debbie Leung, Joungkeun Lim, Peter Shor,
  On the Capacity of Erasure Channel Assisted by Back Classical Communication,
  arXiv:0710.5943v4, Phys. Rev. Lett. 103, 240505 (2009), first page / Eq. (1).
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/0710.5943
  Retrieved source refs: turn146view0, turn146view1.

Why the trial's actual assumption does not imply that false claim:
for a d-dimensional erasure channel with p<1, the no-erasure Kraus operator
is sqrt(1-p) times the full-rank input isometry. Over n slots the all-survived
operator has rank d^n. Indeed this cannot be repaired by choosing a lower
rank Kraus decomposition: projecting Bob's Choi system onto the no-erasure
sector gives a nonzero maximally entangled state of Schmidt rank d^n.
Local projection cannot increase Schmidt number, so the full block Choi
state itself has Schmidt number d^n. The trial's general bound then gives
only the port-dimension ceiling, irrespective of how rare all-survival is.
It is fully compatible with a feedback improvement below that ceiling.

The rank/Schmidt-number concept is established. Chruscinski and Kossakowski,
"On partially entanglement breaking channels," arXiv:quant-ph/0511244,
Eq. (8) and Theorem 1, identify channel Schmidt number with the minimum
over Kraus representations of the maximum individual operator rank and
discuss its relation to partial entanglement breaking.

Primary source:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0511244
  Retrieved source ref: turn146view2.

The general invariant therefore belongs to existing Schmidt-number
machinery. The explicit causal transcript proof successfully supplies the
missing logical step for this owner problem. A bounded comparison is not
enough to establish historical novelty of that lemma, or of this particular
tree-capacity application. Bowen's "Quantum Feedback Channels",
arXiv:quant-ph/0209076, was also opened, but its definition includes a
backward quantum channel and is not the resource contract reviewed here.
