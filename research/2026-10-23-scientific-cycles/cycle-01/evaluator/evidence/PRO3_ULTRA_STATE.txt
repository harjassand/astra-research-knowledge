Let pi and q be equivalent probability measures, W=dpi/dq, and G be independence
Metropolis-Hastings with proposal q and target pi. The off-diagonal transition is
q(dy) min(1,W(y)/W(x)), with the remaining probability on the current point.
Let L be any fixed pi-reversible Markov kernel and alpha be fixed in (0,1).
The positive lazy mixture is C=(I+alpha G+(1-alpha)L)/2, initialized at q.
Given population KL constraints D(pi||q)<=K and D(q||pi)<=J, T(epsilon) is the
worst-case TV mixing time over this class. The exact remaining frontier is J>K:

max(K,(1-alpha)J) <= liminf epsilon log T(epsilon)
                  <= limsup epsilon log T(epsilon) <= J.

The limit is not proved to exist in that regime. For 0<J<=K it exists and equals
K. K=0 or J=0 forces pi=q. Alpha may not silently scale with epsilon.
