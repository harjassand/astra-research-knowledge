1. PRECISE REDUCTION
Let d=D^(Nr), tau=Tr/d and ||X||_2^2=tau(X*X). Normalize ||X||_2=1 and use
  |psi_X> = d^(-1/2) sum_uv X_uv |u>_A |v>_B.
For the first i physical pairs of task a, define the completely positive
conditional expectation
  E_(a,i)(X)=(I_(D^i)/D^i) tensor Tr_(a,prefix i)(X).
The frozen matrix identity, independently checked against the owner source, is
  p_(a,i)(X)=<psi_X|P_i^(a)|psi_X>=||E_(a,i)(X)||_2^2.
Its entanglement is S(XX*/d), depending only on the singular values of X.

For any fixed nonnegative weights w_(a,i), put
  F_w(X)=sum_(a,i) w_(a,i)||E_(a,i)(X)||_2^2.
Write R=|X|=(X*X)^(1/2) and L=|X*|=(XX*)^(1/2). Then
  F_w(X) <= sqrt(F_w(R) F_w(L))
         <= [F_w(R)+F_w(L)]/2
         <= max{F_w(R),F_w(L)}.                              (A)
Both R and L are positive semidefinite, have normalized squared norm one,
and have exactly the same singular values as X. Thus at least one of these TWO
matrices has unchanged entanglement entropy and no smaller witness sum.
The choice is made once for the entire weighted sum. We do not claim that one
choice increases every individual prefix simultaneously.

2. COMPLETE PROOF OF (A)
We prove a slightly more general elementary statement. For a 2-positive map
Phi between square matrix algebras and every X,
  ||Phi(X)||_2^2 <= ||Phi(|X*|)||_2 ||Phi(|X|)||_2.             (B)
Here all norms on the output use its same normalized trace. Complete positivity
of each E_(a,i) is more than enough for this statement.

Choose a unitary extension U of the polar factor, so X=U|X| and
|X*|=U|X|U*. The operator block matrix
  M = [ |X*|   X  ]
      [  X*   |X| ]
    = [U; I] |X| [U*, I]
is positive semidefinite. Applying Id_2 tensor Phi preserves positivity:
  [ A  B ] >= 0,
  [ B* C ]
where A=Phi(|X*|), B=Phi(X), C=Phi(|X|).

For a positive block matrix there is a contraction K such that
  B=A^(1/2) K C^(1/2).
One may derive this for invertible A,C by the Schur complement. For singular
blocks, add epsilon I to A,C and pass to the limit; this also avoids any inverse
on a kernel. Schatten Holder and the ideal property give, in unnormalized norms,
  ||B||_(HS) <= ||A^(1/2)||_4 ||K C^(1/2)||_4
             <= ||A||_(HS)^(1/2) ||C||_(HS)^(1/2).
Squaring and dividing by the common output dimension proves (B). Thus for each
prefix j=(a,i),
  p_j(X) <= sqrt(p_j(L)p_j(R)).
Multiply by w_j>=0 and sum. Scalar Cauchy-Schwarz yields
  sum_j w_j sqrt(p_j(L)p_j(R))
     <= sqrt[(sum_j w_j p_j(L))(sum_j w_j p_j(R))],
which is (A). Zero weights and zero outputs are harmless. QED.

Primary-source comparison: Christopher King, "Inequalities for trace norms of
2 x 2 block matrices," arXiv:quant-ph/0302069v2, equation (4), records the same
positive-block Schatten estimate used above. Its factorization statement and
Eq. (4) were read in the primary PDF. The proof here spells out the needed

3. EXACT VARIATIONAL CONSEQUENCES FOR THE SCIENTIFIC OBLIGATION
For every fixed D,N,r and every fixed Schmidt probability list lambda, the
maximum of the witness over all pure states with that Schmidt list is attained
with a positive coefficient matrix. Equivalently, if Sigma is the diagonal
matrix with entries sqrt(d lambda_j),
  max_(U,V unitary) F_w(U Sigma V*)
    = max_(U unitary) F_w(U Sigma U*).                        (C)
The inequality from left to right follows from (A): |X| and |X*| lie in the
right-hand conjugacy orbit. The reverse inequality is inclusion. Compactness
ensures both maxima exist in finite dimension. This halves the independent
Schmidt-basis choices without altering the objective or the entanglement.

Let m=ln D and
  Delta_(N,r,D)(X)=m sum_(a,i) p_(a,i)(X)-S(XX*/d).
Then
  sup_(||X||_2=1) Delta_(N,r,D)(X)
    = sup_(X>=0, ||X||_2=1) Delta_(N,r,D)(X).                 (D)
Hence proving the requested bound for positive X, with C_D or any other finite
K_D independent of N,r, proves it for ALL pure states with exactly that constant.
Conversely, failure anywhere has a positive representative with no weaker
violation. There is no approximation or limit in (A)-(D).

If the violation is strict, replace its positive representative Y by
  Y_epsilon=(Y+epsilon I)/||Y+epsilon I||_2.
At fixed finite N,r,D, this converges to Y, preserves positivity and is positive
definite for epsilon>0. Matrix entropy and the finitely many expectations are
continuous, so sufficiently small epsilon retains the strict violation. Thus
strict counterexamples can be sought among positive definite matrices. This
uses no dimension-uniform continuity estimate and makes no joint-limit claim.

4. THE REMAINING PROBLEM AS A DENSITY-MATRIX INEQUALITY
Every positive coefficient matrix has the form X=sqrt(d rho) for a unique density
matrix rho on the r task blocks. Its bipartite pure state is the canonical
purification of rho and has entanglement S(rho). Direct normalization gives
  ||E_(a,i)(sqrt(d rho))||_2^2
    = D^(-i) Tr[(Tr_(a,prefix i) sqrt(rho))^2].
Therefore the complete original uniform theorem is now equivalent to
  S(rho) >= m sum_(a=1)^r sum_(i=1)^N
                D^(-i) Tr[(Tr_(a,prefix i) sqrt(rho))^2]
             -r K_D                                         (E)
for every density matrix rho, uniformly N,r. All partial traces in (E) are
ordinary unnormalized partial traces. The trace outside the square acts on the
remaining factors. The factors D^(-i) are essential.

Positivity is not diagonal support or separability. The eigenvectors of rho can
be entangled across task blocks; (E) still contains the difficult quantum
correlations. No common product eigenbasis follows from (A), and the source's
maximally correlated additivity theorem cannot simply be inserted here.
The stage1 positive rank-one conditional counterfamily is included in this
reduced domain and continues to show why the direct complete conditional
estimate cannot prove (E) at coefficient one.

5. RELATION TO FORMATION COST
The reduction is lossless and uniform, so a proof of (E) with finite K_D would
satisfy exactly the premise of the frozen operational bridge. For iid approximate
preparation of a fixed memory, fix the finite output horizon N first. The frozen
linear-witness calculation then gives
  (ln M_t)/t >= m sum_i p_i -K_D -m N epsilon_t.
Taking the copy limit t->infinity with epsilon_t->0 at that fixed N gives the
resource bound; only afterward is N varied. This implication is conditional on
(E), which is still open. The new reduction does not by itself establish any
new unconditional E_C law, exact output from approximate initialization,
finite-size efficiency, or hardware benefit.
