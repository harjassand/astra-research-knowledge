Scientific attempts and rejected routes
---------------------------------------
A. Considered deriving pointwise near-harmonicity from spectral L2
   smoothing alone. This cannot directly control integration against
   q on the low-W tail, where dq/dpi is unbounded. Rejected before any
   unsupported estimate was used.
B. Recognized that laziness provides a stronger, uniform bound for
   bounded observables: |(I-H) C^t f| <= central-binomial probability.
   Derived it exactly from differences of binomial coefficients, using
   unimodality and the fact 0<=H^j f<=1. This resolves the obstruction
   in A without an initial L2 assumption.
C. On W<1/B, derived |Gg-qg|<=q(W<1/B) directly from the rejection
   identity. Combined B and C to keep the factor 1-alpha in the range
   of g on the low tail. The common scalar qg initially looked like a
   possible first-order loss; the anchor/core Cauchy-Schwarz bound makes
   |qg-m|<=b+a/R+Delta, so its low-tail integral is only second order.
D. Kept the signs of q-pi on low and high W tails. Their weighted
   bounds combine to max{a,(1-alpha)b}, rather than a sum. This yields
   the finite-time inequality (2) in RESULT.txt and the matching upper
   coefficient. No multistate counterconstruction was attempted after
   obtaining this proof.
