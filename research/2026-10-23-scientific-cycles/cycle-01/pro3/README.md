No network, package installation, accelerator, private account or dataset
download is needed. POSIX Python is expected for resource reporting.

executes the new exact-rational diagnostics and principal 80/120-digit
calculations, and compares their scientific outputs to the saved evidence.

are represented by their logarithms; those trajectories were not run.
