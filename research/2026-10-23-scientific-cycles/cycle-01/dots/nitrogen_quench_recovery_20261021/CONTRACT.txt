Scientific question selected 21 October 2026:
Can a product-preserving rapid quench followed by ordinary counterflow sensible heat
recuperation retain enough NOx and recover enough high-grade heat to reduce the
thermal air-to-NOx energy demand below a stated intermediate-product benchmark?
This is NOT a new plasma-excitation mechanism or claim of finished fertilizer SEC.
Hypotheses: (A) cooling below 2100 K renders NO loss negligible while enough heat
remains for competitive operation; (B) finite residence destroys a material fraction,
or temperature-grade/pinch/cold-feed capacity prevents the required recovery.
Discrimination: source-bound thermal kinetic trajectory and consistent enthalpy
ledger, then a finite-conductance counterflow implementation, not a scalar efficiency.

Feed dry79/21N2/O2 at300K,1atm, mass conserved. Heated stream initialized in
thermal equilibrium at3100K (favorable perfect heating, no finite-heater loss).
Delivered intermediate: retained mol NO+NO2 per kg original feed cooled to300K.
N2O is reported separately, not counted as useful product. No oxygen enrichment,
no vacuum, no extra dilution feed. Initial quench can reject heat, later recuperator
returns only useful heat to equal-mass fresh feed, with no credit for discarded heat.
No free energy from chemistry: all species formation/sensible enthalpies included.
Neither thermal kinetics nor assumed equilibrium proves a realizable plasma state.

Primary mechanism baseline: Cantera3.2.0 distributed air.yaml, NASA thermochemistry
and eight ground-state reactions. Alternative initial sensitivity replaces ONLY two
Zeldovich rates with Albrechts et al2026 Table1 (enconman120842); it is not a replay
of their seven-reaction CFD mechanism. Thermochemistry stays in common valid range
300–3100K. Reaction-rate/model/transport uncertainty must remain explicit.

First screening operation:40 prescribed cooling cases, no model fitting to outcomes.
Th3100K; initial quench1e8K/s; handover1400,1800,2100,2400K;
recuperation cooling1e3,1e4,1e5,1e6,1e7K/s;50K hot-end approach.
Scalar recovered heat min(handoff-to-final enthalpy drop,fresh-feed preheat capacity)
is only a favorable upper bound. It is not credited as an actual exchanger result.
Next calculation must impose distributed temperature approach and finite heat transfer.

A1–1.5MJ/molNOx literature economic target is not a universal thermodynamic bound,
not a matched modern plant measurement, and not automatically a finished-product
comparison. Electrical heating efficiency, pumps, real walls, capture and conversion
to nitrate all remain required before practical advantage is claimed. No spending,
hardware, outreach, or reopening GUIDE/halfcell/molecular families.
