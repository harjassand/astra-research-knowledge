\begin{abstract}
A closed unitary device with finite ports and a normal trace-class reservoir cannot combine recurrence under a faithful reference input with an indefinitely independent nonunitary channel. We prove the stronger statement that the complete external process becomes maximally distinguishable from every such independent channel. The proof allows infinite reservoir entropy and uses only marginal return, not return of a purification.

For the maximally mixed reference, recurrence has an additional consequence: one fixed finite spectral truncation approximates every horizon and every causal tester. Established error-correction machinery then gives the full asymptotic port rate. This extra conclusion does not extend to an arbitrary faithful reference. A fixed reversible device on a countable regular tree has a normal faithful product stationary state but exact unassisted quantum capacity $\log_2(2\sqrt{d-1})<\log_2d$ for $d\ge3$. A colored Dyck-word code and a matching error-rank converse establish this capacity for every normal initial reservoir state.

These are supplied proofs, with independent internal challenges and finite diagnostic replay. Historical priority and external certification are not claimed. The completed B2 R1 record is preserved unchanged and is not a premise of these results.
\end{abstract}

\section{Question, scope, and conventions}

The operational question is whether a reservoir that returns arbitrarily close to its initial state can supply fresh independent quantum noise forever. Finite-memory rank arguments alone do not answer it for an infinite-dimensional normal reservoir, especially one with infinite entropy. A second question is whether reference-input recurrence forces maximal quantum communication rate. The answer depends on the reference input: it is affirmative for the maximally mixed reference and false for a general faithful reference.

Let the input and output port be $S\simeq\mathbb C^d$, with $d<\infty$. Let $E$ be a separable Hilbert space and let $\tau\ge0$ be a trace-class operator of trace one. The persistent reservoir is the full type-I system $B(E)$. A single fixed unitary $U:S\otimes E\to S\otimes E$ is used at each call, with a fresh external input port and the previous reservoir. Every persistent register and every stored record is part of $E$. There is no replenishment, discarded internal ancilla, uncounted random source, or internal measurement sink. The initial reservoir is independent of the external input and tester.

The resulting $n$-slot causal process is denoted $\cM_n^\tau$. Its ordinary block channel accepts arbitrary entangled inputs on $S^{\otimes n}$, retains all output ports, and traces only the final $E$. Complete-process statements allow causal adaptive testers, their retained references, and a final joint observation. They concern more than repeated one-use marginals or unpurified stationary output statistics.

For a fixed reference input $\eta>0$, $\Tr\eta=1$, define
\begin{equation}
 \Phi_\eta(X)=\Tr_S U(\eta\otimes X)U^\dagger,
 \qquad \tau_n=\Phi_\eta^n(\tau),
 \qquad \delta_n=\tfrac12\norm{\tau_n-\tau}_1.
 \label{eq:orbit}
\end{equation}
Recurrence means $\liminf_{n\to\infty}\delta_n=0$, along horizons tending to infinity. A fixed nonzero return-error allowance is not recurrence. Throughout,
\[
 \D(\rho,\sigma)=\tfrac12\norm{\rho-\sigma}_1\in[0,1].
\]
Thus a bound $\D\le\epsilon$ is a full trace-norm bound $\norm{\rho-\sigma}_1\le2\epsilon$. The full operational strategy distance is the supremum of full output trace norm over admissible testers and lies in $[0,2]$. The Choi tester gives a lower bound on it, not an equality in general. Entropies and intermediate rates use natural logarithms; final communication rates in qubits use $\log_2$.

\section{Faithful-reference recurrence excludes independent noise}

Choose a purification $\ket{\eta^{1/2}}_{SA}$ of the reference, with $A\simeq\mathbb C^d$. Feed an independent copy at each call and retain every output and every $A$. Write the resulting state on the $2n$ external registers as $\Gamma_n^\eta$. For a proposed memoryless channel $\cN:S\to S$, let
\[
 \theta_\eta=(\cN\otimes\id_A)(\ket{\eta^{1/2}}\bra{\eta^{1/2}}),
 \qquad \lambda=\norm{\theta_\eta}_\infty.
\]

\begin{lemma}[A faithful input detects Choi mixedness]
If $\eta$ is faithful and $\cN$ is not unitary, then $\lambda<1$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The map $A\mapsto(A\otimes I)\ket{\eta^{1/2}}$ is injective. If $\theta_\eta$ were pure, all vectors associated with the Kraus operators of $\cN$ would be proportional, so the Kraus operators would be proportional. The channel would have one Kraus operator $V$ with $V^\dagger V=I$. Equal finite input and output dimensions make $V$ unitary. A mixed density operator in finite dimension has largest eigenvalue strictly below one.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[All-horizon separation]\label{thm:separation}
Under the model above, if $\tau$ is recurrent for a faithful $\eta$, then for every fixed nonunitary channel $\cN$,
\[
 \D(\Gamma_n^\eta,\theta_\eta^{\otimes n})\longrightarrow1.
\]
Consequently the full strategy distance between $\cM_n^\tau$ and $\cN^{\otimes n}$ tends to $2$. No finite-entropy assumption is needed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Purify $\tau$ on an inert mathematical reference $R$, using its spectral decomposition. For any $\epsilon>0$, choose rank-$r$ projections $P$ on $E$ and $Q$ on $R$ with initial masses at least $1-\epsilon$. They can be matching finite collections of Schmidt vectors. Normality, equivalently summability of the eigenvalues, supplies these projections even when $S(\tau)=\infty$.

After the $n$ purified reference inputs and unitary interactions, the joint state on the external registers and $ER$ is pure. Let $\omega_n$ be its $ER$ marginal. Its $E$ marginal is $\tau_n$ and its $R$ marginal is unchanged. The two commuting projections $P\otimes I$ and $I\otimes Q$ therefore give
\begin{align}
 \Tr[(P\otimes Q)\omega_n]
 &\ge \Tr(P\tau_n)+\Tr(Q\tau_R)-1\\
 &\ge1-2\epsilon-\delta_n. \label{eq:union}
\end{align}
This is a marginal estimate: neither a product $ER$ state nor return of its purification has been assumed.

The nonzero spectra of $\omega_n$ and $\Gamma_n^\eta$ coincide by the Schmidt decomposition of the global pure state. Hence the sum of the largest $r^2$ eigenvalues of $\Gamma_n^\eta$ is at least the right side of \eqref{eq:union}. Measure its leading spectral subspace (or the whole space when its dimension is smaller than $r^2$). The same projection has weight at most $r^2\lambda^n$ in $\theta_\eta^{\otimes n}$. The variational formula for trace distance gives the finite-horizon inequality
\begin{equation}
 \D(\Gamma_n^\eta,\theta_\eta^{\otimes n})
 \ge[1-2\epsilon-\delta_n-r^2\lambda^n]_+.
 \label{eq:faithful-bound}
\end{equation}
Along recurrent horizons $n_j\to\infty$, $\delta_{n_j}\to0$ and $\lambda^{n_j}\to0$. Both external-state families are prefix consistent: tracing future outputs and their references recovers the earlier state. Trace-distance contractivity makes the left side nondecreasing in $n$. Its full limit is thus at least $1-2\epsilon$. Let $\epsilon\downarrow0$. The purified-input experiment is an admissible complete tester, so its full trace distance lower-bounds strategy distance, whose upper bound is $2$.
\end{proof}

The proof works for one fixed consistent sequence of unitary interactions $U_j$ as well; the fixed-device model is a special case. For a specified comparator, the actual requirement is that $\theta_\eta$ be mixed. Faithfulness guarantees this for every nonunitary square channel. The projection in the witness can depend on $n$ and the device. No efficient measurement-synthesis theorem or reservoir-independent finite distinguishing horizon follows.

\begin{corollary}[Exact IID noise forces escape]\label{cor:escape}
If the complete process is exactly $\cN^{\otimes n}$ at every horizon, with $\eta$ faithful and $\cN$ nonunitary, then
\[
 \norm{\tau_n}_\infty\to0,\qquad
 \Tr\tau_n^2\to0,\qquad
 \norm{\tau_n-\sigma}_1\to2
\]
for every fixed normal density $\sigma$ on $E$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Now $\norm{\omega_n}_\infty=\lambda^n$. For a rank-$m$ projection $P$ on $E$ and a rank-$r$ projection $Q$ on $R$ with tail at most $\epsilon$,
\[
 \Tr(P\tau_n)\le \Tr[(P\otimes Q)\omega_n]+\epsilon
 \le mr\lambda^n+\epsilon.
\]
Taking the supremum over rank-one $P$, then $n\to\infty$ and $\epsilon\downarrow0$, proves the operator-norm claim. Purity is at most the operator norm. For a fixed normal $\sigma$, choose a finite-rank projection carrying nearly all its mass; its mass under $\tau_n$ vanishes. The corresponding measurement proves the last assertion.
\end{proof}

Equation \eqref{eq:faithful-bound} also states what remains under imperfect returns. A nonzero limiting return allowance leaves that allowance in the bound; it does not justify the exact-recurrence, spectral-rigidity, or capacity conclusions below.

\section{Maximally mixed recurrence gives uniform finite spectral approximation}

Set $\eta=I/d$ and write $U=\sum_{ij}\ket{i}\bra{j}\otimes U_{ij}$. With $K_{ij}=U_{ij}/\sqrt d$,
\[
 \Phi(X)=\sum_a K_aXK_a^\dagger,
 \qquad \sum_a K_a^\dagger K_a=\sum_a K_aK_a^\dagger=I.
\]
There are $d^2$ bounded Kraus operators. The map is bistochastic, unlike a general $\Phi_\eta$.

\begin{lemma}[Hilbert--Schmidt equality and intertwining]\label{lem:hs}
For Hermitian trace-class $X$ and $Y=\Phi(X)$,
\begin{equation}
 \sum_a\norm{K_aX-YK_a}_2^2=\norm X_2^2-\norm{\Phi(X)}_2^2.\label{eq:hs}
\end{equation}
If $\tau$ is recurrent, then at every $t\ge0$,
\begin{equation}
 K_a\tau_t=\tau_{t+1}K_a,\qquad
 U(I\otimes\tau_t)U^\dagger=I\otimes\tau_{t+1}.\label{eq:intertwine}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Expanding the squared norms in \eqref{eq:hs}, the first terms sum to $\Tr X^2$ by $\sum K_a^\dagger K_a=I$, the last terms to $\Tr Y^2$ by $\sum K_aK_a^\dagger=I$, and the two cross terms to $2\Tr[Y\Phi(X)]=2\Tr Y^2$. Trace-class operators are Hilbert--Schmidt; products used here are trace-class and the bounded factors permit cyclicity. This also proves Hilbert--Schmidt contraction without entropy expressions.

The sequence $\norm{\tau_t}_2$ is nonincreasing. Trace-norm recurrence implies Hilbert--Schmidt recurrence since $\norm{Z}_2\le\norm{Z}_1$. A nonincreasing sequence returning arbitrarily close to its initial value is constant. Every summand on the left side of \eqref{eq:hs} vanishes at every $\tau_t$. Recombining the matrix blocks of $U$ proves \eqref{eq:intertwine}.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One truncation works at every horizon]\label{thm:uniform}
For each $\epsilon>0$ there is a normalized finite-rank density $\sigma$ on the original $E$, of rank $r<\infty$, such that the unchanged device initialized in $\sigma$ differs from the original complete process by full strategy distance at most $2\epsilon$, uniformly at every finite horizon. The corresponding normalized Choi states have rank at most $r^2$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose a positive spectral threshold $a$ and set $P_t=\mathbf1_{[a,\infty)}(\tau_t)$. Functional calculus in \eqref{eq:intertwine} gives
\[
 U(I\otimes P_t)U^\dagger=I\otimes P_{t+1}.
\]
These projections have the same finite rank $r$ and the same reservoir mass $q=\Tr(P_t\tau_t)$. Include complete eigenspaces at the threshold; do not select arbitrary vectors through a degenerate eigenvalue. Since $\tau$ is normal, choose $a$ so that $q\ge1-\epsilon$. Set $\sigma=P_0\tau P_0/q$. There is a density $\chi$ on the complementary spectral subspace such that
\[
 \tau=q\sigma+(1-q)\chi,\qquad \norm{\tau-\sigma}_1=2(1-q)\le2\epsilon.
\]
For any horizon and any causal tester, the whole experiment is one CPTP map from the initial reservoir state to the retained tester state. Contractivity gives the same $2\epsilon$ bound with no factor of the horizon. The same $\sigma$ and the same physical $U$ work for all horizons.

The transported subspace property holds for every input, including arbitrary entangled and adaptive inputs: $U$ maps all of $S\otimes P_tE$ onto $S\otimes P_{t+1}E$. Purify $\sigma$ using a reference of dimension at most $r$. In the purified Choi experiment, the final reservoir lies in $P_nE$ of dimension $r$. Its joint dimension with that reference is at most $r^2$, so the external Choi state has rank at most $r^2$.
\end{proof}

Choosing bases in the moving subspaces gives an $r$-dimensional memory with generally time-dependent interactions. This is not a theorem that the infinite reservoir has a fixed finite-dimensional autonomous simulator: moving supports may encode a clock. The physical approximation itself uses the unchanged infinite-space device and a single finite-rank initializer.

Let $\Omega_n=\Gamma_n^{I/d}$, and let $\kappa_k(\Xi)$ be the sum of the largest $k$ eigenvalues of a density $\Xi$. The exact mixture in the proof and its truncated support give
\begin{equation}
 \D(\Omega_n,\Xi_n)\ge[1-\epsilon-\kappa_{r^2}(\Xi_n)]_+.
 \label{eq:kyfan}
\end{equation}
For a fixed nonunitary channel with normalized Choi state $J_{\cN}$, this gives $1-\epsilon-r^2\norm{J_{\cN}}_\infty^n$. More generally, any comparator sequence with vanishing mass in every fixed spectral rank is separated. Positive von Neumann entropy rate alone is not that condition: a half-pure, half-maximally-mixed mixture retains a largest eigenvalue near $1/2$ while its entropy grows linearly.

\begin{corollary}[Zero Choi entropy rate]
Under maximally mixed recurrence, $S(\Omega_n)/n\to0$, even if $S(\tau)=\infty$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The truncated Choi state has entropy at most $2\ln r$, and the full external dimension is $d^{2n}$. For the mixture with tail $\epsilon$, the entropy-of-mixture inequality gives
\[
 S(\Omega_n)\le h_2(\epsilon)+2(1-\epsilon)\ln r+2\epsilon n\ln d.
\]
Take $n\to\infty$ at fixed cutoff, then $\epsilon\downarrow0$. Every entropy used at finite $n$ is finite. This says zero \emph{Choi} entropy rate, not zero entropy rate of the individual output stream.
\end{proof}

\section{Communication consequence and inherited coding machinery}

The coding contract is ordinary unassisted block transmission. An encoder maps a $K_n$-dimensional logical system into $n$ input ports; a decoder acts on the $n$ output ports. Both may be arbitrary CPTP maps, may retain local ancillas during their operation, and have no access to $E$. There is no backward communication or preshared entanglement. Reliability can be measured by entanglement fidelity tending to one; the positive result below gives the stronger vanishing diamond error.

\begin{lemma}[Inherited low-Kraus-rank coding]\label{lem:code}
A channel on a $D$-dimensional system with at most $k$ Kraus operators has an exact quantum error-correcting subspace of dimension at least $\lceil D/k^4\rceil$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $H_1=I,H_2,\ldots,H_m$ be a real Hermitian basis for the self-adjoint operator system spanned by the products $A_i^\dagger A_j$, with $m\le k^2$. Greedily choose $N=\lceil D/m\rceil$ unit vectors $v_a$. When choosing $v_{a+1}$, exclude the span of all $H_jv_b$, $b\le a$. Its dimension is at most $ma<D$ until $N$ vectors have been chosen. Including $I$ ensures orthogonality, and Hermiticity ensures every off-diagonal compression of every $H_j$ vanishes.

The expectation vectors $(\langle v_a,H_jv_a\rangle)_{j=2}^m$ lie in $\mathbb R^{m-1}$. The affine Tverberg theorem states that $N\ge(q-1)m+1$ points can be partitioned into $q$ disjoint groups whose convex hulls intersect. Choose $q=\lceil N/m\rceil=\lceil D/m^2\rceil$. Taking square-root convex weights within each disjoint group produces orthonormal vectors $w_g$ with equal diagonal expectations and zero cross expectations for every $H_j$. Their projection $P$ therefore satisfies
\[
 PA_i^\dagger A_jP=c_{ij}P.
\]
These are the Knill--Laflamme conditions. Diagonalize the positive matrix $(c_{ij})$ to obtain Kraus combinations $B_a$ with $PB_a^\dagger B_bP=\mu_a\delta_{ab}P$. For $\mu_a>0$, $B_aP/\sqrt{\mu_a}$ are isometries with orthogonal ranges. Measure those ranges and apply their inverse isometries; complete the decoder arbitrarily on the unused range. This is CPTP and exactly recovers the code, including entangled logical inputs. Since $m\le k^2$, the stated dimension follows.
\end{proof}

The greedy and convex-partition mechanism is established coding theory \cite{KLV}; the finite-memory and subexponential-Kraus applications are established in \cite{KW,GBM}. The affine-dimension bookkeeping in Lemma~\ref{lem:code} is not presented as a new coding theorem. The underlying affine Tverberg theorem is an explicit external mathematical dependency \cite{Tverberg}.

\begin{theorem}[Full rate under maximally mixed recurrence]\label{thm:fullrate}
Under the assumptions of Theorem~\ref{thm:uniform}, the unassisted quantum capacity is $\log_2d$ qubits per use. There are block codes with full recovered-channel diamond error tending to zero, without access to or reset of the reservoir.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For large $n$, use the complete spectral cutoff $P^{(n)}=\mathbf1_{[e^{-\sqrt n},\infty)}(\tau)$. Its rank obeys $r_n\le e^{\sqrt n}$, and its discarded mass $\epsilon_n$ tends to zero by normality. The truncated $n$-block channel has at most $r_n^2$ Kraus operators. Lemma~\ref{lem:code} supplies an exact code of dimension $K_n$ with
\[
 \ln K_n\ge n\ln d-8\sqrt n.
\]
The actual and truncated channels differ in full diamond norm by at most $2\epsilon_n$. Composing the code and its exact decoder preserves this upper bound. Thus the actual recovered channel has half-diamond error at most $\epsilon_n$ and rate tending to $\ln d$. The finite input-port dimension supplies the converse. Small $n$ for which the cutoff is empty may be ignored or assigned a trivial code.
\end{proof}

The true process need not have subexponential \emph{exact} Kraus rank, as required by the particular perfect-memory definition in \cite{GBM}. It has uniform finite-rank approximations. Normality gives no common rate for $\epsilon_n\downarrow0$, and this proof gives no efficient encoder or decoder, spectral-learning procedure, energy bound, or useful universal blocklength.

\section{A faithful stationary tree reservoir with strictly smaller capacity}

This construction makes the reference-input distinction substantive. It also gives a complete mathematical communication problem with a matching code and converse.

\subsection{The fixed unitary and its normal stationary state}

Fix $d\ge3$ and $b=d-1$. Let $T_d$ be the countable rooted $d$-regular tree. The root has $d$ children with port labels $0,1,\ldots,d-1$. Every other vertex has its parent at port $0$ and its $b$ children at ports $1,\ldots,b$. Set $E=\ell^2(V(T_d))$. If the edge from $v$ to $w$ has port $a$ at $v$ and port $a'$ at $w$, define
\begin{equation}
 U\ket{a}_S\ket v_E=\ket{a'}_S\ket w_E.\label{eq:treeU}
\end{equation}
Directed-edge reversal is a bijection and an involution, so $U$ extends to a fixed unitary with $U^2=I$. Every visited vertex and every stored path remains in the same counted $E$.

Choose $p\in(1/2,1)$ and
\[
 \eta=\operatorname{diag}\left(p,\frac{1-p}{b},\ldots,\frac{1-p}{b}\right),
 \qquad q=\frac{1-p}{p}<1.
\]
Set $\pi(o)=C=(2p-1)/(2p)$. Along every downward edge with parent port $a$, set $\pi(w)=\pi(v)\eta_a/p$. The total mass of level $\ell\ge1$ is $(C/p)q^{\ell-1}$, because the root's outward port probabilities sum to one and every later outward sum is $1-p$. Thus
\[
 \sum_v\pi(v)=C+\frac{C/p}{1-q}=1.
\]
All weights are positive. The density $\tau_\pi=\sum_v\pi(v)\ket v\bra v$ is normal and faithful, with finite entropy. Along each edge,
\[
 \pi(v)\eta_a=\pi(w)\eta_{a'}.
\]
The permutation \eqref{eq:treeU} therefore preserves every joint basis probability, proving the full product stationarity
\begin{equation}
 U(\eta\otimes\tau_\pi)U^\dagger=\eta\otimes\tau_\pi.\label{eq:treeStationary}
\end{equation}
Return is exact at every reference use. No purifier return condition is imposed. When reference inputs are left unpurified, their output stream is simply $\eta^{\otimes n}$; that observation does not identify the complete process.

\subsection{The walk bound}

Under $I/d$ inputs and a vertex-diagonal bath, the reservoir evolves by simple random walk $P$ on the regular tree. Let
\[
 \rho=\frac{2\sqrt b}{d}<1,\qquad a_d=d\rho=2\sqrt b.
\]
Here $P$ is also the self-adjoint walk operator on $\ell^2(V)$ with counting measure. A short weighted Schur estimate proves $\norm P\le\rho$. Put $h(v)=b^{-|v|/2}$. At nonroot vertices, $(Ph)(v)/h(v)=2\sqrt b/d$; at the root it is $1/\sqrt b\le\rho$. For finitely supported $f$, use on each edge
\[
 2|f(v)f(w)|\le\frac{h(w)}{h(v)}|f(v)|^2+
 \frac{h(v)}{h(w)}|f(w)|^2.
\]
Summing and dividing by $d$ bounds $|\langle f,Pf\rangle|$ by $\rho\norm f^2$. Density and self-adjointness give the norm bound. In particular,
\begin{equation}
 P^n(v,w)\le\rho^n,\qquad
 \#\{\text{length-}n\text{ walks from }v\text{ to }w\}
 =d^nP^n(v,w)\le a_d^n.\label{eq:walk}
\end{equation}
This is an infinite-tree proof, not an estimate from a finite truncated tree.

\subsection{A uniform exact code from colored Dyck words}

Write $W_n$ for the ordered product of the $n$ copies of $U$ on the distinct ports and the common bath. For $2m$ inputs, use words whose symbols $1,\ldots,b$ are pushes and whose symbol $0$ is a pop. Require $m$ pushes and $m$ pops, and at every prefix require at least as many pushes as pops. These are Dyck words with $b$ colors on each push. Their number is
\begin{equation}
 K_m=C_m b^m,\qquad C_m=\frac1{m+1}\binom{2m}{m}.\label{eq:dyckcount}
\end{equation}
For any initial vertex $v$, every push enters a child and emits $0$. Every pop returns along an edge opened inside the block and emits that edge's push color. No pop reads a parent edge above the initial vertex, including when the initial vertex is the distinguished root. At the end, the bath has returned exactly to $v$.

Let $F(w)$ be the resulting output word. It is independent of $v$: it contains $0$ at each push position and the matched push color at each pop position. It determines $w$ uniquely, since the zero/nonzero positions recover the parentheses and the usual matching recovers all colors. Therefore
\begin{equation}
 W_{2m}(\ket w\otimes\ket v)=\ket{F(w)}\otimes\ket v
 \quad\text{for every allowed }w\text{ and every }v.\label{eq:dyckmap}
\end{equation}
By linearity and continuity this holds for arbitrary coherent bath states and arbitrary external references. The span of the allowed input words is an exact quantum code, not merely a set of distinguishable classical messages. Encoding is the inclusion of that span; decoding inverts the basis injection $F$ on its output span and is completed arbitrarily elsewhere. The entire bath, including any correlations with an external purification, returns after the block. It need not return after each internal use. Repeating these blocks requires no bath reset.

The elementary bounds
\[
 \frac{4^m}{(2m+1)(m+1)}\le C_m\le4^m
\]
give $\ln K_m/(2m)\to\ln(2\sqrt b)$. Restricting to a power-of-two subspace loses less than one logical qubit per block. Thus this construction achieves $\log_2(2\sqrt b)$ qubits per use with zero error.

\subsection{A matching converse for every normal initializer}

Let $W_n$ be the global permutation unitary for $n$ calls. For a pure vertex initializer $v$, its channel Kraus operators are
\[
 A_{w,v}=\bra wW_n\ket v.
\]
Each is a partial permutation matrix: an input word contributes exactly when its path ends at $w$, and distinct such words have distinct output words by invertibility of $W_n$. Consequently
\begin{equation}
 \rank A_{w,v}=d^nP^n(v,w)\le a_d^n.\label{eq:krausrank}
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[Rank bound for any encoder and decoder]\label{lem:rankfidelity}
If a channel has a Kraus representation in which every Kraus operator has rank at most $M$, then any CPTP encoder and decoder transmitting a $K$-dimensional logical system have entanglement fidelity at most $\min\{1,M/K\}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every Kraus operator $B_\mu$ of the composite encoded and decoded channel is a product of a decoder Kraus operator, a channel Kraus operator, and an encoder Kraus operator. It has rank at most $M$. The trace bound
\[
 |\Tr B_\mu|^2\le\rank(B_\mu)\Tr(B_\mu^\dagger B_\mu)
\]
follows by bounding trace by the sum of singular values and then using Cauchy--Schwarz. Since the composite channel is trace preserving,
\[
 F_e=\frac1{K^2}\sum_\mu|\Tr B_\mu|^2
 \le\frac M{K^2}\Tr I_K=\frac MK.
\]
Countable Kraus families cause no problem: all terms are nonnegative and the trace-preserving sum converges on the finite logical system.
\end{proof}

For any vertex-diagonal initial density, the Kraus family is $\sqrt{\pi(v)}A_{w,v}$. Equations \eqref{eq:krausrank} and Lemma~\ref{lem:rankfidelity} give the explicit finite-block strong-converse bound
\begin{equation}
 F_{e,n}\le\min\{1,a_d^n/K_n\}.\label{eq:diagconverse}
\end{equation}
This already covers the faithful stationary initializer in \eqref{eq:treeStationary}.

For completeness, let the initializer be any normal density $\tau$, with no diagonality or entropy assumption. Choose a projection $P_0$ onto $r$ vertex basis states with $q_0=\Tr(P_0\tau)\ge1-\epsilon$, and normalize $\sigma=P_0\tau P_0/q_0$. Projecting a purification and normalizing shows $\D(\tau,\sigma)\le\sqrt\epsilon$. Each pure vector in a decomposition of $\sigma$ is supported on these $r$ vertices. Its corresponding Kraus operator is a linear combination of at most $r$ operators $A_{w,v}$, and hence has rank at most $r a_d^n$. For the same encoder and decoder,
\begin{equation}
 F_{e,n}(\tau)\le\min\{1,r a_d^n/K_n+\sqrt\epsilon\}.
 \label{eq:normalconverse}
\end{equation}
Here the fidelity difference is an effect probability, so its change is bounded by half trace distance; there is no missing factor of two. At any rate above $\ln a_d$, fix $r,\epsilon$, let $n\to\infty$, and then let $\epsilon\downarrow0$. Entanglement fidelity tends to zero. No uniform exponential decay rate is claimed for arbitrary normal initializers with unrestricted tails.

\begin{theorem}[Exact capacity of the tree device]\label{thm:treecapacity}
For the fixed unitary \eqref{eq:treeU}, every normal initial reservoir state has ordinary unassisted quantum capacity
\begin{equation}
 Q=\log_2(2\sqrt{d-1})\quad\text{qubits per use}.\label{eq:capacity}
\end{equation}
The achievable codes have zero error and return the entire reservoir after each code block. Rates above \eqref{eq:capacity} have entanglement fidelity tending to zero. The vertex-diagonal case has the explicit exponential estimate \eqref{eq:diagconverse}.
\end{theorem}

For $d=3$, the capacity is exactly $3/2$ qubits per use. The available port dimension permits $\log_2 3\simeq1.584963$ qubits per use, so the strict deficit is about $0.084963$ qubits per use. More generally, $2\sqrt{d-1}<d$ for every $d\ge3$. This is a strict counterexample to full capacity from merely faithful-reference recurrence, even with exact stationarity and finite initial entropy.

\subsection{Why uniform finite-rank approximation also fails here}

For a pure vertex initializer $v$, the Choi vectors associated with different $A_{w,v}$ are orthogonal: their input columns have disjoint supports. Their squared norms are the walk counts in \eqref{eq:krausrank}. Thus the nonzero eigenvalues of the standard normalized Choi state are exactly $P^n(v,w)$. For any vertex-diagonal initial density, convexity then gives
\[
 \norm{\Omega_n}_\infty\le\rho^n.
\]
If $\Xi_n$ has rank at most a fixed $R$, its support measurement gives
\[
 \D(\Omega_n,\Xi_n)\ge1-R\rho^n\longrightarrow1.
\]
Thus the faithful stationary tree initializer cannot have the all-horizon finite-rank approximation from Theorem~\ref{thm:uniform}. Both the spectral conclusion and the full-capacity conclusion genuinely require stronger assumptions than arbitrary faithful-reference recurrence.

\section{Boundary examples and invalidated shortcuts}

\subsection{No uniform finite detection time}
An $L$-qubit bath initialized maximally mixed can control a phase on the input with its first bit and cyclically shift after each call. It is stationary under the maximally mixed reference. For $n\le L$, the complete process is exactly independent dephasing. For $n>L$, its Choi state is uniform on $2^L$ periodic Bell-phase words, whereas independent dephasing is uniform on all $2^n$ words. Therefore
\[
 \D(\Omega_n,J_{\rm deph}^{\otimes n})=
 \begin{cases}0,&n\le L,\\1-2^{L-n},&n\ge L.\end{cases}
\]
Taking a direct sum over $L$ gives one fixed unitary with a normal mixture of length sectors. Its tail $\Pr(L\ge n)$ can decay arbitrarily slowly. Consequently a universal detection horizon cannot be obtained from normality and recurrence alone. Finite check counts do not settle the all-horizon theorem.

\subsection{The finite-rank square is real}
For $E\simeq\mathbb C^d$, $\tau=I/d$ and $U=\mathrm{SWAP}$, the reservoir is stationary. The first output is maximally mixed and later outputs are the preceding inputs. The normalized $n$-slot Choi state has exactly $d^2$ equal nonzero eigenvalues. Against independent complete depolarization,
\[
 \D(\Omega_n,I/d^{2n})=1-d^{2-2n}.
\]
This attains the $r^2$ support bound at $r=d$. With ordinary independent inputs all equal to a faithful $\eta$ and bath $\tau=\eta$, the unpurified output stream is nevertheless exactly $\eta^{\otimes n}$. Retained input references expose the delayed correlations.

\subsection{Dropping recurrence, or dropping faithfulness}
The vacuum incomplete tensor product of finite-dimensional cells over $\mathbb Z$ has a countable finite-excitation basis and is separable. Its vacuum is a normal pure state. Apply a finite Stinespring unitary at cell zero and shift so that an unused vacuum cell arrives at zero next time. This fixed unitary implements any given finite-dimensional channel independently forever; all used cells remain in the original reservoir. No cells are appended after initialization. For a reset channel implemented by SWAP, maximally mixed inputs give vacuum probability $d^{-n}$ and half return error $1-d^{-n}$. This violates recurrence, as required.

If the reference is instead the nonfaithful pure vacuum input, that reset reservoir is exactly stationary. A CNOT from the system to the current blank cell similarly gives exact IID dephasing while a pure $\ket0$ reference leaves the bath fixed. Thus faithfulness cannot be removed from the universal theorem. With a faithful diagonal qubit reference having excited weight $p>0$, the latter bath's half return error is $1-(1-p)^n$.

\subsection{Changing the algebra changes the return contract}
A Bernoulli shift on the non-atomic algebra $L^\infty(\{0,1\}^{\mathbb Z})$ has a normal stationary product measure and can supply independent classical phase noise. Normality on that algebra is not normal trace-class return on the full $B(E)$. In a Hilbert-space representation, restricting return to multiplication observables can hide changes of the full density and its coherences. No impossibility for non-atomic stationary baths, conventional nonrecurrent reservoirs, or reduced semigroup dilations is claimed.

\subsection{The unsuccessful extensions that matter}
The initial attempt to extend maximal capacity from an arbitrary faithful reference was false; the tree theorem closes it with a counterexample. The simple two-use push/pop code was suboptimal; coherent superpositions of balanced colored words recover the missing combinatorial rate. A Choi largest-eigenvalue bound alone gave a weaker capacity converse; the rank of each Kraus error, rather than the rank or norm of the whole Choi state, gives the matching result. None of these failures calls for reopening an R1 constant or noisy-Bell programme.

\section{Primary comparisons and the novelty boundary}

Finite-memory capacity is inherited. Kretschmann and Werner's Theorem~1 gives maximal capacities for pure finite-memory channels, with a uniform memory-dimension-squared Kraus bound in Lemma~2 \cite{KW}. Giovannetti, Burgarth and Mancini's perfect-memory criterion uses subexponential exact environment/Kraus growth, and their Theorem~2 gives large exact codes \cite{GBM}. Knill, Laflamme and Viola provide the earlier error-space and convex-partition coding machinery \cite{KLV}. The new proof above uses these results only where explicitly identified; the tree converse and its Dyck code are proved directly.

Ryb\'ar and Ziman's repeatability concerns the same channel on individual inputs and permits finite-memory random-unitary examples \cite{RZ}. Theorem~\ref{thm:separation} concerns a stronger operation: the entire independent process, including retained references and output correlations. Operational distinctions of this kind are developed in process-tensor work \cite{Pollock}. Dilation results for powers of a reduced channel do not impose recurrence of the complete reservoir or identify all intervention-dependent processes \cite{EndeDirr}.

The potentially distinct contribution of this checkpoint is the combination of (i) marginal recurrence and normal-state tightness yielding all-horizon maximal separation, (ii) the infinite-entropy recurrence-to-uniform-spectral-approximation result for $I/d$, and (iii) a sharp faithful-reference counterexample with exactly computed quantum capacity. The ingredients include standard operator inequalities, trace-class compactness, error correction, tree walk bounds and Catalan counting. A bounded primary-source comparison did not locate the precise combined statements or this precise tree-channel capacity theorem. That observation does not establish historical priority. No publication or external scientific acceptance is asserted.
