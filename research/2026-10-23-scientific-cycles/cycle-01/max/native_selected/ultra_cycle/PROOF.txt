2. THE STRONG STOPPING COMPARATOR ALREADY EXTENDS TO IID FORMATION

Theorem 1. Let K in {0,...,N} be the stopping variable of Gamma_N(tau): its
tail is Pr(K>=i)=p_i and Pr(K=0)=0 because the first block is exact. Put
 Z_N=1+(N-1)(1-D^-1).

 E_C^op(tau) >= m E K - ln Z_N.                              (1)
Also E_C^op(tau)>=m E K-H(K). Neither uses E_F additivity or E_R additivity.

Proof. Write P_0=I, P_i=Phi_D^tensor i tensor I. The stopping measurement has
orthogonal effects F_k=P_k-P_(k+1), k<N, and F_N=P_N. For t output chains take
its product measurement, keeping EVERY stopped cell. For any globally A:B
separable output omega, its cell law q satisfies, simultaneously for all tails,
 q(K_a>=i_a for every a with i_a>0) <= D^(-sum_a i_a).          (2)
Indeed the intersection is a single Bell projection of total Schmidt dimension
D^(sum i_a), tensored with residual identity. Any product local state has overlap
at most its reciprocal, and convex averaging proves the separable assertion.
The comparator need not be a product over task copies. The Bell measurement is
only a mathematical converse test, not an LOCC resource operation.

Fix finite L>=0. Set g_L(0)=exp(-L) and g_L(k)=D^k for k>=1. Its increments
are nonnegative. Expanding a product of tail-indicator sums and applying (2),
 E_q product_a g_L(K_a) <= Z_(N,L)^t,

This is where genuine joint quantum compatibility of the separable comparator

Here p'_t is the actually prepared stopped law. This argument can be expressed

Contractivity gives TV(p'_t,p^tensor t)<=epsilon_t. The summed log g_L has

FIRST fix N and L, THEN take t to infinity, and THEN let L to infinity. This

4. QUANTUM SUPPORT COMPATIBILITY FOR GLOBAL STABILIZER BASES

For definiteness D is prime. On n qudits Pauli exponents form F_D^(2n) with
their usual nondegenerate symplectic commutation form. Let G be a maximal
isotropic subspace, dim G=n. Choose consistent phases so its commuting Pauli
operators form an abelian group with no nontrivial scalar. Their joint
eigenspaces are rank one, indexed by characters x in G*, a vector space of
dimension n over F_D. This is a global stabilizer basis. It need not factor
over task blocks. The same argument works for a specified linear prime-power
field stabilizer model; no arbitrary composite-module claim is made here.

For any set S of physical sites define
 H_S={g in G: g is identity on S},  V_S=annihilator(H_S) in G*.
H_S is isotropic on the n-|S| complementary sites. In any nondegenerate
symplectic space of dimension 2l an isotropic subspace has dimension at most l:
W subset W^perp and dim W+dim W^perp=2l. Consequently
 dim V_S=n-dim H_S >= |S|.                                  (8)
Moreover H_(union S)=intersection H_S, so
 V_(union S)=sum V_S.                                       (9)
This is genuine simultaneous quantum information absent from separate Schmidt
envelopes. Put V_(a,i)=V_(task-a prefix i); these are nested for each task.

If X is diagonal in the stabilizer basis, write its diagonal function f(x).
Expand it in the character/Pauli Fourier basis. Normalized physical tracing
over S kills precisely the Pauli terms outside H_S and fixes those in H_S.
On f this is exactly averaging over the label-space translations V_S:
 E_S X <--> A_(V_S)f,  (A_V f)(x)=|V|^-1 sum_(v in V) f(x+v). (10)
Physical Hilbert-Schmidt norm is counting l2 of f. No positivity or product
structure of the eigenvectors is used in this identity.

5. SELF-CONTAINED VECTOR-HALL SELECTION AND CLASSICAL DOMINATION

Lemma. For finite vector subspaces U_1,...,U_l over a field, there exist
linearly independent v_j in U_j iff dim(sum_(j in A)U_j)>=|A| for all A.
Necessity is immediate. Sufficiency follows by induction. If a proper nonempty
set A is tight, select its independent representatives inductively in
W=sum_A U_j, then quotient by W. Each remaining subfamily B has quotient
dimension at least |A union B|-|A|=|B|; induction selects representatives
there, which lift and combine. If there is no proper nonempty tight set,
pick nonzero v_l in U_l and quotient by its line. Every subfamily of the
remaining spaces had dimension at least its cardinality plus one, so its
quotient still meets the condition. Induct and lift, then append v_l.
The base case is immediate. The same proof selects representatives from any
finite spanning list for each U_j, using its nonzero images in each quotient.

Apply the lemma to the n spaces indexed (a,j), U_(a,j)=V_(a,j). For ANY subset
of these labels take i_a to be the largest included j of task a (zero if absent).
Nestedness, (8) and (9) give
 dim sum_(included labels) V_(a,j)
 =dim sum_a V_(a,i_a) >= sum_a i_a >= number of included labels. (11)
This checks ALL Hall subsets, not only full rectangles. There are independent
v_(a,j) in V_(a,j). They form a basis of G*. Define
 W_(a,i)=span{v_(a,j):j<=i} subset V_(a,i).
In coordinates of this selected basis, W_(a,i) translates the first i
independent D-valued coordinates of task a. Since averaging over larger
subspaces is a further orthogonal projection,
 ||A_(V_(a,i)) f||_2^2 <= ||A_(W_(a,i)) f||_2^2.              (12)
One COMMON change of label coordinates works for every task and every prefix.
This is the simultaneous rearrangement justified by actual quantum support.
It is not inferred from positive matrices in general.

6. COMPLETE RESTRICTED CRITICAL TENSOR THEOREM

Theorem 2. If X>=0, Tr X^2=1, is diagonal in any global prime-D stabilizer
basis on rN physical sites, then
 S(X^2)>=m sum_(a,i)||E_(a,i)X||_HS^2-r C_D.                 (13)
C_D is the unchanged valid single-chain constant from Restore; it is not
optimized. The spectrum of X may be arbitrary, including zeros.

Proof. In the chosen coordinates let c(y)=f(sum v_(a,j)y_(a,j)). Its squared
entries form a probability distribution lambda. Its entropy is S(X^2), since
this is merely a permutation of the eigenvalues of X^2. The old scalar
one-chain lemma, applied to conditional amplitudes for task a with all other
coordinates fixed, gives
 m sum_i ||A_(W_(a,i))c||_2^2 <= H(Y_a|Y_others)+C_D.         (14)
To see the normalization, if the other coordinates have probability q_z,
divide that task slice by sqrt(q_z), apply the one-chain lemma, then average.
The sliced squared projection norms sum to the full ones. Zero slices vanish.
Classical conditioning and the entropy chain rule imply
 sum_a H(Y_a|Y_others) <= sum_a H(Y_a|Y_1,...,Y_(a-1))=H(Y).
Sum (14) and use (10),(12). This proves (13), without any quantum conditional
entropy estimate, generic E_F additivity, independence of outputs or scalar
Schmidt-envelope inference. QED.

This covers nondegenerate Bell-diagonal density matrices on two task qubits,
whose entangled eigenvectors rule out local classicality in either direction.
It is therefore strictly broader than task-product eigenbases or recursive CQ.

Mixed-state support corollary. Let e_x be the global stabilizer basis and

Every pure vector in V_G has a coefficient matrix diagonal in this basis,

Merely admitting one stabilizer-state ensemble would NOT suffice for an E_F

8. EXPLICIT MATCHED IID UPPER OPERATION AND PREPARATION ERROR

Let L=ceil(J/delta), M=J+L-1, and let K be uniform on J,...,J+L-1.
The whole unflagged memory is tau=E_K[Phi_d^tensor K tensor chi_d^tensor(M-K)],
with the original exact emit/shift/append device. L>=J. Every prefix j<=J
emits exactly Phi_d^tensor j, independent of the remaining memory. Shifting
the uniform rung distribution by j changes its total variation by j/L, so
the complete-memory return is at most j/L<=delta. No initial-K record remains.

For iid formation sample K_1,...,K_t before resource use, let mu=E K,
and choose fixed Bell inventory
 B_t=ceil[t mu+(L-1)sqrt(t ln(1/e_t)/2)],
where e_t->0 with ln(1/e_t)=o(t), for example e_t=1/t (t>=2).
Supply the deterministic state Phi_(d^B_t). If sum K_a<=B_t, split its B_t
rank-d pairs, use K_a in each memory and append the separable chi tails.
Discard all unused supplied pairs locally and genuinely erase the labels.
If sum K_a>B_t, output a fixed separable complete-memory fallback; this
failure branch is averaged, never conditioned away. Hoeffding's elementary
bounded-sum bound gives failure probability at most e_t when L>1. Thus the
GLOBAL half-trace preparation error is at most e_t, and
 (ln d^B_t)/t <= mu ln d+(L-1)ln d sqrt(ln(1/e_t)/(2t))+ln d/t.
At L=1 the inventory is deterministic and no truncation failure is needed.
At fixed J,delta the rate tends to mu ln d=[J+(L-1)/2]ln d. This proves the
upper in (7) without assuming any general additivity theorem. It is ordinary
Bell consumption with a counted complete initialization and a controlled
global preparation error. It is not a finite-time improvement or a source
of exact Bell output from the approximately prepared input.

The label-coordinate selection in Theorem 2 is a mathematical domination,
not a free physical local transformation across task blocks. The replay's
finite-field arithmetic does not make dense density-matrix preparation efficient.
