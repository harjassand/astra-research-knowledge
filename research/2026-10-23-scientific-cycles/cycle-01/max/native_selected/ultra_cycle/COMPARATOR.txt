STABILIZER ENTROPY MACHINERY IS KNOWN
Gross and Walter, Stabilizer information inequalities from phase space
distributions, J.Math.Phys.54(2013)082201, Theorem1 and equations6-9:
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/1302.6990
The primary proof gives the familiar isotropic-support description and entropy
of normalized stabilizer-code projectors, including reduced projectors. Its
balanced-entropy comparison should not be mistaken for a theorem about arbitrary
spectra diagonal in a stabilizer basis or these sqrt(rho) prefix scores. Here

Hall-selected label basis proves the critical tensor witness. The numerical
projector subcase alone is not the full contribution. Historical priority of
this particular application has not been established by a bounded search.
