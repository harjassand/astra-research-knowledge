AUDIT OF THE PRINCIPAL'S STABILIZER HALL REDUCTION
21 October 2026 | analytic bounded sublemma; no numerical claim

Input supplied by principal
---------------------------
Let V_{a,i} be nested subspaces of an n=rN dimensional vector space over a
field, indexed by task a=1,...,r and prefix i=1,...,N. The principal's
isotropic-support argument establishes, for any prefix choices i_a>=0,

 dim(sum_a V_{a,i_a}) >= sum_a i_a,      V_{a,0}={0}.            (H)

In the stabilizer application V_{a,i} is the character-label direction
annihilating the stabilizer subgroup trivial on task a's first i sites.
Depolarizing that physical prefix is averaging label amplitudes along V_{a,i}.
This audit checks the linear-algebra selection needed after (H), and supplies
an explicit state outside the recursive classical class.

From rectangular dimensions to every Hall subfamily
----------------------------------------------------
For an arbitrary subset J of task-prefix labels (a,j), let i_a be the largest
included j for task a, or zero if none. Nestedness gives

 sum_{(a,j) in J} V_{a,j}=sum_a V_{a,i_a}.

Consequently its dimension is at least sum_a i_a, which is at least |J|.
The last inequality allows missing intermediate prefix labels. Thus (H)
does imply the full linear Hall condition for the n individually indexed
subspaces, not merely for rectangular collections.

Self-contained independent-transversal lemma
--------------------------------------------
Suppose subspaces V_1,...,V_n over a field obey
 dim(sum_{j in J}V_j)>=|J| for every subset J.
There are linearly independent representatives v_j in V_j.

Proof by induction on n. The case n=1 follows from dim V_1>=1.
If a nonempty proper subset J is tight, let W=sum_{j in J}V_j with
dim W=|J|. The restricted family obeys the same inequalities, so choose
independent representatives on J by induction. In the quotient by W, every
K subset of J^c satisfies

 dim(sum_{k in K} pi(V_k))
 =dim(W+sum_{k in K}V_k)-dim W
 >=|J|+|K|-|J|=|K|.

Choose independent representatives for the complementary quotient family
and lift them to their respective V_k. Their union with the representatives
on J is independent.

Otherwise no nonempty proper subset is tight. All those dimensions are
integers and are at least |J|+1. Choose any nonzero v_n in V_n and quotient
by its one-dimensional span L. Every subset J of {1,...,n-1} has
dim(sum_{j in J}pi(V_j))>=dim(sum_{j in J}V_j)-1>=|J|.
Induct in the quotient, lift, and append v_n. This proves the lemma.

Application
------------
Select the n independent vectors v_{a,j} in V_{a,j}. They form a basis of
the ambient n dimensional space. Let
 W_{a,i}=span{v_{a,1},...,v_{a,i}} subset V_{a,i}.
Because all selected vectors are independent, the W_{a,i} are the usual
prefix-coordinate subspaces in a single global coordinate system, with
different tasks using disjoint coordinate sets.

If A_V is uniform averaging along a finite additive subspace V, then it is
an orthogonal projection in the uniform l2 space. For W subset V,
A_V A_W=A_V, so ||A_V c||_2<=||A_W c||_2 for every amplitude vector c.
Thus physical prefix scores dominated by the V averages are simultaneously
bounded by independent-coordinate prefixes W. The classical tensor entropy
argument in RESULT.txt then has exactly the required compatible coordinates.
There is no sorting of independently optimized envelopes in this step.

Dimension caution
------------------
The proof is over a field. For D=p prime this is direct. For D=p^s represented
by s prime-dimensional sites per D-level coordinate, work over F_p: n=srN,
and the isotropic-support inequality must supply
 dim(sum_a V_{a,i_a})>=s sum_a i_a.
Repeat each task-prefix subspace s times in the Hall family, select srN
independent representatives, and group the s representatives at each level
as one D-outcome coordinate. A field-linear F_{p^s} stabilizer formalism also
works provided its symplectic and maximal-isotropic dimensions are stated.
An arbitrary composite-D Pauli module has no automatic vector-space Hall
proof; that case is not established by this audit.

Explicit globally stabilizer-diagonal state outside recursive CQ
----------------------------------------------------------------
Take two qubit tasks, N=1, and their Bell basis
 Phi+ =(|00>+|11>)/sqrt2, Phi-=(|00>-|11>)/sqrt2,
 Psi+ =(|01>+|10>)/sqrt2, Psi-=(|01>-|10>)/sqrt2.
Let rho be diagonal in this basis with probabilities
 (4/10,3/10,2/10,1/10), respectively.

This is a full-rank globally stabilizer-diagonal state, for the maximal
commuting Pauli group generated by X tensor X and Z tensor Z. All four
eigenvalues are distinct, so its eigenvectors are fixed up to phases and
each is entangled across the two task blocks. A CQ state on either task
has a full orthonormal eigenbasis of product vectors, since each classical
block can be diagonalized on the other task. Distinct eigenvalues would
then force a product eigenvector for each eigenvalue, a contradiction.
Thus rho is neither CQ nor QC and is outside the two-task recursive class.

For this example, both physical one-site depolarizations average the label
amplitudes over all of F_2^2. If s=sum_j sqrt(lambda_j), then each actual
prefix score is s^2/4. Hall selects one independent direction for each task;
the resulting independent-coordinate scores dominate these physical scores.
No claim of a nontrivial numerical violation, advantage or optimal constant
is based on this N=1 class-separation example.
