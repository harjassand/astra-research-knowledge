INDEPENDENT CQ AUDIT — 2026-10-21

Verdict: the proposed recursive locally classical boundary is valid, with the
same one-task constant C_D. It does not require a common product eigenbasis.

Conventions. A whole task has Hilbert space (C^D)^{tensor N}. For a density
matrix rho on r tasks, define
 F_{b,i}(rho) = D^{-i} Tr[(Tr_{b,first i} sqrt(rho))^2].
Put m = ln D. Assume the established one-task inequality
 S(sigma) >= m sum_{i=1}^N F_i(sigma) - C_D
for every one-task density matrix sigma, with C_D independent of N.

ONE-STEP LEMMA
Suppose one whole task a is classical in an orthonormal family:
 rho = sum_j p_j |e_j><e_j|_a tensor rho_j,
where rho_j are normalized states on the remaining tasks. Zero-weight terms
are omitted. Then
 (1) F_{b,i}(rho) = sum_j p_j F_{b,i}(rho_j), b != a;
 (2) F_{a,i}(rho) <= F_i(rho_a), rho_a = sum_j p_j |e_j><e_j|;
 (3) S(rho) = S(rho_a) + sum_j p_j S(rho_j).

Proof. Orthogonality gives the exact square root
 sqrt(rho) = sum_j sqrt(p_j) |e_j><e_j| tensor sqrt(rho_j).
For b != a, taking the indicated partial trace leaves the mutually orthogonal
projectors on a. Squaring therefore removes all j != k terms; tracing the
remaining projectors gives (1).

For b = a let A_j = Tr_{a,first i}|e_j><e_j|. Each A_j is positive, and
 F_{a,i}(rho) = D^{-i} sum_{j,k} sqrt(p_j p_k)
                 Tr(A_j A_k) Tr(sqrt(rho_j) sqrt(rho_k)).
Both factors in every summand are real and nonnegative: Tr(AB) >= 0 for
positive A,B. Hilbert--Schmidt Cauchy--Schwarz also gives
 0 <= Tr(sqrt(rho_j) sqrt(rho_k)) <=
      sqrt(Tr(rho_j) Tr(rho_k)) = 1.
Termwise replacement of this factor by 1 gives exactly F_i(rho_a), proving
(2). Finally rho is a direct sum of the blocks p_j rho_j, so its entropy is
H(p) + sum_j p_j S(rho_j), while S(rho_a) = H(p). This proves (3).

RECURSIVE CLASS AND THEOREM
Let C_1 contain all one-task density matrices. For r >= 2 let C_r contain a
state if it admits the above CQ decomposition for at least one whole task a,
with every positive-weight child rho_j in C_{r-1}. The choice of the next
classical task and its local eigenbasis may depend on every preceding branch.
Then every rho in C_r obeys
 S(rho) >= m sum_{b=1}^r sum_{i=1}^N F_{b,i}(rho) - r C_D.

Proof by induction. The one-task inequality proves r=1. At the first CQ split,
the entropy identity, the one-task bound on rho_a, and the induction hypothesis
on each rho_j give
 S(rho) >= m sum_i F_i(rho_a)
         + m sum_j p_j sum_{b != a,i} F_{b,i}(rho_j) - r C_D.
Use (1) on all remaining tasks and (2) on a to obtain the assertion.

An equivalent induction statement allows a child to split into an independent
tensor product of previously certified subtrees; its total cost is the number
of whole task leaves times C_D. This does not enlarge the finite recursive
class, because a tensor product of recursive CQ trees can itself be ordered as
one such tree.

SCOPE / HIDDEN-ISSUE CHECKS
* The local e_j can be arbitrarily entangled among the N sites within task a.
  Thus the result is not limited to a basis aligned with the prefix factors.
* The remaining rho_j can have different eigenbases conditional on j. No single
  common product eigenbasis is assumed.
* An orthogonal-support product decomposition
    rho = sum_j p_j sigma_a^j tensor rho_j,
  sigma_a^j sigma_a^k = 0 for j != k,
  obeys the same one-step proof (replace |e_j><e_j| by sqrt(sigma_a^j)). It is
  already included: diagonalize each sigma_a^j and refine the classical label.
* Every member is separable across whole tasks, but arbitrary separability does
  not supply this CQ decomposition. Mixtures with incompatible first-task
  classical bases are not certified by this argument.
* The entropy identity is essential. Replacing orthogonal e_j by nonorthogonal
  local states breaks both the exact square-root expansion and that identity.
* Positivity is essential to the displayed termwise proof. The parent positive-
  representative reduction may first be applied to the coefficient matrix, but
  it does not by itself imply the resulting density is recursively CQ.
* This proves the true ordinary tensor inequality on a structural class. It
  neither invokes generic E_F additivity nor the disproved complete conditional
  strengthening. It does not settle positive matrices with entangled eigenvectors
  across whole tasks.

No numerical computation or literature search was needed for this audit.
