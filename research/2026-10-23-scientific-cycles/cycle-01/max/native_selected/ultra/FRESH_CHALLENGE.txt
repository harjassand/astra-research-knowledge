1. SIMULTANEOUS SORTED-SCHMIDT DOMINANCE: PASSES

I attempted to break the passage from a maximally correlated coefficient vector
to arbitrary bipartite outputs, in particular by choosing physical prefix bases
incompatible at different i. That does not break the proof.

For a pure output psi, let a_1>=...>=a_q>=0 be its Schmidt roots, q=D^N. For
L=D^i, the Bell-prefix probability is the squared norm of the orthogonal
projection onto Phi_L tensor H_rest, hence
  sqrt(p_i)=max_eta |<psi|Phi_L tensor eta>|.
If the Schmidt roots of eta are b_j, the target's Schmidt roots are b_j/sqrt(L),
each repeated L times. Von Neumann's singular-value trace inequality gives
  sqrt(p_i) <= (1/sqrt(L)) sum_j b_j A_j,
  A_j=sum_{k=(j-1)L+1}^{jL} a_k.
The b_j have squared sum one, so p_i <= (1/L)sum_j A_j^2 = B_i.
Moreover A_j is descending, so the maximizing b_j proportional to A_j respects
the sorting restriction. Only the upper bound is needed.

The same list a is used at every i. Indexing it by little-endian D-ary digits
makes the averaging blocks of size D^i exactly the nested tensor projections
Q_i. No claim is needed that one physical pair of local unitaries optimizes all
prefixes of the original psi. One synthetic maximally correlated vector with
this sorted list realizes every B_i, and the actual p_i are simultaneously
bounded by those quantities. This answers the principal compatibility attack.

I also rederived the entropy induction without using the sorted assumption.
The one-site inequality is H(p)>=m r-C(1-r), r=(sum sqrt(p))^2/D,
with C=2D-m (or the stated qubit choice). For c_xy>=0, q_y=sum_x c_xy^2,
r_y=(sum_x sqrt(p_x|y))^2/D, s=sum_y q_y r_y, and a_y=sqrt(q_y r_y),
the induction defect is at most
  s C + m s + s H(qr/s)-H(q)-sum_y q_y H(p_.|y).
The two estimates
  s H(qr/s)<=H(q),
  sum_y q_y H(p_.|y)>=m s-C(1-s)
give precisely C, with no accumulated horizon penalty. The first estimate is
ordinary classical conditioning on an auxiliary bit, not resource
postselection. Replacing complex c by |c| increases all Q_i norms and preserves
entropy. Convex-roof averaging then proves (A) for arbitrary finite output rho.

The kernels, dimensions, entropy signs, normalization, and direction of each
inequality are consistent. The constant is horizon-independent only at fixed
D, as stated. Joint prefix probabilities are essential; separate one-pair
fidelities cannot replace them.

2. EXACT FACTORY AND NORMAL INFINITE INTERMEDIATE MEMORY: PASSES

The proof need not define E_F on the infinite intermediate memory at all.
Fix a preselected factory branch r, with a finite integer Bell rank M_r, and a
finite reuse horizon N. Include all outcomes of that branch's factory and all
reuse calls, and finally discard every system except the finite external output.
This is an LOCC preparation from Phi_{M_r} and separable local ancillas.

A precise justification of the Schmidt-number step is available:
  (i) A pure initial vector, including local pure ancillas, has Schmidt rank
      M_r. Product local Kraus operators never raise that rank.
 (ii) Normal local CP maps on the separable type-I Hilbert spaces in R1 admit
      countable Kraus realizations. Local mixed normal ancillas have countable
      spectral decompositions. Resolving these indices yields mixtures of
      rank-at-most-M_r vectors. Partial local traces also have such Kraus
      resolutions, so discarding the intermediate memory causes no problem.
(iii) Standard continuous-outcome instruments, countable terminating protocols,
      or normal LOCC limits give finite-output states in the trace-norm closure
      of these mixtures. In the fixed finite output space, the convex hull of
      normalized rank-at-most-M_r projectors is compact, hence closed.
Thus the finite output has Schmidt number at most M_r and E_F<=ln M_r.

This argument is about LOCC, not about an arbitrary normal CP map. Normality by
itself would not supply a rank bound. The usual LOCC/LOCC-limit interpretation
of the factory definition is sufficient. The R1 device is even explicitly
finite-round and finite-outcome at every finite horizon. If a future text uses
some more general meaning of "normal LOCC", its operational-limit definition
should be stated instead of assuming that normality alone proves the result.

For countably many preselected input branches, countable convexity of finite-
dimensional E_F gives
  E_F(output)<=sum_r w_r E_F(output_r)<=sum_r w_r ln M_r.
One can justify countable convexity by a finite branch prefix plus one normalized
tail, then send the tail weight to zero; the output E_F is bounded by N ln D.
No subtraction of infinite entropies and no continuity estimate on the memory
is involved. The tail limit is taken at each fixed finite N.

This does not claim E_F(output_r)<=ln M_r for an outcome-dependent amount of
Bell supply selected AFTER a successful postselection. Here r fixes the incoming
Bell inventory before the factory, and every subsequent outcome is averaged.
Each supplied branch's Bell rank, all incoming entanglement, and every retained
flag must be counted. The stated definition meets those requirements.

3. STOPPING, COST LIMITS, AND THE NO-LOG LAW: PASSES

Exact first-block purity gives T(tau)=Phi_D tensor tau_1 and S(tau)=tau_1.
The CP success map S need not be LOCC. This is harmless: S is used only to
compute linear joint-prefix probabilities, while the entire unconditioned
output preparation used for the resource bound is LOCC.

Writing p_k=Tr S^k(tau),
  p_k = 1-sum_{i=0}^{k-2} Tr S^i(tau-tau_1).
Every (S*)^i(I) is an effect, and tau-tau_1 is trace zero with positive-part
trace at most delta. Therefore p_k>=[1-(k-1)delta]_+. This is one fixed
correlated output process; it assumes no independence or recurrence.

Combining the finite-output witness and the factory inequality at EACH finite N
proves (B). Taking the factory infimum afterward is legitimate because the same
lower bound holds for every factory. For 0<delta<=1, N=ceil(1/delta) gives the
stated m/(2delta)+m/2-C_D bound. At delta=0, no exchange of infima or infinite
output limit is necessary: every purported finite-cost factory must exceed
Nm-C_D for each integer N, a contradiction.

The erased-label factory has finite support K=J,...,J+L-1,
L=ceil(J/delta), and supplies mean [J+(L-1)/2]ln d <=m/(2delta)+m.
Its unflagged full memory is exactly the R1 reservoir. The established return
formula applies because L>=J>=j. It meets every j<=J return condition, and
therefore also terminal return. Both task infima consequently lie in the
claimed bounded-width interval for each fixed J,d. No negative divergent
logarithmic correction can remain for this exact preselected average inventory
resource. This statement does not extend to the general iid E_C optimum.

4. A REAL OPTIONAL FLAG COUNTEREXAMPLE AND REPAIR

PROOF.txt's optional sentence says that "Retaining K in memory instead" gives
return at most j/L. For the channel actually specified immediately before it
(emit/shift/append, with no action on a K flag), this is false.

Let the two parties retain orthogonal copies of the initial K label. Then
  tau_flag=(1/L)sum_{K=J}^{J+L-1} |KK><KK|_flags tensor rho_{M,K},
where M=J+L-1. If the flags remain unchanged, after j<=J calls the complete
memory is
  tau_flag'=(1/L)sum_K |KK><KK|_flags tensor rho_{M,K-j}.
Orthogonal flag blocks imply
  T(tau_flag,tau_flag')=(1/L)sum_K T(rho_{M,K},rho_{M,K-j}).
The common tensor factors cancel, leaving T(Phi_d^tensorj,chi_d^tensorj).
Since the latter chi is the normalized identity on a d^j-dimensional correlated
subspace containing Phi_d^tensorj, this distance is exactly 1-d^-j.
For example d=2,j=1,L=10 gives 1/2, which exceeds j/L=1/10.

Repair options:
  A. Delete the optional flagged sentence. The unflagged exact factory already
     proves every upper bound actually needed.
  B. Explicitly use a different extended fixed device whose shared classical
     flag is the CURRENT inventory and is decremented, K->max(K-1,0), on every
     call. Initial flags are K=J,...,J+L-1. At j<=J, their range shifts by j;
     the L-j overlapping current-inventory blocks coincide exactly. The
     complete return distance is j/L. Both local parties can update their
     classical flags by LOCC and no extra Bell supply is needed.
An immutable record of the original sampled K must not be retained in option B;
it would reinstate the counterexample. This illustrates why "count every
retained input/resource flag" is a substantive condition.

5. THE PARTICULAR MC RESERVOIR AND E_C-E_R: PASSES

For tau_N, direct prefix overlaps give
  sum_i p_i=(N+1)/2+(1/N)sum_{h=1}^{N-1}(N-h)D^-h.
The correction is nonnegative and bounded at fixed D. Thus (A) yields
E_F(tau_N)>=B_N-C_D. The explicit finite rung preparation gives
E_F(tau_N)<=C_av^ex(tau_N)<=B_N, B_N=(N+1)ln D/2.

I checked the additivity hypothesis against the primary Vidal-Duer-Cirac
paper, not merely its abstract. Its theorem is exactly strong additivity for
states supported on a subspace whose partial-trace restriction is entanglement
breaking; see theorem/equation (12), page 2 of the PDF:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0112131
For V=span{|xx>}, Tr_B restricted through |x>->|xx> is complete dephasing,
which is measure-and-prepare and therefore entanglement breaking. This applies
in the finite dimension D^N at every fixed N, giving E_C(tau_N)=E_F(tau_N).

There is also a short direct MC check. Any pure vector in V tensor H_ab can
be written sum_x sqrt(p_x)|xx> tensor |eta_x>_ab. Tracing B and b produces
orthogonal x blocks, so its entanglement is
  H(p)+sum_x p_x E(eta_x).
The first term upper-bounds the E_F of its V marginal, and the second
upper-bounds the E_F of its ab marginal. Applying this to every pure vector
in a decomposition of tau_N tensor omega, and then using convexity, gives the
strong-additivity lower bound. Tensor-product decompositions give the reverse
bound. This independently checks the specialization needed here.

Winter-Yang, Theorem 9 and its proof, also confirms coherence-of-formation
additivity and the MC identification:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.07975
Provenance qualification: their displayed proof of Theorem 9 explicitly invokes
Vidal-Duer-Cirac for MC E_F additivity. Therefore this is a second matched
formulation, not an independent underlying source theorem. PROOF.txt's wording
"second matched route" is acceptable if it is not marketed as independent
literature corroboration.

I rechecked R1's spectral ledger symbolically: the commuting phase-string
ensemble obeys H(Z)=H(K)+(N-EK)ln D-H(K|Z), whence
  E_R(tau_N)=B_N-ln N+h_N, 0<=h_N<=c_D.
Combining this identity with the finite sandwich for E_F, E_C, and exact
average factory cost gives precisely
  ln N-C_D-c_D <= cost(tau_N)-E_R(tau_N) <= ln N.
There is no interchange of N->infinity with the iid copy limit: the additivity
and E_C=E_F identity are applied at fixed N first. The claimed gap is therefore
ln N+O_D(1) for this particular state family. No claim about general MC convex-
roof evaluation, generic memory E_C, finite block synthesis efficiency, or a
universal tensorization of (A) follows.
