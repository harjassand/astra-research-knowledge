THE RESULTS

Let m=lnD,D>=2. There is a finite constant C_D independent of horizonN such that
for EVERY state rho on (C^D)^tensorN_A tensor (C^D)^tensorN_B,
  E_F(rho) >= m sum_{i=1}^N Tr[(Phi_D^tensori tensor I)rho] - C_D.           (A)
One can take C_D=min{2D-lnD, lnD/(1-D^-1/2)^2}. For D=2 an elementary two-point inequality permits
C_2=2-ln2. Constants are not optimized. The prefix projections are joint across
ALL i released pairs; they are not products of marginal accept probabilities.
No independence between outputs is assumed.

For R1's same fixed normal LOCC device, complete counted tensor-product memory,
exact first J rank-d Bell outputs, and complete-memory return half-trace error
at most delta after Jcalls, put D=d^J. Define C_av^ex(tau) as the exact expected
supplied Bell cost of a factory for tau, specified below. For EVERY N>=1,
  C_av^ex(tau) >= m sum_{k=1}^N [1-(k-1)delta]_+ - C_D.                  (B)
Also C_av^ex>=m. In particular
  C_av^ex(tau) >= m/(2delta)+m/2-C_D,    0<delta<=1.                    (C)
Finite-dimensional initial E_F obeys the same inequalities by LOCC monotonicity.
For delta=0 no finite expected-cost exact factory can do this task.

The optimally required C_av^ex under terminal or all-prefix return consequently
satisfies, for fixed J,d,
  C_av,end^*(J,d,delta) = C_av,all^*(J,d,delta)
                        + O_{J,d}(1)
                      = J ln d/(2delta)+O_{J,d}(1).                    (D)
The first equality only denotes bounded asymptotic difference, not exact finite-
delta equality. More precisely both optima lie between m/(2delta)+m/2-C_D
and m/(2delta)+m. There is NO negative divergent logarithmic term under this
resource. We do not determine either optimum's bounded additive constant.

For the actual R1 unflagged reservoir

  chi_D=(1/D)sum_a |aa><aa|,

  B_N-C_D <= E_C(tau_N)=E_F(tau_N) <= C_av^ex(tau_N) <= B_N.            (E)

2. ELEMENTARY ENTROPY VERSUS NESTED-AVERAGING LEMMA

Let v=D^-1/2(1,...,1), and Q_i=|v><v|^tensori tensor I on a complex D^N-vector c
of norm1. Let H(c^2) mean H((|c_z|^2)_z). For all N,
  H(c^2) >= m sum_i ||Q_i c||^2-C_D.                                  (G)
We supply a self-contained induction; no dimension substitution/continuity bound.

One site. For p a D-outcome distribution, let a=D^-1/2 sum_x sqrt(p_x), r=a^2.
Using ln t<=t-1,
  KL(p||u_D)<=chi^2(p,u_D)=D sum_x(p_x-1/D)^2.
Set f_x=sqrt(Dp_x), with uniform average E_u f^2=1,E_u f=a. Then
chi^2=Var_u(f^2)=(1/(2D^2))sum_{x,y}(f_x-f_y)^2(f_x+f_y)^2.
For x!=y, f_x^2+f_y^2<=D, so (f_x+f_y)^2<=2D. Diagonal terms vanish.
Hence chi^2<=2D Var_u f=2D(1-r). With L_D=2D,C_D=L_D-m this yields
  H(p)>=m-L_D(1-r)=m r-C_D(1-r).                                    (H)
This first proof uses C_D=2D-lnD, which is positive. For D=2, the smaller L_2=2 follows directly: put x=|2p_1-1|,
f(x)=KL(p||u_2),g(x)=1-sqrt(1-x^2). At0 both vanish; f'=atanh x and
 g'=x/sqrt(1-x^2)>=atanh x, since their derivatives are ordered.
Thus f<=g=2(1-r); endpoints by continuity. This is not a claim of the optimum
constant in (G), nor a reopening of R1's additive-constant problem.

Complex phases. The kernels Q_i have nonnegative matrix entries, so
||Q_i c||<=||Q_i |c|||. Replacing c by entrywise absolute values preserves entropy
and increases all overlaps. It suffices to prove (G) for c>=0.

Induction, including any residual finite/countable coordinate. For N=0,
-H(c^2)<=0<=C_D. Split c_{xy}, with x the first D-valued coordinate and y all
remaining coordinates. Put q_y=sum_x c_xy^2, p_{x|y}=c_xy^2/q_y when q_y>0,
r_y=(sum_x sqrt(p_{x|y}))^2/D, and a_y=D^-1/2 sum_x c_xy=sqrt(q_y r_y).
Let s=sum_y a_y^2=sum_y q_y r_y. All probabilities at q_y=0 contribute0.
The first projection has squared norm s. Factoring off its uniform first
coordinate, Q_i c corresponds to Q_{i-1}a for i>=2. Applying the induction
to a/sqrt(s) and the chain rule gives
 m sum_i||Q_i c||^2-H(c^2)
 <= s C_D+m s+s H((q_y r_y/s)_y)-H(q)-sum_y q_y H(p_.|y).

The entropy term sH(qr/s)<=H(q): take Y~q and an auxiliary CLASSICAL accept
bit A with Pr(A=1|Y=y)=r_y. Then
H(Y)>=H(Y|A)>=sH(Y|A=1). This is an entropy identity used to prove a bound;
no accept branch is physically postselected as a resource operation.
Averaging (H) gives sum_y q_y H(p_.|y)>=m s-C_D(1-s). Substitution gives at
most sC_D+C_D(1-s)=C_D. This closes the induction. If entropy is infinite,
(G) is automatic; for finite entropy all required marginal/conditional terms
are finite. For our main finite-output theorem every space here is finite.

A second elementary bound controls D-dependence. Replace amplitudes by their
absolute values and sort their squared values p_j decreasing. For every equal-
size block length D^i, consecutive sorted blocks maximize the sum of squared
block sums: their block-sum vector majorizes any other grouping's vector,
because the sum of its largest k groups is the sum of the largest kD^i entries.
Convexity of the square proves the assertion. Thus one common sorted vector
simultaneously upper-bounds every Q_i overlap.

Partition sorted indices into A_0={1} and A_t={D^(t-1)+1,...,D^t},1<=t<=N;
let q_t be shell probability mass. Since p_j<=1/j,
 H(p)>=m sum_{t>=1}(t-1)q_t.
For a given i, every shell t>i is a union of complete D^i blocks; Cauchy-Schwarz
bounds its contribution to ||Q_i c||^2 by q_t. The first D^i entries form one
block. Its contribution is at most
 D^-i (sum_{t=0}^i sqrt(|A_t|)sqrt(q_t))^2
 <=(sum_{t=0}^i D^{-(i-t)/2}sqrt(q_t))^2.
Summing these inner terms over i is at most (1-D^-1/2)^-2: convolution with
the nonnegative geometric kernel has l2 norm at most its l1 norm times the
l2 norm of sqrt(q), which is1. This last inequality also follows directly by
weighted Cauchy-Schwarz and summing the geometric series. Each outer shell
q_t was charged at most t-1 times. Therefore
 m sum_i||Q_i c||^2-H(p)<=m/(1-D^-1/2)^2.
Combining the independent valid bounds permits the stated minimum C_D.
In particular C_D<=kappa m for ALL D>=2, with the explicit universal
kappa=(1-2^-1/2)^-2. Thus the factory lower bound is also
 C_av^ex >= J ln d[1/(2delta)+1/2-kappa],
uniformly in J,d. This is a parameter-control corollary, not the main new
preparation/logarithmic-gap obligation.

An independently developed last-coordinate induction proves a stronger

3. SIMULTANEOUS SCHMIDT DOMINANCE — THE NEW UNIVERSAL STEP

Let |psi> be any pure bipartite state of N D-level output pairs. Write its sorted
Schmidt probabilities lambda_1>=...>=lambda_{D^N}, padding zeros if needed.
Let c=(sqrt(lambda_j)) indexed so the FIRST i D-ary coordinates run through
consecutive blocks of D^i indices (little-endian convention). For each i set
L=D^i and
  B_i=(1/L)sum_j (sum_{k=(j-1)L+1}^{jL}sqrt(lambda_k))^2=||Q_i c||^2.
We prove that its ACTUAL Bell prefix probability p_i is at most B_i, for ALL i
with this single sorted vector, even when psi is not maximally correlated.

The range of Phi_L tensor I consists of |Phi_L> tensor |eta> for arbitrary
rest-pair vectors eta. Thus p_i=max_{||eta||=1}|<psi|Phi_L tensor eta>|^2;
if p_i=0 the statement is immediate. If eta has sorted Schmidt probabilities
r_j, Phi_L tensor eta has sorted Schmidt roots sqrt(r_j/L), each repeated
L times. The singular-value trace inequality bounds the absolute overlap of
any two bipartite vectors by the scalar product of their sorted Schmidt roots.
Consequently
 sqrt(p_i)<=sup_r sum_j sqrt(r_j/L) sum_{k=(j-1)L+1}^{jL}sqrt(lambda_k)
 <=sqrt(B_i), by Cauchy-Schwarz and sum r_j=1.
The optimizing block-sum weights are descending, so the sorted restriction
causes no gap. Only the upper bound is needed. The SAME sorted lambda and
block convention work for every i; we are not optimizing a different spectrum
or adding unrelated independently optimized Schmidt parameters across i.

Entropy of entanglement is H(lambda). Lemma(G) and p_i<=B_i give
 S(psi_A)>=m sum_i p_i-C_D. Every pure ensemble of any mixed rho averages
these prefix expectations to Tr(P_i rho). Taking its convex-roof infimum proves
(A). This retains arbitrary correlations across released outputs. A retained
memory can be discarded before applying the finite-output witness; it is not
