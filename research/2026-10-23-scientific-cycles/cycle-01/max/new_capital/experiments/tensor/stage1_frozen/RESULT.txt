BOUNDED TENSOR TRIAL — STAGE 1
Owner of source and integration: Ultra. Trial participant: trial_tensor_owner.
Date: 21 October 2026. All logarithms natural.

VERDICT
Partial analytic obstruction, not a solution of the tensor witness conjecture.
The direct complete/conditional log-Sobolev strengthening of the source's
single-block entropy inequality is FALSE at the critical coefficient, even
allowing an arbitrary finite D-dependent additive constant. An explicit positive
matrix family forces that constant to grow at least N ln D. More generally the
coefficient of the hierarchical Dirichlet form must be at least 2 if the additive
constant is to be independent of N. This identifies a concrete obstruction to a
standard tensorization route. It does not establish that coefficient 2 suffices.
The original requested tensor inequality remains unresolved. There is no
collective counterexample to that inequality in this report.

1. EXACT MATRIX FORM OF THE ORIGINAL OBLIGATION

Fix D >= 2, N >= 1, r >= 1, m=ln D, and d=D^(Nr). Identify a pure bipartite
vector with a d-by-d coefficient matrix X through
  |psi_X> = d^(-1/2) sum_{u,v} X_uv |u>_A |v>_B,
where tau(X*X)=Tr(X*X)/d=1. The entropy of its A marginal is
  S(psi_A)=ln d - Ent_tau(X*X),
  Ent_tau(Z)=tau(Z ln Z) when tau(Z)=1.
Nonzero spectra of X*X and XX* agree, so either product gives that entropy.

For task block a and prefix i, let k=D^i and define the conditional expectation
  E_(a,i)(X) = (I_prefix/k) tensor Tr_prefix X,
with all other matrix tensor factors retained. The partial trace here is the
ordinary, unnormalized matrix partial trace, not a partial trace of psi.
The normalized Schatten norm is ||X||_(2,tau)^2=tau(X*X).

Projecting |psi_X> onto a Bell vector in that prefix replaces its coefficient
matrix by E_(a,i)(X). Consequently the exact identity is
  <psi_X|P_i^(a)|psi_X> = ||E_(a,i)(X)||_(2,tau)^2.                (1)
This follows also by expanding the Bell contraction, which averages the equal
row/column prefix indices and leaves the remaining coefficient matrix intact.

Define the positive self-adjoint superoperator
  L_(N,r)=m sum_(a,i) (Id-E_(a,i)).
Each E is an orthogonal projection for the normalized Hilbert-Schmidt inner
product. Therefore
  tau(X* L_(N,r)(X)) = m sum_(a,i) (1-||E_(a,i)(X)||_2^2).
The desired tensor witness, with constant K_D per block, is EXACTLY
  Ent_tau(X*X) <= tau(X* L_(N,r)(X)) + r K_D                 (2)
for every normalized coefficient matrix X, without a positivity assumption.
Thus source PROOF.txt proves (2) at r=1 and K_D=C_D, including all nonpositive
and complex X. It does not prove a complete or amplified conditional inequality.
This reformulation uses no E_F additivity assumption.

2. THE NATURAL CONDITIONAL STRENGTHENING AND WHY IT WOULD HELP

For one block S of dimension D^N and an arbitrary spectator R, use normalized
traces throughout and set tau_SR(X*X)=1. Write tau_S for normalized partial trace.
A natural complete strengthening would assert, for arbitrary X,
  J_S(X) := Ent_tau_SR(X*X)
             -Ent_tau_R(tau_S(X*X))
          <= m sum_i (1-||(E_i tensor Id_R)(X)||_2^2) + K_D.    (3)
Here K_D must not depend on N or dim R.

The conditional term in (3), for positive X, is precisely the conditional
entropy expression in the q=2 completely bounded log-Sobolev definition of
Beigi and King, arXiv:1509.02610v3, Definition 3, equation (13); their energy
normalization is alpha q^2. Their Theorem 6 explains why such complete estimates
are an attractive tensorization route. This report tests the particular energy
and critical coefficient actually needed here, with an additive defect.
Primary statement read directly: https://arxiv.org/html/1509.02610v3 .

For clarity about sufficiency, if (3) held for every coefficient matrix X, then
summing it over task blocks would prove (2). Indeed put rho=X*X/d_total. For a
block a of local dimension d_a,
  J_a(X)=ln d_a + S(rho_rest_a)-S(rho).
Quantum entropy's complement form of Shearer's inequality, obtained by repeated
strong subadditivity, gives
  sum_a S(rho_rest_a) >= (r-1) S(rho).
Hence Ent_tau(X*X) <= sum_a J_a(X), and the sum of the right sides of (3) is
exactly the energy in (2) plus r K_D. This explains the proposed missing step.
We do NOT assert that the positive-X version alone automatically handles all
coefficient matrices. The counterexample below is already positive, so it
invalidates either version of this proposed strengthening.

3. EXACT COUNTERFAMILY TO (3)

Let S and R both have dimension d_0=D^N. Give both the same D-ary tensor
factorization. Define
  |Omega_d0> = d_0^(-1/2) sum_{j=1}^{d_0} |j>_S |j>_R,
  Q=|Omega_d0><Omega_d0|,
  X=d_0 Q.
This is a positive matrix, and the total matrix dimension is d_0^2.
Since X^2=d_0^2 Q,
  tau_SR(X^2)=1,
  Ent_tau_SR(X^2)=ln(d_0^2)=2 N m,
  tau_S(X^2)=I_R,
  Ent_tau_R(tau_S(X^2))=0.
Thus
  J_S(X)=2 N m.                                               (4)

Now split the prefix of size k=D^i from a suffix of size h=d_0/k. Up to a
permutation of tensor factors, Q=Q_k tensor Q_h. Direct partial trace gives
  (E_i tensor Id_R)(X)
    = (d_0/k^2) I_(S_prefix) tensor I_(R_prefix) tensor Q_h.
The normalized squared Hilbert-Schmidt norm is therefore
  ||(E_i tensor Id_R)(X)||_2^2
    = (d_0^2/k^4) k^2 / d_0^2
    = k^(-2)=D^(-2i).                                        (5)
There is no asymptotic or continuity estimate in this calculation.

Put
  g_(N,D)=sum_(i=1)^N D^(-2i)
         =(1-D^(-2N))/(D^2-1).
Equations (4)-(5) show that (3) would require
  2 N m <= m (N-g_(N,D)) + K_D,
or
  K_D >= N m + m g_(N,D).                                    (6)
For every fixed D this tends to infinity with N. Thus NO finite constant K_D
can repair (3) at the energy coefficient 1.

More generally, replacing the energy in (3) by a times that energy requires
  K_D >= (2-a) N m + a m g_(N,D).                             (7)
For every fixed real a<2 the right side diverges. Hence a>=2 is a necessary
condition for any uniform estimate of this form. This necessary condition does
not prove a complete inequality at a=2 or at any larger coefficient.

An ordinary marginal entropy chain rule has the same warning sign. For the
physical vector |Omega_d0>_(A1,A2) tensor |Omega_d0>_(B1,B2), the conditional
entropy S(A1|A2)=-N m. Consequently a per-block lower bound on S(A1|A2) by the
Bell-prefix witness minus a constant also fails unboundedly. Internal entanglement
on a single party is unconstrained by the original bipartite problem.

4. WHY THIS IS NOT A COUNTEREXAMPLE TO THE REQUESTED TENSOR WITNESS

Treat S and R in the preceding construction as two task blocks of the coefficient
matrix. The resulting physical bipartite vector is
  |psi_X> = |Omega_d0>_(A1,A2) tensor |conj(Omega_d0)>_(B1,B2).
It is a product across A_all:B_all, so its bipartite entanglement entropy is zero.
Every task's physical prefix has Bell probability D^(-2i). The original witness
therefore has total positive term only 2 m g_(N,D), a bounded quantity.
Its required defect per task is m g_(N,D) <= m/(D^2-1), far below the constants
available in source PROOF.txt (including C_2=2-ln 2).

Equivalently, the full two-block ordinary entropy is 2 N m and the full energy
is 2 m (N-g_(N,D)); their difference is only 2 m g_(N,D). The conditional scheme
counts the strong S:R correlation twice and loses an extensive amount before
summing. This is the precise reason the failure of (3) is compatible with (2).
The result rules out only this direct complete-entropy route at critical
coefficient, not every possible use of hypercontractivity or conditional bounds.
A proof exploiting cancellation between block correlations remains possible.

5. CONDITIONAL OPERATIONAL BRIDGE, WITH EXPLICIT ORDER OF LIMITS

This section is a proved implication, conditional on obtaining the actual tensor
witness (2). It is not an unconditional E_C lower bound.
Suppose (2) holds uniformly for all r,N with a finite K_D. Convex-roof averaging
then gives, for every r-task mixed output omega,
  E_F(omega) >= m sum_(a,i) Tr(P_i^(a) omega)-r K_D.           (8)

Fix an exact initialized memory tau and FIX a finite output horizon N. Let its
normal LOCC device produce the finite output state rho_N=Gamma_N(tau), with
actual joint-prefix probabilities p_i. Consider any approximate iid preparation
protocol supplying a maximally entangled resource of rank M_t and preparing
sigma_t with half-trace distance
  T(sigma_t,tau^(tensor t)) <= epsilon_t, epsilon_t -> 0.
Apply Gamma_N independently to all t memories and discard all remaining memory.
The finite output tilde_rho_t satisfies
  T(tilde_rho_t,rho_N^(tensor t)) <= epsilon_t.
Define W_t=m sum_(a=1)^t sum_(i=1)^N P_i^(a). Since
  0 <= W_t <= m t N I,
the effect characterization of half-trace distance gives
  Tr(W_t tilde_rho_t) >= t m sum_i p_i -m t N epsilon_t.      (9)
Every outcome of the preparation and the device is included. Finite-output
LOCC monotonicity/Schmidt-number reasoning gives E_F(tilde_rho_t)<=ln M_t.
Combining with (8)-(9),
  (ln M_t)/t >= m sum_i p_i -K_D -m N epsilon_t.              (10)
Take t->infinity at this FIXED N. The error term vanishes. Infimizing achievable
rates proves the proposed formation bound at this N. Only afterward may N be
chosen or increased in the original stopping argument. For the exact reference
memory the source gives p_i >= [1-(i-1)delta]_+, yielding its desired resource
law with K_D in place of C_D if the uniform tensor theorem is eventually proved.

This implication needs no dimension-dependent continuity estimate for E_F,
no false assertion that approximate preparation has exact Bell outputs, and no
exchange of t, N, and epsilon limits. Approximate outputs are handled explicitly
by (9). It asserts no finite block-length efficiency and no simultaneous
N=N(t) limit; that would require at least N(t) epsilon_t -> 0 and additional
control of the task family. For infinite normal memories, the implication
applies to any actual trace-norm iid preparation protocols of the stated form;
it does not assume an unstated infinite-dimensional characterization of E_C.

6. CHECKS, FAILED ROUTES, AND REMAINING GAP

Analytic: all normalization factors, dimensions, traces, entropy expressions,
and geometric sums of the obstruction were checked directly above. A deterministic
matrix calculation independently reproduced (4)-(6) for D,N=(2,1),(2,2),(2,3),
(3,1),(3,2), with no random starts or optimization. Code and actual observations
are in check_conditional_obstruction.py and COMPUTE_RECEIPT.json. The largest
matrix was 81 by 81. These are checks of an exact family, not evidence for a
uniform conjecture from small instances.

Unsuccessful or incomplete paths considered in the bounded trial:
* Summing the source's Schmidt-spectrum bound independently over tasks gives
  the same global entropy budget r times and cannot prove (2).
* A direct S(A_block|A_rest) induction fails because that entropy can be
  -N ln D for purely local internal entanglement; explicit family above.
* Complete log-Sobolev tensorization is a credible machinery route, but its
  required critical-coefficient premise is disproved by (6), not merely unproved.
* Standard product depolarizing inequalities with a larger energy coefficient
  do not imply the desired witness: translating back leaves a negative term
  proportional to N per block. The coefficient cannot be dismissed as a constant.
* Squashed-entanglement lower bounds, local Bell tests with resource recycling,
  isotropic twirls, and a joint-prefix spectral rearrangement were considered
  as possible alternative approaches. No sufficient lemma was established and
  none is used in any result here. No physical protocol was claimed.
* Literature searches found the precise ordinary/complete distinction but no
  matched theorem proving the required ordinary tensor estimate. This bounded
  search is not a priority claim, a comprehensive negative result, or evidence
  that a theorem does not exist.

Remaining exact obligation: prove (2) for every complex coefficient matrix and
all N,r with some finite K_D independent of N,r, or exhibit a family violating
that statement. The conditional counterfamily does neither. The original
single-block source, its corrected flags, and its matched MC cost theorem remain
untouched. General E_F additivity was never assumed.
