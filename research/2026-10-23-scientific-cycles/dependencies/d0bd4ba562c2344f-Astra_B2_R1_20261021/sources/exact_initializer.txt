5. Attribution and disposition
This was not settled simply by applying the quoted closest-state theorem. Lami–Shirokov’s Theorem 5(c) assumes a finite resource value when asserting compactness of the minimizer set. Their Proposition 18 additionally assumes a faithful limiting state and convergence of the resource values to a finite limit. Those statements do not directly cover the infinite-resource limit here.
