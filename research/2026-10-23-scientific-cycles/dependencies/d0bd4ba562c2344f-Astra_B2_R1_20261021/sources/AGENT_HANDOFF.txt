MINIMAL CONTENT
Each output includes README.txt stating the actual decision; RESULT.json with
input hashes, model/error convention, proof/calculation paths, observed costs
and unmeasured fields; actual source/data needed for its surviving operation;
and a SHA256 manifest. Numerical submissions give an exact callable entry point
and a small declared replay. Mathematical submissions contain the full argument,
not only a claim ledger. Include negative or inconclusive results and failures.
Do not build a general platform or duplicate the entire historical archive.

COMMON MOLECULAR RESPONSE
For a molecular computation RESULT.json identifies:
  task_id=B2-MOL; role; method; input files and SHA256s;
  exact operator convention and sector;
  target=E0(H_b)-E0(H_a); requested width;
  status=certified | numerical_only | inconclusive | rejected;
  lower/upper endpoints (exact strings where appropriate);
  construction and independent verifier entry points;
  conversion/rounding error treatment;
  executed total costs and any estimates separately.
It is acceptable to use a native implementation interface. An adapter supplied
with the result must make the requested pair/tolerance explicit. Do not force
an invented standard API that obscures a real mathematical difference.

INTEGRATION OWNERSHIP
Max owns collecting the exact named available outputs, validating input/model
compatibility, running the strongest comparison and returning one allocation
recommendation. Dots owns independent relocation and replay of actual received
artifacts and checks whether the advertised guarantee survives transfer. The
principal meta-research evaluator judges continuation from these results.

At natural work checkpoints, search once for a relevant named private handoff.
Do useful independent work while it is unavailable. Do not busy-poll or promise
background monitoring/resumption unless your actual tools provide it. If another
branch finishes later than your active turn, save a runnable collection entry
point/current receipt and mark that submission unintegrated. The remaining
collection task stays agent-owned; do not assign recovery or forwarding to the
user. An absent output is neither a scientific failure nor a passed comparison.

SOURCE TRANSFER VS RECONSTRUCTION
Ultra exports its owned R1 proof when available. Max exports its owned R3 code
when available. Pro1/Pro3 can begin from supplied text without waiting. A new
implementation from a theorem is useful but is not replay of the absent original.
Saving to a private local Mac path alone does not establish cross-chat delivery.
When a tool blocks retrieval or saving, preserve the accessible result, state
the exact limited failure and continue unaffected work. Do not bypass access
controls or assert a successful handoff without a receipt.

EVALUATION SCOPE
The existing evaluation protocol is supplied for a real controlled trial, not
as a mandate to construct another platform. At minimum distinguish observed
performance, an internal proof, a scoped exclusion, missing evidence and a
counterfactual prediction. Do not call a common-packet comparison blind or
compute-matched unless the relevant isolation and resource controls actually
exist. Do not infer model superiority from unequal main-turn elapsed times.
