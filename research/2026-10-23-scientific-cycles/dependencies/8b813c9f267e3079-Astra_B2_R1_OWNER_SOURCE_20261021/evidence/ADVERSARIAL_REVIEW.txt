ADVERSARIAL REVIEW — EXACT BELL EXTRACTION AND FINITE RETURN
Independent mathematical audit; historical novelty not assessed.

VERDICT
The stopping-record lower bound and the unflagged constructions are correct with the qualifications below. The consequence is an additive-O(1) sharp small-return-error law for finite relative entropy of entanglement, at fixed J and d. It is not an exact finite-delta optimizer, an operational preparation-cost theorem, or an experimental certificate of the memory constraint.

1. UNIVERSAL LOWER BOUND
Let T be the complete fixed J-call block, with product external inputs, whose output dimension is D=d^J per party. Assume its joint external output on tau is the pure maximally entangled state Phi_D, and let tau_prime be its final memory marginal. Purity forces T(tau)=Phi_D tensor tau_prime, including every retained register. Put delta=one half ||tau_prime-tau||_1 and m=ln D. Assume R=E_R(tau)<infinity.

Define the normal CP trace-nonincreasing map S(x)=Tr_out[(Phi_D tensor I)T(x)]. Then S(tau)=tau_prime, and p_k=Tr S^k(tau) is the probability that the first k Bell-projector tests all pass in repeated blocks. p_1=1. For k>=2, E=(S*)^(k-1)(I) is an effect, hence
p_k-p_(k-1)=Tr E(tau_prime-tau)>=-delta.
Consequently p_k>=[1-(k-1)delta]_+.

CAUTION: S is not LOCC and need not preserve separability. The following separable comparison instead defers every Bell projection until all k blocks have run. Since a later device acts only on memory and fresh inputs, this deferral preserves the probability of the initial-success event. From any normal separable initializer gamma, the unconditioned k-block output is separable across all Alice outputs versus all Bob outputs. Its overlap with Phi_D^tensor k is at most D^(-k). This also holds for trace-norm closure of finite product mixtures.

Let K be the initial number of successive passes, capped at N, with outcomes {0,1,...,N}. Under tau, p(K=0)=0, while under gamma q(K=k)<=D^(-k) for k>=1. K must formally include 0 even though its tau-law has support {1,...,N}. The entire finite stopped experiment is a normal CPTP measurement. Data processing and the pointwise q bound give
D(tau||gamma)>=D(p_K||q_K)>=m E_p K-H(p_K).
Taking infimum over gamma, using E K=sum_(k=1)^N p_k, and H(p_K)<=ln N yields
R >= m sum_(k=1)^N [1-(k-1)delta]_+ - ln N.
No closest separable minimizer is needed. Infinite relative entropy for an individual gamma creates no problem.

For 0<delta<=1 choose N=ceil(1/delta). Writing N=1/delta+a, 0<=a<1, the sum is
1/(2delta)+1/2+delta a(1-a)/2.
Thus
R >= m/(2delta) - ln(1/delta) + m/2 - ln(1+delta).
The additional elementary bound R>=m should also be retained. At delta=0, N may tend to infinity and gives R=infinity. The displayed coarse finite-delta expression is weaker than the exact supremum over N and can be weaker than R>=m.

2. UNFLAGGED TERMINAL RESERVOIR
For integer D>=2 define chi_D=D^(-1) sum_i |ii><ii| and
rho_k=Phi_D^tensor k tensor chi_D^tensor(N-k),
tau_N=N^(-1) sum_(k=1)^N rho_k.
There is no retained classical preparation label k. A local shift extracts the first pair and appends chi_D, with a finite-outcome LOCC sampling-and-communication implementation for chi_D. Every branch is averaged; a shared random label retained in a separate register must instead be counted as part of memory.

The first output is exactly Phi_D and decouples from memory. The returned memory is tau_prime=N^(-1)sum_(k=0)^(N-1)rho_k. Therefore tau_N-tau_prime=(Phi_D^tensor N-chi_D^tensor N)/N. Since chi_D^tensor N is maximally mixed on the D^N-dimensional maximally correlated support and contains Phi_D^tensor N as one eigenvector,
delta_N=(1-D^(-N))/N.

Both marginals of tau_N are maximally mixed. A maximally correlated state sigma has E_R(sigma)=S(sigma_B)-S(sigma) here: the diagonal separable state chi_D^tensor N attains this quantity; for any separable gamma, gamma<=I_A tensor gamma_B and operator log monotonicity give D(sigma||gamma)>=S(sigma_B)-S(sigma). Thus
E_R(tau_N)=((N+1)/2)ln D-ln N+h_N,
where h_N=H(K|Y), K uniform on {1,...,N}, and conditional on K=k the first k letters of a product-basis outcome Y are the designated plus letter, with the rest uniform on D letters. Given a leading plus run r, the posterior of r-K is truncated geometric with ratio 1/D. Its entropy is bounded by that of the untruncated geometric, so
0<=h_N<=c_D=ln[D/(D-1)]+ln D/(D-1),
uniformly in N. Equality is approached as N grows. Retaining an orthogonal shared k flag removes the -ln N+h_N entropy benefit and gives the average rung entanglement instead.

With N=ceil(1/delta), delta_N<=delta and the upper bound matches
inf E_R = (ln D)/(2delta)-ln(1/delta)+O_D(1)
as delta tends to zero. This is an asymptotic infimum law, not an exact formula at arbitrary delta.

To realize D=d^J on d-dimensional external ports, identify one D-pair with J qudit pairs. Then Phi_D=Phi_d^tensor J and chi_D=chi_d^tensor J. The same fixed one-qudit shift on every call extracts J exact Bell pairs and has the stated return after J calls. Intermediate return is not claimed by this construction.

3. ALL-PREFIX RETURN
Let M=J+L-1 and use consecutive qudit rungs k=J,...,J+L-1:
tau_(J,L)=L^(-1)sum_(k=J)^(J+L-1) Phi_d^tensor k tensor chi_d^tensor(M-k).
Require L>=J for the following formula to hold for every prefix 1<=j<=J. Every such output prefix is exactly Phi_d^tensor j. Cancellation leaves j high rungs minus j low rungs. On the support with the first a=J+L-j qudits in Phi_d, the high-rung sum dominates the low-rung sum when j<=L; outside it the difference is nonpositive. Consequently
delta_j=[j-d^(-L)(d^j-1)/(d-1)]/L.
It is increasing for j<=L, and delta_j<=j/L. Its resource is
E_R=[J+(L-1)/2]ln d-ln L+h, 0<=h<=c_d.
Choose L=ceil(J/delta), which automatically satisfies L>=J for 0<delta<=1. This meets every prefix return bound delta_j<=delta. The terminal universal lower bound with D=d^J applies. For fixed J,d both terminal-only and all-prefix resource infima therefore obey
inf E_R=J ln d/(2delta)-ln(1/delta)+O_(J,d)(1).

4. CORRELATIONS AND CLAIM BOUNDARIES
Exact requested Bell outputs are pure, so those successful prescribed output prefixes really are jointly decoupled from the final memory. This does not imply arbitrary later outputs are iid Bell pairs. The reservoir is ultimately consumable; beyond its guaranteed exact-output prefix its output slots and memory are correlated. The proof retains those correlations in T and in the global stopped test.

The resource is the initial state relative entropy E_R, not the number of Bell pairs expended by a particular preparation algorithm, memory dimension, entanglement of formation, distillable entanglement, or an experimentally measurable quantity. The construction has exponential Hilbert-space size in the number of rungs. Any claimed preparation, implementation, energy, or experimental advantage requires an additional theorem. The lower-bound Bell measurement is a mathematical distinguisher used in relative-entropy data processing; it is not an LOCC-accessible test or an experimental certificate.

No historical novelty claim was checked in this audit. Whether approximate Bell outputs, catalytic return definitions other than full-memory trace distance, or other resource monotones have the same sharp law remains separate.


FOLLOW-UP AUDIT — FINAL PAPER SECTIONS 4–5 AND EQUATION (8)
The final main.tex sections were checked independently. No algebraic flaw found.

A. Smoothed and noisy stopping bounds (17), (18), (19) are valid. Under a kappa-close initializer every finite prefix event changes by at most kappa, irrespective of horizon. The stopping variable then has N+1 possible values, so the ln(N+1) entropy subtraction is appropriate. For noisy output, tau-S(tau)=(tau-Psi(tau))+F(tau), with F(tau)>=0 of trace alpha, implies tr(tau-S(tau))_+<=delta+alpha. This controls every successive effect without any output-memory independence assumption.

B. Finite LOCC verifier V_D=(I+D Phi_D)/(D+1) is correct. The Haar average of equal-outcome local basis tests is U tensor conjugate-U invariant, has trace D, and eigenvalue 1 on Phi_D, uniquely giving V_D. The set of basis-test effects is compact in a finite-dimensional real vector space, so its Haar barycentre is an exact finite convex combination by Caratheodory. Sampling that finite mixture and comparing local outcomes is finite-round, finite-outcome LOCC. Comparison acceptance on SEP is at most c=2/(D+1). If Bell infidelity is at most x<x*=1-1/D, true acceptance is at least v=1-Dx/(D+1)>c, and binary relative entropy is bounded below by D_Ber(v||c)=e_D(x).

C. The Piani resource drop is valid for normal infinite memory when E_R(xi)<infinity: choose a separable relative-entropy approximate minimizer, apply the complete finite LOCC transcript instrument, disintegrate cq relative entropy, and use convexity on conditional memory states. Their average is Psi(xi), since the verification acts on released outputs only. Conditional comparison memories remain SEP. Both resource values are finite by LOCC monotonicity, so there is no infinity subtraction.

D. Contractivity gives T(Psi^i(tau),tau)<=i delta, hence next-block infidelity <=alpha+i delta. The function e_D is decreasing to zero at x*, and extending it by zero afterward makes the left Riemann sum dominate its integral. Therefore delta E_R(tau)>=C_D(alpha) is correct. For a kappa-close finite-resource sigma the statement means delta E_R(sigma)>=C_D(alpha+kappa), nontrivial when alpha+kappa<x*. No return estimate for sigma is needed. The delta=0 contradiction at fidelity above SEP threshold also follows by summing indefinitely.

E. Equation (8) is valid for every t>=0 including t>M, provided the appended chi_d states are freshly and independently sampled, as in the specified device. When t>=M its memory factor is chi_d^tensor M and decouples from all outputs; the output mixture still carries its exhaustion correlations.

F. The phase-noise flagged ladder has exact E_R(omega_alpha^tensor k)=k(ln2-h_2(alpha)), by the maximally correlated identity, and the locally readable flag gives the exact average resource. Its first output factors as omega_alpha tensor tau_prime. At one use, overlapping inventory-flag blocks contribute (1-delta)alpha to joint half trace distance from Phi tensor tau, while the two unmatched edge blocks contribute delta. Thus beta=delta+(1-delta)alpha is exact, not merely an upper bound.

G. The proposition's inventory example correctly gives the same complete first-H external process with return H/L versus 1. For literally universal wording over every possible external process, add an invisible local H+1-state clock, initially |0>, advanced cyclically once per call. It leaves all external observations unchanged and gives return distance 1 at horizon H. This proves no nontrivial universally valid hidden-return upper bound can follow from external data alone. A frozen maximally entangled spectator of arbitrary finite rank similarly rules out every finite resource upper cap by partial-trace monotonicity; ER additivity is not needed. This is a recommended strengthening of proof wording, not an error in the stated inventory calculations.
