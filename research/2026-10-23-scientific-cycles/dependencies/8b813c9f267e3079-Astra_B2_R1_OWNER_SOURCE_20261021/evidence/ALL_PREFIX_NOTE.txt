# Exact Bell stream with all intermediate marginal returns controlled

Logarithms are natural; trace distance is half the trace norm. This is an extension of `UNFLAGGED_RESERVOIR_PROOF.md`, whose logs are instead bits and whose error is the full norm.

Fix the elementary external Bell dimension d>=2, desired exact-use horizon J>=1, and integer L>=J. Put m=ln d, a=1/d, and M=J+L-1. Memory is M d-level pairs. Let phi be the d-dimensional Bell projector and chi=(1/d) sum_i |ii><ii|. Define

    rho_k = phi^(tensor k) tensor chi^(tensor(M-k)), 0<=k<=M,
    tau = (1/L) sum_(k=J)^(J+L-1) rho_k.

The device emits its first memory pair, shifts left, and appends chi by finite-outcome LOCC. This fixed device ignores the external product input. The first J outputs are exactly phi^J. By purity they are independent of one another and of the complete returned memory; no independence is assumed after this horizon.

After j<=J uses,

    tau_j = (1/L) sum_(k=J-j)^(J+L-1-j) rho_k.

The exact memory-return distance is

    delta_j = (1/2)||tau-tau_j||_1
            = j/L - a^(L-j+1)(1-a^j)/[L(1-a)]
            <= j/L.

Proof of the exact distance: factor the common first J-j Bell pairs. On the remaining L+j-1 effective phase coordinates, tau is the uniform mixture of prefix Bell lengths j,...,L+j-1 and tau_j is the uniform mixture of lengths 0,...,L-1. Their difference is (1/L)[sum_(k=L)^(L+j-1) rho_k - sum_(k=0)^(j-1) rho_k]. The states commute in the Bell phase basis. On strings with initial zero-run r<L, only the negative sum contributes. On strings with r>=L, the positive sum dominates, since L>=j and d^L>sum_(k=0)^(j-1)d^k. Therefore the positive spectral subspace is precisely the event r>=L. Its probability under tau is

    j/L + a(1-a^(L-j))/[L(1-a)],

and under tau_j it is a(1-a^L)/[L(1-a)]. Their difference proves the formula. This verifies the full hidden memory return directly; it does not establish external accessibility of that return.

The exact relative entropy resource is

    R = E_R(tau)
      = m[J+(L-1)/2] - ln L + H(K|Z),
    0 <= H(K|Z) <= c_d = -ln(1-a)+m a/(1-a).

Indeed the first J pairs factor as pure Bells. The remaining L-1 pairs are the unflagged uniform prefix mixture of lengths 0,...,L-1. The maximally correlated relative-entropy proof in the companion note applies unchanged. In the effective phase distribution, K is uniform on {0,...,L-1}; given K, the first K phases are zero and the rest uniform. Its posterior gap given zero-run r is a truncated geometric on {0,...,r}, which has entropy at most c_d.

For a specified tolerance delta in (0,1], choose L=ceil(J/delta). Then every intermediate memory obeys delta_j<=j delta/J<=delta. The elementary device supplies J exact Bell pairs with

    R <= Jm/(2delta) - ln(1/delta) + Jm - ln J + c_d,

where the bound uses L< J/delta+1 and -ln L<=-ln(J/delta). The universal lower bound from the J-output Bell block, of total dimension D=d^J, gives

    R >= Jm/(2delta) - ln(1/delta) + Jm/2 - ln(1+delta).

Consequently, even if the operational contract requires small complete-memory marginal return after EVERY elementary use up to J,

    R_min(J,d,delta)
      = J ln(d)/(2delta) - ln(1/delta) + O_(J,d)(1)

as delta tends to zero, for fixed J,d. This does not assert that the O(1) additive constant is sharp. For variable J, the displayed finite bounds provide the proper dependence.

Verification: directly enumerated effective phase strings for d=2,3 and (J,L)=(1,1),(2,2),(2,4),(3,3),(3,5), checking every 1<=j<=J. Every computed half trace norm agreed with the displayed formula to 1e-10.
