1. PIANI 2009, Relative Entropy of Entanglement and Restricted Measurements
https://arxiv.org/html/0904.2705v1
Theorem 1, Eq(4): when measurements on X preserve conditional membership in free reference set P on Y, and P is partial-trace closed,
 E_R^P(rho_XY) >= M E_R^P(rho_X)+E_R^P(rho_Y).
The free set includes bipartite separable states across (Alice-output+Alice-memory):(Bob-output+Bob-memory), with M=LOCC or SEP measurements. Correlations among outputs and between output and retained memory are explicitly allowed. Theorem 2 gives measured E_R faithfulness, convexity, strong LOCC monotonicity, strong superadditivity. Proof is the same cq relative-entropy disintegration mechanism inherited in seed. A finite witness budget plus continuity is therefore standard machinery, not by itself new. The finite-dimensional paper is extended to normal infinite memory in R1 through direct normal cq/data-processing proof and finite approximation, not by silently assuming original finite theorem already infinite-dimensional.

2. LAMI-SHIROKOV 2023, Attainability and lower semi-continuity...
https://arxiv.org/html/2105.08091v3
Theorem 5: weak*-closed cone generated by free set implies relative-entropy infimum attained and trace-norm lower semicontinuity. Finite-value minimizer set compact; uniqueness additionally needs convex free set and faithful input. Corollary 20 applies attainment/lower semicontinuity to arbitrary bipartite states on separable Hilbert space, including infinite dimensions. It does not make infinite E_R usable by subtracting infinities and does not bound entropy changes by trace distance alone. Energy constrained continuity is separate, in Propositions 36 and 39.

3. SON ET AL 2026, Catalytic channels are the only noise-robust catalytic processes
https://arxiv.org/html/2412.06900v2
Latest retrieved v2, Jan 7 2026, published PRL136 050202. Robustness parameter epsilon is a radius of initial SYSTEM preparation noise; catalyst is returned EXACTLY for every input in this ball. Central Fact: this iff same dilation+catalyst returns catalyst for all system inputs (catalytic channel). Theorem 1: in CRNG theory satisfying tensor/product closure, partial-trace closure, convexity, catalytic channel advantage iff resource broadcasting. Theorem 2: minimal composition rules out resource broadcasting/robust catalytic advantage. Entanglement receives no broadcasting via listed theory/no-go arguments. None of these statements quantify fixed nonzero memory return, finite lifetime, or resource-vs-error sharp constant. Do not identify their initial input noise epsilon with R1 return error.

4. YAMASAKI ET AL 2025, Entanglement cost for infinite-dimensional physical systems
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00220-025-05431-1
https://arxiv.org/html/2401.09554v2
Theorem 7 assumes min{S(rho_A),S(rho_B)} finite. All considered entanglement costs (one-way finite-communication LOCC through SEP/strong closures) equal continuous-convex-roof E_f regularized. This supports operational asymptotic consumable comparison for finite-entropy flagged finite reservoirs. It does not identify E_R with preparation cost, and does not cover every normal state having infinite local entropies. v2 arxiv posted May 25 2026 says main result unchanged, continuous convex-roof definition and SEP monotonicity strengthened.

5. VAN LUIJK ET AL 2025, Embezzlement ... classification of von Neumann algebras
https://arxiv.org/html/2401.07299v4
Latest retrieved v4 Apr10 2025. Definition/propositions distinguish a family changing with desired error from one fixed state allowing arbitrary targets and arbitrarily small errors. Original van Dam-Hayden pure coefficients ~1/sqrt(j H_n) do not converge as n grows. Semifinite (including type I tensor-product) algebras have no normal universal embezzling state. Proposition 54 gives kappa(omega)=2 for all normal states in semifinite algebra with separable predual; kappa is worst target, so this should not be paraphrased as every fixed target is distance2. Type III_1 universal embezzlers are outside R1 tensor-product-memory assumptions. Neither result bounds finite mixed E_R/error with the candidate leading constant.

6. RUBBOLI-TOMAMICHEL 2022 (important extra novelty dependency)
Fundamental Limits on Correlated Catalytic State Transformations, PRL129 120506
https://arxiv.org/html/2111.13356v2
Theorem2: some alpha in [1/2,1) resource Renyi divergence additive at target and strictly increased implies catalyst relative-entropy resource Omega(log(1/epsilon)) for epsilon joint PURIFIED distance to target x original catalyst. Exact memory marginal return can be lifted (main-text paragraph after theorem). General mixed catalysts allowed in entanglement section. At alpha=1/2, sqrt(F_free(catalyst)) <= epsilon/[sqrt(F_free(input))-sqrt(F_free(target))]. Product to rank-D maximally entangled gap is 1-1/sqrt(D). This is dimension-independent but only logarithmic E_R bound. Their phrase 'optimal lower bound for embezzlement' must not be construed as sharp mean-E_R 1/(2 delta) tradeoff; it references their particular generic fidelity/resource lower bound. See theorem and exact metric definitions before use.

7. ZANONI-THEURER-GOUR 2024 (strong pure comparator)
Complete Characterization of Entanglement Embezzlement, Quantum8 1368
https://arxiv.org/html/2303.17749v3
https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2024-06-13-1368/
Theorem4: for pure Schmidt vectors p,q, LOCC star trace-conversion distance equals max_k(top-k mass p - top-k mass q). Lemma3: T_star^2/2 <= true trace conversion T <= sqrt(2 T_star), where T uses half norm. Theorem6: universal pure family iff maximum mass of Schmidt blocks l..2l-1 tends zero. Their conclusion explicitly leaves comprehensive theory of mixed-state embezzling families undeveloped. Pure-to-mixed *target conversion* solved there should not be mistaken for mixed *reservoir family* characterization.

TARGETED NOVELTY SEARCH / CONCLUSION
Queried combinations of embezzlement, mixed catalysts, relative entropy of entanglement, trace distance, inverse error, stopping time, and resource cost; inspected above primary papers and references/citing results. No matching universal normal mixed-state E_R law with leading m/(2 delta), exact Bell output and arbitrary output-memory correlations was found. Nearest verified statements are logarithmic or dimension/energy constrained, pure-only optimal conversion, or exact catalyst-return no-go. Search is bounded and cannot establish historical novelty. Safe status: INTERNAL THEOREM CANDIDATE with sharp leading coefficient against matched mixed flagged reservoir; HISTORICAL NOVELTY UNKNOWN.

Main candidate communicated by principal: for exact rank-D Bell block output, m=log D and return delta=half norm,
 R >= m[1/(2delta)+1/2] - log(ceil(1/delta)+1),
from Bell-survival stopping K, p(K>=k)>=1-(k-1)delta, separable q(K>=k)<=exp(-km), and R>=m E K-H(K). This appears distinct from Piani marginal-witness budget: a single complete joint measurement captures all successful prefixes, with global separable Bell overlap. The logarithmic stopping entropy is a precise remaining finite-delta gap; flagged consumable reservoir saturates leading coefficient. External proof review and historical novelty remain open.

PRINCIPAL'S SECOND-ORDER EXTENSION (reported during audit; proof audited elsewhere)
Unflagged nested maximally-correlated mixed reservoir tau_N=(1/N) sum_{k=1}^N Phi_D^k tensor chi_D^(N-k), chi_D=D^{-1}sum_i |ii><ii|. Principal derives delta_N=(1-D^{-N})/N and E_R=(N+1)logD/2-logN+H(K|phase string), with bounded conditional entropy. Combining p_0=0 stopping entropy bound H(K)<=logN yields target asymptotic optimal law
 R*(delta)=logD/(2delta)-log(1/delta)+O_D(1).
Further targeted queries including mixed reservoirs, mean relative entropy, logarithmic correction, inverse trace error and stopping times found no exact match. Historical novelty remains UNKNOWN; this is stronger than the leading flagged comparison. For the known maximally-correlated identity E_R(rho_MC)=S(rho_A)-S(rho_MC), a suitable primary modern source is Zhu-Hayashi-Chen, Coherence and entanglement measures based on Renyi relative entropies, arxiv1706.00390 (2017), which also proves corresponding Renyi identities and additivity. Using that identity should be acknowledged as known; novelty candidate is its nested-reservoir/stopping-law consequence, not the identity.
