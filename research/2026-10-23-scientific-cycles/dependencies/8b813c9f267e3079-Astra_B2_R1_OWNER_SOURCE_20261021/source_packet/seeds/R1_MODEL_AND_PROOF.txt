1. MODEL
Alice and Bob have finite external input ports A,B and outputs A',B'. Retained
local memories C_A,C_B are separable Hilbert spaces. States are normal
trace-class density operators on C_A tensor C_B. All shared quantum registers
retained between calls belong to C. No fresh uncounted shared entanglement.

Lambda: AB C_A C_B -> A'B' C_A C_B is a fixed normal CPTP device implemented by
a finite-round, finite-outcome LOCC protocol. Every outcome branch is retained
in the average operation; no conditioning on successful outcomes only.

Let eta=eta_A tensor eta_B be a faithful (strictly positive) product input and
Phi(xi)=Tr_(A'B') Lambda(eta tensor xi).
P_n^xi denotes the complete n-slot external causal process from initializer xi,
including correlations across slots and responses to adaptive/reference-
entangled testers. F_n is the strategy-norm closure of finite-dimensional
causal LOCC n-slot processes. Fix one strategy-distance normalization and keep
its constants consistent; the supplied bound uses the normalization below.

SEP is the trace-norm closed convex hull of product normal states.
E_R(rho)=inf_(gamma in SEP) D(rho||gamma), with natural logarithms.

2. FINITE FREE TOMOGRAPHY
In dimension d, take the projectors |i><i| and, for every i<j, projectors onto
(|i> +/- |j>)/sqrt(2) and (|i> +/- i|j>)/sqrt(2). There are d(2d-1) projectors;
their sum is (2d-1)I and they span the Hermitian matrices. Effects s/(2d-1)
form an IC POVM. Given faithful eta, prepare
  eta^(1/2) s eta^(1/2) / Tr(eta s)
with probability Tr(eta s)/(2d-1). The average is eta and the ensemble spans.

At each slot independently prepare both local ensembles, retain preparation
labels, apply Lambda, then measure both outputs with local IC POVMs. The finite
transcript has law p_n^xi. Discarding labels and outputs leaves Phi^n(xi).
The entire instrument is free and preserves conditional separability.

Products of the spanning preparations/effects determine the finite process
Choi operator. Finite-dimensional norm equivalence gives a finite K_n with
  dist_strategy(P,Q) <= K_n ||p_n(P)-p_n(Q)||_1.
No useful growth rate, efficient tomography, or uniform-in-n bound is supplied.

3. ENTROPY DROP (INHERITED MECHANISM)
For a finite free instrument I_y with average Psi, choose a separable gamma
with D(rho||gamma)<=E_R(rho)+zeta, assuming E_R(rho)<infinity. Write outputs
as p_y rho_y and q_y gamma_y. Data processing, cq disintegration, and convexity
give
 E_R(rho)+zeta >= D(p||q)+sum_y p_y D(rho_y||gamma_y)
               >= D(p||q)+sum_y p_y E_R(rho_y)
               >= D(p||q)+E_R(Psi(rho)).
No infinity is subtracted. Pinsker gives
 ||p-q||_1 <= sqrt(2[E_R(rho)-E_R(Psi(rho))+zeta]).

4. FINITE-RETURN THEOREM
Fix normal tau and finite horizon n. Put d=dist_strategy(P_n^tau,F_n).
Let sigma be a finite-resource approximant with
  ||sigma-tau||_1 <= epsilon, E_R(sigma)=R<infinity.
Suppose J>=1 integer times t_1<...<t_J satisfy
  t_(j+1)>=t_j+n,
  ||Phi^t_j(tau)-tau||_1 <= epsilon.
Then
  d <= 2 epsilon + K_n sqrt(2R/J).

Proof. Set sigma_t=Phi^t(sigma), r_t=E_R(sigma_t). This is a finite,
nonnegative, decreasing sequence. At the listed times, contractivity gives
||sigma_t-tau||_1<=2epsilon. Any external tester is a CPTP experiment on the
initializer; hence dist(P_n^sigma_t,F_n)>=(d-2epsilon)_+.
Apply section 3 to the n-slot tomography instrument at sigma_t and use a
separable approximate minimizer. Its process belongs to F_n (section 6), so
  (d-2epsilon)_+^2/(2K_n^2) <= r_t-r_(t+n)
after zeta tends to zero. Sum over disjoint windows; the sum is at most R.
This proves the claim, including the trivial case d<=2epsilon.

If d>2epsilon, there are at most 2R K_n^2/(d-2epsilon)^2 pairwise n-separated
returns. Greedy grouping bounds all integer visits by n times the integer part
of that number. The resource is that of the specified approximant, not E_R(tau).

5. NORMAL RECURRENCE AND COMMON APPROXIMATION
Assume liminf_(integer t->infinity) ||Phi^t(tau)-tau||_1=0.
Every normal tau has locally finite-support approximants sigma_epsilon at
arbitrary trace distance epsilon. Their E_R is finite (bounded, for example,
by the log product support dimension). For each fixed epsilon and n, recurrence
supplies arbitrarily many separated returns. Let J->infinity, then epsilon->0.
Thus P_n^tau is in F_n for each finite n, even if E_R(tau)=infinity.

One sequence of normal separable initializers gamma_k works for all fixed n.
Choose sigma_k within 1/k of tau. At sufficiently late times, the decreasing
finite resource sequence obeys r_(k,t)-r_(k,t+k)<=1/k. Choose such a late t_k
also satisfying ||Phi^t_k(tau)-tau||_1<=1/k. A separable relative-entropy
approximate minimizer gamma_k at sigma_(k,t_k), with slack 1/k, gives for n<=k
  dist_strategy(P_n^tau,P_n^gamma_k) <= 2/k+2K_n/sqrt(k).
Convergence is pointwise in n, not uniform over unbounded horizons. No
computable recurrence time or finite preparation cost is supplied.

If E_R(tau)<infinity, recurrence plus lower semicontinuity makes E_R(Phi^t(tau))
constant. A closest normal separable state gamma_* exists by Lami-Shirokov.
The entropy inequality has zero drop for every n; IC tomography therefore
gives P_n^gamma_*=P_n^tau for all finite n, using the SAME Lambda.
gamma_* need not be stationary or a finite mixture. This conclusion has not
been established here for E_R(tau)=infinity.

6. WHY THE CLOSURE IS LOCC, NOT JUST SEP
Approximate a normal separable initializer by finite mixtures of finite-rank
product states. Their trace error bounds every subsequent process error.
At each node of a fixed finite protocol tree, a normal CP instrument with
finite input support has positive trace-class Choi operators. Project retained
output memory to a sufficiently large finite-rank support and reset its tail
to a fixed local state. This preserves branch traces and external reduced
marginals. Choi tail delta yields a conservative instrument error tending to
zero (e.g. 2sqrt(delta)+delta with consistent normalization).
Recursively truncate the finitely many nodes and use a hybrid bound. Finite
direct sums unify supports. The resulting process is finite-dimensional causal
LOCC with the same slot order. Hence a separable initializer produces an
element of F_n. This is an approximation argument, not SEP=LOCC.

Fixed nonzero return error is not recurrence. Postselection is not average
reset. Nonfaithful eta can hide an entangled reservoir on untested inputs.
Singular states and non-tensor-product/type-III local algebras lie outside this
model. A generic CPTP map is not recurrent. An external LOCC-closure theorem
does not prove same-protocol exact removal, or vice versa.

8. NEW EXPLICIT CONSUMABLE RESERVOIR CONTROL
This is a useful starting control, NOT an advance and NOT the original source
fixture. Let each party have a classical flag k in {0,...,L} and L qubits.
Let omega_k consist of perfectly correlated local flags k, k Bell pairs, and
L-k locally padded |0> qubits. Set tau_L=(1/L)sum_(k=1)^L omega_k.

A fixed local controlled-swap protocol ignores the external input. At k>=1 it
releases the last occupied Bell pair, pads that location with |00>, and changes
both local flags to k-1. At k=0 it releases |00> and leaves memory unchanged.
It is a deterministic finite-round finite-outcome LOCC device on finite memory.
Every flag/outcome is counted. For j<=L:
  ||Phi^j(tau_L)-tau_L||_1 = 2j/L.
The trace DISTANCE with the additional factor 1/2 is j/L; do not mix conventions.
The first output is an exact Bell pair. The full j-output state is
  (1/L)sum_(k=1)^L Bell^(tensor min(k,j)) tensor
                         |00><00|^(tensor max(j-k,0)).
The j-th marginal is Bell with mixture weight (L-j+1)/L and |00> otherwise.
Its initial flagged entanglement is E_R(tau_L)=((L+1)/2) ln 2.
After L calls it has been consumed and is not recurrent. This fixture prevents
mistaking a small one-use marginal disturbance for indefinite reusable fuel.
