1. EXACT MODEL
Let G be any finite stationary reversible CTMC generator, with positive
stationary probabilities pi on retained hidden states. Labels are deterministic
and instantaneous. Reversible reducible chains may be admitted; the explicit
six-state fixture below is irreducible. Fix lag tau>0.

Let P and P2 be the visible conditional transition matrices at tau and 2tau,
with positive visible stationary vector p. Work in symmetric coordinates:
 S=D_p^(1/2) P D_p^(-1/2), K=D_p^(1/2) P2 D_p^(-1/2).
For the hidden symmetric semigroup T=D_pi^(1/2) exp(tau G) D_pi^(-1/2), set
 A_(x,i)=sqrt(pi_x/p_i) 1_{label(x)=i}.
Then A>=0, A^T A=I, 0<T<=I, S=A^T T A, K=A^T T^2 A. Moreover T^alpha is
entrywise nonnegative for every alpha>0, because it is the symmetrized CTMC
semigroup at alpha*tau.

Define the observable defect
 D=K-S^2=A^T T(I-AA^T)T A >=0.
D=0 means the visible subspace is invariant. Positive D is hidden memory,
NOT positive entropy production: equilibrium hidden systems usually have D>0.

2. A DIMENSION- AND RATE-INDEPENDENT GAP
Suppose 0<alpha<1 and (S^alpha)_(i,j)=-m<0, with i!=j and m>0.
Then
 ||D||_op >= m^(2/alpha).
If S is positive definite, the stronger trace statement is
 tr(D) >= 2m lambda_min(S)^(2-alpha)/(1-alpha).
Neither statement imposes hidden dimension, rate, or stationary-mass caps.

Basic proof. Let Pi=AA^T and pinch T to
 T0=Pi T Pi+(I-Pi)T(I-Pi).
The off-diagonal block norm gives ||T-T0||=sqrt(||D||).
For positive operators X,Y and 0<alpha<1,
 ||X^alpha-Y^alpha|| <= ||X-Y||^alpha.
This follows from operator monotonicity and
 (Y+delta I)^alpha <= Y^alpha+delta^alpha I, and the reverse inequality.
Compressing T0^alpha gives S^alpha, whereas A^T T^alpha A is nonnegative
entrywise. The negative entry therefore forces m<=||D||^(alpha/2).

Trace proof. Operator Jensen gives
 F=S^alpha-A^T T^alpha A >=0.
Since F_ij<=-m, positivity yields F_ii+F_jj>=2m, hence tr F>=2m.
For t>=0, lambda>0,
 t^alpha >= lambda^alpha+alpha lambda^(alpha-1)(t-lambda)
             -(1-alpha)lambda^(alpha-2)(t-lambda)^2.
To verify, put x=t/lambda and divide the equivalent inequality by x>0:
 x^(alpha-1)>=(2-alpha)-(1-alpha)x, the tangent bound for a convex power.
At x=0 equality holds by continuity in the original inequality.

Let u_k be an eigenvector of S with eigenvalue lambda_k. Apply this scalar
inequality to the spectral measure of T in A u_k. Its mean is lambda_k and
variance is u_k^T D u_k. Summing gives
 tr F <= (1-alpha) tr(S^(alpha-2)D)
       <= (1-alpha)lambda_min(S)^(alpha-2)tr D.
Combine with tr F>=2m. No commutativity of S and D was assumed.

Since D annihilates the stationary vector sqrt(p), its rank is at most m_vis-1.
Consequently ||D||_op >= tr(D)/(m_vis-1) when m_vis>1.

3. A WELL-CONDITIONED FOUR-LABEL TARGET
Uniform p=(1/4,1/4,1/4,1/4), so S=P. Take
 P = [[23/40, 1/40, 1/5, 1/5],
      [1/40, 23/40, 1/5, 1/5],
      [1/5, 1/5, 2/5, 1/5],
      [1/5, 1/5, 1/5, 2/5]].
Its eigenvalues are 1,11/20,1/5,1/5. At alpha=1/2,
 (sqrt(P))_12 = -m,
 m=(sqrt(55)-sqrt(5)-5)/20 = 0.009006525479... .
Therefore EVERY exact finite hidden equilibrium realization of this P has
 tr(D) >= (sqrt(11)-sqrt(5)-1)/25 = 0.003222272514... .
The corresponding operator-norm lower bound from trace/rank is 0.001074090838... .
The basic m^4 bound is valid but much weaker.

Also (log P)_12=(1/4)log[(1/5)/(11/20)^2]<0, excluding a same-four-state
reversible generator. This does NOT exclude hidden equilibrium at one lag.

4. EXACT IRREDUCIBLE SIX-STATE EQUILIBRIUM REALIZATION
This makes the one-lag feasible set nonempty even with irreducibility.
State order: (+,1),(+,3),(+,4),(-,2),(-,3),(-,4).
Each triple has local reset probabilities r=(1/2,1/4,1/4).
The stationary distribution is
 pi=(1/4,1/8,1/8,1/4,1/8,1/8).
Let B be block diagonal with each 3x3 block equal to 1 r^T, and let
 R_global=1 pi^T. Set
 G = beta (B-I) + gamma (R_global-I),
 beta=log(9/2)/tau, gamma=log(10/9)/tau.
Both reset generators obey detailed balance with pi. The global reset gives
strictly positive off-diagonal rates, so G is irreducible. B and R_global are
commuting projections with B R_global=R_global B=R_global.

At tau, a=exp(-gamma tau)=9/10, delta=exp(-beta tau)=2/9, so
 exp(tau G)=a delta I+a(1-delta)B+(1-a)R_global.
At 2tau replace a,delta by their squares.
The resulting visible one-lag matrix is exactly the P in section 3. Its
two-lag matrix is
 K = [[189/400,19/400,6/25,6/25],
      [19/400,189/400,6/25,6/25],
      [6/25,6/25,7/25,6/25],
      [6/25,6/25,6/25,7/25]].
Writing v=(1,-1,0,0)^T,
 D=K-P^2=(49/800) v v^T,
 tr D=||D||_op=49/400=0.1225.
The stationary entropy production is exactly zero by detailed balance.

Thus the exact equilibrium-realization minimum obeys the nonempty bracket
 0.003222272514... <= inf tr D <= 49/400.
No sharpness, minimizer, or attainment theorem is claimed. A prior eight-state

5. REPLAY AND INTERPRETATION
Run python3 verification/check_r2_fixture.py from the package root.
It uses only standard-library Fractions to check semigroup projection algebra,
stationarity, detailed balance, the one-lag matrix, the two-lag matrix and exact
defect. It writes verification/r2_fixture_result.json. The universal bound is
an analytic proof, not something this finite script proves.

A measured D below the required gap for the corresponding S would rule out
this null class. A positive D by itself would not. Empirical use needs joint
uncertainty for p,P,P2 and an observation/dependence model. Frames from one
trajectory are not independent samples merely because the chain is finite.
Failure of the null could reflect nonstationarity, noninstantaneous/noisy labels,
or non-Markov microscopic dynamics; it is not automatically a quantitative
entropy-production estimate. Unknown deterministic coarse-graining is covered;
arbitrary emission/measurement models are not silently included.
