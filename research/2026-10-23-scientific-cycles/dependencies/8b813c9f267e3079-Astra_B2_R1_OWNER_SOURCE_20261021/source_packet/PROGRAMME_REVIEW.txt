Decision: preserve the existing calculations and scoped exclusions. Do not fund

MOLECULAR CERTIFICATION
Source: sources/01_molecular_certification.txt.
The coordinate-Schur/positive absolute-comparison method did not beat ordinary
small-matrix eigensolution plus Cholesky on its six tested molecular matrices.
It still enumerates omitted coordinates. Exact rational intervals certify the
stored rounded, projected finite-basis matrices; they are not a physical chemical
error bar covering integrals, basis truncation, symmetry sectors or a real
catalyst decision.

The strongest result is the scoped exponential obstruction. For n frustrated
three-state fragments, exact energy is -na while the absolute comparison ground
energy is -2na. A certificate at -na-epsilon using this coordinate-Schur class
requires retained size at least
  3^n(na-epsilon)/[(2n+1)(3na-epsilon)] = Omega(3^n/n).
An elementary local PSD certificate solves the witness in O(n). Thus the example
excludes a representation, not efficient certification in general. Connected
finite examples reinforce the mechanism, but do not create a useful molecular
solver. The arbitrary 1 mHa objective lacked a concrete chemical decision.

The current literature already supplies weighted SOS/v2RDM lower-bound
representations and code: Rubin, Low and DePrince, arXiv:2602.05069v2,
https://arxiv.org/html/2602.05069v2 and https://github.com/ncrubin/sosfermion.
Simply switching labels from absolute comparison to local PSD is not a new
research programme. Park this method and retain the scoped theorem.

2. WHY THIS ALLOCATION DIFFERS FROM THE PREVIOUS ONE

Previous work repeatedly demonstrated a favorable proxy, then discovered that
the complete operation could not exploit it: filter spectrum without new TPV
capability, thermal transport without complete reset, fluid mixing without a
mechanical work port, and a small-matrix certificate without scalable chemical
decision value. The response should be to move the full useful-output/resource
contract earlier, not to ask for increasingly elaborate favorable components.

The same danger appears in the new mathematics. Normal tensor-product memory,
faithful tomography, and averaged outcomes are the quantum operation's actual
resource model. Raw residual randomness, allowed adaptive history and input
precision are the Gaussian operation's actual resource model. These are not
late caveats to add after an attractive calculation.

The next batch therefore has complete mathematical interfaces from the outset:
  Ultra proves a universal finite-reuse boundary.
  Pro 1 constructs admissible finite-use devices that challenge it.
  Max owns the whole adaptive Gaussian operation, proof and code.
  Pro 2 attacks one precise exact-removal question as a bounded probe.
  Pro 3 is conditional reserve, with no launch until an actual independent
        numerical blocker and complete handoff exist.

No third quantum/precision worker is automatically made a permanent support
role. The exact-removal probe releases if routine prior theory settles it or if
it produces no tractable next lemma. The precision reserve is activated only
when its answer can change a real workload decision. This is deliberate
allocation, distinct from an unnoticed idle campaign.
