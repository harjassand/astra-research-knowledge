Contract and result

All logarithms are natural. Set
$$\mathsf T(\rho,\sigma)=\tfrac12\|\rho-\sigma\|_1,\qquad
 E_R(\rho)=\inf_{\gamma\in\mathrm{SEP}}D(\rho\Vert\gamma).$$
Thus the seed’s full trace-norm return error is 2δ. The retained memory
is a normal density operator on separable C_(A) ⊗ C_(B); every retained
shared register belongs to it. Each elementary call is the same normal
finite-round, finite-outcome LOCC channel Λ, with finite external ports.
Outcomes are averaged, with no successful-branch postselection and no
uncounted incoming entanglement. Fix the seed’s faithful product
reference input η and let Φ be its induced memory channel.

The concrete task is to emit a rank-d Bell pair on each of the first J
calls under these reference inputs. Define
$$|\varphi_d\rangle=d^{-1/2}\sum_{a=0}^{d-1}|aa\rangle,\qquad
 \varphi_d=|\varphi_d\rangle\langle\varphi_d|,\qquad D=d^J,\quad m=\ln D.$$
The complete first-J output state must be exactly φ_(d)^(⊗J), identified
locally with φ_(D). This is a joint-state condition, not a stipulation
about separate approximate marginals. Exact pure Bell marginals would
imply the joint condition. It automatically implies decoupling of these
outputs from all other registers.

We impose T(Φ^(J)(τ), τ) ≤ δ. The stronger version imposes this at every
1 ≤ j ≤ J. Let $R_{\rm end}^*(J,d,\delta)$ and
$R_{\rm all}^*(J,d,\delta)$ be the infima of the initial E_(R) over all
permitted memories and devices meeting the respective tasks. The same
device is used on every call in each instance; its memory size may
change between members of a reservoir family.

The lower bound is a necessary operational witness for any device that
can do this task. It does not assert that every process outside the
seed’s ℱ_(J) must emit Bell pairs at the same cost. The explicit devices
below ignore external inputs entirely, so their stated external process
is valid against arbitrary adaptive and reference-entangled testers, not
just the reference-input experiment.

Theorem 1 (Finite bound and sharp two-term law). Let 0 < δ ≤ 1 and
R = E_(R)(τ) < ∞. For every positive integer N,
$$R\ge m\sum_{k=1}^N[1-(k-1)\delta]_+
       -\ln\!\left[1+(N-1)(1-D^{-1})\right].                \tag{1}$$
Also R ≥ m. In particular, with N = ⌈1/δ⌉,
$$R\ge m\left(\frac1{2\delta}+\frac12\right)
             -\ln\lceil1/\delta\rceil
 \ge \frac m{2\delta}-\ln(1/\delta)+\frac m2-\ln(1+\delta).
                                                               \tag{2}$$
Define c_(d) = −ln (1 − d⁻¹) + ln d/(d − 1) ≤ 2ln 2. Then
$$\begin{split}
 \frac m{2\delta}-\ln(1/\delta)+\frac m2-\ln(1+\delta)
 &\le R_{\rm end}^*\le R_{\rm all}^*\\
 &\le \frac m{2\delta}-\ln(1/\delta)+m-\ln J+c_d.
\end{split}\tag{3}$$
Consequently, for fixed J, d, both optima equal
$$\boxed{\quad
 R^*(J,d,\delta)=\frac{J\ln d}{2\delta}-\ln(1/\delta)
                       +O_{J,d}(1),\qquad \delta\downarrow0 .
 \quad}                                                    \tag{4}$$
For δ = 0, no finite-E_(R) memory can meet the exact Bell task.

This is a sharp resource–error law, rather than a dimension or energy
substitution in R1’s tomography inequality. It has no tomography
constant, and its construction shows why even the second term cannot be
removed. For qubit outputs, (2) implies the useful finite-use
restriction
$$J\le \frac{2\delta}{(1+\delta)\ln2}
                  \left[R+\ln\lceil1/\delta\rceil\right].    \tag{5}$$
The resource is the initial relative entropy of entanglement, not an
asserted Bell-pair preparation cost or an experimentally certified
quantity.

Universal proof: one complete joint output test

Write 𝒯 for the J-call channel from memory to block output
Q = Q_(A)Q_(B) and memory, under the product reference inputs, and
Ψ = Φ^(J). Purity gives
𝒯(τ) = φ_(D) ⊗ τ₁,   τ₁ = Ψ(τ).
Define the normal completely positive trace-nonincreasing map
S(x) = Tr_(Q)[(φ_(D) ⊗ 𝕀)𝒯(x)(φ_(D) ⊗ 𝕀)].
It satisfies S(τ) = τ₁. The map S need not be LOCC or preserve
separability. No such claim is used.

Repeat the same block channel N times without any resource reset. For
k ≤ N, let p_( ≥ k) be the probability that Bell projections pass on the
first k block outputs. These projections can all be deferred until the
outputs have been released: future channels act only on memory. Thus
p_( ≥ k) = Tr S^(k)(τ),   p_( ≥ 1) = 1.
For i ≥ 0, 0 ≤ (S^(*))^(i)(𝕀) ≤ 𝕀. Because τ − τ₁ has trace zero, its
positive part has trace at most δ. Therefore
Tr S^(i)(τ − τ₁) ≤ δ.
Telescoping yields
p_( ≥ k) ≥ [1 − (k − 1)δ]₊.
This argument keeps the actual correlated state of all output blocks and
the complete memory; it does not replace them by identical marginals.

For a normal separable initializer γ, the unconditioned first-k output
state is separable across all Alice outputs versus all Bob outputs.
Every such state has overlap at most D^(−k) with φ_(D)^(⊗k). This
follows for product pure states by Cauchy–Schwarz, then for their closed
convex hull by linearity and continuity. Hence its corresponding pass
probability obeys
q_( ≥ k) ≤ D^(−k).
It would be incorrect to justify (7) by saying the postselected memory
stays separable. Deferral and the unconditioned joint output give the
result.

Measure the number K ∈ {0, …, N} of consecutive initial Bell passes,
capped at N. Under τ, p₀ = 0, so H(p) ≤ ln N;
$\mathbb E_pK=\sum_{k=1}^Np_{\ge k}$. For the comparison distribution,
q_(k) ≤ q_( ≥ k) ≤ D^(−k). Relative-entropy data processing for this
finite joint measurement gives
$$\begin{split}
 D(\tau\Vert\gamma)
 &\ge D(p\Vert q)
   =-H(p)-\sum_kp_k\ln q_k\\
 &\ge m\mathbb E_pK-H(p)
 \ge m\sum_{k=1}^N[1-(k-1)\delta]_+-\ln N.
\end{split}$$
A slightly stronger finite bound removes avoidable stopping entropy. Put
$Z_q=\sum_{k=1}^N D^kq_k$. Summation by parts and (7) give
$$Z_q=Dq_{\ge1}+\sum_{k=2}^N(D^k-D^{k-1})q_{\ge k}
       \le 1+(N-1)(1-D^{-1}).$$
The log-sum inequality, or normalization of D^(k)q_(k), gives
D(p‖q) ≥ m𝔼_(p)K − ln Z_(q). This proves the stronger finite statement
(1); the preceding entropy bound suffices for (2). One can optimize (1)
over integers N rather than always using ⌈1/δ⌉. Take the infimum over
normal separable γ. No minimizer, compactness, continuity of E_(R), or
subtraction of infinities is needed. N = 1 also gives R ≥ m.

For N = ⌈1/δ⌉, all terms in the sum are positive and
$$A_N=N-\frac{\delta N(N-1)}2
 =\frac1{2\delta}+\frac12+\frac{\delta r(1-r)}2,\quad
 r=N-1/\delta\in[0,1).$$
Also N ≤ (1 + δ)/δ. This proves (1)–(2). If δ = 0, let N → ∞ in
R ≥ Nm − ln N. The constructive part of (3) is proved next. The
refinement in (1) only changes bounded constants; the two-term sharp law
and its matching mixed construction are the substantive claim.

A reservoir without a retained rung label

Put
$$\chi_d=\frac1d\sum_{a=0}^{d-1}|aa\rangle\langle aa|,\qquad
 \rho_{M,k}=\varphi_d^{\otimes k}\otimes\chi_d^{\otimes(M-k)}
 \quad(0\le k\le M).$$
The memory consists of M pairs of local d-level systems. A fixed device
emits its first pair, shifts the other pairs left, and appends χ_(d).
The append uses only LOCC: Alice samples a uniform a, communicates it to
Bob, and both prepare |a⟩. Every branch is included. There is no fresh
shared entanglement. A sampled rung label used during initialization is
genuinely discarded and unavailable to all future calls. If retained
anywhere accessible, it must be counted, and the resource saving below
disappears.

For any distribution w_(k), initialize τ = ∑_(k)w_(k)ρ_(M, k). The full
output–memory state after any t ≥ 0 calls is
Ω_(t) = ∑_(k)w_(k) φ_(d)^(⊗min (k, t)) ⊗ χ_(d)^(⊗(t − min (k, t))) ⊗ ρ_(M, max (k − t, 0)).
In particular, when t ≤ min {k : w_(k) > 0}, the output is exactly
φ_(d)^(⊗t), in tensor product with the returned memory. Equation (8)
also states the later exhaustion branches and all correlations. There is
no recurrence: at late times the finite initial Bell stock is gone.

Exact resource, rather than an upper estimate

All these states are maximally correlated in the common computational
basis, with local marginal 𝕀/d^(M) and diagonal χ_(d)^(⊗M). For
finite-dimensional separable γ_(AB), γ_(AB) ≤ 𝕀_(A) ⊗ γ_(B). Operator
monotonicity of the logarithm, with support regularization, gives
D(τ‖γ) ≥ S(τ_(B)) − S(τ) + D(τ_(B)‖γ_(B)) ≥ Mln d − S(τ).
The separable comparator χ_(d)^(⊗M) attains this bound, so
$$\boxed{\ E_R(\tau)=M\ln d-S(\tau).\ }                       \tag{9}$$
This known maximally correlated-state identity is not itself a novelty
claim.

For spectral calculations use the Bell-phase basis
$$|b_z\rangle=d^{-1/2}\sum_{a=0}^{d-1}e^{2\pi iza/d}|aa\rangle.$$
Here φ_(d) = |b₀⟩⟨b₀| and χ_(d) is the uniform identity on their span.
All ρ_(M, k) commute. Sample K with law w; conditional on K = k, a phase
string Z has its first k entries zero and its other entries independent
uniform d-ary symbols. Thus
E_(R)(τ) = 𝔼[K]ln d − H(K) + H(K ∣ Z).

Terminal-return construction and complete finite spectrum

Take M = JN, with K uniform on {J, 2J, …, NJ}. Group J adjacent local
systems into dimension D = d^(J) and denote the grouped rung by
k = 1, …, N. After J elementary calls the state is
$$\varphi_D\otimes\tau_N',\quad
 \tau_N=\frac1N\sum_{k=1}^N\varphi_D^{\otimes k}\chi_D^{\otimes(N-k)},
 \quad
 \tau_N'=\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\varphi_D^{\otimes k}\chi_D^{\otimes(N-k)}.$$
Tensor symbols between adjacent factors are implicit here. Telescoping
gives
$$\tau_N-\tau_N'=\frac{\varphi_D^{\otimes N}-\chi_D^{\otimes N}}N,\qquad
 \boxed{\ \delta_N=\mathsf T(\tau_N,\tau_N')=\frac{1-D^{-N}}N.\ } \tag{11}$$
The rank-one Bell projector lies inside a correlated subspace on which
χ_(D)^(⊗N) is the normalized identity, proving (11) directly.

For completeness, let ℓ be the initial zero run of the grouped phase
string. The nonzero spectrum is
$$\lambda_\ell=
 \frac{D^{-(N-\ell)}(1-D^{-\ell})}{N(1-D^{-1})}
 \quad(1\le\ell<N),\qquad
 a_\ell=(D-1)D^{N-\ell-1},$$
and
$$\lambda_N=\frac{1-D^{-N}}{N(1-D^{-1})},\qquad a_N=1.
                                                               \tag{12}$$
The ℓ = 0 eigenvalues and the complement of the correlated subspace are
zero. Equations (9) and (12) are an exact N-term resource formula.

Write a = 1/D. Conditional on a zero run ℓ, the gap G = ℓ − K has
truncated geometric distribution
$$\Pr(G=g\mid\ell)=\frac{(1-a)a^g}{1-a^\ell},
       \qquad 0\le g<\ell.$$
Its entropy is
$$h_\ell=c_D+\ln(1-a^\ell)
                   -\frac{\ell m a^\ell}{1-a^\ell},\qquad
 c_D=-\ln(1-a)+\frac{ma}{1-a}.$$
Consequently 0 ≤ h_(ℓ) ≤ c_(D). The zero-run weights are (1 − a^(ℓ))/N
for ℓ < N, and (1 − a^(N))/[N(1 − a)] for ℓ = N. Thus
$$\boxed{\ R_N=\frac{N+1}{2}m-\ln N+h_N^{\rm cond},\qquad
                  0\le h_N^{\rm cond}=H(K\mid Z)\le c_D.\ } \tag{13}$$
In fact $h_N^{\rm cond}=c_D-O_D(N^{-1})$. For fixed D, (11)–(13) give
$$R_N=\frac{m}{2\delta_N}-\ln(1/\delta_N)+\frac m2+c_D+o_D(1). \tag{14}$$
Taking N = ⌈1/δ⌉ proves the terminal upper bound
m/(2δ) − ln (1/δ) + m + c_(D).

The ordinary flagged version has resource exactly (N + 1)m/2. Erasing
the rung label saves ln N − H(K ∣ Z), not a new source of entanglement.
This saving explains the logarithmic term.

Return after every elementary call

To meet the stronger task choose L ≥ J, M = J + L − 1, and
$$\widetilde\tau_{J,L}=\frac1L\sum_{k=J}^{J+L-1}\rho_{M,k}.$$
All first J outputs are exact independent Bell pairs, decoupled from
memory. For 1 ≤ j ≤ J, the returned memory is the uniform mixture of
rungs J − j, …, M − j. Direct comparison gives
$$\boxed{\quad
 \delta_j=\frac1L\left[j-
                   \frac{(d^j-1)d^{-L}}{d-1}\right]
                  \le\frac jL .
 \quad}                                                    \tag{15}$$
Here is a sign check establishing equality, not only the upper bound.
The difference of the two mixtures is L⁻¹(A₊ − A⁻), where
$$A_+=\sum_{k=M-j+1}^{M}\rho_{M,k},\qquad
 A_-=\sum_{k=J-j}^{J-1}\rho_{M,k}.$$
Let Q be the phase subspace with the first M − j + 1 symbols zero. All
positive summands are supported in Q. On Q, A₊ ≥ d^(−(j − 1))Q, whereas
A⁻ is scalar and its eigenvalue is no larger than this when L ≥ j.
Outside Q the difference is negative. Since Tr QA₊ = j and
Tr QA⁻ = (d^(j) − 1)d^(−L)/(d − 1), (15) follows. The hypothesis L ≥ J
is used and must not be dropped.

The same truncated-geometric posterior as above gives
$$\boxed{\ 
 E_R(\widetilde\tau_{J,L})
 =\left[J+\frac{L-1}{2}\right]\ln d-\ln L+\widetilde h,\qquad
                         0\le\widetilde h\le c_d .
 \ }                                                       \tag{16}$$
Choose L = ⌈J/δ⌉. Every prefix returns within δ, and (16) is at most
$$\frac{J\ln d}{2\delta}-\ln(1/\delta)+J\ln d-\ln J+c_d.$$
This proves the remaining part of Theorem 1.

A proposed stronger law is false

For the terminal family, whenever ln N > m/2 + c_(D), (13) gives
R_(N) < Nm/2. By (11),
2δ_(N)R_(N) < m.
Thus m ≤ 2δR is false, despite being asymptotically correct to leading
order. No bound R ≥ m/(2δ) − C_(J, d) can hold with a constant
independent of δ. The unflagged mixed construction is an explicit
permitted counterexample, including all branches and the whole retained
memory. For an exact finite witness choose d = 2, J = 1, N = 16.
Equations (11) and (13) imply
$$\delta=\frac{65535}{1048576},\qquad
 R\le\frac{13}{2}\ln2,\qquad
 \frac{2\delta R}{\ln2}\le\frac{851955}{1048576}<1.$$
This counterexample needs no numerical entropy estimate.

Smoothed resource and imperfect outputs

These extensions state what is proved; they do not claim the noisy
optimum.

Normal states with possibly infinite relative entropy

For κ ∈ [0, 1), let
R_(κ)(τ) = inf_(σ: T(σ, τ) ≤ κ)E_(R)(σ).
For each specified approximant σ, any finite output measurement changes
its probability of a prefix event by at most κ, uniformly in the tested
horizon. Repeating the exact-task stopping proof gives
$$E_R(\sigma)\ge
 m\sum_{k=1}^N[1-\kappa-(k-1)\delta]_+-\ln(N+1).             \tag{17}$$
The entropy bound is now ln (N + 1), since the first test can fail under
σ. Taking the infimum proves (17) for R_(κ). No recurrence of σ, or
return estimate for it, is assumed. For normal τ, locally finite-support
approximants make R_(κ) finite for every κ > 0, but its magnitude is an
additional resource constraint. This result does not solve exact
same-protocol removal at infinite E_(R).

A correlation-safe noisy stopping bound

Let the first block have Bell infidelity
α = 1 − Tr [φ_(D) 𝒯(τ)_(Q)],   T(Ψ(τ), τ) ≤ δ.
Use the same Bell-success map S. Write τ₁ = Ψ(τ) = S(τ) + F(τ), with F
completely positive trace-nonincreasing and Tr F(τ) = α. The one-sided
defect
h = Tr (τ − S(τ))₊ ≤ δ + α
follows from τ − S(τ) = (τ − τ₁) + F(τ). For any effect 0 ≤ E ≤ 𝕀,
Tr E(τ − S(τ)) ≤ h. It follows that
p_( ≥ k) ≥ [1 − α − (k − 1)h]₊.
For any T(σ, τ) ≤ κ with finite E_(R),
$$\boxed{\ 
 E_R(\sigma)\ge
 m\sum_{k=1}^N[1-\alpha-\kappa-(k-1)(\delta+\alpha)]_+
                      -\ln(N+1).
 \ }                                                       \tag{19}$$
This avoids a square-root decoupling penalty by using the positive-part
defect. It permits arbitrary output–memory correlations. It is not sharp
when α > 0 is fixed and δ → 0.

A stronger fixed-noise bound from inherited machinery

For fixed nonzero noise, one can do better using Piani’s
entropy-disintegration mechanism, stated explicitly as known machinery
rather than the new result. There is a finite LOCC verification
experiment on a rank-D output whose accept effect is
$$V_D=\frac{\mathbb I+D\varphi_D}{D+1}.$$
To see finite realizability, average local equal-outcome tests in a
basis and its complex conjugate over Haar bases. The average has this
form by $U\otimes\overline U$ symmetry, trace D, and Bell acceptance
one. A finite convex decomposition exists by finite-dimensional
Caratheodory. A separable state accepts with probability at most
c = 2/(D + 1). An output with Bell infidelity x accepts with
v = 1 − Dx/(D + 1).

Define, for x_(*) = 1 − 1/D,
$$e_D(x)=
 \begin{cases}
 v\ln(v/c)+(1-v)\ln[(1-v)/(1-c)],&0\le x<x_*,\\
 0,&x\ge x_* .
 \end{cases}$$
A normal free instrument with average memory map Ψ obeys
E_(R)(ξ) − E_(R)(Ψ(ξ)) ≥ e_(D)(x)
whenever its output infidelity is at most x < x_(*) and E_(R)(ξ) < ∞.
Indeed choose a separable relative-entropy approximate minimizer; apply
data processing to the LOCC transcript and conditional memory; the
latter remains separable under the comparison input. Convexity gives the
memory term. Coarse-graining to acceptance gives the binary divergence
above. This proof is valid with normal infinite memory and never
subtracts infinities.

Contractivity gives T(Ψ^(i)(τ), τ) ≤ iδ. Its next block output has
infidelity at most α + iδ. Summing resource drops gives
$$R\ge\sum_{i=0}^{M-1}e_D(\alpha+i\delta).$$
For α < x_(*) and δ > 0, the decreasing left Riemann sum yields
$$\boxed{\qquad
 \delta R\ge C_D(\alpha):=
                    \int_\alpha^{x_*}e_D(x)\,dx>0 .
 \qquad}                                                   \tag{20}$$
This genuinely diverges as 1/δ at fixed entangled-output fidelity. For
δ = 0, summing indefinitely rules out finite R. For a κ-approximant
replace α by α + κ; output trace-distance error stays at most κ at every
horizon. Neither (19) nor (20) identifies the optimal noisy coefficient.

Ordinary noisy fuel defeats a tempting extension

It is wrong to replace m in the exact leading coefficient simply by
(1 − α)²m. For J = 1, d = 2, 0 ≤ α ≤ 1/2, let
$$\omega_\alpha=(1-\alpha)\varphi_2^++\alpha\varphi_2^-,
 \qquad |\varphi_2^\pm\rangle=(|00\rangle\pm|11\rangle)/\sqrt2 .$$
Its half trace distance from φ₂⁺ and its Bell infidelity are both α.
Equation (9) gives the exact additive resource
E_(R)(ω_(α)^(⊗k)) = k[ln 2 − h₂(α)],
where h₂(x) = −xln x − (1 − x)ln (1 − x).

Use an ordinary flagged consumable ladder, uniform on k = 1, …, L,
containing k copies of ω_(α), with local padding. It emits ω_(α) exactly
and returns memory with δ = 1/L. Local orthogonal flags imply
$$R=\frac1L\sum_{k=1}^LE_R(\omega_\alpha^{\otimes k})
       = \frac{L+1}{2}\,[\ln2-h_2(\alpha)] .$$
For clarity, the flag identity follows by measuring the locally readable
flags for a lower bound and using a flagged separable comparator for the
upper bound. All failure and exhaustion branches are included. The
complete first output is in product with the returned memory. Its joint
half trace error from φ₂⁺ ⊗ τ is β = δ + (1 − δ)α: the two edge flag
blocks contribute δ, and overlapping flag blocks contribute (1 − δ)α.
Thus the output error, marginal return error, and joint error are all
explicit. In particular
$$\lim_{L\to\infty}\delta R
                  =\frac{\ln2-h_2(\alpha)}2.              \tag{21}$$
At α = 0.1, this is about 0.18403 nats, below (1 − α)²ln 2/2 ≈ 0.28072.
The source is a permitted ordinary consumable state, so a proposed
universal lower bound with the latter coefficient is explicitly false.
An isotropic noisy Bell state has the same single-copy E_(R) and gives a
related subadditive upper bound; the phase-noise choice makes the entire
resource ledger exact. More powerful correlated, unflagged, or
regularized noisy consumables may improve (21). This is a real
unresolved boundary, not a reason to claim sharpness for (19)–(20).

Matched comparators and what is operationally certified

Ordinary consumption.

Without return, exact J-pair output costs at least m in initial E_(R),
attained by J Bell pairs and local swaps. Retaining a consumed inventory
flag can make the return distance one. To require small return, a large
ordinary consumable stock distributed over possible inventories attains
the m/(2δ) leading law. The unflagged construction is itself a
consumable reservoir. It does not outperform an optimally informed
ordinary source under these same definitions.

Preparation and storage.

The all-prefix reservoir has M = J + ⌈J/δ⌉ − 1 local d-level systems per
party and local dimension d^(M). Sampling K, preparing K Bell pairs, and
irreversibly discarding the sample label gives a simple preparation
consuming [J + (L − 1)/2]ln d of supplied Bell entanglement on average,
at most Mln d on a run. The initial E_(R) is smaller by ln L − H(K ∣ Z).
No equality between this E_(R) and optimal preparation cost is asserted.
A shift uses O(M) elementary local swaps in a simple circuit and a
finite classical message to append χ_(d); efficient state synthesis at
cost E_(R) is unresolved. A physical qudit-number Hamiltonian measures
stored registers, not the unretained rung K. An energy model
proportional to K would need a separate sector/flag structure and a new
resource comparison.

Pure catalytic and embezzling families.

The standard harmonic family should not be used as the strongest pure
incumbent. Smooth, sine-profile coherent entanglement ladders can also
extract exact Bell pairs by local isometries with return scaling
R = O(Jln d/δ). In such a pure ladder, coefficients over rung length L
proportional to sin [π(r + 1)/(L + 1)] have shifted-state overlap
1 − O(J²/L²), hence half trace return O(J/L). Its entanglement includes
the Shannon entropy of the coherent rung distribution; it does not
acquire the mixed reservoir’s −ln (1/δ) saving. The universal lower
bound applies to any pure or mixed permitted LOCC competitor, whether or
not described as embezzling. Zanoni–Theurer–Gour give the stronger
general pure-conversion framework; no superiority over every pure
construction is claimed here.

Rubboli–Tomamichel allow mixed catalysts and give general logarithmic
resource divergence with a joint purified-distance error. Our
exact-output construction has joint half trace error exactly δ, because
Ω_(J) = φ_(D) ⊗ τ^(′). For general states, half trace distance T and
purified distance P obey $T\le P\le\sqrt{2T-T^2}$. One cannot compare a
numerical δ in one metric directly with the same number in the other. No
exact-return finite-E_(R) Bell broadcaster exists. Type-III and singular
embezzlers lie outside the normal tensor-product model.

Complete process, correlations, and testing.

The lower bound uses the entire output state through one stopping
measurement. Equation (8) gives the construction’s exact joint
output–memory process at all times. Its initial exact Bell outputs are
decoupled by purity; later outputs are generally correlated with each
other and with remaining memory. For noisy outputs, joint Bell
infidelity, marginal memory error, and joint output–memory error are
distinct. Equations (19)–(21) keep these distinctions. Bell fidelity can
be estimated from external local measurements, but finite samples do not
certify exact equality, and full-J verification can be costly.

Proposition 2 (External data do not certify hidden return or a resource
cap). No arbitrary finite-horizon external experiment alone can
universally upper-certify the complete-memory return error or an initial
E_(R) cap in this uncharacterized-memory model.

Proof. For any horizon H, use the seed’s flagged Bell-inventory shift on
a fixed sufficiently large finite memory. Initializer one is uniform on
inventories H, …, H + L − 1; initializer two has inventory exactly H.
For both, the entire first-H causal process ignores all inputs and emits
φ_(d)^(⊗H), so no adaptive or entangled-reference tester can distinguish
them. At call H, initializer one has return half distance H/L (take
L ≥ H), while initializer two has distance one because its retained
inventory flag changes from H to 0. This is even the same device and
same memory space. An arbitrarily large entangled spectator on which the
device acts trivially also leaves every external process unchanged while
increasing the initial resource arbitrarily: discarding the original
memory lower-bounds the enlarged resource by the spectator Bell
entanglement, without requiring additivity of relative entropy of
entanglement. More generally, an invisible local clock of dimension
H + 1, initially in |0⟩ and advanced once per call, can be appended to
any compatible device. Its clock is orthogonal to its initial state
after H calls, so the complete-memory return distance is one while every
external observation is unchanged. Therefore observed outputs do not
supply the missing hidden-memory upper bounds. ◻

Calibrated memory control, trusted dimension/energy assumptions, or
additional access may permit such certification, but no such connection
is proved here.

Primary-reference and novelty boundary

The exact Bell overlap, relative-entropy data processing, maximally
correlated identity, and geometric entropy estimates are standard
ingredients. The candidate contribution is their sharp mixed-reservoir
stopping law, including the necessary logarithmic correction and the
all-prefix construction. A targeted primary-source check found no
identical optimal law; this is not a historical-priority certificate.

Piani supplies the resource-disintegration mechanism inherited by R1 and
used explicitly in (20). A routine witness-budget extension of that
result is not counted as the main contribution. Lami–Shirokov establish
attainment and lower semicontinuity for normal states; neither implies
trace-distance continuity without resource regularity. The main proof
here avoids minimizers. Son et al.  concern exact catalyst return robust
to a ball of input noise, not fixed nonzero memory-return error.
Yamasaki et al.  identify asymptotic entanglement cost with regularized
convex-roof entanglement under finite-local-entropy assumptions; they do
not identify E_(R) with cost. Van Luijk et al.  distinguish families
from fixed normal type-I embezzlers and explain the type-III boundary.
Rubboli–Tomamichel and Zanoni et al.  are the relevant mixed-catalyst
and optimal pure-conversion comparisons.

Status and exact remaining work

- Internal mathematics: complete analytic proofs and explicit
  finite-dimensional LOCC constructions are supplied. Independent
  internal adversarial checks and finite spectral/return replays
  accompany them. These checks are evidence of their stated scope, not
  external certification.

- Sharp exact-output result: both divergent terms are matched, even with
  return after every slot. The optimal bounded additive constant, and an
  exact finite-δ characterization, remain open.

- Noisy-output frontier: (19)–(20) are universal lower bounds; (21) is
  an ordinary consumable upper bound and counterexample to a tempting
  extension. The sharp noisy coefficient and correction, under complete
  joint error and arbitrary correlations, remain unresolved.

- Historical novelty: UNKNOWN. No claim of first discovery follows from
  this bounded literature check.

- Implementation: a finite shift circuit and explicit states exist.
  Optimal preparation cost and scalable experimental certification of
  hidden-memory return/resource are not established.

- Physical usefulness: NOT ESTABLISHED. No hardware operation, energy
  advantage, purchase, account signup, publication, repository push, or
  other external research chat was performed.
