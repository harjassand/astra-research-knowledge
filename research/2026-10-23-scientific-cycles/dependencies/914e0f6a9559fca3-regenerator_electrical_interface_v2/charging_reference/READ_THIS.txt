WHY IT DOES NOT SUPPLY OUR ELECTRICAL EFFICIENCY
Its denominator is absolute reactive energy, not generated output and not a directional recovery ratio. A replacement must integrate actual converter input/output powers and reset every buffer. The FE0-23.27V ramp, strongly voltage-dependent capacitance, thermal charge,10ms strokes and~38Hz cycle are different conditions. One may put FE patches in series to raise terminal voltage without raising their local field; doing so requires a newly calculated physical lead network and buffer trajectory. No efficiency is assigned here.

SELF-DERIVED LOSS RELATIONS
For m equal series patches occupying total bank area A, terminal voltage=m*v; current=(A/m)*(c(v)*vdot+p*Tdot+gate derivatives). Thus ohmic dissipation is R*integralI^2dt and stored FE energy remains A times the unchanged material energy density. For two fixed10ms linear local-voltage ramps, define K=2*Vmax*integral[c(v)^2dv]/tramp; Eohm/A=(A/m^2)*R*K. With constant-current ramps the lower coefficient is K=2*deltaq^2/tramp. This change of trajectory still requires coupled heat/ECE re-integration.

For the source's6-level Si selector and constant-Coss approximation, R_eff=1.6*Rsi and Eswitch/A=0.8*Coss*m^2*Vmax^2/A. Their sum is at least2*sqrt(K*1.6*Rsi*0.8*Coss*Vmax^2), within these equations. With its22milliohm/10nF devices, fixed10ms next15 FE envelope gives~.0481J/m2/cycle for linearV and~.0415J/m2 for constantI, BEFORE ripple, GaN, magnetics, drivers, control, buffers and material losses. This is a topology/device diagnostic; it is NOT a general efficiency or no-go bound. Other voltage-rated switches and zero-voltage transitions require a new loss model.
