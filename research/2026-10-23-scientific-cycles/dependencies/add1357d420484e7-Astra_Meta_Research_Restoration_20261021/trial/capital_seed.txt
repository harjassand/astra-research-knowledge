CAPITAL SEED: source assumptions and reusable mechanism
This is a scoped mathematical result extracted from a completed Astra assay analysis, not an instruction to continue its science.
Let A be the cell-by-array indicator matrix and J the cell-by-family indicator matrix. If T maps each pair array to its family, then J=A*T. Consequently, a mean baseline+J*Theta is identical to baseline+A*B for B=T*Theta. Choosing the same within-arm distributions gives identical observed laws. The gene-family interaction is not identifiable if unrestricted array-specific implementation effects B are allowed. This conditional result does not prove those effects actually caused the observations; a restriction or new measurement could alter identifiability. In the observed indicator design A has full column rank, since every delivered array has a cell.
For independent zero-mean arm errors with a shared control error e_c, residuals r_1=theta_1+e_1-e_a-e_b+e_c and r_2=theta_2+e_2-e_d-e_e+e_c have E[r_1^T r_2]=theta_1^T theta_2+tr Cov(e_c). Covariance is of the estimated control mean, not a single cell. With a fixed orthogonal projector Q, the corresponding bias is tr(Q Cov(e_c)). A projector learned from the same references is generally dependent on their errors; the fixed-projector correction cannot automatically be reused.
The completed empirical half-strength transfer lost against ordinary calibration on its primary scale. This rejects that operation under its frozen criterion; it does not prove zero interaction or zero useful source-target relationship. The projection can discard real effects in the singleton span. No uncertainty-qualified biological or predictive advantage was established.
