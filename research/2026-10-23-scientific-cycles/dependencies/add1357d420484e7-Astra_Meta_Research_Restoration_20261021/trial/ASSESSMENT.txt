The script verify_trial.py rechecks input hashes and the finite rational fixtures.
It does not certify the universal arguments. The mathematical grading is unblinded

Decision-changing interpretation

The compact seed retained all tested correctness with 90.95% fewer supplied
capital bytes than the full report. That is a document-size fact, not a token,
time, cost or scientific-productivity gain. The no-capital control also succeeded.
No acquired capability, transfer advantage, warm-context penalty, or fresh-instance
advantage is demonstrated.

A common correctness ceiling was observed. Excessive task ease and specification
are plausible explanations; this trial cannot isolate their causal contribution. In particular, stage 2 supplies the proposed Gaussian counterexample;
solving it is an assumption-checking task, not inventing an unknown scientific
construction. The test establishes feasibility of compact assumption-bearing
handoffs for these cases, and rules out presenting this trial as amplification.
It does not prove equivalence of strategies on difficult research.

Limitations and cost

There are three first-stage and six second-stage answers, one instance per
condition in each comparison. There is no statistical power calculation,
randomised question sample, model-token accounting, total-cost comparison,
blind grading, enforced file isolation, or long-lived scientific-context test.
Agents were instructed to read only their assigned files. All observed solutions
identify the assigned support; this is not an adversarial isolation certificate.
Retrieval, seed construction, orchestration and evaluation are additional costs.
Shorter supplied context does not establish a saving after those costs.

The next meta-research test must use a naturally blocked continuation for which
a baseline still has a genuine scientific obligation. Give all treatments the
same access to original evidence; vary the capital representation or retrieval
operation rather than hiding an indispensable fact from controls. Freeze expected
proof obligations and total resource limits before running. Reject the trial as
non-discriminating if all controls already solve the task. No full branch is
assigned merely to expand this small benchmark.
