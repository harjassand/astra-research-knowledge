ASTRA: RESTORING SCIENTIFIC AUTONOMY AND RESEARCH-SYSTEM INTELLIGENCE
Principal meta-research assessment — 21 October 2026

EXECUTIVE DECISION

Restore the user's stated architecture. Keep the scientific discipline learned
from the recent failures, and withdraw the branch-level reserve rules that turned
autonomous investigators into recipients of centrally selected tasks.

The recent allocation contained a real architectural error: a valid requirement
for a dependent worker to receive complete inputs became a requirement that an
autonomous investigator wait for the evaluator to supply an already specified
construction. That prevents the investigator from doing part of its scientific
job. Stopping the guide-transfer operation was justified by its evidence. Making
that operation the only permitted new research direction was a separate choice
for which we have no comparative evidence of superior scientific yield.

The recovered state is more constructive than the guide return alone suggests.
An independent N33 continuation has an actual draft proof, source and scalar
checks. The latest hidden-entropy report describes both an internal rediscovery
failure and a substantive recovery extension. R1, N557 and other programmes retain
scientific capital and unresolved questions. Their correctness, novelty and
usefulness have different evidence levels; none should be flattened into either
"breakthrough achieved" or "nothing useful happened."

This review executed a small controlled comparison of full report, compact seed
and no inherited capital, including warm and fresh follow-ups. All nine answers
were correct on the frozen criteria. The seed was shorter, but the control also
solved the tasks. No research amplification or context-strategy winner is
demonstrated. That negative meta result matters: ordinary reasoning success is
not evidence that an archive or handoff has improved the system.

The immediate change is organizational, supported by explicit role mismatch.
The next scientific test of the architecture is whether autonomous research,
capital development and accurate integration produce useful downstream advances.
Root's job is to investigate that mechanism with evidence, not to choose the
entire scientific portfolio or replace research with perpetual audits.

1. WHAT WAS EXAMINED

This review examined the actual Post-B3 prompts and final Ultra/Max/Dots source
returns, visible progress records and failed attempts; the repository's original
scientific files and integration conventions; newer private hidden-entropy, N33
and IQM reports; and the actual N33 draft PR #112 proof and code. Twenty-one
repository files at the inspected main revision were preserved, with an additional
exact recovered N33 TeX and four newer draft files. Original hashes were checked
where available. N33's original arithmetic checker was rerun successfully.

The inspected main revision is 65633ec33f253deb1e8e01dd47fa91803c1916ac.
N33 draft #112 is pinned at c45beab0b0dbeeb62b596b2a3d9e370338b5eef5.
The source map, original files, retrieval provenance, trial answers and receipts
are included. No scientific owner was reassigned, no retired campaign was
restarted, no public repository was changed and no experiment consumed IQM credits.

Access limits matter. The review has visible messages, mathematical arguments,
source code and execution artifacts, not unexposed internal model reasoning.
The latest R1 proof ZIP and hidden-entropy Round 10 proof ZIP were not recovered
in this bounded pass. Their producer reports are useful evidence about activity
and decisions but are not proof substitutes. Numbered Pro-to-programme ownership
could not be established reliably. The prompts preserve the actual receiving
owner's current work rather than inventing an ownership map.

2. HOW THE ARCHITECTURE DRIFTED

The exact Post-B3 Pro 1 instruction said: "Otherwise remain unallocated. Do not
generate replacement topics." Pro 2 was to remain in reserve until a "source-
complete distinct construction" was allocated by the evaluator. Ultra's guide
experiment was called the only new substantive assignment. Dots activated only
after Ultra's result. Max's required operation ended at integrating receipt and
saved-output consistency, rather than making scientific capital usable in GitHub.
The precise paths and full historical prompts are preserved in evidence/.

This converted a portfolio of autonomous scientific investigators into a serial
pipeline for one centrally chosen problem. It also put the evaluator in the
position of repeatedly selecting a domain and designing its experiment, even
though the evaluator's principal subject should be the research system.

There is an important qualification. The earlier strategic-review request gave
the evaluator genuine discretion over selection and allocation and allowed
resources to remain unused. The error was an architectural choice under that
discretion, not evidence that an unchanged explicit instruction was knowingly
ignored throughout the history. The latest user clarification now resolves the
role boundary.

The reserve instructions also expressly protected existing independently owned
programmes. We therefore cannot infer that all Pros became idle or that all
research stopped. The defect is the transition after a bounded task ends: an
investigator without a separately named active programme was forbidden to
formulate the next one. A complete local task became an absorbing waiting state.

Three different decisions were being collapsed:

* Operation: this construction has failed under its stated assumptions.
* Question: the broader question may be solved, open, blocked or worth pursuing
  through a different construction.
* Mission: the researcher continues to originate and investigate questions.

The evidence for the first decision does not settle the other two. This
distinction belongs in ordinary continuation prompts and checkpoints. It does
not require a new research-management system.

3. WHAT THE SCIENTIFIC RECORD CHANGES

3.1 The guide negative result remains valid within its scope

The original operation admitted 105 guide families and excluded 53. Ordinary
calibration substantially improved on raw additivity. The proposed transferred
correction worsened log-scale point error in both directions, although count-scale
point changes were favorable. It failed the frozen representation requirement.
Only 7 of 399 uncertainty draws completed; seven control omissions were not run.

Dots independently reproduced point scores and exposed a Linux memory-unit bug.
The recorded producer stop on macOS remains supported. The final Max archive
contains Dots' actual assessment and resolves the earlier incomplete acceptance
state. These are useful scientific and verification contributions.

This does not establish absence of biological interaction, impossibility of
prediction, a lower bound on necessary resampling memory, or the inferiority of
autonomous investigators. The conditional aliasing argument is a scoped
identifiability statement. The failed frozen operation should remain closed.

The distinction is central to the restoration: retain the strong comparator and
independent implementation; remove the inference that a failed operation releases
the system from continuing science.

3.2 N33 is a concrete counterexample to a purely exclusion-based diagnosis

The direct path for the original permanence proof returned 404. The indexed
source remained available, and its extracted TeX exactly matched the pinned
SHA256. A missing preferred route did not mean the scientific object was lost.

More importantly, the newer independent continuation exists as draft PR #112,
not only as a chat summary. It reuses the finite-affine permanence geometry to
develop conditional stochastic capture followed by long finite residence.
Its new argument smooths the barrier and accounts for a finite-volume generator
error; the record preserves hypotheses, a quantitative example and limitations.
The scalar checker passed this review's replay.

This is evidence of an investigator building a further scientific object from
earlier capital. It is not a fresh independent proof of the all-network theorem,
external novelty clearance or a technological capability. General constants
remain non-effective, and no compelling measured chemical application has been
established in the recovered record. The correct response is to preserve the
owner's substantive continuation, with its actual unresolved obligations.

The draft also corrects a possible overstatement about integration failure:
main is older than some private returns, but newer scientific work has already
been placed in a draft branch. Main, drafts and private source have different
roles. Treating only main as current research state would lose real work.

3.3 Hidden entropy shows both useful continuity and failed retrieval

The latest recovered Round 10 response explicitly reports finding a result
already present in Round 7 after partially reconstructing it. The investigator
withdraws its novelty claim. This is a direct example of internal rediscovery,
not speculation that long context might be inefficient.

The same response reports a later finite internally reversible recovery extension
under precise source hypotheses. The fixed-q value and finite attainment remain
unresolved. Its original proof archive was not recovered here, so the extension
remains reported evidence in this review.

This pair of events supports a precise target for Ultra and Max: help a
researcher find the strongest usable prior object, with its exact limits, before
spending effort reconstructing or overstating it. It does not justify forcing
all warm investigators to reset; the continuing owner also made the correction
and advanced the argument.

3.4 Other work should retain its proper status

N557's complete public candidate manuscript is usable mathematical capital with
specific finite-mode, finite-energy and independence assumptions. Newer work
remains with its owner. R1's reported sharp finite-reuse result and its matching
constructions should be preserved, with exact source recovery distinguished
from the older catalyst-removal material. Quasiconvexity dossiers distinguish
proved scopes, finite-H claims and the unresolved positive all-affine boundary.

The IQM report records a prospective decision to preserve credits, with no
hardware job. That is an honest evidence-acquisition constraint, not completed
experimental science. Broader research need not wait for unavailable hardware,
and access to a free device alone does not make an experiment discriminating.

These examples oppose a blanket diagnosis that Astra can only reject proposals.
They also do not establish accelerating significance over successive cycles.
There is no cost-matched longitudinal evidence of exponential progress.

4. COMPETING EXPLANATIONS FOR THE RECURRING DIFFICULTIES

Overcentralization is directly observed in the instructions, but it cannot
causally explain every negative scientific result. The water unitary comparison
floor, thermal leakage, unavailable observation model and guide representation
failure are substantive obstacles. A different allocation does not make those
obstacles disappear.

The evidence supports several distinct limitations:

Scientific admissibility. Applied constructions repeatedly looked attractive
before a shared resource, reset, material coupling, baseline or information
constraint was included. More reasoning can expose these omissions, but cannot
manufacture a measurement, low-loss material or unmeasured causal contrast.

Question selection. Verifiable formal targets can be easier to specify than
consequential research needs. That creates a risk of choosing questions for
auditability. The mathematical continuations counter a blanket conclusion:
an independently significant theorem need not beat a device or have immediate
industrial utility. Selection should be scientifically argued by the owner,
not reduced to either practical-impact rhetoric or formal tractability.

Incentive asymmetry. A single fatal inconsistency can settle one construction.
Establishing a useful capability usually requires many conditions to work
together. This makes exclusions easier to finish and communicate. It is a
plausible contributor to the observed pattern, not a measured psychological
property of the models. Preserve exclusions as constraints for future invention
rather than treating their number as the objective.

Retrieval and continuity. The hidden-entropy rediscovery, missing preferred N33
path and source-export problems give concrete evidence. The successful N33
recovery and Dots transfer also show that some barriers are removable with existing
tools. A new memory platform is not yet justified.

Allocation and integration. Independent verification corrected real defects, but
making a powerful scientist an exclusive receiver has an opportunity cost that
was not measured. A receipt can prove source transport without proving that
another research problem became easier. The two activities should be distinguished.

Model and evidence ceilings. Current tools do not supply missing measurements,
arbitrary compute or external scientific acceptance. The record does not isolate
base-model capability from problem difficulty, prompt, context or source quality.
Claims that a different subagent count would overcome those limits are untested.

5. THE CONTROLLED META EXPERIMENT AND ITS NEGATIVE FINDING

The experiment used a real Astra report as inherited capital but synthetic,
fully specified mathematical receiving tasks. Before the answers were produced,
the task statements, reference results, conditions and limitations were frozen.

Three fresh same-runtime instances received either the full scientific report,
a compact assumption-bearing seed, or no inherited result. They solved
identification and transfer-loss questions. The same instances then received
changed assumptions as warm continuations. Three fresh instances received only
the second-stage task and the corresponding support condition.

All nine answers met the predeclared substantive criteria on root's unblinded
review. They correctly handled the full-rank identification criterion, minimum
calibration rows, frozen-versus-target-fitted gain, the rank-deficient counterexample
and the failure of a fixed-projector bias correction when learned from the noise.
An exact rational script rechecks the fixtures and frozen-input hashes; it is
not an automated certificate of the universal arguments.

The capital seed used 1,901 bytes versus 21,005 for the full report, a 90.95%
reduction in supplied source text. That is not measured computation saved:
seed construction, retrieval and integration have costs, and no-capital controls
also succeeded. No treatment demonstrated superior capability.

The task family was therefore non-discriminating at this scale. Stage 2 even
supplied the proposed projector counterexample; handling it correctly tests
premise discipline, not the invention of a new counterconstruction. The test
had one instance per condition, instructional rather than enforced file
isolation, no token/cost telemetry and only two short warm turns. It does not
represent long-lived scientific ownership or compare actual Pro/Sol branches.

The practical decision is to reject a blanket warm/fresh or seed policy based
on this evidence. Keep warm owners where they possess useful technical state;
use a compact seed as an entry point to exact source; use a fresh fork where
independent assumptions or a genuinely different mechanism matter.

A stronger next test must start from a real scientific obligation that remains
nontrivial for an equally resourced baseline. All treatments should have access
to original evidence. The intervention should improve how useful capital is
retrieved or applied, rather than simply hide indispensable data from controls.
If controls already solve the task, report that and stop the fixture.

6. WHAT EXTERNAL RESEARCH DOES AND DOES NOT SUPPORT

The primary multi-agent scaling study examines 260 configurations over six task
families and reports strongly task-dependent benefits and regressions. It
supports matching the coordination pattern to the work; it does not test Astra's
three autonomous researchers selecting scientific questions. It cannot justify
either global centralization or maximal parallelism.

The widely cited multi-turn degradation study compares gradually revealed
instructions with fully specified tasks. It is not a comparison of a scientist
continuing an actual derivation against a fresh scientist given an exact seed.
It supports explicit consolidation of changing premises, not mandatory resets.

Agent Workflow Memory supplies evidence that reusable procedures can improve
some action benchmarks, with important limits on whole-task success and combined
memory methods. Darwin Godel Machine and HyperAgents test changes to agent
procedures, but reported gains involve particular tasks, cost conditions and
baselines. HyperAgents' later transferred-versus-fresh comparison is not
statistically significant in the cited test. None establishes recursive
scientific intelligence for Astra.

Official OpenAI guidance supports isolated subagents and worktrees for suitable
work, while acknowledging overhead and integration concerns. Available tools
and slot limits must be read from the actual branch environment. A paper or
product capability does not establish that a long-running job or schedule is
active in a particular chat.

Primary sources and exact qualifications are in evidence/meta_literature.txt.
The implication is experimental discipline, not importing another project's
large infrastructure or interpreting reported benchmark gains as scientific
progress.

7. A CREDIBLE MECHANISM FOR SCIENTIFIC INTELLIGENCE AMPLIFICATION

The strongest supported candidate is to accumulate reusable scientific
operations, not just outcomes or instructions. A reusable object specifies:
what can be derived, computed or measured; exact assumptions and access; its
proof or executable implementation; its costs; and a nearby case where it fails.

The distinction is operational. "N33 proves permanence" is a result summary.
A finite-affine barrier, with its actual drift hypotheses and limitations, is
material another scientist can transform into a stochastic argument. A guide
negative result is useful when its exact aliasing conditions constrain the next
observation design; it is harmful if compressed into "this biology cannot work."

N33 provides a documented example of capital being used in a later construction.
The current trial shows that possession of a reusable description does not
automatically improve a receiver: sometimes the unaided model already solves
the task. Hidden entropy identifies a different benefit to test—avoiding internal
rediscovery while preserving the ability to extend a proof.

The scientific amplification claim requires an independent investigation to
benefit. That benefit can be a newly resolvable question, a stronger valid theorem,
a useful algorithm or less total computation for the same quality. The comparison
must charge construction, retrieval, validation and integration. Shorter answers,
more saved files or successful uploads do not establish it.

Recursive improvement is a stronger requirement. An acquired method must help
produce or assess a further improved method, which must then transfer to new
questions. Repeating an original success after tuning on its test does not count.
Two or several generations still would not prove indefinite exponential growth.
The current programme has examples of scientific accumulation and potential
ingredients for this loop, but no established recursive growth mechanism.

The proposed mechanism fails if applying the capital costs as much as independent
derivation, if recipient gains vanish after verification, if the assumptions do
not fit actual scientific needs, or if later apparent improvement is test leakage.
The earlier 72-question metabolic transfer trial reportedly found no net
acceleration after checking cost; this round's no-capital controls add another
reason to demand a real counterfactual.

8. RESTORED OPERATING METHODS

The three Pro branches independently own consequential research questions.
Scientific autonomy includes deciding when to continue a hard unresolved
question, exploit an unexpected theorem or switch after a genuinely exhausted
operation. It does not mean ignoring established falsifiers or duplicating another
owner's work. No arbitrary division into mathematics, biology and physics is
imposed merely to make the allocation look diverse.

Ultra concentrates on extending and connecting accumulated capital. It should
personally produce new mathematical or scientific work, including useful
compositions and constructive consequences. It should not be reduced to a
catalogue or assigned every new domain selected by the evaluator.

Max maintains a source-based scientific memory in the existing repository.
Integrating a correction means preserving the original object and recording
which claims depend on it, rather than silently rewriting history. A usable
source route and a successful independent receiving operation matter more than
another dashboard. Public and private material retain their disclosure boundaries.

Dots continues substantial independent investigation and experiments.
Its demonstrated checking ability remains valuable; a bounded critical review
can be a contribution without becoming its permanent exclusive role. It should
report actual execution rather than promise unsupported continuous background work.

The principal investigates research methods. Three distinct questions deserve
continued tests: whether mission-preserving continuations lead to useful
constructions after local failure; whether assumption-bearing scientific capital
helps on real nontrivial receiving work; and where fresh independent review adds
more value than it costs. These are meta-research questions, not reasons to make
all six branches dependent on a centralized experiment.

Use subagents when work separates into distinct mechanisms, lemmas, measurements
or independent checks. Keep tightly dependent reasoning with an integrating owner.
Fork a rival construction before it inherits the first construction's unjustified
premises, where practical. Share exact input and resource definitions even when
the approaches are independent. Early review should attack a premise whose
failure would invalidate the project; later review can test the completed
construction. Endless symmetric audits are not the default.

Maintain a concise current scientific state alongside original artifacts:
question, established objects, assumptions that changed, failed operations,
unresolved obligation and exact sources. Preserve a warm continuation when it
is useful. Context repair and source recovery are different interventions from
starting over; neither should be a ritual.

9. NEXT ACTIONS AND WHAT WOULD REVISE THIS DECISION

The six complete prompts in prompts/ restore the roles while preserving existing
ownership. Each has context, source routes, a scientific success criterion,
operation-level stopping conditions and agent-owned delivery. They require no
user relay or source selection. Three Pro prompts differ only in their known
historical closure context; the unknown current ownership is deliberately not
invented.

The next round should produce independently chosen science from the Pros and
Dots, a substantive capital-based continuation from Ultra, and actual scoped
source integration from Max. This is a mission correction, not a quota of six
new projects. A researcher already pursuing an important question continues it.
The evaluator studies their outcomes and decisions while running only bounded
process experiments needed to distinguish its hypotheses.

The restoration is supported as an architectural correction, not as an already
proved productivity improvement. Evidence that would change its implementation
includes repeated costly duplication despite good source access; a demonstrated
shared scientific bottleneck that needs temporary concentration; systematic
failure of owner-selected problems to expose meaningful evidence; or a matched
process trial showing that a different division of labor produces more important
verified work at comparable cost. One failed autonomous project or one centrally
selected success would not by itself settle that comparison.

The principal's next research should focus on a naturally arising, difficult
receiving obligation and a controlled test of useful scientific capital. That
work can proceed through bounded meta subagents without commandeering the Pro
portfolio. If no operation gives the receiver a verifiable benefit, the correct
conclusion is that the proposed amplification mechanism is unproven or fails
on that workload, followed by a revised scientific hypothesis.

The goal remains ambitious. The defensible current position is that Astra has
real mathematical and verification capital, several unresolved practical limits,
and examples of cumulative extension. It has not demonstrated a trajectory of
recursive or exponential scientific improvement. Restoring the intended roles
creates room to investigate that possibility; only subsequent scientific results
and matched receiving evidence can establish it.
