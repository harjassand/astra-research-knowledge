ADVERSARIAL REVIEW OF THE ROLE-RESTORATION PACKET
21 October 2026

Scope
Read START_HERE.txt, CURRENT_SCIENTIFIC_STATE.txt, all seven continuation
prompts, trial/ASSESSMENT.txt and evidence/trace_audit.txt in full. Also read
the handoff protocol, capital index, recovery note, trial preregistration,

Verdict
The core correction is sound. The three Pro prompts now give investigators
question selection; Ultra has a capital-development mission; Max has a concrete
GitHub integration mission; Dots is an independent persistent scientist. No
numbered Pro-to-programme ownership has been invented. The trace audit properly
acknowledges the earlier evaluator's real delegated discretion and does not
rewrite the history as disobedience to an unchanged mandate. The trial assessment
does not claim demonstrated amplification. No new dashboard, permanent review
layer, compulsory domain assignment or scientific task for every idle slot is
needed.

The following are narrow fixes to the supplied prompts and presentation. None
requires restarting the guide experiment or auditing the inherited theorems.

1. Remove the evaluator's residual authority exception.

Where: prompts/07_META_RESEARCHER_CONTINUE.txt, opening paragraph:
  "Do not prescribe the Pros' scientific questions without overwhelming evidence"

Problem: this leaves the evaluator a unilateral, undefined route back into
central question selection. Strong evidence can justify challenging a premise
or recommending a scoped stop; it does not by itself transfer the user's
assignment of scientific ownership. There is no operational definition or
independent authority for the "overwhelming" threshold.

Necessary fix: say that the evaluator studies and challenges the research
system, presents consequential evidence to the owning investigator, and does
not take over that investigator's question selection unless the user changes
the role assignment. Do not replace the exception with a new approval board.

2. Remove the requirement that Ultra finish a result within one turn.

Where: prompts/01_SOL_ULTRA_CONTINUE.txt:
  "Work through a substantive result or a decisive scoped obstruction in this turn."

Problem: a consequential investigation can make correct, valuable progress
without either finishing a theorem or proving an obstruction in one interaction.
This is an outcome quota with a hidden pressure toward smaller questions or
premature declarations of impossibility. It also conflicts with the same
prompt's protection of an already active substantial investigation. The Pro
instruction to perform substantive research is less problematic: it does not
require a completed result on the same schedule.

Necessary fix: require actual scientific work and an honest checkpoint containing
what was established, the unresolved load-bearing step and the next operation.
A result or decisive obstruction is welcome when earned. An unfinished hard
question is not failure of the restored mission and does not require a replacement
topic. Retain the instruction to stop a specifically falsified method.

3. Give the meta continuation the same recoverable starting context as the others.

Where: prompts/07_META_RESEARCHER_CONTINUE.txt. Unlike prompts 01–06, it does
not name the common archive, how to retrieve it or the evidence files to read.
It starts by referring to "this round", "all nine" answers and an established
architecture mismatch without a source route.

Problem: this is not a self-contained continuation when pasted into a fresh
meta-research conversation. It invites reliance on the summary's authority or
a repeat recovery exercise.

Necessary fix: add the exact archive name and private retrieval instruction;
identify START_HERE.txt, CURRENT_SCIENTIFIC_STATE.txt, the completed restoration
report, evidence/trace_audit.txt and trial/ASSESSMENT.txt as the starting evidence.
Require preservation of the evaluator's actual later checkpoint if one exists.
After final saving, provide a stable private retrieval receipt wherever supported;
do not assert recipient verification or cross-chat access without observing it.
The report being absent while the parent is still writing it is not itself a
finding of missing final content.

4. Mark the recovery note as an intermediate snapshot and expose the old
   closure-evidence route.

Where: state/ACTIVE_STATE_RECOVERY.txt still says the current N33 investigator
source "has not been retrieved here" and that root "is retrieving" hidden
entropy/IQM reports. CURRENT_SCIENTIFIC_STATE.txt correctly records the later
N33 PR #112 retrieval and the recovered private reports.

Problem: both descriptions can be true of successive recovery passes, but the
intermediate note has no prominent instruction that the later consolidated
state supersedes these access-status statements. A recipient can read it as a
contradiction and redo work or downgrade a source already recovered.

Necessary fix: add a short provenance header to the recovery note, explicitly
labelling it the earlier repository-only pass and pointing to the consolidated
state for later retrieval status. Preserve the historical observations below.

A related navigation defect affects the settled boundaries. Several new prompts
refer only to "thermal", "TPV", "molecular" or other retired operations, while

  -> received/Astra_PostB3_ULTRA_GUIDE_RESULT_20261021.zip

For an uncertain inherited boundary, use the exact surviving source/owner
checkpoint; do not infer a ban on the whole domain from a shorthand label.
There is no need to include every historical proof in the restoration archive.

5. Make the small trial's non-result wording uniform.

Where: START_HERE.txt says the trial "shows no transfer advantage";
seeds/CAPITAL_INDEX.txt calls it an "explicit null demonstration of advantage";
trial/ASSESSMENT.txt says the family "was too easy and too fully specified".
The assessment's detailed limitations are otherwise appropriately strong.

Problem: no cost comparison, one realization per condition, a correctness
ceiling and unblinded assessment do not establish a true null effect or a unique
causal explanation for the equal outcomes. The supplied Gaussian construction
does establish that part of the task tested checking rather than discovery.

Necessary fix: consistently say "no transfer or warm/fresh advantage was
demonstrated in this trial". Describe the common correctness ceiling as the
observation and excessive ease/specification as a plausible explanation for
non-discrimination. Compact support was compatible with correct answers here;
the control success means the trial did not show that the seed caused those
answers or transferred a previously unavailable capability. Retain the genuine
90.95% document-byte reduction and its existing cost caveat.

Privacy and continuity findings requiring no new control layer

The supplied handoff protocol correctly separates private upload, receiver
verification and scientific acceptance; forbids using public hosting to repair
a private transfer; and does not demand hidden reasoning export. Max's prompt
preserves private disclosure limits and permits unaffected public-source
integration to continue. START_HERE's restriction on new public disclosure is
compatible with integrating already-public or previously authorized sources.
Do not weaken those protections or treat the privately supplied reports as
publication permission.

An unavailable proof remains a source-limited item, rather than grounds to
reassign its owner or stop all work. Source recovery and a receiving check must
also remain bounded operations: the packet already says not to fabricate access,
not to make other branches wait, and not to idle when usable owned sources exist.
Preserve these explicit provisions. The packet does not prove that all restored
roles have been activated in separate chats, nor that any persistent background
research is running; report restoration of instructions separately from observed
execution and from any later measured gain in scientific capability.
