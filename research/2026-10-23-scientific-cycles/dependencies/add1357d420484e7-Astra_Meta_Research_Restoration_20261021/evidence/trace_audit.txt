21 October 2026 | Scope: local Post-B3 assignment/return artifacts and visible user instructions.
No science was rerun. No repository or original source was modified.

CONCLUSION

The strongest demonstrated architectural error is that dependency-admission rules
were applied to autonomous scientific generators. A rule appropriate for a
dependent verifier — wait for a concrete source-complete result — became the
permission boundary for Pro investigators whose job includes creating questions,
constructions and the evidence needed to judge them. The resulting allocation
gave the principal evaluator a monopoly over new directions, assigned one of those
directions to Ultra, and made Dots/Max downstream receivers. That differs materially
from the user's now-explicit architecture.

This is an interpretation of explicit instructions and observed work, not a measured
causal explanation for all scientific failures. The earlier Post-B3 user prompt
explicitly requested strategic problem selection and allocation, permitted unused
resources and asked for a chosen next opportunity. The evaluator therefore had
real discretion. The error was an architectural choice under that discretion;
the evidence does not support a claim of ignoring an unchanged explicit mandate
throughout the whole history. The current clarification supersedes that allocation.

2. MECHANISMS SUPPORTED BY THE TRACES

A. A task-state/mission-state conflation.
The valid local action is 'do not repeat this disproven operation'. The generated
role state becomes 'do not formulate another question until the evaluator assigns
one'. The latter does not follow scientifically from the former. Mechanism closure,
scientific-question continuation and researcher-mission continuity must be separate.
This can be handled in ordinary prompts/checkpoints; no new platform is needed.

B. Centralisation justified by an untested counterfactual.
The earlier review correctly says there is no evidence that more parallelism would
fix missing information or invalid physical premises (lines 307–310). That does
not imply that removing autonomous question generation improves the portfolio.
Parallel independent invention and parallel repetition of a failed premise are
different interventions. No matched experiment compared them here. The concentrated
guide result does not establish that the unused alternative investigators would
have produced more or less valuable science.

C. Dependency planning crowded out scientific-capital integration.
Source transfer, correct receipt and independent acceptance were repaired, but
Max's task ended at receipt integration. The sources reached the evaluator, yet
this particular allocation did not require a second scientist to retrieve and use
a mathematical/scientific object from GitHub. Delivery success is necessary for
some transfers, but it is not the transfer of a capability into new research.

D. A limited outcome was asked to carry programme-level meaning.
The guide experiment fails its fixed positive point gate and leaves uncertainty
unfinished. It resolves that operation. It does not estimate the discovery yield
of autonomous Pros, capital-mining Ultra, fresh versus warm contexts, or continual
Dots research. The principal's architecture therefore needs its own experiments,
not an inference from another domain experiment's scientific success/failure.

3. STRENGTHS AND COUNTEREXAMPLES THAT MUST SURVIVE THE CORRECTION

The recent programme is not uniformly ineffective:

* The ordinary guide comparator and frozen representation check prevented an
  apparent gain from being promoted into a biological/predictive discovery.
* Dots' independent implementation recreated actual RNA point scores and identified
  a concrete Linux RSS-unit defect. This was substantive independent checking,
  not merely counting files or rerunning the producer script.
* Final Max INTEGRATION.json and DEPENDENCY.json resolve the earlier missing-Dots
  status and explicitly accept only the scoped negative/incomplete conclusion.
* Existing owners were repeatedly protected. Current active work cannot be inferred
  from a general reserve label; owners' actual latest records must take precedence.
* The earlier metabolic transfer trial reused the acquired exact reduction on 72
  new receiving questions and measured its verification overhead. It found no net
  acceleration on that workload. This is real meta-research and a counterexample
  to saying the principal only wrote coordination plans.
* The earlier review explicitly recognises construction and accumulation in R1,
  N33 and hidden entropy (lines 299–306), and the independent significance of
  foundational mathematics (lines 334–341). It should not be rewritten as a blanket
  bias against theory.

The corrected architecture should preserve claim boundaries, strong comparators,
source completeness for dependent work, timely checks and honest resource costs.
It should restore scientific autonomy without authorising repetition of settled
mechanisms, duplicated independently owned work or unlimited review overhead.

4. THREE TESTABLE META-RESEARCH HYPOTHESES

H1 — Mission-preserving continuation increases useful construction after local failure.
Intervention: compare identical archived scientific state under (a) a task-stop
handoff and (b) a task-stop plus explicit continuing autonomous mission. On several
matched cases, assign fresh same-model investigators with equal resources. In both
conditions forbid reopening the excluded construction. Require an actual next
argument/computation, not lists of topics. Blind assessment checks whether the
operation addresses a real remaining question and produces correct new evidence.
Measure useful work and prohibited repetition, with all development/review costs.
An increase in activity alone is not success. The simple difference in permission
compliance is expected by design; the scientific yield is the untested hypothesis.

H2 — Compact seeds preserving assumptions and failure boundaries transfer better
than chronological summaries, without losing constructive freedom.
Intervention: compare a chronological trace, a compact claim/assumption/boundary
seed with exact source pointers, and direct original source on the same held-out
continuation tasks. Freeze tasks/scoring before producing seeds. Count seed-building
and retrieval costs. Check mathematical correctness, omitted assumptions, stale
status errors and useful generalisation. One small success demonstrates a local
transfer only. A true warm-context experiment requires a genuinely continued owner;
a fresh instance shown a long transcript is not an equivalent warm branch.

H3 — Reviews targeted to load-bearing scientific claims preserve more productive
investigator time than treating each owner as a permanent general auditor.
Compare equal-budget constructive-first then independent critical review against
review-heavy continuation, with the same starting problem and source access.
Measure correct completed construction, consequential errors caught, review cost
and duplicate work. Claim-level isolation matters: some early premise checks avoid
wasted construction, so 'all review should be delayed' is not the hypothesis.
The guide Dots review is a positive example of bounded useful criticism, not evidence
that Dots should be an exclusive auditor across all future rounds.

5. CAUSAL AND ACCESS LIMITS

There are no matched controls isolating model capability, allocation, question
difficulty, reasoning duration and source availability across B1–B3. Total reasoning,
engineering and orchestration cost is incomplete. Visible transcripts and proof/
execution artifacts can be analysed; inaccessible internal reasoning is not evidence
we possess. A centralised negative result does not prove autonomous parallel research
would have succeeded. Conversely, a successful theorem does not prove a particular
prompt or subagent count caused it.

The immediate architecture correction is mandated by the user's clarified roles
and supported by explicit scope mismatch. Claims that it improves scientific yield
must be earned through the next observed constructions and controlled process tests.
