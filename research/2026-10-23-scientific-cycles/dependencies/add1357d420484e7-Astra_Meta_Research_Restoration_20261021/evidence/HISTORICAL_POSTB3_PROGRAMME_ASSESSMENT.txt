ASTRA — POST-B3 STRATEGIC INFLECTION REVIEW
Principal programme evaluator | 2026-10-21
Decision: REDIRECT. Preserve the substantive foundational programmes; change
selection and allocation of the capability-building programme.

EXECUTIVE ASSESSMENT

A. Where is Astra today?
Astra is a capable theoretical and computational research collaborator. It has
constructed nontrivial mathematical arguments, executable exact transformations,
useful counterexamples, and a genuine correction to an external physical model.
It has not demonstrated a transformative technological capability or a research
system whose productivity improves recursively. Important mathematical candidates
remain internally assessed rather than externally settled.

B. Are we progressing, accumulating results, or drifting?
All three descriptions have some support, in different parts of the programme.
The foundational chains contain cumulative scientific content. The capability
campaign has accumulated better artifacts and more realistic comparisons, but
frequent changes of target have not produced a sustained scientific advantage.
B3 reduced drift toward convenient toy problems; it did not establish that the
selected operations were important unmet bottlenecks.

C. What capabilities actually improved?
Complete finite-model certification, portable source delivery, explicit treatment
of physical and statistical interfaces, and some mathematical transfer improved.
Dots' composition check was consumed by Max. A fresh receiving calculation here
successfully used the B3 exact reduction on 72 new optimization questions. Its
verification cost, however, erased the measured solver saving. Correct transfer,
new mathematical scope, and net research acceleration must remain distinct.

D. Most consequential weakness?
Astra too often selects a formal operation it can investigate before establishing
that this operation removes the binding obstacle in a consequential scientific
question. This explains more of the record than insufficient branch count.
Sometimes the obstacle is a mature conventional algorithm; sometimes it is a
missing physical operation, inadequate observations, or an inappropriate error
definition. More reasoning on the surrogate does not remove the original obstacle.

E. Continue, redirect, or rebuild?
Redirect. The evidence does not justify discarding the mathematical constructions,
scientific honesty, or tools already developed. It also does not justify another
six-branch batch under the same allocation logic. Keep sustained scientific
ownership; use conventional methods freely; commission new methods only around
a demonstrated scientific limitation. Foundational mathematics retains independent
value and need not acquire an immediate technological customer.

F. Single most important next action?
Use one principal investigator for a bounded experimental-evidence question:
can the observed RNA-perturbation interaction contrasts survive genuinely different
guide constructions and ordinary potency/noise calibration? The actual data and
primary design sources are included. This is a test of whether Astra can obtain
usable intervention evidence, not a new prediction flagship. Other newly available
branches remain in reserve; hidden-entropy, N33, N557 and IQM stay with their owners.

2. SCIENTIFIC TRAJECTORY

2.1 Earlier physical campaigns: increasingly complete accounting, no new engine

The TPV angular proposal had less than 0.1 percentage point ideal gain in its
tested model. The approximately 18% two-cell gain was attainable with ordinary
dichroic splitting. Neither supplied a new capability. The heat-to-work campaign
progressed to a complete conditional regenerative cycle: 18.68 W/m² at 0.505%
efficiency versus 0.374% for its thermoelectric comparator at matched power.
But its inactive liquid connection conducted about 6.596 million W/m²K against
an allowance near 4 W/m²K after assumed 99.9% recovery each way. The ideal selector
was not a physical heat path. These are scoped failures of constructions, not a
universal thermodynamic exclusion. The retired variants remain retired. [E5]

The desalination work developed conservation checks, numerical refinements and
electrical-work accounting. Constant-voltage current improvement could not lower
work per transferred charge after reset. The proposed diaphragm had no mechanical
port in the model. Variable voltage requires displacement work, including a
generally nonzero closed-cycle integral. No matched full desalination advantage
was established. The campaign was correctly parked. [E5]

These campaigns improved whole-operation reasoning. They also reveal a recurrent
failure: advantageous response parameters were initially treated independently
although their physical origins tied them together. Once construction, leakage,
drive, reset and comparator conditions were linked, the supposed headroom vanished.
More independent idealized component searches would not have resolved that.

2.2 Foundational and quantum work: real cumulative content

R1 developed from recurrence into stronger fixed-device/all-horizon statements
and a finite exact-Bell-output resource tradeoff. The internally reconstructed law
is, for fixed J and delta tending to zero,
  E_R,min(J,delta) = J ln(2)/(2 delta) - ln(1/delta) + O_J(1),
where delta is half the trace distance on the complete retained memory.
The mixed reservoir explains why a simpler linear inequality fails. Exact Bell
outputs, hidden-memory return, normal trace-class memories and noisy outputs must
not be conflated. The logarithmic saving is in intrinsic relative entropy of
entanglement; it is not automatically a saving in preparing the reservoir.
Original-owner source recovery remained incomplete; a reconstruction is not
authentication of the missing archive. Historical novelty and physical usefulness
remain separate from internal proof checks. B3 correctly closed that assignment
without commissioning nearby additive-constant work. [E5,E6]

The earlier hidden-state entropy proof explicitly reuses the N512 path-affinity
lemma to turn a two-lag reversible-compression obstruction into an entropy floor.
That is an actual mathematical dependency across investigations. Its old rational
example has a tiny certified bound; it should not stand in for the much stronger
current hidden-entropy programme. [E6]

The current producer-reported Round 9 result concerns unrestricted finite hidden
realizations of a specified alternating-renewal law. Its quartic asymptotic
coefficient is C*=(97+216 sqrt(3))/484. It describes restrictions on every optimal
family, including diverging state count and activity, and a limiting exit-hazard
profile. Fixed-q value and finite attainment remain open. The full current proof
archive was not recovered here, so these are accurately attributed internal
results rather than independently reproduced theorems. They are potentially
substantial, and they contradict the claim that Astra has merely accumulated
unrelated elementary exclusions. [E6]

N33's newer construction turns deterministic affine drift barriers into softmin
functions and controlled stochastic entrance and exponentially long finite
residence. It treats adaptive bounded rates under specified hypotheses. This is
not infinite-time stochastic permanence; the general constants remain
non-effective and complete finite-volume constants are available only for the
one-species example. Its inherited deterministic dependency and an external
overlapping permanence claim limit priority assertions. The current stochastic
construction is a meaningful continuation in its own right. [E6]

N557 transfers a passive entropy interpolation method to an amplifier with a
creation port, including a substantive test-space invariance repair. The proposed
photon-number inequality has potentially broad implications if correct. The
latest October 21 audit still calls it an internally reviewed candidate. Finite
Fock checks and repeated same-model reviews do not close every analytic domain
and limit issue. No exact channel capacity or practical amplifier follows merely
from the candidate inequality. Preserve the owner; do not upgrade its status by
editorial polish or replace its objective with an easier neighbouring lemma. [E6]

IQM's current offline record separates a flag/coherence response from a matched
logical information advantage. No new hardware allocation or expenditure was
performed. Populations alone do not determine a qutrit state. The contingent shot
protocol is not device evidence. Its ownership and reserved credits remain intact.
[E6]

2.3 Gaussian and molecular capability campaigns: interface and resource failures

The Gaussian mathematical tool did not improve the admitted anMC recipient under
its actual information. Ordinary known-parameter regression addressed the relevant
Gaussian calculation, while the expensive part was truncated. This is a transfer
failure, not a refutation of the theorem. [E5]

The B2 molecular target was a rigorous 0.001-Hartree difference interval for two
245,025-dimensional finite Hamiltonian sectors. Combined conventional/local
certificates reached width 0.05181136 Ha, against a proved fixed-family floor
0.05089241 Ha. Further optimization inside that family could improve width only
about 1.77%. Ultra's full-sector unitary comparison and shared-reference attempts
also failed within their stated families. Numerical FCI energies were much cheaper
but were not rigorous lower certificates and cannot be substituted for the same
guarantee. [E5]

The programme's strategic error was not that the constructors failed to optimize
hard enough. The evaluator selected a difficult formal guarantee without first
establishing a consequential scientific decision blocked by that guarantee.
These sources justify closing the tested families, not excluding useful molecular
computation generally. B3 Pro 2's closure therefore fulfilled its assignment.

2.4 B3 Ultra: genuine exact operation, stronger ordinary algorithm wins

For actual iJO1366, with 2,583 reactions and 1,805 metabolites, the final operation
certifies 878 blocked reactions aerobically and 933 after closing oxygen import.
It preserves literal rational coefficients and original finite bounds. Rational
potentials, row operations and a feasible witness prove an exact inverse between
projection onto retained coordinates and zero extension. Every retained objective
and added retained-coordinate constraint is preserved, not just a chosen growth
objective. [E2]

The complete exact replay passed independently at the receiving end: four actual
certificates and nine hostile cases. The construction is not complete for every
bounded polytope merely because its checker retains finite bounds. A small
counterexample in the audit demonstrates that scope without invalidating iJO1366.
Tiny witness fluxes prove mathematical nonzero activity, not biological function.

The matched final comparator changes the outcome. QFCA/SWIFTCC already uses
potential/nullspace structure and a single support-selection LP. With the same
exact finishing requirement, candidate medians were 0.970 versus 0.626 seconds
aerobically and 0.923 versus 0.596 seconds with oxygen import closed: about 1.55
times slower. These are producer same-machine comparisons, not our replay
timings. Lower candidate memory was not a demonstrated scientific bottleneck.
Adopt the stronger ordinary method and keep the exact artifact; do not invest
in rescuing the bespoke speed claim. [E2,P1,P2]

2.5 B3 Pro 3: a real physical correction, no accepted halo conclusion

The external pISO implementation supplied a velocity-squared shape to a wrapper
requiring enclosed mass. With x=r/rc, the required shape is x-atan(x), whereas
the code supplied 1-atan(x)/x. The resulting density behaves as r^-1 centrally
and r^-4 at large radius, rather than the intended core and r^-2 outer profile.
Normalization at the outer radius hides the discrepancy. This is a substantive
physical/code correction, independently checked through integration and asymptotics.
It is not just another numerical exclusion. [E3,P3]

Correcting the model changed evidence scores on actual galaxy data. Yet the
frozen validation did not accept a core/cusp conclusion: NGC5585 failed predictive
adequacy for both families; NGC3109 contributed more than the whole positive
aggregate; alternative integration estimates did not robustly settle the threshold.
Correlated residual modelling also removed the dramatic development-galaxy result.
Three deterministic eligible galaxies do not support a population conclusion.
[E3]

The independent audit checked the density relation, Gaussian conditioning,
held-out-response separation and stored decisions. It did not rerun all 42 fits.
The recorded 1558.51 worker CPU seconds and 6.36 million likelihood calls omit
research and integration time. Further integration may refine numerical evidence;
it cannot by itself resolve physical inadequacy or noncircular-motion ambiguity.
The other empirical candidate failed metadata admission and was not silently
replaced by a third convenient example. [E3]

2.6 B3 Max/Dots and closures: reliability transfer, asynchronous integration gap

Dots finally replayed actual B2 Max contractions and found that a redundant
endpoint summary could be modified to imply a false zero-width answer. The
demonstrated exploit concerned the composition component; the complete tampered
pipeline was not run. Actual untampered endpoints remained consistent. Dots'
corrected binding operation was consumed unchanged by Max. A fresh root replay
here rejected zero-width endpoints, altered inputs and a wrong archive. [E4]

This is a real improvement in a working pipeline's reliability. It is not a new
molecular certificate, a repaired quantum theorem, or an independent-domain
research speedup. Max and Dots also obeyed the one-time check/stop instruction
before the later B3 producer artifacts became available. Their lack of a later
check is partly an allocation-design error: the evaluator scheduled consumers
before a usable product and then told them not to poll. Do not label this agent
failure or repeat it with another waiting branch.

3. NEW TEST: DOES THE B3 TOOL IMPROVE A DIFFERENT CALCULATION?

The principal preregistered 72 distinct production-envelope queries: four products
at nine growth thresholds in each of two regimes. Three alternating repeats
compared full and QFCA-certified reduced models using the same HiGHS dual-simplex
solver, tolerances, original bounds and constraints. All 72 queries matched in
status and optimum across the repeats; infeasible points were retained. Maximum
objective discrepancy was 1.40e-11, maximum balance residual 3.15e-11. [E7]

Median times for 36-query workloads on the receiving machine:
                       Full solve   Reduced solve   Reduced + once-only check
 Aerobic                  2.130 s       1.951 s             2.258 s
 Oxygen import closed     2.298 s       2.022 s             2.469 s

The original certificate was checked once per regime, not per LP. Parsing and
full-matrix building were tiny common costs. Producer construction and research
costs would be additional. Total trial execution was 31.68 seconds, about 110 MiB
peak RSS. Timings varied; native warm-start workflows were not tested. This is
not a universal performance lower bound or a best-possible production baseline.

The result establishes successful semantic transfer and modest solver savings,
but no net end-to-end acceleration for the tested receiving workload. More reuse
could amortize verification. That possibility does not establish a consequential
unmet workload, and both candidate and ordinary method can reuse the same
reduction. A future gain would not retroactively belong to the losing bespoke
algorithm. This trial changes the allocation decision: preserve the asset, stop
the dedicated speed campaign, and do not build a platform around it.

4. STRATEGIC DIAGNOSIS AND COUNTEREXPLANATIONS

The strongest supported diagnosis is a mismatch between tractable operations and
binding scientific limitations. It appears in several distinct forms:

* Physical admissibility: thermal switching and reset defeated an ideal response.
* Information interface: the Gaussian recipient already possessed the supposedly
  expensive information.
* Certificate class: the molecular lower-bound family could not reach its target.
* Conventional frontier: QFCA removed the apparent metabolic computation gap.
* Measurement model: corrected galaxy relative evidence did not imply adequacy.

This is not one universal failure mode. A better comparator cannot supply missing
experimental evidence; a rigorous physical construction cannot prove mathematical
priority; more validation cannot turn a subsecond routine into a major bottleneck.
The intervention must match the particular obstruction.

Competing explanations were tested against counterexamples:

1. 'Astra cannot construct; it only criticizes.' Contradicted by R1 reservoirs,
   exact reductions, N33's stochastic construction and current hidden-entropy
   asymptotics. Construction quality is not the sole limitation.
2. 'There is no accumulation.' Contradicted by explicit mathematical dependencies
   and Dots-to-Max consumption. However, a repeated costed improvement in general
   discovery capability has not been demonstrated.
3. 'More parallelism would fix it.' No evidence. Several obstacles are missing
   information or invalid premises. Independent branches are useful for distinct
   constructions or adversarial checks; redundant simultaneous attacks consume
   integration effort and can share the same wrong representation.
4. 'Every failure means wrong problem selection.' Too strong. Consequential
   research can fail. N557 may be worth pursuing despite difficult verification.
   The concern is repeated investment before the opportunity/comparator interface
   is known, and continuing after a scoped obstruction already answers the decision.
5. 'Demand-first B3 solved selection.' Partly contradicted. It improved realism
   and produced an external physical correction, but a real scientific use is not
   necessarily a consequential unmet bottleneck. One heterogeneous cycle is not
   a controlled causal test of the strategy.
6. 'Better storage and prompts are the central solution.' Original source access
   did improve. Current binding issues remain scientific. A new dashboard would
   not establish a valid intervention, prove a hard analytic step, or supply a
   useful comparator gap.

Removable limitations include late comparator discovery, premature dependent
assignments, missing source delivery, repeated assumption mistakes, and stopping
without preserving reusable evidence. More difficult limitations include scarce
independent experiments, identifiability, correlated same-model proof review,
finite computation and possible reasoning limitations on hard conceptual problems.
The available histories do not isolate model capability from strategy, so this
review cannot guarantee that redirection alone yields transformative discoveries.

5. SERIOUS ALTERNATIVES

Concentrated foundational mathematics is the strongest serious alternative, and
already the best evidence of cumulative content. Important theorems need no
immediate runtime beneficiary. Protect current owners and allow sustained attacks
on consequential unresolved steps. Do not open duplicate targets from stale
repository indexes or turn every nearby bound into another branch. Additional
concentration becomes preferable when a current owner supplies a distinct
load-bearing problem with full assumptions, inherited lemmas and a real gain
from another constructor. [E6]

Automated code/algorithm evolution is a credible amplification mechanism. The
primary AlphaEvolve report describes evaluator-driven program changes and actual
computational deployments, including improvements to an underlying training
operation. Astra currently lacks an admitted comparable primitive with a faithful
evaluator, consequential measured headroom and independent receiving applications.
The observed local environment has 8 CPU cores of quota and 8 GiB RAM, no exposed
GPU or established free large-scale proposal endpoint. Small searches remain
possible. Installing a framework would not create the missing scientific demand.
Retain the method; do not select it as a flagship now. [E9,P4]

Instrument-aware halo inference could address the actual empirical blockage.
Public spectral cubes and established 3D-Barolo methods exist. But no calibrated
raw-cube interface for an affected validation galaxy was admitted here, and even
cube modelling can confuse noncircular motion with halo structure. More reduced
curve fitting would not test the proposed change. Preserve the correction and
stop this campaign rather than assign a speculative cube project. [E3,P5,P6]

Physical-energy invention remains important, but the accessible record supplies
no surviving physical selector/regenerator operation with qualified losses and
an improved complete-engine comparison. No reason was found to reopen retired
variants. Likewise, a new molecular method requires a consequential receiving
need and a genuinely different certificate operation, not another attempt at a
settled class.

Experimental biology offers direct interventions and free primary measurements.
It is attractive because observations can change the model, not because biology
is intrinsically an easier benchmark. The initial premise that simple additive
models dominate the frontier was challenged by an October 2026 paper using
calibrated comparisons and PRESAGE. A convenient Norman pseudobulk also proved
singles-only. Those findings prevented an unjustified predictor assignment.
[E8,P7,P8]

6. NEW TEST: WHAT DOES THE AVAILABLE PERTURBATION EVIDENCE SUPPORT?

The complete WesselsSatija2023 input was acquired: 219,393,529 bytes, publisher MD5
matched; 30,707 THP-1 cells, 21,052 genes, 28 single-target conditions, 158 pair
labels and one control. All pairs have both single-target conditions. Four 10X
lanes and eight HTO labels were inspected in the actual input. Primary
Supplementary Table 7 maps all eight HTOs to THP1 subpools, not independent culture
replicates. The reporting summary describes alternative guides as technical
replication. Separate FACS replication elsewhere in the paper cannot be assigned
to every RNA pair. [E8,P7]

Before expression inspection, the principal froze an all-gene log-normalized
contrast, a random seed, independent partition of all four arms, and a SHA-based
69-development/89-test pair split. For pair ab and cell half h,
  r_ab,h = mean_ab,h - mean_a,h - mean_b,h + mean_control,h.

On the fixed split the median cross-half residual cosine was 0.1973. Deliberately
reusing pooled single/control estimates in both halves increased it to 0.8048.
All 158 cross-products were positive in the independent-arm calculation, but
there is no uncertainty interval or independent biological replication. Shared
single/control observations also make different pair estimates dependent.
The correct conclusion is positive cross-half contrast on this particular
transformed scale, not statistically established epistasis. [E8]

The inflation has a direct explanation. With independent arms, the expectation
of the residual cross-product estimates squared underlying contrast under an
ideal independent cell-sampling model. If estimated single/control means are
shared, their estimation variance is added to that expectation. This is a general
statistical fact, not a new theorem; the actual assay demonstrates its relevance
to Astra's opportunity assessment.

An ordinary global shrinkage coefficient, fitted only on development pairs in
one half, reduced held-out opposite-half MSE from 0.008293 to 0.003554 in one
direction and 0.008017 to 0.003442 in the other. This is not a comparison with the
current best predictor. It shows why a raw additive baseline leaves misleading
headroom. The successful calculation used 32.52 seconds and about 527 MiB peak
RSS; an initial completed calculation failed at JSON serialization and its
additional cost is explicitly unmeasured. No favourable seeds or genes were
selected. [E8]

Decision: do not start a predictive-method flagship. One different source-complete
question remains worth a bounded investment: does a contrast survive distinct
guide sequences/array positions after ordinary potency and noise calibration?
This tests robustness to implementation of the intervention. It cannot establish
out-of-culture biological replication, and it may be non-identifiable in these
data. The next owner must determine that explicitly rather than substitute
lane/HTO halves. This is the recommended limited next experiment.

7. SCIENTIFIC INTELLIGENCE AMPLIFICATION

The strongest plausible mechanism is acquisition of a reusable scientific
operation inside a sustained investigation, followed by independent consumption
and then a further improvement enabled by that consumption. The system may
improve through algorithms, representations, exact lemmas or measurement models;
it need not alter the underlying language-model weights. Persistent files alone
are not the mechanism: later reasoning or computation must actually change.

Capability: answer a scientifically meaningful class of questions more reliably,
with stronger scope, less total resource, or access to previously inaccessible
observables. For the empirical candidate this would initially mean trustworthy
guide-distinct inference, not a virtual-cell model.

Bottleneck: an identifiable missing operation must be separated from absent
information. No algorithm can conjure independent experimental units or distinguish
observationally equivalent causal models without an extra premise.

Construction: use a faithful measurement/perturbation model, ordinary strong
baselines and uncertainty that includes shared controls. Prove what is identifiable
or show an explicit alternative explanation. Only build a new algorithm if a
specific operation remains inadequate. In mathematics, an exact structural lemma
can serve the same role if a second proof genuinely needs it.

Transfer: an independently owned investigation must use the result to obtain a
verified improvement. A second input regime inside one producer script or a
relocated ZIP does not by itself show this. The root metabolic trial is an actual
receiving calculation; it did not show a net resource improvement.

Verification: compare equal information, error/scope, hardware and required output.
Account for development, construction, checking, integration and repeated use.
If a guarantee rather than speed improves, specify why that guarantee changes
the scientific decision. Failed attempts count; producer and receiver timing
cannot be added across machines as a purported precise total.

Compounding: the transferred operation must enable a new investigation that then
improves the operation, creates a second reusable one, or enlarges the accessible
question class. A sequence of such observed dependencies would support recursive
improvement. One successful transfer, larger agent count or more stored knowledge
does not establish an exponential growth law. Costs can rise, gains can diminish
and physical evidence can remain the limiting factor.

Failure conditions: ordinary methods erase the benefit; integration exceeds gain;
the guarantee is irrelevant; the evaluator is exploitable; the recipient lacks
required information; gains disappear outside the producer's examples; or no
second-generation improvement is observed. Current evidence supports the
mechanism as a hypothesis, not an achieved recursive capability.

8. STRATEGIC DECISION AND RESOURCE DEPLOYMENT

Redirect the capability-building programme while preserving the foundational core.
Stop allocating topics to fill branches. A principal should keep a consequential
scientific question fixed through construction, conventional comparison and a
receiving use. A failed component can change the operation without forcing a new
domain. An exact exclusion should stop the excluded family; it should not trigger
another automatically generated neighbouring task.

Active new assignment: Sol Ultra owns the single cross-guide evidence experiment
specified in the accompanying source-complete packet. It includes a design
identifiability check and one frozen analysis, not an architecture sweep, new
dataset campaign, dashboard, or new theorem programme. The result may be a
scoped non-identifiability or insufficient-evidence finding.

Dots is the intended independent receiver once a finished executable operation
exists. Max is the intended integrator after a finished scientific return exists.
Neither is launched as an empty waiting job. Ultra retains responsibility for
complete source delivery and can obtain an independent check with available
subagents at the result checkpoint. If a direct authorised branch-delivery route
exists, it should use it; otherwise it preserves a complete ready receiver packet
and accurately reports delivery state. It assigns no relay work to the user.

No new assignment for Pro 1, Pro 2 or Pro 3. Existing owned work stays in place;
otherwise these branches are in reserve. Reserving them is a deliberate allocation
decision, not a request to start generic audits or independent opportunity searches.
No hidden-entropy, N33, N557 or IQM ownership is changed. No hardware credits,
purchases, outreach or retired physical/molecular campaign are authorized here.

Continue this new direction only if the completed evidence operation supplies a
defensible cross-guide conclusion, identifies the limits of interpretation, and
exposes a consequential unmet operation beyond ordinary calibration and current
compatible predictors. A positive residual alone does not meet that last condition.
If the design cannot distinguish interaction from potency/measurement effects,
preserve the scoped result and do not automatically search for another dataset.

Prefer greater foundational concentration if a protected owner has a source-complete
distinct construction with greater expected consequence, or if an important
candidate obtains independent reconstruction and opens a meaningful new boundary.
Prefer numerical synthesis if two actual receiving problems expose the same
consequential kernel, strong ordinary methods miss the requirement, and a faithful
evaluator exists. Prefer physical experimentation only when admissible observations
and real resource constraints support a discriminating free experiment.

These are conditions for revising the strategy, not promises of future work.
The immediate experiment, its inputs, source identity, scope, comparators and
stop conditions are concrete. It is deliberately smaller than the ambition:
the present evidence justifies learning whether a missing scientific capability
can be acquired, not declaring that Astra has found its route to revolutionary
or exponential progress.
