1. MATHEMATICAL DEFINITION
The computed diameter is
  max k.(p-q), subject to p,q>=0, sum p=sum q=1,
  E.p=E.q=mu, and |B(p-q)|<=2epsilon.
This correctly represents the largest separation between two populations that
could yield at least one common vector of observations with coordinate error
<=epsilon: the two observation intervals overlap iff their means differ by at
most2epsilon. It is worst case over possible observations, not a posterior bound
conditioned on a specified measurement, nor an expected prediction error.

The energy equality in the producer uses e=E/Emax with RHSmu/Emax. This is exactly
the same constraint as the original eV mean; it neither renormalizes the population
nor changes the supplied mean. The reviewer re-evaluated all560 saved primary
population pairs using original-eV equalities. Maximum mean residual was
1.0341e-13eV; normalization residual1.111e-14; feature-constraint excess2.80e-16.
All stored objectives/responses agreed on their saved floating scale.

R and M are linear source outputs. M is the sum of event rates times product-minus-
reactant mode excitation. It is not the conditional per-event ratio M/R, direct
bath heat, net chemistry, retained yield or system efficiency. The code's ground-
state subtraction follows the stated mode-excitation convention.

The normalized diameter divides by that response's mean-only width. It therefore
measures fraction of pre-existing worst-case ambiguity remaining, not relative
error in an actual gas state. About56.5% remaining does not mean56.5% rate error,
chemical inefficiency or a comparable physical percentage. Each temperature and
source-index variant has its own baseline.

3. NOISE AND INFORMATION GEOMETRY
At zero noise, powers2/3/4 and shifted Chebyshev2/3/4 have equal informational
span after the exact normalization/mean rows. Their matched diameters agree to
floating precision. That is a useful admission check.
At positive noise, independent absolute errors on each feature define DIFFERENT
uncertainty sets. The Chebyshev comparison is admissible only because its bounded
functions are treated as separately supplied observables under the explicit
abstract contract. It is not an information gain from numerically changing basis
on the same noisy moment readings. A real basis transformation would require
transporting the uncertainty set. No measured cost/noise equivalence is supplied.

A resolved molecular-level observation would reveal an entire categorical state;
then the same observations support all moments and bins. Counting three features
is therefore not counting samples, apparatus cost, measurement time or physical
information acquisition cost. The59-coordinate control appropriately shows a
stronger mathematical information boundary without a sample-efficiency claim.
Even at full-population positive-coordinate error, accumulation across coordinates
can leave nontrivial ambiguity; zero-noise output is numerically zero as required.

5. OMITTED PRIMARY SIGN TEST AND POST-PRIMARY INDEPENDENT RESULT
The preregistration requested checking opposite signs of compatible M. Primary
code/output contains maximum-diameter pairs but no distinct sign analysis. Such
pairs can both be negative even when some compatible other pair has opposite
signs. This omission is corrected by independent post-primary analysis, explicitly
attributed here; it is not part of the original locked primary result.

  p^(j)=(1-mu/Ej)e0+(mu/Ej)ej, j=1,...,59.

all positive-index coordinates and hence the zeroth coordinate. Consequently a

6. WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES FOR META-RESEARCH
The proposed adaptive threshold summary did not materially outperform the strong
prespecified polynomial control and lost clearly to the equally trained ordinary
moment selection. Retain the negative result; do not promote a new representation
layer on this evidence. The finite-table operation still provides a useful
scientific distinction: optimizing whole-response ambiguity and deciding one
predicate are different tasks. At the native source temperature and fixed mean,
the sign is already determined in the frozen table although magnitude is poorly
identified. At3000K the selected summaries fail even at that sign predicate.

This suggests testing *which question requires additional information* before
optimizing a general surrogate for uncertainty. It is an evidence-supported local
lesson, not a newly invented universal principle, a controlled agent-strategy
advantage or recursive intelligence improvement. The next physical question still
requires reverse flux, population dynamics, source compatibility and full reset;
none is supplied by a better compressed summary. No independent owner's receiving
use of this supplement has yet occurred.
