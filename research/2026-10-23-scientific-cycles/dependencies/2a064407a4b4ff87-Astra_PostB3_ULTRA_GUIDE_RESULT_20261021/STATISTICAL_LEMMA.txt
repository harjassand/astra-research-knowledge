1. Estimand and locally frozen information boundary
===================================================
Let f={A,B} index a design-eligible target family, c in {1,2} its two genuinely
sequence-distinct pair constructions, and g the supplied gene/readout. Let
P_fc, S_Afc, S_Bfc and C_fc denote arm means of the predeclared cell representation.
A singleton must contain the SAME targeting sequence in the SAME array slot as
that target in P_fc; it must replace the other targeting sequence by the matching
NT sequence/slot. Controls should match physical array size/NT construction.

For the supplied starting representation, z_ig=log(1+10000*x_ig/L_i), where L_i
uses exactly the predeclared count layer and gene set. The descriptive interaction
is r_fc=P_fc-S_Afc-S_Bfc+C_fc. It is a contrast of this representation, not a
representation-independent definition of biological epistasis.

Each required arm must pass the outcome-independent n>=5 computational admission.
Freeze target order, the two constructions, slots/sequences, arm membership,
exclusions, all-gene/readout mask, normalization, primary score and sensitivity
before computing pair outcomes. Pooled outcomes were inspected in earlier root
work: this is a local freeze, not historically untouched data.

Direction 1->2: fit ordinary calibration on construction-1 pair responses only.
Direction 2->1: use construction-2 pair responses only. All corresponding singleton
and control data may be supplied equally to every method. In each direction the
counterpart PAIR response, including its own-target-gene expression, is forbidden
for coefficient estimation, hyperparameter selection, readout selection, exclusion,
or choosing a favorable direction. Both directional protocols are frozen before
either directional fit. Reusing a pair as training in the reverse direction does
not invalidate this declared design; the two reported directions are dependent.

2. Ordinary calibration must receive the source pair information too
====================================================================
The correct competitor to an interaction-vector transfer is not merely raw
additivity. A conventional source-only potency calibration is both strong and
cheap. Write s_Ac=S_Afc-C_fc, s_Bc=S_Bfc-C_fc and t_c=P_fc-C_fc.

For each source family, fit two scalar weights using ALL frozen genes:
  lambda_f = argmin_l ||t_source-[s_Asource,s_Bsource] l||^2/G
                         + rho_f ||l-(1,1)||^2.
Predict counterpart pair mean as
  m_ordinary,target=C_target+lambda_A*s_Atarget+lambda_B*s_Btarget.
The same weights are applied to target identities A and B, not blindly to array
slot order. They model target-specific attenuation/amplification in a dual-target
array that transfers across its alternate guide construction. This uses the
source pair response, and it never fits the pair response called the prediction.
It therefore does not violate the ban on fitting per-pair parameters to held-out
responses. It is a deliberately low-dimensional conventional nuisance model.

Choose rho on source data only, or predeclare it from singleton/count design. If
multiple penalties are compared, freeze a small grid and a source-only selection
rule. A nonnegative/bounded fit may be a declared sensitivity; signed weights are
linear calibration rather than necessarily physical knockdown efficacy. Report
rank and condition of the two-column singleton matrix. Ridge makes a solution
unique but does not make the two potencies individually identifiable when the
columns are collinear, weak, or noisy.

Raw additive prediction C+s_A+s_B and ordinary ridge prediction both remain
reported. A source-residual transfer candidate, if requested, can be frozen as
  e_source=t_source-[s_Asource,s_Bsource] lambda_f,
  m_transfer,target=m_ordinary,target+eta*e_source.
eta=1 is a permissible frozen descriptive transfer. Any attenuation eta must be
predeclared or learned from source-only information, never from counterpart pair
responses. No new predictor architecture or trained deep model is required.

A complementary ordinary linear main-effect calibration across families is
  R_source = X B + noise,
where X has one column per target, two 1s per family and R contains matched raw
contrasts. Fit ridge B on source families; predict R_target by X B. This permits
systematic target-specific main-effect miscalibration shared across guide sets.
It has no per-family free response vector. Source-only family CV may choose its
penalty. A projected OLS nuisance-removal diagnostic is possible when rank permits.
This is an optional strengthening, not authority to run a model sweep. Choose
which ordinary model is the declared primary before target pair outcomes.

Equal information means BOTH the ordinary comparator and any transfer receive
source pair responses, source and target singles, controls, and frozen metadata.
A rich transfer must not be credited against a baseline deprived of the source
pair response it uses.

3. Sampling noise and shared references: exact covariance identity
=================================================================
Let a index unique, disjoint cell arms and M_a their sample means. Assume for this
conditional calculation that cells are independent draws within each arm, arms
have independent sampling, finite covariance V_a=Cov(M_a), and arm membership is
fixed. Let l_ja be any fixed contrast/prediction coefficient and R_j=sum_a l_ja M_a.
Then, as full gene-by-gene covariance matrices,
  Cov(R_j,R_k)=sum_a l_ja*l_ka*V_a.
For vectors over the frozen G genes,
  E[R_j'R_k]=r_j'r_k+sum_a l_ja*l_ka*tr(V_a).
Estimate V_a by empirical within-arm covariance/n_a; its trace is the sum of
within-arm per-gene sample variances divided by n_a. Full G-by-G matrices need
not be materialized for this trace correction.

If a family has distinct pair and singleton arms across constructions but the
same control C, its raw cross-guide product has positive sampling contribution
  E[r_hat_f1'r_hat_f2]-r_f1'r_f2=tr(V_C).
Thus a positive raw cross-guide cosine/product is not itself calibrated evidence.
If singles also overlap, include them with their exact coefficient products.
Across families the same singleton/control arm appears repeatedly, so families
are not independent observations. A larger number of families does not create
additional controls or cultures.

Two valid implementations:
(a) Construct a contrast coefficient matrix L from actual arm IDs and propagate
    V using the identity above. Correct a primary raw cross-product by subtracting
    the shared-arm trace term. For fixed linear fitted nuisance maps, incorporate
    those maps into L as well.
(b) Perform one global arm bootstrap per draw: resample each unique arm's cells
    ONCE, recompute that arm mean, reuse it everywhere that arm occurs, refit each
    source calibration/its source-only tuning, and recompute BOTH directions.
    This preserves shared-arm dependence and coefficient uncertainty. Never
    resample each pair's copies of a shared singleton/control independently, and
    never treat genes as independent replicates.

For nonlinear fitted weights or a selected penalty, a trace correction that
pretends the fitted coefficient is fixed is not exact. Refit within the bootstrap
or use a predeclared conditional null simulation. A conventional uncentered
bootstrap propagates uncertainty, but does not by itself remove finite-sample
shared-reference bias from a nonlinear cross-product. If that bias matters, use
explicit correction or a null-centered bootstrap that reproduces the entire
shared-reference pipeline.

A predeclared split into disjoint reference cells can remove the within-family
shared-reference contribution, if counts support it. It spends precision and is
not biological replication. It must not be optimized for agreement or represented
as repeating the earlier closed diagnostic.

4. A concrete test/comparison with honest directional uncertainty
=================================================================
Use one omnibus all-family/all-gene score to avoid selecting favorable pairs:
  Delta_direction = mean_f,g[(P_target-m_ordinary)^2
                             -(P_target-m_transfer)^2].
Positive values mean the predeclared transfer predicts counterpart pair responses
better than the equally informed ordinary calibration on the stated scale.
Report both directions and their average with the same joint arm resampling.
A prespecified descriptive report may also give the corrected cross-guide
interaction inner product/cosine distribution without per-pair discovery claims.

Prediction improvement alone is not a proof of interaction. In particular,
shared noisy singleton/control estimates can make a source interaction estimate
repair a noisy target additive reference. A nuisance-null analysis must reproduce
that advantage. For a fixed eta and source residual e, the score identity is
  Delta=2*eta*(P_target-m_ordinary)'e-eta^2*||e||^2.
Its sampling expectation includes the shared-covariance term
  2*eta*tr Cov(P_target-m_ordinary,e).
This contribution cannot be dismissed merely because pair cells are distinct.
The -eta^2 Var(e) term is the real variance cost of transferring a noisy source.

For a source-only ordinary null simulation, keep actual arm counts/membership,
use pair expectations from the fitted ordinary nuisance model, draw/resample
centered cell residuals under those expectations, preserve singleton/control
sharing, refit source calibrations, and score the target without using its mean
to set the nuisance prediction. Target pair residual distributions/covariances
may support uncertainty estimation; they may not choose prediction parameters.
State the distributional/conditional nature of this bootstrap and any failure
of a null-centered count model to be feasible. A target response cannot be fitted
under a saturated null and then called out of sample.

If the claim requires directional improvement in BOTH directions, use a
prespecified conjunction: both lower bounds positive, or an intersection-union
p-value max(p_1to2,p_2to1). Dependence between directions does not invalidate that
conjunction rule. If only one predeclared omnibus statistic is tested, there is
one primary test. Per-family or per-gene inferential claims require a frozen
family definition and multiplicity correction (for example Holm over families).
Report all admitted families and failures, not only positive estimates.

All intervals/p-values are conditional on this assay, its arm sampling assumptions,
measured cells and source processing. Unknown pre-hashing dependence, shared
culture/transduction and lane effects are not estimated biological variance.
Absence of significance is not evidence of zero interaction.

5. Measurement error in potency regression is observable, not ignorable
=======================================================================
Because s_A=S_A-C, s_B=S_B-C and t=P-C share C, the source least-squares Gram and
right-hand side have noise contributions. For independent pair/single arms:
  E[S_hat'S_hat]-S'S = [[tr(V_A+V_C), tr(V_C)],
                         [tr(V_C), tr(V_B+V_C)]],
  E[S_hat't_hat]-S't = [tr(V_C),tr(V_C)]'.
An errors-in-variables moment sensitivity subtracts these terms before a frozen
ridge/PSD regularization. With tiny counts the corrected Gram may be indefinite;
that is a failure/weak-identification signal, not permission to invert it silently.
A global arm bootstrap refitting the chosen estimator gives conditional sampling
uncertainty; it does not magically identify a guide's true knockdown dose.

6. Exact identifiability tests and explicit competing constructions
==================================================================
6a. Guide sequence versus array position.
If guide1 for each target appears only in slot1 and guide2 only in slot2, guide
identity and position are aliased. With an additive parameterization u_q+v_slot,
adding h_slot to every u_q in that slot and subtracting it from v_slot leaves all
observed means unchanged. A same-sequence slot swap or another crossing is needed
to identify intrinsic sequence versus position effects separately. A matching
singleton contrast can still cancel their COMBINED guide-by-slot main effect.
This failure is not grounds to skip the admissible matched contrast analysis.

6b. Missing mixed guide combinations and knockdown dose.
With only (A1,B2) and (B1,A2), no measurements of (A1,B1)/(A2,B2), same-sequence
slot swaps or dose series can be presumed. Singleton outcomes identify the
implemented singleton response, not the dose achieved when two targeting guides
share an array. Guide-specific target transcript reduction in the held-out PAIR
would itself be a held-out response and cannot tune its prediction.

6c. A restrictive, falsifiable potency explanation.
Let singleton response vectors be s_A1,s_A2,s_B1,s_B2. A shared source-to-counterpart
array-potency null for family f requires scalars lambda_A,lambda_B such that
  [t_1;t_2] = [[s_A1,s_B1],[s_A2,s_B2]] [lambda_A;lambda_B].
This is a two-column rank/span condition in 2G dimensions. It is testable rather
than an arbitrary confounder. Fit its coefficients on SOURCE only, predict the
counterpart and carry sampling covariance. If the source columns are collinear,
individual lambda values are unidentifiable. Counterpart prediction remains
identifiable precisely when every source-null vector is also target-null:
  null(S_source) subseteq null(S_target),
equivalently rank([S_source;S_target])=rank(S_source).
Otherwise two equally fitting source calibrations predict different counterpart
responses. Ridge chooses a convention; it does not supply the absent information.
Use an appropriate numerical tolerance and noise uncertainty, not exact floating
point rank alone.

Explicit ambiguity example: suppose s_A1=s_A2=u and s_B1=s_B2=v and both pair
responses are t_1=t_2=(u+v)/2. Model I has unchanged singleton efficacy in pairs
and a biological interaction theta=-(u+v)/2. Model II has zero biological
interaction and each targeting guide's effect halved in a dual-target array.
They give identical pair and singleton observations and a perfectly repeating
nonzero raw r. The source-only two-scalar ordinary calibration above can explain
this example exactly. Therefore raw cross-guide repetition is insufficient.
A signal outside the fitted singleton span can reject this restrictive linear
potency model, subject to sampling/normalization; it does not identify arbitrary
unmeasured dose-response behavior.

6d. General design-matrix test for the final mechanistic distinction.
Let M encode identified main effects, N the SPECIFIED implementation nuisance,
and Z the candidate biological interaction on all observed arms. A contrast of
interaction coefficients is identifiable only to the extent that its columns
remain outside col([M,N]); a full coefficient block requires
  rank([M,N,Z])-rank([M,N])=number of interaction coefficients.
A shared guide-pair implementation effect that occurs only in each family's
pair arrays has the same incidence column as a shared biological pair effect.
For that nuisance, N contains Z and the rank increment is zero: only their sum
is identified. Explicitly, theta_f -> theta_f+d_f and kappa_f -> kappa_f-d_f
leaves every observed arm mean/likelihood unchanged. This is a precise remaining
mechanistic ambiguity, not an excuse to omit the source-only falsifiable
potency comparison or the descriptive cross-guide analysis.

The source can support a result such as "cross-guide descriptive interaction
survives the declared ordinary linear guide/array-potency and sampling model."
Without the missing crossings/doses/interventions, it cannot establish that all
pair-specific implementation effects have been separated from biological
interaction. Unknown culture independence is a further, separate limitation.

7. Count-scale sensitivity required for an interaction claim
===========================================================
The predeclared log-normalized contrast is vulnerable to compositional/global
RNA shifts and the nonlinearity of log. Run one conventional count-scale
sensitivity with library-size offsets on the SAME admitted cells/arms/genes and
the SAME directional information boundary. For example,
  x_ig ~ Poisson or NB(mean=L_i*exp(alpha_g+guide main effects+declared terms)).
The ordinary model must have no target-pair fitted parameter. Offset likelihood
or count-scale residual agreement is a sensitivity, not a validation of total-RNA
invariance. If using a linear count-rate contrast rather than a likelihood,
name it correctly; it is not automatically an NB/Poisson interaction test.
Overdispersion, structural zeros, dispersion sharing and count-model failures
must be reported. The count-link notion of additivity differs from additive
log1p normalized means; disagreement can reveal scale dependence without proving
which scale is causal. A result only on log-normalized means remains that scoped
descriptive finding.

Recommended fixed nuisance contrast using all four singleton signatures
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
A useful stringent, pair-response-blind nuisance removal is, for each family,
  S_f=[s_A1,s_B2,s_A2,s_B1] (G by4),
  Q_f=I-U_f U_f',
where U_f is a numerically stable orthonormal basis of the FULL empirical column
span of S_f, constructed from singles/control only. Use one frozen numerical SVD
rank tolerance; do not select rank/genes to improve pair agreement. It is not
necessary to form a G-by-G Q matrix: apply v -> v-U(U'v).

For either construction, Q_f annihilates every arbitrary scalar combination of
ALL FOUR observed singleton signatures, stronger than removing only its own two:
  Q_f r_fc = Q_f(P_fc-C),
and Q_f applied to a source-only two-scalar potency-baseline residual gives the
same vector. Thus use the source-only two-scalar ridge efficacy model for the two
directional predictive comparisons, and this all-four-single span for the one
symmetric residual-agreement diagnostic. No held-out pair response sets Q or the
potency model. This directly answers the principal's proposed contrast: YES, it
is admissible and tests residual structure outside that empirical linear-potency
span, with the sampling qualification below.

Do not call Q an identified biological potency model. Its inputs are estimated
single responses, and it can remove a true interaction aligned with singleton
signatures. A null projected score is not evidence of zero total interaction.
Since the same Q is used for both constructions, (Q r1)'(Q r2) is ONE symmetric
statistic, not two independent directional confirmations. The ordinary-prediction
MSE gains 1->2 and2->1 remain two distinct dependent directional comparisons.

Exact versus plug-in shared-control corrections
------------------------------------------------
For raw contrasts, distinct within-family pair/single arms and shared C give
  T_raw,f=r_hat_f1'r_hat_f2 - tr(SampleCov_C)/7.
This is an EXACT unbiased estimator of the true raw contrast inner product under
the stated iid arm-sampling assumptions. Equivalently replace Cbar'Cbar by the
control U-statistic sum_{i!=j} z_Ci'z_Cj/[7*6]. The 42 ordered terms are dependent;
they are not 42 controls. Across families the SAME correction/control is reused.

For a deterministic or independently estimated symmetric idempotent projector Q,
  T_Q,f=(Q r_hat_f1)'(Q r_hat_f2)-tr(Q SampleCov_C Q)/7
is likewise unbiased, and its correction is cheaply
  sum_i ||Q(z_Ci-Cbar)||^2/[7*6].

CRITICAL: the proposed Q is built from the SAME noisy singles/control that enter
the contrasts. Freezing it before pair responses prevents leakage but does NOT
make it statistically independent of those references. Therefore the displayed
projected subtraction is ONLY a plug-in/first-order correction for that Q. It is
not an exact unbiasedness theorem. The raw correction remains exact. Extra
projection-estimation noise/bias must be propagated by re-estimating Q in the
full arm resampling/null procedure and named as conditional/approximate.

Concrete global arm multiplier/null procedure (if computationally preferable)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
For each unique arm a with cell vectors z_ai, draw independent mean-zero,
variance-one multipliers xi_ai, using the SAME multiplier across all genes of
a cell. Define
  delta_a = sum_i xi_ai*(z_ai-zbar_a)/sqrt(n_a*(n_a-1)).
Its conditional covariance is exactly SampleCov_a/n_a. Reuse this ONE delta_a
for every family/direction that uses arm a. In particular the seven control
multipliers are common to all105 families. Recompute Q, source-only potency
weights and both score directions on every draw.

For propagated sampling intervals use M_a*=M_a+delta_a and the frozen pipeline.
For an ordinary-null-centered calibration, set single/control null means to their
observed means, and set BOTH pair-construction null means for a family from its
SOURCE-fitted two-scalar efficacy weights applied to the appropriate singles.
Then use M_a*=mu_a,null+delta_a. Refit on source only in every draw. The observed
target pair mean does not set its null expectation; its centered residual
covariance may estimate sampling variation. Carry out the separately specified
null calibration for each direction if their source-fitted weights differ.

A normal multiplier here is a plug-in Gaussian approximation to sampling means;
a Rademacher choice is a different frozen approximation. Neither is exact at
n_control=7 or supplies absent culture variance. The low-rank empirical control
covariance (at most6) cannot estimate unseen control directions or tail behavior.
Report the number/seed/type of draws and Monte Carlo precision. Do not turn a
small Monte Carlo p-value into precision unsupported by seven controls.

A helpful bounded fragility report is the seven leave-one-control-cell-out
estimates (six controls each), with Q/potency models refitted. Report their full
range/signs without choosing favorable omissions. This is a diagnostic of the
same sampling limitation, not independent confirmation or a new model sweep.

Final recommended evidential phrasing
--------------------------------------
If positive: "A prespecified all-gene residual outside the empirical span of all
four matched singleton signatures repeated across physically distinct guides,
relative to the declared ordinary source-only potency model, under this assay's
sampling/normalization assumptions. Inference is approximate because Q and the
matched control are estimated, with only seven control cells. Pair-specific
implementation versus biological mechanism remains unidentifiable."

conditioning. Do not infer that all interaction is absent, since span projection
removes aligned effects and low-n control uncertainty can dominate all families.
