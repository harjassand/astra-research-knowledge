SCIENTIFIC CONTRACT — CROSS-GUIDE MEASUREMENT AND IDENTIFIABILITY
Owner: Sol Ultra. Strategy/continuation owner: principal programme evaluator.

WHY THIS QUESTION
Reliable combinatorial interventions require separating repeatable pair effects
from perturbation implementation, sampling and representation effects. Astra's
current weakness is acquiring evidence that changes its scientific model before
inventing another method. This public Cas13 RNA perturbation experiment contains
actual interventions and alternative guide constructions; it is not a replacement
for independent culture experiments or clinical evidence.

The original study used THP-1 AML differentiation as its biological context.
It already reported genetic interactions. Rediscovering an interaction or applying
a standard estimator is not automatically new science. The present objective is
a bounded evidence decision: can the source support a guide-distinct conclusion
after ordinary calibration, and what ambiguity remains? It does not authorize
a virtual-cell model, a new prediction architecture or a broad biology campaign.

QUESTION
Does any prespecified interaction contrast repeat across genuinely different
guide constructions after accounting for ordinary guide potency/position,
cell-sampling uncertainty, shared references and normalization? Alternatively,
can those explanations remain observationally equivalent in the available design?

WHAT IS ALREADY KNOWN
The source-complete input has 30,707 cells, 21,052 genes, 28 single-target conditions,
158 pair labels and one control. Source metadata says THP-1 monocytes with
Cas13 RNA knockdown. A comparator configuration's HEK293T description is not
authoritative for this input.

Primary Supplementary Table 7 identifies HTO 21,22,28,29,30,44,51,52 as THP1 subpools.

FACS validations in the paper do not supply replicate RNA-pair measurements here.

The principal already performed an outcome-independent all-gene diagnostic:
per-cell counts normalized to 10,000, natural log1p, balanced cell halves, seed
20261021; each pair and both singles and control partitioned independently.
For r_ab,h=mean_ab,h-mean_a,h-mean_b,h+mean_control,h,
median cross-half cosine=0.1973116. Sharing pooled single/control estimates
artificially raised it to 0.8048332. All 158 disjoint-arm cross-products were
positive, but no calibrated significance or biological replication follows.

A single ordinary shrinkage coefficient fitted on 69 development pairs reduced
MSE on 89 held-out opposite-half pairs by about 57% relative to raw additivity.
This weakens the premise for a new predictor. It is not a current-frontier win.
The root comparison viewed pooled outcomes from all pairs; your new guide split
can be locally frozen but must not be described as historically untouched data.

ADMISSIBLE OPERATION
1. Map actual guide identities to sequences, targets and array positions using
   the supplied original supplement and raw metadata. Do not infer independence
   from g1/g2 names. Inspect how upstream exclusions affect the available array
   pairs. Preserve the source as received; do not silently add back excluded cells.
2. Determine whether at least one nontrivial family has two genuinely distinct
   pair constructions and corresponding single-target/control measurements under
   an interpretable guide/position design. List excluded arms and why. Freeze
   eligibility using design and counts, not favorable expression outcomes. Use
   at least 5 cells per required guide-specific arm as a minimum computational
   admission, not a claim of sufficient statistical power. If the required
   design is absent, return that scoped exclusion and stop.
3. Specify one estimand and two-direction cross-guide comparison before fitting.
   The supplied log-normalized interaction vector is a starting descriptive
   estimand, not a representation-independent biological definition. Use all
   supplied genes or a source-defined training-only/readout mask frozen before
   held-out guide responses. Record any change from the root representation.
4. Establish a strong ordinary calibration: additive and shrunken/regularized
   linear main effects, with guide/array potency and sampling handled where
   identifiable. A conventional count-scale model with library-size offsets is
   a necessary sensitivity check for claiming an interaction rather than a
   normalization artifact. Do not fit unconstrained per-pair parameters to the
   very pair responses being called predictions. Equal information for all sides.
5. Hold the counterpart pair construction out of tuning. Its allowed single
   effects/controls can be supplied equally to all methods if the contract says
   so; its pair response cannot set the calibration. Keep arm estimates disjoint
   or propagate their covariance. Account for shared controls/singles across
   pairs; cells and pairs are not independent culture replicates. Any resampling
   interval is conditional on this assay and its stated sampling assumptions.
6. Determine whether observed agreement exceeds what this ordinary nuisance
   explanation predicts. Report failures and uncertainty, not selected attractive
   pairs. If many contrasts are tested, predeclare multiplicity treatment. If
   the target distinction is unidentifiable, provide an explicit competing
   construction or rank/likelihood argument explaining the ambiguity. Distinguish
   absence of power from evidence of zero interaction.

CONVENTIONAL AND CURRENT COMPARISONS
The immediate scientific comparison is the original published interaction
analysis plus honest ordinary guide/noise-aware calibration under the same data
and estimand. The delivered sources include the actual original supplement,
public study identifiers and current benchmark preprocessing code.

Any additional claim of predictive-method opportunity or superiority requires
the October 2026 calibrated-metrics/PRESAGE comparison under compatible inputs,
splits, readouts and error definitions. Published scores on another split are
not a matched baseline. If a compatible implementation/frozen output is not
available within the bounded resources, leave that claim unestablished; do not
substitute a weak additive comparator. This experiment itself requires no new
deep model training and makes no claim to beat PRESAGE.

SUCCESS AND STOPPING
A useful completed result is a source-bound, uncertainty-qualified cross-guide
contrast surviving the stated ordinary explanations, OR a precise demonstration
that this design cannot support that conclusion. A successful execution alone
is not either. A surviving contrast establishes technical robustness in this
assay, not general biological epistasis or culture-to-culture generalization.

Stop after the design check and one frozen two-direction analysis plus its
declared sensitivity. No architecture sweep, new dataset hunt, favorable seed
selection, unbounded assay reprocessing or automatic new programme. Preserve
partial calculations if a resource limit is decisive. The principal evaluator
decides any later investment; a positive residual does not release more branches.
