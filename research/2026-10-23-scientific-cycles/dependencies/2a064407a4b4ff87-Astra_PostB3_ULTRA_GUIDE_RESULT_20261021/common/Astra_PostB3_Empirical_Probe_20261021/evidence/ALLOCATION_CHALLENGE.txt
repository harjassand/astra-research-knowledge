ADVERSARIAL ALLOCATION REVIEW — 2026-10-21

DECISION: Authorise ONE bounded Ultra evidence experiment, with Dots checking
its completed operation and Max integrating a ready result. Do not authorise
a predictive-method flagship, a new biology campaign, or three parallel Pro
assignments. Preserve protected foundational owners as the cumulative core.

WHY THIS IS JUSTIFIED, DESPITE THE DOMAIN-HOPPING RISK

The new operation tests a concrete uncertainty exposed by actual measurements.
It does not choose biology merely because Ultra is free. Source input and
alternative guide-array identities are present. Root's completed experiment
already changed the scientific premise: independently split four-arm residual
cosine is about 0.197, versus 0.805 when single/control references are shared.
A one-parameter ordinary shrinkage model also substantially beats uncalibrated
additivity. Thus strong-looking residuals and a weak baseline were insufficient.

There remains a specific unresolved question which cell halves cannot answer:
does any interaction-like response survive changing the guide construction,
after accounting for ordinary guide potency and sampling uncertainty? That
question bears on whether this assay can support the next scientific claim.
One bounded answer has decision value even if it closes the opportunity.

Reserving every new allocation would also be defensible if guide identity
were unavailable. Here it is unnecessarily passive: the input contains 367
array labels, including reciprocal gene/guide arrangements and their singles.
This is enough to investigate assay-level identifiability, subject to the
initial structural check below. It is not evidence of independent cultures.

THE DECISION-CHANGING OPERATION

1. Establish the actual experimental unit before reading new contrast results.
Map array labels to their guide sequences, target, position and matching
single-target controls using the preserved original design. For A_g1:B_g2
versus B_g1:A_g2, establish what actually changes. A label suffix is not by
itself proof of different targeting sequences. Tabulate whether the pair,
both position-matched singles and comparable nontargeting controls exist in
each construction. Prespecify eligibility from metadata/counts. If this map
cannot be established, stop with a scoped inadmissibility result; do not
substitute lane, HTO or random-cell divisions.

2. Freeze a cross-guide comparison. Develop the ordinary response model using
one guide construction and a declared development set of pairs, then assess
the counterpart construction without using its pair response to tune the
model. Repeat the direction under the same frozen rule. Its own single-guide
responses may be used only under an explicit, matched information contract.
The pooled responses were already inspected; describe this as a newly frozen
guide comparison, not a historically untouched holdout. All models must get
the same allowed controls and singles. Estimate reference
responses independently between comparisons or propagate their shared
covariance; a reused control cannot create apparent replication.

3. Ask whether the remaining response has reproducible direction and magnitude
beyond an ordinary noise-aware, guide-aware calibrated null, with uncertainty
at the construction/condition level. Cell resampling quantifies conditional
sampling error only. Lanes and HTO subpools are not biological replicates.
Guide activity, position effects, compositional normalisation and sparse
singles can confound the contrast. Check a declared count/response-scale
model alongside the log-normalised estimand; a scale-dependent residual is
not automatically a biological interaction. Do not improve the answer by
selecting genes, guide pairs or normalisations after seeing held-out responses.

4. Return the attainable claim and the next missing evidence. A surviving
result would establish repeatability across the measured technical guide
constructions under stated assumptions, not general human gene epistasis or
out-of-culture generalisation. If guide potency and interaction remain
observationally interchangeable, demonstrate that equivalence or its practical
uncertainty instead of choosing a preferred mechanism.

STRONG-COMPARATOR BOUNDARY

Root's new shrinkage result decisively rejects an additive-only admission
test. Ultra should implement established calibration/noise treatment, not
invent another favourable simple baseline. The 2026 calibrated/PRESAGE
comparison defines the current predictive boundary. If claiming an unmet
prediction opportunity, matched execution or suitable frozen outputs of that
strong comparison are required, with compatible data and guide splits. If
that is unavailable, the prediction-opportunity gate stays unpassed.

This need not turn the evidence experiment into a full PRESAGE reproduction:
the immediate deliverable is whether a guide-distinct signal can be identified
at all. No predictor-advantage claim follows merely from finding residual
signal, and no new predictor should be trained in this allocation.

STOPPING AND RESOURCE DEPLOYMENT

One design-map check and one frozen two-direction analysis; preserve all
eligible conditions and failures. No extra dataset, architecture sweep,
outcome-selected seed search or broader grant of autonomy. Stop if matching
arms fail, technical signal is not estimable beyond noise/calibration, or
ordinary explanations consume the putative opportunity. Even success earns
a new strategic decision, not automatic programme expansion.

Ultra owns the scientific construction and source delivery. Dots independently
checks the actual guide mapping, information split and shared-reference
accounting once executable material exists; Dots should not repeat root's
already completed cell-half test. Max verifies the final claim against the
contract and integrates the result at a natural checkpoint, rather than
becoming a standing relay. Pro capacity stays with protected owners or in
reserve. The evaluator remains responsible for the next allocation.

WHY THIS IS EVIDENCE ACQUISITION, NOT A NEW FLAGSHIP

The ambition is to learn whether an identifiable measurement supports a
scientific question before building a method around it. That directly tests
the programme change from constructing first to establishing the limiting
scientific uncertainty first. It can improve Astra's selection discipline;
it is not demonstrated research amplification until an independent scientific
operation benefits. If it yields only another bounded negative, preserve it
and stop. Do not convert that result into a search for a replacement biology
benchmark. The foundational chains remain the main ongoing body of work.
