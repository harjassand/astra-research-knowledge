WESSELS CaRPool-seq GROUPING PROVENANCE
Independent source examination, 2026-10-21

Decision
--------
No defensible independent culture/transduction validation split is established by
the currently supplied WesselsSatija2023 labels. The eight HTO labels are hashed
THP1 subpools, the four 10X labels are assay lanes, and the authors explicitly
describe the duplicated perturbations as technical replicates with alternative
guide RNAs. These groups support technical robustness checks; they do not by
themselves support an independent biological-replication claim.

This is an evidence limitation, not proof that the authors never separately
cultured or transduced the subpools. None of the inspected primary materials
maps the eight hashes to distinct culture starts, thaws, infection dates, or
independent experimental batches.

Primary factual reconstruction
------------------------------
1. System and intervention. The relevant experiment uses THP1, an acute monocytic
leukemia cell line, with induced Cas13d-mediated RNA knockdown. It is not the
HEK293FT validation experiment elsewhere in the same article. Supplementary
Methods, pages 3-4 of MOESM1, describe clonal THP1-Cas13d line preparation,
RPMI/10% FBS culture, low-MOI lentiviral guide delivery, puromycin selection for
at least five days, and doxycycline induction. The selected single-cell screen
uses MOI below 0.1; population coverage was maintained above 1,000-fold.

2. HTO mapping. Original Supplementary Table 7, rows 27-34, maps HTO-21, HTO-22,
HTO-28, HTO-29, HTO-30, HTO-44, HTO-51 and HTO-52 to Fig4, with the same pool
description: "subpool THP1". These are precisely the eight HTO categories in
the downloaded WesselsSatija2023 matrix. Row 35 maps HTO-54 to the separate
HEK293FT Fig5 experiment. This prevents both treating the hash codes as eight
documented independent cultures and confusing THP1 with the co-assayed HEK cells.

3. Lanes. GEO GSM6596929 is the HTO library for lane 1, and GSM6596930-32 are
the corresponding other lanes in the series. The sample protocol says selected,
tagged cells are counted and loaded into a Chromium Controller. The lane label
identifies processing, not a new cell culture or transduction. The GEO record
contains no culture-replicate identifier or culture-start mapping.

4. Guide arrays. The Reporting Summary, page 2, Replication, explicitly says
"independent technical replicate (alternative gRNAs)" for each THP1 single/double
perturbation. It also reports removal of GFI1 g2 because it failed to reproduce
the GFI1 g1 response. Different guide sequences probe robustness to intervention
implementation and efficiency; they are not independently repeated delivery of
the same guide in separate experimental batches.

5. Separate validations. Extended Data Fig6i reports independently thawed and
infected THP1 cultures for individual-guide CD11b FACS validation. Extended Data
Fig7f calls the paired arrays technical replicates. The former supports selected
surface-protein phenotypes; it does not supply independently replicated full
single-cell transcriptomes for every pair in this matrix. Its replication cannot
be transferred to the latter by sharing a paper or cell-line name.

Defensible and indefensible splits
---------------------------------
* Random cell halves: disjoint cell-sampling estimates within the measured pool.
  They can assess sampling noise conditional on this experiment and reveal an
  unstable interaction contrast. They cannot estimate culture-to-culture variance.
* HTO holdout: a hashed-subpool robustness check. Its interpretation must remain
  conditional on unknown pre-hashing dependence. It cannot be advertised as a
  biological replicate or donor holdout.
* Lane holdout: processing-partition robustness. It is useful against lane-specific
  effects, but is not evidence of independent culture/transduction reproduction.
* Alternative-array holdout: a guide-sequence robustness challenge. It changes the
  implemented perturbation, and hence possibly the degree of target knockdown.
  A difference can be biological efficacy variation rather than measurement error.
* Held-out gene pair: a prediction test within this experimental population. It
  remains possible with frozen preprocessing and no test-response access. Its
  external generalization claim is restricted to this intervention collection.

Consequences for the proposed opportunity
---------------------------------------
Root's cell-sampling contrast diagnostic can proceed under the first interpretation.
Positive agreement can identify reproducible within-pool structure and guide a
future question; it cannot establish an independent biological noise ceiling,
predictor headroom, clinical utility, or the superiority of a new research strategy.
Negative agreement can reject this dataset/contrast at the tested sample sizes,
without proving absence of genetic interaction.

Do not allocate a substantial new prediction programme on an assertion of eight
independent replicates. A legitimate subsequent evidence acquisition would need
an explicit culture/transduction batch mapping or a separate suitable experiment;
the current inspection has not supplied one. No request to the user or author
outreach is needed for this scoped conclusion.
