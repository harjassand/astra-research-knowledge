1. ADMITTED DEVICE THAT RECEIVES THE KNOWN SQUASHING OBSTRUCTION

Let D=L=2, Phi=Phi_2, and chi=(|00><00|+|11><11|)/2. Complete memory is two
qubit pairs plus the sole CURRENT cyclic clock with labels 1,2 at each party:

 tau = (|11><11|_F tensor Phi tensor chi
       +|22><22|_F tensor Phi tensor Phi)/2.

Each call has the specified faithful product external input
eta=(I_2/2)_A tensor(I_2/2)_B. A fixed finite-round, finite-outcome LOCC device
emits slot1, shifts slot2, locally discards eta, prepares chi in the tail by
Alice's fair bit and one classical message to Bob, and flips current clock.
Both computational copies of that bit are contained in the resulting chi pair;
there is no extra immutable historical record. Temporary generation/transcript
registers are actually discarded. This is an explicit normal CPTP LOCC map on
all inputs. Every outcome is averaged. Local entropy disposal and communication
are allowed but not claimed physically costless.

The first output is exactly Phi, independent of the whole residual by purity.
After one use, the clock1 block Phi tensor chi is unchanged; clock2 changes
from Phi tensor Phi to chi tensor chi. Hence complete half-trace return is

 delta = T(tau_1,tau)=(1/2) T(chi^2,Phi^2)=(1/2)(1-1/4)=3/8.

The middle equality follows because chi^2 is the identity divided by4 on the
maximally correlated 4-dimensional support, and Phi^2 is one unit vector there.
The clocks and both queue slots are counted. An extra immutable original-label
record would be a different return model and is not present here.

The actual TWO-SLOT EXTERNAL OUTPUT, with no memory access, is exactly

2. EXACT FINITE-AUXILIARY NONATTAINMENT TRANSFERS TO THIS OUTPUT

For a finite output state zeta define

 e_d(zeta)=inf_{extensions zeta_ABE, dim E<=d} I(A:B|E)/2.

Ahmed--Smith, arXiv:2609.14031v1, Theorem2, proves for 0<c<1 and every integer
d>=1 that e_(3d+2)(rho_c)<e_d(rho_c). This is a primary preprint theorem,
not an Astra discovery. Its normalization is converted from bits to nats here.

Every extension Omega_(A1B1 A2B2 E) of omega factors as

 Omega = Phi_(A1B1) tensor zeta_(A2B2E).

Proof: positivity and the pure A1B1 marginal force support in its rank-one
support tensor the rest. Tracing the rank-one factor gives zeta. Conversely
every extension of rho_(1/2) produces an extension of omega in this way.

Expanding the four entropy terms, I(A1A2:B1B2|E)_Omega
=2 ln2+I(A2:B2|E)_zeta. Thus for EVERY finite d,

 e_d(omega)=ln2+e_d(rho_(1/2)),
 e_(3d+2)(omega)<e_d(omega).

Finite-dimensional extension state sets are compact, so their minima exist;
the strict improvement excludes every finite d from attaining E_sq(omega).
This establishes the receiving obstruction inside the declared operational
model. The known primary theorem is the sole non-elementary premise here.

An extension with computed CMI gives an UPPER bound on E_sq, whereas a supply
converse needs a LOWER bound. Even an exact optimizer at fixed d has the wrong
global certification direction unless a separate dimension-independent dual
inequality is proved. Nonattainment does not imply that useful approximate
upper bounds or valid dimension-independent lower bounds are impossible.

3. RESOURCE, ERROR AND COMPARISON SCOPE

Local clock readout of tau yields one or two exact Bell pairs, with probability
1/2 each. EF(tau)=3 ln2/2 by the matching mixture upper and flagged lower.
The same local flag argument on tensor powers gives EF(tau^r)=3r ln2/2.
Consequently exact genuinely preselected expected Bell supply is 3r ln2/2;
exact deterministic Bell supply is 2r ln2 (positive all-two branch Schmidt rank).
All flags/catalysts are charged; no free entangled catalyst is introduced.

The construction is an ordinary stock queue. Its finite exact initialization
error is zero. Its return error is 3/8 on the COMPLETE memory. This admitted
small example establishes optimizer nonattainment, not asymptotic supply saving
or a practical advantage. For approximate iid initialization, one must price a
GLOBAL complete-memory epsilon and cannot infer an error bound on E_sq from
the no-error factorization. An independent finite-output continuity/converse
argument is needed. No hidden memory readout is assumed or supplied here.
