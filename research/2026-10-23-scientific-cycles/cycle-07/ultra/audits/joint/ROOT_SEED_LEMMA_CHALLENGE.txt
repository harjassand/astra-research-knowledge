INDEPENDENT CHALLENGE — FULL-SUPPORT SEED TO EXACT MC OUTPUT
22 October 2026

Verdict: PASS for finite dimensions and a fixed separable CPTP channel.
The choice of that channel may depend on a complete classical description
of the known input state. No root flaw was found. Exact support and the
absence of an uncounted entangled auxiliary input are load-bearing.

Precise statement. Let H_A0=H_B0=C^s and let H_A1,H_B1 be finite dimensional.
Let P=Phi_s tensor I_A1 tensor I_B1, so

  S=range(P)=span{|Phi_s>} tensor H_A1 tensor H_B1.

Suppose sigma is a density operator supported on S and sigma>=aP for a>0.
Let Gamma be a separable CPTP channel with product Kraus operators
K_l=A_l tensor B_l. Suppose the complete declared output state Gamma(sigma)
is supported on a fixed maximally correlated subspace

  W=span{|i>_A |i>_B:1<=i<=d}.

Then every nonzero K_l(S) is contained in the span of at most s of those
matched basis vectors. Consequently

  SN(Gamma(sigma))<=s,       E_F(Gamma(sigma))<=ln s.            (1)

Proof, including the proposed rank argument.

1. Product-Kraus support. Since sigma>=aP and Gamma(sigma) has no component
outside W, every positive term K_l sigma K_l^dagger has no such component.
Equivalently, for P_(Wperp) the complementary projector,

  0=Tr[P_(Wperp) Gamma(sigma)]
   >=a sum_l ||P_(Wperp) K_l P||_HS^2.

Thus P_(Wperp)K_l P=0 for every l, and K_l(S) subset W. This conclusion
does not rely on cancellation between Kraus branches; positivity excludes
such cancellation.

2. Number of active matched coordinates. Fix one product Kraus A tensor B.
For u in H_A1 and v in H_B1 define, omitting the harmless factor 1/sqrt(s),

  C_ij(u,v)=sum_(x=1)^s <i|A|x,u> <j|B|x,v>.

Because K(S) subset W, C_ij(u,v)=0 for all u,v whenever i!=j. Call i
active if C_ii is not identically zero. For each active i select u_i,v_i
with C_ii(u_i,v_i)!=0. On the active index set define

  M_ij=C_ij(u_i,v_j).

This matrix is diagonal with nonzero diagonal. On the other hand it factors
as M=UV^T, where U_ix=<i|A|x,u_i> and V_jx=<j|B|x,v_j>. Hence

  number of active indices = rank(M) <= s.

The use of v_j in column j is valid: the off-diagonal identity holds for
every pair u,v, including the independently selected u_i,v_j. No complex
conjugate is missing: the maximally entangled ket contraction is bilinear
in the two product-Kraus matrix coefficients.

3. Schmidt number and E_F. Every vector in K(S) lies in the span of at
most s matched product basis vectors, so it has Schmidt rank at most s.
Take any pure-state decomposition of sigma within S and apply every Kraus
operator. The resulting normalized nonzero pure states give a convex
decomposition of Gamma(sigma) into states of Schmidt rank at most s. This
proves the Schmidt-number bound. Their entanglement entropy is at most
ln s, proving the formation bound (1). Local discarded environments may
be refined into product Kraus indices; they do not evade the argument.

QUEUE CONSEQUENCE

For

  omega_(L,N=L)=(1/L) sum_(k=1)^L Phi_D^tensor k
                                    tensor nu^tensor(L-k),
  nu=I_(D^2)/D^2,

all components lie in S=span(Phi_D) tensor H_tail,A tensor H_tail,B, and
the k=1 component gives

  omega_(L,L) >= [1/(L D^(2(L-1)))]
                  Phi_D tensor I_tail,A tensor I_tail,B.

Therefore any output-only separable CPTP channel that puts the whole
output exactly into a fixed MC span has Schmidt number at most D and
E_F at most ln D, regardless of how its Kraus representation depends on
the known input description.

The loss of the queue's linear Bell-prefix score can also be proved without
using the inherited one-chain constant. Partial trace is local, so every
first-i-pair marginal has Schmidt number <=D. Its overlap with Phi_(D^i)
is therefore at most min{1,D/D^i}. To see the usual overlap bound directly,
a normalized Schmidt-rank-q vector has squared overlap with the d-level
maximally entangled ket at most q/d by Cauchy-Schwarz; extend by convexity.
It follows that, with m=ln D,

  m sum_(i=1)^L Tr[Phi_(1..i) Gamma(omega)]
    <= m sum_(i=1)^L D^(1-i)
     = m(1-D^(-L))/(1-D^(-1)).                               (2)

The input's score is at least m(L+1)/2, since conditional inventory k
contributes at least k unit prefix overlaps and E[K]=(L+1)/2. Hence an
exact MC output-only normalizer must lose at least

  m[(L+1)/2-(1-D^(-L))/(1-D^(-1))]

of that score whenever this expression is positive. This is Theta(L)
at fixed D, not a dimension-only constant.

IID EXTENSION

For r independent queues the seed is Phi_D^tensor r, equivalently a
maximally entangled seed of dimension s=D^r. The same argument permits
an arbitrary collective separable normalizer and gives E_F<=r ln D.
If its exact MC span is the standard product-string MC span across the
r output tasks, the actual complementary Q is zero, and the inherited
r-task witness bounds the total score by r(ln D+C_D). This extra score
consequence uses that product-string alignment. The Schmidt-number and
formation conclusions themselves are independent of that alignment.

SCOPE CAVEATS

* This is an output-only channel acting on omega. A device given the
  correlated current clock or other retained memory has a different input
  support, so the Cycle6 controller-assisted repair is not contradicted.
* An entangled catalyst or other shared auxiliary must be incorporated
  into the actual input. It cannot be supplied freely under this lemma.
* Exact MC support is essential here. No dimension-uniform approximate-MC
  stability result follows. The explicit seed lower-bound coefficient is
  exponentially small in L, so naive leakage estimates can be expensive.
* A channel selected for a known sigma is allowed. A nonlinear rule that
  changes branches using inaccessible preparation labels is not a channel
  on the declared input and lies outside the statement.
* The finite-dimensional identity on the seed-tail support has finite
  trace. An infinite-dimensional extension must reformulate the positivity
  premise; a positive multiple of an infinite-rank identity is not a
  trace-class lower bound for a density operator.

Observed work: analytic challenge only; no numerical search or quantum
convex-roof calculation was used. This verifies internal validity and the
receiving obstruction. Historical novelty is not assessed here.
