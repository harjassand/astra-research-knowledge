INDEPENDENT AUDIT — CLASSICAL INVENTORY ACCESS FOR EXACT MC NORMALIZATION
22 October 2026

Verdict: PASS. Randomized labels cannot improve the sharp finite alphabet
bound. The resulting resource is classical information about the inventory,
not a supplied entanglement saving or a proof of an accessible-memory port.

SETUP AND CONDITIONAL SEED BOUND

Let K be uniform on {1,...,L}. A receiver is supplied a classical label C
through any stochastic kernel p(c|k), with at most J positive-probability
labels. Conditioned on C=c the declared quantum input is

  omega_c=sum_k p(k|c) Phi_D^tensor k tensor nu^tensor(L-k),
  nu=I_(D^2)/D^2.

There is no further shared entangled auxiliary or hidden preparation label.
For each nonempty label put t_c=min{k:p(k|c)>0}. Then omega_c has full
support on

  span(Phi_D^tensor t_c) tensor H_tail,A tensor H_tail,B,

because the k=t_c term has a strictly positive identity tail. The exact
seed lemma therefore gives, for every separable CPTP receiving operation
whose complete declared quantum output is exactly MC,

  E_F(output|C=c)<=t_c m,       m=ln D.                        (1)

The seed maximally entangled dimension is D^t_c. This permits an arbitrary
joint operation across all L output pairs, arbitrary classical dependence
of the channel on c, and choosing the channel using the known omega_c.
Positivity means that exact MC support of the averaged output also forces
exact MC support on every positive-probability conditional output.

SHARP ALPHABET BOUND

For q=min{J,L}, write L=q a+b with integers a>=1 and 0<=b<q. Then

  E[K-t_C] >= d_(L,J)
    := [(q-b)a(a-1)+b a(a+1)]/(2L)
     = (L/q-1)/2 + b(q-b)/(2qL).                             (2)

Equality is attainable by a deterministic label of q consecutive balanced
bins, q-b of length a and b of length a+1, with t_c the first element of
each bin. In particular, when J<=L,

  E[K-t_C] >= (L/J-1)/2.                                    (3)

Proof that stochastic labels cannot improve (2). The support definition
implies t_c<=k whenever p(c|k)>0. For any fixed set of distinct thresholds,
replace the stochastic assignment at each k by the largest available
threshold <=k. This weakly increases t_C for each k and hence weakly
decreases E[K-t_C]. The threshold 1 must be available, since K=1 occurs
with positive probability. Ordered distinct thresholds partition the
integer line {1,...,L} into consecutive nonempty bins: all k from one
threshold until just before the next are assigned to that threshold.
Each threshold remains the actual support minimum of its new bin.
Duplicate or unused labels have no advantage.

If the bin lengths are n_1,...,n_q', q'<=min{J,L}, their mean loss is

  [sum_j n_j(n_j-1)]/(2L).

Splitting any bin of length >=2 strictly reduces the numerator, so the
minimum uses q=min{J,L} bins. For fixed q, if n_i>=n_j+2, moving one
point from the longer bin to the shorter changes the numerator by
2(n_j-n_i+1)<0. Thus all minimizing lengths differ by at most one,
giving exactly the balanced integer formula (2).

SCORE CONSEQUENCE

Let S_in=m sum_(i=1)^L Tr[Phi_(1..i) omega] be the actual queue score and
let S_out denote the averaged actual output score on the same pair ports.
Every inventory branch contributes at least k unit prefix overlaps, so

  S_in>=m E[K]=m(L+1)/2.

Apply the inherited one-chain inequality E_F>=score-C_D to every
conditional output and use (1):

  S_out<=m E[t_C]+C_D,
  score loss ell:=S_in-S_out >=m d_(L,J)-C_D.                 (4)

Consequently achieving ell<=ell_0, where ell_0 is a fixed finite loss
budget and ell_0+C_D>=0, requires

  J >= L/[1+2(ell_0+C_D)/m].                                (5)

Thus O(1) score loss at fixed D requires J=Omega(L), or
ln J>=ln L-O(1). This does not claim that those many labels are sufficient
for every protocol; it proves a necessary classical interface size.

A stronger exact frontier avoids the inherited constant. Schmidt number
<=D^t_c gives each i-prefix overlap <=min{1,D^(t_c-i)}, and hence

  S_out|c <=m F_L(t_c),
  F_L(t)=t+sum_(h=1)^(L-t) D^(-h)
        =t+(1-D^(-(L-t)))/(D-1).                             (8)

This upper bound is simultaneously attained for every prefix. Conditional
on c the first t_c pairs are exactly Phi_D^tensor t_c and factor from the
remaining input, since every inventory in its support is >=t_c. Keep those
pairs and locally replace every later pair by |00><00|. The output is the
pure MC state Phi_D^tensor t_c tensor |00><00|^tensor(L-t_c), and its
i-prefix overlap is exactly min{1,D^(t_c-i)}. Thus for every specified
classical channel p(c|k),

  maximum exact-MC output score = m E[F_L(t_C)].               (9)

The receiver needs only c, its specified support minimum t_c, and local
reset control. It does not need the original k. Local replacement entails
fresh pure local preparation and entropy disposal; it is not a unitary
free-energy claim and does not establish a memory-return interface.

In particular one may replace C_D in (4)-(5) by m/(D-1). This receiving
argument is not a proposed new one-chain-constant research programme.

For the balanced q-label operation, put d_min=d_(L,J). Its attained score
lies between

  m[(L+1)/2-d_min]
  and m[(L+1)/2-d_min+1/(D-1)].                              (10)

At fixed D and J<=L this is B-mL/(2J)+O_D(1), where
B=m(L+1)/2. It is within m/(D-1) of the optimal J-label score. Balanced
bins optimize E[t_C] exactly, but they need not optimize E[F_L(t_C)]
exactly because its small nonlinear tail correction depends on thresholds.
No exact optimal-bin claim for the nonlinear objective is made.

For completeness, the exact actual input score includes its private-tail
overlap and is

  S_in=m E[K+(1-D^(-2(L-K)))/(D^2-1)].                       (11)

Thus actual score loss for the attaining operation is exactly (11) minus
(9). It may be negative when sufficiently informative labels permit
replacing private noise by |00>, which increases Bell-prefix overlaps.

OPTIONAL STRONGER INFORMATION CHARGE

The alphabet lower bound can be sharpened to actual classical information.
Put d=E[K-t_C] and define g(d)=(d+1)ln(d+1)-d ln d, with g(0)=0. Then

  I(K:C)>=ln L-g(d),       H(C)>=ln L-g(d).                   (6)

Proof. Let E=K-t_C>=0. For fixed c, K and E determine each other, so
H(K|C)=H(E|C)<=H(E). A nonnegative integer variable of mean d has entropy
at most g(d): for d>0, nonnegativity of its relative entropy against
q_e=(d+1)^(-1)[d/(d+1)]^e gives this expression directly; d=0 is
deterministic. Therefore H(K|C)<=g(d) and (6) follows from H(K)=ln L.

Combining (4) before minimizing d with a score-loss budget ell_0 gives

  I(K:C), H(C) >= ln L-g((ell_0+C_D)/m),                     (7)

or use the direct constant m/(D-1). Thus bounded score loss requires
ln L-O(1) nats of actual inventory information, even if the label alphabet
is unbounded. The derivation is exact and does not invoke a numerical
rate-distortion optimization.

SCOPE AND ACCOUNTING

* This concerns classical side information C and exact MC normalization.
  It does not exclude a different quantum-memory-assisted receiving map.
* Correlating C with K is not free receiver access. A protocol must specify
  how the receiver obtains it, its readout/control operations, and all
  retained copies. Adding an immutable inventory record can change the
  complete memory return error; no return claim is made here.
* Keeping C as a classical output record, rather than discarding it, does
  not evade the conditional bound. All branches are averaged and the
  record is included under the chosen complete-resource convention.
* Exact MC support remains load-bearing. Approximate support requires a
  separately proved quantitative theorem.
* This is a necessary access/score tradeoff, not an advantage over ordinary
  stock, and it does not assign thermodynamic costs to classical entropy.

Observed work: exact analytic proof; no numerical job or external source
recovery was required. Historical novelty is not assessed by this audit.
