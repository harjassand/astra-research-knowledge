This contribution checks one load-bearing compositional lemma. It does not
repeat old numerical fixtures, run the owner's automaton closure, or reassess
the full recovery programme.

The first and only recorded execution of `check_port_interface.py` passed.
It independently enumerates proper four-color assignments on the saved small
graphs, filters by the forbidden bichromatic four-vertex path criterion, and
compares actual signature sets with the exact series/parallel formulas. The
