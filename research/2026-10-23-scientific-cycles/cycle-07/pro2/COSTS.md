## Theoretical cost and error boundary

With a supplied expression, construction and explicit graph output take
O(p+n+m) work/storage. A compressed description can expand exponentially and
that expanded work is charged. Normalization of unused listed descriptions
is also counted in p for this implementation. Action preprocessing and
decoding take O(nq+m) arithmetic with q=4. Exact rational bit complexity is
additional; these are arithmetic counts, not a polynomial-bit bound for all
input encodings. There is no free arbitrary-graph recognizer.

The proved bound uses absolute Frobenius noise and gives operator output error.
For floating output, an independently bounded supported projected-action
residual and action/assembly roundoff are additional errors. Row normalization
alone cannot bound D deltaX. The supplied rational receiving records have
exactly zero projected-action residual, not zero arbitrary raw-noise residual.

The current small exact calculations do not beat the original dense NIST
incumbent or establish an application benefit. The five-query full-spark
general control and ordinary three-query forest control remain applicable.
The forest control's sharper operator constant sqrt(3/2), as well as its
Frobenius constant sqrt(2), is retained. A late source-grounded proof transfers
that operator estimate to all triangle-free star colorings and hence to the
unchanged new LS decoder. It used no new numerical run, did not alter the
saved squared-bound2 observations, and has separately unmetered proof/review
cost. The exact native Cycle2 proof was hash-bound and selected in sources/.
An extra query may be worthwhile for conditioning, but that trade has not
been translated into a matched application runtime claim.
