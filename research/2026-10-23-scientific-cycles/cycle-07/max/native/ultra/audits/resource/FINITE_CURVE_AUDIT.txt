EXACT FINITE INITIALIZATION CURVES: INDEPENDENT ANALYTIC AUDIT
22 October 2026. Root's proposed complete ordinary comparator.
No numerical job or search. Natural logs; T=half trace norm.

TARGET. Let K=(K_1,...,K_r), K_b in{1,...,L}, p_K=L^-r and
S(K)=sum_b K_b. In matched orthogonal local flag sector K, the complete
target is Phi_(d_K) on all active prefix factors, tensor independent
local maximally mixed junk; d_K=D^S(K). This includes all retained flags,
all r memories and their local mixed pair content. Put p_s=Pr[S=s].

A. DETERMINISTIC SUPPLIED RANK M

The root formula is correct:

 e_r(M)=inf_(normal LOCC from Phi_M) T(sigma,tau_tilde^tensor r)
       =1-max_(q_K>=0,sum q_K=1)
                 sum_K min{p_K,q_K f_(M,K)},
 f_(M,K)=min{1,M/d_K}.                                      (1)

LOWER. Apply local complete dephasing of BOTH flag registers; the target
is invariant and half trace distance cannot increase. Let q_K be the
candidate probability of matched local flags K; their sum can be <=1
because candidate flags may mismatch. In every matched block, a source
Phi_M and any normal LOCC factory produce a state of Schmidt number <=M.
This holds branch by branch, after local traces of junk, and under the
finite-output operational closure. Its conditional overlap with the
indicated joint Bell state of rank d_K is <=f_(M,K). The usual proof is
the Cauchy--Schwarz Schmidt-rank overlap bound on every pure component.

Measure matched flags K and project the active prefix factors onto their
joint Bell state, assigning every mismatch or failure to rejection. This
is a bounded mathematical converse effect; the Bell test is not claimed
LOCC. The target has distribution p_K entirely on success outcomes, while
the candidate pass mass in sector K is <=q_K f_(M,K). Classical variation
distance is therefore >=1-sum_K min{p_K,q_K f_(M,K)}. Data processing gives
the same lower bound on T. Adding any missing q mass to matched sectors
cannot decrease that objective, so its optimum has sum q_K=1.

ACHIEVABILITY. First sample K with law q_K and prepare those counted flags.
When M>=d_K convert Phi_M to Phi_(d_K) deterministically by finite LOCC.
When M<d_K, locally embed Phi_M in the d_K active dimensions and apply
the bilateral U tensor conjugate(U) twirl. It has Bell fidelity M/d_K,
so its active state is exactly the isotropic state
 rho_f=f Phi_(d_K)+(1-f)(I-Phi_(d_K))/(d_K^2-1).
The twirl is implementable by a finite distribution of local unitaries:
the Haar average of its finite-dimensional superoperator lies in the
compact convex hull of unitary conjugation superoperators, and finite-
dimensional Caratheodory gives a finite exact convex combination. It may
be extremely expensive to synthesize. Genuinely discard the sampled
twirl record; no successful outcome is selected. Append the same local
mixed junk as in the target. The sector-wise trace norm is exactly

 ||p_K Phi-q_K rho_f||_1
   =|p_K-q_K f|+q_K(1-f),

since the Bell line and its orthogonal complement are orthogonal. Tensor
with identical normalized local junk leaves this norm unchanged. Summing
and using sum p=sum q=1 proves equality in (1).

AGGREGATION. For a fixed inventory s, f_(M,K) depends only on s. The sum
of the sector minima is <=min{p_s,q_s f_(M,s)}, and equality is attained
by assigning q_K=q_s p_K/p_s within each inventory class. Thus

 e_r(M)=1-max_(q_s>=0,sum q_s=1)
                  sum_s min{p_s,q_s min(1,M D^-s)}.            (2)

This is ordinary fractional knapsack: each capped pass mass y_s<=p_s
uses q mass y_s/f_(M,s), with value per unit q equal to f_(M,s).
Fill decreasing f (increasing s), up to cap, until the q budget is used.
Target flag probabilities need not be reproduced in the approximate
candidate. Forcing q_s=p_s can be strictly suboptimal.

B. EXPECTED PRESELECTED BELL SUPPLY

The root's finite LP is exact. Let x_(M,s)>=0 be joint mass of supplied
rank M (chosen BEFORE receipt) and chosen matched inventory s. Then
sum_(M,s)x_(M,s)=1, and achievable pass mass in inventory s is
c_s=sum_M min(1,M D^-s)x_(M,s). With pass credits y_s,

 minimize sum_(M,s) x_(M,s) ln M
 subject to 0<=y_s<=p_s, y_s<=c_s, sum_s y_s>=1-epsilon.       (3)

The lower bound applies the Schmidt-number argument separately to every
preselected supply branch. For the upper, sample the joint pair (M,s)
before resource receipt; choose K within s with target-relative weights,
receive Phi_M, perform the preceding isotropic preparation and append
junk. Mixtures of the isotropic preparations in a fixed sector remain
isotropic, so the achieved half-trace error is exactly
1-sum_s min(p_s,c_s). Duplicate supplier/factory records are genuinely
erased; only the declared target flags remain. This is not postselection.
Ranks above D^(rL) are dominated because their fidelities all saturate at
one and the same preparations are possible at smaller supplied rank.

C. STRONGER LP REDUCTION: ONLY FREE/FULL SUPPLY PER INVENTORY

No exponential enumeration of M is necessary in (3). Fix an inventory s
and let d=D^s>1. A joint mass x_(M,s) with 1<=M<=d has f=M/d. Replace
that mass by M=1 and M=d with proportions 1-theta and theta, where
theta=(M-1)/(d-1). This preserves its pass contribution exactly:

 (1-theta)/d+theta=M/d.

It does not increase supplied cost because concavity of ln on [1,d]
gives theta ln d<=ln M. For M>d, replacing it by M=d preserves unit
fidelity and decreases cost. This conditional replacement is allowed:
the contract permits an arbitrary genuinely preselected joint supply-
choice/output-inventory distribution. It does not hold for a different
contract forcing the supply distribution to be independent of inventory.

Hence an exact reduced LP uses only u_s (free rank-one branch), t_s
(full Bell rank D^s branch), and pass credits y_s:

 minimize sum_s t_s s ln D,
 u_s,t_s,y_s>=0,
 sum_s(u_s+t_s)=1,
 y_s<=p_s,
 y_s<=D^-s u_s+t_s,
 sum_s y_s>=1-epsilon.                                      (4)

This gives a source-complete finite ordinary optimization of polynomial
SIZE in the number r(L-1)+1 of inventory values. Computing the exact
coefficients p_s of [z+...+z^L]^r/L^r still has actual integer arithmetic
cost, and physically synthesizing all dense isotropic twirls may be very
expensive. A small LP is not a demonstrated efficient quantum factory.

At epsilon=0, caps force c_s>=p_s and sum p=1. Because each f<=1, only
full-fidelity mass can contribute; (4) gives the expected exact cost
sum_s p_s s ln D=rB. Formula(2) gives exact deterministic error zero
only for M>=D^(rL). These are checks of conventions, not new additive-
constant optimization in the retired R1 subprogramme.
