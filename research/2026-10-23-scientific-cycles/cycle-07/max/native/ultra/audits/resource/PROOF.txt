COMPLETE-DEVICE AND SUPPLIED-RESOURCE AUDIT: PRODUCT-NOISE STOCK
22 October 2026. Independent bounded analytic challenge. Natural logarithms.
Half-trace distance T(rho,sigma)=||rho-sigma||_1/2.

SCOPE
This is an admitted exact-first-output/complete-return construction, not a
claimed improvement over ordinary consumable Bell stock. It disproves a
uniform optimized-complementary-uncertainty bound even at the useful reuse
horizon. It leaves the regularized formation cost of this non-MC stock open.
No numerical search or new numerical job was run.

1. DEVICE, INITIALIZATION AND COMPLETE STATE

Fix integers D>=2,L>=1. Put m=ln D, let Phi=|Phi_D><Phi_D| and let
nu=(I_D/D)_A tensor (I_D/D)_B. Memory consists of exactly L pair registers,
with local dimension D^L on each side. Put
 rho_k=Phi^tensor k tensor nu^tensor(L-k), 0<=k<=L,
 tau_L=(1/L)sum_(k=1)^L rho_k.

The fixed device emits the first memory pair, shifts the remaining memory
pairs left, and resets the last pair to nu by INDEPENDENT local reset maps.
It is a product of two local CPTP maps, hence a one-round, one-outcome
finite-dimensional LOCC map. It is normal. It can ignore the external
faithful product input. No measurement transcript, shared random seed,
catalyst, inventory flag or retained purification is needed in the device.
Local discarded environments are not retained shared registers. The model
allows such ordinary local CPTP maps; no coherent hidden register is claimed
to be returned. All retained memory is exactly the L pairs stated above.

After t>=0 calls the EXACT joint output-memory state is

 Omega_t=(1/L)sum_(k=1)^L
 [Phi^tensor min(k,t) tensor nu^tensor(t-k)_+ ]_out
 tensor [Phi^tensor(k-t)_+ tensor nu^tensor(L-(k-t)_+)]_mem.       (1)

This retains every output-output and output-memory correlation. In
particular the first output is EXACT Phi, decoupled from memory by purity.
For t>=L,
 Omega_t=[tau_L tensor nu^tensor(t-L)]_out tensor nu^tensor L_mem. (2)
Thus the late product noise and the exhausted memory factor exactly; this
is an identity, not an independence approximation.

At one call the complete-memory difference telescopes:
 tau_L-Phi_mem(tau_L)=(rho_L-rho_0)/L.
Here rho_L=Phi^tensor L is rank one and rho_0=I_(D^(2L))/D^(2L).
They commute, so
 delta_L=T(tau_L,Phi_mem(tau_L))=(1-D^(-2L))/L.                    (3)
There is no return of an immutable K record: such a record would change
the state and the return error. K below is a genuinely erased factory coin.
This finite queue is not recurrent and eventually exhausts.

2. EXACT EXPECTED SUPPLY AND DETERMINISTIC IID PREPARATION

For an exact preselected-resource factory, FIRST draw K uniformly from
{1,...,L}, receive Phi_(D^K), locally identify it with K Bell pairs, append
L-K independent local maximally mixed pairs, and erase K and all records
of K genuinely before device use. The exact supplied charge is

 B_L=E[K]m=(L+1)m/2.                                           (4)

All branches are averaged. No successful outcome is selected. This is the
explicit permitted expected-supply convention, not a statement that the
cost of an arbitrary pure-state ensemble can be charged as Bell inventory.

For a deterministic iid factory of r full memories, choose a Bell-pair
budget b_r=ceil(r(L+1)/2+s_r). Internally sample the whole K-vector with
its product-uniform law. On sum_b K_b<=b_r, discard excess supplied Bell
pairs and prepare exactly the corresponding product rung. On the other
branches prepare a local fallback (for example nu^tensor rL). Erase all
K and overflow records genuinely. The output differs from tau_L^tensor r
in GLOBAL half-trace distance by at most
 p_over=Pr(sum_b K_b>b_r).

For L>=2, bounded-variable Hoeffding gives
 p_over<=exp[-2s_r^2/(r(L-1)^2)].                               (5)
Take s_r=(L-1)sqrt(r ln(1/epsilon_r)/2) and ceil as above. For fixed L
and ln(1/epsilon_r)=o(r), epsilon_r->0 gives the explicit deterministic
supplied rate b_r m/r -> B_L. For L=1 the preparation is exact with r
Bell pairs. Every catalyst is absent and every initial shared register is
already included. The factory has finitely many branches at every r.

Simple local preparation uses O(rL) D-level register work; sample handling
and pair routing have a finite implementation. Each shift call can use
O(L) local swaps per side plus local reset. The stated supplied charge is
ENTANGLEMENT, not total computation. No asymptotically efficient optimal
dilution routine, finite hardware cost or experimental return test follows.

3. ACTUAL PREFIX SCORES AND EXACT-FACTORY LOWER BOUND

Let p_i be the probability that ALL first i emitted pairs pass the Bell
test. A fixed rung K=k gives p_i=1 for i<=k and D^[-2(i-k)] otherwise.
Thus, for i<=L,
 p_i=(L-i+1)/L+(1/L)sum_(h=1)^(i-1) D^(-2h).                    (6)
For i>L it is (1/L)sum_(k=1)^L D^[-2(i-k)]. These are joint-prefix
probabilities; replacing them by products or sums of marginal fidelities
would be incorrect.

At horizon L,
 mu_L=sum_(i=1)^L p_i=(L+1)/2+G_(L,D^2),
 G_(L,D^2)=(1/L)sum_(h=1)^(L-1)(L-h)D^(-2h)>=0.                (7)

The inherited ONE-chain theorem on this actual finite output yields
 E_F(tau_L)>=m mu_L-C_D>=B_L-C_D.
Every exact Bell factory has E_F no larger than its actual supplied mean
Bell inventory. Hence

 B_L-C_D <= C_av^ex(tau_L) <= B_L.                              (8)

This identifies the expected-exact preparation scaling to an O_D(1) gap.
It does NOT prove E_C=E_F for this full-rank-tail, non-MC memory. The old
maximally-correlated support/additivity proof for chi-tail stock does not
apply after replacing chi by nu.

The inherited Cycle3 generic finite-copy test applies to all output states,
so with Z_L=1+(L-1)(1-D^-1) it gives

 m mu_L-ln Z_L <= E_C(tau_L) <= B_L.                            (9)

For L=1 this reads E_C=m. The upper is the explicit iid factory above.
The lower is inherited, not a newly proved regularized-E_F identity. The
logarithmic interval for iid formation remains open for this particular
state unless a further lower or improved factory is supplied. Large Q_*
does not establish any saving inside that interval.

4. IRREDUCIBLE COMPLEMENT UNCERTAINTY: NO SMALL-Q CONSEQUENCE

Run r independent copies of the exact admitted memory for N calls and
discard the retained memory locally. Let sigma_(r,N)=omega_N^tensor r
be the ACTUAL full output, where
 omega_N=(1/L)sum_(k=1)^L
       Phi^tensor min(k,N) tensor nu^tensor(N-k)_+.
Tasks are the r complete N-call trajectories. Define the previous witness's
 Q_a=S(B_(-a)|Y_a),
where Y_a is ANY finite POVM readout on the entire Alice complement A_(-a).
The readout may be joint across the r-1 other trajectories, not a product.

Introduce only in the mathematical proof an extra classical label
K_(-a) for those trajectories' factory rungs. Conditional entropy obeys
 S(B_(-a)|Y_a)>=S(B_(-a)|Y_a K_(-a))                            (10)
by SSA. Conditional on the whole K-vector, Bob's suffix nu factors are
independent maximally mixed D-level systems, independent of EVERY Alice
system and of the remaining Bell-prefix systems. An arbitrary readout on
Alice leaves those Bob factors unchanged and independent, even when the
readout jointly couples all Alice prefixes and suffixes. Consequently

 S(B_(-a)|Y_a,K_(-a)=k)>=m sum_(b!=a)(N-k_b)_+.

Average the right side, which does not depend on the readout, to obtain

 Q_a >= (r-1)m g_L(N),
 Qbar_*=(1/r)sum_a inf_(A_(-a) readout) Q_a
       >=(r-1)m g_L(N),                                      (11)
 g_L(N)=E[(N-K)_+]
       ={N(N-1)/(2L),                 1<=N<=L,
         N-(L+1)/2,                  N>=L.}

The same proof holds for the globally swapped Bob-readout convention.
The label in (10) is a formal genie used only to LOWER-bound Q. It is not
a retained shared flag, a newly supplied resource or an available readout.
This avoids any violation of the exact complete-memory return convention.

At N=L, Qbar_* >=(r-1)(L-1)m/2. Already r=2 gives uncertainty growing
as 1/delta_L along L->infinity. For ANY fixed L>=2 and this fixed finite
horizon, Qbar_* diverges with iid preparation multiplicity r.

The specified horizon N=ceil(1/delta_L) is exactly L+1: for D>=2,L>=1,
 D^(2L)>=4^L>L+1, so 0<L/(D^(2L)-1)<1 and
 1/delta_L=L+L/(D^(2L)-1).
Thus its very prescribed useful horizon satisfies
 Qbar_* >=(r-1)(L+1)m/2=(r-1)B_L.                              (12)
The counterexample is not dependent on appending an arbitrarily long
post-exhaustion output tail. Nevertheless such a tail gives the simpler
bound Qbar_* >=(r-1)(N-L)m directly from (2).

5. CONCLUSION AND COMPARATOR

The actual causal exact-first-output/complete-return assumptions alone do
NOT impose the hoped uniform optimized complementary-uncertainty bound.
They permit arbitrarily large Q_* at the same fixed initial supplied mean
charge and return error; they also permit Q_* of order 1/delta inside the
positive-survival horizon. All output correlations and the full memory
were included. An unconstrained HJW/E_F optimization cannot remove the
explicit independent-local-noise lower bound.

Ordinary chi-tail erased stock under the SAME return convention is the
stronger operational comparator: its exact expected preparation and iid
formation lie within O_D(1) of B_L and its complementary computational
readout has Qbar=0. Product-noise stock is a counterconstruction to a
structural inference, not a demonstrated lower-entanglement reservoir.
Its return delta_L=(1-D^(-2L))/L is comparable to ordinary stock return
(1-D^-L)/L; under an externally fixed error tolerance delta, choose L
large enough that the ACTUAL respective return is <=delta. The admitted
ordinary stock upper remains m/(2delta)+O_D(1), under the same global
initialization convention and the same deterministic/expected distinction.

A useful next theorem must quotient, control or charge irrelevant local
mixedness in a score-preserving way, or use a different converse that
avoids this entropy. It cannot conclude a useful certificate merely from
the largeness or smallness of the old unconditioned Q_* variable. No free
local preprocessing that preserves the complete Bell-prefix score has
been proved here. No generic resource advantage is inferred.
