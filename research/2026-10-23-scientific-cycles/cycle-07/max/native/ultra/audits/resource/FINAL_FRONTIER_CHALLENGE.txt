1. ZERO ERROR DOES NOT CONFUSE THE TWO RESOURCE CLASSES

In the compact expected LP,
 0<=v_s<=y_s<=p_s, sum_s y_s>=1-epsilon,
 sum_s[d_s y_s-(d_s-1)v_s]<=1,
 sum_s p_s=1 and d_s>1.
At epsilon=0, the cap and total-overlap constraints force y_s=p_s for
EVERY inventory. The mass constraint can then be rewritten exactly as
 1+sum_s(d_s-1)(y_s-v_s)<=1.
Every summand is nonnegative; all must vanish, so v_s=y_s=p_s. Thus
 C_av(0)=sum_s p_s ln d_s=r(L+1)ln D/2.
This is expected preselected supply, not deterministic exact supply.

For deterministic rank M, zero error in the exact curve requires
sum_s min(p_s,q_s f_s)=1 with sum q_s=1 and f_s<=1. Equality requires
q_s f_s>=p_s for all s and sum_s q_s f_s>=1. As the latter is also <=1,
every positive q_s must have f_s=1, and all target-positive inventory
values must be present. In particular p_(rL)=L^-r>0 and requires
M>=D^(rL). This proves exact deterministic cost rL ln D, consistently
larger than the exact expected cost. Requiring error EXACTLY zero at every
r is a different limit from allowing positive epsilon_r->0; in that
vanishing-error limit deterministic and expected per-copy rates both B.

2. INVENTORY AGGREGATION CANNOT HIDE INDIVIDUAL FLAG ERROR

For fixed s, all individual K with S(K)=s have the SAME active Bell
dimension d_s and the SAME fidelity cap f_(M,s). If their candidate pass
masses are c_K, then
 sum_(K:S=s) min(p_K,c_K)<=min(p_s,sum_(K:S=s)c_K).
This proves that aggregating flags is a valid converse relaxation.
It is EXACTLY attainable: for every preselected rank M and inventory s,
choose its individual K with probabilities p_K/p_s. The same relative
distribution is used on free and full branches. Then
 c_K=(p_K/p_s)c_s,
and every individual minimum becomes
 min(p_K,c_K)=(p_K/p_s)min(p_s,c_s).
Summing gives equality. Source charge is unchanged because all flags in
the inventory class use the same supplied rank. This uses finite shared
classical sampling, not an unavailable entangled operation.

Coherence between flag sectors cannot beat the curve: local dephasing
leaves the target invariant, preserves admissibility of the factory and
cannot increase half trace distance. Mismatched local flags are rejected
by the converse effect; their mass can be reassigned to a matched sector
without lowering overlap or increasing its supplied cost. All retained
flags remain present in the produced state and in the error test.

3. INDEPENDENT D=2,L=2,r=1 EXACT PRIMAL/DUAL

Here p_1=p_2=1/2 and d_1=2,d_2=4. Measure cost in m=ln2 units.
The compact expected LP becomes
 minimize c=v_1+2v_2,
 0<=v_i<=y_i<=1/2,

 2y_1+4y_2-v_1-3v_2<=1.

Its exact whole-error frontier is
 C_av(epsilon)/m=max{0,3/2-3epsilon}.                         (A)

DUAL CERTIFICATE. Every feasible point obeys the algebraic identity

 c-3(y_1+y_2)+3/2
 =[v_1+3v_2-2y_1-4y_2+1]+[1/2-y_1]+[y_2-v_2]>=0.

Together with overlap>=1-epsilon and c>=0, this proves the lower bound
in(A) without any optimizer or floating point calculation.

PRIMAL. For 0<=epsilon<=1/2, let a=1/2-epsilon and choose
 y_1=1/2, y_2=a, v_1=a, v_2=a.
Then overlap=1-epsilon, mass constraint is equality, all caps hold and
cost=3a. The corresponding supply masses are
 free rank1: 2epsilon, full rank2: a, full rank4: a.
They sum to one. For epsilon>=1/2 the wholly free rank1 branch assigned
to flag1 has half-trace error exactly1/2 and zero supplied entanglement.
Hence(A) is fully attained.

In particular at epsilon=1/4,
 (y_1,y_2,v_1,v_2)=(1/2,1/4,1/4,1/4), c=3/4.
