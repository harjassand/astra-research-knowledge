MATCHED CATALYTIC/EMBEZZLING BOUNDARY
22 October 2026. Bounded primary check, not an exhaustive optimality survey.

Our matched contract charges the initialization of EVERY initial shared
register, including any catalyst, and tests return on the COMPLETE retained
memory. The first Bell output is exact, the complete memory is normal, and
GLOBAL preparation error is separate from the one-step return error. With
vanishing global initialization error deterministic iid supply differs
from exact deterministic Schmidt-rank supply and exact preselected expected
supply. No label is erased merely because it is not observed in a test.

Jonathan--Plenio's original exact catalytic transformations can enable
otherwise forbidden LOCC conversions, but do not create an extra Bell pair
from a product system while returning a finite catalyst exactly: an
additive entanglement monotone, or the inherited repeated-prefix theorem,
rules that out under this contract. This does not deny their established
use when the transformed input itself supplies entanglement.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9905071

Van Dam--Hayden's universal family has squared Schmidt coefficients
1/(j H_n), 1<=j<=n. Their local rearrangement approximates the JOINT target
mu_n tensor Phi_D; its amplitude overlap is at least 1-ln D/ln n. Thus
joint half-trace distance is at most sqrt(2 ln D/ln n). This is a sufficient
approximation bound, not a lower bound on all embezzling protocols. Their
actual construction permits Bell-output error. It does not automatically
meet our exact-Bell-output task. Its initial shared family is charged:
exact one-shot Bell supply needs rank at least n, whereas iid vanishing-
error pure-state formation uses its Schmidt entropy. Substituting an
approximate Bell-output theorem without proving exact extraction would
silently change the task. No such substitution is made here.
Primary paper, Eqs1--4: https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0201041

More tailored exact-output reservoirs or approximate catalysts are not
excluded by an analogy to this family. Any construction satisfying the
matched contract obeys the inherited generic finite-copy supply converse
and, for exact preselected expected factories, the one-chain lower bound.
The product-noise queue provides a specific counterexample to SMALL-Q
STRUCTURE, not to those converses. It proves no advantage over ordinary
chi-tail stock. No strongest finite-size embezzler coefficient or new
noisy-output tradeoff is pursued in this audit.
