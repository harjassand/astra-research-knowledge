1. EXACT IID KOASHI-WINTER/HJW LAW

For finite omega_AB and purification psi_ABE, let rho_BE=Tr_A psi.
For sigma=omega^tensor r and a designated whole-task copy a, the right
side of the purification bipartition after tracing A_-a is

 rho_BE^tensor(r-1) tensor psi_(A_a B_a E_a).

The own-task pure factor lies entirely on the unmeasured right side.
Appending it and tracing it are local channels on that side and leave
E_F unchanged. HJW identifies Alice-complement POVM ensembles with all
pure ensembles of the remaining density. Refining a POVM to rank-one
outcomes cannot increase the objective average B entropy, by entropy
concavity. Thus optimizing all finite POVMs gives exactly

 inf_Y S(B_-a|Y)=E_F(rho_BE^tensor(r-1)).

Finite dimensionality permits the usual finite pure-ensemble convention
for this convex roof; there is no hidden restriction to product readouts.
All tasks have this same value, so their average equals it. Subadditivity
of E_F supplies the regularized limit through Fekete's lemma. No assumed
single-letter identity or additivity is present. This is a correct
receiving use of known KW/HJW machinery, not a cheap method for computing
the resulting E_F.

2. EXACT PRIVATE-NOISE GENIE BOUND

For N emitted slots in branch K=k, the Bob suffix of length (N-k)_+
has state (I_D/D)^tensor(N-k)_+ and factors from all other systems,
including ALL Alice registers. For r copies condition on the entire
actual sector vector Kvec; this is a mathematical cq extension, not a
claim that an extra immutable flag is retained in the physical device.
An arbitrary POVM on Alice complement, including a joint POVM across all
complementary copies, cannot change those independent Bob factors.

Writing n(Kvec)=sum_(b!=a)(N-K_b)_+, for each positive-probability
conditional outcome (Kvec,Y) one therefore has

 S(B_-a|Kvec,Y)=m n(Kvec)+S(B_remaining|Kvec,Y)
                 >=m n(Kvec).

The remaining conditional system is an ordinary finite quantum state;
its entropy is nonnegative. Strong subadditivity gives

 S(B_-a|Y)>=S(B_-a|Y,Kvec),

and averaging the last display over the actual joint law of Kvec,Y
returns the original uniform K marginals. This proves, for EVERY readout,

 Q_a >=(r-1)m b_(N,L),
 b_(N,L)=L^(-1)sum_(k=1)^L(N-k)_+.

The elementary sum is N(N-1)/(2L) for 1<=N<=L, and
N-(L+1)/2 for N>=L, agreeing at N=L. It yields
N=L: (r-1)(L-1)m/2, and N=L+1: (r-1)(L+1)m/2.
The proof applies without alteration after the GLOBAL Alice/Bob swap,
because the actual product-noise suffix is symmetric. Thus (G1)-(G3)
have the stated normalizations and include every mixed branch.

3. MATCHING LEADING UPPER BOUND

Measure every Alice-complement output register in the product
computational basis, separately for each complementary source copy.
In every branch, Alice's entire N-slot marginal is exactly I/D^N.
Therefore the observed computational string Y is uniform independently
of K: H(K|Y)=H(K)=ln L.

Conditioned on K and Y, Bob's Bell-prefix partners are pure known
computational states and his product-noise suffix remains maximally
mixed. Thus the mean conditional Bob entropy is exactly m b_(N,L).
The standard entropy-of-mixing inequality gives

 S(B|Y)<=H(K|Y)+S(B|K,Y)=ln L+m b_(N,L).

For the explicit product readout, the r-1 complementary copies remain
conditionally product, so conditional entropy adds for THIS admitted
readout. Infimizing over every readout is no larger. This proves (G4):

 (r-1)m b_(N,L) <= Qbar_*
                 <= (r-1)[m b_(N,L)+ln L].

The lower bound did not use product-readout optimality. The upper bound
does not assert that this readout is optimal. Together they fix the
leading growing scale at N=L or L+1 without solving a convex roof.
