1. EXACT IID SCALING

Let omega_AB be any finite single-task output, where one task is its ENTIRE
N-slot Alice/Bob output. Fix a purification |psi>_ABE and put
rho_BE=Tr_A |psi><psi|. For sigma=omega^tensor r, task a has complements
A_-a and B_-a. Define Q_a,* as the infimum, over all finite POVMs on A_-a,
of S(B_-a|Y). Then exactly

 Q_a,*(omega^tensor r)=E_F(rho_BE^tensor(r-1)),
 Qbar_*=(1/r)sum_a Q_a,*=E_F(rho_BE^tensor(r-1)).             (1)

For r=1 both sides are zero. Indeed the purification of sigma is
|psi>^tensor r. Trace A_-a, so the KW bipartition is
 B_-a : (A_a B_a E_all).
Its density is rho_BE^tensor(r-1) tensored with the pure own-task state
|psi>_(A_a B_a E_a), which lies wholly on the right of that bipartition.
Tensoring a local pure state preserves E_F, by adding that state to every
ensemble for one direction and locally tracing it for the other. KW gives
(1). No additivity of E_F has been assumed.

Writing E_F^infty(rho)=lim_n E_F(rho^tensor n)/n, subadditivity of E_F and
Fekete's lemma imply

 lim_(r->infinity) Qbar_*/(r-1)=E_F^infty(rho_BE).             (2)

The regularized quantity is the usual entanglement-cost quantity under
its standard asymptotic conventions. That known operational equivalence
is not needed below: a direct finite lower bound suffices. A bound on
Qbar_* independent of r thus cannot follow from iid structure alone.
Swapping Alice/Bob globally gives the same statements with rho_AE.

2. A FIXED, NORMAL, COMPLETE-MEMORY QUEUE

Fix D>=2, L>=1, m=ln D and q=D^-2. Each memory has L D-by-D pairs.
Let Phi denote the pure maximally entangled D pair and

 nu=(I_D/D)_A tensor (I_D/D)_B,
 omega_k=Phi^tensor k tensor nu^tensor(L-k), 0<=k<=L.

A particularly explicit counted memory includes a current shared finite
classical flag, encoded in local orthogonal bases:

 tau_L=(1/L)sum_(k=1)^L |k,k><k,k|_flags tensor omega_k.       (3)

Use the explicit faithful product external input eta=nu on one D-by-D pair.
Each party applies the same local cyclic register permutation: its first
memory register becomes the external output, its other L-1 memory registers
shift one place left, and its external input becomes the last memory
register. Update the CURRENT flags by k -> k-1 for k>1, 1 -> L. The flag
operation is a local permutation too. The entire reuse map is a fixed
finite-dimensional local unitary, hence a normal one-outcome finite-round
LOCC device. It has no internally discarded environment or unlisted local
purifier during reuse. There is no success postselection and no uncounted
catalyst or duplicate immutable historical flag.
All flags retained after a use are included in the compared memory.

Every initial sector k>=1 has first pair exactly Phi. Thus the first
external pair is exact Phi and, by purity, is uncorrelated with its entire
remaining memory. Following the local shift, the j<L flag sectors agree
exactly with their initial sectors omega_j. Only the new flag L sector
has omega_0 in place of omega_L. Therefore

 T(Phi_memory(tau_L),tau_L)
   =(1/L)T(nu^tensor L,Phi^tensor L)
   =(1-D^(-2L))/L=:delta_L.                               (4)

The final equality holds because nu^tensor L is the maximally mixed state
on dimension D^(2L). This is complete-memory half-trace return, including
both flags. Appending a separate immutable original K record WOULD change
the return; it is not part of this construction. See QUEUE_AUDIT.txt.

The source can sample K uniformly BEFORE choosing its Bell supply, supply
exactly K Bell-D pairs in that branch, prepare the other pairs locally,
and retain just the current flag used in (3). All branches are included.
This is the genuinely preselected expected-supply contract with

 C_sup=E[K]m=(L+1)m/2,  initialization error epsilon=0.       (5)

Deterministic supply Lm also prepares one copy exactly by discarding excess
Bell pairs. No claim equates those two resource classes. For r copies the
expected contract supplies r(L+1)m/2 exactly. A deterministic fixed-supply
factory with vanishing GLOBAL error is described in section7.

This is ordinary consumable/erased stock. It has the same leading supplied
cost as the strongest stock comparator, and does not manufacture a saving
by separately charging its mathematical purifying environment.

3. ACTUAL OUTPUTS AND PREFIX SCORES, NOT ONLY MARGINALS

For any horizon N>=L, after discarding the COMPLETE retained memory the
actual output state of (3) is

 omega_(N,L)=(1/L)sum_(k=1)^L Phi^tensor k tensor nu^tensor(N-k). (6)

For N<L the same expression uses Phi^tensor min(k,N) and
nu^tensor(N-min(k,N)); this is the output law used in (G1)-(G2).

The formula retains all interslot correlations. For r independent source
copies the full output is omega_(N,L)^tensor r. The exact Bell-PREFIX
probability for every i>=1 is

 p_i=(1/L)[(L-i+1)_+
          +sum_(k=1)^min(L,i-1) q^(i-k)].                   (7)

In particular a product-noise tail has NONZERO Bell overlap. It must not
be called a zero-score tail. The causal return guarantee still gives
p_i >= [1-(i-1)delta_L]_+, as can also be checked directly.

Because q^L<1/(L+1),

 ceil(1/delta_L)=L+1.                                      (8)

At this natural return horizon, omega_(L+1,L)=omega_(L,L) tensor nu.
Thus the last pair is an independent locally mixed product pair. Under
EVERY Alice-complement POVM in r copies, the r-1 Bob terminal systems
remain independent maximally mixed factors. Consequently, without using
KW or evaluating any convex roof,

 Q_a >= (r-1)m,     Qbar_* >= (r-1)m.                      (9)

The same holds after the GLOBAL Alice/Bob swap. The reservoir is symmetric;
choosing that other total penalty does not repair the obstruction. More
generally N>=L has N-L deterministic noise tail slots and gives
Qbar_* >= (r-1)(N-L)m. This is a real output uncertainty, but locally free
noise rather than an additional shared-entanglement supply.

4. STRONGEST DIRECT LOWER BOUND: PRIVATE-NOISE GENIE

This bound supersedes the logarithmic crosscheck in section4B. It is also
stronger than merely noticing a deterministic terminal-noise factor.
For any horizon N>=1 define the mean number of locally mixed emitted
suffix slots

 b_(N,L)=E_K (N-K)_+
   =N(N-1)/(2L),              1<=N<=L,
   =N-(L+1)/2,               N>=L.                       (G1)

For EVERY Alice-complement POVM on r copies, not only a product readout,

 Q_a >= (r-1)m b_(N,L),
 Qbar_* >= (r-1)m b_(N,L).                               (G2)

Proof. Introduce only as a proof device the classical preparation-sector
vector Kvec=(K_1,...,K_r), with its actual joint output extension. It need
not remain accessible after use and is not added to the physical device.
For the readout Y, SSA/data processing gives
 S(B_-a|Y)>=S(B_-a|Y Kvec).
Conditioned on Kvec, each Bob slot beyond its branch's Bell inventory is
an independent I_D/D factor. These Bob local-noise factors are independent
of ALL Alice systems, so even an arbitrary joint POVM on Alice complement
cannot steer them. For every Kvec,Y, their entropy contributes exactly
m sum_(b !=a)(N-K_b)_+. The remaining Bob state has nonnegative entropy.
Average over Kvec,Y to obtain (G2). This includes all mixed branches and
all interslot correlations. It uses no additivity hypothesis, no guessed
readout and no expensive convex-roof calculation. QED.

The same argument applies in the global opposite orientation because nu
is symmetric. In particular

 N=L:   Qbar_* >=(r-1)(L-1)m/2,
 N=L+1: Qbar_* >=(r-1)(L+1)m/2=(r-1)C_sup.                (G3)

Consequently the ACTUAL causal exact-first-output/complete-return structure
allows linear-in-L irreducible uncertainty at fixed r>=2, even in ordinary
stock with its stock-matched supplied cost. It also allows linear growth
with r at fixed L>=2, even after optimizing over every complementary POVM
and both global side orientations.

For scale, an explicit independent computational-basis Alice readout gives

 Qbar_* <=(r-1)[m b_(N,L)+ln L].                          (G4)

Conditioned on K and Alice's computational labels, each Bell slot fixes
its Bob partner and each local-noise slot leaves I_D/D. Their average Bob
entropy is m b_(N,L). Mixing K can add at most H(K)=ln L entropy. Perform
this readout separately for every complementary copy to obtain (G4).
Thus the lower bound determines the leading growing term without solving
the known E_F optimization. It is not a newly cheap exact E_F formula.

4B. SUBSUMED LOGARITHMIC ENTROPY CROSSCHECK

Even at horizon N=L, a different entropy argument gives a logarithmic
lower bound. The direct bound (G2) is stronger. This section is retained
as a check of the iid KW receiving map and the initial failed estimate,
not as the headline. No E_F
optimization, tomography, distillation claim or hashing theorem is needed.

Choose any orthonormal generalized Bell basis {beta_z}_(z=0)^(D^2-1)
with beta_0=Phi. Both Phi and nu are diagonal in that basis. Thus (6) is
diagonal in its product Bell basis. Let K be its uniform sector and Z the
N Bell-basis labels. Conditional on K=k, the first k labels are zero and
the other N-k labels are independent uniform D^2-label draws. Hence

 S(omega_(N,L))=H(Z)
   =(2N-L-1)m+ln L-H(K|Z).                               (10)

All Alice and Bob marginals are exactly I_(D^N)/D^N, so S(A)=S(B)=Nm.
For N>=L, labels after L are independent of K; put h_L(q)=H(K|Z_1...Z_L).
It is bounded by a D-dependent constant:

 0<=h_L(q)<=H_geo(q):=h2(q)/(1-q)
                 =-ln(1-q)-[q/(1-q)]ln q.                (11)

Proof of (11). Let G be the number of consecutive zero labels in an
infinite uniform-noise tail before the first nonzero label. It is geometric,
Pr(G=g)=(1-q)q^g, g>=0, independent of K. Let J be the first nonzero label
position in the L-slot observation, or L+1 if all observed labels are zero.
Set R=min(G,L-K). Then J=K+R+1, so K is determined by J and R. Therefore

 H(K|Z)<=H(K|J)=H(R|J)<=H(R).

Since E R<=E G=q/(1-q), comparison with the geometric law gives
 H(R)<=-ln(1-q)-E R ln q<=H_geo(q).
All R are finite, so Gibbs' inequality here requires no infinite-entropy
assumption. The first inequality used the coarsening Z -> J, not a claimed
efficient physical Bell measurement. This is an exact entropy calculation.

Now purify omega_(N,L) as |psi>_ABE and define rho_BE=Tr_A psi. For every
bipartite finite density theta_BE and every pure ensemble of it, conditional
entropy concavity gives

 E_F(theta_BE) >= S(E)_theta-S(BE)_theta.                  (12)

Indeed S(B|E)_theta >=sum_j w_j S(B|E)_(pure_j)
=-sum_j w_j E(pure_j); infimize over ensembles. Apply (12) to rho_BE^n.
The entropy difference is additive on tensor powers and, by purity,
S(E)_rho=S(omega_(N,L)) and S(BE)_rho=S(A)_omega=Nm.
Using (1), (10) and (11), for every r>=1 and N>=L,

 Qbar_*(omega_(N,L)^tensor r)
  >= (r-1)[(N-L-1)m+ln L-h_L(q)]
  >= (r-1)[(N-L-1)m+ln L-H_geo(q)].                        (13)

One may replace the bracket by its maximum with zero, and combine (13)
with (9). In particular

 N=L:   Qbar_* >=(r-1)[ln L-m-H_geo(q)],
 N=L+1: Qbar_* >=(r-1)[ln L-H_geo(q)].                     (14)

This independently confirms an unbounded penalty, but the direct private-
noise lower bound (G2) gives its correct leading scale. At fixed L large
enough, (13) also forces
E_F^infty(rho_BE)>0 and hence linear growth with r in the exact iid law.

This is NOT a supplied-entanglement saving. Equations (4)-(5) give

 C_sup = m/(2delta_L)+m/2 - [m L q^L/(2(1-q^L))]
       = m/(2delta_L)+O_D(1).                             (15)

The construction already pays the ordinary stock cost. A necessary large
Q_* condition for possible savings is therefore far from sufficient; the
same condition can be caused by private, locally generated mixed tails.

5. OPTIONAL EXACT FINITE FORMULA FOR THE POSTERIOR ENTROPY

For s>=1 let h_s be the entropy of the truncated geometric law
p_g=(1-q)q^g/(1-q^s), 0<=g<s. The first nonzero index satisfies
 Pr(J=s+1)=(1-q^s)/L, 1<=s<L,
 Pr(J=L+1)=(1-q^L)/(L(1-q)).
Given each J, the posterior K is the corresponding reverse truncated
geometric law. Therefore exactly

 h_L(q) = sum_(s=1)^(L-1) [(1-q^s)/L] h_s
          +[(1-q^L)/(L(1-q))] h_L.                       (16)

The h_L on the right denotes the truncated-geometric entropy, whereas
h_L(q) on the left denotes the posterior entropy defined in section4;
the replay calls these h_trunc and h_posterior to avoid ambiguity.
No quantum matrix optimization is performed. replay.py enumerates the
small classical Bell-label laws only as an admission check, and compares
their entropies with (10), (16), the geometric bound and actual (7).

7. SEPARATE DETERMINISTIC IID FACTORY AND ITS ACTUAL ERROR

For fixed L, r, draw K_1,...,K_r independently uniformly on {1,...,L}.
Choose an integer S<=rL and begin with deterministic Phi_(D^S), equivalently
S Bell-D pairs. If total K<=S, prepare the exact sampled branch of (3)^r
using total K pairs and locally discard unused supply. If total K>S,
prepare a specified product fallback memory (with the same finite flags).
This is a finite normal LOCC map with ALL outcomes included. It can prepare
the local mixed tail nu in every fallback branch as well. The output memory
is within GLOBAL half-trace

 epsilon <=Pr[sum_j K_j>S]
         <=exp[-2(S-r(L+1)/2)^2/(r(L-1)^2)]                (17)

when S>r(L+1)/2 and L>1; L=1 needs no overflow. This is the ordinary
bounded independent-variables Hoeffding bound. One can take
S=ceil(r(L+1)/2+(L-1)r^(2/3)), capped by rL. At each fixed L the supplied
rate S m/r tends to (L+1)m/2 and epsilon tends to zero. This is a global
error statement, not a sum of uncharged per-copy errors.

The ideal product-output lower bounds (G2) and (13) are not silently
transferred to the actual finite-r imperfect initialization. Such a transfer
would require a dimension- and error-dependent entropy/conditional-entropy
continuity bound. However the simpler terminal-tail bound (9) DOES hold
on the actual output of this deterministic factory at N=L+1: every branch,
including fallback, has a final locally appended nu pair, independent of
the other output and all flags. This already rules out a uniform-in-r
Qbar bound at deterministic stock-matched asymptotic supplied rate, with
its finite global initialization error explicitly paid. No assertion about
the error-free deterministic single-copy cost has been changed.

8. WHAT THIS CLOSES, AND WHAT REMAINS

Closed: a theorem that actual exact-first Bell extraction plus complete
trace return alone forces Qbar_*=O(1), uniformly in iid copy count and/or
return horizon, is false. An explicit finite ordinary queue already has
the required growing irreducible quantum penalty in both global side
orientations, without relying on a poor chosen readout.

Not closed: the unrestricted critical Bell-prefix hierarchy, logarithmic
formation gap, an optimized causal-resource normalization, or existence
of a reservoir with genuine stock-beating supplied cost. Large Q_* alone
does not distinguish a saving from locally mixed erased stock. A receiving
theorem must eliminate or charge its private-noise contribution while
retaining the actual Bell-prefix task, or find a different structural
quantity tied to supply. The principal develops that next receiving step.

Internal correctness: complete analytic argument, independent queue audit,
bounded exact/classical replay. Historical novelty: not established;
KW/HJW, convex-roof conditional-entropy lower bound, geometric entropy and
ordinary queue preparation are known machinery. External acceptance,
physical usefulness and full discovery costs remain unknown. No hardware,
spending, paid credits, public disclosure or external outreach occurred.
