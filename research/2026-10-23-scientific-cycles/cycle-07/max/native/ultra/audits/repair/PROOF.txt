1. A USEFUL PENALTY FUNCTION AND ITS EXACT ELEMENTARY PROPERTIES

For any finite bipartite state rho_AB define
 q(rho)=inf_{finite Alice POVM M} sum_y p_y S(rho_{B|y}).
The choice of Alice is fixed globally. Allowing the globally swapped version
makes no difference in the symmetric constructions below.

(a) q is concave. For every fixed POVM, cq conditional entropy is concave,
so for rho=sum_j t_j rho_j and every M,
 Q_M(rho)>=sum_j t_j Q_M(rho_j)>=sum_j t_j q(rho_j).
Take the infimum over M. This also proves the statement when no optimizer is
attained. No E_F additivity, Koashi-Winter optimization, or product readout
assumption is needed.

(b) q is invariant under local unitaries. Conjugate Alice's POVM and Bob's
conditional states. Therefore every random product-unitary channel U satisfies
 q(U(rho))>=q(rho), by (a).

(c) For rho=Phi_D^{tensor k} tensor nu_D^{tensor(n-k)}, with
 nu_D=(I_D/D)_A tensor(I_D/D)_B, one has q(rho)=(n-k) ln D.
For ANY Alice measurement, Bob's n-k private white-noise slots remain an
independent I/D tensor factor, so every conditional entropy is at least
(n-k) ln D. Measuring the k Bell slots in a common computational basis makes
the rest of Bob's conditional state pure, attaining equality. The same argument
works for a tensor product of branches with different k's: the private-noise
counts add. Alice's measurement may act jointly across every branch/copy.

                    - sum_{i=L-c+1}^L d^{-(i-1)}/(d-1)].

4. UNIVERSAL EXACT BELL-PRESERVING SEPARABLE NORMALIZATION IS RIGID

KNOWN RIGIDITY LEMMA (special case of Gheorghiu--Griffiths, arXiv:0705.0369,
Theorem 1; short self-contained proof retained). Let Lambda be a CPTP separable map on C^d tensor C^d, with the SAME
input and output local dimensions, such that Lambda(Phi_d)=Phi_d. Then Lambda
is a random matched bilateral-unitary channel:
 Lambda(rho)=sum_j w_j (U_j tensor conjugate(U_j)) rho
                                  (U_j tensor conjugate(U_j))^dagger.
Consequently it is unital, fixes I/d^2, and by 1(b) cannot decrease q.

PROOF. Use product Kraus A_j tensor B_j. Since the output on Phi_d is pure,
 A_j B_j^T=c_j I for every j, and sum_j |c_j|^2=1. Trace preservation gives
 sum_j ||A_j||_HS^2 ||B_j||_HS^2=d^2.
For c_j!=0, A_j is invertible and B_j^T=c_j A_j^{-1}. If s_1,...,s_d are
its singular values, Cauchy-Schwarz gives
 ||A_j||_HS^2||B_j||_HS^2
 =|c_j|^2 (sum s_l^2)(sum s_l^{-2}) >=d^2 |c_j|^2,
with equality iff every s_l is equal. For c_j=0, each nonzero product Kraus
has strictly positive squared HS norm. Summing the inequalities already equals
d^2, so every nonzero c_j branch saturates and every c_j=0 product Kraus is
zero. Thus A_j=a_j U_j and B_j=b_j conjugate(U_j), with
|a_j b_j|^2=|c_j|^2=w_j. This proves the asserted representation. QED.

Finite-dimensional separable maps admit finite product-Kraus representations;
the theorem includes finite-round LOCC and the finite-dimensional LOCC closure.
It does NOT mean every map that merely maps separable states to separable states.
Changing dimensions, keeping accessible flags, approximate fidelity preservation,
or preserving only one averaged mixed queue state require separate arguments.

A proposed state-independent operation that preserves all Bell-prefix scores
on all admissible full-Bell vertices necessarily fixes the full Phi_{D^N}
vertex. On one task it therefore cannot remove q or private white noise.
Applying such channels separately on the r tasks leaves each complementary
q nondecreasing, since their product is a random product-unitary channel on
the complementary Alice/Bob state. Joint channels mixing task definitions are
outside this last inference. This applies a known pure-state SEP rigidity theorem to retire a universal exact
normalizer shortcut;
it is NOT an obstruction to every state-dependent score-controlled operation.

6. ACTUAL-QUEUE PAIRWISE REPAIR BOUNDARY (FULL NATIVE PROOF ATTACHED)

The proof is not a mere application of the universal full-Bell vertex. For a

 Delta_pair <= -(alpha-beta/D)(1-a).

The coefficient threshold is sharp: the local product reset has
