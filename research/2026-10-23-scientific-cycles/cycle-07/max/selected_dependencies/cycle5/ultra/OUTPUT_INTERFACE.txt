1. TARGET AND SUPPLIED MODEL

The target is a lower certificate for E_F of the ENTIRE finite output sigma,
and consequently for its supplied Bell-entanglement budget under the stated
preparation protocol. It uses the multiscale score O and complementary
readout cost R=sum_a R_a from PROOF.txt. The finite statement is
 E_F(sigma)/r >=Tr(O sigma)/r-Tr(R sigma)/r-C_D.
It holds for every sigma, not merely successful measurement branches.
No exact-support or spectral-gap measurement is needed.

The fixed mathematical operators, their POVM normalization, local circuit
implementation and accuracy are inputs. Generic dense measurements or
conditional predictors can have exponential description/synthesis cost.
The experiment below has not acquired a calibrated physical device.
It does NOT certify complete-memory return delta, global memory preparation
error epsilon, missing retained shared registers, or untrusted initial supply.

2. SCORE TRIALS: KNOWN LOCAL DIRECT-FIDELITY METHOD

Independently prepare the SAME full sigma each time. Arbitrary correlations
inside each rN-output state are allowed; independence is required across
these full-state repetitions for the concentration statements.

Choose (a,i) uniformly among rN prefixes. Choose a tensor-Weyl operator
W uniformly among the D^(2i) operators of that prefix. Alice measures W,
Bob measures conjugate(W), with common shared setting. Let Z be the real
part of the product of their unit-modulus eigenvalue outcomes. Then
 Z in[-1,1], E Z=p_(a,i),
because Phi_(D^i)=D^(-2i)sum_W W tensor conjugate(W). The variable Nm Z
estimates Tr(O sigma)/r and lies in[-Nm,Nm]. Hence sufficient preparation
count for additive precision zeta_O and error probability beta_O is
 n_O>=ceil[2(Nm)^2 ln(2/beta_O)/zeta_O^2].
At fixed D sampling a tensor-Weyl string takes O(N) setting symbols and at
most N single-D-site measurements per party. No enumeration of the whole
Weyl table is required. Gate precision/calibration remain separate costs.

3. LOSS TRIALS: FINITE LOCAL READOUT WITH A FROZEN PREDICTOR

Choose a uniformly random task a for each NEW full-state preparation.
For the classical predictor version, Alice measures complementary POVM A_y,
Bob complementary POVM B_z^T, and they record
 c_a(y,z)=ln[k_(a,z)/pi_a(z|y)].
The rank k of every specified effect is known from the supplied model;
frequencies do not infer the rank of an arbitrary unknown effect.
If 0<=c_a<=B, the random variable is in[0,B] and its mean is exactly
 Tr(R sigma)/r. The sufficient count for precision zeta_R is
 n_R>=ceil[B^2 ln(2/beta_R)/(2 zeta_R^2)].
The certificate is the observed score mean minus its error, minus the
observed loss mean AND its error, minus C_D. A union bound gives confidence
at least 1-beta_O-beta_R. Estimating the UNNORMALIZED sum R to fixed
absolute precision instead has the r^2 range factor; it is not the formula
above. When B=0 the loss is identically zero.

For the conditional quantum-reference version Alice measures A_y and
communicates y; Bob measures (-ln omega_(a,y))^T on the complement. The
same formula holds with B=max_(a,y)[-ln lambda_min omega_(a,y)]. This is
finite one-way LOCC. An arbitrary dense spectral measurement is not thereby
an efficient physical circuit. Bob's conditional reference must be normalized
and strictly positive on the entire relevant complement; tiny eigenvalues
are paid in B or require a proved tail bound.

Freeze the predictor/reference before these certification trials. Learning
it requires separately charged training and independent validation, or a
proved uniform statistical correction. Nothing permits optimizing the same
unpenalized finite sample and calling its training loss an entropy bound.

Each trial consumes an ENTIRE sigma preparation, including r memory
initializations and rN extraction blocks in the finite-device protocol.
Total preparations are n_O+n_R (plus any training), not one reused memory
state. Individual task POVMs need not commute because only one setting is
used per new full-state preparation. No destructive support test is applied
first, and no accepted subensemble replaces the unconditional sigma.

4. KNOWN EFFECT ACCURACY

Suppose an implementation estimates actual observables tilde O/r and
tilde R/r with certified operator-norm errors
 ||(tilde O-O)/r||_infinity<=a_O,
 ||(tilde R-R)/r||_infinity<=a_R.
Then subtract a_O+a_R in the preceding per-task certificate. This follows
directly from |Tr[(tilde A-A)sigma]|<=||tilde A-A||_infinity. Obtaining these
operator-error bounds/calibration is charged additional work; none has been
acquired here. If the apparatus has drift or correlated repeated full-state
preparations, the iid confidence statement needs a replacement model.

5. MISMATCH/FANO OPTION AND NECESSARY ALPHABET LIMIT

A fixed decoder with Bob alphabet size L and error f=Pr(Z!=g(Y)) gives
 H(Z|Y)<=F_L(f), where F_L(u)=h2(v)+v ln(L-1),
 v=min(u,1-1/L), L>=2. L=1 has zero conditional entropy.
Use an upper confidence bound u on f. Direct Bernoulli estimation with
independent full-state trials gives
 u=min(1,f_hat+sqrt[ln(1/beta)/(2n)]).
For zero observed failures the exact binomial upper is 1-beta^(1/n).
If separate task estimates are used, charge every task setting and a union
confidence allocation. Under a common L and uniformly sampled task, Jensen
concavity upperbounds the AVERAGE entropy by F_L of the average failure.
Weighted ranks must still be measured/charged; different alphabets require
their actual weights or a stated common upper bound.

A finite known alphabet or a specified predictor tail is essential. A
rank-one product-state tail law saturates h2(f)+f ln(L-1) with arbitrarily
small f and large entropy. Any uniform confidence rule claiming small
entropy after n all-correct samples must tolerate that record's probability
(1-f)^n under this alternative. Without a known alphabet there is no finite
uniform entropy upper from such data. For large specified L and constant
tail allowance c, the worst-case zero-error sample scale is at least
order ln L ln(1/beta)/c; exact binomial inversion attains this scale.

For a positive predictor over L outcomes, max[-ln pi]>=ln L necessarily.
A floor pi_theta=(1-theta)pi_model+theta/L gives max loss <=ln(L/theta),
but both its actual mean loss and range are charged. Thus a short scalar
readout does not hide the alphabet, model-description or acquisition cost.

6. WHAT THE MEASUREMENT DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

The universal operator inequality is exact within the supplied finite model.
The explicit local trials certify its expectations only with their stated
sampling and known-effect assumptions. The formal optimized quantum penalty
is a known entanglement convex roof under Koashi--Winter; it need not be
cheaply inferable. It is output correlation structure, not an additional
independently priced memory resource.

For the finite reuse consequence, evaluate/bound the loss on the ACTUAL
initialized output sigma. If only its ideal state's loss is known, a
separate transfer bound paying the observable range is needed. The stated
N epsilon term transfers the Bell score only; it does not transfer an
unknown predictor loss for free. All retained memory correlations were
present before the final local discard, which cannot raise E_F.

No laboratory data, hardware purchase, paid credits, public disclosure or
performance advantage was obtained. The replay uses declared finite matrices
to check the new POVM identities; it is not empirical calibration or the
cost of scientific discovery.
