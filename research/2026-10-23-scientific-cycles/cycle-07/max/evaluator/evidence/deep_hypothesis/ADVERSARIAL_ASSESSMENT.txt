ASTRA CYCLE7 — INDEPENDENT INVESTIGATION OF SCIENTIFIC TASTE AND INVARIANTS
22 October2026, Australia/Brisbane

DECISION
Preserve the autonomous scientific architecture. Do not promote a learned
intrinsic-interest score or a generic premise-relaxation recipe into Astra's
central scientific objective. Both remain possible mechanisms, but neither
currently identifies the capability the user wants: generating consequential
new questions, methods and discoveries that change future possibilities.

This assessment executed an exact mathematical identification test, acquired and
inspected a new primary source's actual TeX archive, and transcribed its stronger
pre-promotion comparison. It did not run a new AI model experiment, verify the
external paper's generated Lean proofs, or establish an amplification advantage.

WHAT THE CURRENT NATIVE EVIDENCE CHANGES

1. Pro1's route is not simply a weaker hypothesis. Its old average-over-prefixes
   calculation failed to establish a worst-prefix operator sign. The successful
   step minimized over all prefix density matrices and used a closest-point
   inequality. That is a changed mathematical operation controlling an actual
   adversary. It simultaneously required a combinatorial guard/meander identity
   and uniform nonlinear bounds. 'Relax the bottleneck' does not generate these.
   Sources: native Pro1 FAILED_ROUTES.md and COMPLETE_PROOF.tex.txt.

2. Pro2's family has a real all-depth positive invariant, but the supplied graph
   grammar deliberately controls series parity. The failed even-series rule
   feeds large boundary masses into a slot that is subsequently amplified.
   This is useful new mathematics, yet success on that grammar does not settle
   the original all-partial-two-tree question. It is a counterexample to any
   narrative that increasing theorem depth alone implies increasing reach.
   Sources: native Pro2 PROOF.md sections2--4,7 and CONTINUATION.txt.

3. Pro3's weighted absorbed estimate captures a real coefficient absent from an
   unweighted estimate. It changes what must be bounded, and proves the actual
   full-chain law rather than a norm witness. The old interior proof cannot be
   copied at its singular endpoint. This is productive invention of a functional,
   not increased effort on the previous proxy.
   Source: native Pro3 FAILED_APPROACHES.md and GLOBAL_BOUNDARY_THEOREM.md.

4. Ultra's raw uncertainty functional can increase because of private noise in
   an admitted ordinary-stock architecture. Its optimization over readouts does
   not solve the issue. A counted clock enables a repair, so 'irrelevant noise'
   cannot be deleted by silently granting access. Scientific language has to
   represent resource and access distinctions correctly.
   Source: native Ultra FAILURES_AND_DECISION.txt and PROOF.txt.

5. Dots obtained a real measured comparison after source access changed. The
   argument accepts a scoped whole-device outcome without needing to identify
   every microscopic rate, but preserves unknown wall-plug input and durability.
   This is evidence-route selection. A new internal representation alone would
   not have supplied that measurement.
   Source: native Dots nitrogen_cycle6_20261022/PROCESS_RECORD.txt.

These are observational reconstructions. They do not causally demonstrate one
research strategy, nor do they prove historical novelty or independent expert
acceptance. The shared lesson is narrower than a universal recipe: a research
operation must control distinctions that matter to its actual target, and should
not spend its proof budget controlling distinctions that the target tolerates.
Discovering that boundary is a substantive mathematical or empirical problem.

WHY THE OBVIOUS NEW TESTS FAIL ADMISSION

A. Nuisance-invariance prompts on Ultra's already-known queue would mostly ask
   agents to rediscover a supplied counterexample. Any useful new universal
   normalization would instead duplicate Ultra's owned frontier. A fair control
   must already include ordinary resource monotonicity and optimal readout.

B. Quotienting by arbitrary 'free operations' is invalid. Reversible free
   transformations define an equivalence; one-way free transformations generally
   define only an order. A quotient that makes all local noise additions/removals
   interchangeable can grant unavailable control or erase usable side information.
   Ultra's controller-assisted repair is a concrete reason to retain this issue.

C. A microwave-efficiency partial-identification experiment would reproduce the
   strong ordinary robust/interval optimization already called for by Dots and
   resembles Cycle5's rejected information-design pilot. It cannot qualify a new
   discovery method unless it generates a genuinely different admitted inference
   or measurement operation and survives the same-data conventional optimum.

D. Root's crossed-producer DGM test would distinguish a useful causal question if
   executed with adequate resources. But it remains a coding-improvement proxy,
   not a test that scientific questions or invariants improve. It deserves no
   automatic priority merely because the earlier source audit exposed a confound.

NEW MECHANISM INVESTIGATED: LEARNED SCIENTIFIC TASTE

Primary factual capsule (all facts producer-reported, not new replication):
Patel et al., arXiv:2609.28603v1, define I=100V/L from conditional proof cost and
statement/definition length, and library compression utility U=dV using direct
use count d. They report Spearman.756 on declarations with positive utility.
Their appendix includes raw-proof-length promotion as a control. Before a round's
selection, generated verified candidates have mean I2.332 for interestingness
promotion versus1.694 for proof-length promotion. The paper itself leaves the
future usefulness of generated theorems unresolved. No full row dataset or actual
model/code release was recovered in bounded retrieval. The original29-member
TeX source and official HF metadata are preserved, with exact hashes.

This is a serious candidate mechanism. It attempts to choose novel questions,
not merely solve a fixed benchmark. The pre-promotion comparison is important:
it is stronger than the tautology that selecting high scores yields high scores.
It indicates a changed candidate distribution under the reported trajectories.
Do not dismiss it as pure selection leakage or compare only to random choice.

EXECUTED RESULT

The exact Cartesian test in taste_signal/exact_shared_factor_test.py exhibits
400 synthetic positive-use entries with
  Spearman(I,U)=0.9996624796079071,
  Spearman(I,d)=0,
and exact independence between I and d. The construction varies both proof cost
and statement length. No parameters were fitted to the published.756.

For fixed statement length and n proof-cost levels separated by factor C>m,
with every use count1,...,m at every level, an elementary exact proof gives
  rho(I,U)^2=m^2(n^2-1)/(m^2*n^2-1).
The limit is1 despite exact I-d independence. A separate generic rank calculation
checks343 parameter pairs. Adding uniformly distributed zero-user entries leaves
the conditional positive-use correlation unchanged, while the fraction ever used
can be arbitrarily small. All assertions passed. This is exact synthetic evidence
about an inference, not a reanalysis of empirical Mathlib associations.

This countermodel does NOT refute the paper's compression objective. I can be a
good predictor of U precisely because V is genuinely costly at every reuse.
Nor does it prove that the actual correlation is entirely mechanical. It shows
why a high I-U correlation cannot identify a propensity for future receiving use,
let alone growing capability to invent future scientific breakthroughs. A central
scientific-value policy needs evidence beyond that correlation.

A further distinction: even accurate prediction of future use need not exhaust
foundational mathematical significance. A major theorem can matter independently
of an immediate receiver. Conversely, repeated use in a self-generated family
can be cheap, narrow and circular. No scalar reuse or interestingness metric is
being offered here as the replacement objective.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE DECISION

The strongest next evidence is a prospective intervention on which scientific
abstractions are pursued, with actual independent consequences. Freeze a candidate
pool and the source context. Let independently owned receivers specify difficult
scientific obligations before candidate promotion. Compare the learned taste rule
against unrestricted informed scientific judgment and the strongest explicit
compression/cost rule using the same candidate pool and information. Charge
candidate construction, formalization, proof search, retrieval and integration.
Test whether selected results enable a previously unattained receiving theorem,
construction or decision, then whether the receiver can use that gain to generate
another useful method. Negative and null receipts remain part of the experiment.

This is a proposed test, not an authorized new centrally selected Pro programme.
Its immediate admission bottleneck is a source-complete candidate population with
known generation cost and independent non-saturated receiving obligations. The
current paper's aggregate tables do not supply that population. Astra's actual
Cycle6 results can seed such work, but blindly shuffling existing proofs would
not count as a new discovery test. A future source-complete opportunity may be
stronger than this proposal; no fixed framework should become mandatory.

STRATEGIC CONSEQUENCE

The evaluator should study the discovery of representations, definitions,
experiments and useful questions as first-class scientific outputs. Scientific
intelligence can improve by gaining the ability to formulate a distinction or
invariant that was previously unavailable, even when model weights do not change.
That is plausible from Astra's owner results. It is not established merely by
observing their succession, and it needs a receiving consequence to demonstrate
system-level amplification.

The key change from Cycle6 is to expose selection of scientific value as a
separate unresolved mechanism. Improved result generation does not guarantee
improved selection, and improved selection on an intrinsic score does not prove
improvement in the invention of consequential science. These are separable
operations whose interactions, not independent proxy wins, need investigation.
