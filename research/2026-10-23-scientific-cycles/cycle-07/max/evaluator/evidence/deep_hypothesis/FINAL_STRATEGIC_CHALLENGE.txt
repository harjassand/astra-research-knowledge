ASTRA CYCLE7 — FINAL ADVERSARIAL STRATEGIC CHALLENGE
22 October2026, Australia/Brisbane

PRIMARY CHALLENGE
Preserving autonomous owners is well supported. Making learned scientific taste
the evaluator's next main programme is not yet supported. It is a serious
candidate, but we currently lack a source-complete candidate population, an
admitted learned selector and a consequential independent receiving experiment.
Another round of criticizing proxy metrics could become the same drift we are
trying to correct: increasingly precise statements about what progress is not,
without developing an operation that changes what Astra can discover.

WHAT IS POSITIVE DESPITE THE NEGATIVES
The interestingness study has a stronger result than immediate selection on its
own score. Its appendix's verified candidates before promotion have mean ratio
2.332 under interestingness promotion versus1.694 under the raw-proof-length
policy. This supports the possibility that the accumulated premise environment
changes what subsequently gets generated. It does not establish improved
scientific usefulness. The native Astra developments provide a different positive
signal: operators, boundary interfaces and weighted functionals have actually
changed the set of provable statements. These are more promising starting assets
than a generic argument that creativity can be optimized.

The useful common object may therefore be a generative abstraction: a definition,
proof operation, representation or constructive rule that makes an entire class
of future questions accessible. A theorem can embody such an operation without
being reducible to its use count or proof length. That is an inference from the
records, not a demonstrated universal law of discovery.

WHAT THE TESTS DO NOT ESTABLISH
The shared-factor countermodel refutes an inference from correlation to reuse
propensity; it does not refute prediction of the stipulated compression utility.
A path-dependent difficulty label need not be useless for a fixed proof pipeline;
it is not automatically an estimate of minimum necessary reasoning. Complementary
lemmas can make singleton values misleading; that does not show a practical
bundle selector can find valuable combinations cheaply. An admitted queue whose
raw uncertainty grows because of private noise refutes that proxy's uniformity;
it does not prove that a cheap invariant normalization exists. Collectively these
tests do not show that a new scalar, richer representation, larger context or
more agents will improve discovery. They also do not discredit cumulative
mathematics, intrinsic foundational importance or the ordinary value of reuse.

STRONGEST COMPETING EMPHASIS
The strongest alternative is to increase Astra's ability to construct and verify
new scientific languages and intermediate operations, leaving scientific taste
primarily with autonomous researchers. Present owners are already finding useful
questions and substantial constructions. The bottleneck may be converting their
informal insight into an operation that can be searched, challenged and applied
reliably beyond the originating proof, rather than learning which result to like.

A concrete example is discovering an invariant of a recursively composed system.
A successful abstraction must both be preserved by admissible compositions and
imply the desired global conclusion. It may require a new state variable or a
new functional, not a longer proof in the old vocabulary. Astra's new prefix,
boundary-mass and absorbed-process arguments make this alternative credible.
It can fail: encoding the problem can cost more than solving it; an invariant
language can exclude the decisive idea; ordinary abstract interpretation, duality
or expert mathematical reasoning can already be stronger. Those are strong
controls, not reasons to assume a bespoke agent architecture is necessary.

PROPOSED ROOT EXPERIMENT: INVENT A REUSABLE PROOF OPERATION
This is proposed, not executed, and does not assign or reopen Pro science.

Use completed, source-complete Astra obligations as an admission stage: the
worst-prefix operator sign, recursive boundary-mass control, and weighted
absorbed-process inequality are three candidates. Select only an obligation
whose exact definitions and finite operations can be translated faithfully into
a checked local interface, and whose conventional solution is not a trivial
one-line solver call. Do not select whichever gives the intervention a win.
The historical proof is a validation reference, not new-discovery ground truth.

The intervention is to have an internal investigator construct a reusable
operation: explicit input conditions, an algorithm or exact symbolic map, and a
proof-producing output. It may introduce a new auxiliary variable or lemma.
The strong controls are unrestricted direct mathematical reasoning with the same
source information, and the best applicable ordinary symbolic/convex/invariant
synthesis method. Compare actual verification of a fixed scientific obligation,
not prose quality, stylistic similarity to the historical proof, or benchmark
scores. Charge representation construction and all failed searches.

Then change the target in a way fixed before the candidate operation is exposed:
a nontrivial parameter family, composition or uncertainty model that the original
proof does not already settle. Root must choose a receiving obligation outside
the owners' active frontiers, with an exact source contract and a genuine
scientific consequence. An independent receiver may use the operation and its
full stated premises; the control receives the same underlying scientific
information and comparable resources. Require a proved extension or a certified
failure, not finite numerical success alone. If no such receiving obligation
can be identified without duplicating an owner, the trial is not admitted.

A first positive result would be a new operation that changes which receiving
question can be resolved. A second phase would ask the receiver to derive a
further useful operation and compare it with the original capability. This tests
a candidate amplification step rather than merely counting theorem dependencies.
It uses existing models, local CPU/exact arithmetic and available primary sources;
it needs no training run, external contact or hardware expenditure. Start with
one operation, not a new platform or a large synthetic curriculum.

FEASIBILITY AND STOPPING
This experiment is feasible in principle under current tools but not yet ready
to launch as a fair comparison. Its scientific contract and strong conventional
control must be established first. Stop if the ordinary method already solves
all receiving instances easily; if the invented operation simply restates the
supplied proof; if interface formalization silently supplies the decisive insight;
or if the purported receiver is only the originating task with renamed symbols.
Those gates prevent another saturated reasoning-style experiment.

RECOMMENDATION
Treat learned taste and generative abstraction as competing hypotheses, not as a
new master doctrine. Prioritize whichever first acquires a source-complete,
consequential receiving test with a serious comparator. On present evidence,
generative abstraction has stronger native support; learned taste has promising
external evidence but weaker immediate experimental readiness. Preserve the
owners' current missions and autonomy while root takes responsibility for this
harder, narrower scientific admission decision. No present result establishes
recursive growth, a reliable general creativity score, or a route to exponential
scientific progress.
