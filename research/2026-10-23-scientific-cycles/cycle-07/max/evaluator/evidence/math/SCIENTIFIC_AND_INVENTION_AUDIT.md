## Pro1: actual delta and why it matters

    A_k=(ln2)v_k/8,   k^(3/2)/32<=v_k<=k(k-1), k>=4.

Here v_k minimizes the weighted square of a contracted input observable over
EVERY bath-prefix density. The closest-point condition yields an operator
inequality, including for rank-deficient minimizers. It is not a favorable
prefix average or a prepared new initializer.

A new guarded-token mechanism supplies an all-length lower independent of
the complete prefix. Uniform positivity, entropy and exact nonlinear drift
then allow k=floor(beta^(-8/17)/8), fixed before the horizon. For every fixed
beta<=2^-11 the actual achievable rate is at least

    3/2+(ln2/4096) beta^(64/17).

The same initializer-independent code works pointwise for every fixed normal
bath state in full diamond norm. The actual tau_pi also has the stated product-
block coherent-information limit. Thus this is a rate theorem, not merely
formal susceptibility growth. It eventually exceeds the fixed-block quartic
order under that comparator's exact input-class assumptions. It is not a full
capacity converse, sharp exponent, efficient coding theorem or useful device
performance. The former beta=1/512 test point is outside the new explicit
range. There is no terminal bath-return requirement.

The entire proof chain demonstrates actual use of accumulated capital:

## Pro2: actual delta and why it matters

Cycle5 characterized coordinate-design operator stability by bichromatic cut
mass and exposed why local stability does not automatically survive gluing.
Cycle6 supplies a particular global remedy: three terminal colors rotate
across parallel joins, resetting the low boundary-mass slots. Odd series
composition preserves their orientation. The result is K<=13 at every depth
for the admitted degree-four, triangle-free, chordless series-parallel grammar,
and hence ||Dhat-D||op<=5||E||F from three full-original actions. Cyclic members
exclude every real two-query design, so the worst-case query count is sharp.

This is an all-depth constructive theorem, not a truncation or large-graph
experiment. It has explicit supplied-expression input and charged construction.
The 359- and 218-vertex exact rational receiving calculations validate the
actual operation and produce smaller instance-specific squared constants17
and19. Independent no-producer-import checking reconstructed the stored
supports, actions, projection and error quantities. No downstream scientific
application or total-cost advantage was tested.

The counterexample to naive even-series composition is equally consequential:
properness, acyclicity and terminal separation all survive, yet boundary mass
grows exactly 2^h and the cut inverse diverges. This shows the productive
invariant is more specific than 'use an acyclic coloring' or 'combine stable
pieces.' It rejects the rule, not those graphs or all three/four-query designs.

The full treewidth-two, degree-four arbitrary-real four-versus-five question
remains open. Three-query forest and exact one-subdivision methods are strong
controls on their own subclasses; five queries remain the full-class stable
control. Generic exact acyclic-three identification does not by itself prove
uniform operator-noise stability. No historical priority was established.

## Challenge to the previous 'sufficient route' explanation

It fits important decisions: Pro1 accepts k^(3/2) rather than requiring the
unproved k^2 conjecture; Pro2 permits bounded trees rather than requiring stars.
But it does not explain the principal inventions. Removing an overstrong
condition alone does not produce the input-only swap identity or the global
reset recurrence. Calling all successful steps 'finding a sufficient route'
risks becoming true by definition and therefore untestable.

A sharper retrospective description is that each owner found a restricted
family whose internal mechanism is exact enough to survive a difficult global
quantifier: arbitrary prefix states in Pro1, arbitrary recursion depth in Pro2.
The uniform error/interface proof converted the family into the actual target.
This remains an observation, not a validated universal discovery method.

The causal question for meta-research is what changes the probability of
inventing such a family while retaining those quantifiers, not whether the
result can be summarized afterward using a common vocabulary. Existing records
cannot answer that probability question: failed candidates are incompletely
logged, all-information counterfactuals are missing, and total inference costs
are unknown. A new experiment must generate a genuinely new mechanism or
discriminating artifact and show independent receiving benefit. Merely replaying
these now-known solutions would be a contaminated success test.
