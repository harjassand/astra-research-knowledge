EXACT SHARED-FACTOR COUNTERMODEL

This is an elementary newly executed identification test. Historical mathematical
novelty is not claimed. It is not a model of actual Mathlib and not a theorem
about the scientific value of a particular generated theorem.

Let n>=2, m>=2, and use the uniform population of pairs (j,d) with
j=0,...,n-1 and d=1,...,m. Let C>m, V=C^j and L=100. Define
I=100V/L=V and U=dV. Thus I and d are independent by the Cartesian construction.
Every proof-cost level has every use count exactly once.

Since C>m, every U at level j precedes every U at level j+1. The midrank of I
at level j is R_I=mj+(m+1)/2. The rank of U at pair (j,d) is R_U=mj+d.
Centering ranks, R_U-center=(R_I-center)+(d-(m+1)/2), with independent summands.
Consequently
  Var(R_I)=m^2(n^2-1)/12,
  Var(R_U)=(m^2*n^2-1)/12,
  Cov(R_I,R_U)=Var(R_I),
so
  Spearman(I,U)^2=m^2(n^2-1)/(m^2*n^2-1).
This approaches1 as n grows even though I and d are independent, not merely
uncorrelated. At fixed m the separation C can be chosen arbitrarily above m.

The executable check independently computes generic exact rational midranks,
covariances, and joint count factorization. It verifies the formula at343 pairs
(n,m), n=2,...,50 and m=2,...,8. The fixed C=10 satisfies C>m throughout.

The primary reported finite witnesses also vary the statement length over
L in{100,200}. Each (j,L,d) combination occurs. Independence remains exact.
The ratio and product retain strong rank correlation because V remains the
common broad ordering variable. These are prespecified cases, not fitted to
the primary paper's empirical correlation. Their squared correlations are
  n2:64/105;
  n5:4096/4389;
  n10:69696/70889;
  n50:44408896/44438889.
All have exact I-d rank covariance zero and exact joint independence.

Adding any number of d=0 copies with the same V,L distribution preserves the
conditional d>0 statistic. Therefore that conditional statistic cannot, by
itself, identify the fraction of candidate theorems ever reused. It says nothing
about whether zero-user theorems are intrinsically interesting or might later
become useful; those are different questions.

WHAT THE COUNTERMODEL DOES AND DOES NOT PROVE

It proves that high correlation between I and U is insufficient to infer that I
predicts the reuse count, the chance of future receiving use, or the ability of
a discovery process to generate future useful breakthroughs. The proof applies
even without proof padding, judge error, stochastic variation or model leakage.

It does NOT refute I as a predictor of the explicitly defined U. In this family
I actually predicts U well: high V genuinely makes each use more expensive to
rederive. Compression is a legitimate objective. The result does not show that
the actual primary study's empirical correlation is driven only by V, that its
learned model is ineffective, or that its generated mathematics is unimportant.
Those empirical claims need source rows and independent receiving outcomes.

This distinction matters to Astra: substituting 'library proof compression' for
'capacity to discover important new science' is a change of objective. The former
may be a useful enabling mechanism, but the latter does not follow from a
shared-factor correlation, a graph of dependencies, or a rising optimized score.
