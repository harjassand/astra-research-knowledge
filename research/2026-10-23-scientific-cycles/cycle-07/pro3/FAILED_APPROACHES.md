# Failures, rejected inferences and scope decisions

This record preserves failures that affected the Cycle7 construction. It is
not a claim that every conceivable approach was tried. The complete earlier
record remains in the unchanged Cycle6 archive.

## Counting columns do not survive general proposals

The symmetric proof's counting-column premise cannot be reused for arbitrary
row-stochastic Q. A column can receive flow from arbitrarily many rows. The
new exact 18-state fixture makes an absorbed conjugated local column equal to
5, exceeding the old bound 2. This is a genuine failed premise, not numerical
noise or an adverse mixing theorem by itself.

The repair is to apply Jensen with reverse-proposal coefficients Q_yx. Their
column total uses one outgoing row of Q. After taking the pth power, the
coefficient is Q_xy a_xy^p r_q^(-theta p). The actual rejection diagonal then
subtracts accepted outflow. This proves the weighted energy statement; merely
observing the conjugated off-diagonal bound would not do so.

## Detailed balance is insufficient

Putting a proposal-ratio error in one factor and compensating in the second
factor still preserves target reversibility. That fact says nothing adequate
about proposal-start finite-time transport. The contributed q-only screen
admits a many-to-one family with a positive retained-error limit. The main
theorem therefore claims portability for the actual full reciprocal-flow
screen, not all reversible factorizations of Hastings acceptance.

## Overweighting the reverse proposal is also unsafe

The many-to-one example alone does not establish the full two-power result.
For kappa>eta the principal uses the opposite geometry: one root feeds many
leaves. Native proposal and target ratios are computed per vertex. The retained
event is the complete leaf block, since global proposals can move from one
leaf to another. Replacing block escape by a single leaf's diagonal would
lose valid paths and would not prove the stated full-law sandwich.

## Small mismatch is not uniformly small without a proposal budget

The ceiling m=ceil(n^(4 eta/abs(eta-kappa))) gives the exact inequality
1<=S<=2^abs(eta-kappa). Thus bounded nonzero mismatch can be arbitrarily small
and still cancel the screen on an admitted growing support. This disproves a
uniform-continuity intuition for the unrestricted class. It does not establish
instability on a fixed graph or an ordinary floating-point error theorem.
The state-index and proposal-description costs grow as mismatch shrinks.

The selected next question adds an honest supplied proposal-asymmetry bound.
No sharp theorem for that additional contract is claimed in this return.

## A norm bound is not a lower construction

The main theorem uses the new finite operator bound for the upper only. Its
matching lower transfers the unchanged symmetric native instances by exact
kernel inclusion, including their true diagonals. The companion's different
lower uses native finite positive distributions, an admitted fixed Q, the
entire KL and a proved exact quotient/full-return sandwich. No induced-norm
extremizer is treated as reachable retained mass. No two constructions spend
the same KL budget twice.

## Execution and unselected scope

Both newly frozen rational diagnostics passed on first execution. No failed
scientific run or post-freeze source/input repair is omitted. A second frozen
diagnostic was added for the genuinely new fan-out geometry. A documentation
patch was rejected for a missing patch-line prefix; it changed no scientific
source or output and was corrected before packaging.

The review suggested that an unbounded-mismatch policy might be addressable
by another geometry. This return does not select or accept that extension.
The fixed classification is stated for 0<eta<1 and finite kappa>=0; its
moving-policy obstruction assumes bounded absolute mismatch. Neither an
eta>=1 classification nor sufficiency of eventual matching for vanishing eta
is asserted. The positive alternative-class floor is a lower bound, not an
exact minimax floor.

## Comparators and preserved closures

The same-input mismatch examples favor it strongly. Analytically available
direct block sampling is also allowed. These results are robustness theory,
not an implementation speedup, target-call benchmark or physical experiment.

The native critical laws, first-order laws, fixed-screen, prefix, optional-
prefix and earlier joint-KL questions stay closed. The B3 pISO correction,
failed full halo gate, metadata-admission and R3/anMC closures are preserved.
Protected owners and retired thermal, TPV, half-cell, molecular and GUIDE
operations were not reopened. No unrelated meta-study comparison was rerun.
