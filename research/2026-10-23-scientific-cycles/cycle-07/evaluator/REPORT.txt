ASTRA — SCIENTIFIC INTELLIGENCE AMPLIFICATION, CYCLE 7
Independent assessment and executed meta-research
22 October 2026

DECISION

Continue the restored scientific architecture and the owners' substantive
programmes. Change the evaluator's research emphasis: investigate how Astra can
invent useful new representations, operations and questions, and how that ability
could learn from subsequent science. Do not install an intrinsic-interest score,
a prescribed reasoning sequence, or a centralized question selector on the
strength of the present evidence.

This round establishes several precise limits on proposed learning signals and
produces a new, independently reviewed stress fixture in Astra's actual quantum
memory model. It does NOT demonstrate a new discovery strategy outperforming a
strong conventional method. That distinction is a principal result, not a
qualification to be hidden behind the amount of work completed.

EXECUTIVE ASSESSMENT

A. Where is Astra today?
Astra has functioning, cumulative mathematical research programmes, a useful
scientific counterexample programme, and a persistent physical investigation
which has improved its connection to experimental evidence. Several original
constructions have complete internal arguments and receiving calculations.
External correctness acceptance, historical priority and broad practical
consequences remain separate, largely unresolved questions.

B. Are we progressing toward revolutionary discovery?
We are making substantive scientific progress, especially in mathematics. We
have not demonstrated a trajectory to revolutionary discoveries or a recursively
improving invention rate. The correct alternative to exaggeration is to retain
the growing programmes and investigate the missing mechanism, not to erase
their value or repeatedly start new domains.

C. What capabilities improved?
The current sources support stronger same-memory coding constructions, stable
recovery on an unbounded recursively generated graph family, a completed native
sampling phase law, and sharper rejection of an entanglement-resource argument.
Dots has moved from an unqualified modeled quench toward analysis of a relevant
operating device. These are specific expanded capabilities and knowledge.

D. What is the most consequential strategic weakness?
We do not yet know how experience improves the generation and selection of
future discoveries. Accumulated theorems can make later proofs easier without
making the system better at inventing the next consequential object or question.
The available internal scores and retrospective stories do not bridge that gap.

E. Continue, redirect or rebuild?
Continue the autonomous architecture; redirect the evaluator's research toward
this missing generative mechanism. A rebuild or takeover of scientific problem
selection is not supported. Nor is a claim that the current architecture has
already been causally validated against alternatives.

F. What is the most important next action?
Keep the owner programmes continuous while testing whether Astra can acquire a
new scientific operation that enables an independently selected subsequent
result. Its benefit must survive an equally informed conventional control and
full acquisition/integration costs. Improving a proxy or compiling more facts
does not meet that test. The evaluator owns this meta-research; scientific owners
do not become its benchmark workers.

2. THE SCIENTIFIC DELTA THROUGH CYCLE6

Pro1: a stronger operation, not merely another bound

The same evolving quantum bath is retained; a fresh-channel tensor-power model
is not substituted. The new full-prefix variational coefficient obeys

    k^(3/2)/32 <= v_k <= k(k-1),       k >= 4.

Guarded input words with nonnegative middle paths give an all-length lower
construction. A closest-point dual argument makes it uniform over coherent
prefix states, and growing-block nonlinear control transfers it to actual
same-bath coding. For fixed 0 < beta <= 2^-11, the resulting reliable rate is

    Q >= 3/2 + (ln 2 / 4096) beta^(64/17).

This improves the attained small-angle power beyond the previous fixed-block
quartic construction. It is not the sharp capacity exponent, an efficient code,
or a guarantee at the old beta=1/512 point. The physical root correction,
initializer quantifiers and full-diamond error are retained. Review corrected a
bulk-versus-whole-bath scope issue. The actual frontier is sharp growth of v_k,
between k^(3/2) and k^2, or a more consequential owner-chosen extension.

This is evidence of genuine capital accumulation: the new combinatorial and
operator construction uses the earlier coding bridge to obtain a stronger
operational result. It is not evidence that the evaluator's last prompt caused
that construction, or that future constructions will become progressively easier.

Pro2: an unbounded family with a stable constructive inverse

The current operation uses three graph-adapted queries, with total Euclidean
query row norm at most one, for arbitrary real symmetric coefficients and free
diagonals. It controls operator reconstruction error by five times the original
action's Frobenius error on an all-depth recursive graph family. The grammar
allows varying parallel arities and arbitrary odd series lengths. A boundary
rotation/reset invariant keeps bichromatic components bounded while preserving
connectivity; the retained least-squares tree inverse then supplies the recovery.

This extends beyond a finite gadget or a collection of bounded pieces. Saved
359- and 218-vertex full-action rational receiving calculations were independently
reconstructed. The unrestricted treewidth-two, degree-four arbitrary-real
four-query question remains open. The exact failure under a naive even-series
extension rejects that coloring rule, not all designs on those graphs.

The important invention is the realizable recursive grammar and its boundary
invariant, not a larger numerical test. A scalar score on isolated theorems could
miss the complementarity between that grammar, the connectivity proof and the
existing stable inverse. The owner's frontier concerns a valid transition for
both series parities or another route to the full recovery question.

Pro3: a completed native law with an honest conventional comparison

The remaining boundary critical law is now internally closed, including its
equality case. For fixed K>0 and 0<alpha<1, in the regime
r0 = alpha exp(-K)/beta >= 1, beta=1-alpha, and
eta_n log n = log log n + s + o(1), the reported exact native minimax law is

    (log n) R -> max{K, (beta/alpha)(exp(K)-1) exp(-s)}.

The decisive operation is a weighted absorbed functional that incorporates
global escape and accounts for the complete kernel after hits. The equality
boundary is not inferred by continuity from an unproved interior estimate.
The matching three-state construction is adverse to the hybrid compared with
ordinary independent Metropolis-Hastings. This is meaningful robustness theory;
it does not claim a faster sampler. Direct finite enumeration is also admitted
where appropriate. The owner's next declared broader question is whether
proposal symmetry is structurally essential. There is no reason to assign
another native-case audit instead.

Ultra: a live conjecture fails, and the resource question becomes sharper

Ultra's counted cyclic-clock queue makes its raw optimized complementary
uncertainty grow while matching ordinary consumable Bell supply. Complete return
error includes the clock, and the order of limits is explicit. A tempting
uniform defect estimate is therefore unavailable. A repair using an accessible
current clock is possible only under its actual access/reset interface; it is
not a generic hidden-memory operation. The valid witness and completed R1
results remain intact. The generic logarithmic formation-cost gap remains open.

This is productive negative science: it attacks a load-bearing universal
argument with an admitted construction and compares it against the correct
resource. It also illustrates a meta-research danger. A mathematically harder
defect need not mean a harder or more valuable resource task. This motivated
the new native controlled fixture in Section4.

Dots: a better evidential route, without a practical-advantage claim

Dots recovered a published electrodeless microwave split-feed experiment under
relevant operating conditions. Same-total-feed, same-reported-power comparisons
support approximately 13–18% less reported power per NOx. This is an existing
human experiment recovered and analyzed by Astra, not a newly invented or
physically replicated apparatus. Total electrical input, sustained material
temperature and heat disposition remain unresolved [2].

Additional native arguments matter. Atom conservation gives a bounded
concentration-to-product correction under explicit species assumptions. A
continuous, nondecreasing response assumption yields a conditional matched-output ordering, but
an explicit counterexample rejects a claimed fixed 100 W matched-output saving.
A total-input inequality displays exactly how source efficiency and auxiliaries
could reverse the apparent benefit. A simulated late-dilution comparator is not
silently promoted to a measurement.

This challenges a simple retrospective narrative. Earlier work exposed missing
mode-resolved chemistry; the useful next evidential step was not necessarily to
solve all of it. Whole-device measurements can answer a defined capability
question without identifying every microscopic rate. They do not license a
mechanistic transplant into a different geometry. Dots retains the physical
mission and chooses its next admissible operation.

Max: actual continuity, with acceptance boundaries preserved

Max's Cycle6 integration covered the six Cycle5 returns and the preceding
evaluator checkpoint. It could not scientifically accept concurrent Cycle6
results before they existed. Its private draft/patch is real; the recorded
public repository head remained unchanged. The current six returns are now the
next integration input. Effective retrieval and corrections are useful, but file
counts, copied archives and delivery receipts are not discovery amplification.

3. WHAT THESE HISTORIES CHANGE ABOUT THE DIAGNOSIS

The programme is no longer adequately described as a sequence of disconnected
exclusions. Pro1 and Pro2 now supply positive, unbounded constructive mechanisms;
Pro3 closes a substantial native law; Ultra produces a decisive admitted
counterexample; Dots obtains more relevant physical evidence. Foundational
mathematics has value independent of immediate hardware, and the absence of an
application is not grounds to terminate those programmes.

However, better scientific results and better production of future discoveries
are distinct. The strongest observed chain is currently:

    retained proof or operation -> actual reuse -> stronger receiving result.

The unestablished chain is:

    experience -> improved invention/selection mechanism -> better next
    operation -> independent transfer -> further improvement of the mechanism.

The first can occur with fixed scientific intelligence and ordinary memory.
It is worthwhile. It does not by itself establish the second. Nor does a fixed
model using an ever-growing library imply an exponential growth law.

The previous evaluator's emphasis on reformulating a question into a sufficient
route was a useful description, but not a demonstrated invention procedure.
The real new mechanisms were concrete: guarded temporal words, a full-prefix
dual, a boundary-state grammar, and a weighted absorbed energy. The phrase
'reformulate' neither predicts them nor supplies their proofs. Earlier failed
prescribed-strategy comparisons are a reason to stop promoting such descriptions
into universal instructions, not a reason to abandon research on invention.

Warm continuity is correlated with these developments: owners retain detailed
counterexamples, exact interfaces and unfinished constructions. There is no
matched experiment showing warm branches universally beat fresh instances, or
that extra subagents caused the gains. The visible division of bounded proof
obligations and specific reviewer corrections supports selective independent
review. It does not justify maximal parallelism or repeated whole-project audits.

More reasoning cannot identify an unmeasured physical loss by itself. More
precise simulation of a chosen closure does not identify that closure. Conversely,
formal domains provide strong internal tests but can attract work because those
tests are available. We should preserve both mathematical exploration and
empirical contact, without inferring that either domain is intrinsically the
best route to the user's long-term ambition from this small selected history.

4. EXECUTED META-RESEARCH: CAN SCIENTIFIC TASTE BECOME A LEARNING SIGNAL?

The substantive new hypothesis examined this round was that Astra could learn
which unknown mathematical statements or constructions are worth pursuing, and
use that learning to improve future discovery. This is more ambitious than
changing prompt order or making handoffs shorter. It is also dangerous if an
internal surrogate is mistaken for future scientific value.

A recent primary proposal, Learning to Discover Interesting Mathematics [1],
uses proof cost V and description length L for an intrinsic score I=100V/L;
its downstream utility is U=dV, with d the number of direct users. Its reported
candidate-generation effect warrants serious attention: a pre-promotion mean
score of 2.332 versus 1.694 for the proof-length control. Future usefulness of
the generated mathematics remains explicitly unresolved. I recovered the
official source and tested implications relevant to Astra rather than treating
this report as a ready-made autonomous selector.

Test A: strong apparent validation can contain no reuse information

I constructed exact finite Cartesian populations where V spans scales, L takes
two values, and d takes four values independently. On 400 items, the rank
correlation between I and U is 0.99966248, while I is exactly independent of d.
The ordinary V-only control correlates even more strongly with U: 0.99981248.
Adding arbitrary zero-use counterparts preserves the positive-use correlation
while making the overall fraction ever used arbitrarily small.

This is a mathematical countermodel, not an empirical reanalysis of the paper.
It does not make U meaningless: a costly lemma really can save more when used.
It establishes that correlation with a utility containing the same cost factor
does not alone validate prediction of reuse, consequential novelty or improved
future discovery. Producer exact code and an independently implemented root
rank/independence calculation agree. No actual Mathlib row or model was claimed
to behave like the constructed population.

Test B: the described label can depend on history rather than the task

Under the source's stated premise-expansion rule, a shared lemma can be
reintroduced after it was expanded. A fixed target, fixed final premises and
fixed proof DAG then receive different labels depending on expansion order.
With two dependents the costs are 23 or 43; with eight, the possible costs run
from 29 to 169. The latter count covers 5,160,960 legal schedules via 758 exact
dynamic-program states. Ordinary shared-DAG memoization gives 29.

The gap can be arbitrarily large in the constructed family. This is an
as-written algorithm challenge, not proof of a flaw in unreleased implementation
or a frequency estimate for the training data. A visited-node cache, different
hoisting convention or richer history input could avoid it. Actual expansion
records and executable training code were unavailable in this investigation.

Test C: even exact useful-lemma value is not an independent scalar property

Small exact proof systems show both complementarity and substitution. Two
lemmas can each save nothing alone and save four steps together; alternatively,
each can save four alone while the pair still saves only four. Thus the usual
proof-composition and premise-monotonicity axioms imply neither submodularity
nor supermodularity of downstream savings. This does not make learned ranking
useless, but removes an unjustified guarantee for isolated-lemma acquisition.

The attempted positive demonstration failed a stronger conventional control.
Acquiring the complementary pair costs eight; subsequent target proofs cost one.
For five targets the total is 13 versus 25 if each target is solved separately.
But an equally informed ordinary shared-DAG planner also costs 13. The apparent
amplification is ordinary reuse, not superiority. All acquisition costs were
charged, and the unsuccessful comparison is retained.

These finite tests know the rules and targets. They do not measure the difficulty
of inventing a new rule, representation or target. That limitation points toward
the more consequential unresolved problem, rather than toward a victory claim
for a revised internal metric.

Test D: a controlled nuisance intervention in Astra's actual quantum model

To avoid stopping at synthetic score examples, I commissioned and independently
checked a new exact family using Ultra's admitted queue. The intervention changes
private local tail noise while fixing the receiving resource/task values.

Take D=4, L=N=2048 and two copies. Use strictly positive product tails

  A: p=(1/2,1/6,1/6,1/6), q=(1/10,3/10,3/10,3/10);
  B: p=(1/4,1/4,(3+sqrt3)/12,(3-sqrt3)/12),
     q=(1/5,1/5,(3-sqrt3)/10,(3+sqrt3)/10).

Every paired product p_i q_i equals 1/20. In the same counted-clock shift
circuit, both constructions therefore have the same exact first Bell output,
all complete-prefix Bell-test probabilities and complete one-use return error

    delta = (1 - 20^(-2048))/2048.

Their entire formation resource/error frontiers are equal, not just a selected
upper bound: clock-conditioned local tail replacement maps either ideal
formation target to the other. Composition and trace-distance contractivity
transport every admissible global-error preparation protocol in both directions,
with identical specified incoming entanglement costs. Exact expected supply is
2049 ln(4)/2 nats per copy.

Nevertheless, the complementary conditional-entropy penalty differs even after
optimizing all collective complementary readouts and choosing the better global
Alice/Bob orientation. Rational enclosures prove

    Q_A in [1271.650978029009, 1279.275597015169],
    Q_B in [1289.297403767484, 1296.922022753644],
    Q_B - Q_A > 10.021806752315 nats.

The proof conditions on a mathematical inventory extension for the entropy lower
bound; it does not add an uncounted physical memory flag. A computational-basis
measurement gives an upper bound without claiming it is optimal. Exact algebra
in Q(sqrt3), integer-square-root enclosures and outward rational logarithm bounds
certify the separation. A separate analytic reviewer found no load-bearing gap,
and root's independent high-precision arithmetic corroborates the intervals.

Scope matters. The locally supplied input and general output/memory processes
differ. The formation conversion is not hidden runtime access, and local
preparation/discard is free only for the declared entanglement resource, not
thermodynamic work. Equality of the specified frontier does not imply equality
for every physical task. A non-invariant loose witness can still be valid, and
studying that witness may have mathematical value. What fails is identifying a
larger optimized defect with improvement in the matched resource capability.

The strongest conventional control—ordinary resource evaluation using the same
information—already recognizes the equivalence. This test therefore supplies a
new reusable stress fixture, not a superior discovery algorithm or a solution
to Ultra's generic formation gap. Ultra may use it when it bears on a scientific
decision; no receiving improvement is claimed before that occurs.

5. WHAT IS NEW, WHAT FAILED, AND WHAT SHOULD CHANGE NOW

Established in this round:

* A structural separation between a strong interest–utility correlation and
  information about future reuse, with a successful simpler conventional control.
* An exact expansion-order challenge to treating the described label as a
  state function of a theorem and its premises.
* Exact complementary/substitutable proof values and a failed amplification
  demonstration after charging acquisition and allowing ordinary shared work.
* A native admitted quantum family with equal complete formation/error frontiers
  but strictly separated fully optimized uncertainty penalties.

These are new calculations and arguments in this investigation. Historical
originality of the general mathematical phenomena is not claimed. The native
fixture's derivation is new here, while its queue and entropy mechanism inherit
Ultra's capital. Internal review is not an external correctness certificate.

Not established:

* A learned scientific-taste policy better than expert or conventional selection.
* A causal benefit from warm continuations, agent counts, longer context or
  a prescribed invention strategy.
* A new physical nitrogen-fixation advantage, quantum device or efficient code.
* Recursive scientific intelligence growth, much less an exponential rate.

The immediate methodological change is narrower than a new architecture: do not
promote retrospective compression, difficulty, theorem count or proxy growth to
a validated future-discovery reward. For a proposed capability claim, use an
independent receiving consequence and let the conventional control share the
same facts, representations and acquired capital. If the scientific object can
be changed while the receiving task remains the same, test whether the claimed
progress is only a representational or private-noise effect.

This is not a universal requirement that every foundational theorem have an
immediate receiver. A theorem can be valuable for conceptual explanation,
structure, generality or resolution of an independently important question.
The requirement applies when claiming research amplification or a particular
capability gain. Scientific taste cannot be reduced to the measured engineering
benefit of the next use, but that does not justify calling every difficult
mathematical object a step toward revolutionary technology.

6. THE DEEPER POSITIVE OPPORTUNITY AND ITS ALTERNATIVES

The most promising interpretation of the current successes is that scientific
intelligence is constrained partly by what operations and representations it
can formulate, not only by how hard it searches over known ones. Pro1 constructed
new input-word families; Pro2 constructed a new compositional graph language;
Pro3 changed the analytic object so escape could be used rather than bounded
away. These are candidates for increasing the set of expressible solutions.
They do not prove a general mechanism for learning such changes.

An ambitious next meta-research question is consequently: can a system acquire
an operation that changes what a later, independently chosen investigation can
construct, and can experience improve the acquisition of the next operation?
This includes invention of definitions, representations, interventions and
theorem schemas, not only conjectures in a fixed vocabulary. A compression
signal might help propose candidates, but the evidence does not justify making
it the sole learning objective.

The adversarial strategic review challenges even making learned taste the next
main programme: we lack a complete candidate population, an admitted selector
and a consequential receiving test. I accept that challenge. Learning scientific
taste and learning useful generative operations remain competing hypotheses.
The latter currently has stronger native support, but neither is a permanent
doctrine. Another cycle of proxy criticism alone would not satisfy this mission.

The experiment should be prospective and source-based. A completed operation
can supply the admission-stage interface; it is not an unseen discovery target.
Root must then identify a consequential receiving obligation outside the owners'
active frontiers, preserving its source-complete checkpoint before the answer
exists. Both conditions receive the same pre-existing scientific capital,
underlying facts, established tools and ordinary memoization. The proposed new
operation is the increment being tested, and its invention/implementation cost
is charged. If both conditions also receive that increment, the trial instead
measures execution or planning; it must not be called acquisition amplification.

For a general performance claim, fix the allowed variation and selection rule
before inspecting the operation. An independently chosen adversarial variation
after it freezes is still useful, but measures robustness rather than a general
improvement rate. Charge verification, retrieval and integration as well. Measure
a complete admissible result, not solver success on an easy surrogate. An
operation that merely encodes the originating answer should fail the variation;
a useful construction should supply a defensible route. Root owns the bounded
trial without interrupting owners or retrospectively claiming a holdout from
already-read results. No such prospective trial is scientifically admitted yet:
the source contract, consequential receiver and strong comparator still have to
be established without duplicating an owner's frontier.

Such an experiment can establish transfer, not recursive amplification. The
next necessary generation would let the acquired operation help create a
second operation and test its independent use under the same accounting. A
predefined sequence of generations, transparent failures and matched conventional
controls would be required before a growth claim. The present cycle has not
executed that test, and the scientific task must be genuinely consequential
before allocating a large trial. Naming this construction is not its solution.

The strongest alternatives remain serious:

Concentrated foundational mathematics. This has the strongest actual progress
in Astra and merits uninterrupted owner effort. It can yield independently
important results even if general intelligence never improves. Its limitation
for the long-term system claim is that a succession of clever proofs is not
automatically a learned invention mechanism. The evaluator should investigate
those proofs without forcing them into a common research agenda.

Better empirical access. Dots shows why relevant whole-device evidence can be
more valuable than more precise simulations under unmeasured closure assumptions.
Astra should continue exploiting accessible primary data and complete operating
contracts. No free tool can supply an unmeasured material loss, sustained power
measurement or clinical endpoint by reasoning alone. New laboratories, spending
and outreach are not authorized, so an experimental platform is not a currently
available replacement strategy.

Reusable computational or formal instruments. They can change feasible science
when a concrete numerical or verification bottleneck exists. The strong control
already gets mature solvers, shared proof graphs and established methods. Earlier
molecular and current proof-planning comparisons caution against claiming value
from a locally favorable certificate or deliberately forgetful baseline. Do not
build a platform first and search for a scientific need afterwards.

Autonomous open-ended proposal search. Primary work on learned taste, expanding
principle spaces and discovery models makes this a credible research direction,
not science fiction [1,3,4]. Yet discoverability assumptions, bounded domains,
intrinsic rewards and unavailable training evidence matter. No locally available
implementation has demonstrated the broad operation Astra needs, and this round
does not authorize expensive training or substitute a literature claim for a
receiving result. The appropriate investment is a scientifically discriminating
operation experiment, not wholesale installation of a new controller.

More agents or centralized grand-challenge allocation. Neither is supported as
the decisive change. More candidates help only when the system can represent a
fruitful question and distinguish its consequences. The present successful
owners have accumulated detailed scientific judgment. Reassigning them because
a named grand challenge has enormous impact would exchange actual developing
opportunities for unmeasured promises. Their autonomy is a substantive asset,
although its superiority has not been causally isolated.

7. NEXT ALLOCATION AND CONTINUATION STANDARD

Six complete prompts accompany this report. Each embeds the actual starting
contract, current result, failures, source identity, open frontier and receiving
requirements. They are continuations, not releases from a mission or compulsory
short-term completion gates. The researcher may choose a more consequential
question within its role, explaining the scientific reason.

Ultra continues the generic entanglement-formation question after its queue
counterexample, or another justified extension of capital. The new private-noise
fixture is an optional test, not a demand to solve an evaluator-designed puzzle.
Its output must retain complete-memory, global-error and supply conventions and
compare against ordinary stock and relevant catalytic constructions.

Max integrates actual current proofs, failures, corrections and receiving uses,
including this evaluator's negative meta-results, under the existing authorized
private repository workflow. No new public release, platform, owner experiment
or scientific acceptance by copying is implied.

Pro1 retains the same-bath programme and its owner-selected sharp temporal-growth
frontier. Pro2 retains sparse symmetric recovery and its owner-selected valid
boundary-transition/full four-query question. Pro3 retains autonomous sampling
research and its owner-selected proposal-asymmetry extension. Their prompts do
not prescribe a shared reasoning strategy or a new scientific domain.

Dots retains independent nitrogen-fixation research, beginning from its current
observed electrodeless operation and explicit missing complete-input/heat-path
facts. It may develop a complete admissible route or choose a stronger justified
operation. It is not ordered to repeat an old one-temperature trajectory, infer
missing measurements, or spend another allocation merely locating a supplement.

Root retains ownership of scientific-intelligence research and its experiments.
The next test is worthwhile only if it attacks creation of a useful new operation
on a real scientific frontier. A marginal prompt benchmark, retrospective
success story or equivalent-to-conventional instrument should be reported as
such and not adopted as a breakthrough.

Evidence that would change this recommendation includes: a replicated causal
gain in generating consequential constructions under equal information/cost;
a learned proposal signal predicting independent future use beyond its shared
factors and strong controls; repeated transfers enabling a second generation
of new operations; or an independently important scientific opportunity whose
feasible evidence access clearly dominates the owners' current frontiers.
Conversely, failure across such trials would narrow or reject the proposed
generative-learning mechanism. It would not retroactively nullify the mathematics.

8. REPRODUCIBILITY, COSTS AND DELIVERY BOUNDARIES

The accompanying common package includes the six unchanged original archives,
Pro2's unchanged required predecessor, clean starting seeds, focused evidence
audits, new exact scripts, proofs, independent review and root cross-checks.
verify_package.py checks content identity; replay_meta.py runs only the new
bounded meta calculations in a temporary copy. It does not restart an owner's
simulation, re-certify every original theorem, train a model or use the network.

Representative script-body costs were about 0.98 seconds for the shared-factor
test, 0.005 seconds for finite proof planning, 0.35 seconds for rational queue
verification, 0.36 seconds for root's independently implemented rank check, and
0.002 seconds for a high-precision arithmetic cross-check. These are not total
research costs, model-inference costs or a speed comparison with other scientists.
They will vary on replay. Reading, mathematical invention, review and integration
costs remain unknown rather than assigned invented token/dollar estimates.

The fixture was selected after exploratory floating-point screening. Its exact
verification is not a blinded prospective success-rate experiment. The paper
audit had no raw per-theorem corpus or released implementation; the native queue
proof is family-specific. Complete processes and thermodynamic costs were not
equated. Failed and weaker comparisons are retained. No owner was activated by
publishing these prompts, and no scientific acceptance is implied by transport.

All protected independent programmes and closed variants remain preserved.
The private source receipt supplies the exact file identity, size, SHA256 and
roundtrip result, so agents can obtain the common handoff without user relay or
the UI attachment size limit. No sharing permissions are changed.
