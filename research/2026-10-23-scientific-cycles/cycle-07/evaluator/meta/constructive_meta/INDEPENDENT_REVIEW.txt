2. COMPLETE CYCLIC-CLOCK RETURN

The input inventory K is uniform on1,...,L. The actual counted clock decrements
cyclically in that same set. After one physical shift, old K=j+1 supplies the
new clock block j<L and content Phi^j tensor nu^(L-j), exactly its target

Because Phi^L is an eigenprojector of nu^L, the difference has eigenvalue

memory half-distance delta=(1-lambda^L)/L. The proof includes the current
clock and does not silently trace out that clock.

The statement would change if an additional immutable original-inventory
record were retained. The source expressly excludes such an extra register;
that exclusion is part of the native model and must remain in any handoff.

At external prefix lengthi, conditioning on the initial K gives min(i,K)

not a product of independently estimated unconditional marginals. It remains
valid after the stock is exhausted because the physical shift does not create

4. COLLECTIVE READOUT LOWER BOUND

For a fixed Alice-to-Bob orientation and omitted copya, append a mathematical
classical registerK_-a labeling the original inventory mixture on the other

additional physical retained register or a new operational measurement port.

Apply any joint POVM on all complementary Alice outputs. For a givenK and
outcomeY, every Bob tail still factors as its original independent diag(q).
The measurement can correlate/condition active Bob Bell partners and can
update the posterior onK; it cannot remove entropy from those independent
Bob tails. Consequently

 S(B_-a|Y,K) >= E[number of complementary tail slots] H(q).

The expectation after averagingY is the original inventory expectation,
because a trace-preserving measurement leaves the classicalK marginal intact.
Strong subadditivity gives S(B_-a|Y)>=S(B_-a|Y,K). The active conditional
states contribute nonnegative ordinary von Neumann entropy becauseYK is
classical. Hence EVERY collective POVM satisfies the lower bound. No
measurement additivity or convex-roof optimizer is assumed.

The expected per-copy tail count is
b=E(N-K)_+=N(N-1)/(2L) forN<=L, andN-(L+1)/2 forN>=L.
AtN=L these agree at(L-1)/2. Averaging the identical bounds overa produces
(r-1)bH(q), exactly the printed normalization.
