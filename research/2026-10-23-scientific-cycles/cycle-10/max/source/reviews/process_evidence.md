### 2.3 A counterexample replaced an overbroad mechanism claim with sound bookkeeping

For single-pass `3 HNO2 -> HNO3 + 2 NO + H2O`, no more than one-third of the starting nitrogen becomes nitrate without further chemistry. A proposed lower bound `(3Y-1)/2` on a separate direct route is valid only when initial nitrogen is partitioned into disjoint pools. Sequential disproportionation followed by NO reoxidation breaks its universal interpretation: at complete conversion the second stage makes two-thirds of the nitrate, while the disjoint-pool formula would demand a direct-route fraction of one. The independent challenge retained this counterexample and the owner declined to use the route fraction universally. [D3]

The replacement is a more general conditional conservation argument. If retained nitrate yield is `Y` and every other original nitrogen atom has oxidation state at least `m`, net nitrogen electron export is at least

\[
\max\{0,(5-m)Y+m-3\}
\]

per original nitrogen atom. Taking the conventional minimum `m=-3` gives `max(0,8Y-6)`. Under the calibration/provenance assumptions, the native air value `Y=0.795605` yields `0.36484` electron equivalents per initial nitrogen. This requires a non-nitrogen electron sink or charge export. It does not identify oxygen-atom provenance, molecular oxygen as the sole acceptor, zero nitrogen emission, or complete retention. [D1, D3, D5]

The correction boundary is unusually narrow compared with the broad mechanism language it could tempt. An additive 45.605 mM correction, a 6.08067% overstatement relative to the corrected yield, or a 5.73212% downward correction relative to the apparent yield reaches the `Y=0.75` boundary. Those percentages have different denominators. The five calibration points' largest residual under the saved line is about 45.6393 mM-equivalent. This is descriptive proximity, not a validated matrix-bias estimate or uncertainty interval; it neither proves the redox claim false nor certifies the accuracy margin required to assert it unconditionally. [D5; fresh audit]

The initial air series value is 116.154 mM and then falls sharply before rising. ## 6. Hard obligations for a new meta-research method

The following are tests the evaluator can own. They do not reassign Dots' nitrogen frontier or ask the three Pros to populate a common benchmark.

### A. Preserve the identifiable claim while finding its actual failure boundary

A proposed inference method should correctly identify a nuisance-invariant scientific quantity, state the weakest load-bearing assumptions, construct a concrete counterexample when one is removed, and preserve the positive consequence when the assumptions hold. Dots supplies demanding archived regression cases: common evaporation cancels; selective tracer loss need not; disjoint-route fractions fail under sequential recycling; a redox bound can survive without identifying an oxygen-only mechanism. A method that merely lists caveats or dismisses every uncertain result fails this test. [D3–D5]

These cases are already solved and their outcomes known to the evaluator. Re-solving them can check implementation or expose an error; it cannot honestly be advertised as a prospective independent discovery. For a positive meta claim, select a different, source-complete scientific receiver with an unresolved consequential inference and actual external or exact validation. The ordinary control receives the same accessible evidence and established metrology, conservation, causal inference, and model-checking methods. Charge discovery of the invariant, evidence search, adaptation, and verification. Success requires a new correct conclusion that changes the target question's answer, not a more elaborate checklist.

### B. Learn an operation, not merely supply the answer to one

The prior abstraction experiment gives the relevant operation to the implementations. The prior anisotropic test gives a substantial recipe to the treatment receiver. Neither tests invention of the capability. A stronger study must expose a donor's legitimate concrete operations and scientific property, require an admissible new abstraction/substitution/measurement procedure, and test it on a separate receiving obligation whose answer was not encoded in the recipe. [E1–E3]

The admission bar includes original information/resource contracts, actual realizability or a lifting witness, adversarial cases that defeat tempting shortcuts, a strong source-informed ordinary method, and charged construction/adaptation costs. A merely adjacent algebraic variation risks the already observed saturation: both methods solve it. Even a successful transfer is one generation of useful capital. Recursive improvement requires the receiver to help construct a better capability that then improves another independent investigation; the current record supplies no such chain.

### C. Distinguish an inference failure from a missing-observation barrier

For an empirical test, the evaluator must first possess an already measured public intervention series or equivalent independent outcomes that can discriminate materially different predictions. Dots' actual complete-loop intervention has no such recovered data. Hiding one affine countermodel as the truth would evaluate a synthetic oracle, not identification of the real pilot. Missing data must be an explicit non-admission result, not silently replaced by simulated chemistry. [D6; E4]

A useful method could predict which accessible observation will change an action ranking and then succeed against withheld real outcomes. Its control must have ordinary experimental design and causal methods, and the distinguishing evidence must not be made artificially unavailable only to the control. Under current authorization, use existing data or an independent proof-domain analogue. No hardware, outreach, purchase, or new platform is implied.

### D. Demonstrate that integrated capital changes a real receiver

Max provides a concrete architectural test: begin from a scientific question and the established authorized private entry point; recover the correct current result with its original source, resource contract, strongest comparator, counterexamples, and version correction; then use it.
