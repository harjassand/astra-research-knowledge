## Cost of the mathematical operation

The new native recognizer accepts original `n` and the entire edge list. It
performs iterative block decomposition, exact structural checks, canonical
sorting, cut-role validation and rooting. With ordinary word-RAM dictionary
and set operations, its comparison work is **O(n+m log m)** and preparation
storage **O(n+m)**. It is not claimed to be a free linear recognition oracle.
The hub parameter `p` is discovered and checked to be common to all blocks.

With `q=p+2` stable queries or `q=2p+1` star-control queries, action layout,
component preparation, exact LS decoding and full sparse output take
**O(nq+m)** arithmetic/storage. Reading the `nq` original observation cells
and writing the `n+m` coefficients are included. Fraction bit growth and
exact comparisons are additional to arithmetic counts. This standard-library
implementation is a source-complete reference operation, not an optimized
Hessian or black-box acquisition pipeline.

The two receiving comparisons charge 4/5 and 5/7 original action columns:
21 original columns over four distinct operations. Their software fixtures
compute those columns from saved exact rational `D`; the candidate-projection
check separately accumulates 21 further columns. These are explicit full-action
arithmetic operations, not a claim to have acquired unknown physical data.
The recorded bodies include these accumulations and all original diagonals.
General action acquisition may dominate and is not priced by these examples.

The matched star control is given the same original matrix and exactly the
same absolute Frobenius noise norm, zero-padding extra columns. Its stronger
conditioning is retained: operator constant sqrt(3/2), Frobenius sqrt2,
versus the new sufficient common constant sqrt(3+sqrt5). Saving p-1 actions
does not establish a total-runtime or accuracy advantage. Strong three-query
forest, special A_L and full-class five-query controls remain unchanged.

## Error accounting and observed environment

Every row has unit Euclidean norm, but the noise convention is absolute
Frobenius, not per-column normalization. The saved matrix, noise, outputs and
normal equations use exact rationals. The projected numerical residual is
zero; the raw observation residual is generally nonzero and is saved.
For a supported numerical candidate, the theorem charges

    sqrt(3+sqrt5) * (||E||F + ||Dhat X - Pi Y||F).

Input conversion, acquisition and assembly errors belong in `E` or need their
own bound. Perturbed queries produce `D deltaX`, which is not bounded by the
row-energy cap alone. The independent checker uses exact normal equations
only on these small actual components; it is not the claimed asymptotic decoder.
