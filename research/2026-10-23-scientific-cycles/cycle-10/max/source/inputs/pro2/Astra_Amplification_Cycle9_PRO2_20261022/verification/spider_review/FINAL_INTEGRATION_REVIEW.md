## One nonblocking terminology clarification

In Section 5.1, the phrases "Every tree block's LS" and "Different blocks
use disjoint selected cells" are valid when "blocks" means the
**bichromatic tree components** defined at the start of Section 5. Those
components, across unordered color pairs, do use disjoint action cells.
Original maximal K_(p,k) graph blocks may share an observation cell at a
cut vertex, so they must not be substituted for these components in the
noise-summing argument. The surrounding definition already fixes the
correct interpretation; this is a terminology clarification, not an
additional premise or a gap in the estimate. No main-source edit was made
by this reviewer.
