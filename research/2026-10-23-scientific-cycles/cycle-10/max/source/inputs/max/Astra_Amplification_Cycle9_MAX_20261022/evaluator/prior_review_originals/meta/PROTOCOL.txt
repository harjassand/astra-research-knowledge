ASTRA CYCLE8 — CONSTRUCTIVE TRANSFER EXPERIMENT
22 October 2026. Frozen before the two receiving executions.

Purpose: test whether an explicit reusable proof operation can transfer to a
changed mathematical task, and compare against an equally informed ordinary
investigator. This is exploratory, not a blinded historical novelty test,
population study or general discovery-performance benchmark. The evaluator
and design reviewer have already reasoned about candidate answers. All such
development must be charged, not hidden in the supplied operation.

Source: exact Pro1 Cycle7 proof, SHARP_TEMPORAL_GROWTH.tex and included earlier
proofs in sources/pro1/Astra_Amplification_Cycle7_PRO1_20261022. The source's
actual nonlinear temporal-window problem remains with Pro1 and is not tested.

New task: for integer d>=2 and strictly positive rational eigenvalues p_i
summing to one, replace the two-color space by C^d and use the identical
color state P=diag(p_i) at each colored input. Port reference is
rho_P = |0><0|/2 direct-sum P/2, sigma_P=rho_P^tensor k.
Use the same bulk stack word and charge-pinched coefficient G1, with d
prepend colors, a two-seed root exchange, and sufficiently deep initial bath.
Define H_zeta=Tr_F[(I tensor zeta)G1]+2Q-kI on F=(C^d)^tensor k.
For every k>=2 find min_{zeta density} Tr(sigma_P H_zeta^2), an attainable
full-prefix optimizer, and a valid operator dual/support condition. Competing
prefix states include every coherent and entangled density. No physical
preparation, infinite-bath cutoff, nonlinear/coding consequence or capacity
claim is allowed without an additional proof.

Fixed diagnostics: d=3,P=(4/5,3/20,1/20); d=3,P=(1/2,1/3,1/6);
d=2,P=(9/10,1/10). Exact boundary behavior and small matrix checks are useful
but do not replace the all-k proof. All conditions get these same examples.

Control: a fresh independent investigator receives this task and complete
original Pro1 proof, may use arbitrary standard mathematics, symbolic or
numerical tools and its own scientific judgment. No instruction to use a
deliberately weak method, brute force or an uninformative baseline.

Operation condition: fresh independent investigator gets precisely the same
task, complete source and tools, plus a compact newly extracted/revised sector
operation supplied in OPERATION.txt. That operation's invention and review
are part of treatment acquisition cost. It does not supply an experimental
measurement or an oracle. Neither agent reads the other's work.

Budget: one bounded research turn each, no subagents, no expensive/unbounded
search, at most 180 cumulative CPU seconds of new scripts, and at most 2,500
words in the principal proof/report. Both may finish early. Actual elapsed
time and available script costs are recorded. Internal inference tokens,
model effort and total authoring/review costs are not observable and will
remain unknown. This is not a token-matched randomized trial. If both solve,
no advantage in success or speed will be inferred from wall time alone.

Success: an all-k correct formula and realizable optimizer with the correct
dual/scope; supported by independent analytic adjudication and a separate
native small-instance matrix construction. Count incorrect scalar-dual or
coding transfers as failures to meet the complete task. Do not improve the
intervention after viewing the control result.

What it can establish: specific constructive transfer and correctness of a
compiled scientific operation. What it cannot establish: historical novelty,
general causal improvement, efficient physical coding, higher model intelligence,
recursive amplification, or exponential growth. Adoption depends on actual
usefulness; archive growth alone is not benefit.
