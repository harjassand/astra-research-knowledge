## Calibration changes the inference
The five calibration droplets use nitrate/perchlorate Raman ratios and fixed 1 M perchlorate. If tracer inventory, initial tracer/nitrite ratio and relative optical response are conserved, the reference-equivalent concentration divided by initial nitrite concentration is nitrate moles per initial nitrite mole. Common evaporation cancels. Thus dismissing this result solely as evaporative concentration would be wrong. This correction changed our interpretation.

This invariant is conditional, not a complete nitrogen balance. Initial/imported nitrate, preferential aerosol enrichment, tracer loss, matrix-dependent optical response, additive spectral bias and unmeasured emitted nitrogen remain possible failures. The source air series starts at 116.154 mM, falls rapidly, and later rises. That initial transient is neither established chemical background to subtract mechanically nor a license to certify zero background. ## Exact conditional redox consequence and its limit
For nitrate yield Y from initial nitrite nitrogen at oxidation state +3, if every remaining original nitrogen atom has final oxidation state at least m, net nitrogen electron export is at least max(0,(5-m)Y+m-3) electrons per initial nitrogen. This follows by assigning all other nitrogen the smallest allowed state. With ordinary minimum m=-3, the threshold is Y>3/4. At the uncorrected air value Y=0.795605, the bound is 0.36484 electrons per initial nitrogen. It requires a non-nitrogen electron sink or charge export; it does not prove molecular oxygen is the sole acceptor, identify oxygen-atom provenance, or establish full retained nitrogen.

A 6.08067% apparent/true multiplicative overstatement, equivalently 5.73212% downward correction of the observed yield, or 45.605 mM additive nitrate-equivalent bias removes this stronger conclusion. The largest residual of a simple line fitted to the five calibration points is 45.6393 mM-equivalent. That residual is descriptive, NOT a validated uncertainty bound, actual matrix bias, or proof that the observation fails. Consequently the stronger universal-redox conclusion is not certified by the recovered calibration record.

Pure single-pass 3 HNO2 -> HNO3 + 2 NO + H2O cannot produce more than one-third nitrate without further chemistry. However, a proposed (3Y-1)/2 direct-oxidation fraction applies only to disjoint initial pools. Sequential NO reoxidation invalidates its universal use. The independent proof preserves this counterexample. General oxidation-state bookkeeping, not that route partition, is used here.

A hypothetical isolated conducting-droplet net-charge-storage alternative would need about 82,154 times the Rayleigh charge at radius 5 micrometres and assumed surface tension 0.072 N/m. This is only a conditional uncompensated-charge diagnostic. It is not a measured charge, general plasma bound, exclusion of electron export, or proof of oxygen-only chemistry. Reversible surface creation at those declared reference values is 43.2 J/mol initial N, not actual atomization, optical, humidity, collection or reset energy.
