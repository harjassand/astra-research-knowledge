Lim, Hhan, Kwon (2025), Quantum Sci.Technol.10 025048;
arXiv2308.16032v3, Theorem1 and AppendixA:
https://arxiv.org/html/2308.16032v3
Role: existing separable product-Kraus information/disturbance score method.
Its original score guesses a pure-state label while maintaining that same
pure input. The present theorem's mixed queue, variable pure-prefix target,
coarse last-nonzero receiving map and loss-distribution consequence are proved
separately. This is a close novelty boundary, not an unrelated citation.

Datta (2009), Int.J.QuantumInf.7 475-491;
arXiv0807.2536v4:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0807.2536
Role: separable-domination minimization is the known max-relative entropy
of entanglement / log robustness. The proof uses the displayed domination
premise directly and proves the needed fixed-Phi_M case, without an assumed
resource-oracle implementation.

Ghosh et al. (2004/2005), Locally Accessible Information and Distillation of
Entanglement; arXivquant-ph/0403134:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0403134
Role: checked alternative complementarity approach. The displayed proof is
one-way LOCC. It is not used to assert a two-way fixed-error Bell-loss law;
the entropy-continuity transfer would retain an epsilon*L obstruction here.

Issa, Wagner, Kamath, An Operational Approach to Information Leakage;
arXiv1807.07878, primary author manuscript:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.07878
Role: for classical supplied access, log sum_z max_k p(z|k) is the established
maximal-leakage / order-infinity quantity. We use its explicit finite formula,
not a claim to invent a measure or to have physically estimated it.
