## What those measurements include

- Local transition check: 30 exact cases; excludes serialization.
- Marked adapter: six new specified-root realizations and one fixed certificate
  load (0.02339193399893702 seconds of its body). It does not rerun the inherited
  closure. The marked cases are new interface checks, not complete old suites.
- Theta body: native inputs, recognition, coloring, exact failed-rule original
  actions and scientific JSON serialization; the first failed checker run is
  not included because no timing was retained.
- Input body: construction and serialization of the new mixed native graph.
- Main receiving body: shared fixed certificate preparation, two designs on the
  same theta input, the mixed-atom design, all exact original actions, noiseless
  and noisy inversions, structural and residual checks, and result serialization.
  Shared preparation was 0.034823793001123704 seconds, already inside the body.
- Failure body: actual C4-chain input, exact supported cut witness, full original

- Independent gate: separate algorithms and Fraction normal equations, original

These are reference Python/Fraction operations. No numerical optimization or
precision sweep was performed. Exact rational arithmetic establishes the saved
identities; success status alone is not the certificate.

## Complete resource boundary

The main native preparation performs block decomposition, original-ID remapping,
protected-edge normalization, one actual local coloring per block, at most 24
permutations per attachment and global component preparation. Canonical sorting
gives O(n+m log m) comparison work and O(n+m) storage, plus a one-time fixed
certificate load. The unbounded support is not materialized from a symbolic
universal hard graph; the actual receivers are native 57- and 30-vertex inputs.

For q=4, coloring after local normalization, incidence decoding and sparse
output use O(nq+m) arithmetic/storage. Computing DX from explicitly available
sparse D takes O(q(n+m)) arithmetic, but an application may provide direct
entries more cheaply than four external actions. Action acquisition is charged
according to the real source, not an invented costly oracle. The optional
theta construction avoids the fixed table on its stated intrinsic theta domain.

The independent small-input verifier enumerates cycles and uses exact normal
equations. Its time is not an asymptotic production decoder claim. It is not
proposed as a recognizer or verifier of arbitrary enormous graphs.

For floating arithmetic, assembly/acquisition/conversion error must enter E
or be bounded separately. A supported output with projected residual R pays
sqrt(3+sqrt(5)) (||E||F+||R||F). Probe error contributes D deltaX; the row cap
does not bound that term. Rational bit growth and requested precision are
additional to arithmetic-operation counts. Saved projected residuals are zero.

## Unknown and historical costs

The total mathematical/inference/discovery cost is **unknown**. Source reading,
primary-literature work, authoring, review drafting, proof integration and
coordination were not instrumented as scientific computation. The first failed
theta shape-checker execution has no retained timing; it is not silently charged
as zero. Its failed source and receipt remain under research/theta_explicit/failed/.

Interpreter startup and final receipt serialization are outside some recorded
bodies. Packaging and private transport are recorded separately in delivery
receipts. Summing a few short successful bodies cannot recover these missing
totals. Historical search/failed-route costs remain preserved in the exact
predecessor archives and are not erased or reinterpreted as free inputs.

The original NIST dense incumbent and all molecular/physical closures remain
unchanged. No application runtime, physical benefit, historical originality,
external acceptance or causal research-amplification claim follows from these
costs. Ordinary direct assembly/AD and stronger-conditioning higher-query
controls remain available under their actual premises.
