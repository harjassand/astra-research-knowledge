The two hard remaining scientific obligations are real; they are not evaluator assignments. The more promising meta mechanism visible here is concrete proof feedback that detects invalid interface transfers, but its benefit to future invention remains to be tested on a consequential independent recipient.

## 2. Pro1: actual scientific change and causal dependency

### Current result and limits

For fixed 0 < beta <= 1/16, the same evolving-bath channel now has the attainable lower bound

    Q(V_beta,tau) >= 3/2 + (ln 2 / 65536) beta^2 / ln(e/beta^2).

The stronger unsimplified bound is (ln 2) v_k beta^4/(64k), with k=floor[1/(16 beta^2 ln(e/beta^2))]. One deterministic, unassisted, initializer-independent code sequence has vanishing full-diamond error **pointwise for every fixed normal initializer**. Beta and the inner block are fixed before the outer coding horizon. This is not a uniform finite-horizon bound over all initializers, terminal bath return, fresh-channel coding, or a sharp capacity exponent. [P1:137–180, 537–579]

The source-specific dependency chain is substantive:

1. Cycle7 provides the actual endpoint/color operator, its three-case token normal form, v_k of order k^2 and the scalar full-prefix certificate T_k(sigma H)=v_k I. Arbitrary pair-labelled operators would not suffice. [P2:35–70; P7:278–460]
2. Cycle8 provides exact contracted all-order response b(1-b)F_k(b), with a uniform full-prefix error of order bk v_k. The response window is governed by bk, while the old positive affine density had a much tighter sufficient window. [P2:78–95, 98–226]
3. Cycle9 proves that rare large eigenvalues can be removed cheaply in the reference-weighted trace measure. Even incidences of the *actual named endpoints* bound all even moments, giving a pinched exponential moment on scale k despite a larger extreme eigenvalue. [P1:246–340]
4. The owner changes the input to sigma B_+/(1+epsilon). A global chi-square inequality pays entropy without a small-norm Taylor expansion. The full physical displacement norm pays the nonlinear repair's entire drift cost; the pair-supported response theorem is applied only to the signed affine comparison. [P1:342–535]
5. Earlier root-retaining Lyapunov, spectral-type domination and joint compression proofs turn this density into a reliable same-bath code. The r^2 positive-block factor, auxiliary CPTP normalization and polar repair are retained. [P3:33–142; P4:33–107]

The mathematical receiving use is therefore demonstrated. It does not show that the current owner, without the capital, could not have reconstructed the same lemmas, or that the evaluator's representation advice caused the invention. The optional evaluator anisotropic theorem was not used. [P1:683–689; P5]

### Why the invention succeeded, and which routes failed

The successful change was from controlling the **worst eigenvalue** to controlling the **reference-weighted tail needed to repair positivity**. A repeated-color Rayleigh vector proves lambda_max(H) >= C_k/4 of order k^(3/2). Every normalized affine I-tH+f(Q) with a charge-only repair must satisfy t C_k <= 4. Thus the old family cannot reach the new temporal scale merely by improving its positivity estimate. This is a structural reason to change the family, not just a loose old calculation. [P1:182–244]

Three tempting substitutions fail:

- A nonlinear positive part or exponential does not retain the pair-support response interface. The earlier four-endpoint counterexample has zero first response and nonzero second response.
- Pair labels, total-charge preservation and norm bounds do not imply the even-endpoint moment bound. Reusing one exchange under all labels gives 72 rather than the alleged upper 48 at k=4.
- Commuting with total charge does not imply reference pinching into complete charge patterns. A coherent Dicke reference violates the bound. [P1:311–340, 414–422, 668–680; P6]

Internal review found one genuine exported-premise omission: charge preservation of the general repair requires [sigma,Q]=0. The final source states it and supplies the B=I counterexample. Actual sigma=f(Q) already satisfied it, so this was an interface correction, not a failed operational theorem. [P1:347–419; P6:172–178]

## 3. Ultra: actual scientific change and causal dependency

### Current result

For the actual Phi_D^K tensor nu^(L-K) queue, inaccessible current clocks, complete old memory, one global half-trace error and pointwise per-queue pure-profile loss, the optimal zero-added-aid finite-round LOCC order is

    w_* + 1 = Theta_D(min{L, 1 + ln L + ln(r/epsilon)}), epsilon <= 1/8.

The fixed-error log L lower allows all SEP operations; the log(r/epsilon) lower and fixed-L strong converse allow arbitrary joint CPTP receivers with apparatus independent of the **complete source**. At finite fixed L, any useful w < L-1 forces global error to one as r increases. A paid entangled-ancilla construction reduces original-source loss but consumes explicitly counted new Bell supply and seed/control resources. [U1:14–59, 61–251, 310–409]

This resolves the selected universal law internally and excludes the old uniform-r profile goal. It does not establish a pooled/encoded rate, a formation advantage, a returned catalyst, or a useful physical receiver. The exact/approximate distinction remains material.

### Why the argument works

The reusable capital is not a bag of generic quantum-information keywords:

- The true product-nu tail gives both the nested positive-operator order and the independent computational-tail normalization in the product-Kraus score. A chi tail changes those calculations.
- The earlier actual pure-prefix reference makes a *coarse last-nonzero Lüders readout* possible while retaining the earlier Bell prefix. That converts retained entanglement into a bounded positive success test, so joint error costs epsilon rather than epsilon L.
- A geometric band sum produces the single-queue loss CDF; a collective weighted-volume count handles arbitrary collective SEP maps.
- Geometric Gram overlaps of neighboring prefix vectors supply orthogonal defects for the finite-r CPTP bound. Bell-basis posterior factorization gives the fixed-L strong converse.
- A convex secant prices E ln M directly, even if E M diverges, instead of replacing it by ln E M. [U1:78–225, 253–357; U2]

These are actual receiving steps using previous source and receiver structure. The preceding exact-support obstruction alone could not establish the fixed-error law.

### Failure information is scientifically decisive

The direct entropy/information route incurs an epsilon L continuity penalty, which can swamp log L. Query count is not Bell loss: many nested queries can have rank one on the retained safe prefix. Fourier erasure cannot recover a Bell destroyed by mixing with a product tail; the explicit postmeasurement state is separable. A nonlocal global Bell-projector receiver achieves constant loss at fixed error, showing that support overlap alone cannot establish a CPTP-wide log L law. The SEP restriction is indispensable. [U3; U4]

Complete-source independence is also indispensable: independence of the auxiliary's K marginal does not imply tensor factorization from the source. In the final proof the fixed clock labels are removed when comparing conditional operator fibres and restored for the full direct-sum distance. Literal order between the orthogonal clock-inclusive blocks would be false. These clarifications are now incorporated; earlier drafts should not be presented as the latest theorem. [U1:25–32, 92–102; U5; U6]

### Significance and ordinary comparator

The result expands from one valid receiver upper to a complete order theorem, resource extension and limit-order boundary. It narrows the broader capital story because the sought uniform-r per-queue profile cannot exist under its stated contract, even with unlimited independent computational apparatus.

The strongest ordinary comparator receives the identical actual queue, retained-memory contract, error budget, access, aid and disposal rights, and may use arbitrary collective LOCC. It can execute the exact same upper and paid-aid construction. The original mean preselected source supply remains r(L+1)ln D/2. No relative supply, preparation or total-computation advantage is demonstrated. [U7; U1:395–409]

The numerical tables show how weak order statements can be at finite parameters: at D=2, L=2^20, r=1, epsilon=1/64, the stored necessary SEP loss is 6 while the explicit inherited construction upper is 19,005. ## 5. Counterexamples to “composition implies intelligence amplification”

1. **Same owner, stronger theorem, unchanged discovery policy.** The observed Pro1 chain is equally compatible with an unchanged capable researcher accumulating domain knowledge. No matched acquisition/discovery experiment distinguishes that account.
2. **Composable resource with no relative advantage.** Ultra's receiver is executable by the strongest ordinary-stock comparator. Reusing it in another calculation cannot by itself create a supply advantage.
3. **More machinery cannot satisfy a false target.** Ultra's arbitrary-CPTP strong converse blocks the old fixed-L profile goal even with unlimited independent apparatus.
4. **Valid local interfaces can fail when joined.** Nonlinear repair creates higher endpoint coherences; a charge-commuting reference need not be fully pinched; an uncorrelated marginal need not be a product auxiliary; approximate profile closeness is not exact formation support.
5. **The stronger construction can be worse at the tested parameter or cost.** Pro1's older bound wins at 1/16 and 1/64, and the new large-block input has no efficient implementation.

These counterexamples limit causal and practical claims without diminishing the valid mathematical progress.

## 6. Hard obligations, experiment suitability and a higher-ceiling alternative

### The two actual remaining obligations

**Pro1:** at fixed small r=bk, control the *full signed bath-operator response and charge cost* of the positive spectral correction. The present bound only makes clipping mass a small constant, whereas k epsilon grows relative to the order-r^2 drift budget as b tends to zero. **Ultra:** supply a genuinely declared pooled/encoded output interface and prove a clock-compatible complete-joint-memory rate/decoupling statement, with the equally informed ordinary-stock comparator and total initial assistance charged. Relabeling per-queue reference output or invoking a standard iid distillation slogan does not meet it. [U1:411–429; U7:47–49]

Both are decision-changing science. Both are owner frontiers, so I did not solve them or turn them into evaluator experiments.

### Is there a suitable current transfer experiment here?

Not from the supplied two branches alone. A weighted/sparse endpoint-operator tail problem or a nonuniform-prior queue could be frozen as a new transfer task. However, no independent scientific recipient in these records needs either result. Selecting such tasks because the available capital already fits them would demonstrate at most task-shaped transfer. Re-running the rate derivation, receiver law, or small replay is weaker still.

If a real external-to-donor obligation is later identified, a credible comparison must give the ordinary arm current concentration/operator-moment or product-Kraus methods, source structure, conventional symbolic algebra/SDP tools, and the same resources. It must permit the control to reconstruct the capital and must count that cost. Success is a new verified consequence or changed feasible question; a shorter proof, more completed lemmas, or a lower post-hoc time threshold is insufficient. The earlier evaluator's three-arm Pro2 test supplies a useful local computational result, not this missing recipient.

### Higher-ceiling mechanism supported by these records

The most concrete alternative is **learning admissible operations from counterexample feedback**. Actual scientific challenges found load-bearing missing premises and false proposed bridges in both branches. A finite operator/quantum-instrument model finder could search for witnesses to an attempted lift: violated named-endpoint grading, unpinched reference, correlated auxiliary, incorrect clock fibre, non-TP normalization, or nonlocality hidden inside a “free” measurement. The output must be an explicit state/map/witness, not another verbal critic score.

This is feasible for bounded matrices and existing primitive replay code; it is not a general all-length theorem prover. A strong ordinary control is direct exact symbolic algebra, conventional SDP/counterexample search, and domain-informed manual checking. The missing evaluation remains a fresh consequential conjecture whose acquisition or repair is improved *because* of that feedback, followed by use on a second independent obligation. Current records show useful correction and rejected routes; they do not show a trained or self-improving counterexample policy. Building a universal platform now would get ahead of the evidence.
