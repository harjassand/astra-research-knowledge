UNIVERSAL SEPARABLE-RECEIVER LOGARITHMIC LOSS CANDIDATE
22 October 2026; principal sublemma developed by cycle9_loss_converse.
Status: complete proposed proof below; independent challenges requested.

All logarithms are natural. D>=2 and L>=2 are integers; w>=0 is an integer.
Phi_D is the maximally entangled D-pair and nu=I_D/D tensor I_D/D.
The ACTUAL input conditional on inaccessible uniform K=k is
  sigma_k=Phi_D^k tensor nu^(L-k), k=1,...,L.
The inaccessible K clocks and unchanged product old memory are included in
the joint error. The receiver acts only on the L external D-pairs. No extra
entangled receiving auxiliary or source-correlated local access is supplied.

THEOREM (proposed). Let Gamma be any finite-dimensional trace-preserving
separable operation, including every finite-round finite-outcome LOCC device,
with arbitrary independent local apparatus and even independent shared
classical randomness. Suppose its actual output rho, including inaccessible
K and retained transcript T, is at half-trace distance <=epsilon from a state
  Theta=(1/L) sum_(k,t) p(t|k)|k,t><k,t| tensor
       [Phi_D^n(k,t) tensor |00><00|^(L-n(k,t))],
where max(1,k-w)<=n(k,t)<=k on EVERY target branch. No assumption is made
that n is known to the receiver. In particular n may be min(k,n_actual(t)).
Then for every integer a>=0,
  1-epsilon-D^(-(a+1)) <= (w+a+1) D^(w+a+1)/L.                 (A)
The inequality is useful when its left side is positive. For epsilon<=1/2,
choosing a=1 gives
  (w+2)D^(w+2) >= L/4,
and hence at fixed D
  w >= log_D L - log_D log_D L - O_D(1).
This is an operational Bell-pair LOSS obstruction at fixed error; it does
not require a relative-entropy/convex-roof bound or replace nu by chi.
It is stronger in operation class than LOCC, but excludes entangled aids.

PROOF STEP 1: AN ADMISSIBLE COARSE READOUT THAT KEEPS MOST TARGET BELLS.
Append the following LOCC map to BOTH rho and Theta. Alice measures, on her
L output digits, the last nonzero computational position J. For j>=1 its
projector is
  I^(j-1) tensor (I-|0><0|)_j tensor |0><0|^(L-j);
the j=0 projector is |0...0><0...0|. This is a COARSE local projective
measurement, not an individual Z measurement of all preceding digits.
Alice sends J to Bob. Both retain their first n_J=max(0,J-1) pairs and reset
all other output pairs to |00>. They keep J as a shared classical record.
Call this appended channel C. It uses no entanglement or inaccessible K.
Its communication/local work are proof overhead, not a receiver upper bound.

On an ideal target Phi_D^n with a zero tail, J<=n with certainty. Conditional
on any J=j, C's external output is EXACTLY Phi_D^n_J with a zero tail:
for j>=1 the pre-reset state factors Phi_D^(j-1), the maximally correlated
nonzero-digit state on slot j, and pure zeros beyond. Discarding/resetting
slot j leaves the stated pure prefix. For j=0 it is all zero. Also
  Pr(n-J>a | n) <= D^(-(a+1)),
because for 0<=u<=n, Pr(J<=u)=D^(u-n), with zero probability if u<0.
Consequently the event E={J<=K<=J+w+a} has probability at least
1-D^(-(a+1)) under C(Theta). On that event the fidelity test onto
  v_J=Phi_D^n_J tensor |00>^(L-n_J)
succeeds with certainty. Define the single positive contraction
  W=sum_(k,j):j<=k<=j+w+a |k,j><k,j| tensor |v_J><v_J|,
tensored with identity on any other retained records. Trace contraction and
the variational trace-distance bound give
  Tr W C(rho) >= 1-D^(-(a+1))-epsilon.                         (B)
The inaccessible K in W is used only in a mathematical joint test; the
physical C never reads K. No branch is postselected out of actual operation.

PROOF STEP 2: SEPARABLE SCORE BOUND FOR THE ACTUAL NU-TAIL SOURCE.
The composition C Gamma is trace-preserving separable. Trace away every
retained register except its external output and J. Refine each product
Kraus by local output-discard bases and local classical outcome labels.
Thus it admits product Kraus A_lambda tensor B_lambda, each associated with
one public J=j, satisfying
  sum_lambda A_lambda^*A_lambda tensor B_lambda^*B_lambda=I_A tensor I_B.
Let d=D^L. Write n=n_j=max(0,j-1). Contract both local output tail registers
against their zero vectors, obtaining rectangular maps
  A'_lambda: C^d -> C^(D^n), B'_lambda: C^d -> C^(D^n).
Their Hilbert-Schmidt squared norms do not exceed those of A_lambda,B_lambda.
For a source tail computational pair |u>_A|v>_B of length L-k, the amplitude
of v_J after this product Kraus is
  D^(-(k+n)/2) sum_(x in [D]^k, y in [D]^n)
       A'_(y,xu) B'_(y,xv).
Cauchy-Schwarz bounds its squared modulus by
  D^(-(k+n)) alpha_lambda(k,u) beta_lambda(k,v),
where alpha is the sum of squared A' entries over (x,y) at fixed u and beta
is the analogous B' quantity. Since nu^(L-k) assigns each (u,v) probability
D^(-2(L-k)), and the source prior of k is 1/L, its complete contribution is
  f_(lambda,k) <= D^(-2L+k-n)/L
       * Tr(A'_lambda^* A'_lambda) Tr(B'_lambda^* B'_lambda)
  <= D^(-2L+k-n)/L
       * Tr(A_lambda^* A_lambda) Tr(B_lambda^* B_lambda).        (C)
The sum over u,v factors because the actual tail is the PRODUCT nu tail.
This step cannot be transplanted to a different source without proof.

For fixed lambda and its j, W retains only integers k in [1,L] intersect
[j,j+w+a]. There are at most s=w+a+1 such k. For all of them,
  k-n_j <= s
(for j>=1 use n_j=j-1; for j=0 use n_j=0 and k<=w+a).
Summing (C) over just those k gives at most
  s D^s/(L d^2) Tr(A_lambda^* A_lambda)Tr(B_lambda^* B_lambda).
Finally trace the completeness identity:
  sum_lambda Tr(A_lambda^* A_lambda)Tr(B_lambda^* B_lambda)=d^2.
Therefore
  Tr W C(rho) <= s D^s/L.                                    (D)
Combining (B),(D) proves (A).

POINTS REQUIRING NO EXTRA PREMISE.
1. The appended local coarse measurement preserves the entire prefix below
   J; measuring individual digits instead would invalidate the proof.
2. Transcripts/private outcomes may be arbitrarily correlated with K. They
   are refined in the mathematical product-Kraus representation, then traced;
   no outcome is granted as initial receiver access.
3. The target n(k,t) need not be transcript-measurable. The measured J and
   final dimension n_J are physical common outcomes, so each Kraus has one
   fixed target vector for the Cauchy-Schwarz score bound.
4. The old memory is retained in the premise and traced only in the proof.
   Its independent nu^L factor is unaffected; including it cannot weaken the
   original global-error contract. The inaccessible current K is retained
   in W and never passed to Gamma or C.
5. The theorem is uniform over independent local/separable apparatus, since
   that apparatus can be absorbed into the effective separable map. An
   INDEPENDENT ENTANGLED aid generally destroys this effective-separability
   premise and must be charged by a separate extension.
6. Finite-round LOCC suffices. Any larger separable closure for which the
   finite-dimensional score/error pass continuously inherits the inequality.

CONSEQUENCE AND GAP.
At fixed D and any fixed epsilon<1, choose a constant a so that
D^(-(a+1))<1-epsilon. Then w=Omega(log L). Cycle8's actual deterministic
receiver has w=O(log L+log(1/epsilon)), giving matched logarithmic Bell-loss
order in L at fixed nonmaximal error and zero receiving entanglement/access.
The separately developed nested-support lower bound controls high fidelity
and collective r. This theorem does not settle leading constants, entangled
receiving-aid tradeoffs, or a mean-deficit target. It proves no formation
advantage: ordinary stock under the same interface has the same obstruction.
