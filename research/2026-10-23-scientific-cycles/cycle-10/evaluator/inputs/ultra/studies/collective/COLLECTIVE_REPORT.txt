CYCLE9 COLLECTIVE RECEIVER FRONTIER
Actual full-product-tail queue: a strong converse for the original profile contract
22 October 2026

SUMMARY OF THE RECEIVING CHANGE

The missing collective theorem has a negative answer under the stipulated
per-queue pure-prefix reference. At every fixed finite L, any nontrivial
pointwise deficit w<L-1 forces the ONE global half-trace error to tend to
one as the number r of independent actual queues tends to infinity. This
holds even for arbitrary global quantum channels with unlimited independent
apparatus/entanglement, so communication or a cleverer LOCC decoder cannot
repair it. The obstruction is the positive posterior probability of K_a=1
under every Bell-basis source component, combined with an output event
which is impossible for a reference queue allocated only its first Bell.

This complements the root's stronger finite-r/local-neighbor loss law. Its
strength is a full strong converse at fixed L, and the distinction between
useful successful allocation and mean inventory placed on a rare global
error branch. Its weakness is a constant exponentially small in L. It does
not prove that the single-queue logarithmic loss in L is necessary.

1. EXACT SOURCE, OPERATION AND ERROR INTERFACE

Let D,L>=2 be integers. Each actual queue has an inaccessible current
clock K uniform on 1,...,L and external state
 rho_k=Phi_D^k tensor (I_D/D tensor I_D/D)^(L-k).
The old quantum memory is exactly the preserved nu^L and can be retained
throughout. The r queues, including their clocks, are independent initially.
Gamma acts collectively only on external outputs and declared apparatus
initially in tensor product with the COMPLETE queue source. Its apparatus
may contain arbitrary internal entanglement. All retained transcripts/work
can be included. No correlated
clock information is supplied. Gamma can be any CPTP map; the theorem does
not need an LOCC restriction or a finite computation/communication budget.

Write v_n=Phi_D^n tensor |00>^(L-n), 1<=n<=L. The target Theta has the same
actual clock/transcript/old-memory law, and, after tracing any other retained
registers, is a mixture of tensor_a |v_(N_a)><v_(N_a)| with
 1<=N_a<=K_a on every reference branch.
N may depend on the entire K vector and actual transcript. It need not be
observable. This is the original classical pure-profile reference, not a
coherent superposition of profiles or an unrestricted global Bell buffer.
Assume T(Gamma(Omega^r),Theta)<=epsilon in one GLOBAL half-trace distance.
Every argument below tests the inaccessible clocks only in the mathematical
global comparison. It does not give those clocks to the receiver and does
not claim experimental accessibility of the hidden memory.

2. COMPLETE FINITE-r STRONG-CONVERSE BOUND

Suppose in addition N_a>=max(1,K_a-w) on every reference branch, where w is
an integer 0<=w<=L-1. Define the exact constants
 c=(D^2-1)/(D^(2L)-1),
 beta=(1/L) sum_(k=1)^L [1-D^(-(k-w-1)_+)].
For every integer s=1,...,r,

 epsilon >= Pr[Binomial(r,beta)>=s]-(1-c)^s.                 (A)

The right side may be replaced by its maximum over s and zero. In
particular, if w<L-1, beta>0. Taking s=floor(beta*r/2) for sufficiently
large r yields

 epsilon >= 1-exp(-beta*r/8)-(1-c)^floor(beta*r/2),           (B)

so epsilon tends to one at fixed D,L,w<L-1. This rules out a useful
uniform-r pointwise profile receiver at any fixed global error below one.

COMPLETE PROOF. Use a generalized Bell basis with distinguished zero
vector Phi_D. Each rho_k is diagonal in the product Bell basis. If the
latent basis string y has j leading zero symbols (including the protected
first site), then
 Pr(K=k|y)=D^(2k)/(sum_(h=1)^j D^(2h)), 1<=k<=j.
Hence Pr(K=1|y)>=c. For r queues the posterior K_a are independent given
the complete latent Bell-basis string Y. Any collective channel followed
by any output measurement is a stochastic channel from Y. Thus conditioning
on its outcome does not improve these posterior probabilities once Y is
specified. This is a proof decomposition, not a physical Bell measurement.

On each queue output apply the binary test P=I-|v_1><v_1|, and let S be the
set of queues where P clicks. For an ideal pure profile v_n,
 Pr(click|n)=1-|<v_1,v_n>|^2=1-D^(-(n-1)).
In Theta, a clicked queue therefore never has N_a=1, and consequently
never K_a=1. Conditional on the whole N vector the tests are independent.
Under the pointwise guarantee each click probability is at least
 p_min(K_a)=1-D^(-(K_a-w-1)_+).
Introduce independent uniform variables for the product tests. Coupling
their Bernoulli outcomes below by probability p_min(K_a), and averaging
the iid uniform K_a, shows that the target |S| stochastically dominates
Binomial(r,beta), even though the N_a can be arbitrarily correlated through
the transcript and K vector.

Let E_s be the single global test event
 {|S|>=s and every a in S has K_a!=1}.
It has target probability at least Pr[Binomial(r,beta)>=s]. For the actual
state, conditioning on Y and S leaves the posterior K labels independent:
 Pr(actual E_s|Y,S)<=1_(|S|>=s) product_(a in S)(1-Pr(K_a=1|Y_a))
                 <=(1-c)^s.
Therefore Pr_actual(E_s)<=(1-c)^s. Trace-distance contraction bounds the
target/actual difference of this event by epsilon and proves (A).
Chernoff's elementary binomial lower-tail bound proves (B).

3. MEAN ALLOCATION HAS A DIFFERENT, SHARP ERROR-BUDGET LAW

Without the pointwise lower guarantee, keep only 1<=N_a<=K_a. Let
 M=#{a:N_a>=2}, alpha=1-1/D, gamma=-ln(1-c), mu=(L-1)/2,
 sigma=sqrt((L^2-1)/12). For every integer q with s=floor(alpha*q/2)>=1,

 Pr_Theta(M>=q) <= min(1,[epsilon+exp(-gamma*s)]/
                              [1-exp(-alpha*q/8)]).          (C)

The same event E_s proves this: conditional on M>=q, the target click
count dominates Binomial(q,alpha). The actual event bound is unchanged.

Put A=r^-1 sum_a(K_a-1), X=r^-1 sum_a(N_a-1). On M<q,
X<=(L-1)(q-1)/r. Always X<=A, E A=mu and
E|A-mu|<=sigma/sqrt(r). Consequently

 E sum_a N_a/r <=1+(L-1)(q-1)/r+sigma/sqrt(r)
   +mu*min(1,[epsilon+exp(-gamma*s)]/[1-exp(-alpha*q/8)]).     (D)

Choosing q=ceil(sqrt(r)) shows, at fixed D,L and limsup epsilon_r<=epsilon,

 limsup E sum_a N_a/r <=1+epsilon*(L-1)/2.                    (E)

Multiply by ln D for Bell entanglement in nats. The mean deficit from the
actual average inventory B=(L+1)ln D/2 is at least
 (1-epsilon)*(B-ln D).
Thus vanishing global error collapses the mean reference allocation to
the one Bell pair already exact in every queue.

The coefficient epsilon is asymptotically sharp for this MEAN reference
contract, but this does not produce useful actual extra entanglement.
Actual operation: keep each first Bell and reset all remaining outputs.
Generate and retain one independent shared public flag C, with P(C=1)=p;
charge its local randomness, one classical message and disposal. Reference:
if C=0 use N_a=1 everywhere; if C=1 use N_a=K_a everywhere. It has the same
actual clock/transcript/old-memory law and distance
 p E_K sqrt(1-D^(-sum_a(K_a-1))) <=p,
which tends to p. Its mean allocation is 1+p*(L-1)/2. Taking p=epsilon
saturates (E), with all apparent gain carried on the globally bad branch.
This is an explicit counterconstruction to interpreting a mean-only
reference gain as a useful successfully obtained resource.

4. SEPARATE PRICED INITIAL-ACCESS EXTENSION

The independent bounded challenge file gives a complete additional proof
for a supplied quantum register Z with conditional PRODUCT source
 rho_K tensor zeta_K^Z. This assumption is essential: it is not a theorem
about arbitrary source-Z purifications. Charge b=I(Kvec:Z) in nats as
actual correlated initial access. For the q,s of (C),

 [1-exp(-alpha*q/8)] Pr_Theta(M>=q)
   <=epsilon+min(1,(b+ln 2)/(gamma*s)).                      (F)

The proof compares the cq K,Y,Z law with the Markovized law in which
K and Z are independent conditional on Y. Their relative entropy is
I(K:Z|Y)<=b. The Markovized E_s probability is <=exp(-gamma*s), so binary
relative-entropy data processing gives the actual E_s probability at most
(b+ln2)/(gamma*s). This yields (F). If b=o(r), choose q=o(r), q->infinity
and b/q->0; the same mean limit (E) follows. This is an explicitly priced
information resource, not a claim that it can be measured experimentally.

5. FINITE-R BOUNDS AND EXECUTABLE ADMISSIONS

replay.py uses exact Python Fractions to enumerate the actual Bell-basis
posterior and the optimal classical test E_s on small finite sources. It
also evaluates the exact rational binomial bound (A), and checks the
integer Gram-exponent identities behind the root projector proof.
REPLAY_RESULT.json records PASS. For D=2,L=2,w=0, bound (A) is 0.8673816

not measured errors of a new receiver. Local CPU runtime was about 0.23s.
No scientific optimization, quantum hardware or new physical test was run.

The constants here are exponentially weak in L: c is about D^(-2L).
The root neighboring-source/projector argument gives the more useful
finite-r loss law controlled by D^(-2(w+1)). The two proofs answer distinct
limits. Neither permits the order L->infinity, r->infinity to be exchanged.

6. DECISIVE FAILED CONSTRUCTIVE DIRECTION AND CONTINUATION

I considered running the old receiver at fixed per-copy error, then applying
collective stabilizer distillation to repair the residual bad blocks. That
can plausibly produce a separate globally pure Bell buffer at a rate uniform
in r, paying code redundancy and implementation/control costs. It does not
produce a Bell allocation under each unknown original K_a: purification
does not identify which queues have K_a=1, and putting pooled Bells back
under those unknown capacities reintroduces exactly the event obstruction.
This is a contract failure, not a proof that ordinary pooled distillation is
impossible. I did not run or advertise a favorable pooled calculation as a
solution to the stated per-queue target.

The selected continuation is a declared change to a collective encoded or
pooled output interface, with an actual clock-compatible allocation or
decoding theorem and complete joint-memory accounting. Under the unchanged
fixed-L pointwise profile interface there is no useful uniform-r receiver
to search for. Single-queue optimal loss in L, and sharp growing-L/r/error/
access dependence, remain distinct open questions.

The ordinary-stock comparator receives the identical actual queue and can
use every operation allowed here. No formation advantage is proved. The
proof is elementary finite commuting-source statistics plus a quantum
output support test; historical novelty is UNKNOWN. Internal proof validity,
efficient implementation and physical usefulness are separate statuses.
