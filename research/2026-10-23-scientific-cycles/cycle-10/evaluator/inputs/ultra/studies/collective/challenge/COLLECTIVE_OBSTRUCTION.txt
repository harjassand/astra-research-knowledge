1. SOURCE, RECEIVER AND REFERENCE CONTRACT

Fix integers D>=2, L>=2 and r>=1. Put
  rho_k = Phi_D^{tensor k} tensor nu_D^{tensor(L-k)},
  nu_D = I_(D^2)/D^2, k=1,...,L.
There are r independent slots, with K_a iid uniform on {1,...,L}.
The retained, receiver-inaccessible complete clocks carry the orthogonal
classical vector K=(K_1,...,K_r). Other retained old memory may be included;
it is enough that the receiver's conditional external input is
rho_K = tensor_a rho_(K_a). The receiver cannot act on those clocks or old
memory. It may act collectively on all rL pairs and arbitrary apparatus
initially in tensor product with the complete queue source; mere independence
of its K marginal is not the assumption. It need not be LOCC for the theorem below.
Thus the theorem applies a fortiori to all admissible collective LOCC
receivers, irrespective of communication or computation.

Write the finite-slot pure profile
  |v_n> = |Phi_D>^{tensor n} tensor |00>^{tensor(L-n)}, 1<=n<=L.
The comparison reference has the SAME clock marginal (iid uniform K), and
conditional on K and an arbitrary additional classical profile label N,
its r output slots are tensor_a |v_(N_a)><v_(N_a)|, with
  1 <= N_a <= K_a  on every reference branch.
The reference distribution of N can depend arbitrarily on the entire K,
the actual transcript and independent apparatus records. N need not be
retained or accessible. Extra retained records/memory can be present.
It is only required that this specified pure-product profile ensemble
exists after their appropriate partial trace. A coherent superposition of
different profiles is NOT covered by that reference definition.

Let tau be the complete actual joint state and omega such a reference.
Assume global half trace distance T(tau,omega) <= epsilon. Reading K in the
proof is an information-theoretic distinguishability test permitted by
that GLOBAL comparison. It does NOT give the receiver clock access, and it
is NOT an assertion that hidden-memory return is experimentally measurable.

Define M = #{a:N_a>=2} under the reference, mu=(L-1)/2,
sigma=sqrt((L^2-1)/12), alpha=1-1/D,
  c_L = (D^2-1)/(D^(2L)-1), gamma_L = -ln(1-c_L).
All logarithms in information/resource statements are natural.

2. FINITE-r TAIL THEOREM, NO CORRELATED INITIAL ACCESS

For every integer m with 1<=m<=r and s=floor(alpha*m/2)>=1, put
beta_m=exp(-alpha*m/8). Then

  Pr_omega(M>=m) <=
     min(1, [epsilon+exp(-gamma_L*s)]/[1-beta_m]).              (2)

Consequently the mean Bell allocation obeys

 (1/r) E_omega sum_a N_a <= 1+(L-1)*(m-1)/r + sigma/sqrt(r)
       +mu*min(1,[epsilon+exp(-gamma_L*s)]/[1-beta_m]).          (3)

For every fixed D,L and any sequence of receivers/references with
limsup epsilon_r<=epsilon, (2)-(3) imply

 limsup_(r->infty) (1/r) E sum_a N_a <= 1+epsilon*(L-1)/2.       (4)

In nats, multiplying (4) by ln D gives Bell allocation at most
  ln D + epsilon*(B-ln D),  B=(L+1)ln D/2.
Thus mean deficit from the actual average inventory B is at least
  (1-epsilon)*(B-ln D)
as r tends to infinity at fixed L. In particular, global error tending to
zero forces mean allocation at most one Bell pair per slot, regardless of
collective processing. A useful uniform-r pointwise loss w<L-1 with
reference N_a>=max(1,K_a-w) is impossible at any fixed epsilon<1 whenever
that lower guarantee forces a positive fraction of N_a>=2: (2) then gives
global error tending to one. Statement (4) is the different, weaker
unconditional mean statement; it must not be called a pointwise converse.

3. COMPLETE PROOF OF (2)

Use any generalized Bell basis on a D-pair, whose distinguished index 0 is
|Phi_D>. Each rho_k is diagonal in the product Bell basis. Introduce only
as a mathematical latent variable Y the full product Bell-basis index
string. This grants the proof a statistical refinement; it is NOT a
measurement performed by the receiver and need not preserve entanglement.
Conditional on Y, the source state is its pure product Bell basis vector,
and K->Y->receiver outcome is a Markov chain. This holds for every quantum
channel, because the input is exactly diagonal in that basis.

If j is the number of leading zero indices in one slot's Y, its compatible
labels are k=1,...,j, with posterior probabilities
  Pr(K=k|Y) = D^(2k) / sum_(h=1)^j D^(2h).
Consequently Pr(K=1|Y)>=c_L. Across r independent source slots these
posterior labels are independent conditional on the complete Y vector.
Arbitrary collective receiver measurements do not improve this fact:
conditional on Y, their outcome is independent of K.

On each output slot apply the binary POVM
  P = I-|v_1><v_1|   (flag),   I-P (no flag).
For the ideal profile |v_n>,
  Pr(flag|n) = 1-|<v_1|v_n>|^2 = 1-D^(-(n-1)).
It is zero for n=1 and at least alpha for every n>=2. Conditional on the
entire reference profile vector N, the flags are independent. Let S be
the set of flagged output slots. Under the reference, all a in S have
K_a>=N_a>=2, with certainty. If M>=m, the flag count dominates a binomial
with m trials and success probability alpha. Chernoff's elementary bound
therefore gives
  Pr_omega(|S|>=s and K_a!=1 for every a in S)
     >= (1-beta_m)*Pr_omega(M>=m).                            (5)

For the actual state, conditional on Y and the collective measurement
outcome S, the labels K_a retain their independent posteriors. Hence
  Pr_tau(K_a!=1 for every a in S | Y,S)
      = product_(a in S) [1-Pr(K_a=1|Y_a)]
      <= (1-c_L)^|S|.
Thus, for the single test event E_s={|S|>=s; all selected K_a!=1},
  Pr_tau(E_s) <= (1-c_L)^s = exp(-gamma_L*s).                 (6)
Trace-distance data processing gives
  Pr_omega(E_s) <= epsilon+Pr_tau(E_s).
Combining with (5)-(6) proves (2). No positivity of partial transposition,
entanglement monotone, accessible K estimate, output independence in the
actual state, or bounded-size receiver was used.

4. COMPLETE PROOF OF THE MEAN BOUND

Put X=(1/r)sum_a(N_a-1) and A=(1/r)sum_a(K_a-1).
On M<m, X<=(L-1)(m-1)/r. Always X<=A, since N_a<=K_a.
The reference clock law is exactly iid uniform, so EA=mu and
E|A-mu|<=sqrt(Var A)=sigma/sqrt(r). Therefore
 EX <= (L-1)(m-1)/r + E[A 1_{M>=m}]
    <= (L-1)(m-1)/r + mu Pr(M>=m)+sigma/sqrt(r).
This proves (3). Choose m=ceil(sqrt(r)) for sufficiently large r. The first
term and the variance correction vanish, s tends to infinity, beta tends
to zero, and (4) follows. The convergence constant gamma_L is exponentially
small in L; this is a fixed-L obstruction, not an efficient finite-r bound
uniform in growing L.

5. SHARPNESS AND THE GLOBAL ERROR-BUDGET ARTEFACT

The coefficient epsilon in (4) is asymptotically sharp for this reference
contract, even with no useful improvement in the actual operation.
Actual operation: retain each slot's exact first Bell pair and locally
reset all other pairs to |00>. Produce an independent shared classical
flag C with Pr(C=1)=p, charging the one classical message and the local
coin/disposal work, and retain C in the transcript.
Reference: when C=0 use N_a=1 for all a; when C=1 use N_a=K_a for all a.
The complete reference has exactly the actual old-memory/clock/C law.
Its mean Bell allocation is 1+p*mu. Its distance from the actual output is
  T = p E_K sqrt(1-D^(-sum_a(K_a-1))) <= p,
and tends to p at fixed L>=2 as r->infinity. Taking p=epsilon saturates
(4) asymptotically. This is deliberately a failure-budget construction:
all the apparent extra Bell allocation is placed on the globally bad
branch, and the actual receiver never emits more than the first Bells.
It cannot establish an ordinary-stock advantage or useful successful
extra allocation. This is why mean and pointwise/global-success contracts
must be distinguished.

6. SEPARATE EXTENSION: EXPLICIT CORRELATED INITIAL ACCESS

This extension assumes the actual initial state conditional on K factors
as rho_K tensor zeta_K^Z, where Z is an explicitly supplied receiver
register; zeta_K may be quantum. The complete classical K has the same iid
law, and Z can encode arbitrary correlations across slots. It does NOT
cover arbitrary source-Z entanglement changing that conditional product.
Let b=I(K:Z) in nats, charged as the supplied correlated-access resource.
This is a mathematical resource assumption, not an experimental estimate
or a proposed method to certify hidden memory.

For the same m,s,beta_m, one has

 (1-beta_m) Pr_omega(M>=m)
     <= epsilon + min(1,(b+ln 2)/(gamma_L*s)).                 (7)

For b=0 the sharper exponential (2), not (7), should be used.
If b_r=o(r), (4) remains true: choose integers m_r=o(r), m_r->infinity,
b_r/m_r->0. Thus sublinear actual initial label information cannot repair
a positive successful allocation density at vanishing global error.

Proof: let P_KYZ be the latent Bell-basis cq source above, now with quantum
state zeta_K on Z, and let
 Q_KYZ = sum_y p(y)|y><y| tensor [sum_K p(K|y)|K><K|] tensor zeta_y,
 zeta_y=sum_K p(K|y) zeta_K.
Then D(P||Q)=I(K:Z|Y)<=I(K:Z)=b. The inequality follows from the Markov
condition Y-K-Z: I(Y:Z|K)=0 and the chain rule. Under Q, conditional on Y,
K is independent of Z and retains the product posterior. Applying the
same receiver and tester to P and Q, the Q probability q of E_s is at
most exp(-gamma_L*s). If p is its actual P probability, binary relative-
entropy data processing yields
  b >= d_binary(p||q) >= p*gamma_L*s-h_2(p)
    >= p*gamma_L*s-ln 2.
Together with p>=(1-beta_m)Pr_omega(M>=m)-epsilon this proves (7).
Every receiver transcript and all bidirectional messages are already
included in the channel. Ordinary communication cannot create additional
information about K beyond the full latent Y, so unlike receiver-side
correlated access it cannot bypass this collective obstruction.

7. LIMITS / NOVELTY BOUNDARY / RECEIVING CONSEQUENCE

- This theorem uses the ACTUAL nu-tail queue. A chi-tail replacement is
  unnecessary and would alter the posterior obstruction.
- The proof is an elementary commuting-ensemble/posterior and binary-test
  argument. Historical novelty is UNKNOWN; internal validity and novelty
  are different. It is not a new noisy-search algorithm.
- It does not bound a receiver whose reference can retain extra Bell
  inventory outside the stipulated L-pair profiles, encode Bell pairs
  across different slots, or use a coherent profile superposition.
- No LOCC restriction is needed; the allowed reference interface itself
  is the decisive boundary. General collective entanglement distillation
  to differently allocated Bell registers is not excluded.
- The gamma_L scale makes the theorem asymptotic at fixed finite L. The
  regime L growing with r (and optimal dependence of L,r,epsilon,b) remains
  open. In particular this is not a sharp finite-L Bell-loss law for one
  queue, nor a proof that the O(log L) one-queue loss is optimal.
- A uniform-r useful pointwise-profile receiver at fixed L is impossible.
  A meaningful next constructive frontier would change the declared
  collective output interface (e.g. a global Bell register with an actual
  clock-compatible allocation/readout proof), rather than independently
  applying the old receiver at epsilon/r and calling its vacuity a result.
