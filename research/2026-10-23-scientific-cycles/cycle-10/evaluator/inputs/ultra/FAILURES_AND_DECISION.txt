DECISIVE FAILURES AND ONE CONTINUATION DECISION

1. Entanglement monotonicity on the unflagged nu queue alone does not force
logarithmic loss; the old chi-tail ER shortcut remains invalid. A raw local-
information/entropy bridge would either be one-way only or incur epsilon*L.
Those failed bridges are retained in loss_converse and its earlier candidate.
The CDF/coarse-readout proof avoids both problems.

2. Nested prefix queries show why QUERY COUNT is not a universal Bell-loss
measure: many distinct queries can restrict a safe prefix by rank1. This
is a genuine warning against a tempting false lower bound. Their exact rank
calculation and finite checks are retained. The universal SEP score proof
does not assume that loss equals number of queries or parity rank.

3. Mixed nu-tail Fourier erasure cannot restore a measured Bell: the explicit
postmeasurement example is separable. Giving the receiver a purification would
change its input resource. No such hidden resource is assumed.

4. A global Bell-projector observer DOES achieve constant loss at fixed error.
Thus support overlap alone cannot prove a fixed-error logL bound for ALL
quantum channels. The SEP restriction and supplied resource accounting are
load-bearing. Paid ancilla difference measurements likewise retain all true
prefix Bells; they consume counted entanglement and a priced seed/control.

5. Independence of an auxiliary marginal from K does not imply it tensor-factors
from the complete source. Source-correlated aid can break the common-map order
or latent Markov proof. The explicit preparation premise and an independent
counterexample to the weaker wording are retained in projection_audit.

6. Fixed clock labels must be deleted before comparing conditional-state
operators. Full clock-inclusive blocks are orthogonal and do not satisfy the
neighbor order literally. The CURRENT proof compares common conditional fibres
and restores clocks for the global distance. Earlier unreleased draft bytes,
their exact old hash and the correction procedure are preserved. Integer w/r
and the coarse converter's geometric trailing loss were also clarified.

7. Expected lnM is not lnE M. The final convex-secant bound handles E lnM
directly without assuming a tail cutoff, even if E M diverges. Earlier bounded
subproofs that still label this necessary bound open are superseded by current
PROOF(18), with their original bytes preserved. No expected-cost charge is
transferred merely from an initializer trace-distance triangle.

8. The independent per-copy epsilon/r receiver is a valid finite upper bound,
but was not a uniform-r solution at fixed L. The new arbitrary-channel strong
converse now excludes EVERY useful fixed-loss uniform-r receiver under the
same pointwise profile contract. Pooled distillation changes the contract and
was not used as a favorable replacement calculation.

DECISION. Retire the unchanged fixed-L uniform-r pointwise-profile search.
Develop a POOLED or ENCODED output interface only with an explicit clock-
compatible joint-memory decoupling/rate theorem and the strongest equally
informed ordinary-stock comparator. That is the remaining capital-development
question, not an achieved result. Optimal finite constants and resource/catalyst
tradeoff boundaries remain open. Completed R1, all engineering closures and
other owners' frontiers remain untouched; no background monitoring is promised.
