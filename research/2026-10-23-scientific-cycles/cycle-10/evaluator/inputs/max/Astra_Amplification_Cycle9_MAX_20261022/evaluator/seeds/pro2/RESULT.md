## Implementation and comparison boundary

The structural preparation algorithm can be linear under degree four. The
actual native Python implementation sorts canonical edge lists and is
conservatively O(n+m log m) comparison work and O(n+m) storage. Supplied
expression coloring and the sparse decoder use O(p+nq+m) arithmetic/storage
with a fixed certificate. Query acquisition, rational bit complexity,
floating residuals and assembly/probe errors remain charged.

Classical star/Hessian compression and acyclic substitution are incumbents;
the ordinary three-query forest operation and five-query full-class operation
remain controls. Finite-state decidability on bounded treewidth is established.
The new claim is the explicit nonempty universal closure and its intrinsic
atom consequence under the retained error model. The bounded primary search
does not establish historical priority. No NIST or molecular speedup follows.
