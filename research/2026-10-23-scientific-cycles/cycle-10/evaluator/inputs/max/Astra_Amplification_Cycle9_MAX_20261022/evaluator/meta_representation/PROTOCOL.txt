ASTRA CYCLE9 — FROZEN REPRESENTATION EXPERIMENT
22 October 2026. Written before any experimental search execution.

SCIENTIFIC QUESTION

Does Pro2's proved change of scientific representation materially reduce the
resource cost of its constructive closure search after ordinary cache repair
and antichain implementation are given equally to a strong control?

This is a new causal computational study of an already completed research
operation. It does not take over Pro2's rooted-atom continuation, rerun the
owner's verification suite, claim a new graph theorem, or compare LLM prompts.
The historical search outcomes were known when designing this experiment;
there is no blind novelty or discovery-performance claim.

THE THREE PRESPECIFIED ARMS

A: actual exact neighbor counts with saturated-port projection and the strong
   flag-dominance order. This is the completed monotone_ports control, not the
   weak raw-count or saturation-only failed attempt.
B: the proved occupancy quotient, but dominance of flags only at FIXED
   neighbor-support masks, terminal colors and actual degrees.
C: the proved occupancy quotient and its stronger support/flag dominance,
   corresponding to the final hazard_ports scientific representation.

All three retain actual total terminal degree, proper source realizability,
nonadjacent terminals, disjoint child interiors, global color permutations,
the exact pre-saturation merge test, and all L/R/S/P operation types. The
initial family is the cold path2 family. No saved basis, prior checkpoint,
final certificate or final family table is loaded by the search.

The same common worklist and minimal-mask antichain routine serves all arms.
The dominance keys are:
 A base=(a,b,countsL,countsR), bits=FL | (FR<<4).
 B base=(a,b,dL,NL,dR,NR), bits=FL | (FR<<4).
 C base=(a,b,dL,dR), bits=NL | (FL<<4) | (NR<<8) | (FR<<12).
Within each base, a profile dominates a larger bit mask by subset. Sorting
by popcount puts every proper submask first; keeping only masks with no
earlier submask therefore computes the same exact minimal antichain. This
removes the historical quadratic-versus-mask-antichain implementation
difference. B is a valid weaker guarantee order than C; the exact occupancy
quotient and ordinary fixed-support flag monotonicity justify it.

MATCHED ENGINE AND CACHES

The common engine uses identical admissibility, insertion, obsolete-family
removal, operation queueing and resource accounting. It uses the owner's
priority policy: product of current child family sizes, then key length and
lexicographic key. The resulting work order CAN differ because family sizes,
IDs and scientific dominance differ; no claim of identical transition
sequence is made. This is part of the representation-dependent search.

The transient-output reducer is uncached. All arms use the same bounded
family caches: expanded=512, groups=128, covers=512. Both cache aliases in
active_ports and monotone_ports are updated, so active_ports.apply does not
silently retain an unbounded expansion cache. Scalar-profile canonical/orbit
caches retain the same unbounded policy in all arms; they contain finite
four-color profiles and are separately reported. No checkpoint resume occurs.

The two exact source backends differ in primitive state layout, set/tuple
arithmetic, canonical ordering and transition implementation. Those differences
are inherent in this measured implementation. A versus B probes the occupancy
representation package, not a pure mathematical quotient with all CPU
instructions held fixed. B versus C isolates the additional proved order more
closely, using the same occupancy transition implementation.

RESOURCES, ORDER AND STOPPING

Sequential fresh processes, in order A then B then C, all PYTHONHASHSEED=0.
At most one run per arm. No outcome-based tuning, alternate cache sizes or
additional configurations. The process address-space cap is 512 MiB. Each arm
has at most 240 total CPU seconds and 300 elapsed seconds. The loop stops at
230 measured CPU seconds or 290 elapsed seconds to reserve time for saving;
OS soft/hard CPU limits are 238/240 seconds and the parent enforces 300 wall
seconds. The same family-record ceiling of 2500 applies to every arm.

Preserve ordinary resource stops and failures. They are not graph-coloring or
matrix-recovery obstructions. Closed means the worklist is exhausted; also
check, without re-running transitions, that every final active family's
admissible L/R/S/P key appears in the tested-key ledger. Both historical
representations already have source proofs; this new experiment does not
replace their universal correctness arguments or literal witness certificates.

REPORTING

Save exact source bindings, common driver, protocol hash before execution,
all discovered state families, origins, tested transition keys, history,
closure status, active/discovered family counts, primitive cache counts,
operation counts, body CPU/wall, and whole-process CPU/wall/peak RSS including
startup/import/serialization. Finite output paths and system metadata are
recorded; no sensitive environment is dumped.

One sample per condition does not estimate a timing distribution. Discrete
family/transition counts are more directly attributable to the representation
than an exact speed ratio. Development of the scientific quotient, search code,
new common driver, independent proof work, inference and integration have
unmeasured costs. No net discovery-cost, cross-domain transfer, historical
novelty, automatic abstraction-learning or recursive amplification claim
follows from faster closure here.

FAILURE CONDITIONS FOR THE INTERPRETATION

If the strong control closes within budget, it is false to say the abstraction
was necessary for feasibility under this budget. If the advantage disappears
after matched caching/antichains, do not credit the scientific quotient for
the historical timing difference. If outputs have an empty family or a
semantic mismatch, preserve it and do not claim a sound closure. A successful
representation speedup still does not show Astra learned to invent useful
representations on a subsequent independent problem.
