3. EXACT SOURCE ENTROPY AND FINITE-r CONVERSE

The law of J is p_j=(1-q^j)/L for j<L and

formula, with no D^(2L)-dimensional matrix diagonalization. A truncated
geometric distribution in (8) has mean <=q/(1-q). The maximum entropy at
that mean is bounded by the untruncated geometric entropy c(q), proving
C_L<=c(q). Conditional labels have entropy 2(L-k)lnD; the chain rule gives

For any extension of an output state x_AB, weak monotonicity gives

For completeness, the conditional-entropy continuity bound used here and
below is |S(A|B)_x-S(A|B)_y|<=2a ln dimA+g(a), when T(x,y)=a.
Proof: write x-y=a(gamma_+-gamma_-), with normalized positive Jordan parts.
The common state (x+a gamma_-)/(1+a)=(y+a gamma_+)/(1+a) has two
binary mixture descriptions. Conditional entropy of a mixture lies between
the average and that average plus the binary mixing entropy (concavity and
conditioning on a classical flag). Since each conditional entropy lies in
[-ln dimA,ln dimA], subtracting the two descriptions gives the stated bound.
This is the KNOWN Alicki–Fannes/Winter argument, reproduced to fix constants.
The bound is monotone in a, so an upper bound a<=epsilon suffices.
