1. DOTS' ACTUAL NEW SCIENCE

1.1 A stronger apparatus reference is now available

The retained Maastricht opposed-jet experiment is a 20 SLM, sub-kW gas-production
comparison. A different Cerawave 915 MHz pilot reports 198.9 L of 28.6 wt% nitric
acid, stated as 66.5 kg HNO3 equivalent, over 30.9 operating hours. The latter
hours are cumulative operating time, not a demonstrated uninterrupted endurance
run. The source uses triplicate titration and a gas-drop estimate; the two
estimates agree approximately, within about 12%, without closing a full
species-selective batch nitrogen inventory. [D1-D3]

The original primary pilot is McKinney et al., High-Power Closed-Loop Pilot
System for Nitric Acid Production Using Inductively Coupled Microwave Plasma,
Nitrogen 6(3), 51 (2025), DOI 10.3390/nitrogen6030051.
The ordinary absorption comparator is Vits et al., Absorption Chemistry of
Plasma-Generated NOx Gas for Green Nitric Acid Production, Processes 13(12),
3897 (2025), DOI 10.3390/pr13123897. These are different experiments and neither
supplies the missing matched captured-product comparison on the Maastricht
split-feed platform. Their exact public HTML/text is preserved in [D2].

The new pilot reference represents useful evidence acquisition. It prevents a
speculative component design from competing only against another favorable
component model. It does not justify importing the large pilot's RF efficiency,
absorber, pressure, scale or heat path into the smaller target apparatus.

1.2 The energy and heat claims are correctly separated

The paper's 50 kW absorbed plasma and 60.5 kW generator input, applied to the
reported batch, give about 5.2703 MJ/mol and 6.3771 MJ/mol respectively. Auxiliaries,
PSA oxygen, blowers, compression, off-periods and reset are additional unknowns.
This is a boundary translation, not a newly measured complete plant SEC. [D3]

The reported approximately 60 C recovery water against 25 C surroundings has

paper's prospective 25% heat-to-electric conversion is not admissible at that

1.3 The loop result changes which upgrade can be justified

For a fixed fresh source S and returned-N survival s, with per-pass capture e,
A=S+s(1-e)A and product Q=e A. Therefore

 Q=e S/[1-s(1-e)].

Improving e from .65 to .90 can lower fixed-power SEC by a factor 13/18 at s=0,
but gives no recurring product improvement at s=1. In the latter case it changes
the circulating inventory, not the long-run fresh-N production rate. An upgrade
may still be useful for other reasons; the capture percentage alone does not
establish its energy advantage. [D3]

Dots then removes the artificially fixed fresh-source premise. Let post-source
fixed-N flow be A=f(R), return R=(1-e)A, and Q=e A, with other controls specified.
At a differentiable implicit branch,

 dQ/de = A(1-f'(R))/[1-(1-e)f'(R)].

The sign depends on the source response. The denominator condition is not a
general stability theorem. The actual discrete pass map is locally stable when
|(1-e)f'(R)|<1. Independent review caught and corrected this distinction. The
original wording and review are preserved. [D3,D4]

Three affine positive maps share baseline e=.65, A=1, R=.35 and Q=.65:

 f(R)=1;                upgraded Q=.90;
 f(R)=.65+R;            upgraded Q=.65;
 f(R)=.475+1.5R;        upgraded Q=171/340, about .50294.

Their pass maps remain stable throughout e in [.65,.90]. The 1.5 coefficient is
not a survival probability; it can be decomposed into survival one plus fresh
production .475+.5R. These are nitrogen-balanced logical countermodels, not
validated plasma kinetic laws. They prove non-identification within the stated
unrestricted source-response class. They do not establish that the actual torch
has slope greater than one or that a real 90% absorber would reduce production.

A finite intervention needs f over the traversed return interval, or direct
paired operation data. A local derivative alone cannot justify .65-to-.90
extrapolation. Changes in humidity, O2/Ar composition, flow, temperature, pressure
or power can also change f; a scalar law is not automatically transferable.

1.4 Prior capital was used in a new constructive calculation

The Cycle7 oxidation event-rate convention, molar conversion, 20 SLM normalization
and dimer accounting were actually reused. With net capture chemistry
3 NO2 + H2O -> 2 HNO3 + NO, one captured acid-N needs 1.5 NO-oxidation turnovers
when regenerated NO returns locally. This changes the previous dry-conditioning
calculation instead of relabeling it as a capture plant. [D1,D5]

Within serial once-through PFR/ideal absorber staging with local NO regeneration,
immediate absorption simultaneously maximizes relevant NO and O2 concentrations
at fixed oxidation extent. It therefore gives a gas-reaction-volume infimum,
including arbitrary admitted NO2/N2O4 partition. Finite staged trains approach
the infimum under the stated idealization. At complete capture of terminal
oxidized N and the common 100 ppm NO endpoint, the numerical values are about
574.84 L ordinary and 578.08 L split. These are quadrature results, not physical
confidence bounds or installed plant volumes. [D5]

An explicit selective regenerated-NO return to the PFR inlet beats that serial
infimum, giving about 402.88/407.90 L with the same net product and tail. It closes
its own ideal recycle but assigns no qualifying costs to separation, liquid
inventory, heat, pressure or reset. This is a valid counterexample to a universal
routing extension; it is not a practical device. Both arms receive the same
architecture choices. Preserving this counterexample is constructive scientific
progress: it identifies the exact operation omitted by a tempting general bound.

3. CHALLENGING THE PREVIOUS OBSERVABLE-INVENTION RECOMMENDATION

3.1 What the evidence actually supports

The new Dots work makes the distinction between prediction and intervention
sharper. A baseline fit can leave an action's ranking unidentified. More accurate
numerical solution of the same insufficient model does not supply the missing
response. The evidence therefore supports investigating how Astra recognizes
and resolves observational equivalence relevant to a scientific decision.

However, no unconventional measured quantity was invented here. The relevant
fixed-N flows, inventories, product assay and total energy are standard
conservation and process measurements. The feedback derivative is elementary.
The substantive improvement came from recovering a stronger primary apparatus
reference, applying those ordinary methods to its actual boundary, and preserving
counterexamples instead of promoting optimistic projections. It is not evidence
that a new learned-observable architecture outperformed mature scientific practice.

This is a useful counterexample to a too-specific meta diagnosis: the missing
capability may be access to the necessary intervention evidence, rather than
inability to conceptualize the measurement. Dots now knows the decisive experiment,
but knowing it does not create the observations or the physical control access.
The no-spending/no-outreach boundary makes that separation operationally important.

3.2 Rival explanations that remain plausible

- Better problem-domain knowledge and sharper resource contracts let the same
  general model identify previously missed effects. This is cumulative system
  capability, without identifying a changed general invention policy.
- Existing scientific machinery can suffice when connected to the correct
  operational question. A new research architecture is not needed for each
  useful inference.
- In physical programmes, feedback from reality is often the limiting resource.
  Even mathematically correct broad model families cannot determine an actual
  plant's response without identification.
- In purely mathematical programmes, proofs themselves can supply decisive
  feedback. Their recent successes should not be suppressed because the physical
  programmes have an evidence bottleneck.
- A useful bound can depend on operation topology. The selective-return example
  shows that changing an admitted connection can matter more than improving a
  solver or reasoning prompt. That is a direction to investigate scientifically,
  not a universal instruction to add recycle or relax all bounds.

These explanations can coexist. None of the source records randomizes strategy
or isolates a recursive improvement mechanism. Treating them as exclusive would
force a stronger conclusion than the evidence supports.

3.3 Where the observed learning resides

The recorded receiving uses are mostly successive results within persistent
programmes: Dots uses its own oxidation interface; the mathematical owners use
earlier native lemmas; Ultra extends its own counted queue. The evaluator's
independent receiver experiment establishes portability of one completed
mathematical argument, but the strong ordinary receiver also succeeds. This
pattern supports accumulated domain expertise and transferable source knowledge.
It does not yet isolate a system-wide discovery policy improving across domains.

Max's source records describe preservation, source reading and routing, not a
causal test that the integration system enabled those inventions. Do not infer
that a local index caused a theorem because both occur in the same cycle. At
the same time, failure to prove that causal effect is not proof that integration
is useless: actual source availability is a necessary practical dependency for
many receiving investigations. The distinction is between indispensable memory
and a demonstrated mechanism for learning to invent better future questions.

4. CONSEQUENTIAL META-EXPERIMENT: ADMISSION AND LIMITATION

No currently supplied Dots operation can test whether a learned strategy predicts
the actual finite capture intervention better than a strong ordinary scientist:
the decisive paired data are absent. The pilot's cumulative acid curves and
per-pass figures do not supply an independently held-out intervention response.
The three affine examples are constructed to demonstrate that absence. Using
one of them as a secretly selected truth would test a synthetic oracle, not the
physical claim. A fresh agent duel on differentiating those formulas would also
likely repeat the earlier saturation problem.

A serious next empirical test would require an already measured public intervention
series whose initial observations admit materially different action rankings.
The system would need to propose an attainable distinguishing observation or
experiment, then make a consequential prospective prediction against withheld
actual outcomes. The mature control receives the same evidence, standard causal
and feedback methods, ordinary experimental design and full acquisition costs.
The question must be difficult because the scientific ambiguity is consequential,
not because source information was artificially withheld from the baseline.

That experiment should be admitted only when those data and controls exist.
Neither the present archive nor a small logical counterexample provides them.
Root can investigate a different existing open dataset or a proof-domain analogue;
no owner should be reassigned merely to populate this design. Creating the next
scientific operation and proving its receiving benefit remains the substantive
work, not writing another evaluation framework.

For immediate decisions, the positive result is the source-grounded change in
Dots' question and reference, together with a precise failure of an unqualified
absorber-improvement inference. Preserve it as scientific capital. Do not advertise
it as the evaluator's newly demonstrated intelligence-amplification mechanism.
