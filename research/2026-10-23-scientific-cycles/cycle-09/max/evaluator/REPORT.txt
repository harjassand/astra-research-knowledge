EXECUTIVE ASSESSMENT

Astra is making cumulative mathematical inventions, and it has produced a real
instance of an invention making a subsequent scientific computation much easier.
It has not demonstrated a generally improving ability to invent, or recursive
scientific intelligence. Those are different conclusions and both matter.

The strongest new meta result is a controlled test of Pro2's constructive proof
search. After giving a strong exact-count control the same cache repair and
antichain implementation, the complete proved abstraction used 19.28 times less
observed CPU and 3.33 times less peak memory. A third, intermediate arm identifies
an important cause: the theorem allowing one sufficient scientific guarantee to
replace another contributes much more than merely forgetting multiplicities.
All three arms finish, however. This is a substantial benefit for this search,
not a newly feasible theorem, lower total discovery cost, or general intelligence
gain. Scientific invention, implementation and integration costs remain unknown.

The previous diagnosis placed too much weight on inventing observables as a
missing faculty. Ultra has now invented an admissible information-acquisition
protocol, Pro2 succeeds by discarding information, Pro1 changes the quantity it
bounds, and Pro3 develops a multilevel construction for its existing question.
Dots has identified a conventional measurement whose actual data are absent.
These cases do not support one universal prescription for creativity.

Decision: preserve the restored autonomous architecture and substantially redirect
the evaluator's experimental emphasis. The most promising current investment is
learning to create scientifically valid operations that change which questions
Astra can answer: representations, inference procedures, measurements or physical
connections whose actual admissibility and benefit are demonstrated. The present
evidence most strongly supports proof-backed representation and substitution in
exact research. It does not establish that this is the best route across science.

The next positive claim must require a separate investigation to obtain a new,
verified scientific consequence using an acquired capability, against a strong
ordinary method with invention and integration costs charged. A further generation
must then improve the capability itself. That requirement keeps the ambition large
without treating one fast kernel or one successful transfer as recursive growth.

2. THE SCIENTIFIC TRAJECTORY HAS BECOME MORE CUMULATIVE

2.1 Pro1: an operational consequence from a better analytical representation

Pro1's same-evolving-infinite-bath communication programme now proves

  Q(V_beta,tau) >= 3/2 + (ln 2 / 8192) beta^3,  0 < beta <= 1/8.

The code is deterministic and unassisted; one code sequence is independent of
the initializer and has vanishing full-diamond error pointwise for each fixed
normal initializer. Beta and the inner block are fixed before the outer horizon.
There is no physical bath reset, access, fresh bath, hidden resource or terminal
return promise. These are material operational quantifiers, not decoration.

The new operation contracts the exact coherent response with the scientifically
needed input before bounding the remainder. It inherits Cycle7's explicit H and
scalar first-order certificate, derives an all-order endpoint expression, and
improves the admissible temporal window. The old uncontracted norm was bounding
too much. The source now contains the positivity, entropy, inward drift, actual
root and coding transfer needed to turn that mathematical gain into an admitted
communication statement. This is actual receiving use of earlier capital.

It is not a practical quantum device. The illustrated k=128 input has rank
3^128, and the coding constants are enormous. The exponent is an achievable
floor, not the sharp capacity exponent. The exact nonlinear response is closed;
the owner-selected frontier is the one-sided positive spectrum of H and whether
an admissible positive parametrization allows longer temporal range. New Gibbs
or exponential densities introduce coherences outside the pair-supported lemma.
The preserved four-endpoint counterexample makes that limitation concrete.

2.2 Pro2: a constructive intrinsic graph theorem

Pro2 has advanced from a supplied-expression construction to every finite simple
two-connected chordless graph of treewidth at most two and maximum degree four.
It supplies at most four coordinate actions and a canonical least-squares inverse
with operator error at most sqrt(3/2)||E||_F and Frobenius error at most sqrt(2)||E||_F.
The triangle has its separate three-action treatment. The full arbitrary-real
four-action question for the broader treewidth-two, degree-four class stays open.

Two proved reductions matter. A degree-four terminal cannot participate in another
join or extension, so its remaining color information can become existential after
the real valid merge. At an unsaturated terminal, actual total degree, neighbor-
color presence and repeat flags suffice for admitted future operations; exact
multiplicities are unnecessary. A further monotonicity theorem permits replacement
by guarantees with smaller hazard sets. These are claims about real realizations
and legal future operations, not lossy numerical compression.

The all-depth finite closure contains 73 nonempty families and 11,142 explicit
transition witnesses. A support-preserving normal form covers the intrinsic atom
class. Actual original-action receivers and independent inverse checks are in the
source. Stronger ordinary solutions remain visible: the A_L family already has
a sharp three-action solution, so a four-action demo on that family would lose.

The next missing interface is an actually marked attachment vertex with its real
residual degree. A normalizer's convenient pair of terminals does not make an
arbitrary root available for block composition. This is a meaningful next question,
not a reason to repeat the completed closure or to claim an all-real impossibility
from failure of a coordinate coloring. [S2; CONSTRUCTIVE_CAPITAL.txt]

2.3 Pro3: a complete structural criterion with a strong ordinary comparator

For the admitted proposal-start, one-KL-budget, full-return delayed-acceptance
hybrid, Pro3 proves the exact convergence criterion

  R_n -> 0  iff  eta_n log n / [1 + |kappa_n-eta_n| A_n] -> infinity.

All policies are fixed within a run, and the supremum permits support to grow
between runs. The upper proof retains actual accepted outflow and controls the
whole first-hit exponent. The lower construction uses native expanding/coalescing
proposal trees: repeated local discrepancies can accumulate in a way a single
crossing does not show. A corrected allocation keeps the complete KL budget valid.

The proof uses and extends earlier energy and full-return capital. It does not
show a better practical sampler: ordinary independence MH converges on the adverse
hybrid families and remains the comparator. The frozen small finite lower bounds
are all nonpositive and are not numerical evidence of the asymptotic error floor.
The asymptotic result is analytical. The current frontier is the sharp rate near
v_n of order loglog n, and whether v_n alone determines it. [S3; MATH_DELTA_AND_PROCESS]

2.4 Ultra: observation invention is already occurring

Ultra constructs output-only LOCC on the actual Bell-prefix/product-tail queue.
It approximately normalizes the complete joint state, including hidden memory,
current clocks and all transcripts, without replacing the source by an easier
one. Deterministic disjoint parity queries have a proved fresh conditional law;
adaptive search and affine decoding preserve the retained coherence.

At global half-trace error epsilon, the reference keeps at least max(1,K-3Q)
Bell pairs, with Q=O(log L+log(1/epsilon)). At fixed local dimension D, communication
is logarithmic in each direction and the local controlled-add/decode count is O(L).
Symbol encoding, implementation as D varies, integer control, disposal and all
bad branches remain charged. A matched converse proves necessary information in
each direction or actual initially correlated access. Repeated individual site
measurements cannot be assumed to supply fresh iid answers.

This is a concrete new observable and an admissible operation, not simply a good
idea for one. It changes what the source can supply approximately. It does not
establish optimal Bell loss, an ordinary-stock/formation advantage, or a collective
receiver useful uniformly as the number of blocks grows. Those are the selected
next substantive boundaries. [S4; CONSTRUCTIVE_CAPITAL.txt]

3. WHAT THE NEW EVIDENCE CHANGES IN THE DIAGNOSIS

The hypothesis that Astra mainly produces exclusions because it cannot invent
constructive mechanisms is now too broad. Pro1, Pro2 and Ultra supply constructive
operations, while Pro3 supplies a sharp upper/lower classification. Their direct
dependencies show accumulation within persistent programmes. Scientific progress
has become more cumulative than the earlier sequence of unrelated component
screens. That does not retroactively validate those earlier choices.

The hypothesis that instructing researchers to invent more questions or observables
is the missing amplifier also overreaches. Ultra already did it without a new
validated evaluator prescription. Pro1 retained its question and operator but
changed the proof; Pro2 safely forgot information; Pro3 resolved its existing
question through a new geometry. Dots's decisive missing observables are ordinary
process measurements. The common word 'representation' can be made so broad that
it explains everything after the event; that is not a predictive theory.

A stronger current interpretation is that domain understanding, persistent
ownership and exact feedback let these capable models gradually identify the
scientifically necessary quantity and admissible operation. This is consistent
with the observations but is not a randomized comparison of warm continuations
against fresh researchers. Nor does it establish that parallel contributors were
necessary: their distinct scientific contributions are visible, while the cost
of a capable principal doing everything alone is unobserved.

There are at least three materially different barriers:

1. An important claim is false or too broad under the actual model. A construction
   or counterexample can resolve it. Pro3 and Ultra's exact/approximate distinction
   illustrate this; more favorable numerical output cannot repair a false premise.
2. The claim may be true, but its present representation makes inference too hard.
   Pro1's contraction and Pro2's search reduction address this. More raw enumeration
   or a generic stronger-model prompt can leave the core obstruction unchanged.
3. Available observations do not identify the proposed intervention. Dots exposes
   this barrier. Exact solution of the same insufficient model is not empirical
   qualification, and increased agent count cannot replace missing measurements.

These are diagnostic distinctions drawn from the cases, not a new universal
taxonomy. They suggest different scientific work, so allocation should preserve
owners who understand their current obstruction rather than centrally impose one
allegedly superior reasoning workflow.

4. THE NEW CONTROLLED EXPERIMENT

4.1 Question and strong control

The historical Pro2 record mixes semantic abstraction with cache changes, different
reducers and checkpoint continuation. Its early exact-count run reached roughly
2.19 GB, then a bounded-cache continuation closed successfully. The final quotient
subsequently closed much faster. Crediting the entire memory reduction to the
mathematics, or claiming that the quotient made the first positive closure possible,
would be wrong.

The new experiment asks a narrower causal question: after matching ordinary
implementation improvements, how much of the constructive search burden changes
when the proved representation and guarantee order change? It tests actual code
from an important successful investigation, rather than another easy LLM exercise.

Before any experimental search, the protocol and executable inputs were hash-
frozen. Every arm starts cold from path2, without a saved family basis or final
certificate. One deterministic worklist policy, a common minimal-mask antichain
routine and matched bounded caches are used. Both defining/importing cache aliases
are replaced, so an unbounded cache cannot silently survive in the control.

A: exact neighbor counts with saturated-port projection and flag dominance. This
   is the strongest completed conventional source control, not its weak predecessor.
B: occupancy representation, retaining flag dominance only at fixed support masks.
C: occupancy plus the proved stronger support-and-flag dominance.

The intermediate arm was specified before execution. It separates the contribution
of forgetting exact multiplicities from the contribution of the stronger order.
The state-product priority policy is shared, but actual queue order can differ as
representation changes family sizes. That is a disclosed mediator of this search,
not a claim that every CPU instruction or transition sequence is identical.

One fresh sequential process ran per arm: 240 CPU seconds, 300 wall seconds and
512 MiB address space. There was no outcome-based tuning or second trial. The
history was known at design time; this is not a blind test of scientific invention.

The stronger guarantee order is therefore a major contributor. 'Compression made
the solver fast' omits the more informative mathematical operation: proving that
one realizable guarantee can safely stand in for a more hazardous obligation.

The resulting final obligations differ because dominance changes the closure
basis. Each still supports the same admitted all-depth grammar statement under
the preserved source proofs. All three finish within the preregistered limits.
It would be false to declare the abstraction necessary by choosing a tighter time
cap after seeing these numbers.

A distinct reviewer analytically checked the reducer/order semantics, cache
aliases and original realization requirements. Before execution, new focused
adapter tests compared A/C with their original reducers and all arms with explicit
brute subset relations. They cover fixed real grammar fixtures, saturated ports,
recoloring, idempotence and coverage. A later read-only check verifies frozen
hashes, process outputs, origin degrees, active history and complete final-key

This is not a new literal-witness certification of the graph theorem. Its
scientific interpretation relies on the original proofs plus focused validation
of the changed adapter. A pre-freeze double-serialization defect was corrected
and retained in the development record. Nothing was tuned after outcomes.

The three timed processes used 210.163 summed CPU seconds. Adapter checks used
12.712 CPU seconds. Peak RSS is measured on Linux; the frozen runner's resource
units are Linux-specific and must not be transplanted as a macOS certificate.
Source extraction, proof discovery, model inference, coding and integration costs
are not completely measured. A/B also changes primitive state layout; B/C isolates
the additional order more closely. One selected obligation and one run per arm
do not estimate a timing distribution or population-wide benefit.

The roughly 141.5 CPU seconds saved per final search do not alone justify a major
new infrastructure investment. If invention cost is C and savings per receiving
use are s, pure time amortization would require more than C/s uses; C is unknown,
and repeatedly rerunning an already solved theorem is not a scientific benefit.
The larger possibility is that a similar operation permits an independently
important inference that was otherwise inaccessible. This study does not show

5. THE DEEPER OPPORTUNITY, AND ITS STRONGEST CHALLENGE

A scientific representation should be judged by the conclusions and operations it
preserves, not by how small its encoding is. In the exact setting, two concrete
states can be identified when all relevant admitted continuations observe the
same property. A usable abstract operation also needs a sound way to map valid
inputs and compose them. In constructive problems, the existence of real witnesses
must survive: merely predicting a favorable terminal bit is insufficient.

Sometimes equality is unnecessarily restrictive. A proved simulation or dominance
relation permits a less hazardous realizable guarantee to cover a harder state.
Pro2's C-versus-B comparison measures the benefit of that additional permission.
This is familiar ground in abstract interpretation and program synthesis; it is
not a new general principle discovered here. The novel meta evidence is its
controlled contribution to this particular ongoing scientific programme.

Information acquisition is the complementary case. If states that the scientist
would like to identify actually have different consequences, an attainable query
or intervention must distinguish them, with its disturbance and resource cost.
Ultra supplies a source-specific positive example. Dots supplies a case where
the measurement is conceptualized but unavailable. Treating both as an invitation
to invent a prettier latent representation would conceal the empirical barrier.

The high-ceiling candidate is to make Astra better at constructing these new
scientific operations itself: a new sufficient representation, a new inference
or observation primitive, or a new physical connection that changes the reachable
scientific question. The object learned would be executable or mathematically
specified, with an admissibility argument and a receiving use. A phrase such as
'think in better representations' supplies none of those things.

For proof-backed abstraction, a concrete future construction can propose candidate
state features and transformer rules, seek counterexamples against concrete
operations, refine the representation, prove the surviving rule, and use it in a
new inquiry. The independent control must have ordinary abstraction refinement,
automata minimization, symmetry and domain theory, not naive enumeration. ATLAS
already learns useful abstractions and transformers for program synthesis using
interpolation and constraint solving [L1]. Astra would need to improve its reach
or application to genuinely scientific operations, not rediscover that slogan.

The main objection is powerful: this may remain domain-specific theorem engineering
performed by an unchanged model. Our current study supplies the abstraction to
all relevant implementations; it does not teach the model to invent it. Even a
successful new receiver would show one transferable capability, not a general
discovery faculty. This objection remains unresolved and must control the claim.

6. ALTERNATIVE STRATEGIES CONSIDERED

Long-lived autonomous scientists with more complementary subagents remain the
best supported operating base. The actual contributors supplied distinct lemmas,
counterexamples and constructions that principals integrated. The evidence does
not justify imposing an optimal number of agents, routine fresh resets or a new
central question selector. Strong conventional research repeatedly matched the
evaluator's prescribed reasoning strategies. We should stop treating richer
instructions themselves as a demonstrated capability acquisition.

Reusable executable and provable scientific operations deserve a larger share of
the evaluator's research effort because their admissibility and receiving benefit
can be tested. The new experiment supplies a positive local result. But a general
platform is premature until a second investigation demonstrates a consequential
gain including adaptation cost. Preserve and reuse actual operations now; do not
turn owners into infrastructure producers or require a universal intermediate
language before they can investigate their own questions.

Stronger empirical feedback could be more transformative for physical science.
Dots has already reached an identification boundary that more exact simulation
cannot remove. Under current resources, the legitimate next possibilities are
existing measured intervention series, open experimental data, or a different
owned operation with available evidence. No new laboratory, spending or outreach
is authorized. A hidden synthetic source law would not test improved scientific
inference about the real pilot. It would only test a constructed oracle.

Foundational mathematics remains a serious high-value route. It has exact feedback,
and the programmes now produce operational theorems, intrinsic graph coverage and
sharp structural criteria. That does not make each theorem revolutionary. Broader
consequences, historical comparison and the relation to major questions still
matter. The appropriate response is independent consequential research by the
owners, not abandoning mathematics because practical engineering screens stalled.

Automated hypothesis tournaments, evolutionary search and memory structures have
external evidence worth studying, but their evaluations often have a supplied
search space and fixed objective. Arbor reports transfer of an optimized search
harness to other tasks and explicitly limits its present claims about open-ended
science [L2]. Co-Scientist and Robin report experimentally tested biomedical
hypotheses with scientists and real laboratory work [L3,L4]. Those are stronger
connections to reality than a model-only ranking. They do not show that adopting
their coordination structure without their evidence access would solve Astra's
physical bottleneck, or prove repeated recursive discovery improvement.

Direct model retraining might change the general invention policy, but this cycle
contains no matched training experiment, weight update or attributable improvement
of that kind. We should neither claim it nor forbid it as a future route. The
accessible near-term mechanism is system-level improvement through retained
operations, evidence and tested research capabilities, with fixed models entirely
consistent with the present observations.

7. WHAT WOULD CONSTITUTE RECURSIVE AMPLIFICATION

There are three progressively stronger claims:

First, earlier science helps produce later science. This is now observed: Pro1's
exact identity changes a later achievable rate; Pro2's port theory supports an
intrinsic constructive theorem; Ultra's exact queue structure supports a new
receiver; Dots's oxidation convention changes a later capture calculation.

Second, an acquired operation improves an independent investigation against its
strongest ordinary approach. Prior source transfer is real, but the strongest
control also solved the previous evaluator theorem. The new representation test
shows local computation benefit on the donor task, not this independent receiving
advantage. We have not yet established this broader claim at the level sought.

Third, using the acquired capability helps create a better capability, which then
improves another independent scientific investigation. That would begin to test
recursive system improvement. One chain is still not exponential growth: repeated
generations, novelty, significance, changing difficulty and all resource costs
would need to be examined. No growth rate is estimated here.

For the proposed operation-learning mechanism, the decisive test is therefore a
receiver with an actual unresolved scientific obligation, not a small adjacent
identity. The new operation must preserve the original objective, information and
resource contract, obtain a verified consequence, and survive a matched ordinary
control. If all value comes from task-specific hand engineering that costs more
than the receiver saves, or the control obtains the same consequence, the claimed
amplification must be weakened. If later learned operations fail to transfer, the
recursive hypothesis fails even if the donor theorem is valuable.

The evaluator retains ownership of finding and executing that next discriminating
test. No owner is reassigned to populate it. No suitable independent receiver is
claimed merely by renaming one of their still-owned frontiers. This cycle delivers
a completed causal diagnosis and a provisional scientific direction, not a fake
second generation or an unscheduled future experiment.

8. STRATEGIC DECISION AND NEXT ALLOCATION

Preserve the restored architecture. Redirect the evaluator from generic reasoning
prescriptions toward investigating new admissible scientific operations and their
actual receiving consequences. Treat proof-backed representations and substitution
as the strongest currently evidenced candidate, while keeping empirical access,
foundational mathematical invention and other mechanisms live alternatives.

Pro1 retains its independent same-bath programme and the selected positive-spectrum
frontier. Pro2 retains stable sparse recovery and the actual marked-root interface.
Pro3 retains sampling and its selected sharp-rate/same-v question. Each may choose
a stronger consequential question in light of newer local work. They are not
allocated replicas of the evaluator's method.

Ultra extends scientific capital through its actual loss/error/access or collective
receiver boundary, or another genuine connection of comparable significance. Max
integrates the new generation and the explicit review correction through the
existing authorized private repository workflow. Dots continues independent
nitrogen science at an identifiable complete-loop operation, with freedom to
select another justified question if the needed evidence is inaccessible.

The six prompts contain the actual source identities, native seeds, mathematical
or physical starting contract, strongest comparator, current frontier and separate
scientific success/nonclaim boundaries. They preserve ownership and agent-handled
private delivery. No branch is asked to repeat this meta experiment, install a new
platform or terminate its long-term mission at its next source checkpoint.

Evidence that would change this direction includes a successful independent
receiving use with all costs charged; repeated failure of representation invention
against mature ordinary methods; a source of real intervention data changing the
empirical opportunity; or a substantially more consequential result from another
mechanism. A second local speedup alone would not settle the architecture question.
