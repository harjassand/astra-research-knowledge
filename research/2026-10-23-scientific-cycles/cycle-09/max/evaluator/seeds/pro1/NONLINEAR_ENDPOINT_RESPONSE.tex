\begin{abstract}
The preceding checkpoint solved the full-prefix susceptibility exactly.
The remaining operational restriction was a coarse nonlinear bound on the
temporal block length. We retain the same explicit input direction and prove
an exact all-order response formula. Common output charges force the two
histories selected by this input to agree on every spectator move/stay
choice. Unitarity then cancels the potentially extensive output-charge factor.
The surviving endpoint operators form a positive-weight Bernstein sum,
while their final bath overlaps remain intact.

The input-weighted remainder is bounded by
\(b^2[v_k+\binom{k}{2}(k-2)]\), uniformly on the entire coherent prefix,
where \(b=\beta^2\). The actual density is positive, has entropy above
\(3k/2\), and has strict exact inward operator drift whenever \(bk^2\le1/16\).
The inherited root-retaining coding operation therefore gives, for every
fixed \(0<\beta\le1/8\),
\[
 \Qcap(V_\beta,\tau)\ge
 \frac32+\frac{\ln2}{8192}\beta^3,
\]
using one deterministic initializer-independent code sequence with pointwise
vanishing full-diamond error for every fixed normal initializer. The bath is
never reset or accessed; no terminal return is imposed.

Exact finite counterexamples show why the first-order scalar certificate
does not persist coefficientwise, why a deleted history call does not replace
the original input optimizer, and why general charge-preserving coherences
need not satisfy the two-endpoint formula. This is an achievable lower bound,
not a sharp capacity exponent, efficient code construction or priority claim.
\end{abstract}

At most \(k\) initial colors are consumed; final untouched tails agree in

For a prefix density \(\zeta\), define

 H_\zeta=\Tr_F[(I\otimes\zeta)G_1]+2Q-kI,\qquad

\begin{align}
 v_k&=\min_{\zeta}\Tr(\sigma H_\zeta^2)
 =\frac{k(k-1)}8-\frac18

The walk \(S_n\) is simple symmetric and starts at zero. The minimum admits

 \frac{k^2}{32}\le v_k\le\frac{k(k-1)}8,\quad

Also \(H=H^*\), \([H,Q]=[H,\sigma]=0\), and \(H\) is input-offdiagonal.

Here \(h\) is the unmatched earlier push count, \(d\) the unmatched

\section{Exact response from paired coherent histories}

\begin{theorem}[All-order endpoint response]\label{thm:response}
For every \(k\ge2\) and \(0\le b\le1\), there is a Hermitian operator
polynomial \(F_k(b)\) on the entire prefix \(F\) such that
\begin{align}
 \Tr_A[(\sigma H\otimes I)\mathcal G_k(b)]
   &=b(1-b)F_k(b),\qquad F_k(0)=v_kI,\label{eq:Fidentity}\\
 \norm{F_k(b)-v_kI}
   &\le N[1-(1-b)^{k-2}]
    \le N(k-2)b.\label{eq:Fbound}
\end{align}
In particular
\begin{equation}\label{eq:contracted}
 \norm{\Tr_A[(\sigma H\otimes I)R_b]}
 \le b^2v_k+b(1-b)N[1-(1-b)^{k-2}]
 \le b^2[v_k+N(k-2)].
\end{equation}
Every inequality is an operator norm bound, not an expectation at one
selected prefix.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Charge selection and the cancellation of output charge}

For a stay mask \(M\subseteq\{0,\ldots,k-1\}\), let \(U_M\) be the ordered
word with \(I\) at \(M\) and \(U\) elsewhere. Its coherent amplitude is
\[
 \alpha^{k-|M|}(i\beta)^{|M|}U_M.
\]
On a bulk computational charge pattern \(x\), the output charge at a site
is \(x_\ell\) for a stay and \(1-x_\ell\) for a move. Suppose two initial
patterns differ only at endpoints \(i,j\), with opposite charges there.
A common output charge word forces the two histories to make identical
choices at every spectator and complementary choices at both endpoints.

Fix the common spectator stay set \(S\). Its paired scalar weight is
\[
 w_S(b)=b^{|S|}a^{k-2-|S|},\qquad
 \sum_{S\subseteq[k]\setminus\{i,j\}}w_S(b)=1.
\]
The endpoints supply \(ba\). Use a ket stay mask
\(s=(s_i,s_j)\), with ket input charges \(01\), and let \(R_s\) denote
the compressed raw cross-history operator. The bra mask is complementary:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
s&\text{bra stay mask}&\text{phase sign}&
       \text{output endpoint charge}\\ \hline
00&11&-1&1\\
01&10&+1&2\\
10&01&+1&0\\
11&00&-1&1
\end{array}
\]
Each \(R_s\) has norm at most one. This retains all input color and final
bath overlaps.

Keep the spectator calls fixed to \(U\) or \(I\), with only the endpoints
equal to \(V_\beta\). This auxiliary word is unitary. Its offdiagonal
input block in \(W^*W\) vanishes, and the four terms have a common \(ba\)
factor. Hence, for \(0<b<1\), and then as a polynomial identity,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:unitarity}
 -R_{00}+R_{01}+R_{10}-R_{11}=0.
\end{equation}
The spectator output charge is constant on the selected charge-pattern
block. Equation~\eqref{eq:unitarity} cancels that entire contribution to
\(W^*QW\), and also cancels the constant one in the endpoint charge.
The surviving block in \(Q-W^*QW\) is
\[
 R_{10}-R_{01},
\]
a difference of two contractions. There is no factor \(k\) from
\(\norm Q\).

Let \(B_{ij}^S\) be this block, its adjoint and their direct sum over
the complete charge patterns. The pattern pairs are disjoint, so
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Bnorm}
 \norm{B_{ij}^S}\le2.
\end{equation}
Equivalently it is the \(i,j\) endpoint component of the first coefficient
of the ordered word with spectator calls \(S\) skipped, embedded on the
original input space. This is an analytic representation only; the actual
communication process still uses and counts all \(k\) calls.

Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:A}
 A_{ij,S}=\Tr_A[(\sigma H_{ij}\otimes I)B_{ij}^S].
\end{equation}
The original \(k\)-call \(H_{ij}\) remains in this formula. It is not replaced
by an optimizer for a shorter word. Exact history pairing now gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Bernstein}
 F_k(b)=\sum_{i<j}\ \sum_{S\subseteq[k]\setminus\{i,j\}}
          b^{|S|}a^{k-2-|S|}A_{ij,S}.
\end{equation}
Each \(A_{ij,S}\) is Hermitian by input partial-trace cyclicity. No
commutation of \(H_{ij}\) with \(B_{ij}^S\) is required.

The positive spectator weights arise after matching the two coherent
histories. Equation~\eqref{eq:Bernstein} does not make the physical
device a classical independent-error channel.

\subsection{The weighted trace norm that makes the estimate uniform}

\begin{lemma}[Endpoint trace budget]\label{lem:trace}
For the actual inherited \(H\),
\[
 \norm{\sigma H_{ij}}_1\le\frac14,\qquad
 \norm{A_{ij,S}}\le\frac12.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Conditional on a complete charge pattern with \(q\) colors, the input
color weight is \(I/2^q\). In the first case of \eqref{eq:Hblocks},
\[
 |P(I-\SWAP)|=I-\SWAP,\qquad
 2^{-q}\Tr(I-\SWAP)=\frac12.
\]
The swap has normalized trace \(1/2\) on two qubits. In the critical
case \(|P/2|=I/2\), with the same normalized trace. The final case is
zero. The two orientations have identical singular-value sums, while
their combined endpoint-charge probability under \(\sigma\) is \(1/2\).
Since \(H_{ij}\) commutes with \(\sigma\),
\[
 \norm{\sigma H_{ij}}_1
 =\Tr(\sigma|H_{ij}|)\le\frac12\frac12=\frac14.
\]

For a Hermitian input operator \(X\) and bounded joint operator \(B\),
\[
 \norm{\Tr_A[(X\otimes I)B]}\le\norm X_1\norm B.
\]
Diagonalize \(X\), bound the norm of each corresponding input diagonal
block of \(B\), and sum with the absolute eigenvalues. This remains valid
with an infinite bath. Apply it to \eqref{eq:A} and \eqref{eq:Bnorm}.
\end{proof}

The empty-mask certificate is
\(\sum_{i<j}A_{ij,\varnothing}=v_kI\), by \eqref{eq:scalar}.
For each pair, the nonempty-mask mass is \(1-a^{k-2}\).
Lemma~\ref{lem:trace} and the triangle inequality give
\[
 \left\|\sum_Sw_S A_{ij,S}-A_{ij,\varnothing}\right\|
 \le1-a^{k-2}.
\]
Sum over \(N\) pairs and use Bernoulli's inequality to prove
\eqref{eq:Fbound}. Finally
\[
 \Tr_A[(\sigma H\otimes I)R_b]
 =ba(F_k(b)-v_kI)-b^2v_kI,
\]
because \(H\) is input-offdiagonal. This proves
Theorem~\ref{thm:response}.

\section{Positive input, entropy, and exact physical drift}

The following proof is uniform in the growing block length.
Let \(\ell=\ln2\), \(v=v_k\), and define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rho}
 m=\frac{3\ell v}{16},\qquad
 X=-\frac\ell2\sigma H,\qquad
 Y=\frac{4m}{k}\sigma(Q-k/2),\qquad
 \rho=\sigma+bX+b^2Y.
\end{equation}
Impose
\begin{equation}\label{eq:window}
 k\ge2,\qquad b>0,\qquad bk^2\le\frac1{16}.
\end{equation}
It follows that \(b\le1/64\).

\subsection{Normalization and positivity}

The variance of \(Q\) under \(\sigma\) is \(k/4\). Thus
\[
 \Tr X=\Tr QX=\Tr Y=0,\qquad \Tr QY=m,
\]
and \(\Tr\rho=1\). All relevant matrices commute with \(\sigma\);
\(H\) also commutes with \(Q\). Put
\(Z=\sigma^{-1}(\rho-\sigma)\). From \eqref{eq:inherited},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Z}
 \norm Z
 \le\frac\ell2 bk^2+\frac{3\ell}{8}b^2v
 \le\ell bk^2(1/2+3b/8)<\frac1{16}<\frac12.
\end{equation}
Therefore \(\rho\) is a strictly positive, normalized, charge-preserving
input density.

\subsection{Exact diagonal cancellation and inward drift}

For a fixed computational input word, different bulk move/stay histories
have different output charge words. Its exact drift is
\((1-b)(2Q-k)\). By linearity on diagonal signed inputs,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:diagonal}
 D_b(\sigma)=0,\qquad D_b(Y)=2m(1-b)I.
\end{equation}
These identities hold at every \(b\) on the entire bulk prefix.
The remainder is never charged against all of \(\sigma\).

Combining \eqref{eq:Fidentity}, \eqref{eq:rho} and
\eqref{eq:diagonal} gives the exact formula
\begin{equation}\label{eq:driftformula}
 D_b(\rho)=\ell b^2(1-b)\left[\frac{3v}{8}I-\frac12F_k(b)\right].
\end{equation}
Because \(v\ge N/8\),
\[
 \frac{\norm{F_k(b)-vI}}v\le8b(k-2).
\]
For \(k\ge2\), \(k^2\ge8(k-2)\); hence \eqref{eq:window} yields
\(b(k-2)\le1/128\) and \(F_k(b)\ge(15/16)vI\).
Consequently the physical bulk drift satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:exactdrift}
 P_{\ge k}D_b(\rho)P_{\ge k}
 \le-\kappa P_{\ge k},\qquad
 \kappa=\frac{\ell v b^2(1-b)}{16}>0.
\end{equation}
Indeed \eqref{eq:driftformula} gives the stronger coefficient \(3/32\)
instead of \(1/16\). Also \(\kappa\le1/16384<k\) under
\eqref{eq:window}. No inward sign is claimed at the root.

\subsection{Entropy with a controlled remainder}

The exact identity is
\[
 S(\rho)-3k/2=mb^2-D_2(\rho\Vert\sigma).
\]
Input offdiagonality eliminates the \(H,Q\) cross term, so
\[
 \Tr(\sigma Z^2)
 =\frac{\ell^2vb^2}{4}+\frac{9\ell^2v^2b^4}{64k}.
\]
For \(|z|\le1/2\),
\[
 |(1+z)\ln(1+z)-z-z^2/2|\le2|z|^3/3.
\]
Joint spectral calculus is legitimate because \([Z,\sigma]=0\).
Writing \(u=b^2v/k\), the upper error in \(D_2\) beyond
\(\ell vb^2/8\), divided by \(\ell vb^2/16\), is at most
\[
 \frac98u+\norm Z\left[\frac83+\frac32u\right].
\]
Under \eqref{eq:window}, \(u\le1/2048\) and \(\norm Z<1/16\).
The last expression is at most \(1/6+39/65536<1/2\).
It follows that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:entropy}
 S(\rho)-3k/2\ge\frac{\ell vb^2}{32}
              \ge\frac{\ell b^2k^2}{1024}.
\end{equation}
This is not a fixed-\(k\) asymptotic remainder.

\subsection{Completion of the operational theorem}

For \eqref{eq:kchoice} and \(0<\beta\le1/8\),
\[
 k\ge2,\qquad k\ge\frac1{8\beta},\qquad bk^2\le\frac1{16}.
\]
Equation~\eqref{eq:exactdrift} meets every hypothesis of the inherited
operator-drift coding theorem, reproduced in

Dividing \eqref{eq:entropy} by \(k\) gives \eqref{eq:rate}.
The same inherited theorem proves the actual \(\tau_\pi\) coherent-information
limit and the pointwise all-normal full-diamond statement.
This completes Theorem~\ref{thm:rate}.

\section{Exact higher coefficients and failed extrapolations}
\label{sec:failures}

\subsection{An all-length coefficient interface}

Let \(D_{2r}\) be the charge pinching of the raw physical history sum
\[
 \sum_{|M|+|N|=2r}i^{|N|-|M|}
       [QU_M^*U_N-U_M^*QU_N].
\]
This bounded-displacement expression retains final bath overlaps.
Equal-charge bulk pairs have even total stay count, so exactly
\[
 \mathcal G_k(b)=\sum_{r=0}^k b^r(1-b)^{k-r}D_{2r}.
\]
In particular
\[
 G_2=D_4-(k-1)D_2+\binom{k}{2}D_0.
\]
Since \(D_0\) is diagonal and \(H\) is offdiagonal, the next contracted
coefficient \(K_2=\Tr_A[(\sigma H\otimes I)G_2]\) is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:K2}
 K_2=\sum_{i<j}\sum_{r\notin\{i,j\}}A_{ij,\{r\}}
                  -(k-1)v_kI.
\end{equation}
More generally,
\[
 \Tr_A[(\sigma H\otimes I)G_p]
 =
 \sum_{i<j}\sum_{|S|\le p-1}
 (-1)^{p-1-|S|}
 \binom{k-1-|S|}{p-1-|S|}A_{ij,S}.
\]
The all-order norm theorem controls their aggregate without assuming
coefficientwise cancellation.

\subsection{The scalar first certificate does not persist}

The included exact rational physical-history certificate gives
\[
 K_2=
 \begin{cases}
 -I/8,&k=2,\\
 -23I/32,&k=3,\\
 -113I/64-(3/64)\SWAP_{01},&k=4.
 \end{cases}
\]
For \(k=4\), the swap acts on the first two bath colors, with identity on
the others and the seed. Its symmetric and antisymmetric eigenvalues are
\(-29/16\) and \(-55/32\). Thus the first-order scalar certificate cannot
be extended coefficientwise. The complete \(16\times16\) rational matrix
is checked against this formula in the source. This is a finite
counterexample, not an extrapolated large-\(k\) growth law.

\subsection{Deleting a history call preserves the original input}

For \(k=3\), summing the single-deletion contractions at deleted sites
\(r=0,1,2\), respectively, gives
\[
 I/8,\qquad I/32,\qquad I/8.
\]
Their sum is \(9I/32\), whereas substituting the shorter optimizer each
time would give \(3v_2I=3I/8\). Equation~\eqref{eq:K2} correctly gives
\(-23I/32\). The analytic deleted-call word does not erase the input
correlations encoded by the original \(H\).

\subsection{Charge preservation alone is insufficient}

At \(k=4\), take input bra \(0101\), ket \(1010\), and prefix \(1211\).
The exact physical matrix entry is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fourendpoint}
 \bra{0101,1211}\mathcal G_4(b)\ket{1010,1211}
       =b^2(1-b)^2.
\end{equation}
These inputs have the same total charge but differ at four sites.
Their stay masks must complement at all four sites. With bit \(j\)
denoting a bra stay at call \(j\), the nonzero signed displacement terms are
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrr}
 \text{mask}&1&4&5&7&8&11&13&14\\\hline
 \text{term}&-1&-1&2&-1&1&1&-1&1
\end{array}
\]
and sum to one. The certificate retains every output and final bath word;
an independent prepend/pop implementation reproduces the entry without
importing the main representation routine.

For \(J=\ket{0101}\bra{1010}+\mathrm{h.c.}\), the corresponding diagonal
prefix expectation of \(\Tr_A[(\sigma J\otimes I)\mathcal G_4(b)]\)
is \(b^2(1-b)^2/32\), although \(J\) has no pair-supported component.
Small such perturbations of the positive \(\sigma\) are admissible.
Thus pair support is an essential premise of the reusable response formula.

\subsection{The root remains a physical boundary}

With one call, input port \(\ket0\), and the vacuum seed in the \(+1\)
eigenvector of \(R\), both \(U\) and \(I\) preserve port charge and depth.
The actual drift is zero, whereas the falsely extended bulk formula

bulk response formula changes the complete physical model.

For the unchanged pair construction, write
