The reusable guard/meander lemma requires every middle partial sum to be
nonnegative. Positive final height alone does not suffice, even with a guard.

For charge word `100111`, endpoints i=1,j=5 (zero based), guard g=0 and
middle `011`, the final middle height is +1 but the first step dips below
zero. Let input colors be `[g,0,0,a,b,z]` and bath `[w1,w2,...]`.
One toggle order restores the bath; the other leaves `[a,w2,...]` and
puts w1 into the guard output. Thus a real bath coordinate enters the
word difference. It cannot be replaced by an input-only operator tensor
identity. The exact token receipt records this counterexample.

The guard is independently necessary for the sufficient identity. Word
`011` with endpoints0,2 has a nonnegative middle but no guard. Again one
order restores the bath while the other swaps an input color with its
initial top color. Neither witness proves that all such patterns have
zero useful susceptibility; they invalidate the respective shortcuts.
