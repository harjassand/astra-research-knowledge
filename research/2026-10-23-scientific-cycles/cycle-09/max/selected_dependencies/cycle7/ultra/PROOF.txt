2. FULL-SUPPORT ENTANGLED-SEED LEMMA

THEOREM. Let the finite input support be
 S=span{|Phi_K>_(A0B0)} tensor H_A1 tensor H_B1.
Let rho be strictly positive on S and have no support outside S. Let Gamma be
a separable CP map to finite local output spaces, and suppose Gamma(rho) has
EXACT support inside
 W=span{|e_i>_A tensor |f_i>_B:i in I},
where both displayed local families are orthonormal. Then EVERY product Kraus
operator restricted to S has image in the span of at most K matched basis
vectors. Consequently every nonzero normalized Gamma(sigma), for sigma
supported in S, has Schmidt number <=K. If Gamma is TP,
 EF(Gamma(rho))<=ln K.                                    (2.1)
The channel and output bases may be chosen after knowing rho. No restriction
to separate pair processing or input-independent channel selection is imposed.

PROOF. Write product Kraus A_l tensor B_l. Positivity and exact support give
 0=Tr[P_Wperp Gamma(rho)]=sum_l ||P_Wperp(A_l tensor B_l)rho^1/2||_2^2.
Each summand vanishes. Because rho^1/2 has range S, every Kraus maps S into W.
Fix one Kraus. Its action on Phi_K tensor |u>_A1|v>_B1 has coefficient
 C_ij(u,v)=K^-1/2 sum_(x=1)^K A_(i,xu) B_(j,xv).
All off-diagonal coefficients vanish for every u,v; coefficients outside the
displayed output families vanish too. For each active index i, choose u_i,v_i
with C_ii(u_i,v_i) nonzero. The matrix
 G_ij=sum_x A_(i,xu_i)B_(j,xv_j)
on active indices is diagonal and invertible. It factors through C^K, so its
rank, hence its number of active indices, is <=K. This bilinear contraction
is the actual vector amplitude; no complex conjugation is missing.
All vectors in the Kraus image thus have Schmidt rank <=K. Applying all Kraus
operators to any pure decomposition of sigma gives a pure decomposition of
the output with that rank bound. Every component entropy is <=ln K. QED.

In finite dimensions separable maps have finite product-Kraus descriptions,
and Schmidt-number bounded sets are closed. All finite-round LOCC maps and
their finite-dimensional norm closure are covered. Trace-nonincreasing branches
obey the normalized support lemma, but probability and supplied costs must not
be removed by postselection. Our operational corollaries use TP averaged maps.
Local pure work, local discard and classical communication are already allowed.
Retaining or tracing an output auxiliary does not supply a missing input record.
