A. UNASSISTED RECEIVER
Known omega; arbitrary finite-dimensional TP SEP/LOCC, any joint processing,
local pure work/discard/classical communication, no source-correlated record or
uncharged entangled auxiliary. Main ports must end EXACTLY in one MC subspace.
Optimal operation: keep first exact Bell; reset all other output pairs to|00>.
This attains the proved finite score bound and loses Theta(L) actual score.
Adaptive LOCC lies inside SEP; postselection is excluded from the averaged score.

It computes minimum posterior inventory a_z and keeps its first a_z Bells,
resetting all later pairs. Exact maximum score lnD E F_L(a_z); no EF, tomography
or unknown optimal quantum measurement is called. For finite J-label designs,
exact rational DP returns best threshold intervals and the actual control table.
The strongest ordinary method receives this same known distribution and DP.

On this device the terminal clock at N=L is K. Reading/coherently computing a
coarse threshold label from this known register is an implementable extra

COMPLETE original memory marginal unchanged, including correlations among its
registers. Output-memory correlations change as the displayed branch law says.
At most L-1 D-site blanks/discards per party; decode/reset controls, threshold

new label copies enlarge complete memory and need another return analysis.
