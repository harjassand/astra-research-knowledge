# Cycle 8: measured microwave-to-captured-product evidence

22 October 2026. Bounded primary-literature check. Decision: actual microwave-to-aqueous-acid coupling exists, including an independent gas/liquid cross-check, but no matched whole-system gain for the Maastricht opposed-jet target is established. The strongest retrieved measurement uses a different 915 MHz, 50 kW Cerawave system. Ordinary oxidation/absorption is not a hypothetical weak comparator: a directly relevant synthetic-gas experiment measures absorption and nitrate/nitrite evolution without plasma and exposes transient inventory effects.

## 1. Actual coupled pilot, not merely inlet NOx

McKinney, Rao, Grushnikova, Ushiroda, Kesler, Dvorak and Jevtic, *High-Power Closed-Loop Pilot System for Nitric Acid Production Using Inductively Coupled Microwave Plasma*, Nitrogen 6(3) (2025) 51, published 28 June 2025. https://doi.org/10.3390/nitrogen6030051 ; https://www.mdpi.com/2504-3129/6/3/51 . Full publisher HTML and extracted text archived as pilot.html/pilot.txt; Figure 7 archived and visually read as pilot_fig7.png. Section references below refer to this primary article.

### What is measured

- Sections 2.3, 3 and 4: 198.9 L of 28.6 wt% nitric acid, stated as 66.5 kg HNO3 on 100% basis, from fresh water after 30.9 operating hours. This was roughly 40% of working hours during the testing period, NOT a demonstrated uninterrupted 30.9-hour endurance run. Liquid acid was titrated in triplicate after homogenization, with 0.1 M or 2 M NaOH depending on acid concentration. This titrant is analytical consumption, not a process alkaline absorbent.
- Section 4: 1545 kWh = 5562 MJ delivered to plasma, yielding approximately 5.3 MJ/mol from acid titration. An independent estimate from gas concentration drop across absorption plus 1300 slpm circulation gives 5.93 MJ/mol, within 12% of titration estimate. This is genuine captured-product evidence stronger than plasma inlet-NOx metrics or concentration alone. It is still not a closed, species-resolved, uncertainty-certified whole-batch N balance.
- Figure 7 exact printed values: pre-absorption total NOx 1.355%, post-absorption 0.484% (4840 ppm) at low acid concentration (<5%). The text rounds absorption to 65%; the concentration ratio gives ~64.3%. Those values characterize a pass through the absorber, NOT net plant capture fraction or final vent compliance. Recirculated gas retains considerable NOx.
- The gas analyzer measures NO directly and NO2 by converter subtraction, but Figure 7 supplies total NOx only. No usable numerical paired post-absorber NO/NO2 split or time-integrated purge-N balance was recovered. N2O was reportedly undetectable (<1 ppm) with a separate analyzer. Titration alone is not a nitrate/nitrite-selective assay.

### Equipment and material boundary

- 915 MHz industrial magnetron, Cerawave inductively coupled torch: materially different from the Maastricht 2.45 GHz 20 SLM, 400–700 W opposed jets. Do not transplant efficiencies, matching or capture fractions.
- Batch plasma flow 1300 slpm; auxiliary air 40 slpm. Approximately 50 slpm total air replenishment (40 auxiliary + 10 resupply), plus ~18 slpm PSA gas (95% O2, 5% Ar), sustains 20% oxygen and oxidation-vessel pressure 13.5 psig. This is not simply fresh air as sole consumed gas service: oxygen separation is part of the system.
- Oxidation vessel: diameter 90 cm, height 1.5 m, stated ~1 m3 and ~45 s loop-gas residence time. Treat those as authors' reported operating descriptors, not recalibrated ideal-gas residence measurements.
- Two parallel bubble columns, each 30 cm diameter, 3 m tall, 19 sintered diffusers, initial ~100 L water each. No peroxide or alkali process addition identified. Water/acid circulated and removed by acid-resistant pump. Liquid carryover recovered through demister/condenser and returned automatically; no complete endpoint holdup/sampling/reset accounting supplied.
- Argon is managed by a small gas leak near blowers, diluted in facility exhaust; analyzer exhaust adds purge. Closed-loop does NOT mean zero emitted N. A quantitative final purge flow/speciation balance was not recovered. The paper's dilution statement is not a common product/NO-slip performance specification.
- Three heat exchangers, two variable-speed blowers, compressor, PSA oxygen generator, pumps and facility extraction matter. HEX1 circulates ~100 L/min water and reports ~60% plasma power recovery to a 7500 L tank at 60 C. HEX2 cools gas from ~100 C to room temperature; HEX3 cools after compression. Cone surface is 300–450 C and separately force-air-cooled.

### Energy, reset and claims boundary

Section 2.1 states 50 kW absorbed plasma requires 60.5 kW generator input (82.6% grid-to-plasma), with reflected power <500 W. Section 4 explicitly restricts 5.3 MJ/mol to power delivered to plasma. Multiplying the rounded 5.3 by 60.5/50 gives ~6.41 MJ/mol at generator input alone, BEFORE auxiliary electricity. This is a transparent boundary translation using their numbers, not a whole-plant measured SEC.

The 3.5 MJ/mol NOx-production figure differs from captured-acid 5.3. Improving absorber efficiency from 65% to 90% to obtain 3.7 MJ/mol is prospective, not tested capture gain. Further 3.15 MJ/mol assumes conversion of recovered heat to electricity at 25% and is not observed electrical recovery. No ordinary-train matched comparison or opposed/single-jet matched captured-product experiment appears here. Quench/bypass operation also changes gas residence time and flow, so cannot isolate an intrinsic capture mechanism.

Acid accumulation slows above ~2.4 M. The model predicts absorption falling 66%→57% from 0→28.6 wt% acid; those endpoints are modeled, not separate endpoint measured capture efficiencies. Batch shutdown, final gas removal, restart, cleanup and complete thermal/reset electricity are not closed. Admit the measured pilot coupling; do not certify full-system superiority from it.

## 2. Strong ordinary absorption control and a numerical reporting warning

Vits, De Winter, Hollevoet and Martens, *Absorption Chemistry of Plasma-Generated NOx Gas for Green Nitric Acid Production*, Processes 13(12) (2025) 3897, published 2 December 2025. https://doi.org/10.3390/pr13123897 ; https://www.mdpi.com/2227-9717/13/12/3897 . Full HTML/text archived as absorption.html/absorption.txt. This uses bottled gas deliberately mimicking plasma, not actual coupled microwave discharge. It is primary experimental evidence for an ordinary comparator, not proof of plasma synergy.

- Sections 2–3: feed 1% NO, 20% O2, 79% N2; upstream ordinary oxidation gives measured NO2/(NO+NO2)=0.56 ± 0.008. A 30-minute bypass precedes absorption for stabilization and verification. Countercurrent packed column uses 6 mm glass Raschig rings; empty bed volume 780 mL; liquid circulation 375 mL/min; initial liquid 400 mL; 293 K cryostat.
- IMPORTANT INTERNAL INCONSISTENCY: section 2.1 says gas flow 3 L/h, whereas section 2.2 and results say 3 L/min. These differ by factor 60. Do NOT silently select 3 L/min and call the data a quantitative closed N balance. Initial liquid concentrations/inventory are far more compatible with the low-flow scale, but that inference does not resolve the published conflict. No outreach or supplement chase is authorized or performed.
- Initial solution acidified using H2SO4 or H3PO4, pH held 1–5 by 0.2 M KOH dosing. Ordinary controls must receive the SAME acid/base chemical resources; constant pH is not a free property of water. Integrated KOH consumption, evolving liquid volume and sample-withdrawal inventory are not supplied sufficiently to close reset/resources.
- Hourly IC measures nitrate and total nitrite (HNO2 + NO2− after pH7 buffering); actual HNO2 speciation is inferred from pKa=3.20, not measured independently.
- At pH1/2/3 after 6 h, nitrate is 12.6/14.0/10.4 mM; total nitrite 3.37/3.86/5.41 mM; inferred HNO2 3.35/3.63/3.32 mM. At pH4/5 after 30 h, nitrate 40.3/14.9 mM, total nitrite 21.6/38.4 mM. Neither higher-pH total-nitrite inventory reaches plateau in 30 h. These are not equal-time or equal-product-quality comparisons.
- Outlet total NOx increases toward ~1800 ppm at pH1–3; final removal 82.5/83.9/83.0%. The paper says initial ~800 ppm and ~95% removal, themselves not exact consistent arithmetic for 1% feed; use final measured figures with caution rather than manufacture precision. Slow HNO2 accumulation and regenerated NO explain why a favorable startup removal value cannot be extrapolated indefinitely. The authors acknowledge outlet NO can combine unabsorbed feed NO and NO released by disproportionation; not separately identified experimentally.
- Direct volume intervention at pH1: 250 versus 400 mL gives similar removal (83.6 versus 82.5%), total nitrite 4.07 versus 3.37 mM and nitrate 19.6 versus 12.6 mM after 6 h. Lower concentration with larger liquid inventory is NOT equivalent to higher capture yield. This is a particularly clean warning against concentration-only benefit claims.
- No measured plasma power or whole-train energy comparison. Oxidizing agents were not added. Further oxidation/storage/recapture suggestions are proposed routes, not demonstrated complete reset-and-capture gains.

## 3. Bounded additional leads, not upgraded to full evidence

Search returned two recent actual microwave-coupling abstracts:
1. *Batch-wise nitrogen fixation from atmospheric air for on-farm fertilizer production using a 10 kW microwave induction-coupled plasma (MICP) torch*: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666790826001369 . Abstract reports 60 L water, 30/60/90/300-minute torch periods; oxygen <6% reduces reaction rate; acid continues forming after torch shutdown. Best reported 60-minute torch +30-minute recirculation/replenishment produces 0.162 M at 6.82 MJ/mol. Full-page retrieval returned 403. Do not assert complete energy or N balance from abstract. Useful independent warning that afterglow gas inventory must be counted.
2. *Atmospheric-pressure microwave plasma torch for energy-efficient NOx generation and direct production of high-concentration plasma-activated water for on-site fertilizer*: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666821126000323 . Abstract reports 1:1 N2/O2, 1500 J/L, 1.86 MJ/mol TOTAL NOx, 5 wt% nitrate, 16 h operation. These are distinct gas-production and liquid-concentration metrics, not enough to establish whole-system capture gain. Full-page returned 403. No further unavailable full-text/supplement search undertaken.

Targeted Maastricht/Butterworth/van Rooij absorption searches did not identify measured gas-to-liquid coupling on the opposed-jet platform. That is a bounded negative search, not proof no publication exists. Existing target gas-only evidence remains unchanged.
