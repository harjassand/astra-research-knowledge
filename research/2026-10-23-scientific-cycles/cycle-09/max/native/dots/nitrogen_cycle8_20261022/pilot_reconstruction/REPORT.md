# Move the comparison to captured product, without promoting the projected upgrades

## Primary operation actually observed

McKinney et al., Nitrogen6(3)(2025)51, DOI10.3390/nitrogen6030051, reports a constructed Cerawave microwave nitric-acid pilot. This is a different915MHz,50kW absorbed-power, recirculating apparatus, not the20SLM Maastricht2.45GHz split-jet comparison. Its closed gas loop includes oxidation, water absorption, liquid recovery, oxygen preparation, cooling and purge. The observed liquid batch is stronger evidence than an inlet NOx figure or a numerical cooling history.

The paper reports approximately200L initial fresh water,198.9L final28.6wt% acid,66.5kg HNO3-equivalent product,30.9 operating hours, and50kW absorbed plasma power. Those operating hours were roughly40% of the testing period's working hours, not30.9h uninterrupted thermal endurance. Titration used triplicate samples; the main record is acidity expressed as HNO3, not an independently selective nitrate/nitrite assay. I retain the authors' reported nitric-acid production while keeping that measurement boundary explicit. Initial/final gas and liquid holdup, sampled liquid, losses and full reset are not closed in the retrieved record.

## Recovered heat: genuine apparatus feature, restricted grade

HEX1 reports approximately30kW recovery to a7500L water tank maintained near60C; a second exchanger cools process gas further and a third cools compressed gas. This supports coexistence of acid production and reported heat transfer in that apparatus. It does not prove delivery to a real ammonia-stripping service, repeated tank reset, or electricity generation.

Against an explicitly assumed25C environment, a60C heat port has a reversible work fraction10.5058%. If the100L/min water flow returns from a60C tank and carries30kW, constant-cp water would leave at64.306C. Even that hottest-temperature ceiling is11.6478%; its finite-stream incremental exergy from64.306 back to60C is about3.324kW. Thus25% conversion of the demonstrated hot-water duty to electricity is not admitted. A25% Carnot fraction at25C ambient requires a source at least124.38C, and an actual engine needs more. This does NOT exclude a different upstream high-temperature recovery path before the gas heat is degraded, nor does it constrain an avoided-heat-pump electricity credit in the same way. Those are different systems and product contracts.

A7500kg water tank heated from an ASSUMED25C to60C stores1.09725GJ. If30kW is recovered throughout30.9h, the total3.3372GJ exceeds that storage by2.23995GJ. A sustained external demand or rejection path must take the difference; intermittent cooling also can. This is a balance condition, not an accusation that no sink existed. The source does not supply a batch-integrated useful-load heat record. The same joules cannot simultaneously count as tank storage, process heat service and exported electricity.

## The next scientific decision is the closed-loop response, not a capture percentage

FEEDBACK_DERIVATION.md proves the elementary closed-loop relation and supplies three exact source-response countermodels with identical baseline observed flows. Upgrading the absorber from65% to90% can improve, leave unchanged or worsen the complete loop's product rate depending on how returned gas changes post-torch fixed-N generation. The last case uses a source-response gain, NOT an impossible survival probability. These are logical identifiability counterexamples, not empirical plasma models.

A fixed-source, survival-only subclass already shows zero steady benefit when every uncaptured molecule survives to the next pass. Improvements may then reduce inventory and shutdown time rather than recurring production. If returned NOx is destroyed, capture can genuinely help. If changing return gas changes fresh formation, even the sign requires its response. The actual discriminating observation is paired fixed-N molar flow before/after the source and through product/purge under the actual absorber intervention, together with total power and all state changes. A local derivative is not a finite-upgrade prediction. Direct complete-operation comparison can answer the capability question without isolating every microscopic reaction.

The ordinary comparator receives the same efficient packed absorber, oxidation, allowed recycle, chemical resources and heat-use options. Source3.7/3.15MJ/mol projections are not accepted plant performance. No comparison to an unmatched1–1.5MJ/mol literature number is manufactured.

## Regeneration-counted component: useful, but not a universal optimum

The companion serial/local-return proof uses the inherited evaluated ambient reaction convention and adds net water-absorption regeneration. Each captured acid-N entails1.5NO oxidation turnovers and0.5NO regenerated for reoxidation. Within its explicit ideal serial class, local immediate absorption is an infimum even allowing arbitrary NO2/N2O4 partition. The same terminal100ppmNO criterion PLUS complete terminal oxidized-N capture gives574.84L ordinary and578.08L split gas-reaction volume, compared with the old dry-conditioning~0.4m3. These are not absorber sizes or hardware claims.

A selective regenerated-NO return to the inlet defeats any attempted universal extension: an ideal relaxed construction gives402.88/407.90L with the same net acid and tail. This counterexample pays its extra stream in the material balance, but has no qualified selective separation, liquid chemistry, transfer energy, finite inventory or pressure realization. It identifies the exact missing routing constraint, not a free improvement. Parent60-digit quadrature independently corroborates both limiting integrals; finite-stage convergence is also preserved.

The restriction to net water disproportionation excludes other ordinary pathways, including NO/NO2 coabsorption, stabilized nitrite and aqueous nitrite oxidation. These may avoid the selected regeneration burden but must match nitrate/nitrite quality and count acid/base/oxidant preparation and reset. Nothing proves the serial route superior to those alternatives.

## Meaningful failed attempts and limits

The ordinary synthetic-NOx absorption paper recovered alongside the pilot has conflicting3L/h and3L/min gas-flow units, variable pH maintained by added KOH, evolving liquid nitrite and NO release. It is not a source of a closed numerical capture/energy advantage. It does show why startup disappearance and liquid concentration alone are insufficient. The measured Maastricht split/single-flow captured-product comparison remains unavailable in the bounded search; the observed gas-production gain is preserved.

I inspected original Figures11/12 to ask whether the batch's slope change identifies return survival. Sparse cumulative concentration points, one-sigma bars, changing acid state/volume and absent paired loop-flow records do not distinguish fresh-source response from capture and inventory effects. No return-survival coefficient was fitted. The source-based critique is therefore about identifiability, not a claimed kinetic refutation from the curve. An initial local HTML parsing attempt lacked BeautifulSoup; exact image URLs were instead read with the standard library, and original images downloaded. No author code or old solver was executed.

Decision: keep the actual captured-acid pilot as the stronger whole-apparatus reference, retain the Maastricht production comparison separately, and require a complete closed-loop product/energy response before selecting an absorber upgrade as an efficiency invention. No complete reset, practical superiority, historical originality or amplification claim is established. The nitrogen mission remains active.
