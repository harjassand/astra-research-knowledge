1. THE TWO ANSWERS ARE THE SAME THEOREM

Let P=diag(p_1,...,p_d)>0, sum p_i=1, and s=1-Tr(P^2). The threshold t>0 is
the unique solution of sum_i (1-t/p_i)_+=1. Let

 z_i=(1-t/p_i)_+, Z=diag(z_i),
 B=Tr[P(I-Z)^2],
 C_k=sum_{n=0}^{k-2}(k-1-n)Pr(S_n=0).

Both reports prove

 v_k(P)=s k(k-1)/4 + (B-s)C_k/2,
 zeta_*=sum_i z_i (|i><i|)^tensor k.

The control denotes 2s by A, yielding the equivalent
A k(k-1)/8+(2B-A)C_k/4. Both admit all k>=2 and all strictly positive P.
Rational eigenvalues give exact rational thresholds, coefficients and values.
Their extension in the written formula to positive real eigenvalues is also
justified by the same finite-dimensional argument, not only numeric continuity.

2. ARBITRARY COHERENT AND ENTANGLED PREFIXES ARE COVERED

The prepend/pop word identity is independent of color dimension and density.
Matched factors permute input color wires and restore the bath; cancellation
is an algebraic identity on the deep core, so it also holds on superpositions.
Undoing the unmatched prefix after insertion versus popping gives the same
h>d, h=d and h<d classification as in the original source.

The reference charge law remains fair. Conditional on q colored inputs, its
color weight is P^tensor q, and input permutations preserve that weight.
Consequently the input/input square is

 Tr[P^tensor2 (I-SWAP)^2]=2(1-Tr(P^2))=2s.

The critical input/bath swap contracts to the full reduced density R_m without
transposition. Its square is f(R_m)=Tr[P(I-R_m)^2]. For an arbitrary Hermitian
density R, including a marginal of an entangled complex prefix,

 f(R)=sum_i p_i(1-R_ii)^2
      +sum_{i<j}(p_i+p_j)|R_ij|^2.

Deleting off-diagonals preserves positive semidefiniteness and trace, and can
only lower f. Strict positivity of P makes the diagonal problem strictly convex.
The simplex KKT conditions give the stated threshold solution. At least two
weights are positive: one active color would force t=0, inconsistent with
strictly positive excluded p_i. Threshold equalities p_i=t give z_i=0.

Thus every critical sector has cost at least B. Every other export sector is
nonnegative for arbitrary competitors. No assertion of commutation between an
arbitrary H_zeta and sigma_P is needed or valid.

4. THE NON-SCALAR DUAL AND ITS SUPPORT CONDITION ARE CORRECT

At the optimum only, H_* commutes with sigma_P: its blocks consist of wire
permutations, input/input swaps and the diagonal Z=f(P). Define

 D=P(I-Z)=diag(min(p_i,t)), Delta=tI-D=diag((t-p_i)_+).

The critical cross contraction against the raw input/bath block is

 Tr(D)I-D=B I+Delta.

The identity follows from Tr(DZ)=t Tr(Z)=t and
B=Tr(D(I-Z))=Tr(D)-t. The input/input sectors give their fixed scalar cost;
the vanishing export blocks contribute zero. Therefore, with the positive
critical-coordinate coefficients w_{k,m} defined in the reports,

 T_k(sigma_P H_*)=v_k(P)I+sum_{m=0}^{k-2}w_{k,m}Delta^(m) >= v_k(P)I.

Every listed w_{k,m}>0: an all-pop prefix of length m with adjacent endpoints
already supplies positive probability. Sum_m w_{k,m}=C_k/2. The final prefix
coordinate is untouched in this coefficient. The kernel is correctly stated as

 span{|i>:p_i>=t}^tensor(k-1) tensor C^d.

The chosen optimizer is supported in this kernel. Importantly, kernel support
alone is not sufficient: arbitrary density matrices on that subspace can have
wrong marginals or export-sector terms. Both receivers retain the exact identity

 Tr(sigma_P H_zeta^2)
 = v_k+2Tr[zeta(T_k(sigma_P H_*)-v_k I)]
   +Tr[sigma_P(H_zeta-H_*)^2].

It is valid without commutation for H_zeta because the cross term is evaluated
at the commuting H_*. Strict positivity of sigma_P makes equality imply
H_zeta=H_*. Both papers correctly give the general gradient at other candidates
as T_k((sigma_P H+H sigma_P)/2).

The scalar dual survives exactly when min_i p_i>=t. Rank deficiency does not

7. STRATEGIC ADJUDICATION

Do not elevate compact sector-operation prompts or further nearby Pro1 variants
to the programme's primary amplification mechanism. This task is richer than
substituting a uniform color dimension: it contains noncommuting competitors,
active-set boundaries and a repaired operator dual. Nevertheless, both strong
receivers solve it from accessible capital. Another easy variation would mainly
measure accumulated proof knowledge again. Retain the theorem and optional
operation as specific capital, preserve Pro1's actual nonlinear frontier, and
do not claim the evaluator has invented a better general research process.

A more consequential, non-prescriptive research direction is whether Astra can
invent OBSERVABLES or empirical interventions that distinguish scientifically
important explanations which its current representations cannot distinguish.
This addresses acquisition of new scientific information, rather than rearranging
already supplied exact identities. The physical histories supply the reason:
mode-energy partition and complete power boundaries can remain unidentified
despite correct simulations and proofs. More inference over the same insufficient
evidence cannot create the missing distinction.

For an executable evaluator study, the next admission obligation should be a
source-complete public dataset with independently existing held-out conditions,
a consequential unresolved inference question, and demonstrably competing models
that fit the current observations. Root would study whether a system can propose
and justify a new variable/observable, identify an accessible discriminating
condition, and obtain a verified new prediction or identification consequence.
The strong comparator receives the same evidence and established causal,
statistical, symmetry and model-selection methods. If the proposed observable is
just a standard sufficient statistic or the evidence cannot distinguish models,
that is a scoped result; no invention is claimed. Evidence acquisition and its
scientific cost are included. No laboratory purchase, outreach, forced owner
assignment or new platform is implied.

This direction is a research hypothesis, not a demonstrated solution and not a
reason to interrupt the mathematical programmes. It is justified as a higher-
ceiling alternative to another adjacent algebraic transfer test because it asks
whether Astra can expand what is learnable from reality. The currently supplied
returns do not yet identify the right admitted dataset/problem; that is root's
meta-scientific investigation, not work to delegate to the user.

Historical novelty, external correctness acceptance and practical consequence
remain unestablished for this new variational result. No causal or recursive
