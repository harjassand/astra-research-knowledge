1. NECESSARY ASSUMPTIONS AND INTERFACES

  K-independent LOCC channel on the accessible outputs and supplied receiver
  auxiliaries. The original hidden reference is not read, changed, or replaced.
* All initially Bob-accessible quantum and classical resources belong to
  B_initial. Independent entanglement has zero I(K;B_initial), whereas an
  actually supplied correlated record or resource is charged by its actual
  mutual information. The analogous statement holds for Alice.
* Every information-bearing message, choice of speaker, timing decision and
  stop/null signal is included in the declared communication model. Public

* The entropy charge uses ordinary Shannon entropies of finite/countable
  classical messages, with +infinity permitted. Differential entropy of an

  prefix-free complete codes avoid this ambiguity.
* The ideal target has the stated labelled Bell prefix and zero suffix on the
  same external sites. Its joint distance includes the actual hidden K and
  all declared receiver outputs. External marginal approximation alone is
  insufficient.
* The information bound (5), its symmetric form (5a), and the stated proof of
  (12) use the explicit global epsilon<=1/2 range. No independent-copy or
  per-copy replacement of this error is made.
* For (5)/(5a), n<=K and E(K-n)<=w suffice. The stronger (5b)/(5c) require the
  POINTWISE bound K-n<=w. The revised (5d) covers the mean-only case.

2. TARGET LAST-NONZERO GAP: PASS

On either party's target marginal, conditional on (K,T,n), digits 1,...,n
are independent uniform D-ary digits and later digits are zero. Therefore
Z=n-J is nonnegative and

  Pr(Z>=a | n)=D^(-a) for 1<=a<=n,
  E Z <= sum_(a>=1) D^(-a) = 1/(D-1)=mu_D.

For G=K-J=(K-n)+Z, G>=0 and EG<=w+mu_D. At fixed J, K and G are in bijection,
so H(K|J)=H(G|J)<=H(G). A nonnegative integer variable of mean at most u has
entropy at most

  g(u)=(u+1)ln(u+1)-u ln u.

Indeed relative entropy to q_g=(1+u)^(-1)(u/(1+u))^g gives
H(G)<=ln(1+u)+EG ln((1+u)/u)<=g(u). Here u>=mu_D>0, so no endpoint ambiguity
arises. No independence of K, n and the transcript is required.

Thus each ideal local receiver output has I(K;J)>=ln L-g(w+mu_D). The
random, unobserved branch index n is only used to establish the distribution
and need not be made available to the measurement or the receiver.

3. CLASSICAL CONDITIONAL-ENTROPY CONTINUITY: PASS

For distributions p,q of (K,J) at total variation d, their common-mass
decomposition is p=(1-d)r+d p' and q=(1-d)r+d q'. A mixture flag gives

  H_p(K|J)=(1-d)H_r(K|J)+d H_p'(K|J)+e_p,
  H_q(K|J)=(1-d)H_r(K|J)+d H_q'(K|J)+e_q,

where 0<=e_p,e_q<=h2(d). Since every component conditional entropy lies in
[0,ln L], their difference has magnitude at most d ln L+h2(d), not twice
that binary-entropy term. The d=0 case follows directly.

Both actual and target K marginals are exactly uniform for the exact-source
execution. Measurement contracts trace distance, and d ln L+h2(d) is
nondecreasing for 0<=d<=1/2. This establishes (9) with the stated coefficient
(1-epsilon)ln L and one h2(epsilon).

4. INTERACTIVE QUANTUM/CLASSICAL INFORMATION FLOW: PASS

The strongest form of the message step is obtained by writing the complete
receiving system immediately before a forward message as B=RT, with T an
orthogonal classical copy of the public past. Alice's nonselective local
instrument leaves KRT unchanged by no signalling. On receipt of a classical
message C,

  Delta I = I(K;C|RT)
          = H(C|RT)-H(C|KRT)
          <= H(C|RT)
          <= H(C|T).

The first inequality holds because the conditional entropy of a classical
register given any quantum system is nonnegative. The last inequality is
strong subadditivity. Arbitrary initially shared entanglement and quantum
side information do not change either fact.

Bob's local instruments, including creation and retention of feedback
outcomes, are CPTP maps and obey data processing. Sending Alice a classical
copy of information already held by Bob does not add information to Bob.
Summing the forward increments proves (10).

For a protocol that actually discards classical records, retaining copies
in a mathematical bookkeeping extension is legitimate: classical copying
does not alter the actual execution or its message distribution, and tracing
out the extra copies recovers the declared final output. These proof-only
records are not claimed as actual retained resources with a return error.
The revised proof explicitly makes this distinction.

Swapping Alice and Bob establishes (5a), because both initial source local
marginals are independent of K and both target local marginals have the
same last-nonzero information. Therefore neither direction can be omitted
when the corresponding no-access bound R is positive.

5. ONE-WAY BOUNDS AND CORRECTION HISTORY

Under pointwise K-n<=w, the revised (5b)/(5c) PASS. The party receiving no
messages remains K-independent by no signalling. Its actual distribution
of (K,J) is a product with uniform K. For any fixed J, at most w+a+1 values
of K obey 0<=K-J<=w+a. Thus actual Pr(E_a)<=(w+a+1)/L. The target obeys
Pr(E_a)>=1-D^(-(a+1)). Their event-probability difference lower-bounds total
variation and hence the full joint trace error. Taking
a=floor(log_D L) uses D^(-(a+1))<=1/L. These arguments do not need an
epsilon<=1/2 restriction.

The initial unrestricted reading of these sharp one-way bounds was FALSE
for the mean-only target relaxation. The following exact counterexample was
identified during audit and led to the now-explicit pointwise qualification:

  D=2, L=1024, w=1;
  n(k)=0 for k<=44, and n(k)=k for k>=45.

This target satisfies E(K-n)=44*45/(2*1024)=990/1024<1. A zero-communication
local reset of every external pair produces |00>^L independently of K and
has full joint trace error

  epsilon=(1/1024) sum_(k=45)^1024 sqrt(1-2^(-k))
         <980/1024.

The unrestricted mean-only reading of (5c) would instead demand
epsilon>=1-(1+10+2)/1024=1011/1024, a contradiction. The current proof
excludes this reading and preserves (5)/(5a)'s valid mean relaxation.

The added mean-only bound (5d) PASS. For integer s>=0, Markov gives
Pr(K-n>=s+1)<=w/(s+1). With probability at least
1-w/(s+1)-D^(-(a+1)), both K-n<=s and n-J<=a hold, so
0<=K-J<=s+a. Its actual independent probability is at most (s+a+1)/L.
This is precisely (5d); no independence between the two target gap terms
is needed for the union bound.

6. ACCESS AND MEASURED-SITE SCOPE: PASS AFTER CLARIFICATION

An entropy lower bound converts into a measured-site count only when all
forward communication, including stopping, has the asserted complete
encoding with C_forward<=q ln D or C_forward<=E q ln D. It is not a
universal lower bound on measured pairs. The current file states this.

The zero-communication reset witness requires BOTH local controllers to
know K: they keep the known active Bell prefix and locally reset the suffix.
A record available only to Bob leaves Alice's I_initial,A zero; (5a) forbids
the same zero-communication claim whenever R>0. The initial potentially
one-sided wording was corrected explicitly in the current proof.

7. UNRESTRICTED COLLECTIVE LAW (12): PASS

For r independent exact sources, the untouched classical vector K has
uniform alphabet {1,...,L}^r and entropy r ln L. Each target marginal task
has its own last-nonzero statistic J_a and gap entropy bounded by
g(w_a+mu_D), even with a transcript correlated across all tasks. Therefore

  H(K_vector|J_vector)
      <= sum_a H(K_a|J_vector)
      <= sum_a H(K_a|J_a)
      <= sum_a g(w_a+mu_D).

The common-mass argument applies once to the full K-vector alphabet L^r,
charging epsilon r ln L plus ONE h2(epsilon). The interactive information
flow argument applies to the entire joint protocol. Product processing,
per-task communication allocation and independent target transcripts are
unnecessary. Actual initial receiving information must be the vector
mutual information I(K_vector;B_initial), not an unjustified sum of local
surrogates. The per-task asymptotic conclusion is read in the stated
large-L regime with w_a=O(ln L).

8. GLOBAL INITIALIZATION TRIANGLE: PASS WITH THE REVISED SCOPE

For the SAME fixed channel Gamma, complete input states Omega and
Omega_tilde at distance epsilon_init, and
T(Gamma(Omega_tilde),Theta)<=epsilon_norm,

  T(Gamma(Omega),Theta)<=epsilon_init+epsilon_norm.

This is a full-state contraction and triangle argument. A deterministic
declared message-alphabet budget applies to both executions, so it can be
used in (5) with the larger error when the sum is <=1/2. The relevant input
registers and supplied resources must be the same in this comparison.

The argument does not equate source-dependent entropy/expected-length
charges on the two executions. Moreover, approximate initialization may
change the actual K marginal. One must either analyze actual K entropy,
actual initial mutual information and actual message statistics directly,
or provide a suitable finite-alphabet continuity transfer. The revised
proof now states this and does not insert (5)'s uniform-K expression into
an arbitrary approximate execution.

No external-marginal, local-error or independent-copy approximation can
replace this complete joint error without a new argument. A stock with a
freshly sampled clock is not the same joint comparator.
