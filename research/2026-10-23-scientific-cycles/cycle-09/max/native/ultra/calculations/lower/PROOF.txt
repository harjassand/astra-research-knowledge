ASTRA CYCLE8: A ROBUST COMMUNICATION/ACCESS PRICE FOR JOINT QUEUE NORMALIZATION
22 October 2026. Independent bounded lemma cycle8_robust_lower.
Natural logarithms and half trace distance throughout.

STATUS AND SCOPE

This is an approximate, source-specific, joint-target obstruction. It does not
give a lower bound on lost Bell pairs, close the generic formation gap, or
identify a new entanglement resource. It applies to arbitrary interactive
output-only LOCC, not only a specified search algorithm. Independent auxiliary
entanglement of any amount does not remove this communication obligation.
Both communication directions are required: with no supplied local inventory
access, no one-way LOCC can approximate the stated near-full-prefix joint target
at error for which the bound below is positive.
The proof consists of elementary entropy/data-processing facts. Historical
novelty of the receiving use is not asserted. No quantum optimization is used.

1. EXACT SOURCE AND ACCESS CONTRACT

Fix D>=2, L>=1 and m=ln D. Let Phi=Phi_D, nu=I_D/D tensor I_D/D. After L uses
of the counted-current-clock queue, the complete actual joint state is

 Omega_L=(1/L) sum_(k=1)^L |kk><kk|_F tensor
           (Phi^k tensor nu^(L-k))_out tensor nu^L_memorypairs.       (1)

The fixed local-unitary device and faithful eta=nu, exact first Bell output,
complete return delta=(1-D^(-2L))/L and ordinary-stock supply are unchanged.
The two copies of F are the CURRENT clocks, not extra immutable records.

The normalizer acts on outputs and explicitly provided receiving auxiliaries;
it acts as identity on the complete hidden memory, including F. Thus K is an
inaccessible classical reference for this stage. Bob's accessible output has
state I_(D^L)/D^L for every K. Adding independent local work, supplied Bell
states, or independent charged catalysts leaves I(K;B_initial)=0. Entanglement
is not a free correlated inventory record.

More generally, let B_initial be ALL initially accessible Bob registers and
put I_initial=I(K;B_initial). For an actually supplied undisturbing classical
record Z_B generated from K, with auxiliary/input state independent of K
conditional on Z_B, I_initial=I(K;Z_B). For arbitrary correlated receiving
resources use their actual I_initial; do not replace it by the entropy of a
record which has not been supplied. Clock access, if granted, belongs here.

2. JOINT TARGET FAMILY AND THE ROBUST LAW

Let Theta be a target on the SAME external D-pairs and unchanged memory. It
may include arbitrary classical transcript T and other declared receiver
outputs. Its K marginal is uniform. Conditional on (K=k,T=t), its quantum
external state is

              Phi_D^n tensor |00><00|^(L-n),
       n=n(k,t),              0 <= k-n <= w,                         (2)

where w>=0 is an integer and 0<=n<=L. The transcript may be correlated with K.
Extra independent target registers do not matter. The theorem also holds if
the constraint in (2) is only a mean constraint E(K-n)<=w with n<=K always.
All target branches and K correlations are averaged; no postselection occurs.

Suppose a finite-round output-only LOCC normalizer Gamma, with all records and
outcome branches included or actually discarded as declared, produces rho
with GLOBAL joint half-trace error

                       T(rho,Theta)<=epsilon<=1/2.                  (3)

Let C_j denote its jth Alice-to-Bob classical message, including a distinguished
stop/null value whenever that value carries information. Let T_<j be the
public classical transcript preceding that message. Define the entropy charge

                   C_forward=sum_j H(C_j | T_<j).                  (4)

Every entropy is evaluated on the actual exact-source execution. The fixed
alphabet charge sum_j ln |alphabet(C_j)| is a larger valid charge. For a
variable-length implementation, a prefix-free code or an equivalent complete
timing/stop convention is needed; free stopping signals are not omitted.
Messages use finite or countable discrete alphabets and Shannon entropies,
possibly +infinity. A continuous analog signal requires an explicit digitized
coding/interface model; its differential entropy is not a communication charge.

Define h2(x)=-x ln x-(1-x)ln(1-x), and

       g(u)=(u+1)ln(u+1)-u ln u,        g(0)=0,
       mu_D=1/(D-1).

THEOREM. Under (1)--(4),

 C_forward + I_initial >=
        (1-epsilon)ln L - g(w+mu_D) - h2(epsilon).                  (5)

Take the positive part if the right side is negative. In particular, for
global epsilon<=1/2 and w=O(ln L), the required forward information is
Omega(ln L) in the absence of a supplied correlated record. For w=0 and
epsilon=0 it is at least ln L-g(mu_D).

SYMMETRIC COROLLARY. Put R=(1-epsilon)ln L-g(w+mu_D)-h2(epsilon). Apply the
same proof after globally swapping Alice and Bob. For respective initially
accessible systems with information I_initial,A and I_initial,B, one has

       C_(A->B)+I_initial,B >= R,
       C_(B->A)+I_initial,A >= R.                                 (5a)

Thus with no correlated receiving access, total two-direction entropy charge
is at least 2[R]_+. If R>0, one-way LOCC in either direction is IMPOSSIBLE,
even with arbitrary independent entangled supply. This is a joint-target
information obstruction, not an entanglement-consumption assertion.

SHARP-SCALE ONE-WAY COROLLARY. Under the POINTWISE deficit premise (2), without
correlated receiving access, every one-way output-only LOCC, in either
direction, obeys for every integer a>=0

 epsilon >= [1-D^(-(a+1))-(w+a+1)/L]_+.                            (5b)

This statement does not require epsilon<=1/2. Taking a=floor(log_D L) yields

 epsilon >= [1-(w+floor(log_D L)+2)/L]_+.                          (5c)

Thus if w=O(ln L), a one-way receiver has nearly maximal global joint error;
arbitrarily large independent entangled supply does not change this fact.
The mean-only relaxation following (2) applies to (5),(5a), NOT to (5b),(5c).
For only E(K-n)<=w, Markov's inequality instead gives, for integers s,a>=0,

 epsilon >= [1-w/(s+1)-D^(-(a+1))-(s+a+1)/L]_+.                    (5d)

3. PROOF OF THE TARGET INFORMATION LOWER BOUND

Bob measures all his quantum D-sites in the computational basis and records
J, the index of the LAST nonzero digit, or J=0 when every digit is zero. Under
(2), conditional on n, the first n digits are independent uniform D-ary digits
and all later digits are zero. The number n-J of trailing zero digits obeys

 Pr(n-J >= a | n)=D^(-a),  1<=a<=n,
 E(n-J | n)=sum_(a=1)^n D^(-a)<=mu_D.                                (6)

Consequently G=K-J=(K-n)+(n-J)>=0 and EG<=w+mu_D. Since K is determined by
(J,G),

 H(K|J)=H(G|J)<=H(G)<=g(w+mu_D).                                    (7)

The last inequality is the maximum-entropy property of the geometric law:
relative entropy to the geometric distribution of mean u gives H(G)<=g(u)
whenever EG<=u. It is valid without any independence among K,n,T. In the ideal
target, therefore I(K;J)>=ln L-g(w+mu_D).

Measurement and partial trace contract (3), so the classical joint laws p,q of
(K,J) for rho and Theta have total variation at most epsilon. Both have the
same uniform K marginal because Gamma never acts on F.

For completeness, the needed conditional-entropy continuity is elementary.
For two classical laws at variation d, write
 p=(1-d)r+d p', q=(1-d)r+d q'
by their common mass. Adding a mixture flag shows that H(K|J) lies between the
weighted component conditional entropies and that value plus h2(d). Since
0<=H(K|J)<=ln L, this proves

                 |H_p(K|J)-H_q(K|J)|<=d ln L+h2(d).                 (8)

The right side is increasing on 0<=d<=1/2. Equations (7),(8) yield

       I_rho(K;B_final)>=I_rho(K;J)
                >=(1-epsilon)ln L-g(w+mu_D)-h2(epsilon).            (9)

If rho also contains a receiver transcript, Bob can ignore it in (9). Its
existence cannot weaken the bound. Keeping its information in B_final can
only increase the required information flow.

4. PROOF OF THE INTERACTIVE INFORMATION-FLOW UPPER BOUND

Track ALL accessible Bob registers, including his local outcomes, work and
copies of every message. If the actual receiver discards classical outcomes or
transcripts, retain additional copies ONLY in the proof's bookkeeping extension
and trace them to recover the declared protocol. Classical copying leaves all
actual marginals and message statistics unchanged. This is not an actual added
retained memory with an asserted return error. Data processing from the enlarged
final system still bounds the actual final output information from above.
A Bob local CPTP map cannot increase I(K;B) by data
processing. An Alice local instrument, before its outcome is sent, leaves KB
unchanged by no signaling. Sending an Alice classical outcome C to Bob can
increase his information by exactly I(K;C|B), at most H(C), because C is
classical and H(C|KB)>=0. Condition on the public preceding transcript to get
the sharper bound H(C|T_past). Bob-to-Alice feedback is generated locally from
Bob's registers and therefore creates no additional information about K in
Bob's complete retained accessible system. Iterating through the finite
protocol gives

                   I(K;B_final)<=I_initial+C_forward.              (10)

Independent entangled auxiliaries, catalytic returns, arbitrary quantum work,
and adaptive choices do not affect this argument. They are already inside
the tracked accessible systems. Combining (9) and (10) proves (5).

To prove (5b) under the pointwise premise, take the party who receives no
classical messages. Its initial
accessible marginal is independent of K. All its operations are local, and
the other party's averaged local operations do not signal, so its final
accessible marginal remains independent of K, even with independent shared
entanglement. Measure its last-nonzero index J. For the event

                   E_a={0<=K-J<=w+a},

the actual independent distribution has Pr(E_a)<=(w+a+1)/L: for any fixed J
there are at most w+a+1 permitted values of the uniform K. The target obeys
Pr(E_a)>=1-D^(-(a+1)), because K-n<=w and the trailing-zero run n-J has tail
Pr(n-J>=a+1)<=D^(-(a+1)). The difference of these event probabilities is at
most their total variation, and hence at most the full joint trace error.
This proves (5b); D^(-(floor(log_D L)+1))<=1/L gives (5c). Neither the target
nor the actual outcome branches have been postselected.
For the mean-only version, Pr(K-n>=s+1)<=w/(s+1). The same event proof uses
E_(s,a)={0<=K-J<=s+a}, loses at most this Markov probability and the trailing
zero tail D^(-(a+1)), and has independent actual probability at most
(s+a+1)/L. This proves (5d).

5. MEASURED-SITE AND ACCESS CONSEQUENCES WITH THEIR EXACT PREMISES

If a search protocol's ONLY forward messages are q measured D-ary site
outcomes, with at most ln D nats per site including its complete stopping
description, then C_forward<=q ln D, and (5) gives

 q >= [(1-epsilon)ln L-g(w+mu_D)-h2(epsilon)-I_initial]_+ / ln D.    (11)

For variable q, the same conclusion uses E q only when a complete actual
prefix-free/stop encoding proves C_forward<=m E q. A free stopping label or
unpriced separate message invalidates that conversion, not theorem (5).
This is not a universal lower bound on measured pairs: a protocol with an
additional forward classical information source must charge that source too.

If no communication is permitted, (5a) is instead a necessary inventory-access
price on EACH receiving side. When BOTH local controllers can read their known
current K clock, each has ln L nats of inventory information, and they can keep
the active Bell prefix and reset its suffix exactly. A K record available only
on one side does not suffice without communication to the other side. Thus
the access scale in (5) is matched to a dimension-only additive term at w=0.
This is an actual known-clock interface, not a mathematical decomposition
identified with an experimental readout. An extra immutable initial K copy
would be a different complete-return device and is not inserted here.

6. GLOBAL INITIALIZATION ERROR AND COMPARATOR SCOPE

The information law also covers UNRESTRICTED collective normalization of r
independent complete queue sources, without a per-copy error substitution.
Let the target, conditional on the uniform vector K=(K_1,...,K_r) and an
arbitrary correlated transcript, have task a in the form (2) with
0<=K_a-n_a<=w_a. Let its GLOBAL joint error be epsilon<=1/2. Then in EACH
direction the entire interactive protocol obeys

 C_direction + I(K;initial receiving system)
   >= (1-epsilon) r ln L-sum_a g(w_a+mu_D)-h2(epsilon).             (12)

Proof: measure each receiving task's last-nonzero J_a. Conditional entropy
subadditivity gives H(K|J)<=sum_a H(K_a|J_a)<=sum_a g(w_a+mu_D).
The classical variable K has alphabet L^r, so (8) charges epsilon r ln L plus
ONE h2(epsilon) for the global error. The same interactive proof (10) works
for all joint processing. No product processing or independent target
transcripts are assumed. In particular amortization does not make the
communication per task vanish when w=O(ln L); this still is not a Bell-loss
or formation converse.

If a fixed normalizer with a deterministic declared message-alphabet budget
receives an epsilon_init-close complete initializer and its actual joint
output is epsilon_norm-close to the reference target, apply the SAME channel
to the exact source. Contractivity and the triangle inequality give (3) with
epsilon=epsilon_init+epsilon_norm, provided this is <=1/2. The alphabet budget
prices both executions, so (5) remains a valid necessary bound with that larger
error. For entropy/expected communication charges evaluated only on the actual
approximate initializer, this argument alone does NOT transfer their values;
one must separately apply the information-flow/target-reconstruction argument
using the actual K law, actual I_initial and actual message statistics, or
price a finite-alphabet continuity bound. The actual approximate K need not be
uniform, so the simplified expression (5) cannot just be inserted unchanged.
No independent-copy/local-error substitution is made for global initialization
error.

The theorem concerns the JOINT target, including the actual hidden current
clock. An independent replacement stock with a freshly sampled inventory can
match an external marginal while failing the clock correlations completely.
It is not an admissible comparator for (3). The strongest ordinary comparator
receives the same outputs, independent supply, actual access and communication
privileges, and obeys (5). Full TWO-SIDED clock access permits a
zero-extra-entanglement reset operation and saturates its information scale.
A receiver with no clock
can instead pay communication/search costs. No additional Bell-supply lower
bound is proved here; arbitrarily many independent Bell pairs do not by
themselves transmit the hidden inventory to Bob.

This obstruction does not cover a weaker target which merely has approximate
MC support but permits K-independent local marginals. Such a target need not
reveal K locally. It also does not establish a stock advantage or a costless
normalizer. The entanglement supply, all retained records and complete return
of any enlarged memory remain separate obligations. Physical communication,
reset and disposal costs are not identified with entanglement supply.
