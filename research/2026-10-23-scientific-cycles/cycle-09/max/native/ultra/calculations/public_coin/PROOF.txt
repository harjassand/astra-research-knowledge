1. SOURCE, ACCESS, TARGET AND MAIN STATEMENT

Fix qubit pairs, L>=2, Phi=|00+11><00+11|/2, nu=I_2/2 tensor I_2/2.
Use the exact counted-current-clock finite queue of Cycle6. After its L calls,
the complete joint state is

 Omega=(1/L) sum_(k=1)^L |kk><kk|_F tensor
             [Phi^k tensor nu^(L-k)]_O tensor nu^L_M.              (1)

F denotes the two CURRENT clock registers, not an extra inventory record. All
of F,M are retained hidden memory for this receiving stage. The operation acts
as identity on them and cannot read them. The queue's same fixed device,
faithful eta=nu, exact first Bell output and complete one-call return distance
delta=(1-2^(-2L))/L remain those of Cycle6. This output-only operation does NOT
turn its receiving apparatus into a returned reusable catalyst.

Let 0<epsilon<=1/2. Put
 H=ceil(log_2 L),
 T0=ceil(2H+20 ln(3/epsilon)),
 s=g=ceil(log_2(3/epsilon)),
 Q=15 T0+s, and w=Q+g.                                           (2)
These conservative constants are explicit, not optimized. A fixed finite-round
LOCC operation Gamma, with no additional entanglement and no correlated clock
access, produces rho such that there is an EXPLICIT reference Theta on the
same external systems and COMPLETE old memory satisfying

 T(rho,Theta)<=epsilon,                                          (3)
 Theta=(1/L) sum_k sum_(R,t) p(R,t|k)|kk><kk|_F tensor nu^L_M
       tensor |RR,tt><RR,tt|_classical
       tensor [Phi^nstar(k,R,t) tensor |00><00|^(L-nstar)],         (4)
 max(1,k-w)<=nstar(k,R,t)<=k on EVERY reference branch.            (5)

p is the ACTUAL, unpostselected public coin and transcript law; it is neither
chosen retrospectively to improve the bound nor assumed independent of k.
The formula defining nstar below makes (4) unique once the operation is fixed.
Classical R,t are two matching copies, one per party; they may instead actually
be discarded, in which case (3) follows by trace contraction. Every retained
register is included. Local tail outcomes/work are actually discarded after
use; the proof first includes them and then traces them, without a return claim.

O=m sum_(i=1)^L Phi_(1..i), m=ln2, acts on the external pairs. With
B=(L+1)m/2, (5) gives

 Tr O Theta >= B-wm,
 Tr O rho >= B-wm-Lm epsilon.                                    (6)

Thus for fixed epsilon, the normalized target loses only O(log L) Bell pairs
on every reference branch, although exact source-specific MC normalization
without added entanglement has score bounded by O(1) (Cycle7). There is no
hidden clock access, inaccessible posterior selector, or output independence
premise. The first physical Bell pair stays EXACT on every actual branch.

The operation uses at most Q parity measurements and sends at most Q bits in
EACH direction. For this public-coin version the shared independent seed has
Q(L-1) fair bits, explicitly supplied initially or generated/transmitted at that
additional communication cost. Its retained two copies are in (4). It is not
entanglement and does not carry initial inventory information. This sizeable
control cost is essential to the stated finite resource contract. Local gate,
work, reset and source preparation costs are in Section 6.

2. THE COMMUTING PARITY ORACLE, WITH ALL CORRELATIONS

On a fixed k branch, all computational-basis difference digits e_i=b_i-a_i
(mod2) vanish for i<=k; digits for i>k are iid fair. This is a mathematical
coupling of the diagonal measurement statistics, NOT an accessible sample of
the Bell prefix. One may adjoin the classical suffix computational digits in
an extension because nu is diagonal. All our measurements are local Z-parity
measurements and hence commute. Consequently the entire adaptive transcript
has exactly the statistics of a fixed difference vector e with this law.
No actual individual Z measurement of an unmeasured Bell digit is made here.

Define J=min{i:e_i!=0}-1, or J=L if there is no such digit. Then J>=k and

 Pr(J-k>g | k)<=2^(-(g+1)).                                      (7)

A raw oracle query at threshold j, 2<=j<=L, takes a fresh independent uniform
binary row h on coordinates 2,...,j. Each party coherently computes its local
Z parity into a pure local ancilla, measures it, and sends the resulting bit
to the other party. Both retain the pair of bits in t. The answer is YES when
they match. A threshold j<=1 is synthetic YES; j>=L+1 is synthetic NO, requiring
no physical row. Rows omit site1, which is never touched by the operation.
For a physical query the parity difference is h dot e_(2..j). In the analysis
filtration reveal seed rows ONLY when used: condition on e, all earlier used
rows/outcomes and the resulting query choices, NOT unused seed bits. The next
row is uniform in this filtration. The specified controller ignores unused
seed rows even though both parties could inspect them. Thus

 j<=J: YES with certainty;
 j>J:  YES with probability 1/2.                                (8)

Independence is not asserted conditional on the full pre-supplied R; the final
joint reference may retain R without changing this probability calculation.

This remains true under adaptive choice of j and repeated overlapping queries.
The seed contains independent full rows in advance; row number selects the
next unused row and its restriction, independent of the selected threshold.
If J=L, no physical mismatch ever occurs, which is harmless.

Throughout the operation maintain

 U=min({L} union {j-1: a RAW physical query at j has a mismatch}).   (9)

Every mismatch proves j>J, so U>=J>=k on EVERY actual branch. Match outcomes
are never interpreted as exact absence of a suffix error.

3. EXPLICIT ROBUST SEARCH (KNOWN MACHINERY, PROVED HERE)

An amplified oracle at j is YES iff five independent raw queries all say YES.
Each invocation uses five fresh rows (can reserve all five even after an early
mismatch), so it has one-sided error at most 1/32, conditionally on any past
and e. The synthetic endpoints are exact. Record all its raw mismatches in U.

Construct the ordinary balanced binary interval tree on integer leaves 1..L.
A node [a,b], a<b, has children [a,floor((a+b)/2)] and
[floor((a+b)/2)+1,b]. Attach a single-child verification chain of length T0 to
each leaf; every chain node has the same singleton interval as that leaf.
Start at the root. Perform exactly T0 steps, with no free stopping signal:

 (i) Query the amplified predicates J>=a and J>=b+1 (both defined by (8)).
     Membership passes iff the first says YES and the second NO.
 (ii) On a failed membership check move to the parent, or stay at root.
 (iii) On a passed check at an internal interval query J>=mid+1 and descend
       right on YES, left on NO. At a leaf/chain node descend its chain.

At most three amplified predicates, hence 15 raw queries, occur per step.
The fixed tree and reserved seed slots make all null branches explicit.

Let the true path be the root-to-J path followed by J's verification chain.
For any visited vertex v put
 P(v)=2 depth(LCA(v,true path))-depth(v).
Depth of the last common ancestor is well defined on the truncated tree;
we only visit depths <=T0, so the attached length is sufficient.
At a vertex on the true path, correct membership checks and a correct split
move one edge along that path, increasing P by 1. At a vertex off the true
path, correct membership checks fail and move one edge toward the true path,
also increasing P by 1. At root a failure can cause a self-loop, still an
increment >=-1. Every other step changes P by +/-1. A union bound over the
at-most-three predicate calls shows, conditionally on every past and e,

 Pr(the step does not increase P by1)<=3/32<=1/8.                  (10)

Let B_t count those bad steps. For any adaptive process satisfying (10),
iterated conditional expectation yields E[2^B_T0]<=(9/8)^T0. Thus

 Pr(B_T0>=T0/4)<=exp(-gamma T0),
 gamma=(ln2)/4-ln(9/8)>1/20.                                    (11)

Since P_final>=T0-2B_T0 and T0>=2H, outside this bad event P_final>H.
Every internal true-path node has depth <H or <=H-1; every off-path vertex
has P<=H-1. Therefore the final node is on the TRUE J verification chain.
Read its leaf label Jhat. If the final vertex is internal, define Jhat=1.
Equations (2),(11) give

 Pr(Jhat!=J | e)<=exp(-gamma T0)<=epsilon/3.                     (12)

Finally query the raw oracle s further times at Jhat+1. If Jhat=L, the
synthetic NO gives U=L directly. Otherwise a correct Jhat yields a mismatch
except with probability 2^-s<=epsilon/3. Update U from all physical mismatches
including these. On successful search/validation U=J; always U>=J. Combined
with (7), except on total probability <=epsilon we have

 c=max(1,U-g)<=k,  while on EVERY branch c>=max(1,k-g).             (13)

There are at most Q raw queries. The search confidence bound is a classical
probability statement; it does not postselect or permit omission of failures.

4. EXACT QUANTUM DECODING ON EVERY SAFE BRANCH

After determining c, each party measures its own sites i>c in Z and retains
the local values only until the following correction. Take H_c to be the
matrix of all measured physical parity rows restricted to sites2..c and let
r=rank_F2 H_c. Query rows and c are known to both parties from R,t. Write the
local parity equations as H_c x=u_A and H_c x=u_B after subtracting each
party's measured tail contributions from its measured parity outcomes.

Fix k, R, complete public transcript and BOTH tail strings in a proof extension.
On a safe branch c<=k the first c source pairs began as Phi^c. All diagonal
projectors commute, and their restriction to this prefix is precisely the
displayed system of equations. Since the computational strings of a Bell
prefix agree, u_A=u_B in all independent row constraints on a nonzero branch.
The resulting normalized prefix is the pure equal-phase affine-code state

 2^(-(c-r)/2) sum_(x: H_c x_(2..c)=u) |x>_A |x>_B.               (14)

There are no discarded phase signs: Z-parity projection and tail Z projection
are diagonal with entries 0 or1. Equation (14) also holds after conditioning on
an adaptive path, because each fixed path specifies just a sequence of these
commuting projectors; feedforward adds no extra constraint inside that path.

Use a common deterministic Gaussian elimination of H_c. Each party applies
the corresponding invertible binary coordinate transformation and translates
its own pivot coordinates by its own syndrome. This maps its affine subspace
onto r zero pivots and c-r free coordinates. Coordinate1 is independent of all
rows and is retained as the first Bell pair. Permute the other free coordinates
to sites2,...,c-r, reset pivots and measured tail sites to zero. Both parties use
the same linear map and, on safe branches, the same relevant syndrome. Hence
the final quantum output is EXACTLY

                Phi^n tensor |00><00|^(L-n), n=c-r.              (15)

On an unsafe branch, apply the identical well-defined local circuits using
each party's own syndrome, even when they disagree; no correctness assertion
is needed. Site1 remains an untouched exact Bell pair, independent of the rest,
on all branches. Since r<=min(Q,c-1), on every branch the declared integer n
satisfies n>=max(1,k-g-Q). This does NOT say that an unsafe output actually
contains n Bell pairs. n is a circuit-defined register allocation there.

Tail outcomes, syndrome work and measurement ancillas are now actually
discarded, with entropy export counted. They are not retained hidden memory
whose return is assumed. If a user instead retains them, extend the same
reference with their full actual joint law before claiming (3).

5. FULL JOINT COUPLING AND SCORE

Set nstar=max(1,min(k,n)). On every branch (5) follows from n>=k-w. In the
extension conditioning also on the local tail outcomes, define the reference
quantum output by (15) with n replaced by nstar, with the SAME actual law of
all classical conditioning registers, and the same full old memory. On a safe
branch nstar=n and the actual conditional state equals the reference. On any
other branch half trace distance is at most1. Averaging and (13) give (3) for
the extension and, by tracing actual discarded tail/work registers, for (4).
Old nu^L memory pairs are independent, and its CURRENT clock k is retained
throughout this argument. Thus there is no output-memory decoupling shortcut.

For a pure nstar-Bell prefix with zero tail, at least its first nstar prefix
projectors have expectation1, so score >=m nstar. Averaging uniform k gives
Tr O Theta>=m(EK-w)=B-mw. Since 0<=O<=Lm I and rho,Theta have the same trace,
|Tr O(rho-Theta)|<=Lm T(rho,Theta); this proves (6). The matching R,t copies
can be included in a standard product-string MC external basis if desired.
The old nu-memory is NOT claimed to be MC.

For a constant contribution from the trace-distance error term choose, for
example, epsilon=1/L (L>=2). Then w=O(log L), total score loss <=wm+m. Thus the logarithmic
loss is not obtained by hiding an extensive L epsilon score term. Applying the
operation independently to r queues with per-task epsilon/r gives one GLOBAL
joint error <=epsilon and w=O(logL+log(r/epsilon)); no per-copy/global error
substitution or claim of independence of the source's own output slots is made.
At fixed L, w grows with r and eventually the guarantee is vacuous. No uniform
fixed-L, r->infinity formation consequence follows from this union bound.

6. RESOURCE AND IMPLEMENTATION ACCOUNTING

The complete initial reservoir supply remains exactly B nats in the preselected
mean entanglement convention of Cycle6. Deterministic exact supply is L ln2;
the local memory dimension is L2^L. No entangled receiver ancilla is used.
The receiver acts AFTER the L uses, and its apparatus and export channels are
not part of a returned cycle. Thus this is not an economy beyond ordinary stock.

Public coins: Q(L-1) fair bits, two matching copies initially independent of k.
Each physical query uses the next unused row, restricting it to its chosen
threshold. Retaining the seed consumes that many classical bits per party;
discarding it exports that entropy. If all shared randomness must be created
by messaging, Alice can send the entire seed at its full Q(L-1)-bit cost before
the operation; the O(logL) ONLINE two-direction bound then describes only the
subsequent messages. Do not label that implementation O(logL) total communication.

Messages: at most Q one-bit messages in each direction, fixed reserved slots
and explicit null messages if padding is wanted. Public transcript up to2Q bits
per party. Query indices, U,c,r are deterministic classical computations from
the declared seed/transcript and tree, not extra inventory information.

Local work: at most Q parity ancillas per party if retained until decoding;
they may be reused after recording/reset. At most L-1 final tail measurements,
and at most L+O(Q) local temporary outcome bits per party. Parity computations
use <=Q(L-1) local CNOTs per party; Gaussian elimination costs O(LQ^2+Q^3)
binary operations with a simple dense algorithm, and the resulting invertible
coordinate map can be compiled using O(LQ+Q^2+L) CNOTs/swaps. Source/register
initialization and L calls remain additional work. No gate depth, noise
tolerance, thermal reset cost, experimental access, or practical advantage is
established. A deterministic disjoint-support search variant, if audited,
is recorded separately and can remove this public-coin seed cost.

7. WHAT IS OPEN AND WHAT CHANGES

Exact support forced only one source Bell pair to survive Cycle7 normalization
of this full-rank-tail source. The present finite LOCC operation, acting on the
same actual source with no source-correlated access, reaches a joint pure-prefix
MC reference at any specified global error while retaining k-O(logL+log1/epsilon)
Bell pairs. It explains exactly which premise in the exact-support theorem is
lost: unsafe branches remain and are paid in global trace distance, rather
than being silently removed. It is an APPROXIMATE-NORMALIZATION upper bound.

The independent lower theorem prices interactive communication and supplied
inventory access for this very same target. For w=O(logL), epsilon=1/L it
requires lnL-O(lnlnL) nats in EACH direction without supplied access; the
public-coin online O(logL) construction matches the logarithmic order. This
does NOT prove that logarithmic Bell loss is optimal. Nor does it lower bound
ordinary formation below B, close the original generic formation gap, or
establish historical novelty. The exact all-branch theorem and the approximate
operation coexist without contradiction. The remaining substantive boundary
is a universal loss-versus-error/access law (or a better source-specific
operation) under a fully matched control/ancilla contract.
