DETERMINISTIC-COLOR RECEIVING OPERATION

PROOF.txt: complete approximate output-only construction and resource scope.
colored_search.py: exact receiver control; an implicit lazy integer tree makes
each fractional-median weight update O(log L) integer operations. Its callback
is a real local parity instrument, not a hidden inventory oracle.
INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.txt: bounded independent analytic and implementation audit.
audit_integer_tree.py / IMPLEMENTATION_AUDIT_CHECK.json: 128 fixed exact

replay_fixture.py / RESULT.json / TIMING.json: one declared million-site

empirical proof of the global trace-distance theorem or a density-matrix replay.

Portable commands (standard Python 3.10+ only):
  python3 audit_integer_tree.py
  python3 replay_fixture.py --outdir /path/to/fresh/result-directory

Code and proof are source-specific to the counted queue with independent nu
tails and known coordinate order. Every D>=2 is supported by the actual
disjoint coefficient-one mod-D parity construction, including composite D.
The mathematical K appears only in proofs and fixture generation. The receiver
never accesses K or the counted clock. Actual fresh source-tail entropy is
used; no additional shared random seed or entanglement is supplied.

Original physical fresh-site binary-search idea was not substituted silently:
its near-boundary oracle needs an indifference-band proof. The conservative
fractional-median lemma supplies such a proof, but the receiving operation
improved further by querying disjoint color PARITIES, preserving all but one
Bell degree of freedom per nonempty parity equation after explicit decoding.
The older public-coin fixed-latent-J tree is a separate root construction and
is preserved as an independently transparent alternative with its full seed
cost. A standard noisy-search citation alone would not have justified either
fresh-site or adaptive conditional quantum independence.

No ordinary-stock resource advantage, universal Bell-loss lower bound,
historical novelty, efficient generic normalization, or physical reset/energy
benefit is claimed. Approximate MC support is paid in joint trace distance;
the actual output is not declared exactly MC or Q=0. Generic iid formation
limits are not solved by independent per-copy normalization with error/r.
