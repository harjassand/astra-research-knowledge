2. ONE FRESH BELL PAIR IMPLEMENTS THE NONLOCAL DIFFERENCE INSTRUMENT

Let h be a specified Z_D row on sites2,...,j, and let H_A(x_A),H_B(x_B) be
the two local weighted sums mod D. A fresh ancillary pair starts in
D^-1/2 sum_z |z,z>. Each party applies the LOCAL controlled addition

 |x_A,z> -> |x_A,z+H_A(x_A)>,
 |x_B,z> -> |x_B,z+H_B(x_B)>.

Measure BOTH ancillary halves in Z, obtaining a,b, and exchange those symbols.
The conditional data Kraus operator is exactly

 K_(a,b)=D^-1/2 P_(b-a),
 P_s= sum_(x_A,x_B: H_B(x_B)-H_A(x_A)=s)
                  |x_A,x_B><x_A,x_B|.                            (A1)

Indeed the amplitude is nonzero precisely when the original ancillary digit
z equals both a-H_A and b-H_B. There is then exactly one such z. In particular
(A1) measures the NONLOCAL DIFFERENCE, not the individual local data sums.
Its normalization is complete: for each difference s there are exactly D
outcome pairs (a,b), so sum_(a,b) K_(a,b)^dagger K_(a,b)=I.

This instrument is LOCC because its sole nonlocal aid is the counted fresh
Phi_D. Ancilla outcomes a,b are uniform among the D compatible common shifts,
but their difference is the measured syndrome. The auxiliary has been measured
and is consumed. The operation is valid for EVERY integer D>=2.
