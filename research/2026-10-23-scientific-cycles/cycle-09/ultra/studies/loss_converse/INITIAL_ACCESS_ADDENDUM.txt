PROOF.
Append the same actual coarse last-nonzero readout C and positive joint test
on only (K,J,external output); trace Z if it is retained. Its actual score is
  S=sum_z sum_k (1/L)p(z|k) s_(k,z),
where s_(k,z)>=0 is the CP/fidelity score conditional on that supplied pair.
By positivity and tau_(k,z)<=kappa_z zeta_z,
  S<=sum_z kappa_z max_k p(z|k)
      * [sum_k (1/L) s_(k,z; zeta_z)].
For each fixed z, supplying independent separable zeta_z makes the effective
operation on sigma_k separable. The bracket is at most the common score
constant D(D^(b+a+1)-1)/[(D-1)L] by the exact product-tail Kraus argument.
Summing gives A times that constant. Countable sums are nonnegative, so
Tonelli/monotone convergence justifies their order; if A diverges the bound
is vacuous, not finite. The reference lower bound is the same as before,
because its last-nonzero trailing law depends only on each target N. This
proves (A2), and the same CDF-tail integration proves (A3).

EXPLICIT CASES.
Independent shared classical randomness: p(z|k)=p(z), kappa_z=1, hence A=1.
Its entropy/creation cost remains an upper-construction implementation cost,
but it cannot evade this loss converse even if granted for free.

Deterministic q-label coarse inventory access: z=f(k) with q nonempty cells,
kappa_z=1 gives A=q. Both-party exact K access gives A=L and makes the loss
obstruction vacuous, as required by exact keep-K/reset-tail.

No receiving entanglement: kappa_z=1. The classical quantity
  log A=log sum_z max_k p(z|k)
is the known maximal-leakage / order-infinity access quantity. We use its
displayed formula directly and do not assert a novel information measure.
If kappa_z<=kappa_max, then A<=kappa_max exp(maximal leakage).

Independent preselected Bell rank M: p(M|k)=p(M), tau_M=Phi_M,
kappa_M=M gives A=E M. The supplied label may be discarded later; its initial
presence and the total initial entangled state are still charged. An expected
preselected log M budget does not control E M and cannot be substituted.

GENERAL PURE / MIXED AUXILIARIES.
For an independent tau, minimizing log kappa over tau<=kappa zeta_sep is the

Here no optimization oracle or physical certificate of kappa is assumed.
For a pure state with Schmidt coefficients sqrt(lambda_i), its familiar

WHY INTERNAL TRANSCRIPTS ARE NOT FREE INITIAL ACCESS.
Conditioning on a measurement transcript produced by the protocol generally
changes the quantum source away from sigma_k. It therefore fails the explicit
conditional-product premise sigma_k tensor tau_(k,z) used above. The formula
A cannot be applied to an internally generated transcript as if that record
had been supplied before the operation. Every retained record still appears
in the joint error; initial access and generated information remain distinct.

WHY A SHANNON-ONLY CLAIM IS NOT MADE.
Erasure-style access reveals K with probability p and otherwise a common
blank. It has A=1+p(L-1), while I(K;Z)=p log L. These quantities can differ
greatly. Our law is stated under the displayed one-shot access/resource
constraint, not an unproved conversion from a mutual-information, entropy,
energy, average formation-cost or experimentally accessible bound.
