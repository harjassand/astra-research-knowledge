INDEPENDENT CHECK OF THE PARENT'S DECISIVE SEP CONVERSE
22 October 2026. Bounded reviewer: fixed_error_lo.

I independently expanded the Kraus contraction and checked the proposed
last-nonzero converter. I find the argument valid under the stated pointwise
reference allocation contract. It bypasses the epsilon L obstruction in the
entropy method in the companion file. The construction/proof is the parent's;
this is its independent algebra/interface check, not a duplicate novelty claim.

SETUP
K uniform1..L, actual sigma_k=Phi_D^k tensor nu^(L-k). Gamma is output-only
LOCC (indeed an arbitrary separable CPTP map on these finite ports suffices).
No extra entangled auxiliary or K-correlated apparatus is supplied. Complete
joint error to a reference pure prefix Phi^nstar zeros is <=epsilon, where
max(1,k-w)<=nstar(k,t)<=k on every reference branch, and K and actual public
transcript are part of the comparison. Independent local ancillas are absorbed
into Gamma. No reference formation cost or support is asserted for the actual.

CONVERTER
Alice performs the COARSE projective measurement J=last nonzero digit index
in her computational output string (J=0 if the whole string is zero). This
measurement leaves the coordinates before J coherent. She sends J to Bob.
Both keep only their first n_J=max(0,J-1) sites and reset all other sites.

On Phi_D^nstar zeros the projector for J=j>=1 is identity on sites<j,
the nonzero projector at j, and zero projectors after j. The first j-1 pairs
remain EXACT Phi_D^(j-1). If j=0 there are no retained pairs and the fidelity
test is the scalar1. No fine computational-basis measurement of the prefix
is performed. This distinction is necessary: measuring the entire string
would destroy the very Bell state used in the bound.

For integer a>=0, the target has
  Pr[nstar-J>a] <= D^(-(a+1)).
This is the probability of at least a+1 trailing zero digits, truncated when
nstar<a+1. On the complementary target event,
  J<=nstar<=K<=nstar+w<=J+w+a.
Hence the final target has probability >=1-D^(-(a+1)) of simultaneously
K in the band [J,J+w+a] and passing the Phi_D^n_J test on retained pairs.
The actual final state passes this JOINT test with probability
  S >= 1-epsilon-D^(-(a+1)),
by complete trace contraction and the operational characterization of trace
distance. The test may mathematically read retained K; the physical receiver
does not. That is a legitimate test of the declared complete joint target.

PRODUCT-KRAUS BOUND
Include the converter in Gamma. Refine all local discarded environments and
all classical branches into product Kraus operators A_lambda tensor B_lambda,
with fixed J=J_lambda per branch. Put
  r=D^k, t=D^(L-k), d=D^n_J, N=D^L.
Let A map C^N to C^d and similarly B, after the branch's discarded tail has
been refined into local Kraus indices. Padding outputs or using a direct sum
over J gives the same trace-preservation identity. In input coordinates
x=1..r, u,v=1..t and output a=1..d, the subnormalized Bell fidelity, including
the uniform prior 1/L, is EXACTLY

 f_(lambda,k) = [L t^2 d r]^(-1)
       sum_(u,v) |sum_(a,x) A_(a,xu) B_(a,xv)|^2.

There is no hidden complex-conjugation premise: the Bell amplitude uses A B;
ordinary absolute-value Cauchy-Schwarz still bounds it by the products of
their squared Frobenius norms. For each u,v, Cauchy-Schwarz on (a,x) yields

 f_(lambda,k)
 <= [L t^2 d r]^(-1)
       [sum_(u,a,x)|A_(a,xu)|^2]
       [sum_(v,a,x)|B_(a,xv)|^2]
  = L^(-1) D^(-2L+k-n_J) Tr(A^dagger A) Tr(B^dagger B).

If k is in the eligible band, k-n_J<=w+a+1. Its number of integer values is
at most floor(w+a)+1<=w+a+1. For J=0, the k-n_J inequality is stronger, not
weaker; no special unbounded contribution is missed. Summing all product
Kraus branches uses ONLY
 sum_lambda A_lambda^dagger A_lambda tensor B_lambda^dagger B_lambda=I_(N^2),
and its trace gives
 sum_lambda Tr(A_lambda^dagger A_lambda)Tr(B_lambda^dagger B_lambda)=D^(2L).
Therefore

 S <= [(w+a+1)D^(w+a+1)]/L,

and the rigorous necessary condition is

 1-epsilon-D^(-(a+1)) <= [(w+a+1)D^(w+a+1)]/L.                 (SEP)

This holds for EVERY integer a>=0. For any fixed epsilon<1, choose a constant
a with D^(-(a+1))<1-epsilon. Taking logs shows, at fixed D,
 w >= [ln L-ln ln L-O_(D,epsilon)(1)]/ln D.
Thus logarithmic Bell loss is necessary even at fixed nonzero global error,
for this strong POINTWISE allocation reference contract. Together with the
actual Cycle8 operation this matches the loss order at fixed D and fixed
epsilon. It does not give a sharp constant and does not become a mean-loss
theorem merely by replacing the pointwise w with its expectation.

AUDIT LIMITS / REQUIRED WORDING
1. Gamma and the converter are separable on the actual unassisted input;
   independent entangled auxiliary supply would change the Kraus contraction
   budget and must be charged rather than silently allowed.
2. No K-dependent selector or receiver access enters the physical converter.
   K occurs only in the final mathematical JOINT success test.
3. Public T and discarded apparatus may be refined into Kraus labels, but
   averaging all labels is essential; branch conditioning is not free.
4. The actual tail must be full product nu, giving t^(-2). Substituting a
   classical-correlated chi tail changes the calculation and invalidates this
   exact matched source bound.
5. The target needs nstar<=K and nstar>=K-w on each reference branch.
6. The coarse J measurement preserves the first J-1 Bell pairs; a fine
   computational measurement of all prefix coordinates would be invalid.
7. No source return, catalytic receiver reset, physical implementation benefit
   or historical novelty follows from the analytic result alone.
