BOUNDED LOCC CHALLENGE: AN INFORMATION / ENTANGLEMENT INVARIANT
22 October 2026. Internal proof candidate; independent audit required.

QUESTION AND SCOPE
The fixed-nonzero-error question remains unresolved here. No constant-loss
receiver has been constructed. No Omega(log L) Bell-loss law at fixed error
has been proved. The result below is stronger in the small-global-error
regime and identifies exactly the error bridge that fails at fixed error.

All logs are natural. h(x)=-x ln x-(1-x)ln(1-x), and
g(u)=(u+1)ln(u+1)-u ln u for u>=0, with the continuous conventions.
m=ln D. Source K is uniform on 1,...,L and the actual accessible state is
sigma_k=Phi_D^k tensor nu^(L-k), nu=(I_D/D) tensor (I_D/D).
The hidden retained current clocks and memory are unchanged and inaccessible.
Only finite-round finite-outcome LOCC on the external outputs and independent
local apparatus is allowed. Every public message, including timing/null/stop
information, is part of the complete public transcript T. Private apparatus
may be discarded. There is no extra entanglement or initial correlated access.

THE ENTROPIC NECESSARY CONDITION
Suppose the actual joint (K,T,external-output) state is within GLOBAL half
trace epsilon, 0<=epsilon<=1/2, of a reference with the SAME joint law p(k,t)
and conditional external state
       Phi_D^{n(k,t)} tensor |00><00|^{L-n(k,t)},
where max(1,k-w)<=n(k,t)<=k on every reference branch. Then

 w m + epsilon L m + g(w+1/(D-1)) + 2h(epsilon)
                         >= (1-epsilon) ln L.                 (A)

This allows arbitrary interactive LOCC, not only parity-query receivers.
There is no claim that the reference support is the actual support, and the
formation cost of the reference is never substituted into an actual witness.

CONSEQUENCE
At epsilon=1/L and fixed D,
       w >= [ln L-ln ln L-O_D(1)]/ln D.                        (B)
More generally epsilon=o(ln L/L) implies w>=(1-o(1))ln L/ln D.
The actual Cycle8 receiver's O(log L) loss at epsilon=1/L is thus the right
order if (A)'s invariant passes the independent audit. Its constants are not
optimized. At fixed epsilon>0 the epsilon L term dominates, so (A) DOES NOT
resolve whether constant-in-L loss is possible. No asymptotic assertion at
fixed epsilon follows from this proof.

1. A LOCAL-PURIFICATION INFORMATION INVARIANT

For a general classical label K and an accessible ensemble rho_k on A,B,
choose a pure state |psi_k> on (A,E_A),(B,E_B) reducing to rho_k. E_A,E_B are
inaccessible mathematical local purifiers. They are NOT supplied to the
receiving laboratories and are NEVER put into the information quantity.
Let E_0=sum_k p_k S(psi_{k,B E_B}). Assume finite dimensions here.
Include every actual local apparatus register in A or B, but not E_A,E_B.
For the actual evolving accessible state and public transcript T define

 F = I(K; A T) + I(K; B T) - I(K;T).                            (C)

Let E be the average entropy of entanglement of a purification conditional
on K and T. Purify every local channel by keeping its environment on that
side of this mathematical cut. A public classical message is treated as a
measurement outcome copied to the two sides, and is conditioned upon. Thus
the conditioned global state remains pure. The added environments are not
physically available; they serve only in E. Initially and after each public
message this construction is well defined, including arbitrary discarded
work, private measurements and fresh local randomness.

Claim: E+F cannot increase under actual finite-round LOCC.

Proof for an A-to-B public classical message X, conditional on the previous
transcript T. The sender's actual map A -> A' X is a channel. Hence
     I(K;A' X T) <= I(K;A T).
The receiver's pre-message actual register B is unchanged on average. The
chain rule therefore gives
 F_after-F_before
   <= I(K;X | B T)-I(K;X | T)
    = I(X;B | K T)-I(X;B | T)
   <= I(X;B | K T).                                            (D)
For every fixed K,T the before state on the two purified sides is pure.
Entropy concavity gives the exact identity for the sender's outcome X,
     E_before-E_after = I(X; B E_B' | K T),                     (E)
where E_B' includes the receiver's inaccessible initial purifier and local
discard environments. Data processing on B E_B' -> B bounds (D) by (E).
The same argument holds with Alice and Bob exchanged. Between public
messages actual local channels can only decrease F by data processing;
purifying them locally leaves E unchanged. Induction proves the claim.

Important access distinction: the marginal on B E_B can depend on K at
initialization even when the actual B marginal does not. We do not assume
otherwise. Equation (C) uses ONLY actual A,B. The purifier occurs only in
the CONDITIONAL-K identity (E), followed by data processing to actual B.
Putting the purifier in (C) would silently grant initial inventory access and
would invalidate the stated zero initial-information budget.

For the queue choose |psi_k> to be k Bell pairs and independent local
purifications of each of the 2(L-k) maximally mixed tail registers, with unused
purifier registers padded pure. E_0=E[K]m=B=(L+1)m/2. Each actual local input
and independent local apparatus marginal is K-independent, so F_0=0. Thus
              F_final + E_final <= B.                         (F)
Discarding mathematical purifiers locally at fixed k,t gives
 E_final >= sum_(k,t) p(k,t) E_F(rho_{external|k,t}).             (G)
Also F_final>=I(K;A T) and F_final>=I(K;B T), since each local information
contains I(K;T). No assumption of globally accessible private records is used.

2. TARGET ALLOCATION FORCES ACTUAL LOCAL INVENTORY INFORMATION

On the reference, Alice measures her external data in the computational
basis and defines ell to be the last nonzero digit's index (ell=0 if all
digits are zero). Conditional on n, her first n digits are uniform iid D-ary
digits and the rest are zero. Consequently ell<=n<=K and
          E[K-ell] <= w + 1/(D-1) = u.
Indeed n-ell is the truncated trailing-zero count, whose mean is no larger
than sum_(j>=1) D^{-j}=1/(D-1). Let G=K-ell. Given ell,T, K is determined by
G, so
 H(K|ell,T)<=H(G)<=g(EG)<=g(u).
The entropy bound H(G)<=g(EG) follows by comparison with the geometric law
of mean EG. It covers correlated n(K,T), not just deterministic n(K).
Thus reference I(K;ell,T)>=ln L-g(u).

Measuring the actual state gives total variation delta<=epsilon from that
same reference law. For classical K with L values, arbitrary classical Y,
     |H_P(K|Y)-H_Q(K|Y)|<=delta ln L+h(delta).
A proof: split P,Q into their common component of weight 1-delta and their
two disjoint remainders of weight delta; introduce the component flag. The
conditional entropy's deviation from the weighted conditional entropies is
between 0 and h(delta). Subtract and bound each remainder entropy by ln L.
Because epsilon<=1/2, replace delta by epsilon. Actual K remains uniform,
and data processing gives
 I_actual(K;A T) >= (1-epsilon)ln L-g(u)-h(epsilon).              (H)
The computational measurement here is solely an information lower bound;
the actual receiving protocol need not perform it and is not charged its
entanglement destruction. It is a measurement of actual accessible outputs.

3. GLOBAL TRACE ERROR'S ENTANGLEMENT BRIDGE

Since K,T are classical and the actual and reference have the same p(k,t),
their half trace error is sum p(k,t) e_(k,t), where e_(k,t) is the conditional
external half trace distance. On a branch mathematically discard all output
slots after n=n(k,t). This is an analytical monotonicity step at fixed k,t,
not a receiver with hidden K access. The remaining state is within e=e_(k,t)
of Phi_d, d=D^n. Its Bell fidelity f is at least 1-e.

For every separable state the Bell overlap is at most 1/d. Binary measurement
{Phi_d,I-Phi_d} and relative-entropy data processing imply
 E_F(rho)>=E_R(rho)>=d_bin(f || 1/d), when f>=1/d.
In particular, for ALL e in [0,1],
              E_F(rho)>=(1-e)ln d-h(e).                        (I)
If 1-e>=1/d, binary divergence is increasing above 1/d and is bounded below
by (1-e)ln d-h(e). If 1-e<1/d, the right side is nonpositive because
h(x)>=x ln(1/x)>=x ln d for x=1-e<=1/d, and E_F>=0 suffices.

Equations (G),(I), n<=L, entropy concavity and sum p e<=epsilon give
 E_final >= E[n]m-epsilon Lm-h(epsilon)
          >= B-wm-epsilon Lm-h(epsilon).                       (J)
For epsilon<=1/2, Jensen followed by monotonicity handles h(sum p e).

Finally combine (F), F_final>=I(K;A T), (H), and (J) to obtain (A).
At epsilon=1/L use g(u)<=ln(u+1)+1; either w already exceeds a constant
multiple of ln L, or g(w+1/(D-1))<=ln ln L+O_D(1), proving (B).

4. WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE

The factor epsilon L is the allowed concentration of all global bad-branch
mass on outputs with entanglement of order L. It is not a new resource
assumption or an experimental-access claim. A fixed-error Omega(log L) law
would require a smooth/high-fidelity refinement of this information-loss
argument, a different structural obstruction, or a direct impossibility
proof. Dropping epsilon L is unjustified. Neither entropy continuity nor
the known chi-tail E_R=B-ln L shortcut supplies that step for the actual
nu-tail source.

Many parity queries need not destroy one independent Bell degree each:
their restrictions to the true prefix can have low rank. Thus communication
Omega(log L) is not by itself a Bell-loss Omega(log L) proof. In particular,
queries all to the right of the true boundary can have identical prefix
restriction. An argument based only on query count or classical noisy-search
lower bounds would assume the conclusion for general coherent LOCC.

Known machinery / novelty boundary: information versus remaining-entanglement
complementarity is established research territory. Ghosh et al.,
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0403134 (v1, 2004), explicitly formulate such a
tradeoff and prove a one-way inequality in their equations (2),(3). Badziag
et al., https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0304040 (2003), give a universal locally
accessible-information bound. The invariant above is proved directly for the
actual mixed queue and arbitrary finite-round LOCC with inaccessible local
purifiers kept separate from actual information. No historical-novelty claim
is made. Parent should audit the invariant and compare its scope before
using the source-specific corollary as new scientific capital.

No numerical experiment, physical implementation or total-cost advantage is
claimed. This is a bounded analytic obligation, not a standalone owner return.
