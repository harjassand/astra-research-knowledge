STRONGEST MATCHED ORDINARY CONTROL AND PRECISE CONSEQUENCE

The ordinary control receives the IDENTICAL actual queue, old-memory reference,
global error, per-queue output-register allocation, initial access, total aid,
control freedom and disposal rights. It may use arbitrary collective LOCC;
our lower bounds even grant all SEP or all global channels where stated.
Source-internal Bell pairs may be shifted/consumed/used as aids by a collective
protocol: the collective product-Kraus bound already includes those choices.
No source-internal resource is incorrectly required to be independent per task.

Zero additional entanglement/access: the existing admissible Cycle8 receiver
provides the upper. The new LOWER bounds prove the same asymptotic pointwise
loss/error order at fixed D:
 w_*+1=Theta_D(min{L,1+lnL+ln(r/epsilon)}),0<epsilon<=1/8.
Fixed-error logL loss is necessary under SEP; log(r/epsilon) loss is necessary
even for arbitrary collective quantum channels. At fixed L every fixed useful
w<L-1 drives global error to1 as r grows. This is stronger than saying the
independent epsilon/r implementation becomes vacuous.

Ordinary stock can run the identical Cycle8 upper and is subject to the identical
new laws. Original expected preselected source supply is r(L+1)lnD/2; deterministic
exact supply is rLlnD. No formation, preparation or total-computation advantage
over that equally informed ordinary stock is claimed.

Supplied inventory access can remove the obstruction: knowing each K permits
exact keep-K/reset-tail. It is an ACTUAL access resource, not the mathematical
mixture label. C9 prices one-shot Bell-retention access with max-likelihood
factor A; C8 separately prices communication using actual mutual information.
Those two quantities answer different questions and cannot be substituted.

Paid entangled aid can also replace ORIGINAL-source Bell loss. The explicit
nonlocal-difference LOCC receiver uses Q=O(logL+log1/epsilon) consumable Phi_D
aids and an explicitly charged Q(L-1)-symbol shared/communicated seed, retaining
source loss O(log_D1/epsilon). At fixed error this is constant source loss,
but its added total initial Bell supply is logarithmic in L. Equations(9),(13)
and(18) give necessary aid/access/supply tradeoffs. No catalyst is presumed to
return; any demanded complete joint return must be included and its total
initial preparation cost remains charged. Optimal finite tradeoff constants,
general catalytic advantages and physical usefulness are not established.

The unrestricted global Bell-test comparator achieves error<=r D^-2(w+1)
without logL source loss, showing that the separable-operation restriction
is substantive. Its Bell tests are not free LOCC. The explicit expensive
teleport realization or more economical paid parity construction is priced;
neither is inserted into the zero-aid benchmark as a free operation.

Pooled/encoded Bell outputs may evade the fixed per-queue profile obstruction,
but need a DIFFERENT declared output interface and an actual joint-memory
rate/decoupling proof. No pooled success is reported as a solution of this task.
