# Strong ordinary comparison

The exact target/proposal information contract is shared by the hybrid and
ordinary proposal-start independence MH. The preserved ordinary minimax law
has amplitude K at scale1/log n. The new theorem therefore quantifies how an
added screened local mechanism can worsen robustness. It gives no smaller
general error or practical speedup.

On the SAME new transport witnesses, let q_i=bz^i,
pi_i=p_T n^-2(J-i),p_T=(log n)^-4, and let the core complete the probabilities.
The exact original-chain bound is

    TV(q G^j,pi) <= p_T/(1-n^-2)
                   + bz/[(1-b)(1-z)] + b^(j+1).

It follows by first reaching the core and contracting the remaining original
G evolution; it includes returns. Since V=-log z divided by loglog n tends
to infinity, this is O((log n)^-4) at j/n->t>0, including
j=floor(alpha n/2). In transport-dominated cases the hybrid has a much slower
logarithmic lower. The comparison is adverse to the hybrid and is retained.

The frozen exact fixtures also compute G at n and at floor(alpha n/2),
with exact full laws and the bound above. These finite values are diagnostics,
not estimates of the asymptotic rate or wall-time benchmarks. The coalescence
fixture fails K=1 admission and cannot be used for an admitted comparison.

Direct sampling of the analytically disclosed target remains allowed. A
recipient with its complete table may enumerate and sample it. We do not
hide those methods to create an apparent use for the local screen.

For the three same-v policies at K=1,alpha=1/2, the risk exponents are
approximately0.64842,0.69315 and0.31326. Pure IMH has exponent1. Those three
hybrid values compare suprema under explicit policies; they do not establish
a fixed-instance ordering, a target-call gain or a recommended implementation.
