## Primary literature checked at this checkpoint

Chris Sherlock and Anthony Lee, *Variance bounding of delayed-acceptance
kernels*, arXiv:1706.02142v4, Corollary2 and Example3:
https://arxiv.org/html/1706.02142v4

They establish inheritance of variance bounding under bounded surrogate
ratios and exhibit harmful delayed-acceptance modification of an independence
sampler. This is prior context for robustness and degradation, not the new
moving-horizon minimax rate under one KL and a public reciprocal-proposal
budget. The factorized acceptance primitive is not claimed as new.

Christophe Andrieu, Anthony Lee, Sam Power and Andi Q. Wang,
*Comparison of Markov chains via weak Poincare inequalities with application
to pseudo-marginal MCMC*, Annals of Statistics50(2022),3592–3618,
DOI10.1214/22-AOS2241, Lemma25 and Proposition27:
https://awllee.github.io/papers/AOS2241.pdf

These give the ordinary IMH Dirichlet representation and weight-tail weak
Poincare control. They are primary context for the inherited smoothing
operation. The new result concerns reachable transport, actual global exits
and sharp proposal-start TV rates over the specified growing finite class.
