ASTRA CYCLE9 — REPRESENTATION EXPERIMENT RESULT
22 October 2026

RESULT

A proved representation and stronger simulation order substantially reduced
the computational cost of Pro2's already completed constructive closure
search, even after the strong exact-count control received the same bounded
caches and the same antichain algorithm. All three arms closed. Therefore this
experiment does NOT establish that the abstraction was necessary for feasibility
under the prespecified budget, or that it made a previously inaccessible theorem
possible. It establishes a substantial cost difference for this research
operation, with the qualifications below.

WHAT THE INTERMEDIATE ARM CHANGES

A to B changes exact neighbor multiplicities into occupancy plus actual total
degree, while holding the dominance order to flags at fixed supports. In these
implementations it reduces observed CPU by a factor of 2.807, checked transitions
by a factor of 1.238, and active family-profile entries by a factor of 1.517.
This is a representation-package comparison: state layout, canonical ordering,
primitive transitions and resulting schedule also differ. It is not a pure
mathematical-quotient intervention with all CPU instructions fixed.

B to C changes only the permitted dominance relation within the same occupancy
backend and common engine: smaller support masks can now simulate larger masks,
as well as smaller flags. Observed CPU falls by a factor of 6.870, checked
transitions by 3.965, and active profile entries by 6.732. This comparison more
closely isolates the scientific value of the stronger proved order, though the
resulting work order and cache contents necessarily respond to that order.

A to C gives 19.284 times lower observed CPU, 4.909 times fewer checked
transitions, and 3.333 times lower peak RSS. The independent discrete reductions
make it implausible that ordinary cache repair alone explains the advantage:
the same family-cache bounds and common antichain routine were already present
in A. Conversely, this does not attribute the entire historical improvement
from the old unbounded-cache attempt to scientific abstraction. The strong
bounded-cache control succeeds here, using about 102 MB rather than gigabytes.

SCIENTIFIC SEMANTICS

The preserved source proof establishes a four-color star-coloring interface
for nonadjacent terminals and disjoint child interiors. Exact source state
records terminal colors, per-color neighbor counts, and far-end hazard flags.
A saturated degree-four port is projected only because no later admitted
operation can touch it; the creating merge is checked before projection.

The occupancy quotient retains actual degree and support. In valid children,
an internal repeated-neighbor-color obstruction can newly appear only when
both child supports contain that color. Consequently an admissible merge is
determined by d1+d2<=4 and N1 & N2 & (F1|F2)==0. Actual multiplicities remain in
the realizing coloring. The proved support/flag order permits smaller masks
as witnesses for larger target bounds at fixed actual degree and terminal
colors. This is an existence-preserving simulation guarantee, not permission
to discard degree, choose independent endpoint recolorings, or claim arbitrary
graph classes. Arm B uses the weaker valid fixed-support flag order.

The common reducer groups by the arm's exact base, sorts masks by popcount,
and keeps a mask iff no previous mask is its submask. Every strict submask has
smaller popcount, so the procedure computes the exact minimal antichain for
that order. It removes the former quadratic-versus-mask-reduction confound.

Independent focused adapter checks passed before the timed searches. Each arm
used 65 actual path2/L/R/S/P fixtures through two fixed layers, including SAT4;
1,223 same-degree coverage pairs; idempotence; and 24 global recolorings.
A and C agreed with preserved original reducer bodies and an independent brute
relation. B agreed with its explicit fixed-support brute relation. Assertions
were 4,332 / 3,044 / 4,332. These checks support the new adapter. They are not
universal mathematical proofs or fresh complete literal-witness certification.

WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES FOR META-RESEARCH

For this completed Pro2 research operation, a scientific proof specifying what
information future operations actually need, together with a proved simulation
order, yields a material computational advantage after standard engineering
improvements are shared with the control. The intermediate arm shows that
permitting safe substitution of weaker obligations matters more than merely
encoding the same obligations compactly.

This supports investigating scientific representations that remove unnecessary
distinctions while preserving constructive obligations. It does not justify a
mandatory reasoning script, replacement of autonomous research, or an assertion
that Astra can routinely invent those representations on demand. Pro2 already
invented the representation; the experiment measures its computational effect.

The hypothesis that the abstraction is required for closure at this budget is
falsified: A also closes. The hypothesis that the measured historical difference
is only the old cache defect is contradicted by the controlled A/B/C result.
The stronger claim that representation invention improves total discovery cost
or future independent discoveries remains untested.

LIMITATIONS AND COST

There is one selected completed task and one sample per condition. Historical
search outcomes were known when the experiment was designed. No general timing
distribution, blind discovery success rate, cross-domain transfer, historical
novelty, or recursively improving research intelligence follows. The active
profile counts are obligations under different proved semantics, not identically
meaningful raw scientific states. No resource stop occurred; none is treated as
a mathematical obstruction.

The three timed processes used 210.162818 summed CPU seconds and 210.375627 summed
wall seconds. Separate adapter checks reported 12.711914 CPU seconds. Source
reading, proof development, model inference, experimental development and
integration costs are not fully measured. No net programme-level cost advantage
is claimed. Original Pro2 source and its ongoing mission remain untouched.
