## 2. A positive force can tolerate rapid, noncommuting bath motion

Let \(D\) contain the accessible logical operands, \(M\) the retained medium, and \(R\) an arbitrary reference. Introduce a meter with position \(x\) and momentum \(p\), with \(\hbar=1\).

For a specified logical Hermitian involution \(Q\), suppose one native interrogation has Hamiltonian

\[
\boxed{
H_Q(t)
=
I_D\otimes H_M(t)
+\frac{p^2}{2m}
-Q\otimes G_M(t)\otimes x .
}
\]

The essential force qualification is

\[
g_-(t)I_M\le G_M(t)\le g_+(t)I_M.
\]

The principal case has constant bounds \(0<g_-\le g_+\). The operator \(G_M(t)\) can be unknown. It need not commute with \(H_M(t)\), or with its own values at other times.

At the start, the meter momentum is supported in

\[
[-p_0,p_0].
\]

This support qualification is an actual apparatus resource. The meter need not be pure, and the theorem permits correlations with retained systems.

The core proof covers arbitrarily large finite-dimensional media with the stated regularity and propagator assumptions. Its bounds do not grow with the medium’s dimension. Infinite-dimensional extensions require the corresponding domain and propagation conditions.

### The support theorem

Write

\[
Q=\Pi_+-\Pi_-,
\qquad
\Pi_q=\frac{I+qQ}{2},
\]

and define the lower and upper impulses

\[
\Gamma_-=\int_0^T g_-(t)\,dt,
\qquad
\Gamma_+=\int_0^T g_+(t)\,dt.
\]

At time \(T\), the two logical branches have momentum supports contained in

\[
\begin{aligned}
Q=+1:\quad&
[-p_0+\Gamma_-,\;p_0+\Gamma_+],\\[2mm]
Q=-1:\quad&
[-p_0-\Gamma_+,\;p_0-\Gamma_-].
\end{aligned}
\]

Consequently, if the readout has a separating margin \(r\), then

\[
\boxed{\Gamma_->p_0+r}
\]

is sufficient for an exact measurement of \(Q\) in the ideal model.

For constant bounds, a sufficient duration is

\[
T>\frac{p_0+r}{g_-}.
\]

There is no condition requiring \(\|H_M\|T\) or \(\|[H_M,G_M]\|T^2\) to be small.

### Why this remains true when the bath moves rapidly

The proof follows momentum support through short-time factors.

Evolution under \(H_M\) mixes internal memory components at each momentum without moving their momentum support. Evolution under \(p^2/(2m)\) changes momentum-dependent phases without moving support.

For a force step, resolve \(G_M\) spectrally. On an eigenvalue \(\gamma\),

\[
e^{+iqxG_M\,dt}\psi(p)
=
\psi(p-q\gamma\,dt).
\]

Every component therefore moves by an amount between \(g_-dt\) and \(g_+dt\) on the positive branch. Internal mixing cannot create amplitude outside the union of those translated intervals. The negative branch has the opposite bounds. Induction and the strong product-formula limit give the result.

**Those factors are a proof device, not a pulse program.** The physical operation uses one native force handle while the bath and meter evolve forward simultaneously.

This also explains the relation to the earlier local-orbit machinery. Rapid unitary rotation of \(G_M\) preserves its spectral bounds. The proof therefore needs no frozen-orbit approximation. The earlier local-regularity bounds remain valid and useful for their original controls; they are not being replaced by an unjustified stronger estimate.

The condition has some tolerance beyond the symmetric model. An additional unknown common force \(F_M\) is harmless if

\[
\|F_M(t)\|\le f(t)<g_-(t).
\]

The separating lower bound becomes \(g_-(t)-f(t)\). Neither \(F_M\) nor \(G_M\) has to commute with the bath. More generally, several measured eigenspaces can be assigned different force operators whenever their resulting momentum intervals are disjoint.

## 3. The full measurement action is what makes this computationally useful

Correct probabilities are insufficient. We need the measurement to preserve all logical coherence that the intended measurement should preserve.

Because \(Q\) is conserved during the interrogation,

\[
U_Q(T)=\Pi_+\otimes U_+(T)+\Pi_-\otimes U_-(T).
\]

Let \(P_B\) project onto the admitted initial meter band, and let \(R_q\) denote the final sign-readout projector. The support theorem gives

\[
R_qU_qP_B=U_qP_B,
\qquad
R_{-q}U_qP_B=0.
\]

Hence each measurement branch has the form

\[
\boxed{
K_q=\Pi_q\otimes V_q,
\qquad
V_q^\dagger V_q=I
}
\]

on the admitted source domain.

This says more than “the recorded bit is correct.” Every logical vector with the same value of \(q\) experiences the **same memory isometry**. The medium may retain information about the measured outcome. It acquires no additional information distinguishing logical vectors inside that outcome’s eigenspace.

The identity holds for arbitrary initial logical–memory–reference correlations.

### The failure that distinguishes this from scalar effects alone

For an interrogation written as

\[
W=\sum_x |x\rangle K_x,
\]

memory-independent outcome probabilities require scalar diagonal operators \(K_x^\dagger K_x\). A memory-independent coherent meter state requires scalar **cross operators** \(K_y^\dagger K_x\) as well.

For example,

\[
K_0=\sqrt p\,I,
\qquad
K_1=\sqrt{1-p}\,Z
\]

has scalar effects but a memory-dependent cross operator. A classical outcome identity does not automatically become a coherent phase-estimation oracle.

There is an especially important physical counterexample here. Suppose the intended measurement is parity,

\[
Q=Z_1Z_2,
\]

but the force coefficient secretly depends on a logical bit:

\[
H_{\rm force}
=
-x\,Z_1Z_2\bigl(I+\lambda Z_1\bigr),
\qquad 0<\lambda<1.
\]

Every force eigenvalue is positive. Both \(|00\rangle\) and \(|11\rangle\) can report even parity perfectly. But their kicks have different magnitudes. Once the packets separate, the meter distinguishes those two states and destroys their coherence.

An initial even Bell state then has fidelity \(1/2\) after the meter is ignored, despite perfect reported parity. The runnable counterexample reproduces this.

That is why the coefficient must be \(G_M\): it must act identically on the logical vectors inside each measured sector. **A qualification of the POVM effects alone cannot establish a useful parity instrument.**

## 4. Four measurements give an exact reusable CNOT

Let \(C,T\) be the payload qubits and \(A\) an ancillary logical qubit. The ancillary qubit may initially be dirty and arbitrarily correlated with \(C,T,M,R\).

Perform these measurements chronologically:

\[
\boxed{
X_A,\qquad Z_CZ_A,\qquad X_AX_T,\qquad Z_A .
}
\]

Record bits \(a,b,c,d\), where an outcome bit denotes eigenvalue \((-1)^{\rm bit}\). Update the logical Pauli frame by

\[
F_{abcd}
=
Z_C^{\,c\oplus a}
X_T^{\,b\oplus d}.
\]

For the product \(L_{abcd}\) of the four logical projectors, direct multiplication gives

\[
\boxed{
F_{abcd}L_{abcd}
=
\frac{s_{abcd}}{\sqrt8}\,
\mathrm{CNOT}_{C,T}
\otimes
|d_z\rangle\langle a_x|_A,
\qquad
s_{abcd}\in\{+1,-1\}.
}
\]

I checked all sixteen identities using exact integer arithmetic, with projector denominators cleared.

Now restore the medium. Each measurement contributes a factor \(\Pi_q\otimes V_q\). The memory factors commute past the logical projectors, although the memory factors need not commute with one another. Therefore every corrected-frame branch becomes

\[
K^{\rm frame}_{abcd}
=
\mathrm{CNOT}_{C,T}\otimes B_{abcd},
\]

where \(B_{abcd}\) includes the dirty-ancilla map, the forward evolution of the medium, the meters, and the records. Completeness gives

\[
\sum_{abcd} B_{abcd}^\dagger B_{abcd}=I.
\]

Thus, **in the recorded logical frame**, the full channel is

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal U_{\mathrm{CNOT}}
\otimes
\mathcal R_{A,M,\mathrm{apparatus}} .
}
\]

This establishes the useful operation on arbitrary correlated inputs. It does not merely average over a convenient bath state.

The first \(X_A\) measurement is a real preparation and entropy-export operation. A prepared pure ancilla has not been supplied for free. The interacting medium \(M\) itself is never prepared.

Parity-based CNOT constructions are established ordinary methods. The point of using one here is that its branch algebra gives a stringent test of whether the proposed uncertain-medium instrument supports computation. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="3"}

### What the frame means physically

The physical output carries a known Pauli byproduct. Subsequent measurement choices and final readout can account for it classically. A standalone output in a prescribed laboratory frame needs an actual correction interface, or an output interface that accepts the frame.

Also, \(\mathcal R\) is a **memory channel**, including measurement records and apparatus disposal or renewal. It need not be a bath unitary \(V_M\). That additional measurement resource is part of the mechanism’s resource account.

## 5. The capability extends beyond an isolated entangler

The same factorization supports gate teleportation through initially dirty resource registers.

For a one-qubit gate \(U\), use two resource qubits \(A,B\). Prepare its Choi resource by measuring the commuting observables

\[
X_A\otimes UX_BU^\dagger,
\qquad
Z_A\otimes UZ_BU^\dagger.
\]

Then Bell-measure the input and \(A\), using \(XX\) and \(ZZ\). The output moves to \(B\). Each recorded branch factors into the desired gate, an outcome-dependent frame, and a map on the initially arbitrary resource registers.

## 6. What survives repeated use and arbitrary memory histories

After one operation, the medium may have changed substantially and become correlated with all preceding records. The next operation does not require estimating that new state.

It requires the same two qualifications to remain true:

1. The next force has the stipulated logical-sector structure and spectral bounds.
2. Its meter is supplied or renewed into the admitted source class.

The instrument identity then applies to every conditional history. Induction through a corrected measurement program gives the desired logical operation tensor a memory channel.

That is the exact ideal-model reuse theorem.

For finite imperfections, suppose the source has a **uniform** momentum-tail bound \(\eta_j\). Let \(\delta_{{\rm read},j}\) bound the readout instrument error, and let \(R_j(t)\) be a Hamiltonian departure from the qualified force model. A sufficient per-measurement bound is

\[
\epsilon_j
\le
\sqrt{2\eta_j}
+
\delta_{{\rm read},j}
+
\int_0^{T_j}\|R_j(t)\|\,dt.
\]

For unbounded departures, the derivation uses the corresponding norm restricted to the actually prepared source subspace.

The readout term must cover unwanted disturbance as well as bit errors. The within-parity counterexample shows why.

For a finite computation,

\[
\frac12
\left\|
\mathcal C_{\rm actual}
-
\mathcal U_{\rm intended}\otimes\mathcal R_M
\right\|_\diamond
\le
\sum_j\epsilon_j
+
\text{holding-error contributions},
\]

with the classical frame incorporated. Holding includes time spent waiting for source preparation, readout, and controller action.

No iid bath assumption enters this bound. It is uniform over retained memory and reference states.

At fixed nonzero component error, this does not prove indefinitely long reliable computation. Fault-tolerant amplification still needs its own local-noise, leakage, source, and scheduling hypotheses. The result removes one coupled obstruction—gain estimation in a moving medium—without claiming that every other physical error disappears.

## 7. Duration, preparation, readout, work, and qualification

### Fine gain calibration disappears after qualification

The protocol does not need an estimate of \(G_M\). It can work even when \(G_M\) is a quantum operator with no useful scalar-gain interpretation.

For example,

\[
G_M=g_0\bigl[I+\alpha\,\mathbf n\cdot\sigma\bigr],
\qquad |\alpha|\le a<1,
\]

has a lower bound \(g_0(1-a)\) without knowing \(\mathbf n\), the memory state, or the bath Hamiltonian.

The identity contribution is an actual native physical contribution. The original traceless \(\mathbf b\cdot\sigma\) handles do not satisfy this positivity condition. I have not derived the force bank from them.

### Interrogation time can depend weakly on the target error

For a Gaussian momentum source with standard deviation \(\sigma_p\),

\[
\eta
=
\operatorname{erfc}\!\left(\frac{p_0}{\sqrt2\,\sigma_p}\right)
\le
e^{-p_0^2/(2\sigma_p^2)}.
\]

To make the support-tail contribution at most \(\epsilon\), it suffices to choose

\[
p_0\ge
\sigma_p\sqrt{2\log(2/\epsilon^2)}
\]

and then

\[
\boxed{
T>
\frac{\sigma_p\sqrt{2\log(2/\epsilon^2)}+r}{g_-}.
}
\]

Thus that contribution requires duration

\[
O\!\left(\sqrt{\log(1/\epsilon)}\right)
\]

at fixed source width and lower force bound.

This is a bound for the qualified force model and source tail. It does not suppress an observable-axis error, an unqualified readout, or a fixed unwanted logical coupling.

There is no precise pulse area to hit. Extra positive impulse preserves the logical measurement, while increasing meter excursion and work. The required stability is preservation of the force bounds and logical-sector structure throughout use.

### Meter renewal remains a physical operation

The dirty-ancilla CNOT uses four force interrogations and four sign readouts. Every interrogation requires an admitted meter source or an actual renewal procedure. The displaced meter and its record must be retained, reset, or discarded through a real physical process.

On the compact-support component,

\[

\]

so the meter kinetic energy is bounded by

\[
E_{\rm kin}\le
\frac{(p_0+g_+T)^2}{2m}.
\]

That is a meter bound, not a complete apparatus-energy estimate. The derivation also gives a finite switching-work bound for a fixed native force when the source has a finite position second moment. Arbitrarily varying source coefficients require an additional variation and work budget.

Finite spatial interaction regions, force curvature, source generation, detector range, and data holding remain part of the physical implementation.

### The coarse promise cannot be inferred from successful shots alone

Consider

\[
G_{\rm good}=\operatorname{diag}(1,1),
\qquad
G_{\rm bad}=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1).
\]

On an initially occupied first memory basis state, both behave identically. A later memory history can populate the second state and reverse the second device’s readout.

A calibration record on an unknown memory state therefore cannot certify the operator inequality over all future histories. The promise needs a structural source qualification or a separately justified state-uniform bound.

That qualification is part of the unresolved physical work.

## 8. Failures retained in the construction

Several failures constrain what can be built from this theorem.

- **An indefinite force can measure the wrong joint observable.** With \(G_M=Z_M\), the sign meter measures \(QZ_M\), allowing the dirty memory to reverse the logical outcome.
- **A zero-force sector can be dark forever.** If an allowed memory state remains in the kernel of \(G_M\), no finite uniform interrogation time works.
- **Correct parity effects can coexist with destructive logical disturbance.** The positive logical-dependent force above destroys an even Bell state while reporting parity correctly.
- **A trapped pointer can return to its starting momentum.** For a harmonic restoring force, even a constant scalar drive gives a kick proportional to \(\sin(\omega T)\). The stipulated meter dynamics matter.
- **A fixed unwanted logical coupling leaves an exposure error.** More shots do not remove it.

These are explicit counterexamples and model boundaries, rather than qualifications attached after a favorable simulation.
