# What actually controls connector stability: killed occupation, not mixing
Status: new unreviewed synthesis of local exact identities. This does not enlarge either frozen theorem or establish general weakly reversible recurrence.

For one conservative fast excursion, stop at the first clearing, activation, or immigration. Let

    T_x=E_x tau,   U_x=E_x integral_0^tau A_t dt.

At fixed B the actual event identities are

    P_x(activation first)=k_T B U_x,
    P_x(immigration first)=k_I T_x.

The clearing compensator gives, for the physical post-clearing A count,

    E_x[(1+A_after)^-m; clearing first]
       <=C(B)_q T_x.

These identities identify two different quantities. The dangerous low-B upward escape is controlled by the inverse-moment RESET LAW, hence by T_x. The favorable extra-activation opportunity is controlled by U_x. Replacing both by a frozen instantaneous A or by a presumed stationary mean discards the crucial comparison.

At the fixed section B=q, the direct relative error is therefore controlled by T_x/U_x. The strong absolute mean used in the frozen family is one sufficient way to make that ratio small, because pointwise hazard comparison gives U_x>=cN^(-(m-1)). It is not logically necessary: an autocatalytic fast module can have T_x of order logN/N^(m-1), yet T_x/U_x decays as a positive power because the occupation measure moves to growing A before clearing.

The first reset must remain distinguished from subsequent worst-case resets. If its inverse-moment bound is t_x<=CT_x and later reset bounds are p_0(N), the actual finite walk gives

    upward/bad escape
      <=Ct_x+C sum_(ell=1)^(m-1)t_x^(ell/m)p_0^((m+1-ell)/m).

Suppose T_x/U_x<=e_N uniformly, U_x>=u_0=cN^(-(m-1)), and p_0 bounds all later reset laws. Dividing by the actual activation opportunity bounds the relative error by

    C e_N
    +C sum_(ell=1)^(m-1) e_N^(ell/m)
         [p_0/u_0]^(1-ell/m) p_0^(1/m).                  (G)

Thus a verifiable power decay of (G), rather than an absolute fast mixing or absorption exponent, is the relevant local connector condition. It is earned in the pure-source family from strict-power accessibility, and is a candidate in the A-catalytic family from logarithmic drift plus mesoscopic occupation.

The formula is not a global theorem by itself. It still requires actual active/inactive seeding treatment, weighted repair of every bad branch, and a complementary unbounded-direction certificate. Those are precisely the obligations addressed in the associated constructions; they cannot be replaced by assuming a global Lyapunov function.
