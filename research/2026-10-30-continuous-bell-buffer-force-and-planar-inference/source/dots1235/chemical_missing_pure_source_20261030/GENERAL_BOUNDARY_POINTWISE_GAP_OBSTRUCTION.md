# A structural obstruction to making the new relative-gap premise universal
Status: personal analytic falsification; not a new recurrence theorem. Prepared in response to the instruction to reassess the larger target rather than automatically add parameter families.

## The proposed universalization to test
The reviewed siphon theorem controls dangerous immigration against corrective activation through a pointwise incoming-state ratio T/U->0, where T is stopped time and U is A-occupation. Could weak reversibility of a conservative fast graph itself force this ratio to vanish uniformly?

No. It already fails on a positive-deficiency, non-complex-balanced, homogeneous fast family. Therefore a general boundary-recurrence principle must carry a composition-dependent section value or a vector of occupation costs; it cannot simply assume the reviewed scalar ratio is always available.

## Explicit witness
Take composition species A,C, degree m=5, common catalyst qB with any q>5, and fast chain

    3A+2C <-> 4A+C <-> 5A.

Use forward/backward coefficients (2,1) and (1,3), and the same connector0->A+B->5A+qB->0. Work at fixed B=q; absorb its positive catalyst factor into constants. Start at A=3,C=N-3 and stop at the first clearing, immigration or activation. Let T_N and U_N be its time and A-occupation.

The only enabled fast source at A=3 is3A+2C, whose total rate is Theta(N^2). Clearing is disabled, and the competing connector rates are O(1). The first full-state holding time therefore gives

    T_N >=cN^-2.                                     (1)

## Matching occupation upper bound
Set F(A)=C/[A N^2]+C'/N^4 on the conserved fast shell. For A<=epsilon N, source3A+2C supplies A-producing rate >=c A^3N^2. Its negative 1/A increment has size >=c'/A^2, so Q(1/A)<=-c A N^2 after absorbing A-decreasing contributions, which have one additional factor A/N. Hence QF<=-cC A in this region.

For A>=epsilon N, |Q(1/A)|<=CN^3, so the possible positive QF is O(N). The clearing killing term acting on C/(A N^2) is at least cN^2, and therefore dominates both that positive term and the desired cost A<=N. The added positive N^-4 term is harmless and can be used for fixed threshold adjustment. Bounded competing killing only helps. Choosing constants and large N gives

    (Q-k_clear-k_competitors)F <=-A.

Finite-shell stopped Dynkin yields U_N<=F(3)<=CN^-2. Combining with (1),

    T_N/U_N >=c_0>0.                                (2)

Thus no uniform vanishing relative time/occupation bound is possible, even logarithmically. This is an analytic first-holding/supersolution obstruction, not a numerical failure.

## This is not a transience example
The full graph has five complexes, one linkage class and rank3, so deficiency1. Endpoint/interior complex-balance equations would require A/C=2 and A/C=1/3 simultaneously; the connector attaches only at5A+qB and cannot fix this. It is not automatically a deficiency-zero/product-form fixture. Nevertheless (2) says nothing about recurrence. It only falsifies a universal pointwise scalar gap.

## Consequential next obligation
A low-A incoming section state can spend its whole short dangerous interval at A=O(1), so danger and correction occur on the same scale. The vast majority of fast clearings, however, may deliver a different A distribution. A globally negative section drift may therefore exist after assigning a nonconstant value to incoming composition, even when the constant-in-composition section weight has no vanishing error ratio.

The next general construction should solve or dominate that actual boundary return operator with a composition-dependent potential, using graph-derived incoming/outgoing moment transport. It must not assume the scalar T/U gap, a supplied stationary phase law, or a finite cap. The incoming-moment lemma in this directory is relevant capital; it is not yet the solution.
