# Inclusive physical fault paths without CP hybrid generators
2026-10-30. New exact norm lemma; independent review pending.

## Result

Let L_actual(u)=L0(u)+sum_e N_e(u) generate normal joint CPTP propagators on every interval of a finite time horizon [0,T], with the usual bounded-perturbation/Duhamel regularity. The perturbations may be HP/non-CP and share an arbitrary retained bath. No decomposition into physical noisy jumps is required.

Assign perturbation labels/times to disjoint BOX LABELS B_i (their physical supports may overlap). Let N_i(u) be the sum assigned to box i and

    nu_i = integral_0^T ||N_i(u)||_cb du < infinity.

For a prescribed finite set F of m boxes, hold all other perturbations at their actual values. Let U_x(T,0) be the propagator generated by

    L_x(u) = L_actual(u) - sum_(i in F) (1-x_i) N_i(u),
    x in [0,1]^F.

The hybrid propagator U_x need not be positive. Define the mixed finite difference

    E_F = sum_(A subset F) (-1)^(m-|A|) U_(1_A)(T,0).

Then

    ||E_F||_diamond <= product_(i in F) (exp(nu_i)-1).    (1)

For uniformly bounded-volume/time boxes with nu_i<=C eta Delta, this is the desired physical-box local fault-path bound with strength exp(C eta Delta)-1, independent of total system size, total elapsed time outside these boxes, bath dimension and the number of unmarked faults. It does not yet convert physical boxes into the QCA's completed virtual update locations.

## Proof

1. Write L_x=L_actual-D_x, where D_x=sum_i(1-x_i)N_i. Variation of constants around the ACTUAL CPTP propagator and Gronwall give, on every interval [a,b],

    ||U_x(b,a)||_diamond
       <= exp[integral_a^b sum_i(1-x_i)||N_i(u)||_cb du]. (2)

Only the selected boxes occur in this exponent. Positivity of U_x is not asserted or needed. All unrestricted noise remains inside the actual CPTP comparison propagator.

2. Differentiate once with respect to each x_i. The mixed derivative of U_x is the sum over the m! temporal orderings of a time-ordered product with one N_i insertion for each selected box and U_x propagators between them. On each product, the intervening intervals partition [0,T]. Multiplying (2) across those intervals costs ONLY

    exp[sum_i(1-x_i)nu_i],

not its (m+1)-st power. The ordered integration simplices and permutations partition the full m-fold time cube (coincident times have measure zero). Hence

    ||partial_F U_x||_diamond
       <= exp[sum_i(1-x_i)nu_i] product_i nu_i.          (3)

The maps need not commute. Different support boxes may share system sites and bath factors.

3. Repeated fundamental theorem of calculus gives

    E_F = integral_[0,1]^F partial_F U_x dx.

Integrating (3) separately in each x_i yields

    product_i [nu_i integral_0^1 exp((1-x_i)nu_i) dx_i]
       = product_i (exp(nu_i)-1),

with the zero-nu case understood continuously. This proves (1).

## Fault-path interpretation

For finite N and T the bounded perturbation expansion is norm-convergent. Inclusion-exclusion cancels every term with zero insertions from at least one selected box; all terms with at least one insertion from EVERY selected box survive, including arbitrarily many insertions from unselected boxes. Thus E_F is an inclusive fault-path sum, not an exactly-m-root estimate. The proof avoids the inadmissible global absolute Dyson factor exp(O(eta N T)).

If only a per-site incident budget sum_(e containing v)||N_e||_cb<=eta is given, assign each system-supported term to one anchor in its support. A box with at most v_0 anchors and duration Delta then has nu_i<=v_0 eta Delta (or the corresponding explicit overlap constant). Bath-only evolution is held fixed in every hybrid and is part of the assumed actual normal propagator; no uncontrolled cancellation of unbounded bath pieces is used. Infinite lattices require an additional existence/local-limit argument; the uniform finite-system estimate is sufficient for finite-size threshold work.

## What this DOES NOT prove

It does not make nominal SUCCESS counters physical under calibration, and it does not condition actual dynamics on such fictitious records. It also does not by itself show that a virtual QCA fault is witnessed by enough physical boxes: finite execution delays, native dirty bursts, and asynchronous clock repair still require a deterministic/witness relation and a jointly summable service-defect bound. The separate nominal burst kernel can be used only on explicitly exposed nominal segments; an unjustified combination would recreate the original gap.

The lemma is a general hybrid/Duhamel estimate, not a novelty claim. Its useful role here is to identify a rigorous inclusive resummation that respects the broad joint-generator noise model.

## Primary comparison checked

The inclusive condition itself is standard: Aliferis–Gottesman–Preskill, [quant-ph/0504218](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0504218), Section 8 / local-noise definition, already sums unrestricted extra faults; Terhal–Burkard, [quant-ph/0402104](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0402104), Appendix A, resum the remaining evolution after a first Hamiltonian fault. The present note is a direct bounded-generator mixed-difference formulation useful for non-CP local perturbation pieces, not a claim to have discovered fault-path resummation.
