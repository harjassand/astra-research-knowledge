The conventional route is strong

The comparison retains exact bucket conditionals and samples in reverse elimination order. It does not rerun a full elimination for each sampled bit.

On a larger 64-factor, 96-variable counting instance, the learned route and elimination again agreed exactly. The executed Pfaffian evaluation took about one second, while min-fill elimination took about a third of a second.

These experiments establish correctness and an unfavorable small-instance performance comparison. They do not establish a practical advantage.

The structural distinction is nevertheless real: the boundary construction has a cost bound independent of treewidth when its acquired face parameter and arithmetic costs remain bounded. The tested meshes were small enough for ordinary elimination to work well.

8. What this adds to the mission

The most consequential result here is the connection between representation acquisition and the future operations that the representation must support.

A small hidden model is not sufficient. One also needs to know what happens when the solver asks questions in the original coordinates.

This construction answers that question in a precise setting:

Local tensor uniqueness yields explicit compatibility invariants.
Those invariants lead to actual compatible bases, rather than supplied coordinates.
Failed direct compatibility identifies fields that may still be handled geometrically.
Original observations become boundary operations.
A constructed observation order keeps those operations tractable.
Signed internal calculations produce exact probabilities and samples only after full reconstruction.

The general boundary theorem reaches beyond the particular ternary Ising acquisition procedure. Any legitimately acquired planar matchgate representation can use the same weighted-parity closure operation, subject to its arithmetic costs. Conversely, the theorem supplies no general recognizer for arbitrary circuits or arbitrary tensor networks.

The ingredients—tensor decomposition, holographic transformations, planar Ising reductions, parity signatures and Pfaffians—are established. The result built here is a complete connection between compatible-basis acquisition, signed readout, exposed-face cost and exact reconstruction of original-variable samples. I have proved and executed that connection; its external novelty and practical application advantage remain unresolved.

The scientific obstruction has moved to a concrete place: finding raw problem collections where compatible signed coordinates and a favorable readout geometry can be acquired cheaply enough to outperform a strong compiled conventional solver. The present work supplies a usable theorem and executable mechanism for that question, without assuming that a tiny final representation has already paid for its discovery or its consumers.
