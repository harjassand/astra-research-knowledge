# Source inspection receipts

Inspected 15 October 2026 through the web tool. These notes establish what was
read, not independent reproduction of the source results or novelty clearance.

1. Mortensen, Okkels and Bruus (2005), PRE 71,057301. Author/institution-hosted
   publisher PDF inspected, especially section III B, equations 10–11. Supplies
   the rectangular Poiseuille series used here and the constant-cross-section,
   steady Newtonian no-slip scope. URL:
   https://backend.orbit.dtu.dk/ws/files/4757131/Asger.pdf
2. Unger et al. (2000), Science 288,113–116. Author-hosted full PDF inspected,
   particularly page 114. Pressure-deflected control-channel membranes close flow
   channels; switching valves and pumps are demonstrated. Original rounded
   channels sealed more readily than rectangular ones. Its 80 C cure procedure
   is not evidence of a candidate-compatible sub-40 C fabrication process. URL:
   https://web.mit.edu/thorsen/www/113.pdf
3. Shin et al. (2016), PNAS 113,257–261, DOI10.1073/pnas.1511484112. Publisher
   full HTML and SI equations S8–S11 inspected. The model has zero net volume
   flux with nonzero diffusioosmotic countercirculation, invalidating the leap
   from scalar net-flux obstruction to no exchange. URL:
   https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1511484112
4. US4800953A (1989), full patent description and claims inspected. One-opening
   passage, supply/return baffle, closed-end U-turn and root barrier are explicit.
   Full text says its retaining arms prevent later removal after proper seating;
   do not cite it as a chemically erasable or freely removable divider. URL:
   https://patents.google.com/patent/US4800953A/en
5. US20080003325A1 (2008), full detailed embodiment inspected. Explicit blind-hole
   divider ending short of the bottom, elongated U-shaped coolant passage, supply
   and return path, close-fitting side seals. URL:
   https://patents.google.com/patent/US20080003325A1/en
6. Hornik et al. (2023 issue; online21December2022), Micromachines14,16. Author
   institution-hosted full PDF inspected, especially clearance procedure on page4.
   The measured endpoint is partial optical clearance; NaOH, sonication, 80 C
   heating and manual static pressure are part of that process. URL:
   https://nps.edu/documents/141875175/141876408/Hornik_etal_Micromachines_2022.pdf/a69507eb-7858-9393-450b-7283e6be913a?t=1683062430885
7. Huang, Li, Xie and Yang, arXiv2502.09485v1 (2025). Full author preprint HTML
   inspected; definition of torsion -Laplacian(u)=1 and section5 states the
   classical ball optimum. The report uses only that classical inequality, not
   this preprint's claimed new flow proof. URL:
   https://arxiv.org/html/2502.09485v1
8. On-demand generation and removal of alginate biocompatible microvalves for flow
   control in microfluidics (2016), Sensors and ActuatorsB234,1–7,
   DOI10.1016/j.snb.2016.04.140. Publisher-indexed abstract/intro text returned
   EDTA erasure of calcium-alginate valves. Repeated direct full-page opens failed;
   no exact formulation, seal strength or joint-process compatibility is claimed.
   URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.snb.2016.04.140
9. Stoeber, Liepmann and Muller, Microflow Control Using Thermally Responsive
   Triblock Copolymers, microTAS2003, pp183–186. Full conference primary PDF
   inspected. F127 locally gels under resistive heating and can reversibly block
   a channel; example16.6wt%,21C,50x20micrometre channel,reported stop within35ms.
   This supports the thermal gate comparator, not the candidate's exact
   millimetre-scale fabrication/material/temperature compatibility. URL:
   https://www.rsc.org/binaries/loc/2003/volume1/046-161.pdf
