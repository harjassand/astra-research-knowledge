# Calculation corrections

15 October 2026. Before the report was issued, an intermediate message and first
`hydraulic_certificate.json` used a Wheatstone gate comparator of 11 tau: nine tau
to displace a complete three-edge path followed by two tau to sweep the two
previously closed edges. That calculation does not imply complete initial-support
removal. Material discharged from AB after opening can recontaminate BD.

The final script uses an explicit complete one-dimensional plug protocol of ten
tau: flush A-C-B-D for six tau, until the clean front exits BC; open AB and CD;
flush the two final outer paths for four tau. BC remains clean and stagnant. The
feed volume is six V0, compared with five V0 for an ideal zero-thickness Euler
route. This is a model comparison, not a measured clearance result. No general
optimality or Pareto dominance is claimed.
