## Strongest completed mathematical result

The native-computation investigator derived a quantitative limit on using a specimen's own instability as a scientific computer. Consider

\[
U_s(y)=by^4/4+\gamma y^2/2,\qquad
U_u(y)=by^4/4-\gamma y^2/2.
\]

Their Hessians at zero are respectively +gamma and -gamma. Grant exact reset to zero, perfect observation of the entire continuous noisy trajectory, known positive mobility mu and inverse temperature beta, and any common causal randomized control with bounded gain |g_t|<=G and force |f_t|<=F. Admitted predictable policies have a well-defined unique nonexplosive controlled law and cannot depend on the unknown model label:

\[
dY=\mu[(g_t+\theta\gamma)Y-bY^3+f_t]dt+\sqrt{2\mu/\beta}\,dW,
\qquad\theta\in\{-1,+1\}.
\]

For any eta>0, put C=G+gamma+eta/2, H=1/beta+F^2/(2eta), and

\[
s_*={C+\sqrt{C^2+4bH}\over2b}.
\]

The controlled second moment is at most s_* for all time. Girsanov and that moment bound give

\[
D_{KL}(P_u^{[0,T]}\Vert P_s^{[0,T]})
\le \mu\beta\gamma^2T s_*.
\]

Consequently, a test with error at most delta<1/2 under each model requires

\[
T\ge { (1-2\delta)\log[(1-\delta)/\delta]
\over \mu\beta\gamma^2s_*}.
\]

The same bound applies to a finite collection of exact-reset trials with deterministic total interrogation cap, including policies adapted across trials. For fixed bounded controls and fixed mu,beta,gamma, necessary time diverges at least as sqrt(b), despite a fixed local negative-curvature margin. This is a genuine information lower bound for this admitted experiment class, rather than a conservative sample-size estimate. It excludes changes of temperature/mobility, unbounded forcing, hard displacement clamps, and direct force-law measurements.

The mechanism is strong nonlinear confinement: the unstable model's minima are only sqrt(gamma/b) away, and its release energy gamma^2/(4b) vanishes. The trajectories rarely explore a force difference large enough to accumulate information. No hidden coordinate, missing sensor bandwidth, zero-noise mode or unknown reset is needed for this obstruction. At mu=beta=gamma=1, b=10^12 and T=100 without controls, the bound gives equal-prior error at least about 0.496464. These are model units, not measured laboratory times. Arbitrarily large b is part of the mathematical family; no evidence establishes that a real substrate realizes that entire limit or lacks a useful physical cutoff.

The scientific decision changed is whether a local stiffness margin justifies a fast native instability screen. It does not. A successful device needs an acquired nonlinear range/release-energy bound or an intervention outside this bounded class. Established laser/OPO threshold spectroscopy and direct force-displacement metrology remain the comparators. This result protects foundational mathematics independently of the physical manufacturing lead; it does not itself expand a demonstrated technological capability.

## Strongest physical construction after successive revisions

The manufacturing lead began with trapped support in enclosed channels. The first idea paired channel ends at junctions into a temporary serial clearance route, then erased the partitions. An Euler-parity counterexample showed junction pairing alone cannot cover an arbitrary graph between chosen ports. Longitudinal septa repair the graph but add hydraulic resistance, wall-strength requirements and a second removal burden.

The strongest surviving narrow construction is **a temporarily routed attached loop**. Its final graph has a main line s-v-t and a loop v-b-c-v attached at one junction. Initially a small removable partition at v pairs the inlet with the loop entrance and the loop return with the outlet. Wash therefore follows s-v-b-c-v-t through full-width channels. After primary support removal, erase only the proximal junction partition; the intended attached-loop geometry is restored without a new final access hole.

In a scalar, passive, steady incompressible hydraulic graph, the final one-attachment loop has zero net edge flux for all terminal pressures and edge-deletion valve configurations. The temporary split creates throughflow. This changes a real transport operation rather than estimating an unobserved mechanism. It is not a theorem that the final cavity has no physical exchange: finite-width mouth pressures, recirculation, diffusion, diffusioosmosis, compliance, vacuum cycling and multiphase flow can defeat the graph reduction. A valve that can split the junction can reproduce the routing; restricting a comparator to edge deletion would be too weak.

The proposed consequence is clearing an enclosed loop reservoir or resonant cavity during low-temperature manufacture, followed by the intended final geometry. Neither a qualified application requiring these constraints nor an advantage over assembled/direct fabrication has been obtained. No claim about energy production, high-power cooling, or Kardashev progress follows.

The geometry of a supply-and-return baffle is established cooling technology: the adversary located primary patent descriptions, including US4800953A and US20080003325A1. A particularly strong comparator uses the primary F127 support itself as a locally warmed thermoreversible gate, then cools it to reopen the junction; localized thermal F127 gating was demonstrated in a 2003 silicon/Pyrex microdevice. Its spatial thermal control and transfer to this millimetre-scale host remain unmeasured, but excluding it would make the comparison artificially weak. ### Why the broader blind-channel version is not promoted

The long blind-channel variant needs a longitudinal wall to supply outbound and return lanes. The collaborators found three decisive costs:

1. **Hydraulics.** For a no-slip Newtonian straight duct, resistance is at least 8*pi*mu*L/A^2. Splitting area A into m serially traversed lanes gives an absolute lower bound m^3 times the equal-area circular reference resistance. This is not an m^3 ratio to every original shape. Two width-split shallow rectangular lanes approach a fourfold same-shape cost; height splitting approaches sixteenfold; a square example gives about 9.84-fold. At fixed pressure, both the transport clock and mouth diffusion can scale as L^2; there is no demonstrated length-exponent advantage.
2. **Premature erasure.** The blind-channel adversary derived an exact leaky-lane model with kappa=sqrt(2rg). Distal flow relative to the intact U-turn is kappa*L/sinh(kappa*L). At kappa*L=5 only 6.74% remains; at 10 only 0.0908% remains. Early proximal dissolution can starve the distal wall. Root's paired-arrival dose cancellation is exact for equal-speed, nondepleting plug flow, but is fragile to rate variation and leakage.
3. **Export.** Even perfectly simultaneous wall disappearance can leave dissolved wall material in the now-blind cavity. Delivering reagent everywhere does not evacuate the resulting residue. A final functional endpoint must assay both primary support and secondary material.

The original 25 micrometre Ca-alginate wall is unsupported by the acquired fabrication and modulus evidence. Aluminium foil is stiffer, but a nominal 80 mm by 500 micrometre by 25 micrometre wall contains 2.7 mg Al. Complete alkaline dissolution can generate about 3.67 mL H2 at room conditions and requires about 100 micromoles OH-. Gas generation is not evidence of useful clearing: bubbles can block dissolution, material must leave, and an unvented reaction can pressurize the cavity. Primary aluminium-template work uses concentrated NaOH and warming, reports slow room-temperature dissolution and warns of precipitation/clogging when injection stops. These facts do not close a below-40 C protocol.

The attached-loop mutation removes the distributed-wall penalty and moves secondary removal next to the main flow. It still needs a real root seal, primary-support compatibility, stage-one wall survival, controlled erasure, final geometry and residue qualification. It is a proposed apparatus, not a reproduced physical capability.
