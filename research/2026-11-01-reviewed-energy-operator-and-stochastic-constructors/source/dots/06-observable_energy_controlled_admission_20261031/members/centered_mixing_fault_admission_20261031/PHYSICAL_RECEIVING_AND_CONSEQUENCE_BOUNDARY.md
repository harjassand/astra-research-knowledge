# Physical receiving and consequence boundary for observable-decay admission

31 October 2026. Separate assessment; no alteration of the reviewed exact or robust backward-weight theorems, earlier clamped proofs, executable certificates, or frozen local ZIP. This note identifies what an actual experiment or computing architecture would still have to earn. No physical calibration, gate execution, threshold value, historical priority, or practical advantage is claimed.

## What has been earned

From an ACTUAL block-local source generator, fixed compatible source/data patches, acquired unwanted coupling norms, and an exact or uniformly bounded residual G*K+rK, the backward weight gives an all-selected initialized-dilation fault-amplitude bound. Ideal source-blind controls and retained local instruments do not incur an extra variation/bandwidth penalty in that bound. Their physical errors remain part of the model and their execution costs remain real. Initial finite source/data/reference correlations and later source reuse are allowed.

This is stronger than inferring a high-order fault model from a small reduced-channel error or measured covariance. It is a certificate of a specified microscopic open-system model, not construction of the model itself. The initialized Markov ports and locality/commutation assumptions remain essential.

## Isolated-source calibration is insufficient

An isolated source can exhibit G*X=-rX while its joint dynamics, once coupled to data and driven during gates, is not the assumed sum of that same source GKSL generator and the stated Hamiltonian. An apparatus must establish the actual joint model, or bound its discrepancy uniformly over the operating controls and relevant states. Fitting one stationary correlation curve does not establish the required generator action or the common dilation.

Increasing r is not a free hardware knob. The source's reservoir correlation time, spectral response, drive strength, pulse width and coupler behavior constrain when a local Markov description is valid. A rotating-frame transformation also transforms the coupling and controls; it cannot silently delete source precession while leaving a static X interaction unchanged. Such a term contributes to the actual residual unless a separately valid exact model receives it.

The new bound removes an ADMISSION penalty for ideal local control variation. It does not remove the task's target gates, preparation, measurements, feed-forward, local apparatus noise, calibration or power requirements. A noisy control register with broad fanout is not covered merely by calling it a record. Unknown cross-patch source couplings or shared spectators are outside the disjoint-patch proof unless separately bounded.

## High temperature does not preserve the cold activity saving

For a thermal qubit with population loss lambda and injection a, its stationary excited population is a/(lambda+a). The canonical stationary loss flux and injection flux are each lambda*a/(lambda+a). Their total is

    2 lambda*a/(lambda+a).

The observable rate is r=(lambda+a)/2. If a=k lambda at fixed k>0, the total canonical jump rate is [4k/(1+k)^2]r. It therefore grows linearly with broadening r; at equal rates lambda=a=r it is exactly r per source. This differs from a cold/bounded-injection energy certificate whose count can remain small as damping grows. During general driven operation the stationary formula is not substituted for the actual count; it already suffices to refute a universal low-activity claim at high temperature.

For the isotropic model G=(r/4)(D[X]+D[Y]+D[Z]), the displayed Pauli-jump unraveling has total instantaneous canonical rate3r/4, independent of the state. It can be viewed mathematically as ordinary random source-Pauli kicks. Implementing such kicks with finite physical controls must pay their rate and errors; it is not an instruction that arbitrarily fast ideal pulses exist. Different realizations/unravelings can have different records and hardware costs.

Jump activity is not automatically energy, dissipated heat, power, or a gate count. Those require a physical realization and energy accounting. Conversely, an efficient mathematical admission certificate does not imply a cheap simulation or cheap physical generation of the full source/field process.

## Strong ordinary constructions remain the baseline

The one-axis thermal X coupling has an exact classical telegraph logical description under source-blind system controls, because span{I,X} is closed under source adjoint evolution and multiplication by the coupling. Its constant-size ODE is an ordinary winner. Warm motional narrowing at fixed interaction strength is not a new physical suppression mechanism.

Damped-qubit adiabatic elimination already produces the second-order dissipator used in the cold benchmark. Zanardi, Marshall and Campos Venuti's dissipative universal simulation construction explicitly uses coherently coupled dissipating qubits; its Proposition1 is a steady-subspace effective-semigroup estimate, not by itself an every-selected-location amplitude certificate. [Primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/1506.04311).

Ng–Preskill already derives inclusive fault-amplitude control using a Gaussian correlation integral rather than raw instantaneous operator norm. Its Wick and initial-field assumptions are substantive. The present finite non-Gaussian source model is different, but bypassing a raw bandwidth norm alone is not new. [Primary paper](https://preskill.caltech.edu/pubs/preskill-2009-gaussian.pdf).

Ordinary local/non-Markovian fault-tolerance receiving, known control compilation, randomized/decoupling constructions, and exact finite-matrix propagation must receive identical generator, coupling, support and control information in a fair comparison. No absence-of-title search establishes that they cannot derive a similarly strong certificate. The current work has not established an implementation-cost advantage over such methods.

## Conditional fault-tolerance consequence that is actually earned

Suppose EVERY gate, preparation, measurement and idle location needed by an existing local-amplitude fault-tolerance consumer meets the actual joint source model, common-dilation/support premises and uniform parameter bounds, with all remaining imperfections received by that same consumer. Its existing positive amplitude threshold is an external task parameter; no new numerical threshold is selected here. The theorem can then supply a circuit-length-independent source rate sufficient for admission. For exact observable decay, the displayed radius bound has the scaling

    r = O(c²*tau/eta_target² + c/eta_target).

The radius must also obey R>=2 and R<=r/(4c). In the robust case one must additionally make 2R*d_J/r small at that chosen radius; stating only that the residual is “small” without this receiving condition is insufficient. Coupling, patch size and control-model bounds must themselves remain uniform as the computation grows.

This separates the new sufficient admission calculation from directly applying a raw instantaneous-norm test proportional to c*tau, which would not improve merely by increasing r. It does NOT prove superiority to every ordinary microscopic analysis; Gaussian and other structured methods can also exploit decay. Nor does it provide an improved physical control service. The standard initialized-isometry/local-amplitude receiving interface and real gadget assumptions remain those already supplied by the ordinary threshold framework, including the inspected AGP Section11 and Astra N1483 receiver. [AGP primary](https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0504218v3). No absent P2 card locator is inferred.

Rate independent of circuit LENGTH does not mean total cost independent of device size or time: local source generation, high-temperature jump activity, target gates, measurement/reset services and calibration remain extensive and paid. This is the precise conditional enabling consequence, not a demonstrated hardware operating point.

## Consequential-operation judgment

The meaningful present result is controlled LOCAL ADMISSION from actual observable decay, including warm/nonprimitive sources and local instruments, without requiring vacuum preparation or a full-source mixing certificate. It could reduce the information needed for a mathematically rigorous device noise model: local generator actions and support/control bounds rather than reconstruction of the entire bath state or global process. That is conditional on those local data genuinely describing the operated joint apparatus.

No paid hardware operating regime or new computation has yet been demonstrated. At fixed diffusion c=sqrt(r)b, the leading c*sqrt(tau/r)=b*sqrt(tau) noise scale remains. Residual bias can also remain at arbitrarily large r. Thermal broadening may require activity proportional to r and finite Markov bandwidth. These are decisive reasons not to label the current result a civilization-level capability or to make another source family the default next objective.

Preserve the theorem as useful analytical capital. A further physical claim should be driven by a specified task and real implementation whose total target-gate, source-generation, control, calibration and entropy-removal costs beat or enable something beyond an equally informed ordinary construction. More local matrix frontends or cleaner sufficient constants alone do not establish that claim.
