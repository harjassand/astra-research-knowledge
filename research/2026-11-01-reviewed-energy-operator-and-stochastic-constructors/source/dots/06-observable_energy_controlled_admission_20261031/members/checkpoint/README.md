## Ordinary wins and unavoidable scales

The single-axis thermal model has an exact telegraph/constant-size ODE solution. Ordinary elimination already gives its leading dissipator. The benchmark proves the finite-rate probability scale c²T/lambda and initialized-isometry amplitude scale c*sqrt(T/lambda), with nonvanishing fixed-diffusion disturbance. Gaussian and other microscopic fault methods receive the same source information; no advantage over their strongest valid use is claimed.

Source-only mediator inequalities compare coherent operation with scalar-centered noise AND physical scalar activity. Scalar jump components and data-dependent excited Hamiltonians prevent a blanket activity-to-logical-noise no-go. The tradeoff does not replace a finite-rate implementation certificate.
