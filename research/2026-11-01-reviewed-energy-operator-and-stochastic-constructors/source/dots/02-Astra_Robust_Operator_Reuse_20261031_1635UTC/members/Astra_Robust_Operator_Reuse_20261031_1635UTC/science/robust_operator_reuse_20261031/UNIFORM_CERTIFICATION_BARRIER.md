# A scoped acquisition/verification obstruction, not a limit on learning

Author proof, 31 October 2026. The decision problem below concerns **optimal
uniform validation over all externally supplied words**, in a general
substochastic input language. It does not make the constructive observable
certificate in PROOF.md intractable: that certificate is sound but incomplete,
and its basis search may honestly return full dimension. It also does not
establish a limit on cumulative learning under other task distributions or
input representations. Probabilistic-automaton decision hardness is established
prior machinery; no historical novelty is claimed for this reduction.

## Finite task and gap

Given two nonnegative rational column-substochastic matrices A_0,A_1, rational
b>=0 with ||b||_1=1, c in [0,1]^N, a horizon h written in unary, and threshold
tau, consider the proposed zero-output compressed representation. Ask whether

    c^T A_w b <= tau   for every binary word w of length h.

Every one-step operator is an explicitly checkable l1 contraction. Evaluating
one supplied word is polynomial rational arithmetic. The universal validation
problem is coNP-complete. The hardness persists under an inverse-polynomial
robustness margin and when the exact generated matrix algebra is full.

## 1. Direct ordinary 3SAT reduction

Let a 3CNF formula have h>=2 variables and m>=1 clauses. Form states
(clause j, time k, satisfied flag s), with 1<=j<=m, 0<=k<=h, s in {0,1}.
There are N=2m(h+1) states. The letter a in {0,1} advances time k to k+1 and
updates the flag according to whether assigning variable k+1 the value a
satisfies a literal in clause j. At k=h the state is absorbing. The resulting
transition matrices P_0,P_1 are deterministic column-stochastic matrices.

Start uniformly on (j,0,0), and let c select the states (j,h,1). A length-h
word is precisely an assignment, and

    c^T P_w b = number of clauses satisfied / m.

Set q=1-1/(2h), A_a=q P_a, and

    tau=q^h*(1-1/(2m)).

If the formula is satisfiable, some word has value q^h, exceeding tau by
gamma=q^h/(2m). If unsatisfiable, every word has value at most q^h*(1-1/m),
below tau by at least the same gamma. Bernoulli's inequality gives q^h>=1/2,
so gamma>=1/(4m). All entries and the horizon have polynomial descriptions.

The complement of universal validation is in NP: a violating word is a
polynomial-size witness and its rational matrix product can be checked with
polynomial bit growth. Thus the decision problem is coNP-complete. There is
no exponential-precision threshold hidden in this construction. Multiplying
c and tau by a rational eta in (0,1] makes the absolute requested tolerance
small, with the gap reduced by exactly eta. This is NOT hardness at every
fixed relative-error target; the distinction being resolved is of order 1/m.

## 2. Stability of the decision gap

For another contraction pair A'_a with ||A'_a-A_a||_1<=zeta, contractive
telescoping gives

    |c^T A'_w b-c^T A_w b| <= h*zeta.

Consequently perturbations with h*zeta<=gamma/4 leave a margin of at least
3gamma/4 on either side of the original threshold. The hardness is not
based on an exact equality test or unstable cancellation.

## 3. Arbitrarily small perturbations can also force full exact algebra

Let D=diag(d_1,...,d_N), where d_i=i/[2(N+1)] are distinct positive rationals,
and X=11^T/(2N). Both are nonnegative contractions. Define

    A_0(rho)=(1-rho)A_0+rho D,
    A_1(rho)=(1-rho)A_1+rho X.

They remain nonnegative contractions and differ from A_a by at most 2rho in
induced l1 norm. Choose any positive rational eta no larger than
q^h/(16mh), or a still smaller externally requested perturbation budget.

Consider the N^2 word matrices

    A_0(rho)^i A_1(rho) A_0(rho)^j,   0<=i,j<N.

The determinant F(rho) of their vectorizations is a rational polynomial of
degree at most Ddeg=N^2(2N-1). At rho=1 these words equal D^i X D^j. Their
vectorizations are independent: the two Vandermonde matrices generated by
the distinct d_i are invertible, and every entry of X is nonzero. Thus F is
not identically zero.

Among the Ddeg+1 distinct rational points rho=k*eta/(Ddeg+1), one must have
F(rho)!=0. Exact elimination can find it in polynomial bit cost. All failed
determinant/rank tests are paid; this is a high-degree polynomial reduction,
not a proposed practical acquisition routine. At the selected point the
generated algebra is all M_N(Q), while the task perturbation is at most
2h*rho<=gamma/4 and the SAT/UNSAT threshold remains separated. Taking eta
arbitrarily smaller preserves both conclusions with the input precision paid.

This explicitly separates full exact algebra generation from stable finite
task behavior. A complete optimal compression/threshold verifier cannot be
assumed cheap just because one candidate representation is tiny.

## Strong comparison and constructive escape

An equally informed ordinary method can retain the matrices, the original
clause formula if recovered, and all previous word evaluations. Each new word
can still be evaluated in polynomial time; no planning subproblem is needed.
The obstruction is to a complete, uniformly decisive offline validator, not
to those on-demand methods or to every useful retained abstraction.

The common-equilibrium metric and two-sided leakage certificate in PROOF.md
provide an explicit incomplete alternative: acquire/check finite local
inequalities, then answer every word inside the accepted horizon. They do not
solve the worst-word optimization problem. A failed certificate causes basis
refinement or full-space fallback, not a claim that no useful structure exists.
The associated learned representation can be used equally by ordinary Krylov,
spectral, randomized-range and model-reduction methods.
