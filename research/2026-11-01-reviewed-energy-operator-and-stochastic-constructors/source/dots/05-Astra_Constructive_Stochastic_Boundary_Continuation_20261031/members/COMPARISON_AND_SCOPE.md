## Conventional machinery receives credit

- Finite killed generators, positive inverse matrices, monotone graphical coupling, exponential conjugation, polynomial bounds, and Foster theory are conventional. This package constructs and verifies the needed objects from raw lists; it does not claim invention of those tools.
- The coming-down step in the cubic-only intermediate package belongs to the conventional super-Lyapunov/hitting-time toolkit. The primary appendix C of Cappelletti, Ortiz-Muñoz, Anderson and Winfree (2018) discusses super-Lyapunov bounds and uniform control for CTMCs: https://www.dna.caltech.edu/Papers/CRNdistributions_arXiv2018v1.pdf . Only the mathematical comparison is used; its distribution-design constructions are not part of this task.
- Bibbona, Kim and Wiuf (2020), theorem4.1, proves strong recurrence properties for its autocatalytic class with positive inflow and outflow for every species: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7423442/ . The present satellites need not have direct outflows, so that theorem cannot simply be applied as stated. Non-complex balance alone is never presented as novelty.
- Hoessly's product-form decomposition theorem requires compatible shared factors: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00285-021-01620-3 . Its actual conditions were inspected earlier. Incompatible Poisson demands in the examples rule out that particular easy factorization, not all possible decompositions or prior recurrence proofs.

## A simpler independent nonexplosion baseline

For the linear-service partial-row class, total count can increase only through the constant-rate immigration channels. Stars and neutral channels conserve total count. Hence total count is bounded by its initial value plus the immigration Poisson count over each finite time interval; rates are bounded on that random finite state set. This already proves nonexplosion by an elementary argument. The coverage Foster proof is valid but is not credited with inventing nonexplosion for this class. Positive recurrence and acquisition of compatible boundary drift remain the substantive obligations.

## The next unclosed structural obstruction

The support construction relies on service-star reactions preserving the last positive hub copy. A general cross-species reaction can instead erase a service species' last copy at a rate amplified by another unbounded count. The finite constant-rate protected-support comparison then need not apply. No theorem in this package resolves that general boundary-erasure mechanism or the arbitrary-dimensional weak-reversibility conjecture.
