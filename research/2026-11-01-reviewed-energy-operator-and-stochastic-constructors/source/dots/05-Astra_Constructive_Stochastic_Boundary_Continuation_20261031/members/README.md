## Main result

The culminating coverage-support construction acquires a stability certificate for a restricted raw class with a strongly connected monomolecular service core, reversible partial satellite-star rows, direct hub deaths, and arbitrary service-preserving total-preserving neutral channels. It does not assume a stationary fiber law, favorable pivot, successful finite LP, or uniform-return oracle. Its finite tilted support table handles every uncovered satellite face; a rational small-exponential search is proved to terminate.

This is NOT general weak reversibility, a novelty/priority clearance, external refereeing, an efficient complete algorithm, or a practical mixing bound. Its acquired exponential parameter may be close to1 and its constant extremely large.

## Real failures and costs are included

- The first exact Dirichlet patches in two examples create positive drift on infinite fringe fibers. Solving a finite killed chain alone is not accepted as a global certificate.
- A partial-row floating LP candidate has positive satellite coefficients on an infinite ray. Exact rational verification rejects it; a numerical “optimal” status is not a proof.
- The nonlinear two-inactive clock actually paid a300-state exact solve, approximately340 seconds in that observation and4,769-bit fractions. On exactly the same raw network, protected acquisition used4 states/11-bit fractions while keeping the same6-state patch. This is not a uniform complexity benchmark.
- The coverage example's compact Foster constant has conservative unreduced numerator/denominator expansion bounds around26.8 billion bits. The powers were not expanded. The certificate stores an exact factored expression and explicitly charged exponents.
- On the same partial-row example, the conventional LP finder acquired T=2 and much better explicit bounds, whereas the terminating coverage route acquired T=65/64. The routes have different qualitative and quantitative strengths.
