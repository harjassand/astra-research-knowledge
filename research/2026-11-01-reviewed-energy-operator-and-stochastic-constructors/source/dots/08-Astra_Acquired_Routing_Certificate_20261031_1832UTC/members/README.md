## Scientific result

a positive block representation and a uniform scalar word-error certificate.

stochastic coarse action and integer replay are all constructed and charged.
The same outgoing observations certify leakage and fine coarse action using
separate simultaneous confidence events. A single good operator event covers
future adaptively selected words, not arbitrary new observables or feedback.

Both have n=4096, L=24, epsilon=.02, delta=.001. These are sufficient paid
schedules, not necessary prices. The source counts were generated by aggregate
synthetic multinomials; billions of ordinary calls were **not individually
executed**. Source-simulation timing is not real acquisition timing.

The statistical theorem requires authentic iid transitions, fixed batches
chosen nonanticipatingly and fresh independent norm probes. Exact replay does
not authenticate these premises, physical/model truth, or a posterior error
probability conditional on a rare admitted transcript. Mock seeds and NumPy
multinomials are fixtures, not adversarial-source guarantees.

using a certified same-count true spread above .0636474. All 50,000 integer

## Comparisons and limitations

Ordinary community/spectral/Krylov/range methods may use this acquired
certificate and retain all observations. No exclusive new learner or practical
advantage is claimed. The best implemented retention method improves the
earlier 4.315-billion-call schedule using exactly its observation prefix.
The sharpened retained-prefix Monte Carlo budget (11.2996 billion calls) is a
sufficient comparator, not an optimal lower bound. Stronger variance/KL and
goal-oriented alternatives remain relevant. Known aggregation, collision
testing, matrix concentration and randomized norm methods are credited.
Historical novelty and civilisation-level consequence are not established.

Failure regimes include large column mass, rare/singleton input blocks,
no useful complement gap, failed partition acquisition, the stronger scaled-
L2 rather than tolerant-TV promise, and violated sampling/probe provenance.
The old receiving defects and patched source are retained explicitly as
history; they are not current PASS evidence.
