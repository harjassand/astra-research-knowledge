# Source scope and ordinary comparators

Read-only source check: 2026-10-31. This is a mathematical construction and an internal audit, not historical priority clearance. No external upload, outreach, publication or operational chemistry is involved.

## Primary literature actually inspected

- [Anderson, Cappelletti and Kim (2020), *Stochastically modeled weakly reversible reaction networks with a single linkage class*](https://doi.org/10.1017/jpr.2020.28). The accessible publisher abstract specifies a binary, single-linkage-class theorem with a pure complex for every species and an n-step embedded-chain method. The present mediated example has three linkage classes; that stated theorem is not a direct application. Only this accessible abstract, not its full proof, was used for this comparison. No claim that all methods in that paper have been excluded.
- [Bibbona, Kim and Wiuf (2020), *Stationary distributions of systems with discreteness-induced transitions*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7423442/), equation (4.1), Theorem 4.1 and Appendix A. Their stated recurrence result assumes positive inflow and outflow for every species. Our satellites have neither, so the direct total-count exponential drift argument does not cover this example. Their Foster approach and count-based comparison are conventional baselines, not ingredients claimed as new here.
- [ConStRAINeD, results and publications](https://constrained.polito.it/publications/), Objective 1 and item 16. The project announces all-two-species weakly reversible recurrence, but item 16 remains under **In preparation** with no paper link. We have not inspected the proof and do not quote its details as an established tool. The project states that arbitrary dimension remains open. The present work does not resolve that question.

The earlier accepted packages retain their own task-first public-repository provenance and broader tier, decomposition, first-order, and affine/queueing comparisons. They are not silently replaced or relabeled by this package. No new theorem in this package relies on an inaccessible or uninspected source as a proof premise.

It does not exclude sampled-time, excursion, additive, queueing, tier or path methods. An acquired alternative aggregate (P,N) succeeds.
5. Old pivot/terminal-gap/protected/coverage sufficient finders were run on the same raw list. UNKNOWN and recognizer rejection are not instability claims and are not historical-priority evidence.
