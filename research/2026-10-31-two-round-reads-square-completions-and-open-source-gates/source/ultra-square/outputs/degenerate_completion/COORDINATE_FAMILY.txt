ALL DIAGONAL QUADRATIC DIRECTIONS: ROC AND QC STABILIZABILITY COINCIDE
31 October 2026. Constructive exact proof; the fixed leading F is unchanged.

THEOREM

Let Q(A)=q_a a^2+q_b b^2+q_c c^2+q_d d^2, all q_i>=0, and let I be
the set of its positive coefficients. There is an explicit real linear
space N_I of cubics such that, for EVERY homogeneous cubic C,

 some finite nu makes F+C+nu Q globally ROC
   <=> C belongs to N_I
   <=> some finite nu makes F+C+nu Q globally QC.             (1)

All sixteen spaces and constructive heat lifts are computed exactly in
coordinate_family.py; the literal results are coordinate_family.json.
Their dimensions are:

 positive support of Q                         dim N_I
 ------------------------------------------------------
 all four coordinates                              12
 any three coordinates                              8
 one full output row: {a,b} or {c,d}                  4
 opposite entries: {a,d} or {b,c}                    4
 one full spatial column: {a,c} or {b,d}              2
 at most one coordinate                             0

This is a classification of FINITE STABILIZABILITY along every diagonal
positive-semidefinite quadratic direction. It is not ROC=QC at every
coefficient nu. In particular it leaves the 1859 fixture open. Arbitrary
quadratics with cross terms have not been classified.

For nonzero Q, put q_min=min_{i in I} q_i. Each N_I has a supplied basis
with coefficient l1 norm one in every column. For C=sum t_j C_(I,j),
the single uniform choice

              nu=2^106 (sum |t_j|)^2 / q_min                (2)

is sufficient. Smaller support-specific proven powers, from 86 to 106,
are retained in the data. These are existence bounds. They have no
asserted numerical advantage over the separate 4096 theorem.

The theorem transports under every proper output rotation preserving F.
In particular it also classifies all quadratic forms

 Q(A)=(A e1)^T P(A e1)+(A e2)^T R(A e2)

where P,R are positive-semidefinite symmetric 2 by 2 matrices that
commute. Such P,R have a common orthonormal output eigenbasis, which
can be chosen to have determinant +1. Transport the cubic to that basis,
apply (1), then transport back. This includes a continuous family of
nondiagonal forms, with no change to the fixed leading F.

Use the same twenty-dimensional space of closed linear two-forms and

 X_C(A)=(1/2)matrix[C'' heat + Omega_C(A)(B1,B2)]+skew(A).

The program reconstructs the wedge halves and the four exact closure
relations, so X+X^T has precisely the required corrected cubic density.
There is no optimization or positive-semidefinite solver in this step.

The expressions are homogeneous quadratics in u, so evaluating their

                         X(A) K_I=0 for every A.           (3)
