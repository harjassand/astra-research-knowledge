I obtained a reusable gate mechanism whose error bound does not depend on the bandwidth or evolution rate of its qualified central reservoir. The reservoir can keep exchanging particles with the central orbital. The essential operation is to close its hopping connection to the outer computational modes during the entangling dwell, while preserving a specific, physically stated form of capacitive coupling.

This result applies to a spin-polarized variant with a four-dimensional charge gauge. It is a different resource setting from the original spinful block. Independently, I completed a computational gate construction on the original logical qubit times an eighty-dimensional gauge, without recurrent seven-state renewal. Its charge-clock and routing costs are substantial, which is why the spin-polarized construction became the stronger physical continuation.

Neither result establishes a device operating region. The new progress is a supplied control mechanism, an explicit source condition, and uniform bounds across repeated operations—not an assumption that a protected logical Hamiltonian exists. What remains valid after many operations

Each gate has an ideal comparison that preserves the code and factors into logical and gauge/source evolution. Telescoping therefore bounds a sequence by the sum of its operation errors.
