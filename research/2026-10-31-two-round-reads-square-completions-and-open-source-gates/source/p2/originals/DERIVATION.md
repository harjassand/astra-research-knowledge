# Computation on a full charge representation without recurrent gauge cooling

Working research, 30 October 2026. The preceding central-port, accessibility,
screening, renewal, active-response and failure results are unchanged. These
are new root-working results, with exact algebra, a certified finite pulse
root, and explicitly labelled numerical examples. No complete device
operating region or experimental advantage is established.

## 1. The construction and its actual consequence

The two-dimensional standard representation of permutations of three outer
spinful orbitals remains the logical carrier. In the four-orbital Fock
space it occurs as E tensor C^80. A real central source can act arbitrarily
on the multiplicity factor without acting on E.

The new construction supplies an ideal computational gate alphabet on
this entire component, rather than only its seven-state charge sector or
a renewed ground gauge:

* Exact outer orbital permutations, with the fermionic signs included.
* A capacitive controlled phase between the parities of two selected
  antisymmetric orbital modes.

The second operation is generated by a finite, all-Fock Hubbard mode
sorter, a physical number-number interaction and forward physical
unsorting. No clean oscillator or initialized parity ancilla is used.
The local permutation alphabet plus this interaction is dense in SO(4).
Established real simulation then gives universal quantum computation from
initialized inputs with one additional logical rebit. It does not enact
arbitrary complex unitaries on unknown inputs without an additional phase
resource.

The exact source compatibility during the gate uses its microscopic form.
Center-to-outer hopping is paused. Arbitrary retained source interactions
through central operators, total outer number and collective outer spin
can continue. All supplied controls commute with this source throughout
the path, even while the original representation subspace is temporarily
rotated. A generic abstract invariant gauge Hamiltonian need not commute
with that path.

This removes seven-state cooling from the ideal coherent gate sequence.
Initial logical preparation, finite orbital loading, calibration, routing,
readout and error correction retain their physical costs.

The physical comparison then produces a second, distinct construction.
For a qualified spin-polarized star, local continuous gates are quadratic
and exact on E tensor C^4. Balanced capacitance between two triangles,
with staggered dark-mode offsets, supplies a uniformly approximate
entangler. A final refinement factors an arbitrarily broad central
reservoir out exactly: only center-to-leaf hopping is paused during the
entangler. Central loading and an interacting retained reservoir may
continue. Section 9.1 gives an explicit active error bound independent of
the source bandwidth, and counts imperfect isolation separately. This
stronger source statement is narrower in coupling geometry than an
arbitrary invariant-bath claim and uses a stricter spin/orbital loading
qualification than the full eighty-dimensional gauge construction.

## 2. A permutation is a fermion parity

For two selected spatial orbitals i,j define

\[
b_{-,\sigma}=(c_{i\sigma}-c_{j\sigma})/\sqrt2,
\qquad n_-=n_{-,\uparrow}+n_{-,\downarrow}.
\]

The fermionic orbital transposition fixes the symmetric modes and changes
the sign of both antisymmetric modes. Hence, on the entire Fock space,

\[
P_{ij}=\Gamma(ij)=(-1)^{n_-}
=(1-2n_{-,\uparrow})(1-2n_{-,\downarrow})
=1-2n_-+4D_-.
\]

For n_-=0,1,2 this also equals 2(n_--1)^2-1. Thus an independently supplied
shifted interaction on this mode would give continuous exp(-i theta P).
That identity alone does not supply the interaction in an equal-U device.

For two blocks and any integer occupations,

\[
e^{-i\pi n_A n_B}
=I-2\frac{I-(-1)^{n_A}}2\frac{I-(-1)^{n_B}}2.
\]

After sorting and unsorting, this is

\[
\boxed{C_Z(P_A,P_B)=I-\tfrac12(I-P_A)(I-P_B).}
\]

On E_A tensor E_B it is a logical CZ tensor identity on both gauge spaces.
On the other irreps it remains a well-defined function of the same
permutations. In particular it preserves a good block's E component even
beside a bad irrep in another block.

The number-number gate has this parity-only property at integer multiples
of pi. A generic capacitive angle distinguishes n=0 from n=2. The fixed
nontrivial angle is sufficient below; continuous parity phases are not
assumed.

## 3. A useful obstruction and why it does not end computation

With equal onsite U and arbitrary quadratic number-conserving controls,
the one-particle and one-hole dynamics are related by particle-hole
duality. For m spinful sites, in corresponding bases,

\[
H_1=h(t),\qquad
H_{2m-1}=[(m-1)U+\operatorname{tr}h(t)]I-h(t)^T.
\]

Thus U_hole is a scalar phase times U_1^*. Uniform pair charging with
equal row sums only changes the scalar in these extreme sectors.
The spinful orbital permutation has determinant +1 on the one-body space,
so its one-hole representation is the complex conjugate of its
one-particle representation.

A target exp(-i theta P) in both sectors therefore requires exp(2i theta
P) scalar, hence theta in (pi/2)Z. This remains true with extra identical
sites if the same equal-U control setting is retained. Unequal interactions,
pair sources, or another physical control can change the setting; they
are additional resources.

There is also an instantaneous quadratic-generator obstruction on the
whole spinful E tensor C^80 component. The one-electron E demands the
ordinary E action; in the two-electron singlet, Sym^2(E)=A1+E. The X/Z
one-body generators leak to A1, and the remaining Y acts as -2Y on the
equivalent E copy. A common nontrivial logical generator cannot satisfy
both. Quadratic pairing has no same-number block that repairs this.

Neither result forbids a finite pulse path through other charge states,
or a discrete gate set augmented by a genuine interaction. Those are the
two escapes actually used here.

The following positive palindrome realizes B_x=exp(i pi K/4) on every

The total time is 6 pi hbar/U and the hopping area is 9 pi/4. The only

The exact root is not inferred from a rounded residual. The separate

For two modules operated in parallel, phase intervals and the middle

\[

\[
H_C=(U_o-V_o)\sum_jD_j+\tfrac{V_o}{2}N_o(N_o-1).

Use U=U_o-V_o. The total-number term commutes with sorting; the third

## 5. Discrete real universality, with an executable consumer

Choose a real logical basis in which a transposition is Z. The product of
two transpositions supplies R=exp[-i(2 pi/3)Y], up to the harmless choice
of inverse cycle. On two blocks let

\[
A=R_A,\quad B=CZ R_B CZ
=\exp[-i(2\pi/3)Z_AY_B].
\]

Their Pauli generators anticommute. The half-trace of AB in its SU(2)
factor is cos^2(2 pi/3)=1/4. The rational-cosine theorem makes its rotation
angle an irrational multiple of pi, so the group is infinite. A proper
infinite closed subgroup of SU(2) lies in a torus normalizer. Its
non-torus elements have order four, whereas A and B have order three and
do not commute. Their closure must therefore be SU(2), with Lie algebra
spanned by {YI, ZY, XY}.

Swapping the roles of the blocks supplies {IY, YZ, YX}. These two algebras
commute and together span so(4). Hence the physical alphabet is dense in
SO(4). Inverses are supplied: a cycle inverse is two reversed
transpositions; CZ is self-inverse. Standard finite-alphabet synthesis
therefore applies. Neither a continuously tunable partial swap nor a
magical logical-Hamiltonian oracle is needed.

This is an application of established real-computation universality,
not a new universality theorem for real quantum mechanics. One extra
initialized rebit represents real and imaginary amplitudes, preserving
computational-basis output probabilities of ordinary algorithms. This
is not a complex-linear map that converts arbitrary unknown quantum
inputs into their realification. A known |+i> reference would permit
arbitrary complex operations on unknown data using real gates, but that
reference cannot be prepared by this real alphabet from real inputs;
its preparation and stability must be separately supplied.

The ordinary meet-in-the-middle compiler in real_compiler.py enumerates
524285 reduced quaternion words through half-depth17. Building the net
and search tree took about .55s in the recorded run. A search of about
3.5s produced an approximation to exp(-i pi YI/8) with operator error
3.22727e-4, using 34 cycles and 34 parity CZs, or 68 orbital
transpositions and 34 CZs. A generic SU(2) factor target had error
1.79273e-4 with 66 transpositions and 34 CZs. The 4x4 matrix norm was
checked independently of the quaternion distance.

These are synthesis errors only. For example the first word has ideal
duration [1088+68m] pi hbar/U using the un-routed clock primitive and
the q=3 transposition duration8 pi hbar/U. For routed CZ time T_CZ it
costs 34 T_CZ+544 pi hbar/U. All other blocks hold during the entire
word. Every elementary physical error also accumulates; compilation does
not turn physical gate error into the reported synthesis error.

## 6. Correlated-history composition is earned from the actual source

During a gate pause center-to-outer hopping. Permit any retained source
Hamiltonian built from central variables, N_o, collective outer spin and
apparatus operators. Every number-conserving spin-independent outer
control commutes with these system factors. Thus

\[
U_{\rm joint}(T)=U_{\rm ctrl}(T)U_{\rm source}(T)
\]

even when the source Hamiltonian at different times does not commute
with itself. Source readout can be included as a joint instrument.
At the complete gate endpoint the control is the logical gate tensor
identity on G, while the source is identity on L tensor an arbitrary
operation on G and retained apparatus. Resume the symmetric star
afterward. Induction gives the same factorization through arbitrary
repeated operations and admissible histories, including an initially
logical-gauge-reference correlated state.

Conditional records still require care: reading a pre-correlated gauge
can steer a selected logical ensemble. The trace-preserving operation
preserves the promised logical-reference channel. The full joint
operator factorization, not independence of all records, is the theorem.

representation_control.py verifies the physical commutators and the
full E tensor C^80 factor. It also displays a counterexample: symmetric
outer doublon energy is an invariant gauge operator, yet its commutator
with the sorter hopping has norm2. It must be included in the calibrated
control, not treated as an arbitrary unknown source term.

The hold also needs occupation conservation. A pair source that flips
|0> and |2> preserves the parity but causes different auxiliary evolution
conditional on the other block's charge. The saved counterexample has
coherence-error witness .0254 at pair amplitude .1 V. Correct conserved
parity is insufficient to justify a parity-only quantum gate.

## 7. Routing and electrostatics are part of the operation

A finite physical transport mechanism is available under stated controls.
For n identical particles in a rigid well translated by q(t), with
translation-invariant internal interactions, use

\[
H(t)=\sum_{k=1}^n[p_k^2/(2m)+V(x_k-q(t))
-m\ddot q(t)\cdot x_k+\mu(t)]+W(\{x_k-x_l\}).
\]

The accelerated-frame solution is exact. At zero endpoint velocities,
transport equals spatial translation times ordinary internal evolution
and the number phase

\[
\exp\{-in[\tfrac m2\int|\dot q|^2dt+\int\mu dt]/\hbar\}.
\]

This works across n=0,1,2 and all internal states in the domain. The
number phase can be paid or cancelled by a real scalar potential.
For q=L(10s^3-15s^4+6s^5), max acceleration is
10L/(sqrt3 T_tr^2), and the kinetic phase is5mL^2/(7hbar T_tr).
Thus a force budget Fmax requires T_tr >= sqrt[10mL/(sqrt3 Fmax)].
This is established compensated-transport machinery, extended here by
the elementary many-particle accelerated-frame calculation. It does not
remove forces/polarization due to the other unknown charges. Addressable
forces, finite barriers, source isolation and neighboring fields remain
physical qualifications.

No perfect capacitive off switch or exact rational V/U is needed for the
diagonal travel stage. Let the real out-and-back path contribute duration
T0 and area Phi0. Supply two parking separations with Vn>Vf>=0. Choose W
by rounding 2 pi hbar/(Vn-Vf) upward to the onsite clock satisfying
U(T0+W)/hbar=2 pi m. The interval [VfW/hbar,VnW/hbar] then has length at
least2 pi and contains some pi-Phi0+2 pi k. Positive near/far waiting
times solve the corresponding linear equation. In particular

\[
W<2\pi\hbar/(V_n-V_f)+2\pi\hbar/U.
\]

All approach and return phase is included. Constant U during rigid motion
and held occupations are required. Separate nonconstant U profiles would
require additional acquired areas and controls.

For two grounded planes, the earlier positive screened kernel gives a
full pair norm <=4 v2(surface separation). For eight held orbitals,
27 unwanted pairs contribute at most108 v2(Rmin). This includes leakage
charge and both internal and external spectators unless a gauge form was
proved. The earlier lattice incident sum is added for other modules.

An infinite lead can be included with a supplied finite number Q of
allowed electrons per cell and cell distances >=R0+kb:

\[
\|H_{\rm data,lead}\|\le
N_{\rm data}Q\,v_2(R_0)/(1-e^{-q_0b}).
\]

Two-dimensional shell counting gives the squared denominator. This
requires a real finite-band/density domain and a screened feed geometry;
an unmodeled aperture or unlimited continuum charge cannot be discarded.

### Simultaneous phase closure when several local charge pairs matter

The two-parking argument does not erase a coupling during a noncommuting
sorter. For example, leaving the intended edge at the illustrative far
separation5h during the two Gaussian stages gives a conservative action
of order4e-3 from that edge alone. It cannot be discarded under the
27-other-pair bound. Farther parking, a real switch, or an explicit
control treatment is required.

There is a constructive finite solution for all *diagonal* phase areas.
Let F1,...,Fk be commuting operators with integer spectra (common
doublon number and the relevant occupation products). Let k parking
configurations give an invertible acquired coefficient matrix A,
including their actual positive Coulomb coefficients. Let phi be the
complete fixed-transport phase vector and theta the desired vector.
For a positive slack s choose

\[
b_i=\pi\sum_j|(A^{-1})_{ij}|+s,
\quad z=\operatorname{round}[(Ab-\theta+\phi)/(2\pi)],
\quad t=A^{-1}(\theta-\phi+2\pi z).
\]

Every t_i is positive, and t_i<=2 pi sum_j|(A^-1)_ij|+s.
Indeed every component of the rounded phase error is at most pi, so
|t_i-b_i|<=pi sum_j|(A^-1)_ij|. The endpoint is exactly exp(-i sum theta_j
Fj) on every charge history in which these occupations remain conserved.
No inverse time or zero capacitance is supplied.

Full rank can be earned from screened geometry. Use one all-far
configuration and one configuration per pair, bringing only that pair
close. Subtract the all-far column. If each desired close coupling is at
least Vmin and all other couplings are at most vsmall, the resulting
pair matrix is strictly diagonally dominant when m*vsmall<Vmin, for m
pair rows. The common onsite row completes the matrix. The previous
positive-kernel screening law supplies that separation condition.

positive_phase_clock.py implements four active ports and six pair rows
plus a common onsite row from the same disk density. It integrates every
out-and-back path. Three smooth axis-aligned transport stages have a
proved clearance: lift the selected pair to opposite lanes, translate
horizontally, then bring it to its final separation. No other parked
port is crossed. This is an explicit geometric path with its required
force budget, not an asserted laboratory transport speed. The selected
model has phase-matrix condition number about212. Its full travel time
is480 hbar h/K before positive parking waits. The code checks every
joint occupation pattern0,1,2.

This compiler can also correct a known pair-capacitance phase on a
sorter. For isolated sorter pairs, write

\[
H_{\rm pair}=(U_o-V_s)(D_i+D_j)-gK
+\tfrac{V_s}{2}N_{ij}(N_{ij}-1).
\]

The last term commutes with the sorter. At U*=Uo-Vs and T=6 pi hbar/U*,
the extra active-pair phase is gamma(Di+Dj+ni nj), gamma=Vs T/hbar.
An untouched identical orbital has onsite area Uo T/hbar=6 pi+gamma,
so the leftover local phases combine into a common doublon area plus
known density-pair areas. The diagonal compiler can cancel those.
This avoids calling a pair-only capacitance gauge-only or requiring
an exact rational Vs/Uo. Corrections must be placed on the appropriate
sides of noncommuting stages; arbitrary diagonal phases cannot simply
be moved through a sorter.

The broader clock increases movement, acquisition and stability costs.
It needs its actual coefficient matrix and path phases. It does not

particle exchange during the diagonal stage, or charge outside the
included ports and full incident bound. It is a finite physical phase
compiler in the stated number-conserving model, not a claim that the
five-stage ideal CZ is a complete routed device operation.

## 9. A physically different, cheaper-control alternative

For one qualified spin flavor, the same four-site star has

\[
\mathcal H_E=\mathbb C_L^2\otimes\mathcal F(c_0,b),
\qquad\dim G=4,
\]

where b is the symmetric bright mode. E occurs precisely when one of the
two dark modes is occupied. Here P_ij=I-2n_- is quadratic. Equal leaf
potential shifts plus hopping supply n_- directly, and its logical
rotation commutes with every invariant source. No spinful doublon clock
is involved. Spin polarization and one-orbital loading are extra physical
qualifications; this is not the E tensor C^80 hardware with fewer errors.

A full capacitance matrix with equal row and column sums has a useful
projected cancellation. On each valid block,

\[
Pn_iP=(I+n_b+Z_i)/3,\qquad\sum_iZ_i=0.
\]

All logical-gauge cross terms cancel. Coaxial equilateral triangles give
H_L=delta V(XX+ZZ)/6. Identical dark offsets fail: the sector exchange
(1,1)<->(0,2) is resonant and has an exact2/3 leakage-amplitude witness at
the useful gate time, independently of how weak V is made.

With staggered offsets Delta(N_EA+2N_EB), the desired sector is exactly
K=3. Suppose the retained residual after subtracting commuting gauge-only
terms has spectral half-width B<Delta/2. Let g=Delta-2B and w=||QVP||.
A single Sylvester conjugation gives, restoring hbar explicitly,

\[
\|(e^{-iHT/\hbar}-e^{-iH_dT/\hbar})P\|
\le\frac{2\sqrt2w}{g}
+\frac{\sqrt2Tw^2}{\hbar g}e^{2\sqrt2w/g}.
\]

Proof: split Q into spectral intervals above and below P; the ordered
Sylvester bounds give ||S||<=sqrt2 w/g. Choose[S,Hd]=-W. The BCH
remainder is bounded by||S|| w exp(2||S||). Boundary conjugations cost
at most2||S|| and Duhamel gives the time term. PHP factors into logical
plus gauge dynamics by the balanced geometry.

This permits many fast internal gauge cycles. The recorded finite model
has a uniform product-isometry error3.00e-4 with nearly599 gauge-hopping
cycles in the selected time units, and an analytic bound1.03e-3. It is
not an infinite-bath theorem. A broad source can absorb the dark offset;
the earlier local-orbit bounds cannot simply be named as a replacement
for a derived local-bath gap or prethermal argument.

Large exactly uniform charging can be removed from this bandwidth
budget. A tetrahedral four-site geometry has Uc N_block(N_block-1)/2,
which commutes with closed-block controls; uniform interblock monopole
coupling also commutes. Residual nonuniform terms and an open source
remain charged. Full derivation, table and failure witnesses are in
alternatives/DERIVATION.md.

### 9.1 An arbitrarily broad central source can be factored out

The preceding bandwidth condition can be removed from the *actual central
source*, without freezing the source or preparing the bright occupation.
The enabling operation is to close center-to-leaf hopping during the
capacitive segment. The source may continue exchanging particles with
the central orbital. The distinction between central and bright exchange
is essential.

Let NoA,NoB denote total outer occupations, and let the source algebra be
generated by central creation/annihilation operators, their reservoirs,
and NoA,NoB. Allow arbitrary time-dependent interactions within that
algebra. This includes central tunneling to retained leads, arbitrary
interacting lead Hamiltonians, uniform center--leaf capacitance,
center--center capacitance, uniform outer monopole interactions, and
the equal outer pair repulsion Uo No(No-1)/2. Fermionic source terms are
understood as physical even operators, with the usual parity ordering.

The active Hamiltonian is

\[
 H_a=\Delta(N_E^A+2N_E^B)+\delta V D,\qquad
 D=\sum_{i=1}^3 n_i^A n_i^B.
\]

Every source generator commutes with H_a. In particular, H_a contains
only even leaf operators and conserves each No; central creation and
annihilation need not commute with the source's own Hamiltonian. Hence,
for any well-defined source propagator, even with an unbounded source
Hamiltonian,

\[
 U(T)=U_s(T)e^{-iH_aT/\hbar}.
\]

This is exact operator commutation of the bounded active system with
the source algebra, not a global spectral-gap assertion about the bath.
On the valid E tensor E input, U_s acts as identity on both logical
qubits and as an arbitrary joint unitary on gauge, source and retained
apparatus. No source energy cutoff, cooling, initial product state or
slow-source approximation enters this factorization. The active device
still needs its stated one-spin, one-orbital domain. Nonuniform fields
and hopping from the source to bright or dark modes are not in the
commuting source algebra.

Permutation invariance alone is insufficient here. A source-dependent
bright energy n_b tensor B is invariant but generally does not commute
with D. Source-induced symmetric leaf hopping or correlated hopping has
the same problem. Such terms arise as possible corrections to a
microscopic localized-orbital density model and must be derived or
bounded. Closing the center link does not remove them by symmetry.

For a physical lead, restricting its system coupling to these operators
is itself an energy-domain qualification. Spin filtering, orbital
selection, source bandwidth or a justified small excitation amplitude
must prevent loading an excluded spin or orbital. The theorem does not
infer that microscopic qualification from a projected creation operator.
It removes a bound on the retained source dynamics within the qualified
coupling model, not the need to derive that model from a real source.

The actual aligned-triangle capacitance is

\[
 V_{ij}=V_{off}+\delta V\,\delta_{ij}.
\]

Its Voff NoA NoB part is in the source algebra; the δV D part is the
active interaction. Center--leaf entries are uniform by the same physical
rotational symmetry, including finite identically rotated orbital
profiles. The capacitance magnitudes are still acquired physical
integrals. A name for the geometry does not supply their values or their
stability.

#### The improved active gap and explicit bound

On an encoded block, No=1+n_b is either 1 or 2. H_a preserves No, so it
suffices to work on the invariant domain NoA,NoB in {1,2}. On a fixed
number sector, D counts the overlap of two subsets of a three-element
set. Its minimum and maximum are

\[
 D_{min}=\max(0,NoA+NoB-3),\qquad
 D_{max}=\min(NoA,NoB).
\]

Each of the four allowed number sectors has Dmax-Dmin=1. Define the
commuting gauge scalar c(NoA,NoB)=(Dmin+Dmax)/2. It equals 1/2 for
(1,1),(1,2),(2,1), and 3/2 for (2,2). Subtracting δV c therefore leaves

\[
 \|\delta V(D-c)\|=|\delta V|/2.
\]

Since K=N_EA+2N_EB has eigenvalue 3 only at (N_EA,N_EB)=(1,1), the
Sylvester comparison in section 9 now has

\[
 g=\Delta-|\delta V|>0,\qquad
 w=\sqrt2|\delta V|/3.
\]

The latter norm follows from the contact-projector eigenvalues in each
outer-number sector; it is also independently checked on the complete
256-state active Fock space. At the useful entangling time

\[
 T={3\pi\hbar\over2|\delta V|},\qquad
 U_L=\exp[-i\,{\operatorname{sgn}(\delta V)\pi\over4}
                 (X_AX_B+Z_AZ_B)],
\]

there is a joint gauge/source unitary V_GS such that

\[
 \boxed{\|(U(T)-(U_L\otimes V_{GS}))P\|
 \le f(r)={r\over3(1-r)}
 \left[4+\sqrt2\pi\exp\!\left({4r\over3(1-r)}\right)\right],
 \quad r={|\delta V|\over\Delta}<1.}
\]

Here the product is interpreted on the encoded input isometry. In
particular f(r)=2.8142943 r+O(r²). The norm statement extends unchanged
after tensoring an arbitrary reference and places no bound on the
central reservoir's bandwidth, interaction strength or number of cycles.
The source can be broad because it cannot supply a matrix element that
changes the leaf evolution, not because a broad bath has been declared
gapped.

#### Finite local pulses and link closure do not require a Coulomb switch

The physical capacitance need not turn off outside the entangling dwell.
For every NoA,NoB in {0,1,2,3}, the overlap-count interval for D has
width at most one. The same function c=(Dmin+Dmax)/2 therefore gives

\[
 \|D-c(NoA,NoB)\|\le1/2
\]

on the entire two-block spinless Fock space, not only the input domain.
During local gates or closure of the central link, include δV c in the
invariant gauge Hamiltonian. That Hamiltonian may have arbitrary
center--bright hopping and source motion. Each local n_- control commutes
with it, since it commutes with every invariant Hamiltonian. Treat only
δV(D-c) as the unwanted residual. A finite interval of duration tau then
costs at most |δV| tau/(2 hbar), independent of the source rate.

Thus a local pulse of area alpha generated by lambda n_- has its actual
duration tau=|alpha| hbar/|lambda| and incurs this additional capacitive
bound. Closing/reopening the center--bright link and ramping the dark
offsets cost the same bound during their actual durations; the ideal
ramps themselves preserve E. Out-of-domain orbital excitation during
those ramps remains a separate microscopic qualification. The main
entangling dwell still uses the sharper f(r) comparison.

For auxiliary pulse/switch time Taux in a two-block operation, the
complete modeled bound is therefore

\[
 \epsilon\le f(r)+{|\delta V|T_{aux}\over2\hbar}
                 +{1\over\hbar}\int_0^{T_{full}}\|H_{err}(t)\|dt,
 \quad T_{full}={3\pi\hbar\over2|\delta V|}+T_{aux}.
\]

No always-off capacitance or instantaneous local pulse is supplied by
notation. An array also needs its actual spectator incident sum during
all gates, preparation and waiting. Balanced long-range terms can be
factored only when their operator form earns that step; the remaining
tails must be screened, physically routed or explicitly refocused with
the finite pulse cost retained. The two-block result alone is not an
unpriced arbitrary-connectivity architecture.

#### Physical residuals, finite tolerance and repeated use

Uniform internal charging does not enter f(r); nonuniform residuals do.
Let H_err(t) be the complete remaining interaction on the qualified
energy domain and let

\[
 \eta={1\over\hbar}\int_0^{T_{full}}\|H_{err}(t)\|\,dt.
\]

After including loading, ramps, sensing, idles and return in their actual
locations, a segment comparison costs at most f(r)+|δV|Taux/(2 hbar)+eta
plus separately
qualified preparation/readout error. No raw spin orientation outside
the spinless domain is protected. No claim of uniform protection against
an unbounded nonuniform bath coupling follows from this theorem.

For residual center--bright tunneling -sqrt(3)t_off(c0†b+h.c.), the
two-block action is bounded by

\[
 {\sqrt3\over\hbar}\int
 (|t_{A,off}|+|t_{B,off}|)\,dt.
\]

For direct spinless density errors, a safe full-domain contribution is
sum_ij |delta V_ij|, because ||n_i n_j||=1, with all incident neighbors,
leakage states in the permitted domain and occupied source channels
included. The previous screened Green-function and incident-sum
construction can bound such coefficients when its actual boundary,
orbital and occupancy hypotheses hold. It is not replaced by an assigned
small residual. Departures from equal row and column sums create real
logical--bright correlations and must be counted this way.
Off-diagonal orbital/source couplings also belong in that incident sum;
their smallness cannot be inferred from a bound on diagonal capacitance
alone. For example a projected source term O tensor B contributes at
most ||O|| ||B|| to this sufficient norm budget on its qualified source
domain, including all occupied incident channels.

The duration/error tradeoff is explicit. If a time-independent residual
norm sigma bounds the active segment, with other costs kept separately,

\[
 \epsilon\le f(r)+{3\pi\over2}{\sigma/\Delta\over r}.
\]

For small r, the sufficient bound is minimized near
r=sqrt[(3pi/2)(sigma/Delta)/2.8142943], and its leading minimum is
7.2834 sqrt(sigma/Delta). Thus weakening the capacitance forever is not
a remedy for fixed symmetry breaking. This is a conservative sufficient
tradeoff, not a lower bound, an achieved material ratio or a fault-
tolerance threshold. Stronger informed pulse shaping can improve the
actual error but must receive and pay for the same model and calibration.

Local gates may restore symmetric center--bright hopping; their exact
permutation form commutes with every invariant source. Each entangler
closes only that link while leaving the center/source live. For m useful
gates, telescoping gives sum_j[f(r_j)+|δV_j|Taux_j/(2 hbar)+eta_j]
in joint isometry norm for
arbitrary allowed memory/reference histories. It does not assume the
actual intermediate state remains perfectly in the code. The ideal
comparison preserves the code, so prior leakage is already covered by
the earlier term in the telescoping sum. A scalable correction claim
still needs the supplied local fault model, logical initialization and
actual leakage-removal/readout operations.

The endpoint is a reusable logical gate mechanism with a physically
identified open-source algebra. It removes gauge renewal and total bath
bandwidth from that mechanism. It retains an honest charge-isolation,
geometry, polarization and energy-domain qualification.

Calibration may now interrogate the useful logical operation without
preparing its charge gauge. That does not acquire deltaV, Delta or the
control relation for free. Robust phase estimation is an equally
available conventional option: Kimmel--Low--Yoder, arXiv:1502.02677v3,
includes the 2021 erratum correcting the branch-error window from
pi/(2k) to pi/(3k). One must use the corrected analysis, not the older
constants. If the only certified nonideal-operation bound is epsilon
per repetition, a k-fold coherent probe has the sufficient additive
probability-error budget k epsilon plus preparation/readout errors.
Its interrogation time is k Tfull and source/control stability is needed
throughout. Such probes can reduce preparation count when this budget
is small enough; they are not automatically certified for unbounded k.
The linear composition bound is sufficient, not an information-theoretic
limit on more informed estimation or shaped controls.

## 10. Initialization, measurement, correction and claim boundary

Logical state preparation is still needed. Invariant central sources
cannot manufacture E from the vacuum. The earlier qualified singlet-pair
factory is one possible initialized resource; its fill, return, sensor,
holding and qualification costs must be retained in a new layout.

For the distinct spinless alternative, a singlet factory is not its
loading procedure. A concrete finite preparation is to empty a fresh
module, load one verified polarized electron on leaf 1, isolate the
central and third-leaf hopping, apply a resonant leaf-1/leaf-2 beam
splitter for time pi hbar/(4g), then apply a leaf-2 number phase. The
state can thereby be made (c1†-c2†)|vac>/sqrt(2), exactly one dark
particle. These are ordinary single-particle potential and tunneling
operations while the block is being initialized, not an invariant
source magically preparing E. The fresh source, emptying, charge
verification, phase control and spin/orbital filtering are required
resources. No preparation time or error for a device is assigned here.
Subsequent symmetric loading may make the center/bright gauge arbitrary
without another logical preparation. Data gates do not repeat the
emptying procedure.

A destructive spinless logical measurement can close the central link,
sort the selected antisymmetric leaf mode into a physical leaf by an
ordinary two-mode single-particle rotation, and measure that occupation
with a qualified charge detector. The ideal effect is the logical
transposition projector tensor identity on the gauge. Equal internal
pair charging commutes with this number-conserving mode rotation.
Other charge records must be discarded/coarse-grained as specified;
their statistics are not automatically logical-only. Detector renewal
and sorting time remain part of the measurement cost. A destructive
probability measurement does not preserve an arbitrary measured block.

For ideal coherent computation the new gates leave each gauge arbitrary;
there is no seven-state renewal between data operations. A final
destructive charge-parity measurement gives logical Z probabilities.
That is not automatically a nondemolition instrument. Reusable syndrome
measurement may instead use a fresh E ancilla, the supplied controlled
logical operations and a destructive ancilla measurement, with actual
source and detector errors charged.

The familiar teleportation leakage-reduction circuit applies only after
its operations are supplied: prepare an E Bell pair B,C, interact A with
B, destructively Bell-measure A,B, and correct C. For valid A it transfers
the logical state; for leaked A, C remains in E because its native gates
preserve that component even beside a bad neighboring irrep. The new
alphabet can approximate the real H, CNOT and Pauli operations needed by
this circuit. Their compilation error, fresh-source throughput, readout
and extra blocks are additional costs. There is no claim that gauge
cooling, leakage reduction and logical initialization are the same task.

That exact neighboring-irrep statement applies to the full-spinful
permutation/parity alphabet. The spinless projected cancellation beside
N_E=0 or 2 is not by itself a complete leakage-reduction gadget: its
off-sector interaction and the different input sectors need their own
fault-path analysis. The finite-circuit product-isometry theorem does
not silently supply such a threshold construction.

With finite-dimensional allowed domains and a bounded full incident
residual action, ordinary norm composition gives finite-circuit error
bounds, including arbitrary gauge/reference histories. A scalable
fault-tolerance claim additionally needs the actual local fault-path
model, supplied correction gadgets and source/meter qualification.
No numerical threshold or device region is asserted here.

## 11. Comparison and scientific consequence

Fermionic hopping plus interaction-assisted computation, parity phase
identities, noiseless subsystem control and real simulation are established.
The working contribution is the physical full-gauge interface: exact
orbital parity conversion, a certified finite fixed-U sorter, discrete
computational completeness, retained-source composition, and full
travel/charge-exposure laws. The cheap spinless alternative has different
source and noise promises and receives an explicit comparison.

Equally informed conventional controls may use the same extended orbital
model, spectral information, geometry, pulse optimizer and real compiler.
The observed charge-clock sensitivity may favor ordinary singlet-only,
charge-quadrupole, capacitive or cavity gates in a real device. Optical
heralding keeps its actual loss/retry advantage and pays its local sources,
detectors and waiting memory. No raw segment count establishes dominance.

For example, SAGE supplies genuine exchange-based one- and two-qubit
operations and Pauli-blockade readout on a singlet-only encoding; its
entangler has a small interqubit/intraqubit exchange ratio and actual
leakage and charge-noise calculations. This is a strong comparison,
not an abstract logical oracle. The new spinless guarantee is different:
arbitrary allowed central/bright occupations and a specified active
source, with polarization and electric geometry qualification instead
of singlet magnetic protection. Longitudinal cavity gates have explicit
curvature, damping and photon-noise analyses that cannot be discarded
when comparing to the former ideal loop. Barrett--Kok double heralding
can convert loss into reduced success probability in its qualified
optical model; dark counts, mode mismatch and memory decoherence still
affect the accepted resource. It receives the same local-control and
calibration improvements wherever they physically apply.

The exact organizing change is that computational completeness can be
earned over a dirty charge representation by a small discrete physical
alphabet, even when continuous logical generators are obstructed. The
physical comparison then identifies interaction-clock stability and
orbital reduction as the next constraints, and supplies a spinless route
that removes the first of them under a stronger loading qualification.
For the latter route, closing the center--bright link during the entangler
also gives a source-bandwidth-independent theorem. Its usefulness comes
from the specific microscopic source algebra and the balanced physical
capacitance, not from assuming an abstract protected logical interaction.
