GROUPED ACQUISITION OF AN ADAPTIVE-RISK-COMPETITIVE QUADRATIC PREDICTOR

P1 continuation, independent acquisition companion, 30 October 2026 UTC.
This is an internally derived construction, not historical-priority clearance
or a claim of practical superiority. It changes the previous scalar-query
dependence on ambient dimension. It does not provide cheap acquisition of an
unknown Gaussian spectral model.

1. QUESTION AND ACCESS CONTRACT

An unknown real quadratic target on independent uniform signs is promised:

  f(x)=c+sum_i a_i x_i+sum_{i<j} b_ij x_i x_j,   x in {-1,1}^n.

The offline learner may submit a COMPLETE chosen Boolean vector and receive
its exact scalar value f(x). No coefficients, gradient, support list or
group-energy oracle is initially supplied. In deployment a fresh X has
independent uniform signs, and observing coordinate X_i costs one observation.
The comparison class contains every adaptive policy whose expected number
of distinct observations is at most k. Let R_ad(k) denote the infimal Bayes
squared prediction error in that class.

Chosen training evaluations and their complete input vectors are distinct
resources from observing coordinates of a deployed X. All must be charged.
In particular, a public simulator with chosen inputs does not give a direct
measurement of f(X) at an unknown deployed state.

For 0<epsilon<=1 and k>0, the construction below outputs a fixed coordinate
set S of size at most min(n,ceil(288 k/epsilon^2)) and a quadratic predictor
using only X_S. With probability at least1-alpha over offline acquisition,

  E[(f(X)-predictor(X_S))^2] <= (1+epsilon) R_ad(k).

The scalar-query count is

  O((1+k/epsilon^2)(1+log n)log(n/alpha)
      +(1+k/epsilon^2)^2+epsilon^(-1)log(1/alpha)),

before the optional full-interpolation fallback. No coefficient sparsity,
sign constraint, known active support or positive lower bound on nonzero
coefficients is assumed. Exact scalar values are substantive. Under noise,
the same group measurements have an explicit signal-floor cost, but the
purely multiplicative zero-risk conclusion is impossible uniformly; Section7
states this limitation instead of hiding it.

The proof combines ordinary Fourier/ANOVA machinery, a constructed positive
group-energy estimator, best-first tree search, a residual-dependent stopping
rule, and the preceding P1 risk comparison. Group testing itself is established
machinery. The new candidate contribution is the complete joint contract and
its risk-dependent stopping/counting argument, not an invention of grouping.

2. FOUR BOOLEAN VALUES CONSTRUCT ADDITIVE INFLUENCE MASS

Define I_i=a_i^2+sum_{j!=i}b_ij^2. For a coordinate group A let

  J(A)=sum_{i in A}I_i,
  C(A)=sum_i a_i^2 1{i in A}
       +sum_{i<j}b_ij^2 1{{i,j} intersects A}.

J is additive on disjoint groups. C is the remaining MSE after observing every
coordinate outside A. For every group, C(A)<=J(A)<=2 C(A).

Draw independent uniform signs x and fresh replacement signs on A. Let y
equal x outside A and the replacement signs inside A. Let F_A flip all signs
in A. Make four actual scalar queries at x,F_A x,y,F_A y, and compute

  u=[f(x)-f(F_A x)]/2,
  v=[f(x)+f(F_A x)-f(y)-f(F_A y)]/2.

Then

  Z_J=u^2+v^2,                  E Z_J=J(A),
  Z_C=u^2+v^2/2,                E Z_C=C(A).               (1)

Proof. Split f=p(x_out)+ell(x_A,x_out)+q(x_A), where ell consists of the
linear terms in A and edges crossing from A to its complement, while q is
the homogeneous quadratic polynomial on internal edges of A. Flipping A
changes ell's sign and preserves p,q. Thus u=ell and v=q(x_A)-q(y_A).
Orthogonality gives E u^2=L_A+E_cross and E v^2=2 E_internal. Their sum is
exactly the sum of coordinate influences; half the second term instead gives
the support-coverage energy. Arbitrarily signed coefficients cause no
cancellation in these expectations. There is no positive-coefficient promise.

This is an acquired energy operation, not an assumed set-sum oracle. Each
context costs four complete source evaluations. A raw scalar sum of signed
interactions would not have this noncancellation property.

3. A SCALE-FREE RELATIVE VARIANCE BOUND

Both nonnegative estimators in(1) satisfy

  E Z^2 <= 9(E Z)^2,    Var(Z)<=8(E Z)^2.                (2)

The constant9 is asymptotically sharp for this particular estimator. The
argument is given explicitly rather than invoking an unpriced moment oracle.

First, an affine Rademacher linear form w=b+sum_j c_j X_j has

  E w^4 =3(E w^2)^2-2b^4-2sum_j c_j^4 <=3(E w^2)^2.

Conditional on x_out, u is a centered linear form in x_A. Therefore

  E[u^4|x_out] <=3(sum_{i in A} d_i(x_out)^2)^2,

where each d_i is affine. Cauchy-Schwarz and the affine fourth-moment bound
give E(sum_i d_i^2)^2<=3(sum_i E d_i^2)^2. Hence

  E u^4 <=9(E u^2)^2.                                  (3)

For a homogeneous quadratic q=sum_{i<j}b_ij X_iX_j, put Q=E q^2 and let B
be the symmetric zero-diagonal matrix with offdiagonal entries b_ij. Direct
expansion over even-degree four-edge multigraphs gives

  E q^4=3Q^2-2sum_e b_e^4+24sum_{undirected four-cycles} product_e b_e.

Also

  tr(B^4)=2sum_e b_e^4+4sum_{two-edge wedges}b_e^2 b_f^2
          +8sum_{undirected four-cycles}product_e b_e.

Since tr(B^4)<=(tr(B^2))^2=4Q^2, substitution yields E q^4<=15Q^2. The
two q-values in v are independent and centered, so

  E v^4=2E q^4+6Q^2<=36Q^2=9(E v^2)^2.                (4)

Cauchy-Schwarz applied to the mixed term now proves

  E(u^2+t v^2)^2 <=9(E u^2+t E v^2)^2

for t>=0, including t=1 and t=1/2. This proves(2).

For sharpness take all coefficients equal to1 on the complete graph, no
linear terms, and A=[n]. Here u=0 and

  E q^4/Q^2 =3-2/Q+72 choose(n,4)/Q^2 ->15.

Therefore E v^4/(E v^2)^2 ->9. This is a variance statement about the specified
estimator, not an information-theoretic lower bound for all estimators.

4. THE ACTUAL SEARCH AND ITS RISK GUARANTEE

For each requested mass or coverage estimate use a median of independent
block averages. A block of288 contexts has probability at most1/4 of error
exceeding one third of its mean, by(2) and Chebyshev. Take an odd number r of
blocks satisfying

  r>=8 log(6n/alpha).                                   (5)

There are at most2n-1 distinct node-mass tests in a binary index tree and at
most n+1 coverage tests. Conditional on all prior adaptive choices, fresh
contexts have the required law. Hoeffding and a union bound give simultaneous
relative1/3 accuracy for every used test with probability at least1-alpha/2.
True zero energy gives identically zero observations, even at a finite sample
count, in this exact-value contract.

Maintain a balanced binary tree of index intervals. Its frontier partitions
the unselected coordinates. At each frontier node A store

  U_A=(3/2) Jhat(A),   so on the success event J(A)<=U_A<=2J(A).

For the current selected set S, estimate the coverage of its complement and
store

  R_low=(3/4) Chat([n]\\S),  so R(S)/2<=R_low<=R(S).

Use a maximum-priority queue for U_A. Set L=min(n,ceil(288k/epsilon^2)).
For an unconditional query budget, also set

  E_max=min(n-1,floor(h(L+768k/epsilon^2))), h=ceil(log2 n).

ALGORITHM
1. Start S empty; acquire root J and initial remaining coverage.
2. If the frontier is empty, stop.
3. If max_A U_A <= epsilon^2 R_low/(96k), stop by the residual certificate.
4. Pop the maximal-U node. If it is an internal node, split it and acquire
   its two child masses, unless doing so would exceed E_max expansions. In
   that case stop with a failed-acquisition flag. If it is a leaf, add that
   coordinate to S.
5. If |S|=L, stop by the ranked-prefix guarantee. Otherwise, after selecting
   a leaf update the remaining-coverage estimate and continue.

Every source query and operation is prescribed. No optimal adaptive policy,
decomposition oracle or exact influence vector is used.

We give the needed residual facts to make this proof self-contained. For any
fixed S and any adaptive policy pi with expected count<=k, let R_pi denote
its Bayes MSE, p_i its query probabilities, and M=max_{i outside S}I_i. A
quadratic has derivative fourth moment<=3I_i^2. At each terminal history,
the sum of influences over unobserved coordinates is at most twice the
conditional variance. Consequently,

  2R_pi >=sum_i E[1{i unqueried}(D_i f)^2].              (6)

Cauchy-Schwarz bounds the missing queried contribution by sqrt(3p_i)I_i.
The remaining contribution is nonnegative, so its lower bound can be clipped
to I_i(1-sqrt(3p_i))_+. This positive part matters when p_i>1/3.
If every selected coordinate has I_i>=M/2, then

  2R_pi >=(M/2)(|S|-sqrt(3|S|k)).                      (7)

To compare arbitrary S, reveal S for free and then follow pi's original
decisions. The outside-coordinate gain is at most

  sqrt(3)sum_{i outside S} I_i sqrt(p_i)
    <=sqrt(3 (sum_{outside S}I_i)(sum_{outside S}I_i p_i))
    <=sqrt(6 R(S) M k).

Thus

  R(S)<=R_pi+sqrt(6 R(S) M k).                         (8)

For a selected leaf, its upper bound was at least every remaining group's
upper bound, and it was at most2I_i. Every remaining coordinate's influence
is below its group's mass. Hence each selected I_i is at least half of every
currently unselected influence. The property persists as more coordinates
are selected. If the cap is reached, combine(7),(8), or use the preceding
P1 approximate-ranking theorem with eta=1/3 and accuracy epsilon/2, to obtain

  R(S)<=(1+epsilon/2) R_ad(k).                          (9)

Explicitly, (7) gives M<=4R_pi/(L-sqrt(3Lk)); substituting into(8) gives
z<=1+A sqrt(z), with A^2=24k/(L-sqrt(3Lk)). The stated L is sufficient for
z<=1+epsilon/2. Zero R_pi forces M=0 and hence R(S)=0. If L=n, observing
all coordinates gives zero risk directly.

At a residual-certificate stop, M<=max_A U_A<=epsilon^2 R(S)/(96k). Equation(8)
then gives R_pi>=(1-epsilon/4)R(S). Since

  (1+epsilon/2)(1-epsilon/4)>=1  for 0<epsilon<=1,

(9) again follows. Both inequalities hold for every comparison policy, so
an attained optimizer need not exist.

5. WHY QUERY COST HAS NO RISK-DYNAMIC-RANGE FACTOR

Let S_* be the final selected set, R_*=R(S_*), and h=ceil(log2 n). Every
expanded internal node A obeyed, at its expansion time,

  U_A>epsilon^2 R_low/(96k),

so J(A)>epsilon^2 R_current/(384k)>=epsilon^2 R_*/(384k).
At any depth, expanded nodes are disjoint. At most|S_*|<=L of them intersect
S_*. The rest lie in the final unselected set, whose total additive influence
mass is at most2R_*. Thus at most768k/epsilon^2 such nodes occur at that depth.
If R_*=0, every node disjoint from S_* has zero mass and could never have
been expanded; the same count holds separately. Consequently,

  number of expansions E <=h(L+768k/epsilon^2).         (10)

There are1+2E node-mass tests and at most L+1 coverage tests. Every test costs
4*288*r scalar evaluations. This proves the grouped-acquisition query bound
without an unknown log(Var(f)/R_ad) or a search for a minimum signal size.
The argument relies on additive group mass; coverage alone does not give the
same exact disjoint-group accounting.

On the simultaneous calibration event this counting argument also applies
at every intermediate state and after any contemplated expansion. Thus the
E_max guard cannot fire on that event. Off that event the algorithm can
flag failed acquisition and still fit the current selected predictor; no
risk guarantee is asserted for that record. The guard makes the source-call
bound unconditional rather than only a high-probability complexity bound.

6. CONSTRUCT THE PREDICTOR WITHOUT AN O(n) INTERCEPT DESIGN

Only selected nonconstant coefficients are needed. Put x=(1,...,1). Exact
shared Boolean evaluations give

  b_ij=[f(x)-f(x^i)-f(x^j)+f(x^{ij})]/4,
  a_i =[f(x)-f(-x)-f(x^i)+f(-x^i)]/4.

For s=|S| this costs2+2s+choose(s,2) calls. Form q_S from these nonconstant
coefficients. The random residual f(X)-q_S(X_S) has mean c and variance R(S).
This subtraction is constructed and paid before invoking its low variance.

Set eta_c=epsilon/(2+epsilon). With blocks of ceil(4/eta_c) fresh uniform
inputs and an odd number r_c>=8log(2/alpha) of independent blocks, the median
of residual block means obeys

  |chat-c|^2<=eta_c R(S)

with conditional probability at least1-alpha/2. If R(S)=0 the residual is
exactly constant and this statement is exact. The final predictor is
chat+q_S(X_S), whose MSE is exactly R(S)+(chat-c)^2. Combining with(9),

  final MSE <=(1+eta_c)(1+epsilon/2)R_ad(k)
            =(1+epsilon)R_ad(k).

The two acquisition confidence events have combined failure probability at
most alpha. No unconditional multiplicative claim is made on failed training
records. The intercept is statistically acquired; it is not claimed exactly
known when R(S)>0.

This removes the previous exact pairwise-independent design's O(n) source
queries. The saving uses the desired deployment-risk scale, not an externally
supplied tiny mean-error tolerance or an unknown-risk oracle.

7. NOISE, PRECISION AND A DECISIVE ZERO-RISK OBSTRUCTION

For fresh scalar readout errors that are conditionally independent, mean zero
and of variance<=sigma^2 given the submitted inputs,
use TWO independent copies of the four-value experiment at each context.
Let (u+eta_u,v+eta_v) and (u+zeta_u,v+zeta_v) be the resulting pairs. Then

  Ztilde_J=(u+eta_u)(u+zeta_u)+(v+eta_v)(v+zeta_v)

is unbiased for J. The within-bank covariance matrix is bounded in Loewner
order by sigma^2 diag(1/2,1), since all four raw errors are independent. The
banks are independent. Conditional variance and(2) give

  Var(Ztilde_J)<=8J^2+2sigma^2 J+(5/4)sigma^4
                 <=8(J+sigma^2/2)^2.                  (11)

The analogous estimator with half the v cross product is unbiased for C and
has variance<=8C^2+sigma^2 C+sigma^4/2, also covered by the last bound with C.
No fourth moment of scalar readout noise is required.

For a chosen floor lambda>0 and relative parameter eta, a block size

  ceil[32 eta^(-2)(1+sigma^2/(2lambda))^2]

and the same median confidence argument guarantee

  |Mhat-M|<=eta max(M,lambda),   M=J or C.

Each context now costs EIGHT raw scalar calls. This is a complete noisy
measurement construction and cost, but it is NOT a proof that the exact
best-first algorithm above retains its pure multiplicative guarantee when
those floor-contaminated estimates are substituted. An additive-risk
extension needs its own stopping, ranking and coefficient-fit allowance.

For deterministic absolute scalar errors<=delta, the pair errors obey
|uhat-u|<=delta and |vhat-v|<=2delta. Thus

  |Zhat_J-Z_J|<=2sqrt(5)delta sqrt(Z_J)+5delta^2.

The same bound is safe for coverage. Averaging a block preserves the bound
with its exact block average on the right. If the exact block estimates M to
relative1/6, Young's inequality gives

  |Mhat-M|<=M/3+40delta^2=(M+120delta^2)/3.              (12)

A block size1152 and the ordinary median argument therefore acquire this
finite-precision floor even for bounded correlated/adversarial readout errors.
The floor is real and must propagate into the scientific risk conclusion.

There is a simple uniform obstruction. With deterministic scalar error bound
delta>0, the two promised quadratic targets f0=0 and f1=theta x_i, with
0<|theta|<=delta, can return the identical zero record at every chosen Boolean
input. Nevertheless their true targets differ in L2 by |theta|. Any identical
learned predictor incurs MSE at least theta^2/4 on one of them, even if all
deployment coordinates are provided. An informed one-coordinate policy has
zero target MSE for either. No finite-noise uniform pure multiplicative
zero-risk theorem follows from this access. Gaussian readout noise likewise
prevents exact continuous-parameter identification in a finite sample.

8. FULL COST AND REPRESENTATION BILL

Let N_E=1+2E+at most(L+1) denote energy tests. The explicit exact-source call
bill is at most

  4*288*r*N_E +2+2L+choose(L,2)
       +ceil[4(2+epsilon)/epsilon]*r_c.

Every complete input submission costs n sign coordinates in an ordinary dense
interface, so input traffic is O(n times scalar-call count). Target execution
cost T_f(n) also multiplies each scalar call. These are not sublinear-time
claims in n. A local-update or simulator-seed interface is a different resource.

Each four-value context uses n+|A| independent fair signs in the displayed
implementation. Intercept contexts use n. Random-bit generation is therefore
also charged. An optional exact eight-wise-independent generator would suffice
for the degree-eight moment calculations: evaluate a random degree-seven
polynomial over F_(2^r) at2n distinct field points and apply a fixed nonzero
binary linear functional, with r>=ceil(log2(2n)). Its8r random seed bits give
eight-wise independent signs. This reduces seed entropy but requires finite-
field generation of all n coordinates and does not remove input traffic. That
alternative is not implemented in the supplied code.

The binary frontier stores O(L log n) acquired group records in the successful
regime; a dense input buffer still costs O(n). A heap costs logarithmic work per
node operation. The selected predictor has O(L^2) coefficients and dense
deployment evaluation cost O(L^2). Source-dependent sparsity may reduce this
only after coefficients are actually acquired and found to vanish.

With dyadic/rational coefficients whose numerators and common denominator
have at most B bits, evaluation,
differences, squares, block sums and comparisons have polynomial bit complexity
in B, log n and sample counts. Arbitrary separate denominators require actual
denominator growth. Exact tiny influences can require many bits; their
amplitude-independent oracle-call count is not amplitude-independent numerical
or physical cost. The supplied code's paid source has small integer values,
and all block medians and final coefficients use exact rational arithmetic.

Ordinary full quadratic interpolation takes1+n+choose(n,2) scalar calls and
can be cheaper. It may be chosen as a fallback from known call bounds; it is
especially preferable when the observation cap reaches n. The stated result
does not claim optimal acquisition constants or superiority to every sparse-
learning, regression, interpolation or bounded-function method.

11. WHAT DOES NOT TRANSFER TO THE PARENT'S GAUSSIAN SPECTRAL OPENING

Unknown coordinate importance and unknown continuous spectral directions have
different acquisition costs. For f_v(x)=(v^T x)^2 with ||v||=1 and X standard
Gaussian, observing a fixed known L-dimensional span S gives exact residual
MSE2[1-||P_S v||^4]. A known v therefore gives a one-measurement zero-risk
solution. Under bounded-norm scalar model probes with fixed Gaussian readout
noise, learning an unknown orientation to fixed error has an information cost
scaling with ambient dimension; ordinary phase-retrieval/sphere-packing
arguments apply. No formal general lower-bound theorem for the parent's
full adaptive spectral protocol is claimed in this note. Unbounded real
queries, infinite-precision encodings and supplied explicit matrices are
different resources. This grouped Boolean acquisition does not provide a
cheap general Hessian or eigensystem oracle.
