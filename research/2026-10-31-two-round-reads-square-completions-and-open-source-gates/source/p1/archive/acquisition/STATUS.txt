Claim established in the ordinary proof:
For an unknown promised real quadratic on independent uniform signs, exact
scalar membership queries suffice to acquire a fixed-coordinate predictor
whose MSE is at most (1+epsilon) times the informed optimal adaptive risk
with expected coordinate budget k, with probability at least 1-alpha. The
fixed set has at most min(n,ceil(288 k/epsilon^2)) coordinates. The deterministic
source-query budget is

  O((1+k/epsilon^2)(1+log n)log(n/alpha)
     +(1+k/epsilon^2)^2+epsilon^(-1)log(1/alpha)).

The important acquisition operation is explicitly constructed from four
scalar values: squared odd-group response plus squared independent internal-
group response difference has mean equal to additive coordinate influence.
Its variance is at most eight times the squared mean, including arbitrary
signs and dense interactions. The same four values give exact residual-energy
estimation. Best-first search plus a residual stop gives the displayed bound
without an unknown dynamic-range or minimum-nonzero-coefficient term. The
algorithm has an explicit expansion cap; exceeding it is a failed acquisition
event, impossible on the simultaneous calibration event. Intercept acquisition
uses the already constructed selected predictor to reduce its variance to the
remaining deployment risk.

Source promise and costs:
The target's quadratic form and the uniform independent deployment law are
assumed, not inferred from these tests. Chosen full Boolean inputs are stronger
than passive random examples. Every call pays n submitted signs and actual
target execution; exact tiny coefficients require adequate value precision.
The predictor pays O(L^2) coefficients. No cheap unknown Gaussian spectral
model acquisition or sublinear ambient-dimension running time follows.

Noise boundary:
An unbiased eight-raw-call group estimator with explicit signal-floor sample
cost is proved. A bounded-precision floor is also proved. A fully propagated
noisy additive-risk algorithm is NOT claimed. Uniform pure multiplicative
zero-risk learning is impossible with the stated nonzero readout error, even
for an unknown one-coordinate linear target.

Evidence rerun after final audit:
128 exact group/model checks passed both energy identities and moment bounds.
8,192 exact readout-noise configurations passed the unbiasedness/variance test.
The complete n=16 diagnostic used fractional expected comparison budget k=.01,
selected three coordinates, and paid 1,140,791 scalar calls, 18,252,656 submitted
signs, 6,229,920 random signs and 4,563,164 source-term evaluations. Risk was
zero, against exact adaptive optimum 167/12. Ordinary full interpolation needs
137 calls here. The run is therefore explicit evidence of execution and cost,
not practical efficiency or a large-system benchmark.
