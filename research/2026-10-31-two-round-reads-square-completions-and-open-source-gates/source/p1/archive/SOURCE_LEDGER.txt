PRIMARY MATRIX COMPRESSION ANTECEDENT

matrices, Canadian Journal of Mathematics 9, 298-304.
https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1957-036-1
Primary publisher PDF:
https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/AA7ACF7F360A1D44FE4B83007198F57C/S0008414X00044655a.pdf/imbedding_conditions_for_hermitian_and_normal_matrices.pdf
Root and subagent read Theorem 1 on printed page 298 and the real-symmetric
orthogonal version at the end of Section 2, printed page 302. All possible
codimension-k compression spectra satisfy, and can attain, the interlacing
interval conditions. Selecting the admissible eigenvalues nearest zero gives
the signed-tail Frobenius minimum used here. Thus that matrix minimum is a
direct classical corollary, and our pair rotations are an explicit instance.
The remaining information-complexity question concerns adaptive Gaussian
transcripts, the final outcome-dependent read, budgets and noisy experiments.
