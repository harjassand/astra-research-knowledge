G_i=f-E_i f and I_i=E G_i^2. If E G_i^4 <= kappa I_i^2, the top
  L=min(n,ceil(3*kappa*d^2*k/epsilon^2))
influences form a fixed observation set with residual risk at most
(1+epsilon) times every adaptive policy of expected distinct-coordinate
budget at most k, for 0<epsilon<=1. This compares remaining Bayes squared
error, and does not require constructing an optimizing policy.

For an unbiased Boolean quadratic, kappa<=3. The retained sharper sufficient
budget is the smaller of ceil(36*k/epsilon^2) and
ceil((16*k/epsilon)*log(32*e/epsilon)), capped at n. The tail proof explicitly
requires the domain conditions L>=k and D>=k, and retains the nonnegative-part
correction. These are sufficient budgets for the specified selection rule;
they are not universal lower bounds for every fixed algorithm.
