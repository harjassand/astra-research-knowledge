## Assumptions, unsupported claims and coverage

The use checks followed the exact exchange-modulation counterexample and reproductive material-credit passage. More synchronized pulses cannot suppress nuisance in the same invariant exchange direction; inherited reserves and external import cannot be silently credited as reconstructed production. The selected biological and oxygen-control packets explicitly do not establish an acquired living implementation or measured recovery device. This is a statement about those packets, not a corpus-wide impossibility theorem.

The final snapshot normalizes 1,057 registered result records, 235 atlas entries, 75 selected reusable units and 46 current technology records. Topic labels overlap records and kinds: physics 163, chemistry 33, biology 6, materials 23, engineering 8 and learning 14. These are tagged-record counts, not independent-study counts or completeness measurements. Mathematics and quantum information dominate; biological/material/chemical mechanisms remain a selective slice, often conditional models or proposals. Only registered cited source windows are searched. Unregistered archives, withheld material and missing originals are not silently included; the denominator for all potentially extractable mechanisms is unknown.

The next substantive retrieval improvement is contract-aware ranking and explicit support/abstention assessment on independently chosen research tasks, preserving the full-content lexical comparator and the failures above. More indexing alone would not establish that improvement.
