ASTRA: CHAIN THEORY — COMPLETED SCIENTIFIC ASSESSMENT
25 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane

My judgment is to retain the exact finite construction and the useful quantum-network mathematics, but not sustain a dedicated Chain Theory fundamental-physics programme on the present evidence. A bounded return would be justified by a derived current-to-current observable with fixed normalization, a source-faithful new prediction, or a new operational capability. This investigation produced substantive closure theorems, countermodels and a completed information-processing construction; it did not derive electromagnetism or acquire a physical capability.

The strongest surviving opportunity is a support-aware calculus for composing entangled finite systems. We pursued its main obstruction through an exact admissibility criterion, a path/tree classification, LOCC resource bounds, and a complete measurement-channel repair. This is a concrete contribution even though it does not rescue the proposed electromagnetic interpretation. Historical novelty of these applications remains unestablished; the proofs are accepted here at their reconstructed mathematical scope.

2. The decisive physical interface

The strongest coherent physical reading is a candidate normalized internal residue, proposed to become the zero-transfer electromagnetic coupling after a photon identification. The letter explicitly leaves that identification conjectural and supplies no momentum-dependent propagator. Recognizing this scope does not make a completion impossible; we built completions to test what is still free.

The finite scalar has a valid ordinary quantum realization: on a 31,104-dimensional register take the maximally mixed state and an observable with the four record eigenvalues and their multiplicities, zero elsewhere. Its expectation is r. A valid probability effect is M/sqrt(6), whose probability is r/sqrt(6). This measures the defined statistic only. The auxiliary register preparation and measurement are additional operational choices. It is neither an exact single-shot outcome nor a measurement of electromagnetic alpha.

We then gave a sharper bridge nonuniqueness theorem. Fix the same unit charge Q, B(Q,Q)=1, and the same conserved current J. For each kappa>0 add the abelian actions

    L_bare    = -kappa F^2/4 - J.A,
    L_dressed = -kappa F^2/(4r) - J.A.

They have a massless transverse propagator and the same dressed/bare ratio r, while their Coulomb potential is

    V(R) = [r/(4 pi kappa)] q1 q2/R.

Thus their physical alpha is r/(4 pi kappa). Changing kappa with charge and current held fixed changes forces and scattering, not merely field names. Canonical photon normalization moves this coefficient to the vertex; a Ward identity does not remove it. The projection's probability unit and the generator's norm do not themselves determine a gauge kinetic coefficient. Equating the model's bare transmission probability to physical bare alpha=1 would select kappa=1/(4 pi); that is precisely the additional physical bridge equation whose derivation is owed.

These are conditional effective-field completions of the finite dictionary. They are not claimed to satisfy every microscopic axiom as complete worlds. Their force is that even granting the requested propagating field, pole, conserved current and ratio leaves a measurable normalization free. An actual completion must derive its action/current map from the admitted finite operations. A merely static finite statistic also cannot select scale response: positive massless-plus-massive spectral families share its pole residue but differ at finite momentum. A finite two-state Hamiltonian extension similarly preserves the exact static readout while allowing arbitrary oscillation frequency. That generator is an added operation, not a derived Chain act.

The link's six-dimensional representation is (3,2) with hypercharge 1/6; the source itself identifies these as quark-doublet quantum numbers. This does not establish a charged photon contradiction, since its center has not been mapped to electric charge. It identifies a concrete missing representation map to a neutral helicity-one collective mode. Finite local Hilbert spaces can support emergent photons in known quantum-link/spin constructions, but those supply Hamiltonians, constraints and controlled limits. They do not make this missing map automatic.

The exact expression matches the known low-energy constant closely. It was announced after high-precision alpha determinations were available. The final expression has no adjustable numerical parameter; the developmental search distribution and rejected constructions are not known. Consequently neither a probability of coincidence nor a likelihood ratio for the entire framework can be inferred from the digits alone. This is a known-number agreement plus a now-fixed, prospectively falsifiable sequence of further digits.

4. Original network result, carried through its obstruction

General join closure is a more immediate mathematical issue than the numerical constant. Two individually valid flat links need not compose into the type-required flat link. The simplest canonical example needs no disputed support rotation: two qutrit-qubit pairs joined at their qubit copies produce a rank-two far qutrit pair with success probability 1/4. The prescribed type-(3,3) pair has rank three. This challenges unrestricted distillation/join closure. The narrower read-end rule can exclude that join as a read, and the aligned Alpha object with pattern 2->3->2 remains valid with success 1/6. Thus the example is not a refutation of the canonical arithmetic.

We proved the complete canonical path rule. For dimensions d0,...,dk, adjacent edge ranks ri=min(di,d(i+1)), and standard Bell projections at internal copies, the far rank is m=min_j dj and the joint success probability is

    p = m / [(product edge ri)(product internal di)].

The required endpoint type is obtained exactly when there is no interior dimension below both endpoints. On a finite tree, this holds for every possible pair of path endpoints, considered as alternative protocols, if and only if each nonempty dimension superlevel set is connected. The proof is by exact index contraction and unique-path convexity; root finite enumeration checks 1,089 paths and 1,386 labelled trees.

For general supports the exact overlap matrix is C=V^T U W, where V,W embed the two pair supports and U specifies the Bell-join connection. The far Schmidt values are the normalized singular values of C; success is ||C||_F^2/(abd). Full-rank flat output requires equal nonzero singular values. In the rank-two/qutrit case, principal-angle rigidity forces coincident transported supports. This repairs the overbroad assertion that products of scaled partial isometries are automatically scaled partial isometries. Fixed-support gauge matchings are coordinate changes, not counterexamples; the connection transforms with the frames.

This gives three explicit ways forward: restrict the operation domain to compatible joins; retain effective rank, support connection and spectrum as state data; or enlarge the state space and retain a complete success/failure instrument. They add different structure and costs. LOCC cannot fix a true rank bottleneck for free: across a cut of total Schmidt rank m, an auxiliary pure crossing resource of rank K must satisfy mK>=R for a target Bell rank R. A finite pure catalyst returned exactly and uncorrelated cancels from that inequality and cannot enable R>m. Approximate or mixed catalytic return is outside this proof.

Where full rank survives but singular values differ, ordinary heralded local filtering repairs flatness at a calculable cost. For overlap diag(1,c), the join succeeds with (1+c^2)/12 and the optimal two-qubit Bell filter succeeds conditionally with 2c^2/(1+c^2), giving total c^2/6. This is a completed conventional operation with retained failure outcomes; it is not a proof of the original no-failure law. The general filter proof records its established Vidal predecessor and additional operation assumptions.

6. Wider claims: retain the algebra, require the physical map

The compendium is a separate 754-page working draft, not an extension of what Alpha's Lean file proves. Its own status labels and open results matter. We selectively investigated Lorentz structure, the electron anomaly, scale response and topological transport rather than treating a large document as one theorem.

The Lorentz algebra of the chosen mixed Hermitian/anti-Hermitian real space is correct. We explicitly reconstructed its Minkowski Gram, six generators, brackets and finite SL(2,C) coordinate action. This is a useful algebraic correspondence. A nonunitary boost acting as B tensor conjugate(B) changes Bell Schmidt values. The stronger reciprocal action B tensor B^(-T) preserves the Bell contraction, but its deterministic fixed-normalization interpretation is not CPTP. Passive covariance and probabilistic filtering remain valid alternatives. Neither is an emergent spacetime dynamics proof. The compendium itself leaves the admitted order/direction mixing rule open.

The attempted anomaly falsifier did not survive close reconstruction. Its finite internal grades include a zero third grade, but the source does not give a complete Pauli form factor F2(0), the closure term is not numerically specified there, and the electron ratio of reads remains open. A zero combinatorial grade is not a physical QED truncation theorem. Comparing a missing isolated QED coefficient to experimental uncertainty would manufacture a rejection. The corrected audit supplies the exact bridge needed for a meaningful test and uses independent recoil alpha inputs, with current QED theory kept separate. No additional source-faithful new physical prediction was established in this investigation.

Topological transport supplied a stronger exact negative result. We constructed two gapped two-band models identical on an entire momentum loop, hence with identical loop holonomy, but Chern numbers zero and magnitude one. Then we proved the general relative version: if a finite embedded graph in T^2 has a complementary disk, a smooth localized skyrmion in that disk can change the Chern number by any integer while leaving all graph-neighborhood Hamiltonian/projector/holonomy data unchanged. Normalized representatives retain gap two. For pre-existing energy data, restoring the original positive band amplitude preserves those energies and gap as well. This is an exact countermodel to determining a two-dimensional Chern/Hall integer from graph-loop data alone. A rule for the two-dimensional extension could remove the freedom; that is the missing constructive theorem. Analyticity, finite hopping or prescribed interior data would be additional restrictions, not conclusions of this no-go theorem.

The Chern result matters because it replaces an analogy with a precise necessary interface: derive a gapped two-parameter projector and its quantized transport coupling. It does not supply a material or a new Chern insulator. Underlying topological tools and TKNN are established; historical novelty of this specific diagnostic is unknown.

7. Decision and what would change it

Accepted at actual scope: the canonical exact finite arithmetic; conditional selector and representation facts; support-aware gluing/path/tree theorems; exact LOCC rank obstruction; completed noisy-record construction and pinned entropy transfer; the bounded Maxwell-normalization counterfamily; Lorentz coordinate algebra; and the graph-only Chern underdetermination theorem.

Not established: necessity of the full model grammar from the primitive sentence; a complete closed general pair-operation theory without extra support data or restrictions; the physical photon/current map; physical coupling normalization; a new momentum-dependent prediction; an additional source-faithful measured observable; a device, efficiency advantage or civilisation-changing capability; or historical novelty of the new applications.

A derived microscopic current-to-current amplitude fixing kappa, a new parameter-free observable with its complete measurement map, or a controlled emergent massless-mode construction would justify reopening the dedicated-direction decision. A high-precision alpha result agreeing with the locked digits would strengthen the conjecture and justify renewed attention; a consistent exclusion would reject the photon/alpha identification at this version without erasing the finite mathematics. For the surviving network direction, a demonstrable procedure or resource advantage beyond ordinary tensor-network and entanglement-processing methods would supply the practical reason to continue.

The mission-scale upside remains clear in principle: a genuine finite-operation derivation of interactions, or a substantially better quantum-information procedure, could be transformative. Present evidence supplies neither. The next useful research target is sharply stated and bounded; there is no scientific reason here to preserve a large dedicated allocation merely because the numerical match is striking.

Three Sol Max principals and twenty Luna Max workers were launched in the requested fresh bounded contexts without descendants. Some constructive first attacks preceded peer interpretations; all still shared the brief and ambient context, so they were not statistically blind. Cross-reconstruction materially narrowed the conclusions: an initially tempting g-2 rejection was removed, and unrestricted support-rotation criticism was separated from fixed-support gauge matching before the canonical bottleneck example was used. These observations explain corrections, not a claim that worker count improves scientific truth.
