2. What is actually forced and what is added

There are three different layers. A finite tensor-product pair model supplies
dimensions, flat Schmidt spectra and shared-site virtual copies. Alpha then
specifies an operation and read grammar, a universal positive-map condition
for fixing, a particular two-read/one-state star, a register, exchange signs,
weights and a trace unit. Finally it conjectures a physical identification.
These are not interchangeable claims.

Calling a definition a definition is legitimate. The question is whether it
is merely an abbreviation, or a substantive choice of semantics. In this case
the universal-over-positive-maps meaning of fixing, the two-point star, the
record grammar and the dressed insertion are substantive parts of the model.
The resulting number can be exact and parameter-free within that completed
model without being forced by the primitive natural-language sentence alone.
Changing these definitions does not refute the canonical conditional theorem.

The compendium itself describes its sentence and definitions as choices and
lists open boundaries in Chapter 2 (PDF pp39-48). Its section 124, PDF p301,
expressly makes Alpha's definitions, cap and exchanges canonical where wording
differs. Older broader compendium claims cannot extend the narrower standalone
Lean result. The compendium's selected pinned join checks do not quantify over
all independent support embeddings and join connections.

3. Independent reconstruction of selection and finite value

For end dimensions a,b, the steering coefficient matrix of the standard
flat pair has rank min(a,b). Its rows span the far end exactly when a>=b.
This proves the larger-end steering rule for every finite dimension; the
author's fixesFar predicate implements that rank test.

Now impose the imported numerical cap ab<=6, dimensions at least two,
unequal ends (nonzero action of the chosen central generator), and a>=b.
If a>=4 then ab>=8. Thus a<=3, and the only unequal possibility is (3,2).
With exactly two read arms and one unread arm of one type, the star is then
unique in dimensions and read/unread counts, up to relabelling arms. This is
a conditional local classification. It neither guarantees that every allowed
world contains such a star nor derives its three-arm shape from the sentence.

Let V,W be orthogonal traceless letter spaces of dimensions 8 and 3. The
distinct-slot product space has dimensions
8^2+3^2+4(8)(3)=169. Swapping the reads fixes eight diagonal V-tensors and
three diagonal W-tensors. The other 158 products form 79 two-element orbits.
Same-end products split as Sym^2(V)+Sym^2(W), dimension 36+6=42, and
Lambda^2(V)+Lambda^2(W), dimension 28+3=31. The mixed products form 48
even and 48 odd directions. The stipulated qubit commutator realization
adds three orthogonal single-letter differences. With Alpha's exchange rule:

  weight 1:           31+48=79 directions
  weight sqrt(2):     48 directions
  weight sqrt(3):     42 directions
  weight sqrt(6):      3 directions

The denominator rule gives (8*3)*36*36=31104. Consequently the stipulated
readout is exactly (79+48sqrt(2)+42sqrt(3)+3sqrt(6))/31104. Orthogonality
follows from the tensor trace pairing and orthogonal orbit sums/differences.
This reconstructs the count rather than inferring it from decimal agreement.

Lean's main theorem evaluates that finite construction. Its output is a list
of radicand/coefficient pairs and a denominator. It does not construct the
31104-dimensional readout operator space, a real square-root field, a photon
propagator, or the operational generation of every positive map. The program
defines the weight and readout rules before evaluating them. Its successful
execution is strong arithmetic evidence at exactly that scope.

L18's extension was checked mathematically: with n>=2 read arms and one unread
arm of a common type assumed, the same selection argument forces (3,2), and
the same denominator rule gives D_n=24*36^n. It does not supply an n-point
numerator or derive that shape. Independently, keeping the two-read algorithm
with an arbitrary center dimension d and far qubit gives a formal family:
put A=d^2-1; its four coefficients are
(A(A-1)/2+3+6A, 6A, A(A+1)/2+6, 3), denominator 48(A)d^4,
and weights (1,sqrt(2),sqrt(d),sqrt(2d)). Only d=3 survives the cap and
acting-center selector. This is a generalization of the algorithm, not a
new law or an alternative admitted by the canonical selector.

4. Imported read cap and its exact bridge boundary

The numerical positive-map classification is sound: all positive maps
M2->M3 are decomposable; the statement fails M2->M4. Woronowicz's primary
abstract explicitly states both facts:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0034487776900380
The 2->3 classification and 2->4 existence are retained here as named
literature inputs, not newly proved or formalized results.

I independently rebuilt the 3->3 obstruction. Define Phi on 3x3 matrices by
diagonal (x00+x22,x11+x00,x22+x11) and off-diagonal entries -xij.
For a rank-one input zz*, writing x,y,z for squared component moduli, Phi
is D-zz* with D=diag(2x+z,2y+x,2z+y). The rank-one PSD criterion reduces to

  x/(2x+z)+y/(2y+x)+z/(2z+y)<=1.

After multiplying denominators, this is
x^2y+y^2z+z^2x>=3xyz, by AM-GM; boundary cases follow by continuity.
Thus Phi is positive.

Let Omega=sum_i |ii>, and, with indices modulo three, set
rho=|Omega><Omega|+(1/2)sum_i|i,i+1><i,i+1|
                         +2sum_i|i+1,i><i+1,i|.
It is positive. Its partial transpose consists of three unit diagonal
entries and three blocks [[1/2,1],[1,2]], so is positive too. For the Choi
matrix C_Phi, tr(C_Phi rho)=-3/2; tr(rho)=21/2, hence the normalized
expectation is -1/7. A decomposable Choi witness P+Q^Gamma, P,Q>=0, has
nonnegative pairing with every PPT rho. Therefore Phi is not decomposable.
The exact matrix identities and spectra are in the independent code.

Nondecomposable maps propagate to larger matrix dimensions by CP embedding
and compression. If an extension decomposed, compressing back would decompose
the original. Adjoint reversal preserves positivity and decomposability.
Together the 2x4 and 3x3 obstructions cover every dimension pair with product
at least eight; product seven cannot occur with both dimensions at least two.
This proves the threshold from the stated low-dimensional inputs.

What does not follow just from this cone theorem is the operational bridge.
If G denotes maps actually generated by the stated reads, the compositional
argument establishes G subset of the decomposable cone. Identifying every
positive map with an operationally fixed relation additionally needs an
exhaustiveness/semantics statement. G subset D and P=D do not alone imply
G=P. The source treats the universal relation clause as part of fixing.

L16's (4,2) example is a sound separation witness: standard steering has rows
(1,0),(0,1),(0,0),(0,0), so fixesFar is true while the type lies above the
positive-map cap. It distinguishes state-level steering from the stipulated
universal type property. It does not contradict that property when adopted
as the definition of fixing. It is not a countermodel preserving every
canonical definition.

5. Exact obstruction and corrected Bell-network theorem

Root's BELL_NETWORK_COMPLETION.txt and Sol C's SUPPORT_AWARE_GLUING.txt were
independently reconstructed here. For a canonical path d0,...,dk, put
r_i=min(d_i,d_(i+1)). Initial edges are flat pairs on the first r_i coordinates;
each internal site has separate edge copies and is projected onto its full
standard Bell state. All internal Bell bras impose equality of adjacent edge
indices. Exactly m=min_j d_j common indices survive, each with amplitude
1/sqrt[(product_i r_i)(product_internal_i d_i)]. Therefore:

  endpoint state = flat Bell state of Schmidt rank m;
  joint success p=m/[(product_i r_i)(product_internal_i d_i)].

This is an all-length proof, not an extrapolation from finite cases. It is
the dimension-required endpoint type iff no internal dimension is below
min(d0,dk). On a finite dimension-labelled tree, this holds for every pair
of vertices as prospective path endpoints iff every nonempty dimension
superlevel set induces a connected subtree. In one direction, connectedness
keeps the unique path inside the threshold set. In the other, disconnected
superlevel components supply a path with a lower internal bottleneck.
The paths are alternative protocols; the theorem does not assert simultaneous
production of all endpoint Bell pairs. Root's finite path/tree replay is
consistent with, and supplementary to, this proof.

The standard 3->2->3 path already violates unrestricted type-only closure:
both inputs are the prescribed qutrit-qubit pairs, but the qubit join gives
rank two at the far qutrits with p=1/4. A type-(3,3) link requires rank three.
No support rotation or changed weight is used. The narrower read-end rule
can exclude reading these arms at the shared qubit; thus this is an obstruction
to unrestricted DISTILLING/join closure, not to every restricted operation
grammar. The opposite 2->3->2 Alpha join is valid with p=1/6.

For arbitrary support frames V:C^a->C^d and W:C^b->C^d, the far coefficient
matrix under a Bell connection U is, up to conjugate-frame convention,

  C=V^T U W/sqrt(a b d).

The output Schmidt values are the singular values of C after normalization.
It is flat at its actual rank iff the nonzero singular values agree; to be
the prescribed endpoint type it must also have the prescribed rank. Thus
the generic assertion that products of scaled partial isometries remain
scaled partial isometries needs a support-compatibility premise.

My separate exact example uses V=(e1,e2), W=((e1+sqrt(3)e3)/2,e2), a=b=2,
d=3, U=I. Both inputs have flat Schmidt squares (1/2,1/2). Their selected
far state is (|00>+2|11>)/sqrt(5), with squares (1/5,4/5), p=5/48.
This tests physical support alignment relative to a fixed connection. It is
not an invariant U tensor conjugate(U) matching inside an unchanged support,
and is not a counterexample to the fixed aligned count. For equal ranks m
with 2m>d, at least one principal-angle cosine is one; equal nonzero full-rank
singular values therefore force every cosine to be one, so the transported
support subspaces coincide. For two rank-two supports in C3, coincidence is
necessary and sufficient for full-rank flat output.

6. Necessary LOCC resource bound, independently proved

Choose a bipartition across a single tree edge of initial Schmidt rank m.
For any nonzero fully refined LOCC transcript, the branch is a product Kraus
operator, and its coefficient matrix changes as X -> A X B^T. Matrix rank
cannot increase. Supplying an initially independent pure crossing resource
of Schmidt rank K changes the initial rank to mK. An exact pure Bell target
of rank R factors from all residual systems, so R<=mK is necessary.

If the same finite pure catalyst is returned exactly and uncorrelated, the
final Schmidt rank is at least RK. Then RK<=mK implies R<=m: such a catalyst
cannot repair the bottleneck, even on a postselected nonzero branch.
Discarding residual systems does not evade the proof, since an exactly pure
target marginal must factor before the discard. Coarse success transcripts
can be refined; if their target is exactly pure, every nonzero component has
that target. Success renormalization changes amplitudes but not rank.

For multi-edge cuts, use the actual initial total cut Schmidt rank, which
for independent edge pairs is the product of crossing ranks. A single edge
rank is insufficient on a general graph. This is a necessary bound, not an
achievability or full cost theorem. Mixed or approximately returned catalysts,
nonlocal operations across the cut, or additional consumed entanglement are
outside the stated catalyst claim.

7. The left-action issue and final mathematical boundary

I independently found the unread-factor closure issue and checked L04's
repair analysis. Let H=diag(1,-1,0), Z=diag(1,-1). H tensor Z belongs to
sl3 tensor sl2, but left multiplication by iH tensor I2 has qutrit partial
trace 2iZ, so leaves that unread subspace. It is not an ordinary algebra.
Compression P_d(X)=X-tr(X)I/d makes an endomorphism, but its bracket defect
on traceless X,Y,A is [tr(XA)Y-tr(YA)X]/d and need not vanish.

This does not refute the central unit. Q acts as iI and preserves the space.
Moreover L04's alternative of acting on one full read-link M6 factor is a
legitimate representation with Q-unit one. The narrow issue is that the
factor/action must be specified for the general trace-form interpretation;
the ambiguity alone changes neither the candidate numerator nor B(Q,Q)=1.

The warranted outcome is therefore a verified finite conditional count plus
a precise network closure/admissibility problem and its support-aware repair
conditions. A viable broader calculus must restrict allowed joins, or retain
support/rank/singular-spectrum data, or enlarge its state space and keep a
complete success/failure instrument. None of these mathematical completions
derives a momentum-dependent photon propagator or identifies this count with
the electromagnetic coupling. Those claims remain NOT_ESTABLISHED here.
