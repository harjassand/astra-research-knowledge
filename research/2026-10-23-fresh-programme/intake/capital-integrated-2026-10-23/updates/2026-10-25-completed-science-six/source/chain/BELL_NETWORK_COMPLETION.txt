Bell-network bottleneck, admissible joins, and a support-aware repair
Harjas's private ASTRA Chain Theory assessment, 25 October 2026

Scope and source. This result reconstructs ordinary finite-dimensional tensor contractions appearing in Alpha Supplement S1/S7. It does not modify the canonical 172-record calculation. The original work here is the source-specific closure diagnosis, a complete path/tree criterion for the canonical support convention, and a precise operational repair contract. The underlying facts about Schmidt rank, singular values, principal angles and LOCC are established mathematics; historical novelty of the combined application is not claimed.

1. Canonical path theorem

Let a path have endpoint and internal dimensions d_0,...,d_k >= 2. The edge (i,i+1) initially carries the standard flat pair of rank r_i=min(d_i,d_{i+1}), embedded in the first r_i coordinates at either end, with separate local copies at shared sites. At each internal site i, project its two copies onto the standard full Bell ket of rank d_i. Keep the successful branch and retain the normalized internal Bell kets.

Then the far endpoint state is exactly the standard Bell ket of rank

    m = min_{0<=i<=k} d_i,

and the joint success probability in ordinary quantum mechanics is

    p = m / [(product_{i=0}^{k-1} r_i)(product_{i=1}^{k-1} d_i)].

Proof. Expand each edge Bell ket in its common basis. A term is indexed by one value t_i<r_i on each edge. Every internal Bell bra imposes t_{i-1}=t_i and contributes 1/sqrt(d_i). Thus all edge indices equal one t<m. Each surviving endpoint term has amplitude

    1/sqrt[(product r_i)(product internal d_i)].

The endpoint vectors |t,t> are orthogonal. Their m squared amplitudes give p; division by sqrt(p) gives the normalized rank-m Bell ket. The retained internal Bell kets have unit norm. This proves the formula for any path length and any canonical dimensions, not merely for enumerated cases.

Corollary (type-only closure). Alpha defines a link between endpoints of dimensions d_0,d_k to have rank min(d_0,d_k). The composite path has this required type state if and only if every internal dimension is at least min(d_0,d_k). A bottleneck below both endpoint dimensions prevents closure.

Smallest standard example relevant here: d=(3,2,3). Both input links are legal standard maximally entangled qutrit-qubit pairs. The qubit join succeeds with probability 1/4 and leaves a rank-two Bell state at the far qutrits. The resulting far pair is not the required type-(3,3) rank-three Bell state. This uses no support rotation, modified weights or altered definition. Its exact scope is unrestricted distillation/join composition: the narrower end rule excludes reading a (3,2) link at its qubit, and the canonical Alpha object d=(2,3,2) remains valid, succeeds with probability 1/6, and produces a rank-two far pair. Thus it is an obstruction to general closure, not a refutation of the finite arithmetic.

2. Exact tree criterion

For a dimension-labelled finite tree with canonical aligned supports, the path between every pair of vertices, considered as an alternative protocol, yields the type-required flat pair if and only if every nonempty superlevel vertex set {v : d_v >= t} induces a connected subtree. This is not a claim of simultaneous Bell-pair production for all vertex pairs.

Proof. If each superlevel set is connected, the unique path between endpoints u,v remains inside the superlevel set at t=min(d_u,d_v). Every internal label is therefore at least t, and the path theorem gives the required rank. Conversely, if a superlevel set is disconnected, select u,v in different components. Their unique path passes through a vertex of dimension below t, so its output rank is below min(d_u,d_v), contradicting closure. This is a global compatibility criterion, not merely a local prohibition on one displayed triple.

3. General supports require more than dimension compatibility

For two flat pairs of ranks a,b embedded at a shared d-dimensional site by isometries V:C^a->C^d and W:C^b->C^d, define the normalized Bell contraction by <Omega_U|=sum_{kl} U_kl <k,l|/sqrt(d), with U unitary. The far coefficient matrix is exactly

    C = V^T U W / sqrt(a b d).

The normalized far Schmidt coefficients are the singular values of C divided by its Frobenius norm. A flat output of maximal possible rank min(a,b) occurs exactly when C has that rank and all its nonzero singular values coincide. The singular values of the unscaled overlap V^T U W are the cosines of the principal angles between ran(conjugate(V)) and ran(UW). Arbitrary products of scaled partial isometries need not have this property: Alpha Lemma S2's general proof needs a compatibility premise. Gauge changes within a fixed support leave its Bell ket unchanged and do not themselves supply a counterexample; physical support changes and the join connection must be tracked separately.

For equal rank m with 2m>d, the two support subspaces intersect, so at least one principal-angle cosine equals one. If all m singular values are equal and the output has rank m, they must all equal one, forcing the transported supports to coincide. In particular, for two rank-two subspaces of C^3, full-rank flat output requires coincident transported supports. Restricting to that compatibility condition repairs the aligned Alpha join without proving it is forced by the primitive axiom.

4. Operational repairs and their costs

(a) Restrict joins to support-compatible domains, with the matching connection carried explicitly. On canonical trees the superlevel criterion is an exact dimension-only condition. The operation domain is then smaller than unrestricted joins.
(b) Retain effective support rank and singular spectrum as state data. General compositions become well-defined tensor contractions; some allowed outputs are partially entangled or have rank below the endpoint type. This extends the axiom's pair-only state vocabulary.
(c) Use an ordinary complete quantum instrument: keep success and failure outcomes. Its flagged map is completely positive and trace preserving. The normalized success branch is conditional and generally nonlinear as a function of its input. Information-contraction theorems for channels apply to the flagged map, not automatically to that normalized branch.

LOCC resource obstruction. Across an endpoint-separating cut, a product-Kraus successful branch cannot increase pure-state Schmidt rank. A bottleneck rank m cannot be repaired into a target Bell rank R>m using local operations and classical communication alone, even probabilistically. Here m must be the full initial cut Schmidt rank: on a path or tree choose a single edge attaining the bottleneck; on a general network other crossing edges contribute multiplicatively and cannot be ignored. With an auxiliary pure crossing resource of rank K, a necessary condition is mK>=R. A finite pure catalyst returned exactly and uncorrelated with target and all residual systems cannot help: its rank K occurs on both sides of the inequality mK>=RK and cancels. This statement does not cover approximate return, mixed catalysts, global operations across the cut, or consumption of additional entanglement. It is a necessary rank constraint, not a sufficient resource conversion theorem.

5. Consequence for this investigation

The model can retain its canonical local object. A general network interpretation needs an explicit operation domain or richer support/state data. The useful next mathematical target is a gauge-covariant support-aware network calculus with full instrument semantics. None of these repairs selects the electromagnetic coupling, creates a momentum-dependent photon propagator, or establishes a new physical prediction.
