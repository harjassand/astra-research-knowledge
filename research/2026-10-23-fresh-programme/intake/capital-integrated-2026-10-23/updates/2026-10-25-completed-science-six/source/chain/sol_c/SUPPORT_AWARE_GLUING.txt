1. EXACT GENERAL GLUING THEOREM

Let A,C have dimensions n_A,n_C and let two copies B1,B2 of the shared site
have dimension d. Put a=min(n_A,d), b=min(n_C,d). Let M:C^a -> C^d and
N:C^b -> C^d be isometries, and use the first a,b basis directions at A,C.
The input is the product of individually flat pairs

 |psi_M> = (1/sqrt(a)) sum_i |i>_A |M i>_B1,
 |psi_N> = (1/sqrt(b)) sum_j |N j>_B2 |j>_C.

An arbitrary maximally entangled join on B1B2 can be written with unitary W
by defining its bra

 <Omega_W| = (1/sqrt(d)) sum_kl W_kl <k|_B1 <l|_B2.

Set C_W=M^T W N. The transpose here, rather than adjoint, follows from the
Bell bra. The selected projection gives

 (P_W tensor I_AC)(|psi_M> tensor |psi_N>)
   = |Omega_W> tensor vec(C_W)/sqrt(a b d),
 p_success = ||C_W||_F^2/(a b d).

If p_success>0, the far state's squared Schmidt coefficients are precisely

 lambda_i = s_i(C_W)^2 / sum_j s_j(C_W)^2.

Consequently:

 (a) The far state is flat on its actual support iff all nonzero singular
     values of C_W are equal.
 (b) It is the dimension-only target pair of type (n_A,n_C) iff, in addition,
     rank(C_W)=min(n_A,n_C).
 (c) rank(C_W)<=min(n_A,n_C,d). A shared-site dimension smaller than both
     far dimensions makes the target in (b) impossible, even after selecting
     a successful outcome and renormalizing it.

Proof. Expand the two pair kets. Contract their B indices with the displayed
Bell bra. The coefficient of |i>_A|j>_C is
sum_kl M_ki W_kl N_lj/sqrt(a b d), giving the formula. The norm of vec(C_W)
is the Frobenius norm of C_W. The singular-value decomposition is exactly
the Schmidt decomposition of a coefficient matrix. The rank bound follows
from the dimensions of its factors. These arguments prove every clause.

Gauge covariance. Under a passive change M'=U M, N'=V N at the two shared
copies, the connection must transform as W'=conjugate(U) W V^dagger. Then
M'^T W' N'=M^T W N. Independent changes of far support bases multiply this
matrix by unitaries on its left/right and preserve its singular values.
Thus a coordinate change cannot repair a genuine rank or singular-value
defect. A relative physical rotation with W fixed changes the connection
data; it is not the invariant matching of the Alpha source.

Principal-angle form. The squared singular values of C_W are the principal
angle cosines squared between ran(conjugate(M)) and ran(W N). If a<=b and
a+b>d, those spaces have a nonzero intersection and at least one singular
value is 1. A full-rank flat result then forces every singular value to be 1,
equivalently ran(conjugate(M)) is contained in ran(W N). In particular,
for a=b=2,d=3 a full-rank flat result requires equality of the two supports.

Proof of the last claim. C_W^dagger C_W=N^dagger W^dagger P W N with
P=conjugate(M) M^T the orthogonal support projector. The spectrum is the
compression of one support projector to the other, which is also the
definition of the principal-angle squares. A shared unit vector gives
singular value 1. All singular values are at most 1. If they are equal and
one is 1, the compression is identity on the smaller space, forcing
containment. This supplies the precise missing condition in the general
partial-isometry product argument of Alpha Lemma S2. Two scaled partial
isometries do not in general multiply to another scaled partial isometry.

2. MAIN CANONICAL COUNTERMODEL: 3 -> 2 -> 3

Take n_A=n_C=3,d=2 and canonical supports. The two input links are

 (|0,0>+|1,1>)/sqrt(2) on A B1 and B2 C.

A permitted projection onto |Omega_2> at B1B2 succeeds with probability 1/4.
The normalized output factors as

 |Omega_2>_B1B2 tensor (|0,0>+|1,1>)_AC/sqrt(2).

The new far pair lies in C^3 tensor C^3 but has Schmidt rank 2. Alpha's
dimension-only type (3,3) prescribes rank 3. The two qutrit reductions have
spectra (1/2,1/2,0), instead of (1/3,1/3,1/3).

This example uses standard embeddings and no physical support rotation.
It is an obstruction to closure under unrestricted DISTILLING/join, not to
the end rule for a READ: that rule reads a (3,2) link at its qutrit end and
can exclude this shared-qubit site as the centre of that particular read.
A restricted operation grammar can avoid the example, but the restriction
must be supplied and the general closure claim narrowed accordingly.

The aligned alpha object has the opposite pattern, 2 -> 3 -> 2. There
a=b=2,d=3,M=N=the standard inclusion,W=I, and C_W=I_2. Its far pair is
rank 2 and its selected probability is 1/6, exactly as S7/S10 calculate.
The present example does not falsify that special calculation or its record
enumeration. The direct Lean join_probability checks that aligned case.

3. OPTIONAL SUPPORT-ROTATION STRESS TEST, WITH ITS SCOPE

For a=b=2,d=3, choose M=(e0,e1), N=(e0,c e1+s e2), c^2+s^2=1 and W=I.
Then C_W=diag(1,c), p=(1+c^2)/12, and the far spectrum is
(1,c^2)/(1+c^2). At c=3/5 this is (25/34,9/34) with p=17/150;
at c=0 it is a product state with p=1/12. A permutation interchanging e1,e2
already gives the c=0 case. These are valid individually maximally entangled
input pairs, but they physically change a support relative to the fixed join.
They therefore refute an unrestricted partial-isometry composition claim;
they are not counterexamples to fixed-support U tensor conjugate(U) gauge
matchings. Admissibility of these rotations in a stricter Chain grammar
requires a separate decision by that grammar.

For c in (0,1], the local filter F=diag(c,1) on one far qubit recovers a Bell
pair with conditional success 2c^2/(1+c^2); total selected-and-repaired
probability is c^2/6. This conditional probability is optimal under ordinary
LOCC on the two far qubits. For a pure two-qubit state, E=1-lambda_max of
either reduced operator is concave and homogeneous on unnormalized states.
After a local instrument, the opposite reduced operators sum to the old
one, so average E cannot increase. A successful Bell outcome has E=1/2,
giving probability at most 2lambda_min=2c^2/(1+c^2), attained by F.
At c=0 no LOCC branch of nonzero probability can raise Schmidt rank.

4. MULTIHOP AND RESOURCE CONSEQUENCES, INDEPENDENTLY CHECKED

For a canonical path with vertex dimensions d_0,...,d_k and edge ranks
r_i=min(d_i,d_(i+1)), take a standard Bell projection P_(d_i) at every
internal vertex. Let m=min_j d_j. Direct index contraction gives far state
Phi_m and joint success

 p = m / [(product_(i=0)^(k-1) r_i)(product_(i=1)^(k-1) d_i)].

Only the common index t<m survives all contractions; each edge contributes
1/sqrt(r_i), each internal Bell bra 1/sqrt(d_i), and the m surviving outer
terms contribute sqrt(m). The internal output Bell kets are normalized and
factor off. This proves the formula, including unequal adjacent ranks.
It is the intended dimension-only far pair iff no internal dimension lies
below min(d_0,d_k). On a tree this condition for every path is equivalent
to every nonempty dimension superlevel set inducing a connected subtree:
unique paths give each implication. These statements were independently
derived by Root as well; they are shared deductions, not separate audits.

Across an edge cut of rank m, any fixed LOCC transcript branch has a product
Kraus operator across that cut. A coefficient matrix X changes to A X B^T,
whose rank cannot exceed rank(X). A supplied pure auxiliary crossing
resource of Schmidt rank K can permit a far rank-R Bell target only if
mK>=R. Thus K>=ceil(R/m) is necessary. A finite pure catalyst returned
exactly and uncorrelated cannot help: its rank K appears on both sides and
cancels, giving R<=m even for a selected successful branch. This claim is
limited to exact pure catalysts; mixed or approximate catalytic return is
outside this rank proof. A rank bound is not a full entanglement-cost or
achievability theorem.

5. THREE PRECISE REPAIRS AND THEIR COSTS

I. Restrict joins to support-compatible inputs and forbid interior dimension
valleys below the desired far rank. This keeps type-only flat pairs, but
introduces an explicit operation-admission rule and does not follow from
endpoint dimensions alone.

II. Carry the actual Schmidt rank and support connection as state data.
For canonical nested supports, rank along a path is the minimum of all
vertex dimensions, so a discrete rank label repairs this loss of history.
For arbitrary supports, rank alone is insufficient: principal-angle data
control the nonzero Schmidt values. Maintaining flat pairs additionally
requires the singular-value condition of Section 1.

III. Allow general partially entangled states and use a full CP instrument
with a success/failure record. This is closed under ordinary quantum
operations but enlarges the state class and carries probabilities and,
for arbitrary supports, continuous Schmidt data. It is an operational
completion, not a proof of the original no-failure axiom.

Completed instrument. On a finite input space, P is the Bell projector and
Q=I-P. Define

 J(X)=P X P tensor |0><0| + Q X Q tensor |1><1|.

The Kraus completeness P^dagger P+Q^dagger Q=I proves complete positivity
and trace preservation, including for every entangled reference. Keeping
only the first branch and renormalizing is not this channel. N966 cannot
be applied to that normalized selected map as though it were CPTP.

Proposition: J has conditional Umegaki contraction coefficient exactly 1;
by N966 its complete equal-reference SLD coefficient is also exactly 1.
For the upper bound use data processing and subtract the untouched reference
relative entropy. For the lower bound take a trivial reference, sigma=I/D,
and a faithful rho with a different positive weighting of P and Q. Both
are block diagonal, and J is reversibly embedding their two blocks, so it
preserves their positive relative entropy. This also supplies a direct SLD
witness: a nonzero traceless H commuting with P at the faithful sigma.
Thus the full Bell test alone supplies no strict uniform information loss.

Sharp noisy-record corollary. Trace out the quantum output, leaving only
the binary flag S and an arbitrary finite reference R. Prepare a balanced
flag prior pi=(1/2,1/2), and pass S through a binary symmetric classical
channel with error epsilon in [0,1/2], giving Y. Then

 I(Y:R) <= (1-2epsilon)^2 I(S:R),

with the sharp constant, independent of the reference dimension.
This is a bound on the retained flag, not on residual quantum output.

Proof and actual transfer. Write A_0,A_1>=0 for the flag's unnormalized
reference states, Tr A_i=1/2, and K=A_0+A_1. The output weights are
Y_y=sum_i T_yi A_i. N994 gives the fixed-prior Umegaki inequality with
the scalar coefficient eta(T,pi), and these two sides equal the displayed
mutual informations. For a scalar input bias t and lambda=1-2epsilon,
the divergence from a fair bit is

 f(t)=[(1+t)log(1+t)+(1-t)log(1-t)]/2
     =sum_(k>=1) t^(2k)/[2k(2k-1)].

Its positive coefficients give f(lambda t)<=lambda^2 f(t), and the ratio
tends to lambda^2 as t->0. Hence eta=lambda^2. To verify the N993 bridge
explicitly, the difference eta D_input-D_output has entropy coefficients
-eta at A_0 and A_1, +1 at every Y_y, and eta-1 at K, plus linear trace
terms. The A_0 coefficient balance is -eta+sum_y T_y0+eta-1=0, and the
A_1 balance is identical. It is exactly a balanced combination of TWO
fixed positive sources, so the selected theorem applies even when A_0 and
A_1 do not commute. No common physical measurement is inferred.

Realizing these states with J is explicit: for arbitrary positive A_i of
the specified traces, use input P tensor A_0 + Q/(D-1) tensor A_1, with
D>1 and rank(P)=1. Thus this is an actual completed-channel construction,

already holds for a classical binary reference with weak correlation:
A_0=diag(1+t,1-t)/4 and A_1=diag(1-t,1+t)/4 as t tends to zero.

THE ACTUAL ALPHA PRIOR: AN EXACT SHARP COEFFICIENT

The balanced preparation is an added operating condition; the canonical
qutrit-centred join instead has pi_success=1/6. It does not justify replacing
this prior by 1/2. The following exact scalar calculation supplies the correct
N994 receiving coefficient at any faithful prior p in (0,1).

Let lambda=1-2epsilon in [0,1], b=|1-2p|, and

 eta_p(lambda) = lambda atanh(lambda b)/atanh(b)  if b>0,
 eta_(1/2)(lambda)=lambda^2.

At lambda=0 or 1 take the continuous values 0 or 1. Then the fixed-prior
KL contraction coefficient of the binary symmetric channel is exactly
eta_p(lambda). If p!=1/2 and 0<lambda<1, the scalar supremum is attained
at the opposite input probability q=1-p.

Self-contained proof. By symmetry take the prior bias b=2p-1 to be positive
(the value only depends on |b|), and write t=2q-1. Let f be the even function
in the preceding proof. Bernoulli KL is its Bregman divergence:

 D_in = f(t)-f(b)-f'(b)(t-b),
 D_out= f(lambda t)-f(lambda b)-lambda f'(lambda b)(t-b).

Put eta=lambda f'(lambda b)/f'(b) and H(t)=eta f(t)-f(lambda t).
Then eta D_in-D_out=H(t)-H(b), since the linear terms cancel exactly.
For t in (0,1), the function

 g(t)=lambda atanh(lambda t)/atanh(t)

is strictly decreasing for 0<lambda<1. Indeed the derivative ratio for
atanh(lambda t) and atanh(t) is
lambda(1-t^2)/(1-lambda^2 t^2), strictly decreasing; their function ratio
is its weighted average over [0,t] and therefore exceeds its terminal
value and decreases. Thus

 H'(t)=atanh(t)[eta-g(t)]

is negative for t<b and positive for t>b. H is even and has its global
minima exactly at t=+b and t=-b. This proves the inequality for every q,
with equality and positive denominator at q=1-p. If b=0, the positive-power
series proof gives lambda^2, approached as q tends to p. This proves the
formula and all limiting cases without numerical optimization.

The two-source N993 balances written above hold for this eta and arbitrary
prior as well (the prior-dependent log terms are linear traces). N994
therefore gives exactly the same sharp coefficient for arbitrary finite
quantum-reference operator weights. For actual-prior mutual information,
where Tr A_i=pi_i, sharpness is approached by a classical reference having
a rare value with conditional input probability q=1-p, and a second value
whose probability is adjusted so the average input remains p. The second
value contributes O(delta^2) divergence while the rare one contributes
delta D(q||p); their ratio tends to eta. This is a supremum claim, not
an assertion of a finite balanced-prior maximizer.

For the Alpha join p=1/6 and record flip epsilon=1/4,

 eta = log(2)/(2 log(5)) = 0.2153382790366965...

Consequently the completed noisy flag satisfies the exact operating bound

 I(Y:R) <= [log(2)/(2 log(5))] I(S:R)

for every finite R whenever its actual success prior is 1/6. The local
Hessian ratio at p is 5/32, strictly smaller; it is not the KL constant.

The canonical two read-links give an explicit application. With R the far
qubit pair (and independent spectators omitted), let P_far be their rank-one
Bell projector and I_4 their four-dimensional identity. Partial trace gives

 A_success=P_far/6, A_failure=I_4/4-P_far/6, K=I_4/4.

The second operator has eigenvalues 1/12 on P_far and 1/4 on each orthogonal
direction, so it is positive. For binary entropy h in nats,

 I(S:R)=h(1/6)-h(2/3)/4 = 0.2914326667926014...,

       =0.0449644933328251...,

which obeys the bound with positive slack 0.0177921155893687... nats.
These actual canonical reference weights commute. The N994 theorem is
