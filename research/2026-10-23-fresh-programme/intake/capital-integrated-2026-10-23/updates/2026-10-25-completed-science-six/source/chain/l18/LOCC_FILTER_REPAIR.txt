L18 — LOCC filter repair after a flat-link join
Status: proved on paper; rank-two arithmetic/Kraus checks replay in Lean

PURPOSE AND CLAIM BOUNDARY

This is a source-compatible operational completion of the flat-link join calculation in
ChainAlpha.lean. It treats a selected shared-pair Bell projection followed, when desired, by a
local stochastic filter at an endpoint. The general optimal pure-state conversion statement is
Vidal's established one-copy LOCC theorem, not a new entanglement theorem. The source-specific
contribution here is the normalization and composition with the Alpha join, its exact rank-two
specialization, and a serial compatible-overlap calculation. I do not claim literature novelty
for the general conversion formula or experimental feasibility of the added filter.

THEOREM: JOIN AND OPTIMAL FILTER

Let the first and second input links be normalized flat pure states of Schmidt ranks a and b,
respectively, with orthonormal support vectors u_i and v_k in the two d-dimensional systems to be
joined:

  |w_a> = (1/sqrt(a)) sum_{i=1}^a |i>_A |u_i>_S,
  |w_b> = (1/sqrt(b)) sum_{k=1}^b |v_k>_{S'} |k>_B.

Project S S' onto the normalized matched vector

  |w_d> = (1/sqrt(d)) sum_{r=1}^d |r>_S |r>_{S'}.

With C_ik = u_i^T v_k = <overline(u_i) | v_k> in this matched basis, the unnormalized endpoint
state is

  |psi_tilde> = (1/sqrt(a b d)) sum_{i,k} C_ik |i>_A |k>_B.

Write m=min(a,b), assume rank(C)=m, and let its singular values be
s_1 >= ... >= s_m > 0. Put S=sum_j s_j^2=||C||_F^2. Then:

  (i) selected join probability p_J = S/(a b d);
  (ii) conditional endpoint Schmidt amplitudes are s_j/sqrt(S);
  (iii) the optimal stochastic-LOCC probability to convert this endpoint state to a rank-m Bell
       state is p_R = m s_m^2/S;
  (iv) the probability of both the selected join and successful repair in one attempt is
       p_J p_R = m s_m^2/(a b d).

PROOF

The squared norm of |psi_tilde> is (a b d)^(-1) sum_{i,k}|C_ik|^2, which gives (i). The
coefficient matrix of the endpoint vector is C/sqrt(a b d); its singular values are the Schmidt
amplitudes before normalization. Conditioning on the selected join outcome divides them by
sqrt(S), giving (ii).

For the filter, take an SVD C=L diag(s_1,...,s_m) R^dagger. Let alpha_j and beta_j be the
corresponding left and right Schmidt vectors, so the normalized endpoint state is

  |psi> = sum_{j=1}^m (s_j/sqrt(S)) |alpha_j>|beta_j>.

Define two local Kraus operators on A by

  K_s = sum_{j=1}^m (s_m/s_j) |alpha_j><alpha_j|,
  K_f = sum_{j=1}^m sqrt(1-(s_m/s_j)^2) |alpha_j><alpha_j| + P_perp,

where P_perp projects onto the orthogonal complement of the Schmidt support on A. Since
0 < s_m/s_j <= 1, both are well-defined contractions. Their effects add to identity:

  K_s^dagger K_s + K_f^dagger K_f
    = sum_j ((s_m/s_j)^2 + 1-(s_m/s_j)^2)|alpha_j><alpha_j| + P_perp
    = I_A.

The failure branch is explicit:

  K_f|psi> = sum_j (s_j/sqrt(S))*sqrt(1-(s_m/s_j)^2)|alpha_j>|beta_j>.

The success branch is

  K_s|psi> = (s_m/sqrt(S)) sum_j |alpha_j>|beta_j>.

Its squared norm is m s_m^2/S, and its normalized state is the rank-m Bell state in these
Schmidt bases. This proves achievability of (iii), including the failure outcome and completeness
of the local instrument.

For optimality, the normalized source has sorted squared Schmidt coefficients
lambda_j=s_j^2/S. Vidal's pure-state single-copy LOCC formula gives

  P_max = min_{1<=ell<=m}
          (sum_{j=ell}^m lambda_j) / ((m-ell+1)/m).

The denominator is the tail sum of the target's squared Schmidt coefficients (each 1/m). Since
lambda_j >= lambda_m, every ratio is at least m lambda_m; the ell=m ratio equals m lambda_m.
Thus P_max=m lambda_m=m s_m^2/S, matching the filter. Multiplying by p_J proves (iv).

Primary predecessor: G. Vidal, “Entanglement of Pure States for a Single Copy,” Phys. Rev. Lett.
83, 1046 (1999), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.83.1046; full text:
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9902033 . The result above uses Vidal's standard optimality theorem;
it does not claim that theorem as new. Vidal also notes earlier work for the maximally entangled
target special case.

RANK-TWO QUTRIT-JOIN CHECK

For C=diag(1,c), 0<c<=1, a=b=2, d=3, S=1+c^2 and s_min=c. The normalized post-join state is

  (|00> + c|11>)/sqrt(1+c^2).

The success and failure Kraus operators are

  K_s = diag(c,1),       K_f = diag(sqrt(1-c^2),0).

Their effects are diag(c^2,1) and diag(1-c^2,0), which sum to I. The success branch is
c(|00>+|11>)/sqrt(1+c^2), so

  p_J = (1+c^2)/12,
  p_R = 2c^2/(1+c^2),
  p_total = c^2/6.

At c=1 repair is deterministic and p_J=1/6, matching ChainAlpha.lean's flat qutrit join.
At c=0 the endpoint Schmidt rank drops to one and a rank-two Bell target is impossible; the
positive-minimum-singular-value hypothesis is essential.

SERIAL COMPATIBLE RANK-TWO CHAIN

Assume n selected joins in a path of n+1 source links, every source link flat of Schmidt rank
two. At step j, assume the rank-two Schmidt supports are aligned so the overlap is
diag(1,c_j), with 0<c_j<=1; this compatibility is an explicit structural hypothesis. Define
C_0=1, C_j=product_{t=1}^j c_t, and S_j=1+C_j^2. If d_j is the actual dimension of the
j-th shared vertex, the conditional probability of its selected join, given the previous joins,
is

  p_j = S_j/(2 d_j S_{j-1}).

This follows because the new normalized flat rank-two link and the normalized d_j-dimensional
matched projection contribute the factor 1/sqrt(2 d_j), while the compatible overlap changes
(1,C_{j-1}) to (1,C_j). Multiplying telescopes, using S_0=2:

  p_join = product_j p_j
         = (1+C_n^2)/(2^(n+1) product_j d_j).

The final conditional state is (|00>+C_n|11>)/sqrt(1+C_n^2). Its Bell fidelity before repair is

  F = (1+C_n)^2/(2(1+C_n^2)),

and the final optimal repair probability is 2 C_n^2/(1+C_n^2). Therefore

  p_join_and_repair = C_n^2/(2^n product_j d_j).

This is an all-source-links-rank-two path result. It is physically realized, for example, by
alternating two- and three-dimensional vertices: each edge then has rank two, while shared
dimensions d_j alternate according to the path. The single join with d=3 is exactly the example
above. A path containing a (3,3) source edge has rank-three link data under the source's
min-neighbor convention and is outside this serial rank-two theorem; it needs the general
rank-dependent spectrum and a different chain recurrence. I make no rank-two claim for such an
edge. The product C_n controls the conditional fidelity and filter success under the stated
alignment assumption; the d_j values separately set the join normalization.

COVARIANCE

The matched shared-pair vector is invariant under W tensor overline(W), so changing the shared
orthonormal basis together on the two sides does not change the selected Bell projection. Under
endpoint local unitaries U_A and U_B, the coefficient matrix changes as C -> U_A C U_B^T (for
the stated coefficient convention). Its singular values are unchanged, its left Schmidt vectors
change by U_A, and K_s and K_f change by conjugation with U_A. Thus the local instrument and its
probabilities transform covariantly. The Lean file checks the diagonal phase special case;
general covariance here is the direct SVD/unitary argument above.
