## Representation changes are distinct outcomes

The report selected eight prior trajectories and37source passages. This deliberately chosen sample cannot estimate programme-wide frequencies. It distinguishes preserving the same external output with weaker evidence requirements, creating a different useful operation, and improving an intermediate proxy while degrading the next required operation. Warm/compact histories and source-selected examples do not identify causal effects of context, interruption or prompt wording. Four paired transfer examples retain the need to verify receiving preconditions: output alphabets/priors, acquisition access, rarity and normalization cannot be supplied by similar equations.

## Synthetic allocation tradeoff

The reported finite model uses three lines, separate theory/interface gates and nine equal action credits. All policies know the same model and observations; no policy sees the hidden interface-feasibility bit. At its selected base, the gate-count objective reports3.447expected successful gates and.447complete lines; the complete-line objective reports2.136gates and.617lines; a Bayesian index with exact-DP fallback reports2.057gates and.617lines. Planning/calibration/computation cost is unmodeled, so equal action credits do not mean equal total cost. The index loses in selected stress cases. Ten attempts sharing one latent feasibility event report success about.350, versus about.940 under an invalid independence calculation. Actual Astra dependence and probabilities were not estimated. These results are a synthetic counterexample to simple allocation proxies, not an allocation prescription or demonstrated discovery improvement.

## The actual prompt-pilot denominator

There were12completed response starts on THREE retrospective Astra-derived cases: two starts per case per condition, six responses per condition. A competent expert instruction was compared with an added causal-path/weak-link/strong-incumbent scaffold. Evidence packets, requested model/reasoning setting, response fields and220-word cap were shared. The report identifies the twelve trial starts as GPT-6 Luna Max and the investigators/scorers as GPT-6.1 Sol Max; immutable served model versions are not supplied. Prompts/task selection/rubric/assignments were reported frozen before collection, with an amendment allowing one assigned prompt-file read. No rerolls or replacements were reported.

Two Sol scorers judged anonymized exact responses before arm aggregation. One had reviewed generic arm descriptions and response wording may expose condition: this was label-blind, not fully blind. The source reports expert means7.33/8 and7.50/8, scaffold means7.83/8 and7.83/8. Ceiling effects were strong:8and9of12responses received full marks from the respective judges. Every response identified a relevant missing interface. One fairness disagreement and both original judgments were retained by the source.

No proposed next action was executed. The packets strongly cued their bottlenecks. Shared ambient instructions, absent hard context/tool isolation, no held-out cases, no strict full-compute parity, no significance result and no downstream discovery outcome prevent a causal efficacy conclusion. A later material-specific GO arrived after some responses completed; it was not a prerelease gate. The source explicitly says this is not a compliant sealed experiment under Astra's stricter evaluation protocol. ## Useful next hypothesis, still open

The report proposes comparing an exact indistinguishable pair with different desired outcomes against the strongest positive foothold, under identical information and total budgets, and assessing an actually constructed paid observation or useful new operation. Repeating a no-go, assuming a missing sensor/oracle or quietly changing the output would fail that test. This is a proposed research design, not evidence that the treatment works, a causal discovery law or an optimal worker/allocation policy. Later active method research is outside this completed tranche.
