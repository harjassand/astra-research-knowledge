The useful invariant is a positive-functional mass, not the number of accepted
histories or the coefficient mass of a predicate factorization. Root derived
this after reading passive_lag/GAME_AWARE_REPAIR.txt, Section 5. passive_lag
and the independent Sol proof_audit each checked the sharpening separately.

1. Local entropy lemma

Let chi(F,H)=tr(sigma F sigma H), sigma>=0, tr(sigma^2)=1, and let F,H be
positive contractions. Put h=chi(F,H), g=chi(I,H). The positive functional
chi(.,H), normalized by g when g>0, and scalar Jensen for x log x give

  -chi(F log F,H) <= h log(g/h).

If g=0 then h=0 and the contribution is zero. For any nonnegative weights
w_k, set p=sum_k w_k h_k and G_B=sum_k w_k g_k. Weighted log-sum gives

  -sum_k w_k chi(F_k log F_k,H_k)
       <=sum_k w_k h_k log(g_k/h_k)
       <=p log(G_B/p).

The final inequality follows by applying concavity of log with weights
w_k h_k/p. It is sharper than replacing every g_k by1 first. The transposed
statement uses G_A=sum_k w_k chi(F_k,I). These quantities need not equal
the raw coefficient mass Lambda=sum_k w_k. Positivity guarantees p<=G_A,G_B.

2. Exposure telescope

In the pinned R interface, weights and fixed tested-question histories can
also be integrated over exposure flags. The exact live Jensen-gap telescope
is unchanged when tested-mask factors depend only on tested questions, not
on a fresh live input. Its two endpoints are bounded using G_B for the Alice
entropy and G_A for the Bob entropy. Only the relevant endpoint is needed
for each telescope. Uniform bounds at every flag suffice; in the construction
below the masses are also stage-independent by total expectation.

For total tested event probability p>0, m live coordinates and G_A,G_B<=1,
both averaged unnormalized squared errors are therefore at most

  p log(1/p)/m.

The finite common-feature compression error still contains Lambda times the
chosen approximation accuracy. Lambda can be very large. At fixed p and a
positive accuracy slack this can be driven down, without an efficiency or
matrix-density assertion. A rate argument must not confuse that resource
cost with the entropy budget.

3. Partial-matching predicate masks

Assume each supported fixed question pair has a winning relation that is a
PARTIAL MATCHING: each answer has at most one accepted partner in either
direction. Test t coordinates. For every fixed tested question string c,
the product winning relation is again a partial matching on answer strings.
Use singleton factors indexed by its accepted pairs (alpha,gamma):

  F^k=F^alpha, H^k=H^gamma, coefficient=1.

Every accepted Alice answer occurs at most once, and likewise Bob. Thus,
for every exposure flag,

  sum_k F^k <= I, sum_k H^k <= I.

Consequently G_A,G_B<=chi(I,I)=1. The live fine effects retain the original
positive bilinear tested-event/live-answer measure exactly. In particular
the tested event E, probability p, conditional live answer law and joint
referee question law are unchanged. The large number of singleton labels
is still an operational accuracy/dimension cost.

3a. The larger disjoint-block class includes every projection game

The independent proof_audit then identified a broader scope. It is enough
that each fixed-question winning mask is a union of vertex-disjoint complete
bipartite rectangles. Use each such rectangle as one factor. Its Alice
supports are pairwise disjoint and its Bob supports are pairwise disjoint,
so sum F^k<=I and sum H^k<=I exactly as above.

In particular EVERY ordinary one-way projection predicate belongs to this
class. For theta(a,b)=1{pi_xy(a)=b}, group accepted Alice answers by b:

  f_b(a)=1{pi_xy(a)=b}, g_b(b')=1{b'=b}.

The f_b supports are disjoint preimages, even when pi is non-injective;
the g_b supports are singleton columns. Their sums are I for a total map
and <=I for a partial map. Reverse-direction projections work symmetrically.
Products of tested predicates retain this disjoint-block property. Root
independently checked the grouping. Thus the conditional quadratic conclusion
below covers projection games, not merely partial matching games.

4. Referee question law

No tilted prior is required for this sharpening. Because E is an actual
event, P(E|full questions)<=1. Hence

  D(P_full(.|E) || mu^n) <= log(1/p).

Discarding tested questions, entropy to the product of independent live
joint-question laws dominates the sum of live marginal entropies. Pinsker
and Cauchy--Schwarz therefore give mean live TV drift at most
sqrt(log(1/p)/(2m)). Within-round mu can be arbitrarily correlated.

6. Source comparison and correction

For full permutation unique games, a quadratic entangled repetition rate
is established prior: Kempe--Regev--Toner, arxiv0710.0655, Theorem5.2,
omega*(G^n)<=(1-epsilon^2/16)^n. Its SDP proof may also extend the stated
finite-dimensional scope; that implication is being checked, not assumed.
Dinur--Steurer--Vidick, arxiv1310.4113v2, gives general entangled projection
repetition with a larger universal gap power; partial matchings are a subclass.
The precise current priority of the projection/commuting refinement
remains UNKNOWN. A historical no-hit is not priority clearance.

The binary line game in this programme has partial-matching masks. Its exact

that coefficient cost does NOT prove a cubic barrier for the stronger
weighted-mass mechanism. The previous cubic obstruction applies only to
