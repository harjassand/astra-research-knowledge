For the binary line game, exact rectangle compression has optimal cost
Lambda_C=[2-2/(2L-1)]^|C|. Its log cost stays near log2 as the base gap
1/(2L-1) goes to zero. Moreover any nonsignaling pointwise win-preserving
output postprocessor must be the identity on every bulk input. Thus these
two repair mechanisms do not remove that family's cubic source power.
This is a sharp obstruction for the stated mechanisms, not a theorem that
all possible loss-aware R/H reconstructions must have a cubic exponent.
