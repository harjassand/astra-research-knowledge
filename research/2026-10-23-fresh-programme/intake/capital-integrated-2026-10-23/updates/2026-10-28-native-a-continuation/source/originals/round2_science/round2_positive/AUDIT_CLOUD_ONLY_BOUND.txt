AUDIT OF CLOUD-ONLY ACQUISITION LOWER BOUND AND NORMAL FORM
27 October 2026

Audited research/round2_main/CLOUD_ONLY_ACQUISITION_LOWER_BOUND.md,
check_cloud_only_bound.py, and AFFINE_ORACLE_NORMAL_FORM.md.

VERDICT: The positive-kappa lower bound and its exact integer witnesses are
valid under the stated intermediate-oracle contract. It is not an N687
original-value-query lower bound.

Gaussian sufficiency: pre-generate n independent observations and simulate
any bounded adaptive stopping policy by revealing only its requested prefix.
The full sample mean has law N(theta,tau^2/n); the Gaussian residual vector
is independent of the mean and theta. Unrestricted fixed-g evaluations and
auxiliary randomness can therefore be absorbed into a positive Markov
kernel acting on the mean. No adaptivity information is lost.

Fourier constants: bounded complex characteristic tests differ by at most
2 TV. Long-interval coefficients handle nonperiodic kernels. At u=1/S,
z=kappa exp(-1/2)<=1 and J1(z)>=z/2-z^3/16>=7z/16. The target coefficient
is >=7kappa/(16e)>7kappa/48. Subtracting 2epsilon<=kappa/16 leaves a
strict lower bound kappa/12 on exp[-tau^2/(2nr^2)]. Taking logarithms
gives n>=tau^2/[2r^2 log(12/kappa)]. The claim should specify 0<kappa<=1.
With zero center observations the Fourier coefficient is zero, so the same
target coefficient immediately makes the requested uniform error impossible.

Fisher comparison wording: this particular cosine source has
max_theta |m'(theta)|=L exp(-1/2). Thus its EXACT local Fisher necessity is
n>=tau^2 L^2/(e S^2), while tau^2 L^2/S^2 is the conservative generic
Lipschitz scale. Either wording preserves the asserted asymptotic gap, but
the latter should not be called the exact Fisher requirement of the cosine
family. For L=S=tau=1 and r=kappa the exact Fisher scale is 1/e rather
than one; integer feasibility then requires at least one observation.

Exact code: independently reran check_cloud_only_bound.py. All five
rational attenuation comparisons passed. The floating logarithm merely
proposes n0. Whether or not it rounds the mathematical floor correctly,
the exact inequality exp[-1/(2 n0 kappa^2)]<kappa/12 certifies that n0
observations are impossible; monotonicity gives the same for every smaller
positive count. There is no finite-moment inference or source calibration.

Normal form: U=b x+BZ controls every physical query and every queried
source value by induction. Substitution of the orthogonal decomposition
Z=B^dagger(U-b x)+(I-P)Z gives the stated affine response a and independent
reserve. The canonical W identities, the iff condition a=0 for orthogonality
to that reserve, and the unused-slot factor-two repair all check out.
For clarity, replace 'every q_i and returned value' by 'every q_i and
returned source value g(q_i)': the full final output Y need not be a function
of U because its explicit affine response and reserve are retained.

Scientific scope: the result legitimately sharpens the diagnosis that a
small local Fisher cost does not guarantee an accurate nonlinear Gaussian
channel. It excludes cloud-only compression of the hidden center at the
claimed relative accuracy. It leaves open richer source-coupled programs,
the supplied-center N687 task, and value-query constructions retaining
additional center information. The Gaussian normal-form representation is
a constructed way to preserve such information, not yet a nonlinear law
correction or a complete complexity theorem.
