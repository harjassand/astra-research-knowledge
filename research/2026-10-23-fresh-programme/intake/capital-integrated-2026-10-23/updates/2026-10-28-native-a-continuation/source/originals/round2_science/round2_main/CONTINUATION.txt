VALUE-QUERY GAUSSIAN MEANS: ACQUIRED CHANNELS, JOINT REUSE, AND A SHARP INTERFACE FAILURE
27 October 2026

OUTCOME

The general poly(log d,log(1/epsilon)) Gaussian-mean theorem remains open.
This continuation supplies a finite positive compiler for reusing actual
Gaussian-mean outputs, constructs its Gaussian reserve and center-response
metadata, executes a fully value-acquired general Gaussian channel with a
deterministic cap, and proves two quantitative obstructions that distinguish
an inadequate intermediate representation from the original target.

The substantive new reuse formula is

    C_final^{-1}=D^{-1}-A^T Gamma A,
    Gamma_ii=||v_i||^2/r_i^2.

It replaces repeated predecessor evaluation by a single retained record when
this explicit precision matrix and the final variance budget are feasible.
The reserve and external-center gauge are constructed from the actual finite
seed program, not assumed from its marginal law. The general acquired
channel has square-root dimension cost up to logarithms; it is a carefully
charged specialization of existing Gaussian-cloud correction, not a claimed
improvement over that primary method. A full-law radial obstruction proves
that simply lowering its batch size cannot yield polylogarithmic dimension.

The most informative additional failure is that a nonlinear hidden-center
operation can require Omega(tau^2/[r^2 log(S/(Lr))]) independent Gaussian
center observations even with unlimited fixed-source computation. A cheaper
local Fisher-information budget alone cannot justify that operation. The
original single-center value-query task is not subject to this restricted
oracle lower bound. The representation worth retaining is the actual
query-visible Gaussian program and its reserve, including the source
dependence that a mean-only Gaussian cloud can discard.

All frozen source bytes and SHA256 hashes were rechecked: S1--S12 are unchanged.
The first-round coercive inverse, full-law quadratic theorem, exact rational
scalar certificate, universal scalar comparator and all earlier code remain
in their original files. Nothing in this continuation invalidates them.

1. THE ORIGINAL CONTRACT AND THE PRESERVED GAP

The target is EVERY globally L-Lipschitz conservative g=grad F:R^d->R^d,
F in C^{1,1}, with nonconvex sources allowed. At fixed sigma/L>0, output
TV-epsilon N(Eg(G),sigma^2 I) with an EVERY-execution polylogarithmic number
of exact whole-vector g queries and polynomial arithmetic/storage. Gaussian
seeds and real arithmetic are the primitives. No source mean, potential,
derivative, VJP, conditional mean, hidden basis, or current-law oracle is
supplied. Finite-bit outputs need a commonly rounded target and their own
primitive-error analysis.

The preceding variational result solves a coupled divergence linearization
and bounds a smoothed positive correction's full law quadratically. It does
not acquire its general coefficient field S. Its generic first-step bound
is O(d ell^4), not a dimension-normalized reusable recurrence. The scalar
four-query implementation additionally pays for acquisition/certification.
Those limitations have not been erased by the results below.

2. JOINT GAUSSIAN ABSORPTION: AN ACTUAL OUTPUT-REUSE OPERATION

Suppose a finite acquired child program Q_i(c;xi_i), with all source and
arithmetic costs paid, has a known seed vector v_i and radius r_i satisfying

    Q_i(c+r_i h;xi_i+v_i tensor h)=Q_i(c;xi_i)

for every seed and h, preserving every physical query. Its standalone law
has a proved uniform error epsilon_i from N(m_i(c),S_i^2 I). The target
function m_i is an analysis object, not an oracle.

An available record R has joint error epsilon_in from N(m,C tensor I), with
unknown m and known positive covariance C. New intended centers are b+A m.
Let H place v_i/r_i in the i-th child seed block, so H^T H=Gamma. If

    C_new^{-1}=C^{-1}-A^T Gamma A>0,

choose factors C=LL^T and C_new=TT^T and form

    B=[-T^{-1}L; H A L],          B^T B=I.

Draw fresh standard Gaussian slots G, put xi=(I-BB^T)G, and actually return

    R_new,old=R+T xi_0,
    R_new,i=Q_i(b_i+sum_j A_ij R_j;xi_i).

Every argument is observable. The unknown Gaussian error in R is not used.
For analysis only, write R=m+LZ. The gauges make the complete effective seed
array eta=(I-BB^T)G-BZ. It is jointly standard Gaussian, since its covariance
is exactly I. Therefore the retained record and every standalone child
output are mutually independent in the comparison law. The proof is an
identity of full laws, even when each child itself is non-Gaussian.

Data processing and independent child comparisons give

    epsilon_new <= epsilon_in+sum_i epsilon_i.

An old record's error is charged once, regardless of the number of readers
in the layer. Source calls equal the sum of complete child caps, with no
additional calls to retain the old record. All matrices are known scalar
metadata; their finite Cholesky/linear-algebra costs are charged.

For a complete fixed affine DAG with standalone variances D=diag(S_i^2),
the accumulated precision is D^{-1}-A^T Gamma A. Positivity implies every
prefix is feasible. A final weight vector w has covariance w^T C_final w;
it may receive additional Gaussian noise only if that variance is at most
the requested sigma^2. Logical child routines execute once, rather than
once per expanded ancestor tree. This does not prevent other recursion
inside a child; that child's full cost remains in the sum.

For W2 contracts a useful sharper error propagation is

    e_i <= e_i,standalone + L_i sum_{j<i}|A_ij|e_j,
    e <= (I-diag(L_i)|A|)^{-1} e_standalone.

The inverse is a finite nilpotent sum. This preserves attenuation of
internal errors. Output-only sinks have zero columns in A and pay no read
precision; their large standalone output noises need not be assigned to
internal centers.

Complete proofs: round2_reuse/BLOCK_GAUSSIAN_ABSORPTION.md and
round2_main/JOINT_GAUSSIAN_REUSE.md.

3. A GAUSSIAN RESERVE AND COMPATIBLE GAUGE ARE CONSTRUCTED

A known output-response matrix W obeying Q(z+Wu)=Q(z)+u while preserving
all queries gives the exact decomposition

    Q(z)=Q(z-W(W^TW)^{-1}W^Tz)+(W^TW)^{-1}W^Tz.

Orthogonal Gaussian decomposition makes the second term an independent,
query-free reserve of covariance (W^TW)^{-1}. This is stronger than a
marginal approximately Gaussian output and follows from a finite code identity.

The joint absorption layer has an explicit response W_new. If incoming and
child responses certify reserve fraction rho, then

    W_new^T W_new <= rho^{-1}
        blockdiag(C_new^{-1},S_i^{-2}).

Thus the fraction rho is retained through internal DAG layers. An external
center gauge is also constructed. Its increment is bounded by
sqrt(kappa^T Gamma kappa/rho), where kappa is the known offset response.

An important failure occurred during this construction: the new external
gauge and output response need not be orthogonal. The exact test produced
cross Gram (3/16,-27/16,0,0,0,0). Assuming orthogonality would have broken
recursive reuse. Appending one UNUSED Gaussian slot repairs it:

    V_aug=(V,||V||),     W_aug=(W,-V^T W/||V||).

The actual law and all queries stay identical, while V_aug^T W_aug=0.
The cost is a sqrt(2) gauge factor and at most a factor two loss in the
guaranteed reserve covariance per such promotion. These factors and the
rho dependence in the gauge recurrence must be counted. An explicitly
untouched final reserve, already present in S1, can avoid this loss.

A second construction reads the representation directly from affine-query
code. If query inputs have direct Gaussian matrix B and external coefficient
b, while the final output has direct Gaussian matrix D and external c, put

    P=B^dagger B,   U=b x+BZ,
    a=c-D B^dagger b,   R=D(I-P)D^T.

Every physical query is a function of U, and

    Y=a x+Psi(U)+N(0,R tensor I),

with the Gaussian term exactly independent of U. The known affine response
a x can be tracked separately; the residual has a minimum-norm center gauge
-B^dagger b orthogonal to its reserve. Hidden-center uses must propagate
the noise/correlation of the known affine term instead of discarding it.
This normal form requires fixed affine source-query code, not arbitrary
source-dependent branching.

Proof and executed checks: AFFINE_ORACLE_NORMAL_FORM.md,
check_affine_oracle_normal_form.py, NORMAL_FORM_CHECK_RESULTS.txt.

4. A COMPLETE GENERAL VALUE-ONLY CHANNEL WITH A DETERMINISTIC CAP

The strongest completely acquired general construction executed here uses
the primary Gaussian-cloud fluctuation correction. The new explicit
specialization obtains every needed covariance action from source values.

For fixed c,a,r and Gaussian xi, the homogeneous secant

    D(v)=||v||[g(c+a xi+r v/||v||)-g(c+a xi-r v/||v||)]/(2r)

is defined at zero by a fixed convention, still paying two calls. For each
realized seed it is odd and sqrt(2)L-Lipschitz, with norm at most L||v||.
Its mean differs from D(P_a g)(c)v by at most
Lr||v||/(sqrt(2pi)a), uniformly over ALL v. The sample is not asserted to
be a linear matrix. Two fresh compositions acquire a squared heat-Hessian
action in four values. Conservativity supplies symmetry.

For a proposal averaging M Gaussian source inputs, select one of M Brownian
blocks and one time. The covariance action needs four values, independent of
M. Evaluating the SAME realized action at both likelihood-word vectors costs
eight values and retains the necessary cancellation. Both internal smoothing
seeds and the Brownian anchor are shared for that paired evaluation. Every
Brownian point is acquired from a lazy Gaussian bridge and retained across
the entire proposal attempt.

With rho=L/sigma, alpha=2rho^2/M and even p, an explicit full-law estimate is

    TV <= 4d alpha^(K+1) + 12 eta alpha d
          +6B(e_p/B)^p +2rho s sqrt(d),
    e_p=5alpha(sqrt(dp)+p)+3alpha^2 d,

before the separately budgeted caps. Here s is source smoothing, eta the
uniform relative action bias, K the finite likelihood-word order, and B=1/4.
No term is a moment-based law certificate. The proof compares full positive
likelihoods and uses the Brownian exponential-martingale identity.

Explicit choices in the proof include

    p=2 ceil(log(128/epsilon)/log 16),
    M=ceil[256(1+rho^2)(sqrt(dp)+p)],
    K=max(1,ceil(log(64d/epsilon)/log(1/alpha))),
    A=ceil(log_2(64/epsilon)),
    J=ceil(log_2(64A/epsilon)),
    s=epsilon/[32 max(1,rho)sqrt(d)],
    r=s epsilon/[192 max(1,alpha d)].

The ideal-real every-execution original-value cap is

    1+A[M+8JK].

It includes the g(0) offset query, all proposals, rejected proposals, words,
and action calls. Poisson is not an extra primitive: a finite rational
Taylor table and Gaussian-generated uniforms implement its capped law, with
the table error charged. There is no source fitting. Arithmetic is
O(dA[M+JK]+A(JK)^2+AJK log(M+1)), plus polynomial rational-table work;
storage is O(d[M+JK]). At fixed L/sigma, this is sqrt(d) times logarithms,
with logarithmic rather than inverse-polynomial accuracy dependence.

The construction is uniform over acquired centers, so fresh invocations at
adaptive centers compose with the sum of their conditional TV budgets.
It is not assumed to supply a pathwise gauge for the separate DAG compiler:
the rejection decisions need an additional argument before those two
constructions can be combined recursively. They are distinct proved contracts.

Full proof/code/data: round2_challenge/ACQUIRED_CAPPED_GAUSSIAN_CHANNEL.md,
capped_gaussian_channel.py, CAPPED_CHANNEL_RESULTS.txt, and execution CSVs.

5. TWO PRECISE FAILURES DIRECT THE NEXT CONSTRUCTION

First, a bounded global likelihood tilt cannot repair an arbitrarily broad
high-dimensional averaged proposal. For g(x)=lambda x, normalized proposal
Q=N(0,(1+beta)I), beta=lambda^2/(M sigma^2), and target P=N(0,I), a tilt in
[-B,B] can increase any event probability by at most exp(2B). A radius event
and chi-square Chernoff bounds give, for beta<=1,

    TV(P,P_clip) >= 1-exp(-d beta^2/24)
                         -exp(2B-d beta^2/64).

Subtract the paid cap perturbation for the implemented version. If
M=o(sqrt(d)), the error tends to one. More likelihood terms or finer secants
cannot change this. The same affine mean is acquired by g(0), so this is
strictly a failure of that proposal/correction architecture.

Second, exact nonlinear Gaussian-center compression is impossible. A fixed
positive kernel mapping every N(theta,I) to N(m(theta),Sigma), with fixed
Sigma, forces m affine. The joint log-MGF Hessian proves it directly.
The bounded-test refinement is more operational. Let a fixed source receive
theta information ONLY through n independent N(theta,tau^2) observations.
For g(x)=Lr cos(x/r), kappa=Lr/S in (0,1], uniform error at most kappa/32 in
N(Eg(theta+rG),S^2) requires

    n >= tau^2/[2r^2 log(12/kappa)].

Proof: Gaussian sufficiency reduces the observations to N(theta,tau^2/n).
Its first center-frequency Fourier coefficient is attenuated by
exp(-tau^2/(2nr^2)). The target's bounded characteristic test has coefficient
larger than 7kappa/48; TV error removes at most kappa/16. Thus the input
attenuation must be at least kappa/12. This is a full-law inequality and
survives adaptive stopping under the cap. Unlimited fixed-g computation
does not provide another observation of theta.

For L=S=tau=1, r=kappa=1/256, error at most 1/8192 requires at least 4,081
center observations. At kappa=1/4096 and error 1/131072, at least 776,531
are required. Exact rational attenuation certificates are saved. This
does not bound original N687 queries: a source-coupled algorithm can retain
information absent from the cloud-only contract.

Proofs: round2_main/CLOUD_ONLY_ACQUISITION_LOWER_BOUND.md and
round2_positive/GAUSSIAN_SHIFT_REUSE_OBSTRUCTION.md. The lower-bound proof
was independently audited; its generic Fisher-scale comparison was corrected
to distinguish the exact cosine factor exp(-1) from a Lipschitz upper scale.

6. AN ACQUIRED AFFINE BENCHMARK AND THE STRONGEST ALTERNATIVES

For an unknown affine conservative g(v)=Av+c, unknown center supplied only
as X=theta+tau Z, and S>tau L, the positive joint channel is

    B=(S^2 I-tau^2 A^2)^(1/2),
    Y=g(X)+BW,
    X_new=X-tau^2 A B^{-1}W.

The pair is Gaussian and independent, with means Atheta+c and theta, and
retained precision tau^{-2}I-S^{-2}A^2. Shared binomial polynomials acquire
the required matrix actions through g(v)-g(0). The finite implementation
uses exactly 2n+3 values, O(nd) arithmetic and O(d) storage, with joint TV
at most 3sqrt(d) q^(2n+2)/(1-q^2), q=tau L/S<1. Hence fixed q gives
O(log d+log(1/epsilon)) queries. It is a standard matrix-polynomial Gaussian
simulation benchmark, not a novelty or superiority claim. Replacing its
matrix by a random unbiased secant would destroy the joint-Gaussian proof.

The comparison remains candid:

* Ordinary independent averaging plus noise: O(sqrt(d)/epsilon) values.
* The acquired capped Gaussian-cloud specialization: sqrt(d) times logarithms,
  including all source acquisition and every-execution caps.
* Frozen S1: stronger dimension dependence for each fixed positive exponent,
  but its candidate proof and order-dependent constants cannot be promoted
  to a uniform polylog theorem by choosing an increasing order.
* Joint DAG absorption: reduces copied predecessor work when its actual
  precision/gauge/reserve/output-noise tests close; no uniform dominance.
* Unknown affine cloud channel: polylog queries in its stated subclass,
  matched by the equally informed ordinary matrix-polynomial method.

7. EXECUTED EVIDENCE AND REMAINING OBLIGATION

The capped channel executed 96 parameter checks, 200 shared-action checks,
null-event controls, and eight nonseparable multidimensional source outputs.
Per-output calls ranged from 3,489 to 10,177, within deterministic caps
32,257 and 52,641. Rejected proposals and retained bridges were exercised.

The reuse compiler has exact rational covariance/projector/positivity and
response-Gram certificates, including the nonzero orthogonality defect and
its repair. Seventeen-dimensional conservative-source replay preserved every
physical query and output to floating residual below 1.8e-15. The normal-form
test independently checked exact rational metadata and 11-dimensional
nonlinear pathwise identities. The affine channel covered d=5,17,64,129.

This continuation executed 109,097 synthetic source calls including recorded
development reruns: 105,220 capped-channel calls, 2,280 reuse tests, 573 affine
channel calls, and 1,024 normal-form calls. Exact obstruction calculations
needed none. Floating outputs are implementation checks, not continuous-TV
certificates. The mathematical ideal-real proofs are the full-law evidence.

The exact next scientific obligation is an acquired nonlinear transport or
correction acting on the RETAINED QUERY-VISIBLE PROGRAM, with a defect that
improves superlinearly at the dimension-normalized scale. It must close the
actual precision matrix, reserve/gauge growth and total source-call recurrence.
The old variational inverse may still supply its analytical component; it
does not yet supply the required acquisition or coefficient regularity.

Two unproductive shortcuts are now excluded: refining a bounded global tilt
around the same averaged proposal, and replacing source-coupled history by
an unrestricted cheap Gaussian-center oracle. Neither failure is a lower
bound for the original target. The new compiler and normal form preserve
the information and Gaussian noise needed for the next source-aware attack.
