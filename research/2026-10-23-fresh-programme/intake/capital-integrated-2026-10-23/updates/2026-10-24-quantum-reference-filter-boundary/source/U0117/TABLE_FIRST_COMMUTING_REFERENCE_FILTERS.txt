TABLE-FIRST COMMUTING EXTENSION OF WHOLE-COARSE REFERENCE FILTERS
2026-10-24. passive_lag. Source-premised extension; novelty UNKNOWN.

Disposition

The exact reference operation is legal directly in arbitrary commuting
algebras. It need not first be represented in a finite tracial algebra.
After constructing its COMPLETE binary-success/fine correlation family,
the accepted finite-TABLE density premise can instead approximate that
new family. The operator domination hypotheses need not survive this
approximation. A referee-drift term must be retained separately while
passing through approximants: their weighted success density need not
be bounded by one. This note proves that interface and states its limits.

This is not a new proof of the supplied density theorem or S_actual,
not a finite-matrix density assertion, and not an efficient compiler.
Its finite-tensor specialization already has a direct finite proof.

1. Exact operation on a commuting strategy, before any approximation

Let A,B be commuting von Neumann algebras on H, eta a unit vector, and
chi(F,H)=<eta,F H eta>. For each tested-question label c, the original
effects F_u^alpha,G_u^(alpha,a) belong to A and H_v^gamma,K_v^(gamma,b)
belong to B. Local fine sums are their coarse effects; coarse sums may
be <=I. Suppose complete reference POVMs F0^alpha,H0^gamma are independent
of all unchecked questions and
  F_u^alpha<=d_A(c) F0^alpha,
  H_v^gamma<=d_B(c) H0^gamma.                                 (C1)
The constants are finite. No invertibility is needed for (C1).

Write w0_(alpha,gamma)=<eta,F0^alpha H0^gamma eta> and
  p0(c)=sum_(alpha,gamma)theta_c(alpha,gamma)w0_(alpha,gamma).
For p0>0 use the finite direct-sum Hilbert space over accepted branches,
with unit resource vector
  zeta_c=direct_sum_(alpha,gamma)
     sqrt(theta_c(alpha,gamma)/p0) sqrt(F0^alpha)sqrt(H0^gamma)eta. (C2)
Both local algebras contain the branch projections. Thus each player
can read the same NEW offline branch. This does not reveal the other
player's actual after-question outcome.

Bounded Douglas support factorization inside A and B gives positive
branch fine effects C_u^(alpha,a),D_v^(gamma,b) such that
  sqrt(F0^alpha) C_u^(alpha,a) sqrt(F0^alpha)=G_u^(alpha,a)/d_A,
  sqrt(H0^gamma) D_v^(gamma,b) sqrt(H0^gamma)=K_v^(gamma,b)/d_B. (C3)
Their fine sums are <=I by (C1). Complete them by one local failure
effect. All Alice effects commute with all Bob effects, branchwise and
on the direct sum. Cross-algebra commutation, without any trace identity,
now yields
  lambda_c P(1,a;1,b|u,v,c)
     =sum_(alpha,gamma)theta_c chi(G_u^(alpha,a),K_v^(gamma,b)),
  lambda_c=d_A d_B p0(c),                                    (C4)
and exactly
  lambda_c P_A(1|u,c)=d_B sum_(alpha,gamma)theta_c chi(F_u^alpha,H0^gamma),
  lambda_c P_B(1|v,c)=d_A sum_(alpha,gamma)theta_c chi(F0^alpha,H_v^gamma).
                                                               (C5)
They are <=d_B,<=d_A when the original coarse sums are <=I.
The proof of (C4) moves only Alice factors past Bob factors; it never
commutes two same-side factors. For p0=0, domination makes the accepted
table zero; use always-fail instruments and lambda_c=1.

There are finitely many c. A tensor pool of their resources and algebras
is ONE fixed resource before either question arrives. Each side selects
its own hypothesized c locally. Even when those c labels mismatch, the
operators commute: they act in different tensor factors or in commuting
algebras of the same factor. Hence (C2)--(C5) define an all-input family.
The original Hilbert space need not be finite-dimensional; no exact
finite-tracial representation of this arbitrary commuting resource is
being asserted.

2. Smooth version, with an explicit extra invertibility premise

The clipping operation in SMOOTH_DOMINATION_AND_DIRECT_FILTER.txt works
in A,B by the same functional calculus and support operations if each
reference effect has a bounded inverse, for example F0^alpha>=epsilon I
and H0^gamma>=epsilon I for some positive epsilon. This is an explicit
premise in an infinite algebra, not a consequence of finite label sets.
The primed effects are conclusive sub-POVMs, and (C1)--(C5) apply to
those original-label effects without assigning a zero reference abort.

For completeness the loss estimate also has a direct commuting proof.
On the orthogonal accepted (alpha,gamma) stack let
  Psi_(alpha,gamma)=sqrt(F_u^alpha)sqrt(H_v^gamma)eta.
Let L,M be the own-label-controlled positive contractions implementing
the clipped fine instruments. They commute with the acceptance mask and
with each other. For p=||Psi||^2 and p'=||LM Psi||^2,
  ell=p-p'<=delta_A+delta_B,
  ||Psi-LM Psi||^2<=ell.                                     (C6)
The same losing fine effect therefore gives
  alpha'<=[alpha+2sqrt(alpha ell/p)+ell/p]/(1-ell/p), ell<p.
Clipping is performed on full inputs BEFORE taking half-exposure
averages. Only the subsequent fixed reference transport is linear.

3. A general binary-filter table interface, retaining referee drift

Let c be fixed tested questions under their original mu^C law. On one
resource take complete local POVMs with conclusive outputs (1,a_J),
(1,b_J) and failure outputs. Their effects can depend on c and own full
unchecked question string u or v. Let positive finite lambda_c be fixed.
For the unconditioned original question law mu^n, define
  p=E_(c,u,v) lambda_c P(1,1|c,u,v)>0,
  G_A=E_(c,u,v) lambda_c P_A(1|c,u),
  G_B=E_(c,u,v) lambda_c P_B(1|c,v),
  B=max{G_A,G_B},
  Q(c,u,v,a_J,b_J)=mu^n(c,u,v)lambda_c
                                  P(1,a_J;1,b_J|c,u,v)/p.   (C7)
Here p<=G_A,G_B and B>=p. Neither p<=1 nor a pointwise upper bound of
one on lambda_c P(1,1|c,u,v) is required. Let Q_full be the question
marginal, alpha its mean unchecked-coordinate losing probability, and
  D_ref=D(Q_full || mu^n),
  tau=max{log(B/p),D_ref}/m, m=n-|C|.                         (C8)
All zero-probability mu inputs are omitted; finite alphabets make D_ref
finite on the remaining support.

Under the standing R/H/S_actual premises, (C7) implies a legal all-input
one-round strategy, to every positive final accuracy, of loss at most
  alpha+2^16[sqrt(alpha) tau^(1/4)+sqrt(tau)]+accuracy.         (C9)
The following argument gives the required separate-drift version rather
than assuming that a nonphysical weighted density is an ordinary event.

Use the success coarse effects and their actual fine subeffects in R.
At each half-exposure, taking conditional expectations preserves the
fine/coarse sums and positivity. Its unconditioned one-sided averages
remain G_A,G_B by the tower property. Weighted Jensen/log-sum, with
weights lambda_c times the original flag measure, gives
  -U_j<=p log(G_B/p), -Z_j<=p log(G_A/p).
The R entropy telescope therefore yields both average squared errors
<=p log(B/p)/m, up to arbitrarily small compression allowance. This
homogeneous estimate does not require sum of the scalar weights to be
one or p<=1. Equivalently normalize by Lambda=E_c lambda_c: p,G_A,G_B
are all divided by Lambda and their logarithmic ratios stay the same.

H's abstract mass lemma keeps the two referee marginal drifts separate:
  TV(Q,J_A)<=sqrt(e_u/p)+Delta_X,
  TV(Q,J_B)<=sqrt(e_z/p)+Delta_Y.
Only the later ordinary-event specialization bounds those drifts by
log(1/p). Here product-entropy superadditivity, marginalization, Pinsker
and Cauchy--Schwarz give instead
  Delta_X,Delta_Y<=sqrt(D_ref/(2m))<=sqrt(tau/2).              (C10)
Its exact norm marginal identities remain unchanged because lambda_c
is independent of the fresh questions inside each history label.
Consequently all actual history-coupling and S_actual event estimates
apply with the same tau. They yield (C9), including mismatches/defaults.
For tau>=1/16 the displayed bound is already trivial; otherwise choose
the same positive smoothing and finite timeouts as in the frozen proof.
At tau=0 use positive approximation allowances and then take their limit.

4. Approximate the NEW complete table, not the domination premise

Now start from the arbitrary commuting binary-filter family of Section 1.
Apply accepted premise L to its ENTIRE finite input/output table, including
all mismatched c labels and failure outputs. This gives finite-tracial
complete POVMs approximating this table in l1. Their effects need not
satisfy (C1), and their resources need not arise from (C2). Neither is
needed: apply (C7)--(C9) directly to each approximating family with the
same fixed lambda_c and original question law.

At fixed p>0, its p_j,G_A,j,G_B,j and conditional fine tables converge.
Conditional referee KL is also continuous here: the finite question
support and its positive mu atoms are fixed, and x log x is continuous
at zero. Thus alpha_j->alpha and tau_j->tau. Applying (C9) to each
finite-tracial approximant and taking this fixed-p limit proves (C9)
for the original commuting binary-filter table. Every final protocol
remains a legal commuting protocol; no convergence of its resource
vectors is asserted or needed.

It would be invalid instead to use log(1/p_j) for the referee cost solely
because the ORIGINAL weighted table came from an event. Generally the
approximants have lambda_c P_j(1,1|c,u,v)>1 at some inputs. Retaining
D_ref,j in (C8) is what closes the passage.

For an exact reconstruction (C4) of an original event of mass p<=1,
  Q_full/mu^n=P_original(E|questions)/p<=1/p.
Therefore D_ref<=log(1/p). From (C5),
  B<=max{E_c d_A(c),E_c d_B(c)}.
It follows that the same strategy-dependent scalar reference criterion
is valid in the commuting model, with conservative parameter
  tau_ref=log(max{1,E_c d_A,E_c d_B}/p)/m.                   (C11)
The smooth variant substitutes p',alpha' from Section 2 throughout.

5. Exact limits of this extension

L here is the already accepted finite-TABLE density statement for the
commuting model, not finite-matrix or finite-tensor density. R and
S_actual remain their supplied finite-tracial mathematical interfaces.
No quantitative dimension, preparation, spectrum, time, or conditioning
bound follows from this fixed-p continuity argument. Its final protocol
need not lie in the finite-tensor model. The separate direct finite
matrix proof is still the basis for the finite-tensor conclusion.

This is a strategy/event condition. No small common reference, small
payoff-weighted clipping tail, or uniform greedy-history radius theorem
has been supplied for arbitrary games. In particular this extension
does not convert the mask-interface lower bounds into physical-error
lower bounds, and it does not establish a new general repetition rate.
