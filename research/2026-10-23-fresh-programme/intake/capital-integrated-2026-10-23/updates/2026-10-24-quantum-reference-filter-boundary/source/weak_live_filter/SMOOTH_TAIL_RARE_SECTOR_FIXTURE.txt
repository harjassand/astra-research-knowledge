Smooth spectral-tail bound and a rare-sector fixture
2026-10-24; bounded constructive check

1. Spectral tail inequality

For d>0, s>0, and r>=0,

  (r-d)_+ <= d^(-s) r^(1+s).                              (1)

If r<=d this is immediate. If r>d, then r-d<=r and
d^(-s)r^(1+s)=r(r/d)^s>=r. By functional calculus, for any positive
operator R,

  (R-dI)_+ <= d^(-s) R^(1+s).                              (2)

For a full-rank reference POVM F0^alpha, put
R_(u,alpha)=F0^(-1/2) F_u^alpha F0^(-1/2) and
F'_u^alpha=F0^(1/2) min(R_(u,alpha),dI) F0^(1/2). Then

  F_u^alpha-F'_u^alpha
       =F0^(1/2)(R_(u,alpha)-dI)_+F0^(1/2)
       <=d^(-s)F0^(1/2)R_(u,alpha)^(1+s)F0^(1/2).

For chi(A,H)=Tr(sigma A sigma H), positive A,H give monotonicity in
either positive argument. Thus for theta>=0,

  delta_A(d)=E sum_(alpha,gamma) theta(alpha,gamma)
                 chi(F_u^alpha-F'_u^alpha,H_v^gamma)
       <=d^(-s) M_A(s),

  M_A(s)=E sum_(alpha,gamma) theta(alpha,gamma)
                 chi(F0^(alpha)^(1/2)R_(u,alpha)^(1+s)
                     F0^(alpha)^(1/2),H_v^gamma).             (3)

In particular delta_A/p <= d^(-s) M_A(s)/p for original accepted
mass p>0. Equation (3) is a one-sided bound; it does not claim that a
bounded moment alone makes a fixed-d tail small.

2. A compact family with exact radius Theta(N), but small accepted tail

Hilbert space and state. Fix N>=2, nu=N^(-2), and
H=C^2_bulk direct-sum C_rare. Let P_B and P_R be the block projections,
and set

  rho_N=((1-nu)/2) P_B + nu P_R,       sigma_N=sqrt(rho_N).

Then Tr(sigma_N^2)=1. The bulk is a qubit carrying fixed state weight
1-nu, while the orthogonal rare sector has weight nu.

Alice's full input is u in {1,...,N}. Let S_u be Pauli X for even u and
Pauli Z for odd u, embedded on the bulk. With eta=1/4, her coarse labels
are r_1,...,r_N,b_+,b_-, and

  F_u^(r_j)=1{u=j}P_R,
  F_u^(b_+)=(P_B+eta S_u)/2,
  F_u^(b_-)=(P_B-eta S_u)/2.                              (4)

These form a POVM for every u. The bulk effects are positive, and
F_u^(b_+) and F_(u')^(b_+) fail to commute when S_u=X,S_(u')=Z:
their commutator is eta^2[X,Z]/4. Thus the noncommuting dependence
occupies the fixed-probability bulk, not only the rare sector.

Retain the exact original fine instruments: for a in {0,1}, set
G_u^(alpha,a)=F_u^alpha/2. Bob has one coarse outcome gamma=*, with
H_v^*=I and fine effects K_v^( *,b)=I/2 for b=0,1. Take theta(alpha,*)=1
for every Alice label. Uniform u is convenient but the calculations below
are independent of u. The original accepted mass is exactly

  p=E_u chi(I,I)=Tr(rho_N)=1.                             (5)

Common-reference radius. Use the following full-rank, input-independent
reference POVM, where

  e_N=nu/(N+2),
  q_N=(1-nu)/N+e_N=(N+2-2nu)/(N(N+2)),
  c_N=(1-N e_N)/2:

  F0^(r_j)=(1-nu)P_R/N + e_N I,   j=1,...,N,
  F0^(b_+)=F0^(b_-)=c_N P_B+e_N P_R.                      (6)

Each effect is full rank. The N rare labels sum to
(1-nu+N e_N)P_R+N e_N P_B; adding the two bulk labels gives I.
Equivalently, (6) is the convex mixture of weight 1-nu of the
support-adapted block reference F0^(r_j)=P_R/N,
F0^(b_+/-)=P_B/2, and weight nu of the uniform (N+2)-outcome POVM
I/(N+2). The small uniform component supplies full-rank floors.
On the rare block of F0^(r_j), the active effect F_u^(r_u)=P_R has
relative eigenvalue

  D_N=q_N^(-1)=N(N+2)/(N+2-2/N^2).                         (7)

On the bulk, the largest relative eigenvalue of either b effect is
(1+eta)/(2c_N)=(5/4)/(1-1/[N(N+2)])<=10/7. Thus the exact maximum
relative eigenvalue for the reference (6) is D_N>N. The growth is also
unavoidable for every common reference POVM: if
F_u^(r_j)<=d F0^(r_j) for every u,j, choosing u=j and summing over j
gives

  N P_R <= d sum_j F0^(r_j) <= d I,

so d>=N. Consequently the optimal common-reference radius is between
N and D_N, hence Theta(N); the displayed reference itself has the exact
radius D_N. If one allows support inverses and omits the floors, the
block reference above has exact radius N (the bulk ratio is 1+eta=5/4),
matching the universal lower bound. The full-rank version is retained
below so ordinary inverses and the stated smooth-clipping premise apply.

Payoff-weighted moment. Here theta=1 and Bob's effect is I, so
chi(A,I)=Tr(rho_N A). Define f_+=(1+eta)/2=5/8,
f_-=(1-eta)/2=3/8. The relative spectra are explicit: an active rare
label has eigenvalue D_N on P_R and zero on P_B; each bulk label has
eigenvalues f_+/c_N,f_-/c_N on P_B and zero on P_R. Hence, for every
s>0,

  M_A(s)=nu D_N^s
          +(1-nu)c_N^(-s)(f_+^(1+s)+f_-^(1+s)).             (8)

In particular, for s=2 and N>=2,

  nu D_N^2 <=64/49,   c_N>=7/16,
  f_+^3+f_-^3=19/64,
  M_A(2)/p <=64/49+76/49=20/7.                             (9)

Thus the moment-to-accepted-mass ratio stays bounded while the exact
common-reference radius grows linearly in N.

Full-input clipping and abort. Clip (4) at threshold d=2. Since the
bulk relative eigenvalues are <=10/7, the two b effects are unchanged.
For each u, only tag r_u is nonzero, and its clipped effect is

  F'_u^(r_u)=2q_N P_R;   F'_u^(r_j)=0 (j!=u),
  F'_u^(b_+/-)=F_u^(b_+/-).                                (10)

Retain the fine instrument by setting G'_u^(alpha,a)=F'_u^alpha/2.
This is exactly the local contraction after the original coarse
Lueders operation and its original normalized fine POVM: on every
coarse support the normalized fine effects are I/2. Complete the
sub-POVM with the genuine local failure effect

  A_u=I-sum_(alpha,a)G'_u^(alpha,a)=(1-2q_N)P_R.            (11)

It is positive since 2q_N<1. It depends only on Alice's full input u
and her own outcome; Bob remains the complete instrument H=I,K=I/2.
The accepted event loss from clipping is exactly

  delta_A(2)=E_u chi(sum_alpha(F_u^alpha-F'_u^alpha),I)
            =nu(1-2q_N) <=N^(-2),
  delta_B=0,       p'=1-delta_A(2).                          (12)

So a constant clipping threshold turns a Theta(N) exact maximum radius
into a subinstrument with vanishing state-weighted rejection. The generic
moment inequality (9) alone gives only delta_A(d)<=20/(7d^2); for example
at d=sqrt(N), N>=4, it certifies O(1/N), while the exact sector
calculation gives delta_A<=N^(-2). The sharper fixed-d conclusion in
(12) uses the explicit rare-sector location of the spectral tail, not
just the bounded full moment.

Cost and scope. The original accepted mass, reference accepted mass
p0=sum_alpha chi(F0^alpha,I), and their clipped counterparts are all
bounded away from zero (indeed p=p0=1 and p'=1-O(N^(-2))). There is no
rare-event conditioning or rejection sampler hidden in this example.
For the sub-POVM common-reference reconstruction, d_A=2,d_B=1,p0=1,
so its scale is lambda=2. The quantum system dimension stays three, but
the local coarse alphabet and the reference branch register have N+2
labels; their explicit representation costs O(N) basis states (or
O(log N) qubits to encode), and the full-input POVM/control description
also has O(N) entries. If N=2^m, that is exponential in m. This is not a
constant-resource or alphabet-free claim.

This finite family establishes a strict payoff-weighted improvement over
the raw maximum radius for this fixed event and its explicit instruments.
It is not a positive-gap game theorem, and it supplies no uniform
greedy-prefix compatibility or selected-history tail bound.
