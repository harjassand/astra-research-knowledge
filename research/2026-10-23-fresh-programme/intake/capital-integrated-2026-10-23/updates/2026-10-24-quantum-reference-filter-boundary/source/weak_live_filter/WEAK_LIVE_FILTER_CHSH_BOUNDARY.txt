AN OPERATIONAL MASS OBSTRUCTION AT THE WEAK LIVE-TABLE INTERFACE
2026-10-24. passive_lag. Direct commuting-operator proof; novelty UNKNOWN.

Main point

The old coarse histories need not be preserved for a one-sided mass
obstruction to remain. An exact original event/live-table replacement
with complete local success/fine/failure POVMs cannot universally have
BOTH weighted one-sided masses <=1. A concrete legal two-round source
already forces max{G_A,G_B}>1, although its original tested-event mass
is close to one. The proof uses only actual live binary outputs and the
ordinary CHSH vector inequality, with explicit abort effects.

This obstructs a specific EXACT TABLE plus mass-cap-one mechanism.
It does not obstruct arbitrary game-aware payoff-only repair, actual
Jensen gaps, or an answer-independent repetition theorem by another route.
The source game below has value one, and its irrelevant live correlations
may be discarded if only winning probability has to be retained.

1. Binary local filters with arbitrary shared resources

Fresh x,y are independent uniform bits. For each offline label c with
fixed positive weight w_c, let a commuting strategy have complete local
POVMs with conclusive binary outcomes a,b in {0,1} and a failure outcome.
It may have any finite or infinite commuting resource; c and its weights
do not depend on actual fresh x,y. Denote conclusive effects E_x^a,F_y^b.
Set
  S_x=E_x^0+E_x^1<=I, T_y=F_y^0+F_y^1<=I,
  A_x=E_x^0-E_x^1, B_y=F_y^0-F_y^1.
The expectation notation includes sum_c w_c times each resource state;
it need not be normalized. Define
  G_A=(1/2)sum_x <S_x>, G_B=(1/2)sum_y <T_y>,
  p=(1/4)sum_(x,y) <S_x T_y>>0,
  alpha=weighted CHSH losing mass on both success / p.        (W1)
All effects are actual operators of the complete local instruments.
No old coarse effect, classical rectangle certificate, or ancillary
mask kernel appears in (W1).

For any positive E,F with E+F=S<=I, one has
  (E-F)^2<=S.                                               (W2)
For example, on supp S write E-F=sqrt(S)K sqrt(S) with self-adjoint
contraction K. Then (E-F)^2<=sqrt(S)K^2sqrt(S)<=S. This bounded
support proof is also the POVM Kadison inequality; it needs no projective
measurement assumption.

On the weighted direct sum of resource Hilbert spaces, Cauchy--Schwarz
and the parallelogram identity give
  4p(1-2alpha)
    =<A_0 B_0>+<A_0 B_1>+<A_1 B_0>-<A_1 B_1>
    <=sqrt(||A_0 eta||^2+||A_1 eta||^2)
         sqrt(||(B_0+B_1)eta||^2+||(B_0-B_1)eta||^2)
    <=2sqrt(2 G_A G_B).                                    (W3)
The last step is (W2). Thus, for alpha<=1/2,
  max{G_A,G_B}>=sqrt(G_A G_B)
                    >=sqrt(2)p(1-2alpha).                  (W4)
The weights w_c can in particular be original tested-question weights
times lambda_c. Hence (W4) applies to the whole-coarse/B6 interface,
including arbitrary c-dependent resources, normalization, and mismatches.
It is not restricted to a common scalar lambda or a fixed old history.

2. Exact PR table and a classical benchmark

If for every x,y the both-success subtable is s/2 on each winning pair
a xor b=xy and zero on the losing pairs, then p=s,alpha=0 and
  max{mean Alice success,mean Bob success}/s>=sqrt(2).        (W5)
The conclusive probability need not be constant in x,y for (W4).

The exact classical optimum for this symmetric PR table is 3/2. Refine
all randomness so each hidden branch is a deterministic input response
with aborts. No branch with positive weight can produce a losing pair,
because those table entries are zero and all contributions are positive.
It cannot be conclusive on BOTH inputs of BOTH parties: the four XOR
equations would have inconsistent parity. Let A,B in {0,1/2,1} be its
fractions of conclusive inputs. Then 3AB<=A+B on every valid branch.
Averaging gives 3s<=a_bar+b_bar, so max(a_bar,b_bar)/s>=3/2.

Three shared random bits attain that value. Choose the detecting party
uniformly; choose its one conclusive input t uniformly; choose a shared
answer z uniformly. If Alice is detecting, she answers z only when x=t,
while Bob always answers z xor ty. If Bob is detecting, he answers z
only when y=t, while Alice always answers z xor xt. Thus s=1/2 and
a_bar=b_bar=3/4, with uniform PR-winning answers on conclusive branches.
This is an actual finite local construction, with no quantum resource.

The optimal quantum ratio is NOT established here: (W5) gives sqrt(2),
and the classical construction gives the quantum upper bound 3/2.
No NPA relaxation, qubit rank-one restriction, or unproved saturation
of Cauchy--Schwarz is being used as a realization.

3. A genuine original N443 event with required mass above one

Use the finite base game with actual questions x,y in {0,1}, uniform
independent. Alice answers a pair (z,a), Bob a pair (w,b), and the
predicate is
  theta((z,a),(w,b))=1{a xor b=zw}.                         (W6)
It ignores the actual questions. The game has value one (constant
z=w=a=b=0 wins), a limitation retained explicitly below.

Take two independent rounds and test C={1}. A legal repeated strategy
uses one Bell pair and measures it according to the ACTUAL round-two
questions x_2,y_2, with Alice observables Z,X and Bob observables
  (Z+X)/sqrt(2), (Z-X)/sqrt(2).
Let its binary outcomes be a,b. Each player copies the same local pair
(x_2,a) or (y_2,b) into BOTH of that player's round answers. This is one
local joint POVM depending only on the full own question string. It uses
no communication, opposite question, or coherent referee-question oracle.

The original unconditioned live bit table is exactly
  P(a,b|x_2,y_2)=[1+(-1)^(a+b+x_2 y_2)/sqrt(2)]/4.          (W7)
The tested event E=win_1 is precisely a xor b=x_2 y_2. Its mass is
  p0=(1+1/sqrt(2))/2.
The winning probability is the same for every fresh pair, so conditioning
on E leaves the live referee law uniform. Its conditional bit table is
exactly PR: 1/2 on each winning pair and zero otherwise. The FULL live
answer also records z=x_2 and w=y_2 with certainty.

Suppose any all-input exact binary-filter replacement preserves that
original accepted fine table, with arbitrary fixed offline c resources
and scalars lambda_c. In (W1) its weighted p is p0 and alpha is zero.
Therefore
  max{G_A,G_B}>=sqrt(2)p0=(1+sqrt(2))/2>1.                 (W8)
The old tested answers have been forgotten completely in this argument.
The lower bound uses only the required fresh-question/live-bit subtable.
One could even require just its success mass and zero CHSH losing mass;
the full coarse/fine source realization is unnecessary for the bound.

Consequently exact event/live preservation does NOT admit a universal
replacement with G_A,G_B<=1. Its sufficient mass-certificate charge
log(max{G_A,G_B}/p0) is at least log(sqrt(2)), larger than log(1/p0).
This is a lower bound on the MASS CERTIFICATE at this interface, not
on actual conditional-QRE/Jensen gaps or physical reconstruction error.

The value-one caveat is essential. A semantic operation allowed to
replace the live claimed questions and answers by constants can keep
the payoff perfect while discarding the PR fine table. Inequality (W8)
does not rule out that payoff-only repair. It also does not disprove an
answer-free event inequality with different constants or a different
representation mechanism; m=1 here and its existing error bound is loose.

4. Product signs give a further exact-interface resource inequality

For x,y in {0,1}^r, let conclusive outputs be bit strings and require
every conclusive branch to satisfy a_i xor b_i=x_i y_i for all i.
Take parity observables A_x,B_y (0 on abort). If p is total weighted
both-success mass under independent uniform x,y, then
  <A_x B_y>=(-1)^(x dot y) P_weighted(both success|x,y).
The order-N Walsh matrix H, N=2^r, obeys H H*=N I. Exactly as above,
  N^2 p <= N^(3/2)sqrt(G_A G_B),
so
  max{G_A,G_B}/p>=2^(r/2).                                (W9)
There is no assumption of product measurements, independent outcomes,
or constant success probability, and all coarse histories may be rebuilt.
If conclusive parity has conditional losing probability beta, multiply
the right side by 1-2beta (when nonnegative).

Natural postselection constructions for this target already have
log(1/p)=Theta(r). Thus (W9) does NOT by itself establish an additional
answer-entropy rate cost. It is an exact weak-instrument mass boundary,
not an information, Shannon randomness, dimension, or general repetition
lower bound. No classical tensor-product optimality is asserted.

Status

Direct operator proof and explicit finite source/construction supplied.
Independent checking requested. The remaining semantic task can avoid
this obstruction only by changing which fine correlations it must keep,
or by paying nontrivial one-sided masses, rather than merely discarding
the old coarse transcript while promising a universal mass cap one.
