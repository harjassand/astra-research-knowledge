# Scalar-vortex quench: bounded primary comparison

## Finding

Klein et al. do not use the proposed initial condition or operation. Their 2D trapped-GPE initial data are built by multiplying a computed ground state by centered-vortex profiles, \(\psi(r,0)=\mathcal N\psi_{gs}(r)\prod_j p_{q_j}(r-r_j)\). They discuss stirring, phase imprinting, or orbital-angular-momentum light as ways to prepare such vortex-bearing states. Their pair birth/reappearance is subsequent dynamics of seeded vortices (or additional pairs in a seeded tripole), not a quench from a strictly positive, nodeless real state under a scalar radial trap change. Thus their work establishes vortex-pair dynamics, not the particular preparation route here.

The strongest close operational precedent found is Tamura, Chen & Hung (2023), a direct experiment in a nonrotating 2D box-trapped Cs superfluid. They suddenly lower the circular-wall height from about 35 nK to 9 nK. Expansion launches an edge ring-dark wave; it develops a snake instability and forms vortex–antivortex dipoles/a necklace. The experiment uses in-situ absorption images (about 0.8 μm resolution) to follow the density defects. This directly establishes that a trap/density quench, without an imposed vortex phase, can produce vortex–antivortex pairs. It strongly dominates that broad capability claim.

The geometry and mechanism differ from the proposed constructed state: the experiment starts with a finite box edge whose density falls to zero over a healing-length region, and the observed route is edge-wave emission followed by transverse breakup. It does not prepare the everywhere-positive real profile \(c(r^2+a+bx)e^{-r^2/2}\) with \(a>b^2/4\), nor demonstrate its engineered scalar pre-potential followed by a radial harmonic quench. This difference is a scope boundary only; it is not a novelty or advantage conclusion. The exact positive-real-state quench remains unvalidated as an experiment, and no lifetime or performance advantage is established here.

## Scope caution

Tamura et al. establish an experimentally observed density-wave-to-vortex route after a box-wall quench, not a direct experimental realization of the engineered everywhere-positive scalar-trap construction. No source absence is used to infer novelty.
