SOURCE-DEPENDENT COUNTERFEIT TRANSLATION BY A FINITE-DEPTH CIRCUIT
2026-10-24. Central mathematical construction; no hardware/priority claim.
The independent mechanism was frozen first in OWN_OPENING.txt. Root's

1. Operation, source, and observable
-----------------------------------
Take a binary chain A, partitioned into B_k=[kL,(k+1)L-1]. An optional B
rail is untouched. The actual operation below is a finite-depth circuit;
the target is the fine right translation S. Record error means the coupled
classical probability that an output bit differs from the input bit which
S would transport there. This is a one-period test on a fresh product input,
not a norm approximation to S or equality of complete multitime records.

Let P be any reversible basis permutation on each L-bit block, with one
encoded bit q at its LAST physical register and an L-1-bit syndrome s.
Let C_left shift just the q registers one coarse block left. Define
  Omega = S P^-1 C_left P.                                     (1)
The separate shifts in (1) are proof descriptions, not required actuators.

2. Explicit three-layer implementation and strict quantum radius
---------------------------------------------------------------
Let C_right=C_left^-1 and let Pi cyclically rotate each unshifted block
right by one wire. In the active source-to-destination convention,
  S=Pi C_right.
For P_+=S P S^-1, the encoder P_+ acts on disjoint shifted blocks
[kL+1,(k+1)L]. Thus the group identity
  Omega=(S P^-1 S^-1)(S C_left)P=P_+^-1 Pi P                    (2)
is an ACTUAL three-layer block circuit. In state order: encode on the old
blocks, rotate wires within old blocks, decode on blocks shifted by one site.
Every gate uses only the existing L bits. No pure work register is assumed.
In particular Omega has index one by its explicit finite-depth circuit,
without invoking an index-one existence/classification theorem.

For L>=2, both Omega and Omega^-1 have strict QCA radius at most 2L-2.
For a full onsite operator in shifted block [kL+1,(k+1)L], its Heisenberg
cone under (2) stays in that shifted block at the first gate, then in
B_k union B_(k+1) after the rotation and encoder. If i=kL+s, 1<=s<=L,
the endpoint distances are s<=L and 2L-1-s<=2L-2. The inverse cone starts
with an unshifted block and ends in the two intersecting shifted blocks;
it has the same maximum. This is a full quantum-operator support bound,
not only a diagonal classical dependency bound. L=1 gives Omega=I.

The family is L-periodic in space; no one-site translation covariance is
claimed. On a finite ring require L divides its length and at least two
independent coarse blocks for the probability formula below. The index
statement concerns the infinite-chain QCA (or a compatible large-ring
family); a finite ring alone has no nontrivial GNVW index classification.

3. Biased full-rank input: exponentially small error at EVERY site
--------------------------------------------------------------
Fix 0<p<1/2, independently at every input bit, and let L=2m+1 be odd.
Choose the explicit encoder
  q=Maj(x_1,...,x_L),  s_j=x_j XOR x_(j+1), j=1,...,L-1.        (3)
At each fixed syndrome there are exactly two inverse strings, x and its
bitwise complement. Odd L gives them opposite majority labels, proving
that (3) is a bijection. Place (s_1,...,s_(L-1),q) on the physical wires.

Write Q_k=Maj(X_k) and epsilon_L=Pr[Bin(L,p)>L/2]. Independent input
blocks give iid Bernoulli(epsilon_L) labels Q_k. In the co-moving frame
F=S^-1 Omega=P^-1 C_left P, block k retains its own syndrome and replaces
Q_k by Q_(k+1). Therefore
  F(X)_k = X_k             if Q_(k+1)=Q_k,
           complement X_k otherwise.                          (4)
Every fine-site record has the EXACT same mismatch probability
  e_site=2 epsilon_L(1-epsilon_L).                             (5)
An untouched B rail has zero mismatch. A final fine shift merely relabels
these errors, so (5) holds at every physical output site as well.

For rho=2 sqrt(p(1-p))<1, Chernoff's exponential-moment inequality at
t=ln((1-p)/p) gives
  epsilon_L <= rho^L = exp[-L D(1/2 || p)].                    (6)
Thus max-site error, not just spatial average, decays exponentially in
L, while the strict radius is at most 2L-2 and every local input eigenvalue
is bounded below by the fixed p>0. The same exact source distribution is
used for every L. This supplies a genuine asymptotic obstruction to a
source-uniform polynomial-in-radius tag-error certificate for all fixed
positive full-rank floors. It does not identify the optimal exponent over
all QCAs or show that the old sufficient floor bound was numerically sharp.

Individual input labels do not hide an exception. For input bit b put
  r_1=Pr[Bin(L-1,p)>=(L-1)/2],
  r_0=Pr[Bin(L-1,p)>=(L+1)/2].
Then the conditional mismatch is
  e_b=epsilon_L+(1-2 epsilon_L)r_b,                            (7)
and its joint label/mismatch weight is p_b e_b, p_1=p,p_0=1-p.
Both are bounded by rho^L+rho^(L-1), hence vanish exponentially for
each FIXED p<1/2. At p=1/2 the construction instead has e_site=e_b=1/2;
the rare-majority mechanism is absent. There is no uniform exponent as
p tends to 1/2 (for p=1/2-c/sqrt(L) a nonzero CLT-scale error remains).

Logical rarity is optimal within this simple code class. A reversible
L-bit encoder gives exactly 2^(L-1) input strings to each value of q.
For p<1/2, the least probable half of all strings are precisely those
with more than L/2 ones. Consequently every such encoder satisfies
  min{Pr(q=0),Pr(q=1)} >= epsilon_L.                          (8)
An independent neighboring q changes the decoded block with probability
2 epsilon(1-epsilon). Injectivity forces at least one bit to differ then.
Hence the period-average error in this class is at least
2 epsilon_L(1-epsilon_L)/L, whereas (3) attains (5). This pins its optimal
exponential rate within the class up to a polynomial factor. It is not a
general lower bound over all finite-depth circuits or arbitrary QCAs.
Indeed the single central binomial term gives the explicit complement
  epsilon_L >= sqrt(p/(1-p)) rho^L/(L+1),                     (8a)
since the central binomial coefficient is at least 2^L/(L+1).

4. Uniform input: dilute a compensating random bit across many sites
------------------------------------------------------------------
Take M=2^k payload bits and k header bits, L=M+k, all independently fair.
The header H selects a payload J in {1,...,M}. The encoder swaps payload
J with the distinguished last payload wire q, leaving headers unchanged.
This is a reversible controlled swap on the same L bits. In F=S^-1 Omega,
only payload J can change: it becomes the selected bit from the neighboring
block. Thus in input-record coordinates,
  e_payload=1/(2M),  e_header=0,
  max-site error=1/(2M),  period-average error=1/(2L).          (9)
The final S rotates the error pattern one fine site. Thus a fixed output
slot called a 'header' in the old partition can receive a payload error;
(9)'s label-specific profile is in transported INPUT-record coordinates.
The maximum and period average are unchanged. B records still pass exactly.

For ANY reversible encoder of a uniform L-bit block into one binary q and
syndrome, q is fair and independent of the syndrome. Its replacement by an
independent fair q changes the decoded block with probability exactly 1/2.
At least one physical bit differs then, so mean error >=1/(2L). The selector
attains this average bound exactly. Its max error differs from that lower
bound only by (M+k)/M ->1. This optimality is confined to the stated
block-encoder/single-register countershift class; a matching asymptotic
lower bound for arbitrary QCAs is not supplied here.

5. Control, preparation, and cycle resources
-------------------------------------------
Three block layers are not three layers of fixed-arity nearest-neighbor
gates. The elementary arity, geometric diameter, source-code size and
clock/control synthesis grow with L. The majority encoder's last q bit
depends on the first input bit; any two-site nearest-neighbor circuit for
that encoder has depth at least L-1. The within-block cyclic wire rotation
also requires L-1 adjacent SWAPs in the direct implementation. No constant
time bounded-coupling Hamiltonian realization is inferred from block depth.

An explicit ancilla-free logical synthesis of (3) is: use CNOTs from left
to right to form the syndromes while retaining t=x_L; then q=t XOR f(s),
where f(s) is the majority of the syndrome reconstruction with t=0. A sum
of disjoint Boolean minterms implements f by at most 2^(L-1) syndrome-
controlled flips of t, each within the L-bit block. This finite synthesis
does not acquire inexpensive high-fan-in controls or a fixed local gate
set. The uniform selector similarly uses up to M-1 header-controlled
SWAPs, plus whatever elementary decomposition/routing is required.

No measurement, erasure, extra pure tape or unpriced reset is built into
either permutation. The initial biased product state has an extensive
entropy deficit compared with the uniform source. It is the actual source
resource, not an unmentioned catalyst. If it is the Gibbs state of an
identical onsite bit Hamiltonian Delta|1><1|, then Delta=kBT ln((1-p)/p).
For ideal isolated cyclic implementation on a compatible finite ring,
total work equals the final energy increase and is nonnegative by Gibbs
passivity. A direct coupling of input and output gives the bound
  0 <= W/(number of sites) <= Delta e_site.                   (10)
This charges only ideal net cyclic work, not gate time, dissipative controls
or refrigeration. Uniform degenerate bits permit zero ideal net energy
change; that does not make their controls or preparation free.

After a period, the biased output is generally correlated. Reusing it is
not fresh iid preparation. No stationary repeated-period claim follows.
For N coarse blocks on a ring, the chance that at least one block changes
is 1-epsilon_L^N-(1-epsilon_L)^N, tending to one at fixed L as N grows.
This is coupled record mismatch, not automatically marginal-state TV.
No all-input coherent shift, diamond norm approximation, simultaneous
full-record closeness, or boundary-intervention equivalence is claimed.
