Statement
---------
Let Omega be a strict radius-R0 QCA on cells A_i B_i, dA>=2, dB>=1,
D=dA*dB, and let sigma shift A right, fixing B. Prepare both rails in the
MAXIMALLY MIXED product state. Test A input(i-1) against output i and
B input i against output i. Set
  r=R0+1, C2=9+20rD,
  Etr = [ln(dA)/ln(D)]^2 / [1024 r^4 D^2 C2^2].              (1)
If every JOINT input-label/mismatch rate on the 6r-site band
T=[-r,5r-1] is <=Etr, Omega cannot have index one. A missing B factor
when dB=1 is trivial and its error is zero. No one-site translation
invariance or exact unitary channel on A alone is required.

For fixed local dimensions (1) scales as r^-6. The source must actually
be maximally mixed on the relevant preparation region; replacing it by
an arbitrarily fixed biased full-rank state invalidates this threshold.
The result excludes index-one counterfeits of this fixed translation
signature. It does not identify every arbitrary rational QCA index.

Independent proof challenge
---------------------------
1. In normalized canonical trace, a joint component error e gives
||alpha(P_component)-P_component||2<=sqrt(2e), for
alpha=sigma^-1 Omega, radius<=r. Multiplying the two component projectors
costs their SUM, so a full-cell rank-one projector moves by at most
delta=2sqrt(2e). There is no exponential input-density conversion.

2. The transpose/pinching argument works directly in this tracial norm.
For X=alpha(Eab), its compressed/dephased approximation Eab D has
u=2(1+KD)delta error, K=2r, and operator norm ||D||<=1. The defect
||Ebb(D*D-I)||2<=delta+2u is already in the full normalized trace.
It equals the embedded norm needed for Eab(DD*-I); no neighborhood-
dimension factor is silently discarded. The finite diagonal-group
intertwiner T satisfies alpha(Y)T=T Y and
  alpha(Y)-Y=alpha(Y)(I-T)+(T-I)Y.
Thus ||alpha(Y)-Y||2<=2KD delta without inverting T. With
beta=Theta alpha Theta and gamma=alpha beta, the total error is
  4u+delta+2KD delta=(9+10KD)delta=C2 delta.
Gamma has radius<=2r and index(gamma)=index(alpha)^2.

3. On S=[0,4r-1], tensor Weyl telescoping gives error
zeta=4r D C2 delta on each Weyl unitary. The reduced tracial Choi state
J on S plus its reference has EPR overlap >=1-zeta^2/2. Since the EPR
comparison state is pure,
  half||J-Phi_S||1 <= zeta/sqrt(2)=8r D C2 sqrt(e).            (2)
The factors in (2) include the two component projectors. This is not
obtained by summing an exponentially large operator-basis expansion.

4. Independently read primary RWW Proposition4.7, Eqs4.10-4.12. For
gamma radius2r, the left and right collars each have2r physical cells.
They are exactly the two halves of S, so both cross Choi marginals lie
inside S S'. The source's index is LOGARITHMIC; its difference-of-mutual-
informations is half the cross difference. Each cross state has dimension
D^(4r). Each of its individual marginals is EXACTLY maximally mixed,
because gamma preserves the canonical trace. The corresponding identity-
Choi cross state is their maximally mixed product.

5. Independently read Audenaert Theorem1/Eq6: distance means HALF trace
norm, and the bound is |S(rho)-S(sigma)|<=epsilon log(N-1)+h(epsilon),
in consistent logarithm units. Consequently each nonnegative cross MI
is <=4r ln(D)epsilon+h(epsilon). Half their absolute difference is at
most HALF this upper bound, not twice or the sum. If index(Omega)=1,
the fixed log-index of gamma has magnitude2 ln(dA). At
epsilon0=ln(dA)/(4r lnD)<=1/4 the upper bound is instead at most
.5ln(dA)+.5ln2<=ln(dA)<2ln(dA). Substituting (2) yields exactly (1).
This closes the specific normalization/collar/factor concerns internally.

Max versus mean and the remaining gap
-------------------------------------
The theorem applies at every translated cut, including for a nonuniform
QCA. Therefore every consecutive 6r-site test band contains a component
whose joint mismatch exceeds Etr. Partitioning into disjoint bands gives
spatial mean total-component mismatch at least Etr/(6r), when that mean
exists (or the corresponding lower-limit statement). This r^-7 mean
bound is a direct loose corollary, not an optimized asymptotic exponent.

The independent header-selector circuit has max error1/(2M) and period
mean1/[2(M+log2 M)], with radius at most2(M+log2 M)-2. Thus at uniform
input the proved general max lower bound scales r^-6 while an explicit
index-one circuit attains O(r^-1); the mean gap is likewise OPEN. The
selector is optimal only in its block-code/single-countershift class.
At any fixed biased p<1/2 the majority circuit instead attains exp(-c r)
max error. This source-dependent construction is consistent with (1),
and shows why no all-fixed-full-rank-source polynomial replacement follows.
