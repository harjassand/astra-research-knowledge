CLASSICAL TRANSLATION RECORDS AS A FINITE-PATCH CAUSAL-RESOURCE WITNESS
Central derivation, 2026-10-24. All earlier quantum notes remain unchanged.

What changes
-------------
The observed operation need not be an exact QCA, unitary reduced channel,
or even a state-independent channel on the tested patch. Replace that premise
by an explicit local Hamiltonian/coupling budget on a finite patch. Construct
an index-one two-layer circuit approximating the tested observables, and apply
the frozen classical-record theorem to that CIRCUIT. Its index-one obstruction
then gives a quantitative bound on the actual translation-record errors.

An arbitrary exterior/bath is permitted, with arbitrary internal dynamics
and initial correlations, provided its couplings into the patch are charged.
No index is assigned to the actual reduced, noisy or soft-tailed process.
The source-interface gain is a finite causal-resource test, not a new theorem
that one-dimensional finite-time Hamiltonians cannot implement a shift.

1. Local physical contract
---------------------------
Each tested cell has A and B components of dimensions d_A>=2 and d_B>=1;
D=d_A d_B. Fix ell>=1 and the finite interval
  W=[-8ell,24ell-1],  |W|=32ell.
During 0<=t<=T, the known patch Hamiltonian is
  H_W(t)=sum_(x in W) h_x(t)+sum_(x,x+1 in W) v_x(t),
  ||v_x(t)||<=J,  g=J/hbar.                                     (1)
Terms are piecewise continuous/bounded so the propagators exist. Onsite
terms h_x may be arbitrary local controls; their norms do not enter the
propagation bound below, because their product interaction picture preserves
both the support and norm of every bond term.

The full system may include an arbitrary exterior E. Its Hamiltonian H_E(t)
is unrestricted here, apart from well-defined evolution. Allow two end
couplings V_L,V_R, each supported on the corresponding end cell of W and E,
with ||V_L||,||V_R||<=J. Also permit explicitly budgeted bath couplings
  sum_(x in W) K_x(t),
where K_x acts only on patch cell x and E, and
  sum_of_term_norms_at_x ||K_x(t)||/hbar <= lambda.                (2)
The same exterior may be shared by all x; no independence or Markov premise
is used. The operator-norm bound is essential: an unbounded oscillator
coupling is not covered just by calling it weak noise. Setting lambda=0
allows arbitrary exterior dynamics only through the two end bonds.

All dynamics and input interfaces are FIXED independently of the sampled
input labels; the classical reference records are isolated from the drive.
Unaccounted label-dependent classical feedforward is an additional resource.

The prepared patch marginal is diagonal and product on both rails,
  tau_W=product_(i in W)(tau_A,i tensor tau_B,i),
  tau_A,i>=a_* I, tau_B,i>=b_* I, p=a_* b_*>0.                    (3)
Only the required local marginals/floors are used. The full initial W--E
state may have arbitrary correlations compatible with this tau_W. Copying
a local label can dephase exterior correlations, but leaves tau_W unchanged;
the comparison below is state independent and remains valid for that tagged
preparation. No global product state or global density eigenvalue floor is
required.

2. Explicit time-ordered interaction-path bound
------------------------------------------------
Define F_d(z)=sum_(n>=d) z^n/n!, d>=0. For an initial operator A_X supported
on one cell or one nearest-neighbor bond, and a target B_Y, use the smallest
number d of touching interaction bonds needed to reach Y from X (d=0 if
the supports overlap). With onsite terms removed, the standard commutator
recursion, with hbar restored, is
  C_B(X,t) <= C_B(X,0)
      +(2/hbar) sum_(Z touching X) integral_0^t
                         ||v_Z(s)|| C_B(Z,s) ds,                 (4)
  C_B(X,t)=sup_(A supported X) ||[tau_t(A),B]||/||A||.
One obtains (4) by differentiating the commutator, absorbing its homogeneous
commutator generator into a norm-preserving unitary evolution, and bounding
the remaining double commutator by twice the operator norm. This derivation
also applies to time-dependent v_Z(s).

At most three bonds touch an initial bond (two for an initial cell), and
at most three touch each subsequent bond, including a repeated/self bond.
The number of length-n touching paths is therefore <=3^n. Terms with n<d
vanish. The ordered time simplex contributes t^n/n!, and C_B(Z,0)<=2||B||
when Z reaches Y, otherwise zero. Iteration, with the vanishing factorial
remainder, yields the explicit uniform-in-size bound
  ||[tau_(t,s)(A_X),B_Y]||
       <=2||A_X||||B_Y|| F_d(6g|t-s|).                          (5)
The target can also act on an arbitrary spectator E: only whether its W
support meets the last interaction bond enters the recursion. Its exterior
dimension does not enter the norm or path count.

For fixed z,
  F_d(z)<=exp(z) z^d/d!.                                        (6)
Thus these finite-range tails are superexponential in distance, not merely
a fixed-exponent asymptotic estimate. The onsite interaction picture is a
product of local unitaries. For a fixed endpoint t its conjugation of A_X
preserves support and norm, so (5) and the following cut bounds carry back
to the laboratory frame, including arbitrary time-dependent onsite controls.

3. Two-cut truncation, including its distance offset
----------------------------------------------------
Compare a Hamiltonian to the version with two crossing bonds removed, so
an interval Lambda is decoupled. If each cut-bond support has interaction-path
distance at least d from X, Duhamel uses the decoupled inner observable and
an outer norm-isometric evolution. By (5),
  ||tau_full(A_X)-tau_Lambda(A_X)||
       <=4g||A_X|| integral_0^T F_d(6gs)ds
       =(2/3)||A_X||F_(d+1)(6gT).                              (7)
Here integral_0^T F_d(6gs)ds=F_(d+1)(6gT)/(6g); the J=0 case follows by
continuity. This calculation uses only the norms of the two crossing terms
and the inner interval evolution; the exterior evolution is arbitrary.

For a central boundary bond supported on {b,b+1}, use the 2ell-site patch
  Lambda_b=[b-ell+1,b+ell].
Its omitted cut bonds have supports {b-ell,b-ell+1} and
{b+ell,b+ell+1}. Their distance from the central bond is ell-1. Accordingly
the Duhamel integration raises the tail index by one and gives
  ||U_0(t)^dagger v_b(t)U_0(t)
       -U_(0,Lambda_b)(t)^dagger v_b(t)U_(0,Lambda_b)(t)||
       <=(2J/3)F_ell(6gt).                                     (8)
At ell=1 the cut distance is zero and (8) starts at F_1, as it must.

4. Actual construction of the depth-two approximant
-----------------------------------------------------
Partition W into sixteen consecutive blocks of length 2ell. Let H_0 be H_W
with its fifteen interblock bonds removed. Its propagator U_0(t) is one layer
of disjoint 2ell-cell block unitaries. Exactly,
  U_W(t)=U_0(t)U_I(t),
where the interaction-picture generator of U_I is
  K(t)=sum_b U_0(t)^dagger v_b(t)U_0(t).
Replace each summand by its patch-restricted version in (8). The patches
around consecutive boundaries are disjoint 2ell-cell intervals, so their
time-dependent generators commute across patches at all times. The truncated
interaction-picture propagator U_I,ell is therefore one disjoint gate layer.
Define
  C=U_0(T) U_I,ell(T),
and extend C by identity outside W on a mathematical D-dimensional chain.
It is a strict depth-two circuit, hence index(C)=1, with safe radius
  R_C=4ell-2.                                                    (9)
This is a proof approximant, not an extra control operation performed on the
device. The same external experiment supplies only the tag probabilities.

Summing (8) over fifteen boundaries and integrating the generator difference
gives
  ||U_I(T)-U_I,ell(T)|| <=(15/9)F_(ell+1)(6gT).
For a unit-norm tested projector, passage to Heisenberg effects contributes
twice that norm, giving (10/3)F_(ell+1). There is no global system-size factor.

Set r=R_C+1=4ell-1. The companion's tested output band and input-floor region
are
  T0=[-r,5r-1]=[-4ell+1,20ell-6],
  U=[-r-R_C,5r-1+R_C]=[-8ell+3,24ell-8].                        (10)
W contains U. The output band's distances to W's two inside endpoints are
at least 4ell+1 and 4ell+5, exceeding both R_C and an ell cut buffer. Using
the conservative d>=ell-1 version of (7) for the end couplings gives the
outer-window effect error <=(2/3)F_ell. Thus, absent K_x, every tested
component projector has actual-versus-C error at most
  eta_ell=(2/3)F_ell(6gT)+(10/3)F_(ell+1)(6gT)
         <=4F_ell(6gT).                                        (11)
No exact QCA radius is assigned to the actual evolution.

5. A charged non-Markov bath-coupling budget
--------------------------------------------
In Duhamel compare the ACTUAL global evolution to the reference H_W+H_E,
not to a globally local ideal chain. The inner observable is always the
ideal W-only Heisenberg projector O_i(s) tensor I_E. The actual outer global
unitary preserves norms. End terms give the cut error already in (11).
The remaining perturbations satisfy, from (2) and (5),
  sum_x ||[K_x,O_i(s)]||/hbar
       <=2lambda sum_(x in W) F_|x-i|(6gs)
       <=2lambda (1+12gs)exp(6gs),                             (12)
because F_0(z)+2sum_(d>=1)F_d(z)=(1+2z)exp(z). Hence the additional effect
error is bounded by
  nu_lambda=2lambda integral_0^T (1+12gs)exp(6gs)ds
       <=2lambda T(1+12gT)exp(6gT).                            (13)
For g>0 the exact integral is
  nu_lambda=[lambda/(3g)]{(12gT-1)exp(6gT)+1};
at g=0 it is 2lambda T. One may cap the total actual-versus-C effect error
by the trivial norm bound 1. These are coupling-rate/action bounds, not a
claim about dissipated heat, entropy production, or electrical work.

Arbitrary shared E dynamics and W--E initial correlations do not enter
(12)--(13); E is a spectator of the inner reference evolution, not of the
actual one. Norm-bounded K_x may mediate collective exterior shortcuts, but
their coupling cost remains in (13). This is not an unpriced unknown-bath
loophole. The physical source must acquire the operator-norm interface budget.

Alternatively, under a trusted onsite Markov model with jumps L_xa satisfying
sum_a||L_xa||^2<=kappa per cell, the same bound (13), with lambda replaced by
kappa, follows from CP-unital contraction and
||D_x(O)||<=sum_a||L_xa||||[O,L_xa]|| for self-adjoint ideal O. This is a
separate sufficient model, not an inferred Lindblad law or index of a noisy
channel. The bounded-Hamiltonian bath model above needs no Markov assumption.

6. What classical records would violate this resource envelope?
----------------------------------------------------------------
Use the companion's A-input-(i-1) versus A-output-i and B-input-i versus
B-output-i comparisons on T0. Let e_actual denote their JOINT input-label
and mismatch probabilities. For the SAME tagged preparation, norm comparison
gives
  e_C <= e_actual + eta_ell + nu_lambda.                          (14)
The C experiment depends only on tau_W, even if copying the reference changed
its correlations with E. This is why no exterior preparation assumption or
global product density floor is inserted. Reference labels do not select a
different Hamiltonian or drive sequence.

Put
  delta_ell=1/[1540 r D(9+24rD)],
  E_ell=p^(2R_C+1)delta_ell^2/4
       =p^(8ell-3)delta_ell^2/4.                                (15)
If all actual joint component mismatches were <=
  G_ell=E_ell-eta_ell-nu_lambda>0,                               (16)
then all C mismatches would be <=E_ell. The frozen translation-signature
companion would give index(C)=d_A, contradicting its depth-two index 1.
Consequently at least one actual mismatch in this finite tested band must
exceed G_ell whenever that expression is positive.

Thus sufficiently accurate classical records can falsify the specified
duration/coupling/locality/noise envelope. They do not, by themselves, infer
which resource was missing or assign a chiral index to the actual process.
The audited finite patch, dimension, population floor and fixed-source
contract are premises, not conclusions drawn from those same records.

For lambda=0 and any fixed finite gT, D and p>0, a positive gap exists at
some finite ell: (6) makes eta_ell superexponentially small, whereas E_ell
decays only as p^(8ell) times a reciprocal polynomial in ell. No numerical
sweep or fit is needed for that existence statement. The required patch
has 32ell cells and must actually fit the available device; ell cannot be
sent to infinity on fixed finite hardware. With nonzero lambda, the known
noise budget must fit beneath the finite E_ell margin at the chosen ell;
arbitrarily small errors or unbounded noise are not certified for free.

The constants are conservative; the earlier already-small tag thresholds
mean this is not a practical sampling or hardware-advantage claim. Finite
confidence, calibration, reference/reset, local dimension, controls and
the Hamiltonian norm/action bounds all remain resources. No experiment,
purchase, outreach or public release has been performed or authorized.
