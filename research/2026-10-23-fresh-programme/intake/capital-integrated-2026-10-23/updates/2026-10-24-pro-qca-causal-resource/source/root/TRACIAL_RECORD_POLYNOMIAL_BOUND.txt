Claim and exact scope

D=dA*dB. Prepare MAXIMALLY MIXED product inputs on both components. Let
sigma be the partial A shift, with index dA, B fixed. Put r=R0+1 and
C=9+20rD. Test component basis labels on T=[-r,5r-1], A input(i-1) against
output i and B input i against output i. If EVERY joint label-mismatch
probability e_A,i,a,e_B,i,b is at most

 Etr=(log(dA)/log(D))^2/[1024 r^4 D^2 C^2],

then Omega CANNOT have index1. This is an index-one counterfeit exclusion,
not an assertion fixing an arbitrary rational QCA index. No translation

Unknown channels/noisy processes are outside this QCA claim; the frozen

1. Local normalized-trace estimate
Let tau be the canonical normalized trace and ||X||2=sqrt(tau(X*X)).
Put alpha=sigma^-1 Omega, radius<=r. For equal-trace projections P,Q,
||Q-P||2^2=2tau[P(I-Q)P]. Thus actual component tag tests give
||alpha(P_A)-P_A||2<=sqrt(2e), and likewise B. Products give for every
full-cell rank-one basis projector P_i,ab
||alpha(P_i,ab)-P_i,ab||2<=2sqrt(2e)=delta.
These are normalized L2 estimates, without the old exponential density
floor converting probabilities into worst-case operator norms.

2. Transpose cancellation directly in tracial L2
For an inner-block site i and E_ab, let X=alpha(E_ab). Compression gives
F=P_a X P_b=E_ab D0, with ||X-F||2<=2delta and ||D0||op<=1.
For each of K=2r neighbors, [X,alpha(P_jc)]=0 implies
||[X,P_jc]||2<=2delta. Compressing and successively pinching all neighbors
gives diagonal D, ||D||op<=1, with
||X-E_ab D||2<=u=2(1+KD)delta.
The 1/sqrt(D) normalized matrix-unit factor in compressed estimates is
restored when re-embedded, so no dimension-of-the-whole-neighborhood factor
is inserted. From X*X=alpha(P_b),
||E_bb(D*D-I)||2<=delta+2u.

On the neighbor diagonal algebra average T=mean_g alpha(g)g* over the
finite product clock group. It exactly intertwines alpha(Y)T=T Y.
Clock expansions and tensor telescoping give ||T-I||2<=KD delta.
For ||Y||op<=1 in that diagonal algebra,
alpha(Y)-Y=alpha(Y)(I-T)+(T-I)Y,
so ||alpha(Y)-Y||2<=2KD delta. No inverse of T is required.

Let beta=Theta alpha Theta and gamma=alpha beta. Since D is diagonal,
beta(E_ab) is within u of E_ab D*. Combining the previous bounds yields
||gamma(E_ab)-E_ab||2<=4u+delta+2KD delta
 =(9+10KD)delta=C delta.
Gamma has radius<=2r and index(gamma)=index(alpha)^2 by the same GNVW
transpose-index identity imported in the frozen operator-norm proof.

3. Local Choi state, no exponential operator-basis sum
Take S=[0,4r-1], L=4r sites. Every onsite Weyl unitary has D entries and
the trace is invariant under gamma. Tensor telescoping therefore gives
||gamma(W)-W||2<=L D C delta for EVERY tensor Weyl W on S.
Let J be the Choi marginal on S and its reference S', obtained by
tracially averaging the exterior. The EPR overlap satisfies
Fe(J,Phi_S)=mean_W tau[W* gamma(W)]
 >=1-(L D C delta)^2/2.
The mean is real; the squared-distance identity applies because both W
and gamma(W) are unitaries. Fidelity to the PURE EPR state then implies
  half||J-Phi_S||1<=L D C delta/sqrt(2)
                   =8r D C sqrt(e)=epsilon.

4. Primary-verified local index formula and entropy bound
RWW Proposition4.7 Eq4.12 expresses log(index gamma) as one half the
difference of two cross mutual informations of its Choi state. Each collar
has gamma's radius 2r sites, so both cross marginals are contained in S S'
for a cut at the center of S. At identity they are maximally mixed products.
Their individual marginals remain exactly maximally mixed for any QCA.

Each cross state has dimension D^(4r), and distance<=epsilon from its
maximally mixed product. Audenaert Theorem1 gives
0<=I_cross<=4r log(D) epsilon+h(epsilon),
provided epsilon is in the increasing part of the entropy bound; below
epsilon0<=1/4 this is automatic. Since both I_cross lie in [0,B],

 |log(index gamma)|<=B/2
       =2r log(D)epsilon+h(epsilon)/2.

If index(Omega)=1, then index(alpha)=1/dA and index(gamma)=1/dA^2.
The left side is 2log(dA). Put epsilon0=log(dA)/[4r log(D)]<=1/4.
The right side at any epsilon<=epsilon0 is at most
log(dA)/2+log(2)/2<=log(dA)<2log(dA), a contradiction.
The displayed Etr is exactly (epsilon0/[8r D C])^2. QED subject to the
independent review of these new norm/Choi/entropy reductions.

All logarithms above may consistently be natural or base2. Threshold uses

Scientific limits
This replaces an exponential sufficient threshold by a polynomial one
ONLY for maximally mixed probes and a fixed index-one counterfeit target.
It does not optimize the polynomial exponent, close the upper/lower range
gap, establish practical sample cost/calibration, or make a novelty claim.
The group's independently frozen biased-majority construction may show
exponential suppression is inherent when a biased source is held fixed;
that upper bound and this tracial argument are complementary, not conflicting.
