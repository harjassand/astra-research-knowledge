PRIVATE ASTRA P1 — ORDINARY LOSS AND THE PHYSICAL HUSIMI RECORD LAW
2026-10-24. Root proof. Previous proof/code/data remain unchanged.

SCOPE
=====
This is a conditional mathematical construction for a supplied physical model.
The main theorem covers arbitrary physical initial preparations, simultaneous
number-conserving hopping/density interactions, ordinary unscaled equal loss,
and calibrated sparse observation under a common implemented feedback policy.
It proves complete permitted RECORD accuracy, not physical-state trace
reconstruction from a classical mixture. Physical access, initial/controller
correlations, numerical precision and potentially exponential mode dependence
are retained. Historical novelty and practical superiority remain UNKNOWN.

The canonical physical Husimi law replaces the earlier purification-amplitude
diagonal. A backward-effect argument needs no derivatives of the preparation.
Ordinary loss bounds accumulated nonlinear sensitivity and the remaining count
budget. A separate heterodyne interface can acquire initial finite cell weights
and a probability-tail certificate; no such experiment was performed here.
This does not use P3's coherent-overlap verifier or establish that composition.

1. PHYSICAL MODEL AND OBSERVATION
================================
There are m bosonic modes, N=sum_i N_i, b_i=sqrt(theta)a_i, and
  H_theta(t)=a^dagger h(t)a+(theta/2)Nvec^T J(t)Nvec,
  L_full(rho)=-i[H_theta,rho]+d sum_i D[a_i](rho), d>0.       (1)
h is Hermitian, J real symmetric. Their prescribed coefficients are piecewise
bounded on each exposed record prefix. The Hamiltonian always commutes with
total N. No coherent drive, creation bath or replenishment is included in the
main arbitrary-preparation theorem.

Passively mixed output mode u_r has observed jump
  M_r=sqrt(theta d kappa_r)u_r^dagger a
      =sqrt(d kappa_r)u_r^dagger b.
Require theta sum_r kappa_r u_r u_r^dagger<=I, so the remaining loss is a
positive unobserved channel and the full bath is still (1). Orthogonal ports
with theta kappa_r<=1 satisfy this directly. Uniform envelopes are
  j0>=||J||_2, jF>=||J||_F,
  cJ>=||J1+(diag J)/2||_infinity,
  k0>=sum_r kappa_r||u_r||^2,
  kop>=||sum_r kappa_r u_r u_r^dagger||_2, kop<=k0.          (2)
Output mixing, detection fractions and their calibration are physical inputs.

Readout is a finite number of finite bins, each exposing a finite alphabet:
for example no event/at least one event, or no event/first port. A finite
absorbing latch implements it; physical dynamics continues after triggering.
Controls depend on time, completed bins and the actual accessible controller,
not an unread latch or hidden trigger time. Every compared model uses the
SAME fully specified implemented policy, including its fallback branches.
No derivative of that policy with respect to a hidden amplitude is taken.

The joint initial system/controller law is supplied. One can condition on an
accessible initial controller value or retain its classical register throughout.
Normalized accuracy on every rare initial controller value is not inferred from
a joint-law bound. Shared random seeds include a randomized controller.
Its numerical representation and memory remain part of the implemented state.

2. CANONICAL INITIAL LAW AND POSITIVE CURRENT PREDICTOR
=====================================================
For any physical density operator define
  mu_(theta,rho)(dz)=(pi theta)^(-m)
      <z/sqrt(theta)|rho|z/sqrt(theta)> d^(2m)z,
  A_theta(f)=integral f(z)|z/sqrt(theta)><z/sqrt(theta)|
                                      d^(2m)z/(pi theta)^m. (3)
Thus Tr rho A_theta(f)=integral f dmu, A_theta(1)=I, and positivity is exact.

The current positive predictor transports its distribution by
  dot z=-i[h z+diag(J|z|^2)z]-(d/2)z,                      (4)
with registered marked intensities
  lambda_r=d kappa_r|u_r^dagger z|^2.                      (5)
Its initial distribution is (3), jointly with the same controller.
For a bin, transport and multiply by its actual likelihood:
  ell_none=exp(-integral lambda_total),
  ell_OR=1-ell_none,
  ell_first,r=integral lambda_r(t)exp(-integral_0^t lambda_total)dt.
These positive rows integrate all hidden later events. Normalize only after an
exposed outcome. The complete distribution, rather than only a mean amplitude
or covariance, generally changes under the resulting non-Gaussian conditioning.

This predictor is not a claimed quantum-state reconstruction. Replacing even
the vacuum by its Husimi-weighted coherent-state mixture produces a thermal
state of mean one, at trace distance 1/2 from the vacuum for every theta.
The record theorem below is compatible with that failure.

3. EXACT ANTI-WICK GENERATOR AND COUNT IDENTITIES
===============================================
Write D_i=z_i partial_z_i, Dbar_i=bar z_i partial_barz_i and
partial_z=(partial_x-i partial_y)/2. V_H is the backward derivative for the
Hamiltonian part of (4). Direct coherent-state calculus gives
  L_full^* A_theta(f)=A_theta[
    V_H f-(d/2)sum_i(D_i+Dbar_i)f
    +theta R_J f+theta d sum_i partial_z_i partial_barz_i f],
  R_J f=-(i/2)sum_ij J_ij[
       (Dbar_i+1)(Dbar_j+1)-(D_i+1)(D_j+1)]f.              (6)
The general ordinary gain/loss bath part is exactly
  sum_i[(b_i-d_i)(D_i+Dbar_i)/2
                         +theta d_i partial_z_i partial_barz_i].
Only pure loss is used for the main tail and exposure arguments.

For a normalized rotated output mode, z_u=u^dagger z,
  J_r^*A_theta(f)=d kappa_r A_theta[
    (|z_u|^2-theta)f
    -theta(z_u partial_z_u+bar z_u partial_barz_u)f
    +theta^2 partial_z_u partial_barz_u f].                (7)
Non-unit modes carry their norm factors. Observed creation, when considered
separately, has the symbol equal to its scaled rate times |z_u|^2 f.

The identities follow also from A_theta(z^p bar z^q)=b^p(b^dagger)^q. The
independent exact script unscaled_bath_20261024/verify_husimi_generator.py
passed 10500 Gaussian-rational checks on INFINITE Fock ladders: complex h,
cross/self J including a negative diagonal, unequal gain/loss and both observed
dispatches. No finite output-matrix boundary was used. This validates algebra,
not the convergence theorem by numerical extrapolation.

For real f at r=|z| let K1=||grad f||, K2=||Hess f||_op. Expanding (6) gives
  |R_J f|<=(jF/2)r^2 K2+cJ r K1,
  |d sum_i partial_z_i partial_barz_i f|<=(md/2)K2.          (8)
The second-order part of R_J is -(i/2)sum J_ij[
bar z_i bar z_j partial_bar_i partial_bar_j-z_i z_j partial_i partial_j].
Each complex second derivative is bounded by K2/2; the angular first-order
coefficient is J1+(diag J)/2. This fixes the ordering constants.

Exact Husimi positivity is NOT exact classical diffusion. For one self-Kerr
mode H_theta=theta N^2/2, the backward Hamiltonian operator is
  -(r^2-theta)partial_phi
             +(theta/2)(x partial_x+y partial_y)partial_phi.
At (r,0), its real diffusion matrix has eigenvalues +/-theta r^2/2. Loss adds
theta d I/2, leaving theta(d+/-r^2)/2. At r^2>d the positive maximum principle
fails in the negative direction. The exact equation acts on the constrained
set of quantum Husimi densities. Equation (4), with paid residual (6)--(7), is
the positive approximate predictor; the full Q equation is not relabelled a
positive Markov diffusion on arbitrary classical densities.

4. DISSIPATIVE EXPOSURE AND BACKWARD RECORD REGULARITY
====================================================
From a current time t0, set
  y=exp(ds/2)z(t0+s), v=1-exp(-ds), 0<=v<1.
Then
  dy/dv=-i[h(t0+s)/(d(1-v))]y
             -(i/d)diag(J(t0+s)|y|^2)y,                   (9)
and
  lambda_r(t,z)dt=kappa_r(t)|u_r(t)^dagger y|^2 dv.         (10)
The radius |y| stays fixed. The singular linear coefficient is skew-Hermitian,
so it adds no derivative-norm growth. It can be removed in an interaction
picture on every finite physical interval. No limiting amplitude or free
numerical evolution at v=1 is assumed.

Fix ANY event in the complete finite exposed-record space. Its classical
backward probabilities f_(prefix,latch)(t,z) lie in [0,1]. In a radius-r ball,
with j=j0/d, the transformed nonlinear field has
  ||Dv_nl||<=3j r^2, ||D^2v_nl||<=6j r.
The aggregate latch-rate gradient and Hessian bounds are 2k0 r and 2k0.
Terminal record effects are constants in z. Differentiating the backward
finite-state Markov equation and using its contraction/maximum principle gives
  K1'<=3j r^2 K1+2k0 r,
  K2'<=6j r^2 K2+(6j+8k0)r K1+2k0.                       (11)
The 8k0 term comes from two intensity-gradient products and the difference of
two effect gradients. Exposing/resetting a latch merely relabels the effects;
it does not increase these bounds. Controls given a prefix are fixed when
differentiating hidden z, including for a discontinuous numerical policy.

Since the remaining v interval has length <=1, a convenient common bound is
  K1(t,z)<=A1 r exp(a r^2),
  K2(t,z)<=(A2+A3 r^2)exp(a r^2),                          (12)
where
  a=6j0/d, A1=A2=2k0, A3=k0(6j0/d+8k0).
The sharper K1 exponent is 3j0/d. These bounds do not grow with physical time
or number of bins. They retain the interaction/loss ratio and amplitude radius.
The independent time-change audit is in the coupled_positive_core agent's
unscaled_bath directory; an independent variational check is also retained by
coupled_incumbents_metrics.

5. COMPLETE-RECORD RESIDUAL AND AN EXPLICIT LOW-N THEOREM
=======================================================
The quantum/latch generator is the full Lindblad generator in each block plus
the observed jump dispatched to the appropriate classical destination. Applied
to f_dest-f_source, (7) differs from multiplication by (5) by at most
  theta g(1+2r K1)+theta^2 g K2, g=d k0.                   (13)
This uses |f_dest-f_source|<=1 and derivative differences <=2Kj. From (8), the
unconditional drift defect is at most
  theta[(jF/2)r^2 K2+cJ r K1+(md/2)K2].
Combining with (12) yields
  |residual(t,z)|<=exp(a r^2)[C0+C2 r^2+C4 r^4],            (14)
where
  C0=theta[g+(md/2)A2]+theta^2 g A2,
  C2=theta[(jF/2)A2+(cJ+2g)A1+(md/2)A3]+theta^2 g A3,
  C4=theta(jF/2)A3.                                      (15)

Suppose first the physical state is supported on total N<=L. Every Hamiltonian
and hidden/observed loss preserves this upper bound. Its unnormalized quantum
record/latch blocks remain positive and their traces sum to one. Angular
integration of the coherent POVM gives, in every total-number n sector,
  r^2/theta ~ Gamma(n+m,1).
It follows for a theta<1 that every such normalized block obeys
  integral r^(2k)exp(a r^2)dmu <= M_k(theta,L),
  M_k=theta^k (L+m)^(rising k)(1-a theta)^(-(L+m+k)),
  k=0,1,2.                                               (16)
Arbitrary angular coherences are allowed. A finite number cutoff does NOT
remove a theta<1, because Husimi densities retain infinite Gaussian tails.

Differentiate Tr[rho_exact(t) A_theta(f_classical(t))], summing its CQ blocks.
The backward classical equation cancels leading terms; at exposed boundaries
the effects match under the common policy. At terminal time A_theta(f) is the
exact record-event indicator, and at initial time it is integration against the
physical Husimi law. Integrating (14) against positive exact Husimi blocks and
using (16) proves the complete-record bound
  TV(P_quantum^T,P_classical^T)<=epsilon_L(T),
  epsilon_L(T)=min{1,T(C0M0+C2M1+C4M2)}.                   (17)
No normalized rare posterior or hidden-event history is used.

Domain justification: on N<=L the exact law is finite dimensional and its
Husimi kernels are finite polynomials times exp(-r^2/theta). Equation (12) and
a theta<1 justify the integrations by parts in (6)--(7). Piecewise controls
follow by bounded approximation and finite-dimensional/ODE continuity with
the same constants. Arbitrary terminal events require no spatial regularity:
their terminal functions are constants. Thus no global bounded-Hessian
pseudodifferential theorem is imported for the unbounded quartic Hamiltonian.

6. ARBITRARY PREPARATIONS: ONE TAIL CHARGE, NOT A HIDDEN REGULARITY PREMISE
=========================================================================
The allowed quantum instruments are globally number-phase covariant; their
record effects commute with N. Classical records are invariant under a common
phase of z. Therefore dephasing initial total N changes neither compared law.
This is a predictive equivalence, not a claim of physical trace closeness.

After this common dephasing, quantum and classical laws share the same low/high
N mixture weights. For p_>L=Tr rho 1_(N>L),
  TV(P_quantum^T,P_classical^T)
   <=(1-p_>L)epsilon_L(T)+p_>L<=epsilon_L(T)+p_>L.          (18)
Keep the same ACTUAL policy throughout; do not replace it by one newly designed
for the truncated preparation. Linearity of the record map in the initial law
then justifies the single high-sector charge.

If E=theta Tr(Nrho) is supplied, p_>L<=E/[theta(L+1)]. With theta L<=R0^2,
(16) is uniform in occupation. For fixed desired tail tolerance choose R0 and
then theta. This is not an O(theta) claim for all states under only a first
moment bound. To prove uniform convergence in that broader class, let the
cutoff grow slowly: if a>0, theta0<1/a, abar=a/(1-a theta0), choose
  R0^2=log(1/theta)/(2abar), L=floor(R0^2/theta).
Then the tail is O(1/log(1/theta)); the low-sector error is bounded by
O(T sqrt(theta) poly(log(1/theta))). A supplied bounded scaled-number support
or suitable exponential tail gives the stronger fixed-horizon O(theta) result.
No derivative of an initial wavefunction, kernel or Husimi density is required.

A measured probability-tail certificate from Section10 can replace the finite
mean assumption entirely. It need not establish a finite full mean energy.

7. LONG RECORDS AND FINITE REMAINING EVENT MASS
==============================================
Exactly, E[N(T)]=exp(-dT)E[N(0)], even with the permitted feedback controls.
If E is finite, probability of any later registered event is at most
  kop E exp(-dT)                     (quantum),
  kop(E+m theta)exp(-dT)             (Husimi-initialized classical).  (19)
The extra m theta is necessary: integral |z|^2 dmu=E+m theta.

Use deterministic no-event symbols after T in a longer record H>=T. For the
retained N<=L sector, (19) and (17), combined with the same high-sector split,
give the energy-free version
  TV(P_quantum^H,P_classical^H)
   <=p_>L+epsilon_L(T)+kop theta(2L+m)exp(-dT).             (20)
At a bin boundary, append no-event symbols and run the retained controller on
them. At an internal T, retain the UNREAD latch, suppress later triggers, and
expose that stored latch only at its scheduled boundary; later bins have no
events. No extra information reaches the controller. Terminal effects at T
remain constant in z on each prefix/latch block. This is uniform over longer
finite horizons and finite bin counts. Fixed target accuracy admits a paid retained
horizon independent of occupation. With only bounded E, T~log(1/theta)/d also
preserves the convergence in Section6.

The known skew h/(1-v) may keep rotating as v->1. Equation (20) avoids taking
that limit by truncating rare future detections, not by claiming free simulation
of infinite time. Controller processing of zero symbols and actual controls
are still implemented resources. Pumping, drives and hot baths can replenish
information and invalidate this finite-loss budget.

For a predictor which actually stops integration at T, use a direct stronger
composition. Let F_T retain the actual augmented prefix/controller/seed and
unread latch as above, then continue with no future triggers. On N<=L,
  TV(P_quantum^H,F_T P_classical^T)
      <=epsilon_L(T)+kop theta L exp(-dT).
The common low/high split gives
  TV(P_quantum^H,F_T P_classical^T)
      <=p_>L+epsilon_L(T)+kop theta L exp(-dT).             (20b)
Numerical finite-T errors contract under the same F_T. Equation (20) compares
two FULL laws; (20b) is the stopped implementation bound. Applying (20) first
and then truncating the full classical law requires another classical tail.

  epsilon_grid<=2k0 R delta exp(3j0 R^2/d),                (22)
for the whole marked record and hence every permitted coarsening. Integrate
the intensity difference 2d k0 R delta exp(-dt)exp(3j0 R^2/d) to prove (22).
The bound is independent of elapsed time and bin count.

controller. At each bin compute a positive stochastic likelihood row, reweight,
normalize and transport. A cover has O_m((1+R/delta)^(2m)) nodes. The mode and
interaction/radius constants can be large. Raw past histories need not be kept
beyond the controller, but computing every possible history still can be
exponential. Regridding current positions is not free.

Initial grid overflow is paid by its probability. Initial positive cell-mass
error contracts to record error. For validated GLOBAL node-path error e(t),
use the additional intensity bound
  int_0^T d k0 [R_exact(t)+R_num(t)]e(t)dt.
Local integrator errors must first be propagated. Positive stochastic-row

Add (18), (20), or (20b) as appropriate, initial grid overflow, cell-mass error,
(22), flow error and
likelihood arithmetic. Quantum and continuum references use the same grid-based

9. RELATIVE COHERENCE AS THE ACCESS-DEPENDENT CURRENT VARIABLE
============================================================
The global phase is unobservable for this instrument class. Put X=zz^dagger.
It is positive rank one (or zero) and satisfies
  dot X=-i[h+diag(J diag X),X]-dX,
  lambda_r=d kappa_r Tr(u_r u_r^dagger X).                 (23)
The current PROBABILITY LAW of X and controller is sufficient. Its mean matrix
does not generally close either the nonlinear dynamics or no-event likelihoods.

A separating statement is available if calibrated output mixing and detection
weights allow B=sum kappa_r u_r u_r^dagger in a full-dimensional open positive
range, without changing the unconditional bath. Fix any common bounded h,J
control in a short bin. Its no-event probability is
  E exp[-Tr(BY_t)], Y_t=d int_0^t X_s ds>=0.                (24)
If two current laws give every permitted future probability, their Laplace
transforms for Y_t agree on that open B range. Holomorphy on Re B>0 and exponential
tilting extend equality to Fourier transforms of finite measures, hence their
Y_t laws agree. Since Y_t/(dt)->X_0 pointwise as t decreases to zero, the initial
X laws agree. No interaction quench, moment assumption or unbounded physical B
is needed. Analytic uniqueness proves identification, NOT stable economical
tomography from finite noisy records.

Arbitrarily short permitted bins, or a sequence tending to zero, are required
for this separating statement. With a fixed minimum bin duration, necessity
of the full X law is not proved here. Sufficiency and record approximation do
not require arbitrarily short bins.

Thus, for this explicit intervention class, different laws of current relative
coherence are predictively distinguishable. With only a fixed pooled port,
a smaller quotient may suffice. The access premise is real: a physical output
mixer, phase reference and independent detection settings are not supplied by a
generic covariance or overlap verifier. The independent separating proof and
same-covariance count witness are retained in the physical-witness directory.

Exact information content versus finite-accuracy compression: at a fixed theta,
the canonical X law still determines the total-number-dephased physical state.
Indeed its lift by a uniform common phase is precisely that state's Husimi law.
Multiplication of this density by exp(|z|^2/theta) gives the coherent-state
power series whose coefficient of bar(z)^n z^k is the corresponding matrix
element divided by the known theta and factorial factors. Equality of Husimi
laws therefore fixes every matrix element with |n|=|k|. This injectivity is an
analytic statement, not a stable inverse or a positive coherent-mixture decoder.
Consequently an EXACT law of X is not a compression of all quantum information
visible to this symmetry class. The justified savings are in the evolution
law and finite-accuracy record representation. Sections8 and10B pay explicitly
for that approximation, whose complexity can remain large. The separating
theorem gives no lower bound on a finite-accuracy implementation's dimension.

10. SEPARATE OPERATIONAL PREPARATION ACCESS
==========================================
An ideal calibrated multimode heterodyne measurement on a FRESH preparation has
outcome alpha with its physical Q law. Rescale z=sqrt(theta)alpha to sample (3).
This requires repeatable independent copies, the calibrated POVM and its phase
reference, and the supplied joint controller preparation. It is not a
nondestructive reading of the same future controlled run. No samples were
acquired here and no cross-branch measurement interface is assumed.

The same samples can certify the number tail. Fix L and s=theta(L+m+1)/2 before
sampling. With q=mu(|z|^2>=s), angular integration gives
  q=sum_n p_n P[Gamma(n+m,1)>=s/theta],
  p_>L<=q/a_L,
  a_L=P[Gamma(L+m+1,1)>=(L+m+1)/2]
     >=1-(sqrt(e)/2)^(L+m+1)>=1/8.                        (25)
Gamma survival increases with its integer shape. The Chernoff bound follows
from exp(-Gamma); a separate Paley--Zygmund argument also gives 1/8.
This is a high-number PROBABILITY certificate, not a mean-energy estimate.

Fix a partition with C joint controller/amplitude cells, including overflow
and the tail-test boundary. For n independent samples, empirical positive cell
weights obey, with probability at least 1-delta_conf,
  TV(empirical cells,true cells)<=epsilon_stat
   =[sqrt(C-1)+sqrt(2 log(1/delta_conf))]/(2sqrt(n)).         (26)
Cauchy--Schwarz gives expected TV<=sqrt((C-1)/n)/2; replacement of one sample
changes TV by <=1/n, so McDiarmid gives (26). Counts give exact rational weights.
In particular q<=q_hat+epsilon_stat. A calibrated cell-law bias adds to this
allowance. A sharper separate Bernoulli bound may be used for q; zero observed
exceedances give q<=1-delta_tail^(1/n)<=log(1/delta_tail)/n with that confidence.
Use a union bound if separate tail/histogram confidence statements are used.

Choose the grid radius with R^2=s if desired. The same radial data then control
both quantum number-tail and classical grid-overflow errors. The complete
prediction certificate is, for example,
  epsilon_L(T)+p_U+kop theta L exp(-dT)
  +q_grid,U+epsilon_stat+epsilon_grid+epsilon_numerics,      (27)
with separately charged calibration bias. This predicts the same future law
without reconstructing rho. Initial-law sampling accuracy is a confidence
statement; the dynamical terms are mathematical deterministic bounds.

Thresholds/partitions must be fixed before sampling, or use sample splitting/
uniform confidence. A finite small tail is a condition the data can fail.
The cell/sample cost may be exponential in m; physical detector range grows
as R/sqrt(theta). Kinetic, Hamiltonian and count calibration remain separate.
Heterodyne energy testing is established capital: Kanitschar et al.,
arXiv2301.08686 Theorem3/AppendixA, and Lupo--Ouyang PRXQuantum3,010341(2022).
The new conditional interface uses those inputs for this complete record law.
