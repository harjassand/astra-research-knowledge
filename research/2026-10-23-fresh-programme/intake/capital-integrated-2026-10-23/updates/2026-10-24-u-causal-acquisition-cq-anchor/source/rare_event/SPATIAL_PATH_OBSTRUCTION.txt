SPATIAL LOCALITY DOES NOT ACQUIRE A SMALL CAUSAL CERTIFICATE
Lead exact structural reduction, 2026-10-24. No complexity novelty claim.

Model subclass
Take a line of L binary registers, all initially zero. A supplied finite
schedule updates one register at a time. Each update is either a fair
coin overwrite, a known deterministic Boolean function of its current
value and/or its two immediate neighbours, or the identity. The schedule
is allowed to vary with time and is part of the explicit input. Its
length T is polynomial in that input. The final catastrophe event is
one declared register being one. The actual spatial interaction graph
is a PATH: bounded degree, treewidth one and radius-one local reads.
All nonzero local row probabilities are known, in {1/2,1}; zero support
is known too. There is no tiny unknown parameter or model-learning cost.

Claim
A universal polynomial-time relative-1/4 catastrophe estimator for this
class (confidence >=3/4), even with strictly positive catastrophe
probabilities, would imply RP=NP. Hence locality, spatial treewidth one,
known support and p_min=1/2 do not by themselves license a universal
polynomial certificate-acquisition guarantee.

Explicit local compilation, without a hidden global gate
Given a Boolean formula phi on n bits, convert it to a fan-in-two
AND/NOT circuit of polynomial size G. Allocate O(n+G) registers for
the inputs, output of every gate, and spare zero registers. Draw the
n input bits by independent fair-coin local overwrites.

Maintain the known correspondence between stored circuit values and
physical registers. Any requested permutation of L register contents
can be implemented by O(L^2) adjacent swaps. A swap of adjacent bits is
three single-target XOR updates, each reading only its neighbour and
itself. NOT is a single-register update. To compute AND(a,b) without
destroying its inputs, permute the two values and a fresh zero target
to three consecutive cells (a,0,b), then overwrite the central target
by left AND right. Inputs are preserved. If a value must be duplicated,
copy it to a neighbouring spare zero register before arranging the
triple. Every output value is stored and labelled for later use.

Thus the entire circuit is evaluated by a polynomial number of known
radius-one updates. The compiler produces this actual schedule in
polynomial time; it never calls a formula-evaluation oracle on a random
assignment other than the deterministic local circuit it has constructed.
The sequence of original circuit gates is supplied structure, not an
unknown physical law inferred from data.

Add n+2 independent fair-coin registers and compute their conjunction F
by the same local primitives. Finally write the output bit

       catastrophe = phi(input bits) OR F.

The OR is constructed from AND/NOT. The total number of registers and
updates remains polynomial in |phi|+n. The full finite Markov state is
the binary register contents plus the public schedule clock; each row
has at most two successors and is evaluated from local data in polynomial
time. The schedule is not a time-homogeneous universal cellular automaton;
no such stronger claim is needed here.

Positive probability and SAT gap
Let b=2^-(n+2) and s be the number of satisfying assignments. Independent
fair coins give the exact probability

       p(phi)=b+(1-b)s/2^n >0.

If phi is unsatisfiable, p=b. If at least one assignment satisfies it,

       p/b >= 1+4(1-b)=5-2^-n >=4.

For relative tolerance 1/4, the unsatisfiable success interval lies
below 1.25b and the satisfiable one above 3b. Comparing with 2b therefore
decides satisfiability whenever the estimate succeeds. Independent
amplification makes the per-call error O(1/n). Apply the same construction
to successive residual formulas to choose an assignment, and VERIFY
the final assignment before accepting. Unsatisfiable inputs never
accept; satisfiable inputs accept with constant probability. This is
the standard SAT self-reduction and gives RP=NP.

Certificate consequence and exact boundary
Any universal polynomial procedure that builds evaluable true-model
super/subsolutions with polynomial start ratio, plus a polynomial-time
implemented importance/killed proposal or a directly certified relative
interval, would imply the estimator just ruled out conditionally. The
hardness is in the global causal computation, even though all local
probabilities and their support are already known exactly.

The FRONTIER_ACQUISITION theorem instead pays q^w for an explicit causal
frontier. These path-local programs can retain many earlier bits for
later uses; spatial sparsity does not remove that causal memory. The
negative result does not invalidate the positive theorem, nor does it
prove a sharp lower bound on the minimum width of each compiled program.
It identifies why a bounded-frontier or comparable representation promise
must be acquired/verified rather than silently inferred from local
couplings. Fixed homogeneous dynamics, restricted gate families,
monotonicity or additional physical conservation laws may be genuine
further restrictions; they are not covered by this obstruction.
