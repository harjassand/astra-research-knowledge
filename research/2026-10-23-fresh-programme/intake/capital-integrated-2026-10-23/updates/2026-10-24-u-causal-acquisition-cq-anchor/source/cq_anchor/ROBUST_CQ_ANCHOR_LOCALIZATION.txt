--------------------------------------------------

Fix a finite class k=f_c(alpha). The ORIGINAL actual fine Bob steering
functionals tau_(alpha,a|c,X) have coarse sums
  tau_(k|c,X)=sum_(alpha:f_c(alpha)=k),a tau_(alpha,a|c,X).
For each c supply reference normal positive functionals tau0_(k|c),
sum_k tau0_(k|c)(I)=1, and one uniform s>=0 satisfying

 e^(-s) tau0_(k|c) <= tau_(k|c,X) <= e^s tau0_(k|c)      (R1)

in FUNCTIONAL order on the full Bob algebra, for every supported X,k,c.
On zero-prior fresh inputs choose arbitrary complete local POVMs.
References for different c may differ. This is a TWO-SIDED actual quantum

for input-dependent/noncommuting fine refinements; it does not assert

The joint checked event is E={theta_c(alpha,gamma)=1}. Assume
  theta_c(alpha,gamma)=theta_hat_c(f_c(alpha),gamma)       (R2)

2. An actual reference-state subinstrument
--------------------------------------------
Put d=e^s, pi0_k=tau0_k(I), rho0_k=tau0_k/pi0_k. Omit zero-pi rows;
the upper bound in (R1) makes all their actual fine functionals zero.
Sample k with pi0_k offline and prepare the fixed canonical purification
or common-standard-form natural vector of rho0_k. This resource uses
the reference prior, not a fresh-input-dependent or accepted posterior.

For each X,k, use the bounded HJW/RN construction for the fine positive
functionals tau_(alpha,a|X)/(d pi0_k), with alpha in k's fiber. This
gives an Alice SUB-POVM {G_(alpha,a|X,k)} in the opposite algebra. Its
coarse sum C_(X,k) satisfies

 d^(-2) P_k <= C_(X,k) <= P_k,                            (R3)

where P_k is the common Alice cyclic support of the reference vector.
In finite tensors, the corresponding Bob-side matrices are
  G_(alpha,a)=rho0_k^(-1/2)
             [tau_(alpha,a|X)/(d pi0_k)]rho0_k^(-1/2),
and Alice uses their transposes. In standard form the effects lie in N'
and (R3) follows by order preservation/linearity of the RN forms.
All inverses are on P_k. Upper and LOWER bounds in (R1) are used.

Normalize the fine subinstrument on that support:
  F_(alpha,a|X,k)=C_(X,k)^(-1/2) G_(alpha,a|X,k)
                                      C_(X,k)^(-1/2).   (R4)
These sum to P_k; add I-P_k to a default fine outcome in the same fiber.
They are a complete local POVM. The inverse in (R4) has norm at most d.
In finite notation the transpose is applied to the ENTIRE displayed
effect, retaining its correct product orientation. In co, all bounded
functional calculus remains in N'.

If Alice first applies sqrt(C_(X,k)) and then this COMPLETE fine POVM,
the fine effects are EXACTLY G_(alpha,a). Bob runs his ORIGINAL complete
Y instrument. Because he knows the offline k, he can locally keep
theta_hat_c(k,gamma)=1. This target construction gives

 P_target(alpha,a,gamma,b|c,X,Y)
                       =P_original(alpha,a,gamma,b|c,X,Y)/d. (R5)

The actual original complete fine table is reproduced with one common
conclusive scaling, including its accepted Bob substate in finite tensors.
Alice's former quantum workspace is allowed to be replaced; that is the
explicit payoff/fine-table interface. The original joint event has
target mass r=p/d and conditional loss a.

3. Drop Alice's filter; keep the complete fine POVM
----------------------------------------------------
The ACTUAL repaired strategy simply omits sqrt(C_(X,k)), uses the complete
POVM F_(X,k), and retains Bob's original instrument and local theta_hat
check. Thus the new success event E' is Bob-only. Let q=P(E') and a'
be its conditional live loss. No row decoder or fresh opposite question
is supplied; all maps use only the offline c,k and own fresh input.

For proof, let u be the original-prior weighted reference vector after
Bob's original instrument and theta_hat restriction, but before Alice's
complete F measurement. Then ||u||^2=q. Bob operators commute with
C_(X,k) and P_k, including in the matrix-amplified commuting setting;
u is supported in P_k in every block. Set b_s=1-e^(-s). From (R3),

 e^(-2s)q <= ||sqrt(C)u||^2=r=p/d <=q,
 ||(I-sqrt(C))u|| <= b_s sqrt(q).                         (R6)

Apply the SAME complete fine-POVM isometry to u and sqrt(C)u. Let J be
the same fine losing contraction on its output (including the classical
question labels solely for analysis). The resulting repaired/target
accepted vectors w,v have norms sqrt(q),sqrt(r), and

 sqrt(q a')<=sqrt(r a)+b_s sqrt(q).
 Hence sqrt(a')<=sqrt(r/q)sqrt(a)+b_s<=sqrt(a)+b_s.        (R7)

Noncommutation of F and C is harmless: sqrt(C) is applied BEFORE the
fine isometry on the target, and the loss contraction is the same on
both outputs. It is not silently moved through a fine measurement.
There is no comparison of coherent postselected pure-state relative
entropy to its old pure state.

Equation (R6) gives multiplicative event control
  e^(-s)p <=q<=e^s p                                    (R8)
(with q<=1 physically). Normalizing vectors in (R6) also yields
  ||w/sqrt(q)-v/sqrt(r)||<=2b_s.
Consequently the original and repaired CONDITIONAL classical question/
fine-output laws have TV at most min(1,2b_s). In finite tensors the same
bound applies to the retained conditional Bob output quantum state.
The target normalized law is the original one by (R5). No preservation
of Alice's discarded private quantum workspace is claimed.

Importantly (R2) need not hold on the filter-DROPPED table: E' is a
Bob-local auxiliary event and (R6)-(R7) track its possible added checked
branches. On the target (R5), source-null equality is enough. The losing
payoff remains the actual live game's original fine-answer function.

4. Same-model payoff bound, without a rare-event denominator
-------------------------------------------------------------
Assume the reverse referee minorisation coefficient is delta>0, as in
the frozen mirrored one-sided selector. Free inputs have delta=1. The
repaired source has complete Alice measurements and a genuinely Bob-
local event of mass q. By (R8), ln(1/q)<=L+s. Applying the frozen joint
classical/quantum loss transfer and then (R7) gives

 epsilon_model <=[sqrt(a)+(1-e^(-s))
                          +sqrt((L+s)/(delta m))]^2.     (R9)

The model is finite tensor with its OWN epsilon_* or commuting with its
OWN epsilon_co. The normal RN construction proves co locality directly;
no finite-trace inverse or Hilbert-space least-squares operator is
transferred into N. Classical fine tables are the co output scope;
extra specified quantum-output instruments require their compatible
local representation as in the earlier frozen files.

Both sides of (R1) have a distinct role. The upper bound supplies the
actual conclusive fine subinstrument; the lower bound bounds its
distance from identity on EVERY Bob-accepted vector. An average source
error or an upper-only smooth substate bound supplies no such uniform
relative estimate. Nothing here infers the two-sided order from L/m.

5. What is and is not a new source-selection consequence
----------------------------------------------------------
The source family consists of arbitrary positive fine refinements under
the uniform actual coarse order (R1); it was specified before any
fixture. It includes the exact cq anchor at s=0 and genuinely
input-dependent coarse states for s>0. The result proves an actual local
operation, not just a proposed measure of closeness.

If (R1)-(R2) can be justified with the SAME s at every tested set
|C|<ceil(n/2), references may differ across C, and
  1-e^(-s)<=sqrt(epsilon_model)/8,
the binary all-win greedy proof gives the conditional uniform bound
  P(all n wins)<=min{1,exp[s-delta epsilon_model n/128]}.  (R10)
Indeed an assumption L_all+s<delta epsilon n/128 implies, while m>=n/2,
sqrt((L_C+s)/(delta m))<sqrt(epsilon)/8. Together with the displayed
condition and (R9), a_C>9epsilon/16>epsilon/2. After ceil(n/2) tested
coordinates, L_all>=epsilon n/4, a contradiction. This is a uniform
coarse-source assumption, not a theorem that arbitrary strategies have
such references or that they can be found by observing L alone.

A tempting example must be treated candidly: if the ENTIRE actual Alice
full POVM is Loewner bounded above by e^s times ONE coordinate-local
reference POVM, direct positivity already bounds its all-win probability
by e^s times that reference strategy's all-win probability. The resulting
repetition corollary is not evidence for a new rate mechanism. Coarse
references in (R10) need not arise from one globally dominating POVM,
but no general construction/acquisition of them is proved here.

The reference functionals/order, all fine decompositions, and the RN
POVM descriptions must be supplied or separately acquired. Their number
can be exponential; an arbitrary commuting standard form can be infinite

do not certify operator order. Uniform operator bounds control the
implemented effects but do not give an efficient state/process learning
algorithm, numerical precision guarantee, or generic low-information
selection theorem. All novelty/best-prior questions remain UNKNOWN.
