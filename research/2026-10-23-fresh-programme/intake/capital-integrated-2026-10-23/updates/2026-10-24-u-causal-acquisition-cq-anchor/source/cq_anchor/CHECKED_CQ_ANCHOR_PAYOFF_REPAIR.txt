PAYOFF-AWARE CQ ANCHORS: FORGET CHECKED WORKSPACE AND CONTROL SELECTION
2026-10-24. One source family and a local payoff/fine-table repair.
Independently challenged internally at this exact scope; external
correctness/priority UNKNOWN.

The repair changes Alice's workspace and shared resource while retaining
the entire original classical fine table. It therefore can work when a
common filter on the old actual Bob program is impossible. The decisive
certificate is a preselection equality of ACTUAL Bob steering
functionals, not equality of scalar checked-outcome probabilities, a
measured posterior, or a claimed Bob purification of Alice's marginal.
All previous frozen files remain unchanged.

The general version needs only a predicate-relevant class K=f_c(alpha)
and Bob's joint CQ steering state to be independent of fresh Alice
inputs; alpha itself may retain arbitrary fresh-input information.
Sections 1-4 prove the transparent identity-class case. Section 5 gives
the general payoff quotient, Section 6 its linear semantic-error cost,
and Section 7 a source family that FORCES the anchor at every greedy
checked set, giving an actual restricted-strategy repetition bound.

1. Source family, chosen before any fixture
---------------------------------------------
Let G be any finite game with bounded payoff V in [0,1]. Question pairs
are iid from mu over n rounds, with arbitrary within-round correlation.
Fix tested C and live J, m=|J|>=1. Write c=(X_C,Y_C), X=X_J, Y=Y_J.
Use either finite tensors with their own value omega_* or unrestricted
commuting strategies with their own value omega_co.

The original local full-string instruments are arbitrary and complete.
Alice's classical outputs include checked alpha and live/fine a. Bob's
include checked gamma and live/fine b. Let N be the ORIGINAL full Bob
algebra. In the finite-tensor statement take N=B(H_B), so the anchor
is equality of actual density matrices on the entire Bob input space.
In the commuting statement N is his supplied local von Neumann algebra.
The actual Alice instrument produces normal positive steering
functionals tau_(alpha,a|c,X) on N. The proposed source family satisfies

 sum_a tau_(alpha,a|c,X)=tau_(alpha|c)
        for EVERY original-prior supported fresh X,       (A1)
 sum_alpha tau_(alpha|c)=phi_c, phi_c(I)=1.

Thus the joint preselection CQ state (alpha,N) is independent of fresh
Alice X. This is stronger than P(alpha|c,X) being independent of X.
It is an operator/normal-functional equality on the actual Bob system,
not just agreement on a finite set of observed classical effects.
The fine decompositions may depend arbitrarily on the entire fresh X;
they need not commute, be unique, or have small answer alphabets.
Bob's original full-string instrument may depend arbitrarily on Y and
perform a joint measurement across all live coordinates.

The actual joint checked event is
  E={theta_c(alpha,gamma)=1}, theta binary.               (A2)
There is no disjoint-block, projection, mask-lift, or common-program
compatibility premise on theta. Let p=P(E)>0, L=ln(1/p), and let a_loss
be the original conditional mean fraction of live losses given E.

A literal input-independent early Alice coarse instrument implies (A1),
but the theorem only requires the actual cq steering equality. Alice's
original private quantum workspace/instrument can still depend on fresh
X. Arbitrary refinements of any fixed finite normal assemblage
{tau_(alpha|c)} are included, with arbitrary same-model Bob instruments.
This is an unbounded-round source family, not a constant-size fixture.

2. Actual local repair in finite tensors
------------------------------------------
First sample c from its ORIGINAL tested-question prior mu_C as shared
offline randomness, independent of fresh J question pairs. Given c,
put pi_alpha=tau_(alpha|c)(I). Omit zero-pi rows, which have zero fine
steering functional by positivity. Independently of the fresh inputs,
sample alpha with probability pi_alpha and give its classical value to
BOTH players. Prepare one fixed canonical purification of
  rho_alpha=tau_(alpha|c)/pi_alpha                         (A3)
on the ORIGINAL Bob Hilbert space and a new Alice purification space.
The ensemble (c,alpha,resource) is entirely input independent. It is not
the event-conditioned posterior of c or alpha.

For every fresh X, let
  sigma_(a|X,alpha)=tau_(alpha,a|c,X)/pi_alpha,
  sum_a sigma_(a|X,alpha)=rho_alpha.                       (A4)
On P_alpha=supp(rho_alpha), define
  C_(a|X,alpha)=rho_alpha^(-1/2) sigma_(a|X,alpha)
                                      rho_alpha^(-1/2).
These are positive contractions summing to P_alpha. Complete with
I-P_alpha at one default fine answer. Alice measures the TRANSPOSED
completed POVM on the canonical purification
  Omega_(rho_alpha)=sum_j |j>_A tensor sqrt(rho_alpha)|j>_B.
Only a support inverse is used; domination in (A4) bounds all effects.

This produces the ACTUAL original fine steering state exactly:
  pi_alpha sqrt(rho_alpha) C_(a|X,alpha) sqrt(rho_alpha)
       =tau_(alpha,a|c,X).                               (A5)
The support-completion term is zero on rho_alpha. Alice also outputs
the sampled alpha as her original checked record.

Bob runs his ORIGINAL complete Y instrument. He now knows the sampled
alpha, so he locally accepts iff theta_c(alpha,gamma)=1. He need not
decode alpha from nonorthogonal states or be supplied a fresh Alice
question. He may ignore the extra knowledge except in this local check.
His actual original instrument, fine readout, and output quantum system
are retained. For every supported full input and every fine outcome,

 P_new(alpha,a,gamma,b|c,X,Y)
      =tau_(alpha,a|c,X)(B_(gamma,b|c,Y))
      =P_original(alpha,a,gamma,b|c,X,Y).                 (A6)

In finite tensors (A5) is equality of actual Bob subnormalized matrices,
so every unchanged original Bob CP instrument also gives exactly the
same fine-conditioned Bob quantum output. Alice's original private
quantum workspace/global purification is NOT asserted to be preserved.
That is the explicitly permitted forgetting step. Every classical output
used by the referee's payoff, including the actual fine a,b records,
is preserved pointwise. The repair is not obtained by a physical channel
on the old Alice workspace; it prepares a new legal shared resource and
uses an explicit local POVM family.

The joint E has therefore become an actual Bob-only event E' with
  p'=p and a_loss'=a_loss,                               (A7)
and the entire original accepted classical fine table, not only its
mean loss, is unchanged. No comparison of a coherent Lüders state by
relative entropy to its original pure state is used.

3. Direct commuting construction
----------------------------------
Use ONE common standard form of the original N. For each sampled alpha,
prepare its natural-cone vector xi_(rho_alpha). For each fresh X, the
positive normal fine functionals sigma_(a|X,alpha) in (A4) sum to the
SAME rho_alpha. The bounded RN construction from the frozen commuting
selector gives a complete Alice POVM E_(X,alpha)^a in N' satisfying
  <xi_(rho_alpha),E_(X,alpha)^a n xi_(rho_alpha)>
                       =sigma_(a|X,alpha)(n), n in N.     (A8)
One direct proof defines the positive contraction T_sigma by the form
<u xi_rho,T_sigma v xi_rho>=sigma(u* v) on closure(N xi_rho), extends it
by zero, and adds the complement of that cyclic support at a default
answer. It commutes with N and reproduces sigma. This is the same
bounded construction already proved in the frozen normal-state core.

Bob's original effects remain in N. A finite classical direct sum over
the sampled c,alpha gives one shared input-independent commuting state
and one all-input family of opposite POVMs. Thus (A6)-(A7) hold directly
with no tracial, finite-dimensional, or matrix-density premise.
Only the classical fine table is claimed in this commuting value model;
additional specified quantum-output processing needs the compatible
local instrument representation already required by that processing.

4. Source-compatible low-information payoff consequence
----------------------------------------------------------
Assume the REVERSE referee channel has a minorisation
  mu(x|y)=delta nu(x)+(1-delta)kappa(x|y), 0<delta<=1.      (A9)
Free inputs give delta=1. The repaired source plays the same m live
rounds with a complete Alice instrument and a genuinely Bob-local success
event. Its initial c,alpha seed and branch resource use the ORIGINAL
prior and are independent of all fresh question pairs. Consequently the
frozen mirrored one-sided theorem and joint cq loss transfer apply in
the SAME model, with its OWN base gap epsilon=1-omega_model(G):

 epsilon <=[sqrt(a_loss)+sqrt(L/(delta m))]^2.             (A10)

Equivalently,
 a_loss >=[sqrt(epsilon)-sqrt(L/(delta m))]_+^2.           (A11)
Zero selected live loss gives the stronger affinity corollary
  p<=omega_model(G)^(delta m).                            (A12)
These are the already-proved one-sided inequalities applied AFTER the
new exact localization; no fresh scalar optimization is being claimed.

In particular the certificate works for arbitrary m and alphabets: if
p stays bounded below, the selected mean loss approaches at least the
own base gap as m grows. It supplies useful low-L/m control throughout
this actual cq-anchor family, while full original program/workspace
repair can remain unnecessarily difficult. The old private-row example
copies a fresh Alice input into checked alpha and fails (A1); scalar
row dispersion zero does not turn it into this source family.

5. Only the payoff-relevant class needs the fixed CQ anchor
------------------------------------------------------------
Let K=f_c(alpha) be a specified finite class. Replace (A1) by the WEAKER
actual steering equality

 tau_(k|c,X)=sum_(alpha:f_c(alpha)=k),a tau_(alpha,a|c,X)
                         =tau_(k|c) for every fresh X.    (A13)

The full alpha need not be independent of X, even in distribution. The
predicate condition is
  theta_c(alpha,gamma)=theta_hat_c(f_c(alpha),gamma)       (A14)
on the COMPLETE original preselection fine-output support for every
positive-prior input pair. Agreement on all formal alpha,gamma pairs
is sufficient but is not necessary. Agreement only after E is insufficient
because it does not forbid newly accepted branches.

Sample only k offline with pi_k=tau_(k|c)(I), and prepare the fixed
rho_k=tau_(k|c)/pi_k. For this k, the actual fine decompositions are
  sigma_(alpha,a|X,k)=tau_(alpha,a|c,X)/pi_k,
                     alpha restricted to f_c(alpha)=k.
They sum to the SAME rho_k by (A13). Apply the explicit HJW/RN construction
above with fine outcome (alpha,a); use a support-complement default in
that same fiber. The result is
 P_new(k,alpha,a,gamma,b|c,X,Y)
    =1{f_c(alpha)=k} P_original(alpha,a,gamma,b|c,X,Y).     (A15)

Thus the ORIGINAL full classical fine table, including alpha, is
preserved after dropping the added k. Bob receives only the safe
input-independent k seed, runs his original instrument, and locally
accepts theta_hat_c(k,gamma). Condition (A14) gives p'=p and exactly the
original accepted fine law. Equations (A10)-(A12) follow unchanged.
The input-dependent discarded checked information has not been leaked
to Bob: alpha is an actual X-dependent Alice fine outcome in (A15),
while the joint state of k and Bob's input memory is fixed by (A13).

6. Approximate payoff quotient: classical mismatch costs linearly
------------------------------------------------------------------
Keep the exact CQ anchor (A13), but let a specified theta_hat have
  eta=P_original[theta_c(alpha,gamma)
                       !=theta_hat_c(f_c(alpha),gamma)]. (A16)
This is evaluated on the COMPLETE ORIGINAL preselection table. Equation
(A15) preserves that table exactly before selection; only the diagonal
acceptance rule changes. Therefore
  |p'-p|<=eta, p'a_loss'<=p a_loss+eta.
For u=eta/p<1, p'>=p(1-u)>0 and the actual Bob-local candidate satisfies

 epsilon <=[sqrt((a_loss+u)/(1-u))
                +sqrt((L-ln(1-u))/(delta m))]^2.          (A17)

This uses scalar event/loss masses, not a sqrt(eta) disturbance estimate.
False acceptance and omission can be recorded separately if needed, but
no small mismatch or efficient acquisition is assumed. In particular
eta=0 is a literal source-null quotient even when the formal predicate
rows differ outside the source support. It preserves the event and live
fine law exactly through an actual all-input local resource/POVM family.

7. A source family forcing the anchor at EVERY selected history
----------------------------------------------------------------
This corollary concerns BINARY win predicates. Consider any n-round
strategy for which Alice's full POVM has the coordinate-local form
  M_X^(a_1,...,a_n)=product_(i=1)^n M_(i,x_i)^(a_i),      (A18)
where each coordinate is a complete POVM and ALL effects of DISTINCT
coordinates commute, for every choice of their own settings/outcomes.
The shared resource can be arbitrarily entangled across rounds, and
Bob's complete full-string instrument is unrestricted and joint.
No product-state or Bob product-measurement assumption is made. Alice's
actual quantum-output instrument can be any realization of these POVM
effects because the repair is allowed to replace her private workspace.

For ANY tested set C, summing Alice's fine answers on J gives the
coarse effect
  sum_(a_J) M_X^(alpha,a_J)
                  =product_(i in C) M_(i,x_i)^(alpha_i), (A19)
independent of X_J. Its actual Bob steering functional is therefore
independent of fresh Alice X_J on the ENTIRE Bob algebra. This is (A1)
for every C, including a C chosen by the conditional-loss greedy rule.
The newly supplied seed is its ORIGINAL prior, not an accepted-history
oracle. Thus the useful source certificate is forced by this strategy
structure rather than assumed at whichever greedy set happens to work.

For either model, with its own unrestricted base gap epsilon and reverse
minorisation coefficient delta, every strategy in this ONE-PARTY
coordinate-local commuting-measurement family satisfies
  P(all n wins)<=exp[-delta epsilon n/32].                (A20)
The condition is a restriction on the repeated strategy, not on G.

Proof. Let w>0 be its all-win probability and assume, for contradiction,
L_all=ln(1/w)<delta epsilon n/32, with epsilon>0. For a tested C let
E_C mean all C wins, p_C=P(E_C)>=w, L_C=ln(1/p_C)<=L_all. Whenever
|C|<ceil(n/2), m_C=n-|C|>=n/2, so
  L_C/(delta m_C)<epsilon/16.
The exact anchor theorem applies by (A19). It gives conditional mean
live loss a_C>9epsilon/16>epsilon/2. Choose one live coordinate whose
conditional failure is >epsilon/2 and append it to C. This reduces p_C
by a factor at most 1-epsilon/2. After t=ceil(n/2) steps,
  w<=p_C<=(1-epsilon/2)^t,
  L_all>=t[-ln(1-epsilon/2)]>=epsilon n/4,
contradicting the assumption since delta<=1. Zero w or epsilon makes
the claimed bound immediate. This proof also covers n=1.

This resolves the selection gate INSIDE the explicit one-party strategy
family. General full-string Alice instruments can copy arbitrary fresh
inputs into old checked answers and fail (A19); no extension of (A20)
to them is claimed. General bounded/fractional-payoff repetition would
need a separately specified referee-coin treatment; (A20) states only
binary win predicates. No priority or comparison with the best known
rate for this restricted strategy family has been established.

8. Costs, acquisition, and the remaining general gate
-------------------------------------------------------
For finite Bob dimension d_B, each sampled anchor branch needs a purification of
rank at most d_B. The classical seed alphabet is at most the original
tested answer alphabet, or its specified payoff quotient K; no efficient
encoding/compression is asserted.
Describing all fine assemblages/POVMs over full fresh strings can be
exponential. Exact state/operator equality (A1), or (A13), must be justified from
the source model or separately calibrated on the actual Bob system.
Neither scalar checked probabilities nor a finite observed fine table
automatically proves that equality. Bob may not purify an Alice measured
marginal merely because some classical mutual information is small.

The standard-form resource may be infinite dimensional, and the new
Alice algebra may be the full commutant. It establishes a commuting
value statement without an efficient physical compiler. Small spectrum
inverses are bounded only at the resulting effect level; no statistical
or numerical acquisition guarantee follows. The sampled anchor class
is free of fresh information BECAUSE of its actual CQ equality, not
because a classical record is intrinsically harmless to share.

This is a constructive, payoff-aware source family. No theorem here
forces the anchor at a useful greedy history for an unrestricted
full-string strategy, or bounds its approximate failure from L/m alone.
The general source-budget-to-useful-repair problem remains unresolved.
HJW/RN steering and preparing a fixed assemblage are conventional
ingredients; priority for any broader consequence remains UNKNOWN.
