1. Original commuting source and normal positive functionals
-----------------------------------------------------------
Let N be the von Neumann algebra generated by the original Bob full-string
POVM effects, acting on the original shared Hilbert space with unit vector
eta. Original Alice effects commute with N. Set
  phi(n)=<eta,n eta>.
For an Alice-local success sub-POVM M_X^{a,E}, define

  s_(a|X)(n)=<eta,M_X^{a,E} n eta>,
  r_X=sum_a s_(a|X),  p_X=r_X(I),
  p=E_(mu_X^m) p_X>0.                                    (C1)

These are NORMAL positive functionals on N because M_X^{a,E} commutes
with N. Moreover r_X<=phi in functional order: for n>=0,
  phi(n)-r_X(n)=<eta,(I-sum_a M_X^{a,E})n eta> >= 0.
The product is of commuting positive operators. This is the functional
version of R_X<=sigma_B.

If needed restrict N to the support corner s(phi)N s(phi). All dominated
functionals are supported there, and replacing each Bob effect n by
s(phi)n s(phi) preserves all probabilities and POVM completeness in the
corner. The restricted phi is faithful normal, hence the corner is
sigma-finite. It is not required to be semifinite or to possess a trace.

Let Q_X=mu_X^m p_X/p and rho_X=r_X/p_X on Q-supported inputs. These are
normal states on N. The original event again leaves Y^m independent of
X,E under the free question law. All classical conditioning, suffix
averaging, and Bob POVM averaging in S3/S10/S11 of the frozen finite core
therefore make sense with density operators replaced by these normal
functionals. Call the resulting fine assemblage tau_a^x<=rho_x.

2. One common standard form supplies actual opposite POVMs
---------------------------------------------------------
Represent N ONCE in standard form (N,H,J,P), with its natural cone P.
Each normal positive functional rho has its unique representative xi_rho
in P, rho(n)=<xi_rho,n xi_rho>. The representation of N is the SAME for
every x, prefix, and coordinate; only its state vector changes.

Here is the needed bounded Radon--Nikodym lemma, including its proof.
For 0<=tau<=rho, put H_rho=closure(N xi_rho). On this dense orbit set

  <a xi_rho,T_tau b xi_rho>=tau(a* b).                      (C2)

The positive form is well defined and bounded by the Hilbert norm since
tau(a*a)<=rho(a*a)=||a xi_rho||^2; its Cauchy inequality bounds mixed
terms. Thus it defines a positive contraction T_tau on H_rho. For n in N,

  <a xi,T_tau n b xi>=tau(a* n b)
                    =<n* a xi,T_tau b xi>
                    =<a xi,n T_tau b xi>.                (C3)

Hence T_tau commutes with N restricted to H_rho. The subspace H_rho is
reducing for N, so its projection p'_rho lies in N'. Extend T_tau by zero
on H_rho's orthogonal complement; then T_tau belongs to N',
0<=T_tau<=p'_rho, and

  tau(n)=<xi_rho,T_tau n xi_rho>.                          (C4)

For a finite assemblage sum_a tau_a=rho, the sum of these effects is
p'_rho, by (C2). Add I-p'_rho to one default answer. This gives a complete
finite-outcome Alice POVM E_x^a in the COMMON commutant N'. For each x use
rho=rho_x and its actual assemblage tau_a^x. On the comparison vector,

  <xi_(rho_x), E_x^a K_y^b xi_(rho_x)>=tau_a^x(K_y^b).      (C5)

All Bob K_y^b remain in N, so they commute with every E_x^a. No matrix
transpose is needed: the common commutant is the opposite local algebra.
These effects need not lie in the ORIGINAL Alice algebra; the constructed
strategy is legal for the unrestricted commuting value, whose allowed
local algebras may change. No restricted-algebra compilation is claimed.

3. Explicit analytic facts needed from modular entropy
------------------------------------------------------
For normal states rho,sigma on this same standard-form algebra, the needed
canonical affinity statement is

  D_Araki(rho||sigma)
       >= -2 ln <xi_rho,xi_sigma>
       >= ||xi_rho-xi_sigma||^2.                           (C6)

This is the natural-cone inner product, not an optimization over arbitrary
purifications or an input-dependent Uhlmann unitary. One direct route uses
the relative modular spectral measure at xi_rho: its sqrt moment is this
canonical affinity and its negative log moment is D_Araki. Jensen for log
and -ln t>=1-t give (C6) for faithful states. In each actual reconstruction
branch restrict first to the support corner of sigma=rho_bar; every
q-positive rho_x and its fine assemblage are supported there. For possibly
nonfaithful rho_x use rho_(x,t)=(1-t)rho_x+t rho_bar. The reference is
faithful on this corner. Convexity gives limsup_t D(rho_(x,t)||rho_bar)
<=D(rho_x||rho_bar), lower semicontinuity gives the reverse liminf, and
norm-continuity of the natural-cone representative passes the vector
distance to the limit. The standard corner identification preserves these
vectors and their inner products. This is support regularization, not a
trace-class density assumption or a matrix approximation.
Only the REFERENCE support is used. Compressing sigma merely to the
target support s(rho_x), when sigma has off-diagonal blocks across it,
could change the relative entropy and is not done.

The needed upper bound is functional domination:

  rho<=sigma/c  => D_Araki(rho||sigma)<=ln(1/c).             (C7)

There is a short proof using a compatible classical flag, valid also for
nonfaithful normal states. For 0<c<1 write
  sigma=c rho+(1-c)nu,
where nu is a normal state. On N direct_sum N compare
  Upsilon=rho direct_sum 0,
  Xi=c rho direct_sum (1-c)nu.
The direct-sum/scaling formula gives D(Upsilon||Xi)=ln(1/c).
Restriction along the normal unital diagonal embedding n |-> n direct_sum n
forgets the flag. Monotonicity therefore gives D(rho||sigma)<=ln(1/c).
For c=1 the two normalized states coincide. This proof does not use
operator logarithms of density matrices or compression to s(rho).

The remaining required properties are the direct-sum formula for a finite
classical register and Donald's identity for finite normal-state mixtures.
All relative entropies used here are finite due to the domination bound;
no cancellation of infinite entropies is permitted. The Holevo quantities
can be defined directly as sums of relative entropies, avoiding individual
von Neumann entropies in a type-III algebra.

commuting model and is not a finite implementation or finite matrix
