ONE-SIDED INSTRUMENT CONDITIONING: AN ACTUAL LOW-INFORMATION SELECTOR
2026-10-24. Finite-tensor core; no unrestricted two-sided claim.

This is an instrument-dependent conditioning problem. The selected event
may steer Bob to noncommuting, input-dependent quantum states even while
the classical question law remains unchanged. Its fine table is NOT
treated as the original fine instrument under a changed question law.

The useful source invariant is causal: success is a local Alice event,
and Bob's instrument is complete and unconditioned. This supplies an
actual one-sided assemblage factorization on the SAME Bob state system
whose information is budgeted. The general two-sided source lacks that
property; no CQ-to-Petz or arbitrary marginal-extension inference is used.

1. Model and result
-------------------
Let G be an arbitrary finite two-player game with [0,1] payoff V and FREE
question law mu_X tensor mu_Y. Let v=omega*(G), epsilon=1-v, where omega*
is the finite-dimensional tensor value. Answers are arbitrary finite sets.

Fix any m>=1, an input-independent density operator sigma_AB on finite
Hilbert spaces, and arbitrary joint full-string instruments. Alice's local
instrument, on input X in Xcal^m, has fine answers a in Acal^m and a local
success flag. Bob uses a COMPLETE POVM {N_Y^b}_b on input Y in Ycal^m.
Success E may depend on Alice's input and her own instrument outcome;
it cannot depend on Bob's input, outcome, or a later Bob measurement.

Write the successful Alice effects as a sub-POVM {M_X^{a,E}}_a. All local
environments and additional Alice outcomes may be summed into these
effects. Define Bob's positive steering substates

  S_(a|X)=Tr_A[(M_X^{a,E} tensor I) sigma_AB],
  R_X=sum_a S_(a|X),  p_X=Tr R_X,
  p=E_(X~mu_X^m) p_X>0.                                  (S1)

The partial-trace expression is positive: equivalently insert sqrt(M)
on both sides of sigma before tracing A. Since sum_a M_X^{a,E}<=I,

  0<=R_X<=sigma_B.                                        (S2)

The original selected input law is
  Q_X(X)=mu_X^m(X)p_X/p,
  Q_XY=Q_X tensor mu_Y^m.                                 (S3)
For p_X>0 set rho_X=R_X/p_X. Let
  Omega_XB=sum_X Q_X(X)|X><X| tensor rho_X,
  I=I_Omega(X:B),
  D_ref=D(Q_X||mu_X^m),  L=ln(1/p),
and let alpha be the original conditional mean fraction of live losses:
  alpha=(1/m)sum_i E[1-V(X_i,Y_i,a_i,b_i) | E].
All entropies below use natural logarithms.

There is one actual single-copy strategy, prepared before fresh questions,
with loss at most

  alpha + 2sqrt(alpha I/m) + I/m + sqrt(D_ref/(2m)).         (S4)

Therefore the SAME-MODEL base gap obeys

  epsilon <= alpha + 2sqrt(alpha I/m) + I/m
                         + sqrt(D_ref/(2m)),
  I+D_ref <= L.                                           (S5)

In particular a weaker table-only form uses tau=L/m:

  epsilon <= alpha + 2sqrt(alpha tau)+tau+sqrt(tau/2).       (S6)

If the actual success probability is pointwise constant, p_X=p on the
original input support, then D_ref=0 and

  epsilon <= (sqrt(alpha)+sqrt(L/m))^2,
  alpha >= (sqrt(epsilon)-sqrt(L/m))_+^2.                   (S7)

For example, conditional mean loss alpha<=epsilon/4 forces L>=m epsilon/4
in this pointwise-constant family. Thus low L/m genuinely forces a useful
coordinate with substantial actual live loss, independent of answer size.
This is a theorem about these actual one-sided source events, not a claim
that the greedy event of an arbitrary repeated strategy has this form.

2. The original event supplies the entropy budget
-------------------------------------------------
Let Theta=mu_X^m tensor sigma_B, as a classical-quantum state. Equations
(S1)--(S2) give the OPERATOR ORDER statement

  Omega_XB <= Theta/p.                                    (S8)

Log is operator monotone, on the reference support. Hence
  D(Omega||Theta)<=ln(1/p).
This is the actual MIXED Bob marginal after a measured Alice success flag.
It is not the relative entropy of a changed pure vector against its
original pure resource. Supports cause no divergence problem because
R_X<=sigma_B and p_X>0 on the selected input support.

The classical-quantum decomposition and Donald identity give exactly

  D(Omega||Theta)
     =D_ref + sum_X Q_X D(rho_X||sigma_B)
     =D_ref + I + D(rho_bar_B||sigma_B),                   (S9)
  rho_bar_B=sum_X Q_X rho_X.

This proves the budget in (S5), including its nonnegative slack. It applies
to noncommuting R_X and arbitrary Alice CP instruments.

3. The actual all-input one-sided factorization
------------------------------------------------
Order the m live coordinates. For each fixed i and prefix h=X_<i with
Q(h)>0, let q_h(x)=Q(X_i=x|h). Define, for q_h(x)>0,

  tau_a^(i,h,x)
    =sum_u Q(X_>i=u | h,x)
             [sum_(a_string:a_i=a) S_(a_string|h,x,u)]/p_(h,x,u),
  rho_x^(i,h)=sum_a tau_a^(i,h,x).                         (S10)

The sums omit p_X=0 inputs, which have zero Q weight. Each rho_x has trace
one. The target is on the ORIGINAL Bob Hilbert space B; no opposite coarse
answer, purifier environment or quantum question copy has been added.

Bob's live POVM is

  K_y^(i,b)
    =E_(Y_-i~mu_Y^(m-1))
           sum_(b_string:b_i=b) N_(Y_<i,y,Y_>i)^(b_string). (S11)

It is a genuine complete POVM for every y. This averaging is valid because
the FREE input law and Alice-only event leave Y^m independent of X and E.
Then the actual selected live table conditional on h is EXACTLY

  P_E(a,b | X_i=x,Y_i=y,h)=Tr[K_y^(i,b) tau_a^(i,h,x)].      (S12)

Equation (S12) is the consequential factorization. Bob's map is TP (the
identity before his actual POVM). With a Bob-dependent selected event the
analogous operator is generally trace-decreasing, and (S12) is not supplied
by small opposite-local CQ information alone.

4. One fixed legal resource and local POVMs
------------------------------------------
For a fixed (i,h), put rho_bar=sum_x q_h(x)rho_x on B. Prepare its canonical
purification on two copies of B,

  |Gamma_bar>=sum_j |j>_A' tensor sqrt(rho_bar)|j>_B'.

On supp rho_x define C_x^a=rho_x^(-1/2) tau_a^x rho_x^(-1/2). Since
0<=tau_a^x<=rho_x, these are bounded positive contractions and sum to the
support projection P_x. Add I-P_x to one default outcome, obtaining a
POVM {Cbar_x^a}. Alice uses (Cbar_x^a)^T in the fixed canonical basis;
Bob uses the original (S11) matrices. For q_h(x)=0 define any fixed
complete Alice POVM, so the family is defined on EVERY fresh input.

On the comparison vector |Gamma_x>=sum_j |j> tensor sqrt(rho_x)|j>,

  <Gamma_x|(Cbar_x^a)^T tensor K_y^b|Gamma_x>
      =Tr[K_y^b tau_a^x].                                 (S13)

The support-default term vanishes on this comparison vector and remains
in the real strategy. Thus this construction has no hidden abort outcome.
It neither transmits a message nor applies a positive non-CP map to an
entangled subsystem. All actual operations are local POVMs.

For any density operators rho,sigma,

  ||sqrt(rho)-sqrt(sigma)||_HS^2 <= D(rho||sigma).           (S14)

A finite-dimensional proof includes the noncommuting case. In eigenbases
rho=sum_j r_j|j><j| and sigma=sum_k s_k|k><k|, use the probability weights
w_jk=r_j|<j|k>|^2. Jensen for log gives
  log Tr[sqrt(rho)sqrt(sigma)]
    =log sum_jk w_jk sqrt(s_k/r_j)
    >=-(1/2)D(rho||sigma).
Then -log t>=1-t gives (S14). Zero eigenvalues follow by support restriction
or regularization; infinite relative entropy makes the bound vacuous.

Let alpha_(i,h) denote the target conditional loss under q_h tensor mu_Y.
The same losing effect, constructed from these actual POVMs and the game
payoff, lies in [0,I]. Triangle inequality for its square root gives

  beta_(i,h),Q <= alpha_(i,h)
       +2sqrt(alpha_(i,h) H_(i,h))+H_(i,h),
  H_(i,h)=sum_x q_h(x)||sqrt(rho_x)-sqrt(rho_bar)||_HS^2
       <=chi_(i,h)=I_Omega(X_i:B|X_<i=h).                 (S15)

There is no entropy-to-TV-to-purifier square-root loss. The reference
purification is fixed before fresh input; every Alice effect uses only
(i,h,x), and every Bob effect uses only (i,y). Alice's effect is an actual
finite operator computed from the stated assemblage, not an arbitrary
Uhlmann match depending on the opponent's question.

5. Prefix allocation, fresh questions, and the single strategy
--------------------------------------------------------------
The exact quantum chain rule yields

  sum_i E_(h~Q_X<i) chi_(i,h)=I_Omega(X^m:B)=I.              (S16)

The classical product reference gives

  sum_i E_h D(q_h||mu_X)=D_ref.                             (S17)

For each branch (i,h), changing Alice's fresh question law from q_h to mu_X
changes the constructed bounded losing probability by at most
dTV(q_h,mu_X). Pinsker and Jensen bound its mean by sqrt(D_ref/(2m)).
The arbitrary default choices on q_h=0 inputs are included in this bound.

Now sample i uniformly and h from Q_X<i OFFLINE, before receiving the fresh
questions, and prepare that branch's fixed canonical resource. Both players
know this classical seed. The seed law does not depend on the realized
fresh x,y; it is not an oracle for the opponent's question. Average (S15)
and use Cauchy on the cross term, then (S16)--(S17). This constructs the
single strategy in (S4). Its mean loss is at least epsilon by definition
of the finite-tensor base value, proving (S5)--(S7).

6. A physical quantum-message source, not question reweighting
-------------------------------------------------------------
Take ANY entanglement-assisted ONE-WAY quantum protocol: Alice's full-input
instrument produces her answers and a message C of dimension D, possibly
entangled with Bob B. Bob's joint whole-input POVM acts on CB. Replace the
message by one D-dimensional maximally entangled auxiliary pair P_A P_B.
Alice performs the Bell measurement on C P_A and E selects the identity
Bell outcome |Phi_D>=D^(-1/2)sum_j |j j>.

Direct index contraction gives, even with an entangled reference B,

  (<Phi_D|_(C P_A) tensor I)(|psi>_(C B) tensor |Phi_D>_(P_A P_B))
       =D^(-1)|psi>_(P_B B).                               (S18)

The accepted CP map is exactly D^(-2) times identity, with C relabelled as
P_B, no transpose or conjugation on this chosen outcome. Bob always runs
the original POVM on P_B B; he is not told Alice's Bell outcome. Alice's
and Bob's physical operations commute across parties, so no communication
has occurred in the original postselected implementation.

Success is pointwise p_X=D^(-2), regardless of the source quantum state.
The normalized selected instrument is exactly the original one-way
quantum protocol. Thus (S7) applies with L=2lnD. A classical message with
K basis labels instead uses K-dimensional program entanglement, Alice's
controlled cyclic shift and one computational-basis postselection; then
p=K^(-1), and the same bound uses L=lnK.

The conditional Bob state here can genuinely change and fail to commute
across Alice inputs. This cannot be reduced to sampling the old quantum
instrument under another classical question law: that law is unchanged.

Matched positive-gap/useful-excess qubit fixture:
* G is ordinary uniform CHSH, whose quantum value is cos^2(pi/8).
* Alice's fine answer is a=0. Her qubit message is |psi_0>=|0> or
  |psi_1>=(|0>+sqrt(3)|1>)/2 according to x.
* Bob uses b=0 for y=0. For y=1 he distinguishes the two message states
  with the equal-prior Helstrom projectors. Each correct-guess probability
  is 1/2+sqrt(3)/4.
* Conditional CHSH winning probability is 3/4+sqrt(3)/8, about 0.9665,
  strictly ABOVE the no-communication quantum value. Teleportation success
  is exactly 1/4 for every x,y.
* The two selected Bob states have nonzero commutator, while the unselected
  Bob receiver is I/2 independent of x. The success flag is external
  conditioning, not a physically available free communication channel.

The elementary CHSH upper bound follows by Cauchy on self-adjoint binary
contractions and is attained by one Bell pair with the usual 45-degree
observables. Thus the comparison uses a positive quantum base gap, not
only a classical one. The useful excess in this fixture is real, but L/m
is not small (m=1), fully consistent with (S7).

7. Resources, scope, and the general unresolved gate
---------------------------------------------------
For each (i,h), the reconstructed resource has local dimension at most
d_B=dim(original Bob system), INCLUDING any message-receiver register in
the original postselected source. A shared branch label (i,h) has finite
length at most log_2 m+(m-1)log_2|Xcal|. It may select among exponentially
many distinct resource states/effect tables. Computing all assemblages
and averaged POVMs can require exponential sums over full inputs and
outputs. This is an explicit finite existence construction; no efficient
learning, tomography, synthesis or empirical acquisition bound is claimed.
Small eigenvalues do not make the defined POVM effects unbounded, but
uniform numerical precision costs are not supplied.

Teleportation adds one D-dimensional maximally entangled pair and a D^2-
outcome local Bell measurement. An experiment would identify the accepted
data only after collecting Alice's actual flag. The normalized selected
strategy is not an unconditional zero-communication implementation.

The proof so far is finite tensor. An arbitrary commuting source cannot
be declared finite tracial or matrix-valued. A separate table-first
extension under an explicitly accepted complete-table approximation
premise, or a standard-form/Araki relative-entropy proof, would need its
own stated scope; neither is silently assumed above.

The assumptions that do the work are FREE independent question sampling,
one-sided success, and Bob's unconditioned complete local instrument.
Two-sided checked-win conditioning generally replaces (S12) by a
trace-decreasing opposite-input operation. Low original measured CQ charge
alone does not repair that missing factorization. No compiler turning
every useful greedy event into this one-sided family is established.
