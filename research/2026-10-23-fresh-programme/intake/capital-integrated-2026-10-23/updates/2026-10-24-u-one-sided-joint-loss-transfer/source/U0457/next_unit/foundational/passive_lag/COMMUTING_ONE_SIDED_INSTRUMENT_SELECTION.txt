STANDARD-FORM EXTENSION OF THE ONE-SIDED INSTRUMENT SELECTOR
2026-10-24. Separate from frozen finite-tensor core 845aad58...f4395.

This is a direct commuting-model theorem, with an explicit local
Radon--Nikodym POVM construction and the sourced normal-state analytic
imports in Section 3. It asserts no finite-tracial representation, matrix
density, or approximation of the source operators by finite matrices.
No accepted table-density premise is used. Novelty is UNKNOWN.

Let G be a finite game with payoff in [0,1] and FREE question law
mu_X tensor mu_Y. Its m>=1 round question pairs are independent, but
the strategy may use arbitrary full-string joint commuting POVMs.
The event E is Alice-local; Bob's full-string complete POVM is untouched.
Let alpha be the original conditional mean fraction of losses given E.

1. Original commuting source and normal positive functionals
-----------------------------------------------------------
Let N be the von Neumann algebra generated by the original Bob full-string
POVM effects, acting on the original shared Hilbert space with unit vector
eta. Original Alice effects commute with N. Set
  phi(n)=<eta,n eta>.
For an Alice-local success sub-POVM M_X^{a,E}, define

  s_(a|X)(n)=<eta,M_X^{a,E} n eta>,
  r_X=sum_a s_(a|X),  p_X=r_X(I),
  p=E_(mu_X^m) p_X>0.                                    (C1)

These are NORMAL positive functionals on N because M_X^{a,E} commutes
with N. Moreover r_X<=phi in functional order: for n>=0,
  phi(n)-r_X(n)=<eta,(I-sum_a M_X^{a,E})n eta> >= 0.
The product is of commuting positive operators. This is the functional
version of R_X<=sigma_B.

If needed restrict N to the support corner s(phi)N s(phi). All dominated
functionals are supported there, and replacing each Bob effect n by
s(phi)n s(phi) preserves all probabilities and POVM completeness in the
corner. The restricted phi is faithful normal, hence the corner is
sigma-finite. It is not required to be semifinite or to possess a trace.

Let Q_X=mu_X^m p_X/p and rho_X=r_X/p_X on Q-supported inputs. These are
normal states on N. The original event again leaves Y^m independent of
X,E under the free question law. All classical conditioning, suffix
averaging, and Bob POVM averaging in S3/S10/S11 of the frozen finite core
therefore make sense with density operators replaced by these normal
functionals. Call the resulting fine assemblage tau_a^x<=rho_x.

2. One common standard form supplies actual opposite POVMs
---------------------------------------------------------
Represent N ONCE in standard form (N,H,J,P), with its natural cone P.
Each normal positive functional rho has its unique representative xi_rho
in P, rho(n)=<xi_rho,n xi_rho>. The representation of N is the SAME for
every x, prefix, and coordinate; only its state vector changes.

Here is the needed bounded Radon--Nikodym lemma, including its proof.
For 0<=tau<=rho, put H_rho=closure(N xi_rho). On this dense orbit set

  <a xi_rho,T_tau b xi_rho>=tau(a* b).                      (C2)

The positive form is well defined and bounded by the Hilbert norm since
tau(a*a)<=rho(a*a)=||a xi_rho||^2; its Cauchy inequality bounds mixed
terms. Thus it defines a positive contraction T_tau on H_rho. For n in N,

  <a xi,T_tau n b xi>=tau(a* n b)
                    =<n* a xi,T_tau b xi>
                    =<a xi,n T_tau b xi>.                (C3)

Hence T_tau commutes with N restricted to H_rho. The subspace H_rho is
reducing for N, so its projection p'_rho lies in N'. Extend T_tau by zero
on H_rho's orthogonal complement; then T_tau belongs to N',
0<=T_tau<=p'_rho, and

  tau(n)=<xi_rho,T_tau n xi_rho>.                          (C4)

For a finite assemblage sum_a tau_a=rho, the sum of these effects is
p'_rho, by (C2). Add I-p'_rho to one default answer. This gives a complete
finite-outcome Alice POVM E_x^a in the COMMON commutant N'. For each x use
rho=rho_x and its actual assemblage tau_a^x. On the comparison vector,

  <xi_(rho_x), E_x^a K_y^b xi_(rho_x)>=tau_a^x(K_y^b).      (C5)

All Bob K_y^b remain in N, so they commute with every E_x^a. No matrix
transpose is needed: the common commutant is the opposite local algebra.
These effects need not lie in the ORIGINAL Alice algebra; the constructed
strategy is legal for the unrestricted commuting value, whose allowed
local algebras may change. No restricted-algebra compilation is claimed.

3. Explicit analytic facts needed from modular entropy
------------------------------------------------------
For normal states rho,sigma on this same standard-form algebra, the needed
canonical affinity statement is

  D_Araki(rho||sigma)
       >= -2 ln <xi_rho,xi_sigma>
       >= ||xi_rho-xi_sigma||^2.                           (C6)

This is the natural-cone inner product, not an optimization over arbitrary
purifications or an input-dependent Uhlmann unitary. For faithful states
one direct proof uses the relative modular spectral measure at xi_rho:
its sqrt moment is the canonical affinity and its negative log moment is
D_Araki. Scalar Jensen for log and -ln t>=1-t give (C6).

The nonfaithful case has a direct sourced route without assuming a
matrix logarithm or an unproved limiting identity. Hiai [H], Section 2.1,
defines S_(rho,sigma)=J Delta_(rho,sigma)^(1/2) on the supported domain,
and gives S_(rho,sigma)xi_sigma=s(sigma)xi_rho. Consequently
  Q_(1/2)(rho||sigma)
    =<xi_sigma,Delta_(rho,sigma)^(1/2)xi_sigma>
    =<xi_sigma,xi_rho>.
The last equality uses J xi_rho=xi_rho and
Js(sigma)J=s_(N')(sigma), which fixes xi_sigma. Proposition 5.3(3)-(4)
of [H] gives D_1=D_Araki and D_1>=D_(1/2) for ALL normal states.
Since D_(1/2)=-2ln Q_(1/2), this proves (C6) with supports already
handled by the source's definition. The squared norm is
2-2<xi_rho,xi_sigma>. Zero affinity gives an infinite lower bound.

Alternatively, in an actual reconstruction branch one may first restrict
to the support corner of the REFERENCE sigma=rho_bar, since every
q-positive rho_x and its assemblage are supported there, and regularize
rho_x toward faithful rho_bar on that corner. Joint convexity/lower
semicontinuity in [H], Theorem 4.1(i)-(ii), control the entropy limit;
Corollary 4.4(1),(3) supplies the compatible support/perturbation setting.
Only the REFERENCE support is used. Compressing sigma merely to the
target support s(rho_x), when sigma has off-diagonal blocks across it,
could change the relative entropy and is not done.

The needed upper bound is functional domination:

  rho<=sigma/c  => D_Araki(rho||sigma)<=ln(1/c).             (C7)

There is a short proof using a compatible classical flag, valid also for
nonfaithful normal states. For 0<c<1 write
  sigma=c rho+(1-c)nu,
where nu is a normal state. On N direct_sum N compare
  Upsilon=rho direct_sum 0,
  Xi=c rho direct_sum (1-c)nu.
The direct-sum/scaling formula gives D(Upsilon||Xi)=ln(1/c).
Restriction along the normal unital diagonal embedding n |-> n direct_sum n
forgets the flag. Monotonicity therefore gives D(rho||sigma)<=ln(1/c).
For c=1 the two normalized states coincide. This proof does not use
operator logarithms of density matrices or compression to s(rho).

The flag proof is pinned by [H], Proposition 2.3(4), Theorem 4.1(iv), and
Corollary 4.2(1). The latter includes the exact proportional-state value.

For a finite classical register, direct-sum additivity and scalar scaling
give
 D(direct_sum_x q_x rho_x || direct_sum_x mu_x phi)
   =D(q||mu)+sum_x q_x D(rho_x||phi).                     (C7a)
The finite-mixture identity imported here is
 sum_x q_x D(rho_x||phi)
   =sum_x q_x D(rho_x||rho_bar)+D(rho_bar||phi),
 rho_bar=sum_x q_x rho_x.                                (C7b)
Petz [P], Theorem 1.1(1e), states it for positive functionals on arbitrary
C*-algebras, hence includes these normal functionals. When using his
unnormalized formulation, terms q_x ln q_x occur on BOTH sides and
cancel; first-argument scaling alone is not joint homogeneity. Petz
attributes the identity to Donald. Donald's original 1990 full proof was
not independently inspected in this unit; (C7b) is used as Petz's explicit
standard theorem, not claimed as a new identity proved here.

All relative entropies used in the application are finite by (C7).
No cancellation of infinite entropies is permitted. Define Holevo
quantities directly as sums of relative entropies, avoiding individual
von Neumann entropies in a type-III algebra.

4. Same loss-aware local operation and original event budget
-----------------------------------------------------------
On the direct-sum algebra l_infty(Xcal^m) tensor N the normalized selected
normal state Omega has blocks mu_X^m r_X/p. By (C1),
  Omega <= (mu_X^m tensor phi)/p.
Equations (C7), direct-sum decomposition, and Donald identity yield

  D_ref + I(X^m:N)_Omega
           +D_Araki(rho_bar||phi) <= ln(1/p).              (C8)

For a fixed (i,h), set rho_bar=sum_x q_h(x)rho_x. The NEW resource vector
is xi_(rho_bar), independent of fresh x,y. Alice uses (C2)--(C5), Bob the
original averaged K_y. For a bounded losing payoff the operator
  W_xy=sum_ab (1-V_xyab) E_x^a K_y^b
lies in [0,I] because all cross-party factors commute, are positive, and
sum to I. Thus precisely the same vector triangle argument as S15 gives

  beta_Q <= alpha+2sqrt(alpha H)+H,
  H=sum_x q_h(x)||xi_(rho_x)-xi_(rho_bar)||^2
       <=sum_x q_h(x)D_Araki(rho_x||rho_bar).               (C9)

For precision, for every positive prefix u put rho_u=E_Q[rho_X|u].
The mixture order rho_bar>=Q(u)rho_u and rho_h>=q_h(x)rho_hx makes every
prefix divergence finite by (C7). Applying (C7b) to children h,x with
fixed reference rho_bar gives the difference of adjacent prefix sums;
averaging and telescoping yields
 sum_i E_(Q(X_<i)) sum_x q_h(x)D(rho_hx||rho_h)
   =sum_X Q(X)D(rho_X||rho_bar)=I(X^m:N).
No entropy subtraction or coherent copy of a prefix is used.
The classical Q-prefix drift has exactly the same Pinsker bound
as in the finite-tensor proof. A finite direct sum over the offline (i,h)
labels gives one fixed input-independent commuting resource and one
all-input family of bounded local POVMs. The resulting inequality
has the SAME form, with its OWN commuting base gap epsilon_co:

  epsilon_co <= alpha+2sqrt(alpha I/m)+I/m
                         +sqrt(D_ref/(2m)),
  I+D_ref<=ln(1/p).                                       (C10)

In particular pointwise-constant success gives
  epsilon_co <= (sqrt(alpha)+sqrt(ln(1/p)/m))^2.

5. Model and resource boundary
------------------------------
The standard form may be infinite dimensional. This is allowed in the
commuting model and is not a finite implementation or finite matrix
resource guarantee. It changes the representation and, on Alice's side,
may enlarge the local algebra to N'. Its finite input/output probabilities
are reproduced by the explicitly constructed state/effects using the
normal-state analytic facts in Section 3 at their stated scope.

The finite-tensor theorem remains independently proved and frozen. This
theorem does not promote a standard-form vector to a finite-tracial state,
invoke a table-density premise, or infer anything for Bob-dependent event
conditioning. The causal factorization is still one-sided: Bob's original
complete POVM is untouched, and every accepted fine functional is an
Alice assemblage on the SAME N whose divergence is budgeted.
