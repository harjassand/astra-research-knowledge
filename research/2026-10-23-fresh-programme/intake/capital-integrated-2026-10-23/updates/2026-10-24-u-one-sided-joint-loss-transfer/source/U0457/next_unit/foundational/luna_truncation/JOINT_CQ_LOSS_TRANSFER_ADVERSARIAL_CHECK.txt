ADVERSARIAL CHECK: JOINT CQ VECTOR TRANSFER TO THE FRESH LAW
2026-10-24. Check of root's proposed strengthening for the frozen
one-sided channel-minorisation selector.

Disposition
-----------
PASS under the frozen source hypotheses and the common-standard-form
construction. I find no exact obstruction in the proposed vector
transfer. The key point is that the old direct loss-transfer argument
did not use the sharpest common comparison: put the selected law and
the fresh law into one classical-quantum direct sum and include the
fresh Bob input by a common conditional channel. The resulting state
distance charges both selected-input drift and conditional-state
variation at once.

Exact branch calculation
------------------------
Fix an offline branch r. Write q_x=Q(X_i=x|r), mu_x=mu_X(x),
t(y|x)=mu(y|x), and let rho_x be the normalized selected Bob state for
every q_x-positive x. Put rhobar=sum_x q_x rho_x. For q_x=0, no rho_x
is needed. The same legal branch strategy uses a fixed shared
standard-form vector xi_rhobar, Alice POVMs E_x^a in the commutant, and
Bob POVMs K_y^b in the Bob algebra. For q-positive x, the RN/HJW
identity is

  <xi_rho_x, E_x^a K_y^b xi_rho_x> = tau_a^x(K_y^b).

For q-zero x, choose any complete Alice POVM. This makes the branch an
all-input strategy; the loss at such fresh-law labels is paid by the
classical drift term below.

For loss payoff ell_xyab=1-V_xyab in [0,1], define

  W_xy = sum_ab ell_xyab E_x^a K_y^b.

Each product is positive since the two algebras commute. The products
over all (a,b) sum to I, so 0<=W_xy<=I. This remains true for general
bounded [0,1] loss payoffs, not just indicator predicates.

Use the analysis-only direct sum over x,y:

  v = direct_sum_(x,y) sqrt(q_x t(y|x)) xi_rho_x,
  w = direct_sum_(x,y) sqrt(mu_x t(y|x)) xi_rhobar,
  W = direct_sum_(x,y) W_xy.

The vectors are unit vectors. Expanding their expectations and applying
the selected-table identity gives

  <v,Wv> = alpha_r,
  <w,Ww> = beta_r,

where alpha_r is the original branch loss under its actual conditional
law q_x t(y|x), and beta_r is this branch's legal loss under the
original fresh law mu_x t(y|x), on the fixed resource xi_rhobar. In
particular the correlated question law is retained in both sums; the
replacement changes only the x marginal.

Joint CQ distance and its exact budget
--------------------------------------
In standard form for the direct-sum algebra, v and w are the natural
cone vectors of

  omega = direct_sum_x q_x rho_x,
  sigma = direct_sum_x mu_x rhobar,

after the same classical channel x -> (x,y) with transition t(y|x).
That channel is an isometry on the direct-sum vectors because
sum_y t(y|x)=1. It preserves their squared distance and the relative
entropy. Thus, with d_r=D(q||mu_X) and
chi_r=sum_x q_x D(rho_x||rhobar),

  ||v-w||^2 <= D(omega||sigma)
             = d_r + chi_r.

This uses the common natural-cone vectors, not arbitrary input-wise
purification choices. In finite tensor models the shared Hilbert-Schmidt
purification vectors |sqrt(rho_x)> and |sqrt(rhobar)> provide the same
canonical common representation. For q-positive x, supp(rho_x) is
contained in supp(rhobar), so the state relative entropies are
well-defined (possibly infinite before the source budget; finite once
the telescope applies). The classical divergence d_r is finite because
q is absolutely continuous with respect to mu_X.

Since 0<=W<=I,

  sqrt(beta_r)=||sqrt(W)w||
      <= ||sqrt(W)v|| + ||sqrt(W)(w-v)||
      <= sqrt(alpha_r) + sqrt(d_r+chi_r),

and therefore beta_r <= (sqrt(alpha_r)+sqrt(d_r+chi_r))^2.

This explicitly handles zero-q labels. For q_x=0, the v block is zero,
while the w block is present when mu_x t(y|x)>0; the resulting fresh-law
loss is included in ||v-w|| and paid for by D(q||mu_X). No state
rho_x is needed in that zero-q block. If mu_x=0 then q_x=0 as well and
the label has no fresh-law block.

Offline-branch averaging
-----------------------
Let branches include the uniform coordinate i and r distributed by its
selected source marginal. Let alpha=E alpha_r and
h=E(d_r+chi_r). A legal strategy samples this finite branch flag before
fresh inputs and uses that branch's fixed resource and POVMs. It does
not expose (x,y) as shared randomness. By Minkowski in L2,

  sqrt(beta) = ||sqrt(beta_r)||_2
      <= ||sqrt(alpha_r)||_2+||sqrt(d_r+chi_r)||_2
      = sqrt(alpha)+sqrt(h),

so beta <= (sqrt(alpha)+sqrt(h))^2. The frozen entropy telescope gives
h<=ln(1/p)/(delta m), yielding the claimed fresh-law bound. This
averaging uses the original branch weights; no reweighting by the
branch-specific success probability is introduced.

Boundary checks
---------------
* Locality: D11 supplies a single Bob POVM K_y^b indexed only by branch
  r and Bob's fresh y. The accepted assemblage on B is independent of
  Bob's fresh outcome because E is Alice-local. If E depends on Bob's
  fresh input/output, D11 and hence this construction can fail.
* Tensor transpose: canonical purification realizes tau_a^x by the
  support-completed Alice effect M_(a|x)^T on the same reference basis;
  the effect is positive and complete, and Bob K_y remains on the other
  tensor factor. The losing operator is their tensor product sum.
* Commuting model: all rho_x live as normal states on one fixed Bob
  algebra N and one fixed standard-form Hilbert space. The RN effects
  lie in N', Bob effects in N, so W is a positive contraction. No
  input-dependent change of representation is made.
* Mathematical direct sum: x,y blocks are a proof device for a norm and
  expectation identity. The actual strategy samples only (i,r) offline
  and receives the genuine fresh correlated pair according to mu. It
  never samples a fresh pair into shared randomness or gives Alice y.

Conclusion and scope
--------------------
The proposed bound is a sound sharpening of the earlier Pinsker fresh-
law correction under the frozen one-sided factorization and entropy
telescope. It removes the extra sqrt(d_bar/2) term by combining drift
and state-change errors in a single canonical CQ distance. The result
still depends on the frozen causal one-sided event, source entropy
budget, and finite-input branch mixture; it says nothing about
Bob-dependent selection or efficiency of computing the conditional
states and POVMs.
