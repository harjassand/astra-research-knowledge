1. Theorem and exact source scope
---------------------------------
Let G be a finite two-player game with bounded payoff V in [0,1], question
law mu(x,y), and base gap epsilon=1-omega_model(G). Use EITHER the finite
tensor model with its own omega_* OR the unrestricted commuting model
with its own omega_co; these base values are not identified.

Draw m>=1 question PAIRS independently. Allow arbitrary full-string
joint local measurements and an input-independent shared resource.
Alice has an actual success sub-POVM M_X^(a,E), while Bob's original
full-string complete POVM is untouched. E may depend on the full Alice
question string and her actual instrument outcome, including quantum
steering of Bob's system. It does not depend on Bob's question or outcome.
Let p=P(E)>0, L=ln(1/p), and alpha be the original conditional mean
fraction of coordinate losses given E.

Assume, on positive-marginal Alice inputs, the referee channel satisfies
  mu(y|x)=delta nu(y)+(1-delta)kappa(y|x),  0<delta<=1.      (J1)
The maximal such delta is sum_y min_x mu(y|x). This is a condition on
the referee law. Its auxiliary reset flag is not made available online.
Free inputs satisfy (J1) with delta=1. Alice and Bob can be interchanged.

Then a legal base strategy in the SAME model is constructed with loss beta
satisfying, for ARBITRARY input-dependent source success probabilities,

  beta <= (sqrt(alpha)+sqrt(tau))^2,
  epsilon <= (sqrt(alpha)+sqrt(tau))^2,
  tau = ln(1/p)/(delta m).                                (J2)

Equivalently,
  p <= exp[-delta m (sqrt(epsilon)-sqrt(alpha))_+^2].       (J3)

There is no pointwise-constant-success hypothesis in (J2). If alpha<=
epsilon, a sharper exact binary-affinity consequence is

 ln(1/p)/(delta m)
   >= -2ln[sqrt(alpha epsilon)
                     +sqrt((1-alpha)(1-epsilon))].        (J4)

In particular zero selected live loss gives
  p <= (1-epsilon)^(delta m)=omega_model(G)^(delta m).      (J5)
The boundary epsilon=1 is interpreted by the appropriate limits: a
positive-probability zero-loss event of the stated source type cannot
exist. If alpha>epsilon, (J4) is not asserted and (J3) is trivial.

This is a source-compatible one-sided event theorem, not a theorem for an
arbitrary repeated all-win event, which normally depends on both players'
answers. No assumption of an efficient description or finite commuting
resource is concealed in the statement; those costs are below.

2. Actual operation and its allocated cost
------------------------------------------
The operation is exactly the one already proved in
  ONE_SIDED_INSTRUMENT_SELECTION.txt,
  COMMUTING_ONE_SIDED_INSTRUMENT_SELECTION.txt,
  ONE_SIDED_CHANNEL_MINORISATION_SELECTION.txt.
Here are its source registers and local actions, to fix the interface.

On the original Bob algebra let phi be his original normal state and let
s_(a|X) be the actual fine steering subfunctional from Alice's success
instrument. Put r_X=sum_a s_(a|X)<=phi, p_X=r_X(I), rho_X=r_X/p_X.
The selected cq law is
  Q(X,Y)=mu^m(X,Y)p_X/p,  quantum state at (X,Y) is rho_X.
Thus Q(Y|X)=product_j mu(Y_j|X_j), even after selection.

For coordinate i and classical history r=(h,v)=(X_<i,Y_>i), define the
actual same-B fine assemblage
  tau_a^(r,x)=E_(Q(X_>i=u|r,x))
               [sum_(a_string:a_i=a)s_(a_string|h,x,u)]/p_(h,x,u),
  rho_(r,x)=sum_a tau_a^(r,x), q_r(x)=Q(X_i=x|r),
  rho_bar_r=sum_x q_r(x)rho_(r,x).                         (J6)
Zero-p_X terms have zero Q weight and are omitted. Bob uses the complete
POVM K_y^(r,b) obtained from his original full-string POVM by fixing the
current y and suffix v and averaging only Y_<i according to
product_(j<i)mu(.|h_j). It depends on r,y, not on fresh x.

Expansion in the original full fine table gives EXACTLY the conditional
live table tau_a^(r,x)(K_y^(r,b)), with question law q_r(x)mu(y|x).
Alice's new HJW/RN effects E_x^(r,a), complete for every fresh x, realize
this table on the comparison vector xi_(rho_(r,x)). On the fixed resource
xi_(rho_bar_r), the same effects together with Bob's same K_y form the
constructed legal branch strategy. In finite tensors these are canonical
square-root purifications with the appropriate Alice transpose; in the
commuting model they are natural-cone vectors in ONE common standard
form, with Alice RN effects in N' and Bob effects in N. Default outcomes
complete supports and zero-q_r fresh inputs. All cross-party effects
commute in either representation.

For each branch define the classical and quantum costs
  d_r=D(q_r||mu_X),
  chi_r=sum_x q_r(x)D_A(rho_(r,x)||rho_bar_r).              (J7)
The source's actual mixed-state order gives total entropy budget L.
The frozen channel theorem proves, by its hybrid F_i telescope and the
independent Doeblin flag inequality,
  (1/m)sum_i E_(Q(R_i)) [d_r+chi_r] <= L/(delta m)=tau.     (J8)
No coherent Lüders state is compared by relative entropy to its original
pure state. The register in (J6)-(J8) is the actual same Bob system.

3. One block-vector calculation charges the fresh input law
----------------------------------------------------------
Let pi_(i,r)=Q(R_i=r)/m be the OFFLINE shared branch probabilities.
For any branch t=(i,r), abbreviate q=q_r, rho_x=rho_(r,x),
rho_bar=rho_bar_r. On a mathematical direct-sum Hilbert space define

  v_t=direct_sum_(x,y) sqrt[q(x)mu(y|x)] xi_(rho_x),
  w_t=direct_sum_(x,y) sqrt[mu_X(x)mu(y|x)] xi_(rho_bar),
  W_t=direct_sum_(x,y) sum_ab(1-V_xyab) E_x^(t,a) K_y^(t,b).
                                                               (J9)

Each losing operator in (J9) is a positive contraction: its cross-party
factors commute and are complete POVMs. Hence 0<=W_t<=I. The two unit
vectors use the SAME constructed effects, and
  <v_t,W_t v_t>=alpha_t,
  <w_t,W_t w_t>=beta_t.                                  (J10)
The first is the original selected conditional branch loss by (J6).
The second is EXACTLY the constructed legal loss under original fresh
mu, because its quantum resource xi_(rho_bar) is independent of x,y.

The classical x,y block labels are used ONLY to compute expected loss.
They are not a coherent question oracle, an extra shared live transcript,
or a new operation available to the players. Implementation still uses
the fixed resource in each offline branch and the local POVMs E_x,K_y.

These vectors are canonical representatives of the normal cq states
  omega_t=direct_sum_(x,y) q(x)mu(y|x)rho_x,
  sigma_t=direct_sum_(x,y) mu_X(x)mu(y|x)rho_bar.
The finite cq formula gives
  D_A(omega_t||sigma_t)=d_r+chi_r.                         (J11)
Attaching y with the SAME fixed channel on both sides preserves the
canonical inner product and this relative entropy. In particular, zero
q(x) blocks have zero target vector and their fresh-law mass is properly
included; no target probability or input support is silently discarded.

For normal states in a common standard form, the sourced canonical
affinity inequality is
  D_A(omega||sigma)>=-2ln<xi_omega,xi_sigma>
                    >=||xi_omega-xi_sigma||^2.            (J12)
It is ordinary finite-matrix relative entropy in the tensor case. The
commuting companion supplies the general-normal-state proof from Hiai's
supported relative modular definition and Renyi monotonicity, with Petz's
finite-mixture identity imported as an explicit standard theorem.

Now take the additional offline direct sum
  v=direct_sum_t sqrt(pi_t)v_t, w=direct_sum_t sqrt(pi_t)w_t,
  W=direct_sum_t W_t.
Both vectors have norm one. Their normal-state relative entropy is
T=sum_t pi_t(d_r+chi_r)<=tau, and their losing expectations are the
original alpha and the legal mixed-strategy loss beta. Thus

 sqrt(beta)=||sqrt(W)w||
   <=||sqrt(W)v||+||sqrt(W)(w-v)||
   <=sqrt(alpha)+||w-v|| <=sqrt(alpha)+sqrt(T).             (J13)

Equations (J8),(J13), and beta>=epsilon prove (J2)-(J3). This directly
charges the classical input-law change together with the quantum-state
change. A separate Pinsker/TV drift term is unnecessary here.

4. Exact affinity corollary
----------------------------
Let A=<v,w>, a nonnegative canonical inner product. From (J11)-(J12),
  A>=exp(-T/2)>=exp(-tau/2).
Split the SAME inner product using W and I-W. Cauchy-Schwarz gives
  A <=sqrt(alpha beta)+sqrt((1-alpha)(1-beta)).             (J14)
For beta>=epsilon>=alpha, the right side is at most its value at
beta=epsilon. This proves (J4). For alpha=0, (J14) says
  exp(-tau)<=1-beta<=1-epsilon,
which gives (J5).

There is NO relative-entropy data processing through W in this step.
W may involve both commuting algebras and need not lie in Bob's N.
The only assertion about W is the elementary shared-vector Cauchy
inequality (J14); entropy was already used on the cq algebra in (J12).

5. Costs, prior boundary, and the unresolved gate
-------------------------------------------------
The reconstructed quantum resource and local effects are unchanged from
the frozen theorems. For a finite original Bob dimension d_B, each offline
branch uses at most d_B local quantum dimension. There can be up to
sum_i |X|^(i-1)|Y|^(m-i) branches, requiring explicit posterior weights,
assemblages, and suffix/prefix averages to specify the strategy. These
data and computations may be exponential. Small-spectrum inverses do
not create unbounded POVM effects, but no numerical precision, sample,
learning, or efficient physical compiler bound is proved.

The direct commuting construction may use an infinite-dimensional
standard form and replace the original Alice algebra by N'. It proves a
same-model value statement with no finite representation claim. Tensor
and commuting gaps remain separate in every formula.

The actual selection gate is still one-sided: Bob's original complete
POVM is untouched, so Alice's accepted steering functionals form an
assemblage on the SAME Bob system and retain the product question
channel. A Bob-dependent event need not have that interface. Small
referee drift or a measured cq information budget alone does not supply
it. The general Bob-dependent selection problem remains unresolved.

No priority claim is made. Canonical Hellinger/affinity estimates,
Doeblin strong data processing, communication extraction, and anchored
repetition are governing conventional priors. The exact source operation
and strengthened bounds are internally proved at the stated scope;
external novelty and efficient acquisition remain UNKNOWN.
