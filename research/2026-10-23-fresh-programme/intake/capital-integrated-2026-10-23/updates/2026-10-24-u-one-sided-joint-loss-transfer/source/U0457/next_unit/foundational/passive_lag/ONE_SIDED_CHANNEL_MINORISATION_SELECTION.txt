1. Source and statement
-----------------------
Let G have finite question and answer sets, arbitrary bounded payoff
V(x,y,a,b) in [0,1], and one-round law mu(x,y). Across m>=1 rounds draw
the question PAIRS independently. The source strategy may use arbitrary
full-string joint local measurements and arbitrary entanglement.

Assume the forward question channel has a common input-independent part:

 mu(y|x)=delta nu(y)+(1-delta) kappa(y|x),  0<delta<=1,      (D1)

for every x of positive mu_X mass. This is a condition on the known
referee law, not a sampled flag that is made available to either player.
The maximal possible delta is sum_y min_(x:mu_X(x)>0) mu(y|x).
Free inputs have delta=1; full support gives delta>0; general correlated
input laws need not have a positive delta. The two roles may be reversed.

Selection is ONE-SIDED: Alice has a full-string success sub-POVM
M_X^(a,E). Bob's full-string complete POVM N_Y^b is untouched by E.
There is no Bob-dependent outcome in the success definition. Let p>0 be
the original probability of E and alpha the original conditional mean
fraction of coordinate losses given E. Write L=ln(1/p).

For a finite tensor source, define the actual Bob steering substates
S_(a|X)=Tr_A[(M_X^(a,E) tensor I) sigma_AB],
R_X=sum_a S_(a|X), p_X=Tr R_X, rho_X=R_X/p_X. The positive sandwich
form of the partial trace makes S positive. The source gives R_X<=sigma_B.
The normalized selected classical-quantum state is

 Q(X,Y)=mu^m(X,Y) p_X/p,
 Omega_(X,Y,B)=sum_(X,Y) Q(X,Y) |X,Y><X,Y| tensor rho_X.    (D2)

Zero-p_X labels have zero Q mass and are omitted. Crucially,
  Q(Y|X)=product_j mu(Y_j|X_j).                            (D3)
Bob's original physical register B, not a purifier or a copied answer
record, is the quantum register in (D2).

The theorem, proved below, constructs one legal same-model base
strategy with loss at most

 alpha+2 sqrt(alpha chi_bar)+chi_bar+sqrt(d_bar/2),
 d_bar+chi_bar <= L/(delta m).                             (D4)

Consequently epsilon=1-omega_*(G) obeys the same inequality, and in
particular with tau=L/(delta m),
  epsilon <= alpha+2 sqrt(alpha tau)+tau+sqrt(tau/2).       (D5)
The commuting version has its OWN epsilon_co and follows by the explicit
common-standard-form construction and imported normal-state entropy
facts in COMMUTING_ONE_SIDED_INSTRUMENT_SELECTION.txt. No matrix
density premise or equality of tensor/commuting base values is invoked.

If p_X=p on every supported full X, then d_bar=0 and
  epsilon <= (sqrt(alpha)+sqrt(L/(delta m)))^2.            (D6)
Thus alpha<=epsilon/4 forces L>=delta m epsilon/4 at this source interface.
This is not a generic repetition theorem: an arbitrary tested event may
depend on Bob outcomes and fail (D3).

2. Actual local factorization after a dependency-breaking history
----------------------------------------------------------------
For coordinate i use the CLASSICAL history
  R_i=(H,V)=(X_<i,Y_>i).                                  (D7)
It will be sampled offline for the NEW strategy, independently of the new
fresh question pair. It is not an actual public transcript in the source.
For a positive-Q history r=(h,v), let
  q_r(x)=Q(X_i=x|R_i=r),
  w_r,x(u)=Q(X_>i=u|R_i=r,X_i=x).

Define the actual fine assemblage on the SAME B by
  tau_a^(r,x)=sum_u w_r,x(u)
       [sum_(a_string:a_i=a) S_(a_string|h,x,u)]/p_(h,x,u),
  rho_(r,x)=sum_a tau_a^(r,x).                             (D8)
For zero-q_r(x) labels choose an arbitrary state and complete POVM later.
No division by a zero p_X is used in (D8).

Define a Bob POVM, depending only on r and his FRESH y, by
  K_y^(r,b)=sum_z [product_(j<i) mu(z_j|h_j)]
       sum_(b_string:b_i=b) N_(z,y,v)^(b_string).           (D9)
This is complete and positive. In particular it is not indexed by the
fresh Alice question x. The suffix v is part of the offline shared r.

Given the complete X string, E leaves Bob's question distribution exactly
as (D3). Therefore conditioning on r and current (x,y) leaves the prefix
Y_<i distributed as product_(j<i)mu(.|h_j), independently of the residual
X_>i distribution w_r,x. It also gives
  Q(X_i=x,Y_i=y|r)=q_r(x) mu(y|x).                         (D10)
The original selected live fine table conditional on r,x,y is EXACTLY
  Tr[tau_a^(r,x) K_y^(r,b)].                              (D11)
This follows by expanding (D8)-(D9) in the original full outcome law;
no state recovery assumption, chosen purification, or referee oracle is
inserted. Noncommuting quantum steering and full-string measurements are

3. The hybrid information telescope
-----------------------------------
All entropies below use natural logarithms. Conditional quantum mutual
information with classical conditioning is defined as an average of
relative-entropy Holevo quantities. Thus no difference of potentially
infinite von Neumann entropies is required in the commuting version.

Let phi=sigma_B (or the original normal Bob state in the commuting model).
The actual positive-functional order R_X<=phi implies
  B_total:=D(Omega_XB || mu_X^m tensor phi) <= L.           (D12)
Indeed Omega_XB<= (mu_X^m tensor phi)/p. A compatible binary-mixture flag
and data processing prove the entropy upper bound; no coherent Lüders
postselection relative entropy is claimed. Also, (D3) implies that adding
the classical Y string using its fixed channel does not change B_total.

For 0<=i<=m, define
  F_i=E_(Q(Y_>i)) D(Omega_(X_<=i,B |Y_>i)
                            ||mu_X^i tensor phi).         (D13)
At i=0 this is E_(Q(Y^m))D(Omega_(B|Y^m)||phi)>=0;
at i=m it is B_total. Each F_i is finite: data processing on the global
selected cq state gives F_i+D(Q(Y_>i)||mu_Y^(m-i))<=B_total.

For r=R_i set
  d_i=E_r D(q_r||mu_X),
  chi_i=E_r sum_x q_r(x) D(rho_(r,x)||rho_bar_r),
  rho_bar_r=sum_x q_r(x)rho_(r,x).                         (D14)

Twice using finite-mixture Donald identity on the same register gives the
EXACT difference

  F_i-F_(i-1)=d_i+chi_i
                 -I(Y_i : X_<i B | Y_>i)_Omega.           (D15)

For clarity, first peel X_i off F_i. What remains is
E_(Q(Y_>i))D(Omega_(X_<i,B|Y_>i)||mu_X^(i-1) tensor phi),
plus d_i+chi_i. Next express F_(i-1) as that SAME remainder
plus the last mutual information in (D15). All quantities are finite,
so this telescoping does not subtract extended infinities.

Conditioned on any supported Y_>i, the cq state has the exact Markov
structure
  Y_i <- X_i -> (X_<i,B),                                 (D16)
with forward channel mu(.|x). This is an actual property of the state
(D2), since only X and Alice's instrument determine Bob's substate.
Let U=X_<i B. Realize (D1) by an auxiliary mathematical Bernoulli flag
independent of (X_i,U,Y_>i). Revealing it can only increase I(Y_i:U).
The reset branch has zero information, while data processing on the
other branch gives, for each fixed Y_>i,

 I(Y_i:U|Y_>i) <= (1-delta) I(X_i:U|Y_>i)
                <= (1-delta)(d_i+chi_i).                  (D17)

The second inequality uses
I(X_i:U|Y_>i)=I(X_i:X_<i|Y_>i)+chi_i
and
d_i=I(X_i:X_<i|Y_>i)+E_(Q(Y_>i))D(Q(X_i|Y_>i)||mu_X).
The flag is used only in this proof, never supplied online to the players.

Combining (D15)-(D17) yields
  delta sum_i(d_i+chi_i) <= F_m-F_0 <= L.                  (D18)
This is the selection principle: some averaged history/coordinate has a
small ACTUAL same-register assemblage cost. It does not replace this cost
with information in a coarser measured record or a local purifier.

4. Legal all-input loss-aware reconstruction
--------------------------------------------
For each offline branch (i,r) use rho_bar_r as the fixed reference state.
In finite dimensions take its canonical purification. Alice uses the
support-completed HJW POVM for tau_a^(r,x), and Bob uses (D9).
On the comparison purification of rho_(r,x) the same effects reproduce
(D11) exactly. On the fixed reference vector the same losing effect is
a positive contraction. Canonical-vector distance is bounded by relative
entropy, so the vector triangle gives branch loss (under (D10)) at most
  alpha_(i,r)+2 sqrt(alpha_(i,r) H_(i,r))+H_(i,r),
  H_(i,r)<=sum_x q_r(x)D(rho_(r,x)||rho_bar_r).              (D19)

The same operation works in a common standard form: each normal
assemblage tau<=rho has an explicit positive-contraction RN effect in
the common commutant. Complete it on the support complement. Bob stays
in his algebra. The standard-form companion gives that operation and
the natural-cone distance bound; this is not arbitrary Uhlmann matching.

Now feed the constructed branch the ORIGINAL fresh law mu, not (D10).
The question TV cost is EXACTLY
  TV(q_r(x)mu(y|x),mu(x,y))=TV(q_r,mu_X)
                         <=sqrt(D(q_r||mu_X)/2).          (D20)
This charges zero-q_r labels too, on which Alice's POVM is merely completed.
It does not require local knowledge of the other fresh input.

Sample i uniformly and r with its ORIGINAL selected marginal Q(R_i),
independently of fresh inputs. The collection is a finite classical
offline flag. Fixed block resources and block POVMs give one legal
all-input strategy. Cauchy-Schwarz on the loss-aware cross term and
Pinsker/Jensen on (D20) give (D4), with
  chi_bar=(1/m)sum_i chi_i, d_bar=(1/m)sum_i d_i.
Equation (D18) proves the budget. If p_X is constant, Q(X,Y)=mu^m(X,Y),
so X_i is independent of (X_<i,Y_>i), q_r=mu_X, and d_bar=0.

5. Resources and exact scope
----------------------------
For a finite original Bob dimension d_B the new canonical resource has
local dimension at most d_B in each branch. The offline branch count is
at most sum_i |X|^(i-1)|Y|^(m-i). The source matrices, all posterior
weights, and all finite suffix/prefix sums must be known to specify the
new POVMs; they may require exponential data and computation. Bounded
effects exist despite support inverses, but numerical conditioning and
precision costs are not bounded here. No efficient acquisition/compiler
or sample bound is claimed.

The direct commuting operation may use an infinite-dimensional standard
form and enlarge Alice's algebra to the common commutant. It is legal
for the unrestricted commuting value, with no finite-resource claim.

The useful new assumptions are ONE-SIDED causal success and a quantified
noise component of the referee channel. They are not consequences of
small referee drift or low information alone for an arbitrary two-sided
tested event. General positive-gap repetition and a payoff-aware
two-sided selection principle remain open in this programme. Novelty
against communication/anchoring/strong-data-processing literature is
UNKNOWN; the elementary channel flag inequality itself is conventional.
