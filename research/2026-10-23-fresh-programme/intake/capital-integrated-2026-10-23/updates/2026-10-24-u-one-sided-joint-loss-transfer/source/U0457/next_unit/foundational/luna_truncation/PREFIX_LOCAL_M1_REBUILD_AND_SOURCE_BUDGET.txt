PREFIX-LOCAL M1 REBUILD: MESSAGE, ALICE-LOCAL SELECTION, AND PREFIX COST
2026-10-24. Bounded construction check for passive_lag.

Disposition
-----------
For a finite-dimensional one-way protocol with a fixed finite message
register C, averaging all other free-IID coordinates produces a genuine
one-sided assemblage for one live coordinate. The Bob measurement is an
ordinary POVM depending only on the live Bob input; the M1 map is the
identity, including when C is quantum and entangled with Bob's original
system. HJW then supplies one fixed resource before fresh live questions.
The full-string direct input information of CB is at most 2 ln(dim C).

There is also a separate event-conditioned version when success is
Alice-local. Its accepted Bob substate is dominated by Bob's original
marginal. The selected input distribution and a prefix KL decomposition
give the stated payoff bound. This argument does not cover an event
depending on Bob's fresh input or output. All conclusions are finite
tensor/finite-dimensional here; Section 6 records the commuting
standard-form operator check separately.

1. Exact prefix assemblage from a finite one-way message
-------------------------------------------------------
Let the question strings be free IID:
  X=(X_1,...,X_m)~mu_X^m,  Y=(Y_1,...,Y_m)~mu_Y^m,
independently. The initial finite-dimensional shared state is rho_AB and
is fixed before questions. Alice receives X and applies any CP instrument
whose classical output is a full answer string A and whose quantum output
is a message system C, dim(C)=D. The resulting positive substate on CB,
after tracing Alice's residual system, is denoted sigma_(A|X)^(CB).
The sum over A has trace one. It can be noncommuting on C tensor B; no
simultaneous diagonalization or block assumption is made.

Fix coordinate i and a prefix h=X_<i. For a full string X, put
  sigma_(a|X)=sum_(A:A_i=a) sigma_(A|X)^(CB).
Define the live assemblage by averaging the suffix under its original
product law:

  tau_(a|x,h)=E_(X_>i~mu_X^(m-i)) sigma_(a|X=(h,x,X_>i))^(CB),
  rho_(x,h)=sum_a tau_(a|x,h).                              (1)

Each tau is positive, sum_a tau=rho_(x,h), and Tr rho_(x,h)=1. The
one-way receiver's original full-string POVM is {N_Y^b}_b on C tensor B.
For each live y define the marginalized effect

  B_(y,h)^d = E_(Y_-i~mu_Y^(m-1)) sum_(b:b_i=d)
                         N_(Y=(Y_-i,y))^b.                  (2)

For every y, {B_(y,h)^d}_d is a POVM: it is an average of positive
effects, and summing d sums the original complete POVM over all b. It
does not actually depend on h; h is retained in the notation only if the
original protocol has fixed external parameters conditioned on h. Because
the question strings are free IID, X_>i and Y_-i have precisely the
averaging laws in (1)-(2) conditional on h,x,y. Therefore the original
one-way live table, after forgetting the other answers, obeys the exact
identity

  P(A_i=a,B_i=d | h,x,y)
       =Tr[B_(y,h)^d tau_(a|x,h)].                            (3)

This is M1 with Lambda_y=Id. If C is a classical message, represent it
as a diagonal register and (2) is the block-diagonal POVM with c-block
E_(Y_-i) N_(Y,c)^{b_i=d}. If C is quantum, (2) acts jointly on C and B;
the same averaging and POVM normalization proof applies. Alice's
original instrument may be noncommuting across X; only the positive
substates in (1) are used.

2. One all-input resource and support-completed HJW effects
----------------------------------------------------------
For fixed i,h, let nu_h(x)=mu_X(x) (in this unselected case), and set

  rhobar_h=sum_x nu_h(x)rho_(x,h).

Share the canonical purification
  |Omega_rhobar_h>=sum_j |j> tensor sqrt(rhobar_h)|j>
of CB with an Alice purifying register. This state is chosen before x,y.
For each x with nu_h(x)>0, on P_(x,h)=supp(rho_(x,h)) put

  M_(a|x,h)=rho_(x,h)^(-1/2)tau_(a|x,h)rho_(x,h)^(-1/2).

Since 0<=tau_a<=rho_(x,h), these are effects summing to P_(x,h).
Complete one default outcome by adding I-P_(x,h), and let Alice use the
transpose of the completed effect in the canonical basis. The identity

  Tr_A[(M_(a|x,h)^T tensor I)|Omega_(rho_(x,h))><...|]
       =tau_(a|x,h)

uses the usual complex-conjugate/transpose convention and holds without
assuming real matrices. The default term vanishes on the comparison
state because sqrt(rho_(x,h))(I-P_(x,h))=0. On the actual shared state
|Omega_rhobar_h>, the same default effect is retained; it is a genuine
POVM completion, not discarded mass. If nu_h(x)>0 then
supp(rho_(x,h)) is contained in supp(rhobar_h).

For any fresh x outside nu_h's support but in the base alphabet, assign
an arbitrary complete POVM (for example, always output the default
answer). This makes the strategy all-input. Such x has zero weight in
the conditional target table and will be charged by the question-law
total-variation term in Section 5. Bob uses the POVM (2). Thus every
input uses fixed local measurements on the same pre-input resource for
the h branch. To average over h, share h itself as a finite classical
flag, sampled before fresh x,y; its law may be the original prior or the
selected prefix law as specified in Section 5. The resource is then the
finite flagged mixture of the corresponding |Omega_rhobar_h> states.

For a loss predicate, its losing effect on the fixed resource lies
between zero and identity. Comparing |Omega_(rho_(x,h))> to
|Omega_(rhobar_h)> gives, with
  alpha_h=target conditional loss,  chi_h=sum_x nu_h(x)
             D(rho_(x,h)||rhobar_h),
the finite-dimensional bound

  loss_h <= alpha_h+2sqrt(alpha_h chi_h)+chi_h.              (4)

Here D uses natural logarithms. It follows from
||sqrt(rho)-sqrt(sigma)||_2^2<=D(rho||sigma) and the square-root
triangle inequality for the same losing effect on both vectors.
All default outcomes are included in that effect.

3. Input-information cost of a quantum message
-----------------------------------------------
Sum over Alice's whole A outcome and normalize; call the resulting state
on CB for full input X omega_(CB|X). Alice's instrument is trace-
preserving when all A outcomes are retained, so
  Tr_C omega_(CB|X)=rho_B
for every X. Hence the cq state has I(X:B)=0 and

  I(X:CB)=I(X:C|B)<=2 ln D.                                 (5)

The last inequality is the dimension bound
I(X:C|B)=H(C|B)-H(C|XB)<=ln D-(-ln D). It does not require C and B to
commute or be uncorrelated. By the chain rule for the IID input string,

  sum_i E_(h~mu_X^(i-1)) chi_(i,h)=I(X^m:CB)<=2 ln D.        (6)

Thus for a uniform live coordinate the mean chi is at most
2 ln D/m, and averaging (4) gives
  loss <= alpha_bar+2sqrt(alpha_bar*2ln(D)/m)+2ln(D)/m.
If C is a classical register with at most 2^r values, the sharper
I(X:C|B)<=H(C)<=r ln 2 applies.

For a D-dimensional quantum message, standard teleportation with a
pre-shared maximally entangled D-dimensional pair has a Bell outcome 0
whose unnormalized output on CB is exactly D^(-2) omega_(CB|X), even
when C is entangled with B. Explicitly,

  (<Phi_00| tensor I)(|psi>_(CB) tensor |Phi_00>_(A'C))
       =D^(-1)|psi>_(C'B).

The success probability is D^(-2), independent of X and the state. It
therefore multiplies an accepted source mass p by exactly D^(-2), while
leaving its normalized conditional table and question law unchanged.
This is a local Alice Bell-measurement postselection followed by Bob's
fixed identity-branch protocol; it is not a free physical transmission.

4. Generalization to an Alice-local success event
-------------------------------------------------
Now omit quantum communication. Let Alice's event E depend only on her
own input X and a subset of outcomes of her complete local instrument.
For accepted Alice outcomes A, let sigma_(A|X)^B be Bob's positive
substate and define

  R_X=sum_(A accepted) sigma_(A|X)^B <= sigma_B,
  p_X=Tr R_X,  p=E_(X~mu_X^m)p_X.

The domination holds because the accepted outcomes are a CP
trace-nonincreasing part of Alice's local instrument; sigma_B is the
unconditioned Bob marginal. For p_X>0 put rho_X=R_X/p_X and
Q(X)=mu_X^m(X)p_X/p. Inputs with p_X=0 have Q(X)=0 and are omitted.
The normalized selected cq state satisfies

  Omega_(E,XB)=p^(-1)sum_X mu_X^m(X)|X><X| tensor R_X
       <=p^(-1)(mu_X^m tensor sigma_B).

It follows that

  D_ref+I_E(X^m:B)<=ln(1/p),
  D_ref=D(Q||mu_X^m).                                      (7)

Indeed D(Omega_E||mu_X^m tensor sigma_B)<=ln(1/p) by operator
domination; decomposing this relative entropy gives D_ref plus
sum_X Q(X)D(rho_X||sigma_B), and the latter equals
I_E(X^m:B)+D(rhobar_E||sigma_B).

Fix i,h=X_<i with Q(h)>0. Let nu_h(x)=Q(X_i=x|h), and for
nu_h(x)>0 let

  rho_(x,h)=E_(X_>i~Q(.|h,x)) rho_X,
  tau_(a|x,h)=E_(X_>i~Q(.|h,x))
                    sigma_(A_i=a|X)/p_X.                  (8)

Then sum_a tau_(a|x,h)=rho_(x,h), Tr rho_(x,h)=1, and
  rhobar_h=sum_x nu_h(x)rho_(x,h).
The target is the normalized accepted live table conditional on h,x;
(8) is exact since Q(X_>i|h,x) is proportional to
mu_X^(m-i)(X_>i) p_X. Bob's fresh Y remains independent of X,E, so the
same averaged Bob POVM (2), with its original mu_Y suffix law, reproduces
the conditional accepted table exactly. Apply Section 2 to (8).

The mean selected state information over uniform i and Q-distributed h
is a chain-rule quantity:

  chi_bar=E_(i,h) sum_x nu_h(x)
                 D(rho_(x,h)||rhobar_h)
          = I_E(X^m:B)/m
          <= [ln(1/p)-D_ref]/m.                            (9)

Let alpha_bar be the selected conditional losing probability averaged
over i,h,x~nu_h and y~mu_Y. Averaging (4) gives a legal one-round
strategy with loss under that selected x law at most

  alpha_bar+2sqrt(alpha_bar*[ln(1/p)-D_ref]/m)
              +[ln(1/p)-D_ref]/m.                         (10)

To turn it into a base-game strategy, sample h from Q's prefix marginal
as shared randomness before the fresh inputs, but draw fresh x from
mu_X. The only change is the conditional live x law. By chain rule and
Pinsker in natural-log units,

  E_(i,h~Q) TV(Q(X_i|h),mu_X)
       <=sqrt(D_ref/(2m)).                                 (11)

Thus the base-law loss is at most the right side of (10) plus the right
side of (11). The seed h and branch state are fixed before fresh x,y;
no player learns the other side's fresh input. If Q(X_i=x|h)=0 for a
base-supported x, use the arbitrary complete POVM from Section 2; the
mass of those cases is already included in (11). For a uniform
teleportation-success event p_X=D^(-2), Q=mu_X^m and D_ref=0.

5. Why the locality condition matters
------------------------------------
The exact factorization relies on two independent facts: the selected
Alice assemblage is normalized in (8), and the Bob suffix questions have
their unselected product law after conditioning on E. An event that also
tests Bob's fresh input or outcome generally violates the second fact.
Then the suffix average can depend on x or on a, and there need not be a
single y-only POVM. Replacing it by a trace-decreasing postselection map
would reintroduce the M1 gate rather than solve it. The result is
therefore specific to Alice-local selection (including the local
teleportation Bell outcome), not an arbitrary two-sided tested event.

The new HJW resource is a legal comparison/replacement resource for the
single-copy value; it need not be the original shared state. Its purpose
is to show that, under the hypotheses above, an actual all-input local
strategy exists with the stated loss. No uniform dimension, sampling,
or general selection theorem beyond these hypotheses is claimed.
