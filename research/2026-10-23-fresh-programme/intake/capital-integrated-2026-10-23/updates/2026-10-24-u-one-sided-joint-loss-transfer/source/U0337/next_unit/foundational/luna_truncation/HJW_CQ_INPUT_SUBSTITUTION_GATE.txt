CQ INPUT SUBSTITUTION BY HJW: OPERATION, LOSS, AND THE FRESH-Y GATE
2026-10-24. Bounded construction check for passive_lag.

Scope and conclusion
--------------------
For a normalized one-sided assemblage rho_(a|x) on Bob's whole accessible
system, average Holevo information

  tau = sum_x mu(x) D(rho_x || rhobar),
  rho_x=sum_a rho_(a|x), rhobar=sum_x mu(x)rho_x,

does support an actual fixed-resource local input-substitution strategy,
with average losing mass at most

  ell' <= ell + 2 sqrt(ell tau) + tau.                       (1)

But this reproduces the requested fresh-y table only under the operator
factorization stated in Section 2. Small marginal CQ information, or a
conditional record quantity I(X:R|K), does not itself supply that gate.
The construction never gives Bob a copy of x or exposes a dephasing
purification: x is used only by Alice's local POVM; the shared state is a
purification of rhobar fixed before inputs.

1. Normalized HJW input substitution with exact supports
--------------------------------------------------------
Assume finite-dimensional Bob space, mu(x)>0 on the input support, and

  rho_(a|x)>=0,  sum_a rho_(a|x)=rho_x,  Tr rho_x=1.

Then supp(rho_x) is contained in supp(rhobar). Let P_x be supp(rho_x) and
define, with inverse only on P_x,

  M^0_(a|x)=rho_x^(-1/2) rho_(a|x) rho_x^(-1/2).

Since 0<=rho_(a|x)<=rho_x, these are effects summing to P_x. Complete a
POVM by adding I-P_x to one default answer a0:

  Mbar_(a|x)=M^0_(a|x)+1{a=a0}(I-P_x).                       (2)

Fix an orthonormal basis and the canonical purification

  |Omega_rho> = sum_i |i>_A tensor sqrt(rho)|i>_B.

The transpose orientation is
  Tr_A[(Mbar_(a|x)^T tensor I)|Omega_(rho_x)><Omega_(rho_x)|]
    = rho_(a|x).

For each x, choose an Alice-side unitary U_x (extend a support isometry to
a unitary after enlarging the purifying space if necessary) so that

  |psi_x>=(U_x tensor I)|Omega_(rho_x)>,
  |<Omega_rhobar|psi_x>|=F(rho_x,rhobar),

where F=||sqrt(rho_x)sqrt(rhobar)||_1 is root fidelity. Use the actual
Alice POVM

  E_(a|x)=U_x Mbar_(a|x)^T U_x^dagger.                       (3)

It is a complete POVM for every x. On the comparison purification |psi_x>,
the same E_(a|x) produces exactly rho_(a|x), including zero default mass
because sqrt(rho_x)(I-P_x)=0. On the fixed resource |Omega_rhobar>, the
default term in (2) is retained and can have positive mass. It is not
dropped; its payoff is included in (1).

All physical inputs to (3) are x and fixed, precomputed matrices. The
state |psi_x> is only an analytic comparison vector; the players do not
prepare it. U_x is absorbed in Alice's x-only POVM. No actual opposite
input, dephasing-copy register, or input-dependent shared state is used.

2. Exact Bob fresh-y requirement and payoff bound
-------------------------------------------------
Suppose the original fresh-y table has the operator factorization

  p(a,b|x,y)=Tr[N_y^b Lambda_y(rho_(a|x))],                  (4)

where {N_y^b}_b is a POVM and each Lambda_y is a positive trace-preserving
linear map on Bob's whole assemblage space, the same map for every x,a.
Complete positivity is unnecessary. The trace adjoint Lambda_y^* is
positive and unital, so

  B_y^b=Lambda_y^*(N_y^b)

is a valid POVM. Sharing |Omega_rhobar>, Alice's effects (3), and Bob's
effects B_y^b gives a legal all-input local strategy. On |psi_x>, (4) is
reproduced exactly. The use of a positive map is solely an effect pullback;
no non-CP map is executed on an entangled system.

For a loss predicate let

  L_xy=sum_(a,b:loss) E_(a|x) tensor B_y^b,

where E_(a|x) is the actual Alice POVM effect from (3); thus
0<=L_xy<=I. Write

  alpha_xy=<psi_x|L_xy|psi_x>,
  alpha'_xy=<Omega_rhobar|L_xy|Omega_rhobar>,
  d_x^2=||psi_x-Omega_rhobar||^2=2(1-F(rho_x,rhobar)).

The standard relative-entropy/fidelity inequality gives
  D(rho_x||rhobar)>=-2 ln F>=2(1-F)=d_x^2.                (5)
Thus E_x d_x^2<=tau. Since ||L_xy^(1/2)||<=1,

  sqrt(alpha'_xy)<=sqrt(alpha_xy)+d_x,
  alpha'_xy<=alpha_xy+2 sqrt(alpha_xy d_x^2)+d_x^2.

For any actual joint input law with x-marginal mu, averaging and applying
Cauchy-Schwarz proves (1). This is payoff-aware: when the target loss ell
is small, the cross term is 2sqrt(ell tau). If logarithms in D use base 2,
replace tau on the right by (ln 2)tau.

Equation (4) is the exact extra compatibility gate for this sufficient
operation. It is stronger than having the right total state, accepted
effect, or no-signaling marginals. If Bob instead has outcome maps
Lambda_y^b, a corresponding sufficient variant is a common y-only
positive instrument with sum_b Lambda_y^b trace-preserving; its adjoints
then form a POVM. Dependence on x or Alice's a is not allowed unless an
additional shared/local representation removes that dependence.

If Lambda_y is only positive trace-decreasing, then
sum_b Lambda_y^*(N_y^b)=Lambda_y^*(I)=R_y<=I. Completing with I-R_y is a
valid abort outcome, but the original accepted table remains only a
sub-POVM and the abort mass must be charged as loss. A common support
normalization can sometimes repair this (for example a single common
R_y=R with a support Doob normalization); y-dependent or x-dependent
success operators do not automatically give an all-input TP factorization.
The R/H map in POSITIVE_ASSEMBLAGE_REBUILD.txt has R_y indexed by both y
and the retained Alice block, so this gate cannot be assumed there.

3. Trace bookkeeping for an accepted substate
---------------------------------------------
If the accepted assemblage is subnormalized, write

  rho_(a|x)>=0, rho_x=sum_a rho_(a|x), s_x=Tr rho_x<=1,
  hatrho_(a|x)=rho_(a|x)/s_x, hatrho_x=rho_x/s_x.

Assume s_x>0 on the relevant support and that accepted mass is independent
of Bob's y. Let s=sum_x mu_X(x)s_x and
  nu(x)=mu_X(x)s_x/s,
  hatrhobar=sum_x nu(x)hatrho_x=(sum_x mu_X(x)rho_x)/s.
The relevant conditional information is

  tau_acc=sum_x nu(x)D(hatrho_x||hatrhobar),                 (6)

not an unspecified relative entropy between subnormalized matrices. Apply
Sections 1-2 to the normalized hats, and have Alice flip a local success
coin of probability s_x before using the x-POVM. On the target comparison
state this reproduces rho_(a|x) as a substate; on the common resource the
same coin preserves total accepted mass s. Conditional on acceptance, x
has law nu and the loss obeys

  ell'_acc<=ell_acc+2sqrt(ell_acc tau_acc)+tau_acc.           (7)

If abort counts as a loss in the unconditioned game, the safe bound is
  ell'_uncond <= (1-s)+s ell'_acc.
The success coin is local because s_x depends only on Alice's input. If
actual acceptance probability varies with both x and y, this operation
does not preserve the accepted question law: no Alice-only coin can
implement s_xy. That dependence must be handled by the Bob instrument
factorization, or charged as an explicit residual/abort term.

Zero-trace x branches are omitted from nu and can be assigned any complete
Alice POVM after a zero-probability abort. If s_x is a common constant, the
accepted and original x laws coincide. Otherwise an entropy average under
mu cannot silently replace the needed average under nu.

4. What conditional opposite-record CQ information does, and does not, buy
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
For the direct ensemble in Section 1,
  sum_x mu(x)D(rho_x||rhobar)=I(X:B),
where B is the ENTIRE Bob system used in (4). This is sufficient and uses
no copied x register. In contrast, the allocation term

  I_q(Z:R|K,H,W)

is conditional information about a checked record Z and discarded
reference R given retained K on the accepted branch. It is not automatically
the Holevo quantity I(X:B) for the fresh-input ensemble in (4). In
particular,

  I(X:RK)=I(X:K)+I(X:R|K).

HJW on the full Bob system RK needs control of its full ensemble divergence;
the conditional term alone does not control I(X:K). If one discards R and
keeps only K, the target fine table must separately factor through K by a
common positive-TP y-only map. If K is a quantum Bob-local system, one also
cannot condition Alice's POVM on a value of K. A conditional HJW is
legitimate only with an actual local/shared classical control and an
all-input branchwise factorization; a dephasing purification or reference
copy is not a free shared flag.

Consequently the source's ordered mark allocation must first identify the
relevant live-coordinate ensemble and its registers. The checked-record
calculation gives a total bound of the form

  sum_i b_(A,i)<=log(1/p)+I_q(Z:R_J|K_A,H,W)

and the Bob analogue. It does not by itself assert that each b_i equals
the tau in (6), nor that the total record term is small. Only after the
source allocation and a register-matched definition of rho_(a|x) can one
divide a proved total budget by m. The direct HJW lemma is a conditional
operation, not a new entropy allocation theorem.

5. Finite PR obstruction to omitting the factorization gate
-----------------------------------------------------------
Let x,a be bits and Bob's assemblage space a qubit. Set

  rho_(a|x)=1/2 |a><a|,    rho_x=I/2,    rhobar=I/2.

This is a genuine one-sided quantum assemblage (Alice can measure one
half of a Bell pair in the computational basis), and its ensemble Holevo
information is exactly tau=0. Nevertheless the proposed fresh-y PR table

  p(a,b|x,y)=1/2 1{a xor b=xy}                              (8)

cannot factor through any common y-only map and POVM. At y=1, the same
state rho_(0|0)=rho_(0|1)=|0><0| would have to give b=0 with probability
one for x=0 and b=1 with probability one for x=1. A single positive-TP
Lambda_1 and POVM N_1 cannot assign two different outcome distributions
to the same input operator. This pins failure at the operator gate, not at
the HJW normalization or entropy estimate.

The n=2 raw R/H PR face in ACCEPTANCE_EFFECT_LOSES_INSTRUMENT.txt realizes
this kind of missing compatibility after postselection, with event
probability p=1/2 and one live coordinate m=1. Its log2(1/p)/m cost is one
bit, so it falsifies an inference from constant accepted effect or
one-sided marginals, but not an asymptotic O(sqrt(log(1/p)/m)) conclusion.
Repeating independent PR postselections m times makes p=2^-m and keeps the
per-live-coordinate event cost one bit. Hiding only one bad coordinate
among m would dilute a uniform-coordinate defect by 1/m; it is not a
constant-defect m-scale counterexample.

6. Finite obstruction to pulling compressed domination back globally
--------------------------------------------------------------------
This tests the stronger proposed step from posterior-state clipping to
global Loewner domination of Bob's accepted row effects. Let 0<epsilon<1,
and choose four pure qubit states |v_i> with Bloch vectors

  n_1=(1,0,0), n_2=(0,1,0),
  n_3=(sqrt(1-epsilon^2),0,epsilon),
  n_4=(0,sqrt(1-epsilon^2),-epsilon).

Their four rank-one projectors F_i=|v_i><v_i| are informationally
complete: the four Bloch vectors are affinely independent for every
epsilon>0. Alice's input x=i POVM is {F_i,I-F_i}; Bob's y=0,1 POVMs have
accepted effects R_0=P_+=(I+Z)/2 and R_1=P_-=(I-Z)/2, respectively,
with complementary rejection effects. Take the shared Bell state, whose
matrix is sigma=I/sqrt(2), and accept only the (F_i,R_y) row.

The Alice-side postevent steering substate is exactly

  sigma_y^i=sqrt(F_i) sigma R_y^T sigma sqrt(F_i)
          =(1+(-1)^y n_(i,z))/4 F_i,
  barsigma^i=(sigma_0^i+sigma_1^i)/2=F_i/4.               (9)

Therefore for every actual i,y,

  sigma_y^i <= (1+epsilon) barsigma^i.                     (10)

After normalizing the accepted Alice state, it is F_i for both y, so the
postevent quantum state carries zero Y information. The accepted mass is
input-dependent, with probabilities (1+(-1)^y n_(i,z))/4; the trace-aware
binary Y-versus-acceptance information is O(epsilon^2), not zero. Thus this
fixture does not hide trace variation.

For fixed i=3 or 4 and uniform y, let A be the acceptance bit. Then
P(A=1|y)=q+(-1)^y delta up to swapping the y labels, with q=1/4 and
delta=epsilon/4. Its exact mutual information is

  I(Y;A|x=i)=h(q)-[h(q+delta)+h(q-delta)]/2
            =epsilon^2/(6 ln 2)+O(epsilon^4),

where h is binary entropy in bits. For i=1,2 it is zero. If i is also
uniform, average I(Y;A|X) is half the displayed value. This distinguishes
equality of the normalized postevent states from the small, nonzero
information in the success flag.

Nevertheless the averaged postevent substate is the compression

  barsigma^i=sqrt(F_i) sigma Rbar^T sigma sqrt(F_i),
  Rbar=(R_0+R_1)/2=I/2,

while the normalized global row is

  Rbar^(-1/2) R_0 Rbar^(-1/2)=2P_+.

Its largest eigenvalue is 2, so R_0 is not <=(1+epsilon)Rbar for any
epsilon<1. More strongly, suppose an alternative effect R'_0 matched all
four accepted row probabilities. Then
  <v_i|R'_0|v_i>=<v_i|P_+|v_i> for i=1,...,4.
Informational completeness forces R'_0=P_+. Hence no positive-operator-
system extension that preserves the exact observed rows can also satisfy
R'_0<=(1+epsilon)Rbar. The same applies to y=1. The local domination (10)
only tests the quadratic forms on each actual range; it does not control
the missing cross-coherences needed for a global Doob normalization.

This is an exact obstruction to that pullback step, not to approximate
payoff-aware replacement: using Rbar in place of R_y changes each observed
accepted probability by at most epsilon/4 in this family. Such O(epsilon)
error is compatible with a desired O(sqrt(tau)) estimate when the
trace-aware information is O(epsilon^2). A robust approximate extension
may still work; (9)-(10) show it cannot be obtained by claiming the global
Loewner inequality from the compressed ones alone.

Status
------
The HJW input-substitution bound (1), support convention, Uhlmann gauge,
substate bookkeeping, and exact positive-TP gate are established under
their stated hypotheses. Small direct Bob-system Holevo information is
sufficient. Small conditional opposite-record information alone has not
been converted into this direct ensemble or the common-y factorization.
No inference from the source E-budget to tau is claimed without the
register-matched mark allocation.
