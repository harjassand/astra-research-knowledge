This note connects the existing HJW/positive-effect operation to an EXACT

1. A single all-input positive factorization
-------------------------------------------
Work first in one finite Bob input Hilbert space H. Suppose target positive
fine assemblages have the entire input-family factorization

  sigma_(a|x,y)=Lambda_y(tau_(a|x)),
  sum_a tau_(a|x)=rho_x, Tr rho_x=1.                         (M1)

Each Lambda_y must be a POSITIVE TRACE-PRESERVING linear map on the SAME
input matrix algebra, independent of x,a. The maps need not be CP. Bob's
target output POVM is {N_y^b}; then the true final Bob POVM is
  B_y^b=Lambda_y^*(N_y^b).                                  (M2)
These effects are positive and sum to I. They are used directly; no
positive-but-non-CP map is applied to an entangled subsystem.

Let Q_XY be the original conditional question law for the selected live
round, and rho_bar=sum_x Q_X(x)rho_x. On supp rho_x set
  C_x^a=rho_x^(-1/2) tau_(a|x) rho_x^(-1/2),
and complete their sum to I by adding I-supp rho_x to one default answer.
Call the completed POVM Cbar_x^a. The Douglas bounds 0<=tau_a<=rho_x make
these bounded effects, irrespective of small nonzero eigenvalues.

Prepare ONE input-independent canonical purification
  |Omega_rhobar>=sum_j |j>_A tensor sqrt(rho_bar)|j>_B,
and use Alice's effects (Cbar_x^a)^T and Bob's effects (M2). Each operation
uses only its own fresh input. On the comparison vector |Omega_rhox>, the
same pair of POVMs reproduces the target table
  Tr[N_y^b sigma_(a|x,y)]
exactly. The support-default term vanishes on that comparison vector and
is retained in the real strategy. This is one fixed resource and one
family of genuine POVMs, with no fresh opposite-question oracle.

2. The canonical information bound, without Uhlmann
---------------------------------------------------
For density operators rho,sigma,

  ||sqrt(rho)-sqrt(sigma)||_HS^2 <= D(rho||sigma).            (M3)

A direct proof makes its reference explicit. On Hilbert-Schmidt space
use the positive relative modular operator Delta=L_sigma R_rho^(-1) and
the unit vector sqrt(rho), with the inverse restricted to supp rho. Jensen
for log and the spectral measure of Delta^(1/2) gives
  -D(rho||sigma)/2
    <=log <sqrt(rho),Delta^(1/2)sqrt(rho)>
     =log Tr[sqrt(rho)sqrt(sigma)].
Since -log t>=1-t, this bounds D below by twice one minus the affinity,
which is exactly the left side of (M3). Full-rank regularization handles
zero eigenvalues; if D is infinite the inequality is vacuous. No quantum
relative entropy of a pure postselected resource appears.

Define the ACTUAL candidate state family
  Xi_XB=sum_x Q_X(x)|x><x| tensor rho_x.
Its mutual information is
  I_Xi(X:B)=sum_x Q_X(x)D(rho_x||rho_bar).
Thus the mean squared distance of the canonical comparison vectors obeys

  H=sum_x Q_X(x)||sqrt(rho_x)-sqrt(rho_bar)||_HS^2
       <=I_Xi(X:B).                                        (M4)

Let alpha be the target conditional losing probability of any game payoff.
Both target and repaired probabilities use the SAME positive losing effect
L_xy on their respective comparison vectors, with 0<=L_xy<=I. Triangle
inequality for its square root and Cauchy after averaging give

  loss'_Q <= alpha+2sqrt(alpha H)+H
          <= alpha+2sqrt(alpha I_Xi)+I_Xi.                  (M5)

Under the original base question law mu, add at most dTV(Q,mu). This is a
real loss-aware O(sqrt(alpha tau)+tau) operation if this particular Xi has
information <=tau. There is no entropy-to-TV-to-purifier double loss and
no inaccessible Uhlmann unitary.

3. What original-event allocation DOES NOT automatically provide
----------------------------------------------------------------
The selected opposite LOCAL record B_local in
SELECTION_CQ_ALLOCATION_AND_PURIFIER_GAP.txt need not be the B in Xi.
In the natural original accepted instrument, Alice's old coarse label
alpha enters Bob's acceptance rule. Encoding it as a Bob input block gives
the CP map

  [T_y(S)]_gamma=sum_alpha theta(alpha,gamma)
                         sqrt(H_y^gamma) S_alpha sqrt(H_y^gamma),
  [T_y^*(I)]_alpha=R_y^alpha
                  =sum_gamma theta(alpha,gamma)H_y^gamma.   (M6)

This is trace-DECREASING. The extra alpha block is an opposite private
record, not part of the actual Bob local record whose information was
budgeted by the original event. Its conditional information can be large
even when the actual Bob record is independent of fresh Alice input.
Adding that tag silently invalidates the proposed charge identification.

If R_y is one common R and the resulting Doob-normalized input family
really has the normalized totals rho_x in (M1), the map
  S -> T_y(R^(-1/2) S R^(-1/2))
is positive TP on supp R and the operation works. But neither common R
nor a small information cost for the required tagged rho_x follows from
low one-sided local-record information alone. A PR assemblage already
shows that marginal consistency is insufficient for a positive-TP
factorization. This observation is a boundary, not a new source theorem.

The active operator attempt is more precise than invoking substate:
clip the state-side accepted posterior marginals at a common reference,
then try to pull that dominance back to the R_y effects ONLY on the actual
steering operator system, with a positive extension giving true Bob POVMs.
For fixed x,alpha the state-side congruence only tests the compression
  sqrt(F_x^alpha) sigma (R_y^alpha)^T sigma sqrt(F_x^alpha).
Domination in every such input-dependent compression need not imply one
global operator inequality across their span. On the other hand, differences
invisible to every relevant fine effect may legitimately be forgotten.
The question is whether that payoff-restricted order information admits
one positive extension for the whole input family. No such extension is
assumed here, and no small error is obtained from the selection budget
until that physical operator problem is resolved.
