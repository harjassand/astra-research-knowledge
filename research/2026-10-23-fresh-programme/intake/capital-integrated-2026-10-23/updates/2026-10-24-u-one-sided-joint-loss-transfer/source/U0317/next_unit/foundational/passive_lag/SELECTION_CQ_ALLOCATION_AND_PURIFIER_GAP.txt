SELECTION FROM THE ORIGINAL MEASURED EVENT: WHAT IS ACTUALLY BUDGETED
2026-10-24. Active selection attack. General payoff reconstruction OPEN.

Opening and disposition
------------------------
The selected attempt is: allocate the ORIGINAL compatible measured-event
relative entropy to opposite LOCAL records, then rebuild a selected input
using an actual local assemblage/recovery operation. The allocation is
valid and answer-size free. Its output is a mixed/cq object, not a bound
on a coherent local purifier. A new exact half-exposure calculation below
separates those two objects at the desired m-scale: log(1/p)/m tends to
zero, most selected accessible-cq charges tend to zero, but the corresponding
coherent-lift charges stay bounded below.

This falsifies that PARTICULAR cq-to-coherent-local-transition upgrade.
It does NOT falsify mixed positive-TP reconstruction, a payoff-aware
forgetting operation, or the existence of another useful selected history.
The counter's live payoff can be trivial, and forgetting its coherence is
allowed. That restriction is part of the result, not an omitted hypothesis.

1. Original compatible measured state
--------------------------------------
Fix the tested question string c before any remaining fresh inputs. Let
m>=1 live question pairs be independently distributed as mu. Run the
original arbitrary full-string local instruments, retaining their classical
answer records and any remaining local quantum systems. Denote the fully
DEPHASED measured cq state by Omega. Let E be the classical tested-success
event, compatible with those records, and p=Pr(E)>0. Then

  D(Omega_E || Omega)=log(1/p).                              (S1)

This is just restriction to classical blocks. No statement (S1) is made
about a different coherent Lueders resource: its relative entropy against
an original pure state may be infinite.

Trace out ALL Alice outcomes and her remaining quantum system. Write B^
for Bob's own local measured record and remaining quantum system, excluding
his classical Y^m question register. It must not contain an added copy of
Alice's checked answers or an inaccessible purifying environment. Before
conditioning on E, no signaling gives the exact reduced reference

  Omega_(X^m Y^m B^)=mu_XY^tensor m sigma_(B^|Y^m).           (S2)

The state sigma may depend on Bob's entire own question string and on c.
This remains true for arbitrary joint POVMs across rounds: after Alice's
outcomes are discarded, her trace-preserving instrument cannot change
Bob's reduced state. Data processing in (S1) gives relative entropy <=log1/p
to the reference (S2).

2. Free questions: a genuine half-exposure allocation
------------------------------------------------------
If mu=mu_X tensor mu_Y, (S2) is a product of the entire X^m register and
Bob's local record B=Y^m B^. All information below is evaluated in the
SELECTED state Omega_E. Decomposing relative entropy against that original
product reference gives

  I(X^m:B) <= log(1/p).                                     (S3)

The nonnegative selected-vs-original marginal relative entropies have
only been dropped. For
  R_i=(X_<i,Y_>i),   B_i=Y_<=i B^,
the exact quantum/classical chain rules and nonnegative conditional
mutual information give

  sum_i I(X_i:B_i |R_i,E) <= I(X^m:Y^m B^ |E)
                           <=log(1/p).                    (S4)

Indeed split each summand into its Y_<=i contribution and its B^ contribution.
The latter sum is I(X^m:B^|Y^m,E). The former equals
  I(X^m:Y^m|E)-sum_i I(X_i:Y_>i|X_<i,E)
and is at most I(X^m:Y^m|E). Bob's quantum subsystem need not commute with
itself across instrument choices; only the actual single measured state
is used in these entropy identities. Alice has the symmetric allocation.

Consequently a uniform offline index has average charge <=log(1/p)/m.
This is a real selected-record statement. It does not assert R_i is
actually public, or that a channel conditioned on R_i can be implemented
without the source's local guessed-history/mismatch machinery.

3. Arbitrary joint within-round mu: the exact baseline
-------------------------------------------------------
Write rho for the reduced selected state in (S2), Q for its input law,
and D_ref=D(Q_XY||mu^tensor m). Direct decomposition gives

  D(rho||mu^tensor m sigma_(B^|Y^m))
   = D_ref + I_rho(X^m:B^|Y^m)
     +sum_y Q(y)D(rho_(B^|y)||sigma_(B^|y))
   <=log(1/p).                                             (S5)

Thus I(X^m:B^|Y^m)<=log1/p-D_ref. The support conventions are harmless
because the selected state is obtained from the same measured reference
and data processing has already bounded its relative entropy.

One cannot instead call all of I(X^m:Y^m B^) an event cost. Put
  ell_mu(x,y)=log[mu(x,y)/(mu_X(x)mu_Y(y))]
on the support of mu. Comparing with the product-marginal reference gives
the valid, but potentially large, half-exposure bound

  sum_i I(X_i:B_i|R_i,E)
    <=log1/p + E_Q sum_i ell_mu(X_i,Y_i).                    (S6)

The selected expectation on the right is not generally m I_mu(X:Y).
For a one-pair exact example take mu(00)=mu(11)=1/10,
mu(01)=mu(10)=2/5, and E={X!=Y}. Then p=4/5 and selected I(X:Y)=log2.
Here E_Q ell_mu=log(8/5), so (S6) is tight, whereas replacing that selected
expectation by I_mu would give a false smaller bound. This is why a
general-mu local interface needs the source's conditional comparison forms,
not a free-input independence assumption hidden in notation.

4. An exact m-scale accessible-record / purifier separation
-----------------------------------------------------------
Let m>=2. Draw independent uniform bit strings X,Y in {0,1}^m and take
the compatible classical event
  E={X dot Y=0 modulo2},   p=(1+2^-m)/2.                    (S7)
It can be a legal tested event: each player copies its own future question
string into an old tested answer, and the fixed tested predicate checks
their inner product. No player obtains the other's fresh question. The
base alphabet/interface grows with m; no efficient or positive-gap-game
claim is attached to this example. It can have value-one live behavior.
The original measured state is classical and its D_ref is log1/p exactly.

Use the SAME half-exposure R_i=(X_<i,Y_>i). Put a=X_<i, b=Y_>i. Under the
uniform pre-event law each r=(a,b) has probability 2^-(m-1), and
  Pr(E|r)=1/2 except when a=b=0, where it is 3/4.             (S8)

The actual accessible-cq charge is
  c_i=I_Q(X_i:Y_<=i |X_<i,Y_>i).
Let h_AND=log3-(4/3)log2, the mutual information of the uniform law on
the three bit pairs 00,01,10. Exact conditioning yields

  c_i = { (log2)/2 (1-2^-(i-1))2^-(m-i)
                   +3*2^-m h_AND } / (1+2^-m).            (S9)

For b!=0, the unexposed future X coordinates absorb the parity condition,
so this conditional mutual information is zero. For b=0,a!=0, the
relation a dot Y_<i=X_i Y_i reveals X_i only when Y_i=1, giving (log2)/2.
For a=b=0 the remaining law is the three-point AND-zero law. Their
selected branch probabilities give (S9). In particular

  sum_i c_i = { log2[1-(m+1)2^-m]
                         +3m2^-m h_AND }/(1+2^-m)=O(1).    (S10)

Now make the DIFFERENT, coherent analysis lift that purifies all the
remaining dephased label registers. After fixing r, let u_r be the product
of uniform superpositions on the unexposed label bits. Let v_r be obtained
by CZ on the live pair (X_i,Y_i), Z phases indexed by b on Alice's other
unexposed bits, and Z phases indexed by a on Bob's other unexposed bits.
The normalized coherently accepted vector is
  Omega_r=(u_r+v_r)/sqrt(2+delta_(a=0,b=0)).                 (S11)

If a!=0 and b!=0, the two outside branch vectors are orthogonal on BOTH
parties. Trace Alice's other unexposed bits and dephase X_i itself, but
retain Bob's full coherent lift. Bob has an orthogonal outside branch
flag. In its u branch (probability1/2), his live qubit is independent of
X_i. In its v branch (probability1/2), it is |+> or |-> according to X_i,
which are perfectly distinguishable. The branch flag is independent of
X_i, which is uniform. Therefore the CLASSICAL-input coherent-Bob charge
is exactly
  I(X_i:B_coherent |r,E)=(log2)/2                          (S12)
on every such r. Pinching Bob back to the actual classical Y labels makes
both outside flag states and both |+>,|-> states identical diagonally,
and the accessible charge on these r is exactly zero.
Bob can distinguish the branch with the FIXED projector
  I-|+><+|^tensor(i-1)
on his outside prefix registers. It kills u and accepts v for every
nonzero a, so this measurement does not require Bob to learn Alice's a.
Nevertheless R_i remains a conditional analysis label, not an actually
shared transcript in the protocol.

The selected weight of a!=0,b!=0 is
  (1-2^-(i-1))(1-2^-(m-i))/(1+2^-m).
Hence the sum of coherent-lift charges is at least

  [(log2)/2]/(1+2^-m) * [m-4+(m+2)2^(1-m)] =Omega(m).      (S13)

For m-O(log m) interior indices the accessible charge in (S9) is O(1/m)
or smaller while the coherent charge stays near (log2)/2. Thus this is
a selection-scale separation, not a single bad coordinate diluted by
padding. It still concerns the choice of purification interface, not
payoff-aware repair.

5. What the failed upgrade would have asserted
-----------------------------------------------
A cq information selector cannot silently conclude that the same selected
coordinate has a low-information Bob purifier on which Alice can use a
local transition/Uhlmann construction. Equations (S9)-(S13) refute that
implication in an actual low-log1/p-over-m regime.

The coherent lift is an analysis resource; it is not an incoming coherent
question oracle in the original classical protocol. Giving inaccessible
dephasing/purification records to a player enlarges that player's algebra.
Conversely, leaving them out can prevent a proposed Uhlmann unitary from
being local to the actual player. A recovery proof must specify which of
these two interfaces it uses.

The counter does NOT rule out discarding these phases and constructing
new local fine POVMs. Its ordinary live payoff need not depend on any
phase, and the source's history-pinching commutant property allows such
forgetting. It therefore does NOT prove that every useful low-charge
history has large SMOOTH PAYOFF-REPAIR cost, or that no good selected
coordinate exists. That remains the central open gate.

The actual mixed rebuilding route remains: find one input-independent
shared reference assemblage and positive trace-preserving Bob effect
pullbacks, or another legal local operation, that reproduces the relevant
accepted live payoff. The natural accepted instrument is trace-decreasing
and depends on omitted opposite history labels; (S4) alone does not make
its Doob normalization independent of fresh inputs. This condition must
be constructed or falsified using the payoff rather than supplied from
low one-sided cq marginals.
