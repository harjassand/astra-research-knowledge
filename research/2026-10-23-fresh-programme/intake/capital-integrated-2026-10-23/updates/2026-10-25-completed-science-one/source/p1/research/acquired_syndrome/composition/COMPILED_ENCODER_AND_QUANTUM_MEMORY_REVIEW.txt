1. Independent complete transmitted-error quotient
==================================================
I derived the binary linear encoder matrix A from its computational-basis
action and computed its inverse as a permutation of all 128 binary words.
For any physical Pauli X^x Z^z inserted between encoder and inverse decoder,
the decoded Pauli is
  x'=A^-1 x, z'=A^T z.
The exposed logical X/Z bits are its unknown-input output coordinates.
Z readout on zero-prepared auxiliaries returns the corresponding x' bits;
X readout on plus-prepared auxiliaries returns the corresponding z' bits.

This independent matrix calculation agrees exactly with the event propagator
for ALL 4^7 physical transmitted Paulis in both circuits. Each of the 256
eight-bit signatures has 64 physical Pauli preimages, consistent with six
unretained stabilizer degrees of freedom. The minimum nontrivial logical
weight with zero syndrome is three for both circuits.

For independent uniform Pauli transmission noise, I formed an exact joint
signature law by integer weight enumeration, without a Walsh transform. All
256 entries agree with the implementation's rational Walsh convolution at
p=1/100, with all other errors zero. Both circuits have the same standard
decoded error at this noiseless-local-operation control:
  56177396557/35595703125000.
The independent check comprises 33,301 exact rational/integer assertions,
including the physical-memory checks below. No logical/circuit error was found
in the implementations reviewed at the recorded hashes.

2. Precision and performance interpretation
===========================================
The exact path in signature_law is exact when supplied Fraction values, as in
its exact check. Converting a Python float to Fraction preserves that float's
binary value, not an intended decimal literal. Default floating calculations
and their small negative-roundoff clipping are numerical method controls,
not outward certificates. The outer physical certificate must retain its
separate rational/outward arithmetic.

The displayed first-order gate, preparation and readout coefficients are the
multivariate expansion at ALL noise parameters zero. They are not derivatives
at a fixed nonzero weak-preparation/readout error or transmission error.

The nine-CNOT and eleven-CNOT circuits have different fault propagation and
decoder ambiguity despite representing equivalent ideal codes. The shorter
one has more dangerous preparation/readout single faults under the standard
decoder; a gate-count comparison alone does not rank physical performance.
The quantities p and q denote total nonidentity Pauli probabilities in the
declared mixtures (p/3 or q/15 per nonidentity Pauli), not an alternative
depolarizing-strength convention.

The classical maximum-likelihood comparator must be given the same final
six-bit context and, if retained, the same fine weak words. Its best final
Pauli is the maximizer within each context. A known-model optimum is a
validation upper benchmark, not model information actually acquired by the
observer. Acquired tagged weak scores can learn a policy without those
hidden parameters, under the exact scopes in the sibling acquisition source.

3. A genuinely physical noncommuting memory model
================================================
Let the traveling carrier be S and the unread mode M a qubit. Define
  A0=Pz0+c Pz1, A1=-i s Pz1,
  B0=Px0+c Px1, B1=-i s Px1,
where c,s are real and c^2+s^2=1. Let W be a unitary on M. The joint unitary
  U=[I_S tensor A0+X_S tensor A1]
    [I_S tensor B0+Z_S tensor B1](I_S tensor W)
is a product of two controlled rotations and memory evolution.

Apply ACTUAL independent private input/output Pauli frames to each traveling
carrier and retire their keys. Its error instrument on M has four Kraus maps
  K_ab=A_a B_b W,
with carrier error X^a Z^b. The phase needed to express XZ as Hermitian Y is
an overall phase of that Kraus operator and has no CP-map consequence.

Trace preservation follows without a hidden stochastic assumption:
  sum_ab K_ab^dagger K_ab
    =W^dagger sum_b B_b^dagger[sum_a A_a^dagger A_a]B_b W=I.

For the two effects associated with X and Z carrier errors,
  F10=s^2 W^dagger B0 Pz1 B0 W,
  F01=[s^2(1+c^2)/2] W^dagger Px1 W.
Their commutator has operator norm
  ||[F10,F01]||=s^4 |c|(1+c^2)/4,
which is strictly positive for 0<|c|<1. For real planar W the commutator itself
is that positive coefficient times [[0,1],[-1,0]]. Three rational c,s controls
were checked exactly, including c=399/401,s=40/401, whose coefficient is
  81715710720000/1667287938243362801.

Adding independent depolarizing carrier faults with nonidentity probability p
mixes the effects to
  F'_s=(1-4p/3)F_s+(p/3)I.
The commutator is multiplied by (1-4p/3)^2 and remains nonzero except at the
fully depolarizing point or degenerate coupling limits. Local raw CNOT Pauli
faults can be added independently as a controlled performance model, with
their error locations and probabilities explicitly stated.

4. What a sampled quantum trajectory does and does not establish
===============================================================
Sampling the unreceived Kraus label (a,b) using Tr K_ab rho_M K_ab^dagger and
updating the simulator's M state is a valid GENERATIVE unravelling of this
physically framed quantum channel. Across all seven carriers and subsequent
macros, M can continue without a reset. The maps need not commute, and the
updated density matrix is a physical quantum-mode state in the unravelling.
This is not a supplied classical model label or an observed error syndrome.

The sampled state is conditioned on labels unavailable to the observer. It is
NOT automatically the density matrix conditioned only on the actually received
syndrome and outer weak records. A record-conditioned filter must average the
unreceived branches consistently. Hidden-path risk and record-conditioned
predictable risk must be labeled separately in validation. The model-independent
outer accepted-error theorem is uniform in M and does not depend on acquiring
either of these hidden-state descriptions.

If the validation starts the input/reference independently of M and all
framed branches factor as carrier Pauli tensor K_ab, it can propagate the
quantum M and classical Pauli signatures separately along each sampled branch.
The resulting record distribution is the correct unconditioned physical law.
The full theorem permits more general input-memory correlations, but that
does not mean this factorized simulation has tested all of them.

The inner carrier frames are real extra operations: paired local Pauli pulses,
private shared classical settings, matched timing and key retirement at each
of the seven transmissions. Merely relabeling a coherent U as Pauli noise
without those operations would be a different physical model. If their keys
are retained as declared outputs, the above averaged Pauli simulation is not
the full flagged instrument. The outer key has the same separate restriction.

5. Fair comparison and physical boundary
=======================================
The two encoders transmit seven physical carriers for one decoded qubit; direct
uncoded transmission uses one. Equal logical-call counts are not equal carrier
counts, bandwidth or duration. A shared memory has seven updates per encoded
macro and one per direct call, so same-state instantaneous diagnostics must
not be presented as a separately executed same-history baseline. Resource-
matched schedules can be specified, but their full operations must be run or
analytically compared.

The same construction and settings are available to conventional stabilizer
simulation, density/process-tensor filtering and controllers. The achieved
representation saving is an exact task quotient in the stated Clifford/Pauli
model, plus an outer uniform certificate for actual unknown macro behavior.
It is not compression of an arbitrary fine quantum posterior. No hardware
operation or practical device-performance claim is made by these calculations.

6. Executed acquisition and transfer-map review
==============================================
The subsequent execution sources acquire_transmission.py, observe_transmission.py
and quantum_link_model.py were read in full. The record-only observer was run
independently and reproduced 65,536 training uses, 22 changed context repairs,
and 319 accepted payloads among 512 validation attempts. Its accepted-error
weight envelope is the exact rational 1/64. The sharper displayed value
0.01555775822794474 is not a conditional accepted error or a fine-record
posterior. The five simulated accepted payload faults are unreceived
validation-only variables, not inputs to the observer.

The generator samples unreceived macro error/syndrome labels before drawing
current outer axes and secret positions. This order is justified for this
SPECIFIC model: actual inner carrier frame retirement, Pauli local faults and
the ideal Clifford skeleton make each macro branch a logical Pauli tensor a
memory Kraus map, with auxiliary readout eigenbits independent of logical input.
The fixed final repair also cannot change the future M path. It would not be
valid to make this sampling reordering for an arbitrary untwirled nonlinear
input-dependent channel.

Actual outer weak pre/post word generation is also consistent. A Z-prepared
source has a fixed probabilistic Z eigenbit and unbiased X/Y eigenbits. For
paired same-axis weak probes around a Pauli branch, tracing the audited output
removes off-diagonal terms, so sampling that initial eigenbit gives the exact
joint word law. This is a generative identity, not an extra physical projective
probe of the unknown payload. Payload slots receive no outer weak probes.

The implementation supplies a full local Pauli mixing operation before each
finite weak preparation, performs feedback, and retires the resulting words
and dephasing keys. This is a declared actual mixed-state preparation step;
it makes the uniform eigenbit used to generate those retired fine words
legitimate. No pure ancilla is supplied. All six fine inner readout words and
the outer audit words remain in the received records. Strong alternatives may
use their full likelihood rather than only majority contexts.

quantum_macro_transfer_review.py independently constructs every one of the
256 memory CP signature maps by POSITIVE direct dynamic programming, then
compares all four matrix-unit inputs to the implementation's Fourier transfer
maps. Maximum floating discrepancy is about 2.0e-15. The minimum Choi eigenvalue
in this control is positive. It then explicitly enumerates all 64 error words
and three secret positions of a three-slot packet, agreeing with the derivative
recurrence to about 3.3e-16. These are numerical diagnostics backed by the
operator construction, not interval-arithmetic bounds. The exact reviewed
source hashes appear in the generated JSON.
