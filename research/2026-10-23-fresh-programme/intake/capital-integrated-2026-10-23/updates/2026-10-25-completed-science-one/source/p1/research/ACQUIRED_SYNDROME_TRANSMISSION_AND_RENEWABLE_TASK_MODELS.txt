13. Syndrome-only impossibility and fair use of prior methods
===========================================================
A nontrivial logical Pauli commutes with every stabilizer instrument. Coupling
it coherently to an unread physical mode leaves all fine stabilizer words
unchanged while changing a logical Bell state. This is a missing-information
problem that larger classical/tensor representations alone cannot solve.

Even a simple independent-noise family has the ambiguity. Steane bit-flip
syndromes are identical for p and1-p. Standard decoding gives
  p_L(p)=21p2-98p3+210p4-252p5+168p6-48p7,
  p_L(1-p)=1-p_L(p).
At p=.01 the errors are .00200407496752 and .99799592503248, with exactly the
same syndrome law. Identity-dominated noise is additional information.

Wagner et al.'s logical-noise learning theorem is a strong prior result. Its
stated model has independent Pauli components on allowed supports, each union
of two supports correctable, and P_gamma(I)>1/2. It does not assert
identifiability from syndrome data for unrestricted memory-bearing channels.
Our paired logical audits are an additional physical information interface,
paid for with sacrificial calls. They are not syndrome data relabeled as more
informative.

All conventional observers receive the same instruments, weak records, timing,
private settings assumptions, preparations and compiled circuit. Standard CSS
decoders, acquired Bayesian mixtures, density methods and tensor methods may
use the256-weight kernel and exact MAP rule. Fine/analog syndrome information
and adaptive Bayesian sensing are established ideas; no exclusivity to Astra
is asserted. A conventional process-tensor model may predict a richer set of
future interventions when its structure can be acquired. Our finite task
model does not establish that arbitrary process tensors have small bond
dimension, and we have not executed a competitive large tensor-learning study.

Quantum authentication/trap constructions already provide stronger integrity
scaling when their encoding and key assumptions are available. The1/n outer
bound is not a claimed optimal authentication rate. The contribution here is
the complete acquisition/weak-control boundary and explicit learned task law,
not inventing QEC, Pauli twirling, authentication or Bayesian decoding.

14. Adaptive observation: stop when further information cannot change much
=========================================================================
The fixed-length representation opens a further operation: the controller may
choose how long to keep measuring the terminal ancillas. A bounded adaptive
QND decision tree still obeys equation(1), with a branch likelihood formed from
only the probes actually selected and received. Stop/selection predicates are
known functions of that branch, common to all latent mu. No hypothetical
unperformed outcomes are provided to the controller.

Let w_(s,mu) be the current UNNORMALIZED posterior numerators inside the acquired
positive task model, after the actual inner prefix. Define
  T=sum_(s,mu) w_(s,mu),
  G(w)=[sum_mu max_s w_(s,mu) - max_s sum_mu w_(s,mu)]/T.   (12)
The current MAP task error is1-max_s sum_mu w_(s,mu)/T. If a hypothetical ideal
oracle revealed the complete terminal syndrome mu, its smallest error would
be1-sum_mu max_s w_(s,mu)/T. Thus G is EXACTLY the most that learning mu could
improve the correction success under this model. Any further syndrome-only
measurement is a stochastic channel of mu and cannot improve more than G.

This identifies a different retirement criterion from total uncertainty. If
the logical error has irreducible probability .1 independent of mu, G=0 even
though the output error remains .1. No amount of syndrome reading helps; the
controller legitimately asks for zero probes. The unsolved risk is not hidden
by claiming that it was measured away.

A naive greedy policy fails. Let two uniform latent bits determine a logical
X through their parity. Either single eta=.6 weak probe alone leaves the best
correction success at1/2, so its immediate value is exactly zero. Reading both
distinct bits raises success to17/25=.68, a gain9/50=.18. Greedy immediate
information/task-gain stopping would retire too early. The constructed policy
uses complete fair rounds, one actual probe on each of six ancillas, and tests
the full remaining-value bound rather than stopping on zero one-probe gain.
There is no claim that fair rounds are the optimal adaptive sensing policy.

The executed controller stops after a complete round as soon as
  G<=epsilon=1/200,
or after35 complete rounds. It can stop before round1 if G is already small.
Each round uses six local probes on the existing meter schedule; they may run
serially. Naming a round supplies no new parallel measurement port. Both
compared protocols can be allotted a common worst-case schedule of210 local
probe slots plus the bounded between-round computation/feedback allowance.
There are at most35 stop decisions. The unused remainder is padded with
isolated idles. No computation latency is assumed free, and no elapsed-time
improvement is claimed. Likelihood and stop comparisons use finite integers.

For ANY fixed acquired positive weights, the model-averaged excess task error
relative to an ideal revelation of mu is at most
  epsilon+6 t_35.                                        (13)
On early-stopped branches, conditional excess risk equals G<=epsilon. On capped
branches, their nonnegative contribution is no larger than the full35-round
experiment's value gap. Correcting the logical Pauli associated with a majority
estimate of mu gives a comparison rule whose error above perfect-syndrome risk
is at most Pr(any syndrome majority wrong)<=6t_35. MAP is at least as good.
This proves(13) without asserting a per-branch bound on capped outcomes.

If a deployment law W is independently known to satisfy
||W-What||_1<=r with the SAME terminal observation kernel, the frozen policy's
excess risk over W's own perfect-syndrome optimum is at most epsilon+6t_35+r.
The fixed policy's risk and the perfect-syndrome optimum each change by at
most r/2. The calibration-mixture confidence statement does not itself supply
this deployment premise for arbitrary future quantum memory.

Equation(13) is inside the learned task model. The actual unread environment
may have drifted since acquisition. Fresh outer verification applies to the
ENTIRE frozen stopping-and-repair algorithm and protects its accepted-operation
integrity without converting G into actual-world conditional certainty.

15. Bounded renewal and a sharp limit on response complexity
===========================================================
Fix m, a calibration budget N, dyadic precision b, readout cap L_max and packet
length n. Repeatedly acquire a new calibration block, compute positive task
weights, freeze the recovery/stopping algorithm, validate with fresh settings,
and retire the old calibration records. The statistical message is bounded by
  O(4*2^m log(N+1)+4*2^m(b+1)+m log(L_max+1)+log(n+1)) bits,
plus fixed physical/circuit descriptions and bounded working arithmetic.
This storage bound is independent of the number of completed cycles.

The reason retirement is legitimate is precise: the next accepted-operation
effect bound holds uniformly in whatever physical quantum state the discarded
history leaves behind. It does not require that state to forget, and does not
reconstruct it. Learning improves performance when acquired relationships
remain useful; fresh physical checking renews integrity when the model is
insufficient. The retained positive environment effects are not a hidden
classical-state simulator.

Current-task renewal does not imply whole-stream correctness. Per-cycle
statistical failure budgets and accepted-operation allowances compose with
explicit cost. Fixed precision and fixed n do not give a common small-error
event for infinitely many outputs. Private settings are continually consumed;
an indefinitely stocked secret key is not a bounded physical resource unless
an actual private coordination channel supplies its new settings.

A separate lower bound shows why2^m is a genuine worst-case issue. Let K=2^m
and let f be an arbitrary unknown K-bit response table. Consider the MEMORYLESS
physical channel
  G_f(rho)=K^-1 sum_mu X^{f(mu)} rho X^{f(mu)} tensor|mu><mu|.
Even grant an ideal observation of mu and unlimited prior calibration. Retain
only B bits of controller information before a fresh task, with no additional
f-sensitive oracle, query or uncharged program. To correct with average error
epsilon<1/2 uniformly over f requires
  B>=K[1-h2(epsilon)].                                   (14)
Choose f as K independent fair bits. A good correction predicts the random
queried bit f(mu). Conditional subadditivity and the binary decoding bound give
H(f|message)<=K h2(epsilon), hence(14). Nayak's quantum random-access-code bound
gives the corresponding lower bound for total retained quantum/classical
log-dimension. A Bell experiment supplies the mathematical reduction from
general quantum recovery to bit prediction; it is not a newly granted physical
operation for the proposed observer.

If accepted yield at least a>0 is required and conditional accepted error is
at most epsilon, the erasure version gives B>=aK[1-h2(epsilon)]. Without a
yield requirement, arbitrary rejecting controllers can always reject; the
actual fixed finite-unsharp verifier instead retains its poor positive floor
b^(n-1), possibly with every accepted output wrong.

This example has no continuing QUANTUM-STATE memory, but information about
the unknown fixed response law remains useful indefinitely. Its description
cost must not be conflated with a physical quantum mode's dimension or an old
state perturbation's lifetime.

Acquisition can replace retained information, at explicit cost. With no
retained f information, Q fresh uniform-context auxiliary queries, even
granting perfect error labels, give optimal average error
  (1/2)(1-1/K)^Q.
A current-context matched oracle would supply the needed bit directly, but is
a different physical interface. Under the uniform-context interface, the
necessary reacquisition time is exponential in m. The governing proof is
prior_and_limits/CYCLIC_TASK_OBSERVERS_AND_RESPONSE_MEMORY_LIMIT.txt.

The resulting organizing distinction is between lifetime, response complexity
and obtainable new observations. A short state lifetime does not bound an
unknown law's complexity; finite task width does not predict all future
controls; and fresh verification does not supply useful yield by itself.
The positive construction handles all three obligations for a specified
operation, with bounded width and substantial explicit acquisition costs.
