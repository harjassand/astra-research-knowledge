ASTRA — SOFT SYNDROME TASK WEIGHTS FROM ACTUAL WEAK RECORDS
25 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane

Result and scope
================
For a fixed one-qubit quantum operation whose final m ancillary registers are
read by actual finite weak QND Pauli instruments and then discarded, the full
fine readout word has an exact positive representation with 4*2^m latent TASK
weights. The weights can be acquired from outer paired weak Pauli audit scores
and the observed majority syndromes, without receiving logical-error labels or
a native noise model. The acquired weights support recovery using ALL fine
readout information. No table indexed by every possible fine word/count tuple
is needed. This is a task-specific finite representation, exponential in m;
it is not compression of unrestricted physical quantum memory.

The crucial physical elimination is the actual partial trace of readout
ancillas. It removes cross terms between their Pauli eigenvalues. The resulting
known emission kernel describes actual weak records; it is not an assumed
hidden classical model for the evolving quantum environment.

The finite-record estimation theorem controls the calibration mixture of
current-use weights. It does not acquire stationarity or guarantee the next
memory state. Freeze the learned recovery and use a fresh outer acquired
validation block for the delivered task; the retained integrity theorem does
not require the learned representation to predict future occupation correctly.

1. Exact physical elimination of measured and discarded ancillas
================================================================
After all native encode/transmit/decode operations, let L be the one-qubit
logical output, A=A1...Am the readout ancillas, and M arbitrary unread physical
memory. The joint state or channel into LAM may be completely general.
Ancilla i is repeatedly measured in one fixed Pauli basis Pi by the stated
local QND instrument, with no undeclared intervening coupling. Write Pi's
projectors as Pi_(mu_i), mu_i=0,1. For a full local word wi,
  K_wi=sum_mu_i k_wi(mu_i) Pi_(mu_i).
The m instruments act on different local ancillary registers. They may be
sequential in time; their declared local tensor action is what matters.

Finally discard A. For any joint rho_LAM,
  Tr_A[K_w rho K_w*]
    =sum_mu L(w|mu) Tr_A[Pi_mu rho Pi_mu],                    (1)
  L(w|mu)=product_i |k_wi(mu_i)|^2.
Indeed Tr_A[Pi_mu rho Pi_nu]=0 for mu!=nu. This holds with arbitrary quantum
coherence and arbitrary L:M correlations. It does NOT assert that the ancillas
were physically projectively measured before the weak probes, nor that a
retained ancilla has the same backaction as an ideal projective instrument.
An actual private Pauli pre-dephasing is allowed but unnecessary when the
ancilla is subsequently traced out and no intermediate feedback reuses it.

For equal elementary contrast eta and Li repetitions,
  L(w|mu)=product_i q_(mu_i)^ki (1-q_(mu_i))^(Li-ki),
  q_0=(1+eta)/2, q_1=(1-eta)/2,
where ki counts positive marks in wi. Hence the count tuple is an EXACT
sufficient statistic of the full words for this operation. Multiplying by
binom(Li,ki) gives the count likelihood. Those binomial factors are common
to all mu for a given count tuple and may be omitted in a MAP comparison.

2. Positive error/syndrome effects after the actual outer frame operation
========================================================================
Take the raw macro BEFORE its final syndrome-dependent local Pauli recovery.
The declared target here is one-qubit identity transmission. For a different
declared Clifford target V, first factor V mathematically and label relative
errors in that target frame; no physical trusted inverse V is supplied.
Use the retained fresh matched outer Pauli frames and retire their keys.
For each virtual ancilla eigenvalue mu, the averaged macro instrument has
Kraus operators s_L tensor K_(s,mu,ell) for s in {I,X,Y,Z}. Define
  F_(s,mu)=sum_ell K_(s,mu,ell)* K_(s,mu,ell),
  F_(s,mu)>=0, sum_(s,mu) F_(s,mu)=I_M.                       (2)
These operators need not commute. At an actual pre-round memory marginal rho_M,
  W_(s,mu)=Tr[F_(s,mu) rho_M]                               (3)
is a positive joint task-weight vector. The observable joint law for fine
readout w and raw relative Pauli s is
  p(s,w)=sum_mu L(w|mu) W_(s,mu).                            (4)
The labels s and mu are proof coordinates, not information supplied to the
observer. The physical input/output operation, including external references,
is still the one defined by the complete instrument and fresh outer frames.

For m=6 there are D=4*64=256 latent weights. Keeping all L=35 count tuples
instead would require as many as 36^6 context entries. Formula (4) changes the
representation by using a known physical observation kernel, rather than
asserting that all fine contexts are uninformative.

3. Acquisition from received weak audit scores
==============================================
Let C be the received m-bit majority syndrome. Its known emission matrix is
  B_(c,mu)=product_i [(1-t_i) if c_i=mu_i, t_i otherwise],
where t_i is the exact majority tail of the actual local instrument. Let
  Wobs_(s,c)=sum_mu B_(c,mu) W_(s,mu).

On each calibration call choose a fresh uniform outer Pauli axis P in X,Y,Z,
and receive the signed paired weak score z=+1,-1 with common gain gamma.
Let chi_d(P)=+1 when d commutes with P and -1 otherwise. The acquired identity is
  nu_c=E[1{C=c}]=sum_s Wobs_(s,c),
  u_(d,c)=E[1{C=c} chi_d(P) z]
          =gamma[4 Wobs_(d,c)-nu_c]/3.
Thus
  Wobs_(d,c)=[nu_c+3 u_(d,c)/gamma]/4.                        (5)
The actual signed input weak instrument is used in this identity, so input
measurement steering of M is included. Current P and frame settings must not
be handed to the native macro or its internal decoder through another port.

If a known baseline final Pauli d0(C) was physically applied during training,
first undo its score sign: use z_raw=chi_(d0(C))(P) z. The latent s coordinate
must refer to the RAW operation before that correction. Failing to unweight
the actual action would learn the already-corrected error and then correct it
again. If the baseline uses the fine counts, the same unweighting uses that
actual received count context.

All four candidate final corrections have counterfactual scores on the same
call. This is a one-use response identity: it does not assert that changing
past corrections would leave an entire future feedback/memory history fixed.

4. No iid premise for the finite calibration-mixture estimate
============================================================
Assume the native macro and selected baseline controller on round j are fixed
before its fresh outer P/frame/settings. They may depend on the previous
record. Let Wobs_j be the conditional positive weight vector on that actual
history, and barWobs=(1/N)sum_j Wobs_j. Its components are random predictable
history averages, not a stationary unknown population asserted by the observer.

Use the record estimator
  Z_(d,c),j=1{C_j=c}[1+3 chi_d(P_j) z_raw,j/gamma]/4,
  hatWobs=(1/N)sum_j Z_j.
For each j,
  E[Z_j|past]=Wobs_j, sum_(d,c) Z_(d,c),j=1,
  ||Z_j||_1=3/gamma.                                       (6)
For any sign vector theta in {-1,+1}^D, theta dot Z_j lies in
[-3/gamma,3/gamma]. Conditional Hoeffding's lemma followed by the union bound
over the 2^D sign vectors therefore gives, with probability at least 1-delta,
  ||hatWobs-barWobs||_1 <= r_N,
  r_N=(3/gamma) sqrt{2[D log2+log(1/delta)]/N}.               (7)
No sign vectors are enumerated by the observer; this is a proof of the l1 bound.
Equation (7) is a sufficient finite-N radius, not an optimized concentration
bound or a claim that a displayed empirical fit attained that worst-case cost.
Time-uniform variants can pay an explicit time/risk budget or use a suitable
mixture supermartingale. They are not silently supplied by this fixed-N result.

5. Exact inverse conditioning and positive reconstruction
=========================================================
For one bit,
  B_i=[[1-t_i,t_i],[t_i,1-t_i]],
  B_i^-1=(1-2t_i)^-1 [[1-t_i,-t_i],[-t_i,1-t_i]].
For t_i<1/2 its induced l1 norm is 1/(1-2t_i). The full product kernel obeys
  kappa=||B^-1||_(1->1)=product_i (1-2t_i)^-1.                (8)
Apply B^-1 separately to each raw Pauli slice of hatWobs, obtaining U. Its
entries may be negative, but sum U=1. Clip negatives and normalize:
  What=U_+/sum U_+.
This is an l1-nearest probability vector to U. If its negative mass is a,
the projection moves total l1 distance2a; every probability vector requires
at least that movement. On the event (7),
  ||What-barW||_1 <=2 kappa r_N.                             (9)
Here barW=(1/N)sum_j W_j is the actual calibration-average latent task vector.

For equal t, kappa=(1-2t)^-m. One genuinely weak read per ancilla, with
t=(1-eta)/2, gives kappa=eta^-m: the inverse can be exponentially ill conditioned.
Finite repetition changes the physical observation. Since t_L<=exp(-L eta^2/2),
L=O(eta^-2 log(m+1)) suffices to keep kappa bounded at a fixed desired value.
This costs mL actual weak marks per macro, with the corresponding storage time.
The dimension D=4*2^m still grows exponentially with the number of syndrome
bits; bounded inversion does not solve generic decoding complexity.

6. Recovery with all received information and a risk bound
=========================================================
For each newly received count tuple k, form
  score_s(k)=sum_mu L(k|mu) What_(s,mu),
and apply the actual final local Pauli
  d_hat(k)=argmax_s score_s(k).
The observer retains D weights, m temporary counters, and the known local
measurement constants. It need not retain every past fine word. Exact rational
likelihood ratios or explicitly rounded log-likelihoods can be used; precision
costs are separate from the number of parameters.

Let d_star minimize mean raw Pauli/Bell error for the calibration mixture barW
using all counts, and let R_W(d) be that mean error. A Markov observation kernel
contracts l1 distance, and choosing the largest posterior mass yields
  R_barW(d_hat)-R_barW(d_star)
    <=||What-barW||_1 <=2 kappa r_N.                         (10)
Proof. At a count tuple where d_hat differs from d_star, their true mass
difference is at most the sum of the two coordinate estimation errors, because
the estimated mass favors d_hat. Sum those distinct coordinates over all tuples.
The result is at most the l1 distance of the joint (s,k) distributions, which
is bounded by the latent l1 distance.

The best full-count rule is never worse than the best majority-only rule,
because the latter is a member of the full-count decision class. That fact
does not guarantee strict improvement in every model or justify a supplied
unknown prior. Both ordinary filters and Astra observers may use this exact
representation, acquisition identity, projection and decoder.

7. Strict soft-information control and an observation-model failure
==================================================================
Consider one virtual syndrome bit mu with probability Pr(mu=1)=1/10 and raw
logical error s=X iff mu=1. Read it using five actual weak probes, eta=3/5.
The best rule that receives only the majority reports X when the positive
count k<=2. Its error is
  181/3125=.05792.
The full-count MAP rule reports X only for k<=1. At k=2 the likelihood ratio
is4 but the prior odds are1/9, so acting on the majority would over-correct.
Its error is
  101/3125=.03232.
The difference is an exact 80/3125=.0256. This is a controlled physical readout
law, not a general advantage over conventional Bayesian decoding. The acquired
weights enable that ordinary decoder without handing it the prior probability.

The known full instrument is essential. If a deterministic X is inserted after
the first probe of a length-three Z word, initial |0> gives probabilities
  Pr(+,+,-)=.8*.2*.8=.128,
  Pr(-,+,+)=.2*.2*.2=.008.
These words have the same count. The static QND count kernel is then false.
A known intervening schedule can be modeled with a different kernel; unknown
measurement-time dynamics cannot be suppressed by calling the meter's contrast
calibrated. The outer whole-operation integrity theorem can still hold for its
correctly declared arbitrary macro, but this particular compression is lost.

8. What this acquires, and what remains a physical obligation
============================================================
This construction acquires joint task/observed-syndrome laws and separates a
known local observation kernel from unknown native quantum dynamics. It obtains
a usable recovery policy without a native Hamiltonian, a hidden-state label,
or logical errors reported by an oracle. It says nothing about reconstructing
the full quantum memory, and its calibration mixture does not automatically
predict a future distribution. Use the fresh acquired validation on the actual
delivered logical operation.

Reference preservation, private outer settings, retired frame keys, actual
endpoint routing and storage, physical transmission count, finite weak
instrument backaction and precision costs remain as in the governing sources.
No hardware, purchase, outreach, or supplied coherent control port is produced
by estimating these weights. The physical encoder/decoder is separately
constructed from actual native calls and acquired local preparations.

Reproduce:
  python3 research/acquired_syndrome/bootstrap/soft_syndrome_deconvolution_checks.py
The accompanying source performs exact rational operator/kernel identities,
noncommuting positive-memory-effect controls, inverse and projection checks,
and the strict soft-information and non-QND failure calculations. It does not
claim a new sampled acquisition record; root's run supplies that evidence.

The mathematical ingredients (classical deconvolution, simplex projection,
Hoeffding martingales and Bayesian recovery) are prior methods. Their role here
is an explicitly obtained task interface with actual quantum-measurement
elimination and an honest calibration/future-use distinction.

9. Changing the terminal physical observation after acquisition
===============================================================
For the same channel into LAM, replacing 35 final local QND probes per ancilla
by 15 changes only L(w|mu) in (1). The underlying positive effects F_(s,mu) do
not depend on how accurately those final ancillas are weakly read, because
they are then discarded and never interact with the logical output or native
memory again. Thus weights acquired with the stronger 35-probe observation
can be used in a controller for the weaker 15-probe observation.

This statement is about the observation kernel at a fixed pre-readout boundary.
It does not assert equal future memory states conditioned on different records,
or stationarity of the current task weights. If a physical readout-duration
change alters uncontrolled native dynamics, it is a changed macro and must be
included. The executed communication construction keeps the original duration
by padding 20 isolated idle slots; its memory updates at carrier interactions.
The gain is fewer actual weak probes, not shorter storage time. Preparations
and outer validation remain at their declared lengths. Early observations
followed by further ancilla reuse/coupling do not satisfy this elimination.

A bounded adaptive terminal-read decision tree also admits the same identity.
Each branch fixes a sequence of chosen local QND probes and their received
marks. All its Kraus operators are diagonal in the same product Pauli basis;
the branch-selection decisions are known functions of already received marks.
Consequently its likelihood L(branch|mu) is the product of the actually executed
probe likelihoods, and equation (1) still holds after the actual final discard.
The observer may select which ancilla to probe next or when to stop without
learning a native Hamiltonian. The construction supplies no free future risk
guarantee: the controller must be frozen and the delivered macro verified anew.

10. Acquired finite-record implementation and actual shorter-read test
=====================================================================
fit_soft_decoder.py reads only the public local-instrument protocol and the
65,536 received training records. It performs exact rational score inversion,
product-kernel deconvolution and positive clipping/normalization. The fitted
256 probabilities have negative estimated mass .00720439722 before projection.
The inverse norm is1.00041083748. The sufficient99% calibration-mixture l1 and
soft-regret radius is .51362638430: this data set does not acquire a small
worst-case model prediction error merely because its empirical fit looks good.
The separate fresh outer integrity certificate remains essential.

The independent communication/soft_record_observer.py implementation retains
256 nonnegative 24-bit numerators summing exactly to2^24. Exact comparison with
the rational fit gives l1 quantization error2.9566007608474e-6, below1/65536.
Integer likelihood comparisons at eta=3/5 avoid underflow and threshold ambiguity.
dyadic_soft_crosscheck.py checks this representation and all original validation
count tuples at lengths3,7,15,35. Quantization changes9 actual decisions at15
and19 at35; these differences were also checked against exact rational scores.
They are retained effects of finite precision, not silently discarded failures.

The subsequent fresh executed operation physically uses15 inner weak probes
per ancilla with acquired preparations35 and outer audits35. Both the full-count
controller and the strongest majority-only rule derived from the same acquired
dyadic weights are frozen before the fresh run. An independent record-only
paired-score certificate obtains a99% lower bound .007959986095 on the soft
controller's mean one-use risk improvement over that majority controller on
the32,256 actual audited calls. No hidden logical labels, native noise model,
or memory states enter that comparison. It is not a payload-specific improvement
claim; the delivered payloads have their separate accepted-output guarantee.
