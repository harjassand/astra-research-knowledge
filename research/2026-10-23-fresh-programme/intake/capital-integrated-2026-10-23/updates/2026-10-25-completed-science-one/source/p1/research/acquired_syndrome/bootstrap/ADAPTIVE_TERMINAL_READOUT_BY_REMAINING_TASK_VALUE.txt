ASTRA — ADAPTIVE TERMINAL READOUT BY REMAINING TASK VALUE
25 October 2026

Main construction
=================
An observer need not keep measuring until its total uncertainty is small.
Some residual uncertainty cannot be resolved by the physical observation that
remains available. The useful stopping quantity is instead the largest task
improvement that those remaining observations could still deliver.

For the acquired quantum-transmission interface, the receiver has a positive
256-weight task model W_(s,mu), where s is a relative logical Pauli and mu is
the six-bit virtual eigenvalue of ancillas that will be locally weakly read
and then discarded. The observer does not receive s or mu. It acquires W from
actual earlier outer/inner weak records and uses a known terminal QND kernel.
The full quantum native memory is neither reconstructed nor supplied as a
classical label. The raw encoder, physical link and decoder remain inside
the complete operation validated at its exposed logical one-qubit boundary.

This source constructs and executes a bounded controller that asks for more
actual terminal weak probes only while perfect knowledge of mu could still
improve the modeled Pauli decision by more than epsilon. It preserves the
strong ordinary active-Bayesian comparator: given the same acquired weights
and physical instruments, that observer makes exactly the same decisions.

1. Physical interface and known observation kernel
===================================================
After the unknown native encode/transmit/decode macro, let L be its received
logical output, A its six readout ancillas and M any unread quantum memory.
The terminal readout instruments are local QND weak Pauli probes on A. No
subsequent interaction reuses A or lets these readout choices control another
native coupling. Each requested probe has contrast eta=3/5. Its negative mark
has probability 4/5 if the corresponding virtual eigenvalue bit is 1 and 1/5
if it is 0. Each measured ancilla is eventually physically discarded.

For any finite adaptive readout branch v, diagonal QND Kraus operators and the
actual final partial trace give
  Tr_A[K_v rho_LAM K_v*]
     =sum_mu L(v|mu) Tr_A[Pi_mu rho_LAM Pi_mu].              (1)
L(v|mu) is the product of the likelihoods of probes ACTUALLY requested on that
branch. Choices and stopping decisions are known functions of preceding
received marks; they add no unknown emission factor. Cross-mu terms vanish
under the actual discard. This is not an ideal projective meter supplied to
the observer and does not declare the continuing M classical.

The earlier terminal-readout source proves that, after the actual retired
outer frames, the channel has positive task effects F_(s,mu) on M, summing to
I. For a current memory state their weights are Tr(F_(s,mu)rho_M). Acquired
weights approximate an earlier conditional-history mixture of these task
weights. No stationarity is acquired by replacing the physical memory with
the fitted vector; the fresh delivered-operation verification remains vital.

2. Remaining value of perfect syndrome information
==================================================
On a received branch v let
  U_(s,mu)=What_(s,mu) L(v|mu), T=sum_(s,mu) U_(s,mu),
  C=max_s sum_mu U_(s,mu), P=sum_mu max_s U_(s,mu).
The current modeled success probability is C/T. If the observer were given
the exact mu for free, the best success would be P/T. This unavailable perfect
readout is only a mathematical upper benchmark. Define
  G(v)=(P-C)/T.                                           (2)
Convexity of max gives G>=0. Any further syndrome-only experiment is a
stochastic kernel acting on mu. Its optimal mean success cannot exceed P/T:
after learning mu, one could reproduce that additional experiment and use its
same final action. Thus no admissible continuation of terminal readout can
improve modeled success by more than G(v).

This bound concerns the chosen terminal interface. It does not prove that
all physics lacks information about the error or rule out a different
interaction, a new sensor, or an acquired coherent control operation.

The controller stops when G<=epsilon and applies the Pauli attaining C.
The test is an exact integer inequality, so it does not infer smallness from
floating underflow or assume a probability margin that was not acquired.
It may stop with a substantial total logical error when no remaining
syndrome information can resolve that error.

3. Avoiding a decisive greedy-information failure
=================================================
Pure one-step information greed can stop too early. Let two hidden syndrome
bits be independent uniform bits, and let the logical error be X exactly when
their parity is 1. Learning either bit alone leaves parity uniform, so the
expected one-step task improvement is EXACTLY ZERO. At eta=3/5, after one weak
probe of each bit, the Bayes success is 17/25, a gain 9/50=.18 over 1/2. A rule
that refuses every locally zero-value probe never discovers the joint benefit.

The implemented construction therefore uses a fair schedule: one complete
round contains one actual probe on every one of the six readout lanes. It
rechecks (2) after each round and stops after at most 35 rounds. No claim of
globally optimal sensor allocation is made. The fair schedule prevents the
one-step-value trap, while the remaining-perfect-information bound supplies
a meaningful stopping criterion. Bounded lookahead or better scheduling may
improve costs later, but they are not silently assumed in this execution.

A second exactly controlled case distinguishes task value from residual
uncertainty. Suppose every mu has conditional probabilities .9 for I and .1
for X, independent of mu. Then total logical risk is .1 but G=0, and the
controller correctly requests ZERO probes. An absolute uncertainty target
below .1 would chase a risk this readout can never resolve.

Both controls are exact rational tests in adaptive_terminal_observer.py.
They are physically realizable mixtures of logical Pauli errors and local
ancilla eigenstates; no unphysical readout labels need be supplied at runtime.

4. A bounded modeled-regret theorem, including the cap
======================================================
Let m ancillas have identical terminal contrast eta, an odd cap L and majority
tail t_L. Let pi be the fixed fair-round policy that stops at G<=epsilon or
after L probes per ancilla. For ANY positive joint task weights W used by
the policy, its mean excess risk above the perfect-mu Bayes benchmark satisfies
  R_W(pi)-R_W(perfect mu) <= epsilon + m t_L.               (3)

Proof. At a stopped branch v the excess conditional risk relative to learning
mu is exactly G(v). Early-stop branches contribute at most epsilon times their
total probability. For cap branches, couple the mathematical experiment to
a full L-probe word on every lane. This is a proof coupling, not a claim that
unperformed measurements were received or even sampled in the execution.
The cap event is a subset of full-word outcomes; since G is nonnegative, its
contribution is at most E_full[G]. Given the full word, one permissible rule
first estimates mu by coordinatewise majority and then uses the best action
for that estimated mu. Compared with knowing mu, this rule loses success by
at most the probability that any estimated bit is wrong. A union bound gives
at most m t_L, independently of the prior W. The full-word Bayes rule cannot
do worse, so E_full[G]<=m t_L. Adding the early contribution proves (3).

For m=6, eta=3/5, L=35, epsilon=1/200, the exact bound is
  24239390735749180529713/4656612873077392578125000
  = .005205369524250406...
This is a mean result. A capped branch can have G much larger than epsilon.
The execution contains precisely such a branch and preserves it below.

If an actual current deployment task law W independently satisfies
||W-What||_1<=r with the same terminal physical kernel, the frozen learned
controller's regret under W is at most epsilon+m t_L+r. Indeed the fixed
policy risk and the perfect-mu optimal risk are each r/2-Lipschitz in a
probability-vector l1 distance. This robustness statement is conditional on
that actual deployment premise. An earlier calibration-mixture bound does
not establish it for arbitrary future quantum memory.

5. Exact finite arithmetic and explicit physical resources
===========================================================
The acquired model has 256 nonnegative 24-bit numerators with denominator 2^24.
For one received six-mark round b, a column indexed by mu is multiplied by
  4^(6-Hamming(b,mu)).
The common factor 5^-6 cancels from all action and stopping comparisons.
Every update, maximum and comparison is therefore an exact integer operation.
At most 210 requested elementary probes multiply these likelihoods. Runtime
numerators and their sums can be bounded by 445 bits at the declared cap.
The receiver keeps the 256 integers, six small counters, a bounded program and
the current finite record needed for the delivered operation. It need not
keep an expanding posterior over native quantum memory. The dimension is
still 4*2^m; this does not solve arbitrary many-body decoding complexity.

A round does not acquire six parallel meters. With the existing local ports
the six probes can run serially. The common timing allowance reserves 210
elementary probe slots, bounded between-round computation/feedback/routing,
and final recovery. At most 35 round request/stop decisions are needed; unused
slots are isolated padding. Logical storage, local routing, acquired resets
and private outer coordination remain charged. The demonstrated benefit is
fewer probe invocations, not shorter elapsed time or free controller latency.

The entire frozen adaptive controller is part of each macro seen by the outer
secret-slot validator. It never receives the current outer axis, role or frame
key. The validator's positive accepted-error-weight theorem therefore applies
even when the fitted task weights poorly predict the current physical memory.
Rejected unknown inputs are not silently retried or restored.

6. Executed acquisition-controlled operation
============================================
The controller uses exactly the earlier 256 acquired positive weights; no new
native parameters or hidden logical labels enter it. The physical generator
continues the quantum memory from communication/soft_validation_only.npz.
It executes only the native pre-readout portion first, leaving its latent
syndrome strictly plant-side. A new weak sign is sampled only when the
record-only controller requests its next actual round. Unperformed later
signs are NEVER sampled and never used by a comparison observer.

7. Independent received-only verification and honest comparisons
================================================================
verify_adaptive_terminal.py recomputes every chosen round, stop and final
Pauli from only the acquired weights and physically received words. It rejects
extra marks beyond the recorded stop or a premature/missing observation.
All 8192 decisions pass, without reading the native model, latent syndromes,
logical-error labels or physical memory state.

66 of 128 unknown-qubit payload attempts are accepted. The fresh integrity
theorem gives accepted bad weight at most 1/64 (sharper display .0155577582),
not conditional error 1/64 on each accepted output. Under its independent
input/reference conditions, conditioning requires division by the actual
acceptance probability. The separately stored simulation truth contains one
erroneous accepted payload; it is not suppressed as a failed fixture.

A same-record comparison uses the learned MAP action after the first ACTUALLY
performed round, versus the final adaptive action. Both can be computed from
the real received record. The first rule intentionally ignores later marks;
it is a way to measure the acquired information's task value, not the strongest
possible conventional controller. The strong equally informed conventional
active-Bayesian observer uses the full same structure and agrees with our rule.

On 8064 actual audits, the paired received-score statistic has S=310 and Q=368.
The exact eight-lambda betting bound of the companion source, with total
failure probability .01 and rational ln 800<6.7, certifies mean one-use gain
at least 391/10000=.0391 for the final action over that first-round action.
This concerns the actual AUDITED conditional histories. It does not certify
payload-specific gain, a changed counterfactual past, or arbitrary future M.
The outer accepted-output statement governs the payloads separately.

Validation-only labels give actual-history error indicators .0705566 for the
first-round action and .0148926 for the final action. These are correlated
simulation diagnostics, not confidence intervals and not observer inputs.

8. Scope of the advance and the remaining obstruction
======================================================
The construction obtains a useful readout-control operation from acquired task
weights. It distinguishes useful surviving information from uncertainty that
the remaining sensor cannot resolve, gives an explicit finite stop/cap rule,
preserves unknown-qubit transmission and reference coherence as the task, and
continues a physical noncommuting memory without resetting it.

It does not claim a native device advantage over strong existing quantum codes
or Bayesian controllers. It makes their required predictive interface explicit
and usable from the specified weak observations. The finite number of task
weights comes from the exposed logical/syndrome boundary, not from the entropy
of the full environment. Extending such obtained boundaries to larger useful
interacting systems, with controlled acquisition and renewal costs, remains
the consequential scaling problem.
