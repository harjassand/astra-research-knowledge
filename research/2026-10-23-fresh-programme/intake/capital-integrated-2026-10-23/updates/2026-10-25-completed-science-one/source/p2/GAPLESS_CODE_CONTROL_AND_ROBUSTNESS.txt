COHERENT CODE CONTROL THROUGH AN OPEN CONTINUUM
==============================================

This continuation separates four statements:
 (i) an exact sufficient Hamiltonian condition;
 (ii) a constructive Morse example with a rational error certificate;
 (iii) a full magnetic hyperfine-manifold completion of the internal operator;
 (iv) the remaining three-dimensional field, trap, source and noise interfaces.
The last statement is not upgraded to an integrated apparatus.

The scalar derivation, exact Morse identities, numerical comparators and
ordinary sources are in GAPLESS_DISPERSIVE_PHASE.txt. This note adds the
coherent-code theorem, composability, calibration sensitivity, an inclusive
loss instrument, and the significance of the magnetic completion.

1. FINITE CODE THEOREM: THE DIVIDED SOURCE MUST EXIST

Let P:C^d -> H_S be an isometry with H_S P=E P. Its range is an isolated
native eigenspace: dist(E,sigma(H_S|P-perp))=delta>0. It need not be isolated
from the spectrum of H_B. Set

 H(g) = [[H_S, g V^dagger],[g V,H_B]],

and assume it is self-adjoint for the real g under consideration. Construct
a finite-rank map U1 into D(H_B) intersect D(V^dagger) such that

 (H_B-E) U1 = -V P.                                      (1)

This is a range/domain condition in the entire bath, not the formal notation
"divide by a zero denominator" and not only a vanishing Golden Rule rate.
Define

 K=P^dagger V^dagger U1,
 S=V^dagger U1-P K,
 U2=-(H_S-E)^(-1)_(P-perp) S.                            (2)

The source S is orthogonal to P. Require U2 in D(V). Also

 K=-U1^dagger(H_B-E)U1=K^dagger.

For J_g=(P+g^2 U2, g U1)^T, direct multiplication gives the EXACT identity

 H(g) J_g - J_g(E I+g^2 K)
      = (-g^4 U2 K, g^3[V U2-U1 K])^T.                  (3)

There is no need to assert that J_g spans exact eigenvectors. In particular,
there need not be an exact bound state in the continuum for nonzero g.
Put a=||U1||, b=||U2||, r=||V U2-U1 K||, s=||U2 K||.
Writing P_tilde=(P,0)^T, unitarity and Duhamel give

 ||e^(-iH(g)T/hbar)P_tilde
       -P_tilde e^[-i(E I+g^2 K)T/hbar]||
 <= 2 sqrt(g^2 a^2+g^4 b^2)
       +(T/hbar) sqrt(g^6 r^2+g^8 s^2).                 (4)

No missing normalization correction occurs: insert the unnormalized linear
map J_g twice and use its distance to P_tilde. The middle finite-dimensional
evolution is unitary because K is Hermitian. For T=hbar tau/g^2, tau>0,
the nontrivial code operation exp(-i tau K) has error O(|g|).
The operator norm in (4) already covers inputs entangled with an arbitrary
reference. Any bounded-event probability error is <=epsilon, the unhalved
diamond distance of the corresponding channels is <=2epsilon, and probability
outside the intended bare code is <=epsilon^2.

Fixed-duration zero-ended ramps add O(g) by applying Duhamel on the admitted
bare code. For a common ramp f and finite ||VP||, each edge contributes at
most |g| ||VP|| int|f(t)|dt/hbar. Free phases of the edges are included in the
target; the residual inequality covers their omitted dispersive correction.

The scalar theorem applies separately to finitely many distinct native
energies. The root sum of squared column bounds bounds the code isometry.
That version supplies the three-level Morse operation, without requiring a
degenerate internal code or an otherwise hypothetical inert reference.

2. WHAT COHERENCE AND REUSE ACTUALLY FOLLOW

For H_S=H_M tensor I_d and V=G tensor <v|, with ||v||=1,

 K=chi_n |v><v|.

Two available, nonorthogonal and nonparallel bright vectors yield
noncommuting phase operations. This is a conditional control-design statement:
it requires the physical coupling orientations, a shared native potential,
and coherent source admission. The magnetic stretched-pair realization below
does not by itself supply arbitrary bright-vector orientation.

A fixed finite sequence can be composed without assuming an independently
perfect reset after every pulse. If each full physical unitary W_j satisfies
||W_j P_tilde-P_tilde U_j||<=epsilon_j, ordinary telescoping gives

 ||W_L ... W_1 P_tilde-P_tilde U_L ... U_1||
       <=sum_j epsilon_j.                               (5)

Residual bath amplitude from a previous step is carried through by unitarity,
not silently removed. This statement is for the declared closed dynamics and
initial admitted code. It is not a stationary-memory, arbitrary-depth or
unknown-environment-reset theorem. Total time grows and external errors must
also be composed.

3. THE SPECTRAL REGULARITY BEHIND THE CONDITION

For a scalar input with v=Vn and a bath spectral measure mu_v, a normalizable
divided vector requires

 int |E'-E|^(-2) d mu_v(E') < infinity,                 (6)

with the corresponding operator-domain conditions; any exact bath eigenstate
at E must have zero source projection. Near a regular continuum energy, if
the coupling amplitude vanishes to order r and the density behaves as
|E'-E|^nu, local square integrability requires 2r+nu>1. Merely reporting a
zero value at one point without regularity does not establish (6).

For the Morse operator the complete-spectrum transfer identity establishes
(1) directly, including all continuum energies. It is substantially stronger
than assigning a small on-shell scattering rate. The known Morse basis and
identities are reused mathematics, not a new claimed representation.

5. CALIBRATION ERROR DOES NOT DISAPPEAR BY WEAKENING THE DRIVE

Suppose the real operator adds

 D_g=g eta [[0,W^dagger],[W,0]].

A global operator norm for W is not required if its action on the constructed
columns is bounded and the perturbed Hamiltonian is self-adjoint. In fact

 D_g J_g=(g^2 eta W^dagger U1,
          g eta W P+g^3 eta W U2)^T.

At fixed tau, its extra residual allowance is at most

 tau eta [||WP||/|g|+||W^dagger U1||+|g|||WU2||].        (8)

The leading sufficient bound therefore has the form A|g|+B eta/|g|.
The optimal value within this bound is at |g|~sqrt(eta), with error
O(sqrt(eta)) and time O(eta^(-1)). This is a conservative worst-case
certificate, not a necessary law of actual continuum decay. Particular
spectral regularity can give sharper dynamics. Fixed profile error cannot
be declared harmless from the exact-model g->0 limit.

Native energy drift or a bounded Hamiltonian error independent of g can
instead accumulate as delta T/hbar, hence as delta/g^2. Common-mode phases
may cancel in a particular observable; differential code phases do not.
Actual trap departures, transverse fields, detuning errors and finite source
statistics need their own measured or constructed action bounds.

6. AN INCLUSIVE LOSS INSTRUMENT, NOT A CONDITIONED UNITARY BY DEFAULT

For an explicit uniform irreversible Markov sink from the auxiliary sector,
the no-jump bath generator is H_B-i hbar Gamma/2. Define

 u1_Gamma=(E-H_B+i hbar Gamma/2)^(-1)Vn,
 chi_Gamma=<Vn,u1_Gamma>.

Exactly

 -Im chi_Gamma=(hbar Gamma/2)||u1_Gamma||^2.             (9)

The same residual argument works for the contractive no-jump semigroup when
the required domains hold. At a fixed signed phase theta, its limiting
survival as g->0 is

 Y=exp[-hbar Gamma |theta| ||u1_Gamma||^2/|Re chi_Gamma|]. (10)

Reducing g does not remove this loss. On a code consisting of an inert dark
reference and a bright vector v, the limiting no-jump Kraus operator is

 K0=(I-|v><v|)+sqrt(Y)e^(-i theta)|v><v|.                (11)

The lost-record effect is (1-Y)|v><v|. This is a phase plus an amplitude
filter. Survival conditioning changes arbitrary input superpositions, because
success is input dependent. A heralded UNITARY phase would need additional
matched attenuation of the reference, making K_success=sqrt(Y)U_phase,
and charging the corresponding failures and actual heralding interface.
Unobserved photons do not become a reliable success flag merely by notation.

There is a precise surviving distinction from an ordinary embedded source.
Writing A=E-H_B and Vn=A u1, spectral calculus gives

 u1_Gamma=(A+i eta)^(-1)A u1,
 ||u1_Gamma||<=||u1||, eta=hbar Gamma/2.

If A has no zero-energy point component in u1, u1_Gamma->u1 as Gamma->0.
Thus the shaped source's imaginary self-energy vanishes at most linearly
with the external rate. For a smooth unshaped source with nonzero energy
spectral density w_v(E), instead

 -Im <v,(E-H_B+i eta)^(-1)v> -> pi w_v(E).

This is a spectral-measure statement, not an assumed Markov scattering law
for the unshaped gate. It identifies what the transfer notch really removes:
the embedded-continuum imaginary self-energy floor, while leaving external
decay, source scattering and calibration costs explicit.

7. SOURCE SCALING: BALANCED RAMAN IS NOT THE STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE

For a balanced Raman implementation at fixed detuning, effective g is
proportional to each beam's intensity. Optical scattering can then scale as
g while the phase time scales as g^-2, giving exposure proportional to1/g.
That is a failure of the balanced implementation, not a universal Raman bound.

An equally informed controller can hold the auxiliary/Stokes leg fixed and
scale the source leg proportional to g. Source-state scattering then scales
as g^2; scattering driven from the dressed auxiliary also scales as g^2.
Their integrated exposure can be O(1). The held-leg incident optical energy
grows as g^-2, and its fixed differential Stark shifts, wrong-state coupling
and cross-pair beats must be included or compensated.

Two independently detuned pairs that supply F and F^2 also generally generate
mixed Raman terms proportional to F^(3/2), at different beat frequencies.
A large beat-frequency separation alone does not permit discarding them in
an unbounded motional continuum: a shifted channel may still be open. An
actual selection-rule cancellation or a continuum-tail residual bound is
needed. A selective three-level Raman model is not automatically a full
ground-manifold atomic realization.

For different excited pathways, emitted amplitudes in each indistinguishable
photon/destination channel must be summed BEFORE squaring. Independent
channels add positive rates. Total Rayleigh photon number is not automatically
logical error, although recoil can spoil the motional operation. A simplified
resolved two-path rate floor cannot be promoted to a universal spin-error
bound. Primary examples of this distinction are Jenkins etal PRX12,021027
(2022), Appendix D, and Ozeri etal PRA75,042329(2007).

Preparation availability A_p obeys exactly the intended source accounting:
expected usable incident inputs = attempts times A_p; obtaining a fixed usable
count costs the inverse A_p. This preparation bookkeeping is separate from
inclusive output loss, which remains in every operation's outcome law.

8. FULL HYPERFINE COMPLETION WITHOUT A TWO-LEVEL ROTATING-WAVE TRUNCATION

For a ground electronic J=S=1/2 atom with positive hyperfine splitting,
take a=|F-=I-1/2,m=F-> and b=|F+=I+1/2,m=F+>. An ideal circular field

 B(q,t)=B(q)[xhat cos(omega_d t)+yhat sin(omega_d t)]

is removed EXACTLY by a rotation generated by total F_z. The full rotating
spin Hamiltonian is H_hf-omega_d F_z plus the static magnetic
operator. Tune hbar omega_d=Delta_hf+Lambda and subtract a's rotating energy.
The lower manifold has offsets r hbar omega_d, r=0,1,...; the upper has
offsets -Lambda+r hbar omega_d, r=0,1,... . Only b is below a.

All spectator offsets are at least Delta_hf. Adding H_M to every internal
state therefore leaves all those full motional continua CLOSED at any admitted
native E_n with E_n-E0<Delta_hf. The intended b continuum can still be open.

The magnetic x operator changes m by +/-1. Parity of m-m_a divides the WHOLE
finite hyperfine manifold into the theorem's S and B sectors. The admitted a
state lies in S and the only open b channel lies in B. The first-order dressing
is the explicit notched Morse vector in b plus ordinary gapped resolvents in
closed spectators. The second-order reduced S resolvent is genuinely gapped.
Thus the general sufficient condition survives the entire finite spin
manifold, with no counterrotating channels silently omitted.

For I=3/2, neglecting the very small nuclear magnetic moment in the displayed
example, Delta_hf=100D, Lambda=.5D, the eight rotating offsets are

 (-.5,0,100,100.5,200.5,201,301,401.5) D.

Normalizing the target magnetic matrix element to1 gives total squared
coupling from a to closed spectators1/3 and full matrix norm2/sqrt3.
The same leading-wall stability condition becomes |g|c(2/sqrt3)<D.
check_circular_hyperfine.py independently evaluates the full spin matrices.

Closed spectators add real dispersive shifts, not zero. For each n,

 |delta chi_n| <= ||G n||^2/[3(100D+E0-E_n)].            (12)

For the three-level example these upper bounds are respectively
(.000820935,.000455060,.000178919)/D. Every closed contribution is nonpositive.
Using that sign improves the full curvature interval to

 .02940376882465/D <= C_full <= .03026703883415/D.

These displayed endpoints are rounded outward; the exact endpoints are
C-b4 and C+(5b2+9b6)/14, using the rational bounds in the independent audit.

It therefore remains nonzero. This is a useful surviving consequence of the
full-manifold completion. The old two-channel numerical error constants are
not reused for this enlarged model.

9. WHAT A REAL MAXWELL FIELD ADDS

At a planar current sheet with a Fourier current K_y=K cos(kx)e^(-i omega t),
let kappa=sqrt(k^2-omega^2/c_light^2)>0. In the vacuum half-space z>0,

 A_y=mu0 K exp(-kappa z)cos(kx)/(2 kappa),
 B_x=mu0 K exp(-kappa z)cos(kx)/2,
 B_z=-mu0 K k exp(-kappa z)sin(kx)/(2 kappa),
 E_y=i omega A_y.

Two orthogonal patterned sheets in temporal quadrature supply the desired
circular field on their common symmetry line. Two decay constants a and2a
with calibrated amplitudes give F-cF^2 there. These are actual solutions of
Maxwell's equations, not an assigned molecular dipole. A finite device must
bound its aperture and unwanted Fourier components.

For a normalizable lateral packet, the same equations force extra fields.
For any packet psi with the indicated moments,

 ||[cos(kx)-1]psi|| <= k^2 sqrt(<x^4>)/2,
 ||sin(kx)psi|| <= |k| sqrt(<x^2>).

The unwanted longitudinal magnetic component is first order in lateral width;
the transverse/counterrotating amplitude mismatch is second order. The exact
one-dimensional spin theorem cannot simply set these operators to zero.
Their action on constructed columns can enter the residual bound (8), or a
larger explicitly controlled transverse calculation. Tightening confinement
costs energy and can change other trap/noise interfaces.

The electric near field satisfies |E|/|B|=omega/kappa. Its induced Stark
potential is O(g^2), hence can accumulate an O(1) phase over g^-2 time. It must
be included or compensated. A full-line extrapolation of an attractive
polarizability term proportional to G^2 includes -g^2 F^4 and can even
destabilize the formal Morse wall. The real sheet occupies a half-space and
bounds the field on the accessible side; it also replaces the ideal Morse
left boundary. Surface forces and actual trapping potentials therefore need
their own state-action residual, not an appeal to an exact all-space Morse fit.

Direct magnetic drive removes the optically excited Raman intermediary.
For a specified resistive source its power is proportional to current squared,
and therefore to g^2; the ideal fixed-phase Joule action can remain constant.
This does not establish zero source cost or classical phase control for free.
Thermal field noise, phase/amplitude statistics, dielectric loss, state-dependent
trapping, transverse leakage and finite-aperture errors still enter.

FINITE-WIDTH FAILURE OF THE FIRST CONSERVATIVE COMPLETION

The normalizable-packet issue was evaluated, rather than left as a generic
imperfection. In the quasistatic sheet limit, use independent lateral Gaussian
coordinates X,Y of rms sigma and s=a sigma. For r=1,2 set A=(1,-c). The
complete squared difference from the ideal circular field, on the source
spin a and the three native motional levels, has the positive Gram matrix

 M(s)=(4/3) sum_(r,t) A_r A_t P F^(r+t)P K_rt(s),
 K_rt=exp[-(r-t)^2 s^2/2]-exp[-r^2 s^2/2]
                 -exp[-t^2 s^2/2]+1.

The factor4/3 follows from D_i^2/d_ab^2=4/3 for the specified electron-spin
moment and from the vanishing symmetric mixed spin moments. Gaussian odd
moments remove the other cross terms. This includes both transverse cosine
mismatch and the compulsory longitudinal sine component. The worst bare
code source defect is eta(s)=sqrt(lambda_max(M(s))).

maxwell_transverse_source_defect.py gives

 s=.1:   eta=.0335177363;
 s=.01:  eta=.00329277491;
 s=.001: eta=.000329221996;
 eta(s)/s -> .329221435564 as s->0.

For the rational-certificate gate tau=26.303716...,g=.001, the leading
norm-only perturbation allowance tau eta/g is respectively881.641,86.6122,
and8.65976: all vacuous. Reducing that term to.01 would require

 a sigma<=1.15476811235e-6.

If confinement is a harmonic transverse oscillator of the same mass, this
means hbar omega_perp/D=1/(64s^2)>=1.171737854e10. Treating tighter confinement
as a free repair is therefore not credible under this certificate.

This is a failure of the worst-case norm comparison, not a theorem that the
actual three-dimensional operation has those errors. It discards the energy
and rapid oscillation of unwanted channels. Repair needs an energy-resolved
residual or an explicitly integrated transverse/Floquet construction. The
longitudinal magnetic term can be open at a high kinetic energy; frequency
separation alone does not close it. Its actual high-energy spectral source
weight is a relevant quantity, unlike the full bare norm.

ENERGY-RESOLVED REPAIR AT A NORMALIZABLE FINITE WIDTH

The dangerous longitudinal channel cannot all be called high frequency.
D_z connects a=|F-,m=1> to |F+,m=1>, with rotating offset100D. Absorption
of a100.5D carrier leaves the LOW radial transfer Lambda=.5D. At linear
order in lateral width its spatial source is F-2cF^2, whose continuum
amplitude at that transfer equals -F: the original notch does not protect it.

The actual transverse energy repairs this first-order channel. A sine maps
the centered harmonic ground state only to odd transverse levels. Therefore
all hyperfine-assisted longitudinal sidebands are below their channel
spectrum if

 hbar omega_perp>max_n(E_n+Lambda-E0)=43D/32.

This requires a sigma<1/sqrt86, rather than1e-6. At a sigma=.1 the oscillator
frequency is1.5625D/hbar and the genuine gaps for n={2,4,6} are respectively

 (21/32,3/8,7/32)D=(.65625,.375,.21875)D.

The wrong-helicity cosine difference has zero ground-state transverse
projection and starts at two transverse quanta, so its analogous low-energy
hyperfine-assisted channel is closed as well. This uses channel spectra and
parity, not a global frequency-averaging assumption.

Compensating each sheet amplitude by exp(r^2s^2/2), s=a sigma, restores its
EXACT transverse-vacuum expectation. Every remaining source error then has
zero transverse-vacuum projection. Its Gram kernel simplifies to

 K_rt_compensated=exp(rt s^2)-1.

At s=.1, the error Gram diagonal on n={2,4,6} is approximately
(.00108964,.00070457,.00030461), and its largest-eigenvalue square root
is.0332304170642. The coupling is not tiny. It is treated with the actual
closed-channel denominators in the dressing, rather than bounded by duration
times its norm.

Absorption that stays within the LOWER hyperfine manifold still has open
high-kinetic-energy channels. Complete Morse moments provide exact tail bounds:

 ||1_[A,infinity)(H_M) F P_code|| <=
     4.783e-28 at A=50D, 8.081e-43 at A=100D;
 ||1_[A,infinity)(H_M) F^2 P_code|| <=
     9.439e-26 at A=50D, 3.212e-40 at A=100D.

These displayed numbers are rounded upward. They follow from
||1_[A,infinity) v||^2<=||H_M^m v||^2/A^(2m), evaluated with exact rational
complete-chain moments and a coherent-code Frobenius bound. Every coefficient
created by H_M^m is retained. No high-energy continuum cutoff is imposed on
the physical evolution. The code is morse_high_energy_source_tails.py.

High transverse excitation is also retained. After compensation the harmonic
coefficients have magnitude (r s)^l/sqrt(l!). For l>=30 their squared sum is
bounded by mu^30/[30!(1-mu/31)], mu=r^2s^2<=.04. For l<=29, a5D-wide
resonant window about E_n+100.5D has radial energy at least

 E_2+100.5D-5D-29(1.5625D)=50.71484375D.

Combining the radial moment bound and the complete harmonic tail gives a
uniform coherent-code source amplitude less than8e-27 in that window for
the LONGITUDINAL INTRA-F absorption channel, charging both lateral axes and

The same harmonic split also bounds the high-energy wrong-helicity cosine
source: its vacuum-removed transverse norm squared is cosh(mu)-1<=sinh(mu),

A generous joint first-order source-window allowance1e-24 therefore covers
these enumerated longitudinal and wrong-helicity absorption channels. It

For such a source v, remove only its resonant-window component from a TRIAL
divided vector:

 u=(E-H_channel)^(-1)(I-P_window)v.

Then ||u||<=||v||/(5D), and the defect of its divided-source equation is
P_window v, bounded above. This is a residual construction covering the
excluded component, not a physical survival filter or a discarded outcome.

One consequence survives to second order. The compensated error sources are
orthogonal to the ideal transverse-ground sources, and free resolvents
preserve that orthogonality. Hence no ideal/error cross term enters chi.
All low error channels have the displayed positive gaps; outside the declared
high-energy windows every additional denominator has absolute value>=5D.
The total extra RETAINED-source second-order coefficient obeys

 |delta chi_n| <= M_nn/gap_n.

The induced change of the nonaffine three-level invariant is bounded by
.00336704/D. Combining with the signed full-spin bound gives

 C_with_transverse_dressing > .02603672/D.

This is a substantive normalizable-source/second-order repair. It is not a
full long-time Floquet certificate: applying the interaction again to the

10. SCIENTIFIC COMPARISON AND SCOPE

Ordinary opposite-sign-detuned control already gives a better generic phase
benchmark in the computed example. Stronger direct scalar force control also
defeated the older Schur reaction benchmark. Those failures remain preserved.
Optimal control, boundary identification and tomography must receive the same
native Hamiltonian knowledge, pulse shaping, profiles, reference access and
source accounting. No competitive claim rests on denying these resources.

Coherent interaction through an open waveguide is already established in
giant-atom theory and experiment. Kannan etal Nature583,775(2020) demonstrated
waveguide-mediated interaction with suppressed radiative decay. Carollo etal
PRResearch2,043184(2020) gives a general non-master-equation account. Rieck
etal's2026 structured-waveguide CZ study treats non-Markovian multi-excitation
effects. These are substantive alternatives, not merely components to cite.

The return of this branch is a usable sufficient condition, a complete-spectrum
Morse synthesis, a coherent nontrivial operation with an exact-rational model
certificate, and a full internal magnetic completion. Historical novelty of
gapless control, a superiority result over existing architectures, and an
integrated civilisation-level capability have not been established. The next
physical discriminator is a bound for the actual three-dimensional field and
trap on the evolving dressed states that leaves the nonaffine phase useful,
including its source and noise costs. That interface is not assumed here.
