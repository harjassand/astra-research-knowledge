COHERENT MASK LIFT: EXACT LOCAL CONSTRUCTION AND DIMENSION-FREE BOUNDARY
2026-10-23; passive_lag. Internal mathematical result, novelty UNKNOWN.

Purpose and disposition

The classical rectangle label is not necessary for the exact R/H event
identity. A fixed ancillary quantum resource can replace it and preserve
every accepted live question/answer entry. The construction below is local
for one complete input family and works for noncommuting original effects.

It does NOT remove the general entropy obstruction. A nuclear-norm bound
shows that every exact conjunctive ancillary-mask lift, in arbitrary
dimension and even with a commuting ancilla, has a nontrivial mass cost on
the L-shaped predicate. The bound tensorizes. Uniform zero overhead is
possible exactly for the already proved disjoint-block predicate class.

This is a boundary for a specified coherent-filter construction, not a
general repetition lower bound. A wholesale replacement of the original
coarse realization can escape it, as Section 7 demonstrates.

1. A quantum success table in place of a rectangle decomposition

Let theta(alpha,gamma) be a finite 0/1 tested predicate. A conjunctive
ancillary-mask lift consists of a fixed normalized bipartite (or commuting)
resource eta, local effects E_alpha and F_gamma, and a scalar lambda>0,
such that

  <eta,E_alpha F_gamma eta> = theta(alpha,gamma)/lambda.       (Q1)

The two effect families commute across players; effects within a family
need not commute. Put
  e_alpha=<eta,E_alpha eta>, f_gamma=<eta,F_gamma eta>.
For any nonzero theta, necessarily lambda>=1. If local success operations
are general contraction Kraus operators and their remaining ancilla is
discarded, their success effects K* K and L* L give precisely (Q1).

Every classical positive rectangle decomposition is a special case:
take a classical direct-sum branch k of probability lambda_k/Lambda,
E_alpha(k)=f_k(alpha), F_gamma(k)=g_k(gamma), lambda=Lambda.
Quantum effects are therefore a genuine enlargement of that construction.

2. Exact event/live-law preservation with actual local effects

FORWARD-CONSTRUCTION MODEL: the tensor construction below is immediate
for finite bipartite ancillary resources, or when the ancillary state is
already supplied in the required finite-tracial standard form. It does
not assert that every arbitrary commuting ancillary state has an exact
finite-tracial representation. No use of approximate Lin TABLE density
is being made to replace an approximate mask by an exact one. Section 4's
LOWER BOUND, in contrast, applies directly to every commuting ancillary
state in its original Hilbert-space representation.

Use the pinned N443 finite-tracial notation
  chi(F,H)=tr(sigma F sigma H), sigma>=0, tr(sigma^2)=1.
For a fixed hypothesized tested history r0 and local inputs x,y, let
  sum_alpha F_x^alpha=I, sum_gamma H_y^gamma=I
be the original tested-answer coarse POVMs, and let fine positive effects
G_x^{alpha,a}, K_y^{gamma,b} sum over a,b to the corresponding coarse
effects. No commutation within either player's family is assumed.

Represent the finite ancillary state in standard form as well; denote its
positive unit vector by sigma_eta and its local/opposite effects by
E_alpha,F_gamma. On the tensor-product algebra use the fixed positive
resource sigma tensor sigma_eta, and define

  Ftilde_x = sum_alpha F_x^alpha tensor E_alpha,
  Htilde_y = sum_gamma H_y^gamma tensor F_gamma,
  Gtilde_x^a = sum_alpha G_x^{alpha,a} tensor E_alpha,
  Ktilde_y^b = sum_gamma K_y^{gamma,b} tensor F_gamma.           (Q2)

These are local positive effects. Since E_alpha<=I,
  0<=Ftilde_x<=sum_alpha F_x^alpha tensor I=I,
and similarly Htilde_y<=I. Fine sums are exactly Ftilde_x,Htilde_y.
The usual bounded support/Douglas and polar transport therefore supplies
genuine local/opposite fine POVMs after R, with no new premise.

Tensor factorization and (Q1) give, for EVERY x,y,a,b,

  lambda chi_tilde(Gtilde_x^a,Ktilde_y^b)
   =sum_{alpha,gamma} theta(alpha,gamma)
                         chi(G_x^{alpha,a},K_y^{gamma,b}).     (Q3)

Thus both the tested-event mass and the full accepted live table are
preserved exactly. There is one merged history, carrying outside weight
lambda; there is no classical common rectangle certificate.

The lift may depend on the TESTED question strings c, never on an actual
opposite fresh question. For finitely many c, tensor together the finitely
many ancillary resources in advance and let each hypothesized c select
its factor. This gives one fixed resource for the whole extended input
family. If two locally guessed histories disagree, each side still has
a legal effect on that same resource. S_actual handles those mismatches
by its existing all-input interface; no fresh-question oracle is introduced.

3. The exact one-sided masses

In (Q2), one-sided traces factor. If P_alpha,Q_gamma denote the original
coarse one-party probabilities at the relevant exposure average, then

  G_A=lambda sum_alpha P_alpha e_alpha,
  G_B=lambda sum_gamma Q_gamma f_gamma.                        (Q4)

Within the forward-construction model just stated, the root-led weighted
Jensen step and original R telescope therefore apply without change,
with these masses in place of the classical ones.
They are again finite-table marginal quantities for a fixed lift. The
referee cost remains log(1/p), independent of lambda. General tracial R
compression can still depend on lambda as an accuracy cost. In the finite
matrix proof, exact finite spectral feature spans remain available.

For a concrete strict improvement over classical scalar factorization,
take theta=L=[[1,1],[0,1]], ancillary |Phi+>, and real rank-one projections
onto vectors (cos(t),sin(t)) at angles
  Alice: alpha0=pi/6, alpha1=pi/2;
  Bob:   gamma0=0,    gamma1=pi/3.
Then q_alpha,gamma=(1/2)cos^2(angle_A-angle_B)=(3/8)L.
With lambda=8/3, every ancillary one-sided success probability is 1/2,
so G_A=G_B=4/3 for all original P,Q. The classical optimized balanced
scalar cost tends to 3/2 in the near-perfect L fixture. This finite
example proves that coherent lifting is not merely a new notation for
the classical positive rectangle mixture. It is not a new gap exponent.

4. Nuclear-norm lower bound for every exact quantum lift

Let P,Q be arbitrary probability vectors on the coarse alphabets and set
  W=diag(sqrt(P)) theta diag(sqrt(Q)).
Then every (Q1) lift satisfies

  ||W||_* <= sqrt(G_A G_B) <= max(G_A,G_B).                    (Q5)

Here ||.||_* is the sum of singular values. Proof: in the joint Hilbert
space put
  u_alpha=sqrt(lambda) E_alpha eta,
  v_gamma=sqrt(lambda) F_gamma eta.
The cross-player effects commute, so
  <u_alpha,v_gamma>=theta(alpha,gamma).
Moreover ||u_alpha||^2=lambda<E_alpha^2><=lambda e_alpha,
and likewise for v. If X,Y have columns sqrt(P_alpha)u_alpha and
sqrt(Q_gamma)v_gamma, then W=X*Y. The trace-norm/Hilbert-Schmidt inequality
gives
  ||W||_*<=||X||_2||Y||_2<=sqrt(G_A G_B).
One direct proof of the first inequality takes a singular-value polar
unitary U for W and uses Hilbert-Schmidt Cauchy-Schwarz on tr(U X*Y).
All column families are finite, so the same proof works in arbitrary
Hilbert dimension; no finite matrix/resource bound is assumed.

5. Near-perfect L obstruction and exact tensor amplification

Take the scalar original coarse marginals
  P=(1-r,r), Q=(r,1-r), 0<r<1/2,
and original scalar product coarse table. Its tested acceptance is
  p=1-r^2.
For theta=L, W is the triangular matrix
  [[sqrt(r(1-r)), 1-r], [0, sqrt(r(1-r))]].
For a 2x2 matrix, ||W||_*^2=||W||_F^2+2|det W|; hence

  max(G_A,G_B)>=sqrt(1+2r-3r^2)=1+r+O(r^2).                  (Q6)

Consequently p->1 does not allow the extra one-sided mass of every
coherent conjunctive mask lift to be O(1-p). It is at least of order
sqrt(1-p). This remains true for state-weighted masses, not merely
uniform worst-case marginal caps.

For t independent copies, use theta=L^{tensor t}, P^{tensor t},Q^{tensor t}.
The corresponding W is W_1^{tensor t}. Singular values of a tensor product
are all pairwise products, so its nuclear norm multiplies. Thus EVERY
collective quantum mask lift, even one that does not tensorize internally,
satisfies

  max(G_A,G_B)>=[sqrt(1+2r-3r^2)]^t.                          (Q7)

Choosing r=1/3 gives the simple bound (2/sqrt(3))^t. This excludes a
subexponential one-sided budget for that exact lift task. It does not
exclude a non-conjunctive, state-adapted rebuilding of the whole table.

6. Uniform zero overhead: exact characterization

Suppose a lift has lambda e_alpha<=1 and lambda f_gamma<=1 for every
coarse answer. This is exactly the condition that G_A,G_B<=1 uniformly
over all possible original coarse marginals. Then the nonzero support of
theta is a union of vertex-disjoint complete bipartite blocks.

Proof requires no dilation. Put t=1/lambda. On an accepted edge,
  <E_alpha F_gamma>=t, <E_alpha>,<F_gamma><=t.
The joint expectation is bounded by either marginal, so both marginals
equal t. Because E^2<=E and F^2<=F,
  ||E_alpha eta-F_gamma eta||^2
   =<E_alpha^2>+<F_gamma^2>-2t<=0.
The two vectors coincide and have norm squared t, since their inner
product is t. Along an accepted bipartite path all these vectors coincide.
If a zero entry joined a row and column in that same connected component,
its joint probability would therefore be their inner product t, a
contradiction. Every nonisolated connected component is complete.

Conversely, if theta has K disjoint complete blocks, share a uniform
classical block k, take E_alpha and F_gamma to project onto the appropriate
block, and choose lambda=K. Isolated vertices have zero effects. Then
q=theta/K and all nonzero ancillary marginals are 1/K. The zero mask has
the trivial zero construction.

In particular, adding arbitrary local coherence to this conjunctive
ancillary-mask interface does not enlarge its uniform zero-budget class.
The nuclear-norm bound also supplies a dimension-free positive overhead
for L, so passing to unbounded resources does not close that boundary.

7. Why this is not the final source-specific obstruction

The original scalar L fixture may have NO fresh-input dependence. Its
whole joint coarse table P tensor Q can then be sampled once as shared
randomness lambda0=(alpha,gamma), after which both players know whether
the event occurred. They can reconstruct its accepted fine table using
that shared branch. This is a legal wholesale replacement, while it is
not an entrywise ancillary success kernel acting on the original effects.

The still stronger full-table Bell/common-flag obstruction is independently
written in ../proof_audit/familywise_classical_flag_boundary.txt. It too
has a weaker-interface escape: at p=1 one may choose a fixed accepted
coarse pair and keep the original live Bell measurements. Neither result
is a no-go theorem for loss-aware source-specific replacement.

The remaining problem is therefore to replace the whole input-indexed
accepted live table by one local family, while preserving the game event
to controlled loss, rather than to improve a mask multiplier. The actual
R/H event/flag structure would have to supply an invariant permitting that
replacement. This note has not established such an invariant or a general
quadratic repetition theorem.
