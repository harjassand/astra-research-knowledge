2. Exact adaptive law and cost result

Each pending proposal keeps the original envelope height U_old and one threshold
u through all refinement. The event is u<W(x)/U_old, so its accepted joint mass
is pi(x)W(x)/Z_U. Conditional on the task's starting history, summing rejection
histories leaves the requested posterior. The post-acceptance tree may correlate
with that sample; fresh corrected future proposals remain valid. Finite-grid
singleton refinement and positive likelihood give almost-sure termination;
expected output cost needs the evidence bound explicitly.

For B=2^d children per split, certifier width<=C2^{-j}, and preproposal
log-likelihood sensitivities Lambda_a, the retained distinct source count obeys
 E N <= min(full_tree,
             (B^{H+1}-1)/(B-1)+2BC2^{-H} E sum_a Lambda_a).
Optimizing fixed H gives O_d(1+(C E sum Lambda_a)^{d/(d+1)}), capped by the
finite tree. The factor B is exponential in d. For output requests, a sufficient
replacement is E sum Lambda_t/c_t, with c_t=Z_L/Z_U acquired at task start.
Groups of q_t outputs require q_t Lambda_t/c_t. These are expectation bounds,
not confidence certificates obtained by plugging in realized adaptive sums.

The bound counts source executions. It does not hide likelihood conversion,
proposal maintenance, exact arithmetic/bit sizes, random bits, storage, rejection
trials, validation, or discovery work. C grows conservatively as 4^K for this
interval program. Reference: EXACT_CONTRACT_AND_INFORMATION_GATE.txt and
AMORTIZED_SOURCE_THEOREM.txt, independently checked by root and luna_ctmc.
