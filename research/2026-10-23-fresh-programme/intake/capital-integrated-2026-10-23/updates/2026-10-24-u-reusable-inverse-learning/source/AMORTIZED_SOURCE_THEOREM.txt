UNIFORM CROSS-TASK SOURCE-CERTIFICATE BOUND
24 October 2026. Lead proof, independently challenged by luna_ctmc.
This is a source-node count, not wall time, an all-algorithm optimum, or a
claim of a new adaptive-rejection principle.

Contract

A deterministic nested dyadic certifier over a d-dimensional box has B=2^d
children per split, common maximum depth J, and interval width at most
C 2^{-j} for each depth-j node. Splitting a node executes every child once;
memoization prevents a second source execution at a visited node. At depth J
the finite-grid interval is exact. The nested interval for a node is a fixed
function of the known program, node, and ancestor intervals, independent of
the future random tape. Child likelihood bounds are exact extrema over that
interval, or at least have the stated ratio bound below.

Before proposal a, its task/prior/tree may depend on all past results. Its
weight W_a is positive on the forward range and log-Lipschitz with constant
Lambda_a, measurable before this proposal. The sampler uses the old envelope
height U_old throughout its pending decision, one independent threshold u,
and the exact proposal pi_C U_C. The current task stays fixed until its one
requested posterior output is completed; later tasks may be fully adaptive.

Theorem

Let N be the total distinct executed source nodes through a deterministic
number A of proposals, including the root. For every integer H in [0,J],

 E N <= min { (B^{J+1}-1)/(B-1),
              (B^{H+1}-1)/(B-1)
                + 2 B C 2^{-H} E[sum_{a=1}^A Lambda_a] }.             (3)

If H=J, the fine term can be set to zero. The same bound holds for a stopping
number of proposals with E sum Lambda_a finite, where the event that proposal
a is requested is determined by its preproposal history. This includes the
first A_M trials needed for M finite posterior outputs under the integrability
condition below.

Proof

Condition on the entire history immediately before a proposal. For any cell
D at depth j below its chosen original leaf, exact extrema and log-Lipschitz
give
 U_D-L_D <= U_D(1-exp(-Lambda_a width I_D))
          <= U_D min(1,Lambda_a C 2^{-j}).
Since the intervals are nested, U_D<=U_old. The event that the same uniform
threshold remains unresolved at D has length
 (U_D-L_D)/U_old <= min(1,Lambda_a C 2^{-j}).             (4)
Do not condition on survival at the previous depth and treat (4) as a fresh
coin. The original threshold and nested uncertainty intervals prove the
unconditional-within-this-proposal bound directly. A particular depth has
at most one candidate-path split, so its expected count is at most (4),
whether that cell was previously known or now needs a split. Already known
nodes can only reduce newly executed children.

Charge all possible node executions through depth H pessimistically. Their
number is at most (B^{H+1}-1)/(B-1). Every remaining split is at depth j>=H,
costs B new child executions at most, and has expected occurrence bounded
by (4). The fine-depth contribution for this proposal is at most
 B Lambda_a C sum_{j=H}^{J-1}2^{-j} <= 2 B Lambda_a C 2^{-H}.
Conditional expectation, summation over proposals, and Fubini prove (3).
The full finite tree gives the other cap. The stopping version uses the same
conditional argument multiplied by the predictable indicator that proposal
a is requested. No source/task independence assumption is needed beyond
fresh proposal/threshold randomness and fixed deterministic certificates.

Consequences and their costs

Write S=E sum Lambda_a. Optimizing H up to dyadic rounding gives
 E N = O_d(1+(C S)^{d/(d+1)}), capped by the full finite tree.
The hidden constants include exponential B=2^d. This is not a dimension-free
or polynomial-in-d claim. When a single bound Lambda applies, S<=Lambda A.
For the logistic code C=(5/6)4^K-1/12, so its conservative guarantee has an
exponential-in-K conditioning cost. A true exact source call is not unit-cost
arithmetic: rational endpoint bit lengths grow under repeated squaring.

For M completed tasks, let Lambda_t be its constant and c_t=Z_L/Z_U its
positive acquired acceptance lower certificate at that task's start.
Conditionally, E[number of its proposals | task-start history] <=1/c_t.
Thus one sufficient replacement is
 S <= E sum_{t=1}^M Lambda_t/c_t,                         (5)
when the RHS is finite. This keeps evidence/sharpness costs visible. If all
tasks have a deterministic Lambda<=Lambda_max and c_t>=c_min>0, source count
is O_d(1+(C Lambda_max M/c_min)^{d/(d+1)}). Neither c_min nor mild Lambda is
free for an arbitrary inverse problem. A small epsilon makes weights positive
but c_min can still be exponentially poor in count n.

These are expectation bounds. A realized adaptive run's sum Lambda_t/c_t
cannot silently replace E sum Lambda_t/c_t as a guaranteed total budget or a
high-probability certificate. Its current c_t is a valid task-start acceptance
bound, but future task choices and certificates remain random. Likewise the
optimizer uses S=E sum Lambda_a or a deterministic bound on that quantity;
choosing H afterward from a realized stopping count is not a new conditional
expectation theorem. No confidence claim about total runtime is being made.

If an execution groups q_t posterior outputs under one fixed task label and
computes c_t only at that group's start, the corresponding sufficient RHS is
E sum_t q_t Lambda_t/c_t, because refinement cannot worsen that starting
acceptance bound. Equivalently index each output as one task in (5). The
finite replay's eight task labels contain 16 outputs each; it has 128 output
requests, not eight, for this cost conversion.

Relation to covering and reuse

The argument balances finite spatial coverage against the rarity of needing
fine certification for a random exact accept/reject threshold. The same
executed forward node can serve any later task. Forgetting all nodes would
pay another source acquisition cost, but it is a weak sole comparator. A
competent retained Moore/interval sampler has this same reusable access and
may inherit the bound. A* and adaptive branch-bound controls may use bounds
more efficiently. This theorem is useful only as a precise sufficient cost
principle for exact learning from acquired forward certificates; it does not
establish advantage over those methods or optimal unknown-source acquisition.

Costs omitted by N and required in an executable comparison

* all rejected proposals and each task's likelihood-bound conversions;
* categorical/tree maintenance and within-cell sampling;
* each source execution's interval iterations and endpoint arithmetic;
* exact rational numerators/denominators and storage, not merely node count;
* ideal random bits and a.s.-terminating rational threshold comparisons;
* source/refinement failures and validation/evaluator enumeration;
* any discovery/symbolic-preprocessing effort outside the selected policy.

The finite exact replay and example are checks of this contract, not a
statistical power study, empirical proof of the exponent, physical-model
validation, or a broad scientific-computation benchmark.
