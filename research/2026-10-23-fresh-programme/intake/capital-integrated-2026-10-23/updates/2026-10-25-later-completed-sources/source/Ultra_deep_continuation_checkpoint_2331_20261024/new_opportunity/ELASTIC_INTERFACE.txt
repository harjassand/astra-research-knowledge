ELASTIC IONIC SKIN AND COMPACTING INTERLAYER
Owned physical continuation, 24 October 2026

The operation being constructed is one-stroke suppression and leveling of metal
deposition roughness by a local increase of ionic resistance, while a continuous
solid ionic skin prevents deposition from simply entering the membrane pores.
The ordinary identical skin and mechanical support get the same properties.
This is a conditional coupled-interface construction, not an acquired lithium
cell or a claim of priority over pressure-dependent pore closure.

1. Why both conductivity and mechanics have to be kept

SPREADING_SKIN.txt derived the exact ionic transfer when the two skin faces move
by the same h. A bending film cannot be treated as a plate with stiffness B q^4
at arbitrarily large q b, nor do its two faces necessarily move identically.
This note removes those two assumptions within linear isotropic elasticity.
It also records the separate finite-amplitude column limit precisely.

Let b be the flat skin thickness, k_n,k_t its normal/tangential ionic
conductivities, j0>0 the mean deposition current, and r>=0 the differential
charge-transfer resistance. The skin has no electronic leakage. Concentration
polarization and electrochemical side reactions are excluded from this model.
Potentials below are electrochemical driving potentials; a separately counted
conservative mechanical energy is not counted again as an arbitrary pressure
shift. Write nu_m=Omega_m/(z F)>0 for deposited metal height per areal charge.

The bottom-face displacement is h, top-face displacement v. Above the skin is
a locally confined porous skeleton with fixed upper backing. Its compression is
controlled by v, not automatically by h. At the flat state its ionic resistance
and derivative are R>0 and R_v; its tangent normal mechanical foundation is K_w.
The remaining electrolyte has Fourier impedance Z_b(q)>0. L(q)=R+Z_b(q).

Constitutive qualification after whole review: k_n and k_t in E4 are fixed
Eulerian coefficients. If crystalline anisotropy axes rotate with the deforming
skin, the first-order off-diagonal conductivity produces a distributed ionic
source; E4 is not that different constitutive law. This is load-bearing for
anisotropic films, not a higher-order correction. Likewise E9's compressive
preload passes through the skin. E1 is the prescribed unstressed incremental
operator used by this model, not an acquired exact tangent of that loaded film.
The small ratio p_s/E about6.7e-5 does not by itself give a uniform error bound
on initial-stress or material-axis effects. Both are outstanding physical gates.

2. Exact elastic normal two-port, with its boundary conditions

Assume an unstressed isotropic elastic skin, Young modulus E, Poisson ratio
v_p, -1<v_p<1/2, and quasistatic plane strain for a Fourier mode q>0. Both faces
have zero tangential traction. Normal displacements are prescribed; subsequently
the top face is equilibrated against the skeleton. These are explicit mechanical
boundary conditions. A bonded, tangentially constrained lithium substrate or a
prestressed crystalline film requires the appropriate different elastic operator.

Set l=q b/2 and c_e=(1-v_p^2)/E. The normal displacement-to-traction matrix has
equal diagonal entries a and off-diagonal entries c, with eigenvalues

 k_+ = q/(2 c_e) [tanh(l)-l sech(l)^2],
 k_- = q/(2 c_e) [coth(l)+l csch(l)^2],
 a=(k_++k_-)/2, c=(k_+-k_-)/2<0.                         (E1)

The tractions are forces conjugate to upward displacement on each face. Thus
the matrix is positive, rather than assigning both face stresses the same
outward-normal convention. One derivation uses Airy stress F(z) exp(i q x):
F''''-2q^2 F''+q^4 F=0, shear-free F'=0 on both faces, and
w=(1-v_p^2) F'''/(E q^2) there. The odd Airy solution gives equal face
displacements and k_+; the even solution gives opposite displacements and k_-.

Top equilibrium c h+(a+K_w)v=0 gives

 m(q)=v/h=-c/(a+K_w),
 k_eff(q)=a-c^2/(a+K_w).                                  (E2)

For K_w>=0, 0<m<1 and k_eff>0. The same skeleton supplies both resistance
feedback and a mechanical force; neither may be erased in the comparator.
At q->0, with M=E/(1-v_p^2),

 m0=1/[1+K_w b/M],  k_eff(0)=K_w m0.

With K_w=0 and q b<<1, k_eff=B q^4+o(q^4), where
B=E b^3/[12(1-v_p^2)]. At q b>>1, m->0 and
k_eff~E q/[2(1-v_p^2)]. The upper porous layer therefore stops following very
short bottom-face corrugations, while the skin has a positive half-space elastic
restoring force. This replaces extrapolation of a thin-plate q^4 law to infinity.

The elastic force becomes a reaction voltage through

 chi(q)=Gamma q^2+nu_m k_eff(q), Gamma>0.                  (E3)

This follows by varying the stored film/skeleton energy with respect to metal
height and using hdot=nu_m j. It assumes this height is the actual mechanical
interface coordinate. A fluid/SEI volume source, independent delamination mode,
or mechanical transport of metal needs extra states and cannot be hidden in
nu_m. Ionic stress contributions must be included in the electrochemical
potential used by the transport law, not added twice.

3. Exact coupled ionic transfer for unequal face motion

Put q_s=q sqrt(k_t/k_n), p=q sqrt(k_n k_t), C=cosh(q_s b),
S=sinh(q_s b), G=j0/k_n. The ionic perturbation u obeys
k_n u''-k_t q^2 u=0, with physical moving-face evaluations

 u(0)=r j_b+(chi-G)h,
 u(b)=W-G v,
 W=-L j_t-j0 R_v v.

The transfer identities are

 W=(r C+S/p)j_b+C(chi-G)h+G v,
 j_t=(C+r p S)j_b+p S(chi-G)h.

Consequently

 j_b/h = N(q)/D(q),
 N=(G-chi)(C+L p S)-m(G+j0 R_v),
 D=r C+S/p+L(C+r p S)>0.                                 (E4)

Setting m=1 recovers S1. Equation E4 includes thickness compression of the skin,
the actual transmitted pore displacement, nonlocal ionic conduction and the
same skeleton's conservative mechanical restoring force. It is a complete
linear normal-mode model under the declared interface conditions.

For R_v>=0, m>=0 and chi>=G, stability is immediate. At long wavelength the
numerator is

 N(0)=G(1-m0)-nu_m K_w m0-m0 j0 R_v.                     (E5)

The first term is skin-thickness shortening; simply setting both faces equal
would omit it. The complete criterion has deposition-volume and material units.
The uniform mode is removed by galvanostatic centering; E5 describes its
long-wave limit.

4. An explicit all-q sufficient certificate for the elastic model

The following certificate avoids a numerical sampling claim. Choose q_bar>0
with Gamma q_bar^2+nu_m k_+(q_bar)>=G. Since k_+ is increasing,
k_eff>=k_+ and Gamma q^2 is increasing, all q>=q_bar are stable when R_v>=0.
Monotonicity follows directly by differentiating k_+: both
tanh(l)-l sech(l)^2 and its positive derivative are nonnegative.

For 0<=q<=q_bar let k_minus_0=2/(b c_e), k_plus_bar=k_+(q_bar), and require
k_minus_0>k_plus_bar. k_- >= k_minus_0. For example, the partial-fraction
identity

 l coth(l)+l^2 csch(l)^2
   =2+4 l^4 sum_{n>=1}(l^2+pi^2 n^2)^(-2)

proves this and its monotonicity. Hence

 m(q)>=m_min=(k_minus_0-k_plus_bar)/
                     (k_minus_0+k_plus_bar+2 K_w)>0.

Let l_bar=q_bar b/2 and

 c_B=[tanh(l_bar)/l_bar] sech(l_bar)^2 /
                         [1+k_plus_bar/k_minus_0].

The integral identity
tanh(l)-l sech(l)^2=integral_0^l 2t tanh(t) sech(t)^2 dt
implies 2k_+>=B q^4 [tanh(l_bar)/l_bar] sech(l_bar)^2.
The harmonic-mean formula for k_eff at K_w=0, and
d k_eff/d K_w=m^2, then give

 k_eff(q)>=c_B B q^4+K_w m_min^2.                        (E6)

Suppose L(q)<=L_max. Set y=q^2, y_bar=q_bar^2,
H(y)=cosh(b sqrt(k_t/k_n) sqrt(y))
     +L_max sqrt(k_n k_t) sqrt(y)
                         sinh(b sqrt(k_t/k_n) sqrt(y)).
Its positive power series makes H convex in y and H(0)=1. Therefore
H(y)<=1+K y, K=[H(y_bar)-1]/y_bar, on this whole interval.
Define w0=1-nu_m K_w m_min^2/G, a0=Gamma/G,
c0=nu_m c_B B/G. Where chi<G, the numerator is bounded above by

 N/G <= w0+(w0 K-a0)y-(c0+a0 K)y^2-c0 K y^3
                         -m_min(1+k_n R_v).             (E7)

Where chi>=G, N is already negative. Thus it suffices that the maximum of
this explicit cubic on [0,y_bar] is strictly below m_min(1+k_n R_v).
The maximum is evaluated at the endpoints and the real quadratic roots of
its derivative. Exact rational upper bounds on the hyperbolic factors and
Bernstein coefficients can certify a chosen fixture; floating root searches
alone do not certify it. This is a sufficient inequality, not a necessary
modulus threshold.

5. Signed membrane stress: separate plate certificate and buckling gate

The original Li3N paper explicitly reports cooling-induced compressive stress.
Consequently its film cannot be identified with the unstressed E1 merely from
its thickness and ionic conductivity. In the thin-plate regime a signed
membrane force T0, tension positive, changes

 chi=chi0+(Gamma+nu_m T0)q^2+nu_m B q^4.

Set a0=(Gamma+nu_m T0)/G, c0=nu_m B/G and
w0=1-chi0/G. On a finite interval ending after the last positive chi=G
root, the same positive-series chord bound gives the cubic

 (1-chi/G)H-1 <= (w0-1)+(w0 K-a0)y
                              -(c0+a0 K)y^2-c0 K y^3.    (E8)

This retains compression; dropping chi when chi<0 would reverse a needed
inequality. For chi0=0 the positive root is
y*=[-A0+sqrt(A0^2+4 nu_m B G)]/(2 nu_m B),
A0=Gamma+nu_m T0. The simpler, looser bound is
(1-chi_min/G)H(y*)-1, chi_min=-min(A0,0)^2/(4nu_m B).

This signed plate calculation does not extend E1 to a prestressed thick layer.
Nor does a stable deposition equation prove mechanical contact stability.
A plate on a positive Winkler support is mechanically nonnegative if
K_w>=max(-T0,0)^2/(4B); otherwise buckling/creep can alter height even at
zero Faradaic current. General residual stress requires the actual initial-stress
elastic boundary-value problem. This is a remaining gate for the primary film.

6. A constitutive skeleton that pays force as well as resistance

An explicit design model is a confined array of incompressible, axially
compressed soft solid pillars, leaving straight liquid-filled pores. The cell
area is fixed. The solid volume per cell area is s, the pillar reference length
d_ref, the current pore/skeleton thickness d. Under the uniaxial neo-Hookean
ansatz lambda=d/d_ref,

 epsilon=1-s/d,
 R(d)=d^2/[k_l(d-s)],
 R_v=[s^2/(d-s)^2-1]/k_l,
 E_s(d)=s mu/2 [lambda^2+2/lambda-3],
 p_s=-d E_s/dd=(s/d_ref)mu(lambda^-2-lambda),
 K_w=d p_s/dv=(s mu/d_ref^2)(2lambda^-3+1).              (E9)

Thus the force needed to compress the pores is not free. The ansatz needs
lateral confinement, attachment, stable nonbuckling pillars and a real drainage
outlet. It is not the constitutive law of a measured separator. The pore-current
pattern still requires the actual skin microcontact problem; a continuous film
with no defects is an essential physical resource.

One declared design fixture uses s=20 micrometres, d=s/.9, d_ref=2s,
k_l=1 S/m, mu=1 MPa. Then epsilon=.1, R=.22222 milliohm m^2,
R_v=80 ohm m, p_s=1.3422 MPa, K_w=1.5830e11 Pa/m. Set b=20 micrometres,
k_n=.052 S/m, k_t/k_n=10, E=20 GPa, v_p=.3, Gamma=5e-11 V m,
j0=10 A/m^2, r=1e-4 ohm m^2, and a 20-micrometre bulk with k_b=.5 S/m.
E, k_t, stress and the skeleton are supplied design specifications. In
particular they are NOT properties acquired together for the reported Li3N film.

At q=10000/m the exact E1--E4 ordinary identical-skin comparator with R_v
artificially held zero, but the SAME R, K_w, E, conductivities and thickness,
has N/G about +.17908 for the actual finite-bulk impedance. Including the
derived compression response changes it to about -3.98005. This is a matched
mechanism attribution within this model.
It is not an experimental comparison with a separator of identical mechanics
but independently tunable electrical derivative. Ordinary coatings and elastic
electrolytes may already be stable in other parameter ranges or finite devices.
The all-q certificate E7 has substantial margin for this fixture. The saved
elastic_interface.py uses exact rational Taylor/remainder bounds for the
hyperbolic functions, a rational lower m, and 128 rational Bernstein polynomial
bounds. Its certified left side is greater than5.15079 and right side less
than1.30476. A separate rational lower bound on the ordinary-skin numerator at
q=10000/m is N/G>.17880, using the actual bulk impedance bound
tanh(x)>=x-x^3/3. Thus the matched instability witness also avoids inferring
an unstable band from a grid. These are certificates for the declared model
parameters, not confidence intervals for measured material properties.

7. Rate and finite mechanical amplitude matter

The long-wave leveling exponent per deposited mean height H_mean is

 d log|h_q|/dH_mean -> N(0)/(j0 D0),
 D0=r+b/k_n+L0,

with the exact N(0) in E5. Its positive leveling length is
ell_level=j0 D0/[-N(0)] when N(0)<0. This charges the series resistance of the
protective skin. The earlier s=1 micrometre,d=1.6 micrometres fixture with a
20-micrometre film levels only after roughly 0.3 mm of mean deposition;
a negative sign alone did not give a useful plating operation.
For the E9 fixture ell_level is about 9.1 micrometres, including skeleton
mechanics and the finite normal compliance of the skin. The skin's normal
ohmic drop is 3.846 mV and the pore drop is 2.222 mV at the stated j0.
The actual BV drop must use eta(j0), not r*j0.
The one-e-fold time is about6750seconds at10A/m^2, so this example is a gradual
leveling operation, not rapid erasure of macroscopic defects.

At q=10000/m and height amplitude 100 nm, qh=.001, q b=.2 and relative
pore-length change is about .0045. The R_v variation is nevertheless about
ten percent because d-s is only 2.22 micrometres. The film bending strain
scale is b q^2 h/2=1e-4; incremental normal loads are much smaller than
the declared 1.34-MPa preload. These are consistency scales, not fracture,
adhesion or nonlinear-stability certificates. At pore wavelengths b>>a_pore,
a thin-plate a_pore^4/B sag formula is inappropriate; a three-dimensional
elastic contact/half-space calculation is required.

The low-porosity design pays a narrow finite compression range. A gap must
stay d>s and within the R_v>0 branch d<2s for the entire protrusion range.
Mean deposition requires the global backing/stack to accommodate mean metal
advance while retaining the declared base compression. Actuation, pressure
work, drainage time and finite contact on stripping remain physical costs.
An ordinary reversible fixed-geometry charge/strip cycle reverses the Ohmic
leveling flow; no stable rechargeable cycle is inferred.

8. Exact finite-amplitude result for the separately declared column limit

There is a useful nonlinear operation, but its scope is narrower than E4.
Assume the local effective branch resistance R_tot(h_i)>0 is actually acquired,
the thin skin follows h and conducts locally in the normal direction, and the
remaining reciprocal bulk network has Y=gI+L_graph, g>0. A nonlocal conservative
mechanical energy E_mech(h) is allowed. The constitutive law is

 u_i=eta(j_i)+j_i R_tot(h_i)+nu_m partial_i E_mech(h).

On mean-zero h define

 F(h)=j0 sum_i integral_0^{h_i}[R_tot(x)-R_tot(0)] dx
                 +nu_m[E_mech(h)-E_mech(0)].             (E10)

With the positive current secants D_i and the matrix A from CONSTRUCTION.txt,
the exact current law is delta j=-A(h) grad F and

 Fdot=-nu_m (grad F)^T A(h) grad F<=0.                    (E11)

The rank-one galvanostatic projection is included in A; noncommuting plate
bending and A do not justify replacing F by Euclidean height variance.
If on a convex height domain the restricted Hessian of F is >=alpha I>0 and
D_i+||Y^-1||<=K_max, then

 F(t)<=F(0) exp[-2 nu_m alpha t/K_max].                   (E12)

Here also require P grad E_mech(0)=0, so the flat state is stationary on the
mean-zero subspace. This holds for the stated homogeneous plate and uniform
skeleton. For an arbitrary conservative energy without this condition, use
the shifted equilibrium and F-F_min instead; convexity alone does not place
the minimizer at zero or justify the subsequent centered-height bound.

For E_mech=(1/(2nu_m))h^T[A0 L_space+nu_m B L_space^2]h,
a sufficient convexity condition is
alpha=j0 inf R_tot' - max(-A0,0)^2/(4nu_m B)>0.
The true skeleton E_s may be included and improves the Hessian when convex.
For |h_i|<a, F(0)<alpha a^2/2 prevents reaching the domain boundary, since
F>=alpha||h||^2/2 there. This is a finite-amplitude invariant sublevel and
convergence statement, not merely a dispersion relation.

It does NOT establish E10 for a spatially conducting deforming skin. E4 closes
that actual coupled interface only to linear order. Uniformly stable frozen
parameter states are also not a proof of a nonlinear free-boundary basin.
Further finite-amplitude claims must control the moving-domain conductor,
normal/thickness strain and mechanical contact. These are retained scientific
gaps rather than assumed equivalence to the column operation.

Implementation/proof separation

ELASTIC_INTERFACE_RESULTS.json records the two exact-rational inequalities and
their fractions. In addition,24 independent4x4 Airy boundary solves agree with
E1 to relative1.57e-15, and independent ionic boundary solves agree with E4 to
absolute2.22e-16 in the tested dimensionless cases. Those finite diagnostics
check signs and implementation. The all-q assertion follows from E6--E7 and
the rational inequalities; the finite-amplitude column theorem follows from
E10--E12. No finite diagnostic supplies a nonlinear continuum skin theorem.

Primary-source and prior-art boundary

Zhang et al. 2019, DOI10.1149/2.0701914jes, already proposes pressure-induced
pore closure redirecting ionic transport, and distinguishes drained from trapped
electrolyte. The current construction does not claim that mechanism as new.
Haataja et al. 2003, DOI10.1149/1.1602456, supplies the strong ordinary chemical
leveler comparator; charged/elastic electrolytes and gradient hosts remain
independent conventional routes.

Li et al., ACS Central Science4(2018)97--104,
https://doi.org/10.1021/acscentsci.7b00480,
https://web.stanford.edu/group/cui_group/papers/Yanbin_Cui_ACSCENTSCI_2018.pdf,
reports textured bonded Li3N films, thickness2--30 micrometres, a measured
normal ionic conductivity up to .052S/m for a20-micrometre liquid-cell fixture,
mechanical bending and retained coating coverage during cycling. The reported
all-solid conductivity fixture was a different200-micrometre film. No same-film
E, in-plane conductivity tensor or signed residual-stress magnitude was acquired
here. Their stated thermal compression prevents treating their film as an
unstressed isotropic elastic layer. A bulk elastic calculation or an old
single-crystal anisotropy measurement is not a same-film conjunction.

The substantive result is the conditional coupled transfer, its explicit all-q
certificate and paid rate/interface requirements, together with a distinct
finite-amplitude column operation. No new physical device, material validation,
rechargeable-cycle stability or advantage over equally informed conventional
design is established.
