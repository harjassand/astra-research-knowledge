Problem and positive construction

The selected missing capability is suppressing geometry-driven electrodeposition
roughening without assuming a flat interface or a nanoscale observation/control
oracle. Fixed positive conductivity grading alone preserves long-wave focusing.
A compacting porous interlayer can increase ionic resistance at a protrusion;
a continuous ionic skin is needed so metal does not simply enter the pores.
Pressure-induced pore closure already belongs to Zhang2019; no priority claim
is made for that mechanism. The owned work has become a coupled transport/load
framework and paid design envelope, rather than a new material claim.

The load path is now derived instead of setting the two skin faces equal. For
an explicitly unstressed isotropic plane-strain skin with shear-free faces, its
normal two-port has eigenvalues

 k+=q/[2c_e](tanh l-l sech²l),
 k-=q/[2c_e](coth l+l csch²l),
 l=qb/2, c_e=(1-v_p²)/E.

For a=(k++k-)/2, c=(k+-k-)/2 and skeleton tangent K_w,
m=v_top/h=-c/(a+K_w) and k_eff=a-c²/(a+K_w). Thus 0<m<1; high-q motion no
longer compresses the distant pores, while the film develops half-space stiffness
E q/[2(1-v_p²)]. This avoids extrapolating a thin-plate q^4 law to infinity.

For fixed Eulerian ionic coefficients, the exact linear moving-face numerator is

 N_frozen=(G-chi)(C+LpS)-m(G+j0R_v),
 chi=Gamma q²+nu_m k_eff, G=j0/k_n,
 C=cosh(qb sqrt(rho)), S=sinh(qb sqrt(rho)),
 p=q sqrt(k_n k_t), rho=k_t/k_n,
 D=rC+S/p+L(C+rpS)>0,  delta j/h=N/D.

The skeleton's ionic derivative R_v and its conservative restoring K_w are both
included and both retained by the identical-skin comparator.

Material rotation corrected the first comparison

For a physical tensor with strain-independent intrinsic eigenvalues whose axes
rotate with the skin, the ionic ODE has a first-order distributed source.
The elastic rotation amplitude Rrot is harmonic, with endpoints1,m. The exact
particular potential -G Rrot cancels the moving-face base-field shifts. Hence

 N_rot=-chi(C+LpS)-j0mR_v
       -j0[(S/p+LC)Rrot'(0)-L Rrot'(b)],
 Rrot'(0)=q[m-cosh(qb)]/sinh(qb),
 Rrot'(b)=q[m cosh(qb)-1]/sinh(qb).

For rho>=1 a positive Green-kernel argument proves N_rot<=N_frozen. The old
responsive all-q sufficient certificate therefore survives this constitutive
repair. Its old ordinary-coating instability witness does not survive as a
physical comparison: the corrected ordinary coating can already be stable.

The pure-electrical long-wave optimum is explicit. With closure margin A=d-s,
epsilon=A/d<1/2 and other series resistance R_fixed,

 R=A/(k_l epsilon²), R_v=(1-2epsilon)R/A,
 ell=A(1+R_fixed/R)/(1-2epsilon)>A.

Under R<=R_max and fixed A, the optimum uses the budget and gives

further constraint. A finite protrusion a needs A>a, and the voltage budget
must hold at the most compressed patch. Gauge-factor gain is therefore not a
free or arbitrarily rapid leveling operation.

Central physical uncertainty and next substantive gate

Li2018 supplies an actual bonded2--30micrometre Li3N coating, normal EIS
conductivity up to.052S/m for one20micrometre liquid-cell fixture, and bending/
cycling coverage. It supplies neither same-film E/tangent, conductivity tensor,
strain dependence nor a measured residual-stress magnitude. Its inferred cooling
compression matters. Even the synthetic wall preload must pass through the
skin, so an unstressed operator is not automatically the exact loaded tangent;
p0/E alone is not a uniform geometric-stiffness bound. Signed plate stress was
derived separately but is not a thick-film constitutive repair.

The actual pore/film interface still needs contact, drainage and suppression of
metal intrusion/delamination. An attractive micro-Fourier smoothing bound is
not being promoted to a pore-homogenization proof: large pore-current contrast
changes the base field and can couple to strain/rotation at first order.
The next justified attack is a loaded, strain-dependent, heterogeneous-interface
closure with an error bound relative to the strict stability margin. No further
fictional material conjunction or curve sampling can supply that step.

Fixed reversible R_v>0 feedback reverses on stripping. Stable rechargeable
cycling, apparatus/material realization, novelty and matched practical advantage
remain unestablished. Conventional pressure mechanisms, chemical levelers,
protective films and gradient hosts are kept as equally informed alternatives.
