SAME-FILM PROPERTY GATE — Li3N coating evidence

Bounded question

Does the demonstrated molten-Li-grown, textured Li3N coating supply, on the same comparable film, a usable elastic/tangent modulus, transport tensor, measured signed residual stress, and direct electronic-blocking measurement? No: the primary demonstrates a thick, bonded coating with useful effective ionic transport and qualitative bend robustness, but does not acquire that joint constitutive/property set. The missing loaded tangent and strain-dependent conductivity remain open physical inputs; do not substitute bulk/single-crystal or separate-film values.

Primary 1 — Li et al., ACS Central Science 4, 97–104 (2018), “Robust Pinhole-free Li3N Solid Electrolyte Grown from Molten Lithium,” DOI 10.1021/acscentsci.7b00480. PubMed full-text link and abstract/figure captions: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29392181/ . Author-hosted manuscript indexed at https://web.stanford.edu/group/cui_group/papers/Yanbin_Cui_ACSCENTSCI_2018.pdf (direct open timed out; targeted search returned the manuscript text and figures). 

What this specimen actually supplies:

- Nitridation of clean molten Li in N2 at 450 °C produces an alpha-Li3N layer directly bonded to Li. It has large, strongly interconnected grains, tens of micrometres, and strong [001] texture with (001) planes parallel to the metal surface (c axis normal). The paper describes controllable practical film thicknesses of 2–30 µm.
- Its bend experiment with SEM shows no film failure at curvature radius 0.8 cm; the comparison nanofilm cracks. This is an empirical robustness check for the Li-backed thick film, not a modulus, yield strain, fracture energy, adhesion energy, or measured force-displacement/tangent-stiffness curve.
- The authors infer built-in compressive film stress on cooling from 450 °C because Li has a higher thermal-expansion coefficient than Li3N. This is a thermal-mismatch explanation; the paper reports no numerical residual-stress value alongside the bend data.
- EIS supplies a scalar effective ionic conductivity, not a tensor: 5.2×10^-4 S cm^-1 (0.052 S m^-1) for a 20-µm film in liquid-electrolyte symmetric cells; a separate 200-µm film/Al-buffer all-solid-state test gives 2.3×10^-4 S cm^-1 (0.023 S m^-1). The former is the closer coating-scale result. The paper does not report a matched through-plane/in-plane pair or strain-dependent conductivity. It is not a 10-nm skin demonstration: 2–30 µm is 200–3000 times 10 nm.
- The article's introductory statement that Li3N generally has electronic conductivity below 10^-12 S cm^-1 is cited background (reference 27), not a separate electronic-leakage measurement on this textured coating. The measured symmetric-cell result is used to infer ionic conductivity.

Primary 2 — von Alpen, Rabenau & Talat, Applied Physics Letters 30, 621–623 (1977), “Ionic conductivity in Li3N single crystals,” DOI 10.1063/1.89283. Article metadata/abstract and direct publisher-PDF link: https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1361418518924682368 . This is a separate Czochralski single-crystal transport measurement: the highest Li-ion conductivity is perpendicular to the hexagonal c axis; anisotropy is about one to two decades over 20–200 °C, with 10^-3 S cm^-1 reported parallel to the Li2N planes at ambient temperature. It makes the texture orientation physically relevant (the high-conduction direction is in-plane if the single-crystal tensor carries over), but it is not the same polycrystalline Li-backed film and does not give that film's tensor. Do not use it to overwrite the 2018 film's measured effective through-plane number.

Acquisition boundary / implication

In the bounded primary check, no same-film numerical E or tangent response, biaxial/residual-stress value, electronic conductivity, or strain-dependent conductivity tensor was found. The 2018 result does demonstrate an actual bonded, pinhole-free, micrometre-scale Li3N layer that survives substantial bending while passing Li ions; it does not furnish the parameter conjunction needed to instantiate the proposed thin mechanically spanning skin. Bulk mechanical constants, generic Li3N electronic-conductivity values, or single-crystal anisotropy remain separate priors. This is an evidence boundary, not a claim that these measurements do not exist elsewhere.
