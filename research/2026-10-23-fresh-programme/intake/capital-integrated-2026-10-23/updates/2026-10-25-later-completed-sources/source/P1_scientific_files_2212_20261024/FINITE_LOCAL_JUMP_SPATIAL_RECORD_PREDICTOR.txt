PRIVATE ASTRA P1 — A FINITE LOCAL JUMP PREDICTOR FOR MONITORED BOSONIC MATTER
2026-10-24. New restricted constructive theorem. Earlier source files unchanged.

WHAT THIS CLOSES
================
The local clipped covariance has a useful spatial tail bound, but its
continuous CIR/Gaussian laws cannot be assigned to a numerical jump process.
This construction uses the implemented process's OWN aggregate moment
recurrence and compares its generator DIRECTLY with the physical quantum
instrument on coherent projectors. All state-changing primitives act on one
or two modes. It includes on-site quartics, cross-density edges, hopping,
finite-bin passive counts and permitted feedback. Uniform finite-order
moments follow in a strong-damping regime.

This is a positive recursively evaluable record law with paid arithmetic,
random bits and action count. It is not an economical posterior after every
given record, an observed experimental advantage, or a claimed first noisy
phase-space simulation method. Its state is the finite current field plus the
actual controller/latch/numerical state; its predictive law is the ensemble.

ordinary on-site Bose-Hubbard term is (theta w_i/2)N_i(N_i-1); a convention
using N_i^2 adds its actual theta-dependent linear rotation to omega_i.

2. ALLOCATED LOCAL COVARIANCE, INCLUDING ON-SITE TERMS
====================================================
Assign positive bath allocations a_i to nonzero on-site terms and a_e to
nonzero cross edges, with

 a_i+sum_(e incident i)a_e<=1.

Absent terms have zero allocation and are omitted. Choose 0<eta<=1. Define

 B_i=eta kappa nu a_i/(theta w_i,max),
 B_e=eta kappa nu a_e/(theta g_e,max),
 chi_i=min(1,B_i/I_i),
 chi_e=min(1,B_e/sqrt(I_i I_j)).                          (3)

Use chi=1 at a zero denominator. Let C_i have its single entry
(C_i)_ii=w_i z_i^2, and C_e have entries
(C_e)_ij=(C_e)_ji=g_e z_i z_j. Let Dq,b denote the usual realification
(theta/2)[[Im C_b,-Re C_b],[-Re C_b,-Im C_b]]. Set

 Dhat=(kappa nu/2)I+sum_i chi_i Dq,i+sum_e chi_e Dq,e.     (4)

It splits into positive 2-real-coordinate on-site blocks, positive
4-real-coordinate edge blocks, and a nonnegative diagonal remainder:

 D_b=(kappa nu a_b/2)Pi_b+chi_b Dq,b.

On its support, 0<=D_b<=kappa nu a_b I. This permits eta=1; no SDE
ellipticity or uniqueness theorem is needed for the finite jump law.

Factor each local block and the diagonal remainder separately. Denote the
resulting embedded real columns by v_l(x), l=1,...,L. There are at most

 L<=4|E_g|+2(number of nonzero on-site terms)+2m.           (5)

Columns act on at most two modes. Zero columns can be kept as charged identity
transitions or omitted. The exact identities needed below are, for each mode
i with its real two-vector x_i,

 sum_l |v_(i,l)|^2=kappa nu,
 sum_l (x_i.v_(i,l))^2=(kappa nu/2)I_i,
 max_(i,l)|v_(i,l)|^2<=Dcol=kappa nu,
 max_l |v_l|^2<=Dcol,
 sum_l |v_l|^2=m kappa nu.                               (6)

They hold for cross and on-site density quartics: the quantum covariance has
zero intensity drift and zero intensity/intensity quadratic variation. In
particular they do not require a thermal conditional state.

There is an explicit stable local square root. If Q_b=chi_b Dq,b and
alpha_b=kappa nu a_b/2, then Q_b^2=r_b^2 I on its support and r_b<=alpha_b.
Set

 u_b=[sqrt(alpha_b+r_b)+sqrt(alpha_b-r_b)]/2,
 sqrt(D_b)=u_b I+Q_b/(2u_b).                              (7)

The denominator remains positive even when eta=1 and a local eigenvalue is
zero. All inputs are algebraic functions of the current coordinates and
supplied coefficients. The diagonal remainder is simpler. This avoids a
global matrix factorization, not the cost of evaluating the local roots.

3. THE POSITIVE INTERNAL JUMP LAW
================================
Choose epsilon>0, tau_h,tau_g,tau_o,tau_d>0, kappa tau_d<=1, and
rho=1-kappa tau_d/2. At a current field/controller the ideal primitives are:

 Noise: each l and sign +/-, x ->x +/-epsilon v_l(x),
        rate1/(2epsilon^2).

 Hopping: each edge e in E_h, apply its exact two-mode unitary
        exp(-i tau_h h_e), rate1/tau_h.

 Cross phase: each e={i,j} in E_g, apply
        z_i ->exp(-i tau_g g_e I_j)z_i,
        z_j ->exp(-i tau_g g_e I_i)z_j, rate1/tau_g.

 On-site/diagonal phase: each i, apply
        z_i ->exp[-i tau_o(omega_i+w_i I_i)]z_i,
        rate1/tau_o.

 Damping: each i, x_i ->rho x_i, rate1/tau_d.             (8)

Counts at lambda_r=c_r|u_r^dagger z|^2 update their prescribed latches only.
The field is constant between events. The total internal rate is the constant

 Lambda_int=L/epsilon^2+|E_h|/tau_h+|E_g|/tau_g
                                      +m/tau_o+m/tau_d. (9)

Every primitive has bounded support. The exact hopping block is
cos(|h_e|tau_h)I-i[sin(|h_e|tau_h)/|h_e|]h_e, with the analytic sinc limit at
zero. Phase frequencies are evaluated from the pre-jump point; their own
flows preserve those intensities exactly. On-site and cross phase flows
preserve every I_i, and hopping preserves the sum I_i+I_j on its edge.

This is a continuous-time sum of positive jump generators. Its coherent-
generator defect is the sum of the local endpoint remainders; no uncharged
cross-flow commutator or deterministic splitting error is being suppressed.

4. OWN AGGREGATE MOMENTS OF THE NUMERICAL PROCESS
================================================
Let F_p(x)=sum_i I_i^p, F_0=m. For the noise primitive, one site's intensity is

 I_i'=I_i +/-2epsilon(x_i.v_(i,l))+epsilon^2|v_(i,l)|^2.

The exact even Taylor expansion and (6) give, pointwise,

 G_noise F_p <=p^2 kappa nu F_(p-1)
       +sum_(r=2)^p A_(p,r)epsilon^(2r-2)kappa nu
                                     Dcol^(r-1)F_(p-r),

 A_(p,r)=sum_(s=0)^min(r,p-r)
                     binom(p,r+s)binom(r+s,2s)4^s.       (10)

For clarity, the summand before its bound is proportional to
I_i^(p-r-s)(x_i.v)^(2s)|v|^(2(r-s)). Cauchy gives
(x_i.v)^2<=I_i|v|^2, and
sum_l |v_(i,l)|^(2r)<=Dcol^(r-1)kappa nu. The r=1 terms
are evaluated using BOTH exact identities in(6), giving p^2 rather than a
generic larger diffusion estimate. There is no factor m in each coefficient;
the extensive dimension is retained in F_0=m.

For a hopping edge with a=|h_ij|, along its exact flow

 d/dr (I_i^p+I_j^p)<=2pa(I_i^p+I_j^p).

Thus its rate1/tau_h jump increases this quantity by at most
[(exp(2pa tau_h)-1)/tau_h](I_i^p+I_j^p). For 0<=a<=H,
monotonicity of (exp(2p a tau_h)-1)/a and the actual row bound imply

 G_hop F_p <=beta_p F_p,
 beta_p=[exp(2pH tau_h)-1]/tau_h,       p>=2,
 beta_1=0 exactly.                                        (11)

The exact p=1 value uses global intensity conservation. This bound is valid
with record-adapted hopping weights; no fixed-edge envelope is needed. Edge
kicks do not require the stronger assumption that all possible contacts are
simultaneously active.

For damping,

 kappa_p=[1-rho^(2p)]/tau_d,
 G_damp F_p=-kappa_p F_p.                                (12)

Let P>=3 be a chosen finite moment order and require

 alpha_p=kappa_p-beta_p>0 for 1<=p<=P.                    (13)

Define U_0=m and recursively

 U_p={p^2 kappa nu U_(p-1)
       +sum_(r=2)^p A_(p,r)epsilon^(2r-2)kappa nu
                                   Dcol^(r-1)U_(p-r)}/alpha_p. (14)

Then E F_p(X_t)<=U_p at every deterministic time under all admitted feedback.
Indeed (10)--(12) give a triangular differential inequality, while
alpha_p<=p kappa ensures U_p>=m p!nu^p by induction, dominating the thermal
initialization. Coordinate-contractive initial rounding also fits. For other
supplied initial laws enlarge U_p to dominate their actual initial moments.

There is a completely rational sufficient step choice. Assume
a=kappa-2H>0. Choose

 tau_d<=a/[(2P-1)kappa^2],
 2PH tau_h<=1,
 tau_h<=a/(16PH^2) when H>0.                             (15)

For p>=2 one may REPLACE beta_p by the rational upper bound
2pH+4p^2H^2tau_h. The inequalities
kappa_p>=p kappa-p(2p-1)kappa^2tau_d/4 and
exp(x)-1<=x+x^2 for0<=x<=1 show alpha_p>=pa/2.
For H=0 omit hopping clocks and take beta_p=0. All U_p are then computed by
finite rational arithmetic when model/step inputs are rational. U_p=m u_p
with u_p independent of m if local parameters and P are fixed.

5. LOCAL CLIPPING UNDER THE IMPLEMENTED LAW
==========================================
Let B be the smallest threshold among nonzero terms in(3), and set

 ell_clip=max_i[(1/2)sum_(e incident i)g_e,max+w_i,max/sqrt(2)]. (16)

The on-site term's half-trace defect is
(|w_i|/sqrt(2))(I_i-B_i)_+; a cross edge's is at most
|g_e|(sqrt(I_i I_j)-B_e)_+. Convexity of positive part therefore gives

 (1/2)E||E_clip||_(CQ,1)
 <=ell_clip sum_i E(I_i-B)_+
 <=ell_clip min_(1<=p<=P) c_p U_p/B^(p-1),
 c_p=(p-1)^(p-1)/p^p for p>1, c_1=1.                    (17)

This is the tail of THIS jump law, using(14). No CIR or stationary Gaussian
law has been transferred from the continuous source. If all quartic terms
vanish, set the clipping allowance to zero and omit B.

6. COHERENT-GENERATOR RESIDUALS WITH EXTENSIVE CONSTANTS
======================================================
Let C2=2+2sqrt(3) and C4=2sqrt(105)+8sqrt(15)+18<70.
For any real direction v, normalized coherent projectors satisfy
||D_v^2 K||1<=C2|v|^2/theta and
||D_v^4 K||1<=C4|v|^4/theta^2. These uniform displacement bounds were proved
in the root finite-jump theorem; they also follow by vacuum commutators.

All following expressions are HALF-trace generator allowances, so they can
be integrated directly to bound complete-record TV.

Noise central differences:

 E_noise=C4 epsilon^2 m kappa nu Dcol/(48theta^2).         (18)

Here sum_l |v_l|^4<=Dcol sum_l|v_l|^2=m kappa nu Dcol.
The current-point covariance is held fixed in each jump's Taylor expansion;
there is no omitted derivative of a covariance factor.

Hopping edge flows:

 E_hop=(tau_h H^2/4)[C2 U1/theta
                                +2sqrt(m U1)/sqrt(theta)]. (19)

On one edge its squared speed and acceleration norm are a^2(I_i+I_j) and
a^2sqrt(I_i+I_j). Their sums obey pathwise
sum_e a_e^2(I_i+I_j)<=H^2 sum_i I_i and
sum_e a_e^2sqrt(I_i+I_j)<=H^2 sum_i sqrt(I_i), again using
the actual Hermitian row budget. The endpoint Taylor remainder of the exact
edge flow proves(19), with no Lipschitz estimate on a combined flow.

Cross-phase flows:

 E_cross=(tau_g d2/4)[C2 U3/theta
                                 +2sqrt(U2 U3)/sqrt(theta)]. (20)

For e={i,j}, squared speed is g_e^2 I_i I_j(I_i+I_j), bounded by
g_e^2(I_i^3+I_j^3). Acceleration norm is at most
g_e^2[I_j^2sqrt(I_i)+I_i^2sqrt(I_j)], bounded by
g_e^2[I_i^(5/2)+I_j^(5/2)]. Summing with d2 and applying Cauchy over modes
and probability gives(20). These are local moments, not powers of total S.

On-site plus diagonal rotations:

 A_o=Omega^2 U1+2Omega W U2+W^2 U3,
 B_o=Omega^2sqrt(m U1)+2Omega W sqrt(U1 U2)+W^2sqrt(U2 U3),
 E_on=(tau_o/4)[C2 A_o/theta+2B_o/sqrt(theta)].            (21)

The exact orbit has constant frequency omega_i+w_i I_i. Its squared speed
and acceleration norm are that frequency squared times I_i and sqrt(I_i),
respectively. This includes the paid normal-order rotation in Omega.

Rational damping steps:

 E_damp=C2 kappa^2 tau_d U1/(16theta).                    (22)

Thus the ideal local finite-jump law satisfies

 TV(P_Q^T,P_jump^T)
 <=delta_init+T[E_clip+E_noise+E_hop+E_cross+E_on+E_damp], (23)

where E_clip is(17). The stronger joint CQ half-trace comparison holds too.
The proof is the exact coherent-kernel identity plus contractive physical CQ
Duhamel. Passive count gain intertwines exactly, and full-minus-dispatch
no-event generators have the same listed defects. Controller/latch routing
is shared. There is no uncharged observation-history factor.

For fixed local degrees and parameters, every U_p=m u_p and every expression
in(17)--(22) grows at most linearly with m. This is the spatial benefit. It
does not remove the m-dependent full-record error or the need for smaller
steps/physical quantum parameter when more modes must be predicted accurately.

7. FINITE COORDINATE PRECISION PRESERVES THE MOMENTS
==================================================
For every true local endpoint y, compute certified intervals[a_j,b_j] of width
at most w for the changed real coordinates. On a grid of spacing Delta,
round toward zero using the interval endpoint closest to zero; if its interval
crosses zero, store zero. Leave all untouched coordinates exactly unchanged.
Then for every changed coordinate

 |yhat_j|<=|y_j|,   |yhat_j-y_j|<=Delta+w.

At most four real coordinates change, so |yhat-y|<=e=2(Delta+w). EVERY
individual intensity and hence every F_p at the rounded endpoint is at most
that of its corresponding ideal endpoint. Therefore(10)--(17) remain true
for the ACTUAL rounded generator. No false covariance identity is asserted
for the rounded endpoints themselves.

The coherent-projector bound
(1/2)||K_y-K_yhat||1<=|y-yhat|/sqrt(theta) adds

 epsilon_round<=T Lambda_int e/sqrt(theta).               (24)

This direct generator bound also applies to the actual asymmetric rounding;
its bias is not ignored. Roots in(7), hopping sinc/cosine and phase exponentials
can be enclosed to any requested absolute accuracy by rational algorithms.
At eta=1 an inner square root can vanish, but the stable formula denominator
stays positive. Supplied irrational coefficients need their own certified
enclosures. A concrete rational coefficient/port menu avoids such extra input.

Finite Gaussian initialization can use signs, lower dyadic uniforms for
half-normal quantiles, a finite coordinate cap, and interval rounding toward
zero. Under the natural coupling every coordinate magnitude decreases, so
E F_p(0)<=m p!nu^p for all p. Its coherent-lift distance, cap probability,
random-bit discretization and quantile enclosure errors contribute to
delta_init as in the root finite-bit construction. It is not called a smooth
or exactly Gaussian numerical law.

8. LOCAL STATE CAP WITH A PAID FIRST-EXIT BOUND
=============================================
Choose R>0 and reset any attempted rounded endpoint to zero in a changed mode
whose intensity exceeds R^2. The capped field then has I_i<=R^2 in every mode.
Compare it to the uncapped rounded process with the same draws until the first
such exit, including any initial exit. They agree up to that event.

For a chosen p<=P the pointwise upper generator polynomial is

 G F_p<=sum_i P_p(I_i),
 P_p(s)=-alpha_p s^p+sum_(r=1)^p a_(p,r)s^(p-r),
 a_(p,1)=p^2 kappa nu,
 a_(p,r)=A_(p,r)epsilon^(2r-2)kappa nu Dcol^(r-1), r>=2.

Take a rational s_star>0 satisfying
sum_r a_(p,r)/s_star^r<=alpha_p. Set
C_p=sum_r a_(p,r)s_star^(p-r). Then P_p(s)<=0 for s>=s_star
and P_p(s)<=C_p otherwise, so G F_p<=m C_p. Optional stopping gives

 epsilon_exit<= [E F_p(0)+T m C_p]/R^(2p)
             <= [m p!nu^p+T m C_p]/R^(2p).                (25)

At the uncapped first exit F_p>=R^(2p), including an overshoot. This is not
the post-reset state. Coordinate rounding has already been included in the
generator inequality. A cap/reset is a real change of model and is paid by
(25), rather than being justified by unproved numerical tail stationarity.

The local cap keeps endpoint arithmetic bounded: a phase frequency is at
most Omega+W R^2 or g_e,max R^2. Local hopping is a constant-size rotation.
There is no global-state enumeration; a path stores2m grid coordinates.

9. EXACT FINITE-BIN UNIFORMIZATION AND ACTION COUNT
==================================================
For a bin with fixed implemented controller tables, uniformize at

 L_tot=Lambda_int+c m R^2.                               (26)

Given N~Poisson(L_tot Delta_bin), make N categorical proposals: a local
internal transition, a permitted latch-changing physical port at its rate,
or a self-loop. Expose/reset the latch only at the original bin boundary,
then update the same controller. No continuous event times need to be stored
or rounded. Reacting to a hidden within-bin trigger would define another
controller and is not allowed by this procedure.

Finite-bit small-mean Poisson sampling, a Poisson proposal-count cap, and
finite-bit categorical draws contribute epsilon_sampling. For example split
each mean mu into ceil(mu) independent Poisson variables of mean<=1, use
factorial-series intervals and tails, and cap N at2ceil(mu)+b. Its tail is
at most2^(-b). If there are J categorical outcomes and at most Nmax proposals,
midpoint B-bit uniforms give total categorical TV at most Nmax J2^(-B), plus
declared probability-evaluation interval errors. These primitive bounds are
proved in the root finite-bit construction and remain unchanged here.

The final completely implemented allowance is the right side of(23), plus

 epsilon_round+epsilon_exit+epsilon_sampling.             (27)

Internal expected actions are T Lambda_int. Uniformization additionally pays
the full c m R^2T rate envelope, even if most readout proposals are self-loops.
There are at most2L+|E_h|+|E_g|+2m+number_of_ports+1 categories. Each internal
update touches at most two modes, but evaluating dense readout ports or a
complicated controller can still be expensive and must be charged. Selection
catalogs, graph data and coefficient precision are part of the input/storage.

A concrete sparse readout class is available: on-site ports and +/- mixed
ports on a bounded-degree observation graph, with rational rate prefactors.
At rational stored coordinates their rates
c I_i and (c/2)[I_i+I_j +/-2Re(bar z_i z_j)] are EXACT rational polynomials,
although the normalized physical mode vectors contain1/sqrt(2). Cache these
weights in a cumulative-weight binary tree. An internal update changes only
the bounded number of readout weights incident on its one or two modes;
tree updates and categorical search cost O(log m) rational arithmetic
operations per proposal when latch masking is also efficient. A global
first-port/OR latch can use one active/inactive mask, and per-port capped
counters change only their own leaf weight. An arbitrary finite latch may
enable or disable many ports at once; its actual mask/controller cost must
then be paid, and no O(log m) bound is asserted for that operation. Constant
internal clocks can be selected by type
and a fixed index catalog, without evaluating every endpoint. Rebuild any
changed controller/port tables at the actual exposed boundary and charge
that work. Arithmetic bit lengths, random draws, physical port access and
controller computation are still counted; this is not an uncharged oracle.

For bounded-degree graphs the catalog size and number of current field
coordinates are O(m). With fixed P, all moment-recursion constants are finite
and linear in m. To keep a fixed TOTAL numerical error as m grows, the noise
and flow steps must shrink; for example the noise bound alone suggests
epsilon^2 proportional to1/m, giving O(m^2) internal noise actions at fixed
other error/physical parameters. This is an honest polynomial action cost,
not an O(m) runtime claim. Clipping itself still imposes the displayed
physical-regime/volume condition. No optimized performance advantage has been
benchmarked against equally informed positive-P, filtering or tensor methods.

If an event probability is estimated from independent generated paths, add
statistical tolerance epsilon_stat using at least
log(2/alpha)/(2epsilon_stat^2) trajectories for failure probability alpha.
Generating the joint record does not solve economical conditioning on an
arbitrarily unlikely supplied record. A true filter needs its own likelihood,
resampling/representation and acquisition analysis.

10. WHY THE FINITE LAW IS NOW CONCRETE
====================================
The stored field is a finite rational grid point. Every endpoint is an
explicit local algebraic/trigonometric operation with certified enclosures.
Every step is a positive transition, every accumulated observation is routed
through the actual finite latch/controller, and all physical/source, moment,
coordinate, state-cap and random-draw allowances have a declared place in
(27). The proof uses the implemented law's aggregate moments throughout.
It neither compares mutually singular diffusion path laws nor transfers the
continuous source's Gaussian/CIR stationary distribution to a jump process.

This supplies a finite positive recursively evaluable many-mode record
predictor for the stated noisy on-site/cross-density bosonic model with
feedback hopping. The positive point law is a predictive representation;
the coherent-mixture quantum state is its comparison certificate. They are
not identified as the same physical object.
