continually create conditional information; allowed feedback may change F and

 H=2 E integral p [sym DF]:Hess log(p/gamma_sigma).         (A)

This identity transfers the nonlinear strain cost into the Hessian dissipation
of the thermal bath. If E||sym DF||_F^2<=Xi^2 uniformly, then

 E I_gamma(p_t)
 <= e^(-kappa t) E I_gamma(p_0)
   +[4 c m/kappa+2 Xi^2/(kappa^2 sigma)](1-e^(-kappa t)).  (B)

Under gamma preparation and the pointwise strain envelope ||sym DF||_F<=L S,
the autonomous radial law supplies Xi^2=L^2 sigma^2 m(m+1). No independent-
intensity premise, angular commutation, or weak interaction assumption is used.
Linear unitary hopping has zero strain and is allowed at arbitrary supplied
strength. Number-conserving quartic Hamiltonian drifts fit the envelope.

This controls one-time spatial representation at every deterministic time,
uniformly in preceding record duration. It is not a finite recursive filter,

1. MODEL, PREPARATION, ACCESS AND REGULARITY
==========================================
Let x in R^(2m), equivalently z in C^m, S=|x|^2 and

 gamma_sigma(x)=(pi sigma)^(-m) exp(-S/sigma).

The signal generator on each retained controller branch is

 L_C f=F_C(x,t).grad f-(kappa/2)x.grad f+(kappa sigma/4)Delta f,
 div F_C=0, x.F_C=0.                                    (1)

F is real and sufficiently smooth (C^3 in x is a clean formulation), with the
usual uniform local derivative envelopes over allowed controls and supplied

Hamiltonians with bounded nonlinear coefficients satisfy these requirements.
The skew linear part may be locally time-integrable. The main quantitative
assumption is, uniformly in permitted C,t,x,

 B_C=sym DF_C,  ||B_C(x,t)||_F<=L S.                      (2)

The more abstract estimate (B) only needs a stable derivative-energy envelope
Xi^2 and an independently justified wellposed approximation; it does not
silently acquire these analytic prerequisites from one expected norm.

There are finitely many Cox channels

 lambda_r=c_r|u_r^dagger z|^2,
 sum_r c_r u_r u_r^dagger<=c I_m.                         (3)

Their coefficients may depend on permitted exposed past records, retained
controller memory/seed and time. The controller has no hidden field, bath-noise
or unread-trigger access. Its actual memory and seed remain part of the
implemented predictor. A finite-bin/first-port/latch observation policy may be
used; for the proof only, refine to the full marked Cox record while keeping
the ACTUAL controller policy unchanged.

The main preparation is gamma_sigma conditional on the retained initial seed
and controller. Independence is sufficient. Conditioning on this auxiliary
fine record gives p_t; put

For this preparation r(0)=0. The extension in (B) includes a paid finite initial
Fisher value when the derivative-energy envelope is supplied for that initial

3. EXACT STRAIN-HESSIAN IDENTITY
================================
Work first with positive regularized filters from section6. Since div F=0 and
F.grad gamma=0, the relative log density is advected by -F.grad w. Its Fisher
contribution is the standard direct differentiation identity

 H=-2 E integral p u^T B u.                              (7)

No estimate is taken yet. Using grad p=p(u-2x/sigma), integrate one derivative:

 H=2 E integral p B:Hess w
   +2 E integral p(div B).u
   -(4/sigma) E integral p(Bx).u.                        (8)

Here (div B)_i=sum_j partial_j B_ij; symmetry makes either index convention
equivalent. Both residual integrals in (8) vanish. The facts are as follows.

First, div F=0 implies

 div B=(Delta F)/2, div(Delta F)=0.

Taking a Laplacian of x.F=0 gives

 x.Delta F=-2 div F=0.

Therefore div B is itself divergence-free and tangent to radius. Second,
differentiating tangency radially gives x^T Bx=0. Moreover

 div(Bx)=trace B+x.div B=div F+(x.Delta F)/2=0.

Thus Bx is also divergence-free and tangent. For ANY such vector field G,

 integral p G.grad log(p/gamma)
 = integral G.grad p+(2/sigma)integral p G.x
 =-integral p div G+(2/sigma)integral p G.x=0.             (9)

Apply (9) to div B and Bx in (8). This proves EXACTLY

 H=2 E integral p B:Hess w.                              (10)

The cancellation uses Gaussian geometry and the displayed conservation laws.
It is not a bounded-Jacobian estimate, a pointwise smallness condition on B,
or a replacement of the filtering density by equilibrium.

4. OBSERVATIONS AND THERMAL DISSIPATION
======================================
The OU contribution to full relative Fisher is

 -kappa r-(kappa sigma/2)E integral p||Hess w||_F^2.        (11)

The count source follows directly from the normalized no-event/jump filter.
Between counts the observation part is -(lambda-lambda_hat)p; at a count the
posterior is p lambda/lambda_hat. Their exact mean Fisher contributions sum to

 J_obs=E sum_r integral p |grad lambda_r|^2/lambda_r.

For the passive channels (3), |grad lambda_r|^2/lambda_r
=4c_r||u_r||^2 off the harmless zero set. Hence

 J_obs<=4 trace(sum c_r u_r u_r^dagger)<=4 c m.            (12)

The no-event and normalization cross terms cancel; they have not been dropped.
Combining (10)--(12), and using the pointwise square completion

 2 B:Y-(kappa sigma/2)||Y||_F^2 <=2||B||_F^2/(kappa sigma),

gives

 r'<=-kappa r+4cm+2 E integral p||B||_F^2/(kappa sigma).   (13)

The abstract bound (B) follows by Gronwall. With (6) and gamma preparation,

 r(t)<=R_strain(1-e^(-kappa t)),
 R_strain=4cm/kappa+2L^2 sigma m(m+1)/kappa^2.             (14)

If the source injection has some other proved upper envelope J_* in place of

5. REGULARIZATION DERIVED FROM THE SAME STRAIN PREMISE
======================================================
No unrelated velocity-growth hypothesis is required for (2). Tangency implies
F(0)=0 and A=DF(0) skew. The identity

 (DF)^T x=-F

gives

 (x.grad)F-F=2Bx.                                       (15)

On a ray x=r e, differentiate F(r e)/r and integrate from0:

 d/dr[F(r e)/r]=2B(r e)e/r,
 |F(x)-Ax|<=L|x|^3.                                    (16)

Thus F_nl=F-Ax has at most cubic growth. Leave Ax UNCUT and define

 F_R=Ax+chi(S/R)F_nl,

where 0<=chi<=1, chi=1 below1, chi=0 above2, and |chi'|<=L_chi.
Both summands remain divergence-free and tangent. The derivative cutoff does
not alter the radial CIR equation. Its strain is

 B_R=chi B+sym[F_nl tensor grad chi(S/R)].

Using (16), its extra term has norm at most

 2L L_chi S^2/R * 1_(S>=R).

Consequently, uniformly over the controller,

 sqrt(E||B_R||_F^2)
 <=L sigma sqrt[m(m+1)]
   +(2L L_chi/R)sqrt(E_gamma[S^4 1_(S>=R)])
 -> L sigma sqrt[m(m+1)].                               (17)

The local uniform regularity premise guarantees the usual globally controlled
derivatives for fixed cutoff. If Ax is rapidly rotating, an adapted orthogonal
rotating frame removes that linear part without changing the noise, Fisher,
strain envelope, or the scalar capacities sum lambda<=cS and
sum |grad lambda|^2/lambda<=4cm used in the proof. A general real orthogonal
rotation need not preserve the literal complex-linear port parametrization;
it preserves these two capacities. For physical unitary hopping the original
complex-linear port form is preserved as well. Linear hopping is not charged
as nonlinear strain by the cutoff construction.

6. PASSAGE THROUGH COUNT-CREATED ZEROS AND COARSENING
====================================================
Use globally saturated positive intensities

 lambda_(eps,M)=eps+lambda/(1+lambda/M).

Their full-gradient source is no greater than the original source because
(h_M')^2 lambda/h_M(lambda)<=1. No radial cutoff is put into the intensities:
that would create an uncharged gradient term. For fixed R,eps,M the signal is
uniformly elliptic, branchwise likelihood ratios are positive and bounded,
and the localized energy identities above are justified. Count-created ideal
zeros, which can have singular instantaneous Hessian dissipation, are not
silently assumed smooth.

Couple original and regularized signals with common Brownian paths and marked

For q channels, on [0,T], the joint-law error is bounded by

 P(sup_(t<=T) S_t>=R)+T[q eps+c^2 m(m+1)sigma^2/M].        (18)

The first term tends to zero. For example its explicit bound is
min{1,2^m exp[kappa mT/2-R/(2sigma)]}, by the radial exponential
supermartingale. The second uses sum lambda<=cS and (5). No continuity of the
feedback policy with respect to a perturbed record is required: it is exactly
the same until the first mismatch.

Mean conditional relative Fisher is lower semicontinuous under this joint-TV
convergence, as seen from its variational formula

 E I_gamma(p_Y)=sup_h E[-2 div_x h(X,Y)
                       +4X.h(X,Y)/sigma-|h(X,Y)|^2],     (19)

with smooth compactly supported spatial tests and bounded measurable dependence

Using (17), the regularized bound (13) therefore gives (14) at each fixed t.
There is no need to pass an infinite Hessian dissipation through a jump.

The actual finite-bin/latch posterior is a conditional mixture of fine-record

7. NUMBER-CONSERVING HAMILTONIANS AND COHERENT HOPPING

For each fixed coefficient tensor, compactness of the unit sphere gives

 L=sup_(|x|=1)||sym DF_nl(x)||_F<infinity,

and homogeneity gives (2). A bounded allowed coefficient family supplies the

all modes and quartic terms beyond intensity-only cross-Kerr interactions.

No independent mode-intensity stationarity is asserted in this larger family.
The total-radius resource alone is sufficient for (14).

8. EXACT GLOBAL-PHASE QUOTIENT IN THE TWO-MODE CROSS-KERR CASE
============================================================
The generic bound can retain unobservable strain from common nonlinear phase.
For the preparation and passive ports here, every filter is invariant under
common phase. Any added Hamiltonian function of S therefore has zero action
on its conditional density and no effect on the permitted record law.

For V=g I1 I2, subtract the common-phase Hamiltonian g S^2/4. The equivalent
conditional dynamics has

 V_rel=-(g/4)(I1-I2)^2,
 omega_rel=(-g D/2,+g D/2), D=I1-I2.

Direct differentiation gives the exact strain norm

 ||B_rel||_F^2=(g^2/2)S^2.                               (20)

For an explicit verification, let A=diag(1,1,-1,-1) and let J be the common
two-plane rotation matrix. Then F_rel=(g/2)D J A x, up to the harmless overall
sign convention, and B_rel=(g/2)[(JAx)(Ax)^T+(Ax)(JAx)^T]. The two vectors
JAx and Ax are orthogonal and both have squared norm S, proving (20).

Passive coherent hopping may be retained at arbitrary supplied strength:
its strain is zero and it preserves the common-phase symmetry. If |g|<=g0
under feedback, then total-radius stationarity gives

 Xi^2<=3 g0^2 sigma^2   (m=2),
 R=8c/kappa+6 g0^2 sigma/kappa^2.                        (21)

At kappa=sigma=c=g0=1 this is R=14, independent of earlier record duration
and compatible with arbitrary permitted coherent hopping. The quotient does

The previously preserved phase-only/angular theorem remains useful where its
