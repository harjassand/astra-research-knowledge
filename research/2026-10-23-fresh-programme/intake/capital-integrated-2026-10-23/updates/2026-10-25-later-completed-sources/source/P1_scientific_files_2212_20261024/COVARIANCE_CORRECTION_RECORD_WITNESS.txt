==========================

observed channels are

 M_+=(sqrt(theta)/sqrt(2))(a1+a2),

Bins are [0,1] and [1,3/2]. In each bin expose the first port + or -, or 0 if
there is no count; the field keeps evolving after a trigger. Both supplied
past protocols g=+1 and g=-1 use g=+1 in the second bin. This is not a change
of controller history without paying for the history: the protocols are
distinct supplied experiments.

EXACT INCUMBENT SECTOR FORMULA
==============================
The preserved exact population/coherence sector argument supplies

 M_h(g,o)=[[-1/2-i g h/2, 1],[o+i g, 1/2+i g h/2]].       (2)

h=theta gives Q and h=0 gives G; o=1 or0 indicates an active or already
triggered first-port latch. For s in[0,1],u in[0,1/2] and past sign p,

 v_s=exp[s M_0(0,1)](1,1)^T,
 r_h(s,u)=exp[u M_h(1,1)]exp[(1-s)M_h(p,0)]v_s,
 W_h=integral_ds_du [2 exp(1+u)(v_s)_1^2/|(r_h)_2|^4].    (3)

Index1 denotes the first component here, not a mode index. W_h is the signed
product of the two exposed nonzero marks. All pooled zero/nonzero masses are
the same in Q and G. Therefore

 TV(Q,G)=|W_theta-W_0|/2,
 Q(A)-G(A)=(W_theta-W_0)/2,
 A={(+,+),(-,-)}.                                        (4)

The derivative of (3) with respect to h is integrated over the homotopy
h in[0,1/16]. Outward dyadic complex interval arithmetic and entire matrix
exponential series enclose that derivative on128 by128 time rectangles.
No sampled quadrature or finite Fock cutoff is used. The new script imports
the preserved interval primitives and the proven sector convention without
modifying those files.
