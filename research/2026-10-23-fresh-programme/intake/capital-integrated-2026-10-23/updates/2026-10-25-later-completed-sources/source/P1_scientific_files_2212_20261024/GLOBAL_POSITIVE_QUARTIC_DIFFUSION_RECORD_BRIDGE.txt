PRIVATE ASTRA P1 — GLOBAL POSITIVE QUARTIC DIFFUSION / COMPLETE RECORD BRIDGE
2026-10-24. New work. Earlier Fisher, Husimi, exact two-bin, and source files
are preserved separately and unchanged. Producer proof; not a novelty claim.

GOVERNING RESULT
================
For a supplied positive coherent-mixture preparation, a thermal gain/loss
bath, general number-conserving quartic Hamiltonians, passive annihilation
counts, and a common permitted-record feedback controller, there is a positive
2m-real-dimensional diffusion whose COMPLETE permitted record law approximates
the quantum record law. The diffusion retains the exact ordinary-P quantum
correction whenever the total amplitude is below an explicit threshold and
attenuates only that correction outside it. The error is bounded by a scalar
CIR excess-tail integral. At thermal preparation that integral is explicit and
exponentially small in kappa/(theta q2), for fixed mode count and coefficients.

This is a different predictor from the earlier isotropic classical SDE. Its
state-dependent real covariance must be evaluated and numerically sampled;
the earlier isotropic sampler does not automatically implement it. The result
does not assert exact ordinary-P positivity, a bounded finite posterior family,
uniform prediction after an arbitrarily rare record, or an acquired preparation.
The positive predictor and the coherent-mixture quantum certificate remain
different mathematical objects.

1. PHYSICAL MODEL, PREPARATION, AND ACCESS
========================================
Set B_j=sqrt(theta)a_j, [B_j,B_k^dagger]=theta delta_jk, theta>0. The physical
generator at a supplied control C and time t is

 H_theta=theta^(-1)[B^dagger h_theta(C,t)B+:W_C,t(B^dagger,B):],
 L_Q rho=-i[H_theta,rho]
              +sum_j[d D[a_j]+b D[a_j^dagger]]rho,
 b=kappa nu/theta, d=kappa(nu+theta)/theta, kappa,nu>0.       (1)

h_theta is Hermitian. W is a real homogeneous polynomial of bidegree (2,2)
in z,bar z. Its normal ordering is part of the definition. Both terms conserve
total quantum number. A density-density convention containing N_i^2 requires
the usual supplied normal-order linear correction; it must not be omitted.

Allowed observed jumps are

 M_r=sqrt(c_r) u_r^dagger B,
 Q_c=sum_r c_r u_r u_r^dagger <= c I,
 theta Q_c <= d I.                                       (2)

The last inequality is the physical monitored-subchannel condition. The
remaining loss channels are positive; gains are unobserved. The c_r, ports,
timing, mixing and access to those output losses are supplied resources.
There is no requirement that observed losses fit inside the NET damping kappa.

The same implemented controller acts on the permitted exposed record, its
retained state, time and retained seed in the two models. It has no access to
the hidden amplitude, mathematical driving noise, or an unread trigger.
Finite first-port/OR/general finite latches and their scheduled exposures are
allowed. Dynamics continues after a latch trigger. Uniform coefficient bounds
below are over all controls the implemented controller can produce. For a
clean construction one may take a finite control menu, piecewise continuous
within bins; locally integrable bounded-coefficient extensions work likewise.

Let

 K_z=|z/sqrt(theta)><z/sqrt(theta)|,
 R_theta(mu)=integral K_z mu(dz).                         (3)

Apply this map separately in every classical initial-controller block. The
physical preparation is this supplied positive coherent mixture, or has joint
CQ half-trace distance at most delta_prep from it. The source measure mu may be
singular or atomic; a density or a finite Fisher score is not required. A
finite initial first radial moment is sufficient for the final tail theorem;
the derivation first uses compactly supported measures and then passes to this
class by positive-mass truncation. Product thermal preparation corresponds to
mu(dz)=(pi nu)^(-m)exp(-|z|^2/nu) dz.

No conclusion extends this preparation premise to an arbitrary quantum state.
One may pay a separately proved delta_prep, but it is not supplied by the
representation itself.

2. EXACT ORDINARY-P QUARTIC GENERATOR
====================================
Write real coordinates X=(x_1,...,x_m,y_1,...,y_m), z=x+iy, and S=|z|^2.
Let

 C_ij(z)=partial_barzi partial_barzj W(z),
 F(z)=-i h_theta z-i partial_barz W(z).

C is complex symmetric and analytic quadratic in z. Choose supplied uniform
constants q2,qF satisfying

 ||C(z)||_op <= q2 S,       ||C(z)||_F <= qF S.             (4)

Finite tensor/sphere bounds suffice; qF=sqrt(m)q2 is a safe choice if
only an operator bound is known. Direct Frobenius bounds can be sharper.

Real and imaginary parts of F are denoted by F_R. Hamiltonian homogeneity and
reality imply div F_R=0 and X.F_R=0. Define real symmetric matrices

 D0=(kappa nu/2) I_(2m),
 Dq(z)=(theta/2) [[Im C, -Re C],[-Re C, -Im C]].            (5)

Here D is the INCREMENT COVARIANCE: a real diffusion with covariance D has
backward second-order term (1/2)D:Hess. All factors below use that convention.

The coherent-projector identities give the exact trace-class kernel equality

 L_Q K_z = [ (F_R-kappa X/2).grad
                         +(1/2)(D0+Dq):Hess ] K_z.       (6)

For the quartic term the part absent from the first-order classical drift is

 -(i theta/2) sum_ij [C_ij partial_zi partial_zj K_z
                   -bar C_ij partial_barzi partial_barzj K_z].  (7)

Wirtinger derivatives partial_z=(partial_x-i partial_y)/2 convert (7) exactly
to (1/2)Dq:Hess K. The thermal bath is exact because d-b=kappa and b theta=kappa
nu. Hopping and any supplied normal-order linear correction are exact too.

The diffusion matrix D0+Dq need not be positive globally. For example a
one-mode Kerr C=g z^2 gives eigenvalues kappa nu/2 +/- theta |g| S/2. This
defeats a globally exact undoubled P diffusion, but does not defeat (6) on the
large region where thermal noise dominates its indefinite component.

Three algebraic identities drive the construction:

 ||Dq||_op=theta ||C||_op/2,
 tr Dq=0,
 X^T Dq X=(theta/2) Im(bar z^T C bar z)=0.                 (8)

For the first identity Dq acts on a complex vector v as -i theta C bar v/2;
its real singular values are those of theta C/2, each repeated. For the last,
Euler homogeneity gives bar z^T C bar z=2W, which is real.

3. A GLOBAL POSITIVE DIFFUSION
=============================
Choose a fixed 0<eta<1 and, when q2>0, set

 B=eta kappa nu/(theta q2),
 chi(S)=1                  if 0<=S<=B,
        B/S                if S>B,
 Dhat(z)=D0+chi(S)Dq(z).                                 (9)

Keep the ORIGINAL drift F_R-kappa X/2, including the original normal-order
linear correction. The reference is the Itô diffusion

 dX=(F_R(X)-kappa X/2)dt+sqrt(Dhat(X)) dW_(2m),
 lambda_r(X)=c_r |u_r^dagger z|^2,                        (10)

with Cox counts routed through the same permitted latches and controller.
The principal positive real square root is one explicit covariance factor.
This process is a law on ordinary 2m-dimensional real phase space, not on a
doubled bra/ket phase space and not a signed or complex-weight ensemble.

By (4),(8),(9),

 (1-eta)(kappa nu/2)I <= Dhat <= (1+eta)(kappa nu/2)I.      (11)

Thus the diffusion is globally uniformly elliptic. For bounded quartic tensor
coefficients chi C is globally bounded and globally Lipschitz: it is quadratic
inside the ball and degree zero in radius outside, with bounded tangential
derivatives and matching values at S=B. Its square root is Lipschitz since the
spectral lower bound is positive. The cubic drift is locally Lipschitz.
Section 4 supplies nonexplosion. Piecewise control changes are concatenated
using the retained controller. If q2=0, W=0; use Dhat=D0 and the bridge is exact.

The continuous but nonsmooth derivative of chi at S=B causes no extra term in
the BACKWARD generator or in (6). A forward Fokker--Planck equation would of
course differentiate the product Dhat times a density. The proof uses Itô on
the smooth kernel K, so no distributional cutoff derivative is discarded.

4. THE TOTAL-RADIUS LAW IS EXACTLY UNCHANGED
==========================================
For every permitted control, (8) and tangency give

 Lhat S=kappa(m nu-S),
 Gammahat(S)=grad S^T Dhat grad S=2 kappa nu S.

Consequently S solves the autonomous scalar CIR martingale problem

 dS=kappa(m nu-S)dt+sqrt(2 kappa nu S)dB_t.                 (12)

Its law is independent of the feedback policy except through the supplied
initial radius law. This is an UNCONDITIONAL statement; a conditional radius
law after a record is generally not Gamma. Nonexplosion follows from this
scalar law, including at the origin. The expected number of classical exposed
or auxiliary counts is at most c integral_0^T E S_t dt, hence finite for finite
T and a finite first initial moment. No count-history factor enters (12).

If the supplied initial marginal radius is Gamma(shape m,scale nu), it stays
so at every deterministic time under feedback. Conditional product Gaussian
preparation independent of the controller is one sufficient physical premise.
The tail result itself only requires the stated initial marginal radius law;
it does not require that the full phase-space marginal remain Gaussian or
that each conditional field posterior be radial.

For any nonstationary supplied radius law, use the exact CIR transition kernel
in the integral below. In particular finite initial first moment gives

 E S_t=m nu+(E S_0-m nu)e^(-kappa t).                     (13)

5. EXACT LOCAL DEFECT AND ITS TRACE NORM
=======================================
The approximate covariance keeps (6) exactly on S<=B. At all other points the
only generator defect is

 A_theta(z)=(1-chi(S)) [-(i theta/2)] sum_ij
       [C_ij partial_zi partial_zj K_z
                  -bar C_ij partial_barzi partial_barzj K_z].  (14)

This is pointwise and uses no derivative or score of the source measure.
Let |alpha>=|z/sqrt(theta)> and let D_alpha be displacement. Then

 sum_ij C_ij partial_zi partial_zj K_z
 =theta^(-1)|v_C><alpha|,
 |v_C>=D_alpha sum_ij C_ij a_i^dagger a_j^dagger|0>.

The ket v_C is orthogonal to alpha and

 ||v_C||^2=2 sum_ij |C_ij|^2=2||C||_F^2.                 (15)

Thus (14) is a self-adjoint rank-two operator with EXACT norm

 ||A_theta(z)||_1=sqrt(2)(1-chi(S))||C(z)||_F
                <=sqrt(2) qF (S-B)_+.                    (16)

The theta from the commutator cancels the theta^(-1) in the two-excitation
derivative. Smallness comes from the rare amplitude tail, not a hidden theta
prefactor or an unproved smoothness assumption. For a positive subnormalized
measure mu, convexity yields ||integral A_theta dmu||_1<=integral||A_theta||_1
dmu. Cancellations can improve this estimate but are not assumed.

6. COUNTS, NO-EVENT EVOLUTION, LATCHES, AND FEEDBACK
==================================================
Annihilation counts have the exact coherent identity

 M_r K_z M_r^dagger=lambda_r(z)K_z.                        (17)

The physical no-event generator is the FULL L_Q minus its active positive
dispatch maps. The reference no-event generator is the full diffusion minus
the identical multiplication/dispatch maps lambda_r. Therefore (14) is also
the entire no-event defect. After an ignored trigger the outgoing and incoming
dispatch cancel in the same block, recovering the full generator. This checks
both gain and no-event pieces; equality of count rates alone would not suffice.

Work on the collection of subnormalized blocks indexed by the actual exposed
record, unread finite latch and retained controller. One can also refine to
the auxiliary fine count record for a proof and then discard it; the controller
still acts only on permitted information. Apply R_theta to each reference
block, giving a positive joint CQ operator sigma(t). Its mass is one.

The actual quantum CQ evolution under the same record-prefix policy is
positive and trace preserving, hence contractive on self-adjoint CQ trace
class. Scheduled exposure, controller updates and latch reset are shared
positive routing maps. The generator identity gives variation of constants:

 (1/2)||rho_Q(T)-sigma(T)||_(CQ,1)
 <=delta_prep+(1/2)integral_0^T E_reference||A_theta(Z_t)||_1 dt
 <=delta_prep+(qF/sqrt(2))integral_0^T E_reference(S_t-B)_+ dt.  (18)

Tracing out the final field and unread variables proves the COMPLETE permitted
record total-variation bound

 TV(P_Q^T,P_hat^T)
 <=min{1,delta_prep+(qF/sqrt(2))integral_0^T E(S_t-B)_+dt}. (19)

No spatial derivative of an arbitrary feedback policy is taken. The control
is fixed on a given CQ prefix. The residuals are weighted by the reference
record masses; summing all masses recovers the unconditional radius law (12).
The observation coefficient c does not appear in (19), but physical access,
expected event count, numerical readout cost and controller size remain paid
premises. The lack of c in this mathematical bound is not an assertion that
observations are free or that their conditional states are economical.

As with the preceding bridge, normalized physical conditional-state error
averaged against the TRUE record law can cost an additional factor 2. For a
particular rare record no uniform normalized-posterior estimate follows from
(19). The asserted result is joint CQ and complete-record prediction.

7. DOMAIN AND LIMIT PASSAGE WITHOUT A DENSITY ASSUMPTION
=======================================================
At fixed theta, K_z is a C-infinity trace-class-valued function. Its first and
second real derivatives have uniform trace-norm bounds of order theta^(-1/2)
and theta^(-1), by displacement covariance and finite one-/two-excitation
formulas. For a compactly supported initial measure, all radial moments of
(12) are finite on compact time intervals. Since |partial_bar W|=|C bar z|
<=q2 S^(3/2), the Itô drift of K has integrable trace norm. Operator-valued Itô
then gives the trace-norm weak/absolutely continuous lifted evolution, first
stopped on compact radius sets and then by moment domination. Counts have
integrable rates. The finite-latch block extension is obtained by the same
Itô product rule and the exact jump identity (17).

On the quantum side, H_theta is a direct sum of finite-total-number Hermitian
blocks, even with pair hopping and other quartics. The thermal number birth/
death process is nonexplosive. Total number has finite moments whenever the
initial coherent mixture has the corresponding radial moments. Finite-number
matrix elements form a common weighted trace-class core; on it (6) is a finite
algebraic identity. Radial moment localization and coherent Poisson tails pass
that identity and the contractive Duhamel inequality to the full quantum
instrument. This passage does not assume an exact positive P evolution.

To allow only E S_0<infinity, restrict the initial positive joint measure to
S_0<=R, without renormalizing. Both positive evolutions of this subprobability
mass satisfy the compact-preparation bound. Their omitted initial mass is
P(S_0>R) and remains that mass under each trace-preserving evolution, adding at
most P(S_0>R) to a half-trace comparison. The residual integral converges as
R tends to infinity: (S_t-B)_+<=S_t and the conditional CIR mean is
m nu+(S_0-m nu)e^(-kappa t), integrable under the original initial law. This
proves (18)--(19) for arbitrary supplied positive coherent mixtures with a
finite first radial moment, including atoms. No postulated density smoothing
or finite initial Fisher information is being charged implicitly.

8. EXPLICIT THERMAL-TAIL CERTIFICATE
===================================
For S_t/nu~Gamma(m,1), set

 b_star=B/nu=eta kappa/(theta q2).

Integrating the Gamma survival polynomial gives exactly

 E(S_t-B)_+=nu exp(-b_star)
                 sum_(j=0)^(m-1) (m-j)b_star^j/j!.

Therefore

 TV(P_Q^T,P_hat^T)
 <=min{1,delta_prep+(T qF nu/sqrt(2)) exp(-b_star)
                   sum_(j=0)^(m-1)(m-j)b_star^j/j!}.       (20)

At fixed m and coefficient envelopes this is exponentially small as theta
tends to zero and permits a correspondingly long mathematical record horizon.
It does not make simulation for an exponentially long horizon cheap. Mode
count remains substantive: a Gamma(m,1) radius is typically of order m, so
b_star must lie meaningfully above that scale for the displayed certificate
to be useful. An extensive q2 or a large number of modes can remove the gain.

If an arbitrary supplied positive preparation has an exponential radial moment,
the CIR moment-generating function gives, uniformly in t>=0,

 E exp(S_t/(2nu)) <=2^m E exp(S_0/(2nu)),
 E(S_t-B)_+ <=2nu *2^m E exp(S_0/(2nu)) exp(-B/(2nu)).

This optional rough bound charges that preparation moment explicitly. The
exact CIR excess-tail integral is usually stronger and remains the theorem.

9. A MODERATE-THETA EXAMPLE AND WHAT CHANGES
===========================================
For two-mode cross Kerr W=g I1 I2, |g|<=g0,

 C=[[0,g z1 z2],[g z1 z2,0]],
 q2=g0/2, qF=g0/sqrt(2)

follow from sqrt(I1 I2)<=S/2. At kappa=nu=g0=1, eta=1/2,
theta=1/16, one has B=b_star=16 and (20) becomes

 TV(P_Q^T,P_hat^T)<=delta_prep+9 T exp(-16).                (21)

For T=2 the mathematical bridge allowance is 18 exp(-16), about 2.026e-6.
This statement compares the quantum model to the NEW clipped-covariance
diffusion. It does not transfer the old two-bin exact formulas, old rounded
positive state, or old isotropic-sampler numerical errors to this diffusion.
No acquired measurement or simulator speedup is asserted by the example.

The constructive state for GENERATING the joint classical record is the current
2m-vector X, current latch, retained controller and numerical/random state.
Prediction after an already acquired record still requires its conditional
law; a particle approximation, density discretization, or other filter needs
its own error and observation-budget certificate. The supplied coherent-mixture
law and kinetics must be evaluated/sampled, rather than inferred for free.

This result changes the earlier mechanism: all quantum second-order terms
are retained where thermal noise makes them compatible with an ordinary
positive diffusion. Only their tail is changed. The earlier forward-Fisher
bridge retains value when one specifically wants the simpler isotropic source,
its established sampler, or its known predictions; it is not overwritten.

changing its name. The quantum bridge itself is complete at the continuous-
