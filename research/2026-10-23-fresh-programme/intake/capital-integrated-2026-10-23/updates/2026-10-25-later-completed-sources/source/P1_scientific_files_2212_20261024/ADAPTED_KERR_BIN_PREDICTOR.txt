1. SOURCE AND ANGLE CONVENTION
For m modes write z_j=sqrt(I_j)exp(i theta_j), with complex Wiener noise
E[dW_j dbarW_l]=delta_jl dt. Take

 dz_j=[-kappa z_j/2-i(omega_j+sum_l G_jl I_l)z_j]dt
                                      +sqrt(kappa sigma)dW_j,

where G is real symmetric. This corresponds to a Hamiltonian quadratic
in intensities. Higher-degree H(I), coherent hopping, and displacement
controls are not included. All kinetics and preparation are supplied.
Piecewise-constant changes of G, omega and the balanced output frame can
be chosen by a specified controller from already exposed outcomes.

Use measure product_j[dI_j dtheta_j/(2pi)]. The stationary product law is
p_eq=sigma^(-m)exp(-sum I_j/sigma). A readout port has

 lambda_r=c_r |u_r^dagger z|^2,
 sum_r c_r u_r u_r^dagger=c I, hence Lambda=c sum_j I_j.

For a two-mode +/- first-port latch, specifically
lambda_+=(c/2)|z1+z2|^2, lambda_-=(c/2)|z1-z2|^2.
Once the first event is latched, subsequent events in that bin are not
read and cannot change the classical source field. Propagation after
that trigger is therefore the unconditional diffusion. No unread-trigger
control is permitted. A vector of separate per-port OR flags would
generally leave an unbalanced active frame and is outside the simple
sector closure below.

Throughout, phi=theta1-theta2 and D=I1-I2. The cross-Kerr drift is
dot z1=-ig I2 z1, dot z2=-ig I1 z2, so dot phi=gD. The density evolves
as cos(phi-gDt) in the bath-free case. The opposite convention
arg(bar z1 z2) reverses the sign in that cosine.

2. EXACT FINE-HISTORY FAMILY
For angular Fourier sector k in Z^m, let s_j=|k_j|/2 and
beta_j=(G^T k)_j. Its radial no-count forward generator is the sum of

 kappa sigma I_j partial_j^2 + kappa(I_j+sigma)partial_j+kappa
       -kappa sigma k_j^2/(4I_j) -(c-i beta_j)I_j,

plus the scalar i k.omega. The ansatz

 p_k(I)=product_j I_j^(s_j) exp(-a_j I_j) P(I)                 (1)

is exactly preserved, with Re a_j>0 and P a finite polynomial. The
apparently singular factor is regular in Cartesian coordinates:
I_j^(|k_j|/2)exp(i k_j theta_j)=z_j^(k_j^+)bar z_j^(k_j^-).

The rate and coefficient equations are

 a_j'=kappa a_j-kappa sigma a_j^2+c-i beta_j,                 (2)
 dot C_n=sum_j kappa sigma(n_j+1)(n_j+|k_j|+1) C_(n+e_j)
    +{sum_j[kappa(1-2sigma a_j)n_j+kappa(s_j+1)
                              -kappa sigma a_j(|k_j|+1)]
                                      +i k.omega} C_n.       (3)

Thus propagation never raises polynomial degree. It generally changes
the widths separately for different past Fourier-sector paths.

For one mode put zeta=c-i beta,
M_zeta=[[-kappa/2,kappa sigma],[zeta,kappa/2]], E=exp(t M_zeta),
u=E11+E12 a0, v=E21+E22 a0. Then a(t)=v/u, and

 T_t[I^s exp(-a0 I)]=exp(kappa t/2)u^(-|k|-1) I^s exp(-a(t)I).

Integer powers avoid any complex-power branch convention. Multiplying
the initial expression by I^n amounts to (-partial_a0)^n. Explicitly,
with r=|k|+1, x=E12/u and y=I/u^2, the multiplier after propagation is

 sum_(l=0)^n binomial(n,l)(r+l)_(n-l) x^(n-l)y^l,            (4)

where (a)_j is the rising factorial. Tensor products give the multimode
formula. M_zeta^2=(kappa^2/4+kappa sigma zeta)I, so entire cosh/sinh
series give a branch-free evaluator.

Well-posedness of (2): if a=u_real+i v_real and Re zeta>=0, then
u_real'=kappa u_real-kappa sigma u_real^2+kappa sigma v_real^2
+Re zeta. Positive initial real part stays positive. Also
d|a|/dt<=kappa|a|+|zeta|, precluding finite-time blowup. Starting at
a0=1/sigma, all sectors generated below retain Re a>=1/sigma; hence
|u(t)|>=exp(kappa t/2), and no denominator approaches zero in this scope.

A count multiplies the density by
lambda_r=sum_(i,j)c_r bar(u_ri)u_rj z_i bar z_j.
For i!=j it shifts k to k'=k+e_i-e_j and multiplies P by

 I_i^(1[k_i<0]) I_j^(1[k_j>0]).                             (5)

For i=j it leaves k unchanged and multiplies P by I_i. These formulas
include k sign crossings, with no negative or fractional polynomial
power. The quantity deg(P)+|k|_1/2 increases by at most one per count.
From a product Gaussian, J fine time-resolved clicks therefore give
deg(P)+|k|_1/2<=J and sum k_j=0.

This degree bound is NOT a bound on the number of retained components.
Each click can create 1+m(m-1) distinct (k,a) components; generally their
different sector histories give different width vectors and cannot be
merged. A safe component bound is [1+m(m-1)]^J, with a finite polynomial
per component. The known fine record/control history and coefficient
precision are real costs.

Record likelihoods are computable: only k=0 terms survive angular
integration, and a monomial contributes product_j n_j!/a_j^(n_j+1).
The full exact filtered density is nonnegative by its Markov/Bayes
construction. Its complex sector terms need not be individually positive;
uncontrolled cancellation is not an acceptable numerical certificate.

3. AN EXACT POSITIVE FIVE-PARAMETER NODE
Take two-mode stationary p_eq, a first +/- trigger at a specified time s,
and unconditional propagation for tau=T-s. Let a_s solve (2) with
zeta=c, a_0=1/sigma, and define

 U_c(s)=E_c11(s)+E_c12(s)/sigma,
 A_s=sigma^(-2)exp(kappa s)/U_c(s)^2,
 q_s(I)=A_s exp[-a_s(I1+I2)], Q_s=A_s/a_s^2.

Here q_s is the no-count subdensity and Q_s its mass. Define

 u0=E_0,11(tau)+E_0,12(tau)a_s,
 b=(E_0,21(tau)+E_0,22(tau)a_s)/u0,
 ug=E_(ig),11(tau)+E_(ig),12(tau)a_s,
 a=(E_(ig),21(tau)+E_(ig),22(tau)a_s)/ug,
 r=E_0,12(tau)u0,
 eta=(u0/|ug|)^4, delta=Re a-b, v=Im a,
 w=1/(1+rb)=exp(-kappa tau)b/a_s.                           (6)

The normalized propagated clicked state is exactly

 f^+/-_(b,w,eta,delta,v)
 =(1-w) b^2 exp(-bS)
   +w b^3 exp(-bS)[S/2 +/- eta exp(-delta S)sqrt(I1I2)
                                                cos(phi-vD)], (7)

where S=I1+I2. Every parameter is real and satisfies
b>0, 0<=w<=1, 0<=eta<=1, delta>=0, v in R.
Thus (7) is normalized and nonnegative: sqrt(I1I2)<=S/2 and
eta exp(-delta S)<=1. The entire current node uses five real parameters
and its sign. A known local phase offset can be added as a sixth real
coordinate for phase interventions; it does not alter positivity.

To establish the cone, compare Re Phi_(ig)^tau(a_s) with
Phi_0^tau(a_s). The former has the additional nonnegative term
kappa sigma(Im a)^2 in its real Riccati equation, so delta>=0. Moreover
log(|ug|/u0)=kappa sigma integral_0^tau delta(t)dt>=0,
giving eta<=1. The radial derivative identity (4) gives r>=0 and (7).
For kappa>0 a_s lies in [1/sigma,1/sigma+c/kappa]; the free b is between
1/sigma and a_s. Therefore w<=exp(-kappa tau).

This cone family is also closed under subsequent no-count propagation
with any balanced c>=0, and under unconditional propagation (c=0).
Given the equivalent radial r=(1-w)/(wb) for w>0, propagate the radial
width b by Phi_c and complex width a=b+delta+iv by Phi_(c+ig).
If U_b=E_c11+E_c12 b and U_a=E_(c+ig),11+E_(c+ig),12 a, then

 r'=r U_b^2+E_c12 U_b, eta'=eta(U_b/|U_a|)^4,
 b'=Phi_c(b), delta'=Re Phi_(c+ig)(a)-b', v'=Im Phi_(c+ig)(a),
 w'=1/(1+r'b').

The unnormalized radial prefactor is multiplied by exp(kappa t)U_b^-4;
its new total mass gives the no-count likelihood. At w=0 use the pure
Gaussian limit directly. The comparison argument preserves the cone.
A further marked count generally creates higher Fourier bands; this
five-parameter family is not asserted closed under arbitrary later clicks.

Positive sampling/evaluation: conditional on the first mixture component
in (7), S~Gamma(2,b), I1/S is uniform, and both phases are uniform.
For its second component, S~Gamma(3,b), I1/S is uniform, and the phase
difference density is proportional to
1+/-2 eta exp(-delta S)sqrt(U(1-U))cos(phi-vD).
Reject a uniform proposed phase with acceptance half this expression;
the mean acceptance probability is 1/2. The global phase is uniform.
All operations are positive. Numerical density evaluation can use
1+A cos t=(1-A)+2A cos^2(t/2), or the minus analogue using sin^2,
with A in [0,1], to avoid spurious negative cancellation.

4. THE EXACT COARSE-BIN STATE IS NOT A FINITE SECTOR SUM
For the actual first-port bin, the signed latch density is

 P_+(T)-P_-(T)=integral_0^T U_free(T-s)[(lambda_+-lambda_-)q_s]ds.

Its k=(1,-1) coefficient is

 sqrt(I1I2) integral_0^T W(s) exp[-a(s)I1-bar(a(s))I2]ds,
 W(s)=c sigma^(-2)exp(kappa T)/[U_c(s)^2 |ug(s)|^4]>0,
 a(s)=Phi_(ig)^(T-s)(a_s).                                 (8)

At the endpoint a'(T)=c-ig, so d(Re a(s))/ds at T equals c>0.
Consequently, for every c>0,T>0 and every real g, the pushforward of
W(s)ds under 2 Re a(s) has a non-atomic positive component. It is a
compact measure on positive rates. On I1=I2=R, divide the coefficient
by R: the result is its Laplace transform.

Any finite sum of sector exponential-polynomial components would instead
give a finite exponential polynomial in R. Such a function satisfies a
nonzero constant-coefficient differential equation P(partial_R)f=0.
The Laplace representation then implies
integral P(-lambda) exp(-lambda R)dmu(lambda)=0. Uniqueness of the
Laplace transform for compact finite measures implies P(-lambda)mu=0,
forcing support on finitely many roots. This contradicts the continuous
component. One elementary uniqueness proof differentiates at zero and
uses polynomial density on the compact rate interval.

Thus ONE finite first-port bin leaves the finite exponential-polynomial
sector family. This failure is already present at g=0; it is not evidence
of specifically quantum or nonlinear impossibility. It does not exclude
richer finite-parameter special functions. For example, when kappa=0,
the same sector integral is a divided difference proportional to

 sqrt(I1I2) exp(-S/sigma)exp(-igTD)
       [1-exp(-(cS-igD)T)]/(cS-igD).

A continuous mixture can therefore have a short special-function
description for a particular record. No universal finite-dimensional
filter impossibility is claimed. Repeated bins generally introduce
additional hidden-time integration variables and/or a growing family.

5. POSITIVE MIDPOINT CURRENT STATE WITH A COMPLETE-LAW BOUND
For each sign r let K_r(s)=U_free(T-s)[lambda_r q_s]. This is a positive
unnormalized current-state density, with mass
z(s)=c Q_s/a_s, independent of the sign and g. Use N equal midpoint
cells, h=T/N, s_j=(j+1/2)h, and

 Q_r^N=h sum_j K_r(s_j), Z_N=h sum_j z(s_j).

The approximate conditional clicked density is Q_r^N/Z_N: a positive
mixture of N five-parameter kernels (7), with weights h z(s_j)/Z_N.
The no-click conditional density is the exact product Gaussian of rate
a_T. Use exact label masses

 p0=Q_T, p_+=p_-=(1-Q_T)/2.                                (9)

These probabilities follow from sign symmetry and no-count survival.
Hence the construction does not alter the past label likelihoods in
order to obtain a posterior bound.

The commutator derivative is
K_r'=U_free(T-s)[J_r L_kill-L_free J_r]q_s.
Since q_s=A_s exp(-a_s S), its input signed density is

 [F.grad lambda_r+kappa(2sigma a_s-1)lambda_r
                        -kappa sigma c-c S lambda_r]q_s.     (10)

Unconditional propagation contracts signed L1. Under the normalized
Gaussian of rate a_s,

 E lambda_r=c/a_s, E(S lambda_r)=3c/a_s^2,
 E|F.grad lambda_r|=2c|g|/(pi a_s^2).

Because a_s>=1/sigma and Q_s<=1,

 ||K_r'(s)||_1
 <=Q_s[c(2|g|/pi+3c)/a_s^2+kappa c(3sigma-1/a_s)]
 <=B=c[(2|g|/pi+3c)sigma^2+3kappa sigma].                    (11)

A midpoint cell has L1 error at most B h^2/4. Thus
E_r=||P_r-Q_r^N||_1<=B T^2/(4N).
Since |p_r-Z_N|<=E_r,

 p_r ||P_r/p_r-Q_r^N/Z_N||_1 <=2E_r.

With the exact masses (9), the joint (exposed label,current field) TV
and the TRUE-label-weighted average conditional-state TV are at most

 epsilon_quad=sum_r E_r=B T^2/(2N).                         (12)

This joint bound contracts through every common future Markov/control/
observation kernel, including feedback based on the retained first label.
It certifies the complete past-plus-future record when future evolution
is evaluated exactly. It does not assert a uniform error for an individual
rare prefix: a conditional bound for r is E_r/p_r. For a generic
approximate joint law, restarting on true label probabilities can cost
an additional likelihood allowance. Here (9) and the explicit branch
normalization establish (12) directly instead of invoking that shortcut.

Concrete unit instance: kappa=sigma=c=|g|=T=1. Using pi>=3 gives B<=20/3.
N=334 therefore gives epsilon_quad<=5/501=0.00998003992015968... .
Each clicked current state is a positive mixture of 334 five-parameter

This is a conservative theorem, not an acquired measurement or a runtime

6. POSITIVE PARAMETER ROUNDING AND LIKELIHOOD ARITHMETIC
Use (b,w,eta,delta,v), not the potentially large r. Rounding within
b>=b_min>0, w,eta in [0,1], delta>=0 preserves positivity exactly.
For cone-preserving parameter paths, direct integration gives

 ||partial_b f||_1<=sqrt(3)/b,
 ||partial_w f||_1<=2,
 ||partial_eta f||_1<=1/2,
 ||partial_delta f||_1<=3/(2b),
 ||partial_v f||_1<=2/(pi b).

For b: the two normalized gamma components have scores shape/b-S;
the angular modulation integrates to zero, so their variances give
the bound sqrt(3)/b. The other estimates integrate |cos| or |sin|
and gamma moments. A local phase offset has L1 derivative at most 1/2.
Therefore the node TV perturbation is at most

 sqrt(3)/(2b_min)|Delta b|+|Delta w|+|Delta eta|/4
       +3/(4b_min)|Delta delta|+1/(pi b_min)|Delta v|.         (13)

The unit certificate can use rational upper constants 1,1,1/4,3/4,1/3.
If mixture weights change from alpha_j to alphahat_j, their additional
TV allowance is (1/2)sum|alpha_j-alphahat_j|. The no-click Gaussian
rate has TV error <=|Delta a|/b_min. Label probabilities require their
own simplex/TV allowance. Summing these with (12) gives a positive
finite-bit implementation, including true-prefix likelihood effects.

For the matrix coefficients, use the entire-function evaluation of
M_zeta with M_zeta^2=(kappa^2/4+kappa sigma zeta)I. Taylor tails can be
bounded by 2R^(n+1)/(n+1)! once the subsequent term ratio is <=1/2;
all arithmetic can be outward rational intervals. In the stated unit

series plus 160-bit outward dyadics suffice. Complex division uses
|u|^2 with an explicitly positive lower endpoint. Rounded node
parameters and weights are stored as finite rationals; the cone is
enforced with clipping and its movement included in (13), not ignored.

The companion implementation records the final interval/error allowance
and finite rounded current state. No floating solver or
physical Fock cutoff is needed. Queries of later finite outcome laws
must also pay their coefficient/quadrature allowances. A positive

7. REMAINING SCOPE AND PREDECESSORS
The five-parameter node is closed under free/balanced-no-count dynamics,
not under every further marked jump. More bins can grow polynomial
degree, distinct widths, Fourier sectors, positive history-mixture nodes
and integration dimension. No fixed finite memory theorem has been
established. A broad angle truncation might control harmonic complexity
but would not by itself compress these radial-width mixtures.

The old exact ordinary-loss first-port benchmark remains unchanged.
Its scalar characteristic flow is the zero-replenishment version of a
special angular band calculation. The recovered hot two-latch benchmark
already uses the m=2, |k_j|=1, constant-polynomial Riccati sector and a
two-dimensional hidden-time integral. The present general fine-history
updates and positive bin-end state make the conditional-current-state
interface explicit; they do not reclassify that benchmark as a closed
all-history filter.
