PRIVATE ASTRA P1 / 2026-10-24 / CONSEQUENCES OF THE NEW CONSTRUCTION

This note specializes the already used joint-CQ simulation principle. It
does not claim that positive phase-space simulation, adaptive sampling,
filtering, or trace-norm contraction is a newly discovered framework.

1. THE STATE-SELECTION CRITERION ACTUALLY USED

A positive current parameter law mu over x is a useful physical predictor
when three concrete interfaces are available:

 (a) a positive normalized certificate kernel K_x and the supplied joint
     preparation/controller law;
 (b) evaluable positive classical transitions for the permitted dynamics,
     instruments, exposed records and actual controller;
 (c) an integrable physical generator defect
       E_x=L_Q(K_x)-G K_x
     in trace norm, plus the analogous instrument defect if it is not zero.

Use subnormalized blocks indexed by permitted records, unread latch and
retained controller. The coherent lift of every reference block is positive.
Shared physical instruments and controller routing contract the joint trace
norm. Variation of constants therefore gives

 delta(T)=(1/2)||rho_Q,CQ(T)-sigma_ref,CQ(T)||1
          <=delta_prep+(1/2)integral E_ref||E_Xt||1 dt.

The current construction supplies (b), including finite precision and random
bits, instead of leaving a formally positive diffusion unevaluated. Its key
physical simplification in(c) is that the EXACT quantum covariance is retained
where the thermal covariance makes it positive. The only continuous-source
defect is a controlled high-amplitude tail. Measurements that multiply coherent
kernels do not add a hidden backaction defect; future control is compared on
the same actual record. The local feedback theorem bounds the relevant tail
even after adaptive mixing destroys a product thermal marginal.

This is a state-selection principle in an operational metric. It does not
identify the sampled classical field with a literal underlying quantum state
or assert that its conditional law has a short finite parameter list. In
particular, the distribution may carry non-Gaussian, correlated information
created by the instrument. The implemented controller and any retained output
statistic are part of the state, not free external memory.

2. A POSTSELECTION CONSEQUENCE

Let C be the trace-norm closed convex set of multimode coherent-state mixtures.
Write the final exact and reference CQ states as blocks

 p_y rho_y and q_y sigma_y,

where each sigma_y belongs to C when q_y>0. The record y can include all
permitted exposures and the final controller. Let

 d_C(rho)=inf_(omega in C) (1/2)||rho-omega||1.

For q_y>0, the triangle inequality gives

 p_y d_C(rho_y)
 <=(1/2)||p_y rho_y-q_y sigma_y||1+(1/2)|p_y-q_y|.

For q_y=0, choose any normalized coherent mixture; the same inequality follows
from d_C<=1. Sum over y and use TV(p,q)<=delta(T):

 sum_y p_y d_C(rho_y)<=2 delta(T).                       (1)

Hence for any a>0,

 P_quantum{d_C(rho_y)>=a}<=min(1,2 delta(T)/a).           (2)

Every coherent mixture is separable across the original physical modes. Thus
the same upper bound applies to the probability of a record whose conditional
state has trace distance at least a from the larger separable-state set.
This is an operational limitation on what the admitted controls/instruments
can prepare with nonnegligible success probability in the certified regime.
It does not rule out strong nonclassicality on sufficiently rare records.

For the continuous two-mode clipped model with theta1/16, eta1/2 and horizon
3/2, exact thermal preparation gives delta<1.51922485871e-6. Consequently a
conditional state at distance>=0.1 from coherent mixtures can occur with
probability at most3.03844971742e-5. This numerical consequence uses the
CONTINUOUS joint-CQ certificate, not an unaudited numerical trajectory.

A common additional final quantum measurement preserves the JOINT CQ error
allowance for the combined record and final measurement statistics. It does
not give a delta bound on each rare normalized posterior. Sampling that final
measurement classically still requires an evaluable kernel/instrument
interface, and an arbitrary new instrument need not preserve the coherent
mixture set C. A trace bound does not acquire a measurement implementation
or an inverse filter.

3. WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH

The nonclassicality bound is not a first resource-theory result and the small
state simulator is not a proof of advantage over positive-P or tensor methods.
Positive phase-space circuit simulation, adaptive sampling and robustness are
explicit in Mari--Eisert (arXiv1208.3660v2), and more general ordered-PQD
conditions appear in Rahimi-Keshari--Ralph--Caves (arXiv1511.06526v3). The
present achievement to assess is the verified physical regime and constructive
tail-residual interface for continually replenished nonlinear dynamics.

In particular, a small trajectory state does not imply an economical inverse
problem after a supplied rare history. Neither the theorem nor the finite
algorithm provides uniform posterior sample complexity, free kinetic access,
or free correlated preparation. The exact coherence-band evaluator remains
the stronger evaluator for the special two-bin experiment where it closes.

4. PHYSICAL SCALING IS NOT A FREE CLASSICALITY PARAMETER

For a density interaction the physical per-quantum Kerr coefficient is
U=theta g (and U_i=theta w_i for an on-site term), while thermal mean
occupation is nbar=nu/theta. The tail exponent is controlled by U relative
to kappa and the permitted hopping budget. It is not improved by merely
renaming the amplitude coordinates or by asserting large occupation.

Indeed a rescaling theta'=r theta, z'=sqrt(r)z, nu'=r nu, g'=g/r
leaves U and nbar fixed. Keeping the physical observed channels fixed also
requires c_r'=c_r/r, so their physical rates and Cox intensities agree.
The field rescaling gives C'=C, D'=rD, B'=rB and qF'=qF/r;
the product qF E(S-B)+ and the physical coherent projector are unchanged.
Reducing theta while holding g,nu fixed specifies a DIFFERENT physical
family: the per-quantum nonlinearity decreases while the thermal occupation
increases. All statements using that asymptotic must retain this premise.

The demonstrated advantage of coordinates is avoiding an occupation-space
enumeration. Runtime, temperature, physical nonlinear strength, clipping
error and preparation access remain substantive resources.
