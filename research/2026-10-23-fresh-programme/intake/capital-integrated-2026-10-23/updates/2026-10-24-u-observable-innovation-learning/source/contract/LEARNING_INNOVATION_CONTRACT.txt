ONE-STREAM ACQUISITION BY AN OBSERVABLE INNOVATION INVARIANT
2026-10-23. Opening frozen in LEARNING_OPENING_FROZEN.txt.
Internal derivation. No novelty, broad learning, or superiority claim.
This supplies a precise scoped family/contract before selecting experiments.

1. Environment and the actual learner interface
------------------------------------------------
Hidden X_t is a sign. There are two actions a in{0,1} and sign observations.
  X_(t+1)=X_t Z_t,      Y_t=X_t E_t.
Conditional on action a_t, Z_t are independent signs of mean lambda_(a_t).
E_t are iid signs of unknown mean eta in[0,1], independent of all Z and the
initial hidden state. State-label inversion accounts for the eta>=0 convention.
The initial hidden state or its distribution need not be known. Parameters
eta,lambda0,lambda1 are NOT supplied to the learner. Only actions and Y are
logged. There is no reset API, hidden-state identifier, or exact matrix oracle.

These symmetry/independent-innovation assumptions are genuine model promises.
The derived features are engineered for this family, not a claim that the
learner discovers the family or a new general intelligence mechanism. A fair
incumbent receives the SAME structural promises, observations, and budget.

2. Exact cancellation and a mixing-free acquisition unit
--------------------------------------------------------
Let t_i=3i and choose exploration actions independently from a known fixed
full-support distribution. Define each block's observed products
  R_i=Y_t Y_(t+1), S_i=Y_(t+1) Y_(t+2), T_i=Y_t Y_(t+2).
Then exactly
  R_i=Z_t E_t E_(t+1),
  S_i=Z_(t+1) E_(t+1) E_(t+2),
  T_i=Z_t Z_(t+1) E_t E_(t+2).                               (2.1)

Consequently the block vectors (a_t,a_(t+1),R_i,S_i,T_i) are independent
across i. The hidden state and bridge innovation Z_(t+2) cancel completely.
No hidden mixing assumption is needed for this statement. The observations
within a block and the features within a block are NOT independent.

Both actions a_t,a_(t+1) must be selected before observing the block and
independently of its observation/transition innovations. In particular,
selecting a_t using Y_t or a_(t+1) using Y_(t+1) can destroy the moment
identities. For the conditional-on-full-schedule confidence statement below,
the ENTIRE exploration action schedule is drawn independently in advance.
Adaptive future block scripts are not covered by that conditioning argument;
they require a separate martingale/random-count analysis. Independent action
coins fixed ex ante require no hidden-state-dependent or curated curriculum.

B blocks consume3B real observations and the corresponding stream actions,
including bridge actions whose data are not used in (2.1). Initial/final
off-by-one steps must be reported by the executable. Discarded steps count.

3. Acquired counterfactual moments and identifiability
-----------------------------------------------------
Set s=eta^2. The observable action-conditioned quantities are
  c_a=E[R|a_t=a]=s lambda_a,
  c_ab=E[T|a_t=a,a_(t+1)=b]=s lambda_a lambda_b.                (3.1)
For any reference b with c_b!=0,
  lambda_a=c_ab/c_b,             s=c_b^2/c_bb.                (3.2)
These identities identify the controlled symmetric transition laws and the
emission magnitude without state labels or calibration of eta. If eta=0,
all observations are independent fair coins under every policy and the
hidden transition parameters are unidentifiable. If lambda0=lambda1=0,
the future hidden states are fresh fair coins, and eta is unidentifiable
from the stationary future. A denominator-near-zero estimate must be treated
as partial identification, not repaired into certainty by clipping a ratio.

For confidence level1-delta, use six features: two c_a and four c_ab.
For each feature, condition on its acquired action counts N_j. Its selected
block products are independent signs, even conditional on the action schedule.
With L=log(12/delta), radius
  r_j=min(2,sqrt(2L/N_j)) for N_j>0, and r_j=2 for N_j=0,
simultaneously covers all six true means with probability at least1-delta.
The statistical unit is an independent acquired BLOCK, not an asserted
independent raw observation. No hidden mixing factor enters this confidence.

Freeze the actual representation
  C={ (s,lambda0,lambda1) in[0,1]x[-1,1]^2:
       |s lambda_a-chat_a|<=r_a,
       |s lambda_a lambda_b-chat_ab|<=r_ab for all a,b }.       (3.3)
It is a compact semialgebraic confidence set, not an exact-moment oracle.
Keep raw counts, propensities, feature sums, radii, stream hash, fit cost,
and any model misspecification/validation failure alongside it.

For a nonzero empirical reference chat_b,
  |lambda_a-chat_ab/chat_b| <=(r_ab+r_b)/|chat_b|,              (3.4)
intersected with[-1,1]. This follows directly from c_ab=lambda_a c_b and
|lambda_a|<=1. A positive lower bound |chat_b|-r_b>0 additionally certifies
that (3.2) is an identified, nondegenerate parametrization.

Computational accounting: an exact robust planner over (3.3) is not free.
A finite interval subdivision can preserve coverage: moment maps are
Lipschitz at most2 for s lambda_a and at most3 for s lambda_a lambda_b
in coordinatewise sup norm. Retain every grid cell whose center satisfies
the expanded radii. Planning error must include the remaining cell diameter.
Do not present a coarse numerical grid as the exact confidence set.

4. Learned initial-prior forgetting, without a reset oracle
----------------------------------------------------------
Suppose C itself certifies some action b has |lambda_b|<=lbar<1. Apply b
for K real steps. Unconditionally its hidden distribution is within
lbar^K/2 of uniform. A stronger FILTER certificate retains every observation.
Write a posterior mean as m=tanh(l). Transition and observation updates are
  l -> atanh(lambda_b tanh(l)),   l -> l+atanh(eta y).
The first map has derivative magnitude at most |lambda_b|; the second
preserves differences. After the first transition, arbitrary initial priors
have logit difference at most 2 atanh(lbar). Hence after K observed b steps,
the posterior TV distance induced by any two initial priors is at most
  atanh(lbar) lbar^(K-1).                                    (4.1)
If eta=1 the observation directly reveals the sign and the same conclusion
holds by an endpoint limit. If lbar=0, one transition completely forgets
the prior. The bound on lbar is ACQUIRED through C. It is separate from
uncertainty in the parameter: (4.1) holds for each parameter in C, not
between different parameters.

This is an approximate washout implemented by ordinary actions. Every
transition, observation, and elapsed step is charged; no trajectory
or physical hidden state is reset. If C still contains |lambda_b|=1 for
both actions, this particular initialization certificate is unavailable and
the learner must retain the current belief uncertainty or abstain. It must
not insert a convenient stationary prior as though a reset had occurred.
The full-history incumbent keeps all observations as well. Once two
same-parameter filters have converged, later transitions cannot increase
their logit difference, since |lambda_a|<=1, and observed updates preserve
it. No data-discard handicap is needed to obtain this certificate.

Crucially, an unconditional nearly uniform hidden distribution is not a
uniform POSTERIOR after reading the washout observations. Planning carries
the parameter-indexed posterior obtained from the actual history. A rigorous
confidence object at time t is the compatible pair set
  { (theta,m_t(theta,m_initial)): theta in C, m_initial in[-1,1] }.
Use the closure of this bounded set. It is compact; with a uniform positive
history-likelihood bound (for example eta bounded below1), the image itself
is already compact. The closure conservatively retains limiting beliefs at
zero-likelihood deterministic endpoints. The true parameter/belief pair is
contained on the same moment-coverage event. This set, or an outer
approximation, must be propagated for robust task values.

5. Reuse and observable-value uncertainty
-----------------------------------------
For any fixed finite-horizon policy pi and two parameters, with the SAME
initial hidden prior, coupling gives a uniform bound on the action/observation
trajectory laws over H actions and H+1 observations:

  TV <= [H max_a|lambda_a-lambda'_a|+(H+1)|eta-eta'|]/2.       (5.1)

This remains true for arbitrary within-horizon feedback: coupled policies
choose the same actions until their observed histories differ. For any
known observable return in[0,1], value discrepancy is at most this TV.
Initialization error adds zeta. Thus C can support simultaneous task-value
intervals for newly revealed bounded observable objectives, without refitting
the dynamics. Every new objective's planning/execution cost is still charged.

A more revealing bound avoids demanding identification of eta and lambda
separately. Let c=max_a|s lambda_a|. Starting from a uniform hidden prior,
for ANY closed-loop policy over T observations,

  TV(observed trajectory, fair-observation reference)
    <=(1/2) sum_{k>=1} binom(T,2k)c^k.                       (5.2)

Proof: expand each fixed observation word's hidden-process likelihood in
products of signs. Its even-subset coefficient of size2k is
eta^(2k) times products of lambda along k disjoint nonempty time intervals,
so its magnitude is at most c^k; odd coefficients vanish. The expansion is
valid pointwise for the action sequence selected by that word's past, hence
also for feedback policies. Sum the absolute density-ratio bound. The
leading term is T(T-1)c/4. This is a finite-horizon upper bound, not an
optimal asymptotic statement in T.

With an ARBITRARY hidden prior, first apply one charged blind action and
then take the T observations. The first odd-subset coefficient is bounded
by |eta lambda_b|<=sqrt(c); the remaining paired intervals contribute c^k.
Even coefficients have the same bound as above. Therefore

  TV <=[(1+sqrt(c))^T-1]/2.                                 (5.3)

These formulas quantify why ill-conditioned hidden-parameter recovery need
not be a task-control failure. If the acquired first-lag moments certify
small c, observable returns can be nearly insensitive to the unidentified
parameters. This statement must use observable objectives and an explicit
horizon; it does not license hidden-state reward functions or arbitrary
long-horizon extrapolation.

6. A sharp near-alias acquisition barrier, not a missing-action example
----------------------------------------------------------------------
There is nevertheless an unavoidable eta^-4 cost for identifying a nonzero
action contrast in this same tiny, fast-mixing family. Let eta in(0,1/2]
be KNOWN (making the lower bound stronger), with initial hidden prior uniform.
Compare
  model0: lambda0=lambda1=1/8;
  model1: lambda0=1/8, lambda1=1/8+d, 0<d<=1/8.
All transition probabilities are strictly positive and uniformly bounded
away from deterministic behavior. Neither model has an exact reset action.
Allow ANY adaptive one-stream exploration policy, not only random actions.

For posterior hidden mean m after an observation, filtering is
  f_lambda(m,y)=(lambda m+eta y)/(1+eta y lambda m).
Starting at the first observation, |m|<=2eta is invariant for lambda<=1/4.
On that range
  |partial_m f| <=16/49<1/3,
  |partial_lambda f| <=128eta/49<8eta/3.
Under the same observed history and chosen actions, the two model filters
therefore differ by at most4eta d. Their next observable sign biases differ
by at most3eta^2 d, and each bias is at most1/8 in magnitude. Consequently
the one-step conditional KL is at most10eta^4 d^2, using KL<=chi-square for
the two Bernoulli laws. Chain rule gives

  D(P_stream,model1 || P_stream,model0) <=10N eta^4 d^2.       (6.1)

The policy's action distribution adds no KL conditional on the same history.
To distinguish the two models with both error probabilities <=delta<1/2,
binary data processing requires KL at least
  (1-2delta)log((1-delta)/delta).
Thus any bounded-budget learner needs

  N >=(1-2delta)log((1-delta)/delta)/(10eta^4 d^2).            (6.2)

The moment procedure reaches this eta^-4 d^-2 scale for estimating the
action-conditioned correlation contrast, up to confidence/allocation constants.
This is a genuine information obstruction even with two hidden states,
uniformly fast mixing, full action access, and an adaptive explorer. It is
not the earlier untried-action or exponentially long secret-trigger example.

For an observable one-step persistence objective, the intervention contrast
is eta^2 d. Resolving a return difference of that scale requires the usual
inverse-squared observable accuracy. Demanding fixed d parameter accuracy
while eta tends to zero adds a statistical burden that a fixed-tolerance
control task may not require. Keep these targets distinct in the benchmark.

7. Structural falsification and the held-out execution contract
--------------------------------------------------------------
Symmetric emissions cannot be silently assumed from a good fit. If instead
E[Y|X]=b+eta X with b!=0, even stationary symmetric hidden transitions have
  Cov(Y0Y1,Y3Y4)=b^2 eta^2 lambda^2(1+lambda)^2,
which is nonzero in general. Thus the exact independent-block confidence
fails for this asymmetric family. An off-family validation must report this
failure, not keep the same confidence guarantee by assertion. A held-out
moment residual test may detect misspecification but cannot universally
certify the full symmetry promise from finite data.

Before revealing the two objectives, freeze C and the learned model(s).
After reveal, use one shared acquired model with a belief-state planner.
An equally informed constrained-HMM likelihood/EM learner using the whole
ordered stream and the SAME planner is the important stateful incumbent;
an action-frequency baseline is insufficient. If a generic PSR/HMM baseline
uses a supplied rank/basis or a different symmetry promise, record that.

Charge exploration; any acquired washout; predictive/model checks; fitting,
memory, interval-grid and planning computations; per-objective validation
trajectories; and actual reuse actions. No model refit on a held-out objective
may be called objective-blind reuse. An evaluator may know ground truth only
behind a separate API/provenance boundary and must not supply it to the
learner or call it an acquired certificate.

8. Useful acquired value certificate, avoiding unstable parameter recovery
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
The executed stream gives a useful example of certifying task values without
claiming a sharp computed confidence interval for the point-model planner.
Write D=c0-c1, A=(lambda0+lambda1)/2, and u_t=eta E[X_t]. Consider the fixed
observable sign-feedback policy: a=0 when Y_t=+1, and a=1 when Y_t=-1.
Elementary conditioning, using E[Y_t|X_t]=eta X_t, gives
  u_(t+1)=A u_t+D/2.                                       (8.1)
For normalized positive-output reward
  r_t=[(1+Y_(t+1))/2-k a_t+k]/(1+k),
  E[r_t]=[1/2+D/4+k/2+(A+k)u_t/2]/(1+k).                   (8.2)
After K fixed washout actions, |u_0|<=r^K for ANY initial hidden prior.
This is an expectation BEFORE the washout observations; it is not a claim
that the conditional posterior after observing them is uniform. The simple
policy ignores intermediate washout observations and uses the current sign;
the full-history comparison planner remains free to retain/use everything.

Obtain intervals for D directly from acquired c0,c1, and an outer interval
for A from the simultaneous lambda intervals. Propagate (8.1)-(8.2) by exact
interval arithmetic. This is conservative and remains valid for every model
in C, without fitting a unique hidden parameter or posterior. Any constant
action has positive-output average reward at most
  [1/2+k+r^K/2]/(1+k),                                    (8.3)
so a positive separation from this upper bound is a useful control statement.
It does not compare against a full-history feedback controller.

For normalized observed-switch reward
  r_t=[(1-Y_t Y_(t+1))/2-k a_t+k]/(1+k),
a constant action a has expectation
  [(1-c_a)/2+k(1-a)]/(1+k).                               (8.4)
The product cancellation makes this formula independent of the initial hidden
distribution. It applies to actions precommitted independently of the fresh
observation noises; selecting each action after reading its current sign is
not the same fixed-action statement. A c-interval can therefore certify the
best of the two constant-action policies, without certifying global feedback
optimality.

On the frozen acquired data, learning_value_certificate.py uses rational
radii enclosing Hoeffding radii: log240<11/2 is proved by a positive rational
Taylor partial sum for exp(11/2), and sqrt(11/N_j) is rounded outward with
integer square arithmetic. It recomputes the lambda outer intervals exactly,
then chooses29 real washout actions. Since atanh(r)<=r/(1-r^2), the rational
observed-filter prior TV bound r^29/(1-r^2) is9.34644e-5 or smaller.

For H=6,k=.04, the sign-feedback positive-output average value lies in
  [.6380372042,.7495531664],
and its lower bound exceeds the blind-constant upper bound by .1187840832.
For k=.12, constant action1's switch value lies in
  [.5486499536,.5782053109],
with advantage at least .1578551417 over constant action0.
All these statements hold simultaneously on the acquired95% coverage event,
under the supplied family promises. The executable uses exact Fraction
arithmetic for this certificate, not a proof assistant. These simple-policy
certificates are separate from the point-model H6 planner performance below.

9. Closed finite execution and known-method boundary
----------------------------------------------------
The execution is complete and frozen in LEARNING_RESULT.txt and
learning_control_results.json. It charges60000 fit transitions,12000 frozen
pre-task validation transitions,2784 ordinary washout transitions and576
goal-control transitions:75360 total,75361 observations, with no reset.
Both acquired models receive exactly the same ordered training prefix and
model-class promises. The likelihood model retains the entire history and
uses16-start constrained forward/backward EM. Both use learning_control.py's
same full-history filter and fully enumerated H6 belief planner. All models
filter every actual action/observation, including other controllers' segments.
Parameters remain frozen for validation and both fixed observable goals.

The two point models' conditional expected regret versus the evaluator's
known-model H6 optimum was numerically tiny on the encountered histories.
That uses evaluator truth, is not an acquired confidence interval, and is not
a claim of significance, novelty, broad superiority, or a general learning
mechanism. The general H6 optimality confidence over compatible parameter/
belief pairs was NOT computed. Current useful confidence is the independent
moment, initial-prior forgetting and simple-policy value certificate.

The operation is model-specific method-of-moments estimation plus ordinary
Bayesian filtering/control. Single-trajectory controlled spectral/PSR learning
already exists (Balle-Maillard2017), as does POMDP control without resets
(Even-Dar-Kakade-Mansour2005), with their own mixing/conditioning/homing and
computational promises. Neither no-reset acquisition nor task-independent
model reuse is claimed as a new mechanism here. The potentially useful
specialization is that this family's exact innovation invariant removes a
hidden-mixing factor from a finite-sample moment confidence and learns a
usable contraction action; the alias lower bound records its information
cost. General unknown model-class discovery remains unachieved.
