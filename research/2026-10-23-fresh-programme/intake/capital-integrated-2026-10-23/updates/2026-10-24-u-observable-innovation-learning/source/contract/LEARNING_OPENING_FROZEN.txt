Concrete candidate invariant
---------------------------
For a controlled binary symmetric hidden process, write
  X_(t+1)=X_t Z_t,  Y_t=X_t E_t,
where X_t,Y_t are signs, Z_t are independent transition innovations with
mean lambda_(a_t), and E_t are independent observation innovations with
unknown mean eta. Exploration actions are independent random coins, not
a curated state-dependent curriculum. Only actions and Y are observed.

Products of consecutive observations cancel the hidden state:
  Y_t Y_(t+1)=Z_t E_t E_(t+1),
  Y_t Y_(t+2)=Z_t Z_(t+1) E_t E_(t+2).
Use nonoverlapping length-three observation blocks. These product features
then have independent innovations ACROSS BLOCKS even if the hidden chain
mixes arbitrarily slowly and its initial state is unknown. This may remove
a mixing-time penalty from acquisition, rather than simply fitting a better
HMM to one fixture.

The action-conditioned moments should satisfy
  c_a=eta^2 lambda_a,   c_ab=eta^2 lambda_a lambda_b.
For nonzero reference c_b, lambda_a=c_ab/c_b; eta^2 follows from c_a/lambda_a.
The estimator must retain a denominator-aware confidence SET and explicitly
refuse parameter certainty when observations are nearly aliased or reference
correlations vanish. It must not obtain exact moments, matrices, hidden
states, resets, or calibrated eta from the environment.

Falsifiable information/computation contract
-------------------------------------------
Derive independent bounded-feature confidence from acquired blocks, prove
identifiability and its degeneracies, and charge every action/observation,
including unused bridge steps. Freeze the learned parameter/confidence
representation before seeing two held-out observable control objectives.
Reuse it with a belief-state planner. Compare against a strong stateful
HMM incumbent using the SAME raw stream and planner, plus an oracle only
as an explicitly evaluator-side ceiling.

If deployment needs a known starting distribution, no reset is allowed.
A self-generated washout action sequence is permissible only with an
explicit known mixing promise and its full real-time cost charged. Prefer
using the continuing observation history and quantified belief uncertainty.

The mathematical opportunity is an exact observable cancellation/acquisition
lemma over a whole controlled family, not a claim of a new general learning
algorithm or foundational cumulative intelligence. Symmetry and independent
innovation assumptions are genuine structural promises; the protocol must
also test or expose failure under asymmetric hidden dynamics/emissions.
Near-alias parameter uncertainty may be harmless for some observable tasks:
do not mistake a parameter-estimation failure for a control impossibility.

The second substantive route is a sharp lower bound showing which missing
excitation/observability/regeneration promise makes one-stream reuse
unidentifiable or too costly. It must improve on merely repeating the
already known missing-action or unbounded-secret-trigger counterexample.
