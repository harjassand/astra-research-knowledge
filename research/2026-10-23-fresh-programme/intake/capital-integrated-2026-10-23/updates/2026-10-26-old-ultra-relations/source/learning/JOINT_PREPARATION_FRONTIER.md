# The correct hard primitive for active joint experiments is claw generation

The one-way-permutation witness-conditioned bounds remain valid, but the
SAME identity/P family has the exact forward joint generator just proved.
Do not retain it as an active-average hardness example. This successor
states a genuinely joint obstruction under an EXPLICIT DIFFERENT
cryptographic premise, with the strongest simple simulator matched exactly.
No hard family is inferred among the actual six programs, and no new
source/fixture/benchmark or empirical cryptographic evidence is introduced.

## 1. A named assumption, including its native information

Assume a family of public pairs of permutations(P_0,P_1) of the SAME finite
domain Z, with efficient known forward code and an efficient EXACT uniform
sampler for Z. The public description includes that native sampler/code;
all its computation/bits are paid. The assumption is that no probabilistic
polynomial-time algorithm given this public description can find a CLAW

    (u_0,u_1) in Z^2 with P_0(u_0)=P_1(u_1)               (1)

with nonnegligible probability over the stated public-instance generation.
This is claw-free-permutation-pair security, not inferred from the mere
existence of an ordinary one-way permutation. The instance generator's
private coins/inversion trapdoor are not supplied. Polynomial prior
histories computable from public forward evaluations are included in the
adversary and do not constitute a hidden additional source of information.
If actual experience contains a claw/trapdoor or other nonsimulatable
capability, this assumption does not apply to that enlarged interface.

## 2. Sharp native-average JOINT preparation boundary

Define native X=(B,U) with independent fair B and uniform U in Z, and

    h(B,U)=P_B(U).                                         (2)

h is public compact code; its output is uniform and every native fibre
has EXACTLY TWO members, one in each branch. Let Pi denote its ideal native
full-fibre product pair law. It has probability1/2 on the event

    C={B!=B', h(X)=h(X'), X,X' valid native inputs}.         (3)

Any efficient active JOINT generator producing C outputs a claw: order
the two addresses by their branch bits and return their Z coordinates.
This reduction does NOT condition on a preexisting native witness, does
not need to invert a supplied challenge, and does not use a rare feature
label. Hence under the stated assumption every polynomial-time joint
generator Q (UNKNOWN retained as an extra output) satisfies

    Q(C)=negligible,       TV(Q,Pi)>=1/2-negligible.       (4)

The ordinary identity-pair generator samples one native X and returns
(X,X). It has both exact native marginals, preserves h, and has TV exactly
1/2 from Pi. Thus the bound is sharp for the actual active-pair interface,
even granting the same complete representation/forward code. The failed
identity/P obstruction was not needed for this theorem and is not silently
reclassified as a claw-free pair.

For f(B,U)=B, every efficient exact-native, h-preserving joint generator
has negligible disagreement, while b_h(f)=1/2. This excludes universal
empirical coverage from such generated relation experiments. It is NOT
an all-learner novelty-decision lower bound: a code-aware analyst given
the two-permutation structure and f=B can prove that distance directly.
The compiler/learning objective and the posterior/pair interface must
remain matched rather than using this obstruction against a different goal.

## 3. The constructive forward cover identifies a real tractable class

For commuting permutations, the product/omit-one construction in the
forward-coupling note provides claws and the exact full conditional joint
law through known forward calls. Such a pair is therefore NOT claw-free;
the two results are consistent, not competing assumptions. More generally,
verified forward words R_0,R_1,Q with P_j R_j=Q directly supply a uniform
claw cover. Approximate identities with certified average mismatch epsilon
give joint-TV at most2epsilon as already proved, and finding such a cover
on a genuinely claw-free family would itself break(1).

The positive mechanism is an actual forward procedure search/compiler,
with all code, permutation, native-law, identity/alignment checks and
budget failures paid. A compact representation alone supplies neither
an efficient common cover nor a full joint sampler; conversely a hard
given-witness inverse does not rule one out. These distinguish the actual
remaining preparation-language problem without a new favorable fixture.

The named cryptographic premise and conventional claw-generation/coupling
ancestry must be checked against primary sources before priority or broader
capability claims. Nothing here proves such families exist, transfers the
barrier to quantum algorithms, or asserts practical hardness at a finite
parameter. No observation contract is silently changed to make a candidate
look better than the strongest retaining/code-aware comparator.

## 4. Primary premise and direct ancestry

[Goldwasser--Micali--Rivest (1988), Section 6.2](https://people.csail.mit.edu/rivest/pubs/GMR88.pdf)
defines the public claw-finding premise used here, including an efficient
uniform domain sampler and forward maps while withholding the inverse
descriptions. Its next subsection explicitly contrasts commuting maps
with claw-free pairs. The reduction above specializes that established
premise to the active-pair observation contract; it is not a new
cryptographic assumption or a novel generic claw-finding obstruction.
