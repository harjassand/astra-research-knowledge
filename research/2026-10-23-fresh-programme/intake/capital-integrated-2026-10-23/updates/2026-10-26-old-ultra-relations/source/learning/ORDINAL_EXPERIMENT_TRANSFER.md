# An acquired nonlinear experiment law from ordinal experience

This closes a positive end-to-end instance of guarded relation retention.
Its purpose is acquiring reusable procedures while numerical old sources
are genuinely unidentifiable. It is a THEOREM, not a new source, fixture,
comparison run or claimed native-distribution performance experiment.
The direct comparison-gradient, polynomial-first-integral, functional-
dependency and conditional-estimation primitives remain conventional;
equally informed retaining methods can adopt the complete operation.

## 1. Acquisition without a cardinal calibration

Use the exact rational comparison interface of ORDINAL_GRADIENT_COMPILER:
the initial old source is S_1=phi_1(H), phi_1 arbitrary strictly increasing,
and H is an UNKNOWN canonical bounded-height integer polynomial of degree
e<=d. In the completeness class H=u_0+q(u_rest) after an unknown invertible
rational affine change. No hidden coordinates, source derivatives or
numerical S_1 values are supplied. The paid compiler uses at most

    T[(2n-1)+n ceil(log2(128G_max^2))]

actual comparisons, with the explicit native input precision, random-grid
critical/rank allocations, rational reconstruction, whole-kernel algebra
and source-episode computation already proved there. On its completeness
event it returns h=alpha H+beta. It ALWAYS checks the literal known-code
identity D_v h=1 for an actually computed rational v; failures/caps return
UNKNOWN. This known-code identity does not by itself certify S_1 semantics.

The initial source must remain live through compilation and its new guard.
All later source episodes used below must permit the actual paid guard
time. There is no callback to S_1 or an earlier source after its expiry.
Numerical S_i cannot be recovered from the entire comparison oracle under
arbitrary increasing recalibrations; no numerical old models are assumed
or silently reconstructed by this theorem.

## 2. Construct a finite law that the nonlinear action actually preserves

Do not pretend that rational translations preserve a preexisting finite
integer grid. Instead compile an explicit NEW finite experimental law.
Complete the nonzero rational v to an invertible rational matrix[v U],
and compute L with Lv=0, LU=I_(n-1). Check both identities and invertibility
by actual rational linear algebra. Choose finite public rational grids
Y of size K>=1 and Z={-b,...,b}^(n-1), b>=1, or any explicitly paid finite
product grid. Define the actual native-address program

    chi(y,z)=Uz+[y-h(Uz)]v.                               (1)

The exact slice identity gives

    h(chi(y,z))=y,       L chi(y,z)=z.                     (2)

Thus chi is injective on Y times Z, with explicit inverse(h,L) on its
image. Let X_chi be that finite image and nu_chi its UNIFORM law, obtained
by sampling y and z uniformly and executing chi. This sampler, its exact
rational coordinates/precision, and the changed evaluation law are actual
acquired procedures. Native address control on Q^n is supplied; neither
unknown-source evaluation on extensions nor an inverse oracle is granted.

For fixed y, fresh uniform z' makes chi(y,z') exactly uniform on that
nu_chi fibre. Hence

    X=chi(Y0,Z0),       X'=chi(Y0,Z1)                     (3)

is a full-fibre conditional pair with BOTH nu_chi marginals. It needs no
unknown source values in preparation and no rare-label rejection. All
nu_chi h-label masses are exactly1/K, by the compiled injectivity proof,
not by an assumed latent distribution. Every chi call costs a literal h
evaluation plus known rational matrix/vector work. All exact numerator/
denominator bit lengths are polynomial in the total h/v/U description
length, d,n,log K, log(b+1) and the ACTUAL maximum rational coordinate
description length L_Y of Y; standard rational-operation bit costs remain
paid, not unit-cost unbounded reals. Exact finite integer draws use fresh
fair bits and finite rejection caps, with all timeout probabilities charged.

This is a capability on the ACQUIRED law nu_chi. It is NOT a generalization
guarantee on the original proposal grid, an arbitrary physical input law,
or tasks whose evaluation distribution cannot be chosen. For an external
law mu, even a subsequent domination argument must pay an actual bound
dmu/dnu_chi and cannot cover points outside X_chi. The distribution change
is a real restriction, not an unnoticed favorable benchmark.

## 3. Guard only actual relations before each source expires

Freeze h,chi,Y,Z and the kernel(3). During each of r selected old source
episodes buy m fresh pairs and genuine equality comparisons S_i(X)=S_i(X').
These sources may be arbitrary functions on X_chi; no true polynomial or
common shear promise is required for SOUNDNESS. Apply the sequential
aggregate guard from GUARDED_RELATION_RETENTION with

    m>=12 ln(1/delta_old)/eta,
    sum_i,t 1{S_i(X_i,t)!=S_i(X'_i,t)}<=m eta/2.           (4)

The joint accepted/error probability is at most delta_old. On its good
event there exists an unobserved map Phi:Y->range(S_1,...,S_r) such that

    E={S!=Phi(h)},        nu_chi(E)<=eta.                  (5)

All deterministic compositions of the ACTUAL old tuple share that SAME E.
Phi need never be fitted or evaluated. Direct equality detection is paid:
a true three-way comparator supplies it, whereas arbitrary tie-breaking
binary preference feedback does not. No numerical old source values are
needed by(4), even when the cardinal calibration has unbounded complexity.

For COMPLETENESS, suppose the actual sources used in the cohort are
S_i=phi_i(H) and the initial h acquisition succeeds. Equations(1)--(2)
then make every old pair an exact equality; the guard accepts unless an
actual preparation/bit cap aborts. Its coverage pays the initial compiler
confidence and costs. An initially wrong h can pass only to the extent
the guarded relation is actually adequate, with(4)'s soundness allocation.
This decouples returned semantic soundness from the untested inner-class
promise. It does not make successful representation discovery universal.

## 4. Fit arbitrary related new functions after all old sources expire

Let a fixed current finite-alphabet f be tau-close under nu_chi to some
composition psi(S) of the actual old tuple. This is a conditional family
coverage statement, not a claim that arbitrary future tasks are related.
By(5) there is an UNKNOWN table g:Y->alphabet with

    nu_chi{f!=g(h)}<=theta=eta+tau.                       (6)

For each known y in Y buy l fresh current values at chi(y,Z), where l
is odd and

    l>=8 ln(K/delta_fit).                                (7)

Store the modal finite-alphabet answer as g_hat(y); strict majority if it
exists is sufficient, and any fixed tie rule is allowed otherwise. If the
conditional error e_y=Pr[f!=g(y)|h=y] exceeds1/4, that collection of labels
has total native mass at most4theta. On every remaining label clean
answers have probability>=3/4, so Hoeffding bounds majority failure by
exp(-l/8). Union over the K labels gives a simultaneous fitting event of
probability>=1-delta_fit. On this event

    nu_chi{f!=g_hat(h)}<=theta+4theta=5(eta+tau).           (8)

No clean-address oracle is used; raw exceptions can be concentrated on
whole fibres. If the right side exceeds1 it is simply vacuous. This proves
coverage with exactly K l current values and K l ACTUAL conditional address
preparations, followed by a closed table-plus-h evaluator. Fresh whole-law
guards with separate allocations supply selective accuracy for arbitrary
out-of-family targets; all attempted fits, rejected candidates and guards
remain paid. After this current source expires, the closed evaluator
continues to run without source callbacks.

K l can be dramatically smaller than |X_chi|=K(2b+1)^(n-1), but no advantage
over an equally informed feature-aware retainer is claimed. That retainer
uses exactly the acquired h,chi and relation evidence and ties. K, code
evaluation time and all acquisition/guard costs determine whether the
operation is useful; a formal small source-answer count alone does not.
The theorem avoids a numerical old-model requirement, rather than hiding
the cost of acquiring h or inventing a universal future-task curriculum.

## 5. New-information experiments and lifetime costs

The SAME frozen old certificate(5) protects ANY later compiled
nu_chi-stationary kernel preserving h, including fresh partial resampling
of z coordinates in the acquired chart. Such kernels need no full-fibre
mixing proof for one-sided diagnostics. For an arbitrary current target,
measured pair disagreement d with precision u gives

    inf_psi nu_chi{f!=psi(S)}>=max(0,(d-u)/2-eta),          (9)

when d denotes the empirical mean. Current pair confidence and actual
preparation caps are additional allocations. Zero signal is inconclusive;
there is no free completeness or spectral gap. Full resampling(3) is also
available here and has the explicitly compiled law, unlike the general
RP=NP-hard joint constraint problem.

Old exposure is the initial ordinal comparison compiler plus r m genuine
equality comparisons, with two chi addresses per equality. New exposure
per related task is K l values plus its whole-law guard; diagnostic calls
are separately paid and never counted as fitting labels unless actually
retained/used. Retained state includes h,v,U,L,grid descriptions, confidence
allocations and each actually learned K-entry current table, not numerical
old sources. Random bits, exact rational construction/evaluation, saturation
search, matrix/kernel solve, guard computation, memory and source-episode
latency are all charged. Summable fresh per-task/attempt allocations support
a conditional lifetime guarantee; reusing a replay seed does not.

This is an explicit example of learning an experimental representation from
ordinal experience, certifying it against actual old relations, and using
it after source expiry without reconstructing numerical old functions.
Its conditional bounded inner language, exact equality/input precision,
controllable experimental evaluation law and actual future-family coverage
remain the substantive supplied/learned boundary. No observations or new
benchmark are executed in this theorem.
