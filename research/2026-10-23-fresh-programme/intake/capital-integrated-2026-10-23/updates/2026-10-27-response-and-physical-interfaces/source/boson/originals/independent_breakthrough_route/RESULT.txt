What was established
--------------------
1. For U1>0,U2<0, the printed uncut positive determinant weight has infinite
   Gaussian integral for every finite Trotter step count and every finite
   chemical potential, including on the full 3x3 source lattice. Two uniform
   HS modes can move any nonzero eigenvalue to the determinant pole. The
   positive weight then has a nonintegrable two-dimensional singularity.
   PROOFS.txt Proposition 2 gives the complete argument. The root independently
   confirmed this argument at its exact scope. Historical novelty is UNKNOWN.
   Its completed review is preserved at
   ../parent/canonical_boson_operation_20261027/POLE_REVIEW.md; production code
   and numerical outputs are outside that mathematical review.

2. The unrestricted grand-canonical Hamiltonian with U2<0 is itself unstable.
   Fock states with n particles of each flavor on one site have energy
   4U2 n^2+O(n). A finite-occupation reference cannot establish a finite uncut
   GC ensemble. The separate stable U2=0, trace-convergent source regime has
   its own no-pole proof and is outside this failure claim.

3. Fixing only total boson number can introduce a sign problem. An exact
   one-site example has conjugate eigenvalues i,-i: its GC weight at fugacity
   1/2 is 4/5, while its total-N=2 canonical coefficient is -1. The separately
   fixed (1,1) contribution is +1. The obstruction exists for every selected
   total N>=1, not only this fixture.

4. Fixing equal, separately conserved populations gives the valid weight
   |h_n(B)|^2, where h_n is a complete homogeneous polynomial. It is finite,
   positive and integrable at every finite M,n,L, even with U2<0. The canonical
   recursion was available in ordinary AFQMC by 2020; this packet does not
   claim its invention. We composed it with the source model, integrated the
   uniform HS modes exactly, and implemented a derivative thermal-energy
   estimator with a finite second moment at finite L.

Ordinary comparator, resource costs and limits
---------------------------------------------
The exact 100-state Fock ED plus finite-L reference took 0.0152 seconds in a
single local observation. The L=80 million-field Gaussian calculation took
36.3 seconds. On this validation fixture, ordinary ED is substantially faster
and more precise. The canonical AFQMC construction uses established methods;
its value here is a valid ensemble and explicit sampling boundary. Dense
Fock-space dimension grows as binomial(n+M-1,n)^2, but this alone is not a
comparison against sparse ED, thermal typicality, tensor networks or optimized
canonical sampling.

The trace recursion uses O((L+n)M^3+n^2) arithmetic per independent field.
Gaussian acquisition, weight concentration, required bit precision,
nonnormal-matrix cancellation, long-product stabilization and any Markov
mixing time are additional costs. No polynomial bound on total work has been
shown. A defective/nonnormal Jordan case and derivative checks pass, without
guaranteeing arbitrary-condition-number stability. Numerical results use
NumPy complex128 and finite-bit PCG64 sampling, with no directed roundoff or
finite-bit sampling-bias enclosure. Standard errors are empirical Monte
Carlo estimates, not rigorous continuum-Gaussian confidence certificates.
