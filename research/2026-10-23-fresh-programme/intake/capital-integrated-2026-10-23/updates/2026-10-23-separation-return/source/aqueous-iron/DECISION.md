# Different physical route: impurity-conditioned aqueous iron reduction

Chosen consequence: remove the lithium-inventory regeneration burden entirely by reducing iron oxide in aqueous alkali. This is an established ordinary technology, not a claimed invention. The live inventive question is whether control of the solid/electrolyte interface can suppress hydrogen without arresting the final Fe(II)-to-metal step, and whether that works on impurity-bearing feed at useful metal throughput. No arbitrary rate simulation is warranted yet.

## New primary evidence and first discrimination
Arumugam et al., ACS Sustainable Chemistry & Engineering13(2025)2633–2640, DOI10.1021/acssuschemeng.4c08451: room-temperature goethite reduction in0.01MNaOH plus5000ppm sodium silicate gives endpoint Fe-atom fractions.340magnetite,.648hydroxide,.012metal; ordinary NaOH gives.445,.550,.005. Their76% Faradaic figure credits reduction into all three phases. Their6.4GJ/t projection assumes complete conversion without hydrogen, not observed full-cell production. The experiment is a three-electrode measurement, not measured wall-plug SEC. Figure3's endpoint times and potentials differ;2.4x endpoint metal fraction is not automatically a matched-time production gain. XRD misses amorphous material, discussed through XAS. A source-acquired manuscript is included with hash. This preserves the actual mechanism observation while rejecting its promotion to demonstrated efficient metal manufacture.

## Exact conditional charge calculation
Let fractions x_m,x_h,x_f represent all original Fe in magnetite, Fe(OH)2 and metal. Fe starts at oxidation state+3. Reduced electron equivalents per initial Fe:
d = x_m/3 + x_h + 3*x_f = 3 - (8*x_m/3 + 2*x_h).
Given mixture efficiency eta=d/q, metal-only efficiency is 3*x_f/q=eta*3*x_f/d.
With the source's declared endpoint and eta, the value is513/14950=3.4314381%, not76%. This is conditional accounting on those phase fractions and their charge definition, not an independent absolute metal assay.73.4% of the full reduction obligation is still absent: exactly(3-d)/3=.7342222. Intermediate reduction has value if later completed, so do not treat all nonmetal charge as irreversible loss.

An absolute metal assay and integrated current would replace this reconstruction. Unobserved amorphous Fe and changing sampled inventory invalidate naïve normalized-phase-to-absolute-yield conversion. Integrating a reference-electrode potential is not integrating full-cell input. The independent check in phase_charge.py obtains d by both stoichiometric reaction count and mean-valence change using exact fractions. Source rounding and phase uncertainty are not exact merely because arithmetic is.

## Strong ordinary counterexample to universal silicate benefit
Maihatchi et al., Journal of Electrochemical Science and Engineering10(2020)95–102, DOI10.5599/jese.751, actually compared hematite/red-mud alkaline electrolysis in the same cell. At110C,12.5MNaOH, red mud gave about20% metal current efficiency at1000A/m2; reducing current gave71% at45A/m2. Hematite exceeded80% over200–1000A/m2. Kaolinite additions impaired efficiency but did not reproduce the full red-mud penalty. Those are different mineral, concentration and temperature conditions from the dilute-goethite experiment, so the studies are not contradictory matched trials. The actual response to impurity state matters; removing every silicon species or adding generic silicate is not an evidence-qualified universal intervention. No efficiency transplant into the molten bath or whole-plant ranking.

## Current attack and stopping boundary
Seek an identified operating comparison for the final Fe(II)-to-Fe conversion with absolute metallic product, full current history, and a strong ordinary high-alkali suspension comparator. Separate grain contacts, surface inhibition, hydrogen and electrolyte conductivity rather than treating reported average valence as metal yield. A physically meaningful result may be a bounded inference about selective hydrogen suppression, not a plant claim. If existing data cannot identify the final step, do not commission hardware or integrate invented rates. Preserve this result and select the next experimentally anchored mechanism.
