NO-RESET WORK TEST AT AN OBSERVABLE ATOMIC RETURN

The null family is broader than source clocks. Suppose at every prescribed
field E(t), Q_E(t) has invariant law pi_E proportional to pi_ref exp(-E),
for one fixed positive pi_ref. Source clocks and every equilibrium kinetic
implementation of the calibrated energy are included. pi_ref, Q_E and the
number of hidden states need not be known to the observer.

For one closed prescribed period tau, let
 M_ij=E_j[exp(-W_cycle) 1{X_tau=i}]. Feynman-Kac gives the weighted forward
equation w'=(Q_E-diag(E'))w. Its solution from pi_E0 is
w_i(t)=pi_ref,i exp(-E_i(t))/Z_E0. Therefore M pi_E0=pi_E0.
M is nonnegative and irreducible; finite irreducible CTMCs with bounded
control give a positive M. Define row-stochastic

 R_ij=M_ij pi_E0,j/pi_E0,i.

Start in actually heralded A at phase0, then repeat the identical closed
control period. Let K be the FIRST k>=1 with X_(k tau)=A. For every first
return loop A=i0,i1,...,ik=A, the product of its M weights equals the
probability of the reversed first-return loop under R; pi factors telescope.
First-return loops reverse bijectively. Recurrence of the finite R chain
therefore proves

                  E_A exp(-W_(K tau)) = 1.

This needs neither a stationary initial law nor hidden generator learning.
One can hold the starting field until A is observed, start the phase-locked
command there, and pay that waiting time/fuel. It is not an energetic reset.
Exact atomic detection at EVERY boundary is assumed in this statement;
arbitrary coarse-block returns or arbitrary stopping rules are not covered.

At successive true atomic returns, the cumulative exp(-W) is a nonnegative
martingale in excursion index. Thus the rule 'reject the null when returned
to A with cumulative W<=-log(1/alpha)' has type-I probability <=alpha, even
with repeated looks at those returns. It is NOT a martingale claim at each
time within an excursion. A predeclared total-cycle deadline can end the
test without rejection; no finite runtime bound follows merely from finite
irreducibility. Under the extracting triangle, renewal-reward theory and
the certified negative periodic mean imply negative mean excursion work;
with exact ongoing observations, this test eventually rejects almost surely.
That is asymptotic power, not an economical finite detection-time claim.

For a hard per-excursion deadline, Z=exp(-W)1{K<=Kmax}, with timeout Z=0,
has expectation <=1. Predictable factors 1-lambda+lambda Z, lambda in[0,1],
give a supermartingale across actual re-heralded trials. Timeouts must not
silently reset the hidden state: hold/rephase until actual A and charge all
waiting and discarded physical resources. The e-value is a statistical
witness, not a ledger that may erase failed trials.

For a non-atomic herald block, endpoint pi ratios do not cancel state by
state. Unknown initial conditional hidden state invalidates the identity.
For example, in the actual source-clock triangle and this control, the
weighted first-return kernel on the block {1,2} has column sums about
0.986458806 and 1.008129570. These are numerical boundary checks, not an

For binary f and closed h with h(0)=h(T)=0, path work is just the observable
jump sum W=sum_j h(t_j) Delta f_j; hidden 1<->2 jumps are unnecessary.
General endpoints include the measured boundary term -[h f]. Noninvasive
readout, field/energy and timing calibration, false-herald error, sensor
phase bias and actuator losses are paid assumptions. A bound U>=W gives a
conservative e-value exp(-U); exponentiating an unbiased noisy estimate is
not valid. Exact noiseless or certified one-sided work intervals are needed
for the stated test, with sensor failure probabilities allocated separately.
