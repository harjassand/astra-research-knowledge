Conjectured theorem (proof appears short and exact)

If actual death probability is ZERO on an interval [0,T] for one g>0, then
P_a rho_g(t)=P_e rho_g(t)=0 throughout its interior. For a common parameter-
independent initial state, the entire joint accessible output is then the
same for every g: the signal generator annihilates the evolving state.
Thus exact zero-risk parameter information is impossible in this active
coherent class. This is stronger than ruling out static passive codes, but
it does NOT imply a uniform positive risk floor as T or controls vary.

Candidate proof

Zero actual death gives integral p_e=0. Continuity and positivity imply
P_e rho(t)=rho(t)P_e=0 for all t. Write Q=I-P_e-P_dead and

 K(t)=P_e[gV+H_c(t)]Q.

The e-to-Q block of rho_dot is zero, so K(t)rho(t)=0. Therefore the positive
expectation f0=Tr[K^dag K rho] vanishes identically.

Differentiate f0. Terms differentiating K vanish because K rho=0. The
Hamiltonian contribution has zero trace because K^dag K annihilates rho on
both sides. The loss contribution vanishes on the live zero-e state. For
the dephasing term the two anticommutator expectations vanish for the same
reason, leaving the NONNEGATIVE expression

 f0'=kappa Tr[K Z rho Z K^dag]
     =kappa ||K Z sqrt(rho)||_HS^2.

Since f0 is identically zero, K Z sqrt(rho)=0. Now f1=Tr[(KZ)^dag KZ rho]
also vanishes identically. Differentiate by exactly the same argument:

 f1'=kappa ||K Z^2 sqrt(rho)||_HS^2=0.

Thus K,KZ,KZ^2 annihilate sqrt(rho). On the physical charge sectors,

 P_a=(Z^2+Z)/2.

Hence K P_a sqrt(rho)=0. But P_e H_c P_a=0 gives

 K P_a=g |e><a| tensor I,

which is g times an isometry on the a sector. Therefore P_a rho=0. No
stationary/passive-code assumption was made; finite moving controls entered
through K(t), and their derivative terms disappeared on the exact null face.

Since both a and e sectors annihilate rho, [V,rho]=0. Uniqueness of the common
g-independent evolution from a g-independent initial state makes the entire
zero-risk trajectory/output independent of g. A unilateral zero-risk claim
at a single g already has this consequence in the stated coherent class.

Two critical gates

1. Quantitative continuity: small integral p_e does not automatically give
   a linear bound on integral p_a. The weak short-prefix counter shows this.
   Near-null expectations can have derivative terms and coherent boundary
   residues of different orders. This exact support argument is NOT proof
   of a duration-independent approximate risk/distinguishability inequality.

2. Added dissipative control: a general extra jump L may have off-diagonal
   L^dag L blocks between a and e, changing the effective e-Q constraint.
   For example a known collective monitored extraction proportional to
   |safe>(<a|+i<e|) can induce non-Hermitian directional/cancellation terms.
   Treating that as a free instantaneous Lindblad control may amount to
   supplying an engineered a-e counter-interaction or an infinite-bandwidth
   reservoir, beyond the bounded finite coherent-controller interface.
   Either derive its actual admitted Hamiltonian/latency, or keep it out.
   Finite external detection after a safely decoupled output is distinct.
