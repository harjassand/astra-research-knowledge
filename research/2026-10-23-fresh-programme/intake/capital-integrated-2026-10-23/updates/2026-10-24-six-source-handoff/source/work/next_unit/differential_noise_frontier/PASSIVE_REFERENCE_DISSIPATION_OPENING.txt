Why a raw occupation lemma is not the right central invariant

A tempting claim is integral p_e >=c integral p_a uniformly over bounded
controls. Arbitrarily short, weak control prefixes already challenge it.
With a known a-to-external-reference port, start in r and use a smooth pulse
H_c(t)=B^2 h f(t/h)(|a><r|+h.c.) on [0,h], where f and f' are bounded.
It honors both norm<=B and derivative<=B^2 for small h. Perturbation theory
gives a amplitude O(B^2 h^2), e amplitude O(g B^2 h^3), so integrated a
occupation is order h^5 while integrated e occupation is order h^7. Fixed
kappa,gamma affect higher orders. This is a PREFIX counter to the raw linear
occupation inequality; it is not a protected experiment, since information
remaining in e and actual finite safe extraction/cleanup cannot be ignored.
It suggests tracking exportable parameter coherence rather than all a energy.
