1. Literal wave model and the shape-uniform contraction

In three spatial dimensions, compare homogeneous background with

 (Delta + k^2[1+q_D(x)])u=0,

where q_D is real, static, measurable, compactly supported in D, |D|=V,
|q_D|<=Q, and 1+q_D is bounded below by a positive constant. Q>0 is fixed
independently of V. A prescribed nonzero q_* 1_D, with q_*>-1, is an
admissible binary alternative; no unknown invisible bonding variable is
needed. This is a scalar multiplicative refractive-contrast model. It is
not the elasticity, Maxwell, sound-hard, or traction-free crack operator.
The homogeneous background can be rescaled to have any fixed positive
index; k here is its wavenumber.

Use ordinary sphere measure, |S^2|=4pi, and outgoing Green function

 G_k(x)=exp(ik|x|)/(4pi|x|),  (Delta+k^2)G_k=-delta.

The exact Lippmann--Schwinger equation is

 u(x)=u_in(x)+k^2 int_D G_k(x-y)q_D(y)u(y)dy.             (W1)

For every measurable D and every x, rearranging the positive radial
kernel into a ball of the same volume gives

 int_D |G_k(x-y)|dy <= c0 V^(2/3),
 c0=(1/2)[3/(4pi)]^(2/3).                               (W2)

Indeed, for R=(3V/(4pi))^(1/3), the ball integral is R^2/2.
Thus the operator in (W1), restricted to L-infinity(D), has norm at most

 theta_k=k^2 Q c0 V^(2/3).

Whenever theta_k<1, the Neumann series converges and

 ||u||_infinity,D <= ||u_in||_infinity,D/(1-theta_k).       (W3)

This is an exact bound on the full multiple-scattering solution, not
discarding higher Born terms. The estimate is uniform over shape,
position and aspect ratio. In particular a fixed-extent thin region is
covered as its volume decreases, so long as its operator and contrast
remain the displayed ones.

2. All incoming and outgoing propagating angular modes

For an incident Herglotz wave

 u_in,g(x)=int_S^2 exp(ik d.x)g(d)dOmega(d),

Cauchy--Schwarz gives ||u_in,g||_infinity <= sqrt(4pi)||g||_2.
With u_sc(r xhat)=exp(ikr)(F_k g)(xhat)/r+o(1/r),

 (F_k g)(xhat)=(k^2/(4pi))int_D exp(-ik xhat.y)q_D(y)u_g(y)dy.

Taking the output L2 sphere norm and then (W3) yields

 ||F_k||_2->2 <= k^2 Q V/(1-theta_k).                    (W4)

The two sqrt(4pi) factors from input evaluation and output norm cancel
the 4pi in the far-field amplitude. In the matching incoming/outgoing
flux convention the complete angular scattering matrix is

 S_D(k)=I+(ik/(2pi))F_k.

Consequently

 ||S_D(k)-I|| <= k^3 Q V/[2pi(1-theta_k)].                (W5)

The same norm holds if a known free-propagation/antipodal convention is
removed by a fixed unitary. Real static contrast conserves flux, so this
complete scalar scattering matrix is unitary. No direction, quadrature,
forward phase, or outgoing angular component has been discarded.

For a fixed finite positive band I=[k_-,k_+], set

 theta_+=k_+^2 Q c0 V^(2/3),
 epsilon_V=k_+^3 Q V/[2pi(1-theta_+)].

If theta_+<=1/2, epsilon_V<=C V, C=k_+^3 Q/pi. The direct-integral
scattering operator on the flux-normalized one-particle space
H=int_I^direct L2(S^2) dnu(k) has ||S_D-I||<=epsilon_V.
Any fixed physical spectral normalization can be absorbed into this
one-particle norm. A time-independent positive scalar wave medium is
the mathematical model here; a Maxwell/causal dispersive material
realization is an additional task, not a corollary.

3. Conditional coherent-probe observation theorem

Now specify the quantum observation interface rather than identify
classical field amplitude with a freely exact measurement. Suppose the
ideal lossless scalar-wave apparatus realizes the number-preserving
bosonic map Gamma(S_D) on the complete asymptotic mode space H.
A finite-norm coherent pulse |alpha> has mean incident wave-quanta
n=||alpha||^2 and leaves as |S_D alpha>. The null output is |alpha>.
The coherent-state expansion gives exactly

 |<alpha|S_D alpha>| = exp[-||(S_D-I)alpha||^2/2]
                      >= exp[-epsilon_V^2 n/2].          (W6)

Here |S_D alpha> denotes a coherent state, not a one-particle state.
All output modes and an ideal reference can be granted to the observer.
The bound therefore includes ideal spatially resolved interferometry.
Granting the reference does not price an actual source/receiver system;
the resource below is incident probe quanta, not total apparatus energy.

For a nonadaptive batch of coherent pulses with total mean incident
number N, (W6) applies to the joint coherent state and every joint POVM.
There is also a precise classical-adaptive version: each next fresh
coherent pulse and its POVM may depend on the preceding classical data;
that pulse is measured before the next one; no surviving quantum memory,
coherent feedback, or recycled output pulse is admitted in this version.
Require sum_t n_t(h)<=N on EVERY controller history/seed. This is a
pathwise bound on conditional coherent MEANS, not a hard bound on the
Poisson-distributed photon number and not merely an expected-history
budget.

For any POVM, outcome Hellinger affinity is at least input-state overlap:

 int sqrt(p0(y)pD(y))dy >= |<psi0|psiD>|.

This follows by applying Cauchy--Schwarz to each positive POVM effect
and then the triangle inequality. At a matched history h, conditional
affinity a_t(h)>=exp[-epsilon_V^2 n_t(h)/2]. Normalize the geometric-mean
outcome kernel sqrt(p0 pD) by a_t. Under the resulting probability law R,
the full transcript affinity equals E_R product_t a_t(H_{t-1}). Hence

 A(P0,PD)>=exp[-epsilon_V^2 N/2],
 TV(P0,PD)<=sqrt(1-exp[-epsilon_V^2 N]).                  (W7)

Stopping is padded with zero-input deterministic outcomes; as the prefix
length increases its affinities decrease to full-record affinity, retaining
the bound. Coarsening to a final decision can only help the lower bound.
Controller randomness is exposed in the transcript and uses the same
conditional law under both hypotheses. Pure-state quantum relative
entropy is NOT used: it need not be finite here.

If each hypothesis must be classified with error <=delta<1/2, then
TV>=1-2delta. Thus this architecture requires

 N >= log[1/(4delta(1-delta))]/epsilon_V^2
   >= log[1/(4delta(1-delta))]/(C^2 V^2).                 (W8)

At delta=1/3 the numerator is log(9/8). This is a necessary resource
bound, not a sufficient reconstruction or classification algorithm for
every contrast/shape. It does not assert a matching worst-case upper
bound. It rules out a uniformly bounded pathwise coherent-mean budget
over a fixed-nonzero-contrast family with V tending to zero. It is not
an intact-law expected-budget theorem.

4. Different quantum/interaction architecture, different bound

Do NOT promote (W8) to arbitrary entangled or multipass illumination.
For an explicit hard query model, one charged interaction applies
I_vacuum direct-sum S_D to a register with at most one incident quantum,
with arbitrary entangled ancillas and hypothesis-independent controls.
Take at most T such interaction slots; unused slots can contain vacuum.
Every repeat passage through the unknown region is a new interaction.
Parallel m-quanta use counts m slots. On its bosonic sector,

 ||S_D^(tensor m)-I|| <= m epsilon_V,

by tensor telescoping, and restriction to the symmetric subspace cannot
increase norm. A one-slot unitary query changes any ancilla-assisted
input in trace distance by at most epsilon_V. Hybrid replacement of
the T queries by identity, with contractivity of all intervening known
operations, gives

 TV(final data0,final dataD)<=T epsilon_V,
 T >= (1-2delta)/epsilon_V >= (1-2delta)/(C V).           (W9)

T is a hard charged-query cap, including controller branches used in
the hybrid argument. No expected-photon-number substitution is made.
An access contract stated only in terms of a different mean energy,
unrestricted quantum-number tails, or an uncharged cavity is not this
query model. Ancillas, spatial data and arbitrary final measurements
are otherwise included.

The weaker inverse-volume scaling is not a proof defect. For a known
single sample mode with relative phase exp(i phi), phi=Theta(V), use
one quantum in a coherent reference/sample path superposition. After
m=nearest(pi/|phi|) paid passages the two candidate outputs have phase
separation pi+O(V) and constant-error discrimination with m=O(1/V).
This is a conditional phase-channel example showing why fresh-single-
pass V^-2 cannot be a universal interaction law. It assumes the known
mode/phase and lossless return routing; it is not an achieved device
or a decoder for arbitrary unknown defects. The routing, dwell, optical
loss and preparation costs remain, in addition to the counted passes.

The completeness/lossless premise in (W9) is essential. Closeness of
only an accessible lossy amplitude block does not imply the same
half-diamond bound: s=1-epsilon has |s-1|=epsilon, but a one-quantum
input loses the quantum with probability 2epsilon-epsilon^2. A lossy
problem needs a bound on the actual full-channel dilation or a direct
per-query channel-distance estimate. This unit assumes real q and
does not infer that estimate for an absorptive defect.

5. What prevents a material-inspection conclusion

Free-space contraction in (W2) is not a uniform resolvent estimate for
a defect in a known finite body, cavity, resonant waveguide, or near-field
source geometry. To extend the argument there, one must actually bound
the background's internal illumination, its Green operator on D, and
its source-to-outgoing transfer. These constants can be large or vary
with V. A bounded refractive contrast alone does not supply them.
Adaptive focusing using only the admitted propagating angular modes
is already covered by (W4); evanescent sources or geometry that creates
new enhancement are different supplied resources. Higher frequencies
are different resources too: C grows as k_+^3.

A hard crack, free traction boundary, perfect conductor, sound-hard
screen, or vanishing ellipticity is not a bounded multiplicative q_D.
Such limits can retain capacity/surface-driven scattering as thickness
decreases. No phase/scattering impossibility for those classes follows
from this proof. Conversely, (W8) shows that ideal phase data alone do
not remove the vanishing-volume coupling in the literal bounded-q
scalar class.

Band-limited finite-norm wavepackets have finite incident quanta; strict
band limitation does not promise a compact temporal support. Pulse
preparation, temporal tails, collection time, detector bandwidth,
finite aperture and actual material dispersion are not priced here.
Long dwell or repeated interaction cannot be counted as a fresh single
pass at unchanged resource. There is no wall-time, dose, hardware,
fatigue-life, net-work, or universal CT qualification consequence.

The meaningful result is a full-channel wave coupling bound plus an
architecture-sensitive acquisition cost. The actual missing inputs
for a specific solid remain its correct vector/boundary operator,
background conditioning, admissible source and complete readout model.
