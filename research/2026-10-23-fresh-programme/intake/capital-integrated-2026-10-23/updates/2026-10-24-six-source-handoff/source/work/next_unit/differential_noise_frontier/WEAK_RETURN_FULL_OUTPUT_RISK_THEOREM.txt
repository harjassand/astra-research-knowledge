WEAK RETURN, FULL OUTPUT, AND ACTUAL DESTRUCTION
24 October 2026. Frozen after root's complete independent read and the bounded
generator, effective-channel, and full-timestamp challenges. Checks pass at
the literal weak-stage scope below; the original strong-controller gate stays open.

This is a quantitative theorem for a specified weak-drive subclass of the
original independent-ancilla interface. It is not a reduction of strong
controllers to that subclass and does not close the all-controller question.
The passive-reference column and exact-active-zero-risk sources are frozen
separately. No fixture or physical demonstration is asserted here.

1. Physical interface and the observations retained

Use live states r,b,a,e, where r is an independent-ancilla b reference. Thus
Z r=-r, Z b=-b, Z a=a, Z e=0. One inaccessible Markov bath gives kappa D[Z],
kappa>0. The unknown g lies in a fixed compact interval I=[ell,u], ell>0,
and couples a to e. The only driven port during a weak stage is b to e:

 H_g,eta(t)=g(|e><a|+|a><e|)+Delta P_e
       +eta[f(eta^2 t)e^(-i omega t)|e><b|+h.c.].                  (1)

The loss jump is sqrt(gamma) J, J=|dead><e|, gamma>0. It is terminal:
all later sample access stops after the first such event, and there is no
new specimen. Independent finite reference/ancilla systems may be retained.
The dephasing bath and the coherent loss environment are inaccessible.

For a stronger observable interface than a final death flag alone, retain
the exact classical first-death timestamp, and any independent ancilla state
remaining in that branch. No coherent cross-time loss-environment record is
granted. On no death, retain the entire live output, not just a Ramsey mean.
Initial states of each weak stage are supported on span{r,b}, with arbitrary
independent reference entanglement. States in dead are a separate classical
terminal sector, never coherently recombined with live states.

Each stage has fixed carrier omega and fixed finite Delta, slow duration S,
and a prescribed complex C^1 envelope f on [0,S], with f(0)=f(S)=0. One may
also take f' zero at endpoints for a globally C^1 waveform after switching
the drive off. The physical duration is S/eta^2. Controls between stages
act on safe b/reference degrees of freedom, have their actual finite strength,
latency, readout and reset costs, and may depend on earlier accessible records.
There is no observation/feedback inside a weak stage except the terminal event.

For each fixed carrier/envelope the original strength/slew bounds hold at
sufficiently small eta, provided the fixed Delta fits the hardware budget:
drive norm <=eta ||f||_infty and derivative <=eta |omega| ||f||_infty
+eta^3 ||f'||_infty. No joint omega(eta)->infinity approximation is claimed.
For a family of adaptive stages, the constants below must be uniform over
the allowed envelopes/carriers, and their accumulated errors must be paid.

2. An elementary finite-dimensional instrument approximation

Rotate both a and e by the carrier. On the LIVE block, remove only the
terminal jump feeding dead, not the unobserved dephasing recycling. The exact
trace-decreasing live equation in slow time s=eta^2 t is

 d rho/ds=eta^(-2)(L0+eta L1(s))rho,                              (2)
 L0 rho=-i[H0,rho]+kappa D[Z](rho)-(gamma/2){P_e,rho},
 H0=g(|e><a|+|a><e|)-omega P_a+(Delta-omega)P_e,
 L1(s)rho=-i[f(s)|e><b|+h.c.,rho].

Let P be compression onto the safe r/b matrix block, Q=1-P as a
superoperator. Then PL0=L0P=0, PL1P=0, and QL0Q is invertible on Q.

Here is a direct stability check, rather than an assumed fast gap. The
fast-to-safe coherences evolve under a two-by-two matrix whose Hermitian
part is -diag(2kappa,(gamma+kappa)/2), hence decay. Within the a/e block,
the completely positive trace-decreasing evolution has trace derivative
-gamma rho_ee. If a nonzero positive state had nonvanishing asymptotic
survival, Cesaro averages would have a nonzero stationary positive limit.
Stationarity forces rho_ee=0; positivity removes its e row/column, and the
e-a equation with g>0 then forces rho_aa=0, a contradiction. Finite dimension
therefore gives strict decay on that block. These blocks span Q. The inverse
and the decay constants are finite uniformly over the fixed compact g interval.
The g=0 or gamma=0 limits are excluded from this uniform assertion.

Write R=(QL0Q)^(-1)Q, extended by zero on P. For an initial safe density
matrix, define

 q1(s)=-R L1(s)p(s),
 q2(s)=-R Q L1(s)q1(s),
 p'(s)=P L1(s)q1(s),                  p(0)=rho_initial.          (3)

All statements can be tensored with an arbitrary finite independent
reference. They concern the full live density matrix. In particular the
dephasing recycling inside the fast block is retained in R.

Put a0=2kappa, lambda=(gamma+kappa)/2, z=a0-i omega, and

 chi_g=[lambda+i(Delta-omega)+g^2/z]^(-1),
 r_g(s)=2|f(s)|^2 Re chi_g,
 h_g(s)= |f(s)|^2 Im chi_g.                                      (4)

Evaluating (3) on the r/b matrix units gives

 p_bb'=-r_g p_bb,             p_rr'=0,
 p_br'=-|f|^2 chi_g p_br,     p_rb'=conjugate equation.           (5)

Indeed, the first-order e-b (or e-r) coherence is -i f chi_g times
the corresponding b input entry. The b population flux obtained from
-i[H_drive,q1] is -2|f|^2 Re chi_g p_bb. Thus p is a positive, trace-
decreasing amplitude-damping/phase no-death evolution. No additional
returned-b dephasing occurs at this order; population AND coherence were
evaluated from the same complete generator.

The second-order lift carries the crucial event-rate identity

 gamma J q2(s) J^dag = r_g(s) J_b p(s) J_b^dag,
 J_b=|dead><b|,                J q1(s)J^dag=Jp(s)J^dag=0.        (6)

For a scalar input this follows from trace balance: L0 q2=p'-L1 q1,
Tr L0 q2=-gamma (q2)_ee, Tr L1 q1=0, and Tr p'=-r_g p_bb.
The same argument after partial trace over the internal system proves (6)
as an operator identity on every independent reference/ancilla. Off-diagonal
b/r inputs have zero e population. This is the full jump-output map, not
only equality of its scalar total mass.

Now lift the live approximation to

 rho_app(s)=p(s)+eta q1(s)+eta^2 q2(s).                           (7)

Direct substitution, with no asymptotic equality hidden, gives its residual

 E_eta(s)=rho_app'-eta^(-2)(L0+eta L1)rho_app
        =eta[q1'-L1q2]+eta^2 q2'.                               (8)

Attach to rho_app the effective continuous death-record density
r_g(s) J_b p(s)J_b^dag ds. By (6), this is EXACTLY the jump density
(gamma/eta^2)J rho_app J^dag ds of the lifted live matrix. Consequently the
augmented approximation has residual only in its live block; its record
equation has no residual. Initial and final dressings vanish because f=0
there. The lift need not itself be a positive density matrix: all residuals
are Hermitian, which is sufficient for the following estimate.

Propagating an arbitrary Hermitian live source X inserted at time s through
the actual dynamics to time S, including all later first-death timestamps
and the final no-death quantum state, is a completely positive trace-
preserving instrument map. It contracts the Hermitian trace norm, also with
an independent reference. Duhamel's formula on this augmented instrument
therefore gives the full joint-output bound

 ||Omega_phys,g,eta - Omega_eff,g||_1
       <= integral_0^S ||E_eta(s)||_1 ds.                        (9)

This controls the continuous timestamp in L1 (and every time binning), the
surviving quantum state, total death probability, and their correlations
with retained independent ancillas. It is stronger than final-state/CDF
convergence. No local-QFI convergence is inferred from it.

For an explicit finite constant, use induced stabilized trace norms and let
 M_R=sup_g ||R||, F0=sup_s ||L1(s)||, F1=sup_s ||L1'(s)||.
All are finite in the stated compact/fixed interface. Since ||p||_1<=1,

 ||q1'|| <= M_R F1+M_R^2 F0^3,
 ||L1q2|| <= M_R^2 F0^3,
 ||q2'|| <= 2M_R^2 F0F1+M_R^3 F0^4.

Thus the right side of (9) is at most

 e_eta=S{eta(M_R F1+2M_R^2F0^3)
          +eta^2(2M_R^2F0F1+M_R^3F0^4)}.                       (10)

If a norm convention defines R only on Q, use its extended superoperator
norm as above; do not omit the Q projection when bounding it. This is a
finite error bound over the paid time S/eta^2, not a statement merely about
low eigenvalues. Endpoint drive values other than zero require the explicit
initial/final dressing norm terms in (9).

3. Residue, finite safe readout, and multiple stages

Because the ideal endpoint is safe, measuring the actual fast-sector
population at stage end has probability <=e_eta/2. Leaving all of that
residue to become eventual death costs at most this probability. A finite
safe b/reference extraction/readout with the drive off has no a-e path
from a safe ideal input. Apply its actual channel/instrument to both the
physical and ideal outputs; contractivity keeps the error in (9). If a
capped detector misses an otherwise safe excitation with probability zeta,
keep that no-click/failure outcome, or pay zeta as an additional instrument
error. Do not assume an exact instantaneous projection or unknown-g reset.

Known safe controls and fresh pre-destruction preparations are allowed
between stages; there is one actual sample, never replaced after death.
For a finite adaptive sequence, uniform per-stage half-trace errors delta_j
(including the chosen finite cleanup/readout errors) yield a total output
distance <=Delta_tot=sum_j delta_j by the usual channel/instrument hybrid.
All intermediate records and adaptive decisions are retained. A family with
a growing number of weak stages needs Delta_tot->0; fixed-stage convergence
alone is insufficient. Unbounded stopping requires convergent finite-cap
outputs/completion, or nontermination remains failure.

4. Exact effective full-output information/actual-risk inequality

The susceptibility has the exact optical-theorem algebra

 Re chi=[lambda+g^2 a0/|z|^2]|chi|^2,
 chi'=-2g chi^2/z,
 |chi'|/Re chi=2g|z|/(lambda|z|^2+g^2 a0)
              <=1/sqrt[C],       C=kappa(gamma+kappa).           (11)

This holds for every fixed real carrier/detuning. An envelope merely
reparametrizes the known effective exposure v=integral |f(s)|^2 ds.
Consider one effective exposure tau with constants r=2Re chi>0 and
h=Im chi, signal projector P_b, q=1-exp(-r tau). The survival Kraus operator
is K0=I+[exp(-(r/2+ih)tau)-1]P_b. The continuous-death-time instrument has
Kraus density

 K_t=sqrt(r) exp(-(r/2+ih)t) exp(i theta(g,t)) |dead,t><b|,
                    0<=t<=tau.                                 (12)

The timestamp is CLASSICAL; theta is only an inaccessible Kraus-environment
phase choice, leaving every branch CP map unchanged. Set

 c=exp(-r tau)h' tau/q,         partial_g theta(g,t)=h't+c.

Then K0^dag K0'+integral K_t^dag K_t' dt=0. The derivative cost is exactly

 beta=K0'^dag K0'+integral K_t'^dag K_t' dt
     =[q r'^2/(4r^2)+exp(-r tau)tau^2 h'^2/q]P_b.

Since exp(-r tau)tau^2/q^2= tau^2/[4sinh^2(r tau/2)]<=1/r^2,

 4 beta <= q[(r'^2+4h'^2)/r^2]P_b <= q P_b/C.                    (13)

An exposure with f identically zero is identity and is omitted, so there
is no r=0 gauge singularity. Orthogonal derivative insertions in this
gauge, including arbitrary parameter-independent controls, coherent
references, measurements and adaptive choices BETWEEN effective stages,
give the exact accessible-output bound

 F_Q(Omega_eff,g) <= (1/C) sum_j q_j Pr_g(active b before j)
                 = rho_eff,g/C.                               (14)

The last equality is the single-terminal-death compensator: stepwise
death increments are disjoint. Neither survivor conditioning nor a hidden
reset is used. Equation (14) retains the continuous timestamp and all
accessible records; it is not a coherence-only bound. For finite caps all
identities are literal. For unbounded stopping use convergent finite-cap
outputs for the finite-parameter consequence, not an unsupported QFI limit.

5. Operational physical consequence, without local-QFI transfer

Let the same physical policy at all g in a connected interval [g0,g1]
have actual total eventual-death probability <=rho_bar and total half-trace
approximation/cleanup error <=Delta_tot, uniformly throughout that interval.
The matched effective policy has rho_eff,g<=rho_bar+Delta_tot. Bures length
and (14) imply its endpoint trace distance is at most

 sin(min{pi/2, |g1-g0| sqrt[(rho_bar+Delta_tot)/C]/2}).             (15)

The two physical endpoint laws have trace distance at most this expression
plus 2Delta_tot. If both binary errors are <=alpha<1/2, then, whenever
1-2alpha-2Delta_tot>0,

 rho_bar+Delta_tot >= (4C/|g1-g0|^2)
                   arcsin^2(1-2alpha-2Delta_tot).               (16)

Uniform estimation within tolerance epsilon at two points separated by more
than 2epsilon implies that binary-test requirement. Thus arbitrarily weak,
long, well-returned virtual spectroscopy in this restricted family cannot
acquire a fixed nontrivial continuous-coupling distinction while total
actual destruction and its accumulated approximation error both vanish.
Taking eta smaller only pays longer duration to approach this obstructed
effective channel. This conclusion includes the death timing statistic,
not only survivors or a protected Ramsey phase.

The risk promise is uniform ALONG the connecting parameter interval.
Endpoint risks alone do not bound the Bures path. For nonuniform risks the
integral (2sqrt C)^(-1) integral sqrt(rho_eff,g) dg is the proper expression.
No local physical QFI bound is asserted from (9): such a transfer would
require a derivative-controlled approximation, especially at rank changes.

6. Resources, incumbents, and remaining boundary

One weak stage pays S/eta^2 elapsed time, one actual preparation, its entire
known waveform and control precision, finite endpoint extraction/readout,
all resets, ancillas, timestamp records and any unsuccessful return.
Each additional stage pays those resources again until actual death. The
approximation theorem requires actual error constants, not only an abstract
small amplitude. It cannot be used with many stages while omitting sum delta_j.
There is no claim that this procedure is an optimal experiment or that the
constant in (16) is sharp for the physical interface.

The algebra uses standard dissipative linear response, full-Liouvillian
adiabatic elimination, channel-extension gauges and Bures testing. Closest
primary method: Kessler, PRA86,012126 (2012), arXiv:1205.5440, gives the
second-order full-Liouvillian resolvent. Reiter--Sorensen, PRA85,032111
(2012), arXiv:1112.2806, supplies effective Hamiltonian/jump methods under
excited-to-ground decay assumptions; its simple non-Hermitian inverse
cannot discard the fast-sector recycling of this Z dephaser. The explicit
lift-plus-record residual calculation above supplies this model's paid-
time/full-timestamp estimate directly; no priority or novelty is inferred.

Strong original controllers manipulating a/e during sensing, moving both
logical branches, running finite-cadence active correction, or implementing
other actual Hamiltonian/measurement interfaces are NOT reduced to this
weak class. The original duration-independent approximate all-controller
frontier remains OPEN. Exact-active-zero-risk support and passive-reference
coherence bounds do not fill that gap. Nor does this result import a
correlated noise-bearing ancilla or a free engineered collective bath.
