Result
======
A selected quantum task can have a bounded predictive description even when
its physical environment retains quantum memory indefinitely. The useful
object is an acquired operator describing how that memory can affect the task.
If each logical-error effect is scalar on the accessible memory port, the
logical error process renews exactly. If only an upper operator bound is
acquired, a uniform, composable fault envelope survives without reconstructing
the environment or assuming that it forgets.

This continuation constructs and executes that alternative to state filtering.
It first learns compact terminal measurement policies, follows their weak
observability obstruction into a quantum boundary construction, defeats an
output-only calibration assumption, and acquires the required effects through
actual finite mixed-state preparations. A stronger conventional phase-feedback
alternative then changes the operation. Its coherent phase port is constructed
from the retained finite-weak one-CNOT primitive and recalibrated through new
physical-model records; an ideal S gate is not silently supplied.

The acquired controller for the general six-control boundary menu has a current
bad-operation allowance

with probability at least .99 over its calibration records, uniformly over
all incoming states of the accessible quantum mode and arbitrary external
quantum correlations. It reuses the same physical mode without resetting or
discarding it during deployment. For a fresh Bell-pair input initially
independent of the apparatus, with an isolated reference, output Bell fidelity
is at least 27/40. This witnesses preserved
entanglement. There is no postselection in this new service.

That result requires a fixed finite interaction boundary during each call.
Its isolation, upstream preparation access, trusted weak instruments and

uncut arbitrary many-body experiment. The records are computer-generated from

  F_(s,b)=p_(s,b) I_A.

substitute for the continuing physical quantum mode in the main construction.

12. Physical observability still limits short representations
=============================================================
A known r-bit parity with one unknown model sign has a bond-two representation
and O(r)evaluation. Let m(w) be the posterior sign from a FULL local weak word,
with kappa_L=E[m(w)^2] and g_L=E|m(w)|. Even granting exact logical-error labels,
learning the model sign with testing error<=1/4 needs
  N >=1/[8 a^2 kappa_L^r]
for fixed finiteL, where a is the response amplitude. The optimal current
correction risk with the model supplied is [1-a g_L^r]/2. Acquisition of a
static model and information about the current physical sector are different.

The strong local comparator repeats probes. L=O(log(r/epsilon)/eta^2) keeps
parity error small with O(rlog(r/epsilon))actual probes. Symmetric sequential
boundaries improve expected cost; capped finite readout includes an explicit
abort whose aggregate effect is scalar. Thus the fixed-L lower bound is not
an unconditional exponential separation from conventional filtering.

A rank-k binary feature map on m terminal sectors can be compiled onto k
existing consumed qubits with at mostk(m-k)+k(k-1)+3(k-1)CNOT calls, without a
fresh clean ancilla. Its exact retained instrument sums the positive sector
effects over the chosen feature. With independent native depolarizing q,
compressing awaygparity readoutputs creates errorfloor
  [1-(1-16q/15)^g]/2.
Fixedaccuracy therefore permits onlyg=O(epsilon/q)at fixedq. A compact logical
feature does not supply a reliable collective meter. The compiled port is a
new actual operation whose faults must be acquired or bounded.

Incompatible readout gives a separate exact failure. Ignored weak Z probes
before an X-based quantum eraser attenuate its recoverable coherence by
(sqrt(1-eta^2))^L. At eta=.6,L35 this is about.000405648. Summing the Z record
does not recreate the omitted physical measurement. Existing full records
cannot invent a missing incompatible control port.

13. Constructing the boundary while preserving a useful interaction
====================================================================
The closed-call premise was not left as an unexplored interface. The next
construction uses a physical exchange interaction between input D and receiver
A, with unread quantum M continuously coupled during the call:

 H=J(XX+YY+ZZ)_DA + H_M + V,
 V=sum_(j in {D,A},alpha) sigma_(j,alpha) tensor B_(j,alpha).

J is fixed but may be unknown. M is a physical quantum system, not a label for
J. Actual synchronized Pauli controls I,XX,YY,ZZ preserve the desired exchange
and average every displayed single-site unwanted coupling to zero. Four equal
dwells with actual pair pulses XX,ZZ,XX,ZZ complete a cycle. Each pair pulse
uses two existing local Pauli operations. This is established interaction-
preserving dynamical decoupling, now charged as a construction of the boundary.

Suppose ||V||<=v and the reference interaction-picture coupling is Lipschitz
with constant kappa. A local sufficient bound is
 kappa <= sum_(j,alpha)||[H_M,B_(j,alpha)]|| +4|J|v_sum,
 v_sum=sum_(j,alpha)||B_(j,alpha)||.
It need not contain the extensive bath norm or dimension. It does require
actual bounded local strengths and bandwidth; low physical information does
not establish these bounds. Midpoint cancellation plus the exact unitary
Duhamel remainder gives, for N cycles of total duration T,

 epsilon_iso <= (kappa/4+v^2/2) T^2/N +4N delta_pair.    (7)

This is a FULL joint-channel half-diamond allowance, uniform over every
receiver/bath/reference state. delta_pair is a uniform pair-pulse error bound.
Finite-width synchronized rotation profiles preserve isotropic exchange
throughout each pulse; their unwanted-coupling allowance can be charged as
v*w per pulse, plus control error, with pulse widths included in the clock.
Residual measurement, routing or operation gaps need separate norm-times-time
allowances. The source includes these conditions; a pulse endpoint alone does
not certify its finite-width implementation. More pulses eventually cost
4N delta_pair, so sending N to infinity is not a free refinement.

Unlike independently decoupling D from everything, these pulses retain the
exchange required for D-to-A transfer. With S=SWAP and theta=2JT,
 U_ex=exp(iJT)[cos(theta) I-i sin(theta) S].
At full-SWAP timing the unknown input, including its reference entanglement,
moves to A without a clean receiver. For partial exchange, actual matched
Paulis on INPUT D and OUTPUT A give exact scalar reference effects
 F_I=[sin^2(theta)+cos^2(theta)/4] I,
 F_X=F_Y=F_Z=[cos^2(theta)/4] I.
Thus the reference transfer visibility is sin^2(2JT), independent of A/M.
This mathematical output relabeling adds no physical SWAP. Old A's state moves
to old D at full exchange. Protected repeated outputs require actual routing
or extraction; the finite record below implements a CURRENT transfer, not a
free protected output stream.

When J is already known, a conventional controller chooses SWAP timing and
uses the design allowance directly; it needs no tomography. When J is unknown,
actual paired weak input-D/output-A audits acquire the useful duration without
learning the quantum bath. The exact class's scalar effects also remove the
need to prepare A for this acquisition. For K tested durations, n calls each,
 gamma=(1-2t_L)^2,
 qhat_T=(3/4)[1-mean(z_T)/gamma],
 r_T=(3/(4 gamma))*sqrt[2 log(2K/delta)/n].
The actual Pauli-event probability differs from its closed reference by at
most epsilon_cal(T). The bounded-score martingale therefore gives, on one
simultaneous confidence event,
 q_current(T)<=qhat_T+r_T+epsilon_cal(T)+epsilon_deploy(T). (8)
Select T by minimizing this acquired upper bound. The calibration and future
operation pay closure separately. Neither the unknown J nor hidden error
labels are needed. The physical exchange class and the uniform design bounds
ARE needed; finite successful scores do not prove them.

14. Scientific interpretation and remaining obstruction
========================================================
The new representation has two parts: a finite set of acquired task effects
and a physical controller chosen to reduce their dependence on the hidden
quantum state. Exact scalar effects remove that dependence. A uniform upper
bad effect gives a weaker but composable operational guarantee. Both differ
from maintaining a compressed posterior after an arbitrary rich record.

The strongest gain is future transfer under an explicitly obtained input
interface. Earlier calibration mixtures described only visited histories.
Here actual input excitation spans the bounded port's operator space, so the
certificate covers states that were not visited. This does not learn an
unbounded environment and does not certify that the bounded port exists.
Its physical closure must be constructed or independently guaranteed.

The live frontier is therefore concrete: important devices with a small,
coherently isolated and controllable interaction boundary, and control menus
whose acquired effects admit a useful uniform envelope. For a boundary that
keeps expanding or admits a hidden bypass, the small description can fail.
For an interface with high observable degree, weak acquisition can remain
expensive. For a richer future task, additional effects or operations must be
acquired. No generic efficient nonlinear simulator or unrestricted quantum
controller follows from the present result.

15. Strong prior and equally informed methods
=============================================
The following primary sources were read. Their ideas are not claimed as new:
* Gregoratti and Werner, Quantum Lost and Found, quant-ph/0209025; classical
  environmental measurement/feedback correction and its access assumptions.
* Gregoratti and Werner, On Quantum Error-Correction by Classical Feedback in
  Discrete Time, quant-ph/0403092; optimal feedback under stated assumptions.
* Trendelkamp-Schroer, Helm and Strunz, arXiv1110.4806; mixed-environment phase
  damping and the possibility that an inappropriate correction is worse.
* Winick etal, arXiv2212.07500, Theorem3 and its definition: randomized compiling
  already tailors non-Markovian noise into Pauli form with environment-dependent
  coefficients. That fact alone does not give scalar effects, an acquired
  worst-inputq, or the specified-fault-setq^|S|bound. Their key-leakage example
  reinforces the actual private-frame condition.
* Lundeen etal, Measuring measurement, arXiv0807.2444; input-probe effect
  tomography and convex reconstruction are established methods.
* Zhou, Leung and Chuang, quant-ph/0002039; one-bit teleportation is established.
* Patel, Markov and Hayes, quant-ph/0302002; efficient binary reversible
  compilation is prior, not an Astra-exclusive representation.
* Bryant, Graph-Based Algorithms for Boolean Function Manipulation (1986),
  https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~bryant/pubdir/ieeetc86.pdf; compact decision graphs
  have useful structure-dependent size and exponential worst cases.
* Huang, Kueng and Preskill, arXiv2002.08953; compact task sketches retain
  measurement-dependent acquisition norms, including exponential observable
  weight dependence for local shadow measurements.
* Valancius, Lennon and Oliva, ICML2024,
  https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/valancius24a.html; nongreedy active feature
  acquisition is established. The present quantum replay boundary and actual
  weak logical scores specify where such ordinary methods can be applied.
* Pancotti, Saravanan and Svore, arXiv2609.00169; tensor noise learning from
  syndrome and logical-observable records is a strong contemporary alternative.
  Richer conventional learned models can improve state/distribution-specific
  control; our uniform task effect does not replace their larger scope.
* Khodjasteh and Lidar, arXiv0803.4320; global decoupling preserving Heisenberg
  computation, rigorous error bounds and finite-pulse costs are established.
* Frydrych, Hoskovec, Alber and Jex, arXiv1409.3424; selective dynamical
  decoupling for state transfer is a strong conventional physical comparator.
* Burgelman etal, arXiv2407.04766, PRX Quantum6,010323(2025); quantum baths can
  make gate error depend on control history even after system resets. Our
  uniform bound explicitly ranges over conditional bath states and does not
  turn a finite-rate DD experiment into an exact constant-error process.

its stated menu, not every physically conceivable adaptive quantum policy.
Source reading, internal checks and executed examples do not certify global
