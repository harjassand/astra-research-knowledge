ASTRA — ACQUIRED CENTRAL EFFECTS AND RESET-FREE TASK RENEWAL
25 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane

Scientific claim and its boundary
================================
A physical memory need not forget for a selected logical task to renew. If the
positive error-branch effects of an actually randomized and measured operation
are scalar on the memory port, the logical error law is independent of that
port's state. Repeating the operation then gives a product Pauli channel on
isolated logical outputs even when the physical probe is recycled and an
arbitrarily large quantum memory changes it between uses.

This source constructs such effects for a controlled single-qubit interaction,
shows a decisive failure of output-only calibration, and gives an operation
that acquires the effects using actual mixed input-probe preparations and weak
logical audits. Approximate scalar effects and merely bounded bad effects have
separate, weaker guarantees. The observer acquires a task operator, not the
state of the environment and not an arbitrary coherent control port.

The consequential task is correction of an unavoidable interaction incurred
while an unknown qubit is transmitted or processed. The data may be entangled
with a reference. If the coupling is freely avoidable while perfect storage
is available, omitting it dominates the benchmark. The construction does not
count deliberately introduced removable noise as a new operation.

1. Physical interface, input and output
======================================
The logical qubit D interacts with a readable probe A. M denotes every other
persistent physical degree of freedom. During one task macro the raw map acts
on D+A, with M a spectator; M may be arbitrarily large, correlated with D,A
and the reference, and may interact arbitrarily with A BETWEEN macros. Past
logical outputs and an external reference are isolated from subsequent device
operations. This bounded interaction boundary is a structural physical
premise. Finite tests on A cannot exclude a hidden direct D-M coupling.
M must also be isolated from A after a calibration replacement and before
that trial's macro. A SWAP with an unread memory in that gap would replace
the actual prepared input and invalidate the effect-tomography experiment.
Likewise, finite calibration cannot establish indefinite stationarity of the
raw operator. Its fixed-map premise and the closed D+A interval remain
structural physical assumptions, separately visible from the acquired effects.

Available operations are actual local Pauli pulses, the stated finite weak
QND Pauli instruments, actual setting selection and feedback, and private
matched Pauli frames on D. The frame keys are physically retired: the target
operation is their averaged channel, not a channel with a retained key flag.
The probe need not be pure, reset, or discarded during deployment.

For an ACQUIRED operator certificate, the same input probe is accessible for
calibration preparation BEFORE the raw interaction. Its actual preparation is
the retained finite-weak-measurement/feedback/private-dephasing replacement
operation. For chosen r in {X,Y,Z}, sign a in {+1,-1}, its output is exactly
  rho_(r,a) = (I + a alpha r)/2,
  rho_AM -> rho_(r,a) tensor Tr_A rho_AM,                (1)
where alpha=1-2t_(L_p)>0 is known from the stipulated meter. The received
preparation words and private dephasing keys are retired as required by that
replacement construction. Thus calibration does reset the small accessible
probe; deployment does not. The cost is explicitly different from assuming
pure calibration inputs or an untouched correlated probe.

An outgoing-only meter does NOT grant this upstream preparation interface.
If A can be recycled and is locally addressable between uses, that addressable
interval supplies the upstream port. If not, the acquired-effect theorem is
unavailable and only a structurally promised interaction theorem remains.
No hardware operation has been performed here.

2. Positive task effects after actual Pauli frames
=================================================
For a fixed setting b, let the entire raw D+A macro, including terminal
measurement and declared feedback, have Kraus operators
  K_l = sum_(s in {I,X,Y,Z}) s_D tensor A_(s,l).
The actual matched Pauli-frame average kills terms s!=t and gives
  T_b = sum_s Ad_(s_D) tensor M_(s,b),
  M_(s,b)(X) = sum_l A_(s,l) X A_(s,l)^* .              (2)
The output of M_s may include a recycled A and discarded internal registers.
Let
  F_(s,b) = M_(s,b)^*(I) >=0,  sum_s F_(s,b)=I_A.       (3)
These effects are on the finite input port A. Any memory outside A during the
macro would instead enlarge their domain, and six preparations on A would no
longer characterize them uniformly. The task effects are not inferred from a
small entropy or a short environmental state lifetime.

Equation (2) is an identity on arbitrary D-A-M-reference inputs, not merely on
product states. It follows by expanding Kraus operators and using the four
Pauli characters. The private frame key cannot be retained as an accessible
output: orthogonal key flags prevent the cancellation used in this identity.

3. Exact central effects renew the logical task without resetting memory
=======================================================================
Suppose, for the actual fixed setting b,
  F_(s,b)=p_(s,b) I_A,  p_(s,b)>=0, sum_s p_(s,b)=1.    (4)
For p_s>0, M_s/p_s is a trace-preserving quantum channel on the memory port.
Each virtual logical Pauli branch is therefore a state-independent classical
choice, followed by a physical memory channel that may retain complicated
information. The observer does not observe this virtual branch label.

After tracing A, for every operator X_AM,
  Tr_A M_s(X_AM) = p_s Tr_A X_AM                       (5)
when M_s acts only on A (and its local output is traced). This is stronger than
a statement about trace probabilities: local partial-trace cyclicity inserts
F_s, which is scalar. Consequently, even for arbitrary initial correlations,
  Tr_A T_b(rho_DAM)
    = (Pauli(p_b)_D tensor id_M)(Tr_A rho_DAM).         (6)

The probe need NOT actually be discarded to obtain a multi-use logical
renewal theorem. Keep A as part of the continuing memory. Between uses allow
any trace-preserving memory-only channel, including noncommuting interactions
with M. For a specified logical-error string (s_1,...,s_K), the composed
positive memory map has effect
  (product_j p_(s_j,b_j)) I.                           (7)
This follows inductively from M_s^*(I)=p_s I; intermediate trace-preserving
maps preserve I in their adjoints. Tracing the memory ONLY after all K uses
then yields exactly
  tensor_(j=1)^K Pauli(p_(.,b_j))                     (8)
on the logical outputs, including inputs entangled across uses and with a
reference. Initial logical-memory correlations are allowed because (7) is an
operator identity. Every logical wire interacts only in its specified macro
and is then isolated. The theorem does not undo earlier unmodeled damage to
that wire before the declared input boundary.

Settings b_j may be fixed, or selected by fresh exogenous randomness independent
of the apparatus and then conditioned on that declared schedule. If future
settings depend on retained fine meter words, the error rates can become
record-dependent, so a single fixed product law is not claimed. Retaining
fine meter words as part of the delivered output can also reveal correlations
between the logical error and the memory. Equations (6)-(8) concern the
declared record-coarsened logical service. The fine-word joint instrument has
its own positive branch representation but need not factor.

This is renewal of an observable task law, not renewal of the physical state.
The quantum memory, including the reused probe, may preserve old correlations
and evolve indefinitely. Preparation resets are absent during deployment.

4. Constructed controlled-involution case with unknown orientation
=================================================================
Let Q=n_x X+n_y Y+n_z Z, with real unit vector n, and suppose the unavoidable
interaction is
  U_Q = |0><0|_D tensor I_A + |1><1|_D tensor Q_A.
The fixed n is an unknown classical interaction parameter, not a quantum
memory state. The observable probe state can change arbitrarily.

Choose one actual available Pauli meter P. Perform odd L weak reads and use
the received majority y=+1 or-1. Its POVM effects are
  E_y=(I+y g P)/2,   g=1-2t_L.
For feedback convention b in {+1,-1}, apply Z_D exactly when by=-1.
There is no ideal P measurement. Write A_Q=(I+Q)/2, B_Q=(I-Q)/2. The twirled
identity-branch effect is
  F_I = A_Q E_b A_Q + B_Q E_(-b) B_Q
      = I/2 + b g (A_Q P A_Q-B_Q P B_Q)/2
      = I/2 + b g {Q,P}/4
      = [1+b g n_P] I/2.                              (9)
The only other branch is Z, with F_Z=[1-b g n_P]I/2. The exact logical service
is dephasing with coefficient lambda=b g n_P. This is uniform over the
arbitrary physical probe/memory state. It requires no pure probe source.

At the correct orientation P=Q, if that meter is actually available, the
ideal branch identity is
  Z_D^mu <mu_Q| U_Q = I_D tensor <mu_Q|.
For Q=X this is the elementary quantum-eraser identity. An arbitrary Q-meter
is not silently supplied here. With only physical X,Y,Z meters, choose the
best of their two feedback conventions. Since max|n_P|>=1/sqrt(3),
  lambda_best >= g/sqrt(3),
  phase-error probability <= [1-g/sqrt(3)]/2.           (10)
This is a useful but imperfect unknown-qubit channel. Extra measurement
precision does not improve its geometric mismatch beyond the best available
axis. Acquiring n does not create a continuously tunable coherent rotation.

One can acquire the axis using actual random setting trials and paired weak
logical audits. If the outer logical Pauli is uniform among X,Y,Z and its
paired gain is gamma, a received sign product z obeys
  E[z | current actual history, setting P] =
       gamma [1+2 g n_P]/3.                           (11)
This follows from the exact I/Z service, including all earlier pre-probe
backaction. For N trials per axis, define
  lambda_hat_P=(3 mean(z)/gamma-1)/2.
With probability at least1-delta, simultaneously for all three axes,
  |lambda_hat_P-g n_P| <=
       (3/gamma) sqrt[ln(6/delta)/(2N)] = e_N.         (12)
This is a bounded martingale bound; iid probe states are unnecessary. Selecting
P,b by the largest estimated absolute coefficient gives actual coefficient
at least g/sqrt(3)-2e_N. Counts are finite integers; only the selected axis,
feedback convention and finite certificate need remain after calibration.

Equation (11) transfers to future states only because (9) was established
as an operator identity for the promised fixed interaction. Finite calibration
on a single incoming probe state cannot itself establish that promise.

5. Decisive output-only acquisition failure
==========================================
Consider two actual controlled-unitary interactions, V=iX and Q=Y. They satisfy
  iX|0> = Y|0> = i|1>.
For every logical input, the two interactions are exactly identical when the
incoming probe is |0>. Every outgoing probe measurement, every logical audit,
and every complete training record under that incoming state has identical
law. Even a fitted unit orientation vector n=(0,1,0) is compatible with both.

On incoming |1>, however,
  iX|1> = i|0>,    Y|1> = -i|0>.
The interactions differ by logical Z. The learned weak-Y readout and usual
Z feedback has dephasing coefficient +g for controlled-Y on every state but
-g for controlled-iX on |1>. At eta=.6,L=3, the training success for both is
.896; after that probe-state change controlled-iX has error .896.

This is exact record indistinguishability followed by a consequential failure,
not a poor numerical fit. A structural promise, or an actual input excitation
operation capable of distinguishing the two operators, is necessary. It is
not enough to say that the probe state has been learned.

6. General controlled V and an acquired operator bound
=====================================================
Now allow an arbitrary fixed unitary V on A during the controlled interaction.
The actual weak-P meter and feedback convention b give
  F_I(P,b) = [I+b g H_P]/2,
  F_Z(P,b) = [I-b g H_P]/2,
  H_P = (P V + V^* P)/2.                              (13)
Proof: use A=(I+V)/2,B=(I-V)/2. Unitarity gives A^*A+B^*B=I and
A^*PA-B^*PB=(PV+V^*P)/2. No state prior enters.

Write H_P=h_0 I+h_vector dot sigma. Its noncentral part is visible as dependence
on the input probe state. The worst supported phase-error probability is
  p_max(P,b) = [1-b g h_0+g |h_vector|]/2.             (14)
It is bounded between0 and1 because F_Z is an effect. A small observed mean
error is insufficient; the operator norm in (14) is the quantity that controls
arbitrary future probe/memory states.

Using the six actual inputs (1), one can obtain the needed coefficients
without pure sources or hidden logical error labels. The general four-effect
acquisition in Section7 applies. For the controlled-V case only I and Z are
present, and h_0,h_vector can be recovered directly from their probabilities.
After its finite certificate is obtained, deployment reuses A without resets.
The certificate is about a fixed actual interaction/menu, not arbitrary
unannounced later changes to V or the trusted measurement instruments.

7. Actual finite-input tomography of the task effects
====================================================
This construction also covers a GENERAL fixed D+A CPTP raw macro and a finite
physical readout/feedback menu of B settings. Treat every setting as an actual
operation, not as a counterfactual readout inferred from incompatible data.
The general Pauli effects F_(s,b) in (3) have only four real coefficients each
when A is a qubit:
  F_(s,b)=f_(s,b,0) I+sum_r f_(s,b,r) r.

For each menu setting b and each of the six actually prepared states (1),
perform N sacrificial macros. The outer logical Pauli P is private and uniform
on X,Y,Z; z is the product of the amplified received input/output signs, with
known paired gain gamma. Let chi_s(P) be+1 when s commutes with P and-1 when it
anticommutes. Then the entirely received score
  Y_s = [1+3 chi_s(P) z/gamma]/4                      (15)
has expectation
  q_(s,b,r,a)=Tr[F_(s,b) rho_(r,a)].                   (16)
The first weak logical audit's backaction is included. There is no known
logical state or observed microscopic Pauli error in (15). On each received
sample sum_s Y_s=1 exactly. Individual Y_s can be negative; they are unbiased
scores, not fabricated positive physical probabilities.

The exact coefficients obey
  f_0 = (1/6) sum_(r,a) q_(r,a),
  f_r = [q_(r,+)-q_(r,-)]/(2 alpha).                  (17)
Use the corresponding empirical averages. The input reset (1) decouples A
from M on calibration trials; fixed raw action on D+A makes the conditional
mean (16) independent of the arbitrary state left in M. Paired audits remain
valid under arbitrary earlier histories. Actual randomized scheduling avoids
silently granting a setting to a counterfactual experiment.

For all 24B probability estimates, a simultaneous martingale Hoeffding bound
is obtained with
  xi = (3/(2 gamma)) sqrt[ln(48B/delta)/(2N)].          (18)
With probability at least1-delta, every |q_hat-q|<=xi. Consequently
  |f_hat_0-f_0|<=xi,
  |f_hat_vector-f_vector| <= sqrt(3) xi/alpha.         (19)
Only finite signed sums and counts are required. Settings, known rational
contrasts and finite-precision arithmetic are additional bounded costs. The
confidence radius can be conservatively rounded upward to a dyadic number.
Choosing the menu after seeing these same estimates is legitimate under the
simultaneous event, although testing an unlimited adaptively invented menu
would require a new allocation or independent data.

An acquired uniform bound on ANY nonidentity Pauli error uses only F_I:
  p_cert(b)=min{1, max{0,
      1-f_hat_(I,b,0)+|f_hat_(I,b,vector)|
        +xi+sqrt(3)xi/alpha}}.                         (20)
On the confidence event, sum_(s!=I)F_(s,b)<=p_cert(b) I. This certificate can
remain small even when separate nonidentity branches are not central. It is
not inferred from a small global-state approximation or a good training yield.

A useful immediate operational consequence requires no extra encoded gates.
Feed one half of a Bell pair as the logical input, initially independent of
the apparatus. After the actual frame retirement the retained reference/output
state is Bell diagonal and its intended Bell weight is at least1-p_cert for
EVERY current physical memory state. If p_cert<1/2, the output is entangled:
every separable two-qubit state has overlap at most1/2 with a maximally
entangled Bell state. Thus a sub-half certificate witnesses quantum
transmission capability at this declared port, not merely improved classical
guessing. This is a mathematical task witness, not an additional supplied
Bell-preparation operation used by the calibration observer. Any laboratory
test requiring that input would have to construct and charge it separately.

To acquire an APPROXIMATELY CENTRAL model, choose a positive probability vector
p_s from the estimated f_s0, for example clipping negative centers and
renormalizing. Its actual, received correction distance must be retained.
Define
  d_s = |f_hat_(s,vector)| + sqrt(3)xi/alpha
         +xi+|f_hat_(s,0)-p_s|.
Then ||F_s-p_sI||<=d_s. The one-use record-coarsened logical marginal obeys
  half-diamond error <= (1/2) sum_s d_s               (21)
relative to Pauli(p) tensor the appropriate ancillary trace, uniformly over
arbitrary correlations. To prove (21), the difference of the positive effect
functionals has completely bounded trace norm ||F_s-p_sI||; local partial
trace and Pauli conjugation are contractions, then sum over s. The norm is
the standard induced diamond norm of the map from D+A to D; references are
included in that definition.

8. What composes under memory, and what does not
==============================================
If a selected actual macro satisfies its uniform acquired bound
  F_bad=sum_(s!=I)F_s <= p I,
then for any specified set T of fault locations in an arbitrary sequence,
  probability(all locations in T are bad) <= p^|T|.   (22)
Sum over all other outcomes. The sum there is trace preserving. At each
specified bad location apply the positive adjoint inequality; arbitrary
intervening memory channels preserve its order. This is an unconditional
positive fault-path bound. It does NOT require independent microscopic noise,
environment resetting, measurement of the virtual error labels or a posterior
for the memory. In the controlled-V family the faults are only logical Z.

In fact the number of bad uses is stochastically dominated by Binomial(K,p),
for a fixed deployment menu with the same bound p at every use. Condition on
any earlier positive virtual-error branches: the next normalized physical
state still has bad probability at most p. Induction on the remaining uses,
or coupling each next Bernoulli choice to a fresh uniform random variable,
then bounds every upper tail by the corresponding binomial tail. Equivalently
  E exp(t number_bad) <= (1-p+p exp(t))^K, t>=0.
The exponential-moment inequality also follows directly by expanding the
product and applying (22). These are statements about positive virtual branch
weights, not assertions that the observer receives the error labels. A future
postselection condition would invalidate this unnormalized stochastic bound.

Unlike the earlier accepted-operation construction, (22) has no global
postselection denominator: every deployment use produces an output. It is
still conditional on the finite calibration confidence event and fixed
physical menu/boundary. It is not a universal fault-tolerance threshold or
proof of economically implemented error correction. Redundancy, gates and
fault-tolerant control must be charged separately if used to exploit (22).

Exact central effects give (8). Approximately central effects give a stronger
K-use marginal allowance of order K times the one-use allowance (21), by
replacing each use with the ideal Pauli step and using channel contractivity.
One convenient proof maps the actual joint logical-memory state to its logical
marginal after each use; memory-only operations do not change that marginal,
and (21) is uniform over the actual joint input. This does not require a
common future-memory channel for the ideal replacement. The target is the
logical marginal, not the joint logical-memory state.

Fixed per-use precision does not yield a fixed total error on an infinite
logical record. If K grows, total-error requirements need renewed statistical
precision, a summable risk allocation or a weaker operational target. A fixed
certificate still enables a bounded per-use service. State-independent task
effects make old physical-state records unnecessary, not physical acquisition
time or certainty about arbitrary model drift.

9. Physically mixed menus can create central effects
===================================================
For a finite actually available menu, choose private setting probabilities w_b,
perform that random setting, and retire the setting key. The averaged effects
are
  F_s(w)=sum_b w_b F_(s,b).
The fresh setting must be independent of the incoming port state, conditional
on the declared past. Keep it private until the A-M preparation/evolution
interval has ended and A is isolated. When the raw interaction is common to
all settings, choosing the terminal meter only AFTER that interaction is the
cleanest causal implementation. If M learns the setting before preparing A,
the different settings can receive different adversarial probe states and the
norm of the averaged effect is no longer a uniform certificate.

Exact two-setting witness: let F_bad,0=|0><0| and F_bad,1=|1><1|. A private
equal mixture on a common input has F_bad=I/2 and certified error at most1/2.
If the setting b leaks and the incoming probe is prepared as |b>, the error
probability is1 on every use. A valid bound for that leaked setting model is
sum_b w_b ||F_bad,b||, which equals1 here. These are different physical
interfaces. This privacy/ordering premise is also needed when learned menu
weights are frozen and only their current random selection is renewed.

The learned coefficient vectors therefore support a small convex optimization:
choose nonnegative w summing to1, reduce each acquired noncentral vector while
maximizing identity weight or minimizing the robust allowance (20). This is a
physical mixture of real operations, not a signed quasi-probability estimator
and not a claim that incompatible measurements were replayed from one word.

A controlled test takes V=exp(i phi) Q_n. For actual Pauli meter P,
  H_P = cos(phi) n_P I + (a Pauli cross-product term).
Signed measurement/feedback weights correspond to a real vector w with
||w||_1<=1. Choosing w=n/||n||_1 cancels the cross-product term and leaves
  lambda = g cos(phi)/||n||_1.                         (23)
The exact checked example n=(3/5,4/5,0), phi=pi/5, L=3, eta=.6 uses X,Y
measurement probabilities3/7,4/7 and has lambda about.4576724711. Its scalar
phase-error probability is about.2711637644. The weights in this algebraic
control are model-given; an executed acquisition must derive its own weights
from (15)-(19), with uncertainty retained. No empirical success is claimed
for those example weights by this source alone.

There is a consequential failure: V=iX has zero scalar part for every H_P,
so any such Pauli-readout/Z-feedback mixture that is exactly central has
lambda=0. It cannot retain a useful uniform quantum channel in this menu.
A physical S phase correction could turn iX into X, but S is an additional
actual coherent operation with its earlier preparation/native-gate/readout
costs and errors. It must be constructed and included in the learned macro;
state tomography does not create it. Infeasibility of a useful acquired menu
is therefore a real result, not permission to assume a better port.

10. Extension in algebra, not an automatically available large-system device
===========================================================================
If an accessible finite probe supports Hermitian anticommuting observables
Gamma_j with {Gamma_i,Gamma_j}=2 delta_ij I, let
Q=sum_j n_j Gamma_j with sum n_j^2=1. The same proof with an actual Gamma_j
meter gives scalar effects [1+b g n_j]I/2. The number of coefficients is the
number of accessible generators; it need not be the full state dimension.

High-weight Gamma measurements are not automatically local weak Pauli ports.
Their coherent compilation, gate errors and routing must be supplied by an
actual construction, such as the separate consumed-terminal parity compiler
where its hypotheses apply. Reusing a retained probe is a different operation
from discarding it in that compiler. No large interacting model, polynomial
complexity for arbitrary boundaries or general simulator follows from this
algebraic extension alone.

11. Strong prior and independent executable checks
==================================================
Environment-assisted quantum correction and quantum erasure are established:
Gregoratti and Werner, Quantum Lost and Found, arXiv:quant-ph/0209025; their
discrete-time feedback paper arXiv:quant-ph/0403092; Trendelkamp-Schroer, Helm,
Strunz, arXiv:1110.4806; Buscemi, arXiv:quant-ph/0611111. The generic results
often assume a pure environment or broad access to its measurement operators.
The exact restricted mixed-probe identity above is derived directly instead
of importing those assumptions. Pauli randomization, detector/effect tomography
and convex experimental design are conventional tools; equally informed
standard density, tensor and controller methods can use every construction.

The useful joint principle here is: physically acquired small task effects
can certify whether a continuing memory matters to the task; an actual
measurement/feedback/randomization menu can sometimes make those effects
scalar, allowing renewal without resetting the memory. This is a scoped
theorem and construction, not a priority claim for its separate ingredients.

orientation_renewal_checks.py directly checks841 identities/instances:
108 scalar-effect cases;72 dense R-D-A-M correlated-input cases;12 two-use
cases with the SAME A recycled and a random noncommuting A-M unitary between
uses;96 general controlled-unitary effect cases;20 finite-mixed-preparation
effect reconstructions;96 actual paired-weak-score identities for arbitrary
two-qubit raw unitaries;78 noncentral-effect binomial-tail checks with recycled
A and noncommuting A-M evolution; exact iX/Y indistinguishability and the
physical mixture control and the leaked-setting counterexample. Maximum
numerical residual is recorded in its JSON,
about8.9e-16. Direct four-frame/Kraus simulation is compared to independently
constructed positive branches and reduced target channels. These are
mathematical execution checks, not laboratory evidence or experimental
certification of the bounded interaction boundary.
