ROBUST TASK ACQUISITION UNDER IMPERFECT PHYSICAL CLOSURE
25 October 2026

Result and distinction
======================
A known uniform design bound on residual system-bath interaction can be carried
through received weak-score acquisition, selected control, and current-task
certification. Six-state tomography does not itself prove that bound or
exclude a hidden bath bypass. The useful gain is a specified physical closure
operation plus an acquired task response, with the two sources of information
kept separate.

The general operator-acquisition result requires finite mixed preparations and
pays their residual operation gap. A stronger isotropic-exchange control has
scalar task effects. It needs no reset of A or M to acquire a useful duration:
the whole current unknown state can vary arbitrarily throughout acquisition.
The actual five-duration experiment obtains a current transfer allowance below
37/1000 from received weak words and public design bounds, without receiving
the native exchange J or a bath model.

1. What uniform closure means
=============================
For each physical action a and actual history h, let G_(a,h) be the complete
actual raw call on logical D, readable A and unread M. A design supplies an
ideal comparison
  G_a^0 tensor B_h
where G_a^0 is a FIXED closed task operation on D+A and B_h acts only on M.
Require the completely bounded, state-uniform relation

  (1/2)||G_(a,h)-(G_a^0 tensor B_h)||_diamond
      <=epsilon_iso(a,h).                                (1)

The norm includes arbitrary untouched references and arbitrary initial
D/A/M correlations. It is not a fit on the calibration input state. It may
be established by a Hamiltonian design calculation, a hardware specification
with explicit scope, or some stronger acquired interface, but not by simply
extrapolating six observed local preparation responses.

Trusted preparations, weak instruments, classical outcome registers and
feedback may be composed on either side of(1), because complete trace-norm
contractivity cannot increase the bound. When the residual coupling continues
during readout or a constructed controller, its whole extra interval must be
included in(1) or charged as a separate gap. A bound for the raw unitary alone
does not silently close the rest of the instrument.

2. A pulse design and its structural blind spot
==============================================
The synchronized Pauli construction analysed in exchange_preserving_closure.py
retains isotropic exchange while averaging single-system-Pauli D/M and A/M
couplings. Its stated sufficient bound for4N physical paired pulses is

  epsilon_iso(T)
   <=min(1, C T^2/N+4N delta_pair+epsilon_gap),
  C=kappa/4+v^2/2,
  kappa<=kappa_M+4|J|v.                                  (2)

Here v bounds the relevant interaction norm, kappa_M bounds the bath
commutators appearing in the design proof, delta_pair is a per-paired-pulse
half-diamond allowance, and epsilon_gap covers remaining exposed interfaces.
Bath Hilbert-space dimension does not appear explicitly, but uniform local
norm/commutator bounds are real assumptions. They must not be inferred from
the mere small dimension of A. The pure bath Hamiltonian norm need not be
bounded if the stated commutator/domain conditions suffice for the proof.

The finite pulse cost matters. With delta_pair>0, the term4N delta_pair grows
as the cycle count grows. The continuous-N lower envelope of the two main
terms is4T sqrt(C delta_pair), attained only as an optimisation of the bound;
it is not licensed to set N infinitely large and obtain exact closure.

A decisive blind spot survives: a bath-modulated exchange
  J_b (XX+YY+ZZ) tensor B_M
transforms in exactly the same way as the desired exchange under collective
Pauli conjugation. A pulse average that retains the latter also retains this
term. It cannot be removed by claiming the average cancels all bath terms.
If its norm has an independent bound u, a conservative additional uT can be
charged by Duhamel; without a bound or a stronger interface, equation(2) is
not a closure theorem for that enlarged physical class.

This obstruction can involve a physical quantum mode M, not just uncertainty
in a classical model label. For example B_M=Z_M can make the transfer rate
different on two quantum sectors, with future M controls rotating those
sectors. Local calibration on one sector does not certify another. The
operator-norm design condition is what blocks that hidden-state failure.

3. Pauli-event perturbations are sharper than generic score perturbations
========================================================================
The retained protocol applies actual matched Pauli frames and retires their
keys, with current audit settings hidden from the raw call. The resulting
real AND ideal operations have positive Pauli instruments, even if the raw
DD pulse errors are coherent. Let F_(a,s)^h be the actual error-label effect
on A/M, and F_(a,s)^0 the ideal closed effect on A.

Applying the two channels in(1) to a maximally entangled logical input and
testing a Bell label is a mathematical probability test. It gives

  ||F_(a,s)^h-F_(a,s)^0 tensor I_M|| <=epsilon_iso(a,h).  (3)

No Bell-state source or Bell measurement is added to the physical observer:
this is the proof of the effect bound. The actual paired weak-score identity
then reads
  E[chi_s(P)z]=gamma(4p_s-1)/3,
so its inversion estimates the actual p_s. Thus the bias in the inferred
Pauli EVENT probability is at most epsilon_iso, not an amplified generic
score-error allowance.

If one knows only that the full classical score channels differ in
half-diamond distanceepsilon, and cannot use the exact Pauli-instrument
identity, a bounded score in[-1,1] can shift by2epsilon. Inversion then gives
the weaker probability bias3epsilon/(2gamma). This is a valid conservative
fallback, but it should not be confused with(3).

Errors of the DD pulses belong INSIDE the raw call in(1); trusted exact outer
Pauli framing can still produce the positive Pauli decomposition. If the
outer framing itself is only approximately correct and no positive stochastic
error decomposition is supplied, a diamond-distance triangle bound may survive
but the positive specified-fault-set probability envelope below does not
automatically follow. A coherent signed perturbation is not a bad-event CP map.

4. Six mixed preparations with a residual replacement gap
=========================================================
Suppose the intended calibration replacement prepares
  sigma_(i,+/-)=(I+/-alpha sigma_i)/2
on A independently of M. Its actual operation may leave a residual gap:
  (1/2)||R_(i,+/-)^h -[sigma_(i,+/-) tensor Tr_A(.)]||_diamond
       <=epsilon_prep.
The M-only comparison can be more general if explicitly declared. The bound
must include correlations and any unretired preparation record relevant to
the claimed output interface.

For any input history, triangle inequality and(3) imply that the mean actual
Pauli score estimates the ideal preparation probability with bias at most
  Delta_cal=epsilon_iso,cal+epsilon_prep.                 (4)
Here epsilon_iso,cal is a uniform bound over the calibration calls of the
specified action. The actual A/M state after a preparation can otherwise be
arbitrary.

WithN samples and the existing simultaneous bounded-score radius eps_p, the
total probability error is<=eps_p+Delta_cal. Reconstructing a qubit effect
by the six mixed preparations therefore gives

  ||Fhat_a-F_a^0|| <=r_stat+K_alpha Delta_cal,
  r_stat=eps_p K_alpha,
  K_alpha=1+sqrt3/alpha.                                (5)

This is a conservative general operator theorem. The scalar coefficient
error is at mosteps_p+Delta_cal, each Bloch coefficient error is at most that
quantity/alpha, and ||aI+v.sigma||=|a|+||v|| gives(5).

Every data-selected positive action mixture is covered by the same event:
  q_ideal <=1-lambda_min(Fhat_mix)+r_stat+K_alpha Delta_cal.
For a future actual call, charge its separate deployment closure allowance:

  q_actual
    <=min(1, 1-lambda_min(Fhat_mix)+r_stat
                    +K_alpha(epsilon_iso,cal+epsilon_prep)
                    +epsilon_iso,deploy).              (6)

For equal calibration/deploymentepsilon, the closure penalty is
(K_alpha+1)epsilon, not just a single epsilon. The calibration can be biased
one way and the deployed device the other. Under only the generic score-channel
fallback, replace Delta_cal by3Delta_cal/(2gamma). No observed six-state fit
is advertised as evidence that the assumed gap is zero.

5. Known exchange versus an acquired unknown duration
=====================================================
If J is known and the closed reference is exactly the swap at T=pi/(4J), the
strongest equally informed controller already obtains current transfer error
<=epsilon_iso(T) directly from the design theorem. Additional tomography is
not needed to claim this bound, and would add acquisition cost. The useful
acquisition question is an unknown fixed J or unknown preserved closed task
response, not re-learning an already supplied exact swap.

For fixed unknown isotropic exchange, up to a global phase,
  U(T)=cos(theta) I-i sin(theta) SWAP,
  theta=2JT.
The target is actual transfer from D to distinct A, so relabel the output
logically in the proof. The target-frame operator is
  SWAP U(T)=cos(theta) SWAP-i sin(theta) I.
Using
  SWAP=(I I+X X+Y Y+Z Z)/2
and actual input-D/output-A matched frames gives exact positive Pauli effects

  F_I^0=[1-3 cos^2(theta)/4] I_A,
  F_X^0=F_Y^0=F_Z^0=[cos^2(theta)/4] I_A.                (7)

Their scalar form is a consequence of the declared exchange symmetry and
target-frame physical operation. It is not inferred from a small sampled
state vector, from low entropy, or from a six-state goodness of fit. It holds
for every initial A/M state, including entanglement with M, and after arbitrary
between-call A/M evolution. The error branch rotates the physical retained
mode; it need not erase its state or its memory.

Consequently acquisition of
  q0(T)=3 cos^2(2JT)/4
needs no reset or known preparation of A. The observer need not identify J:
it can acquire the current task error at each of a finite duration menu and
select a good physical operation. D is actually privately mixed in the
implemented calibration; no pure source is supplied.

ForN_T actual paired weak audits at durationT, define
  qhat_T=3/4*[1-mean(z)/gamma],
  r_T=(3/(4gamma))*sqrt(2 log(2K/delta)/N_T).
The same-history bounded-score theorem and scalar effects give, simultaneously
for theK durations with probability>=1-delta,
  q0(T)<=qhat_T+r_T+epsilon_cal(T).
The certified current deployment bound is therefore

  qcert(T)=min(1,max(0,qhat_T+r_T
                         +epsilon_cal(T)+epsilon_deploy(T))). (8)

Selecting the duration that minimizes(8) is licensed by that simultaneous
event. In contrast with(6), there is no six-state reconstruction factor and
no A-replacement penalty. Negative qhat values can occur from finite signed
scores; they are not negative physical error probabilities. The certified
upper, with all charges, is the claim.

A finite duration menu is not guaranteed to contain an approximate swap for
an unrestricted unknown J. A coverage result requires an explicit J range,
time grid and clock-control premise. The implemented certificate is for the
selected finite menu and does not silently acquire such a global promise.

that delivered output against later reuse requires the explicit isolation or
extraction operation discussed next.

7. Specified-set envelope and its physical scope
===============================================
On the good calibration event, equation(6) or(8) gives a uniform actual
bad-effect bound for each certified current call:
  B_j^*(I)<=q_j I.
For a specified setT of locations, insert the positive bad map at those
locations and the complete nonselected call at all others, with arbitrary
allowed completely positive trace-preserving between-call evolution. Backward
positivity gives
  C_T^*(I)<=(product_(j in T) q_j) I,
  Pr(all locations inT are bad)<=product_(j in T) q_j.    (10)
This includes arbitrary physical M and reference correlations. If control
choices adapt to actual retained records, use a certified uniform bound over
those choices at each location, or carry the explicit history-register bound.
A conditional state inferred from a record is unnecessary.

Equation(10) requires an ACTUAL positive Pauli/error instrument and no uncharged
postselection. It is not implied merely by a diamond-distance estimate for a
coherent signed perturbation. The inherited global-postselection counterexample
still applies if future acceptances are conditioned on. A per-call allowance
also does not mean an infinite output history has bounded total error.

For sustained quantum transfer, already delivered logical outputs must be
isolated from later interactions. A transferD->A places the old A state inD;
either the boundary role must move through explicitly available routing/next
exchange ports, or an actual extraction step must be implemented before the
same physical A is reused. Relabeling a tensor factor in the proof is not a
free physical SWAP or output-extraction operation.

The executed calibration consumes its logical outputs and then the parent
validates one final CURRENT transfer. It does not implement a protected stream
with uncharged extraction. Equation(10) is a structural composition theorem
for a protocol that satisfies those physical conditions, not an assertion that
the current experiment has built such a stream.

8. Strong alternatives and remaining limitation
================================================
Conventional dynamical decoupling, Hamiltonian control, robust process/effect
tomography, density propagation and tensor methods may use the identical pulses,
duration menu, full weak records and design bounds. They can implement the same
observer. There is no claimed exclusive algorithmic advantage.

The substantive gain is that an acquired useful coherent transfer operation
can have a bounded task description despite arbitrary surviving environment
memory. Here that gain comes from deliberately synthesizing an approximately
closed, symmetry-preserving interface and acquiring its timing response. The
physical structure supplies a theorem about every unknown boundary state;
the measurements supply the missing native response parameter through a real
operation. Neither role is substituted for the other.

The unresolved general question includes interactions indistinguishable from
the desired exchange under the available controls, unreliable pulse quality,
unbounded local norm/commutator growth, operation drift and output extraction
for repeated services. Finite local acquisition does not remove those issues.
