ASTRA — EXCHANGE-PRESERVING FINITE-PULSE CLOSURE
25 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane

Question, surviving operation, and scope
=========================================
The acquired-effect construction needs a bounded quantum interface during
each call. Simply assuming that an unread environment cannot interact during
the call leaves a physical interface unbuilt. This source constructs an
approximate closed call for an explicit important Hamiltonian class, using
the already allowed single-qubit Pauli controls. Synchronized pulses suppress
single-site data/environment and receiver/environment interactions while
preserving an actual Heisenberg exchange that transports an UNKNOWN quantum
state to a receiver. No clean receiver state, environment reset or ideal
SWAP gate is supplied.

The interaction-preserving dynamical-decoupling mechanism is established
science. Khodjasteh and Lidar explicitly studied global decoupling that
commutes with Heisenberg computation in2008; selective decoupling for state
transfer is also prior. The gain for this programme is a finite, uniform
joint-channel isolation allowance that can be inserted into the acquired
effect certificate, with a commutant obstruction and the physical strength,
bandwidth and pulse-resource requirements made explicit.

The construction is conditional on an actual exchange interaction and
bounded local unwanted couplings/commutators. Finite calibration records
do not prove those Hamiltonian-class assumptions for an arbitrary hidden
environment. The theorem does not acquire a nonexistent exchange port, nor
exclude bath-modulated exchange by observing a few successful calls.

This selects an alternative exchange-transfer interface. It does not
retroactively prove that the preceding controlled-V marker/phase apparatus
has the Hamiltonian form below; those earlier constructions retain their
own physical boundary premises.

1. Actual Hamiltonian class and control sequence
================================================
Let D be an incoming data qubit, A a receiver qubit in any state, and M an
arbitrarily large quantum environment. During a chosen call suppose

 H=H_ref+V,
 H_ref=J H_ex tensor I_M + I_DA tensor H_M,
 H_ex=X_D X_A+Y_D Y_A+Z_D Z_A,                          (1)

and the unwanted coupling has the form

 V=sum_(alpha=X,Y,Z) [sigma_alpha,D tensor B_D,alpha
                       +sigma_alpha,A tensor B_A,alpha]. (2)

Identity operators on the other controlled qubit are understood. Each
B is a bounded Hermitian bath operator; local fields are included by taking
B proportional to I_M. No bath state, stationarity, mixing time or bound
on dim(M) is assumed. In particular, A may be entangled with M before the
call and M can retain all earlier information. A fixed unknown J is a
classical interaction parameter, distinct from that physical quantum state.

Apply the synchronized pair group

 G={I_D I_A, X_D X_A, Y_D Y_A, Z_D Z_A}.

Every g commutes with H_ex, and (1/4)sum_g g* V g=0. Thus the necessary
exchange survives while the single-site unwanted terms average to zero.
One cycle has four equal free dwells, each tau/4, with actual pair pulses
XX, ZZ, XX, ZZ after the four respective dwells. The cumulative toggling
frames are I,XX,YY,ZZ up to irrelevant phases, and the final frame is I.
Every pair pulse is two local Pauli operations; no entangling pulse is added.

Independent full Pauli decoupling of D alone would also remove the desired
exchange at first order. It therefore fails to preserve the chosen transfer
operation. Synchronized control is consequential here, not just a smaller
storage error in a task that a protected idle buffer already solves.

2. A uniform finite-cycle bound without an extensive bath norm
==============================================================
Define the reference interaction-picture coupling

 V_I(t)=exp(i H_ref t) V exp(-i H_ref t).

Assume physical bounds

 ||V|| <= v,
 ||V_I(t)-V_I(s)|| <= kappa |t-s|.                     (3)

For finite-dimensional M one may take kappa=||[H_ref,V]||. A useful local
bound, which does not contain the extensive number ||H_M||, is

 ||V|| <= v_sum=sum_(j,alpha)||B_j,alpha||,
 kappa <= kappa_M+4|J|v_sum,
 kappa_M=sum_(j,alpha)||[H_M,B_j,alpha]||.               (4)

To obtain the exchange contribution, use H_ex=2 SWAP-I. Its irrelevant
scalar drops from a commutator, leaving norm at most4|J| times the coupling
norm. Local bath Hamiltonians with finite incident interaction norms can
make (4) independent of total bath size. Large irrelevant bath energies
alone do not enlarge this bound. Unbounded bosonic couplings or unbounded
local commutators are NOT covered merely because the bath has low entropy.
For an infinite bath, (3) is the needed operator-norm regularity premise;
a formal commutator must genuinely define a bounded operator before (4)
is used.

For one cycle let W(t) be its reference interaction-picture unitary and
V_tog(t)=g(t)* V_I(t) g(t). Freeze V_I at the cycle midpoint t_c. Since all
four frames have equal dwell and commute with H_ref,

 integral_0^tau g(t)* V_I(t_c) g(t) dt=0.

Hence the first Dyson integral obeys

 ||integral_0^tau V_tog(t) dt||
   <= kappa integral_0^tau |t-t_c|dt
   = kappa tau^2/4.                                   (5)

No convergent infinite Magnus series is assumed. Unitary Duhamel gives
||W(t)-I||<=v t, so the remaining exact integral satisfies

 ||W(tau)-I+i integral V_tog||
   <= integral_0^tau v ||W(t)-I||dt
   <= v^2 tau^2/2.                                    (6)

Define C=kappa/4+v^2/2. A cycle therefore differs from the desired joint
reference evolution by at most C tau^2 in operator norm. For N cycles at
total free-evolution duration T=N tau, unitary telescoping yields

 ||U_DD(T)-exp(-i H_ref T)|| <= C T^2/N.               (7)

The resulting joint channels satisfy the uniform half-diamond bound

 (1/2)||Ad_U_DD - Ad_(U_ex tensor U_M)||_diamond
       <= epsilon_iso=min{1,C T^2/N}.                  (8)

The bound holds on the FULL D+A+M operation, with arbitrary additional
references and arbitrary initial correlations. It is not a bound for one
chosen bath state. Append the same fixed weak instruments, feedback,
classical records or discards to both sides: contractivity preserves (8).
This is the form required to charge imperfect closure in task-effect
acquisition and deployment.

At finite N the actual error rate can still depend on the incoming quantum
memory state and its history, within the allowance. The theorem does not
declare a constant actual gate-error process merely because a single
calibration succeeded. It compares every input to a closed reference using
physical uniform bounds (3).

3. Finite number, accuracy and duration of actual pulses
========================================================
The ideal endpoint sequence uses4N synchronized pair pulses, or8N local
Pauli pulses. This is finite for every declared error target. If the actual
joint pair-pulse maps differ from their reference operations by at most
delta_p in half-diamond norm, uniformly over the coupled registers, their
telescoping allowance adds4N delta_p:

 epsilon_iso <= min{1,C T^2/N +4N delta_p}.             (9)

It is not free to send N to infinity. Ignoring integer/timing constraints,
the optimum of this upper bound has
N=T sqrt[C/(4 delta_p)], minimum4T sqrt(C delta_p).
These are bounds on a stated error budget, not predictions of an optimal
laboratory pulse count or a universal physical error floor.

A finite-width realization can also be stated explicitly. Suppose every
pair pulse lasts w and implements the SAME rotation profile on D and A.
Isotropic exchange commutes with U(t) tensor U(t) throughout that profile,
and H_M commutes with the control. If the calibrated control error is
delta_ctrl, the pulse differs from its ideal endpoint composed with
reference free evolution during w by at most

 delta_p <= v w + delta_ctrl.                          (10)

This follows by applying Duhamel to the toggled unwanted V during the pulse;
no ||H_M|| term is needed. The reference clock now includes pulse durations.
Choose each free dwell (T-4Nw)/(4N)>=0. The same proof uses V_tog=0 in the
reference pulse windows, and the equal-dwell midpoint cancellation still
holds; (9) remains a sufficient bound with TOTAL duration T. It is coarse
but explicit. Finite pulse bandwidth enforces4Nw<=T.

Knowing only a pulse's ideal endpoint does not establish identical temporal
profiles or the uniform delta_ctrl bound. Those are additional control
accuracy requirements, to be calibrated or physically justified separately.
Known Eulerian, concatenated, dynamically corrected and optimized pulse
methods may improve these costs. Conventional controllers receive all such
options; this construction does not claim a new or optimal DD sequence.

4. The interaction really transfers an unknown quantum state
============================================================
Let S denote SWAP_DA. Since H_ex=2S-I,

 U_ex(T)=exp(iJT)[cos(2JT) I-i sin(2JT) S].             (11)

At J>0 and T=pi/(4J), U_ex=exp(-i pi/4)S. The incoming unknown state on D,
including entanglement with an untouched reference, moves to physical A.
The previous state/correlations of A move to old D. No pure receiver or
receiver reset is necessary. Equation (8) gives the uniform transfer
allowance even if the receiver was entangled with M initially.

This is a transfer between distinct physical registers, using an ACTUAL
exchange coupling. A remote link requires a real exchange-connected geometry
or a real forward carrier. A pair of freely colocated registers with perfect
arbitrary transport is not a demonstrated communication advantage. The
operation's surviving consequence is transport through its specified
interaction interface while preserving the unknown data/reference state;
the identity task of leaving data in an already protected buffer does not
complete that transfer.

After the call, physical A is the logical OUTPUT and must be isolated or
extracted by the declared actual routing/buffer operation. It cannot also
be reused as noisy environmental memory while earlier outputs are claimed
isolated. Old D may become the next finite boundary register, or be placed
in continuing memory. A repeated service must specify that port schedule;
mathematical relabeling does not perform a free physical SWAP or extraction.

5. Partial exchange has an exact renewable task law
====================================================
Exact SWAP timing is not necessary to define a useful bounded task model.
Apply a fresh private Pauli P on the incoming physical D and the matched
same P on the outgoing physical A, retiring that outer frame key. To express
the transfer in input/output logical coordinates, mathematically relabel
the output by S; this adds NO physical gate. The relative operator is

 S U_ex(T)=exp(iJT)[cos(theta)S-i sin(theta)I],
 theta=2JT.

Its data-Pauli Kraus coefficients after actual frame averaging are

 K_I=[cos(theta)/2-i sin(theta)] I_A,
 K_X=[cos(theta)/2] X_A,
 K_Y=[cos(theta)/2] Y_A,
 K_Z=[cos(theta)/2] Z_A.                                (12)

Every effect is scalar:

 F_I=[sin^2(theta)+cos^2(theta)/4] I_A,
 F_X=F_Y=F_Z=[cos^2(theta)/4] I_A.                     (13)

The transferred logical service is exactly a depolarizing channel with
visibility lambda=sin^2(2JT), independently of the receiver state or its
physical quantum memory. Between correctly routed calls, that memory may
evolve arbitrarily. Under the structural exchange class, the usual
central-effect renewal theorem applies, without receiver resets during
calibration or deployment. The scalar parameter is a task law, not an
environmental posterior.

If J and the clock are known, choose the full-SWAP duration directly; this
strong conventional controller needs no state tomography. If fixed J or
timing is unknown, use ACTUAL sacrificial transfer calls at a finite declared
duration menu. Paired input-D/output-A weak Pauli audits have received score

 E[z | actual history,T]=gamma sin^2(2JT).              (14)

Thus a duration can be acquired from finite signed counts without preparing
or resetting A, under the exact reference class. No arbitrary coherent gate
is created by knowing J. Imperfect closure needs the separate calibration
and deployment allowances developed in the sibling robust-acquisition
source. The clock, exchange interaction, number of physical calls and weak
audit precision are separate resources. An arbitrary unannounced drift of
J is not acquired by a past finite menu experiment.

6. The decisive control-commutant obstruction
=============================================
Collective Pauli control preserves not only the desired exchange but also
every unwanted term of the form H_ex tensor B_M. A scalar bath modulation
of the same system operator lies in the SAME control-invariant subspace.
The four pulses cannot distinguish it from the desired interaction.

Take the exact three-qubit Hamiltonian

 H=J H_ex tensor(I_M+Z_M),  T=pi/(8J).                  (15)

If M is in its +Z sector, the exchange strength is2J and the call is a SWAP.
If M is in its -Z sector, the coupling vanishes and the call is identity
on the two physical registers. Every proposed collective pulse commutes
with the FULL H, so no number of cycles changes this fact. Cross-port
Pauli framing gives transfer identity probability1 in the first sector,
but only1/4 (fully depolarizing transfer visibility0) in the second.

A stable +Z calibration therefore cannot certify a future -Z environment
from the same successful data. This is an exact interface-identifiability
failure, not a step-size or statistical-precision problem. In the zero-
coupling sector, local Pauli controls alone cannot transfer unknown quantum
information between D and A at all. More of the same pulses cannot solve it.

The theorem must consequently EXCLUDE these bath-modulated exchange terms,
include their relevant memory inside an acquired larger boundary, or charge
an independently justified uniform bound. If an extra term R has ||R||<=u,
Duhamel gives a conservative additional allowance uT, whether or not the
Pauli group suppresses any part of it. This can destroy useful precision;
it must not be dropped. The same applies to direct data-memory terms outside
the class and to unbounded bath bandwidth violating (3).

The organising principle is that useful control must preserve a task
generator while eliminating its nuisance directions. Any nuisance in the
preserved control subspace survives unless another physical resource or
acquired boundary distinguishes it. This is a substantive limit on replacing
an assumed closed interface by dynamical control.

8. Strong prior science and limitations
=======================================
Khodjasteh and Lidar, Rigorous Bounds on the Performance of a Hybrid
Dynamical Decoupling-Quantum Computing Scheme (2008):
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/0803.4320
The paper already treats global decoupling compatible with Heisenberg
computation, finite pulse interval/width costs, and scalability limits.
The present sequence is a conventional method available to every comparator.

Frydrych, Hoskovec, Jex and Alber, Selective dynamical decoupling for quantum
state transfer (2014):
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.3424
The paper studies Pauli-pulse selection that preserves useful state-transfer
dynamics while suppressing unwanted interactions. Transfer, rather than
preservation of a dominated idle fixture, is established prior motivation.

Burgelman et al., Limitations to Dynamical Error Suppression and Gate-Error
Virtualization from Temporally Correlated Nonclassical Noise, PRX Quantum6,
010323 (2025):
  https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.6.010323
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.04766
The paper demonstrates control-history dependence in quantum-bath models
despite reset operations and ideal instantaneous pulses with finite timing.
Its warning is consistent with the present scope: finite-rate pulses do not
establish an exactly constant error law, and a uniform bounded-coupling/
commutator hypothesis cannot be inferred from one benign bath history.

More generally, the proof needs only that the unwanted V has zero average
under the chosen group and satisfies (3). For example, mixed-axis terms
sigma_alpha,D sigma_beta,A tensor B with alpha!=beta are also removed by
this group. Same-axis terms sigma_alpha,D sigma_alpha,A tensor B survive.
This makes the useful boundary a property of the CONTROL COMMUTANT as well
as spatial locality: the preserved interaction algebra determines which
unread environmental parameters must still be bounded or acquired.

No dynamical-decoupling invention, exclusive simulator advantage, generic
environment closure or practical hardware superiority is claimed. The useful
new programme connection is an explicitly charged construction of the
approximate interface needed by acquired task-effect bounds, alongside an
exact retained-interaction obstruction that prevents a blanket conclusion.
