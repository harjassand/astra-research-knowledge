RESULT

The 2017 FPJA experiment supplies a demonstrated frequency-conversion
mechanism but only ONE broadband measurement line. Its frequency-indexed
scattering ports do not provide two existing physical thermal terminations.
The 80/100 mK four-bath calculation in JOSEPHSON_PLATFORM.txt is a conditional
numerical fixture, not an assembled-device refrigeration prediction.

The simplest proposed interface, two parallel series-RLC thermal branches,
FAILS a frequency-wide isolation test. Its small center contamination and
good center impedance conceal a direct hot-to-cold resonance. With the
device port open the exact Planck-weighted bypass is 59.0615 aW, about
32.84 times the fixture's 1.7982 aW cooling margin. The assembled device's
complex admittance can alter that number, so it is not claimed as a measured
or exact assembled-FPJA heat current. It is a concrete obstruction to
declaring this shortcut a completed thermal interface.

A different, published JRM topology gives signal and idler distinct spatial
differential channels. It avoids this particular external parallel-branch
loop. It does not establish zero parasitic heat, measured refrigeration or
a calibrated complete pump budget. The two devices' numbers stay separate.

2. THE SPECIFIED PASSIVE CIRCUIT AND FDT NOISE WEIGHTS

Connect two series-RLC branches in parallel at the converter's external
node. Branch c has resistor R=50 ohm at Tc=80mK, resonant at fa=4.155GHz;
branch h has R=50 ohm at Th=100mK, resonant at fb=5.756GHz. Both have Q=20.
All L and C elements are ideal lossless components in this construction:

  L_s=RQ/omega_s, C_s=1/(RQ omega_s),
  Y_s(omega)=1/[R+i(omega L_s-1/(omega C_s))].

Values:
  branch c: L=38.3044 nH, C=38.3044 fF, conductance FWHM=207.75MHz;
  branch h: L=27.6503 nH, C=27.6503 fF, conductance FWHM=287.80MHz.

The frequency-dependent current noise of each resistor is fixed by its
dissipative admittance G_s=Re Y_s and its own Bose occupation. Independent
noise sources therefore give the effective external occupation at the node

  n_eff(omega)=[G_c n(omega,Tc)+G_h n(omega,Th)]/(G_c+G_h).

This FDT relation is exact for the specified linear circuit. It describes
noise seen by an attached device; it does NOT count direct heat flowing
between the resistors independently of the device.

Let rho=fb/fa, D=Q(rho-1/rho)=13.26926875, t=1/(1+D^2)=0.00564737263.
At either center the wrong-temperature weight is

  v=t/(1+t)=1/(D^2+2)=0.00561565891.

The corresponding total load impedance is

  Z(fa)=49.4446712-i3.68440366 ohm,
  Z(fb)=49.4446712+i3.68440366 ohm.

Relative to a 50-ohm reference, |Gamma|^2=t/(4+5t)=0.00140194652;
the power mismatch is about 0.00609dB. ReY is 1.00564737 times its old value,
while ReZ is 0.988893424 times its old value. Thus even a center analysis
cannot keep the original rates exactly: the coupling-capacitor network
responds to complex impedance, not an independently adjustable loss number.
The actual frequency shift and linewidth change require that network.

The filter admittance also varies over the converter linewidth. For a
coupling-capacitor value Cg, the port contribution is governed by
Y_seen=[1/(i omega Cg)+Z_load]^-1. In a sufficiently weak capacitive limit,
its dissipative part is approximately (omega Cg)^2 Re Z_load; otherwise use
the full expression. Parasitic LC loss, its bath temperature and fabrication
of the stated high-impedance resonators remain UNKNOWN. No component loss
has been selected freely to force feasibility.

If one only inserts the center weights and artificially retains the source
rates, the cold-rate fractions become f_ac=0.954608967 and f_bc=0.005391033.
That incomplete center fixture gives +1.67660aW in the lower cold branch,
-0.156469aW in the upper cold branch, total +1.52013aW at C=1. This is NOT
total circuit cooling: it omits rate changes and the bypass below.

3. EXACT OFF-RESONANT HOT-COLD BYPASS

With the device port open, the two thermal branches form a closed series
loop. Their reactances cancel at

  f*=sqrt(fa fb)=4.890417160GHz.

The energy transmission probability between the two resistors is

  T_hc(f)=4R^2/|Z_c(f)+Z_h(f)|^2
         =4/[4+Q^2(f/fa-fa/f+f/fb-fb/f)^2].

Thus T_hc(f*)=1, even though the unwanted branch conductance is small at
each original mode center. The equivalent loop is a series RLC with

  R_loop=100 ohm,
  L_loop=65.9547069 nH,
  C_loop=16.0584142 fF,
  Q_loop=20.2661648,
  FWHM=241.309454MHz.

Its exact positive-frequency transmission area is

  integral_0^infinity T_hc(f) df = R/(2 L_loop)=379.048004MHz.

The exact thermal current for this declared circuit, using the physical
positive-frequency Planck occupations rather than a frozen Bose factor, is

  P_h->c=integral_0^infinity df h f T_hc(f)
                       [n(f,Th)-n(f,Tc)]
        =59.0614772aW.

The integration includes all frequencies. At f*, the thermal weighting is
h f*[n_h-n_c]=1.60680147e-25J. For a narrow leak around this frequency,
the original C=1 cold margin would permit at most about 11.1912MHz of
transmission area if every other extra cold load were absent. This is an
illustrative narrow-leak budget, not a frequency-independent exact bound.

The 59.0615aW result is exact for the open-device-port RLC model. It cannot
simply be subtracted from the four-bath fixture and called an exact full
refrigerator current: attaching a complex device admittance changes both
the bypass and the mode response. For an unpumped linear device termination
Y_D, the direct transmission becomes

  T_hc(omega)=4 G_c G_h/|Y_c+Y_h+Y_D|^2.

A reactive Y_D shifts or reshapes the resonance. A dissipative Y_D adds its
own bath noise and heat destination; it is not a free isolator. A pumped
frequency converter generally requires a multi-frequency admittance or
Floquet scattering calculation. None is supplied by the original linewidth
table. The open-port counterexample is sufficient to invalidate a claim of
thermal isolation based only on the two center noise weights.

For any redesigned diplexer the relevant criterion is the entire weighted
direct heat integral, plus pump-induced paths and component dissipation.
Narrow-band insertion loss alone does not certify it.

4. DISTINCT-PORT JRM ALTERNATIVE: WHAT IS STRUCTURALLY FIXED

The 2013 JRM is a different topology, not a replacement parameter set for
the 2017 FPJA. Signal and idler are different differential spatial modes.
Independent thermal lines can terminate them without joining the two
resistors at the common node of the failed RLC proposal. In an ideal
symmetric-ring mode model, the quadratic Hamiltonian is diagonal and the
leading allowed three-wave interaction has schematic form

  H_3=hbar g_3 x_s x_i x_p.

A coherent common-mode pump x_p(t) generates the desired bilinear coupling
between signal and idler; with zero coherent pump the leading linear
signal-idler cross coupling vanishes. This symmetry/model statement explains
why the external hot-cold series-loop resonance is not forced by this
topology. The experiment's pump-off conversion control is consistent with it.

The signal's incoming and outgoing waves share its own feed; the idler's
incoming and outgoing waves share another. They need not be separated with
circulators merely to connect thermal terminations, although any actual
measurement circulators and hybrid ports must have their own loss/noise
ledger. The common pump and signal are separated by hybrid parity, not by
an assumption that all physical conductors are unrelated.

This does not prove broadband zero heat leakage. Junction asymmetry,
parasitic direct capacitance, common-mode leakage, unused hybrid terminations,
higher modes, passive mounting/support conduction, conversion-mode pump
noise and signal-induced/pump-induced dissipation still need characterization.
A zero coherent pump mean also does not remove cubic scattering driven by
thermal pump fluctuations; eliminating the leading linear cross-admittance
does not eliminate every nonlinear reservoir-to-reservoir transition.
The source's coherent conversion and pump-off controls do not determine
these thermal currents or the absolute incident/absorbed/recovered pump
power. No refrigeration performance for this topology is computed by
borrowing the 2017 eta or kappa values.
