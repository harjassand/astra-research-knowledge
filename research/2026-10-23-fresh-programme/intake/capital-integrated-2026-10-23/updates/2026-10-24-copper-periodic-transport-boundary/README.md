## Construction and correction

The perturbation samples both concentration and normal flux on the displaced interface. The resulting boundary condition contains `D Czz = c_t`; omitting that term invalidates the earlier all-periodic Jensen argument. That proposed broad inequality is explicitly withdrawn in the preserved first-construction note. The later calculation keeps the full harmonic diffusion admittance, nonzero transverse modes, and the periodic base profile.

For sinusoidal Cu partial flux at fixed mean flux, the completed report gives an analytic small-amplitude coefficient `q0 < 0` along a limit with `D=L=1`, `b=0`, `r0,k → 0+`, and `omega=pi^2/2`. Continuity supplies finite positive `r0,k` examples with a negative local second-order change; `b` can be any fixed finite nonnegative capillarity coefficient. Small positive `Omega` continuation is local. This does not prove complete stabilization, identify a dominant branch outside that regime, or optimize a waveform. The compared protocols match modeled mean Cu charge and time per cycle, but exclude the charge, time and energy required to prepare their periodic base from a common initial state.

The same closure gives a strict modeled electrode driving-work excess for any nonconstant positive periodic flux at equal mean flux. That is a specified electrode contribution, not measured full-cell energy or total entropy production. It is a stability/work tradeoff, not free improvement.

## Physical and observational boundary

The referenced liquid-cell experiment concerns Cu growth on Pt-on-Ti in aqueous CuSO4/H2SO4. Its reported concentration is calculated, not measured. The displayed DC/pulse runs do not match retained Cu flux and time: the selected high-pulse case has six-fold lower mean nominal current and a different cell configuration. The model prescribes Cu partial flux and local kinetics, while a real bath controls total current; local Cu-specific flux, concentration and potential fields, transport coefficients, geometry and initial history are not acquired. Acid alone does not remove migration, speciation or convection. The model also omits moving-front/Stefan corrections, ionic and electrical coupling, and nucleation noise.

The source's image-front tracker is reported to clip or follow branches after about 2.2 s; the observed roughening transition is around 3–4 s. Those images do not provide a calibrated Cu-volume/current map. Prior AC/DC and Floquet electrodeposition work already owns the broad periodic-stability mechanism; the source comparison is excerpt-bounded and does not establish narrower priority. Physical usefulness, novelty and advantage remain `UNKNOWN`/`NOT_ESTABLISHED`.
