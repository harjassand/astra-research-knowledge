The deliberately conditional model

Let z increase from metal into liquid, 0<z<L, on a frozen mean film. D>0,
Omega>0, and r(t)>0 is Cu molar deposition flux per projected area. Base fields:

  C_t=D C_zz, D C_z(0,t)=r(t), C(L,t)=c_infinity,
  r(t)=r0+epsilon cos(omega t),
  c(t)=C(0,t)=c0-epsilon Re[Z exp(i omega t)],
  Z=tanh(L sqrt(i omega/D))/(D sqrt(i omega/D)),
  c_infinity=c0+r0 L/D.

The exact periodic fixed-film base has mean c=c0. Require epsilon<r0 and
epsilon |Z|<c0, so both flux and concentration stay positive. The mean Cu
charge and dilute-model retained Cu per cycle equal DC at the same duration:
Q_Cu/(projected area)=2F r0(2pi/omega), and mean flat advance is Omega r0.
No reverse, chemical etching, side reaction or capacitive charge is modeled.

For a NONZERO transverse Fourier displacement h(t) exp(ikx), use a fixed-z
concentration perturbation w and irreversible first-order local kinetics

  delta r=A(t)[w(0,t)+C_z(0,t)h]-B(t)k²h,
  A=r/c, B=b r, b>=0.

The concentration and its derivative must BOTH be sampled on the displaced
boundary. In particular D Czz=c_t in this fixed-domain base. Therefore

  w_t=D(w_zz-k²w), w(L,t)=0,
  D w_z(0,t)=A w(0,t)+[A r/D-B k²-c_t]h,
  h_t=Omega[A w(0,t)+(A r/D-B k²)h].                 (1)

The boundary signs and global-current condition were independently audited in
luna_hidden_state/PERIODIC_DN_ADVERSARIAL_AUDIT.md. A k!=0 current mode has
zero spatial integral and preserves global current to first order; setting its
LOCAL current perturbation to zero would be a different, incorrect constraint.
The graph area correction is quadratic in height slope. Uniform metal potential
does NOT, however, imply uniform liquid potential. Setting delta phi_liquid=0
at k!=0 is an additional high-conductivity/supporting-electrolyte approximation.

Exact leading-Omega boundary problem

Hold h=1 over one cycle, appropriate when the shape rate is small relative to
the forcing frequency, and define the positive diffusion admittance

  K_m=D sqrt(k²+i m omega/D)coth[L sqrt(k²+i m omega/D)].

The full harmonic Dirichlet-to-Neumann relation gives

  (K+A_convolution)w=F, F=c_t-A r/D+B k².

The mean growth divided by Omega is G=-K_0 w_0. This equality also follows
from mean reaction/normal-flux balance. Unlike a quasistatic replacement K_m=Dk,
the expression retains lateral diffusion phase at every temporal harmonic.
The omitted Czz term in the failed Jensen argument would have removed c_t.

For r=r0+epsilon Re exp(i omega t), write c=c0-epsilon Re[Z exp(i omega t)].
Set r=r0, c=c0 below and expand A=A0+epsilon Re[A1 exp(i omega t)]
+epsilon² A20+(second harmonic)+... . Then

  A0=r/c, A1=(c+r Z)/c²,
  A20=(Re Z/c²+r |Z|²/c³)/2,
  f0=b r k²-A0 r/D,             w0=f0/(K0+A0),
  f1=-i omega Z-(A0+r A1)/D+b k²,
  w1=(f1-A1 w0)/(K1+A0),
  f20=-(r A20+Re A1/2)/D,
  w20=[f20-A20 w0-Re(A1 conjugate(w1))/2]/(K0+A0).

Consequently G(epsilon)=G(0)+epsilon² q0+O(epsilon^4), q0=-K0 w20.
Odd powers vanish by shifting the driving waveform by half a period. The code
periodic_dn_check.py evaluates both this coefficient and the nonlinear periodic
Robin problem. Thirty-six spectral solves (16/32/64 temporal sidebands) agree
with the small-amplitude expansion and mean-flux identity. Some long finite-film
modes have q0<0; a shorter-mode control has q0>0. A reduced growth rate is not
the same as complete stabilization, compactness, or suppression of new nuclei.

Analytic negative sign, rather than a parameter-grid inference

Root's constructive handle takes D=L=1, b=0, c0=c>0, with r0->0+ and
k->0+ through NONZERO spatial modes. Put z=sqrt(i omega). Then

  Z=tanh(z)/z, K1=z coth(z), w1->-tanh²(z),
  q0->Re[sech²(z)]/(2c).

Choose omega=pi²/2, z=(pi/2)(1+i). Since cosh(z)=i sinh(pi/2),

               q0 -> -csch²(pi/2)/(2c) < 0.            (2)

The coefficient is continuous at this limiting positive-concentration point.
Hence finite r0>0 and k>0 exist with q0<0; choose epsilon sufficiently small
relative to r0 and c/|Z|. This proves a local counterexample to a blanket
DC-local-optimality claim WITHIN (1). The zero mode itself is excluded because
uniform galvanostatic potential feedback changes that mode. Small nonzero k
requires a correspondingly wide periodic transverse domain. The r0 limit is
not evidence for a practically useful absolute Cu flux. Interpreting a finite
point with an irreversible Tafel law also needs its reverse branch negligible;
small transport-scaled r0 alone does not supply that kinetic condition.

Why a small finite Omega interval also exists for this same model

At Omega=0, h is constant. Homogeneous w diffusion with w(L)=0 and
D w_z(0)=A(t)w(0), A>0, satisfies

  (1/2)d||w||²/dt=-D||w_z||²-Dk²||w||²-A(t)|w(0)|².

The one-cycle w map is strictly contractive. With the forced h component, the
full monodromy is triangular and its h eigenvalue 1 is simple and isolated.
Equivalently, the periodic equation for v=w/h has an invertible diffusion
linearization at Omega=0; for small Omega it includes

  v_t=D(v_zz-k²v)-Omega[A v(0)+A r/D-Bk²]v.

Smooth/analytic local dependence on Omega and epsilon follows from the
invertible periodic parabolic problem. Its morphological Floquet exponent has
lambda(Omega,epsilon)=Omega G(epsilon)+O(Omega²); its epsilon² coefficient
is Omega q0+O(Omega²). Thus q0<0 implies a negative local correction for a
finite positive Omega interval. This is a local continuation statement. It
does not identify a dominant branch outside that interval or restore omitted
mean-interface advection, Stefan corrections, ions, or electrical coupling.

Root's independent harmonic balance retains all h sidebands with

The matched work comparator

For a fixed concentration-INDEPENDENT standard potential reference, write the
one-way law r=K c exp(a U), a=alpha nF/(RT)>0. U may be chosen as the cathodic
driving potential E_standard-(phi_m-phi_s). The cathodic electrode driving-work
contribution nF rU equals (RT/alpha) r ln[r/(Kc)]. A fixed reference shift adds
a term linear in r, which cancels between equal-mean-flux protocols. Do not
silently call U the concentration-dependent Nernst overpotential: its rate
concentration powers and work expression need rederivation in that convention.

For f(r,c)=r ln(r/c),

  Hessian(f)=[[1/r,-1/c],[-1/c,r/c²]] >=0.

It is jointly convex. Periodic fixed-film conservation fixes mean c=c0 at
fixed mean r=r0, so Jensen gives <r ln[r/(Kc)]> >= r0 ln[r0/(Kc0)].
The sinusoidal excess is explicitly

  epsilon² |c0+r0 Z|²/(4 r0 c0²)+O(epsilon^4)>0.        (3)

Thus the same closure supplies a stability/driving-work TRADEOFF, not a free
waveform improvement. The expression is an electrode contribution with a
specified potential reference; it is not measured full-cell electrical energy,
Landauer heat, or total entropy production. Anode, solution-potential/ohmic,
capacitive and side-reaction work are not acquired. Positive ohmic convex losses
would be an additional cost under the appropriate electrical model.

closed_form_tradeoff_check.py verifies (2), continuity along finite positive
r0,k, a nonzero-mode positive-flux periodic example, and the cost expansion (3).
No kinetic K is experimentally inferred by that check.

Physical and priority stop gates

Ganesh, Pillai and Narayanan, J Eng Math132:16(2022), DOI
10.1007/s10665-021-10199-z, already study low-frequency small AC-on-DC
electrodeposition forcing by perturbation/Floquet analysis. Their indexed primary
manuscript identifies voltage forcing and an equivalent-RMS DC comparison.
This owns the broad AC/Floquet-stabilization explanation. The exact equation,
control and flux normalization comparison is still under primary-source audit;
our galvanostatic derivation alone does not establish narrower novelty.
https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10350049

The largest physical obstacle is that a real Cu bath controls total current,
whereas this model prescribes Cu partial flux and assumes a local derivative
A=r/c at fixed liquid potential. Schneider's rendered movies supply neither
local Cu-specific current nor a measured concentration/potential field. Acid
does not by itself eliminate migration/speciation/convection. The film thickness,
transport and kinetic coefficients, capillarity, local geometry, and initial
history are not jointly acquired for this closure. Exact stagnant-liquid mass
balance instead gives D partial_n C=r(1-Omega c_s), or requires an explicitly
consistent solvent velocity/relative-flux convention. Finite-Omega height
feedback in (1) leaves these independent corrections omitted.

Batched_mean's front operator is visually checked and stable only through
about 2.2s of M1 in a fixed pixel ROI; it begins to clip/follow branches near
ramification. Pixel distances are not a calibrated Cu volume/current map, and
the physical image-axis calibration is unresolved. A single unpaired movie
cannot discriminate this transport mechanism against reaction, surface state,
geometry, or stochastic nucleation at matched useful Cu flux. No further
unavailable-measurement protocol is proposed in this completed unit.

The inherited source set also contains additive-sensitive Cu/Cu morphology and
electrical-response studies with strong conventional DC/pulse/reversal controls.
Their existence prevents interpreting this result as generic pulse superiority.
The source-bounded observation and electrical/chemical gates are the next
decision boundary; no real-metal advantage is established by the exact algebra.
