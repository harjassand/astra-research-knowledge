A. GAMMA-ROOT COUNT FILTER

1. Direct forward-generator identity

For CIR drift kappa(m nu-s), diffusion variance2 kappa nu s, counts c s,
let k=m+n and g_n(s)=beta^k s^(k-1) exp(-beta s)/Gamma(k).
Directly differentiating gives the following coefficients in (L*-cs)g_n/g_n:
  s^-1: kappa nu n(k-1);
  s^0:  kappa[k-nu beta(m+2n)];
  s^1:  kappa nu beta^2-kappa beta-c.
Since partial_beta log g_n=k/beta-s, choosing
  beta'=kappa beta-kappa nu beta^2+c
leaves precisely n delta(g_(n-1)/g_n-1)-(m+n)a, where
delta=kappa nu beta, a=c/beta. Thus the acquired gamma-mixture evolution
has hidden deaths n->n-1 at n delta and observed births n->n+1 at (m+n)a.
This derivation checks the no-count killing coefficient, which must not be
lost during normalization.

2. No-count PGF and likelihood

The unnormalized equation
  W_t=[delta-(delta+a)z]W_z-ma W
is solved by
  W_t(z)=exp[-m integral a]P_0(eta+rho z),
  rho'=-(delta+a)rho, eta'=delta rho, rho0=1, eta0=0.
Indeed differentiating the affine argument reproduces both coefficients.
For q=eta+rho, q'=-a rho. The survival likelihood is
  Z=exp[-m integral a]P_0(q).
For a Poisson/Bernoulli factorization this implies exactly
  lambda_t=lambda0 rho,
  p_t=p0 rho/[1-p0+p0 q].
Consequently p'=-delta p-a p(1-p) and lambda'=-(delta+a)lambda.
The beta comparison at beta=1/nu gives beta>=1/nu for c>=0, so delta>=kappa.
This checks the pathwise damping rate used by the age argument, including
predictable time-dependent c.

3. Count interlacing and a corrected notation

The normalized count update is z[mP+zP']/(m+P'(1)).
For P=exp[lambda(z-1)]R, let
  Q=(m+lambda z)R+zR'.
If R=z^k R_tilde, then Q=z^k Q_tilde, and the secular identity is
  Q_tilde/R_tilde=m+k+lambda z+sum_i z/(z-r_i).
The earlier draft omitted the z^k factor in the left-hand side; this has
been sent to the author and corrected. It is a notational defect, not a
failure of the roots theorem.

For r_i<0, the derivative of the secular function is
  lambda+sum_i(-r_i)/(z-r_i)^2>0.
Pole signs prove interlacing. With lambda>0 there is one extra root left
of all the old negative roots; the new explicit z factor gives another
root at zero. With lambda=0 the leftmost extra root disappears. Repeated
poles have inherited multiplicities. Hence, after sorting Bernoulli parameters
descending, p_new[j+2]<=p_old[j]. At most two new ancestry tags per count
are sufficient, and old retained tags can be assigned without increasing p.
The tags are mathematical accounting, not hidden physical birth labels.

There is a convenient bounded-coordinate root operation. Let z=1-1/p.
For 0<p<1 the secular equation becomes
  F(p)=m+k+lambda-lambda/p
        +sum_i p_i(p-1)/(p-p_i)=0,
  F'(p)=lambda/p^2+sum_i p_i(1-p_i)/(p-p_i)^2>0.
After separating p_i=1 factors, its brackets are the old p_i and0,1.
Rational stored parameters allow exact rational sign checks and bisection.
Each root can be enclosed to absolute width eta in O(log(1/eta)) bisections;
small pole separations do not themselves demand extra accuracy if the whole
interval is already narrower than eta. Evaluating all secular sums still
costs arithmetic in the current number of factors. This does not certify
continuous event times or the rest of the ODE implementation.

4. Poisson replacement and the mean/nonexplosion issue

For a Bernoulli(p) versus Poisson(p), the only deficit of the Poisson mass
relative to Bernoulli occurs at1, so the exact TV is
  p-p exp(-p)=p(1-exp(-p))<=p^2.
Convolution and subsequent Gamma-mixture sampling are stochastic kernels,
so this bounds the represented-radius error. It preserves the index mean
exactly. Replacing several independent factors costs at most the sum p_i^2.

The main proof originally used mean preservation without first establishing
that the shadow-generated counting process is nonexplosive. There is a clean
closure property that supplies this missing justification.

For this factor class, V=Var(N)=lambda+sum p_i(1-p_i)<=mu=E N.
At a count,
  mu+ = mu+1+V/(m+mu) <= mu+2.
Between counts, mu'=-delta mu-aV<=-kappa mu.
Poisson replacement preserves mu. Starting with mu=0 gives pathwise
mu_t<=2Y_t. Therefore the count intensity is at most
  a(m+mu)<=c_max nu(m+2Y_t).
A linear immigration/Yule process dominates this counting process on every
finite horizon and is nonexplosive, with finite first moment. This argument
does not presuppose the desired stationary mean.

Now set q=(m+mu)/beta, the represented E[S|record]. Direct calculation gives
  dq=kappa(m nu-q)dt
       +(1+V/(m+mu))/beta [dY-a(m+mu)dt].
The jump coefficient is at most2nu, and resets leave q unchanged. With
the preceding integrability justification, expectation gives E_shadow q=m nu.
This proves E_shadow Y_T<=c_max m nu T and closes the expectation argument.

6. Infinite Hankel rank check

I independently checked the stationary CIR survival formula in the draft.
With gamma=sqrt(kappa^2+4kappa nu c), q=((gamma-kappa)/(gamma+kappa))^2,

B. THERMAL-ENVELOPE JET REPAIR

7. Infinite-dimensional order bound from charge bands

Band k connects (n,m+k) and(n+k,m), with coefficient
  sqrt(A_k(n)A_k(m))e_k(n,m),
  A_k(x)=(x+1)...(x+k).

the weighted band is an injective weighted shift. Its operator norm equals
the supremum of the weighted coefficient magnitudes. Consequently
  ||tau_R^-1/2 E tau_R^-1/2||
    <=sup diagonal_error
       +2sum_(k>0)sup weighted_band_error.
A finite supremum defines the bounded weighted operator first, so the
unbounded inverse square root causes no implicit domain step:
E=tau_R^1/2 B tau_R^1/2 is trace class and -delta tau_R<=E<=delta tau_R.

For a rectangular Taylor truncation through degrees<p in each geometric
parameter, use

If |q_i-q0_i|<=d_i and R_i=|q0_i|+d_i, then |T_i|<=R_i^n and the geometric
Taylor remainder is <=choose(n,p)d_i^p R_i^(n-p).
This proves the two-term remainder used in thermal_jet_merge.py.

The discrete supremum in that code is also correct algebraically:

For n>=max(1,p), every factor is decreasing, the ratio eventually falls

Actual floating values are diagnostics until directed rounding is added.

Required conditions: 0<=R_i<r_ref<1, nonzero expansion centers for the

8. Positive addition and normalization

Then A=tilde rho+D-rho is positive and

C. ANGULAR AGENT'S DIAGONAL SHIELD

9. The shield used by merge_geometric_sectors.py is valid

The Cauchy remainder gives each k-band entry bound
  |E_ij|<=rho^(n+m)H(n,m)sqrt(A_k(n)A_k(m)),
where H has nonnegative power coefficients. The code uses
  D_k(a,b)=rho^(a+b-k)H(a,b)[A_k(a)+A_k(b)], k>0.
At any Fock index(a,b), there are at most two k-neighbors. For one neighbor,
the old index pair is(a,b-k), for the other(a-k,b). Monotonicity of H and A
on nonnegative integers plus sqrt(AB)<=(A+B)/2 bounds each incident edge by
half the displayed D_k. Thus D_k dominates the absolute row sum.
For k=0 use the diagonal remainder bound itself. The standard quadratic-form
inequality then gives -D<=E<=D. This checks the possible factor-of-two risk.

A potentially tighter shield preserving the same polynomial-geometric class
can use weighted Cauchy on each edge:
  2sqrt(A(n)A(m))u|v_i||v_j|
     <=u[A(m)|v_i|^2+A(n)|v_j|^2].
After shifting to the endpoint indices, the falling-factorial prefactors
vanish below k, removing boundary indicators. This is optional; the current
code's broader monotone shield is already sound.

12. Coarsening and multivariate intensity quotient

For a stationary scalar CIR process, a single nonempty-bin observation has
density
  [Gamma(m,beta0)-a_t Gamma(m,beta_t)]/(1-a_t),
where a_t is the no-count probability and beta_t is the no-count conditional
rate. At common rate beta_t its index PGF is
  { [alpha/(1-(1-alpha)z)]^m-a_t }/(1-a_t),
  alpha=beta0/beta_t.
The constant weight is nonnegative: differentiating
log(a_t beta_t^m/beta0^m) gives m(kappa-kappa nu beta_t)<=0.
The PGF has an uncancelled pole at z=1/(1-alpha), whereas finite Poisson times
Bernoulli factors give an entire function. Thus these fine-count updates cannot
be used as if a bin revealed hidden count times. A new factor class or charged
approximation is required. This failure is observational, not numerical.

For W=(1/2)sum_ij G_ij I_i I_j with real symmetric G and I_j=|z_j|^2,
C_ij=G_ij z_i z_j. Therefore C_ij barz_i barz_j is real. The corrected
covariance annihilates the Hessian contraction on any smooth function phi(I),
including zero cross-variations between I_i and I_j. Without off-diagonal
hopping, the intensity generator is the sum of independent CIR generators.
Separate full mode-count records with known adapted c_j therefore admit
product conditional gamma filters; cross-record feedback changes known control
paths but not this fixed-record factorization. Interferometric ports and
hopping are outside this quotient. It supplies a legitimate interacting-phase
special case, while making the angular blind spot explicit.
