1. PHYSICAL AND OBSERVATIONAL SCOPE
===================================
Use the positive quartic diffusion retained in the frozen bridge. Its radius
S=|z|^2 obeys exactly

 dS = kappa(m nu-S) dt + sqrt(2 kappa nu S) dW.

Number-conserving quartic Hamiltonians, passive hopping, the permitted
clipped covariance correction, and record feedback do not change this
radial law. Suppose the observation interface exposes the AGGREGATE full
count times of a complete port frame, so lambda_total=c_t S. Here
0<=c_t<=c_max is any known predictable control; it can depend on the
aggregate record. Initial S is Gamma(shape m, rate 1/nu).

This is a legitimate controlled special case, with a different observational
interface from the frozen two-bin first-PLUS/MINUS witness. Discarding the
port label is part of this test's stated interface, not an assumed free
operation when predicting the richer interferometric record. The quotient
cannot predict that richer record from S. A conventional scalar CIR filter
has exactly the same information and may use every construction below.

2. EXACT ACQUIRED GAMMA-MIXTURE RECURSION (ESTABLISHED CAPITAL)
============================================================
Let g_n(s;beta) be the Gamma density with shape m+n and rate beta.
Use a probability generating function P(z)=sum_n w_n z^n to represent

 p(s)=sum_n w_n g_n(s;beta).

The common beta is known from controls and evolves, between and through
counts, as

 beta' = kappa beta-kappa nu beta^2+c_t, beta(0)=1/nu.

Hence beta>=1/nu. Define delta=kappa nu beta and a=c_t/beta.
Direct differentiation gives the killed-forward identity

 (L* - c_t s) g_n - beta' partial_beta g_n
   = n delta g_(n-1) - [n delta+(m+n)a] g_n.

At a count, s g_n=(m+n)g_(n+1)/beta. Consequently the index has an
equivalent hidden birth-death immigration observation model:

 unobserved death n -> n-1 at rate n delta;
 observed birth   n -> n+1 at rate (m+n)a.

The gamma mixture is updated solely from its known current parameters and
the actual aggregate count record. No hidden death or radial path is
revealed. Unnormalized no-count PGFs satisfy

 W' = [delta-(delta+a)z] partial_z W - ma W,

and an observed count maps

 P+ = z [mP+z partial_z P]/(m+P'(1)).

The exact finite-polynomial gamma recursion is already present in CIR
filtering literature. In particular Leijdekker–Spreij, arXiv:0802.1407,
derive an Nt+1-component gamma mixture after Nt counts. Pure-death and
birth-death dual filtering, including approximation/pruning, is also
established in Papaspiliopoulos–Ruggiero arXiv:1305.4571 and Kon Kam King
et al. arXiv:2310.00599. We do not claim their filter as new.

3. A POSITIVE NONLINEAR REPRESENTATION CLOSED UNDER EXACT UPDATES
===============================================================
Represent P as

 P(z)=exp[lambda(z-1)] R(z),
 R(z)=product_(j=1)^M (1-p_j+p_j z),
 lambda>=0, 0<=p_j<=1.

Thus N is the sum of an independent Poisson(lambda) variable and M
independent Bernoulli(p_j) variables. The conditional radius is a gamma
mixture of that N. These are computational latent variables, not extra
physical data. The stored state is beta, lambda and the finite p-vector.

The thermal initial state has beta=1/nu, lambda=0, M=0.

No-count update. Put A'=a, rho'=-(delta+a)rho, eta'=delta rho, with
A(0)=0,rho(0)=1,eta(0)=0 over each no-count interval. Then

 W_t(z)=exp(-mA_t) P_0(eta_t+rho_t z).

The exact no-count probability is Z=W_t(1), and the normalized parameters
are

 lambda_t=lambda_0 rho_t,
 p_(j,t)=p_(j,0)rho_t/[1-p_(j,0)+p_(j,0)(eta_t+rho_t)].

Equivalently

 lambda'=-(delta+a)lambda,
 p_j'=-delta p_j-a p_j(1-p_j) <= -kappa p_j.

The common multiplier exp(-mA) is essential for record likelihoods even
though it cancels from the normalized state.

Count update. Let mu=lambda+sum_j p_j. Then

 P+(z) = exp[lambda(z-1)] z Q(z)/(m+mu),
 Q(z)=(m+lambda z)R(z)+zR'(z).

Q has only real nonpositive zeros. To see this first remove any zero
roots R=z^k R_tilde; they remain zero roots and replace m by m+k in
the equation for the remaining roots. For negative roots r_i of R_tilde,

 Q_tilde(z)/R_tilde(z) = m+k+lambda z+sum_i z/(z-r_i),
 Q_tilde=Q/z^k.

Its derivative on each interval between poles is

 lambda+sum_i (-r_i)/(z-r_i)^2 >0,

with the usual limiting statement when there are no negative roots. The
limits at adjacent poles have opposite signs. There is a root between
each consecutive pair of poles and one between the last pole and 0.
If lambda>0 there is also one root to the left of all poles. If lambda=0
there is no such extra root. Multiplicities follow by continuity or by
factoring repeated roots before applying this argument.

Normalizing Q at 1 gives another Bernoulli-factor polynomial. Multiplication
by z adds a Bernoulli parameter 1. The degree increases by at most TWO
per observed count. No event requires knowledge of an unknown posterior.

Operational root finding is particularly simple: the displayed secular
function is monotone between known poles. Certified bisection, with
explicit endpoint brackets, can enclose every new root. There is no need
to invoke a numerically unverified generic polynomial root routine.
In implementation it is better to work directly in Bernoulli p in [0,1]:

 F(p)=m+k-lambda(1-p)/p+sum_i p_i(1-p)/(p_i-p),
 F'(p)=lambda/p^2+sum_i p_i(1-p_i)/(p_i-p)^2>0.

The old p_i are the poles and 0,1 supply the exterior endpoints. With
rational stored inputs, signs at rational midpoints are exact. An absolute
root interval of width eta takes O(log(1/eta)) bisections, independent of
root separation; an already narrower interval needs no bisection. Algebraic
multiplicities are factored first. Total root perturbation allowance is at
most (M+2)eta in density TV. Time propagation/likelihood precision still
requires its own accounting.

4. ROOT LIFETIMES: A STRUCTURAL MEMORY PROPERTY
=============================================
Sort old and new Bernoulli parameters in decreasing order. Interlacing
implies

 p_new[j+2] <= p_old[j]

whenever the indicated entries exist. Thus at a count at most TWO new
roots need fresh birth-time tags; each other new root can inherit the tag
of an old root with no increase in its p value. Any unmatched old tags
can be forgotten. Between counts, p decays at rate at least kappa.

Therefore every root tagged at time b obeys

 p_j(t) <= exp[-kappa(t-b)].

The tags are bookkeeping for the representation, not asserted physical
birth ancestry. They require only the stored old and new roots. This
inequality remains valid with adaptive c_t because beta>=1/nu pathwise.

5. POSITIVE MEAN-PRESERVING REPRESENTATION RESET
==============================================
Replace a Bernoulli(p) factor by a Poisson(p) factor: remove p from the
finite list and add it to lambda. This changes a KNOWN represented
density and is a computational approximation. It preserves normalization,
positivity and the conditional first moment mu exactly.

The elementary coupling bound is

 TV(Bernoulli(p),Poisson(p)) = p(1-exp(-p)) <= p^2.

Convolution and the gamma-mixture channel are contractions, hence the
same p^2 bound applies to the index law and represented radial density.
For simultaneous replacements, sum the p_j^2 bounds. Exact information
preserving repurification from the previous continuation remains valid;
this is a different approximate reset, with an explicit paid error.

Choose a threshold 0<tau<1 and let L=kappa^-1 log(1/tau). Remove each root
by the time its tag reaches age L, or sooner if its actual p<=tau. This
uses no new observation. Every removed root has p<=tau.

6. ENTIRE-RECORD ERROR AND EXPECTED MEMORY THEOREM
================================================
Fix a finite horizon T. Run the exact gamma-root update interspersed with
the preceding resets. Let tilde P denote the generated/shadow law of this
approximate filter. Couple the exact CIR record instrument and reset
injections by the usual branch-summed contraction/telescoping argument.
At time T, for the JOINT full aggregate count record and terminal radius,

 TV(P_exact, P_shadow) <= E_shadow sum_resets p_j^2.

No conditional stability theorem is invoked. This is an entire-record
bound; dividing by the probability of a selected rare record is not allowed.

Both laws use the SAME predictable control functional of their exposed
record. If the controller runs the approximate filter, that same algorithm
is used in the exact physical comparison. The memory of an arbitrary
external long-history controller is not included in the filter bound.

There are initially zero Bernoulli roots. Each count creates at most two,
and each removed root is removed only once. Therefore

 #removed_roots_by_T <= 2 Y_T,

where Y_T is the observed count total under the shadow law. There is no
circular stationarity assumption: for this PGF class Var(N)<=mu, a count
gives mu+=mu+1+Var(N)/(m+mu)<=mu+2, no-count evolution gives
mu'<=-kappa mu, and a reset preserves mu. Thus mu_t<=2Y_t from thermal
start, and count intensity a(m+mu)<=c_max nu(m+2Y) is dominated by a
nonexplosive linear immigration/Yule count process. Finite-horizon moments
exist. More precisely q=(m+mu)/beta satisfies the innovation equation

 dq=kappa(m nu-q)dt
    +[1+Var(N)/(m+mu)]/beta [dY-a(m+mu)dt].

The innovation coefficient is <=2nu, which justifies its mean equation.
The reset
preserves E[S|record]. All exact propagation pieces have the CIR first-
moment drift kappa(m nu-S), and counts averaged over their outcome have
no effect on that unconditional mean. Hence

 E_shadow S_t = m nu,
 E_shadow Y_T = E_shadow integral_0^T c_t S_t dt <= c_max m nu T.

This holds with adaptive c_t. It does not require the reset to preserve
the complete stationary Gamma marginal; generally it does not.

It follows that

 TV(joint record and terminal radius) <= 2 c_max m nu T tau^2.       (A)

At any time t, every retained tag was created by a count in (t-L,t].
Consequently the number M_t of stored Bernoulli roots obeys pathwise

 M_t <= 2 [Y_t-Y_(max(0,t-L))],

apart from a harmless endpoint convention. Thus

 E_shadow M_t <= (2 c_max m nu/kappa) log(1/tau).                  (B)

The same expected-memory inequality holds when the algorithm is driven
by the TRUE physical record: the tag bound is pathwise for every finite
record, and the true CIR law also has E S_t=m nu. Thus the algorithm's
actual online storage claim is not restricted to shadow-generated input.

For target 0<epsilon<2 c_max m nu T, choose

 tau=sqrt[epsilon/(2 c_max m nu T)].

Then the entire-record error is <=epsilon while

 E_shadow M_t <= (c_max m nu/kappa)
                 log[2 c_max m nu T/epsilon].                    (C)

This is logarithmic expected parameter count in the horizon, in place of
an exact gamma polynomial whose degree grows with cumulative counts. The
operation is an acquired online update, and the reset receives only its
own stored parameters. The bound is for the full law, not just a snapshot
of a supplied posterior. Adaptive nonlinear Hamiltonians cause no extra
radial memory, because the entire physical observation interface closes
on S. All constants are explicitly physical.

Control consequence. For any bounded payoff in [0,1] of the record,
implemented controls and terminal radius, the same-policy value error is
at most epsilon. The bound is uniform over the stated bounded predictable
control class. A policy whose shadow-model value is within delta_opt of
the shadow optimum therefore has true-model regret at most
2epsilon+delta_opt. This does not solve the optimization problem or bound
the memory of an arbitrary external policy. For count-record payoffs
transferred to the quantum experiment, add the retained bridge allowance
to the source-law approximation allowance; no angular final-state
information is thereby supplied by the scalar filter.

There is no claim of constant worst-case memory, nor of fixed global TV
over an infinite record at fixed tau. With fixed tau, expected state size
is uniform in time and the certified entire-record error grows linearly
with T. Rare count bursts can need many roots. Real parameters still cost
precision bits; tags require time precision and secular solves cost work.
The theorem bounds parameter count, not all arithmetic operations or
certified finite-bit implementation cost. An elementary high-probability
bound follows from Markov's inequality applied to the count window;
Section 13 gives a stronger tail bound under the actual thermal record.

7. FINITE PRECISION: AN EXPLICIT ROUTE, NOT A FREE ORACLE
======================================================
Bernoulli parameter perturbations have a coupling bound sum_j|p_j-p'_j|.
Poisson parameter perturbation costs at most |lambda-lambda'|. With a
common index law and a rate ratio r=beta'/beta, the gamma-channel bound is

 TV <= sqrt[(m+mu)(r-1-log r)/2],

by convexity of relative entropy and Pinsker. These give an explicit
state-injection allowance for certified root/rate approximations. They do
not alone certify all no-count likelihoods and continuous trigger times:
those must also be enclosed or simulated within a stated finite-output
interface. Numerical tests below are floating-point diagnostics, not a
new full finite-bit record theorem.

8. AN EXACT LOW-DIMENSIONAL SYSTEM WITH INFINITE LINEAR PREDICTIVE RANK
====================================================================
Even the scalar stationary CIR aggregate-count process has no exact finite
linear predictive-state representation for all count words, under c>0.
It suffices to examine equally spaced all-zero words. Let

 a(t)=E_stationary exp[-c integral_0^t S_s ds],
 gamma=sqrt(kappa^2+4 kappa nu c),
 q=((gamma-kappa)/(gamma+kappa))^2 in (0,1),
 Lambda=m(gamma-kappa)/2.

The standard affine CIR transform, or direct Riccati substitution, gives

 a(t)=[(1-q) exp[-(gamma-kappa)t/2]/(1-q exp(-gamma t))]^m
     =sum_(j>=0) w_j exp[-(Lambda+j gamma)t],

 w_j=(1-q)^m (m)_j q^j/j! >0, sum_j w_j=1.

For a sampling interval Delta>0, put x_j=exp[-(Lambda+j gamma)Delta].
Then a_n=a(n Delta)=sum_j w_j x_j^n has infinitely many distinct positive
support points. For every nonzero polynomial v of degree<=r,

 sum_(i,k=0)^r v_i v_k a_(i+k)
     =sum_j w_j [v(x_j)]^2 >0.

Every finite zero-word Hankel matrix is positive definite, so the full
process Hankel rank is infinite. This is not high-dimensional physical
storage; it is an exact linear-realization obstruction caused already by
a replenishing scalar hidden intensity. It does not rule out nonlinear
coordinates, approximate finite-rank models, or the root filter above.
It does refute the inference 'bounded information + scalar physical state
implies finite exact linear predictive rank'.

Truncating this particular zero-word expansion after j=R has a uniform
in-time error bounded by the negative-binomial tail sum_(j>R)w_j. That is
a useful exact benchmark but NOT a certificate for arbitrary count words.

9. THE LIKELY BLIND SPOT, AND WHY THIS IS NOT THE COVARIANCE SOLUTION
=================================================================
The aggregate sensor discards angle information. Interferometric ports
lambda_+/-=|z_1+/-z_2|^2/2 depend on relative phase. For equal-radius
preparations z=(a,a) and z=(a,-a), S is identical but the plus-port rates
are 2a^2 and 0. Future controls can likewise reveal variables that were
invisible to a current sensor. Compression must close under ALL permitted
future controls and observations, not merely match the current intensity.

Thus the gamma-root theorem passes an exact nonlinear radial test and
removes a concrete acquisition/memory obstruction there, but cannot be
advertised as an answer to the retained two-mode covariance witness.
The quantum bridge's covariance defect/tail allowance is untouched. A
port-sensitive extension must keep angular or coherence variables and
prove an analogue of real-rooted closure, damping and paid resets. Simple
radial reduction is precisely the blind spot that challenge exposes.

11. MULTIMODE INTENSITY COROLLARY AND A COARSENING FAILURE
=======================================================
For a diagonal Kerr Hamiltonian

 W(I)=1/2 sum_(i,j) G_ij I_i I_j, I_i=|z_i|^2, G real symmetric,

with no hopping, every I_i is an independent CIR process of shape 1.
The Hamiltonian drift is purely angular. The corrected covariance has
C_ij=G_ij z_i z_j, and its contraction with any Hessian of a function of
(I_1,...,I_m) vanishes: each term is proportional to the real part of
-i G_ij I_i I_j. Hence the quantum covariance correction also drops out
of the complete intensity-vector generator. This remains true for the
radial clip and for predictable choices of G.

If each mode's fine intensity-count record is exposed separately, the
conditional intensity density factorizes into the root filters above,
even if the known efficiencies c_i depend on the joint past record.
The likelihood factorizes once that record fixes the controls. Thus
entire-record errors add as 2nu T sum_i c_(i,max)tau_i^2, and expected
stored-root counts add as (2nu/kappa)sum_i c_(i,max)log(1/tau_i).
The interactions can be strong and nonlinear; their missing storage cost
is justified by the declared observational quotient. Interferometric
ports or hopping invalidate this product quotient.

The full count-time interface is essential to the exact root closure.
For a binary finite bin recording only whether at least one aggregate
count occurred, start at stationary Gamma(m,beta0), beta0=1/nu. Let beta_t
be the no-count Riccati rate and a_t the no-count probability. The terminal
conditional density for a nonempty bin is

 [g_0(s;beta0)-a_t g_0(s;beta_t)]/(1-a_t).

In the common beta_t gamma basis, its index PGF is

 P_yes(z)={ [alpha/(1-(1-alpha)z)]^m-a_t }/(1-a_t),
 alpha=beta0/beta_t in (0,1).

The coefficients are nonnegative (a_t<=alpha^m), but this PGF has a pole
at z=1/(1-alpha). It cannot equal a finite Bernoulli polynomial times a
Poisson exponential, which is entire. Thus hiding the count times breaks
this exact class already in one binary radial bin. One cannot process
unexposed finer counts and claim to have received only the coarser record.
The infinite Hankel-rank obstruction in Section 8 still applies to this
coarsened binary observation process, because its all-zero words are the
same no-count events.

13. TWO FURTHER ANALYTIC COROLLARIES
===================================
These extend the proof; the executed implementation uses the simpler
constant-tau finite-horizon rule above.

A. A physical-record memory tail, not just its expectation.

The autonomous CIR/aggregate-count joint law can be constructed with CIR
Brownian motion independent of a Poisson random measure. Since c_t<=c_max,
the exposed count process is a predictable thinning of the constant-rate
Cox envelope with intensity c_max S_t. Over a time window of length L,
write a=c_max nu L. Conditional on the radial path, that envelope's count
is Poisson with mean c_max integral S. Jensen's inequality in time and the
stationary Gamma moment-generating function yield

 E_true exp(theta Y_window)
 <= [1-a(exp(theta)-1)]^(-m),
 0<theta<log(1+1/a).

This is a negative-binomial MGF envelope, not an assertion that the actual
window count is negative binomial. Together with M<=2Y_window it supplies
an explicit Chernoff bound. For integer k>ma,

 P_true(M>=2k)
 <= [a(k+m)/(k(1+a))]^k [(k+m)/(m(1+a))]^m.

Also E_true M^2 <=4[ma+m(m+1)a^2]. Thus fixed-time arithmetic costs that
are quadratic in retained-root number have finite controlled moments.
This is not a per-count Palm bound or a complete bit-complexity theorem.
The thermal MGF is used only under the true physical source law; the
mean-preserving reset's shadow marginal is not assumed thermal.

B. An anytime global error budget with no supplied terminal horizon.

Choose epsilon>0 and t0>0 so epsilon<2 c_max m nu t0. Give each newly
tagged root born at time b its own threshold

 tau(b)^2 = [epsilon/(2 c_max m nu t0)] (1+b/t0)^(-2).

Its deadline is b+L(b), L(b)=kappa^-1 log(1/tau(b)). All inherited tags
keep their original threshold; at most two fresh tags are created per
count. Prune on that deadline or earlier when the actual p is below the
tag's threshold. Every removed root pays at most tau(b)^2. Therefore

 E_shadow sum_all_resets p_j^2
 <=2 E_shadow integral_0^infinity tau(b)^2 dY_b
 <=2 c_max m nu integral_0^infinity tau(b)^2 db
 =epsilon.

Every finite record horizon, jointly with its terminal radius, has TV
error at most epsilon. The count-record laws on the entire infinite time
axis also have TV at most epsilon by the increasing finite-history
sigma-fields. This does not assert closeness of the whole unobserved
continuous radial path, nor a uniform posterior bound on rare histories.

Since L(b) increases with b, any root still retained at time t was born
after t-L(t). Under either the true or shadow record,

 E M_t <= (2 c_max m nu/kappa)
 [1/2 log(2 c_max m nu t0/epsilon)+log(1+t/t0)].

This anytime algorithm has logarithmically growing expected parameter
count and a single global record-law error budget. Fixed tau instead
has time-uniform expected parameter count but only a finite-horizon
linear error bound. These statements must not be interchanged, and neither
establishes constant-memory accurate conditional filtering forever.

14. EXACT QUANTUM AGGREGATE-NUMBER COROLLARY
==========================================
The companion governing proof
EXACT_QUANTUM_AGGREGATE_POISSON_INTERTWINING.txt strengthens the physical
aggregate-count interpretation without changing the classical theorem above.
Let K be the physical total photon number, S the CIR variable, and J the
auxiliary gamma-mixture index (called N in earlier parts of this note).

For m bosonic modes with constant isotropic bath b=kappa nu/theta,
d=b+kappa, any well-defined [H_t,K]=0, and the complete aggregate monitored
loss instrument theta c_t sum_j a_j rho a_j^dagger, assume
0<=c_t<=c_max and theta c_t<=d. The record contains all aggregate count
times, and c_t is known from the common acquired history. Starting from
the supplied thermal total-number distribution, the physical aggregate
record together with terminal K is EXACTLY the CIR Cox record together
with a terminal Poisson(S_T/theta) draw.

The Poisson kernel intertwines both the no-count and the detected-count
unnormalized maps, not only the unconditional generators. Thus a proved
epsilon joint error for (record,S_T) transfers to (record,K) with the SAME
epsilon. The earlier recommendation to add the quartic bridge allowance
for this complete aggregate-count interface is conservative and unnecessary.
That allowance remains relevant to its broader port-sensitive use; the
angular covariance challenge has not been removed.

The conditional total-number PGF is directly available from the stored
gamma-root coordinates:
  w(z)=beta theta/(beta theta+1-z),
  F_K(z)=w(z)^m P_J(w(z)).
Each gamma component becomes a negative-binomial number distribution.
General number-conserving hopping and strong interactions are allowed in
this pooled quotient. They cannot steer K, precisely because [H_t,K]=0;
c_t changes readout efficiency. This does not establish general nonlinear
control, full-state compression, an acquired model, or a legal replacement
of first-port latch bins by their unobserved fine count times. All existing
precision, expected-memory and rare-history qualifications remain.
