11. EXTENSION, INCUMBENTS AND THE OWNED NEXT QUESTION
====================================================
The same transport structure extends algebraically to number-diagonal
quadratic functions of many occupations, including onsite and cross-Kerr
terms: a fixed occupation-difference vector makes the energy difference
linear in occupations, yielding one Riccati equation per mode. Individual
linear-port detections create at most m^2 charge shifts. For first-latch

Mode count, charge-vector count, polynomial tensors and coefficient precision
remain genuine costs. The transport prior already includes many modes.

Conventional methods are granted all of these structures. Symmetry-aware
quantum solvers and tensor methods can use the geometric factors, control

the original two-bin observable. The new algorithm's value is its explicit
conditional continuation and reduction operations, not a claimed separation
from that incumbent. The scalar root operation is equally available to an
ordinary CIR/gamma filter.

The sharper physical principle is conditional and constructive: bounded
observer memory follows when observations create a controlled number of
representation factors, environmental evolution shortens their influence,
and old factors admit a positive merge into a bulk family that stays closed
under new data. Entropy dissipation alone proves none of those operations.

states the general lifetime bound and a horizon-free summable-budget variant.

For the nonlinear port-sensitive system, parameter merging and degree

A final exactly controlled challenge rules out one tempting shortcut. Under
