## Mechanistic result A: Empty carrier is insufficient as a completion certificate
For upstream 3'-O-acylated DNA donor U–O–CO–P_n, both (1) productive aminoacyl transfer to an aminated downstream adaptor D and (2) hydrolysis to free chain P_n–COOH produce U–OH. A downstream Bst polymerase sensing only U–OH cannot distinguish the two. This is a formally exact observation under the listed reaction states; it does not assert the authors' physical apparatus has large hydrolysis. A similar false completion exists at PKS ACP–SH after productive translocation or failed hydrolysis. Product-qualified next-address state is needed, or acceptable hydrolysis leakage must be experimentally bounded. Chemical code alone cannot manufacture absent observation.

## Mechanistic result B: Ribosomal product custody preserves address for amide/ester step, not exotic motif completion
Let P carry chain P_n and A carry chosen monomer M_j encoded by the codon of the attached transcript T_j.
P_n–O–tRNA_P + H2N–M_j–O–tRNA_A -> tRNA_P–OH + P_n–C(O)–NH–M_j–O–tRNA_A.
Donor hydrolysis -> tRNA_P–OH + free P_n–COOH; leaves the incoming tRNA_A unchanged. Thus checking presence of the intact elongated product on A distinguishes these two chemical outcomes. The ribosome's ordinary elongation cycle structurally supports product custody and GTP-driven translocation, not an independent sensor that guarantees perfect product chemical identity. The same general mechanism can polymerize hydroxy acids via O-nucleophile to form ester links, if matched tRNA chemistry is used (Katoh 2026).
For Lee et al cyclic motifs, a second intramolecular hydrazine-to-ester condensation is REQUIRED after first peptidyl transfer. Thus even correct P->A transfer is NOT a certificate that the ring closed. The published same-system 22% ring / 78% linear example proves this limitation experimentally.

## New constructive attack: Chemical-state-negative proofreading of genotype-linked nonstandard products
Specific chemistry: initial program = distinct mRNA_j; reusable catalyst = shared ribosome and translation proteins; monomers = flexizyme-loaded diester/hydrazinoester tRNAs, already shown to generate programmed hydrazinedione ring. Produce genotype-linked products via established 3'-puromycin mRNA display. After translation, use selective hydrazone ligation (aldehyde) to mark and remove **linear, uncyclized** compounds bearing a free hydrazinyl N; properly cyclized ring has consumed this N and remains unlabelled. The 2025 Lee primary directly showed an uncyclized variant reacts with aromatic aldehyde. Adapt to DNA/RNA-compatible bioconjugation protocols, **but compatibility with intact mRNA fusions and false tagging other functionalities is unacquired**. Immobilized aldehyde scavenger is a speculative engineering realization of this known reaction, NOT an established successful selective purification.
It differs from PnG: destructive failed products can be culled after entire synthesis; intact correctly formed product is not hydrolyzed to edit a carrier. It does not make on-ribosome elongation contingent on cyclization; the potential advance is chemically quality-qualified genotype library generation and selection, rather than perfect autonomous step-level correction. Chemical instruction re-use uses recharged tRNAs / new mRNA molecules, not reusing puromycin-terminated mRNA as catalyst. mRNA-DNA-associated finished molecule is consumed as library object, ribosome is released/reused.

## Quantitative feasibility bound (no assumed rates)
If fraction p=0.22 of genotype-j products have the intended motif and 1-p=0.78 are wrong in the one measured peptide context, and a downstream chemical negative selection retains fraction t of correct molecules and w of wrong, final purity is q=pt/[pt+(1-p)w]. For q≥0.99 require w/t ≤ p(0.01)/(0.99(1-p)) = 0.002849. That is at least 351-fold relative discrimination, regardless of kinetics. If t=0.9, must remove ≥99.744% of incorrect molecules. Two such motifs in the same product independently would produce p²=0.0484 full correctness, and require ~1948-fold selectivity if one final filter. This independence is not measured. Other Lee monomer choices report far better ring fractions, and should be a stronger comparator: use high-cyclization substrates before paying for a filter. Thus chemistry may only be useful for new motifs where changing substrate sacrifices chemical functionality or substituent diversity.
Each library's genotype-to-chemical phenotype map is NOT deterministic when p<1. A functional selection could enrich an mRNA that only occasionally produces the functionally active chemical state; purification of reaction-qualified chemical phenotype before selection is essential when this causes costly false reproducibility.

## Cost and fairness
Compared to timed synthesis (best feedback, separate vessels and separation allowed), ribosome platform requires specially prepared nc-mononers, flexizyme-charged tRNA, GTP-powered elongation, aminoacyl activation and mRNA template; mRNA display also uses costly puromycin linker and product is tethered to nucleic acid. It is suited to high-diversity discovery not presently economical bulk production. Compare with 2026 Johansen-Leete genuine glycopeptide-display result; basic molecular barcoding/cyclic peptide selection ALREADY exists. The new chemistry would be construction of a displayable, structurally nonstandard multi-heterocyclic backbone library with product-state-negative proofreading; physical combination not demonstrated. 2025 Lee ng-scale protein yields and 22% ring example are major capacity limitations.

## Correction from full Lee 2025 double-ring experiment: code address is not freely assignable
Fig. 8 uses two prescribed diester charging addresses: monomer 1 charged to initiator tRNA(fMet)(CAU) decoded at AUG, and monomer 12 charged to tRNA(Pro1E2)(GUC) at a reassigned internal Asp GAC codon, with hydrazine on a separate tRNA(Pro1E2)(ACG) decoded at Arg CGU. Two ring motifs in one ribosomal product were demonstrated. **Mixing two different diesters charged to the same tRNA species or decoded by the same codon does not provide individually selectable instructions**. To realize different chemical commands for two programs in one bath, reassign distinct codons to distinct charged tRNA body/anticodon pairs, or use separated reactors. Moreover, Lee's two-ring Figure 8 controls monomer placement, not arbitrary dynamic program rewrites, proofread ring closure, or genotype-product linkage by mRNA-display puromycin. Lee 2025 22% cyclic is from a particular 6-ring substrate '5', NOT the Figure 8 multiple-ring substrate mixture. Avoid applying the 22% measurement numerically to Fig. 8.

One strong construction is to retain their established AUG + GAC + CGU anticodon assignments for *two chemically different monomer types* in one RNA translation and then add a 3'-puromycin display stage. This implements different placement under codon program but does not yet show per-chain independent late-choice of monomers at the same codon. To achieve that additional operation, a genuinely distinguishable decoding register is needed. A common feed global tRNA charge is not sufficient.

## Exact quantitative library coverage requirement
For a *hypothetical* per-genotype correct ring fraction p and downstream correct-retention t, with N independent successful molecular translation trials per genotype and rare event probability pt, P(at least one right, retained) = 1-(1-pt)^N (fixed N); 95% coverage requires N ≥ ln(0.05)/ln(1-pt). For p=0.22, t=0.9, N≥14 (since 1-.198=.802; ln(.05)/ln(.802)≈13.58). Under Poisson translation count per genotype mean λ instead, coverage is 1-exp(-λpt); λ≥15.13. These are sampling requirements, not data on real mRNA-display throughput or genotype library population distributions. If 22% is due to abortive peptide truncation rather than accessible linear motif, aldehyde capture cannot classify that part; verify target chemical mass.
