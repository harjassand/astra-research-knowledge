# One-shot oriented polymer contact interface — 24 October 2026

This packet preserves a prospective interfacial chemistry and its bounded kinetic audit. It is a hypothesis about a one-time repair at an intended joint, not an acquired material capability or reusable repair process. The candidate, audit and unexecuted checker are pinned in the [source manifest](source_manifest.json).

## Proposed interface

The proposed sequence places tethered furan plus photocaged thiol on one face and tethered maleimide on the other. Initial furan–maleimide Diels–Alder bridges form while thiol is masked. Illumination after assembly is intended to unmask thiol while the maleimide remains Diels–Alder-masked. After damage separates the faces, recontact would let thiol capture exposed maleimide; a later hydrolytic lock is a separate, unassumed step. The design is one-shot and requires a registered joint.

The source's closest strong-host example is fresh multimaleimide/solvent repair of a furan-functionalized epoxy–amine, reported near 90% initial-strength recovery at 20 days. That does not establish this oriented interface. Existing furan–maleimide healing, latent retro-Diels–Alder-to-thiol chemistry and anthracene–maleimide mechano-capture are incumbents. The supplied examples of photocaging do not establish compatibility in this host, light orthogonality, grafting or retention of the intended groups.

## Kinetics and material gate

The audit uses a transient (D\to U\to L) model with reopening, furan and thiol-capture rates. It gives branch probabilities and storage/recontact expressions, but has no fitted rates. The accompanying checker uses illustrative values and is preserved without execution; its output is not material evidence.

The proposal remains gated on same-host measurements of cage retention, functional-group balance before and after illumination, intact aging, free-maleimide yield after fracture versus intact high-load controls, and separated-face versus recontacted-face behavior. A later mode-I recovery and creep comparison would need fresh multimaleimide, conventional two-part and thermal-repair incumbents. No compatibility, kinetics, strength recovery, durability, experiment or advantage is established.
