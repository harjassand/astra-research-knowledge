# Primary-source boundary for the binary trace-entropy transfer

25 October2026. The external governing theorem is Frenkel2023, arXiv2208.12194v4,
Theorem6, https://arxiv.org/html/2208.12194 . It gives Umegaki relative entropy via
signed-pencil negative traces, including unequal traces. The new receiving
construction forms the positive second-derivative measure of a binary testing
curve and applies that theorem to all positive mixtures. No physical measurement
or decoder equivalence follows. Historical priority of this direct consequence
has not been established by the bounded search.

Wang–Gohari2026, arXiv2610.29576v1, https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29576v1 ,
Proposition5 already proves scalar/matrix equality for doubly symmetric binary
sources throughout its matrix-entropy class. The present Umegaki result covers
arbitrary binary-input finite-output channels at their fixed priors, by the exact
two-source transfer. It does NOT resolve that paper's general multi-symbol
matrix-entropy questions or manufacture a t log t SDPI separation from the
three-source trine trace-entropy counterexample.

The earlier local file classical_channel_qubit_memory_proof.md proves a different
statement: arbitrary transmitted alphabet but at-most-qubit reference and a
GLOBAL classical coefficient. The new result instead fixes a binary transmitted
alphabet and permits arbitrary quantum reference at a FIXED prior. Neither is
silently substituted for the other or for the full9-symbol Hesse problem.
