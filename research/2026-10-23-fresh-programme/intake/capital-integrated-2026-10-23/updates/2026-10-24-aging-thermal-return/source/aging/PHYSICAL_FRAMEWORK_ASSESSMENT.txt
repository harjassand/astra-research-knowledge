MATERIAL TIME, HIDDEN THERMAL MEMORY, AND WHAT AN ECHO WOULD MEAN
Lead checkpoint, 23--24 October 2026

The evidence supports separating kinetic timing, structural memory, and the
scattering observation map. It does not currently select a new microscopic
mechanism for Vit4 or establish that its stress field is one scalar. The most
substantive result of this unit is a sharp conditional return/control distinction
inside conventional distributed relaxation: one common clock can coexist with
arbitrarily many visible memory modes and cannot generally refocus them by a
simple returned-temperature or returned-mean protocol. This is conventional
mathematics applied to a physical observation problem, not a claimed new theory.

3. Two physically distinct representations to compare

A. Low-rank frozen displacement family. Suppose u_j(t)=U_j z(t), with the
entire spatial coupling field fixed and z independently calibrated. Full return
of that field is an endpoint property. For tagged-particle scattering its
characteristic factor depends on Delta z. For coherent XPCS the correct
prediction is the collective pair/four-point map, not an automatic product with
a baseline. Full configuration, weights, beam and apparatus return is sufficient
for intensity return. A returned mean FSDP or volume is not that premise.

B. Distributed anelastic memory. Let x_r'=-r(x_r-theta) in one common material
time, with a field u=A x and mean proxies y=B x. Each visible preferred-volume
mode relaxes at its own rate. A returned bath or y can leave A x nonzero. This
representation is compatible with passive linear relaxation and ordinary TN
memory; a separate irreversible-event channel is unnecessary to obtain a missing
echo. Nonseparable Arrhenius rates, evolving couplings, and plastic events are
further alternatives, not implicitly excluded by B.

THERMAL_RETURN_CONTROL_THEOREM.txt gives B's precise consequences. For a finite
equilibrated-reference protocol, returning N distinct positive-rate modes needs
at least N sign changes of the scalar thermal forcing. N+1 equal-material-time
rectangular pulses attain this bound via a polynomial with those modal roots.
For a continuous visible rate support with a finite accumulation point, exact
full return after a nonzero finite integrable input is impossible by transform
uniqueness. These are standard Chebyshev/ensemble-control consequences. They
require calibrated visibility and do not assert that real glasses have acquired
continuous spectra. Finite measurements can at most establish a finite visible
rank under model/error assumptions.

The constructive protocol charges initial amplitude, later dynamic range, total
variation, rate uncertainty and material-time width acquisition. Smooth finite-N
controls exist too; the distinction is not an artifact of instantaneous jumps.
An exact small example returns a mean after two pulses while leaving opposite
mode residuals; the subsequent mean develops a nonzero relaxation hump. That is
a quantitative conventional-memory competitor to a scalar endpoint account.

Classical Muntz theory distinguishes unbounded discrete infinite spectra: a
divergent sum of reciprocal distinct rates forces zero exact-return forcing;
a convergent sum permits exact function-space controls. Such nonzero controls
must have infinitely many sign changes, even if smoothed. Under a separately
specified minimum resolved lobe amplitude a and slew bound v, N-mode return
requires laboratory duration at least 2a(N+1)/v. The resolution promise is
essential; this is control complexity, not a heat or dissipation bound.

For a Gaussian multimode field, a calibrated conditional collective prediction
is possible without inventing a self factor. Write u_j(t)=sum_a U_ja x_a(t)
and define K_jk(t,s)=q^T Cov[u_j(t),u_k(s)]q. Every two-time coherent pair has
attenuation exp[-(K_jj(t,t)+K_kk(s,s)-2K_jk(t,s))/2]. The intensity four-index
expansion uses the same space-time covariance. This extends root's exact map;
it does not acquire its covariance, initial four-point structure or mode-field
couplings from one mean curve. Those remain calibration requirements.

Cumulative independent-event dose is a useful additional null only when the
events leave nonreturning increments with a frozen observation law. It predicts
memory loss along a closed external path. It is not a general synonym for
plasticity: Keim--Arratia directly observe hysteretic, dissipative rearrangements
that return at cycle end in a jammed interfacial material. Thus an echo does not
prove purely elastic motion or zero heat. The material and drive differ from a
thermal metallic glass; the experiment is a logical counterexample, not its
mechanism transfer.
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/1308.6806v2

4. What a positive clock can erase and what survives

A time change can absorb an arbitrary one-channel drift or a probe-induced
component along a fixed trajectory if it is refitted after the intervention.
It cannot remove an independently measured response component transverse to
that trajectory. LEAD_CLOCK_AND_OBSERVATION_PROOF.txt derives the invariant
v_i R_j-v_j R_i and distinguishes clock-only action from the much broader TN
history formalism. The physical FDR ratio is unchanged by a frozen source-time
Jacobian; retiming alone does not repair a measured FDT failure.

The number/order of forcing sign changes, closed path geometry, and physical
work integrals along that path survive an increasing reparameterization. The
sign-change return obstruction is consequently independent of the clock's
speed, once the model and finite material duration hold. A single relaxation
curve, however, can be made exponential by fitting a new clock. Its apparent
number of exponential modes is not an invariant without a separately frozen
common clock and compatible observation/control model. Do not use one fitted
master curve as proof that N modes were acquired.

6. Falsifiable multi-protocol operation and current status

The smallest scientifically meaningful positive program is to freeze a competent
TN/anelastic model, an independently measured common clock/control history, and
the actual observation map on calibration protocols. Then choose held-out
thermal protocols with distinct sign-alternation counts. Record both endpoint
mean and its full accessible recovery tail, together with registered coherent
intensity and local deformation controls. Predict residual channels with the
finite-error bounds; do not infer a field return from a scalar mean. Compare
the same-data conventional TNM/KAHR or mode-spectrum predictor and any claimed
low-rank-field predictor using the same physical assumptions and charged
calibration. A richer model's better fit does not itself identify its mechanism.

If a nontrivial protocol with fewer than N sign changes genuinely returns N
calibrated visible modes, the frozen single-input model is falsified. If only
the mean returns and the tail does not, distributed memory already explains the
logical failure of scalar sufficiency. If a registered intensity recurrence
exceeds a frozen strictly decreasing common-clock master prediction, that narrow
clock null fails without needing a hidden z. None of those outcomes alone
identifies plasticity, equilibrium, total dissipation, or a universal material
clock. The currently public data do not close the positive field-acquisition gate.
