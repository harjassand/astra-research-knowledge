For continuously supported visible rates with a finite accumulation point, exact full return after a nonzero finite integrable input is impossible; the discrete-spectrum alternative is governed by the stated Müntz condition. A visible mean proxy can return while the distributed internal field does not. These are source-reported control consequences with their stated spectral and protocol assumptions, not evidence of a glass-specific mechanism.
