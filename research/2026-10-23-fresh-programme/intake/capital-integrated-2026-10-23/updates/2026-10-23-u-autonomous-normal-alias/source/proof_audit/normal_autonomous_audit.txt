INDEPENDENT AUDIT: NORMAL AUTONOMOUS FLAT-MODE CEILING
2026-10-23. Includes the bounded edge-affinity refinement.

VERDICT

Both claims are valid, and both constants are sharp as dimension-uniform
statements. A directed four-cycle attains alpha=exp(-pi). A biased four-cycle
attains alpha=exp[-pi/tanh(ell/2)] for finite positive ell. The exact scope is
one autonomous real normal Markov generator, uniform stationary reference,
and an exactly returned FLAT logical mode. This is not a result about arbitrary
nonnormal generators, time-varying normal generators, or all spectral modes.
No novelty or thermodynamic-energy statement is made.

1. CONVENTIONS AND PRECISE THEOREM

Column probabilities satisfy p'=Qp. Q is one fixed finite real matrix with
off-diagonal entries Q_ij>=0 and 1^T Q=0. Assume balance Q1=0 and normality
QQ^T=Q^TQ. Write

    S=(Q+Q^T)/2, L=-S, A=(Q-Q^T)/2, Q=-L+A.

Then L is a symmetric positive semidefinite graph Laplacian, A is skew
symmetric, L1=A1=0, and normality is equivalent to [L,A]=0. Balance makes both
Q and Q^T row-conservative Markov generators, as well as column-conservative.
In fact, in the finite real setting normality plus 1^TQ=0 already implies
Q1=0: normality gives ||Qx||=||Q^Tx||, and apply it to x=1. Keeping balance
explicit makes the orientation and physical reference transparent.

Let T>0, z_i in {+1,-1} for every i, z!=0, and assume

    exp(QT) z = -alpha z, alpha>0.

Then alpha<=exp(-pi). The equation itself forces sum_i z_i=0 by mass
conservation, so the flat logical partition is balanced. Finite Q makes its
exponential invertible, so alpha=0 is not an exact returned eigenmode.

If additionally, on every active unordered edge, both rates are positive and

    |log(Q_ij/Q_ji)| <= ell < infinity,

put rho=tanh(ell/2). For ell>0,

    alpha <= exp(-pi/rho).

For ell=0 the generator is symmetric and no negative returned mode with
alpha>0 exists. Interpreting the right side as zero is a useful limit, not an
assertion that a finite-time zero eigenvalue can occur. A one-way edge has
infinite affinity and belongs only to the unrestricted rho=1 ceiling.

2. NORMAL SPECTRAL STEP AND ALL FREQUENCY ALIASES

Since Q is real normal, it has an orthonormal complex eigenbasis. The commuting
symmetric/skew decomposition has eigenvalues -lambda_j+i omega_j, where
lambda_j>=0. On that same eigenvector L has eigenvalue lambda_j and A has
eigenvalue i omega_j. Expand z=sum_j c_j v_j. Exact return means, for each
c_j!=0,

    exp(-lambda_j T) exp(i omega_j T) = -alpha.

Thus lambda_j=a=-log(alpha)/T and omega_j T is an ODD multiple of pi. This
proves, without selecting a principal logarithm or dropping aliases,

    Lz=az,
    ||Az||_2^2=sum_j |c_j|^2 omega_j^2
               >= (pi/T)^2 ||z||_2^2.

The constant a is nonnegative because L is positive semidefinite. In fact
the later comparison makes a strictly positive for a negative mode.

An equivalent geometric interpretation is exp(AT)z=-z: skew evolution must
travel between antipodal vectors on its sphere. Its speed is ||Az||, so even
the shortest allowed rotation has angular distance pi. Neither interpretation
assumes a single oscillatory frequency; superpositions and aliases are covered.

3. FLAT MAXIMUM PRINCIPLE; NO ROW/COLUMN ERROR

For a row-conservative Markov matrix Q and a flat z,

    (Qz)_i=sum_{j!=i}Q_ij(z_j-z_i),
    z_i(Qz)_i<=0.

The same statement holds for Q^T because balance gives Q^T1=0. Using Lz=az,

    z_i(Qz)_i=-a+z_i(Az)_i<=0,
    z_i(Q^Tz)_i=-a-z_i(Az)_i<=0.

Together these imply |(Az)_i|<=a for EVERY coordinate, hence

    ||Az||_2 <= a ||z||_2.

Combine this with the spectral lower bound: pi/T<=a. Therefore
alpha=exp(-aT)<=exp(-pi). No unjustified contraction of a column-stochastic
map in linfinity is used; the row maximum principle is available precisely
because Q and its transpose are both Markov in the balanced convention.

4. EDGE-AFFINITY REFINEMENT

For a positive reciprocal pair of rates u=Q_ij,v=Q_ji,

    |A_ij|/S_ij = |u-v|/(u+v)
                = tanh(|log(u/v)|/2) <= rho.

For inactive pairs u=v=0, |A_ij|<=rho S_ij also holds. Since A1=0, a flat
z gives

    (Az)_i=-2 z_i sum_{j:z_j=-z_i} A_ij.

Since Lz=az and L=-S is a Laplacian,

    a=2 sum_{j:z_j=-z_i} S_ij

at every vertex i. Thus

    |(Az)_i| <= 2 sum_cross |A_ij|
              <= 2rho sum_cross S_ij = rho a.

It follows that ||Az||<=rho a||z||. The SAME alias-safe spectral comparison
now gives pi/T<=rho a, and alpha<=exp(-pi/rho). The proof bounds only the
exact flat returned mode; it is not a global eigenvalue-sector inequality
for every mode or a proof of a general cycle-affinity spectral conjecture.

5. SHARP FOUR-STATE WITNESSES

Let C be the permutation for a directed cycle of length four, acting on
columns. Choose z=(1,1,-1,-1). Directly,

    C^2 z=-z,  C^T z=-Cz.

For rates k_plus,k_minus>=0 with s=k_plus+k_minus>0 and
d=k_plus-k_minus!=0, define

    Q=k_plus C+k_minus C^T-s I.

Q is a real circulant normal irreducible balanced Markov generator (including
the one-way case). On span{z,Cz}, L=s I and A=d C. Consequently

    exp(QT)z = exp(-sT)[cos(dT)z+sin(dT)Cz].

At T=pi/|d| this is -exp(-pi*s/|d|)z. For k_minus=0, s=|d| and the unrestricted
bound exp(-pi) is attained. For both rates positive,

    rho=|d|/s=tanh(|log(k_plus/k_minus)|/2),

and the affinity bound is attained. Reverse circulation changes d's sign,
not the result. Thus the constants cannot be improved uniformly over all
dimensions. This does not assert attainability in every fixed dimension.

With A states 1,2 and B states 3,4, uniform memory has N=2. Since this Q is
balanced, exact mode return is equivalent to a symmetric bit channel with
uniform product memory return. Its flip probability is (1+alpha)/2. The
unrestricted sharp ceiling is approximately 0.521606959, not 1/2.

normal_autonomous_check.py verifies the Markov, normality, flat-mode, skew
rotation, and affinity-saturation identities by exact Fraction matrix
arithmetic for four choices of rational rates. Exponentials shown as decimal
numbers in normal_autonomous_results.json are diagnostics. Sharpness and the
universal theorem follow from the analytic identities above.

6. SCOPE WARNINGS THAT AFFECT THE RESULT

* Q must be ONE FIXED generator. A sequence of symmetric reciprocal-contact
  generators is normal at every instant but can approach the much stronger
  ThermalRecall flip with memory return. Instantaneous normality of a
  time-varying generator is insufficient unless the additional evolution
  structure restores an appropriate commuting effective generator.
* Normality of the final stochastic propagator alone is insufficient. In the
  reciprocal-control closure, put a block-uniformizer U both before and after
  a ThermalRecall sweep P. Then UPU acts as the symmetric bit channel on the
  two-dimensional block span and vanishes on its orthogonal complement; it
  is symmetric/normal but can have negative eigenvalue -(1-2e_N), far larger
  than exp(-pi). It is not thereby the exponential of one normal Markov Q.
* Flat z is essential to the coordinate maximum principle and the uniform
  affinity refinement. A claim for an arbitrary nonflat real eigenvector
  cannot be inferred by this argument. A nonuniform stationary reference
  would require a separately justified weighted version.
* Exact returned mode is essential. An approximate return statement needs
  quantitative stability, especially near nonnormality or frequency aliases;
  no such robustness theorem is established here.
* Nonnormal autonomous generators remain outside this proof. Nothing here
  rules out or supplies a nonnormal escape. Time, energy, and affinity are
  different resources; the sharp witnesses are model constructions, not
  acquired physical demonstrations or priority claims.
