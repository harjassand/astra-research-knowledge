Problem choice

Learn a growing sequence of NEW sign-reversible Hamiltonian coupling strengths
in one stationary, initially uncalibrated Markovian noise environment. Source
experience has to acquire an actual error-correcting experimental interface;
it is not given a noise axis or code. The concrete model is one sensor qubit,
one noiseless logical-tag ancilla, trusted fast controls and parity measurement:
 d rho/dt=-i[theta h.sigma,rho]+gamma[(n.sigma)rho(n.sigma)-rho].
Unknown fixed unit n; gamma known for this opening. Each new unknown theta has
a known controllable signal sign s=+/-1. This models calibration of reversible
couplings/drives, not an arbitrary static field whose sign cannot be reversed.
The procedure may choose the sensed axis h after acquiring noise geometry.
The supplied single-Pauli Lindblad class, sign control, noiseless ancilla and
trusted operations are real promises; their physics is not learned from finite
data. Every preparation, source wait, readout, syndrome measurement, recovery,
echo pulse, and target interrogation must be charged separately.

Candidate operation: learned correction axis plus paired signal reversal

Source trials turn the unknown coupling OFF. Prepare each of three Pauli states,
wait tau, and measure the three Pauli observables (nine setting pairs). The
Bloch response is M=q I+(1-q)nn^T, q=exp(-2gamma tau). Binomial +/-1 outcomes,
not an exact matrix oracle, estimate M and acquire an axis e with a confidence
radius sin angle(e,n)<=s. Fixed settings can be reused at doubling sample counts.
No latent simulator seed, n, or exact channel response is a learner input.

Choose h perpendicular to the acquired e, f=h cross e, and encode
 |0L>=|+h>|0a>, |1L>=|-h>|1a>.
Measure code parity P/Q after every interval Delta; on Q apply E=e.sigma to
the sensor. The exact recovery is R(X)=PXP+E QXQ E. It is a physical instrument,
not a fitted regression map. A logical X is E on the sensor AND X on the tag
ancilla; it preserves code parity and commutes with this recovery.

For each target, use the pair
 evolve(+theta h,Delta) -> recover -> X_L ->
 evolve(-theta h,Delta) -> recover -> X_L.
The sign reversal is an explicit extra interface requirement. A programme that
silently obtains it for a nonreversible unknown field would be invalid.

Exact forward map and the proposed substantive distinction

In coordinates e=x,f=y,h=z, set n=(b,c,a). Sensor Bloch evolution is
 G_theta=2theta cross(h)+2gamma(nn^T-I), M_theta=exp(Delta G_theta).
The recovered logical channel preserves populations and multiplies coherence by
 eta_theta(n)=M_theta,xx+i M_theta,xy.
An unpaired recovery has a noise-induced phase linear in c:
 eta_0=1-2p(a^2+c^2)+2i pbc, p=(1-exp(-2gamma Delta))/2.
Thus imperfect acquired correction is NOT automatically only quadratic residual
dephasing; it can bias the unknown Hamiltonian being learned at first order.

The paired operation instead has EXACT coherence factor
 F_theta(n)=eta_theta(n) conjugate[eta_-theta(n)].
It has zero phase offset at theta=0 for every n and every finite Delta.
Reflection of a leaves eta unchanged; reflection of c conjugates eta while
reversing theta. Hence F_theta is even separately in a and c. Near n=e, both
its phase and attenuation errors are therefore quadratic in axis error. This
is the central proposed operation, not the initial axis-estimation routine.

Provisional uniform finite-cycle bound derived by the lead

Write s^2=1-(n.e)^2=a^2+c^2 and choose n's immaterial sign so b>=0. Both
Bloch semigroups are contractions. Split their generator difference into its
linear term 2gamma(e d^T+d e^T), d=a h+c f, plus a remainder of norm at most
2sqrt(2)gamma s^2. Duhamel expansion gives each single-cycle first-order
remainder <=(2sqrt(2)gamma Delta+2gamma^2 Delta^2)s^2. The pair's first-order
terms cancel by the reflection symmetry, so

 |F_theta(n)-F_theta(e)| <=
       [4sqrt(2)gamma Delta+8gamma^2 Delta^2] s^2.       (A)

This bound is independent of theta because the Hamiltonian part is skew and
the Bloch propagators contract. Telescoping N paired cycles, total target time
T=2N Delta, gives channel-coherence discrepancy at most
       T[2sqrt(2)gamma+4gamma^2 Delta]s^2.              (B)
This could transfer any ideal-aligned NEW-parameter learning protocol to the
actual apparatus with a known total data-law error budget, not merely bound
one plug-in point estimate. Adaptive protocol/diamond-norm factors need audit.
The ideal-aligned finite-Delta reference itself is NOT ideal noiseless Ramsey:
finite recovery cadence produces its own gain and attenuation and must be
modeled or certified. Instantaneous/free infinitely frequent recovery is not
a permitted practical-cost conclusion.

Why source experience could enable later learning

Finite binomial source calibration has s^2=O(1/source_trials) under a fixed
nonzero channel gap. A naive unpaired code can accumulate an O(T s) unknown
phase bias, demanding O(T^2) calibration for fixed data-law accuracy. The paired
operation's provisional O(T s^2) robustness could reduce that to O(T) source
trials, while each new parameter still requires its own paid interrogation.
The source code is reusable across new theta values and h directions in its
perpendicular plane; longer or more reliable future learning asks for further
paid calibration. This is a proposed acquired experimental capability, not a
claim that ordinary programs cannot implement it.

Known-method controls and decisive falsifiers

Strong priors: QEC metrology with known noise, approximate/robust QEC,
noise/channel tomography, adaptive noise learning, phase cycling and echo,
continuous-error-correction metrology, and joint nuisance-parameter estimation.
Need primary-source priority inspection before a novelty claim. A conventional
independently specified procedure with the same source data, reversible signal,
ancilla and operation budget must receive every promise used here. A control
given this exact policy is a replication, not a rejection criterion.

First executable object is the exact one-sensor-plus-ancilla CPTP cycle, checked
against the 3x3 Bloch formula. A concrete falsifier is any valid n,theta,Delta
breaking (A), separate evenness in a,c, or coherence-factor reduction. A second
is a conventional matched protocol already achieving the same calibration/
future-learning reliability tradeoff with no extra mechanism. A third is a
finite-cadence/operation cost that erases the stated advantage. No hardware,
purchases, outreach, accounts or external experiment is proposed.
