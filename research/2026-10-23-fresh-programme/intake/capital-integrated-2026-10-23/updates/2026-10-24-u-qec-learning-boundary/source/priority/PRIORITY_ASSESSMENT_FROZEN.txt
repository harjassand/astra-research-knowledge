Finding

The broad ingredients are established, but I did not find an exact primary
source for the complete acquired-axis + paired finite-cycle recovery map in
the small source set inspected. The source-axis estimation is standard qubit
channel tomography / eigendirection estimation. Finite-cadence QEC-induced
systematic frequency bias is established. Removing a first-order systematic
shift by reversal, symmetrization, echo, or balanced Ramsey signals is also an
established method family. The particular asserted relation
F_theta(n)=eta_theta(n) conjugate(eta_-theta(n)) and its O(T s^2) transfer
bound remain a narrow, source-specific claim, not a priority finding.

Most direct QEC-metrology priority: Rojkov, Layden, Cappellaro, Home and Reiter,
"Bias in Error-Corrected Quantum Sensing," Phys. Rev. Lett. 128, 140503 (2022),
doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.128.140503, https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.05817 . They
show that finite-rate/finite-frequency correction can shift the effective
sensing frequency, with bias that averaging does not remove, and give a
post-processing remedy using an effective-channel model. Their discrete
analysis explicitly writes repeated noise, signal evolution and recovery and
identifies noncommutation between the error-subspace and logical evolution as
the bias mechanism. They also state an open design question about unbiased
QEC sensing and exhibit a GKP sensing construction whose signal/error-subspace
evolutions meet their commutation condition. This establishes that finite-cycle
logical drift and correcting for it are not new broadly. It does not, in the
inspected text, use a learned unknown Pauli axis or this paired +/-signal,
logical-X sequence, and it does not derive the opening's O(s) versus O(s^2)
axis-miscalibration scaling.

Closest phase-bias cancellation precedent: Sanner et al., "Auto-Balanced
Ramsey Spectroscopy" (2017), https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.02630, and the
mathematical generalization by Yudin et al.,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.03365 . These use phase-congruent Ramsey cycles at
different dark times and a feedback/balancing variable to remove
interrogation-induced shifts, with an experimental clock demonstration in
Sanner et al. This makes a broad claim that paired or balanced sequences
cancel coherent phase bias untenable. The protocol is not the same physical
operation: it does not learn a Lindblad axis, correct a sensor-tag code at
finite cadence, or use an external sign reversal combined with a logical
normalizer pulse. It is a necessary strong, independently specified control
for any accuracy claim.

Closest QEC pulse/control overlap: Vezvaee et al., "Demonstration of
high-fidelity entangled logical qubits using transmons," Nature Communications
17, 3281 (2026), https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-70011-3 . Their
normalizer dynamical decoupling uses code-normalizer elements as pulses to
suppress logical errors in an experimental QEC/QED memory setting. A logical
X-type normalizer echo is therefore established operational language, not a
new control primitive. The paper is about memory fidelity and crosstalk; it
does not pair an external +/- metrological signal, cancel a QEC-induced
frequency bias, or acquire the unknown continuous noise axis from paid source
trials.

Learning boundary: Wagner et al., "Learning Logical Pauli Noise in Quantum
Error Correction," Phys. Rev. Lett. 130, 200601 (2023),
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.130.200601, show that syndrome data can
identify logical noise channels for codes that correct the noise. A more
recent experimental predecessor is "Reinforcement learning control of quantum
error correction," Nature 655, 879--884 (2026),
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10759-2; it repurposes QEC error
events to adapt control parameters during computation and demonstrates
stabilization against injected drift. These make learning/reusing noise or
control structure during QEC a crowded broad claim. Their learned quantities,
observations and task are different from source-only Bloch tomography for one
unknown Lindblad axis followed by metrology.

Interface promises that set the boundary

The signal reversal is a substantive physical promise: the target coupling
must be switchable from +theta to -theta while the stationary noise generator
and its recovery action remain the same. A generic Hahn echo or logical pulse
does not supply this. If sign reversal is implemented by conjugating the
sensor, it may also conjugate the Pauli-noise axis and recovery, invalidating
the stated eta_theta / eta_-theta pair. The logical pulse must specifically
be E on the sensor together with X on the tag ancilla so it normalizes the
code and preserves the syndrome instrument. A generic sensor X is not an
equivalent operation.

The proposal also assumes trusted fast gates, parity measurement, feedback,
reset and a noiseless logical-tag ancilla. These are standard ideal-control
assumptions in the QEC-metrology literature; Zhou, Zhang, Preskill and Jiang
explicitly grant noiseless ancillas, perfect joint gates/measurement/reset and
arbitrarily short processing in their 2018 construction:
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-017-02510-3 . They are permissible
mathematical resources, but their durations, failure rates, and calibration
must be charged before practical advantage can be claimed.

Scaling and novelty boundary

Given the exact stated reflection symmetry, absence of a linear term in the
local axis error is a standard parity/symmetrization consequence. Combining
that with s^2=O(1/K) makes O(T/K) versus the unpaired O(T/sqrt(K)) a plausible
scaling for this model. The source-selected papers do not establish that
specific bound, nor that it is new. It is not yet a comparative advantage:
Rojkov-style exact forward-model fitting/post-processing, GABRS-style balanced
interrogation, and learned-noise controls must receive the same source data,
sign control, ancilla/gate assumptions and fully charged operations. The
opening appropriately labels novelty and total-cost benefit NOT_ESTABLISHED.
