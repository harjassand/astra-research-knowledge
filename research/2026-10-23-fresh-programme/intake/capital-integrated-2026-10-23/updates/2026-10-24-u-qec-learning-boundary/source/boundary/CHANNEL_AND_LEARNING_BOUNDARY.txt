Necessary observation-interface correction

The quadratic law is an AVERAGED logical-channel statement. It does not apply
to retained syndrome transcripts. At theta=0 and n=(b,c,0), b=sqrt(1-c^2), let
 p=(1-exp(-2gamma Delta))/2.
A Q syndrome corrected by X applies D=bI+i cZ to the logical qubit. The two
single-Q paths in the pair have probability p(1-p) and conditional D/D-dagger.
For input |+L>, the syndrome-labelled cq output has trace distance
       2p(1-p)|c|
from the aligned instrument, whereas syndrome erasure gives
 F_0=1-4p(1-p)c^2
and trace distance2p(1-p)c^2. These are exact finite-cycle identities. Thus an
O(s^2) adaptive data-law guarantee requires the macro to export only averaged
logical outputs after syndrome erasure. Measurements, feedback and erasure/
reset remain charged. The incumbent may retain the syndrome bits and jointly
learn noise and signal; that stronger access cannot be silently forbidden.
Phase-angle bounds additionally require reference visibility away from zero;
the complex-multiplier/diamond bound does not need that condition.

Finite-cadence correction

Even at n=e, the logical reference is not noiseless Ramsey. At small theta,
the phase slope per free-evolution time is reduced by
       (1-exp(-2gamma Delta))/(2gamma Delta).
It is about .432 at gamma Delta=1. Finite signal also attenuates coherence.
Recovery cadence, full effective-channel likelihood and ordinary bias
postprocessing must be supplied to the incumbent and priced. Infinite-speed
recovery cannot be used as a practical-cost conclusion.

The proposed scalar-learning advantage is NOT established

The naive one-sign plug-in code has a genuine O(s) unknown logical phase.
But the interface explicitly grants signal reversal. A competent independent
control can run recovered Ramsey at both +theta and -theta, estimate both
complex coherence factors and use their odd phase/product in postprocessing.
Its mean has the same evenness/cancellation; joint effective-channel nuisance
fitting is another conventional control. It trades preparations/readouts and
estimator variance against the coherent pair's logical-X toggles and retained
coherence. Hence the opening's O(T^2) versus O(T) calibration comparison is only
against the weak one-sign baseline. It does NOT establish a learning advantage
over the strong sign-reversed control under matched resource budgets.

The coherent macro could have separate operational value for a downstream
coherent experiment, but none is manufactured here merely to save the proposal.
For the intended scalar-coupling-learning problem, the conventional differential
control already supplies the central nuisance cancellation. No target benchmark
was run and no superiority/newness claim is promoted. The physical operation
and exact averaged-channel/instrument distinction are retained as bounded
results; the search for a substantively new learning mechanism moves on.
