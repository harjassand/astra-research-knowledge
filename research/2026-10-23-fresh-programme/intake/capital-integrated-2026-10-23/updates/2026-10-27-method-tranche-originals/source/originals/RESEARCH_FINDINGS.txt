ASTRA AS A DISCOVERY SYSTEM — FIRST META-RESEARCH TRANCHE
25 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane

The strongest current lead is that Astra's discovery progress depends on how it changes representations at failed interfaces, and whether those changes preserve or create a consequential operation. Adding workers, proofs or explicit reasoning instructions is an unreliable proxy for that progress. This is a research hypothesis supported by selected cases and bounded experiments; no improvement in Astra's discovery rate has yet been established.

This investigation used five GPT-6.1 Sol Max worker identities and twelve GPT-6 Luna Max trial starts, within the authorized ceilings. Three Sol investigators studied trajectories, transfers and allocation; two performed methodological and outcome review. The original investigators retained responsibility for their own scientific work. The material is local and private. Existing branches, dispatcher, Capital, retrieval owner and protected Dots were not retasked or modified.

WHAT WAS INVESTIGATED AND TESTED

1. Eight actual trajectories, with 37 exact source passages.

The episodes cover computational learning, heat conversion, quantum entropy search, hidden-process inference, chemical assembly and forced context successors. The sample was deliberately selected from existing coordinator leads; it cannot estimate how frequently a pattern occurs across Astra. Source proofs, literal prompts and receipts were separated from earlier interpretations. All 37 saved passages and their full-file hashes were checked again by the root investigator.

The most consequential distinction is between three kinds of movement:

* A representation weakens the evidence needed for the SAME useful output. The heat certificate replaces full real-frequency response convergence with a sufficient positive-kernel expression in three imaginary-time correlations, while preserving the inclusive heat-deficit quantity. Acquiring and certifying those correlations remains a separate, potentially difficult problem; they are not simply three laboratory-time measurements.
* A representation creates a DIFFERENT useful operation. P3's engineered program-law response and XH1's post-synthesis product qualification are meaningful new operations. They do not acquire the earlier microscopic inference or per-step product-gated progression interfaces. Their value should be assessed on the operations they actually enable.
* A representation improves an intermediate proxy while worsening the next needed operation. A smaller polynomial scaffold loses the inspected derivative/Hessian comparison against the source and Horner programs. Representation size alone therefore failed as a predictor on that exposed workload.

This distinction should preserve ambitious pivots. A surprising new operation can be more valuable than the original target. The research issue is whether the pivot's consequence is made explicit and tested, rather than whether every researcher must keep an old question.

The warm and compact-context histories both contain substantive constructions. They do not identify the causal value of context length, interruption rate or prompt wording. The saved negative measurement also cannot be credited with causing a later representation change when the record says it was never dispatched.

Full evidence and alternative explanations: studies/trajectory_study.txt and trajectory_evidence.json.

2. Four paired cross-domain transfer cases, with concrete passing and failing preconditions.

The repeated operations are objective-specific nullspace tests, variation within an observation fibre, rare-event conditioning, and projected dual residuals. Small exact calculations checked examples of each relevant contract. A shared mathematical pattern can transfer while the stronger scientific conclusion fails because the receiver needs another boundary condition, norm, acquired object or clock.

One particularly sharp generic witness has unconditional distributions within total variation 10^-6, while their conditional distributions on a rare event have total variation one. It blocks a free transfer from broad additive accuracy to rare-event relative accuracy. It is a finite interface counterexample, with no claim of realization in every physical source class.

Astra already records several of these bridges and failures. The new research question concerns the ORDER of work: does finding the failed operation first, then testing a candidate map, outperform equally informed ordinary research? Building another retrieval architecture is not justified by these cases. Current archive status and source revisions remain as recorded; this desk study does not globally reclassify reviewed results.

Full mappings, exact tests and proposed content-matched control: studies/transfer_study.txt and transfer_evidence.json. Source export is 83e729c3195cc8d22035c5d0de9a122d68af49b1, with audited input ancestor 5b6a5737f1685ff22ec1fccb6cd8c31fae990b03. The source directory is a Git-object export; it is not a live checkout or researcher-activity registry. All eleven read-path hashes were independently checked.

3. A reproducible synthetic allocation counterexample.

The model gives three candidate lines a theory gate and a separate interface gate. Every policy pays the same nine credits, knows the same probability model and sees the same outcomes; no policy observes the hidden interface-feasibility bit. Preparation persists and failures update its posterior. Bayesian dynamic programming evaluates the finite horizon.

At the selected base parameters:

Policy                         Expected successful gates   Expected complete lines
Gate-count objective                    3.447                     0.447
Complete-line objective                  2.136                     0.617
Bayesian index with DP fallback          2.057                     0.617

Different objective-optimal policies winning their own objective is expected by construction. The useful result is the concrete size and direction of the tradeoff, including a competent adaptive index and its failure region. These are equal action-credit horizons; planning, calibration and computation costs are not modeled. The index uses an exact-DP fallback, so its end-to-end cheapness is not established. In a hard-theory stress case the cheap index loses materially to full planning. The results do not support a universal 'always work on the interface' rule.

The model also makes correlation visible: ten attempts sharing one latent feasibility condition have success probability about 0.350, while incorrectly treating their marginal success rates as independent gives about 0.940. This is an analytic property of the chosen synthetic model. Astra's actual dependence, probabilities and maturation costs were not estimated.

The root reproduced the base exactly, including replay using explicit hidden states rather than posterior transitions. Sensitivity cases are exploratory. A separate Sol code review in review/allocation_audit.txt found no material transition or information asymmetry and reproduced a hard-theory stress case. It documents the uncharged planning costs and exact-DP fallback. The hard-theory index result is about 28.7% below the programme optimum; another selected stress case is about 6.6% below. Model and full results: studies/allocation_model.py and studies/results.json. These are synthetic research-process calculations, not empirical Astra improvement.

4. A live, fixed 12-response prompt pilot.

Question: does an explicit causal-path/weak-link/strong-incumbent scaffold improve the next research decision beyond a competent expert instruction? Three retrospective Astra-derived evidence packets each received two starts per condition. Evidence, response fields, requested model/reasoning setting and 220-word reporting cap were shared. These are twelve starts on THREE research cases, not twelve independent scientific problems.

Exact prompts, task selection, rubric and all assignments were frozen before response collection. A recorded amendment allowed one assigned prompt-file read, followed by no tools. All twelve completed, used one observed assigned read, and stayed within 217 whitespace-separated words. No rerolls or replacements occurred. The launch tool does not provide hard filesystem/context isolation; workers shared ambient instructions. Actual logs are retained through scoped collection receipts. This pilot is not registered as a compliant sealed experiment under Astra's stricter existing evaluation protocol.

Two Sol scorers evaluated anonymized exact responses before arm aggregation. They did not receive the assignment key. One scorer had reviewed generic arm descriptions, and response language may reveal condition, so this is label-blind scoring. Early methodological advice preceded launch; the material-specific GO arrived after some responses had completed, before that auditor inspected outputs. It was not an enforced prerelease approval gate.

Condition                       Judge 1 mean / 8     Judge 2 mean / 8
Competent expert instruction            7.33                  7.50
Added causal scaffold                   7.83                  7.83

The judges disagreed on one fairness point in one response; both original judgments are preserved. Eight responses received 8/8 from the first judge, nine from the second. All twelve identified a relevant missing interface. The remaining mistakes involved overbroad route abandonment, an incompletely specified informed comparator, and ambiguous handling of a violated conditional constraint. Neither judge found an explicit assertion that a valid conditional theorem was falsified.

The scaffold has a small descriptive rubric edge. That is not evidence of better scientific discovery. All responses proposed broadly similar next actions; bottlenecks were strongly cued by the packets, and no proposed action was executed. There is no held-out task set, strict compute parity, significance result, component attribution or measured downstream discovery outcome.

Mean runtime output-token counters, including reasoning, were approximately 1,210 for the expert instruction and 1,384 for the scaffold. Mean worker elapsed times were 18.1 and 19.7 seconds. Across the twelve workers, reported counters total 831,935 input tokens (779,136 cached) and 15,564 output tokens, including 11,289 reasoning tokens. Reasoning is a subset of output and must not be counted twice. These counters include repeated input, differ with caching, omit root/Sol preparation and judging, and do not identify attributable monetary spend. Full study cost remains UNKNOWN.

All response bytes, source bindings, precollection amendment, scoring reasons and disagreements are in experiment/ and review/. The descriptive aggregate is experiment/RESULTS.json. No comparative rollout is justified by this pilot.

WHAT CHANGED IN THE META-RESEARCH AGENDA

The initial broad prompt-scaffolding question is now lower priority. Recognizing a gap in a supplied packet is too easy to serve as the principal research endpoint. The next trial should ask for an actual constructive escape, and should test whether the resulting decision follows new evidence. Useful evidence-grounding work already tests preserved, withdrawn and reversed evidence; adapting that idea is part of the experimental design, not a claim to have invented it. See https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04915v1 .

The primary next hypothesis is more specific: when both conditions have identical source information, foregrounding an EXACT pair that the current representation cannot distinguish, but whose desired outcomes differ, helps an autonomous researcher construct a paid observation or representation that resolves it. The comparison foregrounds the strongest valid positive foothold instead. Both conditions retain autonomy and may originate their own mechanism. Hypothesis failure includes merely repeating the no-go, smuggling in a sensor/oracle, or changing the target without a consequential surviving operation.

A second question asks whether protecting the exact external output test helps remove a bottleneck, and when that constraint suppresses a more valuable pivot. Same-output success and useful changed-output success must be scored separately. A third asks whether allocation by complementary failure coverage improves complete capability chains at equal full costs. These questions remain open; the synthetic model supplies counterexamples to easy assumptions, not an allocation prescription.

The next constructive pilot should use independently acquired tasks, genuine interface near-misses, actual context/tool isolation, fixed equal information and total budgets, and outcome assessment independent of condition. Earlier exposed cases are development examples. Internal agent audit remains sufficient at its actual scope; experimental independence is a design property and adds no external-specialist acceptance prerequisite for Astra. Construction, valid rejection and new useful operation need prespecified, distinct acceptance tests. Stop after the frozen assigned batch and preserve failures. Existing scientific owners need not be interrupted for an isolated method experiment.

The agenda also needs a later test of opportunity selection outside the inherited corpus. This first tranche is intentionally source-bound and cannot discover all of Astra's missing questions. A method that excels only on familiar, well-documented bottlenecks could still concentrate the programme on the wrong scientific opportunities.

LITERATURE AND ORIGINALITY BOUNDARY

The local hypotheses arose from the inspected Astra trajectories. Their historical novelty is UNKNOWN. Established work already studies goal-directed information, complementary proposals, scientific blind spots and evidence-sensitive evaluation. Primary references, inspection depth and limits are saved in evidence/literature.json. In particular, https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04686v1 separates complementary proposal supply from evidence-sensitive selection while explicitly limiting open-ended generalization. Neither that preprint nor the present pilot establishes an optimal Astra team design.

This tranche makes an original, source-grounded contribution to Astra's own method investigation: it distinguishes which representation changes alter capability, constructs a transparent allocation counterexample with failure regions, and tests a proposed prompt package against a competent control. Its evidence supports further discriminating experiments. The programme's civilisation-changing ambition remains the criterion for choosing which method questions deserve that effort; no mission-level breakthrough is claimed here.
