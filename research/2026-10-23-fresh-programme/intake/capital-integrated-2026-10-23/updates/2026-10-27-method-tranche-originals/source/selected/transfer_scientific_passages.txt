8. Duplication challenge and action

The archive itself says the committor/Dynkin arguments, direct Markov duality, Doob transform/Feynman–Kac, Erlangization, and goal-oriented corrected outputs are standard. N768 explicitly credits decision-risk, goal-driven experiment design, reduced-model and semiparametric precedents. Its literature-screening scope does not establish priority. This report has not conducted an independent current external literature survey. Historical novelty of the proposed workflow therefore remains UNKNOWN, and the working expectation should be that much of it is ordinary expert literature search plus assumption checking.

The strongest way to challenge that expectation is to give ordinary search the same source capital and freedom to use the same techniques. If the intervention wins only because its card preselects an answer that the control must discover unaided, the measured effect is extra information or unpaid curation. If it wins against the content-matched informed control, the defensible claim is a bounded research-order/representation efficiency result on the declared task population. It is still not a new mathematical discovery engine or a technological capability.
