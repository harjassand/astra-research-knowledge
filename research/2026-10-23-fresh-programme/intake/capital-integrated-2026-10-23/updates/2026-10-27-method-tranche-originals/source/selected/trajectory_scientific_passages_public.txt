The strongest pattern in the inspected sources is a change in the object that must be acquired or certified. Consequential movement appears when a representation preserves a useful output while making its evidence requirement weaker, or constructs a retained object that can perform a genuinely new task. More lemmas, smaller code, smooth convergence, and source preservation each answer a different question.

This is a purposive, eight-episode local study, not an audit of Astra or a causal prompt comparison. Three testable meta hypotheses

H1 — Preserving the external discriminator prevents operation substitution from masquerading as bottleneck removal
For a fixed research bottleneck, asking a constructor to preserve one concrete pre-existing external output test while choosing representations freely will yield more valid removals of the named missing interface than a generic next-construction continuation, at the same information and work budget.
Mechanism/evidence: Heat keeps the inclusive deficit and weakens its evidence requirement; P3/XH1 demonstrate valuable new operations that do not satisfy the old target. The discriminating output, rather than a thematic label, makes the difference visible. [T3, T5, T6, T7]
Falsifier: Equal-budget trials show no increase in old-test survival or interface removal, or the constraint suppresses valuable new operations without increasing valid capability changes.
Original testable meta hypothesis of this study; observational support only; not a claimed new general scientific principle.

H2 — An indistinguishable-world failure is a better construction seed than a named no-go
When identical source material contains an exact pair that the current representation cannot distinguish although the desired output differs, foregrounding that pair will more often produce a new observable/retained object that resolves the pair than foregrounding the positive theorem and naming the limitation in prose.
Mechanism/evidence: The pair supplies a minimal sufficient target for representation revision without selecting the mechanism: completion versus identity, whole-word support, and symmetry versus future task space. [T4, T5, T6, T8]
Falsifier: The foregrounded-pair condition merely repeats the no-go, changes the target without a surviving consequence, or adds unjustified sensors/labels at the same or higher rate as controls.
Original testable meta hypothesis of this study; not established causally.

H3 — Context usefulness is mediated by which unresolved discriminators it preserves
At a forced successor boundary, compact context that preserves exact failed discriminators and interface semantics as well as positive sources will retain more valid future-task capability than an equally sized positive-result-only compact context. Warm history may have an advantage only when those missing discriminators cannot be restored economically.
Mechanism/evidence: P1 retained its covariance correction and failures after replacement; the warm/compact acquisition pair independently changes the learned object under the same explicit semantic and failure packet. [T7, T8]
Falsifier: Matched replicated successors show the positive-only packet is equally good on hidden adversarial checks and capability transfer, or longer history dominates even after the relevant discriminators are supplied.
Original testable meta hypothesis; current context observations are confounded and cannot rank warm versus compact.

What this changes in the discovery-method question

The local evidence supports investigating failure-to-representation transformations, not a new reporting architecture. The overlooked risk is operation substitution: a programme can retain its name while moving from an unacquired microscopic interface to a tractable different output. The corresponding opportunity is constructive quotienting: preserve the desired observable consequence while discarding latent distinctions that no legitimate observation identifies. Heat is the clean same-output example; P3 and XH1 show useful changed-output operations; thin-rank and the incomplete-experience constructions expose exactly which future-use claims are still conditional.

A second overlooked limitation is the portability of a negative result. The source corpus contains decisive failures, but the measurement probe explicitly was not dispatched, so it cannot be credited with causing the later learning representation. By contrast, the chemistry continuation literally preserves the completion ambiguity, and the shared acquisition brief literally carries failed centers, labels, and validation contracts into the next construction. Source availability and causal reuse must remain separate.

The study does not justify shortening all contexts, lengthening all contexts, increasing interruptions, or stopping theorem work. It proposes a narrow test: whether exact failed discriminators help autonomous constructors choose representations with a consequential, checkable surviving operation. Existing researchers were not contacted or changed.
