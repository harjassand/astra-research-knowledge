PRINCIPAL THEOREM
Let Q be joint standard Gaussian, delta B=X-mu, tau=B(DX)^T, and R=sym(tau)-I. Assume B,tau bounded in operator norm and sym(tau)>=cI, c>0. For symmetric S set J=SB and u=delta(SB). The closed graph-space equation

 E[delta(SB).delta(TB)]+E<S tau,T> = -E<R,T>

for every admissible symmetric T is coercive. It solves the full linearization weakly, with ||S||_2<=r/c and ||u||_2<=r/(2sqrt(c)), where r=||R||_{L2(F)}. This absorbs the source derivative in the principal operator. It does not treat that derivative as bounded on L2.

For independent Gaussian W and a>0, the positive pushforward X+u+aW has TV distance at most C(a,c)r^2 from N(mu,(1+a^2)I), where

 C(a,c)=sqrt(6)/(2sqrt(c))*(1/a-atan(1/a))+sqrt(2)/(8ca^2).

The proof tests the equation against Hess f(X), where f solves a smoothed Gaussian Stein equation, and Taylor-expands in OUTPUT space. No full Du estimate is needed. The exact weak cancellation is

 E H(X)+E[DH(X).u]-E H(mu+Z)=E[R:D^3f(X)[u]].

The supremum over every Borel event proves full TV. Finite moments do not substitute for this argument. For an implemented conditionally unbiased correction U and full equation residual E0, the proof instead bounds TV by

 C2||E0||_2+C3*r*||u||_2+C4*E||U||^2,
 C2=log(1+a^(-2))/sqrt(2),
 C3=sqrt(6)*(1/a-atan(1/a)), C4=sqrt(2)/(2a^2).

Conditional bias b adds ||b||_2/a. Orthogonality only on a fitted Galerkin basis is insufficient to certify E0. Full domains and proofs are in coercive_audit/SMOOTHED_LAW_NEWTON_THEOREM.md.

ACTUAL FOUR-VALUE SCALAR STEP
For X=h(G)+Z and J=SB, the ideal correction is u=S(X-mu)-div_B S. Query an independent h(G') to replace mu. Its conditional error is centered, with covariance <=ell^2 S S^T. The augmented divergence kernel [SB,-S K(G')] proves global mean preservation. Evaluating S and div_B S remains a separate charge.

For scalar output and an explicitly available coefficient S(G,Z), use the frozen R22 two-query bounded action J_K(G;1) and return
 X=h(G)+Z,
 U=S(G,Z)*(X-h(G'))-S_G(G,Z)*J_K(G;1)-S_Z(G,Z),
 Y=X+U+aW.
Exactly four h-values are used on every execution; no derivative of h or mean is queried. Given G,Z, E[U]=u and Var(U-u)<=ell^2*(S^2+S_G^2). Thus the actual randomized law is covered by the theorem with E U^2<=||u||_2^2+ell^2 E(S^2+S_G^2). Constructing and certifying S are not included in the four runtime queries.

EXACT REMAINING OBLIGATION
The generic initial residual is r<=sqrt(d)*ell^2, so the new first-step estimate is O(d*ell^4). A useful high-dimensional compiler still needs a dimension-normalized correction norm, a reusable coercive Stein representation, and a field acquired from legal values with a proved full equation residual and conditional second moment. Every fitting, source-coupling, arithmetic, storage, precision and stopping-tail cost must be included in a deterministic polylog query cap. The scalar four-value primitive does not supply that missing field.

PREDICTIVE-COUNT INTERFACE: DISCRIMINATING ALTERNATIVE
A rank-two classical CP family separates artificial inverse-core norm inflation from genuine rare-state acquisition cost. Its measured core H=[[1,1],[0,q]] has inverse norm of order 1/q, while its exact observable physical norm is |x0-x1|+|x1|<=3||x||infinity, independent of q. Nevertheless every all-normalized-reachable-input learner needs Omega(log(1/alpha)/(q*epsilon^2)) primitive calls to learn the rare state's read parameter. A better norm cannot supply missing observations.

For the actual reset state, an occupation-sensitive positive coupling gives a whole-transcript bound for every adaptive policy under a single paid per-call abort clock. With confidence rectangle q in[l,u], theta-width e, and gamma=1-delta, the diameter bound is
 [gamma*(u-l)+gamma^2*l*e]/[delta*(1+gamma*l)+gamma*(u-l)+gamma^2*l*e].
Five thousand reset-and-trigger trials with zero successes, 99% confidence and delta=.1 yield a bound 0.00821740491247 while theta remains entirely unlearned. The normalized rare-state bound remains .9. This construction assumes the explicit two-state topology. It does not acquire the observable cone/topology for general unknown CP instruments and is not an unlimited lifetime reuse guarantee. A conventional controlled Markov/HMM learner with the same topology can reproduce it. Full proof and exact-law checks: predictive_work/RARE_MODE_CONFIDENCE_THEOREM.txt.

STRONGER SCALAR COMPARATOR FOUND DURING CHALLENGE
For arbitrary scalar ell-Lipschitz h, four iid queried values V0,V1,V2,V3 suffice for the positive, unfitted channel
 vhat=(V1-V2)^2/2,
 mhat=(V1-V2)^2*(V1-V3),
 Y=V0+(1-vhat/2)*Z-(mhat/6)*(Z^2-1)+aW.
It preserves E h(G) exactly. The full-law Taylor/Stein proof gives
 TV<=19*D4*ell^4/[24*(1+a^2)^2]
      +phi(1)/a^2*((3/4)*ell^4+(8/3)*ell^6),
where D4 is half the L1 norm of the fourth Gaussian density derivative and is <=sqrt(6). This is an ordinary transported-cumulant comparator, not a novelty claim. It has exactly four calls, six scalar Gaussian seeds and constant arithmetic/storage, with no fitted field or mean/derivative oracle. For a=.5 the upper bound is 1.4353975831*ell^4+2.58102106154*ell^6. The vector rank-one covariance version incurs ell^4*d*(d+2)/4 correction second moment even on an affine conservative source. Details: gaussian_challenge/UNIVERSAL_FOUR_VALUE_SCALAR_COMPARATOR.md. Its law proof uses all bounded event tests, not moment matching.
