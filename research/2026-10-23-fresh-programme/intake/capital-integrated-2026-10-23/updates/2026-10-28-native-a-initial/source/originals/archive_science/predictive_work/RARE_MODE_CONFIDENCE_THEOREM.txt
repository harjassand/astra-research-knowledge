RARE MODES: WHAT OBSERVABLE POSITIVITY CAN REMOVE, AND WHAT IT CANNOT
Owned derivation, 27 October 2026. Exact subfamily obstruction and positive
whole-law certificate. No general N952 learner, novelty, or speedup claim.

Purpose and source boundary
---------------------------
The supplied S7 theorem learns stationary CP instruments from joint-event
counts, uses a legal core H to bound a hidden physical quotient norm, and
propagates joint instrument error through arbitrary classical controllers.
S8 and S9 correctly retain vacuous all-input certificates. This note does
not change those numbers. It constructs a family within that interface to
separate two distinct difficulties: an avoidable inverse-core norm penalty
and an unavoidable cost of learning a rarely reachable state's behavior.
S10's acquired local-cell preparation uses different physical assumptions;
none is imported here.

1. A legal rank-two CP family
----------------------------
The inaccessible memory has basis |0>,|1> and starts at |0><0| after each
supplied repeatable episode start. Let 0<q<1 and 0<=theta<=1 be unknown.
Write E_ij=|i><j|. Three calibrated action names are available:

 trigger t, outcome 0: Kraus sqrt(1-q) E_00;
 trigger t, outcome 1: Kraus sqrt(q) E_10 and E_11;

 marker m, outcome 0: Kraus E_00;
 marker m, outcome 1: Kraus E_11;

 read b, outcome 0: Kraus E_00 and sqrt(1-theta) E_01;
 read b, outcome 1: Kraus sqrt(theta) E_01.

Each action's summed effect is I, so these are stationary CP instruments.
All reachable states are diagonal. A trigger from state 0 enters state 1
with probability q, announcing outcome 1; a trigger from state 1 stays there
and announces 1. The marker reads and retains the state. The read produces
Bernoulli(theta) in state 1, always 0 in state 0, and always returns to 0.

Even an adaptive learner can know the family and infer the current classical
state exactly from the action/outcome record. Giving this extra information
only strengthens the lower bound below. No latent state preparation at 1 is
supplied. It must be reached through a paid successful trigger.

Use positive nonpostselected preparations p0=empty and p1=one trigger with
outcome discarded. Use the empty test and marker outcome 1. The exact core is

                 H = [[1, 1], [0, q]].                         (1)

Its rank is two, exactly exhausting the reachable predictive space. A rank-2
architectural promise is valid. The smallest singular value is asymptotic to
q/sqrt(2); ||H^-1||_infinity=1+1/q and
||H^-1||_(infinity->1)=1+2/q.

2. The true observable norm does not blow up with this core
---------------------------------------------------------
Let x=(x0,x1) be the empty-test mass and marker-1 mass. The underlying signed
diagonal state is diag(x0-x1,x1), and the marker distinguishes every nonzero
diagonal direction. Thus the observable quotient norm is exactly

                 ||x||Q=|x0-x1|+|x1|.                         (2)

In particular ||x||infinity<=||x||Q<=3||x||infinity, independently of q.
The physical positive cone in these coordinates is 0<=x1<=x0. The core-based
positive-preparation decomposition in S7 yields a valid but very loose
upper bound of order 1/q on this particular family. There is no defect in
the S7 norm calculation. Its acquired preparation basis poorly represents
the extreme state 1.

Positivity can therefore remove the norm penalty, if this cone can actually
be acquired or is justified by a supplied family. It cannot provide the
missing observations of theta. The next theorem makes that distinction exact.

3. Adaptive acquisition lower bound
----------------------------------
Fix q and consider theta_+=1/2+a, theta_-=1/2-a, with 0<a<=1/4. Allow any
adaptive randomized acquisition strategy using at most M primitive actions,
arbitrary episode starts at 0, and the entire legal observation record.
Let P_+,P_- be its transcript laws. Then

 KL(P_+ || P_-) <= q M kl(Ber(theta_+) || Ber(theta_-))
                <= 16 q M a^2.                                (3)

Proof. The hidden classical state is determined by legal action/outcome
history under both hypotheses. The only differing conditional observation
law is a read b while in state 1. Such a read consumes its entry into state
1 because it resets to 0. Consequently, pathwise, the number R of informative
reads is at most the number E of successful trigger entries from state 0.
With T the number of trigger calls from 0, E_+[E]=q E_+[T]<=qM.
The relative-entropy chain rule, including adaptive choices, gives
KL(P_+||P_-)=E_+[R] kl(Ber(theta_+)||Ber(theta_-)). Finally
kl=2a log((1+2a)/(1-2a))<=16a^2 on a<=1/4. Episode restarts or marker
calls create no informative reads. This argument includes more acquisition
power than a collection of fixed joint-event counts.

Now require a learned law uniformly accurate to TV epsilon on every normalized
reachable input, with coverage at least 1-alpha for each model. From input 1,
consider one read b preceded by the S7 independent abort coin delta, where
gamma=1-delta. Its two terminal laws have separation 2 gamma a. Taking
a=2 epsilon/gamma, for epsilon<=gamma/8, makes separation 4 epsilon.
A learner achieving the stated guarantee induces a hypothesis test with each
error <=alpha. The elementary Bretagnolle-Huber testing inequality yields

       M >= gamma^2 log(1/(4 alpha)) / (64 q epsilon^2),        (4)

for alpha<1/4. The probability theorem follows from (3); no asymptotic
rank argument or numerical test is a premise. Thus a uniform certificate
independent of rare-state reachability cannot hold even at exact rank 2.
Equation (4) is not a lower bound on actual-start whole-law prediction, nor
on a learner supplied with a state-1 preparation oracle.

For completeness, the testing inequality used here is elementary. Let
A=int sqrt(dP dQ) be the Hellinger affinity. Jensen gives A>=exp(-KL(P||Q)/2),
and Cauchy-Schwarz gives TV(P,Q)<=sqrt(1-A^2). Therefore every test's sum of
errors is at least 1-TV>=1-sqrt(1-exp(-KL))>=exp(-KL)/2. If both errors are
at most alpha, KL>=log(1/(4alpha)), which combined with (3) proves (4).

For theta in {0,1}, the simpler no-entry coupling gives a separate sharp
order-1/q obstruction: with probability at least (1-q)^M, no state-1 entry
occurs, and the entire transcript is identical under both models. A confident
all-input diameter below gamma must pay this rare-entry cost.

4. An acquired positive whole-law confidence geometry for the family
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Now retain the actual-start contract rather than demanding normalized accuracy
at every rare state. Stop before every attempted primitive independently
with probability delta. The output is the ENTIRE observed action/outcome
transcript and abort label. Any finite-state or history-dependent randomized
controller is allowed, including one chosen after acquisition. Finite native
termination and discarding parts of the transcript can only reduce TV.

For two models (q,theta),(q',theta'), set
 l=min(q,q'), u=max(q,q'), h=u-l, e=|theta-theta'|.
Couple each trigger and each read maximally until the first visible discrepancy.
While the two states agree at 0, a trigger mismatches with probability h,
sends both to 1 with probability l, and leaves both at 0 with probability 1-u.
While they agree at 1, a read mismatches with probability e and otherwise
resets both to 0. Markers and all other choices are uninformative delays.

The largest possible mismatch probability before the abort clock is the unique
nonnegative Bellman solution

 U0=gamma[h+l U1+(1-u)U0],
 U1=gamma[e+(1-e)U0].                                         (5)

Indeed at state 0, choosing marker or read only gives gamma U0, which is no
larger than U0. At state 1, choosing marker or trigger only gives gamma U1.
The displayed trigger/read choices attain the maximum coupling-failure
probability. The clock makes the Bellman map a contraction. Solving gives

 U0 = [gamma h+gamma^2 l e]
       /[delta(1+gamma l)+gamma h+gamma^2 l e],
 U1 = gamma[e+(1-e)U0].                                      (6)

Consequently the complete output-law TV, uniformly over all controllers from
the reset state, is at most U0. Equation (6) is an explicit positive coupling
certificate; it does not infer full-law TV from moments or selected tests.
The coupling upper bound need not equal statistical TV for interior theta.
For q=q' and theta=0,theta'=1 it IS exact: the first completed informative
read completely distinguishes the models, and U0 is its probability.

Given a simultaneous count-based confidence rectangle
q in [q_L,q_U], theta in [theta_L,theta_U], let w=theta_U-theta_L.
For every pair of compatible models, a uniform diameter certificate is

 D0 = [gamma(q_U-q_L)+gamma^2 q_L w]
       /[delta(1+gamma q_L)+gamma(q_U-q_L)+gamma^2 q_L w].       (7)

To verify the endpoints, the expression in (6) increases with u and e and,
at fixed u,e, decreases with l. Therefore q_L,q_U,w maximize it. A fitted
parameter pair inside the rectangle is a positive causal instrument and
has whole-law error at most D0 against the true member, on the simultaneous
confidence event. No core inverse occurs. Initial state 1 instead has bound
D1=gamma[w+(1-w)D0], which correctly remains large when theta is unlearned.

If one wants output physical memory, coherently referenced quantum operations,
or an unknown CP family rather than these observable classical states, the
theorem does not provide those contracts. The full classical future transcript
does cover any subsequent finite-library reuse under the SAME global stopping
clock. Repeatedly restarting the clock for an unlimited lifetime changes the
task. Multiple macro invocations need a total-lifetime/error budget.

5. A nonvacuous finite acquisition regime with an unlearned mode
-------------------------------------------------------------
Perform N independent reset-and-trigger trials and observe zero successes.
The exact one-sided binomial confidence interval is

        q in [0, 1-alpha^(1/N)],   theta in [0,1].              (8)

Its false-return probability is at most alpha: for any true q exceeding the
upper endpoint, Pr(all N triggers fail)=(1-q)^N<alpha. This is not coverage
conditioned on returning zero. No theta data are fabricated.

With N=5000, alpha=.01, delta=.1, equation (7) gives D0 about .00822 even
though D1=.9. Thus 5000 legal one-step reset episodes certify the complete
law of EVERY adaptive stopped controller from the supplied start within about
0.00822 of the chosen compatible model; they cannot certify all-input reuse.
For an actual q=10^-6, this zero-count return occurs with probability about
0.995. That parameter is used only by the synthetic scorer, never by the
count-only confidence computation. A positive nominal model q=0 can be used
inside the confidence set even though the true process has exact rank 2;
the resulting approximation honestly discards an unresolved rare mode.

6. Independent exact law check
-----------------------------
When q is fixed and the controller always triggers from 0 and reads from 1,
the number K of completed reads before abort is geometric on {0,1,...}:

 Pr(K=k)=(1-r)r^k,   r=gamma^2 q/(delta+gamma q).               (9)

The trigger waiting times and the terminal incomplete cycle are ancillary
to theta. Given K=k, the informative outputs are k independent Bernoulli(theta)
bits. Hence the exact full-transcript distance between two theta values is

 sum_(k>=0) (1-r)r^k TV(Ber(theta)^k, Ber(theta')^k).           (10)

For each k, the product-law TV is exactly one half the L1 distance between
the two binomial(k,theta) mass functions. Truncating after k=n leaves a
rigorous analytic remainder at most r^(n+1). verify_rare_mode.py evaluates
rational examples with Fraction arithmetic, checks this interval against
(6), and verifies equality for theta=0 versus theta=1. The check is based on
the complete law, not a finite list of output moments. Floating evaluation
is used only for displaying exact rational quantities and the zero-count
endpoint; it is not a machine-certified transcendental rounding proof.

7. Costs, comparator, and exact remaining scientific obligation
--------------------------------------------------------------
The lower bound counts ALL physical primitive calls, even under adaptive
acquisition and retained states. Starts and sensing also remain physical
resources. The special zero-count learner stores two integers and evaluates
a scalar confidence endpoint and (7); N reset preparations and N triggers
are charged. Executing any controller pays every native call, expected at
most gamma/delta under the common clock. Sampling a learned classical model
costs O(1) arithmetic per simulated primitive; finite-bit randomness needs a
declared common rounding/primitive-error convention if exact reals are not used.

An equally informed conventional two-state controlled Markov/HMM learner can
use the SAME observed topology, binomial confidence intervals and Bellman
coupling. This note supplies no advantage over that competitor and no claim
of historical novelty. The general S7 task does not supply this topology or
observable positive cone. The substantive discrimination is:

 * the particular inverse-core norm can be avoidable;
 * all-input rare-state accuracy nevertheless has an information lower bound;
 * actual-start adaptive whole-law prediction can remain certifiable while
   the rare mode is entirely unlearned.

The exact next obligation for general N952 is a COMPUTABLE observable
confidence set or positive cone with an acquired occupation-sensitive
whole-law certificate for unknown stationary CP instruments. It must preserve
every count-compatible process, charge model-class/rank assumptions, avoid
pretending that a rare state was prepared for free, and include optimization
and precision costs. Replacing the core inverse by an assumed cone or a
supplied physical model would not meet that obligation. This family neither
provides that general construction nor proves it impossible.
