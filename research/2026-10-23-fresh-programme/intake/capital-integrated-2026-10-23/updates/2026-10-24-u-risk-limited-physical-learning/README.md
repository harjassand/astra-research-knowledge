## Risk-limited acquisition under an explicit killed-channel model

The result assumes one continuously used apparatus. At predictable amplitude `a ∈ (0,1]`, an exposure survives with probability `exp(−κa^s)`; only a survivor returns `Y = θa^r + Normal(0, σ²)`. Death is observed, irreversible, and can occur at most once. The response, noise, hazard and actual-amplitude laws are premises. There is no reset, failed-run pool or free survivor validation. The [0517 root derivation](source/U0517/next_unit/root/ACTUAL_DEATH_RISK_INFORMATION_BOUND.txt), its [stopped-law precursor](source/U0517/next_unit/risk_limited_acquisition/STOPPED_INFORMATION_FRONTIER.txt), and the [0537 closed result](source/U0537/next_unit/risk_limited_acquisition/SCIENTIFIC_RESULT.txt) are preserved separately.

For the full adaptive stopped transcript, `KL(Pθ || Pθ′) = (θ−θ′)² E[Eobs] / (2σ²)`, where `Eobs` sums `a^(2r)` only over surviving readouts. With `ρ` the actual source-death probability and `c` the expected successful-readout count, the corrected risk conversion gives `E[Σsurvivors a^s] ≤ ρ/κ`. Thus, if `s ≤ 2r`, `Eobs ≤ ρ/κ`; if `s > 2r` and `q = 2r/s`, then `Eobs ≤ min{c, c^(1−q)(ρ/κ)^q}`. These are same-reference-law bounds and cover arbitrary predictable actions and stopping. A bound on attempted duration is at least as restrictive because attempts include successes and any fatal final attempt.

For uniform joint completion-alive and `ε`-accuracy at least `1−γ`, the two-point test requires `Q = σ² kl(1−γ || γ)/(2ε²)` observed information. Consequently, `s ≤ 2r` requires `ρ/κ ≥ Q`; for `s > 2r`, it requires both `c ≥ Q` and `c ≥ (κ/ρ)^(2r/(s−2r)) Q^(s/(s−2r))`. The [current actual-risk frontier](source/U0537/next_unit/risk_limited_acquisition/ACTUAL_RISK_FRONTIER.txt) supersedes the earlier additive-hazard statement for this bound; it does not identify expected additive hazard with actual death probability.

The fixed-amplitude, fixed-horizon Gaussian calibration supplies a matching exponent under the declared model: its source completion probability and conditional coverage combine as `(1−ρ)(1−α)`. For later work, a source joint completion-and-coverage guarantee `g` and a uniform later-path hazard bound `b` support joint later survival of at least `g exp(−b)`. The stronger conditional product requires an actual conditional coverage guarantee; the [stopped-information and reuse audit](source/U0537/next_unit/risk_limited_acquisition/luna_batched_mean/ANALYTIC_AUDIT.md) records the distinction. This is a resource frontier for a known statistical interface, not a new estimator or an advantage over conventional likelihood-aware experimental design.

The [survival-only permission result](source/U0517/next_unit/risk_limited_acquisition/LIVE_EVIDENCE_VERSUS_DEATH.txt) and current result preserve the live-evidence/death separation: if safe and unsafe worlds have identical conditional live-output kernels, permission issued alive with probability at least `1−β` in the safe world and at most `α` in the unsafe world forces unsafe-source death of at least `[1−β−α]₊`, without a hazard-ordering assumption. The selected-survivor and optional-stopping counterexamples remain in the [frozen challenge](source/U0517/next_unit/risk_limited_acquisition/luna_batched_mean/OPENING_CHALLENGE_FROZEN.md) and [current audit](source/U0537/next_unit/risk_limited_acquisition/luna_batched_mean/ANALYTIC_AUDIT.md). ## Amended reciprocal phasor-learning construction

No new priority is claimed.

The subsequent [amended construction](source/U0537/next_unit/physical_learning/PHASOR_CONSTRUCTION.md) changes the objective to the network's complex response at its actual carrier frequency. It does not recover a DC-inference gradient with a fast modulation probe. For a grounded passive linear reciprocal RC network, a boundary teacher measures the free output I/Q error, conjugates and holds it, then performs three settled epochs: free response, plus probe, and minus probe. Local detectors extract the complex `2ω` square component. The finite difference yields the conductance gradient exactly for the stated linear model and nonzero probe size, provided the held teacher and network conditions are maintained. I/Q acquisition, phase-conjugating current generation, local sensing, settling, reset, storage and conductance retention all carry cost.
