RISK-LIMITED ACQUISITION: LIVE INFORMATION VERSUS IRREVERSIBLE EVIDENCE
24 October 2026. Lead-owned opening, before worker assignments/new source review.
The exact returning-experiments unit is frozen; do not extend its finite fixture.

Consequential obstruction and concrete central operation

Repeated successful experiments can appear to validate the ability to learn
again, although a hazardous unknown system may simply not have failed yet.
Conversely, sufficiently informative weak probes might acquire a reliable
calibration while causing very little irreversible loss. The missing resource
is not just number of observations: it is live information per irreversible
hazard, with elapsed time and survivor selection charged.

The owned procedure is a dose-constrained calibration gate. Select a probe
amplitude and a finite number of exposures from an explicit readout/hazard
contract. On completion alive, output a confidence interval for theta; then
license a later experiment only if its separately trusted risk functional is
bounded uniformly on that interval. Failed source exposures count as failure,
not discarded training runs. A naive confidence interval conditional on survival
is not permitted for arbitrary adaptive amplitudes/stopping.

This is not a new estimator by renaming a sample mean. The proposed general
principle is an order-of-response versus order-of-damage boundary for the cost
of acquiring reliable knowledge, including a duration lower bound against
arbitrary adaptive Gaussian probing under the same resource caps. Known optimal
experimental design, sequential testing, reliability testing and dose-fractionation
are strong incumbents. No novelty or physical explanation is claimed in advance.

Specified statistical experiment and resource interface

Unknown theta is in a declared bounded real interval, e.g. [-1,1]. At each live
step the learner chooses a in (0,1] predictably from the full past and private
randomness. A primitive exposure takes one time unit plus declared control and
readout cost. It independently survives with probability exp(-kappa*a^s); on
failure an observable absorbing death symbol occurs and no further experiments
can be executed. If it survives, the ordinary scalar readout is

       Y = theta*a^r + sigma*Z,       Z iid standard Gaussian.

Use r>0,s>0,kappa>0,sigma>0. Conditional on the selected action, hazard is
independent of theta and of Gaussian noise. These response, noise and hazard
laws/constants are model premises. Finite successes cannot certify them against
an arbitrary unknown hazard law. They must eventually have a physically or
computationally justified interface if the result is to be applied.

Charge every attempted exposure (including death), control precision, elapsed
time, prior/model specification, discovery/search, failed source, readouts and
retained interval. Let M be attempted exposures and H=kappa*sum a^s additive
hazard. Keep pathwise caps, reference-world expected caps and actual death
probabilities distinct. With a pathwise hazard cap h in the uncensored extension,
death probability is at most1-exp(-h); an expected hazard cap only gives a
weaker union bound unless extra structure is proved. Finite amplitude resolution
or a per-exposure hazard floor can destroy arbitrary weak-probe limits.

First analytical mechanism test — before any synthetic fixture

Let E=sum a^(2r). Ignoring censoring can only strengthen the information upper
bound. The expected full stopped-law KL for theta versus theta' should be

       KL <= (theta-theta')^2/(2*sigma^2) * E_theta[E].

All chosen actions, survival/death, stopping and live outputs must be included;
the action/termination policy is common to alternatives. A reference-world
budget is charged under the same law used by KL. Prove this rather than invoke
conditional-survivor Gaussian likelihoods.

For s<=2r and a<=1, E<=H/kappa. For s>2r, Holder yields, with M<=N and H<=h,

       E <= min{N, N^(1-2r/s)*(h/kappa)^(2r/s)}.

The same form should hold with suitable reference expected M,H by applying
Holder on the probability-times-step measure. A binary testing reduction should
force E of order sigma^2/epsilon^2 for reliable epsilon-precision, including the
logarithmic confidence factor. Thus at fixed hazard, the s>2r duration exponent
is N of order epsilon^(-2s/(s-2r)); for s<=2r arbitrary precision is unavailable
at fixed hazard. This lower bound must cover adaptive inputs and stopping,
not merely a particular weak-pulse schedule.

Concrete achievable policy

For a nonadaptive constant amplitude a and N attempted exposures, choose
E0=2*sigma^2*log(2/alpha)/epsilon^2 and set a=(E0/N)^(1/(2r)), N>=E0.
Then the complete-data Gaussian confidence interval has radius epsilon and
statistical error<=alpha. Fixed amplitudes make readouts independent of survival;
joint completion-and-confidence probability is at least exp(-H)*(1-alpha),
where H=kappa*E0^(s/(2r))*N^(1-s/(2r)).

If s>2r, choosing
 N>=max{E0,(kappa/h)^(2r/(s-2r))*E0^(s/(s-2r))}
makes H<=h and matches the hypothesized duration exponent. Weak control can
exchange irreversible risk for diverging time. If s<=2r, weaker probes cannot
make fixed-information acquisition arbitrarily safe under this contract.
This is a conditional feasibility/resource result, not free information or an
overall speed advantage over conventional experiment design.

Reusable consequence, without manufacturing a downstream benchmark

If a genuine later experiment e has a separately justified hazard envelope
h_e(theta,u), a source-acquired interval I licenses control u only when
sup_(theta in I) h_e(theta,u) is within the remaining budget. The interval changes
that warranted set. An example bound M*L^2*(theta-u)^2 would require calibration
precision proportional to M^-1/2 for exposure M; it is merely a conditional
interface implication until an actual experiment supplies that envelope.
Adaptive later choices need a simultaneous source interval and predictable
budget accounting. Do not invent a special new task to rescue a replication tie.

Second gate: survival-only impossibility

Contrast a model where theta changes only hazard and all live outputs coincide.
Suppose a source permission decision is made alive with probability>=1-beta in
the safe world but <=alpha in an unsafe world. Couple policies on the same live
outputs/randomness. The unsafe world's source death probability should be at
least1-beta-alpha. Hence a uniformly low-risk source cannot obtain a strong
safety license from survival alone. With informative live outputs, derive the
appropriate separation of live-law evidence from destructive evidence; do not
mistake a survivor-selected posterior for an unconditional risk guarantee.

Proceed only if these analytical gates survive adversarial audit. No benchmark,
parameter sweep, invented physical exponent or application claim is authorized
by this opening. A decisive corrected impossibility or changed exponent is a
useful outcome. Preserve failures and compare with competent independently
specified likelihood/optimal-design/reliability methods.
