ACTUAL IRREVERSIBLE RISK FRONTIER — STRONGER THAN A DOSE CAP
24 October 2026. Root-improved theorem after lead opening and first derivation;
independently checked by sharp_statistics before this write-up. The earlier
STOPPED_INFORMATION_FRONTIER.txt remains valid as a dose-resource theorem.

1. Interface and exact killed-law information

Use exactly the death-BEFORE-readout source interface already declared. A common
policy chooses amplitude a_t in (0,1] predictably while alive; survival has
probability p_t=exp(-kappa*a_t^s), independent of theta and of the fresh Gaussian
given the action. If it survives the readout is theta*a_t^r+sigma*Z_t. Death is
observed, absorbing and can occur only once. Private randomization, all chosen
actions, stopping, death and live readouts belong to the full transcript.

Let J_t indicate an attempted exposure, B_t its survival indicator,
C=sum J_t*B_t the successful readout count, and
E_obs=sum J_t*B_t*a_t^(2r) the actually observed information exposure.
At reference theta define

 rho_theta=P_theta(source death), c_theta=E_theta[C].

Rho is the ACTUAL death probability, not expected cumulative hazard. No cap on
an unobserved no-death continuation or additive dose is assumed in this theorem.

The already proved full stopped-law identity is

 KL(P_theta||P_theta')
  = (theta-theta')^2/(2*sigma^2) * E_theta[E_obs].              (1)

2. Actual-risk conversion

At most one exposure is fatal. The compensator identities therefore give

 rho_theta=E_theta sum J_t*(1-p_t),
 E_theta sum J_t*B_t*a_t^s=E_theta sum J_t*p_t*a_t^s.           (2)

For x=kappa*a^s>=0, x*exp(-x)<=1-exp(-x), since exp(x)>=1+x.
Multiplying pathwise at each predictable action and summing in (2) yields

       E_theta sum J_t*B_t*a_t^s <= rho_theta/kappa.           (3)

This is the useful improvement over attempted-dose accounting: survival weights
the Gaussian information, and the same survival weight converts its companion
a^s moment directly into actual irreversible probability. Death/no-observation
contributes no Gaussian information to (1). Truncate and use monotone convergence
if the number of attempted exposures is unbounded. If c_theta is infinite the
duration bound is vacuous; (3) and the s<=2r ceiling still hold.

3. Adaptive actual-risk information frontier

If s<=2r, a^(2r)<=a^s, so

               E_theta[E_obs] <= rho_theta/kappa.            (4)

If s>2r, write q=2r/s in (0,1). Apply Holder to the OBSERVED probability-times-
step measure mu(domega,t)=P_theta(domega)*J_t(omega)*B_t(omega).
Its mass is c_theta; its a^s integral is at most rho_theta/kappa by (3).
Consequently

 E_theta[E_obs]
 <= min{c_theta, c_theta^(1-q)*(rho_theta/kappa)^q}.            (5)

Equations (1),(4),(5) cover arbitrary adaptive amplitudes and stopping, with
costs and risk under the SAME reference world. Since C<=M, replacing c_theta
by expected attempted exposures gives a valid weaker duration converse. No
claim that survival-conditioned outputs remain Gaussian is used.

4. Reliable precision against all adaptive policies

Assume joint completion alive AND epsilon accuracy has probability>=1-gamma
for every theta in [-1,1], with gamma<1/2 and epsilon<1. Death/nontermination
count as failure. The two-point test of the earlier theorem gives

 Q_gamma=sigma^2*kl(1-gamma||gamma)/(2*epsilon^2)
       <= E_theta0[E_obs]

at the fixed reference theta0=-1: compare theta1=-1+d and take d down to2epsilon.
If successful-readout cost is
uniformly bounded by c and actual source death probability by rho, then

 s<=2r:  rho/kappa >= Q_gamma;                               (6)

 s>2r:   c>=Q_gamma and
          c>=(kappa/rho)^(2r/(s-2r))*Q_gamma^(s/(s-2r)).       (7)

If only expected attempted cost m is bounded, replace c by m in (7). Joint
success also entails rho_theta<=gamma in each world, so rho can in particular
be replaced by gamma for a necessary condition. The result is intrinsically
about actual irreversible risk and precision, not a supplied dose accountant.

The reference theta0=-1 is fixed while theta1 varies, so caps specifically at
that reference suffice; uniform caps also suffice. With a cap only at a
different fixed reference and an admissible pair separated by d>2epsilon,
replace Q_gamma by 2*sigma^2*kl(1-gamma||gamma)/d^2 unless that reference admits
the limiting separation. Do not infer a cap at a moving reference from a cap
at one unrelated parameter.

5. Constructive policy, censoring, and asymptotic saturation

For committed constant a and horizon N, let h=kappa*a^s and p=exp(-h).
The actual probability of death is rho=1-p^N, and

       E[C]=p*(1-p^N)/(1-p)=p*rho/(1-p).

All observed amplitudes are equal, so Holder in (5) is an equality before the
risk conversion. The a^s moment is exactly

 a^s*E[C] = (rho/kappa)*h/(exp(h)-1).                        (8)

The only slack in (3) is h/(exp(h)-1)<=1, tending to1 as a tends to0. Thus the
observed-information/risk frontier is asymptotically saturated by constant weak
probes, rather than relying on replacement of death probability by an arbitrary
additive hazard bound.

On full completion, the sample-mean calibration has Gaussian variance
sigma^2/(N*a^(2r)). Fixed design makes completion independent of that Gaussian
vector. Choosing N*a^(2r)>=2*sigma^2*log(2/alpha)/epsilon^2 gives total
completion-and-accuracy probability>=(1-rho)*(1-alpha).

For a requested risk rho_bar in (0,1), use the constructive formula in the
earlier theorem with design hazard h_design=-log(1-rho_bar); it guarantees actual
rho<=rho_bar. Its expected attempts/readouts differ from maximum N by bounded
factors when rho_bar is fixed, and it matches the epsilon and risk exponents
of the adaptive converse. It is not proved finite-sample optimal for every
confidence/risk allocation; conventional likelihood-aware sequential design
can improve constants and decision rules.

6. Observation-order and physical limits

Death-before-readout matters. If every fatal exposure also yields its full
Gaussian measurement, the exact KL uses ATTEMPTED rather than surviving
information. The root conversion (3) then cannot be applied to that full
information count. Since a<=1 gives h<=kappa, the looser inequality
h<=[kappa/(1-exp(-kappa))]*(1-exp(-h)) supplies a finite-factor repair when kappa
is finite, but the exact constant and interface have changed. Readout after
irreversible alteration may not even be the same parameter channel.

The response, fixed-noise, hazard and actual amplitude laws remain premises.
Unknown gain, finite amplitude floor, per-attempt/background risk, cumulative
fatigue, nonstationary specimen and a Poisson/shot-noise weak limit can change
or remove the favorable regime. This theorem does not supply their calibration
or claim a physically new weak-probe mechanism. Strong constrained experimental
design and sequential testing remain the statistical incumbents.

7. General live-information-per-death obstruction

The useful mechanism is not specific to power laws. At a reachable live history
z and chosen action a, suppose survival p(z,a) is the same under two candidate
parameters and their live-readout KL is k(z,a). If a trusted class implies

           p(z,a)*k(z,a) <= R*(1-p(z,a))                     (9)

at EVERY such history/action, the full stopped transcript satisfies

           KL(P_0||P_1) <= R*rho_0.                         (10)

Proof: sum the one-step conditional KL under reference0 and use the disjoint
fatal-event compensator, exactly as in (2). Hence binary inferential errors
<=beta,alpha require kl(1-beta||alpha)<=R*rho_0. This is a conditional quantitative
barrier against all learning/control policies at this interface, not a demand
that an estimator use any particular representation.

All paid source experience belongs in that transcript. An allegedly learned
ability cannot escape (10) if the same finite ratio bound still applies to all
of its reachable controls. A constructive escape must expose a larger live-
information/failure ratio, a different observation law, regeneration/replicas,
or a weaker objective; prior source information must be charged if its law
differs between the alternatives. A new target parameter whose source-history
law is identical across its alternatives starts with zero source KL, even if
the acquired controls can change its subsequent ratio.

For the Gaussian power family k=(theta-theta')^2*a^(2r)/(2*sigma^2), (9) uses
R=(theta-theta')^2/(2*sigma^2)*sup_a a^(2r)/(exp(kappa*a^s)-1). This is finite
for s<=2r and diverges in the weak limit for s>2r. Divergence alone is not a
sufficiency theorem for arbitrary non-Gaussian kernels; the concrete Gaussian
policy supplies sufficiency only for its declared model. Different survival
laws add the hazard-symbol KL term and need the separate live/death treatment.
