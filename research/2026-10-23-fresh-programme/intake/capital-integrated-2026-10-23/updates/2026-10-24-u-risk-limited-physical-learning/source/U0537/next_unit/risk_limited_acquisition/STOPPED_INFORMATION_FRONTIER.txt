STOPPED INFORMATION / HAZARD / DURATION FRONTIER
24 October 2026. Lead derivation from LEAD_OPENING_FROZEN.txt.
Conditional theorem; independent audits and primary-method boundary pending.

1. Full-law interface (no survivor-conditioned likelihood shortcut)

Unknown theta lies in [-1,1]. A common randomized policy chooses an amplitude
a_t in (0,1] from its past action, live readout and death history, or stops.
Once dead it cannot choose further exposures. An attempted exposure first
survives with probability p(a)=exp(-kappa*a^s), independently of theta given
a and independently of the new Gaussian. If it survives it yields
Y_t~Normal(theta*a_t^r,sigma^2). On death it yields the absorbing symbol dagger
and no Y_t. All actions, stopping, and death belong to the transcript. Fresh
policy randomness has the same law under all theta and is included if needed.

Let J_t indicate that exposure t is attempted alive before its outcome, and
B_t its survival indicator. Define stopped resources

 M=sum J_t, H=kappa*sum J_t*a_t^s,
 E_att=sum J_t*a_t^(2r), E_obs=sum J_t*B_t*a_t^(2r).

An attempted but fatal exposure costs time/control and hazard, although it
provides no Gaussian readout. Sums end at voluntary stopping or death. Assume
the relevant reference expectations are finite when using them as budgets.

Theorem 1. For the complete stopped laws P_theta,P_theta',

 KL(P_theta || P_theta')
 = (theta-theta')^2/(2*sigma^2) * E_theta[E_obs]
 <= (theta-theta')^2/(2*sigma^2) * E_theta[E_att].                (1)

Proof for a bounded number of attempts: apply the relative-entropy chain rule
to the full transcript padded by an absorbing stop marker. Given the same past
and chosen a, the action kernel, stop decision and Bernoulli survival law are
identical under both parameters. The death branch contributes zero conditional
KL. The live branch contributes p(a) times the Gaussian KL,
p(a)*(theta-theta')^2*a^(2r)/(2*sigma^2). Sum and take the reference expectation.
Predictability gives E sum J_t*p(a_t)*a_t^(2r)=E[E_obs]. For unbounded stopping,
apply this identity to the successively truncated transcript sigma-fields and
use monotone convergence of relative entropy and of the nonnegative exposure
sum. The last inequality drops B_t. Every budget on the right is under theta,
the SAME reference law as the left. It is not a budget under theta'. QED.

Conditional survival can depend on past Gaussian readouts through adaptive
a_t and stopping. Thus Y's generally cease to be an iid Gaussian sample after
conditioning on source completion. Equation (1) did not condition on completion.

2. Frontier against arbitrary adaptive probing

Write N0=E_theta[M] and h0=E_theta[H], not death probability. If s<=2r, then
a^(2r)<=a^s on (0,1], and

 E_theta[E_att] <= h0/kappa.                                  (2)

If s>2r, put q=2r/s in (0,1). Holder on the measure
mu(domega,t)=P_theta(domega)*J_t(omega), or equivalently on the stopped
probability-times-step space, gives

 E_theta[E_att]
 <= N0^(1-q)*(h0/kappa)^q,
 E_theta[E_att] <= N0.                                        (3)

This covers random exposure counts, arbitrary adaptive amplitudes and stopping.
Pathwise caps M<=N and H<=h imply N0<=N,h0<=h, but are not required for (2)-(3).
Actual risk must still be accounted separately: expected additive hazard is a
resource, not identical to the probability of at least one irreversible event.

3. Reliability and precision lower bound

Require a procedure to complete alive AND output |theta_hat-theta|<=epsilon
with probability at least1-gamma for every theta in [-1,1], gamma<1/2 and
epsilon<1. Death and nontermination count as failure. Suppose it satisfies
the reference budgets N0<=N,h0<=h at each parameter, or at the reference of
the following two-point test.

Let theta0=-1,theta1=-1+d for 2epsilon<d<=2. The events that an estimate is
within epsilon of these two values are disjoint. Declaring theta0 exactly when
the procedure succeeds within epsilon of theta0 defines a test with errors
<=gamma under both parameters, even if every death is declared theta1. Data
processing therefore gives

 KL(P_theta0 || P_theta1) >= k_gamma,
 k_gamma=(1-2gamma)*log((1-gamma)/gamma).                       (4)

Combine (1)-(4), then take d down to2epsilon. With

       Q= sigma^2*k_gamma/(2*epsilon^2),

every such procedure must satisfy

 s<=2r:  h/kappa >= Q;                                       (5)

 s>2r:   N>=Q and
          N>=(kappa/h)^(2r/(s-2r))*Q^(s/(s-2r)).               (6)

The lower bound concerns reliable precision under the stated Gaussian/hazard
interface and charged source budgets. It is not an all-physics information
bound and does not constrain a different live observation law.

4. Achievable constant-amplitude calibration gate

Choose a finite committed N and constant a in (0,1]. Let

 E0=2*sigma^2*log(2/alpha)/epsilon^2,
 a=(E0/N)^(1/(2r)), N>=E0.

If all N exposures complete alive, return theta_hat=mean(Y)/a^r and interval
I=[theta_hat-epsilon,theta_hat+epsilon] intersect [-1,1]. If this intersection
is empty, report FAIL; do not emit it as a certificate. An empty interval can
occur only on the statistical error event under the stipulated model. Death
or failure to finish emits no usable calibration.

The nonadaptive fixed design makes survival independent of all Gaussian
readouts. Conditional on completion the estimate is Normal(theta,sigma^2/E0),
and P(|theta_hat-theta|>epsilon | completion)<=alpha. Completion has probability

 p_complete=exp(-H_design),
 H_design=kappa*E0^(s/(2r))*N^(1-s/(2r)).

Thus the UNCONDITIONAL completion-and-coverage probability is at least

             exp(-H_design)*(1-alpha).                       (7)

If s>2r, choose the integer

 N=ceil(max{E0,(kappa/h)^(2r/(s-2r))*E0^(s/(s-2r))}).

Since the power of N in H_design is negative, rounding N upward preserves
H_design<=h. This matches the epsilon, time and hazard exponents of (6), up to
confidence constants. For desired overall failure gamma, for example choose
alpha=gamma/2 and h<=-log(1-gamma/2). Then (7)>=1-gamma.

Weak probing is not free: N diverges like epsilon^(-2s/(s-2r)) at fixed h.
The design uses an ordinary Gaussian estimator and known constrained design;
the content is the resource frontier and its killed-law interpretation.

The pre-censoring exposure upper bound in (3) is attained by constant amplitudes
for fixed N,H. The actual killed-law FI is smaller, since fatal/foregone probes
yield no Gaussian readout. Equation (7), rather than a claim of exact FI
attainment after censoring, supplies the positive guarantee.

For a>0 fixed, expected ATTEMPTS until N completions or death are
 sum_(j=0)^(N-1) p(a)^j=(1-p(a)^N)/(1-p(a)). Expected successful readouts are
 p(a) times that sum. Attempted expected hazard equals kappa*a^s times that
attempt count. Successful runs take all N time units; early source failure
does not create usable learned knowledge.

5. General resource geometry and physical-interface limits

The power exponents summarize a more general problem. If a primitive's
conditional local KL is at most (theta-theta')^2*i(a)/2 and additive hazard is
d(a), then expected occupation measures of ANY adaptive policy obey the same
linear count/hazard constraints as randomized open-loop designs. The largest
possible information is bounded by the upper concave envelope of the curve
(d(a),i(a)), times the allowed exposure count. The power-law curve is concave
when s>2r and convex when s<2r. This is standard constrained experimental-design
geometry; adaptivity does not bypass that resource upper bound. No new theorem
of optimal-design priority is asserted.

The information order is not always twice the order of the mean signal. If
noise variance scales with amplitude or the count law becomes Poisson at low
dose, the conditional KL order must be recomputed. Renaming the amplitude
cannot itself alter the information-versus-hazard geometry.

A finite lower amplitude a_min>0 implies, for s>2r,
 E_att <= a_min^(2r-s)*H/kappa, so arbitrary precision at fixed hazard fails.
A per-attempt hazard floor d0>0 implies M<=h/d0 under a pathwise budget and
also destroys the diverging-duration weak limit. Known command resolution is
not proof of known actual amplitude: an unknown fixed gain g makes the live
mean theta*(g*a)^r and identifies only theta*g^r unless that gain is calibrated
or otherwise constrained. Source amplitudes, noise and hazard laws are not
learned merely by surviving this protocol.

6. Consequence for further experimental design

The acquired interval is reusable: for any actual later experiment with a
separately trusted risk envelope h_e(theta,u), choose a control only if
sup_(theta in I) h_e(theta,u) is below its remaining budget. On the source
coverage event, that warranty holds for every later control selected from the
same interval, including data-dependent choices; conditional hazard/union
accounting must still charge every attempted later experiment. Recalibration
at adaptively chosen checkpoints needs simultaneous/sequential confidence, not
repeated unadjusted fixed-sample intervals. The opening supplies no real device
with such an envelope and makes no manufactured downstream benchmark claim.
