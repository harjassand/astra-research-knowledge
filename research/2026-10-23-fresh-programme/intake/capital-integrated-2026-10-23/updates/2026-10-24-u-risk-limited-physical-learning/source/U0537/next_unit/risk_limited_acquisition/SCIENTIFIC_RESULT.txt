The substantive result

For one continuously used apparatus, an attempted probe of predictable strength
a in (0,1] first survives with probability exp(-kappa*a^s); only a survivor yields
Y=theta*a^r+Normal(0,sigma^2). Death is observed, irreversible and occurs at most
once. Response/noise/hazard laws and actual amplitude are declared prior premises.
The actual theta is acquired by paid live readouts. There is no reset, discarded
failed-run pool, or free conditional-survivor validation.

For every adaptive policy/stopping rule, full stopped-law KL is exactly
(theta-theta')^2/(2sigma^2) times expected observed exposure E=sum_survivors a^(2r).
Root's material refinement replaces additive hazard by ACTUAL death probability
rho: exp(-h)*h<=1-exp(-h) and the single fatal-event compensator imply

 expected sum_survivors a^s <=rho/kappa.

Writing c for expected successful readout count, Holder gives

 s<=2r:  E[E]<=rho/kappa;
 s>2r:   E[E]<=min{c,c^(1-2r/s)*(rho/kappa)^(2r/s)}.

Uniform joint completion-alive/epsilon-accuracy>=1-gamma therefore requires,
with Q=sigma^2*kl(1-gamma||gamma)/(2epsilon^2),

 s<=2r: rho/kappa>=Q;
 s>2r:  c>=Q and c>=(kappa/rho)^(2r/(s-2r))*Q^(s/(s-2r)).

The same duration lower bound holds for attempted exposures, which are >=c.
This is a converse against arbitrary adaptive probing at this interface.
Constant-amplitude, fixed-horizon Gaussian calibration matches its exponents:
joint source survival/coverage>=(1-rho)*(1-alpha), with duration of order
epsilon^(-2s/(s-2r)) for s>2r at fixed positive risk. It uses an ordinary Gaussian
estimator and constrained experimental design. It trades risk for diverging
time; it does not supply free information or show a new faster estimator.

The second result separates live knowledge from destructive validation. If
permissible and impermissible worlds have identical conditional live-output
kernels, any permission issued alive with probability>=1-beta in the former
and <=alpha in the latter forces source death>=1-beta-alpha in the latter.
No hazard ordering is needed. Different immortal live laws add a total-variation
term; their KL cannot be charged under a different killed reference by mistake.

Acquired calibration can license later experiments whose trusted risk envelope
is uniformly bounded on the returned interval. If source joint completion and
coverage is g and every compatible later path has hazard<=b, total valid later
survival is >=g*exp(-b). An unconditional confidence error alpha and completion
c imply only (c-alpha)*exp(-b); the conditional product c*(1-alpha) needs an
actual conditional coverage guarantee such as the fixed-design independent case.
No downstream task was manufactured or measured to make this transfer look useful.
