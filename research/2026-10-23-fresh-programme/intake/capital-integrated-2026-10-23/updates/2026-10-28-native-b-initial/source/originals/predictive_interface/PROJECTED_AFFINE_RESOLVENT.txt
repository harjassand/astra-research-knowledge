A FINITE VALUE REPRESENTATION OF THE AFFINE PROJECTED CORRECTION
27 October 2026. Continuation of AFFINE_NEWTON_INVERSE_AUDIT.txt.

STATUS. In d=1 the source-coupled pointwise Newton inverse is unbounded, but
its projection onto the Gaussian output admits a bounded inverse and a finite,
positive stochastic implementation with one value of the prescribed residual R.
This is NOT yet a legal g-value compiler: arbitrary Stein residual values are
not available under N687. It is a precise missing-interface construction.

1. PROJECTED INVERSE WITH SOURCE CENTERING

Let X=lambda G+aZ, Y=aG-lambda Z, a=sqrt(1-lambda^2)>0. X,Y are independent
standard normals. Gaussian derivatives and divergences rotate:
 partial_Z=a partial_X-lambda partial_Y,
 delta_Z=a delta_X-lambda delta_Y,
 partial_G=lambda partial_X+a partial_Y.
The affine pointwise Newton operator from the audit is
 A=a(N_Z+2)+lambda delta_Z partial_G
   =delta_Z partial_X+2a
   =a(N_X+2)-lambda delta_Y partial_X.                    (1)

For R in L2(gamma_2), define r(X)=E[R(G,Z)|X] and
 j(X)=-a^(-1)(N_X+2)^(-1)r(X), J=j(X), u=delta_Z J.        (2)
Then
 E[A J|X]=-r(X),     E[u|G]=0.                            (3)
The Y term in (1) has zero conditional expectation. Source-centering follows
from Gaussian divergence in Z, not merely from an unconditional mean bound.

The inverse is bounded:
 ||J||_2 <= ||r||_2/(2a) <= ||R||_2/(2a).                 (4)
Writing r=sum r_m e_m(X) gives
 u=a delta_X j-lambda Yj,
 ||u||_2^2=sum_m (a^2 m+1)|r_m|^2/[a^2(m+2)^2]
           <= ||r||_2^2/(4a^2).                         (5)
The scalar coefficient is decreasing for m>=0 when 0<a<=1, so its maximum
is 1/(4a^2). These statements extend from polynomials by L2 continuity and
weak divergence. Equation (3) is output/Stein-test projection, not pointwise
cancellation of the unprojected residual.

At an exact affine Gaussian an actual valid L2 Stein residual has r=0.
Thus (2) is a linear right inverse for prescribed projected forcing, not a
claim that an exact Gaussian needs correction. Actual nonaffine perturbations
produce nonzero first-order forcing, as the audit addendum explains.

2. ONE-VALUE STOCHASTIC IMPLEMENTATION; NO CONDITIONAL-MEAN QUERY

Assume for this section that a residual value R(g,z) can be computed. Draw
 theta uniformly on [0,pi/2], V,W independent standard normals.
Set s=sin(theta), c=cos(theta), and
 x'=sX+cV,
 g'=lambda x'+aW, z'=a x'-lambda W.
Evaluate R(g',z') once and return
 U=-(pi/(2a)) s(cZ-a sV) R(g',z').                       (6)

The angle need not be an extra exact-uniform primitive. Draw independent
standard normals P,Q and set s=|P|/sqrt(P^2+Q^2), c=|Q|/sqrt(P^2+Q^2).
Their first-quadrant angle is uniform. Define any value on the null event
P=Q=0. This uses four fresh Gaussian seeds P,Q,V,W and scalar arithmetic.

EXACT IDENTITIES:
 E[U|G,Z]=u(G,Z),
 E[U|G]=0,
 E[U^2]=pi^2(1+3a^2)/(32a^2) ||R||_2^2.                 (7)

FIRST IDENTITY. The number-operator resolvent is
 (N+2)^(-1)r(x)=integral_0^1 t E[r(tx+sqrt(1-t^2)V)] dt.
Substitute t=sin(theta):
 j(x)=-a^(-1) integral_0^(pi/2) s c E[R(g',z')] dtheta.
Gaussian integration by parts in V gives
 j'(x)=-a^(-1) integral_0^(pi/2) s^2 E[V R(g',z')] dtheta.
Thus Zj(X)-a j'(X) equals the auxiliary expectation of (6).
Smooth approximation justifies the identities first on smooth residuals.
The exact L2 bound below extends them to every L2 residual.

SOURCE CENTERING. Conditional on G and theta, the two Gaussian combinations
 cZ-a sV  and  s aZ+cV
have covariance csa-asc=0 and are independent. The first has mean zero, and
R(g',z') depends only on the second, G and W. Therefore
 E[U|G,theta]=0.
This is valid despite the source correlation of X and Z; no independent-source
replacement has been made.

SECOND MOMENT. Unconditionally x' is standard normal, independent of W. The
coefficient cZ-a sV is independent of (x',W), again by zero covariance, and
has variance c^2+a^2s^2. Hence (g',z') is product standard Gaussian independent
of that coefficient. For each theta,
 E[U^2|theta]=(pi^2/(4a^2))s^2(c^2+a^2s^2)||R||_2^2.
Uniform-theta averages are E[s^2c^2]=1/8 and E[s^4]=3/8. This proves the
constant in (7) exactly. It is not a Monte Carlo estimate.

Sampling t with density 2t in a naive Bismut estimator would have infinite
second moment at t=1. Uniform theta absorbs that endpoint singularity and
gives the finite constant above.

3. POSITIVITY AND FULL-LAW GAUSSIAN-RESERVE REMAINDER

For every eta, X+eta U is an actual positive law with mean equal to E X and
conditional source mean lambda G. Add an independent reserve tau B, B standard
normal and tau>0. Let phi_tau denote its density and define the signed
first-order approximation
 q_eta(y)=E[phi_tau(y-X)-eta u phi_tau'(y-X)].
Then
 (1/2)||density(X+eta U+tau B)-q_eta||_1
       <= eta^2 E[U^2] phi(1)/tau^2,                      (8)
where phi(1)=(2pi)^(-1/2)exp(-1/2). No positivity of q_eta is asserted.

PROOF. Taylor's integral remainder is
 phi_tau(t-eta U)-phi_tau(t)+eta U phi_tau'(t)
  =eta^2 U^2 integral_0^1(1-v)phi_tau''(t-v eta U)dv.
Its expected L1 norm is at most eta^2 E[U^2]||phi_tau''||_1/2.
The identity ||phi_tau''||_1=E|B^2-1|/tau^2=4phi(1)/tau^2 and the factor
1/2 for TV give (8). This is a full-density theorem, not a moment/KS test.

The deterministic-mean and stochastic updates have the same first-order term:
 TV(Law(X+eta U+tau B),Law(X+eta u+tau B))
 <= eta^2 phi(1)(E[U^2]+E[u^2])/tau^2
 <= 2eta^2 phi(1)E[U^2]/tau^2.                            (9)
The reserve is a real variance resource. It must fit within the fixed target
covariance and cannot silently be added after the construction.

4. REMAINING INTERFACE AND COST

Equation (6) uses one R VALUE, not necessarily one original g value. Generic
Stein residuals contain derivatives and a source Stein kernel. This file does
not manufacture them from the permitted g oracle. With accessible R it uses
one residual evaluation, four extra Gaussian scalar seeds P,Q,V,W, and O(1)
real arithmetic including a square root. The scalar constant pi and rounded
arithmetic need ordinary precision accounting for a finite-bit implementation;
no complete bit-complexity certificate is supplied here.

There is also no claim that this affine projected inverse uniformly controls
nonlinear changes of X or quadratically reduces the actual target-law defect.
The positive update and its full-law remainder are valid under the stated
inputs. The next construction must acquire a finite-cost unbiased estimator of
the needed residual, or replace it with a direct paired-g-value identity, while
preserving these correlations, variance bounds and target-noise accounting.
