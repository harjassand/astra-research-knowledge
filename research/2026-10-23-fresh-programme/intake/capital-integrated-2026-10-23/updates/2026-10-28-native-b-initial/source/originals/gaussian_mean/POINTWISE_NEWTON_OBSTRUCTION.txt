POINTWISE NEWTON FAILURE AND A LAW-LEVEL REPLACEMENT
27 October 2026. Derived from the supplied frozen scientific state.

STATUS

This is a mathematical obstruction to the unrestricted pointwise L2 Newton
interface in S3, Sections 17-18, together with a positive, finite value-query
replacement for a restricted first-order operation. It is not a lower bound
for N687, not a completed polylogarithmic Gaussian-mean sampler, and not an
externally validated or historically novel result. The supplied S1-S12 files
are unchanged; all twelve supplied hashes were checked.

The decision-changing conclusion is stronger than observing that K D_G is an
unbounded perturbation: even at an exactly Gaussian AFFINE channel, the full
pointwise linearized equation has no bounded right inverse in Gaussian L2.
Some bounded C-infinity residuals have no L2 solution. An actual uniformly
Lipschitz conservative high-frequency perturbation produces the same bad
first-order forcing. Solving only the OUTPUT-LAW equation avoids this
amplification. That change preserves positivity and exact mean preservation
through a paired-replica update; acquiring a generic corrective direction at
the required query cost remains open.

1. THE SCALAR AFFINE CHANNEL ALREADY DISCRIMINATES THE INTERFACE

Let G,Z be independent standard normal variables, 0<lambda<1, and
  a=sqrt(1-lambda^2), X=lambda G+a Z.
Thus X is exactly standard normal. This comes from the legal gradient
g(x)=lambda x, F(x)=lambda x^2/2; its canonical source Stein kernel is
K=lambda. No difficulty in sampling this particular target is asserted.

Use Gaussian divergence delta_Z=Z-partial_Z and N_Z=delta_Z partial_Z.
The S3 pointwise Newton architecture restricts a correction u to
  delta_Z J=u.
This implies E[u|G]=0 for L2-domain fields. In dimension one there is no
skew-matrix gauge left. The kernel of delta_Z in L2(gamma_Z) is zero:
distributionally its solutions are c exp(Z^2/2), which are not in L2 unless
c=0. Thus J is unique once u is specified.

At this affine channel, the exact S3 linearization is
  L(u,J)=lambda partial_G u+a partial_Z u+a J.
Substituting u=delta_Z J and using partial_Z delta_Z=N_Z+1 gives
  A J := [a(N_Z+2)+lambda delta_Z partial_G]J = -R.       (1)
This calculation includes both copies of the order-one Gaussian baseline.
The '+2' is essential.

2. EXACT INVERSE ON EACH CHAOS AND EXPONENTIAL AMPLIFICATION

Let e_n=He_n/sqrt(n!) be normalized probabilists' Hermite polynomials.
For forcing R=e_n(G), seek J in total Gaussian chaos n:
  J=sum_(j=0)^n c_j e_(n-j)(G)e_j(Z).
Equation (1) is triangular:
  2a c_0=-1,
  a(j+2)c_j+lambda sqrt((n-j+1)j)c_(j-1)=0.
Its unique solution is
  c_j=(-1)^(j+1) a^-1 (lambda/a)^j sqrt(binomial(n,j))
                   /[(j+1)(j+2)].                         (2)

Since u=delta_Z J, orthogonality gives the EXACT expression
  ||partial_Z u||_2^2
   =a^-2 sum_(j=0)^n binomial(n,j)(lambda^2/a^2)^j/(j+2)^2.
Using (j+2)<=n+2 and 1+lambda^2/a^2=a^-2,
  ||partial_Z u||_2 >= a^(-n-1)/(n+2).                    (3)
Similarly
  ||J||_2 >= a^(-n-1)/[(n+1)(n+2)],
  ||u||_2 >= a^(-n-1)/[sqrt(n+1)(n+2)].                  (4)
The input R has norm one. For EVERY nonzero lambda, these grow exponentially
in n. This is not merely failure of one Neumann-series proof: each finite
chaos block is invertible, so any L2 right inverse must agree with (2).

For a distributional L2 solution, testing against finite Hermite products
isolates the same total-chaos block. The operator preserves total degree;
other degrees cannot cancel this block. Hence the obstruction applies to any
L2 solution in the natural weak domain, not just polynomial ansatzes.

Example: lambda=1/2, a=sqrt(3)/2, n=100 gives
||partial_Z u|| approximately 7.87e4. At n=300 it is about 8.38e16.
The checked exact formula, not these rounded values, is the evidence.

3. SHARP SOURCE-SMOOTHING THRESHOLD FOR THIS RESTRICTION

For a SOURCE-ONLY R(G), let P_rho be the OU semigroup, P_rho e_n=rho^n e_n.
Apply (1) to forcing P_rho R. On the source-only subspace,
  rho<=a => ||partial_Z u||_2 <= (1/(2a)) ||R||_2.       (5)
Indeed (j+2)^-2<=1/4 makes the squared norm in degree n at most
  rho^(2n) a^(-2n-2)/4 <=1/(4a^2).
Different total degrees are orthogonal. If rho>a, (3) diverges after
multiplication by rho^n, so no bounded map exists. Thus rho=a is the sharp
threshold for this specified source-only norm comparison.

This is not a bound on arbitrary residuals depending on both G and Z, nor
a completed nonlinear Newton theorem. It identifies an exact smoothing
budget consumed by the problematic pointwise inverse. It explains why a
source-smoothed analytic space can evade (3), while ordinary L2 cannot.

4. BOUNDED SMOOTH FORCINGS CAN HAVE NO L2 SOLUTION

Put r=lambda/a. For the complex forcing exp(ikG), the solution, initially
understood through finite Hermite projections, has the explicit form
  J_k(G,Z)=-(1/a) exp(ikG)
      integral_0^1 t exp[-ik r(1-t)Z+k^2 r^2(1-t)^2/2] dt.   (6)
This follows either by inserting the Hermite generating function in (2), or
by applying A and integrating by parts in t. In particular
  ||J_k||_2^2=(1/a^2) integral_0^1 integral_0^1
                  ts exp[k^2 r^2(1-t)(1-s)] dt ds.          (7)
For cos(kG), the squared norm is
  (1/(2a^2)) integral integral ts
    {exp[k^2 r^2(1-t)(1-s)]
     +exp[-2k^2-k^2 r^2(1-t)(1-s)]} dt ds.                (8)
Both terms are nonnegative. If K=k^2 r^2>=2, restrict t,s to [0,1/K]:
  ||J_cos(kG)||_2^2 >= exp(K-2)/(8a^2 K^4).              (9)
Thus a bounded smooth frequency-k forcing already demands exponentially
large correction norm, of order at least exp(r^2 k^2/2)/k^4.

Now choose 0<beta<r^2/2 and
  R_beta(G)=sum_(m>=1) exp(-beta m^2) cos(mG).             (10)
This is a bounded C-infinity function with every derivative bounded; it is
even entire. Its uniform norm can be made arbitrarily small by multiplication
by a positive scalar. For any even Hermite degree n, the coefficients of
all summands in (10) have the same sign (-1)^(n/2). Formula (2) preserves
that agreement in each output coefficient. Therefore the norm of any
putative solution dominates exp(-beta m^2)||J_cos(mG)||_2 for every m.
By (9) these lower bounds diverge. No L2 solution of (1) exists for (10).
This proves non-surjectivity on a class much smaller than generic L2.

5. ACTUAL CONSERVATIVE C^{1,1} SOURCE PERTURBATIONS

The obstruction is not based on a fictitious stochastic matrix oracle.
Consider, for fixed small eta and each integer k>=1,
  g_(eta,k)(x)=lambda x+eta sin(kx)/k,
  F_(eta,k)(x)=lambda x^2/2-eta cos(kx)/k^2.              (11)
These are C-infinity gradients, globally (lambda+|eta|)-Lipschitz. If
|eta|<lambda their potentials are strongly convex, although convexity is
unnecessary. Their Gaussian means are zero by oddness.

Let f_k=sin(kx)/k and let K_f be its canonical Gaussian Stein kernel,
delta_G K_f=f_k, equivalently K_f=int_0^1 P_t f_k' dt.
The uncorrected positive channel
  X_eta=g_(eta,k)(G)+aZ
has a valid unconditioned Stein coefficient
  T_eta=(lambda+eta K_f)(lambda+eta f_k')+a^2.
Hence its actual first-order residual is
  R_1=lambda(f_k'+K_f).                                (12)
For every Hermite degree n, (K_f)_n=(f_k')_n/(n+1).
Thus the forcing coefficients in (12) are those of cos(kG), multiplied by
lambda(1+1/(n+1)). There is no cancellation. The norm of its solution of
the affine pointwise Newton equation is at least lambda times (9)'s bound
after taking the square root of that squared-norm bound.

The sources (11) remain uniformly Lipschitz and approach the affine source
uniformly as k grows. Yet the first pointwise correction becomes unstable.
Meanwhile a direct conditional-Gaussian coupling gives
  TV(Law(X_eta),gamma_1) <= |eta|/(k a sqrt(2pi)).        (13)
The OUTPUT LAW approaches the target as the correction representation
becomes badly conditioned. This is why demanding pointwise residual
cancellation is scientifically inappropriate without a smoothing/range
restriction. It tries to correct source detail that the output suppresses.

Replacing f_k by f_beta=sum exp(-beta m^2)sin(mx)/m gives a bounded,
globally Lipschitz, C-infinity perturbation of the same affine field. The
first-order residual (12) then has no L2 inverse by Section 4. This refutes
a uniformly L2-controlled differentiable pointwise Newton theorem for all
such perturbations. It does NOT rule out other representations, weaker
domains, non-differentiable updates, or a theorem on a smaller smoothed
residual range. It is not a target-sampling lower bound.

6. REPLACE SOURCE-POINTWISE CANCELLATION BY OUTPUT-LAW CANCELLATION

Return to X=lambda G+aZ and make an exchangeable replica
  X'=lambda G+aZ',
where Z' is independent. X and X' have correlation lambda^2. For any
explicit square-integrable function w define
  D=w(X)-w(X').                                         (14)
This is a finite positive-program ingredient: it uses only Gaussian seeds
and evaluations of w. E[D|G]=0 EXACTLY. Thus X+tD preserves the conditional
mean given G on every real t, without acquiring a conditional mean.

Let T w(x)=E[w(X')|X=x]. This is an ANALYSIS operator; for this affine pair
it is explicitly the known OU operator P_(lambda^2). Its eigenvalue on
e_m is lambda^(2m). For any centered desired marginal velocity v,
  w=(I-T)^-1 v
exists, and
  E[D|X]=v(X),
  E D^2=2 <v,(I-T)^-1 v> <= 2||v||_2^2/a^2.           (15)
For a finite Hermite polynomial v, w is obtained by dividing its m-th
coefficient by 1-lambda^(2m). This is an actual finite arithmetic operation.
It does not require a posterior sampler or mean oracle.

For the test forcing R=e_n(G), only
  E[R|X]=lambda^n e_n(X)
enters the scalar Stein identity. The marginal correction velocity is
  v=-lambda^n sqrt(n+1)/(n+2) e_(n+1)(X).                (16)
Indeed, writing S_Q(f)=E[Q f(Q)-f'(Q)], its first variation at a standard
normal with conditional velocity v is
  E[v(X){f(X)+X f'(X)-f''(X)}].
For v=c e_m this equals c(m+1)E[e_m(X)f(X)]. Equation (16) cancels
E[R f'(X)]=lambda^n sqrt(n+1)E[e_(n+1)(X)f(X)].

The paired implementation has
  E D^2=2 lambda^(2n)(n+1)
          /[(n+2)^2(1-lambda^(2n+2))].                 (17)
This DECAYS exponentially with n, whereas (3) grows exponentially. The
forcing here tests a linearized inverse; an arbitrary R is not asserted
to be the actual Stein residual of the already Gaussian affine law.
Section 5 supplies the actual-source perturbation connection.

The independent helper also derived a different projected inverse with one
residual-value call, explicit bounded L2 variance, and no conditional oracle
at the affine reference. Its full proof is in
../predictive_interface/PROJECTED_AFFINE_RESOLVENT.txt. That residual-value
interface is not automatically the allowed original g-value interface.

7. GENERAL NOISY-LIPSCHITZ SOURCES: THE LAW-LEVEL INVERSE HAS A GAP

Let U=h(G), with h:R^m->R^d globally L-Lipschitz, and let
  Y=U+sZ, Y'=U+sZ', s>0,
with independent standard Gaussians. Denote q=Law(Y) and
T w(y)=E[w(Y')|Y=y]. Then T is positive, self-adjoint and Markov on L2(q).
For centered scalar or vector functions,
  0<=T<=(L^2/(L^2+s^2)) I,
  I-T >= c I, c=s^2/(L^2+s^2).                         (18)

Elementary proof for scalar w: write b(G)=E_Z w(h(G)+sZ). Gaussian
Poincare and ||Dh||<=L give
  Var b <= L^2 E||grad E_Z w(U+sZ)||^2.
The first Gaussian-chaos/Bessel inequality conditional on U gives
  s^2||grad E_Z w(U+sZ)||^2 <= Var_Z w(U+sZ).
Thus Var b <=(L^2/s^2)E Var(w(Y)|G). Total variance proves (18).
Approximation extends the argument from smooth bounded w to L2. Summing
coordinates gives the vector form. The conditional-G variance depends
on G only through U, so no invertibility of h is needed.

THIS CONTRACTION IS KNOWN MATHEMATICS. Klartag and Ordentlich, 'The strong
data processing inequality under the heat flow', arXiv:2406.03427v1,
Theorem 1.1, gives eta_chi2<=C_P/(C_P+s^2), with maximal correlation
equivalence. The Lipschitz Gaussian image has C_P<=L^2. The proof above
reconstructs its specialization, and no novelty is claimed for (18).

Consequently every centered v in L2(q) has an abstract solution w=(I-T)^-1v
and the acquired paired difference, IF w is available, satisfies
  E[w(Y)-w(Y')|Y]=v(Y),
  E||w(Y)-w(Y')||^2 <=(2/c)||v||_2^2.                 (19)
These are law-level bounds independent of dimension and source frequency.
They repair the analytical conditioning problem without claiming w is free.

8. FULL-LAW BOUND FOR THE ACTUAL POSITIVE PAIRED UPDATE

Fix any explicit measurable w with E||D||^2 finite, D=w(Y)-w(Y'), and
an independent reserve V~N(0,I_d), tau>0. The executed output is
  O_t=Y+tD+tau V.                                     (20)
It is positive, finite and preserves E[Y|G]. For h=g this uses ONE g-value
query to produce U, two Gaussian replicas, two evaluations of w, and the
reserve. If evaluating w itself queries g, those calls must be added.

Let P_t=Law(O_t), and let L_w be the signed, mass-zero measure with density
  l_w(x)=-E[D dot grad phi_tau(x-Y)].
For signed mass-zero measures use ||nu||TV=(1/2) integral |dnu|. Then
  ||P_t-P_0-t L_w||TV
     <= phi(1) t^2 E||D||^2/tau^2,                    (21)
where phi(1)=exp(-1/2)/sqrt(2pi).

PROOF. Taylor-expand the translated Gaussian density with the integral
second-order remainder. For any fixed vector d,
  integral |D^2 phi_tau(x)[d,d]| dx
      =4 phi(1)||d||^2/tau^2.
The Taylor integral contributes 1/2 and the TV convention contributes 1/2.
Integrate over the ACTUAL joint law of (Y,D). No independence between D
and Y and no Gaussianity of the correction are assumed. QED.

Equivalently, for every bounded test f the first variation is
  (1/2) E[(w(Y)-w(Y')) dot
           (grad(f*phi_tau)(Y)-grad(f*phi_tau)(Y'))].
This is a full-law identity and remainder, not a finite moment test.
The reserve tau changes variance and must be budgeted from the desired
target variance. It cannot be added for free in an N687 algorithm.

With the ideal solution in (19), (21)'s remainder is at most
  2 phi(1) t^2 ||v||_2^2/(c tau^2).
This is quadratic in a GIVEN correction direction. It is NOT yet quadratic
contraction of the Gaussian sampling error: obtaining the right v and w
from values, controlling residual approximation, and preserving the needed
recursive interface remain necessary.

9. THREE-QUERY APPROXIMATE GAUSSIAN FILLING, WITH CONDITIONAL MEAN PRESERVED

Here the corrective direction is actually acquired from original field
values. Suppose a known decomposition is
  g(x)=lambda x+eta f(x), 0<lambda<1, eta>0,
where f:R^d->R^d is M-Lipschitz. This is an ADDITIONAL structural contract.
Equivalently the residual h=g-lambda id is eta M-Lipschitz; the runtime
does not need eta separately. The mean E g(G)=eta E f(G) is not supplied.
Let a=sqrt(1-lambda^2), tau>0, and choose an integer cap N>=0.

Draw independent standard d-dimensional Gaussians G,Z,Z',V and put
  X=lambda G+aZ, X'=lambda G+aZ'.
Choose a capped geometric index J with
  Pr(J=j)=p_j=(1-lambda^2)lambda^(2j), 0<=j<N,
  Pr(J=tail)=lambda^(2N).
If J=tail set D=0. Otherwise draw one fresh standard Gaussian U, set
  r_j=lambda^(2j+1),
  A_j=r_j X+sqrt(1-r_j^2)U,
  B_j=r_j X'+sqrt(1-r_j^2)U,
  D=-[f(A_j)-f(B_j)]/p_j.
Return the POSITIVE random vector
  O=X+eta[f(G)+D]+tau V.                              (22)

Runtime form using only the original g oracle is
  O=g(G)+aZ+tau V
       -[g(A_j)-g(B_j)-lambda(A_j-B_j)]/p_j
on the active branch; the last term is zero on the tail branch.
There are at most THREE whole-vector g queries on EVERY execution. A
fourth optional g(0) query stabilizes arithmetic with arbitrary offsets.
Generate J with at most N scalar Bernoulli trials, continuation probability
lambda^2. Each trial can use three Gaussian scalars C1,C2,C3 and the uniform
(1+C1/sqrt(C1^2+C2^2+C3^2))/2, as in supplied S4. A measure-zero zero
radius receives any fixed convention. No unbounded loop is used.

COST: O(d+N) surrounding real arithmetic and Gaussian draws, O(d) streaming
storage, and at most three g queries. Scalar powers are maintained by
multiplication through the cap. Coefficients can be as large as 1/p_(N-1);
for N=O(log(d/epsilon)) and fixed lambda this is a fixed polynomial in
d/epsilon. A complete finite-bit implementation still needs a common output
rounding convention and primitive-error budget.

EXACT CONDITIONAL-MEAN IDENTITY:
  E[O|G]=lambda G+eta f(G)=g(G).                       (23)
For each J and U, exchangeability of Z,Z' makes E[D|G,J,U]=0. The reserve
also has conditional mean zero. Thus the operation is an approximate
Gaussian MARTINGALE FILLING, retaining the prescribed source conditional
mean. It is not only an unknown-mean estimate plus unrelated Gaussian noise.

EXACT CONDITIONAL FIRST VARIATION:
  E[f(G)+D | X]=P_(lambda^(2N+1)) f(X).                (24)
Proof: E[f(G)|X]=P_lambda f(X). Conditional on X, averaging the j-th
active numerator gives P_(r_j)f(X)-P_(lambda^2 r_j)f(X). Its p_j cancels
the index probability. The finite sum telescopes, proving (24). The common
U in the two endpoints is essential to the pathwise bound below.

PATHWISE AND SECOND-MOMENT CONTROL:
  ||D|| <= M lambda/(1-lambda^2) ||X-X'||,
  E||D||^2 <= 2 M^2 d lambda^2 (1-lambda^(2N))/a^2.
The first bound holds on EVERY active index, since r_j/p_j=lambda/a^2,
and also on the tail branch. The second multiplies the uniform conditional
bound by the active probability 1-lambda^(2N). With mu_f=E f(G), Gaussian
Poincare and E[D|G]=0 give the sharper variance identity/bound
  E||f(G)+D-mu_f||^2
    =E||f(G)-mu_f||^2+E||D||^2
    <= M^2 d [1+2lambda^2(1-lambda^(2N))/a^2].          (25)
No uncontrolled variance from the randomized correction is discarded.

FULL-LAW GUARANTEE FOR THE IMPLEMENTED PROGRAM:
  TV(Law(O),N(eta mu_f,(1+tau^2)I))
   <= eta M sqrt(d) lambda^(2N+1)/(tau sqrt(2pi))
      +phi(1) eta^2 M^2 d
          [1+2lambda^2(1-lambda^(2N))/a^2]/tau^2.      (26)

Proof: after translating both laws by -eta mu_f, write the perturbation
as eta A, A=f(G)+D-mu_f. The reference X is exactly standard Gaussian.
The first-order signed density is -eta E[A dot grad phi_tau(x-X)].
By (24), its density depends on A only through
b_N(X)=P_(lambda^(2N+1))f(X)-mu_f. OU contraction and Poincare give
||b_N||_2<=lambda^(2N+1)M sqrt(d). The L1 norm of a directional Gaussian
first derivative is sqrt(2/pi)||b||/tau, giving the first term of (26).
Equation (21), applied to the ACTUAL A and (25), gives the second. QED.

This supplies a positive, nonlinear, value-only correction with a full-law
bound and deterministic query cap. The geometrically distributed index is
only a bounded-cost selection of ONE secant pair; there is no signed
probability, missing infinite-forest mass or expected-to-worst-case shortcut.
The cap's residual is the explicit lambda^(2N+1) term.

It DOES NOT solve N687. The d eta^2 term can be large, and (26) is not a
quadratic recurrence for the incoming law error. Increasing N does not
remove it. The actual output and its retained sources have extra correlations
that a next stage must account for. There is no proved recursively closed
class, global contraction or subcritical complete compiler.

STRONGEST SIMPLE COMPARISON: if only the mean-Gaussian marginal is required
and the affine part is known, subtract it and output eta f(G)+sqrt(1+tau^2)V.
S5's exact chi-square argument already gives small-eta error
O(eta^2 M^2 sqrt(d)/(1+tau^2)), often BETTER than bound (26), with one g
query. That alternative has conditional mean eta f(G), not g(G); it does not
supply (23). For a known affine g, Gaussian matrix functional calculus can
also give the exact filling under its covariance condition. Brownian/cloud
completion remains the stronger general mathematical comparator under its
actual derivative/nonlocal acquisition contracts. No practical superiority or
historical novelty is claimed for (22). Its useful acquired capability is the
combination of (23), (24), positivity, bounded original queries and (26).

10. A CONCRETE RECURSION FAILURE AND THE NEXT OBLIGATION

Increasing the cap alone cannot give arbitrary accuracy, even on an affine
source. Take f(x)=x, so g(x)=(lambda+eta)x. At every active index,
  D=-lambda(X-X')/a^2=-lambda(Z-Z')/a.
As N tends to infinity the tail disappears and the EXACT output is Gaussian
with variance
  v_1=1+tau^2+eta^2(1+2lambda^2/a^2),
instead of v_0=1+tau^2. This is a real law defect, not merely a loose bound.
In dimension d its exact TV is
  F_chi2_d(b/v_0)-F_chi2_d(b/v_1),
  b=d v_0 v_1 log(v_1/v_0)/(v_1-v_0).                  (27)
This follows from the single crossing of the two radial Gaussian densities.
For fixed nonzero eta it tends to one as d grows, by concentration of
chi-square divided by d. The ordinary exact affine Gaussian completion
removes this error; claiming a new capability from this test case would be
incorrect. Equation (27) establishes the need for a genuine next correction,
even after the first-order source-correlation problem has been solved.

The new step does not automatically preserve a uniform Lipschitz seed map.
For f_k(x)=sin(kx)/k, each f_k is 1-Lipschitz. On active branch j, put
b_j=sqrt(1-r_j^2). The derivative of D with respect to its Gaussian U is
  partial_U D=-(b_j/p_j)[cos(k(r_j X+b_j U))
                         -cos(k(r_j X'+b_j U))].
Because (X,X') has full support for a>0, its supremum can reach
2b_j/p_j. This grows as lambda^(-2j) as the cap increases. A bounded
correction magnitude in (25) is NOT a bound on the Lipschitz constant of
the whole randomized program. The exact affine-reference telescope does not
justify feeding a corrected nonlinear source back through the same proof.

ACQUIRED NOW: equations (14),(20),(22), exact conditional mean preservation,
the full-law Taylor remainder, exact first-order conditional identity, and
deterministic query/arithmetic costs. Companion code checks exact Hermite
algebra, complete one-dimensional densities, and the implemented capped
factory. Finite numerical integration is a diagnostic; (21),(26) are the
analytic certificates. No finite moment or KS test is used as a TV proof.

For nonlinear h, the operator T in Section 7 involves the posterior G|Y=y.
One unconditional pair (Y,Y') is available from one h value, but iterating
the same latent G is NOT a simulation of T^k. It reuses a fixed source
instead of drawing the required posterior refreshes. A conditional
mean/sampler cannot be inferred from (18).

There is an acquired variational form:
  <z,(I-T)w> = (1/2)E[(z(Y)-z(Y')) dot (w(Y)-w(Y'))].
For supplied explicit finite features, entries can be estimated from legal
pairs. Coercivity (18) controls conditioning relative to the true L2(q)
geometry, but finite-feature bias, covariance estimation, feature dimension,
adaptive selection and full-law residual bounds must be paid. No universal
polylog-query representation follows merely from this form.

THE NEXT SCIENTIFIC OBLIGATION is specific: obtain a recursively usable
output-law residual representation for (22), and a positive value-only next
correction whose actual full-law residual is quadratic in a discrepancy
controlled through source reuse. Its dimension dependence must improve on
the d eta^2 remainder, and its complete source-query tree must have fixed
branching at log-log depth or a proved subcritical tail. Polynomial total
arithmetic and the fixed target noise budget remain requirements. The new
norm/representation must tolerate the explicit seed-derivative growth above.

An alternative route is to prove that the residuals generated by a
source-smoothed compiler stay inside a space meeting Section 3's smoothing
budget. That requires a joint acquisition proof, not simply analyticity.
The unrestricted plain-L2 pointwise inverse should no longer be treated as
a plausible missing lemma. The general Gaussian-mean target remains open.
