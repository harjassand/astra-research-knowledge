STATUS AND SCIENTIFIC DECISION

This file establishes an exact full-law confidence geometry for a stationary
rank-two CP subfamily, and a matching-order native-call information lower bound.
It does not solve acquisition of arbitrary rank-r quantum instruments. It
separates avoidable coordinate-norm losses from genuine information cost, and
identifies an exactly optimal discriminating experiment. No historical novelty

S7 already establishes valid signed-coordinate perturbation and composition

1. LEGAL STATIONARY CP FAMILY

The hidden memory is a qubit with computational states 0 and 1. The supplied
family has an unknown scalar p in [0,1]. Each full-process start is state 0.
There are two legal actions:

 C (charge): 0 changes to 1 with probability p; 1 stays 1. Visible outcome 0.
 R (read): report the hidden bit nondestructively.

C has one visible branch with Kraus operators
 sqrt(1-p)|0><0|, sqrt(p)|1><0|, |1><1|.
The two R branches have Kraus operators |0><0| and |1><1|. The outcome sums
are TP; all maps are CP. Every coherence is discarded. This classical subfamily
of the quantum contract suffices for a lower bound on any full-class learner.

For p>0, reachable states span the diagonal two-dimensional space. Preparations
empty and C, and future tests empty and R=1, give
                  H_p = [[1,1],[0,p]].                            (1)
Predictive rank is exactly two. The inverse infinity norm is 1+1/p. Bad
short-core conditioning thus occurs at fixed rank with positive stationary
instruments. The family structure is a SUPPLIED restriction for our upper
bound. It is not learned from finitely many observed zeros. Only the lower
bound uses it merely as a contained subfamily of the full target class.

2. EXACT FULL-LAW DISTANCE, INCLUDING ADAPTIVE RETAINED-MEMORY REUSE

Let W_p be the first successful charge:
 Pr(W_p=j)=p(1-p)^(j-1), j>=1; W_0=infinity.

Sample W_p ONCE per initial start. A read after n charges returns 1 iff n>=W_p.
For any causal classical controller, including arbitrary macros retaining the
same memory, its entire transcript, termination decisions and final bit are a
common measurable function of W_p and independent policy randomness. Drawing a
new W at every macro would incorrectly reset the memory.

Full-process starts are paid acquisition operations, not additional actions in
this future-policy supremum. The future theorem allows C and R (and the optional
S below) within ONE retained-memory episode. A future task containing several
independent fresh starts requires a separate product/composition bound; the
unbounded-horizon radius in (3) cannot be reused unchanged for those episodes.

For 0<=p<=q<=1 and integer H>=0 define
 d_H(p,q)=max_(k=0,...,H) [(1-p)^k-(1-q)^k],                        (2)
with zero difference at k=0. Let d_infinity be the supremum over finite k.

EXACT OPERATIONAL THEOREM:
 sup_pi TV(P_p^pi,P_q^pi) = d_H(p,q),                              (3)
where policies use at most H charges, arbitrary reads and feedback, and retain
memory across all macros. Output means the full transcript, with an optional
final read, not moments. The upper bound is uniform over any common initial
qubit density matrix, since only its initial zero probability contributes.
The supremum is attained from initial state 0. For unbounded charge use,
replace d_H by d_infinity. Arbitrary policies are not promised finite runtime,
but their measurable path laws still obey the data-processing bound.

PROOF. Policies with at most H charges inspect only
W_p^H=min(W_p,H+1), so their TV is at most the TV of these censored variables.
For 0<p<q<1 the point-mass ratio
 Pr(W_q=j)/Pr(W_p=j)=(q/p)[(1-q)/(1-p)]^(j-1)
decreases with j. The mass difference has one sign change, including the
censored tail. Its TV equals the largest cumulative-probability difference:
 max_(k<=H) [Pr(W_q<=k)-Pr(W_p<=k)]=d_H(p,q).
Endpoint cases follow directly or by limits. If k maximizes (2), C^k followed
by one R has Bernoulli means 1-(1-p)^k and 1-(1-q)^k. It attains that TV.
Using W instead of W^H proves the unbounded statement. QED.

COMPUTATION. For 0<p<q<1 put a=1-p, b=1-q. The real function a^t-b^t is
unimodal and its maximum is at
 t*=log(log(b)/log(a))/log(a/b).                                  (4)
Check floor/ceiling of t*, clipped to [0,H], and endpoints. Stable log1p/expm1
are needed for tiny p. Exact finite-H rational evaluation can use rational
powers and binary search on the consecutive-difference sign. No H_p inverse,
hidden-state estimation or conditional-mean oracle is needed.

A useful elementary bound is
 d_infinity(p,q) <= (q-p)/q, 0<p<=q.                               (5)
Couple charges with identical uniforms. Before either first changes, the first
uniform <=q lands in (p,q] with probability (q-p)/q; otherwise they change
together and all future transcripts agree. The exact formula (2), not this
coarser bound, is used in numerical checks.

3. COUNTS TO A POSITIVE CAUSAL REUSABLE PREDICTOR

Fix block length h>=1 before an independent calibration batch. Execute C^h R
in N fresh starts, recording x successes. This is a legal joint-event count,
Bin(N,q_h(p)), where
 q_h(p)=1-(1-p)^h.                                                (6)
No exact mean enters the learner.

Form an exact two-sided binomial confidence interval [L,U] for q_h with failure
probability alpha. For example Clopper-Pearson: for x>0 the lower endpoint
solves Pr_(Bin(N,L))[X>=x]=alpha/2, and L=0 for x=0; for x<N the upper
endpoint solves Pr_(Bin(N,U))[X<=x]=alpha/2, and U=1 for x=N. Transform:
 ell=1-(1-L)^(1/h), u=1-(1-U)^(1/h).                              (7)

With probability >=1-alpha, p lies in [ell,u]. Select any p_hat in this
interval, possibly from these counts. Implement the positive two-state
stochastic model at p_hat, retaining its bit across every macro. On the SAME
confidence event, simultaneously for every subsequent adaptive policy using
at most H charges,
 TV(P_p^pi,P_phat^pi)
   <= max(d_H(ell,p_hat),d_H(p_hat,u)).                            (8)

The exact confidence-set diameter is d_H(ell,u). For each fixed k the survival
probability is monotone in p, so (2) proves both endpoint claims. No union bound
over future policies or transcript outcomes appears. A minimax center can be
found by scalar bisection equating the two endpoint distances where continuous.

If ell=0<u, the UNBOUNDED diameter is one. Finite zero-success data cannot
exclude zero versus positive hazard in an unrestricted-horizon claim. Finite-H
diameter 1-(1-u)^H can still be useful. Model positivity does not create absent
information.

COSTS. Calibration pays N full starts, Nh charges, N reads, and all unsuccessful
episodes. Streaming storage is O(log N+log h) bits for counts/metadata. The
predictor stores a bit and its parameter representation. Every simulated
charge/read or justified geometric jump is charged. Exact interval arithmetic
can use rational binomial tails and outward rational bisection, polynomial
bit complexity in N,h,b for b-bit accuracy. No near-linear large-N arithmetic
claim is made. Included large-count code uses floating special functions and
checks tail residuals; it is not a validated-arithmetic certificate.

Data-dependent h requires an independent pilot, simultaneous intervals over a
declared pool, or a valid sequential confidence sequence. Pilot calls are paid.

4. PAID INFORMATION LOWER BOUND

Allow ANY adaptive acquisition with arbitrary C/R scheduling, full starts,
outcomes, stopping rules and computation. If B bounds total charge calls on
every execution, the observable-data laws satisfy
 KL(D_p || D_q) <= B kl(Ber(p) || Ber(q)).                         (9)
The expected-budget version replaces B by E_p[B] under integrability.

PROOF. Reveal the hidden bit only to a proof observer. Policy/reset kernels
have no parameter-dependent KL. At state one a charge is deterministic under
both models; at zero it contributes binary KL. Reads deterministically expose
the bit. Apply the chain rule and data processing back to the legal transcript.
Truncation gives the integrable expected-budget case. The learner never
receives these proof-only hidden observations. QED.

Set q=(1+eta)p with 0<p<=1/4, 0<eta<=1. The binary chi-square bound gives
 kl(p||q) <= (q-p)^2/[q(1-q)] <= 2 eta^2 p.                        (10)

Take H=floor(1/p). Since H>=(1-p)/p,
 (1-p)^H >= (1-p)^(1/p) >= (3/4)^4,
 [(1-q)/(1-p)]^H <= exp[-H eta p/(1-p)] <= exp(-eta).
Therefore
 d_H(p,q) >= c0 eta,
 c0=(81/256)(1-exp(-1)) > 0.20.                                  (11)
Here 1-exp(-eta)>=(1-exp(-1))eta on [0,1]. The used monotonicity of
(1-p)^(1/p) follows by differentiating log(1-p)/p.

Suppose an acquired data structure promises epsilon-accurate future laws,
with probability >=1-alpha under either model, alpha<1/2, c0 eta>2epsilon.
This includes predicting the distinguishing event C^H R=1. Valid output sets
under p,q are disjoint; choosing the closer alternative yields a test with
both errors <=alpha. Binary data processing then implies
 KL(D_p||D_q) >= (1-2alpha)log[(1-alpha)/alpha].                    (12)

Combining (9)-(12):
 B >= (1-2alpha)log[(1-alpha)/alpha]/(2 eta^2 p).
For eta=3epsilon/c0<=1,
 B >= c0^2(1-2alpha)log[(1-alpha)/alpha]
                         /(18 p epsilon^2).                      (13)

Thus the paid-call requirement is Omega(p^-1 epsilon^-2 log(1/alpha)) for
horizon order p^-1, already at known rank two. It holds even when the learner
is told the whole family. It does NOT lower-bound N687's single output sample,
which has a different contract.

WELL-CONDITIONED-CORE VARIANT. Add the legal known action S which sets the
memory to 1, with CP channel T_S(X)=Tr(X)|1><1| and visible outcome 0. The
core using preparations empty,S and tests empty,R=1 is exactly
[[1,1],[0,1]], with inverse infinity norm 2, independent of p. S contributes
zero parameter KL, so (9)-(13) are unchanged. It does not improve discrimination
beyond (2): the process remains a common channel of W_p, with S forcing the
bit to 1 thereafter. The time-scale information obstruction therefore persists
with a uniformly well-conditioned core. It is not solely a singular-core bound.

MATCHING-ORDER UPPER REGIME. If a supplied or paid pilot scale chooses h with
hp between fixed positive constants, then q_h stays away from zero and one.
N=O(epsilon^-2 log(1/alpha)) block episodes estimate q_h to O(epsilon);
(7) estimates p to relative O(epsilon). Equations (5),(8) give full adaptive
law error O(epsilon), with Nh=O(p^-1 epsilon^-2 log(1/alpha)) charges. Constants
depend on the declared scale interval. The value of h is not free. Fixed h=1
with variance-sensitive binomial intervals has the same charge order but may
use more full-process starts.

5. THE REPRESENTATION CHANGE AND ITS LIMIT

The representation is a confidence interval in survival probabilities with
EXACT policy-law diameter, rather than a box around H_z H^-1. It explicitly
preserves positivity, causality and memory. Time scale is retained: using a
long preparation C^h gives core [[1,1],[0,q_h(p)]], well-conditioned for hp
order one, but costs h physical calls. Coordinates cannot erase that cost.

For compatible hazards ell,u, the most discriminating next experiment is
C^k R with k maximizing (2). This is an actual acquired-observation decision,
not an unspecified larger shot budget. It may take long physical time despite
one episode and one final read.

The upper theorem relies on the supplied absorbing one-parameter family.
Rank two alone does not imply it. An unmodelled return transition, drift,
imperfect sensor or extra mode invalidates the geometry unless incorporated
and paid for. It is not a universal N952 solution.

7. EXACT REMAINING OBLIGATION

For generic CP instruments, replace this scalar survival family by a COMPUTABLE
set of positive causal processes compatible with the legal joint-event counts.
Then either bound its retained-memory policy-law diameter nonvacuously at the
stated horizon/budget, or exhibit two compatible processes and an affordable
distinguishing policy. An uncomputed supremum over models is not an algorithm.
A convex outer relaxation may be sound but vacuous. Finite-horizon decision-tree
enumeration is exponential and does not supply scalability.

The rank-four summary alone does not determine that diameter. Its raw joint
counts and constraints would be needed to resolve that particular experiment,
but this task requests no repackaging or additional access. This independent
attack yields an exact positive subfamily and necessary information/cost
calibration, not the desired general reusable-acquisition breakthrough.
