The divergence distinction is essential. N676 uses `D2(A||B)=log Tr(A² B^-1)` and `G_A(H)=Tr(H A^-1 H)`; its state-pair expansion is exactly quadratic. Q28's target is Umegaki relative entropy, whose local metric is BKM. SLD uses `Omega_A(H)=(AH+HA)/2`; it is another metric again. N676 explicitly distinguishes its endpoint from SLD and gives a rational instrument separating their complete orders (lines 373–406). Its fixed-generator universality statement also warns that equality of qualitative preorders is not equality of numerical coefficients (lines 192–201). Renaming these objects would not establish Q28's Umegaki cutoff. N676's definition of complete divergence order also imposes no reference-marginal equality, whereas N965/Q28 explicitly distinguish equal-reference and reference-subtracted comparisons.

Q28's retained construction in [finite_reference_proof.md §§2–5](../../review_material/Q28/stage2/finite_reference_proof.md) makes the narrower transfer concrete: the common gauge yields zero-sum vector witnesses; retaining the tangent vectors and both channels' inverse-applied output vectors preserves both free forms; active blocks plus one vacuum realize their ratios with faithful equal-marginal states.
