ASTRA: substantive research-method comparison, 26 October 2026

Human authorization: perform an original experiment with subagents to find methods that improve consequential discovery; do not count unbuilt interfaces, weak comparisons, restricted proxy results or persuasive presentation as the civilisation-level goal.

Question: do added source-grounded construction instructions, and then added independent reformulation, improve actual scientific constructions over competent unrestricted expert research? This is a diagnostic of instruction packages. It is not a test of optimal swarm size or proof of a general discovery law.

Tasks: admit up to three concrete tasks in specimen interrogation, reusable law/representation discovery and programmable nonliving matter. A separate investigator prepares each common evidence packet and executes a strong ordinary baseline. Admit a task BEFORE any trial only if it names a real consequential bottleneck, accessible inputs/operations, an executable incumbent or exact established construction, a meaningful fixed target, and a fair way to check an attempted advance. Reject solved, unidentifiable or inaccessible tasks rather than substituting a trivial exercise. Distinguish source-grounded simulation, physical observations and mathematical proof. Task admission and any exclusions will be recorded before trial outcomes.

Three nested treatments: A competent unrestricted expert baseline; B that same baseline plus explicit source-grounded construction guidance; C B plus explicit independent reformulation before choosing the main construction. All arms may use sources, counterexamples, new representations and alternative mechanisms. Baseline is not handicapped. Interpret contrasts as effects of added instruction packages, not exclusive access to reasoning methods.

Two fresh starts per task/treatment: six researchers per admitted task, planned eighteen total. Run one task block at a time with at most six experimental researchers active. All use the same inherited model/settings, fresh fork_turns=none, no model overrides or child delegation. Verify exposed metadata without pretending to know unexposed backend or billing. Same initial evidence, tool access and work allowances; retrieval, implementation, validation and reformulation belong to the work. No hard equal-compute or filesystem-isolation claim. Each researcher may access only its assigned task/public evidence and own working directory; shared physical filesystem is a confound, checked through public tool traces where available. Live programme branches and Dots remain untouched.

Each trial performs a substantial initial construction round, then a common warm stress-and-completion round. First-stage files are preserved unchanged before the continuation. Ordinary proof/code/data and concise prose reports, no mandatory JSON. Initial allowance is up to twenty minutes of useful work, second allowance up to fifteen; finish a completed scientific unit earlier when appropriate, without padding. Slow external calls and actual model failures are reported separately; no silent model substitution, rerolls or exclusion of inconvenient outcomes. Time is a work allowance, not proof of matched computation. All first outcomes remain in the denominator.

Freeze task inputs, treatment texts, challenge text and randomized assignment mapping before the first trial on that task. Keep task-block outcomes separate. Two repeated starts on one task are not two independent scientific problems. No candidate/source communication between treatments before final outputs are locked.

Outcome review: give an independent source-based reviewer neutral candidate IDs, claims, proofs/code and the common task, without treatment labels or prompts. Record scientific disposition before revealing conditions. Wording may reveal a strategy, so this masks labels rather than guaranteeing perfect blindness. Independently reconstruct/replay every plausible positive before accepting it; a reviewer invention needed to repair the claim does not retroactively make the original a success. Check primary sources and the strongest supported pre-existing incumbent at the same information/operation boundary. Do not dismiss an invention merely because a universal conventional program could later copy it.

Three dispositions:
ACCEPTED POSITIVE: complete substantive advance on the frozen target; governing proof or executable construction checked; no missing critical operation silently supplied; meaningful frontier extension against established science/strong incumbent, with necessary acquisition/full-cycle costs. Foundational mathematics can qualify without a device if its new general consequence is established. Novelty needs a real primary-source comparison, not a failed keyword search. A small score gain alone is insufficient.
DECISIVE NEGATIVE: checked obstruction to a specified mechanism. Useful science, separately reported; not a breakthrough or primary positive.
UNRESOLVED: remaining critical interface, weak/unknown baseline, unconfirmed claim, access/model failure, missing return or merely proposed plan. Preserve the specific reason. Format failure alone is not mathematical failure; inspect actual available scientific artifacts.

No pooled breakthrough-rate claim or automatic rollout from one accepted result. A promising contrast needs a fresh bottleneck replication against A before programme-level preference. Zero positives may reflect task difficulty or inadequate work allowance, not equivalence or a failed civilisation mission. Continue developing any consequential genuine opening; do not lower the threshold to manufacture a winner. Keep one compact study summary and exact scientific artifacts; no live-branch forms, credits ledger, purchases, equipment, outreach, public publication or memory edits.
