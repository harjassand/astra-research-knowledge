**No accepted civilisation-level breakthrough or superior research method was established.** All six researchers completed an initial construction and a common warm stress/completion round. Three fresh review contexts checked the scientific outputs. A substantive finite-reference theorem survived internal correctness audits, but much of its surrounding claim was already in Astra. Its remaining originality and practical consequence are unresolved.

The ambition is unchanged: consequential foundational discoveries and new capabilities, across science and engineering. Correct local results remain useful building blocks. They do not substitute for that ambition.

## The strongest lead, and the concrete mistake in my setup

Q28's second round converts an unbounded quantum-reference optimization, for **classical-input stochastic channel pairs**, into a bounded noncommuting quadratic problem. Two fresh substantive audits reconstructed the proof and singular/zero-denominator cases without a repair. The frozen sufficient reference dimension is n+m_K+m_L. Rational thresholds then have an exact real-algebraic decision formulation. This is computability in principle, not an efficient optimizer or a coherent-quantum-channel theorem. A valid witness can still demand extreme precision; the audit records slow convergence even at a vacuum weight of1e-80.

My starting packet highlighted N967 but omitted directly relevant adjacent Astra proofs. A targeted search of the existing task-first index retrieved N970, which already contains the binary comparison theorem, and N965, which already contains arbitrary-classical-pair conditional/complete equality. I read and SHA256-checked their governing texts at public commit c9cedf3908bff605b19c4a32c1f9912df4422155. Both reviewers then compared those sources, preserving their pre-exposure reviews. Those subclaims are rediscoveries, not new breakthrough credit.

N676 also already contains inverse-action compression and real quantifier elimination at the **Petz D2/RLD** endpoint. It does not literally state the new conditional-Umegaki classical-channel cutoff. The residual increment is that explicit metric/domain connection and finite representation, assembled from close preceding operations. A reviewer separately sharpened the cutoff first to n+m_K, then—after seeing N676—to m_K+1. These are separately credited reviewer inferences, not successes retroactively assigned to the frozen research treatment. Their displayed compression arguments check; no novelty of the compression principle is claimed.

This source comparison is the most concrete organisational finding: correct, relevant Astra knowledge was available but absent from the working context. “Use Astra selectively” did not ensure the relevant theorem was retrieved. On two matched known-item queries, ordinary GitHub connector search returned no hits, while Astra's current task-first engine ranked the relevant N970/N676 result first. That demonstrates a usable route for these queries, not a general ranking of search systems or certification of the whole index.

## What the comparison teaches—and does not

- Explicit representation instructions elicited lists of different mechanisms. Several alternatives were screened briefly before one construction. A list is not evidence of substantive breadth or a higher discovery yield.
- Fresh starts with the same evidence still clustered around passive systems, information limits and related formal representations. This does not identify whether source selection, model priors, tool access or the prompt caused the clustering.
- Warm stress rounds strengthened proofs and conventional comparisons; Q28 also expanded its mathematical scope. Every second round was warm, so there is no warm-versus-fresh causal result.
- Strong ordinary comparisons prevented false wins. Q63's robust comparator and Q95's established confidence machinery matter more than a large count of passing synthetic checks.
- Correctness audits can contribute a sharper construction, as the separately credited cutoff refinement illustrates. Added information, extra work and renewed attention are confounded here; this one trajectory is not a universal coordination law.
- Most importantly, the tested prompt additions did not produce the missing physical operations. More ambitious wording and repeated formal completion have not earned a programme-wide method preference.

Two starts per package on different self-selected questions cannot establish an optimal prompt, discovery probability, worker count or model mix. No claim about Pro versus ExtraHigh or Dots' architecture follows. Zero accepted positives does not prove the methods equivalent, the mission impossible, or long foundational work unproductive. It means this comparison did not satisfy its ambitious positive criterion.

## Decisions

Do not roll out B or C as an established superior method. Preserve Q28's productive context and valid finite formulation, the scoped physical failures and all original source bytes. Use the existing task-first retrieval route for claim-shaped, just-in-time prior comparison; supply a few governing sources rather than a large archive or a new researcher reporting form. This is a case-specific correction supported by the actual rediscovery, not a proved general improvement law.

The next useful context comparison should give a warm owner and a fresh compact handoff the **same live construction, relevant Astra priors, operations and strong incumbent**, so question choice is no longer the dominant difference. That next comparison is proposed, not executed by this study. It must pursue an actual unresolved construction, not another advice response or format-compliance score.

No live programme research role or Dots was scientifically retasked by this experiment. Separately, the coordinator recovered Capital's interrupted selected intake through the dispatcher; PR221's merge is visible at the pinned public commit. That is completed knowledge integration, not mission achievement.
