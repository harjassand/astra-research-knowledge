# Stage2 primary evidence and priority limits

1. Anders Rantzer, *Distributed Control of Positive Systems*,
   arXiv:1203.0047v3, 14 May 2014.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.0047v3
   https://arxiv.org/html/1203.0047v3
   The primary full HTML was retrieved and Section IV, especially Theorem 3
   and its proof, inspected. It already establishes that the Lp gain of a
   scalar stable system with nonnegative impulse response equals its static
   gain. The stationary part of our transient limit is therefore an application
   of known theory. The fault-difference positive cascade and time-varying shunt
   domination are derived explicitly in PROOF_STAGE2.md. No priority claim for
   a new general positivity principle is made.

2. Anders Rantzer, *Scalable control of positive systems*, European Journal of
   Control 24 (2015), 72-80.
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejcon.2015.04.004
   https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S094735801500059X
   The primary indexed publisher abstract was inspected. This is a strong
   established conventional architecture for positive-system analysis and
   control; ordinary control is not limited to a hand-chosen pulse.

3. H. Nyquist, *Thermal Agitation of Electric Charge in Conductors*, Physical
   Review 32 (1928), 110-113.
   https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.32.110
   https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.32.110
   Author-paper scan indexed at:
   https://wwwusers.ts.infn.it/~milotti/Didattica/FondamentiFisiciTecnologiaModerna/LectureNotes/Nyquist-1928.pdf
   The official abstract and indexed original pages, including the noise law,
   were inspected. Low-frequency equilibrium voltage-noise power is proportional
   to resistance and bandwidth. It motivates the explicit quasistatic variance
   model in Proposition B. It does not justify treating arbitrary filtered
   thermal noise as independent readings with that variance. No physical noise
   measurements were performed or imported into the synthetic tests.

The stage1 primary source trail remains at ../stage1/SOURCES.md. Its resistor
inverse-problem and matrix-update antecedents remain applicable. Additional
searches for two-Laplace network capacity/gain identification did not settle
priority of the finite joint-calibration formula. Those incomplete searches
are not evidence of novelty. The use of independent known node capacities
is an explicit hypothesis and resource, not an observed property of an unknown
specimen. No source establishes the missing selective thermal-loss-removal
operation.
