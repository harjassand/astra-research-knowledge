# Single-port network tomography: an exact construction with decisive limits

I constructed an exact inverse operation for arbitrary connected reciprocal
grounded finite networks, implemented it, and proved why it does not provide
the proposed scalable physical capability. The civilisation-changing advance
is **not achieved**. The completed scientific unit is a finite reconstruction
theorem, an optimal-comparator theorem, a calibration obstruction and executable
evidence. Historical novelty of the combined mathematical statement remains
unresolved; it is not presented as an accepted new discovery.

## Initial question and chosen operation

Can a single boundary temperature or voltage sensor recover the complete
hidden diffusion/conductance network by controlled local material changes,
including networks with modes invisible to ordinary boundary interrogation?
This matters for diagnosing inaccessible thermal paths and electrical faults.
The initial capacity-modulation idea was narrowed to calibrated shunt loading:
capacity changes vanish in the steady measurement, while local shunts have an
exact, testable effect. This is a different operation from the inherited
nonlinear thermal-network benchmark in the public packet.

The frozen mathematical target here is an unknown symmetric positive definite
matrix A with nonpositive offdiagonals and a connected graph. A physical
subclass is A=L_w+diag(g), with nonnegative edge conductances and grounded
leaks. One injects a known unit input at node 0 and reads only its steady
response. Every other labeled node has a calibrated selectable shunt. The
number of nodes and the intervention-to-node labels are supplied assumptions.

## Actual construction and claim

For any n and any fixed t>0, exactly n(n+1)/2 scalar responses suffice:
baseline, shunts t and 2t at each non-port node separately, then shunts t at
each non-port pair. Rank-one inversion recovers the inverse matrix's diagonals
and port column. A rank-two equation recovers every remaining entry; an
inverse-M inequality proves which quadratic root is admissible. Inverting that
matrix recovers A. No generic spectrum, tree assumption or derivatives are
needed. The governing proof, including the global root argument, is in
[PROOF.md](PROOF.md), and the executable is [tomography.py](tomography.py).

This count meets the number of free matrix parameters and is minimal for
smooth scalar experiments with a differentiable local inverse. It does not
optimize measurement precision, bits, settling time, energy or physical cost.
The construction uses classical inverse-update algebra. Its precise finite
design and root proof are candidate contributions derived here, with priority
unresolved. A failed keyword search is not used as evidence of invention.

## Decisive result: the strongest comparator for an added local leak

For any such network A_0 and A_1=A_0+delta e_v e_v^T, every additional
nonnegative shunt configuration d makes the one-port difference smaller:

    0 < f_0(d)-f_1(d) <= f_0(0)-f_1(0).

This follows from entrywise inverse monotonicity and differentiation with
respect to every shunt. With independent Gaussian readout noise of the same
variance in every configuration, each loaded observation is an explicit
statistical garbling of an unloaded observation. Consequently **every adaptive
positive-shunt policy of T measurements is dominated by T ordinary unloaded
measurements** for this two-hypothesis task. This comparison is over all
estimators in the declared class, not just the implemented fitting code.

For a unit-conductance path with unit background leak at every node, the
largest possible response to an extra unit leak at depth L is exponentially
small. Exact arithmetic gives:

| Depth L | Maximum signal over all positive shunts | Optimal measurement count for 75% discrimination, noise sd 1e-6 |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 8 | 9.26e-8 | 213 |
| 12 | 4.19e-11 | about 1.03e9 |
| 20 | 8.61e-18 | about 2.45e22 |
| 30 | 3.76e-26 | about 1.29e39 |

The exact risk formula and a general exponential bound are proved in
PROOF.md. The stronger table is [limits_and_bypass.csv](limits_and_bypass.csv);
the earlier, looser bound is separately retained in
[depth_barrier.csv](depth_barrier.csv). Five hundred randomized shunt choices
also checked the monotonicity, but the proof supplies the all-configuration
claim. The noise model is an explicit hypothesis, not a universal physical
noise law. The theorem does not exclude transient or extra-sensor operations.

## Stronger route and its remaining physical operation

I tried removing background loss instead of adding shunts. If all existing
hidden leaks can be switched off, leaving only a known port leak, the
sensor-to-node Green entries become constant. On the unit path the deep-fault
signal is exactly 1/(L+2), which is polynomial rather than exponential.
The executable [limits_and_bypass.py](limits_and_bypass.py) verifies this
with rational arithmetic through depth 30.

This is an actual conditional bypass, but opening known prewired leak switches
is an ordinary operation available to the matched incumbent. The proof does
not build selective, calibrated removal of unknown thermal loss inside an
inaccessible specimen. Established electronic guarding suppresses certain
fixture/cable leaks; it does not supply this general internal operation.
[Keithley's official application note](https://www.tek.com/en/documents/application-note/optimizing-low-current-measurements-4200a-scs-parameter-analyzer)
documents that relevant pre-existing technique.

A second exact obstruction concerns calibration. If actuator gains and internal
capacities are unknown, positive diagonal congruence creates different physical
networks with exactly the same response for every command and frequency,
including time-varying commands in the ideal model. The explicit pair and 140
frequency-command checks are in [calibration_gauge.json](calibration_gauge.json).
Recovering a command-normalized representative does not identify absolute
internal temperatures or heat flows.

The precise unresolved operation is therefore **independently calibrated access
that can remove or compensate hidden background losses, while preserving the
network and providing useful precision without installing an equivalent set
of internal sensors**. Neither the inverse formula nor an assumed actuator
provides it. Switch installation, gain calibration, parasitic changes, reset,
settling and readout precision remain unmeasured acquisition costs. No physical
advantage is claimed without them.
