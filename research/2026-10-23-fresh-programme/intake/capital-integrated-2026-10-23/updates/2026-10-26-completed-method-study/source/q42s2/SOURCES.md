# Stage 2 primary evidence

The preserved stage1 source ledger remains unchanged at
../stage1/SOURCES.md. It supplies the experimentally reported convective heat
circulator, finite-conductance switch component, static Kron mathematics and
time-varying circuit antecedents. None of those components was silently composed
into a measured device here.

The following additional primary sources were inspected on 2026-10-26:

1. F. Dorfler, J. W. Simpson-Porco and F. Bullo, *Electrical Networks and
   Algebraic Graph Theory: Models, Properties, and Applications*, Proceedings
   of the IEEE 106, 977-1005 (2018). Authors' full article:
   https://www.control.utoronto.ca/~jwsimpson/papers/2017k/ .
   Its RC and compartmental-system treatment makes positivity and diagonal
   storage assumptions explicit. Proposition 5.7 gives the established static
   Kron zero-pattern characterization through paths with only internal transit
   vertices. These are antecedents, not discoveries of this trial. Stage2's
   finite-period path lower bound is proved independently in
   REVERSE_TRANSFER_PROOF.md; its novelty is unestablished.

2. Matteo Acciai, Fatemeh Hajiloo, Fabian Hassler and Janine Splettstoesser,
   *Phase-coherent heat circulators with normal or superconducting contacts*,
   Physical Review B 103, 085409 (2021).
   https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.103.085409 .
   The publisher abstract was inspected. It treats a phase-coherent region,
   magnetic field and potentially superconducting phases. Those operations
   differ from the fixed-capacity reciprocal positive diffusion model. The
   finite-contact no-perfect-isolation theorem does not refute this mechanism
   or import its components into the present architecture.

3. Seungwoo Lee, *Finite-stage nonreciprocal thermal circulators for radiative
   energy harvesting: Benchmarks for isolation, termination, and etendue*,
   PNAS Nexus 5(7), pgag249 (2026).
   https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgag249 .
   Primary publisher full text inspected selectively:
   https://academic.oup.com/pnasnexus/article/5/7/pgag249/8738672 .
   Its ideal port-network picture uses a nonreciprocal circulator primitive
   and cold termination, while its practical discussion accounts for finite
   isolation, coupling and termination. That reinforces the distinction between
   a useful approximate device and a supplied ideal selective primitive. It is
   not evidence that reciprocal conductive contacts implement that primitive.

All new numerical data are local model calculations, not source measurements.
No external writes, publications, branch messages, delegation, equipment or
paid research services were used. Search did not establish a new capability or
novelty of the mathematical formulation.
