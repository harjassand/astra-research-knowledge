## Consequential target and three preliminary mechanisms

Initial question: can an autonomous discovery system acquire a genuinely new constitutive law and certify its use outside the previously interrogated operating regime, rather than compile more tasks inside a supplied response family? Reliable prediction of a new regime is the consequential operation sought. A universal success claim is a demanding target; one stable passive counterfamily can disprove it under specified acquisition resources.

Before choosing the main attack I considered three materially different bridges:

1. **Representation search.** Sparse symbolic libraries and learned coordinates can replace the representation of a nonlinear system. The demonstrated SINDy operation is sparse regression in selected variables and a candidate function library (Brunton–Proctor–Kutz, 2016, DOI 10.1073/pnas.1517384113). This is a strong ordinary incumbent, not a novel operation. An unobserved feature may be nearly zero on every allowed experiment while large at the target; enlarging the library does not create its excitation.
2. **Analytic continuation with stable/passive structure.** Perhaps real analyticity, a known positive lower stiffness and a known upper stiffness turn local data into uniformly reliable new-regime predictions. This is stronger than unconstrained function interpolation and has an honest governing model. I selected this as the main construction because it has a decisive proof/test, including an exact optimal ordinary comparator.
3. **Physical rearrangement and spectroscopy.** Instead of predicting a hidden force transition, use passive mechanical series/parallel networks, small oscillatory perturbations, or differential measurements to expose it below the allowed component strain. This is outside the inherited compatible-family compiler representation: the objects are energy-storing mechanical ports. Known mechanical amplification changes displacement/noise/conditioning resources; finite differences do not supply new information beyond their force readings. Direct operation at the target on a sacrificial specimen would acquire the missing information, but changes the allowed operation and does not establish a new safe detector. I preserve this route and derive a passive-equilibrium sensitivity bound; I do not assume a compatible arbitrary material or a new sensor into existence.

The target has not been silently replaced by a score improvement. I sought a bridge for new-regime law acquisition and found a sharp blocker for a specified observation class. This is a negative scientific unit, not a claimed civilisation-changing discovery.

## Source findings and substantive pivot

The public packet warns that uniform regularity and source families are already supplied in the learning foothold. I did not retrieve that archive because the actual gap here is acquisition outside a known family. Primary literature confirms that one must distinguish qualitative analyticity from quantitative excitation:

- Musavi et al., NeurIPS 2024, *Identification of Analytic Nonlinear Dynamical Systems with Non-asymptotic Guarantees*, describes linearly parameterized systems with **known** nonlinear features. See the paper's equation (1), Section 2, and discussion after Theorem 1. Its persistent-excitation constants exist under its hypotheses; the paper does not supply a universal numerical constant for arbitrary analytic features. https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/9b3c956210432122aa5385c355fc7c6e-Paper-Conference.pdf
- Trefethen's 2020 analytic-continuation paper already establishes the general ill-posedness phenomenon and the importance of bounded complex domains. I therefore abandoned any claim that mere analytic-continuation instability is novel. The local object pursued here is an explicit passive, uniformly stiffness-bounded pair, its **exact** finite-sample adaptive-optimal discrimination law, and an extrapolation modulus with a sharp nonzero limiting jump. https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/trefethen/trefethen_ac.pdf ; https://doi.org/10.1007/s10543-020-00802-7
- SafeOpt explicitly uses quantitative regularity (including RKHS and Lipschitz hypotheses). It is an established comparator, not refuted by a family outside a fixed common RKHS norm bound. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v37/sui15.html ; https://proceedings.mlr.press/v37/sui15.pdf
- SINDy is an established successful operation for sparse dynamics in appropriate measured variables/bases. Its success is not a guarantee for every unexcited nonlinear feature. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1517384113 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.03580

Primary-source search also found prior work on extrapolation of **completely monotone** functions. That is a stronger, different derivative-sign class; I do not conflate ordinary monotone force laws with completely monotone functions. Search absence is not novelty evidence. The present proof is self-contained; priority for this exact corollary remains unestablished.

## Abandoned positive construction

The first positive idea was: fit a passive analytic law, use its bounded stiffness to continue safely, and then compile new tasks from the retained law. This fails even after giving the learner the two exact possible laws. A hidden softplus transition can have macroscopic target response, fixed known stiffness bounds and an exponentially small safe-region signature. No symbolic search or retained-history reprocessing can distinguish records that do not carry enough information. The endpoint repeated-measurement incumbent is exactly optimal for the known pair, so there is no algorithmic gain left to claim in that observation model.

A stronger attempted physical bridge was small-signal stiffness spectroscopy. It can expose a transition sooner if a genuinely more precise, separately supported derivative sensor is available. For finite differences of the existing force measurements, the noise amplification cancels the apparent derivative gain; the endpoint force incumbent strictly dominates. An independent derivative sensor would be a different instrument; even a fixed finite derivative order still has an exponentially small safe signature as transition width shrinks. No source-supported universal target-safe sensor was found or constructed here.
