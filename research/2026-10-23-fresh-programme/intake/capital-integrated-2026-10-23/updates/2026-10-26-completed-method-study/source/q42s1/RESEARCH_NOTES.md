# Construction history and abandoned routes

The public assignment and starting packet were read in full. This trial chose a
new question rather than extending the supplied quantum-channel, inverse-family,
ion-interrogation or polymer examples. Only this trial folder, the public packet,
ordinary tool instructions and selectively retrieved external primary sources
were inspected. No other trial, key, evaluator, reviewer or programme chat was
accessed. No subagents, paid research services, model API calls or equipment were
used.

## Initial question and first failure

Can a fixed thermal body/network, using only scheduled reciprocal contacts,
provide a useful DC heat circulator without moving heat-carrying material or
modulating heat capacity? Directional heat routing could reduce thermal coupling
between components, but an improvement in cooling or resource use must be shown
separately.

The first candidate was a two-phase diffusive commutator. Products of symmetric
diffusion propagators need not be symmetric, suggesting a directional effective
generator. This idea fails for the requested DC terminal operation. The adjoint
calculation in Theorem 2 proves that every two-phase schedule is reciprocal after
period averaging, even when its internal sampled map is nonsymmetric. The saved
five-node counterexample has internal monodromy antisymmetry norm 0.0233774 but
DC terminal antisymmetry only 1.57e-16. A nonsymmetric logarithm of the sampled
map would have been the wrong evidence for the physical terminal claim.

## Stronger construction

The replacement uses one constant-capacity body and three sequential contacts.
Solving its periodic recurrence, rather than fitting a numerical response, gives
an exact directed circulant terminal matrix. General n-port and nonuniform-contact
formulas were then constructed. This is a real operation in the declared circuit
model, not merely a proposed experiment.

The ideal model's forward/reverse ratio grows as exp(g P/(3 C)). This initially
looks attractive, but its forward heat-transfer coefficient tends to zero in the
same limit. Finite off-state leakage makes the isolation ratio itself turn over.
The leaky closed form was derived and checked against a general independent
network construction, with contact strengths normalized to the same total mean
conductance. An on/off ratio reported in an actual electrostatic-switch paper
was used solely as a sensitivity value; its unmeasured timing, actuation and
three-contact compatibility were not supplied by assumption.

## The static comparator changed the consequence

The proposed routing is not a generic cooling improvement. Expanding the
periodic energy balance about the harmonic extension of the average graph yields
an exact positive-semidefinite gap. An energy-form Cauchy-Schwarz argument gives
a stronger normalized matrix circle bound. These apply to every finite graph in
the stated model, not just the circulant example. A continuum counterpart follows
under uniform ellipticity and admissible fixed boundary traces.

The matched mean-conductance static star is the global worst-hot-port cooling
optimum on this topology, not a deliberately weak incumbent. If simultaneous
closure is allowed, the full installed all-on graph is stronger still. Conversely,
the averaged static graph must not be called feasible under an unverified
interlocked actuator or nonconvex material budget. The recent autonomous
barrier-switching preprint supplies an explicit example of why mean conductance
and mean barrier-lowering budget are different resources; it does not contradict
the result here.

## Further attempted strengthening and why it did not help

Parallel phase-staggered copies could smooth the instantaneous ripple. With
matched aggregate C and g, however, each of k copies has C/k and g/k and exactly
Y/k. Their sum is the same DC response. A faster response obtained by leaving
each copy's conductance at g would spend k times the contact resource. This route
was rejected algebraically, not promoted as an architectural gain.

Source retrieval also found already established experimental convective thermal
circulation, recent conductivity/capacity-modulated diffusion circulation, and
older time-varying circuit circulators. Thus the broad operation is not new.
The exact comparator identities and leaky formulas have a governing proof here,
but their novelty has not been established. The run does not convert a failed
keyword match into a novelty claim.

## Numerical provenance and limits

Python 3.13 at /usr/local/bin/python3, NumPy 1.26.4, SciPy 1.17.1 and Matplotlib
3.10.8 were available. No dependencies were installed. Network diagnostic seed
is 2026102642; the unequal-contact diagnostic uses its separate seed 2026102643,
so adding that construction does not alter the original network ensemble.

The solver composes augmented matrix exponentials and solves the periodic fixed
point directly. It therefore does not report a finite burn-in as an exact steady
state. Separate adaptive DOP853 integrations from zero initial conditions are
repeated until periodic convergence and provide an independent numerical check.
All results are double-precision local model calculations. Some host BLAS calls
emit floating-point warnings, preserved in run_stderr.txt. Every phase
exponential, composed map and final response is explicitly checked for nonfinite
entries, and none was found. The independent ODE and closed-form comparisons
agree to the errors reported in NUMERICAL_RESULTS.txt. The warning's underlying
runtime cause was not diagnosed, so the log is preserved rather than erased.

No physical dimensions, measured throughput, fabrication tolerance, actuator
work, clock power or extra switch heat capacity were inferred from these
calculations. Periodic preparation is required; cold-body storage cannot be
spent afresh every cycle. This is a completed theoretical/model unit with a
decisive comparator limitation, and an unfinished physical/novelty claim.
