What an actual classical event can encode in a quantum programme
================================================================

STATUS: Internal proof, read in full by root and checked in the bounded
supporting derivations below. This is not external validation. This is a matched obstruction to one
specific reconstruction mechanism: graph dependence enters a pre-input
programme only through the original compatible event selection, while
all fresh-question local readouts remain fixed before the graph is drawn.
It allows arbitrary normal commuting resources and all actual retained
branch outputs. It is not an unrestricted game-value gap, a prohibition
of graph-aware readouts, or an external validation/priority claim.
The earlier frozen pressure theorem and all prior frozen sources are
unchanged. All logarithms here are natural.

Selected mechanism and its actual source
----------------------------------------

The random BG block source has a small checked-event charge per remaining
coordinate and perfect selected live payoff. A natural attempted repair
is to prepare its selected whole-history/quantum resource BEFORE new
inputs, then run fixed local decoding instruments. This grants a new
pre-input shared programme and can discard irrelevant checked workspace.
The theorem below shows that the original selection cannot, by itself,
encode sufficient graph dependence to make such fixed readouts succeed.
It applies beyond finite dimension, maximally entangled states, or trace
representations. The unresolved route is to compile the readouts using
the graph, or use a different resource-acquisition interface.

1. Fixed apparatus, arbitrary state, and the positive edge coefficients
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Fix k>=2, r>=1, N=k^r. The graph Gamma is bipartite on two DISTINCT copies
of [k]^r, with independent Bernoulli(q) ordered cross-side edges,
0<q<1. This is the graph model in BG Section 7, not a symmetric graph.
Questions x,y are independent uniform elements of [k]. Answers are
(l,s),(j,t), and winning means

  l=j, s_l=x, t_l=y, Gamma_st=1.                           (Q1)

Fix BEFORE Gamma is drawn one common representation and finite families
of local complete POVMs A_(l,s)^x, B_(j,t)^y. Every Alice effect commutes
with every Bob effect; no same-side cross-input commutation is assumed.
Invalid outputs can be kept as losing outcomes. Fine records and fixed
local CP processing may be retained; their coarse effects determine Q1.
These are arbitrary quantum-controlled apparatuses: they can act on an
unbounded programme space, private history registers, and arbitrarily
large pre-Gamma ancillary resources. Their operators must nevertheless
be fixed independently of Gamma.

Let M be the von Neumann algebra containing the representation of these
operators, and phi a fixed normal state on M, independent of Gamma.
No density matrix or finite trace is assumed. For each ordered edge
write

  T_st=(1/k^2)sum_(l=1)^r
          A_(l,s)^(s_l) B_(l,t)^(t_l),
  S=sum_st T_st,
  W_Gamma=sum_st Gamma_st T_st.                            (Q2)

Each product in Q2 is positive because its factors commute, and has
norm at most 1. Regrouping S by x=s_l,y=t_l, its summands form a subset
of the complete joint POVM for each x,y. Consequently

  T_st>=0,  ||T_st||<=b:=r/k^2,  S<=I,
  0<=W_Gamma<=S<=I.                                        (Q3)

For any state omega the actual fresh-law game payoff is exactly
omega(W_Gamma). No operator-norm proxy replaces that value.

2. A graph-dependent programme is allowed, with its actual acquisition
----------------------------------------------------------------------

For every Gamma let tau_Gamma be a positive normal functional satisfying

  0<=tau_Gamma<=phi,  p_Gamma=tau_Gamma(I).

When p_Gamma>0 put omega_Gamma=tau_Gamma/p_Gamma. Thus the programme state
may depend on ALL graph bits, including quantum coherences inside the
actual retained branches. The literal gate is

  p_Gamma omega_Gamma<=phi                                (Q4)

as an order on the FULL output algebra. It is not inferred from a small
classical KL divergence, a measured marginal, or coherent pure-state
postselection. It will be forced by the complete dephased source in
Section 4. Define beta_Gamma=omega_Gamma(W_Gamma), with arbitrary beta at
zero-p graphs since those receive no selected mass.

THEOREM. For every a>q and p_*>0,

  Pr_Gamma[ p_Gamma>=p_* and beta_Gamma>=a ]
    <=q(1-q)b/[p_*(a-q)^2].                                (Q5)

There is also the exact weighted mean-square statement

  E_Gamma p_Gamma (beta_Gamma-q)_+^2 <=q(1-q)b.              (Q6)

Here p_Gamma and omega_Gamma may be chosen adaptively from Gamma without
any finite codebook or dimension restriction, provided Q4 holds against
the ONE fixed phi and Q2 is fixed. The positive part in Q6 is essential:
beta-q need not be bounded below by the centered expectation used next.

3. Proof by the actual operator variance
-----------------------------------------

Set Z_Gamma=W_Gamma-qS=sum_st (Gamma_st-q)T_st. Centered independent scalar
coefficients kill every distinct-edge cross term, in its original order:

  E Z_Gamma^2=q(1-q)sum_st T_st^2
             <=q(1-q)b S<=q(1-q)b I.                      (Q7)

No reordering or commutation among the T_st is used. For p_Gamma>0,
state Cauchy-Schwarz, Q4, and S<=I give

  beta_Gamma
   =q omega_Gamma(S)+omega_Gamma(Z_Gamma)
   <=q+sqrt(omega_Gamma(Z_Gamma^2))
   <=q+sqrt(phi(Z_Gamma^2)/p_Gamma).                        (Q8)

Therefore p_Gamma(beta_Gamma-q)_+^2<=phi(Z_Gamma^2) pointwise. Average
using the FIXED phi and Q7 to get Q6. On Q5's event the left side is at
least p_*(a-q)^2, so Markov proves Q5. This is a second-moment tail, not
an exponential concentration claim. Positive-functional Cauchy-Schwarz
is valid on any C*-algebra; normality is needed only for the stated actual
source/output interpretation, not this algebraic inequality.

If E p_Gamma=pbar>0, reweight graphs by their actual acquisition mass:

  dP_selected(Gamma)=p_Gamma dP(Gamma)/pbar.

Cauchy-Schwarz in that probability law yields the useful consequence

  E_selected beta_Gamma
       <=q+sqrt(q(1-q)b/pbar).                             (Q9)

This follows from the positive part in Q6 and does not assume that
p_Gamma is constant or independent of the graph.

4. Why the original compatible instrument really supplies Q4
----------------------------------------------------------------

Start any Gamma-independent complete source instrument, including its
classical question sampling, all supplied fine outcome maps, and any
retained quantum outputs. Its actual DEPHASED fine record is h. Write
its output as a finite direct sum of positive normal branch functionals

  phi=direct_sum_h tau_h,  sum_h tau_h(I)=1.                (Q10)

The record may consist of the two parties' private strings rather than
a common public history. Direct sums are a mathematical description of
their actual classical record/output state, not permission to send it
online. Quantum systems within each h branch need not be dephased.

If the referee's event is the classical selector e_Gamma(h) in [0,1],
restriction of that record gives precisely

  tau_Gamma=direct_sum_h e_Gamma(h)tau_h <=phi,
  p_Gamma=sum_h e_Gamma(h)tau_h(I).                         (Q11)

Every branch's actual retained quantum output is included. If a fine
record is discarded, sum the corresponding positive branch functionals;
the inequality persists. More generally any ONE Gamma-independent normal
CP output map, including local processing, partial trace, classical
copying, or appending independent entanglement, preserves the order.
Thus the source can retain or forget irrelevant workspace before the
fixed fresh-input readout without losing Q4.

This argument concerns the specified complete instrument's classical
output state. It does not restore coherent off-diagonal superpositions
between distinct classical h records, supply an environmental purifier
of that dephasing, or assert F rho F<=rho for an arbitrary coherent
Luders filter. A general jointly postselected quantum vector need not
obey Q4 relative to its original vector state.

One may even GRANT the reconstruction a pre-input shared copy of all
classical h, together with its conditioned branch state. The corresponding
Gamma-independent classical copying map applies to both phi and tau,
so the order remains true. This is a stronger existential resource than
an online LOCC conversion of the given private records. It gives no free
herald or communication protocol. It also gives no fresh opposite input.

A graph register/programme can control the readout only if it is part of
the state covered by Q4 and the same fixed reference phi. Appending a
free deterministic register containing the realized Gamma after selection
is graph-dependent processing outside this source contract. Likewise a
local instrument taking Gamma as an additional free input is graph-aware
and is not fixed in Q2. Those legitimate ordinary-game possibilities
remain outside the theorem, rather than being charged implicitly.

5. The literal low-information BG source and a full-fine payoff failure
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Take the actual r-round BG source strategy on independent old strings
U,V uniform in [k]^r: at round i Alice answers (i,U), Bob (i,V). It uses
no entanglement. Every coordinate win indicator is the SAME bit Gamma_UV.
Testing any nonempty coordinate set therefore selects

  E_Gamma={Gamma_UV=1},
  p_Gamma=|Gamma|/N^2.                                     (Q12)

Its original conditional live payoff is 1 in every remaining round.
The full old-history law conditioned on the event has classical relative
entropy L_Gamma=log(1/p_Gamma) against its product reference. For any fixed
live set of size m, product-reference KL superadditivity, Pinsker, and
Jensen imply mean marginal question-pair TV at most sqrt(L_Gamma/(2m)).
This concerns the actual selected referee law and does not supply local
sampling kernels or a new quantum strategy on independent fresh inputs.

Choose q=k^(-1/5), r=k^(1/3) along the usual integer subsequence. With
probability tending to 1, p_Gamma>=q/2, so for m=Theta(r),
L_Gamma/m=O(log k/k^(1/3))->0. This is the actual low-charge regime, not
a constant two-round fixture. The graph can simultaneously satisfy BG's
density/small-rectangle conditions; the present obstruction does not
require their proof or a quantum gap.

Now prepare the selected programme Q11 from this COMPLETE dephased
source BEFORE sampling NEW independent uniform base questions x,y.
Grant any additional Gamma-independent normal commuting helper state,
retain every original fine record/quantum output, and allow any fixed
Gamma-blind local CP preprocessing/readout family. It fits Q2--Q4.
Old labels U_i,V_i are part of the programme; they are not declared to be
the new fresh x,y. Correlating the new inputs with those old labels
would violate the literal game-effect premise and is not an extraction.

For any such fixed apparatus and reference (allowed to depend on k but
chosen before Gamma), Q5 with p_*=q/2 gives

  Pr[ p_Gamma>=q/2 and beta_Gamma>=a ]
       <=2(1-q)r/[k^2(a-q)^2] ->0                         (Q13)

for every fixed a>0 (and sufficiently large k so a>q). Unlimited
Hilbert-space dimension or arbitrary commuting programmes do not change
this statement. It is uniform over the graph-dependent choice of ordered
programme tau<=phi, not over graph-dependent measurement operators.

There is an exact averaged form with no density-concentration step:
E_Gamma p_Gamma=q. By Q9, under the actual joint experiment in which a
random graph and old source are sampled then E is accepted,

  E_(Gamma|E) beta_Gamma<=q+sqrt((1-q)r/k^2).               (Q14)

Declare one original live coordinate i outside the nonempty checked set
(or a fixed offline mixture of such coordinates), and compare its
question/answer/fine marginal with the candidate FRESH BASE-game output,
using common Gamma/question/answer variables. The original selected
coordinate's payoff is identically 1. The candidate fresh-law output
therefore has mean total-variation discrepancy at least

  1-q-sqrt((1-q)r/k^2),                                   (Q15)

in that declared marginal comparison: its binary winning test witnesses
the lower bound. Any genuine full-instrument comparison retaining this
marginal inherits it by data processing. A single fresh base output is
not itself an m-round reconstruction. Q15 obstructs even payoff repair
of the declared coordinate within this programme/readout interface; it
is not a bound on arbitrary graph-aware base strategies.

The no-small-drift shortcut is literal: the original old questions may
be locally close to uniform after selection, while the programme keeps
the full selected history. Supplying fresh independent inputs does not
let a fixed apparatus identify them with those stored labels. No Bob
purification or classical-to-coherent upgrade is used.

6. Scientific consequence and its remaining quantifier
-------------------------------------------------------

This disproves one actual low-information reconstruction route: the
original compatible event, used as the sole carrier of graph dependence
in a pre-input resource, does not permit fixed graph-blind local readouts
to reproduce the original perfect selected payoff. It remains false even
if the reconstruction receives unlimited pre-Gamma entanglement, a shared
copy of old records, and arbitrary normal commuting branch resources.
The selection order is FORCED here, not a supplied global closeness
condition awaiting acquisition.

The ordinary BG game is public. Its Alice and Bob readouts and its
shared state may all be compiled using Gamma. Such a graph-aware repair
is not refuted, and the typical random graph's unrestricted tensor or
commuting value remains UNKNOWN. No positive unrestricted base gap, slow-
repetition counterexample at that gap, or general selection impossibility
follows. The resource class is not artificially tracial or bounded-rank;
the limitation is the literal channel by which this apparatus acquires
its graph-dependent instructions.

7. Primary comparator and bounded independent challenges
----------------------------------------------------------

The independent centered-summand identity Q7 is elementary. A standard
nearby finite-matrix result is Tropp, User-Friendly Tail Bounds for Sums
of Random Matrices, Theorem 6.1 (bounded matrix Bernstein), which uses
variance ||sum E X_e^2|| but includes a dimension prefactor. It does not
state the adaptive dominated-normal-state result Q5; Q5 is proved here
by state Cauchy-Schwarz, not imported as a stronger matrix Bernstein bound.
  https://tropp.caltech.edu/papers/Tro11-User-Friendly-preprint.pdf

BG's actual graph game and source are Section 7 of Braverman--Garg,
ECCC TR14-095 revision 1. It is explicitly bipartite with independent
cross-side Bernoulli edges:
