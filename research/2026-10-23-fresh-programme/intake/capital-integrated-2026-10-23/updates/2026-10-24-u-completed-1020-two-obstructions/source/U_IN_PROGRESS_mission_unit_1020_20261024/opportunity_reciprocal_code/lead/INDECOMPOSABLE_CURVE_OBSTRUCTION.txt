AN INDECOMPOSABLE BINARY SELF-DUAL CODE WITH A DISTINCT RECIPROCAL CURVE

Status: exact finite algebraic obstruction to the proposed finiteness gate.
It does not resolve the prime-extension graph sampler or assert novelty.
The proof is independent of a code-classification table or numerical fit.

1. Exact acquired witness

Use F_128=F_2[a]/(a^7+a+1), in the usual seven-bit polynomial basis. Let v
be the following ordered list of distinct nonzero field elements, represented
by integers in that basis:

  1,2,3,6,7,9,11,15,16,17,18,19,21,22,23,25,
  30,31,32,36,37,40,43,46,48,49,50,51,53,54,55,56.

Let D <= F_2^32 be generated by the all-ones vector and the coordinate bit
rows of v and v^(-1). The companion exact checker establishes:

  dim D=14; D is self-orthogonal; the full degree<=2 lift of
  (v_i,v_i^(-1)) has rank 31 and the sum of all 32 columns is zero.

All 32 lift columns therefore form a minimal circuit: its kernel is exactly
the span of the full-support all-ones dependency. In particular no proper
nonempty coordinate subset is a degree-two dependency. The checker verifies
field inverses for every nonzero quotient element, all dot products, and the
stated ranks by exact binary elimination. It has no random or floating input.

Acquisition was one deterministic fundamental-circuit computation: insert
the 127 field elements in ascending order into a degree-two column basis.
The first dependent column is 56 and has exactly this support. Verification
does not depend on that acquisition history or on searching other codes.

2. Minimal lift circuits force D to be indecomposable

Suppose D decomposes on a nontrivial coordinate partition A union B. Since
1 belongs to D, both coordinate indicators 1_A and 1_B belong to D. Every
row r in D has its restriction r_A in D as well. Self-orthogonality gives

              sum_(i in A) r_i s_i = r_A dot s_A = 0

for all generator rows r,s. These sums are exactly all degree<=2 monomial
sums on A, including the constant and degree-one cases. Thus A would be a
proper nonempty lift dependency, a contradiction. Hence D is indecomposable.
It is properly self-orthogonal: dim D=14<16=32/2.

3. Root's self-dual doubling construction

For any self-orthogonal D <= F_2^w define

        C_D = { (u+d,u): u in D^perp, d in D } <= F_2^(2w).

The parameterization is injective and has dimension (w-dim D)+dim D=w.
The dot product of two words is u dot e+d dot u'+d dot e, which is zero
because u,u' lie in D^perp and d,e lie in D. Therefore C_D is self-dual.
Its subcode supported on either one full copy is precisely a copy of D.

If D is indecomposable and proper D != D^perp, then C_D is indecomposable.
Indeed, any coordinate decomposition of C_D would restrict to a coordinate
decomposition of each one-copy D subcode. Indecomposability of D forces
each entire copy to lie in one component. The only possible nontrivial
partition is consequently first-copy versus second-copy. But for any
u in D^perp minus D, (u,u) lies in C_D whereas (u,0) does not. That partition
cannot define a code direct sum. This proves the assertion without listing
minimal words or using an implicit notion of connectedness.

For the witness above C_D is an indecomposable [64,32] binary self-dual code.
The checker independently verifies its dimension and pairwise orthogonality.

4. A genuine all-distinct projective curve

Over K=algebraic closure of F_2, both v and v^(-1) lie in D tensor K by
their coordinate-bit expansion. Thus both (v,0),(0,v), and both one-copy
versions of v^(-1), lie in C_D tensor K. For every nonzero t in K,

                 z(t)=(v,t v),
                 z(t)^(-1)=(v^(-1),t^(-1)v^(-1))

belong to C_D tensor K. Within each copy the coordinates remain nonzero
and pairwise distinct. Across copies the only forbidden values are the
finite set t=v_i/v_j. Removing them and t=0 leaves a nonempty open subset
of a projective line. The parameter is genuine projectively: scaling
z(t) to z(t') fixes the first copy v, hence has scalar 1 and t=t'.

This refutes zero-dimensionality even when the binary self-dual code is
indecomposable, all coordinates are nonzero, and all coordinates are
pairwise distinct. It is not the earlier two-value 1+s c counterexample.

5. What it does and does not block

The code is binary, but the displayed line has shape constants in F_128.
Its known points live in fields containing F_128; the construction alone
does not provide points over arbitrarily large prime extension degrees.
It therefore refutes both proposed universal finiteness hypotheses, not
the inverse-graph point list's conjectured growing girth on prime fields.
For that latter task one must handle the fields of definition of actual
geometric components, or construct a fixed-weight relation over arbitrarily
large prime-degree extensions. No such theorem is asserted here.

The graph source remains Mao, arXiv:2607.20318v1, Theorem 1.2 and Sections
3.3--4; its rank mechanism and exact uniform-sampling open question are
imported context, not proved by this code obstruction. The owned candidate
has only aimed at exact marginal order tending to infinity, weaker than
the paper's superlogarithmic degree. No graph-performance replay is useful
until the actual girth gate is resolved.
