# Independent readback: indecomposable reciprocal curve

**Verdict: Sections 2–4 of the lead obstruction are sound.** The exact checker also runs to completion and reports the claimed D parameters, lift rank, and doubled self-dual dimension. This is an algebraic falsifier of universal zero-dimensionality for the stated class, including the indecomposable subclass; it gives no prime-extension sampler result.

For the circuit-to-indecomposability step, a coordinate decomposition D = D_A ⊕ D_B makes 1_A and 1_B elements of D. It also makes every restriction r_A an element of D for r in D. For generator rows r,s, self-orthogonality gives r_A · s_A = 0. Taking r,s among the constant-one row and the 14 bit rows yields vanishing sums on A for the constant, each degree-one monomial, and every degree-two product of feature bits (squares reduce to degree one over F₂). Thus the lifted degree-at-most-2 columns indexed by A sum to zero. The checked rank-31 lift on 32 columns has the sole dependence equal to the full all-one support, ruling out this proper nonempty A. Hence D is indecomposable. The witness has dim D = 14 < 16, so D is a proper subspace of D⊥.

For C_D = {(u+d,u): u in D⊥, d in D}, the parameter map is injective and its dimension is w; the dot product reduces to terms pairing D with D⊥ and D with itself, all zero. Therefore it is self-dual. Since 1 is in D and D is contained in D⊥, (1,1) is in C_D, so it also contains the all-one word. The restrictions of C_D to either single copy are exactly D. A coordinate decomposition of C_D would induce decompositions of these two one-copy subcodes; indecomposability of D forces each full copy to stay in one side. A nontrivial split must separate the two copies, but for u in D⊥ minus D, (u,u) is in C_D while its one-copy restriction (u,0) is not. Contradiction. Thus C_D is indecomposable.

Over K, the algebraic closure of F₂, the seven coordinate bit rows show v and v⁻¹ are in D⊗K. The doubled code contains each in either copy. Hence z(t) = (v,tv) and z(t)⁻¹ = (v⁻¹,t⁻¹v⁻¹) lie in C_D⊗K for nonzero t. Since the listed v_i are distinct nonzero field elements, excluding the finite set t = v_i/v_j makes all 2w coordinates of z(t) nonzero and pairwise distinct. This leaves a nonempty open subset of the projective line over K. The projective parameter is injective because its first block is fixed to nonzero v. Therefore the reciprocal locus is positive-dimensional.

The checker’s polynomial quotient is F₂[x]/(x⁷+x+1); its exact test that every nonzero residue has a multiplicative inverse certifies it is a field. Its listed rank and dot-product checks are exact binary arithmetic. The curve constants are in F₁₂₈, so the proof does **not** imply points over arbitrary prime-degree extensions, resolve growing prime-extension girth, or establish an exact sampler.
