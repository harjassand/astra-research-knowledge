Independent audit: posterior programmes with fixed readouts
2026-10-24

Verdict
-------
The centered second-moment argument and the posterior weighting in Q5--Q9
of POSTERIOR_PROGRAM_FIXED_READOUT_OBSTRUCTION_DRAFT.txt are algebraically
correct under its explicit assumptions: independent Bernoulli graph bits,
Gamma-independent positive coefficients T_e, one fixed normal state phi,
and p_Gamma omega_Gamma <= phi on the same algebra containing T_e.
The complete dephased-record construction in Q10--Q11 supplies that order
for the stated finite-record source and actual retained branch outputs.

The useful scope boundary is exactly the one in the draft: this controls a
programme prepared from selected old records, then used with fresh
independent questions and fixed Gamma-blind readouts. It does not identify
the old selected question law with a new fresh-law strategy. Graph-aware
readouts, or a programme correlated with the realized graph outside the
selection order, are not covered.

Operator and weighted-normalization replay
------------------------------------------
Let S=sum_e T_e <= I, 0<=T_e<=bI, and
Z_Gamma=sum_e (Gamma_e-q)T_e. With xi_e=Gamma_e-q, independence gives

  E Z_Gamma^2
    = sum_(e,f) E(xi_e xi_f) T_e T_f
    = q(1-q) sum_e T_e^2
    <= q(1-q)b S
    <= q(1-q)b I.

The cross terms vanish before any operator reordering, so the T_e need
not commute with one another. For each graph, state Cauchy--Schwarz and
the order premise give

  beta_Gamma = omega_Gamma(W_Gamma)
    = q omega_Gamma(S)+omega_Gamma(Z_Gamma)
    <= q+sqrt(phi(Z_Gamma^2)/p_Gamma).

Thus p_Gamma(beta_Gamma-q)_+^2<=phi(Z_Gamma^2). Averaging is valid because
phi is fixed independently of Gamma. This gives the exact weighted bound

  E_Gamma p_Gamma(beta_Gamma-q)_+^2 <= q(1-q)b.

For pbar=E p_Gamma>0, the accepted-graph law has density
p_Gamma/pbar relative to the graph prior. Therefore

  E_selected (beta-q)_+^2
    = E[p_Gamma(beta-q)_+^2]/pbar
    <= q(1-q)b/pbar.

Since beta<=q+(beta-q)_+, Cauchy--Schwarz under this selected law gives

  E_selected beta <= q+sqrt(q(1-q)b/pbar).

This remains valid when p_Gamma varies with Gamma; there is no missing
factor pbar or square root of pbar. For the literal BG block source,
p_Gamma=|Gamma|/N^2 and E p_Gamma=q exactly, so with b=r/k^2 the bound is
q+sqrt((1-q)r/k^2), as stated in Q14. Q13 likewise follows by substituting
p_*=q/2 into Q5, giving 2(1-q)r/[k^2(a-q)^2].

Q10--Q11 source-order replay
----------------------------
For a complete Gamma-independent source instrument with finite dephased
record h, let tau_h be its positive normal output branch functional,
including each branch's retained quantum workspace. Completeness gives
phi=sum_h tau_h. For a classical selector e_Gamma(h) in [0,1],

  tau_Gamma=sum_h e_Gamma(h)tau_h,
  phi-tau_Gamma=sum_h(1-e_Gamma(h))tau_h >= 0.

Its trace p_Gamma=tau_Gamma(I) is the actual acceptance probability, and
for p_Gamma>0 its normalized branch state omega_Gamma=tau_Gamma/p_Gamma
satisfies the required functional order. Discarding or classically copying
records and any one fixed normal CP postprocessing preserve positive
functional order. Private Alice/Bob records can be represented jointly
for this order statement; that representation is not online
communication. For standard Borel/continuous fine records, replace the
finite direct sum by the corresponding direct integral and require a
measurable selector; the finite BG source itself has finite records.

Two scope cautions
-----------------
1. The order must compare functionals on the same output algebra on which
   the fixed T_e act. Q10--Q11 achieve this by defining phi after the
   complete dephased source instrument and retaining the branch outputs.
   A coherent Lüders postselection on an input vector does not in general
   imply p omega<=phi relative to its premeasurement state. The draft
   states this limit explicitly.
2. Independence is between Gamma and the fixed phi/T_e. A control register
   independent of Gamma can be included in the fixed apparatus and state.
   A register containing the realized Gamma, or readout effects chosen
   after Gamma, destroys the fixed-coefficient/independent-edge variance
   step unless its correlation and selection cost are separately included.

The apparent source paradox is resolved by the fresh-question interface.
In the literal repeated BG source, the old full strings U,V are both
answers and determine the tested live inputs U_i,V_i, so conditioning on
Gamma_UV=1 makes each old live round win with certainty. Q10--Q11 may
retain U,V in a graph-selected programme. But Q5--Q14 score that programme
only after supplying NEW independent uniform base questions and using the
same fixed local readouts. The edge event Gamma_UV=1 does not certify wins
for those new questions. Reusing U_i,V_i as the fresh questions, or
compiling readouts from Gamma, changes the interface and is not implied by
the substate order. Thus the source is a counterexample to a graph-blind
fresh-law repair, not to the variance theorem.

No script or new fixture was used. No unrestricted quantum/commuting
game-value gap is inferred.
