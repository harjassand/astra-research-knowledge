POST-REVIEW CLARIFICATION OF THE FROZEN MODEL-FIRST CHECKPOINT
2026-10-24. Added after authorized peer exposure; original checkpoint unchanged.

Root relayed the open-arm reviewer's correct quantization challenge:
AN+B3 -> A+BN+(3/2)H2 is balanced per nitrogen equivalent, but is not one
isolated elementary bimolecular reaction of two intact molecules. B3 stores
three H atoms; producing1.5 isolated H2 molecules requires an even number of
such turnovers or an explicit reversible odd-H storage intermediate.

The integer formula-level reaction is
 2AN+2B3 -> 2A+2BN+3H2.
Together with two known-family BN+4H2->B5+NH3 and two formal B5->B3+H2
steps it retains the same N2+3H2->2NH3 external sum after N2 cleavage.
The macroscopic gas balance and2.5RTln(phigh/plow) per-NH3 chosen-swing
work remain valid. They do not supply the microscopic reaction order.

The frozen checkpoint's generic bridge association AN+B<->I can be
bimolecular. Its following k_T completion edge is a lumped rate; for the
specific RuH3 receiver it cannot be assigned an intrinsic first-order or
isolated two-body atom-transfer barrier without resolving the odd-H fate.
A possible expanded bookkeeping would require a real H-storage state XH:
 I+X -> A+BN+H2+XH,
 2XH -> 2X+H2.
Here X/XH is an unacquired physical carrier and adds its own kinetics,
state energies and regeneration. This notation is not a proposed material,
does not license a favorable free-H atom, and does not establish those steps.
An interacting pair of bridge complexes is another possible expansion but
would require its own measured/calculated cooperative path. The paired
formula cannot simply be treated as an elementary fourth-order process.

Thus the candidate's central missing operation is stricter than a nominal
Mo-to-Ru N-transfer rate: acquire N relocation, Mo recovery AND an
integer-H-resolved dehydrogenation/recombination mechanism. The site-specific
15N crossover remains useful and should also quantify H2/HD/D2 and any
H-storage species. Observing Ru15N alone would establish relocation, not
complete regeneration of the H inventory. The Eyring target bounds in
cycle_gate_checks.py apply only conditionally to a genuinely specified
elementary bottleneck, not to this unacquired coarse T reaction.

This adds a microscopic closure gate to the original NOT_ESTABLISHED
result. It is not an impossibility theorem for the paired chemistry or for
other N-transfer receivers.
