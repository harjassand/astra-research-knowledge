1. WHOLE INSTRUMENT AND PHASE — PASS IN THE DECLARED SYMMETRIC MODEL

The compact checkpoint first defines a real native bright/dark rotation with
L_j=sqrt(gamma_j)a. At equal native input temperatures, the dark line is
independent of bright input and mechanics and is untouched by the interaction.
The free bright OUTPUT is allowed to be correlated across masses/time and with
mechanics. Its general trace-POVM instrument keeps these correlations; the
scalar B,D thermal density is only an illustrative subcase. This is correct.

For D(q,p)=exp[i(pQ-qP)], measured operator
Y=sqrt(f)P_B+sqrt(1-f)P_D+sqrt((1-eta)/eta)P_V, the identity

 exp(i g a Y)D_B(qa+cb,pb)
 = exp(i sqrt(f)g p ab/2)
   D_B((q-sqrt(f)g)a+cb,pb)
   D_D(-sqrt(1-f)g a,0)D_V(-sqrt((1-eta)/eta)g a,0)

follows directly from D(-sqrt(f)ga,0)D_B and its Weyl cocycle. I verified the
sign and factor independently. A single site has a residual antisymmetric
coherence phase; the identical, spin-swapped second site cancels it. The final
theta(g)=theta_raw+sum sqrt(f)g_k p_k is therefore valid for identical
responses/gains, not arbitrary asymmetric apparatuses. The two global residual
generators commute for precisely the same symmetry reason. Thus the full
joint Gaussian covariance gives a coherent ZZ phase followed by correlated
local Z rotations. A surviving theta is not enough to infer entanglement.

I separately integrated the scalar outcome density for all 16 pairs of spin
labels using 80-point Gauss-Hermite quadrature, at generic nonzero q,c,p,
f=.4, eta=.7, V_B=3,V_D=5 and feedback g=.31. Its Fourier integral agrees
with C exp[-W(g Delta a)^2/2+(g Delta a)L] to 1.2e-16. The diagonal is the
correct Gaussian N(sqrt(f)p_s,W). This checks the nontrivial outcome phase,
not just a dephasing covariance copied from the implementation.

For the general non-Gaussian statement the characteristic sign must follow the
chosen definition of G: their positive G=qP+W corresponds to a residual
exp(-i Delta.G). Its sign is irrelevant to the centered Gaussian covariance,
but mean local phases should not be silently dropped for displaced/nonGaussian
states. No defect follows because the trace-operator instrument fixes it.

2. CANCELLATION, ESTIMATION, AND HOT-DARK FLOOR — PASS

At g=q/sqrt(f), the bright self displacement is canceled for ANY bright output
state. The resulting dark self phase is -sqrt((1-f)/f)q P_D, so its variance
is A(1-f)V_D/f, with independent detector variance A nu/f. This differs from
the Gaussian variance-optimal g. The compact arm correctly reports changed
theta for each Wiener strategy and rescales each candidate by its OWN actual
theta in the entanglement calculation.

The scalar optimum q sqrt(f)V_B/[fV_B+(1-f)V_D+nu] is the Gaussian conditional
mean coefficient. Its posterior variance is independent of the outcome, so
nonlinear scalar phase choices cannot improve that conditional coherence
modulus. This restricted estimation result does not prove an all-instruments
or nonGaussian-QEC impossibility.

The correlated-output lower bound is also valid as stated. With ALL initial
mechanics and bath variances equal to v, collision beam splitters are
orthogonal, while the gravity drift symmetric part has norm |kappa|/(2omega).
Hence V_Bout >= v c0 I, c0=exp[-|kappa|T/omega]. For arbitrary local linear
homodyne gain g, the disjoint own/cross bright coefficients give

 Var(G_A) >= v c0 B
           +v[c0||q-sqrt(f)g||^2+(1-f)||g||^2].

Minimizing this relaxed quadratic gives exactly

 v c0 B + v c0(1-f)/(c0 f+1-f) A.

Passive quadrature/temporal row changes cannot improve this bound: their
normalized row map is a contraction, and the displayed minimization already
relaxes to arbitrary real gains. It is strictly O(v) for fixed T,A>0,f<1.
It does not automatically cover cold initial mechanics, singular limits where
T or amplitude change with v, joint inter-mass record sharing, active unpriced
ancillas or altered reservoirs. These restrictions matter; the compact text
states the all-initial-v contract and does not claim native hardware closure.

3. CONCRETE LATENT CODE DEFECT AND PRECISE REPAIR

native_multiport_check.py correctly computes the no-basis Wiener gain and the
no-basis fixed-phase Lagrange update. It also correctly restricts an ordinary
Wiener fit to range(T) when temporal_basis=T. However, when BOTH
strategy='wiener_fixed_phase' AND temporal_basis=T are supplied, it first fits
within T and then adds a full-space M^{-1}h correction. The final gain generally
leaves the allowed temporal subspace. That combination is a genuine API error.

For the matched-q single-mode basis in my 320-bin check (gamma=.08,kappa=.01,
f=.25,eta=.9,v=10.5), the implemented corrected gain has out-of-subspace norm
.0622116. Both it and the properly restricted repair satisfy the scalar phase
constraint, but their squared norms are .156235 and 2.421972 respectively.
That cost difference demonstrates why silently allowing additional temporal
modes is material. None of the frozen temporal examples calls fixed-phase
with temporal_basis, so all reported frozen cases remain unaffected.

Repair in basis coordinates:

 Mr=T^T M T, cr=T^T c, hr=T^T h,
 a0=Mr^{-1}cr, z=Mr^{-1}hr,
 g=T[a0+z(d-hr^T a0)/(hr^T z)], d=q^T p.

If hr=0 and d!=0 the phase target is infeasible in that basis; if hr=0,d=0
no phase adjustment is needed. Singular Mr requires an explicitly restricted
pseudoinverse/feasibility treatment, rather than division by zero. Root has
sent this repair to the compact code owner with original-byte preservation.

The recorded one-matched-mode case uses ordinary Wiener optimization, not the
deterministic cancellation coefficient. In its special uniform-hot, f=eta=1
fixture, theta differs from full cancellation by only 2.85e-12 (relative
1.57e-10), so equality at displayed precision is correct. It is not an exact
general identity of Wiener gain and cancellation. For an arbitrary one-mode
covariance, imposing the full nonzero phase forces its coefficient to the
deterministic value; retaining one matched mode is sufficient because that
value is explicitly available, not because every optimizer chooses it.

4. PAID INPUT-REFERENCE OPERATION — PASS WITH EXPLICIT COLD/RECEIVER COST

This is more than an unavailable purifier symbol. Coherently rotate the TWO
native incoming lines to B,D, mix D with an actual vacuum V0, route D_t back
through the inverse native rotation, and measure the separate tap R. In one
matched quadrature

 D_t=sqrt(1-r)D+sqrt(r)V0,
 R=sqrt(r)D-sqrt(1-r)V0.

The splitter really produces

 V_Dt=(1-r)v+r/2, V_R=rv+(1-r)/2,
 Cov(D_t,R)=sqrt(r(1-r))(v-1/2).

The determinant is v/2 and the posterior innovation variance is exactly
v/[2(rv+(1-r)/2)], approaching 1/(2r) for fixed r>0 as v grows. I recomputed
this covariance independently over v=10.5,1000.5,1000000.5 and r=.01,.1,1.

The later own-spin feedback retains coefficient q/sqrt(f) on the actual output
Y. Replacing Y by Y-sqrt(1-f)kR therefore leaves the same bright erasure and
ZZ phase; it changes only the independent dark phase to D_t-kR. The reference
record is acquired before the interaction and can be stored; spin-Z correction
is deferred, so there is no need to destructively measure a mode and then
pretend that same mode still enters the device. The surviving D_t is a distinct
physical output of the tap. Equal original input temperatures keep B independent
of D,V0,R; unequal temperatures require the full conditional covariance.

The reference homodyne in the stored paid-reference data is IDEAL. The eta=.9
parameter there belongs to the later OUTPUT detector. With normalized reference
detector vacuum variance nu_R, the actual innovation must be

 V_Dt-Cov(D_t,R)^2/(V_R+nu_R),

whose hot limit is 1/(2r)+(1-r)nu_R/r. The checkpoint does mention this
replacement; an experiment must separately budget both receiver efficiencies,
mode overlap, delay and vacuum preparation. A reference detector with its own
hot loss port does not receive a free vacuum-limited floor.

The paid-reference replay substitutes this exact posterior scalar as dark_v;
it does not explicitly simulate all tap/reference/return-path field modes.
That covariance reduction is sufficient for the ideal white Gaussian,
same-matched-quadrature model because D never couples to mechanics and is
independent of B. It is not a numerical demonstration of an actual broadband
native input network, frequency-dependent rotation, memory kernel or detector.
Those devices/transfer functions would require their full covariance map.

At r=1, D_t is vacuum and the old hot D exits as R; its record is unnecessary.
For v>1/2 every r<1 gives a larger innovation variance. Under the same freely
addressable input rotation and vacuum access, direct dark replacement is the
stronger simpler conventional baseline. If bright input can likewise be cooled,
that comparison is stronger still. The compact arm explicitly preserves these
baselines and makes no unsupported reservoir advantage claim.

5. TEMPORAL READOUT AND BANDWIDTH — PASS WITH THE STATED HARDWARE SPLIT

The exact logical target is one known whole-time phase-space vector v. A LO
selecting its conjugate P_f can erase that self record; it need not reconstruct
arbitrary mechanical x and p. All cross w remains. This is why one matched
temporal mode can suffice while one unrelated boxcar mode leaves an O(n)
component. Matched here includes the known instantaneous phase rotation of v,
not merely its scalar magnitude in an arbitrary fixed LO basis.

For post-homodyne stable electronics h in L1, a complete finite-variance
area statistic obeys integral(h*I)=H_e(0) integral I. If H_e(0)!=0, the already
selected scalar can be calibrated and spin feedback can wait. This does not
recover pre-photodetection quantum fields or permit integrating an infinite
stationary noise baseline. The compact addendum explicitly supplies the finite
window/tail/noise qualifications. It also correctly distinguishes a zero DC
area response from loss of all possible full-trace reconstruction.

For a PASSIVE QUANTUM filter, the physical unused port adds vacuum or its actual
occupation. Inverse-weighted LO g=h/H* requires finite L2 norm and physical
synthesizability, and pays variance (||g||^2-||h||^2)/2 for vacuum. Positive-
measure absent support leaves a hot direction. An isolated zero may instead
make inverse norm infinite without forming an L2 nullspace. This is consistent
with the warm explicit causal one-pole construction and is not the same as
electronically inverting shot-noise-filtered photocurrent.
