NONFLAT CYCLIC QUTRIT CLOCKS: AN EXACT LOCAL GATE AND A COMPILER OBSTRUCTION

Working internal derivation, 2026-10-24. Owner: /root/passive_lag.
This is a warm, source-exposed follow-on, not a cold comparison arm.
No claim of novelty, physical implementation, or a general autonomous-counterflow no-go.

1. Literal model and scope

There are N >= 4 cells on a ring, with independent d-dimensional quantum
registers A_i,B_i, d >= 2. Indices are modulo N. The desired logical unitary
beta moves every A_i input to A_(i+1), and every B_i input to B_(i-1).
Write
  S_i = SWAP(A_i,B_i),
  R_i = SWAP(B_i,A_(i+1)).
Then beta = (product_i R_i)(product_i S_i), with products within either
layer disjoint. An overall logical phase is immaterial.

Attach one PRIVATE qutrit clock to each of the 2N events S_i,R_i. Thus one
may group the two clocks in cell i into a fixed q=9 clock. The clock
initialization considered in this theorem is
  Phi = tensor_product_events phi_e,
where each phi_e is an arbitrary complex unit vector in C^3. This is a
literal allowed spatial product-clock initialization, with the additional
restriction that the two event clocks grouped in a cell are unentangled.
The product-moment proof in Sections 3-5 does NOT cover an arbitrary
entangled q=9 state inside a cell, or direct interactions between clocks.
Section 7 gives a separate, stronger conserved-sector proof for this
specific module and for specified sector-preserving clock interactions.

Set hbar=1 below: H denotes the frequency generator, so the physical
Hamiltonian is hbar H and its evolution is exp(-it H). Define clock matrices
  X = |0><1| + |1><0|,
  C = |0><2| + |2><0| + |1><2| + |2><1|.
For any event involution F_e in {S_i,R_i}, let
  h_e = g_e [ C_e + (X_e + I_e/4) F_e ],    g_e real,
  H = sum_e h_e.                                             (1)
Clock and data factors in each expression commute. Zero g_e is allowed.
Every term is onsite or nearest-neighbor. If |g_e| <= g_*, the norm of each
term is 9|g_e|/4 and a cell meets at most three terms, so an aggregate local
energy bound 27 hbar g_*/4 suffices. Local clock dimension, range, and this
bound are independent of N.

Adjacent h_e generally DO NOT commute: their data SWAPs overlap and fail
to commute. The qutrit transition triangle has edge updates F_e,I,I and
therefore nontrivial holonomy F_e. This is not a scalar flat-history gauge
construction, and it is not a number-conserving single-particle XY clock.

2. Each isolated module is an exact reusable SWAP

For one involution F=F*=F^(-1), put h0=C+X F. In the clock basis
  |+>=(|0>+|1>)/sqrt(2), |->=(|0>-|1>)/sqrt(2), |2>,
X has diagonal entries 1,-1,0 and C couples |+> to |2> with amplitude sqrt(2).
In the data sector F=s, s=+1 or -1, h0 has spectrum
  s=+1: 2,-1,-1;
  s=-1: 1,1,-2.
Equivalently,
  h0^2 = F h0 + 2I,     h0^3 = 3h0 + 2F.                  (2)
For g>0 and t0=2pi/(3g), exp(-it0 g h0)=exp(i 2pi F/3) on
ALL clock states. Including the data-only term gF/4 in (1) gives
  exp(-it0 h) = exp(i pi F/2) tensor I_clock = iF tensor I_clock. (3)
For g<0, t0=2pi/(3|g|) gives -iF instead. Thus the module performs a
SWAP times a scalar phase, with exact return of every clock state. Here
"i times SWAP" is NOT the distinct two-qubit gate customarily named iSWAP.

If the two layers of these modules were driven separately in time at the
isolated revival, they would implement beta up to phase and return clocks.
The candidate autonomous compiler instead uses the single static SUM (1),
allowing arbitrary initial local clock phases and arbitrary real g_e in
an attempt to obtain autonomous sequencing. The theorem below rules out
that summed compiler at ANY observation time, not merely at t0.

3. Endpoint moment constraint

Let V map a logical vector psi to psi tensor Phi. Suppose for some T>0
  exp(-iTH) V = exp(i chi) V beta.                           (4)
The finite-dimensional code projection P=VV* then reduces exp(-iTH).
Since H commutes with exp(-iTH), every compressed moment
  M_k=V*H^k V
commutes with beta. Ordered-polynomial cumulants therefore do as well.
We only need
  K1=M1,  K2=M2-M1^2,
and K3=M3 once M1=0. No analytic continuation or convergence of an
operator-log series is used.

We also use this elementary edge-support fact:
  If O=aI+sum_i a_i S_i+sum_i b_i R_i and [O,beta]=0,
  then all a_i,b_i are zero for N>=4.                       (5)
Proof: use any Hermitian Hilbert-Schmidt orthonormal basis
{I/sqrt(d),T_alpha} of d-by-d matrices. A SWAP on A_i,B_j is
  I/d + sum_alpha T_alpha^(A_i) T_alpha^(B_j).
Its nonidentity terms have exactly that labelled two-register support;
distinct edges give orthogonal such operator coordinates. The original
edge offsets j-i are {0,-1}. Conjugation by beta maps A_i to A_(i+1) and
B_j to B_(j-1), hence these offsets to {-2,-3}. For N>=4 the two sets
are disjoint modulo N. Equality of O and beta O beta* forces every
original two-register coefficient to vanish. This also handles arbitrary
inhomogeneous real/complex a_i,b_i and arbitrary d>=2.

4. First and second moments fix the clock expectations

For each clock write the REAL numbers
  x_e=<phi_e|X|phi_e>,   c_e=<phi_e|C|phi_e>.
These definitions allow arbitrary complex phi_e.
For a general scalar lambda, define
  h_e(lambda)=g_e[C_e+(X_e+lambda I)F_e].
We temporarily keep lambda symbolic; our compiler has lambda=1/4.
Its compressed first moment is
  E(h_e)=g_e[c_e I+(x_e+lambda)F_e].                         (6)
Here E is the product-clock compression. By (5), [K1,beta]=0 implies
  x_e=-lambda                                              (7)
for every active event g_e != 0. Inactive events do not matter.

Private product clocks give, with the data operator order preserved,
  E(h_e h_f)=E(h_e)E(h_f) for e!=f.
Consequently K2 is the sum of the single-event variances even though
the data SWAPs need not commute. From (2),
  h_e(lambda)^2/g_e^2
    = (1+2lambda) F_e h0_e + (2+lambda^2)I.
The coefficient of F_e in
E(h_e^2)-E(h_e)^2 is
  g_e^2 c_e(1-2x_e).
After (7), lambda=1/4, this is (3/2)g_e^2 c_e. A second application
of (5), now to K2, forces
  c_e=0                                                   (8)
for every active event. Thus E(h_e)=0 for every event, and M1=0.
There is no hidden impossibility in satisfying (7)-(8): for example
sqrt(3/8)|+>+sqrt(5/8)|-> has x=-1/4 and c=0. The obstruction is at
the next moment, not the nonexistence of an initial clock state.

5. The unavoidable third moment

The identities (2), with [F,h0]=0, imply
  (h0+lambda F)^3
    =3(1+lambda+lambda^2)h0
      +(2+6lambda+lambda^3)F.                              (9)
Using x=-lambda and c=0 in (9) gives
  E(h_e(lambda)^3)
    =g_e^3(1-lambda)(2lambda+1)(lambda+2)F_e.
At lambda=1/4 this is
  E(h_e^3)=(81/32)g_e^3 F_e.                              (10)

Every term in H^3 with more than one event index has an index appearing
exactly once. Partial expectation over that private clock replaces its
sole h_e by E(h_e)=0. This remains true for interleaved words such as
h_e h_f h_e, because partial expectation is a bimodule map over all
operators not acting on clock f; data noncommutation does not alter it.
Therefore
  K3=M3=(81/32)sum_e g_e^3 F_e.                            (11)
Endpoint condition (4) requires [K3,beta]=0. By (5), every g_e^3=0.
Hence every g_e=0. The resulting identity evolution cannot equal beta
on arbitrary data for d>=2,N>=4. This is the contradiction.

THEOREM. For N>=4 and d>=2, no Hamiltonian (1), no real choice of its
couplings, no product of arbitrary complex private qutrit clock states,
and no observation time T>0 implement exact counterflow with the clock
returned, even up to a global phase. The isolated modules (3) nevertheless
are exact reusable local SWAP modules.

6. Claim boundary and next operative escape

This is a decisive failure of one literal NONFLAT, noncommuting static
cyclic-clock compiler. It does not infer a general pure-clock no-go from
low-order moments, nor does it use the earlier global-winding example as
an approximation. For this particular H, the exact third moment itself
is already incompatible with the required endpoint symmetry.

The general question remains OPEN. Entangled clock states and direct
clock-clock interactions can change the mixed-index cancellations used
in Sections 3-5. Section 7 separately closes the entangled-state escape
for this particular module and specifies which clock-only interactions
are still excluded. Other local modules, sector-breaking clock dynamics,
and data-dependent configuration rules are not generally excluded. Any
escape must be analyzed as an actual local static Hamiltonian; an external
reset, a timed second layer, an unbounded/global rotor, or a clock state
not returned is not a solution of the pinned model.

An independently useful control: commuting finite-range Hamiltonians,
and finite-depth conjugates of onsite Hamiltonians, have a uniform-in-time
finite support bound for Heisenberg evolution. Exact clock reuse would
then give the same bound for every beta^k, contradicting its displacement
k. This excludes those localized architectures, but is NOT the general
interacting theorem and is NOT the main proof above.

Source exposure: prior warm context already contained the hopping-only
XY obstruction, timed two-layer SWAP realization, flat history-gauge
obstruction, and low-order moment/connected-cluster constraints. The
present candidate deliberately supplies a nonflat local cyclic module
with exact isolated return, then tests its interacting static sum. The
matrix identities and obstruction above are direct finite algebra; no
external theorem or priority claim is imported.

7. Stronger proposition: arbitrary pure clocks and sector-preserving interactions

The following proof is independent of the product-moment cancellations.
It applies to an ARBITRARY initial pure state Phi of all 2N qutrit clocks,
including arbitrary within-cell and across-cell entanglement. The initial
logical data are still product with this fixed Phi, and the endpoint is
still (4) for every logical state. It also permits
  H_total = sum_e h_e + H_C,                               (12)
where H_C acts on CLOCKS ONLY and satisfies [H_C,Q_e]=0 for every active
event, with
  Q_e=|->_e< -|,   |->=(|0>-|1>)/sqrt(2).
For the original physical contract, H_C must itself have fixed finite
range and a bounded aggregate local norm. The algebra below does not
otherwise require the clock interactions to commute with one another.

Directly,
  C Q=Q C=0,    X Q=Q X=-Q.
Thus every Q_e commutes with every original h_f, and with H_total under
the stated condition on H_C. On its range, the e-th data coupling is
  h_e Q_e = (-3g_e/4) F_e Q_e.                             (13)

Suppose g_e != 0 and Q_e Phi != 0. Because Q_e acts only on clocks and
commutes with exp(-iT H_total), applying Q_e to (4) gives
  exp(-iT H_total)(psi tensor Phi_e)
    = exp(i chi) beta psi tensor Phi_e,
  Phi_e=Q_e Phi/||Q_e Phi||.                               (14)
This is an inherited linear endpoint identity, not a free preparation or
postselection operation claimed for the original protocol.

The endpoint-to-moment argument of Section 3 applies to any fixed pure
clock vector, so it applies to Phi_e even though Phi_e may be entangled.
No product assumption is needed for its FIRST compressed moment:
  E_(Phi_e)(H_total)
    = scalar I + sum_f g_f[c_f I+(x_f+1/4)F_f].           (15)
The H_C contribution is scalar because H_C has no data action. For event
e, the projected state has x_e=-1,c_e=0. Hence the F_e coefficient in
(15) is -3g_e/4. Edge-support lemma (5) contradicts its required beta
invariance. We conclude
  Q_e Phi=0 for every active event.                       (16)

On ker Q, X has eigenvalues 0,+1, so (16) implies x_e>=0. But the
UNPROJECTED first compressed moment of H_total must also commute with
beta. Its H_C contribution again is scalar; (5) therefore forces
  x_e=-1/4
for every active event. This contradicts x_e>=0. Thus there are no active
events. With all g_e=0, H_total acts only on clocks and cannot implement
nonidentity beta on arbitrary data.

STRONGER PROPOSITION. For N>=4,d>=2, Hamiltonian (12) cannot implement
exact counterflow and return an arbitrary fixed PURE clock state, even
globally entangled, at any T>0. It permits arbitrary clock-only
configuration-dependent interactions satisfying the explicit conserved
Q_e condition; in particular it covers all spatial product q=9 clock
initializations for the original H. No mixed-clock extension is asserted.

The operative obstruction is MODULE-SPECIFIC. The negative eigenspace of
the clock coupling X+I/4 is the conserved |-> sector, where the coupling
is fixed at -3/4. Exact reuse projects the same logical endpoint into
that sector, which is already incompatible with the first moment. Avoiding
the sector forces positive coupling expectation, also incompatible with
the first moment. Clock entanglement cannot change this sign geometry.

An attempted repair must therefore break this argument's premises: for
example, mix |-> with the bright clock subspace through a term not
commuting with Q_e, change the module's clock-coupling sign structure, or
add new data-dependent interactions that alter (15). A term proportional
to |0><0|-|1><1| is a literal onsite sector-breaking clock perturbation,
but it also changes the isolated revival (3), which must then be proved
again. No successful compiler follows merely from breaking the invariant.
