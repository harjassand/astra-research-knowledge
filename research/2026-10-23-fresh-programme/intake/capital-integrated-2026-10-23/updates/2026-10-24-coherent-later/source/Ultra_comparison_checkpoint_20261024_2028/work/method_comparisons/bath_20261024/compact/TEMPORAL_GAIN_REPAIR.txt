POST-REVIEW COMPACT TEMPORAL-ACCESS REPAIR
Root/warm review identified a real latent bug in the frozen script. When
wiener_fixed_phase was combined with temporal_basis=T, its phase correction
used M^-1h, which need not lie in ran(T). No saved case exercised that pair.
The frozen original/checkpoint/data/source log and their hash list are intact.

Revised implementation: native_multiport_check_revised.py.
For M_R=T^T M T, c_R=T^T c and h_R=T^T h, solve in the available coordinates:
 g0=T M_R^-1 c_R,
 z=T M_R^-1 h_R,
 g=g0+z(target-h^Tg0)/(h^Tz).
If h_R=0 and target-h^Tg0 is nonzero, raise explicit infeasibility; if both
are zero retain g0. Tiny NONZERO h_R remains an accessible direction, possibly
with a very large physical gain/precision cost. M must be positive definite on
the declared independent temporal basis.

Focused test: python3 check_temporal_constraint_repair.py.
Results saved as TEMPORAL_CONSTRAINT_REPAIR_RESULTS.json. The old leaked gain
norm outside the allowed one-mode space is .2606865303313; revised leakage and
phase error are zero. An inaccessible nonzero target is rejected. A tiny
nonzero projection is retained. Unrestricted gains match the original formula.
A public evaluate() regression checks a two-bin symmetric Gaussian channel:
only bin1 is accessible, actual and target theta=-.14, gain norm^2=.64,
residual covariance=.425 I. Thus passing the phase constraint by importing a
missing temporal mode would fail this test.

No broad scientific replay/data overwrite was performed for this fix. The
revised script default output is native_multiport_data_revised.json.
