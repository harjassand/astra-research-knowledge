RESULT AND STATUS

For the declared two-native-waveguide thermal amplitude-damping topology, one
known spatiotemporal homodyne quadrature per mass is sufficient to erase the
dominant local-spin bath record. It does not require reconstructing mechanics,
measuring a purifier, measuring both bath quadratures, or real-time mechanical
feedback. The measured mode must be generated from the actual native outputs.

An explicit local collector samples every coupled native output, compensates
propagation delays/phases, and combines the samples with inverse-loss weights.
Even unequal small vacuum taps can then measure the SAME native bright mode,
with a calculable effective vacuum efficiency. Missing an independent hot port
is different. A specified non-bright captured row leaves a hot residual, and
the exact coupled-probe instrument below retains its phase and dephasing.

A finite causal passive quantum filter with nonzero transfer admits a concrete
inverse-matched LO construction. Its finite response costs vacuum efficiency,
not an additional hot dimension, when the target mode is in the inverse domain.
A truly unavailable temporal/spectral subspace leaves a hot projection error.
These are conditional mathematical operations, not acquisition of a native
mechanical bath output. No new principle, historical priority, demonstrated
gravity entangler, or practical advantage is established.

1. DECLARED NATIVE MODEL AND RESOURCES

 Hm=sum_i[p_i^2/(2m)+m omega^2 x_i^2/2]+K xA xB,

This assumes a quantum bilinear Newtonian cross-spring; it is not independent
evidence about a gravitational mediator. No optical/phononic quantum channel
joins apparatus A and B. All collectors, loss ancillas, LO fields, receivers,
and record-dependent spin rotations stay within their own apparatus.

Each oscillator couples through its SAME annihilation operator a to two actual
independent thermal Markov input waveguides. Let c_j=sqrt(gamma_j),
gamma=gamma_1+gamma_2. In quadratures Q=sqrt(m omega/hbar)x,
P=p/sqrt(m hbar omega), [Q,P]=i, the vacuum covariance is I/2. The supplied
beam-splitter/collision dilation has amplitude damping gamma/2 and output

 dY_j=dW_j-c_j R dt,  dR=A0 R dt+sum_j c_j dW_j.

There are two quadratures per port and two local mechanical pairs; the compact
notation repeats the appropriate local blocks. Thermal inputs can have unequal
v_j=n_j+1/2. This is a local quantum-optical D[a] bath, not automatically a true
Ohmic position-friction reservoir or a global Gibbs bath of coupled Hm.

Paid access resources: both chosen native output waveguides must physically

    =exp[i s t Omega(v,w)/2]<y_P|D(t w).

This follows by Weyl factorization and <y_P|D(sv)=exp(-i q s y)<y_P|.

averaging the outcomes the residual is its FULL output characteristic. The

It corrects a known rank-one logical self record, not arbitrary x,p errors.

 rho_ss' -> rho_ss' exp[i theta_h(t)(sA sB-sA' sB')]

6. ACTUALLY GENERATING THE BRIGHT MEASURED OUTPUT

For gamma_j>0, physically route both waveguide ends to one local hybrid. If
propagation phases/delays differ, add passive delay tau_max-tau_j and carrier
phase correction to each earlier path. This is causal waiting, not a quantum
link between the masses. Dispersion must be compensated over the measured
mode. Delay-line loss belongs in the following channel budget.

Without tap loss use bright row h_j=c_j/sqrt(gamma). The orthogonal native
output is dark to the conditional force. It can remain hot and correlated with
the bright output; there is no conditional displacement in it.

More generally each physically routed output is sampled by a vacuum tap

 b_col,j=sqrt(eta_j)b_out,j+sqrt(1-eta_j)v_j,  eta_j>0.

Choose the normalized passive hybrid weights

 h_j=sqrt(gamma_j/eta_j)/sqrt(S), S=sum_j gamma_j/eta_j.

Then its one detected output is exactly

 b_det=sqrt(eta_sp)b_bright+sqrt(1-eta_sp)v_eff,
 eta_sp=gamma/S,
 b_bright=sum_j c_j b_out,j/sqrt(gamma).

The added mode is vacuum, even for unequal native temperatures. The effective
native coefficient equation fixes h, and normalization proves eta_sp maximal
among passive rows that reproduce this exact native bright mode. More receiver
vacuum inputs cannot improve it. A pair of separate homodynes with calibrated
classical weighting is another implementation, but uses two receivers; the
coherent hybrid permits one. This is one bright detector, not unit emitted
power capture. Small eta_j are allowed mathematically and worsen the vacuum
floor; they do not produce an n factor.

If some gamma_j>0 has eta_j=0, the same native bright row cannot be synthesized
by any unitary acting on already captured fields. Independent hot missing
directions remain. This is an actual topology obstruction: the missing port
must be routed, its native coupling removed, or its input/covariance changed
with a paid resource. Passive postprocessing alone cannot replace it.

Thermal loss ancillas invalidate the vacuum-only floor: their contribution is
sum_j h_j^2(1-eta_j)(n_loss,j+1/2), not (1-eta_sp)/2. Merely labelling a lossy
cryogenic line a detector inefficiency does not establish its vacuum state.

7. FINITE RESPONSE, MATCHED LO, AND TRUE MISSING BANDWIDTH

After the spatial collector consider an actual PASSIVE QUANTUM output filter
with causal transfer H_B(Omega)=B/(B-i Omega), Fourier convention exp(iOmega t).
Its complementary transfer R_B=1-H_B is coupled to a vacuum input. The matrix
[[H_B,R_B],[R_B,H_B]] is unitary on the real frequency axis and causal; it is
a legitimate two-port passive resonator filter. It is not an electronic filter
of a completed homodyne current, which filters shot noise and signal together
and has a different information accounting.

Let f be the normalized known native self temporal mode and assume its whole-
line zero extension is H^1. Define an actual predetermined LO waveform

 g=H_B^{-dagger} f/sqrt(J)=(f-f'/B)/sqrt(J),
 J=||H_B^{-dagger}f||^2=1+||f'||^2/B^2.

Then ||g||=1 and H_B^dagger g=f/sqrt(J). The measured output-mode quadrature is

 P_g(out)=sqrt(eta_bw)P_f(native)+sqrt(1-eta_bw)P_vac,
 eta_bw=1/J.

This is not an active causal inverse of a lossy channel: g is a known LO
envelope, and the filter remains causal. Compact smooth f vanishing at its
endpoints makes g compact as well; the adjoint overlap cancels the filter tail
and any pre-window homogeneous filter response. Very small B makes eta_bw
small and the normalized feedback noise/record range large. The LO derivative,
phase/amplitude range, receiver response and carrier hierarchy must be built.
The proof supplies their required waveform, not unlimited modulation hardware.

Spatial taps, this common quantum filter and a vacuum-loss detector give

The complete instrument is section 4 with the FULL native bright response,

10. WHAT THE OPERATION OPENS AND WHAT STILL BLOCKS ACQUISITION

The next concrete interface is a LOCAL inverse-loss bright receiver with a
matched single temporal LO and deferred own-spin phase, not whole-mechanics
feedback. The construction specifies actual hybrid weights, passive delays,
filter scattering, LO waveform, feedback coefficient, and the complete
coherent/dephasing spin map. Before proposing a gravity device, measure native
channel-resolved coupling vectors/temperatures, physical output routing and
loss ancilla temperatures; then synthesize this row and compare the recorded
quadrature correlation to the predicted output kernel. Missing native loss
cannot be hidden inside one detector efficiency.

Finite calibration error leaks the dominant hot mode: relative gain/phase or
mode-vector error zeta produces a residual of order V_max ||v||^2 zeta^2,
alongside cross terms and changed Weyl phase. Keeping that error fixed as n
grows generally requires precision of order n^{-1/2}. Normalized feedback
record and electronics dynamic range also grow with the native hot variance,
even though its ideal residual can be vacuum limited. Spin-coherence time,
feedback calibration, finite LO modulation and mechanical pulse force/slew
remain paid resources. Delayed local Z correction avoids the force-only
instantaneous-actuator cost of whole-mode correction.

Existing microgram maglev position/SQUID interfaces do not demonstrate access
to their dominant flux-creep/intrinsic outputs. A new two-waveguide engineered
port must face same-resource cold input and port-off baselines. A red-sideband
eliminated cavity requires pulse bandwidth << cavity linewidth << omega; the
one-period displayed pulse does not satisfy that hierarchy. The native D[a]
topology and no nongravitational link are supplied model assumptions. A larger
gravity capability remains NOT_ACHIEVED; actual native collectability,
compatible force/spin apparatus, priority and practical advantage are UNKNOWN.
