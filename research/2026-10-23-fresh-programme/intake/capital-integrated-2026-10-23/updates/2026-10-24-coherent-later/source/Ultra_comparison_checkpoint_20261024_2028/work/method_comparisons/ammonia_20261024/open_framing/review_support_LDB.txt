# Independent exact check: detailed balance, ring current, and conditional costs

Scope: this is an independent algebra/unit audit of the requested three-state
ring, gate-work/LDB statements, and conditional numerical conversions. It does
not supply a Mo-to-Ru rate, a physical gate, or process-performance evidence.
The ring formula below applies to a time-homogeneous reversible three-state
Markov chain at fixed controls/reservoirs.

## 1. Exact three-state ring

Use states 0, 1, 2, forward edge i -> i+1 (mod 3) at rate u_i, and reverse
edge i+1 -> i at rate w_i. Directed matrix-tree weights for stationary trees
rooted at states 0, 1, 2 are respectively

    tau_0 = w_0 u_2 + u_1 u_2 + w_0 w_1,
    tau_1 = u_0 w_1 + w_2 w_1 + u_0 u_2,
    tau_2 = w_2 u_1 + u_0 u_1 + w_0 w_2.

Each term is one of the three directed in-trees into its root. Thus
pi_i=tau_i/D, D=tau_0+tau_1+tau_2>0. The current on edge 0 is

    J = u_0 pi_0 - w_0 pi_1
      = (u_0 tau_0 - w_0 tau_1)/D
      = (u_0 u_1 u_2 - w_0 w_1 w_2)/D.

The same current flows on all three edges by stationary probability
conservation. Expanding D gives exactly the nine positive terms in
cycle_gate_checks.py. Therefore sign(J) is the sign of the total cycle
affinity A=ln[(u_0u_1u_2)/(w_0w_1w_2)].

For the file's illustrative rates

    u=(100, 0.1 epsilon, 100),  w=(1, 10 epsilon, 1)   [s^-1],

the middle-edge ratio is u_1/w_1=0.01. With no chemical/work exchange on that
edge, LDB assigns it generalized uphill free energy
Delta G_1^gen = -kBT ln(0.01)=kBT ln(100). The other ratios are 100 and 100,
so total affinity remains ln(100)>0 and forward stationary current is
positive. Direct expansion gives

    D = 10101 + 1040.1 epsilon,
    J = 990 epsilon / (10101 + 1040.1 epsilon) s^-1.

Numerically, J(1)=0.0888601664 s^-1 and
J(10^-6)=9.80100879e-8 s^-1. As epsilon -> 0,

    J/epsilon -> 990/10101 = 0.0980101 s^-1.

Scaling both directions of that edge by epsilon preserves its LDB ratio and
the cycle affinity but makes throughput vanish linearly. This is the precise
thermodynamic/kinetic separation illustrated by the code. Any one edge can be
the uphill edge after cyclic relabeling, provided the other edge ratios leave
positive net affinity. The artificial rates are a mathematical witness, not
an acquired material model.

## 2. Gate-work signs and LDB boundary

At fixed occupation n, changing a control parameter from gate 0 to gate 1
does work ON the machine

    W_on(n)=F_1(n)-F_0(n),
    W_reset(n)=F_0(n)-F_1(n) for 1 -> 0.

If the A gate is switched on while A-bound nitrogen is present and reset only
after A is empty, the net is

    [F(AN,1)-F(AN,0)] + [F(A,0)-F(A,1)] = b_A1-b_A0,

because the empty-gate baseline cancels and the same nitrogen chemical
potential appears in both binding free energies. Likewise, if a bound B
intermediate is switched from receive to release gate and reset after release,
the corresponding net is b_B,release-b_B,receive. Positive values are work
input under the support note's convention. The example values therefore give

    chemistry: [-1.00, -0.10, -0.07] eV/N; sum = -1.17 eV/N,
    gate work: [+0.60, +0.40] eV/N; sum = +1.00 eV/N,
    total: -0.17 eV/N,

so the asserted ledger identity and signs check. If empty-gate energies differ
without cancellation, or switching occurs at other occupancies, the bare
binding-shift formula must be amended to use the full F_g(n) values. Do not
charge the same regeneration both as reservoir fuel and as external work.

For an elementary reversible chemical/thermal jump at fixed gate setting,
the stated convention

    Delta G_e^gen = F_y-F_x - sum_r(mu_r nu_r) - w_e,
    ln(k_xy/k_yx) = -beta Delta G_e^gen

has the correct sign: incoming reservoir matter and positive work on the
machine favor the forward rate ratio. Here nu_r is positive into the system.
An autonomous stochastic redox/ligand gate can be included as ordinary
microstates and must itself obey LDB, with every chemical reservoir included.

A deterministic time-dependent gate switch is different: it is parameter
work F(x,lambda_after)-F(x,lambda_before), not a stochastic edge with a
k_forward/k_reverse ratio. LDB can be used for reaction jumps at each fixed
instantaneous control setting. For a fully specified forward protocol and its
time-reversed protocol, a completed path obeys the usual stochastic first-law
accounting; with a closed internal state, the sum of jump log-rate ratios is
beta times (net incoming-reservoir chemical potential + work delivered by
the protocol), provided the work and all reservoir exchanges are included.
Thus the cycle relation in support_detailed_balance.txt is sound as a
pathwise thermodynamic accounting identity under that convention, but it is
not an intrinsic stationary affinity for a periodically controlled network.
If switching is triggered by a measurement/feedback decision, controller
work and the measurement/feedback information ledger also need a model.

Consequently the three-state tree/current formula is valid for a fixed,
time-homogeneous rate ring. It cannot be directly assigned to a periodically
switched gate or interpreted as a stationary current there; those require a
periodic-state or full driven-path calculation. The fixed-rate numerical
example does not establish the behavior of a controller-driven material.

## 3. Eyring target and measured-rate arithmetic

Using the script's target values, the product flux is

    r_NH3 = FE*j/(3F)
          = 3.38566142e-7 mol s^-1 cm^-2
          = 1.21883811 mmol h^-1 cm^-2.

At the explicitly illustrative density 10^15 modules cm^-2 this is
203.8893 turnovers s^-1 per module. For a unimolecular Eyring-limited step
with transmission coefficient kappa=1, k=(kBT/h)exp(-DeltaG‡°/kBT). For a
bimolecular step with a 1 M standard state and an ideal 20 mM partner,

    k_pseudo = (kBT/h) a_B exp(-DeltaG‡°/kBT),  a_B=0.020.

Hence the largest compatible standard-state barrier is

    DeltaG‡°_max = kBT ln[(kBT/h) a_B / k_target].

The activity factor is required; omitting it overstates the allowed barrier
by kBT ln(1/0.02), about 0.1005 eV at 298.15 K. Independent evaluation gives:

| T (K) | unimolecular max (eV) | bimolecular standard max at 20 mM (eV) |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 298.15 | 0.62022 | 0.51971 |
| 400 | 0.84222 | 0.70737 |
| 573 | 1.22423 | 1.03106 |

These are optimistic ceilings because transmission coefficient 1 is assumed;
they inherit the chosen site inventory and current/FE target. The separate
Woo/Mn exchange figures in the script convert correctly: 5600 M^-1s^-1 at
0.020 M -> 112 s^-1; 0.010 M^-1s^-1 at 0.020 M -> 2.0e-4 s^-1. This audit
checks those unit conversions conditional on the quoted literature inputs;
it does not independently re-audit the source experiments. The two values
belong to different Mn exchange reactions and cannot be substituted for a
Mo-to-B transfer rate.

## 4. Energy and attrition conversions

Using the script's NH3 molar mass 17.03052 g/mol and constants:

- 1 tonne NH3 = 58,718.113 mol. One eV per NH3 is 5.66544 GJ/t or
  1.57373 MWh/t. At three electrons per NH3, an extra 1 V is 16.9963 GJ/t.
- A 450 nm photon is 2.755204 eV; one incident photon per NH3 is
  15.6094 GJ/t or 4.33595 MWh/t before LED, coupling, or quantum-yield losses.
- Stoichiometric 1.5 mol H2 per NH3 is 177.553 kg H2/t. At the explicitly
  assumed 50 kWh/kg, that is 8.87765 MWh/t.
- For 2.5 mol ideal H2 returned from low to high pressure per NH3,
  T=323.15 K and p_high/p_low=1000 give
  2.5 RT ln(1000)=46.3997 kJ/mol NH3 = 2.72450 GJ/t = 0.756807 MWh/t.
  This verifies the reversible ideal-gas work conversion, not the physical
  need for that pressure swing or real compressor energy.
- 300 h at one completed turnover per second is N=1,080,000 turnovers.
  Under independent irreversible loss probability q each turnover, retaining
  half the initial inventory requires (1-q)^N=1/2, hence
  q=1-exp[ln(1/2)/N]=6.41803e-7 per turnover.

These conversions are internally consistent. They are conditional
bookkeeping/target checks and do not constitute acquired process performance,
measured selectivity, or a physical lifetime result.
