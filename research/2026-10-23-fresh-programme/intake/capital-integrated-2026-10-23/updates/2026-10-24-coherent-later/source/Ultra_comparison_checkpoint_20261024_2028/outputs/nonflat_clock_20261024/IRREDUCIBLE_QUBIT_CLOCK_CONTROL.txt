1. Literal module

Let n,m be nonparallel real unit vectors in R^3, and sigma the Pauli vector
on one private qubit clock. Define the Hermitian clock matrices
  C = I/2 + (n/2+m) dot sigma,
  Y = (n/2-m) dot sigma.                                   (1)
For any Hermitian data involution F, put
  h = g (C+Y F),    g real.                                (2)
Use hbar=1, so physical energy is hbar h. For F=s, s=+1 or -1,
  C+Y = I/2+n dot sigma,   spectrum {-1/2,3/2};
  C-Y = I/2+2m dot sigma,  spectrum {-3/2,5/2}.             (3)
At t0=pi/g for g>0, the two sectors therefore have phases +i,-i:
  exp(-it0 h)=iF tensor I_clock.                           (4)
For g<0, t0=pi/|g| gives -iF. Every clock state returns. This is a
scalar phase times SWAP when F is SWAP, not the gate usually named iSWAP.
The module norm is 5|g|/2.

This module breaks the triangle's conserved-sector premise:
  [C,Y]=-2i (n cross m) dot sigma != 0.                    (5)
Two noncommuting Hermitian 2-by-2 matrices have no common nontrivial
reducing clock projection. Thus there is no private clock sign sector
of the kind used in Section 7 of the triangle source.

2. Static counterflow compiler and initialization scope

On N>=4 ring cells, take independent d-dimensional data A_i,B_i, d>=2,
and events F_e consisting of
  S_i=SWAP(A_i,B_i),   R_i=SWAP(B_i,A_(i+1)).
The target beta sends A data right and B data left, so beta=(product R_i)
(product S_i). Attach one private qubit clock per event and use the
single time-independent Hamiltonian
  H=sum_e g_e(C_e+Y_e F_e).                                (6)
Axes and real g_e may be different for different events. Initial clock
state Phi is a product over the 2N PRIVATE EVENT CLOCKS of arbitrary
complex unit vectors. Grouping two clocks in a cell gives fixed q=4;
an entangled state of those two clocks is NOT covered by this theorem.
No extra clock-clock or data-dependent interactions are included in (6).
Every term is onsite or nearest-neighbor. With |g_e|<=g_*, aggregate local
energy norm at most (15/2)hbar g_* suffices, independently of N.

3. Exact endpoint forces an impossible second moment

Suppose exp(-iTH)(psi tensor Phi)=exp(i chi) beta psi tensor Phi for
all data psi at some T>0. For V psi=psi tensor Phi, the code projection
VV* reduces exp(-iTH). Since H commutes with its exponential, all
M_k=V*H^kV commute with beta; in particular K1=M1 and K2=M2-M1^2 do.
This does not assume [H,VV*]=0 or choose a logarithm branch.

An operator aI+sum_i a_i S_i+sum_i b_i R_i commutes with beta only if
all a_i,b_i vanish: its traceless two-register SWAP components have
offsets {0,-1}, whereas beta conjugation gives {-2,-3}, disjoint for
N>=4. Distinct labeled edges have orthogonal traceless components for
every d>=2. This is the same direct edge-support lemma as in the stable
triangle source; no large-ring asymptotic is used.

Write c_e=<C_e>, y_e=<Y_e>. The first moment has edge coefficient g_e y_e,
so every active event satisfies
  y_e=0.                                                  (7)
Private product clocks imply E(h_e h_f)=E(h_e)E(h_f) for e!=f, with
data order retained. Thus
  K2=sum_e [E(h_e^2)-E(h_e)^2].
The coefficient of F_e in the e-th summand is
  g_e^2 [ <C_eY_e+Y_eC_e> - 2c_e y_e ].                    (8)
Pauli algebra gives the exact operator identity
  C Y+Y C = Y - (3/2)I,                                  (9)
because (n/2+m) dot (n/2-m)=1/4-1=-3/4. On (7), (8) is
  -(3/2)g_e^2,
independent of the initial clock state and of n dot m. Therefore
  K2=scalar I-(3/2)sum_e g_e^2 F_e.                        (10)
The edge-support lemma and [K2,beta]=0 force every g_e=0. Clock-only
identity data evolution cannot equal beta on all data for N>=4,d>=2.

THEOREM. No static sum (6), with the stated private product clock
initialization, implements exact reusable-clock counterflow at any T>0.
Every isolated module nevertheless implements an exact reusable SWAP,
and its clock algebra is irreducible rather than sector-preserving.

4. Exact scope of the decision

This checks a literal sector-breaking local module, so removing the
triangle's dark clock sector alone does not solve autonomous sequencing.
The obstruction here is the state-independent mixed second cumulant.
Entangled event clocks, added clock interactions, other irreducible
modules, and general configuration-dependent local Hamiltonians remain
outside this theorem. In particular the general pure-product-clock
counterflow question remains OPEN. No claim follows about approximate
return, clock-reset operations, or a time-dependent two-layer SWAP circuit.
