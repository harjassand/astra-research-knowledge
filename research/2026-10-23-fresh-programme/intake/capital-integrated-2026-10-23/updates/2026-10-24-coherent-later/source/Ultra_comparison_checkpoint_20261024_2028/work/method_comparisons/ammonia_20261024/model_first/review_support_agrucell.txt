Independent source check — AgRu nitrate/HOR cell

Primary: Zhang et al., “Exploiting underpotential deposited hydrogen enables energy-efficient nitrate electroreduction to ammonia,” Nature Communications 17, 4652 (2026), DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-71299-x. Local acquired sources: open main article HTML `open_framing/sources/Zhang_2026_AgRu.html`; SI text/PDF `open_framing/sources/41467_2026_71299_MOESM1_ESM.txt` / `.pdf`; source-data workbook `open_framing/sources/41467_2026_71299_MOESM5_ESM.xlsx` (sheet names below). This is a source-level check of reported operations and data, not an independent experimental replication.

Paired nitrate/HOR operation and concurrent evidence

The main Methods subsection “Paired NO3RR-HOR systems” specifies a 25 °C, membrane-free, two-electrode flow assembly: AgRu-15 cathode (0.5 × 0.5 cm Ag mesh, 0.25 cm²) and commercial 20 wt% Pt/C GDE on carbon paper (2 × 2 cm, 0.5 mg cm−2) HOR anode. The paired LSV is scanned at 10 mV s−1 with H2 at 20 sccm. The 100 h chronoamperometry is at fixed 200 mA cm−2, in flowing 1 M KOH + 100 mM KNO3 at 6 mL min−1, with 10 sccm H2 to the anode; the Methods explicitly says the electrolyte was renewed nine times at 11–12 h intervals.

The source does support the existence of simultaneous net-power output and NH3 production in this nitrate/HOR cell. SI p. 57, Fig. S54 and accompanying text explicitly report a cell operating above 200 mA cm−2 with about 81% NH3 FE at −0.1 V, and the main Results discuss the paired system and refer to Fig. 6c–d. Figure 6d source data (workbook sheet `Figure  6d`, columns A:B, 24 NH3-FE measurements at elapsed times 2–99 h; voltage traces in D:U) shows fixed-current operation with all logged cell voltages negative: −0.11778 to −0.02669 V over 0.00306–100.20778 h. The 24 reported FE values are 76.20903–84.61244%, arithmetic mean 80.99524%. Interpolating the voltage log to those FE sampling times gives −0.09499 to −0.03964 V (mean −0.07151 V); this is a cross-check of concurrent, same-run FE and voltage, not a source-reported paired statistic. Thus the SI’s “about 81% / −0.1 V” is a representative operating claim, while the long run independently supports roughly 81% FE and below-zero cell voltage during 200 mA cm−2 operation. It is not a closed-electrolyte 100 h run: the paper reports nine renewals plus the 6 mL min−1 flow, and does not specify the exchanged volume per renewal in the cited Methods passage.

The paired polarization curves are in workbook `Figure  6c` (10 mM: A:B; 50 mM: D:E; 100 mM: G:H; 500 mM: J:K; 1000 mM: M:N; rows contain j and full-cell voltage). Linear interpolation of the source LSV data at 200 mA cm−2 gives +0.20665 V at 10 mM (A596:B597), −0.08783 V at 50 mM (D97:E98), −0.10903 V at 100 mM (G275:H276), −0.14603 V at 500 mM, and −0.15469 V at 1000 mM. The sign therefore switches between the measured 10 and 50 mM curves at 200 mA cm−2; an exact concentration threshold was not measured. The zero-voltage current crossings are also interpolated, not directly held points: 10 mM, 117.72 mA cm−2 (A389:B390); 50 mM, 305.34 (D126:E127); 100 mM, 346.22 (G384:H385); 500 mM, 433.76 (J390:K391); and 1000 mM, 457.27 (M387:N388). These are 10 mV s−1 LSV outputs, not steady-state concentration sweeps or long-duration holds. The 100 mM point used for stability is one of the directly measured feed concentrations.

Hydrogen and gas accounting boundary

The acquired main Methods and SI quantify dissolved NH3/NH4+, NO2−, and NO3− using separate colorimetric/UV–vis calibrations; they describe 15N labeling for nitrate-derived NH3 and the H2 feed to the anode. I found no cathode H2 Faradaic efficiency, cathode/headspace or outlet gas analysis, or H2 recovery measurement in these acquired article/SI methods and source-data panels. The 81% NH3 FE therefore leaves 19% of charge unassigned by that NH3-only paired-cell FE and cannot be labeled HER or cathodic H2 production from these data. SI Eq. 19’s 579.9 kg H2/day for its 1000 kg NH3/day scenario is an electron-stoichiometric anodic-H2 throughput calculation from total current (one H2 per 2 e−), not a measured fresh-H2 balance. For FE_NH3=0.81, the gross anodic-H2 stoichiometry is 4/0.81 ≈ 4.94 mol H2 per mol NH3. If rho is recovery of cathode-generated H2, the conditional fresh-H2 expression is (4/FE_NH3)(1−rho·FE_H2); this source supplies neither FE_H2 nor rho. The stated 10 sccm anode feed is an imposed flow, not an outlet-consumption assay.

What the nitrite half-cell data establish

SI p. 21, Fig. S20c/e and workbook `Supplementary Figure 20` (S20c AgRu-15 columns S:T; background-subtracted S20e columns AI:AJ) show total nitrite-reduction LSV current in 1 M KOH + 10 mM KNO2. Near +0.1 V vs RHE, the closest S20c point is 0.10060 V / −34.01963 mA cm−2 (S538:T538); after the displayed background subtraction, S20e is 0.10060 V / −31.86005 mA cm−2 (AI532:AJ532). This is total current, not product-specific FE.

The product evidence is separate: SI p. 22, Fig. S21d and workbook `Supplementary Figure 21`, S4:T9 (AgRu-15, 1 M KOH + 10 mM KNO2, 0.1 V vs RHE, 0–100 min) report nitrite-N declining 139.06219→53.30556 μg mL−1 and NH3-N increasing 0→85.75663 μg mL−1. The Methods’ indophenol-blue/NH4Cl calibration makes this a reported liquid-phase NH3 assay in a nitrite-fed half-cell. It does not provide a contemporaneous charge/current trace or nitrite-feed NH3 FE, isotope tracing for nitrite, paired HOR cell voltage, or 200 mA cm−2 nitrite performance. The separately reported S20 current and S21 product curves should not be combined as if they were a single FE measurement. Main Methods says the general 1–100 mM H-type tests use 30 mL electrolyte and 0.6 cm² catalyst area; no full-cell nitrite operation is reported.

Nitrogen-balance independence

The Methods say NH3, NO2−, and NO3− are quantified with distinct procedures and separate calibration standards. In the source workbook, the S21 values are hard-coded numbers, not spreadsheet formulas. But some six-timepoint panels close to an exactly fixed initial nitrogen concentration at displayed precision: S21c (M4:O9) sums to 138.86353–138.86354 μg N mL−1; S21d (S4:T9) sums to 139.06219 throughout; S21f (AD4:AE9) sums to 139.06237 throughout; S21e (X4:Z9) sums to 138.86352–138.86353; S21b (H4:I9) sums to 141.84393–141.84394. S21a (B4:D9) varies 138.81884–138.96368 around the 138.86353 start. The source does not state that these are an independent total-N recovery audit or explain the exact closures; workbook constants cannot resolve whether the reported values were independently measured, adjusted, or derived. Therefore these plotted concentrations cannot be used as independent proof of total-N closure or absence of other N products. At minimum the N-balance status is undocumented/unknown; do not assert that NH3 values are derived solely from the complement either.
