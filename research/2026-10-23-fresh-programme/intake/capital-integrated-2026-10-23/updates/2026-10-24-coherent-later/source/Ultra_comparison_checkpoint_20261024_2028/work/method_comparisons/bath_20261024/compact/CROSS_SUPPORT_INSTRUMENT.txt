## Result

I found no algebraic defect in the arbitrary-row instrument or its gain formulas, under the stated real normalized row, calibrated phase, symmetric apparatus, independent spin/common-output initial state, and standard inefficient-homodyne model. The formulas correctly separate (i) the feedback-dependent coherent spin phase, (ii) the full residual-output characteristic, and (iii) detector vacuum noise. In particular the detector noise contributes `exp[-w λ²((ΔsA)²+(ΔsB)²)/2]` with `w=(1−η)/(2η)` for normalized homodyne output; it is not part of the hot residual characteristic.

## Checks

Let `Ω0=(m/ħ)∫(d e′−d′e)`. At apparatus A, the measured captured self displacement is `v=α rd`; the captured cross displacement is `w=α re`. Write `q=αD0` and `λ=tq`. With the checkpoint’s Weyl convention, `D(v+w)=exp[iΩ(v,w)/2]D(v)D(w)`, and `⟨yP|D(sv)=exp[−iqsy]⟨yP|`. Comparing the original bra after feedback `exp(iλsy)` with a bra carrying residual self displacement `(1−t)sv` leaves branch phase `exp[i t s_A s_B α²Ω0/2]` at A. The symmetric B apparatus contributes the same coefficient with A/B exchanged. On an off-diagonal spin block their sum is therefore

    exp[i t α² Ω0 (sA sB − sA′ sB′)],

so `theta_h(t)=theta_raw+t α²Ω0` has the stated sign and has no missing factor of two. At `h=c/√γ`, `α²=γ`, `t=1`, it reduces to the full-bright correction `theta_raw+γΩ0`.

The residual captured coefficient is exactly `cp=c−tαh`: its projection on `h` is `α(1−t)`, while its projection on `h_perp` remains `β`. Hence the four residual vectors in §4 follow directly. For the two global residual spin generators, their Weyl commutator is

    Ω_A + Ω_B = (cp·c)Ω(rd,re) + (c·cp)Ω(re,rd) = 0.

Thus the residual output characteristic can be evaluated jointly with no omitted spin-spin cocycle, even when the residual modes are correlated or non-Gaussian. Correlations remain in `chi_actual_output`; replacing it with independent thermal factors would be an error, but §4 explicitly avoids that replacement.

For detector inefficiency, a normalized homodyne record can be represented as `y=P+z`, where the independent vacuum contribution has variance `Var(z)=w=(1−η)/(2η)` (vacuum quadrature variance `1/2`). A feedback phase `λs y` multiplies a `Δs` coherence by the characteristic `exp[−wλ²(Δs)²/2]`, matching §4.

For §5, the self-only residual is `q0 cᵀP−λ(hᵀP+z)`. Its variance is

    q0² cᵀVc − 2q0λ cᵀVh + λ²(hᵀVh+w),

which gives the displayed `lambda_star` and `Rmin`. For `V=vI`, the stated decomposition is `v||c_perp||² + [vw/(v+w)](c·h)²`. Expanding the `v→∞` one-hot/one-cold expression gives `u(b−a tanθ)²+a²w/cos²θ`; its optimizer and minimum in §5 are correct for `a≠0`, `cosθ≠0`, `w,u≥0`. The gain `q0 c·h` instead cancels the Euclidean captured displacement; it generally differs from `lambda_star` when detector noise or anisotropic covariance is present. The checkpoint correctly warns that the coherent phase `theta_h(t)` and residual covariance must both change with gain.

## Scope caveats

The Schur-multiplier/characteristic formula is unconditional after integrating the homodyne record; it is not a statement that outcome-conditioned spin states are identical across outcomes. If spins initially correlate with the common output state, a scalar channel multiplier on the spin density matrix need not follow; §2’s product-initial-spin premise is material. The row formulas also require `αD0≠0` for the parameterization `t=λ/(αD0)`; the `α=0` no-own-record case is correctly separated, while a zero pulse `D0=0` is another degenerate case. I found no need to alter the frozen checkpoint.
