WHOLE ADVERSARIAL REVIEW OF THE MODEL-FIRST AMMONIA CONSTRUCTION
2026-10-24. Review after owned open-arm checkpoint was frozen.

Disposition

The specified Mo/Ru cycle is atom-balanced and exposes a useful, falsifiable
central chemistry problem. Its pressure-work identity and stationary ring
calculation check. A substantive repair is needed before its per-N transfer
can be assigned an elementary rate: three receiver hydrides cannot disappear
as 1.5 individual H2 molecules in one isolated two-molecule event. The known
Ru hydrogenolysis establishes real receiver states, but neither the required
Mo-to-Ru transfer nor quantitative carrier reset at common operating conditions
has been acquired. The strongest narrow result is an experimentally specified
acquisition target with a microscopic closure obstruction. New operation,
novelty, useful throughput and process advantage remain NOT_ESTABLISHED.

1. Inventory and the integer-hydrogen repair

A=MoL3 is formal Mo(III); AN=NMoL3 is Mo(VI), L=N(tBu)Ar anionic.
PNP=N(CH2CH2PtBu2)2 is an amido pincer; HPNP has an additional N-H.
BN=RuN(PNP), B3=RuH3(PNP), B5=RuH4(HPNP) are all formal Ru(IV).
B5 has FIVE variable H atoms, four hydrides and one pincer proton; B3 has
three hydrides. The primary author chapter assigns 4 RuH at -8.48 ppm and
1 NH at 4.54 ppm. Those hydrogens cannot be removed from the reset ledger.

The proposed equations balance macroscopically:
 T: AN+B3 -> A+BN+1.5H2.
 H: BN+4H2 -> B5+NH3.
 R: B5 -> B3+H2.
Their sum is AN+1.5H2 -> A+NH3; twice plus 2A+N2->2AN gives HB stoichiometry.
The Mo(VI)->Mo(III) reduction in T is supplied formally by oxidizing three
hydrides to H2. It is internal chemical redox, not three external electrons
or evidence of electrical work extraction.

However, T is a per-N thermodynamic reaction, not an isolated elementary
bimolecular molecular event. Its integer form is
 2AN+2B3 -> 2A+2BN+3H2.
An actual mechanism must pair turnovers or store/recombine the odd H on a
specified intermediate, ligand, surface or carrier. That inventory changes
the kinetic network and potentially the active receiver population. The
integer formula itself does not establish an elementary fourth-order event.
Likewise AN+B3 association can be bimolecular while its completion rate is
not a bare two-body N-transfer constant. A coarse reverse term involving
a_H2^1.5 cannot be silently promoted into one elementary molecular edge.
Microscopic reverse rates require the expanded integer-H states and actual
reservoir exchanges. The same caution applies to half-N2 notation in a
per-N cycle: the known N2 cleavage uses a two-Mo intermediate.

I sent this repair to root before reading the model arm's subsequent
POST_REVIEW_CHEMICAL_REPAIR.txt. That addendum accepts it and preserves the
original checkpoint. Its placeholder H-storage species is explicitly
unacquired; no favorable carrier energy or recombination rate may be inferred.

The Ru primary supports BN hydrogenolysis at 1 bar H2, 50 C, 48 h, giving
about85% B5 and >80% ammonia, with isotope evidence. It also supports
B3+H2 giving B5 quantitatively by NMR, partial B5 dehydrogenation during
isolation, and a small B3 equilibrium population under H2. Thus the
interconversion has qualitative evidence in both directions. A complete,
high-yield pressure-controlled B5->B3 reset and a measured pressure-dependent
equilibrium constant were not acquired. One should not call reset impossible
or wholly unobserved, nor call the source a quantified reset operation.
The 48 h endpoint is not an elementary rate or a demonstrated high-throughput
time course; even the H stage needs matched kinetic acquisition, alongside T.

2. Pressure work is correct conditionally and is not an acquired penalty

If T and R release 1.5+1=2.5 H2 per NH3 at pL and H consumes4 H2 at pH,
then 2.5 mol is internal recycle and1.5 mol is net fresh H2. Reversible ideal
isothermal internal compression costs 2.5RT ln(pH/pL). At323.15 K and ratio
1000, this is46.3997 kJ/mol NH3, about0.757 MWh/t. The arithmetic checks.

If fresh H2 arrives at pS, add1.5RT ln(pH/pS) when compression is required.
For pS=pL total feed-to-H-step compression is4RT ln(pH/pL), not6.5RT ln.
Do not buy4 mol as fresh feed and then add2.5 mol internal gas again, and
do not recharge fresh-feed compression already included in delivered H2.
For different T/R release pressures pT,pR, the corresponding recycle term is
 RT[1.5 ln(pH/pT)+ln(pH/pR)].
Gas separation, nonideal fugacity, dissolved gas and real equipment may add
cost. Heat at another temperature requires its own exergy boundary.

The Ru measurements are at1 bar, not a measurement of distinct favorable
T/H/R pressure windows. The chosen pressure ratio is illustrative. A common
pressure might avoid the swing, provided productive transfer, hydrogenation,
reset and sufficient B3 fraction actually coexist. No need for a swing, and
no swing-free operating window, has been established. This distinction is
already largely explicit in the frozen checkpoint and should be retained.

3. Local detailed balance and current: verified mathematics, limited model

For fixed reservoirs and a time-homogeneous three-state ring, the directed
tree weights in cycle_gate_checks.py solve the stationary master equation.
With forward rates u_i and reverse rates w_i,
 J=(u0*u1*u2-w0*w1*w2)/(tau0+tau1+tau2).
I independently evaluated the stationary probabilities and all three edge
currents with exact rational arithmetic in cross_review_checks.py. At the
illustrative rates (100,.1,100)/(1,10,1), J=.0888601664/s and total affinity
ln100, although the middle edge ratio is .01. Multiplying both directions
of that middle edge by10^-6 leaves affinity unchanged and gives J=9.801e-8/s.
Thus a downhill condition on every individual stage is sufficient for this
forward unicyclic current, not necessary; favorable total affinity supplies
no lower bound on throughput. Added branches can change product selectivity
and require their own network. The arbitrary example is no material model.

The binding/gate ledger also closes: switch work at occupied and empty
states is F1(occupied)-F0(occupied)+F0(empty)-F1(empty), reducing to the
claimed binding shift when the stated baselines cancel. Returning a gate
label at a different occupation does not make the net work zero. Actual
charge passed and electrochemical potentials are needed; formal oxidation
numbers do not determine an external VdQ cost. Chemical gate fuel and the
same electrical work cannot be charged twice.

A deterministic time-dependent gate switch is parameter work at a known
occupation, not an ordinary stochastic edge with intrinsic forward/backward
rates. LDB applies to reaction jumps at each fixed control value; periodic
driving needs a periodic-state calculation or an expanded autonomous
controller network with its reservoirs. The fixed ring is not a stationary
solution for such a switched device. Occupation-triggered feedback additionally
needs its controller/information accounting. The support note's cycle sum
is valid as full pathwise accounting when these exchanges are included, not
as a newly acquired stationary affinity for an unspecified protocol.

4. Barriers and populations: the benchmark remains conditional

The .1 A/cm2,98% FE target corresponds to1.21884 mmol NH3/h/cm2. With the
chosen, unmeasured10^15 modules/cm2 it requires203.89 products/module/s.
At298.15 K and unit transmission, an actually specified bimolecular step
with20 mM ACTIVE partner requires a standard barrier no higher than .51971 eV;
the corresponding unimolecular ceiling is .62022 eV. These are internally
correct Eyring target ceilings, not measured transfer barriers.

The activity must be free reactive B3, not total Ru. If only fraction f of
20mM total Ru is B3, the bimolecular ceiling decreases by kBT ln f. For f=.1
and.01 it becomes .46055 and.40139 eV. Transmission below1, inactive Mo,
bridge trapping and competing H delivery make the target stricter. The
integer-H repair may change the elementary order entirely, so these numbers
cannot be assigned to the current coarse T. No transition state, spin-crossing
rate or active population for the exact Mo/Ru pair was calculated or measured.
The photon, volt, hydrogen and independent-loss lifetime conversions in the
peer script check as labelled accounting; they establish no physical gate,
quantum yield, device lifetime or advantage.

5. Literature: preserve actual direction and close incumbents

Own reading of Rutter's full primary text confirms that its trisanilide A
is the ACCEPTOR: tripodal-silanolate Mo nitride plus A forms a stable
unsymmetric mu-N bridge and mostly fails to finish transfer. In the older
alkoxide example, alkoxide nitride donates N to A, producing AN and a
Mo2(OtBu)6 dimer. The departing reduced donor is trapped by Mo-Mo bonding;
regeneration or interception must be paid if used in a loop. The source's
one prose rendering Mo2(OtBu)3 conflicts with ligand balance and the cited
primary formula; do not treat a suspected typo as a real loss of ligands.

These experiments do not establish the proposed opposite functional role,
AN donating N to RuH3(PNP). They establish chemical relevance of this Mo
scaffold and a real bridge-trapping failure mode. Nor do they prove a
Mo/Ru bridge must trap identically. Full steric ligands, spin states and
competing H-transfer paths matter; the primary warns that truncated models
can misassign mixed-valence bridging complexes.

The broad splitter-to-hydrogenating-carrier concept has substantial prior art.
Wang etal's TM(N)/LiH work reports activated N transfer into Li-N-H species,
hydrogenation and LiH return. It is a materially different solid relay with
continuous catalytic evidence, not proof of this molecular transfer. Direct
cooperative H delivery/PCET to a metal nitride is another established
competitor. The acquired molecular nitride/hydride half-cycles do not by
themselves close an N2/H2 catalytic loop. The defensible novelty statement is
only that the exact Mo-trisanilide-to-Ru-PNP transfer/reset operation was not
established in the audited sources. No exhaustive priority claim is supported.

6. Make the site experiment decisive without a false negative

The Ru976/945 cm^-1 isotope pair is an acquired terminal14N/15N calibration;
the reference15N nitride was made using labelled azide, not Mo transfer.
Use authentic standards and orthogonal structural/metal speciation in the
new matrix. A lone shifted band can be a bridge or a trapped terminal sink.

Prepare terminal15N-AN from purified15N2 with its three ligand N atoms14N;
quantify the initial label and hydrides. In an inert transfer-stage time
course, measure loss of Mo15N, authentic terminal Ru15N, recovered Mo(III) A,
all bridge/NHx species, and H2/HD/D2 plus hydride/pincer-H inventories. The
integer-H fate must close across paired events or a directly identified
storage state. Follow productive Ru15N into15NH3 and both carrier states
back into the next N2-fed turnover at measured gas potentials and work.

Withholding added H2 does not disable H delivery: B3 already carries three
hydrides. RuD3 crossovers can reveal early D delivery to Mo-bound nitrogen,
but scrambling with ligand/solvent/H2 means product H/D alone is not a unique
site-path label. Required controls include gas-purification/isotope blanks,
residual free15N2 with Ru alone, no-Mo/no-Ru/workup blanks, separate14/15NH3,
ligand integrity, total labelled-N inventory, authentic terminal BN response,
and matched hydrogenation controls. Rapid A rebinding/aggregation also needs
time resolution; missing free A alone is not a transfer impossibility proof.

Absence of Ru15N does not rule out fast transient BN. Under a calibrated
constant-flux, first-order-exit model, starting at zero,
 c_BN(t)=v_T(1-exp(-k_exit*t))/k_exit.
For detection limit L at time T this gives
 v_T < L*k_exit/(1-exp(-k_exit*T)).
At quasi-steady state with independently bounded k_exit<=k_max, v_T<=L*k_max.
Without an exit-rate upper bound, a small population supplies no useful
formation-flux bound. An authentic BN spike in the same matrix calibrates
both survival and response. For a productive branch fraction, if k_H<=k_H,max,
then J_NH3,viaBN<=k_H,max*L and f<=k_H,max*L/J_NH3,observed under the same
quasi-steady assumptions. Arbitrary pulses between samples require a
time-resolved observation model; endpoint nondetection is insufficient.

A positive single handoff plus separate hydrogenolysis demonstrates at most
a productive chemical branch. Repeated NH3 formation exceeding all starting
reactive N inventories, restored Mo/Ru/ligand/H states, isotope source closure,
and measured gate/gas handling are required for a catalytic cycle. The present
literature and exact algebra do not supply these observations.
