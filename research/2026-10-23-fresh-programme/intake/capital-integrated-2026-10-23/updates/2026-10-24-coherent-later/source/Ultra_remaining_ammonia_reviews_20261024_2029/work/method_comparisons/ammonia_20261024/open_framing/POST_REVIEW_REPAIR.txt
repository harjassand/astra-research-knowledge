POST-REVIEW REPAIRS TO THE FROZEN OPEN CONSTRUCTION
2026-10-24. Read together with CONSTRUCTION_AND_OBSTRUCTION.txt.

The owned checkpoint, its source-extraction script and original JSON remain
unchanged. This addendum supersedes their interpretation at the places stated
below. The corrections followed authorized peer exposure, not independent
pre-checkpoint discovery. They do not establish a process advantage.

1. Gross anodic hydrogen is not necessarily fresh hydrogen

The original 0.58455 kg H2/kg NH3 at FE_NH3=0.81 is GROSS Faradaic anodic
oxidation, assuming a two-electron HOR anode. Calling it a minimum fresh-H2
requirement was too strong. For one mol NH3, Q=8F/FE_NH3 and gross HOR
consumption is 4/FE_NH3 mol H2. If FE_H2 is the absolute fraction of cathodic
charge making H2, and rho is the fraction returned to the anode at the same
feed state, the conditional fresh demand is

 n_H2,fresh = (4/FE_NH3)(1-rho FE_H2) + purges/losses.

Recovery, purification and compression work must be added separately. They
cannot be assumed zero in an actual plant. FE_H2 is not 1-FE_NH3 unless all
other charge is HER. Nitrite or other partially reduced N can also be retained
and reduced later; their single-pass FE is not a stoichiometric N waste loss.
Fresh H2 for a complete process requires the total N, charge, H2 and other
product inventories over the returned carrier cycle, not NH3 FE alone.

Own source audit: the Zhang primary main's ion-quantification methods specify
NH3, nitrite and nitrate assays, and the paired-cell methods specify 10 sccm
H2 feed in the stability run. They do not supply a simultaneous inlet/outlet
H2 balance, FE_H2 and a demonstrated cathodic-H2 recovery circuit for that
operating point. Searching the entire extracted main/SI and rereading these
methods did not acquire those missing measurements. The source may not be
used to assign either zero HER or 19% HER, or to equate supplied and consumed
gas. Its source-reported 200 mA/cm2, 81% NH3 FE, -0.1 V consumed-voltage
point remains a gross-charge operating result with the original sampling
qualifications. The 10 sccm inlet is an apparatus feed rate, not measured net
reaction consumption or an unavoidable once-through plant feed.

For an explicitly generous HER-only recovery sensitivity at FE_NH3=.81:
FE_H2=.19, rho from 0 to 1, no extra recovery work or losses. Fresh hydrogen
then ranges from 4/.81 to 4 mol/mol NH3, or .584555 to .473490 kg/kg NH3.
At the explicitly assumed 50 kWh/kg H2 and .1 V gross-charge export:

 - Export remains 1.554260 kWh/kg NH3 from 8F V/FE_NH3.
 - H2 work minus exported electricity is 22.120216 to 27.673488 kWh/kg NH3.
 - Matched HB stoichiometric H2 work is 8.877928 kWh/kg NH3.
 - Thus the corresponding HB-overhead thresholds are 13.242288 to 18.795560
   kWh/kg NH3 before adding this route's N fixation and separation.

These are conditional accounting endpoints, not acquired whole-process
bounds. In particular 27.673 and 18.796 are the no-cathodic-H2-credit case,
not universal fresh-H2 lower bounds. Other side products/recycling, H2 loss,
purges, crossover, product conditioning and recovery work can change the
complete-cycle result. At the integrated mean exported voltage .074465 V,
using the same assumed FE, the two illustrative partial-energy endpoints
become 22.517091 and 28.070363 kWh/kg NH3. Neither FE average nor voltage
integration independently closes the product and gas assays.

The ideal matched-state theorem W_route >= G_target+q(w_H-G_split) remains
valid conditionally for the specified complete isothermal oxidation/H2 route;
q=4 is the net stoichiometric nitrate-to-NH3 hydrogen requirement, not 4/.81
inferred from single-pass charge. It is not a plant forecast or a universal
prohibition on waste-N recovery. Full actual process advantage remains UNKNOWN.

2. Split the NO/OER operating points and preserve the feed interface

The original Bunea tuple combined measurements. Independently retrieved
primary main-text passages place 0.51 mmol NH3/cm2/h at 2.1 V and 57% FE.
At 1.9 V, the 8 mL/min 5.2% NO/He test reaches 97% conversion with lower FE;
17 mL/min has higher FE than the preceding 24 mL/min test. The abstract's
93% FE does not establish my asserted simultaneous four-metric point.
Figure 6 bytes/raw values were not acquired after bounded official PDF
attempts timed out. Exact 1.9 V/93% co-occurrence remains UNVERIFIED here.

Accordingly 16.075308 kWh/kg NH3 at 1.9 V/.93, and 22.925561 after adding
the favorable .42 MJ/mol-N absorbed-plasma input, are CONDITIONAL optimistic
combinations, not a confirmed paired operating point. The independently
verified 2.1 V/.57 high-yield case gives 28.988990 kWh/kg NH3 electricity;
adding that same favorable oxidation metric gives 35.839244. These sums omit
feed conditioning, gas loss, NH3 recovery and auxiliaries. No integrated
air-to-NH3 operation is demonstrated by either sum.

The NO/OER version takes gaseous NO, whereas the opening loop takes aqueous
KNO2/KNO3. It requires its own actual plasma-to-gas conditioning/separation
operation, or an explicitly balanced NO-release operation from captured
liquor. A dilute atmospheric plasma stream does not become the PEM's NO/He
feed for free. Acid trapping likewise does not demonstrate regenerated acid
or dry NH3 recovery. The NO/OER version remains a physically distinct next
version with an unclosed upstream interface.

3. What is preserved scientifically

The alkaline nitrite/nitrate counterion balances, raw AgRu source extraction,
positive-potential nitrite half-cell window, concentration-dependent paired
cell data and matched-state electrolysis-dissipation identity survive these
repairs. The reported nitrate/HOR operation is established prior art. The
open construction has not produced a new material response or a closed,
advantageous fresh-air/water ammonia process. Correcting two numerical
overstatements does not justify replacing them with an unmeasured recovery
scheme or a forced best-case NO figure.
