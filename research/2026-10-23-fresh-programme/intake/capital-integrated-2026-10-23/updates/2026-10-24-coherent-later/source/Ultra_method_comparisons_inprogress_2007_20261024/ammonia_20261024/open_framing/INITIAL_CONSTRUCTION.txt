Preserved before reading the paired arm or root formulation. No external primary literature has yet been read in this arm beyond titles/abstract summaries in SHARED_BRIEF.txt. This is an independently chosen operation, not a claim of novel chemistry or a working device.

Question
Can an oxidized atmospheric-nitrogen carrier make distributed ammonia useful if its reduction is operated as a hydrogen-fed power-producing electrochemical operation, rather than counted as another electricity-consuming electrolyser? The answer must include the work required to make the oxidized carrier and to restore its acid/base and counterion inventory. The object is a chemically closed, selective energy-recovery step, not wastewater nitrate credited as free atmospheric nitrogen.

Owned construction V0
(1) Separate or concentrate atmospheric nitrogen and oxygen as required; electrically oxidize N2 with O2 to NO, then capture an oxidized nitrogen stream. NO2-/nitrite is the initial concrete carrier because it avoids one oxidation level and one additional H2 per N relative to nitrate. The specific source operation may yield NO/NO2/HNO2 mixtures and must be measured or derived, not assumed.
(2) Feed nitrite to a separated cathode and water-electrolytic H2 to the anode. Use a proton-conducting, hydroxide-conducting, or ion-balanced architecture selected only after charge and pH closure. Selectively reduce NO2- to NH3 while oxidizing H2 and recovering electrical work.
(3) Recover concentrated NH3, return the water and any acid/base carrier and counterions, and repeat. No consumable salt or proton donor may disappear across the boundary.

Exact reaction skeleton
Acid-form cathode: NO2- + 7 H+ + 6 e- -> NH3 + 2 H2O.
Anode: 3 H2 -> 6 H+ + 6 e-.
Sum: NO2- + H+ + 3 H2 -> NH3 + 2 H2O.
Equivalently: HNO2 + 3 H2 -> NH3 + 2 H2O.
Thus a nitrite salt feed does NOT close the process without supplying one acid equivalent per N or regenerating its cation as hydroxide. At alkaline cathode the equivalent is NO2- + 5 H2O + 6e- -> NH3 + 7 OH-; 3H2+6OH- ->6H2O+6e- gives NO2-+3H2 ->NH3+H2O+OH-. That OH- must be returned to carrier capture or explicitly regenerated.

Upstream charge-storage reaction
One ideal acid-form oxidation: (1/2) N2 + (3/4) O2 + (1/2) H2O -> HNO2.
Then with the reduction sum, full chemical sum before water electrolysis is (1/2)N2 + (3/4)O2 + 3H2 -> NH3 + (3/2)H2O.
Electrolyzing 3H2O ->3H2+(3/2)O2 gives (1/2)N2+(3/2)H2O ->NH3+(3/4)O2, the same air/water stoichiometry as direct ammonia. The extra H2 relative to Haber–Bosch is an energy-recovery obligation, not free reductant.

Thermodynamic identity to derive and evaluate
Let g_ox be reversible free energy per mol N to prepare the actual oxidized carrier from N2, O2, water and restored pH/counterions. Let g_H be reversible water-electrolysis work per mol H2 at matched states. Let g_red be free energy of the complete carrier/H2 reduction reaction, including ion restoration and NH3 product state. In the nitrite route, ideal total = g_ox +3g_H+g_red. Hess law forces this to the same air/water ammonia free energy as HB plus water electrolysis. A fuel-cell output is bounded by -g_red, not the difference of unmatched standard half potentials. Actual total is W_ox +3W_H -W_recovered +W_separation+W_ion-cycle+other losses. Need to derive the inequality needed to beat a matched flexible small-scale electrolytic-H2/HB plant.

Central unresolved physical gate
At useful current density and molar concentration, is there a catalyst/membrane/pH architecture that directs six electrons per nitrite N to recovered NH3, rather than N2, N2O, NO, hydroxylamine or H2, while exporting enough positive cell voltage after crossover, mass transfer, and acid/base/counterion regeneration? The nitrite reduction is thermodynamically favorable but favorable overall free energy does not supply selectivity or a fast six-electron pathway. NO intermediates may couple to N2/N2O or poison the cathode; the local pH and NH3/NH4+ speciation determine recovery and membrane crossover.

Falsification and next version
First find the strongest established H2–nitrite/nitrate fuel-cell and NOx-to-ammonia chemistry, read actual conditions and selectivity. A close pre-existing operation defeats novelty but can answer feasibility. If electrical recovery is only created by a maintained pH gradient or sacrificial metal, reject that operation at the closed boundary. If plasma/oxidative carrier generation requires more energy than any selective return cell can recover, reject ammonia advantage and retain a quantitative obstruction. The next justified version should change the missing chemical operation, not merely insert a favorable rate constant. Product outcome must match NH3 concentration/purity and atmospheric N provenance; 15N2 tracking must cover fixation, capture, carrier and ammonia, with blank NH3/NOx contamination.
