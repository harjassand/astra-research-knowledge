Operation and exact stoichiometry
Let A and B be recoverable ligand-supported metal fragments with nitrogen-free
states A(III), B(III) and nitrido states A(VI)N, B(VI)N. Mo(III)/Mo(VI) is the
first physically grounded family to examine because low-coordinate Mo(III)
can split N2 and molecular Mo-to-Mo nitrido transfer has actual precedent.
Formal states are bookkeeping; a real ligand set and spin states must be acquired.

1. 2 A(III) + N2 -> 2 A(VI)N.
2. A(VI)N + B(III) -> A(III) + B(VI)N, twice per N2.
3. B(VI)N + (3/2) H2 -> B(III) + NH3, twice per N2.
Net: N2 + 3 H2 -> 2 NH3. Water electrolysis supplying the 3 H2 adds
3 H2O -> 3 H2 + (3/2) O2; total N2+3 H2O -> 2 NH3+(3/2) O2.
If instead electrons/protons are used, all acid/base buffers, counterions and
anodic reaction must close: N2+6H+ +6e- ->2NH3 plus a specified water anode.
No SmI2, Li, phosphine, hydride or ligand may be credited as regenerated
merely because a transition metal turns over.

Proposed redox/coordination gate
A ligand state g=0 favors N2 activation; g=1 destabilizes A-N relative to empty
A so that contact with B can transfer N. Reversible electrochemical charge or
reversible binding of one specified ligand may implement the gate. The ligand
and its reservoir must be restored. B may have a second gate h favoring NHx
formation and ammonia release. There is no free adsorbed N atom between sites:
the transfer coordinate includes an A-(mu-N)-B bridge and cleavage to products.
This means precursor association, bridging-state trapping, electron/spin change,
and A/B separation are part of the actual barrier, not favorable inputs.

Thermodynamic obstruction derived before material selection
At fixed T and feed/product chemical potentials define, per transferred N,
 b_A^g=G(A_N^g)-G(A_empty^g)-(1/2)mu_N2,
 b_B^h=G(B_N^h)-G(B_empty^h)-(1/2)mu_N2,
 d=mu_NH3-(1/2)mu_N2-(3/2)mu_H2.
With fixed gates, capture, transfer and hydrogenation free energies are b_A,
 b_B-b_A, d-b_B. Their sum is d. All three downhill requires exactly
 d <= b_B <= b_A <= 0. If d>=0 the passive downhill window is empty
except the reversible zero-affinity case. Even if d<0 it is only width -d:
strong stable nitrides outside it must pay release work or use a different path.
At standard 298 K gas potentials d is only around -0.17 eV/NH3, whereas no
numerical nitride affinity is yet acquired here. This approximate gas value is
orientation, not a source-verified plant datum.

For an A bound-state switch 0->1 and empty-state restoration 1->0,
 W_A,rev = [G(A_N^1)-G(A_N^0)]
         +[G(A_empty^0)-G(A_empty^1)] = b_A^1-b_A^0.
Thus weakening A-N by x costs x reversible work for this switching loop.
The corresponding B expression is b_B^release-b_B^receive. Favorable nitrogen
transfer achieved by changing binding energies is not an independent free knob.
The total chemical-step free energy is d-W_gate; switching returns W_gate,
so the complete stoichiometric sum remains d. Extra actual irreversible gate
work further decreases efficiency. Gate work can buy a useful kinetic pathway,
but cannot establish a thermodynamic energy advantage by itself.

Local detailed balance and missing rate
For an elementary forward/reverse edge r at fixed reservoirs,
 k_r^+/k_r^- = exp[-Delta_r G/(k_B T)]
when k include matching activity factors; for standard rate coefficients the
appropriate equilibrium activity product must be retained. For the full cycle
ln(prod forward propensities/prod reverse propensities)=(W_ext-d)/(k_B T).
A driven gate contributes its actual electrical/chemical work once.
Positive affinity does not supply a rate. For transfer,
 k_T^+ = (k_B T/h)*kappa*exp[-(G_TS-G_A_N_B)/(k_B T)]
with concentration/association factors specified for a bimolecular reaction.
No k_T, G_TS or gate equilibration time is assumed favorable here.
For a sequential per-N module with slow reverse reactions, the mean cycle
time is the sum of elementary waiting times for capture, transfer, the three
hydrogenation events, gating and release. Reversible recrossings increase this
time. A fast capture event cannot compensate for an unacquired slow transfer.

First decisive material gate
Acquire a chemically specified N2-splitting A and B pair for which N transfer
is reversible or rapidly interceptable by H transfer, while recovered empty
fragments remain monomeric/active. A strongly favorable metal-metal dimer or
inert A-(mu-N)-B adduct can make nominal transfer occur yet destroy cycling.
Then acquire the full hydrogenation sequence and hydride/H2 side reaction.
Actual barrier heights, isotope nitrogen provenance and ligand recovery are
required before assigning any throughput or energy advantage.

Closest justified repair if bare-N handoff fails
Consider proton-coupled N transfer: A-N+B-H -> A-H+B-NH with charge-balanced
proton/electron bookkeeping and recovery of B-H by renewable H2/electrons.
This changes the nitrogen species transferred, rather than wishing a stable
nitride into a weak-binding state. It may evade a bare-N transfer bottleneck
because an N-H bond is formed at the transition state, but it may collapse to
ordinary PCET/proton shuttling with nitrogen staying on A. That distinction is
an observable isotope/site-specific question, not a naming choice. Establishing
that the closest viable repair is known PCET is a valid obstruction result;
it would not count as a new operation or a new ammonia production advantage.
