Result

The proposed oxidation/energy-return route reaches a concrete construction and
a quantitative obstruction, not an enabling ammonia discovery. Actual selective
NOx/H2 power production is source-reported for an established alkaline nitrate
cell. It does not supply an attractive fresh air/water ammonia energy balance.
The initially preferable nitrite carrier has a measured positive-potential
reaction window, but an industrial-current full-cell power window and a closed,
concentrated air-to-nitrite carrier remain unacquired. Two chemical operations
have been derived and tested against primary evidence below. Plant advantage,
novelty and a new enabling material response are NOT_ESTABLISHED.

1. Close the alkali carrier without a fictitious acid source

Initial V0 used NO2-+H++3H2 -> NH3+2H2O. A concrete ion architecture is a
common alkaline electrolyte, which avoids an externally maintained pH split:

  Cathode: NO2-+5H2O+6e- -> NH3+7OH-
  Anode:   3H2+6OH- -> 6H2O+6e-
  Net:     KNO2+3H2 -> NH3+KOH+H2O.

The regenerated KOH can capture upstream nitrogen oxides:

  NO+NO2+2KOH -> 2KNO2+H2O.

Starting with N2+O2 ->2NO and then oxidizing half the NO gives the complete
carrier preparation N2+1.5O2+2KOH ->2KNO2+H2O. Combining this with two
reduction turnovers returns both potassium and hydroxide. Finally electrolyze
the required water to H2: the external net is N2+3H2O ->2NH3+1.5O2.
There is no stoichiometric salt consumption or free proton donor in this ideal
loop. There is also no extra energy benefit from counting its seven cathodic
protons/OH equivalents without the six opposing anode equivalents.

This is a REAL reaction specification but not a demonstrated capture cycle.
It requires an appropriate NO:NO2 ratio, efficient absorption, measured
nitrite/nitrate speciation, product stripping, and restored KOH composition.
Excess oxidation gives nitrate. Then KNO3+4H2 ->NH3+KOH+2H2O; the potassium
still closes, but the hydrogen requirement increases. For a nitrate fraction f
in the captured N, n_H2=3+f per NH3 at perfect selectivity. Carbonate, sulfate,
membrane/catalyst loss and purges cannot be silently charged to carrier makeup.
The actual captured-air study in support_N_oxidation reports about 55:45
nitrate:nitrite, initially, with further oxidation during storage. It is not
evidence for a pure, stable nitrite feed.

Product is specified here as recovered NH3, eventually dry if compared with
anhydrous HB product. At pH14 most aqueous ammoniacal N is neutral NH3, but
this is not a separation operation. An acid trap gives an ammonium salt and
consumes an acid equivalent unless that acid is regenerated. The same final
NH3 concentration, purity, pressure and recovery must be matched.

2. Acquire the missing electrochemical operation instead of assigning a rate

The strongest close source found is Zhang et al., Nature Communications17:4652
(2026), DOI10.1038/s41467-026-71299-x. Its actual paired cell is membrane-free,
AgRu-15 cathode/Pt-C hydrogen-oxidation anode, 1M KOH+100mM KNO3,25C,0.25cm2
cathode,200mA/cm2. Main/SI and the original source XLSX were acquired.
SI57 reports about81%NH3FE and -0.1V consumed-voltage; the sign means exported
power. The 100h run renews electrolyte nine times according to the paper.
The headline cheap-NH3 TEA uses fossil H2 and wastewater/electrodialysis nitrate.
Its renewable-H2 input alone is$2.32/kgNH3 (SI table16). This is prior art for
the power-producing reduction operation; it is not air/water loop closure.

This establishes a plausible catalyst and acquired window for nitrite
hydrogenation at positive cathode potential. It does not permit transfer of
nitrate paired-cell81%FE/200mA performance to nitrite or replacement of the
loaded cell voltage with an RHE-referenced cathode potential.

Physical source mechanism: Ag handles nitrate-to-nitrite; Ru interfaces supply
and use underpotential hydrogen while allowing nitrite adsorption. The source
reports that adding H2 over the cathode raises H coverage yet LOWERS activity
and ammonia FE. Thus merely maximizing supplied H is a failed mechanism.
For a conditional competitive-adsorption model, let x=K_H(E), y=K_N C_N and
b=1+y+K_OH. With one H* and one N* in the hydrogenation event,

  r = k_h x y/(b+x)^2,
  dr/dx = k_h y(b-x)/(b+x)^3.

The fastest conversion is at finite x=b, not at saturated H coverage. This is
an explanatory elementary model, not a fitted microkinetic mechanism. The
actual K values, coupling-to-N2 channels and transient coverages are UNKNOWN.
The source DFT *NO->*NOH reaction free-energy step is0.64eV on its Ru/Ag slab
versus0.97eV on Ru. These are source model trends, not a measured activation
barrier that can be inserted into an Arrhenius rate. Operando/current data,
rather than this energy diagram alone, supply the existence evidence above.

Transport is also a physical constraint. At200mA/cm2 nitrite requires molar
delivery j/(6F)=0.003455mol m-2 s-1. At10mM this implies k_m>=3.45e-4m/s;
at100mM the requirement is3.45e-5m/s. No diffusion coefficient or porous-electrode
rate was invented. The measured low-concentration plateau and full-cell
concentration dependence require capture/concentration costs to be paid.

3. Recoverable work after restoring the actual carrier states

Use 298.15K and common1bar gases/pure liquid water for an explicit reference.
The JANAF tables give g_H=237.141kJ/mol for H2O(l)->H2+.5O2 and
g_f(NH3g)=-16.367kJ/mol. Thus G_T for .5N2+1.5H2O->NH3+.75O2 is339.3445kJ/mol.
Actual activities, product recovery, air conditioning and pressure add terms.

For an N carrier at oxidation state z, n=(z+3)/2 H2 is needed per NH3.
Let g_ox and g_red include the SAME regenerated cations/base/product states:

  g_ox+n g_H+g_red=G_T.
  W_ox=g_ox+D_ox; w_H=g_H+D_H; W_return=-g_red-D_red.
  W_total=G_T+D_ox+n D_H+D_red+D_other.

D are nonnegative isothermal dissipations/exergy losses under the specified
work and heat boundary. Any nonambient heat source or heat-integration credit
must appear explicitly. For fixed actual electrolysis w_H, even perfect other
stages retain nD_H. The additional unavoidable electrolysis dissipation against
directN2 is(z/2)D_H. This is an oxidation/H2 route condition, not a universal
no-go for direct electrosynthesis or a discovery of new thermodynamics.

At the EXPLICIT SENSITIVITY input50kWh/kgH2, w_H=362.88kJ/mol:
ideal-other-stage floors are11.687kWh/kgNH3(nitrite),13.738(nitrate),10.662(NO).
These cannot be advertised as achievable plant energies. Finite-rate chemical
losses, carrier preparation irreversibility and product separation worsen them.

At the source nitrate point81%FE,+.1V exported, hydrogen alone needs
0.58455kg/kgNH3. At the same50kWh/kgH2 this costs29.228kWh/kgNH3; electrical
return is only1.554, leaving27.673 BEFORE airN fixation, gas conditioning or
separation. The paper's stripping proxy adds4.603kWh/kgNH3. Using the actual
mean voltage gives a smaller electrical return. Even the UNMEASURED optimistic
nitrite scenario with100%FE and+.1V loaded output needs16.812kWh/kgNH3 before
carrier preparation/separation.

Match conventional greenHB as W_HB=1.5w_H+H_HB, where H_HB includes actual
loop, air separation, product recovery, storage and control at the same scale
and heat boundary. At50kWh/kgH2 its H2-only term is8.878kWh/kgNH3. The acquired
nitrate point could improve energy only if conventional net overhead exceeded
18.796kWh/kgNH3 BEFORE adding the candidate's air fixation and separation.
For the optimistic nitrite scenario the corresponding overhead is7.934.
No such HB overhead was acquired. Flexible HB with H2 buffering is a genuine
incumbent, not excluded by variable power.

Izelaar etal (2024) is a91t/day,99.5mol%NH3 process model with air separation,
water electrolysis,155bar/400C synthesis,heat integration,condensation/recycle.
Its AEL/PEMEL stoichiometric H2 inputs are28.4/32.8GJ/tNH3. Its isolated33.9GJ/t
total is NOT directly combined with50kWh/kgH2 here without identifying Fig4's
configuration. Salmon/Banares-Alcantara(2023) co-model flexibility/buffering;
neither study is a matched1t/day capital estimate for this candidate.

4. Next justified version: remove the extra H2 round trip

The failure motivates direct NO-fed reduction paired with OER instead of HOR:

  Cathode: NO+5H++5e- ->NH3+H2O
  Anode:   2.5H2O ->1.25O2+5H++5e-
  Net:     NO+1.5H2O ->NH3+1.25O2.

This avoids nD_H but must pay OER and NORR kinetic losses. It is an actual
established PEM operation, not a hypothetical favorable catalyst. Bunea etal
ACS Catalysis2023 DOI10.1021/acscatal.3c01600 reports1.9V input,93%NH3FE,
97%NO conversion,0.51mmolNH3 cm-2 h-1 with~5%NO/He andRu/C/IrOx. The H2/HOR
variant has an OCP but detected ammonia only after applied cathodic bias;
OCP alone does not establish the initial energy-return operation. Product
collection in acid is not recovered dry NH3. Latest literature search was
bounded, so this is not asserted to be the strongest possible future NORR.

Own same-state calculation using JANAF NO(g),g_f=86.600kJ/mol:
NO/H2 reduction can reversibly return340.108kJ/mol,Erev=.7050V for NH3(g)+H2O(l),
whereas NO/OER requires252.7445kJ/mol,Erev=.5239V. A quoted .836V potential for
other proton/product states is NOT substituted. At the acquired1.9V/93% point,
the OER/NORR electricity is16.075kWh/kgNH3 before making NO or recoveringNH3.

The low-energy atmospheric plasma source was also examined directly. The actual
PDF at repository.uantwerpen.be/docstore/d:irua:12363 is Hollevoet etal2022,
ChemSusChem DOI10.1002/cssc.202102526. It reports0.42MJ/molNOx from0.1W
ABSORBED plasma power,~200ppm NOx,20–30mg/h. Applied power is0.76W. Its LNT
test uses simulated plasma gas, and its2.1MJ/molNH3 is a summed process estimate.
It is close prior art for the oxidative detour and solid-carrier regeneration.

Even granting its0.42MJ/mol absorbed-power metric as a free-standing system
input and concentrated NO for free, the acquired NO/OER point sums to
22.926kWh/kgNH3. Perfect NO/OER chemistry would still give10.973 before product
and auxiliary losses. Perfect NO/HOR energy recovery with50kWh/kgH2 gives
16.100. Normalizing the same yield to the stated0.76W applied power gives
about3.192MJ/molN,so the oxidation leg ALONE is52.06kWh/kgNH3. This calculation
uses rounded powers and is not an independently metered full plant result.

For an illustrative TOTAL budget12.5kWh/kgNH3 with0.42MJ/molNO already spent,
93%FE NO/OER must use<=.668V before any product/auxiliary work, versus1.9V
acquired and .524V same-state reversible voltage. It therefore leaves only a
small finite-rate loss budget even with a highly favorable plasma boundary.
Changing only the downstream catalyst cannot repair the applied-power case.

5. Scientific checkpoint and accessible next operation

V0's suggestion that an oxidized carrier might return useful electricity is
physically real in a known nitrate cell. Its power budget is far too small
at the acquired conditions to repay water-electrolytic hydrogen and fresh
nitrogen oxidation. The next version(NO/OER) removes that hydrogen penalty,
has acquired chemical throughput, and still fails the current energy budget.
No scalable, cyclic renewable-air/water advantage was obtained.

A useful next experiment is now sharply specified instead of a catalyst list:
take an actually captured-airNOx carrier with measured nitrate fraction and
alkali inventory; run a concentration/current map on the existing AgRu/Pt
architecture while simultaneously measuring loaded voltage, H2 consumption,+NH3,hydroxylamine,NO/N2O/N2,totalN,OH-/carbonate and recovered product. Return
that SAME electrolyte to absorption rather than renewing it. Nitrogen must be
traced from purified15N2 through oxidation/capture to15NH3 with input blanks;
K15NO3 labeling alone certifies only the reduction leg. This tests whether the
ion-closed chemical operation exists under real capture conditions. It does
not yet justify a plant advantage. A claimed enabling advance would need to
change the quantified oxidation/loaded-voltage budget, not just its FE.

operation_calculations.json. It acquires source points, interpolates labelled

existing Luna contexts provided bounded support notes. Root/Sol verified the

of every source and matched small-unit capital advantage remain unestablished.
