Result at this checkpoint

The proposed class can be written as a chemically closed operation, and real
nitrogen transfer is possible. It has not produced a new demonstrated operation
or a competitive renewable-ammonia process here. A central correction is that
nitrogen handoff to a hydrogenating carrier is already an established research
route: TM-nitride/LiH/amide catalysis and nitride–imide chemical loops are strong
incumbents. What remains specifically unacquired in the molecular construction
is dehydrogenative transfer of N2-derived Mo nitride to a real hydrogenating
receiver, with all of the receiver's stored H and ligand protonation restored.

The original fictitious B(III) bookkeeping has therefore been replaced below
by actual Ru–PNP nitrido/polyhydride species. This exposes both a physical
transfer barrier and a pressure/regeneration cost that the simple sketch hid.
It also yields a sharp isotope experiment distinguishing N handoff from an
ordinary H-delivery catalyst. No favorable central-transfer rate is supplied.

1. A chemically specified A/B candidate, with complete stoichiometry

Set Ar=3,5-Me2C6H3 and L= N(tBu)Ar (an anionic amido ligand).
 A = MoL3, the low-coordinate Mo(III) N2-cleaving fragment;
 AN = NMoL3, the corresponding terminal Mo(VI) nitride.
The actual N2-splitting family is documented by Laplaza/Cummins,
Science1995 DOI10.1126/science.268.5212.861 and mechanistic JACS1996
DOI10.1021/ja960574x. The 2024 primary below discusses these exact fragments.

Let PNP = N(CH2CH2PtBu2)2, an anionic amido pincer, and HPNP its neutral
amine form. Use these actual receiver-family states:
 BN = RuN(PNP);
 B3 = RuH3(PNP);
 B5 = RuH4(HPNP).
The labels 3/5 count total stored H atoms outside fixed PNP skeleton; B5 has
four hydrides plus the pincer N–H. These are not interchangeable empty sites.
Askevold etal NatureChem2011 DOI10.1038/nchem.1051 reports Ru-nitride
hydrogenolysis. The corresponding author's primary thesis section
https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/download/1106483/1106483.pdf (printed p96)
explicitly identifies B5 as the product and small equilibrium amounts of B3
under H2. Primary-source excerpt reports about85% B5 and >80% NH3 after
48h at50C under1bar H2, with isotope NMR evidence for nitrido-N becoming NH3.
Those are batch endpoint observations, not an elementary rate constant.
The exact primary author excerpt was read; a local PDF fetch timed out and
a later endpoint returned429. No claim of local full-thesis acquisition.

The proposed complete module is:

 C: 2 A + N2 -> 2 AN.                         [known family chemistry]
 T: AN + B3 -> A + BN + (3/2)H2.              [NEW ACQUISITION REQUIRED]
 H: BN + 4H2 -> B5 + NH3.                     [known receiver hydrogenation]
 R: B5 -> B3 + H2.                           [observed equilibrium manifold;
                                              directional complete reset unproven]

Run T,H,R twice after C. The exact net is N2+3H2->2NH3. All Mo, Ru,
PNP/HPNP and amido ligands return on paper. Charges are neutral throughout.
The chemical redox that returns Mo(VI) to Mo(III) in T must come from
oxidation of receiver hydrides as H2 is eliminated; it is not supplied by
declaring an empty B(III) site. A thermal/electrochemical/photochemical gate
must physically realize that coupled process and be regenerated.

This is a controlled acquisition target, not a report that reaction T occurs.
The strong Mo-amido nitride is known to resist mild N functionalization, the
Ru receiver is H-rich, and a direct Ru-H-to-N(Mo) pathway may dominate. Mixing
the known half-cycles does not establish their thermodynamic or kinetic
compatibility. A,B ligand exchange, ligand hydrogenation, Mo/Ru aggregation,
stable bridging nitride, and H2 evolution must all be explicit competing edges.

2. The actual gate couples transfer and release in opposite directions

For the exact module define actual chemical potentials and solution free
energies at one T:
 gT = G(A)+G(BN)+(3/2)muH2-G(AN)-G(B3),
 gH = G(B5)+muNH3-G(BN)-4muH2,
 gR = G(B3)+muH2-G(B5).
Then gT+gH+gR=G(A)+muNH3-G(AN)-(3/2)muH2.
Adding half of C gives muNH3-(1/2)muN2-(3/2)muH2 exactly.

Lower H2 potential favors T and R because they release H2; higher H2
potential favors H. Therefore the desired receiver cannot independently
accept nitrogen, hydrogenate, and reset at freely chosen H2 chemical
potentials without carrying the gas-pressure work. If T and R occur against
the low-pressure reservoir and H draws from a high-pressure reservoir, then
per NH3 the machine consumes4H2(high) and releases2.5H2(low). Closing the gas
loop requires returning those2.5H2 to the high reservoir. Ideal-gas reversible
compression alone costs

 W_H2,recycle = (5/2) R T ln(p_high/p_low) per mol NH3.

For illustration only,323.15K and1bar/0.001bar give46.40kJ/molNH3,
about2.72GJ/tNH3 (0.757MWh/t). This is not a measured operating requirement;
neither pressure ratio nor equilibrium constants have been acquired for T.
It shows what the gas gate costs if that swing is needed. Mechanical pressure
work, vacuum losses, dissolved H2 transfer and gas purification add to it.
Operating at one H2 potential avoids that pressure swing, but must then
demonstrate both forward transfer and sufficient B3 population there.

Electrical gating requires the actual reversible redox potentials, charge
passed, and return path: Wgate=integral V dQ over the whole protocol. Formal
metal oxidation changes alone do not prove that three external electrons are
pumped per N. Ligand-gating reservoirs are treated the same way. Work can be
recovered where the chemistry supplies it; do not charge or credit it twice.

3. Physical evidence that central transfer, rather than atom bookkeeping,
   is the missing operation

Rutter etal InorgChem2024 DOI10.1021/acs.inorgchem.4c00762, full primary body
acquired in sources/PMC11080062.xml and .txt, studies actual Mo-to-Mo nitride
transfer. Terminal tripodal-silanolate Mo nitrides react promptly with the
trisanilide Mo(III) acceptor, but stop at an isolable unsymmetric mu-nitride;
attempts to force full transfer mostly fail. The paper contrasts an earlier
alkoxide-to-trisanilide transfer where the freed alkoxide Mo(III) fragment is
trapped as an inert Mo–Mo triple-bonded dimer. Thus rapid association can
establish a stable intermediate instead of productive A recovery.

For ligand-preserving alkoxide monomer X=Mo(OtBu)3, dimer DX=X2, and
trisanilide acceptor A, the transfer bookkeeping is
 XN + A -> (1/2)DX + AN.
Any cyclic use must include DX->2X, or a faster N2 interception that prevents
dimerization. It cannot credit dimer-formation energy and silently return X.
The dimer formula must be Mo2(OtBu)6 by atom balance; one plain-text
extraction drops/misreads a subscript. We use X2 to avoid that extraction trap.

A minimal actual transfer network is therefore
 AN+B <-> I=A-(mu-N)-B,
 I -> A+BN                  (productive N relocation),
 I + H2/Hdonor -> ANHx+B'    (H delivery without N relocation),
 I -> D                     (aggregation/ligand destruction),
 A+BN -> AN+B               (back transfer).
Under a low-occupancy steady-state approximation for I,
 v_Ntransfer = k_on[B][AN] k_T /
              (k_off+k_T+k_H*a_Hdonor+k_loss).
This is a conditional kinetic equation, not an acquired k_T. Productive
hydrogenation must subsequently beat BN back-transfer or passivation; A
must reactivate before dimerization. Recrossings can lengthen residence time
without losing yield, so the fraction per bridge visit is not a universal
whole-cycle yield.

Woo etal JACS1991 DOI10.1021/ja00022a040 provides real reversible Mn-porphyrin
N transfer: k≈5.6e3M^-1s^-1 and K≈1.2 for a Mn(V)/Mn(II) pair at20C, while
a chloride-coupled Mn(V)/Mn(III) exchange is only≈0.010M^-1s^-1 at22C.
Those distinct mechanisms demonstrate a large ligand/redox dependence; they
do not supply the Mo/Ru k_T. Neither exchange takes N2 or produces NH3.

4. Why the initial stagewise window is not a no-go theorem

Define bA=G(AN)-G(A)-(1/2)muN2, with an analogous nitrogen affinity bB
for a correctly specified B manifold. At fixed state and fixed standard
activities, bA, bB-bA, d-bB sum to d. The condition
 d<=bB<=bA<=0
is a sufficient test for all three coarse chemical stages to be downhill.
It is not necessary for finite forward turnover: actual activities,
downstream pulling and reversible recrossings permit an uphill edge.
It does not state a universal binding/activation relation. Real N2 splitting
has a transition-state energy, and NH3 adsorption is a different descriptor.

For a three-state reversible ring with forward rates u_i, reverse rates w_i,
the exact stationary current is
 J=(u0*u1*u2-w0*w1*w2)/D,
 D=w0*u2+u1*u2+w0*w1+u0*w1+w2*w1+u0*u2
   +w2*u1+u0*u1+w0*w2 >0.
It is positive whenever the total affinity ln(prod(u)/prod(w)) is positive,
even if the transfer ratio u1/w1<1. Multiplying both transfer directions by
epsilon leaves affinity unchanged but sends J to zero with epsilon.
The supplied script evaluates an illustrative uphill-transfer ring: affinity
ln100, u1/w1=0.01, and J≈0.08886/s. Reducing its transfer prefactor by1e6
reduces J to9.80e-8/s at identical affinity. These are deliberately artificial
rates showing why thermodynamics cannot acquire the missing transfer.

For a bound A gate0->1 and empty A reset1->0, reversible switching work is
 [G(AN,1)-G(AN,0)]+[G(A,0)-G(A,1)]=bA1-bA0.
An analogous receiver term must use its actual H/ligand inventory. This is
an accounting identity for the chosen protocol, not a claim that useful
gating cannot improve kinetics or process efficiency.

5. Strong incumbent and closest scientifically justified repair

Wang etal NatureChem2017 DOI10.1038/nchem.2595 already implements the
operative logic in a TM(N)–LiH composite. The schematic [LiNH] is a mixture
label, not a molecular formula. Balanced phase-level reactions include
 2Mn2N+(3/2)LiH -> Mn4N+(1/2)LiNH2+(1/2)Li2NH,
 Fe4N+(3/2)LiH -> 4Fe+(1/2)LiNH2+(1/2)Li2NH,
 Li2NH+H2 -> LiNH2+LiH,
 LiNH2+H2 -> NH3+LiH.
Each nitride release sum consumes1.5H2/NH3 and restores LiH. Re-nitridation
from N2 returns the TM nitrogen inventory in the proposed full loop. The
primary reports separate548K transfer/uptake tests and continuous10bar
N2:H2=1:3 catalysis, with roughly constant output over10h. This is meaningful
catalytic evidence. It does not individually time-resolve the N-transfer
barrier or close every microscopic phase inventory at one shared condition.
Full-main reading was via a primary-article reproduction; official SI access
was403. Detailed provenance/conditions are in support_physical_sources.txt.

If bare Mo->Ru N transfer fails, the closest supported repair is to form N–H
while transferring N into the Li–N–H receiver rather than trying to hand off
a stable bare nitride. That is real prior chemistry, not a newly invented
operation. It avoids requiring the receiver to be a freely chosen weak
N-binder; its intermediate species and regeneration chemistry are different.
Mn-nitride/BaNH composites (Feng2021 DOI10.1039/D0TA10519H), Li4NH/Co cycling
(Ravi/Makepeace2022 DOI10.1039/D2CC01345B), and Zn-modified LiH/Li2NH with
LiZnN (Wang2023 DOI10.1002/cssc.202300813) are stronger material comparators
than a single adsorbed-N scaling cartoon. Their actual phase compositions,
temperatures and time-on-stream must be matched; support_looping_sources.txt
keeps uptake, cyclic regeneration and abstract-only claims separate.

The other close repair is delivery of H+/e- or H atoms to N that stays on A.
That can be excellent science but is ordinary cooperative hydrogenation/PCET
unless direct N relocation is observed. Kim/Chirik2020 JACS
DOI10.1021/jacs.0c03346 hydrogenates prepared Mn nitride via a Rh-H catalyst.
More strongly, Kim etal JACS2025 DOI10.1021/jacs.4c14966 reports continued
irradiation under N2 regenerating a molecular Mo nitride from its MoH5
product for superstoichiometric N2/H2 batches. The exact2025 repeat count,
quantum yield and reset section S20 remain unacquired; no such numbers are
invented. The2022 predecessor's three-step reset used DBU, UV and ferrocenium
and is not silently treated as reagent-free. The2026 silane-surrogate study
DOI10.1021/jacs.5c22220 has actual hydride/migration evidence and a calculated
first H2-addition dG of+3.9kcal/mol for its Mo system. Silane is a mechanistic
surrogate, not a renewable H-source claim. These sources show why Mo–H/N–H
gating is a serious established competitor to adding an N-transfer stage.

6. What decisive acquisition this construction now enables

Prepare AN with terminal15N from15N2, preserving its three14N amido ligands,
and combine it with independently characterized B3/B5. Follow the terminal
nitride isotope and recovered A before adding the high-H2 hydrogenation dose.
The required observation for T is growth of Ru–15N plus restored Mo(III) A,
with the predicted H2 release, followed by15NH3 and complete B3 recovery.
The Ru primary gives a terminal Ru14N/Ru15N vibrational pair near976/945cm^-1;
site-specific isotope-sensitive spectroscopy and quantitative Mo/Ru speciation
are more diagnostic than ammonia detection alone.15NH3 without an observed
Ru15N branch is consistent with H delivery to Mo and does not prove N handoff.
RuD3/15NMo crossovers can distinguish early H/D delivery from terminal-N
relocation, with all HD/D2/H2 and ligand N–H exchanges included in the balance.

Measure both directional transfer constants, I residence and loss, BN
hydrogenation, B3/B5 equilibrium and Mo re-nitridation in the same solvent,
temperature and H2/N2 potentials. A redox/coordination pulse is useful only
if these acquired rates show a productive branch and faster carrier return
than the matched ungated mixture, at measured net gate work. Terminal15N
in B and product must be reconciled with initial/final nitrogen inventories;
both metal ligands contain other nitrogen that can yield false unlabelled NH3.

An electronic-structure acquisition must retain the full steric amido/pincer
ligands, conformers and relevant spin states of AN+B3, the mu-N bridge,
H-transfer alternatives and A+BN+H2 products. The2024 primary explicitly
warns that heavily truncated ligands can misassign mixed-valence Mo bridges.
Compute/benchmark actual transition states and solution free energies;
assigning a transfer rate from overall affinity or a single binding energy
would not answer the question. No such quantum-chemical calculation has been
run in this arm; required barriers and spin-crossing rates remain UNKNOWN.

The newly precise scientific question is whether a dehydrogenating late-metal
hydride can accept N from an N2-splitting nitride faster than it merely delivers
H, while returning the N2-splitting fragment and hydride manifold under one
closed work budget. The proposed experiment/computation has different
observable outcomes for those alternatives. The question's priority and
answer are unestablished; posing it is not itself an ammonia breakthrough.
