1. SCIENTIFIC RESULT AND WHAT CHANGED
====================================
A one-mode oscillator subject to calibrated incoherent gain and loss, both of
which may be partially observed, has an exact positive current-state hierarchy
for finite binary latch records. Start from a supplied finite number-diagonal
preparation. The hierarchy's discrete order grows by at most one per clicked
pooled bin, irrespective of the number of unobserved physical events. Its only
continuous state parameter is a thermal parameter; its distribution is retained.

The basis consists of established photon-added thermal states. The result is
not discovery of these states, CIR diffusion, quantum filtering, or mixtures.
The constructed interface is simultaneous marked gain/loss closure, first-event
coarse observation, an order bound tied to that observation, and a positive finite
physical approximation with a complete-record error norm. The rate distribution,
controller, kinetic preparation and arithmetic are part of the state/cost.

This repairs a concrete failure of the ordinary positive Poisson/coherent-state
mixture. The repair is a physical density operator, not merely a Cox process
with an auxiliary parameter whose arbitrary reweighting might fail to correspond
to physical state evolution. It remains one mode and number diagonal; generic
spatial interactions and noncommuting coherent control are not included.

2. PHYSICAL INSTRUMENT AND PREPARATION
=====================================
Let a be the oscillator lowering operator. Use
  L rho = b D[a^dagger]rho + d D[a]rho,
  D[A]rho = A rho A^dagger - (A^dagger A rho + rho A^dagger A)/2,
with known b,d >= 0. A Hamiltonian omega(t)N may be included without changing
number-diagonal states. The registered maps are
  J+ rho = kappa+ a^dagger rho a,       0<=kappa+<=b,
  J- rho = kappa- a rho a^dagger,       0<=kappa-<=d.
Thus kappa+=eta+ b and kappa-=eta- d specify scalar known efficiencies.
The no-registered-event generator is L0=L-J+-J-.

Each deterministic bin reports ONE bit: did at least one registered event of
either type occur? The latch does not change the physical bath. Controls within
a bin are specified by completed bits, time and retained accessible controller
state; they do not use an unread flag, latent index, hidden number or trigger time.
Boundary feedback is allowed. Exact and approximate references use the SAME
implemented controller on a common exposed prefix. No ideal-controller substitution
is made. A well-posed nonanticipating policy with finite integrated rates is assumed.

The main preparation is rho0=sum_(k=0)^K0 p0_k |k><k| with p0 supplied. The
realized k is not supplied. More generally allow a supplied positive mixture of
the states below with k<=K0 and theta>=theta_min>0. Its mixture-law acquisition
and evaluation must be supplied, not assumed to be easy because it is positive.
Supplied correlations with a retained classical controller are kept by using
the corresponding conditional preparation for each controller value and averaging
the joint law. They are not replaced by independent marginals. Unretained bath
memory or initial bath correlations are not derived away by assuming a Lindblad
generator; validity and calibration of that generator remain physical premises.

3. THE PHYSICAL POSITIVE BASIS
==============================
For k>=0 and 0<theta<=1 define
  sigma_(k,theta) = sum_(n>=k) binomial(n,k) theta^(k+1)
                                      (1-theta)^(n-k) |n><n|.       (3.1)
The binomial series proves normalization. This is the normalized k-photon-added
thermal state, also the ideal amplification of a k-photon Fock state. Its PGF is
  G_(k,theta)(z)=z^k [theta/(1-(1-theta)z)]^(k+1).                  (3.2)
Its mean is (k+1)/theta-1; theta=1 gives exactly |k><k|.

Hence the initial law in Section2 is already a finite positive mixture with
theta=1. For k>0 the family includes genuinely nonclassical states; positive
mixture here means a mixture of density operators, not a nonnegative ordinary
Glauber-Sudarshan P function. The distinction is operational in Section9.

The radial Husimi density is Gamma(k+1,rate theta):
  q_(k,theta)(x)=theta^(k+1) x^k exp(-theta x)/k!, x>=0.            (3.3)
This is a useful calculation coordinate. Positivity of an arbitrary Q density
does not imply quantum realizability. Here realizability is proved by (3.1).

4. BOTH JUMPS CLOSE, WITH NONNEGATIVE COEFFICIENTS
================================================
Direct number-coefficient comparison or differentiating (3.2) gives
  a^dagger sigma_(k,theta) a = (k+1)/theta sigma_(k+1,theta),
  a sigma_(k,theta) a^dagger =
       k theta sigma_(k-1,theta)
     + (2k+1)(1-theta) sigma_(k,theta)
     + (k+1)(1-theta)^2/theta sigma_(k+1,theta).                  (4.1)
Omit the k-1 term for k=0. Every coefficient is nonnegative, including at theta=1.
Their traces are respectively (k+1)/theta and (k+1)/theta-1, as required.
Each registered event increases the largest basis index by at most one.

The three terms in the loss update are essential. Index k measures the chosen
representation's photon-addition order; it is not the hidden physical number.
In particular a loss event can increase this index without increasing energy.

5. EXACT POSITIVE NO-COUNT TRANSPORT
===================================
Set c=d-kappa-, g=b-d+2kappa-, v=kappa++kappa-. Then
  F(theta)=v-g theta-c theta^2,
  ell_k(theta)=v(k+1)/theta-kappa-.
The exact tangent identity is
  L0 sigma_(k,theta)
    = F(theta) partial_theta sigma_(k,theta)
      + c theta k [sigma_(k-1,theta)-sigma_(k,theta)]
      - ell_k(theta) sigma_(k,theta).                           (5.1)

Consequently one common rate theta and positive unnormalized weights w_k evolve by
  theta_dot=F(theta),
  w_dot_k=c theta[(k+1)w_(k+1)-k w_k]-ell_k(theta)w_k.             (5.2)
This is deterministic parameter transport, downward index transitions and killing.
The scalar +kappa- inside -ell_k must not be dropped.

Proof interface. In PGFs, full gain acts as
  b(z-1)(z partial_z G+G),
loss as d(1-z)partial_z G, and registered maps as
  kappa+ z(z partial_z G+G), kappa- partial_z G.
Substitution of (3.2) proves (4.1) and (5.1) for every k. Alternatively the full
radial Q generator is the forward CIR operator with diffusion d and drift
d+(b-d)x. Subtracting both registered maps yields the forward CIR operator
with coefficients c,g, plus kappa- I-vx. In the gamma basis this is (5.2).
The number-state proof, rather than Q positivity alone, certifies the physical map.

The relevant invariants follow from
  F(theta)=-b theta+d theta(1-theta)+kappa++kappa-(1-theta)^2.
At theta=1, F=kappa+-b<=0; at zero, F=v>=0. Moreover F>=-b theta on [0,1].
For finite rates, 0<theta(t)<=1 and theta(t)>=theta(0)exp(-integral b).
  ell_k = kappa+(k+1)/theta + kappa-[(k+1)/theta-1]>=0.
Thus trace killing equals the actual total registered intensity.
Linear birth-process domination gives nonexplosion on every finite gain clock
for the stated finite-mean preparations. The represented states have finite
moments on each such interval, justifying the generator identities and limits.

6. FIRST-REGISTRATION CONSTRUCTION AND THE DEGREE THEOREM
========================================================
Write T(s,t) for full unobserved physical propagation and T0(s,t) for no
registered event. The two bin instruments satisfy
  I0=T0(0,T),
  I1 rho=integral_0^T T(s,T)[J+(s)+J-(s)]T0(0,s)rho ds.           (6.1)
Equation (6.1) conditions on the first registered event. All subsequent physical
events are integrated into the full channel T(s,T). No first time is disclosed
or assumed measured. The identity follows either by Duhamel's formula for
T-T0 or by the ordinary event expansion; every term is completely positive.

In (5.2) no-count transport only lowers the index. In the full channel set
kappa+=kappa-=0; it too only lowers the index. The middle map (4.1) raises
the maximum index by at most one. Therefore, after any exposed word having m
clicked bins, the exact physical posterior has the form
  rho_h=sum_(k=0)^(K0+m) integral sigma_(k,theta) mu_h(k,dtheta),   (6.2)
where mu_h is a probability measure, theta in (0,1]. It obeys the positive
current recursion defined by (5.2), (4.1), and (6.1).

Different integrated first times lead to different theta, so an exact single
theta or fixed finite list is not asserted. The state is the CURRENT positive
measure mu_h and the controller. It need not retain the raw control history;
the finite approximation in Section8 is a recursive current state.

Two separate gain/loss OR flags are possible. Maintain four latch states and
turn a sign's observation off mathematically after its first trigger, retaining
the physical bath. Each positive flag then costs at most one index increase,
so K<=K0+number of positive flags (at most K0+2M). The quantitative one-dimensional
first-time quadrature below is stated for the simpler pooled bit.

This principle is about an absorbing detector flag, not arbitrary binary data.
A parity bit can flip arbitrarily often in a bin; output alphabet size alone
does not give the same first-registration bound.

7. A METRIC FOR POSITIVE PHYSICAL COMPRESSION
============================================
Use theta=sech(u)^2, u>=0. Summing the negative-binomial series gives exactly
  sum_n sqrt[sigma_(k,sech^2u)(n) sigma_(k,sech^2w)(n)]
       =sech(u-w)^(k+1).                                      (7.1)
The classical affinity/TV inequality, valid here because the density operators
commute, yields
  D(sigma_(k,sech^2u),sigma_(k,sech^2w))
       <=sqrt[1-sech(u-w)^(2(k+1))]
       <=sqrt(k+1)|u-w|,                                      (7.2)
with D=one-half trace norm. The last inequality follows from
log cosh x<=x^2/2 and 1-exp(-y)<=y. It remains valid at the Fock boundary u=0.
An alternative useful bound is D<=1-exp[-(k+1)|log(theta/phi)|].

If the initial parameter obeys u<=U0 and G bounds integrated gain on every
allowed controller history, the exact reachable parameter satisfies
  u<=arcosh(exp(G/2)cosh U0)<=U0+G/2+log2.                      (7.3)
For K=K0+M, rounding u to a mesh within distance delta changes ANY mixture
(6.2) by physical trace distance <=sqrt(K+1)delta. Thus an epsilon approximation
to any such current physical state exists with
  (K+1)[1+ceil(Umax sqrt(K+1)/(2 epsilon))]                     (7.4)
basis states for a nearest-point mesh, up to endpoint convention. The shape
weights must still be computed. This is an existence/geometry statement before
the positive propagation construction; it is not free acquisition of a posterior.
For an implementation using upward rounding by delta at each of M stages, the
safe range is Umax<=U0+G/2+M delta+log2. Upward rounding never leaves theta<=1.

Mean occupation may grow exponentially in G, while this parameter range grows
linearly in G. Trace distance controls all bounded measurement tests, not every
unbounded energy expectation. Energy accuracy needs its own moment allowance.

8. COMPUTABLE POSITIVE BIN ROWS, CONTROLLER AND ARITHMETIC

at rate theta0(s), with survival probability S_j(s). All three quantities follow

on a first registered event at s, apply (4.1) and then full propagation to the
endpoint. Denote the resulting normalized physical state by F_s. Then
  I1 sigma=integral F_s [-d S_j(s)].                            (8.1)
This is a positive integral. Its total mass is 1-S_j(T).

The full first-event quadrature and conservative path-variation bound are in

Root read and checked its five-component formulas, scalar flow convention,

In brief F_s is a positive mixture of five binomial index laws,
all with one final theta_f(s). The five weights are rational functions of a
Riccati coordinate. On S constant-control pieces their variation is bounded;
theta_f(s) is increasing because the no-count rate field exceeds the full field

  V_j <= (8j+36)S + sqrt(j+2) Delta_u.                          (8.2)

Partition s by this variation, not by the possibly enormous count hazard. On
each piece [a,b], choose one F_s and give it EXACT positive mass S_j(a)-S_j(b).
If each piece has oscillation at most eta, the click-row trace error is at most
(1-S_j(T))eta. The required number of representatives is O(S+V_j/eta), independent
of the raw mean photon number. No-event output is already finite and positive.
Riccati evaluations, monotonicity partition/root isolation, survival differences,
binomial coefficients and precision are charged; arbitrary rapidly varying
controls do not receive this finite-S evaluation bound automatically.

Now project every output basis parameter to the fixed u grid and collect positive
weights at the grid nodes. For C=(K+1)J basis states, the implemented current state
is a positive C-vector and its controller. Online propagation uses the selected

A direct arithmetic count is O(C(K+1)R) per bin for R first-time representatives,
plus the scalar evaluation/root/precision costs above. This is a polynomial
state/arithmetic construction with explicit access assumptions, not a uniform
bit-complexity theorem independent of the kinetic input encoding.

Let local normalized joint (bit,current-density) row errors be bounded by
  e_i <= eta_i + sqrt(K+1)delta_i + a_i,
where eta_i is quadrature, delta_i parameter rounding, and a_i certified numerical

  D(Omega_exact,Omega_implemented) <= D_initial+sum_i e_i.       (8.3)
Here Omega=sum_h |h><h| tensor rho_h includes the complete exposed record and

Positive numerical realization: obtain interval-certified positive row weights,

9. FINITE OPERATIONAL FAILURE AND EXPLICIT POSITIVE REPAIR
=========================================================
Prepare vacuum. Apply a gain-only binary bin with integrated gain log(4/3), so
q=exp(-gain)=3/4. The click probability is 1/4. Conditional on that actual bit,
  P(N=n|click)=q(1-q)^(n-1), n>=1.                             (9.1)
Turn gain off, use perfect loss observation, and choose two loss exposures
log(3/2) and log2. Each original photon has probability 1/3 to be lost in the
first bin, 1/3 in the second, and 1/3 to survive. The actual two-bit law is
  (P00,P01,P10,P11)=(3/11,18/55,18/55,4/55).                    (9.2)

Any positive Poisson mixture propagated through this same loss instrument has,
conditional on its latent intensity, two independent equally distributed bits.
Thus it has P(unequal bits)<=1/2, whereas (9.2) gives 36/55. Conditional TV is
at least 17/110. The bounded three-bit witness
  1_(gain click)[1_(unequal loss bits)-1/2]
has range length one, exact mean 17/440 and nonpositive mean under every such
positive-P continuation. This gives a complete-record gap at least 17/440 even
if that restricted model changes its herald probability. It is a failure of a
specified representation/interface, not a universal classical-simulation no-go.

The richer physical cone gives an exact repair:
  rho_click = 3 integral_0^(1/3) sigma_(1,1/(1+v)) dv.            (9.3)
To check it, the n>=1 population is
  3 n integral_0^(1/3) v^(n-1)/(1+v)^(n+1) dv
    =(3/4)(1/4)^(n-1).
No photon-number cutoff or hidden trigger value has been supplied.

Abstract sufficient principle proved by the same Duhamel argument: if full and
no-event CP propagation preserve positive cones C_k without increasing k, and
a registered jump maps C_k into C_(k+r), then each absorbing first-event latch
costs at most r in that order. A finite physical state additionally requires
quantitative approximation of the remaining continuous cone parameters and
computable positive instrument rows. The oscillator supplies these missing
interfaces explicitly; the principle by itself is not a new general effective
theory of interacting matter, nor a replacement for established filtering.

The scientific decision is to keep the PHYSICAL positive-state route as central:
it carries information created by observed gain and loss, predicts a record
that defeats an ordinary positive-P state, and avoids a number cutoff growing

positive cone with comparable parameter geometry under actual mixing/interacting
instruments. Finite stellar order can coexist with rapidly growing polynomial
or tensor dimension, so a dimensionality/computability lemma is still required.
No cross-mode interaction closure is inferred from this one-mode construction.

11. SHARP DETECTOR BOUNDARY: ONE PARITY BIT
=========================================
The grade bound cannot be strengthened to depend merely on the number of output
bits. Pure gain from vacuum with q=exp(-G) gives P(N=n)=q(1-q)^n. If one actual
detector reports the parity of the number of gain events, its odd outcome leaves
positive probabilities on every odd N and zero on every even N. Any component
sigma_(k,theta) with theta<1 assigns positive mass to all sufficiently large
even N. In a positive mixture none can cancel. Hence an exact sigma-mixture
representation of this odd state must use theta=1, namely Fock states, and its
unbounded odd support requires unbounded k. One absorbing OR bit, in contrast,
has k<=1 from vacuum.

There is a quantitative version. Let L be the least odd integer >=3(K+1), and
E={N odd and N>=L}. For any k<=K and theta<=1/2,
  P_sigma(N odd) <= [1+(theta/(2-theta))^(k+1)]/2 <=2/3.
For theta>1/2, Markov's inequality and E N=(k+1)/theta-1 give
  P_sigma(E)<=P_sigma(N>=L)<=2(K+1)/L<=2/3.
Thus every positive mixture of order <=K has P(E)<=2/3. The actual odd posterior
has P(E|odd)=(1-q)^(L-1)>=(1-q)^(3(K+1)). Therefore
  D(rho_odd, any order-K sigma mixture)
      >=(1-q)^(3(K+1))-2/3.                                 (11.1)
Achieving a fixed error epsilon<1/3 requires
  K+1 >= log[1/(2/3+epsilon)]/[3(-log(1-q))],                  (11.2)
which is Omega(exp(G)) as G grows. This is a lower bound for THIS cone, not for
every effective description. A parity-adapted family can avoid it.

The odd herald has probability (1-q)/(2-q), approaching1/2. The fixed bounded
payoff 1_(odd herald)[1_E-2/3] has range length1 and nonpositive expectation for
any model whose odd branch has order<=K. The complete (parity,terminal-number)
record therefore has TV gap at least that herald probability times the positive
part of (11.1). This version does not assume equal model herald probabilities.
The parity instrument and terminal threshold/number test are new allowed tests
in this boundary example; their physical access is a premise, not an acquired
free observation or a contradiction of the OR-bin theorem.

The decisive general lesson is to seek positive families stable under the actual
marked instrument and its detector automaton, then prove metric and evaluation
bounds. Output entropy, excitation, Gaussianity and polynomial degree by
themselves do not select an adequate current physical state.
