- **Recurrent quantum memory:** For the pinned recurrent-tree model, fixed normal independent initial bath, and unassisted no-feedback communication, the report gives a reviewed quadratic-order gain above the `3/2` baseline for `0 < beta <= 1/32`. The exact leading coefficient and physical usefulness remain open. Extensions for initial entanglement and backward feedback have separate hypotheses.
- **Inaccessible entanglement inventory:** The reported universal bounds imply that the loss relative to nominal Bell supply has leading coefficient one in the hidden inventory entropy for growing-entropy finite-prior families, under the stated order of limits. Exact additive second-order loss remains open; a later separable counterexample has no established LOCC implementation or capacity consequence.
- **Adaptive graph sensing:** Under the specified hidden-two-star, positive-weight, full-vector Gaussian sensing contract and matched pathwise control budget, the reviewed theorem gives two adaptive actions versus order-`n` nonadaptive actions for connectivity. This does not establish a practical instrument advantage. Series resistance, routing, noise, switching, settling and leakage remain decisive, and a perfectly floating-terminal interface admits a constant-action ordinary baseline.
- **Quantum entropy with heavy tails:** Internal reviews cover actual heated amplifier-entropy asymptotics for specified finite-energy classes without a finite photon moment above one, including smooth logarithmic envelopes. The general unheated finite-energy conjecture and a useful universal heating threshold remain open.
- **Other scoped mathematics:** The report records internally reviewed operator-algebra non-repair examples, a conditional `FP^NP` quadratic range-avoidance result, and geometric laminate/compiler results with an obstruction only for a specified five-output quotient. General Avoid, `P` versus `NP`, and Morrey's `2x2` problem remain unresolved.
- **Quantum coding and control:** The reviewed code covers a stated source/device family and factor-two unknown-gain interval, with explicit initializer, coherent-operation and public-program assumptions. A later paid-program construction and the unknown-seed factory remain unreviewed or pending full review. The finite guards are enormous and no full code was run.
- **Physical searches:** Historical-data reanalysis and conditional models did not establish a validated new machine or net physical advantage. For example, an ideal gross-work improvement of about 8% was outweighed by a record-reset cost alone; synthetic checks and finite executions are not physical acquisitions.
