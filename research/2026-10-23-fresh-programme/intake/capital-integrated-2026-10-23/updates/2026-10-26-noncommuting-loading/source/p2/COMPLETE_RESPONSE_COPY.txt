## ASTRA — Foundational physics results

Three substantive results emerged from the continuation.

### 1. Cutoff-independent coherent loading theorem

For an explicitly controlled finite-band impurity with a \\(d\\)-dimensional degenerate logical code, I derived a coherent loading bound:

\\[ \boxed{ \\|\text{loading error}\\| \le \frac{d(d+1)\pi+5d^2\pi^2/4}{BT} } \\]

It remains valid for noncommuting bath and controller matrices, including logical states entangled with an external reference.

The bound does not depend on ultraviolet bandwidth or bath-mode count. It does, however, require a calibrated matrix-valued counterterm, and does not apply automatically to a protected subspace within a larger controller.

### 2. An exact obstruction to protected-code loading

I constructed a three-state example where the desired dressed quantum state exists at every coupling strength, yet a competing nearly conserved state prevents efficient preparation.

With the original single-envelope controls, achieving 90% loading probability requires a duration growing at least as \\(\sqrt{\lambda}/a\\), where \\(\lambda\\) measures the strength of the competing source and \\(a\\) its symmetry-breaking penalty.

At \\(\lambda=10^6\\), \\(a=1\\), the proved lower bound on preparation time is approximately 946.7 binding-energy time units.

This is a genuine controllability obstruction, not simply a failed adiabatic approximation.

### 3. Constructive repair through selective source completion

I developed a two-stage mechanism:

First load the logical code while the off-code coupling is disabled. Then restore that coupling with its properly calibrated cross-counterterms.

During restoration, the loaded code remains an exact eigenstate:

\\[ H(t)V=-BV,\qquad \frac{dH}{dt}V=0. \\]

Consequently, restoration adds zero ideal coherent loading error, even when the complementary spectral gap becomes extremely small.

This construction requires independent off-code coupling control and increasingly demanding counterterm calibration. It is not yet an implementation in the native Jacobi-chain interface.

The results were checked with noncommuting finite matrices, direct Schrödinger propagation and high-precision calculations. All executable consistency checks passed.
