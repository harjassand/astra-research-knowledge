1. PHYSICAL CONTRACT OF THE RESTRICTED NETWORK MODEL

There are four junctions A,B,C,D, with two distinguishable reactive arms each.
Every arm belongs to exactly one bond. A bond between two arms of the same
junction is a primary loop. Inter-junction bonds are called non-loop bonds;
this label does NOT assert that they are elastically active under every load.

Two unlocked bonds can exchange their four partners, with either alternative
pairing at rate nu/2. Hence each pair of unlocked bonds has total exchange
rate nu. These rates are symmetric: the zero-load, zero-energy synthetic
pairing ensemble obeys detailed balance. Giving every pair access to exchange
is an intentionally favorable mixing assumption. Geometric restrictions may
make real repair harder; they do not remove the combinatorial counterexample.

An ideal classifier locks each non-loop bond at rate k, never locks a primary
loop, and never misclassifies a bond. Locked bonds cannot exchange during the
operation. Locking is an effectively absorbing kinetic limit over the write
and retention interval, not a finite-affinity thermodynamic rate model.
Actual fuel, reverse rates, finite retention and reset must be added before
using these rates to estimate chemical work or entropy production.

This is a model of conserving arms through associative partner exchange.
It excludes free monofunctional reagents, fresh junctions, chain cleavage,
unlimited partner reservoirs and deliberate unlocking. Those are possible
ways to escape the obstruction, but are different operating resources.

2. AN EXPLICIT POSITIVE-PROBABILITY JAM

Start with bonds AA, BC, CD, DB: a primary loop at A and a triangle B-C-D.
If the three non-loop triangle bonds lock before an exchange occurs, AA is
the only remaining unlocked bond. Two-bond exchange is now impossible, so the
defect is permanent within the declared operation. No bad bond was locked.

The probability of precisely this initial three-lock sequence is

  P_jam >= [3k/(3k+6nu)] [2k/(2k+3nu)] [k/(k+nu)] > 0.        (N1)

At k=nu the explicit lower bound is 1/15. Other paths also jam. The exact
finite-state generator calculation gives

  P_jam = 0.23229926261303044,
  P_final connected and loop-free = 0.6161811789241672,
  P_final loop-free but disconnected = 0.15151955846280188.    (N2)

The calculation enumerates 1,265 reachable states, 1,173 transient states and
92 terminal states. A state contains the complete locked matching and the
complete unlocked matching of distinguishable arms. It is not a mean-field
closure or an independent-bond approximation. Numerical linear-equation
residuals are below 2e-13 in the reported cases.

For k/nu=0.1,1,10 the jam probabilities are respectively
0.1304489862, 0.2322992626, 0.7254323268. Faster irreversible protection can
increase defective arrest even with perfect selectivity. These rate ratios
are synthetic diagnostics of the exact graph, not tuned material rates or
predicted polymer yields.

The isolated L<->B motif with a>0 and zero false-lock rate has eventual
bridge-capture probability one. Here the effective access rate a reaches
zero because other captures exhaust the exchange partners. This is a specific
reason that the isolated-motif result cannot be promoted to a collective
repair guarantee.

3. EXACT FEASIBILITY CONDITION FOR PRIMARY-LOOP ELIMINATION

Let d_i be the number of remaining unlocked arms at junction i and
D=sum_i d_i, an even number. With arbitrary inter-junction multibonds allowed,
a loop-free pairing of those arms exists if and only if

  max_i d_i <= D/2.                                         (N3)

Necessity: all arms of a junction must pair with arms outside that junction.
Sufficiency: repeatedly pair an arm from each of the two currently largest
nonzero groups. If the original largest group saturates D/2 it loses one arm;
after removing two arms, every group's count is at most (D-2)/2. If it did not
saturate, all groups were already at most D/2-1. Thus induction preserves the
condition until no arms remain. Ties do not invalidate the argument. Parallel
edges are allowed; forbidding them adds further graphicality constraints.

The code independently exhausts all 128 even subsets of this instance's
eight arms and compares (N3) with explicit pairing enumeration. This finite
check supplements rather than replaces the preceding general proof.

Suppose a global controller permits a non-loop lock only if the remaining
arms still satisfy (N3). Further assume positive exchange rates connecting
all matchings on the remaining arms. Then eventual loop-free completion is
almost sure in this finite model. Proof: with locked bonds fixed, any matching
can be transformed into any other by successive two-bond partner swaps.
Reach a loop-free completion guaranteed by (N3); locking an edge from that
completion leaves another loop-free completion. Therefore every nonterminal
locked class has a positive-probability route to one more lock. Finiteness and
the monotone increase of the locked count imply eventual absorption without
primary loops. This is an eventual result, not a bounded-time guarantee.

It assumes the ability to classify any candidate non-loop and obtain the
global remaining degree information. Neither is granted by an ordinary force
sensor. A force sensor can miss unloaded inter-junction bonds and respond to
strained loops or entangled structures. These interfaces remain UNKNOWN.

4. REMOVING PRIMARY LOOPS DOES NOT GUARANTEE A USEFUL NETWORK

The stub guard leaves a 0.1515195585 probability of a disconnected, fully
locked loop-free network at k=nu. For example, two doubly bonded pairs have no
primary loops yet do not form one connected four-junction body.

The second guard in the code explicitly asks whether the currently locked
bonds admit SOME connected, loop-free completion. It enumerates remaining
pairings for this small instance. A lock is permitted only when the answer
remains yes. The same finite irreducibility/monotone-lock proof gives eventual
target completion with probability one. This is a global completion oracle,
not a free local molecular reaction. It is included to identify what control
information resolves the obstruction, not to claim that DNA or another
material already supplies that information.

Even connectedness does not certify stiffness, toughness, fatigue life or
manufacturing usefulness. Those require spatial geometry, strand elasticity,
reference lengths and load cases. Replacing a direct spring by two springs
in series can increase graph participation while reducing a measured
stiffness. No elastic performance is inferred from the graph calculation.

6. THE OBSTRUCTION SURVIVES LIGATION POLARITY

The original untyped model is not silently identified with DNA ligation.
The added typed model gives each vertex one 3'OH arm (even stub) and one
5'phosphate arm (odd stub). Only opposite-type pairs are allowed. The
initial bonds are (0,1),(2,5),(4,7),(3,6): A's primary loop and the directed
B-C-D-B cycle. Of the two alternative pairings of two open bonds, exactly
one respects type. Its rate is nu, so total exchange hazard per bond pair
is the SAME nu as in the untyped model. Sequence compatibility is universal
within types. Spatial encounter kinetics and enzyme rates are not supplied.

With residual counts u_i of OH and v_i of phosphate and equal total n of each,
a loop-free pairing exists iff u_i+v_i<=n for every vertex. For Hall's
condition, acceptors from one vertex have n-v_i possible donors; acceptors
spanning two distinct vertices have all n donors. These are necessary and
sufficient. The code checks all 70 balanced subsets against enumeration.
If sequence orthogonality restricts the compatibility graph, this reduction
is invalid; the full Hall condition is required.

At k=nu the independent type-preserving permutation-generator audit gives
EXACT probabilities (connected, disconnected loop-free, jam):
  unrestricted:    (55869/120500, 6187/24100, 8424/30125),
  stub guard:      (17913/24100, 6187/24100, 0),
  connected guard: (1, 0, 0).
Thus typed unrestricted jam probability is 0.2796348547717842 and the
connected target probability is 0.4636431535269710. The lower bound (N1)
remains valid. These are exact conditional graph results, not measured
chemical yields. See ../chemical_control/typed_capture_audit.py and its
companion note/results for the independent generator and rational solve.

For this one-OH/one-phosphate degree-two instance, the completion guard has
a simpler exact characterization: reject any lock that closes a directed
cycle on a proper subset of the four vertices. If no such cycle is locked,
locked components are directed paths; their remaining heads and tails can
be joined into one spanning directed cycle. This proves sufficiency and
matches the exhaustive completion oracle. The criterion still needs
component information; a local exposed nick is not an implementation.

Typed unrestricted/stub/connected guards have respectively 233/225/198
reachable states and 216/216/192 transient states. Their mean absorption
times at equal rates are 2.34784509,3.18674965,3.51555556 in nu^-1. At twice
the connected guard's mean, target mass is 0.934605629 and unfinished mass
0.065394371. Infinite-time certainty remains distinct from finite throughput.

7. WHAT A PHYSICAL REPAIR CONSTRUCTION MUST NOW SUPPLY

Candidate force-gated covalent arrest must address two independent scales:
at the junction, it must protect the actual arm-to-arm load path; across the
sample, it must preserve access to a useful target network. The proposed DNA
ligation construction addresses the first conditionally. It does not solve
the second by virtue of being force sensitive.

Possible escape resources are explicit global sensing/guarded curing,
controlled temporary unlocking of selected good bonds, spare reactive arms,
fresh linker/junction reservoirs, or a reversible scaffold with a verified
final removal step. None is free. For the explicit AA jam, unlocking one
nonincident BC bond permits AA+BC -> AB+AC; it converts the trapped loop into
two inter-junction bonds but changes the graph and the elastic load paths.
Choosing this move requires locating the defect and a compatible partner.

The present result argues against optimizing a standalone local force-lock
rate before resolving those interfaces. A concrete next calculation would
include a supplied same-junction DNA latch and its reverse/unlocking channel
in an embedded spring network, measure all source/fuel changes, and test
retained modulus after removing the temporary scaffold. Until those inputs
exist, global repair performance remains UNKNOWN.
