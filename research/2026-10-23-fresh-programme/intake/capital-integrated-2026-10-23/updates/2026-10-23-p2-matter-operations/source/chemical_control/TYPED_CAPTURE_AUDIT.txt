INDEPENDENT REPRESENTATION AND GENERATOR

Each of four junctions has one even 3'OH arm and one odd 5'phosphate arm.

SYMMETRY AND EXACT SOLUTION

Only a simultaneous relabeling of junctions is allowed: pi becomes p*pi*p^-1,
and a locked tail i becomes locked tail p(i). On actual stub labels, the map is
  2*i+t -> 2*p(i)+t,  t in {0,1}.
Thus each OH arm remains OH and each phosphate arm remains phosphate. There are
NO individual type flips. The optional global exchange of the two types is also
unused. This is a directed, marked graph quotient, not an undirected quotient.

Strong lumpability is checked exactly by comparing rates from every member of
an orbit to each different orbit. Exact rational Gauss-Jordan elimination solves
the quotient absorption equations; three right-hand sides give the outcome
probabilities and a fourth gives the mean absorption time. Every probability is
nonnegative and each row sums to one exactly. The mean time uses units 1/nu.

LOOP-FREE FEASIBILITY: HALL REDUCTION

Let u_i,v_i count remaining OH and phosphate arms at junction i; both totals
equal n because every lock consumes one of each. Assume any opposite-polarity
arms can pair except within the same junction. Then loop-free completion exists
iff u_i+v_i<=n for every i.

Necessity: u_i arms cannot use v_i partners, so u_i<=n-v_i. For sufficiency,
apply Hall's theorem to remaining OH arms. A subset drawn from only junction i
has all n-v_i external phosphate arms as neighbors. A subset spanning two or more
distinct junctions has every phosphate arm as a neighbor. Hence the one-junction
inequalities exhaust Hall's conditions. This argument works for general residual
counts under universal opposite-type compatibility. The audit independently
enumerates all 70 balanced residual stub subsets of the present eight-stub model
and confirms equality with a direct bijection search.

Sequence-specific exclusions require Hall's condition on their actual allowed
graph. They are not covered by the degree-only condition.

CONNECTED FEASIBILITY: DIRECTED PROPER-SUBSET CYCLE GUARD

For the present one-OH/one-phosphate-per-junction model, locked edges form a
partial permutation. Each vertex has locked indegree and outdegree at most one.
Its components are directed paths (including isolated vertices) or directed cycles.

A locked directed cycle on a proper subset is an obstruction: every in/out arm
on those vertices is consumed, so no further edge can connect that component to
the remaining vertices. A full four-vertex cycle is already a valid endpoint.

Conversely, when there is no proper locked cycle, all incomplete components are
directed paths. If there are multiple components, join each path's free outgoing
end to the next component's free incoming start in a cycle through all components.
Distinct components ensure these are non-loop, opposite-type edges. If one path
already spans all four vertices, its endpoint can close to its distinct start.
The resulting full permutation is one directed four-cycle, hence connected and
loop-free. This proves necessity and sufficiency of the directed cycle guard.

This connected-cycle theorem uses exactly one arm of each type per junction,
universal compatibility, and arbitrary future typed re-pairings. It is not a
