model. It is not a measured material response, acquired measurement, device
simulation, finite-temperature retention certificate or practical advantage.

A continuous one-dimensional electrostrictive bar with finite-pitch parallel
piezoelectric bridges generates CELL-AVERAGE squared-order-parameter gates.
The gate is derived from compatible displacement and charge variables; no
independent sitewise quartic or independently adjustable loss is assumed.

A sufficient pitch condition makes the chosen equilibrium path globally
optimal against EVERY admissible spatial configuration, not merely stable
against homogeneous perturbations:

  ell < pi sqrt(kappa/lambda_m),
  lambda_m=max(0,9 b_m^2/(20c)-a),                            (A)

along with a positive scalar gated curvature margin mu defined below.
The stronger all-amplitude comparison follows from semiconvexity and the
cell Poincare inequality. The exact reset identity avoids unnecessarily
tightening (A) during compensated charge return.

The useful operation is retained polarization inversion, -B -> +B. Because
electrostrictive strain depends on q^2, the initial and final piezo strains,
charges, voltages and switch settings can be identical. The electrical gate
is engaged under load, traversed and returned through explicit charge ports.
An ordinary passive resistor dissipates the circulated work. Recovering it
requires a bidirectional electrical port and pays finite-time R I^2 loss.

Static elimination does NOT establish rapid homogeneous tracking. The full
displacement/charge equations and their passive work identity are specified
below; finite sound propagation, mechanical lag, leakage and thermal noise
remain in the physical problem. Root is separately testing those dynamics.

2. ONE COMPATIBLE ENERGY AND GEOMETRY

Take a bar of length L=N ell and cross-sectional area A. The scalar order
parameter q(x) has intrinsic isothermal free-energy density

  f_m(q)=a q^2/2-b_m q^4/4+c q^6/6,  b_m,c>0.

Displacement u(x) is continuous. The transformation strain is alpha q^2;
the material longitudinal modulus C and gradient coefficient kappa are
positive. A piezoelectric bridge connects the endpoints of each interval
I_j=[j ell,(j+1)ell]. It therefore sees the compatible mean strain

  e_j=[u((j+1)ell)-u(j ell)]/ell.

Let z_j be bridge electrode charge per active-cell volume, Cp>0 its clamped
capacitance per active-cell volume, theta its charge/strain coefficient, and
K_E>0 its short-circuit effective stiffness per active volume. Bridge geometry
is already included in these effective quantities. The stored free energy
per cross-sectional area, before adding the conjugate field, is

  Phi/A = sum_j {integral_Ij [f_m(q)+kappa q_x^2/2
                  +C(u_x-alpha q^2)^2/2] dx
               +ell[K_E e_j^2/2+(z_j-theta e_j)^2/(2Cp)]}.     (1)

The field work port is integral h dq; equilibrium uses Phi-A integral hq dx.
All quadratic mechanical/electrical coefficients in (1) are positive. The
negative intrinsic quartic is bounded by c q^6/6. This is a stable stored-
energy model, not an active negative-capacitance assumption.

Possible geometry: a ribbon with polarization transverse to its long axis,
screening electrodes supplying h, and axial piezo bridges. A scalar easy
axis, adequate screening, bridge bonding/no slip and the absence of extra
low-energy order parameters are explicit assumptions. The model does not
claim that a particular BaTiO3 specimen meets them. Full transverse elastic,
electrostatic, domain and defect physics are additional material gates.

Static derivation assumes free or force-balanced overall ends. Fixed total
extension or a grounded substrate generally adds another compatibility term
and must be rederived. The bridges do not independently pin every atom.

3. EXACT STATIC ELIMINATION: A CELL KERNEL, NOT A LOCAL QUARTIC

Write M_j=mean_Ij(q^2). At fixed endpoint extension, minimizing material
displacement gives uniform stress inside each cell and

  u_x=alpha q^2+e_j-alpha M_j.

Thus the minimized material elastic energy is ell C(e_j-alpha M_j)^2/2.
Minimizing the resulting quadratic in e_j gives

  K_D=K_E+theta^2/Cp,
  e_j*=[C alpha M_j+theta z_j/Cp]/(C+K_D).

Define

  u0=2 alpha^2 C K_E/(C+K_E),
  Ceff=Cp+theta^2/(C+K_E),
  Theta=theta C alpha/(C+K_E),
  U=2 Theta^2/Ceff,
  uD=u0+U=2 alpha^2 C K_D/(C+K_D).

The EXACT reduced energy of a cell is

  F_j/A = integral_Ij[f_m(q)+kappa q_x^2/2-hq] dx
            +ell u0 M_j^2/4
            +ell(z_j-Theta M_j)^2/(2Ceff).                    (2)

Independent bridges generate one average q^2 per segment; the kernel follows
from compatible u. At open zero charge z=0, the total gate is uD M_j^2/4.
At zero voltage with charge free to relax, z=Theta M_j, leaving u0 M_j^2/4.
Short-circuit stiffness is NOT zero and cannot be silently removed.

The homogeneous short-circuit potential is

  f0(q)=f_m(q)+u0 q^4/4=a q^2/2-b q^4/4+c q^6/6,
  b=b_m-u0>0.

Assume coexistence of this COMPOSITE, not the unclamped intrinsic material:

  a=3b^2/(16c), B^2=3b/(4c),
  f0(q)=c q^2(q^2-B^2)^2/6 >=0.                             (3)

Actual coexistence and coefficients must be measured in the bonded specimen.

4. GLOBAL SPATIAL PATH THEOREM

Let lambda_m=max(0,9b_m^2/(20c)-a), so f_m''(q)>=-lambda_m for all real q.
For each cell put m_j=mean(q), v_j=q-m_j. Semiconvexity gives

  integral f_m(q) >= ell f_m(m_j)-lambda_m integral v_j^2/2.

The zero-mean Poincare inequality on an interval gives

  integral q_x^2 >= (pi/ell)^2 integral v_j^2.

Also M_j=m_j^2+mean(v_j^2)>=m_j^2, so for any w>=0,

  F_w[q] := sum_j {integral[f_m(q)+kappa q_x^2/2-hq]
                                      +ell w M_j^2/4}
   >=sum_j ell[f_m(m_j)+w m_j^4/4-h m_j]
                     +(gamma/2) integral_0^L(q-m_cell)^2 dx,
  gamma=kappa pi^2/ell^2-lambda_m.                           (4)

This holds for EVERY admissible H1 configuration, with no small-amplitude
or amplitude-cap assumption. It also respects continuity between cells.

Suppose gamma>0 and the homogeneous OPEN-CHARGE potential has margin

  mu=a-9(max(b-U,0))^2/(20c)>0.                              (5)

For U>=b, mu=a. A weaker sufficient requirement is
U/b>1-sqrt(5/12), using (3). No claim that U>=b is necessary is made.

For any selected p in [-B,B], set z_j=0 and

  h(p)=f0'(p)+U p^3=ap+(U-b)p^3+cp^5.

The homogeneous scalar potential is globally strongly convex, with unique
minimum p. Apply (4) with w=uD and scalar strong convexity:

  F[q]-F[p] >= (mu/2) sum_j ell(m_j-p)^2
                        +(gamma/2) integral(q-m_cell)^2
             >= min(mu,gamma)/2 integral(q-p)^2.            (6)

Therefore the selected homogeneous state is the UNIQUE GLOBAL MINIMUM,
against every spatial perturbation and finite-amplitude competitor, for all
N. This is a global path/quadratic-growth theorem. It does not assert that
the energy functional is convex at every unrelated configuration.

The same estimate at w=u0, h=0 uses (3) to prove

  F_short[q]>=sum_j ell f0(m_j)+(gamma/2) integral v_j^2>=0.  (7)

For a connected bar and gamma>0 its only global ground states are uniform
0,+B,-B. Thus the permanently present short-circuit shunt is included in
both the driven path and the restored-state baseline.

5. UNDER-LOAD ENGAGEMENT AND RESET: EXACT ALL-SPATIAL IDENTITY

Choose electrode polarity so Theta>0. Let sigma=+1 or -1, target q=sigma B,
and charge z_j=r Theta B^2 with 0<=r<=1. Apply

  h=sigma U(1-r)B^3.

Subtract the target energy in (2). Using (7), the exact difference is

  F[q]-F[sigma B]
   =F_short[q]
     +sum_j ell {U(M_j-B^2)^2/4
          +U(1-r)B^2 mean_Ij[(q-sigma B)^2]/2} >=0.         (8)

The target is unique for r<1. At r=1 its voltage is zero; closing the short
lets charge follow Theta M_j, recovering the original coexistence baseline.

The original pitch gamma>0 SUFFICES during this compensated charge change.
One could apply a generic fixed-preload semiconvexity bound and demand
ell<pi sqrt[kappa/(lambda_m+U B^2)], but that stricter condition is unnecessary
for this exact path because (8) exploits its positive tangent remainder.

A CLOSED-ACTUATOR POLARITY INVERSION is therefore:

  Initial: q=-B, shorted, z=Theta B^2, e=C alpha B^2/(C+K_E), h=V=0.
  Engage under load: r:1->0, hold q=-B with h=-U(1-r)B^3.
  Traverse: z=0, p:-B->+B, h=f0'(p)+U p^3.
  Return charge: r:0->1, hold q=+B with h=+U(1-r)B^3.
  Final: shorted, h=V=0, and all initial actuator strains/charges restored.

Charge withdrawal under load requires a source; simply opening a short does
not change its existing charge and does not produce the zero-charge gate.
The material's polarization is reversed. Electrostrictive strain depends on
q^2, so the PIEZOELECTRIC SHUNTS' mechanical and electrical endpoint states
are identical. This is a known-input state transfer, with no claim of a NOT
operation on an unknown bit: the initial negative compensating field selects
the negative phase and need not invert both possible starting states.
The conjugate-field electrodes' screening charge and source storage are a
separate physical interface; equality of h endpoints alone does not certify
their full reset. Root's dynamical model explicitly includes that source port.

By comparison, a 0->+B phase-conversion stroke leaves a different piezo strain
and induced charge. Such a stroke must count the retained composite state;
it cannot claim the actuator has completely returned to its initial state.

6. FIXED GATE RANGE, BANDWIDTH AND LOSS FROM THE SAME MODEL

The available electrical gate increment is not an independent parameter:

  U=2 alpha^2 C^2(K_D-K_E)/[(C+K_E)(C+K_D)].                (9)

Let k_e^2=(K_D-K_E)/K_D<1 and x=K_D/C. Then

  U/(2 alpha^2 C)
   =k_e^2 x/[(1+x)(1+(1-k_e^2)x)]
   <=[1-sqrt(1-k_e^2)]/[1+sqrt(1-k_e^2)].                  (10)

The bound is attained algebraically at x=1/sqrt(1-k_e^2), if that geometry
and its other constraints are achievable. Changing geometry also changes
u0 and hence b=b_m-u0; b must not be held independently fixed while optimizing
this expression. The bridge's strain/voltage range and breakdown constraints
may prevent reaching even the algebraic maximum.

For a resistor R_e in the cell-normalized circuit and prescribed harmonic
strain e~exp(i nu t), the same piezo energy gives

  K(nu)=K_E+(K_D-K_E) i nu tau/[1+i nu tau], tau=R_e Cp.    (11)

Its stiffness and loss are inseparable: Im K=(K_D-K_E)nu tau/(1+nu^2 tau^2).
Every finite passive resistor gives K(0)=K_E. Maintaining a quasistatic
open-charge gate therefore requires sufficiently low leakage or charge
control; assigning static K_D and zero damping to an ordinary finite-R shunt
is inconsistent.

Once mechanics is relaxed, the electrical charging capacitance is Ceff, so
tau_e=R_e Ceff. Mechanical bandwidth must be included before using Ceff;
at frequencies where strain cannot relax, the clamped Cp is relevant.

The quasistatic charge swing and maximum piezo voltage on the fixed-target
engagement/return are

  zB=Theta B^2, |V_p,max|=zB/Ceff,
  Wcirculated=U B^4/4=zB^2/(2Ceff).                         (12)

Engagement supplies +Wcirculated; return can in principle deliver it back.
The ideal field work across -B->+B is zero by symmetry, although the field
port exchanges energy during traversal. With charge endpoints separated by
Delta z in duration T, resistor dissipation satisfies

  W_R=integral R_e zdot^2 dt >= R_e(Delta z)^2/T.

For either full charge stroke,

  W_R/Wcirculated >= 2 tau_e/T.                            (13)

An ordinary RC short at fixed q=B dissipates Wcirculated in complete
relaxation. At every finite passive time a residual charge/voltage remains.
A finite-time bidirectional source can drive a smooth endpoint-matched
charge path; its net work is the free-energy change plus (13), mechanical
losses and any residual kinetic/storage energy. Recovery requires that source
actually accept returned energy. Generator/switch losses and wiring leakage
are additional measured quantities, not free coefficients selected here.

7. FULL DISPLACEMENT/CHARGE DYNAMICS AND PASSIVE WORK IDENTITY

Keep u(x,t); do not substitute e*(q,z) into a fast operation. Add kinetic and
circuit-inductor storage to (1):

  E/A=Phi/A+integral rho u_t^2/2 dx+sum_j ell L_e,j zdot_j^2/2.

Here rho>0, zeta>0, eta>=0, L_e>=0 and R_e>=0 are physical coefficients.
For actual cell volume Vcell=A ell, R_e=R_ohm Vcell and L_e=L_henry Vcell;
Cp is capacitance/Vcell. This scaling prevents varying pitch while silently
holding unrelated electrical costs fixed.

Order-parameter dynamics:

  zeta q_t=h-f_m'(q)+kappa q_xx+2C alpha q(u_x-alpha q^2).

Mechanical weak form, for an admissible test displacement v:

  integral rho u_tt v dx
   +integral [C(u_x-alpha q^2)+eta u_xt]v_x dx
   +sum_j S_j[v(x_(j+1))-v(x_j)] = applied boundary force functional,
  S_j=K_E e_j-theta(z_j-theta e_j)/Cp.

Electrical dynamics:

  L_e,j zddot_j+R_e,j zdot_j+(z_j-theta e_j)/Cp=V_j(t).     (14)

Multiplication by velocities and integration gives the exact isothermal
free-energy/kinetic-storage identity

  Edot/A = integral h q_t dx+sum_j ell V_j zdot_j+P_boundary/A
       -integral zeta q_t^2 dx-integral eta u_xt^2 dx
       -sum_j ell R_e,j zdot_j^2.                           (15)

The h source's field storage, source efficiency and leakage lie beyond that
work port and must be added for a wall-energy comparison. Phi is a free
energy: equality of endpoint Phi is not a claim of zero latent heat or equal
internal energy. Thermal heat and entropy belong in the complete cycle.
Adding thermal fluctuations also requires the noise associated with these
dissipative coefficients; damping/noise cannot be chosen independently.

At fixed controls a strict full-energy minimum is passively stable in this
model. It does not follow that a desired changing equilibrium can be tracked
at arbitrary rate. For example, homogeneous q(t) with uniform equilibrium
strain epsilon(t) gives u=epsilon(t)(x-L/2), whose kinetic energy is

  Ekin/A=rho L^3 epsilondot^2/24.

Uniform internal stress has zero spatial divergence and cannot generate
nonzero interior acceleration epsilonddot(x-L/2). Starting/stopping this
affine strain needs elastic waves or distributed body forces. Finite shunt
pitch does not remove the free bar's longest acoustic mode. Equation (A) is
a STATIC spatial-control certificate, not a finite-time scalability result.

8. LEAKAGE DURING TRAVERSAL: DISTINCT FROM COMPENSATED RESET

If a nominally open cell leaks through R_off while mechanics is relaxed,

  R_off zdot=-(z-Theta M)/Ceff.

On a monotone bounded traversal with 0<=M<=B^2 and initial z=0,

  0<=z/(Theta B^2)<=1-exp[-T/(R_off Ceff)].

At fixed leaked charge, the homogeneous quadratic coefficient becomes
a_z=a-U z/Theta. Its convexity margin is mu_z=mu-U z/Theta. The direct
Poincare proof uses lambda_z=lambda_m+U z/Theta when lambda_m>0.
Thus a sufficient robust middle-stage requirement is

  U z_max/Theta < mu,
  kappa pi^2/ell^2 > lambda_m+U z_max/Theta.                (16)

For U=b and z_max/(Theta B^2)=s, mu_z=a(1-4s), so s<1/4 is a simple
scalar requirement. A conservative time window is then
T<R_off Ceff ln(4/3), together with the independently needed material and
mechanical settling times. This is not a blanket tolerance for uncontrolled
leakage: it assumes a compensating field using the actual charge, and all
cells must satisfy the bound. Charge-feedback power/noise must be counted.

Conditions (16) apply to a perturbed MIDDLE traversal. They are not required
for the compensated endpoint charge strokes, where exact identity (8) applies.

10. RETENTION AND DECISIVE MATERIAL/COMPARATOR GATES

At final short circuit and zero h the three phases remain degenerate. The
deterministic positive state is retained as a local minimum, not as a unique
finite-temperature Gibbs endpoint. Large-volume homogeneous barriers cannot
be substituted for the domain nucleation/capture dynamics of a real device.

A useful obstruction already follows from this model: cell Cauchy-Schwarz
gives ell M_j^2<=integral q^4. Therefore

  F_short[q] <= integral [f0(q)+kappa q_x^2/2] dx.

For free ends, translating a local 0/+B domain wall across the bar provides
an escape-path upper bound independent of bar length. The local wall energy
per cross-sectional area is

  sigma0=integral_0^B sqrt(2 kappa f0(q))dq
        =B^4 sqrt(kappa c/3)/4.

Thus an escape from the selected polar state to the central phase has a
barrier no larger than A sigma0 in the free-end idealization, even though a
uniform-coordinate estimate scales with volume. Root is separately developing
retention and dynamical locality; no retention time is inferred from this bound.

Decisive next acquisition: one bonded specimen must supply intrinsic and
short/open-circuit composite constitutive coefficients, electrostriction,
gradient/domain cost, coupling theta/Cp, bridge stiffness and safe voltage,
mechanical inertia/loss, switch leakage and recovered electrical work, plus
spatially resolved written-state and retention statistics. The full vector
elastic/electric kernel must either reduce to this model or be reanalyzed.

A discriminating experiment would hold the measured homogeneous gate response
fixed while changing bridge pitch, then resolve spatial breakup along the
same quasistatic bias path. A homogeneous coercivity trace alone cannot test
(6). A genuine advantage must compare retained inversion fidelity and total
energy/time against optimized direct field, ordinary piezoelectric coercivity
control and other charge/strain protocols on the same boundary. This report
establishes neither superiority nor a novel physical principle.
