1. SHARP RESET AND RETENTION OBSTRUCTIONS

For a connected one-dimensional H1 bar, q has a continuous representative.
In the relaxed short-circuit baseline, each bridge satisfies

  M_j=mean_Ij(q^2),
  e_j=C alpha M_j/(C+K_E),       z_j=Theta M_j.

Assume a nontrivial coupling and the originally polarized actuator state
e_B=C alpha B^2/(C+K_E), z_B=Theta B^2. If every bridge is exactly restored
to this state, then M_j=B^2 for every cell. If additionally |q(x)|<=B, then

  integral_Ij [B^2-q(x)^2] dx=0

forces q^2=B^2 almost everywhere. Continuity on a connected interval forces
one uniform sign: q is identically +B or identically -B. Therefore a genuine
nonuniform retained sign pattern cannot simultaneously have |q|<=B and
restore every relaxed piezo bridge to that uniform polarized baseline.

Options that evade this precise obstruction change the contract: accept a
different retained actuator state near walls; allow |q|>B somewhere to offset
the wall's reduced mean square; use mechanical/electrical decoupling not in
this model; or physically separate the order-parameter domains. Overshoot
alone is not a construction or a proof of zero-field stationarity.

There is a separate amplitude-independent global-energy obstruction. At
zero h and a true short, the existing theorem gives F_short>=0, with only
uniform 0,+B,-B as ground states when gamma>0. A nonuniform pattern therefore
cannot inherit a global-ground-state retention certificate after full control
release. It might be metastable, but then a local barrier, wall motion/pinning
and finite-temperature retention analysis are additional obligations.

The existing -B to +B operation is a known-input state transfer. It does not
become a NOT on an unknown bit or arbitrary-input write merely because each
selected middle-path state is the unique global minimum. Quadratic growth
does not generally rule out other local traps or prove finite-time tracking:
for example x^2+10 sin^2(x)>=x^2 has a unique global minimum at zero and also
a nonzero local minimum between 3pi/4 and pi. This illustrates the logical
gap; it does not assert such traps in the particular gated bar. The root's
finite-time dynamics is separate evidence for its particular known input.

2. WHAT CELLWISE BIAS DOES PROVE, WITHOUT GLOBAL FUNCTIONAL CONVEXITY

Use zero gate charge, w=uD, and define the homogeneous gated potential

  g(s)=f_m(s)+w s^4/4,       g''(s)>=mu>0.

For arbitrary anchors a_j choose cellwise constant h_j=g'(a_j). Define the
piecewise constant anchor a_pc(x)=a_j and

  E_lb=sum_j ell [g(a_j)-h_j a_j].

The ORIGINAL Jensen/Poincare argument remains valid and gives, for every
admissible continuous H1 q with cell means m_j,

  F_h[q]-E_lb
    >= (mu/2) sum_j ell(m_j-a_j)^2
       +(gamma/2) sum_j integral_Ij(q-m_j)^2 dx
    >= (min(mu,gamma)/2) ||q-a_pc||_L2^2.                  (1)

This is an exact all-amplitude bound. It does not assume that the complete
functional is convex. But if neighboring anchors differ, a_pc is not H1 and
does not attain E_lb. The homogeneous proof's equality/cancellation step
cannot be copied independently into adjacent cells of a connected bar.

Here is a usable correction. Supply ANY admissible smooth trial profile r
and compute its full defect, including gradient and cell-average elastic
terms,

  Delta=F_h[r]-E_lb >=0.

Every global minimizer q* of the actual biased energy then obeys

  (mu/2) sum_j ell(mean_j(q*)-a_j)^2
   +(gamma/2) sum_j integral_Ij(q*-mean_j(q*))^2 <= Delta.  (2)

For nonzero anchors, the sufficient condition

  Delta < min_j [mu ell a_j^2/2]                           (3)

guarantees that EVERY global minimizer has the requested sign of the mean
in EVERY cell. If any cell mean had the wrong sign, its first term in (2)
alone would contradict (3). More generally, replace a_j^2 by an allowed
mean-error tolerance squared to certify a chosen readout interval.

Equation (3) is a conditional global-minimizer classification certificate,
not exact pointwise pattern selection. It is a sufficient, possibly very
conservative test. Failure does not prove an actual pattern impossible.
The cell means are the specified readout; tiny subcell reversed domains are
not excluded merely by their signs. No dynamical convergence, zero-field
retention or cyclic actuator reset follows from (1)-(3).

3. EXACT SMOOTH-PROFILE CERTIFICATE AND THE MISSING BIAS INTERFACE

For an intended continuous profile p, set M_p,j=mean_Ij(p^2). A necessary
stationarity condition with ONLY cellwise constant bias is

  f_m'(p(x))+w M_p,j p(x)-kappa p''(x)=h_j (constant on Ij),
  p and p' continuous at interfaces,    p'(0)=p'(L)=0.      (4)

If (4) fails in a cell interior, a compactly supported variation can lower
energy to first order. A jump in p' would require an interface force/source
term not present in the model. A generic chosen smooth interpolation between
cell targets does not automatically satisfy (4). Allowing an arbitrary
spatial bias h(x) instead of one h_j would supply a different control
interface and would require its own source/cost accounting.

If (4) DOES hold, put v=q-p. Exact expansion and cancellation of the first
variation give

  F_h[p+v]-F_h[p]
   = integral {f_m(p+v)-f_m(p)-f_m'(p)v+kappa v'^2/2} dx
     +sum_j { (w M_p,j/2) integral_Ij v^2 dx
              +(w ell/4)[2 mean_Ij(pv)+mean_Ij(v^2)]^2 }. (5)

The last square must be retained in an exact calculation. A conservative
but computable all-amplitude certificate follows by defining

  rho(p)=inf_v 2[f_m(p+v)-f_m(p)-f_m'(p)v]/v^2,

using the continuous extension at v=0. For the sixth-order intrinsic
potential this is the minimum of the explicit quartic in v

  a-3 b_m p^2+5c p^4
  +[-2 b_m p+(20c/3)p^3]v
  +[-b_m/2+5c p^2]v^2+2c p v^3+(c/3)v^4.

Let lambda_* be the lowest Neumann Rayleigh quotient of

  -kappa d^2/dx^2 +rho(p(x))+w M_p,j.

If lambda_*>0, (5) proves

  F_h[q]-F_h[p] >= lambda_* ||q-p||_L2^2/2                (6)

against every amplitude and every compatible spatial competitor. This is
an exact CONDITIONAL extension to a prescribed nonuniform target, without
assuming global functional convexity. Its conditions are additional work:
find an actual p satisfying the available constant-per-cell bias equation
(4), then certify lambda_*>0 or establish positivity using the full square
in (5). The sufficient spectral test can fail even for a target certified
by another argument; for example, dropping the square makes it too weak
for the original homogeneous p=0 gate. No new profile is claimed to have
passed (4)-(6) in this audit.

The present coupled simulation has ONE common field h, not independent h_j.
Neither a mathematical array of biases nor its electronics is already
implemented by that simulation. Actual segmented electrodes, field/fringing
couplings, and source energy/storage/losses are a missing interface.

4. AN EXACT DISCONNECTED-DOMAIN LIMIT

If the order-parameter domains are genuinely disconnected with separate
natural boundary conditions, piecewise constants on different components
are admissible. The original theorem and compensated charge-reset identity
then apply independently to each component. Known initial polarities can be
transferred to a requested componentwise pattern, with each successful
piezo bridge returning to the same q^2=B^2 state. This is a product of the
existing known-state transfers, conditional on physical isolation and
individual bias ports. It is not an arbitrary connected-bulk pattern theorem
or a new experimental capability. Isolation, supporting contacts, stray
fields and source costs have to be included in a device comparison.
