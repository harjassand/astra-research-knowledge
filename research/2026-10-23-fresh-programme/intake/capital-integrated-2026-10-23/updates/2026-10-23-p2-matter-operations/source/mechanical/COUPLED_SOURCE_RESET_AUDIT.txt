1. EXACT DISCRETE PASSIVE IDENTITY

Let D_h=mean(q)+Ch*h and I_h=dot(D_h). The code retains h as a state and adds
L*Ch*h^2/2 to stored energy. The field port is

  V_h,source=h+Rh*I_h,      P_h=L*V_h,source*I_h.

The piezo port of each cell is

  V_c,j=(z_j-theta*e_j)/Cp,
  V_z,source,j=V_c,j+Rz*zdot_j,
  P_z=ell*sum_j V_z,source,j*zdot_j.

Direct differentiation of the discrete material, gradient, elastic, piezo,
kinetic and field-capacitor energy gives

  Edot=P_h+P_z
       -zeta*dx*sum(qdot_i^2)
       -eta*dx*sum(edot_i^2)
       -Rz*ell*sum(zdot_j^2)
       -Rh*L*I_h^2.

All terms have the expected positive losses. The material gradient energy
uses the correct dx factors and natural zero-flux q endpoints. Mechanical
endpoint masses have the correct half-cell weights, and the bridge forces
are equal/opposite at each bridge's two nodes. A -h*q term should NOT be added
to this stored energy: the h coupling is supplied by the electrical work
port and D_h relation. Adding that Gibbs-potential term would double-count it.

At t>=T the implemented equations give

  Ch*hdot=-h/Rh-mean(qdot),     I_h=-h/Rh,
  Rz*zdot_j=-V_c,j.

Therefore both source voltages and powers vanish, and Edot equals minus the
four losses. In particular, h is not forcibly assigned zero at release.
Its remaining storage and the polarization-induced current are dissipated
by the actual terminal circuit. Piezo source work likewise remains constant
while its internal charge/voltage relaxes.

3. NORMALIZATION AND PRECISE RESET SCOPE

The integrated energies, work and heat are PER BAR CROSS-SECTIONAL AREA;
the synthetic calculation sets that area to one. L factors for the common
field port and ell factors for individual piezo bridges are consistent.
Divide by L for mean energy density, or multiply by a specified cross section
to obtain total energy after restoring physical units. The piezo Rz is the
cell-normalized resistance R_ohm*Vcell used in the theorem.

Ch and Rh in the field port are coefficients of h^2/2 in energy density and
I_h^2 in dissipative density. A physical electrode geometry must specify
their conversion to farads/ohms. For example, if q is polarization, h electric
field, electrode area Ae and separation d, then Ch is a permittivity and
Rh=R_ohm*Ae/d. They are not automatically the same normalization as the
piezo charge-density circuit. The present dimensionless model makes no
material or SI capacitance/resistance claim.

Successful final h approximately zero resets the background field-capacitor
storage, and the piezo state returns approximately to its initial value.
However, total generalized field-electrode charge D_h changes from -B to +B
with the intended material polarization. The successful saved run has
D_h,initial=-0.8660254038 and D_h,final=+0.8660255522. This is physically
consistent and the source work is counted, but a statement that EVERY
electrode/source charge returns to its initial state would be incorrect.
The precise claim is zero external source voltages with dynamically relaxed
internal fields and restored piezo actuation, while the useful material and
associated screening charge retain the transferred state.

During the driven stages the code prescribes hdot and piezo current, and
computes the source voltage needed against actual qdot and piezo voltage.
This is a valid ideal bidirectional work-port model. Driver bandwidth,
voltage limits, waveform generation, sensing/control overhead, energy-
recovery efficiency and generator storage are not thereby physically closed.
There is no free h-source inside the modeled boundary; there is also no
wall-plug cost claim justified outside that boundary.
