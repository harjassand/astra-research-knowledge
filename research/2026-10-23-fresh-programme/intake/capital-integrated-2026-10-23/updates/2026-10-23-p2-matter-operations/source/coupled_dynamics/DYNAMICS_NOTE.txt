COMPLETE FINITE-TIME PIEZOELECTRIC GATE CYCLE: SUCCESS, FAILURE, AND COMPARATOR
ASTRA-FRESH-20261023 / P2 / 23 October 2026 / private model result

MAIN RESULT

The compatible finite-pitch global-path theorem survives a full displacement,
charge and source-port implementation in one declared synthetic specimen.
It does not make the operation rate independent. With exactly the same
positive coefficients, a long cycle gives retained known-input transfer
-B -> +B, while a fast cycle ends in the unwanted central q=0 phase.
Both external source voltages are set to zero during the final settling
interval; neither internal field nor strain is silently held at its target.

This is a deterministic continuum-model simulation and proof audit. It is not
a material fit, physical experiment, acquired measurement, thermally activated
retention calculation, new-law claim or certified practical advantage.

1. MODEL AND ACTUAL CONTROL PORTS

Use the positive displacement/piezo energy and sextic intrinsic potential in
../mechanical/COMPATIBLE_PIEZO_GATE.txt, equations (1) and (14)-(15). Retain
q at segment centers, displacement and velocity at nodes, and one charge
coordinate per real piezo bridge. The finite differences are derivatives of
the same discrete energy; gradient and viscous terms use natural free ends.
Mass is rho*dx at interior nodes and half that at the two endpoints.

Add a background bias-field capacitance Ch per sample volume. Its stored
energy is L*Ch*h^2/2 and its charge coordinate per volume is

  D_h = mean(q)+Ch*h.

With a positive series resistance Rh, the actual bias source voltage in the
normalized conjugate coordinates is

  V_h = h+Rh*D_h_dot.

During the driven cycle, a voltage source supplies the value needed to
realize the specified internal h(t). This is an ideal commanded source with
explicit positive series loss; electronic feedback/driver inefficiency is
not inferred. Source work is L*V_h*D_h_dot. After the cycle the actual source
voltage is set to ZERO and the field evolves physically:

  Ch*h_dot = -h/Rh-mean(q_dot).                              (D1)

For a piezo bridge, Vc=(z-theta*e_cell)/Cp and the series relation is

  V_z=Vc+Rz*z_dot.

The controlled charge strokes supply prescribed current z_dot through this
same positive resistor. In the middle stage z=0 is held on an ideal isolated
charge port. A finite off-state PARALLEL leakage path is not included in this
particular simulation; its distinct constraints and matched noise are derived
in ../mechanical/LEAKAGE_NOISE_WINDOW.txt. At final release the actual piezo
source is shorted, V_z=0, so z_dot=-Vc/Rz. There is no final charge teleport.

The complete discrete energy balance is

  E_dot = L V_h D_h_dot + sum_j ell V_z,j z_dot,j
          -zeta dx sum q_dot^2 -eta dx sum strain_dot^2
          -Rz ell sum z_dot^2 -Rh L D_h_dot^2.                (D2)

E includes intrinsic phase, gradient, material elasticity, shunt elasticity,
piezo capacitance, mechanical kinetic and bias capacitance energy. During
the final interval both source-work terms are zero. Any residual stored or
kinetic energy is included in the final reported E rather than discarded.
An actual generator must accept returned energy for the negative electrical
work to be recovered. Switch, wiring, measurement and source inefficiencies
outside these ports remain to be acquired for a wall-energy claim.

3. CLOSED-ACTUATOR KNOWN-INPUT TRANSFER

Start at q=-B, uniform relaxed short strain, z=zB, zero velocities and h=0.
Each ramp uses S(s)=10s^3-15s^4+6s^5, with zero endpoint velocities.

First quarter of duration T: withdraw charge z=zB[1-S(s)] and command
  h=-U B^3 S(s).
Middle half: hold z=0, set reference p=-B+2B S(s), and command
  h=f0'(p)+U p^3.
Last quarter: return z=zB S(s) and command
  h=U B^3[1-S(s)].
There is no friction-cancelling force feedforward. Material and elastic lag
are resolved. At t=T both actual sources are passively shorted for an
additional 100 model-time units, using (D1) and the physical piezo RC equation.

This operates on a known initial polar state. It is not a NOT gate certified
on an unknown input, nor erasure with omitted information-reset cost. For the
nominal quasistatic endpoints q^2 is identical, allowing all piezo strains
and charges to return while polarization changes sign.

for these examples, not proof of all protocol durations or all perturbations.
The exact stored-energy identity is independently checked by a directional
derivative, rather than only by comparing two reused work formulas.

Deterministic persistence is not a finite-temperature lifetime. The restored
energy has 0,+B,-B coexistence, and free-end domain-wall escape can have a
barrier independent of total bar length. Adding noise requires the
fluctuation-dissipation partners of the actual dissipative elements and a
defined physical coarse-graining volume. No arbitrary noise amplitude is
introduced merely to display a favorable retention curve.

5. ONE SAME-SPECIMEN DIRECT-FIELD COMPARATOR

direct_field_comparator.py leaves all piezo ports passively shorted and uses
one smooth direct bias pulse H*sin(pi*t/T)^2, with H=1.5 times the negative-
well homogeneous composite spinodal. Here H=.0672691126. All material,
mechanical, electrical loss, mesh and terminal source conditions are identical.
This pulse family is NOT optimized, so its cost is not a lower bound for
direct writing. The gated protocol also uses a much larger peak conjugate
field; equal duration is not an equal peak-voltage or driver-resource budget.

T=2 does not switch and leaves the original negative state. At T=20 and
100 it transfers to +B, with calculated total heat respectively .134093 and
.273610. Thus at T=20 the simple direct pulse beats the gated cycle on this
cost; at T=100 the gated cycle costs less than this particular pulse. These
crossing results defeat a blanket claim of superiority. Hysteretic spatial
jumps and inertial excursions make the chosen direct-pulse cost nonmonotone
in duration. They do not prove the optimum direct-write cost is nonmonotone.

The point of this comparison is falsification: a rigorous barrier-control
path is not automatically the best useful operation at a finite time and
resource boundary. Optimizing this small conditional device further is not
the current programme priority without a matched material tuple.

6. FAILED IMPLEMENTATION ASSUMPTION RETAINED

Its energy accounting included the required active source work, but calling
that stage a zero-control hold was incorrect: exact h=0 during changing q
requires V_h=Rh*D_h_dot. The source, outputs and trajectories are preserved
under superseded_active_zero_field_hold/ with WHY_SUPERSEDED.txt.
The governing version adds h as a physical state and sets the actual source
voltage to zero, as in (D1). Both driven success/failure conclusions survive.

its step-size factors at terminal zero-current states. Replacing it with the
