1. Literal observation model

There is a fixed, known attenuation body, compared with the same body
after removing a defect D of volume V. The solid has known homogeneous
attenuation coefficient mu>0; the void attenuation is zero. The body is
UNLOADED during the scan. No unknown bonding law or invented hidden
material variable distinguishes these two attenuation models: their
only difference is the removed region D with V>0.

At each incident photon, the experiment may adaptively select any
parallel-beam direction and any normalized nonnegative transverse
position density k(u) obeying sup k<=M. M is an explicit finite focusing/
resolution promise, independent of the unknown defect aperture. Give
the observer MORE information than an ordinary aggregate CT bin: the
exact incident coordinate u as well as the ideal transmitted/not-
transmitted bit. Thus detector pixel localization is not silently
discarded. All controls, past data and independent algorithm randomness
are available; arbitrary nonlinear reconstruction/computation is allowed.

For a ray of solid path length L_0(u), write d(u) for its chord length
through D. The conditional transmission probabilities are

 p_0(u)=exp[-mu L_0(u)],
 p_D(u)=exp[-mu (L_0(u)-d(u))].                         (1)

This is an incoherent, absorption-only Beer--Lambert channel, with the
displayed conditional photon law; unconditional IID samples are not
assumed for an adaptive protocol. Phase, scattering, mechanically loaded
images, acoustic signals and compliance measurements are different
interfaces. A scanner covered by the theorem must factor through these
ideal ray/transmission data; it cannot have extra unmodeled observables.

An ideal point ray is NOT an admitted k: its transverse distribution is
singular, not bounded by M. Nor is arbitrarily narrow/grazing focusing
available at unchanged M. This is load-bearing; a known slit-plane ray
can retain a long chord even as aperture tends to zero.

2. One-photon total variation, including its position

The chosen ray distribution is the same under both hypotheses at a
matched history. Since exp(-x) is 1-Lipschitz on x>=0,

 |p_D(u)-p_0(u)|<=mu d(u).

Fubini's projection identity gives int d(u)du=V for EVERY chosen parallel
direction. Hence the joint law of (u, transmission) obeys

 TV(P_D,P_0)=int k(u)|p_D(u)-p_0(u)|du
             <=mu M V.                                (2)

No lower bound on transmission probability or upper bound away from one
is needed. Near-zero/near-one probabilities and freely revealed incident
positions are included. Downstream imaging and inference cannot increase
this TV. Independent detector noise or quantization cannot help either.

A useful more general form replaces the transverse beam bound by an
expected-track-length bound: if the ray distribution has occupation
density g(x), meaning E[length(ray intersect A)]=int_A g(x)dx, and
sup g<=M, the same inequality follows. Cone/geometric magnification must
actually satisfy such a bound in the critical volume before using this
extension; it is not supplied merely by calling a scanner finite.

3. Fully adaptive and stopped acquisition

For a fixed M, maximally couple the conditional one-photon laws at each
matched history. Controls and stop decisions agree until the first
coupling mismatch. If N_0 is the total incident-photon count under the
intact hypothesis, summing conditional mismatch probabilities over the
calls made by that run gives

 TV(terminal transcript_0, terminal transcript_D)
      <=mu M V E_0[N_0].                               (3)

This charges every incident photon in the acquisition protocol, not
only photons retained after a favorable postselection. N_0 may be
adaptively stopped. Abstention/nontermination counts as failure in the
success requirement; infinite expected photon count already meets the
lower bound.

Any detector with error <=1/3 under both hypotheses requires terminal
TV>=1/3. Consequently

 E_0[N_0]>=1/(3 mu M V).                                (4)

More generally, if the selected focusing bounds and photon attenuation
coefficients vary predictably with j, the same proof gives

 E_0[sum_{j<=N_0} mu_j M_j] >=1/(3V).                   (5)

when each ray law satisfies its stated bound. Thus resolution/focusing
gain cannot be silently made free. Equation (5) is an optical-exposure
resource inequality, NOT a universal physical work or dose formula.
With fixed photon energy at least E_min, emitted/incident energy is at
least E_min E_0[N_0]. An absorbed-dose or wall-time consequence needs an
additional absorption or source-rate bound; neither is assumed here.

It would be incorrect to upgrade (4) unconditionally to V^-2. An
aggregate-beam Bernoulli channel with survival probabilities bounded in
[xi,1-xi] has per-read KL=O(V^2), but coordinate-resolved photons can have
KL of order V. The robust theorem deliberately retains their extra data
and asserts only (4).

4. Fixed-extent defect family and mechanical relevance

For an elementary model, let D_b be a through-thickness elliptical slit
with in-plane semi-axes a,b and thickness t. Fix a,t>0 and let b>0 tend
to zero. Then

 V_b=pi a b t,
 E_0[N_0]>=1/(3 mu M pi a b t).                         (6)

In particular, ONE fixed rule required to have error <=1/3 on intact
material and EVERY b>0 must have infinite E_0[N_0]. The intact hypothesis
and algorithm are the same while the right side diverges. A rule given a
positive minimum-aperture promise is a different, restricted problem.

The intact-versus-slit geometric distinction stays fixed in extent 2a,
even though its unloaded absorption signature vanishes. The theorem
already allows the experimenter to know the candidate slit's position,
orientation and a: uncertainty in those quantities is not needed.

Mechanical relevance is separate from the information proof. In the
classical isotropic, infinite plane-stress plate under remote tension
sigma perpendicular to the ellipse's major axis, Inglis gives

 sigma_tip=sigma(1+2a/b).

Its divergence is NOT an actual unbounded-stress or material-failure
claim. The more relevant sharp-crack limit, a central crack of length
2a, has the finite mode-I driving force

 K_I=sigma sqrt(pi a),
 Delta K_I=Delta sigma sqrt(pi a).                      (7)

Thus a vanishing unloaded aperture need not mean vanishing service crack
driving force in that declared continuum model. This motivates a
fixed-extent thin-slit exclusion requirement; it is not a theorem that
every finite-b ellipse has a specified fatigue life or failure probability.
The rigorous measurement theorem concerns distinguishing positive-volume
D_b from intact material. It does not replace the mechanical modeling
needed to assign a finite-b defect an unacceptable service label.

For a finite component, the appropriate geometry factor, elastic regime,
residual stress, closure, plastic/process-zone scale, material crack-growth
data and environmental/load-ratio conditions must be supplied or acquired.
No unbounded subatomic continuum family is asserted to be physically
realizable. A physical minimum opening/shape or another validated admission
bound could turn (6) into a finite resource requirement. If no such lower
bound is admitted, no UNIFORM finite photon budget can exclude this entire
continuum defect family at the stated error.

No exact zero-width attenuation counterexample with a changed bonding
law is smuggled into the geometric theorem: every D_b above has b>0.
Kissing/weak bonds would require a separate material-interface model.

5. What a qualification claim must add

This is a direct boundary for CT-only acceptance, including AI or any
other reconstruction applied to the same photon data. A list of resolved
pores may give a valuable ranking, while it cannot by itself rule out all
thin crack-like defects in an admitted class with no opening/resolution
margin. More calibration of visible pores does not establish that margin.

An additional operation may help: loaded CT, calibrated compliance/strain,
or acoustic/phase-sensitive inspection can couple to crack surfaces and
elastic response rather than only unloaded missing volume. These are
conventional candidate escapes with different signals and costs. Loading
does NOT remove mass; any image difference must account for displaced
material, outer-shape changes and registration. Safe proof loading needs
independent geometry/material/yield/crack-growth margins and cannot be
declared free or nondamaging without them. No such repair is established
by the present bound.
