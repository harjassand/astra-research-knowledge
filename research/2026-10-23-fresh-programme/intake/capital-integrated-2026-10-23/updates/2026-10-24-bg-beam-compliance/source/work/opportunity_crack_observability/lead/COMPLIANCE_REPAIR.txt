AN APERTURE-INDEPENDENT COMPLIANCE WITNESS, WITH ITS ACQUISITION GATES
2026-10-24. Constructive check of a compatible conventional added operation.
The frozen finite-beam attenuation obstruction is unchanged.

Decision target
Can a compatible measurement close the SPECIFIC thin-aperture blind spot,
without first imaging its microscopic opening? Within the following
declared linear-elastic slit class, yes: a force/displacement compliance
measurement has a positive gap independent of aperture. This is classical
elasticity/fracture-based inspection, not a new NDT method or a general
fatigue qualification theorem. It does not acquire its own material law,
safe probe authority or pristine baseline from the CT data.

1. Finite-body variational operation

Let Omega be a known elastic body with fixed homogeneous outer Dirichlet
supports, no initial/residual stress, and a fixed outer load pattern.
For a traction-free defect D,
let V_D be its admissible displacement space on Omega\D. Write

 a_D(u,v)=int_(Omega\D) epsilon(u):C(x):epsilon(v) dx,

with a fixed positive elastic tensor, and ell(v) for the outer-boundary
work functional of one unit of force. Assume the supported problem is
well posed and the defect does not intersect/change the prescribed
outer load/support boundaries. Unit-force compliance is

 C_D=ell(u_D),     a_D(u_D,v)=ell(v),
 C_D=sup_(v in V_D) [2ell(v)-a_D(v,v)].                  (1)

At force F the conjugate displacement is q=F C_D, and the minimum
potential energy is -F^2 C_D/2.

Let Gamma_a be an interior straight slit of length 2a. The admissible
space V_Gamma allows displacement jumps across its traction-free faces.
If a positive-aperture void D contains Gamma_a, restriction maps every
v in V_Gamma to an admissible field on Omega\D. The outer boundary
work is unchanged and its elastic energy cannot increase. Equation (1)
therefore proves EXACTLY

 C_D>=C_Gamma_a>=C_empty.                               (2)

The first inequality is not an assumed continuity of a sharp-crack
limit. It holds for every admitted positive-aperture ellipse containing
the slit. The removed line is a geometric crack with a different
admissible displacement space; it is not treated as an ordinary
measure-zero deletion that leaves H1(Omega) unchanged.

2. A quantitative finite-body gap for a declared crack family

Now restrict to a homogeneous isotropic linear-elastic body and a specified
central through-thickness mode-I crack family of thickness t, 0<=a<=a_max,
under the SAME spatial load pattern.
Let A be its nominal load area. Require a certified finite-body geometry
factor with

 K_I(a;F)=F Y(a) sqrt(pi a)/A,
 0<Y_min<=Y(a)<=Y_max<infinity.                         (3)

These are substantive forward-mechanics bounds over the declared family,
not a universal statement for arbitrary cracks, orientations or parts.
Let the relevant plane-stress/plane-strain modulus E' obey E'<=E_max.

For total crack length 2a, increasing a creates crack area 2t da. Irwin's
energy-release relation and force-controlled potential energy give

 G=K_I^2/E'=F^2 C_Gamma'(a)/(4t).

Thus, where the standard elastic crack family is differentiable,

 C_Gamma'(a)=4pi t a Y(a)^2/(E' A^2),
 C_Gamma_a-C_empty
     >=2pi t Y_min^2 a^2/(E_max A^2).                  (4)

The same inequality follows by integrating the energy-release identity
in its absolutely continuous form. Equation (2) transfers this bound to
the whole positive-aperture void family D containing Gamma_a. No minimum
opening b is used.

Integrating from zero is a formal scale-free LEFM idealization. If the
validated crack law and Y_min bound start only at a positive a_cut,
integrate from a_cut and use C_Gamma_a_cut>=C_empty instead. For a>a_cut,
the rigorous available gap is then

 C_D-C_empty>=2pi t Y_min^2 (a^2-a_cut^2)/(E_max A^2).   (4a)

This does not invoke a crack law below its validated process/length
scale. The lower geometry-factor bound must hold over the ENTIRE actual
integration interval, not just at its endpoint.

A service crack-driving-force criterion can choose the excluded extent.
If the service force amplitude is F_s>0 and the modeled condition is
K_I(a;F_s)>=K_star, then (3) implies

 a>=a_star=(A K_star/(F_s Y_max))^2/pi.

For 0<a_star<=a_max, every defect in this declared exclusion class has

 C_D>=C_empty+DeltaC_star,
 DeltaC_star=2pi t Y_min^2 a_star^2/(E_max A^2)>0.       (5)

When using (4a), require a_star>a_cut and replace a_star^2 in (5) by
a_star^2-a_cut^2. Crack risks outside that validated length range need
another admission bound; they are not certified by this calculation.

This excludes a specified embedded-crack footprint; K_star is a supplied
service criterion, not a fatigue life inferred from CT. Finite-b notch
growth, other crack locations/orientations, interacting defects, residual
stress or other failure modes are not automatically represented by (3).

3. Actual finite measurement and an acceptance rule

Apply a tensile force with calibrated nominal value F_p>0. Suppose its
actual value lies in [F_p-eta_F,F_p+eta_F], with F_p>eta_F, and the
conjugate displacement q is measured as qhat with |qhat-q|<=eta_q.
Then the acquired interval

 L_C=max{0,(qhat-eta_q)/(F_p+eta_F)},
 U_C=(qhat+eta_q)/(F_p-eta_F)                            (6)

covers C_D. Error enclosures must come from actual force/displacement
calibration, including fixture compliance and drift; repeated readings
alone do not bound systematic bias. If the bounds are statistical,
use their simultaneous coverage probability and charge every read.
Inconsistent/empty intervals give abstention, not a pass.
The displacement bound must also cover dynamic settling, creep and other
departures from the static relation at the chosen FINITE hold time. A
static elasticity model alone does not supply an acquired settling-time
certificate; unbounded waiting is not a free measurement operation.

Separately acquire or conservatively compute a pristine-baseline interval
[L_0,U_0] covering C_empty for this body/material/support/load pattern.
The sound exclusion rule is

       U_C < L_0+DeltaC_star.                           (7)

On simultaneous coverage, (7) rules out EVERY D in (5), regardless of
its unloaded aperture. It does not require locating or reconstructing
the defect. Equality or a wider interval gives abstention, not a pass.
For nonvacuous acceptance of intact parts, the combined upper measurement
excess plus baseline width must be smaller than DeltaC_star. Unknown
modulus, material anisotropy, uncertain supports or unmodeled loads can
swamp this margin; the CT scan has not calibrated them away.

One explicit worst-case feasibility check is available. Let B=U_0-L_0
and kappa=DeltaC_star-B>0. On intact material, (6) gives

 U_C-C_empty <=(2eta_F U_0+2eta_q)/(F_p-eta_F).

Thus this rule accepts every covered intact measurement if

 (2eta_F U_0+2eta_q)/(F_p-eta_F)<kappa.                 (8)

This is a sufficient instrument-margin condition, not an assumption that
arbitrarily precise metrology is obtainable. It exposes the conflict
between low probe force and finite displacement accuracy.

4. Safe authority and complete costs

Equations (1)-(7) are a linear-elastic information operation. To perform
it on an actual component, require an independently validated reversible
probe envelope with actual force bounded by F_safe over the entire
admitted class; the calibration interval must satisfy
0<F_p-eta_F<=F_p+eta_F<=F_safe. A sharp-crack
K bound by itself does NOT certify absence of local yielding, notch
damage, closure/contact effects, crack growth or cycling damage in every
positive-b void. Safe loading is therefore an added material/fixture
premise, not a consequence of the desired acceptance.

For a calibrated force ramp in the ideal elastic model, peak stored
energy is F^2 C_D/2. A physical actuator must supply and potentially
recover it within its force/stroke limits; hysteretic losses, sensor work,
fixture energy and reset/unload time are charged separately. The present
calculation makes no zero-cost or zero-damage hardware claim. No proof
load is being performed or authorized.

The important acquisition change is literal: unloaded attenuation couples
to a vanishing removed volume, whereas a mechanical compliance port
couples to the admissible displacement jump and its elastic energy. The
monotonicity witness removes the aperture floor from this geometrically
restricted exclusion problem. It does NOT remove the need for (3), a
covered pristine baseline, adequate signal precision, safe probe authority
and an independently justified service criterion.

Scientific decision
The aperture-only observability obstruction has a compatible conventional
repair in the stated class. This is not evidence for a new inspection
technology or broad component qualification. If those class, baseline and
safe-authority inputs are unacquired, the physical gate remains open; a
fitted pore score or the mere phrase 'proof load' cannot supply them.
No favorable simulation is needed to establish the conditional mechanism,
and none would validate those missing physical inputs.
