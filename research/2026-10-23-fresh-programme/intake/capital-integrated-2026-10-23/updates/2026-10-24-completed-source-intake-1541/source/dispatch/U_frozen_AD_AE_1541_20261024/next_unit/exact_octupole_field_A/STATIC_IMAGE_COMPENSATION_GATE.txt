1. Literal requirement, not a relabeling of image forces
--------------------------------------------------------
For particle charge q_p=-e and physical Cartesian common mass m, the LC
model requires on the occupied open plane aperture
  V_conf+U_im=V_h=.5m(nu1^2 X1^2+nu2^2 X2^2), nu1>nu2>0,
with kinetic P^2/(2m). This makes its specified non-Gaussian product-action
stock exactly stationary under the BARE Hb, not under the coupled LC gain
dynamics. This note supplies one concrete conditional static-source model
and one obstruction to a conventional bounded-voltage realization.

2. Bounded boundary voltages cannot give EXACT spherical compensation
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Take a conducting spherical cavity of radius a. Its grounded point-charge
image energy, including the essential polarization factor1/2, is
  U_im(X)=-K/(a^2-|X|^2), K=e^2 a/(8pi epsilon0).
If independent electrostatic confinement comes ONLY from a bounded harmonic
potential phi_ext throughout the empty cavity, exact compensation on any
nonempty open z=0 patch requires
  q_p phi_ext(x,y,0)=V_h(x,y)+K/(a^2-x^2-y^2)+constant.
Both sides are real analytic on the connected disk x^2+y^2<a^2, so their
identity on an open patch extends to that entire disk. The right side
diverges at its circular boundary. A harmonic extension with bounded finite
Dirichlet wall voltages is bounded by the maximum principle: contradiction.

This uses the WHOLE-cavity, source-free, bounded-voltage hypotheses, not
3D Earnshaw as a 2D no-go. A static electrostatic saddle can have positive
x,y curvature and an exact reflection-invariant z=0 plane. An analytic
image-compensation trace also has a LOCAL harmonic Cauchy extension. The
obstruction is its bounded continuation to this sphere's wall. Internal
fixed charges or other mechanical forces change that source hypothesis.

3. Explicit finite fixed-charge confinement model, outside the aperture
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Let r=|X| and P_l be Legendre polynomials. The energy-valued harmonic series
  Psi(X)=.5m[nu1^2 x^2+nu2^2 y^2-(nu1^2+nu2^2)z^2]
       +(K/a^2)sum_(n>=0) (r/a)^(2n) P_(2n)(z/r)/P_(2n)(0)
converges smoothly on every compact ball r<a. Each term is a solid zonal
harmonic; P_(2n)(0)=(-1)^n binom(2n,n)/4^n is nonzero and its reciprocal
grows only O(sqrt(n)). The expression at r=0 has its regular polynomial
meaning. On z=0,
  Psi(x,y,0)=V_h(x,y)+K/(a^2-x^2-y^2).

Choose fixed R<R1<R2<a containing the entire occupied trajectory aperture,
and a smooth radial cutoff chi equal1 for r<=R1 and0 for r>=R2. Prescribe
  phi_conf=chi Psi/q_p,
  rho_conf=-epsilon0 Delta(phi_conf).
The charge density is smooth, finite, and supported only in R1<r<R2; its
potential solves the spherical Dirichlet problem with zero wall data, hence
is EXACTLY phi_conf by uniqueness. It has total charge0. On r<=R,z=0,
  q_p phi_conf+U_im=V_h.
No velocity-dependent force or kinetic metric is introduced. Source energy
  .5 integral rho_conf phi_conf
     =epsilon0/2 integral |grad phi_conf|^2
is finite, fixed, and explicitly supplied. This is not the bounded pure-wall
construction ruled out above: its interior source shell breaks harmonicity
outside the aperture.

The source distribution must be rigid, fixed and NONPOLARIZABLE. Under that
model the grounded Green function, pickup weighting w and capacitance C
are unchanged. The fixed-source/particle cross energy is the FULL
q_p phi_conf, not half of it. Its own self/background energy is constant.
Responsive dielectric or conducting supports would change the image Green
function and w and must be recomputed; no such material is acquired here.

4. No hidden LC offset; exact plane is not robust 3D trapping
-------------------------------------------------------------
For w=kappa(xy^3-x^3y), the background-induced circuit offset is
  Q_bg=integral rho_conf w=0.
Parity proves this: chi,Psi,rho_conf are even in x and y separately, while
w is odd in each. More generally, compact support of phi_conf and harmonic
w give integral rho_conf w=-epsilon0 integral phi_conf Delta w=0, with no
boundary term. Thus the LC square (Q-e w)^2/(2C) gains no hidden DC-biased
seed from this background, subject to the LC source's existing mode model.

The total potential is even in z and the exact planar initial state stays
planar. It is NOT a robust 3D trap. At the origin its transverse curvature is
  partial_z^2(Psi+U_im)=-m(nu1^2+nu2^2)-6K/a^4<0.
Exact planar initialization or an independently paid plane constraint is
therefore essential. No cold z mode or free mechanical support is inserted.

5. Approximate bounded electrodes are not blocked by the exact obstruction
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Truncate the series for Psi at finite n=N. It is a harmonic polynomial with
finite wall voltages. On z=0,rho<=R<a, the compensation tail is exactly
  (K/a^2) [(rho^2/a^2)^(N+1)/(1-rho^2/a^2)].
For fixed k its PLANAR C^k error is O(N^k(R/a)^(2N)); constants depend on
R,a,k. The full3D C^k error can safely include an extra sqrt(N). Boundary
amplitude is finite for each N but its ceiling is not uniform as N grows.
Thus finite arbitrary accuracy on a smaller disk is compatible with growing
resources. This sequence supplies no fixed-voltage-cap accuracy theorem.

Such a truncation no longer makes the stipulated bare stock exactly
stationary. Its finite-time LC gain would need a perturbation budget smaller
than the strict, possibly tiny, gain margin. The exact identity obstruction
alone is not a robust finite-time gain obstruction. Neither this observation
nor the fixed-charge construction acquires finite segmented electrodes or
their capacitance matrix.

6. RF, magnetic and mechanical shortcuts do not preserve the same premise
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Martinetz--Hornberger--Stickler (2021), arXiv:2105.00264v2, keeps V_tr general
in Section2. Its Paul-trap Eq41 is driven; Eq53 is an effective high-frequency
pseudopotential. Section6 Eqs60--65 retain physical-momentum micromotion.
They do not establish the specified exact stationary law in physical X,P.

Nor does adding uniform perpendicular B preserve that non-Gaussian product
law. With mechanical momenta,
  dot I1=eB P1P2/(m^2 nu1), dot I2=-eB P1P2/(m^2 nu2)
up to charge/orientation sign. For f=h_*(I1)h_*(I2), the resulting phase-space
derivative is generically nonzero. Diagonalizing magnetic normal modes
changes the physical-coordinate observable w and cannot reuse its coefficients.

A mathematical mechanical potential V_h-U_im is sufficient by declaration.
A gravity surface z=h(x,y) is not its flat-mass realization: kinetic metric
is delta_ij+partial_i h partial_j h. Cams/followers add inertia/constraints;
ordinary Hooke springs supply only V_h. Constant-image ideal plane geometries
are a special escape, but finite edges and the same octupole pickup mode must
be checked together. No ordinary finite static device closes this gate here.
