1. Dynamics and two different outputs
-------------------------------------
 qdot=omega p, pdot=-omega q-g w-gamma p, gamma>=0,
 H=Hb+omega(q^2+p^2)/2+g q w+g^2 w^2/(2omega).
Let m(g,gamma;t)=(E q,E p), m0=(0,P), Jm=P^2/2>0, and
Ecoh=omega|m|^2/2. The initial independent centered radial seed noise has
Jv=E|delta(q,p)|^2/2>0; its energy is not coherent output. All E are expectations.
Observe at the FIXED finite undamped strobe T=2pi N/omega, N>=1.
Absolute charging means Ecoh(g,gamma;T)-omega Jm>0.
An increment over Ecoh(0,gamma;T) can be positive while absolute charging fails.

Put A_gamma=[[0,omega],[-omega,-gamma]], m_gamma(t)=exp(A_gamma t)m0.
At g=0, exactly,
 Ecoh(0,gamma;T)-omega Jm=-gamma omega integral_0^T p_gamma(t)^2 dt.
For every fixed gamma>0 and T>0 this is strictly negative, since p_gamma(0)=P.
Finite-time continuous dependence therefore excludes absolute charging for all
sufficiently small |g| at that fixed gamma,T. Zero-mean additive load noise would
not change this uncoupled coherent-mean deficit; no noisy-aperture claim follows.

2. Joint loss scale and uniform finite-time remainder
----------------------------------------------------
Write the frozen lossless coefficient Gc(T)=ac T^2+dc(T), |dc|<=Kc.
The compact-stock ODE is smooth in (g,gamma). At g=0, E w_free=0, so the order-g
mean response vanishes for EVERY gamma. With the frozen susceptibility chi,
 wbar_1(t)=integral_0^t q_gamma(s)chi(t-s)ds,
 m2_gamma(T)=-integral_0^T exp(A_gamma(T-t))(0,wbar_1(t))dt.
Hence the increment over the freely damped coherent baseline is
 g^2 Gc_gamma(T)+O_T(|g|^3), Gc_gamma=omega m_gamma(T).m2_gamma(T).
This coefficient satisfies Gc_gamma=Gc+O_T(gamma); its sign at arbitrary fixed
gamma is not inferred just from the lossless result. At the selected strobe,
 integral_0^T p_gamma(t)^2dt=Jm T+O_T(gamma), and consequently
 Delta Ecoh=g^2 Gc(T)-gamma omega Jm T
            +O_T(|g|^3+g^2 gamma+gamma^2).                    (1)
The inductive coherent component omega(E p)^2/2 has the same leading expansion.
Uniformity follows from common compact trajectories at fixed T, then bounded
parameter variational ODEs through order3; take gamma near0, not T~1/g.

For gamma=lambda g^2, 0<=lambda<=Lambda<infinity, (1) has a uniform O_T,Lambda
(|g|^3) remainder; lambda has units gamma/g^2, not an implicit dimensionless value.
If Gc(T)>0, the strict leading threshold is
 lambda_crit=Gc(T)/(omega Jm T).
lambda<lambda_crit gives absolute gain for sufficiently small |g|; lambda above
it gives a deficit. Equality is undecided by this expansion. A convergent ratio
gamma/g^2 uses this same test. If gamma->0 but gamma/g^2->infinity, loss dominates
at this fixed T. Thus an O(g^2) loss scale, with an adequate coefficient, is the
correct weak-coupling absolute-gain condition here. It is not needed merely to
define a possibly positive increment above a freely decaying baseline.

3. Strong stock-to-load criterion and full dissipative ledger
-------------------------------------------------------------
Use the frozen constants c0,Ki,Kv,af and fluctuation coefficient Gf(T).
At gamma=lambda g^2 and the same fixed strobe,
 Delta Efluc=g^2[Gf(T)-lambda omega Jv T]+O_T,Lambda(|g|^3),
 Delta Eint=g^2 bint(T)+O_T,Lambda(|g|^3), |bint|<=Ki.
For any fixed finite Lambda, choose a finite integer N FIRST so that
 ac T^2-Kc-Lambda omega Jm T>2c0+Ki,
 af T^2-Kv-Lambda omega Jv T>0.                              (2)
Then choose small nonzero g. Uniformly for 0<=gamma/g^2<=Lambda, coherent gain
exceeds |Eint(0)|+|Eint(T)|, fluctuation gain is positive, and particles lose energy.
Indeed the EXACT ledger is
 Delta Eb+Delta EL+Delta Eint=-Ediss,
 Ediss=gamma omega integral_0^T E p(t)^2dt>=0.                (3)
Here Ediss=g^2 lambda omega(Jm+Jv)T+O_T,Lambda(|g|^3).
The coherent and seed-noise loss terms cancel this term in (3), so the order-g^2
particle-energy coefficient equals the lossless coefficient. Dissipated energy
is a loss, not usable delivered work; seed/stock preparation and reset remain paid.

4. Fixed physical series resistance blocks the large-C family
--------------------------------------------------------------
For the SAME series circuit, gamma=R/L (Martinetz et al., Sec3.1 Eq12),
 L=1/(C omega^2), g^2=e^2 omega/C, so gamma/g^2=R C^2 omega/e^2.
Thus fixed e,omega and gamma=O(g^2) as C->infinity require R=O(C^-2).
Fixed positive R instead has gamma->infinity and g->0. This case does not rely
on the small-gamma Taylor expansion: Sym(A_gamma)=diag(0,-gamma), so
 |m(g,gamma;T)|<=|exp(A_gamma T)(0,P)|+|g|T||w||infinity.
For fixed T>0 the first term tends0 as gamma->infinity (the overdamped roots are
-omega^2/gamma+O(gamma^-3) and -gamma+O(gamma^-1), with initial q=0).
Hence coherent endpoint energy tends0, below the supplied seed omega Jm.
The seed current also grows as sqrt(C); this is not a free low-loss hardware knob.
