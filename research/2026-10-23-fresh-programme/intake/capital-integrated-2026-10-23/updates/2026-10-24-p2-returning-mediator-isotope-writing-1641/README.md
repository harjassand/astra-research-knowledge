## Source-reported construction

The producer redirects the main effort from a graphene isotope-collector route to post-assembly control of local isotope arrangement using a returning silicon mediator. Under the stipulated nearest-neighbour Si–C direct-exchange model, two adjacent hexagons sharing an edge define two closed routes. The source reports that the sequence \(A,B,A^{-1},B^{-1}\), comprising 24 exchanges, returns silicon to its initial site and cycles three selected carbon labels while leaving the other sites fixed.

The source further reports that the two hexagon generators act on nine host sites and generate \(A_9\), with 181,440 reachable label permutations in a finite enumeration. For two isotope species, relabelling identical-isotope atoms removes the odd-permutation obstruction at the level of mass patterns, so the source concludes that every nine-site arrangement with a fixed isotope count is reachable. These are results of the stated direct-swap model; this intake did not independently prove or enumerate them. The source itself notes that silicon positioning, repeated exchanges, and closed hexagon routes have prior demonstrations, and it does not claim new permutation mathematics.

The construction has explicit information limits. A blind fixed permutation leaves an independent, identically distributed isotope field unchanged. Useful writing therefore needs a known initial pattern or information-dependent control. The proposed reversible kinetic read uses isotope-dependent exchange rates, but the isotope contrast is a model output, not an acquired measurement. The cited isotope-imaging region averages roughly four carbon atoms, so it does not supply the assumed free single-site identity map. The short-path observation lemmas apply to true swaps on a fixed honeycomb topology; they do not cover changing bonding topology, replacement, image misclassification, or the real raster-scan process.

## Conditional pilot and its limits

The producer's reduced collision model combines relativistic energy transfer, thermal nuclear velocities, and published effective exchange and knockout thresholds. It adds a local harmonic surrogate, an assumed 50:50 independent isotope inventory, and an approximate neighbour-to-target beam intensity ratio of 0.06. It was calibrated to a reported light-carbon hopping rate at 55 keV. The model outputs an isotope-rate contrast of about 29, but its 60 keV cross-check did not reproduce the nominal reported manipulation rate.

In the source-reported read–prepare–write–verify pilot, 813 of 2,000 attempted cells completed a correct three-site edit and verification. Successful model runs averaged about 98 exchanges and 11 hours of modelled irradiation, before full imaging overhead. These are conditional simulation results, not experimental yield, manufacturing throughput, or an acquired physical operation. The source also gives an illustrative per-operation error budget under independent errors; it is not a measured error rate or a universal requirement.
