PAIR-STATISTIC ACQUISITION CAN BE FAST WHILE FULL STATE MIXING IS SLOW
Lead source-cost construction, 2026-10-24. No algorithm priority or
hardware validation claim. This supplies a particular finite physical-
time model, rather than rename IID snapshot access as a free resource.

Specified kinetic subclass
The unknown tree in UNKNOWN_TREE_ACQUISITION is now promised to be a
star, with unknown centre c and n-1 unknown edge parameters rho_i,
0<rho_min<=|rho_i|<=rho_max<1. Write J_i=atanh(rho_i), beta=1.
The actual continuous-time source is ZERO-FIELD heat-bath Glauber:
each spin has an independent rate-one Poisson clock; at an attempt it
is resampled from its Gibbs conditional distribution. These local clock
rates, kinetic form, unchanged graph and lack of external field are
calibrated/model premises. Static snapshots alone do not establish them.

All spins can be observed in a synchronous nondisturbing snapshot at
charged readout cost. One source is run continuously from an arbitrary
initial configuration/distribution. There is no per-observation physical
reset or stationary global initial-law oracle.

1. Acquire only the needed quotient
In coordinates Y_i=X_c X_i, the stationary law is the PRODUCT measure

 nu(y)=product_{i!=c}(1+rho_i y_i)/2.                    (1)

A leaf heat-bath attempt refreshes Y_i independently from this marginal,
leaving other Y values unchanged. At a centre attempt all Y signs flip,
with rate

 r(y)=1/[1+exp(2 sum_i J_i y_i)].                       (2)

Both rates depend only on y, so the observable quotient is itself a
Markov chain. Since nu(y)/nu(-y)=exp(2 sum_i J_i y_i),

 nu(y) r(y)=nu(-y) r(-y).                              (3)

The centre transition is nu-reversible. The independent rate-one leaf
refresh generator has L2(nu) gap exactly one. Adding the nonnegative
Dirichlet form of (2) cannot reduce the gap, so

             lambda_quotient >= 1.                    (4)

The learner need NOT know c to use this fact. Its pair products are
invariant under a global spin flip. They are functions of Y; equivalently
the computable quotient relative to a fixed reference vertex is
Z_i=X_1 X_i, i!=1. For every centre c the map Z<->Y is a bijection, so
the same gap and minimum-mass bound hold in this observable coordinate.
No unobserved phase or supplied centre label is queried.

2. Finite burn, thinning and acquisition cost
Let a=(1-rho_max)/2. The quotient minimum stationary mass is at least
a^(n-1). Reversibility and (4) give, uniformly from every quotient start,

 d(t):=TV(Law(Y_t),nu) <= (1/2) a^[-(n-1)/2] exp(-t).   (5)

For K planned observations, choose a finite spacing (rounded upward)

 Delta >= (n-1)log(1/a)/2 + log(K/delta_mix).            (6)

Use finite rational input bounds and a certified upward rational choice
of Delta; no exact real-logarithm primitive is assumed.

If each readout incurs a fixed finite elapsed overhead t_read, use actual
spacing Delta_phys=Delta+t_read and read at j*Delta_phys, j=1,...,K.
Snapshots are synchronous and nondisturbing; the site clocks continue
during readout overhead, and the schedule is fixed independently of the
observed states. A sequential maximal-coupling argument
bounds the joint law of the K quotient observations against K independent
nu samples by K d(Delta)<=delta_mix/2. Every pair-correlation statistic
used by the tree learner is a function of those quotients. Its IID
Hoeffding confidence event therefore transfers with only this additive
failure allowance, although the FULL spin snapshots may be very far from
independent or globally stationary.

Use the K required by the statistical learner with failure delta_stat;
the total failure is at most delta_stat+delta_mix (conservatively).
Elapsed source time is T=KDelta_phys, including the paid synchronized
readout overheads; nK binary readouts are recorded. At rate one per site,
the expected number of attempted local updates is nT, with any further
clock-running setup/finalization time included in T as well. Both are
polynomial for inverse-polynomial margins/tolerances, with explicit
dependence on (1-rho_max)^-1, epsilon^-1 and the confidence logarithms.
No unknown source mixing time or global stationary preparation is hidden
in C_snap for this kinetic subclass.

3. Full-state metastability does not contradict this operation
Let A={X_c=+1}. At equilibrium pi(A)=1/2. The stationary flux of centre
flips from A to its complement is

 F=(1/2) sum_y nu(y)nu(-y)/[nu(y)+nu(-y)]
   <=(1/4) sum_y sqrt(nu(y)nu(-y))
   =(1/4) product_i sqrt(1-rho_i^2).                   (7)

Start from pi conditioned on A. This law has density at most two with
respect to pi, a bound preserved by the Markov evolution. The expected
number of OUTGOING A-to-complement jumps is therefore at most 2Ft.
A union/expected-jump-count argument
gives Pr(exit A by time t)<=2Ft, hence full-state TV from equilibrium
is at least 1/2-2Ft. Its 1/4 mixing time is therefore at least

 (8F)^-1 >= (1/2) product_i(1-rho_i^2)^(-1/2),           (8)

exponential at fixed rho_min>0. This also lower-bounds worst fixed-start
mixing by convexity. It is a genuine full-state preparation obstacle,
NOT a lower bound for the pair-statistic acquisition above. The slow
global phase is irrelevant to those invariant observables under the
specified exact zero-field model.

4. Boundaries of what has been acquired
This closes the paid correlation-source operation only for the declared
heat-bath star class with known clock bounds. It does not prove a gap
for arbitrary trees, alternative kinetics, unobserved spins, colored
environmental noise, or biased fields. Rescaling every clock arbitrarily
slowly leaves the equilibrium model unchanged while changing acquisition
time; the clock promise is substantive.

The zero-field equilibrium phase weight is a MODEL consequence, not
empirically estimated from a trace trapped in one phase. The learner
must use only the pair observables for the above confidence transfer;
claims about empirical magnetization or other phase-sensitive statistics
would require a separate source analysis. Common-shock/model-misspecification
validation remains obstructed by RARE_COMMON_SHOCK_BOUNDARY.

This is a lumped-observable/Dirichlet-comparison mechanism with strong
classical ancestry. The scope contribution is an explicit finite
acquisition protocol showing why full-state mixing can overcharge a
specific representation-learning task; no general sampling or physical
performance breakthrough is claimed. No simulation was needed.
