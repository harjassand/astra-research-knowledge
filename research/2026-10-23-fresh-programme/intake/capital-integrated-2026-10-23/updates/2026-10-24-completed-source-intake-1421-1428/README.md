## P2 rendered return: charge-exchange mobility in 3C-SiC

[The completed rendered return](source/dispatch/P2_charge_exchange_mobility_completed_1421_20261024.json) proposes a spatial kinetic model for a silicon–carbon divacancy in cubic silicon carbide. Two published, charge-dependent atomic pivots favor opposite motions; combining them through ordinary charge exchange can translate the reconstructed divacancy motif by an approximately 0.312 nm lattice step. In the stated fast-exchange baseline at 1,200 K and Fermi level about 1.16 eV above the valence-band maximum, the calculated diffusivity is 1.50×10^-21 m^2/s, compared with 3.38×10^-23 m^2/s for the charge-frozen description, about 44 times larger. The source reports a separate small-wavevector generator check of the kinetic calculation to better than 2×10^-8 over its tested finite exchange rates.

These are conditional model predictions, not measured transport or a 44-fold advantage over conventional annealing; the predicted motion is high-temperature, nanometre-scale annealing, not a room-temperature conveyor. The baseline uses a common 10^13 s^-1 attempt frequency, initially retains only charge states +1 and 0, assumes equal degeneracy, assigns a one-half successful antisite exit factor, and omits activation-entropy corrections. At the baseline point, a 1 s^-1 charge-equilibration rate gives about 70% of the fast-exchange diffusivity in the reported finite-rate calculation. The source bounds the omitted charge-state statistical weight but notes that a rare low-barrier state could strengthen the prediction by a factor of a few. The transition rates and charged free-energy/escape network are not experimentally calibrated. Equilibrium charge exchange increases diffusion in this model without producing a directed pump or extracting work.

The intermediate-trap check is also model-based: at the baseline, the source estimates an intermediate-to-divacancy population ratio near 5.9×10^-6 and a mean intermediate residence near 2.1×10^-5 s. “Preserving the complex” means carrying its net missing-atom inventory and recovering the divacancy motif after chemical reconstruction; it does not preserve the same instantaneous structure or a coherent qubit state.

A proposed selective-purification application fails in the same model: the calculated silicon-vacancy-to-carbon-antisite-vacancy conversion rate near the baseline condition is about 0.71 s^-1, so stationary silicon-vacancy qubits would not simply remain while divacancies diffuse over hours. The surviving candidate is narrower: adjust mobility of an already assembled divacancy population. Uniform diffusion does not choose a destination or position a defect, and the source leaves spatial capture, switching/control interfaces and comparison with actual annealing unresolved. Its links and rendered equations are retained in the JSON. The capture receipt records the original code/data attachments as UNKNOWN; no experiment or external review is reported.
