Combining the two cut errors, two approximation errors and (10),

for every H>0. This uses endpoint risks only, not uniform risk along
the intervening parameter interval and not physical-QFI continuity.
If both risks <=rho, taking H of order rho^(2/3) gives an upper
bound tending to zero at order O(rho^(1/3)).

For a uniform epsilon-estimator with unconditional per-endpoint error
<=alpha<1/2, take two points d>2epsilon and b=1-2alpha. Death,
abort and noncompletion count as failure. The two success events
give D>=b. Set

 H_b=min{b/(8C_*), b^2/(8d^2 L_*)}.

The last two terms in (11) sum to at most b/2, so

 max(rho_g0,rho_g1)>=c_* b H_b/4>0.                       (12)

Constants are conservative, and no precision-optimal lower bound is
claimed. The result is the impossibility of maintaining a fixed

For unbounded random call counts, apply the finite result to episode

construction. Nontermination is failure, never a survivor-conditioned
