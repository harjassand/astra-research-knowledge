1. Exact question and conclusion
-------------------------------

For k input settings on each side, let {P_i^x}_{i=1}^M and
{Q_j^y}_{j=1}^M be complete rank-h PVMs on C^{Mh}. The maximally entangled
state, with Bob's effects transposed in its Schmidt basis, gives

  p(i,j|x,y) = Tr(P_i^x Q_j^y)/(Mh).                         (A1)

All outcome marginals are 1/M. A scalar-amplitude quotient would choose arbitrary
complex phases independently at every full input-output cell and set

  A_{(x,i),(y,j)} = exp(i theta_{xyij}) sqrt(p(i,j|x,y))/k,
  B = k sqrt(M) A.

Thus ||A||_F=1 and |B_{(x,i),(y,j)}|=sqrt(Tr(P_i^x Q_j^y)/h).
The proposed dimension-free step was the existence of phases with gamma_2(B)
bounded by a universal constant, equivalently gamma_2(A)<=C/(k sqrt(M)).

THEOREM. For every t=2^r>=4, put k=t/2, h=t, M=t^3. There are actual
complete diagonal rank-h PVM families on C^{t^4} such that EVERY complex phase
completion B of the prescribed magnitudes satisfies

  gamma_2(B) >= k sqrt(t)/(t+k-1) >= (sqrt(2)/3) sqrt(k).    (A2)

Conversely gamma_2(B)<=sqrt(k) for every such phase completion. In this family
the optimal possible growth is therefore Theta(sqrt(k)). In particular the
proposed universal-constant scalar phase quotient is false, already for
classically realizable tables.

Here gamma_2(B)=inf max_r||u_r|| max_s||v_s|| over complex Hilbert-space
factorizations B_{rs}=<u_r,v_s>. This is the factorization/max norm used in the
earlier fixed sign-dictionary construction, not the ordinary matrix rank.

2. Actual PVM construction
--------------------------

Let F_t be the field of order t and let Omega=F_t^4, with D=t^4 points. For
a in F_t define

  v_a=(1,a,a^2,a^3),       H_a={lambda v_a:lambda in F_t}.

The Vandermonde determinant shows that any at most four distinct v_a are
linearly independent over F_t. This remains true in characteristic two. Split
the t parameters into two disjoint sets E_A,E_B, each of cardinality k.

Alice's setting a in E_A has one outcome for each affine coset L of H_a.
There are M=t^3 cosets, each containing h=t points. On the point basis of
C^Omega, use the projection

  P_L^a = sum_{z in L} |z><z|.

These projections are pairwise orthogonal and sum to the identity for each
setting. Bob uses the identical construction for b in E_B. All projections
on both sides are diagonal. Their transposes equal themselves.

Opposite-party directions are distinct, so an Alice line L and a Bob line R
have either zero or one common point. Equation (A1), on the actual resource
|Phi_D>=D^{-1/2} sum_z |z,z>, is exactly

  p(L,R|a,b)=|L intersect R|/t^4.

The same complete conditional table is classical: share one uniform point z
before the inputs and output its H_a- and H_b-cosets. This observation supplies
the strongest simple baseline and prevents interpreting (A2) as a nonlocality
or game-value obstruction.

3. Incidence matrices and the fixed Frobenius norm
--------------------------------------------------

Let N=kM=k t^3. Define real incidence matrices R_A,R_B of size N by D, with
one row per setting/coset and one column per point. Entries are one for point
membership and zero otherwise. Set

  C = |B| = R_A R_B^*/sqrt(t).                              (A3)

This is exact because opposite lines have intersection size at most one.
In particular an entry is either 0 or 1/sqrt(t).

For each fixed pair of opposite directions, every point z gives a distinct
intersecting ordered line pair (L_z,R_z): a pair sharing two points would have
equal directions. There are D=t^4 such pairs. Across the k^2 input pairs,

  ||C||_F^2 = k^2 t^4/t = k^2 t^3.                         (A4)

Each row has exactly t intersecting lines in every Bob setting, hence k t
nonzero entries. Its squared l2 norm is k. This also holds for every phase
completion B and gives gamma_2(B)<=sqrt(k), by the row-vector factorization.

4. Exact fourth Schatten moment
------------------------------

The D by D matrices K_A=R_A^*R_A and K_B=R_B^*R_B are convolution operators
on the additive group of Omega. Specifically their (z,z') entry counts the
chosen directions whose line subgroup contains z'-z. They commute and are
simultaneously diagonalized by additive characters.

Index a character by w in F_t^4 using the field trace to F_2. The character sum
over H_a equals t if w dot v_a=0 and zero otherwise. The equivalence follows
from nondegeneracy of the field-trace pairing and the full scalar range
lambda in F_t. Consequently the eigenvalues of K_A,K_B at w are

  t n_A(w),  t n_B(w),
  n_A(w)=#{a in E_A:w dot v_a=0},
  n_B(w)=#{b in E_B:w dot v_b=0}.

Cyclicity of the finite trace, (A3), and simultaneous diagonalization give

  ||C||_{S4}^4 = t^{-2} Tr[(K_A K_B)^2]
               = t^2 sum_w n_A(w)^2 n_B(w)^2.              (A5)

Expand the sum over ordered a,a' in E_A and b,b' in E_B. Directions across
the two sides are always distinct. If both within-side pairs repeat, there
are k^2 choices and t^2 common annihilating w. If exactly one pair repeats,
there are 2k^2(k-1) choices and t common annihilators. If neither pair repeats,
there are k^2(k-1)^2 choices and one annihilator. These counts use respectively
two, three, and four independent Vandermonde directions. Therefore

  sum_w n_A(w)^2 n_B(w)^2
    = k^2[t^2+2(k-1)t+(k-1)^2]
    = k^2(t+k-1)^2,

  ||C||_{S4}^4 = k^2 t^2 (t+k-1)^2.                       (A6)

The character w=0 is included throughout. No random incidence assumption or
unproved expansion estimate is used.

5. A bound valid for every complex phase choice
----------------------------------------------

For any B with |B|=C, the triangle inequality in each inner product of two
rows gives

  ||B||_{S4}^4
    = sum_{r,r'} |sum_s B_{rs} conjugate(B_{r's})|^2
   <= sum_{r,r'} (sum_s C_{rs} C_{r's})^2
    = ||C||_{S4}^4.                                       (A7)

Its Frobenius norm is fixed by (A4). Schatten interpolation, equivalently
Holder on the singular values, yields

  ||B||_* >= ||B||_F^3 / ||B||_{S4}^2.                     (A8)

For an N by N factorization B=UV^*,

  ||B||_* <= ||U||_F ||V||_F
           <= N max_r||U_r|| max_s||V_s||.

Taking the infimum gives gamma_2(B)>=||B||_*/N. Substituting (A4), (A6),
and (A7) in (A8) proves

  gamma_2(B)
    >= [(k^2 t^3)^{3/2}]/[(k t^3)(k t(t+k-1))]
     = k sqrt(t)/(t+k-1).

With t=2k this is at least (sqrt(2)/3)sqrt(k), proving (A2). The phases may
be arbitrary and may depend on the entire input-output table; no local-phase
or factorized-phase restriction is made.

6. Exact consequence and boundaries
----------------------------------

This retires the universal-constant SINGLE scalar phase-completion premise
for the actual equal-rank PVM/ME family. The internal rank h cannot in general
be erased by that particular scalar square-root/maxnorm representation.

It does not retire convex mixtures of amplitude squares with different entry
magnitudes, other payoff-aware quotients, or classical-mixture decompositions.
It does not prove a random-mask payoff lower bound, an unrestricted quantum or
commuting game gap, a failure of a general local repair, or a useful-excess
selected source. In fact the constructed probability law is classically local.
The earlier rank-one and bounded-spectrum theorems remain unchanged.

The proof uses standard finite-field Vandermonde/character analysis, incidence
matrix identities, elementary Schatten interpolation, and the elementary
nuclear-norm lower bound for gamma_2. The latter is stated for real rectangular
matrices in Linial--Mendelson--Schechtman--Shraibman, Complexity Measures of
Sign Matrices, Combinatorica 27(4) (2007), Lemma 3.4, printed p.448:
https://www2.mta.ac.il/~adish/Pubs/Papers/complexity_matrices.pdf
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00493-007-2160-5
The displayed factorization proof above establishes the complex version used
here directly. The lead reopened the actual primary PDF and checked Lemma 3.4;
the bounded source check independently located the same lemma. Srebro--Shraibman,
COLT 2005, supplies the related sign-atom factorization used by the mechanism
being challenged:
https://www2.mta.ac.il/~adish/Pubs/Papers/SSCOLT05.pdf

7. A matching exact mixture in this same source
----------------------------------------------

PROPOSITION. The preceding probability table nevertheless has an exact mixture
of scalar amplitude squares, each with gamma_2=1/(k sqrt(M)) and Frobenius norm
one. This mixture has a literal local pre-input implementation on the supplied
maximally entangled resource.

For c in F_t put W_c={z in F_t^4:z_0=c}. Every chosen direction v_a has first
coordinate one, so every affine line in every chosen setting meets W_c in
exactly one point. Thus W_c is a common transversal, of cardinality M=t^3.
The point-basis decomposition is

  |Phi_D> = t^{-1/2} sum_c |Phi_{W_c}>.

Both parties locally measuring their first point coordinate therefore yields
the same uniform c and the residual state |Phi_{W_c}>. Each original line PVM
restricted to W_c is complete and rank one, with a line outcome L corresponding
to its unique intersection z_L(c). Its conditional two-party table is

  p_c(L,R|a,b) = M^{-1} 1[z_L(c)=z_R(c)].

If opposite lines intersect at z, exactly one c=z_0 contributes; otherwise none
does. Consequently t^{-1} sum_c p_c equals (A1) pointwise. Define

  A^{(c)}_{(a,L),(b,R)}
    = (k sqrt(M))^{-1} 1[z_L(c)=z_R(c)].

The common point-basis factorization gives gamma_2(A^{(c)})<=1/(k sqrt(M));
the maximum entry gives the reverse inequality. There are k^2 M nonzero entries,
so ||A^{(c)}||_F=1, and

  t^{-1} sum_c |A^{(c)}_{(a,L),(b,R)}|^2
    = p(L,R|a,b)/k^2.                                      (A9)

The shared c is obtained before the fresh inputs; no realized opposite question
is supplied to a party. The proposition concerns this supplied source and its
complete coarse table. If the specified original instruments are the line
Luders projections, their final two-sided nonzero branch is already
|z,z>/sqrt(D), so after tracing the added c flags the c-mixture has that same
final joint branch density on the same output space. Equivalently append the
local deterministic tags c=z_0 to the original final output and retain both flags.
It does change the coherent resource and the Alice-only intermediate state.
No preservation claim is made for arbitrary fine/Kraus instruments or later
operations before the other party's complete projection. In particular (A9)
is fully consistent with the lower bound on every single phase completion.
