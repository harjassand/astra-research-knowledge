6. A sufficient plant-precision envelope, not a mismatch no-go

Suppose nominal and actual poles have the same fixed order, unit DC gain
and positive values in a supplied compact interval, with each error at
most epsilon_tau. If P_hat-P=sum_(k=1)^m d_k D^k, then
|d_k|<=C epsilon_tau. Starting at zero, the actual output discrepancy for
the SMALL NATIVE H_named pulse under its nominal inverse command is

 dH_out=P_actual(D)^(-1)(P_hat-P_actual)(D)H_named.

By (3), the full causal L1 bound is

 integral ||dH_out||dt
 <=C epsilon_tau sum_(k=1)^m integral ||H_named^(k)||dt
 <=C epsilon_tau beta delta^(-m).                        (11)

The sufficient NATIVE-PULSE-ONLY condition to fit (7) is

 epsilon_tau <=c beta^3 delta^(m+2)
                =O(delta^(4m+8)).                       (12)

The same native-only scale is sufficient for a relative L1 command-error
envelope on that small pulse, since its ideal command L1 norm is
O(beta delta^(-m))=O(delta^2). It is NOT the full episode budget.

The fixed safe pump/reset has order-one flat ramps and a paid
O(log(1/delta)) plateau. For unit-DC-gain pole mismatch only derivatives
of its output enter P_hat-P_actual; their full L1 norms are O(1), because
the plateau is constant and the fixed ramp-off is merely time-translated.
Its pole-mismatch output error is therefore O(epsilon_tau), not
O(epsilon_tau delta^2). Its total ideal command L1 norm, however, is
O(1+log(1/delta)). A conservative sufficient WHOLE-EPISODE envelope is

 epsilon_tau<=c beta^4 delta^2,
 epsilon_rel<=c beta^4 delta^2/[1+log(1/delta)],            (13)

where the relative command error is bounded in full L1 norm by
epsilon_rel times the full ideal command L1 norm. Every other additive
command/output error, including finite polynomial synthesis error, must
fit the separate whole-episode O(beta^4 delta^2) integrated budget in
(10). No structural immunity of a perturbed safe-sector reset is used
to improve (13).

Exact zero-state closure belongs to the supplied EXACT plant. Under
mismatch the bounds include the entire causal tail and do not certify
independent zero-state resets between calls. For a whole command train,
linearity and the triangle inequality bound its total output discrepancy
by the sum of the per-command full-tail L1 contributions, including
post-stop tails; a residual is not silently erased at episode boundaries.
The finite-parameter instrument perturbation must use this whole-train
budget, or a separately implemented and paid actuator-state correction.

Equations (12)--(13) are conservative SUFFICIENT guarantees for this proof,

7. Strong conventional comparisons and the remaining physical gate

Time-domain inverse filtering and measured-response predistortion are
established controls. Gustavsson et al., PRL 110,040502 (2013),
https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.2175 , measures pulse distortion using a qubit
and constructs a correction from the inferred transfer response. It
explicitly separates learning that response from applying its inverse.
Rol et al., APL 116,054001 (2020), https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.04818 ,
uses measured on-chip step responses and IIR/FIR correction at finite
hardware resolution. These are competent established controls, not a
cold uncorrected baseline. No strict finite-duration floor follows from
finite poles in either procedure, and their actual empirical calibration
does not supply this specimen model's plant/precision promises.

The quantum ancestry is equally explicit: QEC/approximate-QEC metrology
with noiseless ancillas and fast/arbitrary recovery is established in
Zhou--Zhang--Preskill--Jiang, Nat Commun 9,78 (2018),
https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.02445 . That broad principle neither provides
the absorbing-specimen finite named-port gate nor makes finite actuator
calibration/implementation cost free. Conventional equally informed
predistortion/QEC may implement or improve this construction; no strict
method advantage is asserted from its ordinary executable form.

Most importantly, (1) has transfer product(1+i omega tau_j)^(-1), nonzero
at every finite frequency. It is NOT a hard spectral cutoff. A globally
bandlimited L2 waveform extends analytically; if it vanishes on a time
interval, it is identically zero. Hence a nontrivial exact finite on/off
operation cannot simply be both globally bandlimited and compactly
supported. A meaningful hard-bandwidth interface must instead specify its
always-on tails, initial actuator state, readout/completion and total loss.
Superoscillatory small features cannot be accepted while discarding their
outside-window controls or loss. None of those different hard-bandwidth
interfaces is solved by (1)--(13).
