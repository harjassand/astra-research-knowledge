# The diagonal positive-part budget for an arbitrary noncommuting two-source cone

2026-10-25. This proves a genuine noncommuting class of the proposed positive-part diagonal budget. It does not establish the independent optimized coherence budget.

Let A,X>=0 be arbitrary finite-dimensional operators, not assumed to commute. Suppose

x=alpha_1 A+beta_1 X,
b=alpha_2 A+beta_2 X,
c=alpha_3 A+beta_3 X,

with all six scalar coefficients nonnegative. Then the actual invariant-channel diagonal slack obeys

**Delta_diag(x,b,c)>=k Tr(x-3b/2)_+, k=(1/2)log(32/27).**

In particular this holds for arbitrary noncommuting x,b with c=0, and more generally c=alpha*x+beta*b for alpha,beta>=0. The theorem retains every reference and representation-dimension entropy term in the actual Delta_diag.

## Scalar governing inequality

For nonnegative scalar x,b,c, two already established inequalities apply:

Delta_diag(x,b,c)>=k(x-3b/2),
Delta_diag(x,b,c)>=0.

The first is the complete affine diagonal slack theorem, specialized to scalars; the second is the diagonal entropy-contraction theorem. Their combination gives

Delta_diag(x,b,c)>=k(x-3b/2)_+.

No implication from max(Tr H,0) to Tr H_+ is being made.

## Exact two-source transfer

Initially take A,X faithful. Let mu be the positive binary testing measure from h(t)=Tr(A-tX)_+, as in the independently verified Frenkel signed-pencil entropy representation. It satisfies

Tr(alpha A+beta X)_+=integral (alpha r+beta)_+ mu(dr)

for every real alpha,beta, and represents every balanced trace-entropy expression in positive affine mixtures of A,X by its scalar integral. The input masses are represented by r and 1. This is an abstract binary testing representation, not a common measurement or simultaneous diagonalization.

The nonlinear entropy part of Delta_diag is

2[S(x/4+3b/8)+S(3x/4+c/4)+S(5b/8+3c/4)]
-S(x)-S(b)-S(c)-S(x+b+c).

Its operator arguments sum to zero with these signed coefficients: twice the sum of the three output blocks equals twice (x+b+c). Thus it is balanced in both A and X. All additional terms in the physical diagonal slack are linear trace terms from the representation dimensions; they transfer by the mass and first-moment identities of mu. Consequently

Delta_diag(x,b,c)
=integral Delta_diag(alpha_1 r+beta_1,alpha_2 r+beta_2,alpha_3 r+beta_3) mu(dr).

Apply the scalar positive-part bound pointwise. The signed-pencil identity gives

integral [alpha_1 r+beta_1-(3/2)(alpha_2 r+beta_2)]_+ mu(dr)
=Tr(x-3b/2)_+.

This proves the claim. For singular A,X, replace them by A+epsilon I,X+epsilon I while keeping the six nonnegative coefficients fixed. Finite-dimensional entropy and trace positive part are continuous; take epsilon down to zero. This avoids an unjustified derivative-at-zero formula for a singular testing curve.

## Governing sources and limitations

The binary transfer was independently reconstructed from P. E. Frenkel, *Integral formula for quantum relative entropy implies data processing inequality*, arXiv:2208.12194v4, Theorem 6, https://arxiv.org/html/2208.12194 . The needed exact transfer is already retained in `binary_sector_sharp_attenuation_coherence_theorem.md`. The actual affine slack is proved in `wh3_su3_adjoint_complete_affine_slack_theorem.md`.

Three arbitrary positive operators need not lie in a cone generated by two positive operators. This proof makes no ternary scalarization claim. The separate candidate Gamma(x,b)<=k Tr(x-3b/2)_+ remains unproved for arbitrary noncommuting x,b, even in the c=0 receiving case. Therefore this diagonal-budget theorem does not close the full invariant-channel entropy target.
