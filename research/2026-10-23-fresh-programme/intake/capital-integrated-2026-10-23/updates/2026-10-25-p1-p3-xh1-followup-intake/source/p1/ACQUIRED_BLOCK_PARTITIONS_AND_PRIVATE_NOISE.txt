ACQUIRED BLOCK PARTITIONS AND PRIVATE NOISE
25 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane

Purpose and relationship to the first construction
==================================================
The single finite-dimensional projective observer does not itself control
the dimension of a many-body system. This extension separates two costs:

  * Actual visible local operations keep the conditional physical state in
    a product of blocks of at most B sites.
  * Genuinely private noise contracts order errors, permitting a fixed-bit
    positive representation of every block while marked acquisition and
    controlled interactions continue.

The block structure is a property of the declared acquired physical
experiment, not an unproved factorization of an arbitrary posterior. All
observers receive every mark, cut operation and control. This is a changed
physical protocol, and the cost to the scientific/control objective of
breaking correlations must be assessed separately from simulation error.

1. A product partition that can be maintained from received information
======================================================================
There are N sites of local dimension d>=2. At the beginning of a sweep the
conditional state has the represented form

  rho_h = tensor_(C in P_h) rho_(C,h),    |C|<=B.

The exact observer and computational observer share the partition P_h.
It can be maintained without pretending to observe hidden bath events:

  * An instrument or gate confined to one block updates that block.
  * A gate joining two blocks merges their labels and their density matrix.
  * A visible local measure-and-prepare outcome at site v separates v from
    the REST of its current block. In the simple rank-one projective case,
    the outcome prepares a known pure state at v.

For a general declared local cut instrument
  C_y(X)=tau_y tr(F_y X),    sum_y F_y=I,
the joint conditional output is tau_y at v tensored with the conditional
state of the rest. The operation is physical, received and paid for. It is
not a software deletion of correlations. The rest of the old block must
be kept as one block unless a separate factorization proof is available:
measuring a connecting site can entangle the remaining sites. Graph
disconnection alone is insufficient.

Before a gate whose merged block would exceed B, a declared controller
may cut one or both endpoints and then perform the gate, choosing enough
cuts to satisfy the size cap. This maintains a product partition using
actual operations and their marks. The controller's partition labels and
any policy state must be retained and counted. Adaptive choices may use
the computational observer: the exact same-record comparator uses those
actual choices, not actions from an unavailable exact-state oracle.

An arbitrary entangled initial state is not made a product by partial
private refresh. One must either supply a justified initial product
partition or acquire it physically. For example, local measure-and-prepare
cuts at every site leave a known product state after receiving the initial
outcomes, even if the unknown input was entangled. This is an explicit
initialization interface and backaction cost, not free state tomography.
One need not have predicted the probability of that initial acquisition
word to know the resulting prepared product state.

2. The product projective metric avoids a global-dimension penalty
================================================================
For states on a common product partition,

  h(tensor_C rho_C, tensor_C sigma_C)=sum_C h(rho_C,sigma_C).   (B1)

This follows from multiplicativity of the extremal generalized eigenvalue
ratios. It is established cone geometry (Reeb--Kastoryano--Wolf,
arXiv:1102.5170, Corollary2).

Every actual observed positive branch, gate and cut is nonexpansive in the
GLOBAL Hilbert metric. The partition can change during a sweep without an
extra multiplicative error factor. At the end of that sweep, when the
conditional states again factor over the common partition, (B1) is exact.
No statistical independence of measurement outcomes is being asserted.

3. Version A: private refresh of each current block
===================================================
At the end of a sweep, apply independently to each block C, of size s_C,

  E_C(X)=(1-r)X+r I_(d^s_C)/d^s_C tr X,    0<r<1.

The refresh indicators are NOT observed. This is a real physical channel,
not the observer's quantization operation. For D_C=d^s_C the projective
contraction factor and refreshed eigenvalue floor are

  q_C=D_C(1-r)/[D_C(1-r)+2r],    a_C=r/D_C.

Let q_B=d^B(1-r)/[d^B(1-r)+2r]. Because q_C<=q_B, (B1) implies that this
collection of independent block channels contracts the global error by
q_B, even when the number of blocks is large.

Quantize each refreshed D_C-dimensional block using the positive dyadic
padding operation from PROJECTIVE_RETIREMENT_UNDER_CONTINUING_MARKED_OBSERVATION.txt.
With delta=2^-b, its local projective error is at most

  eta_C=log(1+2D_C^2 delta/r)<=2D_C^2 delta/r.

The sum of independent real state coordinates satisfies

  S(P)=sum_C D_C^2=sum_C d^(2s_C) <= (N/B)d^(2B).       (B2)

Indeed d^(2s)/s increases for integer s>=1, so each term is at most
(s_C/B)d^(2B); then sum s_C=N. Divisibility of N by B is unnecessary.

Let H_n be a same-record global projective-error allowance immediately
after quantization. All measurements/controls until the next refresh are
nonexpansive. Therefore

  H_(n+1) <= q_B H_n + (2delta/r)(N/B)d^(2B).           (B3)

For identical initial states H_0=0, the uniform global trace allowance is

  D_tr(rho_n,rhohat_n)
      <= N d^(2B) delta/[2 r B(1-q_B)].                 (B4)

One sufficient precision is

  b >= ceil log2[N d^(2B)/(2 r B(1-q_B) epsilon)].       (B5)

Also require the elementary padding encoding condition delta<=1/(4d^B).
This is a conservative all-record bound. It holds during the intervening
marked/control operations as well because they do not increase H.

For fixed d,B,r and global tolerance epsilon, the number of state
coordinates grows linearly with N and b grows like log2(N/epsilon).
This is a bounded-in-time description of the declared controlled system,
with polynomial dependence on N at fixed B. It is not a general simulator
for unrestricted N-body dynamics. The block reference density filter is
already a strong conventional method; it receives the same structure.

4. Version B: only independent onsite private noise is needed
=============================================================
The physical refresh need not synchronize a common private coin over an
entire block. Suppose instead that each site independently receives

  e(X)=(1-r0)X+r0 I_d/d tr X,    0<r0<1.

Within a block of s sites, the tensor product channel has the positive
decomposition

  e^tensor_s = r0^s R_(I/d^s) + (1-r0^s)G_s,          (B6)

where G_s is CPTP: group the terms in the independent replacement expansion
other than the all-sites-replaced term and normalize them. This is a proof
decomposition; none of its private labels is observed or supplied to the
filter. Its existence bounds the projective diameter even for entangled
states within the block.

Thus one may take

  a_s=(r0/d)^s,
  q_s=(1-r0^s)/[1-r0^s+2(r0/d)^s],
  q_B=(1-r0^B)/[1-r0^B+2(r0/d)^B] <1.

The quantity (1-r0^s)/(r0/d)^s=(d/r0)^s-d^s increases with s, so q_s<=q_B.
The per-block rounding error is bounded by

  eta_C<=2delta(d^2/r0)^s_C.

The same increasing-ratio argument as(B2) gives

  H_(n+1)<=q_B H_n+2delta(N/B)(d^2/r0)^B,
  D_tr(rho_n,rhohat_n)
      <= N (d^2/r0)^B delta/[2B(1-q_B)].               (B7)

For fixed B this again has linear N and logarithmic precision dependence,
with worse but explicit constants. No unobserved partial reset is promoted
to an acquired product cut: product structure is supplied by the visible
operations and initial preparation, while onsite private noise contracts
error WITHIN each maintained block.

5. Finite state and working cost
================================
Version A or B stores at most (N/B)d^(2B) independent real dyadic coordinates,
each with b+2 bits, with normalization derived from the raw diagonal sum.
Store partition labels (a simple implementation uses O(N log N) bits),
controller memory, known model/gate data, current marks and working arrays
separately. This is a constructive upper bound, not a minimal code length.

To claim bounded working arithmetic per update, also bound the number and
description size of operations between quantization sweeps. With rational
gate/instrument/noise coefficients of fixed bit length and at most F such
operations per component between refreshes, ordinary exact rational matrix
arithmetic has a bit bound depending on d^B, B b, F and the model. Merging
product blocks multiplies only the normalizers of at most B small-site
components. This cost is independent of the age of the experiment.
An unbounded sequence of exact operations without a refresh/quantization
sweep could grow intermediate coefficient bits even if the end-of-sweep
state size is bounded; that loophole is not included in the claim.

Private onsite noise can be applied directly to each block density matrix
without sampling hidden labels. A conventional state filter, positive
mixture implementation or tensor method may do exactly this. No exclusive
representation advantage is claimed.

6. Prediction, control and long-record scope
===========================================
At every history the stated trace allowance bounds every common future
physical experiment started from the encoded state. For any class of
future causal policies whose total payoff lies in[0,1], an optimizer using
the encoded state has true regret at most2epsilon, plus its own planning
error. The cost of optimization is not bounded by the inference theorem.
The scientific cost of the physical cuts or added noise is not numerical
regret relative to an uncut/noiseless experiment; it must be compared as a
different admissible protocol.

A fixed global current-state allowance does not yield one fixed error for
the whole infinite acquired record. The square-summable order-error
schedule from the single-block theorem applies with the SUM eta_n of all
blocks at each sweep. For a finite horizon, accumulate conditional KL or
TV in the usual sequential way. If unknown initial block states are merely
assumed product, include their finite post-refresh projective diameter and
its geometric transient. If the initial acquired cuts prepare known states,
H_0=0 is legitimate.

7. Main obstruction resolved, and the next one not resolved
===========================================================
Product geometry plus acquired bounded blocks removes the exponential-in-N
global-noise contraction constant. It does not obtain those blocks for free.
Rich observations and controls can continue and can create entanglement
within blocks; physical interventions limit how far that correlated quantum
information can grow. Private noise then retires representational errors
instead of requiring their bits to grow forever.

The outstanding scientific issue is whether a useful task tolerates the
necessary visible cuts/noise at an acceptable frequency, or whether a more
general low-cost representation can replace those cuts. A hidden renewal
event, unconditional mixing, or a graph edge deletion does not establish
the required acquired conditional factorization.
