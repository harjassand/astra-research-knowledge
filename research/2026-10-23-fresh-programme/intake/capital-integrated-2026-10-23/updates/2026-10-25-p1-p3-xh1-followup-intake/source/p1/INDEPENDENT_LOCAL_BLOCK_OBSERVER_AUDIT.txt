The physical local cut is a received measure-and-prepare branch
|y><phi_y| with an orthonormal tilted input basis. It separates the measured
endpoint and keeps the conditional rest of the old block as one component.
The scheduler uses current partition labels, never hidden bath flags. Its
two-site gate cap is therefore an acquired exact conditional product
structure. The initial product |0000> is explicitly known. Thus H_0=0 is
legitimate even though that state is pure; identical states have projective
distance zero on their common face.

The weak X map is H K_Z H with H=H_RAW/sqrt2, equivalently
(H_RAW K_Z H_RAW)/2. Both X and Z instrument pairs sum exactly to the
identity effect. The real rational drive R and controlled rotation CR are
unitary. Actual marks determine a common finite controller: sweep phase
modulo four and the latest weak mark.

The per-site private map is
  (3/4)rho+(1/4)(I_i/2 tensor tr_i rho).
replace_site_fraction constructs the correct partial trace, inserts I/2,
and restores site order. Applying it independently at each site preserves
the declared product partition. In a size-two block its full-replacement
CP minorization is1/16 and its eigenvalue floor is1/64. The resulting
projective contraction factor is30/31, not the single-site3/4.

After each sweep, quantize_blocks rounds the real symmetric block entries
to2^-b, adds D*2^-b to each diagonal of a D-dimensional block, and normalizes
by the exact raw diagonal sum. It is the positive padding construction;
no discarded eigenvalues or unencoded ideal state enter the proof. At
N=4,B=2,b=34, the uniform current global-state trace allowance is

  7936*2^-34=31/67108864=4.6193599700927734375e-7.

This is an all-record theorem from product-projective contraction and the
explicit rounding allowance. The dense floating trajectory is only a
diagnostic and record generator. Its state is not passed to the observer.

Coarse finite working arithmetic for the exact declared four-site loop
=====================================================================
Let S=2^b. At a sweep boundary each stored raw block has trace<=S+18,
including the smaller singleton blocks. A hypothetical integer numerator
for the global product therefore has trace T_0<=(S+18)^4. This is a proof
coordinate; the implementation does not form that global matrix.

There are at most eight physical Kraus/unitary operations in one sweep:
two visible cuts, one drive, one controlled rotation, and four weak marks.
Using their integer numerators, all have operator norm at most24. A
positive raw matrix Z with trace T therefore updates to a raw matrix of
trace at most24^2 T=576 T. Normalization merely chooses the new raw trace
as denominator. Subsystem marginals and the known rank-one outputs have
fractions whose denominators divide an available common global raw trace.

Each of four onsite noise operations has the integer form
  Z -> 6Z + I_i tensor tr_i Z,
whose trace is8T. Hence one available uniform common-denominator cap is

  T <= 576^8 * 8^4 * (S+18)^4 < 2^(4b+90),  b>=5.

For the actual Fraction implementation, temporary products and divisions
can use two such rational operands. The fixed matrix coefficient
denominators are at most26; a sandwich brings at most26^2. Allowing the
corresponding numerator factors, sums and normalization cross-products,
and the2S*n+d integer used in rounding, a deliberately loose bound is
8b+224 magnitude bits for core integer temporaries. At b=34 this is496
magnitude bits, less than512, independent of the record's age or word.
Include a sign bit in a physical memory layout. This bound is deliberately
much larger than the observed Fraction-bit diagnostic. Optional dense
floating references, diagnostics and output-record storage are not the
online observer and are excluded from the core bound.
