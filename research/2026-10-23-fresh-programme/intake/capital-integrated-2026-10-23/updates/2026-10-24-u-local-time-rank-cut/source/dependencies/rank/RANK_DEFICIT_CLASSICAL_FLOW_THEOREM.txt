1. Exact finite-collar support-rank identity
------------------------------------------
Let alpha be a strict radius-r QCA on a uniform D-level chain, r>=1,
without a translation-invariance assumption. Choose a cut after site0,
  L1=[-r+1,0], R1=[1,r], S=L1 union R1, |S|=2r.
Let J be its tracial Choi marginal on S_out S_ref. Define cross partitions
  A=L1' R1, B=L1 R1',  M=D^(2r).
Each cross Hilbert space has dimension M. For the positive multiplicative
GNVW index kappa=ind(alpha), the EXACT rank relation is
  rank(J_B)/rank(J_A)=kappa^2.                                (1)
The derivation below extends the primary index characterization to support
rank. It does NOT infer (1) merely from the published von Neumann or
Renyi2 numerical entropy identity, and it does not identify arbitrary
MPO tensor flattenings with these collars without a boundary proof.

Define support fractions
  t_A=rank(J_A)/M, t_B=rank(J_B)/M,
  F0(alpha)=(1/2)ln(t_B/t_A).                                (2)
RWW's exact cross-Choi factorization outside the radius-r collars is
  J_(L'R)=J_A tensor tau_(L2'R2),
  J_(LR')=J_B tensor tau_(L2R2').
Increasing the collars multiplies BOTH ranks and their Hilbert dimensions
by the same tracial-tail dimensions, so t_A,t_B do not depend on the
chosen larger collar. F0 is additive under tensor products of QCAs,
using a common collar radius if their radii differ.

Circuit calibration requires care: an infinite cross-state does not have
a finite Hilbert-space rank. Instead, on its cross quasi-local algebra the
state has a FINITE positive density f_A=M J_A relative to canonical trace,
extended by identity outside the collar. Its support projection p_A is
local and tau_A(p_A)=t_A. A circuit supported entirely in one physical
half induces a trace-preserving automorphism phi of the corresponding
cross algebra. The density becomes phi^-1(f_A), its support becomes
phi^-1(p_A), and its tracial support fraction is unchanged. The same
holds on B. This uses finite support projections, not infinite rank entropy.

Any finite-depth circuit U factors as U=U_L U_R V, with U_L,U_R confined
to the two physical half-chains and V a finite unitary near the cut. One
explicit construction deletes cross-cut gates from each layer to form
U0=U_L U_R; then V=U0*U is a product of cross-cut gates conjugated through
a finite-depth circuit light cone. Thus V has bounded finite support.
The side circuits preserve t_A,t_B as above. The finite Choi vector of V
is pure; A and B within its finite region are complementary reductions
with equal ranks and equal Hilbert dimensions. Hence t_A=t_B and F0=0
for every circuit, including a nonuniform circuit whose chosen collar is
smaller than a particular circuit decomposition's light cone.

For a right shift of a q-dimensional factor by k>0 sites, EPR-pair counting
gives t_A=q^(-2k), t_B=1; thus F0=k ln q. The inverse shift exchanges
A,B. Extra unshifted factors contribute full-rank tracial factors and do
not affect the result. These calibrations are exact.

Now use the standard GNVW classification/RWW Lemma4.5 uniqueness argument.
Tensor alpha with a generalized inverse-index shift beta. If kappa=p/q,
choose beta as a left p-factor shift and right q-factor shift. Then
ind(alpha tensor beta)=1, so that QCA is a finite-depth circuit. Additivity
and the preceding calibrations give
  0=F0(alpha)+F0(beta)=F0(alpha)-ln kappa.
This proves (1). Beta is a mathematical stable-comparison factor, not a
physical auxiliary supplied for free or a programmed operation.

2. Matched records plus FULL output trace constrain either cross state
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The following step holds for any mixed J whose full output marginal is
I/M; exact QCA structure is not otherwise needed for this lemma.
Let
  Pi=sum_x |x>_out|x>_ref <x|_out<x|_ref,
  E=1-Tr(Pi J), G=Pi J Pi.
E is the probability of at least ONE paired computational-label mismatch
in this finite collar, under its coupled tracial Choi record distribution.
After the cross-coordinate reorder, Pi spans {|x>_A|x>_B}. Consequently
  G_A=G_B=diag(g_x),  sum_x g_x=1-E.
Every matched mass g_x is at most the corresponding full output-string
probability, so FULL output traciality gives
  0<=g_x<=1/M,  ||G_A-I/M||1=E.                              (3)
One-site tracial marginals alone would not establish this pointwise bound.

Gentle projection for a normalized mixed state gives
  ||J-Pi J Pi||1<=2 sqrt(E).
Partial trace is contractive on this Hermitian difference. Hence for BOTH
cross marginals,
  half||J_A-I/M||1 <=sqrt(E)+E/2,
  half||J_B-I/M||1 <=sqrt(E)+E/2.                            (4)
Subnormalization of G has explicitly contributed E/2; it has not silently
been normalized or absorbed into the gentle-projection estimate.

Set a=max(kappa^2,kappa^-2). If kappa!=1, equation (1) implies one cross
marginal has rank at most M/a. For a normalized state rho of that rank,
measurement of its support distinguishes it from I/M with advantage at
least 1-1/a. Therefore (4) forces
  sqrt(E)+E/2 >=1-1/a,
  E >= c_a:=[sqrt(3-2/a)-1]^2 >0.                            (5)
No Hilbert-space dimension appears in this positive constant at fixed
index kappa. No claim is made that this constant is optimal.

This is the extra compatibility condition absent from an arbitrary
entropy- or spectrum-compatible embezzling example: high MATCH probability
and FULL tracial output force both cross states close to full-rank uniform
states, whereas exact index imposes a fixed-fraction rank deficit on one.
Approximate closeness of unconstrained embezzling spectra is insufficient.

3. Classical information-flow invariant and tight asymptotic scaling
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Let epsilon_i be the TOTAL input/output computational label mismatch at
site i for alpha under canonical product-tracial input. The union bound
is literal on the same finite coupled Choi table:
  E <=sum_(i in S) epsilon_i.
Together with (5), for every cut,
  sum_(i in S) epsilon_i >=c_a,
  mean_(i in S) epsilon_i >=c_a/(2r),
  max_(i in S) epsilon_i >=c_a/(2r).                          (6)
It is not a claim that small per-site error implies small global-state TV,
nor that spatial averaging certifies complete records. The all-collar
failure E is used only for this finite distribution and the union bound.

For translation-record counterfeiting let Omega have radius R0 and index
one on A_i B_i, dimensions dA>=2,dB>=1, D=dA*dB. Let sigma be the partial
right A shift, B fixed, ind(sigma)=dA. Define
  alpha=sigma^-1 composed with Omega, r=R0+1.
Then kappa=1/dA, a=dA^2, and c_a is a FIXED positive constant. The actual
tags are A-input(i-1)/A-output i and B-input i/B-output i. For the nonuniform
case the exact pairing follows from trace invariance,
  tau[P_ref sigma^-1(Omega(P_out))]
      =tau[sigma(P_ref) Omega(P_out)].                        (7)
No commutation with sigma or exact A-unitary channel is assumed. Sigma
and alpha are proof references, not extra physical controls.

Thus every length-2(R0+1) output collar of an index-one fake has mean and
maximum full-cell translation-record error at least
  c_(dA^2) / [2(R0+1)].                                     (8)
The full-cell error is at most the sum of TOTAL component errors. If the
only supplied quantities are separate JOINT input-label/mismatch masses
bounded by e, then epsilon_i<=(dA+dB)e, yielding the corresponding necessary
per-label maximum at least c_(dA^2)/[2r(dA+dB)]. Conditional label rates
must instead be averaged with their actual uniform label probabilities.
Do not substitute a joint-label mass directly as a component total.

For fixed local dimensions this order1/R lower bound is MATCHED by the
already frozen explicit selector circuit. For qubits it uses k header bits
and M=2^k payloads, L=M+k, maximum error1/(2M), mean1/(2L), three block
layers and radius at most2L-2. General fixed dA uses a base-dA header with
M=dA^k payloads, selected-payload error(1-1/dA)/M and the same layer/range
argument. Taking the largest fitting block gives O(1/R) at available range R.
These upper bounds are spatially PERIODIC circuits; no one-site-TI upper
bound is claimed. Neither block depth nor available range makes fixed-arity
nearest-neighbor gate time or synthesis cost constant.

Consequently the general nonuniform uniform-source counterfeit problem has
THETA(1/R) scaling for both worst-site error and the collar/spatial means,
under the stated fixed local dimensions and exact-QCA class. Arbitrary
quantum phases and overlapping circuit structure are included in the lower
bound; an isolated-block encoder premise is absent. The earlier inverse-

4. Source, locality, noise, and acquisition boundary
----------------------------------------------------
The actual probe contract is product trace on BOTH rails over the finite
backward causal/tag window. For S=[a,b]=[-r+1,r], a sufficient prepared
region is U=[a-max(R0,1),b+R0], as in the frozen direct-Fano theorem. Its
tracial marginal suffices even with exterior correlations, because the
relevant operators have support inside U. Dynamics remains fixed
independently of sampled tags. One-site maximum entropy with unknown
intersite correlations is insufficient.

Biased full-rank product sources do not give these tracial Choi record
probabilities. The frozen majority circuit's exponential every-site error
at fixed bias therefore remains consistent with the tight uniform bound.

Exact locality and *-automorphism structure enter at the rank/index identity.
Tags alone do not establish them. A noisy/nonunitary device does not gain
an index from (8). The frozen finite-patch causal-resource witness can use
its exact index-one CIRCUIT approximant with already charged local
observable budgets; those audited dynamics/bath bounds are still required.
No source preparation, clock, memory reset, control synthesis, hardware
utility, affordable statistics or general index tomography is claimed.
