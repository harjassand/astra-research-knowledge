1. Literal sequential source interface
---------------------------------------
Let G be a finite two-player game with binary winning predicate, and let
question PAIRS be sampled independently from an arbitrary joint law mu.
An n-round strategy has an arbitrary initial finite-tensor shared state
on C A_1 ... A_n B. The private port dimensions, Bob dimension, shared
entanglement, and local answer alphabets need not be bounded uniformly.

Alice acts in the fixed order 1,...,n. Step i is a complete quantum
instrument on C A_i, controlled ONLY by the current own input x_i and
the fixed position i. It emits the classical answer a_i and carries C
to the next step. The memory dimension is at most D; pad smaller spaces
into one D-dimensional wire. A processed A_i can be discarded or kept
as inaccessible output, but cannot interact with a later step. Emitted
answers likewise cannot subsequently control a step except through C.
Bob may use an arbitrary joint instrument of his entire question Y^n.

CRITICAL: C includes EVERY persistent controller and private-history
register that can affect later Alice maps, including classical memory.
There is no free retained input string, answer history, external lookup
controller, or hidden feedforward path. The mathematical full tuple X^n
may be delivered by the game, but this strategy family only reads it
through the stated causal current-input interface. A nominal bound on
quantum memory alone does not imply this source contract.

We will first prove the finite-tensor result. A separate commuting
version requires supplied normal local port/wire operations and the
frozen table-first commuting event premise; it is stated in Section 6.

2. Complete CP wire order, including all reference systems
----------------------------------------------------------
For a D-dimensional wire define
  R_C(Z)=Tr_C(Z) tensor I_C/D.
The following is an inequality of completely positive maps:

                     id_C <=_CP D^2 R_C.                 (M1)

With the unnormalized Choi vector Omega=sum_j |j,j>,
J(id)=|Omega><Omega| and J(R)=I tensor I/D. Thus
D^2 J(R)-J(id)=D I-|Omega><Omega| is positive: its eigenvalue
on Omega is zero, and on its orthogonal complement is D.
Consequently (M1) remains valid with ANY correlated reference, and
after composition before/after by any fixed CP instrument branch.
This includes the entire unmeasured cross-round resource and Bob.

3. A forced coarse reference at every checked set
--------------------------------------------------
Fix a checked set T, t=|T|<n, and write J=[n]\T. Let b(T) be the number
of nonempty contiguous intervals of T in the actual Alice time order.
In an auxiliary circuit insert R_C immediately BEFORE the first step
of EVERY checked interval, including an interval starting at step 1.
Only incoming cuts are needed. Keep the original checked outcomes
alpha=a_T and sum every untested Alice outcome.

Let tau_(alpha|X^n) denote the ORIGINAL actual normal Bob steering
matrix after the checked Alice outcomes have been retained and all
other Alice outputs/workspace forgotten. Let tau0_(alpha|x_T) be the
corresponding matrix of the cut circuit. Then

 tau_(alpha|X^n) <= d_T tau0_(alpha|x_T),
 d_T=D^(2 b(T)) <= D^(2t),
 sum_alpha tau0_(alpha|x_T)=phi_B.                        (M2)

All inequalities are on the FULL Bob operator space, before Bob's own
instrument; phi_B is the original Bob marginal of the initial state.

Proof of the inequality: insert the cuts successively. The entire
remaining circuit, with the chosen checked alpha and summed untested
outcomes, is a CP map. Equation (M1) multiplies its positive output by
at most D^2 at each insertion. Transitivity gives D^(2b), even with
arbitrarily entangled initial ports/memory/B. No classical transcript
probability bound is being substituted for this CP order.

Proof of the reference dependence: after each incoming replacement,
the memory is a fresh I/D, independent of all ports and Bob. Between
checked intervals every instrument outcome is summed, hence that
segment is trace preserving. Trace it and its processed ports out;
it cannot alter the joint state of the retained checked records,
unprocessed checked ports, and Bob. This holds even conditional on
earlier kept alpha, by applying trace preservation to that block of
the joint positive operator. Inductively the reference is exactly the
circuit that runs each checked interval on its own ports with a fresh
I/D memory, discards its outgoing memory, and ignores all other ports.
Its only input dependence is x_T. Cross-round initial entanglement
between different checked blocks is retained, not replaced by a
product state. Completeness yields the last equality in (M2).

An equivalent Heisenberg description is a product of complete checked-
interval POVMs on disjoint A_i blocks, tensored with identity elsewhere.
The ORIGINAL strategy need not have product or bounded-footprint
effects; even one memory qubit can carry an old input arbitrarily far.
