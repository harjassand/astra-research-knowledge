Actual BG block-local selection law and the operator-gate boundary
==================================================================

Scope
-----
This is an analytic replay of the source law for the coordinate-block game
and a scope check on what an ordinary one-round low-dimension value bound
would imply. It does not prove or audit the separate operator-gate theorem.
The conclusion is a source/interface obstruction: joint referee postselection
is not by itself a complete one-round payoff strategy.

Actual block-local strategy
---------------------------
Let the one-round BG-type game have questions x,y in [k], answers (s,ell)
and (t,ell') in [k]^r x [r], and win predicate

    ell=ell',  s_ell=x,  t_ell=y,  (s,t) in Gamma.

In the r-fold game, Alice receives X=(X_1,...,X_r) and Bob receives
Y=(Y_1,...,Y_r), with all question pairs Z_j=(X_j,Y_j) independent and
uniform on [k]^2. In round j use the deterministic local outputs

    Alice: (s,ell)=(X,j),       Bob: (t,ell')=(Y,j).

The label condition holds. Also s_j=X_j and t_j=Y_j. Thus, pointwise on
every full question pair (X,Y),

    V_j(X,Y) = 1{(X,Y) in Gamma}       for every j=1,...,r.

This is an identity of every winning indicator, not just an equality after
averaging. Consequently, for every nonempty tested set C,

    E_C := {V_j=1 for all j in C} = {(X,Y) in Gamma},
    Pr(E_C)=p=|Gamma|/k^(2r),
    Pr(V_i=0 | E_C)=0 for every live i not in C.

The r-fold strategy is genuinely local and uses no quantum resource, shared
randomness, communication, or graph-membership oracle. The referee's ordinary
round predicates are what produce the common all-round event.

Exact original-history information charge
-----------------------------------------
All logarithms below are natural. Write Q for the original product law on
Z=(Z_1,...,Z_r), and Q_E for its
conditional law given E_C. Since Q is uniform and E_C is precisely Gamma,

    Q_E(z) = Q(z) 1{z in Gamma}/p,
    log(Q_E(z)/Q(z)) = log(1/p) on Q_E-support,
    D(Q_E || Q) = log(1/p).

So any original-history referee charge defined as the relative-entropy cost
of this conditioning is exactly K=log(1/p); it is not zero just because the
conditional live loss vanishes. By chain rule this same quantity is

    log(1/p) = sum_j E_{Q_E} D(Q_E(Z_j | Z_1,...,Z_{j-1}) || uniform([k]^2)).

For the unconditional coordinate marginals nu_j=Q_E(Z_j), convexity of KL
and the chain rule give

    sum_j D(nu_j || uniform([k]^2)) <= D(Q_E || Q) = log(1/p).

Therefore for any subset J of M coordinates,

    (1/M) sum_{j in J} TV(nu_j, uniform([k]^2))
       <= sqrt(log(1/p)/(2M)),

by Pinsker and Jensen. Data processing gives the corresponding bounds for
Alice's and Bob's one-sided question marginals. If the graph-density bound
p >= 1/(2 k^(2c)) from the BG template is in force, then
log(1/p) <= log 2 + 2c log k. With r=k^(1/3), the average drift on any
Theta(r)-sized coordinate set tends to zero. For an arbitrary graph, the
exact bound remains log(1/p); small drift requires log(1/p)=o(M).

Why this does not itself invoke a one-round operator gate
--------------------------------------------------------
The selected object is a joint law on the *whole* histories. Its conditional
factor is

    Q_E(X_-i,Y_-i | X_i=x,Y_i=y)
      proportional to 1{(X,Y) in Gamma}
                     Q(X_-i) Q(Y_-i),

with the appropriate normalizer. This expression is generally a joint
kernel: nothing in the entropy calculation factors it into Alice's local
sampler and Bob's local sampler driven by an input-independent shared
random seed. The referee can condition on the common event Gamma; the two
players receive no message telling them that the event occurred or giving
them a jointly sampled pair of complementary histories.

If one takes the source outputs (X,j),(Y,j) under Q_E, they win with
probability one, but their current coordinate questions are distributed as
Q_E(X_i,Y_i), not necessarily as the original product uniform law. The full
conditional answer table is not automatically a normalized local/quantum
response on fresh independent x,y. It can encode a correlation supplied by
postselection on the two full strings. Small average marginal drift alone
does not provide the missing local response kernels or remove this joint
selection.

Thus a theorem of the form “for typical Gamma, every complete one-round
quantum strategy of local Hilbert dimension d <= k^(1/6-eta) has value at
most 1/2” applies if an extraction actually constructs such a complete
strategy under the original independent uniform input law, with all outcome
branches and resources accounted for. If an extraction produced a strategy
with value greater than 1/2 in that precise model and dimension range, it
would contradict the gate. The conditional source table is not that object,
so its zero conditional loss does not contradict the gate.

Classical randomness/control boundary
-------------------------------------
Free shared classical randomness sampled independently of the questions
does not evade a value bound that is uniform over ordinary one-round
strategies: conditioning on its seed gives a mixture of such strategies,
and a linear payoff cannot exceed their maximum. Arbitrarily large local
answer alphabets and computation are also harmless if each response remains
a legal local response to the current one-round input and allowed
input-independent shared seed; the dimension gate must, of course, be
stated uniformly over those POVMs/response maps.

The full strings X and Y in the repeated source are different. They are
locally available because the actual repeated game gives Alice X and Bob Y,
but after referee conditioning their pair is distributed according to
Q_E. Treating those histories as a pre-shared classical control resource
for a fresh one-round game would import the selected cross-party law as an
extra resource. If a proposed complete extraction retains previous local
questions or classical registers, its interface and distribution must be
specified and charged; a bound only on quantum dimension says nothing by
itself about access to a referee-selected correlated history. Conversely,
independent free shared randomness or larger ordinary response alphabets do
not supply that history.

Consequential scope
-------------------
This source calculation is compatible with low original-history entropy
per live coordinate and zero postselected live loss. It blocks the inference
from those facts alone to a bounded-dimension complete-payoff strategy. To
use a typical-Gamma operator gate, one still needs an explicit local
payoff-preserving construction from the source to an ordinary product-input
strategy, including its failure branches, classical control, and quantum
dimension. No unrestricted repetition no-go or operator-gate failure is
claimed here.
