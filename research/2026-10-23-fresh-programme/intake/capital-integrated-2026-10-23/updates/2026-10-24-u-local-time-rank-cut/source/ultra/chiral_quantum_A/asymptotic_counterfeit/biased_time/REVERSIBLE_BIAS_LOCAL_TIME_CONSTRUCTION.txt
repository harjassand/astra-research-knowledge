1. Operation and scope

Use a qubit chain with iid diagonal source tau_p=diag(1-p,p), for a FIXED
0<p<1/2. Every qubit is data whose classical input/output record is tested;
there is no initialized workspace or discarded syndrome. Let S denote the
mathematical fine right shift. We construct an exact index-one circuit Omega
whose classical record at each output site imitates S with small probability
of mismatch. This does not approximate the shift on arbitrary quantum inputs.

Controls are a fixed, label-independent schedule of adjacent SWAP, CNOT,
controlled-V and controlled-V inverse, with V^2=X. Each pulse has duration
tau=pi hbar/J and a two-qubit generator of norm at most J. Parallel pulses
have disjoint supports. This is a PER-BOND strength promise, not a cap J on
the total Hamiltonian. The same exact generator alphabet is specified in
the frozen LOCAL_CONTROL_TIME_RECORD_FRONTIER source.

For L=3^k there is an explicit circuit with
  D_Omega <= 7(L-1)+8k,
  G_Omega = 4Lk+5(L-1) gates per spatial period,
  R_Omega <= 2L-2 in both directions,
and exact every-site record error
  e_k = 2 epsilon_k(1-epsilon_k),
  epsilon_0=p, epsilon_(j+1)=3epsilon_j^2-2epsilon_j^3.       (1)
Here D is nearest-neighbor parallel depth and G is summed gate count, not
wall time. The construction is periodic in L; one-site translation
invariance of the control programme is not assumed. It works on the line,
or a compatible periodic ring with at least two distinct L-site blocks.

Consequently, for alpha=ln(2)/ln(3) and each fixed p in (0,1/2), constants
c_p,C_p>0 exist such that the best fitting member of this actual schedule
at large available time T has
  max_i Pr[output_i != input_(i-1)]
     <= C_p exp[-c_p (JT/hbar)^alpha].                    (2)
The same bound holds at every site, not only after spatial averaging.
No optimal biased-source exponent, exponential-in-T upper, hardware
performance, or novelty clearance is claimed.

2. Three-bit reversible compression, without clean bits

For a physical triple [x,y,z], define
  P3(x,y,z)=(s1=x XOR y, s2=y XOR z, q=Maj(x,y,z)).
It is bijective. For fixed (s1,s2), the two possible input strings are
complements and have opposite majority. Thus q distinguishes the two.

Chronologically apply CNOT(y,x), CNOT(z,y), CNOT(y,z), then
Toffoli(updated x,updated y;z). The first two gates produce s1,s2;
the last output becomes
  q=z_original XOR s2 XOR (s1 AND s2)=Maj(x,y,z).
The Toffoli controls/target are [x,y;z]. Its standard exact five logical
two-qubit word is
  CV(y,z), CNOT(x,y), CV^-1(y,z), CNOT(x,y), CV(x,z).
The target exponent is y-(x XOR y)+x=2xy, so it is exactly controlled-X,
including relative phases. Only CV(x,z) is nonadjacent. A SWAP route,
the adjacent CV, then the inverse SWAP costs three gates and restores the
passenger. Hence the Toffoli costs seven NN gates and P3 costs ten.

3. Actual ternary layout and schedule

On a parent block of L=3n bits, first encode three contiguous n-bit child
blocks in parallel. Each child root q is at its last wire. Their positions
within the parent are n-1,2n-1,3n-1. Route the middle root to L-2 using
n-1 adjacent swaps, then the first root to L-3 using 2n-2 swaps. Neither
route crosses either other selected root. Apply P3 to the final three wires,
then reverse the routes. All passengers return; the new two syndrome bits
occupy the first two child-root slots and the parent q remains last.

Thus the parent overhead is 2(L-3)+10=2L+4. With D_P(1)=G_P(1)=0,
  D_P(L)=D_P(L/3)+2L+4 = 3(L-1)+4k,
  G_P(L)=3G_P(L/3)+2L+4 = 2Lk+2(L-1).                    (3)
All lower-level blocks are disjoint, so the depth recurrence is genuinely
parallel, while the gate-count recurrence includes all three children.

4. Counterflow compiler: the reference shift is not an actuator

Let P be the product of these encoders on L-site blocks. Let C_left shift
only their final-wire q factors one coarse block left, leaving all syndrome
positions fixed. Let Pi cyclically rotate every physical block right by one
site. Since S=Pi C_right, the formal counterflow expression is exactly
  Omega = S P^-1 C_left P = P_shifted^-1 Pi P,
  P_shifted = S P S^-1.                                  (4)
The RIGHT expression is the actual circuit: P, then Pi, then the inverse
encoder on the partition shifted one fine site. It has three block layers;
Pi uses L-1 adjacent swaps per block. Therefore (3) gives the depth/count
in Section1. No physical global shift or nonlocal q channel is assumed.
The direct forward and inverse cones lie in two neighboring L-site blocks,
yielding strict radius at most 2L-2, as in the frozen encoder-independent
factorization. Every fixed L is a finite-depth, index-one circuit.

For any reversible block encoder, if its q has marginal error epsilon and
distinct blocks are iid, q replacement differs with probability
2epsilon(1-epsilon). Decoding is unchanged whenever those q values agree,
so the MAXIMUM site error is at most this quantity, regardless of the
encoder's syndrome law. Thus a stronger matched reversible-compression
incumbent could replace our encoder. Complement symmetry below makes this
particular bound an equality at every site; it is not necessary for an upper.

5. Exact record law and asymptotics

Fix all recursive syndromes. Toggling a parent root q toggles all three
child roots, by the complementary-pair property of P3. Inductively it
complements every one of the L original bits. Consequently a block after
q countershift and decoding is either unchanged or fully complemented.
Neighboring roots are independent Bernoulli(epsilon_k), because they are
functions of distinct iid input blocks. This proves (1). A root need NOT
be independent of its own syndrome; no such assertion is used.

At a single specified bit label b with source probability p_b in {p,1-p},
  Pr[mismatch AND label=b] <= e_k,
  Pr[mismatch | label=b] <= e_k/p_b <= e_k/p.               (5)
These do not provide uniform guarantees after conditioning on an entire
exponentially rare word, syndrome, or long prior record.

For 0<epsilon<1/2,
  epsilon-phi(epsilon)=epsilon(1-epsilon)(1-2epsilon)>0,
  2epsilon^2 <= phi(epsilon) <= 3epsilon^2.
Thus epsilon_k decreases to zero. The lower inequality gives
  epsilon_k >= (1/2)(2p)^(2^k).
Choose a finite j0(p) for which 3epsilon_j0<1. Iterating the upper inequality
gives, for k>=j0,
  epsilon_k <= (1/3)exp[-c0(p) 2^(k-j0)],
  c0(p)=-ln(3epsilon_j0)>0.                                (6)
Hence -ln(epsilon_k)=Theta_p(2^k)=Theta_p(L^alpha). Since
epsilon_k <= e_k <= 2epsilon_k, the same order holds for record error.
The largest L=3^k fitting tau[7(L-1)+8k]<=T has L=Theta(JT/hbar), proving
(2). The constants are not uniform as p approaches1/2; at p=1/2 this
particular encoder has epsilon_k=1/2 and e_k=1/2 for all k.

6. Full resources, source, and work boundary

The length-L source has entropy L h(p) nats and entropy deficit
L[ln2-h(p)] relative to the tracial source. This describes an input state
resource; the unitary preserves total entropy and merely redistributes it.
All L-1 dirty syndrome bits remain tested. No reset, initialized scratch,
postselection, measurement-based feedback, or hidden output discard is
used. Source acquisition/preparation and classical tag/readout storage are
not included in the gate-time bound. The product source generally becomes
correlated; no stationary repeated-cycle, global-record-TV or coherent
channel-equivalence claim follows.

Footprint and strict radius grow linearly in L. The schedule is fixed
independently of sampled labels. Its total pulse action is O(hbar L log L)
per period, while its wall time is O(L hbar/J); parallel total norm may grow
with the number of simultaneously addressed bonds. The control programme,
clock, energy bandwidth, physical addressability and switching are explicit
resources, not acquired hardware capabilities.

For a cyclic operation on a compatible finite ring, take the source as the
Gibbs state of H0=Delta sum_i |1><1|_i, with
Delta=kBT ln[(1-p)/p]. Passivity and reference-shift energy conservation give
  0 <= W/N <= Delta e_k.                                  (7)
The upper bound follows pointwise by bounding each occupation change from
the reference-shifted string by its mismatch indicator. An explicit control
convention is to quench H0 off during the fixed-gate programme and restore
it at the end, or supply declared onsite cancellation. Quench/restoration
are included in endpoint NET work; they do not make peak borrowed energy,
finite switching time, controller loss, source preparation, or reset free.
If finite onsite switching time is required in this ideal control model,
use one duration-tau diagonal ramp H(t)=f(t)H0 at each end, f:1->0 then
0->1. This adds2tau, with onsite norm at most Delta (Delta/2 after a scalar
shift), and only diagonal endpoint phases. Computational records, radius,
index and the work bound are unchanged. It assumes independent onsite-field
control; it is not an acquisition of apparatus slew or controller losses.
Equation(7) is not a bound on total action, dissipation, or full-cycle cost.

7. Scientific status and strongest-incumbent gate

The recursive three-bit operation is established reversible polarization
compression/algorithmic cooling. The scientific content here is its explicit
finite-alphabet NN counterflow realization and resulting source-dependent
record/time law at the stated interface. The earlier tracial Theta1/T
frontier is not contradicted: its source is different.

Kaye's primary "Cooling algorithms based on the 3-bit majority" (2007),
https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0703194v1, Sections1,3.2,4.1, gives the same
closed reversible majority compression, its bias recursion and odd-block
majority optimality. Section4.1 explicitly leaves shuttling/model time to be
specified. Our exact NN schedule supplies that cost; it does not improve the
underlying compressor. Heat-bath schemes in Section4.2 add reset operations
outside this contract. Main agent independently read these source passages.

Schulman--Vazirani, "Scalable NMR Quantum Computation" (1998 preprint),
https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/9804060v1, is a stronger no-clean-workspace
yield incumbent. Its Phases2--3 give polynomial dirty-bit guarantees; the
architecture section improves tape-model time using two tapes/QCA primitives.
Those claims are not a compilation into this fixed NN schedule, and the
stated polynomial error guarantee does not beat (2). It produces many nearly
pure bits, a different objective on which this one-root construction claims
no advantage. Main agent independently read the phase and architecture text.

These two primary checks do not establish a faster matched construction or
an optimality/no-go theorem. An efficient local larger-block majority or
source compressor remains the strongest open adversary. Any such stronger
no-clean, no-reset encoder with a verified faster NN schedule can be inserted
using Section4, without complement symmetry. The complete gate/action/source
ledger does not price acquisition, noise correction or source replenishment;
there is no practical/hardware advantage claim.
