1. Contract and result
----------------------
Consider qubit data A on the infinite chain, or a compatible large periodic
family. An optional fixed-dimensional B rail is stationary in the target.
Input is PRODUCT TRACE on all tested data and its backward causal window.
Record error at site i is the total mismatch between output (A_i,B_i) and
the input pair (A_(i-1),B_i) of the partial right-shift reference.

The data operation is a predetermined time-dependent nearest-neighbor
Hamiltonian of duration T with each bond term's operator norm at most J.
Onsite terms may be included; they can be removed by product interaction
pictures in the lower proof. No unbounded bath/control register coupled
to all data is hidden in this closed contract. The audited fixed dynamics
is independent of sampled input labels. Let u=JT/hbar.

For sufficiently large available spatial windows, the optimal worst-site
error and the appropriate O(1+u)-site collar means have order
  Theta(1/(1+JT/hbar))                                      (1)
at fixed local dimension. The upper is spatially periodic and explicit;
no one-site-TI upper or autonomous clock is claimed. The lower permits
soft Hamiltonian tails and does not assign an exact QCA index to them.
T counts the controlled data evolution; preparation, readout, controller
reset, and external hardware dissipation are separate resources.

2. Uniform headers distributed on a binary tree
------------------------------------------------
Let M=2^k payload bits and M-1 header bits, L=2M-1. Recursively lay out
each block as [left subtree, root header, right subtree]. All L bits are
iid fair source bits, and ALL are part of the tested A translation target.
Headers are not pure workspace or untested static auxiliaries.

Define an encoder P recursively. Encode both child subtrees in parallel,
each returning its selected payload q to its last physical wire. Controlled
by the root header c, Fredkin-swap the two child q values. The right child's
last wire, also the parent's last wire, is now the parent's selected q.
If c=1 it selects left, otherwise right. Headers never change under P.
Each payload is chosen with probability 1/M because its k path headers
are independent fair bits. For every fixed header configuration, P is an
ordinary reversible permutation of payload wires, with no extra register.

The frozen counterflow identity is implemented as actual block layers:
  Omega=P_+^-1 Pi P,                                        (2)
where Pi cyclically rotates the L original block wires right by one and
P_+ is the same encoder on blocks translated one fine site right. This
equals S P^-1 C_left P, with C_left a PROOF shift of the selected q wires.
No physical global shift or high-arity block gate is supplied in the
implementation below. Its forward/inverse QCA radii are at most 2L-2.

In the S-co-moving description, the block retains its own header values
and replaces q by the independent neighboring uniform q. Inverse decoding
changes only the selected payload, with mismatch probability 1/2. Thus
  maximum site error=1/(2M), period mean=1/(2L).              (3)
Every header has zero error in transported-input coordinates; the final
fine shift rotates that pattern in fixed output coordinates. The maximum
and mean are unchanged. This is a source-average statement at each site,
not a worst-case-over-input guarantee or a full multitime record claim.

3. Explicit local gates and their Hamiltonians
------------------------------------------------
Allow the following fixed gates on adjacent qubits, in either orientation:
SWAP, CNOT, controlled-V and its inverse, where V^2=X EXACTLY. Let
P_ct=|1><1|_control tensor |-><-|_target and tau=pi hbar/J.
The fixed two-qubit generators
  H_SWAP=(J/2)(I-SWAP),
  H_CNOT=J P_ct,
  H_CV=-(J/2)P_ct, H_for_CV_inverse=+(J/2)P_ct               (4)
implement those gates exactly after time tau. For V, the X=+1 eigenstate
has eigenvalue1 and the X=-1 eigenstate eigenvalue i, so V^2=X. Generator
norms are J,J,J/2,J/2. These specified controls are the mathematical
physical contract; their acquisition on a particular platform is unproved.

Fredkin(c,a,b) is the chronological sequence
  CNOT(a,b), Toffoli(c,b;a), CNOT(a,b).
An exact Toffoli implementation is the chronological sequence
  CV(b,a), CNOT(c,b), CV_inverse(b,a), CNOT(c,b), CV(c,a).
Its target V exponent is b-(b XOR c)+c=2bc, so it has no control-dependent
phase. In physical order [a,c,b], four of the Fredkin's logical pair gates
act on the outer pair. Implement each by SWAP-route, adjacent pair gate,
SWAP-unroute, restoring the middle passenger. Three other gates are already
adjacent. Thus Fredkin costs at most 4*3+3=15 NN gates, no ancilla.

At a parent with M leaves, child blocks each have length M-1. The left q,
header, right q are at indices M-2,M-1,2M-2. Route right q left to M in
M-2 adjacent SWAPs, apply the local Fredkin, then undo that route. All
intervening passengers return to their original locations. This costs
2(M-2)+15=2M+11 NN time layers. Parents at a given tree level occupy
disjoint intervals and operate in parallel. Therefore
  D_P(M)<=D_P(M/2)+2M+11
        =4(M-1)+11 log2 M, D_P(1)=0.
The rotation Pi takes L-1 adjacent SWAPs. Equation (2), with inverse
encoder on disjoint shifted blocks, has depth and duration bounded by
  D_Omega<=10(M-1)+22 log2 M,
  T_M<=tau [10(M-1)+22 log2 M].                              (5)
All gates and the schedule are fixed before the source is sampled; header
values control quantum gates rather than an outside label-reading program.
Fixed smooth pulse shapes can replace rectangular pulses with a constant
time overhead (the same generator commutes through each amplitude ramp).
No M-dependent angle or growing-precision pulse is required.

Taking the largest fitting power-of-two M in (5) gives maximum error
O(hbar/(JT)) for large time budgets; idle evolution pads unused time.
This is actual local time, not a conversion of free block-gate radius.

4. Matching lower bound with the existing Hamiltonian approximant
----------------------------------------------------------------
Use the frozen bath-free finite-patch construction at
  ell=ceil[100(1+u)], z=6u, W=[-8ell,24ell-1].
Prepare product trace on W, with tags isolated from the fixed dynamics.
The existing actual-Hamiltonian/circuit comparison gives an index-one
two-layer circuit C, identity outside W, with
  R_C=4ell-2, r=R_C+1=4ell-1,
  ||Omega_T(P)-C(P)||<=eta<=4 F_ell(z),
for every tested onsite full-cell rank-one basis projector P, where
F_ell(z)=sum_(n>=ell) z^n/n!. The original finite-patch proof pays the
two physical end bonds, so exterior dynamics/correlations do not require
an infinite controlled input state. The necessary window W must fit.

The rank theorem applied to C's tracial Choi state, NEVER an index of the
actual soft-tailed dynamics, forces collar mean >=c_a/(2r)>=c_a/(8ell),
where a=dA^2 and c_a=[sqrt(3-2/a)-1]^2>1/4 for dA>=2. Use the central
length-2r output collar inside the already certified test band.

The transfer concerns TOTAL mismatch, not a per-label joint mass. With
orthogonal reference labels s and onsite output projectors P_s, its equality
effect is sum_s Pi_tag,s tensor Omega(P_s). The difference from the C effect
is block diagonal in the tags, so its norm is at most max_s eta=eta. Equivalently
each positive conditional tag block contributes at most eta times its
probability, and the probabilities sum to1. Thus total mismatch at each
site, and its collar mean, differ by at most eta, without a dimension factor.

Since z/ell<=.06,
  F_ell(z)<=e^z (ez/ell)^ell<=e^(-ell).
This is a coarse analytical choice, not an optimized constant: e<3 and
e^(.06)<=1/(1-.06) imply e^(1+.06)*(.06e)<27/47<1.
For ell>=100, 4e^(-ell)<=24/ell^3<=1/(64ell)<c_a/(16ell).
Consequently actual collar mean and maximum satisfy
  error>=c_a/(16ell)>=c_a/[1616(1+JT/hbar)].                 (6)
An audited finite bath envelope adds its explicit nu error budget and must
fit this margin. Unknown noise does not inherit the index or (6) for free.

5. Complete control resources and strongest comparisons
--------------------------------------------------------
The total gate count per block, unlike the parallel depth, is
  G_P=2M log2 M+11(M-1),
  G_Omega=4M log2 M+24(M-1).                                 (7)
Aggregate local pulse action is O(M logM hbar) per block, and peak total
Hamiltonian norm can grow O(MJ). The contract bounds EACH bond by J,
not global ||H|| by J. The source footprint, periodic spatial addressing,
control program, gate count and clocks all grow. It is not an autonomous
fixed-size catalyst. A compatible finite ring requires L divides its size;
the lower witness also needs a sufficiently large nonwrapping causal window.

The binary reversible mux/Fredkin tree and SWAP routing are conventional
ingredients. No gate-count or hardware advantage over those is established.
If an EXTERNAL shared random program and a data-only mixture of evolutions
are allowed, an even simpler comparator randomly shifts the origin of
ordinary length-L cyclic block rotations. Each realization uses O(L) NN
time; averaging the origin gives per-site mismatch1/(2L). It already
matches the scaling for that broader classical-record objective. Its random
program label, distribution, spatial coordination and reset are additional
control resources; it is not the one fixed coherent data unitary (2).

Here every header is a tested data bit and the one coherent program has
the stated per-site errors under the known source. The value is a matching
time frontier in that contract, not a claim to outperform the random-program
comparator or ordinary reversible computation.

Product-tracial data remains tracial under a closed unitary, but prior tag
correlations do not reset. Record capacity, preparation and conditional
reset/readout remain paid; no all-time record equivalence follows. Ideal
net cyclic energy work on maximally mixed isolated data can vanish, while
the controller, switching losses, clock and refrigeration costs are outside
that ideal equality. No full-cycle thermodynamic efficiency is inferred.
