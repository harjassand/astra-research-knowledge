Scientific reason

Three-bit primitive
P3 maps (x,y,z) to (s1=x XOR y,s2=y XOR z,q=Maj(x,y,z)). It is a bijection;
fixed syndromes have two complementary inverse strings, opposite majority.
An exact chronological circuit is
  CNOT(y,x), CNOT(z,y), CNOT(y,z), Toffoli(x,y;z),              (1)
where the last controls are the UPDATED s1,s2. After the first two gates,
q=z XOR s2 XOR(s1*s2)=Maj(x,y,z). With physical order[x,y,z], Toffoli
costs7NN two-qubit gates using the already specified CV/CNOT alphabet;
only its final CV(x,z) is an outer-pair gate requiring route/unroute.
Thus P3 costs10 fixed NN gates, no ancillary qubit, no variable angle.

Recursive encoder and actual layout
Let L=3^k input bits iid Bernoulli(p), 0<p<1/2 FIXED, all part of testedA.
Encode three contiguous child blocks in parallel, each with q at its LAST
wire. Their q positions are n-1,2n-1,3n-1, n=L/3. Move middle q right to
L-2 in n-1 adjacent swaps, then first q right toL-3 in2n-2 swaps. The third
q is already last. Apply P3 on the last three wires and undo the routing.
All other passengers return. Two new syndrome bits remain in the first
two child-root positions; the parent q occupies its last wire.
Parent overhead2(L-3)+10=2L+4 NN gates/time layers. Hence
  D_P(L)<=D_P(L/3)+2L+4=3(L-1)+4k.
Use exact old Omega=P_shifted^-1 Pi_block P. It has
  D_Omega<=7(L-1)+8k, T<=[pi hbar/J]D_Omega,                  (2)
under the frozen fixed SWAP/CNOT/CV control alphabet, norm<=J perbond.
Forward/inverse strict radius<=2L-2; all routing is actual NN, all programme
timing fixed independently of sampled labels. Total count conjecturally
G_P=2Lk+2(L-1), G_Omega=4Lk+5(L-1), not confused with parallel time.

Source and record mechanism
Root q is recursive ternary majority. Its error probabilities obey EXACTLY
  epsilon0=p, epsilon_(j+1)=3epsilon_j^2-2epsilon_j^3.         (3)
Disjoint blocks give independent q labels. At fixed FULL recursive syndrome,
changing parent q complements all three child q values; induction then
complements ALL L physical bits. Thus after neighboring q countershift and
decode, each site mismatches the reference shift iff adjacent q's differ:
  e_site=2epsilon_k(1-epsilon_k).                            (4)
This remains every-site, not just mean. Conditional single-label errors
are bounded by e_site/p_min, so no fixed positive-probability label hides it.

For any fixed p<1/2 the recursion decreases to0. After a p-dependent finite
j0, 3epsilon_j0<1 and epsilon_(j+1)<=3epsilon_j^2 yields
  epsilon_k<= (1/3)exp[-c_p 2^k].                            (5)
Also epsilon_(j+1)>=2epsilon_j^2 gives
  epsilon_k>= (1/2)(2p)^(2^k).
Therefore -ln epsilon_k=Theta_p(2^k)=Theta_p(L^(ln2/ln3)).
With (2), available finite control time admits an actual source-dependent
upper e_site<=C_p exp[-c'_p(JT/hbar)^(ln2/ln3)], using largest fittingL.
This is superpolynomial versus the uniform1/T frontier. It does NOT claim
optimal biased-time exponent, exponential-in-T performance or a new cooling
algorithm. The mechanism survives without free arbitrary block gates.
