certificate with a nonvacuous full-law adaptive-reuse regime. The universal
unknown-CP-instrument target is **not achieved**. N687's Gaussian compiler

## Actual acquired construction

Legal normalized core preparations, joint-event counts and the supplied
architectural rank bound give exact observable operators H^{-1}H_z. I
search a finite family of virtual gauges S and verify, uniformly over the
count-confidence set, that

    W_z=S^{-1}H^{-1}H_z S >=0,
    c_0=S^{-1}e_1 >=0,
    sum_o 1^T W_(a,o)=1^T.

The virtual vertices may be signed physical combinations. Positivity of
their predictive evolution is certified from counts; it is not supplied
as a latent CP gauge. True legal histories from the reproducible start
therefore have an exact positive controlled-HMM realization. Direct
simplex coupling avoids the additional physical quotient-norm conversion
in the previous coarse operator certificate. Inverse-core sensitivity
still appears explicitly and cannot be discarded.

If the certified joint outcome/next-state column TV is t, the full H-step
transcript error is at most 1-(1-t)^H. With an independent abort probability
delta before every native call, the complete terminal transcript error is

    b=(1-delta)t/[delta+(1-delta)t].

For M adaptive successive invocations retaining the same memory, the
joint transcript error is at most 1-(1-b)^M. This includes all intermediate
outcomes and abort labels and does not assume independent macro outputs.
The bound is sharp from this local information. It does not certify the
physical quantum terminal state, new coherent interventions, or uniform
conditional predictions after every rare history.

## Stronger representation and decisive limits

I extended the verification to q>=r virtual vertices and an explicit right
inverse. For fixed vertices and lifting map, a positive causal lift can be
certified from an observable residual; candidate-lift feasibility is a
linear program with absolute-value auxiliaries. The construction is proved
in OVERCOMPLETE_CONE.md. An exact rational noisy-qubit fixture has minimal
observable rank three, needs at least four positive classical states, and
has a strictly positive four-state lift. Its identities were executed.
The helper independently verified the lift, the legal-core coordinate
translation and a nonzero-residual uncertainty challenge.
This is a mathematical representation test, not a count acquisition or an
implemented general cone-search algorithm.

A finite uniform-expansion simplex scan on the ORIGINAL pinned rank-four
qubit counts fails even raw positivity; its best raw margin is about
-.27914. This is a finite search failure, not an impossibility theorem for
that fixture.

The exact coherent counterfamily is stronger: irrational qubit rotation
plus projective readout has observable rank three, yet no finite positive
HMM can reproduce all futures. Indeed finite Markov response sequences
approach periodic limits, whereas the qubit cosine remains dense on each
arithmetic progression. No finite positive model uniformly approximates
all such final-event probabilities within less than 1/2. This is a
mechanism-specific all-horizon obstruction; signed OOMs, CP realizations,
finite horizons and paid-clock approximation remain available. The
classical/quantum positive-realization separation is established machinery,
not a claimed new discovery; primary references are in
COHERENT_OBSTRUCTION.md.

The helper also supplies a rank-two compatible-process acquisition
obstruction. A rare reachable state reveals an unknown probe bit, but
R starts and A known activation attempts leave worst-case prediction
failure at least (1/2)(1-eta)^R(1-lambda)^A. A conventional herald-and-probe
strategy attains this exponent with at most R+A+1 native calls. It concerns
uniform normalized reachable inputs; unconditional prediction from the
original start can remain useful without learning the rare mode.

## Comparator, remaining obligation, and files

An ordinary controlled positive-HMM/PSR learner with the SAME counts can
use every certificate, gauge, LP and coupling here. That is the strongest
equally informed comparator, and it matches this construction exactly.
For genuinely coherent processes the appropriate ordinary alternatives
remain signed OOM/PSR and CP/HQMM fitting; a finite classical simplex is
not a universal comparator representation.

The hardest remaining obligation is computable, useful **nonpolyhedral
observable confidence geometry** for arbitrary supplied-rank stationary
CP instruments, with search, conditioning, positivity/causality, rare
histories and adaptive reuse charged at actual acquisition budgets.
Neither the positive regime, overcomplete algebra, nor exact obstruction
completes that task.
