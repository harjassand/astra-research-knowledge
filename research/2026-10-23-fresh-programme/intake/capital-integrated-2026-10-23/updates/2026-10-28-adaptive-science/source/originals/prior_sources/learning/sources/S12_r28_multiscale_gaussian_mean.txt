1. TWO-VALUE POSITIVE GAUSSIAN ROTATION RETAINING A HEAT-ACTION CROSS MOMENT

This is a finite two-query positive channel retaining the actual endpoint
values and their Gaussian radial source, rather than replacing them by an
arbitrary unbiased matrix action. It does NOT have a jointly Gaussian law
with the deterministic heat-Hessian cross block. That distinction is essential.
The Gaussian-preservation proof uses only Gaussian seeds, two g values,
and vector arithmetic once any positive scalar radius map is fixed. The
EXACT heat-action cross moment additionally uses the scalar quantile (1.1);
its finite arithmetic implementation is separately qualified below.

Fix acquired c in R^d, s>0, a deterministic unit direction u, and
0<=alpha<=1/L. Write H_s(c)=E Dg(c+sZ), with weak derivative used only
in the proof. Set m=d+2. Generate independent

 Zperp~N(0,I-u u^T), A~N(0,1), P~N(0,I_d), C~N(0,1),
 R=(A^2+||P||^2+C^2)^(1/2) ~ chi_m.

Let F_n denote the CDF of chi_n, and define the deterministic scalar map

 T(r)=F_3^{-1}(F_{m+2}(r)), r>0.                         (1.1)

Define the two queried endpoints and the bounded secant vector

 x_plus=c+s Zperp+s T(R)u,
 x_minus=c+s Zperp-s T(R)u,
 b=[g(x_plus)-g(x_minus)]/[2s T(R)].                    (1.2)

Thus ||b||<=L on every execution. If b!=0 put w=b/||b||,
beta=alpha ||b||, kappa=sqrt(1-beta^2). Return

 A'=kappa A-beta (w dot P),
 P'=P+w[(kappa-1)(w dot P)+beta A],
 C'=C.                                                (1.3)

If b=0 use the identity map. Also define the retained physical anchor

 X'=c+s Zperp+s A'u.                                  (1.4)

EXACT LAW. The pair (X',P') has product law

 N(c,s^2 I_d) tensor N(0,I_d),                         (1.5)

and it is jointly independent of C'. More precisely the entire transformed
(A',P',C') remains a standard (d+2)-dimensional Gaussian independently of
Zperp. Consequently X' is independent of P'.

Proof. Conditional on (R,Zperp), (A,P,C) is uniform on the radius-R sphere
in R^(d+2). Both endpoints, b, w, beta, and kappa depend only on these
conditioning invariants. Equation (1.3) is an orthogonal rotation in the
plane spanned by the A axis and the output w axis, fixing the other axes.
It therefore preserves the conditional sphere measure and its radius.
Integrate the unchanged chi_m radial distribution and Zperp distribution.
The output direction w may depend on ALL coordinates of the two whole-vector
g values. This does not spoil the argument because w is fixed when the
conditional sphere orientation is varied.

EXACT ORIGINAL-SOURCE / NEW-OUTPUT CROSS MOMENT. Let X=c+sZperp+sAu be the
original source. Then

 E[A P']=alpha H_s(c)u,
 E[(X-c)P'^T]=s alpha u[H_s(c)u]^T.                   (1.6)

Proof. Conditional sphere moments are
 E[A P|R,Zperp]=0, E[A^2|R,Zperp]=R^2/m.
Therefore E[A P'|R,Zperp]=alpha (R^2/m)b.
The size-biased radial measure (r^2/m) Law(chi_m)(dr) is EXACTLY
Law(chi_(m+2))(dr), since E R^2=m. Under that latter law T(R)~chi_3.
Thus

 E[A P']=alpha E_{Zperp,Q~chi_3}
      [g(c+sZperp+sQu)-g(c+sZperp-sQu)]/(2sQ).

The R22 chi_3 identity makes this alpha H_s(c)u. Finally
E[P'|R,Zperp]=0, so the perpendicular contribution to E[(X-c)P'^T]
vanishes. This establishes (1.6) without querying a derivative.

Every Gaussian output remains exact even if T is replaced by ANY measurable
positive scalar approximation: orthogonality conditional on the radius is
unchanged. Only the cross-moment identity is perturbed. Exact use of (1.1)
is an analysis-level scalar transform, not an extra oracle for g. For a
finite arithmetic implementation it can be approximated on truncated radial
ranges using chi CDF evaluation and scalar bisection. Because g is Lipschitz,
the secant map b(q) has variation <=2L |q-q'|/min(q,q') for q,q'>0.
Hence away from the explicitly truncated q=0 tail, scalar quantile accuracy
controls the cross-moment error uniformly over g. A complete bit-complexity
certificate for this scalar implementation was not written in this round;
no full hard-theorem conclusion relies on it.

Full resolvent action variant: independently draw rho uniform on [0,b0],
apply the construction with c replaced by rho c and s=sqrt(1-rho^2), then
E[A P']=alpha K_(b0)(c)u/b0. Equivalently choose alpha multiplied by b0
to obtain alpha K_(b0)(c)u, with the same bounded-angle condition. For
b0=1 the zero smoothing endpoint is a null event.

WHY THIS IS NOT THE MISSING COMPLETION. The ORIGINAL pair (X,P') is not
asserted jointly Gaussian. Its cross moment is (1.6), but higher conditional
cumulants can survive. The NEW pair (X',P') is exactly product Gaussian,
but replacing X by X' also removes the useful cross block. Thus one cannot
substitute this channel for a Gaussian pair with covariance H_s(c).
If u is independently uniform on the unit sphere, averaging the cross
matrix gives s alpha H_s(c)/d for conservative g, not s alpha H_s(c).
This rank-one dilution is charged; no dimension-free simultaneous matrix
channel follows merely by randomizing the input direction.

The potentially useful aspect is that the endpoint-conditioned rotation
is positive and exactly preserves a retained Gaussian anchor. It bypasses
the claim that an unbiased action is itself one symmetric random matrix.
Whether these retained transitions can be assembled with a g-value drift
into a quadratic full-law correction remains OPEN.

2. A FROZEN-SOURCE ANTITHETIC FLOW FAILS ON A GENUINE C^{1,1} GRADIENT

The following is a stress test of a tempting alternative to Section 1,
not a test of the exact R22 chi_3 action factory. Its failure is not an
end-to-end lower bound for the hard target.

For k>=1 take the scalar globally 1-Lipschitz conservative field

 g_k(x)=sin(kx)/k,
 F_k(x)=-cos(kx)/k^2.

Its Gaussian mean is zero. Its heat-Hessian mean at c=0,s=1 is
H_1(0)=E cos(kG)=exp(-k^2/2).

Freeze a Gaussian source X~N(0,1), use central-difference radius delta=1/k,
and acquire the following TWO-VALUE map for arbitrary scalar v:

 B_X(v)=[g_k(X+delta v)-g_k(X-delta v)]/(2delta)
       =a sin v,   a=cos(kX).                         (2.1)

For every frozen source it is an odd, globally 1-Lipschitz conservative
map of v. It is therefore stronger structurally than a generic unbiased
nonlinear vector action. Nevertheless it is not an approximately linear
realized matrix action uniformly over C^{1,1} sources.

The tempting positive rotation flow is

 dQ/dt=-B_X(P)=-a sin P,
 dP/dt= B_X(Q)= a sin Q,                              (2.2)

starting from independent standard Gaussian Q,P, independently of X.
This is a complete Hamiltonian flow, preserves ordinary Euclidean area,
and uses the frozen conservative secant. It does not preserve the product
standard Gaussian law. This failure persists as k tends to infinity even
though g_k tends uniformly to zero and H_1(0) tends to zero.

Reconstructible second-order calculation. Write
V=(-sin P)partial_Q+(sin Q)partial_P, so the flow generator is aV.
For f(Q,P)=Q^2,

 V f=-2Q sin P,
 V^2 f=2 sin^2 P-2Q cos P sin Q.

Gaussian integration gives

 E sin^2 P=(1-exp(-2))/2,
 E cos P=exp(-1/2),
 E[Q sin Q]=exp(-1/2),
 c0:=E[V^2 f]=1-exp(-2)-2exp(-1)>0.                 (2.3)

Thus

 E Q_t^2=1+(t^2/2)E[a^2]c0+O(t^4),
 E[a^2]=(1+exp(-2k^2))/2.                            (2.4)

The remainder bound is uniform in k for sufficiently small fixed t:
|a|<=1 and differentiating f along V four times leaves finite sums of
bounded trigonometric factors and at most a linear factor Q or P; the
bounded-velocity flow changes |Q|+|P| by at most 2|t|. Odd Taylor
expectations vanish by Gaussian sign symmetry. Consequently for fixed
sufficiently small t>0,

 liminf_(k->infinity) [E Q_t^2-1]>0.                 (2.5)

This is a genuine full-law discrepancy, not just an inconclusive covariance
test: a probability law equal to N(0,1) would have second moment one.
For a quantitative bounded-test discrepancy one may truncate Q^2 at M;
the bounded velocities give uniform Gaussian tail control, so choose M
large enough to preserve a fixed fraction of the variance gap. That yields
a strictly positive TV lower bound for this particular candidate flow at
the chosen t, uniformly for all sufficiently large k. No target-sampler
lower bound is claimed.

Replacing a by an equiprobable sign, or mixing the time-t and time-minus-t
flows, cancels the odd powers and preserves the positive t^2 term (2.4).
This explains why antithetic time reversal alone does not make this map a
quadratic full-law correction. If both exact forward and backward maps are
COMPOSED on the same source they cancel identically, producing no correction.

INTERPRETATION. A frozen source can have vanishing MEAN Hessian and a
nonvanishing random local Hessian. Finite central secants at any fixed
precision can exhibit nonlinear angular dependence from a rapidly
oscillating, still globally Lipschitz, gradient. Rotating the noise using
that nonlinear action leaves a second-order even-law defect. Merely keeping
the source label available is insufficient; a successful compiler must
use an exactly Gaussian-preserving lift such as Section 1, evolve the
source in a compensating way, or prove a different cancellation.

3. POSITIVE AVERAGING AND MULTISCALE RENORMALIZATION GATES

For clarity, a separate restricted impossibility test concerns deterministic
linear averaging of independent predecessor samples. Suppose X_i are iid
with mean mu, covariance S^2 I, and a nonzero fourth cumulant tensor C4.
Let Y=sum_(i=1)^b a_i X_i+Z, with independent Gaussian Z and sum_i a_i=1
so the unknown mean is preserved. Then

 cumulant_4(Y)=(sum_i a_i^4) C4,
 sum_i a_i^4 >= b^(-3).                              (3.1)

The first identity is exact cumulant additivity, including arbitrary signed
real coefficients. The inequality is Holder applied to sum_i a_i=1.
Gaussian covariance repair changes no fourth cumulants. For fixed branching
b this is linear response to a small C4, rather than squaring its amplitude.
Antithetic source correlations are not covered by this iid restriction.
The statement is not an end-to-end lower bound and does not rule out using
new g-value queries to cancel the fourth-cumulant influence.

A sufficient new result would still be a positive source-aware update whose
controlled discrepancy eta maps to C eta^2, with constant branching or a
uniformly subcritical capped correction forest. Log-logarithmic depth would
then permit polylogarithmic leaves. No such update was found or proved in
this finite round. The rotation in Section 1 is a concrete positive acquired
operation that retains the source; it has only the cross-moment identity
proved above, and cannot be promoted to a quadratic law update by covariance
matching. The frozen-source construction in Section 2 decisively fails.
