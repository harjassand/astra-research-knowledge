**Q52: the coercive inverse is mathematically supported.** Let

\[
\mathcal D=\{S=S^T\in L^2:\delta(SB)\in L^2\},\qquad
u_S=\delta(SB).
\]

Bounded multiplication by B followed by closed Gaussian divergence makes this maximal graph operator closed: if S_n tends to S and delta(S_n B) tends to u in L2, then S_n B tends to SB and closedness gives delta(SB)=u. Smooth compactly supported symmetric fields belong to the domain by the divergence product rule and are dense in symmetric L2. Under the supplied locally C1 B, locally Lipschitz X and bounded-coefficient assumptions, cutoffs and local first-order mollification also justify the stated core/localization route. One should retain those domain hypotheses when quoting the theorem; arbitrary nonsmooth coefficients do not gain a core theorem simply by notation. The maximal-domain formulation itself is sufficient for the variational argument and the directly admissible test below.

The form need not be symmetric. Nevertheless, for symmetric S,

\[
a(S,S)=\|u_S\|_2^2+E\operatorname{tr}(S^2\operatorname{sym}\tau)
\geq\|u_S\|_2^2+c\|S\|_2^2.
\]

The second term is continuous in the graph norm because tau is bounded. Lax–Milgram applies to this real coercive nonsymmetric form. Testing the solution against itself gives

\[
\|u\|_2^2+c\|S\|_2^2\leq r\|S\|_2,
\quad \|S\|_2\leq r/c,
\quad \|u\|_2\leq r/(2\sqrt c).
\]

The last bound is the maximum of rs−cs²; it is not obtained by separately loosening both terms. Global mean preservation follows from u being a divergence. No conditional preservation of the old G is required here. This is why Q17's obstruction to a conditional-in-Z inverse does not refute this inverse.

The full-law proof is also supported. For T=D²f(X), the product rule yields

\[
\delta(TB)=T(X-\mu)-\tau:D^3f(X).
\]

This field is in L2: the Hessian and third derivative are bounded, X−mu is in L2, and tau is bounded. Thus it is a valid test, not an assumed full derivative estimate for u. Combining the variational equation, the divergence identity for u, and the differentiated Stein equation gives exactly

\[
E h(X)+E Dh(X)u-E h(\mu+Z)
=E[R:D^3f(X)[u]].
\]

For an incomplete solve there is the additional E[E0:D²f(X)] term. Taylor expansion occurs in output space and charges the actual implemented correction's second moment. Gaussian smoothing permits every Borel event test. Hermite Bessel bounds give dimension-independent Frobenius derivative constants; the OU integral is

\[
\int_0^1\frac{s^2\,ds}{(1+a^2-s^2)^{3/2}}
=a^{-1}-\arctan(a^{-1}).
\]

Consequently the stated C(a,c)r² bound, and the full residual/bias/randomized-second-moment variants, follow without an unproved Du bound. The earlier challenge's warning about the uncontrolled full horizontal derivative remains relevant to a pointwise matrix recursion, but does not invalidate this later weak one-step theorem. For the initial source r≤sqrt(d) ell², the guarantee is O(d ell⁴), as the final construction correctly states. The reserve changes the target covariance to (1+a²)I and is a real noise resource.

The scalar all-order analytic argument is supported within its explicitly retained closed horizontal calculus. The identity ||V*S||²=||VS||²+E[tau S²] turns the scalar equation into the positive Dirichlet problem. Truncation gives ||S||infinity≤rho/[2(1−rho)]. The exact update is R+=−SR−S²tau and hence rho+≤rho²(3−rho)/[4(1−rho)²]. For rho≤1/4, the positive multipliers 1+S have comparable graph norms and the corrections are summable. The Stein identities pass to the L2 limit, giving the exact scalar normal law. This does not supply a computable inverse, a scalar-field acquisition algorithm, or the matrix commutator estimate.

**Q17: the obstruction is stronger than a failed Neumann proof and has the stated limited consequence.** At X=lambda G+aZ, a²+lambda²=1, the conditional-source restriction u=delta_Z J gives

\[
A J=[a(N_Z+2)+\lambda\delta_Z\partial_G]J=-R.
\]

The +2 is correct. On total chaos n the triangular inverse has coefficients

\[
c_j={(-1)^{j+1}\over a}\left({\lambda\over a}\right)^j
{\sqrt{\binom nj}\over(j+1)(j+2)}.
\]

Thus ||J||≥a^(−n−1)/[(n+1)(n+2)] and ||partial_Z u||≥a^(−n−1)/(n+2). Polynomial weak tests isolate each chaos block, so adding other degrees cannot evade the inverse. The source-only OU threshold rho≤a follows from the exact squared norm, and rho>a is unbounded. That is not a bound on arbitrary joint residuals.

For the bounded smooth Fourier series sum exp(−beta m²)cos(mG), 0<beta<lambda²/(2a²), all even Hermite coefficients have the same sign. The inverse norm therefore dominates each weighted cosine inverse. The exponential lower bound beats exp(−beta m²), proving absence of a weak L2 solution. The independent audit's Poisson/Hermite proof supplies a second valid route. Importantly, the addendum connects this to actual conservative, globally Lipschitz perturbations: for g_eta=lambda id+eta f, the first residual derivative is lambda(f'+K_f), whose Hermite coefficients are multiplied by 1+1/(n+1), with no cancellation. This rules out the claimed unrestricted differentiable L2 pointwise correction interface even on actual admissible first variations. It is not a lower bound for the target sampler, for all nonlinear correction mechanisms, or for the exact operator at nonzero eta. The exact affine law itself has zero residual.

**Q17: the capped near-affine filling is a valid acquired restricted operation.** The extra source promise is a *known scalar affine reference* g=lambda id+h, 0<lambda<1, with a supplied residual Lipschitz bound eta M. The analysis writes h=eta f; eta and f need not be separately evaluated at runtime. This is not the unrestricted N687 promise, and decomposing any g in this way alone does not make the error small.

The capped geometric law has active probabilities p_j=(1−lambda²)lambda^(2j) and tail lambda^(2N). There is no omitted tail mass. At most three whole-vector g values are used, with one on the tail. A run uses four base d-dimensional Gaussian blocks, an active extra d-dimensional block, and at most 3N scalar Gaussian draws for the finite Bernoulli selector. Arithmetic is O(d+N), storage O(d), with inverse weights potentially growing with N. Conditional on G,J,U, exchanging Z and Z' changes D's sign, proving E[O|G]=g(G) exactly. Given X, the shared-U endpoint means telescope:

\[
E[f(G)+D\mid X]=P_{\lambda^{2N+1}}f(X).
\]

The common Gaussian U is essential to the bound ||D||≤M lambda||X−X'||/(1−lambda²). It gives E||D||²≤2M²d lambda²(1−lambda^(2N))/(1−lambda²). The cross term with f(G)−mu_f vanishes by source centering, so the stated variance bound charges all randomized correction noise.
