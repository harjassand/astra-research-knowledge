NATIVE ISING GEOMETRIC LOOP: A REAL THREE-BODY OPERATION, BUT SERVED
=================================================================

Question and result
-------------------
The scalar operator-notch construction can predict a new interaction if a
first-order rotated coupling is treated as independently available. The
actual rotation family contains compulsory second-order curvature that can
cancel that prediction. This note asks whether a different physical loop,
using only native Ising interactions and local fields, retains an actual
three-body operation after the full rotation family is included.

It does. A staggered-axis commutator loop produces a cluster interaction.
However, under exactly the same uniform-Ising and local-control assumptions,
an exact finite Clifford conjugation produces the same operation with constant
native interaction time and pulse count. This is a useful positive control
and a decisive comparator, not a new high-upside capability claim.

1. Actual available Hamiltonian and exact control frame
------------------------------------------------------

Take a bipartite nearest-neighbor Ising chain, with equal couplings J and
fully specified local fields. In the discussion below hbar=1. Any fixed
native transverse field -h sum X must be canceled by an available local
+h sum X control during the primitive evolution intervals. That consumes
field amplitude h and knowledge of the actual drift; it is not automatic.

For each even site e define

 B_e=J(Z_(e-1)+Z_(e+1)),
 H_A=sum_e Z_e B_e.

Local rotations on the even sublattice give the exact family

 H_theta=sum_e [Z_e cos(theta_e)+X_e sin(theta_e)] B_e.

This is the full trigonometric rotation of native Ising. It has not been
replaced by H_A+theta X_e B_e. For example let

 R(t)=product_e exp[+i theta_e(t)Y_e/2],
 H_lab(t)=H_A+i dot(R)R^dagger
         =H_A-sum_e dot(theta_e)Y_e/2.

The exact R frame has Hamiltonian R^dagger H_A R=H_theta; the local inertial
term cancels the supplied physical local-control Hamiltonian. Thus the
family requires native Ising plus local rotations only. The full mandatory
curvature is present. With finite local rotation speed, native evolution
continues during each pulse and must also be included.

At theta=0,pi/2,pi,3pi/2 the available interaction-frame Hamiltonians are
H_A,H_B,-H_A,-H_B, where

 H_B=sum_e X_e B_e.

All different-center star terms commute: centers are even sites, while every
B_e is built exclusively from odd-site Z operators. Neighboring stars can
share an odd Z without violating this commutativity.

2. Exact conditional-spin group commutator
-----------------------------------------

Consider the ordered cycle

 U_loop(tau)=exp(-i tau H_A) exp(-i tau H_B)
               exp(+i tau H_A) exp(+i tau H_B).

It uses four native interaction intervals of length tau and local axis/sign
changes. A continuous sequence of negative pi/2 even-spin rotations can
implement the required four orientations and close the control frame up to
an irrelevant local -I. Total rotation angle per center is2pi per cycle.

Because the stars commute, diagonalize the neighboring Z values and solve
each center exactly. Put b=J(z_left+z_right), u=tau b, c=cos(u),s=sin(u).
Then the center's complete finite cycle is

 U_b=(1-2s^4)I
      -i[(-2cs^3)X+(2c^2s^2)Y+(2cs^3)Z].              (1)

This formula includes every order in tau. It is not a leading Magnus
Hamiltonian assigned to an unknown device. In particular b=0 gives I,
while b=+/-2J gives an exactly specified conditional spin rotation.

The algebraic commutator is

 [H_A,H_B]=2i sum_e Y_e B_e^2
           =4iJ^2 sum_e [Y_e+Z_(e-1)Y_e Z_(e+1)].       (2)

Thus the small-loop exponent is

 -2i tau^2 sum_e Y_e B_e^2
 =-4iJ^2tau^2 sum_e [Y_e+Z_(e-1)Y_eZ_(e+1)].

A known local Y phase removes the single-site part. The remaining operation
is a three-body cluster phase, a genuine operation beyond a free wait under
native ZZ plus a final product unitary. It can affect string correlations
and stabilizer dynamics. But it does not lie beyond what the SAME controlled
Ising system can already synthesize.

For a desired cluster phase Phi and N cycles choose

     4 N J^2 tau^2=Phi.

The native interaction time is4N tau=Phi/(J^2 tau), and the local rotation
angle is2pi N plus the final2Phi compensation. The conditional X/Z axis
components in (1) are O(Jtau) relative to Y; after a fixed total phase they
give O(Jtau) coherent error. Reducing tau reduces this error but increases
duration and pulse count. At fixed phase N grows as tau^-2.

The construction generalizes algebraically to a bipartite weighted Ising
graph, B_e=sum_j J_ej Z_j. It generates Y_e B_e^2, hence weighted pair terms
among neighbors conditional on the center. This can be understood as a
spin-bus graph transformation. It is not a free uniform thermodynamic claim:
||B_e||<=sum_j |J_ej| grows with unnormalized degree, and pulse/error bounds
must use that local coupling sum. Oscillator-bus geometric gates already
provide a strong, well-established comparator for collective quadratic
couplings.

3. Stronger exact comparator with identical primitives
------------------------------------------------------

Let

 Q=exp[-i(pi/4) sum_edges Z_i Z_j].

For a bulk site with two neighbors, direct Pauli conjugation gives

 Q Y_e Q^dagger=-Z_(e-1)Y_eZ_(e+1).                    (3)

Therefore

 Q exp[+i Phi sum_even Y_e] Q^dagger
   =exp[-i Phi sum_even Z_(e-1)Y_eZ_(e+1)]             (4)

EXACTLY for every Phi. It uses native Ising evolution for pi/(4|J|) on
either side of a local Y rotation. The inverse native interval is implemented
by pi flips on one bipartite sublattice, which reverse all ZZ bonds. No
direct XX primitive and no direct three-body primitive have been granted.

The native interaction exposure is pi/(2|J|), independent of the small-loop
parameter and system size. The ideal local-control angle per center is
2pi+2|Phi|. Coupling equality and calibration are the same resources already
assumed by the loop comparison. Known unequal couplings require separate
refocusing or tailored interaction areas; a uniform-pulse equality must not
be presumed for an arbitrary graph.

This comparator is stronger than merely observing that Trotter simulation
can eventually reproduce the loop. It is an exact finite synthesis of its
declared target with the same native interactions and local fields.

4. Executable finite-cycle comparison
------------------------------------

manybody_geometric_cycle.py evaluates the full3-spin propagators and checks
the parallel-star relation in a5-spin chain. For J=1,Phi=pi/8:

 N cycles   native interaction time    operator-norm error to target
   25          6.266570687               .1243342439
  100         12.533141373               .0625398131
  400         25.066282746               .0313170772
 1600         50.132565493               .0156644534

The exact Clifford comparator uses time pi/2=1.570796327. Its numerical
error is4.69e-17 in ordinary double arithmetic; the exact algebraic identity
(4) is the reason this is zero mathematically. The finite-cycle conditional
formula (1) agrees with direct matrix exponentials to floating precision.

5. Finite local-field strength and thermodynamic scope
-----------------------------------------------------

If the maximum available local Rabi frequency is Omega, a control rotation
of angle alpha takes at least|alpha|/Omega. During pulses the native Ising
acts. On one degree-two star ||Z_e B_e||<=2|J|. A direct Duhamel bound charges
at most2|J| times the total pulse duration for the neglected native drift.
Thus the loop's per-center primitive bound includes

       4pi N |J|/Omega,

before the final correction, while the comparator has a constant number
of pulses and a corresponding O(|J|/Omega) allowance. The actual finite-pulse
Hamiltonian can be propagated or dynamically corrected, but cannot be
replaced by instantaneous gates without charging the interface.

For M independently controlled stars a global operator-norm triangle bound
can scale as M. No volume-independent global-unitary distance is established
by a finite-star result. Local observables in this commuting-star structure
involve only finitely many relevant centers, so their direct local error
bounds can remain independent of total chain length at bounded degree.
That does not imply indefinite heating suppression or uniform accuracy for
arbitrary many-body inputs under additional noncommuting drift/noise.

6. Prior constructions and scientific decision
---------------------------------------------

Primary sources already establish broad versions of the sought capability:

* Lee, Joglekar and Richerme, Phys. Rev. A94,023610(2016),
  "String order via Floquet interactions in atomic systems",
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.05738
  studies driven transverse-field Ising models with effective cluster-like
  three-body interactions in1D and higher-body interactions in higherD.

* Katz, Cetina and Monroe, PRX Quantum4,030311(2023),
  "Programmable N-Body Interactions with Trapped Ions",
  https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/abstract/10.1103/PRXQuantum.4.030311
  constructs programmable higher-body interactions using spin-dependent
  parametric drives and collective motional control, accounting for multiple
  motional modes. This is a different physical resource set, but a strong
  capability comparator rather than an absent field.

* Ekert etal., J.Mod.Opt47,2501(2000),
  "Geometric Quantum Computation",
  https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0004015
  establishes conditional geometric phases with controlled spins.

The useful finding is the physical curvature/comparator audit: a real loop
can retain a genuine higher-body operation, but doing so does not rescue a
novel-capability claim when the exact same primitive controls supply a
shorter exact synthesis. The uniform-Ising cluster phase should be preserved
as research capital and a falsification tool, not promoted as the larger
scientific payoff of the source-shaped open-continuum program.
