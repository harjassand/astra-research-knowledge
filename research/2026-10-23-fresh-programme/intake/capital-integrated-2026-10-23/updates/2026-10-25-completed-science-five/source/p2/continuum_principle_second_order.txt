SECOND-ORDER CONTINUUM SOURCES DO NOT REQUIRE AN EXACT DIVIDED VECTOR
==================================================================

25 October 2026. Independent critical continuation of the normalizable-
dressing construction. This is an elementary finite-time residual theorem,
not a claim to have discovered vanishing-Fermi-Golden-Rule metastability.

1. Precise coherent-code statement
--------------------------------

Let H(g)=H0+g V+g^2 W be self-adjoint for the admitted real g. P:C^d -> H is
an isometry, H0 P=E P, and P^dagger V P=0. Assume a finite-rank map U1 with
P^dagger U1=0 satisfies

 (H0-E)U1=-VP.                                           (1)

The columns must lie in the domains needed below. Define

 K=P^dagger(VU1+WP),
 S=VU1+WP-PK.                                           (2)

For self-adjoint V,W and exact (1),

 K=-U1^dagger(H0-E)U1+P^dagger W P=K^dagger.

P^dagger S=0. Put A=H0-E and, for delta>0,

 Pi_delta=1_{[-delta,delta]}(A),
 U2_delta=-A^(-1)(I-Pi_delta)S.                           (3)

This is an actual bounded spectral multiplier applied to finite source
columns. It is not a physical energy filter. Assume its columns belong to
D(V) intersect D(W); membership in D(H0) follows directly from (3).

For J=P+gU1+g^2 U2_delta, exact multiplication gives

 H(g)J-J(EI+g^2K)
   =g^2 Pi_delta S
    +g^3(VU2_delta+WU1-U1K)
    +g^4(WU2_delta-U2_delta K).                         (4)

Hence at T=hbar tau/g^2, uniformly 0<=t<=T,

 ||exp(-iH(g)t/hbar)P-P exp[-i(EI+g^2K)t/hbar]||
 <=2[|g| ||U1||+g^2||U2_delta||]
   +tau[||Pi_delta S||
         +|g| ||VU2_delta+WU1-U1K||
         +g^2||WU2_delta-U2_delta K||].                 (5)

One may combine orthogonal contributions in quadrature when proved, but the
sum bound (5) is general. This is the full output-isometry norm, so an
arbitrary entangled reference is included. Outside-code probability <=epsilon^2
and unhalved diamond distance <=2epsilon. All escaped amplitudes remain data.

2. A gap-free sufficient condition at second order
-------------------------------------------------

Assume V and W are bounded for the simplest version. If

 1_{ {E} }(H0) S=0,                                    (6)

then ||Pi_delta S|| ->0 as delta->0. Strong convergence suffices because S
has finite-dimensional domain. Taking delta=|g|^alpha with any 0<alpha<1
gives ||U2_delta||<=||S||/delta and makes every other term in (5) vanish.
Thus the finite code operation exp(-i tau K) is obtained with error o(1),
even when the source S has NONZERO continuous spectral density at E and the
formal exact second-order vector -A^(-1)S is nonnormalizable.

For relatively H0-bounded V or W, assume the graph estimate actually needed,
e.g. ||Vx||<=a||Ax||+b||x||, likewise W. Then
||VU2_delta||<=a||S||+b||U2_delta||, and the same conclusion follows.
For arbitrary unbounded couplings, a qualitative diagonal choice of delta(g)
still works if every cutoff column has finite V/W actions. That domain fact
is a substantive interface, not supplied by spectral continuity alone.

This closes a distinction that an all-orders BIC construction can obscure:
an order-g^2 source can resonantly radiate, but on time g^-2 its spectral-window
mass vanishes. It need not be cancelled as strongly as an order-g source.

3. Rates and the sharp conceptual failure
----------------------------------------

Suppose for |x|<x0 the matrix spectral density of S obeys

 S^dagger d1_{(-infty,x]}(A) S <= C |x|^nu dx I_d,
 with -1<nu<1.

Then ||Pi_delta S||=O(delta^[(nu+1)/2]) and
||U2_delta||=O(delta^[(nu-1)/2]). For bounded or graph-bounded V,W, choosing
delta proportional to |g| gives a sufficient error O(|g|^[(nu+1)/2]).
The regular-density case nu=0 therefore permits an O(sqrt(|g|)) full-state
certificate; this is conservative and is not an asserted physical decay law.
For nu=1 the corresponding estimate is O(g sqrt(log(1/g))); nu>1 gives O(g).

If instead S has a resonant POINT projection outside the admitted code, the
window term converges to that nonzero vector. It represents a coherent
order-g^2 Raman/degenerate transition acting for time g^-2. Reducing g does
not remove it. Enlarge the effective degenerate code to include the reachable
point spectrum and compute its full Hermitian K, or change the physical
coupling. If that point eigenspace is infinite and accessible, no finite code
closure follows without an additional invariant-subspace proof.

Thus low discrete resonances and open continuous resonances must be treated
differently. Declaring both 'resonant' and discarding both would be wrong.

4. First-order source defects are not repaired by this theorem
------------------------------------------------------------

If instead of (1) one has AU1=-VP+R1, the residual includes g R1. A
self-adjoint target K is needed; P^dagger R1=0 alone does not guarantee K
Hermitian, since the identity acquires U1^dagger R1. Use exact or separately
symmetrized construction, and retain its anti-Hermitian remainder in S.
With a declared Hermitian K, the extra finite-time allowance is

 tau ||R1||/|g|.                                       (7)

This is the actual status of tiny but nonzero high-energy first-order
windows in a finite-width Maxwell device. An extremely small tail can yield
a useful finite-g bound, but it cannot be relabeled exact asymptotically
lossless control as g->0. Under ordinary limiting-absorption hypotheses, a
nonzero on-shell first-order source density instead yields a finite loss
exponent on the g^-2 scale. A declared lossy instrument may remain useful.

5. Floquet evaluation is an additional interface
-----------------------------------------------

For H(t) periodic, the Sambe operator H0-i hbar d/dt is useful for algebra,
but L2([0,T_drive];H) norm does not bound evaluation at a physical time.
Do NOT substitute a Sambe norm directly for a propagator error.

Write a trial J(t)=sum_l J_l exp(i l omega t) with absolutely convergent
operator-norm Fourier sum. Then

 sup_t ||J(t)-P|| <=sum_l ||(J-P)_l||,
 sup_t ||R(t)|| <=sum_l ||R_l||.

For support on N Fourier harmonics, the sums are <=sqrt(N) times the
corresponding l2 sums of operator norms (or a Hilbert-Schmidt column bound).
The physical Duhamel estimate is 2 sup_t||J(t)-P|| plus the integral of
||R(t)||/hbar. Finite Fourier support through order two is available for a
finite-tone magnetic field and its quadratic Stark term, provided H0 does
not itself mix infinitely many Fourier sectors. Infinite physical transverse
and radial states remain fully present inside each coefficient.

6. Exact bounded toy realization with a divergent second-order vector
---------------------------------------------------------------------

H=C|n> direct-sum C|d> direct-sum L2([-1,1])_B direct-sum L2([-1,1])_C.
The declared dark reference |d> has H0|d>=0 and V|d>=0. H0 is also zero on n
and multiplication by x on both continuous sectors. Let

 f(x)=sqrt(3/8)(1+x),  v(x)=x f(x),
 V=|v_B><n|+|n><v_B|+I_BC+I_CB.

V is bounded with ||V||=sqrt(7/5). On the bright column, the first divided
source is U1=-f_B, K=-1/2, and S=-f_C; all dark columns vanish. The two-state
code operation has K=diag(-1/2,0), so the phase is observable relative to the
declared reference. No normalizable U2 exists because f(0) is nonzero.
Nevertheless U2_delta=f_C/x for |x|>delta, zero otherwise, is admissible.
For 0<delta<=1, exactly

 ||U1||^2=1,
 b^2:=||U2_delta||^2=(3/4)(delta^(-1)-delta),
 d^2:=||Pi_delta S||^2=(3/4)delta+(1/4)delta^3,
 r^2:=||VU2_delta-U1K||^2=(3/4)(delta^(-1)+delta)-5/4.

All three residual pieces have mutually orthogonal support, so a stronger
explicit certificate is

 epsilon<=2 sqrt(g^2+g^4 b^2)
          +tau sqrt(d^2+g^2 r^2+g^4 b^2/4).             (8)

With delta=|g| this is O(sqrt(|g|)), for an order-one phase. The accompanying
script uses exact rational arithmetic for b^2,d^2,r^2, checks the polynomial
integrals, and gives outward-rounded floating evaluations. It discretizes
neither continuum. This supplies a genuine counterexample to the claim
that the second divided vector must exist for the operation to survive.

Diagonalizing B/C gives continua x+g and x-g, each coupled by g v/sqrt(2).
At the bare energy their form factors are O(g), so the leading radiative
width is order g^4 rather than g^2. This agrees with established metastability
theory. It is not a novel continuum-protection mechanism by itself.

7. Strongest equally informed alternative and the novelty boundary
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Ordinary gapped off-resonant control supplies the same exp(-i tau K) operation
with simpler denominators. The previous positive-detuning Morse calculation
already beats the generic signed-phase benchmark when detuning and phase
orientation are freely changeable. The continuum construction needs value
from an acquired control constraint or an inaccessible effective interaction,
not from merely calling its phase 'gapless'.

Likewise, arbitrary finite-rank source synthesis trivially makes arbitrary
Hermitian K possible (up to a global shift): choose normalizable bath vectors
u_j with disjoint energy support, prescribe VP=-(H0-E)U1, and use
K=-U1^dagger(H0-E)U1. This only restates a signed Gram factorization. It does
not acquire a local electromagnetic source, independently oriented code
couplings, reset, or an interaction inaccessible to the comparator. A bath
whose accessible spectrum lies entirely on one side of E already realizes
any finite Hermitian target after a scalar shift. Openness itself confers no
universal-control advantage.

Primary prior that directly constrains the claim:

 * H.D. Cornean, A. Jensen, G. Nenciu, 'Metastable states when the Fermi
   Golden Rule constant vanishes' (2014), arXiv:1311.7502.
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1311.7502
   Under specified resolvent smoothness and bounded perturbation assumptions,
   they construct the first-order Rayleigh-Schrodinger metastable state for
   an embedded nondegenerate eigenvalue and obtain decay/error order epsilon^4.
   Proposition2.11 directly relates the on-shell zero to existence of the
   first divided vector; Theorem2.15 is stronger than a generic claim of
   finite phase before higher-order decay. They explicitly distinguish the
   more complicated degenerate case.

 * J. Derezinski and R. Fruboes, 'Fermi Golden Rule and open quantum systems',
   expanded 2003 lectures, https://www.fuw.edu.pl/~derezins/davies.pdf
   Section3 gives rigorous stationary and time-dependent Davies weak-coupling
   limits with the level-shift operator. A Hermitian limiting level shift is
   the expected no-decay special case, not a new physical principle here.

 * Yue Chang, 'Coherent Control of an Embedded Bound State Without a Spectral
   Gap', preprint2026, https://arxiv.org/html/2606.17685v1
   An existing giant-atom BIC is accessed by frequency control and its
   composition changed by coupling ramps; the stated leakage is linear in
   ramp rate. This is a different control protocol but a strong alternative
   for the broad goal of useful control in an open continuum.

What this continuation contributes to the live construction is narrower and
useful: a full-code finite-time residual route that includes direct g^2
electric/Stark terms; it relaxes the second-order normalizability requirement
to absence of a reachable resonant point component, and identifies the actual
remaining first-order, domain and physical-time evaluation interfaces. Its
potential engineering value depends on obtaining those interfaces for a
consequential interaction. Historical novelty and device superiority remain
unestablished.

8. A second-order physical completion can cancel the entire operation
--------------------------------------------------------------------

There is an exact counterexample to inferring a useful control operation from
the first-order divided-source condition alone. Let B be a self-adjoint
generator with all necessary commutator domains, and consider the actual
Hamiltonian family

 H(g)=exp(-igB) H0 exp(igB).

Its Taylor coefficients are

 V=i[H0,B],
 W=-(1/2)[B,[B,H0]].

The first dressing U1=-i(I-PP^dagger)BP exists under the stated domains and
satisfies (1). The first-order-only dispersive coefficient is

 P^dagger VU1=-P^dagger B(H0-E)BP,

which can be nonzero. But the actual quadratic completion gives

 P^dagger W P=+P^dagger B(H0-E)BP,

so the FULL K vanishes exactly. The family has the same spectrum for every
g; the apparent phase was a missing Hamiltonian term. Pulsed coordinate
changes can still have endpoint/geometric effects, but this is not a sustained
order-g^2 plateau phase. Static pure-gauge minimal coupling and its compulsory
diamagnetic quadratic term give a familiar physical instance of this issue.

This does not prove cancellation of the proposed magnetic Morse phase. The
actual spin magnetic operator and accompanying electric polarizability have
not been shown to form such a unitary orbit. It proves why acquiring W is
essential to the scientific consequence, rather than merely budgeting it as
an arbitrary small implementation error. Its amplitude is g^2 and its action
on the intended g^-2 time is order one.
