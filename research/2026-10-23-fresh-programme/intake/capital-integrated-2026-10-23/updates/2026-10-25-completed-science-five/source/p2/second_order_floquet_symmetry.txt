-----

1. The actual two-axis field has a C4 screw symmetry
--------------------------------------------------

Let the two sheet profiles have equal coefficients in x and y. Write their
radial and transverse factors as

 C(X)=sum_r a_r exp(r^2 s^2/2) F^r cos(k_r X),
 S(X)=sum_r a_r exp(r^2 s^2/2) (k_r/kappa_r) F^r sin(k_r X),

with the corresponding identical functions of Y. In the quasistatic example
k_r=kappa_r=r a, a_1=1,a_2=-1/3, s=a sigma=.1. The exact retarded Maxwell
field has the same symmetry when corresponding x and y modes have equal
k_r,kappa_r and amplitudes; only the numerical compensation factor changes
to exp(k_r^2 sigma^2/2).

With D_i the magnetic-moment matrices and d the chosen target matrix element,
the interaction is

 V(t)=A cos(omega t)+B sin(omega t),
 A=[D_x C(X)-D_z S(X)]/d,
 B=[D_y C(Y)-D_z S(Y)]/d.

The radial coordinate is unchanged by lateral rotation. Define

 Q=exp[-i pi(L_z+F_z)/(2 hbar)].

The coordinate and spin transformations give, EXACTLY,

 Q A Q^dagger=B,     Q B Q^dagger=-A.

Consequently V_-=(A+i B)/2, the coefficient of exp(-i omega t), obeys
Q V_- Q^dagger=-i V_-. The adjoint harmonic obeys the conjugate law.
In Sambe notation a photon index k contributes k hbar omega to H_0.
Absorption changes k by -1 while changing m+l_z by +1 modulo4. Therefore

               m + l_z + k = constant modulo4                  (1)

is conserved by the complete magnetic Hamiltonian. The native source is
|F=1,m=1> tensor transverse vacuum, photon index0, so the constant is1.
No rotating-wave or selected-transition approximation enters (1).

Equal-axis amplitudes and phases and a C4-symmetric transverse trap are real
control assumptions. Anisotropy or unequal pattern errors break (1); their
resonant source action must be charged rather than declared closed.

4. Finite polynomial divided sources avoid a continuum inverse
------------------------------------------------------------

For a finite radial source v and a positive target radial energy T, define

 u_M=(1/T) sum_(j=0)^(M-1) (H_M/T)^j v.

This vector has finite support in the complete Morse Jacobi basis. Exact
multiplication gives

 (H_M-T)u_M+v=(H_M/T)^M v.                              (2)

Every new Jacobi coefficient is retained. Thus an exact source moment is a
complete residual, not a cutoff estimate. Log-convexity of squared moments
implies that the first increase identifies the global best integer M for
this particular polynomial trial. The same method applies to a finite
second-order source generated from a finite first-order trial. Bound and
closed channels instead admit finite Jacobi trials whose full boundary
residual is explicitly evaluated.

---------------------------------------------------

Let the finite-Fourier comparison vector be

Useful surviving consequence
---------------------------

The exact zero-photon spin degeneracy was the potentially fatal discrete
resonance. It is eliminated by a physically meaningful symmetry already

integrated Maxwell/electric/surface apparatus or an advantage over existing
