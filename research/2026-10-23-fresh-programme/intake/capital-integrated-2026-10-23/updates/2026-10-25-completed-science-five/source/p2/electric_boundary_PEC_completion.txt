ONE DECLARED ELECTROMAGNETIC BOUNDARY FOR THE FIELD AND SURFACE POTENTIAL
=====================================================================

This strengthens the earlier independent current-sheet/CP comparison. It is
an integrated ideal electromagnetic boundary model with an externally impressed
source. It is NOT an acquired patterned material, finite-temperature noise model,
native trap realization, or apparatus.

1. EXACT VACUUM FIELD WITH A SEPARATE SOURCE AND CONDUCTOR

Put a perfectly conducting plane at Z=0 and an impressed transverse current
sheet K_y=K cos(kX) exp(-i omega t) at Z=d>0. Let
kappa=sqrt(k^2-omega^2/c_light^2)>0. In the exterior Z>d the TE image solution is

 A_y=mu0 K/(2 kappa) [1-exp(-2 kappa d)]
             exp[-kappa(Z-d)] cos(kX).

The full solution below the sheet is the difference of its direct field and
its image. It has A_y(0)=0 and therefore obeys the conducting plane's E_t=0
boundary condition. Above the sheet, the desired exponential profile is
unchanged; only the relation between current and exterior field changes.

 B_x=kappa A_y,
 B_z=-mu0 K k/(2kappa) [1-exp(-2kappa d)]
                   exp[-kappa(Z-d)] sin(kX),
 E_y=i omega A_y.

An orthogonal sheet pattern in temporal quadrature supplies circular on-axis
magnetic control. For each desired decay r a, set k_r=sqrt((ra)^2+omega^2/c^2).
All finite-width longitudinal fields and their electric partners are retained
by these equations. The exact compensation uses exp(k_r^2 sigma^2/2), with
the quasistatic exp(r^2 a^2 sigma^2/2) used in the displayed numerical costs.

Set F=16exp[-a(Z-d)] on the admitted atomic side Z>=d. To produce magnetic
profile coefficients a_r at the native minimum, the required sheet current is

 K_r=(2B0/mu0) a_r 16^r exp(k_r^2 sigma^2/2)
                         /[1-exp(-2ra d)].

For ad=.5 and r={1,2,3,5}, the current-amplitude factors are
(1.58197670687,1.15651764275,1.05239569649,1.00678365491).
They are source costs, not free field cancellations. For orthogonal spatial
harmonics, the period-and-time average current squared is(1/2)sum_r K_r^2.
A declared sheet resistance R_square would therefore give mean dissipation
R_square sum_r K_r^2/2; no actual resistance or source noise is supplied here.

2. THE SAME CONDUCTING PLANE DETERMINES THE DECLARED CP POTENTIAL

For the zero-temperature ideal-conductor retarded law,

 V_CP(Z)=-C4/Z^4, C4=3hbar c alpha(0)/(32pi^2 epsilon0).

On Z>=d, it is bounded. Write x=a(Z-Z_min), with
Z_min=d+ln16/a and ad=.5. The radial native comparison Hamiltonian is

 H0/D=-(1/64)partial_x^2+(1-exp(-x))^2
                  -eta/(x+ln16+.5)^4,
 x>=-ln16, eta=C4 a^4/D.

This requires no max-function continuation. It is a different model from the
earlier capped CP comparison. Their numerical phase/gate results must be kept
separate. The ideal conductor model means the stated zero-temperature boundary
response, not an actual room-temperature metal.

The source plane atZ=d is NOT itself declared a perfect conductor. The displayed
radial calculation imposes Dirichlet confinement there. That boundary is an
explicit mathematical trap/interface assumption; the impressed current sheet
alone does not create it. A strongly repulsive native barrier and a localization
transfer can replace a literal wall only after its residual is evaluated. The
earlier exact-Morse cutoff calculation demonstrates the method, not the numerical
error constant for this new CP native Hamiltonian.

4. EQUAL-CONTROL COMPARISON AND UNFINISHED INTEGRATION

With exactly the same profiles and native potential, the red/gapped auxiliary
H0+.5D gives nonlinear coefficients

 Z_red=-.0301455919422/D (weak CP),
 Z_red=-.0291255731295/D (strong CP).

The generic phase capability is again already served at least as strongly by
the gapped control. This continuation establishes compatibility of a nonzero
open-continuum coherent response with one explicit ideal electromagnetic
boundary and an included native surface perturbation. It does not establish
a generic gate-resource advantage.

The source sheet is externally impressed. A real pattern's own optical CP
contribution, dielectric supports, resistance, dissipation, phase noise and
thermal fluctuations are not described by making the lower plane a perfect
conductor. Actual native trapping fields and their internal-state dependence
are also not acquired. Finite temperature changes the CP kernel; thezero-T
model cannot be relabeled as room-temperature calibration. These are the
remaining integrated material interfaces, distinct from the now-consistent
ideal Maxwell+surface-potential construction.

Earlier finite-time propagation results belong to the capped CP native model,
not to this shifted PEC geometry. No numerical gate probability is transferred
between those operations. The constructive source and both dressed vectors
are separately available for the new geometry in the BVP data.
