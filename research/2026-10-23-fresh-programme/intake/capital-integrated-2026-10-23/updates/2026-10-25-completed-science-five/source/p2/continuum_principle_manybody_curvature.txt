PHYSICAL SOURCE CURVATURE, MANY-BODY NOTCHES, AND AN EXACT FAILED ACTUATOR
======================================================================

25 October 2026. This is a bounded continuation toward low-heating interaction
engineering, not a broad survey or a claimed new many-body device. The main
result is an actual physical-completion test and a revealing local-source
failure. Ordinary sources and a small executable algebra check are retained.

1. The reusable physical-completion identity
-------------------------------------------

For an actual periodic Hamiltonian family

 H_g(t)=H0+g V(t)+g^2 W(t)+O(g^3),

let Q(t) be periodic, anti-Hermitian, and solve

 V+[H0,Q]-i hbar Qdot=0.                               (1)

Set psi=e^(gQ) phi. Expanding the EXACT transformed generator gives

 e^(-gQ)H_g e^(gQ)-i hbar e^(-gQ) d_t e^(gQ)
   =H0+g^2 C(t)+O(g^3),
 C(t)=W(t)+(1/2)[V(t),Q(t)].                           (2)

The cancellation follows from substituting (1) in the second BCH term. The
source's actual quadratic W enters at the same order as the intended virtual
operation. A first-order 'spectral zero' cannot determine that operation.

For the exactly gauge-equivalent family

 H_g=e^(gQ) H0 e^(-gQ)+i hbar(d_t e^(gQ))e^(-gQ),

one has W=-(1/2)[V,Q] and C=0. Thus a first-order cancellation can represent
only a moving coordinate frame. Equation (2) tells whether a physical source
has curvature left over after that frame change. The identity is elementary
time-dependent unitary transformation mathematics; historical novelty is not
claimed.

The zero Fourier component of C is not automatically the entire effective
Hamiltonian at low drive frequency. Resonant higher harmonics and reachable
degenerate states must be retained, or controlled by the spectral-window
residual theorem. Likewise a finite-chain norm estimate does not establish
uniform local-observable control in the thermodynamic limit.

2. A state-independent operator notch
-------------------------------------

Use nondimensional units hbar=1. Given a Hermitian A, define D=ad_H0 and

 B=(D^2-omega^2)A,
 V(t)=B cos(omega t),
 Q(t)=-[H0,A]cos(omega t)+i omega A sin(omega t).        (3)

Equation (1) holds identically. In the eigenbasis of H0,

 B_mn=[(E_m-E_n)^2-omega^2] A_mn,

so every one-photon matrix element at energy transfer +/-omega vanishes,
independently of the source state. The divided vector has been replaced by
an explicit commutator, with no small energy denominators. A remains a
physical-control/domain requirement; (3) does not manufacture its operator
B from one ordinary field by assertion.

For W=0, the zero-harmonic coefficient is

 Cbar=-(1/4)[B,[H0,A]].                                (4)

This suggests a consequential operation: change interactions inside an
interacting many-body energy spectrum while cancelling leading absorption,
rather than only imparting a phase on one bound state. Established weak-drive
and Floquet methods already suppress heating; the new engineering question
would be an actually accessible B,W pair whose C survives and provides a
valuable interaction under shared source constraints.

3. A purely one-/two-body Ising-chain realization at the algebraic level
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Consider an open or periodic spin chain

 H0=-h sum_i X_i+J sum_<ij> Z_i Z_j,
 A=sum_i Z_i.

For the displayed nearest-neighbor chain,

 [H0,A]=2ih sum_i Y_i,
 D^2 A=4h^2 sum_i Z_i+4hJ sum_<ij>(X_i Z_j+Z_i X_j).

Therefore the source operator is

 B=(4h^2-omega^2)sum_i Z_i
    +4hJ sum_<ij>(X_i Z_j+Z_i X_j).                    (5)

No three-body source term is needed for this choice of A. The putative W=0
response is

 Cbar=-h(4h^2-omega^2)sum_i X_i
       +8h^2J sum_<ij>(Z_i Z_j-X_i X_j).              (6)

The new XX interaction is absent from the native transverse-field ZZ model.
It is not merely a scalar rescaling of H0. But a mathematical expression for
the cross-axis source is not yet a physical actuator. Its natural actuator
completion gives the decisive counterexample below.

4. Tilting the Ising interaction axis cancels the new interaction EXACTLY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

An apparently natural implementation is to tilt the interaction axis:

 H_int(theta)=J sum_<ij>
   (Z_i cos theta+X_i sin theta)(Z_j cos theta+X_j sin theta).

Set theta(t)=4hg cos(omega t) and supply the longitudinal field term in (5)
separately, with the native -h sum X kept fixed. The linear interaction term
is exactly the desired g 4hJ sum(XZ+ZX)cos(omega t). However, expansion of
the ACTUAL interaction also gives

 W_axis(t)=16h^2 J cos^2(omega t) sum(XX-ZZ),
 Wbar_axis=8h^2 J sum(XX-ZZ).                          (7)

Adding (7) to (6) cancels the entire introduced XX and ZZ response. Only
the local-field rescaling -h(4h^2-omega^2)sum X survives. Rotating the native
transverse field along with the interaction changes that local-field term
but does not rescue the new interaction.

This is an acquired physical family and an exact perturbative failure, not a
vague calibration objection. The apparent interaction-engineering payoff
came from retaining the tangent of an axis rotation and dropping its curvature.

The accompanying four-spin matrix check (h=1,J=1/2,omega=3/2) verifies (5),
(6), and the interaction cancellation exactly in binary-representable matrix
arithmetic. It checks (1) at four phases to <2e-15. These are finite algebra
checks, not a thermodynamic or laboratory demonstration.

5. Independent fixed axes repair the algebra but strengthen the comparator
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Let R_+=(Z+X)/sqrt(2), R_-=(Z-X)/sqrt(2). Independently controlled fixed-axis
bilinears obey

 R_+ R_+ - R_- R_- = XZ+ZX,
 R_+ R_+ + R_- R_- = ZZ+XX.

Thus opposite amplitude modulation of two independent axes can produce the
cross-axis source linearly, without the axis-tilt quadratic term. But those
same controls give an XX component directly, together with a known ZZ shift;
with independently adjustable native ZZ they give XX alone. An equally
equipped controller must receive those controls. A claim of new capability
cannot deny it the direct interaction while using it to build (5).

If a specific material supplies AC cross-axis response but cannot supply
the relevant DC/direct combination, that could distinguish the operations.
No such waveform-to-Hamiltonian boundary is acquired here. Superconducting
Floquet-control literature already constructs general bilinear interactions,
and requires the unwanted Stark terms, higher levels and coupler dynamics to
be included rather than assumed zero.

6. Two quadratures: genuine three-body curvature, with an existing alternative
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

For two Hermitian notching seeds A,C, set B_A=(D^2-omega^2)A and B_C similarly.
Use V=B_A cos(omega t)+B_C sin(omega t). A periodic solution is

 Q=-DA cos-DC sin+i omega A sin-i omega C cos.

For W=0 the zero harmonic has a mixed piece

 Cbar_mixed=(i omega/4)([B_C,A]-[B_A,C]).              (8)

Equivalently its bracket is

 -D^2[A,C]+2[DA,DC]+2omega^2[A,C].

For the readily obtained Ising choice A=sum a_i Z_i and C=sum c_i Z_i,
both [A,C] and [DA,DC] vanish. Thus this mixed part is zero. Merely adding
another longitudinal pattern in temporal quadrature does not create a chiral
interaction. Choosing a noncommuting seed generally adds higher-body source
operators to D^2 C, bringing back the missing physical interface.

There is a real closed Hamiltonian family with nonzero three-body curvature:
with H0=0, independent linear exchange controls

 V=A cos(omega t)+B sin(omega t),
 A=J sigma_1.sigma_2, B=J sigma_2.sigma_3, W=0,

give Q=-(i/omega)(A sin-B cos) and

 Cbar=i[A,B]/(2omega)
      =(J^2/omega) sigma_1.(sigma_2 cross sigma_3).     (9)

The three-spin Pauli identity is verified in the executable. This uses only
two-body instantaneous controls and gives a consequential kind of engineered
interaction. However, it is ordinary weak-amplitude/high-frequency-ratio
Floquet commutator engineering, not a consequence of the new continuum
notch. A fixed nontrivial many-body H0 generally prevents using this Q unless
the actual filtered source is supplied. Treating H0 as zero after an
unacquired refocusing sequence would erase the important interface.

Moreover, three-body spin-chirality synthesis has already been experimentally
reported in superconducting circuits: Liu et al, Applied Physics Letters116,
114001(2020), arXiv:2002.01951. The primary abstract describes qubit-frequency
modulation and chiral excitation flow in a three-qubit loop. It is a strong
alternative, not proof of this particular two-exchange waveform or an
extensive chiral material. Claassen et al's 2017 driven-Hubbard construction
also retains physical Peierls coupling and derives scalar chirality at fourth
order; it explicitly finds lower-order virtual-loop contributions cancel.

7. Opportunity decision and exact remaining requirement
--------------------------------------------------------

The operator notch is a promising representation for the difficult question
of interaction engineering inside a dense spectrum. It does not yet establish
a stronger useful operation than the best ordinary controller. The honest
remaining target is an actual local physical family whose BOTH first and
second source derivatives are supplied and for which

 average[W+(1/2)[V,Q]]

contains an operationally useful interaction that survives the equally
equipped direct/echo/Floquet alternative, with finite-time local-error and
heating control. This is more demanding than a generic finite-code phase.
The axis-tilt attempt fails that target exactly; the independent-axis repair
and two-exchange chiral loop both open strong already-served alternatives.
These are precise obstructions for the tried families, not a universal no-go.

The materially different N776 noise-transport foothold does not currently
remove this opportunity problem. Its 97% kinetic oracle guarantee remains
valuable, but its assumed signed D-phase actuator can also cancel D, which
outperforms the noise law. A volume-uniform current theorem would not repair
that physical-control disadvantage. The decision-changing N776 attack would
be an actual finite-bandwidth diagonal-only reservoir interface whose stronger
coherent cancellation is physically unavailable, with its lab-time overhead.
No such interface has been acquired in this continuation.
