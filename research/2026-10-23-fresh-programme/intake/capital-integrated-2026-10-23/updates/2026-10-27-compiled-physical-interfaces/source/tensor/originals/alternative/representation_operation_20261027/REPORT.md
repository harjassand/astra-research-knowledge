## Consequential chain and exact scope

1. Constant-size tensor-pencil recovery gives the local rank-two CP factors.
2. A neighbor tensor supplies a rational determinant quadratic. Its values on
   the two local CP directions give each squared field ratio. Multiplying
   three such ratios produces an intrinsic local test `beta_v=1` in only that
   vertex's quadratic algebra.
3. Within nonzero local hyperdeterminants and nonzero four CP pairings per
   edge, this test is necessary and sufficient for compatible independent-edge

   tractable representations.
4. At good small primes all CP directions and edge roots lie in Fp2. Checked
   symmetrization, a tree sign solve, standard Fisher/FKT and an explicit
   reference matching sign give the original contraction residue. An odd
   connected-component sign forces exact zero.
5. Original-edge projectors are transported through those same gauges. Their
   even part gives Pfaffian derivatives including the scalar prefactor. One
   inverse supplies all edge weights. Hidden spin/dimer occupancy alone would
   be the wrong observable.
6. Clearing original denominators gives an integer height bound
   `H = 2^|E| product_v max|t_v|`. Separate partition and pin CRT moduli greater

   weights are undefined; their pin numerators are not asserted zero.
