COMPLETED MATHEMATICAL CORRECTION: A SUFFICIENT VARIANCE-ERROR BUDGET
28 October 2026

The source-bound extraction review identified one overstatement in U712
v0001 and the selected helper source:the proved upper bound does not show a
necessary preparation-accuracy limit. This informed correction was completed
after that actual review;it is not a fresh/blind independent audit.

For a bounded statistic |f|<=L and TV(Q,P)<=epsilon,the valid inequality is
|Var_Q(f)-Var_P(f)|<=4L^2 epsilon. If L<=C n and ideal variance>=c n^(1+alpha),
the relative bias is bounded above by(4C^2/c)epsilon n^(1-alpha).
Thus epsilon=o(n^(alpha-1)) is SUFFICIENT for this worst-case bound to imply
vanishing relative variance bias. It is not a necessary accuracy lower bound.
An allowed TV bound may be loose;different laws may also preserve both first
moments. For example,a statistic on five outcomes taking values(-2,-1,0,1,2)
has mean0 and variance1 under P(+-1)=1/2 and under Q(+-2)=1/8,Q(0)=3/4,
although the laws differ. Mixing Q with P gives any small fixed TV error
without changing that variance. Scaling the statistic by n keeps this example
at L=2n and variance n^2. This illustrates the bounded-law inference,not a
new claim about physically preparing a specified Gibbs family.

The ideal-information versus actual-derivative distinction and the smooth
state-accuracy/Fisher counterexample remain valid. No new preparation lower
bound or physical performance claim follows. The source helper's literal
stronger wording and the previous curator reconstruction are preserved as
