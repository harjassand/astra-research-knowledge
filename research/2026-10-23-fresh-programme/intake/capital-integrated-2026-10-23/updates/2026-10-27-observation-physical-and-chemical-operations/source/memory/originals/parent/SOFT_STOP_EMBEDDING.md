## The input that this continuation actually uses

The hard-stop model has energy

    E0(x,H) = sum_i c_i(1-x_i^2)/2 - sum_i (g_i H+b_i)x_i
              - sum_(i<j) J_ij x_i x_j,       |x_i| <= 1.

J is reciprocal. At every intended event of the producer's protocol:

1. Intended cells are mutually uncoupled, share a critical field hc and switch
   in the same direction d in {-1,+1}. Their local force at the old corner is
   d h_i(hc)=c_i.
2. Every other coordinate has hard-stop stability margin >=2 at hc for the
   ENTIRE box of intended coordinates in [-1,1]. All endpoint corners have
   stability margin >=1. These are local symbolic conditions for arbitrary N;
   the finite certificate file corroborates the stated contract at N<=4.
3. c_i<=40, sum_j |J_ij|<=25, |g_i|<=2, |b_i|<=10, |H|<=64.

The model uses positive g_i>=1; control levels and critical fields are fixed
independently of N. Mobility is one in the following dimensionless ODE.

## Replace hard stops by a finite C3 potential

On all of R^m add

    Esoft = E0 + (kappa/4) sum_i [(|x_i|-1)_+]^4,
    delta=1/1024,
    kappa = [65(1+delta)+138+1]/delta^3 = 219111489536.

Here a_+=max(a,0). The apparent absolute-value cusp is in a region where the
added term is identically zero. The energy is C3, reciprocal, bounded below
and coercive for each finite network; it has finite coefficients independent
of N. This is a specified model potential, not an acquired component design.

For overdamped gradient dynamics dot x_i=-partial_i Esoft, the box
|x_i|<=1+delta is invariant. At its positive face the outward drive is at most
65(1+delta)+138, whereas the wall force is kappa delta^3, larger by1. The
negative face follows by the same absolute bounds. Thus finite compliance
changes any corner's neighbor field by at most25delta. No stable local minimum
can have a coordinate strictly inside (-1,1), because the energy's corresponding

## What this closes and what it costs

The deterministic local operation need not rely on perfectly rigid stops or an
instantaneous directed relay. It has an explicit finite-compliance gradient
realization with constants independent of N, conditional on the stated local
event contract. This is not a proof of a superior mechanical memory. Existing
interacting electromechanical and magnetic shift registers remain incumbents.

The producer's exact hard-stop work figures DO NOT carry over. Added holds move
switching fields; finite compliance makes non-switching cells move under the
global drive as well. With fixed finite switching speed, their cumulative
viscous loss can scale with every cell and every clock, potentially O(N^2).
The uniform-drive bus, stiff finite walls, damping/inertia, readout and losses
are unacquired. A scalar mathematical H is not a demonstrated physical broadcast
bus.

Bounded deterministic perturbations and thermal fidelity are different claims.
Under a separately justified activated-escape bound per opportunity
p<=A exp(-DeltaE/kBT), M total opportunities require
DeltaE>=kBT log(AM/epsilon) for union-bound failure<=epsilon. If M is proportional
to N^2, whole-pattern error control requires logarithmic barrier growth at this
sufficient bound; neither the prefactor nor a global coupled escape barrier is
acquired here. Single-coordinate barriers cannot silently substitute for the
minimum collective escape path. No finite-temperature reliability is claimed.
