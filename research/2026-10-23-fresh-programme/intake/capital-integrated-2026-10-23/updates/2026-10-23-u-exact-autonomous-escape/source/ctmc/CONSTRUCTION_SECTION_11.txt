11. Exact nonnormal escape with 24 uniform memory levels

The second-order invariant-subspace construction by interface_invention has
now produced an explicit rational 48-state balanced Markov generator Q.
Root independently verified every required rational identity without importing
the producer's code. luna_fast_rotation separately verified them and rejected
negative-rate and broken-intertwiner mutations. The pinned certificate is

  interface_invention/AUTONOMOUS_ESCAPE_RATIONAL_CERTIFICATE.json
  SHA256 8c5baae5d9c33b61afda979f18888911be8aaac1e8a0d64666a47a73aad98d4a.

All 2256 off-diagonal entries are strictly positive; every row and column
sums exactly to zero. In the PROBABILITY-COLUMN convention, Q is therefore
a genuine irreducible CTMC generator and its stationary law is uniform.
The certificate includes an exact rational 48x4 rank-four matrix U whose
first column is z=(1 repeated24,-1 repeated24), with every column mean zero,
and a rational 4x4 matrix K such that

  QU=U(K-aI),    a=4999999773580401/5000000000000000,
  1-a=226419599/5000000000000000>0,
  (K^2+I)(K^2+9I)=0.

The polynomial has four distinct complex roots +/-i and +/-3i, and its
exponential at time pi is -1 on each root. Hence exp(pi K)=-I exactly.
Intertwining powers and then the convergent exponential series give

  exp(pi Q)U=-exp(-pi a)U,
  exp(pi Q)z=-alpha z,    alpha=exp(-pi a)>exp(-pi).

The strict comparison is exact from a<1; no floating exponential resolves
the tiny gap. The first Krylov vector is cyclic for K, so both odd alias
pairs contribute. A nonzero diagonal entry of QQ^T-Q^TQ verifies that Q
is nonnormal, as the normal ceiling requires.

For initial logical input b with independent uniform 24-state memory, the
joint probability column is p_b=(1+(-1)^b z)/48. Since Q1=0,

  exp(pi Q)p_b=(1-(-1)^b alpha z)/48.

Within BOTH final logical blocks every memory entry is identical. Thus the
same fixed time-homogeneous channel implements a bit flip of probability
(1+alpha)/2 and returns the uniform memory independently of the output and
of a classical input reference. It works on every classical bit mixture by
linearity. This is a population channel, not a coherent quantum NOT gate.

The minimum off-diagonal rate is approximately4.16666e-10. In the chosen
