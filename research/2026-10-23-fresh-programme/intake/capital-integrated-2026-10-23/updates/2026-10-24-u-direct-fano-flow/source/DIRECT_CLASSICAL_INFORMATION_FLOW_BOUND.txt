1. General entropy lemma, including arbitrary quantum phases
------------------------------------------------------------
Let rho_AB be ANY finite bipartite quantum state. Choose local orthonormal
measurement bases, with classical outcomes X,Y. Then
  S(rho_A)-S(rho_B) <= H(X|Y),
  S(rho_B)-S(rho_A) <= H(Y|X).                                (1)
No purity, product-state, diagonal-state or Markov assumption is used.

Proof by relative entropy of coherence. For the chosen dephasings Delta,
  C_A=D(rho_A || Delta_A rho_A)=H(X)-S(rho_A),
  C_B=H(Y)-S(rho_B).
Measure B to obtain the ensemble {p_y,rho_A^y}. Joint convexity of D gives
  0<=C_A<=sum_y p_y C(rho_A^y)
       =H(X|Y)-sum_y p_y S(rho_A^y)<=H(X|Y).
Likewise 0<=C_B<=H(Y|X). Substitute into
  S_A-S_B=H(X|Y)-H(Y|X)-C_A+C_B.
The forward bound uses C_A>=0 and C_B<=H(Y|X); the reverse is symmetric.
Zero-probability outcomes are omitted. This proof covers large overlaps,
arbitrary phases and arbitrary classical correlations in rho_AB.

Root independently supplied an alternative proof: purify rho_AB to ABC.
A rank-one B-basis measurement leaves conditional AC pure. By data
processing of Holevo information, chi(A:Y)<=chi(AC:Y)=S_B. Therefore
  S_A=chi(A:Y)+sum_y p_y S(rho_A^y)<=S_B+H(X|Y).
Exchange A and B for the other inequality. Both proofs use standard quantum
entropy ingredients; no new general entropy principle is asserted.

2. An exact index-to-classical-uncertainty inequality
---------------------------------------------------
Let alpha be a strict radius-r one-dimensional QCA on D-level cells,
r>=1, with a chosen onsite product basis. Use its canonical tracial Choi
state, with a primed input/reference copy. At a cut after site0 take
  L1=[-r+1,0], R1=[1,r], S=L1 union R1, |S|=2r.
Let J be the finite Choi marginal on S S', and split this FINITE state as
  A=L1' R1, B=L1 R1'.
Both partitions contain one member of each onsite input/output pair.
Computational measurement gives two length-2r D-valued records X_A,X_B,
ordered by their paired physical cell. Put
  epsilon_i=Pr[input label at i != output label at i].

Primary RWW Proposition4.7/Eqs4.10-4.13 gives the exact entropy identity
  2 ln ind(alpha)=S(B)_J-S(A)_J.                              (2)
Here ind is the POSITIVE multiplicative index; RWW writes its logarithm.
The collars EACH have r sites; no transpose composition is needed.
Applying (1), the classical chain rule and conditioning gives
  2|ln ind(alpha)|
    <=max{H(X_A|X_B),H(X_B|X_A)}
    <=sum_(i in S) f_D(epsilon_i),                            (3)
where f_D(e)=h(e)+e ln(D-1), with h binary entropy in nats.

The Fano step is elementary: for each paired D-valued coordinate let E
flag its mismatch. If E=0 the other record predicts it exactly; if E=1
at most D-1 alternatives remain. Hence H(X_i|Y_i)<=h(epsilon_i)+
epsilon_i ln(D-1). Summing conditional coordinate entropies proves (3).
The corresponding signed forms in (1) select one direction if the index
sign is known. Actual one-leg Choi marginals are tracial, but the entropy
lemma itself did not require that condition. The tracial input is required
to identify the Choi's diagonal probabilities with the observed tag data.

3. Translation-record certificate and literal data normalization
----------------------------------------------------------------
Let Omega be any strict radius-R0 QCA on A_i B_i, dimensions dA>=2,dB>=1,
D=dA*dB. Let sigma be the partial right A shift, B fixed, with
  sigma(P_A,i,a)=P_A,i-1,a, sigma(P_B,i,b)=P_B,i,b,
and ind(sigma)=dA. Define alpha=sigma^-1 composed with Omega, radius
r<=R0+1; use the safe r=R0+1. No exact A channel/factorization is assumed.

The observed reference signature is A-input(i-1) versus A-output i and
B-input i versus B-output i. This equals alpha's Choi diagonal law because
  tau[P_ref sigma^-1(Omega(P_out))]
     =tau[sigma(P_ref) Omega(P_out)].                         (4)
The trace is invariant under sigma. Thus sigma changes the input/reference
pairing in (4); it is not a physical correction. No commutation of Omega
with sigma or translation invariance is used. The alternative alpha'=
Omega composed with sigma^-1 uses shifted A OUTPUT coordinates instead;
it has the same index/radius but is not the same nonuniform automorphism.

For the full-cell pair (input A_(i-1),input B_i) and output (A_i,B_i),
epsilon_i is their TOTAL mismatch probability. If only component totals
delta_A,i and delta_B,i are measured, epsilon_i<=delta_A,i+delta_B,i.
There is also the direct component form
  2|ln ind(alpha)| <=sum_i [f_dA(delta_A,i)+f_dB(delta_B,i)],   (5)
by splitting the two conditional component coordinates before using Fano.
The dB=1 term is zero. An individual joint input-label/mismatch mass is
NOT a component total: a uniform ceiling e on those masses gives only
delta_A,i<=dA e, delta_B,i<=dB e. Conditional label errors must be averaged
with their actual uniform probabilities instead.

Only a finite preparation region is needed. For S=[a,b]=[-r+1,r], prepare
the PRODUCT tracial marginal on BOTH rails in
  U=[a-max(R0,1), b+R0].                                    (6)
It contains the Omega backward cones of outputs S and the compared tags.
For R0>=1 it has 4r-2 cells; R0=0 needs3 cells. Enlarging U is harmless.
Equation (4) and all finite Choi diagonal probabilities then depend only on
this marginal, even if exterior degrees of freedom are correlated with it.
One-site maximally mixed marginals with unknown intersite correlations do
not suffice. Dynamics must stay fixed independently of the sampled tags.

4. Maximum and collar-average error: inverse-Fano lower bound
-------------------------------------------------------------
If Omega has index one, then ind(alpha)=1/dA, so (3) requires
  2 ln(dA) <=sum_(i in S) f_D(epsilon_i).                     (7)
Let t_r be the SMALLEST solution in [0,1-1/D] of
  f_D(t_r)=ln(dA)/r.                                         (8)
It exists since ln(dA)/r<=lnD. By concavity of f_D,
  f_D(mean_(i in S) epsilon_i) >=ln(dA)/r.
Consequently EVERY such cut collar satisfies
  mean_(i in S) epsilon_i >=t_r,
  max_(i in S) epsilon_i >=t_r.                              (9)
This conclusion does not require all errors to lie on the increasing
branch: no smaller mean can attain (8). A sufficient counterfeit exclusion
is the STRICT inequality sum f_D(epsilon_i)<2 ln(dA), or max error<t_r.
The boundary is not an inclusive exclusion; equality can be compatible.

For fixed D,dA and r tending to infinity,
  t_r = [ln(dA)/(r ln r)](1+o(1)).                           (10)
It follows from f_D(t)=t ln(1/t)+O_D(t) as t tends to zero.
No one-site translation invariance is needed: the same inequality holds
at every translated cut, giving both local collar means and spatial mean
lower limits by partitioning into disjoint length-2r intervals. This is a
statement about local classical mismatch, not global record distance.

The frozen uniform selector has max O(1/r), with its actual circuit radius
at most2L-2. Hence only a LOGARITHMIC gap remains between the general
uniform-source lower bound (10) and that explicit index-one upper bound.
The old r^-6 sufficient threshold remains correct but is superseded here
by a different entropy/Fano mechanism. At every fixed biased p<1/2 the
frozen majority construction instead attains exponentially small error;
there is no contradiction because those probabilities are not tracial
Choi probabilities. This source distinction remains essential.

5. Phases cannot improve the isolated-qubit block-code architecture
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The selector's restricted-class mean lower1/(2L) extends to ARBITRARY
QUANTUM encoders, not only reversible basis permutations. Let U encode an
L-qubit block into one logical q qubit and a syndrome, countershift q to
an independent uniform neighboring block, and decode. The block marginal
channel is
  E(rho)=U* [Tr_q(U rho U*) tensor I_q/2] U
        =(1/4) sum_(a=I,X,Y,Z) L_a rho L_a*, L_a=U* a_q U.
For any computational input |x>, its probability to remain EXACTLY x is
  (1+sum_(a=X,Y,Z) |<x|L_a|x>|^2)/4 <=1/2,                  (11)
because those three expectations are the Bloch vector of the logical
qubit reduced state of U|x>. Thus any changed block incurs at least one
bit mismatch, so mean bit error>=1/(2L). Quantum phases in U do not evade
this code-class obstruction. General overlapping QCAs need not have this
isolated depolarized tensor factor; (11) is not their general lower bound.
Closing the logarithmic gap needs a construction outside this architecture
or a stronger invariant for general QCAs. No numerical fixture is needed.

6. Scientific and physical boundary
-----------------------------------
The theorem uses exact *-automorphism and strict QCA locality only for the
primary index identity. It is not an index assigned to an unknown noisy
channel. Under the frozen finite-patch causal-resource envelope, its actual
index-one CIRCUIT approximant may be used with the already charged local
observable error budgets; the observed tags do not establish that envelope.
No coherent A-shift certification, arbitrary index tomography, affordable
finite-shot assay, fixed-time gate synthesis or experimental benefit is
claimed. The entropy lemma and Fano ingredients are standard. The scoped
composition with the primary QCA identity has passed internal independent
algebra/source challenges; novelty has not been cleared.

Closest measurement comparator: Duschatko--Dumitrescu--Potter (2018)
prepare nondynamical ancillas individually entangled with edge sites and
measure chiral mutual information. Their Eq2 uses HALF the standard mutual
information, giving ln2 for a qubit shift. Their Renyi2 interference version
uses two copies of system plus ancillas and SWAP measurements. Its target
includes general chiral index, Floquet bulk/edge separation and repeated-
period slope. Here the Choi state is a PROOF DEVICE, while actual probes
are product-tracial classical records with trusted locality; the target is
the narrower fixed-translation/index-one exclusion. These different access
and target contracts imply neither automatic dominance nor a cheap assay.
