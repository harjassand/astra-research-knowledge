## ASTRA Pro3 — New construction: complete inverse-response acquisition

I established a different transfer mechanism that removes the requirement for a conservative energy potential.

Consider an unknown response law

\\[ F(x)=mx+A(x),\qquad \\|A(x)-A(y)\\|\le a\\|x-y\\|,\quad 0\le a\<m. \\]

The learner can perform ordinary forward experiments \\(x\mapsto F(x)\\), but has no native inverse-response instrument. The constants \\(m,a\\) are supplied and must be justified independently.

Exact inverse-transfer theorem. The inverse exists globally and has the representation

\\[ \boxed{ F^{-1}(p)=\frac{m}{m^2-a^2}p+B(p), \qquad \operatorname{Lip}(B)\le\frac{a}{m^2-a^2}. } \\]

Conversely, every inverse map satisfying this representation corresponds to an admissible forward response law.

Consequently, every old forward observation \\(F(x_i)=p_i\\) becomes an observation of the inverse residual \\(B\\) at \\(p_i\\). For any collection of later forces, the complete joint family of compatible equilibrium positions is determined by finite Euclidean-ball constraints.

This is an exact characterization, not merely an uncertainty enclosure. Its sufficiency follows from classical Lipschitz extension theory, particularly Kirszbraun's theorem.&#x20;

Unlike the preceding convex-dual compiler, this mechanism does not require symmetry, a Hessian, or an underlying energy function. It therefore extends Astra's reusable acquisition framework to certain nonconservative and dissipative response systems.

### Executed control

I constructed a nonlinear two-port source containing an explicitly nonconservative rotational coupling. A 32-point native experiment cycle gave a positive cyclic-monotonicity violation of 0.9325, ruling out the usual convex-gradient representation.

Nevertheless, its globally justified sector contract supports inverse transfer.

Using six ordinary forward readings, including one initial reading and five targeted acquisitions, the programme reduced a future equilibrium-position error certificate to 0.002894. Fifty subsequently specified equilibrium tasks were then enclosed with zero further learner calls.

The independent task setter performed 50 additional source-aware evaluations to construct and check those tasks; they are not counted as learner acquisitions.

### 2. Sparse certificates and a sharp information boundary

For a single future scalar inverse-response question, I proved that, when the compatible equilibrium region has nonempty interior, a sharp certificate can be supported by at most \\(d\\) historical measurements in \\(d\\) dimensions. The learner can retain its full history but produce a small, independently checkable proof for each new question.

The two-dimensional implementation generated exact rational certificates. In the six-reading controls, the best obtained two-reading certificates reduced uncertainty by up to 13.3% relative to the best single-reading certificate. The numerical search is not claimed globally optimal.

A more revealing result concerns nearly saturated observations. Two historical experiments can determine an unqueried equilibrium exactly, but an observed departure \\(\varepsilon\\) from saturation permits hidden transverse uncertainty of

\\[ \sqrt{\frac{\varepsilon(2-\varepsilon)}8}. \\]

At \\(\varepsilon=10^{-8}\\), the compatible equilibrium can still shift by approximately \\(5\times10^{-5}\\). Matching indistinguishable-source constructions establish that this uncertainty is real.

### 3. Transfer to physically constrained operations

I then executed a different operation: finding equilibria when the instrument permits only nonnegative mixtures with a fixed total dose.

The learner used a three-component nonlinear, nonconservative source. Its admissible inputs satisfy

\\[ x_i\ge0,\qquad x_1+x_2+x_3=1. \\]

The requested equilibrium is a constrained variational inequality, rather than necessarily the solution of \\(F(x)=p\\). Under the supplied sector contract, projected forward experiments converge with a certified contraction factor \\(a/m=1/3\\). This is an application of established projection and monotone-operator methods.&#x20;

The vertex and edge tasks were also certified, including cases where the requested constrained operation was not an unconstrained inverse.

All experimental inputs stayed on the permitted simplex.

### Scientific assessment

The main advance is a complete consistency-family representation for inverse operations that does not require an energy potential, followed by a distinct constrained-equilibrium procedure. The limiting factor remains the physical validity contract: finite data cannot establish global sector bounds, and correlated adversarial sensor errors impose a nonzero accuracy floor. Equally informed classical algorithms can reproduce the mechanisms.

All four executable research suites passed an independent clean replay, with byte-identical saved results. No physical discovery or general architecture superiority is claimed.
