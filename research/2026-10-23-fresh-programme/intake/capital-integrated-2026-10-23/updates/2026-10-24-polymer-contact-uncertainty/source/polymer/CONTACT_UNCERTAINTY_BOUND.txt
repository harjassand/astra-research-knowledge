CONTACT-TIME UNCERTAINTY: A BOUND FOR EVERY PRESET ACTIVITY PROFILE

Status: exact conditional control-capacity result. It is not a material law,
proof of physical impossibility, acquired repair operation, or priority claim.
It generalizes PASSIVE_PULSE_BOUND beyond exponential deactivation.

Interface and assumptions

Damage occurs at time zero. Let a(t)>=0 be the productive exchange hazard rate
in a prospective seam, fixed by the damage-triggered chemistry and any preset
schedule, independently of when the faces subsequently contact. For example,
a=kh C with C an active-catalyst concentration. The same profile is assumed for
all possible contact times; contact-induced release, mixing, or self-quenching
is precisely a different operation that can escape this premise.

Contact time tc is uncertain in [0,T]. After contact, the repair is allowed the
same dwell tau>0. Require

 integral_[tc,tc+tau] a(t) dt >= H > 0                  (1)

for EVERY tc in [0,T]. H is a declared productive dose, not automatically a
fracture-recovery fraction. The earliest-contact specimen finishes at tau and
returns immediately to constant load. Its subsequent activity is

 J=integral_[tau,infinity] a(t) dt.                    (2)

Suppose the fully severed repaired seam has no parallel permanent load path and
has added Maxwell-like slip

 u=(ell sigma/G) xi J,   xi=kv/kh>0.                  (3)

Here ell is seam thickness, G its modulus and sigma the service stress. A
permanent or independently locked seam, glass freezing, or contact-dependent
activity invalidates (3) or the shared-profile premise and must be modeled as
an explicit additional operation.

Universal lower bound

Write T=m tau+r, m=floor(T/tau), 0<=r<tau. Then

 J >= m H.                                            (4)

Proof. The allowed contact times tc=j tau for j=1,...,m require dose H on the
disjoint windows [j tau,(j+1)tau]. Every such window is after the earliest
specimen has finished at tau. Sum (1). Endpoints have zero measure for locally
integrable finite rates. The lower bound allows arbitrary nonnegative profiles,
so it also applies to every passive decay law in this class.

This is a delay-UNCERTAINTY bound, not a bound based just on a large known delay.
If the delay is known exactly, a preset contact-timed pulse could avoid the tail.
Keeping repair available over an interval of possible delays creates the cost.

Exact finite-rate optimum

If 0<=a(t)<=A with finite A>0, feasibility first requires H<=A tau. When this
holds, the exact smallest tail in (2) over all such profiles is

 J_min=m H + max(0, H-A(tau-r)).                       (5)

For m=0, the last required window [T,T+tau] can receive at most A(tau-r) dose
before time tau, proving (5)'s additional term. For m>=1, the first m-1 disjoint
windows supply at least (m-1)H after tau. The windows [m tau,(m+1)tau] and
[T,T+tau] each need H; their overlap has length tau-r and contains at most
A(tau-r). Their union therefore needs at least 2H-A(tau-r). Combining this
with (4) proves (5).

For attainment, let d=H/A<=tau. On [0,T+tau], use the tau-periodic rectangular
profile equal to A during the last d of every period, and zero otherwise; set
it to zero outside [0,T+tau]. Every required tau-window contains exactly H.
Its tail consists of m complete periods and the initial r of another period,
giving mH+A max(0,r-(tau-d)), exactly (5). This is sharpness in a mathematical
class of bounded schedules. The repeated clocked pulses are not asserted to
be a realizable passive chemical decay law or a free physical control.

For H=3, tau=1 and T=10.5 in consistent time units, J_min=30 when A>=6; with
A=4 it is 31; and with A=3 it is 31.5. Units and H are illustrative, not material
measurements. No rate can meet (1) if A<3 in this example.

Downtime and a required new operation

If the earliest specimen instead waits unloaded for tw after repair, disjoint
windows starting after tau+tw still give the weaker, general bound

 integral_[tau+tw,infinity] a >= max(0,m-ceil(tw/tau)) H. (6)

The unloaded wait is an explicit resource. Equation (6) is not asserted to be
the exact finite-rate optimum for general tw.

Under the constitutive screen (3), any u_max smaller than
(ell sigma/G) xi J_min excludes every shared bounded activity profile. Escapes
require a premise-changing operation: actual face-contact sensing/mixing, an
independent permanent locking reaction, sufficiently long unloaded waiting, or
an independently controlled removal/shutdown step. Ordinary time-only catalyst
decay cannot provide contact recognition.

Material meaning and strongest incumbent

Furan-maleimide Diels-Alder elastomers already generate complementary reactive
groups at broken bonds and repair after the faces meet. The operation itself is
an incumbent. Their separated faces can consume those groups by same-face
recombination, requiring prompt contact or thermal reactivation. Those systems
have contact-dependent reaction kinetics and therefore are not counterexamples
to (4); the proposed shared-profile premise does not describe them.
 https://doi.org/10.3390/polym15173527
 https://doi.org/10.1016/j.polymer.2018.08.026

For strong furan-functional epoxy-amine hosts, addition of a multimaleimide
solution has already produced about 90% initial-strength recovery after 20 days.
That incumbent pays fresh healing agent, solvent and dwell; it is not evidence
for a catalyst-only autonomous repair or post-heal creep resistance.
 https://doi.org/10.1039/C3PY00084B

The immediate mechanism question is whether a chemically compatible interface
can keep complementary partners available during separation, then form stable
cross-seam links on contact, with same-face passivation, molecular registration,
mobility, stoichiometry and repaired-joint creep measured on the same strong host.
