SOURCE PRECISION AS A CUMULATIVE INVESTMENT: CONDITIONAL EXPOSURE THEOREM
24 October 2026. Companion to PAID_COVERAGE_PRECISION_TRANSFER.txt.

This note derives an allocation consequence, not a new learning architecture.
The statistical procedure actually acquires its source span and checks its
precision from paid data. A separate resource envelope is needed before the
allocation theorem below can be invoked. Uncurated arbitrary source directions
do not supply such an envelope: they can repeat one direction indefinitely.

Resource contract

Assume a retained representation can be refined from source exposure E to E'>E
at additional exposure E'-E, with a valid squared target-bias guarantee A/E,
A>0. The guarantee must hold at all refinement levels used, on one declared
confidence event. Task t announces its required squared error e_t>0 and its
fresh-noise coefficient B_t>0 before the learner chooses exposure. The sufficient
fresh task exposure at source precision E is

       r_t(E)=B_t/(e_t-A/E)=B_t E/(e_t E-A),
       E>A/e_t; otherwise r_t(E)=+infinity.          (1)

The request sequence, its length, and later e_t,B_t are unknown. The contract
charges source exposure cumulatively and fresh task exposure separately. It
does not price eigensolver work, source access calls or storage as free; those
remain separate resource coordinates. Ceilings add at most one fresh-exposure
unit per task. Statistical tail-budget dependence may be included in B_t.

Offline comparator and the exact fixed-horizon tradeoff

For a prefix t define

       F_t=min_E [E+sum_{i<=t} r_i(E)],
       E_t=its unique minimizer.                    (2)

The minimum exists: the objective diverges both at its finite lower domain
endpoint and as E grows. Each r_i is decreasing and strictly convex on its
domain, with
       r_i'(E)=-A B_i/(e_i E-A)^2,
       r_i''(E)=2 A B_i e_i/(e_i E-A)^3>0.
Consequently E_t solves
       sum_{i<=t} A B_i/(e_i E_t-A)^2=1.            (3)

F_t is also the optimum of an offline sequence of refinements: any sequence
with final source exposure E pays E in source exposure and at least sum r_i(E)
in task exposure. Buying its final precision before task 1 cannot increase its
cost under this contract. This is an offline *exposure-envelope* comparator,
not an optimum over all possible statistical algorithms or unknown interfaces.

For t=T identical requests e_i=epsilon^2,B_i=B, (3) gives
       E_T=(A+sqrt(T A B))/epsilon^2,
       r_i(E_T)=(B+sqrt(A B/T))/epsilon^2,
       F_T=(sqrt(A)+sqrt(T B))^2/epsilon^2.          (4)
As the continuation grows, the optimal source precision grows as sqrt(T),
while fresh target exposures approach their intrinsic B/epsilon^2 floor.

An anytime policy, without knowing future requests

On receiving request t, calculate (3), refine source exposure to E_t, and pay
r_t(E_t). The sequence E_t is nondecreasing: adding a decreasing convex term
moves the unique derivative zero to the right. Hence source refinement costs
telescope to E_T; the policy never needs to erase a paid refinement.

Since E_t minimizes the t-term objective and F_{t-1} is the minimum of the
previous objective,
       F_t >= F_{t-1}+r_t(E_t).
Summing gives sum r_t(E_t)<=F_T, with F_0=0. Therefore the total causal exposure
obeys, for EVERY finite request sequence and EVERY prefix,

       E_T+sum_{t<=T}r_t(E_t) <= 2 F_T.             (5)

It is causal here to use the current request's loss r_t: the requested precision
and its tail budget are announced before measurement exposure is bought. This
is not online prediction after hiding the current loss. The proof is the
standard prefix-minimizer / be-the-leader inequality plus monotone investment.
It does not establish a new competitive-analysis method.

If source exposure can only be refined on a geometric grid and each acquired
level is at most q>=1 times the required E_t, use the next grid point. Fresh
task cost only decreases, while final source cost is <=q E_T. Thus
       total exposure <=(q+1)F_T.                  (6)
For doubling the constant is 3. A positive minimum initial exposure produces
the corresponding additive initial-level term. If refinement requires paying
the whole source acquisition again, or the bias envelope changes with the
realized source geometry, this telescoping proof does not apply as written.

Connection and limits for the actual acquired-PCA procedure

The source-run certificate gives OBSERVED pairs (E_j,b_j), including failed
coverage gates, with b_j=beta^2 s_j^2/4. Its target rentals are the same rational
form with b_j in place of A/E. That suffices for actual task certification and
for the ex-post break-even formula in the main note. It does NOT automatically
give one deterministic A, predictable success at unacquired levels, or the
incremental exposure guarantee of this theorem. A statistical source-coverage
or experimentally repeatable refinement promise must justify those separately.
We do not use (5) to claim competitiveness for the uncurated demonstration.

This separates two causal roles of experience: paid source observations acquire
the otherwise unknown future-learning geometry, while its acquired precision
limits the reliability of further learning. Longer or more precise continuation
can make refinement worth paying for. The theorem only quantifies that policy
after the cost-to-precision contract is established.

Closest priority boundaries

Linear representation transfer and its source/target dimension benefit are
established: Tripuraneni, Jin and Jordan, ICML 2021 (primary paper):
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/tripuraneni21a/tripuraneni21a.pdf
Sequential representation refinement is also established: Cao, Liu and Vempala,
AISTATS 2022: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v151/cao22a.html
The paid-refinement versus future running-cost structure is closely related to
additive multislope ski rental, where switching charges the difference in buying
prices: Lotker, Patt-Shamir and Rawitz, SIAM J. Discrete Math. 26 (2012):
https://doi.org/10.1137/100794018 ; author manuscript
https://www.eng.biu.ac.il/~rawitzd/Papers/ski.pdf
The elementary prefix-minimizer step is the known be-the-leader lemma, e.g. the
author's course exposition at
https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/varun.kanade/teaching/OLMAB-AIMS-MT2019/docs/all_lectures.html
No novelty of allocation or PCA follows from this derivation. The bounded
contribution is an explicit paid-source-precision / new-operator-solution
contract, its observed coverage gate, and its honest conditional cost calculus.

Executable check

precision_allocation_check.py verifies the fixed-horizon optimum and break-even
identity with exact rational arithmetic, then checks monotonicity, optimality
derivatives and every-prefix cost inequalities for changing request sequences.
Those floating checks support implementation; the proof above supplies the
mathematical inequality. It is not a learned-operator benchmark or a universal
source-envelope certificate.
