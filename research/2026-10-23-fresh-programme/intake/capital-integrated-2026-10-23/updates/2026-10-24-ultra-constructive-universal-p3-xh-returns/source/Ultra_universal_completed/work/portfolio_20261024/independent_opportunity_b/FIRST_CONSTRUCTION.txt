Problem selected
Recover an arbitrary entangled quantum input after a wide noisy native unitary
kernel, with much smaller trusted workspace/control than a full clean reexecution.
The output must be an actual deterministic CPTP operation, not postselected
expectation values. Native-kernel access, trusted gates and their errors all count.

First closed operation: sparse coherent errors and compressed syndromes
Let known U be Clifford, available once as E o U. Let each noise Kraus operator
lie in span{I,X_i,Y_i,Z_i : i=1,...,n}. Choose output Pauli checks Q_j whose
binary symplectic syndromes separate that set. Set P_j=U†Q_jU. For control bits a,
 V_in(a)=P_r^a_r ... P_1^a_1, V_out(a)=Q_r^a_r ... Q_1^a_1.
Prepare controls |+>^r; apply controlled V_in, the ordinary noisy kernel once,
then controlled V_out† in reverse order; apply H to each control and measure.
For Pauli E,
 V_out(a)† E U V_in(a)=(-1)^(a.s(E)) E U.
Thus syndrome extraction is independent of the logical input even for arbitrary
references and noncommuting Q_j. Correct the unique weight<=1 E matching syndrome.
For Kraus sum K_alpha=sum_e c_alpha,e E_e, the corrected joint amplitude is
sum_e c_alpha,e |s(e)> tensor U|psi>. Discard syndrome: the data operation is
exactly U(.)U† since TP and Pauli orthogonality give sum_alpha,e|c_alpha,e|²=1.

For n<=(4^m-1)/3, the one-dimensional subspaces of F4^m partition all nonzero
binary2m-vectors into triples v,omega v,(1+omega)v. Assign each site one triple
as its X,Z,Y syndromes. This gives r=2m controls. This is established Hamming/
partial-spread coding machinery, not a new coding theorem. A flag-dimension
lower bound3n+1 is only for nondegenerate syndrome extraction, not arbitrary
protected-workspace recovery. Trusted pre/post controlled gates cost at most
2nr, plus preparation/measurement/feedforward and reset. Known U's Pauli images
are supplied/computed from its circuit; neither a controlled-U nor U† call is used.

Remaining concrete resource mechanism to test
Change the native problem to known diagonal C3 kernels
 U_f|x>=(-1)^f(x)|x>, f(x)=sum_(i<j<k) c_ijk x_i x_j x_k overF2.
For X(a)Z(b), conjugation introduces the discrete derivative
 f(x+a)+f(x), which has degree<=2. Therefore the needed precheck is a Clifford
phase/permutation circuit; controlling it uses degree<=3 gates and at most
O(n²) trusted elementary gates per check. Compressed sparse-error syndromes
would use O(n²log n) trusted gates, compared with O(n³) literal dense-CCZ clean
reexecution. The noisy native kernel is still invoked once. The derivative
structure, not Hamming labels, is the candidate resource benefit.

This is CONDITIONAL: CPC or gate teleportation may already exploit precisely
the same derivatives, global synthesis may improve the clean comparator, and
internal local native faults can spread into correlated output errors. A native
output-weight<=1 promise is not acquired from locality of primitive faults.
Next operation is to derive the actual conjugated internal-fault family and
test whether compact distinguishable syndromes/control circuits survive it.
If the best equally informed known approach already has this resource behavior,
the problem selection must pivot; no forced novelty or advantage claim.
