1. Native channel and reference-preserving recovery

The supplied projector Hamiltonian and all local Z dephasing superoperators
commute. Integrating them therefore gives the stated independent output-Z channel
with p_i=(1-exp(-2 integral gamma_i))/2, without treating an internal arbitrary
fault as an output single-qubit Pauli. X/Y faults and leakage are outside this
derivation and must enter the separate model-deviation budget if present.

I reconstructed the control-string identity
  V_out(a)^dagger Z(e) U_f V_in(a)=(-1)^(a.H e) Z(e) U_f.
The declared precheck ordering and reverse postcheck ordering implement this
identity. Walsh readout extracts only the error syndrome, so the argument applies
to arbitrary data and external reference states. Distinct nonzero H columns
separate I,Z_1,...,Z_n. For any Kraus operator in their coherent span, syndrome
readout followed by the corresponding Z correction factors the data/reference
operation as U_f; trace preservation and Pauli orthogonality give unit total
weight. No postselection or prior error-label access is used.

For the full independent channel, every error of weight at least two leaves a
nonidentity residual because the decoder applies a correction of weight at most
one. Thus the nonidentity probability is exactly P_bad, and the normalized
diamond distance is exactly P_bad: the Pauli-mixture upper bound is attained on
the maximally entangled Choi input. The pair-probability union bound is valid.

2. Quadratic controls and the matched synthesis comparison

Translation cancels the cubic part of f(x+q)+f(x). I checked the alternating-form
basis reduction and the recovery of its remaining affine terms by evaluation.
An unconditional binary basis change, controlled canonical quadratic pairs,
controlled affine terms, and its inverse give the claimed controlled derivative.
The protected-control count O(n^2 log n), including O(n log n) CCZ equivalents,
is consistent with this explicit synthesis under all-to-all bounded-arity access.
Classical derivative compilation, storage, ancilla protection, reset/readout,
feedforward, idling and additional routing are separately charged or assumed.

The theorem correctly rejects a dense-monomial CCZ lower bound. Grouping a cubic
polynomial by a chosen variable and reducing the resulting quadratic forms gives
an O(n^2) CCZ upper bound for ordinary synthesis; Clifford volume is separate.
The stated separation concerns total trusted finite-alphabet quantum primitives,
not a claimed cubic non-Clifford count or total computational work.

3. Approximate/adaptive all-trusted expected-count lower bound

I independently checked all three steps. Nonzero degree-at-most-three Boolean
differences have relative weight between 1/8 and 7/8, giving distinct normalized
target-Choi overlaps at most 3/4. Normalized diamond error epsilon bounds Choi
infidelity by epsilon. Refining a channel into fixed primitive Kraus paths and
final computational-basis ancilla outcomes preserves its expected path length.
Markov bounds leave positive probability of a path with length at most 4G and
target fidelity at least 1-2 epsilon. One such normalized path cannot serve two
targets for epsilon<1/16: their overlap would be at least 1-4 epsilon>3/4.

The important clarification is now explicit: fix a finite Kraus refinement of
EVERY bounded-arity CPTP alphabet primitive independently of f, including hidden
noise/reset/discard primitives if allowed. Its constant Kraus rank contributes
only constant bits per location. An arbitrary f-dependent Kraus representation
cannot be counted as an independent physical path description. Final discarded
ancillas add only O(L) outcome bits because a length-L bounded-arity path touches
only O(L) initially simple ancillas. Classical controller size or f-dependent
branching does not increase the number of distinct short quantum path operators.

Read N<=poly(n) as one fixed polynomial workspace bound for the family, not an
unbounded polynomial exponent chosen separately for each f. Alternatively one
can canonically relabel the n+O(L) touched qubits, since untouched |0> ancillas
carry no information and the finite alphabet is position independent. Under this
contract the short-path count 2^O(G log n), hence the generic-family lower bound
Omega(n^3/log n), follows. G may be the Choi-input expected cost or a worst-case
expected cost that upper-bounds it. No efficiently identifiable explicit hard f,
algorithmic hardness assumption, or lower bound on every cubic is obtained.

4. Accuracy, native costs and consequence

The telescoping bound is sound with complete-register trusted error bounds or a
uniform extended-memory contract. It does not cover arbitrary unmodelled memory
correlations. With p_i<=c/n, the stated constant-accuracy example is nonvacuous,
conditional on the supplied native and trusted quality scaling.

For the specified positive-projector Hamiltonian, the exact norm/action ledger
and local-incidence bound are consistent. A bounded local interaction sum J
forces tau>=pi D_f/J; dense f has D_f=Theta(n^2). Meeting p=O(1/n) at the fastest
allowed duration requires dephasing of order J/n^3. Native couplers, calibration,
long-lived syndrome controls and their idling are substantial inputs. These are
costs of the specified implementation, not universal Hamiltonian lower bounds
and not measured work/heat. The required per-term precision is a specification,
not an acquired calibration method.

The resulting conditional trusted-control tradeoff is mathematically supported.
An equally informed coherent-Pauli-check/quantum-filter implementation supplied
with the SAME native kernel can use the same operation. The comparison does not
establish superiority to such an incumbent, scalable physical acquisition,
wall-time/energy advantage, or priority.
