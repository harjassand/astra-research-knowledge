FIRST OWNED CONSTRUCTION — PROJECTED FERMION NORM AND SAMPLING
24 October 2026. physical_constructor_fresh, original warm Sol slot.

QUESTION BEFORE MECHANISM
Can the norm and measurement distribution of a strongly occupancy-projected
fermionic pairing state be acquired with controlled computational resources
from its actual explicit pairing matrix? Such states are used to investigate
strongly correlated materials. Efficiently sampling one variational state does
not establish that it approximates a Hamiltonian's ground state or solves
materials design. Those are distinct scientific obligations.

Take even n, k=n/2, and supplied rational-complex F of size n by n. The target
is the half-filled, no-double-occupancy pairing distribution
  mu(S) = |det F[S,S^c]|^2 / Z,  |S|=n/2,
  Z = sum_{|S|=n/2} |det F[S,S^c]|^2.
The raw input has n^2 entries with declared bit lengths. Individual weights,
exact linear algebra, seeds, precision and output configuration costs are
paid. No partition oracle, ground-state oracle or efficient hidden sampler
is supplied. Zero Z must be distinguished from a positive instance before a
relative approximation/sampling claim. General k is deferred, rather than
silently treating the archive's padding identity as an efficient counter.

STRONGEST INCUMBENT AND SELECTION CHALLENGE
Unprojected Gaussian/determinantal sampling is already efficient. The 2023
Alimohammadi–Anari–Shiragur–Vuong theorem samples nonsymmetric fixed-size
DPPs with nonnegative symmetric part; the skew block L=[0,F;-F^T,0] for real
F already covers squared minors before the hard overlap filter. Their
partition result has O(1) parts. Here there are n hard on-site constraints.
Projected-state VMC and tensor-network contraction are strong practical and
structured exact competitors; neither is denied the same explicit matrix,
block decomposition, rank calculation, caches or successful conditioning.

ACTUAL NEW ARM OPERATION
The present attack changes representation to interference moments of one
explicit Gaussian Pfaffian and then integrates nuisance phases analytically.
It does not assume that the plain phase estimator is efficient.

Let a_i=e_i, b_i=row_i(F)^T, and preserve paired column order
V=[a_1,b_1,...,a_n,b_n]. For z_i independently uniform in {1,-1,i,-i}, set
  M(z)=sum_i z_i(a_i b_i^T-b_i a_i^T)=D_z F-F^T D_z,
  P(z)=Pf M(z),              X(z)=|P(z)|^2.
Pfaffian minor summation gives the exact squarefree polynomial
  P(z)=sum_{|S|=n/2} det V[:,P(S)] product_{i in S}z_i.
The paired coefficient is +/-det F[S,S^c], hence E X=Z exactly.
Four-root phases have exactly the same relevant second/fourth moments as
independent continuous phases: per-coordinate exponents range from -2 to 2.
Thus each estimator sample consumes 2n fair bits and one polynomial-time
rational-complex Pfaffian computation. This identity is standard algebra,
not a novelty claim. It supplies an evaluable random variable, not a bound
on the number of samples. Relative variance controls actual acquisition.

For d_S=det V[:,P(S)], define for disjoint A,B with equal positive size
  K_AB=sum_{R disjoint A union B, |R|=k-|A|}
          d_(R union A) conjugate(d_(R union B)).
Fourier orthogonality gives
  Var(X)=sum_{A,B disjoint, |A|=|B|>=1} |K_AB|^2.
The load-bearing uncertainty is whether physically meaningful matrix
structure allows these exchange-interference modes to be integrated or
bounded without computing the unknown projected partitions themselves.

FIRST DECISIVE CALCULATION, INCLUDING FAILURE
For F a direct sum of m two-site swap matrices, n=2m,
  P(z)=product_j(z_(2j-1)-z_(2j)),
  E X=2^m, E X^2=6^m,
  Var(X)/(E X)^2=(3/2)^m-1.
The target distribution is merely m independent fair choices. Thus plain
phase Monte Carlo is exponentially bad even on trivial, well-conditioned
instances; a claimed general efficient random-Pfaffian sampler is rejected.

There is a constructive correction with an exact paid operation. Since each
phase is squarefree, write P=A+z_i B. Two Pfaffian evaluations at z_i=0,1
obtain A,B, and exact conditional integration replaces X by |A|^2+|B|^2.
For the acquired direct-sum structure this integrates one phase per block,
returns Z exactly in polynomial time, and samples by block backtracking.
Graph connectivity of the actual nonzero matrix entries acquires the blocks;
no latent partition or microscopic rate is queried. At fixed total k the
component number sectors combine by ordinary coefficient convolution.

The generic recursion over s integrated phases can branch into 2^s coefficient
polynomials. Merely invoking Rao–Blackwell does not close that cost. Standard
tensor contraction can also exploit exact blocks and separators, so the easy
block case is an integrity check, not an advantage claim. The next operation
must identify and price a genuinely useful coupling/low-rank structure from
F that avoids this branching while retaining strong on-site projection, or
reveal that the strongest informed tensor method already supplies it.
