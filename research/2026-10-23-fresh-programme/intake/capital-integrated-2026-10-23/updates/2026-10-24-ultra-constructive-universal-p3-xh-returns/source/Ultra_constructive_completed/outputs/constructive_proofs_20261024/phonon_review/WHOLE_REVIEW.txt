1. INDEPENDENT RECONSTRUCTION OF THE LOAD-BEARING PROOF

The class is finite connected positive-mass/passive-internal-spring cells,
one scalar intercell backbone connection K, fixed total mass M, and backbone
mass m>=m_min=1. With D(s)=D0+s vv^T, s in[0,4K], the normalized rigid null
mode obeys |z^T v|^2=1/M, while ||v||^2=1/m.

Rank-one interlacing gives alpha_j<=beta_j<=alpha_(j+1), so the ordered
frequency intervals have disjoint interiors. Endpoint degeneracies and
collapsed/dark bands do not change integrals. The indicator is equivalently
the transmitting-branch multiplicity almost everywhere; all optical bands
are retained. It is not an acoustic-only construction.

The first moment is exactly
  integral 2omega chi =Tr[D(4K)-D0]=4K/m.
For the second, the singular determinant lemma yields
  det[D(4K)]/det'(D0)=4K/M.
Regularizing the acoustic interval at epsilon and subtracting the rigid
cutoff Omega_r=2sqrt(K/M) therefore gives
  integral [chi-1_[0,Omega_r]]/omega=0.
Connectedness and |z^T v|>0 give every finite cell a positive acoustic
interval. Differences of two such indicators vanish near0, making the dual
comparison integrable despite its lambda/omega term. Finite cell matrices
also have bounded frequency support. No eigenvalue simplicity is required.

At theta=1/2 define h=omega^3/sinh^2omega, mu=(h1-h2)/6>0,
lambda=-(4h1-h2)/3<0, F=h+lambda+2mu omega^2. Multiplication by the positive
factor sinh^2omega/omega^2 gives the entire function
  Q=lambda+omega+sum_(n>=1) c_n b_n omega^(2n),
  c_n=2^(2n-1)/(2n)!,
  b_n=2mu+4lambda/[(2n+2)(2n+1)].
The b_n increase strictly. Exact b3<0<b4 makes the first three even
coefficients negative and every subsequent one positive. In Q''/omega^6,
the negative terms have negative powers and the positive terms have
nonnegative powers. Its derivative is strictly positive on(0,infinity).
Termwise differentiation is valid on positive compact intervals. Thus Q''
has at most one positive zero, Q' at most two, and Q at most three.
The roots1,2 and c in(4,5) exhaust these zeros and are simple by Rolle's
theorem. Signs consequently alternate from Q(0)=lambda<0. This proves the
global sign pattern, not merely a sampled/local optimum.
