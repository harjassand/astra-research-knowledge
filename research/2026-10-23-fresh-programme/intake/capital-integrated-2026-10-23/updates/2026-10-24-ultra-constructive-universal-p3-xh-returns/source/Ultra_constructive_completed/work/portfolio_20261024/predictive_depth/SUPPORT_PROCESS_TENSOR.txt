# Finite-memory process-tensor acquisition: positive construction and comparator

## Scope and primary-source exposure

This bounded check read three primary sources directly:

1. Guo, “Memory Complexity of Quantum Processes,” arXiv:2203.01492v2 (2022), especially Secs. II–IV, Eqs. (1)–(7), and App. A. [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2203.01492)
2. White, Pollock, Hollenberg, Modi, and Hill, “Non-Markovian Quantum Process Tomography,” *PRX Quantum* 3, 020344 (2022), especially Secs. II–V, Eqs. (10), (33), (35)–(44), and the IBM-device applications. [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2106.11722) · [journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.3.020344)
3. Cramer et al., “Efficient quantum state tomography,” *Nature Communications* 1, 149 (2010), especially the local-reduction/MPS scheme and its certification qualifications. [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1101.4366) · [journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms1147)

Guo, Modi, and Poletti (arXiv:2004.11038) was only spot-checked for its abstract/data-generation setup and is excluded from the comparison below. No P1/P3/XH construction or new ammonia science was read, and no other source or active construction was edited.

## Positive construction: learn and compile a stationary finite quantum memory

**Access contract.** The target is a repeatable discrete-time process on an accessible `d`-dimensional system `S`. It is generated by the same unitary `U^{SE}` at each step, acting on `S` and a hidden finite-dimensional memory `E`, with `dim E ≤ D`, and a reproducible initial `SE` state. For the bond-`D` statement below, take a pure initial memory or count its purification space within `D`; a correlated initial system requires retaining its initial leg or using the paper's enlarged effective memory. The observer can prepare system inputs, retain reference ancillas, measure accessible outputs, and rerun the process from the same initial condition. The observer neither measures nor resets the actual hidden `E`. Resetting or re-preparing the whole process between shots is an acquisition assumption and cost; if the physical bath keeps memory across nominal runs, the independent-copy protocol does not apply as stated.

At each time, prepare a maximally entangled pair on a reference `R_j` and system input `S_j`; send only `S_j` through the process and keep `R_j`. Pair each `R_j` with its corresponding accessible output `O_j`. Repeated runs estimate reduced states on windows of the Choi sites `(O_j,R_j)`. The reference halves, output quantum states, measurement settings, shot count, and process reset are paid resources. This is an intervention/Choi-probe protocol, not access to hidden bath degrees of freedom.

Guo’s purified process tensor (PPT) construction supplies a positive compiler step. For a process generated by `U^{SE}`, the Choi-pair sequence has a pure sequential purification represented as an MPS whose bond dimension is at most `D`. Their tensor convention includes

\[
T|\alpha\rangle|00\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt d}\sum_{\beta,k,j} U^{j,\alpha}_{k,\beta}|\beta,k,j\rangle,
\qquad B^{j,k}_{\beta,\alpha}=U^{j,\alpha}_{k,\beta}/\sqrt d.
\]

If an upper bound `D` is known, their disentangling construction uses local tomography on windows of `w=⌈log_d D⌉+1` Choi sites, plus boundary/Schmidt information, to reconstruct an MPS representation up to an unobservable unitary gauge on `E`. The right-canonical tensor can be reshaped into an equivalent `S+E` isometry. With exact data from the stated model, this yields a reusable memory realization: compile one system-memory step and repeat it, initializing the simulator memory from the recovered state. It predicts arbitrary future intervention sequences when the step is time independent. It does not identify the original bath basis or microscopic Hamiltonian. If the initial system is correlated with `E`, Guo notes that an enlarged effective memory `D′=dD` may be needed or that the initial-system leg must be retained.

A full density matrix for one `w`-site Choi window has `d^(4w)-1` real parameters. This is only a naive data-size illustration (roughly polynomial in `D`); it is not a finite-shot, conditioning, or total-setting bound established by Guo. Their memory-complexity theorem defines memory size through PPT Schmidt rank; under a nondegenerate dominant transfer-map eigenstate it relates the complexity to `log₂(D)` for a separable initial `SE` state, with an initial-state entropy contribution for correlated starts.

**Finite-data boundary.** Guo’s construction is an exact-information/finite-bond result, not a uniform end-to-end finite-shot guarantee. It invokes local reduced-state tomography and MPS reconstruction. Cramer et al. establish efficient local-MPS reconstruction/certification in covered cases, with certification tied to a parent-Hamiltonian gap; special nongeneric states (including their GHZ-type discussion) need not be identified by the same local data. Their modified MPS-SVT postprocessing lacks a convergence proof. Stability depends on conditioning, gaps, and measurement error. Guo also notes that a noisy reconstructed MPS tensor need not reshape to a unitary; their proposed physical-unitary fit is variational and may have local minima. An implementation therefore needs a well-conditioned fit and held-out prediction checks.

## Closest operational incumbent under comparable access

White et al.’s process-tensor tomography (PTT) is the closest direct, experimentally demonstrated comparator in this bounded source check. It reconstructs a finite-horizon Choi process tensor from informationally complete CP interventions and output-state tomography, without assuming a microscopic `U^{SE}` or known `D`. A complete CP-operation basis has `d^4` maps at each intervention time, giving a generic `O(d^(4k))` setting count for a `k`-step process; maximum-likelihood estimation imposes positivity and causal normalization. The authors demonstrate the protocol on three-step superconducting-processor dynamics and use reconstructed process tensors to select gate sequences with improved multi-time circuit fidelity.

The same paper presents conditional Markov-order compression: for a model of order \(\ell\), overlapping \(\ell\)-step process tensors are stitched to predict a `k`-step sequence, with the authors giving scaling `O(k N_mle^ell)`. This is a direct intervention-to-prediction incumbent for finite horizons. The condition is operational: past/future factorization is after a chosen intermediate instrument, potentially including measure-and-reprepare. Generic quantum processes do not have exact finite *unconditioned* Markov order greater than one; the paper treats generic conditional order as approximate and checks prediction/reconstruction on held-out or random sequences. Here \(\ell\) is a temporal conditioning depth, not PPT bond dimension `D`.

**Paid controls and comparison.** Full PTT needs a minimal complete CP-operation basis. White et al. note that a convenient measure-and-prepare basis requires fast intermediate projective control and re-preparation, which may be noisy or unavailable. With only unitary controls, the reconstructed object is restricted to their span, may not determine a unique positive process tensor, and cannot certify predictions outside that operation class. Their device example also calibrates the final POVM using gate-set tomography. Both PTT and the PPT compiler require repeatable process trials and accessible reference/output measurements; neither gets free bath access or reset.

For a finite prediction horizon, PTT is already a strong answer and may be the lower-cost method. The finite-memory compiler’s potential operational value is a reusable simulator that predicts beyond the training horizon using a fixed memory register, conditional on stationarity, a known/verified low bond dimension, successful reconstruction, and stable one-step reuse. The positive operation is an existing conditional construction, not a novelty claim.

## Decisive instantiation gate

Use a blind, time-independent qubit process with hidden-memory dimension `D=2` or `4`, no hidden-environment measurement, and repeated process re-preparation. Infer only from Bell-pair/reference and output-window data, compile a one-step system-memory realization, and compare held-out output probabilities for intervention sequences longer than the training horizon against fresh process runs. Record prediction error, reset cost, reference storage/readout, settings and shots, fit conditioning, and compiled memory gates. Failure of the physical-isometry fit or held-out long-horizon prediction falsifies this instantiation. Run White-style finite-horizon PTT using the same process access and held-out sequence set as the comparator. No external experiment or physical implementation was performed here.
