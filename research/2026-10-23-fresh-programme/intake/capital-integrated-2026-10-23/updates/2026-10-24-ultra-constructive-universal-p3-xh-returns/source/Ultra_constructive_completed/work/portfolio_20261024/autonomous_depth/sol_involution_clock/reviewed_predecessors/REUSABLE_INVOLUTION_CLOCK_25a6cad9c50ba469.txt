EXACT AUTONOMOUS INVOLUTION WITH A RETURNED LOCAL CLOCK
24 October 2026. Working proof; independent bounded challenges in progress.

Positive operation: a specified Hermitian unitary W is applied to arbitrary
quantum data, while a one-head clock returns exactly to its initial product
state. A concrete nearest-neighbour, fixed-local-dimension instance implements
an n-input controlled phase and returns every clean work bit. Time is linear
in n at bounded aggregate local strength. This changes the earlier constant-
time counterflow question; it does not solve that question.

1. THE PHYSICAL CLOCK AND HAMILTONIAN

Let L>=3. The PHYSICAL clock has exactly L basis states |j>, j mod L.
Let U_0,...,U_(L-1) be data unitaries, and set

  W=U_(L-1)...U_0,                 W=W^dagger, W^2=I.             (1)

Choose alpha>0 and positive frequencies

  w_0=w_(L-1)=alpha sqrt(L/2),
  w_j=alpha sqrt((j+1)(L-j)),       1<=j<=L-2.

The time-independent physical Hamiltonian is

  H=hbar sum_(j=0)^(L-1) w_j
       ( |j+1 mod L><j| tensor U_j
         + |j><j+1 mod L| tensor U_j^dagger ).                  (2)

No vertex L is a physical clock state. A cycle of length 2L used below is a
proof space, not a processor whose distinct endpoints are identified later.

THEOREM. For T=pi/(2 alpha), every data vector psi obeys

  exp(-iHT/hbar)(|0> tensor psi)
       =(-i)^L |0> tensor W psi.                              (3)

The identity is an isometry identity, hence holds with any untouched quantum
reference and by linearity for all density operators. At kT the same clock
state is returned and the data operation is W^k, up to (-i)^(kL). In particular
the second period undoes the first. This is a reusable involutory operation,
not a forward arbitrary-unitary processor or an autonomous halt.

2. GAUGE REDUCTION AND THE TWO-SHEET PROOF

Define V_0=I and V_j=U_(j-1)...U_0 for 1<=j<L. The controlled unitary
G=sum_j |j><j| tensor V_j gauges (2) to identity labels on edges 0->1 through
L-2->L-1 and to W on the edge L-1->0. Since V_0=I, this gauge does not change
the initial or target data at clock0. Decompose data by the orthogonal
projectors Pi_+=(I+W)/2 and Pi_-=(I-W)/2. On the lambda=+1 or -1 sector,
G^dagger H G/hbar is the scalar L-cycle A_lambda with all positive edge
weights w_j except that the boundary edge has weight lambda w_(L-1).

Let B be the adjacency of a scalar 2L-cycle whose edge j->j+1 has weight
w_(j mod L). Define J_lambda:C^L->C^(2L) by

  J_lambda |j>=(|j>+lambda |j+L>)/sqrt(2),  0<=j<L.

Direct inspection of the internal and boundary edges gives

  B J_lambda=J_lambda A_lambda.                               (4)

The weights are palindromic, w_j=w_(L-1-j). Thus reflection j->-j mod2L
commutes with B. Its even subspace contains both |0> and |L>, and has basis

  e_0=|0>, e_j=(|j>+|2L-j>)/sqrt(2) (1<=j<L), e_L=|L>.

In this basis B is a path with off-diagonal coefficients

  sqrt(2)w_0, w_1,...,w_(L-2),sqrt(2)w_(L-1)
       =alpha sqrt((j+1)(L-j)),  j=0,...,L-1.                 (5)

These are exactly the matrix elements of 2 alpha J_x in the symmetric
subspace of L spin-1/2 systems. Equivalently J_x=(1/2)sum_(a=1)^L X_a.
At T, its propagator is product_a exp(-i pi X_a/2), so it sends the all-zero
vector to (-i)^L times the all-one vector. Therefore

  exp(-iBT)|0>=(-i)^L|L>,
  exp(-iBT)|L>=(-i)^L|0>.                                     (6)

Combining (4) and (6),

  exp(-iBT)J_lambda|0>=lambda(-i)^L J_lambda|0>.

Since J_lambda is an isometry, exp(-iA_lambda T)|0> is exactly
lambda(-i)^L|0>; every other PHYSICAL site has amplitude zero. Summing the
two W sectors proves (3). It is not necessary that the U_j commute. The two
directions around the physical cycle give W and W^dagger, which coincide
precisely under (1). The involution premise is load-bearing.

The odd reflection block of B is the principal submatrix on sites1,...,L-1
of (5). Hence ||B||=alpha L, and ||H||<=hbar alpha L on the one-head sector.
The largest w_j is alpha sqrt(floor((L+1)^2/4)). There is no parity exception;
L=2 is excluded because it gives duplicate edges in the stated cycle model.

3. A SCALABLE LOCAL COMPUTATION, NOT AN ABSTRACT NONLOCAL LABEL

Use n>=1 input qubits x_i and n work qubits a_i, initially |0>_a^tensor n.
The input, including any reference, is arbitrary. Put one cell at each
i=0,...,n. Cell i>=1 contains x_i,a_i and two clock qubits f_i,b_i. Cell0
needs only f_0,b_0 and can be padded to the same dimension. Thus every cell
has dimension at most16: input dimension2 and controller/work dimension8.

Define gates

  G_1=CNOT(x_1;a_1),
  G_i=Toffoli(a_(i-1),x_i;a_i),              2<=i<=n,
  C=G_n...G_1.

Each G_i has support in cells i-1,i (G_1 only needs cell1). The physical
clock cycle, in its forward order, is

  f_0,f_1,...,f_n,b_n,b_(n-1),...,b_0, back to f_0.             (7)

There are L=2n+2 vertices and edges. The edge labels in order are

  G_1,...,G_n, Z_(a_n), G_n^dagger,...,G_1^dagger, I.

Consequently their holonomy is

  W=C^dagger Z_(a_n) C,                                     (8)

a Hermitian unitary on the ENTIRE input-and-work Hilbert space. On clean
work it obeys

  W(|psi>_x tensor |0...0>_a)
    = (I-2|1...1><1...1|)_x |psi>_x tensor |0...0>_a.          (9)

Proof of (9): C writes the prefix AND of x_1,...,x_i in a_i on each input
basis vector. Z on a_n supplies (-1)^(x_1...x_n); C^dagger erases every
prefix. Extend the basis identity linearly, including a reference.

Realize a physical clock hop v->v' by

  h_e=hbar w_e (sigma^+_(v') sigma^-_v tensor U_e + h.c.).     (10)

Every term acts within one cell or two neighbouring cells in the open
chain0,...,n. The two turn edges are onsite. The sum preserves total clock
excitation number; its one-excitation restriction is exactly (2), even
though terms on adjacent edges generally do not commute. The initial clock
is the spatial product |1>_(f0) and |0> on every other clock qubit. There is
no head superposition, entangled clock preparation, end identification,
unclocked long-range gate, switched gate layer, or discarded history.

Applying (3) and (9) returns every clock and work qubit exactly and supplies
the n-input controlled phase on arbitrary quantum inputs. The positive
capability is an autonomous, clean-work phase oracle of increasing spatial
extent; it is not universal forward computation with a reset program.

4. STRENGTH, TIME, ENERGY, PRECISION, AND REUSE CONTRACT

Let J be an energy-valued aggregate local-strength budget. Each cell lies
in at most four terms (10); endpoint cells lie in three. Each term has norm
hbar w_e, also outside the one-head sector. The conservative choice

  alpha=J/[2 hbar (L+1)],
  T=pi hbar (L+1)/J                                         (11)

therefore gives sum_(e touching cell i)||h_e||<=J, uniformly in n.
The range is one cell spacing; local Hilbert dimension is fixed. There are
O(n) static terms, O(n) pure work/clock registers, and a programmed spatial
profile of couplings. No translation invariance is assumed. The input and
work have no additional bare Hamiltonian in this specified model.

On the admitted one-head sector ||H||<=hbar alpha L<J/2. At the initial
head position, every input has <H>=0 and <H^2>=hbar^2 alpha^2 L. This is an
energy-spread accounting statement, not free state preparation or a
thermodynamic work advantage; an overall energy offset is immaterial.

For a perturbation that still conserves the one-head sector and changes each
oriented hop block hbar w_j U_j by an operator of norm at most delta,
the block row/column bound gives ||H_tilde-H||<=2delta on that sector.
Duhamel's identity consequently gives

  ||exp(-i H_tilde T/hbar)-exp(-iHT/hbar)||<=2T delta/hbar.     (12)

This controls the complete input-to-output isometry, with any reference,
not just the head transfer probability. Thus inverse-polynomial additive
calibration is sufficient for a prescribed isometry error: delta<=
epsilon hbar/(2T). Specifying the bounded coefficients to O(log(L/epsilon))
bits suffices for this edge-perturbation model. Arbitrary added terms,
leakage, clock-number-changing noise, or faulty initialization require
their own norm/error budget; they are not covered by (12).

At an ideal endpoint the time-derivative norm on the input code is
alpha sqrt(L), by the displayed second moment. Hence an observation-time
offset dt obeys the separate bound

  ||[U(T+dt)-U(T)](|0> tensor .)||<=alpha sqrt(L)|dt|.          (13)

No switching or reset is required between periods, but the data is not
stationary at intermediate times. Observing or replacing the data still
requires the declared temporal interface. Reuse means exact product return
of the controller on every admitted input, not merely its marginal return.
It does not supply fault tolerance, an autonomous halt, or a noisy clock.

5. STRONG PRIORS AND COMPARISON

The main path ingredient is standard: Christandl, Datta, Ekert and Landahl,
PRL92,187902(2004), arXiv:quant-ph/0309131v2, Eqs.(12)-(15), derives the same
engineered weights and endpoint amplitude from a spin rotation:
https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0309131v2 . Feynman's 1986 time-independent
cursor Hamiltonian supplies gate-labeled propagation. Combining these
already supplies exact open-path computation, with the head at its other
endpoint. Our proof explicitly charges the nontrivial cyclic holonomy and
proves physical-head return; it does not import that conclusion from path
transfer alone. No priority claim is made for this composition or extension.

Unitary-labelled graphs and gauge/holonomy are also established machinery;
Bausch, Cubitt and Ozols, arXiv:1605.01718, Secs.5.1-5.3, treats that framework
for circuit-to-Hamiltonian spectra. Its simple-graph gauge theorem requires
identity loop holonomy, unlike (8). Quotient-transfer ancestry includes
Bachman et al., arXiv:1108.0339v2. The direct intertwiners (4)-(6) are supplied
here so that no source's scalar transfer theorem is mistaken for clock reset.

A competent timed comparator applies the same n compute gates, one Z, and
n uncompute gates in O(n) local depth, using the same O(n) clean work. It
matches the asymptotic time of (11) and avoids the engineered coupling
profile, while requiring a temporal schedule. The autonomous implementation
trades that schedule for static couplings and a prepared one-head controller;
no computational speedup or physical advantage is established.

Nagaj-Wocjan, arXiv:0802.0886, provides a stronger universal autonomous
nearest-neighbour computation model with a different high-probability,
polynomial-time output contract; its moving program is not our exact returned
clock. Exact-model differences do not establish an advantage over it.
