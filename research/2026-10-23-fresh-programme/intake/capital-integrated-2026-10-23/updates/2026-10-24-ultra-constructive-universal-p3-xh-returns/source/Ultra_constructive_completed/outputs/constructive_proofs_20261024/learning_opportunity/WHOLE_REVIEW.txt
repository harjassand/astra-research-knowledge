PASSED CONSTRUCTION
1. On the common polar radical L, A(v)=F_t(v)+F_t(0) is linear, and
   F_t(Rz+v)=F_t(Rz)+A(v). Every feasible core has exactly
   2^(n-r-rank A) lifts. Prefix balance from bijectivity therefore forces
   exactly 2^(r+rank A-t) feasible cores for every prefix. The core and kernel
   seed split is a bijection, hence the main sampler is exactly uniform and
   uses n-t fair bits. The compiled affine/core inverse follows because A is
   injective on the final radical. No teacher coordinate enters this algebra.

2. The fixed-median error count is |J+1-N/2|, including discrete ties. The
   beta-median variance and floor coupling yield the stated averaged error;
   this is not a pointwise label guarantee. Seven cube vertices are marginally
   uniform, so their union bound needs no mutual independence. Fresh trials,
   Hoeffding and low-weight interpolation then recover an entire coordinate.
   The ideal-true-prefix coupling handles adaptivity up to the first wrong
   coordinate without conditioning on future successful experiments.

3. The cached variant has a separate valid argument: preassign all true-prefix
   median tables independently of offset tapes, bound the simultaneous
   quantile event, then fix that table and use fresh offsets. Lazy table
   materialization and exact comparison/label caches preserve this law. The
   same first-error coupling links the ideal and acquired-prefix runs. Python
   PRNG fixtures remain implementation diagnostics, not entropy certification.

4. For the supplement, s_t=t-rank A_t and quotient balance give acceptance
   probability exactly 2^(-s_t); uniform accepted cores and equal-size lifts
   give an exact sampler. Expected proposal-bit cost includes rejections.
   The two-point strict-prefix inverse has outer acceptance 1/2. The shear
   example really has b=n-1 but kappa=0; it is correctly acknowledged to lie
   inside the older triangular incumbent's class.

5. The revised runtime qualification is necessary and now correct. The main
   2^b cost is simultaneous with correct acquisition, not an all-branch
   expected FPT bound. An erroneous prefix can have much larger acquired
   polar rank. In the supplement, for confidence allocations in (0,1),
     J_N=N ceil(log2(NL/eta2)),
     M=L max(511,J_64),
     B_t=2^s_t ceil(log2(M/eta1))
   cover coupon collection and every conditional preparation. log2(x)>=ln(x)
   for x>=1 gives the advertised exponential tails. J_N<=J_64 for N<=64,
   so M is a genuine call bound. Every branch has finite caps, including an
   empty erroneous quotient fibre. Coupling to the uncapped correct-prefix
   process gives success at least 1-delta_a-eta1-eta2. It does not justify
   conditioning the correction trials on all later cap successes, nor an
   unconditional expected bound in true kappa. Both cautions are retained.
