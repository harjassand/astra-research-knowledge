It adapts the established Hankel/spectral predictive-state construction to a qubit process with a hidden persistent qubit. The learned object is an observable predictor, not the hidden interaction or environment state.

1. Explicit non-Markov qubit process
====================================
Let S be the accessible qubit and M an inaccessible memory qubit. At every call the same interaction acts,

    U_theta = cos(theta) I - i sin(theta) SWAP_SM,

with a repeatable, initially hidden memory state |0><0|_M. The learner receives no theta, memory state, Hamiltonian, or system-memory operator. The concrete fixture used for the exact witness is theta=pi/4; the formula is only an evaluation fixture, not model input to the learner.

For the witness, prepare S in |1>, apply U, measure S in Z, reprepare S in |1>, and repeat. Since

    U_(pi/4)|1,0> = (|1,0> - i|0,1>)/sqrt(2),
    U_(pi/4)|1,1> = exp(-i pi/4)|1,1>,

an outcome 0 at the first measurement leaves M in |1>, after which the next outcome 0 is impossible. An outcome 1 leaves M in |0>, after which the next outcome 0 has probability 1/2. Thus

    P(o1=0)=1/2,
    P(o2=0 | o1=0)=0,
    P(o2=0 | o1=1)=1/2,
    P(o1=0,o2=0)=0.

The accessible system is reset to the identical state between calls; the previous output still changes the next-output law. A memoryless one-use qubit channel cannot produce these same conditional probabilities. This is a two-call, exact non-Markov witness, not a claim about any measured device.

The fixture has a genuinely quantum memory degree, not only a hidden classical bit: with S=|+> and M=|0>, one partial-swap call at theta=pi/4 leaves the reduced memory coherence <0|rho_M'|1>=(1+i)/4. The Z-history witness above reads a classical slice of this larger process.

2. The learned representation: operational Hankel quotient
==========================================================
For training, use system-only interventions that span the qubit's Hermiticity-preserving operation space: prepare one of four linearly independent states, for example |0>, |1>, |+>, |+i>; choose a Pauli projective readout P in {X,Y,Z}; record o in {+,-}; then prepare the next input state. A word symbol is the preparation and measured branch (j,P,o). This gives a finite alphabet of 24 intervention/outcome symbols. Each instrument consists only of system preparation, system readout, classical recording, and system re-preparation; M is never measured or controlled. The 24 measure-and-prepare branches span the 16-dimensional real space of qubit Hermiticity-preserving maps.

For a hidden qubit, each symbol induces a trace-nonincreasing completely positive linear map T_sigma on the four-real-dimensional Hermitian operator space of M. With an initial memory density rho_M and terminal functional ell(X)=Tr(X), the probability of a word w is

    p(w) = ell T_w(rho_M),
    T_(sigma_1...sigma_n) = T_sigma_n ... T_sigma_1.

Define the observable Hankel matrix by H(u,v)=p(uv), with prefix u and suffix v. Its factorization through vec(rho_M) gives rank(H) <= 4. Its minimal rank r is the predictive state dimension of the observable process: it may be less than four when a memory direction is unreachable or cannot affect any available readout. The quotient identifies exactly the degrees of freedom that affect future probabilities and discards hidden distinctions that no legal experiment can see.

If P={u_i} and V={v_j} are prefix and suffix lists with a nonsingular r-by-r block H_B, define the action block K_sigma[i,j]=p(u_i sigma v_j). Then, in one consistent basis convention, the r-dimensional transition matrices satisfy

    B_sigma^T = H_B^(-1) K_sigma.

The start/readout vectors come from p(v_j) and p(u_i). Matrix products of the fitted B_sigma reproduce every word probability in the exact rank-r class. The representation is only fixed up to an invertible similarity transform; recovering a physically meaningful M state or U is neither needed nor identified. Finite rank plus a complete prefix/suffix basis is the structural condition behind the all-word statement.

This mechanism is ordinary spectral realization applied to operational intervention records. The classical Hankel factorization and its empirical spectral learning are established; this document makes no priority claim. A process-tensor formulation gives the same probabilities as multilinear contractions. The alternative is a compact predictive state and symbol-update table that can be reused for new finite-horizon policies and reward functions without re-fitting a separate model for each task.

3. Acquisition contract and costs
==================================
Required interface promises, to be acquired rather than silently assumed:

- Repeatable complete-process starting condition for independent trials. Resetting S alone does not reset M. Every trajectory start costs C_start in time, hardware access, and any cooling/reset resource. Without a repeatable joint process start, the stated independent-shot confidence bounds do not apply; a separate mixing-time model and burn-in cost would be needed.
- Stable, labeled system interventions and readouts. To claim prediction for arbitrary qubit CP maps, the four preparations and three Pauli readout settings must be an actually spanning, calibrated instrument family. Without that, conclusions are limited to the specific labeled action library. Calibration error and expansion coefficients for unseen maps must be charged; an ill-conditioned basis amplifies them.
- Finite operational rank r<=4 (or another explicit rank cap), and an observable pivot block with singular value sigma_min(H_B)>=kappa>0. Rank is inferred from data, but finite noisy data cannot certify the absence of an arbitrarily weak unseen memory mode without a gap promise. The learner is not given the latent state or transition operators; the rank/gap is a class-level acquisition promise.
- Minimum probability p_min>0 for each prefix history used as a conditioned row. If a target prefix is rarer, either redesign the basis with accessible interventions or report that prediction as unavailable at the budget; do not quote a conditional estimate from an empty/small postselection sample.

A rank-adaptive search grows prefix and suffix lists, using one-symbol extensions to find independent rows/columns and stopping when the chosen finite spaces are closed under every alphabet symbol. In exact arithmetic, a finite r-dimensional reachable/observable space stabilizes after at most r dimension increases on each side. For r=4 and 24 symbols, the core square pivot, 24 action blocks, and start/readout vectors use 16 + 24(16) + 8 = 408 scalar probability cells. The same action blocks test one-symbol closure on both sides; adaptive basis search can add cells if useful pivots are not found immediately. This is O(r^2 |Sigma|), not 24^T full-tensor coordinates. The count assumes a useful pivot appears in the searched finite word lengths; it does not bound how many nearly dependent candidates a noisy search must reject.

For a conditional prediction p(v|u), repeat the chosen prefix u from the standard start, retain trials in which its exact outcome history occurred, and run the suffix experiment without resetting M between u and v. Given N starts and p(u)>=p_min, the number of retained prefixes is typically at least N p_min/2. Hoeffding plus a binomial lower-tail bound gives the conservative per-cell scale

    N = O( log(Q/delta) / (p_min epsilon^2) )

starts to estimate Q conditional cells within additive epsilon with failure probability at most delta. For Q=100, delta=0.05, epsilon=0.05, and p_min=0.1, the direct conditional-sampling calculation is about 36,000 complete starts per cell. The total physical exposure is N times (|u|+|v|) interactions, plus N C_start, the preparation/readout/feedback time, and calibration. No reuse across overlapping trials is credited in this count. As p_min tends to zero, required resources diverge.

A small singular value also makes realization unstable. If every r-by-r Hankel entry has error at most e_H and r e_H <= kappa/2, the inverse perturbation bound gives learned one-symbol operator error O(r^2 e_H/kappa^2). At fixed horizon T and a coordinate-operator norm bound K, product error scales at most as T K^(T-1) times the per-symbol error, times the start/readout coordinate norms. Thus achieving final prediction error epsilon can force e_H on the order of epsilon kappa^2/[r^2 T K^(T-1)]; shot cost then pays roughly kappa^-4, besides p_min^-1, epsilon^-2, log(Q/delta), and reset/exposure. This is a conservative perturbation route, not a minimax lower bound. If the estimated pivot is below the fixed threshold or the inferred rank changes under the error interval, the learner must return UNKNOWN/expand data rather than compile.

In exact data, the observable realization predicts all words in the admitted alphabet. In noisy data, an unconstrained SVD realization need not be CP/causal or even give valid probabilities on long words. A practical implementation must either fit/project a causal process tensor or promise only bounded-horizon predictive accuracy with held-out calibration. A prediction model is not a physical simulator.

4. Matched incumbent and decisive gate
======================================
The closest quantum incumbent is process-tensor tomography (PTT) with a low-memory/low-bond constraint, not unconstrained full-block tomography alone. Unrestricted qubit PTT has 16 operator-basis directions per time slot and a nominal coefficient count exponential in horizon (16^T before causal constraints). White et al.'s PTT work explicitly treats finite-horizon non-Markovian processes, reports efficiency for low-memory processes, and demonstrates predictive control. Guo, Modi, and Poletti give MPO-based machine learning for non-Markovian processes. Those methods may already obtain the same compression; the proposed PSR does not claim to beat them. The fair comparator is rank/bond-4 causal MPO/PTT trained on the exact same sequence data, full-reset count, intervention library, precision, rare-history postselection, and computing budget. The finite-block full PTT is a diagnostic upper baseline at short T.

One decisive bounded test is to hide theta and rho_M from all learners while simulating the exact partial-swap target, give each learner only the legal system-only trajectory records, and freeze a held-out bank of conditional prediction tasks before training. Include the two-call witness and a fixed bank of longer histories/futures plus two distinct policy reward functions. At matched reset and shot budgets, require 95% confidence of at most 0.05 total-variation error on the held-out conditional distributions at the declared history-probability floor. Report rank, pivot kappa, all empty/rare histories, and wall-clock/reset/interaction totals. Failure by rank>4, unstable pivot, or prediction error above 0.05 kills this finite-quotient acquisition contract for the fixture. A pass establishes only predictive reuse for this synthetic finite-memory process. It does not establish hardware performance, a general quantum-process learner, or a method advantage; if the rank-4 MPO/PTT matches or wins, the PSR route is just a compact alternate parameterization.
