Exact operator readback: Eqs. (14), (15), (18), and (55)

Source checked: local primary PDF, DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-75222-2; Eq. (14) is on printed p. 3 and Eq. (55) on printed p. 10.

Take the standard hard-core convention used by the displayed occupation term, sigma_n=|g_n><e_n| and sigma_n^dagger=|e_n><g_n|. For the product state in Eq. (18), |psi>=tensor_n(u_n|g_n>+v_n|e_n>),

  <sigma_n^dagger sigma_m>
    = <sigma_n^dagger><sigma_m>
    = (v_n^* u_n)(u_m^* v_m)
    = u_n v_n^* u_m^* v_m,       n != m.

Thus the interaction contribution to the expectation of the plus-sign Hamiltonian (14) is +sum_{n!=m} G_nm u_n v_n^* u_m^* v_m. Eq. (55), as printed, instead has -sum_{n!=m} G_nm (u_n v_m^* u_m^* v_n). Even in the real-amplitude gauge for the u_n, with real symmetric G, the summed monomials agree (the ordered sum is real), so the overall signs disagree. More basically, the printed monomial changes under an irrelevant independent overall phase on each tensor factor, whereas an expectation value cannot.

Small exact counterexample: N=2, g_1=g_2=g, delta_1=delta_2=delta>0, kappa=g^2/delta, G_12=G_21=kappa, and u_1=u_2=v_1=v_2=1/sqrt(2). Eq. (15) gives tilde-omega=omega_0+kappa. Direct evaluation of Eq. (14) gives

  E_14/hbar = (omega_0+kappa)(1/2+1/2) + kappa(1/4+1/4)
            = omega_0 + 3 kappa/2.

Eq. (55) gives instead E_55/hbar=omega_0+kappa-kappa/2=omega_0+kappa/2. The discrepancy is kappa, so no convention for a common phase of the state repairs the two equations. Consistent rephasing of local excited-state basis vectors also rephases sigma_n and G_nm together and leaves the physical interaction expectation unchanged; it cannot reverse this discrepancy.

There is a second exact check in the homogeneous positive-detuning case. With M=sum_n sigma_n^dagger sigma_n, J^-=sum_n sigma_n, and kappa>0, Eqs. (14)-(15) reduce to

  H_tilde = hbar omega_0 M + hbar kappa sum_{n,m} sigma_n^dagger sigma_m
          = hbar omega_0 M + hbar kappa J^+ J^-.

For omega_0>0 this is positive semidefinite and has vacuum energy zero. Consequently the negative condensate energy asserted from Eq. (55) cannot be an energy lowering of this effective Hamiltonian in this parameter regime. This is a narrow internal consistency result for the printed mean-field expectation and homogeneous positive detuning; it does not audit other corrections or rule on other models/regimes. The earlier collective-SW analysis is unchanged.
