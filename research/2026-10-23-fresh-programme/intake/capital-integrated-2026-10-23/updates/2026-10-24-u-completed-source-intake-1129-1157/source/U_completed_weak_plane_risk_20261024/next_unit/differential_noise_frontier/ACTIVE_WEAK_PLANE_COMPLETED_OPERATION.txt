2. Exact code, error plane, and actual Hamiltonian

Use the explicit computable smooth bump

It satisfies 0<=f<=1, is flat at both endpoints, and is positive on

For a logical qubit with Pauli X, define the isometry

 C=[c I+B b I+A a I+E e X]/n,

 sigma^2=w-z^2, D=(Z-z)C/sigma, F=[C,D], P=FF^dag, Q=I-P.

At f=0, C=c I and D=-b I by smooth continuation. Indeed
sigma^2=h^8 f^2 r_h(f), with r_h=1+O(h^8), smoothly in a fixed compact
neighborhood; divide the numerator of D by h^4 f first. Thus F is a
smooth isometry and F-[c,-b] is O(h^4) in C^2(s).

Z C=z C+sigma D exactly. This is only a FIRST-error plane: ZD need not
lie in P. Its exact outside norm is

 ||Q ZD||^2=4 p_a p_b p_0/sigma^4,
 p_a=A^2/n^2, p_b=B^2/n^2, p_0=(1+E^2)/n^2.

Consequently ||QZD||<=C a0/beta=O(h^4), and
||Q Z^2 C||=sigma ||QZD||<=C a0=O(h^8).
All constants C in this note are uniform for sufficiently small h,
s in [0,1], and g in [ell,u]. They may depend on the fixed profile and
the declared fixed physical rates/bounds.

Use the exact Hermitian frame generator

4. Uniform population lemma

for 0<=t<=delta, where p_b(0)=q(0)=0. The q jump source from c is

be absorbed into the displayed terms. These estimates must hold for

Writing x=p_b/beta^2,y=q/a0^2 reduces (2) to
x'<=C(1+x+y), y'<=C(y+x+sqrt(y)). Uniform small-time comparison

By (1), actual pulse death is at most C(e0^2 delta+a0^2 delta^3).
