ACTIVE PROTEIN STIRRING: MIXING VERSUS PASSIVE CARGO CONCENTRATION
24 October 2026. A conservation/interface result, not an acquired device,
experimental refutation or novelty claim. No simulation or parameter scan.

1. Consequential operation and selection

The candidate is a spatial patch of fuel-consuming molecular machines that
concentrates nonbinding passive cargo through an activity-noise gradient,
without a mean pump flow, capture reaction or membrane filter. This could
be a qualitatively different transport operation from faster random mixing.

Single-enzyme swimming was considered first but not pursued: strong existing
theory already treats its rotational/size limitations (Golestanian,
Phys. Rev. Lett. 115, 108102 (2015), DOI10.1103/PhysRevLett.115.108102).
The selected question is collective passive-cargo accumulation instead.

The strongest directly relevant theoretical incumbent from the start is
Mikhailov and Kapral, PNAS112,E3639--E3644 (2015),
DOI10.1073/pnas.1506825112, arXiv:1503.02610v1. It derives passive diffusion
and drift from prescribed stochastic force dipoles using far-field Oseen
hydrodynamics and a short-distance overlap cutoff. Its continuation,
Kapral and Mikhailov, PhysicaD318--319,100--104 (2016),
DOI10.1016/j.physd.2015.10.024, uses a scalar relation V approximately
2 grad D_A, while acknowledging that the order-one coefficient depends on
the cutoff approximation, and predicts nonuniform stationary cargo.

The acquired source is a THEORETICAL precedent, not a measured active
concentrator. The most direct later primary found in one bounded check,
Koyano, Kitahata and Mikhailov, Phys.Rev.E94,022416 (2016),
DOI10.1103/PhysRevE.94.022416, arXiv:1605.00427, predicts accumulation in
active membrane rafts. It retains far-field hydrodynamics and a radius-sum
cutoff, not resolved contact flow or an independent accumulation experiment.

2. Exact operation-level invariant, before stochastic closure

Let a passive point tracer occupy a periodic fluid domain of volume V, or
a fixed impermeable domain with compatible reflecting boundary. Conditional
on any prescribed smooth velocity history u(x,t), its density obeys

 partial_t p + div(u p) = D0 Laplacian p,
 div u=0,  u dot normal=0,

with constant molecular diffusivity D0, molecular Brownian noise independent
of the prescribed driver, and no binding, absorption or force that acts
differently on tracer and fluid. The velocity/history is fixed
independently of the individual tracer's position, labels or measurement
record. Initial density is uniform CONDITIONAL on the driver history/state.

Then p=1/V is an exact solution for EACH velocity realization, at every
time. Uniqueness and averaging preserve this result. The driver may be
colored, non-Gaussian, arbitrarily powered and spatially nonuniform. A
nonzero local current p u can circulate while div(p u)=0; mean particle
drift alone is not a concentration flux.

More generally, for periodic/no-flux boundaries,

 d/dt integral p ln(p V) dx
       = -D0 integral |grad p|^2/p dx <=0.

Thus even an arbitrarily powered open-loop stirring cycle cannot create
ideal point-cargo concentration free energy from the conditional uniform
preparation. This is the standard volume-preserving-advection principle,
used here to constrain the proposed concentration operation. It does not
preclude enhanced mixing, target capture with an absorbing boundary, work
on another object, or sorting with an additional physical interaction.

Finite time-correlation homogenization cannot remove an exact uniform
invariant measure. It need not yield a local Markov diffusion at all; if it
also yields a circulating effective drift, that drift cannot create density
from uniformity. No universal white-noise approximation is being assumed.

3. The full tensor fixes drift versus flux in the white-noise model

For a continuous Stratonovich limit

 dX_i=sum_a b_ai(X) circle dW_a + sqrt(2D0)dB_i,

each noise vector field must be solenoidal. Then the Ito drift and diffusion
tensor are

 V_i=(1/2)sum_a,j b_aj partial_j b_ai,
 D_ij=D0 delta_ij+(1/2)sum_a b_ai b_aj,
 V_i=partial_j D_ij.

Consequently the Fokker--Planck equation is exactly Fick divergence form,

 partial_t p=partial_i(D_ij partial_j p).

In an isotropic scalar closure this gives V=grad D, not zero Ito drift;
the current is J_i=V_i p-partial_j(D_ij p)=-D_ij partial_j p. Replacing
the full tensor by its trace before differentiating is not justified for
a general activity profile. Neither an Ito/Stratonovich label nor a
nonzero V alone establishes a concentration effect.

For independent fixed dipole sources at r with director e, let G_ij(x-r)
be a smooth divergence-free regularized Stokes Green tensor, and define

 b_i(x;r,e)=e_j e_k partial_(r_k)G_ij(x-r),
 D^A_ij=S integral c(r)<b_i b_j>_e dr,
 V_i=S integral c(r)<b_j partial_(x_j)b_i>_e dr,

where S=integral_0^infinity <m(t)m(0)>dt. Differentiation on a FIXED domain
gives V_i=partial_(x_j)D^A_ij exactly, since partial_(x_j)b_j=0. Director
averaging changes neither this identity nor its coefficient. The source
density c(r) weights source locations; it is not a freely assigned local
amplitude sqrt(c(x)) multiplying a velocity that may cease to be solenoidal.

4. The actual Oseen cutoff produces a nonzero missing surface term

Put R=x-r, s=|R|, n=R/s and retain only s>ell. For the exterior-cutoff
integrals D^ell and V^ell with the definitions above, exact differentiation
of the moving integration domain gives

 partial_(x_j) D^ell_ij=V^ell_i+B_i,
 B_i=S integral_(|x-r|=ell) c(r)<b_i b_j> n_j dS.

The plus sign uses n=(x-r)/ell. An exterior-domain integration-by-parts
normal is -n at its inner boundary and gives the same result. A mask
Theta(s-ell)b has distributional divergence (n dot b)delta(s-ell).
Discarding the inner region therefore is not a smooth solenoidal
regularization or a derived no-penetration/contact boundary condition.

This is a concrete coefficient-level issue. For the 3D Oseen tensor and
isotropic unit director,

 G_ij=(delta_ij+n_i n_j)/(8 pi eta s),
 b_i=n_i[3(e dot n)^2-1]/(8 pi eta s^2),
 <b_i b_j>=A2 n_i n_j/s^4,   A2=(4/5)/(8 pi eta)^2,
 <b_j partial_(x_j)b_i>=-2 A2 n_i/s^5.

Write a_local=4 pi S A2/(3ell). For the local affine contribution
c(x-R)=c(x)-R dot grad c, angular and radial integration yield

 D^ell_ij=a_local c(x) delta_ij,
 V^ell_i=2 a_local partial_i c,
 B_i=-a_local partial_i c.

Thus the factor 2 can arise precisely from the omitted near-field shell:
partial_jD^ell_ij=V^ell_i+B_i. These formulas describe the formal local
affine contribution, or the leading 1/ell terms for a smooth positive
profile varying on a much longer length. An affine density on all of R^3
is not a physical globally positive source preparation.

This result does NOT identify the truncated Oseen drift as a legitimate
point-tracer concentration operator. In the scalar zero-current FPE, a
model retaining V=2 grad D_A and D=D0+D_A would give p proportional to D;
that enrichment is absent from every smooth solenoidal prescribed-flow
model in sections 2--3. A conservative near-field completion must account
for the shell rather than preserving that coefficient by assumption.

5. Physical interface that could change the conclusion

The near-contact/cargo operation is the live missing mechanism. A real
finite cargo can alter the joint hydrodynamic boundary-value problem,
conditional source positions/orientations, lubrication/contact forces and
configuration-space mobility. Those changes must be derived together;
ell equal to a sum of radii is not that derivation. In contrast, the bare
Faxen correction u+(a^2/6)Laplacian u, or a fixed spatial convolution,
commutes with divergence and alone does not create a rectifier.

Moving impermeable bodies do not automatically evade the point-fluid
statement either: with incompressible fluid, material no-penetration
boundaries and conserved accessible volume, a uniform point-solute density
per accessible fluid volume remains a solution. Spatial porosity can change
the density per TOTAL volume already without active fuel; that is a
different baseline. Finite cargo exclusion/backreaction, binding, filtered
boundaries, compressible/viscoelastic phases, or tracer-conditioned control
can change the contract and are not ruled out here.

The source pattern, chemical feed and product removal also belong to a
complete active device. This invariant already allows unlimited prescribed
stirring power, so a more favorable fuel budget cannot rescue concentration
within the same point-advection contract. It does not bound every active
bath, every biomembrane cargo or all finite-particle accumulation mechanisms.
