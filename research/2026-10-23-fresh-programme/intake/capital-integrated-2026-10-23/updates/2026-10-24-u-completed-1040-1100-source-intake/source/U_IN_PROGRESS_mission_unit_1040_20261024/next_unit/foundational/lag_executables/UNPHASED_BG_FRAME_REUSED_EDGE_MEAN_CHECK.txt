Unphased Gamma-aware BG frame: exact mean and whitened signal norm
2026-10-24

Verdict
-------
The proposed formula for E G_t is exact when v_s^y(j)=Gamma_(s,(y,j))
with independent Bernoulli(q) edges and q>0. It correctly accounts for
the reused edge bit and gives the claimed ||B_j||<=3 for 0<q<=1/2. This
checks only the mean before the random per-x normalization; it does not
establish concentration or a bound on the actual normalized score.

Setup and exact score
---------------------
Fix one answer coordinate l. Index each left string by s=(x,i), with
x=s_l and i in [M], M=k^(r-1); index right strings t=(y,j) similarly.
For a row s and question y let v_s^y in C^M have entries
v_s^y(j')=Gamma_(s,(y,j')). Define

  F_i^x=(k M q)^(-1) sum_(y=1)^k v_s^y (v_s^y)^*,
  F_x=sum_i F_i^x,
  F0=E F_x=(1-q)I+qJ.

The mean follows since for one vector E[vv*]=q(1-q)I+q^2J, and there
are k values of y and M row indices i. If F_x is invertible, set
C0=F0^(-1/2), A_i^x=C0 F_i^x C0, A_x=sum_i A_i^x, and
E_i^x=A_x^(-1/2) A_i^x A_x^(-1/2). Then sum_i E_i^x=I. Alice uses
(E_i^x)^T on one half of |Phi_M> and outputs s=(x,i); Bob uses a POVM
{B_j^y}_j and outputs t=(y,j). The transpose ensures

  Pr[i,j|x,y]=M^(-1) Tr(E_i^x B_j^y).

Let

  G_(y,j)=sum_(x,i) Gamma_((x,i),(y,j)) F_i^x,
  W_(y,j)=sum_x A_x^(-1/2)
                  (sum_i Gamma_((x,i),(y,j)) A_i^x)A_x^(-1/2).

The actual score conditional on Bob input y is

  beta_y=(kM)^(-1) sum_j Tr(B_j^y W_(y,j)).

In particular, if W_(y,j)/k<=aI for every j, then for every Bob POVM,
even one chosen from all graph bits,

  beta_y <= (kM)^(-1) sum_j ak Tr(B_j^y)=a.

This is a legitimate operator-norm implication for this explicit ME/frame
strategy because its exact payoff is the displayed POVM trace objective.

Reused-edge computation
-----------------------
Fix a row s and target t=(y0,j0). For a,b in [M],

  E[Gamma_(s,t)(F_i^x)_(a,b)]
    = (kMq)^(-1) sum_(y'=1)^k
        E[Gamma_(s,t)Gamma_(s,(y',a))Gamma_(s,(y',b))].

For a!=b, the cases are:

  a=j0 or b=j0: q^2+(k-1)q^3,
  a,b both different from j0: kq^3.

For a=b:

  a=j0: q+(k-1)q^2,
  a!=j0: kq^2.

The first diagonal case uses Gamma_t^3=Gamma_t, not q^3. The first
off-diagonal case uses Gamma_t^2 Gamma_other=Gamma_t Gamma_other, not
q^3, in the y'=y0 term. Summing this row formula over the kM distinct
rows in G_(y0,j0) yields diagonal entries kq+1-q at j0 and kq elsewhere;
off-diagonal entries q+(k-1)q^2 when exactly one index is j0, and kq^2
otherwise. Equivalently,

  E G_(y0,j0)
   = kq F0 +(1-q)[q(e_j0 1*+1 e_j0*)+(1-2q)e_j0e_j0*].

Whitening the deterministic mean frame
--------------------------------------
Let lambda=1-q+qM, u=e_j-M^(-1)1, and f=M^(-1/2)1. Then
F0=(1-q)P_(f-perp)+lambda P_f and C0=F0^(-1/2). Thus

  B_j=C0(E G_(y,j)-kqF0)C0
      =(1-q){q C0(e_j1*+1e_j*)C0
                     +(1-2q) C0 e_je_j* C0}.

The cross matrix has the explicit form

  C0(e_j1*+1e_j*)C0
    =sqrt(M/((1-q)lambda))(u f*+f u*)+(2/lambda)P_f.

Because u is orthogonal to f and ||u||<=1, its norm is at most
sqrt(M/((1-q)lambda))+2/lambda. Using lambda>=qM and lambda>=1-q,
the cross contribution to ||B_j|| is at most

  q sqrt((1-q)M/lambda)+2q(1-q)/lambda
     <= sqrt(q(1-q))+2q <= 3/2

for 0<q<=1/2. Also ||C0 e_j e_j* C0||=e_j*F0^(-1)e_j<=1/(1-q), so
the remaining term contributes at most 1-2q<=1. Hence ||B_j||<=5/2<3.

Limits
------
The exact identity concerns E G_t, not E W_t: A_x^-1/2 is random and
correlated with the edge bits in G_t. Replacing A_x by I requires a joint
high-probability frame-normalization argument. If a realized F_x is
singular, the displayed inverse construction is not defined on the full
space; use a support inverse and add a complete default/failure effect,
then recompute the score bound. The frame depends on Gamma, so this is a
Gamma-aware Alice apparatus and is outside the fixed-readout posterior
theorem. No value lower bound, unrestricted gap, or purity conclusion is
claimed here.
