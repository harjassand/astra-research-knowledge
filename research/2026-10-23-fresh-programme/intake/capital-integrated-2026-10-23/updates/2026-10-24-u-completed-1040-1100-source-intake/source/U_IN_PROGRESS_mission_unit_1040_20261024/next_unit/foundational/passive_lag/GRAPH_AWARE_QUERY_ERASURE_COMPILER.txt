A graph-aware coherent-edge compiler with acquired references
================================================================

STATUS: Internal construction and failed-compilation proof, read in full
by root and checked in the bounded derivations below. This is not
external validation. Alice's
entire POVM is computed from the realized graph. Bob may optimize from
that whole graph. The selected compiler uses a large finite maximally
entangled resource, an actual fixed local filter, and square-root frame
normalization. Its common references are forced by random-matrix
concentration, not assumed. The proved failure is specific to erasing the

Earlier frozen sources and the intermediate random-phase draft are preserved.
All logarithms are natural unless a bit count is explicitly indicated.

Selected operator move
-----------------------

Start from a new, explicitly graph-aware COHERENT accepted-edge resource.
Trace Bob's old query label, then locally filter its known mean component.
Random oversampling makes the remaining full Bob state almost independent
of Alice's old input. This supplies a cheap common reference and a genuine
all-input Alice POVM on one new ME state. We test whether the original
query-sensitive fine acceptance can then be recovered by an arbitrary
Bob measurement. The whole construction, including the graph dependence
of Alice's operators, is explicit below. It fails at the fine instrument,
even though it successfully acquires the coarse reference it sought.

1. Graph-aware construction and the actual complete measurements
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Let k>=2, r>=2, N=k^r and M=N/k. Split each string as s=(x,i), t=(y,j),
where x,y in [k], i,j in [M]. The two vertex parts are DISTINCT; Gamma_st
are independent Bernoulli(q) bipartite edges, 0<q<=1/2. Questions x,y are
independent uniform elements of [k]. Use the BG answer label l=1, so the
winning predicate is simply

  output s=(x,i), t=(y,j), and Gamma_st=1.                  (U1)

No random phases are used. In H=C^M set

  v_(x,i)^y(j)=Gamma_((x,i),(y,j)),
  F_i^x=(kMq)^(-1) sum_(y=1)^k |v_(x,i)^y><v_(x,i)^y|,
  F_x=sum_i F_i^x.                                         (U2)

Let 1 denote the all-ones vector, J=|1><1|, and define the deterministic
mean and its inverse square root

  F0=(1-q)I+qJ,  C0=F0^(-1/2),
  A_i^x=C0 F_i^x C0,  A_x=sum_i A_i^x.                     (U3)

These matrices are well defined before or after observing Gamma; F0,C0
depend only on the known Bernoulli parameter and dimensions. On the event
that A_x is invertible, set

  D_x=A_x^(-1/2),  E_i^x=D_x A_i^x D_x,
  sum_i E_i^x=I.                                          (U4)

For any singular input use a fixed complete default POVM. This makes the
compiler everywhere defined, while the good event proved below needs no
default. Share |Phi_M>=M^(-1/2)sum_a |a,a>. On fresh x Alice measures
(E_i^x)^T and answers (1,(x,i)); the transpose is in that Schmidt basis.
On fresh y Bob may use ANY complete POVM {B_j^y}_j on H, chosen using all
of Gamma and all details of U2--U4, and answers (1,(y,j)). Other labels
cannot win against Alice's fixed label and may be reassigned to a valid
answer. Bob local ancillas/channels/fine outputs pull back to such a POVM.

Thus Alice is genuinely graph-aware, including all inverse square roots.
Only the offline compiler needs the full graph. At runtime neither party
receives the other fresh question or an extra joint question oracle.

2. Exact payoff, with every Bob POVM included
---------------------------------------------

For t=(y,j) set

  P_(x,t)=sum_i Gamma_((x,i),t) A_i^x,
  G_t=sum_x P_(x,t),
  W_t=sum_x D_x P_(x,t) D_x.                              (U5)

The ME identity gives the exact conditional payoff

  beta_y(B)=(kM)^(-1)sum_j Tr[B_j^y W_(y,j)].                (U6)

If c_-I<=W_t/k<=c_+I simultaneously for every t, then every complete Bob
POVM has c_-<=beta_y(B)<=c_+. Negative c_- may of course be replaced by
zero. The same holds for its graph-adaptive optimizer and its mean over y.
This is a sufficient operator bound for this explicit source, not a claim
that an arbitrary optimized game value equals a candidate operator norm.

3. The common reference is actually acquired
---------------------------------------------

For fixed x the kM vectors v_(x,i)^y, indexed by (i,y), are independent.
Their entries are independent Bernoulli(q), so

  E vv*=q(1-q)I+q^2 J=q F0.

Therefore E F_x=F0 and E A_x=I. Each rank-one summand of A_x has norm
at most

  R_F=1/[kq(1-q)],                                        (U7)

since ||v||^2<=M and ||C0||^2=1/(1-q). Its mean is I/(kM). Its centered
norm is <=R_F, and the sum of centered second moments is <=R_F I:
for a positive summand X<=R_F I, E X^2<=R_F E X, and the positive
(E X)^2 subtraction can be dropped after expanding the centered square.

Finite matrix Bernstein implies for 0<eps<=1

  Pr[||A_x-I||>eps]<=2M exp[-eps^2/(4R_F)].                 (U8)

Union over all k inputs is legitimate. This is FULL operator control,
not only a match of scalar coarse probabilities. It makes U4 a complete
well-conditioned POVM on one fixed ME resource.

4. Exact retained-query signal: the reused bit is not independent
------------------------------------------------------------------

Fix t=(y0,j0), and abbreviate e=|j0>. The N contributions
Y_s=Gamma_st A_i^x, s=(x,i), to G_t are independent across source rows s.
Inside a row, Gamma_st also appears in v_s^y0 and must not be treated
as independent of that vector.

Before whitening, for y!=y0,
  E[Gamma_st v_s^y(v_s^y)*]=q^2 F0.
For y=y0, its j0 diagonal entry is q, its other diagonal entries are q^2,
its j0/off-diagonal entries are q^2, and all remaining off-diagonal entries
are q^3. Summing all y and all N rows gives the exact unwhitened mean

  kq F0+(1-q)[q(|e><1|+|1><e|)+(1-2q)|e><e|].

Consequently

  E G_t=kq I+B_j0,
  B_j=(1-q)C0[q(|j><1|+|1><j|)+(1-2q)|j><j|]C0.           (U9)

The spike has bounded norm independent of M. Indeed
||C0 j||<=1/sqrt(1-q), and
||C0 1||^2=M/(1-q+qM)<=1/q, so

  ||B_j||<=2sqrt(q(1-q))+|1-2q|<=3.                        (U10)

No average over Bob's chosen POVM or over a graph-independent Alice
family is being substituted here. U9 is computed for the actual
Gamma-dependent operators in U3.

Every row contribution is positive and has norm at most
R_G=1/[q(1-q)]. Its centered norm is also <=R_G. Expanding the centered
second moment and using Y_s^2<=R_G Y_s gives

  ||sum_s E(Y_s-EY_s)^2||<=R_G ||E G_t||
       <=R_G(kq+3)=:v_G.                                 (U11)

Within-row dependence has already been included in U9, and different
candidate columns need not be independent. Matrix Bernstein gives,
for z>0 and

  u=2sqrt(v_G z)+2R_G z,

that Pr[||G_t-EG_t||>u]<=2M exp(-z). This u is a conservative upper root
of the usual Bernstein tail equation. Union over N candidates is valid.

5. Normalization and the actual optimized value
------------------------------------------------

On max_x||A_x-I||<=eps<=1/2, ||D_x||<=sqrt(2) and
||D_x-I||<=2eps. Since 0<=P_(x,t)<=A_x, ||P_(x,t)||<=1+eps. Expanding
D_x P D_x-P=(D_x-I)PD_x+P(D_x-I) yields

  ||W_t-G_t||<=8k eps,  for every t.

Combining this with U9--U11 gives simultaneously

  [q-3/k-u/k-8eps]I<=W_t/k<=[q+3/k+u/k+8eps]I.             (U12)

By the exact formula U6 these bound EVERY Bob POVM, including a fully
Gamma-adaptive one, without a Bob net or a fixed-Bob assumption.

For explicit probability let z=4 log N and

  eps=sqrt(4R_F z),
  u=2sqrt(R_G(kq+3)z)+2R_G z,

and assume k is large enough that eps<=1/2. The simultaneous failure
probability is at most

  2kM exp(-z)+2NM exp(-z)<=4N^(-2).                        (U13)

Now take the actual BG scale r=k^(1/3), q=k^(-1/5), along integer
perfect-cube k (or the corresponding floor variant). Then

  eps/q=O(k^(-1/30)sqrt(log k))->0,
  (u/k)/q->0,  1/(kq)->0.

Thus this complete graph-aware compiler has

  beta^*(Gamma)=(1+o(1))q                                 (U14)

with probability tending to 1. This is a FAILURE of the chosen
reconstruction route to acquire constant payoff, not an upper bound on
the unrestricted game. Its Alice operators do use the full graph.

6. The coherent source, actual local filter, and fine-state boundary
--------------------------------------------------------------------

The new graph-aware source is the normalized positive coherent edge state

  |Omega_Gamma>=|Gamma|^(-1/2)sum_st Gamma_st |s>_A|t>_B.    (U15)

Empty graphs may default. This is an explicitly prepared resource, not a
purification supplied for free by the original dephased classical source.
A central offline predicate-query success branch from a uniform
superposition produces U15 with probability |Gamma|/N^2; alternatively
reading/storing the full graph allows direct preparation. A quantum
oracle, if used, is an additional paid preparation interface. It is not
an online question oracle in U4 or a black-box physical advantage claim.

Measure Alice's old X=x and old I=i, then trace out Bob's old Y register.
The actual unnormalized residual Bob-Q state is

  (kMq/|Gamma|) F_i^x.

In particular the exact normalized fine assemblage conditioned on old X=x
is F_i^x/Tr F_x. The Bernoulli parameter q is NOT silently identified
with the realized density |Gamma|/N^2.

The mean component can be removed by an ACTUAL deterministic local
success filter on Q,

  K=sqrt(1-q) C0,  K* K<=I.                               (U16)

Its eigenvalue is 1 on the space orthogonal to 1 and
sqrt((1-q)/(1-q+qM)) on the uniform vector. Add sqrt(I-K*K) as the explicit
abort branch. Neither K nor its failure effect depends on the realized
Gamma or on a fresh question. On success, conditioned on old X=x, the
fine Bob assemblage is exactly

  A_i^x/Tr A_x,
  Pr(K succeeds | old X=x)=(1-q)Tr A_x/Tr F_x.              (U17)

On the U8 good event, F_x=F0^(1/2)A_xF0^(1/2) and Tr F0=M. Thus both
Tr A_x/M and Tr F_x/M lie in [1-eps,1+eps]. The success probability is
(1-q)[1+O(eps)], with its actual <=1 understood; no rare postselection
has been hidden. After successful K the old-X law is proportional to
Tr A_x and is O(eps) in TV from uniform.

The fine family in U17 has a genuine canonical HJW realization with
reference A_x/Tr A_x. U4 replaces it by I/M on a NEW |Phi_M>. The whole
CQ trace distance, retaining i and the actual Bob-Q substate, is O(eps)
(for example <=4eps when eps<=1/2, using
||D_x-I||<=sqrt(2)eps). Any same Bob instrument contracts that distance.
Alice's former private quantum workspace and a purification of the traced
old Y are not retained by this statement. No LOCC conversion of U15 to
the new |Phi_M> reference is asserted; this is an existential compiler
using a separately prepared fixed reference resource.

The query-resolved fine state that gives the original edge support uses
one |v_s^y><v_s^y|, whereas U17 contains the SUM over every old y. HJW
preserves the latter assemblage; it cannot declare the old y to be the
new independently supplied fresh y. U12--U14 test every Bob readout on
the actual retained system and show that this particular erasure cannot
be repaired there merely by a more elaborate graph-aware Bob POVM.

Retaining old Y avoids that sum but restores the original rank problem:
for a fixed old x there are only M coherent row vectors in dimension kM,
so its full Bob coarse state has rank at most M. The strong common
full-space reference acquired above is precisely what the erasure bought.
This is an actual source/instrument tradeoff, not a theorem that every
payoff-sensitive quotient or every use of the coherent resource must fail.

7. Costs and remaining unrestricted obligation
------------------------------------------------

|Phi_M> has Schmidt rank M and log2 M ebits. Gamma itself has N^2 bits;
direct acquisition reads N^2 entries. Full Gram formation, inverse square
roots, and a naive O(kM^3)-entry POVM specification are large offline
resources. Physical numerical implementation needs precision/error
budgets beyond the exact finite proof. U15 is an additional coherent
preparation with local dimension N, and its filter branch is explicitly
paid. No quantum query, sample, runtime, or experimental advantage follows.

The scientific content is that the proposed Gamma-aware operation really
does force cheap full coarse references by random oversampling, yet its
complete fine payoff tends to zero because the old query has been erased.
This is beyond the fixed-profile and graph-blind apparatus restrictions;
it is still one selected, explicitly analyzed compiler. Other joint
encodings, query-retaining operations, non-ME reference choices or
Gamma-aware Alice constructions are not bounded by U14. The typical
random graph's unrestricted tensor and commuting values remain UNKNOWN.
