UNPHASED GAMMA-AWARE COMPILER: MEAN, WHITENING, AND RADIUS CHECK
2026-10-24. luna_truncation. Checks only the unphased algebra and
concentration parameters in the proposed extension.

Let N=kM, s=(x,i), t=(y,j), and v_s^y(j)=Gamma_(s,(y,j)) with independent
Bernoulli(q) edges. Define

  F_i^x=(kMq)^(-1)sum_y |v_s^y><v_s^y|,
  F_x=sum_iF_i^x,
  F_0=(1-q)I+qJ,  C_0=F_0^(-1/2),
  A_i^x=C_0F_i^xC_0,  A_x=sum_i A_i^x.

For q<1, F_0 is positive definite. Since an unphased vector has diagonal
second moments q and off-diagonal second moments q^2,

  E |v_s^y><v_s^y|=qF_0,
  E F_i^x=F_0/M,  E F_x=F_0,
  E A_i^x=I/M, E A_x=I.

The kM independent rank-one summands in A_x are
  (kMq)^(-1)|C_0v_s^y><C_0v_s^y|.
Their norms are at most 1/[kq(1-q)], since ||v||^2<=M and
||C_0||^2=1/(1-q). Thus the stated rank-one frame radius is correct for
the summands of A_x (not for the whole A_i). Matrix Chernoff gives
max_x||A_x-I||<=eps with eps=O(sqrt(logN/(kq(1-q)))) w.h.p.

Exact unphased target mean

Fix t=(y0,j0), let e=|j0> and let 1 be the all-ones vector in C^M.
For one row s=(x,i), X_s=Gamma_(s,t)F_i^x. Its row is independent of
other X_s, though X_s is correlated with the t-edge inside F_i^x. For
y!=y0 the target bit is independent of v_s^y and
E[Gamma_(s,t)|v_s^y><v_s^y|]=q^2F_0. For y=y0, only the target column
coordinate shares that bit. Direct entrywise calculation gives

  E[Gamma_(s,t)|v_s^y><v_s^y|]
    = q^2F_0 + q(1-q)[q(e1*+1e*)+(1-2q)ee*].

Summing over y, dividing by kMq, then summing over the kM rows gives

  E G_t = kqF_0 +(1-q)[q(e1*+1e*)+(1-2q)ee*],
  G_t=sum_s Gamma_(s,t)F_i^x.

This confirms the proposed unphased spike formula, including the
correlation of Gamma_(s,t) with its own row frame.

Whitening the mean and row bounds

Set H_t=C_0(EG_t)C_0=kqI+B_j. The residual is

  B_j=(1-q)[q(C_0e)(C_0 1)*+q(C_0 1)(C_0e)*
                         +(1-2q)(C_0e)(C_0e)*].

The vector 1 is an eigenvector of F_0 with eigenvalue 1-q+qM, while
e has squared projection 1/M onto its span. Consequently

  ||C_0 1||<=q^(-1/2),  ||C_0e||^2<=1/(1-q).

For q<=1/2 this yields
  ||B_j||<=2sqrt(q(1-q))+|1-2q|<=1+sqrt(2)<3,
so the proposed bound ||B_j||<=3 is safe. It follows that
||H_t||<=kq+3.

Each whitened row effect A_i^x is positive and

  ||A_i^x||<=||C_0||^2||F_i^x||<=1/[q(1-q)]=:R,

because ||F_i^x||<=1/q. The independent row contributions
X_s'=Gamma_(s,t)A_i^x satisfy 0<=X_s'<=RI. Their centered radius is
at most R and their matrix variance obeys the correct chain

  sum_s E(X_s'-EX_s')^2
    <= sum_s E(X_s')^2
    <= R sum_s E X_s'
    = R H_t,

so its norm is at most R(kq+3). Matrix Bernstein plus a union bound over
N target columns gives

  u=O(sqrt(R(kq+3)logN)+R logN).

In the stated BG regime q=k^(-1/5), 1-q is bounded below and 1/q<=k,
so this is O(sqrt(k logN)+(logN)/q), as claimed.

The remaining Alice normalization uses D_x=A_x^(-1/2) on the event
max_x||A_x-I||<=eps<=1/2. For P_(x,t)=sum_i Gamma_(s,t)A_i^x<=A_x,
the same estimate
  ||sum_x D_xP_(x,t)D_x-sum_xP_(x,t)||<=8k eps
follows from ||D_x||<=sqrt2, ||D_x-I||<=2eps, and ||P_(x,t)||<=1+eps.
Thus the unphased algebra and claimed radii support the same payoff
argument, with mean H_t=kqI+B_j and a spike contribution bounded in
absolute value by 3/k. No phase-cancellation assumption is needed after
this F_0 whitening.
