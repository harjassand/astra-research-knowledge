POLYNOMIAL DIVISORS DEFEAT THE FIXED-DEGREE INVERSE PREFIX

Status: exact mathematical obstruction to the actual proposed point list,
not merely to a finiteness lemma. No explicit uniform graph sampler or
obstruction to all other samplers is claimed. The source target D->infinity
is weaker than Mao's superlogarithmic marginal degree and still fails here.

1. Statement and literal construction being ruled out

Fix any integer r>=1. For arbitrarily large extension degree ell choose
F_(2^ell)=F_2[alpha], in its polynomial basis, and list the first n nonzero
binary coefficient vectors as u=1,2,...,n. Take any feature map whose
coordinates are Boolean polynomials of degree<=r in the bit coordinates
of (u,u^(-1)). In particular it may be the full degree-r lift.

There is a constant n0(r), independent of ell, the irreducible polynomial
defining alpha, and primality of ell, such that whenever n>=n0(r) and ell
is sufficiently large, these feature columns have a bounded-size nonempty
linear dependency. Consequently the proposed uniform-alternating-form
graph law, even after a uniform vertex permutation, is NOT exactly uniform
on every fixed number of edges eventually. Enlarging the field or choosing
ell prime does not fix this polynomial-basis-prefix point list.

2. A finite set of explicit coefficient points

Choose distinct monic irreducibles p_1,...,p_k in F_2[t], put L=product p_i,
and let d=deg L. For every exponent vector e in {0,1,2}^k define

                A_e=product p_i^(e_i),
                B_e=L^2/A_e=product p_i^(2-e_i).

There are exactly 3^k distinct nonzero monic polynomials A_e by unique
factorization. Both A_e and B_e have degree<=2d. Their padded coefficient
pairs y_e=(coeff A_e,coeff B_e) lie in F_2^M, M=4d+2.
The degree-r Boolean lift has R(M,r)=sum_(j=0)^min(r,M) binomial(M,j) rows.
If 3^k>R(M,r), its columns have a nonzero binary dependency. The constant
row makes its support even. Choose any support minimal among the nonempty
dependencies; this is a genuine circuit of size w<=R(M,r)+1.

For every fixed r such k exists constructively. For example choose one
irreducible of each degree 1,...,k; then d=k(k+1)/2 and R(M,r) grows only
polynomially in k, whereas 3^k grows exponentially. Finite fields supply
an irreducible of each positive degree. No asymptotic code conjecture or
unprovided small-bias space enters this count.

3. Evaluation preserves the ENTIRE fixed finite matroid

Suppose alpha has algebraic degree ell>2d. Evaluation of polynomials of
degree<=2d at alpha is an injective F_2-linear map. L(alpha) is nonzero.
The block map

 H:(a,b) -> (a(alpha), L(alpha)^(-2)b(alpha))

is therefore injective from F_2^M to F_2^(2ell). On y_e it gives exactly

              H(y_e)=(A_e(alpha),A_e(alpha)^(-1)).

An injective binary linear map preserves every dependency of a fixed-degree
Boolean lift, in BOTH directions. Indeed each target degree<=r polynomial
pulls back to degree<=r; conversely choose a binary linear left inverse
of H and pull each source polynomial back along it. Multilinear reduction
on the Boolean cube never increases degree. The two restricted evaluation
row spaces, hence their kernels, coincide. Thus the chosen circuit and
its minimality hold for every ell>2d, not only for generic specializations.
All A_e(alpha) are nonzero and pairwise distinct for the same degree reason.

Moreover A_e(alpha) has polynomial-basis integer label <2^(2d+1). Thus the
ENTIRE finite configuration is in the literal first-n point list once
n>=2^(2d+1)-1. Its denominator was cleared without moving it to arbitrary
field labels. This is the essential prefix gate, absent from a mere
algebraic-closure countercurve or an arbitrary full-field relation.

4. Fully instantiated small-degree counts; no huge circuit replay needed

The first thirteen monic irreducibles, encoded by coefficient bits, are

              2,3,7,11,13,19,25,31,37,41,47,55,59.

The first eight have total degree22. For r=2 this gives M=90 and

                  3^8=6561 > 1+90+4005=4096.

All thirteen have total degree47. For r=3 this gives M=190 and

       3^13=1594323 > 1+190+17955+1125180=1143326.

Thus r=3 already fails once ell>94 and the point prefix contains all
nonzero polynomial labels below 2^95. These are large FIXED thresholds,
not practical performance claims or a request to enumerate a million-
column circuit. The analytic rank count proves a fixed finite circuit
exists. One could acquire it by exact binary Gaussian elimination, but
that execution is unnecessary for this obstruction theorem.

5. Exact graph-marginal failure survives vertex permutation

For the graph law in the opening let v_i be the chosen feature vectors,
and sample a uniform alternating binary form B. Suppose a nonempty fixed
support S has sum_(i in S) v_i=0; let w=|S| and choose a center outside S.
On the w-edge star joining that center to S, the edge parity is identically

               sum_(i in S) B(v_center,v_i)=0.

After a uniform vertex permutation, fix any observed w-edge star. There is
positive probability its leaves map to S and its center lies outside S.
For EVERY permutation, averaging its Walsh character over uniform B is
either 0 or 1, since it is a linear character of the form coefficients.
The favourable permutations therefore give a strictly positive overall
Walsh expectation. Under G(n,1/2) that expectation is zero. Hence this
fixed-size edge marginal is not exactly uniform for any sufficiently
large n. The bias may be tiny: this proof makes no lower-bound claim on
asymptotic low-degree L2 advantage or failure of a strong rank detector.

6. The requested minimal function-field gate is reached directly

Already at r=2, choose a minimal coefficient circuit as above and put
u_i(t)=A_i(t). The points are distinct nonzero elements of F_2(t), with
u_i^(-1)=B_i/L^2. The same injective coefficient-map argument preserves
the minimal degree-two relation over this regular function field and
over EVERY finite specialization of degree>2d. Thus this is an actual
varying configuration with constant field F_2, rather than the F_128
shape constants in the earlier doubled-code line.

It also supplies a positive-dimensional geometrically irreducible F_2
curve in a reciprocal self-dual-code locus after extending the coefficient
row space. The projective map is nonconstant: distinct monic A_i have a
nonconstant ratio. Only finitely many parameter values have degree<=2d;
away from them the relation remains minimal by Section3. No general
classification of the surrounding reciprocal variety is asserted.

Scope: this retires the literal inverse graph with a polynomial-basis
prefix and any fixed-degree feature map. It does not rule out other
enumerations or point designs, mixtures, other exact graph samplers, or
Mao's advised distribution. No new finiteness hypothesis is proposed.
