# Astra method redesign, 26 October 2026

No civilisation-changing breakthrough or generally superior discovery method is established by this round. The useful outcomes are a completed, checked computational operation; a machinery combination whose apparent advantage failed a stronger ordinary comparison; and specific corrections to how I select, equip and coordinate research. The live scientific portfolio and Dots were preserved. These computational experiments do not measure discovery rates in physics, chemistry or biology.

## What failed in the previous design

I had not actually tested several of the coordination questions that mattered. The six investigators were solo constructors, forbidden children and sibling communication. The three packages also allowed different self-selected problems. All first starts were fresh and all continuations warm. Thus the results cannot identify a team policy, model advantage, or warm-versus-cold effect. Three candidate tasks failed preflight; the amended opportunity-selection pilot was useful exploration, but it answered a different question from a matched construction trial.

The common evidence packet omitted exact adjacent Astra knowledge. Q28 reconstructed equality and binary claims already supplied by Dots N965/N970. Its finite Umegaki representation remained useful at a narrower scope; the later reviewer refinement was separately credited. This is a demonstrated knowledge-selection defect. The size of any causal discovery benefit from better retrieval is still unmeasured.

The packet also contained several detailed failed thermal/acquisition routes and one strong quantum foothold. Five investigators chose thermal/mechanical problems and one chose that foothold. This is consistent with context anchoring, but shared expertise and available computational tools are competing explanations. There was no alternate-context condition.

Several selected questions asked for uniform guarantees against arbitrarily slow hidden modes, arbitrarily narrow unseen transitions, or uncalibrated internal controls. Those classes admit exact indistinguishable alternatives. Investigators produced valid obstructions and conditional inverses; those did not acquire the missing physical operation. The prompt allowed a complete scientific unit or decisive blocker, and the continuation foregrounded stress-testing the existing construction. That may have favoured completing an obstruction, but the documents do not reveal private motivations or establish causation. Q17 and Q28 also made substantial constructive pivots, so a blanket claim of inability to invent would be false.

The missing middle was an unresolved operation with available inputs, executable machinery and a genuinely strong incumbent. That does not mean every fundamental theory must immediately build a device. It means that a theory's claimed consequence must identify its actual missing link, and an invention cannot count an assumed interface as an acquired capability.

## New experiment: two real team organisations

Each team had an active lead and exactly two fresh constructive workers, with the same inherited model/settings, priors, code and task. One team used independent whole-problem constructors followed by synthesis. The other worked backward from the desired operation and used complementary certificate/computation constructors. Both could challenge representations and use strong existing methods. This was one comparison of complete policy packages, not an isolated test of backward reasoning alone.

The operation was to turn the accepted finite quantum-reference formula into an executable, globally certified comparison evaluator for positive rational three-input/three-output classical channels. Both received the exact adjacent priors and working numerical tools. They had to construct the global bound rather than assume an optimiser or supplied witness. Four development cases and six withheld cases were fixed before the teams ran. First constructions froze before holdout release; both then continued with the same warm workers. All 172 frozen first-stage source files remained unchanged.

**Both teams completed the stated endpoint.** Independent replay and independently implemented rational certificate checks accepted all ten asymmetric cases and three controls. The maximum asymmetric interval widths were approximately 2.66e-9 and 2.80e-8, far inside the frozen tolerance. Reviewer fresh-process evaluations were below one second on these inputs. These are observed local measurements, with imports/checking/serialization included, not universal runtime guarantees.

The scientific mechanism is substantive. A channel-independent operator covariance permits exact vertex certificates in one regime. Below that regime, a small convex certificate and moment realization cover genuinely noncommuting quantum memory. Denominator-only coordinates remove an unnecessary inverse of K and support singular K. General Gram/moment constructions provide alternative proofs and certificate acquisition. Numerical solvers propose candidates; exact inequalities and witnesses establish the intervals. This is an acquired computational operation rather than another statement that evaluation is possible in principle.

**There is no coordination winner.** Both policies succeeded, and substantial constructions existed in each lead and worker context before synthesis. The problem did not demonstrate a need for three people to close separate indispensable interfaces. The ten matrices test software and mathematics; they are not ten independent research-policy trials. Actual computation was not hard-matched, and the comparison cannot identify a generally optimal headcount or model.

Independent adversarial testing found implementation differences outside the frozen endpoint. One portable implementation needed optional solver packages to narrow several singular/poorly conditioned cases, and retained a valid wide interval on one highly anisotropic input. The other narrowed all nineteen reviewer probes. This is a useful numerical robustness observation, not a general organisational ranking. No uniform precision/time claim for every rational bit length was made. Nine of the ten asymmetric endpoint cases use the easier vertex regime, and all ten returned scalar lower witnesses; the known quantum control supplies the genuinely noncommuting witness. The all-reference proof remains valid, but this instance selection is weak evidence about difficult quantum-witness acquisition.

The audit also found that one batch checker accepted an empty list or duplicated row while omitting a required input. Actual replayed per-row certificates were correct. A separately labelled warm repair now rejects missing/duplicate/unknown results and ambiguous input IDs; ten independently built regression checks passed. Original failures and frozen outcomes remain preserved. The repair does not retroactively become treatment success.

The baseline search was incomplete. A fresh reviewer identified the older free-convex Positivstellensatz and rational-optimisation literature. A post-output implementation of its generic SOS machinery certified upper bounds on all thirteen inputs in about 1.2 seconds, including imports, with a maximum gap of 1.73e-7 to independently checked candidate lower witnesses. It used the newly exposed covariance linearisation, had no independent lower collector and had uninstrumented dependency setup. It is not a pretrial equally efficient full evaluator or proof that the task-specific reduction was already known. It does establish that a claim to inventing general finite SDP/free-positivity machinery would be wrong. The specific implementation, small reduction and historical priority must be distinguished.

Primary literature: [Helton–Klep–McCullough](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.4859), [Volčič, Theorem 5.2 and §5.5](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/forum-of-mathematics-sigma/article/hilberts-17th-problem-in-free-skew-fields/1DC74DC3E0E011210C826FF4DCA24DE1). The older Astra N405 source is relevant single-channel machinery, with its actual source-reported review status; it does not directly settle this two-channel problem.

## New experiment: use existing machinery to supply an exact interface

A separate investigator connected a public finite-precision Gram–Schmidt signing implementation to Astra N24's exact sparse-law compiler. The hypothesis was that an aggressive, possibly biased numerical proposer could be surrounded by exact checks, symmetric branching and exact compression to preserve the required output semantics.

The adapter works. All 180 applicable primary outputs had exact means, quotas, binary atoms and the declared support bound. I independently reimplemented those arithmetic checks without importing the producer code. The invariant is transparent: retaining both opposite branches preserves the parent mean regardless of proposer bias; exact affine compression preserves it again.

**No added capability was demonstrated.** Cheap ordinary controls often tied or beat the imported method. Its only residual lead disappeared when an ordinary exact optimiser was allowed to tackle that same n=16 instance: it produced certified discrepancy 26575/172032 instead of 80357/172032. I independently checked all 16,384 feasible vertices and the separating certificate. The original counts and this selected post-hoc comparison remain separate.

Two further design weaknesses became visible. The initial n<=8 limit on exact global control was too conservative for that particular n=16 case. Also, the conservative per-step envelope was loose in the small primary cases, so zero natural proposal rejections were weak robustness evidence. Separate injected guards exercised rejection/fallback, but they are not natural reliability trials. This does not show the external algorithm is useless in other regimes; it prevents claiming an advantage here. Retain the working adapter, and do not scale this route without a consequential task and a binding regime where strong ordinary methods actually fail.

## Changes to my coordination method

1. **Select the missing operation or explanatory advance before writing a grand prompt.** Backcast from a consequential capability, then check the route forward. Identify which step must actually be invented or acquired, which assumptions are necessities, and which are inherited conventions. Preserve an independent foundational route that may change the explanation itself; do not force every branch into an immediate application test.
2. **Retrieve by operation as well as topic.** “Global positivity of an operator expression” points outside quantum-information vocabulary toward free convex optimisation. Supply a few governing results, working constructors and precise failure scopes. Broad permission to search Astra is inadequate; dumping the whole archive is another untested context intervention. Inspect actual proofs and runnable code across repositories before trusting a title or claimed interface.
3. **Treat representation as a research variable.** Removing the unnecessary inverse of K changed supported inputs and software requirements. That is a concrete example of a representation improvement. Asking for “three mechanisms” previously elicited short screens, not comparable deep construction. Future breadth tests need actual constructive effort on distinct mechanisms.
4. **Allocate helpers to scientific uncertainty.** Independent whole attempts help expose alternatives; complementary roles help when there are real interfaces to close. This case supports neither as a universal default. Keep an active scientific owner; use a small helper on a genuinely different construction or needed operation, and require useful scientific artifacts rather than surveys. Model choice and headcount need their own tests.
5. **Preserve productive state and use fresh contexts deliberately.** Warm completion preserved proofs/code and passed unseen cases. A same-context capacity recovery returned an already completed handoff without repeating research. Neither observation ranks warm against cold discovery. A history-inheriting fork is not independent. Fresh reviewers received the governing scientific objects without the parent diagnosis or policy key; source prose still limited masking.
6. **Make persistence advance the live obstruction.** If a mechanism fails, test a materially different available operation or representation. If the operation is complete, identify a scientifically meaningful next question. More fixtures, tightened constants and indefinitely busy workers are not substitutes for a consequential opening. Do not call a necessary substep the mission.
7. **Compare against the strongest plausible ordinary route early.** Search for transferable machinery before freezing a weak incumbent. Charge acquisition, preparation and verification to the comparison. Preserve selected post-hoc controls as such. A future task can be valuable even if solved with old tools, but it cannot be presented as a new scientific framework merely because our initial baseline missed those tools.
8. **Keep learning and communication small.** Dispatcher updates now carry the scientific delta, governing source and actual decision; transport records stay in their existing location. This reduced observed message bulk. It is not yet evidence of better discovery. Researchers were not given new organisational reporting forms.

The next discriminating method question is opportunity and knowledge selection across genuinely different scientific tasks, followed by continuation/representation order where it can alter a live construction. A new trial needs a capable incumbent and a nontrivial, available operation before more workers launch. A repeat of this easy-to-saturate team comparison would not answer it. Long-horizon discovery, cold versus warm ownership, model choice and programme-wide revolutionary return remain untested.

The appropriate ambition remains a new powerful theory, physical capability or technology. These results improve our ability to pose and execute a discovery attempt; they do not establish that Astra is converging on a civilisation-changing outcome. Protect the productive long-running scientific contexts, including Dots, while improving frontier selection and cross-field knowledge use.
