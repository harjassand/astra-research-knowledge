# Retrospective of the six construction paths

The clearest failure mechanism was a mismatch between self-selected universal capability questions and the operations available to answer them. Researchers repeatedly turned that mismatch into exact information obstructions or conditional constructions. That produced real mathematics, but rarely the missing acquisition or physical operation. A separate, demonstrated context failure caused Q28 to reconstruct existing Astra results. The records do **not** identify a superior instruction package or show that caution, short work periods, or warm continuation alone caused the outcome.

This diagnosis reads the actual plan/amendment, mission/evidence, arms, continuation, and all six researchers’ development notes and both results. It is a retrospective of public scientific artifacts, not a reconstruction of private reasoning or a new correctness audit. No experiment was launched.

**What the design actually selected.** The original plan required admitted, accessible, unsolved fixed tasks and proposed eighteen starts. All three candidates failed admission; the amendment changed the question to “OPPORTUNITY SELECTION AND CONSTRUCTION end-to-end,” with six starts and self-selected questions. This removed the matched-question contrast. The operative allowances were **40 + 30 minutes**, superseding the plan’s 20 + 15; allowances were ceilings, not measured equal computation. A/B/C each already permitted representation changes, source inspection and decisive failures. B added an operations-first procedure; C additionally required three mechanisms, including one outside the inherited formalism. These are bundled, small additions to a demanding common mission. **Six concrete hypotheses, with competing explanations.** These are causal candidates to test, not findings about researchers’ internal motives.

1. **A completed negative was easier to select than an open construction.** The mission allowed finishing for “a complete supported scientific unit or a genuine decisive blocker”; every arm requested an object “or an honest decisive failure.” Q54’s explicit choice for a decisive proof/test is the strongest process evidence. Q54 and Q95 selected universal guarantees for which a single hidden transition or arbitrarily slow mode suffices to defeat the target. This can attract effort toward impossibility construction before a tractable positive operation is selected. Rival explanation: these were important questions whose obstruction was the scientifically correct result, independent of wording. The study does not compare their value with accessible positive opportunities. [Mission:11](/Users/harjas/Documents/ChatGPT/coordination/work/astra-method-experiment-20261026/public/MISSION.txt:11), [arms:2–4](/Users/harjas/Documents/ChatGPT/coordination/work/astra-method-experiment-20261026/ARMS.json:2).

2. **The compact context functioned as a negative topic curriculum.** Although it said “These are resources, not the space of possible science,” the packet concentrated on information loss, failed acquisition, unavailable instruments, thermal models and passive assembly. Five paths selected thermal/mechanical passive-system questions; the sixth selected its highlighted entropy foothold. That clustering is consistent with anchoring, but also with shared prior knowledge and computationally available models. There was no alternate-packet condition. The missing N965/N970 priors are a more definite defect: “selectively use Astra” did not supply the exact adjacent theorem. Two post-outcome known-item searches favored the existing task-first route, but cannot establish a general retrieval law. [Packet:3–15](/Users/harjas/Documents/ChatGPT/coordination/work/astra-method-experiment-20261026/public/STARTING_EVIDENCE.txt:3), [retrieval check:2–8](/Users/harjas/Documents/ChatGPT/coordination/work/astra-method-experiment-20261026/root_checks/RETRIEVAL_ROUTE_CHECK.txt:2).

3. **Rejecting false tasks left an untested middle.** Pretrial screening correctly rejected solved simulators and physical claims lacking raw observations or actuator interfaces. The replacement asked fresh researchers to find consequential questions and complete them in one short block, without a new admission check on their chosen questions. Q54/Q95 then imposed broad observation classes whose uniform targets were inaccessible by construction; Q63 granted calibrated internal controls that its motivating specimen lacked. The design tested neither a source-complete, genuinely unresolved intermediate operation nor extended domain immersion. This is not a reason to revive the rejected geometry or spline benchmarks. Their actual failures remain valid. [Interrogation admission:33–82](/Users/harjas/Documents/ChatGPT/coordination/work/astra-method-experiment-20261026/task_builders/interrogation/TASK.txt:33), [matter admission:27–46](/Users/harjas/Documents/ChatGPT/coordination/work/astra-method-experiment-20261026/task_builders/matter/TASK.txt:27), [amendment:5–9](/Users/harjas/Documents/ChatGPT/coordination/work/astra-method-experiment-20261026/PRETRIAL_AMENDMENT.txt:5).

4. **C’s breadth rule favored cheap screening, not demonstrated exploration.** Its requirement was to “form three materially different mechanisms or problem representations” **before choosing**. Q28’s first two physical routes were rejected in short source-based paragraphs; Q54’s candidates were screened and one proof route developed. Thus C changed the visible menu but supplied no comparable construction effort to the alternatives. Rival: those screens correctly avoided unsupported interfaces. The records cannot show that longer exploration would have produced a bridge, or separate breadth from “outside the inherited formalism.” [Arms:4](/Users/harjas/Documents/ChatGPT/coordination/work/astra-method-experiment-20261026/ARMS.json:4), [Q28 notes:5–15](/Users/harjas/Documents/ChatGPT/coordination/work/astra-method-experiment-20261026/trials/Q28/stage1/RESEARCH_NOTES.md:5).

5. **Stress-first warm continuation encouraged completion of the selected formal object.** The continuation began “Continue the same research task using your preserved first construction,” then ordered competing-explanation and full-domain stress before a stronger positive attempt. Q42/Q63/Q95/Q54 largely extended the same obstruction or conditional model; Q17 and Q28 are substantial counterexamples to total lock-in. Every second round was warm, so productive continuity and path dependence are inseparable. An audit can make a claim more accurate while leaving the missing operation untouched. This order, rather than warm context itself, is a concrete manipulable hypothesis. [Exact continuation](/Users/harjas/Documents/ChatGPT/coordination/work/astra-method-experiment-20261026/CHALLENGE.txt:1).

6. **Role and timescale combined to privilege proof-complete simulation.** Each researcher had to select a question, retrieve priors, supply operations, construct, compare, and assess novelty; reviewers came after outputs froze. Primary-source omissions were therefore discovered after Q28’s construction, and new reviewer construction could not help its frozen outcome. Read-only computational access also made exact passive models much easier to complete than missing material/instrument calibration. The high consequence gate was legitimate, but almost all short-stage progress was scored below it. This does not prove the gate suppressed useful invention, or that more time would suffice. Q17 stopped early with a complete unit; Q28 grew despite an interruption. Actual work distribution and any long-horizon counterfactual remain unknown. **Opportunities left untested.** Q17 identifies finite transition width, hysteresis/lag and loading as the operation-level variables that would matter; endpoint ON/OFF numbers are insufficient. Q42 leaves useful finite isolation with complete actuator/resource accounting open. Q63 leaves configuration-dependent spectral noise and independently supported loss compensation outside its theorem. Q54/Q95 show that coarse task predicates or one-sided engineering tolerances can be useful without acquiring an exact law; conventional methods already realize their present versions. These are real boundaries to investigate, not evidence of a hidden breakthrough. The study also did not test a constructive collaborator during research, a source-complete fresh handoff, a different packet, or longer construction periods. None should be recommended as generally superior from this record.

**One discriminating follow-up to prepare, not launch: reverse construction and audit order on Q28’s actual computational obstruction.** This isolates hypothesis 5 while removing the known source omission and question-choice confound.

The common task would be to turn the accepted finite free formulation into a **certified, numerically controlled evaluator for a preregistered family of asymmetric three-input classical channel pairs**, with proved upper/lower brackets, explicit singular/zero-denominator handling, and measured full construction/evaluation resources. The obstruction is real: the existing proof controls a *fixed supplied witness* but “does not supply a uniformly well-conditioned optimizer or a cheap way to acquire one”; an audited state-lift example still had about 0.00363 ratio error at vacuum weight 1e-80. Merely repeating the equality, cutoff or algebraic decidability would not complete this task. [Frozen proof:136–170](/Users/harjas/Documents/ChatGPT/coordination/work/astra-method-experiment-20261026/review_material/Q28/stage2/finite_reference_proof.md:136), [audit:27](/Users/harjas/Documents/ChatGPT/coordination/work/astra-method-experiment-20261026/reviewers/audit_finite_reference/REVIEW.md:27).

Before admission, implement/check the strongest supported ordinary global method on that exact family. If it already supplies the operation cheaply, reject the task as solved. Freeze the family, required precision/resource bounds, public priors N965/N970/N676, Q28’s accepted proof, separately credited cutoff refinement, and complete incumbent. Both conditions receive exactly that scientific packet and the same final consequence criterion. No unavailable device is assumed.

Use fresh contexts in both conditions, with randomized repeated starts and two equal useful-work blocks. One order first audits the inherited proof/strongest conventional explanation, then constructs the missing evaluator; the other first constructs the evaluator, then performs the identical audit. Only paragraph/activity order changes; both must complete the same final verification. Preserve first-block objects and all failures; audit final artifacts without order labels. Do not use prose quality, number of alternatives, or compliance as the endpoint.

If construction-first repeatedly supplies an independently checked general evaluator while audit-first only strengthens known theorems, that supports an order-induced allocation mechanism at this obstruction. If audit-first wins correctness or both reach the same operation, the proposed mechanism is weakened. If neither completes it, the result remains unable to distinguish task hardness, insufficient allowance, and inadequate methods. A successful evaluator would still need its own originality and consequence assessment; this is a mechanistic test on a real unresolved bridge, not automatic civilisation-level success or a programme rollout.
