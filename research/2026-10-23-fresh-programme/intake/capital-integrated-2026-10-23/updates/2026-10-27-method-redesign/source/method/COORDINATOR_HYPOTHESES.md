# Diagnosis and hypotheses before the new comparison

The last experiment primarily tested complete prompt packages with self-selected questions. It did not test team organisation: each investigator was forbidden subagents and sibling communication. Both instruction and question choice varied; all starts were fresh and all continuations warm. Two starts per package and one short horizon cannot identify an optimal discovery method. The unchanged civilisation-level standard is a mission criterion, not a useful sole response variable for a small mechanistic experiment.

The most concrete errors were in our information and opportunity design. The common packet supplied several detailed failures and one especially strong quantum-theory foothold. Exact adjacent Astra proofs were omitted, and generic permission to retrieve them failed to prevent rediscovery. Several investigators selected universal acquisition guarantees that were defeated by arbitrarily narrow transitions or arbitrarily slow modes. The experiment rewarded a complete scientific unit or decisive blocker and then foregrounded audit in continuation; that offered a tractable endpoint in a proof of nonidentifiability. This is a plausible influence, not a demonstrated account of private motivation. Important impossibility results remain science.

There was also an untested middle between solved demonstrations and fully realised physical breakthroughs: an unresolved operation with its inputs actually available, existing construction machinery and a strong implemented incumbent. Three rejected initial tasks did not show that this middle is empty. External hardware absence is a resource limit, not a refutation of all physical invention. The earlier meta-research tranche explicitly proposed same-information constructive escapes from an exact indistinguishable pair; our self-selected package comparison did not execute that hypothesis.

Hypotheses to investigate:
1. Capability backcasting can improve question choice: identify a major desired operation, work backward to the binding missing primitive, and forward-check every interface against known operations. It may also become a planning trap or suppress unexpected foundational discoveries; useful changed-output pivots must remain visible.
2. First-principles reconstruction should separate measured constraints, mathematical necessities and inherited conventions. A change of physical mechanism may escape a failed observation class; another proof inside that class may not. Discarding all established physics is not an evidential method.
3. Claim-shaped just-in-time retrieval can prevent duplication and expose constructive primitives. The exact N965/N970 miss supports this local correction; two known-item searches do not prove a general retrieval law. Full-corpus dumping can create another context problem.
4. Tool-bearing context may matter more than extra strategy adjectives. N751 to N760 supplies a legal nonlocal transport construction, N24 supplies an exact sparse-law compiler, and primary external repositories provide real candidate-generating machinery. None alone establishes a new discovery; actual compatibility and an ordinary incumbent must be tested.
5. Complementary positive constructors plus an active scientific lead may close a capability chain more reliably than equal numbers of whole-problem attempts followed by selection. Independence can instead win by avoiding a common mistaken decomposition. This is the next isolated team-policy contrast.
6. The order of construction and audit may change effort allocation. Cold/warm history, elapsed depth, additional information and audit order are separate factors. Do not vary them all and label the result a context effect.
7. Communication should transmit scientific deltas and exact source pointers rather than repeated transport receipts. This correction has been deployed to the dispatcher; the next reply was substantially compressed. That is an observed communication change, not evidence of improved discovery.
8. Model selection should follow the actual creative, implementation or source task. Existing Ultra/Luna histories do not identify causal model capability. The new comparison holds inherited models/settings fixed and changes team policy; it does not test model choice.

The first new experiment uses one live unresolved fundamental-computation operation: turn the checked finite quantum-reference representation into an actual global interval evaluator. Both teams get the same priors, proof, numeric tools and competent baseline. One policy uses independent whole-task constructors; the other uses backward reasoning and complementary constructive roles. The package effect cannot identify which ingredient caused a difference. Held-out matrices test the algorithm, not independent replications of the research organisation. A complete checked computational operation can be evidence about a method; it will not be called a civilisation-changing breakthrough. A single pair earns no programme-wide rollout. Preserve Dots and productive programme contexts.

Other candidates remain available: dense compact transport modules are not yet source-complete; the exact-signing proposer interface is executable but may be ordinary recombination; reaction-network synthesis has dependency/verification gaps. They were not silently substituted as easy wins. A second scientific domain is required before any broad team-policy preference.

A separate machinery-composition case study is now assigned to the warm machinery investigator: numerical GSW signing proposals -> exact rational checks -> symmetric branches -> exact sparse-law compilation. The hypothesis is that semantic invariants can be supplied by a checked compiler rather than by an unbiased floating proposer. This could be ordinary recombination; frozen contracts and strong ordinary comparisons must decide actual added capability. It is not part of the paired team-policy contrast.
