# Exact-law bridge: completed case study

**The checked adapter works. No new output capability or superior research method was established.** This is separate from the quantum team comparison and is not a causal prompt experiment.

`checked_interface.compile_exact_law` now connects numerical sign proposals to exact rational row checks, symmetric `+/-` branching and exact sparse-law compression. It supports N24's cube/partition contracts: positive rational weights, exact means, binary atoms, support at most `n+1`, frozen face coordinates and every supplied quota. Failure triggers the existing exact signer. The [governing argument](GOVERNING_ARGUMENT.md) uses no GSW statistical theorem.

## Separately labelled ordinary stress check

A post-hoc specification targeted that sole lead under the same 20-second ceiling. Ordinary global optimisation over all 16,384 face-feasible vertices produced a 14-atom exact-mean law with **certified optimal discrepancy `26575/172032`**, versus GSW64's `80357/172032`.

Construction took 0.747 seconds including startup. A separate stdlib/Fraction audit took 2.527 seconds and verified the law and optimality separator. [POST_HOC_RESULT.json](POST_HOC_RESULT.json) preserves both. This selected follow-up leaves primary counts unchanged; the residual lead did not survive stronger ordinary machinery.

## Failures and actual increment

Primary runs had zero natural proposal rejections, fallbacks or timeouts. Their conservative signing envelope is loose at these dimensions, so that absence is weak reliability evidence. Separate guards retained 328 deliberately failed proposals, including upstream step limits, false-success nonbinary/nonfinite outputs, strict-bound equality and an excessive row sum. Exact fallback succeeded. Five invalid-input controls and eight corrupted law/separator controls were rejected. Full traces and audits are retained.

The useful increment is executable checked interoperability. N24 already supplies the same output contract and describes replacing its signer; proposer/checker separation, symmetric branching and affine compression are established operations. This adapter does not acquire the sharper entropy-sensitive bound, general-matroid rounding, physical capability or a new scientific principle.

Primary subprocesses total 52.394 seconds of recorded wall time; coding, setup and separate audits are additional work. Model/billing costs were not measured. No outside runtime, retrieval checkout or prior report was changed. No further trials were launched after this result.

**Decision:** retain the adapter and exact optimiser as reusable machinery. Do not credit discovery or recommend a research-method rollout.
