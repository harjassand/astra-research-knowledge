# Constructive effective-control comparator

Read in full: `ASTRA_SSH_EMBEDDED_PAIR_RESONANCE_AND_CZ_20261027.md`, source SHA-256 `c44fcc6b9e0f51d0c7fe79e621a727c6165f8e39553bb6e9e07a94cb60ae6af3`. This result tests its section 7 comparator; it does not independently audit the source's infinite-volume spectral theorem.

**Result:** exchange plus local Z phases cannot synthesize CZ, even up to global phase. Adding arbitrary local single-qubit gates gives an exact CZ in total exchange time `pi/(2 |J|)`, which is optimal for the ideal effective control problem. The latter control set is stronger than the phase-only set declared for the source protocol.

Use computational order `00,01,10,11` and the explicit normalization

\[
 H_{\rm ex}=\frac J2(XX+YY),\qquad
 E(\theta)=e^{-i\theta(XX+YY)/2},\quad \theta=Jt.
\]

Thus the exchange matrix on `01,10` is `J X`. From source equation (6),

\[
 J_g=\frac{E_+-E_-}{2}
 =\frac{g^2}{8}-\frac{g^4}{24}+O(g^6)>0
\]

for sufficiently small positive `g`. A known one-memory dynamical phase is removed when identifying this effective Hamiltonian.

1. **Exact phase-only obstruction.** Every Hamiltonian generated by `XX+YY`, `ZI`, `IZ` and a scalar identity preserves excitation number. Split its propagator into the even block on `00,11` and the odd block on `01,10`. Each allowed generator satisfies

   \[
   \operatorname{tr}H_{\rm even}=\operatorname{tr}H_{\rm odd}.
   \]

   Therefore every time-ordered sequence, including simultaneous or arbitrarily interleaved phase controls, obeys

   \[
   \boxed{\det U_{\rm even}=\det U_{\rm odd}.}
   \]

   This follows directly from the determinant evolution equation and is preserved by multiplication. Local occupation phases `exp(i alpha n_L)` and `exp(i beta n_R)`, rather than traceless Pauli rotations, obey the same relation. Global phase multiplies both determinants by the same factor.

   CZ instead has even determinant `-1` and odd determinant `+1`. It violates the invariant, including after any global phase. Arbitrarily long sequences cannot overcome it; the invariant also excludes arbitrarily accurate approximation to CZ by this closed effective control group. A faster entangling operator is available: `E(pi/2)` is inverse iSWAP and `E(pi/4)|10>` is maximally entangled. These are different gate targets.

2. **Explicit CZ with arbitrary local gates.** Write `X_L=X tensor I`, let `H` be the Hadamard, and `S=diag(1,i)`. Since `XX` and `YY` commute and conjugation by `X_L` reverses `YY`,

   \[
   X_L E(\pi/4)X_L E(\pi/4)=e^{-i\pi XX/4}.
   \]

   An exact identity, including the global phase, is

   \[
   \boxed{\mathrm{CZ}=e^{i\pi/4}(S^\dagger\!\otimes S^\dagger)
   (H\!\otimes H)X_LE(\pi/4)X_LE(\pi/4)(H\!\otimes H).}
   \]

   Operations act from right to left. This requires two exchange intervals of duration `pi/(4 J_g)` each. All adjacent local operations can be combined, leaving three local layers: an input layer, one intervening `X_L`, and an output layer. Conjugating the result by `I tensor H` gives CNOT.

   The total exchange time is

   \[
   \boxed{T_{\rm ex}=\frac{\pi}{2J_g}
   =\frac{4\pi}{g^2}[1+O(g^2)].}
   \]

   The source protocol's time `12 pi/g^4` is asymptotically `3/g^2` times this exchange duration. Wall-clock cost is `T_ex + T_local` when the interaction can be suspended during the local layers. If their total duration and other overhead are bounded as `g` decreases, the total remains `O(g^-2)`. Such local controls, their calibration, and their error cost must actually be supplied. Finite-duration pulses under the always-on full SSH Hamiltonian require a separate implementation analysis.

3. **Optimality within the ideal effective problem.** Here arbitrary local gates have zero duration and the exchange strength has fixed magnitude `|J|`. In the magic basis

   \[
   \frac{|00\rangle+|11\rangle}{\sqrt2},\quad
   i\frac{|00\rangle-|11\rangle}{\sqrt2},\quad
   i\frac{|01\rangle+|10\rangle}{\sqrt2},\quad
   \frac{|01\rangle-|10\rangle}{\sqrt2},
   \]

   local SU(2) gates are real orthogonal matrices and the exchange Hamiltonian is `diag(0,0,J,-J)`. For the magic-basis propagator `U`, define `m=U^T U`. Instantaneous local gates leave `m` unchanged. During exchange,

   \[
   \dot m=-iGm,\qquad G=2U^TH_{\rm ex}U^*,
   \]

   so `G` has eigenvalues `2|J|,0,0,-2|J|`. Continuous local Hamiltonians cancel from this derivative as well, because their magic-basis matrices are antisymmetric under transpose.

   After removing global phase to put the target in SU(4), a gate locally equivalent to CZ has `m` eigenvalues `+i,+i,-i,-i`. Suppose the total exchange duration were `T < pi/(2|J|)`. Continuous eigenphase branches starting at zero would all have magnitude below `2|J|T < pi`, so no branch could wind. The two branches ending at positive phase `pi/2` would have sum `pi`. At almost every time their derivative sum is at most the sum of the two largest eigenvalues of `G`, namely `2|J|`, by the variational principle for a Hermitian matrix. Integrating gives `pi <= 2|J|T`, a contradiction. Degeneracies can be resolved using the instantaneous derivative within the degenerate eigenspace. Thus the exhibited duration is minimal. Equivalently, the minimum exchange area for varying strength is `integral |J(t)| dt = pi/2`.

4. **Source and physical boundaries.** The obstruction concerns a pure effective exchange Hamiltonian plus Z controls. It does not apply to the full SSH Hamiltonian: the source specifically derives a connected pair energy `chi_g=-g^4/12+o(g^4)`, which supplies the missing conditional phase. Its `O(g^-4)` echo can therefore escape the effective determinant obstruction if the source's spectral and control premises hold. This test proves no speed lower bound for arbitrary phase-driven manipulations of the full SSH system.

   Taking source equations (6), (11) and its spectral-concentration argument as premises, a free segment of duration `O(g^-2)` approaches the effective exchange code isometry after known local phases: `chi_g T=O(g^2)` and the pair leakage vanishes. A fixed finite composition of ideal arbitrary memory gates would then transfer the exact effective sequence to an asymptotic full-model comparator by the triangle inequality. This remains conditional on the source theorem and on those additional ideal gates; no finite-`g` error threshold or physical pulse construction was obtained here.

   The source protocol explicitly grants local phase pulses. Its faster-CZ comparator additionally uses transverse rotations such as `X` and `H`; these change excitation number and require coherent drive resources beyond those phase pulses. They cannot silently be treated as acquired or free. The source's equal-resource sentence supports a faster entangling iSWAP under the stated effective exchange resources. A faster *same-target CZ* follows only after the stronger local-control assumption is made explicit and costed.
