## Exact phase-only reachable group

Use the computational order `00,01,10,11`, Pauli matrices `X,Y,Z`, and independent ideal controls

\[
H(t)=J(t)(XX+YY)+a(t)ZI+b(t)IZ.
\]

Arbitrary real strengths, nonnegative durations, and independent pulse timing are admitted. Write

\[
A=XX+YY,\quad C=YX-XY,\quad D=ZI-IZ,\quad S=ZI+IZ.
\]

On the one-excitation subspace `01,10`, these act as `2X,2Y,2Z,0`. On `00,11`, only `S` acts, as `2Z`. Thus the exact reachable group is

\[
\boxed{G=\left\{
\begin{pmatrix}
e^{-i\phi}&0&0&0\\
0&u&v&0\\
0&-v^*&u^*&0\\
0&0&0&e^{i\phi}
\end{pmatrix}:\ \phi\in\mathbb R,\ |u|^2+|v|^2=1\right\}.}
\]

Necessity follows by block restriction of the time-dependent Hamiltonian: the middle block is traceless Hermitian, and the two outer energies are opposite. Sufficiency follows because `D` and `A` implement arbitrary `Z-X-Z` Euler rotations in the middle block, while `S` independently supplies any outer phase. Equivalently the Hermitian Lie algebra is exactly `span_R{S,D,A,C}`: `S` is central, `[D,A]=4iC`, `[A,C]=4iD`, and `[C,D]=4iA`. No commutator produces `ZZ`.

If physical occupation controls `n_L=(I-ZI)/2,n_R=(I-IZ)/2` replace traceless Pauli controls, the exact vacuum-fixed group is

\[
G_n=\{\operatorname{diag}(1,V,\det V):V\in U(2)\}.
\]

This differs from `G` only by the permitted global-phase convention. Both satisfy the stronger-than-number-conservation invariant

\[
\boxed{\det U_{\rm even}=\det U_{\rm odd},\qquad
U_{\rm even}=U|_{00,11},\quad U_{\rm odd}=U|_{01,10}.}
\]

The equality is preserved under all compositions and limits, and under an overall phase because both blocks have dimension two. For `CZ=diag(1,1,1,-1)`, the even determinant is `-1` and the odd determinant is `+1`. Hence neither CZ nor any global-phase multiple is reachable. The closed invariant also rules out arbitrarily accurate approximation by exchange-plus-phase sequences. The mere fact that iSWAP is entangling does not remove this obstruction.

## What arbitrary local rotations add

Let `E(theta)=exp[-i theta(XX+YY)]`, `X_L=X tensor I`, and `Q=Hadamard tensor Hadamard`. Then

\[
E(\pi/8)X_LE(\pi/8)X_L=e^{-i\pi XX/4},
\qquad Qe^{-i\pi XX/4}Q=e^{-i\pi ZZ/4}.
\]

The first identity is exact: conjugation by `X_L` changes `XX+YY` to `XX-YY`, and these two Hamiltonians commute. Finally,

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{CZ}=e^{-i\pi/4}
 e^{i\pi(ZI+IZ)/4}\,Q E(\pi/8)X_LE(\pi/8)X_LQ.}
\]

This needs non-phase local gates `X` and Hadamard. Their determinants can be adjusted to single-qubit `SU(2)` by irrelevant phases. For a fixed exchange coefficient `J`, its two exchange segments each take `pi/(8|J|)`; this is a construction, not a time-optimality claim.

For the literal full-iSWAP comparator, put `F=E(pi/4)`, the signed inverse of conventional iSWAP, `R=exp(+i pi XI/4)`, and `B=exp(-i pi IX/4)`. Exact identities are

\[
F(XI)F^\dagger=-ZY,\quad F^\dagger=(Z\otimes Z)F,
\quad BFRF^\dagger B^\dagger=e^{-i\pi ZZ/4}.
\]

The same local phase correction above therefore gives CZ using two full signed iSWAPs and local rotations. The inverse costs one full exchange gate and local phases. This directly validates the source's synthesis statement under the enlarged resource set.

More generally arbitrary local rotations plus nonzero exchange reach all `SU(4)` (and thus all two-qubit unitaries up to global phase). The exact echo isolates `XX`; local conjugations supply all nine two-body Pauli tensors, alongside the six local Pauli generators. These span the full 15-dimensional `su(4)`, so the generated connected subgroup is `SU(4)`.

## Consequence for the source and method evaluation

Source lines 292–319 explicitly assume phase pulses and dynamical phase calibration; line 321 changes to “local single-qubit gates.” If that phrase means only phases, its controlled-phase synthesis inference is false. If it means arbitrary rotations, the synthesis is correct, but “equal-resource” is conditional on acquiring and costing those rotations. With effective `J(g)=Theta(g^2)`, constant-count ideal exchange synthesis has `O(g^-2)` interaction time once the extra local controls are available and full-code exchange errors/leakage are controlled.

This does not contradict the proposed fourth-order echo. The full hard-core SSH-plus-bath Hamiltonian is not exactly the exchange-only two-qubit Hamiltonian. Its claimed connected phase is precisely a non-exchange resource; an effective term `chi n_L n_R = (chi/4)(I-ZI-IZ+ZZ)` contains the missing `ZZ` generator. The proof above is not a lower bound against all control protocols on the full SSH bath, nor an independent verification that the source's fourth-order term and long-time theorem have been established.

For assessing the research method, this corrects one potential premature rejection: faster entangling exchange does not by itself reproduce the target CZ under phase-only controls. The source can be evaluated as a conditional mathematical route to a phase operation using its declared controls. Conversely, the mismatch supplies no device advantage, historical novelty, or general method superiority. A fair comparison must fix the target operation and acquired control set, then include finite-g error, local-drive calibration/time/noise, leakage, memory decay, and preparation/reset costs. One repaired comparator does not establish a general discovery-method winner.
