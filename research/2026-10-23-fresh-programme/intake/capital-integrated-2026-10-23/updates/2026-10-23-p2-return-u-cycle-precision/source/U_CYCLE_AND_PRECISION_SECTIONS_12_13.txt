12. Nearest-neighbor cycle locality still forbids an escape

Let Q be an irreducible balanced time-homogeneous generator supported on a
nearest-neighbor cycle, allowing both directions or a directed cycle. The
classical cyclic-variation-diminishing result of Ben Avraham, Sharon, Zarai
and Margaliot (arXiv1807.02779v1, Definition3, Theorems4(3) and5) implies
that every nonzero solution of x'=Qx eventually has no two adjacent zero
coordinates. Root directly checked these precise statements in the primary
PDF, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.02779. This is the imported source theorem.

Our consequence: E=ker(exp(TQ)+alpha I) has real dimension at most two.
Otherwise the two adjacent coordinate functionals have a nonzero common
kernel vector v in E. But exp(mTQ)v=(-alpha)^m v repeats those adjacent
zeros for every integer m, contradicting the eventual source property.
Q preserves E. A real negative exponential on a real space of dimension
at most two requires exactly one complex-conjugate frequency pair; real
generator eigenvalues exponentiate positively and Jordan terms cannot
remain in the sampled eigenspace. The flat-sign single-frequency argument
of Section10 now implies alpha<=exp(-pi), without assuming normality.

Thus the 48-state escape requires graph coupling beyond a nearest-neighbor
cycle. This is a derived graph restriction on exact returned classical
population modes, not a universal physical locality or efficiency theorem.
No extension to time-dependent generators is inferred here. Historical
priority of this binary-return consequence remains unassessed.

13. Finite rational precision preserves the raw logical gain

For dense V2 only, round every Q entry to the nearest2^-28, giving R and
entry error delta<=2^-29. Set H=I-11^T/48 and Qround=H R H. Because
Q=H Q H, each correction error is at most4delta. This is below the
verified minimum off-diagonal rate; Qround is therefore again a balanced
generator. Its entries have denominator dividing48^2 times2^28. Root
independently performed this exact rational construction and checked every
positive off-diagonal and both balance equations.

Two column-stochastic semigroups satisfy the Duhamel bound
||exp(t Qround)-exp(t Q)||_1<=t||Qround-Q||_1. At time pi, worst input
TV error is at most2pi*48*delta<5.61987e-7. The actual constructed matrix
has the stronger exact-rational bound pi||Qround-Q||_1/2<3.37564e-8,
using pi<22/7. No matrix exponential or floating tolerance is used here.

The ideal V2 gain is
  [exp(-0.99999pi)-exp(-pi)]/2 >1/1600000.
Indeed 3<pi<22/7, exp(pi)<24, and exp(x)-1>x. The bound exp(22/7)<24
was checked with a rational Taylor sum and geometric remainder. Therefore
the rounded generator still has raw logical success gain greater than
5.91243e-7 above the exact normal/four-state return ceiling.
