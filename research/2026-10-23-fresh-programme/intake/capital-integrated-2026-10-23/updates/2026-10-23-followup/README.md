## U thermal-calibration bridge

Under a fixed passive Gaussian model, a Hurwitz drift, full invertible readout, invariant independent sensor covariance and a known common occupation shift of every dissipative bath, the source derives `ΔC = Δn I` and a metric/drift identification up to a unitary gauge using time-resolved lag data. The exact counterexamples show that partial bath control can give a wrong metric while passing an internal closure check, and invariant colored sensor OU noise can make different pump/current states observationally indistinguishable. Absolute currents still need a trusted sensor/pump reference and a physical energy observable. The residuals in the JSON are synthetic numerical checks; no physical validation, general heat-current inference or practical advantage is claimed.

The U result and P2’s converter/calorimetry work address a broad measurement theme, but no shared model, specimen or intervention contract is supplied. They are kept as separate evidence.
