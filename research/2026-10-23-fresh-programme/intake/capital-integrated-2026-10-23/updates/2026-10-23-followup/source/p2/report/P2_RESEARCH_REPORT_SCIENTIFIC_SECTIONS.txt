1. STRONGEST RESULT AND HOW THE INVESTIGATION CHANGED

The strongest retained mechanism is a nonlinear photonic converter whose
spatial susceptibility controls which thermal frequency transitions occur.
The intended operation is to remove heat from a colder body by raising the
frequency of its thermal photons and delivering them to a hotter reservoir.
The drive supplies the difference in photon energy. Frequency-conversion
refrigeration itself is established prior art [S1,S2], not a new principle
discovered here.

The independent work developed three more specific results:

(i) A sharp, analytically optimized loss/contact threshold for a reduced
hot-side converter, including the hot thermal noise that accompanies its
absorption. Material normalization translates it into an optimistic bound
on cold heat per absorbed pump watt.

(ii) An explicit static sign-changing susceptibility that cancels the
dangerous lower-frequency heating transition. Its complete selection rule
contains additional upper-frequency transitions. Charging every resonant
channel preserves cooling in a declared synthetic network, but changes both
the useful heat and the required work.

(iii) A sharp exact-isolation theorem for a different, graded susceptibility.
In an infinite equally spaced scalar cavity ladder with a fixed cosine pump,
isolating just one edge can require a severe stored-pump penalty. For the
worked example it is exactly 121. Finite-band optimization relaxes that
penalty; allowing useful upper cascades performs better than imposing exact
purity in the synthetic comparison.

The easy practical construction did not survive. A mixed-source GaAs
reference assembled from known nonlinear conversion and absorption records
has an absorption-only COP envelope of 8.81e-10 at a diffraction-scale
pumped volume per mode pair. This is not a measured GaAs performance limit
or a theorem against all photonic refrigeration. It rejects that particular
independently favorable two-mode parameter construction and identifies the
missing joint material/field interface. A separate, favorably normalized
three-mode cascade calculation improves the explored ratio to 1.56e-9,
without resolving that gap. The two-mode theorem is not applied to the
cascade as if it were a universal bound.

The work also produced an exact calorimetric ambiguity: opposite signed
cooling increments can have identical normalized temperature-relaxation
curves. A fixed-temperature electrical-substitution measurement resolves
that ambiguity, provided pump-induced changes in thermometry and other heat
paths are independently controlled.

2. PHYSICAL MECHANISM AND CONTRACT

Let omega_c < omega_h and Omega = omega_h - omega_c. The coherent pump gives

  H_int/hbar = g a_h^dagger a_c + g* a_c^dagger a_h

in the resonant interaction frame. The number current I from c to h carries

  Q_c,ideal = hbar omega_c I,
  W_conversion = hbar Omega I,
  Q_h,rejected = hbar omega_h I.

The ideal conversion COP is omega_c/Omega. The forward occupation bias is

  Delta0 = n(omega_c,Tc) - n(omega_h,Th) > 0,
  n(omega,T) = 1/[exp(hbar omega/(k_B T))-1].

For Tc<Th this requires omega_h/omega_c > Th/Tc. Approaching equality reduces
the useful occupation difference to zero; finite heat then becomes fragile
to absorption, leakage and unwanted conversion. The mechanism is powered
refrigeration under ordinary thermodynamics, not energy extraction from
equilibrium or a violation of the second law.

Assumptions of the reduced models are independent thermal Markov baths,
carrier-resolved narrow modes, approximately flat bath spectra over their
linewidths and coherent splittings, and a justified resonant rotating-wave
description. Couplings can exceed linewidths but must remain small compared
with carrier frequencies and neglected detunings. These assumptions have
not been derived from a fabricated structure in this work. Pump-induced
nonthermal noise would require an additional measured noise model.

The full operating ledger is

  Q_net = Q_c,optical - P_abs,cold - Q_other_leaks,
  P_wall = (W_conversion + P_pump,abs + P_unrecovered)/eta_source
           + P_auxiliary,
  COP_wall = Q_net/P_wall.

The definition of each port must avoid double counting. Recoverable coherent
stored pump energy is not repeatedly charged as fresh dissipation. Initial
pump charging, thermal preparation, shutdown/reset, and control costs must
be amortized over a stated operating interval. A thermally discharged object
is not a steady refrigerator. No advantage is claimed here using only the
frequency-ratio COP or by moving absorption to the hot side.

2.1 Finite-duration completion and the steady-rate limit

The numerical COP comparisons below concern steady operating rates with the
declared model costs. They are not computed finite-protocol wall COPs. For
a completed cycle with steady dwell tau, define complete signed residuals

  E_c = tau Q_ss - Q_c,cycle,
  E_w = W_wall,cycle - tau P_ss.

Both rates and residuals must use the same system boundary and costs. Then

  COP_cycle = (tau Q_ss-E_c)/(tau P_ss+E_w),
  COP_cycle <= COP_ss iff P_ss E_c + Q_ss E_w >= 0

for positive cycle work. Neither residual has an automatically favorable
sign. They include transient departure from steady operation, preparation,
reset, cold shutdown deposition, actual source/recovery efficiencies and
all control work. Continuous source inefficiency is proportional to dwell;
it cannot be amortized away like a one-time startup cost.

For a prescribed time-dependent coupling, the laboratory-frame drive power
contains both conversion and ramp work:

  P_drive = hbar Omega I + 2 hbar Re[dot g C],
  C=exp(-i Omega t)<a_h^dagger a_c>, I=2 Im[g C].

Setting g=0 at both endpoints removes the interaction-energy endpoint term
but does not prove zero integrated ramp work. Equivalently, a dynamical pump
model must charge the same transfers once, without adding conversion work
again to measured source power that already contains it.

For coherent stored pump energy U_s=P_abs,ss/kappa_abs and shutdown cold
deposition fraction lambda_c, its cold debit relative to useful dwell heat
is lambda_c(P_abs,ss/Q_ss)/(kappa_abs tau). Passive exponential ringdown never
exactly empties the store at a finite time: a final recovery/dump operation
or an explicit remaining stored-energy term is required. All finite buffers
consumed as resources must be restored or carried in the energy/entropy
ledger. The detailed derivation and a conditional dwell-time error bound
are in thermal_mechanism/FINITE_CYCLE_AUDIT.txt. Actual protocol residuals
and finite-cycle performance remain UNKNOWN.

3. EXACT BATH-CURRENT MODEL AND FAILURE CHANNELS

For two modes j=1,2, let K_j=sum_r kappa_jr, f_j=kappa_jc/K_j, and
nbar_j=sum_r kappa_jr n(omega_j,T_r)/K_j. With C=4|g|^2/(K_1 K_2),

  I = K_1 K_2/(K_1+K_2) * C/(1+C) * (nbar_1-nbar_2),
  N_1 = nbar_1-I/K_1,   N_2 = nbar_2+I/K_2,
  Q_r = sum_j hbar omega_j kappa_jr [n(omega_j,T_r)-N_j].

Hence

  Q_c = Q_star C/(1+C) - L_b,
  Q_star = hbar(omega_1 f_1-omega_2 f_2)
           K_1 K_2/(K_1+K_2)(nbar_1-nbar_2),
  L_b = sum_j hbar omega_j K_j f_j(1-f_j)
        [n(omega_j,Th)-n(omega_j,Tc)].

The resonant first law is Q_c+Q_h+W_conversion=0. Direct covariance solutions
checked 500 random resonant and 500 detuned population cases; the maximum
scaled residual in the saved run is about 9.2e-14. Detuned population agreement
does not certify an exact detuned work formula or a microscopic bath model.

For direct coherent pumping, g=g0 sqrt(N_p), so absorption typically costs
linearly in C. The normalized balance q=C/(1+C)-alpha C-ell has

  C_opt = alpha^(-1/2)-1,
  q_max = (1-sqrt(alpha))^2-ell

when 0<alpha<1 and unlimited drive is allowed. Finite drive bounds tighten
the result. Optimized two-pump mixing can instead produce a sqrt(C) cost;
thermal_full_cycle/model.py gives the general p-power threshold. A different
cost exponent is not itself evidence for a superior material interface.

3.1 Lower-sideband heating: an exact conventional counterexample

An additional hot mode at omega_l=omega_c-Omega can be upconverted into the
cold mode. For equal hot-end linewidths, cooling in the three-mode model
requires

  |g_plus|^2 [n_c-n_h] > |g_minus|^2 [n_l-n_c].

At 20 THz/300 K, upper 24 THz/330 K, lower 16 THz/330 K, the bound is
|g_minus/g_plus|<0.4106408212. A ratio of 0.5 reverses cooling while the
system remains stable, consumes positive drive work, and closes its energy
balance. With the pump-loss and cross-bath margin already nearly exhausted,
the allowed ratio can fall below 0.1. Thus observing an upper converted
sideband is insufficient evidence for cooling.

Proof, precise input fixtures, signed fluxes and numerical counterexamples:
measurement_mechanism/thermal_validity/THERMAL_VALIDITY.txt and
sideband_counterexample.py.

3.2 Dark-mode cancellation does not remove integrated thermal noise

For a symmetric a-m-b chain, terminal linewidth kappa, intermediate linewidth
gamma, and equal coupling g, set C=8g^2/(kappa gamma). At the central analysis
frequency the unwanted intermediate-noise coefficient is 2C/(1+C)^2 and
vanishes at large C. Its integrated flux into one terminal is instead

  Phi_m_to_a = kappa gamma/[2(kappa+gamma)] * C/(1+C) * n_m.

At fixed gamma this increases and saturates. Making the intermediate truly
lossless eliminates its own dissipative noise, but not thermal transmission
from the hot terminal. This is consistent with narrowband dark-state
transfer literature [S3]; it is a failed broadband-noise rescue, not a
universal prohibition on filtered or pulsed converters.

3.3 A withdrawn falsification is retained

An initial counter-rotating calculation appeared to reverse refrigeration
when heat was counted only as bare oscillator occupation. Including the
interaction-energy contribution to Tr[H(t) D_r rho] removed that reversal.
That claim is withdrawn. The code and diagnosis remain in
measurement_mechanism/thermal_validity/floquet_covariance.py and its note.

4. HOT-SIDE MATERIAL LOSS: SHARP OPTIMISTIC GATE

Take low-mode cold contact A, its absorption in the hot converter x, and
high-mode hot contact B. Define

  Delta_a = n(omega_c,Th)-n(omega_c,Tc) > 0.

The same converter overlap that generates g also produces x. For an
appropriate positive hot-bath absorption operator Gamma_h,

  |g|^2 <= x Xi,
  Xi = <V u_h, Gamma_h^+ V u_h>.

This Cauchy-Schwarz bound requires the stated range/domain condition. A
lossless nullspace with finite coupling can escape this finite bound; it
must not be silently excluded or declared impossible. Saturating the bound
is an optimistic design assumption.

At fixed A,B, positive optical cooling requires

  Xi > (A+B) Delta_a/(4 Delta0),
  0 < x < B[4 Xi Delta0-(A+B)Delta_a]/[Delta_a(4 Xi+B)].

Even unlimited coupling requires x/B<Delta0/Delta_a. Increasing B without
limit is not a free heat sink: for fixed g the useful transfer is optimized
at B=2g, with other quantities held fixed.

4.1 Global contact-and-drive theorem

Let g^2=x xi N, a=A/B, X=x/B, z=4xi N/B, d=Delta_a/Delta0, and
S=hbar omega_c B Delta0. Then

  F=Q_c/S = aX[z(1-dX)-d(a+X+1)] /
             [(a+X+1)(a+X+zX)].

If a fraction f of total pump absorption reaches the cold body,

  Q_net/S = F-Hz,
  H = f (Omega/omega_c) kappa_p/(4xi Delta0).

With independently tunable positive a,X,z and fixed material coefficients,
positive cooling exists exactly when H<Hcrit(d), where

  Hcrit(d) = max_(0<r<1) r(1-r)
              [(1+dr)^(1/3)-(dr)^(1/3)]^3.

The maximum is attained at a unique interior point for finite d>0. Its
parameter s is the unique root

  d(1-s^2)=(d+1)s^3(2+s),
  r=s^3/[d(1-s^3)],
  a=(1-r)(1/s-1), X=r(1/s-1), z=d(1+s+s^2)/s^2.

Hcrit tends to 1/4 as d tends to zero and to
(5 sqrt(5)-11)/(8d) at large d. At equality net cooling is zero, not a useful
operating point. For the 20/24 THz, 300/330 K example,
d=1.37162117284 and Hcrit=0.01359807510.

The proof successively eliminates drive, total low-mode contact, and the
remaining bath fraction; it controls endpoints instead of relying on a
finite numerical search box. See matter_mechanism/ALL_CONTACT_AUDIT.txt.
An independent alternative two-variable optimization agrees to approximately
1e-15 relative error at seven d values from 0.001 to 1000. An extreme-small-d
root-bracketing error was diagnosed and corrected with a scaled variable.

This theorem is sufficient only within its independently tunable saturated
model. Real geometry correlates g, linewidths, loss, modal volume and noise;
for an actual converter it is an optimistic necessary screen.

4.2 Complete energy normalization and the mixed-source GaAs test

Use angular frequencies, peak complex fields, intensity attenuation alpha,
and the projected coherent susceptibility chi=2d_eff. With D_j=n_j n_gj,

  U_j = epsilon0 D_j V |E_j|^2/2 = hbar omega_j N_j,
  g0^2 = chi^2 hbar omega_c omega_h Omega /
          (2 epsilon0 V D_c D_h D_p),
  kappa_abs,j = omega_j epsilon_j''/D_j = c alpha_j/n_gj.

For a uniform favorable overlap,

  g^2/(x N_p) = chi^2 hbar omega_h Omega /
                 (2 epsilon0 V D_h D_p epsilon_c''),
  4g^2/(x B_material) <= chi^2 |E_p|^2 /
                         (epsilon_c'' epsilon_h'').

Reducing signal overlap reduces conversion and absorption together. The
high-mode material loss also imposes B>=B_material; independently choosing
all rates small while keeping the coupling fixed is not legitimate.

For M identical independent pairs sharing a pump volume V_p, put v=V_p/M.
The absorption-only normalization is

  H0 = epsilon0 v D_h epsilon_c'' epsilon_p'' Omega /
        (2 chi^2 hbar omega_c omega_h Delta0),
  Q_c/P_pump,abs <= Hcrit(d)/H0.

Positive conversion work and additional source losses lower the wall COP
further. Setting the cold deposition fraction f to zero does not remove
this denominator. Increasing volume and mode count together does not
automatically improve v.

Declared reference inputs, not a complete measured sample:

5. CONSTRUCTIVE STATIC CONTROL AND ITS LIMITS

5.0 Why an extra control channel must be physically independent

For pump controls u, let desired and unwanted couplings be
g_plus=v_plus^dagger u and g_minus=v_minus^dagger u, with absorbed power
P=u^dagger R u and R positive definite. The minimum absorption needed for
a specified desired coupling while exactly nulling the unwanted one is

  P_min,null/P_min,unconstrained = 1/(1-rho^2),
  rho=|v_minus^dagger R^-1 v_plus| /
       sqrt[(v_plus^dagger R^-1 v_plus)(v_minus^dagger R^-1 v_minus)].

Thus rho=1 is a controllability obstruction: the proposed null also removes
the useful coupling. A second knob is useful only if it changes this
absorption-weighted geometry. In the equal-linewidth three-mode model the
fixed-power optimal direction is a generalized eigenvector of two rank-two
matrices. The useful control subspace is spanned by R^-1 v_plus and
R^-1 v_minus. Its numerical implementation and covariance checks are in
thermal_full_cycle/selective_pump.py. A direction-independent cold deposition
fraction is assumed there; a general cold-loss matrix changes the objective.
This conditional calculation motivated the explicit static patterns below,
rather than treating independently tunable sideband couplings as hardware.

5.1 Binary orientation pattern

Take scalar signal modes E_j=sin(j theta), theta=pi x/L, omega_j=j omega_FSR,
and an independently supplied pump E_p=cos(r theta), Omega=r omega_FSR.
For desired cold mode m>r, write M=m+r and choose

  chi(theta)/chi0 = sign[cos(2M theta)].

The exact overlap for j<->j+r is

  J_j = -1/2 sum_(q positive odd) (-1)^((q-1)/2)/q
          [delta_(j,qM-r)+delta_(j,qM)].

Thus J_m=-1/2, the dangerous J_(m-r)=0, and J_(m+r)=-1/2.
All resonant pair-creation overlaps j+k=r also vanish. The full graph is
the disjoint triplets (qM-r,qM,qM+r). For m=10,r=2 these are
(10,12,14), (34,36,38), (58,60,62), and so on.

Frequency normalization matters: g_j is proportional to sqrt(j(j+r))J_j.
The extra 12<->14 coupling is 1.1832 times the target coupling. At high q,
frequency factors cancel the 1/q overlap decay, so those triplets' work
must be counted even though their thermal occupations become small.

Uniform chi gives target overlap pi/4. At unchanged linear profiles and pump
normalization, the pattern needs pi^2/4=2.4674 times the stored pump energy
to restore the same target g. Absorption has this ratio only if pump damping
is also unchanged. It is not a universal multiplier of total wall power.

The illustrative scalar geometry for n=3.28, FSR=2 THz has L=22.85 micrometres,
grating period 1.904 micrometres and regular half-domains 0.952 micrometres.
This is not a proposed validated GaAs cavity: the synthetic 20/24 THz tuple
is separate from the 10.6/9.67 micrometre material scenario above. A real
tensor, dispersion, mirror, pump field and domain-loss calculation is absent.
The cosine pump is not an eigenmode of the same Dirichlet problem as the
sine signal modes. Different pump boundary phases, polarization or external
illumination must supply it. Its energy and losses must then be normalized.

5.2 Exact single-edge grading and a sharp restricted optimum

When m=nr for a positive integer n, put k=2n+1 and choose

  p0(theta)=chi/chi0=cos((2m+r)theta)/[k cos(r theta)]
           ={(-1)^n+2 sum_(ell=1)^n (-1)^(n-ell)cos(2ell r theta)}/k.

The finite-polynomial form removes the apparent singularities. At pump
nodes p0=(-1)^n, and |p0|<=1 everywhere. Since p0 cos(r theta) is a single
cosine harmonic,

  J_j = -pi/[4(2n+1)] delta_(j,m).

Every other resonant number-conserving and pair-creation channel vanishes.
For m/r=5 the stored-pump penalty at matched target g is (2n+1)^2=121.

This coefficient is optimal under the stated infinite-ladder, fixed-pump,
uniform pointwise |chi|<=chi0 constraint. Proof: zero unwanted overlaps fixes
all relevant high cosine moments. Riemann-Lebesgue removes a possible common
moment. Any remaining low polynomial must vanish at all r pump nodes to
allow bounded division by cos(r theta), so it vanishes identically. The
complete bounded nullspace is reflection-odd susceptibility. Reflection
averaging cannot reduce the norm of the fixed even part, which enforces
|J_m|<=pi/[4(2n+1)]. This also covers bounded asymmetric or complex controls.
See matter_mechanism/GRADED_PATTERN_AUDIT.txt for the complete proof.

The theorem does not apply unchanged to a finite band, different pump shapes,
dispersion, allowed leakage, or a loss-weighted rather than pointwise budget.
It gives a benchmark and a control obstruction, not a universal refrigerator
limit. Whether a low-loss signed graded susceptibility can be fabricated
remains UNKNOWN.

covariance, including resonant j+k=r pair creation where imperfections or

FSR=2 THz; all linewidths are equal. These are explicitly synthetic contacts.
Let amplitude=2g_uniform,target/kappa, with stored pump proportional to its
square. Q0=hbar omega_c kappa Delta0/2. Cold pump absorption is stipulated
as 0.05 amplitude^2 Q0, with cold deposition fraction 0.1 and additional
passive leak 0.02 Q0. These coefficients are not derived from GaAs.

At amplitude=2:
  Model                         Net Q_c/Q0     Conversion work/Q0
  Ideal two-mode reference        +0.5800           0.1600
  Uniform crystal, full ladder    -3.7474          45.5860
  Binary pattern, full ladder     +0.7622           0.4455
  Exact single-edge grading       -0.1880           0.0064

Every row additionally spends 2 Q0 in stipulated pump absorption. The binary
pattern's upper cascade increases cold extraction but spends more conversion
work. Its work converges from 0.4455241187 Q0 at 40 modes to 0.4455246943 Q0
at 88 modes; the cold heat is unchanged. Pair creation adds substantial work
to the uniform control and vanishes in the exact pattern.

Fabrication sensitivity was tested with synthetic independent boundary errors,
not measured tolerances. At 5% RMS of a regular half-domain, the 95th-percentile
lower/target coupling ratio was about 0.0762; mean net heat at amplitude=2 was
0.742 Q0 versus 0.762 Q0 ideally. These tests include induced pair creation
but exclude domain-wall loss, altered vector modes and temperature drift.
They establish limited model robustness only. A uniform grating translation
preserves the lower-link null in this symmetric ideal interval.

Finite-band controls were also constructed by linear programming on 192
equal spatial cells with real |chi/chi0|<=1, allowing graded cells. Exact
interval integrals enforce forbidden-edge zeros; discrete primal/dual gaps
and residuals are retained. All modes through 88 are then charged. The LP
maximizes target overlap, not global refrigerator performance.

Suppressing every link below the cold mode, while allowing upper conversion,
gives J_target=0.55025 and matched-target stored-pump penalty 2.0373. Under
the declared absorption assumptions, its maximum model COP is approximately

0.2344 Q0. Enforcing isolation through lower-index j=12 reduces the optimized
COP to 0.1488; through j=16 it is 0.0104. Broader exact suppression removes
positive cooling in the stated drive search. The fictitious ideal pair's
maximum COP is 1.0255, showing the implementation penalty rather than a

Maximum heat and maximum COP are distinct operating points. At its maximum

The binary design gives 0.9260 Q0 at COP 0.1774. These bounded scalar searches
were sampled and refined; global optimality over all device designs is not
claimed. The 192-cell design requires features and graded values that have
not been demonstrated with the needed losses.

An additional cutoff risk was then tested: this finite-band profile has
J_j tending to 0.1424965 at high j, so its upper coupling does not simply
vanish. An independent passive-covariance solution extended the selected

the work changes by 5.18e-10 Q0 and COP changes by -5.46e-10 between 88 and
352 modes. At the maximum-heat point, the changes are 1.76e-6 Q0 and
-4.97e-8 respectively. The reported rounded comparisons survive this check.

A weighted-population inequality also supplies a sufficient analytic
condition against divergent mean energy in this ideal infinite ladder.
For E2=sum_j j^2 N_j, positivity of the covariance gives
dE2/dt <= -(1-beta2)E2 + sum_j j^2 n_j when damping is normalized to one.
Exact overlap integrals in a finite prefix and a bounded-variation estimate
of the entire remaining tail give beta2 about 0.19734 and 0.79896 for these
two operating points, respectively, both below one with substantial margin.
The finite prefix is evaluated numerically; this is not an interval-arithmetic
certificate or a sharp truncation-error bound. The derivation, source code,
raw cutoff changes and roundoff diagnostics are preserved in
thermal_full_cycle/UPPER_TAIL_AUDIT.txt and upper_tail_audit.py. It certifies
no physical transparency band or high-frequency rotating-wave validity.

cascade, Q_c=2.9470e-15 W, P_abs=1.8899e-6 W and conversion work
1.2727e-15 W at the reported point; including work changes its COP to
1.55934637699e-9. Additional all-hot high-frequency triplets add work and
cannot improve that denominator if disconnected from the cold triplet.
Energy closure residuals are below 1e-28 W. These tiny absolute values are
model outputs for one reference volume, not predictions for a fabricated
specimen. A small numerical residual does not validate material assumptions.

The upper cascade is a meaningful control improvement, not an escape from
the tested reference loss budget. Its numerical optimum is not an analytic
global bound over all contact choices, mode graphs or material designs.
The exact model, search limits and parameter values are preserved in
thermal_full_cycle/triad_material_screen.py and its JSON output.

6. WHAT EXISTS, WHAT MUST BE OBTAINED, AND THE TEST

Known interfaces do not yet close this mechanism. Orientation-patterned GaAs
waveguides have been fabricated, including reported propagation loss of
3 dB/cm at 8 micrometres in a much larger-period structure [S9]. That does
not certify the submicrometre domains, three-frequency response or pump
loss required here. Published spatially selective upconversion [S4] is prior
art for control shaping, not evidence for the present thermal interface.

GaAs THz susceptibility is dispersive; different published parameterizations
can reproduce a similar SHG minimum while predicting different absolute
mixing response [S7,S8]. The saved model extrapolations are not measured
confidence intervals. Imaginary susceptibility cannot be assigned free
reversible coherent coupling.

A recent electrically driven graphene metasurface demonstrates 1 GHz
frequency shifts around 10 micrometres, with reported mode-conversion
efficiency about 4e-5 [S10]. It provides a real control precedent at that
frequency. Its one-step ideal temperature lift at 300 K and 30 THz is less
than 0.01 K by the occupation-ratio condition; it does not supply the
multi-terahertz interface for the 15 K material scenario.

The required empirical inputs must coexist on one sample and be normalized
to the same fields:
  - phase-sensitive complex chi(omega_h;omega_c,Omega), tensor orientation
    and temperature dependence;
  - independent intensity attenuation and dispersive energy at all three
    frequencies, including added domain and electrode loss;
  - pump-on noise spectra and the complete conversion matrix, including
    lower sidebands and possible pair-creation channels;
  - actual cold/hot bath participation, usable mode count and pump volume;
  - absorbed pump power, source/port losses, deposition fraction and thermal
    support flow.

A coherent probe can first measure conversion and forbidden-channel
suppression without relying on extremely weak thermal emission. Independent
linear loss and pump absorption must accompany it; fitting conversion and
loss from one output-power curve leaves an identifiability problem. Measured
parameters can then be inserted directly into the saved covariance and
full-cycle calculations. A failed necessary gate would avoid premature
refrigerator fabrication.

If that interface passes, the decisive cooling observation is fixed-
temperature electrical substitution. At fixed true Tc, fixed hot reservoir,
and invariant other heat paths,

  Delta P_heater = Q_conversion_increment-P_abs,cold.

If the unpumped device leaks L_hot into the cold body, absolute cooling is
Q_device,on=Delta P_heater-L_hot, so a positive heater increment alone is not
enough. Independent thermometry, matched absorbed-power/detuned controls,
hot-temperature scans, and a calibrated conversion/noise matrix distinguish
actual extraction from ordinary photothermal or conductance changes.

The photon/work check is Q_c,conversion=hbar omega_c(J_plus+J_minus) and
W_conversion=hbar Omega(J_plus-J_minus), with currents defined outward from
the cold mode. The complete hot rejection must equal net cold removal plus
supplied work after all measured ports are included.

The 2025 GaAs thermal-upconversion paper [S11] is an empirical lead, not
independent proof of the present refrigerator. Its cited raw-data repository
was not retrievable in this session. No raw observations were acquired or reanalyzed.

7. CREDIBLE ALTERNATIVES AND RETAINED OBSTRUCTIONS

Kinetic ion discrimination: a two-control occupancy/routing pump gives a
rate-dependent flux and can exactly null one species at zero load. The
finite-load model shows that this null is cancellation of substantial
bidirectional exchange. For a 10% rate difference in the stated weak-drive
example, a 1% rate error leaks about 10% of the desired flux; a 0.001 k_B T
load can leak more than the desired current. More forceful drive increases
enrichment only with substantial work, before gate and counter-ion costs.
Related ratchets are established [S12,S13] and overlap the pinned N774 route.
The retained opportunity is a dynamically selective gate or counter-ion
interface, not a demonstrated inexpensive close-species separator. Exact
rate equations, noise, load sweeps and energy are in measurement_mechanism/.

Metastable hydride synthesis: Mg2IrH6 is a predicted ambient-pressure high-Tc
phase [S14], while the reported Mg2IrH7 decompression route returns to H5
under the measured conditions [S15]. Using the source phase energies, a
scalar hydrogen chemical potential cannot select the H6 phase below the
H5/H7 tie line. A simple uniform strain estimate is also far too weak.
For irreversible H5->H6->H7 kinetics, the maximum H6 fraction is
r^[-r/(r-1)], r=k67/k56. Reaching 99% needs k56/k67>644.6 in that model.
A common time-dependent multiplier of both rates only reparametrizes time;
it cannot improve the yield. Selectively changing the two barriers is the
missing physical control. Mean hydrogen content of six is not proof of
local H6 coordination. No DFT, synthesis or superconductivity measurement
was executed by this branch. See matter_mechanism/REPORT.txt and code.

These alternatives were investigated independently and retained with their
failure scopes. They were not discarded merely because implementation was
uncertain. The photonic mechanism was taken furthest because its controllable
linear model supports exact falsification, an explicit control construction,
a material-energy screen and a discriminating measurement in one chain.
