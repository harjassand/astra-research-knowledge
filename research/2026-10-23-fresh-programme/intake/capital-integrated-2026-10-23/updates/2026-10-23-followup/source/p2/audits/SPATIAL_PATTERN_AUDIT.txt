VERDICT
The proposed sign-cos orientation pattern exactly removes the lower resonant
conversion link while retaining the target in the specified ideal cavity.
It necessarily adds another equally large RAW spatial overlap above the
target, and infinitely many higher triplets. Quantum energy normalization
makes the higher triplets' couplings much less suppressed than their raw
overlaps suggest. A two-mode performance model is therefore insufficient.

ASSUMPTIONS AND CONVENTIONS
0<theta<pi; E_j=sin(j theta); j is a positive integer; omega_j=j omega_FSR.
The pump has spatial amplitude cos(r theta), frequency Omega=r omega_FSR,
and r is a positive integer. Assume m>r so the proposed lower mode exists.
Set M=m+r and chi(theta)/chi0=p(theta)=sign[cos(2M theta)].
J denotes an unnormalized, dimensionless spatial integral; chi0 and all
electromagnetic normalization factors are kept outside it.

1. EXACT FOURIER AND SUM/DIFFERENCE IDENTITIES
For a resonant number-conserving link j <-> j+r,

  sin(j theta) sin((j+r)theta) cos(r theta)
    = [1+cos(2r theta)-cos(2j theta)-cos(2(j+r)theta)]/4. (1)

Uniform chi therefore gives J_j=pi/4 for all positive j,r. The lower link
is j=m-r; it shares this value if m>r. If m=r its nominal lower mode is
j=0 and does not exist; r=0 is also outside the conversion problem.

Let S_n=integral_0^pi p(theta)cos(n theta)dtheta. Then S_0=0 and

  S_n = 2(-1)^((q-1)/2)/q,  if |n|=2M q, q=1,3,5,...;
        0 otherwise.                                     (2)

This follows from sign(cos u)=(4/pi)sum_{q odd>0}
(-1)^((q-1)/2)cos(q u)/q. Values at the discontinuities do not affect
the integrals. Since r<M, S_(2r)=0. Equations (1)-(2) give

  J_j = -(1/2)sum_{q odd>0} [(-1)^((q-1)/2)/q]
          [delta_(j,qM-r)+delta_(j,qM)].                  (3)

There are no coincidences between these two indicator families because
0<r<M. Thus J_m=-1/2, J_(m-r)=0, and J_(m+r)=-1/2.
The magnitude of the target overlap is exactly 2/pi of uniform chi.

The complete resonant graph consists of disjoint triplets

  (qM-r) <-> qM <-> (qM+r),  q=1,3,5,...,                (4)

with both raw overlaps equal to -(-1)^((q-1)/2)/(2q).
For m=10,r=2: (10,12,14), (34,36,38), (58,60,62), ... .
The link starting indices are 10,12,34,36,58,60,... exactly as proposed.
Independent piecewise numerical quadrature over the sign domains confirmed
links j=8,10,12,14,34,36,58,60 to absolute error below 2e-15 against (3).

For sum-frequency pair creation j+k=r, j,k>=1,

  sin(j theta)sin(k theta)cos(r theta)
    = [cos(2j theta)+cos(2k theta)-1-cos(2r theta)]/4.

All nonconstant frequencies here are below 2M, so their integrals against
p vanish, as does the constant term. Every such ideal resonant pair-creation
matrix element is zero, including j=k=r/2 when r is even. The corresponding
uniform-chi overlap would be -pi/4. This conclusion is restricted to exact
resonance and the scalar profiles; counter-rotating, detuned, or other
polarization channels still require a justified rotating-wave approximation.
The j=k=1,r=2 pair-creation quadrature was -9.95e-16, consistent with zero.
These checks evaluate integrals; they are not experimental measurements.

2. PRECISE CONTROL-RESOLUTION OBSTRUCTION
The mode products before multiplying by the pump are

  E_m E_(m+r)=[cos(r theta)-cos((2m+r)theta)]/2,
  E_m E_(m-r)=[cos(r theta)-cos((2m-r)theta)]/2.

For pump controls in the COSINE subspace span{cos(ell theta):
0<=ell<2m-r}, orthogonality makes these two products indistinguishable:
both overlaps are (1/2)integral f(theta)cos(r theta)dtheta.

The assertion is false for unrestricted low sine as well as cosine controls
on [0,pi]. These two bases are not mutually orthogonal there. For m=10,r=2
and f(theta)=sin(theta), the target and lower overlaps are respectively

  J_plus=-160/483,
  J_minus=-320/969,
  J_plus-J_minus=-160/156009=-0.001025581857... .

This small distinction permits exact algebraic cancellation by combining
controls, although the required amplitudes may be inefficient. Therefore
state the no-distinction result for the specified cosine control subspace,
or for the actual physically accessible subspace after its Gram matrix is
computed. Do not claim a universal spatial-bandwidth impossibility.

3. ENERGY NORMALIZATION AND THE PUMP COST
In an ideal nondispersive cavity with equal modal dielectric normalization,
electric field per photon scales as sqrt(omega_j). At fixed pump amplitude,

  g_j is proportional to sqrt(j(j+r)) J_j,               (5)

not just J_j. Tensor contractions, dispersive energy normalization, and
frequency-dependent chi add further factors in a real material.

Relative to the desired m <-> M edge, the ideal magnitude ratios are

  |g_(qM-r)|/|g_m| = sqrt[(qM-r)(qM)/(mM)]/q,
  |g_(qM)|/|g_m|   = sqrt[(qM)(qM+r)/(mM)]/q.            (6)

For m=10,r=2, the 12<->14 coupling is sqrt(14/10)=1.1832159566
times the target. For q=3 the lower and upper ratios are 1.0645812948 and
1.1254628677. Both ratios tend to sqrt(M/m)=1.0954451150 as q grows.
The 1/q Fourier decay is cancelled asymptotically by the frequency factors.
Thermal occupations decrease at high frequency, and actual dispersion or
material response can detune/suppress these modes. These effects must be
supplied rather than inferred from the Fourier coefficient alone.

The target g is reduced by 2/pi at fixed stored pump energy only if the
linear mode profiles, mode energies, pump profile, and |chi0| are unchanged.
Under exactly those conditions, achieving the same target g requires pump
amplitude pi/2 times larger and stored pump energy N larger by pi^2/4.
If pump damping is also unchanged, its absorption power has that same ratio.
This is not an unconditional ratio of total wall power: additional triplets
change conversion work and pump depletion, and domain fabrication can change
losses. All-hot off-target triplets can consume pump work while contributing
no useful cold extraction. Their work belongs in the full-cycle denominator.

4. PHYSICAL INTERFACE AND ERROR CONDITIONS
The cos(r theta) pump profile is not an eigenfunction of the same Dirichlet
boundary-value problem that gives electric modes sin(j theta). It can be an
independently imposed field, or use different boundary phases, polarization,
or illumination. Its actual realization and stored-energy normalization
must be established. This is a missing-interface condition, not a proof
that the proposed pump is impossible.

The ideal inversion grating has physical period L/M and interior domain
width L/(2M), with half-width end domains. A sign-changing nonlinear tensor
that leaves all linear mode profiles and losses invariant is an assumption.
Achievable micron-scale inversion, vector tensor compatibility, domain-wall

Uniform grating translation p(theta)=sign[cos(2M theta+phi)] preserves the
lower-link null in this symmetric scalar interval; allowed q-harmonic J
values acquire cos(q phi). Independent boundary displacements do leak it.
For baseline interfaces theta_b=(2b+1)pi/(4M), b=0,...,2M-1,

  delta J_j = 2 sum_b (-1)^b sin(j theta_b)
                 sin((j+r)theta_b)cos(r theta_b) delta theta_b

This distinguishes true boundary jitter from a common translation. Real
mirror asymmetry, dispersive/vector profiles, nonuniform |chi|, and added
linear scattering can remove the ideal null even without boundary jitter.
