S1 — BASIC PHOTONIC REFRIGERATION, THEORY / PRIOR ART
Siddharth Buddhiraju, Wei Li and Shanhui Fan, Photonic refrigeration from
time-modulated thermal emission, Physical Review Letters 124, 077402 (2020).
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.124.077402
https://arxiv.org/html/1905.12196v1
Used to establish prior art and inspect frequency conversion, pump work and
the assumed modulation/absorber interface. Not an experimental validation.

S2 — NEAR-FIELD THERMAL CONVERSION, THEORY / PRIOR ART
Renwen Yu and Shanhui Fan, PNAS (2024), DOI 10.1073/pnas.2401514121.
https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2401514121
https://arxiv.org/html/2310.08692v1
Used to inspect a time-modulated near-field heat-transfer construction and
its ideal lossless modulation layer. Its electromagnetic modulation work
does not itself supply a complete pump-source absorption ledger.

S3 — DARK-MODE STATE TRANSFER, THEORY / PRIOR ART
Ying-Dan Wang and Aashish A. Clerk, Using interference for high fidelity
quantum state transfer in optomechanics.
https://arxiv.org/html/1205.5284v2
Used to distinguish narrowband signal/noise cancellation from total
band-integrated thermal flux. Our three-mode calculation is independently
specified; it does not dispute that paper's state-transfer regime.

S4 — SPATIAL MODE SELECTIVITY, EXISTING EXPERIMENT
Santosh Kumar and coauthors, Mode-selective image upconversion.
https://arxiv.org/html/1810.07552
Spatial pump shaping and mode-selective upconversion are prior art. The
record does not provide the present multi-terahertz thermal interface.

S5 — GaAs DIFFERENCE-FREQUENCY GENERATION, EXISTING EXPERIMENT
S. Ya. Tochitsky and coauthors, high-power terahertz generation in GaAs,
Journal of the Optical Society of America B 24, 2509 (2007).
https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAB.24.002509
https://www.seas.ucla.edu/plasma/journals_files/files/journals/2007_Tochitsky_JOSAB.pdf
Room-temperature pulsed DFG anchors a reverse-process interface. Values
used here have distinct status: d_eff about 50 pm/V is a model input;
absorption about 2 cm^-1 at 110 micrometres is fitted. Neither is a same-
sample, independent complete response/loss measurement for a refrigerator.

S6 — GaAs COLD-BAND LOSS, EXISTING CALORIMETRY
J. B. Comly, E. Garmire and A. Yariv, infrared absorption in GaAs (1967).
https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1709080
https://heritageproject.caltech.edu/documents/21366/C2_IR_Absorpt_GaAs_-_comly_garmire.pdf
The reported 10.6-micrometre absorption is 0.006 +/- 0.002 cm^-1.
Incident versus internal intensity/Fresnel normalization remains unresolved
for our use. This datum comes from another crystal and temperature context.

S7 — GaAs THz NONLINEAR DISPERSION, EXISTING SHG EXPERIMENT
T. Dekorsy and coauthors, Physical Review Letters 90, 055508 (2003).
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.90.055508
https://d-nb.info/1100690867/34
Used to investigate ionic/electronic interference in THz susceptibility.
No direct measurement of chi(24;20,4) THz was obtained from this record.

S8 — ALTERNATIVE SUSCEPTIBILITY PARAMETERIZATION, FIRST-PRINCIPLES THEORY
Eric Roman, Jonathan R. Yates, Marek Veithen, David Vanderbilt and Ivo Souza,
Physical Review B 74, 245204 (2006).
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.74.245204
https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0608640
Figure 1 and its parameter definitions support the distinct model scenarios
in material_interface.py. Similar SHG minima do not uniquely identify the
absolute target mixing coefficient. Our extrapolated values are not a
measured interval or a confidence bound.

S9 — ORIENTATION-PATTERNED GaAs, EXISTING FABRICATION / EXPERIMENT
Geoffroy Granger and coauthors, GaAs-chip-based mid-infrared supercontinuum
generation, Light: Science & Applications 12, 252 (2023).
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41377-023-01299-9
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10582246/
Reported waveguide loss and orientation patterning provide a fabrication
precedent. The demonstrated 80–85 micrometre periods do not certify the
roughly 1.9-micrometre scalar design, its tensor response or pump loss.

Additional inspected fabrication record: K. A. Fedorova and coauthors,
Optics Express 21, 16424 (2013), DOI 10.1364/OE.21.016424.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23938493/
Abstract reports 2.1 dB/cm propagation loss. Full-text retrieval was
incomplete in this run; it was not used to establish a minimum domain size.

S10 — MID-IR ELECTRICAL MODULATION, EXISTING EXPERIMENT
Anatoly Efimov and coauthors, Nonreciprocal scattering and implications for
thermal emission control on a mid-infrared spatiotemporally modulated
metasurface, Nature Communications 17, 5774 (2026).
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-72142-z
Primary preprint read: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.00463
The preprint describes 1 GHz conversion around 10 micrometres and about
4e-5 mode-conversion efficiency. This is a coherent optical scattering
measurement; it is not a net-cooling or multi-terahertz-drive measurement.

S11 — GaAs THERMAL UPCONVERSION, EXISTING EXPERIMENT / RAW-DATA LEAD
Ma and coauthors, Breaking Kirchhoff's Law in Nonlinear Thermal Emission.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08544v1
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.08544
Inspected spectra and normalized thermal relaxation motivate our proposed
calorimetric discriminator. No curve was digitized or fitted here.
Cited data link: https://github.com/LALALA-coder007/DATA_SET
Access result in this session: GitHub connector NOT_FOUND/404; web fetch
unavailable. This is not evidence that the dataset does not exist.

S12 — TWO-CONTROL ION RATCHET, EXISTING EXPERIMENT
Grossman and coauthors, bipotentiostatic nanopore pumping (2025 preprint).
https://arxiv.org/html/2511.20174v1
Current pumping is a control precedent; it does not measure the close-
species, finite-enrichment, full-power operation proposed by our toy model.

S13 — ION DIFFUSION RATCHET / AMBIPOLARITY, THEORY
Herman and coauthors, PRX Energy (2023).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.01429
Related ambipolar Poisson-Nernst-Planck treatment:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09096
Used to check prior art and the omitted counter-ion/charge-neutrality
interface. Our load and stochastic-turnover calculations are separate.

S14 — METASTABLE HYDRIDE TARGET, THEORY / REPORTED DFT INPUTS
Kapildeb Dolui and coauthors, Feasible route to high-temperature ambient-
pressure hydride superconductivity, Physical Review Letters 132, 166001 (2024).
https://arxiv.org/html/2310.07562v2
The target is predicted, not synthesized by this branch. The source's phase
energies and volumes support the saved tie-line and elastic arithmetic.
No new DFT calculation was run.

S15 — Mg2IrH7 PRESSURE PATH, EXISTING EXPERIMENT
Shubham Sinha and coauthors, High-pressure stabilization of Mg2IrH7:
Structural proximity to high-Tc superconductivity.
https://arxiv.org/html/2602.23675v1
The examined record reports H7 stabilization and reversion to H5 on
decompression. It does not establish the desired H6 synthesis route.
