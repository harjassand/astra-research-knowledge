OUTCOME
No new material or superconductivity was discovered. A concrete candidate process
is pulse-limited, low-temperature topotactic hydrogen addition to Mg2IrH5,
followed by kinetic arrest before Mg2IrH7 or other competing phases grow. Its
potential useful output is metastable cubic Mg2IrH6. This is a proposed process,
not an executed experiment. Generic insertion/implantation and quenching routes
are already in the primary literature; novelty of a specific implementation is
UNKNOWN. The quantitatively useful result is a selection criterion and two
failed simplifications, supported by source-derived energy arithmetic.

WHY THIS TARGET
The target has published predictions of high-Tc conventional superconductivity
at ambient pressure. Mg2IrH5 is an experimentally obtained nearby precursor.
The 2026 experiment on Mg2IrH7 observed reversion to H5 on decompression, so the
desired intermediate is a specific synthesis gap rather than an entirely
unspecified material. No independent superconducting measurement of Mg2IrH6
was obtained in this investigation. See sources S1-S3.

RESULT 1: SCALAR HYDROGEN CHEMICAL POTENTIAL CANNOT SELECT THE TARGET IN THIS SET
For a neutral bulk phase, grand potential per metal formula is Omega_n=G_n-n*mu_H.
For H6 to be lower than BOTH H5 and H7 requires
  G6-G5 <= mu_H <= G7-G6.
Thus a necessary and sufficient interval for this three-phase comparison is
  G7-2G6+G5 >= 0.
Using the FCC H7 data gives width -0.2858 eV, so no interval exists. Equivalently,
H6 is 0.1429 eV/formula above 0.5 H5 + 0.5 H7. Allowing R-3m H7 makes the deficit
0.54595 eV/formula. The derivation is elementary convex-hull thermodynamics,
not a novel theorem. A voltage that only changes mu_H cannot remove this
nonconvexity. Changing pressure, strain, charge state, interfaces, composition,
temperature or a kinetic path changes the physical problem and is not excluded.
The fact that proton plus electron addition can provide a large chemical
potential does not make voltage equivalent to hydrostatic pressure.

RESULT 2: A SIMPLE COHERENT VOLUME CLAMP IS FAR TOO WEAK IN THIS MODEL
Attempt: clamp each phase at V*=V6 to favor the intermediate. Assume all three
phases have the same bulk modulus B and quadratic isotropic elastic energy
  U_i = B (V_i - V6)^2 / (2 V_i).
This ignores anisotropic epitaxial changes, interface chemistry and nonlinear
response; it is deliberately a narrow volumetric rescue model.
The midpoint competitor is raised relative to H6 by
  0.25 B [(V5-V6)^2/V5 + (V7-V6)^2/V7]
  = 6.19515e-6 B[GPa] eV/formula.
At B=100 GPa this is 0.0006195 eV; at B=1000 GPa it is 0.006195 eV.
To offset the 0.1429 eV deficit requires B=23066.4 GPa.
This rejects this specified volume-clamp construction. It is NOT a proof that
all epitaxial stress or interfaces fail. The similar H5/H6/H7(FCC) volumes,
rather than the larger R-3m H7 volume, are the decisive obstruction.

RESULT 3: EXACT KINETIC SELECTION REQUIREMENT FOR ONE PULSE
Consider a hypothetical uniform, independent-site, irreversible sequence
  H5 --k56--> H6 --k67--> H7,
starting in pure H5, with no direct H5->H7 transition or reverse/reconstructive
loss. Let r=k67/k56 and u=k56*t. Then
  p5=exp(-u),
  p6=(exp(-u)-exp(-r*u))/(r-1),
  p7=1-p5-p6.
The optimum is u*=ln(r)/(r-1), and
  p6,max=r^(-r/(r-1)); for r=1 the limit is 1/e.
For p6,max>=0.99, k56/k67 must be at least 644.63.
At equal rate prefactors, this corresponds to Ea67-Ea56 >=0.16723 eV at 300 K,
or >=0.08361 eV at 150 K. For 95% yield the ratio is 88.37; for 99.9%, 9114.01.
These thresholds are analytic properties of the toy model, NOT measured rates
or a prediction of Mg2IrH6 yield.

PULSE NON-ADVANTAGE WITH COMMON RATE MODULATION
If a waveform changes both rates as k56(t)=a(t)k56,0 and k67(t)=a(t)k67,0, the
same solution holds with exposure u=k56,0 integral a(t)dt. Merely making pulses
shorter or adding pauses does not improve the maximum intermediate yield.
The control must change the RATE RATIO or arrest/remove product selectively.
For Butler-Volmer-like barriers, differing transfer coefficients could change
the ratio, but their values here are UNKNOWN. Cooling could change the ratio
when activation barriers differ, but bulk filling may then become too slow.

MEAN STOICHIOMETRY IS NOT PHASE PURITY
Even perfect charge counting of one added H atom per starting formula can yield
p5=p7=1/2, p6=0, which has mean stoichiometry H6. Therefore coulometry plus XRD
of the nearly identical metal lattice does not establish the target. Local H
coordination/occupancy and phase-sensitive electronic measurements are needed.

PROPOSED IMPLEMENTATION AND THE MISSING INTERFACE
Start from an independently characterized, very thin Mg2IrH5 layer. Supply
hydrogen by a dose-controlled nonequilibrium source while holding low enough
temperature to suppress reconstructive conversion. Pulse until the predicted
intermediate maximum, then arrest hydrogen exchange and cool for measurement.
Repeat through thin-layer processing only if the upper layer does not disturb
the retained product. This architecture aims to avoid both thermodynamic
equilibration and long through-thickness diffusion, but fabrication feasibility,
surface termination, contamination, and the required rate separation are UNKNOWN.
The source's barrier-free relaxation after a hydrogen atom is ALREADY inserted
inside an ideal periodic precursor is not an injection barrier from an external
reservoir. Likewise the source's bulk H tracer-diffusion activation energies in
different perfect compositions are not k56 and k67. They cannot be inserted
into the kinetic model as if they were measured synthesis selectivities.

FULL COST AND MATERIAL ACCOUNTING
This is a synthesis process, not an engine. The transformed feedstock is an
output and need not return to its initial state. A complete production boundary
must charge: precursor preparation; hydrogen production/purification and all
unincorporated H; atom/ion source or electrochemical input; pumping; deposition
and substrate preparation; heating/cooling of sample AND substrate; controls;
encapsulation; discarded/failed material; and recovery/recycling. For a proton
electrode, electrical work is integral V_cell I dt, including side reactions;
corrected incorporated charge is not total electricity. For a beam source,
source/acceleration energy and particle capture efficiency must both be charged.
Retained free energy above the final competitors must be supplied somewhere.
The source static tie-line deficits are not a measured process-energy figure.
No efficiency, cost, throughput or scaling advantage is established.

DISCRIMINATING TEST (PROPOSED, NOT EXECUTED)
At fixed temperature and layer thickness, vary pulse duration/dose and measure
the populations of H5-like, intact octahedral H6-like, and interstitial H7-like
environments with calibrated local vibrational/isotopic probes. Independently
measure released H2 and incorporated H. Fit the full dose/time evolution, not
one endpoint. At least one pulse should show a resolved intermediate maximum
and a rate ratio large enough to support the desired yield. Distinguish direct
mixture formation from sequential kinetics. Repeat at two thicknesses: uniform
kinetics predicts t* independent of thickness; diffusion-limited filling predicts
a strong thickness dependence, typically proportional to thickness squared.
A second control comparison holds integrated exposure constant while changing
pulse shape; identical phase yield supports common-rate modulation. A genuine
waveform advantage must alter selectivity, not merely total dose or heating.
If a putative superconducting state is reached, zero resistance alone is
insufficient: magnetic screening, field dependence, composition/phase analysis
and independent replication remain necessary. None was measured here.

CREDIBLE ALTERNATIVES
1. Direct high-pressure synthesis and decompression: primary 2026 study obtained
   H7 and then H5, a concrete failed access path for H6 under those conditions.
2. Direct H implantation into H5: explicitly proposed in the 2024 primary study;
   may outperform electrochemistry if surface injection and chemical stability
   preclude useful electrochemical selectivity. Damage and heating are charged.
3. Carrier doping of an already stable hydrogen-rich semiconductor: broad
   alternative discussed in Xu et al.; not pursued here because charge screening,
   charge compensation, and actual phase synthesis remain separate requirements.
4. Simple voltage replacement of pressure, or scalar volume clamping: rejected
   in the precisely specified energetic model above.

WHAT CHANGED THE VIEW
Initial attraction was voltage-controlled access to high-pressure-like hydrides.
Prior art already owns that generic direction. Exact source energies show that
the target cannot be selected by a scalar hydrogen chemical potential alone.
Latest available experimental source shows that the nominal dehydrogenation
route bypassed the target. These facts redirect the problem to nonequilibrium
local-site selectivity and product arrest, where a precise missing measurement
can be stated.

EXACT NEXT INVESTIGATION
First acquire or compute surface-resolved barriers for adding H into a missing
IrH6 vertex versus adding H into the subsequent interstitial site, on an explicit
Mg2IrH5 termination, including the electrode/beam boundary. Include reverse loss,
reconstructive H5/H7 nucleation, and chemical diffusion. The test is whether any
accessible temperature/potential window yields k56/k67>645 while filling a
thin layer before loss. A bounded DFT/NEB task must use explicit structures and
boundary conditions; uniform-background charged-bulk calculations are not enough.
If no window survives, reject this pulse construction and redirect to a different
chemistry rather than optimize waveform decoration. This is a suitable compact
handoff; no live external sister-branch message was sent by this worker.
