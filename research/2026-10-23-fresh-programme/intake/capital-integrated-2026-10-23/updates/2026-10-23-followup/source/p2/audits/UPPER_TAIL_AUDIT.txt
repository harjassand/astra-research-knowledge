P2 — finite-band upper-mode work audit
Evidence class: derivation and numerical diagnostics in the synthetic model.
No new material input, physical measurement or physical RWA validation.

RISK
The optimized 192-cell lower-blocked susceptibility does not suppress every
upper conversion. Let F(theta)=p(theta) cos(r theta), and let
C_k=integral_0^pi F(theta) cos(k theta) dtheta. Then

  J_j=[C_r-C_(2j+r)]/2.

For this saved profile C_r=0.2849929867232517, so J_j tends to
0.14249649336162584. Quantum frequency normalization makes g_j grow
asymptotically with j. Therefore decay of hot bath occupation alone does
not justify ignoring high modes: cold-origin photons can be converted
upward repeatedly and consume additional work.

NUMERICAL CUTOFF AUDIT
upper_tail_audit.py uses an independent passive covariance solution. Pair
creation is first checked to vanish for the saved LP profile. With unit
population damping and real symmetric coupling matrix h=U diag(lambda) U^T,

  D_tilde=U^T diag(n_j) U,
  C_tilde_ab=D_tilde_ab/[1+i(lambda_a-lambda_b)],
  C=U C_tilde U^T.

This avoids subtracting vacuum variance in sparsely populated high modes.
It agrees with the existing 88-mode quadrature solution before extending
the cutoff to 176 and 352. No new pump or shape optimization is performed.

At the saved maximum-COP point, amplitude=0.7986020348888063:
  cutoff 88:  work/Q0=0.1537534124754, COP=0.4987836213150;
  cutoff 176: work/Q0=0.1537534129930, COP=0.4987836207687;
  cutoff 352: work/Q0=0.1537534129930, COP=0.4987836207687.

At the saved maximum-heat point, amplitude=3.2332429578113504:
  cutoff 88:  work/Q0=1.0098590248184, COP=0.1764773151875;
  cutoff 176: work/Q0=1.0098607810165, COP=0.1764772654938;
  cutoff 352: work/Q0=1.0098607826470, COP=0.1764772654477.

Cold heat changes only at numerical roundoff in these checks. The stronger
drive needs a larger work cutoff, but neither reported rounded comparison
is reversed. Tiny negative high-mode populations and tail energies of order
machine precision are retained and labeled as numerical roundoff; they are
not physical negative-energy predictions. These checks are empirical
convergence diagnostics of a calculation, not acquired experimental data or
a rigorous numeric truncation-error interval.

ANALYTIC MOMENT CONDITION
For positive covariance C, 2|C_jk|<=N_j+N_k. In the passive chain connecting
j to j+r with coupling g_j, a positive weighted population E_w=sum_j w_j N_j
obeys, for increasing weights w_j and unit population damping,

  dE_w/dt <= -E_w + sum_j w_j n_j
     + sum_j (w_(j+r)-w_j)|g_j|(N_j+N_(j+r)).

Set
  beta_w=sup_j {
    (w_(j+r)-w_j)|g_j| + (w_j-w_(j-r))|g_(j-r)| }/w_j,
where absent lower edges contribute zero. Then

  dE_w/dt <= -(1-beta_w)E_w + sum_j w_j n_j.

If beta_w<1 and the thermal forcing moment is finite, the stationary moment
is bounded by sum_j w_j n_j/(1-beta_w), uniformly in finite mode cutoff.
This supplies a sufficient condition, not a necessary instability threshold.
For w_j=j^2 it bounds the energy in the tail above N by E2/N. Thus a cutoff
family satisfying the condition cannot hide a divergent mean-energy tail.

UNIFORM CONTROL OF THE UNCOMPUTED COUPLING TAIL
The piecewise smooth F is of bounded variation. Integration by parts gives
|C_k|<=TV(F)/k for k>0; boundary terms vanish because sin(k theta)=0 at
0 and pi. A conservative computable variation bound is

  TV(F) <= sum_internal_edges |Delta p cos(r theta_edge)|
           + r sum_cells |p_cell| Delta theta_cell.

The right side is 31.67965085896857 for the saved profile. Consequently

  |J_j| <= |C_r|/2 + TV(F)/[2(2j+r)].

Let K=2 amplitude/[pi sqrt(m(m+r))], so
g_j=K sqrt(j(j+r))J_j. For sites j>=N0>r define

  Jbar=|C_r|/2 + TV(F)/[2(2N0-r)].

For w_j=j, a sufficient tail bound is
  beta1_tail <= 2r K Jbar sqrt(1+r/N0).
For w_j=j^2, a sufficient tail bound is
  beta2_tail <= (2+r/N0) beta1_tail.

The code evaluates every prefix coefficient for j<N0=1000 directly from
exact interval integral formulas, then takes the larger of that prefix
maximum and the analytic tail bound. The resulting numerical beta2 values
are 0.19734003271 at the maximum-COP point and 0.79895648042 at the
maximum-heat point. Both have substantial margin below one. Prefix
evaluation uses floating-point arithmetic; no interval-arithmetic formal
certificate is claimed. The displayed inequality itself is analytic.

PHYSICAL LIMIT
The ideal nondispersive, constant-damping ladder and constant susceptibility
cannot simply be extended through arbitrarily high physical frequencies.
The rotating-wave approximation and material transparency must be justified
over every physically contributing band. This audit resolves an internal
work-truncation concern; it does not supply that missing material interface.
