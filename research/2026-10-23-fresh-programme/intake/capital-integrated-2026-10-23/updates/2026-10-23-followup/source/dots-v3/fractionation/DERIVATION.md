# Temperature-swing candidate and its admission boundary
23 October 2026, parent-only added empirical programme. Continues v1 without changing frozen bytes.

## Primary observation, narrowly summarized
Licht and Wu, JPCC 2011, DOI 10.1021/jp2078715, report binary Li4SiO4 solubility rising from 5.5 to 11 mol/kg Li2CO3 between 750 and 950 C, with similar behavior in air and CO2. Their silica addition releases about two CO2 per SiO2. Saturation produces visible excess salt and increased viscosity; excess dissolves on heating. Fe(III) solubility is about 7 molal at 750 C. These are salt experiments, not the mixed MAC-ore liquidus or a continuous separation. Public author-uploaded full text was read; no local PDF acquired.
Source: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/231648537_STEP_Iron_a_Chemistry_of_Iron_Formation_without_CO2_Emission_Molten_Carbonate_Solubility_and_Electrochemistry_of_Iron_Ore_Impurities

The inherited real-ore composition is from Moradmand et al.2025 DOI10.1016/j.jclepro.2025.146828: Fe .6095, SiO2 .0408, Al2O3 .0246 mass fractions. The declared test loading is .18 ore/.82 initial carbonate. Actual hot species and mixed saturation are not observed by these mass fractions.

## Calculation
On 1kg initial mixture, nominal Si molality is (.18*.0408/.0600843)/.82=.1490589; Fe nominal molality is2.3957862. Thus the binary cold saturation is36.898 times nominal Si concentration. Correcting an illustrative complete carbonate lithiation of Si, Al and Fe gives .1683244 instead. This comparison invalidates assuming an abundant hot Si solid merely from a cooled salt diffraction peak. It does NOT prove zero solid in the real multicomponent bath: cross-interactions and other phases can move the liquidus.

Proposed construction, not admitted operation: accumulate gangue in a controlled hot stream; cool that stream to crystallize silicate; separate crystals; carbonate the separated solid in a distinct zone; return recovered Li carbonate and remaining Fe-bearing liquor. Ordinary fractional crystallization is the comparator and is not claimed novel. The binary endpoint lever rule gives a favorable solvent circulation nSi/(11-5.5)=.202564kg carbonate/kg original-feed Fe. This excludes dissolved solids, held-up mother liquor, startup stock and sensible/latent heat. It is not total circulation or an equipment design.

A lithium bookkeeping constraint is more important than this attractive circulation number. If the only noncarbonate Li host is Li4SiO4 and SiO2 consumes two initial carbonate units per Si, write C=C0-2S and m=S/(M_C*C). Then 2S/C0=2mM_C/(1+2mM_C):44.84% of Li units at5.5m and61.91% at11m reside in silicate. Other Li hosts alter this relation. It is a conditional working-inventory constraint, not an irreversible lithium loss. Carbonating and returning this stock may recover it, but the liquidus and thermal cycle must be established at the actual changing composition.

The Si throughput is1.114103mol per kg original-feed Fe. If all Si leaves as orthosilicate, it carries30.9275kg Li/t original-feed Fe before regeneration. Al as LiAlO2 adds5.49434kg/t. These are stoichiometric capacities, not measured consumption. A chosen1kg Li/t loss screen requires96.7666% return of the Si-associated Li even before Al, carbonate entrainment and other losses. The inherited metasilicate-only scenario is not silently used as orthosilicate accounting.

For a chosen maximum1% loss of original feed Fe, the separated solids must contain Fe/Si<.160728 mol/mol if all incoming Si is purged there. A hypothetical1:1 Fe:Si purge would lose6.22171% of feed Fe. This is a balance challenge, not identification of a real solid. Entrained mother liquor adds Fe loss even if crystals contain no Fe.

## Decision-changing measurement / counterexample
Binary endpoints do not determine mixed-phase selectivity. Two admissible continuations share these endpoints: (a) pure silicate crystals exclude Fe; (b) Fe partitions into a ternary solid or entrained liquid. Neither is selected by present data. Therefore do not simulate an ideal fractional-crystallization advantage as if it were measured.
The needed interface is the recovered Si mass and Fe/Li/Al inventory in both hot-filtered phases over a specified750–950 C loop, together with carbonate/CO2 inventory and flow/heat duty. The proposed1%Fe and1kgLi/t screens are transparent design choices, not literature safety/economic thresholds. A measurement of distribution alone does not close complete energy or life.

## Strong ordinary alternative
A controlled bleed followed by ordinary cooled aqueous carbonate recovery must receive the same Fe and Li recovery assumptions, heat recovery, inventory and resource boundary. Neither may receive free selective separation. Electrolytic Fe depletion before bleeding is another ordinary option; its low-Fe mass transfer, parasitic current and full cell voltage must be observed, not extrapolated from high-Fe runs. A hot loop has no established advantage over these alternatives here.

## Check and error boundary
calculate.py uses exact Fraction arithmetic for balances, including an independent substitution check of the inventory equation. Decimal source values are nominal; neither rational arithmetic nor approximate source endpoints supply measurement uncertainty. No process trajectory or laboratory operation executed. No model of activity, phase nucleation, filtration, corrosion or heat exchange is introduced to force a favorable outcome.

## Further attempted falsification: accumulating the bath is not free
The recovered2013 ESI Table11 provides a same-configuration perturbation, not only solubility: at800C,1A and2h, increasing added orthosilicate reduces assayed Fe0 while the highest loading has lower voltage. Numeric rows and direct electrical quotient are in impurity_rows.json and impurity_check.json. The highest/lowest specific cell-energy ratio is1.26497 despite the lower voltage. Replicate uncertainty was not acquired. This does not identify the cause or establish a transferable damaging concentration, but defeats an inference that dissolved gangue can accumulate harmlessly merely because it remains soluble.

A more basic construction failure: heating a dilute sidestream does not concentrate Si. If the main bath stays near its nominal .149m and neither solvent nor another component is removed, heating to950C and cooling to750C never reaches either binary saturation. The .202564kg/kg lever-rule calculation assumes an already saturated11m stream; it is NOT an acquired concentrator. Repeated feed accumulation could create it only by changing the main working composition/inventory, whose electrolysis performance is unqualified. The cold/hot phase path therefore remains a proposed future interface, not a useful completed operation. Do not launch a favorable simulation that starts with11m as free input.

Scientific decision08:31UTC: reject temperature swing alone as the missing preconcentrator for the nominal feed. Preserve its conditional phase/inventory requirements. Next identified comparison is an ordinary cooled purge with chemical lithium recovery, counting water, heat, Fe recovery, carbonate entrainment and restored composition. Such a route must be supported by actual compatible recovery observations before a process advantage is claimed; existing product-washing assays do not supply bulk-purge recovery.
