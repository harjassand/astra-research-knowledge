## B13. Reuse and other unresolved frontiers

The primitive is a word-vacuum-START joint channel. Actual rare errors
can leave a nonvacuum word correlated with logical messages; the block
map on that nonvacuum subspace is not bounded by this theorem. A simple
absorbing error flag gives a counterexample to inferring local-stochastic
faults: at vacuum it fails with probability p and sets a permanent bad
flag; from that flag it fails forever. Its vacuum-start joint diamond
distance is exactly 2p, but r consecutive faults can have probability p,
not p^r. No such fault law is asserted for this device by the source.

A valid ideal-past/actual-future hybrid gives a B-times-one-block error
bound from a vacuum start, because each replaced ideal-past block has
exact word vacuum while all logical/seed/reference correlations are
allowed in (B9). This does NOT pay program installation, preserve a
fault-tolerant outer threshold or prove bootstrap transport. Those remain
OPEN. It also does not supply a uniform memory bound on arbitrary
nonvacuum words or a free repeated bath reset.
