1. PRECISE AUDIT SCOPE

The exact interaction is V=alpha U+i beta I, alpha=sqrt(1-beta^2), on a
qutrit port and E=C^2_seed tensor F(C^2), with S_j prepending j and R
exchanging only the vacuum seeds. The same bath evolves over N calls.
Q_N+L is conserved. Initial bath and logical input/encoder are independent.
There is no bath access, bath reset, receiver-to-sender feedback, shared
entanglement, postselection, or uncharged call/timing resource. The code
encoder and decoder can be arbitrary CPTP maps, and the physical input
can have arbitrary correlations across calls and charge sectors.

No bath-return, bounded-depth, inward-drift, charge-preservation, finite
initial entropy, or finite mean depth premise enters the upper proof.
The fixed-normal extension below requires one fixed normal initializer
before N tends to infinity. An initializer changing with N is not covered.

The candidate proves a Bell-fidelity converse directly for the actual
logical reference. It does not equate that reference with a purifier of a
general CPTP encoder's physical input. Thus the reference mismatch flagged
in pinned Cycle11 is discharged by a different, reference-sensitive route.

2. CHECKED CHARGE COHERENCES AND PINCHING

For sigma_H=P_{=H}/r_H, r_H=2^(H+1), the initial bath commutes with L.
Conservation implies N_H is covariant from Q_input to Q_output. Thus
N_H Delta_input=Delta_output N_H for total-charge pinchings.
For any reference-output positive state X,
  X <= (N+1)(id tensor Delta_output)(X).
Apply the positive Bell effect after the fixed CPTP decoder. This proves
F_H <= (N+1) F_H^pinched even for an arbitrary coherent encoder.
No claim that the pinched channel is reliable, and no physical pinching,
is needed. Polynomial loss is harmless for the asymptotic exponent.

3. CHECKED CHOI NORMALIZATION AND THE INITIAL PURIFIER

For the block q -> o, use an UNNORMALIZED Bell vector on input charge q.
After the actual unitary and output-charge projection, one pure vector
has reductions J_{H;q,o} on input-copy/output and Omega on E F_H.
They have the same nonzero eigenvalues. The E marginal omega is the
physical bath output from the UNNORMALIZED input P_q, not P_q/d_q.

For positive X on E tensor C^r, pinching the r finite blocks proves
  X <= r (Tr_F X) tensor I_F.
Consequently ||J|| <= r_H ||omega||. The factor r_H is necessary: already
N=1,H=0,beta=0,q=1,o=0 gives ||J||=1 and ||omega||=1/2. This is an exact
counterexample to dropping the initial seed purifier dimension. The proof
retains it and cancels it against the initial shell density 1/r_H.

4. CHECKED EXACT CP TRANSFER AND INTERFERENCE

The only nonzero port Kraus blocks are
  K00=alpha R+i beta I,
  Kj0=alpha S_j^*, K0j=alpha S_j, Kjj=i beta I.
For independent positive variables z,w the weighted CP transfer is
  M_{z,w}(X)=sum_{i,o} z^(q_i) w^(q_o) Koi X Koi^*.
On a radial diagonal X (constant at each full depth shell), this equals
the positive diagonal kernel
  a RXR + a w sum_j S_j^* X S_j + a z sum_j S_j X S_j^*
  + b(1+2zw)X,
because the exact cross term i alpha beta (XR-RX) vanishes there.
This radial diagonal set is invariant. Therefore all
coefficients of M_{z,w}^N(sigma_H) are diagonal seed symmetric. By exact
ordered Kraus expansion, the coefficient of z^q w^o is omega_{H;q,o}.
In particular, at z=w=t,
  M_t^N(sigma_H)=sum_{q,o} t^(q+o) omega_{H;q,o}.
All terms are positive; no coherent-history interference was discarded
for arbitrary encoder inputs. These special Choi evaluation inputs are
diagonal, and CP domination subsequently handles every encoder.

An actual wording error found in Version 3 was repaired in Version 4:
equal values only at the two vacuum seeds do NOT define an invariant
class. For example X=|seed0,word1><seed0,word1| has zero values at both
roots, but one transfer step produces unequal root values, and a second
step can create root coherence. Radial diagonal operators, or diagonal
operators invariant under swapping the two ENTIRE seed trees, do form
the needed class. Every sigma_H and iterate used in the proof is radial,
so the correction does not alter T1--T6 or any constant. This local
counterexample and its exact repair were sent to w49 and the root.

5. SIMPLER SUPER-SOLUTION AND A STRONGER FINITE CONSTANT

Let psi(v)=2^(-L(v)/2). This is positive and bounded, although not a
trace-class density and not a physically prepared state. For the weighted
tree adjacency A_t (edge weight t except central edge weight 1),
  (A_t psi)(l)=2 sqrt(2)t psi(l), l>=1;
  (A_t psi)(0)=1+sqrt(2)t <=2sqrt(2)t psi(0), t>=1/sqrt(2).
The actual two vacuum seeds are retained in the second identity. Thus
  M_t psi <= g_b(t) psi,
  g_b(t)=2sqrt(2)(1-b)t+b(1+2t^2).
Since sigma_H(v)<=psi(v)/(r_H psi(H)), positivity gives
  M_t^N(sigma_H)(v)<=g_b(t)^N psi(v)/(r_H psi(H)).
Conservation fixes final depth l=H+q-o. The positive coefficient and
purifier bounds then give
  ||J_{H;q,o}|| <= s_{H;q,o}(t),
  s=g_b(t)^N t^(-(q+o)) 2^(-(q-o)/2),
and s=0 for H+q-o<0.

The original w49 eigenfunction (1+cL)2^(-L/2), c=1-1/sqrt(2), is also
valid. The simpler function above removes its unnecessary linear depth
factor and improves its displayed prefactor. It does not change the
capacity exponent.

6. CHECKED CP DOMINATION, K^-2, AND ALL DIMENSION FACTORS

The Choi criterion gives
  N_{H;q,o} <=_CP [X -> s_{H;q,o} Tr(X) P_o].
For the actual encoded Bell state Omega_RA, let
  Omega_q=(I_R tensor P_q)Omega(I_R tensor P_q),
  rho_R,q=Tr_A Omega_q.
Because the encoder is trace preserving,
  sum_q rho_R,q=I_R/K, hence rho_R,q<=I_R/K.
This remains true for nonisometric CPTP encoders with discarded ancillas.
For any CPTP decoder D, the normalized Bell overlap satisfies
  Tr Phi_K [rho_R,q tensor D(P_o)]
    =Tr[rho_R,q^T D(P_o)]/K
    <=Tr D(P_o)/K^2
    =binom(N,o)2^o/K^2.
Thus
  F_H <=(N+1) K^-2 sum_{q,o} d_o s_{H;q,o}.

It is important to retain the q,o blocks until this sum. A crude common
output-identity replacement would produce a needless factor 3^N. The
legitimate geometric sum over q-o produces the smaller 2^N factor at
t=sqrt(2). There is no missing input multiplicity d_q: the Choi vector
was unnormalized, and d_q is already included in its transfer evaluation.

7. ACTUAL TAU_PI AND STRONG CONVERSE

For t>1/sqrt(2), fixed o, extending q-o down to -H and up to infinity,
  sum_q s_{H;q,o}
    <=g_b(t)^N t^(-2o) (t sqrt(2))^H / [1-1/(t sqrt(2))].
Using sum_o binom(N,o)2^o t^(-2o)=(1+2/t^2)^N and the exact mixture
  tau_pi=sum_H (2/3)3^(-H) sigma_H,
linearity of physical code fidelity in the initializer yields, whenever
1/sqrt(2)<t<3/sqrt(2),
  F_N(tau_pi) <= min{1, (N+1) C(t) h_b(t)^N/K^2},
  C(t)=2/{[3-t sqrt(2)][1-1/(t sqrt(2))]},
  h_b(t)=g_b(t)(1+2/t^2).
The actual infinite bath purifier is not assigned a finite dimension:
each exact shell was treated with its own purifier, then the physical
channel mixture was averaged. Fidelity is linear in that mixture.

At t=sqrt(2), C(t)=4 and h_b(t)=8+2b, so this independently sharpened
bound is
  F_N(tau_pi) <= min{1, 4(N+1)(8+2b)^N/K^2}.
Section 9 improves its prefactor further to 2 by global Choi domination.
Therefore any rate R>0.5 log_2(8+2b) has F_N->0 exponentially for this
initializer. Full-diamond reliability implies F_N->1, proving
  Q(beta,tau_pi)<=1.5+0.5 log_2(1+beta^2/4).
The port dimension bound log_2(3) can also be applied. The leading small-b
coefficient of this upper is 1/(8 ln 2); no sharp-coefficient claim is made.

8. EXTENSION TO EVERY FIXED NORMAL INITIALIZER, INCLUDING COHERENCES

For a fixed normal density tau, let P_M=P_{<=M} and tau_M=P_M tau P_M.
The trace-class convergence delta_M=||tau-tau_M||_1->0 is standard finite
coordinate approximation and does not require finite entropy/mean depth.
Since tau_pi is diagonal and faithful with minimum eigenvalue
(1/3)6^(-M) on P_M,
  tau_M <=||tau||_infty P_M <=c_M tau_pi,
  c_M=3*6^M ||tau||_infty <=3*6^M.
For each fixed code, F_N(sigma) is a positive linear functional of the
independent initial bath state with 0<=F_N(sigma)<=Tr sigma. In particular
its representing bath effect lies between 0 and I, so
  F_N(tau)<=F_N(tau_M)+delta_M
            <=c_M F_N(tau_pi)+delta_M.
For any rate above the bound, hold M fixed and let N->infinity. Then take
M->infinity. This proves F_N(tau)->0 for every fixed normal tau, even if
it has arbitrary depth/seed coherences and infinite entropy or mean depth.
It does not prove a uniform convergence rate over all normal tau or an
exponential prefactor for arbitrary tails. It does not cover tau_N varying
with N. Codes may be chosen specifically for a given fixed tau; the bound
at tau_pi was uniform over codes, so the comparison still applies.

9. GLOBAL CHOI CLEANUP, FACTOR 2, AND MUTUAL-INFORMATION CONSEQUENCES

After the initial audit was completed, w49 relayed a further simplification
attributed to w38. I independently checked the argument below. Covariance
means J_H only has matrix elements with equal Choi charge difference q-o.
Consequently pinching J_H in OUTPUT charge alone yields exactly
  Delta_B(J_H)=sum_{q,o} J_{H;q,o}.
For a nonzero block, d=q-o>=-H and t sqrt(2)>1, so Section 5 gives
  s_{H;q,o}<=g_b(t)^N (t sqrt(2))^H t^(-2o).
Let B_t=diag(1,t^(-2),t^(-2))^(tensor N). The pinching inequality for
the full unnormalized Choi matrix gives
  J_H <=(N+1)Delta_B(J_H)
      <=(N+1)g_b(t)^N(t sqrt(2))^H I_A tensor B_t.
Thus the FULL, UNPINCHED physical channel obeys CP domination by
  N_H <=_CP [X -> (N+1)g_b(t)^N(t sqrt(2))^H Tr(X) B_t].
Average the exact initial shell mixture, without a geometric q sum. With
Z_t=Tr B_t=(1+2/t^2)^N and sigma_t=B_t/Z_t, this proves
  N_N^(tau_pi) <=_CP C_N(t) [X -> Tr(X) sigma_t],
  C_N(t)=2(N+1) h_b(t)^N/[3-t sqrt(2)].
At t=sqrt(2), sigma_t=diag(1/2,1/4,1/4)^(tensor N), and
  C_N=2(N+1)(8+2b)^N.
The full channel can therefore replace the preceding blockwise sum in
the Bell argument. Its reference marginal is exactly I/K, giving
  F_N(tau_pi)<=min{1,2(N+1)(8+2b)^N/K^2}.
This is the strongest finite-fidelity claim in this audit. It keeps the
same capacity exponent and is compatible with all earlier, weaker bounds.

More generally, for EVERY input/reference density rho_RA, CP domination
gives the actual output bound
  omega_RB<=(C_N)rho_R tensor sigma_t.
Operator monotonicity of logarithm, restricted to the support of rho_R,
proves D_2(omega_RB || rho_R tensor sigma_t)<=log_2 C_N, hence
  I(R:B)_omega<=log_2 C_N.
For a purified physical input, writing Ic=S(B)-S(E F0), one has
  I(R:B)=S(A)+Ic and Ic<=S(A)
by Araki-Lieb. Therefore
  Ic<=min{S(A),log_2 C_N-S(A)}<=0.5 log_2 C_N
     =N[1.5+0.5log_2(1+b/4)]+0.5log_2[2(N+1)].
The untouched actual initializer purifier F0 is included in E F0. Exact
purity of the global dilation also gives the reference leakage lower
  I(R:E F0)=2S(A)-I(R:B)>=[2S(A)-log_2 C_N]_+.
These all-input information bounds are for actual tau_pi. Section 8
independently extends the capacity strong converse, pointwise, to every
fixed normal initializer; no universal finite information slack for
arbitrary initial tails is inferred from that limiting argument.

10. FINITE ADVERSARIAL DIAGNOSTICS AND THEIR LIMITS

independent_check.py implements the original coherent transition rule on
individual computational input words and bath vertices, explicitly sums
colliding complex history amplitudes, purifies each mixed initial shell,
constructs charge-block Gram matrices, and checks:
  - norm preservation and conserved depth/charge;
  - Choi eigenvalue versus bath norm with the purifier rank retained;
  - exact independent-weighted transfer identity;
  - diagonal/radial bath marginals for Choi evaluation inputs;
  - both original and simplified analytic charge-block bounds.
For N<=2 it additionally constructs the FULL unpinched Choi matrix and
checks its exact charge covariance, equality of output pinching with the
q,o block sum, the pinching inequality, and Section 9's global CP bound.
Settings: N=1,2,3; H=0,1,2; beta=0,0.1,1/sqrt(2),1, plus N=4,H=0 for
the same beta values; t=1,sqrt(2),1.9. The run has 40 parameter cases and
348 charge blocks. These are finite floating-point checks, not all-length
proofs or independent experimental measurements. Raw JSON is retained.
