1. EXACT DEVICE AND INITIALIZER

E has basis (s,w), s in {0,1}, w a finite word over {1,2}. L(s,w)=|w|.
S_j prepends j; R exchanges the two vacuum seeds and is zero elsewhere.

 U=[[R,S_1,S_2],[S_1^*,0,0],[S_2^*,0,0]],
 V=alpha U+i beta I, alpha=sqrt(1-beta^2), q=diag(0,1,1),
 W_N=V_{N,E}...V_{1,E}.

U is a self-adjoint involution. W_N conserves total input/output port
charge plus bath depth, including the original R. All calls use the
same evolving bath. No reset, bath access, preshared entanglement,
postselection, reverse quantum wire, or free timing is supplied. A fixed
N-call unassisted protocol with no feedback is represented by one CPTP
encoder into all N inputs, which may be arbitrarily correlated, and a
decoder on all outputs. Local retained encoder systems are allowed.

The bath graph joins every word to its two children and joins the two
vacuum seeds; this is the infinite 3-regular tree. Both central-edge
endpoints have L=0. Write r_H=2^{H+1} and let sigma_H be the uniform
density on the full bath shell of depth H. The actual normal initializer
is exactly

 tau_pi=sum_{H>=0} p_H sigma_H, p_H=(2/3)3^{-H}.              (1)

This decomposition is analytic; no physical measurement, conditioning,
preparation or reset of the initializer is proposed. Its initial purifier
is included when constructing each complementary Choi state below.

2. CHARGE BLOCKS OF THE UNNORMALIZED CHOI MATRIX

Fix H and let N_H be the N-call channel at sigma_H. Let P_q^A and
P_o^B be total input/output charge projectors, q,o=0,...,N, and put

 N_{H;q,o}(X)=P_o^B N_H(P_q^A X P_q^A) P_o^B.               (2)

Because [sigma_H,L]=0 and W_N conserves Q+L, N_H is charge covariant:
N_H(e^{i theta Q_A}Xe^{-i theta Q_A})
=e^{i theta Q_B}N_H(X)e^{-i theta Q_B}.
Its unnormalized Choi matrix J_H therefore commutes with
-Q_A^T+Q_B. If Delta_B pinches only the N+1 output charge sectors,

 Delta_B(J_H)=sum_{q,o}J_{H;q,o},
 J_H <= (N+1)Delta_B(J_H).                                  (3)

The first identity follows because output pinching sets o=o', and charge
covariance then forces q=q'. The second is the finite positive-operator
pinching inequality. Both preserve all coherent input states; no physical
pinching is required.

Purify sigma_H into an untouched F_H of dimension r_H. Apply W_N to
this purification and an UNNORMALIZED maximally entangled vector between
P_q A^N and its copy; project the physical output onto P_o. The resulting
unnormalized vector is pure. Its two reduced operators J_{H;q,o} (on
input-copy and output) and Omega_{H;q,o} (on E F_H) have identical
nonzero spectra. The E part is supported at the single depth

 l=H+q-o; the block vanishes if l<0.                          (4)

For a positive X on E tensor C^r, pinching in the finite second factor
and bounding each positive diagonal block by their sum gives

 X <= r (Tr_{C^r}X) tensor I_r.

Consequently, with omega_{H;q,o}=Tr_F Omega_{H;q,o},

 ||J_{H;q,o}||_infty <= r_H ||omega_{H;q,o}||_infty.           (5)

The purifier dimension factor r_H is explicitly retained. Input charge
and output spaces are finite, and all bath states reached from a fixed
shell in N steps lie at depth <=H+N, so these individual calculations
are finite without replacing the infinite device or hitting a cutoff.

3. EXACT WEIGHTED TRANSFER FOR THESE CHOI MARGINALS

For t>=1 define the normal CP map

 M_t(X)=sum_{i,o in {0,1,2}} t^{q_i+q_o}K_{oi}XK_{oi}^*,
 K_{oi}=<o|V|i>.

The nonzero Kraus blocks are
K_00=alpha R+i beta I,
K_j0=alpha S_j^*, K_0j=alpha S_j, K_jj=i beta I, j=1,2.
Sequential expansion gives exactly

 M_t^N(sigma_H)=sum_{q,o}t^{q+o}omega_{H;q,o}.                 (6)

There is no normalized maximally mixed factor: the input is P_q in the
unnormalized Choi marginal. For RADIAL DIAGONAL bath operators (constant
at every bath depth), the two vacuum seeds have equal values, so the
sole interference term in M_t is i alpha beta(XR-RX)=0. The radial
diagonal class is invariant under M_t and contains sigma_H. On its
diagonal vectors the exact positive kernel is

 M_t=a A_t+b(1+2t^2)I,                                     (7)

where the bath-tree edges have weight t except that the actual central
root edge has weight 1. The identity coefficient b(1+2t^2) includes all
three input/output port choices. Equation (7) is an exact evaluation of
this particular Choi trace calculation; it is not a physical classical
mixture of U and I for arbitrary coherent inputs.

Every summand in (6) is positive. Therefore
omega_{H;q,o}<=t^{-(q+o)}M_t^N(sigma_H). No separate diagonality of
individual omega_{H;q,o} is needed. Its known support (4) permits
compression of the upper bound to depth l.

4. ROOT-RETAINING POSITIVE SUPER-SOLUTION

Set psi(v)=2^{-L(v)/2}. It is positive and bounded. At every nonroot
vertex A_t psi=2sqrt(2)t psi. At either root,

 A_t psi=1+sqrt(2)t <=2sqrt(2)t psi

for t>=1/sqrt(2); we use t>=1. Hence, including the root,

 M_t psi <=g_b(t)psi,
 g_b(t)=2sqrt(2)(1-b)t+b(1+2t^2).                            (8)

The diagonal vector of sigma_H is bounded pointwise by
psi/(r_H psi(H)). Iterating this positive-kernel inequality gives

 [M_t^N(sigma_H)](v)
 <=g_b(t)^N psi(v)/(r_H psi(H)).                              (9)

By (4)-(6),(9), the unnormalized Choi block satisfies

 J_{H;q,o}<=s_{H;q,o} I_{A_q} tensor I_{B_o},
 s_{H;q,o}=g_b(t)^N t^{-(q+o)}2^{-(q-o)/2},                 (10)

when H+q-o>=0, and is zero otherwise. Notice that r_H in (5) cancels
the r_H in (9); neither is discarded. The initial shell may be deep,
and the final shell may escape linearly. No depth bound is used.

5. GLOBAL CP DOMINATION AND EXACT INITIALIZER SUM

Since t sqrt(2)>=1 and q-o>=-H in every nonzero block,

 s_{H;q,o}
 =g_b(t)^N t^{-2o}(t sqrt(2))^{-(q-o)}
 <=g_b(t)^N(t sqrt(2))^H t^{-2o}.                            (11)

Let B_t=sum_o t^{-2o}P_o^B. Combining (3),(10),(11), and summing the
orthogonal input charge projectors, gives

 J_H <=(N+1)g_b(t)^N(t sqrt(2))^H I_A tensor B_t.

The Choi criterion for complete positivity proves

 N_H <=_CP (N+1)g_b(t)^N(t sqrt(2))^H [X->Tr(X)B_t].        (12)

For 1<=t<3/sqrt(2), average (12) over the ACTUAL p_H in (1). The
infinite sum is exactly
sum_H p_H(t sqrt(2))^H=2/(3-t sqrt(2)). Thus

 N_N^{tau_pi} <=_CP A_N(t) R_{zeta_t},                       (13)
 A_N(t)= [2(N+1)/(3-t sqrt(2))] h_b(t)^N,
 h_b(t)=g_b(t)(1+2/t^2),
 zeta_t=B_t/Tr B_t,
 Tr B_t=(1+2/t^2)^N.

The sum converges in trace norm for the channels' finite output and in
the finite Choi matrix. Positive CP order is closed under this sum.
Every initial bath shell and its purifier has been charged explicitly.

At t=sqrt(2), g_b(t)=4+b, h_b(t)=8+2b,
zeta_t=sigma_*^{tensor N}, and 2/(3-t sqrt(2))=2. This proves (T1).

6. ARBITRARY CPTP ENCODER, LOGICAL REFERENCE, AND FIDELITY

Let omega_RA=(id_R tensor encoder)(Phi_K), where R is the untouched
logical reference. Then omega_R=I_R/K regardless of retained or
discarded local encoder ancillas. Applying (13) with its arbitrary
reference, then the CPTP decoder D, gives

 (id_R tensor D N_N^{tau_pi})(omega_RA)
 <=A_N(t)(I_R/K) tensor D(zeta_t).

The Bell overlap of the right-hand normalized product is
Tr[Phi_K((I_R/K)tensor D(zeta_t))]=1/K^2, since Tr D(zeta_t)=1.
This proves F_N<=A_N(t)/K^2 and in particular (T2). The K^2 factor
does not assume an isometric encoder or equate the logical reference
with a purification of the physical input. This is a direct finite-N
coding converse, independent of an imported memoryless capacity theorem.

7. ALL-INPUT MUTUAL INFORMATION, COHERENT INFORMATION AND LEAKAGE

For any state rho_RA (finite R suffices), (13) implies
omega_RB<=(A_N(t))rho_R tensor zeta_t. Operator monotonicity of log,
restricted to supports if rho_R is singular, yields

 D_2(omega_RB || rho_R tensor zeta_t)<=log_2 A_N(t).

The relative-entropy identity
D_2(omega||rho_R tensor zeta_t)
=I(R:B)_omega+D_2(omega_B||zeta_t)
and nonnegativity of the last term prove I(R:B)<=log_2 A_N(t).
At t=sqrt(2) this is (T3).

If R purifies the physical input rho, S(R)=S_2(rho), and
Ic=I(R:B)-S(R). Araki-Lieb also gives Ic<=S(R). Combining these
two inequalities proves (T4). With the actual initial bath purifier F0
retained, the global state R B E_N F0 is pure. Hence

 I(R:E_N F0)=2S(R)-I(R:B),

which proves (T5), including its nonnegative positive-part form.
All relevant entropies are finite: R and B are finite, and the reduced
E_N F0 of this pure global state has rank at most dim(R)dim(B), even
though E_N and F0 themselves may be infinite dimensional.

The all-input result includes arbitrary correlations and coherences among
physical inputs. It establishes leakage when input entropy exceeds the
unrestricted ceiling. Pure push is consistent: its input entropy is zero,
so the lower leakage bound is zero even while its depth grows linearly.
The stronger numerical coefficient c(b) from the sublinear-depth theorem
is not claimed; (T5) uses C_up(b) and explicit logarithmic slack.

 F_N(tau)<=c_M F_N(tau_pi)+||tau-tau_M||_1.

At any rate strictly above C_up, first let N->infinity with M fixed,
