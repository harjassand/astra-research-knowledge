1. The precise primary import and its resource scope
----------------------------------------------------
Mančinska, arXiv:2610.06512v1 (5 October 2026), Section3, Proposition1,
Theorem3 and Corollary4 supply a fixed finite Flagged Magic-Square game
G with five questions per player, five answers per question, uniform
product question law, and an explicit perfect strategy on

 psi=(|0,0>+sqrt(2)sum_(j=1)^4 |j,j>)/3.                 (T1)

The source proves
        omega_*(G)=omega_co(G)=1 > omega_tr(G).           (T2)
Here a tracial table is p(a,b|x,y)=tau(E_a^x F_b^y), with a tracial
state tau on any unital C*-algebra and two complete POVM families.
This is a subclass of commuting tables via left/right multiplication;
the two effect families inside the algebra need not themselves commute.
Every finite maximally entangled strategy is tracial after transposing
Bob in its Schmidt basis. Thus omega_ME<=omega_tr<1.

The strict tracial gap in (T2) is a PRIMARY THEOREM IMPORT, not a result
of the present note. Its proof uses rigid Magic-Square directions and
bridge constraints. Their required zero amplitudes cancel with the
unequal weights in (T1); the corresponding tracial orthogonality
relations are inconsistent. Tracial-value attainment turns that exact
inconsistency into a dimension-independent strict gap. No numerical
lower bound on that gap is supplied here.

Set epsilon=1-omega_tr(G)>0, a fixed constant for this fixed game.
In particular its finite maximally entangled gap is at least epsilon.

Private input-independent ancillas may be absorbed into the POVMs.
Shared classical randomness is a convex mixture and cannot improve
the supremal value. Tensoring another maximally entangled resource
still gives a maximally entangled resource. Supplying a nonmaximally
entangled state such as (T1) is OUTSIDE this restriction and removes
the base gap. General normal vector states in a tracial algebra are
also outside the restriction that the resource vector be the trace.

2. What the proposed state-elimination operation would have to change
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The following finite-matrix description is exact and helps locate the
failed operator move. Write a pure shared state as vec(S), S>=0,
Tr(S^2)=1, using its Schmidt supports. For actual POVMs E_a^x,F_b^y,
define

 R_a^x=S^(1/2) E_a^x S^(1/2),
 T_b^y=S^(1/2) (F_b^y)^T S^(1/2).

Then both families have the SAME positive common sum S, and

       p(a,b|x,y)=Tr(R_a^x T_b^y).                      (T3)

For a forbidden pair in a perfect strategy, positivity gives
R_a^x T_b^y=0. Conversely any such finite positive families with common
sum S reconstruct complete POVMs on supp(S) by congruence with
S^(-1/2), and reproduce (T3). Thus the common weight is part of an
actual operator representation, not merely a scalar normalization.

An invertible contragredient change
 R -> U R U*,  T -> (U*)^(-1) T U^(-1)
preserves all pair traces and zero products; the products change by
similarity. The two total sums become
U S U* and (U*)^(-1) S U^(-1). Their product is U S^2 U^(-1).
They can both be the same scalar multiple of identity only if S has
flat spectrum on its support. This rules out that literal gauge move.
More generally (T2) rules out ANY game-aware replacement into tracial
resources that preserves perfect payoff for every game, even if it
changes the individual fine tables and its dimension is unbounded.
It does not rule out such a replacement for a typical BG graph.

3. Repeated maximally entangled source, with all fine instruments kept
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Fix n. Use n copies of the source's actual local five-outcome PVMs,
with their original answer labels. For each full question tuple q,
let W_q be the joint winning projection for all n coordinates under
these product PVMs. The perfect state satisfies

       W_q psi^tensor n=psi^tensor n                    (T4)

for EVERY q, not just on average. The projection property is convenient
but a positive contraction with this eigenvector would suffice.

For the spectrum (T1), group Schmidt strings by the number a of nonzero
symbols. Let

 D_a=binom(n,a)4^a,
 Phi_a=D_a^(-1/2)sum_(z in {0,...,4}^n: #nonzero(z)=a)|z,z>,
 p_a=binom(n,a)8^a/9^n.

The vectors Phi_a are mutually orthogonal, and each is MAXIMALLY
ENTANGLED on its D_a-dimensional local Schmidt support. Directly,

       psi^tensor n=sum_(a=0)^n sqrt(p_a) Phi_a,
       sum_a p_a=1.                                    (T5)

Choose a mode a_n of this explicit binomial distribution OFFLINE,
before questions arrive. Then p_(a_n)>=1/(n+1). The original paper's
Section1.2 already gives the general Schmidt-type polynomial lower
bound; (T5) just uses the two distinct coefficients of this particular
state rather than resolving all five symbol counts. No priority claim
is made for the type argument.

Since 0<=W_q<=I and W_q psi^tensor n=psi^tensor n,

          W_q >= |psi^tensor n><psi^tensor n|.           (T6)

Indeed its orthogonal complement is invariant and positive. Therefore
the ACTUAL initial resource Phi_(a_n), followed by the unchanged
product fine PVMs, has

 Pr[all n win | full question tuple q]
    =<Phi_(a_n),W_q Phi_(a_n)>
    >=p_(a_n)>=1/(n+1)                                 (T7)

for every q. This is a legal finite maximally entangled, hence tracial,
repeated strategy. One may embed its Schmidt support into the product
5^n-dimensional measurement space by local isometries, or compress
each full-string POVM to that support. No entangled resource with
unequal Schmidt coefficients is introduced in the repeated source.
The shared rank D_(a_n) can be exponential in n; that cost is retained.

All fine answers may be copied to local classical records in the actual
PVM instruments and kept. The referee's win events are functions of
these actual records and questions. The argument does not erase a
checked workspace or infer a physical joint input oracle.

4. A useful selected history with L/m tending to zero
-----------------------------------------------------
Let W_i be the binary win event on coordinate i of THIS fixed source.
For C subset [n], define

 E_C=intersection_(i in C) W_i,
 p_C=Pr(E_C),  L_C=ln(1/p_C).

Equation (T7) implies, for every C,

        p_C>=p_[n]>=1/(n+1),  L_C<=ln(n+1).              (T8)

Choose the following coordinate set OFFLINE from the fixed source's
conditional probabilities, with fixed tie breaking, not by observing a
particular run. Start C empty. While an untested coordinate i has Pr[not W_i | E_C]>=epsilon/2, add one such i. Each
addition changes the actual event probability by

 p_(C union {i})=p_C Pr[W_i|E_C]<=(1-epsilon/2)p_C.

Put lambda=-ln(1-epsilon/2)>0. After t additions,
t lambda<=L_C<=ln(n+1). Hence the process must stop after at most
floor(ln(n+1)/lambda) additions. For all sufficiently large n it stops
before n/2, with m=n-|C| and

 Pr[not W_i|E_C]<epsilon/2 for EVERY remaining i,
 alpha_C=(1/m)sum_(i notin C)Pr[not W_i|E_C]<epsilon/2,
              L_C/m ->0.                              (T9)

This is not an arbitrary choice of a terminal all-win event masquerading
as a useful small checked set. It is a selected C of O(log n) actual
checked coordinates of the original source. At C empty, an unconditional
coordinate marginal is an ordinary tracial strategy, so its loss is at
least epsilon; a first greedy step exists. Such a marginal is obtained
by sampling all other question pairs offline, running the full local
measurements and retaining that coordinate; it supplies no opposite
fresh input to either party.

Only classical measured-event information is used. On the FULL measured
question/outcome distribution,

 D(P_(full|E_C)||P_full)=L_C.                            (T10)

Question-only DPI gives D(Q_questions||mu^tensor n)<=L_C. Product-reference
superadditivity and Pinsker therefore imply, on the remaining questions,

 (1/m)sum_(i notin C) TV(Q_(X_iY_i),mu)
       <=sqrt(L_C/(2m))->0.                             (T11)

No relative-entropy bound is asserted for a coherently postselected pure
resource against its original pure state. Equation (T10) is the
compatible classical-event restriction identity, followed by DPI.

5. What is genuinely impossible, and what is not
-------------------------------------------------
The original selected live payoff in (T9) exceeds the base tracial value
by more than epsilon/2. Every COMPLETE ordinary fresh-input tracial
strategy has payoff <=1-epsilon. Thus any extraction into that resource
class loses at least epsilon/2 in the mean live payoff, although both
L_C/m and the mean referee drift tend to zero. This includes arbitrary
finite maximally entangled resources of NEW dimensions, arbitrary
input-independent classical seeds, and private ancillas. Restricting
the extraction merely to the original shared dimension is not the issue.

Equivalently, consider the selected full live question/answer law,
with i sampled uniformly offline from the remaining coordinates.
Any ordinary tracial fresh-law behavior is at joint total-variation
distance at least epsilon/2, by the difference of the bounded binary
winning statistic. The small quantity (T11) shows that the constant
discrepancy cannot be explained by persistent marginal referee drift.
This is an obstruction for a COMPLETE fresh response strategy, not a
lower bound on the success probability of an arbitrary joint-abort box.

The resource closure is the essential limitation. Joint event conditioning
need not yield a complete tracial correlation. Separately, a local branch
filter or a fresh HJW resource can introduce a nonflat Schmidt spectrum.
No pure-state or entanglement inference is drawn from the measured CQ
record. In the unrestricted class, the original
single-round state (T1) already achieves perfect payoff. Thus

     epsilon_co=epsilon_*=0,   epsilon_tr>0              (T12)

for this game. We do not substitute epsilon_tr into an unrestricted
co/tensor theorem. In particular this is neither a general positive-
quantum-gap slow-repetition example nor an obstruction to the frozen
one-sided selector in its own unrestricted model.

It is a sharp warning for attempts to remove the positive standard-form
resource vector by replacing it with the trace. A finite tracial
ALGEBRA containing a positive vector is a larger model than a tracial
STATE. The earlier finite-matrix source constructions permit nonflat
positive Schmidt vectors and are not contradicted by this result.
