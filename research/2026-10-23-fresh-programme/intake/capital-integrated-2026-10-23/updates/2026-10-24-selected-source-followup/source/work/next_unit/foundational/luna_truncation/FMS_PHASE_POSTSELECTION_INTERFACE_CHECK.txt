FMS PHASE-SOURCE POSTSELECTION INTERFACE CHECK
2026-10-24. Bounded check of the actual checked-instrument construction.

Let dθ=dθ/(2π), ψθ=(|00>+e^{iθ}√2 Σ_{j=1}^4|jj>)/3, and
Q_n=∫ |ψθ><ψθ|^{⊗n} dθ, with one common phase across all coordinates.
Fix checked coordinates C, |C|=t, and live coordinates L, |L|=m.
For a checked question q=(x,y), let E_a^x,F_b^y be the original local
fine PVMs and let the checked acceptance CP map sum the product Lüders
maps E_a^x⊗F_b^y over the actual winning outcome pairs. The checked
question is sampled from the original uniform μ on 5×5 and then forgotten.

At fixed θ, the averaged one-coordinate acceptance probability is
f(θ)=E_{q~μ} Σ_{a,b:win}<ψθ|E_a^x⊗F_b^y|ψθ>
     =1-(4/225)(1-cos θ).
For t independent checked questions, the product fine measurements and
the product phase-conditioned source give acceptance weight f(θ)^t.
For each fixed θ, the checked instrument acts only on C; after summing
actual winning fine outcomes and tracing out C, its output on L is exactly
f(θ)^t |ψθ><ψθ|^{⊗m}. Therefore the unnormalized residual state from Q_n
is ∫ f(θ)^t |ψθ><ψθ|^{⊗m}dθ, and its trace is
Z_t=∫f(θ)^t dθ. The conditioned residual density is exactly
  ρ_t^(m)=Z_t^{-1}∫ f(θ)^t |ψθ><ψθ|^{⊗m}dθ.
Since f(θ)≥217/225, Z_t>0 for every finite t.

Forgetting checked questions and fine answers is essential to this
statement: it means summing the physical accepted CP maps, with no record
used by the live strategy. The reweighting by f^t comes from conditioning
on actual win outcomes, not from selecting question tuples alone. A
prep-type flag may likewise be discarded or ignored. Since the source is
the same density Q_n, its actual instrument calculation is linear and
does not require players to know either θ or the type label.

The live questions are independent of the checked experiment and of the
source. Hence their law remains exactly μ^m. Conditional on the checked
event and after forgetting all checked registers, the live pair state is
ρ_t^(m); applying the same original live fine PVMs gives exactly the same
full live classical fine table and live quantum workspace as the
postselected source. Preparing ρ_t^(m) directly before the live inputs is
therefore a legal resource replacement in the unrestricted tensor model.
The conditional event is a mathematical preparation step, not an
online joint measurement or a required LOCC process from Q_n.

The replacement generally leaves the explicit maximally-entangled-type
mixture: f^t has nonzero Fourier coefficients, so ρ_t^(m) has coherences
between different nonzero-symbol-count sectors. For example, when t=1,
f has first Fourier coefficient 2/225; for m≥1 this gives a nonzero
matrix element between |0...0,0...0> and a basis pair with one nonzero
symbol. Thus do not claim the replacement is tracial. This construction
also supplies no positive unrestricted FMS base gap, since ψ_0 itself
has perfect value under the same original fine PVMs.

Scope: this verifies the checked-instrument-to-live-resource interface
given the stated FMS formula for f(θ). It does not independently rederive
the FMS PVMs or prove any relative-entropy estimate for ρ_t^(m).
