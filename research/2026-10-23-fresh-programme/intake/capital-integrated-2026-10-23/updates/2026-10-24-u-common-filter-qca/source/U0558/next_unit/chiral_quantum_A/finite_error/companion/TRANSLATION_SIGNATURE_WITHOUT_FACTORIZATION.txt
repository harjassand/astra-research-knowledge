CLASSICAL TRANSLATION RECORDS CERTIFY INDEX WITHOUT AN A-CHANNEL PREMISE
Companion theorem, 2026-10-24. Both earlier quantum notes remain frozen.

Result
-------
The exact unitary-A-channel/factorization premise is unnecessary for a
SUFFICIENT index certificate. Test the FULL joint A+B QCA directly: record
the A input labels at their prescribed shifted output locations and the B
input labels at their original locations. A known full-rank product input
on BOTH rails converts small mismatches into local operator bounds. The
frozen transpose/intertwiner argument then fixes the full QCA index.

This conclusion permits uncontrolled quantum phases and even dephasing of
the reduced A channel. It certifies index under trusted exact-QCA structure,
not a coherent A shift, arbitrary channel identity, or universal tomography.

1. Exact operation and measurement contract
--------------------------------------------
Each cell i in Z carries A_i of dimension d_A>=2 and B_i of dimension d_B>=1.
Put D=d_A d_B. Let Omega be a full joint strict radius-R QCA automorphism on
the ordinary tensor-product qudit chain, with known integer R>=0. No
factorization of Omega, A-unitary reduced channel, or auxiliary state-return
premise is imposed.

Use product bases and the mathematical partial-shift reference
  sigma(P_A,i,a)=P_A,i-1,a,   sigma(P_B,i,b)=P_B,i,b.                 (1)
Here P_A,i,a and P_B,i,b are component basis projectors, including identity
on the other component and cells. Choose the index orientation in which
index(sigma)=d_A; reversing the convention reciprocates all indices below.
Sigma is a proof reference. Its inverse is NOT an implemented correction.

The input preparation is diagonal and product on both rails:
  tau=product_i (tau_A,i tensor tau_B,i),
  tau_A,i>=a_* I_A,  tau_B,i>=b_* I_B,
  p=a_* b_*>0.                                                   (2)
Thus each full-cell marginal is >=p I_D. Bounds on only one rail do not
supply this full input floor. The product/basis condition ensures copying
an input label to a classical reference leaves the joint A+B marginal tau
unchanged. Classical copies/readout are assumed ideal here.

For each tested output cell i use these comparisons, in separate or compatible
simultaneous experiments:
  A test: input label at A_(i-1) versus output label at A_i;
  B test: input label at B_i versus output label at B_i.             (3)
Let e_A,i,a and e_B,i,b be the JOINT probabilities of the indicated input
label and a mismatch. Only component label statistics are measured; a
separate experiment for every combined (a,b) label is not required.

2. Explicit threshold and finite coverage
------------------------------------------
Put
  r=R+1,  C=9+24 r D,
  delta_0=1/[1540 r D C],
  m_A=R+max(R,1)+1,  m_B=2R+1.                                  (4)
Choose the 4r-site inner proof block S=[0,4r-1] and test output cells
  T=S_r=[-r,5r-1],  |T|=6r.                                    (5)
At every i in T suppose, for every pair of labels a,b,
  sqrt(e_A,i,a/p^m_A)+sqrt(e_B,i,b/p^m_B) <= delta_0.              (6)
Then
  index(Omega)=d_A.                                              (7)
In particular a finite-depth full joint QCA, whose index is 1, cannot match
this classical translation signature at the stated error level.

A convenient sufficient allocation, not an optimized one, is
  e_A,i,a <= p^m_A delta_0^2/4,
  e_B,i,b <= p^m_B delta_0^2/4.                                  (8)
Bounding each component site's TOTAL mismatch by the corresponding value
in (8) also suffices, since every joint label contribution is smaller.

For R>=1, m_A=m_B=2R+1. The input-density floors in (2) are needed on exactly
the union of the supports in the proof:
  U=[-r-max(R,1), 5r-1+R],  |U|=6r+R+max(R,1).                   (9)
This has 8R+6 cells when R>=1; at R=0 it has 7 cells. A simpler conservative
choice is the 8r-site interval [-2r,6r-1], which contains U. The A reference
labels originate in T-1, and the B reference labels in T. No translation
invariance is needed with this finite contiguous coverage.

3. Probability-to-operator conversion for the full joint input
--------------------------------------------------------------
Let Q_A=Omega(P_A,i,a) and P_A'=P_A,i-1,a. The A mismatch is
  e_A,i,a=Tr[tau_Gamma P_A'(I-Q_A)P_A'],                          (10)
where Gamma_A=[i-max(R,1),i+R], of size m_A. It contains BOTH the support of
Q_A (within [i-R,i+R]) and the shifted reference projector P_A'. The larger
left collar matters when R=0. Similarly
  e_B,i,b=Tr[tau_Gamma P_B,i,b(I-Omega(P_B,i,b))P_B,i,b],          (11)
with Gamma_B=[i-R,i+R], of size m_B. These are finite ordinary traces.

Q_A and P_A' have equal local ranks D^m_A/d_A, and the two B projectors have
equal local ranks D^m_B/d_B. This follows from preservation of the canonical
normalized trace (or the unital matrix-unit relations on each component).
The equal-rank projection bound from the frozen theorem therefore yields
  ||Omega(P_A,i,a)-sigma(P_A,i,a)|| <= sqrt(e_A,i,a/p^m_A),
  ||Omega(P_B,i,b)-P_B,i,b|| <= sqrt(e_B,i,b/p^m_B).                (12)
No density lower bound for a fictitious corrected experiment is inserted.
All quantities in (10)--(12) belong to the actual Omega tag experiments.

If label-conditioned errors are used instead, retain their normalization.
For example the compressed A density floor is at least
q_A,i-1,a a_*^(m_A-1)b_*^m_A, where q_A,i-1,a is the input label probability.
The analogous B floor is q_B,i,b a_*^m_B b_*^(m_B-1). Dividing by q converts
joint error to conditional error; rare-label acquisition is still a resource.

4. Reduction to the frozen operator-core theorem
------------------------------------------------
Define alpha=sigma^(-1) composed with Omega. It is a strict QCA of radius
at most r=R+1. The inverse reference is isometric, so (12) implies
  ||alpha(P_A,i,a)-P_A,i,a||<=delta_A,i,a,
  ||alpha(P_B,i,b)-P_B,i,b||<=delta_B,i,b,
with the square-root values in (12). For the FULL-cell rank-one projector
P_i,ab=P_A,i,a P_B,i,b, multiplicativity gives
  alpha(P_i,ab)=alpha(P_A,i,a) alpha(P_B,i,b),
  ||alpha(P_i,ab)-P_i,ab||<=delta_A,i,a+delta_B,i,b<=delta_0.       (13)
Both factors and their images have norm 1. This is an algebraic inference,
not a claim that the output state or dynamics factorizes.

Apply Sections 3--4 (the OPERATOR core) of FINITE_ERROR_CLASSICAL_INDEX.txt
to alpha at full-cell dimension D and radius r, using (13) on T. That proof
does not need a data channel: its matrix-unit/transpose/group-intertwiner
steps assume only a QCA and these projector bounds. It gives index(alpha)=1.
Multiplicativity then gives index(Omega)=index(sigma)index(alpha)=d_A. QED.
In particular, the p^m factor is paid once in (12); it is not paid a second
time by pretending to prepare and measure alpha physically.

The frozen core imports GNVW's transpose-index identity and Ranard--Walter--
Witteveen Proposition 4.10's 1/192 local-norm index stability. Its proof
constructs a mathematical transpose-paired QCA close to identity, rather
than certifying the full channel from the classical records. Those proof
objects require no physical transpose, inverse shift, or Weyl-group actuator.

5. What the certificate deliberately does not certify
------------------------------------------------------
Exact classical translation records do not imply a coherent A shift.
For qubits let C_Z be the onsite controlled-Z circuit on A_i B_i and set
  Omega=sigma composed with Ad_(C_Z).
C_Z fixes every component basis projector, so (3) is matched exactly. Yet,
with B in any full-rank diagonal qubit state, the reduced A channel has its
shifted off-diagonal entries multiplied by Tr(Z tau_B), of modulus strictly
less than 1. It is a dephasing channel, not a unitary shift. The full joint
Omega is unitary and has index d_A, as (7) correctly states.

This is a sufficient translation-like signature, not universal index
tomography: a QCA of index d_A can scramble these particular labels and fail
the test. Unknown/nonunitary channels, hidden baths, erroneous label copies,
uncertain radius, soft Lieb--Robinson tails and arbitrary finite-ring index
claims remain outside the theorem. The trusted structural premise is now
the FULL exact joint QCA, not an independently certified unitary A channel.
No auxiliary marginal-return measurement is required for (7).

If Omega and the chosen basis are exactly one-cell translation covariant,
one cell's two component tests supply all bounds by symmetry. That symmetry
is an extra premise. For arbitrary nonuniform QCAs the finite band (5) is
required; isolated/even-site sparse tests remain vulnerable to the frozen
radius-2 bypass examples. No symmetry is inferred from records at one cell.

6. Resources and the very small sufficient error scale
-------------------------------------------------------
The source-interface gain is removal of the exact-A-channel/coherence test;
the remaining measurements are classical input-label copies and output basis
readout on both rails. Separate-position experiments can reuse one reference
at a time. Parallel tests can share repetitions by paying additional record
capital. Known population floors, preparation/readout calibration, strict
unitary-QCA structure, reset or correlated uncomputation, controls and data
acquisition remain paid premises. No hardware experiment is acquired here.

One arithmetic audit illustrates the current proof's conservative scale:
  d_A=d_B=2, R=1, a_*=b_*=1/2,
  D=4, r=2, C=201, delta_0=1/2476320.
Both joint-error allocations in (8) equal
  1/1569833150054400 = approximately 6.37e-16.
There are 12 output cells, 14 input-floor cells, and 24 component-site rates.

For perspective ONLY, under trusted independent repeat preparations, an
elementary zero-mismatch confidence construction for K component-site rates
and family error eta would require n repetitions per rate satisfying
  n >= log(K/eta)/[-log(1-e_min)],                                (14)
where e_min is the smallest allocated TOTAL-mismatch threshold. This follows
from P(no mismatch | e>=e_min)<=(1-e_min)^n and a union bound. In the displayed
case, K=24, eta=.05 gives about 9.7e15 repetitions for each rate. Parallel
readout may share repetitions, with extra records; (14) is neither a lower
bound nor an optimal protocol. It shows that the present sufficient constant
is not a practical sampling guarantee. Non-independent drift/calibration is
not repaired by this binomial calculation.

The mathematical statement closes a factorization gate in the source
interface. It does not establish affordable measurement, a new chiral gate,
or a novelty/practical-advantage claim. No laboratory action is authorized.
