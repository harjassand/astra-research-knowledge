1. Actual source and the common output space

Let c=(X_C,Y_C). As in the frozen response-class construction, a new

the accepted posterior at this step. Write X=X_J, Y=Y_J.

Use a finite-tensor original strategy. Alice has a COMPLETE actual

  W_(c,Y):H_B -> H_out
be a Stinespring isometry of that Bob instrument. The common output

records, output quantum system, and a purifying environment. Classical
records can be duplicated to an environment before tracing it out.

The actual joint event is E={theta_c(alpha,gamma)=1}, theta binary.
If Pi_(c,alpha) is the diagonal projection onto its accepted Bob record

These are contractions into the SAME H_out. Their dependence on the
private alpha does not make them Bob-local instructions. The compiler
below constructs one new operator from the whole specified family.

Let rho_(l|c,X) be Bob's actual subnormalized steering density matrix

The state sigma_(B,c) is independent of fresh X,Y. Extra input-independent

Fix c,Y for Sections 2-3. Use ORIGINAL conditional probabilities
  w_X=mu_J(X|Y)=product_(i in J)mu(X_i|Y_i).
Only positive-marginal c,Y are needed in these formulas. At a source-null
c or Y choose an arbitrary complete local instrument, such as always
failing with fixed legal answers, to define one all-input family.
These weights specify and analyze the local compiler offline; they are
not supplied to Bob as a realized X. Set sigma=sigma_(B,c), and define

 r_(c,Y)=sum_(X,l) w_X Tr[V_(alpha,Y) rho_(l|X) V_(alpha,Y)*],

2. Explicit common contraction and its exact error
---------------------------------------------------
Here c,Y are fixed and their subscripts suppressed. Let sigma^+ be the
Moore-Penrose inverse on P_B=supp(sigma), zero on its complement. All
rho_l in (F2) are supported in P_B; hence K=K P_B. Define
  A0=K sigma^+, A0=U |A0|,
  A=U min(|A0|,I).                                       (F4)
The minimum is spectral functional calculus of ONE positive matrix.
A0 and its positive singular-value tail need not be bounded uniformly
over a sequence of sources, but the ACTUAL repaired A satisfies ||A||<=1.
Its output is H_out and its input is the original Bob memory. It depends
on c,Y and full calibrated source data, never on the realized X or l.

Bob applies the complete local success/failure instrument
  success: A,
  failure: J_default sqrt(I-A*A),                         (F5)
where J_default embeds into a new orthogonal private output sector with
fixed legal original answer labels. The success branch retains the
original fine-output space H_out; classical records are read in the same
basis and unused environments may be discarded. This is an actual CP
instrument. It does not give Bob alpha or run different maps on guessed
rows. Alice's ORIGINAL complete fine instrument stays unchanged.

The exact squared fine-vector error of the success branch at c,Y is
  d_(c,Y)=sum_(X,l)w_X ||(A-V_(alpha,Y))sqrt(rho_(l|X))||_HS^2.
                                                               (F6)
It has the computable expression

 d = r-Tr[K sigma^+ K*]
              +Tr[sigma (|K sigma^+|-I)_+^2].             (F7)

The first term detects incompatible linear relations among the actual
input programs. The second detects the noncontractive part of the
least-squares action. Both are nonnegative. Singular-value clipping in
(F4) is EXPLICIT and sufficient; no claim of weighted optimality is made.

Here is a full proof, including singular supports. Let D be the finite
direct sum of one H_B copy for each positive-weight (X,l), and define
horizontal operators from D by their columns
  L_(X,l)=sqrt(w_X)sqrt(rho_(l|X)),
  R_(X,l)=V_(alpha,Y) L_(X,l).                             (F8)
Then
  LL*=sigma, RL*=K, ||R||_HS^2=r.
For the pseudoinverse L^+=L* sigma^+, put P=L^+L. This is the projection
onto ran(L*) in D, and A0=R L^+, A0 L=R P. Consequently
  AL-R=(A-A0)L-R(I-P).                                    (F9)
The two right-side summands have orthogonal DOMAIN supports P and I-P,
so their Hilbert-Schmidt cross term is zero. The polar support U*U
contains the support of (|A0|-I)_+, and thus

 ||AL-R||_HS^2
   =||R(I-P)||_HS^2+Tr[sigma(|A0|-I)_+^2],
 ||R(I-P)||_HS^2=r-Tr[K sigma^+ K*].                       (F10)

These prove (F7); (F6) is exactly the column norm. Also 0<=d<=r, since
  Tr[sigma(|A0|-I)_+^2]<=Tr[sigma |A0|^2]
                         =Tr[K sigma^+ K*].              (F11)
All identities remain valid for singular sigma without an inverse on its
kernel. On a zero-r branch K=0, A=0, d=0 and success never occurs.

For an ACTUAL row purification omega_l, possibly including Alice's
retained quantum output and any external reference,
  ||(I tensor (A-V_alpha))omega_l||^2
    =Tr[(A-V_alpha)* (A-V_alpha)rho_l].                    (F12)
Therefore (F6) is the FULL actual fine-vector error, not just an error
between Bob reduced states. The compiler can forget the explicit block
matrix L,R after computing K,sigma,r; it does not need a block label at
runtime. Source description/acquisition costs remain below.

3. Exact compatibility is Gram order, with an actual filter
------------------------------------------------------------
There exists a common contraction B:H_B->H_out with
  B L=R                                                     (F13)
if and only if
  R*R<=L*L on D.                                           (F14)
Necessity follows by multiplying B*B<=I. Conversely (F14) implies
||R z||<=||L z|| for all z, so define B(Lz)=Rz on ran(L).
This is well defined, linear and contractive; extend it by zero on
ran(L)'s orthogonal complement. It is exactly B=R L^+=A0.
Thus (F14) makes the compiler's clipping inactive and its relation
residual zero. Conversely d=0 gives AL=R, so (F14) follows.

Because w_X>0 on the retained columns, BL_l=R_l means
  B sqrt(rho_(l|X))=V_alpha sqrt(rho_(l|X)).                (F15)
It therefore acts correctly on the ENTIRE actual support of that row,
including any actual entangled reference/Alice workspace. Completing B
as in (F5) reproduces the ORIGINAL joint event and its entire accepted

vary: they do not need to be a common scalar or a normalized isometry.

This is a constructive compatibility test and operator, not the assertion

5. A strict nonorthogonal escape from exact decoding
------------------------------------------------------
This example checks that (F14) is more than a way to recover a hidden
classical row. On Bob's qutrit use pure programs
  psi0=(3,3sqrt(3),8)/10, psi1=(8,3sqrt(3),3)/10.
Their norms are one and their overlap is 3/4. The equal-prior ensemble
is prepared by a COMPLETE Alice POVM on a purification of its mean;
the mean has support dimension two. A support-complement default has
probability zero, so there is no extra Alice herald.

The original Bob instrument is the complete gamma=0,1,2 computational
basis projective measurement, retaining gamma and its postmeasurement
state. Row 0 accepts gamma in {0,1}; row 1 accepts gamma in {1,2}.
Both original row success probabilities are s=9/25. The normalized
accepted program vectors are
  v0=(1,sqrt(3),0)/2, v1=(0,sqrt(3),1)/2,
again with overlap 3/4. The real orthogonal matrix

 T=(1/35)[[-3, -8sqrt(3), 32],
          [8sqrt(3), 29, 8sqrt(3)],
          [32, -8sqrt(3), -3]]                            (F23)

sends psi0 to v0 and psi1 to v1. Hence Bob's single coin of mass 9/25,
followed by T and the ORIGINAL gamma instrument, reproduces each
original accepted fine Stinespring vector EXACTLY. Its success operator
B=sqrt(s) W_original T satisfies (F13)-(F14). The compiler (F4) therefore
also has Delta=0 on this source support, independently of how it is
completed off that support.

The distinct formal rows are not perfectly decodable from the actual
programs, whose optimal equal-prior binary error is (4-sqrt(7))/8>0.
Nor can Bob perfectly decode the original event verdict AFTER the
original gamma instrument: at gamma=0 and 2 both rows have positive
probability and opposite verdicts, while their retained postmeasurement
Bob states coincide. The common filter changes the ORIGINAL rejected
ensemble and preserves the accepted fine ensemble; it needs neither
decoder. This is a compatibility separation, not a positive-gap or
useful low-L_event/m repeated-game example.

6. Costs, model limits, and the remaining acquisition/selection gate
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The compiler consists of specified finite matrices K,sigma, a supported
inverse, one polar decomposition/singular-value clipping, and an actual
local success/failure dilation. It can be implemented on the original
Bob memory with his fine-output space plus a private default sector.
Alice's original instrument/resource are retained. There is no shared
row label, communication, or new entanglement in this repair step.

K and r may require full conditioned source/instrument descriptions at
every c,Y. Formula (F3) sums over exponentially many fresh Alice strings
and coarse rows in general. It defines a Bob-Y-indexed family OFFLINE;
it is not an efficient acquisition procedure and cannot be inferred from
scalar row pass probabilities or a final classical fine table alone.
Small eigenvalues in sigma^+ can make calculation/calibration sensitive;
boundedness of the clipped implemented A does not give numerical or
finite-sample robustness for the inferred certificate. A claimed useful
application must acquire/justify Delta relative to p, potentially at
rare-event precision, and account for all settings and workspace costs.

Finite tensor locality is essential to the stated compiler: its matrices
and their polar/clipped functions act on Bob's B(H_B). In an arbitrary
commuting representation, an ordinary Hilbert-space least-squares
operator could lie outside Bob's algebra N and fail to commute with
Alice; type-III normal states need not be trace-density matrices. No
unrestricted commuting implementation of (F3)-(F4) is asserted.
CONDITIONALLY, if a commuting source supplies an actual local contraction
B in a finite matrix amplification of N with a source-vector defect
certificate as in (F17), its completion is local and the frozen mirrored
normal-state selector gives (F20)-(F22) with its OWN epsilon_co. That is
a supplied-operator consequence, not a transfer of the finite compiler.

The full actual fine-output comparison is sufficient and may overcharge
workspace irrelevant to the referee payoff. No lower bound on optimal
payoff-only reconstruction follows from a large Delta. In particular the
old coherent-purifier information obstruction cannot be repurposed as
a payoff-repair no-go. Conversely the construction is not justified by
merely forgetting its program relations or assuming a measured cq
entropy budget controls this cross-row operator defect.

The substantive general problem remains: can an ORIGINAL-event budget
and a useful low-L_event/m greedy regime force a coordinate/history with

The present theorem supplies one concrete, programmable compatibility
mechanism and an explicit failure certificate for that mechanism. It
