LOCAL COUNTERFLOW, AUXILIARY RETURN AND CLASSICAL REFERENCE RECORDS
Current scientific note, 2026-10-24. Earlier physical units remain frozen.

Result and status
-----------------
An ordinary two-layer SWAP circuit implements a one-way data translation
while an extensive, homogeneous auxiliary rail returns exactly as a state.
Its hidden opposite translation is invisible to arbitrary stroboscopic
data-only process records, but a local classical correlation tag detects it.
Two exact obstructions clarify what stronger return contracts require:
 (i) a full-rank product auxiliary with simple GLOBAL spectrum cannot supply
     the compensating chiral index under uniform local-circuit assumptions;
 (ii) complete return of the local classical label records also forbids that
      compensation, including for homogeneous, degenerate Gibbs products.
The index inputs are established GNVW results. These are elementary scoped
operational consequences, not a priority claim, new QCA or new Floquet phase.
The full extensive conveyor/return/probe experiment has not been acquired.

1. Constructed operation and actual capital
-------------------------------------------
On a periodic N-cell ring, i mod N, put a data qudit A_i and auxiliary B_i of
equal dimension d>1 in every cell. Apply two disjoint local matching layers:
  L1 = product_i SWAP(A_i,B_i),
  L2 = product_i SWAP(B_i,A_{i+1}).
Tracking input registers gives
  W=L2 L1 = S_A(right) tensor S_B(left).
The inverse auxiliary shift is an essential part of the full operation.
There is one equal qudit of auxiliary capital per cell, dimension d^N in
finite volume. This is not a fixed-total-size catalyst as N tends to infinity.
The periodic wraparound link is part of the geometry; open-chain endpoints
need additional input/output handling and are not covered by this contract.

For any shift-invariant tau_B and arbitrary rho_AR independent of B,
  (W tensor I_R)(rho_AR tensor tau_B)(W^dagger tensor I_R)
    = (S_A tensor I_R) rho_AR (S_A^dagger tensor I_R) tensor tau_B.       (1)
An identical-site product Gibbs state is one example; nontraciality is no
obstacle. More generally, a translation-invariant interacting Gibbs state
may be shift-invariant too. State return alone does not mean operator return.

All adaptive instruments on A and external references BETWEEN completed
periods have exactly the same record law under W and S_A tensor I_B.
Induction applies (1) after each conditional instrument outcome. Arbitrary
tomography of the untagged B state also sees no difference. This is a pairwise
indistinguishability statement for that preparation/intervention contract,
not a claim about micromotion, changed B preparations, or all topological
observables. Even noninvariant B is invisible to A alone because W factorizes.

For identical noninteracting onsite Hamiltonians h, each ideal bond pulse
  H(t)=h tensor I+I tensor h+g(t) SWAP,  g(t0)=g(tf)=0
commutes with SWAP. In the interaction picture, integral g dt=pi*hbar/2
gives -i SWAP. The laboratory gate also contains known free onsite evolution.
The ideal interaction work is
  integral dot(g)<SWAP> dt = <SWAP>[g(tf)-g(t0)] = 0.                  (2)
The onsite energy is unchanged by the complete homogeneous permutation.
With |g|<=gmax, each layer requires at least pi*hbar/(2gmax) pulse time.
This assumes the ideal commuting Hamiltonian; clock, pulse generation,
control-line loss, calibration, refrigeration, fabrication and gate errors
are neither zero nor acquired by (2). The device is externally clocked.
No work claim is made for arbitrary interacting or disordered onsite hosts.

2. Why a full-rank auxiliary makes the hidden channel independent
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Lemma. Let tau_B>0 and W_AB be unitary in finite dimensions. If
  Tr_B W(rho_A tensor tau_B)W^dagger = U_A rho_A U_A^dagger
for every rho_A, then W=U_A tensor V_B with V_B unitary.

Proof. Diagonalize tau_B=sum_j p_j|j><j| with every p_j>0. The channel Kraus
operators are sqrt(p_j)<i|W|j>. A unitary channel has rank-one Choi matrix,
so every such Kraus operator is a scalar multiple of U_A. Division by
sqrt(p_j) gives W=U_A tensor V_B; unitarity gives unitarity of V_B. QED.

Consequently exact auxiliary marginal return is just [V_B,tau_B]=0 and the
output is automatically product with arbitrary data/reference input. Full
return of a full-Schmidt-rank purification of B would instead force
V_B=e^(i theta)I_B. Full rank is essential: unused auxiliary subspaces are
unconstrained without it. Approximate versions require additional bounds.

3. A local classical tag: smaller interface than purification
-------------------------------------------------------------
Let tau=sum_j p_j|j><j| and tau_B=tau^tensorN, N>=2. Replace the preparation
at B_0 by a CLASSICAL copied label with an external record C:
  omega_BC = sum_j p_j |j,j><j,j|_(B0,C) tensor tau_(other B sites).
Tracing C still gives EXACTLY the same full B marginal tau^tensorN.
Measure equality of the basis labels at the ORIGINAL B_0 and C. Then
  acceptance under I_B = 1,
  acceptance under S_B(left) = sum_j p_j^2.                            (3)
The tag instead reappears intact at B_{-1}C after the conveyor. A maximally
mixed qubit has acceptance 1 versus 1/2. The gap tends to zero near a pure
tau, so there is no state-independent gap for arbitrary thermal states.
This readout needs B/reference access but no entangled projector or quantum
coherence between energy eigenstates. It detects this identity-versus-shift
pair, not an arbitrary hidden channel; diagonal phases pass it.

An energy-basis controlled copy into a pure, degenerate blank C can be an
energy-preserving permutation with zero ideal system work. It leaves the
joint entropy H(p) unchanged while using a blank record and creating mutual
information H(p). Conditional inverse copying with the correlated site
uncomputes the record at zero ideal work. If that side information is lost,
reset of the isolated degenerate record exports entropy H(p), with ideal
Landauer heat at least k_B T H(p). There is no fixed positive reset bill when
useful correlated side information remains accessible. Readout/control and
record storage still require an actual implementation.

An alternative ENTANGLED tag |psi>=sum_j sqrt(p_j)|j,j> has projector
acceptance 1 versus sum_j p_j^3 at the original site (1 versus 1/4 for a
maximally mixed qubit). It is a stronger/different resource. Its preparation
and possibly energy-coherent joint readout are paid; neither single tag is
a universal channel certificate. Both exact statistics are replayed in the
existing luna_one_probe/reference_probe_check.py.

4. Two exact local-circuit obstructions
----------------------------------------
Use an infinite-chain QCA, or a family of larger rings whose locality/depth
bounds and local bases are uniform and have a consistent QCA limit. Choose
index convention ind(S_A)=d and ind(S_B(left))=1/d. GNVW Theorem 9 gives
multiplicativity and index 1 for finite-depth local circuits. Proposition 10
says QCAs preserving a product diagonal algebra and agreeing there have
equal index; in particular, a QCA fixing that algebra pointwise has index 1.
These are properties of the full operator action, not of one chosen state.

4a. Generic PRODUCT auxiliary, exact state return.
Assume tau_B,N is a full-rank PRODUCT state with simple global spectrum in
its ONSITE PRODUCT eigenbasis, and an exact data shift is implemented and
tau_B,N returned for every input. The lemma gives W_N=S_A tensor V_B,N.
Return plus simple global spectrum makes V_B,N diagonal in that local product
basis. Its local limit fixes the diagonal algebra pointwise, so ind(V_B)=1.
But ind(W)=1 and ind(S_A)=d require ind(V_B)=1/d, a contradiction.

Product-local eigenbasis is essential: SIMPLE GLOBAL SPECTRUM ALONE does not
suffice. An interacting translation-invariant Hamiltonian can have simple
global spectrum and a shift-invariant Gibbs state. Its global eigenbasis
need not be local. Likewise this argument does not assign a nontrivial index
to a single finite ring, or cover arbitrary quasilocal Hamiltonian tails.
Homogeneous Gibbs products have permutation degeneracies and allow (1).

4b. Complete LOCAL CLASSICAL RECORD return, without spectral simplicity.
Assume tau_B=product_i tau_i>0 in chosen onsite bases and the exact unitary
data-channel premise of the lemma. In separate tests at every site i, copy
the local basis label j to a reference C_i and require original-site label
equality with probability ONE. The conditional B input for label j has full
support on P_ij H_B, where P_ij is that onsite basis projector. A zero average
mismatch, with every label weight positive, therefore forces
  (I-P_ij)V_B P_ij=0 for every i,j.
Unitarity gives [V_B,P_ij]=0. Thus V_B fixes the entire local product diagonal
algebra pointwise, has index 1 by Proposition 10, and cannot cancel ind(S_A).
Equivalently, require exact return of a simultaneous complete classical
record. Separate site tests suffice under the same fixed channel/preparation.

This condition is weaker than full purification return: diagonal (including
controlled) phases may remain, so it does not force V_B=I. Complete site and
label coverage, exact pass probabilities, full support, locality and the
unitary-data-channel premise are ASSUMPTIONS of the certificate. They have
not been universally acquired in an experiment. Without the data-channel
premise, controlled data operations can preserve every auxiliary label while
producing a nonunitary mixture on A. A single chosen tag does not provide the
complete coverage; translation symmetry cannot be inferred from that tag.

5. Exactness has a sharp practical boundary
--------------------------------------------
On any fixed finite ring, generic simple-spectrum product tau_B can be made
arbitrarily close to a homogeneous product tau_0. With trace distance D,
  D(S_B tau_B S_B^dagger,tau_B) <= 2 D(tau_B,tau_0).                    (4)
The same ordinary conveyor therefore returns such a generic product state
with arbitrarily small error and keeps the A-only channel exactly unchanged.
No gap-independent approximate-return obstruction follows from 4a. Likewise
average classical-label pass probabilities can miss large rare-sector
changes when input weights are tiny; 4b is not a finite-data error theorem.
The fixed pair statistic (3) has a finite contrast when tau is not too pure.

6. Acquired physical primitive and strongest established comparison
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Kandel et al., Nature 573 (2019), DOI 10.1038/s41586-019-1566-8, experimentally
transfer both single-spin states and one part of an entangled pair in a
four-dot GaAs array by barrier-gate-controlled Heisenberg exchange. Their
Hamiltonian hJ(XX+YY+ZZ)/4 implements SWAP at time 1/(2J); local magnetic
gradients cause errors. This acquires an actual local SWAP/transfer primitive,
not the periodic two-rail network, thermal return, or complete record assay.
Source: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1566-8
Author primary PDF: https://manfragroup.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Coherent-spin-state-transfer-via-Heisenberg-exchange.pdf

The direct full-cycle incumbent is an ordinary balanced SWAP shift register;
it has the same auxiliary capital, gates and clock as our constructed W.
GNVW already classifies this balanced flow as index 1, and its Proposition 10
supplies the exact diagonal-algebra input for 4a/4b. We claim no improved
communication gate, new chiral unitary, or practical advantage over it.
Primary: Gross, Nesme, Vogts and Werner, arXiv:0910.3675v2, Theorem 9 and
Proposition 10, https://arxiv.org/html/0910.3675v2 .

boundary-only assay, and our bosonic-qudit conveyor is not claimed to
reproduce it. The proposed contribution is the precise return-contract and

Their linked Zenodo files were restricted/request-only when checked; no data
