## Foundational passive-lag constructions

### Common finite-tensor filter

The [common-filter source](source/UCOMMON/next_unit/foundational/passive_lag/PROGRAM_FRAME_JOINT_EVENT_REPAIR.txt) constructs one contraction on Bob's original memory and output space from the complete original finite-tensor source. It uses the original-prior question weights and actual steering states, forms the program Gram data, applies a supported pseudoinverse and one spectral clipping, then completes the contraction to an actual Bob-local success/failure instrument. The filter does not receive Alice's realized fresh input, private row or event verdict, and Alice's complete instrument remains unchanged.

The source gives an exact weighted fine-vector error: a relation-residual term plus the noncontractive singular-value tail. Exact repair is equivalent to the Gram-order condition `R*R ≤ L*L`, which is precisely the compatibility condition for a common contraction on the supplied finite-tensor source. Its explicit nonorthogonal qutrit example has program overlap `3/4` and positive optimal decoding error while the one common filter reproduces the accepted fine instrument exactly. That is a compatibility example, not a general repeated-game theorem.

The resulting payoff transfer depends on the actual source-weighted defect `Delta` relative to the original event probability `p`. Low `ln(1/p)/m`, small referee drift or a measured cq budget alone does not provide this certificate or a useful coordinate. The complete calibrated source and instrument tables can be exponentially large; rare-event precision and small-eigenvalue sensitivity remain costs. The full fine-output comparison can overcharge information irrelevant to payoff, so a large defect is not an impossibility result for weaker payoff-only reconstruction. The matrix pseudoinverse construction is finite-tensor: an ordinary matrix polar factor does not establish membership in an arbitrary commuting algebra. A commuting-model consequence requires a separately supplied local contraction and defect certificate.

### Recoverable response classes

The [response-class source](source/UCOMMON/next_unit/foundational/passive_lag/RECOVERABLE_RESPONSE_CLASS_LOCALIZATION.txt) gives a separate operational route. A finite response class must reproduce the actual checked predicate on the support of the complete preselection output law, and Bob must have a complete decoder POVM on his actual pre-instrument system. Its error is averaged under the original prior before either event. A Bob-local gentle decoder is inserted before Bob's original complete fine instrument; posterior sampling or event-conditioned decoder error does not satisfy the premise.

Under the stated reverse-channel minorisation `delta > 0`, with `m` live coordinates, the source transfers the localized event to a legal strategy in the same model. Its bound retains the actual event masses `p` and `p'` and preselection decoder error `e`:

`epsilon ≤ [sqrt(p a/p') + sqrt(2e/p') + sqrt(ln(1/p')/(delta m))]^2`.

When `t = sqrt(2e/p) < 1`, it gives `p' ≥ p(1−t)^2` and the equivalent source bound with `a`, `t` and `p`. The [localization audit](source/U0558/next_unit/foundational/luna_mark_correlation/RECOVERABLE_RESPONSE_CLASS_LOCALIZATION_AUDIT.md), [private-row obstruction](source/U0558/next_unit/foundational/passive_lag/PRIVATE_ROW_POLAR_GLUING_OBSTRUCTION.txt), [row-localization check](source/U0558/next_unit/foundational/luna_truncation/COHERENT_ROW_GUESS_LOCALIZATION_CHECK.txt), and [decoder/instrument source](source/U0558/next_unit/foundational/lag_executables/GENTLE_DECODER_THEN_ORIGINAL_COMPLEX_INSTRUMENT.txt) retain its local and failure boundaries.

This criterion does not prove that a useful decoder is efficiently or uniformly acquirable. When `p` is rare, controlling `e/p` or predicate-mismatch mass can require precision on the order of `p`; a decoder family can also require exponentially much source description. The commuting claim is separately stated in the source using local effects and normal-state selector foundations. The gentle-measurement prior, finite checks and standard-form dependencies are pinned in the manifest; none was replayed here. ## Finite-error classical signature for a full joint QCA

The [parent finite-error theorem](source/U0558/next_unit/chiral_quantum_A/finite_error/FINITE_ERROR_CLASSICAL_INDEX.txt) assumes an already established strict-radius QCA automorphism and a full-rank product auxiliary preparation with a known local density floor. A finite contiguous band of same-site classical label tests, at its sufficient mismatch threshold, forces the hidden auxiliary automorphism to have index one. The parent retains its exact data-channel/factorization premise; isolated or parity-only site coverage has the recorded radius-two counterexamples.

The [companion theorem](source/U0558/next_unit/chiral_quantum_A/finite_error/companion/TRANSLATION_SIGNATURE_WITHOUT_FACTORIZATION.txt) changes the observable interface. It removes the exact A-channel/factorization premise by applying the parent operator-core argument to a mathematical relative QCA built from the exact **joint** two-rail QCA and a partial-shift reference. It still assumes a trusted exact joint QCA, known strict radius, full-rank two-rail product preparation and finite contiguous coverage. Its result certifies the stated index of the full joint QCA from classical label records; it does not certify coherent A-channel transfer. The source's controlled-Z example demonstrates that exact classical records can coexist with dephasing of the reduced A channel.

The sufficient thresholds are very small. In the stated illustration `d_A = d_B = 2`, `R = 1`, `a_* = b_* = 1/2`, the companion uses `delta_0 = 1/2,476,320`; its sufficient joint-mismatch allocations are about `6.37×10⁻¹⁶`. Under the additional idealized assumption of independent repeated preparations, its elementary zero-mismatch confidence illustration gives about `9.7×10¹⁵` repetitions per rate. That is neither an optimal protocol nor a lower bound, and no finite-shot or hardware validation is established. The [joint-rail audit](source/U0558/next_unit/chiral_quantum_A/finite_error/companion/luna_one_probe/JOINT_COMPANION_AUDIT.md), [parent finite-error audit](source/U0558/next_unit/chiral_quantum_A/finite_error/luna_one_probe/FINITE_ERROR_AND_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md), [local-norm source gate](source/U0558/next_unit/chiral_quantum_A/finite_error/physical_sources/QCA_INDEX_LOCAL_NORM_GATE.md), [frozen state-return context](source/U0537/next_unit/chiral_quantum_A/STATE_RETURN_AND_CHIRAL_FLOW.txt), and [GNVW continuity comparison](source/U0537/next_unit/chiral_quantum_A/finite_error/luna_circuit_sources/GNVW_LOCAL_INDEX_CONTINUITY.md) preserve the named proof and resource boundaries.
