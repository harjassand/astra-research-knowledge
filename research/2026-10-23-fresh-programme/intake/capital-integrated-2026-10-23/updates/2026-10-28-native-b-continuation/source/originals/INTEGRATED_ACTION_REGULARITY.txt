INTEGRATED REGULARITY OF THE ACQUIRED HEAT ACTION AND COVARIANCE WORD
27 October 2026. Original-value interface; no second derivative of g assumed.

RESULT

The chi_3 singularity is integrable at the level of squared first derivatives.
There is a useful dimension-free mean-square Lipschitz interface in the INPUT
DIRECTION, stable under fresh action composition. The full Gaussian source
Dirichlet energy, however, has a sharp quadratic dependence on dimension.
Uniform higher derivative moments fail, including fourth moments in dimension
one. Detailed lower bounds are in SOBOLEV_CLOSURE_OBSTRUCTIONS.txt.

A separate positive receipt survives at the level of laws: after an independent
Gaussian reserve, the action and its two-action word are dimension-free
Lipschitz kernels in their available center. This uses Gaussian integration
by parts in the perpendicular seeds and does not assert an intrinsic-law
Poincare inequality or recursive source-class closure.

1. NOTATION AND WEAK DERIVATIVES

Let g:R^d->R^d be globally L-Lipschitz. Conservativity is needed for the
covariance interpretation of the word, but not for the derivative bounds.
Fix s>0 and c. For input v!=0 write
 r=||v||, u=v/r, P=I-u u^T.
Let z~N(0,I_d), a~N(0,I_3), q=||a||, and
 x_+=c+sPz+sq u, x_-=c+sPz-sq u,
 J=J_s(c;v)=r[g(x_+)-g(x_-)]/(2sq).
At v=0 return zero. Put B_+=Dg(x_+), B_-=Dg(x_-),
Delta B=B_+-B_-, Bbar=(B_++B_-)/2, and b=J/r.

All following derivatives are weak derivatives and hold almost everywhere.
The Gaussian endpoint laws are absolutely continuous, so the a.e. derivative
Dg is sufficient. No D^2g occurs. The zero-radius event has probability zero;
the displayed integrable estimates justify Gaussian Sobolev membership.
The homogeneous map v->J is continuous at zero and is Lipschitz for each
fixed seed realization with q>0. Weak chain rules handle inner zero outputs;
the derivative of a Sobolev map vanishes a.e. on its zero level set.

For h in R^d, direct differentiation gives

 D_cJ h = r Delta B h/(2sq),                                  (1)
 D_zJ h = r Delta B P h/(2q),                                 (2)
 partial_q J = (r Bbar u-J)/q,                               (3)
 D_vJ h = b(u dot h)+Bbar P h
          -Delta B[(u dot z)P h+u(z dot P h)]/(2q).           (4)

Since ||B_+||,||B_-||<=L, ||b||<=L. For chi_3,
E q^(-2)=1 and E q^(-1)=sqrt(2/pi). Its direction a/q is uniform on the
two-sphere and independent of q. These facts supply the exact integrability
threshold behind the bounds below.

2. SECOND-MOMENT RECEIPTS FOR A SINGLE ACTION

Condition on arbitrary acquired c,v,h before drawing the fresh action seeds.
Equations (1)-(3) imply

 E||D_cJ h||^2 <= L^2 r^2 ||h||^2/s^2,
 E||D_zJ h||^2 <= L^2 r^2 ||P h||^2,
 E||D_aJ||_HS^2 <=4L^2 r^2.                                 (5)

For (4), split the four displayed vector terms and use the sum-of-four-squares
bound. Conditional on v, u dot z is standard normal and z dot P h has variance
||P h||^2. Independence of q yields

 E||D_vJ h||^2 <=4L^2[(u dot h)^2+3||P h||^2]
              <=12L^2||h||^2.                               (6)

The constant is uniform in d,s,c,v. In particular, using the SAME seeds at
two inputs and integrating (4) along their line segment gives the genuine
coupling receipt

 E||J_s(c;v)-J_s(c;w)||^2 <=12L^2||v-w||^2.                   (7)

This is a mean-square Lipschitz random-map property, not a claim that each
realization has Lipschitz constant sqrt(12)L.

Now let the input V also be a fresh standard d-Gaussian. Summing (5)-(6)
over input coordinate directions gives

 E||D_VJ||_HS^2 <=12L^2d,
 E||D_zJ||_HS^2 <=L^2d(d-1),
 E||D_aJ||_HS^2 <=4L^2d,
 E||D_cJ||_HS^2 <=L^2d^2/s^2.                               (8)

The perpendicular calculation uses E[||V||^2 P]= (d-1)I.
Thus at a fixed center the full Gaussian Dirichlet energy is at most
L^2(d^2+15d). If c=c0+sqrt(t)W with W an additional independent Gaussian,
the center block adds tL^2d^2/s^2. At s^2=1-t this yields

 E||D_(all Gaussian seeds) J||_HS^2
 <=L^2[d^2/(1-t)+15d].                                      (9)

This is finite at every fixed t<1, but it is not the O(L^2d) source energy
that a globally L-Lipschitz Gaussian image would have.

3. FRESH TWO-ACTION WORD

Let Q=J_1(c;v) and A(c;v)=J_2(c;Q), with independent action seeds and the
same fixed c,s. Conditional on the entire inner computation, (6) applies
to any acquired perturbation of Q. Consequently

 E||D_vA h||^2 <=144L^4||h||^2,
 E||A(c;v)-A(c;w)||^2 <=144L^4||v-w||^2.                     (10)

For the center derivative, split the direct outer contribution and the
propagated inner contribution. Their L2 norms are at most L^2r||h||/s and
sqrt(12)L^2r||h||/s. Since (1+sqrt(12))^2<20,

 E||D_cA h||^2 <=20L^4r^2||h||^2/s^2.                        (11)

For fresh Gaussian V, the separate source blocks satisfy

 input V:                 E||D_V A||_HS^2 <=144L^4d;
 inner perpendicular z1: E||D_z1 A||_HS^2 <=12L^4d(d-1);
 inner radial a1:        E||D_a1 A||_HS^2 <=48L^4d;
 outer perpendicular z2: E||D_z2 A||_HS^2 <=L^4d(d-1);
 outer radial a2:        E||D_a2 A||_HS^2 <=4L^4d.

Hence the fixed-center total is at most L^4(13d^2+183d). An independent
Gaussian center c0+sqrt(t)W adds at most 20tL^4d^2/s^2. All constants are
explicit; none assumes a modulus of continuity for Dg or an evaluated
derivative oracle. Further fresh words preserve the input-direction estimate
by multiplication of the corresponding mean-square constants. New center
and seed blocks must still be charged separately.

4. A DIMENSION-FREE CENTER STABILITY BOUND FOR SMOOTHED LAWS

Let V,N be independent standard d-Gaussians, with V independent of the
available center and all action seeds. Let nu>0 be an independent Gaussian
reserve scale and write c1=sqrt(2/pi). For any two fixed available centers,

 TV(Law(J_s(c;V)+nu N), Law(J_s(c';V)+nu N))
 <=[c1+c1^3 L/nu] ||c-c'||/(2s).                             (12)

For the fresh two-action word,

 TV(Law(A(c;V)+nu N), Law(A(c';V)+nu N))
 <=[2c1+(1+sqrt(12))c1^3 L^2/nu] ||c-c'||/(2s).               (13)

Both estimates may be capped at one. No fitted law or conditional oracle is
used: these are stability theorems for the already specified legal programs.

PROOF OF (12). For a measurable test f with ||f||infinity<=1, smooth it with
the reserve: phi(y)=E f(y+nu N). Then ||phi||infinity<=1 and
||grad phi||infinity<=c1/nu. At fixed input direction u, split a center
perturbation h as P h+u(u dot h). By (1)-(2), the perpendicular contribution
to the derivative of E phi(J) is

 s^(-1) E[phi(J)(z dot P h)],

after Gaussian integration by parts. Its absolute value is at most
c1||h||/s, with no derivative of g remaining. The parallel derivative obeys

 ||D_cJ[u(u dot h)]|| <= L|V dot h|/(sq).

Therefore its smoothed test contribution is at most
(c1/nu)(L/s) E|V dot h| E q^(-1)
=c1^3 L||h||/(s nu). Integrate along the center segment and divide by two
for TV. Weak differentiation and the L2 bounds above justify the operations.

PROOF OF (13). There are two center contributions. Split the inner one
relative to u=V/||V||, and the direct outer one relative to w=Q/||Q||.
The two perpendicular pieces integrate by parts in z1 and z2 respectively,
each costing at most c1||h||/s. The direction in the z2 integration is
independent of its fresh seeds. The inner perpendicular identity includes
the full dependence of the outer query direction on Q through the chain rule.

For the inner parallel piece, conditional on its acquired perturbation of Q,
(6) bounds the propagated outer derivative in L1 by sqrt(12)L times its
norm. Its cost is at most sqrt(12)c1^3 L^2||h||/(s nu).
For the direct outer parallel piece, the S4 fixed-center convex-order receipt
gives E|Q dot h|<=L c1||h||. Independence of the outer q and the same reserve
gradient bound give c1^3 L^2||h||/(s nu). Sum and divide by two.

The same proof works conditionally on any prior history that fixes c,c' and
leaves V and the action seeds fresh. If the input or center perturbation
is selected using those same fresh seeds, the isotropic projection estimates
used above need not hold. That dependence is part of the interface contract.

4A. RETAINED INPUT AND ACQUIRED BROWNIAN ANCHORS

Root continuation. The same argument controls an actually reusable JOINT
kernel. Let tau,alpha be arbitrary real coefficients and define

 K_c=Law(V, tau V+alpha A(c;V)+nu N).

For the two-action word at fixed s,

 TV(K_c,K_c')
 <=[2c1+(1+sqrt(12))c1^3 |alpha| L^2/nu]
       ||c-c'||/(2s).                                      (14)

To prove this use an arbitrary bounded test f(v,y), smooth only its y
coordinate with nu N, and repeat the z1,z2 integrations by parts above.
The retained V and baseline tau V are constant in those integrations.
Only the two parallel derivative bounds acquire the factor |alpha|.
All estimates hold for arbitrary such joint tests, not merely output-only
tests. The constant is independent of tau and d. It is deliberately loose
at alpha=0, where the laws in fact agree exactly.

For a translated source g(x+G), the covariance action uses acquired anchors
T uniform(0,1), W standard Gaussian, c=x+sqrt(T)W, s=sqrt(1-T).
Retain (T,W,V), use fresh internal secant seeds, and let K_x be the law of

 (T,W,V, tau V+alpha A_(g,x)(V)+nu N).

Applying (14) conditional on T,W and using integral_0^1(1-t)^(-1/2)dt=2
gives the dimension-free bound

 TV(K_x,K_x')
 <=[2c1+(1+sqrt(12))c1^3 |alpha| L^2/nu] ||x-x'||.           (15)

This costs the same at-most-four original g queries, O(d) real arithmetic
and storage as the actual covariance action. Its independent nu reserve is
explicit and must be charged wherever the kernel is used. Independent prior
state may also be retained, and the argument then applied conditionally.

The internal perpendicular/radial action seeds and their query endpoints
are NOT included in the retained state for (14)-(15). Exposing them to a
later test would invalidate the particular integration-by-parts argument.
Likewise, (14)-(15) are stability between implemented kernels at different
available centers, not a claim that their output is conditionally Gaussian
given V or that a posterior source law has been acquired. The main theorem's
Gaussian approximation remains a separate full-law statement.

5. SCIENTIFIC SCOPE

The usable positive interface is (7),(10): fresh input errors propagate in
mean square at a dimension-free rate. Equations (12)-(13) show that an
independent reserve can turn Gaussian integration by parts into a stable
center-to-output LAW kernel even when the sampled map has large derivatives.
These receipts may be useful for an augmented-state or conditional-kernel
representation, but do not acquire covariance/conditional operations for an
arbitrary later nonlinear value circuit.

The negative results in the companion file rule out replacing global source
Lipschitzness by a uniformly O(d) Gaussian Dirichlet norm of this same sampled
representation, or by a uniform W^{1,p} bound for p>=3. A weak Poincare
inequality on the output law could conceivably use a different representation
or proof; the source-coordinate failures alone do not decide that question.
The main acquired covariance and fourth-order law theorem is unaffected.
