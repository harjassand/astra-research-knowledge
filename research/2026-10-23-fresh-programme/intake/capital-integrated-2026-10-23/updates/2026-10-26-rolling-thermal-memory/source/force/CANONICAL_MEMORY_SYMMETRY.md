## 1. Literal fixed-resource Hamiltonian

Keep the same Gaussian mechanical support, actual terminal masses and
identical finite-band Rubin leads. Y=v^T q is the relative port coordinate
and the spring form includes k_0Y^2/2, k_0>0. Add a single compact charged
rotor with periodic angle phi, integer N=-i partial_phi, fixed energy
E_R=hbar^2/(2I)>0 and offset n_g=1/2+eta:

    H_eta=H_G+E_R(N-1/2-eta)^2-lambda Y Q,
    Q=2cos phi, ||Q||=2.                              (1)

There is no helical phi-gY coordinate, shrinking pitch or increasing
normal recoil. I, lambda, k_0, the complete masses and the lead band are
fixed. An offset/vector-potential control and a literal Y cos(phi) force
coupling are actual added interfaces. Their physical conjunction is not
acquired by naming a charge qubit. A constant 2lambda^2/k_0 can be added
if a nonnegative interaction-potential convention is desired; it does
not change the states or the following response identities.

Use finite far-pinned chains first. The operator is lower bounded because
the force is bounded and the mechanical spring is positive. The rotor
has its COMPLETE infinite charge ladder. Its compact position does not
turn its kinetic energy into a bounded operator.

## 2. Two exact anticommuting symmetries

Let R_phi f(phi)=f(-phi) and define

    J=exp(i phi)R_phi, T_pi=exp(i pi N).

J is a Hermitian unitary with

    J^2=1, J N J=1-N, J Q J=Q,
    T_pi Q T_pi^dagger=-Q, T_pi J T_pi^dagger=-J.       (2)

At eta=0, [H_0,J]=0 EXACTLY, including the mechanical force coupling
and every lead atom. Let P_m invert all Gaussian mechanical coordinates
and momenta, and C=P_m T_pi. The centered Gaussian Hamiltonian is invariant
under P_m, and Y and Q both change sign under C. Hence

    [H_eta,C]=0 for EVERY eta,
    C J C^dagger=-J.                                 (3)

At eta=0, (2)--(3) pair the entire finite-regulator spectrum, not just its
lowest doublet: choose an energy eigenvector of J; C gives an orthogonal
opposite-J eigenvector at the same energy. The restricted sector partition
functions are identical at every temperature. In the local lead limit
these statements mean equality/relations of the two sector KMS limits;
no infinite product-parity Hilbert-space unitary is asserted.

The detuning operation is explicitly

    H_eta-H_0=-2E_R eta (N-1/2)+E_R eta^2,
    J H_eta J=H_-eta.                                (4)

N-1/2 is purely OFF-SECTOR with respect to J. In particular there is no
eta-independent intersector transition generated by the full microscopic
Hamiltonian at eta=0. This is an actual selection rule, not the statement
that inversion symmetry alone suppresses an unknown tunnelling remainder.
It does not by itself give a uniform relative transition-amplitude bound.
The actuator in (4) is unbounded on the charge ladder; it must be treated
with its actual kinetic moment, not assigned a free Pauli-operator norm.

An undesired fixed cosine term proportional to Q commutes with J but
breaks C and biases the two sectors. An angular sine term generally breaks
J. Such defects are not included in the exact protection premise.

## 3. Exact folding exposes what the doublet approximation discards

Gauge psi(phi)=exp(i phi/2)f(phi). Then f is antiperiodic on[-pi,pi],
the kinetic energy is E_R(-i partial_phi)^2, and J is ordinary reflection.
The plus-J sector folds to [0,pi] with Neumann condition at0 and Dirichlet
condition atpi. The minus-J sector has the opposite boundary conditions.
Reflection phi->pi-phi identifies the latter with the same ND interval,
while sending Q to -Q.

Thus at eta=0 the FULL Hamiltonian is exactly equivalent to

    H_G+E_R(-partial_xi^2)_ND

The sector label is exact, but an infinite normal coordinate xi remains.
Its free ground function sqrt(2/pi)cos(xi/2) has energy E_R/4, its next
energy is9E_R/4, and

    <2cos xi>_ground=1, <(2cos xi)^2>_ground=2.         (6)

In the ND basis cos[(n+1/2)xi], 2cos xi has a diagonal element1 only at

## 4. All mechanical and rotor kinetic resources stay finite

There is also a direct uniform bound on the ROTOR kinetic energy, not just
the mechanical part. Put alpha=k_0/2 and H_G,alpha=H_G-alpha Y^2/2.
The scalar square inequality gives

    H_eta >= H_G,alpha+E_R(N-n_g)^2-C_0,
    C_0=4lambda^2/k_0.                               (7)

Let V_half(T_0) be the full Gaussian port variance with the port spring
halved. It is bounded, for example, by

    V_half(T_0)<=2k_B T_0/k_0
                              +hbar/sqrt(2B_0 k_0).

Gibbs variation with the free Gaussian state times the nearest charge

    E_R <(N-n_g)^2> <=2{C_0+E_R/4+(k_0/4)V_half(T_0)

ratio is bounded by integrating its port-spring derivative; the free

    sum_(n integer) exp[-t(n-n_g)^2]<=1+sqrt(pi/t).

The latter follows by the positive-coefficient Poisson theta formula,

All terms in (8) are finite at fixed physical parameters. The energy is
the actual minimally coupled angular kinetic energy. Canonical N^2 is

Thus the compact internal memory does NOT have the divergent physical

## 5. A nonzero FULL sector dipole at finite coupling

The sectors are not merely abstract parity labels. A finite quantitative
bound gives opposite static mechanical displacements without discarding
the higher rotor states. Work at eta=0 and restrict to plus J. Let Pi_ex
be the free ND excited-state projector (n>=1), and set

    t=beta E_R, K_*=C_0+(k_0/4)V_half(T_0).

Subtract E_R Pi_ex from H_0. Convexity of its log partition function in
that bounded penalty, the free Gaussian-times-normal-ground trial and
the operator bound (7) imply

    <Pi_ex>_+ <=K_*/E_R
        +(1/t)log[1+exp(-t)/(1-exp(-3t))].             (9)

Indeed the modified normal excitation energies are
E_R[n(n+1)-1]>=E_R n^2 for n>=1. Its excitation trace is at most
exp(-t)/(1-exp(-3t)), since n^2>=1+3(n-1). The ground factor E_R/4
cancels EXACTLY between the partition bounds. No unbounded-moment
conclusion is obtained from this population estimate.

For example choose the FIXED energy E_R>=128K_* and
E_R/(k_B T_0)>=10. Then (9)<1/64. On the reduced normal state, its trace
distance from the free ND ground state is at most sqrt(<Pi_ex>_+).

    <Q>_+ >=1-4sqrt(<Pi_ex>_+) >1/2.                 (10)

The exact mechanical equilibrium equation gives

    <Y>_+=lambda C <Q>_+,
    <Y>_-=-<Y>_+.

In the infinite-chain local limit C=1/k_0, so for lambda>0

    <Y>_+>lambda/(2k_0), <Y>_-<-lambda/(2k_0).         (11)

Every constant in this existence choice can be fixed once; E_R need not
