## Conditional observation map

For point scatterers with real weights, uniform illumination and a fixed, frozen, zero-mean Gaussian displacement field independent of initial positions, the source retains the off-diagonal spatial covariance terms in the exact two-time coherent amplitude. The intensity correlation is an exact four-index sum, with repeated indices allowed; its initial-configuration dependence requires four-point statistics, not only `S(q)`. A Siegert reduction is an additional assumption.

In that model, global translation is invisible to ideal intensity, affine strain moves speckles, and finite-beam illumination changes particle weights. These are conditional map properties, not a measured displacement field, acquired Gaussian law or demonstrated material mechanism. The source's literature audit identifies prior work on collective scattering and mechanical XPCS echoes, so this integration makes no novelty claim.

## Data and computation boundary

The audited Figshare deposit contains one-dimensional `g2` curves and six TTCF heatmap PNGs at a fixed q band and timing. It does not provide raw detector pixel frames, numeric heatmap matrices or q-resolved speckle data. The supplied files therefore do not support the proposed affine-speckle-drift versus density-decorrelation reanalysis. A selected sample workbook, event-correlation data file, image and README are retained with attribution; the full deposit is not copied.
