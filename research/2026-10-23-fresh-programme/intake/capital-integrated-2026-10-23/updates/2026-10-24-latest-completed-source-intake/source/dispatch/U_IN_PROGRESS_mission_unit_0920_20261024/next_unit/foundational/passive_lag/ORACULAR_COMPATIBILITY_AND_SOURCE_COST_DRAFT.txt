Ordinary compatibility tests and the cost to the literal BG source
================================================================

STATUS: New internal operator/source derivation. Main source-cost statement
has independent bounded quantum/no-signaling and exact finite checks. The
operator classicality implication is submitted for independent challenge.
Priority/external correctness UNKNOWN. No frozen prior file is edited.

Selected move
-------------

An ordinary verifier can make one player supply a joint answer to the two
original questions while asking the other player to reproduce a secretly
chosen component. This is an actual consistency mechanism, not a supplied
operator-order assumption. For full-support original question laws, perfect
commuting play forces an original LOCAL correlation. However, the literal
BG repeated whole-string source cannot be carried through this verifier at
polynomial success: the second player is missing linear-many input symbols.
This is a source-compatible failed extension, not a general no-go theorem.

1. Game and exact operator assertion
------------------------------------

Let G have finite question sets X,Y, finite answer sets A,B, a binary
predicate V, and a full-support question law mu(x,y)>0. Form O(G):

  1. Sample (x,y)~mu and independent fair tag sigma in {L,R}.
  2. Alice receives the whole pair (x,y) and answers (a,b).
  3. Bob receives (L,x) or (R,y) and answers a' or b'.
  4. Require V(x,y,a,b)=1 and equality of the tagged component.

This is one ordinary finite two-player one-round game. It changes G. In
particular Bob does NOT receive the other question or Alice's full pair.

Claim O1. If O(G) has a perfect commuting-operator strategy, G has a perfect
classical strategy. Conversely a perfect classical G strategy supplies a
perfect classical O(G) strategy. Consequently, if omega_cl(G)<1, then

  omega_co(O(G))<1.                                           (O1)

The strict inequality is qualitative; no uniform bound in omega_cl(G),
question count, or answer count is asserted below.

2. Actual consistency mirrors give a trace
------------------------------------------

Use common finite local Naimark amplifications to make every actual finite
measurement a PVM without changing the complete table or cross-party
commutation. The common amplification is fixed before questions. No
same-side commutation across different questions is added.

Write Alice's context PVM as M_ab^(x,y), with commuting coarse PVMs

  P_a^(x,y)=sum_b M_ab^(x,y),
  Q_b^(x,y)=sum_a M_ab^(x,y).

Bob's tagged PVMs are R_a^x and S_b^y. Let eta be the common unit vector.
Perfect consistency and completeness give, for every x,y,a,b,

  P_a^(x,y) eta=R_a^x eta,
  Q_b^(x,y) eta=S_b^y eta.                                   (O2)

For example the first equality follows because the squared difference is
twice the same-tag disagreement mass, zero at perfect consistency.
Full support is essential here: every context (x,y) is actually checked.

Choose fixed x0,y0. The fixed Alice effects P_a^(x,y0) and Q_b^(x0,y)
are mirrors on eta of EVERY Bob generator R_a^x,S_b^y. They commute with
all Bob operators because they belong to the opposite local algebra.

The vector state tau on Bob's generated C*-algebra is therefore a trace.
Explicitly, move each Bob word onto the fixed Alice mirrors on eta, in
reversed order, commute that Alice word across any other Bob word, and
move it back. For Bob polynomials U,W this yields tau(UW)=tau(WU).
Continuity extends the identity to the whole generated C*-algebra.

This operation is a proof of the actual vector-state property, not an
assumption that the original state or either local algebra was tracial.

3. Each tested context forces the two variable algebras to commute
------------------------------------------------------------------

For each x,y,a,b, use the ACTUAL joint Alice context from that same pair.
It gives

  R_a^x S_b^y eta
   = R_a^x Q_b^(x,y) eta
   = Q_b^(x,y) R_a^x eta
   = Q_b^(x,y) P_a^(x,y) eta
   = P_a^(x,y) Q_b^(x,y) eta
   = S_b^y R_a^x eta.                                      (O3)

Thus the commutator annihilates eta. Pass to the finite von Neumann GNS
algebra of the trace tau, quotienting its trace-null ideal. In that
representation the trace is faithful and (O3) makes R_a^x,S_b^y commute
as operators. Equivalently, trace cyclicity shows that a trace-null
commutator also annihilates every polynomial vector, so its GNS operator
is zero. No commutation between different x-side measurements or between
different y-side measurements is required.

The two von Neumann subalgebras

  N_L=W*(R_a^x:x,a),    N_R=W*(S_b^y:y,b)

therefore commute. Moreover,

  M_ab^(x,y) eta=P_a^(x,y) Q_b^(x,y) eta
                  =R_a^x S_b^y eta,

so Alice's actual context distribution equals

  p(a,b|x,y)=tau(R_a^x S_b^y).                              (O4)

The original predicate is checked by O(G), so this table wins G perfectly.

4. A trace on the two commuting variable algebras is local
---------------------------------------------------------

Let E_L be the tau-preserving conditional expectation from the finite
von Neumann algebra N_L vee N_R onto N_L. This standard expectation can
be constructed by the L2(tau) orthogonal projection; it is positive, unital,
normal, and N_L-bimodular. For S in N_R, its commutation with N_L and
bimodularity imply E_L(S) is in the center Z(N_L).

Put v_y^b=E_L(S_b^y), a positive central partition of unity. If E_Z is
the trace-preserving expectation of N_L onto its center, put
u_x^a=E_Z(R_a^x), also a positive partition of unity. Then

  tau(R_a^x S_b^y)=tau(R_a^x v_y^b)=tau(u_x^a v_y^b).        (O5)

Represent the commutative center as functions on a probability space.
Equation O5 is precisely a local shared-variable representation

  p(a,b|x,y)=int u_x^a(lambda) v_y^b(lambda) dpi(lambda).

Finite tables lie in the finite-dimensional local polytope. A perfect
local table yields a perfect deterministic assignment, establishing O1's
first implication. Its converse is immediate by answering the assignment.

Finally finite commuting correlation sets are compact (states on the
universal maximal tensor-product measurement algebra). Their payoff is
continuous and attained. Absence of a perfect strategy therefore gives
the strict inequality O1.

This is not a generic statement about PCP oracularization: if some original
input pairs are absent, O3 is available only for tested pairs. General
contextual systems can admit perfect entangled oracularized play without
a perfect classical assignment.

5. The literal r-round BG source
--------------------------------

Fix k>=2,r>=1 and a public graph Gamma subset [k]^r x [k]^r. Its density is

  qhat=|Gamma|/k^(2r).

The original graph game has independent uniform one-round questions x,y
in [k] and answers (s,l),(t,j), where s,t are strings in [k]^r and l,j in
[r]. It accepts iff l=j, s_l=x, t_l=y, and (s,t) in Gamma. This specifies
the ordinary BG template; no random-graph property is needed for this
section.

In r repetitions the literal source answers

  Alice: (X^r,i),   Bob: (Y^r,i),   in coordinate i.

All original coordinate wins are the same event (X^r,Y^r) in Gamma, so the
original all-win probability is qhat. For a BG graph this can be polynomial
in k even though r grows.

Now test the literal source through O(G)^r. Alice receives the complete
arrays X,Y and, in coordinate i, supplies BOTH answers

  ((X,i),(Y,i)).                                             (O6)

Bob receives a tag string sigma in {L,R}^r and the visible array

  Z_i=X_i if sigma_i=L,  Z_i=Y_i if sigma_i=R.                (O7)

He may use an arbitrary complete full-string quantum instrument with
unlimited prior entanglement and retained private workspace. Alice may
also perform any complete local instrument with hidden outcomes, provided
the checked public answers remain exactly O6. There is no communication,
unannounced Alice postselection, or external acceptance flag supplied to Bob.

6. An exact unrestricted-quantum missing-data bound
--------------------------------------------------

For every fixed X,Y, Alice's complete local channel, after summing all her
hidden outcomes, leaves Bob's reduced state unchanged. This is the
no-signaling identity

  Tr_A[(E_(X,Y) tensor id)(rho_AB)]=rho_B.                    (O8)

It is valid with arbitrary prior entanglement. Hence conditioned on Bob's
actual question (sigma,Z), his outcome law is independent of all missing
symbols. The same conclusion is immediate for any no-signaling source
whose prescribed public O6 answers are deterministic.

If sigma contains BOTH tags, consistency in at least one L coordinate
forces Bob to output the entire X string exactly, and consistency in at
least one R coordinate forces the entire Y string exactly. There are r
independent uniform missing k-ary symbols: Y_i on L coordinates and X_i on
R coordinates. Thus a fixed Bob output tuple can pass all consistency
checks for at most one of their k^r completions. Randomized or quantum
outputs are a convex mixture of such guesses, so for each mixed sigma,Z,

  P(all consistency | sigma,Z)<=k^(-r).                      (O9)

If all tags are L, Bob knows all X; if all tags are R, he knows all Y.
These two homogeneous masks have combined mass 2^(1-r). Graph checks
only reduce success. Therefore

  P_source(all O(G) rounds win)
    <=2^(1-r)+(1-2^(1-r))k^(-r).                             (O10)

This upper bound is independent of dimension, Schmidt number, shared
randomness, and collective Bob measurement complexity. It charges actual
unavailable classical data, not a purification inferred from a marginal.

In particular, at k>=2 the right-hand side is <=3*2^(-r). Its logarithmic
cost per repeated coordinate is at least ln2-ln3/r, rather than o(1).
The literal slow-repetition source does not survive this transformation.

7. Exact optimum for any graph and the random-graph consequence
---------------------------------------------------------------

For a mixed mask sigma and visible z, let F_(sigma,z) be the graph fiber
of all completions (X,Y) compatible with z. It has exactly k^r candidate
pairs. Let N_Gamma count the mixed (sigma,z) contexts whose fiber contains
at least one edge of Gamma. Then the OPTIMAL success for the literal
source, allowing all Bob resources above, is exactly

  p_opt=2^(1-r)qhat + N_Gamma/[2^r k^(2r)].                 (O11)

For homogeneous tags, the total graph contribution is 2^(1-r)qhat. For a
mixed context, every successful output determines a single missing
completion. If its fiber is empty, no output wins; otherwise Bob can
preprogram one edge representative of that public fiber and wins for
exactly one of the k^r completions. Choosing that representative is Bob-
local processing of sigma,z and the public Gamma; it uses no hidden-input
oracle. This realizes O11 with a deterministic lookup, so entanglement
cannot improve it. No cheap lookup/table-construction claim is made.

For independent Bernoulli(q) edges, each mixed fiber is empty with
probability (1-q)^(k^r)<=exp(-q k^r). There are at most 2^r k^r such
contexts, so with probability at least

  1-2^r k^r exp(-q k^r),                                   (O12)

every mixed fiber is nonempty. On that event,

  p_opt=2^(1-r)qhat+(1-2^(1-r))k^(-r).                     (O13)

For the usual BG scaling r=k^(1/3), q=k^(-1/5), the failure probability in
O12 tends to zero very rapidly. No induced-subgraph or quantum-value claim
is needed to prove O12. The original graph event has mass about q, while
the actual oracularized source costs exponentially in r. Conditioning on
Gamma after computing a guess does not evade O9: O9 is an unconditional
preselection success bound before that additional check.

8. A close primary comparator, with its actual parameter cost
--------------------------------------------------------------

Ito--Kobayashi--Matsumoto, arXiv:0810.0693v1, Section 4.1 Lemma 8, uses a
different dummy-question oracularization of a three-query PCP and proves

  omega_co(G') <= 1-c(1-omega_cl(PCP))^2/Q^2,
  c=(1+15sqrt(2))^(-2),                                    (O14)

where Q is the number of proof positions. This is a genuine answer-size-
independent commuting soundness theorem, but the displayed Q dependence
does not certify a uniform constant gap in the growing BG parameter.
Encoding the whole n-round answer table as one PCP uses Q=3k^n; applying
that theorem to that encoding is not the same as repeating the transform
of the base game. Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0810.0693 .

The exact O1 mechanism and O9 source failure above do not claim to replace
that theorem or to prove an improved quantitative soundness bound.

9. Consequence and remaining gate
---------------------------------

This attempt supplies an actual operator compatibility mechanism in an
ordinary verifier and identifies why the tempting classical source transfer
fails. Compatibility is not free: the new verifier changes which side has
the data needed for the preserved whole-string answers. Its success cost
is enforced even against arbitrary quantum resources.

It does NOT settle the original unrestricted selection problem. O1 has no
uniform quantitative base-gap estimate here; O10 is only for the literal
deterministic whole-string source O6, not all strategies for O(G)^r. A
different payoff-aware strategy may discard that global record, and no
claim is made about its repeated value. Nor does the original BG event
itself acquire the new consistency mirrors without changing the verifier.
