2. A dimension-free quadratic a-block occupation bound

For one X=X_g, the exact energy identity is

 d||X||^2/dt=-kappa||[Z,X]||^2-2gamma||Pe X||^2.                 (2)

Thus E_Z=integral||[Z,X]||^2<=1/kappa and
E_e=integral||PeX||^2<=1/(2gamma). Positivity X<=I also gives the separate
actual-risk bound E_e<=rho_g(T)/gamma. Neither bound controls integral pa.

Identify the equally dimensional a/e ancillary blocks by |a><e|. Put
U=X_aa, Y=X_ea, let e(t)=||PeX|| and z(t)=||[Z,X]||, and let Q denote
the remaining LIVE sectors besides a,e. All Q charges are 0 or -1, so
||X_Qa||<=z. Write A=Hc_aa, E=Hc_ee, C=Hc_eQ, F=Hc_aQ.
Their operator norms are <=B. With lambda=(kappa+gamma)/2,

 Ydot=-ig U+R_Y,
 Udot=-i[A,U]+R_U,

 ||R_Y||<=(u+3B+lambda)e+Bz,
 ||R_U||<=2u e+2Bz.                                           (3)

The first formula follows from
R_Y=ig X_ee-iE Y+iY A-iC X_Qa+iX_eQ F^dag-lambda Y.
The second uses R_U=-ig(Y-Y^dag)-iF X_Qa+iX_aQ F^dag.
The missing known e-a block is load-bearing: the coefficient of U in
Ydot is exactly -ig and cannot be canceled by Hc.

Use the fixed multiplier m(t)=2 Im Tr(Y^dag U). Block orthogonality gives
||X||^2>=||U||^2+2||Y||^2, hence
|m|<=||U||^2+||Y||^2<=||X||^2<=1.
Its leading derivative is 2g||U||^2. Cauchy--Schwarz in (3),
including the aa commutator term, gives with K1=u+5B+lambda

 |m'-2g||U||^2|
 <=2K1 e||U||+2Bz||U||+4u e^2+4B e z.                         (4)

Let J=integral||U||^2 and A0=K1/sqrt(2gamma)+B/sqrt(kappa).
Integrating (4), using |m(T)-m(0)|<=2 and Young's inequality, yields

 J <=[2+A0^2/ell+2u/gamma+4B/sqrt(2gamma kappa)]/ell.

Since the off-aa part of PaX is charged by [Z,X], define the explicit
finite, dimension-free constant

 C_a=1/kappa+

Then, for EVERY bounded waveform Hc and every T,

 integral_0^T ||Pa X_g(t)||^2 dt <= C_a.                         (6)

This is quadratic density-block occupation, not integral Tr Pa X. It is a
partial hypocoercive energy estimate with a mandatory coupling and no moving

3. Full surviving-state difference charged by actual death

Let X_i=X_gi start from the SAME parameter-independent joint density, and
Delta=X_0-X_1, d=|g0-g1|>0. Relabel the two endpoints in this proof, if

does not need to know this proof-only choice. Then

 Deltadot=L_g0(Delta)-i(g0-g1)[V,X_1].

The homogeneous live generator is HS contractive. Block Cauchy--Schwarz
gives the explicit source bound

 |Tr Delta[V,X_1]|
 <=2[||Pe Delta|| ||Pa X_1||+||Pa Delta|| ||Pe X_1||].            (7)

Therefore, using (6), positivity, and
integral||PeDelta||^2<=2(rho_0+rho_1)/gamma,

 ||Delta(T)||^2 <=4d{
 sqrt[2C_a(rho_0+rho_1)/gamma]+2sqrt[C_a rho_1/gamma]}.

Put r=(rho_0+rho_1)/2. The first term in braces is
2sqrt(C_a r/gamma); the second is at most 2sqrt(C_a r/gamma) because the
source endpoint was chosen to have rho_1=min(rho_0,rho_1)<=r. Hence

 ||Delta(T)||^2 <=16d sqrt(C_a r/gamma).                          (8)

No purified-overlap/window lemma or local-QFI limit is used here. The source
is paired with a finite a-block energy and an actual-risk e-block energy.

4. Untouched references and remaining controller gates

An arbitrary untouched reference can be included at the cost of replacing
D by D_active^2: decompose a common input into pure states, each has Schmidt
rank <=D_active across active/reference; controls leave that reference
support invariant. Trace distance is convex and rho^(1/4) is concave, so
apply (9) to each component using its AVERAGE endpoint risk r_l, not its
maximum endpoint risk. Their weighted average is exactly the two-world
average r of the original mixed input, so Jensen preserves all constants.

output is part of D_active; only a reference untouched for the ENTIRE run
