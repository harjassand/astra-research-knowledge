Synchronous FMS completion H: phase-dependent source calculation
=================================================================

Scope
-----
This is an exact check for the specified H measurement strategy, not a
calculation of the unrestricted value of H or its repetitions. The FMS
state and rank-one bases at theta=0 are those in Mančinska,
[arXiv:2610.06512v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06512), Section 3.2,
Proposition 1, equations (12)--(15). The phase family used here is

    |psi_theta> = (|00> + exp(i theta) sqrt(2) sum_(j=1)^4 |jj>)/3.

Assume H samples uniformly from Z x Z, where Z=X disjoint-union Y and
|X|=|Y|=5. On X x Y it uses the original FMS predicate; on Y x X it uses
the transposed predicate. Equal questions require equal answers, while
distinct same-side pairs are free. Alice uses the original rank-one PVMs
on X and the entrywise conjugates of Bob's original PVMs on Y. Bob uses
their transpose counterparts. Thus for an equal question z, the two local
bases are transpose-matched. For the standard maximally-correlated-basis
formula, outcomes associated with vectors v,w have amplitude

    (1/3) <v|D_theta|w>,
    D_theta = |0><0| + sqrt(2) exp(i theta) Pi_core.

1. Equal-question consistency
-----------------------------
For any of the six MS questions z, its rank-one basis is the flag |0>
together with four orthonormal core vectors. D_theta is diagonal with one
flag eigenvalue and a constant core eigenvalue, so every off-diagonal
matched-basis amplitude vanishes. The total matching probability is

    1/9 + 4*(2/9) = 1.

For a bridge question, the basis has the two sign vectors

    |t^a> = (|0> + a|c>)/sqrt(2),  a=+1,-1,

and three core vectors orthogonal to |c>. Its only nonzero off-diagonal
matched-basis amplitudes are between the two sign outcomes:

    (1/3)<t^+|D_theta|t^->
      = (1 - sqrt(2) exp(i theta))/6,

and the reverse transition has the same modulus. Every core/core
off-diagonal amplitude vanishes because D_theta is constant on the core;
sign/core amplitudes vanish by orthogonality to |c>. Therefore the
per-question consistency and loss are exactly

    c_bridge(theta) = 1 - |1-sqrt(2)exp(i theta)|^2/18
                    = (15 + 2 sqrt(2) cos(theta))/18,
    ell_bridge(theta) = (3 - 2 sqrt(2) cos(theta))/18.

This is strictly less than perfect consistency at theta=0 as well: the
nonmaximally weighted flag/core basis cannot be perfectly synchronized by
the same bridge PVM on both sides.

2. Uniform mean success in H
----------------------------
There are 100 ordered question pairs. Of these:

  * 25 pairs in X x Y have FMS success averaged over that block
    f_theta = 1 - 4(1-cos(theta))/225;
  * 25 pairs in Y x X have the same average by the transposed construction;
  * 40 distinct same-side pairs are free and win with certainty;
  * 6 diagonal MS pairs are perfectly consistent;
  * 4 diagonal bridge pairs have consistency c_bridge(theta).

Consequently the exact mean success of this source strategy is

    h(theta) = (40 + 6 + 4 c_bridge(theta) + 50 f_theta)/100
             = [886 + (8 + 4 sqrt(2)) cos(theta)]/900
             = [443 + (4 + 2 sqrt(2)) cos(theta)]/450.

The coefficient of cos(theta) is positive, so

    max_theta h(theta) = h(0)
      = (447 + 2 sqrt(2))/450 < 1,
    1-h(0) = (3 - 2 sqrt(2))/450 > 0.

Thus the chosen H completion destroys the algebraically perfect point of
the original FMS game, even though the extra diagonal loss is small.

3. Common-phase n-copy source
-----------------------------
For one common theta across n tensor copies, use the product of the same
local rank-one instruments in all coordinates. Conditional on theta, the
state, questions, and measurements factor across coordinates, so the
probability that all n H coordinates win is h(theta)^n. Averaging the
common phase gives exactly

    P_source(all n win) = (1/(2 pi)) integral_0^(2 pi) h(theta)^n d theta.

For example, writing A=443/450 and B=(4+2 sqrt(2))/450, this is exactly

    sum_(j=0)^floor(n/2) binom(n,2j) A^(n-2j) B^(2j) binom(2j,j)/4^j,

since the odd cosine moments vanish. In particular,

    P_source(all n win) <= ((447+2 sqrt(2))/450)^n,

which is exponential decay for this actual common-phase product-instrument
source. It is not an upper bound on the unrestricted entangled value of
H^(tensor n), nor on other instruments or resources.
