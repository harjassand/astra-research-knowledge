# Check of the O1–O5 commuting-to-local implication

I checked only the finite-game operator argument in `ORACULAR_COMPATIBILITY_AND_SOURCE_COST_DRAFT.txt`, O1–O5. Under its hypotheses, the reduction is sound: a perfect commuting strategy for the stated oracularization yields a perfect local table for the original game. In particular, allowing arbitrary noncommutativity among distinct (x)-measurements or among distinct (y)-measurements does not leave a context/quantum-coloring loophole.

## What the actual consistency checks imply

For the finite family of measurements, one may use a common local Naimark dilation on each side. It preserves the state table and cross-party commutation: the dilated local projections are built from that party's effects and a private ancilla, while the other party is extended by the identity on the ancilla. This is only a proof dilation, not an extra resource assumed in the game.

Write (P_a^{xy}) and (Q_b^{xy}) for Alice's two coarse PVMs from her joint context, and (R_a^x,S_b^y) for Bob's component PVMs. Perfect consistency on a tested input gives zero disagreement mass. Since the compared projections commute across parties,
\[
 \|(P_a^{xy}-R_a^x)\eta\|^2
 =\langle\eta,(P_a^{xy}+R_a^x-2P_a^{xy}R_a^x)\eta\rangle=0,
\]
and similarly (Q_b^{xy}\eta=S_b^y\eta). Full support makes this valid for every (x,y), not just the support graph of a sparse verifier.

For fixed (x_0,y_0), (P_a^{x,y_0}) mirrors (R_a^x) on the vector for every (x,a), and (Q_b^{x_0,y}) mirrors (S_b^y) for every (y,b). The mirrors belong to Alice's algebra and commute with Bob's entire algebra. Replacing letters of a Bob word by their mirrors in reverse order proves the vector state on the Bob-generated C*-algebra is tracial. This does not presume a tracial source state.

For each pair (x,y), the two coarse PVMs in Alice's joint PVM commute. The O3 chain is valid with the order shown:
\[
R_a^xS_b^y\eta=R_a^xQ_b^{xy}\eta
=Q_b^{xy}R_a^x\eta=Q_b^{xy}P_a^{xy}\eta
=P_a^{xy}Q_b^{xy}\eta=S_b^yR_a^x\eta.
\]
The last equality uses (P_a^{xy}\) commuting with Bob's (S_b^y). Thus each cross commutator has trace-square zero. In the faithful tracial GNS von Neumann algebra it is the zero operator, so the von Neumann algebras generated by all (R_a^x) and all (S_b^y) commute. No same-side commutation was inferred.

Finally, (M_{ab}^{xy}=P_a^{xy}Q_b^{xy}), and the mirror relations give the table entry 
\[
 \langle\eta,M_{ab}^{xy}\eta\rangle=\tau(R_a^xS_b^y).
\]
For the finite von Neumann algebra generated by Bob's operators, the trace-preserving conditional expectation (E_L) onto (N_L=W^*(R_a^x)) exists. For (S\in N_R=W^*(S_b^y)), bimodularity and (SN_L=N_LS) imply (E_L(S)\in Z(N_L)). Let (E_Z:N_L\to Z(N_L)) be the trace-preserving expectation. Then
\[
\tau(R_a^x S_b^y)
=\tau(R_a^x E_L(S_b^y))
=\tau(E_Z(R_a^x)E_L(S_b^y)).
\]
Both factors are positive central partitions of unity. Representing the center as a commutative probability algebra gives a shared-variable local model. A finite perfect local table has a deterministic perfect component, so it supplies a perfect classical strategy for (G).

## Scope and caveats

The proof uses full support exactly where it needs a mirror and a within-context commutation relation for every pair (x,y). If some pairs are omitted, O3 is unavailable for those pairs, and this reduction does not apply as written. The conditional expectation step is valid in the faithful finite tracial GNS algebra; it does not require the original local algebras to commute within either side, nor does it rely on a finite-dimensional representation. Consequently the strict value conclusion also needs the stated finite-alphabet compactness/attainment argument after perfect play has been excluded.
