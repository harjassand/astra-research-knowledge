1. Actual controller class, not a claim about all finite programs

Keep the original sample generator: unknown g in I=[ell,u], ell>0,
H_g=gV+H_c(t), V=(|e><a|+h.c.) tensor I, kappa D[Z],
Z=(P_a-P_b) tensor I, kappa>0, and terminal gamma D[J],
J=|dead><e| tensor I, gamma>0. The first terminal jump permanently disables
sample access. H_c is parameter independent, bounded and C^1 with the
declared strength/slew, Pe H_c Pa=0, and no coupling to/from dead.

Fix a FINITE library of physically implemented control primitives j=1,...,m.
Primitive j has a fixed positive duration t_j, a specified admissible
Hamiltonian waveform, and finite working-register dimension D_j<=D. The
waveforms may be strong and may move both logical branches, encode/decode
an active error-correcting code, couple to finite meters, route memory,
or release/reprepare a probe. Every such physical action, smooth connection,
readout and reset must actually be in the library, with its time and risk.
Boundary control values must match when primitives are concatenated, or the
finite connection pulse is another paid primitive.

A primitive may also use known, bounded, local working-register detector/
reset jumps I_sample tensor M_l(t). Their exact finite rates and monitored
records are part of its instrument. These jumps cannot supply an a/e
crossblock or change the sample's physical noise into correlated noise.
Additional output modes can be put into the finite working register with
an explicit sample-vacuum sector and actual bounded transfer Hamiltonian.
No instantaneous sample measurement, reset, or collective Markov a/e link
is supplied. The terminal-dead sample sector remains absorbing throughout.

Arbitrarily large retained independent references/old records may be idle
spectators. Each primitive acts on the sample and at most D working-register
degrees of freedom; choosing/routing other stored data uses the actual
compiled interface. External history may choose the next primitive, but
must not secretly generate a new continuously adjustable pulse, larger
active arity, or a different uncharged detector. Its classical processing
time is paid or occurs while the sample is in an actual safe state.

The library is fixed throughout the theorem. A finite program can generate
an infinite family of new waveform durations/amplitudes; such a program is
NOT a finite compiled library in this sense. Increasing the library, active
arity, pulse resolution or readout hardware can change the constant below.
There is no bound here uniform in those refinements.

2. Local occupation and death effects

For one primitive starting with an arbitrary joint live input, let Phi_j,g(s)
be its unconditional evolution, retaining the terminal-dead sector and
including all physical detector/reset recycling. Define positive effects

 A_j(g)=integral_0^(t_j) Phi_j,g(s)^*(Pa) ds,
 W_j(g)=integral_0^(t_j) gamma Phi_j,g(s)^*(Pe) ds.                (1)

For input sigma, Tr A_j sigma is actual accumulated a occupation, and
Tr W_j sigma is actual probability of first terminal death during the
primitive. Terminal absorbing means 0<=W_j<=I. Also 0<=A_j<=t_j I.
These are effects of the complete physical primitive, not ideal gates or
no-jump amplitudes. All measured outcomes may be retained; their sum gives
the same effects. Time-stamped records are not discarded from the output.

Let K_j(g)=ker W_j(g). The exact-active-zero-risk support theorem implies

 K_j(g) is independent of g in I; call it K_j,
 A_j(g) vanishes on K_j for every g.                             (2)

Proof: for any vector v in K_j(g0), the positive initial density |v><v|
has zero accumulated death at g0. The accepted support argument gives
Pa rho=Pe rho=0 throughout the primitive, so V annihilates that trajectory.
The same initial state and common controls therefore have precisely the
same trajectory at every other g. In particular zero loss holds at each g.
Interchanging g and g0 proves equality of the kernels, not just inclusion.
Zero a occupation gives A_j v=0 by positivity, including its crossblocks.

The same exact support proof survives the stated LOCAL ancilla jumps.
They preserve the e-free face, so the e-Q block still gives K rho=0 for
K=Pe(gV+H_c)(I-Pe-Pdead). Differentiating Tr K^dag K rho at its zero face
adds nonnegative terms ||K(I tensor M_l)sqrt(rho)||_HS^2. The kappa term
still forces KZ sqrt(rho)=0. Repeating for KZ similarly forces KZ^2;
Pa=(Z^2+Z)/2 and KPa=g|e><a| give Pa rho=0. No ideal meter or free reset
is invoked by this extension. A different dissipative crossblock need not
obey the argument and is outside the theorem.

3. A finite library gives a uniform linear gain

Let Pi_j project onto K_j^perp in the local sample-plus-working space.
If this complement is nonzero, define

 lambda_j=min_(g in I) lambda_min[Pi_j W_j(g) Pi_j | K_j^perp].   (3)

For each fixed g the compressed effect is positive definite by definition
of its kernel. The kernel is FIXED, the dimension is finite, W_j(g) is
continuous in g, and I is compact; hence lambda_j>0. This uses no
compactness over varying waveforms/kernels. From (1),(2),

 A_j(g)<=t_j Pi_j<=(t_j/lambda_j)W_j(g), all g in I.             (4)

If W_j is identically zero, A_j is identically zero and its gain is zero.
Set

 C_library=max_j t_j/lambda_j                                   (5)

over the nonzero cases. The maximum is finite. It has units of time and
depends on the ACTUAL primitives, hardware, noise and parameter interval.
The certificate is quantitative but may be extremely pessimistic. There is
no claimed useful numeric value without computing/certifying these effects.

Untouched spectators do not change this constant. On a reference space R,
the two effects are A_j tensor I_R and W_j tensor I_R, so (4) holds on
arbitrarily entangled inputs. A larger stored history does not by itself
worsen lambda_j. A larger ACTIVE working register or new joint pulse is a
different library; its constant must be rechecked. Finite-arity swaps and
readout/routing commands can themselves be library primitives, with their
actual sample evolution during their duration included.

4. Arbitrary long and adaptive composition

At a primitive boundary, let sigma_g,h be the unconditional positive live
substate for history h. The actual common controller chooses j(h). Apply
(4) to every sigma_g,h and sum all boundary histories and primitive attempts.
Every term of W is a disjoint first-death increment, since there is only one
sample and no later access after death. Therefore

 I_a,g:=E_g integral Pa dt <= C_library rho_g,                    (6)

where rho_g is actual total eventual destruction through the complete run.
This holds for arbitrary sequence length, arbitrary history-dependent
choices, parameter-dependent posterior states, and all retained records.
It includes source calibration primitives as well as later target use.
It does not require a stationary input, an independent-reset sample, or a
weak/gentle gate. It is a policy-independent certificate for this library.

Preparation, cleanup, physical readout and reset must be included among the
attempts. Stopping halfway through a primitive and exporting an ideal
sample state is not a covered completed procedure unless that stopped
waveform/cleanup is itself a member of the finite library. A stopping flag
at an allowed boundary is followed by the actual compiled finite cleanup;
unreturned fast residue is not erased. For indefinitely continued policies,
Tonelli gives (6) for the nonnegative accumulated occupation and terminal
risk by finite caps. A completed-output discrimination consequence requires
the specified returned instruments to converge, or nontermination is failure.

5. Full-output risk consequence

Begin from a common g-independent state, or include all earlier g-bearing
source experience in the paid chain. Purify the common instruments, local
baths and controller; V vanishes on dead. The established overlap telescope
for two parameter values gives, for root fidelity f of their full accessible
outputs,

 1-f <= |Delta g|[sqrt(I_a,0 I_e,1)+sqrt(I_e,0 I_a,1)],
 I_e,i=integral Pe_i dt=rho_i/gamma.                             (7)

Thus (6) yields the duration-independent bound

 f >= max{0,1-2|Delta g|sqrt(C_library/gamma)sqrt(rho_0 rho_1)}.  (8)

It covers diagonal information, surviving coherences, all meter records,
first-death times, memory and moving references. It is not a one-reference
fringe bound. If a binary test has both errors <=alpha<1/2, data processing
gives f<=2sqrt(alpha(1-alpha)), so

 sqrt(rho_0 rho_1) >=
 [1-2sqrt(alpha(1-alpha))]/[2|Delta g|sqrt(C_library/gamma)].      (9)

In particular, actual risk cannot vanish at fixed nontrivial binary accuracy
by making a fixed-library adaptive procedure arbitrarily long or by storing
more old data in untouched references. Both risks in (9) are the endpoint
risks; no risk promise on intermediate g is needed for this overlap argument.
Uniform continuous-parameter precision implies such binary tests at any two
points separated by more than twice the requested error tolerance.

6. What is and is not certified computationally

Equations (1)--(5) define finite-dimensional effect certificates. With a
certified projector onto K_j and validated ODE evaluation for the actual
waveform, a positive lambda lower bound can be certified by a finite grid:
||partial_g W_j||<=2 t_j ||V|| by CPTP Duhamel. If an operator-norm-validated
grid approximation has error epsilon_num and maximum distance delta_g to
the nearest node, then the minimum compressed grid eigenvalue minus
(epsilon_num+2 t_j||V||delta_g) is a valid lower bound. Refinement eventually
certifies positivity when the stated exact kernel is known. Arbitrary smooth
black-box waveforms do not automatically provide an effectively identifiable
kernel or cheap interval ODE solve; no such oracle is claimed here.

For a time-independent explicitly specified primitive, its zero-loss input
space can be obtained as the largest subspace in ker Pa intersect ker Pe
invariant under Z, the local reset matrices, and the KNOWN no-click drift
-iH_c-kappa Z^2/2-gamma Pe/2-sum_l (I tensor M_l^dag M_l)/2. On that
subspace the unknown V vanishes, so the calculation is independent of g.
Without ancillary jumps this reduces to invariance under H_c and Z. With
non-normal reset matrices one must not replace the no-click drift merely
by H_c: their anticommutator terms remain part of the invariance test.
For arbitrary smooth time-dependent primitives, (2) remains an existence
statement and an actual effective constant needs the corresponding kernel
certificate. No favorable numerical example is needed for the theorem.

7. Boundary and conventional meaning

This is finite-dimensional positive-effect observability plus the exact
noise-power support lemma and the existing overlap argument. It makes no
priority/novelty claim over those methods. Its substantive restriction is
a fixed finite library of ACTUAL bounded local control primitives, not
finite code length, and its gain constant is not asserted uniform over new
waveform designs. A controller choosing continuously tinier gates, larger
active arity, newly tuned waveforms after learning, stronger meters, or an
extra correlated bath is outside that uniform certificate until its new
primitives are analyzed. A supplied standard program can generate such a
family, so compilation alone does not settle the original general question.

The stronger completed-window inequality for ALL smooth bounded waveforms
is still unproved. This theorem supplies a quantitative obstruction for
strong compiled adaptive controls, including finite-cadence corrections
that are physically implemented in that interface. It does not infer a
general all-control no-go from the weak-return or passive-reference results.
