# Synchronous completion inherits the FMS tracial gap

## Game and claim

Let \(G\) be the finite FMS game from Mančinska, [arXiv:2610.06512v1, Theorem 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.06512), for which \(\omega_{\rm tr}(G)<1\). Make a synchronous game \(H\) with common question set \(Z=X\sqcup Y\), where \(X,Y\) are the two original question sets. Each \(z\in Z\) has its own typed answer set \(A_z\), used by both players when asked \(z\). On \((z,z)\), require equal answers; on \(X\times Y\), use the original FMS predicate; on \(Y\times X\), use its transpose; and on distinct pairs within \(X\times X\) or \(Y\times Y\), accept every answer pair. The referee samples uniformly from \(Z\times Z\).

Then \(\omega_{\rm co}(H)<1\). The proof imports only the strict tracial gap for \(G\); the synchronous commuting-strategy reduction below is exact for arbitrary POVM effects and arbitrary commuting representations.

## From perfect synchrony to a tracial state

Suppose a commuting-operator strategy for \(H\) is perfect. Take its GNS representation, with unit vector \(\Omega\), Alice effects \(A_z^a\), and Bob effects \(B_z^a\). The two generated local algebras commute. Every diagonal pair \((z,z)\) is tested with positive question probability, so perfect synchrony gives
\[
\phi(A_z^aB_z^b)=0\quad(a\ne b),
\]
where \(\phi(T)=\langle\Omega,T\Omega\rangle\). Completeness and this support condition imply, for each \(z,a\),
\[
\phi(A_z^a)=\phi(B_z^a)=\phi(A_z^aB_z^a).
\]
The effects \(A_z^a,B_z^a\) commute, are positive contractions, and satisfy \((A_z^a)^2\le A_z^a\), \((B_z^a)^2\le B_z^a\). Hence
\[
0\le\phi\bigl((A_z^a-B_z^a)^2\bigr)
=\phi((A_z^a)^2)+\phi((B_z^a)^2)-2\phi(A_z^aB_z^a)\le0.
\]
Thus \((A_z^a-B_z^a)\Omega=0\). This uses the actual POVM effects; it assumes neither projectivity nor a Naimark dilation.

Let \(\mathcal A=C^*(A_z^a:z\in Z,a\in A_z)\) and define \(\tau=\phi|_{\mathcal A}\). For a word \(P=A_{z_1}^{a_1}\cdots A_{z_r}^{a_r}\), repeated use of \(A_z^a\Omega=B_z^a\Omega\) and cross-party commutation gives
\[
P\Omega=\widetilde P\Omega,
\qquad
\widetilde P=B_{z_r}^{a_r}\cdots B_{z_1}^{a_1}.
\]
Extend this reversal linearly to polynomials; since the generators are self-adjoint, \(\widetilde{P^*}=\widetilde P^*\). For polynomials \(P,Q\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\tau(PQ)
&=\langle P^*\Omega,Q\Omega\rangle
=\langle\widetilde P^*\Omega,\widetilde Q\Omega\rangle
=\langle\Omega,\widetilde P\widetilde Q\Omega\rangle\\
&=\langle\Omega,\widetilde P Q\Omega\rangle
=\langle\Omega,Q\widetilde P\Omega\rangle
=\langle\Omega,QP\Omega\rangle
=\tau(QP).
\end{aligned}
\]
Here \(\widetilde P\) commutes with Alice's algebra, \(\widetilde Q\Omega=Q\Omega\), and \(\widetilde P\Omega=P\Omega\). By continuity, \(\tau\) is a tracial state on all of \(\mathcal A\).

For \(x\in X,y\in Y\), synchrony also gives
\[
\phi(A_x^aB_y^b)=\phi(A_x^aA_y^b)=\tau(A_x^aA_y^b).
\]
Consequently, \(E_x^a=A_x^a\) and \(F_y^b=A_y^b\), with state \(\tau\), form a complete tracial FMS strategy. If the commuting strategy for \(H\) were perfect, this strategy would win every FMS input pair perfectly, contradicting \(\omega_{\rm tr}(G)<1\). The transpose block and free same-side off-diagonal cells do not weaken this implication.

## Why the commuting value is strictly below one

No perfect commuting correlation exists by the reduction above. To pass from nonattainment of perfect play to a strict value gap, use compactness for finite question/answer sets: take the universal unital C*-algebras generated by the two finite POVM families and their maximal tensor product. States on this unital C*-algebra form a weak-* compact set; by its universal property, its state correlations are exactly the commuting-operator correlations. The finite table of probabilities is a continuous image of that state space, hence compact, and the game payoff is continuous. Therefore \(\omega_{\rm co}(H)\) is attained. If it equalled one, there would be a perfect commuting strategy, already ruled out. Thus \(\omega_{\rm co}(H)<1\).

The conclusion concerns this explicit synchronous completion. It is not a claim about arbitrary synchronizations or about preserving traciality under the separate postselected instrument in the phase-twirl construction.
