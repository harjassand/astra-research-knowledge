RECOGNITION CONTROL: A SCOPED GEOMETRY OBSTRUCTION AND A NAMED ERASURE TEST
2026-10-24. New companion. Frozen prior mechanism/failure bytes are unchanged.
Status: no compatible reusable copying/passivation cycle acquired.

1. What the flexible-linker calculation actually rules out

Consider the open-mask reference ensemble of the earlier three-state model.
H is anchored to monomer M at b, the input lock L is anchored at a, and the
nonthreaded contour order is b -> M tag -> a. Assume zero-offset phantom
Gaussian linkers, independent increments, and identical contact/orientation
factors for H binding the two identical tag sequences. In per-coordinate
variance units,
  v_M = N_H + N_M(b->tag),
  v_L = N_H + N_M(b->a) + N_Lreturn >= v_M.
The contact density gives c_L/c_M=(v_M/v_L)^(3/2)<=1. This is computed in
the OPEN ensemble with the stated anchors, not by treating an already
closed loop as an independent coil. Rigid/preorganized endpoints, nonzero
offsets, unequal capture factors, excluded volume or additional closure
constraints can invalidate the ordering; this is not a theorem about all
DNA geometries.

Retain the exact earlier finite-state definitions
  p_pre = c_M/(K_d+c_L+c_M),
  p_post = c_M/[c_M+(K_d+[L]) B_T],
with K_d>0, B_T>=1, [L]>=0 and the same c_M before/after admission.
If p_post>=1-delta, then K_d<=delta*c_M/(1-delta). Therefore c_L<=c_M
forces
  p_pre >= (1-delta)/(2-delta).                         (1)
Equality is possible in this model at c_L=c_M, B_T=1, [L]=0 and the
corresponding K_d. A nearly complete post-mask population would thus coexist
with about half of the inputs already masked in this realization.

A separate kinetic statement needs an additional rate premise. For closed
protector C -> open O at u>0, O -> monomer-mask M at v>0, and O -> C at
w>=0, the mean first-passage time from C is
  t_C=(u+v+w)/(uv).
If u,v are unchanged, w_pre<=v and w_post>=0, then
  t_pre/t_post <= (u+2v)/(u+v) < 2.                    (2)
An equilibrium concentration ordering alone does not force the on-rate
ordering. With common capture/on-rate factors it supplies that premise.
This result concerns MEAN times, not a claimed full waiting-time ordering.

These calculations were independently checked by luna_nonnormal; root
provided the original geometry/race derivation. The existing premature
masking path remains chemically possible even outside this special model.
The special model only quantifies why a flexible return linker does not
itself supply a favorable gate.

2. An actual alternative actuator, not a freely assigned cage

OWN_OPERATION.txt under recognition_erasure_A specifies an RNA recognition
tail on a DNA body-tethered adapter. A DNA template captures this tail and
invades the monomer's long DNA forward port. RNase H consumes hybridized
RNA while leaving the template DNA and assembly DNA intact. The next
backbone interaction uses HMSD to displace the template's long contact.

This is a named chemical operation. Cycling-probe technology already uses
RNase-H cleavage of chimeric probes to recycle a DNA target (Duck et al.
1990, PMID2400595); recognition destruction/release is not a new principle.
The candidate gives up enzyme-free operation. Each prepared incoming
monomer supplies an RNA tail; tails are consumed on actual cleavage during
incorporation or early erasure. A d_E dissociation can retain an intact tail.

The proposed separate adapter admits a nonthreaded domain ordering, but
capture on the adapter followed by invasion of the monomer is a remote
toehold operation. The original source's contiguous TMSD rate cannot be
copied to it. RNA cutting can occur as soon as the recognition hybrid forms,
before lock displacement: this is a concrete input-destruction path, not an
admission-specific AND. Conversely, completion before cutting is not always
failure: an uncleaved recognition hybrid can retain a completed product for
later enzyme action. Actual fragment release must still occur.

3. Exact minimal race, with both early and late losses retained

This is a per-encounter conditional model, not fitted chemistry. Let E be
tag-captured but not admitted, A admitted/uncompleted/uncut, A_e admitted
and erased, C_t completed but tag-held, and S completed and erased.
  E -> A at a; early destruction at c_E; encounter loss at d_E.
  A -> A_e at c_A; A -> C_t at g; anchor-loss failure at d_A.
  A_e -> S at g; anchor-loss failure at d_A.
  C_t -> S at c_C; uncleared-product escape at d_C.
All rates are nonnegative, with productive denominators positive. The
exact probability of S for one initial E encounter is
  P_S = [a/(a+c_E+d_E)]
        * [c_A*g/(g+d_A) + g*c_C/(c_C+d_C)]
          /(c_A+g+d_A).                               (3)

If d_C=0 and c_C>0, the second factor reduces to g/(g+d_A), irrespective
of c_A. That limit permits arbitrarily slow late erasure only because the
tag-held product never escapes and observation time is unbounded. For
d_C>0 and common c_E=c_A=c_C=c, the passivated-completion probability
vanishes both as c->0 and c->infinity (with a,g fixed and d_E>=0). A useful
window needs actual admission and residence-time separation. Rebinding of
escaped input/product, enzyme saturation/occupancy, multiple cuts and
fragment binding require extra states; they are not silently successful
cycles in (3).

Early incoming-tag erasure is NOT automatically an irreversible chain
failure. An explicit retry extension has a free template site F -> E at
kappa, E -> admitted A at a, E -> fragment-held D at c, E -> F at d, and
D -> F at rho. Here an early-erased incoming complex is no longer usable
unless explicitly rearmed; the already-grown chain frontier is assumed
to remain attached throughout F,E,D. With kappa,a,rho>0, the mean time to
admission from F is
  T_F=(a+c+d)/(a*kappa)+1/a+c/(a*rho),                  (4)
and the expected number of early-erasure events before admission is c/a.
These count consumed recognition adapters, not automatically destroyed
whole DNA bodies. Rearming/removing the retained adapter stem needs an
actual operation. Fresh pools or a clamped feed are paid premises.

With an independent actual frontier-loss hazard lambda>0 in all F,E,D
states, admission before loss instead has exact probability
  q = kappa*a/[kappa*a + lambda*(kappa+a+c+d+lambda)
                 + kappa*c*lambda/(rho+lambda)].        (5)
Thus q=1 at lambda=0 and q<1 for positive lambda; q'(0)=-T_F. Independent
identically reset stages have uninterrupted success q^N. This is a scoped
renewal processivity model. It neither inherits an early-cut-only q^N loss
when genuine retries are available, nor acquires recovery after frontier
detachment. Actual rho, finite-pool drift and product reattachment remain
physical obligations.

4. Identity, source setting and full-cycle discriminator

In the source copier the recognition-domain word is part of the copied
output and supplies demonstrated downstream recognition. Digesting that
word does not preserve this function. More narrowly: if the only input-
identity-dependent material is discarded and all retained bodies are the
same, the retained product has no information about the chosen word.
This is conditional, NOT a universal claim that cleavage deletes all
information. The actual source also has retained fluorophore/poly(T)
reporter labels. A declared reporter/payload encoding can retain identities,
but then one must state and read that output contract, including order for
a longer word, and compare complete templated synthesis accordingly. No
undeclared duplicate barcode/payload is inserted to declare copying solved.

Hogrefe et al.1990 directly observe RNA-tract cleavage in chimeric hybrids;
their products retain DNA/RNA pieces, so neither clean tag removal nor
rapid fragment departure follows from enzyme specificity. A four-rN tract
inside a longer duplex also does not establish efficient cutting of a short
standalone recognition hybrid. The primary source data acquired here do
not establish the proposed adapter's cut distribution or remote-admission
kinetics. Captured short-tag cleavage c_E must be measured/acquired for this
substrate, not declared equal to c_A or zero from a chosen toehold length.

The joint-chemistry gate is PARTLY ACQUIRED, rather than generically
incompatible. Del Grosso et al.2022 directly demonstrate RNA-invader TMSD
plus RNase-H reset and a downstream capture/output cascade. The actual SI
printedS6 specifies a Tris/Mg/Na medium, with the basic reset measured at
45C and the cascade at37C. Its completed-invasion lumped graph omits our
early-capture state. Its cleavage-rate unit/concentration convention is
not transferred. MainXML and the actual SI are cached; root independently
acquired/read the SI. The Cabello dimer kinetics include25C experiments;
the specific copying/trimer setting uses30C and high-salt TAE. Transfer
to that HMSD setting is a real reimplementation gate, not an impossibility
deduced merely from EDTA or temperature differences.

An actual substrate-level constraint is also acquired. Lee et al.2022
compare RNA:DNA hybrids with dsDNA flanks and3'/5' DNA overhangs. Their
3' ssDNA overhang supports processive RNA removal, while filled/no-overhang
and5' variants are distributive. Thus an admission step that FILLS such a
3' overhang can remove an enzyme-favorable feature, rather than activate
it. This is an adverse conditional prediction for a matching local layout;
the proposed remote adapter can have a three-way junction not present in
those linear substrates, so the source does not supply our c_E/c_A ratio.
Their FRET clearance is not assumed to prove a chemically zero-rN stub.

The cycle must count RNA/adaptor preparation, hydrolytic products by actual
species, released input locks, enzyme occupancy/loss, any medium/feed and
product clearance. The template must remain chemically intact and reusable.
It must also preserve the desired copied/translated output, rather than
trade that output away for an easier release measurement. Strong baselines
remain unmodified HMSD, the source's putative longer-template mechanism,
cycling-probe chemistry and complete enzymatic/staged templated synthesis.

Decision at this checkpoint: the flexible-linker realization has an explicit
negative gate; the erasure alternative has a real incumbent actuator and a
literal topology, but no acquired complete copying/assembly operation or
performance advantage. A favorable kinetic graph alone does not close
buffer, substrate, fragment, identity and turnover interfaces.
