# Binary pair-rest-length coupling: a finite-amplitude representation and acquisition gate

24 October 2026. Frozen after root full consequential read, with bounded independent algebra, transport, source, and energy checks. Accepted scope: the exact balanced periodic binary/two-network model, its interface-density bound and macroscopic bulk benchmark, six-cell negative witness, explicit controlled model exchanges/mechanical path, and zero-temperature passive retention. This is not external certification, a measured alloy, a physical atom-exchange protocol, a thermal lifetime, a novelty conclusion, or a hardware advantage.

## 1. The missing operation being resolved

The preceding registry theorem used a continuous composition and a prescribed quartic chemical free energy. That is a legitimate mesoscopic model, but it did not establish that literal binary species with local conserved moves can supply its rest-length coupling and a finite-amplitude patterned state. A mathematical linear instability of a continuous field cannot answer that binary representation question.

We therefore retain binary labels, a conventional pair-dependent natural length, nearest-neighbor chemical preference, two mechanical networks, and finite registry. No atomic quartic free energy is introduced. We prove compatible protective and negative-trial inequalities directly, then exhibit a finite sequence of allowed model exchanges and bounded mechanical controls that reaches the protected sublevel. The physical realization of the exchange events remains separate.

A passive binary elastic/registry model is selected over a driven reaction pattern because it tests the particular conservative representation gap directly. Ordinary competing Ising interactions and binary Frenkel--Kontorova models are strong incumbents. This is not a claim to have invented finite-domain binary matter.

## 2. Complete local binary energy and specimen

Take N sites on a periodic ring of reference length L=Nh, with N a multiple of six. Labels sigma_i are exactly plus or minus one, with sum sigma_i=0. They represent neutral species in the model; changing their position does not change their number. Define the symmetric pair mismatch

\[
m_i=\frac m2(\sigma_i+\sigma_{i+1}),\qquad 0<m<1.
\tag{1}
\]

The upper bond's preferred length is h(1+m_i), positive for all label pairs. This is the symmetric linear Vegard pair rule: plus/plus, mixed, and minus/minus prefer h(1+m), h, and h(1-m), respectively. Instantaneous bond positivity is a separate admissibility issue; positive preferred lengths alone do not forbid crossing in a harmonic model.

Retain full unwrapped periodic displacements u_i,v_i of both networks and the energy

\[
\mathcal E(\sigma,u,v)=
h\sum_i\left[
 \frac C2(D_hu_i-m_i)^2+\frac D2(D_hv_i)^2
 +V(u_i-v_i)\right]
-J\sum_i\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1},
\tag{2}
\]

where D_hz_i=(z_(i+1)-z_i)/h, C,D,J are positive, and

\[
V(w)=V_0[1-\cos(\beta w)],\quad
\beta=2\pi/d,\quad K=\beta^2V_0,\quad
S=\frac{CD}{C+D},\quad \xi^2=S/K.
\tag{3}
\]

Reference bond connectivity, total species inventory, length, and zero winding are fixed. A common rigid translation is irrelevant. The two-network energy is the literal model: V(u_i-v_i) is a relative node-paired registry coupling. An external spatial substrate cos(2pi x_i/d), x_i=ih+u_i, would require h/d integer to reduce to V(u_i), or else the actual site-dependent substrate phases must be retained. No such shortcut is used in (2).

With r_i=(C u_i+D v_i)/(C+D) and w_i=u_i-v_i, the exact identity is

\[
\frac C2(D_hu_i-m_i)^2+\frac D2(D_hv_i)^2
=\frac{C+D}{2}\left(D_hr_i-\frac C{C+D}m_i\right)^2
 +\frac S2(D_hw_i-m_i)^2.
\tag{4}
\]

Since sum m_i=m sum sigma_i=0, the common square can be relaxed to zero with periodic r. It is retained, nonnegative, whenever the actual mechanics is not relaxed.

## 3. Exact interface bound, including arbitrary registry hops

Use the following self-contained constants and corrector:

\[
q(w)=\sqrt{2S V(w)},\quad
m_c=\frac1{Sd}\int_0^d q(w)dw=\frac4\pi\sqrt{V_0/S},
\quad R'(w)=q(w)-S m_c,
\tag{5}
\]

with bounded d-periodic R. Its derivative is globally Lipschitz with Lq=sqrt(SK). Set

\[
\nu_h=h\sqrt{K/S}=h/\xi,\qquad
r_h=\frac{\sqrt{\nu_h^2+4}-\nu_h}{2}.
\tag{6}
\]

The exact finite-hop calibration from the frozen registry derivation is

\[
\frac S{2h}(\Delta-h\mu)^2+hV(w)
\ge\frac{hS}{2}\mu^2+
 \frac\mu{m_c}[R(w+\Delta)-R(w)],
\qquad |\mu|\le m_c r_h.
\tag{7}
\]

For clarity, it follows from the global Taylor remainder
|Delta R-R'(w)Delta|<=Lq Delta^2/2, followed by completion with
S_eff=S-h |mu|Lq/mc. The condition (|mu|/mc)^2+nu_h |mu|/mc<=1 makes the remainder nonnegative. It is not a continuum kink-cost substitution and holds for any Delta, including multiple registry periods.

Assume

\[
0<m<m_c r_h,\qquad 0<J\le h S m^2/2.
\tag{8}
\]

For aligned labels the non-corrector contribution in (7) minus the chemical bond is h S m^2/2-J>=0. For unlike labels it is J>=0. Summing the corrector gives

\[
\sum_i\frac{m_i}{m_c}[R(w_{i+1})-R(w_i)]
=\sum_i\frac{m_{i-1}-m_i}{m_c}R(w_i).
\]

Furthermore

\[
\sum_i|m_i-m_{i-1}|
\le m\sum_i|\sigma_{i+1}-\sigma_i|.
\tag{9}
\]

Thus the full, possibly nonrelaxed energy obeys

\[
\mathcal E\ge-C_b\,\operatorname{TV}(\sigma),\qquad
C_b=\frac{m\|R\|_\infty}{m_c},\quad
\operatorname{TV}(\sigma)=\sum_i|\sigma_{i+1}-\sigma_i|=2N_{\rm wall}.
\tag{10}
\]

There is no bound on slip number or local relative displacement. Chemical segregation inside a registry defect is part of the binary configuration being bounded.

The useful particular choice

\[
J=h S m^2/4
\tag{11}
\]

makes the non-corrector baseline exactly J for both bond types, hence strengthens (10) to

\[
\boxed{\quad \mathcal E\ge N J-C_b\operatorname{TV}(\sigma).
\quad}
\tag{12}
\]

The additive energy convention is fixed by (2). Its invariant content is the gap to the extensive benchmark NJ: adding the same constant to every state shifts benchmark and trial together.

Any thermodynamic sequence with Nwall/L tending to zero therefore has limiting lower energy density at least J/h. Balanced macroscopic plus/minus domains attain this bulk value: take w=0 at a registry minimum and the periodic common strain in (4), with at most a finite number of label walls. In fact at (11) their relaxed energy is NJ exactly. The bound establishes the macroscopic-sequence bulk benchmark, not a finite-L minimizer or a claim that every mixed morphology is excluded.

## 4. A six-cell witness proves compatibility

On six consecutive sites choose

\[
\sigma=(+,+,+,-,-,-),\qquad
m_i=(m,m,0,-m,-m,0),
\tag{13}
\]

and

\[
u_i=w_i^*=hm(-1,0,1,1,0,-1),\qquad v_i=0.
\tag{14}
\]

The cyclic wraparound is included. Every upper bond has D_hu_i=m_i, so both elastic terms in (2) vanish, with positive instantaneous lengths h(1+m_i). There are two unlike chemical bonds, sum sigma_i sigma_(i+1)=2, and the mean squared relative displacement is 2h^2m^2/3. Since V(w)<=K w^2/2,

\[
\frac{\mathcal E_*}{6h}
\le-\frac J{3h}+\frac K3 h^2m^2.
\tag{15}
\]

At (11), this is strictly negative if

\[
\boxed{\quad h/\xi<1/2,\qquad
0<m<\min\{1,m_c r_h\}.\quad}
\tag{16}
\]

These conditions are compatible: choose the positive mechanical/registry parameters with h/xi<1/2, then a positive m within the stated interval, and J from (11). No numerical fixture or parameter grid is required. The exact trial energy is 4h V0[1-cos(beta hm)]-2J; the displayed upper bound suffices without a small-angle approximation.

Repeating this six-cell witness on rings with N a multiple of six preserves composition, winding, material parameters, and its negative density. It lies strictly below the macroscopic bulk benchmark J/h in the same specimen contract. This is an entirely finite-amplitude comparison; no continuous-composition linear instability or quartic expansion is used.

## 5. Model acquisition: three exchanges plus a paid finite mechanical path

The alternating six-site stock (+,-,+,-,+,-) has m_i=0 and, at full clamp u=v=0, energy 6J. This is supplied non-equilibrium stock, not free chemical input. Three adjacent model exchanges reach the target while conserving labels within each six-site block:

\[
(+-+-+-)\ \xrightarrow{(2,3)}\ (++--+-)
\ \xrightarrow{(4,5)}\ (++-+--)
\ \xrightarrow{(3,4)}\ (+++---),
\tag{17}
\]

where indices are one-based. A swap of unlike adjacent labels leaves their own mixed pair unchanged and changes both outer pairs. Both their natural lengths and chemical bonds must be charged. The registry potential does not change at fixed u,v.

It would be incorrect to hold w=0 and silently re-relax the common strain for free. We instead clamp the literal full u=v=0 during the exchanges. Then

\[
\mathcal E(\sigma,0,0)
=\frac{NhC m^2}{4}
 +B_C\sum_i\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1},\qquad
B_C=\frac{hC m^2}{4}-J
=\frac{h(C-S)m^2}{4}>0.
\tag{18}
\]

The four sums along (17) are -6,-2,-2,+2. The three endpoint-energy costs are therefore 4BC,0,4BC, totalling 2h(C-S)m^2 per six-cell block. These are model transition-energy changes. They do not price an unrepresented physical activation barrier, exchange time, vacancy, or atom-passing route.

With labels now fixed, use the smooth finite path u_i(t)=s(t)w_i^*, v_i(t)=0, with s increasing from zero to one and vanishing endpoint velocity. Its exact full energy is

\[
\mathcal E(s)=2h C m^2(1-s)^2
 +4h V_0[1-\cos(\beta hm s)]-2J.
\tag{19}
\]

Local forces can enforce this path in the declared overdamped model. With eta_ui=h^(-1) partial E/partial u_i, the required force density is eta_ui+Gamma_u udot_i, and analogously for v; the latter may hold nonzero forces at zero displacement. All forces are finite along this finite six-cell path. No common-strain elimination is hidden in the preparation.

The signed mechanical control work is exactly

\[
W_{\rm mech}=\mathcal E(1)-\mathcal E(0)
 +4\Gamma_u h^3m^2\int\dot s(t)^2dt.
\tag{20}
\]

The finite path therefore reaches the strictly negative energy (15), with its viscous loss paid. A slow path lowers this modeled loss but supplies no claim of finite-rate hardware cost or free force authority. The separate positive and recovered control work, clamps, and readout belong to a real device ledger.

Equation (17) is reachability in an explicitly permitted local exchange state graph, not a continuous atomic trajectory. Literal strictly one-dimensional ordered atoms cannot generally pass one another merely because a Monte Carlo identity-swap operator is allowed. Vacancies, interstitials, transverse paths, internal-state transfer, or a specified chemical reaction would have to provide actual exchanges and pay their intermediates. This construction does not invent such a material mechanism.

## 6. Passive retention and the finite-temperature boundary

Between swaps let full mechanics obey

\[
\Gamma_u\dot u_i=-h^{-1}\partial_{u_i}\mathcal E,
\qquad
\Gamma_v\dot v_i=-h^{-1}\partial_{v_i}\mathcal E.
\tag{21}
\]

Then Edot=-h sum(Gamma_u udot_i^2+Gamma_v vdot_i^2)<=0. A zero-temperature Kawasaki rule may accept an adjacent unlike-label exchange only if its full energy change at the current u,v is nonpositive. It conserves total label count, and every accepted event lowers or preserves the same energy. Energy released by a model jump goes to the implicit bath; no momentum impulse or finite atomic activation trajectory is supplied by that rule.

Once preparation reaches E/L<=-q0<0, (12) gives on every subsequent such passive path

\[
\frac{N_{\rm wall}}L\ge\frac{J/h+q_0}{2C_b}>0.
\tag{22}
\]

This does not prove that passive dynamics starting in arbitrary stock will assemble the witness, that every exchange is kinetically accessible, that the eventual state is unique, or that a chosen period is selected.

At positive temperature a detailed-balance Metropolis exchange accepts an uphill proposal with factor exp(-Delta E/(kBT)); mechanical noise must also satisfy fluctuation-dissipation. Neither energy monotonicity nor the all-time pathwise bound (22) survives that replacement automatically. Thermal equilibrium/interface statistics, activation barriers, and a useful finite lifetime are separate unproved questions.

The closed isothermal model ledger is

\[
\Delta\mathcal E=W_{\rm mechanical}+W_{\rm exchange}
 +W_{\rm parameter}-D_{\rm continuous}-D_{\rm jumps}.
\tag{23}
\]

The preparation consumes the alternating stock's chemical/frustration energy as well as any supplied positive control work. A cycle that returns stock, both networks, registry/winding, parameters, and controller state must pay the total losses. Neither pattern retention nor a negative signed preparation work establishes a work source or a cheap reset.

## 7. Strong incumbents and the remaining physical gate

Patrykiejew--Sokolowski (2014) already represents the broad binary-elastic-registry wall mechanism computationally. Its one-dimensional finite FK toy has pair-specific rest lengths/stiffnesses and species-dependent cosine registry; its wall composition/placement are parameterized rather than the exact fully annealed model (2). Its two-dimensional Lennard--Jones/graphite canonical Monte Carlo moves atoms and exchanges species identities at fixed composition, and produces composition-dependent walls and finite commensurate domains. It therefore defeats a broad new binary-wall mechanism claim. Its simulations are not a measured Ar/Kr exchange operation or a demonstrated version of (16).

Fratzl--Penrose (1995) supplies binary chemical preference, local elastic springs and Kawasaki-type diffusion in coherent phase separation, without this periodic registry. Ordinary nearest-neighbor ferromagnetic Ising at fixed half composition minimizes to two walls; conventional frustrated/competing Ising models can instead stabilize finite blocks by adding a competing interaction. The relative mechanical network and registry in (2) are precisely extra energy resources and must not be hidden in an equal-resource comparison. No superiority over an optimized conventional competing-interaction model is proved.

The exact result closes the fictitious-quartic/binary compatibility gap for one conservative finite-state/mechanical setting. It does not calibrate a real composition-dependent force law, produce an actual conserved exchange route, establish finite-temperature protection, or show an advantage over the strong conventional ancestors. Both networks, registry interaction and support, transport mechanism, admissible strain/contact domain, and preparation/reset controls remain in the physical inventory.
