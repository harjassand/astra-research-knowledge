Ordinary density separation is the strongest simplification for admissible Al
23 October 2026; Capital read-only revision 0451552424b4679fa9a73faf5849ea5a4af21cf6

Decision. For one-sided acceptance, the Al powder endpoint does not require an
individual polarizability, mass or volume estimate. A calibrated density gate is
an equally informed conventional comparator. No source checked instantiates a
30-micrometre Al, 1%-closed-void, per-output-grain certificate. Do not label the
following Archimedes reduction a new separation technology.

The operation and proof. Let m be unchanged dry particle mass, Vm metal volume,
Vt the relevant filled-particle volume, and Vx the volume excluded from a bath.
Assume Vm >= (1-eta)m/rho_max, closed-void fraction phi <= 1-Vm/Vt, and Vx >= Vt.
The last relation allows accessible surface pores to fill; additional unwetted
open pores or trapped gas only increase Vx. A freely sinking particle in a
stationary bath of density rho_f has m/Vx > rho_f. Therefore

  phi <= 1 - (1-eta)m/(rho_max Vx)
       < 1 - (1-eta)rho_f/rho_max.

Use actual covered bounds rho_f,L, eta_U and rho_max,U. The sufficient setpoint
is rho_f,L >= rho_max,U(1-phi*)/(1-eta_U). Ideal pure Al with rho_max=2700 kg/m3,
eta=0 and phi*=0.01 gives 2673 kg/m3. A justified material upper density bound
is required for validity; a material lower density bound helps only with yield.
The figure 2700 is an assumption, not a measured upper bound for EN AC-43000.

This gate covers accumulated sub-resolution closed pores, unlike an unqualified
segmented-voxel count. It does not distinguish closed and unwetted open pores;
that distinction is unnecessary for conservative acceptance. Fluid entry into a
nominally closed cavity, dissolution, uptake, shell fracture, coating change or
unaccounted nonmetal mass can change the particle or violate the volume/mass
premises. Neither a bulk alloy assay nor a mean oxide thickness establishes the
maximum per-particle premises.

What was physically measured in primary sources, and what was not:

* Horn, 2025 TUM dissertation, Powder Cross-Contamination in Laser Powder Bed
  Fusion of Metals, section 6.4.2, Table 31, printed p118, used a few grams of
  EN AC-43000 Al alloy and CW106C Cu powders in nominal 3.1-g/cm3 sodium
  polytungstate (SPT), in a 150-mL valved vessel. After one hour Al floated and
  Cu sank. SEM-EDX measured 1 particle-% foreign material in the top Al fraction
  and 33 particle-% in the bottom fraction; collection by draining contaminated
  the latter. This demonstrates real powder/bath handling, not a near-Al-density
  gate, target-size bin, closed-void measurement, density uncertainty, singlet
  coverage or before/after surface/mass preservation. At 3.1 Al cannot pass the
  proposed SINK gate. https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/doc/1767227/document.pdf
  Metadata: https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/node?id=1767227

Finite acquisition. Downward LAB displacement is not a buoyancy-sign assay.
An upward-floating grain can move downward through startup momentum or a
downward fluid flow, indefinitely in the latter case. A sufficient late-window
test is v_observed > u_U + H(T) + E, where u_U bounds downward fluid velocity,
H(T) bounds all startup/history slip when terminal slip is nonpositive, and E
bounds readout error. In a validated scalar linear-drag model, H(T)=B exp(-T/
tau_max) follows from |v0-v_inf|<=B and tau<=tau_max. These envelopes must be
acquired; they are not proved by Stokes' formula or two late images. Reject an
ambiguous grain. Conditional per-grain false-acceptance bounds can be summed
for a lot without independence, but a useful accepted yield remains separate.

There is no uniform finite pass time at the threshold: under ideal sphere
Stokes drag, v_inf=g d^2 rho_s(phi*-phi)/(18 mu). For assumed d=30 micrometres,
rho_s=2700 and mu=0.01 Pa s, dense Al moves 1.324 micrometres/s in the ideal bath,
whereas phi=0.0099 moves only 0.01324 micrometres/s. A 1-mm drift then takes
about 12.6 min or 21.0 h respectively. These are model scales, not measured SPT
viscosity or achieved rates. A density margin and validated nuisance floor are
necessary for a finite useful-yield promise.

Aggregation is a max-tail failure. Equal exterior volumes of dense Al and a
grain with 1.5% closed void have average density 2679.75 kg/m3 and can co-sink
at 2673, carrying the bad constituent into the accepted output. If the endpoint
instead defines the permanent aggregate as one particle, its averaged porosity
is a different endpoint. Output-grain singulation/deaggregation and collection
must match the certificate's units; a successful bulk class split is not a
maximum-grain guarantee.

Matched imaging. Du Plessis et al. 2018 actually image small metal powder with
0.7-micrometre voxels and about 3-h scans, resolving 3-D open/closed morphology;
no arbitrary sub-voxel pore-volume upper bound or 1% Al coverage is supplied.
Jaenisch et al. 2020 demonstrate one-projection radiographic pores on 100-150-
micrometre MnFePSi, with overlap/orientation misses; the quoted smaller-particle
visibility limits are simulations. A single spherical 1% void in 30-micrometre
Al would be 6.46 micrometres across, while 1% split among thousands of smaller
voids need not be individually resolved. The density gate sacrifices morphology
and requires a compatible bath and per-particle material bounds; imaging returns
morphology but needs sub-resolution and contrast bounds. Neither checked source
achieves the common maximum-grain endpoint.

The retained electric alternative is an ideal interface identity only. Fixed
orientation/charge, exact affine free-space E=e+Gx and linear finite-body response
give J=dF/dx=qG+G alpha G. Cyclic traceless diagonal G_i satisfy sum G_i^2=2g^2 I,
so sum Tr(J_i/m)=2g^2 Tr(alpha)/m. A fixed invertible linear position readout
preserves the trace; constant offsets/permanent dipoles cancel. RF secular
frequencies are Floquet observables and cannot simply be substituted for J/m.
Orientation changes can cause severe negative bias. Take a prolate outer
conductor with depolarization factors (2/5,2/5,1/5), hence alpha/(epsilon0 V)
eigenvalues (5/2,5/2,5), and a shielded 2% cavity. If its long axis has cosine
4/5 to each probe's null direction in that probe's distinct orientation, the
apparent trace coefficient is 177/20 rather than 10. The executable gives a
spurious phi upper bound 1/295 (0.339%), below 1%. This is outside the identity's
fixed-orientation premise and shows why that premise must be acquired.
At g=1e9 V/m2, ideal Al gives sum trace about 5.9e4 s^-2; this is curvature,
requiring displacement/response rather than a uniform-field force. No matched
advantage over density separation or imaging emerged, so it is not preferred.
