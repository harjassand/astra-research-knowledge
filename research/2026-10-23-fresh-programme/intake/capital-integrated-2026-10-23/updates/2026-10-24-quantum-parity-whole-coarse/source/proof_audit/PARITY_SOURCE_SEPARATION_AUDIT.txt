Verdict

The pinned construction and weighted mass separation are valid. The
collective-mask quantifier survives arbitrary exact nonnegative covers
and commuting ancillary kernels. The precise conclusion is a lower
bound on sqrt(G_A G_B), hence on their MAXIMUM, not on each individually.
The four-branch resource is valid for the explicitly parity-dependent
fine family. Arbitrary coarse-label-dependent fine refinements retain
the general domination construction but need not admit this compression.

This separates exact fine-table reconstruction representations and their
one-sided mass certificates. It proves neither a positive-gap repetition
improvement nor an unavoidable physical/Jensen/conditional-QRE error for
every use of a mask representation. The draft explicitly withholds a
general game-level consequence and overall sampler resource bounds.

1. Actual source instrument and its conventions

Let t>=5, r=1/sqrt(t), eta fixed in (0,1), d=1+eta. The weights w,z are
strictly positive product distributions. Write beta=1-gamma; then alpha
and beta are independent Bernoulli(r) strings before the tested event.
The mask forbids alpha_i=beta_i=1 at every tested coordinate.

For e=(-1)^|alpha| and f=(-1)^|beta|, both unconditioned means equal
  m=(1-2r)^t>0.
Thus k_e=(e-m)/(1+m) has weighted mean zero and |k_e|<=1. With
sigma=I_2/sqrt(2), the proposed effects
  F_u^alpha=w_alpha(I+eta k_e S_u),
  H_v^gamma=z_gamma(I+eta k_f T_v)
are strictly positive complete POVMs. Their actual one-party probabilities
are exactly w_alpha,z_gamma because S_u,T_v are traceless. The common
references w_alpha I,z_gamma I dominate them with factors d,d.

Directions X and Z at distinct fresh inputs give noncommuting original
coarse effects. Positive refinements sqrt(F) P sqrt(F) are legitimate
for every local fine POVM P. Choosing a Y fine POVM also makes these fine
effects noncommute with the corresponding X/Z coarse effect. No hidden
joint sharp measurement or replacement disturbance channel is assumed.

The displayed H,T,Q are RIGHT/OPPOSITE matrices. Their physical Bob
operators are transposed in the fixed Schmidt basis. This convention is
essential even when S,T use only real X,Z but the fine Q uses Y. Treating
a displayed Y as its physical untransposed Bob effect would change signs.
The pinned draft states the correct convention.

This is a genuine finite joint-string strategy at a fixed tested set C:
ignore its actual tested question values, use the displayed full unchecked
own string to select S or T, and output the coarse string and local fine
string according to the displayed joint POVM. No cross-input consistency
or one-round tensor-product assumption is required of a repeated strategy.
Compatibility or controlled radii for EVERY other selected C is a further
condition, not established by this fixed-C construction.

2. Exact probability formulas and normalization

For one coordinate, direct summation over the three allowed pairs gives
  E(theta)=1-r^2,
  E(theta (-1)^alpha)=E(theta (-1)^beta)=(1-r)^2,
  E(theta (-1)^(alpha+beta))=(1-r)(1-3r).
Independent tested coordinates therefore give
  a=(1-r^2)^t, b=(1-r)^(2t), c=[(1-r)(1-3r)]^t,
  Z=(c-2mb+m^2 a)/(1+m)^2.
Here Z=E(theta k_e k_f), so |Z|<=a. The coarse joint probability is
  w_alpha z_gamma[1+eta^2 k_e k_f Tr(S_u T_v)/2],
and consequently the original accepted probability is EXACTLY
  p_(u,v)=a+eta^2 Z Tr(S_u T_v)/2.

Under independent uniform m_fresh-bit input strings, with m_fresh>=1
and X/Z chosen by own input parity, the directions are independently
balanced; their mean overlap is 1/2. Thus p=a+(eta^2/2)Z as claimed.
For arbitrary fresh laws the average overlap replaces 1/2. The uniform
bounds (1-eta^2)a<=p_(u,v)<=(1+eta^2)a hold. Since a=(1-1/t)^t>=1/4,
the accepted mass is bounded away from zero for every FIXED eta<1.
The constants are not uniform as eta tends to one.

3. Four shared branches are an exact semantic quotient

Fourier inversion of the accepted parity character moments gives
  W_(e,f)=[a+(e+f)b+ef c]/4.
These are nonnegative because they are the sums of theta w_alpha z_gamma
over the corresponding parity classes, and their sum is a. Thus
pi_(e,f)=W_(e,f)/a is a valid distribution, including at t values where
c is negative. No positivity approximation is being made.

For the specified fine family, remove the factors w_alpha,z_gamma from
the square roots. The remaining fine operator depends only on own parity:
  A_(u,e)^a=L_(u,e) P_(u,e)^a L_(u,e),
  B_(v,f)^b=M_(v,f) Q_(v,f)^b M_(v,f),
where L^2=I+eta k_e S_u and M^2=I+eta k_f T_v. Hence the entire original
accepted fine subtable equals
  T_(u,v)^(a,b)=sum_(e,f) W_(e,f) chi(A_(u,e)^a,B_(v,f)^b).

An explicit positive standard-form replacement vector is the 8x8 matrix
  sigma_new=direct_sum_(e,f) sqrt(pi_(e,f)) I_2/sqrt(2).
Its ordinary Hilbert-Schmidt norm is one. Alice's effects are the direct
sum over e,f of A_(u,e)^a/d, and Bob's opposite effects are the direct
sum of B_(v,f)^b/d. These depend only on own input and the shared branch.
Their sums are <=I because I+eta k_e S_u<=d I, and failure complements
are positive. They form one complete all-input local POVM family.

The expectation on sigma_new is T/(d^2 a) entrywise. Thus lambda=d^2 a
is correct, including when lambda<1. The physical state can be a shared
classical pair (e,f) plus Phi+, or its displayed coherent label version.
All used effects are block diagonal, so both give the same fine table.
No old alpha,gamma table is claimed preserved by this quotient.

Tracelessness gives a stronger exact mass calculation: for EVERY input
and selected branch, each local success probability is 1/d. Therefore
  G_A=G_B=lambda/d=d a.
The simultaneous-success probability is p_(u,v)/(d^2 a), and its lower
bound (1-eta)/(1+eta) is correct. Individual failures remain actual POVM
outcomes. The construction does not let separated players detect joint
success or repeat until joint success without the source's charged
reconstruction protocol.

If arbitrary positive fine refinements depend on the full alpha/gamma
rather than parity, the general reference-POVM construction still works
with the same exact masses. The four-branch reduction is then unavailable
without further argument; an accepted reference history can require its
original 2t stored label bits. The pinned theorem imposes the needed
fine parity restriction explicitly.

4. Universal weighted-mask bound, including collective kernels

This paragraph concerns an INDEPENDENT ancillary exact mask kernel:
effects 0<=E_alpha,B_gamma<=I, commuting across players, in a state omega,
with lambda omega(E_alpha B_gamma)=theta(alpha,gamma). In the GNS Hilbert
space define x_alpha=E_alpha Omega, y_gamma=B_gamma Omega. Their inner
products are theta/lambda, and
  ||x_alpha||^2=omega(E_alpha^2)<=omega(E_alpha),
  ||y_gamma||^2<=omega(B_gamma).
This holds also for infinite-dimensional commuting ancillas. The finite
weighted matrix factorization and Schatten/Frobenius inequality give
  ||diag(sqrt(w)) theta diag(sqrt(z))||_*
    <=lambda sqrt[(sum_alpha w_alpha omega(E_alpha))
                  (sum_gamma z_gamma omega(B_gamma))]
    =sqrt(G_A G_B).
The last equality uses the original source's EXACT marginals w,z and
the independent ancillary tensor construction. No tensorization of the
ANCILLARY kernel, input-by-input dimension bound, or product rectangle
cover is assumed.

For an arbitrary exact nonnegative classical cover theta=sum_j lambda_j
e_j f_j^T, the same conclusion follows directly by rank-one nuclear norm,
e_j^2<=e_j, f_j^2<=f_j and Cauchy-Schwarz over j. It also follows by putting
all cover components into one shared classical ancillary state. Covers
that merely overcount accepted entries, approximate kernels, negative
coefficients, or state-dependent whole-table replacements are different
interfaces and are not silently included in this quantifier.

For one L factor the weighted matrix is
  [[sqrt(r(1-r)),1-r],[0,sqrt(r(1-r))]].
Its squared trace norm is Frobenius norm squared plus twice its determinant:
  (1-r^2)+2r(1-r)=1+2r-3r^2.
Singular values tensorize, so the t-factor norm is exactly
  nu_t=[sqrt(1+2r-3r^2)]^t.
For r=t^(-1/2), log(nu_t)=sqrt(t)-5/2+O(t^(-1/2)). Thus every exact
conjunctive kernel has max(G_A,G_B)>=nu_t, whereas the new family has
G_A=G_B=d a=O(1). Collective classical covers cannot remove this lower
bound. Their known two/three-copy cap improvements concern a different
uniform optimization and do not contradict it.

5. What resource and entropy conclusions actually follow

The replacement has local Hilbert dimension <=8, or one qubit each plus
two shared classical bits. Four explicitly specified real probabilities
depend on t. This counts the physical state/register dimension, not
precision bits, input reading, description of arbitrary full-input fine
POVMs, or the complete downstream R/H/S construction. The concrete binary
fine choice has a compact description; generic fine families may not.

The original coarse instrument also admits the stated compact sequential
implementation: first use binary parity effects
  p_e(I+eta k_e S_u), p_e=(1+e m)/2,
then sample alpha from w conditional on parity e. Its individual Kraus
operator is sqrt(w_alpha) sqrt(I+eta k_e S_u)=sqrt(F_u^alpha).
Ordinary rejection sampling uses O(t) Bernoulli draws in expectation
because both p_e are bounded away from zero. Exact-real probabilities
are mathematical data; no finite exact gate/bit model has been supplied.

There is also a distinct elementary resource boundary: rank(theta)=2^t.
A finite-dimensional exact tensor mask kernel on local dimensions D_A,D_B
has correlation-matrix rank <=min(D_A^2,D_B^2). Thus total local register
dimension (INCLUDING classical labels) must be >=2^(t/2). If K shared
classical components are counted separately and each quantum local
dimension is <=D, the correct tradeoff is K D^2>=2^t, or b+2q>=t.
Classical exact covers require >=2^t components by ordinary matrix rank.
This is not an entropy lower bound and does not force quantum qubits
when arbitrarily large shared classical labels are treated as free.

The mass inequality is a LOWER bound on the actual mask masses and hence
on the sufficient budget log(max(G_A,G_B)/p). It is NOT a lower bound on
actual entropy-telescope differences, conditional-QRE charges, Jensen
gaps, physical branch error, or final strategy loss. At the eta=0 boundary
the original coarse effects are fresh-input-independent and all their
source Jensen gaps vanish despite the identical weighted-mask mass
lower bound. This diagnoses why such a stronger inference is invalid.

For fixed eta the new mass/referee certificate is O(1/m_fresh), while the
uniform actual-mass certificate of every exact conjunctive mask lift is
at least Omega(sqrt(t)/m_fresh). This is a rigorous separation of those
certificates plus an explicit fine-table rebuild. The L game itself has
value one. No positive-gap unrestricted strategy family, greedy-selected
history invariant, quantum-versus-classical rate advantage, or general
repetition improvement is established by this example.
