EXACT PARITY FAMILY: CONSTANT RESOURCE VS A GROWING MASK COST
2026-10-24. passive_lag. Internal construction; novelty UNKNOWN.

Claim and exact scope

There is an explicit family of actual finite local coarse/fine POVMs,
with arbitrarily long tested history, noncommuting fresh-input dependence,
and original tested-event mass bounded away from zero, for which:
  (i) every conjunctive ancillary mask lift has one-sided mass at least
      exp(Theta(sqrt(t)));
  (ii) a whole-coarse local replacement preserves EVERY accepted live
      fine entry, with masses at most 1+eta;
  (iii) for the fine family specified here, the replacement uses only
      a Bell pair and TWO shared classical bits, independently of t.

This is a source/event reconstruction result. It is NOT a positive-gap
game repetition theorem, a generic answer-entropy removal theorem, or
a finite-time implementation of the entire R/H/S sampler. The L mask
alone has value one. The independent classical tensor-cap correction
remains in force: block covers beat the supplied quantum product L lift;
no asymptotic quantum-vs-classical rate advantage is established there.

1. Original actual instrument family

Fix t>=5, r=t^(-1/2), 0<eta<1, and let
  w_alpha=product_i (1-r)^(1-alpha_i) r^(alpha_i),
  z_gamma=product_i r^(1-gamma_i) (1-r)^(gamma_i),
where alpha,gamma are t-bit strings. Use the tested mask
  theta(alpha,gamma)=product_i 1{not(alpha_i=1,gamma_i=0)}
                   =L^(tensor t)(alpha,gamma).

Write
  A(alpha)=(-1)^(number of ones in alpha),
  B(gamma)=(-1)^(number of zeros in gamma),
  m_t=(1-2r)^t,
  k_e=(e-m_t)/(1+m_t), e in {+1,-1}.
Then E_w A=E_z B=m_t, so E_w k_A=E_z k_B=0; |k_e|<=1.

Let the original shared resource be Phi+, represented by sigma=I_2/sqrt(2)
with ordinary matrix trace. Let u,v denote full unchecked own questions.
Choose traceless Hermitian Pauli directions S_u,T_v of norm one, using
at least X and Z across different inputs. Define

  F_u^alpha=w_alpha[I+eta k_(A(alpha)) S_u],
  H_v^gamma=z_gamma[I+eta k_(B(gamma)) T_v].                  (P1)

Both families are POVMs because their weighted perturbations sum to zero.
They are positive and have actual one-party probabilities exactly w,z.
Across fresh inputs their effects need not commute. Bob's displayed
operators are right/opposite representatives; physical Bob operators
are their transposes in the fixed Schmidt basis.

For each e, choose ANY finite local fine POVM P_(u,e)^a and, for each f,
ANY Q_(v,f)^b, on the qubit. They can vary noncommutatively with input.
For example, with S_u=X or Z use a rank-one Y fine POVM, which does not
commute with the coarse effect. Set

  G_u^(alpha,a)=sqrt(F_u^alpha) P_(u,A(alpha))^a sqrt(F_u^alpha),
  K_v^(gamma,b)=sqrt(H_v^gamma) Q_(v,B(gamma))^b sqrt(H_v^gamma). (P2)

These are complete actual joint coarse/fine POVMs. a,b may designate
entire unchecked answer strings; a fixed small fine POVM can output its
bit in prescribed positions if a concrete finite family is wanted.
Only the ORIGINAL fine dependence through parity is assumed here.

2. Exact tested-event probability, including its fresh-input dependence

Let
  a_t=(1-r^2)^t,
  b_t=(1-r)^(2t),
  c_t=[(1-r)(1-3r)]^t,
  Z_t=[c_t-2m_t b_t+m_t^2 a_t]/(1+m_t)^2.                   (P3)

The per-coordinate accepted weights for (alpha_i,gamma_i)=(00),(01),(11)
are r(1-r),(1-r)^2,r(1-r). Their accepted parity moments are respectively
  E(theta)=1-r^2,
  E(theta A_i)=E(theta B_i)=(1-r)^2,
  E(theta A_i B_i)=(1-r)(1-3r).
Independence across tested positions proves (P3), with
  Z_t=E_(w,z)[theta k_A k_B].

Since chi(F_u^alpha,H_v^gamma)
  =w_alpha z_gamma[1+eta^2 k_A k_B Tr(S_u T_v)/2],
the EXACT original accepted mass at EVERY full fresh pair is

  p_(u,v)=a_t+eta^2 Z_t Tr(S_u T_v)/2.                       (P4)

Under any specified fresh joint law, p=a_t+eta^2 Z_t rho_bar, where
rho_bar=E Tr(S_uT_v)/2. For a concrete law, take independent uniform
question bits and S_u=X/Z according to the parity of u, T_v=X/Z according
to the parity of v. Then rho_bar=1/2 and
  p=a_t+(eta^2/2)Z_t.                                       (P5)

The elementary bound |Z_t|<=a_t gives
  (1-eta^2)a_t<=p_(u,v)<=(1+eta^2)a_t.
Here a_t=(1-1/t)^t is bounded below by 1/4 and tends to exp(-1).
Thus p=Theta(1) uniformly in t. The exact formulas, rather than just
these inequalities, specify the resource and normalization.

3. Collapse the accepted reference to four shared branches

Let e=A(alpha), f=B(gamma). Define four nonnegative weights

  pi_(e,f)=[a_t+(e+f)b_t+ef c_t]/(4a_t).                    (P6)

These sum to one. Their positivity follows directly from their meaning:
  a_t pi_(e,f)=sum_(alpha:A=e,gamma:B=f)theta w_alpha z_gamma.
They are the two-bit parity law under the accepted scalar reference.

Prepare BEFORE fresh inputs the state
  zeta=sum_(e,f) sqrt(pi_(e,f)) |e,f>_A |e,f>_B tensor Phi+.
Equivalently, share the classical pair (e,f) drawn from pi and a Bell pair.
The displayed pure state has local Hilbert dimension at most 8; the
classical description is two shared bits plus one qubit per player.
It depends on t through four explicit probabilities, not an exponential
table of old history labels.

Let d=1+eta and L_(u,e)=sqrt(I+eta k_e S_u), M_(v,f)=sqrt(I+eta k_f T_v).
Define the local success/fine effects

  C_(u,e)^a=(1/d)L_(u,e) P_(u,e)^a L_(u,e),
  D_(v,f)^b=(1/d)M_(v,f) Q_(v,f)^b M_(v,f).                 (P7)

Their sums are (I+eta k_e S_u)/d and (I+eta k_f T_v)/d, hence effects
at most I. Complete each by its positive failure complement. These are
genuine all-input complete local POVMs on the SAME resource zeta.
They depend only on own u or v and the shared branch; there is no fresh
opposite-input oracle and no post-input state preparation.

4. Exact full fine law and one-sided masses

The original accepted fine table is
  T_(u,v)^(a,b)=sum_(alpha,gamma)theta chi(G_u^(alpha,a),K_v^(gamma,b)).
Using (P2), remove the scalar w,z from each matrix square root and group
the remaining expression by e,f. Equation (P6) then gives EXACTLY

  lambda P_zeta(success a,success b|u,v)=T_(u,v)^(a,b),
  lambda=d^2 a_t.                                           (P8)

This preserves every fine entry for every input pair after forgetting
the old coarse labels. It is a new instrument, not a fine-table claim
about those old labels. It therefore evades the earlier full-marker
common-classical-flag obstruction as well as the literal mask boundary.

Because the Bell marginal is I/2 and Pauli trace is zero, each one-sided
success probability on zeta is EXACTLY 1/d, even after the reference
parity branch is selected. Thus
  G_A=G_B=lambda/d=d a_t,                                   (P9)
while the original event mass is (P4) or (P5). The same bounded masses
survive all source half-exposure averages by linearity of (P7).
The scalar source budget is at most
  [log(1/p)+log(1+eta)]/m=O(1/m),
using the unchanged original referee law. Retaining the actual one-sided
charges log(d a_t/p) is sharper; the referee cost is separately bounded.

The pair's actual simultaneous success probability is p_(u,v)/(d^2 a_t),
at least (1-eta)/(1+eta). Failures are genuine POVM outcomes, not a free
heralded coordination primitive. The final source extraction continues
to use its actual all-input sampler and its charged timeout/mismatch
terms; (P8) alone does not permit locally repeating until both succeed.

5. Why every conjunctive mask lift still pays a growing mass

The original one-party marginals of (P1) are exactly the biased product
weights w,z for EVERY u,v. Any exact conjunctive ancillary mask kernel,
even collective, commuting, and arbitrarily large, must satisfy

  max(G_A,G_B)>=sqrt(G_A G_B)
      >=||diag(sqrt(w)) theta diag(sqrt(z))||_*
      =[sqrt(1+2r-3r^2)]^t.                                 (P10)

The tensor identity for the weighted matrix is exact; its single-factor
trace norm was proved in the previous unit. With r=t^(-1/2), its log is
Theta(sqrt(t)), so (P10) grows unboundedly while (P9) remains bounded.
This is a bound on the MAXIMUM of the two masses, not each individually.

The classical two/three-copy cover correction does not contradict (P10):
those are upper bounds on a different uniform cap and still allow the
weighted nuclear lower bound. They invalidate extrapolating the finite
4/3-vs-3/2 advantage into an asymptotic quantum advantage, but do not
invalidate this whole-coarse-vs-conjunctive separation.

6. Actual representation costs and unresolved scope

For the replacement, only four real weights (P6), two values k_e, one
Bell pair, and constant-size qubit effects are required. No dense 2^t
matrix, exponential list of input states, or exact old-history table
is part of its resource interface. Reading the actual local input u/v
and computing its chosen Pauli direction still has the stated input's
own cost. The four probabilities are given by explicit scalar powers;
an exact-real model is being used. Uniform finite-gate/bit-precision costs
for the entire subsequent source sampler have NOT been proved.

The original family also has a compact instrument description. Group
alpha by parity e. Its binary coarse qubit effects are
  [(1+e m_t)/2][I+eta k_e S_u].
After this weak binary POVM, sample alpha from w conditional on that
parity. The Kraus operator for alpha is exactly sqrt(F_u^alpha).
The conditional parity probabilities are bounded away from zero in the
displayed r regime, so ordinary rejection sampling needs O(t) classical
draws in expectation. Then perform its prescribed fine POVM. Bob's
construction is symmetric. This describes the actual fine instrument,
not a sharp Pauli measurement with a different disturbance channel.

Consequently this family supplies a concrete, finite, all-input semantic
rebuild with a quantified asymptotic advantage over the specifically
obstructed mask interface. A general useful repetition rate would still
need the domination or another low-cost reference invariant at the
greedy-selected histories of unrestricted positive-gap strategies.
That hypothesis is not established by this explicit source family.
