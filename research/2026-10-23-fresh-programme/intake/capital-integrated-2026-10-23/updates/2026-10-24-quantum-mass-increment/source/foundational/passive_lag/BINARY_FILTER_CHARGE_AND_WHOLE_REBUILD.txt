BINARY FILTER CHARGES AND A WHOLE-COARSE REBUILD CRITERION
2026-10-24. passive_lag. Internal derivation; novelty UNKNOWN.

Purpose

The frozen quantum predicate-mass theorem uses a concrete ancillary mask
filter and the R/H telescope. This note identifies the same one-sided
mass quantity through NEW local binary instruments and conditional quantum
relative entropy. It also gives a concrete whole-coarse replacement outside
entrywise mask multiplication, under an explicit strategy-dependent
invariant. Neither result proves a universal answer-free reconstruction.

All Hilbert spaces and instruments in the forward construction are finite.
Logarithms in this note are natural. To compare with Jain's base-two
charges, divide every displayed entropy by ln(2). This note does not
change the frozen theorem file or its 2^-75 constant.

1. An exact parent conditional-charge inequality

Let h have an offline prior pi(h), independent of the m fresh question
pairs drawn from mu^m. Given h, let Alice and Bob use arbitrary local
isometries on one pre-shared resource. Their outputs include binary flags
s_A,s_B, two copies of each flag that will be omitted in the parent
comparison, and an ADDITIONAL copy of own flag retained inside K_A or K_B.
All live answers and the other local workspace are retained too.

Omit/dephase only the two displayed joint-flag copies to form the
preselection marginal chi_h on R K_A K_B. Let W={s_A=s_B=1}. Set
  e(h)=P(W|h), a(h)=P(s_A=1|h), b(h)=P(s_B=1|h),
  e=E_pi e(h)>0, A=E_pi a(h), B=E_pi b(h),
  q(h)=pi(h)e(h)/e.
The target chi_h^W is the normalized W branch on the same residual
systems. It is obtained by conditioning BEFORE removing the displayed
copies, rather than by requiring that a mixed state have a locally
accessible purification.

For supported h the mixture identity implies
  chi_h >= e(h) chi_h^W.
Operator monotonicity of log therefore gives
  D(chi_h^(W,RK_A)||chi_h^(RK_A)) <=log(1/e(h)).              (B1)
Measuring the extra retained own flag in K_A gives
  D(chi_h^(W,K_A)||chi_h^(K_A)) >=log(1/a(h)).                (B2)
This uses the exact target flag value 1 and the preselection probability
a(h); it does not suppose that either state is classical on its other
systems. Consequently the nonnegative conditional relative entropy

  d_A(h)=D(chi_h^(W,RK_A)||chi_h^(RK_A))
             -D(chi_h^(W,K_A)||chi_h^(K_A))

satisfies
  0<=d_A(h)<=log(a(h)/e(h)).                                (B3)
The corresponding bound holds with b(h) for Bob.

Define r_A(h)=pi(h)a(h)/A. Since e(h)<=a(h), all q-supported terms
are also r_A-supported. A direct classical identity gives

  E_q log(a(h)/e(h))=log(A/e)-D(q||r_A)<=log(A/e).            (B4)

Hence
  E_q d_A(h)<=log(A/e), E_q d_B(h)<=log(B/e).                 (B5)
No answer alphabet, flag entropy, or bound on a purification dimension
enters. This does not make the joint event free: A/e and B/e measure
how much more often one side can succeed than both sides succeed.

If the retained own-flag copy were omitted as well, (B2) would be lost.
Then the crude bound is log(1/e), which can contain the large ancillary
normalization. The copy is produced inside a NEW local dilation, rather
than copied from inaccessible old checked records after postselection.

2. Complete local binary POVMs preserving the original fine table

For each tested question string c, let T_c(u,v,a_J,b_J) denote the
ORIGINAL subnormalized accepted table on ALL unchecked question strings
u,v and all their original answer strings a_J,b_J. Its total mass may
depend on u,v. The required whole-coarse replacement witness is:

  one finite resource zeta_c independent of u,v;
  local complete POVMs M_(c,u)^(s,a_J), N_(c,v)^(t,b_J);
  lambda_c>0;
  lambda_c P_zeta_c(s=t=1,a_J,b_J|u,v)=T_c(u,v,a_J,b_J).     (B6)

Only OWN u or v may index the respective POVM. Equation (B6) is an
actual all-input fine-table realization, not just local marginal agreement
or a factorization of the total acceptance effect. It is stronger than
preserving only one selected live coordinate, but weaker than reproducing
the old coarse histories. It may be strategy-dependent and nonconjunctive.

For the explicit mask operation, take the finite pool of the frozen note,
  M_(c,u)^(1,a_J)=sum_alpha G_(c,u)^(alpha,a_J) tensor E_alpha^c,
  M_(c,u)^(0,a_*)=I-sum_(a_J)M_(c,u)^(1,a_J),
and set other failure outcomes zero. The analogous Bob POVM is positive
and complete. The tensor identity proves (B6) for ALL unchecked answers.
There is no old alpha/gamma register in this NEW POVM. Dilate it directly:

  U_(c,u) xi=sum_(s,a_J)|s,s> |s>_(retained flag)
                         |a_J> sqrt(M_(c,u)^(s,a_J)) xi.

The analogous V_(c,v) is also an isometry. All registers are finite.
Conditioning on W=11 leaves a PURE copied-input target because the
NEW isometries and their environments are retained. Old incompatible
history labels have not been traced out of an already-fixed pure target.

Prepare an offline tensor pool of all zeta_c if their resources differ.
Each hypothesized c selects its factor, so this remains one all-input
resource. Use prior pi(c)=mu^C(c)lambda_c/Lambda, where
Lambda=E_(mu^C)lambda_c. With p the original tested-event probability,
(B6) gives e=p/Lambda and the exact original conditional live law.
Let the ORIGINAL unconditioned fresh-question means define

  G_A=E_c lambda_c E_(u~mu_X^m)P_zeta_c(s_A=1|c,u),
  G_B=E_c lambda_c E_(v~mu_Y^m)P_zeta_c(s_B=1|c,v).           (B7)

Then A=G_A/Lambda, B=G_B/Lambda. Substitution in (B5) gives

  mean parent d_A<=log(G_A/p), mean parent d_B<=log(G_B/p).   (B8)

This is the exact normalization cancellation in the frozen mass proof,
now expressed through complete local instruments and conditional QRE.
The literal mask construction satisfies the bounds on G_A,G_B already
proved there. An alternative witness satisfying (B6) with smaller masses
could escape the entrywise mask obstruction. Merely postulating an
efficient scalar kernel, a mixed target, or a jointly defined POVM does
not satisfy (B6).

purification generated by U_(c,u); the extra own flag is simply part of

binary outputs were literal answers at coordinates in C is used here.

The accepted branch is W=11, so its NEW checked transcript is a fixed

  sum_i b_(A,i)<=log(G_A/p),
  sum_i b_(B,i)<=log(G_B/p).                                 (B9)

The separate question-law comparison must use the ORIGINAL mu^n and the

4. An actual nonconjunctive whole-coarse operation

Here is a sufficient strategy-dependent invariant beyond mask geometry.
For each c, suppose the ORIGINAL coarse tested POVMs are independent of
ALL unchecked questions:
  F_(c,u)^alpha=F_c^alpha for every u,
  H_(c,v)^gamma=H_c^gamma for every v.                       (B10)
Fine effects G_(c,u)^(alpha,a_J),K_(c,v)^(gamma,b_J) can still vary
arbitrarily with own questions, provided they sum to those coarse effects.
No commutation of these fine effects is required.

Put w_(c,alpha,gamma)=chi(F_c^alpha,H_c^gamma) and
  p_c=sum_(alpha,gamma)theta_c(alpha,gamma)w_(c,alpha,gamma).
This p_c is independent of fresh u,v. For w>0, prepare the normalized
finite bipartite vector represented by
  xi_(c,alpha,gamma)=sqrt(F_c^alpha) sigma sqrt(H_c^gamma)/sqrt(w).
On the accepted resource, share the CLASSICAL branch (alpha,gamma) with
probability theta_c(alpha,gamma)w/p_c and that conditional vector.
Each branch/state is prepared before fresh questions. Support-normalize
the fine effects to get local POVMs C_(c,u,alpha)^(a_J),
D_(c,v,gamma)^(b_J). Their branch expectation is exactly
  chi(G_(c,u)^(alpha,a_J),K_(c,v)^(gamma,b_J))/w.
The shared accepted resource therefore realizes T_c/p_c for every u,v.

Finally share a Bernoulli(p_c) coin; both flags succeed if it is 1, and
otherwise both fail. This gives (B6) with lambda_c=1 and
  P(s_A=1|c,u)=P(s_B=1|c,v)=p_c.
Hence G_A=G_B=p, and the two parent charges in (B8) are exactly zero.
The original accepted fresh-question law is unchanged (mu^m), so the
question-law charge is zero as well. For p_c=0 use the always-fail branch.

This is a legal replacement of the whole coarse realization for ANY
predicate, including the L mask, rather than an ancillary entrywise
success kernel. Both old coarse outcomes become shareable because they
can be sampled jointly before the fresh questions under (B10). The old
conjunctive nuclear-norm obstruction does not apply to this operation.
It rigorously explains an escape from that obstruction, but it supplies
no universal bound when tested answers depend on unchecked questions.

The hypothesis cannot be weakened to independence of the TOTAL accepted
effect or equality of local marginal states: the fixed-effect PR example
already has W_uv=(1/2)I for every u,v yet its accepted live table is
nonquantum. The whole fine instrument, not only W, must be retained.

Status

(B1)--(B8) are a self-contained exact entropy/instrument lemma. (B9)
uses the named generic ordered-comparison source identity after the
explicit local-isometry substitution above. (B10) yields a concrete
whole-coarse operation with zero charge for arbitrary predicates.
No universal low-mass witness for (B6) has been constructed. The live
problem is to obtain such witnesses, or controlled payoff-preserving
approximations, when coarse tested outcomes depend on many unchecked
questions. No external novelty, efficiency, or public result is claimed.
