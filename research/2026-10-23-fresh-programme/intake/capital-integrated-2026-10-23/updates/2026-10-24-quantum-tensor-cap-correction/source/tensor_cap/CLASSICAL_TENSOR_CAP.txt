Exact classical rectangle caps for the Boolean inclusion mask
2026-10-24

Setup
-----
Index rows and columns by subsets S,T of [t], equivalently t-bit strings,
and let theta_t(S,T)=1 exactly when S is a subset of T. Thus theta_t is
L^{tensor t} for L=[[1,1],[0,1]]. An exact positive rectangle cover is

  theta_t = sum_R lambda_R 1_{A_R} 1_{B_R},   lambda_R >= 0,

where each nonempty rectangle A_R x B_R is contained in the support of
theta_t. Its balanced local cap is

  cap = max( max_S sum_{R:S in A_R} lambda_R,
             max_T sum_{R:T in B_R} lambda_R ).

Allowing fuzzy local factors e_S,f_T in [0,1] cannot lower this optimum.
For each shared-randomness value, introduce independent uniform thresholds
u,v and set A={S:e_S>=u}, B={T:f_T>=v}. Then
E[1_{S in A}1_{T in B}]=E[e_S f_T], while the row and column incidences
are exactly E[e_S] and E[f_T]. Since there are finitely many rectangles,
the induced measure can be aggregated into a finite nonnegative cover.

Exact optima at t=2 and t=3
---------------------------
The exact orbit-reduced rational LPs, with one variable per simultaneous
coordinate-permutation orbit of rectangles, give

  cap(theta_2) = 7/4 < (3/2)^2,
  cap(theta_3) = 23/10 < (3/2)^3.

These are exact optima, not just numerical LP values: the tables below give
primal covers, and the rational dual weights following them lower-bound
every rectangle cover. In each table row, the listed coefficient is put on
every DISTINCT image of the listed rectangle under simultaneous coordinate
permutations. The script in this directory expands these orbits and checks
all equalities and inequalities using exact fractions.

  1/2            {00}               {00,01,10}

Its row loads by Hamming rank 0,1,2 are (7/4,7/4,1), and its column

Matching lower certificates
----------------------------
For nonnegative row weights u_S, column weights v_T, and cell weights
y_{S,T}, suppose

  sum_{S subset T, S in A, T in B} y_{S,T}
       <= sum_{S in A} u_S + sum_{T in B} v_T

for every allowed rectangle, and sum_S u_S + sum_T v_T = 1. For any exact
cover with local loads r_S,c_T and cap K, summing this inequality with
weights lambda_R gives

  sum_{S subset T} y_{S,T}
       <= sum_S u_S r_S + sum_T v_T c_T <= K.

The following rational weights attain the primal caps. Write k=|S|,
l=|T|. Any unlisted y value is zero.

t=2:
  y_{S,T}=1/4 for every allowed rank pair except (k,l)=(1,2), where it is 0;
  u_S=1/4 if k=0, otherwise 0;
  v_T=1/4 if l=1 or l=2, otherwise 0.
Then sum u+sum v=1 and sum y=7/4.

t=3:
  y_{S,T}=1/5 for rank pairs (0,0),(3,3);
  y_{S,T}=1/10 for (0,2),(0,3),(1,1),(1,2),(1,3),(2,2);
  y_{S,T}=0 for (0,1),(2,3);
  u_S=1/5 if k=0, 1/10 if k=1, otherwise 0;
  v_T=1/10 if l=2, 1/5 if l=3, otherwise 0.
Then sum u+sum v=1 and sum y=23/10.

The stated rectangle inequalities were exhaustively checked over all
37 allowed rectangles for t=2 and all 653 for t=3. The included checker
`classical_tensor_cap_lp.py` also verifies every primal coverage and load.
These certificates establish the optima without relying on floating-point
optimization or an NPA relaxation.

Tensor consequence and direction of comparison
-----------------------------------------------
Rectangle covers tensor: multiplying coefficients and taking product row
and column sets multiplies every local incidence load. Therefore, for
even t=2k,

  cap(theta_t) <= (7/4)^k.

For odd t=3+2k (k>=0),

  cap(theta_t) <= (23/10)(7/4)^k.

The supplied quantum lift has cap at most (4/3)^t. The classical covers
above are already strictly smaller than that tensorized quantum bound for
every t>=2: for even t the ratio is (63/64)^k; for odd t>=3 it is
(621/640)(63/64)^k. Thus the base L quantum/classical gap does NOT tensorize
as a classical lower bound (3/2)^t. The comparison actually reverses for
the displayed tensorized quantum construction: a classical cover has a
strictly smaller cap, with asymptotic per-coordinate factor sqrt(7)/2,
whose square is 7/4 < 16/9=(4/3)^2. This does not rule out some different
quantum construction with a smaller cap; it does show that tensoring the
given single-copy quantum witness is not a classical advantage on these
product masks.
