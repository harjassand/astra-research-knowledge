## 5. Complete execution: purification, teleportation, precision and acquisition

A capacity statement alone would leave a substantial implementation gap. A small explicit purification routine supplies finite-error communication without requiring a large-block decoding oracle.

### An explicit finite purification circuit

Twirling produces a Bell-diagonal state with probabilities
\[
(1-e,e/3,e/3,e/3).
\]
Use the five-qubit code with stabilizers
\[
XZZXI,\quad IXZZX,\quad XIXZZ,\quad ZXIXZ.
\]
Each party measures the four local stabilizers, compares syndromes, accepts matching syndromes, corrects their common value, and decodes one pair.

Enumerating all \(4^5=1024\) Paulis gives stabilizer and normalizer weight enumerators
\[
1+15x^4,\qquad 1+30x^3+15x^4+18x^5.
\]
Writing \(u=1-e,\ x=e/3\), one round has
\[
A(e)=u^5+30x^3u^2+15x^4u+18x^5,
\]
\[
e'=\frac{30x^3u^2+18x^5}{A(e)}.
\tag{16}
\]
For \(e\le0.15\), \(e'\le2e^3\). Each round is a fixed-size Clifford circuit; its acquisition is ordinary binary symplectic elimination on five qubits.

After \(r\) levels, the expected number of source windows is bounded by
\[
\boxed{
E_r\le
\frac1{p_{\rm raw}}
\prod_{j=0}^{r-1}\frac5{A(e_j)}.
}
\tag{17}
\]
Because the errors decrease cubically,
\[
r=O(\log\log(1/\varepsilon)),
\qquad
E_r=
O\!\left[
\frac1{p_{\rm raw}}
\bigl(\log(1/\varepsilon)\bigr)^{\log_3 5}
\right].
\]
Depth-first preparation needs \(O(r)\) stored pairs; failed attempts and failed purification blocks are included in (17).

A finite deadline is also available. Cap an independent run at \(2E_r\) source attempts. Markov’s inequality bounds its abort probability by \(1/2\). Repeating at most \(\lceil\log_2(1/\beta)\rceil\) capped runs gives abort probability at most \(\beta\).

Once a pair is ready, Alice introduces the payload and performs an ordinary Bell measurement. Bob applies the communicated Pauli correction. For Bell-diagonal error \(e_r\), the resulting channel satisfies
\[
\frac12\|\mathcal T-\mathrm{id}\|_\diamond=e_r,
\]
including payloads entangled with inaccessible references.

For a finite network path, allocate the error budget among links, prepare their pairs, and Bell-swap them. Diamond errors add under composition. This reaches the quantum-data communication destination rather than stopping at a positive entanglement witness.

### Finite control programs

The analytic pulse need not be represented with infinite precision. Put
\[

\le

\tag{18}
\]
A quantized piecewise-linear program achieves this with
\[
J=O(\tau S/\delta_r)
\]

Choose the total raw-operation error proportional to the proved \(p_{\rm raw}\), rather than merely small in absolute terms. If the unnormalized accepted-state error is \(E\le\epsilon_{\rm dev}p_{\rm raw}\), then
\[
F_{\rm implemented}\ge
\frac{F_{\rm raw}-\epsilon_{\rm dev}}{1+\epsilon_{\rm dev}}.
\tag{19}
\]
Equations (18)–(19) explicitly price the increased precision needed for rare success. They are conservative; they do not imply a fixed hardware error floor is harmless as \(\eta\to0\).

\[
N_g\epsilon_g+\nu\mathcal V+\epsilon_{\rm prep/reset},
\]
where \(N_g\) counts **all** local gates, including failed trials, and \(\mathcal V\) is total stored-qubit time. The usual hybrid argument bounds the abort-inclusive channel error by this sum. Arbitrarily small communication error requires correspondingly accurate local operations or independently justified fault-tolerant local memories.
