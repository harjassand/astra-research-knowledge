## Closest primary literature

1. Klep, Povh and Volcic, *Minimizer Extraction in Polynomial Optimization Is
   Robust*, SIAM J. Optimization 28 (2018), 3177–3207,
   https://doi.org/10.1137/17M1152061 . Whitened insertion matrices, singular
   quotient construction, approximate flatness and positive Schur residuals
   are established. The focused review records exact sections and equations.
2. Nielsen et al., *Gate Set Tomography*, Quantum 5, 557 (2021),
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.07301 . Native fiducial Gram and insertion
   acquisition, shared gauges and SPAM self-calibration are strong existing
   capabilities. The inspected realization uses a target dimension and
   informational completeness; this programme instead supplies residual
   certificates under a narrower reciprocal source contract.
3. Colbrook, Ayton and Szoke, *Residual dynamic mode decomposition: robust
   and verified Koopmanism*, JFM 955, A21 (2023),
   https://doi.org/10.1017/jfm.2022.1052 . The three-matrix residual against
   the actual infinite-dimensional operator is already established.
4. Conradie et al., *Trustworthy Koopman Operator Learning*, 2026,
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15091 . The current paper and its published
   implementation were inspected as contemporary trustworthy-learning
   comparators; no claim of novelty is based on ignoring recent Koopman work.
5. Uy and Peherstorfer, *Probabilistic error estimation for non-intrusive
   reduced models learned from data of systems governed by linear parabolic
   partial differential equations*, ESAIM M2AN 55 (2021), 735–761,
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.05890 ; implementation
   https://github.com/wayneisaacuy/OpInfErrEst . Nonintrusive residual
   certification is already a research programme; its state-trajectory

6. Borcea, Garnier, Mamonov and Zimmerling, *When Data Driven Reduced Order
