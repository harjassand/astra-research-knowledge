# Material corrections and limits

This note accompanies the proofs and immutable reviewed receipts. It records
changes in interpretation without rewriting earlier executed datasets.

## Counts, precision, and physical execution

An exact or narrow scalar interval is an input to an echo experiment. The
number of intervals does not acquire their accuracy. The g=.001 synthetic
dissipation example explicitly assumes raw ADC error 1e-14. Its approximately
935-fold certificate improvement is not a laboratory speedup.

The quoted hypothetical qubit shot budget of about 5.61e14 used amplitude
precision 1e-6, 32 amplitudes and failure .01. It is not the shot budget for
the separately executed raw ADC radius 1e-8, and no such shots were executed.

Positive Markov experiments execute integer-RNG trajectories in software.
They charge preparations, primitive updates and sensor reports separately.
The source symmetry, nonintrusiveness, preparation and sensor warranties
are not acquired by these simulations.

## Logical trials are not necessarily physical resets

The first membership receipt conservatively charged every logical trial as
a reset. Under exact recognition of the actual state b, a trajectory ending
at b can start the next trial without preparation. The corrected upper for
four million logical trials is 299,681 preparations; primitive and reading
counts remain 6,452,016 and 4,587,663. Source-law independence follows from
Markov restart, not from pretending a coarse label specifies a state.

The final infinite-source acquisition has an independently replayed exact
preparation count of 1,758,723. Its direct comparator reports 13,715; the
saved direct counts alone certify [13,715,13,717] because the last boundary
bit was not preserved. This two-preparation metadata limitation does not
affect inference, and is not described as an exact independent replay.

## Confidence is simultaneous, not conditional on emission

The guarantees bound the probability that any used interval or emitted
certificate is false. They do not assert a posterior probability conditional
on the algorithm having emitted a certificate. In particular the phrase
conditional certificate in early positive-interface notes must be read as
conditioning the mathematical claim on its source/access premises, not as
a frequentist conditional-coverage theorem.

Shared counts, rows, kernels and program evaluations can be correlated.
The constructions use simultaneous confidence events and coefficient
aggregation; they do not assume those reused quantities are independent.

## Source forms and actual range conditions

A bare inverse-dissipation pseudoinverse is insufficient: it can erase
residual components in an invariant kernel. The general theorem requires
an actual bounded factorization through the square root of source
dissipation. The absorption/Schur proof establishes the form domain through
Abel limits even when the global inverse is unbounded.

A positive-entry first-return tail need not be PSD. The full return forms
and even-time sum have the required positivity, while censored tails use
entrywise domination and symmetric operator-norm bounds. The scoped
Schur review includes an exact counterexample to the invalid tail shortcut.

## What the removed gap premise means

The original unweighted all-word compiler already needed no strict hidden
source contraction margin. The new observation construction removes that
margin from the more informative inverse-dissipation acquisition and the
estimated-map transfer. It does not establish the first imaginable gap-free
all-word predictor. A norm-one source family separates the two certificates:
the old unweighted bound stays at least 1/4, while the new weighted bound is
O(g). This is not a lower bound against every equally informed method.

The full-state zero-residual calibration lower bound remains. Matching the
upper and lower g^-3 exponents requires matching their fixed accuracy
constants, as explicitly done in the cost review; q is also charged.

## Interfaces, future language, and reuse

Observed irreversible loss is stronger than a timeout or an undifferentiated
outside report. Sensor robustness retains exact boundary labels; bounded
live-versus-absorbed errors are charged by H times the per-report warranty.
The experiment does not establish that warranty.

A coarse cell is not an exact boundary state. COARSE_BOUNDARY_OBSTRUCTION.md
proves identical coarse first-return laws with later discrepancy above .48.
An actual resampling operation repairs closure only for the changed program
language and incurs its own execution cost.

All-word bounds concern arbitrary fixed finite operation sequences, including
sequences chosen after acquisition. They do not establish a guarantee for
policies conditioned on intermediate outcomes within a new run. Block
certificates cover their specified block language. Program optimization,
especially over exponentially many words, is a separate problem.

## Scientific attribution and evidence

Gram/GNS realization, residual model reduction, primal-dual correction,
Julia dilation, dissipation estimates, stochastic complementation and
Markov aggregation are established. The scoped contribution is the
constructed experimental acquisition-to-program guarantee with its stated
interfaces and complete costs. Strong same-data Galerkin interval propagation
is included and wins the main finite-program comparison. No historical
priority proof or universal superiority claim is made.

Completed internal reviews retain their stated scope and frozen hashes.
Later refinements have new files and receipts. They are not silently covered
by earlier reviews. Accepted Astra undecidability foundations remain intact;
no unrestricted hidden-system learning or decision procedure is claimed.

## Coarse survival continuation

Standard DKW empirical-distribution estimation already solves uniform
single-action survival prediction. A positive Hankel determinant is only a
dimension lower bound, and acquiring a finite-rank closure is unnecessary for
that particular prediction task. The executed DKW comparator and its complete
physical-time ledger are preserved.

Individual lifetime laws do not determine mixed responses: an exact two-state
hidden-swap construction preserves every marginal law while reversing a
three-step ordering preference. The new common-preparation theorem bounds
switched tails from those marginals; paid mixed-word observations still supply
the remaining response information.

The coarse switching theorem requires positivity preserving, selfadjointness
and a shared positive v with A_i v<=v. Positive semidefiniteness alone is not
order positivity. This is a native observation specialization of established
weak-Poincare energy arguments, not a new general no-gap decay principle.

The finite observed prefix cover certifies all-word survival directly; its
common-preparation theory guarantees a finite cutoff for a defined source
class. The emitted two-state model is an approximation to an infinite source,
not a hidden-rank upper bound. Its certificate controls unconditional absolute
survival errors only. It can have a finite mean lifetime while the actual
source has infinite mean, and says nothing uniform about conditional survivor
states or relative probabilities of very rare outcomes.

The source-form route is not necessary for uniform prediction. On the new
countable-block fixture, inverse-dissipation forms diverge for all finite
word-feature embeddings with strictly stable computational maps, while the
coarse prefix cover succeeds. This does not invalidate the earlier boundary
form theorem or exclude unweighted, interval, convex or other methods.

Same-data moment fitting spends no extra confidence when every passing model
is checked against one simultaneous cover event. The specified old boxed
scalar-residual compiler is vacuous on these counts; this is not a comparison
against the best possible moment-SDP or all equally informed algorithms.

The hidden-coloring lower bound grants a known strict norm gap and uses four
controls. It requires successful reusable prediction with a single uniform
accuracy event; mere soundness of possibly perpetual abstention is weaker.
The source state space is enormous, and permutations can have negative
eigenvalues. No lower bound for a positive-semidefinite-only class, or for
a fixed gap independent of horizon, is claimed. Its comparison upper route
charges actual preparations, control lengths and observations; future exact
prediction may still require exponential arithmetic.

The adaptive cover-cost theorem uses new fresh batches after each selected
word is fixed. Its confidence spending is not retroactively applied to the
fixed balanced-tape receipt. Its cover-size parameter is a sufficient
resource for that algorithm, not a universal information lower bound.

The later adaptive comparator has its own independent acquisition and .001
false-certificate budget. Its terminal midpoint can stay nonzero forever
within the absolute error tolerance. It needs no lifetime pilot for the
finite cover it actually emits, and its source-time saving is attributed to
ordinary adaptive allocation and midpoint prediction. It does not inherit
or separately validate the balanced receipt's compact two-state model.
