## 1. An exact physical singlet check

Let \(P_0\) project four data spins onto total spin zero. Choose one data spin \(i\), let \(\mathbf R\) be the total spin of the other three, and prepare helpers \(a,b\) in their joint singlet \(|S\rangle\). Set \(\hbar=1\).

Four simultaneous antiferromagnetic exchange links implement

\[
H_\star=J\bigl(\mathbf R\!\cdot\!\mathbf s_a+
                    \mathbf s_i\!\cdot\!\mathbf s_b\bigr),
\qquad U=e^{-i\pi H_\star/J}.
\]

The accepted operator of one query is exactly

\[
K=\langle S|U|S\rangle=-iP_0-\frac12Q_R,
\]

where \(Q_R\) projects onto rest-three spin \(3/2\). Its singular values are therefore \(1,\frac12,0\).

That discrete response permits an exact cancellation. Keep the **same helpers coherent**, apply helper singlet phases \(R(\phi)=e^{i\phi P_{S,ab}}\), and choose \(\phi=\arccos(1/3)\). Then

\[
C=U R(\phi)U^\dagger R(-\phi)U
\]

satisfies

\[
\boxed{\langle S|C|S\rangle
=K\,\frac{4K^\dagger K-I}{3}
=-iP_0.}
\]

Thus the accepted quantum operation is exactly

\[
\rho\longmapsto P_0\rho P_0.
\]

Every logical superposition is preserved, including entanglement with a reference. Every nonzero-total-spin sector is rejected. Summing the actual fine triplet outcomes gives rejected effect \(I-P_0\); those branches become located whole-block erasures.

The implementation requires **five control settings, fourteen edge-pulse instances, one helper pair and one final singlet/triplet read**. All exchange strengths can remain positive. Its coherent duration is

\[
T_{\rm check}=\frac{5\pi}{J}+\frac{2\pi}{J_h}.
\]

There is no additional logical-sector energy penalty or slow resonant transfer requirement. Charge and orbital admission remain necessary in a microscopic implementation.

The constituent ideas have clear predecessors: four-spin singlet encoding and exchange universality, collective exchange of the three-spin gauge degree of freedom, and exact phase amplification are established. The candidate contribution here is their explicit assembly into this full-singlet instrument and its connected physical-control construction. \frac12\|\mathcal E-\mathcal V\|_\diamond

## 3. The decisive limitations are now operational tests

Three failures materially constrain the result.

**Helper coherence is essential.** Destroying singlet/triplet coherence between queries produces leaked-state false acceptance of \(3/8\), or \(7/16\) when both intervening coherences are lost. Clean code states still pass perfectly. Testing only good inputs would miss the failure.

**Code membership does not certify every gate error.** Logical exchange-angle errors can remain entirely inside the singlet code. A magnetic fault near the end of the checker can also survive as unflagged leakage. The construction therefore does not yet supply a fault-tolerant checking schedule.

**The microscopic control theorem has a boundary.** Concurrent logical controls commute exactly with the ideal spin checker. Simultaneous Hubbard hopping does not inherit that identity. The completed finite-charge compiler uses sequential pair controls instead. Higher orbitals, switching bandwidth, residual OFF hopping and sensor backaction still need qualification on one actual device.

The task choice does remove one earlier burden: rejected helper triplets need not preserve their coherence through final sensing, because every such outcome erases the affected data block. Their coherence **during the queries** remains indispensable.
