EXACT EXCHANGE FILTERING OF A FOUR-SPIN SINGLET QUBIT
Research construction and limits, 31 October 2026
Authoring agent: producer-workspace/autonomous_gate_obstruction; root owns synthesis.

STATUS
This is an exact finite six-spin interaction-model construction, not a fitted
quantum-dot device window and not a circuit-level fault-tolerance theorem.
It replaces attempted unknown-error recovery by a concrete erasure instrument.
Its clean branch is protected by representation multiplicity, while its ability
to reject leakage is engineered by calibrated energy transfer. All helpers,
their preparation, removal, measurement, and subsequent reset are resources.

1. CAPABILITY AND REASON FOR THE CHANGE

Let four spin-1/2 data particles encode a qubit in their two-dimensional J_D=0
subspace P0. Denote the other data sectors by P1 (dimension nine) and P2
(dimension five). A single local vector error S_i^a maps P0 entirely into P1.

The singlet code detects such errors but cannot correct their unknown locations.
More strongly, section 10 proves that even postselected, perfectly clean recovery
of an unknown single-spin error has zero success probability if only the data
and any subsequent cooling apparatus are accessible. Monitoring later cooling
photons does not repair information already left in the original noise channel.

The present task is instead to reject every J_D>0 component, preserve arbitrary
logical coherence on clean input, and produce a located whole-block erasure
whenever a helper is found outside its singlet state. This is a useful physical
instrument for a larger code, not a claim that the unknown errored qubit was
recovered. At finite coupling a flagged data block need not have returned to P0;
it is explicitly discarded/reloaded or represented by an abstract erasure state.

2. ONE COMPATIBLE FINITE INTERACTION MODEL

Units: hbar=1; all S and s are spin-1/2 operators, not Pauli matrices.
Data gap Hamiltonian:

    H_D = (Delta/2) J_D^2
        = Delta sum_{i<j} S_i dot S_j + 3 Delta/2.

Thus the four data spins have equal antiferromagnetic exchange Delta on all six
edges. Their J_D=0,1,2 energies are 0, Delta, 3 Delta.

A fresh two-spin helper is prepared in its singlet |s>. Its Hamiltonian is

    H_H = (delta_h/2) J_H^2,

so its triplet is higher by delta_h. Only one data-helper exchange edge is on:

    V_i = J S_i dot s_1,
    H_i = H_D + H_H + V_i.                              (1)

Both signs Delta>0 and J>0 are ordinary antiferromagnetic exchanges. The helper
pair is removed or its link is fully switched off after a contact. The singlet
versus triplet result is read. Fine operations within the triplet may be arbitrary
because that entire outcome is discarded as an erasure. No triplet-preserving
readout, spin-polarized helper, local magnetic rotation, or photon Bell analysis
is needed by this model. This relaxes several burdens of the inherited routes.

The uniform six-edge data interaction and its stability are real extra demands.
During filtering, arbitrary unequal intra-data exchanges or other crosslinks
are not admitted as invisible perturbations. They must be calibrated away or
included in the physical error budget.

3. EXACT CLEAN-BRANCH PROTECTION AT ANY GAP OR CONTACT STRENGTH

Write R_i=sum_{j!=i} S_j for the other three data spins. Their spin-1/2 sector has
a multiplicity two. For J_D=0, that multiplicity is precisely the logical qubit:

    data code = logical multiplicity (dimension two)
                tensor singlet of S_i and R_i.

Hamiltonian (1) acts identically on the multiplicity, for any Delta, delta_h, J,
and time. It also preserves total spin of data plus helper. Starting from data
J_D=0 and helper singlet, total spin is zero. If the outgoing helper is again a
singlet, the data must again have J_D=0. Consequently

    K_i(t) P0 = alpha(t) P0,
    K_i(t) = <s| exp(-i H_i t) |s>.                    (2)

There is no finite-gap approximation in (2). Virtual excursions cause a scalar
survival amplitude and false erasures, not unflagged logical phase or amplitude
distortion. Arbitrary time dependence of the three scalar coefficients preserves
this assertion. An operator-valued source multiplying only these three scalar
spin operators also leaves the clean logical factor as identity, although the
uniform leakage-rejection rate below then requires separate qualification.

The complete no-triplet operator is

    K_i = alpha P0 + c_b B_i + c_d D_i + c_2 P2,        (3)

where

    D_i = P1 [R_i^2 - 3/4]/3,
    B_i = P1 - D_i.

D_i is the rank-three part of J_D=1 with R_i=3/2; B_i has rank six and R_i=1/2.
Each coefficient is scalar on its sector. Equation (3) follows from SU(2),
conservation of R_i^2, and identity action on the multiplicity of R_i=1/2.
It also makes full histories tractable without discarding unmeasured helpers.

4. EXACT J_D=1 REJECTION AT FINITE DELTA/J

Choose delta_h=Delta. Within the bright R_i=1/2 sector the collective R_i is
another spin-1/2. The relevant total-spin-one multiplicity basis is

    |t,s>, |s,t>, |t,t>_{J_total=1}.

Here the first entry denotes S_i+R_i and the second the helper. Up to harmless
basis phases, the exact three-by-three Hamiltonian is

    diag(Delta, Delta, 2 Delta)
        + J [[0, 1/4, 1/(2 sqrt(2))],
             [1/4, 0, -1/(2 sqrt(2))],
             [1/(2 sqrt(2)), -1/(2 sqrt(2)), -1/4]].   (4)

The symmetric state (|t,s>+|s,t>)/sqrt(2) has energy Delta+J/4.
The antisymmetric state couples to |t,t> with strength J/2; the energy difference
in that two-dimensional block is Delta. Let

    Omega = sqrt(Delta^2+J^2)/2.

The exact no-triplet bright amplitude is

    c_b(t) = (1/2) [ exp[-i(Delta+J/4)t]
              + exp[-i(3 Delta/2-J/4)t]
                  (cos(Omega t)+i Delta sin(Omega t)/(2 Omega)) ].  (5)

For every integer n>=2, set

    r_n = [n^2 + sqrt(n^4-(2n-1)^2)]/(2n-1),
    Delta/J = r_n,
    tau_1 = 2 n pi / sqrt(Delta^2+J^2).               (6)

Then Omega tau_1=n pi and

    n(Delta-J)/sqrt(Delta^2+J^2) = n-1.

The two terms in (5) cancel exactly. Thus c_b(tau_1)=0 at a finite gap,
and no rotating-wave, secular, Markov, or infinite-gap limit is used.
As n grows, r_n~n+1/2 and tau_1 approaches 2 pi/J.

After one such contact any accepted J_D=1 component lies in D_i. For i!=j,

    D_i D_j D_i = D_i/9,
    ||D_j D_i|| = 1/3.                               (7)

One derivation identifies the J_D=1 multiplicity with the standard representation
of S4: D_i is a line along e_i-(1,1,1,1)/4, tensor the spin-one representation.
These four normalized lines have pairwise inner-product magnitude 1/3.

Use N fresh helpers, alternating between any two different data sites. Because
|c_d|<=1, the exact all-singlet amplitude on J_D=1 obeys

    ||K_{i_N} ... K_{i_1} P1|| <= 3^{-(N-1)}.         (8)

The corresponding worst-input miss probability is at most 9^{-(N-1)}.
Neither an independent source reset nor a measurement between contacts is used.
Outgoing helpers may be retained coherently and measured jointly at the end.

5. J_D=2 REJECTION, INCLUDING THE SECTOR REACHED DURING MULTI-BLOCK GATES

It would be insufficient to assume that only J_D=1 occurs at the endpoint of a
multi-block exchange gate. A single local error changes GLOBAL total spin from
zero to one, but subsequent exchange can distribute that spin among blocks,
including local J_D=2 components. The filter therefore also rejects P2.

For a J_D=2 input, R_i=3/2. In total spin two the three states are

    A = data J_D=2, helper singlet;
    B = data J_D=1, helper triplet, coupled to total spin two;
    C = data J_D=2, helper triplet, coupled to total spin two.

The exact local exchange in this basis is

    J [[0, sqrt(6)/8, sqrt(6)/8],
       [sqrt(6)/8, -1/8, -3/8],
       [sqrt(6)/8, -3/8, -1/8]].                     (9)

Take delta_h=2 Delta+J/8 and subtract 3 Delta from the energy. Defining
g=sqrt(6) J/8, the full Hamiltonian becomes

    H_2 = [[0, g, g], [g, 0, -3J/8], [g, -3J/8, 2 Delta]].  (10)

The reference A-B Rabi pulse of duration

    tau_2 = pi/(2g) = 4 pi/(sqrt(6) J)

has zero return amplitude on A. Its coupling to off-resonant C has norm
v=sqrt(15) J/8. Applying the inherited finite local energy-transfer bound with
bare gap 2 Delta, within-band norm g and no external commutator gives

    |c_2| <= a_2
      := v/Delta + tau_2 exp(v/Delta) v(2g+v)/(2 Delta).   (11)

This is an amplitude bound, not a population bound. For Delta/J sufficiently
large (e.g. r_10), a_2<1. It is about 1.845 J/Delta asymptotically.

No-triplet K_i is scalar on P2 and preserves all data J sectors. Thus M contacts
at this second helper gap suppress P2 amplitude by at least a_2^M. Such contacts
cannot increase the P1 norm already suppressed by the first stage. Conversely
the first-stage contacts cannot increase P2 norm. Together,

    K_clean = A P0 + B_leak,
    P0 B_leak=B_leak P0=0,
    ||B_leak|| <= b := max[3^{-(N-1)}, min(1,a_2)^M].    (12)

A calibrated exact-return option is also available numerically: tune delta_h
and tau_2 to make the complex return amplitude in (10)'s untuned parent vanish.
For Delta/J=r_10=10.430442580616917, a solution is
delta_h/J=20.988162030680435 and tau_2 J=5.040974880362170.
The calculated return amplitude is 2.3e-15. This is a numerical construction,
not an analytic existence or roundoff-certified zero. The rigorous result uses
(11); no main theorem depends on the numerical zero.

6. CLEAN INPUT FALSE ERASURES AND THE FULL INSTRUMENT

On code input, only a two-dimensional ancillary problem remains. In its basis
(data singlet/helper singlet; data spin-one/helper triplet coupled to total zero),
the Hamiltonian is

    [[0, -sqrt(3)J/4],
     [-sqrt(3)J/4, Delta+delta_h-J/2]].               (13)

Its excitation probability is bounded exactly by

    p_false <= 3 J^2 / [4(Delta+delta_h-J/2)^2+3 J^2]. (14)

For input entirely in P0, the complete N+M-contact instrument followed by an
explicit erasure/reset of every triplet-flagged data block is the ordinary
state-independent erasure channel:

    rho -> |A|^2 rho tensor |clean><clean|
           +(1-|A|^2) Tr(rho) |erased><erased|.

All histories with at least one triplet flag are retained in the detailed
instrument before this declared erasure/reset. Their logical content is not
claimed to be recoverable. Only the all-singlet branch is accepted. This avoids
assuming full-triplet preservation in the helper detector.

For an arbitrary P0+P1+P2 input including coherent code/leakage superpositions,
the miss probability on an entirely leaked input is at most p=b^2, but a full
instrument comparison must also account for accepted cross-sector coherence.
A conservative half-diamond bound to the ideal coarse reject-leakage instrument
with the same code erasure probability is

    error <= b+b^2 = sqrt(p)+p.                     (15)

One proof uses the exact no-triplet Kraus operator A P0+B_leak, bounds the
off-diagonal accepted terms by O(b), and combines the accepted and erased leaked
probabilities by O(b^2). A probability-only p allowance would be invalid on a
coherent leaked input.

Misclassification, preparation error, source-induced asymmetry, extra orbital
states and link isolation errors add their actual joint-channel allowances.
N+M independent readout reset assumptions are unnecessary; one may bound each
conditional operation on its actual history and telescope those bounds.

7. WHAT A SINGLE GLOBAL VECTOR ERROR CAN AND CANNOT DO

Suppose a multi-block computation starts in a product of singlet codes and uses
only SU(2)-scalar exchange Hamiltonians. Throughout the computation its global
spin is zero, even if intermediate local blocks are leaked. A single local vector
operator at any time maps it to global spin one. All later exchange evolution
keeps that global spin one. An ideal product projection onto all local J_D=0
therefore rejects that event independently of its time or subsequent spreading.

The same statement applies to local vector faults during filter interactions
when the fresh helpers and their complete outgoing spin records are included:
the initial total spin of data plus singlet helpers is zero. If all helper outputs
are singlets, a single vector fault leaves nonzero spin in the remaining data.
It cannot quietly become a logical P0 error under the stated scalar dynamics.

This selection statement is NOT a claim of uniformly small late-fault leakage.
A fault after the final useful detector contact has no remaining filter tail and
may leave a leaked data block with an all-singlet record. A following detection
cycle, an outer leakage-safe gate design, or a larger declared erasure support is
needed before asserting a fault-tolerant circuit gadget. A fault in a late pulse
does not receive the full exponent in (12).

Scalar errors such as unequal pair-exchange errors preserve global spin and can
act logically. They are not detected by this principle. Two magnetic errors can
also return to global spin zero. No general stochastic threshold, no protection
against arbitrary two-body noise, and no fault-tolerant universal architecture
is established here.

8. ACTUAL RESOURCES AND ELAPSED TIME

The model's common resources are:
  * one electron in each data and helper orbital, with an admitted orbital window;
  * a calibrated uniform six-edge data exchange Delta during filtering;
  * switchable single-edge exchange J to either of two data sites;
  * helper singlets and tunable helper gap Delta or 2 Delta+J/8;
  * a place to remove/store helper pairs, or time to measure and reprepare them;
  * a reliable singlet-vs-triplet flag test, followed by a maintained classical
    erasure record and reset/reload of flagged data blocks;
  * coherent data storage during all of those operations.

With serial helper reuse the real duration is

    T_filter = N tau_1 + M tau_2
               + sum(load + attach + detach + read + reset times)
               + all gap ramps and switching guards.

Parallel helper readout can reduce the elapsed read portion only by paying for
the extra prepared pairs, transport/storage, detectors and source isolation.
No claim of logarithmic total time follows if the available coupling, gap or
coherence budget itself must scale with the target error.

An illustrative dimensionless exact calculation at Delta/J=r_50=50.48524274,
N=8 and M=3 gives: interaction duration 65.16319/J; clean-code false-erasure
probability 6.7388e-4; J1 accepted amplitude 4.5714e-4; J2 accepted amplitude
2.0278e-7. The rigorous common amplitude bound is 4.5725e-4, its leakage-only
probability is 2.0908e-7, and its coherent instrument allowance is 4.5746e-4.
These are one self-consistent spin Hamiltonian's quantities. They are NOT a
device parameter fit, and omit no durations by calling them coherent gates.

The one-edge waveform itself is unusually benign: errors in J(t), Delta(t), or
delta_h(t) can change rejection and false-erasure rates, but preserve scalar
clean action so long as the symmetry and one-edge structure remain exact.
Nonuniform intra-data exchange drift lacks that protection.

9. COMPARISON AND WHAT HAS ACTUALLY CHANGED

Four-spin energetic singlet protection and exchange universality are established
(Bacon, Brown and Whaley, 2001). Exchange leakage-reduction procedures with
prepared singlet auxiliaries are also established; Langrock and DiVincenzo's
three-spin reset-if-leaked construction uses as few as fourteen nearest-neighbor
exchange pulses and yields a useful flag. It does not require this filter's
uniform fully connected data gap. It is a strong direct hardware comparator.

Heinz, Sharma and Kattemolle's August 2026 singlet-only exchange proposal uses
flagged XXXX/ZZZZ stabilizer circuits and polarized auxiliary single-spin qubits
with access to single-spin control. It explicitly analyzes circuit faults.
Siegel and Benjamin's 2026 singlet-triplet architecture supplies transfer-based
leakage conversion and outer decoding. Ordinary erasure conversion is therefore
not new, and lower pulse count alone is not an advantage proof.

The specific constructive addition here is an exact, finite-gap, exchange-only
energy filter whose accepted code action is identity for every logical state,
whose calibrated leakage attenuation is derived from a physical six-spin
Hamiltonian, and whose finite-gap disturbance is diverted into a state-independent
erasure branch. It also supplies all-J rejection needed for the root's global
spin-selection gate mechanism. It trades local spin control and long compiled
syndrome circuits for a symmetric gap and prepared singlet energy carriers.

Bosonic autonomous correction and error-transparent gates are stronger on a
different task: they repair specified errors without throwing away the logical
qubit. Their physical implementations already include photon-number-selective
drives and reservoirs. This filter does not claim equivalent recovery and does
not justify replacing those technologies without a full comparative device
budget. The 2026 trapped-ion AQEC experiment is another real maintained-resource
comparator; the ability to cool entropy is not an unclaimed new resource.

10. WHY POSTSELECTED UNKNOWN-ERROR RECOVERY FAILS, EVEN AFTER PERFECT COOLING READOUT

Use logical basis

    |0> = |s_12 s_34>,
    |1> = (|t+_12 t-_34> + |t-_12 t+_34> - |t0_12 t0_34>)/sqrt(3).

In J_D=1,m=0 use

    u=|s_12 t0_34>, v=|t0_12 s_34>,
    w=(|t+_12 t-_34>-|t-_12 t+_34>)/sqrt(2).

The four z-error maps E_i=S_i^z P0 are

    E1: |0>->v/2;  |1>->w/sqrt(6)-u/(2 sqrt(3));
    E2: |0>->-v/2; |1>->w/sqrt(6)+u/(2 sqrt(3));
    E3: |0>->u/2;  |1>->-w/sqrt(6)-v/(2 sqrt(3));
    E4: |0>->-u/2; |1>->-w/sqrt(6)+v/(2 sqrt(3)).

Refine any accepted recovery instrument into fine Kraus branches R. Exact
logical recovery on every unknown error requires R E_i=c_i I (a fixed known
unitary can be removed). From the first two errors, Rw=0, Rv=2c_1|0>, and
Ru=-2 sqrt(3)c_1|1>. From the third error's first column Ru=2c_3|0>. Therefore
c_1=c_3=0 and Ru=Rv=Rw=0. Applying the same reasoning in three Cartesian
orientations shows that R vanishes on all nine dimensions of J_D=1.

Thus there is no nonzero perfectly clean accepted branch uniform over unknown
local-spin errors. It is stronger than a statement about a chosen photon basis:
arbitrary coherent processing and complete measurement of all SUBSEQUENT cooling
outputs are already included in R. Access to the ORIGINAL error environment,
known error location, a restricted noise set, or a different code escapes it.

Known-location errors are different: for fixed i,
P0 S_i^a S_i^b P0=delta_ab P0/4, so their six-dimensional error space factors as
logical qubit tensor a three-dimensional syndrome and is correctable.

Spectral tagging cannot manufacture the missing site syndrome without changing
the error images. If a Hermitian excited-state Hamiltonian obeyed H E_i=omega_i
E_i on these images, then (omega_i-omega_j)E_i^dagger E_j=0. The cross products
are nonzero, so overlapping error images cannot acquire distinct sharp energies.
The erasure filter avoids this impossible discrimination rather than assuming it.
