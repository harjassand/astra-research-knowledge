A CALIBRATED ENTANGLING OPERATION WITH PHYSICAL SINGLET VERIFICATION
31 October 2026. Units hbar=1; energies in U, times in 1/U.

STATUS AND SCOPE

This note joins the proposed entangler, two actual composite verifiers, helper
preparation and spin-to-charge conversion in ONE finite-U Hubbard model. It
uses the physical graphs and complete pulse durations, not separately favorable
effective couplings. It retains a failed naive calibration before giving the
corrected construction. Sequential local gates close a universal compiler in
the same one-orbital interaction model.

The quantum charge checks are ideal, spin-blind measurement operations with
explicitly charged times and errors in any device use. Reservoir loading,
higher orbitals, uncontrolled tunnelling, anisotropic exchange, spin noise and
measured coherence have not been qualified for a fabricated device. The numbers
below are a checked finite-model construction, not an experimental fidelity or
an overall advantage over an existing processor. Numerical convergence is not
a rigorous interval certificate.

1. ONE SHARED PHYSICAL MODEL

Use repulsive spin-independent Hubbard sites with onsite U and controlled
real hoppings. The four-spin verifier uses three data-to-helper star links,
a disjoint data-to-helper link, and a helper phase link. The entangler uses
all six internal links in each four-data-site block and four crosslinks.
All operations isolate unlisted links. At least two prepared helper pairs are
needed to verify the two blocks in parallel. Their charge checks and readouts
must also be independently accessible.

The selected parameters are

  intra-block entangler, verifier and helper hopping: t/U=0.0707106781;
  entangler cross hopping:                           t_c/U=0.07598721084;
  onsite interaction:                                the SAME U on all sites;
  smooth hopping turn-on and turn-off:                40/U each;
  helper conversion detuning endpoint:               1.5U.

Thus a common hopping ceiling of at least0.076U covers every operation. The
cross coupling is not silently assumed equal to the intra-block coupling in
the finite Hubbard model. Their nominal exchange ratio is1.15481124234.
Helper conversion uses t below this same ceiling. The separately proved
isolated-pair local controls also stay below0.0707106781U.

The extra orbital/blockade condition remains physical: a higher-orbital or
valley triplet must not enter the conversion window. The one-orbital model
has excluded that state by construction, not proved its absence in silicon.

2. THE DENSE-GRAPH FAILURE AND ITS RESOLUTION

The exact spin Hamiltonian in FAST_SINGLET_EXCHANGE_GATE.txt has a sqrt(7)
revival when its sixteen pair exchanges are equal. It is tempting to use the
same pair-calibrated Hubbard clock that works well for the verifier. That is
wrong at the selected finite U: the dense graph has additional virtual paths
whose leading effects are not all in the verifier's harmless commutant.

At U/J_nom=50, t/U=0.0707106781, equal hopping on all sixteen links, and40/U
ramps, full4900-state propagation gives

  worst code rejection:      0.1093600;
  residual charge anomaly:   5.43e-6;
  conditional logical phase: 1.09370093 instead of1.53354366.

This is primarily spin leakage and a wrong logical phase, not failed charge
closure. It is preserved in hubbard_entangler_results.json.

We then calibrate TWO continuous parameters: cross hopping and the plateau
duration. No additional interaction is inserted. Four pair-permutation
symmetries and global SU(2) reduce the |11> evolution to an86-dimensional
invariant subspace of the full4900-state charge Hilbert space. This is an
EXACT symmetry reduction retaining all charge states in that sector; it is
not a three-level or spin-only approximation.

The static three-lowest-level ratio suggests a cross exchange ratio near
1.171423. Including both finite ramps and optimizing the return gives

  cross nominal exchange ratio: 1.15481124233933;
  hold time:                     99.6086847971/U;
  two ramps plus hold:           179.6086847971/U.

The optimization used150 objective evaluations on one specified Hamiltonian
and is classical model calibration. It does not prove a global optimum or
measure an experimental device. The resulting parameters were independently
propagated on ALL FOUR logical inputs in the full4900-state Hubbard space.

At160 steps per ramp the full calculation gives

  worst code rejection:      6.31784458e-6;
  worst charge anomaly:      6.28648343e-6;
  off-diagonal logical norm: 1.83e-13;
  conditional phase:         phi_c=1.31595944430817.

The other single-qubit phases are calibrated too. In the ordered logical
basis00,01,10,11 they are approximately

  (-1.248497694, -0.970713459, -0.970713459, 0.623030220).

Removing the known common and one-qubit phases leaves CP(phi_c). We do not
call the change of entangling phase a correction to the old target; instead,

The full-state80-to160-step refinement changes the single-occupation output

3. JOIN THE ACTUAL VERIFIERS, NOT IDEAL PROJECTORS

After the entangler, perform a paid spin-blind charge check on the eight data
sites. If any site is not singly occupied, flag BOTH logical blocks and stop
this gate attempt. Otherwise apply the two prepared-helper verifiers in
parallel. Their onsite U, hopping, ramps and pulse clocks are exactly those
in phase4_hubbard/FINITE_HUBBARD_CLOSURE.txt. Retain every intermediate charge
sector in each six-site pulse sequence; check final site occupations before
helper spin-to-charge conversion.

For one verifier, SU(2) and permutations of the three star leaves imply

 K_F=alpha P0+b B_i+d D_i+c P2,                          (1)

where B_i,D_i split J=1 by rest-three spin, and P2 is the full quintet. The
four complex coefficients are obtained from the complete400-state Hubbard
propagation, not set to their ideal values. Symmetry gives the SAME scalar
coefficient on every magnetic component, so these coefficients reconstruct
the entire sixteen-dimensional accepted operator. Scalar residuals at the
tested precision are below2e-12.

Let G be the256-by4 single-occupation output of the actual entangler, including
all spin leakage. The complete accepted gate-plus-check operator is

                 A=(K_F tensor K_F) G.                          (2)

This composition is physically legitimate because the joining charge check
was explicitly performed and its failure is a recorded erasure. Without
that check, residual charge amplitudes would have to be propagated through
the larger twelve-site process; equation(2) would not justify dropping them.

The filters each take1370.3430837/U, including all ten ramps. Because ideal
gate control preserves GLOBAL total spin zero, its residual spin leakage
after the charge check occurs in matching nonzero spins on BOTH blocks.
Both physical filters must therefore miss it. The complete calculation
finds accepted residual spin-leakage amplitude3.76e-10, not merely a bound
from one filter's worst miss probability.

Any flag in this two-block operation conservatively erases BOTH participants.
A flag does not recover an unknown spin error, or certify that its partner
was untouched. Actual data replacement and outer-code recovery remain
operations with their own times and resources.

4. PREPARATION AND READ CONVERSION IN THE SAME MODEL

Each helper pair is prepared by the reversed480/U charge-separation sequence
in the finite-Hubbard note, followed by a charge herald. On a successful(1,1)
herald its state is exactly a singlet in this model, since total spin is zero
throughout. Preparation failures are known before the helper touches data;
retries and the data's waiting exposure must be charged. Two independent
pairs can be prepared in parallel if the hardware provides both routes.

The final helper conversion also takes480/U. For definiteness the joined
calculation accepts ONLY the selected right-double-occupation signal D_R.
Its false-negative probability on a helper singlet is

                   q_read=2.443258388507008e-5.                  (3)

All triplets remain(1,1) identically in the admitted model. The other double
endpoint D_L has probability6.02e-8 for a singlet; counting it as a singlet
signal would slightly improve(3). The stricter D_R-only event used here is
why(3) exceeds the S_11 population2.43723761742892e-5 quoted elsewhere.

Every final charge result has its own record. On success, each fine D_R
conversion branch multiplies K_F by the same scalar for every data state.
Thus two read conversions replace(2) by

                     A_read=(1-q_read) A.                       (4)

There is no hidden assumption of preserving a triplet's magnetic state in
this readout; the whole triplet result is an erasure. Real charge assignment
errors, premature singlet-triplet relaxation and sensor-induced disturbances
are additional physical errors, not included in(3).

5. ACCESSIBLE FULL-INSTRUMENT BOUND ON VALID ENCODED INPUTS

Let V be the isometry of the calibrated ideal diagonal entangling gate,
including the known common filter phase. Replace every flagged two-block
output by a marked erasure, retaining its classical record. For any input
entangled with an external reference, let

  delta=||A_read-V||,
  p=max eigenvalue(I-A_read^dagger A_read).

The accepted CP-map difference is at most2delta in diamond norm, because
both A_read and V are contractions. The whole rejected instrument, however
finely its record is resolved, has diamond norm at mostp: its effect is
I-A_read^dagger A_read. Therefore its distance to an ideal always-accepted
gate obeys the analytic inequality

   (1/2)||M_actual-M_ideal||_diamond <= delta+p/2.                 (5)

This is the ACCESSIBLE measured-and-erased instrument, with any discarded
reservoir/erased data traced in the operational definition. It is not a
coherently retained Stinespring-field guarantee. The domain is arbitrary
VALID two-logical-qubit input plus reference, not arbitrary initial orbital
occupation. Ideal charge measurements are part of the stated model.

Evaluating(5) on the propagated operators gives

  delta                       3.39324070e-5;
  p                           6.78636625e-5;
  evaluated upper bound       6.78642382e-5.

The inequality is analytic; these numerical values are not rigorously
interval-certified constants. The observed integration refinements and
operator residuals are recorded separately. No sensor, loading, anisotropic
noise, extra-orbital, temperature or experimental calibration error has been
included by calling this number a hardware fidelity.

6. LOCAL OPERATIONS AND AN ACTUAL FINITE-U CZ COMPILER

Finite-U isolated-pair controls need not use an effective exchange formula.
For a zero-bias pair the singlet couples to symmetric double occupation via
[[0,-2t],[-2t,U]]. Let Omega=sqrt(U^2+16t^2). A rectangular contact with

  tau=2pi m/Omega,
  theta=pi m(1-U/Omega)

returns charge EXACTLY and implements exp(i theta P_S). For any desired
positive theta modulo2pi, choose m large enough to keep t below the stated
ceiling. Its time is(2pi m-2theta)/U. Switching bandwidth and higher orbitals
remain qualified separately; exact return here is within the admitted
finite one-orbital Hamiltonian.

These pair rotations act on a singlet-encoded block about two axes separated
by60degrees. A fixed conjugation with theta_axis=acos(-1/3) produces an axis
orthogonal to Z, giving an explicit five-pair-pulse decomposition of any
single-qubit unitary. The program verifies each two-site charge revival and
the complete logical decomposition.

Use the calibrated phi_c in the three-native-gate CZ construction of
FAST_SINGLET_EXCHANGE_GATE.txt. It gives beta=1.2270093162 and axis-z component
0.3370549142. The supplied conservative serial compiler consists of

  three physical checked entangling cells;
  twenty-two exact finite-U local pair contacts;
  known local phase corrections included in those contacts.

The target CZ matrix residual is6.36e-16 up to a global phase. This verifies
the compiler algebra; it is not a prediction of measured gate fidelity.
An independent targeted audit propagated all twenty-two local pair contacts
in the full36-state four-site Hubbard model using their actual physical
edges, and assembled the chronological CZ sequence. Its largest local
charge/spin-return amplitude was1.49e-14 and its compiled CZ residual was
1.98e-14. See phase4_spin_choice/HUBBARD_GATECELL_AUDIT.txt.
The local contacts take2608.5487372/U. Three cells with one successful
parallel preparation attempt and read conversion take7529.8553056/U.
Their combined SPECIFIED control duration is therefore

                    10138.4040428/U.                            (6)

Before adding device errors, the formal sum of the numerically evaluated

implemented-channel bounds; treating a finite calculation as such a device

7. FULL ELAPSED TIME AND WHAT REMAINS PHYSICAL

For one native successful operation, specified control time is

  480/U preparation +179.6086848/U gate +1370.3430837/U verification
             +480/U read conversion =2509.9517685/U.

Add actual reservoir loading/reset, routing and detachment, four stages of
charge sensing/heralding as used above, classical latency, any preparation
retries, and flagged data replacement/recovery. Parallel verification and
preparation require two helper pairs and independent channels; with fewer
resources their times add. No estimate of a spin-coherence allowance may use
only179.6/U while discarding the much longer preparation, checking and sensing.

After a successful D_R read, the helper pair occupies a unique pure
charge/spin state. It can undergo the same heralded separation sequence
before reuse; fresh reservoir electrons need not be loaded after every
success. Failed branches still require a specified reset/loading path.
This reuse follows a completed measurement record and does not assume that
an unmeasured correlated auxiliary has reset itself.

For bounded spin-breaking Hamiltonian norm beta, a conservative exposure term
is beta*T_elapsed. A stochastic rate model uses its integrated rate. Residual
OFF hopping, finite charge sensors and extra orbitals have their own complete-
duration channel allowances. The inherited local energy-transfer theorem can
help qualify their ACTUAL couplings; it does not manufacture the required
spectra or sensor performance.

The original concurrent-control theorem is exact for Heisenberg exchanges.
Concurrent Hubbard hopping was tested and was not exactly commuting. This
joined construction therefore uses the qualified sequential pair controls
instead. It does not cite the ideal commutator as a microscopic gate proof.

The achieved physical-model capability is a calibrated universal exchange
operation with an explicit full-singlet acceptance test and a complete
erasure outlet. A fabricated layout with this dense graph and all external
resources, a fault-tolerant schedule for errors during checking, and a fair
system-level comparison with strong existing exchange/erasure hardware remain
unearned. Those are concrete next decisions, not new hidden premises of the
finite-spin theorem.
