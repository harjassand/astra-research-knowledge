PHASE 4: REPLACE A WEAK TRANSFER CHANNEL WITH AN EXACT EXCHANGE VERIFIER
31 October 2026

Chosen question
---------------
Can ordinary calibrated exchange and one prepared singlet pair implement a
complete check of an encoded singlet qubit, preserve its arbitrary logical
state on acceptance, reject ALL spin leakage, and remain compatible with
useful logical operations?

The physical obstruction selected is the cost and compatibility of leakage
detection during computation. This is narrower than a complete quantum
computing architecture, but more consequential than improving the inherited
weak-carrier readout while its strong calibrated comparator remains available.

The target changed deliberately. Uniformity beside every unprepared source is
retained as an inherited theorem, but is not imposed on this apparatus. Singlet
preparation, matched relative exchange control, coherent storage of two helper
spins, calibration and geometry are paid resources. No claim of polarization-
free helpers means that their joint singlet state was unprepared.

Resource model and output
-------------------------
Four data spins encode a qubit in their J=0 multiplicity. A two-spin singlet
helper is prepared, coupled through a three-edge exchange star plus a separate
exchange edge, reused coherently, and finally read in the singlet/triplet basis.
The desired output is a complete quantum instrument, not just a correct label:
acceptance must equal rho -> P0 rho P0 for arbitrary input/reference, and every
rejected branch must be identified as an erasure requiring actual replacement
or outer recovery. Both J=1 and J=2 must be rejected.

Useful operations required by the consequence are local logical control,
an entangling gate, preparation, final readout, and the treatment of a rejected
block. The exchange-level local and pair controls are constructed explicitly.
Finite Hubbard charge dynamics, preparation and charge conversion are studied
separately in one parameter set. Detector calibration, extra orbital admission,
fabrication, thermal/source characterization and measured complete-duration
coherence are not silently acquired by the spin theorem.

Why the carrier is no longer primary
------------------------------------
In the inherited perpendicular Coulomb geometry, both useful transfer and
ordinary data hopping carry the same small data-orbital overlap. Their ratio
contains an extra quadrupole/dipole geometric suppression. Ordinary calibrated
singlet capture uses the same OFF barrier and charge sensor, while avoiding
the fresh orbital carrier and its longer spin and orbital exposure. The full
linked calculation is in phase4_spin_choice/COLD_CARRIER_RESOURCE_DECISION.txt.
It is a scoped comparison and does not invalidate the local energy-transfer
theorem, the source-uniform cold-carrier instrument, or collisional cooling.

Constructive sequence and decisive outcomes
------------------------------------------
1. Energy-selective reset with a three-spin code and one exchange edge has a
   spectral-moment obstruction to an exact constant-contact reset. Its accurate
   gap-assisted limit is slower than strong established compiled alternatives.

2. Four-spin singlets permit a clean accepted branch under local exchange
   cooling, but blind cooling cannot recover unknown local-spin errors. The
   selected operation is erasure detection, not a claimed correction of an
   uncorrectable error set. Alternating-site energy filters work but still pay
   detuning, false flags and repeated helper contacts.

3. Replacing energy selection by a matched exchange geometry gives a single
   query with singular values 1,1/2,0 on code and leakage sectors. These values
   are derived from the actual spin Hamiltonian.

4. Coherently reusing the SAME helper pair and inserting two singlet phases
   implements the cubic (4x^3-x)/3. Three queries become an EXACT full singlet
   projector. This removes the need for a large energy gap or repeated fresh
   carriers in the ideal spin mechanism.

5. Every exchange among the other three data spins commutes with the complete
   verifier. Those exchanges generate the full logical SU(2), so local gates
   and verification coexist without assuming their compatibility.

6. A separate explicit eight-spin exchange graph has an exactly returning
   entangling pulse at 2pi/(sqrt(7) Delta). Three such contacts plus stated local
   rotations compile CZ. This pays dense connectivity; a sparse established
   exchange compiler remains an admissible replacement.

7. Full finite-charge calculations test whether the spin mechanism survives
   outside its projection. Simple pair calibration is essential. The leading
   additional uniform-star correction lies in the rest-spin commutant and is
   harmless to the ideal filter, while charge excursions and higher corrections
   remain explicitly propagated. This supplies a finite-model construction,
   not an experimental device claim.

8. The verifier has scoped active-coupler fault detection, but a late magnetic
   fault can leave unflagged leakage. Correlated vector dissipation can filter
   logical amplitudes even when individual jumps are rejected. Exchange-angle
   errors of logical controls are not diagnosed by singlet membership.

9. Destroying helper singlet/triplet coherence between queries gives leaked
   false-accept probability 3/8 or 7/16 while good input still passes perfectly.
   This is the decisive resource counterexample: final good-state fidelity
   alone is not a valid test of the apparatus.
