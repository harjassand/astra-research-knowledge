ENERGY-SELECTIVE EXCHANGE LEAKAGE RESET: EXACT BLOCKS AND LIMITS
31 October 2026

Status and purpose
------------------
This complementary construction replaces the weak perpendicular Coulomb
carrier with ordinary exchange and a fresh singlet pair. It earns an
approximate reset of all four leaked spin projections of a three-spin
exchange-only qubit, preserves a commuting logical control axis, and gives
an exact obstruction to perfect reset by one constant edge. It does not
outperform calibrated reset-if-leaked sequences merely by taking a large
gap. The root investigation has therefore moved to a four-spin singlet
erasure filter; this note preserves the useful three-spin calculation.

1. Physical operations and full Hamiltonian
------------------------------------------
Let spins 1,2,3 be data and spins 4,5 a helper pair. Write S_i=sigma_i/2
and hbar=1. The data code has S_D=1/2 and dimension four (logical qubit
times gauge spin). The four-dimensional S_D=3/2 sector is leakage. An
equal antiferromagnetic exchange triangle has, up to a scalar,

 H_D=Delta P_L,   Delta=3 J_D/2,
 P_L=(S_D^2-3/4 I)/3.

The helpers are loaded as a singlet, have an adjustable positive singlet-
triplet splitting J_H, and meet the data through one exchange edge:

 H=Delta P_L+J_H P_HT+g S_3.S_4.                         (1)

Prepared occupations, calibrated exchange values, switching, and loading
the helper singlet are resources. Following a contact, BOTH helper
electrons are removed or replaced; they can carry away angular momentum.
This is a finite unitary contact followed by physical discard, not an
assumed spin-relaxation Lindblad jump. If retained instead, their correlated
state must be tracked and no reset theorem follows by relabelling them as
fresh. Loading can use a double occupation in a single admitted orbital,
whose two-electron ground state is a singlet, followed by separation.
The loading, ramps, isolation, and transport times all enter elapsed time.

The exchange model itself requires ordinary charge and orbital admission.
For a Hubbard realization J approximately 4 t^2/U is a low-energy limit;
finite J/U charge admixture, higher orbitals, valley modes, spin-orbit terms,
and magnetic gradients do not disappear from the physical budget.

2. Exact multiplicity blocks in the complete five-spin model
----------------------------------------------------------
Both total spin and S_12^2 commute with (1). Define logical zero by
S_12=0, logical one by S_12=1 within S_D=1/2. The following matrices are
exact, not fitted effective models. State phases fix displayed signs.

Leaked input has total J=3/2 and S_12=1. In the basis

 A=(data 3/2, helper singlet),
 B=(data 1/2 logical one, helper triplet) coupled to J=3/2,
 C=(data 3/2, helper triplet) coupled to J=3/2,

 H_L=diag(Delta,J_H,Delta+J_H)+g M_L,

 M_L = [ 0             -1/(2 sqrt3)   sqrt15/12 ]
       [ -1/(2 sqrt3)  -1/12         sqrt5/6   ]
       [ sqrt15/12      sqrt5/6       -1/6      ].       (2)

The same multiplicity matrix applies to every magnetic projection
m=-3/2,-1/2,1/2,3/2. Thus the protocol does not privilege a gauge axis.

For valid logical zero, total J=1/2 and S_12=0. In the helper singlet,
helper triplet basis,

 H_0=diag(0,J_H)+g M_0,
 M_0 = [ 0          sqrt3/4 ]
       [ sqrt3/4    -1/2   ].                           (3)

For valid logical one, total J=1/2 and S_12=1, use

 a=(data 1/2, helper singlet),
 b=(data 1/2, helper triplet) coupled to J=1/2,
 c=(data 3/2, helper triplet) coupled to J=1/2.

 H_1=diag(0,J_H,Delta+J_H)+g M_1,
 M_1 = [ 0             -1/(4 sqrt3)    1/sqrt6  ]
       [ -1/(4 sqrt3)   1/6            sqrt2/6 ]
       [ 1/sqrt6        sqrt2/6        -5/12   ].        (4)

Equations (2)-(4) contain every branch reached from arbitrary data states
and a fresh helper singlet. Other total-spin sectors in the full 32-state
space are unreachable from this initialized domain. The companion program
constructs the entire 32 by 32 Hamiltonian and verifies these blocks and
the instrument, including all leaked magnetic projections.

3. Exact obstruction to a perfect constant single-edge contact
-------------------------------------------------------------
A perfect LRU using (1) would map A entirely into B: B is the unique
valid-data state in the fixed J=3/2, S_12=1 multiplicity space. If
exp(-i H_L T) A=e^{i phi} B, then A and B must have equal spectral
moments, because the propagator commutes with every power of H_L.

The first moment requires

 J_H=Delta+g/12.

At this value the second moments differ by

 <B|H_L^2|B>-<A|H_L^2|A>
   =g^2[(sqrt5/6)^2-(sqrt15/12)^2]
   =5 g^2/144.                                        (5)

For any g nonzero this is a contradiction. No waiting time, no finite
choice of Delta, and no constant spectator-pair energy shift can repair
it. This is a no-go for this Hamiltonian and one constant contact, not
for connected multi-edge devices, shaped controls, coherent composite
contacts, or general exchange LRUs.

4. A controlled energy-selective regime
---------------------------------------
For r=g/Delta much less than one, tune the active A-B resonance and
eliminate C perturbatively. Its second-order contribution is

 Sigma_AB=-(g^2/Delta) outer(v,v),
 v=(sqrt15/12,sqrt5/6).

This yields the analytic, non-optimized prescription

 J_H=Delta+g/12+5g^2/(144Delta),
 lambda_eff= g/(2sqrt3) [1+5g/(12Delta)],
 T=pi/(2 lambda_eff).                                  (6)

The useful transfer is first order in g. Equation (5) still precludes
exact transfer; the admixture of C and finite-gap code excursions remain.

The leading valid-code energy shifts are

 deltaE_0=-3g^2/(16Delta),
 deltaE_1=-5g^2/(48Delta),
 deltaE_0-deltaE_1=-g^2/(12Delta).

A calibrated logical Z phase can be corrected afterward. Equivalently a
negative increment delta J_12=-g^2/(12Delta) to an already positive J_12
compensates at this order. It changes both A and B equally and therefore
does not disturb the intended leakage resonance.

5. Exact instrument on valid code inputs
----------------------------------------
Let J_C append the helper singlet to the four-dimensional data code. The
helper-singlet Kraus operator restricted to the code is exactly

 K_S=J_C^dagger exp(-iHT) J_C
     =diag(k_0,k_0,k_1,k_1).                            (7)

This follows from total SU(2) and S_12^2 conservation. In particular it
does not acquire hidden gauge dependence. Correct arg(k_1/k_0) by a known
pair-12 exchange, so that K_S is positive diagonal. Let

 p=max(1-|k_0|^2,1-|k_1|^2).

The sum of all helper-triplet-output Kraus effects on code inputs is at
most p I. Including data leakage in these branches, the reduced channel
on code inputs obeys

 (1/2)||E_code-id_code||_diamond
     <= 1-sqrt(1-p)+p/2.                               (8)

Proof: the corrected singlet-Kraus contraction differs from identity by
at most 1-sqrt(1-p). The triangle inequality for its two-sided channel
action gives the same half-diamond bound; the remaining CP subchannel has
diamond norm at most p. This includes arbitrary reference entanglement.
It does not claim a full instrument bound for arbitrary coherent
superpositions between the code and leaked sectors. Those coherences
require an additional comparison channel and generally square-root terms.

Exact numerical checks using (6), with Delta=1:

 .20    5.02283   8.12705e-3             1.88844e-2    1.89294e-2
 .10    5.22374   4.57694e-3             2.33408e-3    2.33476e-3
 .05    5.33035   2.86848e-4             1.63804e-3    1.63837e-3
 .02    5.39643   3.03993e-5             2.79853e-4    2.79862e-4

All four leaked projections have the same residual-leakage effect within
roundoff. The nonmonotonic constants are finite-time phase interference;
the table is an exact model calculation, not a uniform asymptotic bound
or a claim about a material device. Full precision is in
exchange_lru_results.json, reproducible by check_exchange_lru.py.

6. What can run concurrently
----------------------------
For arbitrary time-dependent scalar coefficients in (1),

 [S_1.S_2,H(t)]=0.                                     (9)

The same holds when J_12(t) is independently driven. The gate therefore
factors exactly from the contact and supplies a complete logical Z axis
while reset is active. This is more than an energy-limit approximation.

The other pair exchanges S_2.S_3 and S_1.S_3 do not generally commute with
the cross edge. Attaching the helper to another spectator spin can change
the available commuting axis but requires another port and its controls.
Cross-block entangling exchanges usually fail to commute with the local
S_D^2 penalty even when their edge does not share the helper contact spin.
Thus (9) does not license unrestricted universal computation concurrently
with this reset. For a four-spin singlet block, exchanges among the three
spins OTHER than the contacted spin offer a larger commuting algebra; the
root construction exploits that separate fact.

7. Linked duration, calibration, and spin-noise burden
-----------------------------------------------------
If every available pair exchange is bounded by J_max, then

 Delta<=3J_max/2,
 T approximately pi sqrt3/g
   >= (2pi/sqrt3)/(r J_max).                           (10)

At r=.02 the analytic contact takes about 180/J_max, before helper loading,
isolation and removal. A compiled LRU may use more switches but maximal
exchange strengths, so pulse count alone is not a fair speed comparison.
The available short calibrated alternatives must be timed at the same
J_max and connectivity.

If the differential resonance uncertainty is sigma_delta=alpha Delta,
a representative linked error budget includes

 epsilon ~ A r^2+B (alpha/r)^2+gamma T+epsilon_load
           +epsilon_switch+epsilon_projection.         (11)

The first term is finite-gap mixing, the second detuning of the active
Rabi transfer. Their optimum is r of order sqrt(alpha), leaving an error
of order alpha, before exposure and loading costs. Merely increasing the
gap cannot freely improve both. The exact coefficients depend on the
calibrated pulse and noise spectrum; (11) states the competing scaling,
not an established experimental fit or a universal lower bound.

Abrupt helper removal turns residual virtual triplet occupation into
actual disturbance. Smooth ramps might improve this at additional time
and with phase calibration, but this note has not proved a superior
ramped protocol. Repeated fresh contacts also accumulate code disturbance;
they are not a free route to arbitrary accuracy.

8. Strong comparison and scientific conclusion
----------------------------------------------
Langrock and DiVincenzo, arXiv:2012.09517, provide an exact compiled
reset-if-leaked operation with two singlet-initialized helper spins and
fourteen nearest-neighbor exchanges in nine layers. Flagging and identity
on valid logical states together require nineteen exchanges in ten layers;
the fourteen-exchange version should not be called the flagged version.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.09517

The Nature 2026 silicon processor uses initialized ancillas and 21/24-
exchange reset-if-leaked units over thirty time steps to control leakage
accumulation. This is a demonstrated consumer, not an ideal universality
argument. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10754-7

The present calculation adds an explicit energy-selective first-order
contact, its complete reachable spectrum, a useful commuting control,
and a sharp spectral-moment obstruction. It has not established a useful
advantage over those calibrated operations. Its main constructive lesson
is to pursue exactly harmless detection or a stronger connected contact,
rather than assume a large energy penalty makes an arbitrary weak reset
fast or accurate at fixed physical resources.

Finally, reset is not recovery of lost logical information. For the
four-spin singlet code P_0, P_0 S_i^alpha P_0=0 detects local vector errors,
but P_0 S_1^z S_2^z P_0 has eigenvalues -1/4 and 1/12, violating the
Knill-Laflamme scalar condition for correcting arbitrary unknown-site
single-spin errors. Blind cooling back into a singlet manifold can therefore
change the logical state. Detection with a declared erasure is the honest
capability unless an additional code and recovery operation are supplied.
