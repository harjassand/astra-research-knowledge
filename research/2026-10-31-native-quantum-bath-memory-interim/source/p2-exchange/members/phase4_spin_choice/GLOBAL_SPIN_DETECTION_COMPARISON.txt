SINGLET ERASURE FILTER: COMPARATOR AND NOISE-SCOPE AUDIT
31 October 2026

This is a complementary audit of the root construction. It distinguishes
established representation theory from the concrete exchange instrument,
and supplies counterexamples to stronger noise and recovery claims.

1. What was already established
-------------------------------
Bacon, Kempe, Lidar and Whaley, Universal Fault-Tolerant Computation on
Decoherence-Free Subspaces, PRL85,1758 (2000), explicitly identify the
four-spin singlet code as a distance-two code detecting arbitrary
single-spin errors. Their exchange entangler traverses the fourteen-
dimensional eight-spin global singlet space and returns to the product
of two local singlet codes. Exchange universality within singlet spaces
and protection during a global singlet excursion are established capital.
The continuous-time vector-selection observation below is a direct
corollary, not a defensible standalone novelty claim.
Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/9909058
Relevant locations: distance-two statement near equation4; encoded
controlled-phase construction near the end of the universal-gates section.

Heinz, Sharma and Kattemolle, Exchange-only qubit stabilized by a
single-spin qubit, arXiv:2608.14214 (2026), use a three-edge equal-exchange
star to transfer the three-spin gauge into an auxiliary spin. They also
measure XXXX and ZZZZ for a four-spin singlet-only code with two auxiliary
Loss-DiVincenzo spins, single-spin rotations, and flagged measurement
circuits. Their fault model includes arbitrary two-spin depolarizing
faults, and their specially constructed gate claims concern pi rotations
and swaps, not an established universal fault-tolerant gate set. This is
the closest direct prior use of the root filter's star ingredient.
Primary: https://arxiv.org/html/2608.14214v1
Relevant locations: II.1 equation1, II.2, II.3, IV.2 and IV.3.

There is therefore no claim that singlet encoding, error detection,
collective gauge transfer, or universal exchange control was discovered
here. The candidate contribution is an economical, exactly computed full
singlet-admission instrument assembled from those physical ingredients,
and the operations that can continue while it is applied.

2. The new explicit instrument and its actual resources
-------------------------------------------------------
This section records the root result for comparison; the root and the
independent filter agent own the full derivation and 64-state audit.
Let i be one data spin, R the total spin of the other three, and a,b
fresh helper spins in their singlet. The base contact is

 H_F=J(R.S_a+S_i.S_b),    U=exp[-i(pi/J)H_F].           (1)

There are FOUR pair exchanges: three matched star edges to a, and one
disjoint edge i-b. The two terms commute. For R=1/2 the contact exchanges
the R gauge and i with the two helpers, with phase -i. For R=3/2,
R.S_a has eigenvalues 3/4 and -5/4, so its pi contact is the scalar
exp(-3pi i/4); the full contact is -SWAP_i,b. Appending and contracting
the helper singlet gives

 K=<S_ab|U|S_ab>=-i P_0-(1/2)D_i,                     (2)

where P_0 is the entire two-dimensional four-data-spin singlet code and
D_i projects onto rest-three spin R=3/2. D_i contains one J=1 copy and
all J=2 states. The other J=1 copies have zero pass amplitude. Thus
same-site repetition alone suppresses any incoming leakage's pass
probability by at least 4^-N, with exactly unit pass on the clean code.

The root's coherent completion sets phi=acos(1/3),
R_phi=exp(i phi P_Sab), and uses

 C=U R_phi U^dagger R_-phi U.

Its helper-singlet compression is

 K_C=K(4K^dagger K-I)/3=-i P_0.                        (3)

The relevant singular values 1,1/2,0 are filtered exactly. This is a
finite exact amplitude-polynomial construction; it does not require a
large detuning or an approximate reservoir jump. With positive exchange,
U^dagger is the same four-edge contact for duration 3pi/J. The two helper
phases can be implemented by positive helper exchange with total phase
area 2pi. The coherent interaction time is therefore

 T_F=5pi/J+2pi/J_h.                                    (4)

Resource count: four data spins; two prepared singlet helpers; three
simultaneous four-edge contact intervals; two helper-exchange intervals;
one final binary helper singlet/triplet measurement. That is five control
settings but FOURTEEN pair-exchange activations. With J_h=J, (4) is 7pi/J.
Switching, helper preparation, routing, final charge conversion, sensing,
and isolation are additional elapsed operations, not hidden in (4).

The complete noiseless accepted branch is the exact CP map
rho -> P_0 rho P_0, including reference entanglement and arbitrary
coherences between incoming spin sectors. The flag branch may discard
the data and output an erasure label; it does not need to preserve leaked
spin orientation or diagnose which spin was faulty. This is an exact
erasure instrument. It is not a claim of a nondemolition identity within
the whole rejected J>0 space.

Every exchange acting solely among the rest-three data spins commutes
with all terms in the five-setting filter. Those exchanges generate the
full encoded single-qubit algebra in the singlet code. Therefore arbitrary
time-dependent local gates made from them factor from the ENTIRE filter.
Matched star calibration, helper phase calibration, and clean exchange
projection are still maintained controls. No generic fault-tolerance
claim follows merely from the exact noiseless factorization.

3. Strong conventional comparisons at the actual task
-----------------------------------------------------
The flagged SOEO stabilizer circuits above require single-spin-addressable
helpers and a larger compiled readout circuit, and address a stronger
circuit-fault model. The exact exchange filter uses only a singlet resource
and binary S/T readout and directly decides full J=0 membership. A fair
comparison must include its simultaneous star matching and five-setting
duration. Neither its lower setting count nor its exact ideal Kraus
operator proves superior logical error rates under real control noise.

Langrock and DiVincenzo, arXiv:2012.09517, give exact reset-if-leaked
sequences for a three-spin encoding: fourteen nearest-neighbor exchanges
over nine layers without the full flagging requirement; nineteen over
ten layers when flagging and valid-code identity are both imposed.
The hardware Nature2026 processor uses 21/24-exchange LRUs over thirty
time steps. These solve reset, a related but different task; comparing
fourteen individual activations with fourteen sequential pulses while
ignoring connectivity, simultaneous controls and duration would mislead.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.09517
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10754-7

Siegel and Benjamin, arXiv:2601.10461 (2026), provide a strong alternate
erasure architecture for two-spin S/T0 codes. Four calibrated exchange/
Zeeman H,CZ operations transfer a state to a fresh singlet pair and reading
the old pair flags and resets leakage. Their circuit and decoding study
retain the physical noise of these gates. This encoding detects single-
spin bit-flip leakage but leaves phase-type logical noise. Their richer
S,T0,T+/- readout can be weakened at a downstream decoding cost. The new
four-spin filter instead treats all local vector directions using SU(2)
symmetry, at the cost of more data spins and matched star connectivity.
https://arxiv.org/html/2601.10461v1

For entangling gates, Foulk et al.'s SAGE construction uses a sparse
T-shaped four-spin graph and one interblock exchange. Its reported weak-
coupling scale is J_c^2/J_0, with intrinsic leakage cost of order
(J_c/J_0)^2. The root's exact finite-ratio revival gate trades that weak-
coupling time scale for two internal K4 graphs and four matched crosslinks.
A single elapsed interaction interval was already available in SAGE and
earlier adiabatic supercoherent architectures. The relevant potential
increment is exact return at a fast finite-ratio pulse, not the phrase
"single pulse." https://arxiv.org/html/2501.18589v2

Targeted searches also inspected Weinstein-Hellberg-Levy's supercoherent
cluster construction (quant-ph/0506032), Weinstein-Hellberg's scalable
six-spin modification (quant-ph/0606157), and the few-body correction
analysis of Woodworth-Mizel-Lidar (quant-ph/0504165). They establish close
prior gate/control approaches and emphasize coupling-graph burdens.
No identical root sqrt7 revival graph or exact five-setting filter was

4. Exact vector-insertion selection rule and its scope
----------------------------------------------------
Let P be the product of local singlet-code projectors on a spin register.
Let U_0(t2,t1) be any time-dependent SU(2)-scalar control, including
interblock exchange. An ideal complete gate must return the data to P,
but intermediate states may explore the larger GLOBAL J=0 manifold.
Each single-spin vector S_i^alpha maps global J=0 into global J=1.
Consequently, at every insertion time t,

 P U_0(T,t) S_i^alpha U_0(t,0) P=0.                    (5)

The result holds for arbitrary linear combinations and coefficients that
are operators on a separate bath. A scalar source-controlled exchange
Hamiltonian can be included in U_0. No classical-field assumption or
minimum energy gap is needed for this representation-theoretic statement.

For a bounded coherent perturbation V(t)=sum_i,alpha S_i^alpha B_i,alpha(t),
let eta=integral_0^T ||V(t)|| dt in the scalar-control interaction picture.
The accepted first Dyson term vanishes. The norm of the remaining accepted
amplitude error is at most exp(eta)-1-eta. This is second order in small
Hamiltonian exposure, not an exact statement that all accepted errors
vanish. Two vector insertions can return to global J=0 and act logically.

Independent stochastic single-spin Pauli jumps also satisfy a simple
first-event rejection statement, provided their no-jump attenuation is
scalar. Each (S_i^alpha)^2=I/4 makes that condition natural for independent
Hermitian component jumps. Gate errors of arbitrary two-spin form are
outside this statement. In particular, a scalar exchange-angle fault
S_1.S_2 acts inside the singlet code as diag(-3/4,1/4) and passes every
total-spin membership test while changing the logical phase.

5. Existing Pauli stabilizers also catch the first vector insertion
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The simultaneous +1 eigenspace of XXXX and ZZZZ has dimension FOUR:
two J=0 states, no J=1 states, and two J=2 states. Thus these checks are
not full singlet admission. This distinction justifies full J filtering
for arbitrary leakage and for J=2 residuals after entangling operations.

It does NOT give an exclusive advantage for equation (5). If every block
passes both Pauli checks, the register is invariant under global pi
rotations about x and z. A global J=1 vector has no state invariant under
both. Equivalently, the products of all X and of all Z commute with every
scalar exchange and detect any single physical Pauli. Thus the existing
checks also catch a single vector fault at arbitrary exchange-gate time,
even if that error spreads to many physical spins. The full singlet
filter's distinction is the smaller accepted space and its concrete
exchange-only implementation, not a new first-order selection rule.

6. A decisive complete-channel caveat: correlated vector dissipation
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Rejecting each vector JUMP does not by itself imply that the complete
accepted Lindblad channel is harmless to first order in its rate. For
the four-spin code take the spatially correlated Hermitian jump

 L=S_1^z+S_2^z.

Then P_0 L P_0=0 but

 P_0 L^dagger L P_0=(1/3)P_0 S_12^2 P_0
                   =diag(0,2/3).                     (6)

For a dissipator gamma D[L], endpoint singlet postselection gives

 E_accept(rho)=rho-(gamma T/2){diag(0,2/3),rho}
                 +O((gamma T)^2).                    (7)

The first jump is rejected, but no-jump survival weakly learns the logical
state. For input logical |+>, the conditional trace distance from the
input is gamma T/6+O((gamma T)^2), while rejection is gamma T/3 to first
order. This is a physical counterexample to treating arbitrary correlated
vector Lindblad noise as pure erasure to first order in its rate.

The exact dephasing-channel calculation confirms this: at gamma T=.001,
acceptance=.9996669165 and conditional trace distance=.0001665973; the
distance divided by gamma T approaches 1/6. The distinction is not a
conflict with the coherent Dyson statement: a Markovian rate is already
second order in a microscopic system-bath coupling, and a bath elimination
can generate non-scalar conditional survival at that order.

7. Detection is not logical recovery
------------------------------------
The same finite spin calculation gives

 spec(P_0 S_1^z S_2^z P_0)={-1/4,1/12}.

This violates the scalar Knill-Laflamme condition for correcting arbitrary
unknown-site vector faults using only the four-spin singlet code. An
outlet that cools every leaked state back into J=0 is therefore not by
itself a recovery channel for the stored logical information. The exact
filter correctly outputs a flag and permits a higher-level erasure code
to use that location. Loading replacements, re-encoding, syndrome timing
and higher-level recovery remain operations in such a consequence.

The exact five-setting filter is the strongest concrete operation in this
