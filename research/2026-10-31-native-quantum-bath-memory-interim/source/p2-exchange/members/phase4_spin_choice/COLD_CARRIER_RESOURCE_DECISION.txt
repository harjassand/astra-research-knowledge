COLD COULOMB CARRIERS: LINKED RESOURCE COMPARISON AND RESEARCH CHOICE
31 October 2026

Decision
--------
Do not retain the perpendicular quadrupole cold-carrier S/T cycle as the
primary capability once an arbitrary active source is optional. Preserve
its exact interaction, admission theorem, and source-uniform instrument as
research capital. For a prepared/calibrated apparatus, ordinary one-body
singlet-to-charge capture is a substantially stronger comparator than the
failed naive exchange phase pulse. It uses the same data orbitals, detuning,
charge sensor, OFF barrier, and triplet-preserving spin symmetry, while
avoiding the weak quadrupole coupling and fresh orbital carriers.

This is a scoped engineering decision, not a universal impossibility result
for collisional cooling or a claim that every exchange gate dominates every
weak capacitive interaction.

1. The linked geometric scaling
-------------------------------
Use the actual perpendicular geometry derived in
../phase3_fast_detector/COULOMB_ENERGY_CARRIER.txt. The data transition density
has a quadrupole Q_D; the helper energy doublet has a transition dipole d_H.
At separation R,

 lambda = (3C/2) Q_D d_H/R^4 + O(R^-6),
 kappa_static = -C Q_D/(2R^3) + O(R^-5).

For localized data wells, Q_D is proportional to their overlap S_D times
a_D^2. A helper energy doublet has d_H of order a_H without an additional
helper-overlap penalty. Ordinary data hopping has scale

 t_D ~ E_orb S_D,
 |lambda|/|t_D| ~ A [C/E_orb] a_D^2 a_H/R^4,             (1)

where A is a dimensionless orbital-shape factor. The SAME S_D cancels in
this ratio. Reducing data overlap therefore suppresses useful Coulomb
transfer and ordinary one-body transfer together. It does not make the
Coulomb route relatively faster. The exact finite-separation coefficients
in the previous work make this comparison quantitative.

For the compact box data modes on [-a,a], E_unit=hbar^2/(ma^2), and

 t_D = 3 pi^2 E_unit/16 =1.850550825 E_unit.

The exact Coulomb quadrature gives the following |lambda| in units C/a:

 R/a      |lambda|/(C/a)       |lambda|/t_D divided by chi
 1.5       .0094474529          .00510521
 2         .0028091862          .00151803
 4         .0001642624          .0000887641
 8         .0000100858          .00000545016

Here chi=(C/a)/E_unit. In the weak or moderate interaction regime chi=1,
the direct one-body capture is about 196, 659, 11266, or 183481 times faster,
respectively. These are comparisons INSIDE the explicit projected model,
not measured device numbers. Taking chi very large cannot be justified by
keeping this same box-orbital projection: interaction-induced distortions
and higher orbitals must then be recalculated.

For a true double well t_D is adjustable; equation (1), rather than the box
number, is the useful linked scaling. A close helper or specially shaped
orbital may improve A. Such an improvement must be calculated; there is
no generally available separation limit in which the R^-4 matrix element
becomes stronger than the direct transfer already used to define the data
doublet.

2. Compare the complete actual task
-----------------------------------
In the calibrated selected-charge two-level system, both couplings have
the Hubbard singlet matrix factor sqrt(2). A capture followed by a return
therefore requires at least the following coherent interaction times for
simple pi/2 transfers:

 T_direct,int = pi/(sqrt(2)|t_D|),
 T_cold,int   = pi/(sqrt(2)|lambda|).

Give both protocols the SAME two full-charge reads, data-energy ramps,
OFF isolation, and code loading. The cold protocol additionally pays fresh
ground helpers, helper compensation, loading/routing and removal. Even if
all those extra times are set to zero, its interaction-time ratio is exactly
|t_D/lambda|. When sensing dominates the elapsed time this ratio is diluted,
but the cold construction still has no time advantage from the weaker
matrix element or extra helper operations.

The direct comparator does not have to infer S/T from a calibrated exchange
phase. It coherently transfers the singlet charge, freezes the charge, reads
it, and returns it. Triplet orientations remain untouched in the same
spin-independent two-orbital domain. Calibrated adiabatic or composite
capture can trade a constant or accuracy-dependent overhead for robustness;
the added robustness must be compared against the actual large speed margin.

An exchange phase gate instead has J approximately 4t_D^2/U. Since J is
quadratic in S_D while lambda is linear, at extremely small overlap a cold
carrier can be faster than J. That fact does NOT defeat the stronger direct
charge-capture comparator for a full S/T instrument. It also does not show
a complete computation is faster, since a coherent exchange processor and
a measurement-only processor have different routing and ancilla costs.

3. The same geometry links filtering and elapsed spin exposure
-------------------------------------------------------------
Without scalar cancellation, ordinary hopping t_D is an OFF-RESONANT nuisance
during the weak lambda transfer. Its virtual shift is of order t_D^2/Delta.
Over time of order 1/lambda, the coherent cost is of order

 t_D^2/(Delta |lambda|).

The local source theorem adds M/(Delta |lambda|), with
M=||[H_E,V_off]|| (plus the admitted conditional shifts). A source commutator
may be independent of total source size, but it is not independent of the
long useful-transfer time. At target coherent error epsilon, an indicative
sufficient condition is

 Delta >> [t_D^2+M]/(epsilon |lambda|).                 (2)

The helper gap must simultaneously lie below the admitted higher-orbital
and unwanted-charge thresholds. Thus the weak useful matrix element makes
the source/orbital filtering burden MORE demanding, not less. This is a
linked resource inequality, not a list of independent adjustable knobs.

Known scalar one-body terms can sometimes be cancelled with calibrated
controls, so (2) is not a universal lower bound. That cancellation should
be used if this apparatus is built. It still does not remove the speed
comparison in section 2, and makes the relevance of the original arbitrary-
source information contract narrower.

During charge sensing, raising the data barrier suppresses bare, source-
induced and probe-induced hopping through the same transition densities.
The recorded-detector isolation theorem can replace a linear OFF-hopping
debit by a quadratic one when its hypotheses hold. The direct capture
comparator can use exactly the same barrier and detector improvement. It
is not an exclusive advantage of the cold-carrier cycle.

For an intrinsic spin-noise rate gamma_0, the exposure cost is at least
of order gamma_0*T. The cold route cannot evade its larger T by retaining
only the active interaction interval. Finite data/helper barriers introduce
an exchange/spin disturbance that decreases approximately exponentially
with separation, whereas |lambda| decreases as R^-4. A transparent scaling
model for the combined exposure is

 error_spin(R) ~ const/S_D * R^4
                [gamma_0+gamma_ex,0 exp(-2R/xi)].        (3)

The precise exponential prefactor must come from actual orbital tails.
Equation (3) already proves that arbitrarily increasing separation does
not yield a free high-fidelity limit when gamma_0>0: the intrinsic term
grows as R^4. The direct comparator has no added helper-exchange channel
and no R-dependent interaction slowdown.

4. What the inherited construction still adds
--------------------------------------------
The active-source admission and complete instrument results remain useful
when source dynamics cannot be calibrated or prepared and the relevant
local commutator is controlled. They are organizing results that ordinary
dissipative or calibrated hardware can also use. Their value should not
be confused with a practical speed or fidelity advantage of this particular
quadrupole apparatus.

The chosen next operation should use a larger established interaction and
solve a more direct computational burden. Exchange-only leakage reduction
is such a burden: leaked spin states accumulate, can spread errors through
subsequent gates, and already require substantial compiled control in
current hardware. A prepared singlet pair supplies the angular-momentum
outlet. Ordinary exchange couples it to a leaked block in first order.

5. Stronger opening and present status
-------------------------------------
The accompanying ENERGY_SELECTIVE_EXCHANGE_LRU.txt derives a five-spin
energy-selective reset-if-leaked primitive. It uses an equal-exchange data
triangle, a singlet helper pair, and one cross exchange. The useful matrix
element is g/(2sqrt(3)), with no spatial quadrupole suppression. It also
commutes EXACTLY with the data exchange on the other two spins, allowing
one full encoded control axis during the contact.

The single-edge CONSTANT contact cannot be an exact perfect LRU: the
leaked and desired outgoing states fail a necessary spectral-moment
condition. The gap-assisted limit is accurate but slow when the gap and
cross exchange are constrained by the same maximum available J. Resonance
noise also competes with increasing the gap. Therefore this calculation
identifies a consequential operation and a useful obstruction; it does not
yet establish an advantage over compiled reset-if-leaked sequences.

The promising work is a genuinely short connected/contact construction or
a computation-compatible leakage-removal mechanism that overcomes this
obstruction under equal connectivity, maximum exchange, helper preparation,
noise, and total duration. The root investigation has now selected an exact
four-spin singlet erasure filter: a matched three-edge exchange star plus
a disjoint swap edge, coherently completed with two helper phases. It uses
one prepared singlet helper pair and one final binary S/T read, permits
concurrent complete local control on the other three data spins, and
requires no large-gap limit. GLOBAL_SPIN_DETECTION_COMPARISON.txt records
the exact five-setting resource count, closest primary comparators, and
the complete-noise-model caveats. This is the stronger current opening;
the present carrier and three-spin reset calculations remain complementary
capital and decisive comparisons rather than parallel device commitments.

Primary comparisons
-------------------
Langrock and DiVincenzo, A reset-if-leaked procedure for encoded spin qubits,
arXiv:2012.09517 (2020).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.09517
The shortest construction uses fourteen exchanges over nine layers and
two extra spins. Its flaggable version requires additional operations.

HRL Quantum Team and Collaborators, A digitally controlled silicon quantum
processing unit, Nature 655,1154-1159 (2026), published 29 July 2026.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10754-7
The reported QEC experiments use initialized ancillas and 21/24-pulse
reset-if-leaked units over 30 time steps. Their explicit observed role is
preventing leakage accumulation. A new proposal must compare with this
complete operation, rather than merely asserting ideal EO universality.

Broz et al., Demonstration of an always-on exchange-only spin qubit,
Nature Communications 17,4794 (2026).
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-70943-w
Always-on exchange, energy-gap protection and simultaneous control already
have experimental consumers. They are components, not the new result.
