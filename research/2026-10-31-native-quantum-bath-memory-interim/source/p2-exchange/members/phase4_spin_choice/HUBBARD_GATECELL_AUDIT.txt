This charge projection occurs only AFTER the completed gate, not between

On a pass, each data block has exactly four singly occupied sites, which

For disjoint parallel verifiers with all unwanted links OFF, their

The two disjoint six-site blocks each have even fermion number, so the

The factorizability does require the specified independent verifier

3. Why the16-dimensional verifier reconstruction is complete
-----------------------------------------------------------
K_F is a16 by16 FOUR-SPIN operator, not an operator on sixteen spins.
The underlying six-site Hubbard Hamiltonian, helper singlet, and accepted
charge/read event commute with data SU(2) and permutations of the three
star leaves. The four-spin space decomposes into four inequivalent
SU(2)-by-S3 sectors:

 J=0, rest spin1/2, dimension2;
 J=1, rest spin1/2, dimension6;
 J=1, rest spin3/2, dimension3;
 J=2, rest spin3/2, dimension5.

Each sector occurs once as an irreducible product representation, so K_F
is scalar on each. The m=0 calculation contains every multiplicity; its
sector scalar coefficients therefore determine the entire spin operator.
The stated polynomial projectors onto J=0,1,2 and rest spin1/2 are correct.
The selected numerical residuals from the scalar form are below2e-12.

This argument holds for the chosen verifier with no concurrent extra
rest hopping. It does NOT restore the exact spin-model concurrency claim
at finite Hubbard U. The earlier simultaneous-hopping counterexample
remains applicable. The new complete gate uses sequential local pair
controls and therefore does not depend on that failed commutation.

4. The conservative helper read factor is correct
-----------------------------------------------
The helper spin-to-charge sweep retains the complete singlet basis
(D_L,S_11,D_R), while every triplet remains in charge(1,1) exactly in the
stated spin-independent one-orbital model. The joining script deliberately
accepts ONLY D_R. Let its singlet transition amplitude be r. Then

 |r|^2=1-q,  q=2.443258388507008e-5.

Conditioning one read on D_R multiplies its preceding singlet-projection
Kraus operator by r. Two independent reads multiply (1) by r^2, whose
magnitude is1-q. The script's A_read=(1-q)A drops only an overall phase,
which is irrelevant for the CP map. It is the correct amplitude factor;
using(1-q)^2 here would incorrectly insert a probability as an amplitude.

This q is a false rejection of a CLEAN helper singlet under D_R-only
acceptance. It is not the probability of accepting a leaked triplet.
D_L is also necessarily a singlet in the model, but rejecting it is a
valid conservative convention. The earlier pair note's slightly smaller
false-triplet figure accepted either double-charge endpoint; the two
conventions must not be silently mixed.

The preceding helper preparation is also coherent and complete within

on every successful branch. Failures are reset/retried BEFORE contact with
unknown data. Their cost is a probability and time resource, not an

5. Proof and precise scope of the accessible-instrument bound
------------------------------------------------------------
Let A_r denote the complete accepted operator, including both helper read
conversions. Let W be the ideal calibrated logical isometry, with any
chosen overall phase. Suppose

 delta=||A_r-W||,
 p=||I-A_r^dagger A_r||,

where A_r is a contraction and p is the maximum rejection probability on
the four-dimensional logical input space. Map every rejected state to an
orthogonal erasure output, with arbitrary retained classical failure
records. Its CP subchannel has diamond norm p. For the accepted branch,

 ||A_r(.)A_r^dagger-W(.)W^dagger||_diamond
    <=(||A_r||+||W||)||A_r-W||<=2delta.

The two flagged output branches occupy orthogonal classical sectors.
Consequently the complete accessible gate-or-erasure instrument obeys

 (1/2)||E_cell-U_calibrated||_diamond <= delta+p/2.       (2)

This proof includes arbitrary entangled references. Erasing data on
failure is essential to the claimed task; no identity or logical recovery
within a failed sector is asserted. An unspecified inaccessible detector
environment is not certified by (2); the calculation uses ideal charge
checks and specified final outcomes. A real charge-read instrument error
must be added separately.
