1. Exact finite transfer

Use the finite circuit/Pauli-history receiver of COHERENT_ALTERNATIVE.txt.
Each actual supported noise map N_i is CPTP, F_i=N_i-Id, ||F_i||_diamond<=eta_i.
Let b(S) in {0,1} be a fixed conservative badness predicate on fault subsets;
when b(S)=0, the same decoder corrects every Pauli history on S. Assume
b(empty)=0. The finite circuit is multilinear in its noise slots.

Write Phi_S for the expansion term with F at exactly S and Id elsewhere.
The formal bad contribution is Psi_bad=sum_S b(S) Phi_S. Its complement is
Id_L tensor a linear bath map by the universal Pauli-history certificate.
The complete decoded Phi is CPTP, even though Psi_bad need not be CP.

Define the Boolean Mobius coefficients
 a(U)=sum_(V subset U) (-1)^(|U|-|V|) b(V).
Then b(S)=sum_(U subset S) a(U), and EXACTLY
 Psi_bad=sum_U a(U) Phi_[at least U].
In Phi_[at least U], F_i is forced at U and every other noise slot is the
unrestricted actual N_i. CPTP contractivity therefore gives
 ||Phi_[at least U]||_diamond<=product_(i in U) eta_i.
With the CP comparison projection P from COHERENT_ALTERNATIVE.txt, obtain
 inf_(Lambda_B CPTP) ||Phi-Id_L tensor Lambda_B||_diamond
 <=min(2, 2 sum_U |a(U)| product_(i in U) eta_i).             (M1)
Shared finite bath, arbitrary initial logical-bath-reference entanglement,
ideal correction Kraus records and all supports are retained. This is a
coherent fault-norm certificate; a(U) are signed coefficients, not probabilities.

2. A sufficient local structure with a size-uniform bound

Suppose the N fault slots form a graph of maximum degree D, and b(S)=1 iff
the induced graph on S has a connected component of at least k vertices.
This is an ADDITIONAL receiver structure to check; it is not inferred merely
from a small stochastic badness probability or from eroder stability.

If U has a connected component C with |C|<k, then b(V) for V subset U is
independent of every bit in C. The alternating sum over V intersect C
cancels, hence a(U)=0. Also |a(U)|<=2^|U|. Thus nonzero coefficients live
only on unions of dangerous connected components, each of size at least k.
Small safe components elsewhere have canceled ALGEBRAICALLY.

Let d=max(1,D), eta_i<=eta, and q=2 d^2 eta<1. A connected set of m vertices
with a chosen root is encoded by a canonical spanning-tree traversal of
length 2(m-1), so there are at most d^(2m-2) such sets per root. Therefore
 A:=sum_(connected C, |C|>=k) (2eta)^|C|
   <= N q^k/[d^2(1-q)].
Every possible U is a family of pairwise separated polymers C. Ignoring
exclusions only increases the positive sum, which is at most
 product_C [1+(2eta)^|C|]-1 <=exp(A)-1.
Combining with (M1) gives a norm bound 2(exp(A)-1). When A<1 the purely
rational certificate 2A/(1-A) is sufficient. In particular, for A<=1/2,
the joint error is at most 4A. There is no exp(N eta) safe-volume penalty.

For N polynomial in L times storage time, k proportional to L, and a fixed
eta below 1/(2d^2), this has the required exponential-in-L lifetime form,
IF the actual fixed-decoder receiver has the stated cluster property.
No physical architecture is obtained merely by writing that contract.

4. Actual transfer attempt to an eroder: a counterexample to the shortcut

It would be tempting to infer the cluster property from an eroder alone.
That inference is false. Consider the attractive one-dimensional AND eroder
 x_i(t+1)=x_i(t) x_(i+1)(t)
on a length-L ring, with births OR-ed in at specified times. Every finite
island on the infinite line erodes in time proportional to its length.
Cold start is zero, and 'bad' means the final ring is entirely one.
At least L births are necessary, because a site never becomes one without
a birth at that site.

For L=12, place births at sites 0,...,10 at time 0. After four updates the
ones are at 0,...,6. Place births at sites 7,...,11 at time 4. The ring is
now all ones. Every one of these 16 births is necessary: deleting any one
makes the final state nonuniform. This is therefore a minimal bad witness,
and its full-set Mobius coefficient is exactly one.

Yet in the nearest-neighbor spacetime graph, the two birth components have
sizes 11 and 5, both below k=L=12. They are separated by four time units.
The same construction works with a time separation larger than any fixed
graph-neighborhood radius. Consequently a static adjacency of birth flags
does not provide the needed Mobius sparsity even for an attractive eroder.
This is not a counterexample to the cluster theorem, whose hypothesis fails,
nor to Astra's asynchronous memory theorem.

5. What remains, and why the objective is unchanged

For Astra's sweep, damage is a causal history, not a static cluster of births.
The next receiving obligation is a LOCAL Mobius/witness bound for its
actual stopped envelope predicate, including unions of overlapping Toom
explanations. One must count a causal boundary/certificate, not all safe
spacetime volume or an unbounded raw fault genealogy. The source's known
quadratic genealogy failures must remain excluded.

More precisely, condition on the exogenous corrector clocks h, form the
finite-time badness predicate b_h and its coefficients a_h(U), and let eta_i
be the bounded local coherent noise strength at each micro-location.
It suffices to bound
 E_h sum_U |a_h(U)| product_(i in U) eta_i
by a size-growing-lifetime tail, uniformly in a justified continuum
refinement. Uniform success for every possible clock realization is not
required. An ordinary stochastic tail for E_h P_noise(b_h) is a different
quantity and does not discharge this receiving obligation. This expected
signed-witness norm is the next concrete target for the retained programme.

The native-bath transfer is already complete under its stated structure.
The present resummation supplies a second, floor-free coherent route for
receivers with the proved cluster property and fixes an executed norm-scan
failure. It does not yet discharge the causal-envelope condition or the
noise-stable 2D synchronizer. Those are still the larger capability target,
rather than reasons to declare a finite example a scalable quantum memory.
