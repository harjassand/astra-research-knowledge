1. Start with the strongest ordinary method

Hamiltonian fault-path methods already handle weak local coherent couplings
in fault-tolerant quantum CIRCUITS, without adding physical depolarization.
Aliferis-Gottesman-Preskill, quant-ph/0504218v3, is a primary reference for the
local non-Markovian fault-path interface. A bath-only Hamiltonian is free to
be absorbed in the interaction picture; the bounded memory-bath terms still
need a charged operator strength and support. An unbounded physical bath
operator is not made bounded by calling it noise.

Here is an elementary finite receiver which captures that method's relevant
resummation. It is proved explicitly; no unread circuit theorem is imported
as a theorem for an autonomous sweep.

2. A finite decision-tree transfer

Fix a finite ordered circuit of ideal correction channels and supported
noise channels N_i on A_i tensor B, all CPTP. Let F_i=N_i-Id and suppose
||F_i||_diamond<=eta_i. A universal Pauli-history certificate, with the same
fixed decoder as in NATIVE_BATH_PROOF.txt, classifies fault subsets as good.
Good means every supported Pauli choice and correction Kraus record is
logically scalar. The classifier must be fixed independently of the quantum
input, not a test on normalized postselected states.

A decision tree queries fault bits in any adaptive order, each at most once.
At a leaf, queried zero positions are Id, queried one positions are F_i,
and unqueried positions remain the ACTUAL N_i. The multilinear circuit
expansion is exactly the sum of the leaf maps. A good leaf must certify
goodness for ALL completions of its unqueried bits. A bad leaf need not say
that all those completions really fail; it may conservatively stop early.

Unqueried N_i, zero-branch identities, encoders, ideal corrections and the
decoder have diamond norm one. Therefore a bad leaf's map has norm at most
the product of eta_i over its queried one positions. Define
 W_tree=sum_(bad leaves) product_(queried one i) eta_i.
Good leaf maps are Id_L tensor linear bath maps by Pauli expansion and the
universal certificate; they need not be CP.

Define a linear projection on joint-channel space:
 P(Phi)=Id_L tensor Lambda_Phi,
 Lambda_Phi(X_B)=Tr_L Phi((I_L/d_L) tensor X_B).
It has diamond operator norm <=1, fixes all Id_L tensor bath maps, and takes
a CPTP Phi to a CPTP bath comparison channel. Applying I-P to the exact leaf
expansion gives
 inf_(Lambda CPTP) ||Phi-Id_L tensor Lambda||_diamond
     <=min(2,2 W_tree).                                    (F1)
All input/reference and persistent-bath quantifiers are retained. This is
a norm certificate, not a physical fault probability or a Poisson unraveling.

For the conservative classifier 'at least k faulty locations', query in
chronological order and stop at the kth one. It has exactly binomial(N,k)
bad leaves for uniform eta, so W_tree=binomial(N,k) eta^k. Later noise remains
unrestricted CPTP and has already been resummed; there is no gratuitous
exp(N eta) factor. The finite three-location five-qubit example uses this
existing conventional mechanism. It does not constitute a new architecture.

3. Why that first conventional construction does not settle the sweep

A naive exact-subset triangle sum is
 sum_bad eta^|S|=(1+eta)^N P_Bernoulli(eta/(1+eta))(bad).
It is unusable at large volume*time. The decision tree removes that factor
for fixed-size gadgets, but a global scan can still query many irrelevant
faults before finding a dangerous connected component.

This is an ACTUAL receiver limitation, not a counterexample to memory:
consider a line of N bits whose badness means a run of k ones. A classical
union bound is (N-k+1) eta^k. In a left-to-right decision tree, any prefix made
from pairs 00 or 10, padded with zeros and followed by k ones, is a distinct
bad leaf. Every prefix is free of a k-run for k>=2 and ends in zero. Hence
 W_scan >= eta^k (1+eta)^floor((N-k)/2).
For eta=1/50, N=L^2, k=L, L=512, exact rational log enclosures show this
lower bound exceeds one by hundreds of logarithmic units while the classical
union bound is exponentially tiny. The ordinary global scan has failed at
its intended size-uniform consequence despite the excellent stochastic tail.

The missing operation is a LOCAL resummation/certificate whose norm cost is
controlled by a dangerous connected witness, without re-counting arbitrary
safe faults elsewhere. Astra's universal Pauli-history event supplies the
correctable operator span. Its probability tail alone does not specify that
norm-resummation operation. A better decision tree, local recovery cut,
cluster expansion or different representation might supply it; failure of
this scan is not a no-go for those alternatives.

4. Continuous-time and autonomy boundary

Trotterization expresses bounded local continuous Hamiltonians as local
CPTP unitary micro-locations. Formula (F1) applies at each finite resolution.
A continuum/scalable theorem needs a W_tree estimate uniform in the
refinement, volume and storage horizon with its actual supports and rates.
Taking the limit does not itself supply that estimate.

Likewise, a local asynchronous synchronizer must preserve quantum operation
history and local error propagation. Mean service time or individual
exponential waiting tails do not imply a joint local-noise bound when many
labels share a server. The earlier rejected gate-clock construction supplies
no such noise-stable synchronizer. Neither does the present native-bath
transfer. The 2D clock issue and the generic coherent local-resummation issue
are distinct receiving obligations.

The structured native-bath theorem is the completed positive branch of this
investigation. Arbitrary pure continuous coupling and a low-dimensional
autonomous architecture remain the sustained capability target; they have
not been replaced by a finite five-qubit success claim.
