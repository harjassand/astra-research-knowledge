1. Receiver and source contract

M is a finite stabilizer-code memory, L its logical space, B an arbitrary
finite persistent bath, V:L->M an encoding isometry, and Dec a FIXED CPTP
decoder. Full syndrome projectors are Pi_s. Exact initialization is V tensor
I_B, allowing arbitrary initial L-B-reference entanglement. Nothing acts on
the untouched reference. Diamond norms are unhalved and are on the joint L-B
channel, not on a bath assignment presumed independent of a logical input.

Ideal corrections occur at independent, exogenous Poisson clocks. A complete
correction proposal is a CPTP map with Kraus Q_j Pi_j (possibly a sum of
syndrome projectors), scalar on every encoded Pauli orbit before its Pauli
correction. The full-pattern or lumped-inactive completion must be specified;
this argument does not identify them with the original active-only jumps.

INPUT CERTIFICATE (Astra N881's indispensable ingredient). For a fixed ordered
schedule of corrector proposals and marked local fault supports A_c, there is
a good predicate G such that, for EVERY choice of a full-patch Pauli at EVERY
marked fault, every surviving correction/decoder Kraus record satisfies
 D_s E_final V = scalar * I_L.
The same G and fixed decoder work simultaneously for all Pauli histories,
including accumulated logical homology, not just current syndrome support.
For independent dominating fault clocks of rates lambda_c, assume
 P_dom(G fails) <= delta(T).
Goodness can be chosen downward closed in the marked fault set: if a larger
schedule is universally good, substituting identities makes any sub-schedule
good. This is not a pointwise-in-error failure statement.

The source's actual universal support, transient coverage, stopped-torus and
homology hypotheses remain required. The new argument does not prove its
probability estimate, convert 4D geometry to 2D, or improve its coarse constants.

2. Physical native generator and the quiet-evolution condition

Consider the ACTUAL joint M-B generator
 L_native = sum_v kappa_v(C_v-Id) - i[H_q, .]
              + L_B + sum_(c,a) D[J_(c,a)],
where D[J](rho)=J rho J^dagger - {J^dagger J,rho}/2.
L_B acts only on B. Each J_(c,a) is supported on the fixed memory patch A_c
and B; bath interactions at different centers can share the same B and need
not commute. All operators/generators are bounded in this finite system.

Two structural/calibrated premises are required:

 (i) sum_a J_(c,a)^dagger J_(c,a) <= lambda_c I_MB for every center c.
     This is an OPERATOR upper bound, hence a conditional hazard bound for
     every joint state and every past record. An average-rate fit is not it.

 (ii) H_q and the TOTAL escape operator G=sum_(c,a) J^dagger J are
      syndrome-scalar on M:
        H_q=sum_s Pi_s tensor H_s,
        G=sum_s Pi_s tensor G_s.
      Bath operators H_s,G_s may differ with s and need not commute.

Bath-only Hamiltonians are a special case of (ii). The condition also permits
QND syndrome-bath shifts. It excludes an uncharged Hamiltonian that creates
syndrome or acts nontrivially within a logical syndrome space. A generic
operator that merely commutes with stabilizers need not be syndrome-scalar:
it can be a logical operator. That distinction is essential.

No artificial memory depolarization or bath reset is added. Conversely, (ii)
is a genuine physical structure premise, not a property inferred from a
small measured jump rate. Pauli/bath factorizable jumps have bath-only escape
effects, but a generic coherent sum of Pauli jumps need not satisfy (ii).
The jump representation can be chosen mathematically for the same generator.
No photon-counting instrument, measured jump record or bath-reset device must
be physically added for this unconditional channel theorem.

3. Good records factor as identity on L, even though record probabilities
   depend on the quantum state

Use the usual quantum-jump instrument for the J faults and an independent
Poisson-channel representation for the complete ideal correctors. No-jump
evolution, after omitting scalar corrector-clock survival factors, has
propagator generated by -i H_q-G/2. It is exactly
 sum_s Pi_s tensor W_s(t2,t1),
with time ordering retained within each bath W_s. Bath-only jumps can be
inserted without altering memory support. Thus on E V tensor I_B, quiet
evolution becomes E V tensor a bath operator depending on the syndrome of E.

At a counted fault, expand its FULL patch operator in Paulis,
 J_(c,a)=sum_(P on A_c) P tensor B_(c,a,P).
Keep all bath operators in their chronological order. For each complete
instrument record, each term follows a definite Pauli history. Quiet factors
are bath-only on that history; ideal syndrome factors either kill the term
or perform the legal Pauli correction. On a good schedule the INPUT
CERTIFICATE therefore makes the final recovered Kraus operator
 A_omega=I_L tensor B_omega.
This is a linear-operator identity on all L-B inputs. It is not a normalized
conditional-channel assertion and does not identify a coherent expansion
with a realized classical Pauli trajectory.

Integrating all good records gives a CP, generally trace-DECREASING map
 Phi_good=Id_L tensor Lambda_good.
Its record probabilities can depend on B, including a B initially entangled
with L. Individual normalized outcomes can steer L. Only the averaged final
channel is claimed to preserve L approximately.

4. Classical domination bounds an operator-valued bad effect

For any initial joint M-B-reference state, the conditional intensity of a
fault at c is
 Tr[(sum_a J_(c,a)^dagger J_(c,a)) rho_cond] <= lambda_c.
The normalized quantum trajectory is a piecewise deterministic process.
Ordinary marked-point-process thinning couples its counted faults to fresh
independent Poisson candidates of rates lambda_c, keeping each actual fault
at one candidate point. The corrector clocks remain independent exogenous
clocks and can be shared in this coupling. The accepting probability and
post-jump quantum state may depend on the past quantum state; the candidates
need not be a physical CPTP noise decomposition. This is a probability
coupling for EACH input state. Its bound is uniform over all those inputs.

If the dominating schedule is universally good, the actual sub-schedule is
good. Consequently the true instrument probability of a bad record is at
most delta(T), for every joint input state. Let Phi_bad be the CP map obtained
by integrating the actual bad records after Dec. Then
 ||Phi_bad||_diamond=||Phi_bad^dagger(I)||_infinity <= delta(T).
The equality is the standard norm identity for a CP map. There is no required
input-independent mixture over normalized conditional channels.

5. CP completion, and the joint diamond theorem

The decoded total map Phi is CPTP and Phi=Id_L tensor Lambda_good+Phi_bad.
Taking adjoints at identity shows
 Phi_bad^dagger(I)=I_L tensor E_B,
 E_B=I_B-Lambda_good^dagger(I_B), 0<=E_B<=delta(T) I_B.
The map S_B(rho)=sqrt(E_B) rho sqrt(E_B) completes the good bath channel:
 Lambda=Lambda_good+S_B is CPTP. Therefore
 ||Phi-Id_L tensor Lambda||_diamond
 <=||Phi_bad||_diamond+||Id_L tensor S_B||_diamond
 <=2 delta(T), capped by 2.                                      (N1)
This holds after tensoring any reference identity. The bath channel can
depend on T, the actual noise and correctors, but not on the input L-B state.
Its square-root completion is an analytical comparison, not a physical bath
reset or a postselection protocol.

This is a positive transfer for a native quantum-conditioned fault process.
It does not claim that an arbitrary state-dependent clock is harmless. The
quiet-evolution condition is exactly what permits the good-record factor;
the conditional OPERATOR rate bounds are exactly what permit domination.
Removing either requires a new argument, not a reuse of (N1).

6. Continuous coherent coupling along matched native dissipative directions

Take Hermitian K_a supported on A_a tensor B such that K_a^2 is syndrome-scalar.
Suppose the ACTUAL native noise includes, for gamma_a>0,
 gamma_a D[K_a] - i h_a[K_a,.].
The established displaced-jump identity gives EXACTLY this generator as

Indeed

and expanding D[J_a] gives gamma_a D[K_a]-i h_a[K_a,.]. Thus (ii) holds and
one can take
 lambda_c=sum_(a centered at c) [gamma_a ||K_a||^2+h_a^2/gamma_a]. (N2)

6.1 A strict native-generator example outside direct finite uniformization

For one memory qubit and one bath qubit, let P=X, B=|0><0|, K=P tensor B.
For any gamma>0 and real h, take the actual generator
 E=gamma D[K]-i h[K,.].
Use normalized Choi matrices, u=vec(I_4)/2, v=vec(I tensor Z)/2.
They are orthonormal, and the Choi compression of Id+E/r is
 [1-gamma/(2r)    -gamma/(4r)]
 [-gamma/(4r)              0].
Its determinant is -gamma^2/(16r^2)<0 for EVERY finite r>0. The h term has
zero compression on this subspace. Thus no rate makes Id+E/r CPTP, even
though E is a valid native GKSL generator satisfying the theorem's escape
condition. This is not the old memory-only-floor/joint-Hamiltonian example:
the native dissipator and coherent coupling here are already MATCHED.

One can strengthen the distinction on the same memory-bath Hilbert space.
Suppose E=L_B^0+r(Psi-Id), with L_B^0 any bath-only GKSL generator and Psi CPTP.
Orthogonally to u, E's conditional Choi matrix is gamma |k><k|, where
k=vec(K)/2 is orthogonal to every traceless bath-only operator vector.
Positivity of the conditional Choi matrix of E-L_B^0 forces every bath-only
dissipative jump in L_B^0 to be scalar: otherwise its positive weight on a
bath-only direction would be subtracted from a zero diagonal. Thus L_B^0
can only contribute a bath Hamiltonian. Subtracting a Hermitian bath
Hamiltonian changes the u-v Choi element by a purely imaginary number;
the real part -gamma/(4r) remains nonzero while the v-v entry stays zero.
The compression is still indefinite. No such finite-proposal plus genuine
bath-only Markov-generator decomposition exists without enlarging the state
space or changing the generator. This argument does not exclude other
dilations or approximations. The present theorem needs neither decomposition.

Memory-only ideal correction channels do not fill that bath-only Choi kernel:
their Kraus vectors have bath identity, orthogonal to v. The same obstruction
therefore persists when such corrections are included. For a noncommuting
catalog P_i tensor B_i with traceless P_i and a nonconstant sum gamma_i B_i^2,
the bath-only escape direction gives the corresponding obstruction. The
finite control uses B_i=(I+Z)/2,(I+X)/2,(I+Y)/2.

In particular K_a=P_(A_a) tensor B_a with B_a Hermitian has
 K_a^2=I_M tensor B_a^2.
Different B_a can be noncommuting on the SAME persistent bath; arbitrary
bath-only dynamics is permitted. There is no bath-dimension Choi-ball factor
in (N2). Every overlapping patch/center incidence must still be charged in
the source's actual per-cell total-rate convention.

This uses a MATCHED NATIVE dissipator D[P tensor B]. A memory-only dissipator
D[P] is not that resource. The source's memory-only Choi counterexample
remains valid. Gamma cannot be optimized as though a bath were free to change.
For gamma=0 and h!=0, this representation supplies no finite rate; arbitrary
pure continuous coupling is NOT covered. Thus no unrestricted Hamiltonian
stability ball or free added-noise removal has been claimed.

8. Conventional machinery, attempted stronger consequences, and limits

Quantum jump/no-jump calculus is conventional: Dalibard-Castin-Molmer,
Phys. Rev. Lett. 68, 580 (1992), and Molmer-Castin-Dalibard, JOSA B 10,
524 (1993), especially Eqs. (6)-(12) of the latter:
 https://pro.college-de-france.fr/jean.dalibard/publi3/osa_93.pdf
Conditional thinning, CP norm identities and error-correction operator spans
are also standard. The displaced-jump prior and structured rate cost are
explicitly retained in the N881 sources. No first-principles invention of
these tools or priority for the combined theorem has been established.

Lebreuilly et al., Autonomous quantum error correction and quantum
computation (2021), https://arxiv.org/html/2103.05007v1 , Eq. (5) and its
error-set discussion already explicitly account for no-jump backaction
sum F^dagger F in AutoQEC. The importance of no-jump errors is not a new
discovery here. The present sufficient condition makes that evolution
harmless on each universal Pauli orbit so a specific geometric certificate
can receive a native quantum-conditioned process.

Shang and Franca, Hamiltonian dynamics from pure dissipation (2026),
https://arxiv.org/html/2604.18533v1 , Eq. (4), explicitly gives the identity
used in Section 6. Their gauge discussion and the older GKSL gauge freedom
are prior tools. No physical dissipator is synthesized by algebraically
rewriting a native generator.

Shtanko et al., Bounds on Autonomous Quantum Error Correction (2025),
 https://arxiv.org/html/2308.16233v2
already treats general dissipative error strings, including E^dagger E, and
shows why a global decoder is not a scalable constant-rate architecture.
Our finite five-qubit global-reset construction is an interface control,
not a new scalable memory, and must not be compared to a weak Pauli-only
incumbent that is denied this ordinary machinery.

For the stronger goal of arbitrary continuous local Hamiltonian coupling,
conventional coherent fault-path methods are the first serious alternative.
They avoid physical noise floors in fault-tolerant circuits. A naive absolute
Dyson sum has exponential volume*time cost. A stochastic bad-schedule tail
does not by itself supply a bounded local resummation of signed/coherent
fault paths. A decision-tree transfer below gives a finite exact criterion;
it is not yet a size-uniform witness norm for the 4D sweep or a 2D clock.
This gap is left open rather than claiming generic Hamiltonian robustness.

The low-dimensional fully autonomous objective remains alive. The 2D QCA
construction 2601.20818v3 and the periodic transfer 2610.05589v1 do not by
themselves supply a noise-stable time-independent local synchronization
operation. In particular, individual exponential service tails do not prove
joint fault locality. The new native-bath theorem does not fix that clock.

syndrome-logical basis using Gaussian integer arithmetic. It verifies all
15 single-qubit Pauli corrections and an explicit weight-three logical X.
The ideal global recovery resets the 16-state syndrome register; this is
the conventional global decoder, not a scalable physical operation.

There is one persistent bath qubit, with H_B=X/3+Z/5, no bath reset, and three
native terms with K_i equal to Y_1 tensor (I+Z)/2,
Y_2 tensor (I+X)/2, and X_4 tensor (I+Y)/2. Every gamma_i=1/100 and h_i=1/2000.
The equivalent jumps are K_i/10+i I/200. Their individual hazard caps are
401/40000; the hazard on the first bath projector differs by a factor 401
between bath |0> and |1>. Counting them as a fixed mean-rate process is false.

At recovery rate kappa=1 and horizon T=10, a good record has at most one
counted fault in each correction gap. A three-state absorbing classical
chain bounds badness at the sum of the center caps. Positive rational
uniformization certifies a joint diamond bound 0.01540508506248315.
An ordinary global-count analyst can instead use a certified total operator
hazard cap 3/125, improving this to 0.009923200714488286. This is the stronger
same-information comparator, and is explicitly retained.

The 12288-dimensional flagged GKSL generator was propagated on 16 input
