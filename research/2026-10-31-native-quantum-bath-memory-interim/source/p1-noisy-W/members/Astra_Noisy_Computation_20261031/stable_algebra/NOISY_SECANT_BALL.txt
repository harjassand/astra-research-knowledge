NOISY SECANT BALLS: AN EXACT RANK-TWO FEJER CORRECTION
31 October 2026

Purpose. This is a conventional least-change/set-membership component that
sharpens the parent's cumulative solver construction. It is not claimed as a
historically new update rule. Unlike fitting an unreliable secant equality, it
projects onto the entire observation-compatible convex set.

1. ONE HERMITIAN SOURCE (REAL FORM)

Let B=B^T be the current model, u a unit query direction, and y the observed
action. Suppose every comparator of interest C satisfies

  ||Cu-y|| <= omega.

For example, experimental error eta and an unknown comparator's operator-norm
departure zeta from the actual source imply this when omega>=eta+zeta.
Define the closed convex set

  K={X=X^T : ||Xu-y||<=omega}.

Let B'=Proj_K(B) in Frobenius norm. Orthogonal-projection geometry gives, for
every C in K,

  ||B'-C||_F^2 <= ||B-C||_F^2 - ||B'-B||_F^2.

Writing t=||y-Bu||, any feasible update Delta satisfies

  ||Delta||_F >= ||Delta u|| >= (t-omega)_+.

Consequently each correction with t>tau>omega drops the squared Frobenius
potential by at least (tau-omega)^2. If C also has spectrum in [mu,L], clipping
the eigenvalues of B' to that interval is a second convex projection and cannot
increase the potential. Clipping need not preserve membership in K; the
cumulative potential proof does not require it to.

The margin threshold is t>omega, not t>2omega. The lower bound is sharp:
when e=y-Bu is parallel to u, the nearest feasible correction has norm exactly
t-omega.

2. EXPLICIT RANK-TWO FORMULA

Write e=y-Bu=a u+b, where a=u^T e and b is orthogonal to u. If t<=omega,
return B unchanged. If omega=0, use v=e. Otherwise find the unique lambda>0
satisfying

  a^2/(1+lambda)^2 + 4||b||^2/(2+lambda)^2 = omega^2.

The left side is continuous and strictly decreasing from t^2 to zero unless
e=0, which is already handled. Scalar bracketing and bisection therefore suffice.
Set

  v = lambda*a/(1+lambda) u + lambda/(2+lambda) b,
  Delta = v u^T + u v^T - (u^T v)u u^T,
  B'=B+Delta.

The update has rank at most two. To derive it, the least-Frobenius-norm symmetric
matrix with Delta u=v is the displayed Powell-symmetric-Broyden form, and

  ||Delta||_F^2 = v_parallel^2 + 2||v_perpendicular||^2.

Minimize this quadratic form subject to ||e-v||<=omega. The Lagrange multiplier
equations give the scalar formula above. This derivation also establishes global
optimality; no useful subspace or large optimizer is supplied as an oracle.

3. A MIXTURE OF SOURCES

For models B_j and query weights theta_j, put B_theta=sum_j theta_j B_j and
s=sum_j theta_j^2>0. A noisy observation of the mixture imposes

  ||sum_j theta_j X_j u-y||<=omega.

The product-Frobenius projection is

  B_j'=B_j+(theta_j/s)Delta,

where Delta is the rank-two update above for B_theta. The squared update norm
is ||Delta||_F^2/s, so the potential drop is at least

  (t-omega)^2 / s.

On a simplex, s<=1, yielding at least (t-omega)^2. More generally, bounded
weight l2 norm gives the corresponding explicit denominator. Independently
clipping each B_j to [mu,L] remains Fejer to every admissible comparator tuple.

4. COMPLEX HERMITIAN DATA

Hermitian X requires u*Xu to be real. For complex noisy y, decompose e into
a u+b with a=u*e and b perpendicular to u. The imaginary component Im(a)u
cannot be fitted by a Hermitian update. If |Im(a)|>omega the compatible
Hermitian set is empty: reject the advertised error model. Otherwise replace
a by Re(a) and omega by sqrt(omega^2-Im(a)^2), then apply the same formula with
conjugate transposes and real Frobenius inner product. The potential statement
remains valid. A claimed comparator guarantees nonemptiness.

5. STRUCTURE AND COST

Starting from B_j=a_j I with a_j in [mu,L], retain a common orthonormal basis S
for all update directions. Every update adds at most span{u,e}; after M
corrections dim S<=2M. Clipping operates only on the small matrices on S:
outside S the model remains a_j I. Model-vector products and inverses therefore
have ordinary low-rank-update costs, paid explicitly by the parent construction.

The existence of a finite potential bound does not itself show few original
actions unless all failed validations, correction probes and numerical work are
counted. Nor does it supply an optimal epsilon dependence.
