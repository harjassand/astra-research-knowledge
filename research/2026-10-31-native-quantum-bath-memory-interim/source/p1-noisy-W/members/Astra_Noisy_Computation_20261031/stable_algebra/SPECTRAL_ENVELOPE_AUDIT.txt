FINAL SCOPED AUDIT OF operational_learning/theorem.md
31 October 2026

Conclusion: the cell-vertex global certificate and its packing termination
argument are valid under their stated assumptions. The constructive noisy
Chebyshev filter earns the approximate minimum-eigenvector property, at the
explicitly severe source-precision and computational costs stated. It does not
receive an eigenvalue oracle for free.

Two clarifications were incorporated directly into the theorem:
1. The high/low filter decision uses max filtered-vector norm>a/2, with numerical
   norm error included or enclosed. The pass/stop gap supports that threshold.
2. Under a further uniform pencil perturbation rho, the stored LP value V gives
   the interval[V-epsilon-rho,V+rho] for the new true optimum. The selected
   design's regret is epsilon+2rho, not automatically epsilon+rho.

CHEBYSHEV ACTION-ERROR CHECK

Write K=H/B, ||K||<=1, and let e_j be the difference between the noisy recurrence
vector and T_j(K)g. Its forcing terms obey ||d_j||<=nu(1+||e_j||). The second-kind
Chebyshev polynomials satisfy ||U_j(K)||<=j+1. Variation of constants therefore
gives

 ||e_j|| <=nu*j^2 +2nu*sum_(i=1)^(j-1)(j-i)||e_i||.

Using sum_(i<j)(j-i)i^2=j^2(j^2-1)/12 proves by induction the stated
||e_j||<=2nu*j^2 whenever nu*k^2<=1/2. Degree-k filter coefficients have
magnitude<=2, giving total action error<=4nu*k^3. The claimed source condition
nu<=rho/(8k^3) therefore reserves half the polynomial error budget for digital
computation. This is deterministic under adaptive norm-bounded action errors.

FILTER AND CONFIDENCE CHECK

Hoeffding's inequality yields the claimed binomial-majority pass/stop bounds.
Paley-Zygmund gives probability at least3/16 of minimum-mode overlap>=1/(2sqrt n)
for each isotropic spherical start. The same minimum eigenvector is used for
all thresholds, so one overlap event suffices for the entire bisection.

The recurrence width obeys W_next<=W/2+h, so the stated test count gives width
at most4h. The final threshold places the minimum mode in the pass band and
all modes above lambda_min+6h in the stop band. The selected output has norm
greater than a/2, and its tail is at most2rho. Hence the remaining Rayleigh
error is at most6h+2B*(4rho/a)^2=6h+h/8<tau. The u+h>B fallback is also valid:
the certified lower bracket is then greater than B-5h.

ACTION ACCOUNTING

Each of r starts needs exactly k combined H(z) actions for T_1,...,T_k.
The threshold changes only polynomial coefficients; all stored vectors are
reused. Recording the selected witness's coefficient expectations needs one
additional action of each coefficient. Thus (d+1)(rk+1) is a valid worst-case
original coefficient-action count per spectral call. Zero control coefficients
may reduce an implementation's actual count. There is no hidden physical
bisection multiplier in the stored-vector implementation.

The working storage O(nrk) and vector combination work O(nrkT), plus polynomial
coefficient computation, remain material. If N is the packing bound on plane
additions and s is the number of domain inequalities, an explicit conservative
bound on spectral calls is

  J <= 1 + sum_(r=1)^N r*binomial(r-1+s,d).

This counts all vertex tests, including failed tests and the final successful
scan. The geometric algorithm can be very expensive as control dimension grows.

GLOBAL CELL CERTIFICATE

On each active polytope, the stored envelope is affine and f is concave, so
their gap is convex. Vertex upper bounds therefore imply the gap bound over
the entire cell. Pairwise separation of added witness points follows from
Lipschitz continuity and the epsilon-2kappa margin. Degenerate empty cells are
discarded; lower-dimensional nonempty cells still have vertices. If M+L=0,
the one-plane constant case is handled directly instead of dividing by zero.

FINITE-ARITHMETIC STATUS

The general noisy filter is an analytical construction. The supplied prototype
does NOT implement that filter or interval/outward arithmetic for its recurrence,
norms, coefficient expectations, witness normalization, cell enumeration, or LP
certificates. It instead uses randomized Lanczos with a published exact-arithmetic
iteration guarantee and a fixed coherent operator perturbation. Floating-point
roundoff and geometric computations are empirically checked, not formally enclosed.

For a full finite-bit implementation, stored plane coefficients can be rounded
to rationals with their rounding error added to the experimental budget, and
cells can then be enumerated with exact rational arithmetic for rational D.
The filter's digital errors and normalization need explicit enclosures and
allocation of the remaining rho/2 budget. These are ordinary available
computations, but they were not executed as a certified implementation here.

Accordingly the N=32768 experiment is evidence for the coherent-calibration
prototype and retained-decision mechanism. It must not be presented as an
implementation of the general adaptive bounded-query-noise guarantee or as
formal numerical certification of every cell vertex.

PRIMARY COMPARISON STATUS

Spectral cuts, convex sandwich envelopes, random-start eigensolvers, and robust
reduced models have strong predecessors acknowledged in the note. The audited
result is a scoped constructive synthesis with a complete noise/access bill;
this audit does not establish historical novelty or optimality.
