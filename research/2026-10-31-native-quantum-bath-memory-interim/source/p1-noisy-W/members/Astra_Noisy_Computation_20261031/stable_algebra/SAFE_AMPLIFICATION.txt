RESIDUAL-DIRECTED EXPERIMENTS WITH SAFE HALFSPACE UPDATES
31 October 2026

This note supplies an acceleration component for the parent's cumulative
operator learner. The general lifetime theorem remains the parent's projection
argument. No improved worst-case rank-r correction count is asserted here.

1. A FAILED TASK CREATES AN INFORMATIVE NEXT EXPERIMENT

Let A and the current model B be Hermitian, v unit, and y an imperfect action
with ||y-Av||<=eta. Put r=y-Bv, a=||r||>0, and w=r/a. Then, for E=A-B,

 Re <v,Ew> = Re <Ev,r/a>
           = a-Re <y-Av,r/a> >=a-eta.

Therefore ||Ew||>=a-eta. A new action on w has observed model residual norm
at least a-2eta. This statement uses Hermiticity but does not assume a small
algebra, a low-rank residual, or a useful subspace supplied in advance.

The new direction is meaningful when the observed failed-task residual is
larger than the experimental error. If a is comparable to eta, normalization
can amplify noise and this argument provides no useful lower bound.

For complex observations, first replace y by y-i Im(v*y)v. Hermiticity gives
Im(v*Av)=0, and this orthogonal projection cannot increase ||y-Av||. It makes
the projected observation compatible with the scalar Hermitian identity.

2. PRESERVING THE GENERAL LIFETIME GUARANTEE

The model can remain unchanged while performing one or two residual-directed
experiments. Each observation supplies a supporting halfspace as in
HALFSPACE_CORRECTION.txt. Applying any one valid correction preserves that
potential argument. Selecting the observation with greatest calculated
projection gain also preserves it. All extra source actions are charged.

For the primary update using only known experimental eta, let C be an unknown
comparator with ||A-C||<=zeta. For a residual norm a>eta, define

 G=sym(r v*), h=a(a-eta), Delta=hG/||G||_F^2.

Then <G,C-B>>=a(a-eta-zeta), so

 ||B-C||_F^2-||B+Delta-C||_F^2
 >=h*(h-2a zeta)/||G||_F^2.

When a>eta+2zeta, the right side is at least

 (a-eta)(a-eta-2zeta),

since ||G||_F^2<=a^2. For a generator mixture, divide by sum theta_j^2.
Spectral clipping remains nonexpansive toward admissible comparator matrices.
The algorithm need not know zeta; it enters the comparison theorem.

3. THREE-ACTION APPROXIMATE RANK-ONE SPECIALIZATION

Assume a fixed source

 A=beta I+lambda uu*+E, ||u||=1, ||E||<=zeta,

with unknown u and real lambda, known beta, and per-action error eta. The tail
E may be generic and full rank. Write delta=eta+zeta. The unobserved comparator
C=beta I+lambda uu* is used only in the analysis.

Suppose a task with unit v0 exposes observed residual

 r0=O(v0)-beta v0, t0=||r0||>=8delta, t0>0.

Leave B=beta I unchanged during two more experiments:

 w1=r0/t0, r1=O(w1)-beta w1, t1=||r1||,
 w2=r1/t1, r2=O(w2)-beta w2, t2=||r2||.

Apply the supporting-halfspace update using the FINAL observation:

 G=sym(r2 w2*), h=t2(t2-eta), Delta=hG/||G||_F^2.

Then

 ||Delta-lambda uu*|| <5delta.

Thus the scalar-plus-rank-two model beta I+Delta approximates actual A in
operator norm by less than5eta+6zeta. If C has spectrum in [mu,L], clipping
that model to [mu,L] gives a conservative bound10eta+11zeta against A.
Only three original actions were used, including the initial task validation.
No hidden eigenvector, useful probe, or decomposition was supplied.

PROOF OF THE CONSTANT

Relative to C, each action has error at most delta. The first residual gives
|lambda|>=t0-delta>=7delta and c1=|u*w1|>=sqrt(63/64)>=7/8. Therefore

 t1>=|lambda|c1-delta >=(41/56)|lambda|.

The perpendicular component of w2 to u has norm s at most

 s<=delta/t1 <=(56/41)delta/|lambda| <(7/5)delta/|lambda|<=1/5.

Let c=|u*w2|. Then c>=sqrt(24/25)>97/100. The final residual norm obeys

 t2>=|lambda|c-delta >(4/5)|lambda|,
 delta/t2<=(5/4)delta/|lambda|<=5/28.

Phase-align u and w2 for the calculation. With G0=sym(lambda uu* w2 w2*),
the two-dimensional matrix of G0-lambda uu* has entries

 lambda*[[-s^2,cs/2],[cs/2,0]].

Hence ||G0-lambda uu*||<=|lambda|s(s+c/2)<=49delta/50.
The final experimental/comparator error changes G by at most delta, giving
||G-lambda uu*||<2delta.

For a=t2 and b=w2*r2, put g2=||G||_F^2. The perpendicular component of r2
has norm at most |lambda|s+delta<12delta/5. Its imaginary parallel component
has magnitude at most delta (and vanishes if the preprocessing above is used).
Therefore

 0<=a^2-g2=(Im b)^2+||r2-b w2||^2/2 <=(97/25)delta^2,
 g2>=(7/8)a^2.

For alpha=a(a-eta)/g2, alpha<=8/7 and

 |alpha-1|<=[a delta+(97/25)delta^2]/[(7/8)a^2]
           <=(237/98)delta/|lambda|.

Combining these inequalities,

 ||alpha G-lambda uu*||
 <(16/7+237/98)delta=(461/98)delta<5delta.

Clipping changes an operator lying within5delta of C by at most5delta in
operator norm, because its eigenvalues can lie at most5delta outside [mu,L].
The triangle inequality gives the conservative10delta comparison bound.

The result is exact when delta=0 and the exposed residual is nonzero.

4. SCOPE OF THE ACCELERATION

The three-action reconstruction bound applies to updating from the final
directed observation. It is NOT automatically a bound for choosing whichever
observation has the largest computed gain under noise. The max-gain algorithm
retains the general lifetime theorem, while this specialization demonstrates
what a directed-experiment policy can additionally acquire.

The rank-one-comparator promise is an analysis condition, not something three
experiments certify against all high-rank alternatives. A general algorithm
must retain validation for future tasks. The actual source may have a full-rank
tail within the stated zeta; no exact-algebra assertion is made.

For higher rank, repeated noisy Nystrom deflation can accumulate error and
destroy positivity. Block methods need a conditioned sampled core or a separate
adaptive acquisition proof. Those bills are not removed by this specialization.

5. WHY THIS HELPS THE SCIENTIFIC QUESTION

Uniform acquisition can fail because no experiment is aligned with a hidden
mode. An actual task that exposes a residual supplies a new direction; directed
experiments can amplify its useful signal while the adversarial error bound
remains unchanged. The general learner retains safe halfspace updates even
when the optimistic low-rank explanation is wrong. This combines established
power/Nystrom and projection machinery; the scoped acquisition separation and
the complete cost/precision account are the relevant research claims.
