TASK-TRIGGERED AMPLIFICATION OF AN APPROXIMATE RANK-ONE DEFECT
31 October 2026

This is a constructive supporting result, not the main scientific destination.
It shows why a failed task may expose an informative experiment that uniform
offline acquisition could not economically find. The update is a signed rank-one
Nystrom construction, so its algebraic formula is established machinery.

MODEL

The fixed Hermitian source is

  A=aI+lambda uu*+E,  ||u||=1, ||E||<=zeta,

where a is known but u and real lambda are unknown. A chosen-action experiment
returns O(v)=Av+e(v), with arbitrary possibly adaptive error

  ||e(v)||<=eta||v||.

Put delta=eta+zeta. The source may be dense and full rank, and the perturbation
E may have full rank. Its exact generated algebra is not assumed small.

Suppose an actual task produces a unit v and measured residual

  r=O(v)-a v,  t=||r||>=8delta,

with t>0. This is an observed event, not an assumed useful probe direction.
The algorithm sets w=r/t and makes one new chosen-action experiment

  z=O(w)-a w,  h=Re(w*z),  B=z z*/h.

Then h is nonzero and has the sign of lambda, and

  ||lambda uu*-B|| < 4delta,
  ||A-(aI+B)|| < 4eta+5zeta.

PROOF

Relative to the rank-one comparator, each experiment has norm error at most
delta. Since r=lambda(u*v)u+f with ||f||<=delta,

  |lambda|>=t-delta>=7delta,
  c=|u*w|>=sqrt(1-(delta/t)^2)>=sqrt(63/64).

Write z=z0+g, z0=lambda(u*w)u, ||g||<=delta. Let h0=lambda c^2. Then
|h-h0|<=delta and |h|>=|lambda|c^2-delta>0. The rank-one identity
z0 z0*/h0=lambda uu* gives

 ||B-lambda uu*||
 <=(2|lambda|c delta+delta^2+|lambda|delta)
                  /(|lambda|c^2-delta)
 =delta (2c+1+delta/|lambda|)/(c^2-delta/|lambda|)
 <=delta (3+1/7)/(63/64-1/7)
 =delta*1408/377 <4delta.

Add ||E||<=zeta to obtain the actual-source bound. When delta=0 the nonzero
residual recovers the rank-one defect exactly after the additional experiment.

COMPUTATIONAL CONSEQUENCE

The learned model Ahat=aI+B is scalar plus rank one. Its eigenvalues, inverse,
resolvents and action on arbitrary future right-hand sides are inexpensive.
For lambda_min(Ahat)>=muhat>0 and xi=4eta+5zeta,

  ||I-A Ahat^(-1)|| <=xi/muhat.

If the actual A is also bounded below by mu>0,

  ||A^(-1)-Ahat^(-1)|| <=xi/(mu*muhat).

This is a uniform statement over all later right-hand sides under the stated
rank-one-comparator promise. The two experiments cannot certify that structural
promise against arbitrary higher-rank alternatives. Generic tail E is permitted
only through its stated operator-norm bound. The result does not justify
arbitrary accuracy below the experimental error scale.

INTERPRETATION

A weakly aligned task can have residual of order delta while the hidden defect
has order-one strength. Normalizing the observed residual now produces a strongly
aligned second experiment: its signal is of order |lambda|, not the original
small overlap. The source error is still bounded by the same eta. This is a
strict active gain over simply repeating weak random probes, but it uses standard
Nystrom/power-iteration structure and is not a claimed new decomposition method.

GENERAL-RANK OBSTACLE

Naively repeating rank-one noisy Nystrom deflation accumulates model error;
the next residual need not be positive and the effective noise can grow.
Block Nystrom requires a conditioning bill for the sampled core. No rank-r,
constant-noise, dimension-free lifetime bound is asserted by this note.
The parent's convex observation-set correction avoids that unjustified step.
