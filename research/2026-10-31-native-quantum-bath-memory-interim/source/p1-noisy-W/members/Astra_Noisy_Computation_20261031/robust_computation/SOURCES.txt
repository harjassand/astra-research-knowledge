B. Main direct mathematical and computational predecessors

1. K. Crammer, O. Dekel, J. Keshet, S. Shalev-Shwartz, Y. Singer.
   Online Passive-Aggressive Algorithms. JMLR7,551--585,2006.
   https://jmlr.org/papers/volume7/crammer06a/crammer06a.pdf
   Role: least-change halfspace projection and comparator potential analysis.
   The root proof's progress lemma is an instance of this established logic;
   vectorizing a Hermitian tuple does not make it a new learning primitive.

2. U. Subedi, V. Raman, A. Tewari.
   Online Infinite-Dimensional Regression: Learning Linear Operators.
   ALT2024, PMLR237,1113--1133.
   https://proceedings.mlr.press/v237/subedi24a.html
   https://proceedings.mlr.press/v237/subedi24a/subedi24a.pdf
   Role: online operator learning under Schatten bounds; prior separation
   between online learnability and sequential uniform convergence.
   Their uniform-convergence model is not identical to the noisy offline
   source-query service here, but the conceptual predecessor is direct.

3. J. Dunagan, N. J. A. Harvey.
   Iteratively Constructing Preconditioners via the Conjugate Gradient Method.
   STOC2007.
   https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~nickhar/papers/CG/DunaganHarvey-CG.pdf
   Role: failed progress revealing a useful update, retained low-rank
   preconditioners, and amortization across related systems/right-hand sides.
   This is a required strong comparator, not an incidental citation.

4. J. E. Dennis Jr., R. B. Schnabel.
   Least Change Secant Updates for Quasi-Newton Methods. SIAM Review21(4),1979.
   https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/1021091
   Role: ordinary symmetric secant updates, including the PSB approach used
   in the controlled comparison. The direct PSB noise theorem is proved in
   robust_solver/CONVENTIONAL_COMPARISON.txt.

5. R. Fletcher. A new low rank quasi-Newton update scheme for nonlinear
   programming. Research manuscript,2005.
   https://optimization-online.org/wp-content/uploads/2005/08/1192.pdf
   Role: conventional positive-definite quasi-Newton formulas and comparison.

6. Secant Penalized BFGS: A Noise Robust Quasi-Newton Method Via Penalizing
   The Secant Condition. Primary manuscript,2020.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.01275
   Role: noise-robust quasi-Newton is already a developed research line.
   The package does not implement all existing noisy methods or establish
   state-of-the-art performance.

7. R. Jiang, A. Mokhtari. Online-learning-guided quasi-Newton work,2024.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02626
   Role: online matrix learning used to improve optimization rates.
   This limits claims that adapting an operator model during solution is new.

8. Z. Frangella, J. A. Tropp, M. Udell.
   Randomized Nystrom Preconditioning. SIAM J. Matrix Analysis and Applications,2023.
   https://tropp.caltech.edu/papers/FTU23-Randomized-Nystrom-SIMAX.pdf
   Role: strong low-rank retaining baseline and established effective-dimension
   preconditioning. No claim that a new rank-one formula supersedes this work.

9. M. Hardt, E. Price.
   The Noisy Power Method: A Meta Algorithm with Applications. NeurIPS2014.
   https://web.mit.edu/~ecprice/www/papers/npm.pdf
   Role: noise-aware amplification/eigenvector acquisition.
   The task-exposed warm direction in this package is explicitly charged.

10. S. Har-Peled and M. Jones. A Proof of Dudley's Convex Approximation,2019.
    https://sarielhp.org/p/17/dudley_approx/
    Role: strong geometric approximation comparator, distinct from acquisition
    of a numerical certificate through a noisy physical interface.
