INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE DEMAND-REVEALED COMPUTATION THEOREM

VERDICT

No material flaw found in the exact-arithmetic theorem. The noisy mixture
interface, sound halfspace, approximate-comparator decrease, solution certificate,
spectral comparison quantity, and scalar-plus-low-rank implementation agree.
The finite-precision extension needed an explicit acceptance/correction guard
band; that issue was reported and the root's revised section 9 repairs it.
The numerical implementation remains empirical, not outward-rounded validation.
No historical-priority or practical-superiority conclusion follows from this audit.

MATHEMATICAL CHECKS

1. Convex weights preserve the per-action norm error eta. Projecting away the
   imaginary parallel component is real-orthogonal projection onto a space
   containing the true Hermitian action and cannot increase that error.
2. With v*r real, ||sym(rv*)||_F^2=(||r||^2+(v*r)^2)/2. The lower bound on this
   denominator and the earned halfspace h=a(a-eta) are correct.
3. Against every fixed C within operator distance zeta, expanding the squared
   product-Frobenius distance and then clipping gives the stated decrease
   (a-eta)(a-eta-2zeta)/||theta||_2^2. The comparator is analysis-only; no advice
   or unmeasured subspace is used. Telescoping gives the lifetime bound.
4. The accepted-candidate certificate bounds RELATIVE SOLUTION ERROR by
   (a+eta)/(mu-a-eta), not merely relative residual. This derivation is sound.
5. E_zeta is exactly the minimum comparison energy: Weyl bounds eigenvalue
   displacement, and moving eigenvalues toward each alpha_j attains the bound.
6. Updates have support in span{v,r}; clipping leaves alpha_j unchanged on the
   orthogonal complement. The common retained span and the explicit reduced
   inverse formula are valid. The stated dense local-work/storage bounds are
   conservative; optional directed observations also incur their local model
   application work, not just their physical-action bill.
7. Directed residual amplification uses Hermiticity correctly. Keeping the
   original failed observation prevents optional probes from invalidating the
   correction theorem. No universal improvement in the lifetime bound follows.
8. The individual-response variant in section 8A is sound: a failed convex
   mixture has a failing component, and changing another generator does not
   stale a retained observation for an unchanged generator. Its U individual
   updates and F failed task trials must remain distinct counters.

FINITE PRECISION: ISSUE FOUND AND REPAIR

An outward upper residual enclosure U>tau does not imply that the actual norm
exceeds tau. The original finite-precision paragraph omitted this distinction.
The revised rule is sufficient: enforce an enclosure width omega_num and internal
candidate residual chi with omega_num+chi <= tau_out-tau_in. A rejected upper
bound then implies ideal model defect a>tau_in. Use eta_eff in the ideal
halfspace, approximate THAT update, and charge scalar/norm, basis construction,
truncation, and clipping errors in the full product-Frobenius error nu_t.

For zeta<(tau_in-eta_eff)/2, the corrected numerator is E(C)+V_num and denominator
is (tau_in-eta_eff)(tau_in-eta_eff-2zeta). Acceptance uses tau_out+eta_eff in the
solution certificate. Spectral feasibility and the bound on nu_t are genuine
requirements, not conclusions from an ordinary double-precision eigensolve.
The midpoint mixing repair is algebraically correct: an operator-rho approximation
to a matrix in [mu,L], mixed with the midpoint at weight
2rho/(L-mu+2rho), is feasible and within 2rho of the exact projection.

The fixed real-source masking oracle, sphere-moment bound, adaptive baseline
transcript coupling, positive-trace gap, nonlinear uniform inverse-service gap,
and precision-work calculation all check out. The original proof was real-only. The collaborator has now supplied section 4A,

This is a comparison of source-free uniform service with continuing task-revealed
experiments. It is not a lower bound on the positive algorithm's number of
corrections. The known two-point spectrum gives an ordinary one-action inverse
formula on the separator family; the manuscript properly retains that comparator.

ATTRIBUTION LIMIT

Raman, Subedi and Tewari (ALT 2024), Online Infinite-Dimensional Regression:
Learning Linear Operators, already establish online operator learnability under
Schatten bounds and a separation from sequential uniform convergence, including
related batch results. Broad online-versus-uniform learnability is not new here.
Their uniform-convergence notion should not be silently equated with this exact
noisy offline source-query service, but the conceptual predecessor is direct.
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v237/subedi24a/subedi24a.pdf

Crammer et al. (JMLR 2006), Online Passive-Aggressive Algorithms, is also a very
close mathematical predecessor. Normalize this proof's feature as g=G/a, with
margin loss a-eta and comparator violation at most zeta. The usual PA potential
inequality yields the same (a-eta)(a-eta-2zeta)/||g||^2 structure. The candidate
research addition must therefore be narrowed to the combined calibrated-noise,
approximate-comparator, verified mixture-inverse service, compact implementation,
and explicit offline-access comparison. Novelty of that combination is unproved.
https://jmlr.org/papers/volume7/crammer06a/crammer06a.pdf
