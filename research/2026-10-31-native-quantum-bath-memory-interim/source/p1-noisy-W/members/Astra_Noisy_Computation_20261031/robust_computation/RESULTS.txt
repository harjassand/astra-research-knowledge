5. USEFUL FAILURES THAT CHANGE THE CONSTRUCTION

a. A small closure residual is neither an exact algebra certificate nor a
   licence to normalize noise into a new basis. The main solver never performs
   a source-algebra closure test.

b. A converged eigenpair may be the wrong eigenvalue. An eigenvector residual
   alone cannot certify the minimum eigenvalue or worst-case stability.
   The envelope proof supplies a global spectral acquisition construction.

c. Finite pointwise agreement does not certify the envelope. A new spectral
   mode can matter at a vertex of an active-plane cell. The uniform certificate
   checks all those vertices.

d. An outward UPPER residual bound above the correction threshold does not
   prove the true defect exceeds that threshold. The finite-precision repair
   uses a guard band, a bounded enclosure width, an internal solve residual,
   and a summable full-matrix update-error budget.

e. Bounded errors in separate measured columns are not a single perturbation
   of the same operator norm. Let exact columns be orthonormal u_i and measured
   columns be z_i=u_i+eta h, with h perpendicular to their span. Each action
   error iseta, yet ZZ* differs from UU* by at leasteta sqrt(r).
   At r=128,eta=.001, the observed operator error is .0113778895 although the
   sampled core condition number is exactly one. This defeats a naive
   higher-rank transfer of the successful rank-one construction.

f. A rigorous three-action rank-one amplification rule survives full-rank
   tails. Across eight complex numerical cases its maximum learned-comparator
   error was .00170368, below the proved .010 bound. This does not establish
   an analogous rank-r result or validate every max-gain probe selector.

g. The exponential offline separator has a known two-point spectrum and an
   ordinary affine inverse formula. It proves an access-timing distinction,
   not superiority of the newly implemented solver.

h. The retaining PSB method matches the learning contract and wins the small
   controlled comparison. Treat the conventional update as a useful component;
   do not market the halfspace rule as a new capability.
