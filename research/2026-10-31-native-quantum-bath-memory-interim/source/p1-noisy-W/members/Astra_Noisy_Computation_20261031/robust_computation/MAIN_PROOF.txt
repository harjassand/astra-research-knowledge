WHAT IMPERFECT EXPERIMENTS CAN ACQUIRE FOR RELIABLE LATER COMPUTATION
Astra foundational learning/computation branch — 31 October 2026

STATUS AND SCIENTIFIC OBJECT

The completed central construction is a reusable solver that learns through
certified requests. It has a finite lifetime budget of failed checks under
bounded, potentially biased and query-dependent action error. The budget is
controlled by a resolution-dependent spectral comparison quantity, not exact
algebra dimension. Generic full-rank perturbations can leave that quantity small.

A matching investigation of information access shows why this change of object
matters: preparing an accurate source-free service for every future right-hand
side can require exponentially many fixed-accuracy experiments, even for sources
with condition number two and generated algebra dimension two. The later task
itself can reveal a useful probe. Buying better measurement accuracy instead has
an explicit dimension-dependent calibration price in a specified cost model.

The ingredients of the positive learner are established: online operator learning,
least-change/halfspace corrections, spectral projection, low-rank matrix updates,
and residual validation. Dunagan--Harvey already amortize acquired preconditioners
over related solves. The proposed research addition is the combined noisy access,
approximate-comparator, all-request service theorem and the contrasting information
boundary. Historical priority for this combination and practical superiority are
not established. This is not a claimed general theory of intelligence.

The previous exact algebra compiler is accepted at its earned scope. This work
does not re-audit it or assume its robustness. No exact block structure is used
by the construction below. The auxiliary spectral-envelope construction provides
a different, explicitly costed offline capability under stronger resolution.

1. SOURCE, NOISE AND TASK CONTRACT

There are k fixed Hermitian operators A_j on C^N and known scalar bounds

  0 < mu I <= A_j <= L I.                                  (1.1)

The bounds are input conditions, not conclusions inferred from finitely many
noisy actions. They give coercivity, not a useful basis or solver. A common way
to meet this contract is a known regularization floor added to bounded positive
operators; its physical availability and cost are application premises.

An original action on v returns y_j with

  ||y_j-A_j v|| <= eta ||v||.                              (1.2)

eta is a calibrated instrument bound. Errors may be biased, nonlinear in v,
history-dependent, and adversarial, provided (1.2) holds at every action. No
averaging gain or single fixed perturbation matrix is assumed. A full vector is
both supplied and returned, so coordinate handling costs are paid. If requesting
accuracy eta costs c(eta), every action below pays c(eta). The noise bound cannot
be learned uniformly from repeatability alone without further assumptions.

A later task supplies theta in the k-simplex and a right-hand side b, requesting

  x_* = A(theta)^(-1)b,  A(theta)=sum_j theta_j A_j.         (1.3)

Both theta and b may depend on all past outputs, experiments and retained state.
No independence or task distribution is required. The final answer must have a
certified relative solution error. All original source calls made while answering
are counted; this is explicitly not a source-free offline service.

One action of the mixture costs |supp(theta)| original actions. Its combined
error obeys eta||v|| since theta is nonnegative and sums to one. The sources need
not commute. Their generated algebra may be the full matrix algebra.

2. MODEL AND EXACT LOCAL COMPUTATION

Choose scalar alpha_j in [mu,L], for example alpha_j=mu. Initialize B_j=alpha_j I.
No latent subspace, rank, or comparator is given. Maintain Hermitian B_j in

  K = {B : mu I <= B <= L I}.

For each task compute x=B(theta)^(-1)b using the acquired compact representation
described in section7. If b=0, return zero without an experiment. Otherwise set
v=x/||x|| and obtain a mixture action y approximately A(theta)v.

Hermitian sources permit one useful noise reduction. Replace

  y <- y - i Im(v* y) v.                                  (2.1)

This is real-orthogonal projection onto {z : Im(v*z)=0}; the true action lies in
that real subspace. Thus (2.1) cannot increase its error. Define

  r = y-B(theta)v,  a=||r||.                              (2.2)

The model action is known. After (2.1), v*r is real. The theorem first uses exact
local arithmetic; section9 states how finite computation enters the contract.

Choose an acceptance threshold tau with

  eta < tau,  tau+eta < mu.                               (2.3)

If a<=tau, return x. Otherwise correct the model and retry the same requested task.

3. EVERY RETURNED ANSWER IS CERTIFIED

The true defect on the normalized candidate satisfies

  ||(A(theta)-B(theta))v|| <= a+eta.

Because B(theta)x=b,

  ||A(theta)x-b|| <= (a+eta)||x||.                         (3.1)

Coercivity of A(theta) gives

  ||x-x_*|| <= rho ||x||,  rho=(a+eta)/mu < 1.

Using ||x||<=||x_*||+||x-x_*|| yields the relative solution certificate

  ||x-x_*||/||x_*|| <= (a+eta)/(mu-a-eta).                 (3.2)

Also ||b||=||B(theta)x||>=mu||x||, so relative residual is at most(a+eta)/mu.
Those two relative error statements must not be confused.

To request relative solution accuracy epsilon, it suffices to choose

  tau+eta <= epsilon mu/(1+epsilon).                      (3.3)

The known mu fixes the resolution at which inversion is meaningful. No promise
for unrestricted rational queries or vanishing coercivity is inferred.

4. A FAILED CHECK SUPPLIES A SOUND MATRIX HALFSPACE

Use the real Hilbert--Schmidt product <X,Y>=Re Tr(X*Y) on Hermitian matrices.
For an observation with a>tau, define

  G_j = theta_j sym(r v*),  sym(X)=(X+X*)/2,
  h = a(a-eta),
  g2 = sum_j ||G_j||_F^2.                                (4.1)

The true source tuple satisfies

  sum_j <G_j,A_j-B_j>
  = Re[r* (A(theta)-B(theta))v]
  >= a(a-eta)=h.                                        (4.2)

Thus (4.2) is an earned halfspace containing the true operators. It does not fit
the noisy response as if it were exact. Since a>eta and v*r is real,

  g2 = ||theta||_2^2 (a^2+(v*r)^2)/2,
  ||theta||_2^2 a^2/2 <= g2 <= ||theta||_2^2 a^2.          (4.3)

Without (2.1), the general formula instead contains Re((v*r)^2), not
(Re(v*r))^2. The projection makes the denominator bound explicit and harmless.

Project the current tuple onto the halfspace and then onto the spectral interval:

  B_j^+ = clip_[mu,L](B_j + (h/g2)G_j).                  (4.4)

Spectral clipping replaces each eigenvalue by its nearest point of [mu,L]. It is
the Frobenius projection onto K. Both operations are explicit. Equation(4.4)
is a conventional halfspace/least-change correction, not a new optimization
primitive or an assumed solver oracle.

5. THE LIFETIME COMPARISON THEOREM

For analysis ONLY, take any fixed Hermitian comparison tuple C_j in K such that

  ||A_j-C_j|| <= zeta for every j.                       (5.1)

Neither C_j nor zeta is supplied to or computed by the learner. Put

  Phi(B;C)=sum_j ||B_j-C_j||_F^2,
  E(C)=sum_j ||C_j-alpha_j I||_F^2.                     (5.2)

LEMMA. For an update from an observation of size a>eta+2zeta,

  Phi(B^+;C) <= Phi(B;C)
       - (a-eta)(a-eta-2zeta)/||theta||_2^2.             (5.3)

PROOF. The mixture comparison defect has norm at mostzeta on a unit v. Thus

  <G,C-B> >= a(a-eta-zeta)=h-a zeta.

Before clipping, expansion of squared distance gives

  Phi(B+(h/g2)G;C)
  <= Phi(B;C) - [h^2-2h a zeta]/g2.

Use g2<=||theta||_2^2 a^2 and h=a(a-eta). Projection onto K cannot increase
distance to C_j in K. This proves(5.3). QED.

THEOREM 5.1. Fix tau satisfying(2.3). For every arbitrary sequence of tasks,
every allowed sequence of action errors, and every comparison tuple satisfying
(5.1) with zeta<(tau-eta)/2, the total number M of failed checks/model corrections
over the entire sequence obeys

  M <= E(C)/[(tau-eta)(tau-eta-2zeta)].                   (5.4)

More strongly, the sum over updates of the quantities in(5.3) is at mostE(C).
The theorem is simultaneous over all valid comparison tuples: the execution
does not choose a favourable comparator or receive it as advice.

PROOF. Each failed check has a>tau, ||theta||_2^2<=1, and consumes at least the
denominator in(5.4) of the nonnegative potential. Telescope. QED.

Taking C_j=A_j and zeta=0 always proves finite termination of every task and a
finite total correction count in finite N. Useful approximate structure improves
the bill; it is not needed for validity. For T nonzero tasks, the basic algorithm
uses T+M mixture experiments and at most k(T+M) original actions.

The accepted check still costs an experiment. Finite corrections do not imply
that the learner can identify a time after which all future checks may be skipped.
The offline obstruction in section10 explains why that stronger conclusion fails.

6. A COMPLEXITY QUANTITY THAT SURVIVES GENERIC PERTURBATIONS

Define, without giving it to the learner,

  E_zeta(A;alpha) = sum_j sum_i
                   (|lambda_i(A_j)-alpha_j|-zeta)_+^2.  (6.1)

This is exactly the infimum of E(C) over C_j in K at operator distance at mostzeta
from A_j. To attain it, retain each A_j eigenvector and move its eigenvalue toward
alpha_j by min(zeta,|lambda-alpha_j|). The new value stays in [mu,L]. Conversely,
ordered eigenvalues of C_j differ from those of A_j by at mostzeta, so no smaller
sum of squared deviations from alpha_j is possible. This is a characterization
of the comparison quantity, not an eigendecomposition step used by the learner.

Consequently the algorithm obeys the oracle inequality

  M <= inf_[0<=zeta<(tau-eta)/2]
       E_zeta(A;alpha)/[(tau-eta)(tau-eta-2zeta)].         (6.2)

If A_j=alpha_j I+K_j+F_j, with alpha_j I+K_j in K,
rank(K_j)<=r_j, ||K_j||<=beta_j and ||F_j||<=zeta, then

  E_zeta <= sum_j r_j beta_j^2.                         (6.3)

F_j may be generic and full rank. It may make the generated algebra full M_N.
Neither exact multiplicity nor a thresholded algebra-closure test is involved.
Perturbations below the useful task resolution are absorbed in the comparison
width; detectable directions pay through their squared spectral departure.

This quantity depends on the chosen simple initialization. For example a large
unlearned scalar shift can make a poor initialization expensive. There is no claim
that it captures every tractable operator or that the displayed bill is optimal.
The eigenvalue expression is related to established spectral thresholding and
Schatten-norm learning; the contribution claimed is its role in the full service
and access comparison, not discovery of a new matrix norm.

7. THE DOWNSTREAM SOLVER IS EXPLICIT AND ITS WORK IS PAID

Each update direction has support in span{v,r}; spectral clipping does not alter
the scalar action alpha_j outside the accumulated span. After M updates there
is a common orthonormal U with s<=min(N,2M) such that

  B_j=alpha_j I+U D_j U*.

The learner acquires U from the actual update vectors. It receives no useful
span in advance. For a mixture, put alpha(theta)=sum theta_j alpha_j and
S(theta)=alpha(theta)I_s+sum theta_j D_j. Then

  B(theta)^(-1)b
  = (b-UU*b)/alpha(theta) + U S(theta)^(-1)U*b.           (7.1)

S(theta) has eigenvalues in [mu,L]. Standard small-matrix factorization therefore
computes the candidate; an opaque downstream planning or inverse oracle is not
used. This also solves the strongly convex quadratic minimization
(1/2)x*A(theta)x-Re(b*x) to the associated residual/solution accuracy.

Put s_bar=max(1,s) to include vector handling before the first learned direction.
Per trial, dense local arithmetic is O(N s_bar+k s_bar^2+s_bar^3), plus the
original action costs. Each correction extends U by at most two vectors and
costs O(N s_bar+k s_bar^3) in conservative local arithmetic, plus all actions.
Storage is O(N s_bar+k s_bar^2), including working vectors.
The N-dimensional basis remains necessary for vector outputs. These costs can
dominate source savings; a source-count theorem is not a wall-time advantage.

Ordinary retaining quasi-Newton, recycled Krylov, and learned-preconditioner
methods are entitled to the same source interface, retained state and baseline.
The comparison must include their calibration and source use on the same errors.

8. DIRECTED EXPERIMENTS CAN AMPLIFY AN EARNED DISCREPANCY

The basic theorem already suffices. An optional construction can spend p extra
mixture actions after each failure, keeping B fixed during that burst.

Given r=E v+e with E=A(theta)-B(theta) Hermitian, ||v||=1 and ||e||<=eta, set
w=r/||r||. Then

  ||Ew|| >= Re<v,Ew> = Re<Ev,w> >= ||r||-eta.             (8.1)

A new noisy residual observation along w therefore has norm at least||r||-2eta.
There is no random-initialization overlap hypothesis in(8.1): the task failure
has supplied the initial direction. Repeat if desired. Include the original
observation in the candidate set. Choose only observations with a>tau, for
example the one with largest computed true-source Fejer gain h^2/g2, and apply
the same update(4.4).

Every chosen observation obeys the same proof. The total correction bound(5.4)
survives, while the original action bound becomes

  k[T+(p+1)M].                                          (8.2)

This optional policy can reduce M and retained dimension, but no general theorem
improving(5.4) for arbitrary higher-rank sources has been proved. The separate
rank-one analysis in stable_algebra proves a stronger fixed-sequence result;
its small operator-error guarantee is not automatically inherited by the max-gain
selector. Noisy power iteration and warm-start Nyström are established ingredients.

8A. RETAIN ALL INDIVIDUAL RESPONSES ALREADY PAID FOR

A mixture experiment obtains each queried y_j, not merely their sum. A stronger
implementation retains these responses. At a common unit v define individual
r_j=y_j-B_jv after the Hermitian-action projection. If the mixture check fails,
at least one queried individual residual exceeds tau, since

  ||sum theta_j r_j|| <= sum theta_j||r_j|| <= max_j||r_j||.

One may apply the single-generator halfspace rule to every such individual, with
the other models fixed. Each individual update decreases the total comparison
potential by at least(tau-eta)(tau-eta-2zeta). Thus the SAME bound(5.4) holds for
the number U of individual model updates, and the number F of failed task trials
is at mostU. Several useful updates can be earned from one paid mixture action.

Directed experiments may target just one generator; each costs one original
action. With at mostp such actions per failed trial, source work is at most

  k(T+F)+pF <= kT+(k+p)U.                               (8A.1)

All residuals used to update a given generator are evaluated against its current
model before that generator is changed. The common retained span still has
dimension at most2U. This improvement uses information already returned by the
interface and must also be granted to the conventional comparison methods.

For s_bar=max(1,min(N,2U)), a conservative total local-arithmetic bound is

  O((T+(p+1)U)(N s_bar+k s_bar^2+s_bar^3)
      + kU s_bar^3),                                  (8A.2)

with storage O(N s_bar+k s_bar^2), in addition to the source bill (8A.1). This
uses streaming selection during directed bursts. It deliberately
charges the local model applications in directed bursts as well as candidate
solves and spectral clipping. When an action costs c(eta), multiply (8A.1) by
c(eta); do not interpret the count as dimension-free physical work. This is an
arithmetic-operation bound. A complete bit-complexity analysis for certified
local numerics is not supplied by the prototype.

9. LOCAL NUMERICAL PRECISION IS PART OF THE BILL

The source-error guarantee is deterministic and robust to(1.2); it does not
automatically certify a double-precision implementation. In particular, neither
numerical orthogonalization nor eigenvalue clipping is an exact rank certificate.

A sufficient finite-computation variant requires a guard band. Suppose the
internal candidate residual satisfies ||B(theta)x-b||<=chi||x||. Compute an
outward enclosure of the normalized observed requested residual with width at
mostomega_num, and accept only when its upper endpoint is at mosttau_out. An
unsuccessful test then implies actual observed requested residual greater than
tau_out-omega_num, hence model defect greater than tau_out-omega_num-chi.
Choose tau_in>eta_eff and enforce

  omega_num+chi <= tau_out-tau_in.

Local action/model arithmetic error is included in eta_eff. Approximate the exact
halfspace update at certified precision; include scalar and norm errors in the
matrix-update budget nu_t below. Use tau_in in the correction denominator
and tau_out+eta_eff in the output certificate. Without the enclosure-width bill,
rejecting an upper bound does NOT establish a real failed direction. An ambiguous
test is refined until the guard-band requirement holds; no source action is
silently substituted for missing internal precision.

For matrix updates, let the computed clipped tuple stay in K and differ from
the exact projected update by at most nu_t in product Frobenius norm. The diameter
of K^k is D=sqrt(kN)(L-mu). Relative to any C in K^k, the potential loss in one
numerical update is at most

  2D nu_t+nu_t^2.                                       (9.1)

Thus summable, explicitly budgeted update errors add
V_num=sum_t(2D nu_t+nu_t^2) to the numerator in(5.4). For example nu_t decreasing
as(t+1)^(-2) makes this finite. It is unnecessary to promise exact eigenvectors.

Such an update is computable on the small retained span. Enclose the exact
clipped matrix to operator errorrho, then mix the approximation with the midpoint
of [mu,L] by weight 2rho/(L-mu+2rho). This restores spectral feasibility. The
change from the exact projection is at most2rho in operator norm, hence at most
2rho sqrt(s) in Frobenius norm per component. Choosing certified rho to meet
nu_t and paying small-matrix precision and bit growth gives the stated budget.
The known scalar action outside the retained span remains untouched.

The supplied NumPy implementation is an empirical check, not this outward-rounded
implementation. The exact rational halfspace fixture verifies the algebraic
potential identity separately. No noise estimate is inferred from small residuals.
This section supplies a sufficient error budget conditional on certified local
operations; it is not a complete implemented finite-bit compiler.

10. TASK-BLIND OFFLINE ACQUISITION HAS A DIFFERENT INFORMATION COST

The full ordinary proof is in robust_solver/NOISY_OFFLINE_OBSTRUCTION.txt.
Fix known unit e and unknown unit u perpendicular to e, and set

  A_0=lambda I+beta ee*,
  A_u=lambda I+beta(ee*+uu*).

Both have generated algebra dimension two and condition number1+beta/lambda.
At lambda=beta=1 they are well conditioned with no small spectral scale. A fixed,
continuous, homogeneous masking oracle returns exactly A_0v when
beta|u*v|<=eta||v||, while obeying the same bounded-error contract everywhere.
The proof covers both real and complex adaptive probes, with N>=3 and
0<eta<beta. In the complex case use radial soft thresholding of u*v; no
restriction to real probes is imposed on a Hermitian-source algorithm.

For any randomized adaptive acquisition making q task-blind queries, fixed-source
transcript coupling and a spherical-cap bound give indistinguishability probability
at least1-2q exp[-(N-1)eta^2/(2beta^2)]. Consequently an offline retained service
correct for every right-hand side with relative solution error
epsilon<beta/(2lambda+beta), on one event of probability1-delta, requires

  q >= ((1-2delta)/2) exp[(N-1)eta^2/(2beta^2)],          (10.1)

where 0<delta<1/2. This is worst-case query complexity under the declared error
contract, allowing unlimited local computation.

The output service may be nonlinear. The proof does not merely forbid recovering
an explicit matrix. A fixed positive polynomial trace query with trace one versus
two yields the same bound whenepsilon<1/3. The source and masking map are fixed
before the algorithm coins; a source chosen after seeing the probes is not used.

If a query can choose eta_t at work cost eta_t^(-p), adaptive precision instead
has an unavoidable worst-case bill

  B >= ((1-2delta)/2)[e(N-1)/(p beta^2)]^(p/2).           (10.2)

For detecting the promised extra rank-one mode (and its trace-one-versus-two
query), random probes at accuracyeta=beta/[4sqrt(N-1)] give a matching
O(N^(p/2) log(1/delta)) upper dimension dependence. This upper bound is for
detection, not arbitrary offline inverse reconstruction. The price model is a
declared calibrated-adversarial-oracle model, not a theorem about iid noise.

In contrast, the online solver above has E_0<=2 on this same family relative to
alpha=1. Its failures are bounded independently of N, and it serves T requests
with T plus a finite correction budget of experiments. This is a separation
between task-blind compilation and request-revealed experimentation, not between
new and conventional online algorithms.

Indeed, the separator family has a known two-point spectrum and hence the
ordinary formula

  A^(-1)=((2lambda+beta)I-A)/[lambda(lambda+beta)].        (10.3)

One action on each later b already implements its inverse approximately. It is
therefore an information-boundary example, NOT a performance win for the learner.
The positive numerical evidence instead uses mixed-spectrum noncommuting families.

10A. A RESOLUTION FLOOR THAT NO ADAPTIVITY REMOVES

Suppose L>=mu+2eta. The two scalar sources mu I and(mu+2eta)I admit the same
oracle(midpoint) action (mu+eta)v, with allowed erroreta||v|| on every v. Their
relative-solution balls for a nonzero b are disjoint if

  epsilon < eta/(mu+eta).

Thus even unlimited task-directed experimentation cannot provide that accuracy
uniformly under(1.2). The positive theorem's requirement eta=O(epsilon mu) has
the correct noise/coercivity scale, though its constants and correction count are
not claimed minimax. Known coercivity is an operational resource, not a substitute
for resolution. A single fixed linear perturbation already suffices for this
scalar floor; the stronger exponential hiding result uses the broader per-action
error class and must not be transferred to coherent-noise models without proof.

11. ALTERNATIVE OFFLINE COMPUTATIONAL KNOWLEDGE

The collaborator construction in operational_learning/theorem.md acquires a
piecewise-affine envelope of the minimum eigenvalue of a Hermitian pencil over
a fixed low-dimensional control polytope. Its learned spectral witnesses compile
subsequently supplied convex resource-constrained design problems into explicit
linear programs. This survives full generated algebra dimension and does not
receive an invariant subspace or downstream planning oracle.

Its cell-vertex test has a uniform proof: the gap between an active affine plane
and a concave minimum-eigenvalue function is convex on that cell. The maximum is
therefore attained at a vertex. Failed vertices add witness planes; a separation
packing argument bounds additions in fixed control dimension. This is closely
related to established Sandwich/Benson and spectral-bundle methods.

The spectral experiment is not free. Under arbitrary bounded action error, its
general construction requires accuracy shrinking roughly as N^(-1/2), with
additional polynomial/logarithmic factors, and charges every coefficient action.
Its larger numerical fixture instead assumes fixed coherent operator errors and
audits floating-point Lanczos empirically. These are separate models. The simple
full-column acquisition baseline can beat its conservative filter construction.
The branch preserves this alternative and its costs rather than conflating it
with the fixed-noise demand-driven theorem.

12. SCOPE, COMPARATORS AND REMAINING SCIENTIFIC GAP

The mechanism makes a specified class of later solves reliably executable while
learning their reusable operator corrections. It does not solve arbitrary planning,
learn unrestricted rational calculus, guarantee small algebra, or certify global
operator accuracy from a few quiet residuals. It does not eliminate N-dimensional
input/output work. Poor spectral initialization or diffuse resolvable structure
can make its comparison bill large.

Online operator learning under Schatten bounds, least-change secant methods,
noisy quasi-Newton updates, randomized Nyström preconditioning, and CG-based
cumulative preconditioner construction are strong predecessors. The literal
mechanism can be implemented using ordinary versions of those ingredients.
The most defensible result is the explicit link between experiment resolution,
the timing of task information, comparison complexity, and paid reliable recourse.

The optional amplification tests improve the first fixture, but are not a general
optimality theorem. Comparisons with ordinary retaining solvers are preserved in
the accompanying reports even if they win. This work establishes neither a
civilisation-changing result nor historical priority. Its exact assertions and
useful failures are retained as a concrete research result, not replaced by an
unexecuted agenda.

The strongest attribution qualifications are explicit. Subedi--Raman--Tewari
(ALT2024) already separate operator online learnability from sequential uniform
convergence. Passive-aggressive regression (Crammer et al.,2006) already supplies
the supporting-halfspace potential inequality after vectorization. A broad
online-versus-uniform-learning claim, or that progress inequality alone, is not
new. The ordinary PSB secant update also earns a bounded-noise lifetime theorem;
see robust_solver/CONVENTIONAL_COMPARISON.txt. In the fixed-task experiment PSB
with two directed probes uses415 source actions, versus420 for the stronger
individual halfspace implementation. This rules out a demonstrated superiority
claim for the new update and supports treating the result as a specific acquired
service/access theorem built from conventional mechanisms.

Source locators, numerical evidence, precise qualifications and reproduction
commands accompany the final package. Astra source scope follows the user-given
common corpus revision cb87e72296824ae2a677ccd337025d02d20cbb0a and the accepted
exact-compiler statements; no new portfolio integration or repository write is
