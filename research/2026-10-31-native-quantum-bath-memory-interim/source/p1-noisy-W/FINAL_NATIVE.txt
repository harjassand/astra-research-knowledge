## 1. What the learner acquires

The computational capability is to answer adaptively requested systems

\[
A(\theta)x=b,\qquad A(\theta)=\sum_{j=1}^{k}\theta_jA_j,
\]

where \(\theta\) lies in the simplex. The operators may be noncommuting and generate the full matrix algebra.

The premises are explicit:

\[
\mu I\preceq A_j\preceq LI,\qquad
\|\mathcal O_j(v)-A_jv\|\le \eta\|v\|.
\]

The spectral bounds and experimental error bound are supplied contracts. Errors may be biased, nonlinear in the query, and dependent on previous experiments. Full-vector actions remain the access model, and every original action is counted.

The learner begins with scalar models \(B_j=\alpha_jI\). For each request it:

1. Solves the current learned model.
2. Experiments on the normalized candidate solution.
3. Returns the candidate when its measured defect certifies the requested accuracy.
4. Otherwise uses that failed check to correct the retained model and retries.

Let \(a\) be the measured normalized defect. An accepted answer has the relative **solution-error** certificate

\[
\frac{\|x-x_\star\|}{\|x_\star\|}
\le
\frac{a+\eta}{\mu-a-\eta}.
\]

Thus an acceptance threshold \(\tau\) delivers accuracy \(\varepsilon\) whenever

\[
\eta<\tau,\qquad
\tau+\eta\le \frac{\varepsilon\mu}{1+\varepsilon}.
\]

The acquired basis and downstream solver are explicit. Every correction introduces at most two directions. With a common retained basis \(Q\),

\[
B_j=\alpha_jI+QD_jQ^\ast.
\]

A future candidate requires vector projections and a solve in the learned span. Neither that span nor a useful reduced solver is supplied in advance.

### The lifetime theorem

For analysis, consider **any unknown** comparison operators \(C_j\) satisfying

\[
\mu I\preceq C_j\preceq LI,
\qquad
\|A_j-C_j\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le\zeta.
\]

The learner never receives these operators or \(\zeta\). A noise-aware halfspace correction gives

\[
U\le
\frac{\sum_j\|C_j-\alpha_jI\|_F^2}
{(\tau-\eta)(\tau-\eta-2\zeta)},
\qquad
\zeta<\frac{\tau-\eta}{2},
\]

where \(U\) counts individual model corrections over the **entire future request stream**.

The proof tracks the squared distance from the learned models to \(C\). Each failed check earns a sound matrix halfspace. Projecting onto it, followed by spectral clipping, decreases that potential by a fixed positive amount. This remains true for adaptively selected requests and adversarial allowed errors.

For \(T\) nonzero requests and at most \(p\) additional directed probes per failed request, the original-action bill is

\[
\boxed{\quad kT+(k+p)U.\quad}
\]

The \(kT\) validation cost remains. The theorem does not identify a time after which all future checks can safely be omitted.

## 2. Structure that survives imperfect experiments

The best comparison energy has an explicit characterization:

\[
E_\zeta
=
\sum_{j,i}
\left(
|\lambda_i(A_j)-\alpha_j|-\zeta
\right)_+^2.
\]

This measures spectral departure from the simple initialization that remains above the comparison resolution. It is an analytical characterization; computing the eigenvalues is not part of acquisition.

For example, suppose

\[
A_j=\alpha_jI+K_j+F_j,
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}(K_j)\le r_j,\quad
\|K_j\|\le\beta_j,\quad
\|F_j\|\le\zeta,
\]

with the comparison core inside the prescribed spectral interval. Then

\[
E_\zeta\le\sum_jr_j\beta_j^2.
\]

The \(F_j\) can be generic and full rank. They can destroy the small exact algebra while leaving the lifetime correction bound useful. This avoids making a numerical closure threshold carry an exact algebraic meaning.

The computational bill also includes the representation. After \(U\) updates, its dimension is at most \(\min(N,2U)\). For retained dimension \(s\ge1\), storage is \(O(Ns+ks^2)\), and a candidate solve costs \(O(Ns+ks^2+s^3)\) local arithmetic, in addition to experiments. These costs can dominate the savings in source actions.

A finite-precision extension requires an acceptance guard band, controlled residual-enclosure width, and a summable matrix-update error budget. Those conditions are proved sufficient in the record. **The supplied NumPy implementation is empirical software, not an outward-rounded implementation of that extension.**

## 3. Why the timing of task information matters

Consider a known unit vector \(e\), an unknown unit vector \(u\perp e\), and

\[
A_0=I+ee^\ast,\qquad
A_u=I+ee^\ast+uu^\ast.
\]

Both sources have condition number two and generated algebra dimension two.

A fixed, continuous, calibrated error rule can hide the extra mode whenever

\[
|u^\ast v|\le\eta\|v\|.
\]

Along the resulting baseline transcript, even adaptive probes have no information about \(u\). Sphere concentration then gives the following lower bound.

For \(N\ge3\), \(0<\eta<1\), and success probability \(1-\delta\), acquiring a source-free inverse service that is simultaneously accurate for every future right-hand side, with relative error below \(1/3\), requires

\[
q\ge
\frac{1-2\delta}{2}
\exp\!\left(\frac{(N-1)\eta^2}{2}\right).
\]

The same obstruction applies to the single positive trace query \(\operatorname{tr}(A-I)\), whose values are one and two. The source and error rule are fixed before the learner’s random coins. The proof covers complex probes as well as real ones.

This establishes an information limitation without a small denominator or ill-conditioned inverse. It specifically concerns calibrated, potentially query-dependent error; it is not a lower bound for unbiased independent noise or a single fixed perturbation matrix.

Buying finer measurements changes the result. Under the explicit price model \(c(\eta)=\eta^{-p}\), detecting the promised additional mode has precision-work dimension dependence

\[
\Theta(N^{p/2})
\]

at fixed confidence. The upper bound purchases accuracy proportional to \(N^{-1/2}\). Its scope is promised detection, not arbitrary offline inverse reconstruction.

There is an essential conventional comparator: this particular two-point-spectrum family has an affine inverse formula, so one action on a later right-hand side already suffices for approximate solution. **The example establishes the information boundary; it supplies no performance victory for the learned solver.** The ordinary proof is in :chatgpt-content-reference{index="4"}[NOISY_OFFLINE_OBSTRUCTION.txt](producer-workspace/NOISY_OFFLINE_OBSTRUCTION.txt).

## 5. A separate construction earns computation without later experiments

The complementary attack acquires a certified envelope of

\[
f(u)=\lambda_{\min}\!\left(H_0+\sum_i u_iH_i\right)
\]

over a fixed, low-dimensional control polytope. Its retained affine witnesses turn future linear-price and polytopal-resource design requests into explicit linear programs.

The uniform certificate checks every vertex of every active-plane cell. On such a cell the envelope is affine and \(f\) is concave, so the approximation gap is convex and controlled by its vertex values. The construction also earns its spectral measurements; an ordinary eigenpair residual is insufficient.

In the 32,768-coordinate numerical example, acquisition retained seven planes and used **17,612 original coefficient actions**. Sixty subsequent design requests required zero additional physical actions. That experiment used coherent fixed-operator errors. The separate general bounded-query-error proof requires substantially finer acquisition precision. These models remain distinct in the preserved construction.

## Scientific conclusion

**The earned computational knowledge is a retained model with an explicit accuracy contract, a concrete downstream algorithm, and a bound on further experimental work.** For the main construction, that knowledge supports reliable adaptive computation through a finite cumulative correction budget. For the separate design construction, stronger acquisition conditions support a reusable representation that eliminates later experiments.

The investigation also rules out several tempting shortcuts: quiet residuals do not establish global knowledge; individually small column errors need not form a comparably small operator perturbation; and the successful rank-one amplification argument does not automatically extend to higher rank.

This is a completed construction and a precise information boundary. It has not yet earned a new general tractability principle or a superior learning algorithm. The conventional successes and the failed extensions are preserved with the proofs, rather than absorbed into an inflated discovery claim.
