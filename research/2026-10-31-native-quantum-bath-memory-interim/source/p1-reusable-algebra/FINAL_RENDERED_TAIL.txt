Failures that changed the mechanism or its interpretation

Several failures are preserved because they constrain the scientific claim.

Numerical leakage destroyed compression. On the same target and eight probes, sequential residual normalization expanded to all 528 coordinates and used 1,056 matvecs. Batched frontier SVD recovered the expected 208-dimensional space with 416 calls. The failed method had the smaller final invariance residual: full ambient space is trivially invariant. Small residuals alone do not certify useful acquisition.

The preliminary experiment had an avoidable unknown. Its three-component fixture left only one multiplicity unresolved. Known

There is also a strict boundary on cumulative reuse. A new operator selected after seeing the probes can vanish on their entire acquired space while acting nontrivially elsewhere. The package supplies a separate exact admission mechanism using fresh witnesses, but successful relation admission does not transfer the old trace confidence guarantee to an adaptively enlarged algebra. Fresh trace acquisition remains necessary.

The completed work establishes an exact acquisition principle, a uniform trace theorem, a retained uncertainty mechanism, explicit computational consequences, an inherited accuracy lower bound, and executable realizations. The stronger foundational claim depends on extending this capability to approximately structured sources with stable finite precision while retaining a credible advantage against the strongest ordinary methods. That gap remains open, and the proof and failed constructions identify precisely where it must be closed.
