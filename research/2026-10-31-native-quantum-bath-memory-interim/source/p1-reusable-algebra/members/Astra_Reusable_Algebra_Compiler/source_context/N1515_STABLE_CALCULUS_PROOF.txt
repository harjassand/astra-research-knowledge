ASTRA — ACQUIRING A STABLE CALCULUS OF EXPERIMENTS
31 October 2026

Status and main conclusion
==========================

This investigation constructs a finite-noise compiler for a specified family of
hidden contraction operators. It acquires its retained feature programs and
their approximate operation rules from scalar return correlations. It is not
given hidden coordinates, a complete predictive basis, a Gram eigenvalue floor,
an equivalence oracle, or a representation-construction oracle. Its returned
matrices have column l1 norm at most one. Consequently, local errors and readout
uncertainty do not acquire a condition-number multiplier during later use.

The price is explicit geometric complexity. A finite packing bound guarantees
termination, but can be exponential in linear dimension. A rank-two system can
require arbitrarily many atoms for this stability geometry and can lack any
finite exact such representation. Strong ordinary retaining learners win the
first finite fixture and the exact spectral counterfixture. Their constructions
are adopted as alternatives, not excluded from the comparison.

The completed contribution is therefore a constructive bridge from noisy,
physically specified inner-product experiments to a stable reusable operation
model, with acquisition, failed attempts, computation, precision, and future
execution charged. It is not a demonstrated general efficiency advantage,
historical novelty claim, general POMDP learner, or theory of general intelligence.

The smaller recovery-controller theorem in recovery_access.md is separate. It
proves a complete lifetime minimax law Theta(H^2+TH) for fresh independent target
bits. There an ordinary retaining model learner attains the same law.

2. Exact input/output contract
==============================

Let H be a REAL Hilbert space. There are m unknown linear maps T_a:H->H with
operator norm at most one, and a seed f with norm at most one. For a finite word
w=(a_1,...,a_l), define

    v_empty=f,
    v_(w a)=T_a v_w.

The learner stores w as an executable descriptor. It does not see v_w. All
reachable vectors lie in the unit ball. Several seeds are allowed, but they are
charged separately; the clean theorem is stated for one seed.

There is an actual scalar experiment for every requested pair (u,v). Each fresh
episode returns Z in [-1,1] with conditional expectation

    E[Z | the history before this experiment] = <v_u,v_v>.

Fresh conditionally unbiased episodes suffice for Hoeffding/Azuma bounds. Bounded
but biased noise is not enough. The source is fixed during acquisition and use.
The contract includes the cost of initial preparation, both word executions,
endpoint measurements, and every discarded or unsuccessful episode.

A finite readout library g_1,...,g_q has norm at most one. The experiment for
<g_j,v_w> is also available and charged. The future task is to predict

    F_j(w)=<g_j,T_(a_l)...T_(a_1) f>.

The output is a dictionary of actually acquired word features v_i, rational
matrices M_a, and readout estimates hhat_j such that

    ||M_a||_(1->1)<=1,
    ||T_a v_i - sum_k (M_a)_(k i) v_k|| <= rho,
    |(hhat_j)_i-<g_j,v_i>|<=beta.

On one acquisition confidence event, EVERY later word w of length L obeys

    |F_j(w)-hhat_j M_(a_L)...M_(a_1)e_start| <= L rho+beta.   (1)

There is no union-bound charge per later word. Words can be selected adaptively
using already acquired information: the event certifies the whole stated class.
This does not give arbitrary conditioned hidden states, an arbitrary new readout,
complete record TV, or reactive policy laws. Those are different tasks.

Soundness of a completed residual certificate needs NO rank promise. Finite
dimension or finite packing is used only to prove termination and bound cost.
Thus hidden higher-dimensional directions do not invalidate a genuinely measured
small residual; the contraction and real Gram-experiment premises remain essential.

3. One physical realization of the scalar experiment
====================================================

Let pi be a genuine stationary distribution of a hidden Markov system. Each
available control is a reversible Markov kernel preserving THE SAME pi. A
known endpoint observable f takes values in [-1,1]. The kernel acts on functions
in L2(pi), is self-adjoint, and is a contraction by conditional Jensen.

For v_u=T_u f and v_v=T_v f,

    <v_u,v_v> = <f,T_u^* T_v f>.

An episode starts from pi, reads f, executes the chronological kernel word for
T_u^* T_v, then reads f again. The product of the endpoint readings is in
[-1,1] and has this mean. Since each primitive is self-adjoint, the adjoint word
uses the same available primitives in reversed operator order. No hidden-state
copy, hidden coordinate, independent same-state continuation, microscopic noise
replay, or chosen hidden-state reset is supplied.

This is the common-stationary-weight special case of Astra N1251's acquired
return-word interface. Its more general weighted-reversible experiments can
have exponentially growing observation weights. If a scalar report is bounded
by R rather than one, its sufficient shot count scales as R^2. That cost cannot
be deleted. Different invariant weights do not automatically give a common
contractive Hilbert norm for the deployment theorem.

Stationary preparation, detailed balance, endpoint-read validity, source
stability, and the availability of the actual chronological controls are
premises. A finite visible fit does not establish them. If they are only
approximate, a separately warranted bound on their systematic moment bias must
be included in tau below. Repetition reduces stochastic error, not unknown bias.

4. The stable representation: bounded signed combinations
========================================================

Given a retained dictionary V=(v_1,...,v_N), put

    K=aconv{v_1,...,v_N}
     ={Vc: ||c||_1<=1}.

For a successor y=T_a v_i, seek c in the l1 unit ball minimizing

    R(c)=||Vc-y||^2
        =g_yy-2 k^T c+c^T G c,                          (2)

where G_(ij)=<v_i,v_j> and k_i=<v_i,y>. All entries are acquired using actual
word-pair experiments; y itself is another actual word feature.

Suppose every scalar entry used here is estimated within tau. For ALL feasible
c, including a c chosen from those estimates,

    |Rhat(c)-R(c)|
       <= tau +2tau||c||_1+tau||c||_1^2
       <=4tau.                                         (3)

This is the load-bearing statistical advantage of the coefficient constraint.
The bound contains neither N nor an inverse smallest Gram eigenvalue. It does
not assert that the unconstrained best linear representation has small l1 norm.

Absolute convex hulls and invariant-polytope expansion are established prior
machinery, as is kernel conditional-gradient optimization. We use them here
with acquired noisy entries and an explicit bounded-coefficient error ledger.

5. Constructing the coefficients without a decomposition oracle
==============================================================

The true gradient of (2) is 2(Gc-k). Estimated entries give a coordinatewise
gradient error at most 4tau whenever ||c||_1<=1. Minimize the estimated linear
objective over signed atoms s in {+/-e_1,...,+/-e_N}. This is just a scan of the
gradient coordinates; no continuous search oracle is needed. Its excess true
linear objective is at most 8tau.

Run Frank-Wolfe with gamma_t=2/(t+2). Let R_* be the true minimum. The image of
the feasible set has diameter at most two. Therefore

    h_(t+1) <= (1-gamma_t)h_t+8tau gamma_t+4gamma_t^2,
    h_t=R(c_t)-R_*.

Induction gives

    h_T <=16/(T+2)+8tau.                                (4)

Use

    tau=rho^2/256,   T=ceil(256/rho^2).

Then h_T<=3rho^2/32. Accept a coefficient vector when

    Rhat(c)<=3rho^2/4.

By (3), accepted columns have true residual squared at most 49rho^2/64, which
is smaller than rho^2. If no acceptance occurs by the T-step cap, then

    R_* >41rho^2/64 >rho^2/2.                            (5)

In that case append the actual successor y to the dictionary. Its distance
from the old absolute hull, and hence from every old center, is >rho/sqrt(2).
There is no rejection of the whole source: this is paid representation growth.

Optional early rejection uses the computed Frank-Wolfe gap gap_hat. The true
minimum is at least Rhat(c)-gap_hat-12tau, so that lower bound exceeding
rho^2/2 also justifies growth. This accelerates the executable without changing
the maximum-iteration guarantee.

Before the convex solve, run an atomic guard: compute the noisy squared distance
to every retained v_j (optionally also -v_j), and accept the corresponding signed
unit column if its estimate is <=3rho^2/4. This is redundant on the all-good
event but essential for the unconditional work guarantee in section 8.

Literal word labels and unordered literal word-pair estimates are cached.
Already retained literal descriptors are deduplicated. Every unprocessed
(center,action) pair is processed. An accepted old column remains valid when
new centers are appended: pad it with zeros. A new center gets all operations.

6. Rational coefficients, arithmetic, and numerical execution
============================================================

There is no denominator-product explosion in the fixed FW schedule. After T
updates,

    c_T=z_T/D_T,
    z_T=sum_(t=0)^(T-1) (t+1)s_t,
    D_T=T(T+1)/2.

z_T is an integer vector and ||z_T||_1<=D_T. Each coefficient needs O(log T)
bits in addition to an index. Maintain Ghat z_T; each update adds one signed
multiple of a Gram column. A FW step therefore costs O(N) arithmetic after the
Gram entries are cached. All training FW work is O(m N^2 rho^-2), plus ordinary
cache construction and scalar estimation. This is a concrete algorithm, not
an assumed convex optimizer. For rigorous bit implementation, use rational
sample means and comparisons or outward-rounded intervals.

Deployment multiplies learned sparse signed matrices by coefficient vectors.
It costs at most O(L N min(N,T)) arithmetic per explicit word, or O(LN^2) in
the dense bound. A discrete atomic table can instead use O(L) lookups. Thus
the improved convex representation changes the execution bill and is not free.
Exact rational execution may have bit lengths O(L log D_T) plus stored-data
lengths. That growth must be charged.

A fixed-precision alternative preserves the stability proof. Round each stored
matrix entry toward zero on a dyadic grid, so its column l1 norm stays <=1;
round each propagated coordinate toward zero too. Each of these steps adds at
most N*2^-b of l1 error. An extra budget eta_num is ensured by, for example,

    2 L N 2^-b +2^-b <=eta_num.

Readout rounding is included in the last term. Fixed precision therefore needs
b=O(log(LN/eta_num)) bits after the rational coefficient construction. No
physical source calls occur during deployment. The full error bound is
Lrho+beta+eta_num, with any source-model bias separately paid in acquisition.

The delivered numerical diagnostic uses floating-point matrix arithmetic with
large comparison margins, and audits actual residuals. It is not a certified
outward-rounded implementation of every possible nearly tied instance.

7. Why the learned model remains useful through arbitrary words
==============================================================

Let c_0=e_start and c_(t+1)=M_(a_(t+1))c_t. Column l1 norm <=1 implies
||c_t||_1<=1 at every step. Define e_t=T_(a_t)...T_(a_1)f-Vc_t. Then

    e_(t+1)=T_(a_(t+1))e_t
            +(T_(a_(t+1))V-VM_(a_(t+1)))c_t.

The source maps are contractions and every residual column has norm <=rho.
Thus ||e_(t+1)||<=||e_t||+rho, giving ||e_L||<=Lrho. For ||g_j||<=1,
the propagated feature error contributes at most Lrho. Readout entry errors
contribute at most beta||c_L||_1<=beta. This proves (1).

For a requested maximum program length H and total statistical approximation
epsilon, one possible choice is rho=epsilon/(2H), beta=epsilon/2. Numerical
rounding needs its own reserved share if nonzero. This certifies every word
within H, even though there are exponentially many such words.

If one selects an approximately maximizing open-loop word using this model,
uniform error epsilon gives regret at most 2epsilon plus optimization slack.
But finding that word can still require exponential planning computation. The
theorem does not give a free planner, feedback controller, complete generative
model, or normalized rare-event conditioning.

8. Termination and the complete acquisition bill
================================================

Let P(rho) be a finite upper bound on the number of reachable unit vectors whose
pairwise distances exceed rho/sqrt(2). P is not an input to the algorithm. On
the simultaneous good estimation event, each added center is separated from
all earlier centers, so with one seed N<=P(rho). A finite number s of initially
retained seeds gives the safe bound N<=s+P(rho).

If the reachable span is contained in a real r-dimensional space, a volume
packing bound gives

    P(rho) <= (1+2sqrt(2)/rho)^r.                         (6)

More generally, total boundedness of the reachable set at the requested scale
is enough. Infinite microscopic dimension does not by itself prevent a finite
compiler. Conversely, neither finite linear rank nor a small final basis
guarantees a small P or a small convex dictionary.

Assign the kth previously unseen scalar Gram estimate failure allowance

    delta_k=delta_G/[k(k+1)].

A sufficient number of independent/conditionally unbiased bounded episodes is

    n_k=ceil[2 tau^-2 log(2/delta_k)].                    (7)

These allowances sum to delta_G even with adaptively chosen words and unknown
final K. The number of distinct Gram jobs is O(mN^2), including failed convex
fits and the atomic guard. Readout acquisition costs qN more scalar jobs at
tolerance beta, with a separate summable confidence budget delta_R. Total
correctness confidence is at least 1-delta_G-delta_R.

An added center extends an earlier retained word by one action. With one empty
seed its word length is <=N-1; a candidate has length <=N. Thus a Gram episode
uses at most 2N primitive control calls. In the unit-cost control model:

    Gram episodes = O(mN^2 rho^-4 log((mN+1)/delta_G)),
    Gram controls = O(mN^3 rho^-4 log((mN+1)/delta_G)).    (8)

There is also one stationary preparation and two endpoint measurements per
episode. If preparation costs c_p primitive units, add c_p times the episode
count. Variable control durations, energy, and readout times are paid using the
actual word costs, rather than interpreting (8) as a physical wall-clock bound.
Readouts cost O(qN beta^-2 log((qN+1)/delta_R)) episodes; multiply by their actual
word/preparation costs, including any long descriptor of g_j.

The polynomial in N is explicit, but (6) is exponential in r. After substituting
rho=epsilon/(2H), the worst-case dimension dependence remains severe. No
polynomial-in-(r,H,1/epsilon) acquisition theorem is established.

Exceptional events and expected work
-----------------------------------

A mere high-probability termination claim would leave an unconditional expected
cost gap. The atomic guard repairs this. Let B be the number of bad cached Gram
estimates. Any two too-close distinct retained centers must involve a bad norm
estimate or their bad cross estimate; otherwise the later one would have passed
the atomic guard. Remove a center for each bad norm and both endpoints for each
bad cross entry. At most 2B centers are removed, and the rest are separated.
Therefore on every finite run prefix,

    N <= s+P(rho)+2B.                                   (9)

Fresh conditional failure bounds imply

    E exp(tB) <= exp(delta_G (exp(t)-1)),   t>=0.

One proves this by conditioning one query block at a time and then taking the
limit. Hence B is finite almost surely and has bounded moments of every fixed
order. Every inner optimization has a deterministic iteration cap. The whole
compiler terminates almost surely when P is finite, and has polynomial expected
work in s+P, the inverse tolerances, m, q, and confidence logarithms.

This proof depends on the literal-pair cache and atomic guard. A bad old Gram
entry could otherwise corrupt every subsequent convex solve; simply transplanting
the metric-cover expected-cost argument to an unguarded optimizer would be wrong.

9. Cumulative reuse, extension, and exact atomic cases
====================================================

New operations: retain the old features, moment estimates, readouts, and columns.
Process the new operation on existing centers, then all operations on any new
centers. Old columns pad with zeros and keep their already established bounds.
The same summable confidence ledger covers future acquisition. The cost depends
on actual newly processed pairs and newly required moments, not on restarting
all earlier fitting. Finite total cost still requires finite relevant packing.

New readouts: calibrate their values on the retained centers, with their supplied
norm and actual measurement/preparation costs. An unmeasured arbitrary g is not
free. New source kernels, lost stationarity, or source drift are not covered by
old confidence events.

Finer tolerances or longer horizons: refine moment precision and reconsider
columns at the new tolerance. Rank need not increase, but the precision bill
can increase. No claim is made that the particular refinement schedule is
instance-optimal. Preserve the old certified version separately.

Exact atomic corollary: suppose, as an additional WARRANTED global promise,
distinct reachable feature vectors have distance at least gamma>rho. Run the
atomic nearest-feature compiler. Every good merge is then an actual equality,
and all transitions are exact. Readout error is beta at every finite horizon,
without Lrho. If a separated finite readout alphabet is also promised, sufficiently
accurate readout calibration can decode exact values.

For signed-atom reuse, use the analogous promised separation modulo sign and
store the exact sign. This is the branch adopted in the finite signed-orbit
fixture; it is the same conventional retaining frame algorithm in that family.

CAUTION: a global gap between individual orbit points does NOT imply that a
nearby arbitrary convex combination is exactly equal to the successor. The gap
corollary is restricted to atomic matching. The generic convex compiler remains
approximate unless an additional exact-relation justification is supplied.

10. Structural limitation: stable polytope complexity is not rank
================================================================

Consider two reflections on the circle with uniform measure, applied to the
span of sine and cosine. They are self-adjoint, positive Markov operators and
L2 isometries. Their product is a planar rotation. For irrational rotation
angle divided by pi, a rank-two Euclidean-isometric realization is exact.

If a finite dictionary V spanning this plane admitted exact intertwining

    T_a V=V M_a,   ||M_a||_(1->1)<=1,

then its finite polygon K=aconv(V) would obey T_a K subset K for both reflections.
They preserve area; each inclusion is equality. K is therefore invariant under
the irrational rotation and its dense powers, so K must be a disk. A finite
full-dimensional polygon cannot be a disk. Contradiction.

Thus this compiler's exact stable geometry can be unavailable even at rank two.
This does not refute the finite-rho theorem, ordinary low-rank realization, or
the possibility of acquiring a better stability norm. It identifies the next
hard problem: discover economical coordinates AND an appropriate stability
geometry from the allowed observations, paying their precision and computation.

11. Executions, strong alternatives, and preserved failures
==========================================================

Finite noisy fixture (simulated, not hardware)
--------------------------------------------

The hidden system has 12 independent bits (4096 microscopic states), one observed
bit, and three unknown reversible signed-coordinate controls. Both learners get
the same additional unit signed-orthonormal-orbit promise and the same bounded
return-moment source. Their confidence allowance is 0.01 each, scalar tolerance
0.2, and all preparations/endpoint reads/control lengths are counted.

The first metric table retains 24 states, uses 878 scalar estimates, 741073
simulated episodes, and 6048373 primitive control steps. An ordinary retaining
signed-frame learner retains 12 directions, uses 224 estimates, 158896 episodes,
and 1139078 primitive steps. Both have zero full-transition-table audit errors
and zero disagreements on 1400 later programs, including length 1000. Neither
uses new source calls in those later predictions. The ordinary learner wins.

The binomial batching samples the EXACT aggregate law of the episode endpoint
products. It does not physically replay millions of control steps on a device;
those steps are charged in the source ledger. CPU simulation time is not a
physical speed measurement. Hidden truth is used only by the simulator and
post-acquisition audits, not by either learner's decisions.

The signed-frame branch is adopted for that promise. The general convex theorem
explains bounded signed reuse beyond literal equality; the fixture does not
establish that the general noisy Frank-Wolfe compiler is cheaper.

Continuous-geometry exact diagnostics
------------------------------------

For a fixed planar rotation and its inverse, exact-moment diagnostics compare
the initial metric table with the bounded-combination compiler. At rho=0.0375,
the metric table has 81 centers, 6725 Gram queries, and a 279047 primitive-word
bill. Bounded combinations use 10 atoms, 152 Gram queries, a 934 word-step bill,
and 169 FW iterations. All learned columns have l1 norm <=1; the largest true
local residual is about 0.0316606, below rho.

This storage/acquisition improvement does NOT imply pointwise dominance: signed
convex approximations can damp correlations. The continuous diagnostic's largest
observed prediction error is about 0.333722 across its tested words; all tested
errors satisfy the theorem's length-dependent bound. The first metric table is
more accurate on some words. These are exact-moment numerical diagnostics, not
a fully executed finite-noise calibration at rho=0.0375.

The stronger exact rational counterfixture, in metric_cover_counterfixture.py,
uses two self-adjoint Markov reflections. An ordinary Gram-closure learner
discovers rank two from 12 short moments and 26 primitive word steps; it is not
handed the basis. Its basis condition number is rho^-2, so its exact-data win
does not prove a free noisy extrapolation win. Source-access and numerical
contracts remain separate. Its full results and bit lengths are preserved.

Incremental intervention diagnostic
----------------------------------

A separate four-hidden-state system has uniform stationarity and symmetric
stochastic controls. The first two controls fix the seed, so their whole known
operation orbit has one retained atom. A third actual control activates missing
directions. Reusing the old cache and coefficient columns at rho=0.05, the
compiler expands to 25 atoms, acquires 1319 additional scalar moments, and pays
11281 additional primitive word steps and 1169 FW iterations. The largest local
residual is about 0.0432514. The original columns and moments are preserved.

This is an exact-moment numerical test of incremental closure, including genuinely
noncommuting controls. It is not an efficiency win over a small full model: the
physical system has only four hidden states. It shows that the operation supplying
the missing information was actually queried rather than inferred from unchanged
baseline data. No general finite-noise acquisition was executed for this fixture.

Preserved failed routes
----------------------

* Static novelty rank and exact operator-law acquisition were already Astra
  capital, not new results here.
* Naive Gram-Schmidt/BFS can require tiny moment precision. No general
  polynomial-in-rank finite-noise discovery proof was completed.
* The membership-only hidden-password lower bound does not transfer unchanged
  to Gram access: norm experiments can expose correct prefixes. It was rejected.
* A rank count is not a precision certificate. Longer tasks may demand more
  accurate calibration in exactly the same span.
* A small residual is not an unlimited-horizon equality. The executable's
  0.01-radian rotation merges into one state at rho=0.02 but predicts 1 instead
  of approximately 0.000796327 at length 157. The Lrho bound remains valid.
* Squared norms alone do not provide cross moments. Vectors e1,e2,-e1 all have
  unit norm, but squared distances from e1 are 0,2,4.
* The atomic orbit-gap premise cannot certify arbitrary convex relations.
* An unguarded noisy convex solver can have an expected-runtime hole on its
  exceptional event. The atomic guard is part of the proved algorithm.
* A finite l1-stable model can be much larger than the best ordinary linear
  model, and may fail to exist exactly even when rank is two.

12. Prior credit and evidence status
===================================

Established methods used constructively include invariant-polytope expansion
(Guglielmi--Protasov), implicit-kernel conditional gradient (Bach--Lacoste-Julien--
Obozinski), symbolic abstraction/approximate bisimulation (Pola--Girard--Tabuada
and later data-driven work), observable-operator/spectral realization, and
ordinary retaining control/model learning. Astra N1251 supplies the relevant
physical moment-acquisition precursor at its actual assumptions and costs.
SOURCES.txt gives exact links and the boundaries checked.

The proof was reconstructed by the root and a collaborating mathematical agent
with disclosed shared construction. Another agent produced the strong spectral
counterfixture and prior-art comparison. These are internal mathematical reviews,
not blind external review or proof-assistant verification. Programs were run in
Python 3.12.14 with numpy 2.3.5. Finite numerical checks establish only their
tested claims. The primary generic theorem is analytical.

Historical novelty is unresolved. Practical advantage over the best retaining
learner is unestablished. The completed work narrows a concrete foundational
opening: acquiring a stability-compatible representation from real experiments
rather than assuming that small rank supplies one. A general efficient solution
to that problem remains open in this investigation.
