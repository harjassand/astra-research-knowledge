PRIMARY EXTERNAL RESEARCH

1. Takanori Maehara and Kazuo Murota, A Numerical Algorithm for Block-Diagonal
   Decomposition of Matrix *-Algebras with General Irreducible Components,
   METR2008-26.
   https://www.keisu.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/data/2008/METR08-26.pdf
   Existing decomposition from generators without a supplied algebra structure.

2. Yuanzhao Zhang and Adilson E. Motter, Symmetry-independent stability analysis
   of synchronization patterns, arXiv:2003.05461.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.05461
   Existing automatic simultaneous block decomposition is a strong comparator.

3. Etienne de Klerk, Dmitrii V. Pasechnik and Alexander Schrijver, Reduction of
   symmetric semidefinite programs using the regular *-representation.
   https://homepages.cwi.nl/~lex/files/symm.pdf
   Existing faithful regular representation and semidefinite reduction.

4. Raphael A. Meyer, Cameron Musco, Christopher Musco and David P. Woodruff,
   Hutch++: Optimal Stochastic Trace Estimation, arXiv:2010.09649.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.09649
   Inverse-epsilon single-PSD-trace query complexity is already established.

5. Optimal Sketching for Trace Estimation, NeurIPS2021.
   https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/hash/c77bfda61a0204d445185053e6a9a8fe-Abstract.html
   Included in the strong trace-estimation comparison.

6. Tyler Chen, Thomas Trogdon and Shashanka Ubaru, Randomized matrix-free
   quadrature: unified and uniform bounds for stochastic Lanczos quadrature
   and the kernel polynomial method, arXiv:2204.01941v3.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.01941
   Uniform quadrature guarantees already address families of matrix functions.

7. Fabio Matti, Haoze He, Daniel Kressner and Hei Yin Lam, Stochastic trace
   estimation for parameter-dependent matrices applied to spectral density
   approximation, arXiv:2502.18626v2; SIAM J. Sci. Comput.48 (2026), A2287-A2313.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.18626
   Fixed probes and Chebyshev interpolation already reuse work across parameters.

8. Arvind K. Saibaba and Ilse C. F. Ipsen, Stochastic Trace Optimization of
   Parameter Dependent Matrices Based on Statistical Learning Theory,
   arXiv:2508.05764.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05764
   Uniform trace optimization via covering and chaining bounds is prior work.

9. William Swartworth and David P. Woodruff, Optimal Eigenvalue Approximation via
   Sketching, arXiv:2304.09281v1, Appendix A, especially Theorem23.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.09281
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.09281
   The random-projector rank-distinguishing lower bound supplies the inherited
   Omega(1/epsilon) accuracy lower bound in MAIN_PROOF section9.

10. Mark Rudelson and Roman Vershynin, Hanson-Wright inequality and sub-gaussian
    concentration, arXiv:1306.2872.
    https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.2872
    Used for bounded-grid quadratic-form concentration in the finite-bit corollary.

11. Shared MeatAxe, ZSP invariant-subspace spin-up documentation.
    https://users.fmi.uni-jena.de/~king/SharedMeatAxe/prog_zsp.html
    Conventional invariant-subspace generation, inspected by a collaborator.
