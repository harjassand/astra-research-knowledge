PRESERVED FAILURES AND CORRECTIONS

1. Numerical closure leakage. On identical target and eight probes, sequential
   residual normalization expanded to all528 coordinates and1056 matvecs.
   Batched frontier SVD recovered dimension208 with416 calls. The respective
   residuals were1.37e-15 and2.30e-12: the smaller residual concealed worse
   compression. The repaired fixture is not a general stability theorem.

2. Preliminary trace fixture. The three-component experiment left only one
   unresolved multiplicity after the two small blocks saturated. Known N then
   determines it exactly. That avoidable stochastic error is documented and
   the nontrivial four-component experiment replaces it as the main evidence.

3. Gamma constant. A preliminary two-sided exponent with denominator3 was too
   optimistic near epsilon1. The final complex theorem uses denominator4;
   the real Gaussian fixture uses denominator8.

4. Confidence inversion. If threshold>=observed energy, the Gamma shape interval
   has lower endpoint0; it does not have two positive roots. The proof and code
   handle this case. Known structural lower bounds are then imposed.

5. Exactness model. General source roots are real, not necessarily algebraic
   over Q. The ideal theorem explicitly charges exact-root operations. The
   finite-bit result is separately restricted to exact rational sources and
   finite-grid randomness, with algebraic-field and bit costs retained.

6. Equality is unresolved. s=q r only establishes m>=q r. It cannot be treated
   as a recovered multiplicity. Full ambient dimension and known total N can
   supply additional exact deductions and are not discarded.

7. Changing sources. An operator chosen after seeing probes can vanish on their
   acquired cyclic space. Fixed-algebra trace confidence cannot be carried to
   arbitrary adaptive new generators. Fresh challenges and trace data are paid.
   Cumulative *-algebra admission assumes Hermitian generators or separately
   available, admitted and charged adjoint actions.

8. Program implementation. An early cumulative prototype cached word evaluations
   using id(temporary_word); Python identity reuse could corrupt that cache.
   The executed version retains Word objects as keys. The abandoned SymPy
   dependency was replaced with standard-library Fraction arithmetic.

9. Planning shortcut. The included four-state rational construction has entries
   at least3/16 and a common uniform stationary distribution, yet exact stagewise
   open-loop planning encodes PARTITION. Its terminal reward is
     3/4-(1/8)*alpha^n*(sum sigma_i a_i)^2.
   Eighty exact sequence checks pass. The hard gap is exponentially small;
   this is not a fixed-accuracy hardness result and does not concern ordinary
   observed-state feedback MDP planning. It blocks an unjustified inference
   from small predictive representation to universal cheap planning.

10. Historical and practical significance. Established methods supply major
    ingredients and a retaining conventional algebra learner can implement this
    mechanism. The proved combined contract is preserved without a priority or
    overall speedup claim. Approximate-structure acquisition, stable finite
    precision, and a strong practical comparator remain substantive open work.
