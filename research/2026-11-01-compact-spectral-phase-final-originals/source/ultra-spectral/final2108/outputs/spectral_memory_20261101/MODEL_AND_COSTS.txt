Physical architecture
=====================
For n data spins, the degree-18 expander has 9n data-data Ising couplers.
There are 9n auxiliary spins, 9n data-auxiliary couplers, 9n one-body
auxiliary fields and 9n one-body auxiliary loss channels. Total: 10n qubits,
18n pair couplers, maximum coupling degree 27 on data and one on auxiliaries.
Auxiliary frequencies take nine nominal values, 4J,...,36J. No physical
syndrome projectors, high-arity jumps or perturbative synthesis tree occur.
The operator-valued spectral resolvent and two-layer unitary frame are
analytical devices; they do not require hardware tables or gates.

The code stores ONE logical qubit. Its ordinary quantum distance is one.
It has large effective distance only relative to the strong algebra in
SPECTRAL_PHASE.txt. It has no positive asymptotic quantum rate. Encoding
and the final minimum-weight repetition decoder are ideal mathematical
interfaces here. Syndrome extraction, reliable quantum decoding, control
electronics, readout noise, and their wall-clock costs have not been built.
For repetition syndromes, noiseless classical reconstruction/majority can
be done in O(n) work on a connected graph; this is not a noisy decoder circuit.

The supplied finite layout is the bipartite double cover of an LPS p=17,
q=13 graph: 2,184 data vertices, 19,656 auxiliaries, 21,840 qubits total,
and 39,312 pair couplers. Exact finite arithmetic checks simplicity,
degree and connectivity. The base's computed nontrivial adjacency maximum
is 7.850855075327148. The FAMILY spectral premise comes from the LPS
theorem; the floating eigenvalue is a diagnostic, not an interval proof.
This member is not claimed to exceed the very coarse proof's minimum
block size. No dynamics of this 21,840-qubit system was simulated.

Rates, tolerances, and acquisition
=================================
The proof selects finite constants J>>Gamma>>g, with kappa=4g^2/Gamma,
independently of block size and horizon. It supplies a nonzero existential
threshold for delta/kappa; it supplies no practical threshold or engineering
parameter set for a large block. All constants from finite local cones
can be bounded by gate norms and commutator/Taylor estimates; no global
diagonalization is required to establish their finiteness. Those coarse
constants may be extremely unfavorable.

The static structural calibration window is part of the physical premise:
 sum_{j~i}|J_ij-J|<=Gamma/8,
 |omega_(i,l)-4Jl|<=Gamma/4,
 Gamma/2<=gamma_a<=2Gamma,
 g^2/2<=|g_a|^2<=3g^2/2.
Uniform per-bond error <=Gamma/144 is sufficient for the first condition.
Its relative precision tightens as J/Gamma increases. Auxiliary frequency
tolerances are linewidth-scale absolute tolerances. The order-one range
of individual coupling/linewidth variations is already included in the
derived rate bounds; arbitrary remaining faults are not free.

There are no nominal one-data-qubit fields in this model. Uncompensated
fields, parasitic interactions, spurious drive axes, and loss channels must
fit either the stated structural window or the perturbation budgets. A
large laboratory qubit splitting cannot silently be dropped. A rotating-
frame or driven implementation requires its own pumps, phase/frequency
stability and microscopic open-system derivation, none supplied here.

After Hadamards on data the model has data XX ferromagnet couplers and
data-Z/auxiliary-flip couplers. Dominant physical Z dephasing then lies in
the admitted algebra, including coherent Z_i B_i with a shared finite B.
Data relaxation and transverse single-data-axis faults break it. An exact
parity selection rule would be useful if a physical device actually has it.
The model does not acquire that rule or the residual fault bias by declaring
them. All odd-data-axis coupler faults belong in the symmetry-breaking
budget eta, even if the nominal coupler is strongly symmetric.

Entropy disposal and activity
=============================
There is no scheduled reset operation, but the one-body losses continuously
use an entropy sink. A finite persistent B does not provide an unlimited
fresh sink; B models additional correlated noise, while the nominal loss
channels remain supplied Markov resources.

The auxiliary occupation obeys, for the actual modeled state,
 d<N>/dt <=-(1/2)sum_a gamma_a<n_a>
            +2sum_a |g_a|^2/gamma_a+q delta n.
Here 2|g_a|sqrt(<n_a>) <=(gamma_a/2)<n_a>+2|g_a|^2/gamma_a,
and each individually TP q-local perturbation changes N by at most
q||V_a||_diamond. Hence the nominal loss-event functional obeys

 sum_a gamma_a integral_0^T <n_a(t)>dt
   <=18n+(27kappa+2q delta)nT,
 and also <=18Gamma nT.                                         (R1)

With an additional local symmetry-breaking budget eta, replace delta by
delta+eta in this activity estimate. For additive physical GKSL faults
with the nominal channels retained, (R1) is an expected nominal event count.
For signed calibration differences it is a nominal channel functional;
identification with actual detected emissions needs a specified channel
decomposition. The actual calibrated gamma_a are used throughout.

The largest bare auxiliary gap is <=36J+Gamma/4. Using n_a of norm one
for each auxiliary field, the sum of nominal Hamiltonian term norms is
bounded by [189J+37Gamma/16+9sqrt(3/2)g]n in the window (5), and equals
(189J+9g)n for uniform real couplings. Integrated Hamiltonian resources
thus scale as O(JnT), while correction occurs at kappa<<J. These quantities
are not electric power or heat per emission. No microscopic reservoir,
pump work, refrigerator coefficient of performance, finite reservoir
bandwidth or all-time GKSL validity is derived. In particular, this is not
a claim that a closed two-body Hamiltonian is a self-correcting quantum
memory in an ordinary thermal environment.

Thermal auxiliary gains are strong-symmetry-preserving but must fit delta.
If an additionally supplied thermal model has gain gamma_a exp(-omega_a/kBT),
a sufficient budget condition is
 36 Gamma exp[-(4J-Gamma/4)/(kBT)]<=delta_thermal,
because ||D[sigma^+]||_diamond<=2 and there are nine auxiliaries per data.
No thermal law or zero-temperature reservoir is assumed acquired by (R1).

Graph locality and rate trade
=============================
The expander has long connections at fixed density in two or three ordinary
spatial dimensions. Wiring length, propagation delay, crosstalk and control
delivery are not O(1) merely because the coupling graph has fixed degree.
No 2D/3D geometric realization with fixed local energy/rate cost is proved.

At exact strong symmetry, the candidate logical bound is
 C_phys n(1+T) exp(-b n/2), for 10n physical qubits and one logical qubit.
Nonzero symmetry breaking adds eta nT. For a target error e at time T,
both C_phys n(1+T)exp(-b n/2)<=e/2 and eta nT<=e/2 must hold.
Thus increasing n without improving eta eventually loses the quantum
benefit. A larger inner encoding/energy could help only if it suppresses
the WHOLE-device eta, including controller/coupler faults. No bosonic-cat
or other inner-device guarantee is imported here.

Strong published comparators and primary sources
================================================
Kapit--Chalker--Simon, Passive correction of quantum logical errors in a
driven, dissipative system (2014), section II:
 https://arxiv.org/html/1408.0959
The ferromagnetic three-spin example already uses resonant, rapidly lossy
shadow qubits and has unwanted off-resonant processes. Resonant cooling
and this small model are prior mechanisms. This work's candidate addition
is the finite-controller expander-phase argument with the specified quantum
noise/correlation interface, not the discovery of shadow-qubit correction.

Lieu--Liu--Gorshkov, Candidate for a passively protected quantum memory in
two dimensions (2023 preprint version; published 2024), Ising and photonic
sections:
 https://arxiv.org/html/2205.09767
The strongly symmetric Ising quantum-memory mechanism and its failure
under symmetry-breaking phase errors are established context. Their
photonic construction addresses bias using cat physics. A full physical
cat/Ising device robustness certificate is not acquired from that proposal.
The present expander model gives up ordinary geometric locality.

Lubotzky--Phillips--Sarnak, Ramanujan graphs (1988), section 2 and theorem 4.1:
 https://courses.grainger.illinois.edu/cs598sgt/sp2015/LPS.pdf
The Cayley construction and Ramanujan family bound are imported. The
double cover, cut-density estimates, auxiliary layout and finite checks
are derived here. The q=13 fixture uses the quadratic-residue PSL case.

Autonomous Quantum Memories from Classical Stochastic Processes (2026),
definition 2.22 and the autocorrelation-to-memory interface:
 https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05589v1
The comparison class has stabilizer-algebra Hamiltonians and single-Pauli
syndrome-rate jumps with additional sector-rate restrictions. The full
coherent data/auxiliary Hamiltonian here is not silently identified with
that exact class; only the diagonal ground moment has rates (8).

Dunnweber--Styliaris--Trivedi, Quantum Memory and Autonomous Computation
in Two Dimensions (2026), construction and discussion:
 https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20818v1
This is a strong genuinely autonomous 2D baseline with a hierarchical
local-qudit rule. Its continuous perturbation theorem covers local unitary
jump-operator perturbations; the discussion leaves general nonunitary
Lindblad perturbations open. Its clocks/registers have actual costs; they
cannot be dismissed as perfect external control. It protects a different
noise class in a different geometry, so neither result dominates the other.

Bharti--Haug--Khu--Tanggara, Single-Shot Error Correction at Optimal
Spacetime Cost (2026), sections 2.2, 3.1, 3.5, 3.6:
 https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03684v1
Strong scheduled Tanner recovery has constant-depth rounds and O(n)
locations, live qubits and classical work, with decoder waits charged.
Its local-stochastic fault-set premise differs from arbitrary small coherent
faults at every location. Coherent operations/shared environment within
a fixed fault path do not remove that distinction. The earlier Tanner
comparison retains these scopes. The compact one-qubit strong-algebra
memory supplies no generic cost or rate advantage over this baseline.

Paletta--Leverrier--Mirrahimi--Vuillot, High-performance local decoders for
defect matching in 1D, abstract of v3 (2025):
 https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10162v3
Small classical local rules and biased-cat repetition memories are a
relevant compact comparison: code-capacity behavior is proved and noisy-
measurement behavior studied numerically. No controller quantum theorem
is imported from an abstract. This is a priority lead, not a dominance claim.
