ROOT ACTIVE CONSTRUCTION: from contact permission to whole-product load
29 October 2026. Internally derived; independent review not yet performed.

Question. Can a local southwest two-contact growth operation also compile
a mechanically scalable structure, rather than granting stability for free?

Strong incumbent: Jilek et al., Self-Stabilizing Self-Assembly (2022),
https://arxiv.org/html/2202.12534 , Sections III--VII. Opposite tile drives
and checkerboard assemblies are PRIOR. Its hundreds-of-tiles result is a
Box2D simulation, not a physical experiment. It explicitly replaces actual
bristle dynamics with applied forces and torques; a future-work paragraph
reports unreliable preliminary mechanical bristle drives. This construction
is a conditional analysis of the missing load interface, not a new physical
actuator or a claim of priority over that research.

1. Net force cancellation alone is insufficient.
Two connected components with opposite total loads +nF and -nF have zero
whole-object load. Any single joint separating them still transmits nF.
More generally, static equilibrium requires that every subset's total load
be carried by its boundary joints and its external supports. This is just
force balance, not a new theorem or an elastic stability criterion.

2. Stronger growth invariant.
Let tiles occupy D subset positive integer Z^2. Start with rigid seed nodes
on both coordinate axes, and permit a tile (i,j) only when (i-1,j) AND
(i,j-1) exist. Every prefix is a downset: each row is a prefix 1..L_j and
each column a prefix 1..M_i. This follows by induction on additions.
The input gate must really enforce these designated contacts; arbitrary
two-contact permission, detachments, holes and component fusion do not
automatically preserve this invariant.

3. Explicit statically admissible central stresses.
Assume ideal point centers, zero applied torque, quasistatic body force
 f_(i,j) = (-1)^(i+j) (Fx,Fy).
For horizontal edge ((i-1,j),(i,j)) set signed tension
 Tx(i,j) = Fx sum_(a=i..L_j) (-1)^(a+j),
and for vertical edge ((i,j-1),(i,j))
 Ty(i,j) = Fy sum_(b=j..M_i) (-1)^(i+b).
At the free ends set Tx(L_j+1,j)=Ty(i,M_i+1)=0.
Then Tx(i,j)-Tx(i+1,j)=f_x(i,j), and the analogous identity
holds in y. Each body is in force and torque balance, with |Tx|<=|Fx|
and |Ty|<=|Fy|, independent of size and growth order. Seed edges are
included and must have those capacities. All forces act along bond axes.
Uniform drive has tail tension (L_j-i+1)Fx instead, so the cancellation
really removes that size factor at this statics-only interface.

4. This does NOT prove physical stability: a decisive compatibility debt.
Take an even L by L square, horizontal force only, and central Hookean
springs of stiffness k. Horizontal displacement is determined separately
on each row because vertical central springs have zero linear transverse
stiffness. With the left seed pinned,
 ux(L,j) = (-1)^j (L/2) Fx/k.
Adjacent row endpoints therefore differ by L Fx/k. Their vertical bonds
lose the prescribed near-registered geometry at large L even though all
the proposed horizontal forces stay <=Fx. Linear vertical central springs
are shear-floppy; nonlinear geometry, shear/angle joints or substrate
constraints cannot be granted without acquisition. A positive force-flow
certificate is not a compatible small-deformation elastic realization.

5. Exact counter-drive is also an unpaid promise.
For f=(sigma+delta)F on a rectangular L by L array (L even), the whole
load is delta F L^2. If the ONLY external supports are the 2L seed joints
of force capacity Fc, necessary equilibrium requires
 |delta| |F| L <= 2 Fc.
Any fixed coherent drive bias restores a finite maximum side length.
Floor friction or distributed supports can change this result, but then
their response and effect on free-tile encounters must be acquired. Random
zero-mean errors are a different model and do not erase the coherent case.

Decision. Retain the downset stress compiler as an exact conditional
interface; do not promote it to unlimited manufacture. The physical
operation that matters is local drive suppression/redistribution on
commitment, or an acquired shear-stable registered joint + drive model.
These may beat exact counter-drive calibration. Native commitment and
actual actuation remain separate active worker questions. No physical
experiment or practical superiority follows from the consumer below.
