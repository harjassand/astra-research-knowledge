FROM REGISTERED CONTACT CODES TO A FULL DIPOLE-PAIR MODEL
Root construction,29October2026. Internally derived; independent review
pending. No hardware, fixed moment stability, agitation law or assembly
operation is claimed. Magnetostatics is prior physics, not a new law.

MODEL. Two parallel faces carry fixed normal point dipoles s_i*m, with
s_i in{0,+1,-1}, at square-grid sites of pitch l. Absorb the facing-normal
sign into the code convention, so the intended mate has equal GLOBAL
moments. Set C=mu0/(4pi). The complete face-face interaction is

 E=C*m^2 sum_(i,j) s_i t_j K_h(x_i-y_j),
 K_h(r)=(|r|^2-2h^2)/(|r|^2+h^2)^(5/2).

Here h is plane separation, not a force threshold. This expression includes
EVERY dipole pair, not only pixels directly opposite each other. The model
assumes moments remain fixed despite their fields. Soft magnetic material
with unknown coercive/history response is not automatically this model.

REGISTERED FRINGING THEOREM. Let each face have w active pixels, and let
its relative in-plane pose be a square-grid rotation/reflection and an
integer-grid translation. At exact registration, the zero-lateral-distance
pairs contribute -S*c, where S=2C*w*m^2/h^3 and c is the signed overlap/w.
Assume h/l<=1/4. Every remaining lateral separation has length at least l,
and 0<=K_h(r)<=1/|r|^3. For a fixed receiving pixel, count square-lattice
points in max-norm shells: shell j has8j points of Euclidean distance>=j*l.
Therefore its total omitted absolute interaction is at most

 C*m^2*l^-3 *8 sum_(j>=1) j^-2 < (40/3)C*m^2/l^3.

The last bound is elementary rational arithmetic:
sum_(j>=1)j^-2 <= sum_(j=1)^5 j^-2 + integral_5^infty x^-2 dx
 =5989/3600 <5/3.
Summing over at most w receiving pixels proves the UNIFORM face score bound

             |(-E/S)-c| <= epsilon=(20/3)(h/l)^3.          (M1)

It holds for every finite aperture, signed/sparse pattern and registered
grid displacement. No statistical pixel cancellation is used in the fringe
bound. Registered transforms preserve the grid; a mirror is a mathematical
code transform, not automatically an available rigid 3D face orientation.

SMALL CONTINUOUS POSE TOLERANCE. Suppose deviation from such a registered
pose moves every source pixel by at most a*h in-plane, a<=1/4. For a face
radius R_face, it suffices that |translation|+R_face*|angle|<=a*h.
Then every nonzero lattice separation is at least (1-a*h/l) times its
original length. The fringing bound becomes

 epsilon=(20/3)(h/l)^3/(1-a*h/l)^3.                       (M2)

For a formerly coincident pair, the attractive kernel relative to its peak
is f(u)=(1-u^2/2)/(1+u^2)^(5/2), 0<=u<=a. Bernoulli's inequality gives
1-3a^2<=f(u)<=1. Intended-mate overlap therefore loses at most3a^2.

For a GENERAL SIGNED-correlation code, different pixels under a small angle
have different f(u_i); cancellation can be lost. The safe per-face score
error relative to ideal correlation is 3a^2+epsilon. A two-match/four-contact
cooperative score gap is then at least

                  1-5rho-24a^2-8epsilon.                 (M3)

If every wrong code pair instead has ABSOLUTE support overlap<=rho*w,
the wrong weighted overlap remains<=rho. Alternatively a uniform translation
gives every coincident pair the same f, preserving signed cancellation.
For either of these stronger contracts the better gap is

                  1-5rho-6a^2-8epsilon.                  (M4)

These follow by comparing one correct+three maximally attractive wrong
contacts with two correct+two maximally repulsive wrong contacts. All
matched glues have unit ideal strength. More than two matches only raises
the minimum when rho<1. No generic weighted tile-alphabet compiler is
claimed by this lemma.

For a=1/20,h/l=1/10,rho=1/6, BOTH rational lower gaps are positive. The
consumer magnetostatic_bridge.py calculates them exactly and independently
sums full pair energies on a source-generated8x8 Hadamard face at selected
poses. The finite energy sums are float diagnostics, not a real material
calibration or a proof by testing. The all-pattern inequalities above are
the proof. They do not claim that this source face supplies the required
rho or a universal assembly palette.

WHAT HAS BEEN CONSTRUCTED. A code satisfying the stated correlation or
support contract has a rigorously bounded contact-energy implementation
in this FULL fixed-dipole pair model, with an explicit continuous pose
tolerance. The angular tolerance decreases as h/R_face: increased face
resolution does not supply registration or a large capture basin for free.

WHAT REMAINS UNPAID. Four-face ADDITIVITY assumes other magnetic interactions
are absent or bounded separately. Cross-face fields, other/remote tiles,
moment/coercivity variation, finite mounting/material geometry, magnet
writing/reset, out-of-plane tilt, collisions/registration, energy barriers,
detachment statistics, spurious nucleation and whole-product error/cost are
not acquired. Balanced zero-net patterns may help remote-field decay but
that is not proved or silently supplied here. A scalar contact-score
threshold is NOT a native physical commitment mechanism. M1--M4 must not
be presented as autonomous cooperative growth or programmable manufacture.
