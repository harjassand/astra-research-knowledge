# A collective positive-Kraus oracle class, separate from local positive-block claims

28 October 2026. Short candidate observation, no novelty claim or independent receiving. This may be an existing Lüders/environment-assisted recovery fact. It is preserved as an alternative, not as a reason to divert the main acquisition work.

Let the logical input/output have arbitrary dimension d. Suppose a physical source has

    N(rho)=U[sum_a K_a rho K_a tensor sigma_a]Udagger,
    K_a>=0, sigma_a>=0,
    sum_a Tr(sigma_a) K_a²=I_d.

The sigma_a can be noncommuting emitted gauge states. The displayed condition makes the source trace preserving. The exact extraction D_star=Tr_G Ad_Udagger is a FULL collective CPTP entanglement-fidelity oracle for this particular class, with

    F_e(D_star N)=d^(-2) sum_a Tr(sigma_a)(Tr K_a)².

Proof: write sigma_a=sum_r |v_ar><v_ar| and let D_j be arbitrary recovery Kraus operators. Set R_jar=D_j U(I tensor |v_ar>). Then sum_j R_jar†R_jar=||v_ar||²I. Positivity of K_a gives the Hilbert–Schmidt Cauchy–Schwarz inequality

    |Tr(R_jar K_a)|²<=Tr(K_a) Tr(K_a R_jar†R_jar).

Sum over j,r,a and divide by d². The result is exactly the displayed fidelity, achieved by discarding the gauge after undoing U. No supplied readable flag is needed. This proves a genuine joint logical objective, unlike composing the one-qubit marginal oracles in the separate strict counterexample.

This is narrower in another direction: after gauge discard the native logical channel is unital because its Kraus operators are positive. Ordinary amplitude-damping jumps are not positive Hermitian Kraus operators, so the nonunital positive-operator qubit theorem is not a special case of this observation. An arbitrary channel does not acquire this representation by merely taking polar decompositions and discarding their differing unitary factors.

Unknown U acquisition, strict curvature, finite positive-Kraus fitting, useful scaling and hardware costs are NOT supplied by this note. A known U/noise representation is not an operational discovery mechanism. Complete continuing-memory outputs, joint repeated-use strategies and capacity claims remain outside this one-use reduced-channel statement. Historical priority should be checked before any novelty interpretation.
