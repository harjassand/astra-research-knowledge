## 4. Local support discovery: what survives and what does not

If a candidate output interval is disjoint from the support of B's full qubit algebra, its contrast partial trace is zero even when the direction varies by sector. Average conjugation by {I,B_X,B_Y,B_Z}; this annihilates every sum_a B_a A_aj while preserving the partial trace over the support. Thus a confidence-positive contrast still certifies overlap, and geometric expansion can cover B.

However, this does NOT prove the expanded interval retains a commuting coefficient algebra or a positive metric. That new obligation is exactly the envelope premise in §1. One may instead use an actual larger envelope containing the accessible flag/noise algebra and pay for it. Passing a positive Hhat guard alone does not prove common algebra membership. The guarantee remains conditional rather than falsely turning support discovery into noise-family certification.

## 5. Noncommuting spectators mark a real boundary

Consider C_X=X tensor A and C_Z=Z tensor B with positive, noncommuting A,B. Then

    H_XZ=−iY tensor [A,B]/2.

This generally contains the logical algebra itself and fails centrality. Therefore H=Acoef² and the cancellation proof no longer hold.
