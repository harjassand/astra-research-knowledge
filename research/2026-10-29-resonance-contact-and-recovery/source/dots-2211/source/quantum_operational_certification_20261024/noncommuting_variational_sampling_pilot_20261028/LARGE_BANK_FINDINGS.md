# One predeclared fresh bank passes a native-oracle guard

28 October 2026. Author result; independent large-bank audit pending. The earlier 12-bank study and its independently audited failures remain unchanged.

## Decision made before the data

LARGE_BANK_PREDECLARATION.json fixed one fresh bank of N=4,194,304 blocks, seed202610282052, the same physical source, beta=.001, at most16 declared patch/bank processes, L=14, and candidate block gap kappa=1/25. The target was an unnormalized accepted mean native-oracle regret at most .01. There was no adaptive increase in N or post hoc relaxation of kappa.

The received joint-Hilbert–Schmidt confidence theorem was checked BEFORE sampling. The exact check exp(14)>49*16/.001 covers the declared union. The simulation uses one logical XYZ input and two physical Pauli readouts per block: 4,194,304 new individually drawn blocks, 4,194,304 input preparations and 8,388,608 output readouts. This bank is independent of the old banks, not a counted-again prefix. Total acquired blocks in the combined pilot are now4,538,368.

## Simulator precision is not hidden in the statistics

The same exact source mixer is defined by the exponential of a fixed rational Hermitian matrix. Degree40 rational Taylor arithmetic generates approximate Born probabilities with a proved operator remainder. Each probability row is rationally normalized and converted to an exact52-bit integer cumulative distribution. Uniform setting and52-bit outcome draws generate the saved counts. The exact whole-bank record-TV upper bound is below the predeclared1e-8 allowance.

The first program invocation was stopped during symbolic probability-table PREPARATION, before any random draw or count file. The original program is preserved. Expanding rational entries at each Taylor step fixed the computational issue without changing source, seed, N, confidence budget or acceptance rule. No failed bank was discarded.

## What passed

The ordinary full-CPTP SDP is only a numerical candidate generator. Its rounded axes were converted to a known exact rational Cayley unitary. Exact algebra verified unitarity, the own-commutant6-by-6 block's strict positivity at the fixed kappa=.04, and the full Frobenius fit residual. Every source parameter remained excluded from candidate generation.

The actual bounds were:

- Exact outward residual upper: .004434269691.
- Joint-Hilbert–Schmidt confidence radius: .0387750418768.
- Native one-use oracle-regret confidence bound: .0058345143943.
- Observed guard plus beta and sampling allowance: .0068345243943, below the predeclared .01 decision threshold.

These are different claims:

1. The statistical procedure supplies a simultaneous high-probability native-regret confidence bound; the true source lies outside its event with probability at most beta plus the explicit sampling discrepancy.
2. The PREDECLARED accept/abstain rule guarantees an UNNORMALIZED ACCEPTED mean regret at most .01. The smaller observed random guard plus beta is not substituted for an unconditional expectation. Conditional-on-acceptance risk would require its acceptance denominator.

No forced-service guarantee is supplied on abstention. No posterior guarantee for this particular accepted record is asserted. Ideal known rational controls are specified, not compiled into hardware or physically executed.

## Same-data conventional comparison and remaining failure

Post hoc source scoring of the actual rational receiver gives native Bell loss .19000833994, versus native oracle loss .19. The same-data ordinary full-CPTP SDP gives .19000834002. Both numerical SDP success flags are true in this new bank, with a numerical primal–dual gap1.10e-11. The four-start unitary optimizer again misses its extremely strict gradient stopping threshold; that flag is retained and does not govern the exact positive-fit certificate.

Raw signs, scalar whitening and operator whitening have native losses .1969625, .1924966 and .1964244 on the SAME bank. Their source-contract violations remain, despite the larger sample. This is evidence that the positive-operator representation resolves this fixture's structural whitening obstruction. It is NOT evidence of superiority over an equally informed ordinary recovery SDP, which matches the fitted performance.

The virtual worst-gauge inverse Bell loss is3.3364e-5 by post hoc scoring, but that is NOT certified by the native task guard. The uniform operator-inverse bound2e/kappa is about2.16 and still fails the earlier delta=.1 target. A narrow native-oracle certificate does not become a high-accuracy complete-memory or arbitrary-future-noise guarantee.

The source's oracle Bell loss is still19%, so this is not a useful fault-tolerant memory demonstration. Four million calibration blocks for a two-output-qubit example are a large cost. No hardware, sampled unknown payload, time-evolving retained-memory trajectory, optimal code discovery, scaling advantage or practical quantum-computing capability was demonstrated.
