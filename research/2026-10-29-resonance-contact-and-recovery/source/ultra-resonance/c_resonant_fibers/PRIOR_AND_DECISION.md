# Scientific disposition: a constructed narrow regime and a real representation failure

The useful result of this branch is not its enormous printed N or time. It is
the explicitly paid distinction between an exact commutant and an
interaction-scale resonant fiber. The former can give an order-one wrong
weak-coupling prediction even on the SAME native NN+1/3NNN source. The latter
retains the scaled detuning in a coherent collective equation. Both that
repair and the mean-field mechanism have strong established antecedents.
**A new civilisation-level or practical predictive capability is NOT_ESTABLISHED.**

## What was actually built

`PROOF.md` derives an exact collective pair-spin Hamiltonian for the finite
momentum grid, pays a finite-volume oscillatory antiderivative rather than
assuming it is bounded uniformly, proves a quantitative product-marginal
approximation, and acquires its nonlinear Bloch evolution. The initial pure
Gaussian state and observable are given explicitly. There is no ETH,
Markov/local-equilibrium assumption, free quantum-state oracle or inferred
physical preparation in the chain.

The exact source compiler checked784 literal two-particle Fourier vertices
and64 paired-band entries in a cyclotomic field. The actual compact source
N2^128,lambda=N^-8 was then evaluated with48 acquired positive quadrature
nodes and512 exact-interval Taylor steps. Its original-model expectation
lies in

    [-0.10927576484299249, -0.10927561115965030].

The coupling is extraordinarily weak and the clock2^1023 is extraordinarily
long. That is a specified mathematical Hamiltonian, not a currently isolated
physical device, a thermodynamic-limit-first prediction or an empirical
result. The main positive operation is a restricted, compact mathematical
prediction from a fully known source; it does not establish a broad useful
simulation improvement. The code is the one declared query, not a complete
general-purpose implementation of the whole theorem family.

`PROJECTOR_OBSTRUCTION.md` changes the actual question from a formal
discontinuity to a dynamical comparison. For N>=16 and the stated small
lambda, a flux phi=lambda^2 leaves the true bounded pair coherence near zero

predicts a value differing by more than4/5. The analytic repair keeps
c=phi/lambda, adding the explicit on-site field
`2J sin(c lambda)/lambda sin(k)` to the finite-density Bloch equation.
Its mean-field error and rotational stability bounds remain the same. No
second source run, detuning grid or enclosure-refinement wave was performed.

## Strong primary antecedents and the actual scope comparison

**Bertini--Fagotti2015 is controlling.**
[Pre-relaxation in weakly interacting models](https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.07260)
already develops time-averaged weak interactions and a self-consistent
mean-field mapping for polynomials of noncommuting local charges. I read its
complete relevant PDF pages10--18 and visually checked pages11,14,16. Its
section3 discusses the oscillatory primitive; the application assumes the
discarded term is negligible. Its section4 explicitly names the anomalous-
term premise. Section5 establishes the mean-field mechanism in its stated
thermodynamic/analytic class. It is not fair to claim we invented a nonlinear
collective replacement for a many-body state. The finite-volume, extreme-
weak-coupling and specifically paired input here pays a narrower joint limit;
it does not turn that paper's thermodynamic-first assumptions into acquired
generic facts. A source-aware ordinary resonant-HFB/pre-relaxation solver may
use these SAME equations and the same quadrature/Taylor tools. There is no
operation or speed separation from that informed competitor.

**Modern generic weak-fermion simulation is a stronger baseline than ED.**
Zhao--Marvian--Tong2026,
[arXiv:2608.19448](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19448), Theorem1/Corollary2
already controls normalized-Frobenius simulation at small constant lambda*t
and permits an unrestricted quadratic part. Its arbitrary efficiently
Majorana-evaluable initial-state local-observable algorithm has a different
geometric time condition. I read the primary governing introduction,
Theorem1, Corollary2 and their norm/input distinctions; the full HTML bytes
are retained. Frobenius accuracy alone is not automatically the expectation
accuracy in this particular pure initial ray. Conversely our special paired
state, exact dispersion and extreme lambda(N) do not supersede its broad
source class. Neither method is charged with reconstructing a full state as
a deliberately weak comparator.

**The detuning repair's few-body channel is established scattering physics.**
The Chebyshev/relative-coordinate Jacobi channel in
PROJECTOR_OBSTRUCTION reduces a finite-range interaction to finitely many
boundary potentials. The nearest-range bound state and threshold are already
the hard-core special case of Valiente--Petrosyan2009,
[arXiv:0903.4574](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.4574), and the broader finite-range
two-body Green-function operation is covered by Kornilovitch,
[arXiv:2305.02548](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.02548).
For the first I inspected the author-uploaded primary special-case text
(case ii); for the latter the primary abstract/operation statement, not an
exhaustive full-text priority comparison. No complete PDF bytes for those
two references are claimed in this package. The bound-pair energy/threshold
is independently derived here and is not advertised as new.

**The first static-sector candidate was killed by a literal same-target prior.**
Smith et al2017 already gives paired-sector determinant/echo spin
correlations, not just incoherent occupation averages. The sibling
`c_static_sector_coherence/EARLY_PRIOR_STOP.md` preserves that failure.
No static-sector fixture collection was built after learning this.

These primary sources and source-aware ordinary methods remove any generic
new-representation/fast-simulator claim. Historical priority for a particular
quantitative composition remains UNKNOWN, which does not supply leverage.

## What remains missing, precisely

At fixed physically useful coupling and large N, the paid averaging error
here grows like lambda*N^6*tau. It does not close the useful infinite-volume
problem. The missing input is a substantially stronger, state/observable-
sensitive oscillatory-cancellation estimate that survives dense near-
resonances, while retaining detunings at the interaction scale. Treating
every nonzero energy difference as infinitely fast is explicitly unsafe.
The older pre-relaxation source already flags rather than supplies the most
general version of that input.

Thus the better question is not whether a finite commutant has a compact
description. It is whether the finite-width, interaction-scale coherent
fibers of a useful input family admit a closed computable evolution with
paid errors at fixed coupling/density. We have constructed that fiber and
its nonlinear equation in the declared weak finite-volume family; we have
not proved it for a generic chaotic late-time state. No generic hardness
claim settles that surviving question, and no arbitrary closure is granted.

The branch should be **deprioritized as a new broad capability lead**. The
source compiler, explicit detuning counterexample and corrected fiber are
retained as mathematical tools and a discriminating test for proposed
long-time representations. A huge formal source/time demonstration is not
a reason to expand the fixture or improve its last digits.
