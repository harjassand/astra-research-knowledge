# Primary sources, bounded search, and what is actually new here

The continuous proof in this directory is derived in full, not imported from an
unseen theorem, sampled oracle, or another agent's acceptance label. All components
below are ordinary retained machinery. Historical priority of this particular
radial-p-concavity capacity sandwich has NOT been established by a bounded search.

- Bovier, *Metastability: a potential theoretic approach* (author-hosted primary):
  https://wt.iam.uni-bonn.de/fileadmin/WT/Inhalt/people/Anton_Bovier/publications/fest.pdf
  Read in the preceding accepted construction. Capacity/Dirichlet and flow
  variational machinery are standard; this is not a claim to invent them.
- Mangeat, Guerin and Dean, *Dispersion in two dimensional channels—the Fick-Jacobs
  approximation revisited*, arXiv1710.02699v2:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.02699
  Primary body freshly reopened here, especially SecVII Eqs98–103. A common
  fixed-width through-channel gives a positive lower bound on effective diffusivity,
  including slow-transverse-motion limits. This is a genuine prior fixed-channel
  mechanism, not just blind rare-event simulation. Its observable is periodic
  diffusivity in hard channels, not this full-fiber Gibbs capacity/mass certificate.
- Wang and Drazer, *Transport of Brownian particles in a narrow, slowly-varying
  serpentine channel*, primary arXiv1406.3226:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1406.3226
  Freshly opened introduction, process, effective-diffusion equations85–91 and
  conclusion. Soft-potential and hard-channel reductions/asymptotic corrections
  are established ordinary alternatives. This work addresses narrow slowly-varying
  periodic channels, mobility and diffusivity; it does not supply the particular
  unknown-M, mixed-degree full-capacity outer certificate proved here. That is a
  scope comparison, not evidence that no other prior theorem does.
- Petersen, Lichtinger and Covino, *Committors and Reaction Rates from Trial
  Functions That Violate the Boundary Conditions*, arXiv2608.02536v1:
  https://arxiv.org/html/2608.02536v1
  Primary main Eqs3–14 freshly reopened. One-dimensional projected profile/Gram
  solves use existing equilibrium or reweighted data, not crossing trajectories.
  The exact flux-fidelity variational upper bound is retained. Their Eq10 replaces
  flux-weighted boundary fidelity by basin averages; the text explicitly calls
  this approximate and notes that the variational upper direction can be lost.
  Do not turn that caveat into dismissal of strong empirical or certified variants.
- Loper, Zhou and Geman, *Capacities and the Free Passage of Entropic Barriers*,
  arXiv1811.00205v2: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.00205
  Primary body inspected in the earlier parallel-channel construction. Local
  capacity methods for small-target hitting probabilities in epsilon-flat settings
  already avoid passage-time estimation. Entropic-barrier tractability itself
  is therefore not new here; the present input/observable/guarantee differ.
