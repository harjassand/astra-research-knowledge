# Primary-source comparison and scope

This proof uses conventional stochastic-process tools. It is not a claim to invent
time changes, fluid approximation, support closure, Foster theory, or occupation laws.

* **Darling (2002), Theorem 2.8 and Section 3.2.** The primary 16-page preprint was
  opened and its theorem/proof inspected. It gives finite-time O(1/N) fluid
  approximation under specified drift, noise and Lipschitz hypotheses, using a
  compensated martingale and a differential-inequality comparison. That is a strong
  ordinary baseline for our Section 4. We explicitly rederive network-dependent
  constants and uniformity over every point of the simplex; no novelty is claimed
  for the martingale/Gronwall step.
  [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0210109).

* **Barbour, Hamza, Kaspi and Klebaner (2014 version).** The primary abstract and
  linked HTML were opened. The abstract describes a branching-process stage followed
  by a deterministic approximation with a random time delay when leaving an absorbing
  boundary. This is relevant conventional boundary-process context. Our construction
  instead exactly strips a common seed and time-changes before applying a uniformly
  activating finite-time approximation. We do not claim their general method fails,
  nor that our support condition covers their setting.
  [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.5788).

* **Anderson, Cappelletti and Kim (2020).** The previously inspected publisher abstract
  explicitly uses a multi-step embedded-chain method for binary single-linkage
  networks with a pure complex for every species. Sampled-chain Foster analysis is
  therefore an established reaction-network approach, not a novel premise here.
  The current raw class can be cubic or higher and have multiple linkages; its
  specific sampled clock and acquisition must be proved, not inferred from that
  abstract. [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1017/jpr.2020.28).

* **Dai and Meyn (1995).** The earlier campaign inspected the primary queueing paper's
  fluid/recurrence/moment results. Those supply strong conventional context, but the
  present all-exponential conclusion is earned by the explicit first-core event
  probabilities and sampled t^N drift, not a generic implication from fluid stability.
  [Primary paper](https://people.orie.cornell.edu/jdai/publications/daiMeyn95.pdf).

The retained earlier packages also credit classical product-Poisson equilibrium
theorems, Petri-net support/siphon geometry, and exact finite-chain methods. The
new independent controls solve killed finite fibers with rational Gaussian
elimination; they are a paid comparator, not a stationary-law assumption or an
infinite-tail proof.
