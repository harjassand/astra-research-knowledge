## Whole ordinary proof reconstruction

### 1. Raw acquisition and the exact geometric family

The raw syntax checks exact integer nonnegative complexes, common dimension n>=3,
and exact strictly positive int/Fraction rates. Duplicate channels are combined;
validated null channels are discarded. The sole count-changing pair must be 0↔A.
All other channels are binary reversible hub/active edges. Every square-free A-free
hub has a 2A amplification pair. Every satellite is covered by at least one arm;
full incidence to every hub is not assumed. Connected hub components are acquired
by two-coloring and checking every cross-shore edge. Isolated non-hub satellites
are free passengers, not falsely rejected or added to the component count k.

For R=A+Σmin(L,U), remove the arm species before inserting its destination hub.
Removing a satellite decreases the relevant minimum by zero or one; inserting the
hub increases its component minimum by exactly one. This remains true when source
species and destination hub share a component or a species. Hence forward arms
change R by 0 or −1, reverse arms by 0 or +1, and 2A/hub edges by −1/+1.

At R=1 no internal decrement is enabled. At R=0, A=0 and no hub has both endpoint
counts positive, so every internal source vanishes. This does not say A=0 alone
is inactive, and does not claim R itself is conserved.

A strictly larger shore, or a free satellite, always supplies R-neutral capture.
Its total population is at least N−2R+A. Potentially decrementing arm species
have total count at most 2(R−A), including equal-shore ties. Consequently the
negative-inventory forward rate is at most K_f A R. Complete bipartiteness yields
Σ_h x_i x_j=Σ_components LU≥(R−A)^2/k. Half the amplification rate gives precisely
β(R−A)^2. These statements hold for sparse incidence and cross-component arms.

### 2. Every positive rate and every finite t>1

ε=min(1,(λ+β)/2) is strictly below λ+β, so every backward denominator is positive.
The recurrence multiplier exceeds one; g is positive and decreasing, including
the join g_K=1+c/K. K≥ceil(2c) gives c/r≤1/2 on its tail. Direct substitution and
r/(r+1)≥1/2 prove inequality (3) there; the finite head gives equality. The stated

D=μ(1−1/t)>0. J≥K+1 places both g_(r−1) and g_r in their tail for r≥J.

### 3. The active/latent correction and reverse channels

2/R^2+R(1/R^2−1/(R+1)^2)≤11/(R+1)^2. At R=1 this bound remains valid even for

The adverse seed-decreasing channels are bounded by 11K_f, and *all* reverse

### 4. Physical immigration and death are fully charged

under core jumps. For birth, its scaled contribution is at most
qλ[11t+(t−1)/4]/(N+1)≤12qλt/(N+1).

### 6. Nonexplosion, irreducibility, invariant law and moments

Internal jumps preserve N, births have constant rate λ, and deaths only lower N.
Thus N(s)≤N(0)+P(s), with a rate-λ Poisson immigration process. On a bounded time
interval that upper bound is finite almost surely, and finite count sublevels
contain finitely many states with finite bounded jump rates. Explosion is excluded.

Every covered satellite X has the enabled path
0→A→2A→H→A+X→X, choosing a hub incident to its arm; all edges reverse. Positive
translation preserves enabling. Repeating translated singleton paths constructs
or removes any count vector, proving full-orthant irreducibility without a fiber
hypothesis or an unproved closed-class assertion.

The Foster conclusion does not assume π(W)<∞ before proving it. Localized
integrating-factor Dynkin followed by nonexplosion and Fatou yields
P_s W≤e^(−ζs)W+C_F(1−e^(−ζs))/ζ. Alternatively, all finite-time integrability is
available directly: W≤Ht^N and total propensities are polynomial in N, which is
Poisson dominated over bounded intervals. Normlikeness makes occupation averages
tight; the ordinary countable-state Foster argument gives an invariant probability
and positive recurrence. Fatou on those averages gives π(W)≤C_F/ζ, hence
π(t^N)≤C_F/ζ. Irreducibility gives uniqueness. Since the construction works for
each arbitrary finite t>1, the same unique invariant law has all exponential
moments. A finite sample of t values is not the justification for that universal
quantifier.

## Obstructions and exact graph boundary

The four-species fixture is genuinely non-complex-balanced at its stated rate 2
reverse arm: the leaf balance equations are inconsistent. The all-unit-rate
control is detailed balanced and already has product-Poisson stability, so neither
representation obstruction should be used as evidence of recurrence novelty.

The first-core immigration lower bound at (0,n,1,1) is λ/(λ+5), independent of n;
the two-step bound from (1,n,0,0) also stays positive. This invalidates transfer of
the old single-core O(1/N) argument, not recurrence itself.

For arbitrary positive normlike F(A,N), the two pure-B comparison rays imply
u_n≤ρ' u_(n−1)+M with ρ'<1, contradicting normlikeness. This is a physical
one-step geometric-Foster representation no-go, not an exclusion of sampled-time,
composition-dependent, or nongeometric alternatives.

For t^N g_R, pure satellite states force g_0>t g_1. At A=1 the death contribution
is then positive and fast arms are R-neutral, forcing g_2<g_1/t. At A=2, fast
arms remain neutral, death is positive, and 2A→hub has total rate 2a. Dropping only
positive birth destinations leaves the positive bound
[2a(1−1/t)−λ/t]g_1 whenever 2a(t−1)>λ. This proves precisely the stated profile-only
failure, not failure of the composition correction.

For integer capacitated matching, every edge removal lowers ν by between 1 and 2.
The universal drop-exactly-one property is equivalent to every nontrivial component
being complete bipartite. The triangle capacity (1,1,2) and induced-P4 unit-capacity
witnesses produce drop 2→0. Conversely a connected triangle-free/P4-free graph is
complete bipartite by the neighborhood argument given in the note. This is the
sharp boundary of *that matching-removal property only*. Missing hub edges or
odd cycles are UNKNOWN for this constructor, not instability or a general-WR
counterexample. There is no claim to classify all inventories or correctors.

In fact the author's P4 and triangle controls use `special=False`: every included
reversible pair has unit rates. Their concentration vector of ones is detailed
balanced, and ordinary product-Poisson proves their recurrence and all exponential
moments despite the constructor's structural UNKNOWN. This is a particularly
concrete reason not to confuse the exact inventory boundary with stability.
