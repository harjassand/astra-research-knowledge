# Uniform coverage forces a genuinely global information interface

This is an immediate corollary of the separately frozen ROOT randomized proof
RANDOMIZED_LOCAL_PROBE_OBSTRUCTION_ROOT.md, SHA256
b24a7775dd2403233f771cf1ec19e79138bb1a85939a71fa72cc162a8afaf47c.
It does not strengthen or reinterpret the earlier deterministic/pathwise proof.
Independent receiving of the root proof and this corollary is separate from the
raw executable audit.

Let an adaptive randomized local value/finite-jet algorithm terminate almost
surely on the nominal F0. Suppose it claims uniform lower-confidence coverage

    for every F in the declared smooth compact-bump class,
    P_F(report L>k_F) <= alpha.

Set q=P_F0(report L>0). The root theorem gives, for every epsilon>0, ONE FIXED
alternative F_epsilon with false-positive probability at least q-epsilon.
Uniform coverage implies q-epsilon<=alpha. Letting epsilon decrease to zero,

    P_F0(report L>0) <= alpha.

Thus for alpha<1/2 the same procedure cannot both have uniform confidence1-alpha
and return a positive finite-rate lower bound on the nominal model with
probability at least1-alpha. Returning UNKNOWN or0 often enough is not
contradicted. This is a fixed-alternative randomized statement, not an adversary
choosing a potential after seeing the realized random seed.

## Consequence for using the earned robustness capability

The raw receiver's success is conditional on information it REALLY receives:
a complete exact rational program with bounded degree, validated denominator,
nonnegative atom grammar and global polynomial inequalities. It does not learn
those premises or an unknown physical microscopic model from a few force probes.
The new information reduction comes after admission: a radial envelope suffices
for the conductance prediction without evaluating internal partitions, angular
laws, committors or hidden transverse mobility.

To turn that into physical inference, a next consumer needs an independently
justified global information contract, not simply more local samples of the same
unrestricted class. A fixed finite-dimensional model class, independently bounded
regularity plus certified global coverage, or an actual interval-enclosure oracle
could change the problem. Their acquisition, calibration, evaluation and error
costs must be paid. A derivative bound estimated only from the same finite point
samples is not automatically such a contract. Known degree/interpolation is one
ordinary option, not a free useful-basis oracle.

Trajectory observations, equilibrium sampling and richer measurements are outside
this local-oracle theorem. They are neither ruled out nor made cheap by it. Their
information content, noise, mixing and rare-event access would need a distinct
actual task and proof. Likewise probabilistic typical-model guarantees under a
specified prior differ from uniform worst-case coverage and require that prior
as explicit information. No new laboratory data, empirical parameter accuracy,
physical validation or experimental operation has been supplied here.
