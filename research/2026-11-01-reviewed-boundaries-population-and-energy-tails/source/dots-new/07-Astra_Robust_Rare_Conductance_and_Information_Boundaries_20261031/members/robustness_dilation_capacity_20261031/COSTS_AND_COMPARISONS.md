## Ordinary global comparisons get the same information

A naive global bound from the supplied critical-flat envelope uses sup delta*e
=1/8000 and incurs width factor(8001/7999)^1024≈1.29175 even before nominal slack.
That is NOT the strongest retaining comparison on this actual raw fixture.

A solver seeing the full raw input can detect that atom deviations are only
+/-delta/2, and may change the static anchor coefficients individually to absorb
uniform angular rescalings. Give it that improvement. Set a=1/16000. A negative-
perturbation quadratic atom has normalized radius spanning[1-a,1] as y varies.
Any global radial envelopes with arbitrary static anchor U' and scalar radius
constants L,H must satisfy H/L>=1/(1-a): evaluate along that coordinate as
z->0 and take the ratio of its largest and smallest quadratic coefficients.
The same range occurs separately inside the slab and in the left basin for the
flat profile, so separate slab/basin global constants do not remove this cost.

This ratio is achievable: rescale each static anchor atom by its minimum profile
radius to the corresponding degree. Every resulting atom radius lies in[1,R],
R=1/(1-a); positive-perturbation atoms have ratio1+a<=R. Thus the BEST pure global
radial-envelope comparison of this form has width penalty at least

    R^(2m)=(16000/15999)^1024 >1.066,

before its nominal structural/numerical slack. The rational comparison was actually
computed; global_comparison_check.json records an exact base/exponent representation
and the inequality, avoiding gratuitous huge decimal serialization. This is NOT
a lower bound for every ordinary analytic/variational method. It only shows what
is lost by compressing LOCATION information into global radial constants.

Ordinary localized Dirichlet/mass comparison, supplied the SAME scalar envelope
and Jensen certificate, produces exactly our1.00845 bracket. It is not an
independent pre-existing competitor that somehow defeats a proof by being able
to implement that same proof. Historical priority remains unresolved. The scoped
result is an acquired sufficient representation retaining the error location.

## Other strong alternatives and genuine failures

- Bounded-density/Holley–Stroock style comparison is retained with its correct
  oscillation and normalization. Polynomial-in-z perturbations here generally
  have unbounded additive energy oscillation, even for tiny profile errors.
- Global energy-amplitude comparison remains valid with homogeneous exponentm/p,
  or the coarser mixed-degree exponentm/d_min. Uniform atom rescaling belongs in
  the anchor family rather than being falsely charged as shape error. This does
  not assert equality of an actual fixed-M rate under energy rescaling.
- Existing free-energy, sampled variational and full elliptic solvers receive the
  same raw structure and may tighten our intrinsic slack, solve other inputs or
  use richer data. No formal complexity separation or hardness theorem is claimed.
- Generic rotations against sharp angular energy need actual conditioning/envelope
  control; a small Euclidean angle alone does not suffice. Unsupported rotated
  atoms are UNKNOWN, not certified harmless.
- Infinitesimal higher-degree tail terms can leave bounded sample sets nearly
  unchanged while destroying a proposed GLOBAL envelope. The full numerator/
  degree and projective positivity checks, not samples, protect this consumer.
- Bernstein subdivision is sufficient, not complete: even a nonnegative square
  with a non-dyadic interior zero can be UNKNOWN at a finite budget. This is not
  a proof that the underlying rate is unidentifiable from stronger methods.
- Missing raw grammar, insufficient precision/seed/grid/tail/positivity/operand
  budgets, exact-string limits and an intrinsically too-wide outer bracket are
  explicit UNKNOWN outcomes. All failed/interrupted preliminary attempts are
  preserved and distinguished from successful final receiving.

## What the robustness boundary means

The sensitive-profile proof shows exp(Theta(m epsilon)) amplification is REAL;
one cannot promise sub1% prediction from arbitrary fixed small profile-error
budgets without charging parameter accuracy. The flat-profile proof and finite
run show a global sup bound is NOT a universal physical accuracy floor: where
the defect occurs matters. Its role relative to resistance and basin mass can
be received without evaluating the actual high-dimensional partition.

The separate deterministic and randomized local-probe obstructions explain why
this symbolic success is not automatically physical inference from a few samples.
Global structural information must come from somewhere and its acquisition must
be accounted for. There is no hardware/biological operation, external publication,
experimental calibration, arbitrary-start MFPT equivalence or scientific novelty
score in this package. The next missing operation is a justified physical
information interface, not merely a larger exactly supplied polynomial catalog.
