The gauge investigation has exposed a real obstacle: the longer slab that gives us the complete matter spectrum also makes the bulk respond more strongly to a shared gauge field. We can’t assume the desired interaction stays fixed as the device grows. We tested one possible fix, confining the gauge field near the surface. In a controlled limit it creates a new unwanted mirror boundary, so it doesn’t solve the problem. I’m working on whether that new boundary can itself be gapped while retaining the gauge field and the desired particles. No interacting chiral gauge theory has been established yet.
