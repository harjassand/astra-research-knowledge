## 1. Precise theorem

Let
\[
\Gamma:M_a\rightarrow M_b
\]
be a finite-dimensional quantum channel with an exact Choi matrix whose real and imaginary entries are rational.

Write \(E\sim\Gamma\) when \(E\) and \(\Gamma\) have the same **complete, all-reference RLD/maximal-information profile**, in the sense of the earlier accepted classification.

The problem is to decide whether
\[
\boxed{
\forall k\ge2,\quad
\exists E_k\sim\Gamma,\ \exists\mathcal B_k
\quad\text{with}\quad
\operatorname{Tr}_{\widehat j}\mathcal B_k=E_k
\quad(1\le j\le k).
}
\]

Here \(\mathcal B_k\) is a completely positive, trace-preserving channel with \(k\) output systems. The realisation \(E_k\) may change with \(k\).

### What the algorithm returns

| Answer | Guaranteed output |
|---|---|
| **Yes** | For every requested finite \(k\), an exact rational \(\mathcal B_k\) with identical same-profile marginals, each of output dimension at most \(b^2\). |
| **No** | A finite algebraic witness and a computable order \(k_0\) for which **no finite-dimensional channel in the entire profile class** has such an extension. |

No capacity oracle, separability oracle, or supplied broadcast construction is required.

For a fixed rational source that passes the test, construction of a compressed certificate has **polynomial bit complexity in the numerical receiver count \(k\)**. A deterministic physical circuit also has polynomial size in \(k\) at fixed local dimensions. This efficiency statement does not extend to varying channel dimensions.

### The exact finite criterion

Let
\[
S=\operatorname{ran}J_\Gamma\subset B\otimes R.
\]
Introduce \(A\cong\mathbb C^b\), and set \(Y=A\otimes S\). Define
\[
P=\mathbb C[x_{ij}:1\le i,j\le b],\qquad
N=P\,Y^\circ\subset P\otimes R^*,
\]
where \(Y^\circ\) is the algebraic annihilator, and put
\[
\ell=\sum_i x_{ii}.
\]

Then the answer is yes exactly when
\[
\boxed{(N:\ell^\infty)_1=N_1.}
\]

In words: saturating the relation module by the prescribed Bell linear form must create no new degree-one relation. This can be decided by finite exact polynomial elimination.

The **new result is the equivalence between this algebraic condition and the full physical synthesis problem**. Saturation itself is established computer-algebra machinery.

### The hardest operation that was resolved

For each \(k\), define the support compatible with every receiver:
\[
T_k(S)=
\bigcap_{j=1}^k
\left(S_{B_jR}\otimes B_{\widehat j}\right).

The initial difficulty was turning support compatibility into a positive, normalised broadcast channel. Separate symmetrisation of auxiliary systems and receiver systems loses legitimate sectors in higher dimensions.

The successful construction instead uses

\left(\operatorname{Sym}^k(A\otimes B)\otimes R\right)

The remaining proof has three steps:

1. **Polynomial duality:** \(D_k\) is the dual of the degree-\(k\) component of \(M=(P\otimes R^*)/N\).

## 5. Limitations and physical costs

### Profile preservation does not preserve every operational capability

“Complete” refers to the specified all-reference RLD/maximal-information family. It does not mean equality of every possible communication task, measurement statistic, or capacity.

The positive-capacity source and zero-capacity realisation above make that distinction explicit.

### The realisation changes with receiver count

The theorem proves
\[
\forall k\;\exists E_k.
\]
It does not prove
\[
\exists E\;\forall k.
\]

### Recovering an existing unknown output is probabilistic

The physical inverse instrument satisfies
\[
\mathcal I_sF_s=p_s\,\operatorname{id},
\qquad
p_s=\frac{b^2s^2}{1+(b^2-1)s^2}.
\]

Failure consumes the unknown input. All-success recovery across \(n\) separate uses pays \(p_s^n\). Polynomial construction size therefore does not imply efficient recovery or a positive asymptotic ordinary communication rate.

The actual complement is
\[
F_s^c(X)
=(1-p_s)X+p_s\frac{I_b}{b}\operatorname{Tr}X.
\]
For \(F_s\Gamma\), the source environment is retained:
\[
(F_s^c\otimes\operatorname{id}_{E_\Gamma})
(V_\Gamma X V_\Gamma^\dagger).
\]

### The decision boundary is sensitive to exact support

Adding arbitrarily small full-rank Choi noise can change a negative answer to a positive one. The theorem is therefore not, by itself, a robust experimental classifier from finite-precision tomography.

### Efficiency and optimality remain restricted

The polynomial construction bound fixes the source, including its dimensions and coefficients. General saturation can be expensive. The sufficient local output bound \(b^2\) is not asserted to be minimal, and the negative certificate need not identify the first forbidden order.

### Ordinary quantum-capacity approximation remains unresolved

The theorem does **not** give a total algorithm approximating ordinary unassisted \(Q\) within \(1/4\) bit per use.

For admitted profiles, the capacity spectrum is
\[
[0,Q(\Gamma_0)],
\]
but the greatest-representative endpoint \(Q(\Gamma_0)\) remains symbolic. This spectrum consequence uses fixed-dimensional capacity continuity; it does not evaluate that endpoint. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="4"}
