# Significance and capability boundary

## What is established

The earlier boundary learner supplied isolated total-variation accuracy. This proof supplies a stronger interface: empirical pair-correlation errors control weighted L1 error RELATIVE to the true partition function, uniformly over nonnegative external weights with nonnegative Fourier coefficients. This includes arbitrary-strength consistent boundary fields and ferromagnetic environments, and the errors compose across independently learned blocks without assuming intermediate reconstructed tensors are positive.

For product boundary fields the operation is complete: one passive data summary supports later, adaptively chosen field profiles; relative normalization estimates; and a polynomial-time normalized response sampler/evaluator. The sample bound does not depend on hidden bulk size, field strength beyond encoded bit cost, or the inverse probability of same-sign evidence. Exact rational tests include events of probability 2^-20 and 2^-40.

This is a substantive extension beyond ordinary isolated TV learning. It resolves the stated absolute-to-relative stability step for an explicit environment cone, rather than assuming rare-event accuracy follows from TV distance.

## What remains established prior work

Boundary Pfaffian identities, exact boundary-field response calculations, specified-model ferromagnetic approximation, and multiplicative approximation of some exponentially small observables already exist. They are the comparison baseline. The precise noisy-data interface guarantee may or may not be new; the literature checks do not establish priority or superiority over the strongest adapted existing method.

## Remaining capability gap

For general compactly described environments, the proof is an error certificate, not a complete efficient contraction or sampling algorithm. Only the product-field receiver is explicitly implemented mathematically. General opposing fields are excluded, with an explicit rare-event counterexample. Passive normalized observations do not identify absolute isolated block partition factors or free energies.

The construction assumes correctly ordered boundary snapshots from an isolated zero-field block satisfying the exact model promise. It does not certify that promise, provide independent equilibrium data, or reconstruct the hidden physical system. No non-generated scientific deployment or matched-resource practical advantage is demonstrated.

## Assessment

Preserve as an independently checked relative-response and composition theorem with a complete restricted response algorithm. It materially strengthens the previous packet, but does not yet establish a civilisation-changing capability. A consequential next step requires a justified tractable receiver for useful assembled environments, or a real intervention problem where the finite-sample guarantee provides an otherwise missing operation.
