# Independent adversarial audit: blind stationary tomography

4 November 2026. Separate derivation and tests; the author's internal tests were not inspected.

## Verdict
No counterexample was found to the exact theorem or the conditional noise statement under the stated contract. The proof supports recovery of the named-state generator up to one positive clock factor and recovery of every unknown selected pair. This is not a guarantee that interventions are local, an experimentally acquired control interface, a uniform sample-complexity theorem, or a priority finding.

## Independent mathematical checks
1. Finite response identity. Writing W'=W+(t−1)b u^T and subtracting stationary equations gives Wd=−(t−1)(u^T pi')b. Irreducibility makes W restricted to the zero-sum space invertible. A zero response is equivalent to zero baseline edge current under positive nonunit symmetric scaling. No infinitesimal-response assumption is hidden here.
2. Projective data and graph acquisition. Multiplication by W_H^-1 and independent nonzero column amplitudes preserves the entire incidence-column matroid, not only triangle tests. A spanning 3-connected target graph is therefore identifiable up to vertex relabelling by established graphic-matroid reconstruction and Whitney uniqueness. The explicit basis/fundamental-circuit binary representation route avoids relying on an unstated algorithm that recognizes arbitrary real matrices from support alone. The graphic promise is essential.
3. Linear-map rigidity. For any candidate K, P^-1 K_H W_H^-1 has every incidence vector as an eigenvector. On a simple cycle the unique dependence has all coefficients nonzero, forcing equal eigenvalues. Connectedness of the cycle matroid propagates that equality to all edges; spanning extends it to H. This also covers zero eigenvalues, so an extra singular solution has not been omitted. K pi=0 fixes the final complementary direction.
4. Physical labels. The unknown permutation affects output rows only, because measured coordinates fix input columns. For positive scale each column has one negative diagonal entry; at least two positive outgoing entries rule out the opposite scale sign. Thus equal stationary probabilities do not create a label ambiguity. Extra unperturbed one-way transitions add nonnegative off-diagonal entries and do not invalidate this argument.
5. Small cases. A triangle has three nonparallel response lines in a 2D space, and every permutation of its edges is induced by a vertex permutation. For K4, line-graph triangles alone are ambiguous: stars and graph triangles both give 3-cliques. The supplied independence test distinguishes them correctly. K 1,3 is not a competing same-matroid representation of K3: its three edges are independent.
6. Finite controls. t=1−alpha/D has the correct sign and is unchanged by common clock scaling. D=0 cannot be compatible with a nonzero active response under the promised finite control. Positivity and stationarity of the reconstructed interventions are a sufficient mathematical fit, not a physical validation of locality.
7. Arbitrary extra edges. Response directions encode the selected graph while W_H^-1 includes all transitions. Therefore the calculation can identify unperturbed directed rates as claimed; it does not assume the selected graph equals the generator graph.

## Noise constants and complexity
The stated entry-error model gives response-submatrix operator error at most 2 n eta by a Frobenius bound. Weyl's inequality then justifies the gamma/2 rank threshold and gamma/8 numerical accuracy. The margin is over every independent subset queried or potentially queried; it is an input promise, not a quantity established from finite data.

For the reconstruction operator L, perturbing K pi=0 contributes at most n² eta² to squared Frobenius error. The m(n−2) response-support equations contribute at most 4 mn(n−2)eta². Thus the supplied A0 is valid. A unit near-minimum-residual vector has exact residual at most 2 E+rho. The spectral gap sigma bounds its distance to the one-dimensional kernel; sign alignment gives the factor sqrt(2). The zeta/3 condition correctly identifies the unique negative entry versus at least two negative entries under opposite orientation. Small true zero off-diagonal rates need not remain nonnegative after unconstrained noisy fitting, as the proof acknowledges.

The added forest bound also checks independently: each tree-edge coefficient is a cut sum of B_F x, giving sigma_min(B_F)≥1/n; combining this with |alpha_e|≥d_min/(sqrt(2)||W_H^-1||) yields gamma≥d_min/(sqrt(2)n kappa). The optional positive-part/diagonal repair has squared Frobenius error at most n times the original off-diagonal error squared, so its sqrt(n)delta guarantee is valid. It need not preserve stationarity or unit norm.

Trace error for the raw row-reordered estimate is at most sqrt(n)delta. The displayed trace-normalization bound uses this raw estimate. If the repaired generator is normalized instead, delta must be replaced throughout by sqrt(n)delta, including the admissibility condition. The author applied this sequencing clarification in the audited final revision. The displayed normalization bound follows by bounding the perturbed trace away from zero. Hoeffding plus a union bound gives the stated iid probability-coordinate error. These calculations do not license applying the iid sample count to correlated trajectory frames without mixing/effective-sample analysis.

Exact rational rank, homogeneous linear equations and a polynomial graphic reconstruction reduction have polynomial bit complexity in the rational input size. The finite controls are rational ratios after choosing a rational clock. Noise precision must include the requested tolerance bit lengths, while sample cost depends on inverse physical margins. No efficient certification of the promised margins is supplied or implied.

## Independent exact tests
A separately written SymPy test generated 18 random rational models: three each on K3,K4,K5,the 5-vertex wheel,the 6-vertex wheel,and K3,3. Sparse models contained 1–4 additional directed unperturbed transitions. Both increased and decreased rates were used (t in {1/3,2/3,3/2,4}); all selected baseline currents were checked nonzero. Abstract vertex labels were randomly permuted.

For every case the independently assembled reconstruction system had nullity 1, the negative-entry rule recovered the exact row permutation, the recovered generator equaled a positive rational multiple of the original, and all finite t values were recovered exactly. Independent K4/K5 star reconstruction also recovered the original stars. Tests diagnose errors; they do not replace the mathematical proof or independently implement the general graphic-matroid subroutine.

A nineteenth exact case used the explicit uniform-stationary generator W=[[-30,9,8,13],[11,-30,6,13],[12,14,-30,4],[7,7,16,-30]] and all six half-strength controls. It also gave nullity 1 and exact recovery, directly testing that stationary-probability degeneracy does not cause label ambiguity.

The test summary is retained locally as checks.json. The proof-only ZIP excludes scripts, data and third-party material.

## Explicit boundaries that must remain attached
* Dropping locality destroys identification from stationary distributions alone. For any positive probability vector p, the generator p*1^T−I has stationary distribution p. Thus an arbitrary family of observed positive snapshots can always be explained by unrelated global interventions; a fitted local model does not certify the apparatus.
* As t approaches 1 on a fixed nonzero-current edge, its response tends to zero linearly, so gamma tends to zero. Likewise response vanishes as current approaches equilibrium. No uniform noise-independent recovery rate follows from exact identifiability.
* Clock remains fundamentally unidentifiable: scaling all rates and their interventions by the same positive constant leaves all snapshots unchanged.
* Three-connectivity is sufficient, not shown necessary. Positivity may resolve some 2-isomorphic graph ambiguities, but this proof does not classify them. Conversely weaker coverage cannot be silently assumed to retain the theorem.

## Prior-art comparison and novelty limit
The main computational reconstruction primitive is directly anticipated by Bixby and Cunningham, 'Converting Linear Programs to Network Problems' (1980), https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.5.3.321 : row operations and nonzero column scaling reconstruct network form. Seymour's graphic recognition algorithm and Whitney uniqueness supply established combinatorial machinery. This audit verified the Bixby/Cunningham publisher abstract and does not claim independent implementation or reproof of those algorithms.

Aslyamov and Esposito's 2024 static-response work, https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.133.107103 , already relates response to incidence topology. Bebon and Speck's 2026 finite-edge mutual-linearity result, https://doi.org/10.1103/jcm3-57d8 , already supplies finite stationary response-line structure. The credible distinction in the present assembled theorem is recovering unknown intervention endpoints and aligning them to measured state names by generator positivity, under the stated spanning coverage promise. A search did not identify an exact prior match, but does not establish priority.

Micali and Zhu's 2016 'Reconstructing Markov processes from independent and anonymous experiments', https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dam.2015.06.035 , is not the same-data theorem: it observes anonymous visited-state equality patterns along trajectories and reconstructs a neighbourhood up to isomorphism. Its anonymous terminology must not be mistaken for stationary unlabelled intervention data. Direct named trajectories and calibrated resets supply stronger information/control and should remain practical comparators. No end-to-end advantage over them is established here.

Two additional primary unknown-input comparators were inspected in full, including their model assumptions. Ayazoglu–Sznaier–Ozay (2011), *Blind Identification of Sparse Dynamic Networks and Applications*, https://skoge.folk.ntnu.no/prost/proceedings/cdc-ecc-2011/data/papers/1580.pdf , uses observed node time series, autoregressive links, sparse topology and infrequently changing piecewise-constant unobserved inputs. It is genuine blind network identification, but does not give the present stationary local-pair response-line theorem. Hayden–Yuan–Gonçalves (2013), https://doi.org/10.23919/ECC.2013.6669768 , allows steady-state data and unknown perturbation amplitudes. Crucially, Corollary 1 and Section III retain a square diagonal input structure: each indexed experiment directly and uniquely affects its corresponding measured state. Section III-B explicitly perturbs the first named state and constructs an equivalent input at that state. Thus its unknown-amplitude assumption does not remove target-location calibration as the present contract does. Neither comparison supports claiming that blind or unknown-input network inference itself is new.

## Conclusion
The scoped theorem survives this independent adversarial review. Retain all coverage, current, locality, state-observation and noise-margin promises. Present it as a conditional inverse-problem result built from known response and matroid operations; physical acquisition, priority and major practical consequence remain unresolved.
