There is now a concrete gauge-side construction for the actual open model: by pairing it with a reflected copy, we can build a neutral state satisfying Gauss’s law and control its minimally gauged evolution for a finite time. The construction has been internally checked, but it doubles the particles again and preparation may be extremely expensive.

The key limitation is unchanged: this finite-time result does not establish the interacting gauge-theory vacuum or its long-time, low-energy behavior. I’m working on that gap rather than treating the new construction as the end goal.
