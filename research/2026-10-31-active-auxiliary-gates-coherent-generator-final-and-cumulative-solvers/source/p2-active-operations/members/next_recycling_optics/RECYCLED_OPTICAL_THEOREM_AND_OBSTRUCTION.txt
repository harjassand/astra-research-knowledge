RECYCLED OPTICAL TRANSPORT: A PROVED WINDOW AND A SOURCE-SPECTRUM OBSTRUCTION
Research checkpoint, 2026-10-31

This develops the complete L=0,S=1 -> L'=1,S=1 optical model already retained
in physical_window/COMMON_MODE_TRIPOD_INTERFACE.txt. It does not replace the
earlier active-source tripod theorem, local/pair controls, or real compiler.
It gives the previously missing all-recycling contraction in one explicit
degenerate-complement window. It also supplies a decisive counterexample to
extending the split-manifold protection claim using large atomic detuning and
positive bounded gain alone. The counterexample survives optimal recovery
AFTER the fixed Cartesian polarization record is classicalized, with a
quantitatively smaller error than unconditioned recovery. Changing how the
radiation is measured changes this conclusion: an optimized first-photon
analyzer improves the error, and a joint Bell measurement of harmful and
recovery photons permits exact correction in the limiting model. Those
distinct resources are separated explicitly below. No microscopic atomic
device is qualified here.

1. Model and resources

Use linewidth Gamma=1 as the time unit. Ground states are |g,x>,|g,y>,|g,z>.
Excited states are |i,j>, with i the Cartesian orbital index and j the spin
index. Set epsilon=z, D_z v=z tensor v, and

  P0=|vec(I)/sqrt(3)><vec(I)/sqrt(3)|,
  P1=(I-F)/2, P2=(I+F)/2-P0,

where F swaps the two excited Cartesian indices. The internal Hamiltonian is

  H = (Delta1 P1+Delta2 P2) tensor I_E
      + sqrt(3)(D_z+D_z^dagger) tensor G(t)
      + I_internal tensor H_E(t).

G is Hermitian for the positive result below. Every physical Cartesian
emission is retained:

  L_l=D_l^dagger tensor I_E, l=x,y,z,
  sum_l L_l^dagger L_l=I_excited tensor I_E.

The zero on ground blocks in these formulas is understood. A positive common
gain and all three Cartesian decay channels are admitted resources, not
derived Maxwell/vacuum/trap properties. Code/helper loading and controlled
polarization paths remain operations. The retained source may start in any
state and may evolve noncommutatively and arbitrarily rapidly through its
common H_E(t). Full well-posedness is assumed; a finite-dimensional source
suffices for both counterexamples and the certificate proof extends to
bounded G on a well-posed arbitrary source domain.

2. Exact all-recycling contraction, without an assumed spectral gap

Set Delta1=Delta2=20 and ||G(t)-I||<=1/4. The exact invariant subsystem S is

  S=span{g_x,g_y,e_zx,e_zy}=C^2_logical tensor C^2_optical.

Every restricted fine jump acts as I_logical tensor an optical/source
operator. For example only L_z acts nontrivially within S. The complement R
has eight dimensions. Its density block evolves autonomously under a
trace-decreasing, completely positive recycled generator; no-emission
evolution is not an adequate substitute for this generator.

The only coherently driven transient block has basis

  b=g_z, r=e_zz, q=(e_xx+e_yy)/sqrt(2).

On this block, at G=I,

  H3 = [[0,sqrt(3),0],
        [sqrt(3),40/3,-20sqrt(2)/3],
        [0,-20sqrt(2)/3,20/3]].

The r photon recycles to b, with jump |b><r|. Both q emission channels leave
R and return to the protected ground code. Thus the transient adjoint is

  L3^*(P)=i[H3,P]+|r><b|P|b><r|-(Q P+P Q)/2,
  Q=|r><r|+|q><q|.

The recycling term |r><b|P|b><r| is essential.

Solve L3^*(P3)=-I exactly. In nonorthonormal coordinates
S3=diag(sqrt(3),1,sqrt(2)), let P'=S3^dagger P3 S3=X+iY. An exact solution is

  X = [[2127753/208384, -135/407, -8265/13024],
       [-135/407,2035657/625152,-175/1776],
       [-8265/13024,-175/1776,30743/4884]],

  Y = [[0,-1/2,-3175/3256],
       [1/2,0,4195/26048],
       [3175/3256,-4195/26048,0]].

Extend P3 to P_R on all eight transient states by assigning value 1 to
(e_xx-e_yy)/sqrt(2), e_xy, e_yx and value 1+P_bb to e_xz,e_yz. Here
P_bb=709251/208384. The latter two levels emit back to b, explaining the
additional mean residence time P_bb. With V=sqrt(3)(|r><b|+|b><r|), extended
by zero to the other five coordinates, the following are EXACT inequalities:

  I <= P_R <= (9/2)I,
  ||i[V,P_R]|| < 8/5,
  L_{G=I}^*(P_R)=-I.

The companion exact_recycling_certificate.py verifies the Poisson equation
with rational arithmetic, and proves the inequalities using positive leading
principal minors. Its JSON contains every rational witness. Floating-point
checks are diagnostics, not the basis of the strict inequalities.

For any admitted quantum source, the full adjoint applied to P_R tensor I_E is

  L_t^*(P_R tensor I_E)=-I+i[V,P_R] tensor (G(t)-I)
                     <= -(3/5)I
                     <= -(2/15)(P_R tensor I_E).

The common H_E term cancels exactly. This calculation does not diagonalize
H_E and does not assume [G,H_E]=0 or bound either derivative. It gives

  Pr(R at t) <= (9/2) exp[-2(t-s)/15] Pr(R at s).

Restoring physical units with mean gain Gamma, complement detuning 20 Gamma,
and ||G-Gamma I||<=Gamma/4 gives rate 2 Gamma/15. The tighter numerical
certificate has rate 0.141599499 Gamma and prefactor 4.40357705. The exact
rate 2/15 is retained for a clean independently checkable theorem.

There is also an exact complex-gain extension relevant to the earlier
common-mode field construction. Write A=I+B with ||B||<=1/4 and use
sqrt(3)(D_z tensor A+D_z^dagger tensor A^dagger). The two drive quadratures

  ||i[Vy,P_R]|| < 13/10.

Since ||Re B||,||Im B||<=1/4, with no commutation assumption,

  L_t^*(P_R tensor I) <= -I+(8/5+13/10)I/4

Thus the fully complex ball has survival prefactor 9/2 and rate 11/180.
The common field A=I+(1/4)exp(ikz) lies in this domain for arbitrary source

The result is a concrete recycling stability proof in specified windows.
It is not a global optimization, a statement about all positive gains, or a
qualification of an actual atom whose J=1 and J=2 levels are split.

3. Moving-subsystem consequence of the certificate

There is now a proved, rather than assumed, route from the static subsystem
to motion in this exact-degeneracy model. Let epsilon(s) be real, smooth,
unit length, s=t/T, and use a parallel dark frame D(s). The earlier exact
connection identity gives J^dagger J'=I_optical tensor D^T D'=0 in this
parallel frame. Let K(s) be the Hermitian frame generator on all 12 levels.
Write k=sup||K_RS|| and r=sup||K_RR||. If a=sup||epsilon'||, then

  k <= sqrt(2)a, r <= 2a.

Take M=9/2, lambda=2/15 for the Hermitian domain (lambda=11/180 for the complex
ball), and mu=lambda-2Mr/T>0. For an initial state entirely in S, let
v(t)=Tr[(P_R tensor I)rho_RR(t)]. The full recycled equations imply

  v' <= -mu v + (2sqrt(M)k/T)sqrt(v),
  sqrt(v(t)) <= 2sqrt(M)k/(mu T),
  p_R(t) <= 4M k^2/(mu^2 T^2).

Proof: the static terms obey the certificate, K_RR changes the Lyapunov
function by at most 2Mr v/T, and Cauchy-Schwarz for a positive block density
operator bounds the S/R forcing by 2sqrt(M)k sqrt(v)/T.

An additional structural fact makes this useful for the logical channel:
in the instantaneous co-rotating polarization basis, each Cartesian L_l has
either L_SS=0 or L_SR=0. Specifically L_z has L_SR=0, while L_x and L_y have
L_SS=0. In any unitary mixture of these field channels the SUM of the cross
terms still vanishes. Thus the unconditional S/S block receives no term
linear in rho_SR from photon recycling. Its unwanted contributions are
bounded by

  ||d rho_SS/dt - L_SS(rho_SS)||_1
      <= (2k/T)sqrt(p_R) + p_R.

Duhamel contraction in the S subsystem gives a full-time S/S error at most

  d_SS <= [4sqrt(M) k^2/mu + 4M k^2/mu^2]/T.

The difference of full output states from the parallel ideal evolution is
at most d_SS+p_R+2sqrt(p_R) in trace norm. The same inequalities hold with a
reference and retained source. Consequently they give a half-diamond upper
bound (d_SS+p_R+2sqrt(p_R))/2 for the joint modeled output, hence O(1/T).

For an instrument retaining every emission time and every polarization in
the CO-ROTATING basis, append that record to each jump and repeat the block
argument. The S dynamics is then a completely positive, trace-preserving
instrument; Cauchy-Schwarz summed over histories gives the same forcing
bounds. This instrument requires a known time-dependent polarization analyzer
following the control. That is an additional relative-control resource, not
source information. For fixed laboratory polarization records, cross-term
cancellation generally occurs only after summing the records, so the same
instrument bound has NOT been proved here. The unconditional channel bound
does hold. No rare-total-emission assumption occurs in either assertion.
The same bound is also not claimed for an undetected quantum-field output
whose coherent amplitudes are kept between distinct emission histories.

After the path, an explicitly scheduled common drive-off ramp preserves the
exact S factorization. Charge its duration tau_r. Optical settling for tau
adds at most sqrt(2) exp(-tau/2) to the return error, as in the previous
endpoint calculation. Actual duration is T+tau_r+tau. Loading, control
polarization, source coupling, and the maintained decay resource still have
to be supplied. This conservative proof establishes existence; its constants
do not establish useful gate times or an advantage over conventional gates.

4. Why the full gain norm becomes a resource with recycling

The earlier direct-outlet tripod theorem can use accretivity bounds on Re A
without bounding Im A. The recycled optical chain cannot inherit that claim.
Take the scalar source A=1+iM. Its real part is identically 1. A phase change
of b converts the drive in the 3-state chain to |A|=sqrt(1+M^2).

As M grows, the b/r doublet is split by Omega=sqrt(3)|A|. The q outlet is then
off-resonant from both dressed components. In the fast-drive interaction
picture the q-coupling oscillates; its finite-time average vanishes. The
averaged r->b recycling remains entirely in the b/r doublet. Starting at b,
the survival probability therefore tends to 1 for every fixed time as
M->infinity. This directly disproves any uniform exponential-escape bound
based only on the inherited fixed bounds Re A=1, Gamma, and Delta.

Finite-matrix eigenvalues confirm the expected strong-drive asymptotic

  escape gap ~ 2 Gamma Delta^2/(27 |A|^2).

This asymptotic coefficient is a diagnostic/perturbative expression; the
finite-time averaging argument is enough for the no-uniform-bound result.
For Delta=20, Gamma=1, M=1000 the computed gap is 2.9627179e-5 and gap*|A|^2
is 29.627209, approaching 2*20^2/27=29.629630.

5. Actual split manifolds: positive bounded gain does not make detuning safe

There is a stronger obstruction which persists after bounding the full gain
norm. Set

  Delta1=D, Delta2=2D,
  G=I+eta X_E, eta=1/4,
  H_E=(D/2)Z_E.

Then 3I/4 <= G <= 5I/4 for every D. The source is a two-level system. An
allowed initial source state is its upper Z level. This is a valid test of a
claim uniform over unprepared source states, not a proposal requiring that
source preparation. A maximally mixed source gives a related obstruction.

For any logical v in span{x,y}, source energy can resonantly convert

  |g,v> tensor |up>  <->
  |J1,v> tensor |down>,

  |J1,v>=(|z> tensor v - v tensor |z>)/sqrt(2).

The Rabi matrix element is eta sqrt(3/2), independent of D. This is an exact
energy resonance of the source-plus-atom system; the bare optical detuning
does not describe it. In the D->infinity secular limit, other unwanted
carrier/sideband terms vanish while this one remains.

The first J1 photon has two kinds of fine outcomes:

  L_z |J1,v> = v/sqrt(2),
  L_x |J1,v> = -v_x |g,z>/sqrt(2),
  L_y |J1,v> = -v_y |g,z>/sqrt(2).

With probability 1/2 it returns the unknown logical vector unchanged. With
probability 1/2 it leaves the fixed bright state and transfers the logical
label to the x/y photon. Complete resonant J0 optical pumping subsequently
returns bright to the code. Unconditioned return is maximally mixed by the
x/y symmetry. After complete pumping and return, the logical channel is

  E(rho)=(1/2)rho+(1/2)I_2 Tr(rho)/2.

For a maximally entangled logical/reference input its trace distance from
the ideal output is 3/8. This statement can be taken as the ordered limit
D->infinity followed by long pumping/return. A joint family with pumping
time increasing sufficiently slowly compared with D has the same limit.
It is not a claim that one may take infinite pumping time at fixed D.

For eta=1/4 the resonant no-photon amplitudes are explicitly

  c_g(t)=exp(-t/4)[cos(t/sqrt(32))+sqrt(2)sin(t/sqrt(32))],
  c_e(t)=-i sqrt(3)exp(-t/4)sin(t/sqrt(32)).

Their squared norm is at most 9 exp(-t/2). A drive duration 50 makes incomplete
first-emission probability below 1.3e-10 in the resonant limit. Recovery
photons and their durations still have to be retained; the full numerical
model below retains them.

5a. Exact crossover for a fixed, finite complement splitting

The obstruction does not require Delta2/Delta1=2. Keep the actual splitting
delta fixed, take Delta1=D, Delta2=D+delta, and retain H_E=(D/2)Z. In the
large-D limit, before its first photon each logical vector follows the
same three-level amplitude problem

  g=|g,v,up>, r=|z,v,down>, t=|v,z,down>,

  H_first = [[0,kappa,0],
             [kappa,delta/2,delta/2],
             [0,delta/2,delta/2]],
  kappa=sqrt(3) eta g0,
  N=-i H_first-(Gamma/2)diag(0,1,1).

The r photon is logically identical on every v. The t photon transfers v
to the field and leaves bright. The integrated harmful first-photon
probability is EXACTLY

  B = delta^2(delta^2+2kappa^2+Gamma^2)
      /[2delta^4+(4kappa^2+3Gamma^2)delta^2+(2kappa^2+Gamma^2)^2].

This formula follows by integrating the no-photon density equations to the
first jump, or by solving N^dagger X+X N=-Gamma|t><t| and taking X_gg. Using
Gamma=1, put d=delta/2, u=kappa, and let R,T be the integrated populations
of r,t, x,y,z the integrated coherences gr,gt,rt. The integrated equations
give R+T=1, Im x=1/(2u), T=2d Im z,

  z=-iu y-id(2T-1),
  0=-iu z+id x+(id-1/2)y,
  Re x=-2d(Im x+Im y).

Eliminating these coherences yields

  T = [4d^2(2d^2+u^2+1/2)]
      /[2u^2(2d^2+u^2+1/2)+(1+4d^2)(4d^2+u^2+1/2)],

which is B above after restoring Gamma. The delta=0 case follows directly
or by continuity. No recycling is omitted: this is an exact instrument
calculation UP TO the first emission, and the subsequent optical recovery
is treated as the full branch-dependent process described above.

The natural-return channel is (1-B)id+B depolarization. After the stipulated
Cartesian polarization history has been measured, arbitrary recovery using
that classical record has optimal Bell-input error B/2, reached by replacing
the bad branch with dephasing. Natural
return has Bell-input error 3B/4. The mixed-source values are one half of
these errors. For Gamma=g0=1, eta=1/4, and the inherited splitting delta=2,

  B=1376/3129=.439757110898,
  natural return error=344/1043=.329817833174,
  best fixed-Cartesian-record recovery error=688/3129=.219878555449.

As delta->0, B~delta^2/(2kappa^2+Gamma^2). As a split line becomes well
resolved, B->1/2. Thus the exact-degeneracy cancellation and the resolved-
line information loss are connected by one explicit response law. Bare
detuning D does not appear in this law because the active source supplies
the resonant energy. The companion code compares this formula with an
independent continuous-Lyapunov solve across nine splittings; the largest
absolute discrepancy is below 9.4e-15.

6. Recovery comparison after the FIXED Cartesian record is measured

The 3/8 unconditioned error is not the correct optimum if all Cartesian
emission records are available. After the harmful x/y outcome, the recorded
label is known. Conditional resetting to that recorded label changes the
bad branch from complete depolarization into dephasing. The resulting
channel is

  E_feedback=(1/2)id+(1/2)D_xy,

with Bell-input trace error 1/4. This is optimal AFTER that fixed Cartesian
polarization measurement: in the harmful branch (probability 1/2), the atomic/source system
has lost entanglement with an external reference. Any recovery acting on
that system and a classical record leaves that branch separable. A separable
two-qubit state has Bell fidelity at most 1/2. Hence total Bell fidelity is
at most 3/4 and trace error is at least 1/4. Preparing the recorded label
attains the bound. Later smooth drive-off and optical settling cannot undo
this information loss, although they must be charged when performing an
actual cycle.

An initially maximally mixed source places half its weight in the resonant
upper level. The limiting natural-return error is 3/16; the lower bound with
optimal fixed-Cartesian-record recovery is 1/8. Thus the failure is not confined
to an unusually pure source state.

This lower bound does not cover changing the photon analyzer BEFORE its
outcome is classicalized. The following two calculations show why a general
phrase such as "classical environment-assisted recovery" would overstate it.
Detected erasures can also be handled by a larger error-correcting code;
that code's operations and exposure must be charged separately.

6a. Optimizing an arbitrary measurement of the FIRST emission

Before bright-state recovery, the first-photon limiting isometry is

  V|psi> = sqrt(1-B)|psi>_code |z>
           +sqrt(B)|bright>|psi>_xy.

The field vectors include normalized temporal wavepackets. They are mutually
orthogonal by polarization, and the temporal wavepacket within the x/y pair
is identical. The source is in the same lower state in both terms. Thus
there are three orthogonal environmental modes in this particular isometry;
no source flag is being silently dropped. An arbitrary measurement that
coherently mixes these modes is a stronger operation than detecting the
original Cartesian record.

For a rank-one environment POVM vector u_j, let x_j=|u_j,z|^2 and
y_j=||u_j,xy||^2. Completeness gives sum x_j=1, sum y_j=2. The conditional
atomic Kraus effect is

  K_j^dagger K_j=(1-B)x_j I+B|u_j,xy><u_j,xy|.

Optimal polar correction on the atomic code-plus-bright qutrit gives
conditional Bell overlap (Tr sqrt(K_j^dagger K_j))^2/4. Refining a POVM to
rank-one outcomes cannot hurt the optimum. Therefore

  F_e <= 1/2 + (1/2) sum_j sqrt[(1-B)x_j((1-B)x_j+B y_j)]
      <= [1+sqrt(1-B^2)]/2.

The final inequality is Cauchy-Schwarz. A three-mode Fourier analyzer has
y_j=2x_j and attains equality. Its polar-corrected qubit channel is

  p_I id+p_X X(.)X+p_Y Y(.)Y,
  p_I=(1+sqrt(1-B^2))/2,
  p_X=p_Y=(1-sqrt(1-B^2))/4.

Thus the optimal Bell-input trace error for this FIRST-emission resource is

  [1-sqrt(1-B^2)]/2.

At B=1/2 this is .0669872981, rather than the fixed-Cartesian-record .25.
At B=1376/3129 it is .0509416287. This optimization grants a coherent
three-mode photon analyzer and arbitrary conditional qutrit polar control;
neither is supplied by a bare photon counter. The bound applies only when
the recovery resource is this first-emission isometry. It is NOT a lower
bound after allowing additional coherently collected recovery radiation.

6b. Full recovery radiation permits perfect environment-assisted correction

After complete J0 bright pumping, each harmful Cartesian history has a
logical Kraus operator

  K_{k j alpha}=sqrt(B/2)c_alpha |k><j|, k,j in {x,y}.

Here j is the first harmful photon's polarization, k the final return
photon's polarization, and alpha the common timing and intermediate z-photon
history, normalized by sum_alpha |c_alpha|^2=1 (or the corresponding
continuous-history integral). The scalar waveform is independent of j,k
by the exact Cartesian symmetry. The field can therefore retain the two
polarization qubits coherently even though all intermediate recycling and
waiting times are present.

A Bell-basis measurement of the four (k,j) field labels unitarily mixes
these Kraus operators into

  sqrt(B/4)c_alpha {I,X,Y,Z}.

Pauli feedforward makes each corrected Kraus operator a scalar identity.
The good sqrt(1-B)I branch is already the identity. Thus an ideal joint
measurement of the harmful and recovery photons, followed by known local
Pauli correction, EXACTLY recovers the logical input in this limiting model.
It does not require interference between the one-photon good sector and
the multiphoton harmful sector. The operation is an explicit instance of
environment-assisted random-unitary correction, not a new impossibility
result; see Gregoratti and Werner below.

Physically, the first harmful photon carries the input qubit and the final
J0 return photon is entangled with the recovered atom. Their joint Bell
measurement teleports the information back. The required coherent optical
collection, polarization-preserving storage/routing, joint photon analyzer,
and feedback are additional physical operations. Classical Cartesian
photocounts acquired earlier cannot implement that analyzer retroactively.
A passive detector specification does not establish a deterministic Bell
measurement, usable photon-storage duration, or comparable success rate.

Recycling is a resource as well as a cost here. Each resonant J0 emission
returns to code with probability 2/3, so a harmful event requires on average
3/2 additional recovery photons. In the isolated resolved-line limit the
mean first-emission time is 2/Gamma+Gamma/(2kappa^2), and the mean bright
return time is (3/2)[2/Gamma+Gamma/(4g0^2)]. These are means, not deadline
guarantees; a physical cycle must also budget the waiting-time tail and
optical memory exposure. A further exact operator calculation in
STATIC_SPLIT_PHOTON_PAIR_MEMORY.txt extends the fixed-axis pair recovery
identity to arbitrary common quantum sources in the stipulated outlet
model. No microscopic collection/storage/Bell-analyzer device is qualified.

environment_recovery_checks.py independently verifies the Fourier/polar
first-photon optimum and the full-recovery Bell/Pauli identity. These
comparators materially narrow the obstruction: positive gain and bare
detuning do not supply passive protection or fixed-record recoverability,
while suitably controlled access to the quantum radiation can restore the
information. A complete architecture comparison must specify which
radiation operation is actually available. The companion fixed-axis theorem
also makes the stopping issue explicit: a finite control latency/ramp after
a completed pair may leave an odd photon after settling, which requires
stored-photon repumping and another completed pair. It supplies a uniform
small-ramp-area probability bound rather than assuming free stopping.

8. Organizing consequence and comparison to established work

The relevant elimination denominators involve the WHOLE admitted source:

  Delta_j + ad_{H_E} - i Gamma/2,

acting on the coupling operator. Equivalently, a source matrix element which
lowers source energy by omega sees detuning Delta_j-omega. Here
||[H_E,G]||=eta D grows even though G's spectrum never changes. A local
source-frequency restriction, a controlled derivative/commutator estimate,
or a tail bound on the coupled source transitions is needed before replacing
the resolvent by 1/Delta_j. A bound on bare optical detuning, a positive gain,
or a highly mixed source state alone cannot supply this control.

The counterexample requires that the admitted source domain contains the
energy-lowering transition D and permits population in its upper state.
A fixed low-energy envelope or a local bandwidth restriction may exclude
that state, and is therefore a substantive resource rather than a loophole
in the calculation. A single fixed source domain can also contain the
whole adverse family: take a direct sum of two-level blocks with gaps D_n
and let G be the direct sum of I+eta X. Its global gain remains uniformly
positive and bounded, while each admitted upper block produces the relevant
resonance at D=D_n. With a fixed mean-energy envelope, the allowed high-block
population must instead be charged in the resulting error estimate.

This is an organizing constraint on the domain of an effective theory. It
is not a universal prohibition of warm auxiliaries. On the exact-degenerate
branch, the fine jumps act identically on every logical vector and the
source resonance is harmless to logical information. On the actual split
branch it reaches a fine emission which transfers logical information.

Conventional reservoir engineering already provides invariance, attraction,
and dissipative holonomy tools. The explicit common Lyapunov witness here
adds an auditable robustness calculation for this particular nonstationary
quantum-source model; those established tools may reuse it. It establishes
no priority or general performance advantage. Likewise known-frequency hot
ion gates provide a serious conventional baseline: independence from the
motional state is not ignorance of the coupled transition frequencies.
Calibrated controls using the same source information may avoid or exploit
sidebands. The present counterexample invalidates an information-free
detuning claim, not those informed schemes.
