ABSORBING UNITARY TRANSPORT WITH AN ACTIVE QUANTUM SOURCE
Research checkpoint, 2026-10-31. Not a qualified microscopic device.

1. QUESTION AND RESULT

Can the inverse-error time of the inherited lossy dark gate be changed without
learning or preparing its active quantum source? Yes, at the circuit-Hamiltonian
level, if the irreversible outlet carries a complete copy of the logical space.
The useful event is then absorption, rather than survival without absorption.
There is an explicit two-local data/helper construction for a two-qubit
entangler. It still requires microscopic common-gain engineering; this file
does not promote that joint physical contract into an earned device.

Logical system L, helper R=span{g,e,f}, source E. Initially R is PREPARED in g;
L and E may be arbitrary and entangled with an external reference. For any
fixed logical unitary M, consider

 H = I_LR tensor H_E(t) + |e><e| tensor D_E(t)
     + M_L tensor |e><g| tensor A_E(t)
     + M_L^dagger tensor |g><e| tensor A_E(t)^dagger,

 J = sqrt(kappa) I_L tensor |f><e| tensor I_E.             (1)

There is no coupling out of f other than H_E. All optical fine channels may
instead be I_L tensor |f_mu><e| tensor B_mu, with separate absorbing flags,
provided sum B_mu^dagger B_mu=kappa I_E. Merely having the same total decay
effect is NOT enough if individual B_mu act nontrivially on L.

Assume g I <= Re A_E(t) <= h I, g>0, D=D^dagger, ||D||<=d.
No commutation of A,D,H_E is assumed. No source-state or speed knowledge is
needed. The finite-dimensional proof is exact; infinite-dimensional cases
need their own domain and well-posedness qualification.

Define the unitary change of frame

 G = I_L tensor |g><g| + M_L tensor (|e><e|+|f><f|).

Then G^dagger H G=I_L tensor h_RE and G^dagger J G=I_L tensor j_RE.
Consequently, the complete process is a conjugated source/helper process,
including every emission history. In particular, each history ending at f
has a fine operation M_L tensor B_history. No optical record need be collected
for the gate. Emission times, modes and the active source can all be retained.
For initially correlated L/E inputs, conditioning on a particular history
can still steer L through the source factor B_history. "Exact" here refers
to the complete factorized branch operation, not to a normalized reduced
logical state after conditioning. The unconditional channel bound below
uses a trace-preserving source channel and applies to arbitrary correlations.
The emission spectrum may depend on E, but it is identical on every logical
vector, because the whole source/helper/bath Hamiltonian has the same gauge
factorization when its physical bath coupling acts on the helper only.

The rate estimate below uses the stated Markov loss. The exact f-sector
factorization itself also applies to a microscopic bath if its full
Hamiltonian has this gauge factorization; irreversibility and a settling rate
would then need a separate proof.

2. EXPONENTIAL COMPLETION AND COMPLETE CHANNEL BOUND

In the transformed frame, the no-emission g/e equations are exactly the
inherited fixed-bright equations:

 u'=-i A^dagger v,
 v'=-i A u -(kappa/2+iD)v,

after moving to the H_E interaction picture. The proved constants are

 alpha=min{1, kappa/[2h+(kappa/2+d)^2/g]},
 C=sqrt[(2+alpha)/(2-alpha)],
 lambda=alpha*g/[2(2+alpha)].

The no-emission propagator satisfies ||K_no(t,s)||<=C exp[-lambda(t-s)].
Thus, starting from g,

 p_survive(T) <= p(T):=min{1,C^2 exp[-2lambda T]}          (2)

uniformly over all source/data/reference inputs. There can be at most one
emission, because f is absorbing.

Let Phi_T be the full transformed helper/source channel, with helper input g.
Its successful, emitted component has output f, and its zero-emission
component is supported on g/e. The physical channel and the ideal channel

       M_L (.) M_L^dagger tensor Phi_T

coincide on every emitted history. Their two no-emission CP maps each have
diamond norm at most p(T). Their difference therefore has half-diamond norm
at most p(T). This is a joint bound retaining the source and helper and the
photon-counting record:

  (1/2)||E_T - (M channel) tensor Phi_T||_diamond <= p(T). (3)

If the entire radiation field is coherently retained, zero- and one-photon
amplitudes need not be incoherent, and the generic bound is of order sqrt(p),
not p. It is still exponential in T. For the field-traced/recorded instrument,
T >= [log(C^2/epsilon)]/(2lambda) suffices.

The source need NOT undergo its free evolution: Phi_T includes the unknown
backaction of driving and decay. This is intentional. The logical gate is
decoupled from that backaction, and global source bounds permit later reuse.
Any proposed domain restricted by energy still needs an actual-history
envelope. Other live logical carriers must not be hidden inside E.

3. EXPLICIT LOCAL AND PAIR HAMILTONIANS

For Hermitian A, write sigma_x=|e><g|+|g><e| and
sigma_y=-i|e><g|+i|g><e| on the helper's g/e doublet. With this convention,
if M=C+iS with Hermitian C,S, the coupling in (1) is

       C tensor sigma_x tensor A - S tensor sigma_y tensor A.

Two commuting Hermitian involutions P,Q satisfy

       M_theta=cos(theta)P+i sin(theta)Q,
       M_theta^dagger M_theta=I.                         (4)

For P=Z_A and Q=Z_B, the physical interaction is the SUM

 cos(theta) Z_A tensor sigma_x tensor A
 - sin(theta) Z_B tensor sigma_y tensor A.               (5)

These are two data/helper pair interactions, not a directly stipulated
two-data-qubit gate. They must genuinely share the same operator A. For
clarity, "pair" here counts data and helper factors only: if E is a separate
physical quantum subsystem, each displayed term also acts on E, and strict
microscopic two-body locality has NOT been proved. A real coupler must derive
that common modulation without installing an extra multibody interaction
oracle. For
non-Hermitian A the decomposition has corresponding source quadratures;
the factorized formula (1), not (5), is the definition.

At theta=pi/4,

 M = (Z_A+iZ_B)/sqrt(2)
   = exp(i*pi/4) S_A S_B^dagger CZ,

where S=diag(1,i). Hence S_A^dagger S_B M is CZ up to global phase.
For local P=Z,Q=I,

 M_theta=diag(exp(i theta),-exp(-i theta)).

theta=pi/4 gives exp(i*pi/4) S;
theta=3pi/8 gives exp(i*3pi/8) T, T=diag(1,exp(i*pi/4)).
The identity term is a helper drive with the SAME unknown gain A.
For a real reflection F_beta, taking M=F_beta directly gives the reflection.
In particular H may be used as M. Thus an explicit compiler uses one transport
for H, one for T, three for CZ, and five for CNOT. Relative amplitudes, signs
and quadratures remain calibrated controls. The old realification compiler
remains valid; this construction supplies complex controls rather than
silently obtaining them from real controls.

The physical gap is now sharp: engineer (5), the ordinary helper drive needed
for local phase gates, and data-independent e->f decay on one source domain.
No atomic level scheme, rotating-wave window or engineered-bath bandwidth has
been demonstrated here. This is more explicit than a unitary-jump oracle,
but is still a circuit-Hamiltonian mechanism.

4. LOADING, RETURN, DURATION AND COMPOSITION

Only E is unprepared. Helper g and the populated logical Hilbert space are
prepared resources. Logical states do not leak to a/e/f, because these are
tensor-factor helper states, not the data's own levels. Data is therefore
present in a complete d-dimensional manifold at g, e and f.

After turning off the coupling with a finite envelope preserving M, a local
reset may damp both e and f to g at rate r. With exact reset channels
sqrt(r)|g><e| and sqrt(r)|g><f| acting on the helper only, the reset time to
diamond error delta is at most r^{-1} log(2/delta^2) by the usual
amplitude-damping-to-replacement bound. Its outlet and control switching are
additional maintained resources. A fresh helper can replace this reset but
then helper preparation and supply must be counted.

During a drive ramp M is fixed, so its exact gauge remains valid. Completion
can be estimated using a constant plateau; plateau, ramps and reset all count
toward duration. Reset before switching from M_j to M_{j+1} is essential:
an excited helper with the prior logical frame cannot be treated as loaded g.

For N gates, global source bounds and explicit helper resets give a simple
sum bound. At common error allowance epsilon/N for each gate and each reset,
total plateau plus reset duration is O[N log(N/epsilon)], apart from routing,
readout, loading, exposure to extraneous noise and control error. This is a
construction within (1), not a fault-tolerance result or a device advantage.

If an added Hamiltonian mismatch has norm <=delta_H during duration T, the
generic half-diamond debit is <=integral delta_H dt. Thus waiting longer does
not remove a fixed implementation mismatch. Relative phase/gain errors can
destroy the unitary factorization and produce an accuracy floor.

4.1 A precise connection to non-Markovian fault-path criteria

Suppose each complete, locally supported gate location INCLUDING helper
reset has an ALL-INPUT bound

 E_j = U_j tensor Phi_j + Delta_j,
 ||Delta_j||_diamond <= eta_j,

where Phi_j is a source CPTP map and the same source may be retained at every
location. For any specified r faulty locations, sum the circuit expansion
over arbitrary faults at all other locations. This sum equals a time-ordered
product with Delta_j at those r locations and actual CPTP maps E_k at the
others. Its diamond norm is at most the product of the r eta_j values.
No independence, source reset or Markov assumption is needed. A constant
complete-location error can therefore satisfy a conventional local
non-Markovian fault-path strength condition.

This lemma does NOT by itself prove a fault-tolerance threshold for this
hardware: preparation, measurement, idle evolution, routing, parallel
scheduling and a fault-tolerant circuit construction must also be qualified.
It does identify the precise missing step in applying threshold reasoning to
the earlier CODE-RESTRICTED dark theorem. Its Delta_j could output leakage,
and the next restricted estimate could not be multiplied on that input.
Here L remains a full tensor-factor data Hilbert space throughout, and an
all-state helper reset can remove that specific obstruction. The source's
unpreparedness alone is not the obstruction. Terhal and Burkard,
PRA 71, 012336 (2005), arXiv:quant-ph/0402104, and Aliferis and Terhal,
QIC 7, 139-156 (2007), arXiv:quant-ph/0511065, are the relevant established
non-Markovian and leakage-fault comparisons.

5. WHY THIS DOES NOT CONTRADICT THE ABSORBING-FLAG CONVERSE

The earlier converse had an untouched logical spectator, a rank-one excited
level, and an absorbing flag which represented loss of the logical system.
Here EVERY logical vector is transported through a complete copy of L, and
the absorbing output still carries the whole logical state. An emission is
the desired gate event, not a failure. The rank/which-state distinction is the
physical resource responsible for the changed time scaling.

For an inaccessible source and mere record-dependent UNITARY feedback, a
branch can implement an exact logical unitary on all logical/source inputs
only if its fine operations factor as U_branch tensor B_fine on the code.
Outcome probabilities independent of the logical state are insufficient:
a global unitary can still entangle L with E. The two-local common A in (5)
enforces the stronger factorization. Independent positive gains do not.

6. GENERAL GRAPH FORM

Assign a unitary U_v to every helper vertex v and let G=sum_v U_v tensor |v><v|.
A transition edge u->v has logical factor U_v U_u^dagger. If every Hamiltonian
edge and every fine bath edge has this form, conjugation by G removes the
logical system from the entire auxiliary dynamics. A loaded vertex r with
U_r=I and successful absorption at t applies U_t. Backtracking cancels
without commutation assumptions on source coefficients. On a graph with
cycles, consistency requires the ordered unitary product around each cycle
to be identity (up to a source-independent global phase that can be assigned
consistently). Nontrivial loop products generally create path dependence.

This is an organising gauge identity, not a claim that arbitrary logical
edge labels can be engineered for free. Construction (5) matters because its
entangling label is a sum of two explicit pairwise couplings.

7. PRIOR ART AND COMPARISON

Unitary operations attached to dissipative clock motion are established:
Verstraete, Wolf and Cirac, Nature Physics 5, 633-636 (2009),
https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.1447 . Their universal dissipative computing model is
the appropriate conceptual comparison; dissipation-powered gates themselves
are not new here.

Gautier, Mirrahimi and Sarlette, PRX Quantum 4, 040316 (2023),
https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.00760 and doi:10.1103/PRXQuantum.4.040316,
especially Section VIII, propose a discrete dissipative gate using an
ancillary qubit that accepts precisely one photon. They explicitly discuss
exponential gate-error suppression and the implementation limitations for
controlled gates. The present active-source uniform estimate and two-local
unitary excitation matrix should be compared with that construction.

Lim, Beige and Kwek, PRL 95, 030505 (2005),
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0408043 , and Lim et al., PRA 73, 012304 (2006),
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0508218 , give strong repeat-until-success
optical comparisons. Our absorbing flag avoids requiring photon collection,
but trades for a fully information-preserving helper outlet and shared-gain
couplings. No practical superiority follows without an admitted device.

Conventional calibrated control with exactly the same Hamiltonian resources
can also use this gauge, or may execute a faster coherent pulse if it knows
the actual source dynamics. The claim is uniform reliability using only
coarse gain bounds, not beating an equally informed optimizer by assertion.
