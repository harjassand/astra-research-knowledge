Frangella, Tropp, Udell (2023), Randomized Nystrom Preconditioning.
https://tropp.caltech.edu/papers/FTU23-Randomized-Nystrom-SIMAX.pdf
DOI 10.1137/21M1466244. Relevant: Theorem1.1, section5.4, Theorem5.5, AppendixC.
Already provides matrix-free preconditioning, adaptive sketch size, residual
power estimation and actual conjugate-gradient correction. Its rank scale is
effective dimension. Our investigation does not claim those mechanisms as new.

Kressner, Lam (2024), Randomized low-rank approximation of parameter-dependent
matrices, Numerical Linear Algebra with Applications31(6):e2576.
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/nla.2576
Relevant: section2.2, Algorithms4-5, Theorems4,7,10,11.
Already retains constant input sketches and cached component actions for affine
families. Main bounds concern integrated approximation error, with additional
uniform guarantees under regularity assumptions. Affine reuse itself is prior
art, not the potentially distinctive part of the present determinant theorem.

Kressner, Latz, Massei, Ullmann (2020), Certified and fast computations with
shallow covariance kernels, Foundations of Data Science2(4):487-512.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.09187
https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/fods.2020022
Relevant: Algorithm2, section3.3, conclusion.
Already constructs adaptive common column sets, affine caches and residual
certificates across a supplied finite parameter surrogate. Its task is
covariance approximation and Gaussian-law error. This rules out claiming
adaptive certified common approximation alone as the new contribution.

Calandriello, Lazaric, Valko (2016), Analysis of Nystrom method with sequential
ridge leverage scores.
https://proceedings.mlr.press/r14/calandriello16a.html
Relevant retaining comparator: incremental kernel sketches and intermediate-time
guarantees. Growing data kernels and changing task operators are different
source contracts; their names alone do not prove separation.

Sun, Gomez, Schmidhuber (2012), On the Size of the Online Kernel Sparsification
Dictionary.
https://people.idsia.ch/~sun/doc/icml12-oks.pdf
Determinant accounting for accepted dictionary novelty has clear precedent.

Schafer, Owhadi (2024), Sparse Recovery of Elliptic Solvers from Matrix-Vector
Products.
https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/22M154226X
Important stronger scientific solver comparator when its geometric and elliptic

Galanti, Shah, Fortgang, Hazan (2026), Spectral Distillation: From Nonlinear
Dynamics to Linear State-Space Models.
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.05416
Relevant: Theorem3.1, AppendicesA-B, conclusion. The full primary HTML was

and observer-complexity terms. Closed-loop rollout/control is left open.
It is useful prediction machinery; it does not acquire the inverse solver or
planner used here. The complete copyrighted primary text is not in this bundle.

ASSESSMENT

The inspected sources do not establish priority for the exact pathwise bound
on witnessed additions using a retrospective PSD envelope. That bound is a
candidate contribution of this investigation. A literature search is not a
novelty proof. Standard retained Gaussian methods also benefit from a fixed
common-envelope analysis. The actual compact Gaussian range comparator wins
our observed source-call comparison. Full matrix retention wins at the small
fixture's ambient size. Those outcomes govern the scientific interpretation.
