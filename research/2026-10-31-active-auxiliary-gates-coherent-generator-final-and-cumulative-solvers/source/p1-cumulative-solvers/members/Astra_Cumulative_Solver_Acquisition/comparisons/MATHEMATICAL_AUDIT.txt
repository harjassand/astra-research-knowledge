2. INDEPENDENT CHECK OF THE HIDDEN-ENVELOPE POTENTIAL

Let Q have orthonormal columns and K be positive semidefinite. Set
  E_Q(K)=K-KQ(Q^T KQ)^dagger Q^T K.
For every vector x,
  x^T E_Q(K)x = inf_{z in range(Q)} (x+z)^T K(x+z).
Consequently E_Q(K)>=0, E_Q(K)Q=0, and K<=C implies E_Q(K)<=E_Q(C).
Singular Q^T KQ causes no issue: positivity implies KQ kills the nullspace
of Q^T KQ, so the variational formula and generalized Schur complement hold.

Suppose x is a unit vector perpendicular to range(Q), and
  x^T E_Q(K)x > tau > 0.
The block determinant identity gives

 det(I+[Q x]^T C[Q x]/tau) / det(I+Q^T CQ/tau)
 = 1 + (x^T Cx - x^T CQ(tau I+Q^T CQ)^(-1)Q^T Cx)/tau
 >= 1 + x^T E_Q(C)x/tau
 >= 1 + x^T E_Q(K)x/tau
 > 2.

The first inequality uses that the regularized inverse is no larger than the
pseudoinverse on the relevant range. For any orthonormal final Q,
  det(I+Q^T CQ/tau) <= det(I+C/tau),
by eigenvalue interlacing. Therefore a sequence of such accepted new inputs
has length J < log(det(I+C/tau))/log(2), with weak <= a harmless relaxation.
This is pathwise and requires no access to C. It is useful only when an actual
common PSD envelope with small potential exists.

When C has finite rank r, each power witness E_Q(K)^q g belongs to range(C),
and the already retained exact Q does too. Thus J<=r independently. The final
bound may use min(n, rank(C), logdet(I+C/tau)/log(2)).

3. INDEPENDENT CHECK OF THE POWER WITNESS

Let E>=0 annihilate Q and g be a Rademacher sign vector in R^n. For integer
q>=ceil(log_4(2n)), define y=E^q g. If
  2 ||y||^2 > (mu/2)^(2q),
then the spectral moment inequality gives
  y^T E y / ||y||^2 >= (||y||^2/n)^(1/(2q)) > mu/4.
Since y is perpendicular to Q, its normalization is a valid potential witness
with tau=mu/4. This implication is deterministic.

For the acceptance direction, let v be a top unit eigenvector of E.
E[(v^Tg)^2]=1 and E[(v^Tg)^4]<=3. Paley-Zygmund gives
  Pr((v^Tg)^2>=1/2)>=1/12.
On this event, 2||E^qg||^2 >= ||E||^(2q). Hence if s independent fresh sign
probes all satisfy 2||E^qg||^2<=(mu/2)^(2q), a false acceptance when
||E||>mu/2 has probability at most (11/12)^s. This remains valid conditionally
on the earlier transcript only if the current K and Q are fixed before these
fresh probes. Future matrices may depend on earlier outcomes. Allocate failure
probability over every test, including repeated attempts in the same task.

4. ACCESSIBLE ENVELOPE COROLLARY AND ITS LIMIT

If C can actually be queried and 0<=K_theta<=C is a justified source property,
acquire one Q with ||E_Q(C)||<=mu/2. Then the same Q automatically satisfies
||E_Q(K_theta)||<=mu/2 for EVERY allowed theta, including later adaptive choices.
For P_theta=mu I+N_Q(K_theta),
  P_theta <= mu I+K_theta <= (3/2)P_theta.
The preconditioned condition number is at most 3/2 for every right-hand side.

No new validation probes are needed for later theta once the joint envelope
certificate holds. If K_theta=sum_j theta_j B_j with B_j>=0 and independently
justified bounds 0<=theta_j<=c_j, one constructive envelope is C=sum_j c_j B_j.
It costs the actual component matvecs to apply C and to cache B_j Q. Ordinary
retained Gaussian Nystrom with the SAME envelope, cache, and certificate is
fully entitled to this corollary. This may be the stronger practical choice.

Even an UNOBSERVED envelope can support the ordinary comparator's analysis.
For a fixed C independent of the initial Gaussian sketch, any event on which
||E_Omega(C)||<=mu/4 controls all future K<=C, including adaptive choices.
The mu/4 margin also makes the stated conservative sign-power test pass for
every sign vector, since 2n(mu/4)^(2q)<=(mu/2)^(2q). Ordinary Gaussian block
growth can therefore stop uniformly after a fixed-matrix Nystrom rank threshold
for C is reached, without ever querying C. The standard rank theorem, its
probability and conditioning must still be paid. If C has exact rank r and
its range is fixed independently of an r-column Gaussian Omega, then the
restriction of Omega to that range is invertible with probability one, so
N_Omega(K)=K for every K<=C simultaneously in exact arithmetic. This is a
particularly strong baseline. The pathwise witnessed determinant bound has
different quantifiers; hidden-envelope reuse alone does not establish a new
algorithmic advantage over Gaussian retention.

This corollary is an elementary consequence of standard shorted-operator
monotonicity; no priority is claimed. A finite set of successful past probes
alone does not establish the domination premise for unobserved future matrices.

5. COST AND PRECISION AUDIT

For T tasks and J additions, the indicated sign-power routine needs at most
T+J tests. Each uses q=O(log n) matvecs per sign vector and s=O(log(test/delta))
independent signs under a summable confidence allocation. The component cache
cost is mJ B_j-matvecs, with O(m n J) stored numbers. Reassembling a changing
K_theta Q costs O(m n j) arithmetic, and the projected factorization can cost
O(j^3) each task. These do not vanish when no new directions are discovered.
The final iterative solve still pays true operator products, residual checks,
low-rank preconditioner applications and numerical work.

Exact real arithmetic is not a floating-point certificate. Exact rational
matvecs and unnormalized rational basis columns permit a finite rational
implementation, but rank/pseudoinverse calculations, power iterates, divisions
and bit lengths must be charged. Approximate products require additional error
controls; the exact determinant argument does not automatically cover noisy
Q, approximate positivity or unvalidated residual norms. In particular,
projecting a true rank-one signal against an almost-correct noisy basis can
produce a normalized direction almost orthogonal to that true signal space.
An exact rank budget cannot simply be transferred to such a noisy process.

No uniform cheapness follows without a modest envelope. New orthogonal
rank-one operators on successive tasks require learning new directions, and
ordinary CG can win when each current right-hand side is easy. A measured
speedup against fresh solves is weaker than a comparison against retained
Gaussian sketches, recycling Krylov and task-specific classical solvers.
