FINITE RATIONAL RANDOMIZED PSD NORM TESTER
Companion to cumulative Nystrom acquisition. 31 October 2026.
No historical novelty claim; elementary power-method and moment argument.

Let R be a real symmetric positive-semidefinite d-by-d matrix, d>=1.
Given tolerance tau>0 and confidence delta in (0,1), choose integer p such
that 2^(2p)>=2d, and M with (11/12)^M<=delta. Both choices can be made by
integer/rational comparisons, without trusting numerical logarithms.
For M independent uniform sign vectors g in {-1,+1}^d compute q=R^p g.
If q=0, set rho_g=0; otherwise compute exactly

 rho_g=(q^T R q)/(q^T q).

Set rho=max_g rho_g. Then ALWAYS 0<=rho<=||R||, and

       Pr(||R||<=2rho) >= 1-delta.                          (P1)

Proof. If lambda=||R||=0 this is immediate. Fix a unit eigenvector u for
lambda. For Z=u^T g, EZ^2=1 and EZ^4=3-2 sum_i u_i^4<=3. Paley-Zygmund gives

 Pr(Z^2>=1/2) >= (1-1/2)^2 (EZ^2)^2/EZ^4 >=1/12.

Expand in an orthonormal eigenbasis. In q^T(R-lambda/2 I)q the contribution
from u is at least lambda^(2p+1)/4 on this event. Terms with eigenvalues
>=lambda/2 are nonnegative. All remaining terms together are bounded below by

 -(lambda/2)*(lambda/2)^(2p)*||g||^2
 =-d lambda^(2p+1)/2^(2p+1).

This is at least -lambda^(2p+1)/4 by the choice of p. Thus the quadratic form
is nonnegative and rho_g>=lambda/2. Independence of the M signs gives failure
at most (11/12)^M. The lower estimate rho<=lambda is just Rayleigh's bound.

Consequently a two-outcome acquisition step can be:
 - ACCEPT if 2rho<=tau, with the statement ||R||<=tau on the good event.
 - Otherwise retain a tested q with rho_q>tau/2.

Every rejection therefore provides a deterministic Rayleigh violation above
tau/2, even on failed probability paths. If R annihilates the currently
retained columns V, each nonzero q=R^p g is Euclidean orthogonal to range(V)
and hence independent. No approximate eigenvector or spectral gap is assumed.
If ||R||<=tau/2, acceptance is deterministic. At intermediate norms the test
may either accept validly or add an independent direction. If ||R||>tau,
false acceptance probability is <=delta.

The per-call operator cost is M*(p+1) applications of R, plus vector products
and sign generation O(M*d) random bits. p=O(log d), M=O(log 1/delta).
For adaptive calls use fresh signs and delta_s with sum delta_s<=delta_total.
The conditional bound applies to the matrix fixed by past data, giving joint
soundness of all accepted certificates. No union over future task parameters
is obtained automatically: each accepted parameter query must enter this
ledger, unless an independent uniform structural envelope was certified.

If R is rational and its matvec is exact, all q and rho are rational. Retain
unnormalized q: no irrational Euclidean basis normalization is necessary.
Nystrom matrices may use rational V with exact rank/pseudoinverse arithmetic;
orthonormal bases can remain proof-only. Matrix inversion, powers, integer bit
length, comparisons and random-bit costs must still be paid. This lemma gives
a finite construction, not a binary64 stability theorem.
