ASTRA: CUMULATIVE ACQUISITION OF CORRECTABLE SOLVERS
31 October 2026

QUESTION CHOSEN

Can failures while constructing a solver become permanent computational
knowledge, with total acquisition controlled by shared structure rather than
the number of later problems? Can the resulting computation attain finer
accuracy without relearning its representation to that final accuracy?

STRONGEST RESULT

For a sequence of finite positive-semidefinite operators A_t, paid actual
matrix-vector products can acquire a retained input space S. No useful basis,
rank or common spectral envelope is supplied. A finite randomized test either
certifies a constant-quality Nystrom preconditioner or produces a genuinely
new direction. Every rejected test has a deterministic quantitative witness.

For any PSD matrix C that dominates all task operators considered, the total
number J of acquired directions satisfies, on EVERY random path,

 J <= min(n, rank(C), log_2 det(I+4C/mu)),

where the equations have the form (lambda I+A_t)x=b and lambda>=mu>0.
C is a retrospective analysis quantity; the learner need not know or query
it. If the envelope has a decaying spectrum, the bound can be useful even
without an exact low-rank common subspace.

The confidence statement is separate: with probability at least 1-delta,
every issued operator certificate is sound, across the full adaptive history.
On that event, the explicit retained preconditioner P satisfies

 P <= lambda I+A_t <= (3/2)P.

Actual residual correction then halves energy-norm error per Richardson step,
uniformly for every later right-hand side. A finite iteration cap and an
actual-residual stopping check make failure charged and explicit. Ordinary
preconditioned CG is also implemented and is the stronger experimental choice.

The precision of the acquired approximation is relative to mu, not the final
solution epsilon. Finer solutions still pay true operator applications,
numerical precision, and O(log(1/epsilon)) correction steps.

WHAT IS SUPPLIED, WHAT IS ACQUIRED

Supplied: a finite numerical coordinate space; actual forward matrix-vector
access to fixed symmetric PSD operators; and the positive regularization
floor mu. The cached-family specialization additionally supplies named PSD
components B_j and the exact composition law A(theta)=sum theta_j B_j with
theta_j>=0. These source properties are not learned or validated by the test.

Acquired: input directions, their actual primitive responses, the small
task-specific matrices, the inverse-preconditioning operation, and valid
operator-domain certificates. Neither inverse answers nor a low-dimensional
eigenspace are supplied.

This is a scientific-computing source, not P1's scalar endpoint-return source.
One matrix-vector call returns n scalar coordinates and can itself be costly.
No conversion of the old physical experiment into this richer operation has
been established. QUADRATIC_TASK_PROOF.txt is preserved unchanged as separate
inherited capital.

UNIFORM DOMAIN REUSE

For a given parameter box 0<=theta<=u, the accessible operator C=sum u_j B_j
dominates the whole family. Acquiring on C once covers all later parameters
and all right-hand sides in that box. Cache B_j v for every acquired v; later
preconditioners can be assembled without new training responses. A growing
space only improves an old certificate. The executable also retains already
certified parameter boxes and reuses them when a later task is dominated.

The main proof allows arbitrary changing PSD operators. Affine caching is a
specialization that avoids rebuilding old matrix actions for each new task.
Without that supplied composition law, current A_t V must be queried and paid.

RESULTS ACTUALLY EXECUTED

1. Exact rational fixture: n=6, m=2, no hidden basis supplied to the learner.
   It acquired two directions through three tests. It paid 4 primitive cache
   calls, 188 certificate calls (including 4 additional audit calls), and
   9 solve calls: 201 total. Three later tasks needed no new training calls.
   Exact determinant growth and exact solution-error inequalities passed.
   Selected recorded rational denominators reached 9,973 bits; source vector
   entries reached 3,749 bits. This is finite, not cheap exact arithmetic.

2. Numerical comparison: n=192, m=3, 512 new parameter/right-hand-side tasks,
   relative energy-error target 1e-8. All acquisition and final residual calls
   are included. Persistent basis/cache storage excludes temporary arrays.

   Method                       Dimension   Primitive calls  Stored floats
   Residual-witness acquisition        8             5262           6144
   Retained Gaussian input sketch     32             6729          24576
   Retained Gaussian range method      8             5070           6144
   Ordinary CG                         -            15822              0
   Retained full matrix acquisition  192              576         110592

   The ordinary Gaussian range method is the preferred compact method on
   this fixture: it attains the same space size and fewer calls. Full matrix
   acquisition wins source-call count. Its dense storage/assembly/Cholesky
   work is paid; small timings do not establish an application advantage.
   All of these are binary64 diagnostics, not interval-certified runs of
   the exact probabilistic theorem.

3. Online diagnostic: 75 tasks, with a genuinely new direction at tasks
   1, 26 and 51. Three rejected and three accepted tests; 72 certificate
   reuses. Total: 9 cache + 576 certificate + 300 solve = 885 primitive calls.
   Global confidence numbering and actual costs are retained. The earlier
   implementation that needlessly repeated tests is preserved as a failure.

4. Independent finite checks cover systematic-noise indistinguishability,
   near-singular rotation, and a finite rational Rademacher PSD norm test.

INTERPRETATION

This is a complete conditional acquisition-and-execution theorem, with ordinary
proofs, explicit finite arithmetic and concrete limitations. The inspected
literature already contains adaptive Nystrom preconditioning, residual norm
tests, constant sketches across parameter families, affine caching, and
certified common approximations. The potentially distinctive theorem is the
pathwise determinant accounting for actual witnessed additions across a
changing PSD stream. Historical novelty for that exact statement is unresolved.

The work establishes neither a general theory of intelligence nor practical
superiority over strong retaining algorithms. The computational mechanism is
real within its source contract, and the strong ordinary methods are part of
the construction and comparison. No repository integration or publication was
performed; Capital can integrate these ordinary artifacts separately.
