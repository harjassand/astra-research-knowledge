FINITE-RECORD METROLOGY OF REQUIRED HIDDEN KINETIC CAPACITY
Solo derivation, 31 October 2026. A maximum rate, not mean activity or heat.

1. THE PHYSICAL OPERATION

Observe a fixed deterministic binary label of a stationary, homogeneous
hidden CTMC at a known independent Poisson clock of rate nu. No hidden
state count, rates, stationary probabilities or mixing bound is input.
Let kappa=max_x q_x (essential supremum on stationary support for Borel
models), the MAXIMUM total microscopic escape rate. It is not the
stationary mean jump activity, and not an entropy-production rate.

We construct a finite-confidence lower bound on kappa from one dependent
record, sharp on equilibrium two-state kinetics. This result needs no
physical reversal convention, because it makes no thermodynamic claim.
Stationary preparation, noninvasive deterministic state sensing and an
independent calibrated Poisson clock are still external premises.

Let D_N count changes between N consecutive inspected binary labels.
If kappa<=Lambda, set

       r=Lambda/(nu+2Lambda), c=1-r.

For EVERY such hidden model and every theta>=0,

        E exp(theta D_N)<=[c+r exp(theta)]^N.              (M1)

This is exactly the binomial mgf with parameter r, despite dependence
and arbitrary hidden state count. It is a stationary norm inequality,
not an assertion that the observed likelihood ratio is a martingale.

2. PROOF FROM ONE COHERENT GENERATOR

Write P=I+Q/Lambda and s=Lambda/(nu+Lambda). P is a contraction on L2(pi).
Its disk automorphism

             C=(P-sI)(I-sP)^(-1)

is a contraction: multiplying the defect on both sides gives

 I-C*C=(1-s^2)(I-sP*)^(-1)(I-P*P)(I-sP)^(-1)>=0.

The TRUE independent-inspection kernel B=nu(nu I-Q)^(-1) satisfies

                       B=cI+rC.                          (M2)

For F multiplication by the binary label f=+/-1, F*F=I. The edge tilt
for S_N=sum f(Y_j)f(Y_{j+1})=N-2D_N is

 K_lambda=cosh(lambda) B-sinh(lambda) FBF
         =c exp(-lambda)I+r[cosh(lambda)C-sinh(lambda)FCF].

Therefore ||K_lambda||<=c exp(-lambda)+r exp(lambda). Stationarity writes
the N-edge expectation as <1,K_lambda^N 1>, so operator norms give
E exp(-lambda S_N)<=[c exp(-lambda)+r exp(lambda)]^N.
Put theta=2lambda and cancel exp(-lambda N) to obtain(M1).
No conditional observed concentration, spectral gap, independence of
overlapping pairs, or hidden-clock observations were assumed.

SHARPNESS. For the equilibrium two-state CTMC with both rates Lambda,
P is the deterministic flip. Its C equals that flip, and B=cI+r flip.
The observed changes are iid Bernoulli(r), and(M1) is equality for every
N and theta. Thus ordinary two-state binomial calibration is attained.
The contribution is its validity for the WHOLE hidden CTMC class, not
a sampling advantage over the correctly specified two-state model.

3. CONFIDENCE INVERSION AND STOPPING

For observed d>Nr, Chernoff optimization in(M1) gives the monotone tail

       Pr{D_N>=d}<=exp[-N kl(d/N||r)].                    (M3)

Optimizing a MONOTONE tail threshold is not an uncharged selection among
e-values. For each observed count find a conservative probability lower
endpoint r_low with N kl(d/N||r_low)>=log(1/alpha), r_low<d/N.
If r_low<1/2, the resulting rate endpoint is

               kappa_low=nu r_low/(1-2r_low).             (M4)

It has fixed-horizon error<=alpha uniformly over all hidden CTMCs. If the
count rejects even r=1/2 at that risk, every finite cap is rejected under
the stated model; the implementation labels that event instead of claiming
an observed infinite physical rate. It can be a confidence failure or a
failure of the preparation/clock/sensing/model premises.

kinetic.py encloses all logs and bisects using exact rational arithmetic.
Ambiguous logarithmic comparisons cannot produce an unjustified rejection.
Its default probability tolerance is 2^(-32-bit_length(N)), which tends
to zero; a fixed user-chosen precision alone would not prove exact
asymptotic consistency.
At deterministic dense checkpoints j it spends alpha/[j(j+1)]. It reports
the latest lower endpoint between checkpoints. Union bound gives

             Pr{all reported kappa_low<=kappa}>=1-alpha.  (M5)

The same dynamics must continue. A target crossing is a valid stopping
decision. There is no future-entropy allowance: the target is one fixed
global rate parameter, not a random entropy total that can decrease.
The latest-endpoint convention also avoids retaining a rare early
overestimate forever when discussing almost-sure limits.

The iterable API keeps one bit, a count and a flip count, plus exact
arithmetic and optional checkpoint history. The JSON CLI loads its input.
Known clock rate nu is the physical time scale; uncalibrated count alone
does not determine a dimensional rate. Clock energy and calibration are
not supplied by the program.

4. A SOLVED COHERENT KINETIC INVERSE PROBLEM

For the COMPLETE stationary BALANCED binary Markov inspection law with
flip probability p in(0,1/2), the least possible maximum hidden escape rate
over ALL compatible bounded hidden generators is EXACTLY

                   kappa_min=nu p/(1-2p).                (M6)

The lower bound follows from(M1) or its mean; the equilibrium two-state
generator with that rate realizes the entire law. The latest checkpoint
band converges to(M6) almost surely on this law, by the ergodic theorem
and vanishing kl confidence radius. It cannot recover arbitrarily faster
invisible dynamics. No other honest band for the same class can have
limsup above(M6) with probability greater than its coverage risk, by
applying it to the attaining two-state model.

More generally a stationary ergodic inspected record with limiting flip
fraction p<1/2 gives this moment-based kinetic floor, which need not be
the complete-law minimum for a non-Markov observed process.

A balanced binary Markov inspection law with p>1/2 is incompatible with
ANY bounded hidden CTMC. The iid boundary p=1/2 is also not exactly
realizable with a nonconstant deterministic state label: (M2) implies
C1=E f(Y0)f(Y1)>=nu/(nu+2Lambda)>0 for every finite Lambda. Such a law
may arise as an infinite-bandwidth limit or from a different sensing
interface, not a finite-CTMC realization. At p=0, the reducible constant-
label realization has zero kinetic requirement; irreducible approximation
is a different exact-law question.

5. ORDINARY COMPARATORS AND WHAT IS ACQUIRED

A simpler conditional hazard argument only gives flip probability at most
Lambda/(nu+Lambda): a label change needs a hidden jump before inspection.
Its valid CI maps a binomial probability lower endpoint to
nu p_low/(1-p_low). kinetic.py implements it as the row_hazard comparator,
with the same record, checkpoint schedule and error allocation. This
comparator can apply to weaker/nonstationary hypotheses. The sharp ordinary
two-state analysis gives the SAME calibration as(M4); no computational or
sample advantage over that correctly specified model is claimed.

The fresh controls use one stationary preparation per record, source rate4
and null rate1, clock nu=1, identical binary readout and observation counts.
Both physical generators obey detailed balance and have entropy production
ZERO. They are a kinetic test, not evidence of dissipation. The source's
observed flips are iid with p=4/9, proved from the exact resolvent. Its
finite-power calculation uses this source fact only, never as null input.

The simulator draws competing hidden-update/inspection event types and
marginalizes their exponential waiting times. It acquires synthetic ordered
labels and latent update counts, not realized continuous elapsed times.
The receipt reports expected model duration N/nu, all PRNG bits, event counts,
CPU time and this timing marginalization explicitly. No physical clock,
stationary preparation, sensing calibration or device is acquired.

N185 is respected: dormant states can arbitrarily reduce stationary MEAN
activity or dissipation while preserving active paths, but they do not
reduce the MAXIMUM escape rate of the active states. The new target is
the least kinetic capacity compatible with the observations, not a renamed
global mean. The complete generator entropy minimization and its open
finite-path-dual transfer retain their separate scopes.
