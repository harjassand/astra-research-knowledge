1. THE PHYSICAL QUESTION AND THE RESOURCE BEING SUPPLIED

Given partial observations, what is the least entropy production of ONE
stationary time-homogeneous continuous-time Markov dynamics that explains
ALL the observed times? The root minima in virtual_midpoint/ do not answer
this: separately chosen roots need not form a semigroup. This construction
replaces those roots by one common uniformization flow.

Fix a supplied finite escape-rate cap Lambda>0. The class consists of
stationary all-even pure-jump Markov generators Q on standard-Borel hidden
spaces with total exit rate<=Lambda and a fixed finite-alphabet label.
All finite CTMCs satisfying the cap are included. Reducible models are
allowed. The attaining model below is not claimed finite-state. Its precise
finite-state operational interpretation is established in Section6.

The cap is externally supplied UPPER kinetic information, not a mixing
bound. It is NOT estimated from an observation record. Adjoining an
arbitrarily fast independent
reversible hidden bit leaves every observed law and the entropy rate
unchanged, while making the hidden escape rate arbitrarily large. No
passive observation, even an exact continuous observed law, supplies an
upper cap on all compatible hidden dynamics.

Use a known independent Poisson INSPECTION clock of rate nu>0, and initially
observe its label at a stationary start. Only ordered labels need be retained
for the exact-law theorem. Uniformization has a different clock of rate
Lambda. The two clocks MUST NOT be identified:

 P=I+Q/Lambda,
 B_nu=nu(nu I-Q)^(-1)=nu[(nu+Lambda)I-Lambda P]^(-1).       (G1)

P is the hidden uniformization kernel. B_nu is the actual kernel between
independent inspections. Confusing B_nu with I+Q/nu is generally false.
The receiver includes an exact two-state counterexample with both clock
rates equal2. Neither hidden uniformization events nor virtual self-jumps
are acquired observations.

2. A SINGLE-INSPECTION-CLOCK ALIAS-FREE THEOREM

Within the union of bounded-generator classes, the COMPLETE ordered label
law at one known independent Poisson clock determines the complete
continuous-time observed law. Conversely that continuous law determines
the inspected law. Thus the compatible hidden-generator sets, and their
least physical entropy rates, are identical under the two exact-law inputs.
This is not reconstruction of individual hidden states or invisible excess
dissipation. Exact-law invertibility is not a finite-data stability claim.

The existing fixed-time observation interface can ALSO be used directly:
GRID_INTERFACE.txt constructs its exact same-generator flow constraints by
uniformly convergent Poisson series. It does not reinterpret old time-grid
records as random inspections or acquire any extra samples.

Here is a constructive proof with its conditioning cost. Work in L2(pi),
let A=-Q and B=B_nu, D=I-B. Markov contractions give Re<h,Ah>>=0 and
||A||<=2Lambda. If x=(I+A/nu)y, then
||x||^2>=||y||^2+||Ay||^2/nu^2. Consequently

          ||D||<=rho=2Lambda/sqrt(nu^2+4Lambda^2)<1.       (G2)

Also Q=-nu D(I-D)^(-1). With a=nu t the analytic generating function

 exp[-az/(1-z)]=sum_{n>=0}c_n(a)z^n,
 c_0=1, c_n=sum_{j=1}^n binom(n-1,j-1)(-a)^j/j!,         (G3)

gives exp(tQ)=sum c_n(a)D^n. These are generalized Laguerre coefficients;
neither Laguerre mathematics nor alias-free random sampling is claimed new.
On |z|=s<1, Re[z/(1-z)]>=-s/(1+s). Cauchy's inequality and s increasing
to1 imply |c_n(a)|<=exp(a/2). Thus the degree-K polynomial

 A_K(B)=sum_{n=0}^K c_n(a)(I-B)^n=sum_{j=0}^K a_j B^j

has the UNIFORM operator error

 ||exp(tQ)-A_K(B)||<=exp(nu t/2)rho^(K+1)/(1-rho).         (G4)

Ordered inspection-word laws determine every expression with label
projectors separated by integer powers B^j: sum over unrecorded intermediate
labels. The j=0 case merely multiplies two label projectors at the same
point. Replacing each deterministic-time semigroup by A_K in a word
probability and taking K to infinity proves the theorem. If the individual
operator errors are epsilon_i, the word error is at most
product_i(1+epsilon_i)-1, since label projectors and true semigroups are
contractions. All finite-dimensional laws, hence the finite-jump labelled
path law, are determined. Two bounded models may have different caps;
their maximum supplies a common finite bound for this equality argument.

For correlation reconstruction the EXACT coefficient sensitivity is
kappa_K=sum_j|a_j|: moment errors bounded by delta cause reconstruction
error<=kappa_K delta in addition to(G4). General words pay products of
these norms. Large cancellation is not free information. aliasfree.py
computes the coefficients, conservative error and a sufficient independent-
preparation budget. Its budget is not a minimax lower bound. Section7 gives
a separate physical instability theorem when no common cap is supplied.

3. COMPACT PREDICTIVE GENERATOR FLOWS

For observed alphabet A and words w of length<=L, let
r^+_w(x),r^-_w(x) be the conditional future/past INSPECTION-word probabilities
under B_nu and its actual stationary reverse. Let r_empty=1. At a state
z=(label,r^+,r^-) use the same compact cube or prefix-simplex as the
preserved predictive_flow construction. The variable Gamma is ONE
probability measure on K_L x K_L with equal marginals mu. Impose, for every
bounded continuous test F, every letter b and |w|<L,

 int F(z){Lambda r^+_{bw}(z')-(nu+Lambda)r^+_{bw}(z)
                      +nu 1_{label(z)=b}r^+_w(z)}dGamma=0, (G5)

 int F(z'){Lambda r^-_{bw}(z)-(nu+Lambda)r^-_{bw}(z')
                     +nu 1_{label(z')=b}r^-_w(z')}dGamma=0.(G6)

The data constraints are

 int 1_{label(z)=a}r^-_u(z)r^+_v(z)dmu=p(u^R a v),        (G7)

for |u|,|v|<=L. The r^- word order moves away into the past. All equations
are LINEAR in the single flow Gamma, with fixed continuous integrands.
They enforce a common generator, not separately fitted pair flows or roots.

Forward construction. For any physical Q in the capped class, push its
uniformization flow pi P through the true inspection predictive coordinates.
The resolvent recursion
[(nu+Lambda)I-Lambda P]r_{bw}=nu 1_label=b r_w
proves(G5)-(G6). Conditional past/future independence at the present state
proves(G7). Relative-entropy data processing decreases the flow cost.

Converse. Disintegrate any feasible Gamma as mu(dz)P(z,dz'). It defines
the actual bounded generator Q=Lambda(P-I), with ONE semigroup exp(tQ).
The inverse of (nu+Lambda)I-Lambda P exists on bounded functions by a
positive Neumann series, with norm<=1/nu. Induction on |w| makes(G5)
identify the UNIQUE true B_nu forward predictions. The stationary reverse
and(G6) do the same for backward predictions. Thus the actual process
realizes(G7), rather than merely satisfying a moment relaxation.

Its exact entropy-production rate is

                      sigma(Q)=Lambda D(Gamma||Gamma^T). (G8)

Off-diagonal uniformization flows are the physical jump flows divided by
Lambda; diagonal self-jumps have zero entropy. Equivalently, the full
uniformized-path likelihood ratio equals the physical path likelihood
ratio because the virtual holds contribute zero. No detector entropy is
added to the target.

Therefore the all-hidden-model inverse problem for the supplied inspection
word law is EXACTLY the convex minimization of(G8) under(G5)-(G7). It is
attained on a compact standard-Borel space whenever feasible (the cost may
be infinite). Weak closedness follows from continuous integrands, and
relative entropy is jointly weakly lower semicontinuous. Empty feasible
sets must be distinguished from feasible models with infinite cost.

4. ONE COMPLETE OBSERVED LAW, ONE ATTAINING PHYSICAL DYNAMICS

Use the compact countable product of ALL finite forward/backward inspection
word coordinates and impose(G5)-(G7) at every finite word. Nested compact
entropy sublevels prove

 I_Lambda(P_nu)=min_Q sigma(Q)=sup_L I_{Lambda,L}(p_L).      (G9)

The converse always constructs Q=Lambda(P-I), so all physical times belong
to that same Q. By Section2, with a complete inspected law this is also the
least capped-generator entropy compatible with the full continuous-time
OBSERVED law. The former unrelated-root compatibility obstruction is thus
removed at this stated bounded-generator scope. ROOT_LIMIT.txt also proves
that near-identity roots with a COMMON microscopic cap converge to this
coherent physical minimum for ordinary grid-law inputs, with an explicit
O(1/m) coarse-word coherence error. It is a population completion of the
root approach, not a new finite-record confidence transfer.

There is no exact finite-state attainment assertion. The result includes
measurable bounded pure-jump generators and reducible models. For a larger
cap Lambda'>=Lambda, the same Q uses
P'=I+(Lambda/Lambda')(P-I), and Lambda'e(P')=Lambda e(P).
Thus I_Lambda is nonincreasing as the cap increases. The infimum over caps
describes the union of bounded-generator classes; attainment or computable
tail control for that uncapped infimum is not proved.

6. EXACT FINITE-CTMC OPERATIONAL CLOSURE

Although an exact attaining model may use infinitely many hidden states,
I_Lambda equals the robust finite-state physical limit. In particular,

 I_Lambda(P_Y)=lim_{j->infinity} inf{sigma(Q):Q finite irreducible CTMC,
      exit<=Lambda, TV(Law_Q(Y_[0,j]),P_Y|_[0,j])<=1/j}.    (G12)

Here P_Y is a feasible complete continuous-time observed law. The finite
path total-variation condition is stronger than matching a few moments.

For the upper direction, start from the attaining Borel Q and uniformization
P. Enrich the quantization proof with P's FINE uniformization-word fields,
not just inspection fields. The old unbiased predictive quantizer then
produces a finite stationary flow J with e(J)<=e(P), and its actual fine
word laws approach those of P through any fixed length as depth and mesh
increase. For a physical horizon T, couple the common rate-Lambda Poisson
events. Conditional on at most2L events, differences of fine labelled-word
laws bound the FULL labelled-path TV; the remaining cost is the Poisson
tail Pr{Pois(Lambda T)>2L}. Directed mesh errors and that tail go to zero.
The resulting finite Q_grid keeps its escape cap and sigma<=sigma(Q).
If irreducibility is needed, replace its P by(1-epsilon)P+epsilon Pi_mu.
This adds reversible refresh, makes every finite transition positive,
keeps the cap and stationary law, and cannot increase entropy by convexity.
Its finite-path change vanishes with epsilon. No physical model is claimed
to implement this approximation for free; this is a constructive class theorem.

For the lower direction, take finite capped approximants with bounded cost
and the path TV convergence in(G12). Their inspection-word laws converge:
truncate the random inspection-window duration at T, use path TV on T,
and then send T to infinity to remove its Gamma tail. Push their
uniformization flows into the compact inspection coordinates. Any weak
subsequence realizes all inspection word constraints, hence the correct
continuous observed law by Section2, with no larger limiting entropy.
This proves(G12). Without a cap, this compactness argument is unavailable.

7. FINITE ACQUISITION, ALIASING AND THE CLOCK CHOICE

Exact law is not a finite sample. For(G4), obtaining each lag correlation
to precision delta using independent stationary windows is sufficient with
n>=2log[2(K+1)/alpha]/delta^2 preparations. The same window supplies all
lags, but independence is ACROSS preparations. It uses n(K+1) labels,
nK Poisson intervals and expected model duration nK/nu. The actual duration,
reset work, clock bandwidth and stationary preparation are additional
resources. Reusing overlapping windows from one trace does not supply n.

If the clock rate can be chosen, an ordinary positive-coefficient alternative
is Erlang averaging: choose k intervals at nu=k/t. Then
||B_nu^k-exp(tQ)||<=2Lambda t/sqrt k. It needs no signed amplification,
but buys high clock rate and k readouts per preparation. For the narrower
task of ONE prescribed-time correlation, the equally informed direct
experiment observes only at0 and t on each independent stationary start.
It is cheaper if that timed-readout interface is actually available. No
general practical advantage over that ordinary experiment is claimed.

NO COMMON CAP: there is also a genuine finite-data obstruction, separate
from an inefficient inversion algorithm. INSTABILITY.txt gives finite,
irreducible, reciprocal rotating-reset CTMCs whose ordered Poisson records
of any fixed total number of intervals are arbitrarily close, while their
fixed-time correlations remain separated by a constant. This persists with
independent preparations. A known Poisson rate makes exact laws identifiable;
it does not create unlimited finite-bandwidth resolution.

The cap itself is not enough to remove Astra N185: a base model with strict
rate-cap slack still admits an arbitrarily weak dormant bridge within the
same cap. Thus a single trajectory cannot silently acquire the ensemble
law or an honest consistent global mean rate. This new convex inverse
problem consumes exact laws, valid probability intervals, or an explicitly
paid independent-preparation protocol. A realised-path transfer of its
FULL generator dual is not proved here; the earlier path machinery remains
valid at its own scope.

There IS a finite-record kinetic consequence of the same resolvent disk,
proved in METROLOGY.txt and implemented in kinetic.py. If kappa<=Lambda,
the inspected binary change count has mgf no larger than Binomial(N,r),
r=Lambda/(nu+2Lambda), uniformly over all stationary hidden models. This
is sharp at the equilibrium two-state CTMC. Exact rational confidence
inversion and predeclared checkpoints give a LOWER bound on the maximum
escape rate, without a hidden count or mixing input. This lower bound
cannot supply the UPPER cap needed by the entropy minimization. Its fresh
controls have zero physical entropy production. Neither result estimates
stationary mean activity or supplies a physical device for free.
Read KINETIC_INCUMBENT.txt with that frozen proof: mgf sharpness does not
establish exact binomial-tail domination. A stronger ordinary exact-tail
incumbent beats the Chernoff band on the same two-state validation records.
