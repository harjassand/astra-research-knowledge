SHARPNESS AND THE STRONGER ORDINARY INCUMBENT
31 October 2026. Clarification after the frozen kinetic draws; no redraws.

The phrase "same binomial calibration" in the frozen METROLOGY.txt and
kinetic_design.json refers to the BINOMIAL MGF and its CHERNOFF inversion.
M1 is sharp as an mgf inequality. It does not by itself prove that each
discrete tail is bounded by the exact binomial tail, nor that the
implemented confidence endpoint is statistically optimal. That stronger
tail statement for the unrestricted hidden CTMC class remains UNPROVED.
The finite cycle search in kinetic_tail_search.json did not refute it;
that floating diagnostic does not establish it.

On the specified validation sources, observed flips really ARE iid
Bernoulli. An ordinary exact binomial-tail (Clopper-Pearson) lower interval
is therefore a stronger matched incumbent than ordinary Chernoff
inversion. We implemented that incumbent on the SAME already acquired
records, clock rate, checkpoints and error allocations. The initial
frozen protocol and draws remain preserved; this comparison is a
post-draw analysis with no additional acquisitions or selected data.

For N intervals, d>0 changes and null probability q<d/N,

 Pr{Bin(N,q)>=d}=binom(N,d)q^d(1-q)^(N-d) S,
 S=1+rho_d+rho_d rho_(d+1)+...,
 rho_k=[(N-k)/(k+1)] q/(1-q).

These ratios decrease and are below one. Positive fixed-point arithmetic
rounds every recurrence down/up. A truncated upper sum adds the remaining
geometric tail. The large integer binomial coefficient is computed
exactly, then enclosed between its leading-96-bit dyadic truncation and
the next dyadic value. Certified rational logarithms enclose the pmf and
S; no large-factorial floating approximation decides validity.

SciPy inverse-beta values propose endpoints only. For each proposed lower
q the receiver proves log(tail_upper)<=log(alpha_lower), and for the
upper q proves log(tail_lower)>=log(alpha_upper). Thus it brackets the
exact ordinary lower endpoint. The binomial-to-kinetic transformation
q -> nu q/(1-2q) applies to these iid-flip sources. A general hidden
record is not licensed to use that extra iid premise.
