This connects the preserved virtual midpoint/quarter roots to PROOF.txt.
It adds a common externally supplied maximum escape-rate cap Lambda.

Fix a physical grid spacing Delta>0 and a feasible complete stationary
observed law p_grid on that grid, with a deterministic finite state label.
Let I_Lambda(p_grid) be the least stationary all-even entropy-production
rate of a bounded standard-Borel pure-jump generator Q, escape<=Lambda,
whose Delta-grid observations have that ENTIRE law. GRID_INTERFACE.txt
establishes the attained coherent-generator minimization at this scope.

For integer m>=Lambda*Delta, put h=Delta/m. Let

 W_m=inf{e(R)/h : R stationary hidden Markov kernel,
    R(x,{x})>=1-Lambda*h, and observing every m steps
    gives the COMPLETE law p_grid}.                        (R1)

The state space and R may vary with m. Exact finite hidden-state
attainment is not asserted. Diagonal holding is a microscopic cap,
not a condition on the observed label alone. Every such root is

               R=(1-Lambda*h)I+Lambda*h P                 (R2)

for a stationary Markov kernel P. Thus it has an ACTUAL generator
Q_m=(R-I)/h=Lambda(P-I), cap<=Lambda, and

                   sigma(Q_m)=e(R)/h.                    (R3)

This entropy identity is exact: every off-diagonal flow is scaled by
1/h and the diagonal holding flow has zero reversal cost. The physical
semigroup of Q_m need not equal R^m at finite m; that error is charged.

THEOREM:

 W_m<=I_Lambda(p_grid),
             lim_(m->infinity) W_m=I_Lambda(p_grid).       (R4)

If m' is a multiple of m, W_m<=W_m'. In particular, the admissible
dyadic subsequence is nondecreasing and has this same physical limit.
This convergence does not supply a useful universal computation rate.

UPPER DIRECTION. Any compatible Q has R=exp(hQ). Its off-diagonal
probability is at most the chance of a hidden uniformization event,
1-exp(-Lambda*h)<=Lambda*h. Its m-step grid law is exactly p_grid.
Data processing the stationary physical path of duration h to its two
endpoints gives e(R)<=h*sigma(Q). Taking the infimum proves the upper
bound, including the distinction between endpoint entropy and path cost.

LOWER DIRECTION, WITH EXPLICIT PHYSICAL COHERENCE ERROR. Given (R2),
one step of R uses a Bernoulli(Lambda*h) count of P updates. One step
of exp(hQ_m) uses a Poisson(Lambda*h) count. Their count TV is at most
(Lambda*h)^2: at p=Lambda*h<=1 it is exactly p*(1-exp(-p))<=p^2.
Hence so is the worst starting-state transition TV.
Coupling m successive steps gives

 sup_x TV(R^m(x,.),exp(Delta Q_m)(x,.))
                       <=Lambda^2*Delta^2/m.              (R5)

For any observed word of length ell on the coarse grid, the TV error
is at most (ell-1)*Lambda^2*Delta^2/m. These are COARSE WORD laws;
discrete jumps at fixed grid times are not claimed close in full
continuous-path TV to physical jumps at continuously distributed times.

Take roots along a sequence with bounded near-minimal costs. Their
actual Q_m have the common cap. Push their uniformization flows into
the compact coherent-generator predictive coordinates of PROOF.txt.
The physical coarse word laws converge to p_grid by (R5). Compactness,

actual cap-Lambda generator with the EXACT law p_grid and entropy
rate at most liminf e(R_m)/h. This gives I_Lambda<=liminf W_m.
Together with the upper bound it proves (R4). The same argument forces
divergence if the physical minimum is infinite.

MONOTONE MULTIPLES. If m'=k*m and R' is admissible at h'=Delta/m',
then R=(R')^k is admissible at h=k*h': a union bound gives its holding
constraint, its m-step observed law is unchanged, and stationary
path data processing gives e((R')^k)<=k*e(R'). Thus W_m<=W_m'.
