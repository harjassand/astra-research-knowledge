Take a driven finite base(Q,pi,f), both labels present, with distinct
positive cross-label hazards within each label. Choose one active state
a_b of each label b and let p_b=Pr_pi(f=b). For any w in(0,1),epsilon>0,
append ONE dormant state d_b per label. Keep all old off-diagonal rates;
put

 q_(a_b,d_b)=epsilon,
 q_(d_b,a_b)=w*pi_(a_b)*epsilon/[(1-w)*p_b],
 f(d_b)=b.

There are no other new edges. Rows sum to zero. The extended stationary
law is w*pi on old states and(1-w)*p_b at d_b. Each bridge is detailed
balanced. Consequently

 sigma_0(Q_extended)=w*sigma_0(Q),                        (F1)

where sigma_0 is the ORIGINAL zero-field stationary entropy rate, not
the driven experiment's entropy or realised work. The visible stationary

hazard0, one in each label; all old crossing hazards are unchanged and

is finite, irreducible and reciprocal, and lies within the population
identifiable simple-hazard class itself.

 P_extended(E)>=w*exp(-epsilon*T)*P_base(E).               (F2)

 P_extended(E)>=w*E_base[1_E exp(-epsilon*T_N)]
