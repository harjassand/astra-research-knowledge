The passive coherent-generator programme minimizes over explanations of
observations. Even every passive timed law need not determine how a system
responds to an energy control. We attacked that difference by constructing
two strictly positive reciprocal eight-state generators with exactly equal
passive timed laws and opposite work signs under the SAME finite closed
protocol. Their fuel consumption is charged; this is not a new free engine.
The first smooth-response-to-staircase shortcut failed and is preserved in
opening_receipt.json. Retuning the actual finite protocol and enclosing its
periodic state gives the certified construction in work_receipt.json.

This suggested a more consequential question: can a calibrated observable
energy control make the physical hidden coordinates identifiable, or force
a thermodynamic quotient even when more hidden states remain? The result
below solves an uncapped FINITE-hidden-generator population minimum on a
specified controlled-law class. This consequence justified pursuing the
opening instead of automatically adding another passive hierarchy layer.

It pays for stronger information. Passive lower kinetic bounds do not
supply an upper cap, and the intervention contract below is not silently
available from earlier records. The passive finite-path machinery remains
valuable when such control is unavailable. All earlier artifacts remain.

2. THE ACTUAL CONTROL AND OBSERVATION CONTRACT

Let Q be a finite stationary CTMC, with unknown finite state count, finite
rates, deterministic binary label f_i in{-1,+1}, and stationary pi positive
on the relevant support. Reciprocal support is assumed for finite entropy.
All states are even under time reversal. No common upper escape-rate cap,
mixing bound or microscopic state count is supplied to the population result.

There are TWO calibrated energy settings, h=0 and h=log t, t>0,t!=1.
The required finite-amplitude microscopic rate law is

        Q_t(i,j)=Q(i,j) t^((f_j-f_i)/2), i!=j;            (A1)

diagonals make rows sum to zero. In kBT units the energy is U_h(i)=-h*f_i.
This keeps the geometric mean of reciprocal edge rates fixed. Local detailed
balance of RATE RATIOS alone does NOT establish(A1): an unmeasured symmetric
barrier response can multiply both directions. Actuator energy calibration,
that barrier qualification, noninvasive deterministic sensing and timing
are external premises, not outputs of a successful model fit.

At a known independent Poisson clock nu, choose either setting after an
inspection, hold it until the next inspection, read the label, then choose
the next setting. Initial preparation is the base stationary pi. The input
to this theorem is the COMPLETE collection of controlled word probabilities
for prescribed finite action sequences. Exact word probabilities are not
a finite trajectory or free independent preparations.

For setting a in{0,1}, with Q_0=Q,Q_1=Q_t, define

 B_a=nu(nu I-Q_a)^(-1),   D_b=diag(1{f=b}),
 T_(a,b)=B_a D_b.
 p(w)=pi T_(a1,b1)...T_(ak,bk)1.                          (A2)

These resolvents are invertible for every finite generator, without knowing
its largest rate. This uses the previous clock machinery in a different
role: its exact inverse exposes controlled generators, rather than solving
a passive capped entropy optimisation. Random waiting times are unrecorded
but the dimensional clock rate nu must be calibrated.

3. WHAT THE CONTROL EXPOSES

Let C=diag(c_i), c_i=sum_(j:f_j!=f_i) Q(i,j), the microscopic escape
hazard ACROSS THE LABEL CUT. C is not the total hidden escape rate and
not a stationary average. It is a state multiplication operator.

From any common minimal linear realization of the controlled law, form

 B_a=sum_b T_(a,b),  D_b=B_0^(-1)T_(0,b),
 Q_a=nu[I-B_a^(-1)].                                    (A3)

Write D_+=D_(+1),D_-=D_(-1), and

 R_t=D_+ Q D_+ +D_- Q D_-
                 +t^(-1)D_+ Q D_- +t D_- Q D_+.
 F_t=(1-t^(-1))D_+ +(1-t)D_-.

Direct off-diagonal scaling and diagonal row-sum compensation give

                  Q_t=R_t+F_t C,
                  C=F_t^(-1)(Q_t-R_t).                  (A4)

F_t is invertible. All these are finite-dimensional matrix operations in
ONE common observable representation. Separate fits of Q and Q_t in
unrelated bases would not justify(A4).

The inverse of an invertible matrix belongs to its unital generated
algebra (Cayley-Hamilton). Hence D_b,Q_a,C belong to the algebra generated
by the observable T's. In physical coordinates D_b and C are commuting
diagonal real multiplication operators. Similarity hides that basis but
cannot destroy its joint eigenvalues or moment identities.

CONTROL COMPLETENESS, EVEN WITHOUT JOINT SIMPLICITY. If the same calibrated
law(A1) is supplied on a larger field range, then for ANY multiplier s>0

 Q_s=D_+QD_+ +D_-QD_- +s^(-1)D_+QD_- +s D_-QD_+
                  +[(1-s^(-1))D_+ +(1-s)D_-]C.           (A4b)

It belongs to the algebra already exposed by the TWO measured settings.
Its matrix exponential and Poisson resolvent pass through the same minimal
representation. Thus the complete two-setting law determines ALL binary
output laws and potential-work distributions under finite piecewise-constant
field protocols in the qualified range, including unmeasured field levels
and deterministic durations. This is not prediction from passive data,
nor an extrapolation licensed by two empirical calibration points alone.
A symmetric rate prefactor equal to1 at the two tested levels but different
elsewhere preserves local detailed balance and destroys that extrapolation.
The code retains this explicit counterexample. Ordinary observable-operator
prediction, with this calibrated algebra, is an equally informed incumbent.

4. FULL STATE IDENTIFICATION IN THE SIMPLE PHYSICAL CASE

If the pairs (f_i,c_i) are all distinct, their joint spectral projectors
E_i are polynomials in D_+ and C. These projectors are physical single-state
indicators. Starting from1, E_i1=e_i spans every state; starting from the
positive pi, pi E_i=pi_i e_i^T spans every row. Thus the controlled Hankel
matrix H_(u,v)=p(uv) has rank exactly the physical state count n.

Minimal observable operators are determined by the complete controlled law
up to similarity. The joint projectors fix the physical coordinate vectors
E_i1. Their normalization fixes the remaining diagonal scaling. Q,pi and f
are therefore identified up to a label-preserving permutation. This is a
finite-model population identifiability statement, not identification of
an unknown state count from finitely many noisy words.

5. THE STRONGER THERMODYNAMIC MINIMUM, WITHOUT ASSUMING ALL STATES SIMPLE

Let the supplied controlled law have finite minimal rank r. In its minimal
representation, suppose the commuting pair(D_+,C) has r distinct joint
eigenpairs: each joint eigenspace is one-dimensional. Call this OBSERVABLE
joint simplicity. It is true for every model in Section4, but larger hidden
models with repeated hazards are also allowed as explanations of that law.

THEOREM. Under(A1)-(A2) and observable joint simplicity, the algebraically
recovered r-state generator Q_can is a valid physical realization and

 min_{all compatible FINITE hidden CTMCs satisfying(A1)} sigma(Q)
                         =sigma(Q_can).                 (A5)

No common microscopic rate cap is needed. The actual apparatus may have
additional hidden entropy production; (A5) identifies its exact least
compatible generator cost, not its entire unobserved heating.
SELECTIVE_GATE.txt extends this result from the energy-field example to
a uniform label-cut kinetic gate. Its two numerical strength factors
can be identified from the population operators rather than supplied.

PROOF. Any finite compatible realization gives a representation of the
observable word algebra. Passing to a minimal realization is a quotient
homomorphism: it preserves sums, products and inverses of invertible
matrices. This follows directly by quotienting the reachable row space
by vectors annihilating all future words. Thus(A3)-(A4) are the images of
the actual D_b,Q_a and diagonal hazard C, including in a nonminimal model.

For each label b let p_b be the polynomial whose distinct roots are the
joint hazard eigenvalues of C on that label's MINIMAL subspace. Then
D_b p_b(C) is zero in the minimal representation. Consequently the
observable moment of its square vanishes:

       0=pi D_b p_b(C)^2 1
        =sum_(i:f_i=b) pi_i p_b(c_i)^2.                  (A6)

Positive pi implies that EVERY physical state's (label,hazard) belongs
to the minimal joint spectrum. Conversely every minimal eigenpair has
positive probability; otherwise its physical nonnegative indicator has
zero mass and hence zero representation on stationary support.

The r joint projectors therefore represent an actual deterministic
partition into r(label,hazard) cells in EVERY compatible model. In the
minimal joint-projector basis they are the coordinate projectors. Set

 pi_can(alpha)=pi E_alpha1,
 Q_can(alpha,beta)=pi E_alpha Q E_beta1/pi_can(alpha)
                                                  for alpha!=beta. (A7)

Off-diagonal entries are nonnegative stationary aggregate jump flows;
the diagonal is their negative row sum. pi_can is stationary. The
minimal representation of Q is this same Q_can, since every joint
projector is rank one and E_alpha1 fixes its coordinate vector.

All canonical CELL word probabilities, with arbitrary time gaps and
controlled generators, equal those generated by these minimal operators.
Indeed the physical cell projectors are polynomials in the observable
algebra, as are Q_a and their matrix exponentials; expanding their products
and using the common representation preserves every scalar word moment.
The projected cell law from stationary preparation is therefore exactly
the Q_can Markov law at zero field. This is a quotient IN LAW.

Do NOT replace that conclusion by strong lumpability at arbitrary hidden
initializations. A stationary weakly lumpable expansion can have the same
entire controlled law while its individual states have different outgoing
rates to a cell. The explicit expansion control retains this counterexample.
The entropy argument only needs stationary path data processing.

For the entropy comparison, aggregate the stationary directed jump flows
J_ij=pi_i Q(i,j) by this actual cell partition. Their off-diagonal cell
flows are exactly pi_can(alpha)Q_can(alpha,beta) by(A7). The log-sum
inequality decreases relative entropy under that aggregation; microscopic
flows within a cell have nonnegative total reversal cost. Consequently
sigma(Q)>=sigma(Q_can). This direct flow proof needs neither strong
lumpability nor a finite-data extrapolation of a population mean.

Each canonical cell has a constant actual cross-label hazard c_alpha.
Hence(A7) has exactly that cross-label rate and its controlled aggregate
generator obeys(A1). The canonical matrices reproduce every original
binary controlled word probability, so Q_can itself is admissible. This
attains the lower bound and proves(A5).

6. WHY THIS DOES NOT IDENTIFY INVISIBLE EXCESS HEAT

Tensor Q_can with an arbitrary independent driven hidden cycle and retain
the same label/actuator on the canonical coordinate. Every passive and
controlled observation remains unchanged. Entropy rates add. The actual
entropy rate is therefore unbounded above among compatible models.
The larger realization repeats each(label,hazard); the minimal observable
joint spectrum and(A5) are unchanged. A fitted canonical realization is
not a measurement of all microscopic channels. Generator-level entropy
equals total physical entropy only when the declared Markov states and
transition channels resolve the intended thermodynamic description.

7. FINITE ALGORITHMS, ACQUISITION AND FAILURE BOUNDARIES

Given prefix/suffix word bases U,V of size r and a PROMISE that the complete
controlled Hankel rank is at most r, an invertible H=(p(uv)) and the four
shifted matrices H^c=(p(ucv)) give T_hat_c=H^(-1)H^c. The constant column

construction is shared with conventional spectral system identification.
The new step in this argument is the calibrated hazard algebra and its
positive thermodynamic quotient, not invention of Hankel realization.

Without a supplied rank bound, finite-rank population identifiability does
not certify that a finite noisy prefix has found every mode. Finite error
control also pays for Hankel conditioning, matrix inverses, the joint
hazard gaps, minimum stationary masses and actuator calibration. Near
degeneracy can make these arbitrarily bad. No uniform useful sample
complexity or complete finite-record entropy controller is proved here.

It reconstructs physical coordinates and checks positivity, stationarity,
