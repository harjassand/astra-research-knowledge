### 1. The new theorem

Consider \(m\) fixed-size cells. Each cell has dimension \(d_0=2^q\), and its commuting Markovian dephasing leaves sectors of dimensions
\[
d_1,\ldots,d_s,\qquad \sum_\lambda d_\lambda=d_0.
\]
Assume an acquired damping bound: a matrix entry changing sector labels in \(j\) cells decays at least \(j\) times a qualified local rate. The integrated rate must remain positive as the system grows. Independent local dephasing satisfies this condition; globally shared fields can violate it through cancellations.

With a fully mixed independent auxiliary source, the exact asymptotic storage rate is
\[
\boxed{
C_{\mathrm{mixed}}
=\sum_\lambda \frac{d_\lambda}{d_0}\log_2 d_\lambda
=q-H\!\left(\frac{d_\lambda}{d_0}\right)
}
\]
logical qubits per cell.

This includes both directions:

- **Below this rate:** an acquired encoder and its actual inverse receiver achieve exponentially small **whole-register diamond error**, including arbitrary entangled references.
- **Above this rate:** entanglement fidelity tends to zero exponentially, even with **arbitrary predetermined Hamiltonian control and arbitrary final CPTP recovery**, at every fixed positive exposure.

The converse permits every CPTP encoder satisfying
\[
E(I_k/k)=I_d/d.
\]
Closed unitary encoding with a mixed gauge is one concrete subclass. During storage, the theorem excludes imported purity, resets, a noiseless memory and accessible preparation or bath records.

| Local noise sectors | Exact mixed-source rate |
|---|---:|
| Independent single-spin dephasing: \((1,1)\) | Zero asymptotic rate |
| Matched spin pairs: \((1,2,1)\) | \(1/2\) logical qubit per pair, or \(1/4\) per physical spin |
| Three-spin cells: \((1,2,5)\) | \(1.701205\ldots\) logical qubits per cell |
| Two equal rank-2 sectors: \((2,2)\) | One logical qubit per cell |

The quantity itself is familiar coherent information at maximally mixed input. Ordinary complete-pinching capacity instead optimizes the input and equals \(\log_2 d_{\max}\). The source restriction matters: selecting a large protected subspace generally requires a different preparation resource. The direct-sum capacity machinery is established; the additional result here is the acquired full-source construction and the finite-exposure converse against coherent control. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

### 2. The decisive mechanism: constrain each row before averaging

The first global count gave the wrong organizing quantity. Rare large sectors could dominate the average dimension even though they occupy too little of the physical source.

For any current unital completely positive embedding \(T\), a normalized complete logical basis obeys
\[
\sum_a |T(F_a)_{xy}|^2\le k,
\qquad
\sum_{y,a}|T(F_a)_{xy}|^2\le k^2.
\]
Consequently, if a weakly damped band contains \(V_x\) entries in physical row \(x\), its capacity for the traceless logical algebra is bounded by
\[
\boxed{
c=\frac1d\sum_x\min\{k^2-1,\;kV_x-1\}.
}
\]

**The minimum must be taken in each row before averaging.** This prevents rare large sectors from carrying most of the complete logical algebra.

The inequality remains true throughout arbitrary coherent control. Hamiltonian evolution contributes zero to the real Hilbert–Schmidt norm derivative, while norm outside the retained band pays its damping rate. A recovery bound then covers every final receiver.

For sectors \((1,2,5)\), the earlier un-clipped count suggests \(1.906890\ldots\) bits per cell. Rowwise clipping gives the correct threshold, \(1.701205\ldots\). This failed route and its exact numerical comparison are retained.

Three independent proof audits found no substantive defect. Separate checks covered nonmultiplicative UCP maps, complex encoders, noncommuting Hamiltonians, exact row counts and the numerical qualification.

### 3. What this changes about the physical requirements

**The exponential-space obstruction under independent dephasing is now representation-independent within the stated source contract.** At fixed positive exposure and fixed useful whole-register accuracy,
\[
\ell=O(\log n),
\]
even with arbitrary coherent encoding and control. The inherited Hamming construction attains logarithmic width in its admitted time/error regime.

The finite calculation also remains explicit. For \(n=1024\), \(\ell=512\), and desired diamond error at most \(0.1\), the controlled scalar clock requires integrated minimum dephasing exposure no greater than
\[
0.000449440936467.
\]
That is a necessary ceiling, not an attained operating point.

**Subextensive source purity cannot raise the new asymptotic rate.** For a fixed complete protocol, fidelity is an effect of its auxiliary source:
\[
F_e(\sigma)=\operatorname{Tr}(M\sigma),\qquad 0\le M\le I.
\]
A tracial-source ceiling \(B\) implies
\[
\log_2 g-S(\sigma)
\ge f\log_2(1/B)-h_2(f)
\]
when fidelity \(f\) is required. Above the capacity, \(B\) is exponentially small. Therefore achieving a higher rate requires an **extensive initial entropy deficit**, even for correlated or entangled auxiliary states. Entropy deficit remains a necessary resource, not a preparation algorithm or a heat-cost formula.

**Correlation accuracy has an acquired finite requirement.** For the matched-pair construction, residual independent exposure must scale as \(O(1/n)\) to preserve a positive-density register at fixed useful accuracy. The universal converse requires this order; the explicit pair channel achieves it with a sufficiently small constant.

For example, at 1,024 physical spins, 128 logical qubits and \(\gamma T=1\), the qualified routing error changes from approximately \(1.94\times10^{-31}\) at perfect pair correlation to \(0.0254355930986051\) at correlation \(0.9999\). ### 4. The representation and receiver were actually constructed

The encoder partitions each joint sector of dimension \(D\) into complete groups of \(k\) basis states. All leftovers form complete bad groups, so the map is a permutation of the **entire** source. Its inverse produces
\[
\mathcal T=(1-\beta)\operatorname{Id}+\beta\mathcal T_{\mathrm{bad}},
\qquad
\beta=\mathbb E\!\left[\frac{D\bmod k}{D}\right],
\]
and therefore
\[
\|\mathcal T-\operatorname{Id}\|_\diamond\le2\beta.
\]
No branch is postselected.
