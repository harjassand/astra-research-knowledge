Source contract and theorem: fixed-size cells have known local sector ranks d_lambda summing to d0. Matrix entries differing in sector labels in j cells have instantaneous decay at least j r_*(t), with integrated r_* bounded below independently of cell count. Independent qualified local commuting dephasing satisfies this premise; a globally shared field can violate it through cancellations. The arbitrary reference-entangled logical input is tensored with an independent fully mixed gauge. Accessible gauge purification, preparation records, resets and entropy outlets during storage are excluded. The preview states the rate mu=sum(d_lambda/d0) log2(d_lambda), with below-rate whole-register achievability and an above-rate strong converse at fixed positive exposure; the converse admits CPTP encoders mapping the maximally mixed logical state to the maximally mixed physical state, arbitrary predetermined Hamiltonian control and final CPTP recovery.

Converse mechanism: for each physical row, UCP bounds constrain logical-basis matrix entries. The source clips each row's contribution by min{k^2-1,k V_x-1} before averaging. A sector-change band bounds retained squared norm; Hamiltonian control has zero real Hilbert-Schmidt norm derivative, while off-band terms pay damping. A recovery bound and type concentration then give the reported exponential converse. The rowwise clipping prevents rare large sectors from dominating a global average.

Constructive mechanism: each joint sector is partitioned into complete logical groups of k states; leftovers are grouped without postselection so the permutation covers the entire source. An inverse permutation and discarding the gauge give a mixture of the identity channel and a bad channel with diamond error at most twice the leftover fraction. Typical-sector concentration drives that fraction down below the stated rate. Supplied sector basis, reversible compilation, temporary clean workspace and uncertainty remain real resources.

SOURCES.md distinguishes this source-restricted capacity from established coherent-information/direct-sum results and complete pinching's optimized log2(d_max) capacity. It cites Chessa and Giovannetti, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2008.00494, and ordinary positive dephasing capacity under unrestricted coding resources, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1504.04617. It also identifies pure-stock and nonunital alternatives, and states that the new source restriction does not rule them out. The generic compiler's very large upper bounds do not establish a hardware advantage or lower bound on optimized alternatives.
