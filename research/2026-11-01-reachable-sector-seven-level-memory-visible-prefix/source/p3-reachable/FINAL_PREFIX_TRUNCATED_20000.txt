## 2. The actual mechanism

### Encoding and error states

The construction uses the established binomial-code family, rather than claiming a new code. Its two codewords are

\[
|C_0\rangle=\frac{|0\rangle+\sqrt3|4\rangle}{2},
\qquad
|C_1\rangle=\frac{\sqrt3|2\rangle+|6\rangle}{2}.
\]

Binomial codes supply finite superpositions designed to correct specified combinations of loss and dephasing errors. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

Here the declared data constituent has exactly seven levels. Define

\[
a=\sum_{m=1}^{6}\sqrt m\,|m-1\rangle\langle m|,
\qquad
n=\sum_{m=0}^{6}m|m\rangle\langle m|.
\]

Complete the code to the orthonormal frame

\[
\begin{aligned}
|L_0\rangle&=|3\rangle,
&
|L_1\rangle&=\frac{|1\rangle+|5\rangle}{\sqrt2},\\
|D_0\rangle&=\frac{-\sqrt3|0\rangle+|4\rangle}{2},
&
|D_1\rangle&=\frac{-|2\rangle+\sqrt3|6\rangle}{2},\\
|J\rangle&=\frac{|1\rangle-|5\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Then

\[
a|C_b\rangle=\sqrt3|L_b\rangle,
\qquad
(n-3)|C_b\rangle=\sqrt3|D_b\rangle.
\]

Loss and phase errors therefore enter two distinct recovery sectors, with equal amplitudes for the two logical basis states.

### A useful completion outside those sectors

The central construction is

\[
\boxed{
A_\ell=
\sum_{b=0}^{1}|C_b\rangle\langle L_b|
-\frac1{\sqrt3}|D_1\rangle\langle J|,
\qquad
A_d=
\sum_{b=0}^{1}|C_b\rangle\langle D_b|.
}
\]

The extra \(J\)-term has a concrete purpose. It drains an otherwise troublesome state and **cancels the five-quantum transition** in the ordinary loss-repair operator. After cancellation,

\[
\begin{aligned}
A_\ell={}&
\tfrac12|0\rangle\langle3|
+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}|4\rangle\langle3|
+\sqrt{\tfrac23}|2\rangle\langle1|\\
&+\tfrac1{\sqrt6}|2\rangle\langle5|
+\tfrac1{\sqrt2}|6\rangle\langle5|.
\end{aligned}
\]

The phase operator has eight nonzero entries:

\[
\begin{aligned}
A_d={}&
\tfrac{\sqrt3}{4}
\left(-|0\rangle\langle0|-|2\rangle\langle2|
+|4\rangle\langle4|+|6\rangle\langle6|\right)\\
&+\tfrac14|0\rangle\langle4|
-\tfrac34|4\rangle\langle0|
+\tfrac34|2\rangle\langle6|
-\tfrac14|6\rangle\langle2|.
\end{aligned}
\]

There are five loss and eight phase amplitudes. Their magnitudes range from \(1/4\) to \(\sqrt3/2\); the largest data-number change is four.

With two controller qubits,

\[
H=g\left(
A_\ell\sigma_\ell^+
+A_\ell^\dagger\sigma_\ell^-
+A_d\sigma_d^+
+A_d^\dagger\sigma_d^-
\right),
\]

and

\[
\mathcal G_0(\rho)
=
-i[H,\rho]
+2g\mathcal D[\sigma_\ell^-](\rho)
+2g\mathcal D[\sigma_d^-](\rho).
\]

These are the actual finite matrices used in both the proof and channel calculation.

On the intended recovery paths, the loss interaction coherently exchanges

\[
|L_b,00\rangle\longleftrightarrow|C_b,10\rangle,
\]

and the phase interaction exchanges

\[
|D_b,00\rangle\longleftrightarrow|C_b,01\rangle.
\]

Reset completes the return to \(|C_b,00\rangle\), preserving the same logical amplitudes on both paths.

The second reset qubit is a deliberate resource choice. The preserved one-controller construction needed a parity-energy field to remove destructive interference between recovery paths. Separating the two recovery ports eliminates that field and the auxiliary detunings.

## 3. Recovery is allowed to be logically wrong

Define the ten-dimensional sector

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal K={}&
\operatorname{span}\{C_b,L_b,D_b:b=0,1\}\otimes|00\rangle\\
&\oplus
\operatorname{span}\{C_b:b=0,1\}
\otimes\operatorname{span}\{|10\rangle,|01\rangle\}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The exact matrices establish that:

- The Hamiltonian and each reset preserve \(\mathcal K\).
- Recovery preserves the logical information on \(\mathcal K\).
- Every ambient noise jump maps clean encoded data into \(\mathcal K\).
- The corresponding no-jump products also map the clean code into \(\mathcal K\).

Controller errors are included in these statements. An \(X\) or \(Y\) error on a controller initially puts the encoded data on one of the controller-excited branches above. The resulting intended dynamics still preserve its logical state.

The final decoder traces out the controllers and maps each pair \(C_b,L_b,D_b\) back to logical \(b\). Its remaining two Kraus operators map \(J\) to the maximally mixed logical state. Explicitly,

\[
K_C=\sum_b|b\rangle\langle C_b|,\quad
K_L=\sum_b|b\rangle\langle L_b|,\quad
K_D=\sum_b|b\rangle\langle D_b|,
\]

together with

\[
K_{J0}=|0\rangle\langle J|/\sqrt2,\qquad
K_{J1}=|1\rangle\langle J|/\sqrt2.
\]

Their adjoint products sum to \(I_7\).

Now consider \(|J,00\rangle\). It initially decodes to \(I_2/2\), but the actual noise-free recovery eventually sends it to \(|C_1,00\rangle\), which decodes to \(|1\rangle\langle1|\).

**The recovery therefore changes the declared logical output on some physical states.** This is the freedom that permits the useful completion and transition cancellation.

Nor is this merely a different global tensor factorization. A logical-qubit algebra commuting with both resets and their adjoints would have to act within the seven-dimensional data factor. There is no unital representation of the full qubit matrix algebra on an odd-dimensional space.

The boundary interface remains conventional: one encoder, controller initialization, physical storage, and one final CPTP decoder at the requested output time. Readout does not wait for a recovery to finish.

Let \(P\) project onto clean encoded data with controllers in vacuum, and set

The second inequality includes all subsequent faults after entry into the dangerous sector. No projection onto either sector is physically performed.

These are population and integrated-occupation statements, not assumptions that a reset photon always marks completed correction.

The response witnesses are small: 41 and 29 nonzero upper-triangle entries, respectively, with rational and \(\sqrt3\)-rational coefficients. Exact positivity checks admit them. Floating-point eigensolvers propose useful matrices; they do not decide whether the final certificate passes.

### The additional step needed for the logical channel

Occupation alone is insufficient here. For

\[
F_\alpha=\mathcal R^\dagger(\sigma_\alpha),
\]

the actual intended recovery obeys

\[
\mathcal G_0^\dagger F_\alpha
=
Q_b\mathcal G_0^\dagger F_\alpha Q_b,
\qquad
\|\mathcal G_0^\dagger F_\alpha\|=g/\sqrt3.
\]

That charges recovery-induced logical damage to dangerous occupation.

But the noise only satisfies

\[
P\mathcal N^\dagger F_\alpha P=0.
\]

Its clean rows and columns need not vanish. Number-dephasing no-jump evolution creates logical-dependent coherences between clean and error sectors. Applying the earlier occupation-only proof would miss them.

The replacement is to bound the **noise-weighted response of the actual output observables**:

\[
k_\alpha(u)=
\left\|
\mathcal N^\dagger e^{u\mathcal G_0^\dagger}
\mathcal N^\dagger F_\alpha
\right\|.
\]

An exact variation-of-constants identity makes the logical contribution contain two noise factors. Harmless internal dynamics need not be close in whole-channel norm.

For the concrete certificate, I used three Hermitian proof observables \(C_\alpha\), with

\[
PC_\alpha P=0,\qquad
E_\alpha=
\mathcal G_0^\dagger C_\alpha+\mathcal N^\dagger F_\alpha.
\]

Then

\[
(\mathcal G_0+\gamma\mathcal N)^\dagger
(F_\alpha+\gamma C_\alpha)
=
\mathcal G_0^\dagger F_\alpha
+\gamma E_\alpha
+\gamma^2\mathcal N^\dagger C_\alpha.
\]

The return witness also controls the endpoint correction and residual through their transient response. This is important: an approximate corrector residual is charged quadratically in noise, rather than producing an artificial first-order error floor.

The admitted bounds are

\[
g\|C_\alpha\|\le(17,17,18),\qquad
g\|\mathcal N^\dagger C_\alpha\|\le(323,322,134),
\]

with residual enclosures of order \(10^{-11}\) and a response-integral bound below \(29/g\). These yield the displayed \(671\) and \(31668\) theorem constants.

The \(C_\alpha\) are proof objects. They are never applied to the device and do not replace its decoder.

Reference correlations are retained using

\[
\left\|
\operatorname{Tr}_{\rm phys}[(A\otimes I_R)\rho]
\right\|_1\le\|A\|,
\]

improved to \(\|A\|q_b\) when \(A=Q_bAQ_b\), followed by the qubit Pauli expansion. This establishes a channel guarantee for arbitrary unknown inputs.

## 7. The remaining obstruction: preserving quantum indistinguishability during implementation
