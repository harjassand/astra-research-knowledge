I completed a different constructive result: an exact arithmetic description of entire ensembles of finite chain complexes. It gives exact counts, uniform generation of actual matrices, a reversible conversion between different ring arithmetics, and one finite matrix that determines the counts at every ring depth—including their precise growth and a limiting law for the homology reduction.

The previous quantum constructions are preserved, including the complete-profile classification, greatest representative, minimum output dimension and rational compiler. The distinction between constructing a described channel and processing an existing device’s output remains explicit; success, failure and unknown-input costs remain charged.

4. A stronger consequence: actual conversion between ring arithmetics

Equality of counts alone would leave the conversion problem untouched. The same coordinates give a deterministic bijection between the actual complex sets, preserving the entire residue differential.

The algorithm fixes one basis-selection rule depending only on the residue tuple. It then:

extracts the gauge coordinates and minimal homology problem in the source ring;
recursively transfers the smaller problem;
retains the free digit and gauge coordinates;
reconstructs using the target ring’s arithmetic;
reverses the common residue basis change.

The reverse procedure recovers the original matrices exactly.

This conversion uses no count table or enumeration of rank profiles. Under the explicit arithmetic model, it has polynomial cost even when the number of grades varies.
