# Additive Gaussian-noise comparator: primary-source check

Date: 2026-11-02. Convention: [q,p]=i and independent quadrature displacements have variance v=sigma^2. Unassisted quantum communication, no shared entanglement, no two-way classical side assistance. This is a focused primary-source review, not a proof of exhaustive absence of all unpublished results.

## Bottom line

I found no primary result certifying positive unassisted capacity at any v>1/e. The strongest verified general analytic positivity region remains 0<v<1/e, via R(v)=[log2(1/(e v))]_+. Known data-processing upper bounds force Q=0 at v>=1/2. Thus the concrete new-operation target is any finite-energy, finite-block input with rigorously positive coherent information at a specified v in (1/e,1/2). Such a witness is enough to prove positive asymptotic unassisted rate by coding the block channel; merely a drifting finite-size threshold is not.

The comparison is not unfairly against infinite-energy-only prior: for fixed v<1/e, thermal-state coherent information tends to a strictly positive value as mean energy N tends to infinity. Hence some finite N already has positive information. The infinite-energy limit determines the threshold but does not prevent finite-energy positive rate below it.

## Primary result-by-result audit

1. Lin and Noh, *Exploring the quantum capacity of a Gaussian random displacement channel using GKP codes and maximum likelihood decoding*, PRA111,052445(2025), https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04277v2 . Eq2 states the above capacity interval. Their distance-39 surface-square example has computed positive logical-Pauli hashing rate at sigma=.598 (v=.357604). High-resolution finite-size crossings are close to sigma=1/sqrt(e), with no demonstrated crossing of the hashing limit. Monte Carlo code-performance estimates are not certified capacity values.

2. Subramanian, Zheng and Jiang, *Achievable rates for concatenated square GKP codes*, PRXQuantum6,040342(2025), https://arxiv.org/html/2505.10499v1 . Large-prime-dimensional square GKP with analog information approaches log2(1/(e v)), removing earlier integer-rate restrictions. This is attainment of the standard displacement coherent-information bound, NOT a proof of full additive-noise capacity. Their pure-loss/amplification capacity result concerns different channels. Resource warning: the explicit polar scheme in Eq37/39 subtracts consumed Bell pairs, (|I|-|E|)/N; Appendix B explicitly invokes entanglement assistance. I found no vanishing-entanglement/unassisted implementation theorem in v1 (the only arXiv version listed). The same coherent information supports unassisted existence by LSD independently, but one must not silently call that efficient polar implementation unassisted. The published final PDF confirms and makes the consumption explicit, Eq39/41 and Appendix B2: https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/pdf/10.1103/56vj-z7h1 . Their finite-energy discussion uses a twirling approximation and does not itself prove exact finite-energy channel equivalence.

3. Zhang, Wu and Guo, *Concatenation of the GKP code with the XZZX surface code*, PRA107,062408(2023), https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.04383 . This is the important apparent counterexample: abstract claims threshold sigma approximately .67, v approximately .4489, using a rectangular inner GKP with lambda=2.1. Lin–Noh Appendix F explicitly revisits the claim: data only go through distance9; pairwise crossings decrease with distance; the data cannot confidently establish a threshold above 1/sqrt(e). Thus this is a published numerical threshold claim to disclose, not an established positive-rate comparator that can be omitted or treated as certified.

4. Raveendran et al., *Finite Rate QLDPC-GKP Coding Scheme that Surpasses the CSS Hamming Bound*, Quantum6,767(2022), https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2022-07-20-767/ and https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.07029 . Reported code-family thresholds around sigma=.557 and .547 are below .6065. The improved CSS Hamming benchmark is not the bosonic additive-noise hashing benchmark.

5. Agarwal et al., *Enhanced quantum capacity thresholds from symmetry*, https://arxiv.org/html/2605.09138v2 (May2026). Genuine substantial new degenerate-code improvement, but for qubit Pauli channels: reported independent-XZ threshold p=.118371 at block30 and depolarizing per-Pauli threshold p=.064657 at block45. No bosonic additive-noise conclusion is established. For comparison, a square GKP qubit with hard syndrome correction has independent bit/phase flip probability

   p(v)=sum_{m in Z}[Phi((2m+3/2)sqrt(pi/v))-Phi((2m+1/2)sqrt(pi/v))].

   Direct evaluation gives p(1/e)=.14396501752264257. Their p=.118371 corresponds to v=.3220552216653111, well below 1/e. Thus simply concatenating their strongest reported independent-XZ witness with square GKP and discarding analog information does not reach the target. This calculation does NOT exclude using their symmetry mechanism with analog syndrome information, squeezing, or a different inner code; those would need new construction/evaluation rather than borrowing the published threshold.

6. Noh, Pirandola and Jiang, *Enhanced energy-constrained quantum communication over bosonic Gaussian channels*, NatCommun11,457(2020), https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-14329-6 . Correlated Gaussian multimode/time-sharing inputs improve energy-constrained rates, including additive noise. This does not by itself extend the unconstrained positivity region: time sharing cannot turn a collection of nonpositive single-mode coherent-information values into a positive one.

7. Barber and Pirandola, *PPT-assisted capacities of bosonic Gaussian channels*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26783 , revised26Oct2026. Exact additive-noise capacity under completely PPT-preserving assistance is a different, strictly stronger resource model. It does not resolve unassisted positivity.

8. Mele et al., *Bosonic quantum communication beyond the thermal threshold*, https://arxiv.org/html/2607.27449v1 . Certified non-Gaussian positivity at thermal environment nu=1, transmissivity eta=.7841 is important but not an additive-noise witness. The article does not report the high-temperature/additive limit. Sender amplification gives N_v=Phi_eta,nu o A_(1/eta), v=(1-eta)(nu+1), so a positive additive witness at v>.4318 would improve that thermal benchmark; the implication cannot be reversed. Conversely Phi_eta,nu=L_eta o N_[(1-eta)nu/eta], so its known positive point only directly gives additive positivity at v=.2753475..., already inside the standard region.

## Remaining construction barrier

The rho>beta gate remains a sufficient, not necessary, criterion. The independent E8 and diagonal symplectic-squeeze search did not produce a positive gate beyond hashing; negative diagnostics are saved in E8_AND_ZAK_INDEPENDENT_NOTE.md. The exact local-Zak finite-energy bridge is credible once a strict ideal margin exists, but is not that missing margin and does not supply a practical finite-resource syndrome decoder. A seed/witness with a certified positive margin beyond 1/e remains indispensable.
