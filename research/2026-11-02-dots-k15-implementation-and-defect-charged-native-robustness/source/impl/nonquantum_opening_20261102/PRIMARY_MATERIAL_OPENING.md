# Primary operating ledger: damage-tolerant Li-metal electrolytes

2026-11-02. Independent selector. **No new device or positive all-cycle containment theorem claimed.**

## Useful result

The consequential missing operation is excluding a spanning metallic path despite **bulk nucleation, fracture, and cycling**, rather than maintaining a pristine planar interface. Pure geometric steering fails: any nonzero vertical allowance admits a thin spanning filament; finite volume alone cannot exclude it without a physical minimum cross-section or crossing-work bound. A sharpened guard construction must minimize insertion free energy over crack formation, plastic relaxation, phase change, and electronic access—not assume an intact membrane. No acquired material or construction in this pass establishes that bound.

The final bounded material check, lithiated SmNiO3 as a self-insulating electronic guard, fails its leakage gate with the published material parameters. Do not promote it as a positive witness.

## 4. Bounded Mott-insulating guard check: negative witness

Sun et al., PNAS115,9672–9677(2018), "Strongly correlated perovskite lithium ion shuttles":
https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1805029115
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6166818/
Author copy: https://yildizgroup.scripts.mit.edu/home/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/pnas_2018.pdf

Reported saturated Li-SNO resistivity~10^8mΩ·cm; ionic conductivity3.1e-3S/cm, activation barrier~0.24eV. Films80–200nm; full electronic-insulating/color transition after~2h lithiation. Li:Ni~0.86. Cycling:5μA/cm²,0.5–4.2V vsLi,50 cycles,96.8% mean CE, using liquid electrolyte/polymer separator. Transparency persists through cycling. Synthesis includes500°C,1,500psi oxygen annealing for24h. No acquired evidence establishes equilibrium insertion plateau, stability against Li0, or near-zero-potential reverse Mott reset. Switching charge was not directly acquired.

**Own decisive check:** 10^8mΩ·cm=10^5Ω·cm, hence sigma_e≈10^-5S/cm—four orders MORE electronic conductivity than the native garnet in section1. At200nm, electronic area resistance is~2Ωcm²;100mV would drive50mA/cm² absent other series resistance. In-plane measurements do not prove this exact through-plane leakage, but cannot witness a better guard without an independently demonstrated anisotropy. High ON/OFF ratio relative to metallic SNO is insufficient; the absolute OFF leakage is the essential gate. The material therefore supplies no credible positive firewall construction. A generic pre-lithiated-oxide coating is neither new nor a recovered reset mechanism.

## Verdict

Earned contribution: actual operating/source ledger, strong competing mechanisms, and rejection of the tempting geometry-only and SmNiO3 witnesses. Remaining robust-insertion-gap construction is substantive but unearned. Do not turn a conditional energy inequality whose premise is an unbreakable guard into a claimed scientific advance.
