# Selection failures and retained boundaries

This is NOT a positive-opening report. Three local routes have been checked and rejected as the current mission's answer.

1. Generic nonequilibrium transport of metastable Gibbs measures. Independent C-infinity hidden-well construction in PROOF.md and check_construction.py closes the paid-discovery gate negatively. Eight-dimensional finite diagnostics pass. He–Zhang 2025 already proves stronger oracle lower bounds. No novelty claim.
2. Explicit frustrated long-range Ising chains. A positive algebraic route exists: approximate r^-alpha by an acquired O(log^2(n beta/epsilon))-term positive exponential sum; carry each exponential interaction history through a rounded deterministic finite-state recursion; a pointwise energy budget yields absolute logZ and full Gibbs-law accuracy, in exp(O(log^3(n beta/epsilon))) work. Canonical magnetization may be tracked exactly. This would remove mixing at growing beta but is an acquired variant of Kac–Baker/Ruelle/history-grid/MPO machinery, with an astronomical conservative state count even at moderate n. It does not pass the consequence gate. Strong comparators: Hilgert–Mayer 2001 https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0111063 ; Achutha–Kim–Kimura–Kuwahara 2025 https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02819 ; Crosson–Harrow 2021 https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.02144 . No full solver fixture was commissioned.
3. Near-harmonic natural-Hamiltonian universality. Astra N1226 at pinned 4ec446825778d0fb7ff7c6017818964a84f5dcd7 is stronger than its provisional summary: full V2 source Section9 already supplies a torus tube and finite-Fourier contraction/effective interface. It does not solve finite-precision/noisy initialization, transverse robustness, or polynomial/analytic realization. Do not call it the missing capability.
