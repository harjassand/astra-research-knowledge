# Exact canonical trace-polymer representation after induced-edge completion

2026-11-02. Constructor proof using the actual union-seeded decoder design. Root's explicit T-plus repair below is NEW representation bookkeeping; it is not silently attributed to the older removal-only theorem. Subject to the already-declared actual decoder's fixed-stencil/component-local properties, this proves forward and reverse assembly.

## 1. Precise representation

Let G be the fixed bounded-degree ambient space-scale graph containing packet-owner connectors, every branch's map/output/correction stencils, residual-row operands, cross-layer dependencies, physical prepass gates and the finite terminal bag. The deterministic decoder is unchanged.

For a valid syndrome sigma, form its existing charged full trace T(sigma). Let V(sigma) be its charged vertex set, with the existing finite labels for input values, corrections, output values, branch choices and gate data. Define

  T_plus(sigma) = G[V(sigma)],

retaining the same labels. Thus add every ambient edge whose endpoints are ALREADY in V(sigma). Add no vertex, activate no new gate, and perform no recursive zero closure.

This operation can only merge whole existing trace components. Every new component's base syndrome remains a valid removable union by the earned restriction/telescoping theorem. Its vertex count is unchanged and remains at most C times its occupied base-check mass. Ambient degree stays bounded.

## 2. Explicit decoder premises used beyond deletion

The actual join states the following structural rules; these are essential to reverse assembly.

(A) At each layer, the component-activation graph is generated by bounded fixed footprints of the occupied input packet blocks, with fixed graph edges/overlap rules. Both branch maps and all potential collisions are included before choosing a branch. There is no activation predicate requiring an unrecorded conjunction of two or more separate components.

(B) Each full input packet is owned by one component; all operands of a potentially shared output packet or correction coordinate lie in the pre-choice dependency stencils. Component outputs and correction coordinates lie inside these stencils.

(C) Component choices and outputs are deterministic functions of that component's labeled inputs only. There is no global enumeration tie-breaker, history state, or off-component control bit.

(D) Nonzero coarse outputs alone seed the next layer. A halo zero does not recursively seed another footprint. The physical prepass and residual rows use complete bounded gate/operand stencils, including gates whose outputs cancel.

(E) The finite terminal bag appears iff terminal input is nonzero and contains every terminal input/correction coordinate; the zero decoder output is zero.

The old injective-removal estimate alone would not imply (A)-(E). Here they are properties of the specified union-seeded all-branch decoder.

## 3. Polymers

A polymer gamma is a nonempty labeled induced trace

  gamma=T_plus(tau_gamma)

that is connected and has valid nonzero base syndrome tau_gamma. Its mass is m(gamma)=|tau_gamma|. Only canonical labels generated by replaying the decoder on tau_gamma are admitted.

Declare gamma and eta incompatible if their charged vertex sets share a vertex or have an edge of G between them. This is hard-core incompatibility on complete charged stencils, not only on occupied checks.

## 4. Forward decomposition

Given sigma, take the connected components gamma_i of T_plus(sigma). Each is a union of whole original T components, so its restricted base syndrome tau_i is valid. Existing deletion stability gives that rerunning on tau_i retains exactly that component's charged record: any vertex shared with a discarded component would have merged them; every retained seeded stencil keeps its occupied owner; complete operands preserve correction identities. Induced-edge completion then gives T_plus(tau_i)=gamma_i.

The tau_i have disjoint complete input packet blocks and sum to sigma. The gamma_i are mutually nonadjacent in G because they are components of the induced graph G[V(sigma)]. They are therefore compatible polymers. Uniqueness follows from uniqueness of the canonical decoder trace and its connected-component decomposition.

## 5. Reverse assembly, by layer induction

Take any finite compatible collection gamma_1,...,gamma_r and set sigma=sum_i tau_i. Validity follows because every tau_i is a valid coboundary. For the packet sector, compatibility ensures disjoint initial packet blocks, so no initial cancellation occurs between polymers. For the opposite-sector physical prepass, start instead with disjoint physical occupied leaves: distinct polymers contributing to the same F row share its activated gate and are incompatible, including when a standalone XOR output is zero. The prepass therefore reproduces the old outputs without cross cancellation, after which the induced dual packet blocks are disjoint. No unrecorded physical packet partition is assumed.

At the first layer, the occupied inputs are exactly the disjoint union of the inputs in the individual runs. By (A), their seeded footprints are the same union of individual fixed footprints. No two distinct polymer footprints can meet or acquire a fixed dependency edge: any such connection would lie in G between already recorded charged vertices and violate compatibility. Therefore each active component is exactly one component from an individual run. By (C), its choice, correction and outputs are identical to that run.

Potential cross-polymer output or correction cancellation is impossible by (B): a coordinate potentially receiving both contributions would belong to both complete charged stencils, or their fixed dependency connection, making the polymers incompatible. Hence the next-layer nonzero input is exactly the disjoint union of individual outputs.

Repeat the argument at every layer. Condition (D) prevents a newly assembled collection of incidental zeros from generating a latent gate or component. Complete prepass gates obey the same union argument; no missing cancellation operand appears only after assembly. At the terminal level at most one polymer has nonzero input, since any two such polymers would share the terminal bag. Condition (E) therefore reproduces the terminal action exactly.

Consequently V(sigma)=disjoint union_i V(tau_i), with all canonical labels unchanged. Compatibility excludes cross edges in G, so

  T_plus(sum_i tau_i)=disjoint union_i gamma_i.

This proves the reverse map and exact bijection.

## 6. Exact commuting Gibbs gas

For a stabilizer reference with energy cost epsilon per violated check (epsilon=2J for -J sum S_a), every valid syndrome sector has the same logical dimension. Therefore the full syndrome partition function, up to that constant multiplicity and the vacuum energy, is exactly

  Xi = sum_{compatible collections Gamma} product_{gamma in Gamma} z_gamma,
  z_gamma=exp[-beta epsilon m(gamma)].

This is not a comparison measure or a truncated approximation. Both CSS sectors can be represented separately or in a disjoint-copy ambient graph; stabilizer relations only restrict which canonical polymers exist. They are not ignored.

The charged trace bound |V(gamma)|<=C m and bounded-degree ambient graph imply a bound A^m on labeled polymers of mass m containing any fixed vertex. Taking a(gamma)=a m(gamma),

  sum_{eta incompatible gamma} |z_eta| exp[a m(eta)]
     <= C' m(gamma) sum_{k>=1} A^k exp[-(beta epsilon-a)k].

For sufficiently cold beta, the right side is <=a m(gamma), giving ordinary hard-core KP convergence and all rooted connected/Ursell estimates. This is stronger than a two-point clustering assertion and supplies a legitimate reference for joint bounded-observable cumulants.

## 7. Scope of the new result

- Native clock free energy induces additional weak interactions between syndrome polymers; the bare product activities above describe the commuting reference only.
- Time-dependent quantum source coefficients are not bounded functions of one static syndrome with beta-uniform norm. The annealed Hölder/orbit step must be combined with this gas without losing positivity or denominator cancellation.
- Polynomial stabilizer fillings may enlarge observable dependence regions; their incidence must be included in any cumulant bound.
- This representation alone does not establish native quantum state preparation, physical thermalization, or noisy WRITE.

## 8. Minimal failure criterion / repair boundary

The T-plus repair resolves missing edges between already charged vertices. It does NOT repair a decoder whose assembly creates genuinely NEW unrecorded vertices through a latent multi-input predicate, off-component branch decision, or recursive zero activation. Such a failure would require adding the missing bounded potential footprint to the original charge accounting, and proving the enlarged shape remains O(m). The actual all-branch, all-collision, input-seeded design is what excludes this failure.
