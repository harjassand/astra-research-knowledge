# Cost and comparison boundary of finite Jensen receiving

This is the exact finite intermediate inequality from the accepted parallel
construction, not another hidden high-dimensional solver. Its new operation is
ONE scalar resistance integral, evaluated by paid rational powers, integer
fourth roots and monotone Darboux sums. It inherits all raw acquisition, geometric
tail, fixed-precision, budget and serialization limits of the accepted quartic
engine. The old radial factor is still computed as an unused intermediate, and
can cause UNKNOWN; that cost/limitation is not erased by the new mathematics.

The same external quadrature tolerance controls inherited numerical work. A
separately supplied desired full-width ratio is checked exactly. It is NOT an
instruction to loop until success: if the current finite bracket is too wide,
UNKNOWN is correct. Even infinite numerical precision cannot remove all slack
in the Jensen lower bound at fixed m/kappa. The consumer is sound incomplete.

All power/precision budgets are retained, and max_points now caps inherited
function evaluations PLUS all new integrand evaluations, even at reused physical
coordinates. This conservative global charging prevents a separate wrapper from
silently bypassing the caller's scalar-work cap. Verification repeats all paid
operations, including the costly m512 execution. Timers start after a raw Python
dictionary has been created; import, fixture creation, JSON input/output and
complete external audit work are additional costs, not included in reported
producer seconds. Dense exponent-vector input size remains fully charged.

The new scalar function uses powered-rational bits O(m*(input bits+log N)), then
adds4P bits for the fourth-root shift. A final N-cell sum adds N+1 such function
values. These are real bigint/accumulation costs. The largest power-bit proxy is
not a claim of whole-program bit budget or memory accounting.

Strong retaining comparison: an equally informed ordinary implementation of the
newly derived finite parallel/Jensen inequality gets exactly the same result.
This does not demonstrate a separation from ordinary methods, nor establish that
an independent located prior paper already supplied this finite raw-polynomial
certificate. The accepted parallel ledger retains the genuine hard-channel
fixed-channel bound, local-capacity/flatness methods, sampled committor profiles,
and ordinary scalar free-energy computation. None is deliberately restricted to
blind rare crossing samples. Full PDE or other stronger variational constructions
may close the remaining gap better; no general lower bound against them is made.

For unit parameters the raw 513D chain has a proven sub-1% bracket around a rate
of order 10^-37 without computing its angular partition function. This is an
executed finite consequence, distinct from asymptotic exactness. It does not
license arbitrary-start inverse-rate waiting times or a measured physical rate.
