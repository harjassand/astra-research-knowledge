# Information reduction, costs and strongest retaining alternatives

## What information is and is not acquired

The entire raw potential is still read and verified by the accepted aligned
quartic parser: physical axis, homogeneous degree, kappa, all coefficients,
coercivity and complete F=vU identity. One cannot ignore an extra coefficient
because angular details happen to disappear after the model is received.

The new task supplies NO unknown matrix or numerical mobility oracle. It asks
for a statement quantified over every finite constant SPD transverse matrix
under an explicit block/FDT model interface. Those are different access semantics
from a claim about a particular unidentified laboratory process. The latter's
membership in this family is not authenticated by raw potential coefficients.
The receiver binds that exclusion and refuses other family tags.

Unknown transverse matrix entries, shape, scale and conditioning do not appear
in either scalar endpoint formula. There is therefore no hidden O(m^3) matrix
inversion or diagonalization executed. The proof's M^-1 Lyapunov function and
limiting matrices are analytical existence objects, not algorithmic inputs.
Acquisition of a specifically supplied rational M, a matrix learning algorithm,
or recovery of gamma from a target rate are not implemented or claimed.

## Numerical work actually paid

The receiver reruns the entire accepted finite-Jensen operation from ORIGINAL raw
input and external beta/tolerance/full-width/budgets. This includes the unused
radial-factor intermediate and its possible UNKNOWN outcomes, input scanning,
all adaptive old scalar sums, explicit unbounded tail and the added resistance.
The 513D example pays 131,074 scalar evaluations and bigint/root work; family
receiving actually repeats that computation. In-memory timing excludes fixture,
module/proof hashing/import and JSON costs, which are additional real work.

New proof/model descriptors and strict recursive comparisons cost only their
explicit record size. No high-dimensional matrix is read because none is in this
universally quantified input. Shared descriptor aliases are avoided by fresh
nested records. Optional float displays/timings/work diagnostics remain outside
verified mathematical fields. Output is a closed numerical OUTER enclosure; the
exact open lower endpoint is not numerically evaluated. Tight requested width
can still fail due to intrinsic Jensen slack or finite budgets.

## Why the exact-range theorem is not a free inverse solver

Weak H1 compactness proves the infinite-mobility limit; a finite but possibly
unknown angular energy proves the zero-mobility limit. Concavity/continuity
proves each intermediate rate is attained. None supplies a paid finite gamma
or an executable search bracket for one. The code reports this mathematical
characterization, not an invented realizing mobility or Gamma integral value.
The two exact endpoints are limits, never claimed attained by a finite SPD M.

The strongest useful information consequence is partial identification: the
observable can be tightly bounded despite completely unidentified transverse
rates. Conversely, if a correctly specified target conductance is outside this
certified family enclosure, no choice of its hidden constant SPD matrix can
repair the discrepancy. That is a mathematical family exclusion, not validation
of an experiment or an assertion that every interior numerical value is attained
(the numerical enclosure can extend outside the exact open range).

## Ordinary retaining comparisons

Rayleigh/Dirichlet monotonicity, an infimum of affine energies being concave,
weak Hilbert compactness, local elliptic regularity and trace closure are ordinary
mathematical ingredients. A fully informed ordinary implementation using the
same exact-range proof and accepted finite scalar engine gets the same output.
This is not a separation from ordinary computation and not evidence that a
located prior work already supplied this particular acquired range theorem.
Historical matching remains open.

The accepted parallel ledger already retains genuine primary fixed-channel
lower bounds (Mangeat–Guérin–Dean), local-capacity/flatness prediction
(Loper–Zhou–Geman), sampled variational committor profiles and exact homogeneous
free-energy calculations. Those strong comparators remain; no claim rests only
on comparison against naive inverse-rate trajectory counting. Full PDE/current
methods may yield better information about a fixed M or an inverse problem.

A general potential's slow/fast transverse variational limits can be considered
with additional premises, but this source-bound received class remains the
explicit homogeneous polynomial family. No arbitrary positivity, unknown basis,
noisy coefficients, nonreversible stirring or changed physical metric is smuggled
into the parser. The new scalar-exact range characterization is separate from
all earlier accepted artifacts until independently reviewed.
