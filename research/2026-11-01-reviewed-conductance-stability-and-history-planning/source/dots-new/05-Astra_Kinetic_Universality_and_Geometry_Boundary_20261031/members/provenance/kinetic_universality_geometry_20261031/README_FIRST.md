# Kinetic universality and its paid geometry boundary

Start here. This uniquely named provenance folder supplements, and does not
replace, any inherited README, manifest, proof, or acceptance receipt.

## What is actually available

The accepted raw coupled-quartic/finite-Jensen receiver can provide a sub-1%
closed OUTER enclosure of the full equilibrium capacity/mass conductance for
EVERY fixed finite constant SPD transverse mobility M, without identifying its
entries or relaxation rates, AFTER the no-cross diag(1,M), reversible/FDT model
interface is justified. The accepted 513D example has exact enclosure ratio
about 1.00672978, below 1.01. The exact rational certificate is authoritative.
This is not authentication of a particular physical process from its potential.

The exact attainable OPEN range endpoints are mathematical formulas. They are
not both returned numerical endpoints; the finite Jensen lower bound is an
outer bound, not the exact lower endpoint. No inverse solver or realizing
mobility matrix is supplied. Endpoint limits are not attained by finite SPD
members. No arbitrary-start waiting time, finite-time crossing CDF, rapid
mixing, or physical implementation is certified.

## The sharp counter-boundary

For the separately received fixed 2D potential and full-fiber basins, allowing
D=[[1,b],[b,c]] with c>b^2 changes the problem. Capacity tends to zero if and
only if BOTH 1-b^2/c tends to zero AND |b| tends to infinity. The exact smooth
patch satisfies every basin fiber, and each finite actual diffusion is globally
nonexplosive and reversible with the same invariant law and D_yy=1.

This does NOT say that any cross covariance destroys prediction: bounded cross
amplitude retains a positive lower bound. The construction pays vanishing
conditional longitudinal mobility, diverging transverse/cross noise, severe
conditioning and exact parameter/precision costs. Its finite counterexample
certifies conductance strictly below 1.441e-6; do NOT round this to <=1.44e-6,
because the exact rational upper bound contains a positive tail term.

The separately reviewed high-dimensional Gaussian corollary transfers the
same paid obstruction to a relative freely-equilibrated benchmark; it does not
extend the quartic receiver automatically.
