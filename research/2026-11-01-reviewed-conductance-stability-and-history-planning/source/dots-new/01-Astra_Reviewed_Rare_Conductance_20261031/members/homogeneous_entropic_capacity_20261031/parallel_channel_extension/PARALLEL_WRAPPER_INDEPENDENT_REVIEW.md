## Actual operation and theorem join

The wrapper checks the inherited module hash before import and delegates the raw polynomial recognition, physical axis/m/degree acquisition, global received grammar and finite scalar integral bounds to that exact module. It does not accept a supplied fiber sampler, reaction coordinate, partition function or Gamma integral.

With the acquired positive rational kappa it constructs the exact rational

    s=(16+23*kappa)/(14*(1+kappa)).

At the inherited integer precision p, root_bounds(s,p,2) returns an upper integer h with (h/2^p)^2>=s. The pre-operation check bits(s)+2p<=max_power_bits correctly bounds the shifted numerator used by the root operation. The factor f=h/2^p is positive and at least C_parallel. Thus, if [l,u] is the inherited outward k_free interval, [l/f,u] encloses the actual capacity/mass. The endpoint orientation is correct. No assertion that the outward factor is exact is needed.

The wrapper replaces the schema, theorem method, factor fields and full-rate interval; it does not mutate the inherited raw or scalar source. Its full-rate upper endpoint and free-rate interval are unchanged. Strict positivity comes from the accepted raw grammar and inherited positive mass/resistance admission. The fixed numerical precision is sufficient for these executed cases; general budget exhaustion still returns UNKNOWN.

## Receiving and limitations

verify() recomputes certify() from the independently supplied raw polynomial and external task parameters. It strictly binds method/schema/scalar source hash, exact squared factor, task, basins, observable, factor, numerical grid/cutoff/tail/precision, full acquisition record except timing, and every exact endpoint of all four intervals. The external task-key allowlist rejects unknown parameters. Inherited strict rational/integer checks reject bool/float aliases where those are prohibited.

Timing fields, display decimals and other diagnostic work counters are not part of the verified mathematical interval. Altering a display float does not alter the exact certificate and is intentionally accepted. A caller must use exact endpoint fields and the verifier's declared capacity scope, not treat arbitrary extra metadata or prose as authenticated. This is exact known-model scientific receiving, not physical-model calibration, signed provenance or a sampler/MFPT certificate.

The wrapper still executes the inherited radial factor computation as an unused intermediate. Its time, bit growth, serialization limits and possible refusals are real. A tiny d_min or beta can therefore affect this implementation's admission even though the NEW theorem's kinetic factor does not depend on them. Mathematical independence of the factor is not zero acquisition cost or completeness of this wrapper. The existing explicit raw grammar and budgets remain unchanged; arbitrary rotated/positive polynomial recognition is not added.
