# Strong retaining comparisons for the parallel construction

This continuation was found by challenging the previously frozen radial-current
certificate, not by assuming that its mechanism was optimal. It uses the SAME
raw task and q/MA/R0 information. The radial theorem remains valid but is not the
strongest known construction on the unit homogeneous family.

1. Weighted one-dimensional Cauchy, Dirichlet lower bounds obtained by discarding
   nonnegative energy terms, and series/parallel Thomson principles are ordinary
   classical machinery. We do not claim those principles as new. The derived
   homogeneous energy/Gamma/Jensen/slab-contraction estimate supplies the explicit
   uniform profile factor; a located prior theorem giving precisely this factor
   and acquired raw consumer has not been established. Adding our estimate to an
   ordinary comparator produces the same result, not an independent prior win.

2. Mangeat, Guérin, Dean, *Dispersion in two dimensional channels—the Fick-Jacobs
   approximation revisited*, primary arXiv1710.02699v2:
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.02699
   PDF opened; SectionVII Eq98–103 and introductory scope read. They derive the
   lower bound D_eff/D>=h_min/<h> using an unobstructed common channel, including
   a nonzero limit with slow transverse equilibration. Their exact numerical
   framework avoids Brownian trajectory simulation. This is a real prior analogue
   of our fixed-channel idea, not merely a weak trajectory-sampling comparator.
   It concerns periodic hard-wall diffusivity, not the present soft-potential
   full-basin capacity/mass. A common-channel-style bound in our problem uses
   R(t)<=exp(a t), giving k_lower/k_free=R0/a^alpha; this can decay like
   alpha^(-1/2). Retaining all energy channels and the Gamma/contraction comparison
   gives our uniform multiplicative guarantee. No general superiority is claimed.

3. Loper, Zhou, Geman, *Capacities and the Free Passage of Entropic Barriers*,
   primary arXiv1811.00205v2 (earlier title *Overcoming Entropic Barriers by Capacity
   Hopping*): https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.00205
   PDF opened; introduction, Theorem2 and capacity/locality scope read. They obtain
   nearly initial-state-invariant small-target hitting probabilities from local
   capacities under epsilon-flatness, with local Monte Carlo estimators. The
   paper explicitly distinguishes this hitting-probability question from time
   information. Thus capacities/local invariance already support accelerated
   entropic-barrier understanding. Their assumptions and target differ from our
   full-fiber rate certificate, which does not acquire epsilon-flatness or target
   neighborhoods. No claim to invent capacity-based avoidance of rare trajectories.

4. The earlier consumer retains the strong sampled committor/profile comparator
   of Petersen–Lichtinger–Covino(2026), including its approximate boundary-fidelity
   caveat, and the ordinary exact homogeneity/virial/free-energy alternatives.
   Those ledger files and source hashes are dependencies, not silently amended.
