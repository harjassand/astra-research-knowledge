# Primary sources and conventional machinery

All research here is read-only, purely mathematical and non-operational. The theorem is internally constructed and audited; historical priority is not cleared. No external publication, outreach or upload is involved.

## Forward-support and invariant-face geometry

[Angeli, De Leenheer and Sontag, *A Petri net approach to the study of persistence in chemical reaction networks*](https://www.sontaglab.org/FTPDIR/angeli_leenheer_sontag_math_biosciences_MBS-D-06-00188R1.pdf), Definition 3.3 and Proposition 5.5, were inspected in the author-hosted 25-page manuscript. The paper relates siphons to forward-invariant coordinate faces. Our finite support closure and complementary closed-face geometry are ordinary instances of that framework, not a newly invented graph principle. We use it only for the highest-order conservative vector field, not as a claimed general persistence or stochastic recurrence theorem.

## Fluid and converse-Lyapunov comparison

[Dai and Meyn (1995), *Stability and convergence of moments for multiclass queueing networks via fluid limit models*](https://people.orie.cornell.edu/jdai/publications/daiMeyn95.pdf), Theorem 4.1, Proposition 6.1 and Theorem 6.2, were inspected in the accessible author-hosted full paper. Fluid stability supplying recurrence and Lyapunov/moment control is a strong conventional comparator. Their model and hypotheses are queue-specific; we do not treat the theorem as a ready-made all-exponential result for our generator. Our finite-time Euler-sum coboundary is an explicit ordinary Lyapunov acquisition construction in this spirit, with all constants derived directly.

[Gamarnik and Meyn, *On Exponential Ergodicity of Multiclass Queueing Networks*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0612544), accessible primary abstract, describes a stable-fluid queueing example without exponential stationary tails. Only abstract scope was inspected. It prevents equating generic fluid stability with our all-exponential conclusion. That conclusion is instead proved by the exact count-normalized generator inequality, constant immigration and linear death.

## Pólya coefficient positivity

[Powers and Reznick, *A new bound for Pólya's Theorem with applications to polynomials positive on polyhedra*](https://www.math.emory.edu/~vicki/pub/polya.pdf), accessible author-hosted file, currently exposes only its one-page introduction. That page states the classical strict-simplex-positivity to coefficient-positivity principle. We credit Pólya rather than claiming a new positivity theorem. We do not claim to have inspected the quantitative bound in the full article; our conservative falling-factorial degree-elevation estimate is proved completely in the package.
