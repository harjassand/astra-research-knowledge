ACQUISITION, LIFETIME SEMANTICS, AND A SIGNED RESPONSE CERTIFICATE
Ordinary proof and executable validation, 2026-10-31

1. Exact scope discovery from imperfect component observations
--------------------------------------------------------------
Each source p_j:{0,1}^N->[0,1] is an unknown d-junta. Every relevant coordinate
has some context at which its single-coordinate effect is at least gamma.
The learner has only chosen-context independent Bernoulli experiments.
Neither its response table nor scope is supplied. Context preparation is
charged; the fixture charges N bit-settings per represented trial.

Let J be the currently witnessed scope. For each assignment of J, independently
sample L uniform completions outside J. Conditional on the past, a particular
assignment to the remaining true parents is absent with probability at most
exp(-L/2^d). With at most B completion tests, L>=2^d log(2B 2^d/delta) ensures
all patterns are covered in every tested block except with probability delta/2.
Let Q be a deterministic cap on all distinct mean assays. Radius r and at least
log(4Q/delta)/(2r^2) independent trials per assay ensure every estimated mean
is within r except with probability delta/2. Both factors of two are necessary
for this two-event allocation. Adaptive context choices do not invalidate the
bound: apply the estimate tail bound conditionally to each fresh chosen query.

Use r=gamma/(256 max(1,d)). If all tested completion ranges are at most gamma/2,
coverage implies no parent is missing: the gamma witness of any missing parent
occurs within the block for the witness's current-J assignment, and its range
cannot shrink by more than 2r. Otherwise a witnessed endpoint pair has true
contrast >gamma/2-2r. The pair agrees on J.

Flip its differing outside-J coordinates in a fixed order. The resulting chain
need not be monotone; ordinary one-child sign bisection would be unjustified.
Bisect intervals and prune any interval whose empirical endpoint contrast is
at most t=gamma/(16 max(1,d)). Search both surviving children if necessary.
Every surviving interval has true endpoint contrast at least t-2r>0, so it
contains at least one genuinely relevant coordinate. Disjoint intervals at a
given depth can therefore number at most d. The depth is ceil(log2 N).

If the search had no surviving singleton, the terminal pruned intervals would
partition the chain. At most d of those intervals can contain true parents;
all others have exactly zero true endpoint contrast. The triangle inequality
would bound the total contrast by d(t+2r)<gamma/4, contradicting the initial
true contrast >gamma/2-2r. A surviving singleton therefore names a true parent.
At most d enrichments are possible.

One deterministic cap is B=M(d+1)2^d and
Q=B L+M d^2 ceil(log2 N)+1. If a bad observation transcript causes more than d
live intervals or exhausts a cap, the implementation returns a marked failure;
it never silently treats that transcript as a certified dictionary. These caps
are not reached on the two confidence events. The zero-junta promise requires
no discovery trials. The sharper actual count of assignment blocks across
successive scope sizes is M sum_{s=0}^d 2^s, not M(d+1)2^d.

The code uses an exact rational upper bound for logarithms:
ln(x)<=ceil(log2(x)) for x>=1. Confidence caps therefore do not require a
floating-point logarithm to round in a favorable direction.

2. What is retained and the precise lifetime statement
------------------------------------------------------
After discovering J_j, retain an experimental address (j,c) for each assignment
c of J_j. Executing it means setting x_{J_j}=c, setting other coordinates to
zero, and taking a fresh physical observation. No estimated response means
are used as constants in this dictionary. On successful scope acquisition,
each address has exactly the correct response law for every full context that
projects to c.

Conditional on every successful acquisition transcript, all future fresh
experiments are independent with the true requested means. Any adaptive policy
may use the whole past to choose its next certified probability program. Each
factory therefore has its ideal conditional law, and induction gives every
finite future transcript its ideal conditional joint law. The associated laws
on infinite transcripts agree as well, by equality on cylinder events.

For an unconditional comparison, couple ideal and implemented executions with
the same acquisition transcript, policy randomness, and future source and
factory randomness. On the one scope-success event they agree forever. Hence
their full transcript laws differ in total variation by at most delta. This
does not compare a conditional policy distribution with an unconditional one,
and does not claim that a finite retained data set supplies unlimited free
independent samples. Every new physical source call is charged.

3. Explicit relative derivative certificate for a signed matrix program
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Let M(p)=M0+sum_i p_i M_i have rational signed coefficient matrices. Let R be a
rational upper bound on sup_{p in cube}||M(p)||_2, and let
L=max(1,sum_i ||M_i||_2), using explicit rational norm upper bounds. Set
A=I+MM^T, X=A^{-1}, v=b^T X b, f=v/(1+v), h=f(1-f), with rational unit b.
Actual rational elimination computes Xb; no inverse or downstream-solver
oracle is supplied. A is positive definite on the whole cube, so elimination
pivots can be chosen with nonzero constant terms at every rational grid point.

Let B=1+2RL+L^2. First derivatives of A have norm at most 2RL, second
derivatives at most 2L^2, and higher derivatives vanish. For |alpha|=k,
differentiate AX=I. Grouping the multivariate Leibniz coefficients by order
gives sum_{|beta|=j,beta<=alpha} binom(alpha,beta)=binom(k,j). Since ||X||<=1,
induction therefore yields

 ||partial^alpha X||
 <= k![(2RL) B^(k-1) + L^2 B^(k-2)] <= k! B^k,    k>=2,

with k=1 handled directly by 2RL<=B. Thus |partial^alpha v|<=k!B^k.
Put g=(1+v)^(-1). Its normalized inverse recurrence is bounded by
c_0<=1 and c_k<=sum_{j=1}^k B^j c_{k-j}; hence c_k<=2^(k-1)B^k for k>=1,
and the simpler bound |partial^alpha g|<=k!(2B)^k suffices.

Because f=1-g, the same bound holds for positive-order derivatives of f.
Because h=g-g^2, Leibniz gives

 |partial^alpha h| <= (k+2) k!(2B)^k <= k!(6B)^k,    k>=1.

The spectral inequalities give 1/(1+R^2)<=v<=1, and thus
1/(2+R^2)<=f<=1/2 and h>=eta=1/[2(2+R^2)]. Consequently the integer

 K=ceil(12(1+2RL+L^2)(2+R^2))

satisfies |partial^alpha f|<=k!K^k h and
|partial^alpha h|<=k!K^k h at every positive order needed by the compiler;
the order-zero h inequality is tautological. This is an explicit analytic
certificate, not an empirical smoothness assertion.

Expected compiler cost is polynomial in this K, hence in the magnitudes R,L.
It is not automatically polynomial in log R or log L. If the intended consumer
uses M normalized by a rational sum-of-norms upper bound, then R,L can both be
at most one (apart from the harmless L>=1 convention), giving a fixed K.
Normalization changes the requested numerical response and cannot be silently
applied to an unnormalized user query.

Exact finite jets can be obtained by rational elimination in the truncated
multivariate polynomial ring. For fixed derivative order, rational inverse
entries and their derivatives have determinant/minor denominators of only
polynomially growing degree. Standard determinant height bounds then give
polynomial bit lengths in matrix dimension, coefficient bit length, and grid
argument bit length. This does not assert a universal numerical advantage:
the signed consumer is an explicit supported operation, not a lower-bound
separation from every conventional exactification method.

4. Executed acquisition fixture
-------------------------------
Files: acquisition_dictionary.py, acquisition_fixture_demo.py,
acquisition_fixture_results.json. The separate hidden fixture has N=24,
M=3, d=3, gamma=1/4, delta=1/100 and dyadic nonmonotone tables. The learner
receives only the chosen-context CountSource interface and public promises.
The hidden scopes are (1,5), (2,8,13), and (4,11,20). They are used solely by
the source and the post-learning validator, never by the learner.

The run acquired all three exact scopes with 4,057 distinct mean assays,
108,527,616 represented bit trials per assay, and therefore
440,296,538,112 charged physical bit trials. It also charges
10,567,116,914,688 context-setting bit operations. The chain searches used
25 queried midpoints and 19 pruned intervals; maximum simultaneously live
intervals was two. Discovery orders included (2,13,8) and (4,20,11), so address
canonicalization is not accidentally relying on discovery order.

The acquired dictionary has 20 entries. Exact validation checked 1,280
full-context/address identifications. A later address policy executed 256
fresh calls, with each next address depending on the previous output.
Those calls are included in the source's separate total-trial ledger.

The code uses independent NumPy binomial simulation draws at exactly
representable dyadic parameters to avoid materializing hundreds of billions
of bits. All represented individual trials remain charged. This is an
executable observation-to-dictionary check, not a physical deployment, a
claim of optimal calibration, or an execution of the generic enormous n0
factory. Its sufficient acquisition bill is intentionally reported intact.
