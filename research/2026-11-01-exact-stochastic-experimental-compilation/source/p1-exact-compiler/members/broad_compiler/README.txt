EXACT STOCHASTIC COMPILATION -- SCIENTIFIC CONCLUSION AND CODE

Start with PROOF.txt. The new main construction compiles a rational probability
program at UNKNOWN experimental means into an exactly distributed finite bit,
with polynomial expected source calls AND bit computation under explicit
relative-derivative and rational-evaluation conditions. It avoids an
exponential multivariate Bernstein coefficient sum by sampling a bounded
Taylor correction from a computable sum-of-products envelope.

The broader capability is reusable exact stochastic execution of acquired
experimental knowledge. The knowledge is an executable source dictionary plus
an ordinary arithmetic program and a cost/regularity proof. Numeric constants
need not be fitted to a fixed epsilon. Fresh experiments remain a paid resource.

Strong conventional machinery already solves the positive-factor/monotone-
circuit endpoint; that construction is adopted and preserved. The surviving
candidate ingredient is the succinct finite-jet compiler, including signed
numeric programs without a supplied positive proposal sampler. This is a
theoretical advance candidate, not an established priority claim, practical
speedup, or a solution of arbitrary planning/inference.

The generic wrapper is intentionally NOT run at its conservative theorem n0:
even the 2-parameter signed demonstration's loose certificate gives about
3.78e22 initial source calls. That is a sufficient polynomial existence bound,
not a practical bill or an asserted lower bound. The complete toy factory has
a much cheaper ordinary direct construction; it checks the algorithm's exact
arithmetic, not comparative advantage. Likewise, the discovery fixture's
4.40e11 represented bit trials are disclosed rather than counted as a few
binomial batches. All simulated source counts are represented experiments,
not physical laboratory runs. Seeded PRNGs stand in for ideal independent bits.

The proofs, construction code, exact checks and informative failures are the
deliverable. The numerical evidence is deliberately narrower than the theorem.
SOURCES.txt records the primary ancestry and scope of each comparison.

The complete bivariate fixture connects the acquired dictionary to the actual
randomized-elevation factory using only fresh bits. It returns 500 outputs,
uses 76,544 fresh execution calls after acquisition, and checks a nonzero
Taylor remainder on every vector of an exhaustive 612-vector transition.
Production uses 52 univariate terms at that transition; enumeration is only
the independent validator. Its polynomial target also has a cheaper ordinary
16-coin construction, so this remains a bridge/correctness demonstration.
