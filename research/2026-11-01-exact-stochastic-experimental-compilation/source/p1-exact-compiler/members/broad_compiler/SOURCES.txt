PRIMARY RESEARCH USED FOR THE COMPARISON

Nacu and Peres, Fast simulation of new coins from old (2005).
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0309222
Established analytic-function Bernoulli factories and tail behavior; not a
novelty claim for transforming unknown coin biases into known functions.

Holtz, Nazarov and Peres, New coins from old, smoothly (2011).
https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.1936
Established smoothness/positive-Bernstein-expansion/tail-order relationship.

Latuszynski, Kosmidis, Papaspiliopoulos and Roberts, Simulating events of
unknown probabilities via reverse time martingales (2011).
https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.4018
The reverse-time envelope framework is an adopted component. Our direct
exchangeability proof states the exact transition identities used here.

Morina, Latuszynski, Nayar and Wendland, From the Bernoulli Factory to a Dice
Enterprise via Perfect Sampling of Markov Chains (2022).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.09229
Constructive rational/multivariate factories. Existence and rational
transformations are not the claimed new component.

Dughmi, Hartline, Kleinberg and Niazadeh, Bernoulli Factories and Black-Box
Reductions in Mechanism Design (STOC 2017; JACM version 2021).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.04143
https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~rdk/papers/stoc17-rad.pdf
Polynomial exponential race, empirical maximum shift, and computational
expectations-from-samples setting. These power the strong conventional
positive-proposal construction preserved in broad_control.

Huber, Optimal linear Bernoulli factories for small mean problems.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.00843
The explicit scalar linear factory is used by the alternative shifted-rejection
implementation and is not a new component of this work.

Stumpf-Fetizon and Goncalves, Scalable Bernoulli Factory MCMC for Intractable
Marginalised Posteriors, version 3, 29 August 2026.
https://arxiv.org/html/2505.05438v3
Polynomial divide-and-conquer execution of exact marginal MCMC transitions
under specified assumptions; does not itself furnish generic exact IID output
from any compact predictor.

Koskela, Karvonen, Latuszynski and Spano, A debiased Bernoulli factory and
unbiased estimation of a probability, version 2, 1 September 2026.
https://arxiv.org/html/2510.01941v2
Univariate smooth-probability debiasing with bounded outputs. Its section 4
explicitly discusses coefficient-construction/implementability difficulties.
The present candidate contribution is the growing-dimensional, relative-scale,
succinct finite-jet computation and its total bit cost, not debiasing itself.

Aguilar, Jerbi, Eisert and Bittel, Efficient learning of Clifford disentanglers
and typical t-doped unitaries with exponentially more T gates, September 2026.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27565
Strong same-data quadratic-Bell comparator for the separately preserved
paired-drift construction. That alternative is not promoted as the main
advance.

Akshay, Bansal and Mahla, Certifying Almost-Sure Reachability in Unknown MDPs.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18119
Ashok, Kretinsky and Weininger, PAC Statistical Model Checking for Markov
Decision Processes and Stochastic Games.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.04403
Support-first/robust-model-checking comparison for the separate acquired
certificate construction, whose cheap certificate does not earn cheap reset.
