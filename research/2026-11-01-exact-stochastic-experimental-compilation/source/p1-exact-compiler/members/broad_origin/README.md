# Independent capability-origin investigation

- `paired_drift_factorization.md`: complete proof, acquired decoder contract,
  acquisition/execution costs, prior-art qualification, and noise limitation.
- `twin_factorization.py`: exact finite-field learner and Clifford decoder
  synthesis; run with Python and NumPy.
- `twin_factorization_results.json`: twelve successful ideal fixtures and
  three rejected 2% unheralded-corruption fixtures.
- `other_openings_and_failures.md`: the discarded excursion/resolvent route,
  conventional comparisons, and limits on the broader algebraic route.

The paired construction is concrete but is not claimed to beat the strongest
same-observation conventional method. The September 2026 hidden-Clifford
quadratic-symmetry learner is a close and powerful prior route. No external
repository was modified, and no original Astra theorem is used as a premise.
