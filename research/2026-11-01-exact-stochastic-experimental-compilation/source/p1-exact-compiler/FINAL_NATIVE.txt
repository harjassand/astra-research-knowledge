## 1. The capability: learn how to execute with unknown quantities

The organising question became:

> Can experiments acquire a reusable program whose later stochastic outputs have exactly the intended law, while the numerical quantities inside that program remain unknown?

This matters when probabilities become components of larger computations: conditional simulation, randomized decisions, acceptance steps, or adaptive experimental procedures. A fixed numerical approximation can introduce a persistent bias into every subsequent use. An executable correction can preserve the intended probability law at each use.

The retained knowledge has three parts:

- **An experimental dictionary:** finite addresses for reproducible local experiments.
- **An ordinary numerical program:** a concrete calculation defining the requested probability.
- **A regularity and execution certificate:** conditions under which fresh experimental bits can execute that calculation exactly in distribution.

Fresh observations remain a charged resource. This is not a finite dataset becoming an unlimited exact simulator. The construction makes future evidence computationally usable without first turning every unknown quantity into a permanently calibrated number.

## 2. The strongest conventional route changed the destination

The initial proposed endpoint—exact conditional sampling in positive factor models—did not survive comparison as the main new contribution.

I constructed a strong route using a coarse learned proposal, logarithmic coin transformations, shifted exponential acceptance, and capped retries with fresh proposals. Its established ingredients include Dughmi and colleagues’ polynomial exponential-race construction and Huber’s linear Bernoulli factories. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

The useful property is that **correctness holds for every proposal**, including a poor one. A good proposal makes acceptance reasonably frequent. After a fixed number of failures, the algorithm can discard the proposal and learn another without biasing the accepted output. Thus constant-probability coarse acquisition can support an exact service with bounded expected cost.

That route already covers:

- Positive factor models with an actually constructed numerical sampler.
- Monotone addition–multiplication circuits, through recursive sampling of their monomials.
- Further families, including appropriate tree and determinantal constructions, where the required positive proposal can be built.

I retained and implemented it. I did not make another positive-factor endpoint the scientific destination.

The broader literature also already establishes smooth-function Bernoulli factories, rational factories, reverse-time envelope methods, and bounded debiasing. These are substantial predecessors. The recent debiasing paper explicitly identifies coefficient construction and implementability as difficulties. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}

**The surviving opening is computational:** exact execution from a succinct numerical program, including programs using subtraction, without supplying a positive sampling representation or expanding all its coefficients.

## 3. The theorem I developed

Let \(p_1,\ldots,p_m\) be fixed unknown means of independent, stationary Bernoulli experiments. A call to experiment \(i\) returns one fresh bit with mean \(p_i\).

A later query specifies a rational probability calculation

\[
f:[0,1]^m\longrightarrow(0,1),
\qquad h=f(1-f).
\]

The construction requires two paid conditions.

First, the actual numerical program must evaluate \(f\) and its derivatives through order twelve at rational arguments using polynomially many bit operations, with polynomial operand sizes. A small count of ideal real-arithmetic operations is insufficient.

Second, there must be a known \(K\ge2\) such that

\[
|\partial^\alpha f(p)|
\le |\alpha|!\,K^{|\alpha|}h(p),
\qquad 1\le|\alpha|\le12,
\]

with analogous bounds for derivatives of \(h\) through order eight.

**Under these conditions, the compiler produces a bit with probability exactly \(f(p)\), terminates almost surely, and has polynomial expected experimental calls, fair-bit use, and bit computation in \(m\), \(K\), and the numerical program’s declared size.**

The bound has no reciprocal-\(f(p)\) or reciprocal-\((1-f(p))\) factor. Tiny output probabilities are allowed. Ill-conditioning is still priced through \(K\), and numerical operand growth is separately charged.

Fresh executions also support adaptive use: conditional on each past transcript, the next output has the law specified by the next admissible program.

This is a theorem about a new computational operation. It does not make arbitrary inference or planning efficient. The numerical calculation defining the query and its regularity certificate must themselves be constructed.

### Why the relative condition is useful

For a positive partition quotient

\[
f(p)=\frac{F(p)}{F(p)+G(p)},
\]

suppose \(F,G\) have nonnegative coefficients in positive affine primitive factors bounded below by \(a>0\), and their monomials have degree at most \(D\). The proof derives the required relative bounds with

\[
K=O(D/a).
\]

The partition functions themselves can be exponentially small. Their small common scale does not force an exponential calibration bill.

This certificate can be used even when the actual numerical evaluator employs a determinant or another signed calculation. Where a positive proposal sampler is available, the conventional route remains an adopted alternative.

## 4. The computational mechanism

Ordinary multivariate Bernstein constructions present a serious hidden cost. After doubling the number of experimental observations, the old counts conditioned on the new counts follow a product of hypergeometric laws. Directly computing the required coefficient update can require up to

\[
(n+1)^m
\]

terms.

A procedure with a good experimental sample bound can therefore still be computationally unusable.

The construction replaces that sum with an **exact randomized correction**.

### Compatible envelopes with enough decay

I constructed coefficient functions

\[
a_n=g_n-\frac{C}{n^3}h,
\qquad
b_n=g_n+\frac{C}{n^3}h,
\]

where \(g_n\) includes explicit first- and second-order bias corrections. An operator identity cancels the first two orders of the hypergeometric update.

The resulting envelopes have three essential properties:

1. They remain valid probabilities.
2. Their successive acceptance increments have a strictly positive margin.
3. The probability of needing another level decreases as \(O(n^{-3})\).

These are coefficientwise compatibility statements, not just approximate agreement with \(f\).

### A correction that can actually be sampled

Expand the coefficient function around the new empirical point through degree seven. Its polynomial expectation is computed from fixed-order hypergeometric moments.

For the remainder, construct a bound of the form

\[
E(Y)=T\,h(x)\,m^7
\left(\sum_iY_i^8\right)
\prod_j(1+|Y_j|)^{2K}.
\]

This expression is a sum of \(m\) product terms. Its normalizer and its normalized sampling law therefore require only univariate tables.

Sampling from this law gives an unbiased remainder estimate with a deterministic magnitude bound. The positive margin between the envelopes makes the resulting acceptance-mass estimates **pointwise nonnegative**. An ordinary three-way Bernoulli race then implements the exact accept-one, accept-zero, and continue probabilities.

That positivity step is indispensable. An unbiased signed estimate is not automatically an executable probability.

### Paying for bits

The univariate weights have an explicit common-denominator integer representation. A stage can be implemented in

\[
\operatorname{poly}(\text{input})\,n^2\operatorname{polylog}n
\]

bit operations. Combining this with \(O(n^{-3})\) continuation makes the expected total work finite and polynomial.

This is why I retained the stronger third-order envelopes. The simpler \(n^{-3/2}\) construction pays for experimental calls and unit-cost arithmetic, but does not pay for this conservative exact-integer implementation.

## 5. Acquisition and numerical execution are both constructed

I did not assume the useful experimental dictionary was supplied.

The acquisition construction starts with controlled component experiments whose responses depend on unknown small subsets of binary inputs. Responses may be nonmonotone. Under an explicit influence-resolution promise, noisy experiments discover the relevant input subsets.

The procedure uses randomized completions and a chain search that explores both children when necessary. It retains **callable experimental addresses**, rather than fitted numerical response tables. A later request to an address performs a fresh experiment at its canonical setting.

This gives a stronger lifetime statement than a fixed per-query approximation:

> Conditional on every successful acquisition transcript, all later adaptive executions have their ideal conditional laws. Comparing ideal and implemented services with the same acquisition transcript and query policy, the entire future transcript has total-variation error at most the single discovery failure probability.

The resolution promise matters. Finite noisy evidence cannot uniformly distinguish an absent dependency from an arbitrarily weak one. Approximate scopes give approximate semantics; they do not inherit this lifetime exactness.

I also built an explicit signed numerical consumer. For a matrix pencil \(M(p)\),

\[
A(p)=I+M(p)M(p)^\top,\qquad
v(p)=b^\top A(p)^{-1}b,\qquad
f(p)=\frac{v(p)}{1+v(p)},
\]

the code forms the matrix, performs rational elimination, and differentiates the actual calculation using finite Taylor coefficients. The proof supplies an explicit derivative certificate. No inverse or sampling oracle is supplied.

This is an illustration of the broader compiler’s interface, not a claim of a new matrix-solver advantage. The program is in :chatgpt-content-reference{index="7"}[rational_program.py](producer-workspace/rational_program.py).

## 7. Useful failures and alternatives remain part of the capital

Several boundaries sharpened the construction.

- **Finite retained data cannot supply unlimited exact fresh outputs.** Fisher-information data processing already rules this out for the identity operation \(f(p)=p\). Continued experimental work is a real resource.
- **Unknown sensor bias is not corrected automatically.** Exactness concerns the actual stationary experimental law. An unidentifiable noiseless mean remains unidentifiable.
- **Interior zeros can make exact factories impossible.** The relative-regularity condition excludes genuine information obstructions; it is not decorative.
- **Cheap certificates do not buy cheap physical access.** The support-only reachability alternative acquired a compact certificate while its example still required about \(1.11\times10^{30}\) physical transitions to reset.
- **Exact hidden algebra can disappear under noise.** The independently originated paired-drift construction learned a tensor decomposition and synthesized a decoder, but unheralded contamination destroyed its exact quadratic kernel.

One collaborator began without the cascade formulation in its initial context; the model, tools, and workspace remained shared. Its paired-experiment construction was preserved with the strong September 2026 Clifford-disentangler comparison. That predecessor prevented treating the alternative as the main new capability. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}

## Scientific conclusion

The acquired knowledge can be **a program for using future imperfect evidence while preserving exact stochastic semantics**.

The construction earns a specific new operation: polynomial-cost multivariate correction from a succinct numerical calculation, with growing dimension, relative probability bounds, and finite-bit execution all accounted for. It supports numerical programs that do not arrive with a positive sampling representation.

The positive-model endpoint already has strong conventional machinery, which is now adopted and preserved. The broader compiler theorem is the result worth carrying forward. Its proofs and executable components are complete at the stated contract; practical constants, a deployment-scale consequence, and priority beyond the checked predecessors remain unearned.
