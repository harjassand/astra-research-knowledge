# Consequence audit: completing the TPF free-fermion/rotor interface

**Checked 2026-11-02. Verdict:** I did not find an established construction satisfying *all* of the same-resource obligations below that makes the proposed interface theorem redundant. But 'first nonperturbative U(1) chiral gauge theory' is already an obsolete objective. The worthwhile target is a quantitatively controlled, strictly bounded-range **continuous-time Hamiltonian** realization, not merely anomaly matching or a Euclidean measure. Completing the interface alone does not supply finite rotor resources, the full Standard Model, or an interacting continuum theorem.

## What the target actually closes

TPF §4 separates an exactly controlled disentangler/bottom-boundary construction from an unproved symmetric, topologically trivial interface between free-fermion and commuting-projector SPT slabs. Section 4.1 does not supply the fluctuation Hamiltonian or a gap proof. Proving that interface would replace its central dynamical conjecture with a construction, allowing the proposed hypercharge sector (including sterile neutrinos) to inherit a controlled mirror-gapping mechanism. **Its rotor Hilbert spaces remain infinite-dimensional.** Section 5 explicitly warns that the disentanglers do not preserve naive finite-angular-momentum domains: correlated discontinuities/twisted domains appear. Consequently finite rotor count is not finite local Hilbert dimension, and bounded interaction range is not bounded operator norm. [TPF v2, §§4–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.04304v2)

**My assessment:** a useful interface theorem must add an explicit local operator/domain, symmetry commutation, a volume-uniform gap, and triviality/no residual topological sector. Merely proving equality of response polynomials or matching anomaly inflow does not establish these properties.

## Strongest alternatives, with decisive differences

| Comparator | What already exists | Why it does—or does not—obsolete this target |
|---|---|---|
| **Lüscher, anomaly-free U(1)** | Nonperturbative finite-volume chiral lattice construction with exact gauge invariance, including topological sectors, within its admissibility/locality and charge conditions. | Already defeats a first-U(1)-definition claim. It is a Ginsparg–Wilson/measure construction, not an explicit strictly bounded-range continuous-time Hamiltonian with a paid rotor cutoff. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/9811032) |
| **Craig, Oct. 2026 overlap measure** | Claims a gauge-invariant exponentially local Euclidean measure for a class including the full Standard Model on admissible fields. Its conclusion explicitly leaves reflection positivity compatible with admissibility and the interacting continuum theory open. | This is consequential recent prior art, but not the requested Hamiltonian substitute. I checked its stated result and limitations, **not its entire proof**; classify it as a recent candidate construction, not an independently certified settled theorem. [Primary v1, abstract and conclusion](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09093v1) |
| **Domain walls / Kaplan finite-boundary proposal** | Domain-wall slabs realize chiral boundaries; ordinary finite slabs retain the opposite boundary and exponentially small finite-thickness effects. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.05998) |

SMG cannot be dismissed by a universal no-go slogan either. Golterman–Shamir's current published-version analysis gives **conditional** generalized Nielsen–Ninomiya constraints and explicit tests involving composite interpolating fields/propagator zeros; it does not declare every SMG construction impossible. Its §VI expressly withholds a final verdict for the model examined. A new construction must identify which hypotheses it avoids and must check the complete physical spectrum, not merely elementary mirror propagators. [Primary v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.20436v3)

## What would actually make the target obsolete?

**A result to search for, not one found in this pass:** an explicit anomaly-free 3+1D U(1) Weyl Hamiltonian with (i) finite-range spatial terms and continuous unitary time, (ii) exact gauge constraints, (iii) proved mirror-free low-energy spectrum including composite channels, (iv) finite ancillary species and certified local Hilbert cutoffs, and (v) quantitative continuum matching at the same costs. A strengthened Wen/SMG theorem meeting those conditions would eliminate the need to solve this particular rotor interface as a route to that capability. A constructive reflection-positive overlap-to-local-Hamiltonian conversion with the same resources could do so too. An anomaly classification, existence of some gapped boundary, or gauge-invariant Euclidean measure alone does not.

If the actual goal is only an admissible Euclidean anomaly-free U(1) regulator, the Lüscher alternative already suffices; pursuing this interface for that goal would be poorly motivated. If the goal is full-SM Euclidean measure existence, the recent Craig claim must be assessed first rather than ignored.

## Capability ladder and required proof receipts

These are my proposed acceptance tests, not claims made by the cited papers:

1. **Interface theorem:** explicit bounded-range interaction; common self-adjoint domain; exact U(1)V x U(1)A and fermion-parity symmetry; unique trivial interface phase with gap bounded below independently of spatial volume. Include all vortex/reconnection configurations, not just a fixed smooth mass texture.
2. **Gauged regulator:** apply the known on-site symmetry transformation and gauging, then prove a scale-separated regime where gauging does not introduce unwanted gapless mirror/interface/composite modes. Specify slab thickness, boundary tunnelling error, ancillary modes, and physical Gauss law.
3. **Finite resources:** establish gauge-compatible truncation error as a function of energy window E, volume V, time T and epsilon. A simple angular-momentum cutoff is not automatically valid for transformed domains. Track local dimension, interaction norms, range and ancilla count. This is a separate result from item 1.
4. **Continuum control:** identify the target chiral correlators, tune allowed relevant operators, and bound regulator/mirror effects as spacing decreases. Distinguish matching the low-energy free Weyl spectrum from constructing the interacting gauge continuum.
5. **Beyond hypercharge:** independently supply SU(3), SU(2), their joint action/global structure, Yukawa/Higgs sectors and anomaly/global-sector checks. Hypercharge assignments alone are not a Hamiltonian Standard Model.

**Consequence if all relevant receipts are obtained:** a direct unitary regulator and a controlled route to real-time quantum simulation of the specified anomaly-free chiral theory, avoiding reliance on an unproved strong mirror phase or Euclidean-to-Hamiltonian reconstruction. That would be a substantive constructive advance. It would not itself give efficient classical simulation, practical qubit counts, new phenomenology, or the full Standard Model. If only the formal interface is closed, the achievement is narrower: one important missing Hamiltonian existence/gap step, with the truncation and continuum bottlenecks still outstanding.
