# Exact lattice charge cannot follow a rounding jump through a smooth gapped family

Root reconciliation, 2026-11-02.

Let H_f(phi) be a finite-dimensional fermion Hamiltonian depending continuously on the rotor coordinates and commuting with a fixed, integer-spectrum axial charge Q_f. If H_f has a unique ground state separated by a nonzero gap in a neighborhood of phi0, its ground spectral projector is continuous there. Its axial charge is both continuous and integer-valued, hence constant. For a free number-conserving realization this is the familiar local constancy of the occupied-orbital rank while the one-body Fermi gap is nonzero. The interacting finite-dimensional argument does not require Gaussian structure.

The exact decoration ground axial charge is the lattice cochain self-linking s(phi). This integer jumps on some rounded-edge walls, such as dphi_e=1/2. If s changes across a wall but H_f stays continuous and gapped through it, the separated slab ground charge cannot change by minus that jump. The desired combined endpoint ground charges then differ. No axial-invariant transfer, local or global, has a nonzero matrix element between these ground states.

Therefore every all-configuration construction of the proposed separated ground-state family must do at least one of the following:
1. Arrange a charged slab spectral crossing at every relevant rounding wall, then gap the COMBINED slab/decoration crossing by a physical partial transfer.
2. Use piecewise/discontinuous slab coefficients or twisted domains tied consistently to the same cochain walls.
3. Replace the premise of a separated ground-state product family and construct the full interacting interface directly.

This is not a no-go for the TPF interface or for a continuum texture approach. In particular, a coarse smooth trigonometric mass ansatz can fail while a refined, piecewise cochain-consistent texture might work. Its hierarchy must be paid: Wilson UV spacing much smaller than inverse boundary mass, itself smaller than coarse rotor cell size. Exact vector covariance, individual rotor periodicity, the complete occupied slab charge and the joint interface gap all remain checks.

Current actual-slab evidence is recorded by the constructor: one-vertex Freudenthal rotor move with s:0 -> -1, but unchanged complete occupied rank at thicknesses 3 and 5 and masses .5,2,8 for both tested smooth mass prescriptions. Those are failures of those prescriptions. A far chiral boundary finite-size gap is not an interface gap. Exact periodic zero modes are not to be assigned arbitrary occupation to create desired charge matching.

The elementary gate above is root's reasoning based on the actual charge-matching requirement, not a claimed novel impossibility theorem.
