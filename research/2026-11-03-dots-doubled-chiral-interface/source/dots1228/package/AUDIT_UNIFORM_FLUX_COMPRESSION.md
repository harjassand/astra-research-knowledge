## 1. Ordinary twists: the decisive check

Choose the seam representation of a flat background a(theta), with prescribed integer species charges n,m. On each contractible projector star X_i there is a real local lift alpha_j with a_jk=alpha_k-alpha_j, using the selected oriented edge convention. The large-torus hypothesis ensures that a star has no noncontractible cycle and no conflicting identification of copies of one physical site.

The matrix kernel of D_theta p_i D_theta† is proportional to

exp{i[S_theta(phi)-S_theta(phi with phi_i replaced by phi_i')]}

and delta functions on spectator sites. All triangles not containing the updated site cancel. In the remaining triangles, replacing each edge difference by dphi+a is exactly replacing the local angles by phi+alpha. Integration over the updated angle is Haar-invariant. Thus the kernel is the zero-twist local kernel conjugated by ordinary onsite translations. This is an operator identity on physical rotor L2 space, not an identification of a differently parameterized family. Periodic branch representatives do not change it because the original integer-coefficient phase kernel is well-defined modulo individual angle periods.

Two lifts differ by a constant translation on the connected star; the local kernel depends on differences and is invariant under that constant. Across a seam, the lift coefficients of theta are integer multiples of n,m. After momentum compression, their onsite charges are integer and bounded, so the compressed kernel is a finite trigonometric polynomial, periodic under integer shifts of theta. Compression commutes with every local translation. Therefore the compressed family really is the usual flat-twist family of the compressed zero-flux Hamiltonian.

This proof would fail on a support wrapping the torus, for an unrelated background coupling, or if the cutoff preserved only global charge instead of individual onsite translations. Root excludes those cases correctly.

## 2. Exact gap before compression and Fourier estimates

Multiplication by the phase is a bounded unitary despite angular discontinuities. Conjugation of a product-projector precursor therefore gives a unique ground vector and gap one at every twist. The norm bound v is immediate. For any finite ancillary factors, the precursor must include their unique-state penalties; otherwise their free degeneracy remains.

For a fixed-angle slice, each floor edge argument has slope 0 or +/-1. Flat twists move its intercept, not its slope. Only incident triangles contribute to the coordinate derivative. Their number is bounded by local degree, and fixed K,n,m bound all branch coefficients. The proposed B and R are conservative counts provided D counts all oriented phase triangles in the actual phase expression. Counting up to two threshold crossings per incident edge/species term and the periodic endpoint is safe. Fermionic vector-valued tails require no component-count factor: integration by parts uses the Hilbert-space norm of the whole vector.

For each coordinate j and Fourier m!=0,

||Omega_hat_j(m)|| <= (B_j+2R_j)/(2pi |m|).

Squaring, integrating other angles, summing |m|>N and applying the commuting-projector union bound yields

sup_theta ||(1-P_N)Omega_theta||^2 <= sum_j(B_j+2R_j)^2/(2pi^2 N).

The estimate does not assume finite bare kinetic energy. Floor boundaries have measure zero and moving-boundary dependence is handled by Fubini. As theta changes, the measure of swept cut neighborhoods vanishes and the integrand stays bounded, giving local L2-continuity. This is enough for continuous bundle theory.

For the broader circuit statement in FINITE_ROTOR_REGULATORS, modulo/shear pullbacks may be piecewise integer-affine rather than one global affine expression. Enumerate every finite branch and its cuts. Fixed depth/degree/charges keep this local enumeration bounded independently of volume; no corresponding obstacle appears in the direct DW phase.

## 3. Compression estimates and bundle topology

Let tau=||(1-P)Omega||^2. Since H Omega=0,

<P Omega,H P Omega>=<(1-P)Omega,H(1-P)Omega><=v tau.

The normalized projected vector has energy <=delta=v tau/(1-tau). Rayleigh–Ritz/minmax on the restricted subspace gives lambda_0(H_N)<=delta and lambda_1(H_N)>=1. In particular delta<1 gives a unique finite ground vector eta_N and gap >=1-delta. The form inequality H>=I-|Omega><Omega| implies

1-|<Omega,eta_N>|^2 <=lambda_0(H_N)<=delta.

Thus the rank-one projector distance is <=sqrt(delta). This is exact, without any assumption about commutators of compressed terms.

At each twist, the finite ground line has nonzero overlap with the exact line. The map Pi_N(theta) restricted to the exact line is a continuous nonzero fiber map and therefore a line-bundle isomorphism. Pi_N is smooth because H_N is a finite smooth family with a uniform gap; Pi is continuous from the L2 wavefunction argument. Local sections inherit the same scalar transition law. Hence Chern classes agree exactly. No twist derivative of the discontinuous infinite-rotor multiplier is needed.

The transition law supplied by DW agrees with the note. Its scalar gauge makes one edge trivial and gives the other the winding n^T K m, subject to the paper's orientation convention. The claim is equality of the ground-bundle Chern invariant, not convergence of Berry curvature at each theta.

## 4. Resources and numerical assembly

For projector error epsilon and a fixed positive gap, tau=O(epsilon^2/v) suffices, hence N=O(v d Gamma^2/epsilon^2). With fixed species, charges, local degree and v,d proportional to volume, the stated quadratic-volume momentum cutoff follows. Each site's dimension is (2N+1)^kappa. Total Hilbert dimension remains exponential in volume even though the number of qubits needed to label it is polynomial.

Compressing a term on a fixed support means compressing only that support; outside tensor factors act as identities on the retained spaces. Spatial range is therefore unchanged. Local matrix entries involve fixed-dimensional integrals over finitely many known floor cells and bounded oscillatory factors. At fixed local complexity, direct certified quadrature is polynomial in N and inverse entry accuracy, with an unfavorable but fixed exponent.

To retain the topological conclusion after numerical assembly, approximate the zero-flux local terms inside exact onsite charge blocks and define all twists by exact finite onsite conjugation. Then the total operator-norm error is uniform in theta. If it is smaller than a suitable fraction of the uniform gap, straight-line interpolation of exact and assembled finite Hamiltonians stays gapped and preserves the Chern number. It is not enough to fit unrelated matrices at independent twist samples. This is a useful implementation detail to append.

## 5. No-go and domain stress tests

- Compression generally changes products: P h_i P h_j P is not P h_i h_j P. Therefore the finite local terms need not commute or remain projectors. No finite-dimensional commuting-projector Hall theorem is contradicted.
- No uniform fixed-N thermodynamic claim is obtained. The proof's cutoff grows with volume, and the gap proof cannot simply be reused at fixed N as volume diverges.
- The original bounded Hamiltonian has no domain obstruction: every retained momentum vector is in its domain. By contrast physical compression of S(sum L^2)S† can fail at the quadratic-form/domain level; the note correctly keeps those constructions separate.
- The original flat-projector low-energy sector is infinitely dimensional. Its ground-line truncation does not uniformly approximate all low-energy states. The arbitrary-state obstruction in the companion note remains valid.
- Exact onsite U(1) on finite matter qudits is allowed. Exact unitary finite-ladder gauge-link shifts are not; the trace obstruction and nonunitary truncated-shift alternative in the companion note are correct. The present twist construction uses background onsite rotations, not a dynamical unitary link truncation.

**Scope acquired:** a physical finite-dimensional, finite-range, exactly symmetric, generally noncommuting finite-volume regulator of the completed DW Hall ground bundle with uniform flat-twist gap. **Not acquired:** driven response, arbitrary low-energy-state approximation, fixed-cutoff thermodynamic Hall transport, dynamical gauge links, or the missing free-fermion/rotor interface.
