## Doubling: exact claim that cannot be blurred
The candidate keeps the second copy's axial eigenvalues while complex-conjugating the fast matrices and reversing vector labels. Since the mixed anomaly coefficient involves A V², reversing V alone does NOT negate that coefficient; it doubles. The free four-spatial-dimensional topological invariant also needs the explicit real-structure/orientation check, not the slogan 'complex conjugate means inverse'. Conventional purification often uses the dual action that DOES reverse relevant charges. Accidentally adopting that dual would prove only an easy inverse-stack trivialization.

If the intended representation audit succeeds, the theorem relates DOUBLED free and DOUBLED rotor realizations of the same anomalous building block. Even if this implies 2[free]=2[rotor], one cannot cancel the factor two without excluding a possible torsion difference or supplying an undoubling construction. Nor can two identical chiral copies automatically be removed by symmetry-preserving bilinear masses.
