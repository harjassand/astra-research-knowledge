# Targeted primary comparison: retained-memory recovery

Read on 28 October 2026, 15:58–16:00 UTC. Internal scope clarification, not a priority claim, literature survey, theorem revision, or empirical acquisition report. This note does not mutate any received source.

## Three actual access contracts

**Raza, Caro, Eisert and Khatri, Online learning of quantum processes.** The primary v1 manuscript §2.2 defines prediction against classical test descriptions; for its familiar convex prediction losses the target value b_t is explicitly revealed. The regret comparator is a fixed hypothesis evaluated on the encountered test/loss sequence. Sections 3.3 and 6.1 genuinely include non-Markovian combs: non-Markovian online learning is therefore established prior work. The distinct shadow-tomography interface queries a fixed multi-time process repeatedly. Its acquisition proof supplies a fresh Bell pair at each comb time, sends one half through the process, and jointly Bell-measures output and retained half, obtaining a Pauli-error trajectory. Those are stronger calibration resources than our strict ordinary-input interface; the comb prediction task is also different from physically recovering an unknown payload on the one ongoing acquisition occupation. This is an interface comparison, not an impossibility claim against adaptations of their methods. [Primary manuscript, §§2.2, 3.3, 6.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.04250v1).

**Bansal, George, Ghosh, Sikora and Zheng, Online learning of a panoply of quantum objects.** Section 1.1.2 presents a finite-horizon adversarial convex problem and reveals the entire loss function after each action; the optimization framework can use a subgradient. Section 3.3 specializes the PSD feasible set to CPTP Choi matrices and the co-objects to interactive channel measurements. Corollary 3.5 supplies sublinear channel regret. Thus online convex optimization over the channel spectrahedron is conventional, not our contribution. A physical bit from an unknown source does not directly reveal that whole channel loss or its gradient. Our received sources explicitly construct conditional unbiased gradients or a positive-loss bandit estimator from actual ordinary measurements and pay for physical decoder realization. This does not imply their full-information interface is weaker; it addresses a different resource contract. [Primary article, §§1.1.2, 3.3](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s42484-025-00300-5).

**Bhardwaj, Takou, Lin and Brown, Adaptive Estimation of Drifting Noise in Quantum Error Correction.** The actual data are syndrome histories from an existing QEC circuit, used to estimate time-varying detector-error probabilities and update a decoder. Section II models independent detector-error events; Appendix A explicitly assumes Markovian temporal event independence in its variance calculation. The paper develops sliding/overlapping-window frequency and lag analyses, and tests repetition/surface-code noise scenarios. Its 1/f section uses time-varying Pauli dephasing probabilities motivated by Gaussian/non-Gaussian fluctuator coherence functions. These are important practical conventional baselines, not a demonstrated arbitrary coherent retained-memory recovery interface. Its ground-truth-versus-estimated DEM decoding comparison is not our fixed recovery map evaluated on the learner's actually visited conditional quantum-memory states. Nor does our worst-case theorem establish better practical decoding than its syndrome-driven methods. [Primary paper, §§II–VI and Appendix A](https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/pdf/10.1103/z1hc-nqw5).

## What the received local chain actually adds—and does not

The candidate contribution is an end-to-end resource composition, conditional on its declared complete-port and trusted-storage interface: (i) acquire the needed feedback using ordinary prepared states and actual measurement/return bits; (ii) keep every recovery action exactly CP/TP and finitely compilable; (iii) retain arbitrary inaccessible quantum memory without claiming a source model or resets; (iv) turn expected prequential regret into the averaged complete channel of an unknown payload with its reference intact. The comparator is tied to the *declared ideal controller's actual occupation*, not to the memory path an oracle policy would have generated. The common CP-dominant algebra is a supplied sufficient model for a common full-CPTP oracle, not learned from finite outcomes.

The slow result uses conventional projected online gradients. The fast result uses conventional positive-loss self-concordant bandit ideas; the source itself gives the precise Rakhlin–Sridharan primary import and derives its actual noisy-bit conditional law rather than asserting a black-box transfer. Zinkevich-type projected regret and Rakhlin–Sridharan small-loss learning deserve conventional credit. No claim of first online channel learning, first non-Markovian learning, or a new abstract bandit rate is made. Existing Astra complete-role sealing is an accepted prior interface, not rebranded here.

The received mixed temporal service removes the single-payload limitation only with held inputs, isolated outputs, private roles, a fixed shortfall fallback and the stated cumulative marginal comparator. The received joint selector source removes the fixed-encoder limitation only for a supplied real selector-state family; its source cap remains epsilon^(-2). Positive raw anchor noise can remain large even at a perfect selector, so the earlier fast-bandit theorem cannot simply be substituted to claim fast joint discovery.

## Negative practical evidence governs next work

The separately received root pilot is adverse to the conservative theorem-step bandit at 512 calls: a structure-aware ordinary Pauli/Procrustes learner performs substantially better. Its own source, acquisition records, replay and receipt—not this note—govern numerical values and each policy's separate occupation. Its manifest is deliberately not duplicated in this branch package. The finite bandit theorem remains valid, but no small-budget practical advantage is claimed. The original worst-case learner-generated spectral floor also entails an O(N)-bit precision bill; a bounded-center refinement is a separate pending construction, not silently patched into received sources.

The next local-q construction is pending and outside the freeze. Its prospective conventional components are local product-Pauli shadows, martingale concentration, contraction-ball optimization, and spectral rounding. The load-bearing question is whether those acquired local generators compose into one physical simultaneous q-logical recovery retaining the full joint Bell reference under arbitrary correlated gauge memory. Independent marginal fidelities alone do not answer it.
