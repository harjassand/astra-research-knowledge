This distinction matters because a residual of order one can land mostly in excitations whose energies grow with system size. Its unweighted norm then looks unpromising, while its inverse-energy-weighted norm is small.

The underlying elimination of the complementary space is established Feshbach–Schur machinery. Merely writing these matrices does not make them computable. The work here earns them through an exact excitation distribution and then bounds the necessary inverse moments using a few ordinary moments. Dusson–Sigal–Stamm

That is the structural question worth retaining:

Can a small retained subspace and the spectral distribution of its leakage be acquired and certified more cheaply than a complete predictive model or a full eigenstate?

This run answers it constructively for one restricted family. It does not yet supply a general acquisition method.

6. Comparison with equally informed conventional methods

The strongest comparisons materially limit the claim.

Established approach	What it already provides	Consequence for this work
Bravyi–Gosset quantum ferromagnet simulation	A partition-function approximation algorithm covering this transverse-field Ising family on arbitrary graphs; additive ground-energy approximation follows by taking sufficiently large inverse temperature.	Polynomial energy computability of the star is already known. No new tractability class follows from this certificate.
Feshbach–Schur and subspace perturbation methods	Effective operators, resolvent reconstruction and eigenvector estimates after eliminating a complementary space.	The block algebra is established. The specific work is to earn inexpensive, explicit leakage moments and finite-size bounds.
Conditional-product and tensor representations	An equally informed solver can use the same two product sectors and the same compact cat representation.	Analytic access to the sectors must be given to the comparator too. No matched runtime advantage was established.
Kirkwood–Thomas / weak-interaction algorithms	Polynomial approximation guarantees for bounded-degree systems in a sufficiently weak interaction regime. Guiding distributions and auxiliary dynamics	Optimization of auxiliary dynamics through guiding functions and variance objectives.	Learned guides and residual optimization are established ideas; this run has not demonstrated a learned-model advantage.

The relevant primary sources are Bravyi–Gosset, Dusson–Sigal–Stamm, Bravyi–DiVincenzo–Loss, and Ray–Chan.

The construction is also perturbative in a precise sense: a central flip of norm one couples exactly solved bath sectors. Nonvanishing edge couplings do not turn it into a general solution of strongly interacting lattices.

The literature screen was focused, not exhaustive priority clearance. I have not established historical novelty for the specialized theorem.

9. Verification, source use and the surviving frontier

The main construction received a separate internal mathematical review, including its symmetry-sector argument and preparation circuit. I reconstructed the central identities and reran the executable calculation. Small-system diagonalizations checked the energy and fidelity inequalities; independent small rational calculations checked the moment bounds; circuit simulations checked the explicit preparation.

The large-instance certificates rest on exact rational inequalities, not extrapolation from those small diagnostics. This is ordinary internal mathematical and computational verification, not formal proof-assistant verification or external peer review.

Useful executable and review files are:

 — rational certificates and small spectral diagnostics.

 — independent internal review and additional state-preparation analysis.
audit_star_checks.py
 — independent moment and circuit checks.
ratio_certificate.py
 — local rational verification of the uniform rate theorem.
check_local_consequences.py
 — diagnostics for the local-observable and path consequences.

I followed the public Astra repository through its 
pinned start navigation and the governing sources for N1251, N1330, N1331 and N1087. They informed the distinction between acquiring a complete predictive model and acquiring the information a particular task needs. Their access, positivity, acquisition and confidence premises were not silently transferred into this proof. The exact source paths and limits of the retrieved views are recorded in the research record.

The decisive unresolved problem is now more specific:

Earn a small retained subspace and useful inverse spectral moments of its leakage for genuinely interacting conditional sectors, with total acquisition and validation costs competitive with equally informed conventional methods.

Lanczos methods, Stieltjes quadrature, tensor methods, certified resolvent estimates and learned representations all belong in that comparison. Naming a spectral measure does not acquire it, and obtaining an attractive trial state does not validate its unseen errors.

The completed star calculation shows that global local-sampler contraction is not necessary for a useful whole-state certificate. It also shows why unweighted residual variance can miss the quality of a state approximation. Extending those facts into an efficient acquisition principle for interacting sectors remains the consequential scientific step; a broad learning advantage or civilisation-changing discovery has not yet been earned.
