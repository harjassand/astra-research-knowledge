## 1. An exact evaluator despite unrestricted quantum references

For a channel \(\Phi\), a faithful state \(\rho_{AR}\), and a Hermitian tangent \(H_{AR}\), define the RLD quadratic form
\[
g_\Phi(\rho,H)
=
\operatorname{Tr}\!\left[
\Phi_R(H)\Phi_R(\rho)^{-1}\Phi_R(H)
\right],
\qquad
\Phi_R=\Phi\otimes\operatorname{id}_R.
\]
The inverse is taken on the channel’s effective output support.

The complete comparison asks whether
\[
g_\Phi(\rho,H)\ge g_\Psi(\rho,H)
\]
for every finite reference \(R\), every faithful density operator \(\rho\), and every traceless Hermitian \(H\).

**The constructed theorem gives a terminating exact algorithm for this question on finite rational channels, without a gap promise.** It also gives a reference-dimension cutoff computable from the channel descriptions. For fixed input and output dimensions, a cutoff can be chosen uniformly over the coefficients and output-support ranks.

### The operation that makes this possible

The crucial step is to retain an operator on the reference:
\[
T(\rho,H)=
\operatorname{Tr}_{B}\!\left[
\Phi_R(H)\Phi_R(\rho)^{-1}\Phi_R(H)
\right]
-
\operatorname{Tr}_{C}\!\left[
\Psi_R(H)\Psi_R(\rho)^{-1}\Psi_R(H)
\right].
\]

For every invertible reference matrix \(F\),
\[
T(F\rho F^\dagger,FHF^\dagger)
=
F\,T(\rho,H)\,F^\dagger,
\]
with identity factors on the physical input understood.

Consequently, universal scalar comparison is equivalent to
\[
T(\rho,H)\succeq0
\]
at every finite reference dimension. If \(T\) has a negative direction, an invertible reference congruence turns it into a negative scalar trace. Normalization and the traceless-tangent constraint can then be restored because the channel-independent terms in
\[
g_\Phi(\rho,H+t\rho)
=
g_\Phi(\rho,H)+2t\operatorname{Tr}H+t^2\operatorname{Tr}\rho
\]
cancel in the comparison.

This is a transformation of mathematical witnesses. It is not a physical filter supplied to a communication protocol.

The reference-valued expression can then be compiled into **one scalar free rational expression**: its variables are noncommuting Hermitian matrices representing the reference blocks of \(\rho\) and \(H\). The physical constraint \(\rho>0\) becomes the interior of a monic free spectrahedron. Exact support padding and ordered Schur-complement inverses ensure that this compilation neither loses valid physical inputs nor introduces a hidden invertibility assumption.

Volčič’s rational convex Positivstellensatz supplies the remaining operation: a finite matrix witness bound for failure of operator positivity on that domain. A sufficient cutoff produced by the construction is

\max\!\left\{

E^3s^{4t}

Here \(s,t,E\) are integers computed directly from the compiled expression: a subexpression count, a recursively defined expression complexity, and a domain-preserving linear-representation size. Their complete recursions are supplied in the proof and compiler.

At reference dimension \(R_{\max}\), failure of comparison is a finite existential formula over real polynomial equalities and inequalities. Complete real quantifier elimination decides it, including exact boundary cases.

The cutoff is enormous. The result establishes **termination and exactness**, without asserting practical general-purpose efficiency.

## 2. Identical complete profiles, radically different physical channels

The operational construction uses actual quantum flags.

Let \(U_0,U_1,U_2,U_3\) be the Pauli unitaries and
\[
\mathcal D_\delta(X)
=
(1-\delta)X+\delta\,\operatorname{Tr}(X)\frac{I_2}{2}.
\]
Define
\[
\Psi_\delta(X)
=
\frac14\sum_{j=0}^{3}
|j\rangle\langle j|_F
\otimes
U_j\mathcal D_\delta(X)U_j^\dagger.
\]

This channel physically outputs the four-dimensional orthogonal flag and the data qubit. Its output dimension is eight.

For a rational parameter \(m\ge1\), put
\[
a=\frac{m^2-1}{m^2+1},
\qquad
b=\frac{2m}{m^2+1},
\]
and choose normalized, linearly independent flags
\[
v_0=e_0,\qquad v_j=ae_0+be_j\quad(j=1,2,3).
\]
The second channel is
\[
\Phi_{\delta,m}(X)
=
\frac14\sum_{j=0}^{3}
|v_j\rangle\langle v_j|_F
\otimes
U_j\mathcal D_\delta(X)U_j^\dagger.
\]

Both descriptions are rational and CPTP. The passage from \(\Psi\) to \(\Phi\) is a physical channel: measure the orthogonal flag, prepare the corresponding \(v_j\), and retain the data system. Nonorthogonal quantum flags are established capacity machinery; the construction here uses them to obtain an exact all-input information equivalence together with a certified operational separation. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}

### Why the information profiles agree exactly

For positive operators define the maximal perspective
\[
P_f(A,B)
=
B^{1/2}f(B^{-1/2}AB^{-1/2})B^{1/2}.
\]
It obeys the invertible-congruence identity
\[
P_f(SAS^\dagger,SBS^\dagger)
=
S\,P_f(A,B)\,S^\dagger.
\]

Let \(S\) have columns \(v_j\). The outputs of \(\Psi\) are block diagonal in the flag, and \(\Phi\) is their congruence by \(S\). Every column of \(S\) has norm one. These two facts give
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Tr}_{FB}
P_f(\Phi_R\rho,\Phi_R\sigma)
=
\operatorname{Tr}_{FB}
P_f(\Psi_R\rho,\Psi_R\sigma).
}
\]

This equality retains an operator on the untouched reference. For \(n\) uses, the same argument applies to \(S^{\otimes n}\). Thus it holds for:

- every finite reference;
- every tensor power;
- arbitrary correlated inputs across those uses;
- the maximal divergences and RLD perspectives covered by the construction.

The scope matters: **both arguments pass through the same channel**. The theorem does not assert invariance for comparison against arbitrary external output states, including the optimizers in maximal-Rains bounds.
