LOCAL RARE-DEFECT COMPARISON FOR INTERACTING BOSONIC RECORDS
1 November 2026. New solo derivation. No independent audit or novelty claim.

Result and its boundary
----------------------
The retained Astra P1 proof supplies an exact coherent-projector generator
identity and a global positive clipped diffusion. Its thermal tail bound
depends on the total occupation of ALL modes. I construct a local version
for bounded-support quartic interactions with fixed physical dynamics and
thermal coherent-mixture preparation. It has local positive noise factors
and a sum of bounded-size Gamma tails, rather than a Gamma tail whose shape
is the number of modes. Adaptive selection of permitted measured output
ports/latches is allowed; feedback changing the field Hamiltonian or bath is
not included in the invariant-marginal result below.

This is a continuous-law quantum/record comparison. It does not supply an
error-certified finite-precision SDE integrator, economical conditioning on
an arbitrary observed history, general nonclassical preparation, arbitrary
field feedback, a complexity separation, or a new experimentally accessible
regime. Existing positive-P simulations are a serious, often stronger
practical incumbent, not an ablation to be ignored.

1. Exact source model and source identity
-----------------------------------------
There are M bosonic modes, B_j=sqrt(theta) a_j. The actual quantum generator is

 L_Q rho = -i[H_theta(t),rho]
           +sum_j [d D[a_j]+b D[a_j^dagger]]rho,
 H_theta = theta^(-1)[B^dagger h(t)B+sum_e :W_e(B^dagger,B):],
 b=kappa nu/theta, d=kappa(nu+theta)/theta.                 (1)

h is Hermitian. Every real W_e is homogeneous of bidegree (2,2), supported
on a set A_e of r_e<=r modes. Its coefficients and the deterministic/exogenous
physical controls are supplied. h may include number-conserving hopping.
The preparations are the same supplied positive coherent mixtures as in P1;
for the explicit bound take the thermal product mixture

 K_z=|z/sqrt(theta)><z/sqrt(theta)|,
 mu(dz)=(pi nu)^(-M) exp(-|z|^2/nu) dz.                   (2)

An initial half-trace preparation error delta_prep can be added. This does
not acquire (2) from observations or approximate an arbitrary quantum input.

Let X=(Re z,Im z). For each interaction define C_e=(partial_bar_i
partial_bar_j W_e) on A_e and its embedded real covariance

 D_e=(theta/2) [[Im C_e,-Re C_e],[-Re C_e,-Im C_e]].        (3)

The exact source kernel identity is

 L_Q K_z = [b_cl(X).grad +(1/2)(D_0+sum_e D_e):Hess]K_z,
 D_0=(kappa nu/2) I,
 b_cl=real(-ihz-i partial_bar sum_e W_e)-kappa X/2.        (4)

The trace norm of the residual from attenuating the e-th correction is

 ||defect_e(z)||_1 = sqrt(2)(1-chi_e)||C_e(z)||_F.         (5)

These are the actual governing identities of the pinned P1 source, Sections
2 and 5-7. A separate finite-matrix check below includes hopping, onsite
Kerr, cross-density and pair hopping. Ordinary-P calculus, CP Duhamel
comparison and coherent count identities are conventional ingredients.

2. Local construction; no dense covariance oracle
------------------------------------------------
Supply computable bounds

 ||C_e(z)||_op <= q_(2,e) S_e,
 ||C_e(z)||_F <= q_(F,e) S_e,
 S_e=sum_(j in A_e)|z_j|^2.                              (6)

For bounded-size polynomial terms these can be obtained by finite sums of
coefficient magnitudes; an optimized tensor norm oracle is unnecessary.
Choose a_e>0 with sum_(e:j in A_e) a_e<=1 for every mode. For maximum incidence
D, a_e=1/D is a valid explicit allocation. Fix 0<eta<1 and define

 B_e=eta a_e kappa nu/(theta q_(2,e)),
 chi_e=min(1,B_e/S_e),
 D_hat=D_0+sum_e chi_e D_e.                              (7)

Terms with q_(2,e)=0 have no defect and need no cutoff. Each local correction
satisfies ||chi_e D_e||<=eta a_e kappa nu/2. Consequently

 (1-eta)D_0 <= D_hat <= (1+eta)D_0.                      (8)

The ORIGINAL classical Hamiltonian drift is retained. This changes the
comparison process, not the physical quantum generator or its noise floor.

There is an explicit local PSD decomposition:

 D_hat=(kappa nu/2)(I-sum_e a_e Pi_(A_e))
       +sum_e [(a_e kappa nu/2)Pi_(A_e)+chi_e D_e].        (9)

Every bracket lives on 2r_e real coordinates and is uniformly positive
there. Independent local Brownian vectors with those small matrix roots,
plus the diagonal residual, have exactly covariance D_hat. For fixed r,D,
coefficient evaluation and noise-factor application cost O(number of terms)
per proposed numerical step, apart from the separately paid hopping update.
Local PSD factorization by summing noise terms is standard; it is not claimed
as a new matrix factorization algorithm. This statement does not choose a
time step or certify the finite-precision simulation error.

3. Why local cutoffs preserve the thermal marginal
--------------------------------------------------
The useful additional identity is termwise. In its 2r_e real variables D_e
is symmetric, homogeneous quadratic, entrywise harmonic, traceless, and

    X_e^T D_e X_e=0.                                    (10)

Holomorphic quadratic C_e gives harmonic entries; Euler homogeneity and
reality of W_e give the radial quadratic form identity. Apply a Laplacian to
(10): 0=2 tr D_e+4 X_e.div D_e+X_e^T(Delta D_e)X_e, hence
X_e.div D_e=0. A second Laplacian, using linear div D_e, gives divdiv D_e=0.

For EVERY scalar radial function g(S_e), including the distributional cutoff
limit in (7),

 divdiv[g(S_e)D_e]
   =g divdiv D_e+4g' X_e.div D_e+2g' tr D_e
                    +4g'' X_e^T D_e X_e =0.             (11)

Take g=chi_e exp(-S_e/nu). The possible derivative jump at B_e contributes
zero by these same polynomial identities. The real Hamiltonian drift is
divergence free and tangent to the total radius; it separately preserves mu.
The OU part with covariance D_0 also preserves mu. Therefore the full local
positive comparison diffusion has the EXACT invariant Gaussian marginal mu.
This holds under arbitrary deterministic time dependence within the bounds.

Nonexplosion does not rely only on a formal stationary density: trace D_e=0
and X_e^T D_e X_e=0 also leave the total-radius CIR law unchanged,

 dS=kappa(M nu-S)dt+sqrt(2 kappa nu S)dB_t.                (12)

Local cut covariance terms are bounded and Lipschitz, the diffusion is
uniformly elliptic, and the polynomial drift is tangent apart from damping.
The source's moment-localization/core argument then applies. Invariance and
uniqueness identify the thermal marginal, so each S_e/nu is Gamma(r_e,1)
at deterministic times. No conditional-Gaussian claim after a record is made.

4. Complete records, rather than merely one-time states
------------------------------------------------------
Use the same permitted annihilation counts as P1,

 M_r=sqrt(c_r) u_r^dagger B,
 lambda_r(z)=c_r |u_r^dagger z|^2,
 theta sum_r c_r u_r u_r^dagger <= d I.                   (13)

Their coherent jump action is exactly M_r K_z M_r^dagger=lambda_r(z)K_z.
The identical dispatch multiplication is subtracted from both no-event
generators. Thus the residual is ONLY sum_e defect_e, including on no-count
branches. Latches, censoring, reset and a retained sensing policy are shared
positive record-routing maps. Sensing choices may depend on past records,
provided they do not change the physical field generator in (1).

CP contractivity on the complete subnormalized record blocks gives

 (1/2)||rho_Q,CQ(T)-R_theta(mu_hat,CQ(T))||_1
 <= delta_prep +sum_e q_(F,e)/sqrt(2)
                          integral_0^T E(S_e(t)-B_e)_+ dt.  (14)

Summing record masses leaves the unconditioned reference diffusion, so (11)
supplies its local Gamma marginals even under adaptive sensing. With
beta_e=B_e/nu the explicit bound is

 TV(P_Q^T,P_hat^T)
 <= min(1,delta_prep + (T nu/sqrt(2)) sum_e q_(F,e)
          exp(-beta_e) sum_(k=0)^(r_e-1) (r_e-k) beta_e^k/k!).  (15)

At bounded r,D and bounded local coefficient envelopes, beta_e is a LOCAL
noise/quantum-nonlinearity ratio. Number of modes enters through the number
of terms, not through the Gamma shape or a global covariance-norm bound.
This gives an intensive local defect exponentially small in that ratio and
linear accumulation over spacetime. It does not make an exponentially long
record cheap to generate, and full-record TV is not a uniform guarantee for
an arbitrarily rare conditioned record.

5. Nonvacuous calculation and observation target
-----------------------------------------------
For onsite Kerr terms W_j=(g/2)|z_j|^4, arbitrary number-conserving hopping,
a_j=1, q_(2,j)=q_(F,j)=|g|. Thus

    TV <= delta_prep +(M T |g| nu/sqrt(2))
                      exp[-eta kappa/(theta |g|)].       (16)

For M=1000, T=100, kappa=64, theta=nu=g=1, eta=1/2,

    TV < delta_prep +8.954917337833550e-10.

The old global radius has mean 1000 and cutoff 32. Jensen gives
E(S-32)_+>=968, so the old corresponding global certificate is vacuous.
The new number is an analytic continuous-law certificate, not an executed
1000-mode simulation, statistical confidence, or physical observation.

The record target matters. Pooled total counts do not detect number-conserving
Hamiltonians; the existing scalar CIR quotient is then stronger and exact.
Interferometric ports are different. For two thermal modes of mean n, port
b=(a_1+a_2)/sqrt(2), and H=(U/2)n_1(n_1-1), the purely Hamiltonian contribution
to the two-click intensity curvature is

    G_2''(0)=-U^2 n^3(3n+1),                             (17)

even though the unconditioned density is stationary. The code derives this
from exact geometric moments and checks it against a Fock matrix. Thus the
new record statement is not just a redundant representation of an unchanged
thermal one-time state or of the exact pooled-count scalar process.

6. A live failure and matched incumbents
---------------------------------------
Do not extend (11) to arbitrary feedback on the field. With independent
thermal intensities S_1,S_2 of mean nu, observe port 2 at rate c S_2 and SWAP
the modes after its count. This legal record-based physical feedback changes
the mode-1 mean at the initial instant by

    c E[S_2(S_2-S_1)] = c nu^2 >0.                       (18)

The local Gaussian marginal is immediately lost. Global CIR conservation
does not repair this local step. Driven/nonstationary preparations and field
feedback need a new local tail theorem, not reuse of (15).

Strong ordinary comparators are exact positive-P (Deuar et al., 2012.02014),
conditional phase-space filters (Hush et al.), trace-norm semiclassical
correspondence (Hernandez--Ranard--Riedel, 2307.05326v3; Li, 2408.16953), and
general PSD Fokker-Planck criteria (Yoneya et al., 2608.06056v2). Existing
positive-P already simulates large interacting driven systems in regimes
with adequate dissipation, often including much stronger interactions and
more general preparation than (16). No runtime win is established here.
The useful new-to-this-investigation construction is the local radial
clipping/invariance/complete-record certificate, not positivity or scalability
by themselves. Historical novelty remains unassessed.
