1 November 2026. Solo research. General threshold remains open.

1. A physical merger defeats the independent-component potential
---------------------------------------------------------------
Take Z plaquette errors in one xy plane of the actual 4D toric model. In one
row put two length-ell strips, separated by one missing face. Their syndromes
are two rectangular loops, each diameter ell. A single Z on the gap face
merges them into a length-m strip, m=2ell+1. All lengths can be chosen well
inside a contractible torus box. A further adjacent face can make the merged
state pivotal for a fixed diameter threshold.

This is the native correction law: before the merger there are exactly two
active outgoing-pair predicates, one at each left corner; after it there is
one. Each fires at rate kappa and removes the leftmost face of its strip.
It is not a birth-cluster proxy or an assumption about every clock schedule.

Try the natural extension of the Young potential

    f(s)=sum_(syndrome components C) 16^(diam(C)-m).

Set kappa=1, h=1/32, gamma=1/4. The two states have

    f_b=2*16^(ell-m), f_a=1,
    Q_0 f_b=-(15/16) f_b, Q_0 f_a=-(15/16) f_a.

The gap Hamiltonian h Z_gap supplies an off-diagonal entry
i h(f_a-f_b) in L^*(diag f). Negative semidefiniteness of
L^*F+gamma F-epsilon I requires the principal determinant

    [(11/16)f_b+epsilon][11/16+epsilon]
                    -h^2(1-f_b)^2 >=0.                  (1)

For epsilon=8^(-m), exact native torus checks at ell=3,6,10 give a negative
determinant. As ell grows, any such source must instead approach at least

    [sqrt((11/16)^2+4h^2)-11/16]/2 >0.                  (2)

This refutes THIS additive potential, not a nonadditive halo and not the
physical memory. The previously earned Young-diagram barrier is unchanged.
The missing observable must charge configurations that are about to merge,
even when every current component is subthreshold. A polynomial count of
components or the old AND-birth counterexample cannot supply that charge.

2. Exact one-plane reduction to a coherently grouped quantum Toom model
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Let e_f in {0,1} mark whether a Z error occupies an xy plaquette in one
periodic plane. In its invariant physical sector the relevant X-edge checks
have eigenvalues

    S_bottom=(-1)^(e_f+e_down),
    S_left  =(-1)^(e_f+e_left).

Consequently the native pair corrector is active iff the left and lower
neighbours agree and the central spin disagrees. Choose virtual Pauli Z_f
with eigenvalue (-1)^e_f and virtual X_f that toggles e_f. Then

    J_f = X_f (I-Z_f Z_left)(I-Z_f Z_down)/4,
    H_fault=sum_f h_f X_f tensor B_f.                    (3)

This covers arbitrary nucleation, mergers and shapes in that plane, and
unconditional local physical Z Hamiltonians: no Young-shape predicate is
inserted into the perturbation. For Hermitian B_f, ||B_f||<=1, they couple
to one persistent bath. Actual ideal correction is kappa sum_f D[J_f], or
the distinct completed version with D[I-P_f] added. Both are continuous-time
generators, not a discrete PCA used without a transfer proof.

The identification has a genuine logical degree of freedom. On a full plane
two error patterns with the same syndrome differ by the whole logical Z
membrane. Choose a physical logical-X eigenbasis: the virtual repetition-code
states |0...0>, |1...1> represent that encoded qubit. The other five logical
qubits are spectators. Physical Z_f becomes virtual X_f; the code plus all
plane errors has exactly this virtual sector. This is an exact operator
reduction, not the construction of a physical 2D toric quantum memory.
The inherited fixed decoder must still be carried through the reduction.

All 512 configurations of a 3x3 plane and every local predicate were checked
exactly (4608 cases), including complement symmetry. The all-size identity
is simply the displayed stabilizer eigenvalue calculation. The multi-plane,
both-CSS, generic local-Hamiltonian threshold does not follow from it.

3. Why an ordinary ordered NEC phase is not yet the quantum memory
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The primary Brighi--Biella NEC paper, arXiv:2506.19011v2, studies nearby
kinetically constrained spin dynamics with coherent Hamiltonians. Cluster
mean-field calculations find bistability and island reabsorption. It is a
serious phenomenon to investigate, not a rigorous reference-uniform memory
theorem. Its orientation-resolved spin-raising/lowering jumps, Eqs. (2)-(3),
also require a generator check before importing their phase diagram.

The relevant distinction is exact and can be tested on three spins. At zero
classical error the orientation-resolved majority actions are

    A_down=|000><100|, A_up=|111><011|.

The native coherent grouping has J=A_down+A_up. It commutes with the global
spin complement. The split Lindbladian uses D[A_down]+D[A_up] instead of
D[J]. The diagonal population update agrees, but on |100><011| the jump part
is respectively |000><111| and zero. With an untouched reference, correction
of (|100>|0_R>+|011>|1_R>)/sqrt(2) has Bell fidelity 1 for J and 1/2 for the
split instrument. Both flip the same classical wrong spin.

Both displayed maps are completed to CPTP channels with the same inactive
Kraus I-P, P=|100><100|+|011><011|. That Kraus annihilates this test input.
The fidelity comparison is therefore for the complete proposal channels,
not an assertion of trace preservation after postselecting an outcome.

Thus classical magnetisation bistability does not certify retention of an
unknown qubit. Under coherent drive even population dynamics can differ,
because the changed syndrome coherences feed back into populations. One
cannot silently import the split model's quantum phase diagram either.
Unreading a bath record does not physically replace the split dissipators
by D[J]; unitary Kraus mixing retains a complementary jump channel. The
native N881 generator already specifies J, but its physical implementation
is a supplied resource, not acquired by this argument.

The exact correction test was executed, with the reference and full maps.
This makes the possible connection precise without allowing a favourable
unravelling or measurement to perform the central discovery.

4. Recommendation for the next substantive construction
--------------------------------------------------------
The highest-upside immediate question is whether the coherently grouped
model (3) has a size-uniform information-preserving phase under genuinely
coherent local perturbations, and which local eroder mechanisms suffice.
This is a sharper research object than an absolute sum of static witnesses.

Use the earned fixed-decoder CP comparison as one tool. Its positive
observable must include coherent precursor layers for nucleation and
incipient mergers; the independent-component version fails by (1). A
contractive signed-prefix construction is also eligible. The argument must
keep actual clocks, syndrome coherence, arbitrary shared-bath/reference
correlations and a justified continuum. No positive classical tail is an
automatic substitute.

An initial one-CSS Toom result would be a real restricted step. A complete
memory result still needs both CSS sectors and allowed faults, followed by
a separate low-dimensional controller or a justified architecture. The
4D theorem, 2D synchronous QCA threshold and time-independent 2D threshold
are three different claims. The conventional 2601.20818v3 continuous-time
extension is outlined with a formal threshold proof left open; 2610.05589
also distinguishes Floquet from time-independent transfer. Neither is
relabelled as missing ordinary quantum fault tolerance.
