TWO MATERIALLY DIFFERENT OPENINGS THAT DID NOT WIN THIS COMPARISON
1 November 2026. These are tests of proposed connections, not accusations
that the Astra source authors claimed the broader consequences.

1. Certified strongly correlated fermionic states -> superconducting matter
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Complete success could mean a controllable, physically realized strongly
correlated charged phase, or a generally useful certified route to its
microscopic prediction. That consequence would be enormous. The actual
Astra N948 ingredient is narrower: given a full pairing matrix F, certify
a weak-bridge dimer representation and compute the hard-projected norm and
fixed-support SPIN observables. Its gauge search and polymer bounds are
useful ingredients. They are not a ground-state or materials oracle.

The decisive connection already fails at the state contract. N948 imposes
EXACTLY ONE fermion on EVERY site. For disjoint bonds A={i,j}, B={k,l}, let
Delta_A=c_(i,up)c_(j,down)-c_(i,down)c_(j,up). Then

   P_one Delta_A^dagger Delta_B P_one=0, A != B.          (1)

An annihilated site not recreated stays empty; a newly created occupied site
becomes double. The same site-support argument makes the full charge-pair
density matrix block diagonal in unordered site pairs. The local spin block
dimension is bounded. In particular the singlet-bond Gram matrix is diagonal
and every diagonal entry is <=2, uniformly in system size. Thus this exact
full-occupancy family has no extensive charged-pair eigenvalue (charge ODLRO).
This does not rule out spin order, spin liquids or useful variational energies.

Allowing holes changes the problem rather than fixing it for free. The source
identity psi(D F D)=(product_i D_i)psi(F) uses every site once. At non-full
occupancy the occupied subset changes between terms and the factor is not
common. On four sites with reference bonds 12 and 34, one pair and
D=diag(2,2,1,1), the occupation of site 1 changes from 1/2 to 16/17. At full
occupancy the same transformation is just a scalar 4 and leaves the state
unchanged. Exact rational-complex Fock tests retain these facts and a doped
off-diagonal pair correlator of 1.

The next genuine construction would need a charge-carrying, hole-admitting
certificate which preserves the physical state under all transformations,
plus a Hamiltonian/energy or phase-stiffness argument. A supplied pairing
matrix with an anomalous expectation is not that argument. Number projection
can manufacture a coherent pair state even for independent dimers, with no
inter-dimer charge stiffness. Such a state does not acquire a finite-T material.

The strong ordinary approach is established doped projected-BCS/RVB
variational Monte Carlo, tensor methods and ordinary cluster expansions in
their valid regimes; the original proof explicitly acknowledges these.
The primary RVB literature distinguishes the full-occupancy spin problem
from a doped charge-carrying state (e.g. Anderson et al., cond-mat/0311467).
This opening remains potentially important but lacks an acquired physical
bridge and loses to the memory/record leads on present accessibility.

2. Exact representation acquisition -> cumulative scientific/algorithm learning
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Complete success would let a learner acquire reusable representations that
make later discovery cheaper, with total observation, search, execution and
verification costs included. This is at least as consequential as the other
destinations. The actual L01 ingredient is an explicit Volterra/scalar-grid
detector and exact minimal realization learner for proper recognizable
NONCOMMUTATIVE series, supplied an exact FULL MATRIX-OUTPUT evaluation oracle.
It charges query dimensions, full responses, coefficient heights and algebra.
It does not supply that oracle from ordinary scalar observations.

Two discriminating tests show why that distinction carries the central work:

* AB-BA vanishes at every commuting scalar substitution yet is nonzero on
  the legal jointly nilpotent matrices A=E12, B=E23, where it equals E13.
  Scalar substitution data cannot distinguish it from zero, even noiselessly.
* Even exact word-membership access plus a rank promise does not suffice for
  polynomial acquisition in general. For a uniformly unknown length-ell word
  w*, f(w)=1_(w=w*) has a rank-(ell+1) chain realization. A membership query
  tests one candidate; q queries succeed with probability at most q/2^ell.
  Longer or shorter words give zero. An exact ell=8 control enumerates all
  256 words and checks the unique nonzero response of the explicit automaton.

These are different interfaces: the first tests scalar substitutions; the
second tests word observations. Neither refutes L01's richer full-response
oracle theorem, white-box algebraic acquisition, or learning with additional
information. Conversely an equivalence query revealing a hidden word, an
intermediate trace, an informative curriculum, or a coherent matrix oracle
must be acquired and charged, not renamed as the solution.

The strongest equally informed ordinary alternatives are white-box reachable-
space methods when source is supplied, active/spectral automata learning
under adequate access and conditioning, and program-library systems such as
DreamCoder when task structure and reusable program priors are available.
DreamCoder is a genuine prior for compositional library learning, not a proof
of universal acquisition. A useful Astra opening would have to construct an
affordable, stable observation-to-representation bridge that beats those
matched methods; the exact algebraic detector alone does not do so.

This is a high-upside research area. I did not find a serious new constructive
foothold in the legal data interface in this turn, so it is not the leader.
