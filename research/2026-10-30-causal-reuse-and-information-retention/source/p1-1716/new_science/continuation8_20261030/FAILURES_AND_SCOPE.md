## Mechanism selection

- A common random activity clock is a real constructive alternative. At fixed
  mean speed its stationary two-state occupation freedom broadens the scalar
  current tails; a deterministic duty schedule using the same speed ports
  removes that broadening. Correlation alone is not a benefit. The main mechanism
  instead selects a collective current through event-coupled memory.
- The ordinary all-to-all Gaussian pair bath preserves an entire finite-system
  density law while eliminating total current. That is stronger density-law
  equality than the exclusion construction, at a different supplied bath.
  This comparison defeats broad novelty based only on unchanged diffusion and
  reduced circulation noise.
- Opposed-polarity event memory gives a useful exact transport lock and a
  productive conversion cycle. Tight coupling, stall relations and one-bit
  Brownian join gates have direct predecessors. This follow-on is retained as
  an application rather than promoted to a new thermodynamic principle.

## Proof failures that changed the construction

- A crude bounded estimate on u L_ex(A_H/A) could not remove the external
  clock. The successful second corrector uses the stronger local variation
  estimate Delta_i L_ex(A_H/A)=O(1/A), followed by division by A. It pays for
  rare small activity through the separate inverse-activity theorem.
- Typical equilibrium concentration of A is insufficient for a speed-N path
  LDP. The finite-test spatial-energy/occupation compactness proof supplies
  the required superexponential inverse-activity bound.
- D A=0 does not imply D f(A)=0. The accepted quotient expansion keeps its
  nonlinear remainder. No chain-rule cancellation is asserted for jump rates.
- For phase averaging, a supremum of a sum cannot replace a sum of individual
  coefficient suprema. The proof uses the valid O(N) squared-coefficient bound
  and kappa_N -> infinity.
- An exact event-pair trace changes the clock. The preserved four-state example
  has different stationary occupation fractions in its conserved sectors.
  An unconditional mean clock fraction does not identify actual elapsed time.
  The second-corrector construction works directly in elapsed physical time.
- A standing single sinusoidal density path may have zero cost separation.
  A growing traveling wave away from half filling supplies a nonzero separation.
  The numerical small example verifies algebra, not practical significance.
- The coupled-current two-count error formula presumes every selected event
  still flips the bit. A stuck bit defeats that bound. The receiving review
  supplied this challenge; the fault-accounting addendum explicitly counts
  missed updates and gives the complete eventwise mismatch identity.

## Physical, information and performance scope

The action concerns the declared density/current record, not an arbitrary joint
apparatus, timing and selector record. Retained records are allowed only where
the actual conditional initializer and full future innovation law meet the
theorem. A record exposing future native or phase noise changes that law.

The actual associated deterministic initial state is used in the LDP receiving
argument. An exp(O(N)) reference-density ceiling transfers superexponential
replacement estimates; it does not transfer finite-action probabilities for
free. Random initial profiles may carry their own static action. The stronger
stationary fluctuation initialization is separately paid.

The operation is not specified by equilibrium weights alone. Unknown selected
mean rates, phase-dependent amplitude coefficients, nonreciprocal errors or
unmeasured composition of interventions fall outside the hidden-kinetics
supplier. Native-event/gate compatibility, independent phase control and
atomic updates are actual requirements. A one-bit memory still couples to
order N^3 native events over the diffusive observation horizon in the interior
stationary regime. Removing its external reset clock does not remove those
updates, interaction range, sensing or actuator bandwidth.

The invariant canonical law and variational quasipotential remain unchanged.
The action increases costs of selected dynamical histories; this does not
automatically increase equilibrium state barriers or improve every rare-event
objective. The stationary circulating-force response is reduced along with
the corresponding noise. No unchanged response with freely improved precision
is claimed.

No new end-to-end finite-data calibration theorem, quantitative finite-N error,
apparatus, energy budget, practical speedup, guaranteed conversion power or
general control of arbitrary unknown kinetics has been established. The earlier
vacuous long-record bound and ill-conditioned calibration fixture remain useful
evidence, not repaired merely by these population constructions.
