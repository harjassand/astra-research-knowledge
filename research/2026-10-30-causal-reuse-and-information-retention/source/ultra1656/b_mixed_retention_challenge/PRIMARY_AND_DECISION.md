## Strong ordinary construction on the SAME information

With private weights r_i>0, standard unambiguous discrimination already
produces a finite heralded inverse. One explicit success probability is
`p=1/||sum_i P_i/r_i||`, with POVM effects `p P_i/r_i`; numerical
optimization of the POVM may improve it. Instrument DPI gives
`J_tau>=p J_id`, even with changing reference marginals, because the
failure flag then carries exactly the reference marginal. Every
uniform-success unambiguous instrument has p<=rmin. For two pure
outputs with overlap s, the optimal p is1-s, whereas rmin=1-s^2.

The proposed operation replaces this possibly nonsharp physical
success bound by the exact Umegaki coefficient, while retaining full
conditional semantics and arbitrary finite reference dimensions. It
does so by a Hilbert--Schmidt projection whose individual effects
cannot generally form one POVM. That distinction is mathematically
substantive, but it is not a superior physical label-recovery device.

## What must remain separate

The formula would solve the minimum-retention question for finite cq
preparations on exact input matrices. It does not solve arbitrary
pairwise CQ channel comparison, prove necessity of the general
two-unrestricted-family SDP condition, or grant a finite reference
cutoff in other comparison problems. It gives neither a quantum
capacity advantage nor a practical experiment without calibration.

Full-rank contamination makes the universal reverse factor infinite,
even at arbitrarily small contamination. Hence exact support admission
is genuinely required. Restricting priors, changing the loss to a
fixed-reference one, or asking for additive approximate inversion
would lead to different potentially robust operations; no such
replacement is silently included.
