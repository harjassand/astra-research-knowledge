arXiv2411.07536v1, Theorem1.3 and §§3,5,6,7:
https://arxiv.org/html/2411.07536v1 . The official versioned HTML was read and cached;

teacher-free sampler. Mixture-density L1 sketches, distributional spanners, robust

and credit those operations. The present global-KL assumption does not make Q a
conditional oracle for P, nor does Q have supplied low rank.

Root's OPERATION and LOCALIZATION_LEMMAS were also read. Their ordinary robust
projection identity is retained. The failed fresh-only child-coverage proof and

gradient sketch: a fresh-row mixture controls the first argument, arbitrary joint
children occur only inside a bounded clipped logarithm, and signed full states
are controlled in negative MASS. The direct child-normalization bound preserves
the token KL in a complete sequence telescope. Historical priority of this
specific extension is UNKNOWN; no claim is made that KL projection itself is new.

## The value oracle is a consequential resource

PROOF permits positive-Q conditional samples AND warranted next-token probability
VALUES at a requested number of bits. No P conditional, target likelihood, chosen
prefix reset or physical experiment is provided. Target trajectories, teacher
computation, random bits and arbitrary precision all have real costs.

The full-future floor is theta*a^L. Arbitrary signed children can nearly cancel,
so full-density/log reference evaluation can require absolute precision
exponential in T in VALUE, with polynomial bit length. The proposed algorithm is
polynomial relative to a probability-value service with polynomial cost in those
bits. Its ACTUAL cost C_Q(T,bits) must be multiplied in. Replacing that service by
conditional-SAMPLE estimates can take exponential samples. Thus this does NOT
establish the sample-only misspecified-teacher extension of Liu--Moitra.

JOINT_RESTRICTIONS caches Q(o f|h) at positive-Q fresh prefixes, not Q(f|h o).
Its stopping/interval rule avoids zero-child oracle calls. Cached numerical
entries are not presumed globally consistent densities: virtual TRUE Q rows
provide exact mass1, coefficient sum1 is exact, and explicit error/slack bounds
pay token-normalization and empirical-gradient discrepancies. The ideal full
state is not retained as an evaluator after compilation.

## General cost rather than an astronomical run

The formal schedule is an explicit fixed polynomial. With x=A/tau it bounds
D<=x^41, N<=x^61 and M<=x^1200 per cut. A conservative teacher-call majorant is
O(T^2[NM+(O+1)(r+1)D+M+D]); P sampling uses at most TN full trajectories.
The maximum probability precision is polynomial in T log(O/a),logN,logM,logD
and log(1/tau). Formal geometry uses M*N entries; output joint/marginal arrays
use O(T O(r+1)D) scalars. If feature strings are retained as provenance they add
O(T^2 D log O) bits; inference does not need those strings. Each coefficient
state and convex program is finite and polynomial-size. The primitive service
cost and full-trajectory acquisition cost are additional multiplicative factors.

At Delta0 the schedule remains an enormous fixed polynomial in1/epsilon. For
nonzero Delta, the Holder exponent1/16384 and coefficient128A^3 usually make the
guarantee vacuous. A nonvacuous requested tolerance can require fantastically
small supplied Delta. Neither the formal sample counts nor interval SVD/LP
algorithm have been executed at this schedule. The claims are a proposed
constructive mathematical proof, not benchmarked practical model acquisition.

## Strong ordinary comparison on IDENTICAL source/teacher access

A complete teacher prefix trie is the strongest elementary short-horizon control.
ordinary_trie.py was executed ON THE SAME declared T8 input after the main run,
without rerunning the P sampler, geometry, compiler or optimizer. It uses255 Q
next-value calls, zero P samples,255 cached categorical rows and zero teacher
calls after compilation. It took0.000877792s to compile and0.000101500s for all256
outputs, and attained TV0.01690634597, KL0.003396289370. It therefore beats the
engineering compiler's TV, query count and execution time; our KL is slightly
smaller. Its O(O^T) general size/query cost does not meet the proposed theorem.

The predeclared ordinary two-Bernoulli EM used the SAME256 P trajectories and
two fixed starts,500 iterations each. It took0.016641834s and attained TV0.059016717.
It has a stronger two-component iid-family promise than general predictive rank2
and needs no teacher. Its speed cannot be dismissed; its different accuracy
does not prove our general method's usefulness. No all-solver dominance follows.

Ordinary spectral/TT learners retain their actual visibility/conditioning and
positivity resources. The already established same-target conditional learner
does not by itself query a different arbitrary-rank teacher. An equally informed
ordinary method may adopt the proposed asymmetric receiver; this package does
not exclude that possibility or convert adoption into a historical novelty claim.
