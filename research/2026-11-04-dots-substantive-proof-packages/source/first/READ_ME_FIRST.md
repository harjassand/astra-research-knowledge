# Substantive research capital: proofs and constructions

Compiled 4 November 2026. These are research working documents, not independently published or externally verified results. We selected complete arguments and substantial constructions rather than code or routine exploratory screens. No Python files are included. Prior literature and source dependencies are identified within the documents. Internal audits are not external peer review. Numerical and implementation claims remain subject to the caveats in each document. Some original notes refer to supporting files not included in this proof-only package.

1. Quantum memory: a conditional three-body-qubit construction and ideal preparation, with independent internal audits. The model has stringent assumptions and extremely cold resource requirements. No general noisy-write-to-long-hold or practical advantage has been established.
2. Quantum heat engine: internal challenge to a published paper's energy accounting, including finite-unitary model and an example. Not externally verified; scope is the stated contact model, not all engines or the entire paper.
3. Mean lifetime: common-form upper bound and normalized asymptotic bounds, plus independent internal audit and exact countermodel. The varying-rate correction in the documents is important.
4. Jensen inequality: conditional separated-potential certificate and local nonseparated stability extension. Does not solve unrestricted quasiconvexity.
5. Cochain construction: explicit algebra, filling and distance for the stated intrinsic-page completion. Not a thermal-lifetime proof or a claim for all external source incidences.
6. Autonomous clock: a finite-reserve construction and physical guiding-center test. Neither a realized device nor a same-resource advantage.
7. Impurity spectroscopy: restricted mathematical localization theorem and Schur construction. Distinguish exploratory floating-point results from exact small finite-bath audits; no general solver speedup established.
8. Glass swap annealing: conditional equilibrium/ensemble argument and attempted physical transfer. Exclusive, fast transfer and practical speedup remain unbuilt.
