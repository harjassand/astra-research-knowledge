# Complete subgap spectroscopy by uniform Sylvester localization

Proposed proof, 2026-11-03. Independent audit requested. This is a restricted massive-bath result, not an efficient general excited-state or extensive-interaction theorem.

## Assumptions and statement

Let H=dΓ(h)+V on fermionic Fock space (optionally tensored with a finite-dimensional impurity of dimension d_I). Assume gI≤h≤RI, g>0, and V is bounded, self-adjoint, globally even, and supported on the impurity and the CAR algebra of an m-dimensional bath source space S. Set v=||V|| and E0=inf spec H. Both particle-number-nonconserving and strong interactions are allowed. All parity sectors must be included in E0 and in the intermediate annihilation map. Fix 0<δ<g and a=g−δ.

Block Krylov spaces K_L=span{S,hS,...,h^(L−1)S} have dimension ≤mL. Let Π_L project onto the full Fock space on K_L with its orthogonal complement in vacuum, retaining the entire finite impurity. The compressed Hamiltonian is H_L=Π_L HΠ_L restricted to ran Π_L. It is an actual Galerkin compression, not an ad hoc chain modification.

Choose α=log(1+δ/(4R)), C=4 sqrt(m) v/δ, η_L=C exp(−αL), and b=2mR. Then for the full spectral projector P=1_[E0,E0+a](H),

  ||(1−Π_L)P|| ≤ η_L.

For η_L<1, P has finite rank ≤d_I 2^(mL). In particular the bottom is an eigenvalue, without a prior ground-existence assumption. If λ_k≤E0+a is the kth full eigenvalue, counting multiplicity, and μ_k is the kth eigenvalue of H_L, then

  λ_k ≤ μ_k ≤ λ_k + ε_L,
  ε_L=(2a η_L²+bη_L)/(1−η_L²).

The lower bound is the minmax principle and supplies the no-spurious-level guarantee. For t≤E0+a and ε_L small,

  N_H(t−ε_L) ≤ N_HL(t) ≤ N_H(t).

A sharp count is certified only when the requested cutoff is separated from a true eigenvalue by the approximation allowance. Ground-aligned excitation energies differ by at most ε_L because both λ_k and λ_1 are shifted upward by numbers in [0,ε_L]. For a finite matrix input h, computing a block Krylov compression and diagonalizing its full Fock matrix yields polynomial cost in bath size and 1/ε at fixed m,d_I,R/δ,v. Explicitly L=O((R/δ) log[(1+sqrt(m)v/δ)(1+mR)/ε]), and matrix dimension is d_I exp(O(mL)). This is not uniform polynomial complexity as m or R/δ grows, and a continuum input still needs a certified one-particle matrix-element oracle.

## Proof: uniform over the entire spectral subspace

Use the collective annihilation map Aψ=Σ_j a_jψ⊗e_j. Choose the conjugate one-particle convention if necessary so the coefficient operator in the commutator is h rather than its transpose. Either convention preserves positivity and the Krylov chain. A* A=N. On P the map AP is bounded, since gN≤H+v and hence ||AP||²≤(E0+a+v)/g. Here H is bounded below by −v and E0≤v.

With Bψ=Σ_j [V,a_j]ψ⊗e_j, the exact commutator gives

  (H⊗I+I⊗h) AP − AP H_P = B P.

B is supported on S in its one-particle coordinate, and ||B||≤2sqrt(m)v. This bound uses the finite orthonormal source list, not a repeated list of interaction monomials. Evenness is essential: it ensures [V,a_j]=0 for modes outside S.

After subtracting E0, the left generator is F⊗I+I⊗h, where F=H−E0≥0, and the right generator F_P lies in [0,a]. Their separation is at least δ. The unique bounded solution is

  AP = integral_0^∞ [exp(−tF)⊗exp(−th)] B P exp(tF_P) dt.

This is an operator identity on ran P, not separate estimates on a chosen eigenbasis. Therefore it cannot incur a factor sqrt(rank P).

The cyclic subspace generated by S is reducing for h. Its orthogonal complement has B=0, so the same Sylvester uniqueness proves its annihilation map vanishes on P. Thus all subgap states have the dark bath in vacuum.

In the block Krylov decomposition, h is block tridiagonal and each off-diagonal block has norm at most R. Let X be block depth, starting at zero on S. For bounded truncated weights W_M=exp(α min(X,M)),

  ||W_M h W_M^(−1)−h|| ≤ 2R(exp(α)−1)=δ/2.

Indeed the upper and lower block shifts each have norm ≤R, and the neighboring weight ratios are between exp(−α) and exp(α). Consequently the non-self-adjoint weighted one-particle generator has numerical real part ≥g−δ/2 and its semigroup norm is ≤exp[−t(g−δ/2)]. Since W_M B=B, weighting the Sylvester integral gives

  ||(I⊗W_M) AP|| ≤ 2||B||/δ ≤ C.

Monotone passage M→∞ gives the same quadratic bound for the unbounded weight. Because 1−Π_L≤N_outside_KL as forms and the discarded cyclic blocks have depth ≥L,

  ||(1−Π_L)ψ||² ≤ <ψ,N_outside_KL ψ> ≤ C² exp(−2αL)||ψ||²

for every ψ in ran P. Infinite-dimensional statements follow from the bounded-weight identity on the natural number-operator form domain; no unbounded exponential conjugation is silently assumed.

## Proof: no hidden multiplicity or Ritz pollution

If η_L<1, Π_L restricted to ran P is injective and has bounded inverse onto its image, proving finite rank. Every nonempty spectral neighborhood of E0 has a nonzero projector inside P; finite rank then forces E0 to be an attained discrete eigenvalue.

Only the one-body hopping across the final Krylov block connects ran Π_L and its complement. It has at most m singular channels. The CAR norm bound therefore gives ||Π_L H(1−Π_L)||≤b=2mR, independent of L and v. The impurity V commutes with Π_L.

For any unit vector ψ in the span P_k of the first k eigenvectors, write φ=Π_Lψ and q=(1−Π_L)ψ. Then ||φ||²≥1−η_L². Since Fψ lies in P_k and ||F|P_k||≤a,

  <φ,Fφ>=<ψ,Fψ>−<q,Fψ>−<φ,Fq>
             ≤(λ_k−E0)+aη_L²+bη_L.

The first correction is quadratic because <q,Fψ>=<q,(1−Π_L)Fψ>, whose modulus is ≤aη_L². Dividing by ||φ||² yields the displayed ε_L after using λ_k−E0≤a. The k-dimensional trial space Π_L ran P_k and minmax prove the upper bound. Ordinary variational minmax proves the lower bound, including when no kth isolated eigenvalue below the threshold exists. Thus additional compressed levels cannot appear below a cutoff absent corresponding full levels.

## Limits and source comparison

At or above E0+g, a decoupled bath mode of energy g is already a counterexample to complete localization near the impurity. The positive margin δ is mathematically essential. No internal separation between subgap eigenvalues is needed for energy/multiplicity approximation, but stable individual eigenvectors require their usual isolation gap. This theorem does not by itself give an efficient algorithm for gapless baths, finite-temperature real-time correlations, or an area-growing impurity.

Jiang, Ju, Parekh, Rayudu and Zhao, arXiv:2610.02167v2, was checked directly on 2026-11-03: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.02167v2 . Its Section 1.3 identifies excited-state extensions as an open direction; its established ground/thermal algorithms use stronger bandwise occupation compression and handle regimes excluded here. Krylov representations and impurity compression are therefore not new claims. The candidate addition here is a whole-subspace Sylvester estimate below the first massive-bath threshold, with multiplicity-preserving variational spectroscopy. Literature priority beyond that comparator has not been established.

## Effective algorithm, unknown E0, and certified decisions

The construction of K_L and H_L never uses E0. For a requested relative energy window τ≤g−δ, build the truncation using margin δ/2 and a tolerance e≤δ/4. Its smallest eigenvalue μ_1 supplies the computed bracket E0∈[μ_1−e,μ_1]. Every true excitation λ_k−E0≤τ has |(μ_k−μ_1)−(λ_k−E0)|≤e. Conversely, a computed excitation μ_k−μ_1≤τ+e obeys λ_k≤μ_k≤E0+τ+2e≤E0+g−δ/2, so the same theorem applies to it as well. Thus outputting all μ_k−μ_1≤τ+e cannot miss a true requested level, and the only uncertain membership is a width-O(e) band at the cutoff. If the true spectrum avoids [τ−2e,τ+2e], counting computed excitations ≤τ gives the exact multiplicity count. For a gap decision, the first two ordered levels are counted including ground degeneracy; a promise gap or a nonzero numerical separation is necessary to distinguish an exact degeneracy from an arbitrarily tiny splitting. No algorithmic equality decision is claimed without such a promise.

For finite-volume inputs with rational real/imaginary matrix entries of bit length B, a positive-gap promise g and upper bound R, and an explicitly specified finite-source V, chain formation is polynomial in n,L,B and the requested output precision. One robust theoretical implementation constructs the rational Krylov matrix, removes exactly dependent columns by rational elimination, and orthonormalizes its retained Gram matrix to controlled precision. Rational powers through degree L have polynomial bit height. A nonzero Gram determinant then supplies a lower bound 2^(−poly(n,L,B)) on the smallest nonzero Gram eigenvalue, so the conditioning does not require more than polynomially many precision bits. This is a bit-complexity construction, not a claim that naive floating-point Lanczos is numerically reliable. Stable rank decisions must use exact dependence or certified singular-value bounds; an arbitrary numerical deflation threshold is not justified.

If p=dim K_L, a one-body compression error ξ changes dΓ(h_L) by at most pξ; source/interaction realization errors are separately bounded by CAR telescoping. Set these and dense diagonalization errors to a total ζ. The computed Hermitian matrix then has ||H_tilde_L−H_L||≤ζ. Weyl gives certified eigenvalue intervals [μ_tilde_k−ζ,μ_tilde_k+ζ]. Exact no-pollution belongs to H_L; approximate arithmetic inherits it only after subtracting this ζ allowance. Relative energies acquire at most 2ζ additional uncertainty. Dense matrix assembly and certified Hermitian eigenvalue approximation have polynomial arithmetic and bit cost in D=d_I2^p and input precision. Dependence on v enters the logarithm controlling L and the coefficient bit size, not an unmentioned condition-number oracle. Very large m or R/δ still make this algorithm infeasible despite fixed-parameter polynomial accuracy scaling.

For infinitely many bath modes, the theorem is analytic in the specified positive-energy Fock representation and has uniform constants. Finite-volume bounds are uniform only when g,R,m,v are uniform. Turning an abstract infinite h into a finite algorithm requires certified access to the finitely many Krylov moments or matrix operations, with their cost included. The proof does not establish convergence between inequivalent many-body representations, justify arbitrary ultraviolet limits, or turn nonlocal positive-energy source vectors into local physical vectors. Those are separate model-specific obligations in the chiral-interface application.

## Numerical algebra checks

`check_subgap.py` tests eight random eight-mode massive chains with strong density, quartic and pairing impurity terms, at five compression sizes each. All 40 checks passed: maximum collective Sylvester residual 3.32e−14; minimum ordered Ritz shift −1.96e−14 (roundoff). Results are in `check_results.json`. This validates conventions and variational implementation, not the infinite-dimensional theorem. The conservative analytic localization bound is generally greater than one at these tiny sizes, so these tests do not establish useful practical constants or empirical polynomial advantage.
