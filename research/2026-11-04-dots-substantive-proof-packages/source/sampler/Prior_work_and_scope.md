## Primary comparison literature
1. Jerrum and Sinclair, 1993, Polynomial-Time Approximation Algorithms for the Ising Model. https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/0222066 . Established ferromagnetic partition-function approximation, not our algorithmic discovery.
2. Feng, Guo, Wang, 2023, Swendsen-Wang dynamics for the ferromagnetic Ising model with external fields. Information and Computation 294, 105066. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ic.2023.105066 . Published primary PDF: https://www.pure.ed.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/370864187/Swensden_Wang_FENG_DOA27062023_VOR_CC_BY.pdf . Theorem 1.1 and equation (33) supply the fallback mixing guarantee. Important: the constants depend on coupling range; the present proof explicitly preprocesses that range instead of hiding it. The source also explains why ordinary pinning self-reduction can introduce inconsistent fields.
3. Chen and Zhang, SODA 2023 / revised August 18, 2023, A Near-Linear Time Sampler for the Ising Model with External Field. https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.09391 and https://arxiv.org/html/2207.09391v2 . Theorem 1.1 requires fixed positive margins beta>=1+delta_beta and lambda<=1-delta_lambda (after a global flip). This is the strong backend comparator for the fixed-margin audit family. Do not assert a margin-independent near-linear bound.

Goldberg and Jerrum, The Complexity of Ferromagnetic Ising with Local Fields. https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/leslieann.goldberg/papers/ising.pdf ; journal https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/combinatorics-probability-and-computing/article/abs/complexity-of-ferromagnetic-ising-with-local-fields/B0FADE05C770C294D26F802E789C646D .
