# Decision: the attempted local characteristic descent did not acquire an algorithm

We constructed a general exact tangent-update compiler for the ordinary
tensor plus move, then used it on the actual public AlphaTensor rank47
4-by-4 decomposition. The entire one-plus class fails unrestricted mod4
lifting: 12972 ordered moves,6486 distinct expanded states. All27924 flips
from those states fail even an enlarged necessary image test. No repaired
rank47 or rank48 characteristic-zero algorithm was acquired.

The complete two-move boundary is the useful negative outcome. It goes
beyond the already-known obstruction at the original point, but says
nothing about longer structural walks, another modular point, F3 searches,
ramified/even-denominator coefficients, or direct complex/rational searches.
Those alternatives are not ruled out. The construction does not justify
more undirected compute or a capability/novelty claim.

The native scheme is flip-free. Independent equation-row elimination
agrees with column elimination on its Jacobian rank2117 and inconsistency.
The same row code accepts the native Strassen positive control. Whole
Jacobian solutions and the compiler agree on the same64 candidate proposals;
raw derivative-image/residual checks and32 whole post-flip systems pass.
The full scans took15.1563s and28.6214s after imports. The code, proof,
native data, controls and costs are retained together.

Published plus/flip/Hensel search is the ordinary mechanism, not our
invention. Rational48 algorithms already exist. The small Python comparison
shows our quotient compilation is usable; it does not compare to optimized
incumbent implementations or establish a discovery advantage. The strongest
informed ordinary alternatives retain the same native data and the known
characteristic-zero48 construction.

The wave ends at a substantive scoped failure. Resume only with a genuine
new structural acquisition rule that escapes this boundary, or evidence
of repeatable successful repair under an equal-input, paid comparison.
There is no proposed physical experiment, model oracle, large-cluster
allocation or new autonomous search hidden in this conclusion.
