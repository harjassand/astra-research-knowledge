# Primary comparators and exact claim boundary

This checkpoint combines established tools with the constructed positive reservoir/source/first-event interfaces. Correctness review is not a priority search. The following comparisons do not establish the absence of a stronger method elsewhere.

- Saira et al., Equivalent qubit dynamics under classical and quantum noise, Phys. Rev. A75,012308 (2007), https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0605241 . This is a direct classical/quantum noise representation comparator. Its longitudinal thermal-defect realization uses both upward and downward jumps; it is not, from the inspected construction, the transverse ground-initialized one-way damping realization and rate-independent jump-count bound used here.
- Borras and Marvian, Quantum simulation algorithms based on quantum trajectories, Quantum10,2063 (2026), https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2026-04-13-2063/ . Their scalar total-jump-rate setting applies directly to the conventional sign-register dilation. It is a strong informed trajectory comparator. The new amplitude-damped representation has state-dependent rates, which is why its holding-time/no-jump operations had to be constructed separately rather than inherited for free.
- Low and Wiebe, Hamiltonian Simulation in the Interaction Picture, https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.00675 . Coherent Hamiltonian simulation, interaction pictures and high-precision amplification techniques are ordinary machinery. The earlier single-operator checkpoint used their time-dependent simulation interface; the present construction instead acquires a complete first-event instrument from positive virtual-path kernels.
- Li et al., Scalable Gradients for Stochastic Differential Equations, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v108/li20i.html ; Jelinčič, Foster and Kidger, Single-seed generation of Brownian paths and integrals, https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.06464 . Lazy repeatable stochastic paths are established. They do not justify ignoring noncommuting path order or assuming the new quantum Kraus source.
- Bouland, Dandapani and Prakash, A quantum spectral method for simulating stochastic processes, https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.06719 . Efficient quantum amplitude representations of stochastic paths already exist. Their individual-time digital-value interface differs from a reversible trajectory oracle, and neither interface alone supplies the first-event compiler constructed here.
- An, Onwunta and Yang, Fast-forwarding quantum algorithms for linear dissipative differential equations, https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2026-01-27-1986/ . Its strict-dissipativity/state-preparation interface is not immediately the complete trace-preserving controlled process with a stationary zero mode. This is an applicability distinction, not an impossibility claim about adaptations.
- Gao, Ji and Liu, Levy-Khintchine Structure Enables Fast-Forwardable Lindbladian Simulation, https://doi.org/10.1103/qy4b-gzlq . The accessible abstract already establishes fast-forwarded twirling families and diffusion-scale lower bounds. Authorization-restricted full text was not used. Do not attribute uninspected detailed scope to it.

Doob transforms, finite-state Markov bridges, positive matrix majorants, row/column block encodings, generalized amplitude amplification and Chebyshev stability are established mathematical tools. The contribution under review is their fully accounted connection to a retained noncommuting colored-noise process, including the source acquisition and finite-precision joins.

For small explicit Hilbert spaces, dense matrix exponentiation of the exact classical-sign or quantum-reservoir generator is already a strong ordinary route and can have logarithmic dependence on a large rate through scaling and squaring. No advantage over it is claimed in that regime. For large systems, the stated theorem uses supplied efficient quantum operator encodings; their availability and practical cost must be assessed for an actual application.
