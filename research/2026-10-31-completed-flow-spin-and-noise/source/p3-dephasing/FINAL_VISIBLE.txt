
I developed a tractability principle for interacting quantum dynamics with partial dephasing: every increase in the retained operator’s weight can be charged to coherence that the physical noise damps. The resulting certificate allows an exponentially large undamped sector and requires no supplied relaxation gap.

For dense signed Ising interactions, the controlling interaction strength is a certified matrix spectral norm. This yields an explicit finite representation for specified correlations, with a cutoff uniform in time. A further construction gives certified frequency responses, including zero-linewidth transverse responses for exactly described rational models.

The result has a substantial limitation: its current worst-case representation is expensive at weak dephasing. I completed qualified 64-spin frequency calculations, but those runs use strong dephasing, where established measurement-and-feedback methods also provide a useful representation. The advance is the structural theorem and its output guarantees; a practical advantage in the weak-dephasing regime remains unestablished.

The complete account, including proofs, costs, executions, comparisons and preserved failures, is in 
RESEARCH_RECORD.md
.

6. What survives the strongest comparisons

The comparison materially narrowed the claim.

Weighted Pauli propagation is established machinery. Facelli and collaborators already prove weighted-Frobenius contraction using the conjugated-skew/sinh mechanism. Their weight can be optimized for every positive damping strength in their fully damped class. Moreover, their existing full gap gives an informed conventional route to a time-uniform Galerkin cutoff. Uniformity in time alone is therefore not a separate advance here. The new physical certificate uses coherence count rather than full Pauli weight and admits the exponentially large undamped kernel. 
arxiv.org

The strongest fully damped result has better precision complexity. Angrisani and collaborators obtain polynomial dependence jointly on system size, time and inverse accuracy under their assumptions. They explicitly exclude single-axis dephasing because it leaves nonidentity directions undamped. Our theorem supplies a positive result for a specified part of that excluded physical setting, while retaining weaker worst-case precision dependence. Their ordinary propagation engine can adopt the new certificate. 
arxiv.org

I used Astra’s start navigation and governing sources, including N1214’s distinction between a small discarded tail and the error of an actual projected computation.
