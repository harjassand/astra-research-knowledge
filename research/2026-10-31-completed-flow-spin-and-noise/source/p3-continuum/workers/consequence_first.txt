Independent assessment

I pursued transport first, then a concrete way to acquire local coherence from ordinary computational-basis snapshots. Neither currently supports a major end-to-end computational advance. The transport construction reaches an inverse-problem bottleneck that the sparse-observable receiver does not solve. The snapshot construction yields a precise positive theorem, a simple useful amplitude-ratio lemma, and strong acquisition lower bounds. Its missing premise is identifiable: sufficiently local or efficiently learnable conditional odds of the Born distribution.

I also found a decisive comparator for the warm-main construction: Crosson–Slezak already explicitly implement their rapidly mixing high-temperature worldline method in continuous time, with runtime independent of the Trotter number. The new wrapper must therefore earn its significance through a stronger acquired conditional-source contract or a larger certified parameter regime.

No local execution environment was available, so I could not save notes or run numerical checks. Everything below is analytic, with primary-source checks where stated. Repository reads were pinned to commit 93f9f92a6dcdf33d3f13b5ea11c0447c2b5f67a6; there were no external writes.
