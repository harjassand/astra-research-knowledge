Result: a fixed physical grid can support the exact continuum target

I obtained a transfer theorem from an acquired coarse imaginary-time path sampler to the continuum conditional law. The proposal grid depends on the Hamiltonian, temperature and structural certificate, but does not grow with the requested output accuracy.

The mechanism is explicit: slightly increase the proposal’s spin-flip rates, reconstruct continuous paths between its grid points, and accept them with a computable correction. The acceptance probability is bounded below uniformly over the endpoint filters and prefixes required by the state compiler.

This changes the complete composition with the conditional-sample prediction theorem. Once the coarse source’s acquisition and sampling costs are paid, physical time discretization need no longer add an inverse-accuracy factor to the receiver’s
