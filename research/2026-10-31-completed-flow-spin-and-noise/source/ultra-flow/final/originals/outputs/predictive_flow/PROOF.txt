PREDICTIVE FLOWS AND THE LEAST HIDDEN IRREVERSIBILITY
Solo construction, 31 October 2026.

1. CONTRACT AND CONSEQUENCE

The objects here are stationary DISCRETE-TIME Markov chains on arbitrary
standard-Borel state spaces, with a finite-alphabet deterministic observation
at each time. Stochastic memoryless emissions are included by adjoining an
independent emission seed. Reducible and periodic chains are allowed. Physical
time reversal is the ordinary all-even reversal, with no hidden parity map.

For stationary flow Gamma(dx,dy)=pi(dx)P(x,dy), write Gamma^T for its image
under (x,y)->(y,x). The cost is

                 e(P)=D(Gamma || Gamma^T), in nats/step.       (1)

This is path irreversibility of the Markov chain. It is not a claim about the
heat of a device without a thermodynamic model. A sampled stationary all-even
CTMC satisfies e(P_Delta)<=Delta*sigma_CT by path-space data processing, so
the bounds below also give sigma_CT>=bound/Delta. Attainment by a discrete
chain does not establish CTMC embeddability or sharpness within CTMCs.

Given the EXACT law p of a block of 2L+1 observed symbols, the problem is the
least (1) over all such hidden chains reproducing that block. No hidden-state
count, rate bound, spectral gap, finite predictive rank, preparation device,
or finite-data confidence interval is supplied as an extra premise.

Theorem. This unrestricted problem is exactly a convex minimization of
relative entropy over probability measures on a fixed COMPACT space of
forward/backward predictive probabilities and their stationary flow. Its
minimum is attained in a standard-Borel hidden model. Finite convex programs
constructed in Section 5 are valid lower bounds and converge to the minimum.

The fixed space is indexed by the OBSERVED alphabet and word length, not an
assumed number of microscopic states. This changes the inverse problem from
nonconvex fitting at a chosen hidden size to a complete, size-free convex
formulation. Its discretizations can be enormous. No polynomial runtime,
useful general statistical rate, or hardware capability is asserted.

2. THE COMPACT PREDICTIVE-FLOW CONSTRUCTION

Let A be the alphabet, s=|A|, W_L the nonempty words of length <=L, and
m=|W_L|=sum_(j=1)^L s^j. Define the compact disjoint union

               K_L=A x [0,1]^m x [0,1]^m.

A point z=(a,r^+,r^-) has a present label a and proposed forward/backward
word probabilities. Set r^+_empty=r^-_empty=1. The coordinate r^-_u uses
the order moving AWAY into the past: u_1 is the previous symbol, u_2 the
one before it. Thus its corresponding chronological past word is u^R.

The variable is a probability measure Gamma on K_L x K_L with equal first
and second marginals mu. Require, for each b in A, |w|<L and every real
continuous test function f on K_L,

 int f(z)[1_{a(z')=b} r^+_w(z')-r^+_{bw}(z)] dGamma=0,       (2)
 int f(z')[1_{a(z)=b} r^-_w(z)-r^-_{bw}(z')] dGamma=0.        (3)

These are LINEAR constraints on the measure, including all conditioning
tests. Keeping only the unconditional averages in (2)-(3) would be wrong.

The data constraints, also linear, are

 int 1_{a(z)=a} r^-_u(z)r^+_v(z) dmu
                           =p(u^R a v), |u|,|v|<=L.        (4)

All right sides are central marginals of the supplied block law. There is
no pointwise product of unknown measures in (4). The integrand is a fixed
continuous polynomial of the coordinate variables. Call this convex
weakly closed feasible set C_L(p).

3. EXACT REALIZATION, NOT A MOMENT RELAXATION

From a hidden chain X, let Z(X) consist of its present observed label and
its true conditional forward and backward word probabilities to length L.
Regular conditional kernels exist on standard-Borel spaces. Push pi and
pi P through Z and Z x Z. The Markov property and its stationary backward
version give (2)-(3), after conditioning on Z. Past and future are
independent given X_0, so (4) holds. Data processing gives

        D((Z x Z)_*Gamma_X || ((Z x Z)_*Gamma_X)^T)<=e(P).

Conversely take ANY Gamma in C_L(p). Disintegrate it as
Gamma(dz,dz')=mu(dz)T(z,dz'). Equal marginals make T stationary; its
stationary backward kernel is obtained from Gamma^T. Equations (2)-(3),
with every continuous test f, identify the corresponding conditional
expectations mu-almost everywhere. Induct on word length, starting at
r_empty=1: r^+_w and r^-_w are precisely the true conditional forward
and backward word probabilities for T and the deterministic label a(z).
Past/future conditional independence in this ACTUAL chain now gives (4),
so it realizes the entire supplied block. Its cost is exactly
D(Gamma||Gamma^T).

Consequently, writing E_L(p) for the original all-hidden-model infimum,

          E_L(p)=min_(Gamma in C_L(p)) D(Gamma||Gamma^T).     (5)

K_L x K_L is compact metric. Stationarity, (2)-(4), and normalization
are weakly closed, since labels have the discrete topology and all
integrands are continuous. Relative entropy is jointly weakly lower
semicontinuous; transpose is continuous. Thus C_L(p) is compact and
(5) is attained whenever it is nonempty, allowing the value +infinity.
An inconsistent supplied block has empty feasible set, detected separately.

The entropy objective is convex because relative entropy is jointly convex
and Gamma->Gamma^T is linear. Mixtures of hidden models are permitted;
ergodicity or irreducibility would change this convex contract.

In particular E_L(p)=0 EXACTLY when p has a stationary reversible hidden
realization. The set of such block laws is compact and convex. A block
outside that set admits a strict bounded LINEAR word statistic separating
it from every reversible hidden model, with no hidden-cardinality bound.
This existence statement does not supply a useful numerical margin.

4. COMPLETE OBSERVED LAWS AND THE FINITE-STATE DISTINCTION

For a stationary observed process P let p_L be its length-(2L+1) law.
The same construction on

 K_infinity=A x [0,1]^(all finite nonempty words) x [0,1]^(same)

uses a compact metrizable countable product. Require (2)-(4) for every
finite word. Realization and entropy data processing hold exactly as
above. Nested compact sublevel sets then prove

 min_{all standard-Borel hidden models of COMPLETE P} e
                         =sup_L E_L(p_L).                  (6)

For clarity, finite-level optimizers can be embedded by filling unused
coordinates with zero. All finite-level constraints are retained and
the embedding is injective, so it preserves relative entropy. If the
supremum in (6) is finite, compactness of the nested feasible cost
sublevels supplies an attaining infinite-coordinate flow. If it is
infinite, every full realization has infinite cost. A full stationary
process always has a deterministic-shift Markov realization on its
two-sided path space, though its cost can be infinite.

This is an unpriced STANDARD-BOREL minimum. It must not be renamed an
attained minimum over exact FINITE-state models. That class can have
different closure/attainment questions. What follows for finite models
is the robust observational identity

 E_L(p)=lim_(epsilon down 0) inf{e(Q):Q finite-state,
                             ||p_L(Q)-p||_1<=epsilon}.      (7)

To see the <= direction, push any approximate finite models through the
compact predictive coordinates and take a bounded-cost subsequence.
For the reverse direction quantize a minimizer of (5) as in Section 5.
The resulting finite chain has no greater cost and reproduces the
block to an error tending to zero. No exact-law finite realization is
manufactured from this approximation.

5. CONVERGENT FINITE CONVEX PROGRAMS WITH EXACT DATA MOMENTS

Choose integer k>=1, mesh h=1/k, and grid

 G_k=A x {0,h,...,1}^m x {0,h,...,1}^m.

Its size is N=s(k+1)^(2m). The variable is a nonnegative N by N flow J
with row sums and column sums equal to mu, and total mass one. Minimize

                       sum_ij J_ij log(J_ij/J_ji).          (8)

Use the usual relative-entropy conventions at zero. Impose EXACT data
moments (4) on the grid. For each grid row i, b and |w|<L impose

 |sum_{j:a(j)=b} J_ij r^+_w(j)-mu_i r^+_{bw}(i)|<=h mu_i,   (9)
 |sum_{j:a(j)=b} J_ji r^-_w(j)-mu_i r^-_{bw}(i)|<=h mu_i.    (10)

These are linear inequalities; (8) is convex and can use exponential
cones. Thus each level is a genuine finite convex program. Let v_k be
its value. Then

                0<=v_k<=E_L(p),     lim_k v_k=E_L(p).      (11)

The exact moments in (4) are possible because the quantizer is UNBIASED.
Round each coordinate independently to its two neighboring grid points
with the unique probabilities giving its exact original mean. Round the
forward and backward groups independently conditional on z; preserve a.
Call this Markov quantizer R_k. Applying R_k independently at both ends
of Gamma gives J. Its marginals agree and data processing decreases
relative entropy. Conditional independence of the two coordinate groups
preserves r^-_u r^+_v in expectation EXACTLY, even when u=v.

For (9), averaging the NEXT rounded coordinate restores its original
value exactly. Equation (2) can be tested against the current-cell
weight R_k(z,i), a continuous piecewise multilinear function on each
label component. The only remaining error is replacing r^+_{bw}(z)
by its rounded grid value, at distance <=h whenever R_k(z,i)>0.
This gives h mu_i, and (3) gives (10). Consequently quantized exact
models are feasible at every grid level, proving the lower-bound side
of (11). Using nearest-point rounding on both groups would generally
lose the exact cross-moment property.

For convergence, embed optimal or almost optimal grid flows in the
same compact space K_L x K_L. Along a weakly convergent subsequence,
stationarity and (4) pass to the limit. Multiply (9) by any bounded
continuous f at the current grid point and sum rows. Its integrated
residual is at most h||f||_infinity, which tends to zero. This proves
(2) in the limit; (10) proves (3). Entropy lower semicontinuity yields
liminf v_k>=E_L(p), proving (11), including divergence to infinity.
The values need not be monotone on these grids; their running maximum
is also a valid lower bound and converges.

Every feasible grid flow itself defines a finite hidden chain. Equations
(9)-(10) imply by induction that its conditional word probabilities
are within |w|h of their grid coordinates. Its actual probability of
each full length-(2L+1) word is therefore within 2Lh of p. Its entire
block L1 discrepancy is at most 2L s^(2L+1)h. This proves the constructive
finite-model approximation used in (7). Approximate row consistency
must not be passed off as exact data reproduction by the grid chain.

5A. A COMPLETE LP HIERARCHY FOR EQUILIBRIUM WORD CERTIFICATES

Add J=J^T to each finite grid program and ask only for feasibility.
Every true reversible hidden model quantizes to a feasible symmetric
grid flow, with the exact data moments and the same row tolerance h.
Conversely, if symmetric grid flows exist along an unbounded sequence
of mesh denominators, compactness and (9)-(10) give an exact symmetric
flow in C_L(p). Thus

 p has NO reversible hidden realization if and only if some finite
 symmetric-grid LINEAR program is infeasible.                (11a)

This is a complete semidecision for incompatibility, not a guaranteed
finite stopping rule for equilibrium-compatible inputs. It uses no
unknown bound on hidden cardinality or finite predictive rank.
Completeness refers to the exact rational LP hierarchy. The current
finite-precision proposal driver may miss a small margin; its failure
to certify is never an equilibrium conclusion. Exact rational LP
algorithms exist independently of this numerical convenience.

For rational input p, an infeasible rational grid LP has a rational
Farkas certificate. Equivalently minimize the maximum absolute error
of its word-moment equations. A positive optimum has a rational dual
that is a bounded linear word statistic with nonnegative expectation
under EVERY reversible hidden model and negative expectation on p.
The dual is an observable certificate because all actual models have
quantized feasible outer flows, not because a finite grid is assumed
to contain their exact predictive states.

The implementation work/predictive_flow_lp.py constructs this LP for
the binary triple control. It rationalizes the proposed dual, enforces
its sign and soft-error-column constraints, and adjusts its single
normalization multiplier to check EVERY flow column exactly. The
separate standard-library receiver repeats those rational inequalities.
No floating solver success or locally fitted model supplies validity.

For the control below the k=8 program has 162 predictive grid states
and 13,203 symmetric-flow columns. Its rational dual gap is 3/128.
The compiled fixed word statistic has a rigorous sufficient iid
validation budget of 1,258 stationary windows, with each error below
0.025. This is a synthetic control, not an acquired physical sample.
That is a held-out budget for a frozen statistic constructed from the
known fixture, not the cost of learning an unknown law from those same
1,258 windows. The finite-count workflow in Section 7 is separate.
An equally informed ordinary reflection statistic needs only 512
windows under the same conservative calculation. There is NO sampling
advantage on this control. The compiler's general all-hidden guarantee
and completeness, rather than an artificial weak comparator, are the
substantive construction being tested.

5B. A SMALLER PREFIX-CONSISTENT SIMPLEX GRID

The cube grid is not necessary. All the shorter word coordinates are
prefix marginals of a distribution q on the s^L words of length L.
Use instead the count simplex {n/k:n in N^(s^L), sum n=k}, independently
for the forward and backward groups. The state count becomes

                N=s*binom(k+s^L-1,s^L-1)^2.               (11b)

This includes the binary L=1 specialization already used above: one
scalar probability per group, the other being its complement.

There is an unbiased quantizer with error <=1/k for EVERY prefix
marginal, so (9)-(11) and the LP completeness proof remain valid.
Order the leaf words lexicographically. For a single U uniform on [0,1),
put C_j=k sum_(i<=j)q_i and

                  n_j=floor(C_j+U)-floor(C_(j-1)+U).

The counts are nonnegative, sum to k, and E[n_j/k]=q_j. Every prefix
is a contiguous interval of this ordering. Its count error is the
difference of two fractional parts, so its probability error is <1/k.
Use INDEPENDENT U's for the two groups to preserve every data cross
moment exactly. The output probabilities of this quantizer depend
continuously on q: a small perturbation changes the finite floor
pattern only on a set of U's of vanishing measure. Thus its cell
weights are admissible continuous tests in (2)-(3).

This is systematic-resampling mathematics, not a claim to invent
resampling. Its role here is to preserve all observed cross-moments
while uniformly controlling every predictive prefix. The simplex-grid
LP uses only full-length data moments: all shorter equations follow
by prefix normalization and marginalization. The receiver checks this
construction with length-two future/past distributions and actual
five-word laws, for both an irreversible source and a reversible
hidden-memory null.

6. AN EXACT SHARP CONTROL: REVERSIBLE RECORD, POSITIVE MINIMUM COST

Let a stationary hidden chain have a binary observed f in {-1,+1}.
Put C_j=E[f(Y_0)f(Y_j)] and

                  b=sqrt(max(0,C_1^2-C_2)).

Then every such hidden model obeys

             e >= 2 b artanh(b),                          (12)

with the continuous value 0 at b=0 and +infinity at b=1.

Proof. At the current hidden state write u=Pf, v=P^*f,
x=(u+v)/2, d=(u-v)/2. Stationarity gives

 C_2=<v,u>=E[x^2-d^2],  C_1=<f,x>,  E[d^2]>=C_1^2-C_2.

Apply entropy data processing to the next binary observation, in BOTH
orientations of the stationary flow. The two full-flow relative
entropies agree by transposition, so their averaged coarse-grained
bound is valid. The half-Jeffreys divergence of the two Bernoulli
laws with means x+d and x-d is

 j(x,d)=(d/2)log(((1+d)^2-x^2)/((1-d)^2-x^2))
                        >=2|d|artanh|d|.

The inequality follows by differentiating in x^2; the expression is
increasing there. The function phi(t)=2sqrt(t)artanh(sqrt(t)) has
nonnegative power series 2 sum_(n>=1) t^n/(2n-1), so it is increasing
and convex on [0,1). Jensen gives (12). Extended boundary cases follow
by monotone limits. This uses no assumption on hidden dimension.

For EVERY 0<b<1, (12) is the EXACT minimum for a particular binary
three-word law and also for a complete time-reversal-invariant record.
Use four states (s,t) in {-1,+1}^2, with stationary mass 1/4 and
observed f=s. From (s,t), move to

 (s,-t) with probability (1+b s t)/2,
 (-s,t) with probability (1-b s t)/2.                     (13)

The matrix is doubly stochastic. It has Pf=b t, P^*f=-b t, so C_1=0,
C_2=-b^2. Its cost is exactly

                 b log((1+b)/(1-b))=2b artanh b.

The involution theta(s,t)=(s,-t) preserves the observed label and
conjugates the transition matrix to its transpose. Applying theta to
a reversed hidden path proves that EVERY observed word has exactly
its reverse's probability. Hence ordinary observed path-reversal KL
is zero at every word length. The positive minimum follows already
from the three-word law and is attained by (13). It is not inferred
from a finite search. The matrix (13) is periodic and is not asserted
to embed in a continuous-time chain.

At b=1/2 the eight three-word probabilities are

 p(i,a,j)=[1-(1/4)f(i)f(j)]/8,

and the exact least cost is (log 3)/2, about 0.549306 nats/step.
The receiver checks all rational matrix identities, the predictive
flow constraints, and unbiased grid quantizations at several meshes.
The analytic argument proves the universal bound and all-word reversal;
finite checks are not substituted for those proofs.

The control fails ordinary reflection PSD because C_2<0. It is NOT
an improvement of N507/N541's example that passes ALL reflection CP
tests. For that harder source, Sections 2-5 provide a complete convex
route, but no useful grid level, numerical margin or acquisition
advantage has yet been established.

Two exact negative controls retain the critical interfaces. Keeping
only unconditional versions of (2)-(3) admits a zero-entropy diagonal
flow on the control's predictive points with all the right data cross-
moments; its proposed coordinates are not its actual predictions.
Likewise, using a shared rounding seed for the forward and backward
groups biases their cross-moment even when their original coordinates
are equal. A separate reversible three-state hidden-memory null is
accepted by the exact compiled statistic.

7. EVIDENCE, INFORMATION AND THE OPPORTUNITY DECISION

The input p is a block distribution. Real data require a simultaneous
confidence region for it; substitute that convex region for equality
in (4) to obtain a conservative inference problem. Independent
stationary windows give multinomial confidence sets. Arbitrarily
dependent windows from one trajectory do NOT acquire such a guarantee
without mixing or other justified information. Unknown physical
parities and observation memory are also outside the contract.

There is an explicit version using only iid word COUNTS, so an exact
law oracle is not required to use the rejection compiler. For N
independent stationary windows and M=s^(2L+1) word categories let p_hat
be their frequencies. Simultaneous Hoeffding bounds give

 Pr{||p_hat-p||_infinity<=sqrt(log(2M/alpha)/(2N))}>=1-alpha.

A grid-LP dual written d(p)=z+c.p with ||c||_1<=1 satisfies d(q)<=0
for every reversible hidden q. Reject only if its certified d(p_hat)
exceeds ||c||_1 times this simultaneous radius. This remains valid
when the dual and grid level were SELECTED USING THE SAME COUNTS:
on the simultaneous event, every such valid inequality is safe.
Using a selected statistic with an unadjusted fixed-statistic error
bar on its training counts would not have this guarantee. Searching
different word lengths requires corresponding simultaneous coverage.

work/predictive_flow_counts.py implements this finite-count contract
and a matched ordinary-reflection comparator. Its data are synthetic
iid stationary triples, with preparations, letters and transitions
counted. The compiler sees the empirical probabilities only. It does
not receive the generating hidden transition matrix. Exact rational
dual verification and a rational squared-radius comparison determine
rejection; the two finite simulations do not establish coverage.
Coverage is supplied by the probability argument above.

In the retained run both cases used 20,000 independent stationary
preparations, 60,000 observed letters and 40,000 simulated transitions.
For the irreversible source the empirical certified gap is exactly
367/16000, the dual data norm is one, and the conservative simultaneous
squared radius is 1/4000. Their rational comparison rejects equilibrium.
The reversible hidden-memory null is not rejected. The ordinary
reflection comparator reaches the same two decisions; there is no
claimed computational or statistical advantage over it. These are
finite synthetic controls of a general theorem and compiler.

The cube bound s(k+1)^(2m), with m=sum s^j, is enormous. The smaller
implemented simplex bound (11b) is still large at increasing L;
there are N^2 flow entries before symmetry or sparsity. This is a
complete computational construction, not a claim of efficient general
inference. Any low-rank, geometric, sparse or adaptive implementation
must retain the unconditional lower-bound/realization obligations.

The preserved quartic/Hessian route has an important exact consequence:
an actual negative conic-ROC Hessian quartic would construct a full
two-by-two Morrey separation via the coupling-preserving extension.
Its central missing input remains that ACTUAL negative field; the
completed boundary theorems do not provide one.

Two materially different first attacks were made before choosing this
flow construction. A nonlinear-hazard coreset route to compressed
Bernoulli-polynomial noise was derived, but Astra N753 already supplies
a stronger affine-pair framework. It does not justify a newly enabled
sampling claim. The derivation is preserved in work/bernoulli_hazard_note.txt.
For the N541 seed, minimum-L2 unbiased estimators from one through
seven future letters improved conditional feature cost only from
10.91096768 to 7.63124376. That floating diagnostic, in
work/equilibrium_feature_probe.py/json, does not remove its enormous
certified sampling barrier and proves no universal limit on improvement.

The flow route was selected because its central construction can be
completed without supplying a hidden architecture, and it gives an
exact variational value, actual realizers, and convergent convex lower
programs. It has NOT yet made the difficult N507 high-order test
practical. The sharp binary control tests the construction at its
actual, easier scope.
