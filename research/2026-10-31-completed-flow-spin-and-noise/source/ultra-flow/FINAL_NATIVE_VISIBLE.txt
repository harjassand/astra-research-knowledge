I reopened selection and chose **inferring hidden irreversibility without fixing the hidden architecture**.

The quartic route retains its constructive implication for Morrey’s problem, but still needs an actual negative Hessian field. My compressed-noise attack encountered stronger existing N753 machinery. Longer-record feature optimization also failed to remove N541’s enormous sampling barrier.

The resulting construction replaces hidden-model fitting with a convex problem. For an observed block law \(p\), I derived
\[
E_L(p)=\min_{\Gamma\in\mathcal C_L(p)}
D(\Gamma\|\Gamma^\top),
\]
where \(\Gamma\) is a stationary flow between forward and backward predictive probabilities. The domain depends on the observed alphabet and word length; no hidden-state count is supplied.

The [written proof](producer-workspace/PROOF.txt) establishes:

- **Attainment** over standard-Borel hidden Markov models, including a corresponding minimum for a complete observed process.
- **Convergent finite convex approximations.** At zero cost, these become a complete LP hierarchy for excluding reversible hidden realizations.
- **Observable certificates from counts.** Rational LP duals produce word statistics valid for every hidden architecture. Simultaneous confidence bounds permit choosing the statistic using the same counts.

The [compiler](producer-workspace/compile_lp.py) and [exact receiver](producer-workspace/receive.py) are implemented. Exact verification checked all **13,203 flow columns** of a compiled certificate.

A sharp control has a completely time-symmetric observed record yet forces minimum hidden cost
\[
\tfrac12\log 3\approx0.549306
\]
nats per discrete step, attained by an explicit four-state chain. A separate workflow received only counts from 20,000 simulated stationary windows: it rejected this source and did not reject a reversible hidden-memory null.

The ordinary reflection comparator reached the same decisions and needs fewer validation windows on this control—**512 versus 1,258**. No sampling advantage is established. Existing inference from time-symmetric statistics is already substantive prior art, including [Skinner–Dunkel](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8106300/).

The strongest result here is the general convex realization and certificate construction. Its grids remain large, and it has not made N507’s harder example practical. Historical novelty remains unestablished. The attainment theorem concerns discrete models on standard-Borel spaces; exact finite-state and continuous-time realization questions retain their separate scope.
