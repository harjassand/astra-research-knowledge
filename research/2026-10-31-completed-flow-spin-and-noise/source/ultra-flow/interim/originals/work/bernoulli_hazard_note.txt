An alternative compressed-noise mechanism, not a new sampling capability claim
31 October 2026, solo derivation during opportunity selection.

For a once-per-slot noise law (1-p)delta_0+p Law(F(U)), 0<=p<=1/2,
set alpha=2p. Exactly the same noise is obtained by proposing F(U)
with probability alpha and then adding it with an unexposed fair bit.
Conditional on proposed marks, the endpoint is uniform on their span.
Process only the first proposed mark outside the current span W, then
continue on the independent unprocessed suffix. There are at most D
rank increases. Original marks, not summary marks, must be sampled.

A slot's probability of no active proposal is 1-alpha q(W), where
q(W)=Pr(F(U) outside W). Thus its first-active survival hazard is
g(W)=-log(1-alpha q(W)), additive across independent occurrences.
This nonlinear hazard is NOT an additive parity-cut intensity.

Cap each slot hazard at B>=1. Coupling original and capped first-active
scans across any number of occurrences has error at most
exp(-B)/(1-exp(-B)): every capped slot is active with probability at
least 1-exp(-B), so repeated missed capped slots form a geometric tail.
At most D+1 scans make total error <=2(D+1)exp(-B). B logarithmic in
D/epsilon suffices, including alpha=1 and q=1.

The capped function obeys alpha q <= min(g,B) <=2B alpha q.
For an explicit list of typed mark laws with positive weights w_i,
peel groups by descending w_i alpha_i, retaining a group in a round
when its coefficient span adds a new direction. Each round selects
at most D groups. A group in round k crossing W has, in each earlier
round, a crossing group of at least its weight. For degree d every
nonzero outside probability is at least 2^-d. Consequently each
group's contribution is at most 2^(d+1)B/k times the total hazard.
Bernstein sampling with retention min(1,A/k), and a union bound over
at most 2^(D^2) subspaces, gives a polynomial uniform hazard coreset.
Pushing a group through a linear suffix replaces W by its preimage;
the same simultaneous guarantee covers every linear context.

Uniform latent samples estimate all leaf outside probabilities. The
capped hazard has logarithmic sensitivity at most exp(B), which is
polynomial after the above cap. Use fresh latent rejection samples
from the ORIGINAL polynomial law for an actual outside mark; its
acceptance probability is at least 2^-d. Merge/reduce hazard groups,
conditional first-active grammar descent, and a suffix stack then
give an alternative approximate endpoint construction. Logs, retries,
bit precision, preprocessing failure and all conditioning errors must
be charged. No complete implementation or practical advantage was
established for this alternative in this turn.

The selection stopped here after reading Astra N753's actual proof:
updates/ER/package/research_results_bundle/controlled_noise/
compressed_discrete_noise_affine_span_sampler.md. Its affine-pair
identity J+B(J+J')=d J, with the essential OFFSET update, already
gives exact expected-polynomial sampling for bounded-degree slots,
plus categorical/sparse-polynomial extensions and rank-budget
Poissonization. Therefore the present hazard derivation cannot be
promoted to a new compressed-noise capability merely by changing
the summary or avoiding a Poisson approximation. No novelty or
competitive advantage is asserted.
