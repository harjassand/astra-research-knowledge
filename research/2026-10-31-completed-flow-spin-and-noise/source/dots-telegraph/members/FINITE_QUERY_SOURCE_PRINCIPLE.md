# A finite-query source principle for retained stochastic memory

Working theorem, 31 October 2026. This principle uses standard limited-independence quantum-query reasoning; no novelty is claimed for that ingredient. Its application to the fully priced telegraph source is under construction.

## Proposition
Let a source be defined by a finite table f:D->R of independent uniform finite words. Let a fixed, finite quantum circuit C_f access this table only by the standard reversible value oracle and its inverse at most q times in total. Arbitrary intervening channels, measurements, retained records and external references are permitted, provided they do not access the private implementation of f by another route. The average visible output channel is identical if the uniform table is replaced by any exactly 2q-wise independent family of functions on D.

Proof: write each oracle matrix element as a sum of indicators 1[f(x)=y] multiplying f-independent basis transitions. Every ket amplitude after q calls has polynomial degree at most q in those indicators, and every density-matrix element after ket/bra multiplication has degree at most 2q. A term refers to at most 2q distinct table addresses; repeated inconsistent indicators make that term zero. Its expectation is determined entirely by the joint law at those addresses. The two source ensembles therefore have equal output density matrices for every input, including an arbitrary reference. Purify or defer intermediate instruments to include adaptation and records. Tracing private registers preserves equality. The argument counts oracle calls, not gates, paths, or classical evaluation addresses encountered in a single branch.

## Constructive finite family
Encode D injectively in a finite field F of characteristic two and encode each random word as a fixed set of output bits. Choose a polynomial of degree at most 2q-1 with independent uniform field coefficients. Its evaluations at any 2q distinct field points are uniform by invertibility of the Vandermonde matrix. If the output word is narrower than the field, project to its needed bits. Choose the field width at least the address width and random-word width. Horner evaluation uses O(q) field operations per query; storing the coefficients uses O(q) field words. This cost must be included, so the result is a polynomial-cost compiler, not a constant-time pseudorandom oracle. Standard finite-field construction/arithmetic is an additional charged finite computation.

## Correct coupling order
Suppose the iid finite-table algorithm is coupled to an ideal continuous source with failure probability eta and path error at most d on the complement. Suppose the downstream experiment has uniform path-continuity modulus Ld. Then its finite-table output differs from the ideal averaged output by at most 2eta+Ld in trace norm, plus downstream synthesis errors. Replace the finite iid table by the limited-independent table using the proposition, with zero additional output error.

It is generally false that the limited-independent family's entire latent path has the ideal source law, or that a union bound controlling all its nodes follows from the iid argument. The result is equivalence for the declared finite-query consumer. The private seed may be retained across all its interventions, preserving the same observable correlations within the compiled horizon/budget. Unbounded reuse, exposure of the seed, or new oracle routes invalidate the stated guarantee.

## Source accounting for the telegraph proposal
Conditional on a separately sampled private Poisson flip count N, a node address identifies one splitting variable of the two Dirichlet trees. Pack all capped sampler random bits needed at that node into its random word. A reversible node evaluation is then a deterministic finite map of that word and its integer shape parameters. Repeated calls return exactly the same split. Variable coherent addresses and binary search do not defeat the proposition; they do increase q. Uncomputation is counted.

For fixed N<=Nmax, the conceptual full iid table contains O(Nmax) nodes. A coupling failure of eta/O(Nmax) per node controls the whole iid tree without generating it. A constructive sampler whose bit cost is polynomial in log Nmax and log(Nmax/eta) therefore avoids a linear acquisition cost. This is where a claimed trajectory compression theorem must actually earn its access model, rather than assuming a free oracle.
