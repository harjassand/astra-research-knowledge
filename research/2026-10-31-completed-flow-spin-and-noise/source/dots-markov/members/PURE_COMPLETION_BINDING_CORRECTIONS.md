# Binding corrections and scope for the pure completion

31 October 2026. The independent review accepts the structural/process/activity theorem, not the proposed general compiler.

In the frozen source's Section 2, the displayed diagonal identity is C_xx=2lambda sum_(y!=x) q_xy, twice the exit rate. Its unrestricted row-generator sum would be zero and is not intended. The subsequent derivation uses the exit rate.

The source constant c is a certified lower bound for C0 on the orthogonal complement of psi. Equivalently C0=-(S+S^dagger), S=diag(pi)^(-1/2) Q^T diag(pi)^(1/2); this is an additive-reversibilization/Dirichlet-form gap. It is not the possibly complex eigenvalue gap of a nonnormal Q.

The count-generating-function and tail bounds depend explicitly on c^-1 and pi_min^-1. They do not provide a pathwise rate bound or a thinning sampler. Stationary probabilities, state preparation, source matrices, gap certification and operator encodings must be supplied or acquired at their real costs. Pure-invariant quantum extensions and dark-state criteria have prior literature, so neither the property nor this internally checked construction has a certified novelty claim.

For the separate excursion-compiler candidate, b must be an available normalization for a precision-capable block encoding of K and its projected blocks. A numerical upper bound on ||K|| alone does not supply that encoding. A concrete admissible choice is the paid LCU sum of supplied Ai normalizations times the centered source-function sup norms. This is an implementation requirement, not a change to the activity theorem's B0 variance norm.

The same exit-rate convention also applies in Section 4 of the frozen source: the no-jump diagonal is -lambda sum_(y!=x) q_xy. Its displayed unrestricted sum_y shorthand must not be interpreted as the zero row sum of Q. This second location changes no intended derivation.
