# General explicit Markov noise: a complete source-conditioned compiler

Research checkpoint, 31 October 2026. The final receiving ledger and independent whole review govern the scope; original candidate headers and source hashes are preserved. This is constructive mathematical existence, not an implemented quantum compiler, practical speedup, or novelty certificate.

## New capability
The source may now be any known, explicitly described finite irreducible Markov chain, including correlated and nonreversible source dynamics. Its state selects bounded, possibly noncommuting system Hamiltonians. The construction preserves the full controlled system process while avoiding a number of simulation operations proportional to the source's global rapid-switching multiplier.

This broader statement has real source costs. Stationary probabilities and their preparation, a certified additive-reversibilization gap, explicit reservoir matrices and precision-capable system encodings must be supplied or acquired. Poor conditioning can make the algorithm expensive. It does not efficiently simulate an unknown or succinct exponentially large source merely because that source is called Markovian.

## Exact model and resource statement
Let the classical source have generator lambda Q, with known stationary pi. Center its source-dependent system coupling A(z) under pi; any deterministic mean is included in H0 and its physical cost remains charged. The Hamiltonian is H0+sqrt(lambda)A(z(t)). The reservoir begins in the stated stationary representation once, and is retained across prescribed system instruments and all private event branches.

With accessible encoding normalization b for the centered coupling, certified source gap c, and the source matrices D0,C0 defined in the proofs, set

mu=lambda*c*T, B=b^2*T/c, R=tr(C0)/c,
D=||D0||/c, Pi=1/min(pi), H=alpha0*T.

Simulator overhead is polynomial in explicit source dimension d, 1+H+B+p, R,D,Pi, input/register lengths, log(2+mu), and log(1/epsilon). Encoding/acquisition costs are paid. Arbitrary consumer instruments are external operations, or their actual implementation costs are added; bounding their number p does not bound their gates.

Accuracy is unconditional output trace norm, uniformly over reference-entangled inputs and adaptive system operations at the prescribed slots, with external records retained. Private reservoir states, event times and jump labels are not available to the consumer. Arbitrary adaptive times, public microscopic paths and normalized rare postselection are outside scope.

## What was constructed
1. An exact pure stationary quantum completion of the classical source, preserving its entire commuting observable algebra and therefore the controlled system process.
2. A source-conditioned jump-count bound independent of lambda. This is not a pathwise intensity/thinning claim.
3. An exact ground/fast-sector expansion, with positive two-state bridges and explicit Poisson self-insertions retaining the noncommuting operator order.
4. Normalization-one access to finite reservoir contractions, through charged dissipative matrix arithmetic and finite unitary dilation/synthesis. No system-sized matrix exponential or free Kraus oracle is assumed.
5. A complete first-event isometry with an integrable normalizer, finite-bit source generation, amplification, actual retained jump outputs and a count-capped private-time error ledger.

## Comparison and remaining limits
Quantum extensions preserving classical observable algebras and pure invariant states have prior literature. Doob transforms, dark-state criteria, matrix majorants, classical finite arithmetic and amplitude amplification are credited in the reviews. The low-rate branch uses an ordinary general Lindblad algorithm with its normalization and encoding costs charged. No absence of an equivalent prior complete theorem has been established.

This explicit-state theorem does not quantitatively supersede the earlier factorized many-telegraph theorem: listing all states of a product source could be exponentially larger, and Pi may be costly. Keep the structured and explicit-source results distinct. The earlier practical audit likewise remains: a polynomial existence theorem alone does not earn a useful hardware crossover.
