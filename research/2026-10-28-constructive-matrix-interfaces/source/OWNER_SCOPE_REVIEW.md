relevant ranks in only three ways: (ell,rho), (ell+1,rho), (ell+1,rho+1).

in E. The summand is (f0-f1)1_K+(f1-f2)1_L+f2 1_E. Each signed affine mass is
zero or a signed power of two, obtained by elimination. Consequently only
the original 2^c characters are summed, with no exponential prefix factor.
