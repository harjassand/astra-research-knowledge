\begin{abstract}
The recovered chain-of-diamonds counter admits every affine phase for two
matrix factors but requires their nonzero target carriers to coincide.
We remove that equality restriction. Two arbitrary target carriers and
their phase subcarriers form two flags of length two. Adjoining a candidate
image-containing subspace gives the finite $E_6$ subspace problem. An
explicit catalogue of 29 injective types, fixed Hom dimensions, and exact
orbit--stabilizer weights compute every required intersection census.
Production enumerates neither field elements nor matrices nor subspaces.
The classical catalogue works over every $\F_{2^a}$ using zero-one
representatives; no new counting oracle is assumed. All raw algebra,
one-copy, and ordinary-circuit syntax hypotheses remain. This is a
polynomial counting extension, not a sampler or an unrestricted theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Retained acquisition and precise scope}
Let $L$ be the explicit ordinary Boolean DAG length, with $n$ independent
inputs, $m$ frontier outputs, $G$ gates, and $N=n+m+G+2=O(L)$ after the
retained input-ancestry guard. Keep the exact quadratic propagation,
recognized reversible suffix, paired polar-adjoint split, and affine Hom
acquisition of the recovered proofs. These are checked properties of
actual coefficients. Internal gates are not independent inputs.

The acquired nucleus is $S\simeq M_p(\F_q)$, $q=2^a$,
$\dim_2U=\dim_2O=apd$. Original right inputs map bijectively to
$W\subseteq\Hom_S(U,O)$; evaluation uses its affine translate $T_0+W$.
The reverse trace-annihilator $\mathcal D$ has dimension $c$, and $T_0$
induces an actual binary character $\ell$. Retain the matrix certificate
\[
 L=L_1\oplus L_2,\quad L_i\simeq M_{s_i}(\F_q),\quad
 d=h_1s_1+h_2s_2,\quad c=a(s_1^2+s_2^2).
\]
Each factor's actual scalar action agrees with $S$; each $h_i$ is a
positive integer; $\dim_2\mathcal D_i=as_i^2$; and the common kernel
of $\mathcal D$ is zero. The recovered regular-module argument certifies
an $S$-linear isomorphism $D_0:O\to U$ with $\mathcal D=LD_0$.
No algorithm constructs or searches for $D_0$.

Convert the original target, including output constants, to $y\in O$;
inconsistent output dependencies return zero. Manufacture
\[
 E_i=\sum_{D\in\text{basis }\mathcal D_i}
 \operatorname{rowspace}_2(S\to U,\ s\mapsto sDy)\subseteq S^*.
\]
Retain the zero-or-one joint simple-copy test
$\dim_2\operatorname{span}_2\{sDy:s\in S,D\in\mathcal D_i\}
\in\{0,ap s_i\}$. In proof coordinates the nonzero target is
$C_i\otimes w_i$, $w_i\ne0$, with $C_i$ a $p$-by-$s_i$ matrix.
The carrier is $E_i=\im C_i$ of natural dimension $g_i=\rank C_i$.
Zero targets have $g_i=0$.

The retained affine-flag acquisition translates $\ell_i$ by actual left
multiplication, solves its annihilator $K_i$, and constructs $t_i,F_i$.
With that proof's explicit transpose convention, the variable is
$A_i\in M_{s_i}(\F_q)$, its phase is
$(-1)^{\operatorname{Tr}_{q/2}\operatorname{tr}(Q_iA_i)}$, and
\[
 t_i=\rank Q_i,\qquad F_i=C_i\ker Q_i\subseteq E_i,\qquad
 f_i=\dim F_i,\quad
 \max(0,g_i-t_i)\le f_i\le\min(g_i,s_i-t_i).
\]
All spaces are actual right $S$-submodules. Binary dimensions are divided
by $ap$ only after this module origin has been established.

The new result removes equality of the nonzero $E_i$. Set
$R=E_1+E_2$, $k=\dim R$. There are no additional nesting or equality
requirements. $D_0$ makes $k$ the original target rank. Natural dimensions
refer to the finite-field lattice corresponding to the actual $S$-module
under Morita equivalence; no such coordinates are supplied to the code.

\section{Local signed polynomials for unequal carriers}
For $J\le R$, put $j=\dim J$, $l_i=\dim(J\cap E_i)$,
$e_i=\dim(J\cap F_i)$. The condition $\im(C_iA_i)\subseteq J$
restricts every column to $C_i^{-1}(J\cap E_i)$, of dimension
$r_i=s_i-g_i+l_i$. Its intersection with $\ker Q_i$ has dimension
$s_i-t_i-f_i+e_i$, because $C_i|_{\ker Q_i}$ has kernel dimension
$s_i-t_i-f_i$ and image $F_i$. Thus the restricted character rank is
\[
 \tau_i=t_i-g_i+l_i+f_i-e_i.\tag{1}
\]
For all $r$-by-$s$ matrices with character rank $\tau$, signed rank
coefficients satisfy the unitriangular relations
\[
 \gb{r-\tau}{v}q^{sv}=\sum_{u=0}^{v}P_u\gb{r-u}{v-u}.
 \tag{2}
\]
Count pairs consisting of a matrix and a containing $v$-image subspace.
Character orthogonality leaves exactly the subspaces in the character
kernel, each with sum $q^{sv}$, proving (2). Set
$P_{s,r,\tau,h}(x)=\sum_uP_ux^{hu}$. At the five-dimensional statistic
$z=(j,l_1,e_1,l_2,e_2)$ the contained signed polynomial is
\[
 P_{s_1,r_1,\tau_1,h_1}(x)P_{s_2,r_2,\tau_2,h_2}(x).\tag{3}
\]
Negative coefficients are retained; they are not sampling probabilities.

\section{A fixed $E_6$ classification with explicit representatives}
The data are an injective representation of
\[
 J\longrightarrow R\longleftarrow E_1\longleftarrow F_1,
 \qquad R\longleftarrow E_2\longleftarrow F_2.\tag{4}
\]
The three arms have lengths $1,2,2$, so the graph is $E_6$.
Its 29 injective indecomposables have these prime-field representatives:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Eighteen thin types: $R=\langle e_1\rangle$; independently
$(E_i,F_i)=(0,0),(R,0),(R,R)$ and $J=0$ or $R$.
\item Nine plane types: $R=\langle e_1,e_2\rangle$,
$J=\langle e_1+e_2\rangle$. Independently choose
\[
 (E_1,F_1)=(\langle e_1\rangle,0),
 (\langle e_1\rangle,\langle e_1\rangle),
 (R,\langle e_1\rangle),
\]
and the three analogous choices for the second arm with $e_2$.
\item Two three-dimensional types: $R=\langle e_1,e_2,e_3\rangle$,
$E_1=\langle e_1,e_2\rangle$, $F_1=\langle e_1\rangle$,
$E_2=\langle e_2,e_3\rangle$, $F_2=\langle e_3\rangle$;
$J=\langle e_1+e_2+e_3\rangle$ or
$\langle e_1+e_2,e_2+e_3\rangle$.
\end{enumerate}
All maps are inclusions. Thin types have only scalar endomorphisms.
Plane types force preservation of three distinct lines, again giving
only scalars. In the last two types the base flags force a diagonal map
on $F_1$, $E_1\cap E_2$, and $F_2$; preserving $J$ equates the three
diagonal entries. Thus every endomorphism ring is exactly the base
field, including after algebraic closure.

\begin{lemma}
Every injective representation of (4) over every $\F_{2^a}$ is a unique
direct sum of the listed 29 types.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Over an algebraic closure, Gabriel's theorem identifies the
indecomposables with the 36 positive roots of $E_6$. The 29 dimension
vectors above are distinct. The other seven roots have central dimension
zero: the simple at $J$ and the three interval roots on each two-vertex
arm. Their evident zero-one representatives also have scalar
endomorphisms. Together these are all positive roots. Production checks
this fixed catalogue by simple reflections, generating the 72 roots and
comparing the 36 nonnegative vectors. Every representative is absolutely
indecomposable, proving completeness over the algebraic closure.

For descent, let $V$ be any representation over $\F_q$. Its algebraic
closure decomposes into a sum $M$ of these prime-field representatives.
The isomorphism uses only finitely many algebraic coefficients, so is
defined over a finite extension of degree $r$. Forgetting that extension
gives $V^{\oplus r}\simeq M^{\oplus r}$ over $\F_q$. Krull--Schmidt
over $\F_q$ yields $V\simeq M$. This elementary Noether--Deuring argument
introduces no descent search. Injectivity of arrows holds on every
summand, excluding the seven zero-central types; Krull--Schmidt gives
uniqueness.
\end{proof}
The same list is classical in the $(2,3,3)$ finite multiple-flag problem
of Magyar--Weyman--Zelevinsky. We claim its new counting assembly here,
not a new classification. No $q^C$ enumeration is used to construct it.

\section{Actual base geometry and orbit weights}
Put $(V_0,V_1,V_2,V_3)=(0,F_1,E_1,R)$ and
$(W_0,W_1,W_2,W_3)=(0,F_2,E_2,R)$. Acquire
\[
 b_{uv}=\dim(V_u\cap W_v)-\dim(V_{u-1}\cap W_v)
 -\dim(V_u\cap W_{v-1})+\dim(V_{u-1}\cap W_{v-1}).\tag{5}
\]
These nine nonnegative simultaneous-basis multiplicities are obtained by
actual binary intersections and division by $ap$. Choose complements of
$(V_{u-1}\cap W_v)+(V_u\cap W_{v-1})$ in $V_u\cap W_v$ successively
to prove simultaneous splitting. Their dimensions sum to $k$, and
$b_{33}=0$ since $R=E_1+E_2$.

For each fixed type $T_\alpha$, precompute its nine-vector $b(T_\alpha)$
and five-vector $z(T_\alpha)$. Both add on direct sums. Enumerate
nonnegative multiplicities satisfying
\[
 \sum_\alpha m_\alpha b(T_\alpha)=b.\tag{6}
\]
This selects exactly the representations having the acquired pair of
flags. It does not require a supplied decomposition of actual matrices.

Solve each fixed commuting-square system over $\F_2$ once, giving
$h_{\alpha\beta}=\dim\Hom(T_\alpha,T_\beta)$. The binary coefficient
ranks are unchanged over $\F_{2^a}$. For
$M=\bigoplus T_\alpha^{m_\alpha}$, the semisimple quotient of
$\End M$ is $\prod_\alpha M_{m_\alpha}(\F_q)$: off-diagonal maps
between distinct indecomposables and diagonal radicals form its radical.
The radical dimension is
$\sum m_\alpha m_\beta h_{\alpha\beta}-\sum m_\alpha^2$.
Units are precisely lifts of units of the quotient, so
\[
 A(m):=|\Aut M|=
 q^{\sum_{\alpha,\beta}m_\alpha m_\beta h_{\alpha\beta}-\sum_\alpha m_\alpha^2}
 \prod_\alpha\prod_{v=0}^{m_\alpha-1}(q^{m_\alpha}-q^v).\tag{7}
\]
Let $m^0$ be the thin-only multiplicities describing the acquired base
flags with $J=0$. Their automorphism group is the ambient group $G$
preserving both flags, of order $A(m^0)$. A multiplicity vector (6)
specifies one $G$-orbit on $J$. Its stabilizer is $\Aut M$, since all
other vertex maps are uniquely determined by the ambient map through
the injective arrows. Its exact positive weight is
\[
 w(m)=A(m^0)/A(m).\tag{8}
\]
The code checks exact divisibility and sums weights at
$z=\sum m_\alpha z(T_\alpha)$. For each $j$ it verifies total weight
$\gb{k}{j}$. This explicitly computes the census; it is not a reduction
to an assumed Hall-polynomial or orbit-counting oracle.

\section{Reconstruction of the original-input count}
Weight (3) by (8) and sum at each $j$, obtaining
\[
 M_j(x)=\sum_{\substack{J\le R\\\dim J=j}}
 \sum_{D:\operatorname{carrier}(Dy)\subseteq J}
              (-1)^{\ell(D)}x^{\rank_2D/(ap)}.
\]
A fixed $\rho$-carrier lies in $\gb{k-\rho}{j-\rho}$ candidate
$j$-spaces. Thus the signed exact-join polynomials are
\[
 H_j(x)=M_j(x)-\sum_{\rho<j}\gb{k-\rho}{j-\rho}H_\rho(x),
 \qquad H_{t,\rho}=[x^t]H_\rho(x).\tag{9}
\]
\begin{theorem}
Under the retained two-factor actual-algebra and one-copy hypotheses,
every affine Hom offset and every pair of component target carriers
are admitted. The exact original-input count is
\[
 \boxed{2^{-c}\sum_{t,\rho}H_{t,\rho}F_{p,d,d,q}(k,t,\rho).}\tag{10}
\]
Here $F$ is the recovered proved signed universal-product fiber,
evaluated by its finite Gaussian dimension sum. Recognition, legitimate
declines, exact arithmetic and reconstruction are deterministic
polynomial time and polynomial space in the explicit DAG length.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The local character proof gives (3). Classification and orbit--stabilizer
give the exact number of subspaces at every statistic. Hence the moment
sum counts every actual candidate once, and (9) recovers the precise
signed histogram in the acquired Fourier formula, proving (10).
Implementation checks the zero character, total signed mass, target and
dual ranks, final divisibility, and $0\le\#\le2^n$. The retained affine
input reconstruction is bijective, with all constants and the corrected
left translation. Hom completion introduces no independent inputs.
\end{proof}

\section{Uniform costs, raw families, and limits}
There are eleven non-thin types. Enumerate their multiplicities first,
each at most $k$. The remaining base multiplicities are thin; each of
the at most eight occupied cells chooses the split between $J=0$ and
$J=R$. Thus at most $(k+1)^{19}$ tuples are visited. This fixed exponent
is paid, not presented as a practical-speed bound. Stream the profiles
into at most $(k+1)^5$ statistic positions. Rank polynomials have degree
at most $d$, and there are $k+1$ moments.

Automorphism and Gaussian integers have $O(ak^2)$ bits. Signed moment
and reconstruction intermediates have
$O(a(k^2+s_1^2+s_2^2)+n)$ bits, covered by $O(N^2)$.
With naive polynomial multiplication and integer arithmetic, the safe
bound $O(N^{30})$ bit operations includes earlier algebra acquisition
and the new census. Space is polynomial; the final answer has at most
$n+1$ bits. No exponential factor in the varying field degree or matrix
dimension is hidden as a constant.

The retained raw repeated-block family uses
$\mathcal D=\{\operatorname{diag}(A,A,B,B):A,B\in M_s(\F_q)\}$,
$p=s\ge2$, $d=4s$, $n=18as^2$, $c=2as^2=n/9$.
Replace the former target $(C_0,0,C_0,0)$ by
$(C_1,0,C_2,0)$ with arbitrary $C_1,C_2\in M_s(\F_q)$.
Source-free acquisition is unchanged, and right affine offsets remain
arbitrary. Dense binary input hiding, output mixing and recognized
reversible suffixes retain their charged description costs. The new
consumer handles arbitrary ranks and overlap of the two carriers and
phase subcarriers. The one-copy requirement still excludes general
targets spread over multiple joint simple copies.

The result supplies no uniform sampler. Signed coefficients are not
branching probabilities, and conditioning can destroy the semisimple
dual. Three general factors are not covered by this fixed $E_6$
classification. Polynomial-time counting for arbitrary circuits remains
unproved. No competitive speed claim follows from the fixture sizes.
