\begin{abstract}
We combine manufactured noncommutative dual algebras with a manufactured
finite geometry of target submodules. Individual dual matrices may change
their target image subspace; the component targets may overlap completely.
The replacement computation counts images contained in a candidate submodule.
Verified complete levels make this count depend only on dimension, through
Grassmann intersection numbers. Mobius inversion then reconstructs the full
dual-rank and target-rank histogram in polynomial time. Both the factors and
the target geometry are acquired from actual binary coefficients. No
isomorphism, invertible dual element, matrix units, or subspace decomposition
is supplied or searched for. This homogeneous construction is a restricted
ordinary-circuit compiler, not an unrestricted polynomial counting theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Actual algebra acquisition}
Retain the source-free affine Hom acquisition. The actual simple left nucleus
is $S\simeq M_p(\F_q)$, $q=2^a$, and the actual binary modules satisfy
$\dim_2U=\dim_2O=apd$. The right inputs embed bijectively into a subspace
$W\subseteq\Hom_S(U,O)$ with acquired annihilator
$\mathcal D\subseteq\Hom_S(O,U)$ of dimension $c$.
An acquired Hom offset is allowed here only when its character is zero on
$\mathcal D$, so the translated slice equals $W$. Arbitrary affine character
phases for a single matrix factor are handled by the separate two-moment
construction, not silently included in this theorem.

Manufacture the full actual multiplier algebra
\[
 L=\{A\in\End_S(U):A\mathcal D\subseteq\mathcal D\}.
\]
Solve for its center and verify injective Frobenius on that center. Refine the
fixed-space idempotent basis to primitive central idempotents $P_i$ without
enumerating their $2^b$ sums. On the actual image $U_i=P_iU$, restrict
$P_iL$ and apply the full simple-matrix-algebra sandwich certificate.
Require that the resulting center is exactly the restriction of the actual
scalar field of $S$, not merely a field of equal degree. This verifies
\[
 L=\bigoplus_{i=1}^bL_i,\quad L_i\simeq M_{s_i}(\F_q),
 \quad \dim_2U_i=ap h_i s_i,\quad d=\sum_i h_i s_i.
\]
The simple-factor tests are necessary: a reduced center alone would not rule
out a radical in a noncommutative algebra. Every $h_i$ is a checked positive
integer. Finally acquire $\mathcal D_i=P_i\mathcal D$ and check
\begin{equation}\label{eq:regular}
 \dim_2\mathcal D_i=a s_i^2\quad\text{for every }i,
 \qquad\bigcap_{D\in\mathcal D}\ker D=0.
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[Certified isomorphism without finding it]
There exists an $S$-linear isomorphism $D_0:O\to U$ such that
$\mathcal D=LD_0$. No step of the algorithm needs $D_0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By semisimplicity and the dimensions in \eqref{eq:regular}, each
$\mathcal D_i$ is isomorphic to the regular left $L_i$-module.
Thus some $L$-module isomorphism $\phi:L\to\mathcal D$ exists.
Put $D_0=\phi(1)$ for the proof. Then $\phi(A)=AD_0$ and
$\bigcap_D\ker D=\ker D_0$, since $D_0$ itself belongs to $\mathcal D$.
The common-kernel check makes $D_0$ injective. Equal dimensions make it
bijective. It is $S$-linear because every member of $\mathcal D$ is.
The representation and dimension tests, rather than a search for a cyclic
generator, certify every premise of this argument.
\end{proof}

\section{Acquiring each target carrier without the isomorphism}
Convert the original marked target to $y\in O$, checking all output
dependencies and constants. For an actual basis $D_{ij}$ of $\mathcal D_i$
manufacture
\[
 J_i=\operatorname{span}_2\{sD_{ij}y:s\in S,\ j\},
 \qquad
 R_i=\sum_j\operatorname{rowspace}_2(S\to U,\ s\mapsto sD_{ij}y).
\]
The maps are constructed using the actual binary basis of $S$; their row
spaces have a common ambient space $S^*$. Each is a right $S$-submodule.
Require
\[
 \dim_2J_i\in\{0,ap s_i\}.
\]
This is the zero-or-one joint simple-module-copy condition. Set
$g_i=\dim_2R_i/(ap)$ and $R=\sum_iR_i$, $k=\dim_2R/(ap)$.

\begin{lemma}[Carrier and local image census]
The submodule $R_i$ is exactly the row space of
$s\mapsto sP_iD_0y$. Its natural dimension satisfies $g_i\le s_i$.
For each natural $\ell$-submodule $T\subseteq R_i$, the polynomial counting
$D\in\mathcal D_i$ whose target orbit row space is contained in $T$,
weighted by $x^{\rank_2D/(ap)}$, is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:local}
 B_{i,\ell}(x)=\sum_{u=0}^{\min(s_i,s_i-g_i+\ell)}
 {s_i-g_i+\ell\brack u}_q
 \prod_{v=0}^{u-1}(q^{s_i}-q^v)\,x^{h_i u}.
\end{equation}
In particular it depends on $T$ only through $\ell$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every $D\in\mathcal D_i$ equals $AP_iD_0$, with $A\in L_i$ commuting
with $S$. The common kernel of the orbit maps $s\mapsto sDy$ is therefore
the kernel for $P_iD_0$: one implication uses commutation, and the other
uses the fact that $P_iD_0$ is itself in $\mathcal D_i$. Taking annihilators
proves the first assertion. This obtains the carrier without constructing
its proof-only generator.

In proof coordinates $U_i=\F_q^p\otimes\F_q^{s_i}\otimes\F_q^{h_i}$.
The joint-copy test says $P_iD_0y=C_i\otimes w_i$, where $w_i\ne0$ and
$C_i$ is a $p\times s_i$ matrix of rank $g_i$, or that the vector is zero.
The row-space carrier corresponds to the column space of $C_i$.
An element of $L_i$ is an arbitrary $s_i\times s_i$ matrix $A_i$;
its represented natural rank is $h_i\rank A_i$, and its target image is
the column space of $C_iA_i$ up to a harmless transpose convention.
The condition that this image lie in $T$ says that every column of $A_i$
lies in $C_i^{-1}T$, of dimension $s_i-g_i+\ell$.
Choose a rank-$u$ image there and a surjection from $\F_q^{s_i}$ onto it.
These choices give \eqref{eq:local}. The same formula covers $g_i=0$.
\end{proof}

For different factors the images of $D$ in $U$ are a direct sum; hence its
natural rank is $\sum_i h_i\rank A_i$. Its target orbit kernel is the
intersection of the component orbit kernels, so its target natural rank is
the dimension of the join of the component row submodules in $R$.
The full join $R$ has the original target rank $k$, since $D_0$ is an
$S$-linear isomorphism. None of these statements requires the $R_i$ to be
independent.

\section{Manufacturing a dimension-only contained count}
Group identical canonical row spaces $T=R_i$. For their common dimension $g$
form the complete vector of polynomials
\[
 B_{T,\ell}(x)=\prod_{i:R_i=T} B_{i,\ell}(x),\qquad0\le\ell\le g.
\]
At each present dimension $g$, require that these vectors agree for every
distinct $T$, and that their number is exactly ${k\brack g}_q$.
Every such $T$ is already a right $S$-submodule of $R$. The cardinality check
therefore certifies the entire Grassmann level. Denote its common vector by
$B_{g,\ell}$. An absent level contributes no factors. No missing submodules
are enumerated or assumed free.

For a fixed natural $j$-submodule $J\subseteq R$, the number of natural
$g$-submodules $T$ satisfying $\dim(T\cap J)=\ell$ is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:intersection}
 I_{k,g;j,\ell}={j\brack\ell}_q{k-j\brack g-\ell}_q
                 q^{(j-\ell)(g-\ell)}.
\end{equation}
Choose the intersection, the image in $R/J$, and a graph map into the
remaining $j-\ell$ dimensions of $J$ to obtain this classical formula.
Consequently the rank polynomial for all dual maps whose target image is
contained in $J$ is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:contained}
 A_j(x)=\prod_{g\ {m present}}
 \prod_{\ell=\max(0,g+j-k)}^{\min(g,j)}
 B_{g,\ell}(x)^{I_{k,g;j,\ell}}.
\end{equation}
Every factor's image must lie in $R_i\cap J$, exactly the event counted by
\eqref{eq:local}. Thus the contained count depends only on $j$, despite
arbitrary overlap of the complete target levels.

\begin{theorem}[Matrix factors with interacting target geometry]
Under the actual algebra, joint-copy and complete-level tests, define
\[
 E_\rho(x)=\sum_{j=0}^\rho {\rho\brack j}_q
       (-1)^{\rho-j}q^{\binom{\rho-j}{2}}A_j(x).
\]
Then the full character-rank histogram is
\[
 H(x,z)=\sum_{D\in\mathcal D}
 x^{\rank_2D/(ap)}z^{\dim_2S(Dy)/(ap)}
 =\sum_{\rho=0}^k{k\brack\rho}_q E_\rho(x)z^\rho.
\]
It has at most $(d+1)(k+1)$ coefficient positions. If $H_{t,\rho}$ are its
coefficients, the exact original-input count is
\[
 \boxed{2^{-c}\sum_{t,\rho}H_{t,\rho}F_{p,d,d,q}(k,t,\rho),}
\]
where $F$ is the previously proved signed universal-product fiber.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply finite-subspace-lattice Mobius inversion to the contained counts
\eqref{eq:contained}. It gives $E_\rho$ for each exact join of dimension
$\rho$; there are ${k\brack\rho}_q$ such joins. The resulting histogram
counts every actual dual element once. The existing exact character
orthogonality formula groups its terms by precisely these two ranks.
The original-input bijection and all marked output constants are retained
by the frontend, so the count is not inflated by internal gates or Hom
completion. The implementation verifies the total character mass $2^c$
and exact final divisibility.
\end{proof}

\section{Uniform costs and a growing ordinary-input family}
For $N=n+m+G+2=O(L)$, all actual module dimensions are $O(N)$ and algebra
dimensions are $O(N^2)$. There are at most $d$ factors, and every displayed
polynomial has degree at most $d$. Each exponent in \eqref{eq:contained} is
at most the number of actual carriers in its verified complete level.
Repeated squaring never creates degree beyond $d$; large failed Gaussian
cardinalities cause rejection before any expansion. Coefficients, Mobius
terms and reconstruction intermediates have $O(N^2)$ bits. Naive linear
algebra, simple-algebra certificates and integer arithmetic therefore give
the safe bound $O(N^{16})$ bit operations and polynomial space, including
acquisition and legitimate declines. The output has at most $n+1$ bits.

A concrete all-size family has $b$ equal factors $M_s(\F_q)$, each repeated
twice on the inner space, with $p=s\ge2$ and $d=2bs$. Its dual is
\[
 \mathcal D=\left\{\operatorname{diag}(A_1,A_1,\ldots,A_b,A_b):
                  A_i\in M_s(\F_q)\right\}.
\]
The original right input $W=\mathcal D^\perp$ consists of all $d\times d$
matrices whose two diagonal $s\times s$ blocks in each pair agree; all other
entries are free. For $Z=(C_0,0,\ldots,C_0,0)$, all nonzero target carriers
are equal. They form the full top level of their common carrier space,
irrespective of $b$ or $\rank C_0\le s$. Thus the geometry test passes with
fully overlapping targets. The exact independent-input count and codimension
are
\[
 n=ab(4b+1)s^2,\qquad c=ab s^2.
\]
For $b=2$, $c=n/9$ grows linearly with the original input count; both dual
matrix degree and accepted target rank grow with $s$.

The complete polar adjoint algebra is $L^{\rm op}\times L$. The idealizers
of $W$ are $L$, by trace annihilation and $1\in L$. Off-diagonal polar
blocks vanish by two distinct output rows and by commutators of cross-block
matrix units; $I_d\in W$ because the repeated blocks occur twice in
characteristic two. Its central atoms are paired by adjoint. Their two
image dimensions are $2s^2$ and $(4b-1)s^2$, so the dimension rule chooses
one consistent half in every pair. Trying the two global orientations
recovers the intended left input. Right multiplications by $W$ generate
$M_d(\F_q)$, so its actual left nucleus is exactly $M_p(\F_q)$.
These statements justify source-free acquisition before any representation
is provided. Dense invertible binary input/output changes, input translations
and recognized reversible register suffixes are permitted; their actual gate
description length remains charged. Syntax completeness is only claimed for
the explicit quadratic frontier and recognized suffix encodings.

\section{Executed evidence and retained failures}
Code and audit are \path{matrix_target_geometry.py} and
\path{matrix_target_geometry_checks.py}. The native two-factor binary case
has $s=p=b=2$, 72 original inputs, 24,423 gates and 500 nonlinear reversible
updates. The actual factors, hidden original coordinates, dual and target
geometry are acquired. Six rank pairs give the exact count
\[
 71\,175\,785\,712\,451\,584.
\]
All 16 common $2\times2$ targets agree with literal enumeration of the
actual 256 dual matrices and the earlier exact character formula.
An independent oracle enumerates all 65,536 left matrices and solves the
original right-input linear equations. It finds 64,734 consistent left
matrices, each with $2^{40}$ right solutions, and the same final count.
Another 128 original gate evaluations agree with the native reference.
A target with two distinct lines is explicitly declined because those two
lines do not form the three-point binary projective line.

A separate formula-stage execution uses four $M_5(\F_2)$ factors with
twofold multiplicity and a common rank-five target. It compresses codimension
100 to 51 rank pairs and obtains an exact 1,500-bit population for parameters
with $n=1700$. Raw acquisition of a 1700-input gate program was not executed;
the data explicitly distinguish the formula stage. None of these checks is
a comparative speed claim.

Finite matrix-rank populations, Grassmann intersection numbers and lattice
Mobius inversion have classical antecedents, including Delsarte's
bilinear-forms association schemes and finite-module rank-metric duality;
see Ravagnani, arXiv:1410.1333. The established composite result is the
manufacture of both actions and target geometry, with exact original-input
reconstruction. Arbitrary target arrangements and general affine phases on
multiple factors remain outside this branch. The separate zero-diagonal
target reduction locates \#P-complete all-target counting even inside the
commutative diagonal case. That is a conditional complexity obstruction,
not an impossibility proof, and not a solution of unrestricted polynomial
Boolean counting.
