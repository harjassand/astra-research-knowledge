\begin{abstract}
The source-free matrix-product compiler required the right input to fill an
entire module-homomorphism space. We remove this requirement. From the actual
binary coefficients we manufacture that larger space, its reverse trace
pairing, and the annihilator of the actual input image. A signed universal
evaluation fiber depends on only three acquired ranks and has a polynomial
integer formula. Consequently codimension $c$ costs $2^c$ character evaluations,
without enumerating input assignments or target values. This is polynomial
when $c=O(\log L)$, where $L$ is the explicit ordinary-circuit description
length. No bound on $c$ is claimed for arbitrary circuits. Classical module
evaluation, matrix-product populations, and finite-space Mobius inversion are
ingredients, not asserted historical novelties.
\end{abstract}

\section{Input and acquired representation}
The raw input is an ordinary fan-in-two Boolean DAG with $n$ declared independent
inputs, $G$ explicit gates, and one output. Wire indices are binary. Put
$L=(n+G+1)\lceil\log_2(n+G+2)\rceil$ on admitted inputs. The frontend
propagates exact degree-at-most-two ANFs, marks higher-degree values unknown,
and peels literal NOT, CNOT, and Toffoli updates from a complete terminal AND.
It never truncates an unknown high-degree polynomial. Terminal DAG traversal
visits each wire once, even if a shared subcomputation unfolds exponentially.
Peeling strictly decreases predecessor wire indices. Acceptance produces
actual quadratic registers $Q_i$ and a single target $b$; the output is one
precisely on $Q(x)=b$.

The source-free joint-adjoint procedure constructs a bijective affine map
$x=x_0+J(u,v)$ such that
\[
 Q(x_0+J(u,v))=q+B(u,v),\qquad B:U\times V\longrightarrow O.
\]
Independent marked cross forms give the actual output basis; dependencies and
constants are retained and checked against $b$. Both one-sided radicals are
zero. The left nucleus $S$ is acquired by solving
$B(Au,v)=C B(u,v)$. Its output representation is faithful. Binary center,
Frobenius, and full sandwich-rank certificates verify
\[
 S\cong M_p(\F_q),\quad q=2^a,\qquad
 \dim_2U=apd,\quad \dim_2O=apr.
\]
No field generator, matrix units, or canonical matrix-algebra isomorphism is
constructed. Both orientations of the manufactured split are examined; among
certified simple nuclei the code chooses the least codimension. This is two
explicit trials, not a search over decompositions.

Write $H=\Hom_S(U,O)$. The map $\iota:V\to H$,
$\iota(v)(u)=B(u,v)$, is injective by the right-radical test. We allow a proper
image $W=\iota(V)$ and acquire
\[
 c=\dim_2H-\dim_2V=adr-\dim_2V\geq0.
\]
The old full-product consumer explicitly declines $c>0$. Syntax recognition
is sufficient rather than complete: equivalent encodings may be declined.

\section{Manufacturing the dual without a matrix basis}
Solve binary linear systems for $H=\Hom_S(U,O)$,
$H'=\Hom_S(O,U)$, and $E=\End_S(U)$. In the actual action matrices these
are $TA_i=C_iT$, their reverse, and commutation with $A_i$. Their dimensions
are $adr,adr,ad^2$. By the simple-module structure theorem
$E\cong M_d(\F_q)$, up to opposite convention. Solve for a nonzero binary
functional $\tau:E\to\F_2$ annihilating all commutators. This space has
dimension $a$.

\begin{lemma}
The pairing $(D,T)\mapsto\tau(DT)$ identifies $H'$ with the binary dual of $H$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
In abstract matrix coordinates, every nonzero such functional is
$\tau(Z)=\operatorname{Tr}_{\F_q/\F_2}(\gamma\operatorname{tr}Z)$ for a
nonzero $\gamma\in\F_q$. Both the rectangular matrix trace pairing and the
field trace pairing are nondegenerate. The program additionally checks full
binary rank of the actual pairing matrix. It does not use the trace of a
large represented binary matrix, which can vanish at even multiplicity.
\end{proof}

Express $\iota(v_j)$ in the acquired basis of $H$, solve for
$W^\perp\subseteq H^*$, and convert its basis by this pairing to actual
matrices $D:O\to U$. These are polynomial binary linear systems. The small
character family is manufactured rather than furnished by an oracle.

\section{The signed full-space fiber}
For the derivation only, choose abstract coordinates
\[
 u=X\in\F_q^{p\times d},\quad T=Y\in\F_q^{d\times r},\quad
 y=C\in\F_q^{p\times r},\quad D\in\F_q^{r\times d}.
\]
Absorb $\gamma$ into $D$. Let $k=\rank C$, $t=\rank D$,
$\rho=\rank(CD)$, and $w=t-\rho$. Define
\[
 F_{p,d,r,q}(k,t,\rho)=\sum_{XY=C}
 (-1)^{\operatorname{Tr}_{\F_q/\F_2}\operatorname{tr}(DY)}.
\]
Set ${N\brack j}_q=0$ outside $0\leq j\leq N$.

\begin{theorem}[Polynomial signed-fiber formula]
For consistent ranks,
\begin{align*}
 F_{p,d,r,q}(k,t,\rho)
 &=\sum_{h=\max(0,r-d)}^{r-k}
 \sum_{\ell=\max(0,h-(r-k-w),h+t-r)}^{\min(h,w)}
 {w\brack\ell}_q {r-k-w\brack h-\ell}_q\,(-1)^{t-\ell}\\
 &\quad\cdot q^{(w-\ell)(h-\ell)+p(d-r+h)+\binom{t-\ell}{2}}
 \prod_{j=0}^{r-h-t+\ell-1}(q^d-q^{t-\ell+j}).
\end{align*}
An empty sum is zero, an empty product is one, and all displayed exponents
are nonnegative on the summation range.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
View $Y$ as a map $R=\F_q^r\to\F_q^d$. For $H_0=\ker Y$ of dimension
$h$, the equation $XY=C$ is solvable exactly when $H_0\subseteq K=\ker C$;
then it has $q^{p(d-r+h)}$ choices of $X$. Necessarily $h\geq r-d$.
For fixed $H_0$, subspace-lattice Mobius inversion gives
\[
 \sum_{\ker Y=H_0}\psi(\operatorname{tr}DY)
 =\sum_{J\supseteq H_0}\mu(H_0,J)
       \sum_{Y:J\subseteq\ker Y}\psi(\operatorname{tr}DY),
\]
where $\psi(z)=(-1)^{\operatorname{Tr}z}$ and
$\mu(H_0,J)=(-1)^{j-h}q^{\binom{j-h}{2}}$, $j=\dim J$.
The inner sum vanishes unless $T_0=\operatorname{im}D\subseteq J$;
otherwise it is $q^{d(r-j)}$. Put $\ell=\dim(H_0\cap T_0)$ and
$s=h+t-\ell=\dim(H_0+T_0)$. The finite $q$-binomial identity yields
\begin{align*}
 &\sum_{j=s}^r {r-s\brack j-s}_q(-1)^{j-h}
       q^{\binom{j-h}{2}+d(r-j)}\\
 &\hspace{1cm}=(-1)^{t-\ell}q^{\binom{t-\ell}{2}}
       \prod_{j=0}^{r-s-1}(q^d-q^{t-\ell+j}).
\end{align*}
Finally $\dim(K\cap T_0)=t-\rank(CD)=w$. The number of $h$-spaces of $K$
whose intersection with this fixed $w$-space has dimension $\ell$ is
\[
 {w\brack\ell}_q {r-k-w\brack h-\ell}_q q^{(w-\ell)(h-\ell)}.
\]
Choose the intersection, its quotient image, and a graph map from that quotient
to the remaining $w-\ell$ dimensions. Multiply these factors and sum.
\end{proof}

No abstract matrix coordinates are required to evaluate the formula:
\[
 k=\frac{\dim_2(Sy)}{ap},\qquad
 t=\frac{\rank_2D}{ap},\qquad
 \rho=\frac{\dim_2(SD(y))}{ap}.
\]
These equalities follow from the natural simple $S$-module multiplicities;
the code verifies all divisibilities.

\section{Exact reconstruction and cost}
\begin{theorem}[Acquired proper-slice count]
For an accepted raw circuit and target $y$ consistent with the actual output
dependencies, the number of satisfying original inputs is
\[
 \boxed{2^{-c}\sum_{D\in W^\perp}
 F_{p,d,r,q}\left(k,\frac{\rank_2D}{ap},
                        \frac{\dim_2(SD(y))}{ap}\right).}
\]
For an affine Hom-space slice $T_0+W$, multiply the term associated with
$\lambda_D$ by $(-1)^{\lambda_D(T_0)}$. The current native frontend acquires
the centered linear slice; the standalone consumer also accepts an offset
specified in the acquired Hom coordinates.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Binary character orthogonality says
$1_W(T)=2^{-c}\sum_{\lambda\in W^\perp}(-1)^{\lambda(T)}$.
Insert it into the full evaluation fiber and use the preceding formula.
The affine version introduces the stated phase. The map $V\to W$ is a
bijection, as is the original coordinate map $x_0+J(u,v)$. Internal circuit
gates and manufactured Hom coordinates introduce no independent-input
multiplicity. An inconsistent original output dependency gives zero first.
\end{proof}

For a conservative uniform bit bound put $N=n+m+G+2$, where $m$ is the acquired
register count; $N=O(L)$. Actual module dimensions are $O(N)$; Hom and
endomorphism dimensions are $O(N^2)$. Their defining systems have polynomial
binary size. Naive binary elimination, multiplication checks, and the
simple-algebra sandwich certificate fit within $O(N^{12})$ bit operations;
$O(N^{14})$ also covers exact quadratic propagation and frontend bookkeeping.
Each character formula has $O(N^2)$ terms and at most $O(N)$ products per term
when Gaussian binomials are evaluated directly. All intermediate integers have
$O(N^2)$ bits, including the character sum: $c\leq\dim_2H=O(N^2)$.
Schoolbook arithmetic therefore gives the safe bound
\[
 O\bigl(N^{14}+2^cN^8\bigr)
\]
bit operations and polynomial space with streamed characters. The final count
has at most $n+1$ bits. For fixed $K$, $c\leq K\log_2L$ gives polynomial time.
This is a measured certificate condition, not a theorem about every circuit.
The frontend rejects enormous unused-input declarations before dense
allocation: zero common polar radical requires every declared input to be an
output ancestor, hence fan-in two forces $n\leq G+1$.

\section{Executed evidence and boundary}
Code: \texttt{unsaturated\_bilinear\_counter.py}. Its independent audit
\texttt{unsaturated\_bilinear\_checks.py} enumerates actual $(X,Y)$ pairs,
performs a literal binary Walsh transform, and compares all tested $(C,D)$
characters with the formula. Five initial cases over $\F_2,\F_4$ make 9,024
character comparisons. Dense affine-hidden proper slices include binary
codimension one inside an $\F_4$ Hom space; the actual slice need not be
$\F_q$-linear. All target values are compared with original-input histograms,
failed admissions are recorded, and ordinary gate programs contain nonlinear
reversible updates. Exact code, gate fixtures, and machine-readable results
are preserved with this proof. These are correctness checks, not speed claims.

The four hidden-slice audits enumerate 303,616 original assignments and compare
4,480 target populations. Larger ordinary DAGs in
\texttt{unsaturated\_native\_growth.py} each contain 500 nonlinear reversible
updates. A 32-input, 4,599-gate example has $c=3$ and count $832768$; a
27-input, 3,691-gate example has $c=3$ and count $30240$. The production
consumer uses eight characters in each case. Independently, enumeration of
respectively 131,072 and 32,768 actual right inputs followed by row-space
solves gives the same counts. This independent reference does not use the
signed-fiber formula. Both cases also pass 128 original gate evaluations.

Exact counting does not alone justify rejection sampling of an affine slice
from the full fiber. Let $X\in\F_2^{p\times2}$, $Y\in\F_2^{2\times2}$ and
target $XY=0$. The full population is $2^{2p}+9\,2^p+6$, from the $1,9,6$
matrices $Y$ of ranks $0,1,2$. The one-condition affine slice $Y_{11}=1$
contains four rank-one and four rank-two matrices, and its population is
$4\,2^p+4$. Full-fiber rejection therefore has acceptance probability
asymptotic to $4\,2^{-p}$ even for $c=1$. This is a failure of that sampling
mechanism, not of the exact two-character counter or of every possible sampler.

The arbitrary dual rank distribution has not been compressed. Its character
sum can still be exponential; the split, affine-center, or simple-nucleus
certificates can also fail. These are boundaries of this construction, not
unconditional lower bounds on other representations. Polynomial counting for
arbitrary Boolean circuits is not established, and no such claim is inferred
from the governing fixed-width ETH theorem.

\section{Primary antecedents}
James B. Wilson, \emph{Division, adjoints, and dualities of bilinear maps},
arXiv:1007.4329, Section 2.3, develops evaluation
$U\times\Hom_R(U,O)\to O$ and its universal relation to a bimap.
The left nucleus has established computational-algebra implementations; see
Magma, \emph{Linear Invariants}, including its rectangular product example.
H. Wei and X. Zheng, \emph{The number of solutions to the bilinear matrix
equation over a finite field}, Journal of Statistical Planning and Inference
94 (2001), 359--369, DOI 10.1016/S0378-3758(00)00266-4, treats classical
matrix-product populations. Finite-matrix Fourier transforms and
$q$-Krawtchouk machinery have substantial antecedents, including
K. Kawamura, arXiv:2008.13408. The signed formula is proved here directly;
historical novelty is not asserted. The construction under test is the actual
coefficient acquisition and ordinary-input interface, including the proper
binary slice, rather than the classical ingredients individually.
