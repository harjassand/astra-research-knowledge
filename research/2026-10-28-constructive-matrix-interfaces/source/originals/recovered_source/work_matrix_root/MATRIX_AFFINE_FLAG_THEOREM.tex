\title{Acquired phase-kernel flags for affine matrix-factor counting}
\author{Astra P3 constructive counting research}\date{27 October 2026}

\begin{abstract}
We acquire the interaction of arbitrary affine characters with a common
target carrier as actual submodules. When these form a nested flag, a
transfer over intersection dimensions computes the complete signed
dual-rank and target-rank histogram. The flag is verified, and neither its
length nor the number or sizes of the matrix factors are bounded constants.
No character, subspace, flag-position sequence or useful decomposition is
enumerated. The result is a restricted ordinary-circuit counter with full
acquisition and original-input reconstruction, not an unrestricted
polynomial Boolean counting theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Actual input and acquired action}
The input is an ordinary indexed Boolean gate DAG of length $L$, with
$n$ independent input bits, $G$ gates and $m$ marked frontier bits.
Write $N=n+m+G+2=O(L)$ after the explicit ancestry guard. Retain the exact
quadratic propagation, recognized reversible suffix, paired polar-adjoint
atoms and affine Hom acquisition of the preceding proofs. These are checked
properties of actual coefficients, not supplied representations.

The acquired nucleus is $S\simeq M_p(\F_q)$, $q=2^a$, with
$\dim_2U=\dim_2O=apd$. The original right input maps bijectively onto
$W\subseteq\Hom_S(U,O)$, and the original expression is evaluation on
the acquired affine slice $T_0+W$. Its annihilator is
$\mathcal D\subseteq\Hom_S(O,U)$ of binary dimension $c$, with actual
character $\ell:\mathcal D\to\F_2$ induced by $T_0$.
The target $y\in O$ includes the original output constants; inconsistent
linear output dependencies return zero immediately.

The full multiplier acquisition in
\path{MATRIX_TARGET_GEOMETRY_THEOREM.tex} verifies
\[
 L=\{A\in\operatorname{End}_S(U):A\mathcal D\subseteq\mathcal D\}
   =\bigoplus_{i=1}^b L_i,\qquad L_i\simeq M_{s_i}(\F_q),
 \quad d=\sum_i h_i s_i,\quad c=a\sum_i s_i^2.
\]
It checks each simple factor, equality of its actual scalar action with
that of $S$, $\dim_2(P_iU)=ap h_i s_i$, and
$\dim_2\mathcal D_i=as_i^2$ for $\mathcal D_i=P_i\mathcal D$.
The common kernel of $\mathcal D$ is zero. Regular-module dimensions
therefore certify an $S$-linear isomorphism $D_0:O\to U$ with
$\mathcal D=LD_0$. Neither $D_0$ nor matrix units are constructed or
searched for. They are used only in the correctness proof.

\section{Manufacturing phase carriers}
For an actual basis $D_{ij}$ of each $\mathcal D_i$, acquire in the common
ambient space $S^*$ the row submodule
\[
 E_i=\sum_j\operatorname{rowspace}_2(S\to U,\ s\mapsto sD_{ij}y).
\]
Require the joint span of all $sD_{ij}y$ to have dimension $0$ or
$ap s_i$. Thus each nonzero target component occupies exactly one joint
simple-module copy. Require all nonzero $E_i$ to coincide; write the
common carrier as $R$, with natural dimension $k=\dim_2R/(ap)$.
If all components vanish, set $R=0$ and $k=0$.

Put $\ell_i=\ell|_{\mathcal D_i}$ and compute the actual functional span
and its annihilator
\[
 \mathcal O_i=\operatorname{span}_2\{D\mapsto\ell_i(AD):
                  A\text{ in a basis of }L_i\},\qquad
 K_i=\bigcap_{\lambda\in\mathcal O_i}\ker\lambda.
\]
Every product is formed on represented binary matrices and reduced in the
actual dual basis. Acquire
\[
 t_i=\frac{\dim_2\mathcal O_i}{as_i},\qquad
 F_i=\sum_{D\text{ in a basis of }K_i}
          \operatorname{rowspace}_2(s\mapsto sDy),\qquad
 f_i=\frac{\dim_2F_i}{ap}.
\]
Check every division, $\dim_2K_i=as_i(s_i-t_i)$, and actual inclusion
$F_i\subseteq E_i$ by row reduction.

\begin{lemma}[Meaning of the acquired data]\label{lem:phase}
In proof coordinates the nonzero component target is represented by a
rank-$k$ map $C_i:\F_q^{s_i}\to\F_q^p$, and its variable dual matrix
by $A_i\in M_{s_i}(\F_q)$, with target carrier $\im(C_iA_i)$.
For a matrix $Q_i$ the character is
$(-1)^{\Tr_{\F_q/\F_2}\operatorname{tr}(Q_iA_i)}$ and
\[
 t_i=\rank Q_i,\qquad F_i=C_i\ker Q_i,\qquad
 \max(0,k-t_i)\le f_i\le\min(k,s_i-t_i).
\]
The identification is between actual right $S$-submodules and their natural
finite-field subspaces; it is not supplied to the algorithm.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let the original multiplier matrix be $M_i$ on the second tensor factor of
$\F_q^p\otimes\F_q^{s_i}\otimes\F_q^{h_i}$. The joint-copy condition
writes $P_iD_0y=C_i\otimes w_i$ with $w_i\ne0$. Set
$A_i=M_i^{\mathsf T}$, so its target matrix is $C_iA_i$.
Nondegeneracy of the field and matrix trace pairings gives a unique $B_i$
with $\ell_i(M_iD_0)=\Tr\operatorname{tr}(B_iM_i)$; take
$Q_i=B_i^{\mathsf T}$. Left translates correspond to matrices $B_iT$
and have binary span dimension $as_i\rank B_i$. Their annihilator is
$M_iB_i=0$, since cyclicity of trace gives
$\operatorname{tr}(B_iT M_i)=\operatorname{tr}(M_iB_iT)$.
After transpose this is $Q_iA_i=0$, or $\im A_i\subseteq\ker Q_i$.
Summing their target images therefore gives exactly $C_i\ker Q_i$.
The stated inequalities follow by restricting $C_i$ to $\ker Q_i$.
This proves the carrier identity with the left-action and transpose
conventions explicitly aligned.
\end{proof}

Require the distinct $F_i$ belonging to nonzero targets, together with
$0$ and $R$, to be nested by actual row-space inclusion. They give the
acquired flag $0=V_0<\cdots<V_e=R$. Repeated subspaces are grouped.
There is no constant bound on $e$. Zero-target components need no flag
condition.

\section{Signed local rank polynomials}
For a fixed $j$-subspace $J\le R$, let $\eta_i=\dim(J\cap F_i)$.
The condition $\im(C_iA_i)\subseteq J$ restricts the image of $A_i$ to
$C_i^{-1}J$, of dimension $r_i=s_i-k+j$. Its intersection with
$\ker Q_i$ has dimension $(s_i-t_i-f_i)+\eta_i$, so the restricted
character has rank
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tau}
 \tau_i=t_i-k+j+f_i-\eta_i.
\end{equation}

For all $r$-by-$s$ matrices with character-matrix rank $\tau$, let $P_u$
be the signed population at rank $u$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:local}
 {r-\tau\brack v}_q q^{sv}
   =\sum_{u=0}^v P_u{r-u\brack v-u}_q,\qquad 0\le v\le r.
\end{equation}
To prove this, sum over $v$-spaces containing the image of a matrix.
Character orthogonality leaves the $v$-spaces killed by the character
matrix, each contributing $q^{sv}$. A rank-$u$ image has
${r-u\brack v-u}_q$ containing $v$-spaces. Thus triangular subtraction
manufactures $P_{s,r,\tau}(x)=\sum_uP_ux^u$ without listing matrices or
subspaces. It has degree at most $\min(s,r)$, constant coefficient one,
and total mass $q^{sr}$ for $\tau=0$, zero otherwise.

The local polynomial is $P_{s_i,r_i,\tau_i}(x^{h_i})$.
For zero-target components use $P_{s_i,s_i,t_i}(x^{h_i})$ and multiply
them into an initial polynomial $Z(x)$. Negative coefficients are retained.

\section{Unbounded flag transfer and reconstruction}
Fix $j\in\{0,\ldots,k\}$. At a flag transition from ambient dimension
$v$ to $v+\delta$, a fixed previous intersection of dimension $e$ has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:transfer}
 {\delta\brack\eta-e}_q q^{(v-e)(\eta-e)}
\end{equation}
extensions of dimension $\eta$ meeting the old space exactly in that
intersection. Choose the quotient image and its graph into the old space
modulo that intersection. This proves the transition count.

Start with $Z(x)$ in state zero. At each flag space $V$ of dimension $f$,
apply \eqref{eq:transfer} and multiply the local polynomials for factors
with $F_i=V$, using \eqref{eq:tau} and intersection dimension $\eta$.
Keep $\eta\le j$ and $j-\eta\le k-f$. At $R$ the only state is $j$.
Its polynomial is the exact containment moment
\[
 M_j(x)=\sum_{\substack{J\le R\\\dim J=j}}
         \sum_{D:\,\operatorname{carrier}(Dy)\subseteq J}
                    (-1)^{\ell(D)}x^{\rank_2D/(ap)}.
\]
States store only an intersection dimension and a polynomial of degree
at most $d$, not all intersection sequences or actual subspaces.

The ranks of the factors add, and their target carriers join inside $R$.
A join of dimension $\rho$ belongs to
${k-\rho\brack j-\rho}_q$ candidate spaces. Hence the exact signed
rank histogram $H_\rho(x)$ is reconstructed by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:inverse}
 H_j(x)=M_j(x)-\sum_{\rho<j}{k-\rho\brack j-\rho}_q H_\rho(x).
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}
Under the tested acquisition, one-copy, common-carrier and phase-flag
conditions, put $H_{t,\rho}=[x^t]H_\rho(x)$. The number of satisfying
assignments to the original independent bits is
\[
 \boxed{2^{-c}\sum_{t,\rho}H_{t,\rho}F_{p,d,d,q}(k,t,\rho),}
\]
where $F$ is the previously proved signed universal-product fiber formula.
Acquisition, legitimate declines, exact arithmetic and reconstruction
take $O(N^{18})$ bit operations and polynomial space. The flag branch
includes every affine phase for a single matrix factor. It does not
assert unrestricted counting or uniform sampling for this entire family.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Equations \eqref{eq:local}--\eqref{eq:inverse} compute precisely the
signed histogram required by affine character orthogonality. The existence
of the certified isomorphism $D_0$ ensures that $R$ has the original
target rank. The affine frontend keeps every constant and the actual
bijection back to original inputs, including its left-input translation.
Hom completion introduces no independent inputs. The implementation
checks rank bounds, the zero-matrix coefficient, signed character mass,
exact divisibility by $2^c$, and $0\le\#\le2^n$.

There are at most $d$ factors, $k+1$ flag dimensions, $k+1$ moments and
$k+1$ intersection states per level. Every polynomial has degree at most
$d$. Local triangular inversion and naive multiplication use polynomially
many integer operations. Absolute moment sums are bounded by $2^c$ times
finite-subspace counts. Subsequent inversion and the universal-fiber
formula likewise use $O(N^2)$-bit intermediates. The displayed conservative
bound dominates the earlier polynomial algebra acquisition and these
additional linear systems and finite sums, with bit operations charged.
For one factor there is just one acquired $F_1$, automatically nested
between $0$ and $R$; hence every affine phase is admitted.
\end{proof}

\section{Raw families and executed evidence}
The raw repeated-block family of
\path{RAW_PRODUCT_MATRIX_DUAL_FAMILY.tex} has $b$ factors $M_s(\F_q)$,
each repeated twice, with $p=s\ge2$, $d=2bs$, and
\[
 n=ab(4b+1)s^2,\qquad c=ab s^2.
\]
Its full binary polar adjoint is $L^{\rm op}\times L$, its paired atoms
admit deterministic half selection, and the actual left nucleus is
$M_s(\F_q)$. Use target $(C_0,0,\ldots,C_0,0)$. Right affine offsets
give arbitrary phase matrices $Q_i$. With invertible $C_0$, choosing their
kernels along a flag gives acquired carriers $C_0\ker Q_i$.
Both flag length and target rank grow: $b=s+1$ realizes every dimension
$0,1,\ldots,s$. For $b=2$, $c=n/9$. Dense invertible binary changes,
translations and recognized reversible suffixes retain their actual
description costs. No syntactic completeness claim is made for all
equivalent encodings.

Production code is \path{matrix_affine_flag_counter.py}; results are in
\path{matrix_affine_flag_check_results.json}. For two binary $M_2$ factors
at a common full-rank target, all 256 actual character words were tested.
The 202 admitted phases agree with literal enumeration of all 256 actual
dual matrices and full character reconstruction. The remaining 54 are
declined specifically for nonnested carriers.

The native affine fixture has 72 original inputs, 24,691 gates and 500
nonlinear reversible updates. Its offset lies outside the original right
subspace. The acquired flag has dimensions $0,1,2$ and six rank profiles
give population $71\,219\,766\,177\,562\,624$.
An independent oracle enumerates all 65,536 original left matrices and
solves the original affine right equations, obtaining the same number;
128 original-gate checks also agree. Thirty-two acquired $\F_4$
single-factor offsets agree with the separate two-moment consumer and
literal dual histograms.

The independent audit in
\path{work_partition_free_agent/matrix_affine_flag_independent_audit_results.json}
checks 63 rectangular character polynomials against 16,497 matrices over
$\F_2,\F_4,\F_8$; 300 actual phase acquisitions and 172,416 signed
actual-character comparisons; and four actual-column-space transfers.
The last include a five-level binary flag of codimension 80, verified
using 327,680 local matrices rather than $2^{80}$ global characters.
Nonsymmetric phases test the transpose-sensitive carrier identity.

A formula-only execution with six $M_5(\F_2)$ factors uses every flag
dimension $0,1,\ldots,5$. It compresses codimension 150 to 81 profiles
and returns a 3,450-bit count for parameters with $n=3750$.
Raw acquisition of a 3,750-input circuit was not executed. These are
correctness checks, not comparative speed claims. Finite matrix-rank
character transforms and Gaussian subspace counts are classical; this
construction couples them to manufactured phase carriers and exact
native-input reconstruction. Nonnested carriers require a separately
proved consumer.
