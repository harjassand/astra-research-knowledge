# A finite classical compiler acquired from a non-EB noise operation

An exact fixed-state classical simulator was acquired for a concrete qutrit process and for an entangling two-qutrit gate under a stated noise model. The construction includes an exact counterexample to naive tensorization, a repaired17-state local frame, and rational stochastic kernels that compose at arbitrary width and depth. This is a mathematical/acquisition result. No new tractability class, physical calibration, speed advantage or civilisation-level capability is established.

The independent choice was recorded in CHOICE.md before another route's developing choice was exchanged. ## Structural proof and the strongest incumbent

The construction led to a sharp, all-dimensional EA/filter criterion for Phi_c: equal local noise is entanglement annihilating exactly when c>=(1+sqrt(3))/2. An independent proof builds a nonnegative factorization of the Schmidt block and samples finite phase products. A quadratic phase family over an odd finite field needs only p^2 assignments, p>=d prime; sampling does not enumerate them. This gives an explicit continuous-state trajectory comparator without a separability oracle. Numerical replay covered1800 spectra in six dimensions, and an independently implemented local Kraus calculation verified96 complete rotated output matrices to1.06e-15.

The boundary is already known. [Lami and Huber2016, Theorem7 and Corollary8](https://arxiv.org/html/1603.02158) classify a broader three-parameter map. After full transpose, our family has alpha=beta=-1/c and gamma=1/c^2; their inequality gamma<=alpha+beta+2 is exactly our threshold. Their proof is constructive through a Hadamard CP map. Our proof and sampler are an acquired specialization/replay, not a new EA theorem. The2013 Filippov-Ziman sufficient/necessary gap was closed in2016 and is not an open target.

[Harrow and Nielsen2003, Theorem4 and SectionIII.C](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0301108) already give efficient approximate sampling for separability-preserving bounded-arity gates, including qudits. Thus a claim that this noise regime newly makes circuits classically tractable would be false. The finite cached rational compiler addresses a narrower interface: fixed physical state labels and acquired exact kernels, with no per-step separability search or growing state precision. A measured runtime or precision advantage over an equally informed continuous-state sampler has not been demonstrated.

The current [Cichy et al. v2, published2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05745v2) provides locally entanglement-optimal trajectory unravelings with error guarantees for arbitrary single-qubit noise, using tensor networks. It can address regimes retaining entanglement and is a stronger current computational comparator; it does not directly assert our fixed-frame qutrit contract. The linked public Julia README was read and its commit pinned, but the code was not installed or benchmarked. Standard polyhedral positive-realization theory predates this construction. [Vieira, Ku and Budroni v2/2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.16789v2) explicitly charge the intermediate classical dimension when EB channels reproduce temporal statistics; our15/17-state overhead must likewise be retained. The conservative general Holevo-length bound used here is d^4.

## What the result does and does not buy

The original ambition was a principled boundary between finite classical memory and quantum processes that require infinite exact classical memory, a distinction exhibited by [Fanizza, Lumbreras and Winter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.11225). The acquired kernels are reusable operations toward that question. They provide actual state sets and causal transitions, not just EB membership or selected favourable trajectories. The tensor counterexample prevents the most tempting incorrect extension, and its repaired frame shows how a separation witness can guide acquisition.

The current operation still depends on the specified, strongly noisy transpose-depolarizing model and supplied matrices. The generic finite-word bound is very large; finite-data instrument identification, conditioning/rare-branch guarantees, physical channel calibration and efficient dimension scaling have not been obtained. The17-state table is4.1MB and its preparation is part of the resource contract; classical state labels do not make that table free. Exact rational sampling uses ideal fair bits and potentially rejection; fixed deterministic random-bit caps require an explicit approximation/truncation contract.

There is no observed hardware or practical quantum-computing advantage. No broad arbitrarily-many-copy entanglement statement follows from this two-site result. The useful continuation is acquisition of compact frames for channels/gates beyond already known separability-preserving regimes, or a sharp limitation on that acquisition. Root's separate general-frame result should be assessed with its own boundary proof and state-count cost. Historical novelty of the finite-frame bridge has not been established by this bounded literature review.
