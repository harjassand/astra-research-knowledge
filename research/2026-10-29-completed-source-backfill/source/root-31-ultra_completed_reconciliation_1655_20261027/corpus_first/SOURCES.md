- [Lami-Huber2016](https://arxiv.org/html/1603.02158), arXiv1603.02158v1, Theorem7 and proof, Corollary8 and proof: governing exact EA classification. The full proof was read and its printed theorem page visually checked. The source allows positive local depolarizing maps beyond their CP interval. Full transposition therefore maps its classification directly to the physical Werner channels used here. gamma<=alpha+beta+2, with alpha=beta=-1/c, gamma=1/c^2, is exactly the sharpc boundary. A frontier/novelty claim was stopped when this stronger prior was found. The source already offers a constructive Hadamard-CP separability proof.
- [Filippov-Ziman2013v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.6525): fully read, including the original depolarizing EA bounds. Its gap is historical and was closed by Lami-Huber2016. It must not be described as a current open problem.
- [Harrow-Nielsen2003](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0301108), Theorem4, its complete sampling/error proof, and SectionIII.C: equally informed tractability comparator. The theorem explicitly extends to bounded-arity qudit gates and separability-preserving gates. It includes acquisition by a finite-precision search; our fixed rational frame is narrower and does not newly establish efficient sampling in this regime.
- [Lami-Giovannetti2015v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.2517v4), Werner-channel Example5 and generalized filtering discussion: unfiltered/unitary filtered index2 and the extreme channel's nonunitary-filter protection were known. This prevents confusing ordinary iteration with arbitrary filtering. Our specialc boundary is credited to the stronger2016 classification.
- [Vieira-Ku-Budroni2025v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.16789v2), temporal classical model and memory-count discussion: an EB channel's intermediate state count is part of the comparison. Our fixed-frame state count, static kernel table and randomness must likewise be charged. No same-dimension classicality claim is made.
- [Fanizza-Lumbreras-Winter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.11225), published2024: Sections2.1-2.3 and the noisy-process discussion provide the finite-positive-realization criterion and explicit finite-quantum/infinite-classical process separation motivating the target.
