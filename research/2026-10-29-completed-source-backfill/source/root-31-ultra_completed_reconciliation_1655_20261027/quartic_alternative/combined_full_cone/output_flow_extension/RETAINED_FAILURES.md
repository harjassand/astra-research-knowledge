# Retained alternatives and limitations

The old common heat functional has nonzero mixed-flow pairings, so it
cannot simply be reused for every real skew coefficient. A new constant
common dual constrained to annihilate all nine mixed-energy channels did
not return a negative objective; this is not an impossibility proof for
all possible common parameter-dual constructions.

The pure-target gamma-zero flow primal returned a negative numerical
margin, but its dual paired the determinant-square channels with the
wrong signs for unrestricted nonnegative removal. A constant pure dual
annihilating both mixed channels also failed to yield a negative objective.
Those numerical results are not the theorem. The tight exact ROC gamma
gate and exact affine family supply the all-parameter proof.

The operator averaging argument keeps the complete target energy fixed.
An unknown PC component, unknown heat component and unknown general flow
create a bilinear coupling; their simultaneous average cannot be replaced
by the product of their separate averages. No result for that enlarged
combined class is asserted.

The no-go applies to closed homogeneous-quadratic two-forms and a global
bilinear polynomial SOS density. It does not exclude a nonnegative
non-SOS density, a norm-multiplied heat density, other corrections or
nonlinear flows. It is not a statement that the energy is non-QC.
