# Independent scientific choice

27 October 2026. Before any exchange of developing choices with the other
initial route, I selected a uniform-gap construction for the pure spin-two
antiferromagnetic Heisenberg chain. The consequential operation is turning
finite, rigorously bounded thermodynamic data into a proof for every sufficiently
large even ring, beyond frustration-free Hamiltonians.

The spin-one gap is **not** an open target here. OpenAI's 24 September paper at
repository revision 7ef5c0cc5ca4b0e072b7a050fca09ab24bc6fa3a proves an explicit
positive gap on all sufficiently large even rings. Its directory last changed
at adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a. Tasaki's primary exposition, revised
19 October, already extends the analytic two-purity criterion to every spin and
explicitly suggests spin two as a further computational target. I claim neither
that extension nor the purity bootstrap.

The hard bridge to attack is rigorous finite initialization at a much smaller
gap and longer correlation length: replace the original full exponential-column
thermal calculation with low-energy spectra **plus a certified spectral-count
or trace tail**. A variational MPS, an extrapolated gap, or a sampled collection
of low eigenvalues does not supply that tail. I will construct the actual spin
two Hamiltonians, test the bootstrap's finite-input tolerance, and retain all
cancellation, dimension, and certification costs. This choice is not a claim
that a spin-two proof is absent from every source; the versioned primary search
has identified no such proof, and further source checking remains active.

Selective Astra search at the common published revision returned no Haldane
entry. N778/N779 provide environment-uniform energy/free-energy comparisons;
their packets retain source-derived/unreviewed status and do not automatically
certify a gap or count low-energy states. Their entropy penalty must be paid.

The strongest ordinary comparators include the original full-column proof,
symmetry-resolved exact diagonalization, DMRG/MPS, sign-free quantum Monte Carlo,
MPO thermal methods, validated inertia/trace methods, and certified gap SDP
hierarchies with attention to whether their bound is an upper or a lower bound.
