  Dacorogna--Marcellini Energy*, arXiv:2608.06367v1.
  https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06367v1

  isotropic rank-one convex integrands*, arXiv:2112.10563v1.
  https://arxiv.org/html/2112.10563v1

  relevant conformal-matrix discussion, and the displayed Proposition 3.7

  This source does not decide QC of the new polynomial.
