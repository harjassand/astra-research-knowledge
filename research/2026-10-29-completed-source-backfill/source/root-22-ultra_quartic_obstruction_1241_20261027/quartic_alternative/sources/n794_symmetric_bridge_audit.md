## 2. The proposed symmetries are exact

Let

`S=[[0,1],[1,0]]`, `D=diag(1,-1)`, `J=diag(1,-1)`.

- Under `A -> AS`, `u` and `v` exchange, `w` is preserved, and `q` reverses. Thus `q(u-v)` is preserved and `F(AS)=F(A)`.
- Under `A -> JAD`, the coordinates become `(a,-b,-c,d)`. Consequently `u,v,q` are preserved and `w` reverses. Thus `F(JAD)=F(A)`.

The right actions generated by `S,D` form a finite dihedral group. The left action associated with `D` is `J`, and the left action associated with `S` is identity. This defines a finite group of actual symmetries of `F`; using `D` alone without `J` would be incorrect.

The polynomial `q²` is invariant under every one of these orthogonal left/right actions. Hence the same symmetries hold for `G=F-gamma q²`, for every fixed real gamma.

## 3. Exact covariance of the heat/closed-form/SOS certificate

Suppose, for some constant symmetric positive-definite spatial diffusion matrix `M`, a fixed `gamma>=0`, a closed homogeneous-quadratic matrix-space two-form `omega`, and a bilinear SOS Gram `R0>=0`, there is an identity on all compatible jets

`E_(M,G)(A;B1,B2) + omega_A(B1,B2) = Z^T R0 Z`,

where

`E_(M,G) = sum_(i,j=1,2) Mij D²G(A)[Bi,Bj]`

and compatibility is `B1 e2 = B2 e1`.

For a symmetry pair `(L,R)`, set `T(A)=LAR` and transform jets by

`B'_i = T(sum_k R_(ki) B_k)`.

These are precisely the jets of `v'(x)=L v(Rx)`; compatibility is preserved. Invariance of `G` gives

`E_(M,G)(T(A);B'_1,B'_2) = E_(R M R^T,G)(A;B1,B2)`.

Alternation gives

`omega_(T(A))(B'_1,B'_2) = det(R) (T^*omega)_A(B1,B2)`.

Thus the transformed correction is `det(R) T^*omega`. It remains homogeneous quadratic and closed, since exterior differentiation commutes with pullback. The 24 bilinear monomials transform by an invertible linear substitution, so the transformed Gram is a congruence of `R0` and remains PSD. No orthogonality of the particular unnormalized six-jet coordinates is needed.

Average these transformed identities over the finite symmetry group. Conjugation by `D` kills the off-diagonal entry of `M`; conjugation by `S` equalizes the diagonal entries. Therefore

`average_R R M R^T = (tr M/2) I = mu I`, with `mu>0`.

Closedness and the SOS cone are preserved by this finite average. Dividing by `mu` gives an **identity-diffusion** closed-quadratic-two-form/bilinear-SOS certificate for the same `F-gamma q²`. The value of gamma is unchanged.

Consequently:

**An exact separator excluding the full identity-diffusion cone for every `gamma>=0` also excludes every constant-SPD spatial-diffusion certificate of this same closed-form/SOS type for this candidate.**

This argument does not require the general anisotropic-certificate parameter set to be convex: it averages one fixed gamma and the finite orbit of one hypothetical certificate.

## 4. Determinant-subtraction and dual-sign caveat

A separator only at `gamma=0` is insufficient. The corpus explicitly proves that `q²` is quasiconvex but its full energy need not decrease under componentwise heat: `updates/EM/package/genesis_compatibility/recovered/astra_genesis_compatibility/RESEARCH_NOTE.md`, section 6, especially lines 234–260. Thus one must not assume that adding `gamma q²` preserves the direct heat/SOS certificate cone.

For a dual functional `ell` annihilating every admitted closed-form correction and nonnegative on every bilinear SOS, it is sufficient to certify exactly

`ell(E_I F)<0` and `ell(E_I q²)>=0`.

Then for every `gamma>=0`,

`ell(E_I(F-gamma q²)) = ell(E_I F)-gamma ell(E_I q²)<0`.

Strict positivity on the second quantity is convenient for rationalization but exact zero would also suffice. Approximate zero has no such implication. Adding a sufficiently small rational positive dual interior can provide a margin, provided the first strict inequality and exact annihilator constraints survive; these are obligations for the actual rational certificate, not consequences of numerical feasibility.
