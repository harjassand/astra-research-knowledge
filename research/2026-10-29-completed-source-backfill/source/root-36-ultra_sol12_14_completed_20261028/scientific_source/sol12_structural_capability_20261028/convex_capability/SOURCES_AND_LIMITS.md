# Primary comparison and exact hypothesis boundary

Sources were read directly on28 October2026. No claim that a prior
theorem directly proves the specialized70 representation is made.

* [Azagra–Le Gruyer–Mudarra, Theorem2.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.02235):
  a convex C1,1 extension from a jet with a finite CW1,1 constant M,
  with a convex-envelope formula. This is established jet-extension
  machinery. It is not a global analytic finite-SDP theorem, and an
  unbounded quartic jet need not have one global gradient-Lipschitz M.
* [Azagra–Mudarra, Theorem1.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.05226):
  smooth convex extension under Whitney/convex-Whitney conditions on a
  compact convex set. The full minors graph is noncompact and nonconvex,
  so those hypotheses cannot simply be substituted. This source also
  makes clear that positivity of a Hessian alone is not a general
  smooth-extension criterion.
* [Helton–Nie](https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.4068) and their
  [local-to-global work](https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.4017): their retrieved
  primary abstracts give SDP representation conditions using positive
  Lagrange Hessians, SOS concavity and curvature, principally on compact
  semialgebraic sets. Thus SOS/curvature-to-SDP machinery is prior;
  the literal fixed70, noncompact, graph-matching construction remains
  a specialized result rather than a new generic SDP method.
* [Miroshnikov–Tzavaras](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.0820), introduction,
  properties E1–E3 and H1–H5: polyconvex elastodynamics has an established
  augmented convex-entropy formulation, with compatibility constraints.
  The quantitative convergence theorem imposes global smoothness and
  growth hypotheses. Our70 lift is smooth only near the graph, and those
  global assumptions have not been checked. Merely writing its augmented
  equations does not acquire that convergence result.
* [Christoforou–Galanopoulou–Tzavaras](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.01582),
  Section2: symmetrizable polyconvex thermoelastic extensions and their
  constraint preservation are established. Neither the temperature
  structure nor physical constitutive calibration has been supplied by
  the present quartic toy model.
