Absence of an identical statement in these sources is not a novelty proof.

   envelopes. Read in author-uploaded full text. These supply the ordinary

   inequalities and trace-exponential inequalities are dual, including

   variational proof in the primary journal text. Our entropy/dual
   equivalence and SDPI interpretation are direct instances, not new
