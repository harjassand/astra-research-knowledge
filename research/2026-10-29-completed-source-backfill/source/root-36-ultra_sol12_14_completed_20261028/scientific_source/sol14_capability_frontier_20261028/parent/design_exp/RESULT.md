# Mechanism failure leaves a different entropy question

The peer's weighted-design hemisphere criterion exactly decides its
full convex-order mechanism. Failure of that mechanism does not decide
the exponential/entropy inequality. Root therefore tested a distinct
constructive route to actual entropy counterexamples.

For rational y in the qutrit probability simplex, a uniform independent
three-phase twirl of (sqrt(y0),sqrt(y1),sqrt(y2)) converts the six
Dirichlet moment constraints into a finite projective2design. A linear
program can optimize exp(2 k dot y) at fixed diagonal K over such
cubature weights. A positive result would need exact moment recovery
and rigorous exponential/entropy certification before being a counterexample.

The executed bounded screen used grids12,24,48 and25 K choices per grid.
All75 LP values stayed below the conjectured exponential bound; the
largest log ratio was about-0.00129225. PROPOSALS.json retains every
support and residual. Cost0.167s after imports. This is a floating finite
screen, not a global theorem, an LP upper certificate over the continuous
simplex, or a hardness result. No certified entropy counterexample was
acquired and no further unstructured runs are left running.
