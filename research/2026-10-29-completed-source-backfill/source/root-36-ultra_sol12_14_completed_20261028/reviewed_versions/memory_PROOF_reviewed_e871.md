## General sufficient receiver

Let S_i=|s_i><s_i| be a qualified qutrit SIC, p a probability vector,
sigma_i arbitrary finite-dimensional density matrices, and

    Omega_IL = sum_i p_i |i><i| tensor sigma_i,
    M_QL = sum_i p_i S_i tensor sigma_i, C = sum_i p_i sigma_i.

Write d(p)=2S(sum_i p_i S_i)-H(p), chi=I(I:L)_Omega. The exact identity

    2S(Q|L)_M-S(I|L)_Omega
      = d(p)+chi-2I(Q:L)_M

and ordinary data processing I(Q:L)<=chi prove

    2S(Q|L)-S(I|L) >= d(p)-chi.                         (1)

Consequently any acquired upper bound on chi below an acquired lower
bound on d(p) pays the matrix-weight sign. No commuting weights,
classical memory, memory dimension bound, entropy whitening or assumed
complete coefficient is needed. A Holevo-information acquisition remains
an input; it is not inferred from knowing p. The unrestricted problem
requires a sharper structural inequality when this budget fails.
