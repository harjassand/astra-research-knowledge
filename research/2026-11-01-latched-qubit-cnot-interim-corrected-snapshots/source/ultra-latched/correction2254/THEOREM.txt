2. Actual flag-conditioned Hamiltonian, before arity compilation
================================================================
Add m=n=N/2 PHYSICAL flag qubits f_i, one per DATA pair a_i,b_i. Define

 n_i=|1><1|_fi, U_i=CNOT_(a_i -> b_i), U_all=product_i U_i,

not a physical gate pulse, an ideal readout, or an instruction to the

On logical Paulis this follows from X_Aj->X_Aj X_Bj, Z_Bj->Z_Aj Z_Bj,
with Z_Aj and X_Bj unchanged, and the identical stabilizer relations.

where omega_f can be any fixed finite real number. Old losses on C are
unchanged: W acts only on DATA and flags. Add ONE-BODY flag losses
sqrt(Gamma_f) sigma^-_fi, with Gamma_f chosen later.

All Hamiltonian terms in (3) commute with EVERY n_i, not just their sum.
For a DATA-port interaction h P_a X_c, conjugation produces

 h/2 [P_a X_c + P_a Z_f X_c
                  +(U_i P_a U_i)X_c -(U_i P_a U_i)Z_f X_c],   (4)

when P_a changes under CNOT; when unchanged it remains h P_a X_c.
U_i P_a U_i has at most two DATA Paulis. Thus maximum arity is FOUR,
with at most four Pauli monomials per affected old term. DATA fields
have arity at most three. Controller-only triples remain unchanged.
There are no primitive syndrome-table projectors in this description.
