Physical operations and full costs
==================================
The protected hardware has two identical CSS/Tanner DATA blocks, every
branch controller, every old/new mediator, and one physical flag per
DATA pair. Its Hamiltonian is static. Its nominal dissipators are local
one-qubit lowering losses. No intermediate measurements, externally
timed gates or ideal reset cycles occur during its autonomous operation.

The physical input is ideal encoded unknown DATA and all flags |0> for
storage or |1> for the one-shot gate. All other controllers/mediators
may be arbitrary and correlated with logical input, bath and reference.
The m=N/2 initialized pure flags are a paid preparation resource. They
are not reusable pure scratch. After operation they and the bath are
retained. A reservoir derivation, entropy/energy cost and noisy initial
encoding/flag factory are not supplied by the abstract one-body losses.
Finite upward flag/controller rates and calibration errors during
operation ARE admitted at the theorem's positive noise budget.

Final readout is the same IDEAL ordinary two-block DATA syndrome recovery
for storage and gate, followed by fixed decoding. It never reads flags
or controllers and contains no CNOT. Its physical noisy implementation,
time, scratch, and connectivity are unearned output resources. No ideal
native forest inverse is included in this qubit realization's readout;
small compiler frames are handled by positive comparisons in the proof.

The gate is the simultaneous CNOT on all k matched logical pairs. It is
not addressability of one chosen logical pair. The finished operation
can be held protected; a new gate, reusable sequencing, non-Clifford
logic and a universal autonomous processor are separate problems.

Explicit overhead bound
=======================
Let nu_m N bound the SOURCE three-body/degree-three memory's qubits,
including DATA. Its number of nontrivial coupling terms is at most
(3/2)nu_m N. After W, at most four terms replace each, and at most 3N
original DATA fields each contribute four monomials. Thus a loose bound
on nontrivial conditional couplings is

 m_g <= (6 nu_m+12)N.

One four-to-three stage adds at most m_g mediators and leaves at most
m_3<=2m_g couplings. Port isolation adds <=3m_3 mediators; fresh-port
fork reduction adds <=9m_3. Consequently

 N_physical <=(nu_m+1/2)N+25m_g
             <=(151nu_m+301)N.                              (R1)

This loose linear bound is an existence statement with a potentially
astronomical multiplier, not an efficient device. The source already has
nu_m <=9+13h(8/c)4^h with huge fixed code/table support h. The final
positive physical rate is therefore potentially unusably small.

Before the final compiler, coupling count per qubit is <=48: a DATA pair
has six source couplers and six possible DATA fields, each expanding
into at most four monomials. Other old controllers have count <=12;
first-stage mediators have count two. The extra stage count can be
bounded by 1+3+2ceil(log2 48)=16. Every stage needs its own finite scale
and excitation/path/source allowance. This can worsen the already severe
hierarchy. After all stages, maximum arity, coupling count and ordinary
nominal graph degree are three. Original flags have at most one Z X_port
coupling; original DATA use only pairwise endpoint couplings.

Couplings can be long range. Degree three is not 2D/3D locality, bounded
wire length, finite propagation delay in a geometric embedding, or
microscopic hardware availability. Controller-only XXX triples remain
primitive resources. The earlier two-body-only obstruction is preserved;
this gate construction does not solve it.

Nonzero noise allowance on the ENTIRE hardware
==============================================
The actual perturbation bound is sum_Z ||V_Z||diamond<=delta N at every
time, with fixed q physical-qubit support, arbitrary common finite bath,
and actual total GKSL/CPTP dynamics. No exact reference balance appears.
All DATA, controller, mediator, flag and coupler faults count. Norms of
signed calibration differences count by absolute norm, not cancellation.

For M=N_physical<=mu N, a familiar sufficient incident-norm condition is

 sup_i sum_(Z containing i)||V_Z||diamond<=eta_phys,
 eta_phys<=delta/mu.                                        (R2)

This gives sum_Z norms<=eta_phys M<=delta N. eta_phys is positive and
independent of size, but can be extremely small. Noise partners need
not be geometric or fixed over time. The degree-three claim concerns
the NOMINAL Hamiltonian, not the union of all admitted noise supports.

For example a field/coupler calibration dh costs <=2||dh||; a gain jump
of rate u costs <=2u; a rate change dg in D[sigma^-] costs <=2|dg|;
J->J+E costs <=4||J||||E||+2||E||^2. Relative precision must be assessed
against actual finite coupler/gap/loss strengths at EVERY compiler stage.
Its numerical tolerance is not supplied just by saying 'fixed threshold'.

A small UNKNOWN fraction of completely wrong bounded components is also
covered within the declared qubit model. If changing one component alters
bounded-body terms of total generator-difference norm <=R_def, then fM
defective components cost <=f mu R_def N. Choose
f<=delta/(mu R_def). This is a positive size-independent fraction, even
for a missing finite-rate reset. It excludes undeclared leakage levels,
unbounded fanout/rates and a faulty global decoder. Such existence-level
defect tolerance may have an unusably small numerical constant.

Choose compiler constants first, then Gamma_f within the remaining
certificate noise allowance, then delta below both memory and flag-count
requirements. The latency is

 tau_gate = log(4/a)/[Gamma_f(1-e^-1)], a=rho/4.

It is size-independent but can be enormous. This requires continuous
correction/driven reservoir service for the full latency and holding
time. No calculation of advantageous wall-clock time, power, bandwidth,
control precision or total experiment cost has been earned.

Finite checks and what they actually test
========================================
coherent_decoder_controls.py uses two [[7,1,3]] Steane blocks and the full
two-logical-qubit Choi reference. The ordinary decoder is fixed before
the target is defined. It tests coherent missing-column flags correlated
with a retained bath, plus coherent Y corruption on the same column.
The joint decoded logical/reference/flag/bath factorization discrepancy
is zero to returned floating precision; all 42 single-DATA Pauli cases
also pass. Time-zero output is IDENTITY: its Choi trace distance from
CNOT is sqrt(3). Two unfinished columns produce distance 0.875. This is
finite code algebra, not controller dynamics or an asymptotic threshold.

quantum_flag_controls.py builds a FULL five-qubit DATA/controller/flag/
bath GKSL model. It includes coherent flag-controller and DATA-bath
couplings, a controller loss-basis error, upward flag loss, all DATA
axes and a signed negative loss calibration. Exact physical/frame
superoperator equality passes. The complete-loss outgoing identity
residual is 8.21e-17; the nominal moment identity residual is 2.23e-16.
Its weighted fault source is 3.59e-5 below the charged bound 0.001833.
The actual flag population at time 60 is 6.39e-5. It is not a code memory.

compiler_flag_controls.py constructs all terms, fields and one-body
mediators for a flag-conditioned noncommuting nine-qubit target.
Illustrative compilation uses 338 qubits and has maximum Hamiltonian
arity/coupling count/graph degree 3, with the original flag in exactly
one Z coupling. Full quantum subdivision/frame commutators with flag
number vanish. The transformed flag-loss operator differs by 0.04419:
loss dressing is real and is paid, rather than omitted. These are
illustrative scales, not a phase-qualified Tanner parameter set.

stochastic_gate_controls.py samples 3000 quantum stabilizer trajectories
per setting on two Steane blocks, retaining their Choi reference.
IMPORTANT: its memory primitive is IDEAL full-block syndrome recovery
at rate 1, NOT the finite-controller/three-body hardware. This isolates
the gate mechanism; it does not simulate or validate the full device.
The autonomous recovery is correctly flag-conjugated in physical space.
All DATA Pauli axes and upward flag pumping are included in the noisy
case. Raw trajectories use the exact Lindblad event law for this fixture.

Meaningful failures and unresolved frontier
===========================================
The fast-flag control sets Gamma_f=1000 with the same rate 1 recovery.
All flags finish, but target CNOT Choi fidelity is 0.25: approximately
the IDENTITY operation. Completion does not certify the logical gate.
The positive memory certificate AND sufficiently slow flag faults are
essential, not just a final flag-density bound.

The two-unfinished-column code control fails at distance 0.875. A finite
code does not correct arbitrarily many unfinished flags. Linear code
distance and the extensive tail, not all-finished waiting, earn the
family result. Conversely a good final flag count alone cannot repair
an earlier lost logical state, as the fast-flag case demonstrates.

The current Astra clock/data bridge N1345 already records an obstruction
to promoting liveness or physical-time fault insertions into a reusable
noisy correction scheduler. Its clean-clock result is accepted; the
generic DATA semantic bridge remains open. The present one-shot gate
avoids that bridge, and does not pretend to solve it. N1256's paid
depolarizing-ballast conversion to arbitrary CPTP events likewise does
not supply a missing scheduler threshold or turn nonunital noise into
the primary 2D paper's unitary-jump noise class or complete its outline.
