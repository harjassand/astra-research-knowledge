    - heading "1. Consequential target" [level=2]
    - paragraph: Can we discover and certify near-optimal interventions in a complex physical system without first identifying its microscopic dynamics?
    - paragraph: A successful general solution would substantially change how we control stochastic chemical reactions, molecular assembly, biological processes and energy systems.
