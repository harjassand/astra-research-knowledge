Completed result: adaptive thermodynamic inference with paid sampling

I selected and completed a new constructive target: a scientific computation that learns which experiments to run, reconstructs a free-energy landscape, and decides when to stop while retaining a rigorous confidence guarantee—and actually generates the equilibrium samples that the inference requires.

The result includes a variance correction that recovers Bennett acceptance-ratio efficiency, exact rational certificates, explicit stopping bounds, a protocol learner with a proved cost objective, and an implemented equilibrium sampler. The full construction was executed on a network of four nine-spin Hamiltonians.

The deliverables are:

Astra_Adaptive_Thermodynamic_Inference.pdf — the 15-page report, with theorem statements, proofs, costs, comparisons, and results.
Astra_Adaptive_Thermodynamic_Inference_Package.zip — implementations, complete numerical records, independent proofs, exact Astra sources, and the earlier arithmetic and quantum archive preserved unchanged.

The organising principle is that learning the experimental design and certifying the scientific conclusion can use the same accumulating data. The certification need not trust the learner’s ranking, assume Gaussian work, or hide equilibrium preparation behind a sampling oracle.

9. Scientific contribution and preserved alternatives

The completed result joins several strong existing operations into one explicit procedure: efficient bidirectional estimation, adaptive design, time-uniform confidence, exact arithmetic, network consistency, and acquired equilibrium sampling. Its central correction and resource proofs specify how these operations can coexist without giving the controller unearned information.

The scope remains concrete. The construction supplies exact simulation for the stated finite models. It does not supply laboratory equilibrium preparation, remove critical slowing down, acquire unknown instrument calibration, or demonstrate a practical advantage over every established free-energy method. Historical priority of the exact combined construction remains unestablished.

The separately initialised constructive perspectives also produced two retained results:

Quantum broadcasting within a complete-profile class. Every full-Choi-rank channel has an explicitly compiled representative with the same complete maximal/RLD profile and a symmetric extension to any prescribed finite number of receivers. The two-receiver case gives an antidegradable, zero-capacity representative. The actual heralded inverse and its success probability are included; failed unknown inputs remain consumed. The rank-deficient converse remains open.
A sharp bosonic resource frontier from accepted N557. The independent attack obtained an exact worst-case coherent-information frontier under entropy and energy caps, together with a paid finite-register cutoff and a terminating universal distillation compiler. Its decoder need not be efficient. The older qEPI already supplies the qualitative finite-gain capability, so the earned improvement is the sharp resource threshold.

The previous arithmetic bijection, exact ensembles, cross-ring converter, all-depth law, and earlier quantum constructions are embedded unchanged in the new package. Their fixed-grade complexity, supplied arithmetic interfaces, and conversion limits remain explicit. Channel compilation from a description remains distinct from converting an existing device output, with success, failure, and unknown-input costs paid.

Ordinary unassisted quantum-capacity approximation remains a separate unresolved question.
