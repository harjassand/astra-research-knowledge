# P2 extension: physically sourced alpha-amine/thiol failure chemistry and a smaller viable construction class
**3 November 2026.** An independent positive physical-mechanism extension to README, still not an integrated device or measured production advantage. This amendment does not replace earlier construction/negative findings.

## The new physical observation is chemical, not another DNA receipt
Native chemical ligation between purified old C-terminal thioester A and a single incoming N-terminal cysteine B produces a reversible S-linked intermediate T, then an intramolecular S→N shift consumes B's **N-terminal alpha amine** and forms the intended stable backbone amide C. Wrong S-linked bonds keep B's free alpha amine; some wrong stable SIDE attachments W also preserve B's alpha amine. In the restricted class in which (a) old A's free N terminus is protected, (b) only one purified B identity occupies each reaction chamber, (c) all other reactive amines/electrophiles are protected, (d) the intended N-alpha amide is the only allowed stable interfragment chemistry, the conjunction of (i) after donor-peel + sulfur-thioester challenge, a stable A-to-B connection, and (ii) no accessible B N-terminal 1,2-aminothiol, is chemically equivalent to intended NCL completion under that *chemical grammar*. This is a structural proof, not a generic chemical fidelity assay.

**Real failure-discriminating reagent:** 2-cyanobenzothiazole (CBT) reacts selectively with free N-terminal cysteine **1,2-aminothiol** to form a thiazoline; this is physically demonstrated with k2 about **9.19 M^-1 s^-1** under its cited aqueous assay, PMID 25560068, original https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4878437/. NCL product leaves cysteine thiol free but consumes its alpha-amine; internal monothiols typically form reversible CBT adducts instead of stable terminal thiazoline, but **not universally**: 2020 single-molecule study https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7496719/ reports potentially persistent internal-cysteine thiol monoadducts in some sequence/geometry regimes. 2-pyridinecarboxaldehydes (2PCA) provide a different terminal alpha-amine chemical probe, 2015 original DOI 10.1038/nchembio.1792, but 2025 kinetic re-examination DOI 10.1021/jacsau.5c00238 reveals slow and reversible chemistry and 2PCA fails at some terminal sequence contexts (e.g. Pro at position 2). Neither is a free, perfectly selective reader.

**Proposed real operation:** after reversible donor docking has been broken, challenge residual S-thioesters and wash so U/T return to independent fragments; then expose only physically retained A-B candidate to a CBT probe covalently carrying an affinity handle. Incomplete/wrong-side products whose free N-terminal cysteine reacts are captured and discarded by an external affinity medium; correctly N-acylated products should remain in the effluent. Reagent itself is a destructive selective *rejector* of invalid modules; no hidden thermal innovations or perfect digital failure bit is required. However this must work on the same molecule and with source-qualified thiol side reactions, handle interference, tag removal, and actual old-unit protection. It is currently an assembled **hypothesis**, not experimentally demonstrated.

## Derive measured-input selectivity and damage headroom rather than assuming a magic error number
For a restricted, well-mixed single-reaction compartment, let incoming free-N-Cys invalid product W be irreversibly affinity-tagged at pseudo-first-order rate kW = (9.19 M^-1 s^-1)*[CBT], conditional on accessible terminal Cys. Let C have *unwanted* irreversible affinity capture or product damage at unknown hazard kC. Conditional W/C odds after challenge t satisfy
  r_after = r_before * exp[-(kW-kC)t]
in this exact constant-hazard model. Source assays acquire kW for one model class, but **DO NOT provide** kC for our actual growing product, nor a uniform bound on all inaccessible W conformations. A physical reaction mechanism must also carry W in the flow, side-product and incomplete N-terminus subclasses; a single kW is not universal. Without chemical access, r_after cannot be certified.

For M independently qualified fragments, N=M-1 peptide joins, prior wrong/correct odds r, and final wrong-junction budget epsW, require
  t >= log(N*r/epsW)/(kW-kC).
Even when the filter enriches purity, global challenge can damage correct old bonds. For a balanced M=2^L tree, total correct bond exposure B=M*L-(M-1); ideal sequential requires B=M(M-1)/2. If kC bounds relevant irreversible damage uniformly, correct-bond survival >= exp(-kC*t*B), for successful ancestry without extra repeats. At [CBT]=1mM, kW≈0.00919/s (published k2, **illustrative input concentration**):
- **M=1024 fragments, r=0.01, epsW=0.005**: t>=~829.56s (if kC negligible), Btree=9217, Bserial=523776. To preserve >=99.5% of correct bonds through this challenge requires kC<=6.56e-10/s for tree (an effective kW/kC of >=14 million) versus <=1.15e-11/s serial. This is a decisive **unmeasured** and very demanding gate, not evidence it is satisfied.
- **M=64 prequalified peptide fragments of 16 amino acids each, ~1024 residues total**: only 63 interfragment joins; t>=526.25s, tree B=321, serial B=2016. To preserve >=99.5% correct bonds through this challenge, kC<=2.97e-8/s, effective kW/kC >=310,000. The six ideal parallel tree levels require ~52.6 min total CBT challenge time, BEFORE peptide synthesis, multi-hour NCL, handling, washing, tagging, affinity isolation, chemical readback, retries and wasted modules. Hypothetical kC=1e-8/s gives modeled retention 99.83% over those ideal exposures; no published rate for that system validates it.
Thus **reducing the number of chemically distinct joins by prebuilding and prequalifying 16-residue modules** buys a much larger real physical margin than improving a stochastic likelihood ratio. It is established convergent-synthesis geometry, now connected to an explicit chemical rejector. The 16-aa modules cannot be treated as free; their own SPPS purity, yields, tags and stereochemistry must be fully charged.

## Positive structural and negative indistinguishability table
Observed post-donor peel:
- U (unjoined): no product-to-donor stable bridge; rejected by connectivity/flush.
- T (S-linked): apparent product bridge, but alpha-amine remains unconsumed; post-thiolysis disappears or becomes CBT-tagged; source may contain unusually inaccessible species.
- W_side (wrong permanent side bond): bridge survives but incoming N-Cys alpha amine stays free; CBT labels/removes if sufficiently accessible.
- C (true native amide): bridge survives and incoming N-Cys alpha amine has been consumed; nominally CBT-negative.
- W_alpha_wrong (wrong donor identity or wrong alpha-amide connection): BOTH tests look like C. Only verified chemical input isolation/functional-group grammar, or independent structure-resolving analysis, rules it out. A DNA-only observation is **provably insufficient**.
- C_damaged or inaccessible W may confound both probes; no hidden-mode uniform claim.

This is a physical **two-predicate assay proposal** built from established distinct chemical functional-group transformations, not a generalized perfect molecule-verification oracle. Native peptide synthesis, staged NCL, high-purity module selection, and LC-MS/MS are the strong current comparator, not a passive reporter.

## Decisive sequence-constrained pilot
Single original support, one purified N-terminal-Cys B (known non-Pro second residue for optional 2PCA), old protected A with only one C-terminal thioester. Use true NCL, separately pre-enriched S-thioester intermediate, hydrolyzed A, and a deliberately wrong stable side-bond analog. Subject each to **the same** DNA docking displacement, wash, thiolysis, CBT-affinity trap and old-to-new transfer. Independently determine final backbone junction by LC-MS/MS, and determine soluble/adsorbed fragment inventories. A paired experiment tests kW and kC simultaneously with increasing product-module size, including repeated challenge. Source-reported 9.19 M^-1s^-1 for isolated free Cys and the 2019 3-fragment templated NCL are *qualified components*, not integrated rates. Reject if correct internal cysteines acquire persistent CBT modification above the required bound, if wrong stable N-alpha bonds occur, if old DNA handles inhibit chemistry, or if LC-MS/MS contradicts the two-predicate signal.
