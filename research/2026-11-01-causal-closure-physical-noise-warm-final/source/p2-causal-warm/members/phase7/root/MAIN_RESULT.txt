1. RESULT AND SCIENTIFIC DECISION
=================================

I replaced the requirement that every meter return near vacuum by a more
precise operational requirement: the complete joint action of the apparatus,
including its unobserved output, retained state, and recorded outcomes, must
remain in the code's fixed gauge algebra or a declared correctable extension;
the actual receiver must continue to deliver the syndrome information needed
by the implemented recovery. Both requirements must also cover idle and
basis-change intervals. A good reported parity bit alone is insufficient.

The resulting construction has four connected parts:

 * A finite two-mode physical parity operation whose nominal whole-space
   map preserves both parity sectors for arbitrary initial oscillator state.
 * A finite pre/post homodyne receiver that rejects both unknown initial
   mode operators, with an exact complete instrument and a quantified signal
   penalty. A homogeneous fresh thermal line is allowed in a specified case.
 * An actual repeated Bacon-Shor receiver that tolerates an error left from
   the preceding cycle plus a new localized fault, without resetting memory.
   Bounded persistent strong memory and permanently wrong reports have
   explicit, separately charged extensions.
 * A complete repeated encoded-CNOT transaction using an ordinary hot-bus
   entangler, real finite preparation/readout, and a redundant classical
   controller. The same error-family boundary is restored after the gate.

These are exact results in the stated Hamiltonian/instrument model and
deterministic circuit fault contract. They are not a demonstrated hardware
advantage, a new general threshold theorem, or a measured logical error rate.
The key unresolved step is a quantitatively adequate joint admission of
continuous weak non-gauge noise, including intrinsic loss and control error,
over the actual retained-state operation history. The derivations below
make that step much more specific and prevent a false independence argument.

The ordinary competition is strong. Warm-bus gates, interaction-algebra
QEC, non-Markovian fault tolerance, and complete GPI cat gadgets already
establish broad possibilities. I adopted their useful mechanisms rather
than claiming that retaining quantum memory is itself a new capability.
The enabling target of this continuation is narrower: finite syndrome
extraction with an unprepared reciprocal apparatus, without a per-use
purification delay, joined to an actual repeated consumer.

3. A PHYSICAL HALF-TURN THAT PRESERVES BOTH PARITY SECTORS
=======================================================

Replace the three-mode meter by storage a and finite readout m:

 H(t)=g(t)(a^dag m+m^dag a)+chi(t) Z1 n_a
      +u(t) Z2(a+a^dag),
 L_main=sqrt(Gamma)m,  L_storage=sqrt(kappa_a)a.

The controls have disjoint supports. Readout windows have chi=u=0 and g(t)
on. The intervening mapping window has g=0 and chi,u on. Finite smooth
ramps are explicitly included. Actual switch isolation is a physical
condition; a changed pole, stray force, or residual chi is not deleted by
notation. Longitudinal forces are off during all incompatible basis changes.

First take kappa_a=0 during mapping, choose u(t)=lambda chi(t), and let
theta(T)=integral chi(t)dt=pi. With S=Z1Z2,

 H/chi=Z1(a^dag+lambda S)(a+lambda S)-lambda^2 Z1.

Since exp(-i pi Z1 n_a)=(-1)^n_a for both signs of Z1,

 U_map=exp(i pi lambda^2 Z1) D_a(-2lambda S) P_a,
 P_a=(-1)^n_a.                                      (3.1)

After the actual known local Z1 correction, the displacement depends only
on parity and the remaining oscillator parity is data independent. Equation
(3.1) is an operator identity on the entire oscillator Hilbert space. It
does not require vacuum, a coherent input, a classical mixture, or absence
of correlations with prior data and apparatus.

Because pre/post readout is data independent, the COMPLETE output and
retained-memory map is

 V=P_+ tensor V_+ +P_- tensor V_-.                    (3.2)

Every state and coherence inside either parity sector receives the same
apparatus action. This is stronger than making only one measured quadrature
parity dependent. If S is a gauge generator, (3.2) acts only on gauge and
apparatus, leaving the protected logical subsystem intact.

The forced-oscillator algebra is established qubus machinery [8]. The actual
construction here uses a positive half-turn, a conditioned force, a finite
reciprocal readout mode, and finite observation of unknown retained memory.
Its added switching controls are charged rather than treated as free.

4. THE FINITE RECEIVER AND COMPLETE INSTRUMENT
============================================

Write the main output over the entire pre/map/post interval as

 b_out(t)=H(t)x_initial+B b_fresh+s h(t),

where H has two initial-mode columns, x_initial=(a,m), and h is the injected
parity signal. For the nominal construction the whole passive scattering
map is data independent. Choose

 h_perp=(I-P_H)h,
 f=h_perp/||h_perp||,
 N_eff=||h_perp||^2.                                (4.1)

Here P_H is ordinary orthogonal projection onto the two response columns.
The actual waveform is supplied in code. In this device it has one fixed
quadrature phase and a signed real envelope, so ordinary homodyne followed
by classical temporal weighting realizes it. No quantum optical delay line
or ideal nonGaussian receiver is silently supplied.

The annihilator of f has ZERO coefficient on both initial oscillator
operators. With fresh vacuum fields it is a fresh vacuum annihilator plus
s sqrt(N_eff). The one-dimensional displaced-annihilator kernel proves

 V_s|psi> = |s sqrt(N_eff)>_f tensor W_s|psi>          (4.2)

for every initial apparatus ket. Thus the complete instrument, including
arbitrary initial data-memory-reference correlations, factors through one
known pointer while keeping the actual W_s. This proof does not substitute
a scalar overlap for unknown retained quantum memory.

With efficiency eta and a sign-threshold homodyne report,

 q=Phi[-2 sqrt(eta N_eff)], gamma=exp(-2N_eff)/2,
 e=q+gamma^2/q.                                     (4.3)

The exact selected-pointer instrument has the completely positive
decomposition

 M=(1-e)(W composed with P)+e(W composed with B),     (4.4)

where P is the correctly reported projective parity instrument. The
unnormalized bad Kraus operators are

 Kbad,+=(gamma/sqrt(q))P_+ +sqrt(q)P_-,
 Kbad,-=sqrt(q)P_+ +(gamma/sqrt(q))P_-.

Their total effect is eI. Equation(4.4) therefore has bad-component trace
exactly e times the input trace for EVERY correlated input. Conditional
mathematical tags can consequently be expanded through successive uses
without resetting memory. The controller never sees these analytical tags;
they are not perfect experimental flags. The derived constant weight is
why a readout-only product/union bound is legitimate here. It must not be
copied to other physical noise merely because its marginal rate is small.

The factor exp(-2N_eff), rather than exp(-2N_raw), is essential. Unmeasured
raw modes can have operator-valued memory overlaps. Their extra cold-state
dephasing cannot be imported as free projectivity.

For the lossy mapping with kappa_a>0 and all fresh fields vacuum, the same
selected-mode kernel proof works when the actual two initial columns are
nulled. Then W_z may depend on individual Z1. The full four-branch instrument
is explicitly

 rho -> sum_(z,z') G_r(s_z,s_z') |z><z'|
                   tensor W_z rho_(z,z') W_(z')^dag,

with the scalar Gram kernel of the selected pointer and the actual Gaussian
conditional isometries generated by the displayed Hamiltonian and outlets.
It is a complete operator-valued instrument, not a one-coherence numerical
certificate. Its nongauge W_z must be admitted by the fault analysis.

5. A MINIMAX BOUND AND A GENUINELY WARM FRESH LINE
================================================

There is a matching information-theoretic obstruction for the fixed passive
channel. If arbitrary initial apparatus states may depend on parity, the
best worst-case discrimination from the entire accessible output equals
the coherent-state Helstrom error at N_eff:

 q_minimax=[1-sqrt(1-exp(-4 eta N_eff))]/2.           (5.1)

The converse is constructive. Choose initial coherent amplitudes
alpha_s=-s H^+ P_H h. The entire accessible output then has waveform
s h_perp; the old state has canceled all signal parallel to its response.
No output POVM can beat Helstrom on those two allowed states. Conversely,
the mode in(4.1) removes every unknown initial operator and supplies exactly
that coherent pair. This proves a fixed-channel lower bound against all
receivers, not merely an optimization over linear filters.

Actual schedules use homodyne(4.3), not the uncharged optimal quantum receiver
in(5.1). Extra prior records or an energy restriction excluding the witness
change the minimax problem; an equally informed Bayesian conventional
receiver should exploit them. The result is not a global device lower bound.

A useful failure follows immediately: with only a post-observation after
one displacement, the new signal is in the same response column as the
unknown initial storage amplitude. Rejecting the latter removes ALL signal.
The finite two-mode calculation gives N_eff about 9.1e-30 in this case.
Adding a finite pre-observation makes the useful orthogonal signal nonzero.
No approach to vacuum is needed.

Fresh input need not be vacuum in every exact case. For kappa_a=0, the entire
passive scattering is common to the data branches. A homogeneous unsqueezed
white thermal main line of occupation n_b is invariant under a fixed
field-mode basis change. The selected mode factors as a thermal pointer
from arbitrary retained initial memory. Its exact outcome Gram kernel is

 G_r(s,s')=Phi[r sqrt(eta)(s+s')sqrt(N_eff)/sqrt(1+2eta n_b)]
          *exp[-(2n_b+1)N_eff(s-s')^2/2].            (5.2)

Hence q_n=Phi[-2sqrt(eta N_eff)/sqrt(1+2eta n_b)],
gamma_n=0.5exp[-2(2n_b+1)N_eff], and e_n=q_n+gamma_n^2/q_n
give the same constant-weight CP decomposition with retained memory.
The thermal discrimination and coherence factors are different; replacing
N everywhere by a single effective SNR would give the wrong instrument.

This full factorization is not asserted for arbitrary colored inputs,
squeezing, unequal-temperature ports, initial field-memory correlations,
or finite kappa_a with a warm main line and cold storage line. In those
cases a uniform report POVM may survive while the scalar full instrument
does not. Fresh fields remain a real entropy resource, as emphasized by
the retrieved Astra entropy-access context. No entropy-free correction is
claimed.

6. REAL STORAGE LOSS, NO-JUMP FILTERING, AND CONTROL ERROR
=======================================================

With finite kappa_a, set

 u(t)=lambda chi(t) exp(-kappa_a t/2), theta(T)=pi.

Integration gives the exact endpoint

 a(T)=-exp(-kappa_a T/2)a(0)
      -2lambda exp(-kappa_a T/2)S+fresh noise.       (6.1)

The unknown initial coefficient is still data independent, even with smooth
ramps. Nevertheless the storage field emitted DURING the turn depends on
individual Z1. That outlet cannot be ignored because the endpoint closes.

Define

 C=kappa_a integral exp(-kappa_a t)[1-exp(-2i theta(t))]dt,
 K=integral chi(t)exp(-kappa_a t)dt.

For the MAPPING interval, the exact relative isometry overlap within parity
s, acting on storage at the mapping entrance, is

 Q_s=V_map,-^dag V_map,+=exp(i2lambda^2K)
                       D(-lambda s)(1-C)^n D(lambda s). (6.2)

Here n is the storage number operator AT MAP ENTRY. This includes the
complete lost field, coherent action phase and no-jump filtering. It is
the correct map-interval object for nonthermal correlated memory. For the
whole pre/map/post primitive the overlap is instead
V_pre^dag[Q_s tensor I]V_pre, with the actual pre-observation isometry and
fresh fields retained. The post evolution is common to the compared branches.
The scalar 1-C is a contraction because it is a convex combination of
unit-circle phases and the final survival coefficient.

For a coherent amplitude beta at the mapping entrance,

 F_s(beta)=exp(i2lambda^2K)exp[-C|beta+lambda s|^2].  (6.3)

For an independent thermal storage state of mean n at the mapping entrance,

 F_th=exp(i2lambda^2K)/(1+nC)
      *exp[-C lambda^2/(1+nC)].                     (6.4)

The reported pZ=(1-|F|)/2 is only the corresponding same-sector coherence
diagnostic after its known phase correction. It is not a logical error
rate and is not a state-uniform fault probability. An independent truncated
Lindblad integration checks(6.4), including every jump and no-jump term:
the cutoff-60 discrepancies are below 7.4e-10 for the tested n=0,0.01,0.3.
The cutoff-40 n=0.3 result differs by 1.08e-7 and is retained rather than
silently equated with convergence of a whole nonlinear device.

An energy-constrained complete mapping-dilation bound follows from(6.2),
where E is the storage mean occupation at the mapping entrance:

 ||(V_map,+-exp(i2lambda^2K)V_map,-)|psi>||^2
 <=2|C|(sqrt(E)+|lambda|)^2.                        (6.5)

There is NO state-uniform small error at nonzero storage loss over
unbounded oscillator energies. The entire diagonal pair algebra does,
however, satisfy

 Alg(Zi,Zj)=span{I,Zi} Alg(ZiZj).                    (6.6)

For a weight-two gauge this is one physical representative times gauge,
with arbitrary operators on retained memory. Severe pair dephasing can
therefore be correctable by the real consumer. Strong disturbance in every
column eventually generates a logical operator; accurate reports alone
cannot save that situation.

Control calibration remains consequential. In the lossless model a relative
turn-area error 0.01 gives cold same-parity pZ=0.0022150 after optimal known
phase correction; at mapping n=3 the value is 0.02572. A relative area error
0.001 gives 2.2206e-5 cold and 2.7370e-4 at n=3. Thus the short operation does
not make pulse calibration irrelevant. Residual couplings during readout
can additionally scatter the old field and spoil receiver rejection.

7. REPEATED ENERGY IS BOUNDED WITHOUT BEING VACUUM
================================================

With FRESH VACUUM input fields, the nominal endpoint map
x_(k+1)=Kx_k+dS_k+fresh annihilation noise gives

 sqrt(E_n)<=||K^n||sqrt(E_0)+sum_(j=0)^(n-1)||K^j d||. (7.1)

This allows noncommuting Heisenberg parity histories; ||S_k||=1 is enough.
The finite pre/post passive matrices make ||K||<1. Their drive-generated
asymptotic ensemble mean occupation is finite even though it is reused immediately.
For arbitrary data-only sign histories during the map, a looser bound uses
p=||E_pre||, q=||E_post|| and U=integral|u|dt:

 sqrt(E_n)<=(pq)^n sqrt(E_0)+qU[1-(pq)^n]/(1-pq).    (7.2)

Thermal injection, arbitrary heating faults, changed couplings or uncontrolled
force amplitudes need additional terms. The preceding thermal-pointer theorem
does not remove thermal occupation from the apparatus energy balance.
Equations (7.1)-(7.2) retain the initial-energy transient;
the tabulated asymptotic ceilings are not universal caps on the first operation.

Crucially, these are ENSEMBLE bounds, not bounds after every normalized
measurement outcome. Postselection can produce much larger conditional
energy. A controlled displacement followed by an odd-cat selection has
unconditional occupation |alpha|^2 but conditional occupation
|alpha|^2*coth(|alpha|^2), approaching 1 as alpha goes to zero. Mean exposure
therefore cannot justify p^r probabilities for r loss events. This is the
remaining probability-admission obstruction, preserved explicitly.

8. ACTUAL REPEATED RECOVERY: OLD ERROR PLUS NEXT FAULT
====================================================

Use an R by C Bacon-Shor code with horizontal XX and vertical ZZ gauge
generators. A Pauli error is described modulo gauge by its X row-parity
vector x and Z column-parity vector z. For odd R,C, minimum-weight component
decoding corrects |x|<=(R-1)/2 and |z|<=(C-1)/2.

For a fixed exceptional dispersive-participant set D, define the CLOSED
joint algebra

 A_D=span{E_D G} tensor B(all memory,baths,old records).

E_D is any Pauli on D and G is the fixed gauge algebra. Since Paulis
normalize G, multiplication and adjoints preserve A_D. Retained apparatus
can be arbitrarily hot, strong and correlated on D without creating a new
independent site merely because it is used again. With forces off, an
unexpected chi during a Hadamard produces a general one-site error on its
same participant; the proof already includes X,Y,Z there. It does not
permit an uncharged second force or arbitrary larger data support.

The receiver groups sensing by physical Z axis. It applies a global H layer,
repeats complete horizontal physical-ZZ snapshots for the original XX
syndrome, corrects the component frame, restores H once, and repeats vertical
ZZ snapshots. Strong same-axis backaction commutes with the syndrome being
read, even while it changes the other error component.

For at most s fresh localized events per COMPLETE cycle, require s+1
consecutive identical FULL same-axis syndrome vectors, with a cap(s+1)^2
snapshots per axis. A failure of agreement is an actual observed flag;
outside the promised budget a wrong run can also be accepted. The flag is
not a detector of every fault.

Let b_X,b_Z count rows/columns touched by D. A sufficient invariant is

 t_X>=b_X+2s, t_Z>=b_Z+2s.                           (8.1)

At the boundary, X has at most s late fresh row representatives. Z has
columns(D) plus at most s late fresh representatives. Before the next
correction, its true syndrome can contain the old residual s, the fixed
persistent support b, and the new cycle's s. Equation(8.1) counts all three.
An accepted run contains an uncorrupted snapshot. Correcting its actual
syndrome leaves only later fresh faults and the allowed regenerated D
component. The same boundary returns, for arbitrarily many cycles.

This is a statement about the full linear error span with memory coefficients,
so it includes coherent errors and entanglement with a reference. It does not
replace the final physical recovery by a semantic ideal decoder. The actual
snapshot projectors, record rule and frame updates implement the recovery.

Concrete results:

 * 5x5: all nominal retained memory gauge-only, one fresh event/cycle.
 * 7x7: additionally one unknown fixed strong dispersive participant,
   one fresh event/cycle, initially intact calibrated reports.
 * 5x5 with that strong participant fails: persistent column 0, old residual
   column 1 and new column 2 form weight 3, whose syndrome also belongs to a
   weight-2 complement. Minimum-weight correction applies logical Z.

The last failure is preserved. The old error was not discarded to obtain a
more favorable certificate. If one physical fault can affect w representatives,
replace s by the corresponding total representative budget. A common
switching failure leaving both members of an intra-block pair coupled during
a nongauge basis rotation can exceed the one-site contract.

Parallelism is real in the declared hardware. All same-axis module

propagates through its incident star into

 span{I,Xk,Yk,Zk} Alg{S_kj} tensor B(memory),

so it still has one representative modulo gauge. It can spoil many reports

9. PERSISTENT BROKEN REPORTERS AND NOISY CLASSICAL CONTROL
=======================================================

A permanently wrong reporter defeats simple temporal agreement. We therefore
constructed an ordinary spatial redundancy option without a perfect erasure
flag. Pair-gauge outcomes on a graph H form its binary cut code. Its distance
is the edge connectivity lambda(H). With lambda>=2b+1, b arbitrary bad edge
reports can be corrected regardless of their time correlations.

The decoder uses 2b+1 edge-disjoint paths from a fixed root to each vertex.
Each path XOR estimates the relative vertex sign; majority recovers it.
Reconstructing edges gives an actual discrepancy flag, with no guarantee
outside the budget. Good edges remain connected and their projective good
components establish the quantum gauge values being reconstructed.

For 7 vertices and b=1, the explicit 11-edge graph has minimum cut 3. Eleven
is optimal within simple seven-vertex graphs because every vertex needs
degree 3. Triplicating the 6 path checks needs 18 modules and the complete
graph needs 21. The 11-edge version requires non-nearest-neighbor connections;
edge count
does not establish hardware advantage. The global one-strong-module support
bound is still necessary: correcting one bad edge independently in every
row would not protect arbitrary strong data disturbance in every row.

The 7x7 redundant implementation uses 154 two-mode modules, 308 meter calls
normally and at most 462 under one fresh event, with the same 4T/6T parallel
sensing calendar. This is a costly sufficient construction.

Classical processing is also constructed rather than assumed perfect.
Five isolated copies of the actual finite receiver/frame state machine
are refreshed through five separate bitwise majority-of-five voters. At
the update boundary allow one bad replica; one fresh replica fault gives
at most two wrong candidates. Every good voter therefore sees at least
three identical correct candidates. A fault at a refresh voter can leave
only its own output replica wrong, restoring the boundary invariant.
Refresh is hardwired, unconditional, and writes every state bit on its
fixed phase. Corrupt application state or a program counter cannot skip
repair. All mutable configuration influencing future commands must be
included in the refreshed state or counted as a persistent pod defect.

Raw quantum detector mistakes shared by the five copies remain the same
physical report faults handled above. Physical actuation is voted locally
per data port; one failed final local voter is a real quantum control fault
and consumes the EXISTING quantum cycle budget. One actuator failure in
every classical update cannot be hidden behind a one-fault quantum promise.
Final outputs remain five encoded words instead of passing through one
unprotected final voter. Local timing/register/gate faults have explicit
domains; shared clocks, power or fanout that damage multiple domains must
be charged accordingly. This is not asynchronous Byzantine consensus.

An explicit majority-of-five implementation uses 12 two-input AND/OR gates
at depth 5. Five independent voters cost 60W such gates per W-bit state update,
in addition to five actual decoder copies and 10W state/candidate register
bits. One permanently bad classical replica plus an old transient and a new
fault requires seven replicas, not five. This is ordinary classical fault
tolerance with a supplied construction and counterexamples, not a new
classical reliability principle or an unmeasured physical failure rate.

10. A USEFUL GATE, REAL PREPARATION, AND A REPEATABLE TRANSACTION
=============================================================

The useful entangler is deliberately an ordinary forced-oscillator loop.
Add an explicit unconditional oscillator drive to the available controls:

 H(t)=chi(t)[Z1 n_a+(lambda0+lambda2 Z2)(a+a^dag)],
 integral chi=2pi, g=0.

The exact whole-space unitary is

 U_loop=exp[i2pi(lambda0^2+lambda2^2)Z1]
        exp[i4pi lambda0 lambda2 Z1Z2] tensor I_a.  (10.1)

At lambda0=lambda2=1/4,
Z1 S2 U_loop=i CZ, with S2=diag(1,i). Thus chronological
H2 -> U_loop -> S2 -> H2, plus a Z1 Pauli-frame update, implements CNOT.
Two real H pulses and one real phase pulse are a sufficient local-gate
implementation; a phase-capable conventional compiler can optimize them.
No pulse-count lower bound or new geometric-gate discovery is claimed.

With chi_max=2g and 0.1/g endpoint ramps, the loop takes 3.24159265/g and
its peak total branch force is g. At finite kappa_a, compensating both
forces by exp(-kappa_a t/2) gives exactly zero final driven displacement
and a data-independent surviving coefficient exp(-kappa_a T/2). The coherent
action-phase coefficient also changes from 2pi to
Phi_kappa=integral chi(t)exp(-kappa_a t)[1-cos(theta(t))]dt.
That changes both local and entangling phases and must be calibrated or
included in the admitted control/noise term. Lost photons and vacuum noise
still carry data dependence; phase correction cannot erase that information.
These disturbances act on the
intended pair only: for an interblock transversal gate this is at most
one site PER block, including arbitrary retained apparatus correlations.

For two 7x7 blocks, each incoming boundary has at most one residual X row
and one residual Z column. Ideal transversal CNOT maps their maxima to
(|xA|,|zA|;|xB|,|zB|)<=(1,2;2,1). One fresh localized event across the
ENTIRE gate, local gates, both recoveries and idles raises them to at most
(2,3;3,2), within t=3. The actual recovery then restores the same residual
boundary on each block. The old error, imported error and new fault are

This transaction uses b=0 for nominal nongauge persistent support. Persistent
off-gauge modules, imported support from additional blocks or two-site

extended to arbitrary common control failures.

42 distinct dispersive sources in each axis. Coalescing at each same-axis
block boundary gives at most 84 such rotations per block, 168 for both
recoveries, or they require an explicitly implemented continuous phase-frame
compiler with accurate phase-controlled pulses. Parallel recovery on the

6T under the one-event transaction, plus the loop and actual local/controller

measurement uses 49 meters; terminal X also uses 49 real H pulses.
These large sufficient resources do not establish a win over GPI/qutrit or
erasure hardware. No non-Clifford resource is assumed; universality is not

12. VALIDATION, FAILURES AND WHAT REMAINS OPEN
===========================================

Verified mathematics and computations include the entire-space identities,
the full selected-mode CP instrument, finite two-mode transfer integration,
the exact loss overlap with an independent Lindblad check, both repeated
energy recurrences, the actual noisy temporal receiver and parallel star
support, spatial broken-reporter decoding, classical repeated-state refresh,
single-qubit preparation and a complete encoded-CNOT boundary transaction.

The discrete ledger includes 7,344 complete-cycle cases for 5x5, 329,728 for
7x7, 768 one-bad-edge records, 229,376 algebra generator-closure checks and 59,392
gate-transaction receiver histories, with zero in-contract violations.
The actual classical receiver update passes 17,408 state/history cases;
five-pod old-plus-new receiving-voter cases and the seven-pod persistent
extension also pass, including per-recipient equivocation by faulty sources.
These check the declared algebraic circuit abstraction; they do not pretend
to be a full bosonic many-qubit device simulation. Separate proof is what
extends the abstraction to arbitrary joint memory coefficients.

Informative failures preserved in this packet include: post-only receiver
has zero robust signal; inherited pulse fails under warm-state rejection;
fast control raises retained energy and loss;1%turn-area error is damaging;
all-column strong off-gauge memory contains a logical operator; 5x5 fails with
persistent+old+new support; temporal repetition cannot defeat one permanent
wrong reporter; two bad edges can fool the one-edge cut decoder; 3 classical
replicas fail on old+new corruption; and ensemble energy is not a conditional
fault intensity. These failures determine the operating contract.

The research choice is to retain the finite observation/gauge-algebra route
and the ordinary hot-bus gate, while treating joint physical noise admission
as the decisive next obstruction. The complete retained-memory overlap(6.2)
and contraction(7.1) make that obstruction quantitative. To turn this into
a demonstrated computing/resource advantage requires a physical bound on
the joint weak fault family or its coherent amplitudes, including the actual
steady and faulted apparatus; an isolation/calibration model valid across
the required photon range; and equal-resource optimization against the
matched-qutrit/GPI/erasure route. Neither a mean-energy bound nor a small
one-shot pZ is enough to supply the missing joint bound.

No per-use vacuum renewal is necessary in the constructed nominal interface.
Actual errors still need physical containment and a real receiver. This
continuation supplies both at the deterministic bounded-fault level and
specifies exactly where a hardware-level reliability claim must be earned.
