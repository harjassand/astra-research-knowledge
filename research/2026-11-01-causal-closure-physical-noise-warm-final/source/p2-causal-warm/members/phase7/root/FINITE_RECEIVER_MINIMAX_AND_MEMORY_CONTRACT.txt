FINITE QUANTUM RECEIVER WITH UNRESTRICTED RETAINED INITIAL MEMORY
1 November 2026. Root construction and proof.

Purpose and attribution
=======================

This note states exactly what finite observation can replace, and what it
cannot. The finite temporal-mode rejection principle already appears in
Astra N1520, OUTPUT_MODE_THEOREM.md, retrieved at the current Astra revision.
Ordinary least-squares projection supplies its linear optimization. We do
not claim either as new. The additions here are a useful minimax statement,
its physical realization by the two-mode half-turn protocol, and its link
to the repeated, fault-contained consumer in this packet.

1. Linear quantum model and actual information available
=======================================================

Let x be p initial apparatus annihilators. Assume a finite passive linear
input-output evolution whose entire homogeneous scattering transformation
is independent of the data label s=+/-1. On the accessible output field,

 b_out = H x + B b_in + s h.                         (1)

H is a bounded map from C^p to the finite-window output one-particle Hilbert
space. h is a square-integrable classical displacement waveform. All fresh
input modes are vacuum for this first result. The initial apparatus may be
arbitrary, nonGaussian and entangled with the data and an external reference.
The initial apparatus is not assumed independent of s. The input field is
fresh and independent of that joint state; delayed returning fields must
instead be included among retained degrees of freedom if applicable.

Set h_perp=(I-P_H)h, where P_H is orthogonal projection onto range(H).
For p finite the range is closed. If h_perp is nonzero, the normalized mode

 f=h_perp/||h_perp||                                (2)

satisfies f^dag H=0 and f^dag h=||h_perp||. Its annihilator is

 b_f = b_vac + s sqrt(N_eff),  N_eff=||h_perp||^2.   (3)

The commutator and passive isometry give [b_vac,b_vac^dag]=1. This is a
normalized linear combination of fresh input annihilators only.

The displaced-annihilator kernel is one-dimensional. Thus for every initial
apparatus ket, the conditional full isometry factors as

 V_s |psi> = |s sqrt(N_eff)>_f tensor W_s |psi>.     (4)

Equation(4), not equality of first or second moments, proves that measuring
f produces a state-independent report contract while retaining W_s and all
its correlations. Extension to arbitrary initial data-memory-reference
density operators follows by linearity. The retained W_s is neither vacuum
reset nor an assumed scalar overlap.

2. Minimax quantum discrimination: a matching obstruction
========================================================

Claim. For equal prior s and unrestricted initial apparatus states allowed
to depend on s, the optimal worst-case parity-discrimination error using the
entire accessible output field is precisely the Helstrom error for the two
coherent states in(4):

 q_quantum = [1-sqrt(1-exp(-4 N_eff))]/2.            (5)

Uniform efficiency eta changes N_eff to eta N_eff if it is a common
data-independent vacuum attenuation on the whole accessible field.

Proof of achievability. Isolate the canonical mode f and perform the optimal
binary coherent-state receiver. Equation(4) makes its risk independent of the
unknown initial apparatus. This is an information-theoretic statement; the
physical receiver used in our construction is homodyne, not an uncharged
Helstrom or Dolinar apparatus.

Proof of converse. Let a=H^+ P_H h, using the Moore-Penrose inverse. Choose
the allowed initial coherent state |alpha_s> with alpha_s=-s a. For these
inputs, the entire accessible output is coherent with amplitude

 H alpha_s+s h=s h_perp.

All orthogonal accessible modes have identical zero amplitudes. Any receiver
must distinguish just the two states in(5), so its worst-case error cannot
be smaller than their Helstrom error. This pair of allowed initial states
is an explicit adversarial witness, not a classical model imposed on all
possible quantum inputs. It suffices for the minimax lower bound.

Consequences:

 * If h lies in range(H), NO quantum receiver can uniformly distinguish the
   parity better than a guess. Unknown old coherent amplitudes can exactly
   hide the entire new displacement.
 * Partial observation before injection can make h_perp nonzero without
   making the internal state close to vacuum.
 * A prior energy or state-distribution bound can allow better use of the
   parallel component. A Bayesian/Kalman-type conventional receiver should
   use that information if it is genuinely available. The unrestricted
   minimax theorem must not be used as its lower bound.
 * The theorem bounds the receiver's information, not unobserved logical
   disturbance. For that, the complete W_s or W_z remains essential.

For a homodyne apparatus with a programmable local-oscillator phase and
classical temporal weighting, (2) is the ordinary matched filter after
rejecting the unknown initial response. It has

 q_hom=Phi[-2 sqrt(eta N_eff)].                     (6)

In the actual two-mode protocol all waveforms have a common quadrature,
so fixed-phase homodyne and a signed real weight suffice. Classical weighting
does not require storing pre-observation photons in a quantum delay line.
The information-theoretic coherent receiver in(5) WOULD require additional
physical temporal-mode processing/nonGaussian detection; its lower error
is not used as a free improvement in any schedule or recovery ledger.

3. One-mode limit and the finite two-mode construction
====================================================

For identical partial readout windows of amplitude attenuation r and an
intermediate map a->-a+dS, normalized emitted windows have initial column
sqrt(1-r^2)(1,-r) and signal (0,d sqrt(1-r^2)). Their orthogonal mode gives

 N_eff=d^2(1-r^2)/(1+r^2).                          (7)

This is d^2*tanh(kappa_read*tau/2) for r=exp(-kappa_read*tau/2).
Post-only observation has signal collinear with the unknown initial-storage
column and therefore N_eff=0. A partial pre-observation is sufficient; a
near-vacuum state is not necessary.

Our numerical schedule does NOT use this eliminated one-mode model. It
retains storage a plus finite readout m, damping Gamma on m, and actual
finite g(t) ramps. The receiver rejects both initial columns over all three
pre/map/post intervals. The post-only counterexample is computed directly
in that two-mode model and gives N_eff approximately 9.1e-30 despite a
nonzero raw signal. Numerical roundoff is the only residual in this zero.

4. Physical realization and both parity sectors
==============================================

During the mapping interval,

 H(t)=chi(t)[Z1 n_a+lambda Z2(a+a^dag)],
 integral chi=pi, g=0.

Finite smooth ramps are exact because H(t) is a scalar envelope times one
fixed operator. Completing the square and using integer oscillator number,

 U=exp(i pi lambda^2 Z1) D(-2lambda S) P_a,
 S=Z1Z2, P_a=(-1)^n.                               (8)

After the actual local Z1 phase correction, the complete operator depends
on the data only through S. Pre/post readout has chi=u=0, so the full passive
scattering is data independent. The whole protocol, including unobserved
output and retained modes, is sector controlled. This establishes logical
preservation inside BOTH parity sectors without assuming a stored state.

This is a specialization of established forced-oscillator/qubus control
algebra (van Loock et al., PRA78,022303), not a claim to have invented
conditional displacement or hot-bus gates. The real extra actuator is the
ability to separate readout g(t) from dispersive mapping chi(t), with no
unaccounted residual data coupling in idle and basis-change intervals.

5. Repeated semantics: the algebra must be fixed
===============================================

Choose a fixed physical Pauli gauge algebra G for the code. If every nominal
completed operation, with all retained memory and outcomes, belongs to
G tensor B(M), its action on the protected subsystem is I_L tensor a map on
gauge+memory. Arbitrarily many compositions retain this property.

Each physical Pauli E normalizes G. Consequently an actual sequence with
fault components E1,...,Er can be regrouped into

 (Er ... E1) V_history, V_history in G tensor B(M),

including adaptive histories and operator-valued memory coefficients.
The physical PRODUCT of errors, including an old surviving error, determines
the correction family. A new gauge name cannot erase an old error.

The separate repeated-receiver proof supplies the real consumer: entire
same-axis syndrome vectors, finite repetition, actual flags, Pauli-frame
updates, spatial redundancy for broken reporters, and a boundary invariant.
The present theorem alone does not supply correction or a physical noise
probability. Generic interaction-algebra QEC and non-Markovian fault tolerance
are established ordinary theory; our question is whether the actual finite
apparatus meets their hypotheses.

6. Exact physical limitations
============================

Positive storage loss during mapping still leaks the intermediate individual
qubit rotation. With u=lambda chi exp(-kappa_a t/2), the endpoint initial
coefficient remains -exp(-kappa_a T/2) and the parity displacement remains
exact. The measured mode can still reject all initial operators with fresh
vacuum inputs. But its retained W_z may depend on individual Z1.

The exact mapping-loss overlap is

 Q_s=exp(i2lambda^2 K) D(-lambda s)(1-C)^n D(lambda s),
 C=kappa_a integral exp(-kappa_a t)[1-exp(-2i theta(t))]dt,
 K=integral chi(t) exp(-kappa_a t)dt.                 (9)

This is the complete lost-field AND no-jump result, not a jump-rate proxy.
For nonzero kappa_a, no state-uniform small error follows over unbounded
initial oscillator energy. It is a local error times gauge, which the actual
consumer can contain for the declared support/fault family. Weak background
loss in every module needs a real joint noise admission; a one-shot thermal
overlap does not furnish that admission.

Likewise, exact rejection of a continuum of unknown frequency poles over
a finite observation interval forces the filter to vanish (analytic
uniqueness of its Fourier/Laplace transform). Approximate calibration needs
an operating-energy/moment budget or a correctable support bound. These are
physical requirements, not a reason to reinstate a universal vacuum reset.

Finally, fresh output fields and classical records are genuine entropy
outlets. Avoiding repeated apparatus purification does not create entropy-
free error correction or tolerate a bath that arbitrarily changes the
incoming field during every observation.
