2. WHY BRACKETING IS NECESSARY AND WHAT IT COSTS

After a single parity-dependent displacement, a post-only receiver sees

signal. The pre-observation records a distinct temporal portion of that
same initial response, allowing a difference mode that rejects the initial
operator but retains a displacement injected between the two windows.

A useful exact one-mode limiting comparator has amplitude attenuation r
in each of two identical readout windows. Its two normalized emitted modes

7. ENERGY AND IMPERFECT-CONTROL CONDITIONS

 sqrt(E_next) <= r sqrt(E_current)+d.

This gives sqrt(E_k)<=r^k sqrt(E_0)+d(1-r^k)/(1-r), retaining history and

These restrictions are physically consequential. They do not restore a
