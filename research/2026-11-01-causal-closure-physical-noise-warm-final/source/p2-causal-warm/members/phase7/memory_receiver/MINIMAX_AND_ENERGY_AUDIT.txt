INDEPENDENT AUDIT: FINITE QUANTUM RECEIVER MINIMAX AND ENERGY CONTRACT
1 November 2026. Audit of root/FINITE_RECEIVER_MINIMAX_AND_MEMORY_CONTRACT.txt
and the energy fields in root/bracketed_control_comparison.json.

VERDICT

The stated unrestricted-memory quantum minimax equality is correct for its
declared passive channel and receiver access. Both its converse and its
achievability extend to an initial apparatus correlated with the data and
an inaccessible reference. The converse's parity-dependent coherent states
are a legitimate finite-energy witness because those correlations are
expressly allowed. No claim about optimal physical homodyne hardware follows
from the information-theoretic Helstrom achievability.

The energy formulas are valid ensemble RMS bounds for the declared nominal
or bounded-force sign-fault evolution. Their table entries are drive-induced
asymptotic ceilings, not universal finite-round caps from arbitrary initial
energy, and not bounds conditioned on every individual measurement record.
They cannot supply a product-probability admission for background loss.

1. PRECISE QUANTUM MINIMAX STATEMENT

Fix the physical passive input-output evolution and the entire field that
the receiver is allowed to access. Its annihilation operators satisfy

 b_out = H x + B b_fresh + s h,

where H has finite-dimensional domain, s = +/-1, the homogeneous passive
scattering is independent of s, and all fresh inputs are independent vacuum.
Let Hrange = range(H) and h_perp = (I-P_Hrange)h. Define N = ||h_perp||^2.

For a POVM R on that entire accessible field, define equal-prior risk

 risk(R; rho_+,rho_-) = (Pr_R[-|+] + Pr_R[+|-])/2.

The apparatus density operators rho_s can be arbitrary and different. They
can arise as the two parity blocks of one allowed joint data-apparatus state.
The minimax value is

 inf_R sup_(rho_+,rho_-) risk = (1-sqrt(1-exp(-4N)))/2.        (A1)

The same value is obtained for worst conditional error over the two labels:
the symmetric coherent-state receiver has equal errors on +/- and the
average-risk converse lower-bounds maximum conditional risk.

Achievability. For N > 0, f = h_perp/sqrt(N) has no initial-apparatus
coefficient. Its canonical annihilator is a normalized combination of fresh
vacuum annihilators plus s sqrt(N). The displaced-annihilator kernel forces
the full conditional isometry to factor as

 V_s |psi> = |s sqrt(N)>_f tensor W_s|psi>.

The argument holds for every initial apparatus ket and its purifications.
A Helstrom measurement of f therefore has the stated risk for every allowed
input, without requiring the receiver to know rho_s. For N = 0, guessing
achieves risk 1/2.

Converse. Because H has finite rank, its range is closed. Let

 a = H^+ P_Hrange h,
 alpha_s = -s a.

Choose the allowed initial apparatus coherent state |alpha_s>. A passive
linear network with coherent initial modes and fresh vacuum produces a
multimode coherent output. Its accessible displacement is

 H alpha_s + s h = s h_perp.

Thus the receiver's ENTIRE accessible field consists of |s sqrt(N)> in f
and identical vacuum in every orthogonal mode. Its optimal error is exactly
the Helstrom value. This one admissible pair establishes the lower bound
against arbitrary output POVMs. There is no reduction to classical nuisance
noise or to first moments of general quantum inputs.

The required adversarial witness energy is finite:

 E_witness = ||H^+ P_Hrange h||^2.

If an initial-energy restriction still permits this pair, the same lower
bound persists. If it excludes the pair, this particular converse no longer
establishes the optimum. A genuinely acquired initial-state distribution or
record may help an ordinary Bayesian receiver use the parallel signal.

Uniform vacuum attenuation eta on the entire accessible field sends
H -> sqrt(eta)H and h -> sqrt(eta)h. For eta > 0 the nuisance range is
unchanged, so (A1) uses eta N. Eta = 0 gives the trivial risk 1/2.

2. WHAT THE MINIMAX RESULT DOES NOT ASSERT

The receiver is restricted to the declared accessible output. Extra measured
apparatus ports, access to retained internal modes, a correlated reference,
or old records restricting rho_s change the decision problem. Those resources
must be added to the model or used as a prior; they cannot be silently denied
to a conventional competitor.

The theorem optimizes receiver POVMs for a FIXED physical control/channel.
Control changes alter H and h. The quantum lower bound is not a global bound
over alternate physical couplers, drives, encodings or fresh preparations.

A temporal quantum mode and its optimal binary coherent-state measurement
are legitimate abstract POVMs. A physical implementation may require coherent
temporal-mode processing and a nonGaussian receiver. The construction's
actual fixed-phase homodyne plus signed classical weighting instead has

 q_hom = Phi[-2 sqrt(eta N)].

The smaller Helstrom error cannot be inserted into its resource ledger.

The equality is a discrimination result. For arbitrary input quantum
coherences, retained W_s and unobserved outputs determine the complete
instrument. Root's all-state half-turn map supplies the additional required
sector control; output discrimination by itself does not.

3. EXACT NOMINAL ENSEMBLE ENERGY RECURRENCE

Suppose every nominal completed operation has the same endpoint homogeneous
matrix K, the same endpoint displacement vector d, fresh vacuum inputs, and

 x_(k+1) = K x_k + d S_k + v_k.                     (A2)

Here the equation is interpreted on a common Heisenberg dilation. S_k is the
actual bounded Heisenberg parity operator for that use. Its dependence on
earlier data operations, records and apparatus correlations is allowed;
||S_k|| <= 1 is enough. v_k contains only fresh annihilation operators, with
coefficients adapted to preceding degrees of freedom when applicable.
Consequently v_k kills the initial joint ket with vacuum fresh inputs.

Purify the entire input, including old records, and call the joint ket Psi.
Iterating (A2) and applying the ordinary norm triangle inequality gives

 sqrt(E_n)
 = ||x_n Psi||
 <= ||K^n|| sqrt(E_0) + sum_(j=0)^(n-1) ||K^j d||.  (A3)

No commutation of the different S_k is used. Ideal measurements and adaptive
data-only control can be included by their dilations, provided the physical
K and d are unchanged across those branches. If the physical schedule itself
varies with the record, products of the actual matrices replace K^j.

For a stable K, put B = sum_(j>=0) ||K^j d||. Then B^2 bounds the
drive-generated energy from E_0 = 0 at every round and is the asymptotic
ensemble ceiling for every fixed finite E_0. It is not a finite-round cap
uniform over unrestricted initial energy. The missing transient is exactly
the first term of (A3).

The less sharp scalar bound is

 sqrt(E_n) <= ||K||^n sqrt(E_0)
              + ||d|| (1-||K||^n)/(1-||K||),        (A4)

when ||K|| < 1. The table's loose_boundary_energy_bound is the square of
the asymptotic second term in (A4).

4. ROBUST SIGN-HISTORY ENERGY RECURRENCE

Let p = ||Epre||, q = ||Epost||, and

 U = integral_mapping |u(t)| dt.

For bounded data-Pauli sign histories during mapping, the homogeneous
mapping propagator remains contractive. Variation of constants bounds its
driven RMS contribution by U. Readout windows contain no drive. Hence

 sqrt(E_(k+1)) <= p q sqrt(E_k) + q U.              (A5)

Writing r = p q < 1 gives the explicit finite-round result

 sqrt(E_n) <= r^n sqrt(E_0) + q U (1-r^n)/(1-r).     (A6)

The table's robust_boundary_energy_bound is [q U/(1-r)]^2, the asymptotic
drive-induced ceiling. Its mapping-entry ceiling is p^2 times that number.
The mapping peak bound is [p sqrt(Erob)+U]^2; it also exceeds the boundary
ceiling because q <= 1. All these tabulated ceilings need the transient
when E_0 is not already below the corresponding invariant bound.

The sign-history qualification matters. An arbitrary apparatus heating fault,
an unbounded drive fault, or a faulty readout coupling is not covered by a
fixed U and p,q. Fresh thermal inputs also need their covariance contribution;
the saved energy tables use fresh vacuum.

5. WHY NONE OF THIS IS A CONDITIONAL LOSS-FAULT MODEL

E_n is the expectation after retaining and averaging all outcome records.
It is not the energy in every normalized postselected branch. For a record
event A of probability p_A, the immediate bound is only

 E[n | A] <= E[n]/p_A.

The denominator can be arbitrarily small. An elementary physical example
starts with a control qubit in |+> and a vacuum oscillator, applies a bounded
controlled displacement +/-alpha, and measures the control in the X basis.
The unconditional oscillator occupation is |alpha|^2. Conditional on the
minus outcome, it is the odd cat, whose occupation is

 |alpha|^2 coth(|alpha|^2) -> 1 as alpha -> 0,

while the ensemble energy tends to zero. More elaborate postselection can
produce much stronger tail amplification. Bounded ensemble RMS energy is
therefore insufficient for a uniform energy bound on every history.

An integrated ensemble photon exposure can bound an expected photon-count
number and hence a one-event probability by a union/Markov bound. It cannot
by itself imply that r separated storage-loss events have probability p^r.
Such a claim needs conditional intensity bounds, suitable factorial moments,
or a valid non-Markovian noise admission. Complete no-jump filtering also
remains part of the quantum error and cannot be replaced by the count rate.

This limitation does not invalidate the separately proved constant-trace
CP admission of the selected pointer's binary measurement noise. That
admission comes from its complete scalar Gram instrument, not from energy.

6. NUMERICAL AUDIT OF THE SAVED ENERGY TABLES

All twelve rows of bracketed_control_comparison.json were independently
reconstructed from their saved complex K and d values. The nominal sums,
the robust formula [q U/(1-pq)]^2 and the contractions p q agree with the
saved values. All saved ||K|| and p q are strictly below one.

The numerical nominal sum stops after its current positive term is less than
1e-14. Its omitted positive tail is bounded by

 ||K|| * last_term / (1-||K||).

Adding this tail changes the squared result by less than 6e-13 in all twelve
saved rows. It is negligible at reported precision, but a literal upper-bound
implementation should add the tail rather than truncate it. The ODE and
quadrature are floating-point numerical calculations, not interval-certified
physical bounds.

No additional device simulations were run for this audit. The minimax and
energy conclusions above follow from the channel algebra, with the saved
tables checked only for arithmetic and contract consistency.
