ASTRA P2 — CAUSAL MEMORY CLOSURE AND PHYSICAL NOISE ADMISSION
Phase8 research synthesis. 1 November2026.

RESULT AND DECISION
===================
The strongest surviving result is a physical criterion for repeatable quantum
operations with retained apparatus, together with a construction that reaches
an actual recovery and CNOT consumer. The apparatus need not be returned to
vacuum. Its old information must be prevented from entering the useful output,
or confined to a physically specified error/gauge domain which the implemented
receiver corrects. Small physical deviations then need a JOINT admission over
the complete experiment, including rare states and all classical records.

This continuation supplies four connected pieces beyond the preserved work:

1. An exact finite-loss force-bus channel on arbitrary correlated old states,
   distinguishing incoming memory rejection from outgoing displacement return.
2. A finite physical startup instrument which earns an exponential energy tail,
   followed by a real finite robust preparation/readout receiver.
3. A complete-history replacement/noise theorem, extended to persistent local
   apparatus defects with physically bounded onset intensities.
4. A recompilation of the preserved noisy7x7 CNOT consumer using the new exact
   loss channel, with full parallel and idle exposure retained.

The result is foundational and model-specific. It is NOT an experimentally
established operating regime or a total resource advantage. The strongest
ordinary finite dirty-mediator, GPI/qutrit, cat, erasure and optimized hot-bus
routes remain serious alternatives. The broad facts that a warm mediator can
be reused and that a remembering bath can be tolerated are existing science.
What is constructed here is the physical path from an unbounded retained
apparatus and its real receiver to a valid joint error contract.

The hardware comparison changed the research decision. A demonstrated pumped
conditional force also retains dispersive/nonlinear terms tied to its physical
generation. Our exact common-scattering premise is not supplied by that
demonstration. Full-life control and residual-coupling exposure also make the
present sufficient fixture expensive. The research priority is therefore the
microscopic interaction/record criterion and its physical admission, with a
finite mediator as the default strong comparator, rather than another small
improvement to the isolated oscillator waveform.

The earlier two-mode parity construction, repeated recovery, finite classical
controller and consumed CNOT remain unchanged in the cumulative packet. Their
claims are not strengthened retroactively. The new force bus is a DIFFERENT
effective apparatus with its own controls, port, receiver and conditions.

7. JOINT WEAK NOISE AND PERSISTENT APPARATUS DEFECTS

Exact nominal positive CP tags, completed diagonal calibration differences,

evolution, therefore has one representative modulo the actual gauge and may

faults cannot be absorbed across several snapshots. During basis changes,
nominal forces are off and residual couplings retain their real supports.

For component j, let b_j be its positive-tag plus completed-calibration norm
budget, and W_j its actual local generator-norm exposure. The audited Dyson
majorant is exp(W_j z)+b_j z. With B=sum_j(b_j+W_j), the degree>=2 bad-map
norm is at most R=exp(B)-1-B. A single physical correlated two-site jump of
weight O(p) does not become O(p^2) by relabeling its two sites. It needs a
larger correctable support or an explicitly linear bad term.

For many rectangles, the correctable signed sum need not be CP. A safe

the leading small-error approximation absent a further positive resummation.

9. THE MICROSCOPIC OBSTRUCTION AND A SHARP ECHO RESULT
=====================================================
Touzard et al.'s demonstrated pumped conditional displacement retains
dispersion and Kerr in its effective Hamiltonian [R11]. Their force relation,
in the present conventions, implies chi_eff=F^2/(alpha|xi|^2). Strong force
and an independently vanishing chi_eff cannot be borrowed from that device.
Royer's favorable longitudinal gate analysis explicitly studies the purely
longitudinal case [R10]. Other circuit proposals retain second-order or cubic
corrections [R12,R13]. These are relevant implementation conditions, not weak
baselines to dismiss.

The collaborator proved a useful exact boundary for passive branch-dependent
scattering. Let V_z map old modes into ALL retained and emitted outputs.
For a nonzero preserved coherence independent of every old state, vacuum
fresh inputs require V_z=V_z' and V_z^dag(d_z-d_z')=0. A mismatch in V gives
old-amplitude-squared distinguishability; a residual displacement projection
gives old-amplitude-linear phase sensitivity.

For dispersive echo H_z=z y(t)chi(t)n plus linear forces and positive loss,
the radiated old-mode response is proportional to exp[-i z theta(t)], with

 exp[-2|alpha|^2 integral kappa(t)e^(-K(t))sin^2(theta(t))dt]. (15)

10. WHAT IS NEWLY ENABLED, AND WHAT IS NOT
=========================================
Within the explicit linear/thermal model, the construction supplies a finite
physical route from retained correlated memory to repeated noisy recovery and
a consumed CNOT, without per-use vacuum preparation or an assumed iid meter
fault coin. It includes a physical initial acceptance instrument, finite
readout/ADC, actual loss, and complete-history weak/strong noise semantics.

Its general value is an ordered design test:

 * Match the homogeneous old-state response on every preserved coherence.
 * Remove relevant old displacement influence and prove actual record semantics.
 * Earn state tails only where errors remain energy sensitive; use physical
   support containment for strong defective domains.
 * Derive joint strengths, then feed them to the actual old-plus-new receiver.
 * Compare at the complete quantum task with every wait and actuator counted.

This is a substantive construction and explanatory theory, not a new universal
fault-tolerance theorem or proof that oscillator mediation is best. Bounded
dirty-qubit mediation already supplies the abstract no-reset gate capability.
GPI/qutrit and cat/erasure designs may use fewer physical resources for the
chosen logical task. Their physical receiver and fault models still matter,
but this apparatus has not beaten their strongest informed implementations.

The next enabling capability is microscopic boundedness or exact protected
interaction algebra without sacrificing useful gates and acquired receiver
information. The common-scattering force construction is now a complete
reference model for that search; the finite mediator is the strongest ordinary
alternative to beat. A direct parity/gauge-conditioned interaction is another
real opening, provided its multibody actuator and record-inclusive receiver
are earned. Enlarging the present sufficient fixture does not establish that
capability or a civilizational hardware advantage.
