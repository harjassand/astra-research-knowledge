FINITE ECHO, CONTINUING LOSS, AND THE OLD-APPARATUS INTERFACE
Phase8 physical-mechanisms collaborator. 1 November2026.

DECISION
========
No credible additional finite-control construction has been earned that removes
the microscopic chi Z n and Kerr obstruction while outperforming the bounded
dirty-qubit/GPI alternatives at the complete repeated task. In a clearly defined
passive class, there is an exact obstruction: matching the final bus state is
insufficient because a conditional homogeneous response is continuously emitted.
Finite echo can greatly improve an ENERGY-RESTRICTED error budget; it cannot
make that response independent of an unrestricted old state. The rigorous
finite-energy and receiver machinery must remain part of a physical claim.

1. AN AFFINE EXTENSION OF THE ROOT CONTINUUM CRITERION
===================================================
Assume finitely many old harmonic modes M, independent vacuum fresh inputs, a
finite interval, passive quadratic branch Hamiltonians plus prescribed linear
forces, and all retained AND emitted output modes in the dilation. Data labels z
are conserved between ideal sign reversals; a known final data frame is removed.
On branch z the isometry is

 W_z = exp(i phi_z) D_out(d_z) Gamma(S_z)|vac_F>,

where V_z denotes the OLD columns of S_z. V_z is an isometry from old one-particle
space to the complete output space. Finite square-integrable forces give finite
displacements d_z. This description also grants arbitrary common passive mixing
and arbitrary finite prescribed linear forces. It does not include squeezing,
nonlinear feedback, noncommuting finite-width data pulses, or Kerr.

For one coherence z,z', preservation with a nonzero factor independent of EVERY
old state is equivalent to

            V_z=V_z',        V_z^dag(d_z'-d_z)=0.              (1)

These are statements on the COMPLETE emitted waveform, not only its endpoint.
Proof: coherent old input alpha gives output coherent amplitudes V_z alpha+d_z.
The overlap modulus is

 exp[-|| (V_z-V_z')alpha + d_z-d_z' ||^2/2].                 (2)

If V_z differs, this modulus tends to zero on a ray alpha while it is nonzero
at alpha=0. It cannot be an old-state-independent nonzero scalar. If V_z=V is
common, the relative isometry is, up to a branch-only phase,

 exp[-||P_(V-perp)(d_z'-d_z)||^2/2] D_M(V^dag(d_z'-d_z)).     (3)

The remaining Weyl operator is scalar exactly when its displacement vanishes.
Equations(1)--(3) prove the complete reduced-data interface by expanding arbitrary
data--old-apparatus--reference inputs in data blocks. They do not assume an
old product state. If all retained conditional displacements are also common,
vacuum factorization gives the stronger joint data/retained product interface.

This is a vacuum extension, not a claim that arbitrary unequal-temperature or
colored fresh inputs factor under branch-dependent S_z. The root theorem's
homogeneous thermal product proof retains its stated common-S hypothesis.

The root projected-form criterion Q^dag A Q!=0 is sound within its specified
common, data-independent S model and available force channels. Dispersive chi Z n
changes S_z itself; it cannot simply be included among the scalar pole errors
while retaining that proof. Matching homogeneous old columns is the preceding
necessary step. If old and retained mode counts differ, the linear displacement
constraint count is at most p_old+p_ret complex equations, rather than always2p.

2. EXACT FINITE-ECHO OBSTRUCTION FOR ONE LOSING MODE
==================================================
Grant the controller perfect instantaneous qubit pi pulses. In their toggling
frame let

 H_z(t)=z y(t) chi(t) n + f_z(t)a^dag + f_z*(t)a,
 L(t)=sqrt(kappa(t))a,
 theta(t)=integral_0^t y(s)chi(s)ds,
 K(t)=integral_0^t kappa(s)ds.

y is any finite sign sequence, chi is integrable, and theta is continuous.
The homogeneous old-mode column is exactly

 v_z = [ exp(-K(T)/2-iz theta(T)),
         sqrt(kappa(t)) exp(-K(t)/2-iz theta(t)), 0<=t<=T ]. (4)

For force-free input |alpha>, the full output overlap magnitude is

 exp{-2|alpha|^2 [exp(-K(T))sin^2(theta(T))
                 +integral kappa(t)exp(-K(t))sin^2(theta(t))dt]}.
                                                                  (5)

This includes every photon/no-jump contribution through the exact dilation.
It is not a postselected jump-count argument. Linear forces add the finite
displacement in(2) and cannot cancel its leading |alpha|^2 coefficient.

Even if the usual echo closes theta(T) to an integer multiple of pi, the
radiated part is equal on the two branches only if sin(theta(t))=0 almost
everywhere kappa(t)>0. If loss is positive almost everywhere on the interval,
continuity and theta(0)=0 force theta(t)=0 throughout, hence y(t)chi(t)=0 a.e.
Every nontrivial finite dispersive echo therefore violates(1).

For each finite echo an arbitrarily large coherent input can erase the intended
coherence. Its distance from an ideally preserved |+> coherence approaches1/2,
even after optimizing a known branch phase. Increasing pulse count can suppress
the error for each fixed energy. Taking that limit does not commute with the
supremum over unrestricted initial energy.

Precise exceptions: genuinely loss-free intervals can contain nontrivial closed
phase evolution; an exactly dark subspace can hide it from the outlets; leakage
of a gauge-only label may be acceptable to the actual receiver. An unmonitored
positive outlet is sufficient for the obstruction. Perfect monitoring/erasure
or turning off intrinsic storage loss is an additional physical capability.

3. MULTIMODE FULL-RANK-LOSS VERSION
=================================
Let the passive bus have branch-dependent Hermitian one-particle Hamiltonian
h_z(t), common output coupling matrix C(t), and homogeneous fundamental matrix

 Qdot_z(t)=[-i h_z(t)-C(t)^dag C(t)/2]Q_z(t),  Q_z(0)=I.

For branch pair z,z', condition(1) necessarily contains

 Q_z(T)=Q_z'(T),
 C(t)Q_z(t)=C(t)Q_z'(t) almost everywhere.                    (6)

If C(t) has full column rank almost everywhere, its second equation gives
Q_z=Q_z' a.e., hence everywhere by continuity. Finite-dimensional fundamental
matrices are invertible at finite times. Differentiating then gives

                         h_z(t)=h_z'(t) a.e.                 (7)

Thus a passive mediator with independent continuing loss on EVERY mode cannot
hide a nontrivial conditional frequency/mixing evolution by a finite endpoint
echo. Ordinary linear forces do not change this conclusion. A common-loss
network with a genuine nullspace is different; one must prove invariance of
the dark subspace and include all intrinsic outlets. A nominal dark collective
mode plus independent local storage losses does not satisfy that exception.

4. FINITE ENERGY CAN STILL MAKE ORDINARY ECHO VERY USEFUL
=======================================================
For constant kappa,chi, define the old-column inner product

 q = exp(-kappa T-2i theta(T))
          +integral_0^T kappa exp(-kappa t-2i theta(t))dt.     (8)

For a coherent input of mean occupation nbar the data multiplier is
exp[nbar(q-1)]; for old Fock number n it is q^n. These exact formulas distinguish
phase and magnitude. A Hahn echo gives, for small kappa T and chi T,

 1-Re(q) = kappa chi^2 T^3/6 + higher orders,
 Im(q)   = -kappa chi T^2/2 + higher orders.                 (9)

At kappa=1e-4, chi=.01, T=1.84, nbar=320, the dephasing probability inferred
from magnitude alone is about1.66e-6, but the unknown-occupation phase is about
-5.42e-4 radians. The former MUST NOT be presented as the complete gate error.
The sufficient absolute-norm bounds in MICROSCOPIC_COMPATIBILITY_OBSTRUCTION
are therefore not necessary device limits, but simply deleting the phase is
also wrong.

A +,-,+ echo with intervals near T/4,T/2,T/4 cancels the leading phase.
Adjusting the first and last intervals at KNOWN kappa,chi can make Im(q)=0
exactly while preserving theta(T)=0. The program finds this adjustment and
the residual positive magnitude loss. It is ordinary calibrated control, not
an arbitrary-state cure or the complete forced parity gate.

There is an exact CP interpretation in this force-free, phase-balanced,
vacuum case. Write the emitted old column as h_c plus/minus h_s, where
h_c=sqrt(kappa)e^(-kappa t/2)cos(theta) and
h_s=-i sqrt(kappa)e^(-kappa t/2)sin(theta). Im(q)=0 makes h_c and h_s orthogonal;
the common retained amplitude is orthogonal to h_s too. After tracing the
outlet mode h_s, its photon-number parity applies I or Z to data. Thus the
bad effect on the OLD apparatus is

                  E_bad = [I-q^n]/2.                       (10)

For 0<=q<=1 and input n<=N, E_bad <=[1-q^N]I/2. The probability depends on
the old apparatus; no independent fault coin has been assumed. This provides
a useful EXACT finite-energy component, but it does not establish the same
decomposition with active entangling forces, thermal fresh inputs, Kerr, or
finite imperfect echo pulses. It does not replace the whole-history tail cap.

5. ACTUATORS AND FAULT-DURING-ECHO SEMANTICS
==========================================
The no-go grants zero-duration perfect pi pulses, so their actual cost cannot
rescue an unrestricted-state claim in this class. For a constructive finite-
energy schedule their cost cannot be omitted: during finite X control,
chi Z n rotates into noncommuting Z/Y terms and detunes the pulse by chi n.
At chi=.01, N320, the spread is3.2 in the same rate units. A cap50 local drive
does not make that coupling vanish. Composite/adiabatic control is a possible
ordinary improvement, requiring its own exposure and error admission.

Changing the sign of a pump force is not generally a sign reversal of chi:
the demonstrated pumped relation links residual chi to a squared force gain.
An independently tunable opposite dispersion would be a new earned actuator.

A fault during an echo changes the complete emitted history, not merely the
final phase. A later good closed-force operation can refuse influence from
surviving memory; it cannot unemit radiation. During one actual diagonal ZZ
gauge operation an arbitrary diagonal fault may still have ONE correctable
representative modulo that gauge. The present bare-coherence obstruction is
not a claim that one such event defeats the inherited noisy receiver. A
many-module claim nevertheless needs joint admission: unrestricted damaging
old occupations at enough modules can produce an uncorrectable pattern.

Exact frame compensation for chi Z_k n also asks for new interactions. A force
on another data observable Z_j becomes proportional to

 Z_j exp(i Z_k theta) = Z_j cos(theta)+i Z_j Z_k sin(theta).

For j!=k the compensating quadrature includes a parity-conditioned force.
Classical pump phase tracking alone cannot supply this operator-valued control.

6. KERR AND A SHARP PROTECTED-ALGEBRA ALTERNATIVE
==============================================
The preceding theorem concerns conditional PASSIVE quadratic evolution; it is
not a general nonlinear no-go. Oscillator parity/phase rotations leave n(n-1)
invariant, so changing the sign of a linear force does not time reverse Kerr.
Under H_K=K n(n-1)/2,

                       a(t)=a exp[-iK(n-1)t].                (11)

The interaction-picture force is number dependent; the c-number commutator
and terminating Magnus argument used for the linear bus no longer applies.
Known revivals, negative Kerr or additional number-dependent drives may be
useful, but no such finite noisy mechanism has been joined here. Common Kerr
can be put in a reference only after proving the corresponding instrument.

Pointwise operator algebra offers a real alternative: if unwanted Hamiltonians
and all outlet operators act as I_L tensor operators on a gauge/apparatus
factor, they cannot disturb the unconditioned logical factor. A direct parity
coupling H=(Z1Z2) tensor F_M protects BOTH parity-sector coherences pointwise
against arbitrary bus self dynamics. However, that is a stronger physical
multi-body actuator than the linear single-data forces being synthesized.
Moreover, a reported measurement of an old apparatus initially correlated
with logical data can reveal that correlation. Protected Hamiltonian algebra
alone does not establish the record-inclusive interface or reliable parity
discrimination. Its actual receiver must still be proved.

Bounded dirty-qubit and GPI/qutrit routes already exploit finite algebras and
avoid unbounded n amplification. They receive the same encoded consumer and
readout resources. No new device advantage over them follows from this note.
