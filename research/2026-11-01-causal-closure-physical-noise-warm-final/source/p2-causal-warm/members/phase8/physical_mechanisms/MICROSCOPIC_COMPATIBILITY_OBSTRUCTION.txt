MICROSCOPIC COMPATIBILITY: WHAT THE PRESENT PHYSICAL CERTIFICATE DOES NOT EARN
Phase8 physical-mechanisms collaborator. 1 November2026.

DECISION
========
The exact hot-bus construction is a useful physical-channel principle and a
serious ordinary competitor. Existing longitudinal-control papers do NOT,
by themselves, supply the combined strong-force, weak-loss, negligible-
dispersive, negligible-nonlinearity operating regime used in the current
certificate. The most directly demonstrated pump mechanism links the desired
force to a residual dispersive term. Setting that term to zero independently
would be an invented actuator capability.

The response should retain the mathematical/architectural advance and explicitly
withhold a device or total-resource advantage. A bounded-mediator ordinary
route remains attractive because these large-occupation amplification terms
are absent from its finite-dimensional norm budget. This is not a proof that
all longitudinal buses lose: an earned symmetry/echo or a stronger weighted
admission might avoid the crude sufficient bounds below.

1. PRIMARY SOURCE CHECKS
========================

Touzard et al., arXiv1809.06964 / PRL122,080502, effective Hamiltonian Eq1:

 H=-alpha q†²q²/2 + ζ(t)(q†q-1/2)(a+a†) - χ(q†q)n.

Thus resonant conditional displacement is demonstrated TOGETHER WITH residual
q†q n coupling. In the paper's device, alpha/2pi=221MHz,
chi/2pi approximately100kHz, and zeta/2pi=1.28MHz for the displayed pulse.
Its supplement derives ζ=sqrt(2alpha chi)|xi| with xi=drive/detuning and
reports that heating makes the measurement non-QND around |xi|≈1.
The filter-mode implementation has a related square-root coupling relation
and a Purcell/lifetime tradeoff. Nonadiabatic turn-on adds qubit-transition
terms; a ramp slow compared with inverse detuning suppresses them, not an
instantaneous switch. Reference:
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.06964
 Relevant sections: Eq1 and main apparatus parameters; supplement driven
 Josephson expansion and 'With the filter mode'.

Royer et al., arXiv1603.04424 / Quantum1,11, distinguish their exact
longitudinal Hamiltonian from perturbative dispersive gates. The stated absence
of residual Hamiltonian terms is conditional on that exact longitudinal model.
The main text explicitly says the favorable gate analysis assumes no χ n Z,
and recommends reducing or avoiding it. Their approximate polaron-frame master
equation's supplement also states vacuum-based simplifications and secular
conditions. It cannot be cited as a microscopic proof of χ=0 for every physical
implementation. Reference:
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/1603.04424

Didier, Bourassa and Blais, arXiv1504.04002 / PRL115,203601, analyze a
longitudinal-readout circuit. The supplement includes second-order interactions
(a+a†)^2(Λx X+Λz Z). Their displayed parameters give a dispersive-like
chi_z/2pi about2.6MHz (5.3MHz in a lumped-element limit), as well as resonator
Kerr K/2pi=48kHz for a13-junction array. Arrays reduce Kerr approximately as
inverse array-length squared in that model; they do not make it exactly zero.
Reference:
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/1504.04002
 Relevant sections: array nonlinearity and 'Higher-order interaction terms'.

Richer and DiVincenzo, arXiv1511.06138 / PRB93,134501, offer a more promising
symmetry route. Their coupling is even in the qubit variable and odd in the
resonator variable, cos(phi_q/2)sin(phi_r/2), suppressing the unwanted even
resonator interaction in the ideal symmetric circuit. Projection/expansion
still gives Z[(a+a†)+O((a+a†)^3)]. Junction arrays can reduce the higher-order
terms; their Eq17 explicitly uses a series approximation. Junction asymmetry
adds an unwanted term with different parity, and the paper treats this in an
appendix. Two-level truncation, finite array modes, physical modulation and
asymmetry therefore remain part of a microscopic admission.
Reference:
 https://arxiv.org/pdf/1511.06138
 Relevant sections: Eqs15–17 and AppendixB.

2. A CORRECT INPUT-SECTOR DUHAMEL BOUND
=====================================

It would be incorrect to write ||n||<=N throughout a driven pulse merely
because the input had n<=N. The linear reference dynamics can leave that sector.
Here is a sufficient bound which DOES allow that excursion.

Inside a fixed-Z force window the reference Heisenberg oscillator is

 a(t)=r(t)a0 + beta_Z(t)+sqrt(1-r(t)^2)b_t,

where b_t is a fresh thermal mode with occupation<=nb. It is independent of
arbitrary data-apparatus-reference correlations in the old input. beta_Z acts
on the commuting data labels and has operator norm<=B. On input P_(n<=N), let
A=sqrt(N)+B and define

 F_m=sum_(k=0)^m binom(m,k) A^(m-k) sqrt(k!) nb^(k/2).

The triangle inequality and thermal factorial moments give
||a^m U_ref(t)P_N||=||a(t)^m P_N||<=F_m. Hence

 ||n U_ref P_N|| <=sqrt(F_2^2+F_1^2),
 ||n(n-1) U_ref P_N|| <=sqrt(F_4^2+4F_3^2+2F_2^2).            (1)

The second identity uses [n(n-1)]²=n_under4+4 n_under3+2 n_under2.
This is a dilation/vector bound with the fresh thermal purification. It permits
reference occupation above N. During data-only control intervals with force
off one may use B=0; the exposure still counts. Each fixed-Z primitive begins
at an input sector supplied by root's replacement lemma.

For N320, B1.502 (covering the declared readout force at the lower actual loss
rate and the smaller gate excursion), and nb=.1, (1) gives

 C_n=388.8978930,
 C_nn=151693.7976.

For a microscopic residual Hamiltonian

 V(t)=sum_j chi_j(t) Z_j n + [K(t)/2] n(n-1),

Duhamel on the full physical dilation gives the sufficient restricted-input
amplitude budget

 epsilon_micro <= integral [C_n sum_j|chi_j(t)|
                             +(C_nn/2)|K(t)|]dt.             (2)

Data-independent Kerr is included because during a force operation it changes
the field trajectories and can couple to data; a common nonlinear bus term
cannot be silently removed while continuing to use a linear-channel theorem.
One can potentially improve this by including an exactly solvable common
nonlinear part in the reference, but that is a different physical proof.

THESE ARE SUFFICIENT WORST-INPUT-SECTOR BOUNDS, NOT NECESSARY HARDWARE LIMITS.
They are intentionally conservative and may be improved by symmetry, echoed
controls, error-specific receiver analysis or a sharper weighted fault-path
bound. Their failure says this certificate does not apply to a device, not
that the device cannot be useful.

3. WHY THE DEMONSTRATED PUMP DOES NOT MEET THIS CERTIFICATE FOR FREE
================================================================

Using our conventions, F=ζ/2 and chi_eff=chi/2 is the coefficient of Z n.
Touzard's relation gives

 chi_eff = F²/[alpha |xi|²].                                 (3)

For an alpha/g ratio of1000, |xi|<=1 and F/g=3, this yields
chi_eff/g>=.009. Even at the smaller data-force peak1.43988g of the one-bus
parity schedule it yields chi_eff/g>=.0020733. These values are far outside
the crude sufficient allowances in(2). The comparison uses the SAME physical
relation; it does not combine a strong desired force from one device with an
unrelated zero residual from another.

Reducing g relative to alpha can reduce the dimensionless residual but makes
all real gates/readout/control waits longer. Any proposal that uses that move
must recompute data T1/T2 exposure; it does not obtain a free reliable regime.
The source's successful readout discrimination and QND figures are valuable
experimental ingredients, not evidence for our entire repeated encoded task at
an arbitrary desired accuracy.

Known echo/dynamical-decoupling approaches and genuinely symmetry-protected
longitudinal couplers must remain strong competitors. Echo can suppress a
static chi phase but intermediate photon leakage and pulse faults remain in
the actual instrument; inserting an ideal sign reversal after invoking the
zero-chi theorem is invalid. A higher-order geometric shaping calculation
alone likewise cannot certify the nonlinear microscopic Hamiltonian.

4. WHAT SHOULD SURVIVE
=====================

The causal retained-memory interface, the exact fresh-noise decomposition,
the one-bus both-sector parity map, finite robust receiver, and whole-history
admission machinery all survive this compatibility failure. They specify a
concrete task for a microscopic coupler or an alternative bounded mediator.
The next device claim needs actual conditional-force physics, matched port
switching, calibrated dispersion/nonlinearity, data/control error, and the
same receiver resources in one joined model. Until then the result is an
explanatory/construction advance with explicit physical conditions, not a
hardware advantage over GPI/qutrit, cat, erasure or bounded dirty-ancilla routes.
