PHASE 8: DAMPED HOT-BUS GATES WITH AN EXACT RETAINED-STATE INTERFACE
Physical-mechanisms collaborator. 1 November 2026.

DECISION
========
Replace the number-dependent dispersive parity map, for the next comparator,
by ordinary longitudinal/conditional-force geometric gates. Their homogeneous
bosonic scattering is independent of the data at every time. This removes the
mechanism by which old photon number amplifies storage-loss disturbance.
There is still radiation-induced dephasing, and calibration can still spoil the
construction. We derive the whole retained-state channel and a one-bus parity
consumer; we do not infer reliability from small mean oscillator energy.

The underlying longitudinal geometric gate and dissipation-aware loop shaping
are established. Our specific useful contribution here is their exact
retained-state and CP-admission treatment, including two closure conditions,
orthogonal noise trajectories, and a complete parity instrument on both sectors.
The ordinary route must be allowed to adopt this construction with attribution.
No global hardware advantage or microscopic device realization is claimed.

1. EFFECTIVE MODEL AND TWO DIFFERENT CLOSURES
===========================================

Use one harmonic mode a with H_z(t)=f_z(t) a^dag+f_z(t)^* a,
f_z=sum_j z_j f_j, and loss L=sqrt(kappa) a. The finite fresh input is vacuum,
or a white thermal input of occupation n_b, independent of the old apparatus,
data and reference. Initially their joint state is arbitrary and correlated.
The rotating-frame harmonic frequency is known. Qubit Z operators commute
during the force window. Forces are finite and differentiable, not instantaneous
kicks. A finite readout buffer can be present, but its coupling g must actually
be zero throughout this gate; its arbitrary old state is then a spectator.

Let beta_j solve beta_dot=-kappa beta/2-i f_j with beta_j(0)=0. In the
interaction picture of the entire free system+field dilation, the drive is
linear in initial mode operators. Magnus terminates after its second term:

 U_I,z = exp(i phi_z) D_input(xi_z).

The initial oscillator component is

 xi_old,j = -i integral_0^T exp(-kappa t/2) f_j(t) dt.            (1)

This is the INCOMING closure. If it vanishes for every j, relative controlled
isometries obey V_z^dag V_z' = c(z,z') I_old after taking the fresh field state.
Consequently the reduced DATA channel is independent of every old apparatus
state and every old data-apparatus-reference correlation, not just of a mean.
Incoming closure is sufficient for no apparatus-to-data influence.

The conditional final oscillator displacement is

 beta_j(T)=-i exp(-kappa T/2) integral exp(+kappa t/2)f_j(t)dt.  (2)

This is the OUTGOING closure. It is a different condition. Endpoint closure
alone does not prove incoming independence. For example a nonzero positive
real beta(t) with zero endpoints has xi_old=kappa integral exp(-kappa t/2)
beta(t)dt, which is nonzero. An arbitrarily large coherent old amplitude then
produces an order-one data phase despite a perfectly closed endpoint.

With BOTH closures, the controlled displacement has no component in the old
input mode and no component in the retained final oscillator. It occupies fresh
field modes which are orthogonal to the bath direction entering the retained
oscillator. Choose a passive basis containing that finite fresh-mode subspace.
Vacuum, or homogeneous white thermal input, factors over this basis. The exact
reduced joint channel therefore factors:

 Lambda_DM = Phi_D tensor E_free,M.                           (3)

This is an equality on arbitrary joint DM and arbitrary external references.
Gate radiation is traced and nonreturning in this channel statement. A later
measurement/use of a retained radiation record is a larger instrument requiring
its own proof. The product identity is stronger than a statement about means.
E_free is the actual attenuating/thermalizing channel during the full window;
it is not a reset. Arbitrarily hot or nonGaussian old memory is allowed within
the declared linear model. Fresh thermal fluctuations are retained in Phi_D.

For several independent Markov loss ports all coupling to a, tracing every
outgoing port gives the same reduced master equation with total kappa and
n_eff=sum_l kappa_l n_l/kappa. Thus the reduced DM theorem can use n_eff even
if port temperatures differ. This statement does NOT supply a factorization
of an instrument which measures selected unequal-temperature outgoing ports;
conditional receiver histories must be derived for that actual instrument.

Only incoming closure is required when EVERY subsequent device satisfies
Tr_M Lambda=Phi_D Tr_M for arbitrary correlated inputs. DATA-SIDE interventions and
measurements then compose exactly using reduced data channels; postselection
does not reintroduce old memory. Outgoing closure remains valuable for a
product retained channel, energy accounting, and compatibility with consumers
which do not have this uniform incoming property. Root is developing this
more general causal principle; do not present outgoing closure as universally
necessary for reliable repeatability.

2. RADIATION GRAM MATRIX AND EXACT LOCAL PAULI NOISE
==================================================

Choose beta_1=x(t) real and beta_2=i y(t), with both endpoints zero, both
incoming closures, and integral x(t)y(t)dt=0. Then

 f_1=i[x_dot+kappa x/2], f_2=-[y_dot+kappa y/2],
 Theta=integral (x y_dot-y x_dot)dt,
 Gx=kappa integral x^2 dt, Gy=kappa integral y^2 dt.             (4)

The coherent gate is exp(i Theta Z1 Z2). The two radiation vectors have Gram
matrix diag(Gx,Gy). There is no imaginary cross-Gram term, and their fresh
thermal modes are independent. Tracing those modes gives EXACT independent
Pauli Z channels, not an independent-fault assumption:

 p_j=[1-exp{-2(2n_b+1)G_j}]/2.
 Phi_D = U_Theta o [(1-p_1)Id+p_1 Ad_Z1]
                   o [(1-p_2)Id+p_2 Ad_Z2].                   (5)

Equation(3) and (5) provide a true repeated-use CP decomposition. The old bus
can contain a previous fault's arbitrary state; the next nominal gate still
has the same channel. The selected fresh-input assumption is essential.
Unmodelled Kerr, detuning, residual exchange coupling or output feedback can
violate it or the closures and must be admitted as real noise.

3. FINITE SMOOTH CONSTRUCTION
============================

Let q=pi t/T, X=sin^2(q) sin(2q),
Y=sin^2(q)[cos(2q)+1/2]. Both X,Y and their first derivatives vanish at the
endpoints, and both have zero integral. Define

 x=A exp(kappa t/2)X,
 y=B exp(kappa t/2)(Y-cX),
 c=integral exp(kappa t)XY / integral exp(kappa t)X^2.           (6)

Thus integral exp(-kappa t/2)beta_j=0. Integration by parts gives (1)=0;
the zero endpoint gives (2)=0. The definition of c gives integral xy=0.
Choose AB so Theta=pi/4, then choose A/B for the actual force/noise objective.
All ramps are already in (6), and the force begins and ends at zero.

The script double_closed_hot_bus.py provides waveforms and exact-integral
numerical checks. It has two-qubit schedules at per-actuator caps. Those caps
must NOT be used as the total shared-bus force cap of the parity construction.

For the ONE-BUS PARITY construction below, the actual largest force is

 max_t sqrt(4 |f_x(t)|^2+|f_y(t)|^2),                           (7)

because J_D=Z1+Z2 can have magnitude 2. There are THREE real conditional-force
actuators: one on each data qubit and one on the readout ancilla. The two data
actuators share the x waveform; this does not make them physically one control.

optimize_one_bus_parity.py optimizes only the scale ratio A/B and the shortest
cap-satisfying duration in family (6). At kappa=1e-4, total force cap 3, it gives
A/B scale ratio 0.7498337568 relative to equal radiation energies. The safe
rounded schedule T=1.840 has:

 total force peak                 2.99705500196
 per data force peak              1.43988243322
 ancilla force peak               2.76946536902
 peak force slews                 6.72586198, 17.28379548
 integral x^2 dt                  0.0511172904321
 integral y^2 dt                  0.161699200632
 Gx                              5.11172904321e-6
 Gy                              1.61699200632e-5.

The raw optimization used sampled peaks; the rounded schedule was checked on
100001 points with finite margin. This is not interval arithmetic or a global
optimal-control certificate. The resulting loss contribution below is a
complete physical contribution in this model, not a total operation error.

4. ONE BUS, ONE ANCILLA, BOTH PARITY SECTORS
==========================================

Use the same bus with force

 f_z(t)=(Z1+Z2)f_x(t)+Z_A f_y(t),

where A is a bounded ancilla. It implements

 U=exp[i(pi/4) Z_A(Z1+Z2)].                                  (8)

Prepare A in |+>, apply (8), measure X_A, and add Z1 to the actual Pauli frame
when X_A=-. The exact zero-noise Kraus operators are

 K_(X=+)=P_-; K_(X=-)=i Z1 P_+.

Thus the frame correction produces a true parity instrument preserving BOTH
sectors. There are no extra physical S gates. Real ancilla preparation and
readout are still required; they are not replaced by these two ideal kets.

The radiation channel has independent Gaussian collective dephasing in
J_D=Z1+Z2 and ordinary ancilla Z dephasing. Inside an odd parity sector,
J_D=0, so its data error is zero. Inside even parity, the coherence is multiplied
by exp[-8(2n_b+1)Gx], exactly equivalent to a Z1 error with probability

 p_D,+=[1-exp{-8(2n_b+1)Gx}]/2; p_D,-=0,
 p_A=[1-exp{-2(2n_b+1)Gy}]/2.                                (9)

The bad data effect is p_D,+ P_+ <= p_D,+ I. It need not be a scalar effect
or an independently tossed coin. Iterating this CP inequality proves, for
specified operation locations and arbitrary actual preceding histories,
probability at most product p_D,max for their bad tags. The independent
ancilla Z channel is derived from orthogonal fresh field modes. An ancilla Z
error both flips the parity report and, after the declared correction, adds
Z1 to the data frame. Those two consequences are the SAME event.

For schedule(7), vacuum fields:
 p_D,+=2.04464981022e-5; p_A=1.61696585997e-5;
 union upper bound for either=3.66161567018e-5.
At homogeneous fresh n_b=0.1 these become 2.45356973873e-5 and
1.94035275692e-5. Initial bus occupation does not enter these expressions.

A single ancilla error during this diagonal interaction can induce a diagonal
operator on the two data qubits and corrupt the report. The diagonal algebra
span{I,Z1,Z2,Z1Z2}=span{I,Z1} Alg(Z1Z2) is one data-error representative modulo
this measured gauge. A data fault on one participant remains at that support
up to the same gauge, ancilla and bus operators. This is the physical support
property needed by the inherited receiver; it does not repair arbitrary
control errors that flip both data qubits or let a module touch extra sites.

5. CONSTANT COMPLEX GAIN ERRORS AND THE RANDOM-KEY CAVEAT
======================================================

Let the actual constant within-window force gains be g1,g2,gA. They may drift
between operations. Each gain multiplies an already null waveform, so both
closures remain exact without any bound on old bus energy. The geometric
angles become theta_j=(pi/4)Re(g_j^*gA); write delta_j=theta_j-pi/4.
The reduced noise within parity s is still an exact Z1 channel:

 p_D,s=[1-exp{-2(2n_b+1)Gx |g1+s g2|^2}]/2,
 p_A=[1-exp{-2(2n_b+1)Gy |gA|^2}]/2.                          (10)

These bounds may be maximized over an admitted constant-gain range. If a gain
varies DURING the pulse, it is a shape error, not covered by this theorem.

A whole-pulse timing translation f_j(t-tau_j) is another allowed physical
control variation if finite guard intervals contain the entire waveform. Both
Laplace integrals are multiplied by nonzero exponentials, so both zeros remain
exact. A common shift preserves phase and noise. Relative shifts can change the
geometric phase and the radiation Gram matrix, but do not couple old bus energy
back to the data. Their resulting finite-dimensional data-channel error must be
computed/admitted, and every guard interval contributes actual data/control
waiting exposure. General time-dependent stretching or distortion is different.


Default complete-instrument treatment: gain errors leave coherent wrong-parity
amplitudes O(|delta1|+|delta2|). Their joint local coherent-noise admission can
use the bounded operator norm of delta1 Z1 Z_A+delta2 Z2 Z_A. It is independent
of bus energy and requires no random-bit assumption. A small marginal wrong
report probability is not an exact CP correct-report decomposition here.

There is a potentially cheaper ordinary randomized-compiling option. Flip the
ancilla force with a random orientation r=+/-1. At ideal angles,
U_-=-(Z1Z2)U_+, so a negative orientation requires the known parity Pauli-frame
factor S=Z1Z2. Merely reversing the pulse and forgetting that factor DOES NOT
twirl the coherent error. After the correct orientation and outcome frames,
and AFTER AVERAGING A TRUE FRESH UNBIASED ORIENTATION, the exact instrument is
projective parity with sector-dependent report error

 p_cal,+ = sin^2(delta1+delta2),
 p_cal,- = sin^2(delta1-delta2).                              (11)

This calibration report flip does not add a data error after those frames.
It composes with (10); the ancilla Z event adds Z1 and flips the report, while
the data bath adds only Z1. verify_one_bus_parity.py directly verifies the
complete 4x4 Schur matrices, including unequal complex gains and thermal input.
Its largest discrepancy is 1.11e-16. Omitting the orientation frame leaves
cross-sector coefficients 0.01735 in the saved stress example.

CRITICAL SCOPE: equation(11) is for the orientation-averaged quantum/report
channel. If the complete delivered instrument retains the orientation key,
each key block still has coherent O(delta) error. A later apparatus or receiver
allowed to use that key can invalidate a claimed fresh Pauli-coin admission.
One must also earn sign-covariant hardware response: the same admitted gain
must govern both orientations. A key alone does not fix sign-dependent errors.
One must earn physical/operational key retirement after the frame has actually
been compiled, with no later coupling to its record, or prove a task-level
randomized-compiling statement with that allowed information. Generating bits,
protecting the controller, implementing frames, and retiring their record have
finite exposure and faults. This note does NOT grant a perfect random coin,
perfect erasure of its record, or free extra physical frame pulses. The default
recommendation is the bounded coherent treatment above until those conditions
are proved for the actual consumer.

6. POLE ERRORS, SENSITIVITY, AND WHAT THIS DOES NOT SOLVE
=======================================================

An actual detuning Delta and damping kappa' change the incoming coefficient to
-i integral exp[(-kappa'/2+iDelta)t] f_j(t)dt, and the outgoing coefficient to
-i integral exp[-(kappa'/2+iDelta)(T-t)] f_j(t)dt. Neither is generally zero.
The simple family already has strong low-frequency cancellation: the two
nominal roots +/-kappa/2 nearly coincide for small kappa, giving a near double
zero. This is genuine coefficient cancellation, not a state-uniform norm.

hot_bus_pole_sensitivity.py keeps BOTH the earlier T=1.28 two-qubit
per-actuator schedule and the actual T=1.84 shared-parity schedule. For the
latter, kappa_nom=1e-4, ratio=.7498337568074147:
 Delta=.001: incoming amplitudes (8.56235e-8,3.5301e-11);
 Delta=.01:  incoming amplitudes (8.52025e-6,3.50837e-8);
 +/-10% kappa alone: largest amplitude about 4.473e-11.
Outgoing amplitudes are essentially the same in these examples. The data
combination in the parity gate can multiply the x coefficient by 2. These are
actual single-window coefficients, not bounds for all faulty histories.

Higher derivative flatness can be imposed by linear constraints
integral t^q exp(+/-kappa t/2) f_j(t)dt=0 for q=0,...,p.
A sufficiently rich finite smooth basis has a nullspace. It costs time, force
or phase-space area and is not a free improvement. No such optimized higher-
order construction is claimed in this packet. An unknown continuum of poles
cannot be rejected exactly by a nonzero finite-window force.

Real pole/shape errors therefore require a finite-life moment/tail or coherent
bath admission, as root and the joint-noise collaborator are developing. So do
nonlinear actuator corrections, residual data coupling during readout, and
faults altering the declared linear couplings. The old retained-state argument
is strongest for gain changes, not for arbitrary hardware perturbations.

7. REAL PREPARATION, READOUT, SWITCHING, AND WAITING
==================================================

The same storage a can be coupled, through a finite controlled beam splitter
g(t), to a damped readout mode m. During the gate g=0; during ancilla readout,
both DATA conditional forces are zero, and the ancilla alone supplies a
longitudinal readout force. Reject both initial-mode response columns in the
finite homodyne filter, as in the inherited finite receiver. A rank-one
ancilla-Z projective good component has uniform admission even for arbitrary
old apparatus; it supports actual measurement+conditional-X preparation, then
an actual H for |+>. The terminal X readout uses another actual H followed by
that finite Z readout. Root is constructing the explicit readout waveform and
its own complete instrument, rather than borrowing an incompatible ideal bit.

The real macro duration contains ancilla preparation, conditional X if needed,
two H operations, gate T, both finite converter-switch intervals, readout,
decision latency and any required settling guard. If readout waveforms already
include switching, do not add the same time twice. During every interval,
continuing data/control noise must be admitted. With data forces actually zero,
loss or heating of the uncoupled bus during idle/switching does not damage data;
its resulting arbitrary state is accepted by the next nominal incoming-null
operation. This is the reason a long cooldown is unnecessary in this model.
It does not make data waiting noise vanish. A residual force is an actual shape
error and belongs in the joint noise budget.

No fresh final ancilla, flawless physical flag, noiseless controller, or
instantaneous return-to-vacuum is used in the mechanism theorem. The complete
receiver must use the actual finite ancilla contract and the inherited repeated
boundary invariant, including a surviving old error plus a new event.
