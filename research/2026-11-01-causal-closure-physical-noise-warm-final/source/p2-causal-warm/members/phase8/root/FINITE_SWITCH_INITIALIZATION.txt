FINITE INITIALIZATION OF THE STRONG-DEFECT SWITCH MODEL
Astra P2 / Phase8. Ordinary finite preparation joined to the hybrid bound.

The hybrid onset proof must not silently start every hardware defect variable
in a perfect working state. Here is an explicit sufficient initialization for
the PARTICULAR two-state local switch model. It is not an empirical claim
about all tunable couplers, metastable devices, or common electronics.

Let working/bad be two classical pointer sectors. During a real preparation
interval tau, a local repair transition bad->working has rate at least mu_-,
and the reverse transition has rate at most lambda_+. Both are bounded
physical Markov transition rates; the other apparatus can remain correlated.
The pointer population obeys

 p_bad'<=lambda_+-(lambda_++mu_-)p_bad.

Consequently, for ANY initial population,

 q0= lambda_+/(lambda_++mu_-)
     +mu_-/(lambda_++mu_-)*exp[-(lambda_++mu_-)tau]

is a uniform upper bound on bad population at the end of this finite interval.
Equivalently the local completed preparation's bad-sector effect is <=q0 I.
This is a true positive effect bound, not a presumed independent error coin.
With private local preparation channels, arbitrary initial correlations among
switches do not spoil the joint subset bound product_i q0_i.

If the finite switch is represented quantum mechanically, the argument uses
block-diagonal working/bad controlled dynamics and jump transitions between
them. Off-diagonal pointer operators never feed the measured diagonal sector
or data reduced channel in that model; the analytical sector labels are valid.
Transverse coherent defect dynamics outside this structure require a bounded
extra-generator or dephasing comparison. They are not covered by pretending a
quantum fluctuation is a classical coin.

Combine these initial positive tags with first-onset intensities after
preparation. Their counting generating function is bounded coefficient-wise
by exp[z(sum_i q0_i+sum_i integral lambda_bar_i dt)]. Hence the ever-bad-domain
tail may use

 Lambda_effective=sum_i q0_i+sum_i integral_actual_lifetime lambda_bar_i(t)dt

in Lambda_effective^(b+1)/(b+1)!. Already counted initial domains remain
ever-bad in the proof, whether or not they physically repair. A shared reset
source that can damage several domains is one common physical event and does
not satisfy a fictitious product of local preparations.

The actual tau, switching transients, force exposure, and thermal changes must
be added to startup. The4.15/g oscillator admission figure does NOT include an
unacquired switch initialization duration. If these intervals overlap, that
overlap and the corresponding energy envelope must be physically justified.
Otherwise use their actual serial sum. Any heating before oscillator admission
changes its initial mean-energy budget and availability calculation.

No numerical mu,lambda or switch fidelity has been acquired here. This is the
finite mechanism/parameter condition by which the initial switch requirement
can be supplied. If the hardware does not meet it, initial bad domains must be
bounded by another physical preparation or charged as an initial global error.
It cannot be omitted from an end-to-end logical accuracy claim.
