1. What must be removed from an old apparatus?
=============================================
Let D be the useful data, M the apparatus that survives an operation, R an
arbitrary reference, and F the fresh input field. M can be nonGaussian, warm,
and entangled with D and R. F is independent of DMR. Returning field modes are
part of M, not fresh F. The delivered record is C; analog records that are
actually retained and later used must also be included.

The weakest useful exact interface is record-inclusive absence of influence:

 Tr_M' Lambda_DC M' = I_DC o Tr_M                         (1)

for one fixed quantum-classical instrument I and EVERY input DMR. This is an
identity of complete maps, not a statement only on product inputs. Composing
such instruments with arbitrary causal data/record operations preserves (1)
over every unnormalized history. Induct on the operations, keeping R and all
previous records. Conditional probabilities may be arbitrarily large on a
rare normalized branch; the complete-history identity does not require a
conditional energy estimate.

Outcome-summed absence of influence is weaker than (1). A later measurement of
an old apparatus correlated with D can condition D even if the averaged D map
was independent of M. Thus neither a data marginal nor an average cavity
occupation is the desired instrument contract.

A stronger interface, used for the main fault construction, is

 Lambda_DM = Phi_D tensor E_M.                           (2)

E_M can attenuate, thermalize and retain arbitrary old information. It need not
prepare vacuum or any pure state. Equation(2) makes the nominal data noise
independent of the old apparatus and keeps a new local fault from using a
surviving bath record through a later unmarked operation.

2. Constructive criterion for passive linear mediators

Let P_old project onto incoming M and P_ret onto outgoing M'. Require

 P_old S^dag d_j=0,       P_ret d_j=0.                    (4)

These are two different linear constraints on each applied waveform.

operator with incoming displacement S^dag(d_z-d_z'). If its old component is

arbitrary colored/squeezed/returning fields are not automatically admitted.
