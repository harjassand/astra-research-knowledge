FINITE HETERODYNE ADMISSION BEFORE USEFUL QUANTUM INPUT
Astra P2 / Phase 8. Root construction. 1 November 2026.

Problem and result
==================
The repeated-use proof needs an exponential moment, not an initial mean alone.
Finite passive cooling cannot turn an arbitrary heavy-tailed input into a
uniform exponential-moment family. The earlier n<=100 precondition is therefore
replaced, in this OPTIONAL explicit startup route, by a real finite heterodyne
accept/abort instrument. The initial mean is used only for availability and a
once-only startup noise comparison. The accepted instrument earns the moment.

This occurs before useful quantum input is loaded or encoded. It is not a
measurement of a running logical qubit. All retained-state correlations created
during later operations remain allowed. The extra simultaneous two-quadrature
receiver is a real physical capability and is also available to competitors.
Neither arbitrary initially correlated useful data nor a free startup flag is
assumed. Raw heterodyne values are internal and retired after the accept bit.

1. The actual acceptance instrument
==================================
Passively cool with the same physical port, then observe it for a finite
window T. Uniformly integrate both heterodyne quadratures: f(t)=1/sqrt(T).
Use two16-bit ADCs on[-16,16]. Their step is1/2048 in normalized complex-amplitude
coordinates. Accept exactly when the integer squared radius is at most the
radius corresponding to R=4. Saturation at +/-16 produces an abort. The sum
of two squares and comparison fit a40-bit signed protected register.

This is a finite analog integrator followed by two finite ADCs and an integer
test; it does not require an optical quantum mode selector or photon counter.
The physical integrator response and detector covariance/efficiency are stated
model conditions. Their faults and actual decision latency are charged below.

Let delta_R=sqrt(2)/(4096), R_plus=4+delta_R, R_minus=4-delta_R.
Every actually accepted uncorrupted ADC record has true |beta|<=R_plus.
Conversely |beta|<=R_minus guarantees acceptance. A clipped integrated output
cannot be accepted because its integer magnitude already exceeds the radius.

For ideal heterodyne on one mode, the disk effect is

 E_R=integral_(|beta|<=R) |beta><beta| d^2 beta/pi.

For ANY c>0, its positive-operator bound is

 E_R <= exp(cR^2)/(1+c) * (1+c)^(-n).                  (1)

Proof: 1_(|beta|<=R)<=exp[c(R^2-|beta|^2)]. Integrate the Gaussian-weighted
coherent-state POVM in the number basis. This is an operator inequality;
it remains valid on arbitrary old apparatus-reference correlations.

2. Joint retained-state tilted effect
====================================
The passive attenuator has surviving intensity r. Its collective complementary
output b and retained mode are a beam-splitter pair with one homogeneous fresh
thermal mode of occupation n_b. An actual normalized measured temporal mode
has overlap squared mu with b. Every remaining orthogonal output mode is an
independent thermal mode because the fresh field is homogeneous. Intrinsic
and readout loss ports are both included in r and in this decomposition.

Let physical heterodyne efficiency be eta_d. Put eta0=eta_d c/(1+c). Tracing
the thermal mode mismatch in the majorant(1) gives

 A=[1+eta0(1-mu)n_b]^(-1),   eta=eta0 mu A.             (2)

Thus the effective output effect is A :exp(-eta b^dag b):, multiplied by
exp(cR_plus^2)/(1+c). This is merely a positive majorant for the real disk
acceptance. No no-click detector or ideal photon flag is introduced.

For t=exp(theta)-1, write

 u=r t-eta(1-r),
 v=sqrt[r(1-r)](t+eta),
 w=(1-r)t-eta r.

In the joint output, e^(theta n_ret) commutes with the acceptance effect.
Normal ordering and the thermal P-representation Gaussian integral give

 T_accept^*(e^(theta n_ret))
 <= K [1+(u+n_b t eta)/(1-n_b w)]^(n_old),            (3)

 K=exp(cR_plus^2)/(1+c) * A/(1-n_b w).

One convenient derivation expands the joint exponent as
:exp[u n_old+v(a^dag b+b^dag a)+w b^dag b]:.
Integrating the thermal variable yields coefficient
u+n_b v^2/(1-n_b w). The identity u w-v^2=-t eta
gives(3). Assume1-n_b w>0. If

             t(r+n_b eta)<=eta(1-r),                 (4)

the number base is at most one, and the accepted tilted effect is bounded
by K I on the ENTIRE old oscillator space. Thus

 Tr[e^(theta n_ret) T_accept(rho)] <= K Tr(rho)        (5)

for any initial state, even one with no finite exponential moments.
This is the physically earned tail admission missing from a mean-only bound.

3. Finite uniform numbers and real availability
==============================================
Use the same port envelopes as the main meter: intrinsic kappa in
[9e-5,1.1e-4]g, readout flat rate in[3.996,4.004]g, sin^2 opening and closing
ramps each .1/g, homogeneous n_b<=.1, |Delta|<=1e-6g, and eta_d in[.5,1].
The filter observation is T=.8/g. Set theta=.8,c=1.

The full window gives r<=.06097618. The central flat portion alone bounds
the constant filter's old-response integral below .63160936. Discarding both
positive ramp contributions gives mu>=.49866298. Hence eta>=.12312259,
and the two sides of(4) are at most .08981799 and at least .11561505.
These are robust margins; there is no unknown-pole matched filter.

Uniformly, A<=1 and1-n_b w>=1-.1[exp(.8)-1]. Therefore

 log K <=15.440356 before finalization.                (6)

The real .05/g finalization slot also has an exponential-moment bound. Under
force-free thermal attenuation r_f, normal ordering gives
M_out<=M_in/[1-n_b(1-r_f)(e^theta-1)]. With the readout port off and intrinsic
rate<=1.1e-4g, this adds at most6.741e-7 to log M_.8. This term is included
in the final JSON; a mean estimate alone would not justify that interval.

For one isolated module, 1.2/g preceding cooling from an admitted mean
Tr(n rho)<=100, allowing residual force<=1e-7g, gives mean<=1.371304.
Markov's inequality for the ACTUAL heterodyne radius gives acceptance at
least .8455 when eta_d may be as high as1. Normalizing(5) then still gives
log M_.8<15.61, below the repeated-operation requirement20. The total
startup is1.2+.8+.05=2.05/g, including finite decision/finalization time.
See the exact numerical output rather than rounded text for the tighter value.

The mean is used here ONLY to lower-bound a real success probability. Equation
(5), not the mean, supplies the high-energy tail of the accepted state.
If the initial mean requirement is unavailable, the unnormalized theorem(5)
still holds, but no uniform availability or conditional-quality claim follows.

4. Many correlated initial modes: do not multiply conditioned acceptances
=======================================================================
For m modules, arbitrary cross-mode correlations are permitted. Local accepted
maps commute. Equation(3) for one module, and acceptance effects<=I on every
other module, imply

 Tr[e^(theta n_i) rho_all-accepted] <= K

for every i. A lower bound on the JOINT success probability is still needed
before normalizing. Marginal success estimates cannot simply be multiplied.

A longer finite preceding cooldown supplies a safe bound. Let its retained
intensity be r0. The complete initial-to-detected phase-insensitive channel
has intensity a_i<=r0 and added thermal occupation N_i<=n_b. It factors as
pure loss of transmissivity a_i/G_i followed by a quantum-limited amplifier
G_i=1+N_i. The radial heterodyne effect is diagonal, so old number coherences
do not affect its marginal probability. From an old Fock state n, the event
that no initial photon survives the loss has probability(1-a_i/G_i)^n. That
component produces a thermal heterodyne outcome with tail exp(-R_minus^2/G_i).
Bounding the remaining component by probability one gives

 P_i(abort)<=exp[-R_minus^2/(1+n_b)]+r0 <n_i>.

The ordinary union bound, without independence, yields

 P(all accept)>=1-m exp[-R_minus^2/(1+n_b)]-r0 sum_i<n_i>. (7)

For preceding cooldown3.3/g with the same finite ramps, and up to217 modules
of initial mean at most100 each, the reference success bound exceeds .939.
Its full parallel startup is3.3+.8+.05=4.15/g. Equation(5) divided by this
joint probability gives log M_.8<15.51 for every accepted module, still below20.
The40-module memory array has a larger success margin. These are illustrative
apparatus counts, not a newly compiled CNOT resource advantage.

Parallel startup needs actual parallel readout/integration channels; serial
hardware pays serial waiting and all intervening exposure. Two quadrature
chains per observed port, their ADCs, protected registers and retirement are
real additional resources. The same option belongs to every conventional
competitor using the same physical ports.

5. Startup residuals are paid once, not turned into an invalid high moment
=======================================================================
Take the comparison reference to have zero unwanted force during startup,
but the SAME actual damping, detuning, thermal line, receiver and ADC. A
bounded unwanted linear force of size u gives the dilation estimate

 epsilon_start <= sum_i integral u_i(t)
                     [sqrt(E_i(t))+sqrt(E_i(t)+1)]dt.  (8)

Here epsilon_start is a HALF trace-distance bound. The actual prefix mean
E_i(t) is physically bounded by the damped energy inequality, including all
ramps and finalization. The code uses the flat cooling-rate floor to integrate
sqrt(E)+sqrt(E+1), while discarding cooling during the opening ramp and using
the ensuing conservative energy at every remaining interval. The previous
loose E<=100 calculation is preserved as a diagnostic. The complete217-mode
startup cost is the SUM of the actual integrated amplitude bounds, not a
collection of independent probabilities. No time interval is removed.

First compare the ACTUAL entire startup experiment with this reference.
Then run the subsequent ACTUAL noisy computation on the reference's accepted
state. Contractivity pays epsilon_start once at the beginning, and the later
moment proof applies to this comparison experiment. A small trace distance
alone would not imply a finite moment for the actual accepted state; that
invalid inference is explicitly avoided.

Physical detector/ADC/register/decision faults and startup switch outages add
their actual startup map-distance or jump-intensity contributions. A loose
conditional-state comparison costs at most2 epsilon_start/(p_ref-epsilon_start),
assuming a positive denominator. Keeping an explicit abort outcome permits
the simpler unnormalized whole-experiment bound. No hidden trial is free:
the present protocol makes one finite attempt; an abort returns before loading
useful quantum input. A retry policy requires its own full elapsed-time ledger.

Scientific scope
================
This is a constructive finite admission primitive in the stated linear,
homogeneous-thermal detector model. It reduces an assumed hard photon cap
to an initial mean condition plus a real acceptance measurement and explicit
availability/accuracy cost. It does not supply a measured analog receiver,
unconditional success from arbitrary infinite energy, or a new hardware win.
The independent audit is in fresh_physical_route; reproduction is
finite_heterodyne_admission.py and its JSON result.
