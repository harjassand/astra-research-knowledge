Purpose and actual gain
=======================
The earlier compact Ising block protects a quantum input only in a strong
logical-symmetry noise algebra. Here the code protects BOTH Pauli sectors.
Finite coherent controllers replace the engineered DATA dissipators of a
thermal memory by bounded-support HAMILTONIAN ports and ONE-SITE controller
thermal channels. The actual data/controller evolution is quantum and may
be strongly non-Markovian after the controllers are traced out. There is
no g/Gamma limit, no secular data-only generator and no nT implementation
error. The nominal product thermal encoder remains the reference encoder.

This is a different restricted model: it requires a common stationary
thermal REFERENCE for the WHOLE specified device, including its couplers
and controller reservoirs. It admits thermal creation and repair of every
single-qubit X,Y,Z error. It does not require an exact conserved logical
flip or declare one bare-qubit error type absent. Energy selectors remain
bounded-arity physical Hamiltonian primitives; no two-body compiler is proved.

1. Input thermal-code interface and existing result actually used
===============================================================
Let H_D=sum_(a=1)^m (I-S_a)/2 be a commuting Pauli stabilizer Hamiltonian
on n data qubits, with one logical qubit, check weight <=w, incidence <=D,
and bounded range/density in the supplied geometry. Fix ONE Pauli decoder
and its unitary recovery coordinates

 U_rec H_D U_rec^dagger=I_Q tensor H_S,
 tau_D=exp(-beta H_D)/Z=(I_Q/2) tensor tau_S.                       (1)

Valid syndrome constraints and redundant checks are included in H_S; no
independently assignable syndrome oracle is assumed. Corrected logical
Paulis form the full matrix algebra on Q in these same coordinates.

For every physical data support X of size <=Q_0, require a support-specific
syndrome projector P_X, commuting with tau_D and the corrected logical
algebra, such that

 [A,O_mu]P_X=0 for EVERY operator A supported in X,
 mu=X,Y,Z, and Tr(tau_D(I-P_X))<=p_(Q_0).                          (2)

This is FULL support-algebra protection, including A^dagger and A^dagger A.
P_X may depend on the support but not the unknown operator, logical input,
controller/bath state, or reference. Do not take a global union over all
supports: estimates are applied per term and then summed.

For the primary-qualified even RX/RZ family, the pinned, reviewed Astra
N1239 proof gives this exact shared-decoder interface at fixed beta>beta_c:

 p_Q <=4 exp[(ln4+beta D)Q] 2^(-2^(k-1)),
 n_k<=5 G_fam^(2k), alpha=ln2/(2 ln G_fam)>0.                     (3)

Consequently p_Q<=C_Q exp(-c n_k^alpha) for each fixed Q. The family is
local at bounded density in R^3 at its QUALIFIED fixed construction scale.
The primary paper's explicitly embedded variant has a different proof
status; it is not substituted for this qualified family. (3) is imported,
not re-proved by the five-qubit numerical fixture.

The ordinary stationary/module theorem, reconstructed in the same accepted
source, already protects a nominal thermal encoder under individually
reference-stationary local blocks. It is the strongest existing comparator,
not a new memory criterion claimed here. Sections 2-5 construct finite
physical ports to which it applies without eliminating controllers.
Sections 6-8 give the additional joint correlated-input/resource statement.

2. Exact local energy ports
===========================
For a one-site Pauli seed P_i, let T be its anticommuting checks, t=|T|<=D.
In a joint check eigenconfiguration s_a=+/-1, the energy change is
Delta_P=sum_(a in T) s_a. For omega>0 with omega=t,t-2,...>0, define

 A_(i,P,omega)=P_i Pi_(Delta_P=-omega),
 [H_D,A_(i,P,omega)]=-omega A_(i,P,omega).                         (4)

If t is even also include A_(i,P,0)=P_i Pi_(Delta_P=0), which is Hermitian.
Every A has norm <=1, support inside the whole touching-check neighborhood
of i, size <=Q_b=1+Dw, and bounded physical diameter. Exactly

 P_i=A_(i,P,0)+sum_(omega>0)(A_(i,P,omega)+A_(i,P,omega)^dagger),   (5)

with the zero term omitted when absent. These are finite algebraic check
projectors. They are PHYSICAL coupling operators here, not supplied for
free by frequency labels or by a rotating-wave approximation.

For each seed P=X,Y,Z and each frequency, add a qubit c_a with
reference Hamiltonian omega_a n_a and interaction

 V_a=g_a A_a sigma_a^+ + conjugate(g_a) A_a^dagger sigma_a^-.
 H_ref=H_D+sum_a omega_a n_a,
 H_phys=H_ref+sum_a V_a.                                         (6)

The zero-frequency formula uses Hermitian A_0 and a zero-gap qubit. Supply
one-body channels Gamma_a^- D[sigma_a^-]+Gamma_a^+ D[sigma_a^+] with

 Gamma_a^+ / Gamma_a^- =exp(-beta omega_a), Gamma_a^->0.           (7)

All strengths/rates can be FIXED finite constants independent of n,T.
The proof imposes no ordering between |g_a|, Gamma_a^-, and defect energy.
Coupling amplitudes and phases may be unknown within finite local budgets.

An alternative uses one private multilevel controller per data site: ground
|0> and one level |a> of reference energy omega_a for every local port.
Replace sigma_a^+ by |a><0| and use paired jumps |0><a|,|a><0| obeying (7).
The controller Gibbs state is diag(exp(-beta omega_c))/Z_c. It is still
ONE-SITE loss/gain on a finite-dimensional physical system. Realizing these
transitions on a qubit register would require a further compilation; it is
not one-qubit loss merely because the multilevel controller is one site.

3. The full finite device has the SAME product reference
========================================================
Put tau_C=exp(-beta H_C)/Z_C. Directly from (4),

 [H_ref,V_a]=0, [V_a,tau_D tensor tau_C]=0.                        (8)

Each loss/gain PAIR in (7) fixes tau_C by its two-level population equation.
Therefore the ACTUAL full finite generator

 L=-i[H_phys,.]+sum_a(Gamma_a^- D[sigma_a^-]
                              +Gamma_a^+ D[sigma_a^+])            (9)

fixes sigma=tau_D tensor tau_C EXACTLY. This calculation is on the full
controller Hilbert space, including multiply occupied independent flags.
The multilevel version obeys the same identity.

The stationary reference is generally NOT Gibbs(H_phys). These one-site
reservoirs are engineered model resources; a natural bath for the full
interacting Hamiltonian is not derived. No work-free thermal-device claim
is transferred from the primary self-correcting Hamiltonian result.

The reference is independent of g_a, coupling phases and reservoir speed.
Diagonal controller-frequency shifts and stabilizer-diagonal data fields
also commute with it. Such Hamiltonian shifts do not invalidate (8)-(9)
IF the selector operators and gain/loss ratios remain referenced to the
same nominal H_ref. A natural bath whose rates shift with the physical gap
is not automatically covered by this conditional calibration statement.

Every seed has a positive-frequency component at omega=t when t>0.
At finite beta its controller can be thermally excited, then transfer that
excitation into the data error P_i. The reverse coherent transition plus
loss repairs it. Both directions are present for all X,Y,Z seeds. This
argument uses actual nonzero matrix elements, not inferred classical rates.

4. Protection of the actual ports and the quantum thermal-input bound
====================================================================
All check projectors commute with the corrected logical algebra globally.
Thus a seed's good projector for P_i also protects its A_(i,P,omega) and
the Hermitian V_a in (6). Energy-selecting checks do not themselves add a
logical error. One may use p_1 for each such nominal port. For arbitrary
additional local operators use the full support-algebra p_Q of (2).

Here is the source stationary/module estimate in the present coordinates.
For a purified Q/R input Psi, Schmidt rank <=2, let
 pi=sigma tensor I_R and B=2Psi_QR tensor I_(S,C), so Enc(Psi)=B pi.
If a local block G fixes sigma and has a common full-algebra good P,
then G(BPpi)=B G(Ppi)=-B G((I-P)pi). Since ||B||=2,
|| (I-P)pi ||_1<=2p, triangle and complete boundedness give

 ||(G tensor Id_R) Enc(Psi)||_1<=8||G||_diamond p.                (10)

For a port Hamiltonian, ||-i[V_a,.]||_diamond<=2|g_a|. H_ref and the
controller thermal pairs have ZERO defect on Enc(Psi), independently of
their norms/rates. Apply (10) per port and use full CPTP Duhamel contraction.
For R_max ports per data site, |g_a|<=g_max,

 ||Phi_T Enc_(S,C) - Enc_(S,C)||_diamond
        <=min(2,16 R_max g_max n p_1 T).                         (11)

The output in (11) retains DATA, controllers and reference. After the fixed
data decoder it is a joint Q/C comparison to Id_Q and the SAME tau_C.
No controller is reset or discarded in the physical evolution. Additional
individually reference-stationary local blocks of total diamond norm <=J n
add at most 8J n p_Q T. Balanced controller-only faults have exactly zero
encoder defect. Their indirect effect is already inside the full dynamics.

With (3), (11) is C nT exp(-c n^alpha) at FIXED finite physical parameters.
This earns simultaneous protection of both logical error types for unknown
inputs/reference correlations in a finite coherent-controller model.
It is not a data-only adiabatic approximation and has no nT/gap remainder.

5. It is a metastable quantum memory, without an exact logical symmetry
======================================================================
Take every nominal seed port nonzero and every loss/gain rate positive.
The single-controller star transitions generate the FULL controller matrix
algebra. An operator commuting with every jump and adjoint must therefore
have the form A_D tensor I_C. Commuting also with H_phys forces A_D to
commute with every A_(i,P,omega), hence with each P_i by (5). These Paulis
generate the full data algebra, so A_D is scalar.

For completeness, a faithful stationary sigma gives the dissipation identity
 L^*(X^dagger X)-L^*(X^dagger)X-X^dagger L^*(X)
                         =sum_j [J_j,X]^dagger[J_j,X].
For a fixed point (or an imaginary-axis eigenoperator), tracing against
sigma makes the right-hand side zero. All commutators above vanish; the
Hamiltonian then makes the eigenoperator scalar. Thus the finite nominal
generator has a unique stationary state and no nontrivial peripheral
logical algebra. Its long quantum lifetime is a rare-error consequence,
not an exact conserved logical flip with an excluded complementary axis.
This also rules out crediting the zero-coupling frozen-data limit.

6. Additional joint theorem: an arbitrary correlated finite bath

a faithful reference tau_E, normally tau_C tensor tau_B, and require the
actual joint generator to fix

 sigma=(I_Q/2) tensor tau_S tensor tau_E.                         (12)

This includes the nominal ports, unknown reference-energy-conserving
couplings on fixed physical data/controller supports and the SAME B,
and declared balanced dissipators. A sufficient bath coupling condition
is conservation of H_D+H_C+H_B with tau_B proportional to exp(-beta H_B).
It permits individual DATA energy changes and both Pauli sectors: the
bath/controller supplies the compensating energy. Couplings can be coherent,
correlated, time dependent and unknown, with finite local strength budgets.
Arbitrary dimension/norm bath-only dynamics fixing tau_B has no logical
defect. A bare claim 'finite bath' without (12) or a resource bound is not
this theorem's premise.

Encode only by adding the nominal tau_S and the fixed U_rec^dagger. The
input rho_QER may have ARBITRARY Q/E/R correlations, subject solely to

 rho_E <= M tau_E,  M>=1.                                      (13)

This constrains the AUXILIARY MARGINAL, not its purification or Q/R input.
Define K_0=ln M; minimizing it gives D_max(rho_E||tau_E). The reference may be arbitrarily large
and untouched; its correlations are not charged as if it supplied work.

Require a local operator decomposition of L with Hermitian H_a and jumps

 sum_a ||H_a||<=h n, sum_a ||J_a||^2<=j n.                        (14)

The actual L is GKSL; the sufficient admission above gives such a positive

Global reference-energy neutrality of a local Hamiltonian sum suffices:

7. Logical erasure, the needed product condition, and proof
==========================================================
In recovery coordinates let E_Q(A)=I_Q tensor Tr_Q(A)/2, a norm-contracting
conditional expectation. It is an ANALYTICAL operation only. Set

 H_a^0=E_Q(H_a), J_a^0=E_Q(J_a),
 L^0=-i[sum_a H_a^0,.]+sum_a D[J_a^0].                            (15)

L^0 is a genuine CPTP generator and factors as Id_Q tensor an auxiliary
generator. It evolves the EXISTING C,B; it does not reset them. It may be
nonlocal, which is harmless for this comparison. Define Lambda_E,T^0 by
adding tau_S, applying that auxiliary process, and tracing S.

For a good projector P of a full physical support algebra, twirling the
logical Paulis shows (A-E_Q A)P=P(A-E_Q A)=0. Thus the logical-bad part
is doubly supported on B_a=I-P. For a jump, the anticommutator difference

 K_a=J_a^dagger J_a-(J_a^0)^dagger J_a^0

is ALSO doubly supported on B_a. The proof is not just source-column
protection: write J=I tensor j_0+sum_(mu=X,Y,Z) sigma_mu tensor j_mu.
Then
 E_Q(J^dagger J)-(E_QJ)^dagger(E_QJ)
                   =I tensor sum_(mu!=0) j_mu^dagger j_mu.
Every j_mu P vanishes. Full-algebra protection additionally applies to
J^dagger J, so K_a P=0, and Hermiticity gives P K_a=0. Its norm is
<=2||J_a||^2. Omitting the product condition can give a false zero-error
bound even at zero bad population; a finite countermodel is provided.

For any positive joint state rho, including R, let p_a=Tr(B_a rho).
Hilbert-Schmidt Cauchy-Schwarz gives ||B_a rho||_1<=sqrt(p_a). Hence

 ||[-i(H_a-H_a^0),rho]||_1<=4||H_a||sqrt(p_a),
 ||(D[J_a]-D[J_a^0])(rho)||_1<=6||J_a||^2 sqrt(p_a).              (16)

For the second bound, split the jump difference as
(J-J0)rho J^dagger+J0 rho(J-J0)^dagger (cost <=4||J||^2 sqrt(p_a))
and the K_a anticommutator (cost <=2||J||^2 sqrt(p_a)).

The elementary pinching inequality for a two-dimensional Q gives
 rho_QE<=2 I_Q tensor rho_E.
After adding tau_S, (13) therefore implies rho_system(0)<=4M sigma.
The full ACTUAL CPTP process fixes sigma, so at every time
 rho_system(t)<=4M sigma, and p_a(t)<=4M p_Q.                     (17)
No order inequality for rho_system,R is needed: (16) depends only on
this SYSTEM bad probability and is already uniform over R.

Duhamel with the L^0 CPTP SUFFIX and the ACTUAL prefix, then (14)-(17),
proves, for every input satisfying (13), with ONE input-independent map:

 ||(Dec_D tensor Id_ER)Phi_T Enc_S(rho_QER)
               -(Id_Q tensor Lambda_E,T^0 tensor Id_R)(rho_QER)||_1
       <=min(2,(8h+12j)nT sqrt(M p_Q)).                          (18)

Time-dependent generators work with uniform budgets and the time-ordered
comparison. This is a COMPLETE joint reference-trace estimate on the
specified marginal-domination domain. It is NOT an unconstrained diamond
norm over arbitrary E inputs. The ordinary Q-only diamond theorem (11)
is stronger, linear in p, for the uncorrelated nominal thermal auxiliary.

8. Resource consequence and preparation
=======================================
If p_Q<=C_Q exp(-c n^alpha), the joint theorem gives

 error <=C nT exp[(K_0-c n^alpha)/2].                             (19)

It tolerates arbitrary bath memory and Q/C/B/R correlations with, for
example, K_0<=theta c n^alpha for any fixed theta<1, retaining a
stretched-exponential storage interval. This charges the INITIAL available
athermality/negentropy relative to the specified reference, not merely
energy consumed during storage. A battery can return to its original
state after using an energy barrier catalytically. Available resource,
rather than net consumed work, matters. No unrestricted cold/hot-bath
capability is inferred from saying B is finite.

M=1 permits ANY initial Q/E/R correlations whose E marginal is tau_E.
A pure/hot set of O(n) controllers can instead have ln M=O(n), which defeats
this sufficient bound when alpha<1. Thus the arbitrary-controller-state
premise of the earlier Ising/Tanner results is NOT inherited for free.
The hot-bath finite control illustrates the importance of this distinction;
it is not a many-body failure theorem.

The unperturbed reference factors into the SAME nominal syndrome state
tau_S and explicit product controller Gibbs states even at finite g.
The current accepted N1243 ordinary-polymer initializer supplies the
ideal nominal data encoder to diamond accuracy 2epsilon in EXPECTED work
polynomial in n and log(1/epsilon), under its qualified family predicates,
fixed low-temperature decay and bit-cost conditions. The controller
probabilities are explicit finite thermal coins; at beta=4 ln B0 they
are rational. No noncommuting Gibbs state of H_phys is requested.
Add the preparation channel error 2epsilon to (11). This is ideal
computation/control preparation, not noisy physical initialization.

9. Cost, surviving gates, and the earlier branches
================================================
At most R_max=3(floor(D/2)+1) ports occur per data site. The qubit version
uses <=(1+R_max)n qubits and <=2R_max n one-qubit thermal channels, with
coupling arity <=1+Q_b and bounded incidence/range. The multilevel version
uses n controller systems of dimension <=R_max+1. Its transitions are
one-body in that physical alphabet. All constants may be very unfavorable.

The inherited 3D geometry survives adding these private local controllers:
their sites and whole-check-star couplers stay inside fixed-size balls,
and density increases only by a constant. The data check terms and energy
selectors are still supplied high-arity resources of bounded support.
There is no generic two-body endpoint, efficient native pulse synthesis,
microscopic reservoir proof or demonstrated hardware advantage.

Whole-device stationarity is an actual obligation. An unfiltered X_i X_c
coupling contains counter-rotating channels and generally fails to fix the
same Gibbs reference at finite coupling; a two-qubit countermodel shows
the defect. This does not disprove a protected phase for such a model.
Unbalanced controller faults, wrong energy selectors, arbitrary charged
coherent perturbations and generic spurious coupler axes are not silently
admitted. The earlier generic-noise transfer remains available at its
own hypotheses; its failed generic two-body step remains failed.

The photonic-Ising proposal already combines complementary mechanisms.
Its low-energy bias and microscopic/RWA constructions do not by themselves
provide this full finite-controller proof: one must control its leakage
and all logical-bad coupler effects. A separate exact toy model has
P_ground H P_ground=0 and no bare logical noise, yet controller relaxation
causes logical dephasing. That refutes a projected-bias-only transfer;
it is NOT a derivation of a defect in the actual photonic-Ising device.

The new candidate opening is therefore a full-quantum, three-dimensional,
finite-controller realization of the qualified thermal-code interface,
with a joint correlated-bath resource theorem. Exact balanced-reference
engineering and the actual selector/reservoir implementation remain the
physical gates. The ordinary thermal-memory and trace/module mathematics
are credited; priority of this composition remains UNKNOWN.
