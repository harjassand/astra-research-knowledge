Physical resource contract
===========================
For native incidence D and check weight w, define Q_b=1+Dw and
R_max=3(floor(D/2)+1). There are three Pauli seeds per data site and at
most floor(D/2)+1 positive/zero-frequency ports per seed.

Independent-qubit implementation:
 <=R_max n auxiliary qubits and (1+R_max)n total qubits;
 <=R_max n bounded-support Hamiltonian couplers;
 <=2R_max n one-qubit loss/gain channels;
 maximum coupling arity <=Q_b+1, with native check arity w retained.
Data incidence increases by at most a fixed factor R_max[1+D(w-1)],
in addition to the native checks. Frequencies are integers from0 to D
in native defect-energy units. All parameters are finite and fixed in n,T.

Multilevel implementation:
 one private controller per data site of dimension <=R_max+1, with one
ground and one level per port. Its star transitions are one-BODY in this
declared physical alphabet. Encoding that qudit into qubits does not turn
them into one-qubit jumps; that would be another compiler/resource problem.

The selectors A=P_i Pi_(Delta=-omega) have norm<=1 but are still actual
many-data-qubit operators. A Pauli expansion can have up to2^D monomials
per port. The model does not acquire independent access to those controls
or hide this dependence inside a resonance label. The exact obstruction
in SELECTOR_OBSTRUCTION.txt explains a real limitation of selector removal.
The earlier three-body bounded-degree transfer cannot automatically be
applied: its pointwise positive-certificate premise has not been supplied
for this equilibrium-witness model.

For the qualified 3D input, added private controllers and whole-check-star
couplers stay in fixed physical balls, preserving nominal bounded density
and range. The arbitrary shared-B extension counts DATA/controller support
and norms; it does not provide a geometric construction of the bath network.
The inherited family, scale, beta threshold and proof constants may be
extremely unfavorable; no practical parameter threshold is computed.

Both X/Y/Z faults and whole-device calibration
=============================================
Every nominal X/Y/Z seed has nonzero thermal creation/repair paths. The
nominal generator is primitive at finite beta with all g_a and rates
nonzero: its controller jump algebra is full, and commuting with all
port components forces commuting with all single-qubit data Paulis.
Thus there is no exact conserved logical parity that silently excludes
one complementary error type.

Whole-device stationarity is nevertheless restrictive. The selectors and
thermal ratios are actual premises. Unknown amplitudes/phases, neutral
coherent hopping and explicitly reference-balanced dissipative errors are
admitted at finite strengths, including faults on controller/coupler supports
that qualify the full local algebra in the large code. Arbitrary generic
axis errors, wrong selectors and unbalanced bath-rate errors are not thereby
admitted. Hamiltonian detunings can preserve the reference only with the
gain/loss ratios and selector operators held to the same nominal reference.

If a valid reference-preserving model and a full actual CPTP model differ
by a further local generator budget eta n, contractive Duhamel adds eta nT.
For THIS full-distance quantum code that is a crude upper bound, not the
exact phase-error obstruction proved for the earlier repetition block.
No generic nonequilibrium threshold follows here.

The correlated-input theorem uses only rho_CB<=M tau_CB. It retains the
same input bath in an input-independent comparison channel and allows any
correlations with Q and an untouched R. The cost ln M is reference
max-relative entropy of the ACCESSIBLE auxiliary marginal. It includes
C-B correlations, energy and negentropy; net consumed energy alone is
insufficient. Purifying a fixed auxiliary marginal into an untouched R
does not increase the theorem's cost. A bath/controller with ln M of
order n can defeat a bound whose protective barrier is n^alpha, alpha<1.
Certifying M for an unknown bath is an additional acquisition requirement;
individual marginal temperatures do not bound joint C-B correlations.

Entropy sink, initialization and readout
======================================
For a qubit port with Gamma^-<=Gamma_max and Gamma^+<=Gamma^-, the total
instantaneous jump hazard is <=Gamma_max. Thus nominal events are bounded
by R_max Gamma_max nT. A uniform-rate multilevel controller obeys the same
R_max Gamma ceiling; its Gibbs-state activity is exactly

             2 Gamma (Z_C-1)/Z_C <=2 Gamma.

These are event counts, not refrigerator power. Reference gaps are <=D,
but the actual interacting-H energy/work per engineered jump and the
reservoir bandwidth/Markov validity are not derived. The finite persistent
B is additional noise memory, not a free replacement for the continual
fresh entropy sink in the loss/gain channels. Sustaining the generator
for T requires sustaining those bath resources for T.

The ideal nominal data initializer has the accepted expected bit-work
contract above. Explicit product controller states require additional

probabilities are rational; finite accuracy and bit lengths are paid.

domain has a measured/certified M; it does not turn arbitrary hot controllers

Failed transfers retained as executable results
==============================================
1. Unfiltered two-qubit coupling has a nonzero Gibbs-reference defect,
while the matched exchange coupling has zero defect. This rejects exact
stationary-reference transfer, not all unfiltered memory phases.
2. A two-level logical system coupled to a detuned lossy qubit through

no bare logical noise, yet loses coherence. At g=.4,Gamma=1,detuning8,
time100 its logical dephasing diamond error is .3952963863. Thus projected
coupler bias does not imply full-device bias. This is NOT a model-specific
counterexample to the photonic-Ising proposal.

Lindblad defect at zero bad population. Full support-algebra protection,
including J^dagger J, is used and tested in the new joint theorem.
