1 November 2026. Solo exact, scope-limited derivation and Pauli control.
This is not a two-body quantum-memory impossibility theorem.

1. A lowering port needs a local ground-state annihilator
========================================================
Let H>=0, P_G its ground projector, and rho_G=P_G/Tr(P_G). Suppose a
region X has a faithful marginal rho_G,X>=s I_X, s>0. If A supported
entirely on X obeys [H,A]=-omega A with omega>0, then A P_G=0: its
image would otherwise have negative energy. But

 0=Tr(rho_G A^dagger A)=Tr(rho_G,X A^dagger A)
                         >=s Tr_X(A^dagger A),

so A=0. This is the elementary faithful-marginal annihilation argument,
related to known local-passivity/quantum-code rigidity; novelty of that
fact is not claimed. Its consequence here is specific: an individually
matched thermal port cannot use that data region. A finite controller
level does not turn the forbidden A into an allowed lowering operator.

For a stabilizer code rho_G=2^-n sum_(S in stabilizer group) S.
If no nonidentity stabilizer is supported in X, then rho_G,X=I/2^|X|.
The obstruction depends on local stabilizer support, not merely distance.

The [[5,1,3]] perfect-code fixture has all15 nonidentity stabilizers of
weight FOUR. Every marginal on <=3 data sites is maximally mixed.
Therefore each nonzero individually matched LOWERING port uses >=4
data sites, or >=5 sites including its controller. Some implemented
ports attain data support4; other ports use5. This fixture is not the
primary three-dimensional RX/RZ family.

This does not rule out collective cancellations across different supports,
a nonproduct reference, another code, or another protection certificate.

2. No bilinear controller coupling under a Pauli criterion
=========================================================
Let H_D=sum_a h_a S_a, h_a!=0, with distinct Hermitian Pauli checks.
Assume (i) every one-data-qubit Pauli P anticommutes with some S_a;
(ii) each anticommuting S_aP has data weight >=2; (iii) these products
are distinct up to phase for different pairs (a,P). A sufficient condition
for (ii)-(iii) is check weight>=2 and weight(S_a S_b)>2 for distinct
checks. The perfect-code fixture satisfies these conditions.

For ANY finite controller system and ANY controller Hamiltonian H_C, set

 V=sum_(one-data-qubit P) P tensor B_P+I_D tensor B_0.

The B_P may act jointly on all controllers. Then [H_D,V] is a sum of
the distinct Pauli words S_aP of data weight>=2, with nonzero scalar
multiples of B_P as coefficients. By contrast [H_C,V] has data weight
at most one. Orthogonality of the data Pauli basis gives

 [H_D+H_C,V]=0 ==> B_P=0 for every nonidentity one-site P.          (O1)

This already includes possible cancellations between sites and axes;
the forbidden higher-data-weight coefficients are independent.

For beta>0 the full-rank product reference
sigma=Gibbs_beta(H_D) tensor Gibbs_beta(H_C) has the same commutant
as H_D+H_C. If controller-only dissipators fix their reference and the
free Hamiltonians commute with it, stationarity requires [V,sigma]=0.
Thus (O1) excludes genuine bilinear data/controller couplers for this
stationary-product-reference route under (i)-(iii), regardless of rates.

Even individually balanced controller reservoirs are not essential to this
obstruction. Absorb I_D tensor B_0 into an arbitrary controller-only
generator L_C and call the remaining data-dependent coupling V_D.
Under (i), Tr(tau_D P)=0 for every one-site P: conjugation by an
anticommuting check changes its sign and fixes tau_D. Partial trace of
[-i[H_D+V_D,.]+Id_D tensor L_C](tau_D tensor tau_C)=0 therefore forces
L_C(tau_C)=0. The remaining equation is [V_D,tau_D tensor tau_C]=0.
For any faithful tau_C put H_C^mod=-beta^-1 log tau_C and repeat (O1).
Thus retuning only controller-side reservoirs cannot rescue this product
reference while keeping genuine one-data-site Hamiltonian couplings.

The finite exact check is the Hilbert-Schmidt Gram matrix of [H_D,P]
for all15 one-site Paulis: diag(32 t_P), with t_P>=1 the anticommuting
check count. Their Pauli words have weight4. For a controller qubit of
gap2, the complete45-column bilinear commutator Gram matrix is exactly
diag(64[t_P+4]) for controller X,Y and diag(64 t_P) for controller Z.
Its kernel is zero. The program checks these integer Pauli identities
and every <=3-site ground marginal without a floating rank threshold.

3. Meaning of the failed shortcut
=================================
THERMAL_PORT_THEOREM.txt supplies the selectors as actual Hamiltonian
operators. It does not acquire them by attaching an unfiltered Xi Xc
coupler and naming a resonance. The latter generally fails exact
product-Gibbs stationarity; the two-qubit guard shows a nonzero defect.

These are obstructions to this reference route and stated check/code
conditions. They do not exclude two-body autonomous memories or prove
failure of an unfiltered protected phase. Nonproduct stationary states,
different code/gauge structures, or nonequilibrium positive certificates
may avoid the premises. Such a route needs a new argument. The earlier
protected-phase transfer and its failed generic two-body extension retain
their separate scopes.
