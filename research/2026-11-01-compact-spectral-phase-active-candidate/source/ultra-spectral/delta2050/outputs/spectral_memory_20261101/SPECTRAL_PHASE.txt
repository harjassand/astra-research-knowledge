COMPACT SPECTRAL ISING MEMORY: A RESTRICTED-NOISE PHASE CANDIDATE
1 November 2026. Solo analytic derivation, with executable finite controls.
No independent audit, priority, experimental realization, or practical
advantage is asserted. Earlier Tanner and arity-transfer results are preserved.

Result and quantifiers
======================
There is an infinite family of bounded-degree graphs and a time-independent
model with n DATA qubits, 9n auxiliary qubits, TWO-BODY Hamiltonian couplings,
and ONE-BODY auxiliary losses. For a fixed locality allowance q, sufficiently
small but FIXED g/Gamma, sufficiently large but FIXED J/Gamma, and a nonzero
FIXED strong-symmetry-preserving perturbation threshold delta, the candidate
bound is

 inf_Lambda || (Dec_D x Id_CB) Phi_T (Enc_D x Id_CB)
                           - Id_Q x Lambda_CB ||_diamond
       <= min(2, C_phys n(1+T) exp(-b n/2)),                       (1)

for every T>=0 and sufficiently large n in the family. Lambda is CPTP on
the existing C,B input/output, not a replacement bath or a reinitialization.
There is ONE unknown logical qubit Q. All initial Q/C/B/reference correlations
are allowed. B is an arbitrary finite persistent bath; bounds are independent
of its dimension and reference. The data alone are ideally encoded/decoded.
The physical evolution in (1) contains the finite controllers throughout.

Crucial restriction: each allowed local Hamiltonian/jump operator commutes
with G=product_i X_i on DATA. Mere covariance of the generator under Ad_G
does not suffice. This is an algebra-restricted quantum memory; its ordinary
quantum code distance is one. It is neither a generic-noise two-body compiler
nor a positive-rate quantum code. Section 10 gives the symmetry-breaking cost.

1. Graph, code, and a density lemma
==================================
Take a connected bipartite d=18 regular graph with adjacency A, such that
the largest eigenvalue on the complement of the constant vector is <=17/2.
The eigenvalue -18 is harmless for this upper bound. LPS Ramanujan graphs
with p=17, followed by a bipartite double cover, give an infinite family;
their nontrivial absolute eigenvalues are <=2 sqrt(17)<17/2. The finite
arithmetic construction in expander_construction.py is an illustrative member.

Encode |0> as |0^n>, |1> as |1^n>. A Z configuration is an error subset S,
up to complement. Set w=min(|S|,n-|S|) and

 U=sum_edges (1-Z_i Z_j)/2, alpha=1/72, h=37/4,
 rho=alpha/4=1/288, r=floor(rho n), s=1/333.

For a subset of size w, the standard constant-vector decomposition gives

 U=d w-1_S^t A 1_S >=(d-17/2) w(1-w/n).                         (2)

In particular, for w<=alpha n,
 U >=(d-17/2-d alpha)w=h w, so w<=4U/37.
Let H be erroneous vertices with at least 10 opposite neighbors. Summing
the number of opposite neighbors over the erroneous vertices gives

 U<=9w+9|H|, hence |H|>=U/9-w>=U/333.                            (3)

Each such spin flip decreases U by at least two. The chosen smaller
representative gives the same conclusion in the complementary basin.
Equation (2), monotone as a function of w<=n/2, also implies
 U>=h r whenever w>=r (for the above rho). This last statement controls
initial tails outside the small-error region, not just boundary states.

Use P=1_{w<r}. A fixed decoder chooses a minimum-weight representative of
each edge syndrome, corrects it, and discards the syndrome. Choose a fixed
tie rule far from P. Safe representatives are unique. For a Q-local data
operator commuting with G, safe-to-safe matrix elements are scalar on the
logical qubit provided 2r+Q<n: expand in Paulis. Commutation with G forces
even Z/Y parity. This Z part is a repetition stabilizer; the X part cannot
differ from the safe filling by the global logical X. Thus in the recovery
frame every safe local operator is Id_Q tensor an operator on syndrome,C,B.
The same statement applies to local left/right operator expansions of maps.

2. Actual finite device and calibration window
==============================================
Each data vertex i has auxiliary qubits a=(i,l), l=1,...,9. Write
 n_a=sigma_a^+ sigma_a^-, and set (hbar=1)

 H_D=-sum_edges J_ij Z_i Z_j,
 H_0=H_D+sum_a omega_a n_a,
 V_phys=sum_a X_i (g_a sigma_a^+ + conjugate(g_a) sigma_a^-),
 L_nom=-i[H_0+V_phys,.]+sum_a gamma_a D[sigma_a^-].                (4)

Every coupling in (4) is two-body, and each loss is one-body. The nine
nominal frequencies are omega_(i,l)=4Jl. No syndrome projectors, table,
multispin jumps, measurement, pulse sequence, or controller reset are
physical primitives in this model.

Let epsilon=g/Gamma, kappa=4g^2/Gamma, K=J/Gamma. Admit the structural
static calibration window

 sum_{j~i}|J_ij-J|<=Gamma/8, |omega_(i,l)-4Jl|<=Gamma/4,
 Gamma/2<=gamma_a<=2Gamma,
 g^2/2<=|g_a|^2<=3g^2/2.                                        (5)

Auxiliary coupling phases may be arbitrary. These are explicit tolerances,
not uncharged generic calibration faults. Noncommuting extra terms enter
the perturbation budget below. In particular, odd-data-axis coupling errors
are symmetry-breaking faults, not covered by (5).

3. Local spectral resolvent and a two-layer analytical frame
===========================================================
Let Delta_i=X_i H_D X_i-H_D, and Pi_(i,Delta) its diagonal spectral projectors.
They are used ONLY in the proof. Put V_i=sum_l X_i[(g_a/g)sigma_a^+ +h.c.],
so V_phys=Gamma epsilon sum_i V_i. For each a=(i,l), define

 C_a=sum_Delta [(g_a/g)/(gamma_a/(2Gamma)
                       -i(omega_a+Delta)/Gamma)] X_i Pi_(i,Delta),
 G_a=C_a sigma_a^+ + C_a^dagger sigma_a^-,  G_i=sum_l G_a,
 D_0^*=i[H_0/Gamma,.]+sum_a(gamma_a/Gamma) D[sigma_a^-]^*.

The operator X_i Pi_Delta sigma_a^+ is an eigenoperator of D_0^* with
eigenvalue -gamma_a/(2Gamma)+i(omega_a+Delta)/Gamma. Therefore

 D_0^*G_i=-V_i, ||G_a||<=4 sqrt(3/2),
 i[H_0/Gamma,G_i]=-V_i+sum_l(gamma_a/(2Gamma))G_a.                 (6)

The norm of the last commutator is bounded independently of K>=1.
This identity, rather than ||H_0||, controls every Taylor derivative of
the conjugated fast Hamiltonian. There is no hidden K epsilon^3 source.

For nonadjacent i,j, [G_i,G_j]=0: their data flips are on different centers
and any shared neighbor appears diagonally. With black/white bipartition,

 W=W_white W_black, W_color=product_{i in color} exp(-i epsilon G_i). (7)

Each layer consists of commuting, possibly overlapping gates. This is an
analytical unitary, never a physical schedule. A primitive local map has
a finite cone after these two layers. For example, a one-site operator's
cone lies within data graph radius three and auxiliary centers of radius
two; a crude bound is 5527 data plus 2925 auxiliary qubits. Unions of q
such cones suffice for a q-local perturbation. Their bounded overlap is
independent of n,epsilon,K,B. These very loose proof constants do not add
physical qubits. All generators G_i and W commute with the global G.

For any data observable X, the damping bimodule identity and Hamiltonian
Jacobi identity give
 [D_0^*,i[G_i,.]]X=i[D_0^*G_i,X]=-i[V_i,X].
Thus the FIRST transformed coefficient on the entire data operator space
vanishes on ALL controller states. No ground-state averaging is used.

4. Exact local charges and the ground moment
============================================
Define K_i=Z_i product_l Z_(i,l)^aux. H_0,V_phys,G_j and W commute with
EVERY K_i. A nominal loss sigma_(i,l)^- is odd under K_i and even under
all other K_j. Consequently, exactly at finite epsilon:

 (a) The all-controller-ground block of W(H_0+V_phys)W^dagger is diagonal
     in every data Z_i. Its commutator with any diagonal data weight is zero.
 (b) The ground-to-ground block of j'_a=W sigma_a^- W^dagger flips ONLY
     data i, times a diagonal function. It does not resolve orientations.
 (c) j'_a has ground block i epsilon C_a+O(epsilon^3), while its
     ground-to-excited-controller block is O(epsilon^2).

To see the orders in (c), the first commutator involves only G_i and
[G_i,sigma_a^-]=-C_a Z_a. Hence it is controller-diagonal. Global
controller parity sends epsilon to -epsilon and j'_a to -j'_a, making
the ground block odd in epsilon. All estimates are local and uniform in K
by (6) and bounded gate norms.

It follows directly from the transformed Lindblad expression that its
ground-block diagonal-data moment, at order epsilon^2, has single-flip rates

 R_a(z)=|g_a|^2 gamma_a /
              [(gamma_a/2)^2+(omega_a+Delta_i(z))^2].              (8)

This statement is about a moment of the FULL quantum generator. No secular
classical Markov approximation to its full channel is asserted. It is also
independent of any auxiliary excitation weight through this order.
The diagonal Lamb Hamiltonian has no such moment contribution.

Coherent orientation pairing is essential. C_a includes both orientations
in one operator. Replacing it by separately recorded up/down transitions
has the same classical rates but can erase logical coherence. The exact
algebra controls check this distinction.

5. Uniform finite-controller remainder: explicit local decomposition
===================================================================
Take F=exp(beta U+lambda N), N=sum_a n_a, lambda=beta epsilon, with fixed
0<beta<=beta_0<=1/2. Here beta_0 is sufficiently small for the following
finite-cone weighted norm bounds. All constants C below depend only on
d=18, nine modes, q, cone incidence and the window (5); they are uniform
for K>=1 and sufficiently small epsilon. They may be very large.

There is an exact decomposition, on the FULL physical space,

 F^-1/2 (Ad_W L_nom Ad_Wdagger)^*(F) F^-1/2
   =-sum_a gamma_a(1-exp(-lambda)) n_a +Phi_beta+sum_i A_i,         (9)
 Phi_beta(z)=sum_a R_a(z)[exp(beta(U(z^i)-U(z)))-1],
 ||A_i||<=C Gamma beta epsilon^2,
 ||P_(0,i) A_i P_(0,i)||<=C Gamma beta epsilon^4.                 (10)

P_(0,i) sets all auxiliaries in A_i's local cone to ground; no initial
ground-state condition is imposed. A_i is Hermitian. Its support can include
one extra shell of diagonal data neighbors from the weight F. Cone overlap
remains a fixed constant.

Here are details for obtaining (9)-(10), including local grouping. Decompose
W H_0 W^dagger-H_0 using the integral of each color-layer derivative. For a
black center i its term is
 W_white integral_0^epsilon W_black(t)(-i[G_i,H_0])W_black(t)^dagger dt
 W_white^dagger; for a white center omit the outer black change and use
the white-layer integral. Add Gamma epsilon W V_i W^dagger and the nine
transformed losses at i to this center's group. All these maps are TP.
The first Hamiltonian change is Gamma epsilon sum_l(gamma_a/(2Gamma))G_a
by (6); its adjoint on data cancels the first loss change at this SAME
center. Thus each group's first coefficient vanishes on every data X.
For F_data exp(lambda N), that coefficient costs only O(Gamma epsilon
lambda). All remaining full-space coefficients start at O(Gamma epsilon^2).

For a local TP map M, far commuting factors of F cancel, and M^*(I)=0
gives ||F^-1/2 M^*(F)F^-1/2||<=C beta ||M||_diamond when lambda<=beta.
Finite-cone derivatives of exp(-i epsilon G_i) are bounded by powers of
2||G_i||. Apply this bound to the above exact integrals, using (6) for
their first H_0 commutator. Taylor's integral remainder is uniform in K.
This proves the full norm in (10), including the epsilon lambda term.

Every Hamiltonian term in those integral groups commutes with all K_j,
so its ground-block weighted commutator is zero separately. For each
transformed loss, part (c) makes the ground-to-ground dissipator equal
to epsilon^2 D[C_a] plus O(epsilon^4); ground-to-excited contributions
start at epsilon^4. The second-order term is exactly its summand in
Phi_beta. TP and differentiation in beta give the beta factor also in
this ground remainder. This proves the SECOND norm in (10) per local
group; it is not inferred from cancellation of a global ground block.

Let Q_i=I-P_(0,i), D=C Gamma beta epsilon^2 and d_0=C Gamma beta epsilon^4.
The elementary two-block bound for a Hermitian A_i is

 A_i <=(d_0+D^2/t)I+(D+t)Q_i, t>0.                              (11)

Since sum_i Q_i<=I_ov N for a fixed overlap I_ov, take
t=Gamma lambda/(32 I_ov). For sufficiently small epsilon, absorb the
excited terms in (11) into the exact fast loss in (9). This leaves

 sum_i A_i -sum_a gamma_a(1-exp(-lambda))n_a
     <= C beta kappa epsilon n -mu N,
 mu>=Gamma beta epsilon/16.                                     (12)

The O(Gamma beta epsilon^3) Schur source is O(beta kappa epsilon).
This is a direct positive-operator bound valid for occupied controllers
and all times, not an O(nT epsilon) eliminated-channel approximation.

6. Cooling drift and admitted shared-bath noise
==============================================
If i has k opposite neighbors, its uncalibrated energy change is
4J(9-k). Equation (5) changes this by <=Gamma/4. For k>=10 select
l=k-9; then |omega_a+Delta_i|<=Gamma/2 and (8) gives R_a>=kappa/20.
For k<=9 every mode obeys |omega_a+Delta_i|>=4J-Gamma/2>=7J/2,
so R_a<=3kappa/(49K^2)<=kappa/(16K^2). Together with (3), on P

 Phi_beta <= [9kappa/(16K^2)](exp(18beta)-1)n-gamma_U U,
 gamma_U=[kappa s/20](1-exp(-2beta))>=kappa s beta/20.             (13)

Extra modes on an improving spin only help. Lorentzian upward tails are
retained as a source; they are never declared zero.

Allow time-dependent HP perturbations V_a(t), each individually TP at
generator level, supported on <=q physical data/controller qubits and the
SAME arbitrary finite B, identity elsewhere. Require

 sum_a ||V_a(t)||_diamond<=delta n.                              (14)

Each map must have a local left/right operator expansion using operators
that commute with G. Add arbitrary bath-only GKSL dynamics. The entire
actual joint generator must be GKSL. Ordinary local Hamiltonians and
Lindblad dissipators from this strong algebra satisfy the assumptions;
signed TP calibration differences from that algebra are also allowed.
This is stronger than superoperator covariance. Controller-only faults
are unrestricted within the finite q-local budget.

After W each map is still TP, strongly symmetric and supported in a fixed
cone. The weighted TP bound in section 5 gives an added source <=C beta
delta n, independent of B and any reference. This includes coherent data
X faults, even-data-Z faults, arbitrary mixed strong jumps, controller
faults, and controller thermal gains. It does not include data Z_i faults.

There is a useful compression detail. For any full actual GKSL L', F>=0
commuting with P, and K(X)=P L'(PXP)P,

 K^*(PFP)<=P L'^*(F)P.                                          (15)

Indeed the difference is P sum_j L_j^dagger (I-P)F(I-P)L_j P>=0;
Hamiltonian and anticommutator terms vanish in this difference. Use the
FULL TP weighted bound before (15). Applying a TP beta-small estimate
directly to the non-TP map acting on P would be invalid at beta=0.

Equations (12)-(15) prove the safe positive certificate

 K^*(FP)<=[a n-gamma_U U-mu N]FP,
 a=C beta[kappa(epsilon+K^-2)+delta].                            (16)

This proof includes the full retained-jump escape in the CP/TNI process.
Let zeta=min(gamma_U/beta,mu/lambda); for small epsilon,
zeta=gamma_U/beta>=kappa s/20. Thus (16) is the protected-phase
certificate [a n-zeta log F]FP.

7. Parameter choice, arbitrary controllers, and boundary tails
==============================================================
Let theta=beta h rho/16. All constants in (16) are finite and uniform in
K, so one can choose fixed epsilon>0 sufficiently small, then fixed
K>=1 sufficiently large, then fixed delta/kappa>0 sufficiently small
such that

 a/zeta<=theta,
 (9+C_W) beta epsilon<=theta, C_W beta epsilon<=theta.            (17)

There is no threshold value for hardware claimed here. Gamma sets the
time unit; kappa=4Gamma epsilon^2. These constants are independent of n,T.
The rate/linewidth hierarchy is J>>Gamma>>g, not an arity gadget tree.

For the CP/TNI safe evolution and any reference, m=tr(F sigma) satisfies
 m'<=a n m-zeta m log m.
For trace p<=1 this follows from
 tr(F log F sigma)>=m log(m/p)>=m log m.
Hence log m/n<=max(log m(0)/n,a/zeta), uniformly in time.

The INITIAL controller state may have every auxiliary excited and arbitrary
logical/bath/reference correlations. Before the analytical frame, U=0 and
N<=9n. For each color use, as a comparison process, the unitary path inside
P generated by the compressed sum P(sum_i G_i)P. This path is TP on P and
logical-transparent. Its weighted commutator bound gives a log moment
increase <=C_W beta epsilon n for both layers together. Thus the safe
initial log moment density is <=(9+C_W)beta epsilon, without requiring a
prepared controller. The final compressed inverse-frame path costs the
other term in (17). These paths are analytical comparison maps only.

Fix Q to bound data support of all transformed local Hamiltonians/jumps
and perturbation operator expansions. It depends on q but not n. For
n sufficiently large, 2r+Q<n and r-2Q>=rho n/2. Every safe state within
boundary width 2Q has U>=h rho n/2, by (2). Equations (16)-(17) and the
two frame paths therefore give boundary weight <=exp(-b n), where one
may conservatively take b=beta h rho/4. These estimates apply along the
compressed startup path as well. An alternative initial-projection
comparison can use the global consequence U>=h r of (2); it is not needed
when comparing the unitary paths directly as above.

8. From positive tails to the quantum channel statement
=======================================================
In the repetition recovery frame, every safe compressed local operator
is Id_Q tensor an operator on syndrome,C,B, by section 1 and strong
symmetry. The compressed physical evolution and compressed analytical
frame paths therefore factor EXACTLY on Q. Their CP/TNI maps need not
be trace preserving; this is handled by a logical-independent CPTP
completion on the auxiliary space. Arbitrary initial Q/C/B correlations
are retained by this tensor identity.

For completeness, the comparison uses the actual full CPTP suffix.
For a positive safe state sigma, set P_int=1_{w<r-2Q}. A local map M
cannot take P_int sigma P_int outside P. Consequently

 ||(M-P M(.)P)(sigma)||_1
   <=2||M||_diamond ||sigma-P_int sigma P_int||_1
   <=4||M||_diamond sqrt(tr((P-P_int)sigma)).                     (19)

The last bound is gentle truncation for a subnormalized positive state,
and remains valid with any reference. This proves the boundary estimate
also for the individually signed TP perturbations; it does not require
a local positive jump representation of each perturbation. There are
O(n) nominal terms plus the norm-budgeted local perturbations, with a
finite summed norm density C_phys. Bath-only maps commute with P and
contribute exactly zero to this comparison, even if their norm is large.
Duhamel's formula with the full CPTP suffix and safe CP/TNI prefix gives
C_phys n T exp(-b n/2). Comparing each actual analytical unitary frame
path to its compressed unitary path in the same way costs at most
C_phys n exp(-b n/2). The paths start inside P, so no controller
initialization or postselected projection is introduced.

Apply the ordinary fixed DATA decoder and trace the syndrome. The safe
part is Id_Q tensor a CP/TNI map Xi on the existing C,B. Its trace defect
is bounded by the above comparison to the full trace-preserving process.
The map Xi(.)+tr[(I-Xi^*(I)) .] omega_CB, with any fixed output state
omega_CB, is a logical-independent CPTP completion Lambda; the completion
costs at most another copy of the trace-defect bound. This is a comparison
channel, not a physical replacement of the stored bath. All estimates
hold with every external reference, proving the joint diamond statement
with a changed C_phys. This supplies the unknown-input quantum meaning
of (1), beyond population survival.

All uses of locality here are graph/support locality. No two- or
three-dimensional finite-density wiring model is proved.

9. What the structural simplification achieves
==============================================
The physical controller has nine resonances and nine loss channels per
data spin. It has no explicit majority lookup, no conditional high-arity
repair primitive, and no perturbative high-arity synthesis. The local
charges make its ground moment classical without making its full channel
classical. The finite-controller remainder is absorbed directly by damping,
with a uniform-in-K source that can be smaller than the expander drift.
This is the candidate route to (1) with only 10n two-level systems and
maximum coupling degree 27. It uses one stored qubit and a strong noise
algebra to buy that simplification. The previous all-logical-qubits,
generic-noise Tanner construction and its failed generic two-body extension
are different claims and remain as written.

10. Symmetry-breaking price and exact obstruction
=================================================
Suppose the actual model differs from a valid strong model by W_break,
sum ||W_break||_diamond<=eta n, with both complete propagators CPTP.
Contractive Duhamel comparison adds eta n T to (1). Thus at fixed eta>0
the theorem supplies no exponential-in-n full quantum lifetime.

The limitation is real. Add eta_0 sum_i D[Z_i] to the nominal model plus
any admitted strong noise. Then exactly, on the FULL device,

 L^*(G)=-2 n eta_0 G,
 Dec_D^*(X_Q)=G,
 <X_Q(T)> for initial |+_Q> = exp(-2 n eta_0 T).                  (18)

Every candidate Id_Q tensor Lambda has logical X expectation one for
this input, so its joint diamond error is at least
1-exp(-2 n eta_0 T). No syndrome-rate cooling can remove this error.
This noise is weakly G-covariant, illustrating why that condition is
insufficient. With an ideal corrector it changes no classical populations.

After Hadamards on DATA, the same architecture has H_D=-sum J_ij X_i X_j,
data-Z/auxiliary-flip couplings, G=product Z_i. Dominant longitudinal
dephasing Z_i B_i and D[Z_i] now belong to the admitted algebra. Ordinary
relaxation and transverse Hamiltonian/coupler faults break it. This gives
a physically meaningful biased-noise interpretation, not a certified
device bias. All symmetry-breaking faults, including coupler faults,
must be included in eta. A claim that eta falls with cat energy or another
inner encoding requires that WHOLE-device derivation separately.

For nonzero eta the useful explicit trade is
C_phys n(1+T)exp(-b n/2)+eta nT. Increasing the block indefinitely
worsens the second term. Choosing a block of logarithmic size in an
available noise-bias ratio is a conditional finite-resource trade, not
an acquired generic phase or an unbounded-lifetime assertion at fixed bias.
