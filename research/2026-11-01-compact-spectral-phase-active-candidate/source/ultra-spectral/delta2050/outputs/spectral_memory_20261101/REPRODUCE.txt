Scope of controls
=================
spectral_filter_algebra: exact single-center Poisson identity, full-data
first-order cancellation, diagonal second-order moment, and coherent
orientation-pairing versus orientation-resolved jumps.
overlap_spectral_algebra: exact overlapping centers, noncommuting frame
gates, full-data first-order cancellation and auxiliary-weight-independent
ground second order. These fixtures have fewer modes/degree than the family.
expander_construction: exact group arithmetic and graph/layout invariants,
plus a floating eigenvalue diagnostic. Family expansion is a primary-source
theorem. This program does not simulate the many-body memory.
finite_spectral_moment: full finite-controller operator diagnostics including
excited flags, increasing J/Gamma, and an additional common-bath control.
quantum_memory_controls: floating finite unknown-input/reference channels,
retained output flags, hot starts, symmetry-breaking floor, stronger ideal
scheduled/autonomous benchmarks, and actual-model jump activity. Its charge
reduction is checked against the independent full six-qubit Lindbladian.
calibrated_multimode_controls: complex couplings, unequal linewidths and
shifted energies, exact charge structure, full first-order cancellation,
and predicted quadratic/full versus quartic/ground remainder scaling.

The controls do not prove the family theorem, useful parameter thresholds,
novelty, a microscopic reservoir, a physical noise bias, or practical
advantage. SPECTRAL_PHASE.txt contains the solo analytic proof candidate;
MODEL_AND_COSTS.txt contains source scopes and the physical/benchmark limits.
