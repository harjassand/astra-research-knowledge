The correlation calculation gives a useful classification: eight explicit compatible fields detect every cubic direction beyond translation. Cubics whose correlation vanishes for every field are exactly the translation cubics. Thus the old translation argument covers precisely four of the twelve directions.

I’m now testing a general route to complete faces: if a leading quartic admits only translation cubics, its entire polynomial face reduces to a quadratic convexity problem. I’m applying that criterion to the Dacorogna–Marcellini endpoint, whose continuous family of acoustic zeros differs from our current two-orbit geometry.
