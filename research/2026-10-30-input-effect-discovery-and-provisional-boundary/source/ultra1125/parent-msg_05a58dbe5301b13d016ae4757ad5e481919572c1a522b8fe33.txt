I’ve eliminated the background matrix from the rank-one condition by an exact Schur complement. A complication appears near the acoustic zeros: the pseudoinverse at the zero misses constraints that survive in the limit.

I’m also using the Fourier formulation to isolate genuine gradient interactions. A resonant three-wave test can probe a variational gap that an isolated wave cannot. The quartic Fourier machinery in [Bandeira–Pedregal](https://matematicas.uclm.es/omeva/wp-content/preprints/preprint10-08.pdf) supports that change of attack; its all-mode characterization does not itself settle this boundary.
