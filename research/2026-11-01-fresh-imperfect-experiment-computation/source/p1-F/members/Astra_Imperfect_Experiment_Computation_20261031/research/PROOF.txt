ASTRA — TASK-TRIGGERED ACQUISITION FROM IMPERFECT OPERATOR ACTIONS
31 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane
Completed ordinary construction; final conclusions and executed checks are recorded separately.

1. THE QUESTION AND THE CHANGE OF CONTRACT

The target is a continuing stream of reliable, full-vector linear solves and
convex response-design computations for an unknown fixed Hermitian operator
family. Experiments return imperfect chosen vector actions. The learner must
construct its own retained representation and solver.

We do not attempt to recover the exact generated algebra. We do not claim that
one finite approximate closure can answer all rational expressions. We retain
the exact-algebra compiler and its trace theorem at the scope given in the
user's prompt; this construction supplies a different computational service.

The source remains available. A previously unseen task direction can require a
new experiment. A successful task is returned only with a residual certificate
covering the current source uncertainty. A failed task supplies an additional
operator-action direction, retained for future tasks. This is an explicit
exchange of universal offline identification for continuing task-triggered
measurement, not a claim of free source-free deployment.

The principal theorem is an end-to-end synthesis of established Galerkin,
residual, greedy-width and recycling tools. Its potentially useful content is
the combined acquisition/certification/lifetime/precision statement below.
Historical originality and practical superiority are not established.

2. INPUT, OUTPUT AND COST CONTRACT

Work over R^n or C^n with Euclidean norm and Hermitian adjoint *. The unknown
fixed primitive operators are B_1,...,B_k, with known ||B_j|| <= L. For every
chosen v and requested tolerance d>0, the primitive action service returns

    O_j(v,d) = B_j v + e,          ||e|| <= d ||v||.              (2.1)

Errors may be biased, correlated, history-dependent and adversarial. Linearity
is a property of the fixed B_j, not of the reported answers. A smaller d must
be an actual available experimental capability. Repetition alone does not
remove deterministic bias. A statistical realization may supply the displayed
guarantees on a joint confidence event; confidence and repetitions are paid.

A future task is (theta,alpha,b), where sum_j |theta_j| <= 1, alpha is known,

    B(theta) = sum_j theta_j B_j,
    A(theta) = alpha I + B(theta),
    A(theta) >= mu I,             mu > 0 known.                (2.2)

Theta is real for Hermitian primitives. The coercivity warranty is supplied
by the declared task family or proved from primitive bounds, for example
alpha >= L+mu. It is not inferred from a small compressed matrix. Allowing
alpha>L+mu is sufficient, not necessary. We permit a smaller warranted mu.
Assume |alpha|+L <= M_A for the finite-preparation variant.

The task may depend arbitrarily on the entire previous transcript and on every
earlier answer. The required output is x with

    ||x - A(theta)^(-1)b|| <= epsilon ||A(theta)^(-1)b||,        (2.3)

where 0<epsilon<1 is fixed for this theorem. Set

    tau = mu epsilon/(1+epsilon),       sigma = tau/16.         (2.4)

The model includes full n-vector input preparation, output delivery, storage,
small dense solves and QR work. A source call is not a scalar observation.
The optional precision-price model is C_0 d^(-p), p>0, per primitive action;
it is a declared model, not an experimentally established time/energy law.

Connection to the original Hermitian-action interface. If only Hermitian
A_j actions are supplied, with a warranted bound ||A_j||<=G, G>=1, we may
construct a PSD primitive B_j=A_j^2 without receiving a positive source or a
solver as an oracle. For a requested B_j tolerance delta<=G^2, query

    y=O_{A_j}(v,d),       z=O_{A_j}(y,d),       d=delta/(3G).

Then

    ||z-A_j^2 v|| <= [2Gd+d^2]||v|| <= (7/9)delta||v||.

The two genuine source actions and preparation of y are charged. Thus
alpha I+sum theta_j A_j^2, theta in the simplex and alpha>0, has the known
coercivity floor mu=alpha by construction. No experimentally inferred hidden
eigenvalue gap is needed. The primitive norm bound is L=G^2; if original
tolerance price is d^(-p), each constructed action costs at most
2(3G/delta)^p plus ordinary vector work. Numerical image width is still a
separate resource; squaring does not make an arbitrary source compressible.

3. WHAT IS RETAINED

The learner keeps an orthonormal matrix U of acquired action directions.
Initially U has no columns. For each j it retains Y_j approximating B_j U.
The primitive action history is retained with its actual tolerance labels.

At cache rank r, let c be a power of two with r+1 <= c, minimal unless a
previous larger capacity is already in use. Query every newly appended cache
column at tolerance sigma/sqrt(c). When c doubles, remeasure all current
columns at the new tolerance. Then, for any transient orthonormal extension
V=[U,q] measured at the same tolerance,

    Y_{j,V} = B_j V + E_{j,V},        ||E_{j,V}|| <= sigma,     (3.1)

because its Frobenius norm is at most sigma sqrt(dim(V)/c).
For a task, define Y = sum theta_j Y_{j,V}. Thus

    Y = B(theta)V + E,               ||E|| <= sigma.           (3.2)

This square-root-rank calibration is essential. Per-column error sigma does
not imply a matrix operator-error bound sigma. Correlated noise does not
average away. The cost of the tighter column tolerances is charged in Section 8.

4. ALGORITHM: ORDINARY REAL-ARITHMETIC VERSION

For b=0, return x=0. Otherwise repeat:

  (a) Compute b_perp=(I-UU*)b. If nonzero, prepare
      q=b_perp/||b_perp||, query each active primitive directly on q, and put
      V=[U,q]. If zero, V=U. Never obtain the normalized q action by dividing
      a small difference of old noisy answers by ||b_perp||.

  (b) Form Y as in (3.2), and

          H = alpha I + (V*Y + Y*V)/2.

      Solve the explicit small system H z=V*b, and set x=Vz. This is the
      constructed downstream solver; no high-dimensional inverse is supplied.

  (c) Compute r_hat=b-alpha x-Yz and the certified bound

          c_res=||r_hat||+sigma ||x||.

      If c_res <= tau ||x||, return x.

  (d) Otherwise form the normalized task-action report

          f_hat=Yz/||x||,
          w=(I-UU*) f_hat.

      Append w/||w|| to U. Remeasure/refill the cache according to Section 3,
      and repeat the same task. The next b_perp is prepared and measured anew.

The algorithm does not add a direction because a tiny closure residual happens
to be nonzero. Every addition is supported by a failed answer certificate and
has a strictly positive quantitative separation proved below.

5. SOUNDNESS AND FAILURE-TO-INFORMATION LEMMA

Let A=A(theta), B=B(theta). Since V is orthonormal,

    H = V* A V + sym(V*E),       lambda_min(H) >= mu-sigma > 0.

The small solve is therefore well-defined and its conditioning is controlled
by the warranted source band, not by an acquired Gram eigenvalue. Also x!=0
when b!=0 and b belongs to span(V).

The true residual r=b-Ax satisfies

    r = r_hat + Ez,       ||r|| <= c_res,      ||z||=||x||.    (5.1)

Consequently an accepted output satisfies

    ||x-x_*|| <= ||r||/mu <= epsilon/(1+epsilon) ||x||
                <= epsilon ||x_*||,

where the final inequality follows by the triangle inequality. This proves
(2.3) for each task. No union over tasks is needed for deterministic action
guarantees. For stochastic reports the theorem holds on the one event where
the adaptively requested action enclosures all hold.

Now suppose the check fails. Let C=V*Y. The computed Galerkin equation gives

    V*r_hat = (sym(C)-C)z.

Because the true V*BV is Hermitian, ||sym(C)-C|| <= sigma. Hence

    ||V*r_hat|| <= sigma ||x||.

Since b lies in span(V),

    (I-VV*)r_hat = -(I-VV*)Yz.

It follows that

    c_res <= ||(I-VV*)Yz|| + 2 sigma ||x||.

Failure forces

    dist(f_hat,span(U)) >= dist(f_hat,span(V))
                          > tau-2sigma =: g.                 (5.2)

Furthermore, putting v=x/||x||,

    f_hat = B(theta)v + e_hat,  ||e_hat||<=sigma,  ||v||=1,   (5.3)
    ||f_hat|| <= L+sigma =: M.

Equation (5.3) is legitimate even though no new source action was made at x:
it follows from fixed-source linearity and the acquired operator norm bound
on E. It would be illegitimate with only uncombined per-column error sigma.

Thus each failed solve yields a separated, bounded, imperfect observation of
one actual task-action image. All appended directions are orthonormal. There
can be at most n additions. At full span the check necessarily passes because
c_res<=2sigma||x||<tau||x||. Every task terminates in exact arithmetic.

6. LIFETIME BOUND FROM THE WIDTH OF ACTUAL TASK-ACTION IMAGES

Consider any finite or infinite adaptive task stream and let R be the total
number of failed checks/enrichments. For its accepted raw image sequence, let

    F = { B(theta_t) v_t : t indexes failed checks }.

This is the encountered sequence, not all possible operator products and not
the complete algebra. Suppose there exists an r-dimensional subspace S such
that

    sup_{f in F} dist(f,S) <= eta,
    eta + sigma <= g/2,             g=tau-2sigma.              (6.1)

Neither r, S, nor eta is supplied to the learner or used to choose an action.
They only describe when the acquired task sequence admits a small retained
representation. A convenient stronger sufficient condition is

    max_j ||(I-P_S)B_j|| <= eta,                               (6.2)

which covers every allowed theta and every task vector. General full-rank
perturbations satisfying (6.2) are allowed. No exact rank, algebra membership,
commutativity, eigenvalue separation or hidden useful basis is assumed.

THEOREM. Under (6.1), if r>=1,

    R <= min{ n, r max[4, 2+4 log_2((L+sigma)/g)] }.           (6.3)

If all actual images have norm at most eta with eta+sigma<g, R=0.

PROOF. Let fhat_1,...,fhat_m be any first m accepted reports, as columns of Fm.
Gram-Schmidt diagonal norms exceed g, hence

    det(Fm*Fm) >= g^(2m).

Each column has norm at most M=L+sigma. Its distance from S is at most
h=eta+sigma. The top r squared singular values sum to at most m M^2, and the
remaining squared singular values sum to at most m h^2. AM-GM gives, for m>r,

    g^(2m) <= (m M^2/r)^r [m h^2/(m-r)]^(m-r).               (6.4)

For m>=2r and h<=g/2, set t=m/r. Then

    1 <= [t(M/g)^2]^r 2^(-(m-r)),
    t-1 <= log_2(t)+2log_2(M/g).

For t>=4, log_2(t)<=t/2, giving t<=2+4log_2(M/g). The cases t<4
give the other branch in (6.3). Since this bounds every finite prefix, it also
bounds an infinite sequence. QED.

The triangular determinant argument is established reduced-basis machinery,
specifically the projection/AM-GM lemma in DeVore–Petrova–Wojtaszczyk (2013).
No novelty is claimed for that lemma. The connection established here is that
an actually computed, noisy, failed solve produces exactly the separated
sequence to which it applies, without maximizing error over all future tasks
and without receiving exact solution snapshots.

An alternative, sometimes weaker, spectral bound follows from Hermiticity.
For k=1, a newly appended unit direction u has |u*Bv|>g-sigma, so
u*B^2u>(g-sigma)^2. For every 0<=a<(g-sigma)^2,

    R <= Tr((B^2-aI)_+)/((g-sigma)^2-a).                       (6.5)

Indeed the u are orthonormal and B^2-aI <= (B^2-aI)_+.
For multiple primitives replace B^2 by sum_j B_j^2, since ||theta||_1<=1.
This bound needs no approximate-rank promise, but may equal or exceed n.

7. SAFE PREPARATION AND FINITE COMPUTATION

The real-arithmetic algorithm normalizes an arbitrarily small b_perp, although
it never normalizes a tiny learned residual. A preparation-aware variant omits
b_perp when

    ||b_perp|| <= beta ||b||,
    beta <= min(1/2, tau/[16(M_A+sigma)]).                    (7.1)

If omitted, V=U and the known b_perp remains explicitly in the residual check.
The check and soundness proof are unchanged. Since ||H||<=M_A+sigma,

    (1-beta)||b|| <= ||V*b|| <= (M_A+sigma)||x||,
    ||b_perp|| <= tau ||x||/8.

The failure-to-information argument now gives

    dist(f_hat,span(U)) > tau-2sigma-tau/8 = 3tau/4.           (7.2)

Use g=3tau/4 in (6.1)-(6.4). With sigma=tau/16, a sufficient tail condition
is eta<=5tau/16; the conservative eta<=tau/8 used in examples has slack.
If b_perp is included, its normalized preparation has norm denominator at
least beta||b||. Every new cache direction has denominator at least g. These
two explicit margins replace an uncharged exact-zero or tiny-pivot decision.

The theorem above is a real-arithmetic theorem with an external-noise budget.
A validated finite implementation must additionally enclose orthogonality
error, primitive preparation error, accumulated vector arithmetic, the small
solve backward error and the computed residual norm. These debits can be
included in sigma and the stopping test; the strict constants leave room.
No machine-independent bit-complexity theorem or formal floating-point proof
is asserted here. The executed NumPy checks are finite diagnostics and do not
certify every rounding path. A separate appendix describes a rational
implementation route and what remains unimplemented.

8. TOTAL COST, INCLUDING EXPERIMENTAL PRECISION

Let T be the number of nonzero completed tasks and R the number of enrichments.
A task iteration queries at most one current b_perp per active primitive.
There are T+R such iterations. Cache columns are queried when appended and
requeried when the power-of-two capacity doubles. The latter refresh sizes
form a geometric series. Thus the number of primitive vector actions is

    k O(T+R),                                                (8.1)

with explicit accounting available in the implementation. This counts all
failed solves and all calibration refreshes. A task whose b already lies in
the cache span can require zero new action queries. Sparse theta can reduce
the per-task active-primitive count; all primitives are cached for future use.

For a declared price C_0 d^(-p), a column measured at capacity c costs
C_0 sigma^(-p)c^(p/2). At the final capacity c<=2(R+1), geometric summation
gives

    W <= C_p k C_0 sigma^(-p)
             [(R+1)^(1+p/2)+T(R+1)^(p/2)].                  (8.2)

For p=2 this is

    W = O(k C_0 sigma^(-2)[R^2+TR+T+1]).                      (8.3)

The source-action saving does not remove the R-dependent precision bill.
The formula is an upper bound; actual work can be much smaller if most tasks
arrive before the cache grows or need no transient direction.

Stored full vectors require O(knR+nR) scalar memory, not O(R) scalar memory.
Simple arithmetic for T+R task iterations is

    O((T+R)[kn(R+1)+k(R+1)^2+(R+1)^3]),                       (8.4)

plus actual primitive source work and QR/refresh work. Every full-vector
output costs Omega(n). Low-rank updates and cached reduced products can lower
constants, but are not needed for the theorem. This is a useful certificate
and source-information bound, not a claim of free offline or online work.

For confidence-based sources, an unknown task horizon can use an index-wise
failure allocation delta_t=6 delta/(pi^2 t^2) across primitive calls. All
reports then hold together with probability at least 1-delta. Any resulting
log(t/delta) repetition factors remain in total experimental cost. A fixed
confidence source event cannot be assumed to cover infinitely many newly
made stochastic measurements at no cost.

9. WHY THIS DOES NOT DISCARD HIDDEN COMPONENTS

A tail below today's response tolerance is retained as a quantified error,
not declared absent. A later task that couples strongly to an unresolved
component fails its residual check and triggers an actual new action.
If a later task asks for smaller epsilon or smaller coercivity mu, the cache
must be recalibrated and may need more directions. The theorem is for a fixed
accuracy floor; it is not a bound uniform as epsilon or mu tends to zero.

The learned model is not guaranteed to answer an arbitrary rational expression.
Every requested inverse is in the warranted mu-coercive task family, and its
computed residual is checked. The diag(0,eta)/eta obstruction is consequently
not bypassed by assertion: resolving such a denominator explicitly changes
the admissible accuracy and precision scale.

The result is also distinct from claiming arbitrary planning is efficient.
Section 10 constructs one convex planning consumer. Combinatorial planning,
unbounded feedback or arbitrary symbolic programs remain outside the theorem.

10. A CONSTRUCTED DOWNSTREAM CONVEX DESIGN OPERATION

Let B_j>=0, ||B_j||<=L, theta in the probability simplex, alpha>=mu>0, and

    F(theta)=b* [alpha I+sum theta_j B_j]^(-1)b + c*theta.

The inverse quadratic form is convex; its gradient is

    partial_j F = c_j - x_*^* B_j x_*,
    x_*=[alpha I+sum theta_j B_j]^(-1)b.

Use the above learner to obtain x and an absolute error bound d_x=c_res/mu.
The primitive Y_j z approximates B_j x with error at most sigma||x||.
Therefore the real computed value

    ghat_j=c_j-Re(x*Y_j z)

has a simultaneous deterministic enclosure

    |ghat_j-partial_j F|
       <= sigma||x||^2 + L d_x(2||x||+d_x) =: E_j.           (10.1)

For any current feasible theta, convexity bounds true regret by the
Frank-Wolfe gap. From intervals [l_j,u_j]=[ghat_j-E_j,ghat_j+E_j], an
explicit sound bound is

    F(theta)-min F <= max_i [sum_{j!=i}theta_j u_j
                              +(theta_i-1)l_i].              (10.2)

This is obtained by maximizing each linear expression theta*g-g_i over
the gradient box. It uses only k explicit candidates, not a planner oracle.
An upper bound below the requested design tolerance certifies the continuous
simplex optimum to that tolerance, regardless of how theta was proposed.

For an actual search algorithm, choose i minimizing ghat_i, use the simplex
vertex e_i, and update theta by the usual convex combination. The Hessian
operator norm is at most 2k L^2||b||^2/mu^3. Standard smooth conditional-gradient
analysis yields a finite convergence bound with an additive term controlled
by the gradient enclosure; choose the linear-solve/measurement tolerance to
make that term below the desired optimization tolerance. Exact constants and
the independently reconstructed proof are in the planning appendix. This is
ordinary convex optimization supplied with an acquired, certified numerical
oracle. It is not a claim of a new optimization method.

A sharper explicit simplex-chord curvature bound is

    C=2 L^2||b||^2/alpha^3.

With gradient radii at most epsilon_design/16, use step sizes 2/(t+2) and the
computable gap ghat*theta-min_j ghat_j+2 max_j E_j. The algorithm must encounter
a valid gap at most epsilon_design within

    2 max{1, ceil(8 C/epsilon_design)}

audited iterates. The proof sums the descent inequality over t=m,...,2m-1;
its minimum computed gap is at most 4C/(m+2)+7 max E_j. The planning appendix
also gives explicit source/solve tolerances sufficient to attain the required
gradient radii. The same retained action cache can serve all these iterates.

11. COMPARATORS AND REMAINING SCIENTIFIC BOUNDARY

Strong conventional methods already retain information:
  * Krylov subspace recycling and augmented/deflated iterations;
  * greedy reduced bases, including noisy-snapshot robustness;
  * adaptive randomized range finding and Nystrom preconditioners;
  * structured/hierarchical matrix learning when the structure class is supplied;
  * relative-energy model reduction and passive component libraries.

The present construction must be compared to these methods with identical
source access, accuracy, precision price, storage and future task sequence.
Restarting a solver from zero is useful as a diagnostic but is not the strongest
competitor. Finite experiments cannot establish universal superiority or
historical novelty. Actual results and corrections appear in RESULTS.txt.

The sharp supporting lower bound is in foundations_agent/precision_observability.md.
Even A=I+a uu*, with condition number at most two and exact algebra dimension
two, can hide its useful direction under bounded relative action errors.
Universal source-free compilation has an ambient-dimensional precision cost.
This explains the live-source contract; it does not prove that every future
task always needs a new measurement.

The main remaining advance would be a demonstrable total-cost improvement
over equally informed retaining numerical methods on a consequential family,
or a sharper general theorem for an acquired task geometry. The present
work constructs a robust continuing computation service and its accounting;
it does not claim to have achieved civilisation-changing capability.
