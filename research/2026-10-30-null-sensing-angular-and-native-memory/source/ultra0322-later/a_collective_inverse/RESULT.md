is an EXACT CPTP output of Q iid normalized Choi copies. It is not merely
approximately simulated one reflection at a time. Therefore arbitrary
joint task-level processing of that bank cannot remove its quadratic-
rank copy obstruction. This includes full-throughput, all-reference
decoders of an independently supplied native flat mixed payload, provided
there are no additional original-source calls.

    Q >= r^2[log k-h(e)-e log(k-1)]/(16k^2),
    Q >= r^2[log 2-h(e)]/64  for k=2,

available O(k^2 r^2/epsilon) inverse WITHIN this bank class. It proves
neither its epsilon dependence nor its online depth optimal.

  marginal rho has iid-Choi simulation using
  Q ceil(chi/delta) copies, chi=k^Q||rho||_op. The entire post-query
  workspace can be retained. In particular chi=1 gives an exact Q-copy

  <=b can be simulated with a FINITE common-frame Bell queue of at most
  ceil(Q k^(2b)/delta) copies, using only the supplied probe preparations

  All retries use ONE preacquired common-frame pool; no new frame is
  assumed to align. Arbitrary-known-matrix filters improve the copy-only

  ceil(sum_i m_i chi_i/delta). Their matrix acquisition is a separate cost.

queries at fixed k,b,error. The complete decoding error is e+delta+zeta
