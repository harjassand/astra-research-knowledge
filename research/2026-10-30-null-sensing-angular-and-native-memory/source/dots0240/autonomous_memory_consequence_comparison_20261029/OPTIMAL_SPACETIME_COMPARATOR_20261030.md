# Fresh strong comparator: 2610.03684v1
Read 2026-10-30. Primary: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03684 (2 October 2026), especially Theorem 1.1, Sections 2.1–2.2, Lemmas 3.1–3.2 and the readout/Clifford scope. This is a source comparison, not an independent audit of that paper.

Bharti–Haug–Khu–Tanggara construct Tanner-code memory with N=O(K+log(S/epsilon)) and O(SN) quantum locations. Their fixed-depth cycles include noisy syndrome extraction, decoder waits and correction; classical processing and records are reliable, with fixed parallel latency and non-growing routing delay. Initialization and mathematical recovery are encoded interfaces. Their random fault locations obey a local-stochastic law independent of logical input; each selected fault may be an arbitrary CPTP operation with retained shared environment. They explicitly exclude coherent superposition of fault-location sets, including weak coherent overrotation at every location. The construction also covers specified bounded-spread Clifford layers and destructive readout.

This is a stronger comparator than independent surface-code patches. Constant rate and exponential lifetime alone are not the proposed Astra distinction. Astra's finite-rate simple compiler instead supplies a time-independent noisy controller and tolerates sustained bounded continuous local perturbations, while retaining bounded-body graph interactions and ideal framed input/output. No superiority in constants, hardware cost or practical performance is established.
