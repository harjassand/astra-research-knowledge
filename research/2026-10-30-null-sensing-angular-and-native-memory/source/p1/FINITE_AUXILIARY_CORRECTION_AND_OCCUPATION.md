# Finite auxiliary correction and a uniform occupation certificate

30 October 2026. Root physical-model construction for the companion adaptive null-sensing theorem. This supplies a finite coherent correction Hamiltonian in place of an assumed effective irreversible syndrome jump. It retains an actual Markov reset reservoir as an explicit operation. It is not a manufactured device or a microscopic derivation of that reservoir.

## 1. Three physical qubits and a persistent environment

Let s be the sensing qubit, a a protected memory qubit, and r a reset qubit. The true signal and actual reference field give

\[
H_\delta=\frac{\theta-u}{2}Z_s,\qquad \delta=\theta-u.
\tag{1}
\]

Supply the simultaneous finite correction coupling

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Pi_-&=(I-Z_sZ_a)/2,\quad F=X_s\Pi_-,\\
H_c&=g(F\otimes r_+ + F^\dagger\otimes r_-)\\
   &=\frac g2(X_sX_r+Y_sZ_aY_r),\qquad 0<g<\infty,
\end{aligned}                                                    \tag{2}
\]

and the actual reset operation gamma D[r_-], with gamma finite and D[L]rho = Lrho L^dagger - {L^dagger L,rho}/2. A finite-temperature upward term is included in Section 5. The three-body term in (2), the signal-cancelling field, the protected memory, and the reservoir are physical resources. They cannot be inferred from a sensor's native noise generator.

Use the unitary coordinate change U = CNOT from a to s. In the transformed coordinates the original s label becomes the clock/gauge qubit q and a becomes the logical qubit L. Exactly,

\[
UZ_sU=Z_qZ_L,\quad UX_sU=X_q,\quad U\Pi_-U=n_q,
\quad UFU=q_-,                                      \tag{3}
\]

where n_q = |1><1| and q_- = |0><1|. Therefore

\[
H_\delta=\delta Z_LZ_q/2,\qquad
H_c=g(q_-r_+ + q_+r_-).                              \tag{4}
\]

Every correction/reset operation acts as the identity on L, exactly, at every finite g and gamma. We keep r in the dynamics throughout. There is no weak-excitation elimination, infinite recovery-rate limit or imposed classical clock trajectory.

The simplest permitted native error has joint jump operators X_q tensor B_b(t) on q and a persistent environment H, with

\[
\sum_b B_b(t)^\dagger B_b(t)\le\Lambda I_H.             \tag{5}
\]

Hidden-only Hamiltonians and Lindblad terms may be arbitrary and need not mix. Their state, law and number of degrees of freedom need not be learned. The source may depend on all old records, commands and time, provided (5) holds conditionally throughout service and no term acts on L. This includes a frozen hidden label, a changing hidden Markov chain, and a quantum hidden memory carried by the B_b. It does not include physical Z_s dephasing, which acts on L in these coordinates.

Initially the clock/reset/environment state is arbitrary, even correlated. Only L is prepared separately as in the companion receiver. The next theorem is uniform over that entire initial state.

## 2. A positive storage operator for finite correction

Order the q,r basis as 00,01,10,11. Define

\[
V=\begin{pmatrix}
0&0&0&0\\
0&1/\gamma&i/(2g)&0\\
0&-i/(2g)&1/\gamma+\gamma/(4g^2)&0\\
0&0&0&2/\gamma+\gamma/(4g^2)
\end{pmatrix}.                                      \tag{6}
\]

Let C be the finite correction generator with Hamiltonian H_c and downward reset gamma D[r_-]. Then

\[
V\ge0,\qquad c:=\|V\|=2/\gamma+\gamma/(4g^2),\qquad
\mathcal C^*(V)=-n_q.                               \tag{7}
\]

**Proof.** The middle block has positive diagonal and determinant 1/gamma^2. Its trace is c, so its largest eigenvalue is at most c. The 11 entry equals c and all other eigenvalues are nonnegative. For the last identity, insert (6) into

\[
\mathcal C^*(V)=i[H_c,V]
 +\gamma\left(r_+Vr_- -\{n_r,V\}/2\right).
\]

The imaginary coherence in (6) cancels the finite exchange-current terms; the remaining diagonal is -n_q. This is an exact algebraic equality. The runnable check verifies it independently at several finite g/gamma ratios.

The operator V also has the interpretation of expected accumulated clock error during passive finite correction: under C alone, its expectation decreases at the rate E n_q. Unlike a population-only bound, it handles coherences generated by finite exchange. Its bounded range is the entire warm-initialization penalty.

## 3. Uniform exposure with hidden memory and a warm clock

Let N_t be the native generator in (5), plus hidden-only evolution. Because V acts only on q,r,

\[
\mathcal N_t^*(V)
 = (X_qVX_q-V)\otimes\sum_b B_b^\dagger B_b
 \le \Lambda c I.                                  \tag{8}
\]

The negative V term is retained in the identity; positivity of V and the activity bound give the upper bound. Hidden-only terms vanish on V.

For the zero-residual comparison dynamics L_0,t = C + N_t, the identity Z_q = I-2n_q gives

\[
Z_q=I+2\mathcal L_{0,t}^*V-2\mathcal N_t^*V
\ge (1-2\Lambda c)I+2\mathcal L_{0,t}^*V.
\tag{9}
\]

Integrating its expectation yields the uniform warm-state certificate

\[
s_T=\int_0^T E_0 Z_q(t)dt
\ge (1-2\Lambda c)T-2c.                             \tag{10}
\]

No law for the hidden state was substituted into this inequality. It holds conditional on any old record, and for any conditional initial clock/environment state. If the actual clock/reset input is 00, its initial V expectation is zero and the boundary penalty vanishes; the adaptive receiver does not rely on that stronger preparation.

The signal Hamiltonian need not commute with V. Equation (10) is deliberately a *zero-residual comparison* certificate. Applying it without correction to the full nonzero-residual trajectory would be a different and unjustified claim. The companion Duhamel argument converts this null certificate into the actual signed response at small residual; it includes signal backreaction on the finite coherent clock.

## 4. The more general occupation principle

The preceding argument is not specific to this clock. Suppose a physical operation on a nuisance system supplies Hermitian S with ||S|| <= 1, positive bounded V, and a known v_* such that

\[
S\ge v_* I+\mathcal L_{0,t}^*V                  \tag{11}
\]

for every allowed conditional zero-residual evolution. Then, for arbitrary initial nuisance state,

\[
\int_0^T E_0 S_tdt\ge v_*T-\|V\|.                  \tag{12}
\]

Thus a storage inequality supplies the physical response information needed by global adaptive null sensing. Complete predictive-state learning, all-input channel certification, full clock-path measurement and bath initialization are unnecessary for this consequence. The required information is a robust operator inequality for the actual controlled source. A finite-data claim would still need a warranted acquisition route for its operator/rate premises; no such device certification is silently supplied here.

For arbitrary additional clock/environment Lindblad jumps J_b with sum J_b^dagger J_b <= Lambda_gen I, positivity and the anticommutator norm give

\[
\mathcal N_{\rm gen,t}^*V\le 2\Lambda_{\rm gen}c I.
\tag{13}
\]

A clock/environment Hamiltonian perturbation H_n with ||H_n|| <= h contributes at most 2hc I. These bounds are sufficient, not optimal and not impossibility results. All such disturbances must remain logical identity. A small norm error that acts directly on L belongs instead to the time-growing logical error budget in the companion proof.

## 5. Finite-temperature reset: no perfect cold-state premise

Include an actual upward reset jump gamma_up D[r_+] together with the downward gamma D[r_-]. Use the same positive V; the upward channel is a bounded clock disturbance. From (13),

\[
s_T\ge [1-2\Lambda c-4\gamma_{\uparrow}c]T-2c.
\tag{14}
\]

Take g = gamma = r and write gamma_up = nu r. Then c = 9/(4r). For a warranted initial frequency interval of width W, choose the fixed rates

\[
r\ge18(\Lambda+W),\qquad 0\le\nu\le1/36.
\tag{15}
\]

At every interrogation time T >= 1/W, (14) gives

\[
{s_T\over T}\ge1-\frac{9(\Lambda+W)}{2r}-9\nu\ge\frac12.
\tag{16}
\]

All rates and Hamiltonian norms are finite and independent of the final target epsilon. The initial state can remain warm. The upward-rate tolerance is nonzero. The constants were selected for a simple proof; they are not a claimed threshold for this or any other apparatus.

If a genuinely thermal reset bath has a warranted excitation/relaxation ratio nu = exp(-E_r/(k_B T_bath)), (15) allows a finite bath temperature and finite gap. That rate ratio requires an actual reservoir and coupling regime. A local Lindblad symbol on an interacting qubit is not by itself a derivation of heat, detailed balance, or the complete thermodynamic cycle. In particular, assigning E_r to every jump and calling their sum total apparatus work would be unjustified without the physical reservoir model.

## 6. What must be built, measured and paid for

The exact physical force/interface specified here is the signal term (1), the two-/three-body coupling (2), actual reset rates, protected memory a, logical preparation/readout, and a reference generator capable of supplying u. The coordinate change (3) does not supply these controls. For a device claim the coupler, reservoir bandwidth, phase stability, latency, heating and perturbations of the sensor must be established in that device.

The explicit supplier improves on an assumed effective jump sqrt(kappa) X_s Pi_- by retaining the finite reset mode and its reciprocal coherent backaction. It does not remove the reservoir, the protected memory or all microscopic control requirements. A Hamiltonian with the wrong phase can add a logical term; that error is not covered merely by a similar measured parity relaxation rate.

During total interrogation time mathcal T, the expected number of downward reset emissions is at most gamma mathcal T; upward events are at most gamma_up mathcal T; native jumps are at most Lambda mathcal T. They are counting bounds. An exponential-moment bound for a counting process of predictable intensity at most a is E exp(zN) <= exp[a mathcal T(e^z-1)], including the corresponding tail budget. Counts neither price the continuous drive nor pay for a bath.

The complete service uses a constant number of qubits (plus its persistent environment), fixed correction Hamiltonian amplitude g, fixed reservoir rates, and logical coherence for the longest T, which is order inverse epsilon. It also requires a calibrated frequency reference accurate to order epsilon and timing/readout/preparation meeting the companion mean-error budget. Preparation and readout counts grow as log^2 inverse epsilon in the conservative receiver. Standby power, reservoir maintenance, gate energy, sensor coupling, heat removal, classical computation, communication, timing and final restoration remain real costs. No total-work advantage over ordinary QEC or a conventional controller with these same ports is proved.

No reset of the hidden environment is required between trials. The local logical preparation can change its conditional state, including through extra retained records. This is why the certificate was proved uniformly over warm clock/environment states. A final demand to restore the hidden bath to its original physical state is a separate task and is not part of this sensing theorem.

## 7. Binding failures and comparison scope

* For a fixed nonzero residual, finite correction generally leaves phase-scale bias and long-time decoherence. A classical telegraph approximation cannot be substituted for the finite coherent supplier without a justified limiting argument. Adaptive residual control, not a claim of perfect arbitrary-field evolution, is the receiving mechanism.
* Adding physical Z_a dephasing acts directly on L. It is invisible to clock occupation and defeats unlimited sensing time. Physical Z_s noise also has a logical component. No aggregate noise-rate measurement establishes the required operator algebra.
* A constant reference offset shifts the null and limits absolute accuracy. A parameter drifting during service requires a tracking problem with its own information bound.
* The correction strength cannot be set from stationary weights alone. The actual activity/perturbation envelope after the controlled coupling is installed must be warranted.
* A standard robust phase-estimation controller with the same operations can use exactly this result. Finite-time digital correction may provide an alternative occupation certificate. No claim is made that this autonomous supplier uniquely enables the task.
* The complete-ensemble guarantee from continuation 12 is stronger and may serve other tasks. This task-specific response theorem does not inherit that arbitrary-future-intervention guarantee, or native-path preservation, or equality of full apparatus records.
