## 1. Exact triangular inverse and valid raw congruence

Write `A=sqrt(S)`, `C=sqrt(I-S)`, with S identity on G=I-B and bounded uniformly away from zero, and let Psi erase only the private owner to universally good Q0,W0. The local CP map is

    T(O)=A O A+C Psi*(O) C.

Since A is identity on the erased target and C vanishes there,

    Psi*(T(O))=Psi*(O),
    T^{-1}(Y)=A^{-1}Y A^{-1}-D Psi*(Y) D,
    D=A^{-1}C=C A^{-1}.                            (1.1)

The order around Psi*(Y) is essential; it need not commute with D. Direct substitution proves (1.1) on every operator, including all coherences. It is a bounded local HP unital inverse, not a positive inverse.

On `Z={Y:P_Q0,W0 Y P_Q0,W0=0}=ker Psi*`, the second term vanishes exactly. Congruence preserves Z because A and its inverse act as identity on that whole target subspace. Thus the raw source calculation can legitimately use the positive congruence on Z. Its raw drift lies in Z because good Q0,W0 has no DATA-changing source and all own controller transitions there preserve DATA and every flag. The raw negative bad target and good terminal target also lie in Z. Their order inequality therefore survives the inverse. The accepted strict raw bad service is obtained without treating the inverse as positive globally.

Exterior credit commutation gives an exact sum of bounded-support HP identity-annihilating maps `Lhat_h=T_h^{-1} L_h* T_h`. The full normalized operator is not claimed to be GKLS. On the invariant all-good block it agrees with the actual native generator.

Unassigned free-resource monitors must either be retained as extra local terms or assigned once each to an incident owner. The latter is a harmless exact regrouping: such terms commute that owner's credit and all actual flags, and annihilate its raw V and credited raw V. They do not change the accepted raw inequality. Their physical support must then enter that owner's guard support. No monitor term may be omitted after multiplying by activity weights.

## 3. Noncircular guards and strong cavity commutation

## 4. Relative circuit comparison; no extensive norm or order monotonicity

The global fixed coloring/order and bounded-change anchor rule are essential. If B=A union D for a bounded local completion, the selected gate sets differ in only O(1) gates. Common gates cancel outside the bounded structural cone of these differences. Therefore the quotient and inverse quotient contain O(1) bounded gates/inverses, and

    exp(-C delta) G_A^h <= G_B^h <= exp(C delta) G_A^h.

The same argument compares different cavity indices:

    exp(-C delta) G_A^h <= G_B^g <= exp(C delta) G_A^h,      (4.1)

because changing h to g deletes/restores only two bounded gate sets. For buffered charging, g is in the bounded neighborhood of h, so the associated completion/anchor patches are also bounded. ## 6. Both activation effects and a unified relative bound

Let F_A=f_A G_A and F_A,cav=f_A G_A^h. Their difference vanishes on the h-bad block and preserves h's flag, but this does NOT mean the dirty credit preserves that equality. In fact

    B_h[T_h(F_A)-T_h(F_A,cav)]B_h
      =C_h Psi_h*(F_A-F_A,cav) C_h,                  (6.1)

which can be nonzero. Differentiating this extra bad-source term is part of the true similarity. In addition, applying the inverse to the resulting drift produces the target subtraction in (1.1). Both are generally O(delta), not zero.

There is a compact exact proof controlling all of them simultaneously. The localized quotient is

    Z_A=Y_A(Y_A^h)^{-1},
    Q_A=Z_A^dagger Z_A-I,
    ||Q_A||<=C delta,

with support in a uniformly bounded patch around h. All these factors commute raw factors. Therefore

    F_A-F_A,cav=(Y_A^h)^dagger f_A Q_A Y_A^h,
    Lhat_h(F_A-F_A,cav)
      =(Y_A^h)^dagger Lhat_h(f_A Q_A) Y_A^h.         (6.2)

Equation (6.2) uses exact cavity bimodularity and the COMPLETE local inverse, not its positive-congruence restriction.

Split f_A into unaffected positive factors and the O(1) factors meeting this bounded patch. The former pass through Lhat_h by locality; the latter have a uniform finite spectral ratio. Since Lhat_h is a bounded local HP map and Q_A has norm O(delta),

    +/- Lhat_h(f_A Q_A)<=C delta f_A.

Congruence yields the crucial relative estimate

    +/- E_h,A <=C delta f_A G_A^h,
    E_h,A=Lhat_h(F_A-F_A,cav).                       (6.3)

Constants may depend on the fixed inverse conditioning and token parameters, but not A or N. Error terms are zero unless A selected a deleted gate, so their animals meet a fixed patch near h. Equation (6.3) is an actual local estimate, not an invalid global expansion G_A-I.

Let X_h be a sufficiently generous fixed local buffer containing the supports relevant to the intended clean calculation and the linked activation terms. Put
