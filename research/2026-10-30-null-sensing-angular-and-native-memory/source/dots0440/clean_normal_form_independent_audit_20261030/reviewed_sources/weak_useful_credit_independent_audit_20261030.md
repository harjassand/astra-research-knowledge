Therefore the killed K0 has one simple semisimple zero eigenvalue and all other eigenvalues strictly in the left half-plane. ## 3. Laurent expansion and spectral excess
Actual clean fast jumps are parity covariant; the weak edge alone changes I versus busy parity. Hence `K_-epsilon=Ad_Z K_epsilon Ad_Z`. Analytic finite-matrix perturbation of the simple zero eigenvalue gives an even real slow eigenvalue. The actual event contraction bounds its real part above by `T^{-1}log(1-c epsilon^2)`, forcing

    lambda(epsilon)=-a epsilon^2+O(epsilon^4), a>0.

The remaining resolvent is uniformly bounded. Applied to I this gives

    R_epsilon=c0 epsilon^-2 Q_return+epsilon^-1 Y+Z_epsilon,
    c0=1/a>0,  ||Z_epsilon||<=M.

The normalization uses the entire idle monitor density; since Tr(sigma_I I)=1, the leading coefficient on the whole idle block is c0 I, not a rank-one projector. Parity removes an odd homogeneous Q_return term.

The first forcing is `[H_init,Q_return]`, supported only in I/A and A/I. That coherence subspace is invariant under the full K0*. Fast Hamiltonians preserve families. Fixed-phase reset gains annihilate I/A offdiagonal observables. Hub-label monitor gains do likewise. A resource-label monitor can preserve an I/A coherence when its labels coincide, but cannot move it to C or Q. Losses preserve families. Consequently Y lies only in I/A plus A/I. This is the actual monitor-sensitive structural fact needed for the excess bound.

The negative gap `-c0 p_A epsilon^-2` on A absorbs the epsilon^-1 I/A cross term by Young's inequality. C also has a strict gap, while the degenerate I/Q maximal-eigenvalue space has no Y compression. Therefore

    R_epsilon <= (c0 epsilon^-2+C) I

for a fixed C. Idle diagonal deviations are O(1), and idle/busy offdiagonals are O(epsilon^-1). Choosing `Rmax=c0 epsilon^-2+C` and `beta=b epsilon^2` gives analytic, parity-covariant S and B=I-S, with S>=I/2 and

    B_II=O(epsilon^2), B_I,busy=O(epsilon), B_busy,busy=O(1).

The constants can be chosen uniformly over the finite allowed local controller types. This proof would not follow from an arbitrary terminal tail or an arbitrary degenerate idle manifold.

## 4. Analytic CP map and exact isolated drift
For a fixed parity-invariant ready state, the ready-evaluation map followed by Phi_h is CP. Embedding its output with positive controller effect B_h is CP as well. Thus

    Q_h(O)=sqrt(S_h) O sqrt(S_h)
           +B_h tensor Phi_h*(E_ready(O))

is unital CP. It is analytic because sqrt(S_h) is analytic above the fixed spectral lower bound and the other branch is linear in B_h. No analytic principal square root of the possibly singular B_h is needed. The virtual ready reset is analytic bookkeeping, not a new physical reset.

On DATA-only V, `Q_h(V)=Phi_h*V+S_h d_h`, where `d_h=V-Phi_h*V>=0`. Idempotence is used for the successful Phi_h, not for the generally nonidempotent abort/success excursion mixture. The killed equation and complete terminal instrument give exactly

    L_h* Q_h,t(V)
      =-[(1-t beta_h Rmax_h) A_T,h+t beta_h I] d_h,
    Q_h,t=(1-t)Id+t Q_h.

The latter channel is linearly invertible for fixed t<1/4 because `||t(Q_h-Id)||_cb<=2t<1`; this is not an inverse-CP assertion.

The neighbor-ready guard is an actual finite-support projection. On the clean guarded sector every resource belongs either to h or is free, giving the stated isolated-controller/spectator isometry. The virtual ready branch clears only h's own tags and preserves every other BUSY flag. Extending S by identity and B by zero off guard produces a genuine local unital CP map retaining cross-guard no-jump coherences. The finite colored circuit is analytic and local.

## 5. Exact pair preservation
For any adjacent pair e={h,j}, each guarded credit fixes `P_e=P_hP_j`. Endpoint credits are disabled if the other endpoint is busy; a different owner's credit preserves both flags. Since the maps are unital CP, P_e belongs to their multiplicative domains. Every collision projection generated by the commuting pair projections is therefore fixed and in the multiplicative domain of the whole circuit.

This proves exact collision-sector preservation, not just a leading-order statement. In particular a virtual erase can remove an isolated busy owner but cannot pull an order-one collision penalty into an all-ready source. The singleton comparison `T(P_h)>=(1-t)P_h` is also valid, but is not by itself a DATA-weighted product inequality.

## 6. Repair of the old coherent negative-family gap
All claims here concern **actual `L_epsilon* T_epsilon(V)`**, not `T_epsilon^{-1} L_epsilon* T_epsilon(V)`.

The original U/U wording did not define a positive family across different independent activity masks. Subtracting only its epsilon=0 value would allow an uncontrolled O(epsilon) derivative. A concrete repair is available. Let G_h be the neighbor-ready guard and set

    Z_h(epsilon)=G_h[(1-t beta_h Rmax_h)A_T,h+t beta_h I]d_h >=0,
    N_epsilon=sum_h T_without_h,epsilon(Z_h(epsilon)) >=0.

`T_without_h` means the same ordered CP circuit with h's map omitted. The coefficient commutes d_h, and every other map is CP, so N is a genuine positive operator for every epsilon. This is a coherent comparator, not a separate selection of a negative value on each mask.

Take a buffered finite local cone containing every relevant guard and generator term, and compress to its collision-free busy corner U. The constant and first Taylor coefficients of

    U [L_epsilon* T_epsilon(V)+N_epsilon] U

vanish. The coefficient proof is:
1. At epsilon=0 all active owners in a collision-free mask are isolated and have disjoint full DATA/monitor footprints. Their actual isolated identities factor through the other positive credits and give exactly -N_0.
2. A first-order change between independent masks either creates/removes a nonadjacent isolated owner, or enters a collision. In the former case the isolated identity is exact before differentiation and the disjoint channels commute, so its derivative is the derivative of -N. The latter case is removed by U compression.
3. A fast ready-return can open a neighboring guard. If that neighbor was active, the source contains a collision. If it was ready, its ready diagonal credit differs from identity only at O(epsilon^2); its order-epsilon offdiagonal leads to a collision at the source. Hence no additional first-order U/U contribution survives.
4. Shared resource monitors are included in the actual isolated contribution. Nonadjacent active owners have disjoint resource-monitor supports. An inactive overlapping owner's first-order contribution either enters a collision or is covered by the same ready-block argument. No shared monitor is modeled as dephasing or omitted.

Analyticity in a fixed local cone gives the residual local U/U norm O(epsilon^2). Positivity of N then yields the claimed local upper bound without taking an inverse of a negative family.

For actual L*T, disjoint circuit factors **commute out and remain as outer CP factors**; they do not disappear algebraically. Group linked local differences before estimating them, retain those outer norm-one positive maps, and use bounded depth/incidence to control each local remainder. This repairs the manuscript's overly terse “cancel” wording. A separate DATA-weighted animal surgery is still needed to turn these local estimates into the intended global memory drift.

The other local block orders follow with the same comparator:
- all-ready diagonal: exact coefficient `-t epsilon^2 sum_h b_h d_h`; cross-owner words cannot return to all-ready at order two; parity removes odd diagonal orders, so remainder O(epsilon^4);
- all-ready/independent-busy: the first-order term is isolated cancellation; at order two, two nonadjacent initiations factor into exact disjoint isolated identities. Adjacent creation enters collision. Thus this block is O(epsilon^3);
- all-ready/collision: at least two weak toggles, hence O(epsilon^2);
- independent-busy/collision: one weak toggle can suffice, hence O(epsilon);
- collision corner: a fixed local O(1) bound suffices.

Here U contains arbitrary independent sets, not just one busy owner. The buffer must include all activity/guard projections influencing the linked term. These are local coefficient statements with bounded constants, not a completed weighted-basin theorem.

    R_epsilon|C = R_C  exactly, with ||R_C||<infinity,

    Q_h(A_T d_h)=sqrt(S_C) A_T sqrt(S_C) d_h
