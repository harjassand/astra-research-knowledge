# Exact pair preservation by the guarded useful-credit circuit
2026-10-30. Clean legal range. Applies to the analytic virtual-reset construction in `ANALYTIC_GUARDED_USEFUL_CREDIT.md`.

Let P_h be actual BUSY flags and let e={h,j} be an edge of the FULL read/write overlap graph. Every guarded useful-credit map Q_k,epsilon,t fixes P_e=P_hP_j EXACTLY, for every epsilon, not only to leading order.

* If k is neither endpoint, its local operations may change only P_k and fix both endpoint projections. Its guard and credit coefficients commute them. Thus Q_k(P_e)=P_e.
* If k=h, then P_j=1 disables its guard. There S_h=I and B_h=0. On the enabled guard P_j=0, P_e=0 before and after its virtual ready reset. Therefore Q_h(P_e)=P_e. The j case is symmetric.

A unital CP map fixing a projection has that projection in its multiplicative domain. Consequently for the full ordered circuit T,

    T(P_e O)=P_e T(O),
    T(O P_e)=T(O)P_e.

Every collision projection generated by these commuting adjacent-pair projections is also fixed. In particular T(V) is block diagonal between collision and collision-free sectors when V is. The circuit does not create, erase or coherently mix an actual adjacent collision. It may virtually erase an ISOLATED busy hub, which is harmless for this statement.

For singleton P_h, all other hub maps fix it. Its own interpolated map has an explicit identity branch and hence

    Q_h,t(P_h)>=(1-t)P_h.

Positivity and the other maps' fixed-projection property give

    T(P_h)>=(1-t)P_h.

For a DATA-weighted charge, one still needs the local relative comparison around h to move a clock coefficient through the finite circuit; the unweighted inequality alone is not a blanket operator-product rule. Only the boundedly many gates near h are involved, so the existing local-orbit comparison can supply a fixed factor, rather than an exponential-in-animal-size loss.

## Why this helps combine activity and useful service
For the animal-local activity product G_A, all pair gates are identity in a collision-free mask. Thus

    G_A|collision-free = I+O(epsilon^2),
    G_A|all-ready=I,

and G_A commutes every BUSY flag. Its epsilon=0 excess G_A,0-I is supported entirely in collision sectors. Since T fixes those sectors, its virtual reset CANNOT pull this order-one pair credit into an all-ready source. Pair-credit creation under the physical weak Hamiltonian still occurs only from an already-busy state.

The singleton activity modification is O(epsilon^2) and has zero ready block. Under the useful circuit its ready diagonal is O(epsilon^4) and its ready/busy block O(epsilon^3): the only own-credit ready/busy amplitude is O(epsilon). These orders persist under the finite circuit and its collision-preserving guards.

Therefore, in a direct L*T(V_activity) calculation, useful-credit perturbations of the preexisting pair penalty are pair-supported O(t), while those of the singleton penalty are O(t epsilon^2) on busy states, O(t epsilon^3) across ready/busy, and O(t epsilon^4) in ready. This is the structural alternative to applying a generic O(t^2) remainder everywhere or passing a negative operator through T^{-1}.

A feasible qualitative parameter order is: fixed physical rates and animal constants; fixed B; A sufficiently large for the activity hierarchy; delta sufficiently small; t sufficiently small compared with delta B and delta A; finally epsilon sufficiently small that all O(epsilon^4) ready sources are dominated by useful t epsilon^2 service. All are fixed positive numbers independent of system size and memory time.
