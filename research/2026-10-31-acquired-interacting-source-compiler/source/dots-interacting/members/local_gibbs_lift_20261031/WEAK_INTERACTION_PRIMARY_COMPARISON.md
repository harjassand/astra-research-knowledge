# Weak-interaction rapid mixing: ordinary primary comparator

31 October 2026. Scope comparison, not a novelty assessment.

Zhan, Ding, Huhn, Gray, Preskill, Chan and Lin, *Rapid quantum ground state preparation via dissipative dynamics*, PRX16,011004 (2026), arXiv2503.15827v3, already establishes logarithmic-size mixing for certain weakly interacting spin and fermion systems. Theorem2 and AppendixH use filtered jump operators (Equation4), with system-size-independent small-interaction conditions; their argument controls an oscillator norm. The paper also discusses actual block-encoding/quadrature and simulation resources rather than treating jumps as free. Primary text inspected: https://arxiv.org/html/2503.15827v3 .

Thus logarithmic mixing near a product pure state is not, by itself, a new consequence of our local-energy calculation. Our specified direction-merged jumps differ from their frequency-filtered construction and are constrained to preserve a particular classical heat-bath diagonal law. A transfer of their theorem to these exact jumps requires checking hypotheses, not merely matching ground states. Conversely, our explicit cross-drift inequality is a sufficient certificate for one constrained realization, not a claim to supersede their broader preparation methods or establish priority.

The consequential question remains whether the acquired, law-preserving source plus controlled fast simulation yields a useful task-level advantage after state preparation, coupling access and all runtime costs. Neither logarithmic return alone nor another checked family establishes that.
