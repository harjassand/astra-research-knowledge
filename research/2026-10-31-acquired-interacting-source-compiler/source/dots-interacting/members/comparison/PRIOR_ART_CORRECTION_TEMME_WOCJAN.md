# Prior-art correction: quantized classical couplings

31 October 2026. This addendum leaves the frozen proofs unchanged but corrects how their scientific contribution must be described.

**The general reverse-map lift is established machinery, not a discovery of this project.** Temme and Wocjan, *Quantized Markov Chain Couplings that Prepare Qsamples*, Quantum9,1951 (2025), arXiv2504.02651v2, Equation57 gives the same stationary-weighted inverse-map Kraus construction. Lemma16 bounds quantum infidelity by classical coalescence probability divided by the minimum stationary probability. Theorem20 explicitly requires efficient images, preimages, random-choice probabilities and stationary ratios. The primary text was actually read: https://arxiv.org/html/2504.02651v2 .

Our reverse-map proof is therefore an independently checked receiving derivation with target-law orientation and access qualifications, not a new quantization principle. The threshold-coupling lift is likewise a specialization of that established approach, as the independent reviewer has derived. Neither should be promoted as an original broad lifting theorem.

The separately proved forced-overlap/permutation-oracle obstruction and complete controlled fast-source compiler require their own comparison; prior existence does not automatically validate or invalidate those conjunctions. The proposed local-energy drift estimate concerns a particular direction-merged realization and may improve a conservative return bound, but has not been shown to be new or consequential. No literature-priority claim is made.
