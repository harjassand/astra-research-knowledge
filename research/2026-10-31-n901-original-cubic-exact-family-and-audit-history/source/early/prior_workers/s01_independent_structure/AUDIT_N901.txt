2. The surviving pointwise certificate, independently reconstructed

Let lambda=kappa/(4sigma^2). For arbitrary real X,Y,Z and fixed r>0,

  J_r([[Z,Y],[-X,-sigma Z]]) >= lambda Z^2(Y+sigma X)^2.      (P)

Subtract the right side and write D=A+B Z^2+C Z^4. Direct fresh symbolic
expansion gives

  A=J_r([[0,Y],[-X,0]]),
  B=(r+2)[(9r^3+18r^2+8r)Y^2
            -(18r^3+50r^2+44r+12)XY]/[12sigma^2],
  C=(r+2)(9r^3+36r^2+40r+12)/24 > 0.

The exact factorisation is

  4AC-B^2 = r(sigma X+Y)^2 S_r(X,Y)/(144sigma^4),

where

  S_r = 36r^7 X^2
       +r^6(252X^2+144XY)
       +r^5(709X^2+972XY+54Y^2)
       +r^4(1050X^2+2566XY+360Y^2)
       +r^3(889X^2+3410XY+881Y^2)
       +r^2(432X^2+2408XY+1004Y^2)
       +r(112X^2+856XY+536Y^2)
       +12X^2+120XY+108Y^2.

All sign branches are covered as follows.

* If XY<=0, then B>=0. Since A>=0 and C>0, D>=0 for every Z.
  This includes X=0 or Y=0; if both vanish, D=CZ^4.
* If XY>0, all X^2,XY,Y^2 terms in S_r are nonnegative and S_r>0.
  Thus 4AC-B^2>=0 and
     D=C(Z^2+B/(2C))^2+(4AC-B^2)/(4C)>=0.

For completeness, positivity A>=0 does not require assuming quasiconvexity.
For any F, let h=ab+cd and t=(2r+1)/3. The exact identity is

  J_r(F)=alpha (u,v,j) M (u,v,j)^T +(4alpha/3)h^2,

  M=[[t^2,1/3,2t],[1/3,1,-2],[2t,-2,16/3+1/alpha]].

Its leading minors are (2r+1)^2/9, 4r(r+1)/9, 16(r+1)/27,
all strictly positive for r>0. Hence M is positive definite and J_r>=0.

This argument makes no assumption that X,Y,Z are positive. In particular, the
positive-variable declaration in l14's symbolic checker is not used to infer
the missing sign branches.

3. What (P) actually proves, including W^{2,4}

Taking X=sigma p, Y=w, Z=q and applying Cauchy--Schwarz gives

  kappa <pqw+sigma^2 p^2q>^2 <= 4sigma^2 <p^2> Q.            (S)

Indeed <p q(w+sigma^2p)>^2 <= <p^2><q^2(w+sigma^2p)^2>,
and (P) bounds the second factor. No division by <p^2> is required.

All statements extend directly to periodic W^{2,4}: the Hessian entries are
L^4, their quadratic products are L^2, cubic products are L^{4/3} and hence
integrable on the normalized finite torus, and quartics are L^1. The algebra
holds almost everywhere and the displayed Cauchy inequality is legitimate.
Alternatively strong periodic W^{2,4} smoothing passes every integral to the
limit by Holder. There is no boundary term, concentration restriction, assumed
wave equation, or unsupported product of distributions.

This regularity check validates (S), not (T).

4. Exact smooth falsifier of the claimed identity

Let psi=cos x+cos y+cos(x+y), on the 2pi-periodic square torus with normalized
average. Then

  q=-cos(x+y),
  p=-cos x-cos(x+y),
  w=-cos y-cos(x+y),

and exact Fourier integration gives

  <q^3>=0,   <pqw>=-1/4,   <p^2q>=0.

Thus the claimed identity <q^3>=<pqw> is false already for smooth psi.
It is not rescued by W^{2,4} extension or by periodic integration by parts.

5. No finite comparison between the two cubic functionals

At r=1 (sigma=2), take the smooth trigonometric polynomial

  psi=cos(x+y)+cos(x-2y)+cos(2x-y)
      +cos(5x)+cos(5y)+(51/15625)cos(5x+5y).

Fresh exact rational Fourier convolution gives

  <q^3>=-6,   <pqw>=-12,   <p^2q>=3.

Consequently

  <q^3+4p^2q>=6,   <pqw+4p^2q>=0.

No finite multiplicative constant K can give
|target cubic|<=K|surrogate cubic| for all smooth compatible psi. The proof
cannot be repaired simply by weakening that missing identity to such a
comparison. This is still not a negative instance of (T): the right-hand side
of (T) is not zero for this field.

6. The unchanged general-U transfer is refuted

On U=sigma a+d=0, (P) is equivalent to

  J_r(F)>=lambda a^2(b-sigma c)^2.

For the unrestricted matrix F0=[[1,0],[1,0]], however, the difference is

  J_r(F0)-lambda a^2(b-sigma c)^2
     =r(7r^2+4r-2)/[12(r+2)].

It is negative for 0<r<(3sqrt(2)-2)/7. At r=1/10 it equals -17/2800.
Here U=sigma is nonzero.

Compatibility does not restore this particular extension: f=g=sin x gives
D(f,g)=cos x F0 and hence the averaged difference is (3/8) times that scalar.
At r=1/10 it is exactly -51/22400. This refutes only the unchanged bound (P)
outside the acoustic-null slice; it does not refute the original conformal
Jensen/quasiconvexity target or rule out different coupled algebra.
