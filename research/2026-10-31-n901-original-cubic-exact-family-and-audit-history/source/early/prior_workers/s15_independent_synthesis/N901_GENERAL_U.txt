2. The proposed cubic Hessian linkage is false

For a scalar stream potential phi, define
  p=phi_xx, q=phi_xy, w=phi_yy,
  X=sigma p, Y=w, Z=q.
The exact U=0 field is V=[[Z,Y],[-X,-sigma Z]]. Equation(17) is
  kappa <Z(Z^2+X^2)>^2 <= 4<X^2><J_r(V)>.

L14's verified pointwise quartic algebra concerns

Its Cauchy-Schwarz consequence controls

The actual cubic is
  I=<q^3+sigma^2p^2q> = I_tilde + D,
  D=<q^3-pqw> = -<phi_xy det D^2phi>.
The proposed identity D=0 is false.

For phi=cos x+cos y+cos(x+y), exactly

This is an actual smooth compatible Hessian. It falsifies the linkage, not
equation(17): the ORIGINAL cubic I is zero for this particular example.

3. Exact Fourier geometry of the missing cubic defect

Use phi(x)=sum_k phi_hat(k) exp(i k.x), so Hessian coefficients are negative
quadratic frequency monomials. For every ordered frequency triad k+l+m=0,
the fully symmetrized multiplier of D is

  T_D(k,l,m) = (k cross l)^2
              (k_x k_y+l_x l_y+m_x m_y)/6.                 (S15.1)

Here k cross l=k_x l_y-k_y l_x. To verify, write
  k=(p,q), l=(u,v), m=(-p-u,-q-v).
The difference between the symmetrized multipliers of q^3 and pqw is exactly
  (pv-qu)^2(2pq+pv+qu+2uv)/6.
The last quadratic factor is sum_i k_ix k_iy.

This proves a precise structural fact: the omitted defect consists entirely
of noncollinear frequency-triad interactions. A field supported on two
nonparallel characteristic lines has no noncollinear resonant triple unless
the zero mode is present; its Hessian zero mode vanishes. Thus the special
characteristic proof's cancellation does not extend by directional averaging
to arbitrary Hessians. It omits exactly the new noncollinear triad term.

Equation(S15.1) gives no sign: reflection of one spatial coordinate changes
the second factor's sign while leaving the area square fixed. An estimate
must control D together with the original cubic and quartic, rather than
declare it zero or discard it as favorable.

The bound from the surviving certificate is only
  kappa (I-D)^2 <= 4<X^2> Q.                              (S15.2)
To recover(17), one still needs a compatible signed budget for D using the
actual residual quartic, or some different inequality. A bound on D alone
with an uncontrolled additive factor does not yield the sharp unit threshold.

4. No alternative homogeneous cubic local Hessian null identity exists

Elementary proposition. If a homogeneous cubic polynomial P(p,q,w) satisfies
  <P(phi_xx,phi_xy,phi_yy)>=0
for every real smooth periodic phi, then P is identically zero.

Proof. First use phi(x)=H(k.x) with k an integer covector. Choose a smooth
periodic H whose second derivative has zero mean but nonzero third moment;
for example H''=cos theta+cos(2theta), whose third moment is 3/4. Homogeneity
then forces P(k_x^2,k_x k_y,k_y^2)=0. Rational directions are dense, so P
vanishes on the rank-one quadratic cone pw=q^2. Direct polynomial division
(or comparing the seven coefficients of the resulting binary sextic) yields
  P=(pw-q^2)(A p+B q+C w)
for real constants A,B,C.

Now test
  phi_mn=cos(mx)+cos(ny)+cos(mx+ny), m,n positive integers.
A direct three-cosine average gives
  <P(D^2phi_mn)>=-m^2 n^2[2A m^2+Bmn+2C n^2]/4.
The three tests (m,n)=(1,1),(1,2),(2,1) force
  [[2,1,2],[2,2,8],[8,2,2]] [A,B,C]^T=0.
That matrix has determinant12, hence A=B=C=0. QED.

This excludes repairs which merely replace q^3-pqw by some other universally
zero homogeneous cubic polynomial of the local scalar Hessian. It does not
exclude nonlocal estimates, lower-derivative-dependent divergence identities,
or a different nonlinear analytic argument.

This is a self-contained reconstructed special case, not a priority claim:
Ball-Currie-Olver(1981), Theorem4.1, classifies higher-order null Lagrangians
as affine combinations of Jacobians of lower-order derivatives. In dimension2
these Jacobians have degree at most2, consistent with the proposition. The
primary paper's introduction and stated classification were inspected:
https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/~ball/Papers/Ball,%20Currie%20&%20Olver%201981.pdf
No general new null-Lagrangian classification is claimed here.

5. Exact weighted Hodge representation of arbitrary general-U perturbations

For arbitrary real periodic f,g in W^{1,4}, decompose the mean-zero vector
  (sigma f,g)=(psi_y,-psi_x)+(chi_x,chi_y).
The potentials psi,chi are W^{2,4}, by the standard periodic Fourier Hodge
construction; constants do not affect any field below. For completeness,
at each nonzero integer k, the two vectors (ik_y,-ik_x) and (ik_x,ik_y)
form an invertible orthogonal basis, which defines the two scalar potentials.
The corresponding second derivatives are order-zero Fourier multipliers.

Write
  p=psi_xx, q=psi_xy, w=psi_yy,
  A=chi_xx, B=chi_xy, C=chi_yy.
Then, exactly,
  a=(q+A)/sigma, b=(w+B)/sigma,
  c=-p+B, d=-q+C, U=A+C=Delta chi.                  (S15.3)
At chi=0 this is the N901 stream-potential class, after replacing the source's
scalar potential phi by psi/sigma. General-U is exactly chi unrestricted.

The acoustic energy decomposes as
  A_acoustic = kappa<p^2> + <(Delta chi)^2+kappa B^2>
               -2kappa<p B>.                            (S15.4)
The mixed term is a genuine compatible interaction. Ordinary Hodge orthogonality
does not remove it, because the acoustic metric is different.

For fixed psi, minimization of this quadratic energy over chi is explicit.
The Euler equation and nonzero-frequency multiplier are
  (Delta^2+kappa partial_xxyy)chi=kappa partial_xxxy psi,
  chi_hat(k)= kappa k_x^3 k_y
              /(|k|^4+kappa k_x^2k_y^2) psi_hat(k).
Consequently
  min_chi A_acoustic
    = sum_{k!=0} kappa k_x^4 |k|^4
       /(|k|^4+kappa k_x^2k_y^2) |psi_hat(k)|^2,
  4kappa/(4+kappa) ||psi_xx||_2^2
    <= min_chi A_acoustic <= kappa||psi_xx||_2^2.       (S15.5)
Both constants are attained on suitable single directions (including diagonal
directions for the lower one). These are quadratic facts only; minimization of
the full Jensen energy also changes its cubic and quartic terms.

6. Explicit obstruction to adding a nonnegative general-U reserve

Take r=1, sigma=2, kappa=1. Let psi=cos(x+y), chi=t psi, and define f,g by
(S15.3). If H=psi''(x+y)=-cos(x+y), then
  V=H [[(1+t)/2,(1+t)/2],[t-1,t-1]].
This is a genuine smooth periodic rank-one gradient field. Its third moments
vanish, and exact integration gives
  E_t(s)=s^2[4t^2+(1-t)^2]/2
          +3(5t^2-6t+5)^2/256.
In particular
  E_0(s)=s^2/2+75/256,
  E_(1/5)(s)=2s^2/5+3/16,
  E_(1/5)(s)-E_0(s)=-s^2/10-27/256<0.              (S15.6)
Thus the natural stream projection chi->0 increases the energy, even on this
elementary compatible family. There can be no universal extension of the form
  E(psi,chi,s) >= E(psi,0,s) + a nonnegative chi reserve.
Both energies in(S15.6) are positive. This is an obstruction to a proposed
proof transfer, NOT a negative Jensen field or a quasiconvexity counterexample.
The broad possibility that scalar-row projections raise J_r energy was already
known in N874; this exact weighted-Hodge example independently reconstructs the
failure for the present general-U decomposition rather than claiming priority.

7. The first genuinely coupled cubic term

For chi=t eta, denote eta Hessian entries by A,B,C and e=A+C. Differentiating
the exact cubic P at t=0 gives the following local density before averaging:

  P_1 = e[-(r+2)q^2/sigma^2+r p^2+2pw/sigma]
        -kappa A[3q^2/sigma^3+p^2/sigma]
        +2kappa pq B/sigma.                              (S15.7)

The quadratic background term has first variation -2kappa<p eta_xy>; the
