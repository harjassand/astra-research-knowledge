## Strong ordinary alternatives are admitted

[Bravyi--Terhal, 0907.2807v2](https://arxiv.org/html/0907.2807), Section 6,
is the controlling ordinary receiver. Its theorem assumes exact commutation
of the protected observables/projector with the Hamiltonian, but its trace
proof is the direct antecedent of the local stationary-module estimate used
here. The finite-frequency nominal Davies locality and block norm estimates
are also already explicit there. We do not claim a new generic memory or
stationarity principle. The independent whole-support projector pays the
extra L†L protection needed to simplify its block decomposition.

[Alicki et al., 0811.0033](https://arxiv.org/html/0811.0033), Section II.1,
already uses the thermal virtual subsystem and autocorrelations. The present
nominal thermal encoder is that ordinary construction, with an explicit
classical rejection sampler and its actual cost, rather than a new native
thermal preparation operation.

[Drechsler--Oreg--Aharonov, 2610.05589v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05589v1),
Definitions 2.22 and 2.35, requires more than Kraus operators of the form
Pauli times a full-syndrome function: each marginal CSS-sector flip rate or
probability must belong to its own sector algebra. Its Section 1.8 explicitly
separates general coherent/mixed Markovian errors from the proved framework.
The exact joint-detuning kernel here fails that sufficient reduction, not
quantum memory itself. Our neutral class may couple sectors and still has a
stationary full-quantum proof; no sector-independent closure is asserted.

[Wallman--Emerson, 1512.01098](https://arxiv.org/html/1512.01098), Theorem 1,
already establishes randomized-compiling noise tailoring under its stated
easy/hard gate assumptions. [Winick et al., 2212.07500](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.07500)
extends the analysis to non-Markovian and gate-dependent settings with its
specific hypotheses. These are stronger same-control incumbents if random
gates, compilation and feedback resources are allowed. Finite-rate passive
stabilizer dephasing is not the same control contract. We did not rebrand a
Pauli-frame protocol or silently assume its output is a continuous-time
Markov Pauli semigroup.

[Oreshkov--Brun, 0705.2342](https://arxiv.org/html/0705.2342) already studies
continuous error correction for Hamiltonian bath couplings and Zeno scaling,
including its time-scale qualifications. [Kessler, 1205.5440](https://arxiv.org/html/1205.5440)
provides arbitrary-order dissipative Schrieffer--Wolff theory. Those methods
are ordinary predecessors to a fast-dephasing expansion, not a proof of
dimension-uniform long-time memory for this fixed controller.

General active fault-tolerance theorems already protect against coherent and
nonprobabilistic local noise when their gates, measurements, resets, classical
processing and timing are granted. The broad existence of a controlled
coherent-noise memory is therefore not an unmet consequence. The unsolved
scope here is the specified fixed nominal local recovery architecture with
unknown charged Hamiltonian and unbalanced dissipative errors.
