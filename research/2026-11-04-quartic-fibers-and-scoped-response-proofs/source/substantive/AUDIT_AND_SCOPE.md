# Audit and scope of the substantive proof update

4 November 2026. Private research archive. No external publication or sharing.

The strongest complete work in this update is an explicit information obstruction
for recovering growing-time response from specified scalar Euclidean oracles.
The two positive spectral adversaries have response transitions in a fixed band,
fixed temporal smearing, and a response separation bounded below independently of
the time index. At zero temperature the raw Gaussian query lower bound grows
exponentially with the target time. The fixed-temperature extension gives a
superexponential lower bound with fixed temperature and bounded Hamiltonian norm.
The displayed proofs supply these claims; floating-point examples are diagnostics.

This is not a lower bound for algorithms given the explicit Hamiltonian and
observable, for all QMC observation laws, or for one fixed system at every time.
The finite-temperature Hamiltonian includes an interaction acting on all n
qubits. Its structural description makes the fixture easy to solve. No local
many-body hardness, quantum computational advantage, generic response capability,
formal verification, external review or historical novelty is established.

The independent constructor selected the initial Euclidean-response question and
built the zero-temperature adversary, statistical proofs and finite optimal
positivity comparator. Root separately derived the normalized fixed-temperature
construction. The constructor audited it, tightened the uniform difference bound
by a factor of two, and supplied a degeneracy realization. Root supplied its
compact n-qubit expression; the constructor checked it, including n=1. These are
collaborative developments, not independent discoveries of every final step.

Root read both complete proof documents and checked the binomial moment identity,
normalization, maximum Laplace difference, adaptive Gaussian chain-rule/Pinsker
constants, Bernoulli coupling, smoothing qualification, known-moment null space,

odd indices n=3,11,41, Omega=2.3, sigma=0.7 and beta=0.2,1,10. It passed 55 exact
rational moment checks, 45 thermal ordinate/stable-difference checks and nine
thermal response-gap checks. The constructor's larger separate receipts are
summarized in the proof documents. These finite checks do not replace the proofs.

The strongest same-data benchmark implemented was the optimal finite positive-
measure LP with known atom locations and the declared exact moment constraints.
The adversarial pair attains the remaining interval, so a different valid
inference algorithm cannot erase the ambiguity under the same information. The

Root independently selected fusion alpha-to-fuel heat conversion as the physical
opening after setting aside microscopic thermal memory. The fusion document

scope than that exact theorem. The independent constructor did not reproduce
the full kinetic solver or acquire its physical assumptions.

At a chosen, uncalibrated conserving relaxation rate, a high instantaneous heat
fraction survives only a narrow parallel source bandwidth in the tested branch.
Resonant retuning does not remove the measured boundary. A Dirichlet-end column
would require a length about 3.7 km at the illustrative 100 eV alpha parallel
temperature. Exactly perpendicular modes under other boundary conditions are an
explicit escape. No nonlinear saturation, absolute extraction power, realistic
fusion distribution, or equal-resource reactor advantage has been acquired.
