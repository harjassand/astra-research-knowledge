ASTRA ASSEMBLY INVESTIGATION — SECOND-STAGE CONSTRUCTION
2026-11-01

DECISION

The consequential opening survives, but the preferred mechanism has changed.
Temporary guides are no longer the main route. I constructed a completion-
dependent physical recovery operation, exposed and removed an avoidable
recycling bypass, then allowed a stronger conventional recovery process to
take over most of that service. Its possible integration advantage is too
narrow and unmeasured to carry the ambition.

The principal target is now manufacture of a continuous, chemically specified
23S RNA without taking that RNA from an existing ribosome, followed by
repeatable full translation. The independent covalent construction supplied
an exact material route. I reconciled it with the functional recovery work,
complete cost accounting and a matched preparation experiment that separates
chemical specification, material damage and inherited folding history.

There is no new wet-lab result, demonstrated resource advantage, closed
factory or proven general assembly principle here. The return consists of
specific molecular constructions, actual structural/chemical source analysis,
ordinary proofs, executable evidence, and failures that changed the choice.

WHY THIS CAPABILITY MATTERS

A manufactured ribosome is a potentially general machine for making other
protein machines. Existing purified-component constructions still leave
important dependencies on native RNA, undefined extracts, supplied factors,
modified precursors or living-system production. Removing the native-RNA
dependence would enable deliberate chemical construction of the machine's
central scaffold, including chemical states that a DNA sequence alone does
not specify. That is a substantial molecular-manufacturing capability even
before autonomous reproduction is possible.

The target is a material object with known chemical composition and measured
working service. Recovering its rRNA genotype through reverse transcription
and PCR is a different service; those operations do not reproduce its full
modification pattern or assembled state. Normal recycling can re-pair
subunits, so the retained identity is a23S/50S lineage, not a permanent70S pair.

WHAT THE STRONGEST EXISTING CONSTRUCTIONS CHANGED

Aoyama2022 already achieved complete sfGFP synthesis using all recombinant
ribosomal proteins with native large-subunit RNAs and a transcribed small-
subunit RNA. It is therefore unjustified to sell another guide as the missing
universal assembly helper. The remaining native23S dependence is more precise
and consequential. Its full protein output does not establish universal
fidelity, active-particle yield or working lifetime. [1]

The2025 autonomous-biogenesis experiment is an even stronger scope warning:
new subunits can be made from genes inside an optimized extract with native
seed ribosomes. This is genuine progress. It does not give a chemically
specified, extract-independent construction, and the authors explicitly
identify reproduction-yield limitations. [2]

Natural remodeling machinery also contains much of the initially desired
selectivity. RsmB can promote a16S-domain state that capturesS7 and can leave
through catalysis-associated release. CsdA can repeatedly unfold vulnerable
RNA while protein-bound and mature structures become more resistant. These
are experimentally real local operations. They do not establish complete
newly manufactured ribosomes, but they prevent claiming that graded reset
or a temporary chaperone is itself our invention. [3,4]

THE STRUCTURAL AND FUNCTIONAL FAILURES WERE MATERIAL

The mature contact graph did not describe the ASO-trapped intermediate.
Deposited9DYG coordinates and the associated primary study show a relocated
H98 making a protein anchor withuL13. Matched-backbone distances differ from
the mature4YBB geometry. The trap structure is5.27Angstrom and theuL13 model
contains backbone atoms only; numerical distances are not energetic
measurements. The source also suggests that an H98/uL13 contact can have a
productive role before replacement byH97. Nonnative contact is not sufficient
to identify an inhibitory contact. [5]

TheC2498 region has no deposited atoms in that intermediate. That is missing
structural information, not evidence that an oligo can access it. The exact
RA15/RA20/RB0811 ASO sequences could not be recovered from the blocked XLSX
supplement and remain unknown in this record. There is no simulated binding
energy masquerading as postbinding accessibility.

Published photofragmentable RNA bandages can release inhibition in a different
system, but some strongly binding versions fail to restore expression even
after photolysis. This does not earn clearance from a protein-containing
assembly intermediate. The dual-substrate C/D-sRNP idea also failed as a
ready-made solution: real stimulated product release exists on short
substrates, but retargeting, salt/temperature compatibility and a genuine
assembly-readiness signal are unearned. Both routes are preserved. [6,7]

Function has a similarly sharp boundary. A chemically gapped Thermus23S
construct could catalyze a simple peptide-transfer reaction but failed an
RF1-dependent termination reaction until covalent continuity was restored.
This was not full-protein translation; it cannot be used as a demonstrated
false positive of a full-reporter gate. It is strong evidence against treating
one peptide bond as proof of a reusable manufacturing machine. [8]

THE EXACT CHEMICAL CONSTRUCTION

The reference is ENAV00331.1,2904nt, cross-checked against4YBBentity22 after
mapping the deposited modified base to its parent letter. A25-site chemical
target was assembled from structure annotation and primary modification
maps. Native occupancies, especiallyho5C2501, must not be equated to uniform
complete modification. The target is explicit and can be varied.

Initial construction:
  9 chemically synthesized patches:386nt total;
  10 IVT spacers:2518nt total;
  19 fragments;18 native backbone joins;
  9 ternary ligation operations in3 purified tiers;
  23 chemical marks plus2 written byRlmKL/SAM.

Exact sequences, all junctions, long DNA splints and processing requirements
are generated by construct_patch_route.py. The long-splint ligation method
uses actual public machinery demonstrated on structured mRNAs; transfer to
the proposed full rRNA is a hypothesis. Splints are removed before protein
assembly. This avoids making bound-guide release a hidden prerequisite for
manufacturing the RNA. It does not establish subsequent productive folding. [9]

RlmKL is retained rather than pretending that an internalm7G synthesis route
was earned. It has actual naked-RNA substrates, including full-length RNA
from a deletion strain and transcribed domainV. Acceptance and modification
occupancy on this manufactured substrate still require measurement. [10]

The hardest mixed80mer was refined during construction. A proposed split
placesD2449 andPsi2457 in a52nt piece, and the denseCm2498/ho5C2501/m2A2503/
Psi2504 cluster in a28nt piece:

  Positions2489-2516, canonical:
  UGUUUGGCACCUCGAUGUCGGCUCAUCA
  Chemical target:
  UGUUUGGCA[Cm]CU[ho5C]G[m2A][Psi]GUCGGCUCAUCA

This is a chemically specific object to construct, not just a named region.
It localizes incompatible-processing risk and moves the unusual mixed cluster
within the length of publishedho5C-containing oligos. It does not demonstrate
compatibility of all four modifications. Splitting into single-modification-
class pieces is not a free escape: m2A2503 must then occupy at most2nt because
ho5C2501 andPsi2504 flank it. Efficient joining of that dinucleotide is not
earned. Actual T4Rnl1/rSAP/T4Rnl2 precedents cover several modified monomers,
but not the requiredho5C/m2A terminal substrates. [11,12]

The refined route has20fragments,19joins and10ligation operations, still
3tiers. The other76/78nt patches retain their own synthesis requirements.
The new junction's local flank containsCm, so an unmodified nearest-neighbor
duplex estimate cannot be silently reused for it.

ROOT CONSTRUCTION: CATALYTIC COMPLETION AS A POSSIBLE PHYSICAL EXIT

The constructed route challenges a candidate with a full reporter followed
by a distal affinity handle, an exit-tunnel spacer andUAG. A defined translation
system omits release/rescue factors, leaving a peptidyl-tRNA-linked complex.
Capture the product, wash, then applyRF1 alone and collect physically released
ribosomes. Recycle only after that collection is separated from the support.

Two real objections changed the design.
First, simultaneousRF3/RRF/EF-G can promote intact peptidyl-tRNA drop-off,
releasing a candidate without the desired ester hydrolysis. The original
simultaneous-recycling design was rejected. Intrinsic drop-off, support
leakage and aberrant hydrolysis remain even after separating recycling. [13]
Second, one successful chain can capture passengers on a shared polysome
message. The input must be a physically prepared single-initiation cohort,
not bulk translation with a nominal1:1 ratio. Published purified initiation
complexes followed by elongation-only mixtures make a complete protein and
provide a concrete starting operation. Recovery and residual passenger bounds
for this exact reporter remain unmeasured. [14]

There is strong prior art for every nearby service. RISE selects newly
assembled ribosomes through their own translated product, but its published
recovery destroys complexes and amplifies rRNA genotype. Intact competitive
elution and puromycin release already supply physical-recovery competitors.
Full-protein RF-triggered release assays also exist. The proposed integration
must beat competitive recovery followed by the same termination challenge
and affinity depletion, not just compare against destructive genetic recovery.
No such advantage has been measured. [15,16]

THEOREMS THAT CHANGED THE DECISION

Letp be the incoming fraction meeting a declared future service, a its
whole-process acceptance probability, andb bad-particle acceptance. Then

  accepted purity =pa/[pa+(1-p)b].

For99% purity from1% useful input, b/a must be at most1.02030405e-4.
This is a specification, not a prediction. At a=.5, proving a sufficiently
smallb from zero false events needs58721 independent negative challenges for
an exact one-sided95% binomial bound. Unrepresented defects are not covered.
Capture-conditioned release CDFs are not whole-process acceptance rates.

Hydrolysis and physical escape are distinct steps. In a conditional two-step
exponential calculation, genuine collection begins quadratically in time
while one-step leakage begins linearly; the shortest pulse need not be the
cleanest. Actual distributions should determine collection windows. One
audit corrected a real proof error: upper CDF bounds cannot simply be
differenced to bound false mass in an interval. Use direct interval bounds
or an upper-at-end minus lower-at-start bound.

Repeated tests do not give independent error suppression. Persistent
cheaters remain correlated. Repeated recovery also consumes the same physical
object: hypothetical95% per-round survival leaves59.87% after10rounds;80%
leaves10.74%. These are algebraic witnesses, not ribosome measurements.

Most important, purification alone cannot increase total correct output if
bad particles do not inhibit good ones, no repair/assembly occurs, and
selection does not improve survivors. At a common deadline, the retained
subset cannot outperform direct use of the original useful subset. Testing
and transport only subtract capacity. Increased purity is a separate service.

The compiled mathematics was independently checked, including300 random
unitless competing-exit cases against a matrix exponential. Maximum
disagreement was9.58e-15. This validates the formulas, not the proposed
physical parameters. No favorable physical rate was supplied.
The original random seed and sampler were not saved; the recorded audit
result remains, while its exact random sample is not reproducible. The
deterministic calculations and saved matrix-generator method are executable.

RESET DID NOT RESCUE AN UNEARNED YIELD CLAIM

EngA/ObgE can restore reporter production after deliberate EDTA disruption
of native ribosomes. That makes resetting a real operation to investigate.
It does not show conversion of our manufacturing rejects, repeated recovery,
chemistry retention or absolute active-particle yield. A source amount
inconsistency and separation of its assembly estimate from reset data are
retained in RESET_COMPETITOR.txt. [17]

The strongest rival is global factors-only maturation, which may preserve
successful particles while repairing unsuccessful ones. If it supplies at
least as much remaining correct-service capacity as both the exported and
reset branches for every relevant material state, it dominates any mixture
of those branches before selection costs. Selective export needs a real
repairable population requiring a treatment incompatible with protecting
successes, or a separately scarce purity condition, to justify itself.

A homogeneous retry calculation was retained only conditionally. Bad
chemistry that cannot be corrected is not a new independent assembly attempt.
The state-resolved retry kernel, loss, false exits and resource costs must
be measured. Neither annealing nor EDTA writes missing modifications.

THE SCIENTIFIC CONCENTRATION

The decisive next physical construction is the dense28nt chemical patch and
the matched native cut/reseal route, with complete chemical checks and
full-service measurements. It tests a specific material bottleneck before
building a complicated harvesting loop. The four principal preparation arms
are nativeRNA, nativeRNA through the splint/removal history, nativeRNA cut
and resealed at the planned seams, and completely manufacturedRNA.

These controls do not magically prove all microscopic histories equal.
They make otherwise confounded outcomes informative. If the manufactured
RNA succeeds, the native-scaffold dependence is removed for the measured
specification. If native cut/reseal fails, seam/preparation failure must be
resolved first. If cut/reseal succeeds and manufacturedRNA fails, chemistry,
occupancy and accessible folding remain explicit alternatives.

For recovery, use the strongest conventional construction until the integrated
RF exit earns an advantage. For resetting, split actual rejects among extra
time, factors-only maturation, EDTA/restoration and conventional reassembly.
Compare incremental correct output, originalRNA recovery and lifetime at a
common deadline. False-negative useful particles in the reject pool prevent
activity-after-reset from being a standalone repair proof.
