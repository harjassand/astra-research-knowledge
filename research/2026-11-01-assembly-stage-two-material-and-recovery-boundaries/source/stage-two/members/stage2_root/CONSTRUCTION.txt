CHEMICAL ASSEMBLY AND COMPLETION-DEPENDENT RECOVERY
Research record, 2026-11-01

STATUS
This is a source-constrained molecular construction and an experimental
decision, not a new wet-lab result. No assembly yield, kinetic discrimination,
fidelity or lifetime is inferred from a contact graph or assigned to the new
construct. Existing catalytic selection, ribosome display, covalent RNA
ligation and natural remodeling are credited as existing machinery.

THE SERVICE
Manufacture a continuous, chemically specified 23S RNA without extracting
that RNA from a preexisting ribosome; assemble it with explicit structural
proteins; recover the same manufactured 50S/23S lineage after a complete
translation challenge; and obtain repeated, accurate protein output over a
declared working interval. Small subunits, tRNAs, translation factors, energy
regeneration and processing enzymes remain explicit supplied components.

The long-term consequence would be a chemically programmable route to
large RNA-protein machines, with the ribosome as an unusually consequential
first target. It would remove a native-RNA/extract dependence in the productive
path. It would not by itself produce a self-replicating factory, manufacture
modified monomers, or eliminate biological production of supplied enzymes.

The independent chemical construction is now the main manufacturing lead.
The root completion-exit construction is a candidate qualification/harvesting
operation within it. The informed conventional competitor already provides
most of the same recovery service. I do not make uniqueness or total-cost
advantage claims for the proposed RF exit.

1. MAKE THE RNA BEFORE ASKING A PROTEIN-CONTAINING INTERMEDIATE TO RELEASE A GUIDE

The exact reference, modification map, patches, spacers, seams and splints
are in stage2_alternative/data. Native 3'-5' continuity is retained. Nine
modified patches plus ten IVT spacers cover all2904nt. Nine ternary ligation
operations seal18seams. Splints are removed BEFORE protein assembly. RlmKL
and SAM supply two methylations that are not assumed chemically accessible
in the same way as the other23 marks. The specified pattern is a25-site
target, not a claim of identical occupancy in every native preparation.

Actual prior operations: exact-end RNase-H processing; T4RNA-ligase2 on long
DNA splints; DNA removal; RlmKL on naked native-derived modified23S and IVT
domainV; conventional magnesium/temperature assembly with individually
purified recombinant structural proteins. Transfers to the complete new
substrate remain transfers. DALI's mRNA yields are NOT rRNA yield estimates.

The modified76-80nt patches are an essential chemical gap. Literature access
to individual modified monomers or shorter RNAs does not establish mixed
protecting-group compatibility, deprotection survival or preparative purity.
Splitting the dense2501-2504 cluster produces very short pieces, which cannot
be granted efficient hybridization/ligation. The independent alternative
keeps that failed workaround and the actual enzymatic-extension precedents.

The preferred refinement now splits positions2437-2516 into DP52
(2437-2488, D/Psi) and DENSE28 (2489-2516, Cm/ho5C/m2A/Psi). This permits
separate synthesis/deprotection of D from the ho5C-containing block and
localizes the mixed-chemistry experiment. DENSE28 remains unmade. The complete
route becomes20fragments,19joins,9ternary and1binary operation in3tiers.
Use its separate per-leaf ledger in refined_cluster_route.json; do not apply
the original287nt-tail formula after changing the tree. The other76/78nt
patches still require validation. CHEMISTRY_DECOMPOSITION.md preserves the
exact short-fragment obstruction and the newly retrieved monomer-extension
evidence. No yield gain is inferred from the regrouped tree.

This order avoids having to invade and then vacate a protein-containing
intermediate merely to make the chemical scaffold. It does not solve its
subsequent folding. DNA/RNA duplex energies in the compiler are local
diagnostics, not proof of rRNA accessibility or productive folding.

2. SEPARATE CHEMICAL SPECIFICATION FROM INHERITED ASSEMBLY HISTORY

The central root question is sharpened by an important ambiguity in the
best existing reconstitutions: native-derived RNA carries both covalent
chemistry and preparation/folding history. A successful native-RNA
reconstitution does not prove that a newly manufactured chemical copy will
follow the same accessible path at finite time and cost.

Use a matched material-transplant construction:

A. Native-derived continuous23S, conventional extraction and assembly.
B. The same native RNA exposed to the long-splint preparation/removal and
   purification history, without intended backbone cleavage.
C. Native-derived RNA cut at the proposed manufacturing seams, purified and
   resealed through the same ligation tree. Verify all native junctions.
D. The complete chemical-patch/IVT-spacer RNA, through the same tree.
E. A deliberately hypo-modified manufactured control, with matched seams
   and processing, to localize a chemistry dependence rather than assert it.

Use the same protein preparations, final RNA amounts, translation components
and challenge messages. Retain absolute input accounting as a separate
manufacturing comparison; equal final RNA is the mechanistic comparison.

Interpretation must remain conditional:
- A succeeds, B fails: the added processing or altered folding state matters.
  This does not distinguish physical damage from a history-dependent trap.
- B succeeds, C fails: cutting, end preparation, ligation or fragment history
  introduces a problem; a full-length band is not proof of a correct product.
- C succeeds, D fails: the synthetic chemical specification, its occupancy,
  chemical errors, or its accessible folding ensemble remains inadequate.
  This alone does not prove an irreducible biological helper is required.
- D succeeds with repeated complete service: native23S sourcing is removed
  for that measured specification and service. Cost, scope and lifetime
  still need comparison; an improved percentage is not enough.

Long DNA/RNA hybrid preparation is an operational attempt to replace native
folding history, not an assertion that every microscopic memory is erased.
Verify hybrid formation and material chemistry; do not identify missing
cryo-EM density with accessible RNA. A factor-addition arm may expose the
remaining route, but its production, fuel and removal belong in the service.

This matched construction makes failure informative before an elaborate
renewal system is built. Its purpose is to distinguish missing chemistry,
processing damage and inaccessible folding, not merely score reporter output.

3. PREPARE A SINGLE-ROUND FUNCTIONAL CHALLENGE

Proposed reporter architecture:
  initiation region -- complete reporter domain -- distal3xFLAG handle --
  exit-tunnel spacer -- terminalAla -- UAG.

The distal tag requires near-completion before affinity capture. The spacer
must put the complete handle outside the exit tunnel while the ester-linked
terminal residue remains in the P site. Its exact sequence/length must be
chosen and tested for this construct; an assumed universally safe spacer is
not supplied. In particular avoid known arrest motifs and do not assume a
low-complexity linker is neutral. Full product identity and reporter activity
are separate measurements. Internal initiation, deletion and mistranslation
can preserve a tag; the tag alone does not prove fidelity.

Actual starting operation: purified70S initiation complexes followed by an
elongation-only mixture. O'Connor et al2020 purified initiation complexes
through1.1M sucrose; supplying extra subunits/IFs/initiator created their
polysome comparator. Buhr et al2016 independently obtained full-protein
single-round nascent chains. These are primary preparation precedents, not
published validation of this reporter/stop/affinity combination.

Omit class-I release factors, RF3, RRF and rescue systems during the stall.
Use a defined preparation, not an unspecified extract with hidden rescue
activities. Quantify residual free subunits and initiation components. A
sucrose cushion does not itself separate empty70S from initiation complexes.
Preparative monosome separation and stoichiometry measurements may still be
required. Their losses and time are part of whole-process acceptance.

The exact E.coli full-reporter UAG stall remains to be validated. Full-protein
RF-omission/readdition is established in Termi-Luc/yeast systems, which helps
locate the transfer gap but does not supply bacterial gate kinetics.

4. CAPTURE AND USE COGNATE CHEMISTRY AS A POSSIBLE EXIT

Capture the nascent3xFLAG product under conditions that preserve the RNP.
The proposed load-bearing path is
  support / affinity handle / nascent peptide / ester / tRNA / ribosome.
The slash denotes a connection, not a claim that every connection is covalent.
Wash and measure support leakage and nonspecific recovery. Existing RISE
establishes functional nascent-product capture but has different reporter,
stall, wash and recovery details; its ratios cannot be transplanted.

Add cognateRF1 alone for the UAG pulse. Collect time-resolved eluate. Only
after the eluate is physically separated from the support add the required
termination/recycling components. The original simultaneous release/recycling
design is rejected: RF3/RRF/EF-G can promote nonhydrolytic peptidyl-tRNA
drop-off. RF1 is charged in sufficient stoichiometric supply for the pulse;
efficient on-support RF cycling is not assumed.

Intrinsic peptidyl-tRNA dissociation, affinity dissociation and aberrant
hydrolysis remain. Hydrolysis and physical escape must both be measured.
Use a chemistry-sensitive peptide-versus-peptidyl-tRNA assay under conditions
that do not hydrolyze the ester during measurement; a fluorescence change
alone need not identify the bond that broke. Assay design and recovery of
the relevant species are part of validation, not supplied perfect observers.

Quantify RF1 active concentration and GGQ methylation. Published short-peptide
rates depend strongly on terminal residue and RF methylation; they are not
full-reporter exit rates. No-RF, noncognateRF2/UAG, altered-stop and defective
RNA controls expose different leakage classes. A noncognate pre-pulse might
remove indiscriminate releasers, but is an optional tested refinement, not
assumed independent error suppression.

Collection is a probabilistic enrichment, not an exact catalytic certificate.
PROOFS.txt identifies bypasses and the stringent false-acceptance requirement
when useful particles are initially rare. A conventional affinity-recovery
route with a later RF-only pulse and affinity depletion can construct the
same termination discrimination; it has additional handling costs. Compare
it instead of claiming that only our support arrangement can supply it.

5. RECYCLE, RECHALLENGE AND MEASURE USEFUL LIFE

Recycle in a separate vessel, remove spent reporter/message/competitors and
identify the retained manufactured RNA. Normal recycling does not preserve
a fixed70S pair. The relevant lineage is each23S/50S scaffold; imported30S
helpers must be explicit in any isolated-large-subunit test.

Challenge the recovered pool with a different full message, a different
reporter measurement and a different termination pair, such as UGA/RF2.
Measure complete product amount and identity, erroneous products, RNA amount,
activity over time and further useful rounds. A finite panel establishes only
that panel and observed horizon. It is not universal translational fidelity.

An RNA sequence barcode or affinity tag can change assembly/lifetime. Prefer
chemical lineage measurements on the chosen native sequence where feasible,
and characterize any label. Residual native ribosomes in supplied components
must be measured and bounded; reporter signal alone can come from them.
Small manufactured amounts are particularly vulnerable to this confound.

6. KEEP ALL THREE PHYSICAL-RECOVERY CONSTRUCTIONS

Split a common captured batch among:
(i) competitive affinity elution, then termination/recycling;
(ii) puromycin release, cleanup and recycling;
(iii) cognateRF-only support exit, then physically separate recycling.

Use bacterial-compatible, independently qualified conditions. A mammalian
NS-IP protocol demonstrates relevant elution chemistry but is not a validated
recipe for active bacterial recovery: its LiCl/EDTA conditions differ and its
puromycin recipe contains an arithmetic inconsistency. Do not copy its table
concentration. Intact ribosome-display complex recovery by competition is
older prior art; activity after our complete process must still be measured.

Compare total correctly produced protein per original manufactured scaffold,
per time and per specified resource budget, while meeting the same error and
chemical-state constraints. The RF route is useful only if its extra
discrimination or integrated handling pays for its losses. No advantage has
yet been demonstrated.

7. RESET IS A CONDITIONAL MANUFACTURING BRANCH, NOT THE CLAIMED ADVANCE

EngA/ObgE can restore activity to deliberately EDTA-disrupted native
ribosomes. That is a real reset lead. It is not evidence for repeatedly
recovering our rejected synthetic assemblies. Incorrect modifications,
backbone defects and irreversibly damaged RNA do not become correct by
waiting, heating or adding a remodeling protein.

Split ACTUAL rejects among added time, factors-only maturation, EDTA plus
restoration/factors, and conventional reconstitution. Compare the whole
original batch under factors-only maturation as well. Protecting successes
has a yield rationale only if resetting needed for other particles damages
them enough to repay selection and transport. Global repair that preserves
good particles can dominate the selector. The state-resolved proof is in
stage2_existing/RESET_COMPETITOR.txt.

Rejects contain false-negative good particles. Activity after treatment is
not, by itself, proof that failed material was repaired. Compare incremental
correct-service capacity and physical recovery at a common deadline.

The Sato2026 reset Methods also have a fivefold amount inconsistency:
0.5uL x5uM is2.5pmol, whereas the text states0.5pmol. Preserve it and resolve
before adopting a quantitative protocol. Its separate approximately60%
assembly-activity estimate is not the reset recovery fraction.

8. DECISION

No new universal assembly gate, manufacture yield improvement or closed
factory is claimed. The temporary-guide programme is not the default route.
The consequential surviving construction is chemically specified continuous
RNA manufacture plus full-service recovery, with conventional assembly,
affinity recovery and remodeling as strong ingredients and rivals.

Concentrate next physical construction on the mixed-modification chemistry
and the matched native cut/reseal control. Use established recovery to avoid
making an unproved RF integration prerequisite. Test the RF branch only
against the equally informed conventional version. If chemically manufactured
RNA works but the gate loses, retain the chemical advance. If the native
cut/reseal control fails, fix material preparation before claiming a deep
folding limitation. This ordering preserves the ambition while making failure
identify the next missing operation.
