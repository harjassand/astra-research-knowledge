USEFUL FAILURES AND PRECISE RETAINED BOUNDARIES
2026-11-01. Companion to CONSTRUCTION.txt and the first-stage archive.

H98 RELEASE BY MATURE-CONTACT REASONING
Rejected as an earned mechanism. The trapped9DYG state acquires a protein
anchor absent from the mature-contact simplification. Its missing sidechains
and missingC2498 domain do not provide energies or accessibility. H98/uL13
can also participate productively in the source's proposed assembly route.
Prehybridization rescue does not establish postbinding release in a protein-
containing intermediate. ASO sequences remain unavailable from the blocked
supplement; no invented sequence is present in the construction.

H98 TAGGING AS A HARMLESS HANDLE
The2023 H98-MS2 study, DOI10.1261/rna.079690.123, shows that ordinary function
can coexist with impaired resistance to a60C challenge. An addedA that
restores the native quartet changes the thermal phenotype. This is evidence
to measure lifetime after engineering; it is not a numerical lifetime model
at normal translation temperatures. Geometry and transient activity alone
do not make a modification neutral.

PHOTOLYTIC RNA BANDAGES
Richards2008, DOI10.1016/j.bmcl.2008.09.093, demonstrates one two-arm2'OMe
construction with inhibited expression and substantial restoration after
photolysis. Other strongly binding versions fail to restore expression.
This earns a real cleavage/release lead but not vacating a ribosomal
protein-containing intermediate, or a reusable guide without chemical
replacement. UV burden, residual arms, damage and release kinetics matter.

RSMB AS A NOVEL UNIVERSAL HANDOFF
Not claimed. Wei/Woodson2026, DOI10.1126/sciadv.aef7741, already supplies
specific16S3'-domain remodeling, changedS7/S19 interactions and catalysis-
associated release. The exact isolated-domain experiment is narrower than
active30S manufacture. StableS7 capture requires prompt cooperative protein
access; simply removingRsmB and waiting is not equivalent. The static
affinity calculation uses mutant/substrate/product mimics and cannot be
substituted for WT driven kinetics. See RSMB_OPERATION.json and the
strongest-native-construction record.

S7 SURFACE CAPTURE
Duss2019, DOI10.1016/j.cell.2019.10.035, earns native cooperativeS7 capture
and useful activity of the reported labeling construct. It does not earn an
arbitrary surface attachment, surface-compatible reconstitution, or a free
time window before competence decays. Reducing conditions also conflict with
a simple disulfide attachment. Surface capture through an incorporated
ribosomal protein already exists in Levy2020, DOI10.1126/sciadv.aaz6020.
Its newly assemblingSSU evidence is not complete new-SSU translation.

U7 SUPPORT PHOTORELEASE
The U7 RNP reconstitution/purification procedure (PMC8724919) supplies a real
photocleavable-biotin support release with downstream processing activity.
Its long handling, UV and EDTA conditions are not automatically compatible
with the short-livedS7 competent population. Thus generic support release
is existing machinery, while the specific time/chemistry transfer remains.

C/D S-RNP TRIGGERED PRODUCT RELEASE
Graziadei2016 (PMC4836650) demonstratesD'-substrate stimulated release of a
slowD-site product in a realL7Ae/Nop5/fibrillarin/guide/SAM complex. That is
substantive machinery. Our proposed retarget to large rRNA was not earned:
the source uses short substrates and different temperature/salt conditions.
Adding an external trigger does not mean the RNP senses productive assembly.
The source's Results/Methods substrate/enzyme concentration descriptions
also differ. Later conformational work shows that sequence/overhang context
matters, so a terminal-base affinity rule does not compile the full mechanism.
Native site-specific writers remain a stronger comparator where available.

CSD A SELECTIVE RESET
Sun/Woodson2025, DOI10.1093/nar/gkaf104, already demonstrates ATP-driven
unfolding of unstable16S-domain structures with protection enhanced by
ribosomal proteins. Excess helicase can destroy the useful capture regime.
The source does not establish whole-subunit synthesis or an ideal universal
reset. Our proposed proof of selective reset cannot count this existing
physical principle as new, or assume its selectivity transfers to23S.

CIRCULAR PERMUTATION AND RNA TOPOLOGY
Dong2024, DOI10.1093/nar/gkae636, changes transcription start/order but retains
much of the assembly docking hierarchy; reporter delays do not isolate every
assembly/function cause. It is not a freely programmable assembly-order
compiler. The2026 naturally circular23S report, DOI10.1186/s13059-025-03903-0,
suggests a topology lead, but did not establish whether circularization
precedes assembly in the inspected evidence. No new topological mechanism
or knot-based advantage is claimed here.

MINIMAL PTC PATCH AS FULL FACTORY SPECIFICATION
Rejected. The old fragment-reaction localization is not a demonstrated
minimal chemistry set for processive translation, fidelity, termination,
recycling and life. Bao2024, DOI10.1080/15476286.2024.2368305, directly
exposes the need to separate modification effects, fragmentation and
remaining chemistry. The2026 Larsson study, DOI10.1093/nar/gkag800, further
supports effects of hypo-modification on alternativePTC structures and
thermal stability; its assays do not supply our full synthetic-rRNA service.
The gappedThermus termination failure is preserved separately with exact
substrate and species in GAPPED_TERMINATION_BOUNDARY.json.

CHEMICAL COMPATIBILITY BY SUBDIVISION
Splitting the80nt patch into six single-modification-class blocks creates
two2nt pieces. In particular the flanking modifications bound the maximum
length of the m2A2503 block. Local sequence enumeration confirms that this is
not a boundary-choice accident. Modified-monomer extension has real primary
precedents, but not all required terminal substrates. The52+28 route retains
a mixed-chemistry experiment and adds a join. Both accurate trees and costs
are saved; no favorable q is installed as a predicted physical yield.

COMPLETION-DEPENDENT EXIT AS AN EXACT CERTIFICATE
Rejected. Recycling-promoted nonhydrolytic drop-off, intrinsic drop-off,
affinity leakage, abnormal hydrolysis and passenger ribosomes are distinct
paths. RF-only collection before recycling removes one reagent-enabled path.
Single-initiation cohorts earn a preparation operation, not zero error.
Prior functional selection, full-protein release assays and intact affinity
recovery constrain novelty. The conventional competitor can reconstruct the
same termination discrimination with a second separation.

PURIFICATION OR GEOMETRIC RETRIES AS NEW CAPACITY
Rejected under the stated premises. Selection alone removes machines and
time. Repeated test errors can be correlated. Reset does not correct wrong
chemistry by assumption. A successful reset of deliberately disrupted native
particles is not conversion of manufacturing rejects. Factors-only global
maturation is a serious comparator that may eliminate the rationale for
physical selection. All three findings changed the final priority.
