STRONGEST EXISTING CONSTRUCTION AND ITS BOUNDARY
2026-11-01; independently constructed competitor for the Astra assembly investigation.
No laboratory work was performed. Results below distinguish demonstrated operations,
compositions not yet tested together, and exact mathematical accounting.

1. DECISION

Use the Aoyama 2022 physical assembly route as the conventional comparator whenever
temperature and magnesium cycling are allowed. It already avoids the need for an
unspecified assembly proteome: all 33 large-subunit proteins are individually
recombinant. With native 5S/23S rRNA, it makes 50S that supports sfGFP synthesis;
integration with transcript-based, 21-protein R-iSAT makes full-length sfGFP with
all 54 ribosomal proteins recombinant. This is not a closed molecular factory:
native large-subunit rRNA, native tRNA preparations and supplied translation
machinery remain inputs. [S1,S2]

For the complete service requested here, neither native protein removal nor a
temporary assembly gate alone is the unresolved dependence. The useful frontier
is complete active assembly from renewable large-subunit RNA, with measured
service fidelity and useful lifetime. A gate earns importance if it crosses that
frontier, or creates a reusable operation unavailable to the strong local
chaperones below.

2. CONCRETE CONVENTIONAL PROCESS C0

Source protocol rather than an invented rate schedule [S1,S2]:

INPUTS AND PREPARATION
- Native 5S/23S RNA: 240 pmol equivalents in a 250-uL assembly batch.
- 33 purified recombinant LSU proteins: 270 pmol nominal equivalents; 32 proteins
  equimolar, bL12 fourfold. Protein pool first uses 6 M urea, then three 45-minute
  dialysis exchanges. bL20 initially requires denaturant storage; protein
  purification and buffer exchange are real operations, not ideal free helpers.
- 30S branch: 16S DNA template and 21 recombinant proteins, assembled in PURE.
- Supplied PURE translation/transcription components, amino acids, tRNAs, energy
  and nucleotide feed, and reporter template.

OPERATIONS
- LSU assembly: 44 C / 4 mM Mg, 20 min, then 50 C / 20 mM Mg, 90 min, in
  400 mM ammonium chloride. Concentrate and exchange to translation-compatible
  ribosome buffer before using it as a translation component.
- R-iSAT coupling: 200 nM reconstituted 50S, 500 nM of each SSU protein,
  20 nM 16S DNA and 5 nM sfGFP DNA; observe at 37 C.
- Synthesis over a 12-hour observation and full-length gel band establish a
  functioning translation route. They do not establish a 12-hour ribosome
  lifetime, a count of active particles or a proteome-wide error rate.

The ordinary method uses 110 minutes of assembly incubation plus substantial
preparation. Those costs cannot be silently charged only to a novel route.
Conversely, thermal and salt steps are allowed physical operations; a new route
cannot claim that unknown chaperones are essential by excluding this comparator.

Recovery, active-particle yield and total per-ribosome productivity are not
numerically identified here. Protein synthesis from 33P is lower than TP50,
especially for full sfGFP compared with the short HiBiT peptide. Supplementing
the measured protein methylations on uL3/uL11 did not close that difference. [S1]

3. WHAT STRONGER EXISTING CONSTRUCTIONS ADD

C1: NEAR-PHYSIOLOGICAL ASSEMBLY
EngA + ObgE allow translation-compatible assembly and restoration after an EDTA
disruption, using native rRNA and native-derived TP70. This supplies a valuable
remodeling operation, but TP70 contains other factors; the two GTPases were not
proved necessary and sufficient. The reported fluorescence-derived assembly
estimate is conditional on translating activity assumptions. It does not close
the transcript-based 23S problem. [S3]

C2: NASCENT COMPONENTS FROM GENES
Kosaka 2025 makes active nascent SSU and LSU from rRNA and all ribosomal-protein
genes in S150 extract, with orthogonal/antibiotic reporter discrimination. It
earns coupled transcription, translation and assembly; the soluble proteome
is an explicit dependence. Avidan 2026 instead cosynthesizes LSU components
in a minimal system on DNA brushes and obtains late protein-binding
intermediates after adding six factor genes. It does not demonstrate complete
functional LSU regeneration. A particle or occupancy readout is not a bridge
between these endpoints. [S4,S5]

C3: THERMOPHILE OR CHEMICAL BYPASS
Thermus aquaticus unmodified 23S/5S transcripts give 22% native peptidyl-transferase
and 30% poly(U) activity with native-derived proteins. Bacillus stearothermophilus
transcripts also support peptidyl-transferase activity. These constructions
weaken any universal claim that RNA modifications are indispensable for peptide
chemistry; they do not earn full heterogeneous protein translation with a
fully recombinant thermophile protein set. E. coli osmolyte/ketolide assistance
improves transcript reconstitution by up to 100-fold from a roughly 10,000-fold
deficit, but its endpoint is also fragment chemistry. Persistent additives,
inhibitory ligand removal and full translation remain costs to earn. [S6-S8]

4. MODIFICATION IS NOT A SIMPLE MISSING-INGREDIENT CHECKLIST

The old native ~80-nt critical segment does not establish the minimum chemistry
for complete translation. Bao 2024 directly compared continuous native-derived
RNA from five modification-enzyme knockouts against WT RNA, using crossed TP50
sources. Reconstitution had only a threefold fragment-assay penalty associated
with the RNA. The authors identify differing modifications and non-covalent
RNA fragmentation among explanations for the much larger old loss. Full
heterogeneous protein synthesis was not the reconstitution endpoint. [S9]

This makes covalent continuity a scientifically serious comparator to adding
more modified patches. It does not establish that modifications can all be
discarded: 2026 structural/kinetic work finds that larger PTC modification losses
produce alternative conformations, reduced thermal stability and slower peptide
bond formation. A temporary guide cannot permanently compensate for a native
state that is unstable after the guide leaves. [S10]

Enzymatic preparation has an access problem rather than an unlimited helper:
- RlmKL modifies naked RNA, not mature 50S, and directly unwinds H74 on transcript
  duplex substrates. Its two methylation activities can be used before closure.
- RlmE can promote late 45S-to-50S conversion, but the reconstitution used a
  crude-ribosome wash fraction, not only isolated RlmE + a defined RNA substrate.
- RlmB and catalytically inactive RlmL can assist assembly in modification-poor
  cellular backgrounds; those results demonstrate mechanisms and redundancy,
  not sufficiency of a purified cocktail for an unmodified transcript. [S11-S13]

Therefore adding all known enzymes at once is not an earned construction.
An upstream edit can become inaccessible after protein assembly; a late edit
can require an intermediate unavailable before partial assembly. The conventional
next attempt is a staged, measured chemical/assembly process with per-site
modification analysis. Its sufficiency is still a new experimental result.

5. A SELECTIVE, TEMPORARY, PRODUCTIVELY RELEASING GATE ALREADY EXISTS

Wei and Woodson (16 October 2026) provide the sharpest competing local operation.
RsmB recruits S7 while antagonizing S19 at the 16S 3-prime domain. Methyltransferase
cycle completion weakens binding and promotes release; inactive RsmB can become
trapped and inhibit growth. Structural probing identifies a changed helix
junction. A two-stage salt-switch preparation also releases RsmB before most S7
binding occurs, leaving a metastable RNA that S7 can capture. [S14]

Measured numbers are transcribed in RSMB_OPERATION.json. The central distinction
is the evidence: this operation has binding, residence, remodeling, downstream
capture and release data. The earlier arbitrary chosen-rate gate does not.

What remains unearned by that operation:
- Complete 30S active yield, error spectrum and lifetime in the same assay.
- A gate exit condition that directly certifies S7 capture or structural readiness.
- Portability to arbitrary contacts or to the difficult synthetic LSU service.

What it already earns and must not be claimed as novel:
- Selective transient recruitment and exclusion of a later binding partner.
- Chemical-state-dependent release from RNA.
- A local persistent handoff: promptly supplied S7 maintains competence even
  after RsmB has left. Delaying S7 for 10 minutes loses that preparation benefit.

6. EXECUTABLE DISCRIMINATING RESULTS

Run: python stage2_existing/compare_existing.py

A. Native-scaffold conservation. In the earned C0 network, every mature LSU,
precursor and joined ribosome carries one imported/native 23S token. Assembly,
remodeling, subunit joining/splitting and protein repair conserve that token.
The exact integer stoichiometric left-null calculation gives

  active50S + joined70S <= initial native23S + imported native23S.

Thus C0 cannot amplify ribosome number without new native RNA imports. This is
an accounting theorem about the earned network, not an impossibility result
for synthetic RNA. A successful synthetic-23S assembly edge changes the network
and removes the bound. This is why renewable complete RNA matters.

B. Equilibrium discrimination is insufficiently sharp by itself. For two
equilibrated binding states with constants Ks and Kp, capture 1-epsilon and
product occupancy at most epsilon at one enzyme concentration require

  Kp/Ks >= ((1-epsilon)/epsilon)^2.

Using the measured C375A substrate-mimic/product comparison only, Ks=15.4 nM,
Kp=249 nM, best equal error is 19.9% at 61.9 nM enzyme; 90/10 requires ratio81,
whereas the measured ratio is16.17. This does NOT constrain catalytic WT release:
the substrate number is an inactive mimic and catalysis is driven. It prevents
silently turning an affinity difference into an ideal exclusion operation.

C. Fluorescence does not identify active yield. In a simplified fixed-time
measurement Y=N*a*k*q, where a is active fraction, k per-active-particle output
and q reporter success, multiple (a,k,q) have identical Y. Exact witness cases
are included. A full-length fluorescent band establishes a functioning route,
but not the number of functional particles, their error rates or their lifetime.

7. CONSTRUCTIVE CONSEQUENCE FOR THE CENTRAL INVENTION

Keep C0 as the simplest serious benchmark. Treat RsmB/RlmKL as available physical
operations and their failures as design constraints. A new guide architecture
must demonstrate something consequential that they do not: for example, access
to and productive stabilization of a complete synthetic LSU, or an earned
general way to make release conditional on completion of the needed local
interaction. A transient binder plus a graph or an attractive fitted rate does
not suffice. The modifier/patch route and contact-control route should be
reconciled only where the preparation, access and functional endpoints match.
