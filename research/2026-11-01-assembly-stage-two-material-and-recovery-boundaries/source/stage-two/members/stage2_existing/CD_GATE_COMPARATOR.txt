CONCRETE COMPETING CONSTRUCTIONS AND ATTACK ON THE BOX C/D GATE
Requested follow-up from root, 2026-11-01.

Candidate supplied by root: use a reconstituted archaeal box C/D sRNP to bind a
23S target through guide D; add an independently addressed D-prime substrate
to stimulate release after target methylation. Targets considered: G2251 or
C2498. Root owns this proposed application; the underlying allosteric coupling

1. THE EARNED STARTING OPERATION

Graziadei et al.2016, DOI10.1261/rna.054320.115
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4836650/
https://rnajournal.cshlp.org/content/22/5/764.full

The sR26 complex has a genuine release problem: substrate D can be bound and
methylated but has inefficient release unless D-prime is supplied. Addition of
the other substrate at110 min stimulates turnover. The substrate-only
radiometric assay and free-product-sensitive NMR jointly distinguish poor
release from failure to methylate. Native D/D-prime substrates are14 nt long.
Assays used50 C,150 or500 mM NaCl,0 or1 mM Mg. These are not protein-occupied
23S intermediates under4-20 mM Mg.

Internal source inconsistency that must be preserved: Results specifies
0.5 uM substrate /0.15 uM enzyme under multiple turnover. Methods reverses the
description as guide:substrate~3:1 (0.50:0.15 uM). Do not silently infer a precise
enzyme-economy claim from one version. Qualitative coupling survives this issue.

2. THE PROTEIN/RNA COMPLEX IS A SUBSTANTIAL PHYSICAL OBJECT

Graziadei et al.2020, DOI10.7554/eLife.50027
https://elifesciences.org/articles/50027
Data/structures: DOI10.5061/dryad.q573n5tdv; SASBDBproject860.

The mono-RNP is approximately190 kDa: two copies of each of L7Ae, Nop5 and
fibrillarin, plus one guide RNA. Di-RNP carries twice the components. Apo/holo
loading can change oligomeric state. Guide/substrate sequence away from the
methylated nucleotide changes the population of fibrillarin's catalytically
competent conformation. The authors explicitly say equilibrium structures do
not establish transition kinetics, and substrate overhangs may matter.

Consequences: retargeting a native arm cannot inherit its release rate from
terminal-base-pair strength. Long23S arms and neighboring proteins may change
geometry, occupancy and turnover. Capture at a buried site needs an independently
earned opening pathway. Product-release allostery does not solve capture.

3. THE DIRECT CHEMICAL-WRITER COMPARATOR IS STRONG AT C2498

Punekar et al.2012, DOI10.1093/nar/gks727
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3488215/
https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/40/20/10507/2414563

Purified RlmM modifies fully unmodified IVT full-length23S and isolated domainV,
independently of other rRNA modifications, to near the WT extent by primer
extension. Reported reaction completion exceeds80% by45 min. Published inputs:
200 nM RNA,100 nM enzyme,1 mM SAM,37 C after RNA prewarming;10 mM Mg and100 mM
NH4Cl, with glycerol and detergent. RlmM is one protein type, no guide or trigger.

Purta et al.2009, DOI10.1111/j.1365-2958.2009.06709.x
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19400805/
RlmM modifies naked23S, not assembled50S or ribosomes.

Thus an experiment that only makes Cm2498 on free RNA does not select the C/D
architecture. The needed comparative function is selective residence plus
addressed release during assembly. For G2251, native RlmB is the relevant enzyme
and has an independently demonstrated assembly role, but equivalent direct
IVT-substrate kinetics were not retrieved here; do not invent them.

4. A SIMPLER EXTERNALLY COMMANDED RELEASE CONSTRUCTION

Richards et al.2008, DOI10.1016/j.bmcl.2008.09.093
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2593108/

RNA bandages join short2-prime-O-methyl antisense arms through one photolabile
linker. The successful design has6- and12-nt arms and a4-ntgap. At10-fold excess
it lowers GFP translation to30%;5 min near-UV restores95%, with luciferase and
RNA-irradiation controls. Too-short designs leak; too-stable cleaved arms still
inhibit. Melting-temperature difference alone fails to predict performance.

This is an actual alternative to antidote invasion: break the helper backbone,
making an essential arm too short to persist. It consumes a manufactured guide
per operation and uses an optical command. It does not write a permanent mark
onto the target and needs no three-protein release machine. Its source does not
demonstrate ribosomal-subunit assembly or detailed release kinetics from a
protein-containing rRNA intermediate. Exact irradiation timing relative to
addition of reticulocyte lysate requires the supplement; do not count the paper
as direct observation of escape from an already occupied ribosomal precursor.

Recent related primary example: DOI10.1021/acssynbio.6c00668, a coumarin-linked
DNA Lock activated by456 nm irradiation, restores cell-free RNA-switch-directed
translation. It remains an RNA-switch/translation construction, not ribosome
assembly. The2008 source alone is enough to require a photochemical comparator.

5. TWO EXACT LOGICAL BOUNDARIES

A. One methylation write per target site. Without a demethylation operation,
the reaction R(2-primeOH)+SAM -> R(2-primeOMe)+SAH can occur at most once for
that ribose. Therefore repeating an identical chemically driven clamp cycle on
the same molecule cannot repeatedly use methyl-transfer completion as its
energy-consuming release event. This is NOT an objection to one-pass assembly
of fresh unmodified molecules. Nor does it prove the marked RNA cannot bind:
guide complementarity remains. Product-rebinding exclusion is a separate
measurement that the architecture must earn.

B. A trigger is not readiness evidence. If a freely added D-prime oligo is
supplied identically to ready and unready substrates, its arrival conveys no
information about their difference. It is a valid external release command.
To call it an autonomous completion signal, the architecture must cause its
availability or productive binding to depend on the completed interaction.
Merely choosing an endogenous rRNA sequence that appears later in transcription
reports polymerase progress, not necessarily local folding or protein capture.

6. MINIMAL DISCRIMINATING COMPARISON

The fair competition is between an ordinary native writer plus physical staging,
a photofragmentable guide, and the addressed C/D gate, acting on the SAME longRNA
and SAME protein-containing intermediate. First establish helper occupancy,
then trigger release. Separate free target, free helper fragments, methylation
occupancy, damaged RNA, and downstream protein capture. Run pre-methylated target
as a rebinding/reset control. Measure subsequent complete translation after
helper removal. This resolves the actual scientific uncertainty; substituting
a14mer, changing Mg/temperature only for the candidate, or using final
fluorescence alone would not.

Only a gain that survives the native RlmM/RlmB + physical-stage route and the
photofragmentable helper justifies carrying the new hardware burden. The C/D
architecture's strongest potential advantage is sequence-addressed release in
a multiplexed assembly mixture or a demonstrably state-coupled second input.
Neither is established by the published native sR26 operation alone.
