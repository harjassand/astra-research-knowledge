SELECTION AND RECOVERY: CONVENTIONAL CONSTRUCTIONS AND THE REMAINING GAP
Research date: 2026-11-01. No laboratory experiment was executed.

1. What existing machinery already supplies

The full recombinant protein-set construction is described in
CONSTRUCTION_AND_BOUNDARY.txt. Aoyama2022 plus R-iSAT provides an appropriate
baseline: ordinary physical assembly, native modified 23S/5S, transcribed16S,
all54 recombinant ribosomal proteins, PURE protein production. Selecting an
already active subset does not remove its native23S supply dependence.

Surface capture of newly assembling ribosomal particles through a tagged
required ribosomal protein is already demonstrated. Levy et al.2020,
10.1126/sciadv.aaz6020, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7159907/,
expressed rRNA/r-proteins/assembly factors from DNA brushes and captured
particles using an HA-tagged r-protein and surface anti-HA. Secondary/tertiary
protein dependence and LSU association were measured. Activity of immobilized
preexisting LSU was separately demonstrated; complete translation by the newly
assembled SSU was not. This source does not demonstrate a harvesting/reset
manufacturing loop, but capture through an incorporated ribosomal protein is
an existing operation. Protease cleavage of a separate affinity handle while
preserving the native RNP interface is also ordinary tandem affinity
purification, not a new release principle by itself.

An S7 dwell filter must be compared with the actual native cooperative
construction. Duss et al.2019, 10.1016/j.cell.2019.10.035,
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7006226/, showed that secondary
proteins S9/S13/S19 promote early rRNA folding and stabilize S7. S7 alone's
long-lived component is approximately20s at20C and6s at35C; the three
secondary proteins can stabilize binding for minutes. Stable S7 precedes S3
incorporation by approximately4min (kobs0.24+-0.01/min), demonstrating further
slow assembly after S7 capture. The S7(S83C)/deletion157-178 labeling construct
is not intact WT, but the source explicitly reports preserved binding,
comparable reconstitution, and ability to form active ribosomes. This resolves
the concern that the mutation itself necessarily prevents function; a new
surface tether still needs validation.

Selective reset while productive assemblies acquire protection is already a
directly demonstrated local RNP principle. Sun and Woodson2025,
10.1093/nar/gkaf104, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11840561/,
used CsdA/ATP to repeatedly unfold unstable16S structures. At4mM Mg,20nM
CsdA, Figure5 reports unfolding rates around0.7/s for free5prime-domain RNA,
0.07/s withS4, and0.035/s withS4/S16/S17/S20. Adding10nM S4 to already
unfolded RNA restores folded occupancy while CsdA remains. Excess CsdA can
abolish S4 binding; native30S resists persistent unfolding. Thus the competitor
is chemically explicit and already contains graded protection. It does not
establish complete synthetic30S/50S active yield or an arbitrarily selective
reset. Data/code: https://doi.org/10.7281/T1/Z73MRN .

2. Strong functional-selection baseline

Hammerling et al.2020 RISE, 10.1038/s41467-020-14705-2,
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7048773/, assembles ribosomes in
iSAT (TP70 plus S150) and selects via their own synthesized nascent peptide.
The chosen N-terminal3xFLAG handle yielded124-fold WT/inactive specificity
before wash optimization; increasing washes5->10 reduced specific capture29%
and nonspecific capture95%, giving >1000-fold WT/inactive specificity.
These are selectivity ratios against a particular inactive control, not
absolute recovery, universal translational fidelity, or same-particle reuse.

RISE captures for1h at4C, uses high-Mg washes, and elutes with50mM EDTA,
then purifies rRNA and amplifies its genotype. It does not preserve the
assembled RNP or its exact chemical state for repeated service.

Intact RNC recovery itself is old. Hanes and Pluckthun1997,
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC24609/, explicitly permits
competitive ligand elution of whole displayed complexes as an alternative
to EDTA destruction. The modern NS-IP protocol, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/
articles/PMC10518778/, elutes ribosome-mRNA with puromycin or the whole RNC
with FLAG peptide. These supply a strong physical recovery competitor.
Neither is the same as selecting for cognate release-factor termination and
then demonstrating repeated use of the same chemically prepared ribosomes.

For a fair conventional comparator to a new RF gate, assemble with the best
available conventional method, translate a full-length C-terminal-handle
message in defined translation lacking the appropriate release factor,
capture the displayed complexes, then compare three release branches:
(i) competitive FLAG elution followed by ordinary termination/recycling;
(ii) puromycin elution followed by removal of puromycin and recycling;
(iii) the proposed cognate RF-only elution, followed by physically separate
recycling. The first two do not certify termination, but already retain the
same chemical objects. They must be compared on total active ribosome
recovery and subsequent protein output, not only selection specificity.
This combined comparator is a construction from earned operations, not a
published validation of the complete integrated process.

3. Mechanistic failure of simultaneous termination/recycling elution

The proposed peptide tether is held to the ribosome by peptidyl-tRNA. There
are at least two exits that release the ribosome while leaving the captured
nascent protein on the support:

  A. Cognate RF hydrolyzes peptide-tRNA; ribosome dissociates from peptide.
  B. Intact peptidyl-tRNA drops off; ribosome dissociates from peptide-tRNA.

The same measured ribosome eluate cannot distinguish A from B. Adding RRF,
EF-G and/or RF3 before the separation may actively promote B. This is not
merely a hypothetical pathway. Grentzmann et al.1998,
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1369674/, reports RF3/RRF/GTP
release of nonhydrolyzed fMet-tRNA. Heurgue-Hamard et al.1998,
10.1093/emboj/17.3.808, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1170429/,
demonstrates peptidyl-tRNA dissociation promoted by RF3/RRF withEF-G.
The role in early short-peptide drop-off has modern direct support:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10192219/ . Long nascent chains
should change the rate; they do not justify silently deleting the pathway.

The clean implementation consequence is to collect a timed RF1-only eluate
away from the support before adding the recycling machinery. RF3 can also
be withheld until the post-elution stage if adequate stoichiometric RF1 is
available. This charges extra RF1 and separation time but prevents the
known recycling-induced bypass from being mistaken for a termination test.
Factor-free release background and peptide-versus-peptidyl-tRNA chemistry
on the beads must still be measured.

4. Other complete-service leaks

Polysome hitchhiking is a logical physical failure: one completed tagged
nascent chain captures a shared message, which also carries trailing
ribosomes. Releasing the successful front particle can elute that entire
message-associated group. Thus not every recovered ribosome necessarily
made the full chain. Defined one-ribosome-per-message loading, followed by
removal of free subunits and initiation factors before elongation, is a
possible control; mRNA excess alone only supplies statistical suppression.
This is an inference from polysome topology, not measured leakage for the
new gate. The required single-loading process must preserve assembly yield
and is part of the resource account.

A C-terminal handle suppresses ordinary early truncation capture, but does
not exclude internal initiation, in-frame deletions, or mistranslated chains
that retain the tag. An intact N-terminal functional domain plus the distal
tag provides a stronger completion test. No finite reporter panel certifies
all codons, all sequences, or all future lifetime. Repeated tests can enrich
for a specific service, but correlated defects and recurring passengers do
not disappear by assuming independent errors.

RF-dependent release does not alone prove correct stop discrimination:
near-cognate/sense-codon termination is possible, especially without
competing aa-tRNAs. Include cognate-stop versus wrong-stop/RF controls and
measure factor-free release. Relevant primary work:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6093235/ .

RF chemistry matters. Pierson et al.2016,10.1016/j.celrep.2016.08.085,
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5079439/, found substrate-dependent
effects of GGQ methylation on bacterial RF1/RF2 release, especially for
Pro/Gly terminal residues. The spacer's terminal residue and RF methylation
state are therefore part of the specification. A published spontaneous
hydrolysis rate near1e-4/s refers to ribosome-bound fMet-Lys dipeptidyl-tRNA
(https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2491474/); do not transplant it to
full reporter, different RNA chemistry, or the bead-bound geometry.

The new2026 full-protein yPURE release assay,
10.3389/fmolb.2026.1933983, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13672221/,
shows RF omission can form stable full-nLuc pretermination complexes. It
warns that short HiBiT yields large RF-independent background and that
recycling/rescue factors must also be omitted. It is not an intact-ribosome
recovery/reuse demonstration and cannot substitute for bacterial kinetics.

After normal recycling,30S and50S mix and reassociate. Chemical identity
can remain attached to individual rRNA/subunits, while a particular original
70S pair is not preserved. Claims should specify which object is retained.

5. Cost/advantage boundary

Selection alone has no route to make additional active particles. If a test
does not repair or assemble particles, its accepted active count cannot
exceed the input active count. It can increase active fraction and reduce
future wasted downstream work. Repeat selection compounds losses from
loading, capture, washing, hydrolysis, collection and recycling. If each
round has active-particle recovery q_i, recovery after n rounds is the
product of the q_i; this accounting requires no chosen kinetic rates.

All message production, amino-acid activation, translation factors, tRNAs,
energy regeneration, tags/antibodies or resins, buffer exchange, refrigeration,
and discarded peptides are charged. Full reporter synthesis is itself
useful evidence but consumes a service cycle. Whether that cost is small
depends on subsequent lifetime and output, which remain measurements.

The potentially substantive remaining distinction is recoverable testing
of nonheritable chemical ribosome states, with an ordered catalytic
completion condition, while preserving enough of the same subunits to
measure their next service cycle. I did not find a source establishing this
entire RF-gated recovery service; absence in this targeted search is not a
novelty proof. The strongest competing construction already recovers intact
RNCs without this extra certificate. Neither route yet establishes a new
RNA-manufacturing reaction, complete synthetic23S active yield, or total
factory advantage. It is a candidate enabling operation whose decisive
measurement is net reusable active output after the same initial chemistry
and same total input budget.
