# Exact circuit evaluation cannot use one universally small Boolean algebra

This is an elementary obstruction to one candidate extension mechanism, not a lower bound on CircuitSAT and not a novelty claim.

## Full exactness

Let A be a finite-dimensional unital algebra over Q, let e_1,...,e_n be commuting idempotents, and let lambda:A->Q be linear. Suppose evaluation of every Boolean arithmetic expression f is exact for uniform counting:

lambda(f(e_1,...,e_n)) = sum_{x in {0,1}^n} f(x).

Then dim_Q A >= 2^n.

For each assignment a define p_a = product_i (a_i e_i + (1-a_i)(1-e_i)). The p_a are pairwise orthogonal idempotents. Exactness on the size-O(n) conjunction selecting a gives lambda(p_a)=1, so p_a is nonzero. If sum_a c_a p_a=0, multiplication by p_b gives c_b p_b=0, and hence c_b=0. Thus the 2^n elements p_a are linearly independent.

The assumptions can be weakened: exactness need only hold on the 2^n point indicators, with the homomorphic Boolean operations retained. A need not be commutative outside the subalgebra generated by the e_i.

## Degree-limited exactness

For d<=n let M_d={S subset [n]: |S|<=d}, and e_S=product_{i in S} e_i. Suppose lambda(e_S e_T)=2^(n-|S union T|) for every S,T in M_d. Then dim_Q A >= sum_{j=0}^d binomial(n,j).

The matrix G_{S,T}=2^(n-|S union T|) is the Gram matrix of the linearly independent squarefree Boolean monomials m_S(x)=product_{i in S}x_i under the positive inner product sum_x f(x)g(x). Therefore G is positive definite. A linear dependence sum c_S e_S=0, multiplied by e_T and followed by lambda, gives Gc=0; hence c=0. In particular, exact uniform counting for products of degree-d polynomials already needs this many algebra coordinates in a universal homomorphic evaluator.

## Related internal-state boundary

For an invertible binary linear map y=B x, the tensor T(x,y)=1[y=B x] is a permutation matrix of order 2^n over Q. Its matrix rank is 2^n. Any exact representation sum_{j=1}^r u_j(x)v_j(y) therefore needs r>=2^n, even though B has a polynomial-size description and can be evaluated in polynomial time. Unique internal values do not imply small separable boundary rank.

## Limits

None of these statements excludes instance-dependent representations, cancellation between signed components, families of deformations plus reconstruction, or a non-homomorphic algorithm that computes only one requested count. The supplied ETH proof uses precisely a richer strategy than a single universal homomorphic evaluator. The obstruction tells us which tempting generalizations cannot preserve both small state dimension and ordinary gate composition.

## Primary literature checks

- Ryan Williams, Improving Exhaustive Search Implies Superpolynomial Lower Bounds, author manuscript: https://people.csail.mit.edu/rrw/improved-algs-lbs2.pdf . Theorems 1.1 and 1.2 relate general CircuitSAT savings in original input count to circuit lower bounds. This is an established motivation and difficulty boundary, not a proof that stronger algorithms are impossible.
- Andraschko, Danner, Kreuzer, SAT Solving Using XOR-OR-AND Normal Forms (2024): https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11786-024-00594-x . It constructs compact 2-XNF representations from Boolean polynomial input and compares 2-Xornado with CryptoMiniSat, xnfSAT, Bosphorus, PolyBoRi and SBVA-CaDiCaL. An affine/XOR-heavy compiler must compare with this literature; stripping XOR-only gates is not by itself new.
- Beame, Li, Roy, Suciu, Exact Model Counting of Query Expressions: Limitations of Propositional Methods (2017), author PDF: https://users.cs.duke.edu/~sudeepa/papers/TODS2017-modelcounting.pdf . Establishes exponential limitations of particular compilation targets. These are representation-family lower bounds, not general counting lower bounds.
- Darwiche and Marquis, A Knowledge Compilation Map (2002): https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.1819 . Distinguishes the cost of compiling an input from tractable queries after a suitable representation is supplied.

## Several universal algebras do not evade the bound

If j=1,...,q branches use algebras A_j, commuting idempotents e_{i,j}, and a fixed linear reconstruction lambda on their direct sum, set A=direct_sum_j A_j and e_i=(e_{i,1},...,e_{i,q}). The preceding proof applies verbatim and gives sum_j dim A_j >= 2^n. This conclusion depends on universality across all input functions and preservation of homomorphic Boolean gate operations in every branch. It does not apply to the occurrence-specific deformations in the supplied ETH proof, which are not Boolean-valued away from the restoration point.
