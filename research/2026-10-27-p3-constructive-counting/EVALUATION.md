# Retrieval and source-use evaluation

Ten fixed probes were formulated by the intake owner after reading the sources. They are **source-informed**, not blind, independent or held out. Seven concern acquired constructions, one locates the native-only local-algebra continuation, and two ask for unsupported transfers. Ranking code, model weights, concepts and query aliases were not tuned to these probes. Alias entries contain only the new card titles.

All thirty method outputs and the input version are retained in [RETRIEVAL_EVALUATION.json](RETRIEVAL_EVALUATION.json). [SOURCE_ROUTE_READS.json](SOURCE_ROUTE_READS.json) preserves actual bounded source actions and returned text. Those source passages were read against the full proofs and substantive audits; their mathematical scope is assessed in [INTAKE_REVIEW.md](INTAKE_REVIEW.md).

P09 asks whether these packages establish polynomial counting for arbitrary Boolean circuits. They do not. The source's exact final-degree recognition obstruction and every positive construction's structural contract prevent that transfer. The rankers still return candidates, including unrelated historical circuit claims; **no automatic evidence-aware abstention is claimed**.

P10 asks whether total field counts automatically give equally cheap arbitrary binary-prefix counts. The governing field proof's “Counting is the demonstrated capability” passage explicitly denies that inference. Content and agent ranking reach the correct family; structured search misses it in five. Reading the actual limitation is necessary. This is an unsupported transfer check, not another positive locating success.

The bounded-read check exposed a real navigation defect: a narrowed source response could inherit a header quote outside its returned range. The fix drops that quote from the narrowed reference while retaining the complete original citation in the packet. It applies to record, scientific-window and evidence-window reads. A regression test checks the actual field proof's prefix-limitation range and confirms full reads keep their original anchor. The source bytes, citation authority and ranking are unchanged.
