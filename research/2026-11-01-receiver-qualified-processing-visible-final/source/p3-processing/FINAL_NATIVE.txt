## 1. What the new theorem earns

Consider \(m\) physical blocks of dimension \(d\). During a gate window, the actual deterministic controls act within these blocks and may be imperfect or entangling inside a block. The noise has the form

\[
\mathcal L_t(\rho)
=-i\!\left[\sum_j H_j(t),\rho\right]
+\sum_a\mathcal D\!\left[\sum_j L_{aj}(t)\right](\rho),
\]

where the \(L_{aj}\) are Hermitian and block-local. Shared noise directions allow dense spatial correlations of unrestricted rank. Independent Hermitian residual noise is included in the same model.

Let \(p_j\) be the **actual gate’s entanglement infidelity relative to its intended target**, including systematic control error. Acquire

\[
b\ge \frac1m\sum_j p_j .
\]

For Clifford targets, randomized product Pauli preparations and product Pauli measurements suffice. Correlations among blocks within a shot are allowed. Local preparation and measurement inaccuracies enter as an explicit bias.

The crucial step is an irreversible-volume witness. If

\[
a_j=\int\sum_a \frac{\operatorname{tr}L_{aj}(t)^2}{d}\,dt,
\]

then

\[
\boxed{\quad p_j\ge \frac{1-e^{-d^2a_j}}{d^2}.\quad}
\]

An unknown deterministic control error can rotate a channel, but cannot restore volume lost to this Markovian noise. Channel determinants and Gaussian concentration are established machinery; the contribution here is their combination with actual-gate acquisition and the receiver’s coherent error span. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

The witness saturates, so I do **not** invert an average contrast to claim every rate is small. Instead, choose \(z<1/d^2\). The blocks with \(p_j>z\) form a proof-only bad set whose size is bounded by

\[
q\le \left\lfloor\frac{mb}{z}\right\rfloor .
\]

Their locations are never learned. The code pays for them as arbitrary faults. On the remaining blocks,

\[
\sum_{\text{remaining }j}a_j
\le c_zmb,
\qquad
c_z=\frac{-\log(1-d^2z)}{d^2z}.
\]

The second step controls the entire finite gate trajectory. A Gaussian Lipschitz bound survives arbitrary deterministic noncommuting controls within the admitted blocks. Together with a sharp matrix inequality, it yields the following result.

If the declared common receiver corrects arbitrary operators on at most \(t\) blocks, put \(h=t+1-q\). For \(x>\sqrt{mb}\), the complete corrected operation has **full diamond error**, including arbitrary reference systems, bounded by

\[
\boxed{
\delta\le
4\sum_{\ell=h}^{m}
\frac{\bigl[\sqrt{(d-1)m}\,x\bigr]^\ell}{\ell!}
+
2\exp\!\left[
-\frac{(x-\sqrt{mb})^2}{2c_zmb}
\right].
}
\]

No physical twirl is needed for this bound. The first term is the coherent fault-path remainder beyond the receiver’s capacity. The second is the Gaussian exceptional-event probability.

The circuit version keeps faults in their actual temporal order. Locations may revisit the same qubit. Its essential premise is that the **actual continuation corrects the common span of faults at those locations**; output-code distance alone does not establish that property. Outcome-dependent selection of later controls needs a separately qualified conditional-stage argument.

### The acquisition consequence

For fixed block dimension and fixed corrected fraction \(\alpha=(t+1)/m\), the theorem gives the sufficient scale

\[
b=O\!\left(\frac{\alpha^2}{(d-1)\log(1/\delta)}\right).
\]

I derived explicit finite constants and a useful-acceptance guarantee, not just a certificate that happens to accept a favorable record. Under stated margins on the true mean and probe bias, the number of script repetitions can be

\[
K=O\!\left(\log(1/\delta)\log(1/\beta)\right),
\]

where \(\beta\) is the calibration failure probability.

This still costs \(K\) actual scripts and \(O(Km)\) physical preparations, measurements and classical records. Each distinct operating context must be acquired or covered by a proved shared bound. The code and receiver must supply their claimed correction fraction at their actual circuit cost.

The information reduction is substantive: within the admitted model, a receiver can qualify processing without making the full-register generator norm small. The Gaussian law, support geometry, stationarity and probe quality remain physical premises; endpoint data do not establish them against arbitrary alternatives.

## 4. A broader principle emerged from the independent construction

A collaborator started from the desired processing capability without inheriting the preferred Gaussian bound. That route used offline verified teleportation resources, building on established gate teleportation and adversarial state verification. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="4"}

It produced an exact two-setting source test, then tried to accept all physical states that a source-side decoder could correct. The first consumer failed decisively:

- A three-bit repetition code corrects the live-data error \(X_1\).
- It separately corrects the source-input error \(X_2\).
- Ordinary Bell decoding sees their combination \(X_1X_2\), supplies \(X_3\), and leaves logical \(X_1X_2X_3\).

Both isolated decoded logical certificates are perfect. The actual composed logical channel has full diamond error **two**.

Two constructive repairs survived:

1. **Reserve a shared correction budget** for source, incoming data and Bell-operation faults.
2. **Supply verified source-syndrome labels**, and subtract that source frame before decoding the data syndrome.

The resulting principle is broader than the present noise family:

> Accuracy defined after a recovery does not automatically compose through a physical consumer that omits that recovery. The consumer must accept the joint physical error domain and the syndrome/frame information it actually receives.

An implemented recovery or another compatible decoder can also satisfy this requirement. The two repairs are sufficient constructions, not universal necessities.
