A more consequential opening has emerged in quantum memory. Building on an existing 3D passive-memory proposal, we have a candidate construction that replaces its higher-body interactions with local three-body qubit interactions while retaining long-lived thermal storage under a specified noise model. Two internal reviews have checked different parts of the argument and caught corrections that are being incorporated. If the full proof holds, this would make an abstract protected-memory design closer to the kinds of interactions hardware can implement. It is not yet a practical memory: the required temperature and interaction strengths may be extremely demanding, and efficient initialization and robustness to general hardware errors remain open. It has not had external review.
