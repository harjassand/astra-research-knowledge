# CP cavities with an infinite bath and unbounded free dynamics
30 October 2026. Root construction candidate, independent audit pending.

## Consequence and exact added structure
The finite-dimensional bath in the accepted CP-cavity theorem is unnecessary when all unbounded dynamics have a supplied common bath-only CP semigroup and the remaining generator is completely bounded. The local fault strength is unchanged and does not contain the bath Hamiltonian norm, bath dimension, or a global time-volume exponential. This does NOT cover unbounded system-bath couplings such as unrestricted position coupling. It is not a claim of novelty over established bounded-interaction Hamiltonian fault tolerance.

Let the system S have finitely many finite-dimensional sites. Let B be a separable Hilbert space, possibly infinite. On trace-class operators assume a strongly continuous normal CPTP semigroup V_t on B with generator B_0, extended as identity on S and reference. Unitary evolution generated by any self-adjoint unbounded bath Hamiltonian is an example. Work in completely bounded trace norm, with all statements uniform over finite reference ampliations (and normal extensions when defined).

On each finite scheduled time interval the actual dynamics is the bounded perturbation of B_0 by K(t), where K is strongly measurable on trace class, has integrable completely bounded norm, and the resulting propagators are CPTP on EVERY subinterval. A piecewise-constant finite schedule is sufficient. Decompose

  K(t)=L_id(t)+D_B(t)+sum_e N_e(t),

where D_B is bounded and bath-only (not required separately CP), L_id is the intended bounded finite-system gate generator on disjoint active blocks, and N_e are bounded HP maps of system support at most q and incident norm at most eta. Centering is used only if required by the earlier TP formulation, not for the TNI version. Each finite device has finite integral of ||K||_cb; it may grow with device size. No uniform bound on that total is needed in the final local fault estimate.

The common bath base is an ADDITIONAL structural hypothesis relative to an entirely arbitrary unbounded generator. We are not defining partial traces of an unspecified unbounded generator on an unjustified domain.

## 1. Reduced propagator and reduced bounded perturbation
Fix R to be a union of currently active intended gate blocks on the scheduled interval. Let E_R append maximally mixed I_R/d_R and let C_R trace R out. These normal complete contractions commute with V_t in the evident intertwining sense, and C_R E_R=Id. Set

  Q_(t,s)=C_R T_(t,s) E_R,
  K_R(t)=C_R K(t) E_R.

The bounded-perturbation propagator W_(t,s) for B_0+K_R(t) exists by the convergent Dyson series based on V_t. We prove it CP/TP without claiming K_R separately generates CP dynamics.

Put h_(t,s)=integral_s^t ||K(u)||_cb du. The zero-order Dyson terms of Q and W are V_(t-s). Their first-order terms agree EXACTLY:

 C_R integral V_(t-u) K(u) V_(u-s) du E_R
   = integral V_(t-u) K_R(u) V_(u-s) du.

Every order-r Dyson term has complete norm at most h^r/r!, because V is completely contractive. Since ||K_R||<=||K||, both remainders beyond first order have norm at most e^h-1-h. Hence

  ||Q_(t,s)-W_(t,s)||_cb <= 2(e^h-1-h) <= h^2 e^h.       (IB1)

This bound uses no norm differentiability of V_t at zero. All integrals may be interpreted strongly on trace class, with the displayed complete-norm majorants.

Partition [s,t] into intervals with h_i=integral_i||K|| and max h_i tending to zero. Each Q_i is CPTP. Each W_i has norm at most exp(h_i). The telescoping product estimate gives

 ||product_i Q_i-W_(t,s)||_cb
    <= exp(2h_total) sum_i h_i^2
    <= exp(2h_total) h_total max_i h_i ->0.              (IB2)

(The deliberately conservative exponential only proves convergence of this limiting construction.) Thus W is CPTP as a complete-norm limit of CPTP maps. CP and trace preservation hold on the entire trace class. Its generator is the defined bath base plus bounded K_R, with the standard bounded-perturbation domain; no new generator domain is postulated.

The same proof works if the actual subinterval propagators are CP/TNI: each Q_i is CP/TNI and their limit is CP/TNI. The bath base can remain CPTP.

## 2. Actual cavities and interpolation
Restore the isolated intended generators on R. Their independent CPTP propagator tensored with the just-earned reduced complement gives a CP cavity, with generator

  B_0 + L_id + P_R(K-L_id),
  P_R(A)=Id_R tensor C_R A E_R.

The reduced intended generators on R vanish because they are trace annihilating; intended complement gates are unchanged. These identities concern bounded maps and are domain-safe. The unbounded B_0 remains exactly the same for every cavity.

For every interpolation parameter z, the earlier multilinear convex combination of cavities likewise has the common base B_0 and a bounded perturbation K_z. It generates CP propagators: convexly average the exact cavity maps over each common subinterval, using the fixed multilinear weights at z. The average is CP/TNI; its zero- and first-order Dyson terms agree with those of B_0+K_z, and the same second-order estimate/product convergence proves CP/TNI. No rescaling of a time-dependent generator into successive time fractions is used. Time-dependent convex weights can be treated by bounded measurable approximation; the intended theorem needs fixed z and a finite schedule only. This is not an appeal to an unproved domain-sensitive sum theorem for two unrelated unbounded generators.

When selected locations change at gate boundaries, concatenate the finitely many interval constructions. All have the common bath base. Intended instantaneous CPTP gates at declared boundaries remain allowed as in the finite theorem.

## 3. Local derivatives and unchanged fault law
All z-dependence is in bounded K_z. Its parameter derivatives are bounded and annihilate bath-only terms, including D_B; B_0 has no parameter derivative. Bounded-perturbation variation of constants around the CP interpolated propagators therefore gives the same ordered-set-partition derivative formula as the accepted finite theorem. Between insertions these exact propagators have norm at most one, rather than exp(h_total).

Consequently

 ||partial_A K_z(t)||_cb
   <=2^|A| sum_(e touches every selected location in A) eta_e(t),

with zero derivative for |A|>q. The same rooted partition estimate and exact Mobius fault maps give

 ||sum_(F contains S_0) E_F||_cb <= x^|S_0|,
 x=[2*3^(q-1) g eta tau]^(1/q) <=1.

There is NO exp(h_total) in this result. The exponential in (IB2) disappears when the limiting cavity is established; it is not a fault estimate or resource cost. Arbitrary bath initial states and finite-reference entanglement are covered by the complete norms. Bath-only evolution, including arbitrary free high energies, remains in every fault map.

Likewise exact-target defects, finite system reset receiving, fixed native system-only cuts, and the TNI hybrid argument transfer provided their other assumptions are met. The native cut must commute with the supplied free bath base, as a system-only cut does. This is a receiving observation, not a new whole infinite-bath device verdict; every later common-chart, moment and logical-identity proof must separately be valid for normal infinite-bath coefficients or limits.

## Boundary and comparison
The theorem handles arbitrary unbounded FREE bath dynamics with bounded system-bath perturbations. Bounded Weyl/displacement couplings or finite-dimensional spin couplings to bounded bath operators fit; a coupling to a+a^dagger on the entire oscillator Hilbert space does not fit merely because a low-energy state is expected. A finite energy cutoff requires its own error control. The bath base must be supplied, not inferred from finite reduced tomography or the total CP hypothesis alone.

Existing bounded Hamiltonian non-Markovian fault tolerance already allows very general baths. The specific candidate extension here is the CP-cavity proof for local HP decompositions with only total CP-divisible dynamics and a common unbounded bath base, retaining the same signed-superoperator interface. Historical novelty and practical advantage are unestablished. No previously accepted finite source has been edited or broadened by this note.
