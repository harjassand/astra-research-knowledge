# Strong ordinary comparators and a screened next direction
30 October 2026. Primary-source screen, not an audit of those papers.

The thought of amortizing a polynomial-size resource factory across many constant-rate native banks does not by itself create a new constant-space-overhead fault-tolerance principle. The following primary results must be compared before selecting that next task.

1. Christandl, Fawzi and Goswami, arXiv:2512.02760v1 / PRX Quantum6,040334 (2025), https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.02760 . Introduction and abstract prove constant qubit overhead for a general circuit-level diamond-norm perturbation model, including coherent and amplitude-damping errors. The stated noise replaces each circuit gate by a nearby channel. This is not automatically the same interface as a shared retained bath with inclusive signed-fault bounds; that receiving step would need proof. Their introduction also discusses serializing gates in earlier constant-space schemes. Thus neither coherent noise alone nor factory amortization alone is a new consequence here.

2. Yamasaki and collaborators, arXiv:2411.03683, https://arxiv.org/html/2411.03683 , circuit-model section: constant-space/polylog-time work includes the nonzero latency of classical computation but assumes that computation noiseless. It uses local stochastic Pauli noise. Merely waiting while a classical calculation runs is therefore already in the strong comparator. A claim about physical noisy feedforward must count its actual hardware and computation, as our receiver does, without asserting the other paper already supplies that extension.

3. Han and Liu, arXiv:2609.28461v2 (24September2026), https://arxiv.org/html/2609.28461 , sections2 and3: their reported purely logarithmic-time, constant-space construction uses a local stochastic supported-fault model and noiseless polynomial-size classical circuits. Classical depth is charged to physical time; classical hardware is excluded from quantum width. Their storage/active-subspace transfers and reusable ancillary workspace make a substantially stronger comparator than an elementary concatenated circuit when discussing asymptotic resource overhead. This screen is not verification of their theorem.

Current decision: finish the actual one-step and repeated native Clifford receiving proof because it closes a concrete physical interface. Do not advertise its potential factory-sharing corollary as a new constant-overhead theorem. The remaining scientifically distinct questions include whole-device accounting with noisy classical processors, retained correlated environments, bounded interaction/access geometry, and whether the native memory changes a complete resource comparison. None is settled by adding standard magic-state injection or naming a universal gate set. Any later universal extension must earn a consequence beyond an ordinary FT helper doing the same computation.
