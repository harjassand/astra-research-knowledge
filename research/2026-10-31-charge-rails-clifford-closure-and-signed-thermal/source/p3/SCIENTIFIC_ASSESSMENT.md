The main advance is a broader conditional algorithm, not another small numerical fixture. For the acquired decomposition `J=D0+S+A_+A_+^T-N`, the positive conditional variance inequality gives the all-tilt trace bound `Tr Cov(Y_-) <= p beta Tr(N)/(1-alpha)`. Conditional independence between sites, not between times within a site, makes the trace sum `p M delta=p beta`. This removes time-mesh size from the negative rejection exponent while still charging vector dimension, sampling and arithmetic. A finite sampled center and the correctly shifted positive normalizer supply the construction; an optimal-center oracle is not assumed.

The guarantee is still materially restricted. Polynomial overhead requires a fixed retained positive dimension and physical/contraction parameters, and a negative fluctuation budget of logarithmic order in system size. Negative rank may grow, but generic extensive negative trace remains expensive. The positive quadrature has `n^{O(d)}` dependence; this is not `f(d)n^c` tractability or a general solver for frustrated models. Trace-sensitive acquisition needs residual precision of order `1/n`. Different constraints may need new centers and guides.

P3 also replaces a periodic basis that fails at twisted boundaries with an interval cosine construction whose primitives vanish at each original boundary. That earns closure for the stated replica sewing, sign-flip seams and compatible pins, with susceptibility at the longer replica temperature. A first-order polynomial-strength temporal fallback repairs the sharper bound's potentially exponential row-strength constant. Fixed-time physical pin insertions still require their own bridge; finite-grid pinning closure is not that bridge. These are useful representation changes around concrete failures.

The conditional-sample state result is a second reusable opening. Add-one Bernoulli estimates and forward-KL chain control give an averaged output-state error bounded by `2 sqrt(m[log((N+2)/(N+1))+zeta_b log(N+2)])`. A random tape shared across counterfactual prefixes can be used without demanding a simultaneously good event at every prefix; each fixed query still needs the stipulated independent ideal batch and approximation budget. Reversible computation, conditional sample generation, guide acquisition and gate precision are charged. This is a quantum-gate construction, not a performed quantum computation, and its usefulness depends on earning economical conditional samples.

Executions remain distinct. The new 12-spin run generated 32 complete finite-grid paths in 10.7344 seconds, with reported joint TV bound 0.016611951 under its random-word/arithmetic contract, but reused the earlier 450.552-second, 13.585-billion-word setup. The separate 8-spin relative-coherence/entropy calculation uses full 256-dimensional interval matrices, not the general sewn solver; binary64 direct exponentiation was faster but uncertified. Eight small rational negative-mode fixtures test centers and identities, not a production signed-model algorithm. These establish scoped implementations and checks, not whole-method practical advantage.

Primary comparisons sharpen rather than erase the contribution. [Koehler–Lee–Risteski, Theorem 1.1 and Appendix B](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v178/koehler22a/koehler22a.pdf) already give global sampling and variational negative reweighting. Appendix B is a general distribution-with-tilts result, not solely an Ising observation. Its displayed functional becomes P3's centering functional in feature coordinates; strong convexity alone is therefore not the new principle. The quantum weighted fluctuation trace, compatible boundaries and actual conditional computation are the earned inputs. [Hastings et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/1001.2335) already supply replica/Swap entropy estimation and ratio improvements. Neither replication nor avoiding a naive inverse-purity estimator is new by itself.

[Vuong, Theorems 1/2, formal Theorem 20 and Theorem 33](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.02079) supply the relevant tractability/hardness and pinned-count state-preparation ancestry. P3's warm-state versus hard fixed-order Renyi entropy statement follows by the exact cold-normalizer identity and the imported hard family. It is a useful output-dependent hardness corollary, not a new hardness reduction or hardness for its bounded-positive-rank class. The newer sample/KL route changes the computational interface; it does not supply a quantum device or speedup. Relevant primary bodies were read; Firecrawl was unavailable and ordinary web retrieval supplied them.

Compared with the previous round, this is genuine mathematical and computational progress: signed interactions and required output boundaries are earned rather than handed to an oracle. It still does not establish a civilisation-level breakthrough or a compelling complete-resource advantage. The unresolved positive-mode cost matters because it limits which consequential physical questions become accessible, not because every remaining technical limitation must be removed indiscriminately.
