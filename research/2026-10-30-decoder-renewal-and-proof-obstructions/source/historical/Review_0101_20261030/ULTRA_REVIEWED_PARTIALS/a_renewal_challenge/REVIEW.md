## 1. Exactness does constrain every positive Kraus branch

For a fixed U and the identity Bell input, exact success has Choi matrix
p_U |Omega_k><Omega_k|. If a positive sum has rank one, each positive
summand has range in that same ray: test a vector orthogonal to the ray
and use that all its nonnegative quadratic forms sum to zero. Thus every
resolved source-label tuple q, known-control environment h and payload
label j has R_qh(U)U_j proportional to I_k. There is no cancellation of
wrong positive branches. The claim remains true at U with p_U=0, where
all those summands are zero.

This is about the unnormalized instrument, before ANY Born division.
Normalizing individual successful states would be an illegitimate way
to introduce U-dependent rational amplitude functions and evade the proof.
Exact complete-channel success also forces state-independent success
probability: the identity Bell ray already characterizes a scalar identity
CP subchannel. A basis-state-only or expectation-only recovery would not
give this conclusion and is not the theorem's target.

A fixed-Q success comb has finite-dimensional input/output/query wires.
Standard finite-dimensional comb realization gives a finite ancillary
Kraus realization, so this is not a hidden bounded-memory restriction.
Arbitrary known intermediate measurement outcomes can be deferred or
aggregated into that success comb. Even a continuous classical measurement
description does not evade the argument: one may use the finite aggregate
success-comb realization (or use a common almost-everywhere branch set
from a countable dense subset of U and polynomial continuity). Every
finite-query known-control coefficient is source independent.

## 2. Adaptive and offline programmes retain holomorphic amplitudes

In a fixed unnormalized forward-query branch, a source call contributes
one matrix U_j/sqrt(r). Known gates, ancillary states and measurement
Kraus matrices contribute constants. Multiplying and contracting these
finite tensors makes the payload-to-logical amplitude a holomorphic
polynomial of total degree Q, multilinear in SLOT variables. A transpose
generated by a known Bell wire does not conjugate an unknown matrix entry.
Complex conjugates enter the density matrix only after pairing amplitudes.

Adaptive query inputs do not change this fact: at a fixed prior outcome
branch the next controller matrix is known, while the earlier source
entries remain inside the amplitude. The queried input may be an unknown
qubit entangled with the payload, programme and all retained memory.
Reconstructing a classical estimate and choosing a gate from that estimate
also gives a fixed known gate once the measurement outcome is fixed.

Offline programme acquisition is simply an initial part of this same
network. Its calls are counted, and it cannot be replaced by a normalized
U-dependent advice state supplied at no cost. If its preparation repeats
until success, the entire finite stopping prefix is still the branch being
analyzed. The theorem would not apply to an adjoint/dilation oracle or
free U-dependent advice; neither is granted.

## 3. The unused-block argument really kills the whole branch

Suppose l is absent from q. Then R_qh contains no entry of U_l. Right
rotating that k-column block by a nonscalar V in U(k) leaves R unchanged,
preserves orthogonality of every column and stays in the universally
correct source family. From R U_l=a I_k and R U_l V=a' I_k it follows
that a V=a' I_k. Since k>=2 one can choose nonscalar V, forcing a=0.
Consequently R(U)U_l=0 for EVERY source unitary.

This is where stopping at orthogonality would be too weak: at a single
unitary, R could annihilate only that missing k-space. The polynomial
continuation pays the stronger conclusion. For any polynomial P zero on
U(d), P(W exp(zH)) is entire and zero for imaginary z, with W unitary and
H Hermitian. It is identically zero. Polar decomposition reaches every
invertible complex matrix at z=1, and density then reaches every matrix.
Apply this entrywise to R(M)M_l. The missing block M_l is now freely
variable while R is fixed, so R itself is identically zero.

The rectangular D>=kr version is also valid: regard a polynomial in the
first kr columns as a polynomial in all entries of a D-by-D matrix. Its
zero on U(D) implies its zero on all complex square matrices, whose first
columns are arbitrary rectangular matrices. There is no assumption that
the free variation must itself preserve the physical source promise.

## 4. The all-gauge Schur lift is a new, stronger restriction

For the flat source, U'=U(I_k tensor W) leaves the physical N unchanged.
For the SAME controller branch h, forward-slot multilinearity gives
T_U'h=T_Uh W^tensor Q. No gate has been rotated on the inaccessible
environment. Apply the unused-block zero in the rotated family to all
tuples in a fixed coordinate hyperplane. This forces T_Uh to annihilate
the unitary orbit span of (C^(r-1))^tensor Q.

In Schur--Weyl form the fixed hyperplane tensor contains every shape of
height<r, with its ENTIRE Specht multiplicity space. The U(r) orbit of
the nonzero irrep part spans the full corresponding U(r) irrep. Shapes
of height r cannot occur in a hyperplane. Thus the orbit span is exactly
the sum of all height<r blocks, not a fixed-vector or fixed-multiplicity
ansatz. Its orthogonal complement is root's Pi_r.

There is a real transpose issue, and it is paid. T maps a flag basis ket
to an operator coefficient, whereas the joint Stinespring state stores
those coefficients as flag kets. Support therefore initially reads
Pi_r^T. The central symmetric-group projector is a real inverse-paired
character sum, so Pi_r^T=Pi_r. Multiplying its operator coefficients by
the payload isometry U_j does not change this query-environment support.
No payload environment was counted as an extra queried sample.

## 5. The environment product is true before conditioning, even adaptively

Directly, K_l^*K_j=delta_lj I/r, so the complementary map is
X -> Tr(X)I_r/r. This is a COMPLETE replacement channel, not only the
statement that every standalone environment has the same marginal. On
an input entangled with arbitrary previous inaccessible environments and

The theorem uses neither false assertion. A positive success substate

## 6. RSK and variable stopping: a distributional comparison

The full-height weight is sum_(height lambda=r) f_lambda s_lambda(1^r)/r^Q.
The word-RSK identity equates it to the probability of an r-letter uniform
word having a strictly decreasing subsequence of length r. That subsequence
must be r,r-1,...,1. Greedy waiting produces r independent geometric(1/r)
waits. Equivalently the required number of successes by Q is binomial,
which gives root's exact b_r(Q), union bound and quadratic fixed-success
lower bound.

This last equality is NOT a pathwise statement about flags measured on
the actual quantum source. For example the height-two antisymmetric
vector |12>-|21> has a nonzero coefficient on the word12, which has no
decreasing2,1 subsequence. The proof does not make that invalid inference.
It equates a TRACE/DIMENSION with the separate RSK word distribution.

For a variable-length exact inverse, the sum of finite-time success Choi
operators is supported on the same identity Bell ray. Therefore success
by time Q remains an exact subinstrument. Pad already stopped branches
with ordinary dummy source queries while preserving their logical output.
The fixed-Q theorem bounds Pr(Q_stop<=Q) by b_r(Q). Summing the resulting
tail inequalities proves E Q_stop>=r^2. This uses stochastic domination,
not compatible measurements of growing Young diagrams, and allows all
failure history to be retained. Infinite expectation remains possible.

The weaker coupon proof does have a fixed-basis latent-word coupling;
