## 2. Two elementary Bell facts

For an s-qubit state rho, write p_b for its two-copy Bell outcome law. A
four-copy Bell DIFFERENCE has law Q_rho(a)=sum_b p_b p_(b+a).

For ANY Hermitian Pauli P_a,

    Q_rho(a)>=|Tr(rho P_a)|^2/4^s.                              (1)

Proof: P_a on one half of a Bell pair permutes Bell labels b->b+a, up to
phases. Positivity of rho tensor rho bounds every corresponding off-diagonal
entry by sqrt(p_b p_(b+a)). Taking the trace against P_a tensor I gives
|Tr rho P_a|<=sum_b sqrt(p_b p_(b+a)). Cauchy with4^s terms proves(1).

For a Pauli-label subgroup H and a coset a+H, let E_(a+H) be the four-copy
Bell-difference POVM event. Applying any h in H to ANY single copy shifts
the outcome by h and leaves this event invariant. Thus E_(a+H) commutes with
each individual-copy signed H-generator and with Pi_H^tensor4, where Pi_H
is the common +1 eigenspace projector. Since E is positive and block diagonal,

    E>=Pi_H^tensor4 E Pi_H^tensor4.                             (2)

This is an ANALYTICAL positivity inequality. Neither H nor its projector is
known or implemented. In particular it asserts no convex clean/fault mixture.

Bell differences are Clifford covariant at the label level. For example their
Fourier coefficients are (Tr rho P_b)^4; conjugation permutes these unsigned
Paulis and preserves the binary symplectic pairing. The fourth power removes
all signs, proving the stated covariance even for complex Clifford frames.

    Pr_J[BellDiff in a+H]

## 4. A literal finite acquisition procedure

Maintain the known binary span W and a list of accepted signed joint Paulis.
At each of2q stages draw at most

    M=ceil[2*4^(t+1) log(4q/delta)]

independent Bell differences. Reject a candidate immediately if its reference
label lies in W. Otherwise directly measure that full candidate Pauli on
fresh independent actual J copies. Estimate its +/-1 expectation to accuracy
gamma=tau/8, with failure at most delta/(2L) per tested candidate, where
L=2qM bounds all candidates. At most

    V=ceil[2 gamma^-2 log(4L/delta)]

copies per candidate suffice by Hoeffding. Accept iff the estimate's absolute
value is >=1-tau/2; put its estimated sign on the accepted Pauli, enlarge W,
and proceed. Return an explicit ABORT if a stage exhausts M candidates.

On all accurate validations, an accepted signed observable has expectation
>=1-5tau/8>=1-tau, with the correct sign. Every true S candidate passes,
since eta<=tau/8 makes its absolute expectation>=1-tau/4. By(3), at each
unfinished stage a good new-direction candidate has conditional probability
at least1/[2*4^(t+1)]. The failure across2q stages is at most delta/2; all
validation failures cost at most delta/2. No exponential union over possible
adaptive subspaces is used. Total actual Choi/source calls are bounded by

    4L+L V,

polynomial in q,4^t,1/tau,log(1/delta); classical work also pays n-bit masks,
sample records, binary elimination and the actual Clifford gate compiler.
This conservative Hoeffding bill can be improved with justified binomial
failure tests; it is not a source lower bound. This theorem supplies no
physical gate/measurement error warrant beyond the stated actual-source model.

## 6. Ideal Clifford rigidity removes the generic dimension loss

This separate transfer needs only N0=U(rho tensor sigma)U* with U Clifford
and arbitrary mixed sigma; pure stabilizers and small t were used to ACQUIRE
the frame, not in this rigidity argument. Suppose actual accepted correlations
are >=1-tau and 1-tau-2eta>0. Their IDEAL correlations are nonzero.

Conjugating any physical Pauli through U gives an input-logical Pauli tensor
a junk Pauli. Its correlation with a reference Pauli is zero unless its
logical label is exactly that reference Pauli's transpose, by Bell trace
orthogonality. Thus all2q independent selected labels match their logical
labels EXACTLY in the ideal encoding. After R, the Heisenberg images of all
logical Pauli generators have their original logical labels times junk Paulis.
The latter commute, since input and output logical symplectic relations agree.
Multiplication gives the SAME property for every logical Pauli. Tracing sigma
therefore makes R N0 a Pauli-DIAGONAL CPTP map, hence a Pauli channel.

For a Pauli channel, half diamond distance to the identity equals its nonidentity
Pauli weight, exactly1-F_e. The upper bound follows by the triangle inequality

    half||R N-id||_diamond
