# Full resource and execution boundary

The governing positive construction is C_COHERENT_FEEDBACK.md. Per payload
it consumes two NEW physical source calls, plus its original encoding if
that call is included in the total bill. One Bell programme can be made
offline; one source call must occur AFTER payload-dependent coherent
feedback. Preparing both online avoids advance programme storage but
adds another source latency to the payload's coherence interval.

Peak live physical history is three D-level carriers. The retained Pauli
index has two qubits, the group register needs three padded qubits, and
the SWAP-control flag is uncomputed before the second call. The final
known24->2 decoder may be completed to a32->2 channel on padding and
implemented with at most a64-dimensional discarded environment (a known
seven-qubit unitary suffices). Its precision/synthesis is finite known
classical work, not an unknown recovery factory. The source provider's
hidden workspace/preparation and absolute latency are NOT_ACQUIRED.

There are two full-register controlled SWAPs for sector computation and
uncomputation. A deliberately loose implementation of six group-controlled
permutations uses at most twelve more full-register controlled swaps.
Each costs O(logD) known elementary gates on a specified padded qubit
register. Logical amplitude preparation, group inversion/uniform-state
preparation and the final fixed decoder have constant dimension. Their
known matrix entries, relative phases and finite-control precision are
paid; no noisy-control/memory threshold or native runtime is acquired.
Two independent internal fair bits choose the optional Pauli twirl and
are discarded after correction. Fresh bits/programmes/junk are used for
later payloads. A shared exposed seed is a different channel contract.

## What actually ran

All source/hardware/quantum-circuit calls were ZERO. No stochastic Haar
grid, quantum state, artificial source trajectory, experimental data or
unknown-U numerical fixture was generated.

* check_exterior_hole.py checked exact output Choi contractions at
  r=1,2,3 for the first circuit, with its analytic all-r admission formula.
* evaluate_symmetric_branch.py performed one exact Q=r=32 partition/branch
  census for the analytic single-spin family:8349 partitions,43820 positive
  branches, with exact normalizations/moments. It is not a source run.
* relative_swap_feedback.py proposed final decoders for two fixed extreme
  phase choices on the SAME rank-two source family. Both were inferior
  and remain retained. Their solver outputs are not certificates.
* coherent_pauli_feedback.py made ONE final-decoder proposal for the
  successful coherent Pauli controller, then exact rational CP/TP repair.
  A syntax typo was fixed before that run emitted any receipt. The three
  floating proposals across these controller designs are the full solver
  count; none is a claimed exact optimum.
* receive_feedback_family.py checked the same saved decoder and extracted
  its SYMBOLIC all-r coefficients. A later exact marginal/shape guard was
  added and consumed unchanged data. No source/model/optimizer was rerun.
* receive_parallel_dual.py independently rebuilt root's existing SAME-case
  rational dual with stdlib Fractions. It imported no root producer and
  made no source/solver/RNG call.

Each receipt contains its actual after-import time. Total research,
reading, downloads, source-level debugging, Python imports and wall time
were not separately instrumented, so no whole-cycle runtime is invented.
Root's independent literal controller consumer and own optimization/dual
acquisition are additional work, not hidden in C's timings.

The original symmetric-phase proposal filenames are preserved as emitted
before later CLI sector naming. They describe an historical failed
controller, not the final coherent-Pauli operation. Input count/Pauli-seed
metadata additions did not change its exact accepted numerical values.
All provenance/cost labels distinguish a theoretical required source bill
from actual mathematical computation.

## What is not inferred

The all-r family is not a near-perfect inverse: its fidelity tends to1/4.
The rank-two exact adaptive gain does not imply linear-r high-accuracy
recovery, a uniform all-r advantage over parallel strategies, a repeated
independent-success law, a finite-temperature device or practical benefit.
The failed branches have no established restoring draw. Published pure
unitary/isometry and strong ordinary RDS/Petz operations retain their full
scope. Priority is UNKNOWN and physical acquisition is NOT_ACQUIRED.
