# Prior, failed interfaces, and the surviving question

The native target was exact success-or-draw inversion of the memoryless
flat mixed source, with encoded payload restored on a failure and all
paid history allowed. The accepted whole-instrument root theorem in
`root_comb/EXACT_HERALDING_SCHUR.md` settles its proposed subquadratic use:
exact capped success is at most Pr[Bin(Q,1/r)>=r], and any a.s. exact
Las Vegas inverse has expected query count at least r^2. C independently
reconstructed the latent-Kraus holomorphic zero, every Kraus gauge,
full-height environment support, adaptive replacer marginal, RSK and
stopping-time steps before the present approximate construction. Its
acceptance is an input here, not an invitation to reopen its correctness.

This includes a genuinely restoring failed branch with retained history.
It does NOT give a quadratic lower bound for approximate physical-query
decoding. The exact nonzero-success threshold is still only bracketed:
the general obstruction gives Q>=r, while C's exterior construction uses
2r-1 queries with success 1/[4 r^(2r) Catalan_r]. That small branch's
failure was discarded, not restored. We do not infer a 2r-1 lower bound.

The pure-source incumbents have their full scope. The unitary
success-or-draw supermap theorem of [Dong et al., arXiv:2011.01055v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.01055)
is a physical restoring-failure operation
for its stated unitary/supermap setting. The actual four-forward-query
qubit-isometry inverse in [arXiv:2401.10137v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10137)
solves the pure r=1 endpoint. Its
published off-code conversion is not a restoring inverse for a fresh
mixed payload: the admitted exact map is depolarizing with identity
weight 1/r^2 in the perfect-unitary case. Retaining different garbage or
using another circuit is not excluded by that observation. These primary
theorems were read at their governing equations in ROOT28; their cached
paths/hashes remain pinned, without relabeling them current-source news.

Root's fixed canonical memory-port identity gives a real known isometry
with an old M port and a fresh Bell query pair at its uniform point. It
does not restore the old data on M away from that point. Root correctly
keeps the full physical history in its unclosed controller and leaves
the scalable final decoder unpaid. Closing the old two-query logical
output is separately entanglement breaking at its actual saved
coefficients; its strict adaptive-versus-parallel fidelity gap survives,
but that closed output cannot seed quantum renewal.

C's present operation changes the history policy more carefully: keep
EVERY known reference and arbitrary known-memory processing, but discard
the spent physical programme after each relative-SWAP cell. This has an
EXACT finite logical recurrence, so no Gaussian, mixing, classical-sector,
or reset premise is inserted. The general A/B instrument and first-cross
bound show that this repair still requires quadratic-r query cost for
fixed high accuracy. The result applies to arbitrary known sector
isometries, not merely a tuned small-angle family or a particular final
decoder. It is an architecture obstruction, not another fixture.

The ordinary [density-matrix exponentiation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.0401)
/ Hamiltonian-matrix-exponentiation route
already acquires a 1/r signal against constant collision variance. ROOT26
paid its sharper source-conditioned second moment explicitly. The strong
flat RDS/Petz construction already supplies near-perfect recovery with
O(k^2 r^2/epsilon) offline forward calls and a consumed protected programme;
its online query latency is zero but its gates/storage remain real costs.
Thus a new quadratic construction would not be a capability advance.
The present result explains why this tempting physical history-disposal
variant cannot improve that rank scaling. It is not a speed claim and
does not compare its incomplete final extraction against a complete
ordinary circuit as if both were implementations.

Root's persistent-junk process gives a different positive route: if the
payload plus four source slots keep one hidden isometry, the ordinary
four-query isometry inverse works; the complete error is bounded by the
probability of a hidden-label change. That is a stronger JOINT oracle,
not a free reinterpretation of the same one-use channel. Its actual
physical retention/reset contract remains unacquired. Its frame-free
SWAP diagnostic does not by itself certify an arbitrary adaptive process.

The sharp surviving computational question is now to preserve or
transform physical source-frame information without erasing the
coherences needed for extraction, and still supply a competitive final
decoder. A different physical interaction, a compact retained physical
representation, or an acquired persistent-source architecture could
escape this theorem. General approximate near-linear-r forward-only
recovery remains OPEN. Priority of the present specialized theorem is
UNKNOWN; generic instrument, interference and Cauchy machinery is ordinary.
