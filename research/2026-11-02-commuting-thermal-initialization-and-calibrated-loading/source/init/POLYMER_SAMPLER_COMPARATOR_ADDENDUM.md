## Decisive established result
Chen–Galanis–Goldberg–Perkins–Stewart–Vigoda, *Fast algorithms at low temperatures via Markov chains*, arXiv1901.06653v6 (13 April2021), https://arxiv.org/pdf/1901.06653 . Definition1/Theorem2 uses precisely the proposed inequality sum_{eta incompatible gamma}|eta|w_eta ≤ theta|gamma| and gives single-polymer mixing O(N log(N/epsilon)). Definition3 demands membership and weight evaluation in poly(|gamma|). Definition4 strengthens small activity to w_gamma≤exp(−tau|gamma|), tau≥5+3log((q−1)Delta), on bounded-degree hosts with q labels. Theorem5 then gives sampling time O(N log(N/epsilon) log(1/epsilon)), without enumerating the full logarithmic-size dictionary. The host need not be a Euclidean lattice or an expander; those are applications, not general-theorem requirements. This is the credible cheaper alternative, conditional on the local algorithmic premises. Their real-weight computation model still needs an explicit finite-bit implementation charge.

## Mapping to the proposed operation (assessment)
Use marked objects as host vertices and stencil-overlap as edges. Trace components are connected labeled subsets; mutually compatible components have host distance at least two, matching the theorem's subset-polymer model. With N=O(n), fixed finite label alphabet, bounded host degree, and trace size m≤A|x_gamma|, the existing result applies at beta J_min/A at least its sampling threshold (after counting actual labels/degree).

This mapping does NOT establish any of the following for RX/RZ: a finite bounded-degree gate-level circuit, exact accepting-syndrome equivalence, unique splitting/recombination, uniform A, or local candidate validation. In particular, a global polynomial-time decoder does not imply poly(m)-time membership. If validating one small trace requires replaying an O(n)-size circuit, polynomial total sampling may remain possible with that extra charge, but the near-linear theorem cannot simply be quoted. A local certificate should check wire consistency, deterministic gate truth tables, rejecting flags and every boundary/fanout zero constraint using random-access local circuit data. This is a question for the binding audit, not an assumed feature.

A valid decoder that carries each isolated excitation through log n levels without sufficient annihilation/contraction can violate constant A. Non-LDPC metachecks are therefore not cured merely by calling the execution a polymer. The geometry/trace-energy contract is the useful mathematical content; the sampler is an established consumer.
