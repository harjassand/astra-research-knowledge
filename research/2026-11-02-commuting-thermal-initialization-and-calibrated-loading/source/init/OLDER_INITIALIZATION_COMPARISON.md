# Earlier finite-temperature initialization machinery

Primary source inspected by root: Bombin, Chhajlany, Horodecki and Martin-Delgado, *Self-Correcting Quantum Computers*, arXiv:0907.5228v2, https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.5228 .

Section VI.E supplies a joint syndrome/error contour argument for sudden local noise, not the present exact-H post-loading uniform-time theorem. Section VIII.A proposes external-field and code-deformation initialization. Its switching protocol explicitly assumes size-independent thermalization while restoring a protecting Hamiltonian sector. Section VIII.C charges gate-time energy injection qualitatively and requires cooling between operations. The paper distinguishes its rigorous memory/sudden-noise results from those further physical initialization arguments.

Use this as established machinery and a warning: an ideal transient followed by asserted cooling is not the missing return theorem. Our classical syndrome-sampler candidate is a constructive commuting-state alternative, but it still does not earn noisy physical return or generic interacting-bath preparation. No priority claim is made for thermal initialization, dressed logical operators, sudden-noise contours, or code deformation.
