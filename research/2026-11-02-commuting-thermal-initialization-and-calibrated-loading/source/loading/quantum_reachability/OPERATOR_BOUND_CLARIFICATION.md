# Operator-gas scope clarification

The independent receipt accepts the actual stabilizer H0 plus onsite bath reference, with memory-only Pauli noise. In that case the reference and its Pauli conjugate commute, so the stated local energy-change bound controls the similarity transform.

For a broader arbitrary commuting reference with non-Pauli local terms, use the SAFE bound B=2 sum of norms of reference terms incident on the fault support. Do not replace it by ||H_ref-P H_ref P|| without additional commutation. The independent receipt and executable counterexample preserve this distinction.

The positive quantum preimage theorem still supplies no preparation algorithm, bath access or logical-input fidelity. Sector-conditioned references and dimer constructions are separate exploratory interfaces, not a completed protected noisy memory.
