# Positive backward-noise thermalization: targeted prior/comparator gate
2026-11-01. Scope: supplied encoded input; known independent terminal Pauli channels; target commuting H0 thermal encoding. Positive efficient sampling remains a construction obligation.

## Closest primary mechanism
van Enter–Fernández–den Hollander–Redig, *Possible loss and recovery of Gibbsianness during the stochastic evolution of Gibbs measures*, arXiv math-ph/0105001v1, 2May2001 (PDF also prints a later dateline), https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0105001 . Theorem4.1 proves short-time FORWARD preservation of Gibbsianness for finite-range initial interactions and dynamics. Its independent-flip proof §4 pp14–15 uses connected error islands with activity epsilon^|gamma| exp[H(sigma^gamma)−H(sigma)], an exponential absolute activity bound, and uniformly convergent logarithmic cluster expansions for local density ratios. The general setup is a lattice spin configuration space, with stated translation-invariant finite-range rates. This is not a theorem asserting positive inverse-noise preparation, nor an efficient sampler. Nevertheless it is a very close existing derivation, not merely a shared keyword. “Backward process” elsewhere in this work is conditional time reversal, not the linear inverse of the forward channel.

## What is routine and what is not (assessment, not theorem import)
For an involution T and C=(1−q)I+qT, C^{-1}=((1−q)I−qT)/(1−2q). For multiple commuting translations, applying the inverse to a Gibbs density yields an error-set sum with activity −q/(1−q), times a positive prefactor. Consequently the proposed signed-polymer zero-freeness argument is a short analytic-continuation adaptation of established error-island cluster machinery, provided the actual energy-difference factorization and uniform absolute bounds are earned. A real nonzero finite-volume partition sum connected continuously to its positive q=0 value stays positive. Normalization then follows algebraically from C preserving total mass. This inference is not the stated forward theorem.

Finite-volume continuity alone is insufficient: the allowed q could shrink with n. The substantive estimate is a size-independent disk of zero-freeness for EVERY syndrome and EVERY inverse prefix. The constrained valid-syndrome space must be preserved by each physical Pauli translation; unsupported invalid configurations cannot be supplied by analytic notation. Logical correctness is separate from matching the syndrome marginal: the proposed rare-bad-path/decoder-frame lift is needed for arbitrary logical inputs and entangled references.

## Positive sampling is a separate gate
The signed error gas is an evaluation device, NOT a probability law to sample. Positivity of its total partition function does not make individual activities positive or prove efficient sampling of p_pre. A local log-density correction can in principle provide computable birth ratios for an existing positive trace-polymer representation. It must come with a uniform local increment bound, controlled influence tails, and a mixing proof for the INTERACTING positive gas. Theorem5 of Chen et al., https://arxiv.org/pdf/1901.06653 , applies to nonnegative factorized hard-core polymer weights with its decay/computability premises; it cannot be imported unchanged after adding arbitrary interactions between trace polymers. A controlled perturbative extension is plausible, but must be quantified. Existing ordinary Gibbs-state deconvolution of observables is not a same-capability dominator.

## Cheap sanity baseline and calibration charge
For one unconstrained Bernoulli excitation of target probability p, terminal flip probability q gives required pre-probability (p−q)/(1−2q). Positivity requires q≤p≤1−q. This exactly exhibits why an allowed noise level below the low-temperature excitation scale is necessary, rather than an unusually generous fault threshold. It also explains the construction as pre-cooling/pre-distortion, not physical inversion of arbitrary noise.

If the calibrated channel is C_hat and actual channel is C, and the prepared pre-state has trace error delta_p, then the actual output error is bounded by delta_p+||C−C_hat||_diamond, in addition to logical-lift error. For independent single-Pauli flips, telescoping yields ||C−C_hat||_diamond≤2 sum_i|q_i−qhat_i|. Thus O(delta/n) per-site accuracy suffices for a global delta target. Exact Gibbs output requires exact calibration; fixed unknown miscalibration is not covered. This bound may be conservative for logical memory, but a sharper admission theorem is needed before relaxing it.

## Fair active comparison
With only a supplied encoded input, classical randomization, allowed Pauli preparation operations and one unavoidable terminal noise layer, active fault tolerance does not automatically provide an EXACT physical Gibbs output either. A correction round after that layer changes the timing/access contract. Extra encoding followed by an ideal final decoder also adds resources. Therefore generic active FT is not a tautological dominator of this exact mixed-state-output operation.

With noisy syndrome measurements, resets, local gates and continued feedback allowed equally, conventional FT can protect the supplied logical input without exact thermal-state calibration. That is already a stronger route for logical retention/control under a broader noise model, while consuming continuing control/entropy export. It does not make exact physical Gibbs-state synthesis free. Compare total loading cost and ultimate passive-storage admission, not just the exact marginal identity.

## Judgment
No inspected primary theorem already supplies this complete constrained-syndrome positive-sampling plus logical-output contract. But the signed inverse/zero-free positivity principle strongly overlaps standard short-time Gibbs perturbation machinery and should not be marketed as a new general paradigm. The possible useful increment is a certified noisy thermal loader for this code, IF polynomial positive sampling and physical reconstruction are earned. It remains substantially short of generic coherent noisy loading or preparation of the interacting memory–bath Gibbs state. Stop further novelty search here; the decisive work is the actual sampler/physical admission and its calibrated-resource benchmark.
