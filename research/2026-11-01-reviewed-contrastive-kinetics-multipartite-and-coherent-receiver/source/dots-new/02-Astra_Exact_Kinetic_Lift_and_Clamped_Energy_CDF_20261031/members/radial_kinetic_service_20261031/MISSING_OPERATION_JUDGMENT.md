# What this kinetic boundary earns, and where the next hard step really is

## Useful result first

For the acquired homogeneous radial-plus-tangent generator, the entire (Y,U)
path law is independent of unknown angular dynamics and the angular shape of U,
once alpha, initial energy, beta and longitudinal profile are fixed. Unlike the
previous conductance-only bounds, this equality covers every observable stopping
time/functional defined on (Y,U) and every initial angular configuration. Full
transverse SPD is allowed away from the unvisited origin. Arbitrarily slow,
strong or nonmixing tangent motion does not invalidate the reduction.

This is a meaningful exact observable-autonomy statement, but it requires an
actual special mobility. It is built from ordinary Markov-generator closure,
Euler identities, divergence-form dynamics, squared-Bessel boundary theory and
known thermostat machinery. Historical novelty is unresolved. It is not a new
CIR engine, an improvement over ordinary Gaussian radial reduction, or evidence
of civilization-changing physical access.

## Finite execution, not a formal generator alone

The first complete mathematical consumer reads a raw positive homogeneous
energy program and its declared all-pair tangent implementation. It computes
terminal energy probabilities for an externally specified clamp protocol with
outward exact arithmetic. For m=512,p=4 the potential contains 1,023 monomials;
523,776 exponent entries are actually scanned. Changing angular interaction
strength from20 to10^100 and tangent weight from1 to10^80 gives the IDENTICAL
terminal probability enclosure

    [0.09834889399763794, 0.09834889400961049]

at threshold64 after clamp positions (-1,0,1/2) held for times (1/3,1/2,2/3),
beta=kappa=1, initial z=e1. The requested absolute width is10^-10; all intervals
are exact rationals and verified again from the original raw task. Timings are
~0.06s per production case, not controlled performance benchmarks. The scalar
work is1,799 guarded operations,7 exponential calls,283 Taylor terms and16
Poisson mixture terms. Actual physical derivative evaluation, noise generation,
clamping, tangent integration and model identification are NOT executed.

For a noncentral initial energy256 the separate replay used842 Poisson terms
across its two bounds,6,659 guarded operations. Thus the scalar service honestly
pays noncentrality rather than pretending every query has fixed cost.

The ordinary noncentral-Gamma engine already wins this finite scalar task.
Its role here is to demonstrate that the received exact lift can feed an actual
kinetic query. Implementing more scalar special functions or more admitted
polynomial examples would not by itself be the strongest next scientific step.

## Genuine failures and missing operation

The identical potential under ordinary Euclidean mobility generally does not
close on energy. For U=z1^4+16z2^4+z3^4+z4^4, the points e1 and e2/2 both have
U=1, but |grad U|^2 is16 versus64 and (Delta U-|grad U|^2) is-4 versus-16.
The actual instantaneous energy laws differ. No equilibrium-partition identity,
known alpha, or accurate conductance certificate repairs this missing kinetic
information. The constructed tensor is a substantive model/control premise.

The autonomous process dY=-v'(Y)u dt+sqrt(2/beta)dW coupled to the CIR energy
cannot be simulated exactly by applying a frozen-y CIR transition at finite
steps and calling the result exact. A finite-horizon or mean first-passage query
needs a separately paid two-dimensional solver, time/path error control,
origin and infinite-domain treatment, initialization and rare-event accuracy.
The present clamp protocol changes the task explicitly and does not solve that
harder autonomous operation. No MFPT or numerical autonomous trajectory is
included in this result.

A bounded-domain PDE/Dynkin barrier construction might provide a rigorous next
consumer. However, naive Lyapunov tail truncation over a rare-event horizon can
be prohibitively expensive, and a two-dimensional representation alone does
not prove polynomial rare-event complexity. Standard verified PDE, potential
theory, importance sampling and CIR splitting methods must receive exactly the
same model information and costs. A superior solver must earn its accuracy,
not infer it from dimensionality reduction.

Finally, no finite collection of generic local physical probes certifies that
an unknown system obeys the exact global mobility/energy grammar. The accepted
robustness information obstruction remains relevant. To use the law in an
experiment, one needs a justified structural model or a paid calibration/error
interface for actual drift, covariance, radial actuation and initialization.
A construction at that interface, or a truly useful certified autonomous latency
consumer, is a more consequential next question than expanding this grammar.
