every satellite has at least one arm. The hub graph must be complete multipartite

complement graph acquires the parts from the raw reaction list.

For part totals Z_j, set M=max Z_j, R=N-M and P=(1+eta)R-eta A. The construction is

    W=t^N [1+1/P-epsilon A/(N+1)]      when R>=1,

Both eta and epsilon are acquired positive rational constants. Near an inactive
single-part face, the weighted inverse deficit earns fast seed-amplification drift.

from hub reverse reactions, while its positive capture drift is paid by actual
A-death. All ties, finite counts and loss of the last active seed are included.

For arbitrary positive rates in this class, the theorem proves full-orthant
irreducibility, positive recurrence and every finite exponential stationary
