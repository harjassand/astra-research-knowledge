CONTROLLED KINETICS AROUND A SOLVABLE INTERACTING REFERENCE
Root construction, 1 November 2026

Status and scientific meaning
-----------------------------
This is a constructive mathematical result and a partially implemented algorithm.
It is not a demonstrated new material, a general molecular simulator, or an
established historical-priority claim. Its primitives have substantial prior art:
reversible return identities, free-fermion/matchgate contraction, preconditioned
inverse approximation, exponential sums, and tensor methods for absorption.

The particular synthesis constructed here evaluates scalar kinetic inverse moments
by finite words of local operators inserted into an interacting Gaussian reference.
It avoids requiring a compressible full solution vector. At fixed interaction
contrast, locality and requested accuracy, it has polynomial arithmetic work in
system size and in the logarithm of spectral stiffness. This is a more precise claim than
'rare events without waiting', which an ordinary target-start estimator also earns.
The constants and accuracy exponent are serious limitations, written below.

1. Physical model and input contract
-----------------------------------
There are n (even, n>=4) spins on a periodic chain with energy

    beta H(sigma) = -sum_i J_i sigma_i sigma_(i+1).

J_i in this note already includes beta. Couplings may have either sign. Local
attempt frequencies nu_i are strictly positive, finite, and explicitly supplied.
No unknown force law, measured response or equilibrating helper is supplied.
The service is a calculation from this exact model, not a measurement of a material.
For bit complexity, inputs have finite effective encodings with certified p-bit
evaluation cost polynomial in their encoded length and p. Arbitrary real-number
oracles are not admitted. Local rate tables and elementary functions of rational
encoded parameters can be evaluated under this contract.

Use domain walls eta_i=(1-sigma_i sigma_(i+1))/2. The wall count is even. Quotienting
the two global orientations gives exactly 2^(n-1) states. This lumping is exact for
zero-field spin flips. The target b is no domain walls: EITHER uniform orientation,
not a specified one of them. Adding a field or resolving global orientation requires
a different construction and is not silently covered.

A spin flip toggles neighboring walls i,j=i+1. Its heat-bath rates are

    c_i = nu_i/(1+exp(2(J_i+J_j)))       for 00 -> 11,
    a_i = nu_i-c_i                       for 11 -> 00,
    d_i = nu_i/(1+exp(2(J_j-J_i)))       for 10 -> 01,
    e_i = nu_i-d_i                       for 01 -> 10.

They obey a_i+c_i=d_i+e_i=nu_i. The stationary wall law is proportional to
exp(-2 sum_i J_i eta_i), restricted to even parity. In particular

    pi_b = 2 / {prod_i(1+exp(-2J_i)) [1+prod_i tanh(J_i)]}.

The initial law in the main theorem is exactly pi conditioned on eta != b.
This is a defined theoretical initial distribution. No physical preparation of
this law is claimed. The output is its mean first arrival at b.

Now change local rates by a multiplier A_i(eta) that depends on at most r wall
coordinates and is identical at both endpoints of its flip. Let ell be the total
number of coordinates in a complete modified generator term, including both
flipped sites; ell<=r+2:

    A_i(eta)=A_i(eta xor {i,i+1}),    0<m <= A_i <= M < infinity.

The Gibbs law is therefore unchanged by detailed balance. All local tables and
their bounds can be checked at cost O(n 2^ell). The multipliers may destroy the
free-fermion structure; the executable uses A_i=1+epsilon eta_(i+2), and a separate
audit uses A_i=exp[0.7 z_(i+2) z_(i+3)], z=2eta-1. These are genuinely coupled
kinetics, not independent coordinates. They do not change equilibrium energies.

2. The reference operation is earned
------------------------------------
Write column-probability generator Q0, Pi=diag(pi), g_x=sqrt(pi_x), and

    K0=-Pi^(-1/2) Q0 Pi^(1/2).

K0 is symmetric, nonnegative, with kernel span(g). The Jordan-Wigner representation
of Q0 has the form

    Q0_sym = c0 + f^dag D f + (f^dag A f^dag)/2 - (f A f)/2,

with D symmetric and A antisymmetric. For an interior edge i,j=i+1,

    D_ii += c_i-d_i,  D_jj += c_i-e_i,
    D_ij = D_ji += sqrt(d_i e_i),
    A_ij = -A_ji += sqrt(c_i a_i),  c0=-sum_i c_i.

Across the periodic boundary multiply the off-diagonal hopping and oriented pairing
coefficients by -1 in the even sector. The quartic diagonal term cancels precisely
because a+c=d+e. This cancellation is checked independently against a generator
constructed directly from spin energy differences.

Diagonalizing the real symmetric 2n by 2n Bogoliubov matrix gives positive energies
epsilon_j and canonical matrices U,V. In quasiparticle variables d, K0=sum_j
epsilon_j d_j^dag d_j. The physical even-sector gap is epsilon_1+epsilon_2.
A conservative upper spectral bound is Lambda=2 sum_i nu_i. Small eigenvalues need
adequate precision; the six-site stiff stress test demonstrates that double precision
can falsely replace a ~1.5e-40 gap by ~1.7e-15.

Let Z=-U^(-1)V, with the conventions used in gaussian_kinetics.py, and
E(t)=diag(exp(-epsilon_j t)). The no-wall state expanded in quasiparticles gives

    R(t)=P_b(X_t=b)
        = pi_b sqrt det[I + Z^T E(t) Z E(t)]
        = pi_b sqrt det[I + W(t)^T W(t)],

    W(t)=E(t)^(1/2) Z E(t)^(1/2).

The formula is a Gaussian overlap, not a new free-fermion theorem. Its stable
ground-state form avoids multiplying an exponentially growing determinant by a
vanishing scalar. It costs polynomial work in n at each t, not 2^(n-1). The exact
normalization at t=0 checks pi_b independently against the Gibbs formula.

3. A return identity and its strong conventional competitor
---------------------------------------------------------
For any finite irreducible reversible chain with the same notation,

    G=K^+ = integral_0^infinity [exp(-Kt)-g g^T] dt,
    W=<b,G b>=integral_0^infinity [P_b(X_t=b)-pi_b]dt,
    mean = W/[pi_b(1-pi_b)].                                  (1)

Proof: first-arrival renewal gives G_xb(s)=F_xb(s)G_bb(s). For stationary initial
conditions, the numerator is pi_b/s. Split G_bb(s)=pi_b/s+R_s, differentiate the
result at s=0, and remove the initial mass pi_b at b. Equivalently this follows
from the reversible Poisson equation. This identity and broader relaxation-to-
first-passage duality are conventional.

The conventional alternative is important. Starting simulated paths at b, observe
L_T=integral_0^T 1_b(X_t)dt. Then E[L_T]-pi_b T is the truncated W. Given a gap g0,
the remaining tail is at most (1-pi_b)exp(-g0 T)/g0. Since W >=(1-pi_b)/Lambda,
a horizon O(g0^-1 log[Lambda/(delta g0)]) controls relative truncation error.
The Markov occupation convolution gives E[L_T^2] <=2(E[L_T])^2. In the rare-target
regime, fixed-confidence relative accuracy therefore needs O(delta^-2) independent
paths, not 1/pi_b paths. This alternative was executed on the actual 256-site
nonintegrable model. Rare-waiting independence is NOT a distinctive contribution.

Its event-by-event cost can depend on Lambda/g0. Exact local recycling (MCAMC),
adiabatic elimination, and tensor solvers can remove some or all of this burden in
particular systems. No lower bound against every conventional algorithm is claimed.

4. Scalar inverse approximation without a full solution vector
-----------------------------------------------------------
Let K be the perturbed symmetrized generator, on H=g^perp. Detailed balance and the
edgewise multiplier bounds give the quadratic-form comparison

    m K0 <= K <= M K0.                                       (2)

Set c=(m+M)/2, R=(c K0)^+, E=K-cK0, and

    C=R^(1/2) E R^(1/2),  rho=(M-m)/(M+m)<1.

On H, ||C||<=rho and

    W=<v,(I+C)^(-1)v>,  v=R^(1/2)|b>.

The degree-k Neumann scalar approximation is

    W_k=sum_(j=0)^k (-1)^j <b,R(E R)^j b>.

THEOREM 1. |W_k-W| <= rho^(k+1) W.

Proof: the remainder is (-C)^(k+1)(I+C)^(-1). These commuting symmetric operators
have a common spectral resolution. For every spectral value x in [-rho,rho],
|x^(k+1)/(1+x)| <=rho^(k+1)/(1+x). Integrate against the positive scalar spectral
measure of v. This sharper relative result avoids bounding numerator and denominator
separately. It is independent of pi_b and the reference gap.

If rho=0, K=cK0 and the reference inverse already gives the exact answer. The
following polynomial formula is used only for 0<rho<1.

A faster degree-k polynomial uses the classical relative Chebyshev inverse:

    q_(k+1)(x)=T_(k+1)(-x/rho)/T_(k+1)(1/rho),
    p_k(x)=[1-q_(k+1)(x)]/(1+x).

The numerator vanishes at x=-1, so p_k is a polynomial. Replacing the inverse by
p_k(C) has relative scalar error at most

    Delta_k=1/|T_(k+1)(1/rho)|
           = 2 r0^(k+1)/(1+r0^(2k+2)),
    r0=(sqrt(M)-sqrt(m))/(sqrt(M)+sqrt(m)).                    (3)

The Chebyshev approximation itself is classical. Here its scalar moments are
computed without a full 2^(n-1)-component Krylov vector or an assumed low tensor rank.

5. What each scalar moment costs
-------------------------------
Decompose E=sum_(i=1)^s E_i into complete local modifications (s<=n for a single
local term at each site). Each E_i is symmetric and annihilates g, separately.
Each has a fermion/Majorana expansion of at most 4^ell local monomials. Diagonal
spectators add occupations; the flipped pair is adjacent, with the fixed parity
boundary sign retained, so no uncharged Jordan-Wigner string is introduced.
Endpoint-invariance makes the multiplier commute with its local heat-bath term,
whose norm is nu_i. Consequently ||E_i||<=(M-m)nu_i/2. A conservative sum of
Majorana coefficient magnitudes is at most 4^ell ||E_i||, obtainable directly
from its finite table. Local expansion magnitudes are therefore paid inputs.

For j>=1, the required scalar coefficient is the (j+1)-fold time integral of

    <b, P(t0) E_i1 P(t1) ... E_ij P(tj) b>,

summed over i1,...,ij; P(t)=exp(-c K0 t). Since every complete E_i annihilates g,
stationary projectors drop out between these complete local terms. Crucially,
individual monomials need not annihilate g. Their separate infinite integrals can
diverge. All monomials in a local word must be summed at common finite quadrature
nodes before invoking convergence or a tail bound.

Each time-ordered word is an ordinary Gaussian amplitude with at most O(ell j)
insertions. Wick/Pfaffian evaluation is polynomial in n and ell j, times the 4^(ell j)
monomial expansion. This is the representation change: expand the local departure
from solvability, and contract the interacting reference exactly.

For a direct cutoff proof, let B=sum_i ||E_i|| and let g0 be the reference gap.
The omitted part outside [0,T]^(j+1) is at most

    (j+1)(1-pi_b) B^j exp(-c g0 T)/(c g0)^(j+1).

Together with W >=(1-pi_b)/(M Lambda), this gives an explicit finite cutoff. It
does not imply that one must simulate up to this possibly enormous physical time.

6. Exponential quadrature and polylogarithmic stiffness dependence
-------------------------------------------------
For x in [g,Lambda], approximate 1/x=int_0^infinity exp(-xt)dt with a positive
geometric left sum. For 0<eta<1 choose

    t_min=eta/(4 Lambda), t_max=log(4/eta)/g,
    t_(l+1)=(1+eta/2)t_l, w_l=t_(l+1)-t_l,

ending at the first t_L>=t_max. Then

    (1-eta/2)/x <= sum_l w_l exp(-x t_l) <=(1+eta/2)/x.       (4)

The lower bound follows by discarding initial and terminal integral tails, each
at most eta/(4x). For the upper bound, w_l exp(-x t_l) is at most
(1+eta/2) times the integral over [t_l/(1+eta/2),t_l]. Those intervals are disjoint.
Thus the node count is

    q=O(eta^-1[log(Lambda/g)+log(1/eta)+log log(1/eta)]).       (5)

This is elementary exponential-sum machinery, not an invention of exponential
quadrature. Importantly its count depends on log(Lambda/g), not Lambda/g.

Apply this to R on H. Use centered semigroups; retain the exact stationary
projection algebraically. Let Rtilde approximate R relatively by eta (use the
slightly looser eta notation). The computed polynomial moments can be interpreted
as exact moments for Ktilde=Rtilde^(-1)+E. On H,

    ||K^(-1/2)(Ktilde-K)K^(-1/2)||
           <= d=eta/[(1-eta)(1-rho)].

For d<1 the relative inverse quadratic-form error is at most d/(1-d). Also the
new preconditioned E norm is at most (1+eta)rho. Use that enlarged interval in (3).
Hence eta=O(delta(1-rho)) controls quadrature independently of the reference gap.
There is no hidden requirement eta=O(g/Lambda).
For a concrete conservative allocation with 0<delta<=1, take
eta<=delta(1-rho)/16, assign the enlarged-interval polynomial error at most delta/4,
and reserve the remaining error budget for arithmetic and input evaluation.

THEOREM 2 (constructive work statement). With s complete local perturbation terms,
fixed total locality ell, fixed contrast M/m, and fixed relative target accuracy delta,
the mean (1) is computable by finite sums with work bounded schematically by

    sum_(j=0)^k s^j 4^(ell j) q^(j+1) poly(n,j,ell,precision), (6)

where k comes from (3), q from (5), and the finite arithmetic precision can be
chosen polynomial in the input bit length, n, k, log(Lambda/g), log(1/delta),
and log[1/(pi_b(1-pi_b))]. The required factorization is specified by a backward
operator residual, not relative accuracy of every single-particle eigenvalue or
eigenvector. Thus the construction is polynomial in n and in log(Lambda/g)
at fixed ell, contrast and accuracy. It is NOT
a fully polynomial scheme in accuracy or contrast. At s=n, n^k can be impractical.
At fixed s (a few nonintegrable defects in an extended interacting reference),
the word count can be substantially smaller.

7. Why numerical conditioning has not been moved into an oracle
--------------------------------------------------------------
Naive Bogoliubov charts and Heisenberg factors exp(+epsilon t) can be ill conditioned.
The executable deliberately demonstrates a failure of double precision at a gap
far below machine resolution, then recovers the gap and a nontrivial slow plateau
using 90-digit Decimal algebra. This is a precision test, not a large-system win.

The proof has three parts: a backward residual for the quadratic generator,
absolute accuracy for each Gaussian amplitude, and an explicit signed-sum budget.
Normalize c Lambda to one for this argument; restore physical units afterwards.

Let T be the largest quadrature time and A_abs the sum of absolute coefficients of
all weighted local/source words, including the polynomial coefficients. This is a
finite computable upper bound. For a centered source f, use the positive lower
bound W_lower=||f||^2/(M Lambda). Factor the quadratic reference canonically with
full operator residual ||cK0-Khat0||<=zeta. Retain its scalar term and physical
parity sector. A matrix-coefficient residual can be converted to this operator
bound by summing the absolute coefficients of the quadratic Majorana terms; this
costs at most a polynomial factor in n. For t<=T, Duhamel's identity gives

    ||exp(-cK0 t)-exp(-Khat0 t)|| <= t zeta exp(t zeta).

Choose zeta <= min{c g0/10, const * delta W_lower/[A_abs (k+1) T]}, with a
sufficiently small fixed constant. A telescoping product bounds the error in the
whole signed sum. A certified positive lower spectral bound is acquired by
refining the symmetric factorization until its operator residual resolves the
physical even-sector gap; this adds logarithmic gap precision, not a supplied
spectral oracle.
There is no need to resolve individual eigenvectors across a near degeneracy, or
to approximate every single-particle energy relatively. Some single-particle
energies can be much smaller than the physical even-sector gap. Approximate
eigenvectors and energies suffice when their reconstructed Hamiltonian has the
stated residual. Any rounding-induced negative scalar or discarded unresolved
energy is charged in that same residual. Centering uses the exact projection
algebraically; its numerical evaluation and the ground-state source can be
controlled using the physical even-sector gap and their residuals. Parity is never
inferred by ignoring an unresolved eigenvalue.

In canonical coordinates an attenuation diag(1,exp(-epsilon t)) is a vacuum
matrix element of a fermionic beam splitter with a fresh auxiliary mode and
cos(theta)=exp(-epsilon t). Use fresh modes at every semigroup segment. The scalar
factor is retained separately; its possible growth is bounded by exp(T zeta).
Each even Majorana monomial is a norm-one Gaussian unitary, including its known
phase. Defer all auxiliary vacuum postselections to the endpoint. One word is then
a phase-sensitive Fock amplitude of a unitary Gaussian circuit on N=O(n(j+1))
modes, multiplied by known scalars. Auxiliary modes are arithmetic variables,
not a supplied physical reservoir. Gaussian endpoints are prepared by canonical
Gaussian rotations, with their phase fixed; general sources incur the explicit
finite term expansion in section 10.

Bounded circuit entries alone would not prove a stable contraction. Here is a
constructive chart algorithm. After each two-mode gate, compute a Fock occupation
string by successively choosing a conditional mode outcome of probability at
least 1/2. Finite precision can use the slack threshold 1/3. Each conditional
state remains Gaussian. The selected Fock probability is at least 3^(-N), so its
amplitude is at least 3^(-N/2). In the Thouless chart with that string as vacuum,
all singular values of the normalized U block are at most one, while
|det U| equals this Fock probability. Hence sigma_min(U)>=3^(-N). The chart inverse
has at most exponential magnitude and requires only polynomial extra bits.

Track the reference amplitude and phase, not just the covariance. A two-mode gate
makes a new reference amplitude a sum of at most four old Fock amplitudes. Each
is the old reference amplitude times a Pfaffian from its chart. Use maximum-entry
complete-pivot Pfaffian elimination with an absolute-error budget, or approximate
the chart by rational entries and evaluate its Pfaffian by exact fraction-free
elimination. The latter route gives a direct finite-bit algorithm: coefficients
of minors have polynomial bit length, so the polynomial number of arithmetic
operations has polynomial bit cost. The chart entries are bounded exponentially
in N, and the Pfaffian is a degree-N/2 polynomial with at most (N-1)!! terms.
A conservative polynomial perturbation bound therefore costs O(N^2) extra
bits per update, plus the requested absolute accuracy and gate-count budget.
This remains polynomial even with a conservative accumulation over all gates.
No relative accuracy is demanded of a desired amplitude that happens to be zero
or nearly zero. Its absolute error is budgeted before the signed sum. Conditional
covariance updates also use probabilities bounded below by 1/3; their accumulated
rounding error is covered by the same polynomial precision budget. This supplies
an absolute-error algorithm rather than assuming that a Pfaffian has a good chart.

For order j, absolute coefficient amplification is bounded by (sum_l w_l)^(j+1)
times powers of the sum of local monomial magnitudes, source coefficient sums,
and the explicit polynomial coefficients. Since sum_l w_l=O(g^-1 log(1/eta)),
log A_abs=O((k+1)log(Lambda/g)+k ell+k log s), plus source, contrast, accuracy and
coefficient bits. Allocate each amplitude error at most const*delta W_lower/A_abs.
The preceding bounds make both log(1/zeta) and that absolute-error precision
polynomial in the quantities charged in theorem 2 and in the source condition
number. This is not an operation count proportional to 1/g. Arithmetic on the
large time arguments costs their bit length. Very small attenuations may be set
to zero only with this same absolute-error budget.

The current code implements real first-insertion Wick contractions and a separate
high-precision small-system stress test. It does NOT implement this complete
high-order phase-tracked arbitrary-precision circuit backend. Its ordinary double
precision logistic rates are evaluated in complementary form to avoid subtraction
loss; extreme couplings, nearly singular charts, or underflow still require the
arbitrary-precision route and stable partition arithmetic. Numerical checks are
limited to their reported parameter ranges.

8. A useful one-insertion enclosure
---------------------------------
For A_i=1+epsilon eta_(i+2), epsilon>=0, write K=K0+epsilon E1, where
0<=E1<=K0. Let W0=<b,G0 b>, C1=<b,G0 E1 G0 b>, mu=C1/W0 in [0,1].
The spectral theorem represents W/W0 as E[1/(1+epsilon X)] for X in [0,1]
with E[X]=mu. Jensen's inequality and the endpoint secant give

    W0/(1+epsilon mu) <= W <= W0[1-epsilon mu/(1+epsilon)].   (7)

Both moments are earned from the Gaussian reference. C1 is a two-time integral
of quartic local insertions, evaluated in the root code without configuration
enumeration. Equation (7) is conventional convexity applied to those new computed
inputs; it is not a substitute for the complete cost statement.

9. Executed scope and boundaries
-------------------------------
The reference implementation runs through n=256, where the even-wall space has
2^255 configurations. Its computed mean is about 3.7428e127 microscopic time units
for J=-0.4, nu=1. This reference is conventionally soluble; the number is not the
scientific advance.

The genuinely nonintegrable first insertion was computed without enumeration at
n=64, including a heterogeneous clock example with two fast updates and 62 slower
ones. Independent dense generators check the reference, gap, full Nambu
contractions, local insertion, physical mean, Neumann and Chebyshev inequalities.
Numerical quadrature refinement supports the first-insertion values. These are
floating point checks; displayed numerical enclosures are evaluations of proved
operator bounds, not end-to-end directed-rounding certificates.

The current implementation does not execute all local words at high order on
large n. The theorem constructs that algorithm and pays its full word count.

Hard constraints m=0, unbounded M/m, unknown energies/rates, equilibrium-changing
perturbations, arbitrary initial laws, arbitrary target predicates, and arbitrary
three-dimensional molecular Hamiltonians are outside the result. No all-deadline
relative risk bound follows merely from a mean or a real-axis Laplace transform.

Local physical reactions conditioned on r sites generally have configuration-space
rank 2^(n-r). A handful of named local reactions does not become a low-rank boundary.
Resolved Gaussian jump trajectories are a separate conventional route; unresolved
channels can break Gaussian posterior closure. Neither is used as an ideal helper.

10. A second physical service: equilibrium Poisson response
---------------------------------------------------------
The construction is not intrinsically limited to a singleton target. For any
centered observable A, set f=(A-<A>_pi)g. Reversibility gives

    chi_A=integral_0^infinity Cov_pi(A(X_t),A(X_0))dt=<f,K^+ f>.

Theorems 1 and 2 apply with b replaced by f, because their error argument uses a
positive scalar spectral measure, not a target-state identity. If f is an explicitly
given polynomial number of bounded-degree fermionic monomials applied to a Gaussian
state, the same finite Gaussian word contractions earn its moments. Charge the
number L of source terms (giving an L^2 pair expansion) and their coefficient
precision. Centering and ||f|| must also be calculated, not supplied as ideal
observations. A relative target requires chi_A>0; nearly cancelling source terms
cost precision relative to their coefficient sum, as specified below. The centered
energy source below is already an
orthogonal two-quasiparticle expansion. Do not infer a response for
an arbitrary observable merely because it has a short verbal description.

More explicitly, if f=sum_l a_l phi_l with ||phi_l||<=1, charge
kappa_f=(sum_l |a_l|)^2/||f||^2. The additional signed-source precision is
O(log kappa_f); it is not automatically bounded by the number L of terms.

For the concrete dimensionless spin energy (up to its constant)

    A=2 sum_i J_i eta_i,

let B=U^T diag(2J_i) V and F=B-B^T. The centered source in the reference ground
state is sum_(j<k) F_jk d_j^dag d_k^dag |g>. Therefore

    Var_pi A=sum_(j<k) |F_jk|^2,
    chi_A,0=sum_(j<k) |F_jk|^2/(epsilon_j+epsilon_k).

This acquires a second physical observable with polynomial-size spectral data.
poisson_observables.py verifies it against independent dense inverses and applies
the perturbed inverse expansion to the actual nonintegrable dynamics. It also
evaluates disordered references through n=256. The service is integrated equilibrium
energy noise in a bath-coupled model. It is not thermal conductivity: obtaining a
transport coefficient from a difference of traffic and Poisson terms may require
additional cancellation control, conservation laws and a physical thermodynamic
limit. Those have not been earned here.

11. Scientific decision
-----------------------
The surviving construction is a rigorous, vector-free expansion around a solvable
INTERACTING reference, including a polylogarithmic-stiffness cost route. The individual
operator tricks are conventional and the broad novelty question is unresolved.
Existing tensor absorption solvers, target-start sampling and multiscale averaging
remain strong competitors. No total practical advantage over the best of them has
been earned. This work establishes a bounded mathematical opening and executable
ingredients. It does not yet establish the civilisation-changing foundation sought
by the mandate. A substantial next bridge would have to control extensive interaction
words or much stronger kinetic/energetic departures without exponential costs.
