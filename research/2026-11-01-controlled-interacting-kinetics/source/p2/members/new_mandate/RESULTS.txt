ASTRA — A NEW INVESTIGATION INTO COMPUTABLE LONG-TIME PHYSICS
1 November 2026

RESULT

I constructed a conditional computational principle for long-time physical response:
use an exactly tractable INTERACTING reference process, retain its full dynamical
memory algebraically, and calculate bounded nonintegrable changes through scalar
operator-insertion moments. The reference is not required to be a collection of
independent particles, and the calculation does not require a compressible full
many-body solution vector.

The central theorem supplies controlled mean first-arrival times and equilibrium
Poisson responses. At fixed locality, kinetic contrast and accuracy, its formal work
is polynomial in system size and polylogarithmic in the reference spectral stiffness.
I constructed the reference, the perturbative scalar contraction, ordinary proofs,
finite numerical checks, a large reference calculation, a large first-insertion
calculation, and a high-precision stiffness failure/recovery.

This is a bounded mathematical opening. I have NOT established the requested
civilisation-changing foundation, historical novelty, a general molecular simulator,
or a total practical advantage over the strongest existing methods. Strong positive
competitors repeatedly changed the question and remain included. The next serious
obstruction is the number of interaction words needed for extensive or strongly
nonintegrable matter, not an unmeasured molecular helper.

THE CONSEQUENTIAL QUESTION

Can long-time changes and low-frequency response of interacting matter be calculated
from microscopic laws without storing its exponentially large state space or
simulating every fast event through its slowest relaxation time?

A broad solution would improve the ability to predict aging, collective assembly,
fluctuations and transport before waiting for those processes. That broad capability
is not supplied by the present one-dimensional model. The construction is intended
as a possible mathematical bridge, with the limits kept explicit.

WHY THE FIRST QUESTIONS WERE REPLACED

Weighted ensemble already preserves kinetics under arbitrary adaptive bins; exact
milestoning already retains phase-point/time information without an arbitrary
memory-loss assumption. A conventional solver here computed competing pathway fluxes
on a public double-path potential. A contact/reaction-coordinate simplification
gave the wrong pathway weights, but the conventional full generator fixed it.

Current Astra at commit 7ccbd09b6852ed12a3e54ca2bb131f5b2e5d218c has a stronger
high-dimensional restricted construction than that low-dimensional example: a
well-source current obtains actual mean passage and absolute exponential-law control
for a full-Gibbs-prepared homogeneous quartic family without angular mixing. Its
full-domain source and preparation conditions are preserved, not imported into the
new model. Exact governing sources and hashes are in conventional/astra/.

The subsequent 'compact return law gives a rare lifetime' idea is also conventional.
More decisively, an ordinary target-start occupation estimator can supply the same
mean service without rare first-arrival waiting. It was run on the actual 256-site
nonintegrable model, giving a mean estimate 3.65980e127 and a 95% interval
[3.20542e127,4.26428e127] from 1024 ordinary paths in about 4.76 seconds here.
The shared target weight was 1.25538e-130. The confidence formulas are proved;
their numeric inputs use floating point. Enormous waiting times alone are not an
advantage claim.

An additional proposed local event interface was displaced by nonunitary matchgate
and Gaussian jump machinery. The exact sum-over-classical-probabilities bra is a
Gaussian dual in a fixed parity sector, so replacing Born probabilities by classical
linear normalization does not create a new principle. Physical local reactions also
generally have exponentially large configuration-space support; few reaction names
do not earn a finite boundary.

THE ROOT CONSTRUCTION

The physical reference is a finite, disordered, zero-field Ising ring with actual
heat-bath spin-flip rates. In domain-wall coordinates it has 2^(n-1) states. Its
symmetrized generator is quadratic in fermionic variables because pair creation,
annihilation and hopping rates obey the free-fermion cancellation a+c=d+e.
All physical probabilities are classical. Fermions are a computational representation.

Perturb each local rate by a bounded local factor that has the same value on both
ends of the transition. The equilibrium law is unchanged, but spectator dependence
generally makes the kinetics non-Gaussian. For instance, multiply flip i by
1+0.7 eta_(i+2). An independent audit also uses a two-spectator exponential factor.

Let K0 and K be the positive symmetrized reference and perturbed generators,
respectively. The local bounds imply m K0 <= K <= M K0 on the mean-zero subspace.
For c=(m+M)/2, R=(cK0)^+, E=K-cK0 and C=R^(1/2)ER^(1/2),

    ||C|| <= rho=(M-m)/(M+m).

The relevant scalar response is a quadratic form of (I+C)^(-1). Its degree-k
Neumann error is at most rho^(k+1) times the true response. Chebyshev inverse
polynomials improve this to 1/|T_(k+1)(1/rho)|. These approximation facts are
classical; the constructed operation is their evaluation as finite, phase-sensitive
Gaussian insertion words around an interacting reference, without storing the
2^(n-1)-dimensional inverse solution.

Each reference inverse is approximated by an explicit positive exponential sum.
The number of semigroup evaluation times grows as log(Lambda/g), where g and Lambda
bound the nonzero reference spectrum. Even a huge evaluation time is a matrix
exponential argument, not a trajectory length. A bounded Gaussian-circuit dilation
accounts for arithmetic precision and avoids hiding exponentially growing
intermediate transformations in an oracle.

The paid work is of the form s^k 4^(ell k) q^(k+1) poly(n,k,precision), summed over
orders, for s perturbed local terms, total support ell including the flipped sites,
and q exponential-sum nodes. At fixed
contrast and accuracy this is polynomial in n and log(Lambda/g). A backward operator
residual and a phase-tracked occupation-chart construction pay for finite precision;
relative accuracy of every single-particle eigenvalue is unnecessary. It is not fully
polynomial in accuracy. With s=n, n^k can be prohibitive. A few nonintegrable defects
in an extended solvable interacting bath is a more immediately useful subclass.

ACTUAL RESULTS

1. Physical and algebraic checks.
   An independent generator reconstructed directly from spin energy differences
   agrees with the Gaussian reference mean to 2.47e-14 relative error and its gap
   to 8.40e-14. All Nambu contractions agree with explicit Fock matrices to
   7.89e-15 absolute error after the complementary-rate numerical repair. Mixed-sign
   couplings, unequal rates and a rare target
   were included. A separate perturbation audit verified the sharper Neumann and
   Chebyshev bounds through degree eight on a different non-Gaussian rate rule.

2. Large reference.
   At n=256, J=-0.4 and unit attempt rates, the code calculates the no-wall target
   mean as 3.7428037427e127 microscopic time units using 512-dimensional spectral
   data. This is an exactly soluble reference, not the claimed advance. The target
   means either uniform spin orientation; the orientation quotient is explicit.

3. Nonintegrable insertion without state enumeration.
   At n=64, J=-0.4, two attempt rates equal one and the other 62 equal 0.001,
   the reference mean is 3.1378129066e32 and the target probability is about
   5.63e-33. The exact equilibrium entrance-interval approximation misses the mean
   by a factor about 3.03, because return clustering matters.
   A 160 by 32 quadrature of the Gaussian quartic insertion yields C1/W0=0.0170471908.
   A 256 by 64 refinement changes the coefficient by 2.11e-9 relative.
   Jensen/secant operator bounds for the 0.7 spectator multiplier then give

       [3.1008108270e32, 3.1157872439e32],

   width about 0.483% of the lower endpoint. These decimal endpoints use refined
   floating-point quadrature. The operator enclosure is proved; an end-to-end
   directed-rounding numerical enclosure has not been implemented.

4. Stiffness and a failed numerical representation.
   Two separated attempt rates of 1e-40 leave a substantial intermediate return
   plateau in a six-site model. At 90-digit precision, the gap is
   1.50442484144e-40; double precision reports a spurious ~1.67818e-15 instead.
   The physical mean from a conventional high-precision dense solve is
   6.79920616818e39. The Gaussian return calculation tracks the slow plateau to
   times of order 1e40. The implementation now refuses a mean calculation when its
   double-precision gap is unresolved. This stress test does not defeat conventional
   multiscale averaging: its parity sectors make an effective slow model accessible.

5. A second, earned observable.
   The same theorem applies to integrated equilibrium autocovariance chi_A of a
   centered observable. For energy in the disordered Ising model, the Gaussian
   source has only two-quasiparticle components, whose amplitudes are calculated.
   The code verifies the variance, reference chi and nonintegrable inverse expansion
   against dense generators, and evaluates disordered references through n=256.
   This is energy fluctuation/relaxation noise, not a claim about thermal conductivity.

All numerical quantities are model calculations in explicitly defined microscopic
units, not empirical predictions for a fabricated material. The model parameters
were not fitted to a real substance.

STRONG CONTENDERS AND USEFUL FAILURES

The independent equilibrium contender constructed exact planar partition functions,
checked them against enumeration, and calculated a 4096-spin disordered example.
It also built frustrated nonplanar networks that reduce exactly to ferromagnets.
Strong conventional thermodynamic constructions therefore displaced that opening.

It retained a constructive many-body interaction cell: four finite hidden spins with
Hadamard-signed pair couplings leave a pure four-terminal parity interaction for
every temperature. A smaller conventional one-hidden-spin cell defeats a simple
gadget economy claim at one chosen temperature. A finite fixed pairwise Hamiltonian
cannot emulate a nonzero fixed higher-body Hamiltonian coefficient over an open
temperature interval; an analytic expansion at beta=0 proves the obstruction.
Interaction-type synthesis and fixed-Hamiltonian emulation are distinct services.
These exact constructions remain capital, not an asserted new device.

DECISION

The result worth retaining is a vector-free, controlled extension of solvable
interacting dynamics, including a paid path through kinetic stiffness and a second
physical response observable. This is substantially beyond a chosen-rate sketch.

The strongest standard tensor absorption construction (Masetti/Robol, public kaes),
target-start sampling, exact local recycling and adiabatic elimination remain
constructive competitors. In homogeneous systems the Gaussian source factorizes in
paired momentum modes, making the transformed conventional tensor competitor even
stronger. Disorder is the more meaningful general setting for the scalar Gaussian
representation. A total performance superiority has not been demonstrated there.

The central unresolved bridge is now explicit: contract extensive nonintegrable
interaction words, or admit much stronger kinetic/energetic changes, without merely
moving exponential complexity into tensor ranks, local preparation, spectral
precision or a hidden bath. That bridge would change the scale of the result. The
present report does not claim to have crossed it.
