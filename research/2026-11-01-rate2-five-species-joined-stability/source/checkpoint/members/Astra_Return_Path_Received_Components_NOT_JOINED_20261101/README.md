This is a self-contained scientific checkpoint of source-bound reviewed components.

from every sufficiently large state. No recurrence or stationary exponential-moment
claim for that network follows from this package. No novelty claim is made.

## Missing operation

The paid charging prefix ends in a low-debt region, possibly with extremely
unbalanced arms. The dyadic stage requires a much more specific actual entrance.
The interior block requires fixed-positive B,C,D,E fractions. No received result
here uniformly joins these regions while charging continuing reactions,
immigration, failed attempts, repeat crossings, and eventual **actual fractional
population reduction**. A special initial ray or deterministic attraction alone
does not provide the consumer's uniform hypothesis (H).

Thus these are compatible pieces of a return-path construction, not a completed
stability argument. Pending global phase/gluing and the N-denominator repair are not included or
promoted. The uniform initial-growth component received independent acceptance
before this freeze and is included only in its stated M-hit-or-B-half-exit scope.

## Controlling corrections and suppliers

- In the rate-2 obstruction display, a possibly negative finite K0 uses the
  exact term `K0(1-exp(-zeta T))/zeta`, or K0 is replaced by `max(K0,0)` before
  the coarser bound. The independent receipt records this harmless clarification.
- Falling-factorial propensities are used throughout; `2A` means `A(A-1)`.
  Rate-2 components have `CD->AE` rate 2; the root comparison has every rate 1.
- **Dyadic total-band exit has priority over a simultaneous L hit.** Only an
  in-band L hit restarts the next analysis stage. The terminal endpoint needs
  `+1`, or one stops at the first actual `L>=delta N`. Upper exit needs its own
  headroom/immigration payment. Lower exit is relative to fixed reference N,
  not automatically a fractional loss from every permitted entrance total.
- The uniform initial-growth receiver has **no total-band stop**. Its B-half
  exit may be produced by population-conserving AB->CD. It cannot be relabeled
  reduction, balanced-arm entry, or an input to the dyadic lemma without proof.
- No physical state or reference N is reset. Analysis clocks may restart only
  at actually received continuing-state entrances.
- Interior removal imports only the already-received deterministic single-linkage
  global-attraction theorem: David F. Anderson, arXiv:1101.0761v6, Corollary 4.11.
  Exact hypotheses, source receipt, drift-equivalent realization, finite-cover
  entry, entropy contraction, and paid stochastic windows are included. The
  actual chain is not asserted complex balanced; no stochastic mixing is assumed.
- The sampled consumer pays the entire holding interval. Its physical-time
  exponential moment uses an exponent strictly smaller than the sampled exponent.
  Its earlier overbroad pointwise-obstruction wording was corrected; retained
  previous drafts are explicitly audit history, not active theorems.
