## Result A: Conservation+external randomization is NOT a novel rare-event control bridge
For newly formed methane from +4 carbon, 8F M_CH4<=Q_cathodic+E_other (properly normalized stored or externally donated reducing equivalents) and separately M_CH4 <= available carbon. An initially stored gas/dissolved methane inventory must be counted when measuring collected methane; source gas GC mixes oxidation/reduction streams and uses an explicit dilution correction. A command z is actual **voltage/reagent**: it does NOT set/log the thermal innovations. Actual charge/current can be measured AFTER a command; a pre-command pathwise envelope needs input current compliance and all relevant inventories. With a 100% FE ceiling, the 60% FE source has a loose U and no useful universal relative efficiency guarantee.

**Exact countermodel:** a single 2C cathodic reduction pulse with the same available carbon, Cu and current can produce FE_CH4 60% or 1%, with the residual charge consumed by H2 (2 electrons per H2); both satisfy exactly 8 electrons per methane and identical charge. Thus charge/carbon do not identify response or the sign of the preferred pulse policy. Delayed release of preloaded methane gives another same-S, same-command, different-next-S counterexample.

With randomized externally logged commands A_k and shared actual conditional physical chemistry kernel K after each realized command, the ONLY justified likelihood is product q(A_k|O_past)/pi(A_k|O_past), positive-support assumed. Internal reaction-noise laws cancel without being actuated or observed. An exact two-step rational model with action-dependent hidden transitions verifies E_pi[L Y]=E_q[Y]. This is established causal off-policy evaluation, not new Doob sampling, and an equally informed ordinary method TIES.

## Result B: quantitatively check the true 500h headroom, not FE alone
If one holds representative (NOT time-integrated source data) area 2cm2, reduction j=.2A/cm2, reduction 5s, oxidation 5s, reduction |V|=3.9V, reduction FE_CH4=.6, then 2C reduction/pulse creates 1.55464micromol CH4, with reduction-only 7.8J/pulse. Over 500h that would be 180000 cycles, 4.489g CH4 and E_red=1.404MJ. Reduction-only E/CH4=(8F*3.9)/.6=5.017MJ/mol, or LHV(CH4)/E_red=17.75% using source LHV=55.5MJ/kg. **NOT whole-cycle measured efficiency.** Include oxidation signed current*voltage, pumps, refresh, gas conditioning, standby, acid, Py and device depreciation. A 2026 15MW PEM+Sabatier heat-integrated **model** reports 51.10% overall efficiency under separate process assumptions (https://www.tib-op.org/ojs/index.php/isec/article/view/3367), not directly matched experimental superiority. The industrial comparator cannot be weaker.

If oxidation shortens 5s->1s at unchanged reduction current/5s duration, duty goes 0.5 to 0.8333 and methane/hour improves only if FE_new>0.36; CH4/kWh still depends on measured total cycle energy, FE, catalyst loss and degradation. Ordinary waveform optimization, using SAME ports and GC, deserves full credit. Short-time FE gain alone is not 500h life advantage.

## Result C: physical candidate that makes the internal Cu history unnecessary for *metal custody*, not for CH4 yield
Keep source's loaded CEM as baseline. Add an **isolated copper catch/digest/reconstitution side-loop**, using external pumps, valves, electrochemical stripping, filter, isolated acid/redox conversion and ligand/pH restoration; count soluble/particulate Cu and adherent wall deposits independently. Precursor returns only after actual Cu mass, speciation and Py/alkalinity are measured. Treat any inaccessible Cu trapped in old hardware as irreversible loss. Standard Cu(OH)2+2H+->Cu2++2H2O chemistry is available, but Cu0 needs electrochemical/oxidant treatment, global catholyte acidification would consume bicarbonate and perturb CO2 reduction, and Cu+ chemistry/precipitation is more complex. **This physical loop is not built or source-qualified.**

For the inventory vector x of all Cu-containing species and internal reaction stoichiometry S, the copper atom-count row c satisfies c*S_internal=0. Thus c*x(T)=c*x(0)+Cu_in-Cu_out regardless of hidden internal kinetic rates. An actual sealed, collected and fully digested side-stream can physically standardize the recovered METAL precursor; it does **not** reset Ag support structure, adsorbed intermediates, gas holdup, membrane damage or catalytic selectivity. This is established conservation/chemical engineering, not novel mathematics.

Suppose the actual fraction lambda of usable stock at risk every 5s/5s cycle, and real independent recovery fraction r into prepared precursor; then M_N=M_0[1-lambda(1-r)]^N in this explicit model. For 90% stock survival over N=180000, lambda(1-r)<=1-0.9^(1/180000)=5.85336e-7. If and ONLY IF hypothetical lambda=1e-5, r>=.9414664. At 3.6mg/L * 500mL the starting bulk Cu is only 1.8mg; losing <10% over 180000 cycles means <=1 ng/cycle on average. The paper does NOT supply lambda or r. Even complete replacement of the 500mL catholyte every 48h adds only ~19mg of fresh Cu in 500h, so copper make-up MATERIAL cost alone likely cannot justify a complex recycler; seek a measured process productivity or downtime benefit.

Existing physical sensors (not adopted as all-state oracles): Speck/Cherevko 2020 online Cu ICP-MS, doi:10.1016/j.elecom.2020.106739, demonstrates dissolution strongly dependent on pH and that anodic charge is not itself dissolved Cu mass. 2024 *Nature Catalysis*, doi:10.1038/s41929-023-01070-8, observes Cu+ dissolved/redeposited near cathodic Cu electrodes using ICP-MS and UV-vis; near-electrode recapture can hide Cu flux from outlet sensors. Existing imaging/ICP evidence does not warrant complete mass capture on this CEM reactor.
