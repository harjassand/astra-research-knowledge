## Primary literature checked

1. Littman–Sutton–Singh, NIPS 2001, *Predictive Representations of State*.
   Read the definition, finite POMDP-to-PSR construction, closure lemma and
   main theorem in the primary PDF. The closed test basis is established
   prior machinery, not a new Astra invention.
   https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2001/file/1e4d36177d71bbb3558e43af9577d70e-Paper.pdf
2. Huang–Liang–Yang, ICLR 2024, *Provably Efficient UCB-type Algorithms For
   Learning Predictive State Representations*. Read primary abstract and
   Assumption 1/Propositions 1–2 passages. Their well-conditioning parameter
   controls all future functional norms; the results are not a rank-only,
   horizon-free acquisition theorem. Their existing positive algorithms
   must remain comparators for a new general predictive-learning claim.
   https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/cbfa01fae7875353d954429795d954f5-Paper-Conference.pdf
3. Adhikary–Srinivasan–Gordon–Boots, AISTATS 2020, *Expressiveness and Learning
   of Hidden Quantum Markov Models*. Primary abstract checked; N1148 and
   N1166 independently cite its detailed Liouville/Choi comparison. No
   deeper theorem from this paper is imported into our ordinary proof.
   https://proceedings.mlr.press/v108/adhikary20a.html
4. Higgins et al., Nature 2007, *Entanglement-free Heisenberg-limited phase
   estimation*, arXiv 0709.2996v2. Primary abstract checked for the established
   role of repeated coherent interrogation versus independent uses. Our
   two-point lower bound is proved directly and is not claimed as a new
   metrological scaling law.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.2996

## New-work status

`COHERENT_PREDICTION_DEPTH_TRADEOFF.md` contains an ordinary self-contained
proof, not a formal proof-assistant certificate. The Python check passed 24
parameter cases and 526 floating affinity diagnostics, plus exact rational
core inversion, Neumann constants, Machin/sine acquisition-threshold bounds
and an exact exponential-error-budget control. It generated 2,332 individual
synthetic binary outcomes and saved their bytes/hashes. The classical
two-hypothesis learner succeeds in both retained cases; its confidence claim
is the Hoeffding proof with the charged argument rounding, not two successes.

No independent internal review of this new lemma is claimed. No empirical
physical source, new quantum gate, universal prediction algorithm, practical
superiority, or historical novelty has been established. No required input
is supplied by rank notation or by a printed large H.
