## Main results

Start with **CLOCK_CEILING_COMPLETION.md** for the latest construction and
**POSITIVE_PREDICTIVE_REALIZATION.md** for its free-clock predecessor.
**OPEN_MODULE_COMPLETION.md** supplies the directed composition extension:
regions can carry boundary current and need not be stationary in isolation.
Their boundary flow imbalance is preserved until reassembly.

On a labelled port boundary, stationary conditional mean consequences are
specified by a Schur matrix S, retained weights w_B, left and right hidden
population loads, and a residual residence scalar c. Separate regional
records preserve means between any disjoint unions of declared whole
regions, provided every cross-region edge endpoint is retained. The
underlying hidden state dimension and transition matrix need not be
identified. Full survival laws and arbitrary hidden initial preparations
are not preserved.

For directed dynamics, the eliminated population can be represented by
a nonnegative entrance/exit residence matrix M. Its two margins are
v_R=b_R-w_B and v_L=b_L-w_B. Its off-diagonal support is constrained by
the trace flow F_ij=-S_ij. With free clocks, define

    J_* = min_M sum_{i!=j} M_ij^2/F_ij.

For positive eliminated mass, a finite positive CTMC realizes the record
exactly iff this transport is feasible and 2c>J_*. Every implementation
with k hidden states obeys k>=J_*/(2c-J_*) when J_*>0. A fixed two-port
family attains this bound with k=n, although its mean stays bounded and
the record has a constant number of entries. Balanced entrance/exit
loads admit a simple exact construction on at most 2m states even with
directed boundary circulation. Reversibility is sufficient, not necessary.

At relative accuracy epsilon, positive realizations of size O(m^2/epsilon)
preserve S, both loads and port weights exactly and change c by at most
a factor 1+epsilon. This controls every declared nonnegative mean
consequence uniformly, rather than only absolute survival errors. The
sharp two-port family needs order 1/epsilon states in this fixed-record
accuracy class. Precision in entries claimed exact remains paid.

The next attack removed unbounded clocks. For hidden rate ceiling Lambda
and port ceilings ell_i, the usable self-return flux at port i is limited
by a_i=w_i ell_i-sum_{j!=i}F_ij. Uniformization gives a piecewise-linear
minimum second moment for each residence/flow pair. Minimizing its sum
over the allowed transportation set is an ordinary finite LP. A finite
positive realization under these ceilings exists exactly when the port
and transport conditions hold and 2c is at least this LP value. Equality
is attained by explicit adjacent-Erlang channels. Slow exponential
adjustments supply larger c without violating the ceilings.

The same two-port family requires hidden ceiling Lambda>=n in its fixed
time calibration, as well as at least n hidden states. This separates
the amount of predictive information, positive state complexity, and
clock resources. For all these theorems, zero hidden mass is treated
separately and requires c=0.

## Information actually acquired and reused

Two native synthetic assays produced individual, retained trajectories:
4,194,304 trials at four ports and 6,291,456 at six ports. Both charge
every reset, native hold and hidden excursion. Their recorded native
elapsed times are approximately 8.10 million and 12.17 million source
clock units. Clocks, reset access, timestamp tolerance, an exponential
tail envelope, and the region separator are stated premises. They are
not acquired merely by observing no exceptional event. The sampler is
idealized numerical execution, not physical validation or a complete
floating-point distributional proof.

The six-port record certifies all 12 disjoint nonempty source/target
region-union means with simultaneous failure bound 3/5000 and maximum
upper/lower interval factor below 1.557794. The consumer uses actual
excursion information and no private generator. A public 12-state
positive representative realizes its prepared point record exactly;
each of its means lies in the corresponding source interval.

That representative has port rates <=3/2 and hidden rates <=1, checked
from its exact generator with no new source observations. Its original
unit native port rates are not preserved. Rational coefficients reach
3184 bits. State count therefore does not acquire coefficient precision
or physical preparation for free.

The new general directed and clock-ceiling theorems use exact supplied
records. They are not new directed statistical acquisitions. Directed
left loads require duration resolved jointly with the return endpoint;
reversible path-ratio estimators cannot simply be transferred.

The clock LP implementation uses conventional HiGHS proposals, then exact
rational primal/dual certificates and full generator elimination. Its
saved run has 11 certified optima, two exact infeasibility certificates
and 57 full target-mean comparisons. Independent implementations check
different equality and variance-inflation constructions. These are
finite mathematical controls with no source assays.

Useful failures are retained. A matrix inverse order bound does not
bound arbitrary directional bilinear means; robust row inequalities
repair the statistical consumer. Matching global c does not preserve
whole-region queries; regional c_j must survive. Native hazards require
retained port masses. Native diagonal event flux cannot have zero
interior duration. Zero hidden mass cannot hide a positive c. Capping
port rates does not preserve them exactly. Small mean records need not
preserve a survival law or have a small positive implementation.

## Strong conventional routes and limits

Schur solves, stochastic complementation, graph transformation, exact
milestoning, scalar phase fitting, robust M-matrix methods and LP/QP are
credited working machinery. An equally informed small Schur solve is
the natural route for mean-only evaluation; simulating the positive
completion is not a demonstrated speedup. A separate local birth-death
assay confirms that an enormous printed mean can be handled by ordinary
local estimates and a recurrence when the requisite state access exists.

The contribution under investigation is the exact stationary network
completion criterion, its relation to consequential information, and
its constructive resource boundary. The targeted primary search found
no matching calibrated-record theorem among the sources inspected, but
priority remains unsettled. Automatic discovery of a useful small
separator, acquisition of needed tails/support and precise finite-rate
implementation remain separate unsolved obligations.
