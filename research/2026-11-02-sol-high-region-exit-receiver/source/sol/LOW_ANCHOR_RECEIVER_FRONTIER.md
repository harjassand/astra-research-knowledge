# Why the completed high receiver cannot simply receive the old face stop

This is an exact failure of an OUTPUT CONTRACT, not a recurrence counterexample.
Keep the successful high-region proofs unchanged.

Take A=D=0 and B=C=E=F=n, with n positive and even. In the old bounded-anchor
normal cell, residual pair differences are zero and each residual minority
is even. Core shifts preserve both facts. Let J0 be the sum of the two
residual half-minorities; initially J0=n. At a normal phase i, its physical
work differs from residual work by the finite loan

    ell_0=0, ell_1=3, ell_2=2, ell_3=1.

Thus the total physical Psi along the normal cell is J0+ell_i+ell_j.
The complete actual first-unexpected-source stop has the following own-port
output work increments relative to its residual baseline:

    phase1/source0: 6,
    phase2/source0: 5,
    phase2/source1: a_work+2,
    phase3/source0: 4,
    phase3/source1: a_work+1,
    phase3/source2: 3.

The peer phase loan is nonnegative and must also be added. These identities
hold at EVERY actual core location, including signed residual endpoint
buffers, because a core shift changes each minority by an even number.
Consequently EVERY actual killing output has Psi>=J0+3. At the input both
positive anchor terms g_i vanish; at the outputs they are nonnegative. The
new common high receiver therefore satisfies, at that exact old stop,

    E[U_out-U_in]>=C[6(J0+c0)+9]>0.                     (F1)

No concentration or numerical fixture is used. The previous first-kill time
is already earned, so (F1) is a genuine mismatch with a finite actual episode.
Calling both receivers quadratic cannot repair it. The old central/star
theorems retain their accepted scope and remain useful operations; this
particular high-work metric does not receive them at coefficient one.

Normalizing the low-anchor phases by their exact source packets removes the
finite ell loans, but leaves the angular problem: Psi gives an order-one
weight to each newly unpaired molecule, multiplied by a large common
inventory in Psi^2. A radial-plus-small-angular-energy receiver does not have
that cusp. Merely forcing every first switch to decrease the workload would
discard genuine delayed repayment.

Two positive routes remain live, and neither is supplied as a premise:

1. Continue the actual low-anchor trace until its newly created unpaired
   inventories have been repaid, retaining both ports and the actual high
   excursion kernel (E1). Its clock may grow polynomially; the complete
   ordinary one-port C3 return bound is already O(initial_population^2), so
   a slow return alone is not a failure. The missing work is the joint trace
   bound with the other unbounded port, not another fixed cold-head minorization.
2. Replace the global receiver by compatible stopped costs or a smooth
   radial/angular construction. The high workload and exact four-event
   operator can remain auxiliary bounds instead of being imposed as the
   final recurrence energy. The accepted Astra U1411/U1454 constructions show
   the correct methodology: construct finite actual values, retain exterior
   leakage, and earn complementary negative operations. Their particular
   residuals and interfaces do not transfer to this raw list automatically.

Neither a stationary fiber nor a uniform gap supplies the missing boundary
law. No full six-species theorem or general recurrence principle is claimed.
