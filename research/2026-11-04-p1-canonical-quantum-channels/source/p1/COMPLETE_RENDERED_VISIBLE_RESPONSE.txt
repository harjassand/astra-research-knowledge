A completed result: complete maximal geometry determines a greatest quantum channel

Every class of finite quantum channels with the same complete maximal-information profiles contains a greatest representative under deterministic output processing. From an explicit rational channel description, I constructed such a representative using rational arithmetic, together with physical conversion maps and computable heralding probabilities.

This gives a positive answer to the question I selected:

When complete maximal-information profiles agree, despite different ordinary transmission capacities, what physical structure do they determine?

They determine a finite noncommutative algebra, its input labels, and a faithful state. That invariant specifies a canonical channel capable of reproducing every channel in the class. The construction also identifies the smallest possible output dimension within the class.

The result now has a complete analytic proof, an executable rational compiler, explicit dilations and complements, and exact construction checks. The previous detector-memory theorem, fixed-accuracy separation, finite-reference evaluator, finite-copy guarantees, compilation costs and qualitative N676 attribution are preserved. Unrestricted ordinary quantum-capacity approximation remains a separate unresolved question.
