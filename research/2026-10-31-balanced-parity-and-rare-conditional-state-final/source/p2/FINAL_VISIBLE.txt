The strongest result is now a balanced parity instrument

I developed a way to turn the uncertain interaction into a reusable measurement: give both parity outcomes a conclusive detection route, and stop as soon as either route succeeds. This changes the resource law. The entire valid logical space acquires a uniform stopping probability, including coherent superpositions of the two parities and arbitrary correlations with the retained source.

The resulting construction can produce a Bell resource without accurately accumulating a prescribed entangling phase. It tolerates noncommuting source evolution under a qualified operator-gain bound. Its physical errors accumulate according to the probability that interrogation continues, rather than automatically according to the maximum number of rounds.

I also derived a three-cell Coulomb geometry that produces the required interaction through symmetry, and repaired the local gate in the actual longitudinal model after exposing a serious failure of a small projected model.

These are a structural result and stronger microscopic components, not a completed physical resource cycle. The remaining join is now explicit: common operator gain, orbital return, finite local control, and meter analysis and renewal must coexist without revealing information inside a measured sector. Earlier accepted constructions and their failure boundaries remain intact; the results below are new working derivations.
