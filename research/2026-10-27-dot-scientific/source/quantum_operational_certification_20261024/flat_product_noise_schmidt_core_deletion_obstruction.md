# Flat physical product noise still cannot be freely deleted outside the main Schmidt core

This exact obstruction lies inside the already-paid isotropic operator-valued family. It is not an entropy-target counterexample. It distinguishes the newly proved paid finite-Krylov value compression from an invalid exact compression to the main's Schmidt support.

Let Phi3 denote the normalized maximally entangled qutrit projector, P0=|0><0| on a two-dimensional bath, and

 B_epsilon=[[t,epsilon sqrt(t)],[epsilon sqrt(t),epsilon^2]], t>0,
 A_epsilon=Phi3 tensor P0+I9 tensor B_epsilon.

The actual physical dimension is3 and reference is C3 tensor C2. This is a pure main plus PRODUCT noise with FLAT physical factor I3/3 (reference noise 3I3 tensor B_epsilon). B_epsilon is PSD of rank one. Compressing the reference to the main's three-dimensional Schmidt core C3 tensor|0> gives A0 exactly.

Using W3(Phi3)=P_antisym/3 and E(Phi3)=I9/9 gives the exact homogeneous deficit

 Delta(A_epsilon)=f(B_epsilon+P0)-4f(B_epsilon)
                   -6f(B_epsilon+P0/3)+9f(B_epsilon+P0/9),
 f(X)=Tr XlogX.

For c>0, B_epsilon+cP0 has trace t+c+epsilon^2 and determinant c epsilon^2. Its new small eigenvalue is

 [c/(t+c)]epsilon^2+O(epsilon^4).

The rank-one B_epsilon term has only the positive eigenvalue t+epsilon^2 and is analytic. Consequently

 Delta(A_epsilon)-Delta(A0)
  =c_t epsilon^2 log(epsilon^2)+O(epsilon^2),
 c_t=1/(t+1)-2/(t+1/3)+1/(t+1/9)
    =-4(3t-1)/[(t+1)(3t+1)(9t+1)].

For every 0<t<1/3, c_t>0, so the actual deficit strictly DECREASES upon adding the hidden reference component. At t=1/9, c_t=9/10, while Delta(A0)=(10/9)log(5/4)>0. Trace normalization divides the leading coefficient by TrA0=1+9t and adds only an O(epsilon^2) correction, so the same strict direction holds for normalized sources.

Thus even an isotropic physical product baseline does not permit free compression to the pure main's Schmidt support. The finite-rank determinant/Krylov construction avoids this obstruction by retaining enough reference moments and paying the remainder in terms of an explicit negative margin.
