# Generic equal-trace Jensen surrogate fails at relative peak2

27 October 2026. Exact method boundary, not a physical WH entropy counterexample.

The generic two-source functional used as a lower bound in the relative-peak proofs is

J(X,τ)=TrX[logX+logτ−2log((X+3τ)/4)].

The monotone-pencil argument proves J≥0 for equal-trace positive pairs with X≤(9/5)τ. It cannot establish J≥0 for ALL such pairs at peak2, because the following commuting two-dimensional example is negative:

τ=diag(1,15), X=diag(2,14).

These are faithful, have equal trace16 and satisfy X≤2τ. Directly,

J=2log(32/25)+14log(3360/3481)<−1/1000.

The associated actual marginal identity τ=EX is NOT present; the physical subsystem here is not a qutrit. Thus this is not a counterexample to Δ≥0, not a counterexample to a source-native peak2 theorem, and not a sharp bound on the best received peak. Its purpose is to preserve an exact barrier against extrapolating generic order-and-trace scalar calibration to peak2. A further physical extension must retain additional E/N source structure, pay the actual channel Jensen defect, or use a different entropy representation.
