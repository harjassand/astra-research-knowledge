Z=T tensor U+I18 tensor V, with T=P_Omega3 tensor C+I3 tensor Y0.

lambda I3, where lambda=(TrC+TrY0)TrU+6TrV.

reset-only source with zero deficit. Thus base-source zero and lifted-source
zero are distinct cases. No source theorem or constant changes.
