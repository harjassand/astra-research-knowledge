# Native full-temperature and weak-imperfection corollary: explicit resource substitution

2026-11-02. Candidate corollary for the whole-family audit, using the earlier reviewed compiler as a component. This does not claim that the new physical code has maximum check weight seven.

## Required inputs now supplied by the small-scale join
The declared source-compatible deterministic family has bounded 3D geometry/degree, one logical qubit, positive polynomial X/Z distance, and polynomial-radius stabilizer filling in ACTUAL check generators. The packet component decoder and dual augmented full traces give a bounded-degree connected witness family whose fixed top witness tau is a valid removable coboundary. The unstable-event contour has |tau| growing as a positive power of n, with at most exponential-in-|tau| entropy. The code's commuting Gibbs bad-event bound alone would not have sufficed; the removable full-trace extension is the important additional input.

These are the relevant assumptions of the full-spectrum clock compiler and the subsequent mixed-source generic-perturbation proof from Deliveries82–83. Neither construction requires the random-embedding family specifically.

## Honest bounded local dimension
Let w_max be a uniform upper bound on the weights of the actual X and Z check generators in the fixed physical bases. Every source face is a triangle, quadrilateral or pentagon. Relevant edge face degree is at most four, so an edge stalk contributes at most three qubits. The source density bound implies at most thirty incident abstract faces at a vertex; each contributes at most two edge germs. Thus a conservative bound is

 w_X<=60*3=180,     w_Z<=5*3=15.

This is a deliberately loose sufficient bound, not a measured minimal weight or a lower bound. It assumes the unchanged source cell inventory and no additional physical subdivisions; virtual proof refinements do not alter the Hamiltonian. One can safely pad to K=max(3,w_max), for example K=180 under this conservative admission. A better basis/vertex-type analysis may reduce it, but SEVEN-level clocks cannot be claimed without that work.

The exact Pauli-prefix clock algebra and marked-winding rod proof are already stated for every FIXED K>=3. Substitute this actual K in their explicit constants. The generic mixed-source bound becomes

 q(v)=K^(b_c) d_B^(b_B) exp(2 beta t b_c)[exp(beta C_local v)-1],

with the corresponding fixed local support/dimension constants. No proof step requires K to grow with n. A very large fixed clock and fantastically small sufficient v are material costs, not practical engineering tolerances.

## Resulting mathematical model
Choose fixed t/Delta and a compact sufficiently-low-temperature interval with strict rod and witness margins. For a sufficiently small fixed local perturbation strength, the native two-body data/clock Hamiltonian plus bounded onsite-spin environment and weak local V has actual projected Gibbs weight ratios

 log[W_V(s)/W_V(s+tau)] <= -B_eff |tau| + o(1)

for the valid removable witnesses, with B_eff above the derived contour entropy threshold. Polynomial filling controls source influence and polynomial distance controls the n-anchored exceptional-source remainder, uniformly in bath volume. The new decoder's actual bounded-local interaction footprints are admitted by the same finite Pauli-prefix enlargement and general syndrome-translation ratio as in Delivery83.

The existing exact-H logical-twirl consumer then yields

 error(T)<=min{2,6 v n T [2p(n)+2sqrt(beta v n p(n))]},
 p(n)<=poly(n)exp(-c n^eta),

for ideal overwrite of the fixed decoded logical qubit in the ACTUAL joint Gibbs state and ideal terminal decoding, with the same Hamiltonian throughout hold. Incoming input/reference is initially independent of that Gibbs resource. No Davies limit or microscopic-to-Markov approximation is used in this corollary.

## Limits that remain
This is a source-compatible small-geometric-scale construction plus a general physical compilation theorem, not a practical small quantum memory. Its fixed energy ratios, clock dimension and sufficient imperfection tolerance can be prohibitive. It does not solve noisy WRITE, autonomous equilibration into the needed correlated gauge state, actual noisy access-Hamiltonian admission, or unbounded-dimensional/strong nonequilibrium environments. Previously reviewed conditional noisy READ remains a separately charged service theorem.

The original scale-eight geometry is not altered by padding clock dimension, but local device complexity and energy budgets are enlarged by the compiler. The source's geometric routing theorem remains imported rather than independently reimplemented as a full coordinate generator. Historical novelty beyond the scoped source/machinery comparison remains unestablished.
