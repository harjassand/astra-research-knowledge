## Essential unresolved gates
Generic extensive perturbation stability at a fixed nonzero strength uniformly over all sufficiently low temperatures is NOT proved. Virtual defects create a nonzero floor, and logical histories require genuine normalization/resummation. The excursion framework is a research proposal except for its separately proved lemmas. Fixed-impurity comparisons do not earn an extensive-disorder theorem and may be superseded by logical cleaning.

Physical noisy WRITE, preparation of the actual joint thermal state, switching exposure and a useful operating point remain open. Ideal SWAP is a mathematical consumer, not a free physical operation. The K^-2 counterterm alternative and direction-sensitive pin candidate are unproved family-level proposals. Neither a small numerical test nor an audit certifies a new physical capability by itself.
