EXACT PAIR DYNAMICS AND A COHERENT AUTONOMOUS STRIP DELAY
1 November 2026. Actual native active/completed generators kept separate.

1. Exact two-configuration propagation, without throwing away phases
-------------------------------------------------------------------
For a virtual spin configuration s, let p_i(s)=1 iff its central spin
differs from BOTH left and down neighbours. Under the native corrector,
a dyad |s><t| has the following unperturbed evolution:

  p_i(s)=p_i(t)=1: simultaneous flip (s,t)->(s^i,t^i), rate kappa;
  p_i(s)=p_i(t)=0: no event;
  p_i(s)!=p_i(t): killing, rate c kappa,

where c=1 for completed correction and c=1/2 for active correction. Thus
the unperturbed dyad propagator is an exact positive substochastic kernel
on pairs. Their XOR d=s XOR t is preserved between coherent insertions.
Global complements have equal predicates everywhere and no killing; this
is consistent with the strong logical symmetry in RECEIVER.txt.

For a persistent bath, write rho_(s,t) for its operator-valued block. The
FULL generator with unconditional H=sum_i h_i X_i tensor B_i is exactly

 dot rho_(s,t)=L_B rho_(s,t)
  +kappa sum_i p_i(s^i)p_i(t^i)rho_(s^i,t^i)
  -kappa sum_i alpha_i(s,t)rho_(s,t)
  -i sum_i h_i[B_i rho_(s^i,t)-rho_(s,t^i)B_i],          (16)

 alpha_i=(p_i(s)+p_i(t))/2                       [active],
 alpha_i=p_i(s)+p_i(t)-p_i(s)p_i(t)              [completed].

No Markov approximation or bath reset is made in (16). A Duhamel expansion
may use the killed kernel BETWEEN insertions, but the +/-i phases and the
left/right, noncommuting bath multiplications must remain. The kernel is
not itself a stochastic replacement for the driven quantum system.

2. A local disagreement can survive a long common error background
------------------------------------------------------------------
In one row, take s to occupy x=0,...,ell-1 and t to occupy x=0,...,ell,
with the same left end. The configurations differ at ONE far-right face.
There is no torus wrap-around. Until the common strip is gone, the only
active site in both configurations is the common moving left end. All
other corrector predicates are inactive. After ell common removals,
s is vacuum and t is one face; their predicates then disagree.

For completed correction, the exact trace norm survival is

 ||exp(t L_c)(|s><t|)||_1
        =exp(-kappa t) sum_(j=0)^ell (kappa t)^j/j!.     (17)

The successive dyads have pairwise orthogonal left labels and pairwise
orthogonal right labels, so their positive coefficient sum really equals
the trace norm. For active correction the lifetime is instead a sum of
ell Exp(kappa) services and one Exp(kappa/2) final killing service. In both
models its Laplace transform is

 integral_0^infinity exp(-z t) survival(t) dt
   =[1-(kappa/(kappa+z))^ell c kappa/(c kappa+z)]/z.      (18)

The respective mean survival times are (ell+1)/kappa and (ell+2)/kappa.
This excludes a return bound C exp(-c_0 kappa t), or a bounded zero-
frequency coherence inverse, with constants uniform in background size.
It does NOT exclude a uniform eigenvalue gap: the finite chain has a
long triangular/Jordan transient. Confusing gap with norm relaxation
would discard the actual effect illustrated by (17).

3. A genuinely driven invariant sector: delayed Rabi response
-------------------------------------------------------------
Keep the common strip as a specified initial background. Now apply the
UNCONDITIONAL Hamiltonian h X_tip at its right tip x=ell. No shape or
syndrome predicate multiplies that fault. Let j=0,...,ell count common
left-end removals and q=0,1 be the tip spin. The 2(ell+1) configurations
are an invariant subspace of the full native corrector plus this drive.

For j<ell, the only active corrector is the common left end, its jump is
|j+1><j| tensor I_tip, and the tip predicate is identically zero. The
counter advances at rate kappa independently of the tip/bath state.
At j=ell the sole possible corrector is tip amplitude damping. Its rate
is kappa in both models; completed correction also has the actual inactive
dephasing channel. Their subsequent driven quantum laws are different.

The counter starts diagonal and stays diagonal. The completed model's
other inactive terms only dephase counter off-diagonals and therefore
do not alter this reduction. The activation delay is exactly
tau~Gamma(ell,kappa). With any tip/bath Hamiltonian h X_tip tensor B+H_B,
let U_t be its BEFORE-activation channel and F_t the continuing-drive,
native tip-correction channel AFTER activation. Then exactly

 rho_tip,B(T)=Pr(tau>T) U_T(rho_0)
       +integral_0^T f_tau(s) F_(T-s) U_s(rho_0) ds.     (19)

The same persistent bath is present before and after the random activation;
there is no restart at s. An arbitrary initial bath/reference extension
also passes through this identity. Independent bath noise can be included
in both channels without changing the argument.

For B=I and tip initially zero, positivity of the integral gives

  Pr(tip=1 at T)>=Pr(tau>T) sin^2(h T).                 (20)

At h/kappa=1/64, T=32 pi/kappa and ell=512, this lower bound exceeds
1-exp(-420). Indeed mu=kappa T=32 pi<ell, and the Poisson Chernoff bound
for Pr(Poisson(mu)>=ell) is exp[-mu+ell(1+ln(mu/ell))]. Use
25/8<pi<22/7, mu>100, ell/mu>56/11 and ln(56/11)>13/8 to bound its exponent
by -100+512(1-13/8)=-420. The latter logarithm inequality has an exact
rational Taylor certificate in coherent_strip_delay_20261101.py.

Thus small h/kappa does not imply a pointwise small tip occupation in
arbitrary backgrounds. More generally take T=pi/(2h) and ell>>kappa/h:
the coherent tip can rotate almost fully while a classical stochastic
fault rate C h^2/kappa would give at most C h^2 T/kappa=O(h/kappa).
No fixed C makes that a background-uniform physical-channel compiler.

4. Why this is NOT a counterexample to the logical phase
--------------------------------------------------------
The long initial strip is not the cold encoded input of the target memory.
Its probability/cost under continual faults must be paid. Choose L large
enough that ell+1 is below the inherited component-filling cutoff. Every
state in (19) then lies in one fixed contractible correctable region.
The SAME fixed CSS decoder corrects their entire Pauli span, including
all coherences and arbitrary bath/reference correlations. Their decoded
logical error effect is exactly zero despite the order-one tip response.

This distinction is central: a background-uniform full-space h^2/kappa
compiler fails here, but a receiver-only logical comparison has not been
refuted. The reduced criterion in RECEIVER.txt can discard this harmless
coherence. A successful global proof must control logical bridges and
their precursor background cost, rather than every local coherence.

5. Another failed shortcut: the full absolute pair norm
-------------------------------------------------------
For independent coherent spins starting at vacuum, before adding any
corrector, sum_(s,t)|rho_(s,t)|=(1+|sin(2hT)|)^N. For the ACTUAL grouped
corrector the initial right derivative is still 2Nh, since its action on
the initial code density matrix is zero. The large sum counts harmless
local coherence. It cannot be a uniformly normalized fault probability
or the desired logical effect. Replacing (16) by its full absolute-value
process therefore recreates an exponential safe-volume penalty.
