QUANTUM ISLAND RETURN AND UNCONDITIONAL DRIVEN RESPONSE
1 November 2026. Solo derivation. Local building block, not a global phase.

1. A closed island of the actual generator
-----------------------------------------
Use the virtual one-plane model (1) of RECEIVER.txt. Initially all errors
are confined to an a by b rectangle R in a contractible torus patch; the
exterior is uniformly healthy. The rectangle is closed under the noiseless
Toom corrector: a zero spin outside it cannot have BOTH predecessor spins
equal to one. This is an invariant sector of the actual periodic generator,
not a physical boundary pinning operation. Take the torus large enough to
exclude wrap-around. For use with the inherited local filling decoder,
choose the whole rectangle and its boundary below that decoder's small-
component diameter cutoff.

Let S be the rectangle's error-configuration Hilbert space. Both code values
are allowed: the exterior is their respective common virtual spin value,
and the errors are defined relative to that value. Global-complement
symmetry makes the following S dynamics identical in those two sectors.
It acts trivially on the chosen logical qubit in this contractible recovery
frame. Its other logical qubits may again be included in the bath.

The local diagnostic in Sections 2-5 has a healthy exterior and optionally
drive INSIDE R. A continually driven torus has no such permanently healthy
exterior. That hypothesis is never silently supplied to the global problem.

2. Classical clearance tail, with a common permanent-zero certificate
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Every J_i=X_i P_i is a partial permutation of the configuration basis.
The adjoint of BOTH native correctors maps diagonal observables to diagonal
observables. Their common restriction is the ordinary rate-kappa classical
Toom generator. In particular the adjoint effect of being outside the
vacuum at time t is diagonal. The added inactive dissipators in the
completed model have zero action on all diagonal observables.

For this population calculation, use independent rate-kappa site clocks.
Declare the two outside predecessors permanently clear at time zero. A
site's certificate clears at its first own ring after BOTH predecessor
certificates have cleared. At that ring, both actual predecessor spins are
zero, so the native update sets the central spin to zero regardless of its
previous value. It stays zero thereafter. This dominates every initial
configuration on ONE clock schedule.

If tau_(x,y) is this certified clearance time, it obeys the last-passage
recursion

  tau_(x,y)=max(tau_(x-1,y),tau_(x,y-1))+E_(x,y),        (9)

with exterior tau=0 and independent Exp(kappa) residual services E.
Independence follows by increasing x+y: a predecessor certificate uses
only lower-height site streams, and the site's own future residual is
fresh. The entire rectangle clears by the upper-right certificate.

Put H=a+b-1. There are at most 2^(H-1) directed paths to that corner,
each with H services. Chernoff at kappa/2, with moment 2 per service,
therefore gives the conservative bound

  p_R(t):=max_s Pr_s(S_t!=vacuum)
        <=min(1,4^H exp(-kappa t/2)).                  (10)

This use of site proposal clocks is a representation of the classical
population law. It does NOT identify the active quantum semigroup with
the completed proposal-clock quantum semigroup.

3. The clearance tail controls arbitrary QUANTUM island inputs
--------------------------------------------------------------
Let Phi_0(t) include the unperturbed corrector and any bath-only Lindbladian.
The bath may initially be correlated with S, the logical qubit and an
untouched reference. It is not refreshed. Let

  R_S(X)=|vac><vac| tensor Tr_S X.

Diagonal closure and (10) prove the OPERATOR inequality

  Phi_0(t)^*(I-|vac><vac|)<=p_R(t) I.                  (11)

There is no dimension factor and no bath-state averaging. It controls
the outside-vacuum probability q for every input/reference state.

For any positive output A of trace one, use P=|vac><vac| tensor I, Q=I-P.
The off-diagonal block satisfies ||PAQ||_1<=sqrt(q(1-q)). Also

  A-R_S A=PAQ+QAP+QAQ-|vac><vac| tensor Tr_S(QAQ).

Thus its trace norm is <=2 sqrt(q)+2q. Taking arbitrary references gives

  ||Phi_0(t)-R_S Phi_0(t)||_diamond
       <=epsilon_R(t):=min(2,2 sqrt(p_R(t))+2p_R(t)).    (12)

R_S Phi_0(t) is precisely vacuum reset times the bath-only evolution,
including when the initial state is S/bath/reference entangled. This is a
channel comparison proved with analytical projectors, not a physical reset
or syndrome measurement. The reasoning applies independently to the active
and completed generators because only their proved diagonal restriction
and CP/TP properties were used. It does not transfer their driven laws.

The upper scale is linear in diameter. PAIR_DYNAMICS.txt gives a matching
linear lower scale for an actual coherence, including its exact survival
law. The relevant dynamics can have long nonnormal transients even when
the displayed finite chain has all nonzero eigenvalues bounded away from
zero. A spectral-gap claim alone would not settle this return estimate.

4. An integrable quantum return kernel
---------------------------------------
From (10)-(12),

  epsilon_R(t)<=min(2,4*2^H exp(-kappa t/4)).

Split the integral at t_0=4(H+1)ln(2)/kappa. Direct integration gives

  integral_0^infinity epsilon_R(t) dt
       <=C_R:=8[(H+1)ln(2)+1]/kappa.                   (13)

This is a full completely bounded return bound for the open island. It
does not assume that each newly created coherence decays in time 1/kappa.

5. Pure unconditional coherent drive of the island, with a shared bath
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Now keep the ACTUAL perturbation, V=-i[H_R(t),.], inside R, and suppose
||V||_diamond<=2v. For the allowed sum of unconditionally applied face
faults with norm at most h each, v<=ab h. The bath operators may be
noncommuting, correlated and time dependent, with any bath-only dynamics.
No shape-conditioned perturbation, measurement, reset or physical noise
floor is added. Denote the actual driven channel by Phi(t), and E_0 the
encoding of the island vacuum with arbitrary bath input.

Since R_S commutes with the unperturbed corrector and bath evolution,
Duhamel's formula gives the EXACT identity

  (Id-R_S)Phi(t)E_0
   =integral_0^t (Id-R_S)Phi_0(t,s) V(s) Phi(s)E_0 ds.   (14)

The unperturbed term vanishes because it preserves vacuum. Use (12) for
the first factor and CPTP contractivity for the ACTUAL later factors;
do not expand all harmless later faults. Equations (13)-(14) prove

 ||Phi(t)E_0-R_S Phi(t)E_0||_diamond
       <=min(2,2v C_R)
       =min(2,16v[(H+1)ln(2)+1]/kappa)                 (15)

UNIFORMLY in time, reference and bath dimension/state. The comparison bath
channel is the actual marginal Lambda_t=Tr_S Phi(t)E_0, hence CPTP even
though memory/bath interaction remains present. No independent-bath or
finite-correlation-time assumption entered (14).

For arbitrary initial S/bath/reference inputs the same identity retains
its unperturbed first term and proves the stronger channel return form

  ||Phi(t)-R_S Phi(t)||_diamond
          <=min(2,epsilon_R(t)+2v C_R).                 (15a)

The bath marginal in R_S Phi(t) can depend on the original island input;
the bath may retain that auxiliary error information. Equation (15a)
asserts small syndrome excitation/coherence after the initial return time,
not erasure of information from the persistent bath.

For a 2x2 island, h/kappa=1/1024 and v<=4h, ln(2)<7/10 makes the unhalved
diamond bound (15) at most 19/80. The rational inequality ln(2)<7/10 follows
already from the cubic Taylor lower bound exp(7/10)>2. This bound is valid
for every time and bath input, not inferred from the numerical examples.
It is conservative: the finite entangled-input controls are much smaller.

This is a positive nonlinear-in-time response result for genuinely
unconditional faults. It is not yet an error bound for the continuously
driven periodic system. The global admissible fault class remains all
faces; drive confinement is used ONLY to define this local building block.

6. Where the global phase construction was attempted and remains open
----------------------------------------------------------------------
One can fix a coarse island size b, making (15) small at sufficiently small
h/kappa, while (10) makes sufficiently long quiet erasure windows likely
under native clocks. This supplies a controlled local return kernel.

It does not supply independent rare quantum block faults on a driven torus.
Overlapping islands have coherences, common-bath correlations and evolving
healthy boundaries. Inserting projectors or reset channels at coarse cuts
would alter the physical process. A diamond-norm perturbation of a channel
also need not be a positive rare-fault mixture; a weak unitary is the simple
obstruction. Taking absolute values of all dyads reintroduces exponential
safe-volume growth, as verified in controls.json.

The current phase-stability framework (Rakovszky et al., 2308.15495v2)
likewise does not close this step: its general nonperturbative erosion and
island-count criterion is explicitly Conjecture 8. Its proved perturbative
or path-specific results cannot be promoted to this unconditional,
shared-bath, long-time quantum threshold.

There is also a locality gate: treating an arbitrary shared bath as a local
spatial environment would be unjustified. For disjoint memory sites 1,2,
H_1=h X_1 tensor X_B and H_2=h X_2 tensor Z_B already have a nonzero nested
commutator [H_2,[H_1,Z_1]]=4h^2 Y_1 X_2 tensor Y_B, irrespective of the
spatial separation. The local non-Markovian fault-strength interface can
retain such a bath; a memory-plus-bath spatial Lieb--Robinson estimate
cannot be assumed for it. The local bound (15) needs no such estimate.

The actual next target is therefore a receiver-local estimate of E in
RECEIVER.txt, retaining the rarity and geometry of the common background
and the coherent motion inside it. This could be a positive dressed effect,
a causal quantum cluster resummation, or a different mechanism. It need
not bound every harmless syndrome coherence or the full-space norm. No
such global estimate has been obtained in this turn.

The one-plane lemma does not prove a both-CSS 4D threshold. The full-4D
problem and a physical low-dimensional autonomous controller remain
separate. Nothing here converts a synchronous or Floquet construction
into a time-independent controller.

--------------------------

and exhausts every reachable actual/certificate state for 1x1, 2x1 and 2x2
rectangles. It solves the single-site coherent steady states exactly for
the TWO generators: their excited populations are respectively

It then integrates the actual 2x2 native generators at times 5,25,100 with

with bath and reference; driven inputs have a bath/reference Bell pair.
The unconditional drive uses noncommuting bath operators at every face.

They test the derivation's finite implementation, not asymptotic stability
or a diamond optimization. Exact and floating evidence are separated in
