1. The actual one-plane question
--------------------------------
In the selected primal xy plane, write e_f for a physical Z-plaquette error.
In its invariant sector the native corrector is exactly the virtual-spin rule

  P_f=(I-Z_f Z_left)(I-Z_f Z_down)/4,  J_f=X_f P_f,
  L_a=kappa sum_f D[J_f],
  L_c=kappa sum_f (D[J_f]+D[I-P_f]).                     (1)

These remain two different continuous-time models. Add any bath-only
Lindbladian and the unconditional faults sum_f h_f X_f tensor B_f,
Hermitian B_f, ||B_f||<=1. The bath is one finite persistent system, with
no commutativity, refresh, input-independence or reference assumption.

G=product_f X_f commutes with EVERY displayed jump and Hamiltonian. In the
physical code it is the full-plane logical Z membrane. Choose the physical
logical-X eigenbasis, so G is logical X. The all-zero and all-one virtual
configurations are the two states of that logical qubit.

The fixed decoder is part of this assertion. Each of its Kraus operators
must satisfy D_s G=X_Q D_s. A fixed CSS syndrome decoder with Z-type Pauli
fillings in this one-sector problem has this intertwining property, even
on the fallback syndromes. An arbitrary CPTP fallback need not have it.
On the inherited small-component domain, local filling supplies the usual
homology argument. No favorable decoder is chosen after seeing the drive.

More explicitly, for the inherited split CSS chain-filling decoder write
F_s=Z(D_Z(s_X))X(D_X(s_Z)) and D_s=V^dagger F_s^dagger Pi_s. In this invariant
plane s_Z=0; the component-first rule has D_X(0)=0. Thus every applicable
F_s is Z-type, including ANY fixed primal global-chain fallback on large
syndromes. Both F_s and Pi_s commute with the physical G, while encoding
intertwines G and X_Q. This proves D_s G=X_Q D_s for this whole decoder
family; the particular global Z filling need not be known or optimized.
Appending arbitrary cross-sector logical operations on fallback outcomes
would leave that family and must be checked separately. The finite decoder
below is fully specified, rather than inferred from an unspecified program.

Other logical qubits can safely be included in B for the one-qubit result.
It then allows a comparison channel on those spectators plus the bath; it
does not silently assert protection of all six logical qubits. The latter
requires the separate multi-qubit criterion in Section 4.

2. One operator-valued error effect suffices under STRONG symmetry
----------------------------------------------------------------
Let Phi be the actual encoded/evolved/decoded CPTP map on Q tensor B. If
each of its Kraus operators commutes with X_Q tensor I_B, write

  K_mu=I_Q tensor A_mu+X_Q tensor B_mu,
  E=sum_mu B_mu^dagger B_mu,  delta=||E||_infinity.       (2)

For (1), the commutation holds at the level of Hamiltonian, jump, no-jump,
encoder and decoder operators, hence for a Kraus representation of Phi.
This is stronger than covariance of the channel under X conjugation.

TP and the independence of I_Q and X_Q imply

  sum A_mu^dagger A_mu+E=I_B,
  sum (A_mu^dagger B_mu+B_mu^dagger A_mu)=0.             (3)

E is operational, not a chosen unraveling statistic. For EVERY bath state,

  Tr[(|1><1|_Q tensor I) Phi(|0><0|_Q tensor sigma_B)]
          =Tr(E sigma_B).                              (4)

Thus delta is the worst-bath error probability of one decoded basis input.
No average over bath preparations is substituted. E<=delta I is the
appropriate condition with arbitrary initial logical/bath correlations.

Equivalently, the decoded phase order parameter is the B operator
M_B=<0|Phi^*(Z_Q tensor I)|0>=I_B-2E. The receiving requirement is the
operator inequality M_B>=(1-2delta)I_B over the desired storage horizon.
It is a decoded global order parameter, not a local magnetization or a
scalar average. This is a direct phase target; the later fault-amplitude
representation is one optional way of trying to prove it.

Define Lambda_A=sum A_mu (.) A_mu^dagger and Lambda_B=sum B_mu (.) B_mu^dagger.
Lambda=Lambda_A+Lambda_B is CPTP by (3). It also equals
Tr_Q Phi((I_Q/2) tensor .). Expand Phi-Id_Q tensor Lambda:

  (Ad_X-Id_Q) tensor Lambda_B
  plus the two A/B cross maps.                          (5)

The first term has diamond norm at most 2 delta. The column Stinespring
operators for A and B have norms <=1 and sqrt(delta); consequently EACH
cross map has diamond norm <=sqrt(delta). This remains true after tensoring
an arbitrary untouched reference. Therefore

  ||Phi-Id_Q tensor Lambda||_diamond
             <=min(2,2 sqrt(delta)+2 delta).            (6)

All distances here are unhalved diamond distances. Equation (6) is a
joint logical/B output statement, not merely a reduced logical fidelity.
The dimension of B and the number of physical spins do not occur. No
measurement, reset, noise floor or change of physical generator is made.

For exp(-i theta X_Q), delta=sin^2(theta) and its distance to identity is
2|sin(theta)|, so an error-amplitude/square-root dependence is necessary.

3. Essential exclusions
------------------------
* Conservation of a single observable alone is insufficient. X dephasing
  strongly preserves X but loses the other information; its delta is 1/2.
* Weak X covariance is insufficient: Z dephasing has zero Z-basis error
  and Bell fidelity 1/2. Its Kraus do not all commute with X.
* A bad decoder Z likewise has zero basis-population error and distance 2
  to identity, but fails the decoder intertwining condition.
* U=I_Q tensor |0><0|_B+X_Q tensor |1><1|_B has E=diag(0,1).
  An average bath error 1/1000 can coexist with worst-bath error 1.
* Classical magnetization or the reduced-memory phase diagram of the split
  up/down NEC instrument does not establish (4) for (1). Splitting the
  coherent jump changes reference information and coherent dynamics.

4. Both CSS sectors / all logical qubits: two complementary effects
------------------------------------------------------------------
This receiver does not require strong symmetry. For k logical qubits let
d=2^k and expand ANY decoded Kraus operator in Hermitian tensor Paulis:

  K_mu=sum_P P tensor B_mu,P,
  E_err=sum_(mu,P!=I) B_mu,P^dagger B_mu,P.              (7)

Normalized logical partial trace of TP gives
sum_mu B_mu,I^dagger B_mu,I+E_err=I_B.

Define E_Z as the bath-valued effect of averaging uniformly over the d
computational-basis inputs and rejecting the identical output bitstring.
Define E_X by the same experiment in the product-Hadamard basis. These
are definitions of diagnostic effects; no such physical measurements are
inserted into the dynamics. Pauli phase orthogonality gives exactly

  E_Z=sum_(P has an X or Y factor,mu) B_mu,P^dagger B_mu,P,
  E_X=sum_(P has a Z or Y factor,mu) B_mu,P^dagger B_mu,P.

One way to see the cancellation is to label Paulis by shift u and phase v.
An incorrect Z output requires u!=0. Averaging input a cancels terms with
different v; different u have orthogonal outputs. Exchange u and v for X.

Consequently E_err<=E_Z+E_X in operator order. Put
delta=||E_Z||+||E_X|| and r=d^2-1. With V_P the Kraus column for B_mu,P,
W_err=sum_(P!=I) P tensor V_P has norm <=sqrt(r delta), since
sum_(P!=I) ||V_P psi||^2=<psi|E_err|psi><=delta||psi||^2.
The identity column has norm <=1. Complete its CP bath map by the single
Kraus sqrt(E_err), making a CPTP comparison Lambda. Expanding the Stinespring
operator W_0+W_err now proves

  ||Phi-Id_logical tensor Lambda||_diamond
      <=min(2,2 sqrt(r delta)+(r+1)delta).               (8)

This deliberately conservative bound has constants depending on the FIXED
number of encoded qubits, not on physical volume or bath dimension. Both
effects must be bounded under the full actual dynamics, including both CSS
sectors and their common bath. Single-sector tails cannot be pasted into
(8) without a transfer proof. For the 4D code k=6, d=64 is fixed.

The basic usefulness of two complementary classical fidelities is standard
(Hofmann, quant-ph/0409083). Equations (7)-(8) give the explicit bath-valued
norm receiver used here; no priority claim for that underlying principle.

6. Conventional fault amplitudes can target E directly
-------------------------------------------------------
The local non-Markovian fault-path method (Aliferis--Gottesman--Preskill,
quant-ph/0504218) suggests an amplitude receiver smaller than the old
channel-norm target. Here is the precise finite transfer, not an import of
a circuit threshold as an autonomous theorem.

Fix an ordered finite circuit of native correction channels and local
noise unitaries U_i on A_i tensor the SAME bath. Choose Stinespring
isometries for its correction channels and its fixed decoder. These are
an analysis of the supplied physical channels, not extra interventions.
Set F_i=U_i-I, ||F_i||<=eta_i. A Hamiltonian micro-slot of strength h_i and
duration dt has eta_i<=h_i dt. A bath-only Hamiltonian can be absorbed in
the interaction picture without changing that norm or memory support;
bath-only dissipation can instead be retained as CPTP isometries.

Let W be the whole circuit isometry. The wrong-output amplitude for the
one computational input is

  W_bad=(<1|_Q tensor I) W (|0>_Q tensor I),
  E=W_bad^dagger W_bad.                                (21)

W_bad includes the native correction and decoder environments as output.
Its input is the arbitrary bath; its norm squared is the WORST-bath error
effect norm, with every reference extension retained.

Expand multilinearly in F_i, leaving identity in the other noise slots.
Fix a support predicate b(S) with b(empty)=0 such that b(S)=0 implies zero
wrong-output amplitude for EVERY supported Pauli choice and ideal Kraus
record on S. The inherited universal Pauli-history certificate is one
sufficient conservative source of such b for completed correction. Since
F_i may have identity pieces, the certificate must cover those too. An
upward closure over supported subhistories is sufficient.

Let a(U)=sum_(V subset U)(-1)^(|U|-|V|) b(V). Boolean inversion proves
the EXACT identity

  W_bad=sum_U a(U) W_bad,[at least U],                   (22)

where F_i is forced at i in U and every other noise slot is the ACTUAL
U_i. Projectors, the ideal isometries and those unrestricted actual slots
all have operator norm <=1. Hence

  ||W_bad||<=w:=sum_U |a(U)| product_(i in U) eta_i,
  E<=min(1,w^2) I_B.                                  (23)

No exp(number-of-harmless-slots) is inserted; they were algebraically
resummed before the norm. No CP rare-fault mixture is asserted for a
unitary perturbation. A suitable decision tree gives the analogous
one-branch amplitude bound by the same good-leaf reasoning.

For the actual COMPLETED generator, condition on its exogenous Poisson
corrector clock schedule h and apply a finite Trotter refinement of its
Hamiltonian between clock events. Write M_h for the conditional positive
error effect and w_h for (23). The full effect is their physical clock
average, so

  delta=|| E_clock M_h ||
         <=E_clock min(1,w_h^2).                        (24)

Thus a sufficient joint-channel estimate is (6) with delta replaced by
the RIGHT side of (24), provided the bound is uniform in refinement and
has the required size/time behavior. The clipped second moment is the
actual receiving obligation here. An ordinary stochastic tail, a first
moment of a different signed channel sum, or (E_clock w_h)^2 cannot be
substituted for it. Correlations with the shared bath do not make the
exogenous physical clock law depend on the quantum input.

This conditional-clock use of N881 is for the completed model. The active
model has not acquired that common quantum schedule certificate from

The present change of receiver has not magically proved causal Mobius
