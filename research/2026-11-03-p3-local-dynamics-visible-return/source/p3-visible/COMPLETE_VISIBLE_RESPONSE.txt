Result

I chose recovery of a system’s complete local dynamical law from controlled snapshots, including systems that cannot be reliably initialized as a whole and whose native dynamics may never equilibrate.

I developed a constructive solution for a precise, broad class of stochastic networks. The result includes the observation operator, its stable inverse, acquisition of informative samples on a retained system, unknown interaction neighborhoods, calibrated reset errors, and explicit statistical and physical resource bounds. I also implemented the inference procedures and compiled the control interface into a formal chemical reaction network.

The completed deliverables are:

Astra_Local_Dynamics_From_Snapshots_20261103.pdf
 — 56 pages containing the main result, complete proofs, experiments, comparisons, and physical limits.
Astra_Local_Dynamics_Research_Package_20261103.zip
 — 58 files containing all derivations, executable code, sufficient observation histograms, numerical records, chemical manifests, reproduction instructions, and a source ledger.

The established result is mathematical and computational. The chemical construction is an exact formal model with explicit fuel accounting. Elementary molecular implementation, historical priority, and civilisation-changing impact remain unestablished.
