## What a research agent can actually reuse

For LA05 the lexical top five contain no N1122 family route. The structured
result is `U1122-semilinear-kernel-dimension`. Its exact source route opens
`CONTACT_COUNTER_AUDIT.md:129–141`, which contains the minimal-dependence proof:
normalize a shortest field dependence, square it, apply the inverse matrix and
subtract; the shorter relation forces every coefficient into F2. Thus binary
independence implies field independence. A researcher with the actual
invertible `m`-coordinate Frobenius system can use `kappa<=m` to budget a
streamed `2^kappa` enumeration independently of the field degree. The result
does not say the kernel is zero or that growing-rank enumeration is polynomial.
The recorded interface leads from this premise to the contact counter with
both nonsingularity gates and legal quotient height intact.

For LA02 the returned eight-moment unit leads to
`Local_Algebra_Finite_Moments_Proof.tex:132–187` and the actual callback and final
production alias. The usable premise is an invertible eight-state moment
system with signed masses, separate radial phase and `0^0=1`, requiring seven
nontrivial ordinary cubic point counts. This can guide an implementation of a
qualified terminal. It cannot supply the unimplemented generic backend for
free. The independent F4 control distinguishes `N_AS-q^k` from the tempting
factor-of-two error and from Boolean reduction of ordinary field powers.

LA03 and LA07 supply decisive negative premises. The actual general-module
audit proves the positive-dimensional critical-locus witness; the actual
fixed-F8 hardness audit places information in higher nilpotent coefficients.
The linked incompatible interfaces prevent replacing the contact gates by a
centroid check, or discarding the nilradical before invoking a field decoder.
These are different obstructions with different witnesses and scope.

withheld acceptance because proofs/code/audits were unacquired. A title hit

leads to the acquired sources and nine version-bound accepted units; old bytes

missing from its parent's declared sources; the authority was completed rather
than weakening the wrong-card-source guard.

mechanisms, historical priority, practical advantage, formal certification and

retrieval failure for the task-specific no-value observation (G06) is unchanged
by this focused acquisition and must not be reported as solved here.
