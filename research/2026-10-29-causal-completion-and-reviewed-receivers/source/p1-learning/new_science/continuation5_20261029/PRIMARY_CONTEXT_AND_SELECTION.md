## Primary comparisons that changed the construction

1. **Kresimir Veselic, Bounds for contractive semigroups and second order
systems**, 2004 preprint / 2005 publication. Read Theorem1 and its energy

uniform relative-energy stability is established. Our compressed bound
uses the zero initial omitted component to gain a second coupling factor.
<https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0404120>

2. **Cubitt, Lucia, Michalakis and Perez-Garcia, Stability of local quantum
dissipative systems.** Read the framework and local-stability motivation;

processes. **Rebeschini and van Handel, Comparison Theorems for Gibbs
Measures**, supplies the strong ordinary Dobrushin comparison context.
The helper developed an actual stationary local-calibration bridge and

<https://arxiv.org/pdf/1303.4744>
<https://web.math.princeton.edu/~rvan/dobrushin130819.pdf>

3. **Barre, Dobson, Ottobre and Zatorska, Fast non mean-field networks:
uniform in time averaging.** Read Theorem2.1 and H.2. This already combines
many-particle and averaging limits. Its displayed uniform bound uses
strong confinement and contains an N epsilon averaging term. Our model,
metric and assumptions differ; it would be false to claim invention of
uniform-in-time many-body averaging.
<https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.14230>

4. **Schuh and Souttar, Conditions for uniform in time convergence:
applications to averaging, numerical discretisations and mean-field

This is a strong general receiver when the actual contraction constants
are supplied. We selected a common-equilibrium relative-energy target
whose bound does not degrade with the visible gap.
<https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.05239>

5. **Duong, Hartmann and Ottobre, Coarse graining of stochastic
differential equations: averaging and projection method.** Read its

<https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.14939>

8. **Zhu, Dominy and Venturi, On the estimation of the Mori-Zwanzig memory
