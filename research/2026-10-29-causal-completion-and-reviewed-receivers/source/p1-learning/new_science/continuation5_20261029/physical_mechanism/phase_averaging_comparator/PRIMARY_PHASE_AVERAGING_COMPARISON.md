[Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1143/JPSJ.9.316),
[primary scan](https://bdt.semi.ac.cn/download/0.7962673851190244.pdf).

was retrieved but did not provide extracted text; no full-paper read is claimed.
Its random frequency-modulation model already gives the characteristic
variance-divided-by-motion-rate narrowing scale. This is a decisive predecessor
for the physical idea that rapid modulation averages unknown fluctuations.
It does not by itself prove the present classical kinetic, complete-operator,
uniform-time and volume guarantee. Those statements require their own model
and norm argument.

The Poisson pi-kick comparator is an ordinary independent random-telegraph
modulation. Its two-point correlation is exp(-2 gamma t), obtained directly
from its two-state generator. The local variance and spectral-gap calculation
in the governing note supplies the exact relative-form constant. Calling this
a new motional-narrowing principle would be unjustified.
