## 1. No-pumping predecessors rule out the opening shortcut

S. Rahav, J. Horowitz and C. Jarzynski, *Directed Flow in Nonadiabatic
Stochastic Pumps*, Physical Review Letters 101, 140602 (2008).
[Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0808.0015),
[publisher](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.101.140602).
Read the model, current decomposition, no-pumping argument, and example around
equations (3)–(6), (11)–(13). Their theorem excludes cyclic pumping when only
well depths or only barriers vary in its stated Arrhenius model; the adiabatic
current has a geometric representation. Our common-equilibrium open-loop
zero-current observation is simpler. Neither it nor a commutator is a new
pumping theorem.

V. Y. Chernyak and N. A. Sinitsyn, *Pumping-Restriction Theorem for Stochastic
Networks*, Physical Review Letters 101, 160601 (2008).
[Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0808.0205),
[publisher](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.101.160601).
Read the examples and theorem around equations (5)–(6). The allowed driven
barriers and network cycle space constrain possible pumped currents, including
the absence of sustained current on tree edges. The eight-joint-state tree in
our note is an operational hidden-state instance of that standard topology
obstruction. Its useful distinction is that a visible triangle can still lift
to a microscopic tree.

## 2. Strong conventional geometric pumping

V. Y. Chernyak and N. A. Sinitsyn, *Robust quantization of a molecular motor
motion in a stochastic environment*, Journal of Chemical Physics 131, 181101
(2009). [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0906.3032),
[publisher](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3263821).
Read the model and pumping-quantization theorem with its order of limits. The
controlled finite network gives integer average pump currents when the
adiabatic limit is followed by the low-temperature limit under its degeneracy
conditions. Their qualification that the particle explores the state space
before controls substantially change is explicit. This is a strong established
kinetically robust pump, not an ineffective incumbent. It uses controlled
energies/barriers and sufficient relaxation; the present finite-rate gate uses
state-dependent coupling and a nonequilibrium affinity instead. No speed or
resource advantage between these different suppliers has been established.

[Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0703492),

T. K. Saha, J. N. E. Lucero, J. Ehrich, D. A. Sivak and J. Bechhoefer,
*Maximizing power and velocity of an information engine*, PNAS 118,
e2023356118 (2021). [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.05478),
[publisher](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2023356118).
Read the introduction and reported feedback/energy interface. This is a real

an actual decision boundary it already supplies direction without kinetic
reconstruction. Consequently that broad capability cannot be our invention.
The exact continuous finite-rate autonomous contract is different, not an
automatic improvement.

## 6. Actual failed attacks preserved

1. **Noncommuting equilibrium modes as a motor:** rejected. An asymmetric
   product kernel does not make elementary physical currents nonzero.
2. **Visible ring plus full joint irreducibility guarantees a turn:** rejected
   by the eight-state tree. Extra hidden memory can unfold the apparent ring.
3. **One chemical driving affinity protects every current:** rejected. The
   four-state cross-link example has one fuel edge but opposite transport
   cycles, with exact reversal when the leak exceeds an unknown useful rate.
4. **Small absolute leakage is uniformly harmless:** rejected by the same
   family at arbitrarily small leakage, using an even slower useful branch.
5. **Rate-independent direction gives a useful speed:** rejected by uniform
   native rate scaling. Controller cost per unit transport can diverge.
6. **A fixed-time fluctuation identity grants first-passage independence:**
   rejected without additional bottleneck/renewal premises.
7. **Native equilibrium fixes controller energy cost or an attached load:**
   rejected. Both need their own physical local-detailed-balance/energy map.

The checks instantiate these boundaries, rather than trying to establish an
infinite-time theorem from a favorable simulation. The result is a completed
constructive alternative and limitation, not a claimed major discovery.
