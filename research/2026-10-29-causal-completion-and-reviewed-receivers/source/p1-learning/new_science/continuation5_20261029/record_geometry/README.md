## What changed the research direction

The strongest ordinary receiver found is Veselić's relative-energy
semigroup comparison: exact common equilibrium plus relative kinetic
error gives a uniform-in-time L2 response bound without a visible
spectral gap. Its primary proof is explicitly credited. Root then
developed a more specific second-order **compressed** bound and an
independent local hidden-modulator/bath construction. The independent
algebraic challenge here supports that proposal at its stated finite-
dimensional, common-measure interface. A later governing proof still
needs its own version-specific review.

The concrete supplier is LOCAL_MOBILITY_MOMENT_ACQUISITION.md. For an
actual heat-bath-mixture call T=I-g(H)(I-K), two successive observed flips
give unbiased g and g^2 estimators from every initial visible state.
A stationary local hidden bath supplies serial trials with an elementary
finite variance/confidence bound. A resolvent-pulse variant supplies a
bounded-bias certificate for native continuous rates when their cap is
warranted. This does not learn or assume away a hidden bath gap.

## Receiving recommendation

For generic acquired L2 defects, keep the fixed-region finite-horizon
objective and its density/locality contract. For a consequential all-time
critical-response objective, the stronger supplier should preserve the
target equilibrium exactly and suppress or relatively bound predictive
kinetic fluctuations. Root's compressed energy construction and the
finite mobility-moment supplier target that connection. The independent
bath, stationary hidden start, structural factorization, conditional
deployment preparation and complete physical execution costs remain real
premises; none follows solely from a fitted visible model.
