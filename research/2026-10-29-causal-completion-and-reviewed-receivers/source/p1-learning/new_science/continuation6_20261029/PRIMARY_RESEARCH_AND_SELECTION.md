## Constructive alternatives that changed selection

**Task-directed identification.** The record-geometry role built a
finite-error behavioral LTI controller from visible calibration and
the current visible past. It learns useful new controls on the
persistent specimen without hidden state recovery or calibration
resets. Its exact rational execution and delayed passive-port
counterexample are in record_geometry/. This belongs to established
Willems/DeePC/data-informativity machinery. Task-Optimal Exploration
in Linear Dynamical Systems also supplies task-specific acquisition
under its LTI interface:
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/wagenmaker21a.html . Learning only
what a task requires is not itself a new idea.

**Physical gradients.** Root considered acquired adjoints and
chronological perturbations for device-generated design gradients.
Unifying Physical Backpropagation, arXiv:2608.11585v2, explicitly
states reciprocity/intertwining and reversal conditions for physical
adjoints, including nonlinear trajectories:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11585 . Its abstract establishes close
precedence for the broad idea. We did not independently validate
all details of its 71-page theory or claim that any device supplies
its sufficient operations.

**Visible response design.** The physical-mechanism role constructed
convex inverse design for a fixed-bandwidth sensor using chronological
endpoint experiments. The ordinary likelihood-ratio score, with
correct tangent subtraction and a reward baseline, beat the proposed
forced-insertion method at matched native-call cost. Forcing remains
useful at zero policy weights, but is not exclusive. The full proof,
primary sources, raw records and executed comparison are in
physical_mechanism/. This ruled out a generic new gradient capability.

## Universal experiments and the direct predecessor

Root constructed a contextual KT selector to replace exponential
action-word coverage by polynomial horizon cost for a continuum of
controllers. The record-geometry role then found two load-bearing
predecessors, which root opened and inspected before finalizing claims:

* Cover--Ordentlich, Universal Portfolios with Side Information,
  IEEE TIT 1996: https://doi.org/10.1109/18.485708 . One-hot market
  returns identify portfolio wealth with the action-selector
  likelihood; the side-information theorem gives the KT exponent.
* Peshkin--Mukherjee, Bounds on sample size for policy evaluation in
  Markov environments, COLT 2001: https://arxiv.org/pdf/cs/0105027 .
  Section 5.2 explicitly connects minimax log-loss regret to the
  likelihood-ratio ceiling and polynomial-horizon policy evaluation.
  It addresses costly actual interaction without a generative model.
  This directly preempts the proposed bridge's novelty.

Ordentlich--Cover's hindsight optimum supplies the multinomial
Shtarkov domination constant, not by itself an adaptive task-learning
lower bound:
https://isl.stanford.edu/people/cover/papers/hindsight.pdf .

Peshkin's dissertation retains a constructive-sampler question for
general policy classes in section 6.5:
https://cs.brown.edu/media/filer_public/9b/d2/9bd22737-5b88-470f-a611-50c0bbbf72cf/peshkin.pdf .
This qualification does not make contextual KT new, since the

## Stronger access and the physical followup

PEGASUS explicitly uses a deterministic simulative model with
learner-controlled random inputs: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1301.3878 .
Replaying scenarios through that model is stronger than actual
hidden-state trajectories. It is a fair conventional alternative
when supplied and cannot be inserted for free into this interface.

The physical-mechanism followup therefore investigates the actual
random-input port. Merely seeing the final random bit, recording
its random reference and programming that reference can have
different learning costs even for the same unexposed marginal
kernel. A threshold comparator gives an ordinary construction;
intrinsic unrecorded noise is the decisive attack. This followup
must not be labeled a new comparator or generic microscopic replay.
