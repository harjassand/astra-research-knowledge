## What has actually been constructed

For arbitrary finite n,m, a fixed rational budget 0<c<1 and a strictly positive
square invertible reference channel L, the ENTIRE feasible set of release
channels K is a spectrahedral shadow. It is not a finite reference search,
an outer bound, a tested parameter family, or a hierarchy with an unacquired
cutoff. The common center and K can be selected jointly through affine PSD
blocks. The inverse-action/covariance algebra is load bearing; the square
informative L condition is essential to this derivation. Fixing c and L is
also essential to the stated affine program. Joint optimization of L and c
is not claimed to be one SDP.

This closes a definite finite computational operation: construct a mechanism
with globally bounded relative information loss and near-optimal linear
utility, then apply the certified rational mechanism using only private
integer coins and a classical input symbol. It does not require quantum
reference states to be supplied or learned. Applying K requires raw X access;
a user with only a noisy L record cannot deploy an arbitrary returned K.
That access is granted equally to all comparisons, not smuggled in as a
measurement capability gained by this mathematics.

prove its validity. Its optimal utility is a linear program. The full floor
K_yi>=1/1000 is imposed on that comparator too, and both its rational primal
attainer and nonnegative-multiplier lower bound are checked separately.

At the declared n=12,m=7,c=1/2 source, the new design has cost at most
0.0510210624815883, whereas the same-floor sufficient family has cost at
least 0.0511789044091336. The modest difference is exact at these contracts,
not a large operational advantage. Dropping the floor gives that simpler
family cost 0.050835275835275834, which is BETTER than our returned floored
design. The original expected-improvement assertion failed at that relaxed
comparison. Its source/proposal/failure remain in development/; the source,
floor, budget and objective were not tuned afterward.

Crucially, the postprocessor/replacer LP is NOT the strongest possible
ordinary algorithm. A source-aware extension of N1038, a free-convex program,
or convex optimization with an exact coefficient/separation oracle may
optimize over the same full feasible set and match the answer. That informed
route is admitted, including its own certificate and conditioning costs.
No competitive speedup or new privacy capacity follows from beating the
sufficient LP subfamily. A conventional mathematical consequence has been
made explicit and executable; not every capability here is a new theorem.

## Scientific boundary and next-step value

The useful result is a complete arbitrary-alphabet synthesis and deployment
operation for an open informative class of classical reference channels,
with no quantum-state search. This is more than another fixed-size coefficient
check. Its original core is nevertheless already supplied by accepted
covariance/free-convex machinery, and an informed ordinary route may adopt
the same construction. The numerical utility gap is too small and too
contract dependent to justify a practical-advantage narrative.

The output has a one-use information guarantee for all sources. It is NOT
a differential-privacy guarantee, universal guessing guarantee, guarantee
for Y published alongside an already observed L record, or a constant-factor
claim on correlated repeated releases. Classical repetition alone disproves
the latter promotion. No implementation or physical claim about private fair
bits, input acquisition or calibration is inferred.

without the square-reference inverse, or an operational task whose criterion
is precisely this conditional information order. Root separately owns a
bounded-entry-error calibration receiver; it is outside this frozen packet.
