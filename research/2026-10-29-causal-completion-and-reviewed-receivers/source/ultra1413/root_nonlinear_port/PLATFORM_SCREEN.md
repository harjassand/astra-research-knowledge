## Existing actuator components

Global interaction tuning is an established cold-atom operation. I read
Gustavsson et al., arXiv0710.5083v1, experimental setup and switching/readout
passages: a Cs condensate in a fixed optical lattice, magnetic tuning near
the contact-interaction zero crossing, and finite ramps. This is a credible
component for changing a common interaction multiplier. Its measurement is
destructive momentum imaging, not our one-port complex field instrument.
Magnetic forces, excited bands, changing orbitals and finite-particle
mean-field error are not bounded by that component result.

I also read the circuit/model and control passages of Miano et al.,
arXiv2112.09785v2. Their two-flux gradiometric SNAIL permits separately
selecting linear and nonlinear coefficients; their device contains20
G-SNAILs in ONE resonator. These are not20 independently evolving hidden
network sites. The paper gives a real control component, not a qualification
of uniform fixed-H Kerr switching on our arbitrary network.

## Equal onsite coefficients are not mathematically essential

At the same ideal classical boundary, let the physical nonlinear force be
N_beta(x)_j=beta_j |x_j|^2 x_j, with fixed real nonzero beta_j and a common
switchable multiplier kappa. Cubic polarization gives exactly

    M_beta(g)=V^T diag[beta_j (Vg)_j^2] V.

For each fixed V and nonzero beta, every pair of diagonal entries differs
as a nonzero polynomial in g: distinct orthogonal rows cannot have identical
weighted squares. A continuous generic g therefore separates all axes
almost surely. The coefficients need not be known individually for this
exact axis step. A finite receiver still needs a beta_max bound and the
ACTUAL slice gap. In the reviewed pulse bias replace its coefficients by
2 Lambda beta_max R^3+(3/2)|kappa| beta_max^2 R^5. Tiny coefficients or
gaps are not made free. This is a root-derived extension observation,
not an independently reviewed new source packet or hardware statement.
N597's existing hidden-weight mechanism already prevents generic novelty.

## A physical change of H gives the ordinary method MORE access too

Suppose the actuator instead supplies two repeatable LINEAR settings
H0 and H1=H0+D, where D is diagonal in the unknown physical coordinates
and has distinct entries. Give the ordinary method this same switch.
The already qualified cyclic H0 port Gram can prepare its modal basis
vectors; the same scalar-port observation frame reads each endpoint of
one H1 evolution. Thus n prepared vectors with n scalar readings each
determine U1=exp(-i H1 tau) in the COMMON H0 modal basis. With a warranted
band and tau avoiding logarithm alias, its principal unitary logarithm
determines H1 in that basis. Diagonalizing H1-H0 then determines physical
axes, up to the same signed permutation. Finite conditioning, reset,
switching and precision costs remain explicit; no small-pulse derivative
limit or nonlinear tensor is needed for this exact model argument.

If D is scalar, this extra anchor is absent. If it is degenerate, rotations
within its eigenspaces remain to be resolved. No generic physical D,
qualified switch or advantageous finite receiver was acquired here. The
point is a strong comparator: uncontrolled but repeatable nonuniform
quadratic drift can be informative access for conventional system
identification, rather than a resource only our nonlinear method receives.

Decision: existing platforms make pieces of the proposed actuator plausible,
but do not pay the full source, port, drift and precision contract. The
shortest useful next question is whether a concrete network has a qualified
interaction switch whose new information survives comparison with its
strongest linear/switched-system learner. Broad cubic-coordinate recovery
and the existence of tunable interactions are already established. This
bounded screen supplies no reason to outrank the completed operations or
declare a new technological breakthrough.
