# Mandatory scope clarifications
2026-10-29.

1. The activation theorem has an untouched quantum reference R. Identity on R is not self-compatible. Consequently it refutes the complete inequality, not automatically an inequality for a product of self-compatible channels acting on EVERY quantum subsystem. Applying noise also to R may change the leading entropy asymptotic.
2. It does not refute a direct trace-distance square-root-loss bound. For the exact family, T(ρ_e,Δσρ_e)=(2/3)√(e(1−e)), and T/√L1 tends to1/√(3log2). The divergent logarithm belongs to the relative-entropy intermediate comparison.
3. The unrotated Petz transpose of Φ is Φ, so Φ² is positive in its KMS geometry. General small Umegaki loss is controlled by rotated/averaged universal recovery, not an unrestricted unrotated-Petz remainder. Such recovered channels must not silently be assumed positive KMS squares. These are distinct proposed routes with distinct unresolved obligations.
4. No unrestricted actual-prior N371 counterexample or no-go theorem is claimed. The theorem identifies a failed entropy-transfer mechanism and motivates direct trace/fidelity arguments.
