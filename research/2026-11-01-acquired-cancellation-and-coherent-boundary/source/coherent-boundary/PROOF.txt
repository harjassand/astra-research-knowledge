POSITIVE GEOMETRIC BARRIER FOR COHERENT CORNER GROWTH
31 October 2026. Solo derivation. Scoped all-size argument; no independent audit.

What is established
-------------------
This constructs a positive, diagonal syndrome observable for an interacting
family of actual plaquette membranes, not for a static set of fault births.
Native pair correction competes with pure coherent corner growth. The bound
is uniform in time, volume, a finite persistent bath's dimension and state,
and logical/reference/bath correlations. No dissipator matching the coherent
terms, extra data-noise floor, random pulse sequence, or physical monitor is
introduced. Logical error is assessed with the same fixed component-filling
decoder used in the retained N881 work.

The price is a substantial restriction on the Hamiltonian: its local syndrome
predicates preserve anchored Young-diagram shapes before the boundary is
reached. Ordinary unconditional single-face Pauli perturbations do not satisfy
that restriction. This is NOT the arbitrary-local-Hamiltonian threshold, a
low-dimensional controller, or a claim of practical advantage or novelty.

1. Physical generator and the allowed Hamiltonian
------------------------------------------------
Use the periodic 4D cubical toric code, 6 L^4 data qubits, six logical qubits.
The primal pair action at a vertex v and directions i<j is

    P_a = (I-S_(v,i))(I-S_(v,j))/4,
    J_a = Z_(v;ij) P_a.

The dual action uses the corresponding Z checks and an X face write. There
are 12 L^4 such pairs across the two CSS sectors. The two correctors below
are separate models, each with its own proof:

    L_active = kappa sum_a D[J_a],
    L_complete = kappa sum_a (D[J_a] + D[I-P_a]).           (1)

In the second model the actual complete pair channels have independent
rate-kappa Poisson proposal clocks. No assertion is made for every clock
realization. The first model has its actual active-jump Lindbladian, including
its different syndrome-coherence decay. We do not identify the two dynamics.

Choose all L^2 primal xy planes and all L^2 dual zw planes. A dual zw face is
a physical xy face; hence primal Z and dual X writes can overlap and
anticommute. Let M=2 L^2 be the number of planes across both sectors. In each
plane use intrinsic torus coordinates (x,y)=0,...,L-1, with a chosen anchor
at (L-1,L-1). The four syndrome bits around a face are ordered

    (bottom, left, top, right).

For that face define the add pattern

    a_f = (0,0, 1_(y != L-1), 1_(x != L-1)),
    Pi_a = product_e (I + (-1)^(a_f[e]) S_e)/2,
    A_f = Q_f Pi_a,                                      (2)

where Q_f is the appropriate Z or X write. Q_f toggles all four bits, so
A_f maps the add pattern to its bitwise complement and A_f^dagger reverses it.
The coherent perturbation is

    H_corner = h sum_f (A_f tensor B_f + A_f^dagger tensor B_f^dagger),
    ||B_f|| <= 1.                                       (3)

The B_f act on ONE arbitrary finite persistent bath. They need not commute,
be Hermitian, be independent, or be refreshed. Any bath-only Lindbladian
L_B is allowed. The complete joint generator is either corrector in (1),
plus L_B and -i[H_corner+H_ext,.]. All operators act trivially on a reference.

Each term in (3) has norm <=h: A_f connects orthogonal syndrome-pattern
subspaces. Four weight-six edge checks and their common face have support
on at most 21 data qubits, in a bounded geometric neighbourhood. There are
2 L^4 base terms and bounded incidence per physical qubit. This is a local
many-body perturbation class, not a single-qubit noise model.

H_ext may be any supplied Hermitian extension with H_ext P=0, where P is
the safe syndrome projector defined below. In particular, local bridge terms
at the torus cuts make a logical membrane accessible after escape. They are
specified in Section 8. They change no safe-source transition. The extension
condition is essential; it does not admit arbitrary perturbations of (3).

2. Safe syndrome subspace and actual correction geometry
--------------------------------------------------------
Fix m>=2 with m+O(1)<L/32; for example m=floor(L/64) for sufficiently large L.
In one plane, an allowed membrane is the upper-right order filter in the
anchored m by m box. Its column heights, from left to right, satisfy

    0 <= n_0 <= n_1 <= ... <= n_(m-1) <= m.

Column j occupies its top n_j faces. These are Young diagrams with all
plateaus, corners, and overlaps retained. Let P select all joint syndromes
arising from one such diagram in EVERY selected primal/dual plane, with the
remaining syndrome bits zero. Include the whole six-logical-qubit sector and
the arbitrary bath, not just one logical state. Distinct safe diagrams have
distinct syndromes. Different parallel planes have disjoint occupied syndrome
edges; their components do not join. The opposite CSS sector has its own
independent syndrome labels, even though the Pauli actions can anticommute.

Within P the physical pair predicate is active exactly at each southwest
minimal occupied face. It removes that face. In height coordinates it is

    n_j -> n_j-1 at rate kappa iff n_j > n_(j-1),
    n_(-1)=0.                                           (4)

All other pairs in that sector are inactive. Opposite-sector proposals leave
these syndrome bits unchanged. Thus correction never exits P. This is the
actual native predicate, not an assumed death process for a cluster size.

The local Hamiltonian (2) adds or removes precisely one allowed Young corner.
Inside P, a write changes one column height by one and one row width by one.
It can leave the safe box only at two faces per plane: the next face below
the rightmost column or the next face left of the top row. These exits have
source conditions max(n)=m and max(row widths)=m, respectively. All other
base terms annihilate P. This statement includes the physical periodic edges;
the code checks it with the torus syndrome rather than an open-boundary model.

3. Fixed recovery frame and a positive comparison
------------------------------------------------
Let E_s be the product of the primal Z and dual X Young membranes producing
s, with a fixed order. On safe syndromes E_s differs from the inherited fixed
component filling only by stabilizers and syndrome-dependent phases: both
fill the same contractible component, inside a box of diameter m+O(1)<L/32.
Consequently the isometry

    U(|ell> tensor |s>) = E_s V|ell>

is a recovery frame for the SAME fixed logical decoder. Every safe-source,
safe-target local write is I_logical tensor a syndrome/bath operator in this
frame. This remains true for both CSS sectors together. Their mutual Pauli
phases are retained in the syndrome matrix elements, not discarded.

For operators supported on P define K(X)=P L(PXP) P. Since every corrector
jump maps P into P and its squared modulus is syndrome diagonal, K is a
genuine CPTP generator ON P: Hamiltonian PHP, the retained native jumps,
and the same bath generator. No jump loss occurs in this particular domain.
In the recovery frame it factors

    e^(tK) = Id_logical tensor e^(t k_safe).               (5)

It follows that decoding the comparison evolution is exactly
Id_logical tensor Lambda_B,t for a CPTP bath channel, uniformly for initial
logical/bath/reference correlations. P is an analytical projection, not a
physical measurement. The full evolution is not equated with (5).

4. Positive geometric observable and the eroder estimate
-------------------------------------------------------
For one diagram set w_j=2^(m-1-j) and

    F_v(n) = sum_(j=0)^(m-1) w_j 16^(n_j-m),
    f_0 = F_v(0) = (2^m-1)/16^m < 8^(-m).                (6)

Let F_h be the same formula for row widths from bottom to top. Both are
positive diagonal syndrome OPERATORS, tensored with identity on the whole
logical/bath/reference system. If the vertical extent is m then F_v>=1;
if the horizontal extent is m then F_h>=1.

For any positive-height plateau j=a,...,b, only its leftmost column is active
under (4), and

    w_a >= (1/2) sum_(j=a)^b w_j.

Removing an active bottom face changes its column term by -15/16 times that
term. Summing plateaus, including all zero-height columns, gives the pointwise
diagonal inequality

    L_corrector^*(F_v) <= -gamma_0 F_v + gamma_0 f_0 I,
    gamma_0 = (15/32) kappa.                              (7)

Transposition gives exactly the same bound for F_h. Equation (7) holds for
BOTH native generators (1). On a diagonal syndrome observable the inactive
dephasing term has zero adjoint action; this is a direct calculation, not a
transfer from the completed generator's noise theorem. Cross-sector writes
do not change that observable. Bath-only evolution also annihilates it.

Equal column weights would give only 1/(plateau length) of the potential in
an active corner. The geometric weights remove that size loss, at the cost
of the code source f_0. Its exponential smallness requires 16>2.

5. Coherent bound, including all off-diagonal blocks
--------------------------------------------------
Group all allowed upward writes in column j into R_j, including their bath
operators and the actual CSS phases. Each source height configuration has at
most one target under that column increment, the map of configurations is
injective, and each bath block is a contraction. Hence ||R_j||<=1.
All other column heights are unchanged. On F_j=w_j 16^(n_j-m),

    F_j^(-1/2) i[h(R_j+R_j^dagger),F_j] F_j^(-1/2)
        = i h (4-1/4)(R_j^dagger-R_j)
        <= (15/2) h I.                                  (8)

No bath commutation or stochastic Pauli model is used. A write in column k
commutes with F_j if k!=j, including when its matrix element depends on other
syndromes. Thus, after summing j,

    i[PHP,F_v] <= (15/2) h F_v,
    i[PHP,F_h] <= (15/2) h F_h.                           (9)

The row proof groups the SAME Hamiltonian by row; it is not a second physical
Hamiltonian. Intersecting primal/dual planes preserve one another's syndrome
heights, so the same bound applies to the sum over both sectors. This is a
Loewner-order inequality on the entire safe Hilbert space, including syndrome
coherences and arbitrary bath/reference extensions.

An equivalent exact check uses one PSD 2x2 block per allowed height edge.
Its diagonal entries are h(15/4) f_s and h(15/4) f_t, and the magnitude of
its off-diagonal entry is h(f_t-f_s). With f_t=16 f_s its determinant is zero.
For a bath contraction the normalized block is [[I,iB],[-iB^dagger,I]]>=0.
Each height vertex is incident to at most two such edges per column; unused
diagonal slack is positive. This also proves (8) directly.

6. Uniform quantum boundary concentration
-----------------------------------------
Set F=sum over all M planes of (F_v+F_h), and

    F_code = 2 M f_0,
    gamma = (15/32) kappa - (15/2) h.

Equations (7)-(9) establish the explicit operator certificate

    K^*(F) <= -gamma F + gamma_0 F_code I.                (10)

If 0<=h<=kappa/32 then gamma>=15 kappa/64=gamma_0/2>0.
Starting from the code with ANY joint logical/bath/reference input, positivity
and (10) imply

    Tr(F tau_t) <= F_code [e^(-gamma t)
                         +(gamma_0/gamma)(1-e^(-gamma t))]
                 <= 2 F_code < 4 M 8^(-m).               (11)

This is the sought type of positive quantum occupation estimate for THIS
interacting constrained model. It is not an absolute sum of marked witnesses,
and it is not inferred from stochastic large-deviation tails.

7. Actual logical channel, full bath and continuum
--------------------------------------------------
Let Q=I-P. From safe inputs, each of the two possible exit writes in each
plane has a different target-syndrome range: one plane has just extended
vertically or horizontally beyond m, while all other planes remain safe.
Those ranges are pairwise orthogonal. A bath block has norm <=1. Therefore

    PHQHP <= h^2 sum_planes (B_v+B_h) <= h^2 F,            (12)

where B_v and B_h project onto the two source extent conditions. No extensive
||H|| bound is needed. Additional H_ext P=0 makes no contribution.

All dissipative terms preserve P, so the full residual on tau_t=e^(tK)rho is
just its two Hamiltonian cross blocks. Hilbert-Schmidt Cauchy-Schwarz gives

    ||(L-K) tau_t||_1 <= 2 sqrt(Tr(tau_t PHQHP))
                       <= 4 h sqrt(M) 8^(-m/2).           (13)

Duhamel's exact identity, full CPTP contractivity, and the fixed decoder give

  inf_(Lambda_B CPTP)
    || (Dec tensor Id_B) e^(T L) (Enc tensor Id_B)
                     - Id_logical tensor Lambda_B ||_diamond
       <= min{2, 4 h T sqrt(M) 8^(-m/2)},

  M=2 L^2, m=floor(L/64), h<=kappa/32, L sufficiently large.   (14)

One may take Lambda_B to be the bath output of (5). The input includes the
joint logical and bath system; an untouched arbitrary reference is retained.
There is no Choi dimension factor, bath reset, or independence assumption.
For finite L and a finite bath, these are bounded finite-dimensional GKSL
generators and Duhamel is exact continuous-time calculus. There is no mesh
limit or exchange of thermodynamic and Trotter limits. The constants do not
depend on bath dimension or bath-only dynamics.

With m proportional to L, (14) has exponential size suppression and a linear
time factor at fixed positive h/kappa. In particular it implies the looser
C L^4 (1+kappa T) exp(-c L) form. The only volume factor above is sqrt(M),
from a positive sum of boundary occupations, not exp(volume*time).

8. Local extensions and an accessible logical fault
--------------------------------------------------
The base corner rule alone has a hard wall at the torus cuts: its accessible
membranes never enter row 0 or column 0. That would make a lifetime example
unpersuasive. Add local Hamiltonian bridge terms Q_f Pi_b and their adjoints
with the same bounded bath-block convention:

    at x=0: b=(0,1,1_(y!=L-1),1),
    at y=0: b=(1,0,1,1_(x!=L-1)).                        (15)

The forward and reverse patterns both annihilate every safe diagram:
on column 0 a safe diagram has at most its left edge occupied; on row 0 at
most its bottom edge is occupied. At (0,0) all four bits are zero. Thus
H_bridge P=0 exactly. The extensions add 4 L^3 terms across both CSS families,
still of support at most 21 and bounded degree. Their strengths can be
bounded by h, although (14) needs only their exact annihilation of P.

There is an explicit all-L legal route to a logical membrane. First grow the
(L-1) by (L-1) Young square using corner Hamiltonian writes. Fill column 0
from top to bottom using the vertical bridges, then row 0 from right to left
using horizontal bridges, leaving only face (0,0) empty. Its four boundary
bits are now one; the native pair action fills it. The all-face plane is a
nontrivial toric logical membrane. This path uses L^2-1 coherent writes and
one native correction. For scalar positive bath blocks its leading coherent
path amplitudes have the same phase, and it is not excluded by an exact
support invariant. This is a reachability statement, NOT a lower bound on
the memory's logical failure rate or a useful lifetime estimate.

An exact finite 0/1 shortest-path calculation on a 4 by 4 plane finds an
even shorter legal route, with seven coherent writes and eleven correction
writes. The route is retained in verification.json. It is not used in (14).

9. Limits that remain live
--------------------------
* The fault predicates in (2) and (15) are essential. An unconditional Z_f
  at a face outside the anchored box creates an immediate unsafe component
  from the code. For that actual perturbation the comparison susceptibility
  is h^2 at t=0, and the exponential source used here is false. This does not
  prove failure of the real memory; it falsifies extending this safe domain
  to arbitrary local faults. Random anchors or a hull rooted at one anchor
  do not supply the missing multi-component coherent estimate.
* Native winding strips are dark for the pair corrector. They lie outside
  this safe domain and remain counterexamples to a global relaxation-gap
  argument. No global code-only inverse has been assumed.
* Overlapping Young corners, column plateaus, both CSS phases and one shared
  bath ARE included. Arbitrary disjoint error nucleations, non-Young shapes,
  coalescing independently anchored components and all local-Hamiltonian
  perturbations are NOT included.
* Preparation, all 4D local correction operations, the engineered syndrome
  predicates and ideal final fixed decoder are supplied. There is no 2D
  autonomous controller, hardware experiment, thermal/resource advantage,
  efficient physical implementation, or publication/novelty conclusion.

The advance over the retained one-face calibration is an explicit positive
barrier for exponentially many interacting causal syndrome shapes, with a
native continuous-time logical-channel bound. The general coherent frontier
still requires a representation that handles nucleation and mergers without
the artificial anchor/shape restriction. The old native-bath, receiver,
Moebius, scan and AND-eroder results remain unchanged.
