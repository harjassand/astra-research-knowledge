## The strongest result: acquired protection that remains usable during finite two-body gates

I developed a construction that reduces the continuing correction burden from a specified common-noise component—even when its couplings are unequal and there is no exact noiseless quantum subspace.

The central advance is **an acquired encoding together with a bound on the noise exposure incurred when operating on it**. The construction permits long protected storage, then opens only a small part of that protection for each gate. It includes finite two-body controls, the complete quantum output, and errors that previously leaked outside the deepest code.

A practical advantage in total physical cost remains unestablished. Calibration, source preparation, residual independent noise, and raw loading/release remain substantial obligations. The complete account is in :chatgpt-content-reference{index="5"}[MAIN_RESULT.md](producer-workspace/MAIN_RESULT.md).

### 1. Acquiring protection without exact symmetry

Consider the actual generator
\[
\dot\rho=-i[H(t),\rho]+\sum_{a=1}^{r}\mathcal D[L_a](\rho),
\qquad
L_a=\sum_i w_{ai}Z_i,
\]
where \(r\) is fixed and
\[
\mathcal D[L](\rho)=L\rho L-\tfrac12\{L^2,\rho\}.
\]

A complementary two-qubit encoding replaces two coupling vectors by their difference. I construct pairs with similar acquired vectors, then repeat that operation on the encoded pairs.

After a fixed number \(k\) of rounds, deterministic grouping retains \(\Theta(n)\) logical qubits in blocks of \(b=2^k\) physical qubits. For the general fixed-rank construction,
\[
\max_{a,j}|v_{aj}|=O(n^{-k/r}),
\qquad
\sum_j|v_{aj}|=O(n^{1-k/r}).
\]

The classical acquisition costs \(O(rkn\log n)\) arithmetic/comparison work. The resulting encoder and inverse receiver use \(O(n)\) ordinary gates and no arithmetic quantum workspace. Coefficient intervals enter the certificate explicitly.

This protection can exist when every charge eigenvalue is distinct. Exact noise-algebra dimension therefore misses this finite-time capability; the earlier capacity and entropy results remain valid in their stated long-time and acquired-contraction scopes. The construction and finite constants are proved in :chatgpt-content-reference{index="6"}[THEOREM.md](producer-workspace/THEOREM.md).

Approximate decoherence-free subspaces and pair-based protection are established. Hamann–Sekatski–Dür already construct approximate subspaces for spatially correlated sensing, and Lidar–Bacon–Whaley already combine collective protection with conventional error correction. The contribution here is the acquired hierarchy and its operational control certificate. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="0"}

### 2. The important control obstruction—and its resolution

The first construction had a serious problem. Its two logical basis words differ on every physical qubit in a block. A Hamiltonian acting on fewer than all those qubits cannot directly rotate between them while exactly preserving that two-dimensional space.

I resolved this by retaining a shallower layer of protection during the gate.

Inactive blocks remain in their deep codes. An active block temporarily explores its constituent first-pair spaces. Physical \(XX\), representative \(Z\), and representative-to-representative \(ZZ\) interactions preserve those pair spaces throughout their finite pulses. A CNOT ladder implements the deep logical \(X\) rotation; logical \(Z\) and \(ZZ\) already preserve the deep code.

Let \(A(t)\) denote the active blocks. The complete internal error obeys
\[
\boxed{
\delta_{\mathrm{internal}}
\le
2\int_0^T\sum_a
\left[
\sum_{j\notin A(t)}|v^{\mathrm{deep}}_{aj}|
+
\sum_{p\text{ inside }A(t)}|v^{\mathrm{pair}}_{ap}|
\right]^2dt .
}
\]

This is a **full diamond-distance bound**, uniform over unknown inputs and arbitrary references.

The proof uses an invariant subspace containing the ideal trajectory and an exact Duhamel identity. Subsequent actual evolution contracts each error contribution. Consequently, earlier noisy leakage does **not** have to be perfectly removed before the next gate. Merely bounding the instantaneous ideal state would have been insufficient; the larger invariant subspace is essential.

For one common field, the acquired first-pair coefficients can all be \(O(1/n)\). At fixed \(k\ge2\), this gives:

| Resource | Earned sufficient scaling at fixed accuracy |
|---|---|
| Retained logical width | \(\Theta(n)\) |
| Passive idle duration | \(\Omega(n^{2k-2})\) |
| Serial logical \(X\) gates at fixed control strength | \(\Theta(n^2)\) |
| Largest native interaction support | Two physical qubits |
| Continuing resets during the qualified internal computation | None |

Simultaneous activation has a quadratic common-noise cost. Increasing the encoding depth extends idle life without automatically increasing the two-body gate budget. Pulse precision and additional gate faults remain separately charged.

The proof applies more broadly to Hermitian noise operators, including noncommuting ones, when suitable invariant subspaces can actually be acquired. The coefficient hierarchy supplies that acquisition for the stated commuting model. See :chatgpt-content-reference{index="7"}[ACTIVE_CONTROL.md](producer-workspace/ACTIVE_CONTROL.md).

Universal two-body control inside exact collective decoherence-free subspaces was already established by Kempe–Bacon–Lidar–Whaley. The addition here is the acquired unequal-coupling representation and the controlled excursions between its protection levels. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}

### 3. Conventional coding strengthens the consequence

Giving established methods the same representation produced a materially better result.

An ordinary outer phase-error code can correct the weak residual rotations, rather than requiring every encoded coherence to remain nearly unchanged. I derived its complete receiver bound and an explicit measured-syndrome recovery interface with the collaborator developing coding alternatives.

For the scalar hierarchy, a final trim of **certified** coefficient bounds gives a per-block maximum \(O(n^{-k})\). With a positive-rate outer code, the resulting qualified storage horizon becomes
\[
T=\Omega\!\left(\frac{n^{2k}}{\log(1/\delta)}\right),
\]
at systematic coefficient precision \(O(n^{-k})\). The direct passive certificate gives the \(n^{2k-2}\) horizon at that precision.

The combination includes protected syndrome ancillas, polynomial Clifford circuits, classical decoding time, measurement, and receiver noise. For \(k\ge3\), an \(O(n^2)\) classical wait has a vanishing deep-noise contribution. At \(k=2\), its finite constant needs its own budget.

This uses **one final recovery**, with its records and preparation paid, and no entropy outlet during the storage interval. It is established coding enhanced with the acquired representation. The general protocol is proved; its large-scale quantum execution has not been performed. The full construction is in :chatgpt-content-reference{index="8"}[SCALAR_OUTER_OPERATIONAL_COROLLARY.md](producer-workspace/SCALAR_OUTER_OPERATIONAL_COROLLARY.md).

This comparison also changes acquisition costs: the outer receiver can tolerate substantially poorer coefficient calibration than the passive certificate. Charging both methods the passive precision requirement would erase a real conventional advantage.

### 4. Executions and the complete cost boundary

Exact rational acquisition and circuit construction ran on scalar inputs through **16,384 spins**, plus rank-two and rank-three inputs. A separate 16-spin rational instance had **all 65,536 charge eigenvalues distinct**, while still admitting the approximate construction.

The decisive dynamical test was smaller: an eight-physical-qubit, two-logical-qubit program with 25 finite noisy pulses. Its reference-output trace error was **0.0274588**, below the proved full-diamond bound **0.0685132**. Actual final deep-code leakage was **0.00639434**. Thus the execution directly exercised the leakage issue resolved by the proof. The trace error is a numerical diagnostic, not a computed diamond norm. Results and executable acquisition are in :chatgpt-content-reference{index="9"}[RESULTS.json](producer-workspace/RESULTS.json), :chatgpt-content-reference{index="10"}[acquire.py](producer-workspace/acquire.py), and :chatgpt-content-reference{index="11"}[qualify.py](producer-workspace/qualify.py).

I also closed a finite **bare-input/bare-output resource plan**, rather than stopping at the internal certificate. For 288 logical qubits, a million units of idle time, and five internal logical \(X\) gadgets, its complete modeled error is below \(0.005\), including a calibration-failure allowance.

Its costs are poor:

- The conservative raw boundary circuit asks for control strength around \(1.08\times10^{11}\) in the normalized model units.
- The inherited warm-source procedure supplies 2,016 pure auxiliaries with a very large gate and accuracy bill.
- The specified independent bounded-observation calibration procedure asks for about \(1.59\times10^{22}\) observations.

Those source and calibration operations were **not executed**. These are sufficient costs of chosen procedures, not fundamental lower bounds. They demonstrate why inexpensive representation acquisition from a supplied coefficient table cannot be confused with inexpensive physical acquisition. An encoded physical quantum output can be consumed by subsequent certified gates. Delivering a bare register still requires the actual inverse circuit and its continuing-noise allowance. A fixed independent-noise floor also survives: on a \(b\)-leaf block, independent dephasing becomes logical dephasing at rate \(b\gamma\). Long operation under that floor requires active correction and real entropy outlets.

### 5. Preserved failures and further capital

The complementary constructors developed their alternatives far enough to affect the choice:

- **Noise monitoring:** finite noiseless electronic bandwidth was not intrinsically lost information when the filter endpoint remained available. The underlying monitoring-plus-QEC mechanism was already established. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}
- **Warm reservoir dressing:** finite reversible constructions were earned, but established prior work, a free-off-switch loophole, and a single-mode recurrence alternative defeated superficial advantage claims. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="3"}
- **Mixed syndromes:** the entropy-demand theorem now holds at arbitrary coherent checkpoints:
  \[
  S(\mathcal N\rho)-S(\rho)
  \ge H(S\oplus N\mid C)-H(S\mid C).
  \]
  A tracked mixed syndrome retains fresh entropy demand; an exact receiver example shows that untracked disorder can worsen storage. This is preserved in :chatgpt-content-reference{index="13"}[SYNDROME_UNCERTAINTY_THEOREM.md](producer-workspace/SYNDROME_UNCERTAINTY_THEOREM.md).

All inherited capital remains unchanged.

**The consequential result is a constructive tradeoff between acquired noise correlations, idle lifetime, active quantum operations, and physical interfaces.** It supplies an explicit way to reduce continuing correction of a dominant common-noise component while retaining usable quantum control. The remaining feasibility question is whether that representation can be acquired and maintained—with residual noise, source preparation, and terminal access included—more economically than informed conventional correction.
