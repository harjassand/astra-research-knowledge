7. Contribution, predecessors and remaining gaps

Dynamic sampling and incremental inference have strong predecessors. Feng, He, Sun and Yin use local optimal couplings and probability-based resampling for changing graphical models. Cusumano-Towner and colleagues translate execution traces and correct them using probability-based weights. These are close precedents, with different model classes and available information.

Bernoulli-factory research already supplies nonlinear operations on unknown probabilities, including polynomial-query constructions for exponential-weight choices. That prevented treating generic exact selection from unknown coin means as the new contribution.

The operation developed here is more specific:

Condition a useful coupling on a sample that is already recorded, execute that conditional operation using fresh bits alone, and compose it into exact trace repair and a reversible bridge between models.

The completed work establishes:

Exact recorded-bit transport, including a finite cap that preserves marginals.
Exact joint-law repair for a fixed acyclic binary computation.
Local expected-work guarantees under stated propagation conditions.
Executed repair comparisons against independent local resampling and the stronger early-stopping comparator.
Exact and finite-accuracy freshness lower bounds under their stated information contracts.
A valid posterior bridge, together with a comparison that did not support an inference speedup.

The following remain unestablished:

A useful end-to-end scientific inference advantage beyond the controlled repair benchmark.
General posterior mixing guarantees.
Online acquisition of the depth-dependent stopping policies at an accounted cost.
A general extension to arbitrary cyclic models, changing program structure, or final-output-only simulators.
A practical physical implementation with favorable total resource costs.
Priority relative to all existing coupling and dynamic-sampling constructions.
8. Earlier results and failures remain unchanged

The physical common-null construction, finite correction mechanism, passive-energy theorem and binary returned-law interface retain their original assumptions and review scopes. This work does not supply universal preparation, readout, conditional sampling access or access to an old physical model after irreversible change.

The original sample-only coupling’s exact marginals, expected-accuracy scaling and stronger early-stopping comparator remain preserved.

So do the adverse comparisons:

Ordinary simulation won the earlier finite-accuracy stationary-average task.
The earlier bounded-sensor optimization execution did not beat its zero-experiment certificate: the reported certified radii remained worse than the

0.282843 zero-command guarantee.
The prior roughly 1.2 million expected calendar-window accounting for one coupled pair remains a serious limitation of the passive construction, not a hardware validation.

All new counts above are ideal source flips, not physical calendar windows. The private keys, finite threshold resolution, fixed response model, calibrated instruments and warranted energy accounting of the physical interface remain substantive resources.

A full returned-transcript error bound can pass through a finitely capped computation. It does not, by itself, control an unbounded estimator, an unbounded physical runtime, or a perturbed Markov chain’s stationary law.

The strongest recovered capability is therefore exact maintenance of complete stochastic states through local model revisions under sample-only access. The repair executions support that capability. The posterior failure and freshness bounds identify where its correlation savings stop being a substitute for additional scientific information.
