A meaningful development since my update: we now have a candidate proof that the unwanted extra low-energy states can be excluded for a finite-width device of any fixed cross-sectional size, provided the device is made sufficiently long. An independent internal audit has checked the main construction, including a subtle mismatch between the mathematical descriptions of the two systems. The qualification matters: the required length and computational resources may grow badly with the cross-section. This does not yet establish the fixed-resource large-system result or dynamical gauging, and it has not had external review. I’m checking those remaining steps rather than treating this as the finished breakthrough.
