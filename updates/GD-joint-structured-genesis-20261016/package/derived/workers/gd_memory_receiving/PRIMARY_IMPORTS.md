## Hartarsky and Toninelli: acquired and version checked

The saved PDF and HTML are the explicit **v2** of *Kinetically constrained models out of equilibrium*, arXiv:2212.08437. The arXiv version line is 11 March 2024; the PDF's internal title date is 13 March 2024. The relevant statement is Corollary 9.5, PDF page 25, HTML Section 9.2. It concerns any fixed finite connection radius and an attractive eroder under sufficiently rare iid deaths, using the upper stationary trajectory. Its diameter tail has exponential form. The source proof depends on Toom-contour results; those deeper antecedents were not independently re-proved here.

The receiver's native flags, cold initialization, finite torus and quantum decoder require the additional arguments reconstructed in `RECEIVING_PROOF.md`. They are not literal conclusions of that corollary. Section 10, Eq. (10.6), corroborates the cold-to-stationary comparison direction.

Primary links: [v2 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.08437v2), [v2 Section 9.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.08437v2#S9.SS2).

## Liggett, Schonmann and Stacey: original pages not acquired

The named original is *Domination by product measures*, *Annals of Probability* 25(1), 71-95 (1997), DOI `10.1214/aop/1024404279`. The preserved source packet explicitly imports the qualitative bounded-degree neighborhood-conditional domination theorem. This investigation reconstructs the fixed-primitive-support hypotheses for that import; it does not pretend to have inspected the original proof.

Direct publisher `.pdf` and `.full` endpoints, the legacy publisher download endpoint, JSTOR's article/PDF route, and the author's listed `papers/lss.dvi` were attempted through ordinary public retrieval. The saved direct retrieval receipt records publisher and JSTOR HTML access challenges and an author-file 404. Web searches found corroborating primary applications and the author's bibliography, but no accessible original theorem pages. No challenge was solved, no paywall was bypassed, and no original theorem number is assigned here.

The exact standard import remains: on a fixed bounded-degree dependency graph, sufficiently high uniform healthy probabilities conditional on all vertices outside the closed neighborhood imply domination of an iid healthy field with any prescribed density below one. Independence from the entire distant sigma field verifies that conditional premise for the native slab flags. The imported theorem, rather than internal worker agreement or numerical checks, is the probability premise.

Primary bibliographic routes: [publisher](https://projecteuclid.org/journals/annals-of-probability/volume-25/issue-1/Domination-by-product-measures/10.1214/aop/1024404279.full), [author bibliography](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~rhs/papers.html).
