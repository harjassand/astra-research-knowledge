## Acquisition limits and identity correction

Nature HTML access attempts for several articles encountered cookie/identity-provider failures. Those attempts are not full-text evidence; accessible author PDFs were separately acquired. The DeBry publisher PDF request returned HTML. Its saved response and receipt preserve this failure.

An early prospective filename associated the DeBry publisher URL with Li. Before freezing, the receipt and response filename were corrected to S11_debry_2026_published_attempt, and the two author sources were acquired and read separately. No numerical result was transferred between them.

No raw hardware traces, waveforms, calibration posterior, control-energy measurement, noise catalog for the supplied corrector, or measured common fault/correction rate budget was acquired. These remain NOT_ACQUIRED even though the papers are acquired in full.
