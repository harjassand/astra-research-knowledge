# Before outcome-trace inspection, 2026-10-10 UTC

Question: Does externally imposed rotor motion instantaneously enable torque-generating stator capture, or first populate a latent mechanically primed state that becomes torque-generating after forcing ends?

Selected evidence: Ito, Nakamura, Toyabe (2021), DOI 10.1038/s41467-021-23516-y. Public figshare 14371232.v2, raw 4000-Hz rotational traces. Article authors already establish rotation-enhanced assembly, suggest gear-driven activation and MotB stretching, and report direction asymmetry. Those facts are prior art, not our invention.

The raw archive directory has been read, but no outcome trace has been inspected as of this document. Published figures and manuscript were examined, so this is an analysis plan, not a preregistration.

Candidate comparison: (i) T-cell optogenetic proofreading has outstanding importance and independently manipulated ligand lifetime, but observed cluster persistence and nucleation-memory mechanisms are already directly anticipated by the papers; reporters are often separate cell populations. (ii) osmotic membrane adaptation has many knockout interventions but many jointly changing hydraulic, ionic and endocytic variables, so mechanism identification is weaker. (iii) motor assembly permits external rotation reversal and pulsed cessation with the same observable on subsecond timescales. Select (iii) on identifiability, not the strongest-sounding anomaly.

Primary candidate, to be falsified: unbound D -> primed P during external motion at rate alpha(direction, speed), then P -> torque-generating A at rate beta, competing P -> D at rate gamma. Motion can prepare a state even while no active stator exists. In an OFF interval with negligible new priming, the extra activation density from P decays with beta+gamma; its lifetime is a physical memory of mechanical exposure. Instantaneous memoryless activation predicts a constant baseline OFF hazard after instrument-edge guard time.

Primary statistic: time of first sustained positive autonomous rotation in OFF intervals, relative to the end of the preceding ON pulse. Compare early versus late OFF hazard using at-risk exposure, censoring, cell-level clustering and guard-time robustness. Distinguish delayed torque onset from delayed attachment, since torque alone cannot observe docking. An early spike confined to the guard zone is an edge/measurement effect, not a priming discovery. Negative controls: no-pulse recovery and autonomous recovery; directional reversal is a separate intervention. Consider latent motor-specific rates, declining susceptible pool and glass pinning as alternatives.

Construction/validation: read experimental-conditions workbooks first. Partition by stable cell identifier, not by trace, to prevent repeated-cell leakage. Use deterministic SHA256 of cell identifier for discovery/validation assignment. Start with a small balanced subset of each direction selected without examining outcomes; expand only to improve precision under the original statistic. Never label a hypothesis made from discovery traces independently confirmed until held-out cells are tested without changes to extraction. Preserve exploratory modifications and outcome failures.

Nulls/stop: If OFF onset is unresolvable from displacement/noise, or metadata cannot identify cells/interventions, do not claim a mechanism. If priming and pinning/release are observationally equivalent, conclude the limitation rather than invent molecular attribution. If the candidate is already stated/tested in prior literature, attribute it and only seek a distinct quantitative invariant.

## Frozen validation extraction after discovery, before validation download

The 22-cell discovery subset (8 assisting, 8 hindering, 6 unforced) was inspected. Full plots and provisional detections are retained. The centroid-angle field contains failures; use the long-axis/PCA revolution field, matching the paper's method. Normalize rotation sign to each trace's measured 1–9 s baseline, never assume the forcing label determines autonomous direction. One discovery baseline is negative. A sign-normalization mistake in the first exploratory plotting script is corrected in the frozen test script; its outputs are retained as exploratory only.

Frozen script: analysis/test_off_memory.py. Velocity is the degree-1 Savitzky–Golay derivative of long-axis angle over 401 samples (100.25 ms), then sampled at 100 Hz. Baseline is the median velocity from 1–9 s. Default active threshold is 5% baseline; sensitivity thresholds 2.5%,7.5% are fixed. An onset requires 14 of 15 successive 10-ms samples above threshold. Guards are 100 ms (primary) and 200 ms (sensitivity), longer than the centered derivative's 50-ms lookahead. An initially active OFF or an onset within 50 ms of its guard is unresolved/immediate. A resolved later onset additionally requires initial 100-ms mean velocity less than half threshold and onset at least 100 ms after the guard. Marginal cases are marked ambiguous. Once a first autonomous-motion event is detected, later pulses are not treated as independent first onsets. No hypothesis test is run on overlapping thresholds/guards as if independent.

This is a conservative classification of observable motion, not molecular attachment, occupancy or proof of chemical memory. Validation cells were selected by hashed cell identity before outcomes and have not yet been downloaded/read. Discovery indicated sparse possible late OFF onsets, not compelling post-pulse memory. The test can falsify a strong long-lived, routinely delayed activation mechanism, but cannot exclude sub-100-ms priming or assign any memory to a molecular component. Glass/bearing pinning is retained as a rival.

Logical correction before any validation-trace parsing: require a detected persistent event (finite onset) before classifying it immediate. The first script could otherwise terminate a trace merely because its initial mean exceeded threshold briefly, even when no 150-ms persistent onset existed. Discovery outputs exposed this inconsistency. Initial frozen source and its hash are preserved; corrected source hash is saved separately. No validation outcomes have been read for this correction.
