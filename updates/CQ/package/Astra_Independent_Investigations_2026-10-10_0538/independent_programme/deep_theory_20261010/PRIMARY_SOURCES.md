# Primary literature checked

1. Jan Hladky, Daniel Kral, Sergey Norin, *Counting flags in triangle-free digraphs*, arXiv:0908.2791v4, 22 February 2016.
   - Record: https://arxiv.org/abs/0908.2791
   - Full text: https://arxiv.org/html/0908.2791v4
   - Checked: abstract, Theorems 1.1–1.2, introduction, outregularity discussion.
   - Supports the exact target and the published 0.3465 threshold. It does not establish any of the false stationary expansions attempted here.

2. Blair D. Sullivan, *A Summary of Results and Problems Related to the Caccetta–Haggkvist Conjecture*, AIM, 2006.
   - https://aimath.org/WWN/caccetta/caccetta.pdf
   - Relevant: Conjecture 6.15, recorded Eulerian average second-neighborhood conjecture; attribution to Seymour/Jackson. Independent auditor checked this primary text.
   - The existence of a related open conjecture prevents describing the initial stationary reduction alone as novel mathematical progress.

3. Andrzej Grzesik, *On the Caccetta–Haggkvist Conjecture with a Forbidden Transitive Tournament*, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 24(2), P2.19, 2017.
   - https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/v24i2p19
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.37236/5954
   - Abstract records recursive directed-four-cycle blow-up extremals in its restricted setting. This is relevant structural prior; nested C4 substitutions themselves are not claimed as an invention here.

Searches did not establish novelty of the exact obstruction formulas. No novelty claim is made. Secondary search results and AI-authored research logs were not used as mathematical authority.
