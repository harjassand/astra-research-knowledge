# Independent investigations: preserved evidence, 10 October 2026

No historic foundational breakthrough was established in these investigations. This archive preserves completed constructions, exact counterexamples, unsuccessful empirical tests, and their reproducibility records. It is not independent external certification.

## Results and boundaries
- Reset-word proxy construction disproves a specific MST potential; it does not advance or refute the Cerny conjecture. Union-closed C5 transport obstruction is attributed to its 2016 predecessor.
- Local repair/sorting algebra fails its intended acquisition test; the underlying projection theorem is established prior work.
- Transformer edit certificates and phase-pair sensing were tested and downgraded. Exact phase identities do not establish a new measurement architecture; 1984 and 2015 antecedents are recorded.
- Coded population inference and temporal-order probes have exact constructions but no demonstrated new native acquisition capability.
- Directed-triangle stationary arguments are falsified by explicit 64- and 160-vertex graphs. The original CH conjecture is neither proved nor refuted.
- SrTiO3 analysis yields conditional angular invariants, not a new microscopic mechanism or a statistical rejection based on unknown experimental errors.
- Motor data:44 traces from43 cells, with a protected discovery/validation split. Delayed priming was not identified; it was not ruled out.

## Reproduction and source exclusions
Run scripts from the extracted archive root using their documented dependencies. Original research folders and files retain their paths and bytes. ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.json hashes every included payload.

Large motor raw traces, installed third-party packages, published article PDFs/HTML and copied publication figures are deliberately not included. Source versions, numerical observations, selective-download scripts, archive entry offsets, per-entry checksums and selection manifests are preserved. Reacquiring raw data requires network access to the cited public source. Whole-source-archive MD5 is explicitly unverified where only selected entries were downloaded; each selected entry has its own checks. Existing per-investigation manifests can reference excluded source files; the archive-level manifest defines exactly what this bundle contains.

Do not repurpose the motor validation cells for new exploratory claims. New candidate investigations are outside this completed-results archive. No source claims or internal numerical agreement should be treated as independent verification of a breakthrough.
