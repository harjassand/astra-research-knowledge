# Failed constructions and exact boundaries

## Extending the pair certificate by simply inserting k survival

The supplied all-color endpoint TV theorem is based on one transposition gradient and the identity `(I-T_e)^2=2(I-T_e)`, followed by two-ended pair-discrepancy smoothing. It does not have a k-particle gradient in its stated proof. Replacing its pair survival by a k-particle collision survival would be an unsupported strengthening. A new higher-difference factorization/coupling would be needed; none is constructed here.

The exact original-interchange operation obtained here is the binary order parity of selected labels, with optional signed terminal weights. Its output quantifier is strictly narrower than full endpoint TV. It supplies a reviewable observable perturbation bound, not a stronger all-color sampler theorem.

## Treating collision endpoints as exclusion or labelled interchange endpoints

Two occupied sites on a two-site path give an exact counterexample. Symmetric exclusion at full occupation is stationary with endpoint probability 1. The independent-walker no-collision probability is `exp(-2rt)`. The exterior determinant gives the latter, or the signed interchange permutation-parity expectation; it is not the full occupancy transition. Interchange selected labels can exchange. On a cycle the sorted surviving endpoint also omits cyclic label rotation and winding.

## Using ordinary cycle heat kernels for all k

On an even-k cycle, an allowed wrap move reorders k-1 wedge factors and changes the off-diagonal sign. Ordinary heat minors therefore fail the physical killed generator identity. The antiperiodic wrap and positive 2n-cover repair exactly this sign. Odd k uses the ordinary kernel. No uniform cycle one-particle kernel represents all k collision sectors at once.

## Repairing a branch with fixed edge or state phases

For the embedded three-leaf star loop, the exterior sign holonomy is -1 while all reversible physical edge phases and configuration-state phases cancel. The obstruction embeds for `2<=k<=n-2` with fixed spectators. It is not a lower bound for arbitrary algorithms or covers.

An overstrong version claiming obstruction for every k was rejected: k=n-1 has the Hodge/signless one-particle representation on arbitrary graphs. Its hole chain already has n states, so there is no hidden asymptotic speedup in that exception.

## Making rounded determinants into probabilities

Entrywise down-rounding of a heat matrix does not generally preserve total positivity. The receiver keeps a signed determinant measure and proves its total l1 error before clipping scalar intervals, count coefficients or binary-tree branch masses. The arbitrary-graph parity diagnostic actually has a signed bin coefficient near -0.211; treating that as an unsigned probability would be demonstrably false.

The tree proof additionally requires all mass queries to be exact marginals of the same approximate row matrix. Independently rounding every queried Pfaffian with unrelated error bars destroys that premise and requires a new error ledger.

## Obtaining a rare conditional guarantee from absolute survival error alone

The receiver needs a positive certified survival lower bound s0. Its conditional count error is delta/s0 and its complete endpoint error is at most D delta/s0. If the survival interval reaches zero, no relative/conditional conclusion follows. A returned sample or a numerical positive survival estimate is not a substitute for s0.

The precision requirement grows logarithmically with 1/s0 in the integer construction, but there is no input-uniform lower bound on s0. No claim of uniformly easy rare conditioning is made.

## Removing the tree-depth factor without new structure

If every approximate endpoint minor were nonnegative, exact tree sampling would simply normalize that approximate positive measure, and a delta/s0 bound would suffice. Our row-only finite-bit representation does not certify all such minors at scale. The proved signed-repair receiver therefore retains D. A polynomial-cost positivity-preserving heat approximation could potentially remove it, but is not developed here.

## Treating a mathematical workload as a useful physical application

The executed paths, cycles and star are explicitly supplied synthetic integer models. They establish finite receiving behavior and algebraic diagnostics. No scientific owner supplied an important collision/label-parity target, no experimental response was acquired, and no best validated scientific solver comparison was run. Useful physical applicability and superiority remain open.

## Claiming novelty from compression

Karlin–McGregor, de Bruijn, Stembridge fixed/free endpoints, minor summation and hook/exterior representations are direct strong incumbents. The package does not invent them. The remaining candidate contribution is the explicit finite-bit receiving and signed-prefix sampling contract; its historical priority is UNKNOWN, and an equally informed incumbent can adopt it at the same asymptotic cost.
