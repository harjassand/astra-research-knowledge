## 2. Why ordinary locality cannot be the compression argument

The actual three-particle gap graph has vertices (a,b)>=0 and unoriented edges in directions (1,0), (0,1), and (1,-1), with reflecting boundaries. Its positive Laplacian is the sum of vertex differences along available edges.

The polynomial

\[
h(a,b)=(a-b)(2a+b+1)(a+2b+1)
\]

is harmonic at every vertex, including the boundary. For

\[
b=\delta_{(0,1)}-\delta_{(1,0)},\qquad \langle b,h\rangle=-12.
\]

If the finite-energy pressure gradient solving Lp=b decayed exponentially, connectedness of exterior annuli would make p approach a common constant exponentially. After subtracting the constant, summation by parts against the polynomial h would give -12=<Lp,h>=<p,Lh>=0. Thus exponential locality of this projection is false.

This source is not an artificial unrelated example. With U_z denoting the coefficient in H_diag=U_z sum ZZ, exact identities are

\[
A_0J=0,\quad A_V^2J=-8U_z^2(J-F_{\rm mid}),\quad
F_{\rm mid}=\sum_iZ_{i-1}j_iZ_{i+2},
\]

\[
PAF_{\rm mid}=t^2(V_{1,2}-V_{2,1}).
\]

Here V_(a,b)=sum_x Z_x Z_(x+a) Z_(x+a+b), so the shifted gaps are exactly the displayed dipole. For the earlier U=4 centered-occupation convention, U_z=1. `structural_tests.py` reconstructs the harmonic identities and physical commutators exactly. This obstruction was located in Astra N754's actual source and independently checked here; it does not rule out low-rank approximation or imply that D itself has the same tail.

Three routes were therefore separated:

1. Exponential physical-support locality of the balance projection is unavailable.
2. A short formula for a separable inverse does not guarantee low output rank: even (Lambda X+X Lambda)=11^T gives the full-rank Cauchy matrix X_ij=1/(lambda_i+lambda_j). This exact-rank example is not an approximate-rank lower bound for the quantum class.
3. Conservation can instead be built into a nonlocal functional representation, with its approximation error computed over the entire infinite graph. This route is developed below.

## 3. One-dimensional primitives solve the prescribed-divergence problem

Use outgoing-minus-incoming divergence on the Cartesian orthant:

\[
\partial_\square F(g)=\sum_{i=1}^m[F_i(g)-F_i(g-e_i)],
\]

where an incoming value at coordinate -1 is zero.

### 3.1 Separated version

Suppose an absolutely summable source is explicitly represented as

\[
b(g)=\sum_{a=1}^{R}c_a\prod_{i=1}^m u_{ai}(g_i),\qquad \sum_gb(g)=0.
\]

Let m_ai=sum_s u_ai(s), and choose normalized reference sequences rho_i with sum rho_i=1. Define

\[
p_{ai}(s)=\sum_{\ell=0}^s[u_{ai}(\ell)-m_{ai}\rho_i(\ell)],
\]

\[
F_i(g)=\sum_a c_a
\Big(\prod_{j<i}u_{aj}(g_j)\Big)p_{ai}(g_i)
\Big(\prod_{j>i}m_{aj}\rho_j(g_j)\Big).
\tag{1}
\]

Then **partial_square F=b**. To prove it, take the backward difference in coordinate i, substitute the definition of p_ai, and telescope the products. For each a the result is product u_ai minus product m_ai rho_i. Summing cancels the latter because total mass is zero.

This is a prescribed-divergence construction, not the minimum-energy solution. Its energy must be computed or bounded separately. Sending a large-scale source directly to a point reference can be very expensive in energy despite its short representation.

### 3.2 Matrix-product/tensor-train version

Let

\[
b(g)=B_1(g_1)B_2(g_2)\cdots B_m(g_m),
\]

where B_i is r_(i-1)-by-r_i and r_0=r_m=1. Absolute summability of every matrix entry is assumed for the telescoping identity. For an energy-space correction, also require the primitives H_i to be square summable; this is automatic for the geometric cores below, but does not follow from absolute summability alone. Put

\[
M_i=\sum_{s\ge0}B_i(s),\quad D_i(s)=\rho_i(s)M_i,
\]

\[
H_i(s)=\sum_{\ell=0}^s[B_i(\ell)-\rho_i(\ell)M_i].
\]

Set

\[
F_i=B_1\cdots B_{i-1}H_iD_{i+1}\cdots D_m.
\tag{2}
\]

The products are ordered matrix products. No matrices are commuted. Their telescoping identity proves

\[
\partial_\square F=b-(M_1\cdots M_m)\prod_i\rho_i.
\tag{3}
\]

Thus a mean-zero source is repaired exactly. **Each F_i has the same TT bond dimensions as b.** It is not necessary to expand b into all separated rank-one components.

For rational geometric cores

\[
B_i(s)=\sum_{p=1}^{s_i}A_{ip}q_{ip}^s,\quad |q_{ip}|<1,
\quad \rho_i(s)=(1-r_i)r_i^s,
\]

all matrices are explicit:

\[
M_i=\sum_p\frac{A_{ip}}{1-q_{ip}},\qquad
H_i(s)=M_i r_i^{s+1}-\sum_p\frac{A_{ip}q_{ip}^{s+1}}{1-q_{ip}}.
\tag{4}
\]

Each repaired core has at most one additional geometric pole. The construction has no dependence on a spatial cutoff. Poles, matrix dimensions, rational bit lengths and intermediate numerator/denominator sizes are real inputs and are charged.

### 3.3 Route through the actual exclusion graph

Replace a Cartesian edge g->g+e_i by

\[
g\to g+e_1\to g+e_2\to\cdots\to g+e_i.
\tag{5}
\]

Every step is legal: the leftmost particle first moves left, and the introduced vacancy is transferred through the intervening gaps. Path divergence telescopes. Path length and congestion are at most m, giving

\[
\|\mathcal RF\|_{\rm edge}\le m\|F\|_{\rm grid}.
\tag{6}
\]

The payload for physical move j is the suffix sum F_j+...+F_m in the base coordinate g; its actual source vertex is g+e_(j-1), with e_0=0. This labeling retains parallel physical edges and avoids hiding a coordinate shift.

**The suffix sum does not require rank-m direct addition.** Use the upper-triangular block core

\[
\mathbb B_i(s)=
\begin{pmatrix}B_i(s)&H_i(s)\\0&D_i(s)\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{7}
\]

The upper-right block of its ordered product is the sum of terms with exactly one H. To select F_j+...+F_m, use the original B cores before j, then the block cores from j onward with the top input and bottom output boundaries. Therefore:

> **Rank-preserving exclusion completion.** Each routed edge-family payload has TT bond dimensions at most 2r_i. The graph defect is canceled exactly. Neither a multidimensional Poisson solve nor a geometric grid is constructed.

For normalized quantum balance G=c partial, divide the routed flow by c. The factor is not free and affects energy. In rational unnormalized Majorana coordinates, an explicit quantum lift avoids square roots: a leftward move of particle j is represented by two words with adjacent a,a or b,b singles and all other sites paired, with coefficient -1/(2t). Inserting one zero gap into the source payload gives the full lift. The prototype checks PA_0(lift)=b exactly.

## 4. Multiscale completion controls energy as well as representation

A short feasible flux is not sufficient if its norm is huge. The reference distribution in (1)–(4) is therefore an algorithmic choice.

Define normalized one-dimensional profiles

\[
\rho_j(s)=2^{-j}(1-2^{-j})^s,\quad
\mu_j(g)=\prod_{i=1}^m\rho_j(g_i),\quad \mu_0=\delta_0.
\]

For b_J=mu_J-mu_0, split

\[
b_J=\sum_{j=1}^J(\mu_j-\mu_{j-1}).
\]

Repair each difference using rho_(j-1) as reference, route it, and add. At L=2^j,

\[
\|\rho_j\|_2^2=1/(2L-1)\le1/L,
\quad \|\rho_{j-1}\|_2^2\le2/L.
\]

The primitive between these normalized profiles is

\[
p_j(s)=q_{j-1}^{s+1}-q_j^{s+1},\quad q_j=1-2^{-j},
\]

and ||p_j||_2^2<=L/2. The i-th Cartesian flux has squared norm at most 2^(m-i-1)L^(2-m). Summing over i gives at most 2^(m-1)L^(2-m). Routing (6) consequently gives

\[
\|w^{(j)}\|\le m2^{(m-1)/2}2^{j(1-m/2)}.
\]

By the triangle inequality,

\[
\boxed{\|w_J\|\le m2^{(m-1)/2}
\sum_{j=1}^J2^{j(1-m/2)}.}
\tag{8}
\]

For m>=3,

\[
\boxed{\|w_J\|^2\le
\frac{m^2 2^{m-1}}{(2^{m/2-1}-1)^2}<24m^2.}
\tag{9}
\]

For m=4, the bound is 128. For m=2 the displayed estimate is 8J^2; no dimension-independent energy bound is asserted there. In m=1 the required large-scale energy genuinely grows.

There are O(m^2J) separated routed terms, or comparable TT data with explicit level summation. The geometric scales are written using O(J)-bit dyadic poles. A conservative exact-arithmetic algorithm is polynomial in m,J and all coefficient sizes, not in (2^J)^m. This is a theorem for this explicit source class and its linear combinations, not an assertion that all actual response defects admit such a decomposition cheaply.

`multiscale_repair.py` constructs the m=4,J=40 case, representing scale 2^40 with 478 routed separated atoms. It verifies the exact source equation. The energy bound 128 is analytic; the full 40-level exact norm was not computed. Smaller cases have exact evaluated norms. In contrast, the direct point-reference repair of the same large-scale source has enormous exactly calculated energy (see separable_repair_checks.json). The multiscale step changes that energy bill without changing the physical graph.

## 5. Infinite geometry is contracted, not truncated

For a shifted geometric sequence

\[
u_{q,a}(g)=1_{g\ge a}q^{g-a},\quad |q|<1,
\]

q=0 denotes a point mass at a. Its exact overlap is

\[
\boxed{\langle u_{q,a},u_{r,b}\rangle=
\frac{q^{c-a}r^{c-b}}{1-qr},\quad c=\max(a,b).}
\tag{10}
\]

For separated atoms, multiply one-dimensional overlaps. For matrix-geometric TT cores, form the finite transfer matrices

\[
E_i=\sum_{p,q}\frac{\overline{A_{ip}}\otimes A'_{iq}}{1-\overline{q_{ip}}q'_{iq}}.
\tag{11}
\]

The ordered product E_1...E_m is the full infinite inner product. Real rational data give rational answers. These are infinite sums, not finite samples plus a guessed tail.

For at most s poles and bond dimension R, a safe dense arithmetic count for all routed component norms is O(m^2s^2R^6), with corresponding polynomial storage. This is intentionally conservative. Numerator/denominator growth, close-to-one poles and finite-precision errors are separately paid. The implementation uses exact fractions for final certificates. It does not declare ill-conditioned numerical discovery trustworthy.

The prototype explicitly expands finite prefix windows arising from delayed point masses; their lengths count as input complexity. It does not claim logarithmic cost in an arbitrarily large delay when exact q^a fractions themselves have O(a) bits. Long geometric decay lengths, in contrast, are represented by the rational pole and do not require visiting that many coordinates.

### 5.1 Exact quantum commutators in the same algebra

Within a branch, the action only shifts coordinates, inserts fixed short gaps, or merges a short gap with a free gap. It never needs to enumerate a long gap. \[
\boxed{2\langle b,\phi\rangle-\|\operatorname{grad}\phi\|^2

\[
\boxed{\Gamma=\|w-\operatorname{grad}\phi\|^2,\qquad

## 8. A fully charged route from projections to the interacting inverse

At degree <=N let W_N be the local unprojected skew operator and Pi the exact balance projection. No matrix for Pi is supplied. The compressed Hodge solver approximates its action with an explicit norm certificate (14).

Set

\[
h=(1+M_N^2)^{-1},\qquad \theta=\sqrt{1-h}.
\]

For balanced x_j, compute an exactly balanced q_j approximating Pi(z-W_N x_j) to error at most eta, then update

\[
x_{j+1}=(1-h)x_j+hq_j.
\tag{15}
\]

The ideal map contracts by theta, since for K=Pi W_N Pi,

\[
\|(1-h)e-hKe\|^2=(1-h)^2\|e\|^2+h^2\|Ke\|^2
\le(1-h)\|e\|^2.
\]

Consequently, starting at zero,

\[
\boxed{\|x_j-x_N\|\le\theta^j\|z\|+2\eta.}
\tag{16}
\]

Indeed the geometric error sum is h eta/(1-theta)=(1+theta)eta<=2eta. This controls O((1+M_N^2) log(1/epsilon)) outer iterations without a physical relaxation gap or spatial radius. ### Representation reduction is itself charged

A represented tensor can be rounded without enumerating coordinates: left/right environment Gram matrices use (11), so a bond reduction is a finite weighted singular-value problem. Candidate rational gauge changes can be checked by exact contraction of the full difference. This does not require a minimum singular-value promise. Standard tensor rounding is prior art; the infinite geometric physical index is reduced by the explicit Gram contraction here.

Rounding may break balance. Repair its actual defect with Section 3 and evaluate the **combined** change in the Hilbert norm. If that exceeds the allocated eta, retain more rank or reject the step. A small raw tensor-rounding error is not enough when an ill-chosen conservation repair has high energy. Alternatively, store the coefficient fields of explicit cycles, whose divergence remains zero under coefficient rounding.

This is a rigorous solver architecture and error budget. Its outer iteration estimate is not a bound on the work required to close every inner Hodge gap. The latter depends on the discovered tensor ranks and poles and remains the main unresolved theorem.

## 10. Resource ledger and precise remaining theorem

**Proved repair cost:** polynomial in m, explicitly represented TT ranks, numbers of geometric poles and all rational bit lengths; each routed component has rank <=2 times the source rank. This is unconditional for the stated explicit source format and compatible total mass. Completion energy is either computed or bounded; small energy is not automatic.

**Fixed-dictionary solution cost:** finite Gram assembly, gauge-fixed rational quadratic solve, and full residual verification are polynomial in the explicit dictionary and coefficient dimensions. The dictionary is constructed, not supplied as a correct high-precision witness. Its adequacy is decided by its certificate.

**Precision:** exact rational final data are used in executed certificates. Poles close to one, long delayed point masses, cancellations and intermediate denominator lengths are not unit-cost operations. Inputs outside the supported prototype are rejected or require a separately costed implementation.

**Physical inputs:** full local Hamiltonian coefficients and the original pure-dephasing model are still supplied or separately calibrated. No Gibbs specimens, quantum hardware, simulated artificial noise, infinite state, global spectral gap or uncharged operator inverse is used. Unmodelled memory, leakage, long-range terms, finite temperature and real-device transfer remain outside the result.

**Open theorem, not a hidden assumption:** Along a constructive approximation of the actual degree-N response, produce coefficient fields and Hodge potentials/flows with certified residual tolerances, tensor ranks and pole/state counts bounded by C_physical^N epsilon^-c, and total discovery, rounding, repair, solve, bit and verification costs of the same form.

## 12. Prior research and provenance

Tensor trains, tensor-network contractions, separated exponential sums, prescribed-divergence constructions, Hodge projection, Galerkin methods and inexact Richardson iterations are established tools. Historical novelty of this particular rank-preserving exclusion completion and its use in this quantum response problem remains unestablished.

Bachmayr and Kazeev [R1] explicitly analyze representation ill-conditioning distinct from ordinary matrix conditioning, construct low-rank multilevel preconditioners and give convergence/complexity results for elliptic problems. Thus a short tensor expression must not be treated as a stable efficient inverse. Bachmayr and Dahmen [R2] give adaptive convergent tensor solvers with complexity results under specified solution rank/sparsity structure; detecting such structure is not a new general idea here. Their results do not by themselves prove the rank theorem for the present constrained quantum operator.

FDAOE [R3] already uses fermionic weight and preserves long quadratic strings; this representation choice is credited. It applies artificial damping/extrapolation in its own physical setting. Operator backflow [R4] is a strong transport-specific comparator. No head-to-head superiority over those methods is established by the present Hodge benchmark.
