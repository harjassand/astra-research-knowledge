## 3. New theorem: a local protected sensor in an arbitrary finite-density device

This extends the source's four-candidate sensor search to a precisely defined
many-site class. It does not extend the source's payoff calculation.

Let N hard-core sites have an explicitly listed diagonal nuisance Hamiltonian

    V_xi = sum_l xi_l Q_l,
    Q_l = sum_S c_lS product_(i in S) n_i,

where every monomial has at least two distinct sites. Coefficients xi_l can be
arbitrary unknown values; neither weak interactions nor a norm bound is needed.
Known on-site energies on the sensor/readout block are matched or compensated.
The only phase-sensitive unknown term is the unit-magnitude link p-q. Admit a
constant-size active block B containing p,q and enough empty channels/known
connections to implement the required phase-independent readout when that
unknown link is off. Other interactions may produce an arbitrary finite-density
state outside the sensor.

Construct a guard set Z outside B by taking the union of S minus B over every
listed monomial with |S intersection B|=1. Prepare Z empty and B with exactly one
particle; freeze hopping across the boundary of B and all hopping into Z, and
disable reservoirs acting on B or Z. During interrogation, switch off every
other hopping edge inside B so p-q is isolated. During readout, switch p-q off
and enable only the admitted calibrated known controls inside B. Keep the actual
local pure dephasing. Any additional background Hamiltonian is supported outside
B union Z and is phase independent; it may evolve the unreset background.

**Proposition A.** On this admitted subspace, V_xi acts as identity on the active
one-excitation sector tensor an operator on the remaining background. The
sensor and known readout are therefore independent of every xi_l and of the
remaining background state. The p-q probe has exactly the source's two-level
Bloch law with dephasing gamma=(d_p+d_q)/2; uniform d gives gamma=d.

**Proof.** A monomial with at least two sites in B vanishes because B contains
one particle. A monomial with exactly one active site has an empty guard factor
by construction, and also vanishes. A monomial with no active site acts only on
the background/guards and is identity on the sensor. Frozen hopping and disabled
reservoirs preserve the vacuum guards and active particle number. Local Z noise
does not change those occupations. Thus the restricted Hamiltonian splits into
sensor and background parts; the dissipator splits likewise. A pure local
preparation of the sensor/guards factorizes them from any remaining state. On
the pair coherence, the two local dephasing contributions are d_p/2 and d_q/2.
All other local Z operators act trivially on this encoded coherence. QED.

This is a constructive sufficient compiler: scan the explicit monomial list and
form a union. Its work is linear in that list's incidence length, with ordinary
index/bit costs; it does not enumerate 2^N states or search for an optimal guard.
If each active site belongs to at most r monomials of degree at most k, then
|Z|<=|B|r(k-1). Local reset, freezing and readout can consequently have size
independent of N on bounded-degree models. They still require real calibrated
operations and finite error budgets. On long-range/dense templates the guard
can grow with N. Imperfect guards restore unknown interaction-dependent
detuning/leakage and must be bounded, not ignored. Terms linear in n_i, unknown
non-density interactions, unfreezable transport, extra phase-sensitive links or
unsupported noise invalidate the stated structural theorem.

For a k_B-site active readout block with uniform d, a conservative dephasing
TV budget is k_B*d*t_readout/4 from the jump unravelling. Spectator/guard Z jumps
do not decohere the active state. Known control graph geometry, bandwidth,
loading and switching remain explicit requirements; no constant resource bound
is asserted for an arbitrary graph without the admitted block.

The Hamiltonian-difference half-range remains sqrt(3) for any N because all
nuisances cancel between trine labels and the unknown link is still one link.
Thus the same all-adaptive identification obstruction remains valid on this
extended access class. **A control separation only follows after independently
acquiring the required good-action/loss geometry of that larger device.** No
such larger stationary-output certificate is supplied here. The cheap protected
probe does not make the many-body payoff cheap.

## 4. New theorem: arbitrary detuning pulses enter the exclusion loss quadratically

An objective-specific error can be much smaller than the full state error.
The following is independently derived; it does not import N771's chemical
equations into a quantum system.

During acquisition only, let the isolated-link Hamiltonian contain an unknown
differential detuning delta(t) Z/2 in addition to the fixed trine phase and known
unit hopping. Let delta be measurable with finite L1 area

    b = integral_0^T |delta(t)| dt.

Retain fixed nonnegative physical dephasing gamma, initial north-pole state and
the original phase-independent anti-trine readout. No detuning estimation or
control compensation is assumed.

**Proposition B.** For each true trine label, the bad-action probability satisfies

    |p_bad[delta]-p_bad[0]| <= b^2/6.

In particular a constant detuning |Delta|<=Delta_max costs at most
Delta_max^2*T^2/6. The result holds without a constant-sign pulse promise.

**Proof.** Rotate the true phase to zero. The Bloch generator is

    M_delta(t) = [[-gamma, -delta(t), 0],
                  [delta(t), -gamma, 2],
                  [0, -2, 0]],
    r(0)=(0,0,1),  p_bad=(1-r_y(T))/3.

The baseline dynamics preserve the yz plane and the x axis separately.
The perturbation K(A)=-i[Z/2,A] sends a baseline yz Bloch vector into x. Therefore
every first-order Dyson contribution has zero y readout. Let Phi_delta and
Phi_0 be the perturbed and baseline CPTP propagators. Subtract their Duhamel
identities once more to leave the exact ordered second-order remainder. Its
integrand contains Phi_delta(T,s) K Phi_0(s,t) K rho_0(t), multiplied by
delta(s)delta(t), for 0<t<s<T. All propagators contract trace norm on Hermitian
operators; K preserves Hermiticity/zero trace and has induced trace norm <=1,
since ||[Z/2,A]||_1<=||A||_1. The centered bad-action effect is -Y/3, with norm
1/3, so the absolute observed remainder is at most

    (1/3) integral_(0<t<s<T) |delta(s)delta(t)| dt ds = b^2/6.

The diagonal of the integration square has measure zero. QED.

For constant detuning an independent equivalent argument is that replacing
Delta by -Delta and r_x by -r_x preserves r_y,r_z, so p_bad is even in Delta;
the CPTP second-derivative norm bound gives the same result. This does not say
that the full state changes only quadratically: its x component is generally
linear in Delta.

At T=pi/4 and Delta_max=1/32 the extra bad-action allowance is

    pi^2/98304 < 0.000100398807792.

If separately acquired preparation/readout/timing law errors have TV bound eta_h,
the new valid decision bound is p_bad[0]+b^2/6+eta_h. Setting
eta_h<=1/2000-b^2/6 preserves the source's numeric decision risk while permitting
a much larger full-state perturbation in this particular direction. Hardware
errors are measured against the detuned experiment, not silently included twice.
The same phase-dependent action gap multiplies the probability term only when
the old stationary-output model remains valid.

If detuning persists during readout, bound it there separately (for constant
Delta, a safe additional TV cost is |Delta|*t_readout/2). If it persists during
device operation, the old physical loss certificate is inapplicable until
recertified. Unknown phase drift, hopping-amplitude error, population loss,
arbitrary leakage, correlated preparation error or a different measurement need
not enjoy this quadratic cancellation. This theorem buys one specified error
budget; it supplies neither a new general noise-correcting code nor a hardware
demonstration.

## 5. New obstruction: the generic occupation-pair compiler already contains Exact Cover

Define a restricted computational problem. Input is a list of positive diagonal
one- and two-site density templates with binary rational coefficients, a specified
hopping pair p-q, and the admitted code family

    C_x = span{|p=1,q=0,x>, |p=0,q=1,x>}, x in {0,1}^n.

Question: is there an x for which every nuisance template restricts to a scalar
on C_x? Other hopping is off. This is existence of an invariant computational
occupation-pair sensor, not of every conceivable coherent/QEC/ancilla code.

**Proposition C.** This decision problem is NP-complete, already for templates
whose coefficients are 0 or 1 and whose individual terms have locality at most 2.

**Proof.** Let A be an Exact Cover incidence matrix with rows indexing universe
elements and columns indexing sets. Exact Cover asks for x in {0,1}^n satisfying
Ax=1. Introduce sites p,q and n spectator sites. For row l use

    Q_l = n_p sum_i A_li n_i + n_q.

On the first basis state its eigenvalue is (Ax)_l; on the second it is 1.
The unknown hopping maps these two states into each other and leaves x unchanged.
Thus the pair is nuisance-null for every coefficient exactly when Ax=1.
The reduction has polynomial size and only nonnegative 0/1 local terms. A bit
string x is a polynomial-size witness; evaluating all labels is polynomial in
the explicit incidence length, proving membership in NP. QED.

Each unknown coefficient multiplies its entire displayed Q_l: its grouped local
terms have a known shared coefficient. This proof does not assert hardness when
every individual monomial has an independently unknown coefficient instead.

There is a pure two-body variant with Q_l=n_p sum_i A_li n_i+n_q n_r, **only if
the admission class requires the reference spectator r to be occupied in both
basis states**. Then the same proof holds. If r may instead be empty, x=0 is a
trivial protected pair, so omitting the occupancy condition would make this
reduction false. Neither variant establishes hardness of the original pure
degree>=2 class allowing an empty background: Proposition A is a cheap sufficient
compiler for that class. Neither variant proves hardness for the most general
coherent sensor code, for approximate/noisy codes, for bounded-degree geometric
graphs, or for every algorithm allowed to abandon the stated code family.

The obstruction is useful because it identifies an explicit difference in the
input/control/admission contract that turns a four-pair enumeration into a hard
search. A scalar nullspace written after full Hilbert expansion does not evade
that compressed-input cost. It does not prove P differs from NP; the usual
conditional interpretation of NP-completeness applies.

## 6. Optional sharper all-identifier bound

If q equiprobable accessible states have all pairwise trace distances <=D, then
every q-label measurement has average success <=[1+(q-1)D]/q. Indeed fix a
reference j, sum Tr(rho_j E_i)=1, and bound each i!=j difference by D.
Averaging is unnecessary because the bound holds for each reference. Hence its
worst-label error is at least (q-1)(1-D)/q. For q=3 and the source's hard-cap
distance D=sin(sqrt(3)pi/4), this gives a stronger error bound

    (2/3)[1-sin(sqrt(3)pi/4)] > 0.01470821568954.

This elementary multiway bound is an internally reconstructed improvement of
the *bound*, not a claim of exact optimal identification error or a new general
discrimination theorem. The producer's weaker 0.011031 bound remains valid.

## 8. Primary comparators and useful next research

[Bandyopadhyay et al., Conclusive Exclusion of Quantum States](https://arxiv.org/html/1306.4683v3),
Sections II, III and VII.2, already provides state exclusion, arbitrary subsets,
SDPs/duals and worst-case variants. The anti-trine and generic loss-to-measurement
optimization cannot be promoted as new. The source's candidate distinction is
its paid physical loss construction, realistic nuisance/error interface and
hard-exposure comparison. Priority of that combination remains UNKNOWN.

[Acin, Statistical distinguishability between unitary operations](https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0102064v2)
is a primary comparator for unitary discrimination. The adaptive/noisy angular
argument above is reconstructed directly rather than inferred from an abstract.
[Huang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03030) supplies a strong controlled
many-body learning comparator; it prevents any general claim that model learning
must use full tomography or exponential time.

[Zhou et al., QEC-enhanced metrology](https://arxiv.org/html/1706.02445v2),
the code conditions in Section 0.5 and the SDP in Section 0.14, already develops
codes protecting against noise while retaining a signal, under fast controls
and suitable ancillas. The local density guard construction here retains physical
dephasing and concerns compressed local input plus a finite exclusion loss; it
does not establish a new QEC principle or outperform their metrological theorem.

[Mortimer et al., certified steady-state observables](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.13646v2)
already supplies scalable moment relaxations and stationary-observable bounds.
It is the strongest concrete next payoff-certificate comparator. A worthwhile
new experiment would combine a guarded probe with certified action differences
on an extended interacting device, comparing total loss acquisition/construction
cost with these moment methods and strong decision-aware sensing. Current
certificates must prove a nonvanishing action margin; a small measurement SDP
does not produce that margin by itself.

For the complexity premise, the classical primary reference is
[Karp, Reducibility among Combinatorial Problems (1972)](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4684-2001-2_9).
Primary full-text retrieval attempts were unavailable in this run. Exact Cover's
standard NP-completeness is the named imported premise; the sensor reduction
itself is fully written above and exhaustively checked on small incidence inputs.
No priority claim is made for this local-code hardness formulation.

The deepest useful synthesis is therefore an explicit boundary: local nuisance
rejection can be constructively cheap even in an extended finite-density device;
generic code search can already contain Exact Cover; and neither fact supplies
the large-system physical action-loss table. N771's objective-specific defect
criterion and the detuning theorem share a design strategy, but their distinct
algebras and acquired inputs must not be interchanged. Existing architecture can
store these proofs, conditional interfaces and failures without a new agent role
or generic orchestration layer.
