# Slide-electrification access/identification boundary
Public deposit 10.6084/m9.figshare.32125120 downloaded 2026-10-10; zip MD5 matches published value 8f8074702951a02f2d64f89b982dc662. Main numeric sheets supply trial-level integrated charges (five per temperature), means and standard deviations. Only one current waveform is in the Excel inventory, Fig. 1(b), labeled 20 C OTS_Water; Origin files are figure projects, not an archive of all condition-level raw waveforms. No condition-wise raw waveforms or blank full-cycle controls are listed.

The predeclared induction/transport-versus-deposition validation test consequently cannot be performed. A single representative waveform is not a condition-level dynamical dataset. Even full induction accounting would measure charge redistribution, not identify electrons versus ions. No mechanism was discovered; no claims of electron-transfer identification are made. OTS values were inspected only as discovery data; the other numerical condition values and supplementary holdouts remain unexamined at this point.
