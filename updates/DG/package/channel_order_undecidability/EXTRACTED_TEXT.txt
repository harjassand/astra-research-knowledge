Abstract
Complete symmetric logarithmic derivative information order is co-recursively enumer-
able complete, already for pairs of measurement channels specified by finite strictly positive
POVMs with rational complex entries. Its failure is recursively enumerable complete. The
same classification holds for unrestricted complete Umegaki relative-entropy order, in which
every finite untouched reference and every pair of input states are allowed. There is no
total computable bound, from a rational POVM pair, on a reference dimension sufficient
to detect every failure of complete SLD order.
The proof reduces the finite-dimensional nonlocal-game promise supplied byMIP∗ = RE
to a uniformly gapped polynomial on the actual two-factor normalized Jordan domain. A
quantitative Pauli anchor and local completely positive smoothing establish soundness. An
ordered-word graph construction compiles the polynomial to inverse-Jordan kernels, which
are embedded into normalized strictly positive POVMs with a dimension-independent
global completion. All these reduction steps, and the equivalence between the two channel
orders, are proved below. The nonlocal-game hardness theorem is the external complexity
premise. A further normalization using its perfect consistent strategies gives a computable
positive entropy margin. Finite tensor amplification then proves that the bounded complete
entropy defect cannot be uniformly approximated even at the fixed additive error1/4.

Theorem 14.1 (No total additive approximation algorithm). There is no total algorithm
which, given rational complex strictly positive POVMsE,F and rationalε >0, returns a
rationalv satisfying
|v−V (E,F )|≤ ε.
The conclusion holds with the supremum in(64) restricted to faithful state pairs, for which the
value is finite and nonnegative.
Proof. Given a Turing machine, compute its hard game and the POVMs from the preceding
reduction. In the nonhalting case complete SLD and therefore complete entropy dominance
hold, soV = 0. In the halting case Lemma 12.1 gives a perfect maximally entangled strategy.
Section 12 supplies a faithful Fisher witness and game-computable rational constantsh,κ,δ G
satisfying Lemma 13.1. Apply that lemma withd0 =δG to compute a positive rationalεG
and a faithful entropy witness with defect at leastεG. All constants can be computed without
finding the strategy or its dimension. Thus halting impliesV≥ εG.
A purported approximation algorithm at tolerance εG/4 would return v ≤ εG/4 in the
nonhalting case andv≥ 3εG/4 in the halting case. Comparing withεG/2 would decide halting,
a contradiction.
The primitive gapεG depends effectively on the game. This is enough to amplify to a universal
numerical accuracy obstruction.

15 Scope and relation to existing frameworks
The reduction uses generic complex POVMs and opposite Pauli chirality. It does not transfer
automatically to real or transpose-compatible denominator tuples. Finite cutoffs or lift
characterizations for narrower classes are compatible with the theorem.
The admissible inverse kernels always act on a finite-dimensionalspatial two-factordomain. A
compression that mixes the factors can destroy that domain. Results for free spectrahedra or
unconstrained matrix evaluations, such as the framework in [4], therefore cannot be used to
infer a cutoff here without proving that the spatial domain is preserved.
Complete channel orders and contraction-coefficient frameworks are developed in [2]; estimation
and complete information comparisons are discussed in [3]. These are contextual references,
not substitutes for any of the reduction lemmas above. This note makes no claim of exhaustive
novelty verification. The exact theorem concerns qualitative preorder membership; the additive
theorem concerns the bounded entropy defectV. Neither identifies numerical contraction
coefficients, covers all restricted-marginal variants, or asserts a common Hermiticity-preserving
lift characterization for generic measurement pairs.
Every indispensable new step of the reduction has been included: the honest domain realization,
augmented-anchor estimates, local smoothing, question normalization, finite gapped penalty,
ordered inverse compiler, rational complex numerator implementation, faithful slice embedding,
and uniform global completion. The additive strengthening also includes the Schmidt-sector
argument, dimension-independent faithful normalization, explicit entropy-filter margin, and
finite tensor amplification. The imported nonlocal-game complexity theorem remains the
external premise. The note’s research status remains AI-generated and not peer-reviewed.
