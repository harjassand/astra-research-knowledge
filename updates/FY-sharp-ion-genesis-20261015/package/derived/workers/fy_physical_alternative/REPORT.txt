Independent physical operation screen: selective electrothermal metal recovery
2026-10-15

Decision: PARK_PREEXISTING_OPERATION. No new physical operation, matched advantage,
or qualifying breakthrough is established by this worker.

The strongest independently selected lead was selective conversion of mixed
metal compounds into collected volatile chlorides using brief electrical heating,
with a carbon-assisted stage when needed. Recovering individual useful material
fractions from complicated scrap is consequential. Public product-assay data made
this lead more obtainable than proposals supported only by intermediate signals.
However, the exact operation is already the subject of Deng et al., "Flash
separation of metals by electrothermal chlorination," Nature Chemical Engineering
1, 627-637 (2024), DOI 10.1038/s44286-024-00125-2. This is the strongest incumbent
for the nominated operation and the decisive novelty stop. Its primary preprint
was public on 26 July 2024. Switching pulse duration, voltage or carbon loading
does not by itself establish a new operation.

Contract investigated

Input: a chemically characterized, prepared solid feed, a chlorine supply, an
electrical heater and, for carbochlorination, a carbon reactant. Useful output:
an isolated metal-containing product of specified identity, purity and recovery,
together with accounted residue, gas and auxiliary inventories. A heater trace
or disappearance of the target from a residue is not that complete output.
Costs include preparation, all heat and electrical paths, gas production and
handling, carbon, collection, downstream product conversion, regeneration,
consumables, assay acquisition and accepted-output yield.

The source methods describe a fabricated LED-waste mimic, substrate lift-off
and drying. They also describe traps/NaOH absorption of unused chlorine. The
laboratory data therefore do not demonstrate a raw heterogeneous-scrap operation
with a restored chlorine loop. These are boundaries on this evidence, not a
claim that the published separation fails.

What the calculations establish

The observed settings expose a purity-recovery tradeoff. The lower-yield setting
has the highest reported mean purity. A generic mass-weighted pooling proof
shows that combining already collected fractions cannot exceed their largest
purity when they share a common complete denominator and no further separation
or reaction occurs. On the three published means, pooling cannot exceed
99.18872%; this does not certify a physical upper bound on future outcomes.
Serial reprocessing and new pulse histories are outside that proof.

A GaN-to-GaCl3 inventory identity requires 1.5 mol of Cl2 per mol of Ga in the
chloride product. Recovering unused gas does not restore chlorine exported in
that product. Product conversion and any required chlorine regeneration have
their own balances and costs. This is elementary stoichiometry, not a new result
or a measured energy estimate.

The source Ta scale-up cells use fractions: 0.951 purity and 0.882 yield mean
95.1% and 88.2% at 1 g. The reported source clock of a 1 g batch per 10 minutes
arithmetically gives 144 g/day of feed only under uninterrupted scheduling. It
is not a demonstrated 24-hour product rate. Preparation and collection remain
part of an accepted-output comparison.

The deposited modeled-energy sheet contains a separate header inconsistency:
in "Fig. 10d", B2:D2 say Lower limit/Middle/Higher limit, while each numerical
row has C > B > D and B = (C+D)/2 within rounding. We preserve those bytes and
do not repair the labels or extract an experimental energy advantage from them.
This documentation issue is not evidence against the observed metal separation.

Reopening criterion

No experiment can remove the historical priority of the already published
operation. This lead should reopen as invention only after a distinct useful
operation and a source-compatible causal response have been identified, with
the existing ETC/ETCC process allowed the same feed, controls and output access.
Simply claiming improved recovery from a new pulse setting is insufficient.

If the intended question is engineering validation of the existing operation,
DECISIVE_TEST.txt gives a bounded acquisition gate for real feed, product
identity, all-stream closure, restored auxiliaries and a matched comparator.
That test is not an outstanding premise of a claimed new Astra invention.
