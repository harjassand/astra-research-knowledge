Reopening and acquisition gates

Historical gate

The nominated operation is already published. A new laboratory result reproducing
it cannot establish Astra priority. A distinct output contract or causal action
must be specified before resuming invention work. Parameter adjustment, pulse
time sharing and adding an ordinary reagent-recovery block are not enough by
themselves. No distinct exceptional operation is nominated in this packet.

Bounded engineering extension, if separately pursued

The missing claim is an accepted-product operation on a characterized real feed
with complete inventory and cost closure. It can be tested on the same small
scale as the published system. Larger scale is not needed to acquire the first
missing response.

1. Fix the feed and output before testing.

Use one homogenized, sampled real manufacturing-scrap lot. Identify its prior
processing and measure the metal-bearing and nonmetal inventories. Distinguish
the prepared LED-waste mimic in the primary source from that real lot. Specify
whether the accepted product is a chloride, oxide or elemental metal, and define
purity over the complete product rather than an unspecified subset of detected
elements. Freeze minimum purity, recovery, chemical identity and assay precision
before running the comparison.

2. Measure the complete operation in one regime.

Assign equivalent aliquots to the published ETC route and any distinct proposed
route with both allowed the same feed information, apparatus control and product
collection. Requalify the loaded sample temperature and gas conditions; do not
transplant a different heater's voltage-temperature curve. Use independent
process repeats, separating them from replicate readings of one digest. Include
feed preparation, product removal, trap cleaning, drying and reactor reset.

3. Close the inventories.

Assay the accepted product, residue, gas/trap fractions, heater and reactor uptake,
and all sampling/removal losses. Measure chlorine in supplied gas, unused gas,
product, scrubbers and any regeneration output. Measure carbon consumption where
used. State unexplained residuals and uncertainty. Verify chemical identity and
stored or transient reactor inventory separately. Demonstrate repeated accepted
product collection beyond one changing heater or surface inventory.

4. Test auxiliary restoration directly.

If closed chlorine reuse is part of the claim, show recovered reagent at its
required specification and restore all auxiliary inventories before the next
cycle. Chlorine absorbed in a laboratory scrubber is not regenerated Cl2. Account
for chlorine exported in accepted product and any required subsequent conversion.
Keep regeneration response, work and consumables measured in the same cycle.

5. Compare complete accepted output.

The nearest comparator is the published ETC/ETCC operation, with its actual
preparation and collection. A second process comparator should be the best
available established recovery route for this exact feed and product. Include
all electrical/thermal work, chemical stocks, preparation, separation, regeneration,
handling, consumables, rejected output and lifetime. Model LCA/TEA values remain
model estimates until the corresponding complete process is measured.

Decisive interpretation

Failure to close target or chlorine inventories stops the closed-cycle capability
claim. Failure on real feed stops extrapolation from the prepared mimic. Failure
to beat the exact incumbent stops the claimed new advantage. Passing these tests
would establish only the specified engineering extension; it would not remove
the pre-existing operation or by itself establish a fundamentally new capability.
