7. Two precise limits on enlarging the local correction class
The report contains proofs of two additional structural propositions. Their historical originality is not asserted.
Closedness is necessary within the local quadratic-Hessian class
Suppose \(N(A,D^2\phi)\) is homogeneous quadratic in the compatible Hessian entries, has \(C^2\) coefficients in \(A\), and
\[
\int N(B+D\phi,D^2\phi)=0
\]
for every affine background and every smooth compact perturbation.
Then
\[
\boxed{
N(A,z)=\omega_A(B_1(z),B_2(z)),
\qquad d\omega=0.
}
\]
The proof first identifies a six-dimensional null-symbol space, exactly spanned by the six coordinate two-form pullbacks. The variable-coefficient Euler equation then forces all four components of \(d\omega\) to vanish; arbitrary third jets prevent cancellation.
Therefore, allowing a “nonclosed correction that is nevertheless null on gradients” does not enlarge this particular smooth local quadratic-Hessian class.
Smooth higher-degree dependence does not enlarge local constant-heat positivity
Let \(F\) be a homogeneous quartic. Suppose a closed two-form with \(C^2\) coefficients near \(A=0\) makes its constant-diffusion heat density pointwise nonnegative.
Then its homogeneous-quadratic Taylor part already supplies such a correction.
The argument is a scaling reduction. At \(A=0\), positivity and vanishing on rank-one Hessian jets force the constant correction to vanish. Testing \(tA\) and \(-tA\) eliminates the linear part. Dividing by \(t^2\) and taking the limit retains a closed quadratic correction.
Consequently, simply increasing polynomial degree or introducing smooth nonlinear \(A\)-dependence cannot expand that positivity class.
Neither proposition proves that every positive corrected density has a positive Gram representation. Non-SOS positivity, singular angular corrections, nonlinear diffusions, nonlocal operations, and higher-jet mechanisms remain outside these limitations.
