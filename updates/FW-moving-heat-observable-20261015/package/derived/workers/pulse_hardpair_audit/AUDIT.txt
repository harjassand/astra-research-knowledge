The exact impulse difference is

  g(t)=2A delta [exp(-delta t)-exp(-2 delta t)]>=0,

and its integral is d(t)=A(1-exp(-delta t))^2. Hence all signed scalar currents
with |I|<=Imax have full-path voltage separation at most Imax*d(H); a constant
pulse attains that bound. The midpoint-system response supplies a common
transcript under per-world voltage error epsilon_V whenever

  Imax*d(H)<=2 epsilon_V.

For finite slew, I(0)=0 and |I'|<=Imax/rho are essential. The exact optimum is
the maximal ramp followed by a plateau; replace d(H) by

  K(H,rho)=integral_{max(0,H-rho)}^H d(t)dt/rho.

On the known informed convex model segment, the exact deterministic minimax
absolute Q_T error is d(T)*min(1/2,eta/K), eta=epsilon_V/Imax. Its upper bound
requires the common baseline and family to be supplied; its lower bound also
applies to any larger model class containing the segment.
