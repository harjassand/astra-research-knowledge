SHARP-TO-SHARP LINEAR ORBIT ADDENDUM
15 October 2026. Independent exact structural result, no historical claim.

This addendum supplements STRUCTURAL_PROOF.txt without modifying that frozen
file. It uses the acoustic zero classification proved in its Section 4.
The ambient polynomials and all matrices are real, on all of R^(2 by 2).

Write
 J_r(A)=alpha_r (u+v-2j)^2+beta_r u^2+j^2+r u j,
 alpha_r=3r/[8(r+2)], beta_r=r(r-1)/6,
 r in (-infinity,-2) union (0,infinity).
Let A_r=[[0,b_r],[1,0]], b_r=(2r+1)/3.

CLAIM. If r,s are allowed, P,D are invertible real 2 by 2 matrices, and
c>0, then

 J_r(A)=c J_s(P A D) for every A

implies r=s. Consequently this standard QC-preserving operation cannot
transfer a certificate between the negative and positive sharp branches.

Proof. For a rank-one matrix x tensor n,

 J_r(x tensor n)=|x|^4 g_r(n),
 g_r(n)=alpha_r(n1^2+n2^2)^2+beta_r n1^4.

Both alpha_r and alpha_r+beta_r are positive: the latter equals
r(2r+1)^2/[24(r+2)]. Thus g_r(n)>0 whenever n is nonzero. The presumed
identity on rank-one matrices says

 |x|^4 g_r(n)=c |P x|^4 g_s(D^T n).

Fix any nonzero n. It follows that |P x|^4 is a positive multiple of
|x|^4 for all x. Hence P=rho O with rho>0 and O orthogonal. Absorb rho^4
into c. A left rotation of the source replaces O by
S=diag(1,epsilon), epsilon=det O in {+1,-1}. This step handles both
orientations; left-SO(2) invariance alone does not remove a reflection.

The target contact D^2J_r(I)[e12,e12]=0 now becomes

 D^2J_s(SD)[e1 tensor (second row of D), same]=0.

The second row of D is nonzero. The complete acoustic zero classification
from STRUCTURAL_PROOF.txt, Section 4, says precisely:

 * spatial direction proportional to e2 and background proportional to I;
 * spatial direction proportional to e1 and background proportional to A_s.

Therefore D is proportional either to S or to S A_s. A nonzero scalar in
D contributes its positive fourth power to c.

Conjugation A -> S A S preserves u,v,j, so J_s(S A S)=J_s(A).
There is also the exact discrete right symmetry

 J_s(A A_s)=b_s^2 J_s(A).

Indeed A -> A A_s sends (u,v,j) to (v,b_s^2 u,-b_s j), and substitution
of alpha_s,beta_s gives the displayed identity. Thus either permitted D
makes the source a positive scalar multiple of J_s itself. Uniqueness
of the four-coefficient representation, with coefficient of j^2 normalized
to one, then gives r=s. This proves the claim.

TRANSPOSED IDENTITIES. Even as a polynomial identity, a transposed version
J_r(A)=c J_s(P A^T D) cannot hold for any allowed r,s and invertible P,D.
This statement does not assume transposition preserves quasiconvexity.
On rank-one matrices it would give

 |x|^4 g_r(n)=c |P n|^4 g_s(D^T x).

The left side separates x and n. Consequently g_r must be a positive
square of a quadratic form, and g_s must also be such a square. For
alpha>0, alpha(n1^2+n2^2)^2+beta n1^4 is a real quadratic square only
when beta=0: the odd coefficients force the mixed quadratic coefficient
to vanish, and the n1^2 n2^2 coefficient forces its diagonal coefficients
to agree. For the allowed sharp parameters this means r=s=1. The radial
rank-one restrictions then force both P and D conformal. Left invariance
of the target would force right-SO(2) invariance of J_1, since conjugating
SO(2) by a conformal D gives SO(2). But

 J_1=(u+v)^2/8+(u-v)j/2+3j^2/2

is not right-SO(2)-invariant. A right quarter-turn changes the sign of
the (u-v)j term, which is not the zero polynomial. Contradiction.

SCOPE. This is a proof about invertible left/right linear changes and their
transposed polynomial identities. It does not exclude a different analytic
consequence, nonlinear transformation, or direct positive-branch QC proof.
The negative-branch heat certificate reported by the parent has separate
provenance; this addendum does not validate that file.
