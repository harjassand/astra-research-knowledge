on all real 2 by 2 matrices that are invariant under LEFT SO(2). K is its
rank-one-convex cone. The exposed contact face is

 C={F in K: D^2F(I)[e12,e12]=0}.

The independent proof in STRUCTURAL_PROOF.txt identifies C with
F=alpha H+beta u^2+gamma j^2+delta u j, where H=(u+v-2j)^2,
u=|Ae1|^2, v=|Ae2|^2, j=det A, and

 [[6beta+delta,delta],[delta,gamma]] >= 0,
 [[8alpha,delta],[delta,2beta+delta]] >= 0.

Its extreme rays are H,u^2,j^2 and J_r, r>0 or r<-2, with
alpha=3r/[8(r+2)], beta=r(r-1)/6, gamma=1, delta=r.

are proved, rather than inferred from a numerical Gram representation.

PRIMARY COMPARISONS

1. Bruno and Pasqualotto, "Quasiconvexity for the Dacorogna--Marcellini
Energy," arXiv:2608.06367v1 (6 August 2026).
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06367v1

Read Theorem 2, the heat/exact-form construction, and all of Appendix A.
The theorem requires BOTH left and right rotations. Its quartics are
Q=a s^2+b s j+c j^2, s=|A|^2. Appendix A gives the exact ROC conditions

 [[12a,3b],[3b,4a+2c]]>=0, [[4a,-b],[-b,4a]]>=0.

For a>0 these mean |b|<=4a and c>=-2a+3b^2/(8a), with the determinant
square as the a=0 boundary. The proof reduces to sharp boundary quartics
plus a nonnegative determinant-square remainder and constructs an exact
correction yielding heat monotonicity. Thus these methods and that entire
isotropic quartic theorem are prior. Right invariance is absent for J_r.
The exact transformation obstruction below goes beyond this mismatch:
the sharp rays do not inherit the theorem by the usual invertible linear
changes, left averaging, and finite positive sums of these source quartics.

2. Aubert, "Quelques remarques sur les notions de 1-rang convexite et de
polyconvexite en dimensions 2 et 3," M2AN 22 (1988), 5--28.
https://www.numdam.org/article/M2AN_1988__22_1_5_0.pdf

Read Section 1, Theorem 1.3, Corollary 1.4, and the planar example in
Section 3. Visually checked printed pp.9 and 18. Theorem 1.3 explicitly
uses the planar supporting inequality

 f(M+X)>=f(M)+Df(M):X+lambda det X.

Corollary 1.4 gives its Hessian translation consequence; the following
remark credits Marcellini's quadratic-form result. Our forced multiplier
lambda=r+2, null direction diag(1,-r-1), and cubic support-gap obstruction
specialize this classical machinery. They are not a new general method.
The example f0=(1/3)|M|^4-(1/6)(det M)^2-(2/3)det M|M|^2 is treated on
det M>0; its displayed extension to all matrices takes +infinity when
det M<=0. These domain/finite-value hypotheses differ from the present
finite quartics on every real matrix. This paper supplies no QC theorem
for the present anisotropic family.

3. Pedregal and Bandeira, "Finding new families of rank-one convex
polynomials," Ann. IHP Anal. Non Lineaire 26 (2009), 1621--1634.
https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/16987

Read Theorem 1 and the quartic specialization around printed p.1625;
visually checked the theorem and matrix formulas. Their general smooth
phi1-c phi2 construction locates the admissible ROC interval through
Legendre--Hadamard quotient extrema. For quartics the Hessian is quadratic
in the background, and generalized eigenvalue/matrix positivity tests
produce ROC families. This is a direct precedent for our algebraic
acoustic-matrix approach. It does not convert ROC to QC for arbitrary
left-invariant quartics or classify this specific contact face.

4. Bandeira and Pedregal, "The Role of Non-Negative Polynomials For
Rank-One Convexity and Quasi Convexity," JEPE 2 (2016), 27--36.
http://www.orthogonal-editions.com/ArticlesVol2/2.pdf
Publisher metadata: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF03377390

Read Theorem 1, Lemma 1, Corollary 2 and Section 5; visually checked the
main theorem and determinant matrix. For planar quadratic forms Q,
Lemma 1 says ROC iff Q=S+lambda D with S positive semidefinite and
G^T D G=2det G, crediting Marcellini. Corollary 2 applies this pointwise
to Hessians of arbitrary C2 planar densities. Theorem 1 expresses the
same condition by extrema over det G=+1 and -1. The scalar lambda may
depend on the background. This is an ROC test, not a global QC supporting
potential. Section 5 discusses Fourier interactions for QC; it does not
give a complete anisotropic quartic QC classification. I do not use its
abstract wording as an impossibility theorem about finite planar tests.

EXACT TRANSFER AUDITS

STRUCTURAL_PROOF.txt Section 7 excludes the following precisely specified
inheritance route. Let Q be any globally ROC two-sided invariant quartic,
P,D invertible real matrices, and average Q(P A D) over left SO(2). Its
rank-one restriction is a0 kP |x|^4 [n^T D D^T n]^2. A sharp J_r has
restriction |x|^4[alpha(n1^2+n2^2)^2+beta n1^4], which is such a quadratic
square only when r=1. That exceptional ray remains anisotropic, whereas
the receiving source would be right invariant. Thus no sharp ray is
such an average. Extremality rules out finite positive sums of these
averages, even supplemented by arbitrary convex quartics. That frozen
Section 7 alone makes only a finite-sum claim.

An explicit left average is E=A q_T^2+B q_T j+C j^2, with trace T=1,
A>=0 and |B|<=4A sqrt(det T)<=2A. Any finite coefficient limit equal to
a sharp ray forces r=1 by its rank-one quadratic-square restriction;
the bound then forces the entire limit two-sided invariant, contradicting
J_1. A compact base and sharp-ray extremality upgrade this to exclusion
from the CLOSED positive cone of these orbits plus convex quartics.
This still excludes no nonlinear maps, signed decompositions,
nonpolynomial source classes, or other analytic consequences.

SYMMETRY AND TERMINOLOGY GATES

The sharp family is not right-C4 invariant. A right quarter-turn swaps
u,v and preserves j, giving

 J_r(AQ)-J_r(A)=(v-u)[beta_r(u+v)+r j],

a nonzero polynomial for every allowed r. Describing it simply as an
"orthotropic/C4 quartic" can therefore import a false receiving symmetry.
Any comparison must specify the actual action, domain and determinant
orientation. Fourth-order tensor does not mean fourth-degree energy.
Level-set quasiconvexity from optimization is also a different notion
from Morrey quasiconvexity used throughout this audit.
