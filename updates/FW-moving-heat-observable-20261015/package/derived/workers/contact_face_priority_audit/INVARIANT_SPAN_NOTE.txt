NINE-DIMENSIONAL INVARIANT QUARTIC SPACE: SPANNING ARGUMENT
15 October 2026. Companion to frozen STRUCTURAL_PROOF.txt, Section 1.

Write the columns of A as complex numbers z1=a+i c, z2=b+i d.
Left rotation multiplies both by e^(i theta). Express a homogeneous
quartic in the complex coordinates z1,z2,bar z1,bar z2. Invariance for
every theta forces each monomial to have the same holomorphic and
antiholomorphic degree, hence degree two in each. Thus every real
invariant quartic is a Hermitian form on the three monomials

 Z=(z1^2,z1 z2,z2^2).

Conversely every such Hermitian form is real and invariant. Its three
real diagonal coefficients and three complex off-diagonal coefficients
give a nine-dimensional real space: the nine mixed monomials Z_i bar Z_j
are linearly independent in the complex polynomial ring.

Now u=z1 bar z1, v=z2 bar z2, and bar z1 z2=w+i j. The three diagonal
forms give u^2,uv,v^2. The off-diagonal real/imaginary parts give
uw,uj,vw,vj, w^2-j^2, and wj, up to nonzero scalar factors and signs.
Since uv=w^2+j^2, replacing w^2-j^2 by w^2 is an invertible real basis
change when uv is retained. Hence the basis in STRUCTURAL_PROOF.txt,

 u^2,v^2,uv,w^2,uw,vw,uj,vj,wj,

spans every left-SO(2)-invariant homogeneous real quartic. Its independence
is also checked directly by the existing exact structural verifier.
This is an elementary invariant-coordinate argument, with no priority claim.
