15 October 2026. The frozen files remain unchanged.

The main lower bound and the sharp segment minimax equality are proved for
DETERMINISTIC finite sequential strategies, or deterministic well-posed causal
policies, with arbitrary bounded deterministic observation error. This class is
already sufficient for the stated all-waveform comparison and exact ramp probe.

The sentence about a randomized strategy's equal-prior classification error
being at least one half uses a stronger explicit noise-adversary contract:

  The bounded corruption may be causal and may react to the applied current
  history; it need not be an oblivious fixed function selected before the
  investigator's random seed. It does not need access to that private seed or
  to future random choices.

Indeed, under actual model Z_theta the causal corruption

  e_theta(t) = (1/2-theta)*(g*i)(t)

makes the observed output equal to the midpoint model's output, for every
realized current history. The convolution uses only past applied current and
has magnitude at most the stated bound for the selected hard parameters.
Consequently, conditional on the investigator's own random seed, the observed
transcript is still independent of which endpoint is true. Equal-prior binary
classification error is then at least one half. The same argument extends the
segment minimax lower bound to randomized strategies under this current-reactive
adversarial error model.

No randomized lower bound against an OBLIVIOUS fixed-noise adversary is claimed.
No independent Gaussian, white-noise, stochastic calibration, or repeated-sample
noise law is assumed or ruled out. Such assumptions define different problems.
In summaries, either say deterministic strategies and arbitrary bounded error,
or state the causal current-reactive noise contract when including randomized
strategies. Do not silently combine these two quantifier orders.
