1. Bogoliubov weighted graph bound with precise exponents.

For d modes and an integer k >= 0, on the algebraic Fock domain,

  sum_{m=0}^k sum_{j_1,...,j_m} ||a_{j_1}...a_{j_m} psi||^2
     = <psi, [sum_{m=0}^k N(N-1)...(N-m+1)] psi>.

The m=0 term is 1. This polynomial is comparable, above and below, to
(1+N)^k on the nonnegative integer spectrum. Thus this expression is
equivalent to ||W^{k/2} psi||^2. Every fixed word of length m in annihilation
and creation operators sends each number vector to a uniquely shifted
number vector, with coefficient bounded by (N+m)^{m/2}; consequently its
norm is at most C_m ||W^{m/2} psi||. After a finite-mode Bogoliubov unitary U,
each annihilation operator U^* a_j U is a finite linear combination of
annihilation and creation operators. Expand the words in the first identity
and apply the preceding word bounds. It follows that

  ||W^{k/2} U psi|| <= C_{U,k} ||W^{k/2} psi||.

For the concrete two-mode squeezer on finite-number vectors, the SU(1,1)
disentangling coefficients are a finite polynomial times a geometric tail;
the images belong to every number-power domain. This justifies the operator
calculation on that core. The inequality then extends by closure to the
graph domain, and holds for U^* by the same argument. Tensor products of
the squeezers and the downstream passive rotation retain this property.

Let Phi(X)=Tr_env(U X U^*) be the retained channel and W_tot=1+N_tot.
For the weighted trace class with norm ||X||_(k)=
||W_tot^{k/2} X W_tot^{k/2}||_1, the operator

  K_k=(W_0^{k/2} tensor I) U W_tot^{-k/2}

is bounded: W_0 and W_tot commute after tensor embedding, W_0<=W_tot,
and the graph bound applies. Factoring around K_k gives

  ||W_0^{k/2} Phi(X) W_0^{k/2}||_1 <= ||K_k||^2 ||X||_(k).

This is valid for arbitrary weighted trace-class X, not just positive X.
The exponents needed below are k=6 for states (sandwich W^3), k=4 for
generator expressions (sandwich W^2), and k=7 for seventh state moments.
No operator-power monotonicity under conjugation is asserted.

2. Covariance: prove the generator equality first, without differentiating
an unbounded semigroup in an unspecified topology.

Use the uncentered thermal generator
  L_r X = sum_j [(r+1) D[a_j]X + r D[a_j^*]X].
For Weyl W(z)=exp(sum_j(z_j a_j^*-conj(z_j) a_j)), direct commutator
calculation yields the weak characteristic-function identity

  chi_{L_r X}(z) = -1/2 (z . partial_z + conj(z) . partial_conj(z))chi_X(z)
                  -(r+1/2)|z|^2 chi_X(z).

For example D[a]^* W(z)=-1/2 Euler W(z)-|z|^2 W(z)/2,
whereas D[a^*]^* W(z)=+1/2 Euler W(z)-|z|^2 W(z)/2.
The combination above is therefore the ordinary contractive OU generator.

For the complete joint physical channel,
  c = sqrt(lambda_A)a + sqrt(lambda_B)b^* + sqrt(lambda_D)d,
  lambda_A-lambda_B+lambda_D=1,
  r_0=lambda_A r_A+lambda_B(r_B+1)+lambda_D r_D,
the characteristic function of its retained output is the JOINT input
characteristic function at
  (sqrt(lambda_A)z, -sqrt(lambda_B)conj(z), sqrt(lambda_D)z).
This identity is valid for arbitrary joint input X; product input is not
needed. Restricting the sum of the three Euler derivatives to this linear
argument gives exactly the output Euler derivative. The scalar noise is

  lambda_A(r_A+1/2)+lambda_B(r_B+1/2)+lambda_D(r_D+1/2)=r_0+1/2.

Hence, first for finite-number-supported joint X,
  L_{r_0} Phi(X) = Phi(sum_s L_{r_s} X).
Both expressions are trace class (indeed weighted trace class), and Weyl
characteristic functions determine trace-class operators, so this is an
operator equality, not merely a formal differential identity. The squeezed
output of a finite-support X has every moment by the graph bound.

Now take X=rho_A tensor rho_B tensor tau_D at a minimizing pair. It has
finite seventh total-number moment because (1+sum_s N_s)^7 <=
3^6 sum_s(1+N_s)^7 on the joint number basis. Write T=W_tot^3 X W_tot^3.
For joint number cutoffs P_K, P_K T P_K -> T in trace norm.
The source bounded-factor calculation makes each L_s a continuous map
from sandwich W^3 trace class to sandwich W^2 trace class. For example,

  W^2 a X a^* W^2 = (W^2 a W^{-3}) T (W^{-3} a^* W^2),
  W^2 a^*a X W^2 = (a^*a W^{-1}) T W^{-1}.

All displayed factors are bounded, as are their creation analogues.
Apply channel continuity with k=6 to cutoff states and with k=4 to cutoff
generator expressions. The generator on the output is also continuous
from sandwich W_0^3 to sandwich W_0^2. Thus BOTH sides of covariance
converge in ||W_0^2(.)W_0^2||_1. Since L_D tau_D=0, the full equality is

  L_{r_0} gamma = Phi_A(delta_A)+Phi_B(delta_B), delta_i=L_{r_i}rho_i.

For rho_0=(1-theta)gamma+theta tau_{r_0}, one finally has
  delta_0=(1-theta)sum_i Phi_i(delta_i).
The cutoff joint operators need not remain product; the finite-cutoff
covariance proved above handles this harmlessly.

3. Logarithmic pairings and slicing.

The generator expressions are traceless and W_s^2 delta_s W_s^2 is trace
class. Since 0<=H_s<=C_s W_s^4, W_s^{-2}H_s W_s^{-2} is bounded as a form.
This defines Tr(delta_s H_s) unambiguously by the weighted factorization.
Decompose the self-adjoint T_i=W_i^2 delta_i W_i^2 into its positive and
negative parts. Then alpha_i^+/-=W_i^{-2}(T_i)_+/-W_i^{-2} are positive
trace-class operators with finite fourth moments. Their product with the
other fixed states has finite fourth total-number moment, so the physical
output has finite fourth moment by k=4. Positive slicing applies separately
to each, and subtraction gives

  Tr(delta_0 H_0)=(1-theta)sum_i Tr(delta_i T_i^{slice} H_0).

This is enough to pair the Gibbs Hamiltonian identities. No assumption
that rho_i+t delta_i is a state for two-sided t is required.

4. Entropy production is unchanged, including for the creation port.

Write a'_j=a_j-mu_j, e'=Tr(rho N)-|mu|^2, h=log(1+1/r),
v_{lr}=log(p_l/p_r)/h in an eigenbasis rho=diag(p_l), and
K_{lr}=(r+1)p_r-r p_l. The generator is still UNcentered. Direct
evaluation of its two dissipators gives

  sigma=Tr((L_r rho)(-log rho))/h
       =-sum_{j,l,r} v_{lr} K_{lr}|a'_{j,lr}|^2.

Centering changes only eigenbasis diagonal elements, where v=0. Under the
assumed seventh-moment and logarithm bounds, crossed logarithmic sums are
bounded by C Tr(rho W^5): a' psi has W^2 norm bounded by W^{5/2} psi.
Uncrossed sums use <W^4>_psi <W>_psi <= <W^5>_psi. These make the sum
absolutely convergent; eigenbasis truncation converges in sandwich W^3
trace norm, and the bounded-factor calculation justifies the pairings.

The defect norm computed in source section 04 is
  ||q^down||_{F^down,*}^2=sum(1-v_{lr})K_{lr}|a'_{j,lr}|^2.
Subtract sigma. The remaining sum is
  (r+1)Tr rho sum_j (a'_j)^*a'_j-r Tr rho sum_j a'_j(a'_j)^*
    =(r+1)e'-r(e'+n)=e'-nr,
using the physical CCR. Therefore
  sigma=||q^down||^2-(e'-nr).

For port B the actual generator is exactly the same positive thermal
relaxation L_{r_B}; it is NOT reversed. The reversed defect and metric obey
  q^up(Z)=-conj(q^down(Z^*)), ||Z||_{F^up}=||Z^*||_{F^down},
so their dual norms agree. Consequently its signed metric estimate feeds
the ordinary entropy-production identity without a new generator formula.

5. Exact cancellation with every sign and mean term retained.

Pair the assumed Gibbs identities with delta_i and apply section 3:
  0=sigma_0-(1+epsilon)sum_i w_i sigma_i
       +epsilon sum_i w_i Tr(delta_i N_i)+zeta sum_i Tr(delta_i W_i^4).
Put E'_s=e'_s-nr_s, A_*^2=sum_i w_i||q_i^down||^2,
M_*^2=sum_i w_i|mu_i|^2. The uncentered generator gives
  Tr(delta_i N_i)=-E'_i-|mu_i|^2.
Independence and the signed thermal balance give, after output replacement,
  E'_0=sum_i w_i E'_i+theta(1-theta)|mu_gamma|^2.
Substitution yields the exact identity

  0=||q_0||^2-(1+epsilon)A_*^2-epsilon M_*^2
       -theta(1-theta)|mu_gamma|^2+zeta P,
  P=sum_i Tr(delta_i W_i^4).

In particular, there is NO leftover photon +n or sign error from the
creation port: its vacuum +n has already canceled in the signed balance.

The number drift polynomial is, with x=N_i, r=r_i,
  P_{r,n}(x)=-4x^4+(4nr+12r-6)x^3+(18nr+24r-4)x^2
             +(28nr+14r-1)x+15nr.
Its leading coefficient is -4, so its supremum on nonnegative integers is
finite, uniformly over the minimizing states and zeta (r_i,n are fixed).
Thus P<=C. The preceding signed metric/mixture gate supplies
  ||q_0||^2<=A_*^2+(epsilon/2)(A_*^2+M_*^2).
It follows, retaining the nonpositive mean term or dropping it,
  0 <= -(epsilon/2)(A_*^2+M_*^2)+C zeta,
and both input means and defects vanish as zeta ->0.
