Adaptive follow-up: exact constant-gamma terminal-|x| plateau attack

Outcome

I did not find a constant-gamma terminal-|x| plateau with Q'''<0. A direct, high-precision calculation for an exact rational parameter choice gives Q'''>0 with a comfortable margin. The same example has a strongly negative pointwise Q''' integrand at some x, so pointwise positivity is definitely false; it does not falsify positivity after expectation. The earlier negative Gauss-Hermite values were quadrature aliasing and must not be used as counterexamples. The requested source pin in work/reports/24_probability.txt is corrected to 8aed7fd74eb14622ed5a0a3635a799374296e32a, verified by `git -C work/sources/astra rev-parse HEAD`.

Exact PDE and diffusion law

Take unit-clock terminal value |x|, constant gamma c>0 on [0,S], and let d=S-s. Cole-Hopf gives

  H_c(d,x) = E exp(c|x+sqrt(d)Z|)
           = exp(c^2 d/2) [ exp(cx) N((x+cd)/sqrt(d))
                            + exp(-cx) N((cd-x)/sqrt(d)) ],
  U(s,x) = c^{-1} log H_c(S-s,x),
where N is the standard normal CDF.

The forward diffusion dX=c U_x(s,X)ds+dB is the Doob h-transform of Brownian motion with terminal weight exp(c|B_S|). Thus its time-s density is

  p_s(x)=phi_s(x) H_c(S-s,x)/H_c(S,0).

This gives an exact one-dimensional formula for the expectation in Q'''. Set c=10, d=1, s=1/100, hence S=101/100, and set z=cx, alpha=c^2s=1. Let N be the standard normal CDF and define

  T(z)=exp(z) N(10+z/10)+exp(-z) N(10-z/10),  L(z)=log T(z).

Then H_10(1,x)=exp(50)T(z), H_10(101/100,0)=2exp(101/2)N(sqrt(101)), and the density of z=10X_s is exactly

  phi_1(z) exp(-1/2) T(z) / [2 N(sqrt(101))].

Since U(s,x)=5+(1/10)L(10x) on this time slice,

  g=U_xx=10L'',  h=U_xxx=100L''',  k=U_xxxx=1000L''''.

The plateau identity therefore yields

  Q'''/10^6 = integral_R [phi_1(z)exp(-1/2)T(z)/(2N(sqrt(101)))]
                         [ (L'''')^2 - 12 L''(L''')^2 + 6(L'')^4 ] dz.

High-precision mpmath differentiation and adaptive integration (35 decimal working precision; script included as work/reports/adaptive24_plateau.py) give

  Q'''/10^6 = 0.31068920868539258403253058003,
  Q'''      = 310689.20868539258403253058003.

This is numerical quadrature, not an interval-certified sign proof. It is stable under direct adaptive integration and is far from zero. For context, a coarse scan of the same constant-gamma integral over c in [0.02,30] and alpha=c^2s in [0.001,300] found no negative values; the smallest values occurred in the far-tail regime and were tiny positive quadrature values. This finite scan is not a theorem.

What the calculation does falsify

For z0=arcosh(sqrt(2))=0.8813735870..., the scaled pointwise integrand in the displayed expectation equals

  (L'''')^2 - 12 L''(L''')^2 + 6(L'')^4
      = -2.375000000000000000000049,

so the original integrand at x=z0/10 is about -2,375,000. In the c->infinity boundary-layer limit T(z) tends to 2cosh(z), and the value tends to exactly -19/8. The limit follows by direct substitution: with v=sech^2(z), the integrand is 2v^2(8-48v+45v^2), which is -19/8 at v=1/2. Gaussian-tail corrections at c=10 and z=z0 are below the shown precision. Thus any proof of Q'''>0 must use expectation-level structure; convexity g>=0 or a pointwise sign argument cannot suffice.

I also evaluated the left endpoint of a two-level monotone history: gamma=5 up to s=1/100, then gamma=10 through S=101/100. At the plateau's left endpoint, the forward density is proportional to phi_1(z)T(z)^(1/2); the same profile gives Q'''/10^6 approximately 0.5331437028566371. A coarse finite scan of this two-level family over c, alpha, and gamma_before/c in [0,1] produced no negative value. This also is exploratory numerical evidence, not a universal result.

Conclusion and remaining gate

The requested sharp falsification was not obtained. The exact global constant-gamma model does not furnish it at the tested parameters, and the strongest concrete finding is the negative local integrand despite positive Q'''. The original report's load-bearing unconditional expectation inequality remains unresolved: this follow-up neither proves Q'''>0 for every admissible nondecreasing gamma nor rules out a more complicated admissible history producing Q'''<=0. The next decisive step is an interval-certified search over a parameterized monotone piecewise-constant gamma history; if it finds Q'''<0, report the exact profile, time, density, and certified integral sign. Otherwise a proof still needs a structural expectation inequality, not pointwise positivity.
