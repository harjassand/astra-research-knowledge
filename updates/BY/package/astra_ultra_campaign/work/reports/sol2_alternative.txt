DEPTH SOL 2 — INDEPENDENT AMPLIFIER REDUCTION AND ITS DECISIVE FAILURE
Frozen before reading any candidate manuscript or peer report, 2026-10-10.

Target and status

For arbitrary finite n, independent n-mode finite-energy states A and B,
and c_j=sqrt(G)a_j+sqrt(G-1)b_j^dagger, G>=1, prove
  N(C)>=G N(A)+(G-1)(N(B)+1),
where N(rho)=g^{-1}(S(rho)/n) and
g(r)=(r+1)log(r+1)-r log r. Internal multimode entanglement is allowed.
This all-input target is NOT proved by my independent approach.
I instead rigorously falsified the universal physical-endpoint dilation
inequality that would have reduced it to the pinned passive EPnI.
This counterexample does not refute the amplifier EPnI itself.

Independent reduction attempted

Write chi_rho(z)=Tr rho D(z) in a Weyl convention in which number state k
has characteristic exp(-|z|^2/2)L_k(|z|^2). Full transpose B^T is positive,
finite-energy and entropy preserving; its characteristic reverses the
appropriate phase-space orientation. Let t=2G-1 and eta=G/t. Form the
physical passive output X of A and B^T with weights eta and 1-eta.
Then the amplifier characteristic is exactly
  chi_C(z)=chi_X(sqrt(t)z).
Denote this phase-space dilation D_t. If one could prove, whenever rho
and D_t rho are both states,
  N(D_t rho)+1/2 >= t(N(rho)+1/2),                  (D)
then passive EPnI applied to X would immediately give the target:
t[eta N(A)+(1-eta)N(B)+1/2]-1/2
=G N(A)+(G-1)N(B)+G-1.
All algebra is exact; the indispensable new mechanism was (D).

Decisive falsifier: even physical endpoints do not satisfy (D)

Use one mode, t=3/2, and the explicitly preparable number-diagonal state
  rho=(15|0><0|+74|1><1|+11|2><2|)/100.
Its characteristic is exp(-x/2)(1-(24/25)x+(11/200)x^2), x=|z|^2.
For any finite number-diagonal input with probability generating function
P(z), dilation has the exact generating-function transformation
  Q(z)=2/[t+1-(t-1)z]
       *P(((t+1)z-(t-1))/(t+1-(t-1)z)).
For this rho and t it gives the diagonal output sigma with
  q_m=16(198m^2+324m+1)/(3125*5^m), m=0,1,2,... .
Every q_m is strictly positive. Using x=1/5 and the exact geometric
moments sum x^m=1/(1-x), sum m x^m=x/(1-x)^2 and
sum m^2 x^m=x(1+x)/(1-x)^3 proves sum q_m=1. The cubic moment similarly
gives sum m q_m=169/100. Both endpoints have finite energy.

The output has a conventional channel preparation too: it is the
quantum-limited amplifier with gain 5/4 and vacuum environment applied
to diag(4,522,99)/625. Those three Fock inputs produce the geometric,
shifted negative-binomial-of-order-two, and shifted negative-binomial-
of-order-three laws respectively. Their positive mixture reconstructs
q_m exactly. This is a state-preparation identity, not a claim that D_t
is a realizable channel on arbitrary states.

Certified logarithm bounds establish
  S(rho)>g(1/3), and S(sigma)<g(3/4).
Thus N(rho)>1/3, whereas N(sigma)<3/4, contradicting (D) at t=3/2.
Numerical readings of the certified rational intervals are
  S(rho)=0.7501860068438535,
  g(1/3)=0.7497801928250778,
  S(sigma)=1.1628738020948082,
  g(3/4)=1.1950891832258255.
The certified entropy separation g(3/4)-S(sigma) exceeds
0.0322153811310172. These are not merely floating-point tests.

A second exact obstruction rules out a positive dilation path even for
the special amplifier-normalized passive outputs. Choose G=3, eta=3/5,
A=B=|1><1|. Passive X has probabilities (12/25,1/25,12/25). Although X
and D_5 X (the genuine gain-3 amplifier output) are physical, D_{3/2}X
has photon-one probability -596/15625. No heat-flow argument may assume
that the intermediate dilation path consists of states.

Other independent attempts and their precise gaps

An exact adjoint relation also holds. With parity-transposed environment
E=(Pi B Pi)^T, the amplifier with environment B obeys
  (Lambda_G^B)^*(X)=G^{-n} E_{1/G}^{E}(X)
on suitable trace-class tests. Weyl-kernel change of variables proves
the scale factor. This transfers Schatten norms through dual exponents,
not entropy-constrained minimum-output statements: conjugate exponents
near p=1 move to infinity, and the passive Shannon entropy theorem alone
does not supply the needed sharp norms. No Shannon-duality claim follows.

For thermal environments the passive infinitesimal inequality gives,
at N=N(rho), h=log(1+1/N), loss/gain entropy rates P_- and P_+ satisfying
  (b+1)P_-+bP_+ >= n(b-N)h, b>=0.
Consequently P_->=-nNh and P_-+P_+>=nh. The amplifier infinitesimal
bound instead needs
  bP_-+(b+1)P_+ >= n(b+N+1)h.
Its missing sharp inequality already at b=0 is P_+>=n(N+1)h.
The two passive-derived scalar lower bounds do not imply it. A
multimode gain entropy-production theorem would close thermal-environment
amplifiers, but still would not compare two freely varying environments.

Achieved versus potential significance

Achieved here: exact all-sector counterexample with rigorous entropy
bounds to a tempting independent amplifier reduction, and an exact
nonpositive intermediate path for true amplifier endpoints. This is
useful proof triage, not the requested landmark theorem. No novelty
priority or external validation is asserted. The passive-subclass
dilation inequality restricted exactly to X=A boxplus_eta B^T remains
unproved and is not contradicted by the universal counterexample above.

Next proof gate: replace the false universal dilation principle by a
valid inequality exploiting X's precise two-port product provenance, or
audit a direct signed Bogoliubov variational proof without any phase-space
dilation positivity assumption. Full arbitrary-n finite-energy input
quantifiers must survive that gate.
