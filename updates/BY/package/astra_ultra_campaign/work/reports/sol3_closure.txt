RESULT AND BOUNDARY

The infinite-dimensional analytic gaps identified in Sections 5 and 7 of signed_bogoliubov_epni_candidate.md close for each fixed finite number of modes and each fixed finite gain G>1. Below are proofs of the Gaussian graph bounds, weighted OU differentiation, covariance, logarithmic pairings, existence/Gibbs regularity, and the final thermal limit. No uniform bound as G tends to infinity, as n tends to infinity, or as the fourth-moment penalty tends to zero is asserted or needed.

This is an analytic closure result, not an independent certification of the candidate's signed four-weight interpolation or sharp metric/defect comparison. Conditional on those explicitly identified estimates, the variational argument closes. The distinction matters: the passive EPnI source alone does not imply the signed metric estimate.

1. EXACT GRAPH BOUND; NO OPERATOR-POWER MONOTONICITY

Let H=ell^2(N_0^d), d finite, N=sum_j a_j^dagger a_j and W=1+N. Let U be a finite homogeneous Gaussian unitary with U^dagger a_j U=sum_l(P_jl a_l+Q_jl a_l^dagger). Both U and U^dagger have finite coefficients. For every integer k>=0, U maps D(W^(k/2)) onto itself and

  ||W^(k/2) U psi|| <= C_(U,k)||W^(k/2) psi||.

Proof, including the core issue. For every integer m>=0,

  sum_(j_1,...,j_m) ||a_j1 ... a_jm psi||^2
     = <psi,N(N-1)...(N-m+1)psi>.

Thus the exact identity, not merely an equivalence, is

  ||W^(k/2)psi||^2
   = sum_(m=0)^k S(k+1,m+1)
       sum_(j_1,...,j_m)||a_j1 ... a_jm psi||^2,                 (1)

where S is a Stirling number of the second kind and the empty word is I. Initially use the finite-particle span. Each fixed mixed creation/annihilation word of length m has a fixed number-basis displacement. Its coefficients are bounded by (N+m)^(m/2), so, for m>=1,

  ||word psi|| <= m^(m/2)||W^(m/2)psi||.                        (2)

Images under U of finite-particle vectors have every number moment, so the intertwining products used here have an actual common domain. For the amplifier this follows directly from

  U|0> = G^(-n/2) sum_(k in N_0^n)
               ((G-1)/G)^(|k|/2)|k>_A|k>_B,

up to irrelevant phase convention: any polynomial number weight is summable against its geometric tail. Images of finite-number vectors are finite ladder polynomials on this vacuum. Passive postprocessing preserves total number. For a general homogeneous Gaussian U, write U a U^dagger=P' a+Q' a^dagger. The CCR imply P' invertible and Z=-P'^(-1)Q' symmetric, with ZZ^dagger=I-P'^(-1)P'^(-dagger)<I. The normalized solution of (a-Z a^dagger)psi=0 is a Gaussian exponential; Takagi diagonalization of Z is a passive rotation and reduces it to single-mode squeezed vacua. Their even-number squared coefficients are bounded by a constant times |z|^(2m), |z|<1: the remaining factor is binom(2m,m)/4^m<=1. This proves the same core assertion for every such U.

Put L=max_j sum_l(|P_jl|+|Q_jl|). Expanding each transformed annihilator word and using (2) in (1) gives the explicit permissible constant

  C_(U,k)^2 =
   1+sum_(m=1)^k S(k+1,m+1)d^m L^(2m)m^m.                     (3)

Approximate psi in D(W^(k/2)) by its number cutoffs. Estimate (3) makes their images Cauchy in the graph norm; closedness of W^(k/2) proves the estimate and domain inclusion. Apply the same argument to U^dagger to obtain equality of domains. The needed exponents are k=4,6,7; k=1 also gives the linear energy form bound. In particular no inference of the invalid form A<=B => A^p<=B^p for p>1 occurs.

2. WEIGHTED TRACE SPACES AND GAUSSIAN PARTIAL TRACE

For p>=0 define B_p(W)={W^(-p)YW^(-p): Y trace class}, with norm ||X||_(p)=||Y||_1. For a positive state, membership is exactly Tr rho W^(2p)<infinity. Number cutoffs are dense: ||P_K X P_K-X||_(p)->0.

On a complete input/output system let W_tot=1+N_tot. If W_0=1+N_0 denotes the retained port, then

  K_p=(W_0^p tensor I) U W_tot^(-p)

is bounded with norm at most C_(U,2p) for integer or half-integer p with 2p integer. Indeed (W_0^p tensor I)W_tot^(-p) is a contraction in the output number basis, and the remaining factor is controlled by (3). For X in B_p(W_tot), bounded cutoff/duality followed by trace-class density gives

  W_0^p Tr_env(U X U^dagger) W_0^p
    =Tr_env[K_p(W_tot^p X W_tot^p)K_p^dagger],                 (4)

and therefore norm at most C_(U,2p)^2 ||X||_(p). Partial trace is trace-norm contractive even for nonpositive X, by its bounded-observable dual formula.

For finitely many ports, W_tot<=product_s W_s in the joint number basis. Hence (tensor_s X_s) is in B_p(W_tot) when every X_s is in its local B_p, with norm at most product_s ||X_s||_(p). Also

  Tr(tensor_s rho_s)W_tot^m
      <= L_ports^(m-1) sum_s Tr rho_s W_s^m                  (m>=1),

using W_tot<=sum_s W_s and their commutativity. Thus finite seventh moments of every input imply a finite seventh moment of the retained Gaussian output by (4) at p=7/2. Linear energy bounds require only p=1/2. Cutoff convergence at p=3 and generator convergence at p=2 both survive squeezing with finite constants.

Weak defect traces already require only finite energy. For example a rho=(a rho^(1/2))rho^(1/2) is trace class by Hilbert--Schmidt factorization; the creation and right-product variants have the same property. The k=1 graph bound makes the full transformed state's energy finite as well. A discarded ladder operator can be cut off in its own number basis; its bounded cutoff commutes with every retained bounded observable, and its products with the state converge in trace norm by the same factorization. This justifies weak commutator cancellation and sliced traces without assuming that arbitrary bounded observables preserve ladder-operator domains.

3. OU DIFFERENTIATION IN THE REQUIRED NORM

For r>=0, define

  L_r X=sum_j [(r+1)D[a_j]X+rD[a_j^dagger]X].

The number-shift factors show, for every integer p>=0, that L_r is a bounded linear map B_(p+1)->B_p. For example, putting Y=W^(p+1)XW^(p+1),

  W^p a X a^dagger W^p
    =(W^p a W^(-p-1))Y(W^(-p-1)a^dagger W^p),
  W^p a^dagger a X W^p=(a^dagger a W^(-1))Y W^(-1).

All displayed outer factors and the creation analogues are bounded.

Here is a direct proof of differentiation; characteristic functions alone would not establish this topology. Let T_t^(r) be thermal attenuation with transmissivity e^(-t), obtained by a passive beam splitter against tau_r. The passive dilation and the tensor estimate above give

  ||T_t^(r) X||_(p) <= [Tr tau_r W_bath^(2p)]||X||_(p)        (5)

uniformly for t>=0. Finite joint number cutoffs and continuity on each finite-number sector prove strong continuity in B_p at t=0. Density and (5) extend it to all of B_p.

To identify the weighted generator on the dense finite-number matrices, use the exact channel factorization

  T_t^(r)=A_(g_t) Lambda_(eta_t),
  g_t=1+r(1-e^(-t)),    eta_t=e^(-t)/g_t,                      (6)

where Lambda is vacuum loss and A is a vacuum amplifier. For one-mode |m><l| their number-basis expressions are

  Lambda_eta(|m><l|)
   =sum_(q=0)^min(m,l) sqrt[binom(m,q)binom(l,q)]
      (1-eta)^q eta^((m+l)/2-q)|m-q><l-q|,

  A_g(|m><l|)
   =sum_(q>=0) sqrt[binom(m+q,q)binom(l+q,q)]
      g^(-(m+l)/2-1)((g-1)/g)^q |m+q><l+q|.

For n modes take the product coefficients and multi-index q. These formulas follow by expanding the vacuum environment number vectors in the loss/squeeze dilation. In (6), g'_0=r and eta'_0=-(r+1). The q=0,1 terms give exactly L_r. In the amplifier series the q>=2 terms have B_p norm O(t^2), because the weighted rank-one norm is a fixed polynomial in q and the remaining tail is geometric; this bound is uniform for small t with (g_t-1)/g_t below a fixed number less than one. Thus the derivative at zero exists in B_p for every finite-number matrix.

The B_p strongly continuous semigroup then obeys

  T_t^(r)X-X=int_0^t T_s^(r)L_r X ds

on those matrices. Approximate arbitrary X in B_(p+1) by P_KXP_K, use L_r:B_(p+1)->B_p and (5), and pass to the Bochner integral. Strong continuity gives

  (T_t^(r)X-X)/t -> L_r X in B_p as t decreases to zero.      (7)

In particular Tr rho W^6<infinity suffices for (7) in B_2, and the Gibbs states below have still more regularity. Tr L_r rho=0 follows by continuity of trace. This establishes the requested OU differentiation, although source05 deliberately does not need entropy differentiation.

4. EXACT GAUSSIAN/OU COVARIANCE AT THE MINIMUM

Fix n<infinity, G>1, 0<u<1, positive reference occupations r_A,r_B,r_D, and set

  lambda_A=(1-u)G, lambda_B=(1-u)(G-1), lambda_D=u,
  r_0=lambda_A r_A+lambda_B(r_B+1)+lambda_D r_D.

Then lambda_A-lambda_B+lambda_D=1 and

  sum_s lambda_s(r_s+1/2)=r_0+1/2.                           (8)

The independent-input retained characteristic function is

  chi_gamma(z)=chi_A(sqrt(lambda_A)z)
               chi_B(-sqrt(lambda_B)conjugate(z))
               chi_D(sqrt(lambda_D)z).

Thermal OU sends chi_rho(z) to
chi_rho(e^(-t/2)z)exp[-(r+1/2)(1-e^(-t))|z|^2].
Multiplication and (8) establish the exact channel identity for every t>=0, with the fixed thermal D unchanged.

At a finite-seventh-moment input pair, all local curves are differentiable in B_2 by (7). Tensor multiplication is continuous into joint B_2, and (4) is continuous from joint B_2 to retained B_2. The retained gamma also has finite seventh moment, so its OU curve has a B_2 derivative. Differentiating the exact identity in this Banach space, not just formally in chi, gives

  L_(r_0)gamma=Phi_A(L_(r_A)rho_A)+Phi_B(L_(r_B)rho_B).       (9)

Here Phi_i is linear in the varied input with the other two fixed. For rho_0=(1-theta)gamma+theta tau_(r_0), 0<theta<1,

  delta_0=(1-theta)sum_i Phi_i(delta_i), delta_i=L_(r_i)rho_i.

Every expression belongs to B_2. This proof uses the product-input interface exactly; it does not posit a completely positive time-reversal channel.

5. ATTAINMENT, SLICED LOGARITHM, AND GIBBS REGULARITY

Keep all r_s,u,theta,epsilon positive and fixed; only zeta tends to zero. Put w_i=(1-theta)lambda_i. Expanding the candidate objective up to fixed constants gives

  J_zeta=S(rho_0)/h_0
       -sum_i (1+epsilon)w_i S(rho_i)/h_i
       +epsilon sum_i w_i Tr rho_i N_i
       +zeta sum_i Tr rho_i W_i^4.                          (10)

The entropy bound S(rho_i)<=n g(E_i/n)=O(log(1+E_i)) and S(rho_0)>=0 bound each input energy on every upper sublevel of (10), uniformly for 0<zeta<=zeta_0. At fixed zeta the fourth moments are bounded. Energy compactness gives a trace-norm convergent minimizing subsequence; Gaussian energy is bounded by C(1+E_A+E_B+E_D) from (3) at k=1. Consequently input and output entropies converge. Energy and fourth moments are lower semicontinuous under positive bounded spectral truncation. A minimizing pair therefore exists and has the same uniform energy bound.

The replacement satisfies rho_0>=theta tau_(r_0). Operator monotonicity of the logarithm, via its resolvent formula, gives as forms

  0<=H_0=-log rho_0<=-log(theta tau_(r_0))<=C W_0.           (11)

Define T_i for bounded observables by the unital CP slice of the physical Gaussian channel, and T_iH_0 by increasing bounded spectral truncations. The k=1 graph estimate gives the form inequality
U^dagger(W_0 tensor I)U<=C W_tot. Slicing in fixed finite-energy other ports gives

  0<=T_iH_0<=C_i W_i.                                     (12)

This is the exact replacement for total-number preservation in source03 lines 235–242; it applies to either the annihilation or creation input. The constant can depend on fixed other-port energies, which are bounded on the minimizing sublevel.

Replacing the output entropy by its cross entropy against H_0 yields a majorant equal to J_zeta at the minimum. After dividing by a_i=(1+epsilon)w_i/h_i, its input-dependent part is

  Tr sigma K_i-S(sigma),
  K_i=a_i^(-1)[(1-theta)T_iH_0/h_0
                    +epsilon w_i N_i+zeta W_i^4].

By (12), K_i=k_iW_i^4+W_i^(1/2)B_iW_i^(1/2), where k_i>0 and B_i is bounded positive.

For completeness the Gibbs lemma has no hidden squeezing assumption. Put C_i=k_i I+W_i^(-3/2)B_iW_i^(-3/2). The closed positive form ||C_i^(1/2)W_i^2 psi||^2 on D(W_i^2) has inverse W_i^(-2)C_i^(-1)W_i^(-2), compact and injective. Its eigenvalues u_j have polynomial counting, bounded by the oscillator counting for k_iW_i^4. Each normalized eigenvector obeys

  ||W_i^2 psi_j||^2<=u_j/k_i,
  k_i W_i^(7/2)psi_j=u_j W_i^(-1/2)psi_j-B_i W_i^(1/2)psi_j.

The latter equation holds first coordinatewise against number vectors; its right side is in the Hilbert space. It therefore proves psi_j in D(W_i^(7/2)) and a polynomial bound on that norm in u_j. Exponential Gibbs weights then give
0<Tr exp(-K_i)<infinity and Tr exp(-K_i)W_i^7<infinity.
Relative-entropy nonnegativity uniquely minimizes Tr sigma K_i-S(sigma), so the minimizing input must be exp(-K_i)/Tr exp(-K_i).

It follows that every input is faithful with finite seventh moment, and

  a_i H_i=(1-theta)T_iH_0/h_0
                 +epsilon w_i N_i+zeta W_i^4+c_i I,         (13)

as forms on D(W_i^2), with 0<=H_i<=C_i'W_i^4. Constants in these bounds need not be uniform in zeta. By (4) at p=7/2, gamma, rho_0, and the fixed thermal port have finite seventh moment. This completes every assertion of the analog of source03's regularized-minimum proposition.

The input finite-eigenblock Hessian argument also survives unchanged. Formula (13) gives the exact one-input objective difference

  J_zeta(tilde rho_i)-J_zeta(rho_i)
    =a_i D(tilde rho_i||rho_i)
                  -D(tilde rho_0||rho_0)/h_0 >=0.

Chosen input eigenvectors have finite seventh moment, so finite Hermitian trace-zero eigenblock perturbations are admitted. The input Hessian is sum|A_lr|^2/ell(p_l,p_r); the output Gibbs variational lower bound tested on a centered finite output eigenblock has Hessian ||Z||_0^2/h_0. Optimizing the input perturbation yields

  ||T_iZ-<T_iZ>_i I||_i^2
      <= [(1+epsilon)w_i/(1-theta)^2]||Z||_0^2.

Divide by lambda_i to obtain the candidate component Hessian bound. Finite eigenblock density and the CP slice Schwarz inequality identify its extension on every bounded Z. This does not require lambda_A+lambda_B<=1.

6. LOGARITHMIC PAIRINGS AND ENTROPY PRODUCTION

Write delta_s=L_(r_s)rho_s. Section 3 gives delta_s in B_2 and traceless. For each positive form H_s<=C_sW_s^4, define

  Tr delta_s H_s
    =Tr[(W_s^2 delta_s W_s^2)(W_s^(-2)H_sW_s^(-2))].         (14)

The second factor is bounded. The same definition handles W_s^4, N_s, and T_iH_0. To justify slicing, decompose Y_i=W_i^2 delta_iW_i^2 as Y_(i,+)-Y_(i,-). The positive operators X_(i,+/-)=W_i^(-2)Y_(i,+/-)W_i^(-2) have finite fourth moment. Their joint products and Gaussian outputs also have finite fourth moment by (4). Monotone spectral truncation of H_0 proves the ordinary positive slice identity for each; subtracting them proves

  Tr delta_0 H_0=(1-theta)sum_i Tr delta_i T_iH_0.             (15)

Pairing (13) with delta_i in (14), summing, and using (15) is legitimate and gives the candidate stationarity identity exactly. No positivity of rho_i+t delta_i for two-sided t, and no derivative of entropy along OU, is needed.

The entropy-production identity needs no interaction hypothesis. Here are the summability details. Faithfulness plus Tr rho_s W_s^7<infinity imply every state eigenvector lies in D(W_s^(7/2)). A ladder operator maps D(W^(5/2)) into D(W^2). Thus crossed logarithmic sums
sum_lr p_r(-log p_l)|a'_lr|^2 are bounded by C Tr rho W^5 using H<=CW^4. The uncrossed sums are bounded by the same moment from
(-log p_r)<=C<psi_r,W^4psi_r> and
<W^4>_psi <W>_psi <=<W^5>_psi.
The latter is the scalar nonnegative covariance inequality for increasing functions of W. Eigenbasis cutoffs converge in B_3 and their generator expressions converge in B_2, so all expansions agree with (14).

With h=h(r), v_lr=log(p_l/p_r)/h and K_lr=(r+1)p_r-rp_l, direct dissipator evaluation gives

  Tr delta H/h=-sum_(j,l,r) v_lr K_lr|a'_j,lr|^2.

The ordinary thermal defect's representing vector is (1-v)a', since
K/[h ell(p_l,p_r)]=(1-v)F^down(v). Hence

  ||q^down||_(F^down,*)^2
       =sum_(j,l,r)(1-v_lr)K_lr|a'_j,lr|^2.

Their difference is sum K|a'|^2=e'-nr, proving exactly

  Tr delta H/h=||q^down||_(F^down,*)^2-(e'-nr).              (16)

The creation input still uses its ordinary generator and (16). Reversing the observable/defect adjoint identifies its reversed metric dual norm with this ordinary one; it does not alter the generator or the summability proof.

7. CANCELLATION AND THE LIMIT: EXACT CONDITIONAL CLOSURE

Assume the signed interpolation/defect work supplies, at these minima and with fixed theta chosen after epsilon,

  ||q_0||^2 <= A_*^2+(epsilon/2)(A_*^2+M_*^2),              (17)
  A_*^2=sum_i w_i||q_i^down||^2,
  M_*^2=sum_i w_i|mu_i|^2.

Its constants must depend only on fixed references and auxiliary parameters, not on zeta or the minimizer. This is the precise non-analytic dependency remaining outside this report.

For the squeezed product channel, centered independence gives
e'_gamma=lambda_A e'_A+lambda_B(e'_B+n)+lambda_D n r_D.
Consequently

  e'_0-nr_0=sum_i w_i(e'_i-nr_i)
                  +theta(1-theta)|mu_gamma|^2.

The number drift is Tr delta_i N_i=-(e'_i-nr_i)-|mu_i|^2.
The fourth-moment drift is Tr delta_i W_i^4=Tr rho_i P_i(N_i), where

  P_i(N)=(r_i+1)N[(N)^4-(N+1)^4]
                 +r_i(N+n)[(N+2)^4-(N+1)^4].

Its leading coefficient is -4N^4, so sum_i Tr delta_iW_i^4<=C independent of zeta. Insert these identities and (16) into stationarity. One obtains

  0=||q_0||^2-(1+epsilon)A_*^2-epsilon M_*^2
                  -theta(1-theta)|mu_gamma|^2+zeta P.

With (17), A_*^2+M_*^2<=2C zeta/epsilon. All means and defects tend to zero. Uniform input energies give a subsequential trace-norm limit. For each fixed finite number-basis test Z, its centered F norm is uniformly bounded by a reference-dependent multiple of ||Z||, because F has fixed finite bounds and ell<=the arithmetic mean. Thus q_i(Z)->0. The centering correction tends to zero with mu_i. Both a_j^dagger Z and Z a_j^dagger are bounded finite-rank operators. Trace-norm convergence therefore gives

  (r_i+1)a_j rhohat_i=r_i rhohat_i a_j

as number-basis matrix identities for every j. Iterating the coordinate recurrence kills all off-diagonal entries; each diagonal increment has ratio r_i/(r_i+1). Trace one forces rhohat_i=tau_(r_i), including the absence of internal intermode correlations. This needs no convergence of energies or unbounded first moments.

Gaussian trace-norm continuity and the thermal characteristic function give gamma->tau_(r_0). The replaced output has the same limit. The common energy bounds imply convergence of all entropies. Therefore the entropy portion of J_zeta tends to zero, while its relative-entropy and fourth-moment penalties remain nonnegative. Its liminf is at least zero, contradicting any fixed strictly negative witness bound.

That witness reduction itself is analytic: number cutoffs of arbitrary finite-energy input states converge with bounded energies; the k=1 Gaussian bound gives bounded output energies and therefore entropy convergence. For an amplifier the zero-entropy boundary is especially benign: r_* tends to G a+(G-1)(b+1)>=G-1>0. Positive r_i approximations make their entropy-deviation terms converge to zero even if a_i=0, using g(r)/h(r)->0. Taking r_D=r_* makes r_0=r_*; the signed scalar slack permits any sufficiently small epsilon<=u/(1-u). Small u, then epsilon, then theta preserve strict negativity, followed by sufficiently small zeta. No passage in G,n,u,theta,epsilon is made in the minimizing-state limit.
