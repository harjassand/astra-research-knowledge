SIGNED METRIC AND THERMAL DEFECT TRANSFER FOR A BOSONIC AMPLIFIER
Internal reconstruction, 10 October 2026. This proves the central signed
implication with all its hypotheses, not a freestanding unconditional
entropy photon-number inequality. The general four-weight theorem is
proved in interpolation_reconstruction.txt rather than merely trusted.

1. Exact physical and analytic interfaces

There are finite n>=1 modes at independent input groups A,B. Entanglement
within either n-mode group is allowed. Gain G>1 gives retained modes
c_j=sqrt(G)a_j+sqrt(G-1)b_j^dagger. An independent n-mode thermal group
D is mixed into that output by a passive attenuator of transmission
1-u, u in (0,1). Therefore the final retained modes are
c0_j=sqrt(lambda_A)a_j+sqrt(lambda_B)b_j^dagger+sqrt(lambda_D)d_j,
lambda_A=(1-u)G, lambda_B=(1-u)(G-1), lambda_D=u,
lambda_A-lambda_B+lambda_D=1.
Let gamma be that output on the product state. Define positive references
r_A,r_B,r_D and
r0=lambda_A r_A+lambda_B(r_B+1)+lambda_D r_D.
Then exactly
r0+1=lambda_A(r_A+1)+lambda_B r_B+lambda_D(r_D+1).
In particular, r_D=r*=G r_A+(G-1)(r_B+1) gives r0=r* for every u.
For this choice both slack inequalities hold whenever
0<epsilon<=u/(1-u):
(1+epsilon)[lambda_A r_A+lambda_B(r_B+1)]<=r0,
(1+epsilon)[lambda_A(r_A+1)+lambda_B r_B]<=r0+1.

Introduce output replacement rho0=(1-theta)gamma+theta tau_r0, with
theta in (0,1), and w_i=(1-theta)lambda_i. Let rho_A,rho_B be faithful,
and assume the component Hessian estimate
 ||L_i Z||_i^2 <= (1+epsilon)/(1-theta) ||Z||_0^2.       (H)
Here T_i is the physical bounded observable slice over other product
ports, L_iZ=[T_iZ-Tr(gamma Z)I]/sqrt(lambda_i), and
 ||Z||_s^2=sum_lr h_s ell(p_s,l,p_s,r)|Z_lr|^2,
 h_s=log((r_s+1)/r_s), v_s(l,r)=log(p_s,l/p_s,r)/h_s.
All tests are centered finite eigenblock matrices with their scalar
expectation times I subtracted. They have finitely many distinct v0,
including zero, and are invariant under every function of v0 because
all diagonal entries have the same frequency zero. Consequently they
satisfy both exact domain hypotheses of the interpolation theorem.
The theorem does not require L_i to intertwine modular frequencies.

Assumption H is a result to derive at regularized amplifier minima.
It is not an assertion about arbitrary product input states. The rest
of the metric argument below is an exact consequence of H, product
independence, and the two scalar slack inequalities.

2. The four elementary comparisons

Put b_s(v)=v coth(h_s v/2)/2, continuously b_s(0)=1/h_s, and
R_s,+ = b_s+v/2, R_s,- = b_s-v/2. Their actual squared norms are the
two arithmetic forms Tr rho_s ZZ^dagger and Tr rho_s Z^dagger Z.
For either arithmetic form, the centered one-port observable subspaces
are orthogonal in the joint product state. Partial expectation is the
orthogonal projection onto each such subspace. Their projection norm
squares sum to at most the centered joint variance of the pulled-back
retained observable, irrespective of whether the unitary is passive.
The mixture variance in rho0 is at least (1-theta) times the variance
in gamma. Since w_i/lambda_i=1-theta, both R+ and R- compare:
 sum_i w_i ||L_iZ||_R_i,+^2 <= ||Z||_R_0,+^2,
 sum_i w_i ||L_iZ||_R_i,-^2 <= ||Z||_R_0,-^2.

By H and the scalar slack, the other two comparisons are
 w_A r_A||L_AZ||_A^2+w_B(r_B+1)||L_BZ||_B^2<=r0||Z||_0^2,
 w_A(r_A+1)||L_AZ||_A^2+w_B r_B||L_BZ||_B^2<=(r0+1)||Z||_0^2.
Replacing r_B+1 by r_B in both families is not justified; Section 6
below provides an exact scalar falsifier to keeping ordinary metrics.

3. Interpolation with the correct orientation

At each output and input-A coordinate choose
m_s=1/h_s, x_s=h_s v_s/2, y_s=-h_s/2.
At input B choose the same m_B,x_B but y_B=+h_B/2. The four weights
of the reconstructed theorem are exactly R+,R-, followed by the two
mixed constant families just proved. Thus
 w_A||L_AZ||_F_A,down^2+w_B||L_BZ||_F_B,up^2
      <=||Z||_F_0,down^2,                                      (M)
where
F_down(v)=(1/h) f(hv/2)f(h/2)/f(h(v-1)/2),
F_up(v)=(1/h) f(hv/2)f(h/2)/f(h(v+1)/2)=F_down(-v).
Both removable values v=1 and v=-1 are provided by the first formulas.
Their exact logarithmic-mean identities are
 ell(p_l,p_r)F_down(v)=ell(r p_l,(r+1)p_r),
 ell(p_l,p_r)F_up(v)=ell((r+1)p_l,r p_r).
These follow by taking the logarithmic differences h(v-1) and h(v+1),
respectively. They also supply uniform explicit bounds, not just unknown
constants: the integral ell(a,b)=integral_0^1 a^t b^(1-t)dt gives
 r<=F_down(v)<=r+1, r<=F_up(v)<=r+1 for every real v.       (B)
Hence M extends continuously to the completed centered spaces. On
bounded observables the extension is the actual slice: spectral
compressions converge strongly with adjoints, hence in both arithmetic
norms by dominated convergence; ell<=arithmetic mean gives BKM norm
convergence, and the completely positive unital slice Schwarz bound
gives convergence to the genuine sliced observable. The replacement
rho0>=(1-theta)gamma converts its output arithmetic norm to that in
gamma. These are actual CP slices; no transposition map is substituted.

4. Dual norms and their exact orientation symmetry

For each port mode write d'=d-Tr(rho d)I. On bounded observables define
 q_down(Z)=(r+1)Tr(rho d'^dagger Z)-r Tr(rho Z d'^dagger),
 q_up(Z)=r Tr(rho d' Z)-(r+1)Tr(rho Z d').
All weak products are trace class when the state has finite energy,
because d rho=(d rho^1/2)rho^1/2 and the two factors are Hilbert-Schmidt.
Both functionals vanish on I. Adjoint and cyclic trace give exactly
 q_up(Z)=-conj(q_down(Z^dagger)).
Since ell(p_l,p_r) is symmetric and v(l,r)=-v(r,l), the adjoint is an
antiunitary from the F_up space to the F_down space. It preserves the
centered subspace. Therefore
 ||q_up||_F_up,*=||q_down||_F_down,*                          (D)
including the case where both norms are infinite. At the regularized
minima their finiteness is supplied by the ordinary port entropy
production argument; there is no new reversed-generator identity.

For clarity, the two representing vectors at a faithful regular state
are (1-v)d' in the down metric and -(1+v)d'^dagger in the up metric.
Their squared dual norms are
sum_lr (1-v)[(r+1)p_r-rp_l]|d'_lr|^2,
sum_lr (1+v)[(r+1)p_l-rp_r]|(d'^dagger)_lr|^2.
Index interchange makes them equal term by term. This independently
checks both the sign and the tilted logarithmic-mean choice.

5. Weak signed convolution and the explicit defect estimate

The centered output mean is
mu_gamma=sqrt(lambda_A)mu_A+sqrt(lambda_B)conj(mu_B).
Define raw q_gamma,down at reference r0. Then for every bounded output
observable Z and every mode,
 q_gamma,down(Z)=sqrt(lambda_A)q_A,down(T_AZ)
                 -sqrt(lambda_B)q_B,up(T_BZ).                 (C)
To verify, include D temporarily, whose ordinary thermal defect is
zero. Subtract the candidate RHS from the raw down defect. Centering
constants agree exactly by the displayed mean relation. What remains is
 sqrt(lambda_A)(r0-r_A)Tr Omega[a^dagger,Ztilde]
 +sqrt(lambda_B)(r0+r_B+1)Tr Omega[b,Ztilde]
 +sqrt(lambda_D)(r0-r_D)Tr Omega[d^dagger,Ztilde].
The retained components of a^dagger,b,d^dagger are respectively
sqrt(lambda_A)c0^dagger, -sqrt(lambda_B)c0^dagger,
sqrt(lambda_D)c0^dagger, by canonical commutators or the inverse
Bogoliubov transform. The other components act on discarded modes.
Thus the coefficient of the retained weak commutator is
 lambda_A(r0-r_A)-lambda_B(r0+r_B+1)+lambda_D(r0-r_D)
 =r0(lambda_A-lambda_B+lambda_D)
     -lambda_A r_A-lambda_B(r_B+1)-lambda_D r_D=0.

This is a weak-trace statement, without assigning [a^dagger,Ztilde]
an operator domain. The Gaussian unitary preserves the finite-energy
form domain: a finite Bogoliubov linear combination of creation and
annihilation operators has squared norm at most C times the total
number form. This bound follows by finite sums of Cauchy-Schwarz.
It also holds for the inverse transformation. Thus both the input and
joint output have finite energy and all the indicated operator-state
products are trace class. Cut off each discarded annihilation/creation
operator in its own number basis; each bounded cutoff commutes with
Z tensor I, so its weak commutator trace is zero by cyclicity. The cutoff
products converge in trace norm: factor through rho^1/2 and use the
finite-energy Hilbert-Schmidt tail. This proves C on all bounded Z.

After replacing the output by its thermal mixture, direct centering gives
 q0,down=sum_i s_i w_i q_i,oriented o L_i
           +theta(1-theta)conj(mu_gamma)Tr[(gamma-tau_r0) . ],
s_A=1,s_B=-1. Let
A^2=w_A||q_A,down||_*^2+w_B||q_B,down||_*^2,
M^2=w_A|mu_A|^2+w_B|mu_B|^2.
M and D show that the dual norm of the first term, summed over the n
modes, is at most A. The creation sign disappears only at this final
norm step, after using the correctly oriented metric.

For J(Z)=sqrt(theta(1-theta))Tr[(gamma-tau_r0)Z], mixture variance
gives |J(Z)|^2<=||Z||_R_0,+^2 and |J(Z)|^2<=||Z||_R_0,-^2. The proved
two-weight logarithmic-mean operation, with target scalar weights both
1, and ell(R_0,+,R_0,-)=1/h0, gives
 |J(Z)|^2<=||Z||_0^2/h0<=||Z||_F_0,down^2/(h0 r0).
Also (1-theta)|mu_gamma|^2<=2M^2 by the two-term squared-norm bound.
Therefore
 ||q0,down||_*<=A+sqrt(2theta/(h0 r0))M.
Young's inequality now gives the uniform estimate
 ||q0,down||_*^2<=A^2+(epsilon/2)(A^2+M^2)                   (E)
whenever
 0<theta<=epsilon^2 h0 r0/[4(epsilon+2)].
The constants depend only on fixed references and epsilon, and are
independent of the minimizing state and fourth-moment regularizer.
This is precisely the defect estimate required in the stationarity
cancellation, with an explicit sufficient mixture parameter.

6. An exact falsifier to omitting creation orientation

Take scalar source and two scalar target coordinate spaces, L_A=L_B=I,
r0=1, r_A=r_B=1/10, v0=-5, v_A=v_B=-2, w_A=7/4, w_B=3/4.
The source R+,R- are 5/31,160/31. Each target R+,R- is 1/60,121/60;
their weighted sums are 1/24 and121/24, respectively, both smaller.
The signed constant sums are exactly 1 and2. Hence all four signed
hypotheses are satisfied. The metrics are
 F0_down=105/62,
 FA_down=FB_down=133/180,
 FB_up=11/60.
The correctly oriented weighted target is 103/72<=105/62. Keeping
the down metric at B gives 133/72>105/62, exceeding it by343/2232.
These are exact rational identities, not approximate numerical evidence.
The parameters even obey the raw amplifier thermal balance with G=7/4:
(7/4)(1/10)+(3/4)(11/10)=1. This falsifies the naive inference at the
form-comparison level; it is not a counterexample to the amplifier EPnI.
