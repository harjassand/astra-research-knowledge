Adaptive investigator 18 — exact falsification checks for the signed Bogoliubov candidate
Date: 2026-10-10
Scope: candidate §§2–6 only; no proof of the global variational closure is claimed.

Finding

I found no sign or scalar-normalization counterexample in the displayed identities in §§2–6. Independent checks support the candidate's signed scalar balances, normal/reversed thermal defects, reversed-port minus sign, OU noise normalization, and mixture centering formulas. I did find a sharp adjacent convention trap: the defect conjugation identity requires the operator adjoint Z†. Replacing it by plain Fock transpose Zᵀ reverses the result on a complex off-diagonal observable. This does not refute the candidate, which states Z†, but it is a concrete falsifier for a common transcription of the identity.

Exact scalar thermal balance

Set G=3/2, r_A=1/3, r_B=2/5. Then K=G−1=1/2 and

  r*=G r_A+K(r_B+1)=6/5,
  r*+1=11/5=G(r_A+1)+K r_B.

The tempting passive companion G(r_A+1)+K(r_B+1) equals 27/10, not 11/5. Thus the B-port occupation must be exchanged exactly as the candidate says. With u=1/4 and r_D=r*, the attenuated weights are λ_A=9/8, λ_B=3/8, λ_D=1/4. Direct fraction arithmetic gives λ_A−λ_B+λ_D=1, r_0=6/5=r*, and

  r_0+1=λ_A(r_A+1)+λ_B r_B+λ_D(r_D+1).

The two ε=0 scalar slacks are exactly 3/10=u r_D and 11/20=u(r_D+1). ε=1/4 satisfies both strict inequalities. The OU noise coefficient also checks exactly:

  λ_A(r_A+1/2)+λ_B(r_B+1/2)+λ_D(r_D+1/2)=17/10=r_0+1/2.

The first two thermal identities are algebraically equivalent; the companion uses G−(G−1)=1. More generally, after setting r_D=r*, r_0=r* for every u, and the slacks are u r* and u(r*+1). The exact-fraction test is in work/reports/adaptive18_signed_checks.py.

Number-basis defect check and adjoint trap

For τ_r with q=r/(r+1), its number probabilities are p_k=(1−q)q^k. For the matrix unit Z=|u><v|, the only potentially nonzero normal traces are

  Tr τ_r a†Z = 1_{v=u+1} sqrt(v) p_v,
  Tr τ_r Za† = 1_{v=u+1} sqrt(u+1) p_u.

Since p_{u+1}=q p_u and (r+1)q=r, q_r↓(Z)=0. Likewise,

  Tr τ_r aZ = 1_{u=v+1} sqrt(u) p_v,
  Tr τ_r Za = 1_{u=v+1} sqrt(u) p_u,

so q_r↑(Z)=0 because r=(r+1)q. The script checks both exact symbolic cancellations for all 81 matrix units with 0≤u,v≤8. These are exact infinite-oscillator traces: a finite-support matrix unit makes each trace a single number-basis term, so no truncated thermal state is being mistaken for τ_r. For a displaced thermal state, conjugation by its displacement sends d=a−μ back to a, so the same vanishing holds after centering.

For any density matrix ρ, centered d=a−Trρa, and bounded Z, trace adjunction and cyclicity give

  −conj(q_r↓(Z†))=r Trρ dZ−(r+1)Trρ Zd=q_r↑(Z).

This derives the candidate's adjoint identity without a thermal assumption. To falsify a transpose substitution exactly, take |ψ>=(|0>+|1>)/sqrt(2), ρ=|ψ><ψ|, μ=1/2, r=1, and Z=i|0><1|. On the first three Fock levels the traces give

  q_1↑(Z)=i/4,
  −conj(q_1↓(Z†))=i/4,
  −conj(q_1↓(Zᵀ))=−i/4.

Thus the adjoint is load-bearing. For the metric claim, f is even, so direct substitution gives F_r↑(v)=F_r↓(−v). With the stated BKM weight symmetric under swapping number indices, reindexing the quadratic form yields ||Z||²_{F↑}=||Z†||²_{F↓}. The norm identity is consistent; norms alone cannot detect transpose versus adjoint because both have the same entrywise magnitudes after index swap.

Raw convolution sign

Let K=G−1, pull the bounded output observable back to the two inputs as Z̃, and center both input modes. Expanding the candidate's right-hand side with the definitions of q↓ and q↑, the difference between the output defect and that right-hand side reduces to

  sqrt(G)(r*−r_A) <[a†,Z̃]>
  +sqrt(K)(r*+r_B+1) <[b,Z̃]>.

For an output observable, the pulled-back commutators are sqrt(G)[c†,Z] and −sqrt(K)[c†,Z]. The total coefficient is

  G(r*−r_A)−K(r*+r_B+1)
  =r*−G r_A−K(r_B+1)=0.

This independently confirms the minus sign on q_B↑. If that sign is changed to plus, the two B-order coefficients are no longer opposite: their sum is 2, so they do not form the commutator that the canonical relation cancels. The symbolic check is included in the script. The fixed thermal D port contributes zero because its centered defect vanishes.

Mixture and energy checks

The output replacement formulas in §6 also pass an algebraic check. For a state with mean μ and reference r, write q_ρ(Z)=B_ρ(Z)−conj(μ)TrρZ, where B_ρ is the uncentered two-term trace expression. For ρ_0=(1−θ)γ+θτ_{r_0}, use q_{τ}(Z)=0 and μ_0=(1−θ)μ_γ; linearity gives exactly

  q_0(Z)=(1−θ)q_γ(Z)+θ(1−θ)conj(μ_γ)Tr[(γ−τ_{r_0})Z].

Similarly, Trρ_0N=(1−θ)TrγN+θ n r_0 and |μ_0|²=(1−θ)²|μ_γ|², which yields the stated centered-energy correction θ(1−θ)|μ_γ|². The bound (1−θ)|μ_γ|²≤2Σ_i w_i|μ_i|² follows directly from |x+y|²≤2(|x|²+|y|²) with x=√λ_A μ_A and y=√λ_B conj(μ_B). No independence beyond the channel's product input is hidden in this scalar inequality; independence is needed in the raw centered-energy expansion to eliminate cross covariances.

Status and decisive boundary

Proved here: the displayed scalar equalities and slacks at an exact rational point; their general simplification; the number-basis thermal defect cancellations; the general adjoint-conjugation identity; the raw convolution sign cancellation; and the output-mixture algebra. These are algebraic checks of §§2–6 only. No generic random-input testing was used.

Counterexample found: a version of the reversed defect identity with Zᵀ in place of Z† fails by an exact sign on the stated complex matrix unit. No counterexample found to the candidate's actual Z† formula or its displayed scalar balances.

Still unverified: the full four-weight interpolation theorem's application, the weighted trace topology for OU covariance, and every compactness/Gibbs/Hessian step in §§7 and later. These checks do not establish the amplifier EPnI or signed multiport theorem. They only remove several local sign and normalization risks from §§2–6.

Next proof gate: independently certify the operator-level convolution identity for finite-number-cutoff approximants, with explicit convergence in the weighted topology required by the entropy-production pairing; then separately audit the signed interpolation hypotheses against the exact source theorem. The transpose trap must remain explicit in any implementation: the defect uses Z†, while a Fock transpose is a distinct operation on complex observables.

Context source: De Palma, Mari, and Giovannetti, “A generalization of the Entropy Power Inequality to Bosonic Quantum Systems” (2014), https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.0404. The present report does not audit that paper's theorem or the candidate's full variational closure.
