Target and input interface

For every finite n, independent finite-energy n-mode states rho_A,rho_B,
possibly internally entangled within either group, and G>1, let
c_j=sqrt(G)a_j+sqrt(G-1)b_j^*. Set g(x)=(x+1)log(x+1)-xlog(x),
N(rho)=g^{-1}(S(rho)/n). The target is

  N(C) >= G N(A)+(G-1)(N(B)+1).                       [AEPnI]

The mathematical input is a physical amplifier and supplied states or their
entropies/energy bounds. No efficient entropy acquisition, tomography, shared
reference theorem, or correlated-input theorem is claimed. Noise tomography
could dominate the cost of a practical capacity certificate.

Strongest sharp consequence: exact minimum total correlation

Write a=N(A), b=N(B). The complementary physical output is
d_j=sqrt(G)b_j+sqrt(G-1)a_j^*. Apply AEPnI with the groups exchanged:

  S(C)>=n g(Ga+(G-1)(b+1)),
  S(D)>=n g(Gb+(G-1)(a+1)).

The joint output is a unitary image of rho_A tensor rho_B, so its entropy
is n[g(a)+g(b)]. Consequently the mutual information obeys the sharp bound

  I(C:D) >= n{g(Ga+(G-1)(b+1))+g(Gb+(G-1)(a+1))-g(a)-g(b)}.   [1]

Independent product thermal inputs attain BOTH marginal bounds and hence
[1]. Thus AEPnI solves the global minimization of total correlation created
by two-mode squeezing at prescribed input entropies, for every block length.
This goes beyond optimizing one signal while holding a thermal ancilla fixed.
It is mutual information; for mixed inputs it need not be entanglement.

For arbitrary independent pure group inputs, a=b=0 and the output is pure.
The exact minimum entropy of entanglement across C:D is therefore

  inf_{pure rho_A,rho_B} E(C:D)=n g(G-1).                         [2]

At G=2 this is 2 bits per pair, while amplifier qEPI only yields log(3),
about 1.585 bits. Vacuum/coherent products attain [2]; suitably matched
locally squeezed Gaussian inputs can also attain it, so equality is not
claimed unique. The 2021 Goldberg–Heshami paper establishes a weak-squeezing
linear-entropy extremization, not this every-gain von Neumann statement.
The general pure-input lower bound remains conditional on AEPnI here.

Regularized communication consequence for arbitrary non-Gaussian noise

Let a memoryless amplifier environment be an arbitrary finite-energy one-mode
state sigma, independent of every codeword, with centered energy v_B and
b=g^{-1}(S(sigma)). A fixed output displacement removes its mean. For a
block of k uses the environment is sigma tensor k, so AEPnI applies even to
codewords entangled across all k uses. Every output codeword has entropy at
least k g((G-1)(b+1)), since its input entropy is nonnegative. For average
signal energy <=k N_S, maximum entropy at fixed centered oscillator energy
gives average output entropy <=k g(G N_S+(G-1)(v_B+1)). Therefore

  C_regularized <= g(G N_S+(G-1)(v_B+1))-g((G-1)(b+1)).           [3]

This is in nats/use, holds without isotropic noise covariance, and needs no
coding-additivity assumption. With thermal noise v_B=b it reduces to the
known Gaussian formula. For fixed arbitrary sigma it is generally an upper
bound: AEPnI does NOT determine that channel's actual minimum-output state
or exact capacity. Its [3] is sharper than the analogous qEPI bound obtained
by replacing the subtracted term with log(G+(G-1)exp(S(sigma))).

Primary frontier and novelty boundary

De Palma, Mari, Giovannetti, “A generalization of the Entropy Power Inequality
to Bosonic Quantum Systems” (Nature Photonics 2014), arXiv:1402.0404v2,
https://arxiv.org/html/1402.0404 — amplifier qEPI is Eq. (7); the exact target
above is explicitly conjectured in Eq. (36) of v2 (candidate's Eq. (32)
reference uses a different numbering). This is an established target.

De Palma, Trevisan, Giovannetti, “Gaussian States Minimize the Output Entropy
of One-Mode Quantum Gaussian Channels” (2017),
https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.118.160503 — the
fixed Gaussian-channel one-mode entropy optimizer is already known.

Van Herstraeten, Guha, Cerf, “Classical capacity of quantum non-Gaussian
attenuator and amplifier channels,” arXiv:2312.15623v2, revised Jan. 2026,
https://arxiv.org/html/2312.15623v2 — their capacity upper bound retains an
unknown fixed-environment minimum output entropy; footnote 3 explicitly
restricts that analysis to single-shot capacity. [3] controls all blocks but
does not solve the optimization they identify. No absence-of-prior-art
claim is proved by these bounded literature checks.

Goldberg, Heshami, “How squeezed states both maximize and minimize the same
notion of quantumness” (2021), https://arxiv.org/html/2106.03862 — Section
III.2 is perturbative linear entropy and already discusses matched squeezed
minimizers. It prevents a naive claim of new unique coherent optimality.

amplifier_search.py uses U=exp[s(a^*b^*-ab)], tanh(s)=sqrt((G-1)/G).
For input |m,n> and output |p,q> with p-q=m-n, writing d=p-m,

  <p,q|U|m,n> = sqrt(m!n!p!q!) sum_l
     (-1)^l t^(d+2l) sech(s)^(m+n-2l+1)
       /[l!(d+l)!(m-l)!(n-l)!],
  max(0,-d)<=l<=min(m,n).

This finite sum is a coefficient of the INFINITE physical unitary, not the
exponential of a truncated creation operator. Exact input support was
|0>,...,|3>. The local retained block p,p'<L is obtained by tracing only
q<=L+2: photon-number difference conservation makes that block exact.
Let its trace be 1-q_tail, its normalized entropy S_L, and omitted mean
energy E_tail from the exact full energy identity. Local pinching yields

  (1-q_tail)S_L <= S(C)
     <= h_2(q_tail)+(1-q_tail)S_L+q_tail g(E_tail/q_tail).

The implementation adds explicit numerical allowances, but uses ordinary
floating arithmetic, so these are analytic cutoff bounds with numerical
diagnostics, not certified interval-arithmetic proofs.

Stationarity closure reconstructed

The detailed derivation is in stationarity_reconstruction.txt. For integer
k, the sum of squared annihilator-word norms of length <=k equals a positive
polynomial comparable to (1+N)^k. Bogoliubov substitution expands these
words into creation/annihilation words of the same length, giving the needed
weighted graph bound. On arbitrary joint finite-support operators, the Weyl
OU-generator formula establishes active-channel covariance; the diffusion
coefficient is sum_s lambda_s(r_s+1/2)=r_0+1/2. Extending cutoffs uses k=6
for states and k=4 for generator expressions, closing the sandwich W^2
trace-norm passage. Positive/negative decomposition then gives logarithmic
slicing. This removes the passive proof's reliance on number preservation.

Writing K_lr=(r+1)p_r-r p_l and v_lr=log(p_l/p_r)/h gives
sigma=-sum v K|a'_lr|^2, while the ordinary defect dual norm is
sum(1-v)K|a'_lr|^2. Their difference is e'-nr by the physical CCR. Reversing
port B's METRIC preserves its dual norm; its actual generator remains the
ordinary thermal relaxation. With uncentered number drift the full identity
is exactly

  0=||q_0||^2-(1+epsilon)A_*^2-epsilon M_*^2
       -theta(1-theta)|mu_gamma|^2+zeta P.

The fourth-moment drift polynomial has leading term -4N^4, so P<=C.
The assumed signed defect estimate forces A_*^2+M_*^2<=2C zeta/epsilon.
The source-05 gate thus reconstructs under its preceding hypotheses; this
