H_a rho=-1/2([a,[a^dagger,rho]]+[a^dagger,[a,rho]]),
F_a rho=a rho a^dagger-a^dagger rho a+rho,

The sum of F_a over the complete set of canonical modes is invariant under a Bogoliubov transformation. For the two-mode squeeze this is verified by substituting a=sqrt(G)c-sqrt(G-1)e^dagger and b=sqrt(G)e-sqrt(G-1)c^dagger: all cross terms cancel, and the retained and discarded coefficients each equal G-(G-1)=1.
