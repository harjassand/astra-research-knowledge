Target and interface

rho_A,rho_B, and G>1, the target is
 N(output)>=G N(rho_A)+(G-1)(N(rho_B)+1),
 N(rho)=g^-1(S(rho)/n), g(t)=(t+1)log(t+1)-t log t.
The retained annihilators are c_j=sqrt(G)a_j+sqrt(G-1)b_j^dagger.
Internal entanglement across each input's modes is allowed. There is no
shared-reference or correlated-input interface in this target.

The signed mechanism

For r_s>0 set h_s=log((r_s+1)/r_s), v_s=log(p_l/p_r)/h_s. Use
m_s=1/h_s, x_s=h_s v_s/2, y0=yA=-h_s/2, yB=+h_B/2. The elementary
arithmetic weights are unchanged. The two constant families become
(r0,rA,rB+1) and (r0+1,rA+1,rB), exactly matching
r0=lambda_A rA+lambda_B(rB+1)+lambda_D rD,
r0+1=lambda_A(rA+1)+lambda_B rB+lambda_D(rD+1).
Here lambda_A=(1-u)G, lambda_B=(1-u)(G-1), lambda_D=u. Choosing
rD=G rA+(G-1)(rB+1) makes both scalar slacks valid for any
0<epsilon<=u/(1-u).

The resulting creation metric is F_up(v)=F_down(-v), with exact tilted
means
ell(p_l,p_r)F_down=ell(rp_l,(r+1)p_r),
ell(p_l,p_r)F_up=ell((r+1)p_l,rp_r).
The logarithmic-mean integral immediately proves uniform bounds
r<=F_down,F_up<=r+1. Define centered thermal defects
q_down(Z)=(r+1)Tr rho d'^dagger Z-r Tr rho Z d'^dagger,
q_up(Z)=r Tr rho d' Z-(r+1)Tr rho Z d'.
Then q_up(Z)=-conj(q_down(Z^dagger)), while adjoint is an antiunitary
between the up and down metric spaces. Thus the reversed defect has
exactly the ordinary thermal-relaxation dual norm, without inventing a
new generator or using a non-CP transpose channel.
