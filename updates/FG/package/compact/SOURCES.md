# Primary-source and novelty ledger

Accessed during this investigation on 14 October 2026 (Brisbane). These sources establish lineage and comparisons, not correctness or novelty of the new checkpoint. No external manuscript PDF was analyzed; HTML, abstracts, and an official repository README were used. No third-party full text is redistributed in this packet.

1. Y. S. Elmatad and R. L. Jack, *Space-time phase transitions in the East model with a softened kinetic constraint*, arXiv:1210.1614 (2012). Full HTML inspected. https://arxiv.org/html/1210.1614v1 . Independent benchmark-model lineage; our boundary/time parameterization is stated explicitly.
2. V. Kazeev et al., *Direct Solution of the Chemical Master Equation Using Quantized Tensor Trains*, PLOS Computational Biology (2014), e1003359. Primary publisher text inspected. https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003359 . Existing non-enumerative master-equation computation.
3. I. G. Ion et al., *Tensor-train approximation of the chemical master equation and parameter inference*, arXiv:2106.15188. HTML inspected. https://arxiv.org/html/2106.15188v2 . Existing tensor-state compression and inference.
4. E. Gillman, D. C. Rose and J. P. Garrahan, *Combining Reinforcement Learning and Tensor Networks, with an Application to Dynamical Large Deviations*, arXiv:2209.14089v2 (2024 version). Full HTML inspected. https://arxiv.org/html/2209.14089v2 . ACTeN/East rare-trajectory work; title and 5 April 2024 revision checked against the arXiv abstract.
5. Y. Feng, M. K. Ng, K. Tang and Z. Zhang, *Functional tensor train neural network for solving high-dimensional PDEs*, arXiv:2510.13386v1, 15 October 2025. Full HTML inspected. https://arxiv.org/html/2510.13386v1 . Functional tensor structure and one-dimensional contraction of PDE losses are prior art.
6. N. Mueller, Y. Zhao, S. Badia and T. Cui, *A tensor-train reduced basis solver for parameterized partial differential equations on Cartesian grids*, arXiv:2412.14460v2, revised 5 May 2025. Abstract inspected. https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14460 . Tensor reduced-basis a posteriori estimates; not exhaustively compared theorem by theorem.
7. V. Fanaskov, A. Rudikov and I. Oseledets, *Neural functional a posteriori error estimates*, arXiv:2402.05585v1 (2024). Full HTML and error-bound discussion inspected. https://arxiv.org/html/2402.05585v1 . Established functional error-majorant framework and learned auxiliary fields.
8. *MarkovMPS*, public repository by monalisasroy. Official README read through the GitHub connector; README blob SHA 85f67298d9de166bbe420e28d088eb787beaf78b. https://github.com/monalisasroy/MarkovMPS . Describes tensor-train first-passage solves, source observables, rank adaptation and residual diagnostics. Code was not installed, audited or benchmarked; release priority was not established.
9. W. H. Ryu et al., *Reducing Weighted Ensemble Variance With Optimal Trajectory Management*, arXiv:2504.21663v3, 9 February 2026. Abstract and full HTML inspected. https://arxiv.org/html/2504.21663v3 . Strong trajectory-allocation comparator; not rerun.
10. T. D. Swinburne and D. Perez, *Self-optimized construction of transition rate matrices from accelerated atomistic simulations with Bayesian uncertainty quantification*, Physical Review Materials 2, 053802 (2018), arXiv:1803.05273. Primary abstracts inspected. https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.05273 ; https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevMaterials.2.053802 . TAMMBER already performs uncertainty-directed missing-transition exploration; not rerun.
11. S. M. Lichtinger and R. Covino, *Accelerated descriptor-free path sampling for protein-ligand binding kinetics*, arXiv:2607.15101, 16 July 2026. Abstract inspected. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15101 . AIMMD/learned-committor comparator; reported ligand results are not independently replicated here.
12. S. A. Trygubenko and D. J. Wales, *Graph Transformation Method for Calculating Waiting Times in Markov Chains*, arXiv:cond-mat/0603830 (2006). Abstract checked again during final source verification. https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0603830 . Finite-network first-passage calculations without simulating waiting times are established.
13. A. Debnath, R. Chakrabarti and K. L. Sebastian, *Rate processes with dynamical disorder: A direct variational approach*, J. Chem. Phys. 124, 204111 (2006). Publisher abstract inspected; full paywalled paper not analyzed. https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2200695 . Prior variational upper/lower survival bounds and dynamical-disorder treatment.
14. A. Kells, Z. E. Mihalka, A. Annibale and E. Rosta, *Mean first passage times in variational coarse graining using Markov state models*, J. Chem. Phys. 150, 134107 (2019). PubMed/primary abstract inspected. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30954057/ ; https://doi.org/10.1063/1.5083924 . Prior kinetic coarse-graining and variational relationships.

Additional related sources surfaced include conditional-expectation coarse-graining and transient-current first-passage bounds. Their existence is a reason not to make broad novelty claims; a complete priority review remains unfinished. The conditional-envelope theorem uses standard positive resolvents/variational principles and should be presented as a candidate construction pending that review.
