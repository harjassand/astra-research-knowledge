# Scoped research ledger

## Established by explicit proof and exact checks

1. Sharp quasiconvexity iff rank-one convexity iff |l|<=kappa for the stated anisotropic one-parameter quartic family.
2. Explicit global compatible heat identity with an input-anisotropic cubic exact-form correction.
3. Exact rank-four endpoint Gram matrix: positive four-by-four block and zero Schur complement in Q[kappa].
4. Exact acoustic null witness proving failure of rank-one convexity for every parameter beyond the endpoint.
5. Quantitative interior quasiconvexity, non-polyconvexity for 11/4<|l|<=kappa, and a small explicit nonsymmetric perturbation neighborhood.
6. A narrowly scoped obstruction to obtaining the retained Fstar by positive sums of invertible input-coordinate transforms of the known isotropic boundary atoms, plus determinant squares. This is auxiliary to the actual positive theorem.

## Discovery computations, not theorem premises

- A full 24-by-24 compatible-heat SOS search first found a strict certificate for Fstar.
- Symmetry reduction led to a scalar six-by-six representation; an exact rational interior certificate was acquired.
- Larger quarter-turn cone experiments compared scalar and tensor corrections. Some floating support optimizations differed. No exact scalar-versus-tensor separation was proved.
- Proposed universal positive linear transfers from acoustic blocks did not converge to a feasible identity in the tested formulations. This is not a proof of impossibility.
- A sharp-slice tensor search located the algebraic endpoint. Acoustic equality cases supplied exact kernel constraints. Rational linear algebra over a degree-six field then constructed an endpoint certificate.
- Further exact reduction showed that the tensor terms were unnecessary for this sharp slice: setting both tensor weights to zero leaves a positive scalar certificate. The final proof uses that simpler result.
- A compact five-term geometric one-form was reconstructed from the coordinate primitive and checked by exact exterior differentiation.

## Preserved unresolved mechanism

Use the complete acoustic equality geometry of a general left-SO(2) quartic to determine compatible-correction Gram faces. It remains unproved that such faces always contain a positive correction. Other heat metrics or tensor components may be required; no universal existence theorem is claimed.

## Mathematically distinct attack

A compatible resonant Fourier potential, evaluated with exact moments, can still test a separately certified integrand outside the proved family. An arbitrary measure with correct mean matrix and determinant is not a substitute. No compatible counterexample was found or asserted in this continuation.
