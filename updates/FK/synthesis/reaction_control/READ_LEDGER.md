Output is `results/geometric_witness.json`. This validates the stated algebra and model example only. It does not establish a measured noise law, physical reachability of the event centers, reaction kinetics, retained yield, experimental reproducibility, novelty or material performance. No external paper code was executed, no material parameters were inferred, and no physical experiment or calibrated simulation was run.
