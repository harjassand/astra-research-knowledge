EXACT SECOND-MOMENT ENCLOSURE AND FINITE-SAMPLE TEST
2026-10-10. Internally derived; no external certification or practical experiment.

This supplements work/discrete_critic/RESULT.md at epsilon=1/2. The statistic W,
its universal reversible-null mean E W>=0, exact target mean, and certified
range are defined there. The seed, least-squares marker reconstruction, field,
and all 7,776 words are evaluated with fractions.Fraction.

Let A_ij=ceil(2^40 P_ij), D=2^40. Because P is entrywise nonnegative,
A/D >= P entrywise. For each observed five-letter word w, sum hidden paths
using the integer matrices A and masks for each letter, with initial vector
1 on the matching hidden states. Its integer total n_w divided by 10 D^4 is
at least the true stationary word probability. The initial distribution is
exactly uniform on ten hidden states. No cancellation occurs in this bound.
For exact rational W(w), ceil(W(w)^2) also majorizes its squared value. Hence

 E_target W^2 <= sum_w n_w ceil(W(w)^2)/(10 D^4)
              < 27,034,341,276,573 = V.

The final '<' can harmlessly be replaced by '<='; the verifier records the
exact upper fraction and its ceiling. This is a target moment bound; no such
bound is assumed for arbitrary null models.

The statistic is in [-29,000,000,81,000,000], with range width R=110,000,000.
Its target mean is below -Delta, Delta=29/20,000. For n iid stationary windows
from one fixed law, calculate their sample mean Wbar and unbiased variance s^2.
Set l=log(80), and reject the equilibrium null if

 Wbar + sqrt(2 s^2 l/n) + 7 R l/[3(n-1)] < 0.

Maurer-Pontil (2009), Theorem 4, gives type-I error at most .025 without knowing
the null variance. For target power, Bernstein's bounded-variable inequality
and Maurer-Pontil Theorem 10 each fail with probability at most .0125 and give

 Wbar - E W <= sqrt(2 V l/n) + R l/(3n),
 s <= sqrt(V) + R sqrt(2l/(n-1)).

Since l<5 and n>=2, the sum of the mean upper error and rejection radius is
at most 2 sqrt(10V/n)+40R/n. Therefore the explicitly chosen integer

 n > max(160 V/Delta^2, 80 R/Delta, 2)

makes that sum strictly less than Delta and ensures target power at least .975.
The exact verifier gives

 n = 2,057,310,156,600,085,612,368 stationary windows.

Each window needs five observed letters, so this bound charges more than 10^22
letters, plus independent stationary preparation. It is still impractical.
No actual experiment with this many samples was performed. A consecutive
single trajectory does not provide these iid windows without an additional
acquired dependence bound. The changed discrete-time target is not a sample
of the original CTMC. The proof does not certify physical parity, unknown
measurement memory, novelty, or external correctness.
