PRIMARY LITERATURE AUDIT — HIDDEN NONEQUILIBRIUM
Date: 2026-10-10, Australia/Brisbane. Bounded search; not priority certification.

Main finding
Exact time reversibility of the complete observed path law does not prevent an entropy-production lower bound within a stipulated all-even Markov model class. This principle is established prior work. New claims must concern the exact witness, generality, rigor, observation resources, or finite-sample implementation.

1. Skinner and Dunkel, PNAS 118 e2024300118 (2021)
https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2024300118
Primary full text plus supplement: https://arxiv.org/html/2011.08765v2
Their sigma_2 method uses rates of two successive observable jumps and minimizes microscopic entropy subject to those statistics. It is not confined to a known hidden-state count: the selected local optimization is reduced to at most six internal states per neighbor pair. Figure 2E and supplement XI exhibit exactly time-symmetric complete observed trajectories with a positive lower bound. Thus “detection despite zero observed arrow of time” and “selected statistics with no hidden-state bound” are not new. The bound requires the relevant jump statistics; it is not automatically the optimal bound for an entire observed path law. For a binary alternating renewal process, jump-label sequences alone contain little information, so waiting durations matter.

2. Skinner and Dunkel, PRL 127 198101 (2021)
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.127.198101
https://arxiv.org/html/2105.08681v3
PDF and extracted text saved locally as skinner_waiting.pdf/.txt.
The supplement derives the positive exponential-mixture restriction for equilibrium dwell distributions, including complete monotonicity and CV^2 >= 1. The paper formulates optimization over hidden mechanisms matching full dwell distributions or their moments. It gives a canonical reduction and numerical variance–dissipation frontier, with increasing state-count computations and extrapolation; numerical convergence is not by itself a proof of the exact unrestricted frontier. A new complete-monotonicity or dwell-variance detector must acknowledge this predecessor. It assumes stationary finite CTMC dynamics and the ordinary entropy-production formula.

3. Ehrich, J. Stat. Mech. 083214 (2021)
https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-5468/ac150e
https://arxiv.org/html/2105.08803v2
Sections 4–5 explicitly assume the correct number of hidden states is known. The paper fits full masked path statistics, rather than merely observed currents, and examines a four-state discrete-time network. Section 5 explicitly asks whether adding hidden states and cycles could drive the optimized bound down to observed time-series irreversibility. Do not use its title “tightest bound” as evidence of an unrestricted-dimension solution.

4. Nitzan, Ghosal and Bisker, Phys. Rev. Research 5, 043251 (2023)
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.5.043251
Hierarchical optimization using first- and second-order transitions plus waiting-time moments. The proposed calculation uses hypothesized hidden topology and also compares simpler topologies. It is a relevant executable optimization baseline; the retrieved primary abstract does not establish an analytic infimum over unrestricted hidden states/rates. Detailed implementation not independently reproduced in this audit.

5. Dechant, Garnier-Brun and Sasa, PRL 131, 167101 (2023)
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.131.167101
https://arxiv.org/html/2303.13038
Uses correlation time, short-time diffusivity and observable range to lower-bound entropy production from time-symmetric data. The primary derivation focuses on overdamped diffusion; its appendix gives a driven two-dimensional Gaussian model whose observed single coordinate is fully time reversible. Oscillatory covariance nevertheless reveals dissipation within that model class. Relevant comparator for any claim that passive time-symmetric correlations cannot reveal hidden dissipation. Requires short-time fluctuation information and long-time covariance information, which may exceed a fixed finite-snapshot interface.

6. Dechant, “Thermodynamic constraints on the power spectral density in and out of equilibrium” (2023 preprint)
https://arxiv.org/html/2306.00417
Bounds the power spectrum and gives a bounded-observable dissipation estimator from excess of omega^2 S(omega) above its high-frequency asymptote. It states applicability to Markov jump processes as well as overdamped diffusion. This can reveal dissipation without observable time-reversal breaking. The short-time/high-frequency limit is an acquisition requirement. The HTML display of Eq(5) shows a zero-frequency limit inconsistent with the derivation/text; equations (25)–(26) and prose identify the high-frequency limit, so consult source/PDF before quoting the formula. Discussion explicitly distinguishes underdamped equilibrium oscillations.

7. Contemporary direct challenge: Anonymous (2026), hidden-dissipation-floor
https://github.com/ipitchford/hidden-dissipation-floor
Pinned commit fd8d198fcfe50179bc7897ae043f3753f3c96821.
Primary PAPER.tex, SOURCES.md, CLAIM_STATUS.json and PROVENANCE.md downloaded as floor_*.
Unrefereed, AI-assisted candidate, dated 3 Oct 2026. It claims exact zero-cost closure for finite-realizable two-label alternating-renewal observations: unrestricted finite-architecture infimum is zero iff both complete dwell laws are hyperexponential iff a finite reversible exact realization exists. Its quantitative certificate compares Q to additive reversibilization and uses negative persistence covariance. Fixed-window bound: sigma >= (2p/t) I(a,b), where a=H(t)/p, b=H(2t)/p<a^2 and I is the Bernoulli-pair mutual information. Example floor >0.017568198744. It provides a deterministic error-box corollary but explicitly no confidence procedure or sample complexity. This is a substantial primary novelty comparator, not established external certification.

8. Aznagulov (2026), finite temporal resolution
https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37205v1
Preprint claims a two-clock observation protocol and matched exponential sample cost for normal, fully observed irreversible CTMC alternatives, against every finite hidden equilibrium null without hidden dimension/rate/mixing bounds. It uses Poissonized transition counts and a tilted-operator moment bound on antisymmetric currents, retaining dependence within one trajectory. Such current scores have zero mean when the complete observed law is exactly reversible. Consequently this impressive null-validity mechanism does not directly solve the present reversible-observation target. The primary fixed-dimension realizability question is in https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14623v1; those compactification claims were not audited here.

9. Parity and observation compatibility
Hartich–Godec comment: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-52602-0
Reply: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-52603-z
An equilibrium process can acquire apparent waiting-time irreversibility under a last-visited-state observation rule. The reply identifies the necessary compatibility of coarse graining with time reversal: f Theta = Theta f. Instantaneous deterministic lumping of even variables satisfies it; history-based recording need not. Do not equate arbitrary observed reversal KL with physical entropy production.
A thermal underdamped oscillator is a stronger warning: stationary position paths are exactly reversible, but C_x(t)=C_x(0) exp(-gamma t/2)[cos(Omega t)+gamma sin(Omega t)/(2Omega)] becomes negative. Hidden velocity is odd. An all-even reflection-positivity exclusion cannot rule out this physical equilibrium explanation. General Langevin momentum-reversal identity: https://arxiv.org/html/1006.4914, Prop. 3.2/Eq. (3.11).

10. Verified finite-sample tool: Maurer–Pontil, COLT 2009
https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.3740
https://www.learningtheory.org/colt2009/papers/012.pdf
Primary PDF and extracted text saved as maurer_pontil_2009.pdf/.txt. Theorem 4, affine-scaled: for iid X_i in [a,b], n>=2, with probability >=1-delta,
E[X]-mean <= sqrt(2 s_n^2 log(2/delta)/n) + 7(b-a)log(2/delta)/(3(n-1)),
where s_n^2=sum_i(X_i-mean)^2/(n-1).
For null E[X]>=0, reject if mean+radius<0. This needs no known null variance or distribution. For M preselected scores use log(2M/delta); for two-sided control use log(4/delta). Primary PDF Corollary 5 confirms factor 2 missing in some web extractions.
Theorem 10 also bounds s_n <= sqrt(v)+(b-a)sqrt(2log(1/eta)/(n-1)) with failure <=eta. This yields rigorous alternative power accounting when v is known for planning. Independence is across windows, not within each window. Consecutive dependent windows require another theorem or a paid independence/mixing construction.

Discrete-time caution (direct mathematical observation)
A reversible discrete transition matrix may have negative eigenvalues; arbitrary-lag covariance need not be nonnegative. Present-centered past–future Gram/complete-positivity constraints remain appropriate. Alternatively use even lags. A CTMC statement cannot be silently substituted for a discrete P=I+epsilon Q target.
