A constant-normalization block encoding of an operator \(F\) means a unitary circuit \(W\) satisfying
\[ \left\| 64(\langle 0^a|\otimes I)W(|0^a\rangle\otimes I)-F \right\|\le\varepsilon. \]
The constant \(64\) is inessential, but fixing it prevents normalization costs from being hidden.
Applying this circuit to \(|b\rangle\) and selecting the designated ancilla outcome produces a vector approximately proportional to \(F|b\rangle\). Its success probability is approximately
\[ \frac{\|F|b\rangle\|^2}{64^2}. \]
Efficient block encoding therefore does not mean free preparation of a normalized state when the output norm is tiny.
