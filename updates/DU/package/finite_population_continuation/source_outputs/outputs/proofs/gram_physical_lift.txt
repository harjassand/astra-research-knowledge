# Weighted Gram residuals compile into average-good pure-state transformations

Status: internally reconstructed finite-dimensional argument, 11 October 2026. This interface uses standard Uhlmann/orthogonal-Procrustes geometry. It is not a new foundational primitive or a historical-priority claim.

## Exact interface

Let `p_i >= 0`, `sum p_i = 1`, and let `psi_i` and `phi_i` be unit vectors in finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces `A` and `B`. Let `e_i` be arbitrary unit environment vectors. Define column operators

`W|i> = sqrt(p_i) psi_i`,

`S|i> = sqrt(p_i) phi_i tensor e_i`,

and density Gram matrices `G=W*W`, `K=S*S` on the classical label space. All spaces and vectors are supplied; acquiring or estimating them is outside this statement.

There is a CPTP channel `T:A -> B` with

`sum_i p_i <phi_i|T(P_psi_i)|phi_i> >= 1 - ||G-K||_1`.

The right side may be replaced by the sharper `Froot(G,K)^2`, where `Froot=||sqrt(G)sqrt(K)||_1`. No dependence on ambient dimension, number of states, or smallest positive prior appears in the error bound.

## Proof

Take purifications `|w>=sum_i sqrt(p_i)|i> psi_i` and `|s>=sum_i sqrt(p_i)|i> phi_i e_i` of the transposes of `G` and `K` under the displayed convention. Enlarge the target environment if necessary. Uhlmann's theorem supplies an isometry `U:A -> B tensor E'` with

`|<s|(I tensor U)|w>| = Froot(G,K)`.

Equivalently, finite-dimensional polar decomposition gives the optimal isometric column alignment. Choose a global phase so that this overlap is nonnegative. Set `z_i=<phi_i e_i|U psi_i>`. Then

`sum_i p_i |z_i|^2 >= |sum_i p_i z_i|^2 = Froot(G,K)^2`

by Jensen/Cauchy-Schwarz. After discarding `E'`, the output target fidelity for label `i` is at least `|z_i|^2`. This proves the sharper bound. Fuchs-van de Graaf gives `1-Froot(G,K) <= ||G-K||_1/2`, hence

`1-Froot(G,K)^2 <= ||G-K||_1`.

For reference, the vector-alignment estimate is also

`sum_i p_i ||U psi_i - phi_i e_i||^2 = 2-2Froot(G,K) <= ||G-K||_1`.

The isometry is defined first on the support of `W`; extend it after enlarging the target environment. Zero-prior labels have no role.

## Application to the archived abstract cloning residual

For a finite pure ensemble define `C_ij=<psi_i|psi_j>`, `G_ij=sqrt(p_i p_j) C_ij`, and let `H` be any positive semidefinite correlation matrix. Realize `H_ij=<e_i|e_j>` and take `phi_i=psi_i tensor psi_i`. The target Gram is

`K=G circ C circ H`.

Thus `||G-G circ C circ H||_1 <= epsilon` implies existence of a physical one-to-two cloner with average global infidelity at most `epsilon`. This is an average global-fidelity premise; no uniform per-state fidelity is asserted.

Combining this lift with the internally proved linear theorem in `linear_cloning_estimation.md` yields one EB channel with average pure-state infidelity at most `min(1,2800 epsilon)`, and therefore average half-trace reconstruction error at most `min(1,sqrt(2800 epsilon))` by the pure-target fidelity bound and Jensen.

This answers the exact abstract finite-ensemble modulus question as written in `G-PURE-GRAM-DIMENSION-FREE-STABILITY`, subject to the internal proof candidate's stated validation status. The conclusion is a finite pure-state ensemble theorem; it supplies no mixed-state, diamond-norm, capacity, or efficient unknown-state-acquisition conclusion.

For comparison, the initial lift combined with Astra N410's older quantitative theorem gave average infidelity `min(1,64 epsilon^(1/3))` and average half-trace error `min(1,8 epsilon^(1/6))`. That historical route was conditional on N410 and is not a premise of the new linear estimate.
