# Correlated products with arbitrary support: periodic transport limits

Status: newly derived in this worker, with a complete elementary proof below. This is a candidate extension of the source-reported Astra N674 result, not a claim of historical priority or a foundational breakthrough. No source theorem is needed in the proof. Exact finite-n feasibility is already reported in N674 and is not claimed as a new algorithm here.

## 1. Result

Let S be a finite commutative semigroup of size m, let A be a nonempty subset, and let μ be a probability law with support exactly A. Set α=min_{a∈A} μ(a)>0. Write P_n(μ) for all laws of X_1⋯X_n under arbitrary dependence and identical coordinate marginals μ.

For each a∈A choose its least eventual power index τ_a and period p_a:

    a^{k+p_a}=a^k for every k≥τ_a.

Both τ_a and p_a are at most m. Put τ=max_a τ_a and L=lcm_a p_a. Let e_a be the idempotent power of a, and form the local monoid

    I_a=e_aS,    identity e_a,    u_a=e_aa.

The element u_a is a unit of I_a, with u_a^{p_a}=e_a. Normalize each supported letter by

    r_{a,b}=u_a^{-1}e_ab∈I_a.

In particular r_{a,a}=e_a. Let J_a be the submonoid of I_a generated by the r_{a,b} with b∈A\{a}. For each h∈J_a choose a shortest expression as a product of these generators and retain the corresponding defect word over A\{a}; let d_a(h) be its length. Put

    C_a=max_{h∈J_a} d_a(h),    C=max_a C_a,
    B=min{m−1, Σ_{a∈A}(τ_a−1)}.

A shortest directed Cayley path has no repeated vertices, so

    C≤max_a(|J_a|−1)≤m−1.

For n≥τ+C define a relation E_n⊂A×S by

    a E_n z  ⇔  z∈u_a^nJ_a.

Equivalently each edge has a product-z word of length n with at most C non-a entries. The relation depends only on n modulo L; write E_r for this eventual relation. Let

    T_r(μ)={ν : there is a transport γ on A×S,
                   γ_A=μ, γ_S=ν, supp γ⊂E_r}.

**Theorem.**

1. For every n≥τ+C and every ν∈P_n(μ),

       dist_TV(ν,T_{n mod L}(μ))≤B/n.

2. Put

       N_μ=max{τ+C, 2C+1, ceil(C/α)}.

   For every n≥N_μ and every ν∈T_{n mod L}(μ), there is ν_n∈P_n(μ) with

       ||ν_n−ν||_TV≤C/n.

   The coupling is exchangeable and is a mixture of at most |A||S| word types. Every word type consists of n−d copies of one base letter a and at most d≤C exceptional letters from A.

3. Along each residue class r modulo L, P_n(μ) converges in total-variation Hausdorff distance to T_r(μ), at rate at most max(B,C)/n once n≥N_μ. The set of all subsequential limiting output laws is exactly the finite union ⋃_r T_r(μ).

Since B,C≤m−1, a uniform simpler statement is

    d_H,TV(P_n(μ),T_{n mod L}(μ))≤(m−1)/n

for all n≥max{2m−1,ceil((m−1)/α)}.

The edge relation has a direct interpretation using the original Astra idempotent relation:

    a E_n z  ⇔  [e_az=z and z∈A^n]    (n≥τ+C).

Consequently T_{n mod L}(μ) is exactly the original e_az=z transport polytope, intersected with laws supported on the n-fold product set A^n. That product set is itself L-periodic once n≥max{m,τ+C}.

Zero entries of μ are allowed. Unlike the full-support N674 theorem, a law in T_r(μ) need not be exactly attainable at any finite n. Section 4 gives a sharp obstruction.

## 2. Local-unit normalization and algebraic compression

**Lemma.** For n≥τ+C, the following are equivalent:

1. z∈u_a^nJ_a;
2. e_az=z and z is the product of a length-n word over A;
3. z is the product of a length-n word over A with at least τ_a copies of a.

Every such product has a word with the same length and product and at most C non-a letters.

Proof. The eventual cycle of a is a group with identity e_a, so u_a=e_aa is a unit of I_a and u_a^{p_a}=e_a. If z is a product of n supported letters and e_az=z, commutativity gives

    u_a^{-n}z = product over letters b of (u_a^{-1}e_ab).

Every occurrence of a contributes the identity e_a; the remaining factors show that h=u_a^{-n}z belongs to J_a. This proves 2⇒1.

If h∈J_a has a shortest defect word b_1⋯b_d with d≤C, then, since n−d≥τ_a,

    a^{n−d}b_1⋯b_d
      =u_a^{n−d}(e_ab_1)⋯(e_ab_d)
      =u_a^n r_{a,b_1}⋯r_{a,b_d}
      =u_a^nh=z.

This proves 1⇒3 and the bounded-defect form. Finally, at least τ_a copies of a imply e_a fixes the product, since e_aa^k=a^k for k≥τ_a; hence 3⇒2. QED.

The periodicity of E_n follows from u_a^{p_a}=e_a. No large common-period enumeration is required: construct each at-most-m-state monoid J_a by breadth-first search and use binary powering for u_a^n.

For completeness, at most m−1 occurrences of any word can have e_bz≠z, where z is the final product. Reorder those forbidden letters b_1,…,b_k first. Their prefixes p_i=b_1⋯b_i must be distinct: a repetition p_i=p_j implies the idempotent of the intervening product fixes p_i, and then every individual idempotent in that block fixes p_i and z. Put e=∏_{i=1}^k e_{b_i}. The element p_ke differs from every prefix p_i; otherwise all e_{b_i} would fix that prefix and z. Thus k+1≤m. Here e_{xy}=e_xe_y follows by taking one common sufficiently large idempotent exponent and using commutativity.

This also shows every length-n word has at least one allowed letter once n≥m. Therefore, for n≥max{m,τ+C}, A^n=⋃_a u_a^nJ_a and its eventual period divides L.

## 3. Probability proof and construction

### Necessity

For any admissible coupling define its empirical source-to-product transport

    γ(a,z)=E[(number of a's)/n · 1{product=z}].

Its marginals are μ and ν. By the compression lemma, if (a,z) is outside E_n then this word has fewer than τ_a occurrences of a. This bounds the forbidden count by Σ_a(τ_a−1). The prefix argument above gives the alternative bound m−1. Hence the total transport mass outside E_n is at most B/n.

For each a reroute its forbidden mass to a^n, an allowed target using the empty defect word. This keeps the source marginal μ, produces a law η∈T_{n mod L}(μ), and changes at most B/n probability mass. Hence ||ν−η||_TV≤B/n.

### Sufficiency with exact input marginal

Fix a supported transport γ from μ to ν and set K(a,z)=γ(a,z)/μ(a). For each allowed pair choose one of the compressed product-z words from the definition of E_n. Let c^{a,z}_b be its letter counts and define a row-stochastic matrix on A by

    M(a,b)=Σ_z K(a,z)c^{a,z}_b/n.

Every chosen word has at most C non-base letters, so M(a,a)≥1−δ where δ=C/n.

If C=0 then M=I and no balancing is necessary. Suppose C>0. Since n>2C, write

    M=(1−δ)I+δQ

with Q row-stochastic and δ<1/2. The affine map on the probability simplex

    F(q)=(μ−δ qQ)/(1−δ)

has coordinate sum one. It is nonnegative because μ(b)≥α≥δ and (qQ)(b)≤1. Its ℓ¹ Lipschitz constant is δ/(1−δ)<1. Its unique fixed point q is a probability vector and satisfies qM=μ. Equivalently q=μM^{-1}; positivity follows from the invariant-simplex argument, not from claiming M^{-1} is entrywise nonnegative.

Choose (a,z) with probability q(a)K(a,z), choose the corresponding length-n compressed word, and randomly permute all n positions. Every coordinate has marginal qM=μ, and the product is z. The actual output law is ν_n=qK, whereas the desired law is ν=μK. Since μ=(1−δ)q+δqQ,

    ||q−μ||_TV = δ||q−qQ||_TV≤δ.

Stochastic kernels contract total variation, giving ||ν_n−ν||_TV≤C/n. QED.

Compactness of each transport polytope proves the limiting classification. Finitely many residues ensure every convergent sequence of output laws has a subsequence in one residue class.

### Rational construction and scope of costs

For a supplied multiplication table, A, binary n and rational transport γ, all compressed word witnesses are obtained from at most |A| graphs of at most m states. Fast powering evaluates their products using poly(log n) multiplications. A rational |A|×|A| linear system gives q. The compressed construction consequently has polynomial arithmetic and bit complexity in the supplied table size, rational input length and log n. Writing all n coordinates costs n symbol writes; random permutation or coordinate queries require a separate implementation. This is a finite represented-algebra result, not acquisition of a semigroup representation for an arbitrary problem.

## 4. Exact eventual attainment fails, with sharp inward distance

Take the three-element join semilattice S={a,b,c}, with a and b incomparable and c=a∨b. Let μ(a)=μ(b)=1/2 and μ(c)=0. Then τ_a=τ_b=p_a=p_b=1, L=1, C=1, B=0, and

    E(a)={a,c},     E(b)={b,c}.

Thus ν=(ν(a),ν(b),ν(c))=(1/2,0,1/2) belongs to T_0(μ).

Nevertheless ν is unattainable for every n: product a forces all n letters to be a; product c requires at least one a and one b. If product a already has probability 1/2, its a-count expectation consumes the entire required n/2, leaving no a available on the positive-probability c event.

In fact, for every n≥2,

    dist_TV(ν,P_n(μ))=1/(2n).

For any admissible output η=(x,y,z), the b-count is at most n on product b, at most n−1 on product c, and zero on product a. Therefore

    n/2≤ny+(n−1)z,
    (n−1)x−y≤(n−2)/2.

The target ν violates this last inequality by 1/2. The function taking values n−1, −1, 0 at a,b,c has oscillation n, so total variation distance is at least 1/(2n).

Equality is achieved by the exchangeable mixture

    probability (n−1)/(2n): all a;
    probability 1/(2n): all b;
    probability 1/2: one a and n−1 b, uniformly permuted.

Its input marginal is μ and output law is (1/2−1/(2n),1/(2n),1/2). Thus no uniform o(1/n) Hausdorff rate is possible, and the original full-support exact-attainment conclusion cannot be retained on zero-support boundaries.

## 5. Exact arbitrary-support corollary for finite abelian groups

Let G be finite abelian and A=supp μ. Choose a₀∈A and let

    H=⟨b a₀^{-1}: b∈A⟩.

For every n, all product values lie in the coset a₀^nH. For every sufficiently large n,

    P_n(μ)=all probability laws supported on a₀^nH.

One sufficient threshold is

    n≥max{2(|H|−1)+1, ceil((|H|−1)/α)}.

If |H|=1 the conclusion holds at every n.

Proof. For any base a∈A, the ratios b a^{-1}, b∈A\{a}, generate H as a semigroup, because H is a finite group. A shortest directed Cayley path to any h∈H has length at most |H|−1. Thus every z∈a₀^nH has, for every a, a product-z word with at most |H|−1 non-a letters. In the preceding balancing construction choose the target kernel K(a,z)=ν(z), independently of a. Adjusting the base distribution q does not change the output: qK=ν. All inputs still have marginal μ. The reverse inclusion is the coset invariant. QED.

This removes the full-support-on-G assumption for the exact group conclusion. It retains a support-generated coset obstruction and gives a bounded-defect construction. Historical priority is unresolved.

       ||HV−VH||HS²≥Δ²||Π−VΠV*||HS².

   This reduces arbitrary adjoint-orbit caps uniformly to Grassmannian caps, including nearly degenerate H. The final theorem belongs to the cross_corpus worker; my check found its cap and entropy constants consistent.
