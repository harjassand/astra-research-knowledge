A. Collective replica contraction with one final flag
Source: Astra unmerged PR43, commit 2124bcb0755b87a65c224a086c3633c9b655ab28, N681; exact report updates/DL/package/replica_programmed_quantum_operations/Pasted text.txt lines 101-238.
The source reports F_{sigma,n}=F_sigma+(G_sigma-F_sigma)/n and a flagged diamond error <=1/n for one routed channel invocation. This is an algebraic diagonal/off-diagonal identity.

For a fixed L-stage internally controlled circuit, with independent source slot A_j in state sigma_j at stage j, put tau=tensor_j sigma_j. Treat the entire circuit as one channel C on A_1...A_L and its information register; retain all returned A_j until the end. Applying the same routing operation to n independent blocks of tau gives the entire purification-return success subchannel with one final flag at error <=1/n. If the circuit's Kraus words are products K_{L,alpha_L}...K_{1,alpha_1}, partial trace over distinct source slots factors in temporal order, giving the product of their contracted Kraus operators. Therefore L ceil(1/epsilon) samples suffice for this delayed-flag, buffered-output contract.

This is a direct closure corollary of N681, not a new foundational primitive. It does not simulate L separately flagged adaptive channels whose external user receives a flag after each stage. Intermediate outcome-controlled operations may be internal to the fixed circuit, but inaccessible early flags cannot drive later external input choices. Failure cannot erase outputs already released. Buffer storage, controlled swaps, identified physical channel ports and independent samples are required. Rare conditioning still amplifies absolute error by inverse success probability. N685 (PR46, 982a5018a8ba9f5c3c3824cdcb7b08e94340bcec) supplies a distinct resource theory warning that late and early flags cannot in general be interchanged.

B. Why replacing common resolvent purification by square roots fails
OpenAI Math fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb, family277, sections/01-operators.tex proves a common resolvent purification F_A with F_A*F_A=A and Jensen control
 sum_j q_j (F_Aj-F_B)*(F_Aj-F_B) <= sum_j q_j f(A_j)-f(B), f(t)=t log t, B=sum q_j A_j.
The tempting tensor-compatible replacement F_A=sqrt(A) does NOT satisfy this Loewner inequality, even for two 2x2 effects. Here is an analytic proof, without relying on numerical evidence.

Work in an eigenbasis of C=diag(1,1/4), and let
 P=[[9/25,-12/25],[-12/25,16/25]], a rank-one projection.
For 0<q<1 put B_q=qP+(1-q)C. Define the entropy gap minus the proposed square-root defect:
 D(q)=q f(P)+(1-q)f(C)-f(B_q)
      -q(sqrt(P)-sqrt(B_q))^2
      -(1-q)(sqrt(C)-sqrt(B_q))^2.
Since C>0 all q-dependent matrix functions are differentiable near zero. D(0)=0. Let l=log4. The Frechet derivative of f at diag(1,1/4) has diagonal multipliers 1 and 1-l and off-diagonal multiplier l/3. Direct calculation gives
 D'(0)=[[0,(4l-6)/25],[(4l-6)/25,16(l-1)/25]].
Its determinant is -(4l-6)^2/625<0, since log4<3/2. Hence D'(0) has a strictly negative eigenvalue, and D(q) is not positive semidefinite for all sufficiently small q>0. All input effects and weights may be chosen rational: the failure holds throughout an open positive q interval.

This blocks the exact coefficient-one monoidal square-root substitution in this proof route. It does NOT rule out another tensor-compatible construction, a different norm, a larger universal constant, or multiplayer repetition. Grouping several players as one also loses their local measurement structure. The unresolved next lemma is a tensor-compatible/common-space purification or a different conditioning operation preserving all local POVMs with enough entropy control. No multiplayer repetition theorem is claimed.
