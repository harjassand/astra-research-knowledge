# Finite automata for robust coupling questions at all horizons

11 October 2026. New consequence of the finite-population theorem, independently reconstructed through exact-length reachability. The underlying theorem in ../consequence_semigroup/FINITE_POPULATION_THEOREM.md is preserved unchanged. No historical originality claim is made.

## 1. Independent interface check

The whole-path integral rounding and common filler repair are sound under the stated interface: a finite total deterministic transition table, a deterministic initial state, arbitrary joint input laws, and only unconditional one-coordinate marginal constraints. The rounded layered graph is acyclic, so its integer flow decomposes into D complete paths. Parallel labelled edges preserve distinct letters. Each terminal sink edge is individually rounded, giving terminal TV at most d/(2D), regardless of horizon. The mixture repair changes the entire word law once, by mass beta, rather than changing each transition separately.

An additional exact support requirement on words would invalidate the filler step in general. This is not an omitted requirement in the theorem: terminal output is approximated in TV, which permits beta mass in other terminal states. Similarly, prescribed conditional transition probabilities, blind independence of the chosen letter from the current random state, or fixed joint laws of several coordinates are extra interfaces not covered by the result. An ordinary probabilistic automaton cannot be substituted without establishing those restrictions. Totality of the deterministic table is used because every filler word must define a legal trajectory.

## 2. Same-horizon rational polytope approximation

Let R_n be the exact attainable terminal-state polytope at horizon n under the original fixed marginal mu. Let V_(D,n) be the configurations reachable at **exactly** n edges in the D-particle graph, and put

    G_(D,n)={x/D:x in V_(D,n)},
    P_(D,n)=conv G_(D,n),
    r_D=d/(2D),
    beta_D=d/(D alpha+d),  alpha=min positive atom of mu.

The rounding and repair operations do not alter n. Therefore for every n>=1, simultaneously,

    sup_(p in R_n) dist_TV(p,G_(D,n))<=r_D,
    sup_(g in G_(D,n)) dist_TV(g,R_n)<=beta_D.             (H1)

Since R_n is convex, the second bound extends to P_(D,n): convexly combine the repaired exact-marginal laws, all at the same horizon n. Thus

    d_H(R_n,P_(D,n))<=delta_D:=max{r_D,beta_D}.             (H2)

The finite-population graph has N=binom(D+d-1,d-1) vertices. Its exact-length reachable subsets satisfy the deterministic recurrence

    V_(D,0)={D delta_(q0)},
    V_(D,n+1)=Neighbors(V_(D,n)).                           (H3)

Among the first 2^N+1 subsets, two coincide. Hence there are explicitly computable integers T>=0 and p>=1, with T+p<=2^N, such that

    V_(D,n+p)=V_(D,n),
    P_(D,n+p)=P_(D,n)                 for all n>=T.        (H4)

Consequently the entire sequence (R_n) is uniformly approximable, to any requested terminal accuracy, by an effectively supplied ultimately periodic sequence of rational polytopes. In particular,

    d_H(R_(n+p),R_n)<=2delta_D        for all n>=max{1,T}. (H5)

The stronger assertion is the uniform approximation by one periodic polytope sequence, not merely a small increment between successive reachable sets. Repeated small increments can accumulate and do not establish (H2)-(H4).

For rational mu, this is an effective statement. For arbitrary positive real mu the mathematical finite graph still exists, but exact compilation of its edges requires a representation that decides the budget inequalities.

## 3. Robust horizon languages are regular

Fix a rational target terminal law nu, a rational a>=0, and gamma>0. Write

    v_n=dist_TV(nu,R_n),
    v_(D,n)=min_(g in G_(D,n)) TV(nu,g).

The same-horizon bounds imply

    max{0,v_(D,n)-r_D}<=v_n<=v_(D,n)+beta_D.               (H6)

Assume the following supplied uniform gap promise, for every positive integer n:

    either v_n<=a, or v_n>=a+gamma.                         (H7)

Choose D with max{r_D,beta_D}<gamma/3. Then

    v_n<=a       iff v_(D,n)<a+gamma/2,
    v_n>=a+gamma iff v_(D,n)>a+gamma/2.                    (H8)

The strict comparisons are separated by at least gamma/6. A unary DFA whose states are subsets of the configuration vertices, whose transition is (H3), and whose accepting subsets satisfy (H8), recognizes

    {1^n:n>=1 and v_n<=a}.                                 (H9)

Thus the robust horizon set is computably ultimately periodic. This decides, under the promise, whether a successful horizon exists, whether all horizons succeed, whether infinitely many succeed, and whether every sufficiently large horizon succeeds. The uniform promise is an input condition; no algorithm for verifying it is asserted.

The special case a=0 says that exact attainability becomes regular if every failure is separated from zero terminal distance by one supplied gamma. The nonperiodic exact-cutpoint construction is compatible with this theorem precisely because its unsuccessful horizons approach zero deficit; it has no such uniform gap.

For a bounded rational terminal payoff c, put

    w_n=max_(p in R_n) c dot p,
    w_(D,n)=max_(g in G_(D,n)) c dot g,
    osc(c)=max_s c(s)-min_s c(s).

Then the rational sequence w_(D,n) is ultimately periodic and

    w_(D,n)-osc(c) beta_D <= w_n
       <= w_(D,n)+osc(c) r_D                               (H10)

uniformly in n. The limsup and liminf of w_n are therefore uniformly computable reals: compute the maximum and minimum over one eventual period of w_(D,n), and use (H10). The same statement holds for v_n using (H6). Exact equality between the limsup and liminf, or equality at a cutpoint, is not decided by this approximation theorem.

## 4. A stronger non-unary consequence: marginal-control catalogues

The preceding mechanism is not restricted to one repeated marginal. Let C be a finite control alphabet. A control symbol b specifies a rational marginal mu_b on the input alphabet A, and may also specify a total deterministic transition table f_b:Q x A->Q. A supplied control word w=b_1...b_n is chosen offline. Conditional on that word, the input law may be any coupling satisfying

    X_t ~ mu_(b_t)                 for every t.

Let R_w be its attainable terminal-state polytope. The coupling may depend on the entire supplied word w; a single common noise law shared across all control words is not assumed. The initial state remains deterministic and fixed.

Let alpha be the smallest positive atom among all mu_b. For each b, use b-labelled population edges defined by the usual row and successor counts and the budgets

    |sum_s k_(s,a)-D mu_b(a)|<=d,
    k_(s,a)=0 whenever mu_b(a)=0.                           (H11)

The last condition is essential if supports vary: a zero-probability letter cannot be introduced and then repaired by a nonnegative filler.

Let G_(D,w) be empirical configurations reachable by the exact labelled word w. Whole-path flow rounding uses (H11) at every layer; a zero-flow edge stays zero under its floor/ceiling bounds. Conversely, every population trajectory has coordinate marginal mu'_t with mu'_t(a)=0 on absent letters and mu'_t(a)<=mu_(b_t)(a)+d/D otherwise. The same one-time mixture weight

    beta_D=d/(D alpha+d)

repairs every coordinate using

    q_t(a)=[mu_(b_t)(a)-(1-beta_D)mu'_t(a)]/beta_D.

Thus (H1)-(H2) hold uniformly for **every control word w**, replacing n by w.

Under a uniform target gap promise over all nonempty control words, the language

    {w:dist_TV(nu,R_w)<=a}

is computably regular. Determinize the finite labelled population graph; after reading w its subset state is exactly the endpoint set G_(D,w), and accept using the separated test (H8). There are at most 2^N subset states. A regular restriction on allowed control words is handled by a product with its supplied DFA.

Likewise, the optimal terminal payoff over every horizon and every control word is a computable real: use ordinary reachability in the finite labelled graph, optimize c dot (x/D) over reachable endpoints, and apply the rounding/repair interval. This is a robust optimization theorem for ordered, possibly noncommutative finite dynamics with marginal constraints. It is not a theorem about arbitrary stochastic kernels or ordinary PFA coins.

## 5. Asymptotic attainable laws

For the original unary model, define the tail accumulation set

    L=intersection_(m>=1) closure(union_(n>=m) R_n).

Let G_D^infinity consist of empirical configurations reachable by arbitrarily long directed paths from the population initial vertex. Equivalently, retain every vertex reachable from a directed cycle that itself is reachable from the initial vertex. It is finite and computable. Then

    sup_(p in L) dist_TV(p,G_D^infinity)<=r_D,
    sup_(g in G_D^infinity) dist_TV(g,L)<=beta_D.            (H12)

For the first direction, choose n_j->infinity and p_j in R_(n_j) tending to p; round p_j. One of the finitely many rounded configurations occurs along an unbounded subsequence, hence lies in G_D^infinity and is within r_D of p. For the reverse direction, choose arbitrarily long graph paths ending at g, repair each into an exact-marginal terminal law p_j within beta_D, and take a convergent subsequence in the finite terminal simplex. Its limit belongs to L and remains within beta_D of g.

Therefore L is effectively compact to arbitrary terminal TV accuracy. Neither its exact membership nor exact equality between L and a finite-time reachable set is decided.

## 6. Primary comparison and scope of the consequence

Rabin's original reduction theorem says that an ordinary probabilistic automaton with an isolated cutpoint recognizes a regular language. Primary paper: M. O. Rabin, *Probabilistic automata*, Information and Control 6 (1963), 230-245, Theorem 3, https://doi.org/10.1016/S0019-9958(63)90290-0 ; primary PDF https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/denis.kuperberg/CR18/probas.pdf . Thus a general slogan that a fixed gap restores regularity is old. The present proof concerns a different optimized-correlations semantics, and its new load-bearing input is a uniform-in-word, same-horizon rational polytope approximation with exact coordinate marginals.

The finite population construction and its comparison to Gao et al.'s initialized distributional reachability interface are recorded in ../consequence_semigroup/INTERFACE_AND_CONSEQUENCES.md. No claim that the current consequence is historically new follows merely from these comparisons.
