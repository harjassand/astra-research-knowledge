# One approximate doubling channel compiles to one reusable classical program

11 October 2026. Internal mathematical deduction from the full class-measurement proof of M1, with M1 unchanged. This argument uses the expected loss of an actual estimated state, which is stronger than the fidelity of a measure-and-prepare mixture. Historical originality and external validation remain unresolved.

## 1. Exact theorem

Let H have finite dimension d and let B:T(H)->T(H tensor H) be a supplied CPTP map. Put

    e_B(rho)=1-F(B(rho),rho tensor rho),
    F(rho,sigma)=||sqrt(rho)sqrt(sigma)||_1^2,
    tau=1/2800,
    C=8/(1-sqrt(3/200))^2 < 11,
    K_m=max{2800,24 C+50m}.

There exist, depending only on B, an isometry U:H->C^N with d<=N<=2d, one coordinate-projective measurement, and fixed density matrices sigma_1,...,sigma_N on H, such that **the same U and the same dictionary work simultaneously for every input density matrix rho, every input prior, and every integer m>=1**. Write

    p_y(rho)=<y|U rho U*|y>.

Then the actual proper-estimation loss satisfies

    R_m(rho):=sum_y p_y(rho) [1-F(rho,sigma_y)^m]
             <= min{1,K_m e_B(rho)}.                         (1)

In particular, measuring once, storing y, and conditionally preparing independent copies of sigma_y defines

    Gamma_m(rho)=sum_y p_y(rho) sigma_y^(tensor m),

with

    1-F(Gamma_m(rho),rho^(tensor m)) <= R_m(rho).             (2)

The channels are exactly consistent:

    Tr_last Gamma_(m+1)=Gamma_m.                              (3)

Thus a single classical record programs a sequential generator for all future output counts. The operational measurement uses one pure ancillary qubit; conditional mixed-state preparation can require additional resources. The record has at most 2d values, or ceil(log_2(2d)) bits. Coarse-graining coordinates that prepare the same representative can reduce the stored alphabet.

For any nonempty compact promised model M of density matrices, the same statement holds on M with every sigma_y belonging to M. The compiler may then depend on both B and M. For the full-state model, it depends only on B.

## 2. Proof, including the stronger premise needed beyond M1's statement

The proof of M1 constructs on S={rho:e_B(rho)<=tau} a finite partition into high-overlap classes, minimum-error representatives sigma_c, and a class POVM M_c satisfying

    1-F(rho,sigma_c)<=50 e_B(rho),
    r(rho):=1-Tr(M_c rho)<=C e_B(rho)                       (4)

whenever rho lies in class c. This construction is performed once, independently of m. Its ingredients and all constants are proved in ../astra_obstruction/mixed_linear_classicalization.md; equations (4) are the within-class estimate and the constrained majority/minimax estimate, not an inference from EB reconstruction fidelity.

Spectral polar rounding gives the one fixed isometry U into dimension N<=2d and coordinate output blocks indexed by the same classes. For rho in class c, the probability of a coordinate outside block c is at most

    r_U(rho)<=24 C e_B(rho).                                (5)

Prepare sigma_c for every coordinate inside block c; padded coordinates prepare any fixed representative. Fidelity tensor multiplicativity gives

    F(rho^(tensor m),sigma_c^(tensor m))=F(rho,sigma_c)^m.

The loss from a wrong block is bounded by its probability. On the correct block, Bernoulli's inequality gives

    1-F(rho,sigma_c)^m <= m [1-F(rho,sigma_c)]
                       <=50m e_B(rho).

Hence R_m(rho)<=r_U(rho)+50m e_B(rho)<=(24C+50m)e_B(rho) on S. Outside S, R_m<=1<2800e_B(rho). These two cases prove (1) for all m and rho, with no further choice of measurement or dictionary.

For fixed rho^(tensor m), separate concavity of squared Uhlmann fidelity implies

    F(rho^(tensor m),sum_y p_y sigma_y^(tensor m))
       >=sum_y p_y F(rho^(tensor m),sigma_y^(tensor m)).

This proves (2). Normalization of each sigma_y proves (3).

If S is empty, any basis measurement and one fixed preparation satisfy (1), since e_B>tau everywhere. The same fallback applies if the good subset of a compact promised model is empty. Otherwise restrict the entire class construction to that compact model, which makes every representative proper.

If the rank-one/dilation bound is not requested, use the original class POVM instead. Its stronger constant is

    K_m^unrounded=max{2800,C+50m}.                           (6)

For a uniformly good promised model, the max with 2800 is unnecessary: (24C+50m)epsilon, or (C+50m)epsilon before rounding, is the uniform bound.

## 3. Why ordinary EB reconstruction does not imply this theorem

An EB channel that reconstructs a mixed state can conceal loss by averaging incompatible estimates. Let rho=I_2/2 and measure it in the standard basis, preparing P_0 or P_1. The averaged one-output state is exactly rho, so its reconstruction infidelity is zero. The proper expected one-state loss is 1/2. Reusing that record twice gives (P_0 tensor P_0+P_1 tensor P_1)/2, whose squared fidelity with rho tensor rho is 1/2.

Therefore the assertion (1) must be proved using the class decoder and representatives. It does not follow from M1's displayed EB-mixture inequality alone. This is also why keeping the expected m-copy estimation loss is essential.

## 4. The universal replication horizon

Let a sequence of supplied channels B_n and states/models have uniform product-doubling errors epsilon_n->0. For any sequence m_n of output counts satisfying

    m_n epsilon_n ->0,

the same compiled classical generator has global m_n-output infidelity tending to zero. Its input measurement can be chosen before the output count or downstream application is specified.

This improves the error accumulation obtained by directly composing B: producing m outputs by m-1 sequential applications and applying a Bures triangle estimate gives an O(m^2 epsilon) bound. The compiler uses the structural conclusion that near-product replication reveals a nearly deterministic class, so its state-preparation error tensorizes linearly.

The order m epsilon is universally sharp. For a binary pure-state family of input overlap c=cos(theta), the exact minimax m-copy cloning error is

    a_m(c)=sin^2((arccos(c^m)-arccos(c))/2).                  (7)

The lower bound follows from Bures-angle contractivity and its triangle inequality; an isometry mapping the input pair into the target pair's real plane, centered symmetrically around the two ideals, attains it. In particular, for fixed m and theta->0,

    a_m(c)/a_2(c) -> ((sqrt(m)-1)/(sqrt(2)-1))^2.             (8)

This grows as (3+2sqrt(2))m. It rules out any o(m) universal coefficient even for two pure qubit inputs, even if the m-copy output is allowed to be an arbitrary coherent state, and even when the m-copy channel can be chosen anew for every m. A separate independent proof of (7)-(8) is being developed by miner A.

For a joint sequence theta_n->0, m_n->infinity, epsilon_n=a_2(c_n), and m_n epsilon_n->lambda in (0,infinity), the same exact formula gives

    a_(m_n)(c_n) -> [1-exp(-2(3+2sqrt(2))lambda)]/2 >0.       (9)

For m_n epsilon_n->infinity its limit is 1/2. Thus no universal vanishing-error guarantee can extend past the horizon m epsilon->0. There can be special families with a much longer horizon, including exactly orthogonal states; the claim is a worst-family sharp guarantee.

An independent stronger lower construction is now complete in sharp_replication_lower_bound.md. It uses ONE fixed explicit qubit channel, rather than a family of pair-optimal supplied doublers:

    V|0>=|00>,   V|1>=(|01>+|10>)/sqrt(2),
    B(X)=VXV*,
    |psi_t>=sqrt(1-t)|0>+sqrt(t)|1>.

Its exact doubling loss is

    epsilon(t)=(sqrt(2)-1)^2 t+(4sqrt(2)-5)t^2
                  +(sqrt(2)-1)^2 t^3.

Any converter satisfying a pointwise finite relative-error guarantee must fix P_0 exactly. Every EB channel fixing a pure state kills tangent coherences at that state, and therefore requires

    K_m >=(3+2sqrt(2))m.                                  (9a)

Without an EB assumption, trace-distance contractivity at that same zero-error anchor yields, uniformly over all m-output CPTP converters fixing it,

    |F(C_m(P_t),P_t^(tensor m))-(1-t)^m|<=sqrt(t).

Thus along m epsilon(t)->lambda the error tends to

    1-exp(-(3+2sqrt(2))lambda),                             (9b)

and it tends to ONE if m epsilon(t)->infinity. This strengthens the horizon obstruction for the all-state pointwise compiler interface. The earlier balanced-pair calculation remains useful because it applies also to finite-prior average guarantees, which need not fix an anchor exactly.

## 5. Post hoc quantum decision protocols

Any quantum protocol receiving at most m independent copies of rho, with arbitrary known ancillas, adaptive operations, measurements, and classical post-processing, is a channel applied to rho^(tensor m), possibly discarding unused copies. Feeding it the sequential generator instead changes its final output trace distance by at most

    sqrt(K_m e_B(rho)),                                     (10)

by (2), fidelity/trace-distance comparison, and contractivity. The measurement U and dictionary are independent of the chosen downstream protocol. This is a reusable classical description for all copy-consuming decision tasks within the stated horizon, with the same fixed program.

The promise concerns an unreferenced unknown state. Equation (10) does not assert diamond-norm approximation of an identity channel, recovery of entanglement with an external unknown reference, or an adaptive channel-oracle simulation.

## 6. Acquisition and costs

The supplied object is a full CPTP B, not a device assumed to work correctly on unobserved inputs without calibration. Mathematical compilation uses its good-set geometry and a constrained global POVM optimization. A finite known model gives finitely many state/error constraints; a continuum or an unknown model has no efficient acquisition bound established here.

After acquisition, one input use and one fixed measurement produce a finite record. Output generation uses m conditional state preparations. State-synthesis costs, precision needed to hold a dictionary, the classical storage of the dictionary, and acquisition/calibration of B and U are charged separately and can dominate. No algorithmic speedup or cheap tomography follows from this existence statement.

## 7. Primary comparisons and unresolved novelty

* Chiribella and Yang, *Optimal asymptotic cloning machines*, arXiv:1404.0990v4, Secs. 2.2-2.3 and 3: https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.0990 . The question there is relative global fidelity of optimal cloning versus unrestricted measure-and-prepare at output count M->infinity with input count N fixed. Prepared M-partite states can be entangled, and proper product re-preparation need not be optimal. Equations (1)-(9) concern instead a uniform finite near-perfect product-doubling premise and a joint error/output-count limit. They do not resolve that general conjecture.
* Yang and Chiribella, *Is global asymptotic cloning state estimation?*, arXiv:1306.6427: https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.6427 . This distinguishes global output fidelity from single-clone fidelity and exhibits the relevance of entangled prepared outputs.
* Chiribella, Yang, and Yao, *Quantum replication at the Heisenberg limit*, Nature Communications 4, 2915 (2013): https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms3915 . This studies deterministic and probabilistic replication rates for continuous pure-state families. The supplied theorem here is deterministic; a conditional successful branch is not a CPTP doubling channel without accounting for its success probability.
* Li, Theil, Harrow, and Chuang, *An Exponential Sample-Complexity Advantage for Coherent Quantum Inference*, arXiv:2605.21457v2 (29 September 2026): https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21457v2 . Its EB limit is based on one-site symmetric-extension approximation. Its approximate-cloning sample advantage is expressed through one-site error; its global bound is a binomial-dimension ratio. These interfaces do not directly imply the finite proper expected global-loss compiler proved above.

These are focused comparisons, not a complete priority search. A matching earlier universal theorem or a stronger result elsewhere remains possible. The exact mathematical consequence is proved here independently of novelty.
