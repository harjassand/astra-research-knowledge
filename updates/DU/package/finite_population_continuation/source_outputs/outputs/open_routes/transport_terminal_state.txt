## Main completed result

`score_interpolation_theorem.md`, Sections 1–5, is the main result. Root directly confirmed that both root and `cross_corpus` internally reconstructed its full proof. The source is `rho=e^{-V}` on all of `R^d`, `V in C^1`, `V-kappa|x|^2/2` convex, `kappa>0`, and `S_q=sum_j||partial_jV||_{L^q(rho)}<infinity` for `q=p/(p-2)`, `p>2`. Exactly one target has `int|y|^p dmu<=M`, while the other is arbitrary in `P_2`. With `w=W_2(mu,nu)`,

\[
\|T_\mu-T_\nu\|_{L^2(\rho)}\le w+Cw^{1/3}.
\]

The constant is explicit in `d,kappa,S_q,p,M`. There is no upper curvature assumption. Sharpness over this source class is supplied by a fixed standard Gaussian source and equal-weight compact three-atom target families. This does not assert a matching lower bound for every individual source. The independent score reconstruction is `work/cross_corpus/SCORE_ONLY_TRANSPORT.md`.

The reusable mechanisms are: centered midpoint surplus, a centered adjoint cancellation avoiding uncontrolled raw cell means, conditional cell covariance, source-mean-zero potential Lipschitz stability `F<=sqrt(2/kappa)w`, quadratic monotone derivative variation, and pointwise mode-strip domination by the weighted score energy. The common bad set gives the exact interpolation constant

\[
D^2\le12dF^2/h^2+10h\sum_j(J_{u,j}+J_{v,j})+20d\tau(L).
\]

The proof imports Brenier uniqueness/stability, log-concavity convolution, Gaussian integration, Holder, and Stieltjes/Fubini calculus. It makes no algorithm, target acquisition, numerical complexity, or finite bit precision claim.

\[

## Preserved failed route

Replacing the compact-radius raw mass-to-moment estimate by a bound using only the source second moment is false: Gaussian moving halfspaces at threshold `t` give an uncentered ratio growing as `t^2`. This failure is preserved in `transport_theorem.md`, Section 8. Centering the cell moments and adjoint velocities, rather than ignoring this counterexample, is what enables the radius-free strongly log-concave potential theorem.
