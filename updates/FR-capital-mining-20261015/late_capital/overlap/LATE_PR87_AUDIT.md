## Developed extension: some correlated clocks preserve the certificate

Unconditional gap independence is sufficient, not necessary. Let κ(ds,dt) be a symmetric probability law for the two nonnegative gaps, independent of specimen and readout. Suppose its clock bilinear form is positive semidefinite:

    ∫f(s)f(t) κ(ds,dt) ≥0  for every bounded real f.

Let `M=Eκ(S+T)>0` be finite and `m=M/2`. Retain all the source's CTMC, detector and reversal assumptions. For `fi(s)=[exp(sG)(b−r1)]i`, the geometric-measure expectation is

    ∫W dν = Σi πi bi ∫fi(s)fi(t) κ(ds,dt) ≥0.

Symmetry of κ preserves the forward/reverse score identity. The clock-weighted path entropy has expectation `σ E(S+T)=Mσ`; clock/specimen independence is needed here. Jensen therefore gives `A≥exp(−Mσ/2)=exp(−mσ)`. The source proof now yields exactly the same floor

    σ ≥−log(1−D/c)/m =−(2/M)log(1−D/c).

An explicit sufficient construction is

    κ=∫ρ_z⊗ρ_z μ(dz),

with z sampled independently of the specimen/readout, and gaps iid conditional on z. The geometric score becomes an integral of squares. Shared random gaps `S=T=Z` are included by taking `ρ_z=δ_z`; they are generally maximally correlated, not unconditionally iid. For finite gap support, a symmetric nonnegative probability matrix K that is PSD suffices even without an explicitly supplied mixture decomposition. This is a scoped logical extension, not a claim that every correlated clock works or that PSD can be inferred from gap marginals.

The source's anticorrelated pair matrix `[[0,1/2],[1/2,0]]` has eigenvalues ±1/2, so its existing equilibrium false positive remains valid. The authored checker compares that pair with PSD shared-gap and conditional-mixture clocks. In its exact ring fixture with gaps 0.2/0.4, r=0.9 and m=0.3, the shared-gap floor is about 0.12999437 and the conditional-mixture floor about 0.15055725. Both are valid non-iid clock examples under the extended hypothesis; neither improves the source iid floor or establishes a hardware advantage.

### A quantitative spectral penalty for a finite clock law

For a known finite symmetric clock-pair probability matrix K, let p be its marginal, remove zero-mass indices, and suppose

    Dp^(−1/2) K Dp^(−1/2) ≥−η I,   η≥0.

Because G is sub-Markov, `|fi(s)|≤R=max(r,1−r)`. Hence

    ∫W dν ≥−η R² pB,   pB=Σπi bi=E Y0.

The same measure comparison gives a conservative corrected gap

    D_eff = D−ηR²pB.

When `0<D_eff<c`, `σ≥−log(1−D_eff/c)/m`; otherwise this correction supplies no positive certificate. The PSD case has η=0. The source's anticorrelated two-point example has η=1 and the corrected test returns no false-positive floor. This does not repair state-dependent clock selection or detector memory. K, an upper bound on η, pB, timing mean/upper bound and sampling/calibration errors must be acquired together. Finite total variation alone does not control an unbounded clock mean. A second same-team reviewer reconstructed both the PSD extension and this penalty; that is internal scrutiny, not external validation.

## Decisive calibration obstruction and its precise relation to native exchange

Single-time readout calibration does not certify the source's multitime memoryless detector premise. Consider an autonomous reversible two-state specimen, ordinary path entropy zero, independent of a driven three-state detector ring with the source's q=1/20 generator. Let the output depend only on whether the detector is in its two-state B block. At either specimen state, every one-time output calibration is exactly Bernoulli(2/3), including one-time tests while the specimen is clamped.

A memoryless detector with those same b_i=2/3 would have

    E W=(2/3)(2/3−9/10)²=49/1350>0.

The actual memory-bearing ring detector instead has the source's negative fixed-gap mean, approximately −0.004480876. The authored checker reconstructs the six-state product generator, stationary distribution and the matching score enclosures. It passes **only one-time conditional marginals and one-time clamped calibration**. It fails the truth-conditioned multitime independence requirement. This is not a counterexample to PR87's theorem: if the detector is included in the full hidden state, that all-even CTMC has detector-plus-specimen dissipation. The negative score cannot assign that dissipation to the specimen alone. No equilibrium physical detector is fabricated here.

The native-correlated exchange audit has a different but complementary boundary: `SWAP_S` and `SWAP_S⊗SWAP_E` are identical on every reset-product probe, even under joint output measurement, but differ on native correlated preparations. PR87 does not close that gate, constrain a quantum router's bath action, or resolve reference/job assignment. The only supported transfer is the acquisition discipline: certify the relevant joint instrument/history law, not its separate marginals or a restricted calibration class. Passing one-time clamped detector tests is substantially weaker than passing truth-conditioned multitime tests, and passing product-probe router tests is weaker than constraining its action on native correlations.

A direct use of the snapshot certificate across the quantum exchange cut would change its premises: exchange is an intervention that changes joint associations, may disturb baths, and does not supply a stationary autonomous all-even CTMC. Driven optical writing likewise supplies neither passive stationary snapshots nor an acquired physical reversal model. There is no mathematical map in the inspected material that supplies those missing hypotheses. Shared words such as memory, entropy or parity are not a valid bridge.
