6. Historical comparison and what the checks establish
The relevant comparison is not whether the derivation looks unfamiliar, but whether it creates a capability absent from existing mathematics.

Random even subgraphs. Grimmett and Janson already give the uniform cycle-space construction and the conversion from random-cluster samples to weighted even subgraphs. These are direct antecedents of the finite graph step. 

Gaussian fields and currents. Lupu and Werner explicitly connect Gaussian fields, Ising signs, random currents, and random-cluster models. The principal mathematical ingredients therefore cannot be claimed as new. 

Related graph-sampling tractability. Anand and coauthors’ June 2026 version establishes polynomial-time sampling for a graph distribution over vertex subsets weighted by squared perfect-matching counts. That scope should not be conflated with the unrestricted multiplicity law above, but it is another important existing positive tractability result. 

Astra itself. At commit ac174206cc06e1b3bc43b4ab2d0234d9f437d79c, N417 records count-compressed positive Gaussian sampling through reverse-Schur reconstruction: binomial routing and negative-binomial immigration replace individual-event enumeration. I inspected its dossier and located original proof text. This is sufficient to reject the claimed capability as new to Astra, without treating the archive’s entire theorem or implementation as independently certified.

The accompanying code checked nine one-mode generating-function coefficients symbolically, and checked the four-mode matrix identities, hafnians, event probability, and sign ratio exactly. A separate 30,000-sample floating-point test gave empirical total-variation distance approximately 
0.003393
0.003393 on a specified finite partition of a two-mode output space.

That empirical result is a diagnostic—not a uniform error guarantee, proof of the sampler, or evidence of novelty.

7. The precise unresolved barrier
For this construction, the missing capability is preserving interference before sampling without constructing an exponentially expensive representation.

The positive mechanism succeeds because auxiliary normalizers cancel between two genuine probability distributions. With signed coefficients, that same cancellation no longer supplies a positive conditional law. Discarding signs destroys exact zeros; restoring them through an average sign can require exponential samples. The explicit witness shows that this failure is not merely poor conditioning or insufficient numerical precision.

I did not establish an efficient cancellation-preserving replacement on a substantially broader important class. Nor did the other branches establish a generic fast intersection operation or cheap global readout from succinct representations.

The result is a constructive reconstruction and a set of precise falsifications—not new intellectual power meeting the mission.

6. Verification, prior art, and status
The executable package verifies the signed certificates, their singular matrix inequalities, a two-use tensor certificate, seven exact binary decision cases, nine polynomial-sign fixtures, and the rational four-dimensional reversal above. Sixty additional floating-point tests use noncommuting references of dimensions one through five; these are diagnostics, not universal certificates.

The historical comparison places firm limits on the claim. Signed variance lifts and their sufficiency are prior work. Classical chi-square ordering and erasure-versus-symmetric-channel comparisons also have substantial antecedents. 
 Broad noncommutative rational positivity certificates predate this investigation as well; rephrasing the result as a matrix-inverse inequality would not establish novelty. 

The surviving contribution of this investigation is a complete internal derivation of the classical finite-certificate converse, its finite-reference bound, the binary exact algorithm, and an exact three-input separation. Historical originality and independent correctness remain unverified.

The resource boundary is equally explicit: the binary algorithm operates on supplied rational channel descriptions and is implemented; the arbitrary-input semidefinite representation is finite but has not been promoted to a uniform polynomial-time exact feasibility algorithm; no efficient acquisition theorem for unknown physical channels is supplied.

The original historic objective is therefore not established. What has changed is that one substantial consistency problem now has a constructive solution in the classical-coefficient setting, with its failure to extend by scalar testing demonstrated exactly.
