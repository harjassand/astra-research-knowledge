PROVED EXTENSIONS (complete weak proofs in PROOF.txt)

1. Strict positivity. For connected bounded Lipschitz domains of dimension
   d<4, or connected flat tori of dimension d<4, n in W1,infinity unit,
   and a real rank-one-convex homogeneous quartic polynomial with mu>0,
   every attained nonzero mass-constrained amplitude minimizer is strictly
   positive in the interior. On tori or with positive continuous boundary
   data it has a positive global lower bound. No minimizer smoothness is
   assumed. The proof legally removes the sign constraint, derives its
   weak Euler equation, and uses an epsilon-regularized log estimate and
   Morrey. The planar application is fully covered; d>=4 is not claimed.

2. Uniqueness. Every such fixed-mass amplitude problem has one nonnegative
   minimizer on a connected domain, including zero boundary amplitudes.
   The strict density-interpolation deficit (5.1) and interior positivity
   force the ratio of two minimizers constant; equal mass fixes that ratio.

3. Attained weak dual. On a torus or with boundary amplitude bounded away
   from zero, the minimizer gives an exact maximizing scalar flux in
   X={j in L4/3: div j in L1}. The fixed boundary contribution is defined
   by a weak extension pairing, not by an unproved classical normal trace.
   The residual equals the Lagrange multiplier almost everywhere. This is
   exact primal-dual equality with attainment, and covers every smooth
   affine-boundary test after the source's large constant target shift.

4. Certified radial solver criterion. A positive feasible smooth reference
   with verified scalar residual ell<=g_s<=u has energy suboptimality at
   most M(u-ell), and its relative-gradient error to the unique minimizer
   satisfies integral s^4|grad(r_*/s)|^4<=3M(u-ell)/mu. Positive lower
   reference bounds then turn this into W1,4 and uniform amplitude error
   bounds in dimension below four. This supplies a rigorous a posteriori
   accuracy criterion for the scalar subproblem, not a complexity theorem.

DECISIVE LIMIT AND REPAIRED SOURCE INFERENCE

   F(A)=|A|^4,n=e1,g=x,M=1/5. The unique minimizer is r=x, with energy1,

6. The source's short classification needs a general-background shear.
   Rank-one acoustic checks only at e12 and e11 do not force the remaining
   orientation coefficient L in F=alpha u^2+gamma q^2+q(Lu+Mv+Nw) to vanish.
   F=u^2+q^2+qu passes those two checks, yet fails at diag(1,-3),H=e21.
   After e12 forces M=N=0 and gamma>=0, the general shear coefficient
   2alpha+Lq for all real q forces L=0. The conclusion survives: any
   non-polyconvex member of the full invariant rank-one-convex cone has
   mu>0, so the new radial theorems cover the unresolved members.

   unique and strictly positive by the new theorem. This is a direction-
   optimization obstruction, not a negative energy or full-QC counterexample.
